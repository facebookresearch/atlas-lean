# Roadmap: from a local subgroup to the Monster

Build the intended group from explicit transformations of the Griess algebra:
construct the local subgroup, add the FLM symmetry, then establish finiteness,
order, simplicity, and correspondence with the classical construction.

## Chapters

1. [The local subgroup](local-subgroup/README.md)
2. [The extra FLM symmetry](extra-symmetry/README.md)
3. [Identifying the Monster](monster-identification/README.md)

Each chapter records intended results, prerequisites, completion criteria, and
source questions. The detailed theorem graph awaits the FLM passage audit and
human review. A chapter dependency is not evidence of an existing proof.

The Griess-algebra construction, its identification with the weight-two part of
$V^\natural$, and the VOA generator lifts are external workstreams. Their
interfaces must not assume the finite-group conclusions being proved.

See the [coverage contract](../coverage/README.md) and
[source checklist](../sources/flm.md).
The [six endpoint theorem contracts](../../HUMAN_REVIEW.md) give the minimal
human review surface; chapter lemmas supply their eventual proofs.
