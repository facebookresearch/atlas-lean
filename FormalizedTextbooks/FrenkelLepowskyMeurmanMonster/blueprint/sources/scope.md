# Project-authored scope and external interfaces

These notes record agreed targets. They are not extracted FLM passages and
do not claim that the displayed results have been proved.

## Finite-group targets

- A specific extraspecial plus-type $Q$ with $|Q|=2^{25}$, $|Z(Q)|=2$,
  and $Q/Z(Q)\cong(\mathbb F_2^{24},+)$.
- The Conway quotient $\mathrm{Co}_1=\operatorname{Aut}(\Lambda)/\{\pm1\}$
  for the Leech lattice $\Lambda$, with source-required properties proved.
- The concrete FLM extension $1\to Q\to H\to\mathrm{Co}_1\to1$, specified
  by construction and action, rather than its group shape alone.
- The extra symmetry, concrete generated group $G$, and source-required
  subgroup interactions.
- Separate checked proofs of finiteness, Monster order, simplicity, and
  correspondence with the classical FLM construction.

The eventual endpoint is an injective homomorphism from this verified Monster
model into the VOA automorphism group of $V^\natural$. This PR plans its
finite-group prerequisites.

## Griess-algebra interface

The external algebra workstream supplies the concrete $B$, product, form,
and conventions for the FLM transformations. It must prove an algebra
identification $B\cong(V^\natural)_2$, not just a dimension equality.

Fix any real form, complexification, basis, and product normalization through
the source audit and share those conventions across workstreams.

## VOA interface

The external workstream supplies the FLM construction
$V_\Lambda^+\oplus V_\Lambda^{T,+}$, vertex products, vacuum, and conformal
vector. It constructs generator lifts preserving all mode products, including
twisted-sector combinations.

For the proposed assembly argument it also proves that weight two generates
$V^\natural$. Restriction to weight two is then injective on VOA automorphisms.
The subgroup generated by the lifts restricts isomorphically onto the concrete
group on $B$: generator correspondence gives surjectivity; generation gives
injectivity. Inverting the isomorphism gives the faithful VOA action.
All external inputs must be checked before claiming that embedding is complete.

## Later projects

Full VOA automorphism equality is stronger than the embedding. The decomposition
$(V^\natural)_2\cong\mathbf1\oplus W$, with $W$ irreducible of dimension
196883, needs a separate representation proof; the embedding alone does not
imply it.

Holomorphicity, the graded character formula, and Hauptmodul/genus-zero moonshine
are separate VOA, character, and modularity workstreams. They are not completion
requirements for this PR's finite-group milestones.
