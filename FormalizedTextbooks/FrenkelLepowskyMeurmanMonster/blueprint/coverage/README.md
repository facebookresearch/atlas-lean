## Completion rules

After human review of the coarse roadmap, source-verified declaration-sized
articles may move a core row to DECOMPOSED. That status concerns planning;
it is not a proof-completion claim. Finished mathematical milestones require
the checked results and transitive prerequisites in each chapter's completion
criterion. No timetable or exhaustive-source completion is asserted.
