## Shared definitions to review once

Use the concrete FLM Griess algebra $B$, with its specified product and form,
and the Leech lattice $\Lambda$. Fix the field, basis, and normalization from
the source; neither dimension alone nor an arbitrary algebra specifies $B$.
Set $\mathrm{Co}_1=\operatorname{Isom}(\Lambda)/\{\pm1\}$.
Here $\operatorname{Aut}(B)$ means transformations preserving the specified
algebra product and form.

Lean now fixes $Q$ as an explicit standard coordinate model, with cocycle
$\beta(v,w)=\sum_{i=0}^{11}v_iw_{i+12}$. Its group laws, projection, and
diagonal quadratic form are complete. A comparison with FLM, compatible with
the maps and actions used below, remains to be constructed and reviewed.
The group $H$ and extra transformation $s$ must come from audited FLM formulas,
with the inclusion of $Q$ transported through that comparison.
Define $G$ as the subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(B)$
generated by the image of $H$ and $s$. Do not build any of the conclusions below
into these definitions.

## Six finite-group endpoint theorems

### T1. The concrete local subgroup has the claimed structure and action

For the FLM-constructed $Q$ and $H$:

$$
|Q|=2^{25},\qquad Z(Q)=[Q,Q]=\Phi(Q)\cong C_2,
\qquad Q/Z(Q)\cong(\mathbb F_2^{24},+).
$$

Here $\Phi(Q)$ is the Frattini subgroup. The quadratic form induced by squares
has plus type: after identifying $Z(Q)$ with $\mathbb F_2$, it is isometric to
$q(a,b)=\sum_{i=1}^{12}a_i b_i$ on $\mathbb F_2^{12}\oplus\mathbb F_2^{12}$.

The constructed maps give an exact sequence and a faithful action:

$$
1\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow H\longrightarrow\mathrm{Co}_1
\longrightarrow1,
\qquad
\rho:H\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(B).
$$

Exactness includes injectivity, surjectivity, and image/kernel equality.
The extension and action must match FLM, not merely have the same group shape.
No splitting is asserted.

### T2. The explicit extra symmetry defines the intended generated group

The source-specified transformation satisfies
$s\in\operatorname{Aut}(B)$, and the concrete group under study is exactly

$$
G=\langle\rho(H),s\rangle\leq\operatorname{Aut}(B).
$$

This requires the actual transformation, its inverse, and preservation of the
product and form. Selected generator relations alone do not establish T2.

### T3. The generated group is finite

$$
G\text{ is finite}.
$$

This is an unconditional theorem about the group defined in T2.

### T4. The generated group has the Monster order

$$
|G|=2^{46}3^{20}5^9 7^6 11^2 13^3
17\,19\,23\,29\,31\,41\,47\,59\,71.
$$

### T5. The generated group is simple

$$
G\neq1,\qquad
\forall N\trianglelefteq G,\quad N=1\ \text{or}\ N=G.
$$

### T6. The generated group is the classical Monster

For a source-grounded classical Monster model $M_{\mathrm{classical}}$,
construct a group isomorphism

$$
\iota:M_{\mathrm{classical}}\xrightarrow{\sim}G.
$$

The reference model must be specified independently of this isomorphism.
The source audit must settle that model and comparison route. If the adopted
classical model is FLM's construction itself, the comparison may reduce to
matching its concrete definitions; it must not reduce to renaming an arbitrary
group “Monster.” Order and simplicity alone do not prove T6.

Conjugation, intersection, centralizer, normalizer, and counting lemmas are
proof dependencies behind these endpoints. They need kernel-checked proofs,
but need not all appear in the minimal human-facing theorem list.

## Optional next endpoint: the VOA embedding

This is outside the current finite-group workstream. Once the external VOA
workstream supplies checked definitions and proofs, the assembly theorem is:

**T7.** If $B\cong(V^\natural)_2$ with the required products, weight two
generates $V^\natural$, and every member of a specified generating set of $G$
has a VOA-automorphism lift with the correct restriction, then there exists an
injective homomorphism

$$
M_{\mathrm{classical}}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{VOA}}(V^\natural).
$$

Restriction is injective by weight-two generation. The subgroup generated by
the lifts restricts isomorphically onto $G$, so its inverse, composed with
$\iota$, gives the embedding. Existence of lifts is a separate hypothesis,
not a consequence of weight-two generation. All hypotheses must be discharged
before claiming an unconditional VOA embedding.

## What this review establishes

If humans verify that these concrete definitions and endpoint statements express
the intended mathematics, and Lean's trusted kernel checks their proofs with
no unproved mathematical assumptions, then the specified finite-group project
is established. Humans need not manually inspect every infrastructure lemma.
Proof checking and statement review perform different jobs; checking only the
statements does not certify unbuilt or incomplete proofs.

This is a conditional assurance, not an absolute “100%” guarantee independent
of the trusted kernel, logical axioms, or source interpretation. The completed
endpoints must have no sorry/sorryAx, custom mathematical axioms, native_decide,
or unchecked certificates; the repository permits propext, Quot.sound, and
Classical.choice.

T1–T6 do not establish full VOA automorphism equality, holomorphicity, the
character formula, the irreducible 196883-dimensional representation, or
genus-zero moonshine. Those remain separate results.
