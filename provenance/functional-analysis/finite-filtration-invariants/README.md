# Finite-filtration bases and endomorphism invariants

Four MathlibExt modules construct bases along a finite flag, factor determinants
and characteristic polynomials through invariant graded stages, and give
determinant one for filtration-unipotent maps. Three Atlas consumers cover an
empty stage, a proper ℚ² flag, a nonidentity shear and distinct graded eigenvalues.

source-originals.jsonl preserves four whole UTF-8 originals and their author
notices. files.jsonl binds source hashes and exact archive entries, records Meta
headers and standalone documentation, and names the existing P-to-motive repair.
The characteristic-polynomial proofs adapted from Mathlib's Determinant.lean
carry its source, copyright, Apache-2.0 license and authors in code and records.
