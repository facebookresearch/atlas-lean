# C-star algebra and operator foundations

The added MathlibExt APIs provide reusable nonunital C-star algebra constructions, multiplier and compact-operator interfaces, positive and completely positive maps, tensor/completion tools, and continuous-function support. Original zero, empty-index, independent-universe and norm cases are preserved by the copied APIs.

Existing Atlas declarations are reused. Imports are confined to Mathlib, approved supporting Lean packages and MathlibExt. The provenance records under `provenance/functional-analysis/cstar-foundations/` list the exact added files and hash-preserving documentation adaptations. Declaration pages are generated from those docstrings by the scoped wiki sync.
