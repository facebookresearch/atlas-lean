# Substituting for the outermost bound variable of a formula

Let $L$ be a first-order language, $s$ an $L$-term with free variables in $\alpha$ and one bound
variable, and $\varphi$ a bounded $L$-formula with free variables in $\alpha$ and $m + 1$ bound
variables. Substituting $s$ for the bound variable $0$ in every term of the atomic subformulas of
$\varphi$, with the bound variable of $s$ becoming the bound variable $0$, gives again a bounded
formula with free variables in $\alpha$ and $m + 1$ bound variables; falsum, implication and
universal quantification are kept.
