# Completion steps preserve the equational theory

If $E, R \vdash E', R'$ is a step of [Knuth–Bendix completion](knuth-bendix-completion.md), then the [equational theories](../../equational-logic/equational-consequence-and-e-algebras.md) of $E \cup R$ and $E' \cup R'$ coincide: $\approx_{E \cup R}\ =\ \approx_{E' \cup R'}$, rules $l \to r$ being read as equations $l \approx r$.
