# The Knuth–Bendix ordering is a simplification ordering

For every finite signature, precedence and admissible weight function, the [Knuth–Bendix ordering](knuth-bendix-ordering.md) is a [simplification ordering](simplification-ordering.md) on $T_\Sigma(X)$, and hence a [reduction ordering](reduction-ordering.md).

## Depends on

- [Knuth–Bendix ordering](knuth-bendix-ordering.md)
- [Simplification ordering](simplification-ordering.md)
- [Reduction ordering](reduction-ordering.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Simplification orderings over a finite signature are well-founded](dershowitz-s-theorem-on-simplification-orderings.md)
