# Complete theory of a first-order structure

Let $\mathfrak{A}$ be a structure for a first-order language with equality. The *theory* of $\mathfrak{A}$, also called its *complete theory*, is the set $\operatorname{Th} \mathfrak{A}$ of all sentences $\sigma$ of the language that are true in $\mathfrak{A}$, written $\models_{\mathfrak{A}} \sigma$.
