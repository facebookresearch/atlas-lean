# Presburger axiom: trichotomy of $<$

One of the axioms of Presburger arithmetic, in the language with $0$, $S$, $+$, $<$ and equality, is the sentence

$$\forall x\, \forall y\; \Bigl(\neg(x < y) \to \bigl(\neg(x = y) \to y < x\bigr)\Bigr),$$

where $\neg \varphi$ abbreviates $\varphi \to \bot$. Classically this is the trichotomy $x < y \vee x = y \vee y < x$.
