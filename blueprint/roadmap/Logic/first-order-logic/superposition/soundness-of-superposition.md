# Soundness of superposition

Every inference rule of the [superposition calculus](superposition-calculus.md) is correct: for every inference with premises $C_1, \dots, C_n$ and conclusion $C_0$, with variables universally quantified, $\{C_1, \dots, C_n\} \models C_0$.
