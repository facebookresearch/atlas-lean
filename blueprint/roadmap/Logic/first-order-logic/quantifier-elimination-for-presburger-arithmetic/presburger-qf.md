# Quantifier-free Presburger normal forms

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the quantifier-free Presburger normal forms with $n$ bound variables $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$ are the formulas generated by the following clauses, where $s, t$ range over terms of the Presburger language $\{0, S, +\}$ in the variables $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$ (and no other free variables):

- $\bot$ (falsity);
- the equality $s = t$;
- the inequality $s < t$;
- for $m, r \in \mathbb{N}$, the congruence atom $t \equiv r \pmod{m + 1}$;
- the implication $\psi \to \chi$ of two normal forms $\psi$ and $\chi$.

Negation, conjunction and disjunction are obtained from $\bot$ and $\to$, so these are the Boolean combinations of equalities, inequalities and congruence atoms. The modulus is written $m + 1$, so it is always at least $1$.
