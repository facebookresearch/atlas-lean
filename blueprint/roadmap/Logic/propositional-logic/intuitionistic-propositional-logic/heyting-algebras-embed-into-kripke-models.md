# Heyting algebra valuations are represented by Kripke models

Let $v$ be a valuation in a [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra-semantics-of-ipc.md) $H$ with $0 \ne 1$. The prime filters of $H$, ordered by inclusion, with $F \Vdash p$ iff $v(p) \in F$, form a [Kripke model](kripke-models-for-ipc.md) $\mathcal{C}$ in which, for every formula $\psi$,

$$F \Vdash \psi \iff v(\psi) \in F.$$

Consequently $H, v \models \varphi$ if and only if $\mathcal{C} \Vdash \varphi$. The hypothesis $0 \ne 1$ is needed: the one-element Heyting algebra has no prime filter, and a Kripke model has a nonempty set of states.

**Proof sketch.** Induction on $\psi$; primality handles $\vee$. For $\psi' \to \psi''$ with $v(\psi' \to \psi'') \notin F$, the filter generated by $F \cup \{v(\psi')\}$ omits $v(\psi'')$ and [extends to a prime filter](proper-filters-extend-to-prime-filters.md) that forces $\psi'$ but not $\psi''$. For the consequence, if $v(\varphi) \ne 1$, the proper filter $\{1\}$ omits $v(\varphi)$ and extends to a prime filter not forcing $\varphi$.
