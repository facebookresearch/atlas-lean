# Unique satisfying extension in the parsimonious 3-CNF reduction

Assume every variable of a CNF formula $\varphi$ is below `start`, and let $N$ be the number of new
variables of the parsimonious reduction of $\varphi$ to 3-CNF. Every assignment satisfying
$\varphi$ has exactly one extension that satisfies the reduced formula and agrees with it outside
the new variables `start`, …, `start` $+ N - 1$.
