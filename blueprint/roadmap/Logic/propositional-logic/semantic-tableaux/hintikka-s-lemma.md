# Hintikka's lemma

Every [Hintikka set](hintikka-set.md) of propositional formulas is satisfiable.

**Proof.** Let $\mathcal A(P) = 1$ if $P \in H$ and $\mathcal A(P) = 0$ otherwise, and show $\mathcal A(F) = 1$ for every $F \in H$ by induction on the size of $F$.
