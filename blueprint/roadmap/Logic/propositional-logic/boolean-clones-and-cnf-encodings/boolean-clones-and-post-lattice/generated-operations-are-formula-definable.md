# Generated operations are exactly those computed by formulas

Let $G$ be a family of positive-arity Boolean operations. An $n$-ary operation $f$ belongs to the clone generated by $G$ if and only if it is computed by a formula over the signature with one operation symbol for each member of $G$ and input leaves $x_1,\dots,x_n$. In particular a complete family yields, for every $n \ge 1$, formulas (and hence circuits) over $G$ for every $n$-ary Boolean function; no claim is made for nullary functions, so unary constant functions need an input leaf.

## Proof sketch

Induction on superposition derivations in one direction and on formulas in the other.
