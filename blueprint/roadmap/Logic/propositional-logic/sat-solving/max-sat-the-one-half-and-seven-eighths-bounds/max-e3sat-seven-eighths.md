# MAX-E3SAT seven-eighths bound, counting occurrences

Let $s$ be a finite subset of an index type and $F$ a family of clauses over $n$ variables such that, for every $i \in s$, $F_i$ has exactly three literals on three distinct variables. Then some assignment satisfies $F_i$ for at least $\tfrac78 |s|$ of the indices $i \in s$. A uniformly random assignment satisfies each such clause with probability $7/8$; a clause occurring at several indices counts once per index, and for $s = \emptyset$ the bound is $0$.
