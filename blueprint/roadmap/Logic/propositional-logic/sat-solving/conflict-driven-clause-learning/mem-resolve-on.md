# Literals of the resolution on a literal

For clauses $R$ and $C$ and a literal $\ell = (x, p)$, a literal $m$ belongs to the [resolution of $R$ and $C$ on $\ell$](resolve-on.md) exactly when $m \in R$ and $m \ne \ell$, or $m \in C$ and $m \ne (x, \lnot p)$. No occurrence of $\ell$ or its complement is required.
