# Substituting derivations for hypotheses

Let the type of atoms have decidable equality. Given, for every proposition `A` of a context `Γ'`, a derivation of `A` from `Δ` in a theory `T`, every derivation of `B` from `Γ` in `T` yields a derivation of `B` from `(Γ \ Γ') ∪ Δ`, obtained by replacing each appeal to a hypothesis of `Γ'` by its given derivation. The replacement is not capture-avoiding.
