# Res Proof

Let $\varphi = (c_0, \dots, c_{N-1})$ be a finite list of clauses. Resolution proofs from $\varphi$ of a clause $\Gamma$ are generated by:

- Input: for every $i < N$ there is a proof of $c_i$.
- Resolution: for a variable index $v$ and clauses $C, D$ with $\neg x_v \notin C$ and $x_v \notin D$, proofs of $C \cup \{x_v\}$ and of $D \cup \{\neg x_v\}$ combine into a proof of $C \cup D$.
