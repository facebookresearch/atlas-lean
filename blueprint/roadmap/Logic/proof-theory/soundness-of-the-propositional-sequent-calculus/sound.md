# Sound

Let $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ be finite lists of propositional formulas. If the sequent $\Gamma \vdash \Delta$ is derivable in the sequent calculus LK with cut, then it is true under every valuation $v \colon \mathbb{N} \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$: if every formula of $\Gamma$ is true under $v$, then some formula of $\Delta$ is true under $v$.
