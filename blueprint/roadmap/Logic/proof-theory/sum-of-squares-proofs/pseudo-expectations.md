# Degree-$d$ pseudo-expectations

A *degree-$d$ pseudo-expectation* for a system of equations $E$ (and inequalities $I$) is a linear functional $\tilde{\mathbb{E}}$ on polynomials of degree at most $d$ with $\tilde{\mathbb{E}}[1] = 1$, $\tilde{\mathbb{E}}[h g] = 0$ for every $g \in E$ and $\deg(hg) \le d$, and $\tilde{\mathbb{E}}[s^2 \prod_{f\in S} f] \ge 0$ for every finite $S \subseteq I$ and every $s$ with $\deg(s^2\prod_{f \in S} f) \le d$. Evaluation at a common solution is a pseudo-expectation of every degree. If a degree-$d$ pseudo-expectation exists, there is no Positivstellensatz refutation of degree at most $d$.
