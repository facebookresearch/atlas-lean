# Generalized polynomial calculus degree lower bound via respectful boundary expansion

A CNF formula $\mathcal{U} \wedge \mathcal{E}$ requires polynomial calculus degree greater than $(\delta s - 2\xi)/(2d)$ if its clauses and variables can be clustered so that the clustered incidence graph is an $(s, \delta, \xi, \mathcal{E})$-respectful boundary expander with overlap $d$ and all small subformulas are satisfiable together with $\mathcal{E}$.

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a CNF formula. A partial assignment $\rho$ **respects** $\mathcal{E}$ if every clause of $\mathcal{E}$ either has no variable in the domain of $\rho$ or is satisfied by $\rho$; a set of variables $V$ respects $\mathcal{E}$ if some assignment with domain $V$ respects $\mathcal{E}$. A CNF $F$ is **$\mathcal{E}$-respectfully satisfiable by $V$** if some assignment with domain $V$ satisfies $F$ and respects $\mathcal{E}$.

Let $\mathcal{U}$ be a set of CNF formulas and $\mathcal{V}$ a family of variable sets each respecting $\mathcal{E}$. The **$(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})_{\mathcal{E}}$-graph** is bipartite with left vertices $F \in \mathcal{U}$, right vertices $V \in \mathcal{V}$, and an edge whenever $\mathrm{Vars}(F) \cap V \ne \emptyset$; the neighbours $F, V$ are $\mathcal{E}$-respectful if $F$ is $\mathcal{E}$-respectfully satisfiable by $V$. For $\mathcal{U}' \subseteq \mathcal{U}$, the **respectful boundary** $\partial_{\mathcal{E}}(\mathcal{U}')$ is the family of $V \in \mathcal{V}$ that are an $\mathcal{E}$-respectful neighbour of some $F \in \mathcal{U}'$ and no neighbour of any other member of $\mathcal{U}'$. The graph is an **$(s, \delta, \xi, \mathcal{E})$-respectful boundary expander** if $|\partial_{\mathcal{E}}(\mathcal{U}')| \ge \delta |\mathcal{U}'| - \xi$ for all $\mathcal{U}' \subseteq \mathcal{U}$ with $|\mathcal{U}'| \le s$. The **overlap** of $\mathcal{V}$ is $\mathrm{ol}(\mathcal{V}) = \max_x |\{V \in \mathcal{V} : x \in V\}|$.

**Theorem.** Let $\mathcal{F} = \bigwedge_{F \in \mathcal{U}} \bigwedge_{C \in F} C \wedge \mathcal{E}$ be a CNF formula for which $(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})_{\mathcal{E}}$ is an $(s, \delta, \xi, \mathcal{E})$-expander with overlap $\mathrm{ol}(\mathcal{V}) = d$, and suppose that $\mathcal{U}' \wedge \mathcal{E}$ is satisfiable for every $\mathcal{U}' \subseteq \mathcal{U}$ with $|\mathcal{U}'| \le s$. Then every polynomial calculus refutation of $\mathcal{F}$, over any field, requires degree strictly greater than $(\delta s - 2\xi)/(2d)$.

**Without slack.** If $(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})_{\mathcal{E}}$ is an $(s, \delta, 0, \mathcal{E})$-expander with overlap $d$ and $\mathrm{Vars}(\mathcal{U} \wedge \mathcal{E}) = \bigcup_{V \in \mathcal{V}} V$, the satisfiability hypothesis holds automatically and the degree is greater than $\delta s/(2d)$.

## Proof sketch

Taking $\mathcal{E}$ empty, $\mathcal{U}$ the single clauses and $\mathcal{V}$ the singleton variable sets recovers the Alekhnovich-Razborov bound: if the clause-variable incidence graph is an $(s, \delta)$-boundary expander, the degree exceeds $\delta s/2$. The proof follows the Alekhnovich-Razborov strategy: for each low-degree term one computes a support, a small set of clusters determined by the term's neighbourhood, and reduces modulo the ideal generated by the support together with $\mathcal{E}$; respectful assignments, which are autarkies for $\mathcal{E}$, keep this reduction consistent as terms are multiplied by variables, and Razborov's criterion then yields the degree bound. The framework reproves earlier degree bounds, for example for graph pigeonhole and onto-pigeonhole formulas and subset-cardinality formulas, and gives the first one for the functional pigeonhole principle on expanders.
