# Barrier: functional lower bounds cannot handle Boolean instances

The functional lower bound method cannot prove any lower bound for a Boolean instance against a sufficiently strong proof system, one that can efficiently derive the arithmetized conjunction of its axioms; in particular it cannot give lower bounds against $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege or $\mathrm{TC}^0$-Frege.

**Instance admitting a functional lower bound.** Let $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathbb{F}[\vec{x}]$ be a circuit class closed under partial field-element assignments, and let $\mathcal{F} = \{f_i(\vec{x}) = 0\}_i$ be equations in $\mathcal{C}$ that are unsatisfiable together with $\vec{x}^2 - \vec{x}$. $\mathcal{F}$ admits a functional lower bound against $\mathcal{C}$-$\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ if there is $f \in \mathcal{C}$ with $f, \vec{x}^2 - \vec{x}$ unsatisfiable such that (1) no $g \in \mathcal{C}$ satisfies $g = 1/f$ on $\{0,1\}^n$, and (2) there is a $\mathcal{C}$-$\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ proof of $\mathcal{F}$ from $f = 0$ and the Boolean axioms. In that case $\mathcal{F}$ has no $\mathcal{C}$-$\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ refutation.

**Sufficiently strong proof system.** A proof system $P$ for equations in $\mathcal{C}$ that is simulated by some $\mathcal{C}'$-$\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ with $\mathcal{C}' \supseteq \mathcal{C}$ closed under partial assignments and polynomially many sums of products is sufficiently strong if, for some arithmetization scheme (a translation of Boolean formulas into polynomials preserving their values on the cube), every set of Boolean equations $\{f_i = 0\}_i$ has a $P$-derivation of the arithmetization of $\bigwedge_i f_i$ of size $\mathrm{poly}(\sum_i |f_i|)$.

**Theorem.** The functional lower bound method cannot establish lower bounds for any Boolean instance against sufficiently strong proof systems; formally, this holds when the method is applied to $P$ by lower-bounding $\mathcal{C}'$-$\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ for a class closed under partial assignments and polynomially many sums of products. In particular it cannot establish lower bounds against $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege, $\mathrm{TC}^0$-Frege, or constant-depth $\mathrm{IPS}_{\mathrm{LIN}'}$ when the hard instances are Boolean.

## Expansion

A Boolean instance takes only values in $\{0,1\}$ on the cube. To use the method one must lower-bound a single equation $f = 0$ from which $\mathcal{F}$ is efficiently derivable; a sufficiently strong system derives the arithmetization of $\bigwedge_i f_i$, for example $1 - \prod_i (1 - f_i(\vec{x}))$. Its negation $\prod_i (1 - f_i(\vec{x}))$ vanishes on the whole Boolean cube, so it lies in the ideal generated by the Boolean axioms and $\mathcal{F}$ is refutable from them alone, though not necessarily efficiently; from this one shows that no functional lower bound holds for $g = 1/f$ on the cube, so condition (1) cannot be met. This complements the simulation of $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege by constant-depth IPS over $\mathbb{F}_p$: functional lower bounds alone cannot resolve $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege lower bounds through IPS. Previously known hard instances for fragments of IPS, such as subset sum, are all non-Boolean.
