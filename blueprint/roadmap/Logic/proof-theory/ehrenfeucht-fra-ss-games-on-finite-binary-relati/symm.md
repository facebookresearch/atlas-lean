# Symm

Let $R$ and $S$ be Boolean-valued binary relations on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $P$ be a position, a list of pairs $(a, b)$. If Duplicator wins the $k$-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game between $R$ and $S$ from $P$, then Duplicator wins the $k$-round game between $S$ and $R$ from the position obtained from $P$ by replacing each pair $(a, b)$ with $(b, a)$.
