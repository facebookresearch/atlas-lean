# Mono

Let $R$ and $S$ be Boolean-valued binary relations on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $P$ be a position. If Duplicator wins the $(k+1)$-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game between $R$ and $S$ from $P$, then Duplicator wins the $k$-round game between $R$ and $S$ from $P$.
