# Formula with a shared resolvent

Write $a, b, c, d$ for the variables $0, 1, 3, 4$. The sharing example is the CNF formula with the five clauses $I_1 = a \vee b$, $I_2 = a \vee \neg b$, $I_3 = \neg a \vee c$, $I_4 = \neg a \vee d$, $I_5 = \neg c \vee \neg d$, in that order. In its DAG refutation the clause $a \vee a$, the resolvent of $I_1$ and $I_2$ on $b$, is derived once and used in two branches. The comparison concerns the two exhibited refutations only; no optimality over all tree or DAG refutations and no tree-versus-DAG separation is claimed for this formula.
