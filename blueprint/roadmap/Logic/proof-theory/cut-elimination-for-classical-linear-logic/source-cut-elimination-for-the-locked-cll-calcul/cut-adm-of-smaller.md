# Proposition-size induction step for cut admissibility

The induction step on the size of the cut proposition: if cut is admissible at every proposition
smaller than `a`, it is admissible at `a`. The proof distinguishes three cases by the main
connective: `a` of the form `!b`, `a` of the form `ʔb`, and `a` non-exponential, with neither `a`
nor `a⫠` a why-not proposition.
