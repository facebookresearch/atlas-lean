# Question-mark cut via the bang case, duality and exchange

Cut admissibility at a why-not proposition `ʔa`, assuming it for every smaller proposition: from
cut-free proofs of `⊢ ʔa, Γ` and `⊢ !(a⫠), Δ`, a cut-free proof of `⊢ Γ, Δ`. Exchanging the two
premises reduces it to cut admissibility at the bang proposition `!(a⫠)`, whose dual is `ʔa`.
