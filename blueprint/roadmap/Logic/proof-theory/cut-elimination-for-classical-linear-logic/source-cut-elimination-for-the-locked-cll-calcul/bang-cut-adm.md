# Cut admissibility for a bang proposition

Cut admissibility at a bang proposition `!a`, assuming it for every smaller proposition: from
cut-free proofs of `⊢ !a, Γ` and `⊢ ʔ(a⫠), Δ`, a cut-free proof of `⊢ Γ, Δ`. The cut is commuted
upwards through the proof of `⊢ !a, Γ` until `!a` is introduced, by an axiom or by promotion; at a
promotion it becomes an exponential multicut with one selected copy of `ʔ(a⫠)`.
