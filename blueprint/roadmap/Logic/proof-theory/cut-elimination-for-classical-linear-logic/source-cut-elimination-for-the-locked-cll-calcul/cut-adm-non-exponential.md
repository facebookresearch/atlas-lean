# Cut admissibility for non-exponential propositions

Cut admissibility at a proposition `a` such that neither `a` nor `a⫠` is a why-not proposition,
assuming cut admissibility at every proposition smaller than `a`: from cut-free proofs of
`⊢ a, Γ` and `⊢ a⫠, Δ`, a cut-free proof of `⊢ Γ, Δ`. The proof is by induction on the sum of the
heights of the two premises, commuting the cut upwards past rules that do not introduce the cut
proposition.
