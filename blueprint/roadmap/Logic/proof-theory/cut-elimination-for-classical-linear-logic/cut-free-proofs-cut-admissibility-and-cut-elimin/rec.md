# Recursor for cut-free proofs

Induction on cut-free proofs. To define a value, or prove a property, for every cut-free proof,
it suffices to give it for the axiom, `1` and `⊤` rules and, for each of the rules `⊥`, `⅋`,
`⊗`, `⊕₁`, `⊕₂`, `&`, dereliction, weakening, contraction and promotion, to obtain it for the
conclusion from its values on the premises. There is no case for cut, which a cut-free proof
does not contain.
