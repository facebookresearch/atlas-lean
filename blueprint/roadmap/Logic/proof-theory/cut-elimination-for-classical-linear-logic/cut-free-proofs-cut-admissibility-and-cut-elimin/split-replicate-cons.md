# Case split of a cons against replicated copies

Given an equation `n·a + Γ = b, Δ` of multisets, where `n·a` denotes `n` copies of `a` and `Γ` is a
list, either `b` is one of the copies (`b = a`, `n ≥ 1` and `Δ = (n − 1)·a + Γ`), or `b` belongs to
`Γ` (`Γ = b, Γ'` and `Δ = n·a + Γ'`). The multiset `Δ` is arbitrary; the residual list comes from
`Γ`.
