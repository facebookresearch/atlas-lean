# Locating a selected question mark or a residual head

Given an equation `b, Θ = ʔ(a⫠), …, ʔ(a⫠), Δ` of multisets with `n` copies of `ʔ(a⫠)`, either
`b` is one of the copies (`n = m + 1`, `b = ʔ(a⫠)` and `Θ` is `m` copies together with `Δ`), or
`b` belongs to `Δ` (`Δ = b, Δ'` and `Θ` is the `n` copies together with `Δ'`).
