# Superpolynomial lower bounds for Frege proofs

Exhibit tautologies that require superpolynomial-length proofs in ordinary propositional Frege systems. This is a central proof-complexity barrier.

## What would settle it

Define one complete Frege calculus, formula encoding, and proof-size measure. Give a tautology family and prove that no polynomial bounds the size of its shortest Frege proofs. Soundness of Frege alone is much easier and does not yield the lower bound.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Li, Tzameret and Wang describe establishing any superpolynomial size lower bound for Frege proofs as a major open problem in proof complexity. De Rezende, Engström, Ghannane and Risse prove superpolynomial lower bounds for tree-like semantic systems with bounded line size, a restricted setting.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
