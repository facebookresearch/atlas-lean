# Completeness of Nullstellensatz and PC over the Boolean cube

Over a field $\mathbb{K}$, a system $F$ together with the Boolean axioms has a Nullstellensatz refutation (equivalently, a PC refutation) if and only if $F$ has no common zero in $\{0,1\}^n$; refutations of degree at most $n + \max_{f\in F}\deg f$ always exist in that case. The ideal generated by the Boolean axioms is the ideal of polynomials vanishing on the cube, and $1$ lies in the ideal generated by $F$ and the Boolean axioms iff $F$ has no Boolean zero. In particular a CNF formula is unsatisfiable iff its clause polynomials are refutable.

## Proof sketch

Multilinear polynomials are determined by their values on the cube; the indicator polynomials of the points of the cube form a basis, and $1 = \sum_a \delta_a$ where each $\delta_a$ is a multiple of an input not vanishing at $a$, modulo the Boolean axioms.
