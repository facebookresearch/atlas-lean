# Tarski's undefinability theorem

Let $\mathfrak N$ be the standard model of arithmetic, with domain $\mathbb N$, and let $\sigma \mapsto \ulcorner \sigma \urcorner$ be a fixed effective Gödel numbering of the sentences of the language of arithmetic, computable and decodable by unpairing. For every formula $\tau(x)$ of the language of arithmetic with one free variable there is a sentence $\sigma$ such that $\mathfrak N \models \tau(\overline{\ulcorner \sigma \urcorner})$ fails to be equivalent to $\mathfrak N \models \sigma$. That is, no formula defines the set of Gödel numbers of the sentences true in $\mathfrak N$. The proof applies the diagonal lemma to $\neg \tau$ to obtain a liar sentence.
