# Provability in Peano arithmetic

For a sentence $\sigma$ of the language of arithmetic ($0$, $S$, $+$, $\times$, equality), $\mathrm{PA} \vdash \sigma$ means that $\sigma$ is derivable from the axioms of Peano arithmetic in the classical natural deduction calculus for first-order logic, where sentences are regarded as formulas whose free variables range over the infinite set $\mathbb{N}$ of variable names (so that eigenvariables for universal introduction are always available). This relation is sound and complete for semantic consequence from the Peano axioms.
