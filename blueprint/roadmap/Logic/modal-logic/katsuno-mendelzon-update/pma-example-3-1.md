# Example of a possible-models update

With two atoms $b$ and $m$, updating the models of $(b \wedge \neg m) \vee (\neg b \wedge m)$, namely $\{(1,0), (0,1)\}$, by the models of $b$, namely $\{(1,0), (1,1)\}$, under the possible models approach gives $\{(1,0), (1,1)\}$.

## Sources

- H. Katsuno and A. O. Mendelzon, "On the difference between updating a knowledge base and revising it", *Proceedings of KR'91*, 1991, Example 3.1.
