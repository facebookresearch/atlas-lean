# Katsuno–Mendelzon Example 3.1: the two belief sets intersect

In Example 3.1 of Katsuno and Mendelzon, with two atoms $b$ and $m$ and interpretations written in the coordinate order $(b, m)$, the old base is $A = \{10, 01\}$, the models of $(b \wedge \neg m) \vee (\neg b \wedge m)$, and the new information is $B = \{10, 11\}$, the models of $b$. Then $A \cap B = \{10\}$. Updating $A$ by $B$ with Winslett's operator gives $\{10, 11\}$, whereas the revision postulate (R2) would require the result $A \cap B = \{10\}$, since this intersection is nonempty.
