# Fixed-point property of common knowledge

For every group $G$ of agents, formula $\psi$ and world $w$, $C_G\psi$ holds at $w$ if and only if $\psi$ holds at $w$ and $K_i C_G\psi$ holds at $w$ for every $i \in G$. Here common knowledge quantifies over the worlds reachable by finite chains of $G$-steps with zero steps included (the reflexive transitive closure); in particular $C_\emptyset\psi$ is equivalent to $\psi$, unlike the positive-length reachability of Fagin, Halpern, Moses and Vardi (Reasoning About Knowledge, Section 2.2), where $C_\emptyset\psi$ would be vacuously true.
