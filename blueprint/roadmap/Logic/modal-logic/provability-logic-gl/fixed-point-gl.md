# The de Jongh–Sambin fixed point theorem

Let $p$ be modalised in $A$. There is a formula $H$ in which $p$ does not occur, all of whose atoms
occur in $A$, such that $\mathrm{GL} \vdash H \leftrightarrow A(H)$.

The fixed point is unique up to provable equivalence in GL. The general case follows from the
case of a single boxed occurrence by induction on the number of boxed subformulas of $A$
containing $p$; this reduction is not taken from the source. The theorem is due to de Jongh and
Sambin (1975), as attributed in the source below, which also lists algorithmic proofs by Sambin
and Valentini and by Reidhaar-Olson.
