# Modus ponens with a K theorem

If $\varphi \to \psi$ is a theorem of K and $\varphi$ is a theorem of a normal modal logic
$\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$, then $\psi$ is a theorem of $\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$.

## Informally uses

- [Monotonicity of theorems in the extra axioms](../normal-modal-logics/mono.md): every theorem of K is a theorem of
  $\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$.
