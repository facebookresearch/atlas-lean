# Löb's axiom

For a modal formula $\varphi$, Löb's axiom is

$$\Box(\Box\varphi \to \varphi) \to \Box\varphi .$$

Read with $\Box$ as provability in Peano arithmetic, it is the formalized Löb theorem
$\mathrm{PA} \vdash \mathrm{Prov}(\ulcorner \mathrm{Prov}(\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner) \to \varphi\urcorner) \to
\mathrm{Prov}(\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner)$.
