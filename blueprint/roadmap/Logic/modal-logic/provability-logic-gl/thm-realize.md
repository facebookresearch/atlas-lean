# Realizations of theorems of a normal modal logic are theorems

In a Hilbert–Bernays–Löb provability structure, if $T \vdash f^*(\psi)$ for every extra axiom
$\psi$ of $\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$, then $T \vdash f^*(\varphi)$ for every theorem $\varphi$ of
$\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$.

Proof: induction on the derivation. The propositional axioms and modus ponens are those of $T$,
the distribution axiom is realized by D2 and necessitation by D1.
