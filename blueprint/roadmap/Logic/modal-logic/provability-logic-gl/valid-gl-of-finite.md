# Soundness of GL for finite strict partial orders

Let $M$ be a Kripke model with finitely many worlds whose accessibility relation is transitive and
irreflexive. Every theorem of GL is valid in $M$.

Proof: on a finite set, a transitive irreflexive relation has no infinite ascending chain, so GL
is sound on $M$.
