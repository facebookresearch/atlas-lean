# Arithmetical realization of a modal formula

Given a Hilbert–Bernays–Löb provability structure and a sentence $f(p)$ for each atom $p$, the
realization $f^*$ sends $p$ to $f(p)$, $\bot$ to falsity, $\varphi \to \psi$ to
$f^*(\varphi) \to f^*(\psi)$ and $\Box\varphi$ to $\mathrm{Prov}(f^*(\varphi))$.
