# Extra axioms of the provability logic GL

The provability logic GL (also written L, KW or PrL) is the normal modal logic
$\mathrm{K} \oplus \text{Löb}$: its extra axioms are the instances
$\Box(\Box\varphi \to \varphi) \to \Box\varphi$ of Löb's axiom for all formulas $\varphi$, and
its theorems are closed under modus ponens and necessitation.
