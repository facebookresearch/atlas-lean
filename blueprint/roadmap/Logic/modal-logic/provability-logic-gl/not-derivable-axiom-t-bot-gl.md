# GL does not prove reflection for `⊥`

$\mathrm{GL} \nvdash \Box\bot \to \bot$. Hence GL does not contain the logic T. Moreover no
consistent extension of GL closed under necessitation proves $\Box\bot \to \bot$: necessitation
and Löb's axiom would give $\Box\bot$ and then $\bot$. (Solovay's logic GLS, which adds all
instances of $\Box\psi \to \psi$ to GL, is closed under modus ponens only.)

Proof: in the one-world irreflexive model, $\Box\bot$ holds vacuously and $\bot$ fails, while
every GL theorem is valid there.
