# Atom occurring only under a box

An atom $p$ is modalised in a formula $A$ when every occurrence of $p$ in $A$ lies in the scope of
some $\Box$. For example $p$ is modalised in $\neg\Box p$ and in $\Box(p \to q)$, but not in
$p \to \Box p$. This is the hypothesis of the de Jongh–Sambin fixed point theorem.
