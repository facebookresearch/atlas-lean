# Positive replacement

If $A \vdash B$ is derivable in RC, then $C[A/p] \vdash C[B/p]$ is derivable for every strictly
positive formula $C$ and atom $p$. Since all connectives are monotone, no polarity condition is
needed.

Proof: induction on $C$, using the conjunction rules and the monotonicity rule for
$\langle n \rangle$.
