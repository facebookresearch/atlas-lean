# The order `<₀` on worms

For worms $\alpha$ and $\beta$, $\alpha <_0 \beta$ means $\beta \vdash \langle 0 \rangle\alpha$ in RC:
arithmetically, the reflection principle $\beta$ proves the consistency of $\alpha$. The source
defines it in GLP by $\mathrm{GLP} \vdash \beta \to \langle 0 \rangle\alpha$; the two definitions agree
by Dashkov's theorem.
