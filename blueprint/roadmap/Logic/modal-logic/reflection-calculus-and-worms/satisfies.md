# Truth of a strictly positive formula in a Kripke model

In a Kripke model with relations $(R_n)$ and valuation $V$: $x \Vdash p$ iff $p \in V(x)$;
$x \Vdash \top$ always; $x \Vdash A \wedge B$ iff $x \Vdash A$ and $x \Vdash B$; and
$x \Vdash \langle n \rangle A$ iff some $y$ with $x R_n y$ satisfies $A$. A sequent $A \vdash B$ is
true in the model when every world satisfying $A$ satisfies $B$.
