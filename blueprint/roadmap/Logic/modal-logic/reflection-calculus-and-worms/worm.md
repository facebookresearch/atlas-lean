# Worm

A worm is a closed strictly positive formula without conjunction,
$\langle n_1 \rangle \langle n_2 \rangle \cdots \langle n_k \rangle \top$, written as the word
$n_1 n_2 \cdots n_k$; the empty word is $\top$. Every closed strictly positive formula is
equivalent to a worm.
