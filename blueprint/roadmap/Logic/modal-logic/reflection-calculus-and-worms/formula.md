# Strictly positive formulas

The strictly positive formulas of the Reflection Calculus are generated by

$$A ::= p \mid \top \mid A \wedge A \mid \langle n \rangle A \qquad (n \in \mathbb{N}),$$

with $p$ ranging over atoms. There is no negation, disjunction or box. The source also allows a
modality $\omega$ (full uniform reflection); it is omitted here.

- L. D. Beklemishev, "Positive provability logic for uniform reflection principles", arXiv:1304.4396v3 (2013), *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic* 165 (2014), Section 2.
