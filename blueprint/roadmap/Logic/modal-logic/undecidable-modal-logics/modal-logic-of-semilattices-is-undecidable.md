# The modal logic of semilattices is undecidable

The modal information logic of semilattices, the logic in one binary diamond of all frames given by semilattices, is undecidable; equivalently, the variety generated by complex algebras of semilattices has an undecidable equational theory.

**Theorem (Knudstorp).** Let $\mathsf{SL}$ be the class of semilattices $(S, \cdot)$ (sets with an associative, commutative and idempotent binary operation), each read as an [associative frame](associative-normal-modal-logic.md) with $Rxyz$ iff $x = y \cdot z$. The logic $\mathrm{Log}(\mathsf{SL})$ of $\mathcal L$-formulas valid on every semilattice is undecidable. Equivalently, the variety $\mathrm{Var}(\mathsf{SL}^+)$ generated by the complex algebras of semilattices has an undecidable equational theory.

The same holds for the variety $\mathsf{BSl}$ of Boolean semilattices, the associative BAOs satisfying $x \circ y \approx y \circ x$ and $x \le x \circ x$, that is, for the logic $\mathbf{AK}_2 \oplus (p \circ q) \to (q \circ p) \oplus p \to (p \circ p)$ (Theorem 3.8 of the source); since $\mathsf{BSl}$ is finitely axiomatized, it follows that $\mathsf{BSl}$ is not generated by its finite members (Corollary 3.9). Similarly the modal logics of lattices, modular lattices and distributive lattices of Wang and Wang are undecidable (Theorem 3.10).

## Expansion

Semilattices are associative, so $\mathbf{AK}_2 \subseteq \mathrm{Log}(\mathsf{SL})$, and $(\mathcal P(\mathbb N), \cup)$ is a semilattice, so $\mathrm{Log}(\mathsf{SL}) \subseteq \mathrm{Log}(\mathcal P(\mathbb N), \cup)$. For $\mathsf{BSl}$, the complex operation of $(\mathcal P(\mathbb N), \cup)^+$ is commutative and square-increasing, so $(\mathcal P(\mathbb N), \cup)^+ \in \mathsf{BSl}$.
