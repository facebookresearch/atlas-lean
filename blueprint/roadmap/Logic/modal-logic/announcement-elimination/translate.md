# Translate

The announcement-eliminating translation: for a formula $\varphi$ and a formula $\psi$ that may contain public announcements, the translation of $\psi$ relative to $\varphi$ expresses "after announcing $\varphi$, $\psi$" without announcements, and is defined by recursion on $\psi$. Successive announcements compose their conditions by conjunction. If $\varphi$ contains neither announcements nor common knowledge, then neither does the translation; at the top level the ambient formula is a tautology, so the top-level translation is always free of both.
