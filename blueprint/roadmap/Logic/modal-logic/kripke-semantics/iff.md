# Biconditional

For modal formulas $\varphi$ and $\psi$, the biconditional $\varphi \leftrightarrow \psi$ is the
formula $(\varphi \to \psi) \wedge (\psi \to \varphi)$, with conjunction encoded in the
$\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis. It holds at a world exactly when $\varphi$ and $\psi$ have the same
truth value there.
