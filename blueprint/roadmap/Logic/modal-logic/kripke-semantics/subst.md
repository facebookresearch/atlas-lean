# Substitution of a formula for an atom

For a modal formula $\varphi$, an atom $p$ and a formula $\psi$, the formula $\varphi[p := \psi]$
replaces every occurrence of $p$ in $\varphi$ by $\psi$, commuting with $\bot$, $\to$ and
$\Box$. When $\varphi$ is written $\varphi(p)$, the result is written $\varphi(\psi)$.

## Sources

- P. Blackburn, M. de Rijke, Y. Venema, *Modal Logic*, Cambridge University Press, 2001
  (uniform substitution).
