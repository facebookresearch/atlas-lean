# The translation preserves satisfaction

Let $m$ be a unimodal model on a nonempty type
of worlds, $w$ a world and $\varphi$ a formula of the diamond presentation. Then the translation of $\varphi$
into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis holds at $w$ in the Kripke model of $m$ if and only if
$\varphi$ holds at $w$ in $m$.
