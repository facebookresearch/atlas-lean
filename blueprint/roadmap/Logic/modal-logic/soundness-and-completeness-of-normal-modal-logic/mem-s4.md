# Soundness for the semantic logic S4

Let $\varphi$ be a formula of the diamond presentation. If the translation
of $\varphi$ into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis is a theorem of the Hilbert system S4, then, for
every type of worlds, $\varphi$ belongs to the semantic logic S4: it holds at every world of every
unimodal model on that type whose accessibility relation is reflexive and transitive.
