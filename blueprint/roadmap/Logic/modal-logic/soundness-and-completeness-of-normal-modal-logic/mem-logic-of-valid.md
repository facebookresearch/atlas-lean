# Validity of the translation gives membership in a semantic logic

Let $S$ be a class of unimodal models on a type of worlds and $\varphi$ a formula of the diamond presentation. Suppose that, if
the type of worlds is nonempty, the translation of $\varphi$ into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis is
valid in the Kripke model of every model in $S$. Then $\varphi$ belongs to the semantic logic
of $S$: it holds at every world of every model in $S$.
