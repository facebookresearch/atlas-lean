# Completeness for the semantic logic S5

Let $\varphi$ be a formula of the diamond presentation
over a type of atoms $A$. Then the translation of $\varphi$ into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis is
a theorem of S5 if and only if, for every type of worlds $W$ in the universe of $A$, $\varphi$
belongs to the semantic logic S5 over $W$ and $A$, that is, $\varphi$ holds at every world of
every unimodal model with worlds $W$ whose accessibility relation $R$ is reflexive, transitive and
right Euclidean ($w R u$ and $w R v$ imply $u R v$).
