# Consistency of K4

The formula $\bot$ is not a theorem of K4: every theorem of K4 is valid in the one-world model
whose world sees itself, which is transitive, and $\bot$ fails there.

## Informally uses

- [Consistency of normal modal logics](../normal-modal-logics/not-derivable-bot.md): the
  one-world reflexive model validates every instance of axiom 4.
