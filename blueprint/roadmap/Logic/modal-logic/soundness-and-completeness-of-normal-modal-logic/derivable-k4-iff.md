# K4-theorems are the formulas valid in every transitive Kripke model

A formula $\varphi$ is a theorem of K4 if and only if $\varphi$ is valid in every Kripke model with
transitive accessibility relation whose worlds form a type in the universe of the atoms. The
restriction on universes is harmless: soundness holds for models in every universe, and the
canonical model lives in the universe of the atoms.
