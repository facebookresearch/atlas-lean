# Extra axioms of K4

The modal logic K4 is the normal modal logic $\mathrm{K} \oplus 4$ whose extra axioms are the
instances of the transitivity axiom

$$4:\quad \Box\varphi \to \Box\Box\varphi$$

for all formulas $\varphi$. Its theorems are the closure of these instances, the propositional
axioms and the distribution axiom under modus ponens and necessitation.

## Background

- [Theorems of a normal modal logic](../normal-modal-logics/derivable.md): the Hilbert system
  $\mathrm{K} \oplus \Lambda$ instantiated here with $\Lambda$ the instances of 4.
- [Extra axioms of S4](../normal-modal-logics/s4.md): S4 adds the reflexivity axiom T to K4.
