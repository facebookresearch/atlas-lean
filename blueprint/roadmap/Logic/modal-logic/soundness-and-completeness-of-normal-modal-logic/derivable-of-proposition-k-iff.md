# Completeness for the semantic logic K

Let $\varphi$ be a formula of the diamond presentation over
a type of atoms $A$. Then the translation of $\varphi$ into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis is a
theorem of K if and only if, for every type of worlds $W$ in the universe of $A$, $\varphi$ belongs
to the semantic logic K over $W$ and $A$, that is, $\varphi$ holds at every world of every unimodal
model with worlds $W$.
