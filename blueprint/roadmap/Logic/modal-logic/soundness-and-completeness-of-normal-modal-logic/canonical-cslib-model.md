# The canonical model as a unimodal model

For a set $\Lambda$ of extra axioms, this is the
unimodal model of the semantics of the diamond presentation whose worlds are the maximal consistent sets of
$K \oplus \Lambda$, in which $M$ sees $N$ iff $\varphi \in N$ for every formula $\varphi$ with
$\Box\varphi \in M$, and in which an atom $p$ is true at $M$ iff $p \in M$.

No consistency hypothesis on $K \oplus \Lambda$ is needed here, since a unimodal model may have no
worlds.
