# Completeness for the semantic logic T

For a formula $\varphi$ of the diamond presentation, the translation
of $\varphi$ into the $\bot$, $\to$, $\Box$ basis is a theorem of the Hilbert system T if and only
if, for every type of worlds in the universe of the atoms, $\varphi$ belongs to the semantic logic
T over that type, that is, $\varphi$ holds at every world of every unimodal model on that type whose
accessibility relation is reflexive.
