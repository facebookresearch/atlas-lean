# Soundness of K4 for transitive Kripke models

Let $M$ be a Kripke model whose accessibility relation is transitive. Every theorem of K4 is valid
in $M$: it holds at every world.

## Informally uses

- [Soundness of modal Hilbert systems for Kripke models](../normal-modal-logics/valid.md): it
  suffices that every instance of $\Box\varphi \to \Box\Box\varphi$ is valid in $M$.
- [Axiom 4 is valid on transitive Kripke models](../kripke-semantics/valid-axiom-four.md): the
  instances of 4 are valid when the relation is transitive.

## Sources

- P. Blackburn, M. de Rijke, Y. Venema, *Modal Logic*, Cambridge University Press, 2001, §4.2.
