# Dalal satisfies KM

Let `σ` be a finite set of atoms with decidable equality, and represent each propositional formula over `σ` by its finite set of models (interpretations `σ → Bool`). Dalal's revision operator satisfies the Katsuno–Mendelzon revision postulates (R1)–(R6). This shows that the right-to-left direction of the representation theorem is not vacuous, by an explicitly constructed operator.
