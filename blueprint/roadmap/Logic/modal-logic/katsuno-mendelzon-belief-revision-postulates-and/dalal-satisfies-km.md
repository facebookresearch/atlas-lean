# Dalal's revision satisfies the Katsuno–Mendelzon postulates

Let $\sigma$ be a finite set of atoms with decidable equality, and represent each propositional formula over $\sigma$ by its finite set of models (interpretations $\sigma \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$). Dalal's revision operator, in its total form that revises the empty base by $B$ to $B$ (see the [Dalal key](dalal-key.md)), satisfies the Katsuno–Mendelzon revision postulates (R1)–(R6). This shows, with an explicitly constructed operator, that the right-to-left direction of the representation theorem is not vacuous.
