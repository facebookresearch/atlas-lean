# Dalal Le

Let `σ` be a finite set of atoms with decidable equality, and represent each propositional formula over `σ` by its finite set of models (interpretations `σ → Bool`). The Dalal preorder relative to a model set `A` puts `I` before `J` when the Dalal key of `I` relative to `A` is at most that of `J`, that is, when `I` is at least as close to `A` as `J` in Hamming distance.
