# Intuitionistic modal logics CK and IK

This node covers the intuitionistic modal logics Constructive K (CK) and Intuitionistic K (IK) over
intuitionistic propositional logic with necessity $\Box$ and possibility $\Diamond$ as primitive
operators, their relational semantics, and the logics between them. CK and IK differ in their treatment of $\Diamond$. IK is the logic
specified by Fischer Servi's translation into classical bimodal logic with K and S4 modalities and by the standard
translation into intuitionistic first-order logic. Logics between CK and IK can disagree even on
their $\Diamond$-free fragments, and only some of them are conservative over the standard
axiomatization of intuitionistic modal logic with $\Box$ alone.

## Expansion

A sound expansion fixes one syntax with both modal operators and has separate pages for: the Hilbert
axiomatizations of CK and of IK as derivation relations; birelational (Kripke) models with an
intuitionistic preorder and a modal accessibility relation, and the frame conditions linking them
that each axiom requires; soundness and completeness of CK and of IK for their stated model classes,
each with its own source; and the conservativity or non-conservativity of the intermediate logics
over the $\Box$-only fragment. A primitive modal syntax with classical pointwise semantics does not
cover this node.
