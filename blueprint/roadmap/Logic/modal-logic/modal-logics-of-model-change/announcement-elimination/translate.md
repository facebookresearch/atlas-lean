# Announcement-eliminating translation

The announcement-eliminating translation takes an ambient formula $\varphi$ of the full epistemic language and a body $\psi$ of the [announcement language without common knowledge](ann-formula.md) (atoms, falsity, implication, knowledge and public announcements), and is defined by recursion on $\psi$: an atom $p$ becomes $\varphi \to p$, falsity becomes $\varphi \to \bot$, implication is translated componentwise, $K_i\psi$ becomes $\varphi \to K_i(\text{translation of }\psi)$, and an announcement $[\chi]\psi$ becomes the translation of $\psi$ relative to $\varphi \wedge (\text{translation of }\chi)$, so successive announcements compose their conditions by conjunction. The result [expresses](sat-translate.md) "after announcing $\varphi$, $\psi$". The ambient formula is kept as written, so the translation is [free of announcements and common knowledge](ann-common-free.md) exactly when $\varphi$ is: if $\varphi$ contains an announcement or common knowledge, so does the result. At the top level the ambient formula is the tautology $\bot \to \bot$, so the [top-level translation](translate-top.md) is always free of both.
