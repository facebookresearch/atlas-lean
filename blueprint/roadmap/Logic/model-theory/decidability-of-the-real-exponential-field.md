# Decidability of the real exponential field

Is the first-order theory of $(\mathbb{R}, +, \cdot, <, \exp)$ decidable? An answer would determine the possibility of a complete algorithmic treatment of a broad family of real-exponential statements.

## What would settle it

Specify the first-order language and semantics. Construct a total computable decider whose output agrees with truth for every sentence, or prove undecidability by reduction. A decision procedure conditional on Schanuel's conjecture is not an unconditional solution.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Berarducci and Gallinaro record that Macintyre and Wilkie proved decidability assuming the real Schanuel conjecture and that decidability is equivalent to a weak form of that conjecture; they give a conditional axiomatization. An unconditional answer is not given there.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
