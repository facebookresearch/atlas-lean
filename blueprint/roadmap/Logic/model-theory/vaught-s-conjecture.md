# Vaught's conjecture

Must a complete theory in a countable first-order language have either at most countably many or continuum many countable models up to isomorphism?

## What would settle it

State models, isomorphism classes, and the stated cardinal dichotomy. Do not assume CH to obtain a conditional/trivial version and call it the general solution. A perfect-set formulation is a distinct strengthened target and must be labeled accordingly.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Pillay and Tanović survey the problem, record that it is confirmed for various special classes of theories, and describe Vaught's conjecture as widely open. Counterexamples for infinitary sentences do not refute the first-order conjecture.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
