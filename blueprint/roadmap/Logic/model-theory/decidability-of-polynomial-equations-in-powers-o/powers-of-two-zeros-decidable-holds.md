# Zeros of integer polynomials at powers of two are decidable

The following holds: it is decidable, given a polynomial $P \in \mathbb{Z}[x_1, \dots, x_m]$, whether there are $n_1, \dots, n_m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $P(2^{n_1}, \dots, 2^{n_m}) = 0$. Precisely, the set of list-of-monomials encodings of integer polynomials that have a zero at powers of two is a computable set. The question is related to the Mordell-Lang conjecture for tori.
