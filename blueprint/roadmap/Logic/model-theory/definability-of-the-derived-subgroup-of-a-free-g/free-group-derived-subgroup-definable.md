# Kourovka Problem 1.19: definability of the derived subgroup of a free group

Statement (Kourovka Notebook Problem 1.19, A. I. Mal'cev; Yu. L. Ershov, the question on the derived subgroup): there is a rank $r \ge 2$ such that the derived subgroup $[F_r, F_r]$ of the free group $F_r$ on $r$ generators is first-order definable in $F_r$. Here definable means: there is a formula $\phi(x)$ in one free variable of the first-order language of groups (multiplication, inversion and the identity), with parameters from $F_r$, such that

$$[F_r, F_r] = \{ g \in F_r : F_r \models \phi(g) \}.$$

Rank $1$ is excluded because $[F_1, F_1]$ is trivial and hence definable. It is false; the refutation is [The derived subgroup of a free group of rank at least 2 is not definable](free-group-derived-subgroup-definable-re.md).
