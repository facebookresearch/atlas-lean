# Truth of an atom about an abelian group and its subgroups

Let $A$ be an abelian group, $x : \mathbb{N} \to A$ an element assignment and $X : \mathbb{N} \to \{\text{subgroups of } A\}$ a subgroup assignment. Truth of an atom is defined by:

- $t = 0$ holds if $t(x) = 0$;
- $t \in X_j$ holds if $t(x) \in X_j$;
- $X_i \subseteq X_j$ holds if $X_i \subseteq X_j$ as subgroups of $A$.

Here $t(x)$ is the value of the linear term $t$ under $x$.
