# Linear terms in the language of abelian groups

A term of the language $\{+, -, 0\}$ of abelian groups in the element variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$ is coded as a finite list of pairs $(c, i)$ with $c \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$; the list $((c_1, i_1), \dots, (c_k, i_k))$ stands for the integer linear combination

$$c_1 x_{i_1} + \cdots + c_k x_{i_k},$$

and the empty list stands for $0$.
