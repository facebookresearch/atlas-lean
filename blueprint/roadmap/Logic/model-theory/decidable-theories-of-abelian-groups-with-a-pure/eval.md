# Value of a linear term in an abelian group

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $x : \mathbb{N} \to A$ an assignment of the element variables. The value of the linear term $t = ((c_1, i_1), \dots, (c_k, i_k))$ under $x$ is

$$t(x) = c_1 x_{i_1} + \cdots + c_k x_{i_k} \in A,$$

where $c\, a$ is the integer multiple of $a$; the empty term has value $0$.
