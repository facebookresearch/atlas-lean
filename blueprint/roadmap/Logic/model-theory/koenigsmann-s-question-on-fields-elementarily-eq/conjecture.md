# Koenigsmann's question on fields elementarily equivalent to $\mathbb{Q}$

Question (Koenigsmann). Let $\mathbb{Q}^*$ and $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$ be fields, each elementarily equivalent to $\mathbb{Q}$ in the language of rings, with a field embedding $\mathbb{Q}^* \hookrightarrow \mathbb{Q}^{**}$, so that $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$ is an extension of $\mathbb{Q}^*$. Statement: $\mathbb{Q}^*$ is relatively algebraically closed in $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$, that is, every element of $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$ that is algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}^*$ lies in $\mathbb{Q}^*$.

## Sources

- P. Dittmann, "Irreducibility of polynomials over global fields is diophantine", arXiv:1601.07829, https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.07829
