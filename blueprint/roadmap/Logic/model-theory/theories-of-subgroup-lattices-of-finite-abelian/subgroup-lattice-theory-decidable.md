# Kourovka Problem 3.13: decidability of subgroup-lattice theory of finite abelian groups

Statement (Kourovka Notebook Problem 3.13, Yu. L. Ershov and A. I. Kokorin): the elementary theory of the subgroup lattices of finite abelian groups is decidable. Precisely, the set of coded prenex sentences $s$ of the lattice language $\{\le\}$ such that $s$ holds in the subgroup lattice of every finite abelian group $A$ is a computable set of codes.

Every first-order sentence of the lattice language is effectively equivalent to such a prenex code with a conjunctive normal form matrix over $\le$, so decidability does not depend on the coding. It is false; the refutation is [Undecidability of the subgroup-lattice theory of finite abelian groups](subgroup-lattice-theory-decidable-refute.md).
