# Free groups are recognized by an elementary class of subsemigroup lattices

The following holds: there is a first-order theory $T$ in the language of orders such that for every nonempty semigroup $S$, the lattice of all subsemigroups of $S$ (the empty one included, ordered by inclusion) satisfies $T$ if and only if $S$ is a free group of some rank, $0$ included. A single lattice sentence suffices.
