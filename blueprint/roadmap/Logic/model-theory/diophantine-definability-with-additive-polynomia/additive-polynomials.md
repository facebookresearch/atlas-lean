# Additive polynomials over $\mathbb{F}_p$

For a natural number $p$, the additive polynomials of $\mathbb{F}_p[Z]$ (with $\mathbb{F}_p = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$) are the polynomials all of whose monomials with nonzero coefficient have an exponent that is a power of $p$:

$$\Bigl\{F \in \mathbb{F}_p[Z] : F = \alpha_0 Z + \alpha_1 Z^{p} + \alpha_2 Z^{p^2} + \cdots + \alpha_n Z^{p^n} \text{ for some } n \in \mathbb{N},\ \alpha_i \in \mathbb{F}_p\Bigr\}.$$

The zero polynomial is included; nonzero constants are not.
