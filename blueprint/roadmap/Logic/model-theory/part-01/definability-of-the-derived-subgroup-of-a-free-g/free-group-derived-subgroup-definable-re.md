# The derived subgroup of a free group of rank at least 2 is not definable

This result refutes the proposition stated as [Kourovka Problem 1.19: definability of the derived subgroup of a free group](free-group-derived-subgroup-definable.md). The statement is false: for every rank $r \ge 2$, the derived subgroup $[F_r, F_r]$ of the free group $F_r$ on $r$ generators is not first-order definable in $F_r$. That is, no formula $\phi(x)$ in one free variable of the first-order language of groups (multiplication, inversion and the identity), with parameters from $F_r$, satisfies $[F_r, F_r] = \{ g \in F_r : F_r \models \phi(g) \}$.

The relevant result is that proper non-cyclic subgroups of a non-abelian free group of finite rank are not definable with the free generators as constants; for $r \ge 2$ the derived subgroup is proper and not cyclic.
