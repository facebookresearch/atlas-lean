# Kourovka 18.16: definable endomorphisms of ordered abelian groups

Question (O. V. Belegradek, Kourovka Notebook 18.16). Statement: let $G$ be an ordered abelian group (an abelian group with a linear order compatible with addition) and let $f : G \to G$ be a group endomorphism whose graph $\{(x, f(x)) : x \in G\}$ is definable with parameters from $G$ in the pure language of ordered abelian groups ($0$, $-$, $+$, $\le$). Then $f$ is multiplication by a rational number: there are $m \in \mathbb{Z}$ and an integer $n > 0$ with

$$n\, f(x) = m\, x \quad\text{for all } x \in G.$$

Since ordered abelian groups are torsion-free, this says $f(x) = \tfrac{m}{n} x$.
