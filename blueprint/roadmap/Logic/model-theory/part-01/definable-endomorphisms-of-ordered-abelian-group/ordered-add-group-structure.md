# The canonical structure on an ordered abelian group

Let $G$ be an abelian group (written additively) with a linear order. The canonical structure on $G$ for the pure language of ordered abelian groups interprets the symbols as

$$0 \mapsto 0_G, \qquad -x \mapsto -x, \qquad x + y \mapsto x + y, \qquad x \le y \iff x \le_G y,$$

that is, by the group's zero, negation, addition and order.
