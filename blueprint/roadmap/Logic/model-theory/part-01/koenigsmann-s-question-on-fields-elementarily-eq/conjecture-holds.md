# Koenigsmann's question on fields elementarily equivalent to $\mathbb{Q}$: positive answer

This result proves the proposition stated as [Koenigsmann's question on fields elementarily equivalent to $\mathbb{Q}$](conjecture.md). Let $\mathbb{Q}^* \subseteq \mathbb{Q}^{**}$ be fields, each elementarily equivalent to $\mathbb{Q}$ in the language of rings, where $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$ is an extension of $\mathbb{Q}^*$ by a field embedding. Then every element of $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$ that is algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}^*$ lies in $\mathbb{Q}^*$: $\mathbb{Q}^*$ is relatively algebraically closed in $\mathbb{Q}^{**}$.
