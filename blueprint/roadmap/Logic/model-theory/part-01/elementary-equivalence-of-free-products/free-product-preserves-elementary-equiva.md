# Kourovka Problem 9.66(a): Jónsson's conjecture on free products

Statement (Kourovka Notebook Problem 9.66(a), A. D. Taimanov; B. Jónsson's conjecture, also known as R. L. Vaught's conjecture): elementary equivalence of groups is preserved by free products. For all groups $G_1, G_2, H_1, H_2$, if $G_1 \equiv G_2$ and $H_1 \equiv H_2$, then

$$G_1 * H_1 \equiv G_2 * H_2,$$

where $\equiv$ is elementary equivalence in the first-order language of groups (multiplication, inversion and the identity) and $*$ is the free product.
