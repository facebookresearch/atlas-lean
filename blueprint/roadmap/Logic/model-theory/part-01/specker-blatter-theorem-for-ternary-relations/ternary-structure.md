# The structure on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ given by a ternary relation

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a relation $A \subseteq [n]^3$, where $[n] = \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the structure $([n], A)$ for the ternary language has universe $[n]$ and interprets the ternary symbol $R$ by $A$: for $a, b, c \in [n]$,

$$([n], A) \models R(a, b, c) \iff (a, b, c) \in A.$$
