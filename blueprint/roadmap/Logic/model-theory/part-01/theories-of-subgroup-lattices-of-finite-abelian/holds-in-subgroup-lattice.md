# Truth of a lattice sentence in the subgroup lattice of an abelian group

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $s = (q, \varphi)$ a coded prenex sentence of the lattice language. The sentence $s$ holds in the subgroup lattice of $A$ if the formula with prefix $q$ and matrix $\varphi$, whose first quantifier binds $x_0$, is true in the lattice of subgroups of $A$ (ordered by inclusion) under the assignment sending every variable to the trivial subgroup $\{0\}$. Thus variables not bound by the prefix are read as $\{0\}$.
