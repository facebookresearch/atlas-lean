# Kourovka Problem 2.81(b): recognizing free abelian groups by subsemigroup lattices

Statement (Kourovka Notebook Problem 2.81(b), L. N. Shevrin): some axiomatizable class of lattices recognizes free abelian groups by their subsemigroup lattices. Precisely, there is a first-order theory $T$ in the language of orders such that for every nonempty semigroup $S$, the lattice of all subsemigroups of $S$ satisfies $T$ if and only if $S$ is a free abelian group (isomorphic as a semigroup to a free abelian group of some rank, $0$ included).

The lattice of subsemigroups of $S$ consists of all subsemigroups of $S$, the empty one included, ordered by inclusion. An axiomatizable class of lattices is the class of models of a first-order theory $T$ in the language of orders $\{\le\}$ (the lattice operations are definable from $\le$), so the lattice of subsemigroups is isomorphic to a lattice in the class exactly when it satisfies $T$.
