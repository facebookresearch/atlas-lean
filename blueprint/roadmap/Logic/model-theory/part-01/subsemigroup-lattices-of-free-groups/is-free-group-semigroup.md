# Semigroups that are free groups

A semigroup $S$ is a free group if there is a set $X$ such that $S$ is isomorphic, as a semigroup (by a multiplication-preserving bijection), to the free group $F(X)$ on $X$. The rank $\lvert X \rvert$ may be any cardinal, $0$ included, in which case $S$ is trivial.
