# Semigroups that are free abelian groups

A semigroup $S$ is a free abelian group if there is a set $X$ such that $S$ is isomorphic, as a semigroup (by a multiplication-preserving bijection), to the free abelian group on $X$, written multiplicatively. The rank $\lvert X \rvert$ may be any cardinal, $0$ included.
