# Definable subgroups of free groups

This result proves the proposition stated as [Definable subgroups of free groups are cyclic or the whole group](definable-subgroups-cyclic-or-top.md). Precisely, for every $n \ge 2$, every subgroup of the free group $F_n$ that is definable by a first-order formula in the language of groups with parameters from $F_n$ is cyclic (possibly trivial) or all of $F_n$.
