# Definable subgroups of free groups are cyclic or the whole group

Conjecture (Z. Sela, in Bestvina's list). Let $n \ge 2$ and let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$. Every subgroup $H \le F_n$ that is first-order definable in the language of groups ($1$, ${}^{-1}$, $\cdot$), with parameters from $F_n$ allowed, is either cyclic (possibly trivial) or equal to $F_n$. It holds; the resolution is [Definable subgroups of free groups](definable-subgroups-cyclic-or-top-holds.md).

## Sources
