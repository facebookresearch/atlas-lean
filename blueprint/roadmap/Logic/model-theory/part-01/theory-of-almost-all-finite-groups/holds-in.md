# Truth of a coded sentence in a group

A coded prenex sentence $\varphi = (q, m)$ holds in a group $G$ if $H(0, q, x^{1})$ holds, where $H$ evaluates the quantifier prefix $q$ binding $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in order and then the matrix $m$, and $x^{1}$ is the assignment sending every variable to $1$. Thus variables not bound by the prefix are read as the identity element.
