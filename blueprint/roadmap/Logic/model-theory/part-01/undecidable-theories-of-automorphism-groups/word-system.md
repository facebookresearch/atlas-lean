# Finite systems of group equations and inequations

A word system is a pair $(U, V)$ of finite lists of group words in the variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$, each word a list of letters $(i, b)$ standing for $x_i$ when $b = \mathrm{true}$ and $x_i^{-1}$ when $b = \mathrm{false}$. It represents the system

$$u = 1 \ (u \in U), \qquad v \ne 1 \ (v \in V)$$

of equations and inequations.
