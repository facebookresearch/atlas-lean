# Atomic formulas for abelian groups with pure subgroups

The atomic formulas of the two-sorted language of an abelian group (element variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$) and its pure subgroups (subgroup variables $X_0, X_1, \dots$) are coded in three forms, where $t$ is a linear term $c_1 x_{i_1} + \cdots + c_k x_{i_k}$ and $i, j \in \mathbb{N}$:

- $t = 0$;
- $t \in X_j$;
- $X_i \subseteq X_j$.
