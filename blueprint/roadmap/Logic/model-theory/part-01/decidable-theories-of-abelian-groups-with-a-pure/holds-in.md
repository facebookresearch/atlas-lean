# Truth of a sentence in an abelian group with its pure subgroups

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $s = (q, \varphi)$ a coded two-sorted prenex sentence. The sentence $s$ holds in $(A, \text{pure subgroups of } A)$ if the formula with prefix $q$ and matrix $\varphi$, whose first quantifier binds the variable of index $0$, is true when element quantifiers range over $A$ and subgroup quantifiers range over the pure subgroups of $A$, under the assignment sending every element variable to $0$ and every subgroup variable to the zero subgroup $\{0\}$. Thus unbound variables are read as $0$ and as $\{0\}$.

A subgroup $B$ of an abelian group $A$ is pure if $nA \cap B = nB$ for every integer $n$, that is, whenever $na \in B$ with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $a \in A$, there is $b \in B$ with $nb = na$.
