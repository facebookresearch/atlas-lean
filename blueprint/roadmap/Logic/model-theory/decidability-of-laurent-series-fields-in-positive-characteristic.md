# Decidability of Laurent series fields in positive characteristic

Is the full first-order ring-language theory of $\mathbb{F}_q((t))$ decidable? This is a major gap in valued-field model theory.

## What would settle it

Construct a recursive complete axiomatization with a soundness/completeness proof, or an explicit total truth decider; alternatively prove undecidability. Fix q and the language.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Gitin calls the decidability of Laurent series fields a major open question in the model theory of valued fields, records the decidability of their existential theory, and proves undecidability for some expanded languages; neither answers the full ring-language question.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
