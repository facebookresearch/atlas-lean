# Solvability of a word system in an automorphism group

Let $L$ be a first-order language, $A$ an $L$-structure, and $(U, V)$ a finite system of group equations $u = 1$ ($u \in U$) and inequations $v \ne 1$ ($v \in V$) in the variables $x_0, x_1, \dots$. The system is solvable in $\mathrm{Aut}(A)$ if there is an assignment $x : \mathbb{N} \to \mathrm{Aut}(A)$ of $L$-automorphisms to the variables with

$$u(x) = \mathrm{id}_A \text{ for all } u \in U \quad\text{and}\quad v(x) \ne \mathrm{id}_A \text{ for all } v \in V,$$

where words are evaluated in the group of permutations of $A$ under composition, which contains $\mathrm{Aut}(A)$ as a subgroup.
