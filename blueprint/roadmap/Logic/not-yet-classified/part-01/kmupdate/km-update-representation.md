# Katsuno-Mendelzon update representation theorem

Over a finite set of propositional variables, an update operator on sets of interpretations satisfies the postulates (U1)-(U8) if and only if there is a faithful assignment of a partial preorder $\le_I$ to each interpretation $I$ such that for all $A$ and $B$, the update of $A$ by $B$ is the union, over the models $I$ of $A$, of the $\le_I$-minimal models of $B$.

## Sources

- H. Katsuno and A. O. Mendelzon, \"On the difference between updating a knowledge base and revising it\", *Proceedings of KR 1991*, Theorem 3.1.
