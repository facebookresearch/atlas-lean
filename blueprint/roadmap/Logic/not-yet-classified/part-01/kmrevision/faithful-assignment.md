# Faithful assignment

For a finite set of atoms, a faithful assignment gives each finite set $A$ of interpretations a total preorder $\le_A$ on interpretations (reflexive, transitive and total) such that no model of $A$ is strictly below another model of $A$, and every model of $A$ is strictly below every non-model. Here $I <_A J$ means $I \le_A J$ and not $J \le_A I$ (H. Katsuno and A. O. Mendelzon, "Propositional knowledge base revision and minimal change", Artificial Intelligence 52 (1991), Section 2.2).
