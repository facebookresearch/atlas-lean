# Büchi automaton for an LTL formula

The nondeterministic Büchi automaton of an LTL formula $\varphi$, obtained from its generalized
Büchi tableau by degeneralization: its states are pairs of a tableau state and a counter in
$\{0, \dots, k\}$, where $k$ is the number of until occurrences of $\varphi$, each of which gives
one fairness requirement.
