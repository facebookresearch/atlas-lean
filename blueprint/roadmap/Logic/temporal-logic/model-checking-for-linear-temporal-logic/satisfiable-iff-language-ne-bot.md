# LTL satisfiability is Büchi nonemptiness

An LTL formula is satisfiable iff its Büchi automaton accepts some infinite word. This is the
reduction in Vardi's proof of Theorem 24 (Sistla and Clarke: LTL satisfiability is
PSPACE-complete); the statement here is the reduction, not the complexity bound.
