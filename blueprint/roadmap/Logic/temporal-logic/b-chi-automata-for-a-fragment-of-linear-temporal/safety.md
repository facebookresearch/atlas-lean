# The restricted safety fragment of LTL

The safety formulas over a type of atoms are
generated by the following rules: every propositional formula $\varphi$ is a safety formula; if
$\psi$ and $\rho$ are safety formulas, then so are $\psi \wedge \rho$, $\psi \vee \rho$ and
$X\,\psi$ (next $\psi$); and for every propositional formula $\varphi$, $G\,\varphi$ (always
$\varphi$) is a safety formula.

The operator $G$ is applied only to propositional formulas: for atoms $p$ and $q$, the formula
$G(p \vee X\,q)$ is not in this fragment, since $G$ does not distribute over $\vee$.
