# Expressive power and decision problems of the linear-time μ-calculus

This node covers the expressive power and the decision problems of the linear-time μ-calculus
(νTL), the linear-time counterpart of the modal μ-calculus, which extends linear temporal logic with
least and greatest fixpoint operators. The cited source records, with references to earlier work,
that νTL captures the full class of ω-regular properties and that its satisfiability and
model-checking problems are PSPACE-complete.

complexity; and model checking against finite transition systems, each with its own source.
