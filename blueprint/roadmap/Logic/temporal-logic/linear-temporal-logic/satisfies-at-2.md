# Satisfaction at a position of a run

Let $v$ be a valuation of atoms on states, $\rho$ a run (a sequence of states), $k$ a natural number
and $\varphi$ an LTL formula. Then $\varphi$ holds at position $k$ of $\rho$ when the suffix
$i \mapsto \rho(k + i)$ of $\rho$ satisfies $\varphi$ under $v$. This is
[satisfaction at a position of an ω-word](satisfies-at.md), stated for runs.
