# Büchi automata for the general safety fragment

For every formula $\psi$ of the general safety fragment of LTL (propositional formulas closed under conjunction, disjunction, next, and always applied to a safety formula), there is a nondeterministic Büchi automaton with a finite set of states, over the alphabet of atom valuations (sets of atoms), whose $\omega$-language is the set of words satisfying $\psi$, read as an LTL formula. This is exactly the [automaton construction for full LTL](../model-checking-for-linear-temporal-logic/exists-buchi.md) applied to that formula, with an atom true at a letter exactly when it belongs to it; it adds no conclusion about accepting states. It is weaker than the safety-automaton form of O. Kupferman and M. Y. Vardi, \"Model checking of safety properties\", Formal Methods in System Design 19 (2001), 291-314, in which every state of the automaton is accepting.
