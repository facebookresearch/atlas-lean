# The union of pseudodisks has linear complexity

Let $\Gamma$ be a set of $n \ge 3$ pseudodisks in the plane in general position (no three boundaries pass through a common point). The boundary of their union consists of at most $6n - 12$ arcs, each a connected piece of the boundary of one pseudodisk (Kedem, Livne, Pach and Sharir).
