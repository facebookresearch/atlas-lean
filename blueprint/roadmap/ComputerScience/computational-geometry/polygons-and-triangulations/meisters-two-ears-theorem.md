# Meisters' two ears theorem

Three consecutive vertices $v_1, v_2, v_3$ of a simple polygon $P$ form an *ear* at $v_2$ if the open segment $v_1 v_3$ lies in the interior of $P$, so that $v_1 v_3$ is a diagonal cutting off the triangle $v_1 v_2 v_3$; two ears are *non-overlapping* if their triangles have disjoint interiors. Every simple polygon with $n \ge 4$ vertices has at least two non-overlapping ears.
