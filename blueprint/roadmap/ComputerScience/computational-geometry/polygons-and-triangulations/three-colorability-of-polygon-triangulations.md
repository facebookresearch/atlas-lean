# Three-colorability of polygon triangulations

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a triangulation of a simple polygon and let $G$ be the graph whose vertices are the polygon's vertices and whose edges are the polygon's edges and the diagonals of $\mathcal{T}$. Then $G$ has a proper vertex coloring with three colors, and in every proper $3$-coloring the three vertices of each triangle of $\mathcal{T}$ receive three different colors.
