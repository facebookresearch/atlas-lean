# Crossing criterion for two diagonals of a simple polygon

Let $P = p_0 p_1 \cdots p_{n-1}$ be a simple polygon, and let $i, j, k, l$ be four distinct indices with $i < j$ and $k < l$ such that $p_i p_j$ and $p_k p_l$ are interior diagonals of $P$. The two diagonals cross if and only if $i < k < j < l$ or $k < i < l < j$, that is, if and only if their endpoints alternate along the boundary of $P$.
