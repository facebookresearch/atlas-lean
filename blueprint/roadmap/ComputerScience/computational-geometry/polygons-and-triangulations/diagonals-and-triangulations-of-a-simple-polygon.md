# Diagonals and triangulations of a simple polygon

Let $P$ be a simple polygon with $n \ge 3$ vertices, regarded as the closed region bounded by its boundary curve. A *diagonal* of $P$ is a closed segment joining two vertices of $P$ whose relative interior lies in the interior of $P$. A *triangulation* of $P$ is a partition of $P$ into triangles by a maximal set of diagonals that pairwise meet only in common endpoints; its triangles have their vertices among the vertices of $P$.
