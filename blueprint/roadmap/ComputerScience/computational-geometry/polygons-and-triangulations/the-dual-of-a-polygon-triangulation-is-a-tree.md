# The dual of a polygon triangulation is a tree

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a triangulation of a simple polygon. Its *dual graph* has a node for each triangle and an arc joining two nodes whose triangles share a diagonal. The dual graph is a tree in which every node has degree at most $3$.
