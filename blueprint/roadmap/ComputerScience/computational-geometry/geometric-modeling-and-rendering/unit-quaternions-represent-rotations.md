# Unit quaternions represent rotations

Let $\rho$ be the group homomorphism from the unit quaternions to $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ sending $q$ to the rotation $v \mapsto q v q^{-1}$ of the pure quaternions $\cong \mathbb{R}^3$. Then $\rho$ is surjective: every rotation of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is $\rho(q)$ for some unit quaternion $q$, and since $\rho$ has kernel $\{\pm 1\}$, exactly two unit quaternions $\pm q$ represent it.
