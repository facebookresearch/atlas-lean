# Empty circle property of Voronoi vertices and edges

Let $P$ be a set of $n \ge 2$ sites in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and, for $q \in \mathbb{R}^2$, let $C_P(q)$ be the largest circle centred at $q$ whose interior contains no site. Then $q$ is a vertex of $\mathrm{Vor}(P)$ if and only if $C_P(q)$ passes through at least three sites, and $q$ lies in the relative interior of an edge of $\mathrm{Vor}(P)$ between the cells of $p_i$ and $p_j$ if and only if $C_P(q)$ passes through $p_i$ and $p_j$ and through no other site. In particular every Voronoi vertex is the centre of a circle through three sites with no site in its interior.
