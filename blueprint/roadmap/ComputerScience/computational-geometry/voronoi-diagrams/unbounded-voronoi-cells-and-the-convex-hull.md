# Unbounded Voronoi cells and the convex hull

Let $P$ be a set of $n \ge 2$ sites in $\mathbb{R}^2$. The Voronoi cell $V(p)$ of a site $p \in P$ is unbounded if and only if $p$ lies on the boundary of $\operatorname{conv}(P)$.
