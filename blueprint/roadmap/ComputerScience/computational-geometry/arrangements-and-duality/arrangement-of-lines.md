# Arrangement of lines

Let $L$ be a finite set of lines in the plane. The *arrangement* $\mathcal{A}(L)$ is the subdivision of the plane they induce: its *vertices* are the intersection points of lines of $L$, its *edges* are the maximal connected pieces of the lines of $L$ (segments, rays or whole lines) containing no vertex, and its *faces* are the connected components of the complement of the union of the lines, possibly unbounded. The arrangement is *simple* if no three lines pass through a common point and no two are parallel.
