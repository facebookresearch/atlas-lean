# Lines that maximize halfplane discrepancy

Let $P$ be a finite set of points in $U = [0,1]^2$ and let $h$ be a halfplane of maximum discrepancy with respect to $P$, with bounding line $\ell$. Then either $\ell$ passes through exactly one point of $P$ and that point is the midpoint of the segment $\ell \cap U$, or $\ell$ passes through at least two points of $P$. Otherwise $\ell$ could be translated or rotated about its point of $P$ so that $\mu(h)$ moves away from $\mu_P(h)$ while $\mu_P(h)$ stays fixed.
