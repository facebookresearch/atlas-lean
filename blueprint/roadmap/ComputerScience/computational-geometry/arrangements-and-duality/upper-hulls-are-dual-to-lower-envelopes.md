# Upper hulls are dual to lower envelopes

Let $P$ be a finite set of points in the plane with distinct $x$-coordinates and let $P^* = \{p^* : p \in P\}$. The points of $P$ that are vertices of the upper hull of $P$, in counterclockwise order (from right to left), are exactly the points whose dual lines appear on the lower envelope of $P^*$, in left-to-right order of the envelope; symmetrically for the lower hull and the upper envelope.
