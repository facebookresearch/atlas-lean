# Complexity of a simple line arrangement

Let $\mathcal{A}(L)$ be a simple arrangement of $n$ lines in the plane. Then $\mathcal{A}(L)$ has exactly $\binom{n}{2}$ vertices, $n^2$ edges and $\binom{n}{2} + n + 1$ faces.
