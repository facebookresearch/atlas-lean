# Point–line duality

*Point–line duality* maps each point $p = (p_x, p_y)$ of the plane to the non-vertical line $p^* : y = p_x x - p_y$, and each non-vertical line $\ell : y = \ell_a x - \ell_b$ to the point $\ell^* = (\ell_a, \ell_b)$. The plane of the points $p$ and lines $\ell$ is the *primal* plane and that of their images the *dual* plane; the same formulas map back, so $p^{**} = p$ and $\ell^{**} = \ell$.
