# Fréchet distance between two segments

For two segments $\pi, \sigma : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}^d$ parameterized at constant speed,

$$d_F(\pi, \sigma) = \max\big(\|\pi(0) - \sigma(0)\|, \|\pi(1) - \sigma(1)\|\big),$$

and the uniform parameterization matching $\pi(t)$ with $\sigma(t)$ realizes it.
