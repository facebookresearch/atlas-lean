# Structure of the minimum enclosing disk

Let $S$ be a finite set of at least two points in $\mathbb{R}^2$, no four on a common circle, and let $B$ be a closed disk of minimum radius containing $S$. Then either exactly two points of $S$ lie on the boundary circle of $B$ and they are diametrically opposite, or at least three points of $S$ lie on it and they divide the circle into arcs of angle at most $\pi$.
