# Lower envelopes of partially defined functions

Let $F$ be a collection of $n$ continuous real functions, each defined on a closed real interval, any two of which intersect in at most $s$ points. Then the sequence of functions attaining the lower envelope of $F$, from left to right over the union of their intervals, is an $(n, s + 2)$-Davenport–Schinzel sequence. In particular the lower envelope of $n$ line segments in the plane, none vertical and any two meeting in at most one point, consists of at most $\lambda_3(n)$ pieces.
