# Number of vertices of the trapezoidal map of crossing segments

The trapezoidal map of $r$ segments in general position in the open square $(0,1)^2$ with $k$ pairwise crossings has at most $6r + 3k + 4$ vertices, counting the four corners of the bounding square ($6r + 3k$ without them), with equality when no two segments share an endpoint (a shared endpoint gives $3$ vertices instead of $6$), and the number of trapezoids with tail is proportional to the number of vertices.
