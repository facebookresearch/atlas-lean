# Number of vertices of the trapezoidal map of crossing segments

The trapezoidal map of $r$ segments in general position in the open square $(0,1)^2$, no two of which share an endpoint, with $k$ pairwise crossings has exactly $6r + 3k$ vertices besides the four corners of the bounding square, and the number of trapezoids with tail is proportional to the number of vertices. (Each endpoint and each crossing gives the point itself and the two points where its vertical extensions end.)
