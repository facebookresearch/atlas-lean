# Low equivalence transfers across confined runs

For a policy $\Gamma$ and stores $s, t, s', t'$: if $s$ and $t$ are low-equivalent, $s$ and $s'$ are low-equivalent, and $t$ and $t'$ are low-equivalent, then $s'$ and $t'$ are low-equivalent. Applied to two runs that each leave the low variables unchanged, the final stores agree on low variables even if the runs took different control paths.
