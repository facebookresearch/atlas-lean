# Local actions form a complete lattice

The [local actions](local-actions.md) on a separation algebra $\Sigma$ form a complete lattice in which meets and joins are computed pointwise in the function space $\Sigma \to P(\Sigma)^\top$. For the meet, locality follows from distribution of $\ast\, \{\sigma_1\}$ over intersections, which holds since singletons are precise; for the join it follows from distribution of $\ast$ over unions.
