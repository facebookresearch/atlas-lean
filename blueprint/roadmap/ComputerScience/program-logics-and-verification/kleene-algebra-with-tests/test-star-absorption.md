# Tests preserved by one step are preserved by iteration

Let $K$ be a Kleene algebra and $p, a \in K$ with $p \le 1$ and $p\,p = p$ (an idempotent subidentity, such as a test). If $p\,a = p\,a\,p$, then $p\,a^* = p\,a^*\,p$: a test preserved by one step is preserved by iteration. No structure beyond the Kleene algebra and these two assumptions on $p$ is needed.
