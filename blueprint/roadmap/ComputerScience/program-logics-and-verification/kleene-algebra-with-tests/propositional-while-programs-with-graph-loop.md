# Propositional while programs with graph loop

Propositional while programs are the guarded fragment of Kleene algebra with tests built from actions, Boolean tests, sequencing, if-then-else and while; the graph loop operator adds $t^{\circlearrowleft} = t \cap 1$, the pairs of states that $t$ maps back to themselves.

**Relational models.** A valuation over a non-empty set $B$ assigns to each variable a binary relation on $B$. Terms are interpreted as relations: $1$ (written $\epsilon$) is the identity $\Delta_B$, $0$ is $\emptyset$, composition $t\,u$ is relational composition, $t \cup u$ union, $t^*$ the reflexive transitive closure, and $t \cap u$ intersection. Variables are split into disjoint sets $\mathcal A$ of *actions* and $\mathcal P$ of *primitive tests*, the latter with a fixpoint-free involution $p \mapsto \tilde p$; only valuations with $p \cap \tilde p = 0$ and $p \cup \tilde p = 1$ for all $p \in \mathcal P$ are considered, so $p$ and $\tilde p$ are complementary subsets of the identity. A valuation is *deterministic* if every variable is interpreted as a partial function; $\mathrm{DREL}$ denotes the class of these valuations and $\mathrm{DREL}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ those over finite sets $B$.

**Syntax.** *Tests* are generated by $p \in \mathcal P$, $1$, $0$, $b\,c$ and $b \cup c$; the complement $\bar b$ of a test is defined by $\bar p = \tilde p$, $\bar 1 = 0$, $\bar 0 = 1$ and De Morgan's laws. For terms $t, u$ and a test $b$ put

$$
t +_b u = b\,t \cup \bar b\,u, \qquad t^{(b)} = (b\,t)^* \bar b, \qquad t^{\circlearrowleft} = t \cap 1 .
$$

*Propositional while programs with graph loop* ($\mathrm{PWP}_{\circlearrowleft}$ terms) are generated from tests and actions by composition $t\,u$, conditional $t +_b u$ ("if $b$ then $t$ else $u$"), while $t^{(b)}$ ("while $b$ do $t$") and the graph loop $t^{\circlearrowleft}$. Without $t^{\circlearrowleft}$ they are the propositional while programs, which correspond to guarded Kleene algebra with tests.

**Emptiness.** The *emptiness problem* for deterministic $\mathrm{PWP}_{\circlearrowleft}$ terms asks, given a term $t$, whether $t$ denotes the empty relation in every deterministic valuation (that is, whether $\mathrm{DREL} \models t \le 0$); equivalently, whether $t$ is unsatisfiable.

## Expansion

A deterministic $\mathrm{PWP}_{\circlearrowleft}$ term is a deterministic program over an uninterpreted state space; emptiness says that it never terminates from any state of any deterministic structure. The graph loop $t^{\circlearrowleft}$ is the strong loop predicate of propositional dynamic logic: it is the test that holds at $x$ when $t$ can run from $x$ back to $x$. These terms are a fragment of the positive calculus of relations with transitive closure, interpreted under the test hypotheses above.
