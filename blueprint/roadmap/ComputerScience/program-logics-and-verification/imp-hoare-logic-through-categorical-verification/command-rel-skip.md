# Skip is the identity relation

In the relation category of IMP states, the [execution relation](command-rel.md) of $\mathtt{skip}$ is the identity morphism. Pointwise: $\mathtt{skip}$ executes from a state $s$ to a state $t$ if and only if $t = s$. The two readings convert into each other by evaluating the morphism equality at a pair of states and, conversely, by extensionality of relations.
