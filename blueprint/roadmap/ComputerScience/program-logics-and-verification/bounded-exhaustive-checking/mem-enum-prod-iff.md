# Membership in the budget-split product enumeration

Let $\mathit{enum}_A$ and $\mathit{enum}_B$ have exact support at every bound with respect to $\mathit{size}_A$ and $\mathit{size}_B$: $a \in \mathit{enum}_A(n) \iff \mathit{size}_A(a) \le n$ and $b \in \mathit{enum}_B(n) \iff \mathit{size}_B(b) \le n$ for all $n$, $a$, $b$. Then for every bound $n$, the pair $(a, b)$ belongs to the budget-split product enumeration at $n$ if and only if

$$\mathit{size}_A(a) + \mathit{size}_B(b) \le n.$$
