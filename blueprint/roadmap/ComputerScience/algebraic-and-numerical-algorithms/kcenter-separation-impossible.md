# Metric k-center: separation counting

Let $X$ be a pseudometric space, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $r \in \mathbb{R}$. A finite set of more than $k$ points that are pairwise more than $2r$ apart cannot all lie within distance $r$ of a set of at most $k$ centers. This is the counting step behind the farthest-first $2$-approximation for metric $k$-center.
