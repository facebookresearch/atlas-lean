# Convolution of finitely supported rational weights

The convolution of two finitely supported functions $w_1, w_2 : \mathbb N \to \mathbb Q$: its value
at $n$ is $\sum_{a + b = n} w_1(a)\, w_2(b)$. The inputs are arbitrary finitely supported rational
functions, signed ones included; on the masses of two normalized rational loss laws it gives the
masses of their convolution law.
