# A trace decodes exactly when it joins eleven admissible tokens

For a list of characters `s` and a list `y` of rationals, the [trace decoder](decode-trace.md)
returns `y` on `s` if and only if `s` has at most 300 characters and is obtained by joining with `,`
the [encodings](encode.md) of eleven [admissible](admissible.md) tokens, and `y` is the list of
their [values](value.md).
