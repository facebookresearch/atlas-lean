# Admissible trace token

A [trace token](trace-token.md) is admissible when its numerator and denominator digit lists are
both [runs of one to eleven decimal digits](is-decimal-digits.md), its [numerator](numerator.md) `z`
satisfies `|z| ≤ 10^10`, and its [denominator](denominator.md) `d` satisfies `1 ≤ d ≤ 10^10`.
