# Polynomial Hirsch conjecture for polyhedral diameters (hypothesis)

Conjecture (polynomial Hirsch conjecture; hypothesis). There is a polynomial $p$ such that for all $n \ge d \ge 1$, every $d$-dimensional polyhedron with $n$ facets has combinatorial diameter, the maximum over pairs of vertices of the least number of edges of a connecting edge path, at most $p(n, d)$. The original Hirsch bound $n - d$ is false: Santos constructed a $43$-dimensional polytope with $86$ facets and diameter at least $44$. Any simplex pivot rule with polynomially many pivots on all programs would imply the conjecture, whereas smoothed feasible polyhedra have polynomially bounded diameter by the smoothed analyses of Vershynin and of Bach and Huiberts.
