# Aggregate Law weights

The aggregate loss law of a count law $C$ and a severity law $S$ (finitely supported laws on $\mathbb{N}$ with rational masses) has masses
$$\Pr[\text{aggregate} = m] = \sum_k C(k)\, S^{*k}(m)$$
for every $m$, where $S^{*k}$ is the law of the sum of $k$ independent copies of $S$: the aggregate law is the mixture of the deterministic-count laws $S^{*k}$ with weights $C(k)$. The identity holds as an equality of mass functions and, equivalently, at each $m$.
