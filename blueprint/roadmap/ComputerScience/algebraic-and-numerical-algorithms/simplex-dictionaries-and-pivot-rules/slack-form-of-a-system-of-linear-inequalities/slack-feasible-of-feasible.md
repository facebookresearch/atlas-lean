# A feasible point with its slack is slack-feasible

For a rational $m \times n$ matrix $A$ and $b \in \mathbb{Q}^m$, a point $x \in \mathbb{Q}^n$ is feasible when $A x \le b$ and $x \ge 0$ (componentwise), and a pair $(x, s)$ is slack-feasible when $A x + s = b$, $x \ge 0$ and $s \ge 0$. If $x$ is feasible, then $(x, b - A x)$ is slack-feasible.
