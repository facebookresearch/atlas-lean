# Schönhage's ten-term tensor

**Schönhage's tensor** is the direct sum of an outer product and an inner product: the bilinear map with $7$ left variables $x_1, x_2, x_3, u_1, \dots, u_4$, $7$ right variables $y_1, y_2, y_3, v_1, \dots, v_4$ and $10$ outputs, computing the $9$ products $x_a y_b$ ($1 \le a, b \le 3$) together with the inner product $\sum_{c=1}^{4} u_c v_c$. It is presented by ten **terms**, each a product $L_t \cdot R_t$ of a linear form in the left variables and a linear form in the right variables with integer coefficients, contributing with integer coefficients to the outputs; one term $P_0$ is distinguished as the term carrying the inner-product contribution.
