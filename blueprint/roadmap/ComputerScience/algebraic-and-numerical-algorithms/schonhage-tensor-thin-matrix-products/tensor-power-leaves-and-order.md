# Leaves of the tensor power and their order

Take the $L$-fold tensor power of Schönhage's tensor. A **leaf** is a string $\lambda \in \{\text{terms}\}^L$; its encodings are the products over levels of the left forms and of the right forms. For an output string with $m$ inner levels, a leaf **contributes** if at each level its term contributes to that level's output variable; the **order** of a contributing leaf is the number of inner levels at which it uses a term other than $P_0$. Let $\alpha_t$ and $\beta_t$ be the numbers of requested contributions and of completed leaves of order $t$.
