# Schönhage's ten-term polynomial identity with error term

In the polynomial ring over $\mathbb{Z}$ in the $24$ formal variables (left, right and output variables of Schönhage's tensor), the sum over the ten terms $t$ of $L_t \cdot R_t \cdot O_t$ (with $O_t$ the output form of term $t$) equals the trilinear form $G$ of the wanted outer-plus-inner product plus an explicit error polynomial $E$. The error polynomial cannot be dropped for unrestricted inputs; it vanishes on the inputs that the thin-product construction actually evaluates, which is why that construction restricts which tensor-power leaves contribute.

## Proof sketch

Expand the ten products and compare coefficients; the identity is a finite polynomial identity with integer coefficients.
