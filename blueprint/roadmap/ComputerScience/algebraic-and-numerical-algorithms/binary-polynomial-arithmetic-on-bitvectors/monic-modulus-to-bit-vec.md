# Recovering a monic binary polynomial from its low-coefficient word

Let $s \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $f \in \mathbb{F}_2[X]$ be a monic polynomial of degree $s$. Let $g$ be the width-$s$ binary word formed from the low coefficients $f_0, \dots, f_{s-1}$ of $f$, and let $M_g = X^s + P_g \in \mathbb{F}_2[X]$ be its monic binary modulus. Then

$$M_g = f.$$
