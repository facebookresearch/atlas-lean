# Degree bound on the binary polynomial of a bitvector

For every width $s \in \mathbb{N}$ and every width-$s$ binary word $w$, the degree of its binary polynomial $P_w = \sum_{i=0}^{s-1} w_i X^i \in \mathbb{F}_2[X]$ (taking values in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{-\infty\}$, with $\deg(0) = -\infty$) satisfies

$$\deg(P_w) < s.$$
