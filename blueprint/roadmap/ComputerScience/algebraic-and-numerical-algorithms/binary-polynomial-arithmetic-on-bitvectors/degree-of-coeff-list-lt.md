# Degree bound for a polynomial built from a coefficient list

Let $R$ be a semiring and $(a_0, \dots, a_{n-1})$ a finite list of elements of $R$. Then the polynomial $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a_i X^i \in R[X]$ has degree strictly less than $n$, where the degree takes values in $\{-\infty\} \cup \mathbb{N}$ and the zero polynomial has degree $-\infty$.

No hypothesis on $R$ or on the entries is needed: for the empty list the polynomial is $0$, of degree $-\infty < 0$, and the bound also holds over the zero semiring and when the last entries vanish.
