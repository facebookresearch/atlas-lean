# Rounding modes

The four rounding modes of IEEE 754 binary arithmetic: round to nearest with
ties to even, the default, and the three directed rounding modes the standard requires, toward
$+\infty$, toward $-\infty$ and toward zero.

## Sources

- IEEE Std 754-2019; Goldberg, *What every computer scientist should know about floating-point arithmetic*, ACM Computing Surveys 23(1), 1991, Section 2.3.2.
