# Distinct-position 3SUM

**3SUM (distinct positions).** Given a list $x_1, \dots, x_n$ of integers (repetitions allowed), decide whether there are three pairwise distinct positions $i, j, k$ with $x_i + x_j + x_k = 0$. Three occurrences of $0$ form a solution; a single occurrence does not. Inputs are **polynomially bounded** with exponent $\kappa$ if $|x_i| \le (n+2)^{\kappa}$ for all $i$.
