# Lagrange-reduced pairs

Let $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $u \cdot u > 0$, $|u \cdot v| \le \frac{1}{2}\, u \cdot u$ and $u \cdot u \le v \cdot v$. Then for all integers $(a, b) \ne (0, 0)$,

$$u \cdot u \le \frac{4}{3}\, \|a u + b v\|^2.$$

So in squared length, $u$ is within a factor $4/3$ of the shortest nonzero vector of the lattice spanned by $u$ and $v$.
