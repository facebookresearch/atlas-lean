# Zero extension to a cyclic group agrees with the signal on its support

Let $M$ and $N$ be natural numbers, $x : \{0, \ldots, M-1\} \to \mathbb{C}$ a signal and $x^{(N)}$ its [zero extension to length $N$](zero-extend.md). For every $i \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ with $\operatorname{val} i < M$, $x^{(N)}(i) = x_{\operatorname{val} i}$.
