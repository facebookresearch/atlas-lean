# Injectivity of zero extension to a cyclic group

Let $M$ and $N \ge 1$ be natural numbers. The map $x \mapsto x^{(N)}$, sending a signal $x : \{0, \ldots, M-1\} \to \mathbb{C}$ to its [zero extension to length $N$](zero-extend.md), is injective if and only if $M \le N$. When $N < M$, two signals that differ only at index $N$ have the same extension.
