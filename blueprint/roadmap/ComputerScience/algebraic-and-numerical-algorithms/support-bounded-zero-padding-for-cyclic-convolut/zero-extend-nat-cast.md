# Zero extension to a long enough cyclic group keeps every entry

Let $M \le N$ be natural numbers and $x : \{0, \ldots, M-1\} \to \mathbb{C}$ a signal. Then its [zero extension to length $N$](zero-extend.md) satisfies $x^{(N)}(i \bmod N) = x_i$ for every $i < M$, where $i \bmod N$ is the class of $i$ in $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$.
