# Zero extension of a rectangular array to pairs of natural numbers

For natural numbers $M$ and $K$, an $M \times K$ array is a function $x : \{0, \ldots, M-1\} \times \{0, \ldots, K-1\} \to \mathbb{C}$; its zero extension $\bar x : \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$ is $x(p_1, p_2)$ when $p_1 < M$ and $p_2 < K$, and $0$ otherwise.
