# Hammersley-Clifford for positive weights

Let $V$ and $\alpha$ be finite, $G$ a graph on $V$, and $p$ a strictly positive weight on configurations $x \in \alpha^V$. Then $p$ has a Gibbs factorization over $G$ ($p(x) = \prod_{C} \psi_C(x)$ over a finite family of cliques $C$ of $G$, with each $\psi_C \ge 0$ depending only on $x_C$) if and only if $p$ satisfies the global Markov property (whenever $S$ separates $A$ from $B$ in $G$, $x_A$ and $x_B$ are conditionally independent given $x_S$ under $p$).
