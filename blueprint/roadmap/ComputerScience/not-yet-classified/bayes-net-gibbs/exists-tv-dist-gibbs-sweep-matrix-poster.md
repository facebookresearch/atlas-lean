# Geometric convergence of systematic-scan Gibbs sampling for a Bayesian network

For a Bayesian network over a finite nonempty alphabet whose conditional probability tables are strictly positive, and evidence of nonzero probability, if a systematic-scan Gibbs sweep visits every non-evidence variable, then the powers of the sweep matrix converge geometrically fast in total variation to the posterior $P(z, e)/P(e)$, uniformly in the starting state.
