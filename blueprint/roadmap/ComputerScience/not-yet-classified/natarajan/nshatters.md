# N-shattering

A concept class $C$ (possibly infinite, over an arbitrary label type) N-shatters a finite sample $s$ if there are two labelings $f_0$, $f_1$ of the domain, disagreeing at every point of $s$, such that every subset $t \subseteq s$ is realized by some concept in $C$ that agrees with $f_1$ on $t$ and with $f_0$ on $s \setminus t$.
