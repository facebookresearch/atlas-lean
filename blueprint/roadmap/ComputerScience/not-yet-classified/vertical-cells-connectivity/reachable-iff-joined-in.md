# Cell-graph reachability iff path-connectivity

Let $F$ be an open subset of the plane with a vertical trapezoidal cell decomposition. If $q_I$ lies in cell $c_I$ and $q_G$ in cell $c_G$, then $c_G$ is reachable from $c_I$ in the cell adjacency graph if and only if $q_I$ and $q_G$ are joined by a path in $F$.

## Sources

- S. M. LaValle, *Planning Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press, 2006, Sec. 6.2.2.
