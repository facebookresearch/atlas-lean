# Chandra-Merlin containment criterion

For conjunctive queries $Q$ and $Q'$, $Q$ is contained in $Q'$ (on every database, every answer of $Q$ is an answer of $Q'$) if and only if there is a homomorphism from $Q'$ to $Q$ that maps the head of $Q'$ to the head of $Q$ and every body atom of $Q'$ to a body atom of $Q$.
