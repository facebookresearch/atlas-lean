# No Observed Ancestor Of Latent

In a Bayesian network over a finite nonempty alphabet, no latent node is a descendant of an observed node. This is the hypothesis Tian and Pearl call semi-Markovian (J. Tian and J. Pearl, "A general identification condition for causal effects", AAAI 2002, p. 568): no unobserved variable is a descendant of an observed one. Other sources use "semi-Markovian" for a stronger normal form, in which each unobserved variable is a root with exactly two observed children; the descriptive name here keeps the two conventions apart.
