# Observed Law eq prod q Factor

The factorization of the observed law of a Bayesian network over a finite nonempty alphabet over its c-components (J. Tian and J. Pearl, "A general identification condition for causal effects", AAAI 2002, eqs. (27)–(28)), for every `v` with no positivity hypothesis. No semi-Markovian
hypothesis is needed, so this subsumes the semi-Markovian statement of that source.
