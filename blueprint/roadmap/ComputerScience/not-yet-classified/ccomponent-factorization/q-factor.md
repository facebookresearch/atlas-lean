# Q Factor

`Q[S](v) = P_{v∖s}(s)` (J. Tian and J. Pearl, "A general identification condition for causal effects", AAAI 2002, eq. (28)): the probability of the values `v` on
`S` after `do(V ∖ S = v ∖ s)` on the observed nodes outside `S`; latents are not intervened. The network's alphabet is finite and nonempty.
