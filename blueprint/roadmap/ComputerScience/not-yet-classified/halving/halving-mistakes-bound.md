# Halving mistake bound

Let $C$ be a finite class of Boolean concepts and $S$ a sequence of labeled examples such that some $c \in C$ labels every example of $S$ correctly. Then the number $m$ of mistakes the halving algorithm, started from $C$, makes on $S$ satisfies $2^m \le |C|$; in particular $m \le \lfloor \log_2 |C| \rfloor$.
