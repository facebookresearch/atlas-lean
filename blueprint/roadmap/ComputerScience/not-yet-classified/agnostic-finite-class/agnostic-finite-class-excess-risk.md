# Agnostic excess-risk bound for a finite class

Let $D$ be a probability law on $\alpha \times \{0,1\}$, $h_1, \ldots, h_M$ a nonempty finite class of classifiers with measurable error sets, $m > 0$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$. For an i.i.d. sample of size $m$, the probability that the empirical 0-1 risk minimizer has risk more than $\min_j \mathrm{risk}(h_j) + 2\varepsilon$ is at most $2M e^{-2m\varepsilon^2}$.
