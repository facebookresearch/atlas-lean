# Upper bound on a smoothed class probability at a perturbed input

Let $f$ be a measurable classifier, $\sigma > 0$ and $0 < p < 1$. If the smoothed probability of class $c$ at $x$, under Gaussian noise of scale $\sigma$, is at most $p$, then for every perturbation $\delta$ the smoothed probability of $c$ at $x + \delta$ is at most $\Phi(\Phi^{-1}(p) + \lVert\delta\rVert/\sigma)$, where $\Phi$ is the standard normal distribution function.
