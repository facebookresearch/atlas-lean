# Non-global local minima have positive depth

Let $S$ be a finite state space with energy $V$ and a Markov proposal kernel. If $s$ is a local minimum in Hajek's sense (no state of strictly lower energy is reachable from $s$ along proposal moves that stay at height at most $V(s)$) and $s$ is not a global minimum of $V$, then the depth of $s$, the infimum of $E > 0$ for which some lower state is reachable from $s$ at height at most $V(s) + E$, is positive.
