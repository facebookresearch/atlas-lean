# The critical depth is finite under strong irreducibility

Let a finite state space carry a proposal kernel $R$ and an energy $V$. Suppose the chain is strongly irreducible: for all states $i$ and $j$ there is a height $E$ such that $j$ reaches $i$ along proposal arcs through states of energy at most $E$. Then the critical depth $d^*$, the largest escape depth of a local minimum that is not a global minimum, is finite.
