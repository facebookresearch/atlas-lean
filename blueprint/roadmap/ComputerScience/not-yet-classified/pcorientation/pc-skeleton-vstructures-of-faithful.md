# PC recovers the skeleton and the v-structures

Let $P$ be a probability mass function ($P \ge 0$, $\sum_x P(x) = 1$) on configurations of a Bayesian network whose variables take values in a finite set, and suppose $P$ is faithful to the network's graph. Then the PC algorithm run with the exact conditional-independence oracle of $P$ returns the skeleton of the graph, and its colliders $a \to c \leftarrow d$ are exactly the v-structures of the graph.
