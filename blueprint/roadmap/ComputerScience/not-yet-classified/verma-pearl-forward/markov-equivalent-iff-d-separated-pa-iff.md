# Verma–Pearl characterization of Markov equivalence

For two acyclic directed graphs on the same finite vertex set, given by parent sets, the graphs have the same adjacencies and the same v-structures if and only if they have the same d-separation relations: for all vertex sets $X, Y, Z$, $X$ and $Y$ are d-separated by $Z$ in one graph exactly when they are in the other.

## Sources

- T. Verma and J. Pearl, "Equivalence and synthesis of causal models", *Proceedings of UAI 1990*, Theorem 1.
