# Verma–Pearl characterization of Markov equivalence for Bayesian networks

For two Bayesian networks on $n$ variables and a relabeling $e$ of the variables, the networks have the same adjacencies and the same v-structures along $e$ if and only if they have the same d-separation relations along $e$: $X$ and $Y$ are d-separated by $Z$ in the first network exactly when $e(X)$ and $e(Y)$ are d-separated by $e(Z)$ in the second.

## Sources

- T. Verma and J. Pearl, "Equivalence and synthesis of causal models", *Proceedings of UAI 1990*, Theorem 1.
