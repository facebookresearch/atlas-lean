# Local shortening at a locally convex bend

Let a polyline through consecutive vertices $a, b, c$ be feasible in $F$, let $b$ have a neighborhood $U$ with $U \cap F$ convex, and let $b$ not lie on the segment from $a$ to $c$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there are $a'$ on $[a, b]$ and $c'$ on $[b, c]$ within distance $\varepsilon$ of $b$ such that replacing $b$ by $a', c'$ gives a feasible polyline of strictly smaller length.
