# Matched assignments preserve low equivalence

Let the stores $s$ and $t$ be low-equivalent under policy $\Gamma$, and let $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy $m = n$ whenever $x$ is low ($\Gamma(x) = \mathrm{false}$). Then $s[x \mapsto m]$ and $t[x \mapsto n]$ are low-equivalent; a high variable may receive different values.
