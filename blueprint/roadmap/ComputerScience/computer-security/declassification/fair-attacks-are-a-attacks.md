# Fair attacks are A-attacks

**Proposition.** Every fair attack at a level $\ell$ with $C(\ell) \sqsubseteq C(A)$ and $I(A) \sqsubseteq I(\ell)$, in the sense of [robust declassification](robust-declassification.md), is an $A$-attack in the sense of the
[typing rules for robust declassification](typing-rules-for-robust-declassification.md).

- [Robust declassification](robust-declassification.md)
