# Min-entropy leakage of a deterministic channel under the uniform prior

Let $X$ be a finite nonempty type, $Y$ a finite type and $f : X \to Y$. For the deterministic channel sending $x$ to the point mass at $f(x)$ and the uniform prior on $X$,

$$\mathcal{L}(\mathrm{uniform}_X, C_f) = \log_2 |f(X)|,$$

where $|f(X)|$ is the number of distinct values of $f$.
