# At most one company dataset per conflict class

**Theorem 2.** In the [Chinese Wall model](chinese-wall-model.md), a subject can at most have access to one company dataset in each conflict of interest class.

A subject needs no access initially (Axiom 3); its first request succeeds (Axiom 4), and afterwards
[Theorem 1](chinese-wall-access-stays-in-one-dataset-per-class.md) confines it within that conflict class to the dataset of its first access there.

- [The Chinese Wall model](chinese-wall-model.md)
