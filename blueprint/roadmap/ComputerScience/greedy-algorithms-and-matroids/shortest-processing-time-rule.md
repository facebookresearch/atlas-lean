# The shortest-processing-time rule minimizes total completion time

Consider a finite set of jobs $j$ with processing times $p_j \ge 0$, all released at time $0$, processed without preemption on one machine; $C_j$ is the completion time. Ordering the jobs by nondecreasing $p_j$ minimizes $\sum_jC_j$. The proof exchanges adjacent jobs that violate the order.
