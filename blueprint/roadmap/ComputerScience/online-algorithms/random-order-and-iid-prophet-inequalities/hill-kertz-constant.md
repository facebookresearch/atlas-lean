# The Hill–Kertz constant $\beta \approx 0.745$

Let $\beta \approx 0.7451$ be the unique $\beta \in (0, 1)$ satisfying $\int_0^1 \frac{dy}{y(1 - \ln y) + \frac{1}{\beta} - 1} = 1$. This constant, identified by Kertz, governs the asymptotically worst case of the i.i.d. prophet inequality.
