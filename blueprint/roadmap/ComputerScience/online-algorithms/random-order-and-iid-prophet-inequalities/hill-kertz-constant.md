# The Hill–Kertz constant $\beta \approx 0.745$

Let $\beta \approx 0.7454$ be the unique $\beta \in (0, 1)$ satisfying $\int_0^1 \frac{dy}{y(1 - \ln y) + \frac{1}{\beta} - 1} = 1$. Equivalently $1/\beta \approx 1.3415$ is the unique solution $b$ of the same equation with $\frac{1}{\beta} - 1$ replaced by $b - 1$, the form used by Correa, Foncea, Hoeksma, Oosterwijk and Vredeveld. This constant, identified by Kertz as the limit of the worst-case ratio $\mathbb{E}[\max_i X_i]/\sup_\tau \mathbb{E}[X_\tau]$ for $n$ i.i.d. values, governs the asymptotically worst case of the i.i.d. prophet inequality.
