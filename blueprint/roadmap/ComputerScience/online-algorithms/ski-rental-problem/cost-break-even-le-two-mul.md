# The break-even strategy pays at most twice the optimum

For every purchase price $B \ge 1$ and every season length $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the cost
$\mathrm{cost}_B(s_B, d)$ of the break-even strategy $s_B$ (buy on day $B$) for a season of $d$
days is at most $2 \cdot \mathrm{opt}_B(d)$, twice the offline optimum $\mathrm{opt}_B(d)$. In
particular, the break-even strategy is $2$-competitive.
