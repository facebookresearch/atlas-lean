# Online ski-rental strategies reduce to buy-day strategies

Let $B$ be the buying price and let $A$ be a feasible deterministic strategy for ski rental as an
online problem with one unit request per day. Then there is **one** buy-day strategy $s$ (buy on a
fixed day $t$, or never) such that, for **every** request sequence $\sigma$, the cost of $A$ on
$\sigma$ equals the ski-rental cost of $s$ for a season of $|\sigma|$ days. The same $s$ works for
all request sequences; it is not chosen per sequence.
