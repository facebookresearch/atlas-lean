# Ski rental has no positive additive lower bound

For a buying price $B \ge 1$ and every $c^* > 0$, no additive lower bound of factor $c^*$ holds for
ski rental: the strategy that buys on day $B$ costs at most $2B - 1$ on every season, so it is
$(0, 2B - 1)$-competitive. This contrasts with the strict lower bound of factor $2 - 1/B$, which does hold.
