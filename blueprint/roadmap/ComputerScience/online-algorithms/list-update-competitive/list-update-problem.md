# List Update Problem

List update as an online problem over a fixed item list $M$ (any list): legal requests are items of $M$; an answer is the list after serving the request and rearranging, and a trace is feasible when every answer is a permutation of $M$ and every request is an item of $M$; the cost is the access cost plus paid exchanges, with free forward moves of the accessed item. Restrictions such as distinct items belong to the theorems that need them.
