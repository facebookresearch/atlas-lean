# List Update Problem

List update as an online problem over a fixed item list $M$ (any list): legal requests are items of $M$; an answer is the list after serving the request and rearranging, and a trace is feasible when every answer is a permutation of $M$ and every request is an item of $M$. The cost of a step from list $L$ on request $x$ with answer $L'$ is the [step cost](step-cost.md): the position of $x$ in $L$ plus the least inversion count, comparing first occurrences, between $L'$ and a list obtained from $L$ by a free forward move of $x$. When $M$ has no repeated items this count is the number of paid exchanges. With repeated items it can be smaller: from $[c, a, b, a]$, serving $c$ and answering $[c, a, a, b]$ costs $1$, although that answer needs one paid exchange. Restrictions such as distinct items belong to the theorems that need them.
