# Strict Lower Bound

The strict lower bound $c^\star$ for an online problem: for every feasible strategy $A$ and every $c$ with $0 \le c < c^\star$, there is a legal input $\sigma$ with $c \cdot \mathrm{OPT}(\sigma) < \mathrm{ALG}(\sigma)$. No feasible strategy achieves a factor below $c^\star$ without an additive constant.
