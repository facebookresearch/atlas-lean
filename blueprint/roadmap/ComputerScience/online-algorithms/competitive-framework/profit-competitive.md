# Profit Competitive

Profit-form competitiveness, for maximization with a nonnegative profit function: a strategy $A$ is $(\rho, b)$-profit-competitive when $0 \le \rho \le 1$, $b \ge 0$, $A$ is feasible, and for every legal input $\sigma$, $\rho \cdot \mathrm{OPT}_{\mathrm{profit}}(\sigma) \le \mathrm{ALG}_{\mathrm{profit}}(\sigma) + b$. This is a separate predicate from the cost form; the two are not interchangeable.
