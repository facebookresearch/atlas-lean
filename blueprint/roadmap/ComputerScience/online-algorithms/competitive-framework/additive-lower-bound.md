# Additive Lower Bound

The additive lower bound $c^\star$ for an online problem: for every feasible strategy $A$, every $c$ with $0 \le c < c^\star$ and every $b \ge 0$, there is a legal input $\sigma$ with $c \cdot \mathrm{OPT}(\sigma) + b < \mathrm{ALG}(\sigma)$. It is stronger than the strict lower bound: a feasible strategy with uniformly bounded cost on legal inputs refutes every positive additive lower bound.
