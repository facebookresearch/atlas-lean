# Rand Competitive

Randomized $(c, b)$-competitiveness against an oblivious adversary, for a strategy chosen by a random seed: $c \ge 0$, $b \ge 0$, the strategy is feasible for every seed, and for every legal input $\sigma$ the cost of the run is a measurable function of the seed and its expectation satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ALG}(\sigma)] \le c \cdot \mathrm{OPT}(\sigma) + b$, with the constants outside the expectation.
