# The greedy matching is maximal

Consider a bipartite graph with left vertices $0, \dots, a - 1$ and right vertices
$0, \dots, b - 1$, and the greedy online matching, in which the right vertices arrive in the order
$0, 1, \dots, b - 1$. For every edge between a left vertex $i$ and a right vertex $j$, either $j$
has a partner in the greedy matching or $i$ is the partner of some right vertex.
