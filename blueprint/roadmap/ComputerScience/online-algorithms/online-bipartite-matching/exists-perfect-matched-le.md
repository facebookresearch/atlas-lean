# No deterministic online bipartite matching algorithm beats $1/2$

For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every deterministic online bipartite matching algorithm $A$ with
$2n$ left vertices, there are a sequence $\sigma$ of $2n$ arrivals (neighbour lists) and a perfect
matching $m$ of $\sigma$ with $2n$ entries, while $A$, run on $\sigma$ from the empty history with
no left vertex matched, matches at most $n$ arrivals (Karp, Vazirani and Vazirani).
