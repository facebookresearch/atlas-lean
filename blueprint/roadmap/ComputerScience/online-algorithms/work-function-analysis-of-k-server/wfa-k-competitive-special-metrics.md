# Metrics on which the work function algorithm is $k$-competitive

The work function algorithm is $k$-competitive (so the $k$-server conjecture holds) for $k = 2$ on every metric space, for every $k$ on metric spaces with exactly $k+2$ points, on the real line, and on weighted star metrics.

## Proof sketch

For $k = 2$ Chrobak and Larmore give a potential tailored to two servers. Bartal and Koutsoupias refine the duality-lemma potential: on $k+2$ points every configuration misses exactly two points, and on the line and on weighted stars the maximizing configurations of the duality lemma have a structure (they can be taken to be monotone, respectively to use the center) under which the potential drops by $k$ rather than $2k$ times the increase of the optimum.
