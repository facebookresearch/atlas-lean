# k-fold matroid unions

For a matroid $\mathcal{M} = (E, \mathcal{I})$ and $k \ge 1$, the *$k$-fold union* $\mathcal{M}^{(k)}$ is the matroid on $E$ whose independent sets are the unions $I_1 \cup \dots \cup I_k$ with $I_j \in \mathcal{I}$. By Edmonds' matroid partition theorem its rank function is $r_k(A) = \min_{B \subseteq A}\big(|A \setminus B| + k\, r(B)\big)$. The $k$-fold union of the rank-one uniform matroid is the $k$-uniform matroid.
