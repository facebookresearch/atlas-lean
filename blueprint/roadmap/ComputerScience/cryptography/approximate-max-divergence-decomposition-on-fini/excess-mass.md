# Excess mass of one distribution over another

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. The excess mass is the real number

$$
\sum_{w \in E} \big(P(w) - e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)\big),
$$

where $E$ is the [excess set](excess-set.md) $\{ w \mid e^{\varepsilon} Q(w) < P(w) \}$.
