# Approximate max-divergence bound

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon, \delta \in \mathbb{R}$. The approximate max-divergence bound $D_\infty^\delta(P \,\|\, Q) \le \varepsilon$ holds when for every $S \subseteq \Omega$

$$
\sum_{w \in S} P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} \sum_{w \in S} Q(w) + \delta,
$$

as real numbers.
