# Repaired masses

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. With $c(w) = \min(P(w), e^{\varepsilon} Q(w))$ the [capped envelope](cap-envelope.md) and $t$ the [mixing coefficient](repair-coeff.md), the repaired mass at $w$ is the real number

$$
c(w) + t\,\big(e^{\varepsilon} Q(w) - c(w)\big).
$$
