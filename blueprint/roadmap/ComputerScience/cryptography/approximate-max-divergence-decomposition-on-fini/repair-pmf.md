# Repaired distribution

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \ge 0$. The repaired distribution $\tilde P$ is $P$ itself when the [excess mass](excess-mass.md) of $P$ over $e^{\varepsilon} Q$ is $0$; otherwise it is the probability mass function whose mass at $w$ is the [repaired mass](repair-fun.md) $c(w) + t\,(e^{\varepsilon} Q(w) - c(w))$, which is nonnegative and sums to $1$.
