# Mixing coefficient of the repaired distribution

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. With $m$ the [excess mass](excess-mass.md) of $P$ over $e^{\varepsilon} Q$, the mixing coefficient is

$$
\frac{m}{e^{\varepsilon} - 1 + m},
$$

with division by zero read as $0$.
