# Approximate differential privacy gives approximate max-divergence bounds

Let $M$ be a mechanism with values in probability mass functions on a finite nonempty type. If $M$ is [$(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differentially private](../approximate-differential-privacy/is-approx-dp.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$ and $\mathrm{adj}(d, d')$, then the [approximate max-divergence bound](approx-max-div-le.md) $D_\infty^\delta(M(d) \| M(d')) \le \varepsilon$ holds.
