# Pure max-divergence bound

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. The pure max-divergence bound $D_\infty(P \,\|\, Q) \le \varepsilon$ holds when $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ for every $w \in \Omega$.
