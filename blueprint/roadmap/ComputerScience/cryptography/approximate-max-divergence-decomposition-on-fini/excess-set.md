# Excess set of one distribution over another

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. The excess set is $\{\, w \in \Omega \mid e^{\varepsilon} Q(w) < P(w) \,\}$, the outcomes where $P$ exceeds the $e^{\varepsilon}$-scaled mass of $Q$.
