# A nearby pure bound gives approximate max-divergence

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon, \delta \in \mathbb{R}$. If a probability mass function $\tilde P$ on $\Omega$ satisfies $\mathrm{TV}(P, \tilde P) = \tfrac12 \sum_{w} |P(w) - \tilde P(w)|$ $\le \delta$ and $D_\infty(\tilde P \| Q) \le \varepsilon$ ([pure bound](max-div-le.md)), then the [approximate bound](approx-max-div-le.md) $D_\infty^\delta(P \| Q) \le \varepsilon$ holds.
