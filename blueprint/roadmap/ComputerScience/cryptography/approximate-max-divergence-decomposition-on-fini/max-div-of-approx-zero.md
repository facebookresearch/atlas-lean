# Approximate max-divergence with zero delta is pure

Let $\Omega$ be a finite nonempty type and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$, and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$. If the [approximate max-divergence bound](approx-max-div-le.md) holds with $\delta = 0$, then the [pure bound](max-div-le.md) $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ holds for every $w$.
