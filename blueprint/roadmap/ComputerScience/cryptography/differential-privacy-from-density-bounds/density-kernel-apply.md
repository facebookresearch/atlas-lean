# Evaluating a density kernel over a constant base

Let $\mu$ be an s-finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and $\rho : D \to \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ jointly measurable, with $D$ a measurable space. The kernel with density $\rho$ against the constant kernel $d \mapsto \mu$ evaluates at each $d \in D$ to the measure $\rho(d)\cdot\mu$ with density $\rho(d)$ against $\mu$.
