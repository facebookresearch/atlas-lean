# Partially hiding functional encryption for degree-two private computations

A *secret-key partially hiding functional encryption* (PHFE) scheme for a class $\mathcal{F}$ of functions $f(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$, with public input $\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{Z}_p^{n_{\mathrm{pub}}}$ and private input $\mathbf{y}\in\mathbb{Z}_p^{n_{\mathrm{priv}}}$, consists of algorithms $\mathsf{PPGen}(1^\lambda,1^n)\to\mathsf{crs}$, $\mathsf{Setup}(\mathsf{crs})\to\mathsf{msk}$, $\mathsf{Enc}(\mathsf{msk},(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}))\to(\mathbf{x},\mathsf{ct})$, $\mathsf{KeyGen}(\mathsf{msk},f)\to\mathsf{sk}_f$ and a deterministic $\mathsf{Dec}(\mathsf{sk}_f,(\mathbf{x},\mathsf{ct}))$, with $\mathsf{Dec}(\mathsf{sk}_f,\mathsf{Enc}(\mathsf{msk},(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})))=f(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$ with probability $1$ (or, under *$B$-bounded correctness* for a polynomial bound $B(\lambda)$, decryption runs in time $\mathrm{poly}(\lambda, B)$ and recovers $f(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$ whenever every output coordinate of $f(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$ lies in $\{-B, \dots, B\}$). Encryption needs the master secret key and reveals $\mathbf{x}$ in the clear; ordinary (secret-key) functional encryption is the case of empty public input. *Simulation security* (weakly selective, against collusions) requires a simulator such that, for all polynomially many fixed messages $(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$ and $(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{y}_i)$ and functions $f_j$, the real distribution of $\mathsf{crs}$, a challenge ciphertext of $(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$, ciphertexts of the $(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{y}_i)$ and keys for the $f_j$ is indistinguishable from a simulated one, with simulated setup, in which the challenge ciphertext is produced from $\mathbf{x}$ alone, the other ciphertexts by a simulated encryption of $(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{y}_i)$, and each key for $f_j$ from $f_j$ and the value $f_j(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})$. The class used in the Jain–Lin–Sahai construction of iO is $(\mathsf{arith}\text{-}\mathsf{NC}^{1},\deg 2)$: functions each of whose outputs is a polynomial of degree at most $2$ in the private input whose coefficients are computed from the public input by arithmetic $\mathsf{NC}^{1}$ circuits over $\mathbb{Z}_p$. The efficiency requirement there is *linear efficiency*: the ciphertext has size $\mathrm{poly}(\lambda)\cdot(n_{\mathrm{pub}}+n_{\mathrm{priv}})$, independent of the size and output length of $f$.
