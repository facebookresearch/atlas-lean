# Galois automorphisms rotate plaintext slots

For odd $k$, the map $\sigma_k:X\mapsto X^{k}$ is an automorphism of $R$ and of $R_t$, and $(\mathbb{Z}/2N\mathbb{Z})^{\times}=\langle 5\rangle\times\langle -1\rangle\cong\mathbb{Z}_{N/2}\times\mathbb{Z}_2$. Indexing the slots by $\zeta^{5^{i}}$ and $\zeta^{-5^{i}}$, the automorphism $\sigma_5$ cyclically rotates each of the two rows of $N/2$ slots by one position, and $\sigma_{-1}$ swaps the rows. Applying $\sigma_k$ to a ciphertext $(c_0,c_1)$ gives $(\sigma_k(c_0),\sigma_k(c_1))$ decrypting under $\sigma_k(s)$, so a key-switching key from $\sigma_k(s)$ to $s$ completes a homomorphic rotation.

## Proof sketch

$\sigma_k$ preserves $X^{N}+1$ for odd $k$. The structure of $(\mathbb{Z}/2^{m}\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$ for $m\ge 3$ is cyclic of order $2^{m-2}$ generated by $5$, times $\{\pm 1\}$. Evaluating $\sigma_5(m)$ at $\zeta^{5^{i}}$ gives $m(\zeta^{5^{i+1}})$, a shift of slot indices; decryption commutes with $\sigma_k$ because $\sigma_k(c_0+c_1s)=\sigma_k(c_0)+\sigma_k(c_1)\sigma_k(s)$ and $\sigma_k$ preserves the coefficient norm.
