# Uniformity-preserving maps on the third of four coordinates

Let $f : A \to B$ map the uniform distribution on $A$ to the uniform distribution on $B$, where all the
sets involved are finite and nonempty. Then applying $f$ to the third coordinate maps the uniform
distribution on $P \times Q \times A \times S$ to the uniform distribution on
$P \times Q \times B \times S$.
