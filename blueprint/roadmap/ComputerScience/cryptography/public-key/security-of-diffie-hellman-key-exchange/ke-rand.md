# Random world of the key-exchange experiment

The random world draws $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}/q$, a group element $k \in G$ and coins $s$ uniformly, and
outputs the eavesdropper's bit on the transcript $(g^a, g^b)$, the element $k$ and $s$. The key is
independent of the transcript, as in Katz and Lindell's modified experiment (Section 10.3).
