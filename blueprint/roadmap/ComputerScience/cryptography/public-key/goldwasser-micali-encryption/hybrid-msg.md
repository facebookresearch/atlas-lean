# The $j$-th hybrid message

For $\ell$-bit messages $m$ and $m'$ and $0 \le j \le \ell$, the message whose bits before position $j$
are those of $m'$ and whose bits from position $j$ on are those of $m$. The $0$-th hybrid is $m$, the
$\ell$-th is $m'$, and consecutive hybrids differ in at most one position.
