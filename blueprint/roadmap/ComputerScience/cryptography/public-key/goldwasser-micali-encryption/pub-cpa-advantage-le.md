# Residuosity bounds transfer to Goldwasser–Micali, per adversary

If the [reduction](reduction.md) built from an eavesdropper $A$ has advantage at most $\varepsilon$ in
the [residuosity experiment](qr-exp.md), then the eavesdropping advantage of $A$ against the
[one-bit scheme](scheme.md) is at most $\varepsilon / 2$.
