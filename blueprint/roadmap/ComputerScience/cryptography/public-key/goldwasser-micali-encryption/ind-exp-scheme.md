# Distinguishing Goldwasser–Micali ciphertexts is deciding residuosity

For one-bit messages, the
[indistinguishability experiment](../../security-games/semantic-security-and-indistinguishability/ind-exp.md) of the
[Goldwasser–Micali scheme](scheme.md) on the message $b$, for any distinguisher and coins, is the
[residuosity experiment](qr-exp.md) in world $b$.
