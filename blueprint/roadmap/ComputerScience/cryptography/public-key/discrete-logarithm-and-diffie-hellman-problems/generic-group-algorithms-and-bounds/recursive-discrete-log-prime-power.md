# Recursive discrete logarithm in groups of prime-power order

Let $G$ have order $q^{e}$ with generator $g$, and suppose discrete logarithms in groups of order $q$ can be computed with $T$ group operations. Algorithm RDL computes $\alpha = \log_g u$ recursively: with $f = \lfloor e/2 \rfloor$, it computes $\alpha \bmod q^{f}$ in the subgroup generated by $g^{q^{e-f}}$ applied to $u^{q^{e-f}}$, divides out $g^{\alpha \bmod q^f}$, and recurses on the quotient in the subgroup of order $q^{e-f}$. It uses

$$O\bigl(e\,T + e \log e \log q\bigr)$$

group operations (Boneh and Shoup, Section 16.1.2.1).

## Proof sketch

Correctness follows from the decomposition $\alpha = \alpha_0 + q^{f}\alpha_1$. The cost recursion $C(e) = C(f) + C(e - f) + O(e \log q)$ for the exponentiations, together with exactly $e$ base-case calls of cost $T$ each, solves to the stated bound.
