# DDH advantage of the distinguisher built from a CDH solver

If $g$ generates $G$ and $|G| = q$, the DDH advantage of the distinguisher built from a CDH solver $A$
is $|\mathrm{Adv}^{\mathrm{cdh}}(A) - 1/q|$, where $\mathrm{Adv}^{\mathrm{cdh}}(A)$ is the CDH
advantage of $A$ (Katz and Lindell, Section 8.3.2).
