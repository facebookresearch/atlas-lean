# Comparing tests on uniform samples

Let $\Omega$, $\Omega'$ and $B$ be finite nonempty sets, $e : \Omega' \times B \to \Omega$ a bijection,
and $f : \Omega \to \{0, 1\}$ and $g : \Omega' \to \{0, 1\}$ tests such that $g(a) = 1$ implies
$f(e(a, b)) = 1$ for every $b$. Then $g$ succeeds on a uniform sample of $\Omega'$ with probability at
most that of $f$ on a uniform sample of $\Omega$. The reductions between hardness assumptions add an
independent uniform coordinate $b$ that the inner algorithm ignores.
