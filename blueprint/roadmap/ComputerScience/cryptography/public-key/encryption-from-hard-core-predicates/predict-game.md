# The hard-core prediction experiment

Draw a key pair $(\alpha, \tau)$, coins $r$ and a uniform $x \in D$; the predictor receives $\alpha$ and
$p_\alpha(x)$ and wins when its output equals $b_\alpha(x)$. A hard-core predicate is one for which
every efficient predictor wins with probability at most $\tfrac12$ plus a negligible amount.
