# Polynomial-time key recovery attacks on SIDH

In SIDH, each party publishes the codomain of its secret isogeny $\varphi:E_0\to E$ together with the images under $\varphi$ of a basis of the other party's torsion subgroup. Write $N_1$ for the degree of the attacked secret isogeny and $N_2$ for the order of the torsion subgroup whose image it reveals, so $\{N_1,N_2\}=\{2^{a},3^{b}\}$.

(i) Castryck and Decru recover Bob's secret $3^{b}$-isogeny from his public key and the images of $E_0[2^{a}]$, using Kani's reducibility criterion for $(2^{a'},2^{a'})$-isogenies from products of elliptic curves as a decision tool inside a digit-by-digit search-to-decision reduction. If $\mathrm{End}(E_0)$ is known, the attack runs in heuristic polynomial time, apart from factoring polynomially many integers that depend only on the system parameters. With only the endomorphism $2i$ of the SIKE starting curves it is practical (it breaks the SIKE parameter sets), but its expected asymptotic cost is subexponential, $L_p(1/4)$. Maino, Martindale, Panny, Pope and Wesolowski give an attack of the same kind that needs no endomorphism information on the starting curve and has subexponential complexity.

(ii) Robert's attack works for an arbitrary starting curve. Suppose $N_2>N_1$ (in SIDH one attacks whichever secret isogeny has the smaller degree), $E_0[N_2]\subseteq E_0(\mathbb{F}_{q})$, and a decomposition $N_2-N_1=a_1^{2}+a_2^{2}+a_3^{2}+a_4^{2}$ is given; such a decomposition depends only on the parameters and is found in randomized polynomial time. Then $\varphi$ can be evaluated at any point of $E_0(\mathbb{F}_q)$ with $\widetilde{O}(\ell^{8}\log N_2)$ arithmetic operations over $\mathbb{F}_q$, where $\ell$ is the largest prime factor of $N_2$, by embedding $\varphi$ into an $N_2$-isogeny of abelian varieties of dimension $8$; its kernel then follows by a discrete logarithm in a group of smooth order $N_1$. For SIDH ($\ell\in\{2,3\}$) this is a proven polynomial-time key recovery. Robert's complexity bound for the analogous attack in dimension $4$ is heuristic and needs tweaks of the parameters.

Consequently SIDH and SIKE are broken.

## Proof sketch

Write $N_2=N_1+c$. Given an auxiliary isogeny $\gamma:E_0\to C$ of degree $c$, the diamond formed by $\varphi$ and $\gamma$ yields, by Kani's lemma, an $(N_2,N_2)$-isogeny from $C\times E$ whose kernel, generated by the pairs $(\gamma(P),\varphi(P))$ for $P$ in a basis of $E_0[N_2]$, is computable from the public torsion images; it lands on a product of elliptic curves when the guessed data are correct and, heuristically, almost never otherwise. Castryck and Decru construct $\gamma$ from the known endomorphism ring (through the KLPT algorithm when non-scalar endomorphisms of very small degree are not available directly). Robert replaces $\gamma$ by the endomorphism $\alpha$ of $E_0^{4}$ given by the $4\times 4$ integer matrix built from $a_1,\dots,a_4$, which satisfies $\tilde{\alpha}\circ\alpha=[c]$, and obtains an explicit $N_2$-isogeny of $E_0^{4}\times E^{4}$ whose evaluation reveals $\varphi(P)$; no endomorphism knowledge is needed.
