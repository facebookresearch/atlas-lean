# The quaternion algebra ramified at p and infinity and its maximal orders

For a prime $p$, $B_{p,\infty}$ denotes the unique (up to isomorphism) quaternion algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$ ramified exactly at $p$ and $\infty$; for $p\equiv 3\pmod 4$ it is $\mathbb{Q}\langle i,j\rangle$ with $i^{2}=-1$, $j^{2}=-p$, $ij=-ji$. Its reduced norm is $\mathrm{nrd}(a+bi+cj+dij)=a^{2}+b^{2}+p(c^{2}+d^{2})$ in that presentation. An *order* is a subring that is a lattice of rank $4$; a *maximal order* is one not properly contained in another order. For a maximal order $\mathcal{O}$, a *left $\mathcal{O}$-ideal* $I$ is a lattice with $\mathcal{O}I\subseteq I$; its norm $\mathrm{nrd}(I)$ is the gcd of the reduced norms of its elements, and its right order $\mathcal{O}_R(I)=\{x:Ix\subseteq I\}$ is again maximal. Two left ideals are equivalent if $I'=I\alpha$ for some $\alpha\in B_{p,\infty}^{\times}$.
