# Supersingular l-isogeny graphs

For primes $\ell\ne p$, the *supersingular $\ell$-isogeny graph* $\mathcal{G}_\ell(p)$ has as vertices the supersingular $j$-invariants in $\mathbb{F}_{p^{2}}$ and, for each vertex $j(E)$, one edge for each of the $\ell+1$ cyclic subgroups of order $\ell$ of $E$, joining $j(E)$ to $j(E/G)$. It is an $(\ell+1)$-regular connected multigraph (undirected up to the vertices $j=0,1728$ with extra automorphisms).
