# Circular security from fully composable homomorphic encryption

Does every fully composable homomorphic encryption scheme yield a circular-secure scheme? This would clarify the role of self-encryption assumptions in bootstrapping.

## What would settle it

State the source's composability and circular-security games. Give an efficient transformation from an arbitrary eligible scheme to a possibly different circular-secure scheme, and prove its security reduction. Proving the original scheme itself circular-secure is a stronger and potentially false claim.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Micciancio poses this as Conjecture 1 and notes that the circular-secure scheme may differ from the original composable scheme; he proves that circular security implies full composability.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
