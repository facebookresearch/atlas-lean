# Feasible word

A word $w$ is feasible for the coalition $S$ of the codebook $C$,
$w \in F(C_S)$, when each of its bits is the bit of some member of $S$ in the same column: for
every column $j$ there is $i \in S$ with $w_j = c_{ij}$. No word of positive length is feasible
for the empty coalition.

## Sources

- M. Bun, J. Ullman and S. Vadhan, *Fingerprinting codes and the price of approximate differential privacy*, SIAM J. Comput. 47(5), 2018, arXiv:1311.3158v3, Section 3.1.
