# Pointwise and event-level pure differential privacy agree

For a mechanism $M$ with values in probability mass functions, a relation $\mathrm{adj}$ and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, $M$ is [$\varepsilon$-differentially private](is-dp.md) (bound on point masses) if and only if it is [event-level $\varepsilon$-differentially private](is-dpevent.md) (bound on the mass of every set of outputs).
