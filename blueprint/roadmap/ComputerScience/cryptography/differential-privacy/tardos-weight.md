# Tardos weight

Tardos's weight for the bias $p$ and the bit $b$: $\sqrt{(1 - p)/p}$ for
$b = 1$ and $-\sqrt{p/(1 - p)}$ for $b = 0$. For $p \notin (0, 1)$ both weights are $0$, by the
conventions $x / 0 = 0$ and $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$; Tardos's biases lie in $(0, 1)$.

## Sources

- G. Tardos, *Optimal probabilistic fingerprint codes*, J. ACM 55(2), 2008, Section 2.1.
