# Composition of approximate differential privacy, two mechanisms

If $M_1$ is $(\varepsilon_1, \delta_1)$-differentially private and $M_2$ is
$(\varepsilon_2, \delta_2)$-differentially private, both with values in probability measures, then
releasing both with independent coins is $(\varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2, \delta_1 + \delta_2)$-differentially
private.

## Sources

- C. Dwork and A. Roth, *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 2014, <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html>, Theorem 3.16 for $k = 2$.
