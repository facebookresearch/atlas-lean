# Monotonicity of the privacy parameters

An $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differentially private mechanism is $(\varepsilon', \delta')$-differentially
private whenever $\varepsilon \le \varepsilon'$ and $\delta \le \delta'$: the bound
$M(x)(S) \le e^{\varepsilon} M(y)(S) + \delta$ only weakens.

## Sources

- C. Dwork and A. Roth, *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 2014, <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html>, Definition 2.4 (immediate).
