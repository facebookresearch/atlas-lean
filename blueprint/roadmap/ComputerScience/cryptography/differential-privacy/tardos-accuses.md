# Tardos accusation

Tardos's rule accuses user $i$ on the word $w$ when its score for the
bias vector $p$ exceeds the threshold $Z$ strictly. Tardos takes $Z = 20 c k$ for coalitions of
size at most $c$ and error $\varepsilon$, with $k = \lceil \log(1/\varepsilon) \rceil$.

## Sources

- G. Tardos, *Optimal probabilistic fingerprint codes*, J. ACM 55(2), 2008, Section 2.1.
