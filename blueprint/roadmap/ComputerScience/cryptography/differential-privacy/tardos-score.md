# Tardos score

The accusation score of user $i$ on the word $w$ for the bias vector $p$:
$\sum_{j \,:\, w_j = 1} U_{ij}$, where $U_{ij}$ is the Tardos weight of the bias $p_j$ and the
bit $c_{ij}$.

## Sources

- G. Tardos, *Optimal probabilistic fingerprint codes*, J. ACM 55(2), 2008, Section 2.1.
