# Codebook with independent biased entries

For a bias vector $p \in [0, 1]^d$, the
distribution on codebooks whose entries are independent with $\Pr[c_{ij} = 1] = p_j$: the second
phase of Tardos's construction, given the biases.

## Sources

- G. Tardos, *Optimal probabilistic fingerprint codes*, J. ACM 55(2), 2008, Section 2.1.
