# One-fold composition keeps its budget

If $\varepsilon_0 \ge 0$, $\delta_0 \ge 0$ and for every empty transcript $t$ the mechanism $d \mapsto K_0(t, d)$ is [$(\varepsilon_0, \delta_0)$-differentially private](../approximate-differential-privacy/is-approx-dp.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$, then the [$1$-fold adaptive composition](kfold-compose.md), whose outputs are pairs $(\ast, a)$ with $a \in A_0$, is $(\varepsilon_0, \delta_0)$-differentially private for $\mathrm{adj}$.
