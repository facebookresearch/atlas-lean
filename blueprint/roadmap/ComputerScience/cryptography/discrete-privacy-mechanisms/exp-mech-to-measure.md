# The exponential mechanism agrees with its measure form

Let $R$ be a finite nonempty type with a measurable structure in which singletons are measurable. For every $u$, $\varepsilon$, $\Delta$ and database $d$, the measure induced by the [exponential-mechanism probability mass function](exp-mech.md) at $d$ equals the [exponential mechanism](../laplace-and-exponential-mechanisms/exp-mechanism.md) measure at $d$.
