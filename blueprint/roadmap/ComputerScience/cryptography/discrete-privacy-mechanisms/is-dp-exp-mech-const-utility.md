# Exponential mechanism with database-independent utility is 0-differentially private

Let $R$ be finite and nonempty. If the utility does not depend on the database, $u(d, r) = u(d', r)$ for all $d, d', r$, then for all real $\varepsilon$, $\Delta$ and every relation $\mathrm{adj}$, the [exponential mechanism](exp-mech.md) is [$0$-differentially private](../differential-privacy/is-dp.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$. Its output distribution is the same at every database, though not necessarily uniform.
