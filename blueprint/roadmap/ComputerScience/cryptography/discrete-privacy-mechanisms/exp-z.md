# Partition function of the exponential mechanism

For a finite type $R$, a utility function $u : R \to \mathbb{R}$ and real $\varepsilon$, $\Delta$, the partition function is the total [weight](exp-weight.md)

$$
Z = \sum_{r \in R} \exp\Big(\frac{\varepsilon\, u(r)}{2\Delta}\Big),
$$

computed in the extended nonnegative reals.
