# Randomized response

For $0 \le p \le 1$, randomized response on a bit $b$ is the probability distribution on bits that reports $b$ with probability $p$ and the other bit with probability $1 - p$, with masses given by the [randomized-response masses](rr-mass.md).

## Sources
