# Total unnormalized two-sided geometric mass

For every $\rho \in [0, \infty]$,

$$
\sum_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \rho^{|z|} = (1 - \rho)^{-1} + \rho\,(1 - \rho)^{-1}
$$

in the extended nonnegative reals, where $1 - \rho$ is truncated subtraction (so $1 - \rho = 0$ and $(1-\rho)^{-1} = \infty$ when $\rho \ge 1$).
