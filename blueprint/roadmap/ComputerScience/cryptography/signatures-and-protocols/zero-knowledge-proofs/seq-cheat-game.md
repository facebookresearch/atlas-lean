# The experiment of an adaptive cheating prover over $m$ sequential rounds

For a statement $x$, a finite type $R$ of prover coins, an adaptive prover (commitments $a(r, \ell)$
and answers $z(r, \ell, e)$ depending on the coins $r$ and the earlier challenges $\ell$) and a
number $m$ of rounds, draw $r$ uniformly in $R$ and $m$ independent uniform challenges, and output
whether the verifier [accepts all rounds](all-accept.md).
