# The view of an arbitrary verifier in one round

For a Sigma protocol, a statement $x$ with a witness $w$, and a verifier $V^*$ that chooses its
challenge as an arbitrary function of the commitment and of its own coins $r$: with uniform coins $r$
and uniform prover coins $k$, the verifier's view is $(r, a, z)$, where $a$ is the honest commitment
and $z$ the honest answer to the challenge $V^*(a, r)$.
