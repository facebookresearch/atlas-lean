# Special soundness of Schnorr as a Sigma protocol

If $q$ is prime and $g^q = 1$, [Schnorr's protocol as a Sigma protocol](schnorr-sigma-protocol.md) is
[specially sound](special-sound.md) with the [Schnorr extractor](schnorr-sigma-extract.md): this is the
[extraction property](../schnorr-identification-scheme/extract-spec.md) of the Schnorr scheme.
