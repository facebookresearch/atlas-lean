# Merkle signature forgery experiment

The existential forgery experiment against the $N$-key stateful Merkle signer (Katz and Lindell's
$\mathrm{Sig}\text{-}\mathrm{forge}$, Definition 12.2, for a stateful scheme) draws the secret keys
and the forger's coins uniformly from finite nonempty sets, and outputs $1$ when the forger wins.
