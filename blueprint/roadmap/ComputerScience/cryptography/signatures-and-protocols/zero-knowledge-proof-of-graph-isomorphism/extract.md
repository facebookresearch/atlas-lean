# The extractor

From answers $\sigma$ to a challenge $\alpha$ and $\sigma'$ to a challenge $\alpha'$, the extractor
outputs $\sigma_1^{-1} \circ \sigma_0$, where $\sigma_0$ is the answer to the challenge $0$ and
$\sigma_1$ the answer to the challenge $1$ (that is, $\sigma^{-1} \circ \sigma'$ if $\alpha = 1$ and
$\sigma'^{-1} \circ \sigma$ otherwise).
