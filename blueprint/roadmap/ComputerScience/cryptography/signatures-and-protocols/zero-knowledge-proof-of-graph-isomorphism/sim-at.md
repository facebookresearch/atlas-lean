# The per-challenge simulator

For simple graphs on a finite vertex type $V$, a statement $(G_0, G_1)$ and a challenge $\alpha$, the
simulator draws a uniform permutation
$\sigma$ and outputs the pair $(\sigma(G_\alpha), \sigma)$, computed from the two graphs alone, where
$G_\alpha$ is [the graph selected by $\alpha$](pick-graph.md).
