# Close iff

For bit laws $X(k,z)$ and $Y(k,z)$ let $a(k,z)$ be the absolute difference in their probabilities
of true. The function $k\mapsto\sup_{z\in\{0,1\}^*}a(k,z)$ is negligible **if and only if**
for each exponent $c\in\mathbb N$ there exists a threshold $K\in\mathbb N$ such that
$a(k,z)\le 1/k^c$ for every $k\ge K$ and every finite bit string $z$.
The same $K$ works for all $z$; it may be increased to at least one.
