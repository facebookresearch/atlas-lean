# Degree of a product of two polynomials of degree at most t

Let $F$ be a field, $t \in \mathbb{N}$, and $p, q$ arbitrary polynomials over $F$ with

$$
\deg p \le t \quad\text{and}\quad \deg q \le t,
$$

where $\deg$ is the natural degree (the zero polynomial has natural degree $0$). Then $\deg(pq) \le 2t$. No sharing structure and no exact degree $t$ is assumed; zero polynomials are allowed. In BGW this bounds the degree of the product of two degree-$t$ sharings.
