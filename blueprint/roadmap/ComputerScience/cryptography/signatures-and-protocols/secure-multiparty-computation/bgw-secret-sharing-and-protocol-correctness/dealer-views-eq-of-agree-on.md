# Re-shared subshares hide the values (agreement form)

Let `F` be a field and `Party` a finite type of parties with decidable
equality, with Shamir parameters `params` and a tail sampler `sampler`. If the
coalition `I` has at most `params.threshold` members and `h₁ i = h₂ i` for
every `i ∈ I`, then `dealerViews params sampler I h₁ = dealerViews params
sampler I h₂`.
