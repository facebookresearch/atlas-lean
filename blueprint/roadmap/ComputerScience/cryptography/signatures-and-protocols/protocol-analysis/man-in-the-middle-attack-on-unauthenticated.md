# Man-in-the-middle attack on unauthenticated Diffie–Hellman

In unauthenticated Diffie–Hellman key exchange over a cyclic group generated by $g$, an active adversary that intercepts Alice's message $g^a$ and Bob's message $g^b$ and replaces them with $g^{a'}$ and $g^{b'}$ of its own choice computes the key $g^{ab'}$ that Alice derives and the key $g^{a'b}$ that Bob derives. Hence the adversary can read and alter all traffic between them, and neither party can tell that it does not share a key with the other.
