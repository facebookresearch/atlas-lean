# Classical filter eq

Let $\alpha$ be any type, $p$ a predicate on $\alpha$ with a supplied decision procedure, and $s$ a finite subset of $\alpha$. Filtering $s$ by $p$ using classical decisions gives the same finite set as filtering by $p$ using the supplied decisions. No probability distribution, finiteness of $\alpha$, or computability of the supplied decisions is assumed. Its use here is to rewrite the classically filtered sums produced by pushing a finite distribution forward (`PMF.map_ofFintype`).
