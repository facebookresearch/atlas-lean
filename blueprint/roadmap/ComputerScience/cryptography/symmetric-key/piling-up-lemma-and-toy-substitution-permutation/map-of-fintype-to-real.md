# Pushforward of a finite distribution as a filtered sum

Let `α` and `β` be finite types with `β` having decidable equality, and let `f
: α → ENNReal` with `∑ a, f a = 1`. For `g : α → β` and `b : β`, the
probability of `b` under the pushforward of the distribution `f` along `g` is
the real sum of `f a` over all `a` with `g a = b`.
