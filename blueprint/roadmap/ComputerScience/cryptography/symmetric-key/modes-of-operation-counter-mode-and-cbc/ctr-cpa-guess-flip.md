# The challenge is a one-time pad on a fresh counter range

Let `ℓ ≤ N`. If the challenge counter range starting at `rs` is
disjoint from the counter ranges of the query counters `ρ`, then flipping the
challenge bit `b` and shifting the function `f` on the challenge range by the
difference `v` of the two challenge messages leaves the adversary's view,
hence its guess, unchanged.
