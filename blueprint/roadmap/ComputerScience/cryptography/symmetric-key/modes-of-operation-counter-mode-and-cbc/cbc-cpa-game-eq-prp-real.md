# The real world of the CBC reduction

Let the key space $K$, the block space $X$ and the adversary's coin space $R$ be finite and nonempty, let $X$ be an additive commutative group, and let $E$ be a [block cipher](block-cipher.md). For every CPA adversary against [CBC mode](cbc-scheme.md) with $\ell$-block messages whose coins are uniform on $R$ and which makes at most $q$ encryption queries in total along every path, the CPA game of CBC mode equals the real-world PRP game of $E$ against the CBC reduction with tape length $q$: the real world draws a key $k$ and the reduction's coins uniformly and independently and runs the reduction with oracle $E_k$.
