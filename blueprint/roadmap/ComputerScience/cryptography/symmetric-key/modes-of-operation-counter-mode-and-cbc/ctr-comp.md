# First-phase computations against counter mode

The type of first-phase computations of CPA adversaries against counter mode with $\ell$-block messages: oracle computations whose queries are messages in $Y^\ell$ and whose answers are ciphertexts in $\mathbb{Z}/N \times Y^\ell$, returning a pair $(m_0, m_1)$ of candidate messages **and** a continuation that maps the challenge ciphertext to another such oracle computation returning a bit.
