# Asymptotic eavesdropping security of the stream cipher

Let the admissible eavesdropper families make no queries, and suppose a family of generators is
indistinguishable from uniform relative to a class of distinguisher families that contains the
distinguishers built from every admissible eavesdropper family. Then the stream cipher family is
asymptotically secure against the admissible eavesdropper families (Katz and Lindell, Theorem 3.18).
Expansion is not needed, so the result also covers the one-time pad.
