# Asymptotic PRG security relative to a class of distinguishers

A family of generators is a secure PRG relative to a class of admissible distinguisher families when
it expands and is indistinguishable from uniform relative to that class. This is the part of Katz and
Lindell's Definition 3.14 that can be stated without a complexity model: the generator is not required
to be computable in polynomial time or to have polynomial stretch, and the distinguishers' efficiency
must be supplied through the class.
