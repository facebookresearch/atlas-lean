# Rényi divergence

For probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, the Rényi divergence $D_\alpha(P \| Q)$ is an extended real: if $P$ has [support inside](support.md) that of $Q$ and $\alpha > 1$, it is the [real Rényi divergence](renyi-div-real.md) $\frac{1}{\alpha-1} \log \sum_w P(w)^{\alpha} / Q(w)^{\alpha-1}$; otherwise it is $+\infty$.

## Sources

- I. Mironov, *Rényi Differential Privacy*, IEEE Computer Security Foundations Symposium (CSF), 2017.
