# Formal Rényi moment at orders at most one

For all probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type and every $\alpha \le 1$, the formal [Rényi moment](renyi-moment.md) of order $\alpha$ is $+\infty$. This is a convention of the formalization, matching the formal [Rényi divergence](renyi-div.md), not a property of the standard Rényi moment at those orders.
