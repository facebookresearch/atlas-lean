# Rényi divergence without support inclusion

If $P$ does not have [support inside](support.md) that of $Q$, then for every $\alpha$ the formal [Rényi divergence](renyi-div.md) $D_\alpha(P \| Q)$ is $+\infty$. For $\alpha > 1$ this agrees with the standard Rényi divergence; for $\alpha \le 1$ it is the formal convention.
