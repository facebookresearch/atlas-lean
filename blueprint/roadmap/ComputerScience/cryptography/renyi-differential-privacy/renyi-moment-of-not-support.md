# Rényi moment without support inclusion

If $P$ does not have [support inside](support.md) that of $Q$, then for every $\alpha$ the formal [Rényi moment](renyi-moment.md) of order $\alpha$ is $+\infty$. For $\alpha > 1$ this agrees with the standard Rényi moment; for $\alpha \le 1$ it is the formal convention.
