# Rényi moment

For probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, the Rényi moment of order $\alpha$ is an extended real: if $P$ has [support inside](support.md) that of $Q$ and $\alpha > 1$, it is the [real Rényi moment](renyi-moment-real.md) $\sum_w P(w)^{\alpha} / Q(w)^{\alpha - 1}$; otherwise it is $+\infty$.
