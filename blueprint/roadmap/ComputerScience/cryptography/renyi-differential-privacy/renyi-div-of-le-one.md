# Formal Rényi divergence at orders at most one

For all probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type and every $\alpha \le 1$, the formal [Rényi divergence](renyi-div.md) $D_\alpha(P \| Q)$ is $+\infty$, including $D_1(P \| P) = +\infty$. This is the convention of the formalization, not the standard Rényi divergence at small orders: Mironov's Definition 3 takes the order-one divergence to be the Kullback–Leibler divergence, so that $D_1(P \| P) = 0$, whereas here $D_\alpha = +\infty$ for every $\alpha \le 1$. Only orders $\alpha > 1$ are used for Rényi differential privacy.
