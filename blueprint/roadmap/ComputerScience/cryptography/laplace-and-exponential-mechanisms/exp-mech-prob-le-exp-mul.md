# Output probabilities on neighbouring databases

Let $R$ be a finite nonempty range. If $\varepsilon \ge 0$, $\Delta > 0$ and
$|u(x, r) - u(y, r)| \le \Delta$ for all $r \in R$, then the
[output probabilities](exp-mech-prob.md) satisfy $p_x(r) \le e^{\varepsilon} p_y(r)$ for every
output $r$: the weight of $r$ changes by a factor at
most $e^{\varepsilon/2}$, and so does the normalising sum.
