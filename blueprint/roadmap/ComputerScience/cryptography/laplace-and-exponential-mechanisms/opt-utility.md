# Optimal utility

$\mathrm{OPT}_u(x) = \max_{r \in R} u(x, r)$, the largest utility score of an output on database $x$,
over a finite nonempty range $R$.

## Sources

- C. Dwork and A. Roth, *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 2014, <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html>, Section 3.4 (before Theorem 3.11).
