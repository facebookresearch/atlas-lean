# The exponential mechanism is differentially private

If $\varepsilon \ge 0$, $\Delta > 0$ and $|u(x, r) - u(y, r)| \le \Delta$ for all outputs $r$ whenever
$x$ and $y$ are neighbours, then the exponential mechanism is $\varepsilon$-differentially private.

## Sources

- C. Dwork and A. Roth, *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 2014, <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html>, Theorem 3.10.
- F. McSherry and K. Talwar, *Mechanism design via differential privacy*, FOCS 2007 (the exponential mechanism, cited in Dwork and Roth 2014, Section 3.7).
