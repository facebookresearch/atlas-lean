# Prefix form of the hybrid argument

Prefix form of the hybrid argument: let `p 0, p 1, ...` be probability
distributions on a finite type `α`, let `E` be an event, and suppose that
for every `i < k` the probabilities of `E` under `p i` and `p (i + 1)`
differ by at most `ε`. Then for every `j ≤ k`, the probabilities of `E`
under `p 0` and `p j` differ by at most `j * ε`.
