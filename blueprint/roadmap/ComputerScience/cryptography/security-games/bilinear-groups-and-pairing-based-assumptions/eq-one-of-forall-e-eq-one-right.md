# The right kernel of the pairing is trivial

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. If $y \in G_2$ satisfies $e(x, y) = 1$ for every $x \in G_1$, then $y = 1$.
