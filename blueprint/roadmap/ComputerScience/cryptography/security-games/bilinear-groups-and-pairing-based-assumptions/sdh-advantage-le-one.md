# The q-SDH advantage is at most one

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. Let $t$ be a natural number, the degree parameter of the q-SDH problem (written $t$ because $q$ is the group order). For every q-SDH adversary $A$, the [q-SDH advantage](sdh-advantage.md) of $A$ is at most $1$.
