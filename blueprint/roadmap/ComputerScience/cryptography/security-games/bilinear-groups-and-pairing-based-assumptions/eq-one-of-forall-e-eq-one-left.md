# The left kernel of the pairing is trivial

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. If $x \in G_1$ satisfies $e(x, y) = 1$ for every $y \in G_2$, then $x = 1$.
