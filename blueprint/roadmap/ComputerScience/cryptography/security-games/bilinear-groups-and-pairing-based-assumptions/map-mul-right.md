# The pairing is multiplicative in its second argument

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. For all $x \in G_1$ and $y_1, y_2 \in G_2$,

$$e(x, y_1 y_2) = e(x, y_1)\, e(x, y_2).$$
