# Toy exponent model of a bilinear group

For an integer $q > 1$, the toy exponent model is the [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q$ in which $G_1$, $G_2$ and $G_T$ are all the additive group $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$, written multiplicatively, the three exponent isomorphisms are the identity, and the pairing is multiplication of residues, $e(a, b) = ab$. It shows that the interface can be instantiated for every $q > 1$.
