# Power law for the pairing

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. For all $x \in G_1$, $y \in G_2$ and natural numbers $m$, $n$,

$$e(x^m, y^n) = e(x, y)^{mn}.$$
