# Advantage bound for a pair of decisional Diffie–Hellman distinguishers

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. For a randomized distinguisher $D_1$ on triples of elements of $G_1$, a randomized distinguisher $D_2$ on triples of elements of $G_2$ and a real number $\varepsilon$, the bound holds when the [decisional Diffie–Hellman advantage in $G_1$](ddh1-advantage.md) of $D_1$ and the [decisional Diffie–Hellman advantage in $G_2$](ddh2-advantage.md) of $D_2$ are both at most $\varepsilon$. It concerns the two given distinguishers only; it is not the symmetric external Diffie–Hellman assumption, which bounds the advantage of every admissible distinguisher.
