# The discrete-logarithm adversary always wins the toy BDH experiment

Let $q > 1$. In the [toy exponent model](toy-model.md) of order $q$, the [discrete-logarithm adversary](toy-bdh-adversary.md) wins the [bilinear Diffie–Hellman win experiment](bdh-win.md) with probability one at every $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$.
