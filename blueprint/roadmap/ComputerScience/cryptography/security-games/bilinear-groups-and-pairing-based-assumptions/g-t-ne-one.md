# The target generator is not the identity

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. The generator $g_T = \phi_T(1)$ of $G_T$ is not the identity element of $G_T$.
