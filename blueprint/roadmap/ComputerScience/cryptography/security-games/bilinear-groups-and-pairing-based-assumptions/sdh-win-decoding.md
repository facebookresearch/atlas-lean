# The discrete-logarithm adversary always wins the q-SDH experiment

Let $B$ be a [bilinear group interface](bilinear-group-data.md) of order $q > 1$, with source groups $G_1$, $G_2$, target group $G_T$, exponent isomorphisms $\phi_1$, $\phi_2$, $\phi_T$ from $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ and pairing $e : G_1 \times G_2 \to G_T$. Write $g_1 = \phi_1(1)$, $g_2 = \phi_2(1)$ and $g_T = \phi_T(1)$ for the generators. Let $t$ be a natural number, the degree parameter of the q-SDH problem (written $t$ because $q$ is the group order). For every secret $x \in \mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$, the [discrete-logarithm adversary](sdh-decoding-adversary.md) wins the [q-SDH win experiment](sdh-win.md) at $x$ with probability one: its output $(1 - x, g_1)$ passes the check because $x + (1 - x) = 1$.
