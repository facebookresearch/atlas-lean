# Embedding a public-key CCA adversary with explicit coins

Embedding a public-key chosen-ciphertext adversary $A$ with explicit coins into the monadic framework, where its coins are drawn by a sampling operation, does not change its CCA experiment: for every public-key encryption scheme $S$ and each challenge bit $b$, the monadic public-key CCA experiment of the embedded adversary equals the public-key CCA experiment of $A$ with bit $b$.
