# Embedding a CPA adversary preserves its advantage

For every private-key encryption scheme $S$ and every coin CPA adversary $A$, the [monadic CPA advantage](m-cpa-advantage.md) of its [monadic form](of-coins.md) equals the CPA advantage of $A$. Both are $|\Pr[\text{game} = \text{true}] - \tfrac12|$ of the respective CPA game.
