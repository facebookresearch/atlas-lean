# Distinguishing advantage

The distinguishing advantage between two games $G_0$ and $G_1$ is
$$\mathrm{Adv}(G_0, G_1) = |\Pr[G_0 = 1] - \Pr[G_1 = 1]|,$$
the shape of the advantage of a distinguisher against a pseudorandom function (Katz and Lindell,
Definition 3.25) and of a DDH distinguisher (their Section 8.3.2).
