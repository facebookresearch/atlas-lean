# Monotonicity of a two-stage experiment

Draw $a \leftarrow \mu$ and then, independently, $b \leftarrow \nu$. If, for every $a$ in the support of $\mu$ and every $b$, the outcome $f(a, b)$ implies the outcome $g(a, b)$, then the probability that $f(a, b)$ holds is at most the probability that $g(a, b)$ holds.
