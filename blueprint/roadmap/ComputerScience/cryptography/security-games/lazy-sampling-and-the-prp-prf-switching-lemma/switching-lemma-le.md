# Switching lemma with the birthday bound

Let $D$ be a finite nonempty set with $N$ elements and $c$ an adaptive distinguisher with oracle
access to a function $D \to D$ that makes at most $q$ queries along every path. Its distinguishing
advantage between a uniformly random function $D \to D$ and a uniformly random permutation of $D$ is
at most $q(q - 1)/(2N)$ (Katz and Lindell, Lemma A.15).
