# Splitting a uniform distribution along a coordinate

Let $A$ and $B$ be finite nonempty sets, $S$ a nonempty subset of $A$, $F$ a subset of $B$, and
$g : A \to B$ with $g(S) \subseteq F$, such that the fibres $\{a \in S : g(a) = y\}$ for $y \in F$
all have the same size. Then drawing uniformly from $S$ is the same as drawing $y$ uniformly from
$F$ and then drawing uniformly from the fibre $\{a \in S : g(a) = y\}$.
