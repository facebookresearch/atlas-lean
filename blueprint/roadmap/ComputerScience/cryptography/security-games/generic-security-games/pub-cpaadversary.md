# Eavesdropping adversary against public-key encryption

An eavesdropping adversary against a public-key encryption scheme draws random coins $r$ from a
distribution of its own. From the public key and $r$ it chooses two messages $m_0, m_1$, and from
the public key, the challenge ciphertext and $r$ it outputs a guess $b' \in \{0, 1\}$.
