# Step count of baby-step giant-step

For every natural number $q$, the step count is

$$
m(q) = \lfloor \sqrt{q - 1} \rfloor + 1 ,
$$

with the integer floor square root and truncated subtraction. For $q \ge 1$ this equals $\lceil \sqrt q \rceil$; at $q = 0$ it is $1$, while $\lceil \sqrt 0 \rceil = 0$.
