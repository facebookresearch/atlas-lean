# Collision count of a list of affine forms

Let `q` be prime and let `l` be a list of
affine forms over `ZMod q` (`affEval`). At most `l.length.choose 2` points `x` of `ZMod q` make two
distinct forms of `l` take the same value at `x`.

## Sources

- V. Shoup, *Lower Bounds for Discrete Logarithms and Related Problems*, revised version of the Eurocrypt 1997 paper, Section 2, proof of Theorem 1.
