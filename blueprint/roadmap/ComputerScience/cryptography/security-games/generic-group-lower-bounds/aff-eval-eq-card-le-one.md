# Distinct affine forms agree at most once

Let `q` be prime, and read a pair `u = (a, b)`
over `ZMod q` as the affine form `x ↦ a + b * x` (`affEval`). Two distinct forms `u ≠ v` take the
same value at no more than one point `x` of `ZMod q`.

## Sources

- V. Shoup, *Lower Bounds for Discrete Logarithms and Related Problems*, revised version of the Eurocrypt 1997 paper, Section 2, Lemma 1, in one variable and degree at most 1 over the prime field.
