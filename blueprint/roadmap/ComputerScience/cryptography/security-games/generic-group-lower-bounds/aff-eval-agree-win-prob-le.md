# Agreement probability of two affine forms

Let `q` be prime and let `u ≠ v` be affine
forms over `ZMod q` (`affEval`). For `x` uniform in `ZMod q`, the probability that `u` and `v`
take the same value at `x` is at most `1 / q`.

## Sources

- V. Shoup, *Lower Bounds for Discrete Logarithms and Related Problems*, revised version of the Eurocrypt 1997 paper, Section 2, Lemma 1, in one variable and degree at most 1 over the prime field.
