# ElGamal eavesdropper as a public-key adversary

Let $R$ be finite and nonempty. An ElGamal eavesdropping adversary with coins in $R$, consisting of a choice map from a public key and coins to two messages and a guessing map from a public key, a ciphertext and coins to a bit, becomes a public-key CPA adversary whose coins are uniform on $R$ and whose choice and guessing maps are exactly those of the ElGamal adversary.
