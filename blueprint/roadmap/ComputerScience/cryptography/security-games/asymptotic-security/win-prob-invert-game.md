# Success probability of the inversion experiment

The inversion experiment with $m$ coins outputs $1$ with probability

$$\frac{|\{(x, \omega) \in \{0, 1\}^n \times \{0, 1\}^m : f_n(x') = f_n(x)\}|}{2^{n + m}},$$

the success probability of the inverter, where $x'$ is its answer on $(n, f_n(x), \omega)$ read as an
$n$-bit string.
