# Nonnegative rational multiples of negligible functions

Let $e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Q}$ be negligible and $a \in \mathbb{Q}$ with $a \ge 0$. Then $n \mapsto a \, e(n)$ is negligible. Here $e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Q}$ is negligible when for every $c$ there is $N$ with $e(n) \le 1/n^c$ for all $n \ge N$; this is a one-sided bound, and $e$ itself need not be nonnegative. This is the constant case of the polynomial-multiple part of Katz and Lindell, Proposition 3.6; the real-valued counterpart is [nonnegative real multiples of negligible advantages](const-mul.md).
