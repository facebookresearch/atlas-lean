# Privacy loss of an outcome

For probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a type $\Omega$ and an outcome $w$, the privacy loss is the real number

$$
L_{P \| Q}(w) = \log \frac{P(w)}{Q(w)},
$$

computed on real masses with the conventions $x / 0 = 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$; so the loss is $0$ when $P(w) = 0$ or $Q(w) = 0$.
