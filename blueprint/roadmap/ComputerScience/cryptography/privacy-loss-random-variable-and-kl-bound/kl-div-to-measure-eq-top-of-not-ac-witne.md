# Kullback–Leibler divergence is infinite off the support

Let $\Omega$ be a measurable space with measurable singletons and $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$. If some $a$ has $P(a) \ne 0$ and $Q(a) = 0$, then the Kullback–Leibler divergence of the induced measures is $\mathrm{KL}(P \| Q) = \infty$.
