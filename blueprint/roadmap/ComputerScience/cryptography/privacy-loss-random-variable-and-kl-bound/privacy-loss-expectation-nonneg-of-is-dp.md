# Expected privacy loss of a private mechanism is nonnegative

If $M$ is [$\varepsilon$-differentially private](../differential-privacy/is-dp.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$ and both $\mathrm{adj}(d, d')$ and $\mathrm{adj}(d', d)$ hold, then the [expected privacy loss](privacy-loss-expectation.md) of $M(d)$ against $M(d')$ is nonnegative.
