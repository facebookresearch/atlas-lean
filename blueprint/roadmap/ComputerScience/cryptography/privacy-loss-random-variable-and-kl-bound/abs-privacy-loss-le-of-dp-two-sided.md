# Two-sided bounds bound the absolute privacy loss

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a type $\Omega$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, and assume the two-sided pointwise bounds $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ and $Q(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} P(w)$ for every $w$. Then for every $w$ with $P(w) \ne 0$, the [privacy loss](privacy-loss.md) satisfies $|L_{P\|Q}(w)| \le \varepsilon$.
