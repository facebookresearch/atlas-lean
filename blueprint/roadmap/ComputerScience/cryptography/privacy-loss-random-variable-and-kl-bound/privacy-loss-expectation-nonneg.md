# Expected privacy loss is nonnegative

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a type $\Omega$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, and assume the two-sided pointwise bounds $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ and $Q(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} P(w)$ for every $w$. Then the [expected privacy loss](privacy-loss-expectation.md) is nonnegative (Gibbs' inequality).
