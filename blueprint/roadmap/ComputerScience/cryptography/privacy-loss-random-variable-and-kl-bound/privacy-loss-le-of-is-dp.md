# Privacy loss of a pure differentially private mechanism

If $M$ is [$\varepsilon$-differentially private](../differential-privacy/is-dp.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$ and $\mathrm{adj}(d, d')$, then for every output $o$ the [privacy loss](privacy-loss.md) satisfies $L_{M(d)\|M(d')}(o) \le \varepsilon$. No symmetry of $\mathrm{adj}$ is needed.
