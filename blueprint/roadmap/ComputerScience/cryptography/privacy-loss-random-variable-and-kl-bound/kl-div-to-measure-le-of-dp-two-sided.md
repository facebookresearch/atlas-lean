# Kullback–Leibler bound from two-sided pointwise bounds

Let $\Omega$ be a countable measurable space with measurable singletons, $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, with $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ and $Q(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} P(w)$ for every $w$. Then

$$
\mathrm{KL}(P \,\|\, Q) \le \varepsilon\,(e^{\varepsilon} - 1).
$$

## Sources

- C. Dwork and A. Roth, *The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy*, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(3–4), 2014, <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/Papers/privacybook.pdf>, Lemma 3.18 (discrete case).
