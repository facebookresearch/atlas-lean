# Real value of the Kullback–Leibler divergence

Let $\Omega$ be a countable measurable space with measurable singletons, $P, Q$ probability mass functions on $\Omega$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, with $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ and $Q(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} P(w)$ for every $w$. Then the Kullback–Leibler divergence $\mathrm{KL}(P \| Q)$ of the induced measures, converted to a real number, equals the [expected privacy loss](privacy-loss-expectation.md).
