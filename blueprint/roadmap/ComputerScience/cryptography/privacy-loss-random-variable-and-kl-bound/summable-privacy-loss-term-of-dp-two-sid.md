# The expected privacy loss series converges under two-sided bounds

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a type $\Omega$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, and assume the two-sided pointwise bounds $P(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} Q(w)$ and $Q(w) \le e^{\varepsilon} P(w)$ for every $w$. Then the series $\sum_w P(w)\, L_{P\|Q}(w)$ is summable; each term is bounded in absolute value by $\varepsilon\, P(w)$.
