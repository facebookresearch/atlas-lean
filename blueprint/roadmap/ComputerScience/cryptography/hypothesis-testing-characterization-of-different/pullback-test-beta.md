# Pulling a test back preserves type-II error

For finite types $\Omega, \Omega_2$, a probability mass function $Q$ on $\Omega$, a channel $k$ and $\varphi : \Omega_2 \to \mathbb{R}$, the [type-II error](beta-of.md) under $Q$ of the [pulled-back test](pullback-test.md) $k^*\varphi$ equals the type-II error of $\varphi$ under the composed distribution $Q$ followed by $k$.
