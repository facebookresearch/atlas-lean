# The tradeoff condition bounds the tradeoff function from below

If probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type satisfy the [Kairouz–Oh–Viswanath condition](kovpair.md) with parameters $(\varepsilon, \delta)$, then for every $\alpha$ with $0 \le \alpha \le 1$,

$$
f_{\varepsilon, \delta}(\alpha) \le T(P, Q)(\alpha),
$$

where $f_{\varepsilon,\delta}$ is the [approximate-privacy tradeoff bound](f-eps-delta.md) and $T$ the [tradeoff function](tradeoff.md).

## Sources

- J. Dong, A. Roth and W. J. Su, *Gaussian Differential Privacy*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 2022; arXiv:1905.02383, Proposition 2.5.
