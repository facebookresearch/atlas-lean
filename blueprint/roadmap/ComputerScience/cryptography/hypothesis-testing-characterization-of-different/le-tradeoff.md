# Lower bounds on the tradeoff function

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a finite type, $\alpha \ge 0$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$. If $x \le \beta_Q(\varphi)$ for every [randomized test](is-test.md) $\varphi$ with $\alpha_P(\varphi) \le \alpha$, then $x \le T(P, Q)(\alpha)$, the [tradeoff function](tradeoff.md) at $\alpha$.
