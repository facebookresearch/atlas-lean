# A test-weighted signed sum is bounded by its positive part

Let $\Omega$ be a finite type, $\varphi$ a [randomized test](is-test.md) on $\Omega$, $v : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $\delta \in \mathbb{R}$, and $S = \{ w \mid v(w) > 0 \}$. If $\sum_{w \in S} v(w) \le \delta$, then

$$
\sum_{w \in \Omega} \varphi(w)\, v(w) \le \delta .
$$
