# Pulling a test back preserves type-I error

For finite types $\Omega, \Omega_2$, a probability mass function $P$ on $\Omega$, a channel $k$ and $\varphi : \Omega_2 \to \mathbb{R}$, the [type-I error](alpha-of.md) under $P$ of the [pulled-back test](pullback-test.md) $k^*\varphi$ equals the type-I error of $\varphi$ under the composed distribution $P$ followed by $k$.
