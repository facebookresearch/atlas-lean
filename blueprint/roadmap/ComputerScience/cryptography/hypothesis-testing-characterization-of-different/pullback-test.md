# Pulling a test back through a channel

Let $\Omega, \Omega_2$ be finite types, $k$ a channel assigning to each $w \in \Omega$ a probability mass function $k(w)$ on $\Omega_2$, and $\varphi : \Omega_2 \to \mathbb{R}$. The pulled-back function on $\Omega$ is

$$
(k^*\varphi)(w) = \sum_{y \in \Omega_2} k(w)(y)\, \varphi(y).
$$
