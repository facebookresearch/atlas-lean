# Tradeoff lower bound of approximate differential privacy

For real $\varepsilon$, $\delta$ and $\alpha$,

$$
f_{\varepsilon, \delta}(\alpha) = \max\Big(0,\ 1 - \delta - e^{\varepsilon} \alpha,\ e^{-\varepsilon}(1 - \delta - \alpha)\Big).
$$

## Sources

- J. Dong, A. Roth and W. J. Su, *Gaussian Differential Privacy*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 2022; arXiv:1905.02383, the function $f_{\varepsilon,\delta}$ (Section 2).
