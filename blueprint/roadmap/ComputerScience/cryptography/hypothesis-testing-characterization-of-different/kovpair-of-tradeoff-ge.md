# A tradeoff lower bound gives the tradeoff condition

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a finite type and $\varepsilon, \delta \in \mathbb{R}$. If $f_{\varepsilon, \delta}(\alpha) \le T(P, Q)(\alpha)$ for every $\alpha \in [0, 1]$, then $(P, Q)$ satisfies the [Kairouz–Oh–Viswanath condition](kovpair.md) with parameters $(\varepsilon, \delta)$.

## Sources

- J. Dong, A. Roth and W. J. Su, *Gaussian Differential Privacy*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 2022; arXiv:1905.02383, Proposition 2.5.
