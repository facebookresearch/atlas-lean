# The tradeoff condition at adjacent pairs gives privacy

Let $M$ be a mechanism with values in probability mass functions on a finite type, $\mathrm{adj}$ a relation and $\varepsilon \ge 0$, $\delta \ge 0$. If $(M(d), M(d'))$ satisfies the [Kairouz–Oh–Viswanath condition](kovpair.md) with parameters $(\varepsilon, \delta)$ for every ordered pair with $\mathrm{adj}(d, d')$, then $M$ is [$(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differentially private](is-approx-dpfin.md) for $\mathrm{adj}$. No symmetry of $\mathrm{adj}$ is needed.
