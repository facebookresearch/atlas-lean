# Privacy in both orders gives the tradeoff condition

Let $M$ be [$(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differentially private](is-approx-dpfin.md) with finite outputs for $\mathrm{adj}$. If $\mathrm{adj}(d, d')$ and $\mathrm{adj}(d', d)$ both hold, then $(M(d), M(d'))$ satisfies the [Kairouz–Oh–Viswanath condition](kovpair.md) with parameters $(\varepsilon, \delta)$. No symmetry of $\mathrm{adj}$ is assumed beyond this pair.
