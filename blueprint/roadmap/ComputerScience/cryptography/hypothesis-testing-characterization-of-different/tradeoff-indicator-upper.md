# A rejection set bounds the tradeoff at its own level

For probability mass functions $P, Q$ on a finite type and every $S \subseteq \Omega$, the [tradeoff function](tradeoff.md) at level $P(S)$, the [type-I error](type-i.md) of $S$, is at most the [type-II error](type-ii.md) of $S$:

$$
T(P, Q)\big(P(S)\big) \le Q(\Omega \setminus S).
$$
