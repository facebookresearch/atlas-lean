# Real form of composing a distribution with a channel

For a probability mass function $P$ on a finite type $\Omega$, a channel $k$ from $\Omega$ to probability mass functions on a finite type $\Omega_2$, and $y \in \Omega_2$, the mass at $y$ of the composed distribution (draw $w \sim P$, then $y \sim k(w)$) is, as a real number,

$$
\sum_{w \in \Omega} P(w)\, k(w)(y).
$$
