# A feasible test bounds the tradeoff function

Let $P, Q$ be probability mass functions on a finite type and $\alpha \ge 0$. For every [randomized test](is-test.md) $\varphi$ with $\alpha_P(\varphi) \le \alpha$, the [tradeoff function](tradeoff.md) satisfies $T(P, Q)(\alpha) \le \beta_Q(\varphi)$.
