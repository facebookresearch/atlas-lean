# Soundness of Merkle inclusion proofs for an injective hash

Let $h$ be injective as a function of pairs and let $r$ be the root of a list of $n$ leaves. If a path
for $x$ at position $i$ verifies the claim that leaf $i$ of a tree with $n$ leaves and root $r$ is $x$,
then leaf $i$ of the list is $x$.
