# Theorem 1 (Blichfield)

For any full rank lattice $L$ and measurable set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\operatorname{Vol}(S) > \det(L)$, there exist distinct $z_1, z_2 \in S$ such that $z_1 - z_2 \in L$.
