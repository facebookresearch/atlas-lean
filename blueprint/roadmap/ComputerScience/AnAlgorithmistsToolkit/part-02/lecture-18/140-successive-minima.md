# Definition (Successive minima)

The $i$th successive minimum of lattice $\Lambda$, $\lambda_i(\Lambda)$, is defined to be $\inf\{r \mid \dim(\operatorname{span}(\Lambda \cap \bar{B}(0,r))) \geq i\}$.
