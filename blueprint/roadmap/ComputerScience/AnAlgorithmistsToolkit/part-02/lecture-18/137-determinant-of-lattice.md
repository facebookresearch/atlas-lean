# Definition (Determinant of lattice)

Let $L = L(B)$ be a lattice of rank $n$. We define the determinant of $L$, denoted $\det(L)$, as the $n$-dimensional volume of $P(B)$, i.e.\ $\det(L) = \sqrt{\det(B^\top B)}$. In particular if $L$ is a full rank lattice, $\det(L) = |\det(B)|$.
