# Theorem (Blichfield)

For any full-rank lattice $\Lambda$ and (measurable) set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\operatorname{vol}(S) > \det(\Lambda)$, there exist distinct $z_1, z_2 \in S$ such that $z_1 - z_2 \in \Lambda$.
