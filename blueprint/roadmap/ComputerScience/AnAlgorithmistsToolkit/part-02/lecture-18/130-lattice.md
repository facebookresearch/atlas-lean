# Definition (Lattice)

Given $n$ linearly independent vectors $b_1, \ldots, b_n \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the lattice generated by them is defined as $L(b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n) = \{\sum x_i b_i \mid x_i \in \mathbb{Z}\}$. We refer to $b_1, \ldots, b_n$ as a basis of the lattice. Equivalently, if we define $B$ as the $m \times n$ matrix whose columns are $b_1, \ldots, b_n$, then the lattice generated by $B$ is $L(B) = L(b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n) = \{Bx \mid x \in \mathbb{Z}^n\}$. We say that the rank of the lattice is $n$ and its dimension is $m$. If $n = m$, the lattice is called a full-rank lattice.
