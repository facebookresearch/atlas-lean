# Lemma 3

For a unit vector $x \in \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$, and $f(x) = \sqrt{x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \cdots + x_k^2}$. Let $x$ be a vector randomly chosen with uniform distribution from $\mathbb{S}^{n-1}$ and $M$ be the median of $f(x)$. Then $f(x)$ is sharply concentrated with: $\Pr[|f(x) - M| \geq t] \leq 2 e^{-t^2 n / 2}$.
