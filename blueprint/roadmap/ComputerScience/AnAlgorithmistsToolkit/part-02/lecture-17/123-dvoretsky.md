# Theorem 6 (Dvoretsky)

There is a positive constant $c > 0$ such that, for all $\varepsilon$ and $n$, every $n$-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body has a section within distance $1 + \varepsilon$ of the unit ball of dimension $k \geq \frac{c \varepsilon^2}{\log(1 + \varepsilon^{-1})} \log n$.
