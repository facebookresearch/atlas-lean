# Theorem 8 (One version of the Chernoff bound)

The probability that $R$ fails to $\epsilon$-approximate $\mathbf{E}[R]$ is $\Pr\bigl[|R - \mathbf{E}[R]| \geq \epsilon\, \mathbf{E}[R]\bigr] \leq 2 e^{-n p \epsilon^2 / 12} = 2 e^{-\mathbf{E}[R] \epsilon^2 / 12}$.
