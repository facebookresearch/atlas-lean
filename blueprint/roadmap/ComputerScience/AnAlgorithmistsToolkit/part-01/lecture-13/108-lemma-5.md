# Lemma 5 (Lecture 13, Section 3.3)

Let $L = C \cap \{x_1 \leq \bar{x}_1\}$ be the left side of the cone (which is $x_1$-aligned with vertex at the origin). Then $\frac{1}{2} \geq \frac{V(L)}{V(C)} \geq \frac{1}{e}$.
