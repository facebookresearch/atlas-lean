# Theorem 2 (Brunn's Theorem)

Let $K$ be a convex body, and let $v_K(t)$ be defined as the $(n-1)$-dimensional volume of the parallel slice $K_t = K \cap \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid x_1 = t\}$. Then the function $v_K(t)^{\frac{1}{n-1}}$ is concave.
