# Theorem 4 (Isoperimetric inequality)

For any convex body $K$, with $n$-dimensional volume $V(K)$ and surface area $S(K)$, $\left(\frac{V(K)}{V(B_2^n)}\right)^{1/n} \leq \left(\frac{S(K)}{S(B_2^n)}\right)^{\frac{1}{n-1}}$.
