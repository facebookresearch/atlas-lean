# Theorem 6 (Grunbaum's Theorem)

Let $K$ be a convex body, and divide it into $K_1$ and $K_2$ using a hyperplane. If $K_1$ contains the centroid of $K$, then $\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(K_1)}{\operatorname{Vol}(K)} \geq \frac{1}{e}$.
