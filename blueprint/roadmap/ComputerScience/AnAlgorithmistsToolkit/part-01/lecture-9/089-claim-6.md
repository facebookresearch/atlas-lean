# Claim 6 (Lecture 9, Section 2.1)

Let $G = (V, E_G, w_G)$ and $H = (V, E_H, w_H)$ be weighted graphs on the same vertex set such that $w_G(i,j) \geq w_H(i,j)$ for all edges $(i,j) \in E$. Then, $G \succeq H$.
