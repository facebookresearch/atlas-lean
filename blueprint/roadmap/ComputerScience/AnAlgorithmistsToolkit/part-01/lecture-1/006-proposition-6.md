# Proposition 6 (Lecture 1, Section 4)

The following results hold about the eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ and corresponding eigenvectors $v_i$ of $L_G$: (1) Order the eigenvalues so $\lambda_1 \leq \cdots \leq \lambda_n$, with corresponding eigenvectors $v_1, \ldots, v_n$. Then $v_1 = \mathbf{1}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0$. So for all $i$, $\lambda_i \geq 0$. (2) One can get much information about the graph $G$ from just the first few nontrivial eigenvectors.
