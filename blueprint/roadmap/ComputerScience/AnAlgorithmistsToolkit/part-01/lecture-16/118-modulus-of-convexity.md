# Definition 5 (Modulus of Convexity)

The modulus of convexity $\delta$ for a sphere is $\delta(\epsilon) = \inf\!\left\{1 - \left|\frac{x+y}{2}\right| : x, y \in S^{n-1},\, |x-y| \geq \epsilon\right\}$.
