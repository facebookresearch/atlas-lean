# Theorem 3 (Lecture 16)

If $f$ is Lipschitz, $M$ is its median, and $\epsilon > 0$, then $\operatorname{Vol}(\{x : |f(x) - M| > \epsilon\}) \leq 2 e^{-n\epsilon^2/2}$.
