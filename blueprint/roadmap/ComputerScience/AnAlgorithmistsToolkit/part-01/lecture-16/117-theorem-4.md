# Theorem 4 (Lecture 16)

For any $A \subset S^{n-1}$ and any $\epsilon > 0$, $\operatorname{Vol}(A_{\epsilon}) > 1 - \frac{2 e^{-n\epsilon^2/16}}{\operatorname{Vol}(A)}$.
