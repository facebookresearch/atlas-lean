# Claim 6 (Section 1.2)

For any $t \in (0,1)$, there exists a polynomial $p_k^{(t)}$ with the following properties: (1) $p_k^{(t)}$ is of degree $k$, (2) $p_k^{(t)}(1) = 1$, (3) $|p_k^{(t)}(x)| \leq 2(1 + \sqrt{2t})^{-k}$ for any $x \in [0, 1-t]$.
