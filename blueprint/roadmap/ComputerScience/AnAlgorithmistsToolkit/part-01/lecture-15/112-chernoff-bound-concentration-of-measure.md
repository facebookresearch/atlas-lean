# Theorem 1 (Chernoff bound / Concentration of Measure)

Let $x \in \{\pm 1\}^n$ be independent random variables with $\Pr[x_i = 1] = 0.5$, and $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ satisfying $\sum a_i^2 = 1$ (some can be negative). Then $\Pr\!\left[\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i x_i\right| > t\right] \leq 2 e^{-t^2/2}$.
