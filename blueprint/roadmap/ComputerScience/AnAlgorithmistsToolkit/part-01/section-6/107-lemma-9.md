# Lemma 9

Let $A$ and $B$ be boxes in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Then $\operatorname{Vol}(A \oplus B)^{1/n} \geq \operatorname{Vol}(A)^{1/n} + \operatorname{Vol}(B)^{1/n}$.
