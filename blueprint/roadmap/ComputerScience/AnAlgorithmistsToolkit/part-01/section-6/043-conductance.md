# Definition 7 (Conductance)

For $S \subseteq V(G)$, let $\Phi(S) = \frac{e(S)}{\min\bigl(\sum_{v \in S} d(v),\, \sum_{v \in \bar{S}} d(v)\bigr)}$. We define the conductance to be $\Phi(G) = \min_{S \subset V} \Phi(S)$.
