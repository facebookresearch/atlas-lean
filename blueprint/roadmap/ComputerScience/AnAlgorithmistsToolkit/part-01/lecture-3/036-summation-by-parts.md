# Lemma 8 (Summation by Parts)

For any $z_1 \leq \cdots \leq z_m = 0$, $\sum_{(i,j) \in E'_{-}} |z_i - z_j| \geq \varphi \sum_{i=1}^{m} |z_i|$.
