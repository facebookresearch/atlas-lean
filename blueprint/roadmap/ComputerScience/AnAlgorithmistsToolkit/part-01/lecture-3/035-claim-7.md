# Claim 7

$\frac{\sum_{(i,j) \in E} (y_i - y_j)^2}{\sum_{i \in V} y_i^2} \geq \frac{\sum_{(i,j) \in E'} (y_i - y_j)^2}{\sum_{i \in V} y_i^2}$, where $E'$ is the edge set obtained by replacing any edge $(i,j)$ crossing $y_m = 0$ with two edges $(i,m)$ and $(m,j)$.
