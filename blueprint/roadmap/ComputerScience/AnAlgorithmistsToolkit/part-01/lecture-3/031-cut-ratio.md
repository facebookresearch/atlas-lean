# Definition 3 (Cut Ratio)

The cut ratio $\varphi$ of a cut $S$-$\bar{S}$ is given by $\varphi(S) = e(S) / \min(|S|, |\bar{S}|)$. The cut of minimum ratio is the cut that minimizes $\varphi(S)$. The isoperimetric number of a graph $G$ is the value of the minimum cut, $\varphi(G) = \min_{S \subset V} \varphi(S)$.
