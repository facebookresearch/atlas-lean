# Definition 4 (Determinant)

For a given $n \times n$ matrix $M$, the determinant is given by $\det(M) = \sum_{\pi \in S_n} \operatorname{sgn}(\pi) \prod_{i=1}^{n} M_{i,\pi(i)}$.
