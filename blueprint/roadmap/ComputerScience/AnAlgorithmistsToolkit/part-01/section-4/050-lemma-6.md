# Lemma 6 (Section 4.2.3 (Bounding the $r_k$), Lecture 5)

Let $G$ be a bipartite graph of minimum degree $\geq n/2$. Then every partial matching in $M_{k-1}$ has an augmenting path of length $\leq 3$.
