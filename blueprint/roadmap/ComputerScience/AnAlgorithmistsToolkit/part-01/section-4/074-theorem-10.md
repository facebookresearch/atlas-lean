# Theorem 10

For every initial distribution $p^0$, all $t$, and every $x \in [0, m]$, $I^t(x) \leq \frac{1}{2}\bigl(I^{t-1}(x - 2\varphi_G x) + I^{t-1}(x + 2\varphi_G x)\bigr)$, and for every $x \in [m, 2m]$, $I^t(x) \leq \frac{1}{2}\bigl(I^{t-1}(x - 2\varphi_G(2m - x)) + I^{t-1}(x + 2\varphi_G(2m - x))\bigr)$.
