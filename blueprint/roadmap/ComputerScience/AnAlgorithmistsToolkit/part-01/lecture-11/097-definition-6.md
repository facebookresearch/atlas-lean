# Definition 6 (Lecture 11, Section 5)

Let $K$ and $L$ be two convex bodies. The Banach--Mazur distance $d(K, L)$ is the least positive $d \in \mathbb{R}$ for which there is a linear image $L'$ of $L$ such that $L' \subseteq K \subseteq dL'$, where $dL'$ is the convex body obtained by multiplying every vector in $L'$ by the scalar $d$.
