# Theorem 11 (Lovasz-Simonovits)

For all initial distribution $p^0$ and every $t$, $$I^{t}(x) \leq \min\!\left(\sqrt{x},\, \sqrt{2m-x}\right) \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\varphi_G^{2}\right)^{t} + \frac{x}{2m}.$$
