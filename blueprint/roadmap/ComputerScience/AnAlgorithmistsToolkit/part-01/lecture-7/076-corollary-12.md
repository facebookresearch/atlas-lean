# Corollary 12

For $W$ a set of vertices, and $x = \sum_{w \in W} d_w$, $$\left| \sum_{w \in W} p^t(w) - \pi(w) \right| \leq \min\!\left(\sqrt{x},\, \sqrt{2m - x}\right) \left(1 - \frac{1}{2} \varphi_G^2 \right)^t.$$
