# Lemma 15 (Complete graph)

The Laplacian for the complete graph $K_n$ on $n$ vertices has eigenvalue $0$ with multiplicity $1$ and eigenvalue $n$ with multiplicity $n-1$ and associated eigenspace $\{x \mid x \cdot \mathbf{1} = 0\}$.
