# Lemma 22 (Bounds on λ₂ and λₙ)

Given $\lambda_i$ and $d_i$ as above, $\lambda_2 \leq (\sum_i d_i)/(n-1)$ and $\lambda_n \geq (\sum_i d_i)/(n-1)$.
