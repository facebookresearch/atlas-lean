# Lemma 21 (Sum of the eigenvalues)

Given an $n$-vertex graph $G$ with degrees $d_i$, where $d_{\max} = \max_i d_i$, and Laplacian $L_G$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_i$, then $\sum_i \lambda_i = \sum_i d_i \leq d_{\max} \cdot n$.
