# Definition 18

Let $G = (V, E)$ and $H = (W, F)$. The product graph $G \times H$ has vertex set $V \times W$ and edge set: $\{((v_1, w), (v_2, w)) \mid (v_1, v_2) \in E,\, w \in W\}$ and $\{((v, w_1), (v, w_2)) \mid (w_1, w_2) \in F,\, v \in V\}$.
