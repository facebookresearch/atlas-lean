# Definition 6 (Lecture 2, Section 3.3)

A symmetric matrix $M$ is positive semidefinite (PSD) if $\forall x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $x^\top M x \geq 0$. $M$ is positive definite (PD) if the inequality is strict $\forall x \neq 0$.
