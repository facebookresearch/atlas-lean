# Lemma 3 (Disjoint Union)

The Laplacian of the disjoint union of $G$ and $H$ is the direct sum of $L_G$ and $L_H$, i.e.\ $L_{G \sqcup H} = L_G \oplus L_H = \begin{pmatrix} L_G & 0 \\ 0 & L_H \end{pmatrix}$.
