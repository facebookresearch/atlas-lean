# Theorem 1 (RV Theorem)

For distributions on vectors $y$ where $\|y\| \leq t$ and $\|\mathbf{E}[yy^\top]\|_2 \leq 1$ (where we are using the $\ell_2$ norm) then: $\mathbf{E}\!\left\|\mathbf{E}[yy^\top] - \frac{1}{q} \sum_{i=1}^{q} y_i y_i^\top \right\|_2 \leq k t \sqrt{\frac{\log q}{q}}$.
