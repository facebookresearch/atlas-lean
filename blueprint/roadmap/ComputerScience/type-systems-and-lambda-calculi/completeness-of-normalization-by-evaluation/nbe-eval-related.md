# Beta-eta convertible terms evaluate to related values in related logical environments

Fix a type $B$ of base types. Simple types over $B$ are the base types and the arrows $A \Rightarrow C$; a context $\Gamma$ is a list of simple types, $A :: \Gamma$ extends it by a newest variable of type $A$, and $\mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, A)$ is the type of intrinsically typed de Bruijn terms of type $A$ in $\Gamma$ (variables of type $A$, applications $f\,a$ with $f \in \mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, D \Rightarrow A)$ and $a \in \mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, D)$, and, when $A = D \Rightarrow E$, abstractions $\lambda t$ with body $t \in \mathrm{Tm}(D :: \Gamma, E)$).

For $t \in \mathrm{Tm}(A :: \Gamma, C)$ and $a \in \mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, A)$, $t[a]$ is $t$ under the single-variable substitution sending the newest variable to $a$ and every older variable $x$ to the variable $x$ of $\Gamma$; $f^{\uparrow}$ is $f$ renamed along the successor map from $\Gamma$ to $A :: \Gamma$, and $0$ is the newest variable.

Beta-eta conversion $t =_{\beta\eta} u$ (between terms of the same type in the same context) is the relation generated inductively by reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, the congruences ($f =_{\beta\eta} g$ and $a =_{\beta\eta} b$ give $f\,a =_{\beta\eta} g\,b$; $t =_{\beta\eta} t'$ gives $\lambda t =_{\beta\eta} \lambda t'$), the beta axiom $(\lambda t)\,a =_{\beta\eta} t[a]$ and the eta axiom $\lambda(f^{\uparrow}\,0) =_{\beta\eta} f$.

Kripke semantic values $\mathrm{Sem}(\Delta, A)$ are defined by recursion on $A$: at a base type they are the neutral terms of that type in $\Delta$, and at $A \Rightarrow C$ they are families sending each renaming $\rho$ from $\Delta$ to a context $\Theta$ and each value in $\mathrm{Sem}(\Theta, A)$ to a value in $\mathrm{Sem}(\Theta, C)$. A semantic environment $\eta$ for $\Gamma$ in $\Delta$ assigns to each variable $x$ of $\Gamma$ of type $C$ a value $\eta(x) \in \mathrm{Sem}(\Delta, C)$, and $v \cdot \eta$ is the environment for $A :: \Gamma$ sending the newest variable to $v$ and each older variable $x$ to $\eta(x)$. Evaluation $[\![t]\!]\eta \in \mathrm{Sem}(\Delta, A)$ of $t \in \mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, A)$ is $\eta(x)$ for a variable $x$, $[\![f]\!]\eta\,(\mathrm{id}, [\![a]\!]\eta)$ for $f\,a$, and for $\lambda t$ the family sending $(\rho, v)$ to $[\![t]\!](v \cdot \rho^{*}\eta)$, where $\rho^{*}\eta$ renames every value of $\eta$ along $\rho$.

Relatedness $v \sim_A w$ and logicality of semantic values are defined simultaneously by recursion on the type. At a base type, $v \sim w$ means $v = w$, and every value is logical. At $A \Rightarrow C$, $f \sim g$ means that for every renaming $\rho$ from $\Delta$ to a context $\Theta$ and all logical $a, b \in \mathrm{Sem}(\Theta, A)$ with $a \sim_A b$, $f(\rho, a) \sim_C g(\rho, b)$; and $f$ is logical when it is natural (it sends natural arguments to natural values and commutes with renaming into any further context) and $f \sim f$. An environment is logical when all its values are logical, and environments $\xi, \zeta$ for $\Gamma$ in $\Delta$ are related when $\xi(x) \sim \zeta(x)$ for every variable $x$ of $\Gamma$.

If $t =_{\beta\eta} u$ for $t, u \in \mathrm{Tm}(\Gamma, A)$, then for every context $\Delta$ and all logical semantic environments $\xi, \zeta$ for $\Gamma$ in $\Delta$ that are related, $[\![t]\!]\xi \sim_A [\![u]\!]\zeta$.
