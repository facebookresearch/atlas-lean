# Fundamental theorem of the logical relation for NbE evaluation

Let $t$ be a term of type $A$ in a context $\Gamma$. For every context $\Delta$, the evaluation of
$t$ in a [logical](logical-2.md) semantic environment for $\Gamma$ in $\Delta$ is a
[logical](logical.md) semantic value; and the evaluations of $t$ in two logical semantic
environments $\xi$ and $\zeta$ that are [related](related-2.md) are [related](related.md)
semantic values.
