# The normalized term after a substitution

Let $t$ be a term of type $A$ in a context $\Gamma$ and let $\sigma$ be a substitution from
$\Gamma$ to a context $\Delta$. The normalized term of $t$ with $\sigma$ applied is the term
underlying the reification at type $A$ of the evaluation of $t$ in the environment that sends each
variable $x$ of $\Gamma$ to the evaluation of $\sigma(x)$ in the identity semantic environment of
$\Delta$.
