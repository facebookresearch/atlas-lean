# Closed terms are strongly normalizing for call-by-value reduction

Consider call-by-value reduction $t \to u$ on intrinsically typed terms of the simply typed lambda calculus (left-to-right and weak: an application first reduces its function position, then its argument once the function is a value, and $(\lambda b)\,a$ contracts to the instantiation $b[a]$ only when $a$ is a value, a value being a lambda abstraction; no step goes under a binder). For every simple type $A$, every term $t$ of type $A$ in the empty context is strongly normalizing for call-by-value reduction (accessible for the converse relation: inductively, a term is strongly normalizing when every term it reduces to in one step is).
