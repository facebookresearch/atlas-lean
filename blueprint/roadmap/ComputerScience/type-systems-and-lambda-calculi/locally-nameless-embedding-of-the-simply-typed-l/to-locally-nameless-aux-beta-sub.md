# Translating a raw beta substitution is locally nameless opening

Raw terms over a type $B$ of base types are de Bruijn variables $\mathrm{var}\,n$ ($n \in \mathbb{N}$), applications $\mathrm{app}\,f\,a$, and abstractions $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t$ annotated with a simple type $A$. Raw simultaneous substitution applies a map $\sigma : \mathbb{N} \to$ raw terms by $\mathrm{var}\,n \mapsto \sigma(n)$, homomorphically on applications, and $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t \mapsto \mathrm{lam}\,A\,(t[\sigma^{\uparrow}])$, where $\sigma^{\uparrow}$ sends $0$ to $\mathrm{var}\,0$ and $n+1$ to $\sigma(n)$ renamed by the successor map. Locally nameless terms with natural-number names are bound variables $\mathrm{bvar}\,i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$, a de Bruijn index), free variables $\mathrm{fvar}\,x$ ($x \in \mathbb{N}$), unannotated abstractions $\mathrm{abs}\,t$ and applications $\mathrm{app}\,t\,u$. Opening $\mathrm{openRec}\,k\,u\,t$ replaces every bound variable $\mathrm{bvar}\,k$ of $t$ (with $k$ increased by one under each abstraction) by $u$, without shifting $u$; $\mathrm{open}'\,t\,u = \mathrm{openRec}\,0\,u\,t$. For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a list $\mathit{names}$ of natural numbers, the translation $\mathrm{tr}_{d,\mathit{names}}$ of raw terms to locally nameless terms sends $\mathrm{var}\,n$ to $\mathrm{bvar}\,n$ if $n < d$ and otherwise to $\mathrm{fvar}$ of the entry of $\mathit{names}$ at position $n - d$ (counting from $0$; the default $0$ when $n - d$ is at least the length of $\mathit{names}$), and is homomorphic on applications, with $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t \mapsto \mathrm{abs}\,(\mathrm{tr}_{d+1,\mathit{names}}\,t)$. For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a raw term $a$, $\beta_{d,a}$ sends $n < d$ to $\mathrm{var}\,n$, $d$ to $a$ shifted by $d$ at cutoff $0$, and $n > d$ to $\mathrm{var}\,(n-1)$.

For all raw terms $t$ and $a$, every $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and every list $\mathit{names}$,

$$\mathrm{tr}_{d,\mathit{names}}\,(t[\beta_{d,a}]) = \mathrm{openRec}\,d\,(\mathrm{tr}_{0,\mathit{names}}\,a)\,(\mathrm{tr}_{d+1,\mathit{names}}\,t).$$
