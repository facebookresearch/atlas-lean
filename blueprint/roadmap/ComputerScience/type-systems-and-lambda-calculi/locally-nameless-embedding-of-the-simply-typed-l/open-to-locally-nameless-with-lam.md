# Opening a translated lambda body prepends a fresh name

Locally nameless terms with natural-number names are bound variables $\mathrm{bvar}\,i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$, a de Bruijn index), free variables $\mathrm{fvar}\,x$ ($x \in \mathbb{N}$), unannotated abstractions $\mathrm{abs}\,t$ and applications $\mathrm{app}\,t\,u$. Opening $\mathrm{openRec}\,k\,u\,t$ replaces every bound variable $\mathrm{bvar}\,k$ of $t$ (with $k$ increased by one under each abstraction) by $u$, without shifting $u$; $\mathrm{open}'\,t\,u = \mathrm{openRec}\,0\,u\,t$. For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a list $\mathit{names}$ of natural numbers, the translation $\mathrm{tr}_{d,\mathit{names}}$ of raw terms to locally nameless terms sends $\mathrm{var}\,n$ to $\mathrm{bvar}\,n$ if $n < d$ and otherwise to $\mathrm{fvar}$ of the entry of $\mathit{names}$ at position $n - d$ (counting from $0$; the default $0$ when $n - d$ is at least the length of $\mathit{names}$), and is homomorphic on applications, with $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t \mapsto \mathrm{abs}\,(\mathrm{tr}_{d+1,\mathit{names}}\,t)$. The embedding with names $\mathit{names}$ of an intrinsically typed term $t$ is $\mathrm{tr}_{0,\mathit{names}}$ applied to the erasure of $t$ (variables replaced by their de Bruijn positions in the context, lambdas keeping their parameter types); the canonical embedding $t^{\mathrm{LN}}$ uses $\mathit{names} = [0, 1, \dots, |\Gamma| - 1]$ for a term in context $\Gamma$.

For every list $\mathit{names}$, every term $b$ of type $B$ in $A :: \Gamma$ and every $x \in \mathbb{N}$, opening $\mathrm{tr}_{1,\mathit{names}}$ of the erasure of $b$ (the body of the embedding of $\lambda b$) with $\mathrm{fvar}\,x$ gives the embedding of $b$ with names $x :: \mathit{names}$.
