# Locally nameless embedding of intrinsic terms with chosen names

Raw terms over a type $B$ of base types are de Bruijn variables $\mathrm{var}\,n$ ($n \in \mathbb{N}$), applications $\mathrm{app}\,f\,a$, and abstractions $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t$ annotated with a simple type $A$. The erasure of an intrinsically typed term (a term of type $A$ in a context $\Gamma$, a list of simple types) is the raw term obtained by replacing each variable by its de Bruijn index (its position in $\Gamma$, counting from $0$ at the front), keeping applications, and keeping each lambda's parameter type as annotation. Locally nameless terms with natural-number names are bound variables $\mathrm{bvar}\,i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$, a de Bruijn index), free variables $\mathrm{fvar}\,x$ ($x \in \mathbb{N}$), unannotated abstractions $\mathrm{abs}\,t$ and applications $\mathrm{app}\,t\,u$. For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a list $\mathit{names}$ of natural numbers, the translation $\mathrm{tr}_{d,\mathit{names}}$ of raw terms to locally nameless terms sends $\mathrm{var}\,n$ to $\mathrm{bvar}\,n$ if $n < d$ and otherwise to $\mathrm{fvar}$ of the entry of $\mathit{names}$ at position $n - d$ (counting from $0$; the default $0$ when $n - d$ is at least the length of $\mathit{names}$), and is homomorphic on applications, with $\mathrm{lam}\,A\,t \mapsto \mathrm{abs}\,(\mathrm{tr}_{d+1,\mathit{names}}\,t)$.

For a list $\mathit{names}$ of natural numbers and a term $t$ of type $A$ in context $\Gamma$, the embedding of $t$ with names $\mathit{names}$ is $\mathrm{tr}_{0,\mathit{names}}$ applied to the erasure of $t$. A free source variable at position $j$ of $\Gamma$ becomes $\mathrm{fvar}$ of the entry of $\mathit{names}$ at position $j$ (or $\mathrm{fvar}\,0$ when $\mathit{names}$ is too short), and variables bound inside $t$ become bound variables with the same de Bruijn index.
