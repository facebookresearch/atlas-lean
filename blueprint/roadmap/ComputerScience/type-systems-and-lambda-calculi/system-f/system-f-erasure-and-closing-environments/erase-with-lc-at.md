# Erasure with locally closed images is locally closed

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $\sigma$ an environment assigning to each $i < m$ an untyped term all of whose bound indices are below $k$ (counting enclosing abstractions as in local closure at level $k$), and $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,m}$ a System F term. Then the [erasure](erase-with.md) $|t|_\sigma$ is locally closed at level $k$.
