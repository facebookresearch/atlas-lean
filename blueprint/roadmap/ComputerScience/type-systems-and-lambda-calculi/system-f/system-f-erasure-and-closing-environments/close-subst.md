# Closing commutes with System F term substitution

For every term substitution $\sigma$ from $m$ to $k$ term variables, environment $\eta$ of model terms for $k$ variables and System F term $t$, the [closing](close.md) of the [substitution](../scoped-pure-system-f/subst-2.md) $t[\sigma]$ under $\eta$ equals the closing of $t$ under the environment $i \mapsto$ (closing of $\sigma(i)$ under $\eta$).
