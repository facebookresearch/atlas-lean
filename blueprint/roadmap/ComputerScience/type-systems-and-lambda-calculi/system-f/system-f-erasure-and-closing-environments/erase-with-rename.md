# Erasure of a term-variable renaming

For every renaming $\eta : \{0, \dots, m - 1\} \to \{0, \dots, k - 1\}$, environment $\sigma$ of untyped terms for $k$ variables and System F term $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,m}$, the [erasure](erase-with.md) under $\sigma$ of the [term-variable renaming](../scoped-pure-system-f/rename-2.md) of $t$ by $\eta$ equals the erasure of $t$ under $\sigma \circ \eta$.
