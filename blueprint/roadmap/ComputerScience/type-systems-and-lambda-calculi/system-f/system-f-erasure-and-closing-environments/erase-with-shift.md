# Shifting commutes with erasure under an environment

For every cutoff $c$, environment $\sigma$ of untyped terms and System F term $t$, the [shift](shift-at.md) $\uparrow_c |t|_\sigma$ of the [erasure](erase-with.md) equals the erasure of $t$ under the environment $i \mapsto \uparrow_c \sigma(i)$.
