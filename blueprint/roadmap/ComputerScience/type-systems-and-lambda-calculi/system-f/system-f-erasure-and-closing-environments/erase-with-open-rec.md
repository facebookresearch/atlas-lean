# Opening commutes with erasure under an environment

Let $u$ be a locally closed untyped term, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $\sigma$ an environment of untyped terms for $m$ term variables and $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,m}$. Opening the bound index $k$ with $u$ in the [erasure](erase-with.md) $|t|_\sigma$ gives the erasure of $t$ under the environment $i \mapsto \{k \to u\}\sigma(i)$ of opened images.
