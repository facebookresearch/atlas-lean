# Beta conversion of a closed System F lambda application

For every environment $\sigma$ of model terms for $m$ term variables, type $A$, System F term $b \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,m+1}$ and model term $a$, the application of the [closing](close.md) of $\lambda A.\,b$ to $a$ is [β-equivalent](../the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/beta-eq.md) to the closing of $b$ under the environment sending $0$ to $a$ and $i + 1$ to $\sigma(i)$.
