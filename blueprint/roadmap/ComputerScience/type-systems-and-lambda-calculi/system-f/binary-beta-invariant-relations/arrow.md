# Function-space relation of beta-invariant relations

For [β-invariant relations](rel.md) $A$ and $B$, the relation $A \to B$ relates terms $f$ and $g$ of the untyped term model when for all terms $x$, $y$ with $A(x, y)$ we have $B(f\,x, g\,y)$: related functions send related inputs to related outputs. It is β-invariant.
