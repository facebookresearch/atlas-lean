# Polymorphic identity inhabitants preserve admissible relations

Let $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{0,0}$ be a closed term of the [polymorphic identity type](poly-id-type.md) in the empty context, $A, B \in \mathrm{Ty}_0$ types and $R$ a [β-invariant relation](../binary-beta-invariant-relations/rel.md). If $R(x, y)$ for terms $x$, $y$ of the untyped term model, then $R$ relates the erasure of $t\,[A]$ applied to $x$ and the erasure of $t\,[B]$ applied to $y$.
