# Type-beta conversion preserves the relational interpretation

For $A, B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\Delta}$ with $A \equiv_\beta B$ ([type
β-conversion](../proved-type-beta-conversion-for-pure-f/conv.md)) and every environment $\rho$ of
kinded relations for $\Delta$, $[\![A]\!]_\rho = [\![B]\!]_\rho$ ([relational
interpretation](interpret.md)).
