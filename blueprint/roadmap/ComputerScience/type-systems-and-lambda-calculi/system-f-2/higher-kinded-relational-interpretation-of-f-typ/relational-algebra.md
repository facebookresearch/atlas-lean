# Kind algebra of beta-invariant relations

The relational kind algebra is the [kind
algebra](../semantic-kind-interpretation-and-substitution/kind-algebra.md) on the set $\mathcal{R}$
of [β-invariant relations](../../system-f/binary-beta-invariant-relations/rel.md) on the [untyped
term model](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/model.md) whose arrow
operation is the [relational arrow](../../system-f/binary-beta-invariant-relations/arrow.md) $R \to
R'$ (related functions send related arguments to related results) and whose universal operation at
kind $\kappa$ sends a function $\Phi : [\![\kappa]\!]_{\mathcal{R}} \to \mathcal{R}$ to the
[intersection](../../system-f/binary-beta-invariant-relations/universal.md) $\forall_{a} \Phi(a)$
over all $a \in [\![\kappa]\!]_{\mathcal{R}}$, where $[\![\kappa]\!]_{\mathcal{R}}$ is the
[interpretation of $\kappa$](../semantic-kind-interpretation-and-substitution/sem.md) over
$\mathcal{R}$ (relations at kind $\star$, functions between such interpretations at higher kinds).
