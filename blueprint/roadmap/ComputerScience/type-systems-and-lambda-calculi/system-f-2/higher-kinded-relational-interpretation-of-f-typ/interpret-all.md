# Relational interpretation of an Fω universal type

For $B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\kappa \cdot \Delta}$, every environment $\rho$ of kinded relations
for $\Delta$ and all terms $f$, $g$ of the untyped term model: $[\![\forall_\kappa B]\!]_\rho(f, g)$
holds if and only if $[\![B]\!]_{R \cdot \rho}(f, g)$ holds for every $R \in
[\![\kappa]\!]_{\mathcal{R}}$ ([relational interpretation](interpret.md); $R \cdot \rho$ is the
[extended environment](../semantic-kind-interpretation-and-substitution/extend.md)).
