# Relational interpretation of a weakened Fω type

For $A \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\Delta}$, an environment $\rho$ of kinded relations for $\Delta$
and $R \in [\![\mu]\!]_{\mathcal{R}}$, $[\![A\langle\uparrow\rangle]\!]_{R \cdot \rho} =
[\![A]\!]_\rho$, where $\uparrow : \Delta \to \mu \cdot \Delta$ is the successor renaming: a
weakened type does not depend on the new variable ([relational interpretation](interpret.md)).
