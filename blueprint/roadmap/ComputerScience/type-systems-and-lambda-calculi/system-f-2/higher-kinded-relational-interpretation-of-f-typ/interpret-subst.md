# Relational interpretation of a substituted Fω type

For a [type operator](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/ty.md) $A \in
\mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\Delta}$, a [kind-preserving
substitution](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/sub.md) $\sigma : \Delta \to
\mathrm{E}$ and an environment $\rho$ of kinded relations for $\mathrm{E}$, $[\![A[\sigma]]\!]_\rho
= [\![A]\!]_{\rho'}$ with $\rho'(\alpha_i) = [\![\sigma(\alpha_i)]\!]_\rho$ ([relational
interpretation](interpret.md)).
