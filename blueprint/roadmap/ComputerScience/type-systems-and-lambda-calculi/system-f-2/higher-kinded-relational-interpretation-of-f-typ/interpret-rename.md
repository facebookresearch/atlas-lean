# Relational interpretation of a renamed Fω type

For a [type operator](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/ty.md) $A \in
\mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\Delta}$, a [kind-preserving
renaming](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/kren.md) $\eta : \Delta \to \mathrm{E}$
and an environment $\rho$ of kinded relations for $\mathrm{E}$, $[\![A\langle\eta\rangle]\!]_\rho =
[\![A]\!]_{\rho \circ \eta}$ ([relational interpretation](interpret.md)).
