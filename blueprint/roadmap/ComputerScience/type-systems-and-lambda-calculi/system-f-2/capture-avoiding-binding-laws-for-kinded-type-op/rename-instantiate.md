# Renaming commutes with type-binder instantiation

For a [kind-preserving renaming](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/kren.md) $\rho :
\Delta \to \mathrm{E}$, $B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\mu \cdot \Delta}$ and $A \in
\mathrm{Ty}^{\mu}_{\Delta}$, $(B[A])\langle\rho\rangle =
(B\langle\rho^{\uparrow}\rangle)[A\langle\rho\rangle]$, with the [lifted
renaming](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/lift.md) $\rho^{\uparrow}$ and
[instantiation](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/instantiate.md).
