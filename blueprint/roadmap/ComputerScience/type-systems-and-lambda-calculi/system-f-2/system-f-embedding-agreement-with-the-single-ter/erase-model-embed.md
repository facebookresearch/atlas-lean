# Embedding preserves model erasure

For every System F term $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,0}$ without term variables, the [erasure to the term
model](../f-closing-instances-in-the-single-existing-term/erase-model.md) of its
[embedding](../the-star-forall-system-f-fragment-inside-pure-f/embed-2.md) $\mathcal{E}(t)$ equals
the [System F erasure](../../system-f/system-f-erasure-and-closing-environments/erase-model.md) of
$t$.
