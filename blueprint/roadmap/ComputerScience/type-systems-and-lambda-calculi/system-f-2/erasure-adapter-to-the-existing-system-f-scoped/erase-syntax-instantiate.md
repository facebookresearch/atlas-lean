# Fω erasure commutes with term-binder instantiation

For $b \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m+1}$ and $a \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$, $|b[a]| = |b|[|a|]$, with
the [Fω instantiation](../scoped-pure-f-terms/instantiate.md) on the left, the [System F
instantiation](../../system-f/scoped-pure-system-f/instantiate-2.md) on the right and $|\cdot|$ the
[erasure](erase-syntax.md).
