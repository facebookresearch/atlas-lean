# Fω erasure commutes with term substitution

For every term substitution $\sigma$ assigning to each $i < m$ an Fω term $\sigma(i) \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,k}$ and every [Fω term](../scoped-pure-f-terms/tm.md) $t \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$, $|t[\sigma]| = |t|[|\sigma|]$, where $|\sigma|(i) = |\sigma(i)|$, the left
substitution is the [Fω term substitution](../scoped-pure-f-terms/subst.md), the right one the
[System F term substitution](../../system-f/scoped-pure-system-f/subst-2.md), and $|\cdot|$ the
[erasure](erase-syntax.md).
