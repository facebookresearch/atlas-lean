# Fω erasure commutes with term-variable renaming

For every map $\rho : \{0, \dots, m-1\} \to \{0, \dots, k-1\}$ and every [Fω
term](../scoped-pure-f-terms/tm.md) $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$, $|t\langle\rho\rangle| =
|t|\langle\rho\rangle$, with the [Fω term renaming](../scoped-pure-f-terms/rename.md) on the left,
the [System F term renaming](../../system-f/scoped-pure-system-f/rename-2.md) on the right and
$|\cdot|$ the [erasure](erase-syntax.md).
