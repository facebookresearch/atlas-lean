# Conversion preserves the kind of a universal binder

Let $\mu$, $\nu$ be kinds, $B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\mu \cdot \Delta}$ and $B' \in
\mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\nu \cdot \Delta}$. If $\forall_\mu B \equiv_\beta \forall_\nu B'$ ([type
β-conversion](../proved-type-beta-conversion-for-pure-f/conv.md)) then $\mu = \nu$.
