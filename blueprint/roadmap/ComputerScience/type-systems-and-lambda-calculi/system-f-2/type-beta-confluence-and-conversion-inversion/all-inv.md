# Reducts of quantified types are quantified types

For $B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\mu \cdot \Delta}$ and $C \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\Delta}$, if
$\forall_\mu B \Rrightarrow^* C$ ([multistep reduction](red.md)) then $C = \forall_\mu B'$ for some
$B' \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\mu \cdot \Delta}$ with $B \Rrightarrow^* B'$.
