# Progress for closed Fω terms

Let $\Gamma$ be a term context with no term variables over a kinding context $\Delta$, $t \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,0}$ and $A \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\Delta}$ with $\Gamma \vdash t : A$
([typing](../pure-f-typing-with-proved-type-beta-conversion/typing.md)). Then $t$ is a
[value](value.md) or $t \to t'$ for some $t'$ ([weak call-by-value step](step.md)).
