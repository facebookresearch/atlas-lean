# Canonical forms for Fω values of quantified type

Let $\Gamma$ be a term context, $\kappa$ a kind, $C \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\kappa \cdot \Delta}$
and $t$ a [value](value.md) with $\Gamma \vdash t : \forall_\kappa C$
([typing](../pure-f-typing-with-proved-type-beta-conversion/typing.md)). Then $t = \Lambda_\kappa b$
for some $b \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\kappa \cdot \Delta,m}$.
