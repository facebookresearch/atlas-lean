# Extension of a kinded semantic environment

Let $X$ be a set, $\eta$ a [semantic environment](kenv.md) for $\Delta$ over $X$, $\kappa$ a kind
and $a \in [\![\kappa]\!]_X$. The extended environment $a \cdot \eta$ for $\kappa \cdot \Delta$
sends $\alpha_0$ to $a$ and $\alpha_{i+1}$ to $\eta(\alpha_i)$.
