# Kinded semantic environment

Let $X$ be a set and $\Delta$ a kinding context. A semantic environment $\eta$ for $\Delta$ over $X$
assigns to every [type variable](../intrinsically-kinded-type-operators-for-pure-f/kvar.md)
$\alpha_i$ of kind $\kappa$ in $\Delta$ an element $\eta(\alpha_i) \in [\![\kappa]\!]_X$ of the
[interpretation](sem.md) of its kind.
