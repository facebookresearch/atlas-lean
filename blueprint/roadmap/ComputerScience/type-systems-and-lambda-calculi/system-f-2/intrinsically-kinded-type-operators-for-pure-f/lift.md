# Lifting a renaming under a type binder

For a [kind-preserving renaming](kren.md) $\rho : \Delta \to \mathrm{E}$ and a kind $\mu$, the
lifted renaming $\rho^{\uparrow} : \mu \cdot \Delta \to \mu \cdot \mathrm{E}$ sends $\alpha_0$ to
$\alpha_0$ and $\alpha_{i+1}$ to $\alpha_{j+1}$ where $\alpha_j = \rho(\alpha_i)$.
