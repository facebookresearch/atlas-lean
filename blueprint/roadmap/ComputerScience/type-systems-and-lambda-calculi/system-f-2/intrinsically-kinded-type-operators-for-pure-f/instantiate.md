# Instantiation of an Fω type binder

For $B \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\mu}_{\kappa \cdot \Delta}$ and $A \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\kappa}_{\Delta}$, the
instantiation $B[A] \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\mu}_{\Delta}$ is the [substitution](subst.md) of $B$ by the
[single substitution](single.md) that replaces $\alpha_0$ by $A$ and lowers every other variable by
one.
