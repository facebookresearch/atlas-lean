# Erasure of multistep Fω evaluation is a beta conversion

Let $t, t' \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$ such that $t'$ is reachable from $t$ by finitely many
(possibly zero) [weak call-by-value
steps](../conversion-aware-weak-call-by-value-safety-for-p/step.md). For every assignment $\sigma$
of terms of the untyped term model to the $m$ term variables, the
[closings](../f-closing-instances-in-the-single-existing-term/close.md) $t\{\sigma\}$ and
$t'\{\sigma\}$ are
[β-equivalent](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/beta-eq.md).
