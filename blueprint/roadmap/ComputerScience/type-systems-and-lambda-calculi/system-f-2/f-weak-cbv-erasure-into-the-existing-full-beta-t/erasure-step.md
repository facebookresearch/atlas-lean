# Erasure of an Fω evaluation step is a beta conversion

Let $t, t' \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$ with $t \to t'$ ([weak call-by-value
step](../conversion-aware-weak-call-by-value-safety-for-p/step.md)). For every assignment $\sigma$
of terms of the [untyped term
model](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/model.md) to the $m$ term
variables, the [closings](../f-closing-instances-in-the-single-existing-term/close.md) $t\{\sigma\}$
and $t'\{\sigma\}$ are
[β-equivalent](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/beta-eq.md).
