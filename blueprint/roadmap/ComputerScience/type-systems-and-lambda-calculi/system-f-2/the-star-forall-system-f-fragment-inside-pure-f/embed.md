# Embedding of System F types into Fω

The embedding $\mathcal{E}(A) \in \mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\star^n}$ of a System F type $A \in
\mathrm{Ty}_n$ is its [translation](from-system-f.md) $\mathcal{T}_\iota(A)$, where $\star^n$ is the
kinding context of $n$ copies of $\star$ and $\iota(i) = \alpha_i$ ([star-kinded
variables](of-fin.md)).
