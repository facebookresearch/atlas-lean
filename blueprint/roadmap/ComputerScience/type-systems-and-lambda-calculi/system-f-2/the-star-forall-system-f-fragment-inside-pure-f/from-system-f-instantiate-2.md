# Term translation commutes with term-binder instantiation

For every map $\varphi$ from $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ to $\mathrm{Ty}^{\star}_{\Delta}$, $b \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{n,m+1}$ and $a \in \mathrm{Tm}_{n,m}$, $\mathcal{T}_\varphi(b[a]) =
\mathcal{T}_\varphi(b)[\mathcal{T}_\varphi(a)]$ ([term translation](from-system-f-2.md), [Fω
instantiation](../scoped-pure-f-terms/instantiate.md)).
