# Closing commutes with Fω term substitution

For every term substitution $\sigma$ assigning to each $i < m$ an Fω term $\sigma(i) \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,k}$, environment $\eta$ of model terms for $k$ variables and $t \in
\mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$, the [closing](close.md) of the
[substitution](../scoped-pure-f-terms/subst.md) $t[\sigma]$ under $\eta$ equals the closing of $t$
under the environment $i \mapsto \sigma(i)\{\eta\}$.
