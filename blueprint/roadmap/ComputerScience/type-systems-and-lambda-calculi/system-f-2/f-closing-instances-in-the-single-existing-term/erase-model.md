# Erasure of closed Fω terms to the term model

For an [Fω term](../scoped-pure-f-terms/tm.md) $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,0}$ with no term
variables, its erasure to the [untyped term
model](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/model.md) is its
[closing](close.md) under the empty environment.
