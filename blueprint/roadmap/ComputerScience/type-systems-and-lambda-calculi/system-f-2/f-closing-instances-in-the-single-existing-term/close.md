# Closing Fω terms in the System F term model

For an environment $\sigma$ assigning a term $\sigma(i)$ of the [untyped term
model](../../system-f/the-existing-untyped-beta-conversion-term-model/model.md) to each $i < m$ and
an [Fω term](../scoped-pure-f-terms/tm.md) $t \in \mathrm{Tm}_{\Delta,m}$, the closing $t\{\sigma\}$
is the [System F closing](../../system-f/system-f-erasure-and-closing-environments/close.md)
$|t|\{\sigma\}$ of its [erasure](../erasure-adapter-to-the-existing-system-f-scoped/erase-syntax.md)
$|t|$ to System F terms.
