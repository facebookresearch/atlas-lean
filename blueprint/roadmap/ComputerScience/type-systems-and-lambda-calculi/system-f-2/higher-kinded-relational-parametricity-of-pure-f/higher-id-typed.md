# Typing of the higher-kinded identity

For every term context $\Gamma$ with $m$ variables over a kinding context $\Delta$, the
[higher-kinded identity](higher-id.md) $\Lambda_{\star \Rightarrow \star}\,\Lambda_\star\,
\lambda(\alpha_1\,\alpha_0).\,x_0$ has the [higher-kinded identity type](higher-id-type.md)
$\forall_{\star \Rightarrow \star}\,\forall_\star\,(\alpha_1\,\alpha_0 \to \alpha_1\,\alpha_0)$ in
$\Gamma$ ([typing](../pure-f-typing-with-proved-type-beta-conversion/typing.md)).
