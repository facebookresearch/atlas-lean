# Typed weak head reduction in λΠUN

In [λΠUN](the-type-theory-lambda-pi-u-n.md), the judgement $\Gamma \vdash t \longrightarrow u : A$ performs one weak head step and is generated by
$(\lambda t)\, a \longrightarrow t[a] : G[a]$, $\mathsf{natrec}\, F\, z\, s\, \mathsf{zero} \longrightarrow z$, $\mathsf{natrec}\, F\, z\, s\, (\mathsf{suc}\, t) \longrightarrow (s\, t)\, (\mathsf{natrec}\, F\, z\, s\, t)$ (each under the typing premises of the
corresponding computation rule), reduction of the function in an application ($\Gamma \vdash f\, a \longrightarrow g\, a : G[a]$ if $\Gamma \vdash f \longrightarrow g : \Pi\, F\, G$ and $\Gamma \vdash a : F$) and of the
scrutinee of $\mathsf{natrec}$ ($\Gamma \vdash \mathsf{natrec}\, F\, z\, s\, t \longrightarrow \mathsf{natrec}\, F\, z\, s\, u : F[t]$ if $\Gamma \vdash t \longrightarrow u : \mathsf{N}$, with the premises on $F$, $z$ and $s$
of the computation rules), and conversion along type equality;
$\Gamma \vdash A \longrightarrow B$ is reduction of a type as a term of type $\mathsf{U}$, and $\longrightarrow^*$ is the reflexive–transitive closure, starting from a well-formed type or a well-typed term. Since reduction is typed, it is contained in
conversion: $\Gamma \vdash t \longrightarrow^* u : A$ implies $\Gamma \vdash t = u : A$, and likewise for types. Weak head normal forms do not reduce, and reduction is deterministic: a type or term has at
most one one-step reduct, and two reducts of it to weak head normal forms are syntactically equal.
