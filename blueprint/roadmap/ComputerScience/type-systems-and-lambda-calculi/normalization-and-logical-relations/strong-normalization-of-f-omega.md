# Strong normalization of F-omega

Every well-typed term of System $F_\omega$ is strongly normalizing: there is no infinite sequence of term-level $\beta$-reductions (including reductions inside type annotations) starting from it. The proof extends Girard's reducibility candidates with a kind-indexed interpretation of type constructors.
