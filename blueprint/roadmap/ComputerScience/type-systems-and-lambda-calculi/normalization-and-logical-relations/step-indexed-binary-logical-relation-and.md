# Step-indexed binary logical relation and contextual approximation

For the simply typed lambda calculus with iso-recursive types, the step-indexed binary logical relation $\mathcal{E}_k[A]$ on pairs of terms is compatible with all term constructors and adequate (if two closed terms of base type are related at every index and the first terminates, so does the second, with the same result). Hence logically related terms are contextually approximate; this is the soundness half of Ahmed's characterization.
