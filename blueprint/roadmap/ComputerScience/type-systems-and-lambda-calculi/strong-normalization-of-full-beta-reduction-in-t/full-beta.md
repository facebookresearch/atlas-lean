# Full beta reduction on intrinsically typed terms

In the intrinsically typed simply typed lambda calculus, full beta reduction $t \to_\beta u$ relates two terms of the same type in the same context; it is the least such family of relations closed under four rules: $(\lambda b)\,a \to_\beta b[a]$ at type $B$ in $\Gamma$, for every body $b$ of type $B$ in $A :: \Gamma$ and every argument $a$ of type $A$ in $\Gamma$ (no value restriction), where $b[a]$ replaces the newest variable of $b$ by $a$; if $f \to_\beta f'$ then $f\,a \to_\beta f'\,a$; if $a \to_\beta a'$ then $f\,a \to_\beta f\,a'$ (for any $f$); and if $b \to_\beta b'$ then $\lambda b \to_\beta \lambda b'$. Unlike call-by-value reduction, it contracts any redex, including under lambdas.
