# The canonical locally nameless embedding simulates full beta steps

The embedding with names $\mathit{names}$ of an intrinsically typed term $t$ is $\mathrm{tr}_{0,\mathit{names}}$ applied to the erasure of $t$ (variables replaced by their de Bruijn positions in the context, lambdas keeping their parameter types); the canonical embedding $t^{\mathrm{LN}}$ uses $\mathit{names} = [0, 1, \dots, |\Gamma| - 1]$ for a term in context $\Gamma$. Full beta reduction on locally nameless terms is the compatible closure of beta contraction $\mathrm{app}\,(\mathrm{abs}\,M)\,N \to \mathrm{open}'\,M\,N$ (with $\mathrm{abs}\,M$ and $N$ locally closed), closed under reduction in either position of an application whose other side is locally closed and under abstraction (cofinitely many opened bodies reduce). Intrinsic full beta reduction contracts any redex $(\lambda b)\,a \to b[a]$ (no value restriction) and is closed under both application positions and under lambdas.

For terms $t$ and $u$ of type $A$ in $\Gamma$, if $t \to u$ in one intrinsic full beta step, then $t^{\mathrm{LN}} \to u^{\mathrm{LN}}$ in exactly one locally nameless full beta step.
