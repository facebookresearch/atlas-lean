# Strong normalization of System T is not provable in PA

Expressed in the language of arithmetic through a Gödel numbering of terms and reduction sequences (for every term there is a bound on the
lengths of all its reduction sequences), the [strong normalization of System T](strong-normalization-of-system-t.md) is not provable in Peano arithmetic, provided PA is
consistent.

Otherwise the whole of Gödel's Dialectica consistency proof for Heyting arithmetic, and through Kreisel's theorem for PA, could be formalized in PA, so
PA would prove its own consistency, contradicting Gödel's second incompleteness theorem.
