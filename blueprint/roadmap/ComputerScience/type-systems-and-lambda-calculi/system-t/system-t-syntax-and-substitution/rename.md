# Renaming of System T terms

Given a renaming $\rho$ from a context $\Gamma$ to a context
$\Delta$, the renaming along $\rho$ of a term of type $A$ in context $\Gamma$ is the term of type
$A$ in context $\Delta$ obtained by replacing each variable $x$ by $\rho(x)$. Under a
$\lambda$-binder, $\rho$ is replaced by its lift. Renaming leaves zero unchanged and commutes with
application, successor and the recursor.
