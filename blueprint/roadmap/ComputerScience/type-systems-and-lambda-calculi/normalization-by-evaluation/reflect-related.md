# Reflection realizes the denotation of a neutral term

Fix an interpretation $I$ of the base types. For every type $A$, every environment $\eta$ for a
context $\Gamma$ in $I$ and every neutral term $n$ of type $A$ in $\Gamma$, the reflection of $n$
at type $A$ realizes, at $\eta$, the denotation at $\eta$ of the term underlying $n$.
