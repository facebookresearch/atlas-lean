# Extended polynomials

The *extended polynomials* form the smallest class of functions $\mathbb{N}^m \to \mathbb{N}$ that contains

- the projections $U^m_i(n_1, \dots, n_m) = n_i$ for $1 \le i \le m$,
- the constant functions $n \mapsto k$,
- the signum function $\mathrm{sg}$ with $\mathrm{sg}(0) = 0$ and $\mathrm{sg}(m + 1) = 1$,

and is closed under addition and multiplication on disjoint arguments: if $f : \mathbb{N}^k \to \mathbb{N}$ and $g : \mathbb{N}^l \to \mathbb{N}$ are extended polynomials, so are
$(f + g)(\vec n, \vec m) = f(\vec n) + g(\vec m)$ and $(f \cdot g)(\vec n, \vec m) = f(\vec n) \cdot g(\vec m)$ on $\mathbb{N}^{k + l}$.
