# Receiver-pessimality

Let $n$ proposers and $n$ receivers have strict preferences given by rankings. For every stable
perfect matching $\nu$ and every receiver $w$, $w$ does not strictly prefer her partner in the
Gale-Shapley matching to her partner in $\nu$.
