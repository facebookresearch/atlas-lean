# Augmentation adds exactly the amount on the steps of a duplicate-free path

If the list $p$ has no repeated entry and $(u, v)$ is a pair of consecutive entries of $p$, then $\Delta_p(u, v) = 1$: augmenting along $p$ by $\varepsilon$ raises the skew flow on $(u, v)$ by exactly $\varepsilon$.

## Informally uses

- [Augmentation does not touch pairs at vertices off the path](edge-delta-eq-zero-of-not-mem.md): the steps after the first one never meet the first vertex again.
