# Augmentation decreases a skew flow only against the path

Augmenting a skew flow along a vertex list $p$ by an amount $\varepsilon$ adds $\varepsilon \cdot \Delta_p(u, v)$ to the flow on the ordered pair $(u, v)$, where $\Delta_p(u, v)$ counts the steps $(u, v)$ of $p$ minus the steps $(v, u)$ of $p$. If $(v, u)$ is not a step of $p$, then $\Delta_p(u, v) \ge 0$.
