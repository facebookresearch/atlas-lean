# The bottleneck of a path is attained

On a list $p$ with at least two entries, some pair $(u, v)$ of consecutive entries has residual capacity $c(u, v) - f(u, v)$ equal to the bottleneck of $p$, the minimum of these residual capacities. Such a pair is a *bottleneck arc* of $p$ in the terminology of Edmonds and Karp.
