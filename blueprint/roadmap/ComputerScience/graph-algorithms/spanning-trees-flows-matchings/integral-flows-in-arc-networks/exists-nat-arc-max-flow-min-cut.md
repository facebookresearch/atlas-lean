# Exists nat arc max flow min cut

Integral arc max-flow min-cut: for an arc network with finite vertex and arc sets, a natural capacity on every arc and $s \ne t$, there is a natural arc flow that is arc-feasible, maximizes the signed source value over all real arc-feasible flows, is bounded by every $s$-$t$ out-cut, and attains some $s$-$t$ out-cut. Finiteness matters: infinitely many parallel unit-capacity arcs from $s$ to $t$ admit feasible flows of arbitrarily large finite value.
