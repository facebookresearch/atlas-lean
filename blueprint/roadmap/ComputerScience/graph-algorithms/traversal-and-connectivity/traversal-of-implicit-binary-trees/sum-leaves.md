# Additive weight of the root is the sum over the frontier

Let a branching step on states and a weight $w$ from states to natural numbers satisfy
$w(s) = w(l) + w(h)$ whenever $s$ branches into $(l, h)$. Then for every depth $k$ and state $s$,
$w(s)$ equals the sum of $w(x)$ over the occurrences $x$ of the frontier of $s$ cut at depth $k$,
even when that frontier still contains branching states.
