# Frontier occurrences satisfy the resource invariant

Let $q \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $G(s, p, t)$ be a relation between states and natural numbers such
that if $G(s, p, t)$ and $s$ branches into $(l, h)$, then $G(l, p + 1, t)$ and
$G(h, p + 1, t + q)$. If $G(s, p, t)$ holds, then for every depth $k$ and every state $x$ in the
frontier of $s$ cut at depth $k$ there are $p', t'$ with $G(x, p', t')$.
