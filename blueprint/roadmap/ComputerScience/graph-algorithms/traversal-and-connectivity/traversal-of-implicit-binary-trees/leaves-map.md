# Commuting maps transport the ordered frontier

Let $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ be branching steps on states $S_1$ and $S_2$, and $f : S_1 \to S_2$
a map commuting with them ($f(x)$ is terminal exactly when $x$ is, and if $x$ branches into
$(l, h)$ then $f(x)$ branches into $(f(l), f(h))$). Then for every depth $d$ and every $x \in S_1$,
the frontier of $f(x)$ for $\sigma_2$ cut at depth $d$ is the list of images under $f$ of the
frontier of $x$ for $\sigma_1$ cut at depth $d$, in the same order and with the same
multiplicities.
