# Counting rounds project list-output rounds

For a branching step, a weight $w$ from states to natural numbers, a number $i$ of rounds and lists
$R$ and $O$ of states: applying $i$ weight-counting rounds to the pair ($R$, sum of $w$ over $O$)
gives the pair whose stack is the stack obtained by $i$ depth-first rounds from $(R, O)$ and whose
count is the sum of $w$ over the output obtained by those $i$ rounds.
