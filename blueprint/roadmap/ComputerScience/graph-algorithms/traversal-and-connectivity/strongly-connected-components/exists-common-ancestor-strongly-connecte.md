# Common ancestor of strongly connected vertices

Under the [numbering properties](is-dfsnumbering.md), two vertices $v$ and $w$ of the same strongly
connected component have a common ancestor in the depth-first spanning forest that belongs to
the component: the first vertex of the component.

## Sources

- R. E. Tarjan, *Depth-first search and linear graph algorithms*, SIAM J. Comput. 1(2) (1972)
  146–160, §4, Lemma 10 (first part: $v$ and $w$ have a common ancestor in $F$).
