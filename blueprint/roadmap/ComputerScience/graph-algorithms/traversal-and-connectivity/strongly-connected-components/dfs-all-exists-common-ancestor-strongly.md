# Strongly connected vertices have a common ancestor in their component (depth-first search)

In the [depth-first search of the whole graph](../depth-first-search/dfs-all.md) of a directed graph, two strongly connected
vertices have
a common ancestor in the depth-first spanning forest that lies in their strongly connected
component.
