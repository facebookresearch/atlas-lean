# Inverse of an acyclic structural-equation coefficient matrix

Let `B` be a square matrix over a commutative ring, indexed by the vertices of a finite acyclic directed graph, whose nonzero entries `B i j` lie on edges `j → i` (the column vertex causes the row vertex). Then `(1 - B)⁻¹ = ∑_{k < |V|} B ^ k`.
