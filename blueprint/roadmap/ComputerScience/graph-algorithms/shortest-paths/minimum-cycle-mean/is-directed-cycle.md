# Directed cycle in a weighted edge list

For a list of weighted edge occurrences on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, a directed cycle through $v$ is a nonempty walk from $v$ back to $v$ whose vertices, with $v$ counted once, are pairwise distinct. A loop at $v$ is a cycle with one edge.

## Sources

- R. M. Karp, "A characterization of the minimum cycle mean in a digraph", *Discrete Mathematics* 23(3) (1978), 309–311, Theorem 1 and Lemma 2.
