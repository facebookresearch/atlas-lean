# Minimum cycle mean of a weighted edge list

For a list of weighted edge occurrences, a rational number $\lambda$ is the minimum cycle mean when some [directed cycle](is-directed-cycle.md) $C$ has mean weight $w(C)/|C| = \lambda$, the weight divided by the number of edges, and no directed cycle has a smaller mean weight. It is Karp's $\lambda^*$; it exists exactly when the digraph has a cycle.

## Sources

- R. M. Karp, "A characterization of the minimum cycle mean in a digraph", *Discrete Mathematics* 23(3) (1978), 309–311, Theorem 1 and Lemma 2.
