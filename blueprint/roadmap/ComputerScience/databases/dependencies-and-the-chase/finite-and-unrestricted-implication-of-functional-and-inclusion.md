# Finite and unrestricted implication of functional and inclusion dependencies

For [inclusion dependencies and finite and unrestricted implication](inclusion-dependencies-and-finite-implication.md): finite and unrestricted implication coincide for functional dependencies alone and for inclusion
dependencies alone, but differ for sets of functional and inclusion dependencies taken together; and the
implication problem for functional and inclusion dependencies together is undecidable.
