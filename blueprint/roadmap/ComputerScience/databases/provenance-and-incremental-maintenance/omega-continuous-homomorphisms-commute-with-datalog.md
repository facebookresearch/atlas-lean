# ω-continuous semiring homomorphisms commute with Datalog queries

Let $K$ and $K'$ be commutative $\omega$-continuous semirings and $h : K \to K'$ a semiring homomorphism that preserves
suprema of $\omega$-chains. For every Datalog query $q$ and every $K$-relation $R$, $q(h(R)) = h(q(R))$, where
$h(R)$ applies $h$ to every tag of $R$.
