# Satisfiability of relational calculus queries is undecidable

Satisfiability of relational calculus queries over finite instances is recursively enumerable but not recursive.
Equivalence and containment of relational calculus queries are co-recursively enumerable and not recursive, and so
is domain independence of a relational calculus query.
