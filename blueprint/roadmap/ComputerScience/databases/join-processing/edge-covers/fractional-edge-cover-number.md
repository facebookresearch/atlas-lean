# Fractional edge cover number of a join

The fractional edge cover number $\rho^*$ of a finite named join hypergraph is the minimum of $\sum_{R\in\sigma}x_R$ over all its [fractional edge covers](is-fractional-edge-cover.md). The sum runs over relations, not over attributes or distinct headings. A feasible assignment [attains this minimum](is-least-fractional-edge-cover-number.md).
