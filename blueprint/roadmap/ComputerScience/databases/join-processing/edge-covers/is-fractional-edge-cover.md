# Fractional edge covers of a join

Let $\sigma$ be the finite set of relation names and $V=\bigcup_{R\in\sigma} A_R$ the union of their headings. A fractional edge cover assigns a real weight $x_R\geq 0$ to each $R\in\sigma$, such that

$$
\sum_{R\in\sigma:\,a\in A_R}x_R\geq 1\qquad(a\in V).
$$

Different relations with identical headings have separate weights. There is no requirement that each weight be at most one.

## Sources

- A. Atserias, M. Grohe and D. Marx, *Size Bounds and Query Plans for Relational Joins*, SIAM J. Comput. 42(4) (2013), 1737–1767, §3.1, LP (3.1); revised manuscript, 29 June 2013, p. 6.
