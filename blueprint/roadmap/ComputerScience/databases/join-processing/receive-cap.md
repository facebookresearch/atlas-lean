# Bit budget at a worker

For $p>0$ workers, $M$ input bits, space exponent $\varepsilon$ and multiplier $C$, the receive cap is the natural-number floor of $CM/p^{1-\varepsilon}$. Thus when the real budget is nonnegative, an integer load fits exactly when it is at most the real budget. This cap is zero on an empty input or a sub-bit budget; the natural-number floor also gives zero on negative arguments. The query model uses $C>0$ and $0\leq\varepsilon\leq1$.
