# Minimizations of a union of conjunctive queries are isomorphic

Any two minimizations of a union of conjunctive queries $q$ are
[isomorphic](minimizations-and-cores-of-unions-of-conjunctive-queries.md); consequently any two cores of $q$ are isomorphic.
In the proof, containment between the disjuncts of two minimizations induces a bijection $\tau$ with $q'_i \equiv q''_{\tau(i)}$,
and equivalent disjuncts that are their own cores are isomorphic.
