# Max-min fair allocations are Pareto-efficient

Every [max-min fair](max-min-fair-rate-allocations.md) allocation is [Pareto-efficient](pareto-efficient-rate-allocations.md): by definition, increasing one of its rates forces a decrease of another. The converse fails: a network usually has many Pareto-efficient allocations and at most one max-min fair one.
