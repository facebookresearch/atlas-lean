# The delay bound

Assume a flow constrained by an [arrival curve](arrival-curves.md) $\alpha$ traverses a system that offers a [service curve](service-curves.md) $\beta$. Then the virtual delay satisfies $d(t) \le h(\alpha, \beta)$ for all $t$, where $h$ is the [horizontal deviation](min-plus-algebra/vertical-and-horizontal-deviations.md).
