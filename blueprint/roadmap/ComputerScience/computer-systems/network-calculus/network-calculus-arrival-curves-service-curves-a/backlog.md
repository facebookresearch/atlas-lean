# Backlog

Backlog at time $t$ of input $R$ and output $R'$ (nonnegative real functions): the positive part $\max(R(t) - R'(t), 0)$, as a value in $[0, \infty]$. Readings $1$ and $2$ give backlog $0$, not $-1$.

## Sources

- J.-Y. Le Boudec and P. Thiran, *Network Calculus: A Theory of Deterministic Queuing Systems for the Internet*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2050, Springer, 2001; online version of 2022, <https://leboudec.github.io/netcal/latex/netCalBook.pdf>, whose numbering is used here, Definition 1.1.1 (backlog).
