# Min Plus Conv assoc

Associativity of min-plus convolution at flow level: `(R ⊗ β₁) ⊗ β₂` in
unfolded `ℝ≥0∞` form equals `R ⊗ (β₁ ⊗ β₂)`. This is the final step of the
chain in the proof of Theorem 1.4.6 of Le Boudec and Thiran, *Network Calculus*, online version, `R* ≥ R₁ ⊗ β₂ ≥ (R ⊗ β₁) ⊗ β₂ = R ⊗ (β₁ ⊗ β₂)`.

## Sources

- J.-Y. Le Boudec and P. Thiran, *Network Calculus: A Theory of Deterministic Queuing Systems for the Internet*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2050, Springer, 2001; online version of 2022, <https://leboudec.github.io/netcal/latex/netCalBook.pdf>, whose numbering is used here, Theorem 1.4.6.
