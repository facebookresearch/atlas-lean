# Covering recurrence bounds every covering selection's value

Let $\beta$ be a linearly ordered additive commutative monoid in which addition is monotone. For
every list of groups of knapsack items with values in $\beta$, every target $L$ and every
selection (one item from each group) of total weight at least $L$, the total value of the
selection is at most the covering recurrence at $L$.
