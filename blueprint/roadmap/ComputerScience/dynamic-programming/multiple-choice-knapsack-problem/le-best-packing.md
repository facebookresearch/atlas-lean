# Packing recurrence bounds every feasible selection's value

Let $\beta$ be a linearly ordered additive commutative monoid in which addition is monotone. For
every list of groups of knapsack items with values in $\beta$, every capacity $c$ and every
selection (one item from each group) of total weight at most $c$, the total value of the selection
is at most the packing recurrence at $c$.
