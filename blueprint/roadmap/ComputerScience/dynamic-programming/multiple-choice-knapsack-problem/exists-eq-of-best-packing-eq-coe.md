# Packing recurrence is attained by a feasible selection

Let $\beta$ be a linearly ordered additive commutative monoid. For every list of groups of
knapsack items with values in $\beta$, every capacity $c$ and every $v \in \beta$, if the packing
recurrence at $c$ equals $v$, then some selection (one item from each group) has total weight at
most $c$ and total value $v$.
