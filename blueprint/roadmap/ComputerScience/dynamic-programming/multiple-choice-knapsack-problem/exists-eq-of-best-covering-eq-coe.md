# Covering recurrence is attained by a covering selection

Let $\beta$ be a linearly ordered additive commutative monoid. For every list of groups of
knapsack items with values in $\beta$, every target $L$ and every $v \in \beta$, if the covering
recurrence at $L$ equals $v$, then some selection (one item from each group) has total weight at
least $L$ and total value $v$.
