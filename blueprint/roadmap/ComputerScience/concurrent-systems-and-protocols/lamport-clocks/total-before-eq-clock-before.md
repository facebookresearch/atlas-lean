# Lamport's total order is the order induced by the Lamport timestamps

For every execution, [Lamport's total order](total-before.md) is the [order induced by the clock system](clock-before.md) whose clock is the Lamport timestamp. The two definitions agree by definition; this lemma records it.

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System*, Communications of the ACM 21(7), July 1978, 558–565: "Ordering the Events Totally", p. 561.
