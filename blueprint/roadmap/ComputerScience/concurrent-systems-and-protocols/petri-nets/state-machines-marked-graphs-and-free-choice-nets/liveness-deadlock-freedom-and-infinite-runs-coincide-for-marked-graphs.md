# Liveness, deadlock-freedom and infinite runs coincide for strongly connected marked graphs

Let $N$ be a strongly connected [marked graph](../subclasses-of-petri-nets.md) and $M_0$ a marking. The following are equivalent: $(N, M_0)$ is [live](../liveness-and-deadlock-freedom.md); $(N, M_0)$ is deadlock-free; $(N, M_0)$ has an infinite firing sequence.
