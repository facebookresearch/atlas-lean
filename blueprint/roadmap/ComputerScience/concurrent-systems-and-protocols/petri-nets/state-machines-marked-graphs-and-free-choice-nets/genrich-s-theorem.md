# Genrich's theorem on live and safe markings of marked graphs

Let $N$ be a strongly connected [marked graph](../subclasses-of-petri-nets.md) with at least one place and one transition. Then there is a marking $M_0$ such that $(N, M_0)$ is [live](../liveness-and-deadlock-freedom.md) and [safe](../boundedness-and-safeness.md).
