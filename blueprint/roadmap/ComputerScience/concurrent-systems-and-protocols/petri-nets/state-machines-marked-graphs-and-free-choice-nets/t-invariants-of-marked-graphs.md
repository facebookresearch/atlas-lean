# T-invariants of connected marked graphs

Let $N$ be a connected [marked graph](../subclasses-of-petri-nets.md). A vector $J : T \to \mathbb{Q}$ is a [T-invariant](../invariants-siphons-and-traps/t-invariants-and-reproducing-sequences.md) of $N$ if and only if $J$ is constant: $J(t) = x$ for every transition $t$, for some $x \in \mathbb{Q}$.
