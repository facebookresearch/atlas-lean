# Upward-closed sets of markings have finitely many minimal elements

Every [upward-closed set of markings](upward-closed-sets-of-markings.md) of an ordinary Petri net has finitely many minimal elements, and it is the set of markings that are $\ge$ one of them.

## Depends on

- [Upward-closed sets of markings](upward-closed-sets-of-markings.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Dickson's lemma for finite products of well-quasi-orders](dickson-s-lemma.md)

## Sources

- J. Esparza, *Petri Nets*, lecture notes, Technical University of Munich, version of 18 June 2019, <https://archive.model.in.tum.de/um/courses/petri/SS2019/PNSkript.pdf>, Lemma 3.2.14, its proof by Dickson's lemma, and the remark that follows it, p. 60.
