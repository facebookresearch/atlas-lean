# Predecessors of an upward-closed set of markings are upward closed

If $\mathcal{M}$ is an [upward-closed set of markings](upward-closed-sets-of-markings.md) of an ordinary Petri net, then the [predecessor sets](predecessor-sets-of-markings.md) $\mathrm{pre}(\mathcal{M})$ and $\mathrm{pre}^*(\mathcal{M})$ are upward closed.

## Depends on

- [Upward-closed sets of markings](upward-closed-sets-of-markings.md)
- [Predecessor sets of markings](predecessor-sets-of-markings.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Monotonicity lemma for Petri net firing sequences](monotonicity-lemma-for-firing-sequences.md)
