# Marked traps stay marked

Let $N$ be an ordinary [Petri net](../place-transition-petri-nets.md), let $Q$ be a [trap](../siphons-and-traps.md) of $N$ and let $M [\sigma\rangle M'$. If $M$ puts a token on some place of $Q$, then so does $M'$. Consequently, if $M$ is reachable from $M_0$ and $M_0$ marks a trap, $M$ marks it.
