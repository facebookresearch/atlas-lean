# Unmarked siphons stay unmarked

Let $N$ be an ordinary [Petri net](../place-transition-petri-nets.md), let $S$ be a [siphon](../siphons-and-traps.md) of $N$ and let $M [\sigma\rangle M'$. If $M$ puts no token on any place of $S$, then neither does $M'$. Consequently, if $M$ is reachable from $M_0$ and $M_0$ leaves a siphon unmarked, $M$ leaves it unmarked.
