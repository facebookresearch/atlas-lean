# Token count on circuits of a marked graph is invariant

In a [marked graph](subclasses-of-petri-nets.md) $(G, M_0)$, the number of tokens on a directed circuit is invariant under firing: $M(C) = M_0(C)$ for every directed circuit $C$ and every reachable marking $M$.
