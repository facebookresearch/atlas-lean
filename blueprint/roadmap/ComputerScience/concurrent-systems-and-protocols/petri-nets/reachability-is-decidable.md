# Reachability in Petri nets is decidable

There is an algorithm that decides, given a [Petri net](place-transition-petri-nets.md) $(N, M_0)$ and a marking $M$, whether $M$ is in the [reachability set](reachability-set-and-reachability-graph.md) $R(N, M_0)$. Mayr gave the first complete proof, Kosaraju a simpler one; all known algorithms of that period are non-primitive recursive.
