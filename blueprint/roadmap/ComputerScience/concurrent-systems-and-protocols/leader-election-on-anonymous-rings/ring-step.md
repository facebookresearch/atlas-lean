# One synchronous round on an anonymous ring

Let $S$ be a set of local states, $n$ a natural number, and $\delta : S \times S \times S \to S$ a local transition. The processes are indexed by the integers modulo $n$. One synchronous round sends a configuration $s$ to the configuration in which process $i$ has state $\delta(s(i - 1), s(i), s(i + 1))$, the indices taken modulo $n$. Every process runs the same deterministic transition on the states of its left neighbour, itself and its right neighbour. For $n \ge 1$ the processes form a ring of $n$ processes; for $n = 0$ the index set is $\mathbb{Z}/0 = \mathbb{Z}$, so the processes form an infinite line, process $0$ having neighbours $-1$ and $1$.
