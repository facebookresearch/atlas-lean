# Concatenation of finite-valued service curves

Concatenation of service curves (Le Boudec and Thiran, Theorem 1.4.6) for finite-valued curves: let $R, R_1, R^*$ be cumulative flows and $\beta_1, \beta_2 : [0, \infty) \to [0, \infty)$ finite-valued curves. If $\beta_1$ is a service curve from $R$ to $R_1$ and $\beta_2$ from $R_1$ to $R^*$, then the [concatenated curve](conv-curve.md) $\beta_1 \otimes \beta_2$ is a service curve from $R$ to $R^*$. Curves with infinite values, such as the burst-delay curve $\delta_T$, are not covered.
