# Oral messages, run as a protocol, solve the Byzantine generals problem

If there are more than $3m$ generals, finitely many, then the oral-message protocol for $\mathrm{OM}(m)$
with commander $c$ satisfies IC1 and IC2 after $m + 1$ rounds, for every order, every set of at most $m$
traitors and every behaviour of the traitors (Theorem 1 of Lamport, Shostak and Pease).
