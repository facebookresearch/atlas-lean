# Solvability of the Byzantine generals problem with oral messages

Let the generals form a finite set with at least three elements, and let there be at least two orders.
Some oral-message protocol solves the Byzantine generals problem with commander $c$ and at most $m$
traitors, in some number of rounds, if and only if $3m$ is less than the number of generals. In that
case $\mathrm{OM}(m)$ solves it in $m + 1$ rounds; otherwise no protocol solves it (Lamport, Shostak and
Pease).
