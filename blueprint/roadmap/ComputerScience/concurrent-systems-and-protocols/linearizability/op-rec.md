# Completed operations of a concurrent history

A completed operation records the object it acts on, the operation, its argument and its return value (all natural numbers), and the positions $\ell < h$ of its invocation and its response in the global event order. A concurrent history is a finite list of completed operations.

## Sources

- M. P. Herlihy and J. M. Wing, "Linearizability: a correctness condition for concurrent objects", *ACM Transactions on Programming Languages and Systems* 12 (1990), 463-492.
