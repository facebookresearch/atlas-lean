# Projections of linearizable histories are linearizable

Let a sequential specification make the empty sequence legal for every object. Then the projection of a linearizable history onto any single object is linearizable. This is the projection direction of Herlihy and Wing's locality theorem.

## Sources

- M. P. Herlihy and J. M. Wing, "Linearizability: a correctness condition for concurrent objects", *ACM Transactions on Programming Languages and Systems* 12 (1990), 463-492.
