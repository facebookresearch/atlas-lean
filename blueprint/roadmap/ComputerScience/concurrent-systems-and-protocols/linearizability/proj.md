# Projection of a history onto one object

The projection of a concurrent history onto an object $o$ is the sublist of its completed operations that act on $o$, in the same order.

## Sources

- M. P. Herlihy and J. M. Wing, "Linearizability: a correctness condition for concurrent objects", *ACM Transactions on Programming Languages and Systems* 12 (1990), 463-492.
