# Initial state of the bakery algorithm

The initial state of the bakery algorithm for $N$ processes has every process in its noncritical section and all entries of both arrays zero: $\mathrm{choosing}[i] = 0$ and $\mathrm{number}[i] = 0$ for every $i$ (Lamport 1974: execution begins in the noncritical section; the arrays are initially zero).

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *A New Solution of Dijkstra's Concurrent Programming Problem*, Communications of the ACM 17(8), August 1974, 453–455: p. 454.
