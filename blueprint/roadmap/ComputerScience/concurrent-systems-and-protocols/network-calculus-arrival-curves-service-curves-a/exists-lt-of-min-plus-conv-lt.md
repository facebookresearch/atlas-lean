# Exists lt of min Plus Conv lt

For functions $f, g \colon \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, a time $t \ge 0$ and a bound $c \in [0, \infty]$: if the min-plus convolution satisfies $(f \otimes g)(t) < c$, then there is an $s \in [0, t]$ with $f(t - s) + g(s) < c$. The witness lies below $c$, not below the convolution; the infimum need not be attained.
