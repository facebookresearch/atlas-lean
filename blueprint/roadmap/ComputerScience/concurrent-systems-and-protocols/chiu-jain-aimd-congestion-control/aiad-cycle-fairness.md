# Fairness on the AIAD two-cycle

For the loads $(1, 2)$ and $(2, 3)$ of the [AIAD two-cycle](aiad-cycle-not-tendsto-one.md), Jain's index $F(x) = (x_1 + x_2)^2 / (2(x_1^2 + x_2^2))$ is $9/10$ and $25/26$ respectively. Since the AIAD step alternates between these loads, AIAD does not preserve the fairness index in general (contrast Chiu and Jain, Figure 6: AIAD converges to efficiency but not to fairness).
