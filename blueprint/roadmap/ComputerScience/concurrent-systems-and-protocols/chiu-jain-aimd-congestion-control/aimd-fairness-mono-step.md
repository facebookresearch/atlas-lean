# Aimd fairness mono step

For $n > 0$ users, additive increase $a > 0$, multiplicative decrease factor $0 < b < 1$, any goal, and loads $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ that are componentwise nonnegative with positive total, one AIMD step does not decrease the fairness index (Chiu–Jain, Section 2.3). The load hypotheses matter: with $a = 1$, $b = 1/2$ and goal $1$, the increase sends $(-2, -1)$ to $(-1, 0)$ and lowers the fairness from $9/10$ to $1/2$.
