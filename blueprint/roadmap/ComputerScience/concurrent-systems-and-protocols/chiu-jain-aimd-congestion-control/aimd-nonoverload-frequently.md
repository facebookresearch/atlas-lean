# Non-overload steps recur along the AIMD orbit

For `n ≥ 1` users, additive increase `a > 0`, multiplicative decrease factor `0 < b < 1`, a nonnegative initial load vector with positive total, and a goal `> 0`, non-overload steps (total load at most the goal) recur infinitely often along the AIMD orbit. Together with the [recurrence of overload steps](aimd-overload-frequently.md), the system keeps crossing the goal in both directions, so the load vector need not converge to a fixed point.
