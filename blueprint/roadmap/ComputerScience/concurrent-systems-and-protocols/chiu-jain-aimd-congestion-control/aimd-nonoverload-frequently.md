# Aimd nonoverload frequently

For `n ≥ 1` users, additive increase `a > 0`, multiplicative decrease factor `0 < b < 1`, a nonnegative initial load vector with positive total, and a goal `> 0`, non-overload steps (total load at most the goal) recur infinitely often along the AIMD orbit, so the system keeps crossing the goal in both directions and the load vector need not converge to a fixed point.
