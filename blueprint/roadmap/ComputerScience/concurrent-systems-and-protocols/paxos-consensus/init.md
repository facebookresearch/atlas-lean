# Initial state of single-decree Paxos

The initial state of single-decree Paxos: no prepare request sent, no acceptor has responded to a prepare request, and no promise, proposal or acceptance exists.

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
