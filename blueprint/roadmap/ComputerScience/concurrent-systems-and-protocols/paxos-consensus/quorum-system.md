# Quorum system

A quorum system on a set $A$ of acceptors is a family of finite subsets of $A$, the quorums, any two of which share an acceptor (Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, Section 2.2: "any two majorities have at least one acceptor in common", and the "obvious generalization of a majority"). In particular every quorum is nonempty.

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
