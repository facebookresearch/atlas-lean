# Majorities form a quorum system

Let $A$ be a finite set of acceptors. The finite subsets $S \subseteq A$ with $|A| < 2|S|$, those containing more than half of the acceptors, form a [quorum system](quorum-system.md): any two of them share an acceptor (Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, Section 2.2).

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
