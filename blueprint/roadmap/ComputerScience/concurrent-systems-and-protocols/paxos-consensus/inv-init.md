# The Paxos invariant holds initially

For every quorum system, the [inductive invariant of single-decree Paxos](inv.md) holds in the [initial state](init.md), where every set of messages and acceptances is empty.

## Sources

- L. Lamport, *Paxos Made Simple*, ACM SIGACT News 32(4), December 2001, 51–58; author's version dated 01 Nov 2001: Section 2.2.
