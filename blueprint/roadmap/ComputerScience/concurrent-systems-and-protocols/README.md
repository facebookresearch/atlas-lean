# Concurrent systems and protocols

Specific concurrent or distributed systems and their verified properties: deadlock and its detection, the banker's algorithm, dining philosophers, communication protocols such as the alternating-bit protocol and go-back-N, mutual exclusion (the bakery algorithm), logical time (Lamport and vector clocks), and fault-tolerant agreement with its algorithms and impossibility results (Paxos, Byzantine generals and Byzantine agreement, FLP, the round bound for crash failures). Each page on fault tolerance states its model: synchrony, failure type and message delivery.
