# Executions under crash failures

Let $Q$ be a [synchronous protocol with crash failures](crash-protocol.md), $x : G \to V$ an assignment of inputs and $\pi$ a [crash pattern](crash-pattern.md). The state $s_r(i)$ of process $i$ after $r$ rounds of $Q$ with inputs $x$ and crash pattern $\pi$ is defined by $s_0(i) = \mathrm{init}(i, x_i)$ and $s_{r+1}(i) = \mathrm{recv}(r, i, s_r(i), \mathit{msg})$, where $\mathit{msg}(j) = \mathrm{send}(r, j, s_r(j), i)$ if the message of $j$ to $i$ in round $r$ is [delivered](delivers.md) under $\pi$, and $\mathit{msg}(j) = \bot$ otherwise. The recursion gives every process a state after every round, also after it has crashed; for a crashed process the later values are formal, since none of its messages is delivered any more, and they do not mean that the process keeps running.
