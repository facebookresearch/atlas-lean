# Streams form a complete partial order

For every set $D$, the set $D^\omega$ of [finite and infinite sequences under the prefix order](streams-and-the-prefix-order.md) has the least element $\Lambda$, and every increasing chain $X_1 \sqsubseteq X_2 \sqsubseteq \cdots$ in $D^\omega$ has a least upper bound, the sequence whose initial segments are the $X_n$ and their initial segments. So $D^\omega$ is a complete partial order, and so is every finite product of such orders, ordered componentwise.
