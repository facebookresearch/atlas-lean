# Swapping adjacent binders throughout a process

For a cutoff $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $P[k \leftrightarrow k + 1]$ applies the [swap of the indices](swap-name-at.md) $k$ and $k + 1$ to every name of the [process](proc.md) $P$, with the cutoff increased by one under each binder (input or restriction); it commutes with $\mathbf{0}$, $\mid$, $!$ and $+$.
