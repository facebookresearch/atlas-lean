# Processes of the π-calculus

The processes of the π-calculus are generated by the following constructors, where $a$ and $b$ are [channel names](name.md):

- the empty process $\mathbf{0}$;
- the output $\bar{a}\langle b\rangle.P$, which sends $b$ on $a$ and continues as $P$;
- the input $a(\cdot).P$, which receives a name on $a$; it binds the index $\#0$ in $P$;
- the parallel composition $P \mid Q$;
- the restriction $\nu P$, which binds the index $\#0$ in $P$ to a fresh channel;
- the replication $!P$;
- the choice $P + Q$.
