# Substitution of a name into a π-calculus process at a cutoff

Let $n$ be a [channel name](name.md) and $k \in \mathbb{N}$ a cutoff. The substitution $P[n/k]$ of $n$ for the variable $\#k$ in a [process](proc.md) $P$ applies the [substitution of names](subst-name.md) to every name of $P$, and under a binder (an input or a restriction) continues with cutoff $k + 1$ and with $n$ replaced by its [lifting](lift-name.md) above $0$: for instance $(\nu P)[n/k] = \nu\,(P[\uparrow_0 n/(k + 1)])$ and $(a(\cdot).P)[n/k] = a[n/k](\cdot).P[\uparrow_0 n/(k+1)]$, and the substitution commutes with $\mathbf{0}$, outputs, $\mid$, $!$ and $+$. Every free index of $n$ refers to a binder above the substitution point, so all of them shift under a new binder; lifting at the current cutoff instead would let indices below the cutoff be captured.
