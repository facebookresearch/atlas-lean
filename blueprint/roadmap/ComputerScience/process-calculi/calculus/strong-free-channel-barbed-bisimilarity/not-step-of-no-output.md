# Processes without unguarded output cannot reduce

A π-calculus process $P$ with no [unguarded output](has-unguarded-output.md) has no [reduction](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/step.md): $P \to Q$ for no $Q$. Such a process may still have input barbs, so it need not be equivalent to $\mathbf{0}$.
