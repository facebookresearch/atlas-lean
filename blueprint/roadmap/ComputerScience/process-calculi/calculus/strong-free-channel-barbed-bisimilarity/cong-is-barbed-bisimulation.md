# Structural congruence is a strong barbed bisimulation

[Structural congruence](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/cong.md) is a strong bisimulation of the [decorated reduction system](observed-lts.md): reductions of structurally congruent processes match one for one with structurally congruent targets, and structurally congruent processes have the same barbs.
