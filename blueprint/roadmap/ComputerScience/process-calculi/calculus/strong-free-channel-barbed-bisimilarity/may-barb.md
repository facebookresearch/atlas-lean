# May-barb after finitely many reductions

A π-calculus process $P$ may have the barb $o$ when some process $P'$ reachable from $P$ by finitely many, possibly zero, [reductions](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/step.md) has the [barb](barb.md) $o$. This is a may observation, not a weak equivalence.
