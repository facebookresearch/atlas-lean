# Lifting fixes a locally closed process

If a process $P$ is [scoped at depth $k$](closed-at-2.md), then $\uparrow_k P = P$ ([lifting](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/lift-proc.md) above $k$). In particular a process scoped at depth $0$ is unchanged when it is put under a fresh binder: none of its names is captured.
