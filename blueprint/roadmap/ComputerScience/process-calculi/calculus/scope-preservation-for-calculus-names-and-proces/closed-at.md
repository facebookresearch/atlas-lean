# Name scoped at a binder depth

For a depth $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a [channel name](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/name.md) is scoped at depth $d$ when it is a free channel $c_n$, or a variable $\#i$ with $i < d$: it refers only to a free channel or to one of $d$ surrounding binders. At depth $0$ no variable is scoped.
