# Lifting preserves name scoping

If a [channel name](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/name.md) $b$ is [scoped at depth $n$](closed-at.md), then for every cutoff $k$ its [lifting](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/lift-name.md) above $k$ is scoped at depth $n + 1$.
