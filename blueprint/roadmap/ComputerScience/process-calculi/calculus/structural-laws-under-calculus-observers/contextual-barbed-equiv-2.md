# Structural congruence implies contextual barbed equivalence

If $P \equiv Q$ ([structural congruence](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/cong.md)), then $P$ and $Q$ are [contextually strong barbed equivalent](contextual-barbed-equiv.md): structural congruence is sound under every observer context, with one-for-one matching of reductions and equality of immediate input and output barbs.
