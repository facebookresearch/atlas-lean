# One-hole lexical process contexts

The one-hole contexts of the π-calculus are generated by: the hole $[\,]$; the output and input prefixes $\bar{a}\langle b\rangle.C$ and $a(\cdot).C$; the parallel compositions $C \mid P$ and $P \mid C$; the restriction $\nu C$; the replication $!C$; and the choices $C + P$ and $P + C$, where $C$ is a context, $P$ a [process](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/proc.md) and $a$, $b$ [channel names](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/name.md).
