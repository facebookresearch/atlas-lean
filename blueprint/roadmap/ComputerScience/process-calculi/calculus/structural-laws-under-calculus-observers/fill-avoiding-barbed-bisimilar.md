# Structural laws are barbed sound in capture-avoiding contexts

Let $P \equiv Q$ ([structural congruence](../the-calculus-syntax-substitution-structural-cong/cong.md)) with $P$ and $Q$ both [scoped at depth $0$](../scope-preservation-for-calculus-names-and-proces/closed-at-2.md). Then for every [context](context.md) $C$, the [capture-avoiding fillings](fill-avoiding.md) of $C$ with $P$ and with $Q$ are [strongly barbed bisimilar](../strong-free-channel-barbed-bisimilarity/barbed-bisimilar.md).
