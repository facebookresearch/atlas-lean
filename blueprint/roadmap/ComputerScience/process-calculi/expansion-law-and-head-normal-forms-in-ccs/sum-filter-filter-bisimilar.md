# Filtering summands twice by the same predicate

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. For a list $s$ of summands (pairs $(\alpha, P)$ of a CCS action and a static CCS process), $\Sigma s$ denotes the right-associated finite choice $\alpha_1.P_1 + (\alpha_2.P_2 + (\cdots + (\alpha_n.P_n + 0)))$ of its entries, with $\Sigma [\,] = 0$. For every Boolean-valued predicate $p$ on summands and every list $s$ of summands, write $s|_p$ for the list of entries of $s$ satisfying $p$, in order. Then $\Sigma((s|_p)|_p)$ is strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system of static CCS to $\Sigma(s|_p)$.
