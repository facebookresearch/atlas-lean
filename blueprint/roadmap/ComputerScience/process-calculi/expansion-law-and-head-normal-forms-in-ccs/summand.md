# Summands of a finite CCS sum

Over a type of channel names, a summand is a pair $(\alpha, P)$ of a CCS action $\alpha$ (the silent action $\tau$, an input or an output) and a static CCS process $P$ (a finite CCS process with prefix, choice, parallel composition, restriction and relabelling). It stands for one action-prefixed branch $\alpha.P$ of a finite sum.
