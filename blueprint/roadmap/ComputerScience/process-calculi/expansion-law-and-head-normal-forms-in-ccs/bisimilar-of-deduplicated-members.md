# Processes with the same deduplicated head summands are strongly bisimilar

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. Let $P$ and $Q$ be static CCS processes whose deduplicated head summands have the same entries, that is, every summand is an entry of the list for $P$ if and only if it is an entry of the list for $Q$. Then $P$ and $Q$ are strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system of static CCS. Here the head summands $h(P)$ of a static CCS process $P$ are defined by recursion on $P$: $h(0) = [\,]$; $h(\alpha.P) = [(\alpha, \Sigma h(P))]$; $h(P + Q) = h(P) \mathbin{+\!\!+} h(Q)$; $h(P \mid Q)$ is the list of expansion summands of $h(P)$ and $h(Q)$; $h(P \setminus L)$ is $h(P)$ restricted by $L$; and $h(P[f])$ is $h(P)$ relabelled by $f$. The head normal form of $P$ is $\Sigma h(P)$, where $\Sigma s$ is the right-associated finite choice $\alpha_1.P_1 + (\cdots + (\alpha_n.P_n + 0))$ of a list $s$ of summands. The deduplicated head summands of $P$ are $h(P)$ with repeated entries removed (summands compared by syntactic equality, the last occurrence of each entry kept, and the relative order of the kept entries preserved), and the deduplicated head normal form of $P$ is their finite sum.
