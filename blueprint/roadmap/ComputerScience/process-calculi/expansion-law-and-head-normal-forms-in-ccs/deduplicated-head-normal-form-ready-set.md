# The deduplicated head normal form has the same ready set

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. For every static CCS process $P$, the set of labels of the outgoing transitions of its deduplicated head normal form, in static CCS, equals the set of labels of the outgoing transitions of $P$. Here the head summands $h(P)$ of a static CCS process $P$ are defined by recursion on $P$: $h(0) = [\,]$; $h(\alpha.P) = [(\alpha, \Sigma h(P))]$; $h(P + Q) = h(P) \mathbin{+\!\!+} h(Q)$; $h(P \mid Q)$ is the list of expansion summands of $h(P)$ and $h(Q)$; $h(P \setminus L)$ is $h(P)$ restricted by $L$; and $h(P[f])$ is $h(P)$ relabelled by $f$. The head normal form of $P$ is $\Sigma h(P)$, where $\Sigma s$ is the right-associated finite choice $\alpha_1.P_1 + (\cdots + (\alpha_n.P_n + 0))$ of a list $s$ of summands. The deduplicated head summands of $P$ are $h(P)$ with repeated entries removed (summands compared by syntactic equality, the last occurrence of each entry kept, and the relative order of the kept entries preserved), and the deduplicated head normal form of $P$ is their finite sum.
