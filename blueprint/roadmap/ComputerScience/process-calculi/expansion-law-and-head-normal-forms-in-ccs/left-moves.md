# Left moves of a parallel composition of finite sums

Given a list $s$ of summands (pairs of a CCS action and a static CCS process) and a static CCS process $Q$, the left moves of $s$ against $Q$ are the list obtained from $s$ by replacing each entry $(\alpha, P)$ with $(\alpha, P \mid Q)$, keeping the order.

## Sources

- Robin Milner, *Communication and Concurrency*, Prentice Hall, 1989, Chapter 3, ISBN 978-0-13-115007-2.
