# Right moves of a parallel composition of finite sums

Given a static CCS process $P$ and a list $t$ of summands (pairs of a CCS action and a static CCS process), the right moves of $P$ against $t$ are the list obtained from $t$ by replacing each entry $(\beta, Q)$ with $(\beta, P \mid Q)$, keeping the order.

## Sources

- Robin Milner, *Communication and Concurrency*, Prentice Hall, 1989, Chapter 3, ISBN 978-0-13-115007-2.
