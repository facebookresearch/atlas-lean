# Shared service signature

A service signature is a triple $\sigma = (\pi, r, c)$ of a priority $\pi \in \mathbb{N}$, a
polarity $r \in \{+,-\}$ and a capability $c$ (a rank and a protocol). It describes the endpoint
transferred to the service at each invocation: its polarity $r$ and the capability $c$ it must
carry.
