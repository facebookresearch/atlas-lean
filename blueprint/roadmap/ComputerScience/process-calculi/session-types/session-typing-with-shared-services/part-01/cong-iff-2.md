# Declared service offers are invariant under congruence

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment, $s$ a natural number, $\sigma$ a service signature and $p
\equiv q$ structurally congruent processes. Then $p$ offers $s$ with signature $\sigma$ (that is,
$\Gamma(s) = \sigma$ and $p$ offers $\mathrm{c}_s$ persistently) if and only if $q$ does.
