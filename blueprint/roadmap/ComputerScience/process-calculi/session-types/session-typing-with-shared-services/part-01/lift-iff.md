# Service templates are invariant under lifting

For every service environment $\Gamma$, natural number $k$ and process $p$, $\uparrow_k p$ is a
service template for $\Gamma$ if and only if $p$ is, where $\uparrow_k$ adds one to every variable
index $\ge k$ (templates consist of $\mathbf{0}$ and inputs on declared constant triggers, combined
by $\mid$, $\nu$ and replication).
