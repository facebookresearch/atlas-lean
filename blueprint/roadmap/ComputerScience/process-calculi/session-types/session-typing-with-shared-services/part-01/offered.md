# Declared persistent service offer

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment, $p$ a process, $s$ a natural number and $\sigma$ a service
signature. The process $p$ offers $s$ with signature $\sigma$ when $\Gamma(s) = \sigma$ and $p$
offers $\mathrm{c}_s$ persistently (it contains, under $\mid$ and $\nu$ only, a replication whose
body has an unguarded input on $\mathrm{c}_s$).
