# Qualified progress at reachable closed runtime states

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment and $p$, $q$ processes such that
$\Gamma; \emptyset \vdash_n p$, all services of $\Gamma$ are available in $p$, and $q$ is reachable
from $p$ by finitely many (possibly zero) reduction steps. Then $q$ is idle for $\Gamma$ or $q \to
r$ for some process $r$.
