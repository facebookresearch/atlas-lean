# Subject reduction for runtime typing at balanced interfaces

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment, $\Delta$ a semantically balanced
ownership context and $p$, $q$ processes with $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n p$ and $p \to q$. Then there
is a semantically balanced ownership context $\Xi$ with $\Gamma; \Xi \vdash_n q$ such that $\Xi =
\Delta$ or $\Xi$ is a semantic advance of $\Delta$.
