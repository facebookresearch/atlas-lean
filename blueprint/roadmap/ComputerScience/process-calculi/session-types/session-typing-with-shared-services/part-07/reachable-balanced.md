# Multi-step subject reduction at balanced interfaces

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment, $\Delta$ a semantically balanced
ownership context and $p$, $q$ processes such that $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n p$ and $q$ is reachable
from $p$ by finitely many reduction steps. Then there is a semantically balanced ownership context
$\Xi$ with $\Gamma; \Xi \vdash_n q$ that is reachable from $\Delta$ by finitely many semantic
advances.
