# Qualified progress at every reachable runtime state

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment, $\Delta$ a semantically balanced
ownership context and $p$, $q$ processes such that $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n p$, all services of
$\Gamma$ are available in $p$, and $q$ is reachable from $p$ by finitely many (possibly zero)
reduction steps. Then $q$ is idle for $\Gamma$ or $q \to r$ for some process $r$.
