# Service templates survive environment extension

Let $\Gamma$, $\Xi$ be service environments with $\Xi$ extending $\Gamma$, and $p$ a service
template for $\Gamma$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ and inputs on triggers declared in $\Gamma$ by
$\mid$, $\nu$ and replication). Then $p$ is a service template for $\Xi$.
