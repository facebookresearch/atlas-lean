# Binder lifting preserves separation

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment and $\Delta$ an ownership context separated from $\Gamma$ (no
owned endpoint is named by the trigger $\mathrm{c}_s$ of a declared service). Then for every natural
number $k$, $\uparrow_k\Delta$ is separated from $\Gamma$, where $\uparrow_k$ adds one to every
variable index $\ge k$ in the names of the endpoints.
