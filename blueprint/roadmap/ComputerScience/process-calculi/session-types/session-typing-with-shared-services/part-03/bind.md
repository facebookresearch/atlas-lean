# Binding a private pair preserves separation

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment, $\Delta$ an ownership context separated from $\Gamma$ and $c$
a capability. Then $\nu_c\Delta$ is separated from $\Gamma$, where $\nu_c\Delta$ is
$\uparrow_0\Delta$ extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
