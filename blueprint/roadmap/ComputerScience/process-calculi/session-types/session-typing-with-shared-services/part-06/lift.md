# Lifting preserves guarded frontiers

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment, $p$ a guarded frontier for $\Gamma$ (a parallel composition
of service templates and prefixed processes) and $k$ a natural number. Then $\uparrow_k p$ is a
guarded frontier for $\Gamma$, where $\uparrow_k$ adds one to every variable index $\ge k$.
