# Semantic advance preserves shared-linear separation

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment and $\Delta$, $\Xi$ ownership contexts with $\Delta$ separated
from $\Gamma$ (no owned endpoint is named by a declared trigger). If $\Xi$ is a semantic advance of
$\Delta$ (an endpoint of $\Delta$ and its opposite advanced past a matching send and receive, all
other entries unchanged), then $\Xi$ is separated from $\Gamma$.
