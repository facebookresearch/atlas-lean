# Request-free completed runtime frontiers are service templates

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment, $\Delta$ an ownership context all of
whose capabilities have head $\mathrm{end}$, and $p$ a guarded frontier for $\Gamma$ that is
request-free for $\Gamma$ and satisfies $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n p$. Then $p$ is a service template
for $\Gamma$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ and inputs on declared triggers by $\mid$, $\nu$ and
replication).
