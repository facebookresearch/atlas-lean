# Guarded normal form of a typed process keeps its typing

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Gamma$ a service environment, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a
process with $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n p$. Then there is a process $q$ in guarded normal form for
$\Gamma$ with $p \equiv q$ and $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash_n q$.
