# Service templates are preserved by renamings fixing shared names

Let $\Gamma$ be a service environment, $p$ a service template for $\Gamma$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$
and inputs on declared triggers by $\mid$, $\nu$ and replication) and $f$ a map from names to names
fixing every declared $\mathrm{c}_s$. Then $f(p)$, the capture-free application of $f$ to the names
of $p$ (using $f^{\uparrow}$ under binders), is a service template for $\Gamma$.
