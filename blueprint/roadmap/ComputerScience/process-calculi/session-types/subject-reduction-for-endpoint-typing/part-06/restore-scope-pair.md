# Endpoint typing restores both bound roles to the restricted side

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$, $\Gamma$, $\Xi$ ownership contexts, $p$, $q$ processes and
$c$ a capability with $\Gamma \vdash_{n+1} p$, $\Xi \vdash_{n+1} \uparrow_0 q$ and $\nu_c\Delta =
\Gamma \cup \Xi$. Then $\Gamma[\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c][\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c]
\vdash_{n+1} p$.
