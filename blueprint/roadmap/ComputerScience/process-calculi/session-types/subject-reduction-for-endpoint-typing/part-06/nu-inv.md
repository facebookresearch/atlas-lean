# Inversion of endpoint typing for restriction

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process. If $\Delta \vdash_n
\nu\,p$, then there is a capability $c$ with $\nu_c\Delta \vdash_{n+1} p$, where $\nu_c\Delta$ is
$\uparrow_0\Delta$ extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
