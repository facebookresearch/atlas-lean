# Advancement of a matched dual subject pair

The dual advance relation on ownership contexts has a single rule: if $\Delta(e) = c = (k,S)$,
$\Delta(\bar e) = \bar c$ and $c$ sends with continuation $S'$ (that is, $\mathrm{unf}(S)$ is
${!\mathrm{N}}.S'$ or ${!(r,j,T)}.S'$ for some $r$, $j$, $T$), then $\Delta$ advances to $\Delta[e
\mapsto (k,S')][\bar e \mapsto (k,\overline{S'})]$. Only the subject endpoint and its opposite
change; a delegated payload keeps its entry.
