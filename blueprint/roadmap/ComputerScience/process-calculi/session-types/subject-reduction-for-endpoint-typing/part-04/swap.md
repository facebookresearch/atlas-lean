# Swapping two ambient binders in an ownership context

Let $k$ be a natural number and $\Delta$ an ownership context. The swapped context $\Delta[k
\leftrightarrow k+1]$ maps $(x[k \leftrightarrow k+1])^{r}$ to $\Delta(x^{r})$ for every endpoint
$x^{r}$ in the domain of $\Delta$, where $x[k \leftrightarrow k+1]$ exchanges the variables
$\mathrm{v}_k$ and $\mathrm{v}_{k+1}$ and fixes every other name.
