# Head communication yields a dual advancement

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a compatible ownership context, $a$, $b$ names and $p$, $q$
processes. If $\Delta \vdash_n \overline{a}\langle b\rangle.p \mid a(\cdot).q$, then there is a
compatible ownership context $\Xi$ such that $\Delta$ dual-advances to $\Xi$ and $\Xi \vdash_n p
\mid q\{b\}$.
