# Dual-continuation update leaves other lookups unchanged

For every ownership context $\Delta$, endpoints $e$, $x$ with $x \ne e$ and $x \ne \bar e$,
capability $c$ of rank $k$ and protocol $S$, the context $\Delta[e \mapsto (k,S)][\bar e \mapsto
(k,\overline{S})]$ has the same entry at $x$ as $\Delta$ (or both have none).
