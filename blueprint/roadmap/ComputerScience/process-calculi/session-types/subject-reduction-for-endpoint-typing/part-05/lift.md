# Lifting preserves semantic advancement

Let $\Delta$, $\Xi$ be ownership contexts such that $\Delta$ advances semantically to $\Xi$, and $k$
a natural number. Then $\uparrow_k\Delta$ advances semantically to $\uparrow_k\Xi$, where
$\uparrow_k$ adds one to every variable index $\ge k$ in the names of the endpoints.
