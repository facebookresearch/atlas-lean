# Communication heads give a send-head observation

Let $c$, $d$ be capabilities and $S'$, $U'$ protocols such that $c$ and $d$ have matching
communication heads with continuations $S'$ and $U'$. Then $c$ sends with continuation $S'$: the
head of its protocol is ${!\mathrm{N}}.S'$ or ${!(r,j,T)}.S'$ for some polarity $r$, rank $j$ and
protocol $T$.
