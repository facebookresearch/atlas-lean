# Binder swapping preserves semantic advancement

Let $\Delta$, $\Xi$ be ownership contexts such that $\Delta$ advances semantically to $\Xi$, and $k$
a natural number. Then $\Delta[k \leftrightarrow k+1]$ advances semantically to $\Xi[k
\leftrightarrow k+1]$, where $[k \leftrightarrow k+1]$ exchanges the variables $\mathrm{v}_k$ and
$\mathrm{v}_{k+1}$ in the names of the endpoints.
