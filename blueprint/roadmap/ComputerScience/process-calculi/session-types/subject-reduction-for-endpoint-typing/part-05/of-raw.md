# Raw dual advancement is a semantic advancement

Let $\Delta$, $\Xi$ be ownership contexts. If $\Delta$ dual-advances to $\Xi$ (the subject $e$ with
$\Delta(e) = (k,S)$ and its opposite with $\Delta(\bar e) = (k,\overline{S})$ are replaced by
$(k,S')$ and $(k,\overline{S'})$, where $S$ has a send head with continuation $S'$), then $\Delta$
advances semantically to $\Xi$.
