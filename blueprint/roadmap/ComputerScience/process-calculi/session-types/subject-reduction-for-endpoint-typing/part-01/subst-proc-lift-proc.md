# Substitution cancels process lifting

For every name $b$, natural number $k$ and process $p$, $(\uparrow_k p)\{b/k\} = p$, where
$\uparrow_k p$ adds one to every variable index $\ge k$ (raising the cutoff under binders) and
$\{b/k\}$ substitutes $b$ for the variable at cutoff $k$ (raising the cutoff and lifting $b$ under
binders) and lowers larger indices by one.
