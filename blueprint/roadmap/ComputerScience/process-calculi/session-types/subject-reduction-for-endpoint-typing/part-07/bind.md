# Binding a dual private pair preserves compatibility

Let $\Delta$ be a semantically compatible ownership context and $c$ a capability. Then $\nu_c\Delta$
is semantically compatible, where $\nu_c\Delta$ is $\uparrow_0\Delta$ extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+}
\mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
