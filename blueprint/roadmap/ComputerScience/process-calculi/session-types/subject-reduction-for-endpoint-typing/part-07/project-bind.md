# Semantic advance under a private binding is private or outer

Let $\kappa$ be a capability and $\Delta$, $\Xi$ ownership contexts such that $\nu_\kappa\Delta$
advances semantically to $\Xi$. Then either there is a capability $c$ with the rank of $\kappa$ such
that $\nu_\kappa\emptyset$ advances semantically to $\nu_c\emptyset$ and $\Xi = \nu_c\Delta$, or
$\Xi = \nu_\kappa\Delta'$ for some $\Delta'$ to which $\Delta$ advances semantically.
