# Received-endpoint substitution is injective when the role is fresh

Let $\Delta$ be an ownership context, $b$ a name, $r$ a polarity and $c$ a capability, with $b^{r}$
not in the domain of $\Delta$. Then $x^{r'} \mapsto (x\{b/0\})^{r'}$ is injective on the domain of
$(\uparrow_0\Delta)[\mathrm{v}_0^{r} \mapsto c]$, where $\uparrow_0\Delta$ raises every variable
name index of an endpoint by one and $x\{b/0\}$ replaces $\mathrm{v}_0$ by $b$ and lowers larger
indices by one.
