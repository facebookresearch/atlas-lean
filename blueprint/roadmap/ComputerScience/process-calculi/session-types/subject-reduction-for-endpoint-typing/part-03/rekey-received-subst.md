# Received-endpoint substitution moves the bound role to the sent key

Let $\Delta$ be an ownership context, $b$ a name, $r$ a polarity and $c$ a capability, with $b^{r}$
not in the domain of $\Delta$. Then rekeying $(\uparrow_0\Delta)[\mathrm{v}_0^{r} \mapsto c]$ along
$x^{r'} \mapsto (x\{b/0\})^{r'}$ gives $\Delta[b^{r} \mapsto c]$.
