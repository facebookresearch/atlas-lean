# Dual of an endpoint send head is an endpoint receive head

Let $c$, $t$ be capabilities with $t = \bar c$, $r$ a polarity, $k$ a rank and $T$, $S$ protocols
such that the protocol of $c$ has head ${!(r,k,T)}.S$. Then the protocol of $t$ has head
${?(r,k,T)}.\overline{S}$ (the payload is not dualized).
