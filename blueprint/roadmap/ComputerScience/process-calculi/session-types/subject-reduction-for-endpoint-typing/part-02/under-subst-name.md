# Extending a substitution under a binder raises its cutoff

For every name $b$ and natural number $k$, the extension under one binder of the name substitution
$x \mapsto x\{b/k\}$ is $x \mapsto x\{\uparrow_0 b/k+1\}$; here $x\{b/k\}$ replaces the variable $k$
by $b$, fixes smaller indices and lowers larger ones by one.
