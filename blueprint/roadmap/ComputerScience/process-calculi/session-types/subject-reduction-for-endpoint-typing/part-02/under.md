# Extension of a name map under a binder

Let $f$ be a map from names to names. Its extension under one binder is the map $f^{\uparrow}$ with
$f^{\uparrow}(\mathrm{v}_0) = \mathrm{v}_0$, $f^{\uparrow}(\mathrm{v}_{i+1}) = \uparrow_0
f(\mathrm{v}_i)$ and $f^{\uparrow}(\mathrm{c}_a) = \uparrow_0 f(\mathrm{c}_a)$, where $\uparrow_0$
raises a variable index by one: the new binder is fixed and outer names are transformed by $f$ and
shifted past the binder.
