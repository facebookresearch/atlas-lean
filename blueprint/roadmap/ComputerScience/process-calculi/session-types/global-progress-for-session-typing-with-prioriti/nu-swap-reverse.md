# Swapping two adjacent restrictions back preserves ranked typing

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process. If $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n \nu\,\nu\,(p[0 \leftrightarrow 1])$, where $p[0 \leftrightarrow 1]$ exchanges
the names $\mathrm{v}_0$ and $\mathrm{v}_1$ of $p$ (the two innermost bound names), then $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n \nu\,\nu\,p$.
