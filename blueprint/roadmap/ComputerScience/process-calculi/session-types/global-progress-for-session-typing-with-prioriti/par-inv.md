# Parallel inversion for ranked typing

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$, $q$ processes. If $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p \mid q$, then there are ownership contexts $\Gamma$ and $\Xi$ with disjoint
domains such that $\Delta = \Gamma \cup \Xi$, $\Gamma \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$ and $\Xi
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n q$.
