# Binding a fresh dual pair preserves balance

Let $\Delta$ be a balanced ownership context (whenever $\Delta(e) = c$, also $\Delta(\bar e) = \bar
c$) and $c$ a capability. Then $\nu_c\Delta$ is balanced, where $\nu_c\Delta$ raises the index of
every variable name of an endpoint of $\Delta$ by one and adds $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and
$\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
