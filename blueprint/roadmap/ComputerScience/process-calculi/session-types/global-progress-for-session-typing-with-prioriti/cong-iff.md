# Structural congruence preserves ranked typing

Let $p$ and $q$ be structurally congruent processes, $p \equiv q$ (congruence generated by
commutativity, associativity and unit $\mathbf{0}$ of $\mid$, scope extrusion $(\nu\,p) \mid q
\equiv \nu\,(p \mid \uparrow_0 q)$, $\nu\,\mathbf{0} \equiv \mathbf{0}$, exchange of adjacent
restrictions and the replication law $!p \equiv p \mid {!p}$). Then for every natural number $n$ and
ownership context $\Delta$, $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$ if and only if $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n q$.
