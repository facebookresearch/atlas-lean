# Progress for balanced ranked guard frontiers

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a balanced ownership context (whenever $\Delta(e) = c$, also
$\Delta(\bar e) = \bar c$) and $p$ a guard frontier with $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$. Then $p
\equiv \mathbf{0}$ or $p \to q$ for some process $q$.
