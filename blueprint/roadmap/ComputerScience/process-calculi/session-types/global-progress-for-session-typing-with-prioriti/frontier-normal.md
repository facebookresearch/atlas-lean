# Frontier normal form of finite networks

A process is in frontier normal form if it is of the form $\nu\,\nu \cdots \nu\,p$ (zero or more
restrictions) where $p$ is a guard frontier, i.e. a parallel composition of $\mathbf{0}$s and
output- or input-prefixed processes. Inductively: every guard frontier is in frontier normal form,
and if $p$ is, so is $\nu\,p$.
