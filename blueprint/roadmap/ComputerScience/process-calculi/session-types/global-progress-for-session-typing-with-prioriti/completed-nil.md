# Completed ranked frontiers are congruent to nil

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context all of whose capabilities have head
$\mathrm{end}$, and $p$ a guard frontier (a parallel composition of $\mathbf{0}$s and prefixed
processes). If $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$, then $p \equiv \mathbf{0}$.
