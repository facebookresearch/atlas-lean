# Active delegated payloads rank after the guard

Let $\Delta$ be an ownership context, $e$, $d$ distinct endpoints, $c$ a capability and $k$ a
natural number. If $\mathrm{Min}_\Delta(e,k)$ holds (every endpoint other than $e$ with an active
capability in $\Delta$ has rank $> k$), $\Delta(d) = c$ and the protocol of $c$ does not have head
$\mathrm{end}$, then $k$ is strictly less than the rank of $c$.
