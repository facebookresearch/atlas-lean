# Ranked subject reduction at balanced interfaces

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a balanced ownership context (whenever $\Delta(e) = c$, also
$\Delta(\bar e) = \bar c$) and $p$, $q$ processes with $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$ and $p \to
q$. Then there is a balanced ownership context $\Xi$ with $\Xi \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n q$ such that
$\Xi = \Delta$ or $\Xi = \Delta[e \mapsto (k,S')][\bar e \mapsto (k,\overline{S'})]$ for an endpoint
$e$ with $\Delta(e) = (k,S)$, $\Delta(\bar e) = (k,\overline{S})$ and $\mathrm{unf}(S)$ a send with
continuation $S'$.
