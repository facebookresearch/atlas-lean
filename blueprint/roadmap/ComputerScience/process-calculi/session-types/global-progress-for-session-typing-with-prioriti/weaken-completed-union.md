# Weakening ranked typing by a disjoint completed context

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$, $\Theta$ ownership contexts with disjoint domains and $p$ a
process. Suppose every capability of $\Theta$ has head $\mathrm{end}$ and every endpoint of $\Theta$
has a name scoped at depth $n$. If $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$, then $\Delta \cup \Theta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$.
