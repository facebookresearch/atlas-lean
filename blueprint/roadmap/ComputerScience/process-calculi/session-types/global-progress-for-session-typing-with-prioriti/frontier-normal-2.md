# Ranked-typed processes have a typed frontier normal form

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process with $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n p$. Then there is a process $q$ in frontier normal form (a guard frontier
under zero or more restrictions) with $p \equiv q$ and $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}}_n q$.
