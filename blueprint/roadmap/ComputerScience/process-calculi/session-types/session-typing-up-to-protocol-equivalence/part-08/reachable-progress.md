# Progress at reachable states of closed conversion-ranked networks

Let $n$ be a natural number and $p$, $q$ processes. If $\emptyset \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$
and $q$ is reachable from $p$ by finitely many (possibly zero) reduction steps, then $q \equiv
\mathbf{0}$ or $q \to r$ for some process $r$.
