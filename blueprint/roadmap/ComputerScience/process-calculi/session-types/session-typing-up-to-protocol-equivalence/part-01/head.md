# Tree-equivalent protocols have matching heads

Let $S$, $T$ be protocols with $S \simeq T$. Then $\mathrm{unf}(S)$ and $\mathrm{unf}(T)$ are
related by $\mathcal{H}(\simeq)$: both are $\mathrm{end}$, or both are name sends (respectively
receives) with tree-equivalent continuations, or both are endpoint sends (respectively receives)
with the same polarity and rank and tree-equivalent payloads and continuations.
