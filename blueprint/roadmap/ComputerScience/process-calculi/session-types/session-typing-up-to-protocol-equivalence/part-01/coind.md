# Coinduction principle for protocol tree equivalence

Let $\mathcal{R}$ be a binary relation on protocols such that for all $A \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} B$
the heads $\mathrm{unf}(A)$ and $\mathrm{unf}(B)$ are related by the head match
$\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R})$. Then $S \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} T$ implies $S \simeq T$.
