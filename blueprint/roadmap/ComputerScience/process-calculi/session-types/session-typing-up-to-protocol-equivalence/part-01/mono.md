# Head matching is monotone in the child relation

Let $\mathcal{R} \subseteq \mathcal{S}$ be binary relations on protocols and $S$, $T$ protocols. If
$S$ and $T$ are related by the head match $\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{R})$ (equal heads with
$\mathcal{R}$-related payloads and continuations), then they are related by
$\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{S})$.
