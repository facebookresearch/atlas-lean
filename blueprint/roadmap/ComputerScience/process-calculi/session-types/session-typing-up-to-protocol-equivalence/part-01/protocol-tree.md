# Labelled transition system of protocol-tree observations

The protocol-tree transition system has the protocols as states, the edge labels ${!\mathrm{N}}$,
${?\mathrm{N}}$, ${!(r,k,\beta)}$, ${?(r,k,\beta)}$ as labels, and as transitions the edges $S
\xrightarrow{\ell} T$ determined by the head of $S$ (a name action leads to its continuation; an
endpoint action leads to its payload under branch $\mathrm{pay}$ and to its continuation under
branch $\mathrm{cont}$).
