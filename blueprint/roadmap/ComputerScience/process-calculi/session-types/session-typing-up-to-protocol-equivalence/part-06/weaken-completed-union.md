# Weakening conversion ranked typing by a disjoint completed context

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$, $\Theta$ ownership contexts with disjoint domains such that
every capability of $\Theta$ has head $\mathrm{end}$ and every endpoint of $\Theta$ has a name
scoped at depth $n$, and $p$ a process. If $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$ (conversion
ranked typing), then $\Delta \cup \Theta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$.
