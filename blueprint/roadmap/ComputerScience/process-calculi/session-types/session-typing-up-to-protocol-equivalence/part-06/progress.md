# Progress for semantically balanced conversion frontiers

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a semantically balanced ownership context (whenever $\Delta(e)
= c$, there is $d$ with $\Delta(\bar e) = d$ and $d \approx \bar c$) and $p$ a guard frontier with
$\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$. Then $p \equiv \mathbf{0}$ or $p \to q$ for some process
$q$.
