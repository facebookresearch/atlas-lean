# Conversion head communication yields a semantic advance

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a semantically compatible ownership context, $a$, $b$ names
and $p$, $q$ processes. If $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n \overline{a}\langle b\rangle.p \mid
a(\cdot).q$, then there is a semantically compatible ownership context $\Xi$ with $\Xi
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p \mid q\{b\}$ that is a semantic advance of $\Delta$ (the subject
endpoint and its opposite advanced past a matching send and receive, with tree-equivalent dual
continuations, all other entries unchanged).
