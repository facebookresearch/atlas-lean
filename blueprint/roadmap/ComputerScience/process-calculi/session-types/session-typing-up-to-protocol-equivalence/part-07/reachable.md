# Finite runs keep conversion ranked typing at compatible interfaces

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a semantically compatible ownership context and $p$, $q$
processes such that $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$ and $q$ is reachable from $p$ by
finitely many (possibly zero) reduction steps. Then there is a semantically compatible ownership
context $\Xi$ with $\Xi \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n q$ that is reachable from $\Delta$ by finitely
many (possibly zero) semantic advances.
