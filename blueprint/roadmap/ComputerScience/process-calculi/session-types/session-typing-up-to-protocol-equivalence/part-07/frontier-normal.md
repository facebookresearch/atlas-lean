# Conversion-ranked processes have a typed frontier normal form

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process with $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$. Then there is a process $q$ in frontier normal form (a parallel
composition of $\mathbf{0}$s and prefixed processes under zero or more restrictions) with $p \equiv
q$ and $\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n q$.
