# Conversion ranked subject reduction at balanced interfaces

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ a semantically balanced ownership context (whenever $\Delta(e)
= c$, there is $d$ with $\Delta(\bar e) = d$ and $d \approx \bar c$) and $p$, $q$ processes with
$\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n p$ and $p \to q$. Then there is a semantically balanced
ownership context $\Xi$ with $\Xi \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n q$ such that $\Xi = \Delta$ or $\Xi$
is a semantic advance of $\Delta$.
