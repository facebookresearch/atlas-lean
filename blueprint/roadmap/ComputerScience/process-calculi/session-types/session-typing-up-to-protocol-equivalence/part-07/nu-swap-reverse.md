# Reverse restriction swap preserves conversion ranked typing

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process. If $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n \nu\,\nu\,(p[0 \leftrightarrow 1])$, where $p[0 \leftrightarrow 1]$
exchanges the names $\mathrm{v}_0$ and $\mathrm{v}_1$ of $p$, then $\Delta
\vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n \nu\,\nu\,p$.
