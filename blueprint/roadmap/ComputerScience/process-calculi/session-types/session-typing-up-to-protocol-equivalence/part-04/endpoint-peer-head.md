# Peer of an endpoint send head has an equivalent receive head

Let $c = (k_0,S)$ and $d = (k_1,U)$ be capabilities with $d \approx \bar c$ (equal ranks, and $U$
tree-equivalent to $\overline{S}$), and suppose $\mathrm{unf}(S) = {!(r,k,T)}.S'$. Then there are
protocols $T'$, $U'$ with $\mathrm{unf}(U) = {?(r,k,T')}.U'$, $T' \simeq T$ and $U' \simeq
\overline{S'}$.
