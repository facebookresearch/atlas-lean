# Head update leaves the transmitted payload slot unchanged

Let $\Gamma$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains, $e \ne f$ endpoints and $v$, $c$
capabilities with $\Gamma(f) = v$ and $\Theta(\bar e) = c$. Then for all capabilities $x$, $y$,
$(\Gamma \cup \Theta)[e \mapsto x][\bar e \mapsto y]$ maps $f$ to $v$.
