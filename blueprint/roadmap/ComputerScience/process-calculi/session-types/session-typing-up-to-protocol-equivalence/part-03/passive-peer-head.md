# Peer of a passive send head has an equivalent receive head

Let $c = (k,S)$ and $d = (k',U)$ be capabilities with $d \approx \bar c$ and let $S'$ be a protocol
with $\mathrm{unf}(S) = {!\mathrm{N}}.S'$. Then $\mathrm{unf}(U) = {?\mathrm{N}}.U'$ for some
protocol $U'$ with $U' \simeq \overline{S'}$.
