# Communicating subjects have semantically dual capabilities

Let $\Gamma$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains such that $\Gamma \cup \Theta$ is
semantically compatible, $e$, $f$ endpoints with the same name and $c$, $d$ capabilities with
$\Gamma(e) = c$ and $\Theta(f) = d$. Then $d \approx \bar c$: $d$ has the rank of $c$ and a protocol
tree-equivalent to the dual of the protocol of $c$.
