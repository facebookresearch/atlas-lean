# Owned capabilities with absent names are completed

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $p$ a process with $\Delta
\vdash^{\simeq}_n p$. Let $z$ be an endpoint whose name does not occur in $p$ and $t$ a capability
with $\Delta(z) = t$. Then the protocol of $t$ has head $\mathrm{end}$.
