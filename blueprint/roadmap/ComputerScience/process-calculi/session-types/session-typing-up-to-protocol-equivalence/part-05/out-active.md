# Conversion-ranked output subjects are active

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\Delta$ an ownership context, $a$, $b$ names and $p$ a process. If
$\Delta \vdash^{\mathrm{rk}\simeq}_n \overline{a}\langle b\rangle.p$, then there are an endpoint $e$
with name $a$ and a capability $c$ of rank $k$ such that $\Delta(e) = c$, the protocol of $c$ does
not have head $\mathrm{end}$, and $\mathrm{Min}_\Delta(e,k)$ (every other active endpoint of
$\Delta$ has rank $> k$).
