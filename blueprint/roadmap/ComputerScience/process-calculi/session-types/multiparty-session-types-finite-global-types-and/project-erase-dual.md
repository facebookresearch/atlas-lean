# Two-party projections are dual after erasure

Let participants, payload sorts and labels have decidable equality. Let `p ≠ q` be participants and `G` a global type all of whose interactions are between `p` and `q`. Then the projections of `G` onto `p` and onto `q` are both defined, and erasing the peers of the projection onto `p` gives the dual of the erased projection onto `q`. This bridge between multiparty and binary session types is not a theorem of the source.
