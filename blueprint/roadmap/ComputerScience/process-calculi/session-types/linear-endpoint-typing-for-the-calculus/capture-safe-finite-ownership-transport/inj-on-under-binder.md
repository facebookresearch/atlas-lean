# Rekeying under a binder is injective on fresh and lifted keys

Let $f$ be a map from names to names and $\Delta$ an ownership context such that $x^{r} \mapsto
f(x)^{r}$ is injective on the domain of $\Delta$. Then $x^{r} \mapsto f^{\uparrow}(x)^{r}$ is
injective on the set of endpoints whose name is $\mathrm{v}_0$ or which lie in the domain of
$\uparrow_0\Delta$; here $f^{\uparrow}$ fixes $\mathrm{v}_0$ and sends $\mathrm{v}_{i+1}$,
$\mathrm{c}_a$ to $\uparrow_0 f(\mathrm{v}_i)$, $\uparrow_0 f(\mathrm{c}_a)$.
