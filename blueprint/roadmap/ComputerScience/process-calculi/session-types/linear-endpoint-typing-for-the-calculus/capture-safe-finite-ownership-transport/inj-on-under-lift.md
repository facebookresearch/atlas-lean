# Injectivity of rekeying passes under a binder

Let $f$ be a map from names to names and $\Delta$ an ownership context such that $x^{r} \mapsto
f(x)^{r}$ is injective on the domain of $\Delta$. Then $x^{r} \mapsto f^{\uparrow}(x)^{r}$ is
injective on the domain of $\uparrow_0\Delta$, where $f^{\uparrow}$ fixes $\mathrm{v}_0$ and sends
$\mathrm{v}_{i+1}$ and $\mathrm{c}_a$ to $\uparrow_0 f(\mathrm{v}_i)$ and $\uparrow_0
f(\mathrm{c}_a)$, and $\uparrow_0\Delta$ raises every variable name index of an endpoint of $\Delta$
by one.
