# Rekeying under a binder is the lift of the outer rekeying

Let $f$ be a map from names to names and $\Delta$ an ownership context such that $x^{r} \mapsto
f(x)^{r}$ is injective on the domain of $\Delta$ (so that $x^{r} \mapsto f^{\uparrow}(x)^{r}$ is
injective on the domain of $\uparrow_0\Delta$). Then $f^{\uparrow}_*(\uparrow_0\Delta) =
\uparrow_0(f_*\Delta)$, where $g_*\Theta$ maps $g(x)^{r}$ to $\Theta(x^{r})$, $f^{\uparrow}$ is the
extension of $f$ under one binder ($\mathrm{v}_0$ fixed, other names $y$ sent to $\uparrow_0 f(y')$
with $y = \uparrow_0 y'$) and $\uparrow_0$ raises every variable index by one.
