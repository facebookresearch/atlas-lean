# Combined context after delegation and two carrier updates

Let $\Delta$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains, $e$, $f$, $d$ endpoints with $e$
in the domain of $\Delta$, $f$ in the domain of $\Theta$ and $e \ne d$, and $c$, $t$, $u$
capabilities with $\Delta(d) = u$. Then

$$
(\Delta \setminus d)[e \mapsto c] \cup \Theta[f \mapsto t][d \mapsto u]
= (\Delta \cup \Theta)[e \mapsto c][f \mapsto t].
$$
