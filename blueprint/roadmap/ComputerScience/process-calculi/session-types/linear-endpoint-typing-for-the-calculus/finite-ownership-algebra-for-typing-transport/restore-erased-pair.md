# Reinstalling the bound pair after erasing its slots

For every ownership context $\Gamma$ and capability $c$,

$$
((\Gamma \setminus \mathrm{v}_0^{-}) \setminus \mathrm{v}_0^{+})[\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c][\mathrm{v}_0^{-}
\mapsto \bar c] = \Gamma[\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c][\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c],
$$

where $\mathrm{v}_0$ is the variable with index $0$ and $\bar c$ has the rank of $c$ and the dual
protocol.
