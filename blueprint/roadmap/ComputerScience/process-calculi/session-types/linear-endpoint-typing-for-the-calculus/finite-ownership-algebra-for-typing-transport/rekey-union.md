# Collision-free rekeying commutes with a disjoint union

Let $f$ be a map from endpoints to endpoints and $\Delta$, $\Theta$ ownership contexts with disjoint
domains such that $f$ is injective on the union of their domains. Then $f_*(\Delta \cup \Theta) =
f_*\Delta \cup f_*\Theta$, where $f_*\Xi$ maps $f(d)$ to $\Xi(d)$.
