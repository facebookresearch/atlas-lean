# Context lifting preserves a disjoint union

Let $k$ be a natural number and $\Delta$, $\Theta$ ownership contexts with disjoint domains. Then
$\uparrow_k(\Delta \cup \Theta) = \uparrow_k\Delta \cup \uparrow_k\Theta$, where $\uparrow_k$ adds
one to every variable index $\ge k$ in the names of the endpoints.
