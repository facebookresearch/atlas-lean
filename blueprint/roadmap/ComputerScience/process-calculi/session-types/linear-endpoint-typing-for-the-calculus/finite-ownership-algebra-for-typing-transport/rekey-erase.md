# Collision-free rekeying commutes with erasing an owned key

Let $f$ be a map from endpoints to endpoints, $\Delta$ an ownership context and $e$ an endpoint in
the domain of $\Delta$, such that $f$ is injective on the domain of $\Delta$ (hence on that of
$\Delta \setminus e$). Then $f_*(\Delta \setminus e) = (f_*\Delta) \setminus f(e)$, where
$f_*\Theta$ maps $f(d)$ to $\Theta(d)$ for every $d$ in the domain of $\Theta$.
