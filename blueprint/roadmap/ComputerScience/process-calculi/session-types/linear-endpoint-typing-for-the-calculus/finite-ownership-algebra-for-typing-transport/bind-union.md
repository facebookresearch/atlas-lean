# Restriction binding of a disjoint union

Let $\Delta$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains and $c$ a capability. Then
$\nu_c(\Delta \cup \Theta) = \nu_c\Delta \cup \uparrow_0\Theta$, where $\uparrow_0\Theta$ raises
every variable name index of an endpoint of $\Theta$ by one and $\nu_c\Delta$ is $\uparrow_0\Delta$
extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
