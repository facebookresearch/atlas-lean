# Capability transfer with carrier updates preserves disjointness

Let $\Delta$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains, $e$, $f$, $d$ endpoints with $e$
in the domain of $\Delta$, $f$ in the domain of $\Theta$ and $e \ne d$, and $c$, $t$, $u$
capabilities. Then $(\Delta \setminus d)[e \mapsto c]$ and $\Theta[f \mapsto t][d \mapsto u]$ have
disjoint domains.
