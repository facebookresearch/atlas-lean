# Collision-free rekeying commutes with insertion

Let $f$ be a map from endpoints to endpoints, $\Delta$ an ownership context, $e$ an endpoint and $c$
a capability, such that $f$ is injective on the domain of $\Delta$ together with $e$. Then
$f_*(\Delta[e \mapsto c]) = (f_*\Delta)[f(e) \mapsto c]$, where $f_*\Theta$ maps $f(d)$ to
$\Theta(d)$.
