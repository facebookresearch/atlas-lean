# Updating owned keys on both sides of a disjoint union

Let $\Delta$, $\Theta$ be ownership contexts with disjoint domains, $e$ an endpoint in the domain of
$\Delta$, $d$ an endpoint in the domain of $\Theta$ and $c$, $t$ capabilities. Then $\Delta[e
\mapsto c] \cup \Theta[d \mapsto t] = (\Delta \cup \Theta)[e \mapsto c][d \mapsto t]$.
