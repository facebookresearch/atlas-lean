# Ownership-map equivalence from two-way lookup correspondence

Let $\Delta$, $\Xi$ be ownership contexts. Suppose that whenever $\Delta(e) = c$ there is $d$ with
$\Xi(e) = d$ and $c \approx d$, and whenever $\Xi(e) = d$ there is $c$ with $\Delta(e) = c$ and $c
\approx d$ (where $\approx$ on capabilities means equal ranks and tree-equivalent protocols). Then
$\Delta \approx \Xi$.
