# Binding equivalent fresh pairs preserves ownership-map equivalence

Let $\Delta \approx \Xi$ be equivalent ownership contexts and $c \approx d$ equivalent capabilities
(equal ranks, tree-equivalent protocols). Then $\nu_c\Delta \approx \nu_d\Xi$, where $\nu_c\Delta$
is $\uparrow_0\Delta$ (every variable name index raised by one) extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+}
\mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
