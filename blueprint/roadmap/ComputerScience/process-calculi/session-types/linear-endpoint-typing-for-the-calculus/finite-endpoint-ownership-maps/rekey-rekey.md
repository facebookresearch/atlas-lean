# Composition of collision-free rekeyings

Let $\Delta$ be an ownership context and $f$, $g$ maps from endpoints to endpoints such that $f$ is
injective on the domain of $\Delta$, $g$ is injective on the domain of $f_*\Delta$, and $g \circ f$
is injective on the domain of $\Delta$. Then $g_*(f_*\Delta) = (g \circ f)_*\Delta$.
