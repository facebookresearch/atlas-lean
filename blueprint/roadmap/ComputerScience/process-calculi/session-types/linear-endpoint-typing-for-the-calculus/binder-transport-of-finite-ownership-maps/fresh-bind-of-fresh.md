# Restriction binding preserves absence of an outer key

Let $\Delta$ be an ownership context, $x^{r}$ an endpoint not in the domain of $\Delta$ and $c$ a
capability. Then $(\uparrow_0 x)^{r}$ is not in the domain of $\nu_c\Delta$, the context
$\uparrow_0\Delta$ extended by $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$.
