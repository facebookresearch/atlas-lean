# Entries of a restriction-bound ownership map

For every capability $c$ and ownership context $\Delta$, the multiset of entries of $\nu_c\Delta$ is
the multiset of entries of $\uparrow_0\Delta$ together with the two entries $(\mathrm{v}_0^{+}, c)$
and $(\mathrm{v}_0^{-}, \bar c)$. In particular no entry of $\uparrow_0\Delta$ is overwritten by the
two new endpoints.
