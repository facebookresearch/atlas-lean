# Binding a fresh dual pair under restriction

Let $c$ be a capability and $\Delta$ an ownership context. The restriction context $\nu_c\Delta$ is
obtained from $\uparrow_0\Delta$ (every variable name index of an endpoint raised by one) by
inserting $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto c$ and then $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$, where
$\mathrm{v}_0$ is the name bound by the restriction and $\bar c$ has the rank of $c$ and the dual
protocol.
