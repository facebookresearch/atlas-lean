# Positive role lookup after restriction binding

For every capability $c$ and ownership context $\Delta$, the restriction context $\nu_c\Delta$ maps
$\mathrm{v}_0^{+}$ to $c$, where $\nu_c\Delta$ is $\uparrow_0\Delta$ with $\mathrm{v}_0^{+} \mapsto
c$ and then $\mathrm{v}_0^{-} \mapsto \bar c$ inserted.
