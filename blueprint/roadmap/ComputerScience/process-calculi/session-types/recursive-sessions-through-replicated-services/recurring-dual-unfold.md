# The dual recurring protocol has a receive of itself as head

The head of the dual recurring protocol is a name receive followed by the same protocol:
$\mathrm{unf}(\overline{R}) = {?\mathrm{N}}.\overline{R}$, where $R$ is the cursor at the single
node of the one-node send loop and $\overline{R}$ is the same cursor in reversed orientation.
