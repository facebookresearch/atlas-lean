# Service environment declaring the two triggers

For a polarity $r$, the service environment $\Gamma_r$ declares exactly the two constants $710$ and
$711$, with $\Gamma_r(710) = \sigma_s(r) = (0, r, \kappa)$ and $\Gamma_r(711) = \sigma_r(r) = (0,
\bar r, \bar\kappa)$ (priority, polarity, capability), where $\kappa = (7,R)$ is the recurring
capability.
