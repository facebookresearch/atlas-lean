# Invoking a replicated service is a reduction step

For every natural number $s$, name $b$ and process $q$,

$$
\overline{\mathrm{c}_s}\langle b\rangle.\mathbf{0} \mid {!\mathrm{c}_s(\cdot).q} \;\to\; q\{b\} \mid {!\mathrm{c}_s(\cdot).q},
$$

where $q\{b\}$ replaces the variable $\mathrm{v}_0$ of $q$ by $b$ and lowers larger indices by one.
