# Substitution instantiates the receiver worker's endpoint

For every name $b$, $W_r(\mathrm{v}_0)\{b\} = W_r(b)$, where $W_r(x) =
x(\cdot).\overline{\mathrm{c}_{711}}\langle \uparrow_0 x\rangle.\mathbf{0}$ and $p\{b\}$ replaces
$\mathrm{v}_0$ by $b$ (lifted under binders) and lowers larger variable indices by one.
