# Offered service communicates with a matching shared output

Let $s$ be a natural number and $p$ a process that offers $\mathrm{c}_s$ persistently: $p$ contains,
under parallel compositions and restrictions only, a replication $!q$ such that $q$ has an unguarded
input on $\mathrm{c}_s$ (under parallel compositions, restrictions and replications only). Then for
every name $b$ and process $c$, $\overline{\mathrm{c}_s}\langle b\rangle.c \mid p$ reduces in one
π-calculus step to some process.
