# Semantic advances of the recurring interface are the identity

Let $b$ be a name, $r$ a polarity and $\Xi$ an ownership context that is a semantic advance of
$I_{b,r} = \{b^{r} \mapsto \kappa\} \cup \{b^{\bar r} \mapsto \bar\kappa\}$. Then $\Xi = I_{b,r}$. A
semantic advance of $\Delta$ is a context $\Delta[e \mapsto (k,S')][\bar e \mapsto (k',U')]$ where
$\Delta(e) = (k,S)$, $\Delta(\bar e) = (k',U)$ with $(k',U) \approx (k,\overline{S})$, the heads of
$S$ and $U$ are a matching send and receive (of a name, or of endpoints with the same polarity and
rank and tree-equivalent payloads) with continuations $S'$ and $U'$, and $U'$ is tree-equivalent to
$\overline{S'}$. Since the head of $R$ is ${!\mathrm{N}}.R$, every such advance returns $I_{b,r}$.
