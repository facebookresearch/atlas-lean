# Selection preserves persistent offers

Let $s$ be a natural number and $p$, $q$ processes such that $q$ is obtained from $p$ by
guarded-choice resolution (repeatedly replacing a top-level choice by one of its branches). If $p$
offers $\mathrm{c}_s$ persistently (it contains, under parallel compositions and restrictions only,
a replication whose body has an unguarded input on $\mathrm{c}_s$), then so does $q$.
