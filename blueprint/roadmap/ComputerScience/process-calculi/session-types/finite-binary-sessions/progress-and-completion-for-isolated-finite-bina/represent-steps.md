# Session communication paths are π-calculus reduction paths

Let $r = (p,q)$ and $t = (p',q')$ be pairs of processes. If $t$ is reachable from $r$ by finitely
many (possibly zero) communication steps of binary configurations, then $p' \mid q'$ is reachable
from $p \mid q$ by finitely many (possibly zero) π-calculus reduction steps.
