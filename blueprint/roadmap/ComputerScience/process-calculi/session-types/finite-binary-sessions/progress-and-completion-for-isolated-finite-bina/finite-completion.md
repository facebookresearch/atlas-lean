# Finite completion of isolated finite binary sessions

Let $S$ be a finite binary session type, $\Gamma$ a session context, $a$ a channel number and $r =
(p,q)$ a pair of processes. If $\Gamma \vdash_0 p$, $\overline{\Gamma} \vdash_0 q$, $\Gamma(c) =
\mathrm{end}$ for every $c \ne a$ and $\Gamma(a) = S$, then $(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{0})$ is reachable
from $r$ by finitely many (possibly zero) communication steps of binary configurations.

The result concerns one isolated finite session; it says nothing about recursion or about
normalisation of arbitrary π-calculus processes.
