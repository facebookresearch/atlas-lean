# Advancing the sole channel keeps the single-channel condition

Let $\Gamma$ be a session context, $a$ a channel number and $S$ a session type. If $\Gamma(c) =
\mathrm{end}$ for every $c \ne a$, then $\Gamma[a \mapsto S]$ (which maps $a$ to $S$ and agrees with
$\Gamma$ elsewhere) also maps every $c \ne a$ to $\mathrm{end}$.
