# Communication step of a binary configuration

The communication step is the relation $\to$ on pairs of π-calculus processes generated by the
following two rules, for every natural number $a$, name $b$ and processes $p$, $q$:

- $(\overline{\mathrm{c}_a}\langle b\rangle.p,\ \mathrm{c}_a(\cdot).q) \to (p,\ q\{b\})$;
- $(\mathrm{c}_a(\cdot).p,\ \overline{\mathrm{c}_a}\langle b\rangle.q) \to (p\{b\},\ q)$.

Here $\mathrm{c}_a$ is the free channel constant numbered $a$, $\overline{\mathrm{c}_a}\langle
b\rangle.p$ outputs $b$ on $\mathrm{c}_a$ and continues as $p$, the input $\mathrm{c}_a(\cdot).q$
binds de Bruijn index $0$ in $q$, and $q\{b\}$ replaces the variable $0$ of $q$ by $b$ (lifting $b$
under binders) and lowers every larger variable index by one.
