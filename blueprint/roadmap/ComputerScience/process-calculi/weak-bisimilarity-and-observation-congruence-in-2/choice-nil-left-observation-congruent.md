# The inactive process is a left unit for choice up to observation congruence in finite CCS

Let $A$ be a type of actions with a distinguished silent action $\tau$, and consider the finite CCS processes over $A$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ by prefix $a.P$, choice $P + Q$ and parallel composition $P \mid Q$) with the transitions of finite CCS without communication. For all processes $P$, $\mathbf{0} + P$ and $P$ are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) in this transition system.
