# Parallel composition preserves observation congruence in CCS with communication

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality, and consider the finite CCS processes whose actions are CCS labels (the silent label $\tau$, inputs and outputs on names) with the transition relation of CCS with communication, in which an input and an output on the same name performed by the two components of a parallel composition synchronize to $\tau$. Write $P \Rightarrow P'$ when $P$ reaches $P'$ by zero or more $\tau$-transitions. A weak transition $P \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} P'$ is either the trivial one $P \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} P$ or a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$. Two processes are weakly bisimilar, $P \approx Q$, when some bisimulation of the saturated system (same states, the weak transitions as transitions) relates them. A rooted weak transition from $P$ to $P'$ with label $\mu$ is a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$; it always contains the actual $\mu$-transition, also when $\mu = \tau$. Two processes are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) when every transition $P \xrightarrow{\mu} P'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $Q$ with label $\mu$ to some $Q'$ with $P' \approx Q'$, and every transition $Q \xrightarrow{\mu} Q'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $P$ with label $\mu$ to some $P'$ with $P' \approx Q'$. For all processes $P_1, P_2, Q_1, Q_2$: if $P_1$ and $P_2$ are observation congruent and $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ are observation congruent, then $P_1 \mid Q_1$ and $P_2 \mid Q_2$ are observation congruent.
