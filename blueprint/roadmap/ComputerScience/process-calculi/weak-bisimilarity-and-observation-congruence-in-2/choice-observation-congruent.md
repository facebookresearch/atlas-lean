# Choice preserves observation congruence in finite CCS

Let $A$ be a type of actions with a distinguished silent action $\tau$, and consider the finite CCS processes over $A$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ by prefix $a.P$, choice $P + Q$ and parallel composition $P \mid Q$) with the transitions of finite CCS without communication. For all processes $P_1, P_2, Q_1, Q_2$: if $P_1$ and $P_2$ are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) and $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ are observation congruent, then $P_1 + Q_1$ and $P_2 + Q_2$ are observation congruent.
