# A silent prefix is invisible to weak bisimilarity in finite CCS

Let $A$ be a type of actions with a distinguished silent action $\tau$, and consider the finite CCS processes over $A$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ by prefix $a.P$, choice $P + Q$ and parallel composition $P \mid Q$) with the transitions of finite CCS without communication. For every process $P$, the silent prefix $\tau.P$ is weakly bisimilar to $P$: some bisimulation of the saturated system, whose transitions are the weak transitions, relates $\tau.P$ to $P$.
