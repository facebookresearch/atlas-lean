# Prefix preserves observation congruence in finite CCS

Let $A$ be a type of actions with a distinguished silent action $\tau$, and consider the finite CCS processes over $A$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ by prefix $a.P$, choice $P + Q$ and parallel composition $P \mid Q$) with the transitions of finite CCS without communication. For every action $a$ and all processes $P$, $Q$: if $P$ and $Q$ are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar), then so are $a.P$ and $a.Q$.
