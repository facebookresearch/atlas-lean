# Prefix turns weak bisimilarity into observation congruence in finite CCS

Let $A$ be a type of actions with a distinguished silent action $\tau$, and consider the finite CCS processes over $A$ (built from $\mathbf{0}$ by prefix $a.P$, choice $P + Q$ and parallel composition $P \mid Q$) with the transitions of finite CCS without communication. Write $P \Rightarrow P'$ when $P$ reaches $P'$ by zero or more $\tau$-transitions. A weak transition $P \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} P'$ is either the trivial one $P \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} P$ or a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$. Two processes are weakly bisimilar, $P \approx Q$, when some bisimulation of the saturated system (same states, the weak transitions as transitions) relates them. A rooted weak transition from $P$ to $P'$ with label $\mu$ is a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$; it always contains the actual $\mu$-transition, also when $\mu = \tau$. Two processes are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) when every transition $P \xrightarrow{\mu} P'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $Q$ with label $\mu$ to some $Q'$ with $P' \approx Q'$, and every transition $Q \xrightarrow{\mu} Q'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $P$ with label $\mu$ to some $P'$ with $P' \approx Q'$. For every action $a$ and all processes $P$, $Q$: if $P \approx Q$, then $a.P$ and $a.Q$ are observation congruent.
