# Multistep transitions map into the existential abstraction

Let $L$ be a labelled transition system with states $S$ and labels $\Lambda$, given by its transition relation $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$; its existential abstraction along a function $\alpha : S \to T$ is the system $L_\alpha$ on states $T$ with the same labels in which $a \xrightarrow{\mu} b$ holds exactly when $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$ in $L$ for some $s, t \in S$ with $\alpha(s) = a$ and $\alpha(t) = b$. A multistep transition $s \xrightarrow{\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n} t$ is a chain of transitions $s = s_0 \xrightarrow{\mu_1} s_1 \cdots \xrightarrow{\mu_n} s_n = t$ (for the empty list, $t = s$), and $t$ is reachable from $s$ when such a chain exists for some finite list of labels, possibly empty. For all $s, t \in S$ and every finite list of labels $\mu_1, \dots, \mu_n$, if $s \xrightarrow{\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n} t$ in $L$, then $\alpha(s) \xrightarrow{\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n} \alpha(t)$ in $L_\alpha$, with the same list of labels.
