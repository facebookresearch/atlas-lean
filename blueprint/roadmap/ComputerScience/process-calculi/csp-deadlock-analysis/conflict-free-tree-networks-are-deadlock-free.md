# Tree networks free of strong conflict are deadlock-free

Let $V$ be a [network](networks-of-csp-processes.md) satisfying assumptions (A)–(E) whose communication graph is a tree. If $V$ is free of strong conflict (see [Ungranted requests and conflicts in CSP networks](ungranted-requests-and-conflicts.md)), then $V$ is deadlock-free.
