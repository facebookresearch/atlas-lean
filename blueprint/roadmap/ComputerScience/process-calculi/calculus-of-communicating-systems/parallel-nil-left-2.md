# The inactive process is a left unit for parallel composition in recursive CCS

Let the channel names have decidable equality and let $\Delta$ be a recursive specification, which assigns to every process constant $A$ a recursive CCS process $\Delta(A)$, its defining equation. Strong bisimilarity below is taken in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$, in which a constant has exactly the transitions of its defining process; no guardedness of $\Delta$ is assumed. For every recursive CCS process $P$, the process $0 \mid P$ is strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$ to $P$.
