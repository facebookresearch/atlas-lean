# Restriction distributes over choice up to strong bisimilarity

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. For every finite list $L$ of channel names and all static CCS processes $P$ and $Q$, the process $(P + Q) \setminus L$ is strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system of static CCS to $(P \setminus L) + (Q \setminus L)$.
