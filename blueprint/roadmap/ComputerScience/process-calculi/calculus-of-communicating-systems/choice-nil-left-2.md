# The inactive process is a left unit for choice in static CCS

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. For every static CCS process $P$ (a finite CCS process built from $0$ by action prefix, choice, parallel composition, restriction by a finite list of channel names and relabelling by a finite relabelling table), the choice $0 + P$ of the inactive process $0$ and $P$ is strongly bisimilar to $P$ in the labelled transition system of static CCS.
