# Parallel composition is commutative up to strong bisimilarity in recursive CCS

Let the channel names have decidable equality and let $\Delta$ be a recursive specification, which assigns to every process constant $A$ a recursive CCS process $\Delta(A)$, its defining equation. Strong bisimilarity below is taken in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$, in which a constant has exactly the transitions of its defining process; no guardedness of $\Delta$ is assumed. For all recursive CCS processes $P$ and $Q$, the processes $P \mid Q$ and $Q \mid P$ are strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$.
