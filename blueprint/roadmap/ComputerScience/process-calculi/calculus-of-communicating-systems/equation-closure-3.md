# Reflexive closure of a relation on static CCS processes

Given a relation $E$ on static CCS processes (finite CCS processes built from $0$ by action prefix, choice, parallel composition, restriction by a finite list of channel names and relabelling by a finite relabelling table), its equation closure relates $P$ to $Q$ exactly when $P = Q$ or $E(P, Q)$; that is, it is the reflexive closure of $E$.
