# Parallel composition preserves strong bisimilarity in recursive CCS

Let the channel names have decidable equality and let $\Delta$ be a recursive specification, which assigns to every process constant $A$ a recursive CCS process $\Delta(A)$, its defining equation. Strong bisimilarity below is taken in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$, in which a constant has exactly the transitions of its defining process; no guardedness of $\Delta$ is assumed. For all recursive CCS processes $P_1, P_2, Q_1, Q_2$, if $P_1$ and $P_2$ are strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system generated by $\Delta$ and $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ are strongly bisimilar there, then $P_1 \mid Q_1$ and $P_2 \mid Q_2$ are strongly bisimilar there.
