# A blocked prefix under restriction is strongly bisimilar to the inactive process

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality. Let $L$ be a finite list of channel names and $\alpha$ a CCS action not permitted by $L$, that is, an input or an output on a channel that belongs to $L$. For every static CCS process $P$, the process $(\alpha.P) \setminus L$ is strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system of static CCS to the inactive process $0$.
