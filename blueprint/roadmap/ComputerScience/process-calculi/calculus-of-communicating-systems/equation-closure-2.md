# Reflexive closure of a relation on recursive CCS processes

Given a relation $E$ on recursive CCS processes (built from $0$ and process constants by action prefix, choice, parallel composition, restriction by a finite list of channel names and relabelling by a finite relabelling table), its equation closure relates $P$ to $Q$ exactly when $P = Q$ or $E(P, Q)$; that is, it is the reflexive closure of $E$.
