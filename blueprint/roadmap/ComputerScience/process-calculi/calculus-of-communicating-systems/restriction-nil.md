# Restricting the inactive process gives the inactive process

For every finite list $L$ of channel names, the restriction $0 \setminus L$ of the inactive process is strongly bisimilar in the labelled transition system of static CCS to $0$.

## Sources

- Robin Milner, *Communication and Concurrency*, Prentice Hall, 1989, Chapters 2–3, ISBN 978-0-13-115007-2.
