# Infinite state runs lift to labelled executions

Let $s_0, s_1, s_2, \dots$ be an [infinite run](omega-run.md) of a labelled transition system. Then there are labels $\mu_0, \mu_1, \mu_2, \dots$ such that $s_i \xrightarrow{\mu_i} s_{i+1}$ for every $i$: choosing a witnessing label at each step gives an infinite labelled execution.
