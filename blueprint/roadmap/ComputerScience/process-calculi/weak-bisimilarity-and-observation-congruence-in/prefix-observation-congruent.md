# Prefix turns weak bisimilarity into observation congruence in static CCS

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality, and consider the static CCS processes (prefix by a CCS label, choice, parallel composition with communication, restriction $P \setminus L$ by a finite list $L$ of names, and relabelling $P[f]$ by a finite relabelling $f$) with the transitions of static CCS. Write $P \Rightarrow P'$ when $P$ reaches $P'$ by zero or more $\tau$-transitions. A weak transition $P \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} P'$ is either the trivial one $P \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} P$ or a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$. Two processes are weakly bisimilar, $P \approx Q$, when some bisimulation of the saturated system (same states, the weak transitions as transitions) relates them. A rooted weak transition from $P$ to $P'$ with label $\mu$ is a composite $P \Rightarrow P_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} P_2 \Rightarrow P'$; it always contains the actual $\mu$-transition, also when $\mu = \tau$. Two processes are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) when every transition $P \xrightarrow{\mu} P'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $Q$ with label $\mu$ to some $Q'$ with $P' \approx Q'$, and every transition $Q \xrightarrow{\mu} Q'$ is matched by a rooted weak transition from $P$ with label $\mu$ to some $P'$ with $P' \approx Q'$. For every CCS label $\alpha$ and all processes $P$, $Q$: if $P \approx Q$, then $\alpha.P$ and $\alpha.Q$ are observation congruent.
