# Restriction by the empty list up to observation congruence in static CCS

Let the channel names form a type with decidable equality, and consider the static CCS processes (prefix by a CCS label, choice, parallel composition with communication, restriction $P \setminus L$ by a finite list $L$ of names, and relabelling $P[f]$ by a finite relabelling $f$) with the transitions of static CCS. For every process $P$, the restriction $P \setminus []$ by the empty list of names and $P$ are observation congruent (rooted weakly bisimilar) in this transition system.
