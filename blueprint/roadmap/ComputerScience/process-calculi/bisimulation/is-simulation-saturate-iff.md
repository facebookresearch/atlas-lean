# Simulations into the saturated system are simulations of the saturated system

Let $L$ be a labelled transition system with states $S$ and labels $\Lambda$, given by its transition relation $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$, and let $\tau \in \Lambda$ be a designated silent label. Write $s \Rightarrow t$ for the silent closure, the reflexive-transitive closure of the relation $s \xrightarrow{\tau} t$. The weak transitions $s \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} t$ are the trivial ones $s \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} s$ and the composites $s \Rightarrow s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2 \Rightarrow t$; the saturation $\hat L$ of $L$ is the system on the same states whose transitions are the weak transitions. A relation $R$ on $S$ is a simulation from a system $L_1$ to a system $L_2$ (both on states $S$ with labels $\Lambda$) when, whenever $s_1 \mathrel{R} s_2$ and $s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_1'$ in $L_1$, there is $s_2'$ with $s_2 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2'$ in $L_2$ and $s_1' \mathrel{R} s_2'$. For every relation $R$ on $S$, $R$ is a simulation from $\hat L$ to $\hat L$ if and only if $R$ is a simulation from $L$ to $\hat L$.
