# Rooted silent transitions are nonempty runs of silent steps

Let $L$ be a labelled transition system with states $S$ and labels $\Lambda$, given by its transition relation $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$, and let $\tau \in \Lambda$ be a designated silent label. Write $s \Rightarrow t$ for the silent closure, the reflexive-transitive closure of the relation $s \xrightarrow{\tau} t$. A rooted weak transition $s \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow}_{r} t$ means that $s \Rightarrow s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2 \Rightarrow t$ for some states $s_1, s_2$. Then for all states $s, t$, $s \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow}_{r} t$ if and only if $t$ is reached from $s$ by a nonempty finite chain of $\tau$-transitions, that is, $(s, t)$ lies in the transitive closure of the relation $s \xrightarrow{\tau} t$.
