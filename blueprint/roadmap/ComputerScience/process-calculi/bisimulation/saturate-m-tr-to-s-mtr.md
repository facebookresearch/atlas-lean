# Removing silent labels from a multistep transition of the saturated system

Let $L$ be a labelled transition system with states $S$ and labels $\Lambda$, given by its transition relation $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$, and let $\tau \in \Lambda$ be a designated silent label. Write $s \Rightarrow t$ for the silent closure, the reflexive-transitive closure of the relation $s \xrightarrow{\tau} t$. The weak transitions $s \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} t$ are the trivial ones $s \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} s$ and the composites $s \Rightarrow s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2 \Rightarrow t$; the saturation $\hat L$ of $L$ is the system on the same states whose transitions are the weak transitions. A multistep transition of $\hat L$ with label list $\nu_1, \dots, \nu_m$ is a chain $s = s_0 \stackrel{\nu_1}{\Longrightarrow} s_1 \cdots \stackrel{\nu_m}{\Longrightarrow} s_m = t$ of weak transitions. The saturated multistep transitions $s \stackrel{\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n}{\Longrightarrow} t$ are generated by two rules: $s \stackrel{[\,]}{\Longrightarrow} t$ whenever $s \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} t$ (equivalently $s \Rightarrow t$), and $s \stackrel{\mu\,\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n}{\Longrightarrow} t$ whenever $s \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} s'$ and $s' \stackrel{\mu_1 \cdots \mu_n}{\Longrightarrow} t$ for some $s'$ (the label $\mu$ may itself be $\tau$). Assume equality of labels is decidable, and for a list of labels write $\mathrm{vis}(\cdot)$ for the list with every occurrence of $\tau$ removed, the other labels kept in order. If $s$ reaches $t$ by a multistep transition of $\hat L$ with label list $\nu_1, \dots, \nu_m$, then $s \stackrel{\mathrm{vis}(\nu_1 \cdots \nu_m)}{\Longrightarrow} t$ is a saturated multistep transition.
