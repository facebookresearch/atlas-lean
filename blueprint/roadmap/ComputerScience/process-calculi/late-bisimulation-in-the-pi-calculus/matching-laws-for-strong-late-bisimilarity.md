# Matching laws for strong late bisimilarity

In the [late transition semantics](late-transition-semantics-of-the-pi-calculus.md), for [strong late bisimilarity](strong-late-bisimilarity.md) and every agent $P$: $[x = y] P \mathrel{\dot\sim} 0$ if $x \ne y$, and $[x = x] P \mathrel{\dot\sim} P$.
