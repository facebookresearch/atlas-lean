# Projection onto a signature

Let $S$ be an action signature and $\beta$ a finite sequence of actions. The projection $\beta|S$ of $\beta$ onto $S$ is the subsequence of the actions of $\beta$ that belong to $\mathrm{acts}(S)$, in order (written $\beta|A$ by Lynch and Tuttle for an automaton $A$).

## Sources

- N. A. Lynch, M. R. Tuttle, *An introduction to input/output automata*, CWI Quarterly 2(3), 1989, 219–246.
