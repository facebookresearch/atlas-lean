# Projection onto the first component and external projection commute

Let $S_1$ and $S_2$ be [strongly compatible](strongly-compatible.md) action signatures and $\gamma$ a finite sequence of actions. Keeping the external actions of the [composition](compose-signatures.md) $S_1 \cdot S_2$ in $\gamma$ and then [projecting](proj.md) onto $S_1$ gives the [external projection](ext-proj.md) onto $S_1$ of the projection $\gamma|S_1$.
