# Finite behaviour of an I/O automaton

Let $A$ be an [input/output automaton](ioautomaton.md). A finite sequence $\beta$ of actions is a finite behaviour of $A$ when $\beta$ is the [external projection](ext-proj.md) onto $\mathrm{sig}(A)$ of some finite [schedule](sched.md) of $A$: the external actions of the schedule, in order (Lynch and Tuttle, 1989, §3.1).

## Sources

- N. A. Lynch, M. R. Tuttle, *An introduction to input/output automata*, CWI Quarterly 2(3), 1989, 219–246: §3.1.
