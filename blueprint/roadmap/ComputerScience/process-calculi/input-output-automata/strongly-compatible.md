# Strongly compatible signatures

Two action signatures $S_1$ and $S_2$ are strongly compatible (Lynch and Tuttle, 1989, §3.2, for two signatures) when their output actions are disjoint, $\mathrm{out}(S_1) \cap \mathrm{out}(S_2) = \emptyset$, and the internal actions of each are not actions of the other: $\mathrm{int}(S_1) \cap \mathrm{acts}(S_2) = \emptyset$ and $\mathrm{int}(S_2) \cap \mathrm{acts}(S_1) = \emptyset$.

## Sources

- N. A. Lynch, M. R. Tuttle, *An introduction to input/output automata*, CWI Quarterly 2(3), 1989, 219–246: §3.2.
