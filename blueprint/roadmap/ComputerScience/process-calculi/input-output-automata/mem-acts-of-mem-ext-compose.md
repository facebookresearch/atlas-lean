# External actions of a composition

Let $S_1$ and $S_2$ be [strongly compatible](strongly-compatible.md) action signatures. Every external action of their [composition](compose-signatures.md) is an action of one of the components: $\mathrm{ext}(S_1 \cdot S_2) \subseteq \mathrm{acts}(S_1) \cup \mathrm{acts}(S_2)$.
