# Every partition-refinement iterate from round N on is strong bisimilarity

Let $L$ be a labelled transition system with states $S$ and labels $\Lambda$, given by its transition relation $s \xrightarrow{\mu} t$. The refinement iterates are the relations $\equiv_k$ on $S$ with $\equiv_0$ the total relation (every pair of states related) and $a \equiv_{k+1} b$ exactly when $a \equiv_k b$ and, for every label $\mu$, every transition $a \xrightarrow{\mu} a'$ is matched by some $b \xrightarrow{\mu} b'$ with $a' \equiv_k b'$, and every transition $b \xrightarrow{\mu} b'$ is matched by some $a \xrightarrow{\mu} a'$ with $a' \equiv_k b'$. A relation $R$ on $S$ is a (strong) bisimulation when, whenever $s_1 \mathrel{R} s_2$, for every label $\mu$ each transition $s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_1'$ is matched by some $s_2 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2'$ with $s_1' \mathrel{R} s_2'$, and each transition $s_2 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2'$ is matched by some $s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_1'$ with $s_1' \mathrel{R} s_2'$; two states are bisimilar when some bisimulation relates them. Assume $S$ is finite with $N$ elements; the label type $\Lambda$ is arbitrary (it need not be finite, and $L$ need not be finitely branching). Then for every $i \ge N$ and all states $a, b$, $a \equiv_i b$ if and only if $a$ and $b$ are bisimilar.
