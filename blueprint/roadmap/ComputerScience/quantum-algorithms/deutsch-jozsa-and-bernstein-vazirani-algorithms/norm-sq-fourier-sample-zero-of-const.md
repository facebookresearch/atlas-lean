# Deutsch–Jozsa algorithm, constant case

If $f$ is constant, measuring $H_n O_f H_n |0\cdots0\rangle$ gives $0\cdots0$ with probability $1$
(Cleve, Ekert, Macchiavello and Mosca, Section 3, after equation (3.6)): a constant function is
the case $s = 0$ of the Bernstein–Vazirani problem.

## Informally uses

- [The Bernstein–Vazirani algorithm outputs $s$ with certainty](norm-sq-fourier-sample-of-affine.md): applied with $s = 0$.
