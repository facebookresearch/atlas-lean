# Fourier sampling state

For $f \colon \mathbb{Z}_2^n \to \mathbb{Z}_2$, the Fourier sampling state is the vector

$$H_n\, O_f\, H_n\, |0\cdots0\rangle \in \mathbb{C}^{\mathbb{Z}_2^n},$$

where $H_n$ is the [Hadamard transform](hadamard.md), $O_f$ the [phase oracle](phase-oracle.md) and $|0\cdots0\rangle$ the basis vector at $0$: one query to the phase oracle between two Hadamard transforms (Bernstein and Vazirani, Section 8.4, Theorem 8.4.2; Cleve, Ekert, Macchiavello and Mosca, arXiv:quant-ph/9708016, Figure 4).
