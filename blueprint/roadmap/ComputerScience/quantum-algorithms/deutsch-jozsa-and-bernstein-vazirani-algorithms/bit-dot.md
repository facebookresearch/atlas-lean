# Inner product of bit strings modulo two

For a natural number $n$ and bit strings $s, x \in \mathbb{Z}_2^n$, their inner product modulo two is

$$s \cdot x = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} s_i x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_2$$

(Cleve, Ekert, Macchiavello and Mosca, arXiv:quant-ph/9708016, equation (3.2)). For $n = 0$ it is $0$.
