# The sign matrix is a Hadamard matrix

For every natural number $n$, the Sylvester sign matrix $S_n = \big((-1)^{s \cdot x}\big)_{s, x \in \mathbb{Z}_2^n}$ is a Hadamard matrix of order $2^n$: its entries are $\pm 1$ and its rows are pairwise orthogonal, $S_n S_n^{T} = 2^n I$. This follows from the orthogonality of the parity characters (Bernstein and Vazirani, Section 8.4).
