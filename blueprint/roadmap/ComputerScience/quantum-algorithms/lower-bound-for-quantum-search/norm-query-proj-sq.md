# Born rule for the query register

Let $\iota$ and $W$ be finite sets and $P_y$ the [query projection](query-proj.md) for $y \in \iota$ on $\ell^2(\iota \times W)$. Then for every $v \in \ell^2(\iota \times W)$,

$$\|P_y v\|^2 = \sum_{w \in W} |v(y, w)|^2,$$

the probability that measuring the query register of $v$ gives $y$ when $\|v\| = 1$.
