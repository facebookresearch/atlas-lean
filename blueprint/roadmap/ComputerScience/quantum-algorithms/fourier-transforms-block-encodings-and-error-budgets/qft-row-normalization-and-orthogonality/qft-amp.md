# Quantum Fourier transform amplitude

For a complex number `ω`, a real scale `s` and natural numbers `j` and `k`,
`qftAmp ω s j k = ω ^ (j * k) * s` is the amplitude from the input basis
index `j` to the output index `k`. With `ω = exp (2πi / N)` and `s = 1 / √N`
these are the entries of the quantum Fourier transform matrix on `N` basis
states.
