# Perturbation of a measurement probability under conjugation

Let `n` be a finite type, `U` and `V` unitary complex matrices on `n`, `P`
an orthogonal projection (a self-adjoint idempotent matrix), and `ψ` a unit
vector in `ℂ^n`. Then `|⟨ψ, U* P U ψ⟩ - ⟨ψ, V* P V ψ⟩| ≤ 2 ‖U - V‖`, where
`‖·‖` is the operator norm.
