# Norm inner le of ne

Let unit vectors `ψ x` split as `ψ x = u_false x + u_true x` with `u_false x` orthogonal to `u_true y` for all `x, y`, and with wrong-answer part `‖u_{¬f x} x‖² ≤ ε` for every `x`, where `0 ≤ ε ≤ 1/2`. Then inputs with different answers have nearly orthogonal states: `f x ≠ f y` implies `|⟨ψ x, ψ y⟩| ≤ 2 √(ε(1 - ε))`.
