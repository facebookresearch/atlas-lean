# Block-encoding of a scaled projection

If `P` is an orthogonal projection (a self-adjoint idempotent complex
matrix) on a finite type `ι` and `α ≥ 0`, then `projEncoding P` is an `(α,
0)` block-encoding of `α • P` with a single ancilla qubit (`Fin 2`) and
reference vector `0`: it is unitary and its ancilla block, scaled by `α`,
equals `α • P` exactly.
