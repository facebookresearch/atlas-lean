# Quick Sort expected Time randomly Permute le harmonic

After an ideal Fisher–Yates shuffle of a fixed duplicate-free vector of length $n$, the subsequent quicksort makes at most $2 n H_n$ comparisons in expectation, where $H_n$ is the $n$-th harmonic number. This is not a bound on the combined shuffle-plus-sort cost, and does not claim a PRNG implements the ideal law.
