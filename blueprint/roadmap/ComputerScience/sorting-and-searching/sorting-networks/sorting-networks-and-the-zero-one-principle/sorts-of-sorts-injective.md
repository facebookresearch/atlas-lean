# Sorting distinct inputs suffices

Let $C$ be a comparator network on $n$ wires such that, for every injective assignment
$w : \{0, \dots, n - 1\} \to \mathbb{N}$ of pairwise distinct natural numbers to the wires, the
output of $C$ on $w$ is monotone in the wire order. Then for every linearly ordered type $\alpha$,
the network $C$ sorts every input over $\alpha$.
