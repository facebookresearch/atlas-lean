# A treap is heap-ordered exactly when its priority tree is a min-heap

A treap tree $t$ is heap-ordered (the priority of every node is at most the priority of every node in its left and right subtrees) if and only if the priority tree of $t$ is min-heap ordered for the usual order $\le$ on natural numbers: at every node its key is at most every key in its left and right subtrees.
