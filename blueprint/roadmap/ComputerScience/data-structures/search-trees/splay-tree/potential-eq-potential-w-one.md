# Unit-weight potential is the splay-tree potential

With every weight equal to one, the [weighted potential](potential-w.md) of a binary tree equals
its [splay-tree potential](potential.md): $\Phi_1(t) = \Phi(t)$. This is how the [unit-weight
access lemma](access-lemma.md) is obtained from the [access lemma](access-lemma-weighted.md).

## Background

- [Unit weights give the number of nodes](weight-one.md): the term-by-term identity.
