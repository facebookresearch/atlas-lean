# B-trees of a given minimum degree

For keys with a relation $<$, a tree $T$ is a B-tree of minimum degree $t \in \mathbb{N}$ when
$t \ge 2$, $T$ is ordered, and $T$ is
root-valid at some height. This is the invariant preserved by insertion and by building a tree
from a list.

## Sources

- T. H. Cormen, C. E. Leiserson, R. L. Rivest and C. Stein, *Introduction to Algorithms*, 3rd ed.,
  MIT Press, 2009, Section 18.1 (B-trees of minimum degree $t \ge 2$).
