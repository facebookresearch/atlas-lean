# Attainment of the offline minimum

Fix $n \ge 1$ and an initial `Tree n`, a strict binary search tree containing every
key of $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$. For every finite list of requests in $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$,
there is a natural-number cost attained by a legal plan and least among the costs of
legal plans from that initial tree.
