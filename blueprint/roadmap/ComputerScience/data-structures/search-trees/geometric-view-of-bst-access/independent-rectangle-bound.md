# Independent rectangle bound

Let $X$ be a permutation access sequence, so that no two points of $P(X)$ share a row or a column. A rectangle spanned by two points of $P(X)$ is *unsatisfied* if it contains no other point of $P(X)$. Two rectangles are *independent* if both are unsatisfied and no corner of either rectangle lies strictly inside the other. The *independent rectangle bound* $\mathrm{IRB}(X)$ is the maximum size of a pairwise independent family of rectangles spanned by points of $P(X)$.
