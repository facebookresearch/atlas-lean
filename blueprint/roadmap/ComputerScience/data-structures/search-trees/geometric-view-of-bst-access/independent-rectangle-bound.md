# Independent rectangle bound

Two axis-parallel rectangles are *independent* if neither contains a corner of the other in its interior or on its boundary except as a shared corner, that is, they do not cross so as to admit a common satisfying point. A rectangle spanned by two points of $P(X)$ is *unsatisfied* if it contains no other point of $P(X)$. The *independent rectangle bound* $\mathrm{IRB}(X)$ is the maximum size of a pairwise independent family of unsatisfied rectangles spanned by points of $P(X)$.
