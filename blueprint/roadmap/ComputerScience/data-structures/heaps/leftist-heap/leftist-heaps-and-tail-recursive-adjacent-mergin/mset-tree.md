# Multiset of elements of a leftist heap

The multiset of elements of a leftist heap is defined recursively: the empty heap gives the empty
multiset, and a node $\langle k, x, \ell, r \rangle$ gives the sum of the one-element multiset
$\{x\}$ and the multisets of $\ell$ and of $r$. Elements are counted with multiplicity.
