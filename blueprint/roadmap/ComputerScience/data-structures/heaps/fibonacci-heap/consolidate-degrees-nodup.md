# Consolidation yields distinct root degrees

Over a linear order, for every list of Fibonacci trees, the trees in its consolidation have
pairwise distinct stored degrees. Here the consolidation of the empty list is the empty list, and
the consolidation of a list with first tree $t$ and remaining list $L$ is the insertion of $t$
into the consolidation of $L$ (linking it with a tree of equal stored degree and carrying the
link forward, as in binary addition).
