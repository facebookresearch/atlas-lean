# Peeling threshold of random hypergraphs

For $d \ge 3$ the *peeling threshold* $c_d^{\Delta}$ is the supremum of load factors $c$ such that the random $d$-uniform hypergraph with $m$ vertices and $cm$ hyperedges has an empty $2$-core with probability $1 - o(1)$; equivalently, repeatedly deleting a vertex of degree at most one together with its incident hyperedge eventually removes all hyperedges. Numerically $c_3^{\Delta} \approx 0.818$, strictly below the orientability threshold $c_3^* \approx 0.918$.
