# Find depth is at most the root's rank

Let $p : \alpha \to \alpha$ be a parent map, whose fixed points are the roots, and let
$r : \alpha \to \mathbb{N}$ be a rank function such that every nonroot has smaller rank than
its parent: $r(z) < r(p(z))$ whenever $p(z) \ne z$. If $x \in \alpha$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$
are such that $p^i(x)$ is not a root for every $i < k$, and $p^k(x)$ is a root, then
$k \le r(p^k(x))$: the depth of $x$ is at most the rank of its root.
