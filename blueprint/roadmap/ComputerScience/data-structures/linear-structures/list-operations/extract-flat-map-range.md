# Extracting a member from a uniform-width concatenation

Let $m$ and $w$ be natural numbers and $B_0, B_1, \dots$ a sequence of lists with $B_i$ of length
$w$ for every $i < m$. For every $i < m$, the elements of the concatenation
$B_0 B_1 \cdots B_{m-1}$ at positions $i w, i w + 1, \dots, i w + w - 1$ (positions counted from
$0$) form the list $B_i$.
