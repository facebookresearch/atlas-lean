# Equality of uniformly split lists from equal block extracts

Let $w$ and $m$ be natural numbers and let $L$ and $M$ be lists of length $m w$. For a list and
natural numbers $a \le b$, its block from $a$ to $b$ is the list of its elements at positions
$a, a+1, \dots, b-1$ (positions counted from $0$). If for every $i < m$ the blocks of $L$ and $M$
from $i w$ to $i w + w$ are equal, then $L = M$.
