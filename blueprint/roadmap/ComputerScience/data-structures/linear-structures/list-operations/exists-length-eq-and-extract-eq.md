# Every equal-length list family is realized by its concatenation

Let $w$ and $m$ be natural numbers and $B_0, B_1, \dots$ a sequence of lists with $B_i$ of length
$w$ for every $i < m$. Then there is a list $L$ of length $m w$ such that for every $i < m$ the
elements of $L$ at positions $i w, \dots, i w + w - 1$ (positions counted from $0$) form the list
$B_i$.
