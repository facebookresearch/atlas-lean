# Text with an appended sentinel

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet and $\alpha_\bot$ the alphabet $\alpha$ with a new letter $\bot$
adjoined, ordered below every letter of $\alpha$ when $\alpha$ is ordered. For a text
$t = t_0 \cdots t_{n-1}$ over $\alpha$, the text with sentinel is the word
$t\bot = t_0 \cdots t_{n-1} \bot$ over $\alpha_\bot$ of length $n + 1$. The sentinel occurs in it
exactly once, as its last letter.
