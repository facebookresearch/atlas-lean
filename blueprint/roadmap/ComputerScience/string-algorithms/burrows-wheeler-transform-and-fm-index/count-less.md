# Count of smaller letters in the FM-index

Let $\alpha$ be a linearly ordered alphabet and $\alpha_\bot$ the alphabet $\alpha$ with a new least
letter $\bot$ adjoined. For a list $L$ over $\alpha_\bot$ and a letter $c \in \alpha_\bot$, $C(c)$
is the number of entries of $L$, counted with multiplicity, that are strictly smaller than $c$.
