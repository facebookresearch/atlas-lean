# The sorted rotations of $t\bot$ form its suffix array

For every text $t$ over a linearly ordered alphabet, the list of sorted rotations of $t$ (the
positions $0, \dots, |t|$ of $t\bot$, the text followed by a sentinel smaller than every letter,
sorted by their rotations) equals the suffix array of $t\bot$ (the same positions sorted by their
suffixes).
