# Rotations of $t\bot$ compare as suffixes

Let $t = t_0 \cdots t_{n-1}$ be a text over a linearly ordered alphabet and $u = t\bot$ the text
followed by a sentinel smaller than every letter. For all $i, j \le n$, the rotation
$u_i \cdots u_n u_0 \cdots u_{i-1}$ of $u$ starting at $i$ is lexicographically smaller than the
rotation starting at $j$ if and only if the suffix $u_i \cdots u_n$ is lexicographically smaller
than the suffix $u_j \cdots u_n$.
