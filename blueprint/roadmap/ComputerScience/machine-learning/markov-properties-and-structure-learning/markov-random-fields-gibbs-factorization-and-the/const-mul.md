# Gibbs factorization is preserved by positive scaling

Let $V$ be a finite set of vertices with decidable equality and $\alpha$ a finite set of values. If a weight $p : \alpha^V \to \mathbb{R}$ factorizes over the cliques of a simple graph $G$ on $V$ and $c \ge 0$, then $c \cdot p$ also factorizes over the cliques of $G$, the constant being carried by the potential of the empty clique. Together with the global Markov property of clique-factorized weights, this gives the Markov property of the normalized law $p / \sum_y p(y)$ whenever the total mass is positive.
