# Hammersley–Clifford, pairwise form

Let $V$ be a finite set of vertices with decidable equality and $\alpha$ a finite set of values. Let $G$ be a simple graph on $V$ and $p : \alpha^V \to \mathbb{R}$ a strictly positive weight. If $p$ satisfies the pairwise Markov property for $G$, then $p$ factorizes as a product of nonnegative potentials over the cliques of $G$.
