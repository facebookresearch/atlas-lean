# Collider junction (Berkson's paradox)

Let $X_0$ and $X_1$ be independent fair coins and $X_2 = X_0 \lor X_1$. Then $X_0$ and $X_1$ are not conditionally independent given $X_2$: at $X_0 = X_1 = X_2 = \text{true}$,

$$P(X_0, X_1, X_2)\,P(X_2) = \tfrac14 \cdot \tfrac34 \ne \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12 = P(X_0, X_2)\,P(X_1, X_2).$$
