# A round's factor in terms of its edge

For every real $\varepsilon$, with $\gamma = 1/2 - \varepsilon$,

$$2 \sqrt{\varepsilon (1 - \varepsilon)} = \sqrt{1 - 4 \gamma^2},$$

since $4 \varepsilon (1 - \varepsilon) = 1 - 4 \gamma^2$ (both square roots are taken to be $0$ at negative arguments).

## Sources

- Y. Freund and R. E. Schapire, "A decision-theoretic generalization of on-line learning and an application to boosting", *J. Comput. System Sci.* 55 (1997), 119–139, equation (21), first equality.
