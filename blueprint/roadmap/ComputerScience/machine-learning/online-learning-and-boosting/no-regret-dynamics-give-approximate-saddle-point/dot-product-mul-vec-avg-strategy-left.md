# Payoff of the average row strategy

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ payoff matrix, $T \ge 1$, $p_1, \dots, p_T$ mixed row strategies and $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$. The average row strategy $\bar p = \frac1T \sum_t p_t$ has payoff $\bar p^{\mathsf T} A y = \frac1T \sum_t p_t^{\mathsf T} A y$, the average of the payoffs.
