# Payoff against the average column strategy

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ payoff matrix, $T \ge 1$, $q_1, \dots, q_T$ mixed column strategies and $x \in \mathbb{R}^m$. Against the average column strategy $\bar q = \frac1T \sum_t q_t$, the payoff is $x^{\mathsf T} A \bar q = \frac1T \sum_t x^{\mathsf T} A q_t$, the average of the payoffs.
