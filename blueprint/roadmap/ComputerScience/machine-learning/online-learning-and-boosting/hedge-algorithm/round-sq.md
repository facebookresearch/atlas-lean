# Weighted second moment of a Hedge round

For weights $w$ and losses $\ell$ on $n$
experts, the round's weighted second moment is $\left(\sum_i w_i \ell_i^2\right) / W$, where
$W = \sum_i w_i$ is the total weight (the quotient is $0$ when $W = 0$); the weights are arbitrary real
numbers. For nonnegative weights with $W > 0$, it is $\sum_i x_i \ell_i^2$ for the played distribution
$x = w / W$.
