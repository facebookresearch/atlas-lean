# Coupling smooth sequences with independent samples

Let $x_1, \dots, x_T$ be generated by an adaptive adversary choosing $\sigma$-smooth distributions with respect to $\mu$, and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there is a coupling of $(x_t)_{t \le T}$ with an array $(z_{t,j})_{t \le T, j \le k}$ of i.i.d. samples from $\mu$ such that, with probability at least $1 - T(1-\sigma)^k \ge 1 - Te^{-\sigma k}$, every $x_t$ belongs to $\{z_{t,1}, \dots, z_{t,k}\}$ (Block, Dagan, Golowich and Rakhlin state the weaker form $1 - Te^{-\sigma k}$). Consequently, for a class of VC dimension $d$, events depending on the class's behaviour on the smooth sequence can be bounded through uniform convergence over the $Tk$ i.i.d. points.

## Proof sketch

At each round, realize $x_t \sim D_t$ by rejection sampling from $\mu$: draw $z_{t,1}, z_{t,2}, \dots \sim \mu$ and accept $z_{t,j}$ with probability $\sigma \frac{dD_t}{d\mu}(z_{t,j}) \le 1$. If none of $z_{t,1}, \dots, z_{t,k}$ is accepted, draw $x_t$ from $D_t$ separately. Each proposal is accepted with probability exactly $\sigma$, independently of the past, and the first accepted point has law $D_t$, so all $T$ rounds accept within the first $k$ proposals except with probability $T(1-\sigma)^k$.
