# Adjoint method by augmented-state backpropagation

Finite-horizon adjoint method by augmented-state backpropagation. Let $M_k$ be the layer maps on $\mathbb{R}^d$, $\ell_k$ the stage losses, $b$ an initial cost, $\ell_N$ a final loss, and $x_0 = x$, $x_{k+1} = M_k(x_k)$ the trajectory. Assume that $M_k$ and $\ell_k$ are differentiable at $x_k$ for every $k < n$, that $b$ is differentiable at $x$, and that $\ell_N$ is differentiable at $x_n$. Then the gradient at $x$ of the total cost $b(x) + \sum_{k < n} \ell_k(x_k) + \ell_N(x_n)$ equals the physical component of the backward pass through the $n$ augmented layers, started from the cotangent $(\nabla \ell_N(x_n), 1)$ of the final readout, plus the gradient $\nabla b(x)$ contributed by the lift $x \mapsto (x, b(x))$. No inner-product structure on the augmented product space is used.
