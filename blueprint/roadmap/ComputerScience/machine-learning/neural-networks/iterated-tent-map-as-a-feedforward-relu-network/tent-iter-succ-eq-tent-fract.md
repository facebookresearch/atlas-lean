# Iterated tent map via the fractional part

Proposition 5.1 of Telgarsky's deep learning theory lecture notes (after M. Telgarsky, Benefits of depth in neural networks, COLT 2016): for $0 \le x \le 1$ and every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the $(k+1)$-fold iterate of the tent map at $x$ equals the tent map applied to the fractional part of $2^k x$. The restriction to $[0, 1]$ is essential: outside this interval the two sides can differ.
