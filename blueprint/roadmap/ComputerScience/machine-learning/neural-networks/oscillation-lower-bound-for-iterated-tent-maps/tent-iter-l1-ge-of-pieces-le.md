# Functions with few pieces cannot approximate deep tent iterates

Let $\Delta$ be the tent map on $[0, 1]$ and $\Delta^{k}$ its $k$-fold iterate. For every integer $L \ge 2$ and every function $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ that is piecewise affine with at most $n \le 2^{L^2}$ pieces,

$$\int_0^1 \bigl| \Delta^{L^2 + 2}(x) - g(x) \bigr|\, dx \ge \frac{1}{32}.$$
