# Circular convolution commutes with cyclic shifts

Let $n \ge 1$. Circular convolution on $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ commutes with cyclic shifts: $k \star (\operatorname{shift}_s x) = \operatorname{shift}_s (k \star x)$ for every kernel $k$, signal $x$ and shift $s$, where $(\operatorname{shift}_s x)_i = x_{i - s}$.
