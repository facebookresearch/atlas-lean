# Piece bound for an affine combination

Let $g_1, \dots, g_k : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be piecewise affine, with $g_i$ having at most $n_i$ pieces, and let $a_1, \dots, a_k, b \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $x \mapsto \sum_i a_i g_i(x) + b$ is piecewise affine with at most $\max\bigl(\sum_i n_i, 1\bigr)$ pieces. The maximum with $1$ is needed for $k = 0$, where the combination is the constant $b$, which has one piece.
