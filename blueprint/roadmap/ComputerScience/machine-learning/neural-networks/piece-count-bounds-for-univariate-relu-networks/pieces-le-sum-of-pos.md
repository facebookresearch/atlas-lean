# Piece bound for an affine combination of positively many terms

For $k \ge 1$ functions $g_1, \dots, g_k : \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$, where $g_i$ has at most $n_i$ [affine pieces](pieces-le.md), and for real coefficients $a_1, \dots, a_k$ and $b$, the function $x \mapsto \sum_i a_i g_i(x) + b$ has at most $\sum_i n_i$ affine pieces. The hypothesis $k \ge 1$ is needed: for $k = 0$ the bound would be $0$, and [no function has at most $0$ pieces](pieces-le-zero-none.md).
