# k-CNF is PAC learnable

For fixed $t$ and $k$, the class of $k$-CNF functions over $t$ variables is PAC learnable over its realizable family, by the elimination learner with
$\lceil \log(|\text{k-CNF}| / \delta) / \varepsilon \rceil$ examples. This is the sample-size part of
Valiant's Theorem A; the polynomial running time of the algorithm is not part of this statement.

## Sources

- L. G. Valiant, *A theory of the learnable*, Communications of the ACM 27 (1984), 1134-1142, Section 5, Theorem A.
