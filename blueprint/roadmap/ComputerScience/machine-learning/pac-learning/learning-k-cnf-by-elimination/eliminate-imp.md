# One-sided error: the elimination hypothesis implies the target

If $f$ is a $k$-CNF expression satisfied by every example, then at every assignment where the
elimination output is true, $f$ is true. This is condition (i) of Valiant's definition of
learnability: the hypothesis "never outputs one when it should not".

## Sources

- L. G. Valiant, *A theory of the learnable*, Communications of the ACM 27 (1984), 1134-1142, Section 3, condition (i), and Section 5, proof of Theorem A.
