# Samples consistent with a wrong hypothesis

Let $X$ be a finite set, $c, h : X \to \{0, 1\}$ with $h(x) \ne c(x)$ for some $x$, and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $h$ is consistent with $c$ (agrees with it at every sampled point) on at most $(|X| - 1)^m$ samples $s \in X^m$, because its agreement set has at most $|X| - 1$ points.
