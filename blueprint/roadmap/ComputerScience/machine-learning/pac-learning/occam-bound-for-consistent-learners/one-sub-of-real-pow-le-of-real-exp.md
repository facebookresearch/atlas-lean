# The estimate $(1 - \varepsilon)^m \le e^{-\varepsilon m}$

For real $\varepsilon \ge 0$ and every natural number $m$, $(1 - \varepsilon)^m \le e^{-\varepsilon m}$,
stated in $[0, \infty]$ with truncated subtraction (so the left side is $0$ for $\varepsilon > 1$
and $m \ge 1$); for $\varepsilon \le 1$ it follows from $1 - \varepsilon \le e^{-\varepsilon}$.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Section 2.3.1, the inequality $1 - \epsilon \le e^{-\epsilon}$ used in equation (2.9).
