# Every nonempty finite class is PAC learnable

A nonempty finite set $H$ of hypotheses with measurable disagreement sets is PAC learnable over its realizable family (for all $\varepsilon, \delta \in (0, 1)$, one sample size and one deterministic learner achieve error at most $\varepsilon$ with probability at least $1 - \delta$ under every realizable distribution), with sample complexity at most
$\lceil \log(|H| / \delta) / \varepsilon \rceil$.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Corollary 3.2.
