# Realizable distributions give realizable samples almost surely

Let $D$ be a probability distribution on $X \times Y$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. If some $h \in C$ with a measurable disagreement set has $\operatorname{error}_D(h) = 0$, then $m$
independent examples from $D$ are almost surely realizable by $C$ (some
concept of $C$ agrees with every labelled example), since $h$ itself agrees with all of them with
probability $(1 - 0)^m = 1$.

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Section 2.3.1, the note in Definition 2.1.
