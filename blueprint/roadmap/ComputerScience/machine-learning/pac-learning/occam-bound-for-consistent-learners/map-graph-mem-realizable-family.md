# Labelling a distribution by a target gives a realizable distribution

If $c : X \to Y$ is measurable and some $h \in C$ with a measurable disagreement set has true error
$0$ against $c$ under a distribution $\mu$ on $X$ (the realizability assumption of Shalev-Shwartz
and Ben-David), then the labelled distribution $(x \mapsto (x, c(x)))_* \mu$ belongs to the
realizable family of $C$. This connects the textbook's pair (distribution on instances, labelling
function) to joint distributions on labelled examples.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Definition 2.1.
