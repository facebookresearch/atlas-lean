# Finite traces of a list equal its trace family

Let $\alpha$ have decidable equality, $H$ a finite list of hypotheses $\alpha \to \{\text{true},\text{false}\}$ and $s \subseteq \alpha$ a finite sample. The set of distinct traces $\{x \in s : h(x) = \text{true}\}$ of the hypotheses $h$ in $H$ equals the trace family on $s$ of the concept class $\{c : c \in H\}$ of hypotheses occurring in the list (repeated hypotheses included).
