# Finite concept class bounds its VC dimension

Let $H$ be a nonempty finite set of functions $\alpha \to \{\text{true},\text{false}\}$ and let $C = H$ as a concept class. Then

$$2^{\mathrm{VC}(C)} \le |H| ,$$

that is, $\mathrm{VC}(C) \le \log_2 |H|$, where $\mathrm{VC}(C)$ is the supremum in $\mathbb{N}$ of the sizes of finite sets shattered by $C$.
