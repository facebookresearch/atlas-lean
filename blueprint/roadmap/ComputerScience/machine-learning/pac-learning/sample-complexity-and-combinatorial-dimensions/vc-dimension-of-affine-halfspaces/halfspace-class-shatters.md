# Affine halfspaces shatter the origin and the basis vectors

For every $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the class of affine halfspaces $x \mapsto [0 < \langle w, x\rangle + b]$ in $\mathbb{R}^d$ shatters the set $\{0, e_1, \dots, e_d\}$: for every subset $W'$ of it there are $w \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and $b \in \mathbb{R}$ whose halfspace contains exactly the points of $W'$ among $0, e_1, \dots, e_d$.

One choice takes the bias from the target label at the origin and each weight coordinate from the target label at the matching basis vector.
