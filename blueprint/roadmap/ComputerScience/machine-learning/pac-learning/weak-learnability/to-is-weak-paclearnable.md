# PAC learnability implies weak PAC learnability

If a concept class is PAC learnable over a family of distributions, it is [weakly PAC learnable](is-weak-paclearnable.md) over the same family: take the accuracy $\varepsilon = 1/4$. This is the direction of Schapire's Theorem 1 that he calls trivial.

## Sources

- R. E. Schapire, "The strength of weak learnability", *Machine Learning* 5 (1990), 197–227, Theorem 1 and the remark following it.
