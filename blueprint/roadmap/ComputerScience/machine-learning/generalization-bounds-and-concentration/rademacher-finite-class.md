# Rademacher averages of a finite class

Let $m \ge 1$ and let $H$ be a nonempty finite class of Boolean functions on $m$ sample points, each read through its values $h(1), \dots, h(m)$ encoded as $\pm 1$. The empirical Rademacher average of $H$, the average over all $2^m$ sign vectors $\sigma \in \{\pm 1\}^m$ of $\max_{h \in H} \frac1m \sum_i \sigma_i h(i)$, is at most $\sqrt{2 \log |H| / m}$.
