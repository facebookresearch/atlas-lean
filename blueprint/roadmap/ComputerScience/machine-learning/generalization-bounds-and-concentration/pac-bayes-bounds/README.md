# PAC-Bayes bounds

Generalization bounds for randomized predictors: for a prior $\pi$ fixed before seeing the data, with high probability over an i.i.d. sample the expected risk of every posterior $\rho$ is bounded by its empirical risk plus a complexity term in $\mathrm{KL}(\rho \,\|\, \pi)$. The bound holds simultaneously for all $\rho$ because of the Donsker–Varadhan change of measure inequality, applied to the exponential moments of the generalization gap that Hoeffding's lemma controls.
