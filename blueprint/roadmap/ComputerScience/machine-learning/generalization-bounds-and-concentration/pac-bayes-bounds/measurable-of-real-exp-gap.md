# Joint measurability of the exponentiated generalisation gap

Let $\Theta$ and $\mathcal Z$ be measurable spaces, $D$ a probability measure on $\mathcal Z$ and
$\ell : \Theta \times \mathcal Z \to \mathbb{R}$ jointly measurable. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$
(including $n = 0$, where $r_S = 0$) and every real $\lambda$, the map
$(S, \theta) \mapsto \exp\big(\lambda (R(\theta) - r_S(\theta))\big)$ on
$\mathcal Z^n \times \Theta$, with values in $[0, \infty]$, is measurable, where
$R(\theta) = \int \ell(\theta, z) \, dD(z)$ and $r_S(\theta) = \frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n \ell(\theta, S_i)$.
