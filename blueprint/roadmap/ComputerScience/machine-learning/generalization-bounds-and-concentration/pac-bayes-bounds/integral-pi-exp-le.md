# Exponential moment of the generalisation gap for one parameter

Let $D$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\mathcal Z$, $g : \mathcal Z \to [0, C]$
measurable, $n \ge 1$, and let $S = (S_1, \dots, S_n)$ be an i.i.d. sample from $D$. Then for every
real $\lambda$,

$$\mathbb{E}_S \exp\Big(\lambda \Big(\int g \, dD - \frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n g(S_i)\Big)\Big) \le \exp\Big(\frac{\lambda^2 C^2}{8 n}\Big).$$
