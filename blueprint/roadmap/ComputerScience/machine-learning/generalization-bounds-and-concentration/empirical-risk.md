# Empirical risk

For a sample $z_1, \dots, z_n$ of $n \ge 1$ examples and a loss $L(f, z) = \ell(y, f(x))$, the empirical risk of a predictor $f$ is the average loss on the sample,

$$
\hat R(f) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n L(f, z_i).
$$
