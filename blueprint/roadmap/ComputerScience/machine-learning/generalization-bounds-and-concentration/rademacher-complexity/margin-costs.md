# The margin cost vectors

For a cost $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, labels $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and a set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ of value vectors on a sample, the margin cost vectors are

$$\{(\varphi(y_1 a_1), \dots, \varphi(y_n a_n)) : a \in A\}.$$

With labels $y_i \in \{\pm 1\}$ and $a = (f(x_1), \dots, f(x_n))$, the entries are the costs of the margins $y_i f(x_i)$.
