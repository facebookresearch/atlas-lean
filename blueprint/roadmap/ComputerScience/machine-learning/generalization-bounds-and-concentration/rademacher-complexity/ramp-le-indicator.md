# The ramp is dominated by the margin error

For $\theta > 0$ and every real $u$, $\rho_\theta(u) \le \mathbf{1}[u \le \theta]$, where $\rho_\theta$ is the [ramp cost](ramp.md). So the empirical ramp cost of a classifier is at most the fraction of sample points with margin at most $\theta$, the quantity of the margin bounds of Schapire, Freund, Bartlett and Lee.
