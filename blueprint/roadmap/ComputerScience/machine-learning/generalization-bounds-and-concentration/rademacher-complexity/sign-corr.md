# Correlation with a sign vector

For a sign vector $\sigma \in \{-1, 1\}^n$, encoded by $\varepsilon \in \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^n$ with $\sigma_i = 1$ exactly when $\varepsilon_i$ is true, and a vector $a \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the correlation is $\sum_{i=1}^n \sigma_i a_i$. The same encoding of sign vectors is used for Rademacher type and cotype.
