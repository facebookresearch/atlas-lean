# Scaling

For every real $c$ and every $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, $R(c A) = \lvert c \rvert\, R(A)$. For $c < 0$ the proof reverses every sign, which permutes the sign vectors.

## Sources

- S. Shalev-Shwartz and S. Ben-David, *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press (2014), Lemma 26.6 (the inequality $R(cA + a_0) \le \lvert c \rvert R(A)$).
- P. L. Bartlett and S. Mendelson, *Rademacher and Gaussian complexities: risk bounds and structural results*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 3 (2002) 463–482, <https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume3/bartlett02a/bartlett02a.pdf>, Theorem 12, part 3.
