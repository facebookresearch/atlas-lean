# Monotonicity

If $A \subseteq B \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ with $A$ nonempty and $B$ bounded, then $R(A) \le R(B)$.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett and S. Mendelson, *Rademacher and Gaussian complexities: risk bounds and structural results*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 3 (2002) 463–482, <https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume3/bartlett02a/bartlett02a.pdf>, Theorem 12, part 1.
