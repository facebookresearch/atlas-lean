# Minkowski sums

For nonempty bounded $A, B \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, $R(A + B) = R(A) + R(B)$. Bartlett and Mendelson state the inequality $R(\sum_i F_i) \le \sum_i R(F_i)$ in their absolute-value convention; without the absolute value the supremum of a sum over a product set splits exactly.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett and S. Mendelson, *Rademacher and Gaussian complexities: risk bounds and structural results*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 3 (2002) 463–482, <https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume3/bartlett02a/bartlett02a.pdf>, Theorem 12, part 7 (the inequality in their convention).
