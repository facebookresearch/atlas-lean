# Sign patterns of a set family

For a family $\mathcal{A}$ of subsets of the sample $\{1, \dots, n\}$, the set of sign patterns of its members. For a binary hypothesis class, $\mathcal{A}$ is the family of sets $\{i : h(x_i) = 1\}$, one for each hypothesis $h$, and its VC dimension is at most that of the class.
