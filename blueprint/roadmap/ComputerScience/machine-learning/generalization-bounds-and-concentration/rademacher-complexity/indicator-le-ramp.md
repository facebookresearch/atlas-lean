# The ramp dominates the zero-one loss

For $\theta > 0$ and every real $u$, $\mathbf{1}[u \le 0] \le \rho_\theta(u)$, where $\rho_\theta$ is the [ramp cost](ramp.md). This is the domination hypothesis $\varphi(\alpha) \ge \mathbf{1}(\alpha \le 0)$ of Bartlett and Mendelson's margin bound.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett and S. Mendelson, "Rademacher and Gaussian complexities: risk bounds and structural results", *J. Mach. Learn. Res.* 3 (2002), 463–482, <https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume3/bartlett02a/bartlett02a.pdf>, Theorem 7.
