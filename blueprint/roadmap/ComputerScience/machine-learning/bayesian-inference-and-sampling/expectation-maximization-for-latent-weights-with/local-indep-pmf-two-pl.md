# Response law of two-parameter logistic items

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of items with discriminations $a_i$ and difficulties $b_i$, and let latent state $z$ have ability $\theta_z$. The locally independent response law with the two-parameter logistic success probabilities $\sigma(a_i(\theta_z - b_i))$ is the two-parameter logistic response law at ability $\theta_z$.
