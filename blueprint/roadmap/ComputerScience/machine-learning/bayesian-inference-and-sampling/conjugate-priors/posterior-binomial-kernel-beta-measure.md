# Beta–binomial conjugacy

Let $a, b > 0$ be real and $k \le n$ natural numbers. For the
binomial kernel and a $\mathrm{Beta}(a, b)$ prior, the posterior distribution (the posterior
kernel, the disintegration of the joint law along the observation) after observing $k$ successes in $n$ trials is $\mathrm{Beta}(a + k, b + n - k)$.
