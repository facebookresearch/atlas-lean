# Existence and uniqueness of the mean-field fixed point

Let $\iota$ be finite, $U$ a linear map of $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $\lVert U \rVert \le a < 1$ for
the Euclidean operator norm, and $h \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. Then the
[mean-field map](mean-field-map.md) $m \mapsto \tanh(U m + h)$ has exactly one fixed point; it lies
in the open cube and solves $\operatorname{artanh} m = U m + h$
([fixed points of the mean-field map](mean-field-map-eq-self-iff.md)). The proof is Banach's
fixed-point theorem on the complete space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, which the map sends into the open
cube.
