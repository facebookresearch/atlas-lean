# The mean-field map is a contraction for a coupling of norm below one

Let $\iota$ be finite, $U$ a linear map of $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $h \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. If
$\lVert U \rVert \le a < 1$ for the operator norm of $U$ on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with the Euclidean
($\ell^2$) norm, the [mean-field map](mean-field-map.md) $m \mapsto \tanh(U m + h)$ is a
contraction with constant $a$.
