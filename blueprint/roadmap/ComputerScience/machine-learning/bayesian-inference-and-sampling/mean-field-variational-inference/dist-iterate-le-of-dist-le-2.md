# Error of an approximate fixed-point iteration, closed form

Let $(X, d)$ be a pseudometric space, $C \subseteq X$, $T, T^\star : X \to X$ with $T(C) \subseteq C$,
$T^\star$ $q$-Lipschitz on $C$ with $0 \le q < 1$ and a fixed point $\hat x \in C$, and
$d(T x, T^\star x) \le \zeta$ for every $x \in C$. Then for every $x_0 \in C$ and every
$k \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$d(T^k x_0, \hat x) \le q^k\, d(x_0, \hat x) + \zeta\, \frac{1 - q^k}{1 - q}.$$
