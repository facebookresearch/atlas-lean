# Fixed points of the mean-field map are the solutions of artanh m = U m + h

For every linear map $U$ of $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ and every $h, m \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, $m$ is a
fixed point of the [mean-field map](mean-field-map.md) $m \mapsto \tanh(U m + h)$ exactly when it
lies in the open cube $(-1, 1)^\iota$ and $\operatorname{artanh} m_i = (U m)_i + h_i$ for every
coordinate $i$.
