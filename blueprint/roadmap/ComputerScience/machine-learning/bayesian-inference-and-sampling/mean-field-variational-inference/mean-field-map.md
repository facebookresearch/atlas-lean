# The mean-field fixed-point map

**The mean-field fixed-point map**: for a linear map $U$ of $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ and
$h \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, it is $m \mapsto \tanh(U m + h)$, with $\tanh$ applied coordinatewise, on
$\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with the Euclidean norm. Its fixed points are the solutions of
$\operatorname{artanh} m = U m + h$ in the open cube
([fixed points of the mean-field map](mean-field-map-eq-self-iff.md)); for symmetric $U$ this is
the stationarity equation of the
[quadratic binary mean-field free energy](quadratic-binary-free-energy.md).
