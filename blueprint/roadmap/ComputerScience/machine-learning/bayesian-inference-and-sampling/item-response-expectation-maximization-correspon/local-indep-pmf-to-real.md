# Probability of a response pattern under local independence

For $d$ items with success probabilities $q_1, \dots, q_d \in [0, 1]$, the probability that the locally independent response law assigns to a response pattern $x \in \{0, 1\}^d$ is the product-Bernoulli mass $\prod_j q_j^{x_j} (1 - q_j)^{1 - x_j}$.
