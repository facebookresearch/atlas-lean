# The prior is invariant under posterior sampling

Let $\Omega$ be a nonempty standard Borel space with a finite measure $\mu$, and $\kappa$ a Markov kernel from $\Omega$ to a measurable space $\mathcal{X}$. Then $\mu$ is invariant under the composition of $\kappa$ with its posterior kernel with respect to $\mu$: drawing data through $\kappa$ and then a parameter from the posterior returns $\mu$.
