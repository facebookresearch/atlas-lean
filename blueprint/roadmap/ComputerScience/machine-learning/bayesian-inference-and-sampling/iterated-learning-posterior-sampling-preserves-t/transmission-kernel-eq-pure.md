# Disjoint likelihood supports

Let $H$ be a finite set of hypotheses with prior $\pi$, $D$ a finite set of data, and $L(h)$ a probability distribution on $D$ for each hypothesis $h$. Suppose the prior gives positive mass to every hypothesis and no datum has nonzero likelihood under two distinct hypotheses. Then the posterior given any datum produced by $h$ concentrates on $h$, and the transmission kernel is the identity: $K(h, \cdot)$ is the point mass at $h$.
