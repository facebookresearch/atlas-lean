# Full conditional depends only on the Markov blanket

Let $B$ be a Bayesian network on the nodes $0, \dots, n-1$ (in topological order) with values in a finite nonempty type $\alpha$, with conditional probability tables $p_k(\cdot \mid x_{\mathrm{pa}(k)})$ and joint distribution $P(x) = \prod_{k} p_k(x_k \mid x_{\mathrm{pa}(k)})$. Assume every table entry is strictly positive. Let $E \subseteq \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ be a set of evidence nodes with evidence values $e$; the free (non-evidence) variables are the configurations $z$ of the nodes outside $E$, and $(e, z)$ denotes the full assignment that equals $e$ on $E$ and $z$ off $E$. Write $P(e) = \sum_{x : x|_E = e|_E} P(x)$ for the evidence probability. Assume $P(e) \ne 0$, let $\pi(z) = P(z \mid X_E = e)$ be the posterior of the free variables (which has full support), and let $i \notin E$ be a free node. If two configurations $z, z'$ of the free variables give full assignments $(e, z)$ and $(e, z')$ that agree on every node of the Markov blanket of $i$ (its parents, children and the children's other parents), then the conditional laws of coordinate $i$ under $\pi$ coincide:

$$\pi(z_i = \cdot \mid z_{-i}) = \pi(z'_i = \cdot \mid z'_{-i}) .$$
