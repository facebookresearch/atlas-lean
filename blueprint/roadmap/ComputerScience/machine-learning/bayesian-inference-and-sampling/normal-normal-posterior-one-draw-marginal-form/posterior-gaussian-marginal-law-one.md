# Normal-Normal posterior, marginal form

Let $m_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, a prior variance $v_0 > 0$ and a noise variance $v > 0$. For the prior $N(m_0, v_0)$ on the mean and one Gaussian observation with variance $v$, the posterior kernel of the Gaussian likelihood equals the normal posterior kernel, for almost every observation $x$ with respect to the marginal law of the observation.
