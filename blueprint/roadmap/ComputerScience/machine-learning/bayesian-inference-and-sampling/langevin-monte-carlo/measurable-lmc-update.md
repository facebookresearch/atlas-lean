# The Langevin Monte Carlo update map is measurable

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space with its Borel σ-algebra, $f : E \to \mathbb{R}$ any function and $h \in \mathbb{R}$. The Langevin Monte Carlo update $(\vartheta, \xi) \mapsto \vartheta - h \nabla f(\vartheta) + \sqrt{2h}\, \xi$ is measurable on $E \times E$, because the gradient of any function on a finite-dimensional space is Borel measurable.
