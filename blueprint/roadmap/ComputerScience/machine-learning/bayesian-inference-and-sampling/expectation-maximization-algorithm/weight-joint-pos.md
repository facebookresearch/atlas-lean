# The weight-times-likelihood joint is positive

Let $Z$ be a finite set and let $f(x, z)$ be real numbers indexed by observations $x$ and latent values $z \in Z$, with $f(x, z) > 0$ for all $x$ and $z$. Then for all [strictly positive weights](../../../../Biology/statistical-genetics/gene-constraint-scores-pli-and-loeuf/pos-weights.md) $w$ on $Z$, all $x$ and all $z \in Z$, the [joint](weight-joint.md) $p_w(x, z) = w(z)\,f(x, z)$ is positive.
