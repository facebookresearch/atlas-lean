# Escape depth

For a finite state space with proposal kernel $R$ and energy $V$, the escape depth of a state $s$ is the infimum of $E > 0$ such that some state $t$ with $V(t) < V(s)$ is reachable from $s$ along proposal arcs with every state on the path at energy at most $V(s) + E$. It is $+\infty$ when no such $E$ exists.
