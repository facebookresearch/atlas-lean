# Weak reversibility

For a proposal kernel $R$ and energy $V$ on a finite state space, say that $i$ is reachable from $j$ at height $E$ when $V(j) \le E$ and there is a path from $j$ to $i$ whose steps have positive proposal probability and whose states all have energy at most $E$. The pair is weakly reversible when, for every height $E$ and states $i, j$, $i$ is reachable from $j$ at height $E$ if and only if $j$ is reachable from $i$ at height $E$.
