# Primitivity of the Metropolis–Hastings matrix

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $\pi$ a probability distribution on $\alpha$ with full support
($\pi(x) \ne 0$ for every $x$), and $Q$ a proposal whose support is symmetric ($Q(x, y) \ne 0$
implies $Q(y, x) \ne 0$) and whose transition matrix is primitive (some power has all entries
positive). Then the [Metropolis–Hastings transition matrix](mh-matrix.md) is primitive.
