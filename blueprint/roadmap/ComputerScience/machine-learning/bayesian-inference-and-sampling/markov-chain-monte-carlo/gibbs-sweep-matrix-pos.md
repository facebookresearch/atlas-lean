# Positivity of Gibbs sweep transition probabilities

Let $\pi$ be a probability distribution on the configurations $x : \iota \to S$, with $\iota$ and
$S$ finite, such that $\pi(x) \ne 0$ for every $x$, and let $l$ be a list of coordinates in which
every coordinate occurs. Then every entry $P(x, y)$ of the
[transition matrix of the systematic-scan Gibbs sweep](gibbs-sweep-matrix.md) along $l$ is
positive.
