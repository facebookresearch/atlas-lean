# The assignment step never increases the cost

Let $x_i$ ($i \in \iota$, a finite type) be points in a normed additive commutative group and
$c_0, \dots, c_{k-1}$ centers. For fixed centers, a [nearest-center assignment](nearest.md) $a'$
is optimal: its [$k$-means cost](cost.md) is at most that of any assignment $a$.
