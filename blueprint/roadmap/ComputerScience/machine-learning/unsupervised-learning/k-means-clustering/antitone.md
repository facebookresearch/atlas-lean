# The cost never increases along a run of Lloyd's algorithm

Let $x_i$ ($i \in \iota$, a finite type) be points in a real inner product space. Along a
[run of Lloyd's algorithm](is-run.md) with assignments $a_t$ and centers $c_t$, the
[$k$-means cost](cost.md) of $(a_t, c_t)$ is nonincreasing in $t \in \mathbb{N}$.
