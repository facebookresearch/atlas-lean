# Separable factor matrix

A real $k \times n$ matrix $H$ is separable if every factor $a$ has a pure column: a column $j$ of
$H$ equal to $c \, e_a$ for some $c > 0$, where $e_a$ is the $a$-th unit vector. For $X = W H$ this
makes column $j$ of $X$ a positive multiple of column $a$ of $W$ (a sample containing only factor
$a$). Applied to $W^\top$ it says that every factor has a pure row of $W$ (an "anchor" feature of
factor $a$ alone).
