# Complementary slackness at a fixed point of the $H$ update

Let $m, k$ be finite index sets, $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$ and
$H \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}$. If $H$ is a fixed point of the
[Lee–Seung update of $H$](update-h.md), then $H_{aj} (\nabla_H)_{aj} = 0$ for every entry, where
$\nabla_H = 2 (W^\top W H - W^\top X)$.
