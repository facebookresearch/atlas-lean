# Existence of a factorization of nonnegative-rank inner dimension

Let $X$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix with finitely many columns and nonnegative entries, and let
$r$ be its [nonnegative rank](nonneg-rank.md). Then $X = W H$ for some entrywise nonnegative
$W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}$ and $H \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times n}$.
