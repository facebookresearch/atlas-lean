# The objective is non-increasing along the Lee–Seung iterates

**The objective is non-increasing along the Lee–Seung iterates**: let $m, n, k$ be finite index
sets, $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ with $X \ge 0$, and $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$,
$H \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}$ with $W, H \ge 0$ entrywise. Writing $(W_t, H_t)$ for the result of
$t$ [Lee–Seung sweeps](sweep.md) from $(W, H)$, the objective $\|X - W_t H_t\|_F^2$ is
nonincreasing in $t \in \mathbb{N}$.
