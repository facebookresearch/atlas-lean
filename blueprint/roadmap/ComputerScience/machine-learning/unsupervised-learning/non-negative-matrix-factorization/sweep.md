# One sweep of the Lee–Seung algorithm

Let $m, n, k$ be finite index sets and $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$. One sweep of the Lee–Seung
algorithm sends $(W, H)$ to $(W', H')$, where $H'$ is the [update of $H$](update-h.md) at
$(W, H)$ and $W'$ is the [update of $W$](update-w.md) at $(W, H')$, using the new $H$.
