# Complementary slackness at a fixed point of the $W$ update

Let $n, k$ be finite index sets, $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$ and
$H \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}$. If $W$ is a fixed point of the
[Lee–Seung update of $W$](update-w.md), then $W_{ia} (\nabla_W)_{ia} = 0$ for every entry, where
$\nabla_W = 2 (W H H^\top - X H^\top)$.
