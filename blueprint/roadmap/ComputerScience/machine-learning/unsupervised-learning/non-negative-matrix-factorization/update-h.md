# Lee–Seung multiplicative update of H

The Lee–Seung multiplicative update of $H$ (Lee and Seung 2001, Theorem 1): for finite index sets
$m, k$, $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$ and
$H \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}$, the updated matrix has entries

$$H'_{aj} = H_{aj} \, \frac{(W^\top X)_{aj}}{(W^\top W H)_{aj}},$$

where an entry with a zero denominator becomes $0$.
