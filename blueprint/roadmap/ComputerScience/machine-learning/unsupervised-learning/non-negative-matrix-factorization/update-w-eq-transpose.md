# The W update is the H update of the transposed problem

Let $n, k$ be finite index sets, $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $W \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$
and $H \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}$. The [update of $W$](update-w.md) at $(X, W, H)$ is the
transpose of the [update of $H$](update-h.md) at $(X^\top, H^\top, W^\top)$.
