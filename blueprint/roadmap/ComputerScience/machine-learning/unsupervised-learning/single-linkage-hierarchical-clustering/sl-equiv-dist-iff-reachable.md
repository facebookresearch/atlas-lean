# Single-linkage clusters are the connected components of the Rips graph

In a (pseudo)metric space $M$, two points are [$r$-equivalent](sl-equiv.md) for the distance
exactly when they are joined by a path in the $r$-neighbourhood graph (distinct points adjacent
when at distance at most $r$), the one-skeleton of $\mathrm{VR}(M, r)$. So the single-linkage
clusters at scale $r$ are $\pi_0$ of the Vietoris–Rips complex (Carlsson, *Topology and data*,
Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2).
