# The two-point space

For $\delta \in \mathbb{R}$, the two-point space at distance $\delta$ is the set $\{p, q\}$ with the dissimilarity
$d_\delta(p, p) = d_\delta(q, q) = 0$ and $d_\delta(p, q) = d_\delta(q, p) = \delta$, the space
$(\{p, q\}, \begin{pmatrix} 0 & \delta \\ \delta & 0 \end{pmatrix})$ of Carlsson and Mémoli,
JMLR 11 (2010), Thm. 18 (I). It is a metric exactly when $\delta > 0$.
