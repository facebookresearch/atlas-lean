# Characterization of single linkage (Carlsson–Mémoli)

A [hierarchical clustering method](is-hc-method.md) $T$ satisfying conditions
[(I)](cm-condition-i.md), [(II)](cm-condition-ii.md) and [(III)](cm-condition-iii.md) is single
linkage: $T(X, d) = u^*_d$, the [single-linkage ultrametric](sl-dist.md), for every finite metric
space $(X, d)$ (Carlsson and Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Thm. 18).
