# Single linkage satisfies the Carlsson–Mémoli axioms

Single linkage, the method sending a finite set with dissimilarity $d$ to its
[single-linkage ultrametric](sl-dist.md) $u^*_d$, is a
[hierarchical clustering method](is-hc-method.md) satisfying conditions
[(I)](cm-condition-i.md), [(II)](cm-condition-ii.md) and [(III)](cm-condition-iii.md), so the
hypotheses of Carlsson and Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Thm. 18 are consistent and single linkage is
the method that theorem characterizes.
