# The dendrogram is the family of closed balls of $u^*$

Let $d$ be a dissimilarity on a finite set $X$ and $r \ge 0$. Then $x$ and $y$ are
[$r$-equivalent](sl-equiv.md) if and only if $u^*(x, y) \le r$, where $u^*$ is the
[single-linkage ultrametric](sl-dist.md) (Carlsson and Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Lemma 12 and
Remark 13). Finiteness makes the infimum defining $u^*$ a minimum.
