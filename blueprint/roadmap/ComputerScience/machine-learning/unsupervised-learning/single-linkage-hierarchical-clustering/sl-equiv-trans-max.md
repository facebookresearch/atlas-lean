# Chains concatenate

Let $d$ be a dissimilarity on a set $X$. An $r$-chain from $x$ to $y$ followed by an $s$-chain
from $y$ to $z$ is a $\max(r, s)$-chain from $x$ to $z$: if $x$ and $y$ are
[$r$-equivalent](sl-equiv.md) and $y$ and $z$ are $s$-equivalent, then $x$ and $z$ are
$\max(r, s)$-equivalent. This is the ultrametric inequality for $u^*$ (Carlsson and Mémoli,
JMLR 11 (2010), Example 1 and Example 7).
