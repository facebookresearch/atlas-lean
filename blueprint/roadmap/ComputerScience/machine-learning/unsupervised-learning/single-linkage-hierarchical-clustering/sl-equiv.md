# $r$-equivalence (single-linkage clusters at scale $r$)

For a dissimilarity $d : X \times X \to \mathbb{R}$ and a scale $r \in \mathbb{R}$,
$r$-equivalence is the equivalence relation on $X$ generated by $d(x, y) \le r$: $x$ and $y$ are
$r$-equivalent when they are joined by a finite chain $x = x_0, x_1, \dots, x_k = y$ whose
consecutive points satisfy $d(x_i, x_{i+1}) \le r$ or $d(x_{i+1}, x_i) \le r$. Its classes are the
single-linkage clusters at scale $r$ (Carlsson and Mémoli, JMLR 11 (2010), Example 1; Carlsson,
*Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2).
