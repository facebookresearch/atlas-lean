# Separable data matrix

Let $m_j$ ($j \in \iota$) be vectors in a real inner product space and $r \in \mathbb{N}$. A
separable structure on $(m_j)$ with $r$ archetypes (Gillis–Vavasis Assumption 1) consists of
linearly independent archetypes $w_1, \dots, w_r$ and mixing weights $h_{kj} \ge 0$ with
$\sum_k h_{kj} \le 1$ and $m_j = \sum_k h_{kj} w_k$ for every $j$, such that every archetype is
one of the columns: for each $k$ there is $j$ with $m_j = w_k$.
