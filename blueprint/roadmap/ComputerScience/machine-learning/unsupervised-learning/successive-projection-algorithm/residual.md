# Residual of a column in the successive projection algorithm

Let $m_j$ ($j \in \iota$) be vectors in a real inner product space and $S \subseteq \iota$ a finite
set. The residual $r_S(j)$ of column $j$ is its orthogonal projection onto the orthogonal
complement of the span of the columns $m_i$, $i \in S$.
