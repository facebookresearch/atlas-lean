# Existence of an archetype minimizer on the hull boundary

Let $E$ be a nontrivial real inner product space, $x_1, \dots, x_n \in E$ data points and
$p \ge 2$. If the [residual sum of squares](rss.md) has a minimizer over the
[feasible](feasible.md) archetypes $z_1, \dots, z_p$, then it has a feasible minimizer with every
archetype on the boundary of the convex hull of the data.
