# The RSS vanishes when the archetypes' convex hull contains the data

Cutler–Breiman Proposition 1(iii): let $x_1, \dots, x_n$ be data points and $z_1, \dots, z_p$
archetypes in a real inner product space, with $p \ge 1$. If every data point is a mixture of the
archetypes, the [residual sum of squares](rss.md) vanishes. The hypothesis $p \ge 1$ matters: for
$p = 0$ the hull is empty, the residual sum of squares is $0$ by convention, and its vanishing
does not witness a fit.
