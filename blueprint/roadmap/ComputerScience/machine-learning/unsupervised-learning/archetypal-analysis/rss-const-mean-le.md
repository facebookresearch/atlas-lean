# The sample mean is the optimal single archetype

Cutler–Breiman Proposition 1(i): let $x_1, \dots, x_n$ with $n \ge 1$ be data points in a real
inner product space. With a single archetype, the [sample mean](mean.md) $\bar x$ is
[feasible](feasible.md) and minimizes the [residual sum of squares](rss.md): for every point $z$,
$\mathrm{RSS}(\bar x) \le \mathrm{RSS}(z)$.
