# Feasible archetypes can be moved to the boundary of the hull

This extends the argument of Cutler–Breiman's Proposition 1(ii) to every $p \ge 2$: let $E$ be a nontrivial real inner product space,
$x_1, \dots, x_n \in E$ data points and $p \ge 2$. Any [feasible](feasible.md) archetypes
$z_1, \dots, z_p$ can be replaced by feasible archetypes $z'_1, \dots, z'_p$ on the boundary of
the convex hull of the data whose convex hull contains that of $z_1, \dots, z_p$; in particular
the [residual sum of squares](rss.md) does not increase.
