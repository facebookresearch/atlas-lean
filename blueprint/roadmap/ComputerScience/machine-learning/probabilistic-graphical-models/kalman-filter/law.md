# Law of a belief

The law of a belief with mean $m$ and covariance $P$ is the multivariate Gaussian measure
$N(m, P)$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$, the image of the standard Gaussian measure under
$z \mapsto m + P^{1/2} z$. It is the Gaussian distribution with mean $m$ and covariance $P$ when
$P$ is positive semidefinite (the belief is valid); otherwise the square root is taken to be $0$
and the law is the point mass at $m$.
