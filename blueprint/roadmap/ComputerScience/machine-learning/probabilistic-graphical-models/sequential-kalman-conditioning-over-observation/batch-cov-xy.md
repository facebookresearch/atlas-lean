# Cross-covariance of the final state and the observations

Consider the scalar linear-Gaussian model $x_1 = F x_0 + w$, $y_0 = H_0 x_0 + v_0$, $y_1 = H_1 x_1 + v_1$ with prior mean $m_0$ and variance $P_0$ of $x_0$, process variance $Q$ and observation variances $R_0, R_1$. The cross-covariance vector of $x_1$ with $(y_0, y_1)$ is

$$\Sigma_{xy} = \big(F H_0 P_0,\ H_1 (F^2 P_0 + Q)\big) \in \mathbb{R}^2 .$$
