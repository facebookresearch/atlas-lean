# Determinant of the observation covariance is positive

For real $F, H_0, H_1, R_0, R_1, Q, P_0$ with $P_0 \ge 0$, $Q \ge 0$, $R_0 > 0$ and $R_1 > 0$,

$$0 < (H_0^2 P_0 + R_0)\big(H_1^2 (F^2 P_0 + Q) + R_1\big) - (H_0 H_1 F P_0)^2 .$$

This is the determinant of the covariance matrix of the observation pair; the $P_0^2$ terms cancel, leaving $R_0 R_1$ plus nonnegative terms.
