# Weak union

Let $\alpha$ be a finite type and $P : \alpha^n \to \mathbb{R}$ with $P(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$,
and let $X, W, Y, Z$ be finite sets of nodes with $X \cup W$ disjoint from $Y$. If $X \cup W$ and
$Y$ are conditionally independent given $Z$ under $P$, then $X$ and $Y$ are conditionally
independent given $Z \cup W$.
