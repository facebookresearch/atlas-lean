# Decomposition

Let $\alpha$ be a finite type and $P : \alpha^n \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $X, X', Y, Z$ be finite
sets of nodes with $X' \subseteq X$ and $X$ disjoint from $Y$. If $X$ and $Y$ are conditionally
independent given $Z$ under $P$, then so are $X'$ and $Y$. Here conditional independence is the
identity $P(x_{X \cup Y \cup Z}) P(x_Z) = P(x_{X \cup Z}) P(x_{Y \cup Z})$ for all $x$, where
$P(x_U)$ is the sum of $P(y)$ over the configurations $y$ that agree with $x$ on $U$.
