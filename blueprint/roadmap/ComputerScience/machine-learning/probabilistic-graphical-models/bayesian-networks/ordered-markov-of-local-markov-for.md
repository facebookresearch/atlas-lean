# The local Markov property implies the ordered one

**The local Markov property implies the ordered one** ((L) ⇒ (O)): let $\alpha$ be a finite type,
$P : \alpha^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and $B$ a Bayesian network on the nodes $0, \dots, n - 1$. If $P$
has the local Markov property for the graph of $B$, then it has the ordered Markov property for
the parent sets of $B$. This follows by decomposition: the predecessors of $k$ are
non-descendants of $k$.
