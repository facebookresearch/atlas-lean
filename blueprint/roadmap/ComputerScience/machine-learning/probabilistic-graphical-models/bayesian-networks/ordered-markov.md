# Ordered Markov property of a law

**Ordered Markov property of a law** (Lauritzen 2009, property (O)): let $\alpha$ be a finite
type, $P : \alpha^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\mathrm{pa}(k) \subseteq \{0, \dots, k - 1\}$ parent sets.
Then $P$ has the ordered Markov property for these parent sets if, under $P$, every node $k$ is
conditionally independent of its non-parent predecessors given its parents.
