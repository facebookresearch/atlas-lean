# Fixing more variables decreases the marginal

Let $\alpha$ be a finite type and $P : \alpha^n \to \mathbb{R}$ with $P(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$,
and write $P(x_S)$ for the sum of $P(y)$ over the configurations $y$ that agree with $x$ on $S$.
For finite sets of nodes $S \subseteq T$ and every $x' \in \alpha^n$, $P(x'_T) \le P(x'_S)$.
