# Conditional independence is symmetric

Let $V$ and $\alpha$ be finite sets, $p$ a real weight on assignments $V \to \alpha$, and $A, B, S \subseteq V$. If $A$ and $B$ are conditionally independent given $S$ under $p$, in the marginal sense $p(x_{A \cup B \cup S})\, p(x_S) = p(x_{A \cup S})\, p(x_{B \cup S})$ for every assignment $x$, then so are $B$ and $A$.
