# Linear additive noise models with Gaussian laws are reversible

**Linear additive noise models with Gaussian laws are reversible** (Peters, Mooij, Janzing and
Schölkopf, *Causal discovery with continuous additive noise models*, JMLR 15 (2014), the "if"
direction of Proposition 13). Let $X$ and $N$ be independent measurable real random variables on
a probability space with $X \sim \mathcal{N}(m_1, v_1)$ and $N \sim \mathcal{N}(m_2, v_2)$, where
$v_1, v_2 \ge 0$ (a variance $0$ meaning a point mass). Then for every $\varphi \in \mathbb{R}$
there is $\psi \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $X - \psi (\varphi X + N)$ is independent of
$\varphi X + N$.
