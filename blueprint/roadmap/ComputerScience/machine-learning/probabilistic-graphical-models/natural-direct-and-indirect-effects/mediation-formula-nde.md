# The mediation formula for the NDE

**The mediation formula for the NDE**, a functional of the law `P` alone: P14, eq. (14a)
(`∑_{w,z} [E(Y | x, z, w) - E(Y | x*, z, w)] P(z | x*, w) P(w)`); with `W = ∅` it is P01, eq. (17),
and P14, eq. (16). Here `E(Y | x, z, w)` is the mean of `g(Y)` given `X = x`, `Z = z` and `W = w`
under `P`, and a conditional mean or probability given an event of probability zero is read as `0`.
