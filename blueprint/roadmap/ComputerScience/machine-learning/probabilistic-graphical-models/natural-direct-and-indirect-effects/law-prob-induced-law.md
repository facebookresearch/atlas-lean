# Events of the induced law are events of the noise

For a finite recursive structural causal model `M` with values in a finite set and independent noise
laws `μ`, the weight `P_M(E)` that the induced law `P_M` gives an event `E` of full assignments is
the noise probability `P_μ({u : X(u) ∈ E})` of the noise values whose solution `X(u)` lies in `E`.
