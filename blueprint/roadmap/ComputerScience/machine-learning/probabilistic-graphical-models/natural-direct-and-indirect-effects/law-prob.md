# Weight of an event

The weight `P(E) = ∑_{v ∈ E} P(v)` of an event `E` of full assignments under a real weight `P` on
full assignments, where a full assignment `v` gives each of the finitely many nodes a value in a
finite set `α`, so that the sum is finite. No sign or total is assumed: a singleton of weight `-1`
has weight `-1`. When `P` is a probability law, such as the law a model induces, `P(E)` is the
probability of `E`.
