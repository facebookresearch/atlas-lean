# Weight of an event

The weight `P(E) = ∑_{v ∈ E} P(v)` of an event `E` of full assignments under a real weight `P` on
full assignments. No sign or total is assumed: a singleton of weight `-1` has weight `-1`. When `P`
is a probability law, such as the law a model induces, `P(E)` is the probability of `E`.
