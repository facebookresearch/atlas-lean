# The mediation formula for the NIE

**The mediation formula for the NIE**, a functional of the law `P` alone: P14, eq. (14b)
(`∑_{w,z} E(Y | x*, z, w) [P(z | x, w) - P(z | x*, w)] P(w)`); with `W = ∅` it is P01, eq. (27), and
P14, eq. (17). Here `E(Y | x*, z, w)` is the mean of `g(Y)` given `X = x*`, `Z = z` and `W = w`
under `P`, and a conditional mean or probability given an event of probability zero is read as `0`.
