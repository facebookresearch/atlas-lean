# The mediation formula for the NIE

**The mediation formula for the NIE**, a function of a real weight `P` on full assignments alone,
the nodes taking values in a finite set `α`. For a treatment node `X`, a mediator node `Z`, an
outcome node `Y` read through a real function `g`, a set `W` of nodes and values `x`, `x*`, it is

`∑_{w,z} m(x*, z, w) [P(x, z, w) / P(x, w) - P(x*, z, w) / P(x*, w)] P(w)`,

where `P(x, z, w)`, `P(x, w)` and `P(w)` are the weights of the events `X = x, Z = z, W = w`,
`X = x, W = w` and `W = w`, likewise at `x*`, `m(x*, z, w)` is the weighted mean of `g(Y)` over the
event `X = x*, Z = z, W = w`, and a ratio whose denominator is `0` is `0`. When `P` is a probability
law, the ratios over events of positive probability are the conditional means and probabilities of
P14, eq. (14b), `∑_{w,z} E(Y | x*, z, w) [P(z | x, w) - P(z | x*, w)] P(w)`; with `W = ∅` it is P01,
eq. (27), and P14, eq. (17).
