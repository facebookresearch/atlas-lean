# The NDE is not identified

**The NDE is not identified** from the observational and all interventional laws when a
mediator-outcome confounder (`W`) is affected by the treatment (P14, Fig. 10; Avin, Shpitser and
Pearl 2005, Theorem 4): the kite models with the monotone and with the anti-monotone coupling of `W`
have the same law under every surgery, including none, and natural direct effects `1/8` and `1/4`.
