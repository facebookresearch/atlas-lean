# Assignments contradicting an intervention have probability zero

Let $B$ be a Bayesian network on the nodes $0, \dots, n - 1$ with values in a nonempty type
$\alpha$. Under the joint distribution of the [intervention](intervene.md) $\mathrm{do}(X_k = a)$,
every assignment $x \in \alpha^n$ with $x_k \ne a$ has probability $0$.
