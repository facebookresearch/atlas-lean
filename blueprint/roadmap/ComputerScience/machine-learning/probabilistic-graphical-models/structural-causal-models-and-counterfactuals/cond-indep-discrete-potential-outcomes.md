# Markovian ignorability

For a finite structural causal model with finite noise sets and independent noise laws, a treatment node $t$, an outcome node $y$, treatment values $a_0, a_1$ and a function $g$ of the outcome value, the treatment indicator $[X_t = a_1]$ is conditionally independent of the pair of potential outcomes $(g(Y_{a_0}), g(Y_{a_1}))$ given the values of the parents of $t$.
