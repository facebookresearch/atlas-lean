# The submodel of the intervention $\mathrm{do}(X_W = w)$

**The submodel of the intervention $\mathrm{do}(X_W = w)$** (Pearl 1995, Definition 2; Pearl,
*Statistics Surveys* 2009, eq. (7)): let $M$ be a Markovian structural causal model on the nodes
$0, \dots, n - 1$ with values in $\alpha$, $W$ a finite set of nodes and $w \in \alpha^n$. In the
submodel, the equation of every node $j \in W$ is replaced by the constant $X_j = w_j$ (so $j$
loses its parents), and every other equation and every disturbance law is kept.
