# Consistency of the potential outcomes

For a finite structural causal model with finite noise sets and independent noise laws, a treatment node $t$, an outcome node $y$, treatment values $a_0, a_1$ and a function $g$ of the outcome value: if the treatment takes only the values $a_0$ and $a_1$ at noise values of positive probability, then $g$ of the factual outcome equals almost surely the observed potential outcome, that is, the potential outcome under $a_1$ where the treatment is $a_1$ and under $a_0$ elsewhere.
