# The causal Bayesian network of a structural causal model

Let $M$ be a Markovian structural causal model on the nodes $0, \dots, n - 1$ with values in
$\alpha$, disturbance laws $\pi_k$ and structural equations $f_k$. Its causal Bayesian network
has the same parents, and at node $k$ the table that maps parent values $z$ to the law of
$f_k(z, U_k)$, the image of $\pi_k$ by $f_k(z, \cdot)$.
