# Abduction, action, prediction

For a finite structural causal model with independent noise laws $\mu$, evidence $E$, a node $k$, a value $a$ and an event $F$, the conditional counterfactual probability $P(X_{k \leftarrow a} \in F \mid E)$ is the sum of the posterior weights given $E$ (abduction) of the noise values, in the product of the supports of the noise laws, whose solution after the surgery $do(X_k = a)$ (action) lies in $F$ (prediction). For evidence of probability zero both sides are $0$.
