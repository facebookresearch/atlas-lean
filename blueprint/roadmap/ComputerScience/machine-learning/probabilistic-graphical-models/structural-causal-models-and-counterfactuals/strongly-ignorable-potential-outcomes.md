# Strong ignorability given the parents

For a finite structural causal model with finite noise sets and independent noise laws, a treatment node $t$, an outcome node $y$, treatment values $a_0, a_1$ and a function $g$ of the outcome value: if the treatment mechanism gives $a_1$ probability strictly between $0$ and $1$ at every assignment of the parents of $t$ (overlap), then the treatment indicator $[X_t = a_1]$ is strongly ignorable given the parents of $t$ for the pair of potential outcomes $(g(Y_{a_0}), g(Y_{a_1}))$.
