# A parentless nondegenerate treatment is ignorable

For a finite structural causal model with finite noise sets and independent noise laws, if the treatment node $t$ has no parents and its mechanism gives the value $a_1$ probability strictly between $0$ and $1$, then the treatment indicator $[X_t = a_1]$ is unconditionally strongly ignorable for the pair of potential outcomes $(g(Y_{a_0}), g(Y_{a_1}))$.
