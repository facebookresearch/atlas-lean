# The constant- and switching-response noise weights are probability laws

In both the constant-response model and the switching-response model on $X \to Y$, the uniform weights $p_U(u) = 1/4$ on $u = (u_X, u_Y) \in \{0,1\}^2$ are nonnegative and sum to $1$; that is, $u_X$ and $u_Y$ are independent fair coins.
