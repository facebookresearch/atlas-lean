# Effectiveness

Let $M$ be a Markovian structural causal model on the nodes $0, \dots, n - 1$ with values in
$\alpha$, let $W$ be a finite set of nodes, $w \in \alpha^n$ and $u$ a disturbance value. Under
$\mathrm{do}(X_W = w)$ every node $i \in W$ takes its forced value:
$X_{W \leftarrow w}(u)_i = w_i$.
