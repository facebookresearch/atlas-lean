# Abduction

For a finite structural causal model with independent noise laws $\mu$ and evidence $E$, the posterior weight of a noise value $u$ given $E$: its product noise weight if the factual solution at $u$ satisfies $E$, and $0$ otherwise, divided by the probability of $E$. These are posterior weights when $E$ has positive probability; when $E$ has probability zero the division by zero makes every weight $0$ by convention.
