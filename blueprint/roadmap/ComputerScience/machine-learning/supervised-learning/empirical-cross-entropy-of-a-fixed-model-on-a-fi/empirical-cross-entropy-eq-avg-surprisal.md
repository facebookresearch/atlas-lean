# Empirical cross-entropy as average surprisal

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $S_1, \dots, S_N$ a sample in $\alpha$ with $N \ge 1$, and $q$ a probability distribution on $\alpha$. The cross-entropy of $q$ relative to the empirical law of the sample equals the average surprisal $\frac{1}{N} \sum_n -\log_2 q(S_n)$ of the sample under $q$, with value $\infty$ when some sampled value has probability $0$.
