# Fréchet derivative acts by the Jacobian matrix

Let $F : \mathbb{R}^P \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a continuously differentiable map between Euclidean spaces ($C^1$ on all of $\mathbb{R}^P$). Then for all parameters $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^P$ and directions $v \in \mathbb{R}^P$, the Fréchet derivative of $F$ at $\theta$ applied to $v$ is the Jacobian matrix times $v$:

$$DF(\theta)\, v = J_F(\theta)\, v ,$$

where $J_F(\theta)$ is the $n \times P$ Jacobian matrix of $F$ at $\theta$.
