# Empirical cross-entropy is entropy plus Kullback–Leibler divergence

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set with the discrete measurable structure, $S_1, \dots, S_N$ a sample in $\alpha$ with $N \ge 1$, $\hat p$ its empirical law, and $q$ a probability distribution on $\alpha$. The cross-entropy of $q$ relative to $\hat p$, in bits, equals the Shannon entropy of $\hat p$ plus the Kullback–Leibler divergence of $\hat p$ from $q$ divided by $\ln 2$. No support hypothesis is needed: both sides are $\infty$ when $\hat p$ charges an outcome to which $q$ gives probability $0$.
