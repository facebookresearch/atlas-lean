# When the empirical cross-entropy is infinite

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $S_1, \dots, S_N$ a sample in $\alpha$ with $N \ge 1$, and $q$ a probability distribution on $\alpha$. The cross-entropy of $q$ relative to the empirical law of the sample is infinite if and only if $q(S_n) = 0$ for some sample index $n$.
