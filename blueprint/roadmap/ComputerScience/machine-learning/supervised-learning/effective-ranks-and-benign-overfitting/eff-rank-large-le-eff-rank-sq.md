# $R_k \le r_k^2$

Let $\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge 0$ be the eigenvalues of a covariance operator $\Sigma$, with $\sum_i \lambda_i < \infty$, and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\lambda_{k+1} > 0$. Then $R_k(\Sigma) \le r_k(\Sigma)^2$, because $\lambda_{k+1}^2 \le \sum_{i>k} \lambda_i^2$. Together with the two previous results, $r_k(\Sigma^2) \le r_k(\Sigma) \le R_k(\Sigma) \le r_k(\Sigma)^2$.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett, P. M. Long, G. Lugosi and A. Tsigler, *Benign overfitting in linear regression*, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 117 (2020) 30063–30070; arXiv:1906.11300 (v3), Lemma 5.
