# $r_k$ is at least one

Let $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots \ge 0$ be a summable real sequence (for instance the eigenvalues of a covariance operator) and $k \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\lambda_{k+1} > 0$. Then $1 \le r_k(\Sigma)$: the tail sum contains its first term $\lambda_{k+1}$. Monotonicity of the eigenvalues is not needed.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett, P. M. Long, G. Lugosi and A. Tsigler, *Benign overfitting in linear regression*, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 117 (2020) 30063–30070; arXiv:1906.11300 (v3), Lemma 5.
