# Optimal conditional surrogate risk with the wrong sign

For a loss $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\eta \in \mathbb{R}$, $H^-(\eta) = \inf \{ C_\eta(\alpha) : \alpha (2\eta - 1) \le 0 \}$, the best conditional risk when the sign of the prediction disagrees with that of $2\eta - 1$ (or the prediction is $0$); the infimum is taken in $\mathbb{R}$ and is $0$ by convention when unbounded below.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett, M. I. Jordan and J. D. McAuliffe, *Convexity, classification, and risk bounds*, Journal of the American Statistical Association 101 (2006) 138–156; numbering of the technical report version <https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~bartlett/papers/bjm-ccrb-05.pdf>, Section 2.2.
