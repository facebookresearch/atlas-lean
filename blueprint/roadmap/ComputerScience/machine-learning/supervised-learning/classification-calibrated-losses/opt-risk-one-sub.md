# Symmetry of the optimal risks

$H(1 - \eta) = H(\eta)$ for every $\eta$, by the reflection $\alpha \mapsto -\alpha$.

## Sources

- P. L. Bartlett, M. I. Jordan and J. D. McAuliffe, *Convexity, classification, and risk bounds*, Journal of the American Statistical Association 101 (2006) 138–156; numbering of the technical report version <https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~bartlett/papers/bjm-ccrb-05.pdf>, Lemma 6, part 1.
