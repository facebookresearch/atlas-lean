# Step 2: contraction

Let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in \mathbb{R}^d$ have every coordinate in $[-1, 1]$. Removing the
1-Lipschitz ReLU does not decrease the empirical Rademacher complexity: the
[ReLU neurons on the $\ell^1$ unit ball](l1-neuron-values.md) have empirical Rademacher
complexity at most that of the [linear functionals on the $\ell^1$ unit ball](l1-linear-values.md).
