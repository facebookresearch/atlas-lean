# Step 4: Massart

Let $d \ge 1$ and let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in \mathbb{R}^d$ have every coordinate in $[-1, 1]$. The
$2d$ [signed coordinate vectors](signed-columns.md) have empirical Rademacher complexity at most
$\sqrt{2 \log(2d) / n}$, by Massart's finite-class lemma with radius $\sqrt n$ (for $n = 0$ both
sides are $0$).
