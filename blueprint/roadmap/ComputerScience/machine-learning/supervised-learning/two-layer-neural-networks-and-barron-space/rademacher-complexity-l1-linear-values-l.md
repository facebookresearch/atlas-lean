# Step 3: the $\ell^1$ ball

Let $d \ge 1$ and $x_1, \dots, x_n \in \mathbb{R}^d$. A linear functional over the $\ell^1$ unit
ball is at most its value at one of the signed coordinate vectors $\pm e_j$, so the
[linear functionals with $\lVert u \rVert_1 \le 1$](l1-linear-values.md) have empirical Rademacher
complexity at most that of the $2d$ [signed coordinate functionals](signed-columns.md).
