# Path norm

**Path norm** of a two-layer network with outer weights $a_k \in \mathbb{R}$ and inner weights
$w_k \in \mathbb{R}^d$, $k = 1, \dots, m$: $\lVert \theta \rVert_P = \sum_k \lvert a_k \rvert \lVert w_k \rVert_1$
(E, Ma and Wu, *A priori estimates of the population risk for two-layer neural networks*,
equation (3.2)).
