# Multivariate Gaussian eq with Density gaussian Density

For a finite index set $\iota$, a mean $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and a positive definite precision matrix $S$, the Gaussian density with mean $\mu$ and precision $S$ is the density of the multivariate Gaussian measure with mean $\mu$ and covariance $S^{-1}$ with respect to Lebesgue measure on Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$.
