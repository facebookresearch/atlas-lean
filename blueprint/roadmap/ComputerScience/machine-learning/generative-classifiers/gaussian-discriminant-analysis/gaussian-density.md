# Multivariate Gaussian density with a precision matrix

For a finite index set $\iota$, a mean $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and a real matrix $S$, the Gaussian density with mean $\mu$ and precision matrix $S$ is the multivariate Gaussian density formula on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with mean $\mu$ and $S^{-1}$ in place of the covariance; no condition is imposed on $S$.

For positive definite $S$ it is the density, with respect to Lebesgue measure, of the Gaussian distribution with mean $\mu$ and covariance $S^{-1}$ ([The multivariate Gaussian law has the Gaussian density](multivariate-gaussian-eq-with-density-ga.md)).
