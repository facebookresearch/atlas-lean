# Gaussian Density

For a finite index set $\iota$, the Gaussian density on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with mean $\mu$ and precision matrix $S$ is the multivariate Gaussian density function with mean $\mu$ and covariance $S^{-1}$. For positive definite $S$ it is the density, with respect to Lebesgue measure, of the Gaussian distribution with mean $\mu$ and covariance $S^{-1}$.
