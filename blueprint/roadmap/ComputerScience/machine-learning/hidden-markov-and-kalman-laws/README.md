# Laws of hidden Markov and Kalman models

Probability laws of sequence models: the joint law of the hidden states and observations of a normalized hidden Markov model, the normalization of its observation likelihood, and sequential Gaussian conditioning of a linear Kalman model over a history of observations. The forward-backward, Viterbi and Baum–Welch recursions and the Kalman filter step are under [probabilistic graphical models](../probabilistic-graphical-models/README.md).
