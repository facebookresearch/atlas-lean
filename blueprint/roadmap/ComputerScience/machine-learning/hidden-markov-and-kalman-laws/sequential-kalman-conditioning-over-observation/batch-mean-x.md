# Prior mean of the final state

Consider the scalar linear-Gaussian model $x_1 = F x_0 + w$, $y_0 = H_0 x_0 + v_0$, $y_1 = H_1 x_1 + v_1$ with prior mean $m_0$ and variance $P_0$ of $x_0$, process variance $Q$ and observation variances $R_0, R_1$. The prior mean of the final state $x_1$ is

$$\mathbb{E}[x_1] = F\, m_0 .$$
