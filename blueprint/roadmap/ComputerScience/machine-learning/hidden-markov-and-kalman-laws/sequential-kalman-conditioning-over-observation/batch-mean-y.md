# Prior-mean formula for the observation pair

Let $F, H_0, H_1, Q, R_0, R_1, m_0, P_0$ be real numbers, read as the parameters of the scalar linear model $x_1 = F x_0 + w$, $y_0 = H_0 x_0 + v_0$, $y_1 = H_1 x_1 + v_1$: prior mean $m_0$ and variance $P_0$ of $x_0$, process variance $Q$ and observation variances $R_0, R_1$. The prior-mean formula for the observation pair $(y_0, y_1)$ is the vector

$$\big(H_0\, m_0,\ H_1 F\, m_0\big) \in \mathbb{R}^2 .$$

This is a formula in the parameters. It is the mean of $(y_0, y_1)$ when $x_0$ has mean $m_0$ and the noises $w, v_0, v_1$ are centred.
