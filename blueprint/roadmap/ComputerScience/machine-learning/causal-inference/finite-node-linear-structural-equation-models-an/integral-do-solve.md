# Mean responses to $\operatorname{do}(X_k = a)$ are affine in $a$

Let $B$ be a strictly lower triangular $n \times n$ coefficient matrix and $\varepsilon$ a random noise vector on a probability space with every coordinate integrable. Then the mean response of $X_i$ to $do(X_k = a)$ in the linear structural equations $X = B X + \varepsilon$ is affine in $a$, with slope the total effect $((1 - B)^{-1})_{ik}$ of $X_k$ on $X_i$, the sum of the path coefficients over the directed paths from $k$ to $i$: $E[X_i \mid do(X_k = a)] = E[X_i \mid do(X_k = 0)] + a\, ((1 - B)^{-1})_{ik}$.
