# Simpson reversal in the kidney-stone data

In the three-node Bayesian network encoding the kidney-stone data of Charig et al. (700 patients; node values: large stone, treatment B, success), treatment B has the higher aggregate success rate, $289/350 > 273/350$, while treatment A has the higher success rate in each stratum: $27/29 > 13/15$ for small stones and $192/263 > 11/16$ for large stones.
