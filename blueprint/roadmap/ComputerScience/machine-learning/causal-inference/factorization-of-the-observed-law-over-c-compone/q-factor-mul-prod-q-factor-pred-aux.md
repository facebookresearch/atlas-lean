# Q-factor as a product of conditional Q-factors, inductive step

Let $b$ be a Bayesian network on $n$ nodes with values in a finite nonempty set, and $L$ a set of latent nodes. For $Q[S](v)$, the probability of the values $v$ on a set $S$ of observed nodes after intervening on the observed nodes outside $S$, fix an ordering $\sigma$ of the observed nodes and a bound $m$. If $T$ is contained in the nodes of $H$ ranked below $m$ and no bidirected edge of the latent projection joins $T$ to the other such nodes, then $Q[T](v) \prod_{i \in T} Q[H_{<i}](v) = \prod_{i \in T} Q[\{i\} \cup H_{<i}](v)$, where $H_{<i}$ is the set of nodes of $H$ ranked below $i$ by $\sigma$. This is the prefix form of [Q-factor as a product of conditional Q-factors](q-factor-mul-prod-q-factor-pred.md).
