# Q-factor as a product of conditional Q-factors

Let $b$ be a Bayesian network on $n$ nodes with values in a finite nonempty set, and $L$ a set of latent nodes. Let $Q[S](v)$ be the probability of the values $v$ on a set $S$ of observed nodes after intervening on the observed nodes outside $S$, with the latent nodes not intervened. If $T \subseteq H$ and no bidirected edge of the latent projection joins $T$ to $H \setminus T$, then for every ordering $\sigma$ of the observed nodes, $Q[T](v) \prod_{i \in T} Q[H_{<i}](v) = \prod_{i \in T} Q[\{i\} \cup H_{<i}](v)$, where $H_{<i}$ is the set of nodes of $H$ ranked below $i$ by $\sigma$. This is Lemma 2, equation (21), of J. Tian and J. Pearl, On the testable implications of causal models with hidden variables (UAI 2002), and Lemma 4(ii), equation (71), of their technical report R-290-L, without division; no topological hypothesis on $\sigma$ is needed.
