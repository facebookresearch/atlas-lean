# The regret is nonnegative

For every stochastic bandit $\nu$ with $k \ge 1$ arms and finite means, every policy $\pi$ and every horizon $n$,
the regret is nonnegative: $R_n \ge 0$.

## Sources

- T. Lattimore and Cs. Szepesvári, *Bandit Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press, 2020 (author copy), Lemma 4.4 (a), whose proof is Exercise 4.1. <https://tor-lattimore.com/downloads/book/book.pdf>
