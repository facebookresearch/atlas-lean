# Largest mean of a bandit

The largest mean of a stochastic bandit with $k \ge 1$ arms is $\mu^* = \max_i \mu_i$, the largest of the means
$\mu_1, \dots, \mu_k$, in which an arm whose reward law has no finite first moment has mean $0$ by convention. When
the bandit has finite means, it is the largest mean of the reward laws.
