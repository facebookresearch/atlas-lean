# Sum chain Occupancy

For a row-stochastic matrix $P$, a discount $0 \le \gamma < 1$ and weights $\mu : S \to \mathbb R$ with $\sum_s \mu(s) = 1$, the discounted occupancies $d_\mu(t)$ of the chain sum to one: $\sum_t d_\mu(t) = 1$. Only the total weight is assumed, so some weights may be negative. If the weights are also nonnegative, an initial distribution, each occupancy is nonnegative too, and the occupancies form a probability distribution on the states.
