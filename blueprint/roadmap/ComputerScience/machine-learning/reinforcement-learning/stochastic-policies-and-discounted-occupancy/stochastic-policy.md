# Stochastic policy

A stationary stochastic policy $\pi$ on states $S$ and actions $A$ assigns to each state $s$ a probability distribution $\pi(\cdot \mid s)$ on $A$ with finite support. Deterministic policies $S \to A$ are the special case of Dirac masses.
