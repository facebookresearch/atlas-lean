# One-action MDP of a Markov reward process

The Markov reward process with transition matrix $P$ on $S$ and reward $c : S \to \mathbb{R}$, viewed as a Markov decision process with a single action: the reward of the action in state $s$ is $c(s)$ and its transition probabilities are $P(s, \cdot)$. The value of the Markov reward process is then the value of the only policy of this decision process.
