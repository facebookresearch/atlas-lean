# The randomized-rounding factor

$$\beta_k = 1 - \left(1 - \frac1k\right)^k ,$$

for $k \in \mathbb{N}$, so $\beta_1 = 1$, $\beta_2 = 3/4$, and $\beta_k$ decreases to $1 - 1/e$; for
$k = 0$ the zeroth power is $1$, so $\beta_0 = 0$.

## Sources

- M. X. Goemans and D. P. Williamson, "New 3/4-approximation algorithms for the maximum satisfiability problem", *SIAM J. Discrete Math.* 7(4) (1994), 656–666 (journal version), Lemma 3.1.
