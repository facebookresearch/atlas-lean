# Integrality gaps of linear programming relaxations

Let $\mathcal{R}$ be a relaxation of a minimization integer program $\mathcal{P}$. Its *integrality gap* is $\sup_I \mathrm{OPT}_{\mathcal{P}}(I)/\mathrm{OPT}_{\mathcal{R}}(I)$ over instances $I$ with $\mathrm{OPT}_{\mathcal{R}}(I) > 0$. An algorithm that outputs, on every such instance, a feasible solution of $\mathcal{P}$ of cost at most $\alpha \cdot \mathrm{OPT}_{\mathcal{R}}(I)$ shows that the gap is at most $\alpha$, since $\mathrm{OPT}_{\mathcal{P}}(I)$ is at most the cost of that solution.
