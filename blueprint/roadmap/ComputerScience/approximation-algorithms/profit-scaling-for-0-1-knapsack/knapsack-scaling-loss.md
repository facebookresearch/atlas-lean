# Knapsack scaling loss

For $n$ items with natural weights $w_i$ and profits $p_i$, a natural capacity $W$ and a natural scaling factor $K \ge 1$, some feasible set $S$ (total weight at most $W$) is optimal for the rounded profits $\lfloor p_i / K \rfloor$ (natural-number division), and the optimal knapsack value exceeds the true profit of $S$ by at most $nK$.
