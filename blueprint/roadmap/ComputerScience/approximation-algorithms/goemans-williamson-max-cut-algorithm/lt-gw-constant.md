# Goemans–Williamson Lemma 3.5

$$\alpha > 0.87856.$$

This is the bound stated by Goemans and Williamson. The proof gives the slightly stronger
$\alpha \ge 0.878565$, from the [certified inequality](certified-mul-one-sub-cos-le.md).

## Informally uses

- [The certified inequality](certified-mul-one-sub-cos-le.md): it bounds every ratio
  $\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{\theta}{1-\cos\theta}$ below by $0.878565$.

## Sources

- M. X. Goemans and D. P. Williamson, "Improved approximation algorithms for maximum cut and satisfiability problems using semidefinite programming", *J. ACM* 42(6) (1995), 1115–1145 (journal version), Lemma 3.5.
