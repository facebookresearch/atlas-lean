# Goemans–Williamson Lemma 3.4, angle form

For $0 \le \theta \le \pi$,

$$\frac{\theta}{\pi} \ge \alpha \cdot \frac{1 - \cos\theta}{2}.$$

At $\theta = 0$ both sides vanish; otherwise this is the definition of $\alpha$ as an infimum.
Goemans and Williamson state Lemma 3.4 after the change of variables $y = \cos\theta$; that form
is [Lemma 3.4](gw-constant-mul-le-arccos-div-pi.md).

## Sources

- M. X. Goemans and D. P. Williamson, "Improved approximation algorithms for maximum cut and satisfiability problems using semidefinite programming", *J. ACM* 42(6) (1995), 1115–1145 (journal version), Lemma 3.4 and its proof.
