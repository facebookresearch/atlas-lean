# Goemans–Williamson Lemma 3.4

For $-1 \le y \le 1$,

$$\frac{\arccos y}{\pi} \ge \alpha \cdot \frac{1 - y}{2}.$$

Applied with $y = v_i \cdot v_j$ it compares, pair by pair, the probability that a random
hyperplane separates $v_i$ and $v_j$ with that pair's contribution to the relaxation.
