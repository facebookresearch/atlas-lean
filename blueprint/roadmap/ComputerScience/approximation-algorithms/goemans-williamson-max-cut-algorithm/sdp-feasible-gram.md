# Unit vectors give feasible matrices

For unit vectors $v_i \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the Gram matrix $Y_{ij} = v_i \cdot v_j$ is positive
semidefinite with unit diagonal, and its [semidefinite objective](sdp-objective.md) equals the
[vector objective](vector-objective.md) at $v$.

## Sources

- M. X. Goemans and D. P. Williamson, "Improved approximation algorithms for maximum cut and satisfiability problems using semidefinite programming", *J. ACM* 42(6) (1995), 1115–1145 (journal version), §4.
