# The relaxation objective is bounded on unit vectors

For unit vectors $v_i$, the [relaxation objective](vector-objective.md) is at most
$\sum_{i<j} |w_{ij}|$, because $|1 - v_i \cdot v_j| \le 2$ by the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality.
This is what makes the [relaxation value](relaxation-value.md) a finite supremum.
