# Weighted maximum coverage is submodular

If all weights $w_i$ are nonnegative, the $1$-times coverage function $f_1$, the total weight of
the elements covered at least once (weighted maximum coverage), is
[submodular](is-submodular.md).

## Sources

- G. Liu, A. Dimitrakakis, B. Carter and D. Gifford, "Maximum n-times coverage for vaccine design", *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)*, 2022, arXiv:2101.10902, §2.2.
