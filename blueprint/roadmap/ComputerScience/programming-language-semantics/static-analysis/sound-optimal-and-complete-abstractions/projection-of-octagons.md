# Projection of octagons

Let $m^+$ be a coherent DBM whose octagon is nonempty, over $\mathbb{I} = \mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$, with [strong closure](strong-closure-is-a-normal-form-for-octagons.md) $(m^+)^\bullet$, in the notation of [difference-bound matrices and octagons](difference-bound-matrices-and-octagons.md). For each variable $v_i$,

$$\{t \mid \exists (s_0, \dots, s_{N-1}) \in D^+(m^+).\ s_i = t\} = \bigl[-(m^+)^\bullet_{2i\,2i+1}/2,\ (m^+)^\bullet_{2i+1\,2i}/2\bigr],$$

where a bound is included exactly when it is finite.

**Proof idea.** Every point of the octagon satisfies the two bounds, which are constraints of $(m^+)^\bullet$. By the saturation property of the strong closure each finite bound is attained by a point of the octagon, and the octagon is convex.
