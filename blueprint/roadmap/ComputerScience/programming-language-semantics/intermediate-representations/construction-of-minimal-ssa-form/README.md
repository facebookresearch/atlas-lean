# Construction of minimal SSA form

The construction of Cytron, Ferrante, Rosen, Wegman and Zadeck: $\varphi$-functions are placed at the iterated dominance frontiers of the assignments to each variable, and a walk over the dominator tree renames every mention to a fresh variable.
