# Bounded symbolic execution of an IMP command

A symbolic path is a record of a guard (a Boolean expression), a symbolic store $\sigma : \mathbb{N} \to \mathrm{AExp}$ (expressions over the initial state) and a cost $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Assuming a Boolean expression $b$ on a path $p$ with store $\sigma$ computes $b' = \operatorname{bfold}_\emptyset(b[\sigma])$, constant folding with the empty table of $b$ with $\sigma$ substituted for its variables; it fails (prunes the path) exactly when $b'$ is the literal false, and otherwise returns $p$ with guard $g \wedge b'$, where $g$ is the guard of $p$. A leaf is either a terminal leaf $\operatorname{done}(p)$ or a cutoff leaf $\operatorname{cut}(p)$ carrying a symbolic path $p$. Binding a continuation $h$ (from paths to lists of leaves) over a leaf applies $h$ to the path of a terminal leaf and keeps a cutoff leaf $\operatorname{cut}(q)$ as the one-element list $[\operatorname{cut}(q)]$. For a bound $k$, a guard $g$, a body continuation $B$ (from paths to lists of leaves), fuel $f$ and a path $p$, bounded unrolling $\operatorname{loop}_k(g, B, f, p)$ is the concatenation of two lists. The exit part is $[\operatorname{done}(q)]$ if assuming $\neg g$ on $p$ succeeds with path $q$, and empty if it prunes. The continue part is empty if assuming $g$ on $p$ prunes; otherwise, with $q$ the resulting path, it is $[\operatorname{cut}(q)]$ when $f = 0$ or when the cost of $p$ is at least $k$, and when $f = f' + 1$ and the cost of $p$ is less than $k$ it is the concatenation, over the leaves of $B(q^{+})$ in order, of the leaves obtained by binding $r \mapsto \operatorname{loop}_k(g, B, f', r)$, where $q^{+}$ is $q$ with its cost increased by $1$. Bounded symbolic execution $\operatorname{run}_k(c, p)$ of a command $c$ from a path $p$ with bound $k$ is defined by recursion on $c$: $\mathtt{skip}$ gives $[\operatorname{done}(p)]$; $x := a$ gives $[\operatorname{done}(p')]$, where $p'$ is $p$ with store $\sigma[x \mapsto \operatorname{afold}_\emptyset(a[\sigma])]$; $c_1 ; c_2$ concatenates, over the leaves of $\operatorname{run}_k(c_1, p)$ in order, the result of binding $q \mapsto \operatorname{run}_k(c_2, q)$; $\mathtt{if}\ g\ \mathtt{then}\ c_1\ \mathtt{else}\ c_2$ concatenates $\operatorname{run}_k(c_1, q_1)$, if assuming $g$ on $p$ gives $q_1$ (empty if it prunes), and $\operatorname{run}_k(c_2, q_2)$, if assuming $\neg g$ on $p$ gives $q_2$ (empty if it prunes); and $\mathtt{while}\ g\ \mathtt{do}\ c$ gives $\operatorname{loop}_k(g, q \mapsto \operatorname{run}_k(c, q), k \mathbin{\dot-} n, p)$, where $n$ is the cost of $p$ and $\dot-$ is truncated subtraction.
