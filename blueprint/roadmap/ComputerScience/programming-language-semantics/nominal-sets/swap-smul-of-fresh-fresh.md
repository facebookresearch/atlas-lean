# Swapping two fresh atoms fixes the element

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be an infinite set of atoms and $X$ a set with an action of the group $\mathrm{Perm}_f(\mathbb{A})$ of [finitely supported permutations](fin-perm.md) of $\mathbb{A}$. If the atoms $a$ and $b$ are both [fresh](fresh.md) for $x \in X$, then $(a\ b) \cdot x = x$. The two atoms may be fresh for different supports; the proof uses a third atom $c$ outside both of them and the identity $(a\ b) = (a\ c)(b\ c)(a\ c)$.
