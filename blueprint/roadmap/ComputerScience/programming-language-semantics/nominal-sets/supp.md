# Least finite support of an element

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be an infinite set of atoms and $X$ a [nominal set](nominal-set.md) over $\mathbb{A}$. The support $\mathrm{supp}(x)$ of $x \in X$ is the least finite support of $x$: a finite set of atoms that [supports](supports.md) $x$ and is contained in every finite support of $x$. It exists by the [existence of a least finite support](exists-least-support.md), and it is unique.
