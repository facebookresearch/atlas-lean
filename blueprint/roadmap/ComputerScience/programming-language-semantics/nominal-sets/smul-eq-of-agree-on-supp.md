# Permutations agreeing on the support act equally

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be an infinite set of atoms and $X$ a [nominal set](nominal-set.md) over $\mathbb{A}$. If two finitely supported permutations $\pi$ and $\sigma$ agree on $\mathrm{supp}(x)$, then $\pi \cdot x = \sigma \cdot x$: the permutation $\sigma^{-1}\pi$ fixes the support pointwise, hence fixes $x$.
