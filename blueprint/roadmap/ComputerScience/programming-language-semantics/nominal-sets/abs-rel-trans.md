# Transitivity of α-equivalence

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be an infinite set of atoms and $X$ a [nominal set](nominal-set.md) over $\mathbb{A}$. If $(a, x)$ is [α-equivalent](abs-rel.md) to $(b, y)$ and $(b, y)$ to $(d, z)$, then $(a, x)$ is α-equivalent to $(d, z)$. The proof uses that two permutations agreeing on $\mathrm{supp}(y)$ act equally on $y$.
