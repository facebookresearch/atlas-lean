# Group of finitely supported permutations

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be a set of atoms. The group $\mathrm{Perm}_f(\mathbb{A})$ of finitely supported permutations of $\mathbb{A}$ is the [subgroup](fin-perm-subgroup.md) of the permutations $\pi$ of $\mathbb{A}$ that fix every atom outside some finite set. It acts on $\mathbb{A}$ by $\pi \cdot a = \pi(a)$.
