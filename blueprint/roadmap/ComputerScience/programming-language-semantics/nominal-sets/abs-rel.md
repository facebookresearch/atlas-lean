# α-equivalence relation on atom–element pairs

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be a set of atoms and $X$ a set with an action of the group $\mathrm{Perm}_f(\mathbb{A})$ of [finitely supported permutations](fin-perm.md) of $\mathbb{A}$. Two pairs $(a, x)$ and $(b, y)$ of an atom and an element of $X$ are α-equivalent when either $a = b$ and $x = y$, or $a \ne b$, $a$ is [fresh](fresh.md) for $y$, and $(a\ b) \cdot x = y$: the second pair renames the bound atom of the first by a fresh transposition.
