# Support of an atom abstraction

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be an infinite set of atoms and $X$ a [nominal set](nominal-set.md) over $\mathbb{A}$. The atom abstraction $[\mathbb{A}]X$ is a nominal set, and for every atom $a$ and $x \in X$ the [least support](supp.md) of $[a]x$ is $\mathrm{supp}(x) \setminus \{a\}$.
