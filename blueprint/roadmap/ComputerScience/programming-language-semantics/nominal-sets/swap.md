# Transposition of two atoms

Let $\mathbb{A}$ be a set of atoms. For atoms $a, b \in \mathbb{A}$, the transposition $(a\ b)$ is the permutation of $\mathbb{A}$ that exchanges $a$ and $b$ and fixes every other atom; it fixes every atom outside $\{a, b\}$, so it belongs to the group $\mathrm{Perm}_f(\mathbb{A})$ of finitely supported permutations.
