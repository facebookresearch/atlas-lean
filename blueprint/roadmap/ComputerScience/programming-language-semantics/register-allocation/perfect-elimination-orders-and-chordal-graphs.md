# Perfect elimination orders and chordal graphs

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph. A vertex is *simplicial* if its neighbors form a clique. A *perfect elimination order* is a listing $v_1, \dots, v_n$ of the vertices such that each $v_i$ is simplicial in the subgraph induced by $\{v_i, \dots, v_n\}$. A graph is *chordal* if it has a perfect elimination order.
