# Constant folding and propagation of IMP commands

Let $t : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\bot\}$ be a constant table, recording known values $t(x) = n$ of some variables, and let $\operatorname{afold}_t$ and $\operatorname{bfold}_t$ be constant folding of arithmetic and Boolean expressions with $t$. Command folding $\operatorname{fold}_t(c)$ is the first component of a pass that also returns an outgoing table: $\mathtt{skip}$ gives $(\mathtt{skip}, t)$; $x := a$ gives $x := \operatorname{afold}_t(a)$ with the table $t$ updated at $x$ to $n$ if $\operatorname{afold}_t(a)$ is a constant $n$ and to $\bot$ otherwise; $c_1 ; c_2$ folds $c_1$ with $t$ to $(c_1', t_1)$, then $c_2$ with $t_1$ to $(c_2', t_2)$, and gives $(c_1' ; c_2', t_2)$; $\mathtt{if}\ b\ \mathtt{then}\ c_1\ \mathtt{else}\ c_2$ folds both branches with $t$ to $(c_1', t_1)$ and $(c_2', t_2)$ and gives $\mathtt{if}\ \operatorname{bfold}_t(b)\ \mathtt{then}\ c_1'\ \mathtt{else}\ c_2'$ with the table that, at a variable assigned in $c_1$ or $c_2$, is $t_1(x)$ if $t_1(x) = t_2(x)$ and $\bot$ otherwise, and is $t(x)$ elsewhere; $\mathtt{while}\ b\ \mathtt{do}\ c$ first forgets the entries of $t$ at the variables assigned in $c$, giving $t'$, and gives $\mathtt{while}\ \operatorname{bfold}_{t'}(b)\ \mathtt{do}\ \operatorname{fold}_{t'}(c)$ with table $t'$. The variables assigned in a command are the targets of its assignments. Conditionals and loops are kept even when their guard folds to a truth value.
