# Constant folding of IMP arithmetic expressions

Let $t : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\bot\}$ be a constant table, recording known values $t(x) = n$ of some variables. Arithmetic folding $\operatorname{afold}_t$ is defined by recursion: a constant stays; a variable $x$ becomes the constant $n$ when $t(x) = n$ and stays $x$ when $t(x) = \bot$; for $a_1 + a_2$, $a_1 - a_2$ and $a_1 \cdot a_2$ both operands are folded first, and if both folded operands are constants $m$ and $n$ the result is the constant $m + n$, $m \mathbin{\dot-} n$ (truncated subtraction) or $m \cdot n$; otherwise the operation is rebuilt from the folded operands, except that a difference whose folded left operand is the constant $0$ (and whose folded right operand is not a constant) becomes the constant $0$.
