# The graph model separates $\lambda x.\lambda y.x$ from $\lambda x.\lambda y.y$

The closed normal forms $K = \lambda x.\lambda y.x$ and $K^* = \lambda x.\lambda y.y$ have different denotations in $P(\omega)$, under every valuation of the free and bound variables: applied to $\emptyset$ and then to $\mathbb{N}$, they give $\emptyset$ and $\mathbb{N}$. So the model does not identify all terms. This rules out the degenerate one-point model, in which every equation holds.
