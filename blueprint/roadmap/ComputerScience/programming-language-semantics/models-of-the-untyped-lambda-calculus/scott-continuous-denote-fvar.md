# Denotations are Scott-continuous in the free variables

For every term $M$ and valuation $bs$ of its bound variables, the denotation $\rho \mapsto [\![M]\!]_{\rho, bs}$ is a Scott-continuous function of the valuation $\rho$ of the free variables, ordered pointwise. This is the form of Scott's continuity theorem in which the variables of a term are its free variables (D. Scott, *Data types as lattices*, SIAM J. Comput. 5(3) (1976), 522–587, Theorem 2.1: LAMBDA-definable functions are continuous).
