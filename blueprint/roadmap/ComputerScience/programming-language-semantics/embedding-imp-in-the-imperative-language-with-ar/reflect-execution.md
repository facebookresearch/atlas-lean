# Executions of translated commands project to IMP executions under an injective renaming

Let $\rho$ be an injective renaming from natural numbers to strings, $c$ an IMP command and $s$, $t$ states of the imperative language with arrays. If the translation of $c$ takes $s$ to $t$ in the big-step semantics, then $c$ takes $\pi_\rho(s)$ to $\pi_\rho(t)$ in the big-step semantics of IMP.
