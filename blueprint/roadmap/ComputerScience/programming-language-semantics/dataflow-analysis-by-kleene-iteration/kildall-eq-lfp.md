# A stable Kleene iterate is the least fixed point

Let $A$ be a complete lattice, finite or not, let $F : A \to A$ be monotone, and write $F^k$ for the $k$-fold iterate of $F$, with $F^0$ the identity. If $k \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfies

$$F^k(\bot) = F\bigl(F^k(\bot)\bigr),$$

then $F^k(\bot)$ is the least fixed point of $F$ given by the Knaster–Tarski theorem: $F^k(\bot) = \operatorname{lfp} F$.

No continuity of $F$ is assumed.
