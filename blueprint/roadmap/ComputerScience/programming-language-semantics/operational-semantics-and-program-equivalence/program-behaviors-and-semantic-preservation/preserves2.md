# Preservation of behaviors in form (2)

Let $S$ and $T$ be two operational semantics, each with its own transition relation and set of final configurations, over the same configuration type and the same label type, and let $s$ and $t$ be configurations, the source and the target. Preservation in Leroy's form (2) holds when, if $s$ is safe under $S$ (no configuration reachable from $s$ under $S$ is non-final with no outgoing transition), every behavior of $t$ under $T$ is a behavior of $s$ under $S$. Behaviors are those of the adapted model of this topic: termination or going wrong after a finite trace of labels, or reaction with an infinite trace, with every label observable and no silent divergence; CompCert's model instead relates different languages and includes silent divergence.
