# The Fisher–Yates output list has the uniform permutation law

Forgetting the cost and reading the output vector as a list, the law of the Fisher–Yates shuffle of a
vector $x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ is the law of $(x_{\sigma(0)}, \dots, x_{\sigma(n-1)})$ for a
uniformly random permutation $\sigma$ of the indices. Expected-cost results stated for a uniformly random
rearrangement of $x$ therefore apply to the output of the Fisher–Yates shuffle.
