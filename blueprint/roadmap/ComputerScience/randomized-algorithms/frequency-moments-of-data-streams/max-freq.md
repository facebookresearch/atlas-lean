# Largest multiplicity of a stream

For a natural number $n$ and a stream $l$ of elements of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, with $m_i$ the number of occurrences of $i$ in $l$, the largest multiplicity is

$$F^*_\infty = \max_{0 \le i < n} m_i$$

(N. Alon, Y. Matias and M. Szegedy, *J. Comput. System Sci.* 58 (1999), Section 1). It is $0$ for the empty stream and for $n = 0$.
