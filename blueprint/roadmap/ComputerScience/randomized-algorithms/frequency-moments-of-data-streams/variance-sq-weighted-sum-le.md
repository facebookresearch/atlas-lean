# Variance bound for the AMS estimator

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $\iota$ a finite set, and $(\varepsilon_i)_{i \in \iota}$ measurable random variables with values in $\{-1, 1\}$, mean $0$, and [four-wise independent](four-wise-independent.md): any family of at most four of them is mutually independent. Then for all real weights $(m_i)_{i \in \iota}$,

$$\operatorname{Var}\Big(\Big(\sum_i m_i \varepsilon_i\Big)^2\Big) \le 2 \Big(\sum_i m_i^2\Big)^2$$

(N. Alon, Y. Matias and M. Szegedy, *J. Comput. System Sci.* 58 (1999), proof of Theorem 2.2: $\operatorname{Var}(X) \le 2 F_2^2$).
