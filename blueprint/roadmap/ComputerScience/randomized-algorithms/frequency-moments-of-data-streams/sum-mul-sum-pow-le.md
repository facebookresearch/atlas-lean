# The moment inequality $F_1 F_{2k-1} \le n^{1-1/k} F_k^2$, polynomial form

Let $x_1, \dots, x_n$ be nonnegative real numbers (indexed by a finite set of size $n$) and $k \ge 1$. Then

$$\Big(\Big(\sum_i x_i\Big) \Big(\sum_i x_i^{2k-1}\Big)\Big)^k \le n^{k-1} \Big(\sum_i x_i^k\Big)^{2k},$$

the $k$-th power of the "Fact" in the proof of Theorem 2.1 of N. Alon, Y. Matias and M. Szegedy, *J. Comput. System Sci.* 58 (1999), which is stated there for positive reals. The proof is the source's: $\max_i x_i^k \le \sum_i x_i^k$ and the power mean inequality $(\sum_i x_i / n)^k \le \sum_i x_i^k / n$.
