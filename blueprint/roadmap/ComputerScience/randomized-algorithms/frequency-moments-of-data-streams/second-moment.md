# Second frequency moment of a stream

For a stream $l$ over a finite set $\iota$ with decidable equality, with $m_i$ the number of occurrences of $i$ in $l$, the second frequency moment is the real number

$$F_2 = \sum_{i \in \iota} m_i^2$$

(N. Alon, Y. Matias and M. Szegedy, *J. Comput. System Sci.* 58 (1999), Section 1). It is $0$ for the empty stream.
