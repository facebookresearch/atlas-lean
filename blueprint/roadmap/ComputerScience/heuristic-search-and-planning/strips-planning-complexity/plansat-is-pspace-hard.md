# PLANSAT is PSPACE-hard

Every language decided by a deterministic Turing machine in polynomial space reduces in polynomial time to
PLANSAT, the plan-existence problem for propositional STRIPS planning. PLANSAT remains PSPACE-hard when every operator has at most two positive preconditions and two postconditions. Together with membership in
PSPACE, PLANSAT is PSPACE-complete.
