# Expectimax Chance map

For a list $l$, weights $w : \iota \to \mathbb R$ and chance trees $f(s)$, $s \in \iota$, the chance-node value of the branches $(w(s), f(s))$, for $s$ running through $l$, is the weighted sum $\sum_{s \in l} w(s)\, v(f(s))$ of the expectimax values $v(f(s))$, summed along the list. The weights are arbitrary reals.
