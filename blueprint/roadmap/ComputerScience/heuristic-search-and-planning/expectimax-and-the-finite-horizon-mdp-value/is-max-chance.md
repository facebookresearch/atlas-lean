# Is Max Chance

Trees with no opponent node: leaves, max nodes, chance nodes and affine edges, with no min node anywhere. Every unfolding of an MDP has this property, but not every such tree is an unfolding: $\mathrm{leaf}\ 1$ and a max node with no children are such trees, while an unfolding of depth zero is $\mathrm{leaf}\ 0$ and one of positive depth has a max node over a nonempty action set.
