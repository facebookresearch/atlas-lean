# Edge occurrence of a cyclic vertex list

For a finite list of points $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ of the Euclidean plane $\mathbb{R}^2$, its cyclic edge list is $((v_1, v_2), (v_2, v_3), \dots, (v_{n-1}, v_n), (v_n, v_1))$ (empty for the empty list, and $((v_1, v_1))$ for a one-point list). For an index $i$ smaller than the length of the cyclic edge list, the $i$-th edge occurrence is the entry at position $i$ of the cyclic edge list, counting from $0$.
