# Free space of a polygonal workspace is open

A bounded polygonal workspace consists of an outer simple polygon with region $R_0$ and a finite list of hole polygons with regions $R_1, \dots, R_m$, where each closure $\overline{R_i}$ lies in the interior of $R_0$ and the closures of holes at distinct positions of the list are disjoint. Its free space is $F = \operatorname{int}(R_0) \setminus (R_1 \cup \dots \cup R_m)$. The free space $F$ is open.
