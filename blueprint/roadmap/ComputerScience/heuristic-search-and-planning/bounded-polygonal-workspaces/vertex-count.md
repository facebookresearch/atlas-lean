# Vertex count of a polygonal workspace

A bounded polygonal workspace consists of an outer simple polygon with region $R_0$ and a finite list of hole polygons with regions $R_1, \dots, R_m$, where each closure $\overline{R_i}$ lies in the interior of $R_0$ and the closures of holes at distinct positions of the list are disjoint. Its vertex count is the length of the outer vertex list plus the sum of the lengths of the hole vertex lists.
