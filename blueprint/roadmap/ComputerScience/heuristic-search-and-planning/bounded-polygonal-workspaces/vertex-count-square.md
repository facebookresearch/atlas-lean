# Vertex count of a hole-free quadrilateral workspace

A bounded polygonal workspace consists of an outer simple polygon with region $R_0$ and a finite list of hole polygons with regions $R_1, \dots, R_m$, where each closure $\overline{R_i}$ lies in the interior of $R_0$ and the closures of holes at distinct positions of the list are disjoint. Its vertex count is the number of outer vertices plus the total number of vertices of all holes (list lengths, counted with repetition). If the outer vertex list has length $4$ and the list of holes is empty, the vertex count is $4$.
