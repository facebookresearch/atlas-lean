# General position of a vertex list

A finite list of points $(p_1, \dots, p_n)$ of the Euclidean plane is in general position when its $x$-coordinates are pairwise distinct: $x(p_i) \neq x(p_j)$ for all $1 \le i < j \le n$, where $x(p)$ is the first coordinate. In particular a point repeated in the list violates general position.
