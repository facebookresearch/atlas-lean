# Collision of a planar rigid body

Let $V$ be a real inner product space of dimension $2$ with orientation $o$, let $A_0 \subseteq V$ be a body, $O \subseteq V$ an obstacle set, and let $A(t,\theta) = \{ R_\theta a + t : a \in A_0 \}$ be the planar placement, where $R_\theta$ is rotation by $\theta$ with respect to $o$. For every $t \in V$ and angle $\theta \in \mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$, the configuration $(t,\theta)$ lies in the obstacle region $\{ q : A(q) \cap O \neq \emptyset \}$ if and only if

$$\exists a \in A_0,\ R_\theta a + t \in O .$$
