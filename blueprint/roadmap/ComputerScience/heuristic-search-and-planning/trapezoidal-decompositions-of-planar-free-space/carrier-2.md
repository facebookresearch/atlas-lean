# Carrier of a cell of a trapezoidal decomposition

For a trapezoidal decomposition of $F \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, the carrier of a cell is the carrier of the corresponding trapezoid $T_i$ (the open region between its vertical sides and its lower and upper side lines) if the cell is a trapezoid index $i$, and the open vertical segment of the wall $S_j$ if the cell is a wall index $j$.
