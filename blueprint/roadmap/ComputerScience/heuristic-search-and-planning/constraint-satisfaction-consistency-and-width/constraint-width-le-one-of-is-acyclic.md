# Forests have ordinary width at most one

If the simple constraint graph of a finite
binary CSP is acyclic, its ordinary width is at most $1$. No connectedness or nonemptiness
assumption is required.

## Sources

- V. Kumar, *Algorithms for constraint-satisfaction problems: a survey*, AI Magazine 13(1) (1992), p. 37 (the tree case with at least one edge). <https://cse.unl.edu/~choueiry/Documents/kumar-survey.pdf>
