# A shortest feasible polyline is straight at interior bends

Let $F$ be a set in the plane, and call a polyline feasible when all its segments lie in $F$ (a single point must lie in $F$). Suppose a feasible polyline contains consecutive vertices $a, b, c$ with $b$ in the interior of $F$, and has length at most that of every feasible polyline with the same first and last vertices. Then $b$ lies on the segment from $a$ to $c$.
