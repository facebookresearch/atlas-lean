# Planar placement of a rigid body

Let $V$ be a real inner product space of dimension $2$ with an orientation $o$, and let $A_0 \subseteq V$ be a body. The planar placement sends a configuration $(t, \theta) \in V \times (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})$ to the body rotated by the angle $\theta$ (rotation $R_\theta$ determined by $o$) and then translated by $t$:

$$A(t, \theta) = \{ R_\theta a + t : a \in A_0 \} .$$
