# Vertices of the two-squares visibility graph

The vertex type of the two-squares visibility graph has exactly ten elements, named $s, t, A, B, C, D, E, F, P, Q$: the start, the goal and the eight corners of the squares $[0,1] \times [0,1]$ and $[2,3] \times [0,1]$. Equality of vertices is decidable.
