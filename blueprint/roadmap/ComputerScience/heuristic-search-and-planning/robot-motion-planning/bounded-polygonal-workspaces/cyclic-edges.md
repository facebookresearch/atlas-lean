# Cyclic edge list of a polygon

The cyclic edge list of a finite list of points of the Euclidean plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ is empty for the empty list, and for $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ with $n \ge 1$ it is

$$((v_1, v_2), (v_2, v_3), \dots, (v_{n-1}, v_n), (v_n, v_1)) .$$

For a one-point list $(v_1)$ it is the single degenerate edge $((v_1, v_1))$.
