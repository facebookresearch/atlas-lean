# Vertices of a workspace without holes

A bounded polygonal workspace consists of an outer simple polygon with region $R_0$ and a finite list of hole polygons with regions $R_1, \dots, R_m$, where each closure $\overline{R_i}$ lies in the interior of $R_0$ and the closures of holes at distinct positions of the list are disjoint. Its vertex list is the outer vertex list followed by the vertex lists of the holes, concatenated in order. If the list of holes is empty, this list equals the outer vertex list.
