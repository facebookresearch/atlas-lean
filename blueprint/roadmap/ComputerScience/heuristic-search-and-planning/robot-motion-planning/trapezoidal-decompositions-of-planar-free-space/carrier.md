# Carrier of a vertical trapezoid

A vertical trapezoid $T$ in the Euclidean plane is given by reals $x_1 < x_2$ and affine functions $b, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (the lower and upper side lines) with $b(x_1) \le u(x_1)$, $b(x_2) \le u(x_2)$, and $b(x_1) < u(x_1)$ or $b(x_2) < u(x_2)$. The carrier of $T$ is the open region

$$\{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x_1 < x < x_2,\ b(x) < y < u(x) \} .$$
