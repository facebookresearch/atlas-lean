# Trapezoidal decomposition of a planar set

Let $F \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$. A trapezoidal decomposition of $F$ consists of

- natural numbers $m$ and $k$;
- vertical trapezoids $T_1, \dots, T_m$ (open two-dimensional cells);
- walls $S_1, \dots, S_k$ (open vertical segments, one-dimensional cells);

subject to the conditions

- the carriers of any two distinct cells (trapezoids and walls indexed together) are disjoint;
- the union of the carriers of all trapezoids and walls is exactly $F$.

## Sources

S. M. LaValle, *Planning Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press, 2006, Section 6.2.2.
