# Vertical trapezoid

A vertical trapezoid in the Euclidean plane consists of

- reals $x_1 < x_2$, the $x$-coordinates of its two vertical sides;
- affine functions $b, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the lines carrying its lower and upper sides;

subject to $b(x_1) \le u(x_1)$, $b(x_2) \le u(x_2)$, and $b(x_1) < u(x_1)$ or $b(x_2) < u(x_2)$. It describes the open region $\{ (x, y) : x_1 < x < x_2,\ b(x) < y < u(x) \}$, a triangle when the two lines meet at one of the vertical sides.
