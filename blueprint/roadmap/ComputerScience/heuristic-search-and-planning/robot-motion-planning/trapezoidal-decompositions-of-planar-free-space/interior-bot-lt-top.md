# Upper side lies above the lower side inside a trapezoid

A vertical trapezoid $T$ in the Euclidean plane is given by reals $x_1 < x_2$ and affine functions $b, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (the lower and upper side lines) with $b(x_1) \le u(x_1)$, $b(x_2) \le u(x_2)$, and $b(x_1) < u(x_1)$ or $b(x_2) < u(x_2)$. Its carrier is the open set $\{ (x, y) : x_1 < x < x_2,\ b(x) < y < u(x) \}$. For every $x$ with $x_1 < x < x_2$, $b(x) < u(x)$.
