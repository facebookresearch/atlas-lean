# Wall on the right side of a trapezoid

Let $T$ be a vertical trapezoid with vertical sides at $x_1 < x_2$ and lower and upper side lines $b, u$, and let $S$ be a wall $\{ x_S \} \times (y_1, y_2)$. The wall lies on the closed right side of $T$ when

$$x_2 = x_S, \qquad b(x_2) \le y_1, \qquad y_2 \le u(x_2) .$$

Then $T$ lies to the left of the wall.
