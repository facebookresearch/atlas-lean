# Wall of a trapezoidal decomposition

A wall consists of reals $x$, $y_1$ and $y_2$ with $y_1 < y_2$. It describes the open vertical segment $\{ x \} \times (y_1, y_2)$ in the Euclidean plane, a one-dimensional cell of a vertical decomposition.

## Sources

S. M. LaValle, *Planning Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press, 2006, Section 6.2.2.
