# Cut Leaves

The function $k^{\lfloor d/2 \rfloor}$ of a branching factor $k$ and a height $d$. On a uniform $k$-ary tree that is perfectly ordered, it is the number of leaves alpha-beta evaluates at a cut node of height $d$, a node whose value already fails the window (at most the lower bound at a minimizer node, at least the upper bound at a maximizer node).
