# Visibility between two points around two squares

Consider the closed squares $O_1 = [0,1] \times [0,1]$ and $O_2 = [2,3] \times [0,1]$ in the Euclidean plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the free set $G = \mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \operatorname{int}(O_1 \cup O_2)$. Two points $a, b \in \mathbb{R}^2$ are visible from each other when the closed segment $[a, b]$ lies in $G$. A point is visible from itself exactly when it lies in $G$.
