# Iterates of T_μ differ by powers of P_μ

For a Markov decision process with finitely many states, a deterministic stationary policy $\mu$, every $k \ge 0$ and all $J, J' : S \to \mathbb{R}$, the iterates of the undiscounted Bellman operator of $\mu$ satisfy

$$T_\mu^k J - T_\mu^k J' = P_\mu^k (J - J').$$
