# The SSP of a discounted MDP is substochastic

If $M$ has finitely many states and stochastic transition probabilities and $0 \le \gamma \le 1$, then the transition probabilities $\gamma\, p(s,a,s')$ of its [associated SSP](to-ssp.md) are nonnegative and each row sums to $\gamma \le 1$: they are [substochastic](substochastic.md).
