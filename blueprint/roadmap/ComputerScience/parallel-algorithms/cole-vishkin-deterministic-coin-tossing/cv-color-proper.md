# A Cole–Vishkin step keeps the coloring proper

In one Cole–Vishkin step, a node of color $x$ whose successor has color $y \ne x$ takes the new color $2i + b$, where $i$ is the least bit position at which $x$ and $y$ differ and $b$ is bit $i$ of $x$. For consecutive nodes with colors $x$, $y$, $z$ along a directed path, with $x \ne y$ and $y \ne z$, the new colors of the first two nodes differ.

## Sources

- R. Cole and U. Vishkin, "Deterministic coin tossing with applications to optimal parallel list ranking", *Information and Control* 70 (1986), 32-53.
