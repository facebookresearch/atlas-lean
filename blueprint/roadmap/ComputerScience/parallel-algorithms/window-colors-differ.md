# Window indistinguishability for one-round ring coloring

Let $A$ color each vertex of a directed cycle from its window, the identifiers of its predecessor, itself and its successor. If $A$ properly colors every directed cycle with at least four vertices under every injective assignment of identifiers, then for pairwise distinct identifiers $a, b, c, d$ the windows $(a, b, c)$ and $(b, c, d)$ get different colors.
