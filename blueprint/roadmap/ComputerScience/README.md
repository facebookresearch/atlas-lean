# Computer Science

Computer science studies algorithms, programming-language structures, data abstractions, and their correctness properties.

[Data structures](data-structures/README.md) is the largest field, with over a quarter of the articles. It develops
each structure with its invariants and the cost of its operations:
[search trees and skip lists](data-structures/search-trees/README.md) from binary search trees to AVL, red–black and B-trees,
[heaps and priority queues](data-structures/heaps/README.md), hash tables, tries and union-find.
[Graph algorithms](graph-algorithms/README.md) come with their correctness and cost, from
[shortest paths](graph-algorithms/shortest-paths/README.md) by Dijkstra's algorithm, Bellman–Ford and Floyd–Warshall
to [spanning trees, flows and matchings](graph-algorithms/spanning-trees-flows-matchings/README.md) by the algorithms
of Kruskal, Prim, Edmonds–Karp and Gale–Shapley.
The design and analysis of algorithms continues with
[sorting and searching](sorting-and-searching/README.md),
[dynamic programming](dynamic-programming/README.md),
[greedy algorithms and matroids](greedy-algorithms-and-matroids/README.md),
[approximation](approximation-algorithms/README.md), [online](online-algorithms/README.md),
[randomized](randomized-algorithms/README.md) and [parallel](parallel-algorithms/README.md)
algorithms, [algebraic and numerical algorithms](algebraic-and-numerical-algorithms/README.md),
[computational geometry](computational-geometry/README.md),
[string algorithms](string-algorithms/README.md),
[heuristic search and planning](heuristic-search-and-planning/README.md) and
[quantum algorithms](quantum-algorithms/README.md).
[Cryptography](cryptography/README.md) proves the security of encryption, authentication,
signature and identification schemes by reductions between games, and develops differential
privacy. [Databases](databases/README.md) covers relational algebra, functional dependencies and
normal forms, and transaction concurrency control.
