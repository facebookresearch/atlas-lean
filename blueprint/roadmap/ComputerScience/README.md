# Computer Science

Computer science formalizes algorithms, programming-language structures, data abstractions, and correctness properties.

[Data structures](data-structures/README.md) is the largest field, with over a quarter of the articles. It develops
each structure with its invariants and the cost of its operations:
[search trees](data-structures/search-trees/README.md) from binary search trees to AVL, red–black and B-trees,
[heaps](data-structures/heaps/README.md), hash tables, tries and union-find.
[Graph algorithms](graph-algorithms/README.md) come with their correctness and cost, from
[shortest paths](graph-algorithms/shortest-paths/README.md) by Dijkstra's algorithm, Bellman–Ford and Floyd–Warshall
to [spanning trees, flows and matchings](graph-algorithms/spanning-trees-flows-matchings/README.md) by the algorithms
of Kruskal, Prim, Edmonds–Karp and Gale–Shapley.

[Process calculi](process-calculi/README.md), the second largest field, is mostly
[session types](process-calculi/session-types/README.md) for the π-calculus, with subject reduction and progress for
typed processes. [Type systems and lambda calculi](type-systems-and-lambda-calculi/README.md) has
[Hindley–Milner type inference](type-systems-and-lambda-calculi/hindley-milner-type-inference/README.md), where
Algorithm W is sound and complete and finds principal types, relational parametricity for
[System F](type-systems-and-lambda-calculi/system-f/README.md) and
[System Fω](type-systems-and-lambda-calculi/system-f-2/README.md), and strong normalization of the
[calculus of constructions](type-systems-and-lambda-calculi/calculus-of-constructions/README.md).
[Programming-language semantics](programming-language-semantics/README.md) gives the IMP language operational and
denotational semantics and a correct compiler to a stack machine.

[Machine learning](machine-learning/README.md) covers
[probabilistic graphical models](machine-learning/probabilistic-graphical-models/README.md) such as Bayesian networks
and hidden Markov models, Bayesian inference, supervised learning, and
[online learning and boosting](machine-learning/online-learning-and-boosting/README.md), with Novikoff's perceptron
mistake bound and the Freund–Schapire bound on the training error of AdaBoost.
