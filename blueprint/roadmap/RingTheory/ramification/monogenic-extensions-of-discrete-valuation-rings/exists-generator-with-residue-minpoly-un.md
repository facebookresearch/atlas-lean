# Monogenic generator with a uniformizing residue polynomial

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete
valuation rings with maximal ideals $\mathfrak m_A$, $\mathfrak m_B$ and residue fields
$\kappa_A$, $\kappa_B$, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra that is finitely generated as an
$A$-module and whose structure map is a local homomorphism. If $\kappa_B / \kappa_A$ is
separable, then there are $\alpha \in B$ and a polynomial $F \in A[X]$ such that, writing
$\bar\alpha$ for the residue of $\alpha$ in $\kappa_B$: $A[\alpha] = B$;
$\kappa_A[\bar\alpha] = \kappa_B$; $F$ is monic; the reduction of $F$ modulo $\mathfrak m_A$ is
the minimal polynomial of $\bar\alpha$ over $\kappa_A$; $F'(\alpha)$ is a unit of $B$; and
$\mathfrak m_B$ is the principal ideal generated by $F(\alpha)$.

In general $F$ is not the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $A$: $F(\alpha)$ is a nonzero
uniformizer, whereas the minimal polynomial vanishes at $\alpha$.
