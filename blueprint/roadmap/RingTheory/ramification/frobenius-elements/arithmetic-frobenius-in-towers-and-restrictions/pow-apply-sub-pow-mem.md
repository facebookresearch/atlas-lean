# Powers of a Frobenius map satisfy the powered congruence

Let $S$ be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring $R$, let $Q$ be an ideal of $S$ and
$q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$. If an $R$-algebra endomorphism $\varphi$ of $S$ satisfies
$\varphi(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in S$, then for every $f \in \mathbb{N}$

$$\varphi^f(x) - x^{q^f} \in Q \qquad \text{for every } x \in S.$$
