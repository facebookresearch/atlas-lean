# Frobenius in a tower is a power of the base Frobenius

Let $R \to T \to B$ be homomorphisms of commutative rings forming a tower, with $T$ finitely
generated as an $R$-module, and let $Q$ be a prime ideal of $B$, with $Q_T = Q \cap T$ and
$Q_R = Q \cap R$. Let groups $G$ and $H$ act on $B$ by ring automorphisms, with $G$ fixing the image
of $R$ and $H$ fixing the image of $T$, and let $\iota \colon H \to G$ be a group homomorphism
compatible with the actions: $\iota(h)\, x = h\, x$ for all $h \in H$ and $x \in B$. Here an element
$\sigma$ of a group acting on $B$ by ring automorphisms is an arithmetic Frobenius at a prime ideal
$Q$ of $B$ over $R$ when $\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in B$, where $q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$ is
the cardinality of the residue ring of $R$ below $Q$. Suppose that $\sigma \in G$ is an arithmetic
Frobenius at $Q$ over $R$, that $\tau \in H$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $T$, and that
the inertia subgroup of $Q$ in $G$ is trivial. Then

$$\iota(\tau) = \sigma^{f},$$

where $f$ is the inertia degree of $Q_T$ over $Q_R$. Only $H$ needs to fix $T$.
