# Powers of a Frobenius element satisfy the powered congruence

Let $B$ be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring $R$ and let a group $G$ act on $B$ by ring
automorphisms fixing the image of $R$. If $\sigma \in G$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at an ideal $Q$
of $B$ over $R$, that is, $\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in B$ with $q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$,
then for every $f \in \mathbb{N}$

$$\sigma^f(x) - x^{q^f} \in Q \qquad \text{for every } x \in B.$$
