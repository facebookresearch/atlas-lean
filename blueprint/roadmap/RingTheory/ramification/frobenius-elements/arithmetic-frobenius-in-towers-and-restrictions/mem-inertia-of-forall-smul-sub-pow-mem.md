# Elements with the same powered congruence differ by inertia

Let a group $G$ act by ring automorphisms on a commutative ring $S$, let $Q$ be an ideal of $S$ and
$r \in \mathbb{N}$. If $\sigma, \tau \in G$ satisfy $\sigma(x) - x^r \in Q$ and
$\tau(x) - x^r \in Q$ for every $x \in S$, then $\sigma \tau^{-1}$ lies in the inertia subgroup of
$Q$, the elements $g$ with $g(x) - x \in Q$ for every $x \in S$.
