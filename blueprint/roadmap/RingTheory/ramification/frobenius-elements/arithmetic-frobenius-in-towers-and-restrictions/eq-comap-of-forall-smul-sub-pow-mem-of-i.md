# Restriction of Frobenius equals Frobenius with trivial inertia

Let $R$, $B$ and $C$ be commutative rings with $B$ and $C$ algebras over $R$, let groups $G$ and $H$
act by ring automorphisms on $B$ and on $C$, fixing the image of $R$, and let $\iota \colon C \to B$
be a homomorphism of $R$-algebras and $\rho \colon G \to H$ a group homomorphism that are
compatible: $\iota(\rho(g)\, c) = g\, \iota(c)$ for all $g \in G$ and $c \in C$. Let $Q$ be an ideal
of $B$ and $Q_C = \iota^{-1}(Q)$. Here an element $\sigma$ of a group acting on $B$ by ring
automorphisms is an arithmetic Frobenius at an ideal $Q$ of $B$ over $R$ when
$\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in B$, where $q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$ is the cardinality of the
residue ring of $R$ below $Q$. Suppose that $\sigma \in G$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over
$R$, that $\tau \in H$ satisfies $\tau(y) - y^{q} \in Q_C$ for every $y \in C$, where
$q = |R/(Q_C \cap R)|$, and that the inertia subgroup of $Q_C$ in $H$ is trivial. Then
$\rho(\sigma) = \tau$. This is the equality form of the restriction statement of Proposition 7.22 in
Sutherland's notes, under its unramified hypothesis.
