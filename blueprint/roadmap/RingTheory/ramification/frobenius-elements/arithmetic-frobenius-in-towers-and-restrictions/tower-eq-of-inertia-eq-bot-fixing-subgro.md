# Frobenius tower equality for an intermediate field

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with fraction field $K$, let $L/K$ be a field extension and $E$ an
intermediate field, and let $T$ and $B$ be the integral closures of $R$ in $E$ and in $L$, with the
compatible inclusions $R \to T \to B$ and $T$ finitely generated as an $R$-module. Suppose that
$\mathrm{Aut}_K(L)$ acts on $B$ by ring automorphisms fixing $R$, and that its subgroup
$\mathrm{Aut}_E(L)$ of automorphisms fixing $E$ fixes $T$. Let $Q$ be a prime ideal of $B$. Here an
element $\sigma$ of a group acting on $B$ by ring automorphisms is an arithmetic Frobenius at a
prime ideal $Q$ of $B$ over $R$ when $\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in B$, where
$q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$ is the cardinality of the residue ring of $R$ below $Q$. If
$\sigma \in \mathrm{Aut}_K(L)$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $R$,
$\tau \in \mathrm{Aut}_E(L)$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $T$, and the inertia subgroup of
$Q$ in $\mathrm{Aut}_K(L)$ is trivial, then $\tau = \sigma^f$, where $f$ is the inertia degree of
$Q \cap T$ over $Q \cap R$.

In the number-field case, $R$, $T$ and $B$ are the rings of integers of $K \subseteq E \subseteq L$.
The whole group $\mathrm{Aut}_K(L)$ need not fix $T$.
