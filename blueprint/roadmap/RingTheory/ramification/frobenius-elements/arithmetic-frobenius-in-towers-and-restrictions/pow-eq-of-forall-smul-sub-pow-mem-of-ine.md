# Uniqueness of Frobenius powers with trivial inertia

Let $B$ be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring $R$, let a group $G$ act on $B$ by ring
automorphisms fixing the image of $R$, and let $Q$ be an ideal of $B$ with $q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$.
Here an element $\sigma$ of a group acting on $B$ by ring automorphisms is an arithmetic Frobenius
at an ideal $Q$ of $B$ over $R$ when $\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every $x \in B$, where
$q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$ is the cardinality of the residue ring of $R$ below $Q$. Suppose that
$\sigma \in G$ is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $R$, that $\tau \in G$ and $f \in \mathbb{N}$
satisfy $\tau(x) - x^{q^f} \in Q$ for every $x \in B$, and that the inertia subgroup of $Q$ in $G$
is trivial. Then $\sigma^f = \tau$.
