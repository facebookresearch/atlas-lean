# Intermediate residue cardinality as a power of the base residue cardinality

Let $R \to T \to B$ be homomorphisms of commutative rings forming a tower, with $T$ finitely
generated as an $R$-module, and let $Q$ be a prime ideal of $B$, with $Q_T = Q \cap T$ and
$Q_R = Q \cap R$. Suppose that the residue rings $R/Q_R$ and $T/Q_T$ are finite. Then

$$|T/Q_T| = |R/Q_R|^{f},$$

where $f$ is the inertia degree of $Q_T$ over $Q_R$, the degree of the residue field extension.
