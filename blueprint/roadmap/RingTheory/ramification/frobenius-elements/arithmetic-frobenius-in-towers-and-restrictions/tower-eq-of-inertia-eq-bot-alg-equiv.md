# Frobenius tower equality for algebra automorphisms

Let $R \to T \to B$ be homomorphisms of commutative rings forming a tower, with $T$ finitely
generated as an $R$-module, and let $Q$ be a prime ideal of $B$, with $Q_T = Q \cap T$ and
$Q_R = Q \cap R$. Here an element $\sigma$ of a group acting on $B$ by ring automorphisms is an
arithmetic Frobenius at a prime ideal $Q$ of $B$ over $R$ when $\sigma(x) - x^q \in Q$ for every
$x \in B$, where $q = |R/(Q \cap R)|$ is the cardinality of the residue ring of $R$ below $Q$. Let
$\sigma$ be an $R$-algebra automorphism of $B$ that is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $R$, and
$\tau$ a $T$-algebra automorphism of $B$ that is an arithmetic Frobenius at $Q$ over $T$. If the
inertia subgroup of $Q$ in the group of $R$-algebra automorphisms of $B$ is trivial, then $\tau$,
regarded as an $R$-algebra automorphism, equals $\sigma^f$, where $f$ is the inertia degree of $Q_T$
over $Q_R$.
