# Unramifiedness from a generator with separable reduced minimal polynomial

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, $L/K$ a finite extension, and $S$ the integral closure of $R$ in $L$. Assume $L/K$ is separable. Let $P$ be a nonzero prime of $S$, with $\mathfrak{p} = P \cap R$, and let $x \in S$ be an element generating $L$ as a field over $K$, with minimal polynomial $\mu \in R[X]$ over $R$. If the reduction of $\mu$ in $(R/\mathfrak{p})[X]$ is separable, then $S$ is unramified over $R$ at $P$.

The element $x$ need not generate $S$ as an $R$-algebra. Equivalently, $x$ may be taken in $L$ and integral over $R$: such an element lies in the integral closure $S$ with the same minimal polynomial, and every element of $S$ is an integral element of $L$, so the two presentations have the same hypotheses and conclusion.
