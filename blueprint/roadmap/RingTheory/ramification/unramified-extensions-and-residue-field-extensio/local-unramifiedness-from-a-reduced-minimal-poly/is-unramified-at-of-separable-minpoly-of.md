# Unramifiedness from an integral field generator with separable reduced minimal polynomial

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, $L/K$ a finite extension, and $S$ the integral closure of $R$ in $L$. Assume $L/K$ is separable. Let $P$ be a nonzero prime of $S$, with $\mathfrak{p} = P \cap R$, and let $x \in L$ be integral over $R$ and generate $L$ as a field over $K$, with minimal polynomial $\mu \in R[X]$ over $R$. If the reduction of $\mu$ in $(R/\mathfrak{p})[X]$ is separable, then $S$ is unramified over $R$ at $P$.

Here $S$ is any ring presented as an integral closure of $R$ in $L$.
