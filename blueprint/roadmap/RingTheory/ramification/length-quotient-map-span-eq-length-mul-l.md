# Lengths of principal quotients over a discrete valuation ring

Let `D` be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal `𝔪_D`, let `L` be a
local domain, and let `φ : D → L` be an injective ring homomorphism. For
every nonzero `f ∈ D`, `length_L (L ⧸ (φ f)) = length_D (D ⧸ (f)) · length_L
(L ⧸ 𝔪_D L)`, where `𝔪_D L` is the ideal of `L` generated by `φ(𝔪_D)` and
lengths take values in `ℕ∞`.
