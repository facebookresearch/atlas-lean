# Discriminant ideal of a Dedekind extension

Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind domains, with
$B$ an $A$-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an $A$-module. The discriminant ideal
$\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}$ is the ideal of $A$ generated by the norms $N_{B/A}(x)$ of the elements $x$
of the different ideal $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ of $B$ over $A$.
