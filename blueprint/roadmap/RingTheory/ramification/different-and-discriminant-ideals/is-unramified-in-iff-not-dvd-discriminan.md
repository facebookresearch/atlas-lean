# Unramified primes are those not dividing the discriminant

Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind domains with $B$ an $A$-algebra that is torsion-free and finitely
generated as an $A$-module, and suppose that the extension of fraction fields is separable. Then a
nonzero prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ of $A$ is unramified in $B$, that is, unramified at every prime
of $B$ above it, if and only if $\mathfrak p$ does not divide the discriminant ideal of $B$ over
$A$.
