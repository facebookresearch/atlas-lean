# The norm of the different commutes with localization

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain
with fraction field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension, and let $B$ be the
integral closure of $A$ in $L$, a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$ that is finite and
torsion-free as an $A$-module. Let $S \subseteq A$ be a multiplicative set of nonzerodivisors,
let $A_S \subseteq K$ be the localization of $A$ at $S$ inside $K$, and let $B_S$ be a
localization of $B$ at the image of $S$ that is also an $A$-algebra and an $A_S$-algebra, with
a map $B_S \to L$, such that all composite maps among $A$, $A_S$, $B$, $B_S$, $K$ and $L$
agree. Assume that $B_S$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$, finite and torsion-free
over $A_S$, torsion-free over $B$, and the integral closure of $A_S$ in $L$. For an extension
$R \to T$ of Dedekind domains, write $\mathfrak{D}_{T/R}$ for its different ideal and, for an
ideal $I$ of $T$, write $N_{T/R}(I)$ for the ideal of $R$ generated by the norms of the
elements of $I$. Then the ideal of $A_S$ generated by the image of
$N_{B/A}(\mathfrak{D}_{B/A})$ is $N_{B_S/A_S}(\mathfrak{D}_{B_S/A_S})$.
