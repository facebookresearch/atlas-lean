# Localizations of a monogenic DVR extension at primes over the maximal ideal

Let `O` be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal `𝔪`, and let `M` be
a domain which is an `O`-algebra with `O → M` injective. Let `e ∈ M` be a
root of a monic polynomial `f` over `O` whose reduction modulo `𝔪` is
separable over the residue field. Let `P` be a prime ideal of the subalgebra
`O[e]` containing the extension of `𝔪`. Then the localization `O[e]_P` is a
discrete valuation ring, and its maximal ideal is the extension of `𝔪`, that
is, it is generated by the image of `𝔪`.
