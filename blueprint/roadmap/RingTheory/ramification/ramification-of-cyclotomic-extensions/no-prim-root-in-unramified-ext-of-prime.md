# No primitive p-th root in an unramified DVR extension

Let $p$ be a prime and let $A$ be a complete discrete valuation ring with fraction field $K$ whose
residue field is finite of characteristic $p$ and whose maximal ideal is generated by $p$. Let $L$
be a finite extension of $K$ and $B$ a discrete valuation ring with fraction field $L$, with
compatible homomorphisms $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$, such that the ramification index of
the maximal ideal of $B$ over that of $A$ is $1$. If $K$ contains no primitive $p$-th root of unity,
then neither does $L$.

The proof is a valuation argument: a primitive $p$-th root of unity $b \in B$ reduces to $1$ in the
residue field, which has characteristic $p$, so each factor $1 - b^{k+1}$ with $0 \le k < p - 1$
lies in the maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_B$, while their product is $p$. Hence
$p \in \mathfrak m_B^{p-1}$, which for $p \ge 3$ forces $p \in \mathfrak m_B^2$, contradicting
ramification index $1$. For $p = 2$, the root $-1$ would already lie in $K$.
