# Colength of the maximal ideal as a sum of ramification times inertia

Let `C` be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal `𝔪_C` and fraction
field `K`, and let `D` be a Noetherian local domain of dimension at most one
which is a `C`-algebra such that an element `c ∈ C` maps into the maximal
ideal of `D` exactly when `c ∈ 𝔪_C`, and every element of `D` is congruent
modulo the maximal ideal of `D` to the image of an element of `C`. Let `κ`
be the fraction field of `D`, a `K`-algebra compatibly with `C → D → κ` and
`C → K → κ`, and assume `κ` is a finite separable extension of `K`.

Then the set of valuation subrings of `κ` containing the image of `D` is
finite, and the length of the `D`-module `D ⧸ 𝔪_C D` equals the sum, over
the valuation subrings `B` of `κ` containing the images of `C` and `D` and
such that an element of `C` is a nonunit of `B` exactly when it lies in
`𝔪_C`, of `e(B) · f(B)`, where `e(B)` and `f(B)` are the ramification index
and inertia degree of the maximal ideal of `B` over `𝔪_C`. Lengths take
values in `ℕ∞`.
