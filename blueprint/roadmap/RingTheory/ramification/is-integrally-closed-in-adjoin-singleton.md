# Dedekind's criterion for a squarefree reduction

Let `ϖ` be an irreducible element (a uniformizer) of a discrete valuation
ring `O`, and let `F` be a field that is an `O`-algebra with `O → F`
injective. Let `α ∈ F` be integral over `O`. Suppose that the image of the
minimal polynomial of `α` over `O` in `(O ⧸ (ϖ))[X]` is squarefree, and that
for every `x ∈ F` some power satisfies `ϖ ^ n x ∈ O[α]`. Then `O[α]` is
integrally closed in `F`.
