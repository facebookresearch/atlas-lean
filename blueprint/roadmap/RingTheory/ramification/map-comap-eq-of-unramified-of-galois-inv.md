# Galois-invariant ideals in an unramified extension are extended

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, $L/K$ a finite extension, and $S$ the integral closure of $R$ in $L$. Suppose $L/K$ is Galois and $S$ is unramified over $R$ (formally unramified and of finite type). If an ideal $I \subseteq S$ is invariant under every $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ acting on $S$, then

$$(I \cap R)\,S = I.$$

This includes $I = 0$ and ideals that are not radical.
