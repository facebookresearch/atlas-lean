# Relative norm is the base prime to the inertia degree, separable case

Let $R$ and $S$ be Dedekind domains with $S$ an $R$-algebra that is torsion-free and finitely
generated as an $R$-module, and suppose that the extension of fraction fields is separable. If
$P$ is a maximal ideal of $S$ lying over a maximal ideal $\mathfrak p$ of $R$, then the relative
norm of $P$ is

$$N_{S/R}(P) = \mathfrak p^{f},$$

where $f$ is the inertia degree of $P$ over $\mathfrak p$. Separability replaces the Galois or
perfect residue field hypotheses of other versions.
