# Étale lifts of separable residue extensions

Let `W` be a discrete valuation ring, complete for its maximal-ideal adic
topology, with residue field `κ_W`, and let `k` be a finite separable field
extension of `κ_W`. Then there are a discrete valuation ring `W'`, complete
for its maximal-ideal adic topology, and a `W`-algebra structure on `W'`
such that `W → W'` is an injective local homomorphism and `W'` is finite,
free, flat and étale over `W`, together with an isomorphism of
`κ_W`-algebras between the residue field of `W'` and `k`, with the following
properties: the maximal ideal of `W'` is the extension of the maximal ideal
of `W`; the rank of `W'` over `W` equals `[k : κ_W]`; the group of
`W`-algebra automorphisms of `W'` is finite; and if `k` is Galois over
`κ_W`, the number of these automorphisms equals the rank of `W'` over `W`,
and the elements of `W'` fixed by all of them are exactly the image of `W`.
