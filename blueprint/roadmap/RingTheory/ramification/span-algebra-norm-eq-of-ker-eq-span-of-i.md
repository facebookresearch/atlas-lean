# Norms of generators of the kernel of a residue map

Let `B` be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring `A` that is free
and finite as an `A`-module, and let `χ : B →+* K` be a ring homomorphism to
a field whose restriction to `A` is surjective. Suppose the kernel of `χ` is
the principal ideal generated by `g ∈ B`, the kernel `𝔪` of the restriction
of `χ` to `A` is a maximal ideal, and the localization of `A` at `𝔪` is a
discrete valuation ring. Then the ideal of `A` generated by the algebra norm
$N_{B/A}(g)$ (the determinant of multiplication by $g$ on the free $A$-module $B$) is `𝔪`.
