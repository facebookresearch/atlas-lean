# Bounding algebra maps by the special fiber

Let `R` be a commutative local ring with residue field `κ`, let `B` be a
commutative `R`-algebra that is finitely generated as an `R`-module, and let
`Ω` be any field with an `R`-algebra structure. Then there are only finitely
many `R`-algebra homomorphisms `B → Ω`, and their number is at most the
dimension of the special fiber `κ ⊗_R B` over `κ`. No flatness of `B` and no
algebraic closedness of `Ω` is assumed.
