# Residues detected by monic annihilating polynomials

Let `𝒪 → A` be a local homomorphism of commutative local rings, with `A`
finite and free as an `𝒪`-module, let `F` be a
commutative `𝒪`-algebra, and let `j : A → F` be an injective `𝒪`-algebra
homomorphism. For `x ∈ A` and `c` in the residue field of `𝒪`, the following
are equivalent: there is a monic polynomial `R` over `𝒪` with `R(j x) = 0`
whose reduction modulo the maximal ideal of `𝒪` equals `(X - c) ^ (deg R)`;
and the residue of `x` in the residue field of `A` equals the image of `c`
under the induced map of residue fields.
