# Directed unions of Noetherian local rings

A directed union of Noetherian local subrings, with flat inclusion maps
between comparable stages and with the maximal ideal of each stage extending
to the maximal ideal of any larger stage (`𝔪ᵢ Sⱼ = 𝔪ⱼ`), is itself Noetherian and
local, and is faithfully flat over every stage, with each stage maximal
ideal extending to the maximal ideal of the union.
