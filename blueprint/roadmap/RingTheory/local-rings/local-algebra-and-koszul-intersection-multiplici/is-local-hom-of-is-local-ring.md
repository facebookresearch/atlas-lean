# Integral homomorphisms from a local ring reflect units

Let `R` be a commutative local ring, `S` a nontrivial commutative ring
in an independent universe, and `f : R → S` an integral ring homomorphism.
Then `f` reflects units: if `f(r)` is a unit in `S`, `r` is a unit in `R`.
Thus `f` is a local ring homomorphism.
