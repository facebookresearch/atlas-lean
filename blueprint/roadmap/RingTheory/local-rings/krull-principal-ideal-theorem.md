# Krull's principal ideal theorem for commutative Noetherian rings

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring with identity, let $I$ be a principal ideal of $R$, and let $P$ be a prime ideal of $R$ that is minimal among the prime ideals containing $I$. Then the height of $P$, the supremum of the lengths $n$ of chains $P_0 \subsetneq P_1 \subsetneq \cdots \subsetneq P_n = P$ of prime ideals, is at most $1$.

- David Eisenbud, Commutative Algebra with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 150, Springer-Verlag, 1995.
