# The height of a prime ideal of a commutative ring equals the Krull dimension of the localization at it

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity, let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime ideal of $R$ and let $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$ be the localization of $R$ at $\mathfrak{p}$. Then the Krull dimension of $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$ equals the height of $\mathfrak{p}$, the supremum of the lengths $n$ of chains of prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}_0 \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_1 \subsetneq \dots \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_n = \mathfrak{p}$ of $R$. Both sides lie in $\{0, 1, 2, \dots\} \cup \{\infty\}$, so one is infinite exactly when the other is.
