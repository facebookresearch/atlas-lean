# Krull's height theorem for commutative Noetherian rings

Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian ring with identity, let $S \subseteq R$ be a finite set, and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime ideal of $R$ that is minimal among the prime ideals containing the ideal generated by $S$. Then the height of $\mathfrak{p}$ is at most the number of elements of $S$.

## Sources

- David Eisenbud, Commutative Algebra with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 150, Springer-Verlag, 1995.
