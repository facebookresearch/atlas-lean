# Scalar length equality for two orders of ideal quotienting

Scalar length equality for two orders of ideal quotienting.

For I,J ⊆ R with R commutative, ℓ_R((R/I)/image(J)) = ℓ_R((R/J)/image(I)). The images are
taken under the corresponding canonical quotient maps, and the equality follows from their
canonical R-algebra equivalence. Length may be infinite.
