# Principal quotient length is the residue-weighted sum of local lengths

Let `R` be a Noetherian local domain of Krull dimension at most one, `K` a
chosen fraction field, and `A ⊆ K` a finite `R`-subalgebra. For nonzero `a ∈ R`, put
`Q = A/(aA)`. The natural-number length of `R/(a)` equals the finite sum over primes
`p` of `Q` of the local length of `Q_p` as a module over itself, multiplied by the
residue degree of `Q` at `p` over the residue field of the original base `R`.
Both the local quotient lengths and residue degrees are those of these actual rings.
