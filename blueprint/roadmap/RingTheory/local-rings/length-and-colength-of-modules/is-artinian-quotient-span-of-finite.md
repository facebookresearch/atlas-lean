# Nonzero principal quotients of finite birational subalgebras are Artinian

Let `R` be a Noetherian domain of Krull dimension at most one, `K` a chosen
fraction field, and `A ⊆ K` a finite `R`-subalgebra. For every nonzero `a ∈ R`,
the ring `A/(aA)` is Artinian. The field and base-ring universes are independent.
