# The adic completion of a Noetherian ring is flat over the ring

For an ideal $I$ of a Noetherian commutative ring $R$, the $I$-adic completion $\widehat{R}$ of $R$ is a flat $R$-module. In detail, let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity in which every ideal is finitely generated, and let $I$ be any ideal of $R$. The completion $\widehat{R}$ is the set of sequences $(x_n)_{n \ge 0}$ with $x_n \in R/I^n$ such that the projection $R/I^n \to R/I^m$ sends $x_n$ to $x_m$ whenever $m \le n$, with coordinatewise addition; $R$ acts coordinatewise, $r \cdot (x_n)_n = (r x_n)_n$, which is multiplication by the image $(r + I^n)_n$ of $r$ under the natural ring map $R \to \widehat{R}$. The conclusion is that $\widehat{R}$ is flat over $R$. Mathlib defines flatness by requiring that for every finitely generated $R$-module $P$, taken in the same universe as $R$, and every finitely generated submodule $N \subseteq P$, the map $N \otimes_R \widehat{R} \to P \otimes_R \widehat{R}$ induced by the inclusion is injective; this implies that tensoring with $\widehat{R}$ carries every injective $R$-linear map between arbitrary $R$-modules to an injective map.
