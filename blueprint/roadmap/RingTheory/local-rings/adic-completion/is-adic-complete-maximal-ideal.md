# Adic completeness of multivariate power series

Let $R$ be a commutative local ring that is adically complete for its maximal ideal, that is,
separated and complete for the topology defined by the powers of the maximal ideal, and let
$\sigma$ be a finite set. Then the power series ring $R[[X_i : i \in \sigma]]$, which is again
local, is adically complete for its maximal ideal.
