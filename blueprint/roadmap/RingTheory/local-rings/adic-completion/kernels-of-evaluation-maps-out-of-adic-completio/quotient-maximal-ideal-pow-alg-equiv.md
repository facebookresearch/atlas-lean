# Isomorphism of maximal ideal power quotients of a ring and its completion

Let $A$ be a commutative Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $\widehat{A}$
be its $\mathfrak m$-adic completion, a local ring with maximal ideal $\widehat{\mathfrak m}$, and
let $k$ be a commutative ring over which $A$ is an algebra. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the
quotient of the completion by the $n$-th power of its maximal ideal is isomorphic as a $k$-algebra
to the corresponding quotient of $A$:

$$\widehat{A} / \widehat{\mathfrak m}^n \cong A / \mathfrak m^n,$$

the isomorphism sending the class of a compatible sequence to its component in $A / \mathfrak m^n$.
These quotients need not be finite sets: for $A = \mathbb{C}[[t]]$ and $n = 1$ both are
$\mathbb{C}$.
