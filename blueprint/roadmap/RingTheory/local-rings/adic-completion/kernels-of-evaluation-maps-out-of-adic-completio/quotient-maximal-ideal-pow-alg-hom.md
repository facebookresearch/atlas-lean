# Algebra map between maximal ideal power quotients of a ring and its completion

Let $A$ be a commutative Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $\widehat{A}$
be its $\mathfrak m$-adic completion, a local ring with maximal ideal $\widehat{\mathfrak m}$, and
let $k$ be a commutative ring over which $A$ is an algebra. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, evaluation
at level $n$, which sends a compatible sequence to its component in $A / \mathfrak m^n$, induces a
homomorphism of $k$-algebras $\widehat{A} / \widehat{\mathfrak m}^n \to A / \mathfrak m^n$ between
the quotients by the $n$-th powers of the maximal ideals; it is bijective.
