# Maximal ideal power quotients transported along an isomorphism

Let $A$ be a commutative Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $\widehat{A}$
be its $\mathfrak m$-adic completion, a local ring with maximal ideal $\widehat{\mathfrak m}$, and
let $k$ be a commutative ring over which $A$ is an algebra. Let $B$ be a local ring that is a
$k$-algebra, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_B$, and let $\Phi \colon \widehat{A} \to B$ be an
isomorphism of $k$-algebras. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there is an isomorphism of
$k$-algebras

$$B / \mathfrak m_B^n \cong A / \mathfrak m^n,$$

obtained by transporting the isomorphism
$\widehat{A} / \widehat{\mathfrak m}^n \cong A / \mathfrak m^n$ along $\Phi$.
