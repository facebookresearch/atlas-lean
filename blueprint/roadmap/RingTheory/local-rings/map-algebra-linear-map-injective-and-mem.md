# Adic completion of the invariants of a finite group action

Let `A` be a commutative Noetherian ring, `I` an ideal of `A`, and `S` a
commutative `A`-algebra that is finitely generated as an `A`-module and on
which `A` acts faithfully (the structure map `A → S` is injective). Let a
finite group `G` act on `S` by ring automorphisms commuting with the action
of `A`, and assume that `A` is the ring of invariants: every element of `S`
fixed by `G` comes from `A`.

Then the map of `I`-adic completions `AdicCompletion I A → AdicCompletion I
S` induced by `A → S` (with `S` viewed as an `A`-module) is injective, and
an element `x` of the `I`-adic completion of `S` lies in its image if and
only if `x` is fixed by the completion of the action of every `g ∈ G`.
