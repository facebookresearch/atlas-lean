# Maximal ideals of an integral monogenic algebra over a local ring contain the base maximal ideal

**Maximal ideals of an integral monogenic algebra over a local ring contain the base maximal
ideal.** Let $A$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, let $g \in A[X]$,
and suppose that $A[X]/(g)$ is integral over $A$. Then every maximal ideal $M$ of $A[X]/(g)$
contains the ideal of $A[X]/(g)$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak{m}$.
