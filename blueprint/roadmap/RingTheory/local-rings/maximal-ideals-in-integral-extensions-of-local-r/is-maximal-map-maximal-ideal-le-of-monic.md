# Maximal ideals of a monogenic algebra over a local ring contain the base maximal ideal, monic case

**Maximal ideals of a monogenic algebra over a local ring contain the base maximal ideal,
monic case.** Let $A$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ and let
$g \in A[X]$ be monic. Then every maximal ideal $M$ of $A[X]/(g)$ contains the ideal of
$A[X]/(g)$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak{m}$.
