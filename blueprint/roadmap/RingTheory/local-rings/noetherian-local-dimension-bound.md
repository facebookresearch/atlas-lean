# The Krull dimension of a Noetherian local ring is at most the number of generators of its maximal ideal

Let $(R, \mathfrak m)$ be a commutative Noetherian local ring. Then the Krull dimension of $R$ is at most the minimal number of generators of $\mathfrak m$. In particular, if $\mathfrak m$ can be generated by $n$ elements, then $\dim R \le n$, so $\dim R$ is finite.
