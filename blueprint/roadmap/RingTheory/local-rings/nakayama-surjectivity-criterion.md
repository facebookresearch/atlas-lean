# Nakayama's lemma for maps onto a finite module

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I$ an ideal contained in the Jacobson radical of $R$, $M$ an
$R$-module and $N$ a finitely generated $R$-module. If $f \colon M \to N$ is an $R$-linear map whose
composite with the quotient map $N \to N / IN$ is surjective, then $f$ is surjective.
