# Levelwise comparisons commute with transition maps

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, `𝔭` an ideal
of `A`, `𝔭 B = 𝔭.map (algebraMap A B)` the extended ideal, and `m ≤ n`
natural numbers. For every `y ∈ B ⧸ (𝔭 B) ^ n`, applying the level-`n`
comparison isomorphism `B ⧸ (𝔭 B) ^ n ≃ B ⧸ 𝔭 ^ n • B` and then the
transition map `B ⧸ 𝔭 ^ n • B → B ⧸ 𝔭 ^ m • B` gives the same result as
first applying the transition map `B ⧸ (𝔭 B) ^ n → B ⧸ (𝔭 B) ^ m` and then
the level-`m` comparison isomorphism.
