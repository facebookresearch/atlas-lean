# Inverse restriction-of-scalars comparison on images of elements

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, and let `𝔭` be
an ideal of `A`. The inverse of the `A`-linear isomorphism between the adic
completion of `B` with respect to the extended ideal `𝔭 B = 𝔭.map
(algebraMap A B)` and the `𝔭`-adic completion of the `A`-module `B` sends
the image of each `b ∈ B` in the `𝔭`-adic completion to the image of `b` in
the `𝔭 B`-adic completion.
