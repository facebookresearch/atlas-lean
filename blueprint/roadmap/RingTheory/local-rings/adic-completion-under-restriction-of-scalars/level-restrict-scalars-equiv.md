# Levelwise comparison of adic quotients under restriction of scalars

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, let `𝔭` be an
ideal of `A`, and let `n` be a natural number. Write `𝔭 B` for the extended
ideal `𝔭.map (algebraMap A B)`. This is the `A`-linear isomorphism between
`B ⧸ (𝔭 B) ^ n`, the quotient of `B` by the `n`-th power of the extended
ideal (regarded as an `A`-module), and `B ⧸ 𝔭 ^ n • B`, the quotient of the
`A`-module `B` by the submodule `𝔭 ^ n • B`. It sends the class of each `b ∈
B` to the class of `b`.
