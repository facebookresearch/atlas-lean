# Congruence ideal and cotangent length

Let `𝒪` be a principal ideal domain, `R` and `T` commutative `𝒪`-algebras,
`φ : R → T` a surjective `𝒪`-algebra homomorphism, and `π_R : R → 𝒪`, `π_T :
T → 𝒪` `𝒪`-algebra homomorphisms with `π_T ∘ φ = π_R`. Assume that `ker π_R`
is finitely generated. Let `η` be the congruence ideal, the ideal of `𝒪`
generated by the image under `π_T` of the annihilator of `ker π_T`. Then the
length of the `𝒪`-module `𝒪 ⧸ η` is at most the length of the cotangent
module `ker π_R ⧸ (ker π_R) ^ 2`.
