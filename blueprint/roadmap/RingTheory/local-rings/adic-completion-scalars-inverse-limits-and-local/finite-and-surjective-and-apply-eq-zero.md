# Inverse limits of surjective systems over an adically complete ring

Let `R` be a commutative ring that is `I`-adically complete for an ideal
`I`, and let `(M n)` be a sequence of `R`-modules with `R`-linear transition
maps `t n : M (n + 1) → M n`. Assume that every `t n` is surjective, that `I
^ (n + 1) • M n = 0` for every `n`, that the kernel of `t n` is contained in
`I ^ (n + 1) • M (n + 1)`, and that `M 0` is a finitely generated
`R`-module. Let `L` be a submodule of the product `∏ n, M n` whose elements
are exactly the families `x` with `t n (x (n + 1)) = x n` for all `n`.

Then `L` is a finitely generated `R`-module; for every `n` the projection `L
→ M n` is surjective; and for every `n` and every `x ∈ L`, `x n = 0` if and
only if `x ∈ I ^ (n + 1) • L`.
