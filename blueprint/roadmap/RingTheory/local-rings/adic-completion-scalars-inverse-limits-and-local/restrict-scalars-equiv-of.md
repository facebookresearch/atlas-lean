# Restriction-of-scalars comparison on images of elements

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, and let `𝔭` be
an ideal of `A`. The `A`-linear isomorphism from the adic completion of `B`
with respect to the extended ideal `𝔭 B = 𝔭.map (algebraMap A B)` to the
`𝔭`-adic completion of the `A`-module `B` sends the image of each `b ∈ B` in
the `𝔭 B`-adic completion to the image of `b` in the `𝔭`-adic completion; equivalently, its inverse
sends the image of `b` in the `𝔭`-adic completion to its image in the
`𝔭 B`-adic completion.
