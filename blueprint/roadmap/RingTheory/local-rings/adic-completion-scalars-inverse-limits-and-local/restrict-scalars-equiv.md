# Adic completion under restriction of scalars

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, and let `𝔭` be
an ideal of `A`. The adic completion of `B` with respect to the extended
ideal `𝔭 B = 𝔭.map (algebraMap A B)`, regarded as an `A`-module, is
isomorphic as an `A`-module to the `𝔭`-adic completion of `B` regarded as an
`A`-module. The isomorphism is induced at each level by the identity of `B`.
No Noetherian, completeness, or finiteness hypothesis is imposed.
