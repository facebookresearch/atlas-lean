# Powers of an extended ideal under restriction of scalars

Let `A` and `B` be commutative rings with `B` an `A`-algebra, let `𝔭` be an
ideal of `A`, and let `n` be a natural number. The ideal `(𝔭 B) ^ n` of `B`,
where `𝔭 B = 𝔭.map (algebraMap A B)` is the extended ideal, regarded as an
`A`-submodule of `B`, equals the `A`-submodule `𝔭 ^ n • B`.
