# Depth is invariant under finite local homomorphisms

Let `R → S` be a local homomorphism of commutative local rings making `S` a
finitely generated `R`-module, with `R` Noetherian, and let `M` be a
finitely generated `S`-module, regarded also as an `R`-module through `R →
S`. Then the depth of `M` as an `R`-module with respect to the maximal ideal
of `R` equals its depth as an `S`-module with respect to the maximal ideal
of `S`.
