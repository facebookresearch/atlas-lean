# Regular sequence on a module

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and $M$ an $R$-module. A finite sequence $x_1, \dots, x_n$ of elements of $R$ is *$M$-regular* if two conditions hold. First, for each $i = 1, \dots, n$, multiplication by $x_i$ is injective on the quotient module $M/(x_1, \dots, x_{i-1})M$, where $(x_1, \dots, x_{i-1})$ is the ideal generated by $x_1, \dots, x_{i-1}$; for $i = 1$ this quotient is $M$ itself. Second, $M \neq (x_1, \dots, x_n)M$.
