# Artin–Rees lemma for modules over a commutative Noetherian ring

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring with identity, $I$ an ideal of $R$, $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module and $N \subseteq M$ a submodule. Then there is a natural number $k$ such that $I^n M \cap N = I^{n-k}(I^k M \cap N)$ for every $n \ge k$.

## Sources

- David Eisenbud, Commutative Algebra with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 150, Springer-Verlag, 1995.
