# Cohen–Macaulay local ring

A commutative Noetherian local ring $(R, \mathfrak m)$ is *Cohen–Macaulay* if the depth of $R$, as a module over itself, equals the Krull dimension of $R$. Here the depth of $R$ is the common length of the maximal $R$-regular sequences in $\mathfrak m$.
