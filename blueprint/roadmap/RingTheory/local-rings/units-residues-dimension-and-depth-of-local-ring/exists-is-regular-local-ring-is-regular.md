# Power series over a complete DVR: regularity and dimension

Let `O` be a discrete valuation ring, complete for its maximal-ideal adic
topology, and let `p` be a natural number whose image in `O` is nonzero and
lies in the maximal ideal. Then `O⟦X⟧` is a regular local ring and a regular
ring, it is complete for its maximal-ideal adic topology, the inclusion `O →
O⟦X⟧` is a local homomorphism, the Krull dimension of `O⟦X⟧` is at most two,
and the image of `p` in `O⟦X⟧` is nonzero and lies in its maximal ideal.
