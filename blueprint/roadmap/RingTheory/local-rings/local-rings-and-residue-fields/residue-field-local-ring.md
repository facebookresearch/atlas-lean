# Residue field of a local ring

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity that is *local*: $1 \neq 0$, and whenever $a + b = 1$ in $R$, at least one of $a$ and $b$ is a unit. The non-units of $R$ then form an ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, the unique maximal ideal of $R$. The *residue field* of $R$ is the quotient ring $R/\mathfrak{m}$; it is a field because $\mathfrak{m}$ is maximal.
