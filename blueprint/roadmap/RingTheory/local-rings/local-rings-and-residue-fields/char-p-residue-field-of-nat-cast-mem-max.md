# Characteristic of the residue field of a local ring

Let $A$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $p$ be a prime number.
If $p \cdot 1 \in \mathfrak m$, then the residue field $A / \mathfrak m$ has characteristic $p$.
Primality is needed: $6 \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{(2)}$, the maximal ideal of $\mathbb{Z}_{(2)}$, but the
residue field $\mathbb{F}_2$ has characteristic $2$.
