# Completion at a maximal ideal via localization

Let `C` be a commutative ring and `𝔪` a maximal ideal of `C`. There is a
ring isomorphism `e` from the adic completion of the localization `C_𝔪` with
respect to its maximal ideal onto the `𝔪`-adic completion of `C`, compatible
with the canonical maps from `C`: for every `c ∈ C`, `e` sends the image of
`c` in the completion of `C_𝔪` to the image of `c` in the `𝔪`-adic
completion of `C`.
