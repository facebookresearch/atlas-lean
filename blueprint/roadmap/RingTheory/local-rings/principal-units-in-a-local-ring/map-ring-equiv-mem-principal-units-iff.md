# Ring automorphisms preserve the principal unit filtration

Let `R` be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal `𝔪`, and let `σ` be a
ring automorphism of `R`. For every natural number `k` and every unit `u` of
`R`, `σ u - 1 ∈ 𝔪 ^ k` if and only if `u - 1 ∈ 𝔪 ^ k`; that is, `σ`
preserves the principal units of every level.
