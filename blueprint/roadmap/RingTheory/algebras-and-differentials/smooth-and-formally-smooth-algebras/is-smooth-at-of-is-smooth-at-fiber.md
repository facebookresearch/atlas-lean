# Smoothness at a prime from smoothness of its fibre

Smoothness at one prime of the fiber implies smoothness upstairs.

Let `S` be a flat, finitely presented `R`-algebra, `q` a prime of `S` lying
over `p`, and `Q` a prime of the fiber `p.Fiber S` whose contraction along
`S → p.Fiber S` is `q`. If the fiber is smooth at `Q` alone, then `S` is
smooth at `q`.
