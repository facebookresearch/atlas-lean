# Unramified implies étale under a dimension inequality

Let `R` be a Noetherian integrally closed domain, `S` an `R`-algebra of
finite type, and `q` a prime ideal of `S` at which `S` is unramified over
`R`. If `dim R_{q ∩ R} ≤ dim S_q`, then `S` is étale over `R` at `q` and
`S_q` is flat over `R`.
