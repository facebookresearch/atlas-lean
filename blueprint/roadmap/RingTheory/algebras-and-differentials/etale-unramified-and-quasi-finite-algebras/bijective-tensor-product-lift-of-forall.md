# Galois descent for algebras

Let `𝒪 → 𝒪'` be a flat homomorphism of commutative rings, and let a finite
group `G` act on `𝒪'` by `𝒪`-algebra automorphisms `τ_σ`, Galois in the
sense that the map `𝒪' ⊗_𝒪 𝒪' → ∏_{σ ∈ G} 𝒪'`, `x ⊗ y ↦ (x τ_σ(y))_σ`, is
bijective. Let `A` be a commutative `𝒪'`-algebra with an action `θ` of `G`
by ring automorphisms that is semilinear over `τ`: `θ_σ(l a) = τ_σ(l)
θ_σ(a)` for `l ∈ 𝒪'` and `a ∈ A`. Let `C` be a commutative `𝒪`-algebra with
an injective `𝒪`-algebra homomorphism `ι : C → A` whose image is exactly the
set of elements of `A` fixed by `G`. Then the map `C ⊗_𝒪 𝒪' → A`, `c ⊗ l ↦
ι(c) l`, is bijective.
