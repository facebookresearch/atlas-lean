# Étale algebra maps from Galois-equivariant maps of geometric points

Let `K` be a field and `Ω` an algebraically closed field that is an
algebraic Galois extension of `K`, and let `B` and `C` be étale
`K`-algebras. Every map `Φ` from the `K`-algebra maps `C → Ω` to the
`K`-algebra maps `B → Ω` that commutes with composition by every
`K`-automorphism of `Ω` is induced by a unique `K`-algebra map `ψ : B → C`,
in the sense that `Φ χ = χ ∘ ψ` for every `χ`.
