# Quotients by finite locally free equivalence relations

Let `A`, `B`, `C` be commutative rings with an injective homomorphism `C →
A`, and let `s, t : A → B` be ring homomorphisms such that `B` is finite and
projective as an `A`-module both through `s` and through `t`, the images of
`s` and `t` together generate `B` as a ring, and for every commutative ring
`T` the relation on ring homomorphisms `A → T` given by `x ~ y` if there is
`φ : B → T` with `φ ∘ s = x` and `φ ∘ t = y` is an equivalence relation.
Suppose `s a = t a` exactly when `a` lies in the image of `C`. Then `A` is
finite, projective and faithfully flat over `C`, and the square formed by `C
→ A` (twice), `s` and `t` is a pushout of commutative rings, so `B ≅ A ⊗_C
A` with `s` and `t` the two coprojections.
