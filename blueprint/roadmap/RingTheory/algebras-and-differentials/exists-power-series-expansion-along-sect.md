# Power series expansion

Let `R → A` be a formally smooth homomorphism of commutative rings, and let
`e : A → R` be an `R`-algebra homomorphism (a section) with kernel `J` such
that the cotangent module `J ⧸ J ^ 2` is free of rank one over `R`. Then
there is a ring homomorphism `θ : A → R⟦X⟧` with the following properties:
`θ` sends each `r ∈ R` to the constant series `r`; for every `n` and every
`a ∈ A`, the coefficients of `θ a` in all degrees below `n` vanish if and
only if `a ∈ J ^ n`; and for every `n` and every power series `p ∈ R⟦X⟧`,
some `a ∈ A` has `θ a` agreeing with `p` in all degrees below `n`.
