# Point derivations of an algebra

Let $k$ be a field, $A$ a commutative $k$-algebra, $\mathrm{ev} \colon A \to k$ a homomorphism of
$k$-algebras (a $k$-point of $A$) and $M$ a vector space over $k$. The point derivations of $A$ at
$\mathrm{ev}$ with values in $M$ are the $k$-linear maps $D \colon A \to M$ such that

$$D(ab) = \mathrm{ev}(a)\, D(b) + \mathrm{ev}(b)\, D(a) \qquad \text{for all } a, b \in A.$$

They form a $k$-linear subspace of the space of $k$-linear maps $A \to M$.
