# Maximal étale subalgebra of a finite algebra over a field

Let `k` be a field of exponential characteristic `q` (so `q = 1` in
characteristic zero and `q = p` in characteristic `p > 0`), and let `A` be a
commutative `k`-algebra that is finite-dimensional over `k`. There is a
`k`-subalgebra `P` of `A` that is étale over `k` and contains every étale
`k`-subalgebra of `A`, such that every `x ∈ A` has a power `x ^ (q ^ n)`
that differs from an element of `P` by a nilpotent element.
