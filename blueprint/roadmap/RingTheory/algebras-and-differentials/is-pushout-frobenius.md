# Frobenius pushout square for an étale map

Let `p` be a prime, `A` and `B` commutative rings of characteristic `p`, and
`φ : A → B` an étale ring homomorphism. Then the commutative square `φ ∘
Frob_A = Frob_B ∘ φ`, where `Frob_A` and `Frob_B` are the Frobenius
endomorphisms `x ↦ x ^ p`, is a pushout in the category of commutative
rings: `B`, with the maps `φ : A → B` and `Frob_B : B → B`, is the pushout
of `Frob_A : A → A` and `φ : A → B`. Equivalently, the relative Frobenius of
`B` over `A` is an isomorphism.
