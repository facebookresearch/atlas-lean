# Lifting pure tensors through an idempotent quotient

Let $R$ be a commutative domain with field of fractions $K$, let $H$ be a commutative $R$-algebra and $e \in H$ an idempotent. Assume that for every nonzero $d \in R$ and every $y \in H$ there is $z \in H$ with $d\,z(1 - e) = y(1 - e)$. Let $S$ be a commutative $R$-algebra and $\pi : H \to S$ a surjective $R$-algebra homomorphism with kernel the ideal generated by $1 - e$.

If $\beta \in K \otimes_R H$ and $a \in S$ satisfy $(\mathrm{id}_K \otimes \pi)(\beta) = 1 \otimes a$, then there is $h \in H$ with $\beta = 1 \otimes h$ and $\pi(h) = a$.
