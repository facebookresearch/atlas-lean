# Iterated derivation of a power when $D\varepsilon = r\varepsilon^2$

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $A$ a commutative $R$-algebra and $D$ an $R$-linear derivation of
$A$. If $\varepsilon \in A$ and $r \in R$ satisfy $D(\varepsilon) = r \varepsilon^2$, then for all
natural numbers $n$ and $m$ the $n$-th iterate of $D$ satisfies

$$D^n(\varepsilon^m) = r^n\, m(m + 1) \cdots (m + n - 1)\, \varepsilon^{m + n}.$$

The ascending factorial $m(m + 1) \cdots (m + n - 1)$ is not divided out, so the formula holds in
every characteristic.
