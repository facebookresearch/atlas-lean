# Algebra maps from relation-preserving generator assignments

Let $K$ be a field, let $F$ and $L$ be commutative $K$-algebras, and let $(t_i)_{i \in I}$ and
$(a_i)_{i \in I}$ be families of elements of $F$ and of $L$ indexed by the same set $I$. Suppose
that

- some index $i_0$ has $t_{i_0} = 1$ and $a_{i_0} = 1$;
- for all $i, j \in I$ there is $k \in I$ with $t_k = t_i t_j$ and $a_k = a_i a_j$;
- every finite $K$-linear relation among the $t_i$ also holds among the $a_i$: if
  $\sum_{l=1}^{n} c_l\, t_{i_l} = 0$ with $c_l \in K$, then $\sum_{l=1}^{n} c_l\, a_{i_l} = 0$.

Then there is a homomorphism of $K$-algebras $\chi$ from the $K$-subalgebra of $F$ generated by
the $t_i$ to $L$ with $\chi(t_i) = a_i$ for every $i \in I$. The family $(t_i)$ need not be
injective or finite, and $L$ may have zero divisors.
