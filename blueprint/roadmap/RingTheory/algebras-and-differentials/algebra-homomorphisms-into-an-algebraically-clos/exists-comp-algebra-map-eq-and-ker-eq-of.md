# Lifting maps to algebraically closed fields along integral extensions

Let $S$ be a commutative algebra over a commutative ring $R$, integral over $R$, let $K$ be an
algebraically closed field and $\chi \colon R \to K$ a ring homomorphism. For every prime ideal $Q$
of $S$ lying over $\ker \chi$, that is, whose preimage in $R$ is $\ker \chi$, there is a ring
homomorphism $\chi' \colon S \to K$ that extends $\chi$ and has kernel exactly $Q$.

This is the key step for extending points of a scheme along integral morphisms, for going-up
arguments, and for lifting maps through integral closures: the condition $\ker \chi' = Q$ says the
lifted map has exactly the prescribed residue behavior.
