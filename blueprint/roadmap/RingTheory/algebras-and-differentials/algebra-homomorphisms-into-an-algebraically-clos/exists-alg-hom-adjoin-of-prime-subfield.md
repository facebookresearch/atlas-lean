# Extending a ring character via prime-subfield-valued linear forms

Let $\kappa$ be a field with prime subfield $\kappa_0$, let $B$ be a $\kappa$-algebra, not
necessarily commutative, and let $(c_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of $\kappa$-linear forms
$B \to \kappa$ that separates the points of $B$: if $c_i(b) = 0$ for every $i$, then $b = 0$. Let
$T$ be a commutative ring with ring homomorphisms $r \colon T \to B$ and $\rho \colon T \to \kappa$
such that $c_i(r(t)) \in \kappa_0$ for all $t \in T$ and $i \in I$, and $\rho(t) = 0$ whenever
$r(t) = 0$. Then there is a homomorphism of $\kappa$-algebras $\chi$ from the $\kappa$-subalgebra
of $B$ generated by $r(T)$ to $\kappa$ with $\chi(r(t)) = \rho(t)$ for every $t \in T$.

The family $(c_i)$ may be infinite, and $\kappa$ may have any characteristic.
