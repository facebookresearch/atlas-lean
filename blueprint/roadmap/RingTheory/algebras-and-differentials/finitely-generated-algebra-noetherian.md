# A finitely generated commutative algebra over a Noetherian commutative ring is Noetherian

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring with identity and let $S$ be a commutative $R$-algebra that is finitely generated as an $R$-algebra. Then $S$ is a Noetherian ring.
