# Norm in a product algebra is the product of norms

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $A$ and $B$ be $R$-algebras that are finite free as
$R$-modules. Then for all $x_1 \in A$ and $x_2 \in B$,

$$N_{(A \times B)/R}(x_1, x_2) = N_{A/R}(x_1)\, N_{B/R}(x_2).$$

Left multiplication by $(x_1, x_2)$ is the product of the left multiplications by $x_1$ and by
$x_2$, and the determinant of a product map is the product of the determinants.
