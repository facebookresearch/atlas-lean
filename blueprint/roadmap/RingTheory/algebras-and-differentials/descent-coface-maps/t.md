# Transport along a coface map

Write $f_!N = S \otimes_{R,f} N$ for the extension of scalars of an $R$-module $N$ along a ring homomorphism $f : R \to S$.

Let $A$ and $B$ be commutative rings with $B$ an $A$-algebra, let $i_1, i_2 : B \to B \otimes_A B$ be $b \mapsto b \otimes 1$ and $b \mapsto 1 \otimes b$, let $N$ be a $B$-module, let $c : B \otimes_A B \to B \otimes_A (B \otimes_A B)$ be any ring homomorphism, and let $\varphi : (i_1)_! N \to (i_2)_! N$ be a homomorphism of $(B \otimes_A B)$-modules. The transport of $\varphi$ along $c$ is the homomorphism

$$T_c(\varphi) : (c \circ i_1)_! N \;\cong\; c_!\,(i_1)_! N \xrightarrow{\;c_!\varphi\;} c_!\,(i_2)_! N \;\cong\; (c \circ i_2)_! N,$$

where the outer isomorphisms identify extension of scalars along a composite with the iterated extension.
