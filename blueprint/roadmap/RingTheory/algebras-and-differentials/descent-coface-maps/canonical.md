# Canonical descent isomorphism

Write $f_!N = S \otimes_{R,f} N$ for the extension of scalars of an $R$-module $N$ along a ring homomorphism $f : R \to S$.

Let $A$ and $B$ be commutative rings with $B$ an $A$-algebra via $\iota : A \to B$, let $i_1, i_2 : B \to B \otimes_A B$ be $b \mapsto b \otimes 1$ and $b \mapsto 1 \otimes b$, and let $M$ be an $A$-module. The canonical isomorphism is the isomorphism of $(B \otimes_A B)$-modules

$$(i_1)_!\,\iota_! M \;\cong\; (i_1 \circ \iota)_! M \;=\; (i_2 \circ \iota)_! M \;\cong\; (i_2)_!\,\iota_! M,$$

built from the isomorphisms identifying extension of scalars along a composite with the iterated extension, together with the equality $i_1 \circ \iota = i_2 \circ \iota$ (since $\iota(a) \otimes 1 = 1 \otimes \iota(a)$).
