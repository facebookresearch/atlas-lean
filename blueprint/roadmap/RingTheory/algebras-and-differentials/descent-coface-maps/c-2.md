# Coface map onto the last two factors

For commutative rings $A$ and $B$ with $B$ an $A$-algebra, $c_{23} : B \otimes_A B \to B \otimes_A (B \otimes_A B)$ is the ring homomorphism $x \mapsto 1 \otimes x$, so that

$$c_{23}(b \otimes b') = 1 \otimes (b \otimes b').$$

It places the two factors in positions 2 and 3 and inserts the unit in position 1.
