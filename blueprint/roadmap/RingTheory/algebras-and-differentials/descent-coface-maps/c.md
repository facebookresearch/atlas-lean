# Coface map onto the first two factors

For commutative rings $A$ and $B$ with $B$ an $A$-algebra, $c_{12} : B \otimes_A B \to B \otimes_A (B \otimes_A B)$ is the ring homomorphism $\mathrm{id}_B \otimes (b \mapsto b \otimes 1)$, so that

$$c_{12}(b \otimes b') = b \otimes (b' \otimes 1).$$

It places the two factors in positions 1 and 2 and inserts the unit in position 3.
