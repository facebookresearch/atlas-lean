# Coface map onto the outer factors

For commutative rings $A$ and $B$ with $B$ an $A$-algebra, $c_{13} : B \otimes_A B \to B \otimes_A (B \otimes_A B)$ is the ring homomorphism $\mathrm{id}_B \otimes (b \mapsto 1 \otimes b)$, so that

$$c_{13}(b \otimes b') = b \otimes (1 \otimes b').$$

It places the two factors in positions 1 and 3 and inserts the unit in position 2.
