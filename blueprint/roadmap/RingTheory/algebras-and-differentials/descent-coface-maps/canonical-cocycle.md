# Canonical descent isomorphism satisfies the cocycle condition

Let $A$ and $B$ be commutative rings with $B$ an $A$-algebra via $\iota : A \to B$, and let $M$ be an $A$-module. The canonical isomorphism of $(B \otimes_A B)$-modules

$$(i_1)_!\,\iota_! M \;\cong\; (i_2)_!\,\iota_! M,$$

obtained from $i_1 \circ \iota = i_2 \circ \iota$, where $i_1(b) = b \otimes 1$, $i_2(b) = 1 \otimes b$ and $f_!$ denotes extension of scalars along $f$, satisfies the cocycle condition: its transports along the coface maps $c_{12}, c_{23}, c_{13} : B \otimes_A B \to B \otimes_A (B \otimes_A B)$ satisfy $\varphi_{13} = \varphi_{23} \circ \varphi_{12}$.
