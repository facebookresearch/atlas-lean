# Distinct polynomial variables are weakly regular

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `σ` a type, `n : ℕ`, and `f : Fin n → σ` injective. Then
`List.ofFn (fun i ↦ X_(f i))` is a weakly regular sequence in `MvPolynomial σ R`: each
selected variable acts injectively modulo the ideal generated by the previously selected
variables.
