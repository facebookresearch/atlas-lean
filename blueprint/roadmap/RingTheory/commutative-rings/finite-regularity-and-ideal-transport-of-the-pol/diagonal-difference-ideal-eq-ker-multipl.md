# The diagonal ideal is the multiplication kernel

Let `k` be a commutative ring and `σ` a type. Put `A = MvPolynomial σ k`, `E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ`,
and `δ_i = X_i ⊗ op(1) - 1 ⊗ op(X_i)`. The ideal generated by the variable differences is the
kernel of `µ : E → A`, `µ(a ⊗ op(b)) = a*b`: `diagonalDifferenceIdeal k σ = RingHom.ker
(diagonalMultiplication k σ).toRingHom`.
