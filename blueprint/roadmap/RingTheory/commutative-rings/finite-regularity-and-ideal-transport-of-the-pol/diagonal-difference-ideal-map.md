# Diagonal coordinates identify the variable ideal

Let `k` be a commutative ring and `σ` a type. Put `A = MvPolynomial σ k`, `E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ`,
and `δ_i = X_i ⊗ op(1) - 1 ⊗ op(X_i)`. Under `Ψ = envelopingDiagonalCoordinates k σ : E ≃ₐ[k]
MvPolynomial σ A`, the image ideal of `diagonalDifferenceIdeal k σ` is `idealOfVars σ A`,
generated by the outer polynomial variables.
