# Membership in the diagonal ideal through coordinates

Let `k` be a commutative ring and `σ` a type. Put `A = MvPolynomial σ k`, `E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ`,
and `δ_i = X_i ⊗ op(1) - 1 ⊗ op(X_i)`. For `z : E`, `z ∈ diagonalDifferenceIdeal k σ` if and
only if `envelopingDiagonalCoordinates k σ z ∈ idealOfVars σ A`.
