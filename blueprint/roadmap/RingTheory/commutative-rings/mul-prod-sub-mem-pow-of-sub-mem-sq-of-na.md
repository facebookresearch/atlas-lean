# Products of congruences modulo powers of an ideal

For a prime `q` whose cast lies in an ideal `𝔪`, elements `x₀, x₁ ∈ 𝔪`, and
approximations `P c` to $x_1 + \bar c\, x_0$ modulo $\mathfrak m^2$ for each residue $c$ modulo $q$, where
$\bar c \in \{0, \dots, q-1\}$ is the natural representative of $c$, cast into $R$, the product
of the approximations, multiplied by `x₀`, agrees with `x₀ * x₁ ^ q - x₀ ^ q
* x₁` modulo `𝔪 ^ (q + 2)`.
