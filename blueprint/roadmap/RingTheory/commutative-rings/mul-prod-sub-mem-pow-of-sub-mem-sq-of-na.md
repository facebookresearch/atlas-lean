# Products of congruences modulo powers of an ideal

For a prime `q` whose cast lies in an ideal `𝔪`, elements `x₀, x₁ ∈ 𝔪`, and
approximations `P c` to `x₁ + ((c.val : ℕ) : R) * x₀` modulo `𝔪 ^ 2`, where
`c.val` is the canonical natural representative of `c : ZMod q`, the product
of the approximations, multiplied by `x₀`, agrees with `x₀ * x₁ ^ q - x₀ ^ q
* x₁` modulo `𝔪 ^ (q + 2)`.
