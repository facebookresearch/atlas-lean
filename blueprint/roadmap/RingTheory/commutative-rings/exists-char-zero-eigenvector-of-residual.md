# Characteristic-zero eigenvectors lifting a residual character

Let `T` be a commutative ring that is finitely generated and torsion-free as
a `ℤ`-module, let `M` be a finitely generated faithful `T`-module, and let
`χ : T → k` be a ring homomorphism to a field. Then there is a minimal prime
`𝔭` of `T` contained in `ker χ` such that no nonzero integer lies in `𝔭`,
the quotient `T ⧸ 𝔭` has characteristic zero, and `χ` factors through `T ⧸
𝔭`. Moreover there is a nonzero `x ∈ M` whose annihilator in `T` is exactly
`𝔭`, so that `T` acts on `x` through `T ⧸ 𝔭` (a Deligne–Serre lifting).
