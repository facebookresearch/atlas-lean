# Units characterization of the Jacobson radical of a commutative ring

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and let $J$ be its Jacobson radical, the intersection of all maximal ideals of $R$. For $x \in R$, $x \in J$ if and only if $xy + 1$ is a unit of $R$ for every $y \in R$. Equivalently, replacing $y$ by $-a$, $x \in J$ if and only if $1 - ax$ is a unit for every $a \in R$.
