# A finite-type integral commutative algebra over a commutative ring is module-finite

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and $A$ a commutative $R$-algebra. Suppose that $A$ is finitely generated as an $R$-algebra and that every element of $A$ is integral over $R$, that is, a root of a monic polynomial with coefficients in $R$ (mapped to $A$ by the structure map). Then $A$ is finitely generated as an $R$-module.

## Sources

- David Eisenbud, Commutative Algebra with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 150, Springer-Verlag, 1995.
