# Span descent after adjoining a transcendental constant

Span descent after adjoining an independent transcendental constant.

Let `t` be transcendental over `ℂ`, let `S` be a finite set containing `t`
whose elements are integral over the ring generated by the constants `F` and
`t`, and let `c` be transcendental over `F` whose image remains
transcendental over the field generated by `F` and `S`. Then every element
of the field obtained by adjoining `c` to the constants and `S` which is
integral over the corresponding ring with `t` adjoined lies in the `ℂ`-span
of the elements of the original field integral over that ring.
