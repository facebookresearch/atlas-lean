# Noether normalization for nonzero finite-type commutative algebras over a field

Let $k$ be a field and let $A \neq 0$ be a commutative $k$-algebra that is finitely generated as a $k$-algebra. Then there are a natural number $s$ and an injective homomorphism of $k$-algebras $g\colon k[X_0, \dots, X_{s-1}] \to A$ from the polynomial ring in $s$ variables over $k$ such that $A$, viewed as a module over $k[X_0, \dots, X_{s-1}]$ through $g$, is finitely generated.
