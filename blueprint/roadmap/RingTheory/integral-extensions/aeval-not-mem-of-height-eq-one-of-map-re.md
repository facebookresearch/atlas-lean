# Polynomials with nonzero residue avoid height-one primes

Let `A` be a discrete valuation ring with residue field `κ`, `C` a domain
which is an `A`-algebra, and `x ∈ C` such that the evaluation map `A[X] →
C`, `P ↦ P(x)`, is injective and makes `C` integral over `A[X]`. Let `Q` be
a prime ideal of `C` of height one containing the extension of the maximal
ideal of `A`. If `P ∈ A[X]` has nonzero reduction in `κ[X]`, then `P(x) ∉
Q`.
