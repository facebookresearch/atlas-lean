# Primes of the generic fibre of an integral extension

Primes of the generic fibre `F ⊗[S] B`, where `F` is a fraction ring of the
domain `S` and `B` is integral over `S`, correspond to the minimal primes of
`B`: every prime of the tensor product contracts to a minimal prime of `B`,
and every minimal prime of `B` has a unique prime above it. This holds when
every minimal prime of `B` contracts to zero in `S`.
