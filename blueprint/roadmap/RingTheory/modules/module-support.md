# Support of a module

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and $M$ an $R$-module. The *support* of $M$, written $\operatorname{Supp} M$, is the set of prime ideals $P$ of $R$ for which the localization $M_P$ of $M$ at the multiplicative set $R \setminus P$ is nonzero.
