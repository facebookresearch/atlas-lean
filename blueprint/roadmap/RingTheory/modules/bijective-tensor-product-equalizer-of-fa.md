# Faithfully flat descent for algebras

Let `S → S'` be a faithfully flat homomorphism of commutative rings, and let
`A'` be a commutative `S'`-algebra. Let `φ : A' ⊗_S S' ≅ S' ⊗_S A'` be an
isomorphism of `S`-algebras that is a descent datum: for `s, t ∈ S'` it
sends `s · 1 ⊗ t` to `s ⊗ t · 1`, and it satisfies the cocycle condition on
threefold tensor products. Let `A` be the `S`-subalgebra of `A'` consisting
of the elements `a` with `φ (a ⊗ 1) = 1 ⊗ a`. Then the map `S' ⊗_S A → A'`,
`s ⊗ a ↦ s a`, is bijective, so `A'` is the base change of `A` to `S'`.
