# Support of the quotient M/IM of a finitely generated module

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity, let $M$ be a finitely generated $R$-module and let $I$ be an ideal of $R$. Then the support of the $R$-module $M/IM$ is $\operatorname{Supp}(M/IM) = \operatorname{Supp} M \cap V(I)$. Here $IM$ is the submodule of $M$ generated by the products $am$ with $a \in I$ and $m \in M$, the support $\operatorname{Supp} M$ is the set of prime ideals $P$ of $R$ with $M_P \neq 0$, and $V(I)$ is the set of prime ideals of $R$ that contain $I$.
