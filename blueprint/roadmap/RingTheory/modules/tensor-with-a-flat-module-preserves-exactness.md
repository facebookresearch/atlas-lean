# Tensoring with a flat module preserves exactness

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a flat $R$-module. If $N \xrightarrow{f} N' \xrightarrow{g}
N''$ is an exact sequence of $R$-modules, that is, the image of $f$ is the kernel of $g$, then the
sequence $N \otimes_R M \to N' \otimes_R M \to N'' \otimes_R M$ of the maps $f \otimes
\mathrm{id}_M$ and $g \otimes \mathrm{id}_M$ is exact.

## Sources

- The Stacks Project, Definition 10.39.1, <https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00HB>, where this
  exactness, with $M$ on the left, is the definition of flatness.
