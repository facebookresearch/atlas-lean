# Direct summands of direct sums of finitely generated modules need not be such sums

The negation of Kourovka Problem 2.33: there exist a Noetherian ring $R$ (associative with $1$, not necessarily commutative; ascending chain condition on left ideals), an index type $\iota$, a family $(M_i)_{i\in\iota}$ of finitely generated left $R$-modules and a complemented submodule $N$ of $\bigoplus_{i\in\iota} M_i$ such that $N$ is not isomorphic to any direct sum $\bigoplus_{k\in\kappa} P_k$ of finitely generated left $R$-modules. The rings, index types and modules range over types of the lowest universe.

## Sources

E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), *Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook*, arXiv:1401.0300, Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.33 (V. I. Kuz'minov); answered "Not always" by P. A. Linnell, Nonfree projective modules for integral group rings, *Bull. London Math. Soc.* 14 (1982), 124-126: for a finite non-soluble group $G$ there is an infinitely generated projective $\mathbb{Z}G$-module that is not free and has no nonzero finitely generated direct summand. See also P. A. Linnell, G. Puninski and P. Smith, Idempotent ideals and non-finitely generated projective modules over integral group rings of polycyclic-by-finite groups, *J. Algebra* 305 (2006), 845–858 (arXiv:math/0512446).
