# Direct summands of direct sums of finitely generated modules

The statement (Kourovka Notebook Problem 2.33, V. I. Kuz'minov): for every Noetherian ring $R$ (associative with $1$, not necessarily commutative; Noetherian means the ascending chain condition on left ideals), every index type $\iota$ and every family $(M_i)_{i\in\iota}$ of finitely generated left $R$-modules, every submodule $N$ of the direct sum $\bigoplus_{i\in\iota} M_i$ that has a complement (a submodule $N'$ with $N \oplus N' = \bigoplus_i M_i$, i.e. $N \cap N' = 0$ and $N + N' = \bigoplus_i M_i$) is isomorphic, as a left $R$-module, to a direct sum $\bigoplus_{k\in\kappa} P_k$ of finitely generated left $R$-modules $P_k$ over some index type $\kappa$.

The rings, index types and modules range over types of the lowest universe.

## Sources

E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), *Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook*, arXiv:1401.0300, Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 2.33 (V. I. Kuz'minov).
