# Bases of a module near a prime where it is free

Let `M` be a finitely presented module over a commutative ring `T`, and `p`
a prime of `T` at which `M` is free. If a finite family `m : ι → M` maps to
a basis `1 ⊗ m i` of `κ(p) ⊗[T] M` over the residue field `κ(p)`, then there
is `t ∉ p` such that the images of the `m i` form a basis of the
localization of `M` away from `t`.
