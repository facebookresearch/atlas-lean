# Cluster algebras

Cluster algebras of Fomin and Zelevinsky: exchange matrices and matrix mutation, seeds and seed mutation, the Laurent phenomenon, rank-two cluster algebras and their periodicity in finite type, Somos sequences, and the finite type classification by Cartan–Killing types. Total positivity of matrices is under Linear algebra.

## Related topics

- [Total positivity](../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/total-positivity/README.md): cluster algebras were introduced as an algebraic framework for total positivity (Fomin and Zelevinsky, *Cluster algebras I*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 15 (2002), abstract); for example the six flag minors of $SL_3$ form a cluster algebra of rank one with the single exchange relation $P_2 P_{13} = P_1 P_{23} + P_3 P_{12}$ (Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Example 3.2.1).
- [Tropical varieties](../../AlgebraicGeometry/varieties-and-projective-geometry/tropical-varieties/README.md): tropicalising exchange relations replaces $+$ and $\times$ by $\min$ and $+$; the tropical semifield is the coefficient semifield of cluster algebras of geometric type (Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Section 3.6).
