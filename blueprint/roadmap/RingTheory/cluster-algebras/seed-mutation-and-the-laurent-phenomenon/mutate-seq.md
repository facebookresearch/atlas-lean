# Iterated seed mutation

Given a seed $s$ and a finite sequence $w = (k_1, \dots, k_r)$ of directions, iterated mutation
applies the seed mutations $\mu_{k_1}, \dots, \mu_{k_r}$ to $s$, from left to right (Fomin, Williams
and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Definitions
3.1.7–3.1.8: every seed of the seed pattern through $s$ is reached this way).
