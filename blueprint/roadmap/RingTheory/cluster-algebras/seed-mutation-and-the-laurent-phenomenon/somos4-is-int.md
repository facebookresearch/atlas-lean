# Integrality of the Somos-4 sequence

Every term of the Somos-4 sequence $1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 3, 7, 23, 59, \dots$ is an integer (Fomin,
Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5,
Example 3.4.3, deduced there from the Laurent phenomenon; Fomin and Zelevinsky, *The Laurent
phenomenon*, Adv. in Appl. Math. 28 (2002), Example 3.3, Laurentness of the generalized Somos-4
recurrence).

## Background

- [Laurent phenomenon (coefficient-free)](laurent-phenomenon.md): Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky deduce integrality from it, for the Somos-4 quiver of their Figure 3.8, whose mutations rotate it.
- [First terms of the Somos-4 sequence](somos4-four-to-nine.md): the values $2, 3, 7, 23, 59, 314$.
