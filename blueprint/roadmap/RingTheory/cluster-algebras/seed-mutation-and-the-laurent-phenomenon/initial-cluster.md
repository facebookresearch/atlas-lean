# Initial cluster of the ambient field

The cluster $(X_i)_{i \in n}$ of free generators of the field of rational functions
$\mathbb{Q}(X_i : i \in n)$, the fraction field of $\mathbb{Z}[X_i : i \in n]$ and the ambient field
of a labeled seed (Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*,
arXiv:1608.05735v5, Definition 3.1.1, with no frozen variables).
