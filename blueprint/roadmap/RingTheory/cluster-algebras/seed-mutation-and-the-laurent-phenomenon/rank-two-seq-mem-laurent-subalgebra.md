# Laurent phenomenon in rank two

For positive integers $b$ and $c$, every term of the rank-two exchange recurrence started at the
initial cluster $(X_0, X_1)$ of $\mathbb{Q}(X_0, X_1)$ is a Laurent polynomial in $X_0, X_1$ with
integer coefficients (Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters
1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Section 3.2 with Theorem 3.3.1 for the exchange matrices
$\pm\begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -c & 0 \end{pmatrix}$; Fomin and Zelevinsky, *Cluster algebras I*, J.
Amer. Math. Soc. 15 (2002), Theorem 3.1 and Section 6). It is the case $n = 2$ of the general
Laurent phenomenon for the skew-symmetrizable exchange matrix
$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -c & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, since seed mutation in rank two is the rank-two
recurrence. For the finite types $bc \le 3$ the terms are explicit Laurent polynomials; see [Cluster
algebras of rank two](../cluster-algebras-of-rank-two/README.md).

- [Laurent phenomenon (coefficient-free)](laurent-phenomenon.md): the general statement, of which this is the rank-two case.
