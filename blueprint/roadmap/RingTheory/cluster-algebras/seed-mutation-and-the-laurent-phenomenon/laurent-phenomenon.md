# Laurent phenomenon (coefficient-free)

Let $B$ be a skew-symmetrizable integer $n \times n$ matrix and start from the seed $((X_i), B)$ in
$\mathbb{Q}(X_i : i \in n)$. Every cluster variable of every seed obtained by iterated mutation is a
Laurent polynomial in the $X_i$ with integer coefficients (Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky,
*Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Theorem 3.3.1, in the case
without frozen variables; Fomin and Zelevinsky, *Cluster algebras I*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 15 (2002),
Theorem 3.1). The source theorem also allows frozen variables and expands in every extended cluster;
this statement is the coefficient-free case, relative to the initial cluster, which by relabelling
the initial seed covers expansions in any cluster of the pattern.
