# Somos-4 sequence

The Somos-4 sequence of rational numbers is defined by $z_0 = z_1 = z_2 = z_3 = 1$ and

$$z_{m+4} = \frac{z_{m+3}\, z_{m+1} + z_{m+2}^2}{z_m} \qquad (m \ge 0),$$

the recurrence $z_{m+2}\, z_{m-2} = z_{m+1}\, z_{m-1} + z_m^2$ of Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky,
*Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Example 3.4.3, solved for the
newest term.
