# Laurent polynomials in the initial cluster

The ring $\mathbb{Z}[X_i^{\pm 1}]$ of Laurent polynomials with integer coefficients in the initial
cluster, as the subring of $\mathbb{Q}(X_i : i \in n)$ generated by the $X_i$ and their inverses
(Fomin, Williams and Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*,
arXiv:1608.05735v5, Theorem 3.3.1, where the conclusion is membership in this ring).
