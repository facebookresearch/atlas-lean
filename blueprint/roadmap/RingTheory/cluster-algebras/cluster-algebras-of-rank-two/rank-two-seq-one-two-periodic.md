# Six-periodicity in type $B_2$

Let $(z_k)$ be the rank-two exchange recurrence with $b = 1$ and $c = 2$ over a field, and
suppose that every term $z_k$ is nonzero. Then $z_{k+6} = z_k$ for every $k$: the cluster
variables of the coefficient-free cluster algebra $\mathcal{A}(1, 2)$ repeat with period
$6 = h + 2$, where $h = 4$ is the Coxeter number of $B_2$.
