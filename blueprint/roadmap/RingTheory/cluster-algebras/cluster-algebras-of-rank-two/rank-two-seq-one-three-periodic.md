# Eight-periodicity in type $G_2$

Let $(z_k)$ be the rank-two exchange recurrence with $b = 1$ and $c = 3$ over a field, and
suppose that every term $z_k$ is nonzero. Then $z_{k+8} = z_k$ for every $k$: the cluster
variables of the coefficient-free cluster algebra $\mathcal{A}(1, 3)$ repeat with period
$8 = h + 2$, where $h = 6$ is the Coxeter number of $G_2$.

## Sources

- S. Fomin and A. Zelevinsky, *Cluster algebras I*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 15 (2002), Proposition 6.2 (trivial coefficients).
- S. Fomin, L. Williams and A. Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Exercise 3.2.9.
