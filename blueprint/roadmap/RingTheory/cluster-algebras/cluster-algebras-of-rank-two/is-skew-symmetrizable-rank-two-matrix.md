# The exchange matrix of $\mathcal{A}(b, c)$ is skew-symmetrizable

For positive integers $b$ and $c$ the matrix
$B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -c & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ is skew-symmetrizable: the positive integers
$d_1 = c$ and $d_2 = b$ satisfy $d_i b_{ij} = -d_j b_{ji}$ for all $i, j$.

## Sources

- S. Fomin, L. Williams and A. Zelevinsky, *Introduction to cluster algebras, Chapters 1–3*, arXiv:1608.05735v5, Definition 2.7.4 and Section 3.2, where $b$ and $c$ are both positive or both zero.
