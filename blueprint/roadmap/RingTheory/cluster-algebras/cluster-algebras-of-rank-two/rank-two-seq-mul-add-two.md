# Exchange relation of the rank-two recurrence

Let $(z_k)$ be the rank-two exchange recurrence for $b, c$ over a field. If $z_k \ne 0$, then

$$z_k\, z_{k+2} = z_{k+1}^{e} + 1,$$

with $e = c$ for even $k$ and $e = b$ for odd $k$.
