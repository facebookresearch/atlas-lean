# Exchange matrix of $\mathcal{A}(b, c)$

For natural numbers $b$ and $c$, consider the integer matrix

$$B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -c & 0 \end{pmatrix},$$

whose mutations alternate between $B$ and $-B$. When $b$ and $c$ are both positive, or both zero, it
is the exchange matrix of the rank-two cluster algebra $\mathcal{A}(b, c)$. Otherwise it is not
sign-skew-symmetric: for $b = 0$ and $c = 1$ the entry $b_{21} = -1$ is nonzero while $b_{12} = 0$.
