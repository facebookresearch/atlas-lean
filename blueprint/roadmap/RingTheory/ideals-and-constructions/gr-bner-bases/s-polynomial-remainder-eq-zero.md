# S-polynomials of a Gröbner basis reduce to zero

Let $k$ be a field and fix a monomial order on the monomials of the polynomial ring
$k[x_i : i \in \sigma]$. If $G$ is a Gröbner basis of an ideal $I$ and $g, h \in G$, then every
remainder of the S-polynomial $S(g, h)$ on division by $G$ is zero: the easy direction of
Buchberger's criterion, since $S(g, h) \in I$.
