# Gröbner basis

Let $k$ be a field and fix a monomial order on the monomials of the polynomial ring
$k[x_i : i \in \sigma]$. A set $G$ of polynomials is a Gröbner basis of an ideal $I$ when
$G \subseteq I$ and the leading monomial of every nonzero $f \in I$ is divisible by the leading
monomial of some nonzero $g \in G$. Equivalently, the leading terms of $G$ generate the initial
ideal $\mathrm{in}(I)$. The set $G$ need not be finite.
