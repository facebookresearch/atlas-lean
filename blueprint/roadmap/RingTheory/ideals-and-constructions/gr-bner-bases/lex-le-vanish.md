# Lexicographic order and final segments of variables

Let $\sigma$ be a linearly ordered set and $S \subseteq \sigma$ a final segment: every index outside
$S$ comes before every index in $S$. If an exponent vector $e \in \mathbb{N}^{(\sigma)}$ vanishes
outside $S$, then every exponent vector $d \in \mathbb{N}^{(\sigma)}$ that is lexicographically at
most $e$ vanishes outside $S$.
