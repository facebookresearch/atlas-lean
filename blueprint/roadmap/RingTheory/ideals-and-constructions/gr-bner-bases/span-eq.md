# A Gröbner basis generates its ideal

Let $k$ be a field and fix a monomial order on the monomials of the polynomial ring
$k[x_i : i \in \sigma]$. If $G$ is a Gröbner basis of an ideal $I$, then $I$ is the ideal generated
by $G$. The proof divides by the nonzero elements of $G$, whose leading coefficients are units, and
applies the remainder criterion.
