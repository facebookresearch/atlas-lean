# Milnor squares of ideals with zero intersection

Let `I₁` and `I₂` be ideals of a commutative ring `A` with `I₁ ⊓ I₂ = ⊥`.
Then the map `A → A ⧸ I₁ × A ⧸ I₂` is injective, and its image is exactly
the set of pairs `(x₁, x₂)` whose images in `A ⧸ (I₁ ⊔ I₂)` agree: every
such pair comes from some `a ∈ A`.
