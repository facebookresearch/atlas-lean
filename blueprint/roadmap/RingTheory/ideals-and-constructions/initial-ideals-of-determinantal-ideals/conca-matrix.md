# Row-graded matrix of linear forms

For a field $K$, $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and coefficients $a_{ijk} \in K$, the $m \times n$ matrix $L$ over $K[t_{ij} : i < m, j < n]$ with entries $L_{ij} = \sum_{k < n} a_{ijk}\, t_{ik}$; every entry of row $i$ is a linear form in the variables $t_{i0}, \dots, t_{i,n-1}$ of that row.

Source: A. Conca, *Products of linear spaces, polymatroids and integral posets*, in Kommutative Algebra, Oberwolfach Reports 2 (2005), issue 2 (Report 19/2005), 1087–1090, Conjecture, p. 1089.
