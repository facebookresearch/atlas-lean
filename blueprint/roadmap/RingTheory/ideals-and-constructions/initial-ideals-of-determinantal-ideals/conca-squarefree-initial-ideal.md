# Conca's conjecture on initial ideals of 2-minors of linear-form matrices

This is the proposition that for every infinite field $K$, all $m \le n$, all coefficients $a_{ijk} \in K$ and every monomial order on $K[t_{ij} : i < m, j < n]$, the initial ideal of the ideal $I_2(L)$ of $2$-minors of the matrix $L_{ij} = \sum_{k} a_{ijk}\, t_{ik}$ is square-free in the $\mathbb{Z}^m$-graded sense: it is generated by monomials containing at most one variable from each row (Conca's conjecture, restricted to infinite $K$ and $m \le n$).

Source: A. Conca, *Products of linear spaces, polymatroids and integral posets*, in Kommutative Algebra, Oberwolfach Reports 2 (2005), issue 2 (Report 19/2005), 1087–1090, Conjecture, p. 1089.
