# Square-free monomial ideal in the row grading

For an ideal $J \subseteq K[t_{ij} : i < m, j < n]$, the proposition that $J$ is square-free in the $\mathbb{Z}^m$-graded sense ($\deg t_{ij} = e_i$): $J$ is generated by a set of monomials each containing, for every row $i$, at most one variable $t_{ij}$ and that to the first power.

Source: A. Conca, *Products of linear spaces, polymatroids and integral posets*, in Kommutative Algebra, Oberwolfach Reports 2 (2005), issue 2 (Report 19/2005), 1087–1090, Conjecture, p. 1089.
