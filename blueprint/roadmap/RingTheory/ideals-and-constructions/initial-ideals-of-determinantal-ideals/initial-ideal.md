# Initial ideal for a monomial order

For a field $K$, a monomial order on $K[x_s : s \in \sigma]$ and an ideal $I$, the ideal generated by the leading monomials (with coefficient $1$) of the nonzero elements of $I$.

Source: A. Conca, *Products of linear spaces, polymatroids and integral posets*, in Kommutative Algebra, Oberwolfach Reports 2 (2005), issue 2 (Report 19/2005), 1087–1090, Conjecture, p. 1089.
