# Initial ideals of 2-minors of row-graded linear matrices are square-free

For every infinite field $K$, all $m \le n$, all coefficients $a_{ijk} \in K$ and every monomial order on $K[t_{ij} : i < m, j < n]$, the initial ideal of the ideal $I_2(L)$ of $2$-minors of the matrix $L_{ij} = \sum_{k} a_{ijk}\, t_{ik}$ is square-free in the $\mathbb{Z}^m$-graded sense: it is generated by monomials containing at most one variable from each row. Conca, De Negri and Gorla show that $I_2(L)$ has a universal Gröbner basis of multihomogeneous elements of multidegree at most $e_1 + \dots + e_m$.
