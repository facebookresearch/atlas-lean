# Rational points avoiding finitely many nonzero functions

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field (of any characteristic) and $B$ an integral domain that is a $k$-algebra of finite type. For every finite family $(g_i)_{i \in I}$ of nonzero elements of $B$ there is a $k$-algebra homomorphism $\chi : B \to k$ with $\chi(g_i) \neq 0$ for all $i$. The family may be empty.
