# Integer polynomials take a large value at a small nonnegative integer

Let
$f \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ have degree $d$. Then there is an integer $x$ with $0 \le x \le d$ such that
$|f(x)| \ge d!/2^d$.

## Sources
