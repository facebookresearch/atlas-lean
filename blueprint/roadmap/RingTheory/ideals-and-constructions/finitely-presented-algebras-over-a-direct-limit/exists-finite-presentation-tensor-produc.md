# Finitely presented algebras descend along direct limits

Let `(G i)` be a directed system of commutative rings indexed by a nonempty
directed preorder `ι`, with transition ring homomorphisms `f i j : G i → G
j` for `i ≤ j`, and let `R` be a commutative ring that is a direct limit of
the system via the structure maps `G i → R`. Then every finitely presented
`R`-algebra `A` descends to a finite stage: there are an index `i`, a
finitely presented `G i`-algebra `A₀`, and an isomorphism of `R`-algebras `R
⊗_{G i} A₀ ≅ A`.
