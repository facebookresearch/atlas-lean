# Witt vector addition and ideal powers

Let `p` be a prime, `R` a commutative ring, `I` an ideal of `R`, `c` a
natural number, and `x, y` `p`-typical Witt vectors over `R` such that every
coefficient `x_i` lies in `I` and every coefficient `y_i` lies in `I ^ (p ^
i + c)`. Then for every `n`, `(x + y)_n - x_n ∈ I ^ ((p - 1) n + 1 + c)`.
