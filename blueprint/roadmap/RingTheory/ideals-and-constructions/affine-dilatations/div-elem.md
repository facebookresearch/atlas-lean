# The fraction g / a in an affine dilatation ring

Let `A` be a commutative ring, `I` an ideal of `A`, `a ∈ A`, and `g ∈ I`.
This is the element `g / a` of the affine dilatation ring `A[I/a]`, the
`A`-subalgebra of the localization `A[1/a]` generated by all fractions `h /
a` with `h ∈ I`; its image in `A[1/a]` is the fraction `g / a`.
