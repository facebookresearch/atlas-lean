# Affine dilatation ring

Let `A` be a commutative ring, `I` an ideal of `A`, and `a ∈ A`. The affine
dilatation ring `A[I/a]` of `Spec A` along `I` at `a` is the `A`-subalgebra
of the localization `A[1/a]` generated by the fractions `g / a` for `g ∈ I`,
regarded as a commutative ring in its own right. As an `A`-subalgebra of
`A[1/a]` it is the affine dilatation subalgebra.
