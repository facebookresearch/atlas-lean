# Affine dilatations along finitely generated ideals are of finite type

Let `R` and `A` be commutative rings with `A` an `R`-algebra of finite type,
let `I` be a finitely generated ideal of `A`, and let `a ∈ A`. Then the
affine dilatation ring `A[I/a]`, the `A`-subalgebra of the localization
`A[1/a]` generated by the fractions `g / a` for `g ∈ I`, is an `R`-algebra
of finite type.
