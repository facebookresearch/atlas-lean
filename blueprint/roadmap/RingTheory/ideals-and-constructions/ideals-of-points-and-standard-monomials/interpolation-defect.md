# Generator minus its representation in a monomial basis

For a finite set $B$ of exponent vectors in $d$ variables, an exponent vector $g$ and coefficients $c \colon \mathbb{N}^d \to \mathbb{Q}$, the polynomial $f_g = x^g - \sum_{\alpha \in B} c_\alpha x^\alpha \in \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_d]$.

Source: AIM workshop *Computational algebraic statistics*, problem list, https://aimath.org/WWN/compalgstat/compalgstat.pdf, Question 47 (Pistone), where the polynomials $f_g$ appear in the statement of the question.
