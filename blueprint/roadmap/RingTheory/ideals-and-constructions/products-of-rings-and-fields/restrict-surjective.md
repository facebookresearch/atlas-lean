# Coordinate restriction is surjective

Let $K$ be a field, $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a
family of fields that are $K$-algebras indexed by a type $\iota$, and $S \subseteq \iota$ a
finite subset. The coordinate restriction $\prod_{i \in \iota} F_i \to \prod_{i \in S} F_i$ is
surjective.

A preimage is obtained by extending by zero outside $S$.
