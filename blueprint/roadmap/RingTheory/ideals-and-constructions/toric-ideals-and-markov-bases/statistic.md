# Sufficient-statistic map of a toric model

Let $X$ be a set of cells, possibly infinite, and let $T$ assign to each cell $x$ a finitely
supported exponent vector $T(x) \in \mathbb{N}^{(J)}$, for a set $J$ of parameters. Tables of counts
are finitely supported maps $X \to \mathbb{N}$, moves are finitely supported maps
$X \to \mathbb{Z}$, and the sufficient statistic of a table $a$ is $T(a) = \sum_x a(x)\, T(x)$. This
sufficient statistic is the additive map $a \mapsto T(a)$ from tables of counts to
$\mathbb{N}^{(J)}$; its fibres are the sets of tables with the same sufficient statistic.

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and B. Sturmfels, *Algebraic algorithms for sampling from conditional distributions*, Ann. Statist. 26 (1998), 363–397, equation (1.3).
