# Toric map of the Birkhoff model

For a field $K$ and $p \in \mathbb{N}$, the $K$-algebra homomorphism $K[p_\sigma : \sigma \in S_p] \to K[\psi_{jk} : j, k < p]$ sending the variable $p_\sigma$ of a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, p-1\}$ to $\prod_{j} \psi_{j\,\sigma(j)}$. Its kernel is the toric ideal of the $p^2 \times p!$ matrix whose columns are the $p \times p$ permutation matrices.

Source: AIM workshop *Computational algebraic statistics*, problem list, https://aimath.org/WWN/compalgstat/compalgstat.pdf, Problem 1.
