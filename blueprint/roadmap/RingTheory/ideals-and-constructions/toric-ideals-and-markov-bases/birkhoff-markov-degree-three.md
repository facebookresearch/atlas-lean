# The Diaconis–Eriksson conjecture (Markov degree of the Birkhoff model)

This is the proposition that for every field $K$ and every $p \ge 3$, the toric ideal of the Birkhoff model, the kernel of the $K$-algebra map $K[p_\sigma : \sigma \in S_p] \to K[\psi_{jk} : j, k < p]$, $p_\sigma \mapsto \prod_{j} \psi_{j\,\sigma(j)}$ (the toric ideal of the matrix whose columns are the $p \times p$ permutation matrices) is generated by its binomials $x^u - x^v$ of degree at most $3$ (that is $|u|, |v| \le 3$, with $x^u$ and $x^v$ having the same image), but not by its binomials of degree at most $2$ (the Markov degree of the Birkhoff model is $3$). This degree statement is the Diaconis–Eriksson conjecture. Problem 1 of the AIM workshop on computational algebraic statistics, which motivated it here, asks for more: an explicit Markov basis of the Birkhoff model.

Source: AIM workshop *Computational algebraic statistics*, problem list, https://aimath.org/WWN/compalgstat/compalgstat.pdf, Problem 1 (an explicit Markov basis; the motivation for this statement).
