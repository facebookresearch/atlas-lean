# The Birkhoff model has Markov degree three

For every field $K$ and every $p \ge 3$, the toric ideal of the Birkhoff model, the kernel of the $K$-algebra map $K[p_\sigma : \sigma \in S_p] \to K[\psi_{jk} : j, k < p]$, $p_\sigma \mapsto \prod_{j} \psi_{j\,\sigma(j)}$ (the toric ideal of the matrix whose columns are the $p \times p$ permutation matrices) is generated by its binomials $x^u - x^v$ of degree at most $3$ (that is $|u|, |v| \le 3$, with $x^u$ and $x^v$ having the same image), but not by its binomials of degree at most $2$. This is the Diaconis–Eriksson conjecture. (For $p = 1, 2$ the ideal is zero.)
