# Low-degree binomials in the Birkhoff toric ideal

For a field $K$ and $p, d \in \mathbb{N}$, the set of binomials $x^u - x^v$ in $K[p_\sigma : \sigma \in S_p]$ with exponent vectors $u, v$ of total degree at most $d$ such that $x^u$ and $x^v$ have the same image under the toric map $p_\sigma \mapsto \prod_j \psi_{j\,\sigma(j)}$.

Source: AIM workshop *Computational algebraic statistics*, problem list, https://aimath.org/WWN/compalgstat/compalgstat.pdf, Problem 1.
