# Ghost data compatible with a Frobenius lift come from a Witt vector

Let $p$ be a prime, $R$ a commutative ring and $\sigma$ a ring endomorphism of $R$ that lifts the
Frobenius modulo $p$: $\sigma(a) - a^p \in pR$ for every $a \in R$. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let
$g_0, \dots, g_{n-1} \in R$ satisfy $g_{k+1} - \sigma(g_k) \in p^{k+1} R$ whenever $k + 1 < n$. Then
there is a $p$-typical Witt vector $x$ over $R$ whose ghost components are
$w_k(x) = g_k$ for all $k < n$.
