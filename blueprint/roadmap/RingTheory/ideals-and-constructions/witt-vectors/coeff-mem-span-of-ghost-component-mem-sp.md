# Divisibility of Witt coefficients from divisible ghost components

Let $p$ be a prime and $S$ a commutative ring in which $p$ is a non-zero-divisor and $S/pS$ is
reduced. If $z = (z_0, z_1, \dots)$ is a $p$-typical Witt vector over $S$ and $N \ge 1$ is such that
the ghost component $w_{N-1}(z) = \sum_{i \le N-1} p^i z_i^{p^{N-1-i}}$ lies in $p^N S$, then
$z_j \in pS$ for every $j < N$. For $N = 0$ there is nothing to prove.
