# Ghost components determine Witt vector coefficients

Let $p$ be a prime and $R$ a commutative ring in which $p$ is a non-zero-divisor, and let $n$ be a
natural number. If two $p$-typical Witt vectors $x$ and $y$ over $R$ have equal ghost components
$w_k(x) = w_k(y)$ for all $k < n$, where $w_k(x) = \sum_{i \le k} p^i x_i^{p^{k-i}}$, then their
coefficients agree: $x_k = y_k$ for all $k < n$.
