# Homomorphisms from Witt vectors over finite fields commute with Frobenius

Let $p$ be a prime, $F$ a finite field of characteristic $p$, and $k$ a perfect commutative ring of
characteristic $p$. Every ring homomorphism $\iota \colon W(F) \to W(k)$ between the rings of
$p$-typical Witt vectors commutes with the Witt vector Frobenius $\varphi$:
$\iota(\varphi(x)) = \varphi(\iota(x))$ for every $x \in W(F)$.
