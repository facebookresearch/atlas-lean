# Valuation subrings in which a natural number is a nonunit

For a natural number `q`, a valuation subring of `L` satisfies this
predicate when `q`, viewed in `L`, is a nonunit of the subring. The
definition applies to every natural `q` (it can hold at `q = 4` in
`ℤ_(2)`); for a rational prime `q` it says the subring lies over `q`.
