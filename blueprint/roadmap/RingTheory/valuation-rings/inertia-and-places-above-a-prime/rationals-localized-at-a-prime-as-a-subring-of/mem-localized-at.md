# Membership in the rationals localized at p

For a natural number `p` and a rational number `q`, `q` lies in the subring $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ of $\mathbb{Q}$ (for prime $p$, the localization of $\mathbb{Z}$ at $p$, viewed inside $\mathbb{Q}$) if and only if the denominator of `q` in lowest
terms is coprime to `p`.
