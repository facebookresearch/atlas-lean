# Rationals localized at p

For a natural number `p`, this is the subring of `ℚ` consisting of the
rationals whose denominator in lowest terms is coprime to `p`. When `p` is
prime it is the localization `ℤ_(p)` of `ℤ` at the prime `p`, viewed inside
`ℚ`; for `p = 0` it is `ℤ`, and for `p = 1` it is all of `ℚ`.
