# Power series over a DVR modulo $X - \varpi$ form a complete DVR

Let $O$ be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $\varpi$. Then the quotient
$O[[X]] / (X - \varpi)$ of the power series ring is a discrete valuation ring that is adically
complete for its maximal ideal, and the image of $\varpi$ is a uniformizer of it.
