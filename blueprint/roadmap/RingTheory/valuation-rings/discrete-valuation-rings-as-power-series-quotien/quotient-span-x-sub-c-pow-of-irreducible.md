# Power series over a DVR modulo $X - \varpi^e$ form a local PID

Let $W$ be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $\varpi$ and let $e \ge 1$ be an integer. Then
the ideal $(X - \varpi^e)$ of the power series ring $W[[X]]$ is prime, the quotient
$W[[X]] / (X - \varpi^e)$ is a local principal ideal ring, and the image of $\varpi$ in it is
irreducible.
