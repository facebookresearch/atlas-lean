# Power series over a DVR modulo $X - \varpi^e$ form a DVR

Let $W$ be a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer $\varpi$ and let $e \ge 1$ be an integer. Then
the quotient $W[[X]] / (X - \varpi^e)$ of the power series ring is a discrete valuation ring, and
the image of $\varpi$ is a uniformizer of it. The condition $e \ge 1$ is needed: for $e = 0$ the
series $X - 1$ is a unit and the quotient is the zero ring.
