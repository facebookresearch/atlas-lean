# Values of a valuation on an algebraic extension

In an algebraic extension `F/E`, every nonzero value of a valuation subring
of `F` has a positive power equal to the value of a nonzero element of the
base field: for `g ≠ 0` there are `0 < n` and `c ≠ 0` in `E` with
$v_O(g^n) = v_O(c)$, where $v_O$ is the valuation of the subring $O$ and $c$ is viewed in $F$. No
finite-dimensionality, rank-one, completeness, or triviality hypothesis on
`E` is assumed.
