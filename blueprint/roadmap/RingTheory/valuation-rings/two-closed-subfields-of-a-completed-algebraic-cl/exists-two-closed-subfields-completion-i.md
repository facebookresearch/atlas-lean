# Two closed subfields meeting in the closure of the rationals

Let `r` be a prime, `A` a valuation subring of an algebraic closure `ℚ̄` of
`ℚ` lying over `r`, and `Q_A` the closure of the prime field in the
completion of `ℚ̄` with respect to the valuation of `A`. There are two
closed subfields `L₁` and `L₂` of this completion, each containing `Q_A`,
such that `L₁ ∩ L₂ = Q_A` and each of `L₁`, `L₂` has uncountably many
elements outside `Q_A`.
