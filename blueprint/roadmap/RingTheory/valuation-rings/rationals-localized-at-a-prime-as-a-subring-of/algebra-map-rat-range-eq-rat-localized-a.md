# A discrete valuation ring with fraction field ℚ

Let `R` be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field `ℚ`, and let `p` be
a prime number that is irreducible in `R`. Then the image of `R` in `ℚ` is
the subring of rationals whose denominator in lowest terms is coprime to
`p`, that is, the localization of `ℤ` at `p`.
