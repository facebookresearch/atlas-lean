# Inertia fixes roots of unity of residually invertible order

Let `L/K` be a field extension, `A` a valuation subring of `L`, and `σ` an
element of the inertia subgroup of `A` in the group of `K`-automorphisms of
`L`. If `ζ ∈ L` satisfies `ζ ^ n = 1` for some nonzero natural number `n`
whose image in `L` is a unit of `A` (not a nonunit of `A`), then `σ ζ = ζ`.
