# Inertia subgroup in the full automorphism group

Let `L/K` be a field extension and `A` a valuation subring of `L`. This is
the inertia subgroup of `A`, consisting of the `K`-automorphisms of `L` that
map `A` onto itself and induce the identity on the residue field of `A`,
regarded as a subgroup of the full group of `K`-algebra automorphisms of
`L`.
