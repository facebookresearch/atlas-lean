# Inertia fixes roots of unity of order prime to q

Let `q` be a prime, `A` a valuation subring of an algebraic closure `ℚ̄` of
`ℚ` lying over `q`, and `σ` an element of the inertia subgroup of `A` in
`Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)`. If `ζ ∈ ℚ̄` satisfies `ζ ^ n = 1` for some natural number `n`
not divisible by `q`, then `σ ζ = ζ`.
