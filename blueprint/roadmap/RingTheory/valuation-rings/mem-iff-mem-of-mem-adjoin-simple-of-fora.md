# Valuation subrings agreeing on a simple extension

Let `A₀` be a valuation subring of a field `L` whose maximal ideal is
generated by `ϖ`, let `F` be a field extension of `L` (compatibly with `A₀ →
L → F`), let `x ∈ F`, and let `V`, `V'` be valuation subrings of `F`
containing the image of `A₀`, with `ϖ` a nonunit in each. Suppose that for
every polynomial `P` over `A₀` not divisible by `ϖ` in `A₀[X]`, both `P(x)` and
`P(x)⁻¹` lie in `V` and in `V'`, where `0⁻¹ = 0` (so `P(x)` is zero or a unit
of each subring). Then `V` and `V'` contain the same elements of the subfield `L(x)` of
`F`.
