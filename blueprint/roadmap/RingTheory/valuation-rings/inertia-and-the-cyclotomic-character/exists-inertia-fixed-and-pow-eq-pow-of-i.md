# Kummer descent along inertia

Let `p` be an odd prime, `N` a natural number, and `P` a valuation subring
of an algebraic closure `ℚ̄` of `ℚ` lying over `p`, with inertia subgroup
`I_P` in `Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)`. Let `ζ ∈ ℚ̄` be a primitive `p ^ N`-th root of unity
and `w ∈ ℚ̄`. Suppose that every element of `I_P` fixing `ζ` also fixes `w`,
and that every element of `I_P` fixes `w ^ (p ^ N)`. Then there is `w' ∈ ℚ̄`
fixed by all of `I_P` with `w' ^ (p ^ N) = w ^ (p ^ N)`.
