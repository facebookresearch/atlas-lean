# Inertia realizes the mod p cyclotomic character

Let `p` be a prime and `A` a valuation subring of an algebraic closure `ℚ̄`
of `ℚ` lying over `p`. For every unit `a` of `ℤ/pℤ` there is an element `σ`
of the inertia subgroup of `A` in `Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)` with `σ μ = μ ^ a` for every
`p`-th root of unity `μ`, where the exponent is the least nonnegative
representative of `a`.
