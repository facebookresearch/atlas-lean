# Inertia realizes the mod p^k cyclotomic character

Let `p` be a prime, `k` a natural number, and `A` a valuation subring of an
algebraic closure `ℚ̄` of `ℚ` lying over `p`. For every unit `a` of `ℤ/p^kℤ`
there is an element `σ` of the inertia subgroup of `A` in `Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)` with
`σ μ = μ ^ a` for every `p ^ k`-th root of unity `μ`, where the exponent is
the least nonnegative representative of `a`.
