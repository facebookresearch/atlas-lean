# Single-slope bounds for characteristic polynomials over a complete DVR

Let `W` be a discrete valuation ring, complete for its maximal-ideal adic
topology, with additive valuation `v` taking values in `ℕ∞` (so `v 0 = ⊤`).
Let `O` be a domain which is a `W`-algebra, free of finite rank `n` as a
`W`-module, and let `a ∈ O`. Let `χ` be the characteristic polynomial of
multiplication by `a` on `O`, with coefficients `χ_i`, and let `N(a)` be the
norm of `a`.

Then `χ` is monic of degree `n`; for every `i ≤ n` one has `(n - i) ·
v(N(a)) ≤ n · v(χ_i)` in `ℕ∞`; and `n · v(N(a)) = n · v(χ_0)`. For `a ≠ 0`,
in other words, the Newton polygon of `χ` has a single slope; for `a = 0`,
`χ = Xⁿ` and the inequalities hold with no finite-slope edge.
