# Cardinality of quotients of a DVR by powers of its maximal ideal

For a discrete valuation ring `R` with maximal ideal `𝔪` and residue field
`k`, the quotient by `𝔪 ^ n` has cardinality $|k|^n$ for every $n$, cardinalities taken as natural numbers.

No finiteness assumption on `k` is needed: when `k` is infinite and `n > 0`,
both sides are `0` under the convention that an infinite set has natural-number cardinality $0$, and
for `n = 0` both sides are `1`.
