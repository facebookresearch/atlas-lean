# Normalized discrete valuations determined by their rings

Let `L` and `L'` be fields with a field isomorphism `σ : L → L'`, and let
`w` on `L` and `v` on `L'` be surjective valuations with values in `ℤᵐ⁰`,
the multiplicative integers with zero adjoined. If the valuation subring of
`w` is contained in the preimage under `σ` of the valuation subring of `v`,
then `w = v ∘ σ`.
