# Roots of unity under the residue map of a valuation subring

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, let $A$ be a valuation subring of $K$ with residue field
$k$, and let $m$ be a natural number that is nonzero in $k$. Then the residue map $A \to k$ is
injective on the $m$-th roots of unity of $A$, and every $m$-th root of unity in $k$ is the residue
of an $m$-th root of unity in $A$. Algebraic closure of $K$ is needed for the lifting: for
$A = \mathbb{Z}_{(7)} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ and $m = 3$, the residue field $\mathbb{F}_7$ has three
cube roots of unity, while $\mathbb{Q}$ has only one.
