# Residue fields in algebraic extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$ are algebraic

Let $K$ be an algebraic extension of $\mathbb{Q}$, let $A$ be a valuation subring of $K$, and let
$p$ be a prime such that the residue field of $A$ has characteristic $p$, so that it is an algebra
over $\mathbb{F}_p$. Then the residue field of $A$ is algebraic over $\mathbb{F}_p$. The
characteristic must be positive: for the trivial valuation ring $A = K$ the residue field is $K$,
of characteristic zero.
