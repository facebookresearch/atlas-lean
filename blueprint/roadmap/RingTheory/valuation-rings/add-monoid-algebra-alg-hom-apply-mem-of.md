# Characters of torsion monoid algebras over a valuation ring

Let `A` be a valuation subring of a field `L`, let `G` be an additive monoid
all of whose elements have finite order, and let `χ : A[G] → L` be an
`A`-algebra homomorphism from the monoid algebra. Then `χ` takes all its
values in `A`, and hence factors through an `A`-algebra homomorphism `A[G] →
A`.
