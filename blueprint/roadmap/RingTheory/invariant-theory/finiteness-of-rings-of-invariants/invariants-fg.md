# Finitely generated ring of invariants

For a subgroup $G \le \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$, the proposition that its ring of invariant polynomials is a finitely generated $\mathbb{C}$-algebra: some finite set of invariants generates every invariant as a $\mathbb{C}$-algebra.

Source: D. Hilbert, *Mathematical problems* (Paris, 1900), Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 8 (1902), 437–479, Problem 14.
