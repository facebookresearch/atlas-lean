# Linear algebraic subgroup of GL_n(C)

For a subgroup $G \le \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$, the proposition that $G$ is Zariski closed: there is a set $S$ of polynomials in the $n^2$ matrix entries such that an invertible matrix lies in $G$ exactly when all members of $S$ vanish at its entries.

Source: D. Hilbert, *Mathematical problems* (Paris, 1900), Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 8 (1902), 437–479, Problem 14.
