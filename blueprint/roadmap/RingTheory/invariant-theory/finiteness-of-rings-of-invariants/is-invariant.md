# Invariant polynomial of a matrix group

For a subgroup $G \le \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ and $f \in \mathbb{C}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, the proposition that $f$ is invariant: $f(gx) = f(x)$ as polynomials for every $g \in G$.

Source: D. Hilbert, *Mathematical problems* (Paris, 1900), Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 8 (1902), 437–479, Problem 14.
