# Laplacian algebra of polynomials

For a (unital) $\mathbb{R}$-subalgebra $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, the proposition that $A$ is a Laplacian algebra: $r^2 = \sum_i x_i^2 \in A$ and $\Delta f \in A$ for every $f \in A$, where $\Delta = \sum_i \partial^2/\partial x_i^2$.

Source: F. Morgan and P. Pansu, *A List of Open Problems in Differential Geometry* (2018), Question 23 (M. Radeschi, Spherical submetries); R. A. E. Mendes and M. Radeschi, arXiv:2012.07914, Section 1.
