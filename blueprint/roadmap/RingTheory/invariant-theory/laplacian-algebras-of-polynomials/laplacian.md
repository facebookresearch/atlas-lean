# Laplacian on real polynomials

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f \in \mathbb{R}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, the polynomial $\Delta f = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \partial^2 f / \partial x_i^2$ (formal partial derivatives).

Source: F. Morgan and P. Pansu, *A List of Open Problems in Differential Geometry* (2018), Question 23 (M. Radeschi, Spherical submetries).
