# Maximal algebra of polynomials

For an $\mathbb{R}$-subalgebra $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, the proposition that $A$ is maximal: every polynomial $f$ that is constant on the common level sets of $A$ (so $f(x) = f(y)$ whenever $g(x) = g(y)$ for all $g \in A$, with $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n$) lies in $A$.

Source: F. Morgan and P. Pansu, *A List of Open Problems in Differential Geometry* (2018), Question 23 (M. Radeschi, Spherical submetries); R. A. E. Mendes and M. Radeschi, arXiv:2012.07914, Section 2.
