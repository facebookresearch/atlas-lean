# Laplacian algebras are maximal (Mendes–Radeschi)

For every $n$, every Laplacian algebra (a subalgebra $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ containing $r^2 = \sum_i x_i^2$ and preserved by the Laplacian $\Delta = \sum_i \partial^2/\partial x_i^2$) is maximal (every polynomial $f$ such that $f(x) = f(y)$ whenever $g(x) = g(y)$ for all $g \in A$ lies in $A$).
