# Cooper's question on invariants of 4 × 4 × 8 tensors

This is the proposition that there is a polynomial function $P$ on $\mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^8$ (a polynomial in the $128$ coordinates $T_{ijk}$) that is invariant under $\mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_8(\mathbb{C})$ acting by $T \mapsto (A \otimes B \otimes C)\,T$ and is not constant.

Source: J. Delp, D. Hoffoss and J. F. Manning, *Problems in Groups, Geometry, and Three-Manifolds* (2015), arXiv:1512.04620, item 2.2 (D. Cooper).
