# Cooper's question on invariants of 4 × 4 × 8 tensors

This is the proposition that there is a polynomial function $P$ on $\mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^8$ (a polynomial in the $128$ coordinates $T_{ijk}$) that is invariant under $\mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_8(\mathbb{C})$ acting by $T \mapsto (A \otimes B \otimes C)\,T$ and is not constant. Item 2.2 of the problem collection literally asks for a nonzero invariant polynomial, which the constant $1$ already is; the nonconstant complex formulation stated here is the substantive question, the one studied by Bryan, Reichstein and Van Raamsdonk.

Sources: J. Delp, D. Hoffoss and J. F. Manning, *Problems in Groups, Geometry, and Three-Manifolds* (2015), arXiv:1512.04620, item 2.2 (D. Cooper), the question as posed; J. Bryan, Z. Reichstein and M. Van Raamsdonk, *Existence of locally maximally entangled quantum states via geometric invariant theory*, Ann. Henri Poincaré 19 (2018), https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-018-0682-6, Corollaries 3.2 and 3.3, for the action and the nonconstant formulation.
