# Action of SL4 × SL4 × SL8 on tensors

For $(A, B, C) \in \mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_8(\mathbb{C})$ and a tensor $T \in \mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^8$ given by its coordinates $T_{ijk}$, the tensor $(A \otimes B \otimes C)T$ with coordinates $\sum_{i,j,k} A_{pi} B_{qj} C_{sk} T_{ijk}$.

Source: J. Delp, D. Hoffoss and J. F. Manning, *Problems in Groups, Geometry, and Three-Manifolds* (2015), arXiv:1512.04620, item 2.2 (D. Cooper).
