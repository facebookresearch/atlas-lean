# A non-constant SL4 × SL4 × SL8-invariant on 4 × 4 × 8 tensors

There is a polynomial function $P$ on $\mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^8$ (a polynomial in the $128$ coordinates $T_{ijk}$) that is invariant under $\mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_4(\mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{SL}_8(\mathbb{C})$ acting by $T \mapsto (A \otimes B \otimes C)\,T$ and is not constant. The group has dimension $15 + 15 + 63 = 93 < 128$, and a semisimple group acting on a space of larger dimension has a non-constant polynomial invariant.
