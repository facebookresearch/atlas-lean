# Generators of a principal ideal quotient avoiding a prime element

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, $x \in R$ a prime element, and $I, J$ integral ideals of $R$ not divisible by $(x)$. If the fractional ideal $I J^{-1}$ (the quotient $I / J$ of fractional ideals) is principal, then there are $a, b \in R$, neither divisible by $x$, with

$$I / J = \Big(\frac{a}{b}\Big).$$

The ideals $I$, $J$ need not be principal and $a$, $b$ need not be coprime.
