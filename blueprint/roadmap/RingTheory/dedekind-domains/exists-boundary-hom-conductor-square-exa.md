# Boundary map of a conductor square on Picard groups

Let `A → B` be an injective homomorphism of commutative rings, let `𝔠` be an
ideal of `B` contained in the image of `A`, and let `𝔠_A` be its preimage in
`A`. There is a group homomorphism `δ : (B ⧸ 𝔠)ˣ → Pic A` with the following
properties.

For each unit `u`, the class `δ u` is represented by the `A`-submodule of
`B` consisting of the `x` such that `u · x̄` is the image of an element of
`A` in `B ⧸ 𝔠`. The kernel of `δ` consists of the units of the form `ā ·
t̄`, with `a` a unit of `A ⧸ 𝔠_A` mapped to `B ⧸ 𝔠` and `t` a unit of `B`. A
class in `Pic A` lies in the image of `δ` if and only if it becomes trivial
both in `Pic B` and in `Pic (A ⧸ 𝔠_A)`. Finally, a unit `t` of `B` whose
image in `B ⧸ 𝔠` equals the image of a unit of `A ⧸ 𝔠_A` is the image of a
unit of `A`.
