# Unfolding the definition of a p-ordering

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain, $\mathfrak p$
a nonzero prime ideal of $R$, $S \subseteq R$ and $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a sequence in $R$, and
write $v_n$ for the stage-$n$ prefix-product valuation with respect to $\mathfrak p$ and
$(a_n)$. Then $(a_n)$ is a $\mathfrak p$-ordering of $S$ if and only if $S$ is infinite,
$a_n \in S$ for every $n$, and $v_n(a_n) \le v_n(x)$ for every $n$ and every $x \in S$.
