# Extension of a prime to the completed valuation ring

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with
fraction field $K$, let $v$ be a height-one prime of $A$, and let $\mathcal{O}_v$ be the valuation
ring of the $v$-adic completion of $K$. Then the ideal of $\mathcal{O}_v$ generated by the image of
$v$ under the canonical map $A \to \mathcal{O}_v$ is the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_v$.
