# Inverse of a localized fractional ideal is bounded by localization

Let $A$ be a Noetherian integral domain with fraction field $K$ and $\mathfrak p$ a prime ideal of
$A$. For every fractional ideal $I$ of $A$,

$$(I_{\mathfrak p})^{-1} \subseteq (I^{-1})_{\mathfrak p},$$

as fractional ideals of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ in $K$. It follows from the equality
$(I^{-1})_{\mathfrak p} = (I_{\mathfrak p})^{-1}$.
