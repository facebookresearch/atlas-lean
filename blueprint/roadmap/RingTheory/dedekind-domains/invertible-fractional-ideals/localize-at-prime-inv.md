# Localization commutes with inverting fractional ideals

Let $A$ be a Noetherian integral domain with fraction field $K$ and $\mathfrak p$ a prime ideal of
$A$. For every fractional ideal $I$ of $A$, localization at $\mathfrak p$ commutes with taking
inverses: $(I^{-1})_{\mathfrak p} = (I_{\mathfrak p})^{-1}$, as fractional ideals of
$A_{\mathfrak p}$ in $K$.
