# Finite ideles surject onto the class group

Let `R` be a Dedekind domain with fraction field `K`. There is a surjective
group homomorphism from the group of finite ideles (the units of the finite
adele ring of `R`) onto the ideal class group of `R` whose kernel is the
subgroup generated by the unit ideles (finite ideles that, together with
their inverses, are integral at every height-one prime of `R`) and the
principal ideles (the image of `Kˣ`).
