# Membership in the unit ideles

Let `R` be a Dedekind domain with fraction field `K`. A finite idele `δ`
lies in the subgroup of unit ideles if and only if, for every height-one
prime `v` of `R`, the component of `δ` at `v` lies in the ring of integers
of the completion `K_v`, and the component of `δ⁻¹` at `v` does as well.
