# Unit ideles

Let `R` be a Dedekind domain with fraction field `K`. The unit ideles form
the subgroup of the group of finite ideles (the units of the finite adele
ring of `R`) consisting of those `δ` such that, at every height-one prime
`v` of `R`, the components of both `δ` and `δ⁻¹` lie in the ring of integers
of the completion `K_v`; equivalently, every component of `δ` is a unit of
the local ring of integers.
