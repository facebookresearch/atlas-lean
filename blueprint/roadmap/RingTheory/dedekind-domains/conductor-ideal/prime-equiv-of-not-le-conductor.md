# Bijection of primes away from the conductor

Let $O \to B$ be an injective homomorphism of commutative rings, through which $B$ is an
$O$-algebra, and let $\mathfrak c$ be an ideal of $O$ that clears denominators: for all
$c \in \mathfrak c$ and $b \in B$ the product $cb$ lies in the image of $O$. Suppose moreover that $B$ is a Dedekind domain, that $\mathfrak c \ne 0$, and that
$\mathfrak c + \mathfrak p = O$ for every nonzero prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ of $O$ not
containing $\mathfrak c$. Then contraction $\mathfrak q \mapsto \mathfrak q \cap O$ and extension
$\mathfrak p \mapsto \mathfrak p B$ are mutually inverse bijections between the prime ideals of
$B$ not containing $\mathfrak c B$ and the prime ideals of $O$ not containing $\mathfrak c$,
the zero ideal included on both sides.

For an order $O$ with integral closure $B$ and conductor $\mathfrak c$, this is the bijection
between the primes of $B$ and of $O$ away from the conductor; here $\mathfrak c$ may be any ideal
with the stated properties.
