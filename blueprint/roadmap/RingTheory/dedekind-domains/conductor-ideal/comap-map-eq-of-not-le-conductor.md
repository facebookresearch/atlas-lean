# Contraction after extension fixes primes away from the conductor

Let $O \to B$ be an injective homomorphism of commutative rings, through which $B$ is an
$O$-algebra, and let $\mathfrak c$ be an ideal of $O$ that clears denominators: for all
$c \in \mathfrak c$ and $b \in B$ the product $cb$ lies in the image of $O$. If $\mathfrak p$ is a prime ideal of $O$ not containing $\mathfrak c$, then the
contraction to $O$ of the extended ideal $\mathfrak p B$ is $\mathfrak p$ itself.
