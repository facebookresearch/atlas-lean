# Contraction of a nonzero ideal is nonzero

Let $O \to B$ be an injective homomorphism of commutative rings, through which $B$ is an
$O$-algebra, and let $\mathfrak c$ be an ideal of $O$ that clears denominators: for all
$c \in \mathfrak c$ and $b \in B$ the product $cb$ lies in the image of $O$. Suppose moreover that $B$ is a Dedekind domain and $\mathfrak c \ne 0$. Then every nonzero
ideal $\mathfrak q$ of $B$ has nonzero contraction to $O$: a nonzero element of $\mathfrak c$
times a nonzero element of $\mathfrak q$ is the image of a nonzero element of the contraction.
