# Unique factorisation of ideals in a Dedekind domain

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain. Then the ideals of $A$ under multiplication form a unique factorisation monoid: every
nonzero ideal of $A$ is a product of irreducible ideals, uniquely up to the order of the factors (the unit ideal $A$,
the only invertible element of this monoid, being the empty product), and every irreducible ideal $P$ is prime in this
monoid: if $P$ divides $IJ$, then $P$ divides $I$ or $J$. Here an ideal $I$ divides an ideal $J$ if $J = IK$ for an
ideal $K$ of $A$.

## Sources

- F. Keune, *Number Fields*, Radboud University Press, Nijmegen (2023, version 2023-01), open access,

  ideals, uniquely up to order).
