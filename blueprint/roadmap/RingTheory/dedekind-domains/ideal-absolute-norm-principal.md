# The absolute norm of a principal ideal

Let $S$ be a Dedekind domain that is a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module of finite rank, such as the ring of integers of a
number field, and for an ideal $I$ of $S$ let $N(I)$ be its absolute norm, the number of elements of $S/I$ for
$I \neq 0$ and $0$ for $I = 0$. Then for every $r \in S$

$$N(rS) = \bigl|N_{S/\mathbb{Z}}(r)\bigr|,$$

the absolute value of the determinant of multiplication by $r$ on $S$.
