# Maximal Ideal Pow Quotient Ideal eq

Under `map 𝔪A ≤ 𝔪B ^ n`, the defining ideal `map 𝔪A ⊔ 𝔪B ^ n` collapses
to `𝔪B ^ n`, by `sup_eq_right`.
