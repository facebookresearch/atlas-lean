# Tame Different

Tame different-ideal equality: under `map 𝔪A = 𝔪B ^ e` with
`IsUnit (e : B)`, the different ideal equals `𝔪B ^ (e - 1)`. This is the
correct tame equality, not the false wild equality in ATLAS.
