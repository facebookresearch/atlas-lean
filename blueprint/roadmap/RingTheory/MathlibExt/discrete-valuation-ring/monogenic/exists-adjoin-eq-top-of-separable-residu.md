# Exists adjoin eq top of separable residue Field

A finite extension of discrete valuation rings with separable residue fields is
monogenic: `B` is generated by a single element over `A`. No unramifiedness is assumed;
the ramified case is handled by perturbing a residue-field lift so that the value of
the lifted minimal polynomial is a uniformizer. This is a projection of
`exists_generator_with_residue_minpoly_uniformizer`.
