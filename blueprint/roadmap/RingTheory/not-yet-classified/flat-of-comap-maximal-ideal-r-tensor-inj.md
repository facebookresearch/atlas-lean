# Local criterion for flatness

Let `R` be a Noetherian ring, `S` a Noetherian local
`R`-algebra, `M` a finite `S`-module and `p` the contraction of the maximal ideal of `S` to `R`. If
`p ⊗[R] M → R ⊗[R] M` is injective, then `M` is flat over `R`.

## Sources

- [Stacks Project, Lemma 10.99.7 (Tag 00MK)](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00MK), stated there for a local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings, with the hypothesis that `Tor₁` of the residue field of `R` and `M` vanishes.
