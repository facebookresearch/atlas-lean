# Colon ideals for a square matrix relation

Let `P` be a commutative ring, let `x` and `f` be
`n`-tuples of elements of `P`, and let `g` be an `n × n` matrix over `P` with `f = g • x`.
Suppose the ideal `(f)` contains a weakly regular sequence of length `n`. Then
`(f) : (x) = (f) + (det g)` and `(f) : (det g) = (x)`.

## Sources

- A. Corso, C. Polini and W. V. Vasconcelos, *Links of prime ideals*, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 115 (1994), 431–436, Theorem 2.5 (b) and (c), which it attributes to D. G. Northcott, *A homological investigation of a certain residual ideal*, Math. Ann. 150 (1963), 99–110. It states both identities for a Noetherian ring in which `(f)` has grade `n` and `(f) + (det g) ≠ P`, the second in the form `(f) : ((f) + (det g)) = (x)`; the two colon ideals agree. In a Noetherian ring with `(f) ≠ P` the hypothesis here gives grade `n`: a weakly regular sequence in a proper ideal is regular, and Krull's height theorem bounds the grade of `(f)` by `n`.
