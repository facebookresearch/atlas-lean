# Wiebe's colon-ideal lemma

Let `f = g • x` and suppose `(f)` contains a weakly regular
sequence of length `n`. Then `(f) : (x) = (f) + (det g)` and `(f) : (det g) = (x)`.

## Sources

- A.-M. Simon and J. R. Strooker, *Complete intersections of dimension zero: variations on a theme of Wiebe*, arXiv:math/0703880, Proposition 1.2, stated there for every `f` whose Koszul homology vanishes in positive degrees; they find part of it in H. Wiebe, *Über homologische Invarianten lokaler Ringe*, Math. Ann. 179 (1969), 257–274, Satz 2.
