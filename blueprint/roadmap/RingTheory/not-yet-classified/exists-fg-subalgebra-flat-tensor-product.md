# Noetherian approximation of flatness

If `R ⊗[R₀] B₀` is flat over `R` for a finitely
presented `R₀`-algebra `B₀`, then any finite subset of `R` lies in a finitely generated
`R₀`-subalgebra `R₁` with `R₁ ⊗[R₀] B₀` flat over `R₁`.

## Sources

- [Stacks Project, Lemma 10.168.1 (Tag 02JO)](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02JO), its last assertion, for `R` as the directed union of its finitely generated `R₀`-subalgebras.
