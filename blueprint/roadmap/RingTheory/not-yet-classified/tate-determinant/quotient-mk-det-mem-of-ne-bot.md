# The determinant lies in every nonzero ideal

Over a field `k`, if `f = g • X` and
`k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)` is finite-dimensional, then the class of `det g` is nonzero and lies in every nonzero
ideal of `k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)`.

## Sources

- B. de Smit, K. Rubin and R. Schoof, *Criteria for complete intersections*, in *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem*, Springer, 1997, pp. 343–355, Corollary 2.2.
