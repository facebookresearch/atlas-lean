# Perfect multiplication pairing

Over a field `k`, if `f = g • X` and `k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)` is
finite-dimensional, then some linear form `l` makes the multiplication pairing
`(a, b) ↦ l (a * b)` perfect.

## Sources

- B. de Smit, K. Rubin and R. Schoof, *Criteria for complete intersections*, in *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem*, Springer, 1997, pp. 343–355, Section 2, remark on the Gorenstein condition.
