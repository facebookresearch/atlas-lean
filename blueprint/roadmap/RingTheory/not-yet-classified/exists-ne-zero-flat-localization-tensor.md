# Generic flatness

A finite module over a finite type algebra `B` over a Noetherian domain
`A` becomes flat over `A` after inverting the image of some nonzero `a : A`.

## Sources

- [Stacks Project, Lemma 10.118.1 (Tag 051R)](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/051R), which proves the stronger statement that the localized module is free over the localization of `A`.
