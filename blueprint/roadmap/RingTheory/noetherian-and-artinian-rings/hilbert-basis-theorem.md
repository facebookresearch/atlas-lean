# Hilbert basis theorem: a polynomial ring over a commutative Noetherian ring is Noetherian

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity that is Noetherian, that is, every ideal of $R$ is finitely generated. Then the polynomial ring $R[X]$ in one variable over $R$ is Noetherian.
