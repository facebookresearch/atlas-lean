# A finitely generated module over a Noetherian ring has only finitely many associated primes

A finitely generated module over a Noetherian commutative ring has only finitely many associated primes. In detail, let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity that is Noetherian, meaning that every ideal of $R$ is finitely generated, and let $M$ be a finitely generated $R$-module. A prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ of $R$ is an *associated prime* of $M$ if $\mathfrak{p}$ is the radical of the annihilator $\operatorname{ann}(x) = \{r \in R : rx = 0\}$ of some $x \in M$; since $R$ is Noetherian, this holds exactly when $\mathfrak{p} = \operatorname{ann}(y)$ for some, possibly different, element $y \in M$. Then the set $\operatorname{Ass} M$ of associated primes of $M$ is finite.
