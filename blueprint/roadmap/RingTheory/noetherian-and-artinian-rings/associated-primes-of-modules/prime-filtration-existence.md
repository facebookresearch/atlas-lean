# Prime filtration of a finitely generated module over a Noetherian commutative ring

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring with identity and let $M$ be a finitely generated $R$-module. Then there are submodules $0 = M_0 \subseteq M_1 \subseteq \dots \subseteq M_n = M$ and prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}_1, \dots, \mathfrak{p}_n$ of $R$ such that $M_i/M_{i-1} \cong R/\mathfrak{p}_i$ as $R$-modules for $i = 1, \dots, n$. Each inclusion $M_{i-1} \subseteq M_i$ is strict, because $R/\mathfrak{p}_i \neq 0$; in particular $n = 0$ when $M = 0$.
