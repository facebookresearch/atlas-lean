# Minimal primes over the annihilator of a finitely generated module over a Noetherian ring are associated primes

For a finitely generated module $M$ over a Noetherian commutative ring $R$, every prime ideal that is minimal among the primes of the support $\operatorname{Supp} M = \{P : M_P \neq 0\}$ is an associated prime of $M$; for $M = R/I$ this says that the minimal primes over an ideal $I$ are associated primes of $R/I$. Mathlib states this for the minimal primes over the annihilator: let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring and $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module; then every minimal prime over $\operatorname{Ann}_R M = \{r \in R : rm = 0 \text{ for all } m \in M\}$, that is, every prime ideal that contains $\operatorname{Ann}_R M$ and is minimal among the prime ideals containing it, is an associated prime of $M$. The two statements are equivalent by a separate result: for a finitely generated module, $\operatorname{Supp} M = V(\operatorname{Ann}_R M)$, the set of prime ideals containing $\operatorname{Ann}_R M$, so the minimal elements of $\operatorname{Supp} M$ are exactly the minimal primes over $\operatorname{Ann}_R M$. Here, following Mathlib, an associated prime of $M$ is a prime ideal equal to the radical of the annihilator $\{r \in R : rx = 0\}$ of some $x \in M$; since $R$ is Noetherian, it is then the annihilator of some, possibly different, element of $M$. No locality and no nonvanishing of $M$ is assumed: for $M = 0$ the annihilator is $R$, which lies in no prime ideal, and the support is empty.
