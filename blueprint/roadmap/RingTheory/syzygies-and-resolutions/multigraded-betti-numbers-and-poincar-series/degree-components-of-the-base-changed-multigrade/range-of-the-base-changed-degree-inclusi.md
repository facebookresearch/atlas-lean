# Range of the base-changed degree inclusion

Let $K$ be a field, let $R = K[x_1, \dots, x_n]$, let $M$ be a multigraded polynomial module over
$R$, and let $F$ be a multigraded free resolution of $M$ whose term $F_i$ has the homogeneous basis
$(e_j)_{j \in \iota_i}$. Write $K \otimes_R F$ for the chain complex of $K$-vector spaces obtained
from $F$ by extending scalars along the evaluation $R \to K$ at $x = 0$, and for
$\alpha \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ call the $K$-span of the vectors $1 \otimes e_j$ with $e_j$ of degree
$\alpha$ the degree-$\alpha$ piece of $(K \otimes_R F)_i$. In every homological degree $i$, the
image of the inclusion of the degree-$\alpha$ complex into $K \otimes_R F$ is exactly the
degree-$\alpha$ piece of $(K \otimes_R F)_i$.
