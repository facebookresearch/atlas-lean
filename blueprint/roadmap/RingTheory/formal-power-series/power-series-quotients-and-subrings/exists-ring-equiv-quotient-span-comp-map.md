# Base change of an automorphism of a quotient of a two-variable power series ring

Let `W₀` and `W` be commutative Noetherian rings, `ψ : W₀ → W` a ring
homomorphism, `g₀ ∈ W₀⟦X₀, X₁⟧` and `g ∈ W⟦X₀, X₁⟧` with `(g) = (ψ(g₀))`,
where `ψ(g₀)` applies `ψ` to the coefficients. Write `S₀ = W₀⟦X₀, X₁⟧ /
(g₀)` and `S = W⟦X₀, X₁⟧ / (g)`. Let `θ₀` be a ring automorphism of `S₀`
fixing the classes of constants, and let `M₀` be a `2 × 2` matrix over `W₀`
with `θ₀(X_j) ≡ ∑ i, M₀ i j X_i` modulo the square of the ideal of `S₀`
generated by the classes of `X₀` and `X₁`, for `j = 0, 1`.

Then there is a ring automorphism `θ` of `S` that fixes the classes of
constants, is compatible with `θ₀` (if `θ₀` sends the class of `s` to the
class of `s'`, then `θ` sends the class of `ψ(s)` to the class of `ψ(s')`),
and satisfies `θ(X_j) ≡ ∑ i, ψ(M₀ i j) X_i` modulo the square of the ideal
of `S` generated by the classes of `X₀` and `X₁`.
