# Artinian local polynomial quotients as power-series quotients

For a field `K`, an Artinian local polynomial quotient at the origin is
canonically the quotient of the corresponding formal power-series ring by
the same finite equations: the localization of `K[X]/(g)` at a maximal ideal
containing every variable class, when finite over `K`, is `K`-algebra
isomorphic to `K⟦X⟧/(g)`, by an isomorphism sending each localized
polynomial to its power-series class.
