# Power series vanishing to order k lie in the k-th power of the variable ideal

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `σ` a finite set of variables, and `k` a
natural number. A power series `φ ∈ R⟦X_σ⟧` whose coefficients at all
monomials of total degree less than `k` vanish lies in the `k`-th power of
the ideal generated by the variables `X_s`.
