# Base change of a power series quotient

Let `R` and `S` be commutative rings with `S` an `R`-algebra, `σ` a finite
set, and `φ : ι → R⟦X_σ⟧` a family of power series such that `X_s ^ N` lies
in the ideal generated by the `φ i` for some `N` and every `s`. Then there
is an `S`-algebra isomorphism `S ⊗[R] (R⟦X_σ⟧ / (φ i)) ≃ S⟦X_σ⟧ / (φ i)`,
where on the right the coefficients of each `φ i` are mapped into `S`,
sending `s ⊗ [q]` to `s` times the class of `q` with coefficients mapped
into `S`.
