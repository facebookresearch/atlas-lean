# Adic evaluation commutes with algebra maps

Let `R`, `S`, `S'` be commutative rings with `S` and `S'` `R`-algebras, `σ`
a finite set, `J` a finitely generated ideal of `S` and `J'` an ideal of
`S'`, with `S` `J`-adically complete and `S'` `J'`-adically complete. Let `φ
: S → S'` be an `R`-algebra homomorphism sending `J` into the radical of
`J'`, and let `x : σ → S` have coordinates in the radical of `J`. Then for
every power series `f` over `R`, `φ (f(x)) = f(φ ∘ x)`, where the
evaluations are `J`-adic and `J'`-adic.
