# Power series coordinates on a tower with prescribed special fibres

Let `𝓞` be a commutative ring and `p` a prime that is a nonzerodivisor in
`𝓞`, such that `𝓞` is `p`-adically complete and `𝓞 → ZMod p` is a ring
homomorphism with kernel `(p)`. Let `⋯ → R₀ 2 → R₀ 1 → R₀ 0` be a tower of
commutative `𝓞`-algebras, each free and finite as an `𝓞`-module, with
transition `𝓞`-algebra maps `t₀ v : R₀ (v + 1) → R₀ v`. Let `σ` be finite
and let `x v : σ → R₀ v` be coordinates compatible with the transition maps
and lying in the radical of `(p)`. Suppose that each special-fibre
evaluation `(ZMod p)[X_σ] → ZMod p ⊗[𝓞] R₀ v` at the reduced coordinates is
surjective, and that for every `N` some `v` has kernel contained in the
`N`-th power of the ideal of the variables.

Then the `p`-adic evaluations `π v : 𝓞⟦X_σ⟧ → R₀ v` at `x v` are surjective
`𝓞`-algebra homomorphisms with `π v (X i) = x v i`, compatible with the
transition maps, jointly injective (a series killed by every `π v` is `0`),
and every compatible family `(z v)` is the image of a single power series.
