# Radical coordinates can be substituted

Let `S` be a commutative ring, `J` an ideal of `S`, and `σ` a finite set. If
every coordinate `x s` lies in the radical of `J`, then `x` is a valid point
of evaluation for power series in the variables `σ` with respect to the
`J`-adic topology: each `x s` is topologically nilpotent (and, `σ` being
finite, the coordinates tend to `0` along the cofinite filter).
