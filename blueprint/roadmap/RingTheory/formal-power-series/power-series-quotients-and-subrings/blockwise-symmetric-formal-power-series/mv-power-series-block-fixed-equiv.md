# Blockwise symmetric power series form a power series ring

The `k`-subalgebra of blockwise symmetric formal power series, those fixed
by every independent permutation of the variables within each block, is
`k`-algebra isomorphic to the full formal power series ring in `∑ j, rs j`
variables. The inverse isomorphism sends a power series `f` to the result of
substituting, for the variable indexed by the `i`-th element of block `j`,
the `(i + 1)`-st elementary symmetric polynomial of the variables of block
`j`.

Here `k` is a commutative ring, there are `m` blocks of sizes `rs : Fin m →
ℕ`, and the power series are in `∑ j, rs j` variables, grouped into the
blocks; the product of the symmetric groups of the blocks acts on them by
permuting the variables inside each block independently.
