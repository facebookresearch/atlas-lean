# Chain rule for substitution into a power series

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `a` a multivariate formal power series over
`R` with zero constant coefficient, and `h` a univariate formal power series
over `R`. For every variable `i`, the partial derivative of the composite
`h(a)` with respect to `X_i` is `h'(a) · ∂a/∂X_i`, where `h'` is the formal
derivative of `h`.
