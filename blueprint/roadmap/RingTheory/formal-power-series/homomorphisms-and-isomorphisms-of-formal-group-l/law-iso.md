# Isomorphism of formal group laws

Let `R` be a commutative ring and `F`, `G` one-dimensional formal group laws
over `R`. An isomorphism from `F` to `G` is a homomorphism `φ` from `F` to
`G` (a power series with zero constant coefficient satisfying `φ(F(X₀, X₁))
= G(φ(X₀), φ(X₁))`) whose coefficient of `X` is a unit of `R`.
