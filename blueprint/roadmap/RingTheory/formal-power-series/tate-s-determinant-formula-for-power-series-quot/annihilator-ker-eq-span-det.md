# Tate's determinant formula

Let `𝒪` be a Noetherian ring, let `f₁, …, fₙ` be `n`
relations in the `n`-variable power series ring `𝒪⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧`, and let `g` be an `n × n` matrix
over `𝒪⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧` with `f = g • X`. Suppose `T = 𝒪⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)` is finite free over `𝒪`, and let
`π : T →ₐ[𝒪] 𝒪` be the augmentation induced by the constant coefficient. Then the annihilator of
`ker π` is generated by the class of `det g`, and its image under `π` is generated by
`det g(0)`.

## Sources

- B. de Smit, K. Rubin and R. Schoof, *Criteria for complete intersections*, in *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem* (G. Cornell, J. H. Silverman and G. Stevens, eds.), Springer, 1997, pp. 343–355, Proposition 2.1 (i), stated there over a complete Noetherian local ring and traced back to J. Tate, *Homology of Noetherian rings and local rings*, Illinois J. Math. 1 (1957), 14–27.
