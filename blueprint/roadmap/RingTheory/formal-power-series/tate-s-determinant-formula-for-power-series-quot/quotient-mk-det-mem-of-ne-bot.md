# The determinant lies in every nonzero ideal

Let `k` be a field, let `f₁, …, fₙ` be `n`
relations in the `n`-variable power series ring `k⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧`, and let `g` be an `n × n` matrix
over `k⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧` with `f = g • X`. If `k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)` is finite-dimensional, then the class of
`det g` is nonzero and lies in every nonzero ideal of `k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)`.

## Sources

- B. de Smit, K. Rubin and R. Schoof, *Criteria for complete intersections*, in *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem*, Springer, 1997, pp. 343–355, Corollary 2.2.
