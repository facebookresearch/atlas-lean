# Perfect multiplication pairing

Let `k` be a field, let `f₁, …, fₙ` be `n` relations in
the `n`-variable power series ring `k⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧`, and let `g` be an `n × n` matrix over
`k⟦X₁, …, Xₙ⟧` with `f = g • X`. If `k⟦X⟧ ⧸ (f)` is finite-dimensional, then some linear form `l`
makes the multiplication pairing `(a, b) ↦ l (a * b)` perfect.

## Sources

- B. de Smit, K. Rubin and R. Schoof, *Criteria for complete intersections*, in *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem*, Springer, 1997, pp. 343–355, Section 2, remark on the Gorenstein condition.
