# Existence and uniqueness for formal Burgers

Let `K` have characteristic zero and let `t ≠ x` be variables. For every
viscosity `ν ∈ K`, including `ν = 0`, and every power series `v`, there is a
unique formal power series `u` with `∂u/∂X_t = ν ∂²u/∂X_x² - u ∂u/∂X_x`
whose coefficients at all exponents `n` with `n t = 0` equal those of `v`.
Only this time-zero slice of `v` matters.
