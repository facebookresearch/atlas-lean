# Formal Cole–Hopf transform

For a field `K`, a viscosity `ν ∈ K`, a spatial variable `x : σ` and a
multivariate formal power series `φ` over `K`, the formal Cole–Hopf
transform is `coleHopf ν x φ = -2ν (∂φ/∂X_x) φ⁻¹`, the scaled logarithmic
derivative of `φ` along `x`. Here `φ⁻¹` is the formal inverse of `φ` when
the constant coefficient of `φ` is nonzero; otherwise `φ⁻¹ = 0` and the
transform is `0`.
