# Exponential heat moment series

For a finite index set `s`, amplitudes and frequencies `c k : ι → ℚ`, a
viscosity `ν ∈ ℚ` and an order `n`, `exponentialHeatMoment s c k ν n` is the
rational formal power series in time (variable `0`) and space (variable `1`)
given by `∑ i ∈ s, c i k_i ^ n exp(ν k_i ^ 2 t + k_i x)`, where each
exponential is the bivariate exponential series.
