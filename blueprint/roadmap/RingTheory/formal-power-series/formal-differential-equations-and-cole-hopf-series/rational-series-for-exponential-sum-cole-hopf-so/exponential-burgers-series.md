# Rational Cole–Hopf series of an exponential sum

For a finite index set `s`, amplitudes and frequencies `c k : ι → ℚ` and a
viscosity `ν ∈ ℚ`, `exponentialBurgersSeries s c k ν` is the formal power
series in time (variable `0`) and space (variable `1`) obtained by applying
the Cole–Hopf transform `φ ↦ -2ν (∂_x φ) φ⁻¹` to the exponential heat
profile `φ = ∑ i ∈ s, c i exp(ν k_i ^ 2 t + k_i x)`, expanded as a power
series with rational coefficients.
