# Causal right-hand side

For a field `K` and a variable `i : σ`, a map `F` from multivariate formal
power series over `K` to themselves is causal on axis `i` if for every
natural number `k` and all series `a` and `b` that agree through degree `k`
on axis `i` (equal coefficients at every exponent `n` with `n i ≤ k`), the
images `F a` and `F b` also agree through degree `k` on axis `i`.
