# Inverse of the blockwise symmetric isomorphism

For a formal power series `f` in `∑ j, rs j` variables, the inverse of the
isomorphism between blockwise symmetric power series and the full power
series ring sends `f` to the blockwise symmetric power series obtained from
`f` by substituting, for the variable indexed by the `i`-th element of block
`j`, the `(i + 1)`-st elementary symmetric polynomial of the variables of
block `j`.

Here `k` is a commutative ring, there are `m` blocks of sizes `rs : Fin m →
ℕ`, and the power series are in `∑ j, rs j` variables, grouped into the
blocks; the product of the symmetric groups of the blocks acts on them by
permuting the variables inside each block independently.
