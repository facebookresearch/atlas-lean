# Monoid algebra embedding into Hahn series as a semiring map

Let $\Gamma$ be an ordered cancellative additive commutative monoid and $R$ a commutative semiring.
The embedding of the monoid algebra $R[\Gamma]$ into the semiring $R((\Gamma))$ of Hahn series, the
semiring homomorphism that sends the basis element of $g \in \Gamma$ to the monomial $t^g$, sends
each finitely supported $f \colon \Gamma \to R$ to the Hahn series with the same coefficients,
$\sum_{g} f(g)\, t^g$.
