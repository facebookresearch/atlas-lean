# Value of the variable difference unit

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Write $R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ for the ring of Hahn series in an outer
variable $S$ with exponents in $\mathbb{Z}$ and coefficients Laurent polynomials in an inner
variable $T$. The unit of $R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ given by the variable difference is the element
$T - S$, that is, the monomial $T$ with outer exponent $0$ minus the monomial $S$.
