# Multiplying by the variable difference on diagonal coefficients

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Write $R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ for the ring of Hahn series in an outer
variable $S$ with exponents in $\mathbb{Z}$ and coefficients Laurent polynomials in an inner
variable $T$. For every $f \in R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ and all $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$, the coefficient of
$T^x S^y$ in $(T - S)\, f$ is the difference of two adjacent diagonal coefficients of $f$:

$$[T^x S^y]\,\big((T - S) f\big) = [T^{x-1} S^y]\, f - [T^x S^{y-1}]\, f.$$
