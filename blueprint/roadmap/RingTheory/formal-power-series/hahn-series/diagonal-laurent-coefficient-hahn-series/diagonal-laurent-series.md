# Diagonal Laurent-coefficient Hahn series

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $d, j_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $c \colon \mathbb{Z} \to R$. Write
$R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ for the semiring of Hahn series in an outer variable $S$ with exponents in
$\mathbb{Z}$ and coefficients Laurent polynomials in an inner variable $T$. The diagonal Laurent
series of these data is

$$\sum_{y \ge j_0} c(y)\, T^{d - y} S^{y} \in R[T^{\pm 1}]((S)):$$

its coefficient at $S^y$ is $c(y)\, T^{d - y}$ for $y \ge j_0$ and $0$ otherwise, so all its terms
lie on the diagonal where the exponents of $T$ and $S$ add up to $d$.
