# Variable difference as a unit of Hahn series

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Write $R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ for the ring of Hahn series in an outer
variable $S$ with exponents in $\mathbb{Z}$ and coefficients Laurent polynomials in an inner
variable $T$. The difference $T - S$ of the inner and outer variables is a unit of
$R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$, with inverse the diagonal Laurent series

$$(T - S)^{-1} = \sum_{y \ge 0} T^{-1-y} S^{y}.$$
