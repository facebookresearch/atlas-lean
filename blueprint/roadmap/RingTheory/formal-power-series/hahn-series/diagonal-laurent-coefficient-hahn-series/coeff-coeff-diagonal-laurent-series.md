# Double coefficients of a diagonal Laurent series

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $d, j_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $c \colon \mathbb{Z} \to R$. Write
$R[T^{\pm 1}]((S))$ for the semiring of Hahn series in an outer variable $S$ with exponents in
$\mathbb{Z}$ and coefficients Laurent polynomials in an inner variable $T$. The coefficient of
$T^x S^y$ in the diagonal Laurent series $\sum_{y \ge j_0} c(y)\, T^{d - y} S^{y}$ is $c(y)$ if
$y \ge j_0$ and $x + y = d$, and $0$ otherwise.
