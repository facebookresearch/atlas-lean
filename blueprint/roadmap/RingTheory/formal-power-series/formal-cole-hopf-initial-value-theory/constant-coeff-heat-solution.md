# Constant coefficient of the heat solution

For a field `K`, `ν ∈ K`, variables `t` and `x`, and any power series `b`,
the formal heat solution `heatSolution ν t x b` (the formal solution of
`∂φ/∂X_t = ν ∂²φ/∂X_x²` with the `t`-degree-zero coefficients of `b`) has
the same constant coefficient as `b`.
