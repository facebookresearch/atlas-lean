# Constant coefficient of the heat solution

For a field `K`, `ν ∈ K`, variables `t` and `x`, and any power series `b`,
the formal heat construction `heatSolution ν t x b` (the Picard construction
along `t` for `∂φ/∂X_t = ν ∂²φ/∂X_x²` with the `t`-degree-zero coefficients
of `b`; it solves that equation in characteristic zero when `t ≠ x`) has
the same constant coefficient as `b`.
