# Cole–Hopf transform of the normalized profile

Let `K` have characteristic zero and `ν ≠ 0`. For every variable `x` and
every formal power series `v`, `coleHopf ν x (initialHeatProfile ν x v) =
v`, where `initialHeatProfile ν x v` is the series `b` with `∂b/∂X_x =
-(2ν)⁻¹ v b` whose `x`-degree-zero coefficients are those of `1`.

Here `K` is a field, `σ` an index set of variables, and `coleHopf ν x φ =
-2ν (∂φ/∂X_x) φ⁻¹`, where `φ⁻¹` is the formal inverse of `φ` when its
constant coefficient is nonzero and `0` otherwise.
