# Uniqueness of the Cole–Hopf Burgers solution

Let `K` have characteristic zero, let `t ≠ x` be variables, and let `b` have
nonzero constant coefficient. There is a unique formal power series `u`
solving `∂u/∂X_t = ν ∂²u/∂X_x² - u ∂u/∂X_x` whose coefficients at all
exponents `n` with `n t = 0` equal those of `coleHopf ν x b`.

Here `K` is a field, `σ` an index set of variables, and `coleHopf ν x φ =
-2ν (∂φ/∂X_x) φ⁻¹`, where `φ⁻¹` is the formal inverse of `φ` when its
constant coefficient is nonzero and `0` otherwise.
