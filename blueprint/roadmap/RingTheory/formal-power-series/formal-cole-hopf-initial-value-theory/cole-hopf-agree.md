# Cole–Hopf preserves agreement in time

Let `t ≠ x` be variables and `k` a natural number. If the power series `a`
and `b` agree through degree `k` in `t` (equal coefficients at all exponents
`n` with `n t ≤ k`) and `b` has nonzero constant coefficient, then `coleHopf
ν x a` and `coleHopf ν x b` also agree through degree `k` in `t`.

Here `K` is a field, `σ` an index set of variables, and `coleHopf ν x φ =
-2ν (∂φ/∂X_x) φ⁻¹`, where `φ⁻¹` is the formal inverse of `φ` when its
constant coefficient is nonzero and `0` otherwise.
