# Causality of the Burgers operator

If the time variable `t` and the spatial variable `x` are distinct, then for
every `ν` the Burgers operator `u ↦ ν ∂²u/∂X_x² - u ∂u/∂X_x` is causal on
the axis `t`: if `u` and `u'` have the same coefficients at all exponents
`n` with `n t ≤ k`, then so do their images.
