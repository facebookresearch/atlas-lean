# Adic evaluation as an algebra homomorphism

Let `R` and `S` be commutative rings, `S` an `R`-algebra, `σ` a finite set
of variables, and `J` an ideal of `S` such that `S` is `J`-adically complete
(and Hausdorff). For coordinates `x : σ → S` lying in the radical of `J`,
the `J`-adic evaluation `f ↦ f(x)` is an `R`-algebra homomorphism from the
power series ring `R⟦X_σ⟧` to `S`, sending `X_s` to `x s`.
