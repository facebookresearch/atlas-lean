# Divisibility of low coefficients after a shift

Let `A` be a commutative local domain, `n` a natural number invertible in
`A`, `d` an element of the maximal ideal, `e ∈ A` nonzero, and `u, F ∈ A⟦X⟧`
with `e F = u (X + d) ^ n - X ^ n`. Then `d` divides the coefficient of `X ^
j` in `F` for every `j` with `j + 2 ≤ n`. No assumption on `u` is made.
