# Descending a combination of power series to a subfield

Let `K₀` be a subfield of a field `L`, let `g_1, …, g_n` be power series
over `L` all of whose coefficients lie in `K₀`, and let `c_1, …, c_n ∈ L` be
such that every coefficient of `∑ c_i g_i` lies in `K₀`. Then there are
`c'_1, …, c'_n ∈ K₀` with `∑ c'_i g_i = ∑ c_i g_i`. The `g_i` need not be
linearly independent.
