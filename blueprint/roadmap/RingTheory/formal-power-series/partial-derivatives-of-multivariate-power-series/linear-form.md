# Formal linear form in multivariate power series

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring and $b = (b_i)_{i \in \sigma}$ a family of elements of $R$ indexed
by a set $\sigma$. The linear form of $b$ is the power series $\sum_i b_i X_i$ in
$R[[X_i : i \in \sigma]]$: its coefficient at a monomial $X^d$ is $b_i$ if $X^d = X_i$ has total
degree one, and $0$ otherwise. No finiteness assumption on $\sigma$ or $b$ is needed, since each
monomial involves finitely many variables.
