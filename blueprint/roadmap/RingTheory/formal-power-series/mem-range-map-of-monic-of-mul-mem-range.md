# Integral power series with fraction-field coefficients

Let `R` be a principal ideal domain with fraction field `K`, and let `g ∈
K⟦X⟧` be a root of a monic polynomial whose coefficients lie in `R⟦X⟧`
(mapped into `K⟦X⟧`). If there is a nonzero `h ∈ R⟦X⟧` such that `h g` has
all its coefficients in `R`, then `g` itself has all its coefficients in
`R`, that is, `g` lies in the image of `R⟦X⟧`.
