# Cole–Hopf maps heat solutions to Burgers solutions

Let `K` be a field, `ν ∈ K`, `t x : σ` variables, and `φ` a multivariate
formal power series over `K` with nonzero constant coefficient that solves
the formal heat equation `∂φ/∂X_t = ν ∂²φ/∂X_x²`. Then `u = coleHopf ν x φ =
-2ν (∂φ/∂X_x) φ⁻¹` solves the formal viscous Burgers equation `∂u/∂X_t = ν
∂²u/∂X_x² - u ∂u/∂X_x`. Further variables in `σ` act as parameters.
