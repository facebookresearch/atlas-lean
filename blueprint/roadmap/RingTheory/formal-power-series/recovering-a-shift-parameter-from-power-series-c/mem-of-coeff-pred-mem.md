# Recovering a shift parameter from one coefficient

Let `A` be a commutative local ring, `u ∈ A⟦X⟧` a power series with unit
constant coefficient, `a` an element of the maximal ideal of `A`, and `n ≥
1` a natural number invertible in `A`. If, for an ideal `I` of `A`, the
coefficient of `X ^ (n - 1)` in `u (X - a) ^ n - X ^ n` lies in `I`, then `a
∈ I`.
