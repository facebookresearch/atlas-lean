# Recovering a shift parameter from the low coefficients

Let `A` be a commutative local ring, `n` a natural number invertible in `A`,
`u ∈ A⟦X⟧` a power series with unit constant coefficient, `a` an element of
the maximal ideal of `A`, and `π ∈ A`. If every coefficient of `u (X - a) ^
n - X ^ n` in degree below `n` lies in the principal ideal `(π)`, then `a ∈
(π)`, and every coefficient in a degree `k` with `k + 2 ≤ n` lies in the
ideal `(π a)`.
