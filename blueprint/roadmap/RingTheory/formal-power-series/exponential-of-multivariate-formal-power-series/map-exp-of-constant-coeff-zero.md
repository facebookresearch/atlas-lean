# Coefficient ring maps commute with the formal exponential

Let $R$ be a commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and $R[[X]]$ the ring of formal power series in a
family of variables $(X_i)_{i \in \sigma}$, with $[X^d] f$ the coefficient of the monomial $X^d$ for
a finitely supported exponent $d \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$, $|d| = \sum_i d_i$ its total degree
and $f(0)$ the constant coefficient. Let $S$ be another commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and
$f \colon R \to S$ a ring homomorphism, applied to power series coefficientwise. For every
$w \in R[[X]]$ with $w(0) = 0$,

$$f(\exp(w)) = \exp(f(w)).$$
