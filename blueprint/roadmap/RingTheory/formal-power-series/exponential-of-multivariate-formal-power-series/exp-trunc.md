# Truncated exponential of a multivariate power series

Let $R$ be a commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and $R[[X]]$ the ring of formal power series in a
family of variables $(X_i)_{i \in \sigma}$, with $[X^d] f$ the coefficient of the monomial $X^d$ for
a finitely supported exponent $d \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$, $|d| = \sum_i d_i$ its total degree
and $f(0)$ the constant coefficient. For $w \in R[[X]]$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the truncated
exponential of $w$ is the finite sum

$$\exp_{<N}(w) = \sum_{n < N} \frac{w^n}{n!}.$$

It is defined for every $w$, with no condition on the constant coefficient.
