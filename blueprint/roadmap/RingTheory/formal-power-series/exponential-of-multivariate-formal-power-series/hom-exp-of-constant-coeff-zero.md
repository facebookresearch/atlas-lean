# Filtration-preserving endomorphisms commute with the formal exponential

Let $R$ be a commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and $R[[X]]$ the ring of formal power series in a
family of variables $(X_i)_{i \in \sigma}$, with $[X^d] f$ the coefficient of the monomial $X^d$ for
a finitely supported exponent $d \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$, $|d| = \sum_i d_i$ its total degree
and $f(0)$ the constant coefficient. Let $\Phi$ be a ring endomorphism of $R[[X]]$ that preserves
the total-degree filtration in the following sense: for every exponent $d$, the coefficient
$[X^d]\Phi(f)$ depends only on the coefficients $[X^e] f$ with $|e| \le |d|$. Then for every
$w \in R[[X]]$ with $w(0) = 0$,

$$\Phi(\exp(w)) = \exp(\Phi(w)).$$

The filtration hypothesis implies that $\Phi(w)$ again has zero constant coefficient, so the
right-hand side is defined.
