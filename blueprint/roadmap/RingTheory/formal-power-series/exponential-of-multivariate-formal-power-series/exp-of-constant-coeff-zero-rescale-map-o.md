# Weighted rescaling commutes with the formal exponential

Let $R$ be a commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra, and let $\mathbb{Q}[[X]]$ and $R[[X]]$ be the power
series rings in variables $(X_i)_{i \in \sigma}$, with $[X^d] f$ the coefficient of $X^d$ for a
finitely supported exponent $d \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$ and $f(0)$ the constant coefficient.
Let $\mathrm{wt}$ be an additive weight on exponents,
$\mathrm{wt}(d + e) = \mathrm{wt}(d) + \mathrm{wt}(e)$ with values in $\mathbb{N}$, and let
$\varepsilon \in R$. For $q \in \mathbb{Q}[[X]]$ write $q_{\varepsilon}$ for the series in $R[[X]]$
obtained by mapping the coefficients of $q$ into $R$ and rescaling each variable $X_i$ by
$\varepsilon^{\mathrm{wt}(e_i)}$, where $e_i$ is the exponent of $X_i$. Then for every
$q \in \mathbb{Q}[[X]]$ with $q(0) = 0$, the series $q_{\varepsilon}$ also has zero constant
coefficient and

$$\exp(q_{\varepsilon}) = \bigl(\exp(q)\bigr)_{\varepsilon}.$$
