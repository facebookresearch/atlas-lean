# Multivariate exponential as substitution into exp

Let $R$ be a commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and $R[[X]]$ the ring of formal power series in a
family of variables $(X_i)_{i \in \sigma}$, with $[X^d] f$ the coefficient of the monomial $X^d$ for
a finitely supported exponent $d \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$, $|d| = \sum_i d_i$ its total degree
and $f(0)$ the constant coefficient. For every $w \in R[[X]]$ with $w(0) = 0$, the formal
exponential $\exp(w)$ is the series obtained by substituting $w$ for the variable $t$ in the
univariate exponential series $\exp(t) = \sum_{n \ge 0} t^n / n! \in R[[t]]$.
