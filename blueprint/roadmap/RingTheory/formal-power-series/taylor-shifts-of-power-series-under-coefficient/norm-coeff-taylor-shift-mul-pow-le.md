# Taylor shifts preserve coefficient bounds

Let `L` be a complete nontrivially normed field with an ultrametric absolute
value, let `F ∈ L⟦X⟧` with coefficients `F_n`, and let `ρ > 0` and `M` be
real numbers with `‖F_n‖ ρ ^ n ≤ M` for all `n`. For every `a ∈ L` with `‖a‖
< ρ`, the Taylor shift `G` of `F` by `a` satisfies the same bound: `‖G_n‖ ρ
^ n ≤ M` for all `n`.
