# Coefficients of the Taylor shift

Let `L` be a complete nontrivially normed field with an ultrametric absolute
value, `a ∈ L`, and `F ∈ L⟦X⟧` with coefficients `F_m`. The coefficient of
`X ^ n` in the Taylor shift of `F` by `a` is the infinite sum `∑_{k ≥ 0}
F_{n+k} C(n + k, n) a ^ k`, taken to be `0` when the sum does not converge.
