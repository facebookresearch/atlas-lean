# Absolute convergence on a box inside the majorant radii

Let `a` be majorized by a geometric majorant `m`, let `b : Fin d → ℚ≥0`
satisfy `b i < R_i` for every `i`, and let `x ∈ ℝ^d` satisfy `|x i| ≤ b i`.
Then the series of absolute values `∑ α, |coeff α a · ∏ i, x i ^ α i|`
converges.

Here `a` is a formal power series in `d` variables with rational
coefficients, its term at a real point `x` and exponent `α` is `coeff α a ·
∏ i, x i ^ α i`, and a geometric majorant `m` with bound `M ≥ 0` and radii
`R_i > 0` majorizes `a` when `|coeff α a| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every
`α`.
