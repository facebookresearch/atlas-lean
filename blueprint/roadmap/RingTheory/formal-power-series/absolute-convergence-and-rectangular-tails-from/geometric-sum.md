# Sum of a rational power series at a real point

For a formal power series `a` in `d` variables with rational coefficients
and a real point `x ∈ ℝ^d`, `geometricSum a x` is the unconditional sum `∑'
α, coeff α a · ∏ i, x i ^ α i` over all exponents `α`. By the convention for
unconditional sums, it is `0` when the terms are not summable, so it is the
value of the series only where summability holds.
