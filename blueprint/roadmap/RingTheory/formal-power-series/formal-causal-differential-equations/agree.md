# Agreement through a degree on one axis

For a field `K`, a variable `i : σ`, a natural number `k`, and multivariate
formal power series `a` and `b` over `K` in the variables `σ`, `Agree i k a
b` means that `a` and `b` have the same coefficient at every exponent `n : σ
→₀ ℕ` with `n i ≤ k`. The exponents of the other variables are unrestricted.
