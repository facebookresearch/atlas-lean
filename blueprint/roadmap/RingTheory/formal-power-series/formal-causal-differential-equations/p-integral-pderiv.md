# Integrating a derivative

Let `K` be a field of characteristic zero. For every multivariate formal
power series `f` over `K` and every variable `i`, `pIntegral i (∂f/∂X_i) f =
f`: integrating the derivative along `X_i`, with the `X_i`-degree-zero
coefficients of `f` as boundary values, recovers `f`.
