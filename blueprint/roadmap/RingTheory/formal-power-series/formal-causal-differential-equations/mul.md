# Products preserve agreement on an axis

If `a` agrees with `a'` and `b` agrees with `b'` through degree `k` on axis
`i`, then `a * b` agrees with `a' * b'` through degree `k` on axis `i`: a
coefficient of a product only involves coefficients of the factors of no
larger degree in each variable.

Here `K` is a field, `σ` is an index set of variables, `i : σ` is the
selected axis, and two series agree through degree `k` on axis `i` when
their coefficients coincide at every exponent `n` with `n i ≤ k`.
