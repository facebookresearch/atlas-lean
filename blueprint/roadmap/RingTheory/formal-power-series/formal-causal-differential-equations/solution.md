# Formal solution of a causal equation

For a field `K`, a variable `i : σ`, a map `F` on multivariate formal power
series over `K` and a boundary series `boundary`, `solution i F boundary` is
the power series whose coefficient at each exponent `n` is the coefficient
at `n` of the Picard iterate `approx i F boundary (n i)`, where `approx 0 =
boundary` and `approx (k + 1) = pIntegral i (F (approx k)) boundary`.
