# A prime monic polynomial over a power-series ring

Let `q` be a prime natural number. A monic polynomial of degree `q - 1` over
`W₀⟦T⟧`, where `W₀` is a discrete valuation ring whose maximal ideal is
generated by the image of `q`, whose non-leading
coefficients lie in the maximal ideal and whose constant coefficient is `q`
times a unit, is prime, and its root adjunction is a domain.
