# Majorant of a product of power series

Let `σ` be finite and let `R : σ → ℚ`. If rational power series `a` and `b`
are majorized by geometric majorants with bounds `M_a` and `M_b` and common
radii `R`, then `a * b` is majorized by the geometric majorant with bound
`M_a M_b` and radii `R i / 2`.

A geometric majorant `m` with bound `M ≥ 0` and radii `R_i > 0` majorizes a
rational power series `a` when `|coeff α a| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every
exponent `α`.
