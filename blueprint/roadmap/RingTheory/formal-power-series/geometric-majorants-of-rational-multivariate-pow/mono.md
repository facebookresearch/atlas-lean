# Weakening a majorant

Let `σ` be finite. If a rational power series `a` is majorized by a
geometric majorant `m`, and `m'` is a geometric majorant with a larger bound
($M \le M'$) and smaller radii ($R'_i \le R_i$ for all
$i$), then `a` is majorized by `m'`.

A geometric majorant `m` with bound `M ≥ 0` and radii `R_i > 0` majorizes a
rational power series `a` when `|coeff α a| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every
exponent `α`.
