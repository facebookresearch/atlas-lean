# Majorant for the inverse of a power series

Let `σ` be finite and let `φ` be a rational power series in the variables
`σ` with constant coefficient `c`. Suppose `M ≥ 0`, radii `R_i > 0` and `0 <
r_i < R_i` satisfy `|coeff α φ| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every nonzero
exponent `α`, and `(M / |c|) (∏ i, (1 - r_i / R_i)⁻¹ - 1) ≤ 1`. Then the
formal inverse `φ⁻¹` is majorized by the geometric majorant with bound
`|c|⁻¹` and radii `r`, that is, `|coeff α φ⁻¹| ≤ |c|⁻¹ ∏ i, r_i ^ (-α i)`
for every `α` (with `0⁻¹ = 0`).
