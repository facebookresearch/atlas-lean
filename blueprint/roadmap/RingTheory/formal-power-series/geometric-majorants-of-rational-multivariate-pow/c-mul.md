# Majorant of a scaled power series

Let `σ` be finite. If a rational power series `a` is majorized by a
geometric majorant with bound `M` and radii `R`, then for every rational
`q`, the series `q · a` is majorized by the geometric majorant with bound
`|q| M` and the same radii `R`.

A geometric majorant `m` with bound `M ≥ 0` and radii `R_i > 0` majorizes a
rational power series `a` when `|coeff α a| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every
exponent `α`.
