# Convergence of Taylor shift coefficients

Let `L` be a complete nontrivially normed field with an ultrametric absolute
value, let `F ∈ L⟦X⟧` with coefficients `F_m`, and let `ρ > 0` and `M` be
real numbers with `‖F_m‖ ρ ^ m ≤ M` for all `m`. For every `a ∈ L` with `‖a‖
< ρ` and every `n`, the series `∑_{k ≥ 0} F_{n+k} C(n + k, n) a ^ k`
converges in `L`.
