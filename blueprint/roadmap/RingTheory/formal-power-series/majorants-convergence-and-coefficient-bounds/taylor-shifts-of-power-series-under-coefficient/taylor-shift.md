# Taylor shift of a power series

Let `L` be a complete nontrivially normed field with an ultrametric absolute
value, `a ∈ L`, and `F ∈ L⟦X⟧` with coefficients `F_m`. The Taylor shift of
`F` by `a` is the power series whose coefficient of `X ^ n` is the infinite
sum `∑_{k ≥ 0} F_{n+k} C(n + k, n) a ^ k`, with the convention that a
non-convergent sum is `0`. It is the expansion of `F(X + a)`; all these sums
converge when `‖F_m‖ ρ ^ m` is bounded for some `ρ > ‖a‖`.
