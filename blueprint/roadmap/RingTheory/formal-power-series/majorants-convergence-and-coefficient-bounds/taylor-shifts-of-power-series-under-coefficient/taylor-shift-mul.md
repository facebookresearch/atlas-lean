# Taylor shift is multiplicative

Let `L` be a complete nontrivially normed field with an ultrametric absolute
value, let `F, G ∈ L⟦X⟧`, and let `ρ > 0`, `M`, `M'` be real numbers with
`‖F_n‖ ρ ^ n ≤ M` and `‖G_n‖ ρ ^ n ≤ M'` for all `n`. For every `a ∈ L` with
`‖a‖ < ρ`, the Taylor shift of `F G` by `a` equals the product of the Taylor
shifts of `F` and of `G` by `a`.
