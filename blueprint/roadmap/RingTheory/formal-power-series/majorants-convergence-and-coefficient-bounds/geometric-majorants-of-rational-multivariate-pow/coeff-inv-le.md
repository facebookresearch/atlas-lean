# Coefficient bound for the inverse of a power series

Let `σ` be finite and let `φ` be a rational power series in the variables
`σ` with constant coefficient `c`. Suppose `M ≥ 0`, radii `R_i > 0` and `0 <
r_i < R_i` satisfy `|coeff α φ| ≤ M ∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every nonzero
exponent `α`, and `(M / |c|) (∏ i, (1 - r_i / R_i)⁻¹ - 1) ≤ 1`. Then every
coefficient of the formal inverse satisfies `|coeff α φ⁻¹| ≤ |c|⁻¹ ∏ i, r_i
^ (-α i)`. Equivalently, `φ⁻¹` is majorized by the geometric majorant with
bound `|c|⁻¹` and radii `r`.

For `c = 0` the conventions `φ⁻¹ = 0` and `|c|⁻¹ = 0` make the bound read `0
≤ 0`.
