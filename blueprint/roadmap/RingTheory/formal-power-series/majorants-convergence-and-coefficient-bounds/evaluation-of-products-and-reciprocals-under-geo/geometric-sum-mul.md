# Sum of a product of power series

Let `a` and `b` be rational power series in `d` variables majorized by
geometric majorants `ma` and `mb`. If `r : Fin d → ℚ≥0` is strictly smaller
than the radii of both majorants and `x ∈ ℝ^d` satisfies `|x i| ≤ r i`, then
the sum of `a * b` at `x` is the product of the sums of `a` and `b` at `x`.

Here `d` is a natural number, `a` is a formal power series in `d` variables
with rational coefficients, and its sum at a real point `x ∈ ℝ^d` is `∑' α,
coeff α a · ∏ i, x i ^ α i` (an unconditional sum, taken to be `0` when the
terms are not summable). A geometric majorant `m` with bound `M ≥ 0` and
radii `R_i > 0` majorizes a rational power series `a` when `|coeff α a| ≤ M
∏ i, R_i ^ (-α i)` for every exponent `α`.
