# Mathematical Chemistry

Mathematical chemistry studies quantitative models of chemical systems and reactions.

Undergraduate foundations begin with equilibrium. [Reaction Gibbs energy and equilibrium constants](chemical-thermodynamics/reaction-gibbs-energy-and-equilibrium-constants/README.md) lead to [galvanic cells and the Nernst equation](chemical-thermodynamics/galvanic-cells-and-the-nernst-equation/README.md), with dilute-electrolyte activity corrections modeled by [Debye–Hückel theory](solutions-and-aqueous-equilibria/debye-hueckel-theory-of-electrolytes/README.md); [adsorption equilibria in porous solids](chemical-thermodynamics/adsorption-equilibria-in-porous-solids/README.md) and [physisorption surface areas](analytical-chemistry/physisorption-surface-area-and-porosity/README.md) do the same for gas uptake. The quantum side begins with the [molecular Hamiltonian and Born–Oppenheimer separation](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/molecular-hamiltonian-and-born-oppenheimer/README.md), [Slater determinants](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/slater-determinants-and-second-quantization/README.md) and [Hartree–Fock theory](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/hartree-fock-theory/README.md).

Graduate models follow: [electrode kinetics](chemical-kinetics/electrode-kinetics/README.md), [Marcus theory of electron transfer](chemical-kinetics/rate-laws/marcus-theory-of-electron-transfer/README.md), [lattice-gas models of intercalation electrodes](chemical-thermodynamics/lattice-gas-models-of-intercalation-electrodes/README.md) and [double-layer capacitance](solutions-and-aqueous-equilibria/electric-double-layer-capacitance/README.md) for charge storage; [electron correlation](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/electron-correlation/README.md), [density-functional theory](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/density-functional-theory/README.md) and [molecular photophysics](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/photophysics-of-molecular-emitters/README.md) for electronic structure.

These lead to research models of conductive porous frameworks and electrochemical storage, as studied by Mircea Dincă and collaborators, such as [redox hopping in framework films](chemical-kinetics/electrode-kinetics/redox-hopping-in-framework-films/README.md) and the [optimal affinity for deliverable capacity](chemical-thermodynamics/adsorption-equilibria-in-porous-solids/optimal-affinity-for-deliverable-capacity.md); and to the electronic-structure, molecular-design and autonomous-experiment work of Alán Aspuru-Guzik and collaborators: [quantum algorithms for electronic structure](quantum-chemistry-and-spectroscopy/quantum-algorithms-for-electronic-structure/README.md), [molecular string representations](chemical-informatics-and-molecular-design/molecular-string-representations/README.md), [generative models of chemical space](chemical-informatics-and-molecular-design/generative-molecular-models/README.md) and [autonomous experiment planning](chemical-informatics-and-molecular-design/autonomous-experiment-planning/README.md). These pages state models and their mathematical consequences; measured conductivities, capacities and benchmark scores appear only as context.

Prerequisites from other subjects, not Chemistry results: [finite fermionic Fock space](../Physics/quantum-mechanics/finite-fermionic-fock-space/README.md), [fermion-to-qubit encodings](../ComputerScience/quantum-algorithms/fermion-to-qubit-encodings/README.md), [tight-binding lattice models](../Physics/quantum-mechanics/tight-binding-lattice-models/README.md), [localized-carrier transport](../Physics/quantum-mechanics/localized-carrier-transport/README.md) and [kernel-density acquisition](../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/kernel-density-acquisition/README.md).
