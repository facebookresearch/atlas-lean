# Proton excess of a species

The proton excess of each species relative to `R`: the charge row plus the component rows
weighted by the reference weights `w`, that is `z i + ∑ k, w k * B k i`. It is `0` on the zero
level, `1` on hydronium and `-1` on hydroxide, so it
[counts the protons](proton-excess-eq-of-content.md) a species carries in excess of (positive)
or short of (negative) the zero level.
