# The air–water exchange conserves the total inventory

Let the gas amount `n_g` and the dissolved amount `n_w` of a closed two-compartment system exchange
through the flux `J = k A (H p - c)`, with partial pressure `p = n_g R T / V_g` and dissolved
concentration `c = n_w / V_w`, for real parameters `V_g`, `V_w`, `R`, `T`, `H`, `k` and `A`. If
`n_g' = -J` and `n_w' = J` at every real time, then `n_g(t) + n_w(t) = n_g(0) + n_w(0)` for every
real `t`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `p = 0` when `V_g = 0` and `c = 0` when `V_w = 0`;
physically both volumes are positive.
