# The air–water disequilibrium is an exponential in time

Let the gas amount `n_g` and the dissolved amount `n_w` of a closed two-compartment system with
positive volumes `V_g` and `V_w` exchange through the flux `J = k A (H p - c)`, with partial
pressure `p = n_g R T / V_g` and dissolved concentration `c = n_w / V_w`, for real `R`, `T`, `H`,
`k` and `A`. If `n_g' = -J` and `n_w' = J` at every real time, the disequilibrium `d = H p - c`
satisfies `d(t) = d(0) exp(-r t)` for every real `t`, with `r = k A (H R T / V_g + 1 / V_w)`. No
sign is imposed on `k`, `A`, `H`, `R` and `T`: for `r > 0` the disequilibrium decays, and for
`r < 0` its magnitude grows unless `d(0) = 0`.
