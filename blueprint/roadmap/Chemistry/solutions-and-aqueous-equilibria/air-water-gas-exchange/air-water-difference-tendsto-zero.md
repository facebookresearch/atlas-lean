# The air–water disequilibrium tends to zero for positive parameters

Let the gas amount `n_g` and the dissolved amount `n_w` of a closed two-compartment system exchange
through the flux `J = k A (H p - c)`, with partial pressure `p = n_g R T / V_g` and dissolved
concentration `c = n_w / V_w`, where the transfer coefficient `k`, the interface area `A`, the
Henry constant `H`, the gas constant `R`, the absolute temperature `T` and the volumes `V_g` and
`V_w` are positive. If `n_g' = -J` and `n_w' = J` at every real time, the disequilibrium
`H p(t) - c(t)` tends to `0` as `t → ∞`.
