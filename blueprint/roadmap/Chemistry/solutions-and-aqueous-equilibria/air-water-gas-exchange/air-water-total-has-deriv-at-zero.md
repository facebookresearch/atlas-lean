# The air–water total inventory has zero derivative under exchange

Let the gas amount `n_g` and the dissolved amount `n_w` of a closed two-compartment system exchange
through the flux `J = k A (H p - c)`, with partial pressure `p = n_g R T / V_g` and dissolved
concentration `c = n_w / V_w`, for real parameters `V_g`, `V_w`, `R`, `T`, `H`, `k` and `A`. At a
time `t` where `n_g'(t) = -J(t)` and `n_w'(t) = J(t)`, the total inventory `n_g + n_w` has
derivative `0`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `p = 0` when `V_g = 0` and `c = 0` when
`V_w = 0`; physically both volumes are positive.
