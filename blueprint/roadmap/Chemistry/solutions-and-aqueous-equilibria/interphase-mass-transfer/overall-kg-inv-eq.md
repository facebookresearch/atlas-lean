# Resistance addition for the overall gas-side coefficient

For $H > 0$, $k_G > 0$ and $k_L > 0$, the [overall gas-side coefficient](overall-kg.md)
$K_G = 1 / (1 / k_G + 1 / (H k_L))$ satisfies

$$\frac{1}{K_G} = \frac{1}{k_G} + \frac{1}{H k_L}.$$

The positivity hypotheses are part of the statement; since $(1/x)^{-1} = x$ for every real $x$ (with
$0^{-1} = 0$), the identity of reciprocals holds for all real inputs.
