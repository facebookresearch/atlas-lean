# Overall gas-side mass-transfer coefficient

For a Henry constant $H$ and film coefficients $k_G$, $k_L$, the overall gas-side coefficient on a partial-pressure driving-force basis is defined by

$$K_G = \frac{1}{1/k_G + 1/(H k_L)},$$

that is, $1/K_G = 1/k_G + 1/(H k_L)$, where each quotient with zero denominator is $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$; in particular $K_G = 0$ when $1/k_G + 1/(H k_L) = 0$.
