# Overall gas-side coefficient from film resistances in series

For real $H$, $k_G$ and $k_L$, the overall gas-side mass-transfer coefficient is

$$K_G = \frac{1}{1 / k_G + 1 / (H k_L)},$$

the reciprocal of the gas-film and liquid-film resistances in series, on the basis of a gas
partial-pressure driving force. It is defined for all real inputs with the convention $x / 0 = 0$:
$K_G = k_G$ when $H = 0$ or $k_L = 0$, $K_G = H k_L$ when $k_G = 0$, and $K_G = 0$ when
$1 / k_G + 1 / (H k_L) = 0$. In the physical reading the film coefficients $k_G$, $k_L$ and the
Henry constant $H$ (with the convention $c = H p$ for the equilibrium liquid concentration) are
positive.
