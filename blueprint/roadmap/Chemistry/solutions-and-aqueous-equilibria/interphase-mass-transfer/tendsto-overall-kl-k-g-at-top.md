# As $k_G \to \infty$ the liquid-side overall coefficient tends to $k_L$

Let $H > 0$ and $k_L > 0$. Then the [overall liquid-side coefficient](overall-kl.md)
$K_L = 1 / (1 / k_L + H / k_G)$ tends to $k_L$ as $k_G \to \infty$: when the gas-film resistance
vanishes, the liquid film controls the transfer.
