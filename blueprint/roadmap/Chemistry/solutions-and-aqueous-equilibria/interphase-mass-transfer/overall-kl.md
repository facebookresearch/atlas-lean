# Overall liquid-side mass-transfer coefficient

For a Henry constant $H$ and film coefficients $k_G$ and $k_L$, the overall liquid-side coefficient is defined by film resistances in series, $K_L = 1/(1/k_L + H/k_G)$, on a liquid concentration driving-force basis. By the convention $x/0 = 0$, a reciprocal term is $0$ when its denominator is $0$, and $K_L = 0$ when $1/k_L + H/k_G = 0$.
