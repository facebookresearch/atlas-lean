# Overall liquid-side coefficient from film resistances in series

For real $H$, $k_G$ and $k_L$, the overall liquid-side mass-transfer coefficient is

$$K_L = \frac{1}{1 / k_L + H / k_G},$$

the reciprocal of the liquid-film and gas-film resistances in series, on the basis of a liquid
concentration driving force. It is defined for all real inputs with the convention $x / 0 = 0$:
$K_L = k_L$ when $k_G = 0$, $K_L = k_G / H$ when $k_L = 0$, and $K_L = 0$ when
$1 / k_L + H / k_G = 0$. In the physical reading the film coefficients $k_G$, $k_L$ and the Henry
constant $H$ (with the convention $c = H p$ for the equilibrium liquid concentration) are positive.
