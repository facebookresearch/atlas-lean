# Two-film elimination on the gas basis under interfacial equilibrium

Let $H > 0$, $k_G > 0$ and $k_L > 0$, and let $p_b$, $c_b$ be the bulk gas partial pressure and bulk
liquid concentration, $p_i$, $c_i$ the interfacial ones and $N$ a real number. Assume interfacial
equilibrium under the Henry convention, $c_i = H p_i$, and the two film laws $N = k_G (p_b - p_i)$
(gas film) and $N = k_L (c_i - c_b)$ (liquid film), so that $N$ is the common flux, positive for
absorption into the liquid. Then, with the [overall gas-side coefficient](overall-kg.md)
$K_G = 1 / (1 / k_G + 1 / (H k_L))$,

$$N = K_G \left(p_b - \frac{c_b}{H}\right),$$

the overall coefficient times the gap between the bulk partial pressure and the partial pressure
$c_b / H$ in equilibrium with the bulk liquid.
