# Interfacial molar flux

For a mass-transfer coefficient $k$, a Henry constant $H$ (with the convention that the equilibrium
liquid concentration at partial pressure $p$ is $c^* = H p$) and a liquid concentration $c$, the
molar flux per unit interfacial area is

$$J = k \,(H p - c) .$$

For $k > 0$, positive values are absorption into the liquid and negative values desorption.
