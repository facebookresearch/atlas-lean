# Interphase mass transfer

Transfer of a dissolved gas across a gas–liquid interface: the flux is a mass-transfer coefficient
times the gap between the equilibrium concentration given by Henry's law and the liquid
concentration, and the volumetric rate multiplies it by the interfacial area per unit volume.
