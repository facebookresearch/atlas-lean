# Resistance addition for the overall liquid-side coefficient

For $H > 0$, $k_G > 0$ and $k_L > 0$, the [overall liquid-side coefficient](overall-kl.md)
$K_L = 1 / (1 / k_L + H / k_G)$ satisfies

$$\frac{1}{K_L} = \frac{1}{k_L} + \frac{H}{k_G}.$$

The positivity hypotheses are part of the statement; since $(1/x)^{-1} = x$ for every real $x$ (with
$0^{-1} = 0$), the identity of reciprocals holds for all real inputs.
