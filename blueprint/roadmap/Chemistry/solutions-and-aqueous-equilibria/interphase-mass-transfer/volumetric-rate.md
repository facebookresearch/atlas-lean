# Volumetric mass-transfer rate

For a mass-transfer coefficient $k$, an interfacial area per unit volume $a$, a Henry constant $H$
(equilibrium concentration $c^* = H p$), a partial pressure $p$ and a liquid concentration $c$, the
transfer rate per unit volume is

$$R = k a \,(H p - c) .$$

It equals $a$ times the interfacial flux. For $k a > 0$, positive values are absorption into the
liquid and negative values desorption.
