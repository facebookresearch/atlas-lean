# As $k_L \to \infty$ the gas-side overall coefficient tends to $k_G$

Let $H > 0$ and $k_G > 0$. Then the [overall gas-side coefficient](overall-kg.md)
$K_G = 1 / (1 / k_G + 1 / (H k_L))$ tends to $k_G$ as $k_L \to \infty$: when the liquid-film
resistance vanishes, the gas film controls the transfer.
