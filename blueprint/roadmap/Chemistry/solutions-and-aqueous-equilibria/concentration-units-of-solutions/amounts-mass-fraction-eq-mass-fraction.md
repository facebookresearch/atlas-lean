# Mixture mass fractions as single-solute mass fractions

The [mass fraction of component](../../amounts-and-stoichiometry/amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mass-fraction.md) `i` of a mixture
with amounts `n` and molar masses `M`, `n i * M i / ∑ j, n j * M j`, equals the
[single-solute mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) of the component mass `n i * M i` over the total
mass `∑ j, n j * M j`.
