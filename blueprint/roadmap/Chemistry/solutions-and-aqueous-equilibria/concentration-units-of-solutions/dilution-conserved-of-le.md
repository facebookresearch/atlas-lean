# Dilution conserves c·V and does not raise the concentration

Diluting a solution of positive amount `n` from volume `V₁ > 0` to a measured
final volume `V₂ ≥ V₁` (solvent added, amount unchanged), with `c₁ = n / V₁` and `c₂ = n / V₂`,
keeps `c₁ * V₁ = c₂ * V₂`, and the concentration stays positive and does not rise:
`0 < c₂ ≤ c₁`.
