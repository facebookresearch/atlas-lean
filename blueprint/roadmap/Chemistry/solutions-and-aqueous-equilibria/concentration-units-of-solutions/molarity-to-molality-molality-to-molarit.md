# Converting molality to molarity and back

Round trip: converting molality to molarity and back returns the molality, for `b ≥ 0`
and positive `ρ` and `M`. The converted molarity lies in the physical domain
([nonnegative molalities convert to physical molarities](maps-to-molality-to-molarity.md)), so
the [molarity-to-molality conversion](molarity-to-molality.md) maps that domain onto all of
`b ≥ 0` ([the conversion bijection](bij-on-molarity-to-molality.md)).
