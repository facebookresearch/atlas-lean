# Mass fraction of a solute

Mass fraction `w = mSolute / mSoln`: solute mass over total solution mass. This is the
single-solute ratio `mSolute / mSoln`; it is not the
[mass fraction of a component of a mixture](../../amounts-and-stoichiometry/amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mass-fraction.md), the
family version `n i * M i / ∑ j, n j * M j` over per-species molar masses.
