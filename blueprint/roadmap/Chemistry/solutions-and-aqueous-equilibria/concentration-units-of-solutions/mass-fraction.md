# Mass fraction of a solute

Mass fraction `w = mSolute / mSoln`: solute mass over total solution mass. It is defined for
every real `mSoln`: for `mSoln = 0` the convention `x / 0 = 0` makes it `0`, and the intended
domain is `mSoln > 0`. This is the single-solute ratio `mSolute / mSoln`; it is not the
[mass fraction of a component of a mixture](../../amounts-and-stoichiometry/amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mass-fraction.md), the
family version `n i * M i / ∑ j, n j * M j` over per-species molar masses.
