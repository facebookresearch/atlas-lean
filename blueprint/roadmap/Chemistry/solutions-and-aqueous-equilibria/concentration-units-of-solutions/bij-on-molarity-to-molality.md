# The conversion bijection

For positive `ρ` and `M`, molarity-to-molality is a bijection
from the physical molarity domain `0 ≤ c ∧ c < ρ / M` onto the molalities `b ≥ 0`. A remark
beyond the statement: by the two round trips, its inverse is the
[conversion from molality to molarity](molality-to-molarity.md).
