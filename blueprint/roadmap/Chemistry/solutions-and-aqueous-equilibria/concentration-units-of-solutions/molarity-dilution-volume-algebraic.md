# Algebraic molarity identity for the computed volume

An algebraic identity, not a statement about dilution: for `c₁ * V₁ ≠ 0` and every `c₂`,
`(c₁ * V₁) / (c₁ * V₁ / c₂) = c₂`, so the amount `c₁ * V₁` in the
[computed final volume](dilution-volume.md) has [molarity](molarity.md) `c₂`. At `c₂ = 0` the
computed volume is `0` and the identity holds only by the convention `x / 0 = 0`. For the
physical statement see [dilution to a target concentration](dilution-volume-dilutes.md).
