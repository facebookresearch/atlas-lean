# Mole fractions of a combined mixture as a weighted average

An algebraic identity for the [mole fractions](../../amounts-and-stoichiometry/amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mole-fraction.md) of a
sum `n₁ + n₂` of amount families over the same species: each is the average of the two mole
fractions weighted by the totals `N₁ = ∑ j, n₁ j` and `N₂ = ∑ j, n₂ j`,
`x(n₁ + n₂) i = (N₁ * x(n₁) i + N₂ * x(n₂) i) / (N₁ + N₂)`. Only `N₁ ≠ 0` and `N₂ ≠ 0` are
assumed, so this is not a statement about physical mixing or convexity: totals `-1` and `2`
give weights `-1` and `2`, and when `N₁ + N₂ = 0` both sides are `0` by the convention
`x / 0 = 0`. Mixing two solutions with no reaction adds their amounts; for positive totals see
[mixing as a convex combination](mole-fraction-mix-convex.md).
