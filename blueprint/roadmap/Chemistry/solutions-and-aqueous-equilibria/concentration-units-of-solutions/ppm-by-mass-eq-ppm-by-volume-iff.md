# Mass and volume ppm agree exactly when the densities agree

With solute density `ρs` (`mSolute = ρs * vSolute`) and solution density `ρ ≠ 0`
(`mSoln = ρ * vSoln`), and nonzero volumes `vSolute` and `vSoln`, ppm by mass equals ppm by
volume exactly when the solute and solution densities agree, `ρs = ρ`.
