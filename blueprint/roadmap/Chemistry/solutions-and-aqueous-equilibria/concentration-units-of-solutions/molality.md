# Molality of a solution

Molality `b = n / mSolvent`: amount of solute per mass of solvent, with the mass in a fixed unit
(kg or g). It is defined for every real `mSolvent`: for `mSolvent = 0` the convention
`x / 0 = 0` makes it `0`, and the intended domain is `mSolvent > 0`. Molality is a different
function from [molarity](molarity.md): the denominator here is a solvent mass, not a solution
volume.
