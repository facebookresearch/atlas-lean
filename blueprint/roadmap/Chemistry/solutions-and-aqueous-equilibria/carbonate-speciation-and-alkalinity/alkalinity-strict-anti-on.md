# Alkalinity is strictly decreasing in the hydrogen-ion concentration

For a total dissolved inorganic carbon `C0 ≥ 0` and positive constants `K1`, `K2` and `Kw`, the
alkalinity of the ideal closed carbonate-plus-water system,
`A(h) = C0 (K1 h + 2 K1 K2) / (h² + K1 h + K1 K2) + Kw / h - h`, is strictly decreasing in the
hydrogen-ion concentration `h` on `h > 0`.
