# The three carbonate species sum to the total dissolved carbon

For positive equilibrium constants `K1` and `K2`, a positive hydrogen-ion concentration `h` and a
real total dissolved inorganic carbon `C0`, with `Z = h² + K1 h + K1 K2`, the three species
concentrations `C0 h² / Z` (dissolved CO₂), `C0 K1 h / Z` (bicarbonate) and `C0 K1 K2 / Z`
(carbonate) sum to `C0`.
