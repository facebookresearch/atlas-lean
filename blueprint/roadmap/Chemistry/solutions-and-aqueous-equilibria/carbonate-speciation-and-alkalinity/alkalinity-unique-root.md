# The alkalinity equation has exactly one positive root

For a total dissolved inorganic carbon `C0 ≥ 0`, positive constants `K1`, `K2` and `Kw`, and every
real `A0`, there is exactly one hydrogen-ion concentration `h > 0` with `A(h) = A0`, where
`A(h) = C0 (K1 h + 2 K1 K2) / (h² + K1 h + K1 K2) + Kw / h - h` is the alkalinity of the ideal
closed carbonate-plus-water system.
