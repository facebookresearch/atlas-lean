# Feasible forward extents of the reversed reaction

For the reversed reaction `-ν`: if every initial amount is nonnegative and `ν` produces
some species, the forward-feasible extents of `-ν` form the closed interval from `0` to
`-ξ_min`, where `ξ_min` is the [minimal reverse extent](min-extent.md) of `ν`: `-ξ_min` is the
smallest ratio `n₀ i / ν i` over the species `ν` produces, which are the species `-ν` consumes.
