# The product increment is greatest at the maximal forward extent

With nonnegative initial amounts and a reaction that consumes some species, the increment
`ν P * ξ` of a product species `P` (`0 < ν P`) over the feasible forward extents has greatest
value `ν P * ξ_max`, attained at `ξ = ξ_max`, the [maximal forward extent](max-extent.md).
