# Percent yield at most 100

Let the reaction consume some species, let the theoretical yield `ν P * ξ_max` of the species
`P` be positive, where `ξ_max` is the [maximal forward extent](max-extent.md), and let the
reaction reach an extent `ξ` between `0` and `ξ_max`. If the actual increment of `P` is
`ν P * ξ`, which holds when this reaction is the only source of `P`, the percent yield is at most
`100`. A measured percent yield above `100` therefore signals impure or wet product or another
source of `P`.
