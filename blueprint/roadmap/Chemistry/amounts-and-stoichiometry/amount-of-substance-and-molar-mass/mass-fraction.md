# Mass fraction of a component

The mass fraction `w i = n i * M i / ∑ j, n j * M j` of the component `i` of a mixture with
amounts `n` and molar masses `M`; when the total mass `∑ j, n j * M j` is `0`, every fraction is
`0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`. Applied to mole fractions in place of amounts, it converts
mole fractions to mass fractions
([mole fractions give the same mass fractions as amounts](mass-fraction-mole-fraction.md)).
