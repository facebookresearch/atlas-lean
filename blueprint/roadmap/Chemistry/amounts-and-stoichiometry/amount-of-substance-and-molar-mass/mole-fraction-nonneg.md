# Mole fractions are nonnegative

Mole fractions of nonnegative amounts are nonnegative: for amounts `n i ≥ 0`, every mole fraction
`n i / ∑ j, n j` is nonnegative, with no positive total assumed (for a zero total every fraction is
`0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`). In particular the mole fractions `ṅ i / ∑ j, ṅ j` of a
[process stream](../composition-of-process-streams/stream-flow.md), whose species flow rates `ṅ i`
are nonnegative by definition, are nonnegative.
