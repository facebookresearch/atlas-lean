# Clearing the denominators of nonnegative mole ratios

Clearing denominators of nonnegative ratios: for `r e = a / b ≥ 0` in lowest terms, the
natural number `a * (D / b)`, where `D` is the [common denominator](common-denominator.md); it
equals `D * r e`
([clearing denominators multiplies by the common denominator](clear-denominators-cast.md)).
A negative ratio has a negative numerator, which is truncated to `0`, so it is sent to `0`.
