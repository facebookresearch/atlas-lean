# A molecular formula is a positive multiple of its empirical formula

The textbook procedure, with positive molar masses and a positive integer ratio: let a compound
have formula vector `ν ≠ 0`, empirical formula `p` and molar mass `M > 0`, and let `M_emp > 0` be
the molar mass of `p`. If `M = k * M_emp` for a natural number `k`, then `0 < k` and `ν = k • p`.
The versions without positivity,
[for a formula proportional to a primitive one](eq-nsmul-of-formula-molar-mass-eq.md) and
[from the empirical formula](eq-nsmul-of-empirical-formula-eq.md), conclude only `ν = k • p`.
