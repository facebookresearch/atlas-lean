# The empirical formula is proportional to the mole ratios

The empirical formula `p` of a nonzero vector `r` of nonnegative ratios is proportional to it
with a positive factor: there is `c > 0` with `p e = c * r e` for every `e`. One such factor is
`D / g`, where `D` is the common denominator and `g` the greatest common divisor of the cleared
entries.
