# For nᵢ ≥ 0 with ∑ nᵢ > 0 and Mᵢ > 0, the total mass is positive

A mixture with nonnegative amounts, a positive total amount and positive molar masses has a
positive total mass `∑ i, n i * M i`.

For a process stream, the same statement for its total mass flow has its own page: [Positive molar masses and total flow give positive total mass flow](../process-streams-amount-flow-rates-and-compositio/sum-mass-flow-pos.md).
