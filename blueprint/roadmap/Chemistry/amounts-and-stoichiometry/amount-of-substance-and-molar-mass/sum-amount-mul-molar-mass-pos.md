# For a positive total and positive molar masses, ∑ nᵢ Mᵢ is positive

A mixture with nonnegative amounts, a positive total amount and positive molar masses has a positive
total mass `∑ i, n i * M i`. For a
[process stream](../composition-of-process-streams/stream-flow.md), whose species flow rates `ṅ i`
are nonnegative by definition, with a positive total flow `∑ i, ṅ i` and positive molar masses, this
says that the total [mass flow](../composition-of-process-streams/mass-flow.md) `∑ i, ṅ i * M i` is
positive.
