# Weight percent

For a finite index set $\iota$ of components, molar masses $M : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and amounts $x : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, the weight percent of component $i$ is $100$ times its mass fraction:

$$\mathrm{wt\%}(M, x)_i = 100 \cdot \frac{x_i M_i}{\sum_j x_j M_j},$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
