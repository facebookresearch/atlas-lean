# Arrhenius log identity

Let $y$, $y_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and $A$ be quantities of one common dimension, $E$ a molar energy, $R$ a quantity with the dimension of the gas constant and $T$ an absolute temperature, with positive coordinates $y_{\mathrm{ref}}, A, y, T, R > 0$. Suppose $y$ follows the Arrhenius model

$$y = A \exp\!\left(-\frac{E}{R T}\right),$$

where the exponent is formed as a dimensionless quotient. Then

$$\ln \frac{y}{y_{\mathrm{ref}}} = \ln \frac{A}{y_{\mathrm{ref}}} - \frac{E}{R T},$$

where each logarithm is taken of the dimensionless ratio of two quantities of the same dimension.
