# With no dry solids and positive water mass, the wet basis is $1$

If $m_w > 0$ and the dry-solid mass is $m_s = 0$, the [wet-basis moisture content](wet-basis.md) is

$$w = \frac{m_w}{m_w + 0} = 1 .$$

So the hypothesis $m_s > 0$ of the [bound $w < 1$](wet-basis-lt-one.md) cannot be dropped, and
$w = 1$ lies outside the range $0 \le w < 1$ of the
[conversion to the dry basis](dry-basis-of-wet-basis.md).
