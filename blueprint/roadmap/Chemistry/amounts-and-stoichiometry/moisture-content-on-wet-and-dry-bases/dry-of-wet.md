# Dry basis as a function of the wet basis, $w / (1 - w)$

For a real number $w$, let

$$X(w) = \frac{w}{1 - w} .$$

When $w = m_w / (m_w + m_s)$ is the [wet-basis moisture content](wet-basis.md) of a water mass
$m_w \ge 0$ and a dry-solid mass $m_s > 0$, $X(w)$ is the
[dry-basis content of the same material](dry-basis-of-wet-basis.md), $m_w / m_s$. As $w \to 1^-$,
$X(w)$ [tends to $+\infty$](tendsto-dry-of-wet-at-one.md). The definition takes all real $w$: with
the convention $x / 0 = 0$ its value at $w = 1$ is $0$.
