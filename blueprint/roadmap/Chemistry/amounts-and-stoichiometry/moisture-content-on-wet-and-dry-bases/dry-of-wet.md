# Dry basis as a function of the wet basis, $w / (1 - w)$

For a real number $w$, let

$$X(w) = \frac{w}{1 - w} .$$

For a wet-basis moisture content $0 \le w < 1$ it is the
[dry-basis content of the same material](dry-basis-of-wet-basis.md), and it
[tends to $+\infty$ as $w \to 1^-$](tendsto-dry-of-wet-at-one.md). The definition takes all real
$w$: with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ its value at $w = 1$ is $0$, and for $w > 1$ it is negative.
