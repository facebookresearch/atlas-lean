# Wet basis as a function of the dry basis, $X / (1 + X)$

For a real number $X$, let

$$w(X) = \frac{X}{1 + X} .$$

For a dry-basis moisture content $X \ge 0$ it is the
[wet-basis content of the same material](wet-basis-dry-basis-inv.md), a number in $[0, 1)$. The
definition takes all real $X$: with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ its value at $X = -1$ is $0$.
