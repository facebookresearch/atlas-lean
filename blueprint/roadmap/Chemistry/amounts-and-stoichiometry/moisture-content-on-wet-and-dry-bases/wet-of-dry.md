# Wet basis as a function of the dry basis, $X / (1 + X)$

For a real number $X$, let

$$w(X) = \frac{X}{1 + X} .$$

When $X = m_w / m_s$ is the dry-basis moisture content of a water mass $m_w \ge 0$ and a dry-solid
mass $m_s > 0$, $w(X)$ is the [wet-basis content of the same material](dry-basis-of-wet-basis.md),
$m_w / (m_w + m_s)$. The definition takes all real $X$: with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ its value at
$X = -1$ is $0$.
