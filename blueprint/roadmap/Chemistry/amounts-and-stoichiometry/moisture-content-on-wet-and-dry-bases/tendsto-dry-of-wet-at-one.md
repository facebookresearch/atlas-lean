# The dry basis $w / (1 - w)$ tends to $+\infty$ as $w \to 1^-$

As $w$ tends to $1$ from below, the [dry basis as a function of the wet basis](dry-of-wet.md) tends
to $+\infty$:

$$\lim_{w \to 1^-} \frac{w}{1 - w} = +\infty ,$$

that is, for every $M$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that $w / (1 - w) \ge M$ whenever
$1 - \delta < w < 1$. At $w = 1$ itself the formula gives $0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
