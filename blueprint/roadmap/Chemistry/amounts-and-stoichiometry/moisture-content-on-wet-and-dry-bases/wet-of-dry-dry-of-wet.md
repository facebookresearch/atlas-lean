# For $w < 1$, converting from wet to dry basis and back returns $w$

For every real $w < 1$, the [dry basis](dry-of-wet.md) $X = w / (1 - w)$ and the
[wet basis](wet-of-dry.md) of that $X$ give back $w$:

$$\frac{X}{1 + X} = w \qquad \text{for } X = \frac{w}{1 - w} .$$

The hypothesis covers the physical range $0 \le w < 1$ of wet-basis contents. It excludes $w = 1$,
where the convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives $X = 0$ and the composite is $0$.
