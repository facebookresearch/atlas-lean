# Dry-basis moisture ratio

For moisture mass $m_w$ and dry-solid mass $m_s$, the dry-basis moisture ratio is $X = m_w / m_s$, with the value $0$ when $m_s = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.

The other form has its own page: [Gravimetric water content](../../../EarthScience/hydrology-and-surface-processes/part-02/soil-phase-relations/gravimetric-water-content.md).
