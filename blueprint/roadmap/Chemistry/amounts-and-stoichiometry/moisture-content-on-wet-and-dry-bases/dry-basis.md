# Dry-basis moisture content $m_w / m_s$

For real numbers $m_w$ (the water mass) and $m_s$ (the dry-solid mass), the dry-basis moisture
content is the water mass per unit mass of dry solid,

$$X = \frac{m_w}{m_s} .$$

The intended domain is $m_w \ge 0$ and $m_s > 0$, where [$X \ge 0$](dry-basis-nonneg.md), with no
upper bound. The definition takes all real inputs: with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ it is $0$ when
$m_s = 0$. Both masses are in the same unit, so $X$ is a pure number.
