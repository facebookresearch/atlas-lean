# For $m_w \ge 0$ and $m_s > 0$, the wet-basis content is below $1$

If $m_w \ge 0$ and $m_s > 0$, the [wet-basis moisture content](wet-basis.md) is strictly less than
$1$:

$$w = \frac{m_w}{m_w + m_s} < 1 .$$

Without dry solids, $m_s = 0 < m_w$, it is [exactly $1$](wet-basis-eq-one-of-zero-solids.md).
