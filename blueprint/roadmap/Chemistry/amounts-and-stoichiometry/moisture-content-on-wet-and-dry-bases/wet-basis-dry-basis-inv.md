# For $m_s > 0$ and $m_w \ge 0$, $w = X/(1+X)$

If $m_s > 0$ and $m_w \ge 0$, the [wet-basis](wet-basis.md) and [dry-basis](dry-basis.md) moisture
contents $w = m_w / (m_w + m_s)$ and $X = m_w / m_s$ of the same material satisfy

$$w = \frac{X}{1 + X} .$$

Under these hypotheses $X \ge 0$, so the denominator is positive. Without dry solids,
$m_s = 0 < m_w$, the identity fails: $w = 1$, while the convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives $X = 0$ and
$X / (1 + X) = 0$.

The inverse conversion has its own page:
[For $m_s > 0$ and $m_w \ge 0$, $X = w/(1-w)$](dry-basis-of-wet-basis.md).
