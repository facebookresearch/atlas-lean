# Moisture content on wet and dry bases

A wet solid with water mass $m_w \ge 0$ and dry-solid mass $m_s > 0$ has the wet-basis moisture
content $w = m_w / (m_w + m_s)$, in $[0, 1)$, and the dry-basis moisture content
$X = m_w / m_s \ge 0$. They determine each other through $X = w / (1 - w)$ and $w = X / (1 + X)$,
and $w / (1 - w)$ tends to $+\infty$ as $w \to 1^-$. The definitions take all real inputs, with the
convention $x / 0 = 0$.
