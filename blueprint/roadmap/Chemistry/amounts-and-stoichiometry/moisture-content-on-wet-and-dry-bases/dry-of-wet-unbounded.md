# The dry basis $w / (1 - w)$ is unbounded above on $0 \le w < 1$

For every real $M$ there is a wet-basis content $w$ with $0 \le w < 1$ and

$$\frac{w}{1 - w} > M .$$

So the [dry basis as a function of the wet basis](dry-of-wet.md) is unbounded above on $[0, 1)$.
That it exceeds every bound for all $w$ close enough to $1$ is the
[limit as $w \to 1^-$](tendsto-dry-of-wet-at-one.md).
