# Rougher–cleaner circuit with cleaner-tail recycle

In a circuit where a rougher sends a fraction $r_1 \in [0, 1]$ of the valuable material it receives to a cleaner, the cleaner sends a
fraction $r_2 \in [0, 1]$ of what it receives to the final concentrate, and the cleaner tails return to the rougher feed, the recycle gain
is $r_1(1 - r_2)$. For positive fresh valuable feed and $r_1(1 - r_2) < 1$, the steady overall recovery is

$$\frac{r_1 r_2}{1 - r_1(1 - r_2)} \in [0, 1].$$

When $r_1 = 1$ and $r_2 = 0$ the gain is one and there is no finite steady flow. These are fractions of the valuable material; they do
not determine the gangue or the grades, and flotation kinetics are outside this result.
