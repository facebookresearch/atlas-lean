# Two-product formula

Let a feed of dry mass flow $F > 0$ split into concentrate $C \ge 0$ and tailings $T \ge 0$ with $F = C + T$, and let the grades
$f, c, t \in [0, 1]$ satisfy the component balance $F f = C c + T t$. Then $f$ lies between $t$ and $c$, and if $c \ne t$ the yield is
$C/F = (f - t)/(c - t)$, determined by the assays alone; the recovery of the valuable component, $C c/(F f) = c(f - t)/(f(c - t))$, is
defined when $f > 0$. If $c = t$, then $f = c = t$ and the split is not determined by the assays. Grade and recovery are different
quantities. Flotation kinetics are outside this result.
