# Two-product formula

Let a feed of dry mass flow $F > 0$ split into concentrate $C \ge 0$ and tailings $T \ge 0$ with $F = C + T$, and let the grades
$f, c, t \in [0, 1]$ satisfy the component balance $F f = C c + T t$. Then $f$ lies between $t$ and $c$, and if $c \ne t$, the yield
$C/F = (f - t)/(c - t)$ is determined by the assays alone. The component recovery $C c/(F f)$ is defined when $f > 0$;
if also $c \ne t$, it equals $c(f - t)/(f(c - t))$.

If $c = t$, then $f = c = t$, and every pair $(C', T') = (x, F-x)$ with $0 \le x \le F$ satisfies both balances. Thus the
assays do not determine the split in this case. Grade and recovery are different quantities. Flotation kinetics are outside
this result.
