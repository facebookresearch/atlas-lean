# Rescaling a stream's flows by a nonnegative factor

For a [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and a factor `0 ≤ α`, the
rescaled stream has flow rates `α * ṅ i`. The factor is required to be nonnegative, so that the
rescaled flows are nonnegative and form a stream; `α = 0` gives the [zero stream](zero.md). A change
of the amount unit or of the time unit multiplies every flow rate by one positive factor.
