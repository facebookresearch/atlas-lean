# Mole fractions of every stream are nonnegative

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and every species `i`, the
[mole fraction](../amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mole-fraction.md) `ṅ i / ∑ j, ṅ j` is
nonnegative, since the stream's flows satisfy `0 ≤ ṅ j`. No positive total is assumed: for a zero
total flow, the convention `x / 0 = 0` makes every mole fraction `0`.
