# Mass-flow rates of a stream from molar masses

For a [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and a real vector `M` of molar
masses, the mass-flow rate of species `i` is `ṁ i = ṅ i * M i`. The definition allows every real
`M`: since `0 ≤ ṅ i`, with the positive molar masses of the physical reading the mass flows are
nonnegative, while a negative `M i` gives `ṁ i ≤ 0`.
