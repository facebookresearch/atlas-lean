# Total accumulated amount is the duration times the total flow

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and every real duration
`Δt`, the [accumulated amounts](integrate.md) add up to the duration times the
[total flow](total.md): `∑ i, Δt * ṅ i = Δt * ∑ i, ṅ i`. No sign condition on `Δt` is needed; the
physical reading has `0 ≤ Δt`.
