# Positive molar masses and total flow give positive total mass flow

For a [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i`, molar masses `0 < M i` for
every species `i` and positive [total flow](total.md), `0 < ∑ i, ṅ i`, the total
[mass flow](mass-flow.md) is positive: `0 < ∑ i, ṅ i * M i`. The stream's constraint `0 ≤ ṅ i` is
used: some species has `0 < ṅ i`, and no term of the sum is negative.
