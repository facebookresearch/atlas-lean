# Positive molar masses and total flow give positive total mass flow

For a [process stream](stream-flow.md) with finitely many species, whose species flow rates `ṅ i`
are nonnegative by definition, positive molar masses, `0 < M i` for all `i`, and a positive
[total flow](total.md), `0 < ∑ i, ṅ i`, give a positive total [mass flow](mass-flow.md)
`∑ i, ṅ i * M i`.

The other form has its own page: [For nᵢ ≥ 0 with ∑ nᵢ > 0 and Mᵢ > 0, the total mass is positive](../amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/sum-amount-mul-molar-mass-pos.md).
