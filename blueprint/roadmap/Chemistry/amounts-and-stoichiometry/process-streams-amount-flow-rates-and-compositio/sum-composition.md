# The composition of a stream sums to one

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i`, if the
[composition](composition.md) is defined and equal to `x`, then `∑ i, x i = 1`. The composition is
defined only for a positive total flow, `0 < ∑ j, ṅ j`, and is then `x i = ṅ i / ∑ j, ṅ j`, whose
entries sum to `1`
([mole fractions sum to one](../amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/sum-mole-fraction.md)). A stream
with zero total flow has no composition, and the statement says nothing about it.
