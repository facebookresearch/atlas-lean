# Without positive total mass flow, a stream has no mass composition

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and every real vector `M`
of molar masses, of any signs, if the total [mass flow](mass-flow.md) is not positive,
`∑ i, ṅ i * M i ≤ 0`, then the [mass composition](mass-composition.md) of the stream is undefined.
The stream's constraint `0 ≤ ṅ i` is used: with positive molar masses and positive total flow the
total mass flow is positive.
