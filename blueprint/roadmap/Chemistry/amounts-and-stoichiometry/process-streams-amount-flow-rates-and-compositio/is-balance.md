# Species balance of a process unit

Inlet and outlet streams with nonnegative species flow rates $\dot n^{in}_i$, $\dot n^{out}_i$, together with signed net generation rates $g_i$ and accumulation rates $a_i$, form a species balance when $\dot n^{in}_i - \dot n^{out}_i + g_i = a_i$ for every species $i$.
