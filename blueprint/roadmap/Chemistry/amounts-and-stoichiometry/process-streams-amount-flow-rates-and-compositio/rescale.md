# Rescaling a process stream

For a process stream with species flow rates $\dot n_i$ and a factor $\alpha \ge 0$, the rescaled stream has flow rates $\alpha\,\dot n_i$. For $\alpha > 0$ this is the effect of a change of amount or time unit; $\alpha = 0$ is allowed but sends every stream to the zero stream, which no (invertible) change of units does.
