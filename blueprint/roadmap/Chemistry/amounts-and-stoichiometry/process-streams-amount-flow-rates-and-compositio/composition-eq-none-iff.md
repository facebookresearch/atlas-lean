# A stream has no composition exactly when its total flow is zero

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i`, the
[composition](composition.md) is undefined if and only if the [total flow](total.md) `∑ i, ṅ i` is
`0`. The composition is undefined whenever the total is not positive, and the total is never
negative because the flows are nonnegative, by the stream's constraint.
