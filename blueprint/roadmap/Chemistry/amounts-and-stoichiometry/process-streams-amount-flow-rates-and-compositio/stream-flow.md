# Process stream with nonnegative species flow rates

A process stream over a finite species type `ι`, possibly empty, is a family of real species flow
rates `ṅ i`, one for each species `i`, with the constraint `0 ≤ ṅ i` for every `i`. The flows may
all be `0`, as in the [zero stream](zero.md). They are read as amount-flow rates, in moles per unit
of time after a consistent choice of units; only the real numbers are recorded, and units and
dimensions are not tracked.
