# A nonpositive molar mass leaves a stream without mass composition

For every [process stream](stream-flow.md) with species flow rates `ṅ i` and every real vector `M`
of molar masses, if some species `i` has `M i ≤ 0`, then the [mass composition](mass-composition.md)
of the stream is undefined, whatever the flows, even when `ṅ i = 0` or the total flow is positive.
