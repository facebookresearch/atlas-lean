# Arrhenius identity in base-10 logarithms

Let `y`, `y_ref` and `A` be quantities of one dimension, `E` a molar energy, `R` a gas constant and
`T` an absolute temperature, all with positive values, and suppose `y = A exp(−E / (R T))`. Then
`log(y / y_ref) / log 10 = log(A / y_ref) / log 10 − E / (R T log 10)`, that is,
`log₁₀(y / y_ref) = log₁₀(A / y_ref) − E / (R T ln 10)`. The dimensions are carried by the types:
logarithms are taken only of ratios of quantities of the same dimension, and `E / (R T)` is
dimensionless.
