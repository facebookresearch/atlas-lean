# Weight and atomic percent conversions are mutually inverse

Let `ι` be a finite nonempty set of components with molar masses `M_i > 0`. Then:

- for every `w : ι → ℝ` with all `w_i > 0` and `∑_i w_i = 100`, the [weight percent](wt-pct.md) of
  the [mole fractions](../amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mole-fraction-of-mass-fraction.md)
  computed from the mass fractions `w / 100` is `w`;
- for every `x : ι → ℝ` with all `x_i > 0` and `∑_i x_i = 1`, the [atomic percent](at-pct.md) of the
  mole fractions computed from the [mass fractions](../amount-of-substance-and-molar-mass/mass-fraction.md)
  of `x` equals the atomic percent of `x`.
