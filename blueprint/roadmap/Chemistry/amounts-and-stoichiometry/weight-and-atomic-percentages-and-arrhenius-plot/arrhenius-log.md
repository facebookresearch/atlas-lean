# Arrhenius identity in natural logarithms

Let `y`, `y_ref` and `A` be quantities of one dimension, `E` a molar energy, `R` a gas constant and
`T` an absolute temperature, with `y`, `y_ref`, `A`, `R` and `T` positive and `E` of any sign, and
suppose `y = A exp(−E / (R T))`. Then `log(y / y_ref) = log(A / y_ref) − E / (R T)`. The dimensions
are carried by the types: logarithms are taken only of ratios of quantities of the same dimension,
and `E / (R T)` is dimensionless. No parameter value is asserted.
