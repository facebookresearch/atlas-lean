# The gradient entries are the partial derivatives

For a nonzero slope `b`, the entries `1 / b`, `-1 / b` and `-(y₀ - a) / b ^ 2` of the
[gradient of inverse prediction](inverse-prediction-gradient.md) are the partial derivatives of
`x₀ = (y₀ - a) / b` with respect to `y₀`, `a` and `b`: each one-variable slice has the
corresponding entry as its derivative at the point.
