# Independent drifts scale the internal-standard ratio

With drift factors `d₁` on the analyte and `d₂ ≠ 0` on the standard, and a standard response
`y_s ≠ 0`, the drifted ratio is the drift ratio times the undrifted ratio:
`(d₁ * y_a) / (d₂ * y_s) = (d₁ / d₂) * (y_a / y_s)`. Unequal drifts therefore change a nonzero
ratio ([unequal drifts do not cancel](internal-standard-ratio-independent-drif-2.md)).
