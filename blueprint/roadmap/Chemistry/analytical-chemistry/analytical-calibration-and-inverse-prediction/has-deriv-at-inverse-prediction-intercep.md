# Partial derivative of inverse prediction in the intercept

Partial derivative of inverse prediction with respect to the fitted intercept: for every slope
`b`, the map `t ↦ (y₀ - t) / b` has derivative `-1 / b` at `t = a`. At `b = 0` the map is
constantly `0` and `-1 / b = 0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
