# Dixon's Q statistic

Dixon's Q statistic for observation `j` of the sample `x`: the
[gap to the nearest other observation](nearest-gap.md) over the [sample range](sample-range.md),
and `0` when the range is `0` (no spread, hence no rejection). Dixon's procedure applies it to an
extreme observation of a sample of at least three observations (its
[rejection rule](dixon-reject.md) requires both); for two distinct observations `Q = 1` whatever
the data. Tabulated critical values, and any error-rate meaning of the comparison, assume i.i.d.
normal sampling; the statistic itself assumes nothing about the distribution.
