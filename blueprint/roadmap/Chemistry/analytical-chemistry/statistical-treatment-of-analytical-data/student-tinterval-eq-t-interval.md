# Agreement with the random-variable t-interval

For a sequence of random variables `X` and a critical value `q`, the t-interval of its first `n`
terms at `ω` is the [t-interval](t-interval.md) computed from the sample mean and the
[sample standard deviation](sample-std-dev.md) of `X 0 ω, …, X (n - 1) ω`. For an i.i.d. normal
sample of size `n ≥ 2` with nonzero variance and `0 < α < 1`, with the upper `α / 2` point of
Student's t law on `n - 1` degrees of freedom as critical value, its coverage is
[exactly `1 - α`](t-interval-coverage.md).
