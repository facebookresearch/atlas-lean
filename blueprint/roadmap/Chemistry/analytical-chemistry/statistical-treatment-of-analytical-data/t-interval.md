# Student t-interval for a mean

The t-interval `x̄ ± tcrit * s / √n` as a set, the closed interval
`[x̄ - tcrit * s / √n, x̄ + tcrit * s / √n]`; the definition involves no probability. Its exact
coverage under i.i.d. normal sampling of `n ≥ 2` observations, with the sample mean, the sample
standard deviation and the Student-t quantile, is
[the exact coverage of the two-sided t-interval](t-interval-coverage.md).
