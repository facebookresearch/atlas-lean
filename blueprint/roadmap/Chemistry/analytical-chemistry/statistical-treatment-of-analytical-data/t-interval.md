# Student t-interval for a mean

The t-interval `x̄ ± tcrit * s / √n` as a set, the closed interval
`[x̄ - tcrit * s / √n, x̄ + tcrit * s / √n]`; the definition involves no probability. For `n = 0`
the convention `x / 0 = 0` makes the half-width `0`, so the interval is the single point `x̄`;
the intended domain is `n ≥ 1`. Its exact coverage under i.i.d. normal sampling of `n ≥ 2`
observations, with the sample mean, the sample standard deviation and the Student-t quantile, is
[the exact coverage of the two-sided t-interval](t-interval-coverage.md).
