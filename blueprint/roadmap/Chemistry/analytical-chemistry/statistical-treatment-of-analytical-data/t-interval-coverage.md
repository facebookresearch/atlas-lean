# Exact coverage of the two-sided t-interval

For an i.i.d. `N(μ, v)` sample `X` of size `n ≥ 2` with `v ≠ 0` and `α ∈ (0, 1)`, let `t` be the
`(1 - α/2)`-quantile of Student's t law with `n - 1` degrees of freedom, the upper `α/2` point.
The [t-interval](t-interval.md) `x̄ ± t * s / √n` built from the sample mean `x̄` and the
[sample standard deviation](sample-std-dev.md) `s` covers `μ` with probability exactly `1 - α`.
