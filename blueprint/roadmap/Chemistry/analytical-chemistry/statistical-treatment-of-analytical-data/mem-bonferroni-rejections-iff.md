# Bonferroni rejects exactly the hypotheses with p ≤ α/m

The [Bonferroni procedure](../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/bonferroni-correction/bonferroni-rejections.md) at level `α` rejects
exactly the hypotheses whose p-value is at most the [Bonferroni level](bonferroni-level.md)
`α/m`. For valid p-values and `α ∈ [0, 1]` it
[controls the family-wise error rate](../../../Probability/hypothesis-testing/bonferroni-correction/measure-bonferroni-familywise-error-le.md)
at level `α`.
