# Bound on the mass defect from per-atom bounds

If every per-atom mass defect is bounded in absolute value, `|m i - A i| ≤ δ i` for `i ∈ s`, then
the absolute difference of exact and nominal mass is at most the sum of the bounds:
`|exact mass - nominal mass| ≤ ∑ i ∈ s, δ i`. The bounds `δ` are hypotheses.
