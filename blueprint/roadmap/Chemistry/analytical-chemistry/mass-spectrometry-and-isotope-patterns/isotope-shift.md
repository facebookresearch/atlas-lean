# Nominal mass shift of an isotope

The physical nominal mass shift of an isotope `j ∈ iso`, for a nonempty isotope set `iso`: its
nucleon number `A j` minus the [least nucleon number](isotope-min.md) `A_min` over the set, `A j - A_min`, a subtraction of natural
numbers truncated at `0`, which is exact for `j ∈ iso`.
