# Nominal mass shift of an isotope

The nominal mass shift `A j - A_min` of an index `j` relative to a nonempty isotope set `iso`:
the nucleon number `A j` minus the [least nucleon number](isotope-min.md) `A_min` over the set.
It is defined for every index `j`, as a subtraction of natural numbers truncated at `0`, so it is
`0` when `A j < A_min`. Its physical use is on isotopes `j ∈ iso`, where `A_min ≤ A j` and the
subtraction is exact.
