# Exact (M+1)/M ratio for n carbon atoms

The exact `(M+1)/M` ratio of `n` carbon atoms whose heavy isotope ¹³C has fractional
abundance `p`. It is the ratio of the `X ^ 1` and `X ^ 0` coefficients of `((1 - p) + p X) ^ n`.
Only the carbon atoms are modeled. It is defined when `n = 0` or `p ≠ 1`: at `p = 1` the
monoisotopic coefficient is zero exactly when `n > 0`. With no carbon atoms the denominator is
one and the ratio is zero, including at `p = 1`.
