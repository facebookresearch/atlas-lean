# Nitrogen rule for bond graphs at common valences

The nitrogen rule for a [bond graph](bond-graph.md) whose atoms carry the nine
[elements of the model](element.md), at their model nucleon numbers and
[common valences](common-valences.md); neutrality is not part of the statement.
If every atom is bonded at a common valence of its element, the nominal mass is odd exactly when
the number of nitrogen atoms is odd. The valence balance is not assumed: it follows from
[the valence sum](sum-valence.md). A bond graph records only elements and bond orders, so this
says nothing about charge or unpaired electrons: a charged or radical species with the same bond
graph is not excluded.
