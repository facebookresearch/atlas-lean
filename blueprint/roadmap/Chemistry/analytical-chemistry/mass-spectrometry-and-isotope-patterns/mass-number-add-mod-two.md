# Only nitrogen has odd nucleon number plus valence

For each of the nine [elements of the model](element.md) (C, H, N, O, S, F, Cl, Br, I), at its model
nucleon number and a [common valence](common-valences.md) `v`, the sum of the nucleon number and `v`
is odd exactly for nitrogen: for the eight other elements the nucleon number and the valence have
the same parity.
