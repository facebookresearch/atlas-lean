# Nucleon number as least nucleon number plus shift

In a nonempty isotope set, the nucleon number of every isotope `j` of the set is the least nucleon
number `A_min` plus its shift: `A j = A_min + (A j - A_min)`. The truncated subtraction in the
[shift](isotope-shift.md) loses nothing, since
[the least nucleon number is a lower bound](isotope-min-le.md): `A_min ≤ A j`.
