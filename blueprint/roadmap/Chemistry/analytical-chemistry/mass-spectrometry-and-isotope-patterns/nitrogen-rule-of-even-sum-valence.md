# Nitrogen rule from an even valence sum

The nitrogen rule from the valence-balance hypothesis, for atoms of the nine
[elements of the model](element.md) at their model nucleon numbers. Suppose every atom `i ∈ s` has a
[common valence](common-valences.md) `v i` of its element, and the valences have an even sum. Then the nominal mass
is odd exactly when the number of nitrogen atoms is odd.
