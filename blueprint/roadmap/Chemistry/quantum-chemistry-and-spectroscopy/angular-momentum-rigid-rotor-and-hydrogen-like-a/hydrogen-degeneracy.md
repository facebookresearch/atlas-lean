# Hydrogen-like levels are n²-fold degenerate

Hydrogen degeneracy: for every principal quantum number `n ≥ 1`,
`∑_{l=0}^{n-1} (2 * l + 1) = n ^ 2`. The level `n` has orbital quantum numbers `l < n`, each with
`2 * l + 1` magnetic sublevels, so `n ^ 2` orbital states; the identity says that the first `n` odd
numbers sum to `n ^ 2`.
