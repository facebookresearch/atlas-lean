# Three-dimensional Bohr levels are the hydrogen-like levels with Z = 1

Let a particle of mass `m > 0` move in `ℝ^d` in the Coulomb potential `V(x) = -k / ‖x‖`, with
Rydberg energy `Ry = m * k ^ 2 / (2 * ħ ^ 2)` and Bohr levels `E_n = -Ry / (n + (d - 1) / 2) ^ 2`
for `n = 0, 1, 2, …`. For `d = 3`, `E_n = -Ry / (n + 1) ^ 2` is the hydrogen-like level
`-(Z ^ 2 * R) / (n + 1) ^ 2` with principal quantum number `n + 1`, nuclear charge `Z = 1` and
Rydberg energy `R = Ry`: the nuclear charge is part of the coupling `k`.
