# Continuum Coulomb promises and verifier model

For $n\ge1$ electrons and at least one fixed nucleus, take pairwise distinct rational positions $R_a\in\mathbb Q^3$ and integer charges $Z_a\ge1$. A state has one complex $H^1((\mathbb R^3)^n)$ wave function $\psi_s$ per spin string $s\in\{0,1\}^n$, with spatial weak derivatives tested against real smooth compactly supported functions. Permuting electron positions and their spin labels together changes the state by the sign of the permutation, almost everywhere for each spin string. Normalize by $\sum_s\int |\psi_s|^2=1$. The form is

$$
\mathcal E(\psi)=\sum_s\int \left[\frac12\!\sum_i|\nabla_i\psi_s|^2+
  \left(-\sum_{i,a}\frac{Z_a}{|x_i-R_a|}+\sum_{i<j}\frac1{|x_i-x_j|}\right)|\psi_s|^2\right]dx.
$$

At each collision the source's reciprocal kernel is totalized to zero; the diagonals have Lebesgue measure zero. The source evaluates the kinetic, nuclear attraction and pair repulsion integrals **separately**, using the real Bochner integral (whose value is zero on nonintegrable inputs). The displayed combined integral agrees with that convention on states where the terms are integrable. The energy $E$ is the infimum, in the extended reals, over these normalized states including all spin sectors. There is no nuclear repulsion term in this electronic form and no finite orbital basis. Finiteness of the individual integrals on the stated form domain is the bridge to the physical energy.

An output instance gives the nuclear list, positive electron count and two rational thresholds $a,b$ with $b-a\ge1$. A rational is encoded by a signed binary numerator and a positive binary denominator (not necessarily reduced); distinctness concerns the rational values, not their encodings. A natural binary digit string is encoded by a marker $1$ before each least-significant-first digit and a terminating $0$; the signed coding tags nonnegative $m$ and negative $-m-1$ separately. Lists have a binary length code followed by their entries. The electron count uses unary $1^n0$. Fields concatenate in their declared order: nuclear list (positions and, in the binary-charge model, charges), electron count, lower and upper thresholds. The yes side is $E\le a$ and the no side $E\ge b$, both only on valid inputs.

The source promise class has disjoint yes and no subsets of bit strings. Its verifier generator returns integers $r,w\ge0$ and an ordered gate list, with $w\le r$. It uses exactly $r+1$ wires, indexed from $0$ to $r$. Gates are Hadamard, $T=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\pi/4})$, and CNOT on distinct wires, all with valid indices. The witness occupies the first $w$ wires, every remaining wire starts in zero, and readout accepts exactly when wire $r$ is one. That wire is outside the witness and starts in zero even when $w=r$. Acceptance is the sum of squared moduli of the final amplitudes with an accepting readout. One natural-coefficient polynomial bounds $r$ plus the gate-list length on all inputs of a given bit length. A yes input has some unit witness of acceptance at least $2/3$; on a no input every unit witness has acceptance at most $1/3$.

For both verifier generation and the many-one reduction, the source requires the [pinned `Turing.TM2ComputableInPolyTime` certificate](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/Computability/TuringMachine/Computable.lean#L46-L188) for the indicated input and output encodings. It consists of a deterministic bundled stack machine, with finitely many stacks, instruction labels and internal states, designated input and output stacks with alphabets identified with the encoded input/output symbols, and a polynomial on natural numbers. On **every** encoded input the machine halts with the required encoded output within that polynomial in the encoded input length. The type does not require all auxiliary stack alphabets to be finite. A comparison with a classical finite-alphabet multitape model therefore needs a separate simulation preserving the encodings and polynomial time. The reduction's map carries yes inputs to yes outputs and no inputs to no outputs; there is no classification requirement off promise.

## Expansion

Identify the continuum Sobolev form and prove integrability at the Coulomb diagonals on the weak-derivative domain. Formalize the valid output codecs and show that they have the stated size accounting. Compare the pinned verifier with any canonical quantum-circuit model gate by gate, and prove preservation of the acceptance probabilities and polynomial-time generation before replacing the source promise class.
