# The 3N − 6 rule for nonlinear molecules

A noncollinear configuration of `N` nuclei with positive masses has `3 * N - 6` internal modes
(kinematic count). Since `3 ≤ N` holds automatically, the subtraction is not truncated; the additive
form is [`d + 6 = 3 * N`](finrank-internal-mode-space-add-six.md).
