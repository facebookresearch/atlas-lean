# Rigid rotations transform like an axial vector

Rigid rotations transform like an axial vector (`Rx, Ry, Rz`): the displacement representation maps
the rigid rotation with angular velocity `ω` to the one with angular velocity `det (R g) • R g ω`,
provided `R g (x i - c) = x (π g i) - c` for all group elements `g` and nuclei `i`, where `c` is the
centre of mass.
