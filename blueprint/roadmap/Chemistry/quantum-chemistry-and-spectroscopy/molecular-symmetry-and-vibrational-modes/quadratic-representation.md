# Quadratic-form representation of a point group

The symmetric-square (quadratic-form) representation `S ↦ A * S * Aᵀ` on symmetric matrices. For
orthogonal `A` this is the [action on quadratic forms](quadratic-form-of-tensor-square-represen.md)
`r ↦ rᵀ S r` and the transformation law of a polarizability tensor.
