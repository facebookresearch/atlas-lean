# IR activity in the dipole model

A vibrational mode transforming as the irreducible representation `σ` (an irreducible component of
the internal-mode representation) is IR-active iff `σ` occurs in the vector representation (with
positive multiplicity), i.e. transforms like `x`, `y` or `z`. This is a definition inside the dipole
model; it does not predict intensities or observation, and labels of `σ` refer to the fixed axis
convention of `R`. Activity is defined only for irreducible `σ`. For a vibrational mode, `σ` must
moreover occur in the complexified internal-mode representation.
