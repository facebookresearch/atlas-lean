# The 3N − 5 rule for linear molecules

A linear configuration of `N` nuclei with positive masses (collinear with two distinct positions, so
the affine span is a line and `2 ≤ N`) has `3 * N - 5` internal modes (kinematic count). As `N ≥ 2`,
the subtraction is not truncated; the additive form is
[`d + 5 = 3 * N`](finrank-internal-mode-space-add-five.md).
