# Internal-mode representation of a point group

Let a point group act on Cartesian space by orthogonal matrices `R g` and permute the `N` nuclei by
`π g`, mapping the configuration to itself about its centre of mass `c`:
`R g (x i - c) = x (π g i) - c` for all `g` and `i`. The internal-mode representation is the
displacement representation on the quotient of the displacement space by the rigid translations and
rotations, a subspace that this condition makes invariant. For positive masses the quotient has
dimension `3 * N - 6` if the configuration is noncollinear, and `3 * N - 5` if it is collinear with
two distinct positions.
