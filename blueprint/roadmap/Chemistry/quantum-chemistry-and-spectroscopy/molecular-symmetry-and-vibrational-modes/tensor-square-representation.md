# Tensor square of the vector representation

The tensor square of the vector representation on `3 × 3` complex matrices, `M ↦ A * M * Aᵀ` with
`A` the Cartesian matrix of the group element, under the identification `v ⊗ w ↦ v * wᵀ` (see
[the action on pure tensors](tensor-square-representation-vec-mul-vec.md)).
