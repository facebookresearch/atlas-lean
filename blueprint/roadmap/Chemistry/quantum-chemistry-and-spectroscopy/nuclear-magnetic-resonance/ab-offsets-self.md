# Transition offsets in the A₂ limit of equal shifts

Equivalent shifts (`A₂`): at `νA = νB = ν0` with `J ≥ 0` the four candidate offsets are `ν0 - J`,
`ν0`, `ν0` and `ν0 + J`. For `J > 0` the outer two involve the lower mixed state, which is then the
antisymmetric singlet `αβ - βα`; their transition moments vanish, so the observed spectrum is the
single line `ν0`, not four lines (a remark beyond the statement). For `J < 0` the two mixed states
exchange roles.
