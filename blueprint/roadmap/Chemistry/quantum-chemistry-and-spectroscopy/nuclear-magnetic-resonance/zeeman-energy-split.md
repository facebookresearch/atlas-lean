# Zeeman splitting of a spin-1/2 nucleus

Energy splitting between `m = -1/2` and `m = +1/2`, for `γ > 0`, `hbar > 0` and `B0 > 0`:
`ΔE = γ * hbar * B0`. `γ > 0` fixes the sign convention: for nuclei with negative `γ` the level
ordering flips, the same formula gives a signed frequency, and the observed frequency is its
absolute value.
