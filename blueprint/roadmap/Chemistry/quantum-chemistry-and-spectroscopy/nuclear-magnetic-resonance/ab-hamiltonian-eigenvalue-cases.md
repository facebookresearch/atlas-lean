# Every AB eigenvalue is one of the four energies

Every eigenvalue of `H` is one of the four energies `E_αα = -(νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4`,
`E₊ = -J / 4 + √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2`, `E₋ = -J / 4 - √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2` and
`E_ββ = (νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4`: an eigenvector with a nonzero `αα` or `ββ` component forces `E_αα`
or `E_ββ`; otherwise it lies in the mixed subspace, where `E` solves
`(E + J / 4) ^ 2 = ((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 4`.
