# Maximum multiplicity is attained iff sums are injective

Let each group `i` of equivalent nuclei have exactly `n i * s i + 1` lines (`n i` nuclei, `s i`
twice their spin). The number of distinct combined line positions equals the maximum multiplicity
`∏ i, (n i * s i + 1)` exactly when the sum map, which sends a choice of one line per group to the
sum of the chosen positions, is injective.
