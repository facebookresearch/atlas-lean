# Signed offset from αα to the upper mixed state

Signed transition offset (Hz) of the candidate transition from `αα` to the upper mixed state:
`E₊ - E_αα`, with `E₊ = -J / 4 + √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2` and `E_αα = -(νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4`.
It can be negative; its absolute value is the [line frequency](ab-gap-alpha-plus.md).
