# Weak-coupling limit of the AB line frequencies

Weak-coupling limit of the AB line frequencies: when both resonance frequencies exceed `|J|` and
`νA ≠ νB`, each line frequency lies within `J ^ 2 / (4 |νA - νB|)` of its first-order position: the
line between the lower mixed state and `ββ` of `max νA νB + J / 2`, the line between `αα` and the
upper mixed state of `max νA νB - J / 2`, the line between the upper mixed state and `ββ` of
`min νA νB + J / 2`, and the line between `αα` and the lower mixed state of `min νA νB - J / 2` (as
for the [offsets](ab-offsets-weak-coupling.md)).
