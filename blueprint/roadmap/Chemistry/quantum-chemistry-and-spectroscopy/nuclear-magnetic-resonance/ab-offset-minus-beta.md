# Signed offset from the lower mixed state to ββ

Signed transition offset (Hz) of the candidate transition from the lower mixed state to `ββ`:
`E_ββ - E₋`, with `E_ββ = (νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4` and `E₋ = -J / 4 - √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2`.
It can be negative; its absolute value is the [line frequency](ab-gap-minus-beta.md).
