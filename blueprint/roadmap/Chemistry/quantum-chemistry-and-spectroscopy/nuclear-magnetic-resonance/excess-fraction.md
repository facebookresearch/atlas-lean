# Population excess fraction of a two-level system

The population excess fraction `(e^x - 1) / (e^x + 1)` of a two-level system whose populations are
in the Boltzmann ratio `Nα / Nβ = e^x` (see
[Boltzmann populations](excess-fraction-eq-of-populations.md)). It is signed: the excess
`(Nα - Nβ) / (Nα + Nβ)` of `α` over `β`, negative when `x < 0`. For spin-1/2 nuclei in a field `B0`
at temperature `T`, `x = γ * hbar * B0 / (kB * T)`, with all parameters explicit reals; the
Boltzmann ratio is the model assumption.
