# The four energy levels of the AB system

The energy levels of the AB system: `E` is an eigenvalue of the two-spin Hamiltonian `H` iff it is
one of `E_αα = -(νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4`, `E₊ = -J / 4 + √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2`,
`E₋ = -J / 4 - √((νA - νB) ^ 2 + J ^ 2) / 2` and `E_ββ = (νA + νB) / 2 + J / 4`.
