# Integral ratios equal nucleus ratios under full relaxation

Integrals are proportional to nucleus count: if `ii = C * Ni` and `ij = C * Nj` for a common `C ≠ 0`
(full-relaxation model hypothesis, `C` a real parameter) and `Nj ≠ 0`, then `ii / ij = Ni / Nj`.
Proportionality holds _under full relaxation_; relaxation-weighted integrals are outside this model.
