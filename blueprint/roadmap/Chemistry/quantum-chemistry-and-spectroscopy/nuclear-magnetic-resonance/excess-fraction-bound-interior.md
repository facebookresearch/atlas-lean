# First-order bound on the excess fraction for −1 < x < 1

First-order bound on the open interval: for `-1 < x < 1`, the excess fraction
`ε(x) = (e^x - 1) / (e^x + 1)` satisfies `|ε(x) - x / 2| ≤ x ^ 2`, from the
[lower](excess-fraction-lower.md) and [upper](excess-fraction-upper.md) sandwich bounds.
