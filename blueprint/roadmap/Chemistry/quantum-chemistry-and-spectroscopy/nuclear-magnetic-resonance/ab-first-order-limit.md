# Square-root estimate for the first-order AB limit

First-order limit: for `Δν ≠ 0`, `|√(Δν ^ 2 + J ^ 2) - |Δν|| ≤ J ^ 2 / (2 * |Δν|)`. With
`Δν = νA - νB` this bounds how far the AB transition offsets are from their first-order positions
(see [the weak-coupling limit](ab-offsets-weak-coupling.md)).
