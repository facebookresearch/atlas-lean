# Resolved line count is at most the maximum multiplicity

Resolved-line bound: if each group `i` has exactly `n i * s i + 1` lines (`n i` equivalent nuclei,
`s i` twice their spin), the number of distinct combined positions is at most the maximum
multiplicity `∏ i, (n i * s i + 1)`. Coincident shifts/degeneracies reduce the resolved count; the
product is the _maximum_ resolved multiplicity.
