# On even cycles the flip reflects energies about α

On an even cycle, the eigen-equation `H v = E v` for the Hückel matrix `H` gives
`H (flip v) = (2 * α - E) (flip v)`, where `(flip v) j = (-1) ^ j * v j`. This action identity holds
for every `v`, including `v = 0`; the eigenvalue pairing, with nonzero vectors, is the
[pairing of Hückel energies](huckel-cycle-pairing.md).
