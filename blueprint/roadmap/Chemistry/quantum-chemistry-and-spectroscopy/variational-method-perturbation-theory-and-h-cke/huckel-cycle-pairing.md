# Pairing of Hückel energies on even cycles

Pairing of Hückel energies on even cycles (Coulson–Rushbrooke): on a cycle with an even number of
vertices, if `v ≠ 0` is an eigenvector of the Hückel matrix with eigenvalue `E`, then its
alternating-sign flip `(flip v) j = (-1) ^ j * v j` is a nonzero eigenvector with eigenvalue
`2 * α - E`.
