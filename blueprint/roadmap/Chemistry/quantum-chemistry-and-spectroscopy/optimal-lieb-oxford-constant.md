# Optimal Lieb–Oxford constant

Find the sharp universal lower bound on indirect Coulomb energy in terms of $\int \rho^{4/3}$. This is an exact constraint relevant to density-functional approximations.

## What would settle it

Define the Coulomb expectation, Hartree self-energy, admissible N-particle distributions, and density. Prove the optimal constant C in $E_{\mathrm{ind}} \ge -C \int \rho^{4/3}$, including matching sharpness. The proposed equality to the uniform-electron-gas constant requires that thermodynamic constant to be independently defined, not defined as the answer.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Lewin, Lieb and Seiringer prove that the best constant is at most about 1.58 and record that it is at least 1.44; they cite the conjecture that the best constant equals the negative of the uniform-electron-gas energy constant.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
