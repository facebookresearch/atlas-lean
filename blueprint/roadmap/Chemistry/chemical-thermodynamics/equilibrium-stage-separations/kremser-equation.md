# Kremser equation

Consider a dilute absorber with $N \ge 1$ equilibrium stages, constant liquid and gas flows $L, V > 0$, linear equilibrium
$y = m x$ with $m > 0$ and solute-free entering liquid, and let $A = L/(mV)$ be the absorption factor. The fraction of the
entering solute that is absorbed, when the entering solute flow is positive, is

$$\frac{A^{N+1} - A}{A^{N+1} - 1} \quad (A \ne 1), \qquad \frac{N}{N + 1} \quad (A = 1).$$

For zero entering solute, all solute flows are zero: the displayed number is the linear transfer coefficient, not a ratio of two zero flows.

The value at $A = 1$ is the limit of the rational expression; the fraction lies in $[0, 1]$. The proof solves the linear
recurrence that the stage balances and the equilibrium relation give for the liquid compositions, which is a geometric sum.
Nonlinear equilibrium and particular equipment are outside this result.
