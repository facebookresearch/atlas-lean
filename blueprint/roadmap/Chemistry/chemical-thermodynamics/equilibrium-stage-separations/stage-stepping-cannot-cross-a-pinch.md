# Stage stepping cannot cross a pinch

Consider the rectifying section of a binary distillation column with constant molar overflow, relative volatility $\alpha > 1$,
reflux ratio $R > 0$ and distillate composition $0 < x_D < 1$. Let $y_{\mathrm{eq}}(x) = \alpha x/(1 + (\alpha - 1)x)$ be the
equilibrium curve, $\ell(x) = (R x + x_D)/(R + 1)$ the operating line, and $T(x) = y_{\mathrm{eq}}^{-1}(\ell(x))$ the map from the
liquid composition on one stage to that on the next. Let $0 \le x_p < x_0 \le 1$, and suppose that $T(x_p) = x_p$, that $T$ is
strictly increasing on $[x_p, x_0]$, and that $\ell(x) < y_{\mathrm{eq}}(x)$ for $x_p < x \le x_0$. Then $x_p < T(x) < x$ for
$x_p < x \le x_0$, so the stage compositions $x_0, T(x_0), T^2(x_0), \dots$ decrease strictly and stay above $x_p$: no finite number
of stages reaches or crosses the pinch $x_p$. For $\alpha = 2$, $R = 1$ and $x_D = 3/4$, the start
$x_0 = y_{\mathrm{eq}}^{-1}(x_D) = 3/5$ lies above $x_p = (9 - \sqrt{33})/8$. Minimum-reflux formulas and energy balances are
outside this result.
