# External balance of a countercurrent cascade

In a finite nonempty chain of stages $1, \dots, N$ with positive carrier flows, where the liquid passes from stage $n - 1$ to stage
$n$ and the vapour from stage $n + 1$ to stage $n$, the steady component balances of the stages,
$L_{n-1} x_{n-1} + V_{n+1} y_{n+1} = L_n x_n + V_n y_n$, add up to the balance of the two entering and two leaving streams,
$L_0 x_0 + V_{N+1} y_{N+1} = L_N x_N + V_1 y_1$, because every interstage stream enters one balance and leaves another. No
equilibrium relation is used.
