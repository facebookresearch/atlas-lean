# Equal effusion rates exactly when T/M agree

Equal effusion rates at fixed number density: for `R`, `T₁`, `T₂`, `M₁`, `M₂ > 0`, under the
effusion model for each gas with its own factor, `rate₁ = k₁ * ⟨v⟩(T₁, M₁)` and
`rate₂ = k₂ * ⟨v⟩(T₂, M₂)`, and with equal factors `k₁ = k₂ > 0`,
`rate₁ = rate₂ ↔ T₁ / M₁ = T₂ / M₂`; equal rates need the temperature ratio to compensate the
molar-mass ratio. Equal factors mean the same hole, the same number density and molecular flow: `k`
combines the hole area, the number density and the flow regime, which the model does not separate,
and is held fixed as the temperature changes. This is not a fixed-pressure criterion: at fixed
pressure the number density, and hence `k`, would vary as `1 / T`, and equal fixed-pressure rates
would instead require `M₁ * T₁ = M₂ * T₂`.
