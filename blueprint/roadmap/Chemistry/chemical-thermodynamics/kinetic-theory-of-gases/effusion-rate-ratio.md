# Graham's law of effusion

Graham's law of effusion: for `R, T, M₁, M₂ > 0`, two gases at the same temperature `T`, with rates
proportional to their mean speeds with their own factors (model hypothesis),
`rate₁ = k₁ * ⟨v⟩(T, M₁)` and `rate₂ = k₂ * ⟨v⟩(T, M₂)`, and with equal factors `k₁ = k₂ > 0`,
satisfy `rate₁ / rate₂ = √(M₂ / M₁)`. Equal factors mean the same hole, the same number density and
molecular flow; the model does not separate `k` into these components. At equal temperature, equal
number density is also equal pressure, so this is also the fixed-pressure Graham's law at equal `T`.
