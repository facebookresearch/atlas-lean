# Most probable speed

Most probable speed `v_mp = √(2 * R * T / M)`. It is defined for all real `R`, `T` and `M`: it is
`0` for `M = 0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`, and for `R T / M < 0`, since `√x = 0` for `x < 0`.
The intended domain is `R, T, M > 0`. That it is the unique maximizer of the
Maxwell–Boltzmann density on `[0, ∞)` is a [separate result](is-max-on-mb-speed-iff.md), not part of
the definition.
