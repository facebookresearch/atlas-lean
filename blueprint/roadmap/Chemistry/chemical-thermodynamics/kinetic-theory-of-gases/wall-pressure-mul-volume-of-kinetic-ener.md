# Kinetic pressure law yields the ideal-gas law

If, besides the hypotheses of the [elastic-wall pressure law](wall-pressure-mul-volume.md), the mean
kinetic energy is `Ek = (1 / 2) * m * v2` and `Ek = (3 / 2) * kB * T` (a modelling identification,
assumed rather than derived), then `p * V = N * kB * T`; `kB > 0` and `T > 0` record the physical
domain.
