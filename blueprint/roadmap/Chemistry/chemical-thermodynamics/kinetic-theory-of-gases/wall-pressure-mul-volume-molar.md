# Molar ideal-gas law from kinetic theory

Molar form: under the hypotheses of the [kinetic derivation of the ideal-gas
law](wall-pressure-mul-volume-of-kinetic-ener.md), that is, the elastic-wall model of `N` particles
of mass `m` in a cube of volume `V` with isotropic velocities,
`Ek = (1 / 2) * m * v2 = (3 / 2) * kB * T` and `kB, T > 0`, if moreover `N = n * NA` and
`R = NA * kB`, then `p * V = n * R * T`.
