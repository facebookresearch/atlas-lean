# Effusion rate at fixed number density scales as √(T/M)

Dependence of the effusion rate on `T` and `M`: for `R, T, M > 0`, under the model hypothesis
`rate = k * ⟨v⟩`, `rate = k * √(8 * R / π) * √(T / M)`; for a proportionality factor `k` held fixed,
i.e. at fixed number density, `rate ∝ √(T / M)`. At fixed pressure, `k` would instead vary as
`1 / T` (number density `n = p / (R T)`), so this is not a fixed-pressure scaling.
