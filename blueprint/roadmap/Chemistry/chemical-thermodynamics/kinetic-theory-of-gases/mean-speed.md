# Mean molecular speed

Mean speed `⟨v⟩ = ∫₀^∞ v * f(v) dv` for the [Maxwell–Boltzmann speed density](mb-speed.md) `f`,
defined as the integral; its [closed form](mean-speed-eq.md) is `√(8 * R * T / (π * M))` for
`R, T, M > 0`. For `R, T, M > 0` the density `f` is a probability density on `[0, ∞)` and this is
its mean speed. The integral is defined for all real `R`, `T` and `M`: for `R T = 0` or
`M / (R T) < 0` the density is `0` by its conventions, so `⟨v⟩ = 0`.
