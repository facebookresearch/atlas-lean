# With an ideal vapour the Clapeyron slope becomes L p over R T²

Let `T ≠ 0`, `p ≠ 0` and `R ≠ 0`, and let `s`, `L`, `v₁` and `v₂` be real. If `s = L/(T(v₂ - v₁))`,
the latent-heat form of the Clapeyron relation for the slope `s` of the coexistence curve, and
`v₂ - v₁ = RT/p`, the vapour volume of an ideal gas with the liquid volume neglected, then

`s = Lp/(RT²)`.

In the physical reading `s = dp/dT` with `T` the absolute temperature, `p > 0` the pressure, `L` the
latent heat and `R` the gas constant.
