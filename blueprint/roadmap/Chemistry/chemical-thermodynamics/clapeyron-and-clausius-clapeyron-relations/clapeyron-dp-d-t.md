# Clapeyron relation where the two specific volumes differ

Let `μ₁` and `μ₂` be real functions of a curve parameter, and `v₁`, `v₂`, `s₁`, `s₂`, `p′`, `T′` and
`t` real, with `v₂ - v₁ ≠ 0` and `T′ ≠ 0`. Suppose `μ₁` has the derivative `v₁ p′ - s₁ T′` at `t`,
`μ₂` has the derivative `v₂ p′ - s₂ T′` at `t`, and `μ₁ = μ₂` on a neighbourhood of `t`. Then

`p′/T′ = (s₂ - s₁)/(v₂ - v₁)`.

In the physical reading `μ₁` and `μ₂` are the chemical potentials of two coexisting phases along the
curve, `p′` and `T′` the derivatives of the pressure and the temperature along it, `vᵢ` and `sᵢ` the
specific volumes and entropies, and `p′/T′` the slope `dp/dT` of the coexistence curve; the critical
point, where `v₂ = v₁`, is excluded.
