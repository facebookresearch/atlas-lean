# Integrated Clausius–Clapeyron relation for constant latent heat

Let `0 < T₁ ≤ T₂`, `R > 0` and `L` real. Let `p` be a function of temperature with `p(t) > 0` and
derivative `p′(t) = L p(t)/(R t²)` at every `t` in `[T₁, T₂]`. Then

`log(p(T₂)/p(T₁)) = -(L/R) (1/T₂ - 1/T₁)`.

No sign condition on `L` is needed.
