# Latent-heat form of the Clapeyron relation

Let `μ₁`, `μ₂`, `v₁`, `v₂`, `s₁`, `s₂`, `p′`, `T′` and `t` be as in the
[Clapeyron relation](clapeyron-dp-d-t.md): `v₂ - v₁ ≠ 0`, `T′ ≠ 0`, `μ₁` and `μ₂` with derivatives
`v₁ p′ - s₁ T′` and `v₂ p′ - s₂ T′` at `t` and equal on a neighbourhood of `t`. Let `T ≠ 0` and
`L = T (s₂ - s₁)`. Then

`p′/T′ = L/(T (v₂ - v₁))`.

In the physical reading `T` is the absolute temperature and `L` the latent heat.
