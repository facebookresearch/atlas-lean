# Boltzmann probabilities equal those of the finite canonical ensemble

For every real `β` and every state `i`, the Boltzmann probability `exp (-β * E i) / Z(β)` equals the
probability of `i` at real inverse temperature `β` in the [finite canonical ensemble with counting
measure](canonical-ensemble.md) built from the energies `E`: both are
`exp (-β * E i) / ∑ j, exp (-β * E j)`.
