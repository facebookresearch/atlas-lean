# Mean energy equals that of the finite canonical ensemble

For every real `β`, the mean energy `U(β) = ∑ i, p i * E i` of the Boltzmann distribution equals the
mean energy at real inverse temperature `β` of the [finite canonical ensemble with counting
measure](canonical-ensemble.md) built from the energies `E`: both are the sum over the states of
`E i * exp (-β * E i) / Z(β)`.
