# Boltzmann cross entropy depends only on the mean energy

For `p` with `∑ i, p i = 1`, summing `log (p_β i) = -β * E i - log Z` against `p`, where `p_β` is
the Boltzmann distribution, gives `∑ i, p i * log (p_β i) = -β * ∑ i, p i * E i - log Z`: minus the
cross entropy depends on `p` only through its mean energy.
