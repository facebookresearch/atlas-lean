# Fluctuation–dissipation: kB β² Var E = −kB β² dU/dβ

For a nonempty finite system and all real `kB` and `β`, the heat capacity, defined from the energy
fluctuations as `C = kB * β ^ 2 * Var(E)`, equals `-kB * β ^ 2 * dU/dβ`. For `kB > 0` and `β > 0`
the right side is `dU/dT` at `T = 1 / (kB * β)`.
