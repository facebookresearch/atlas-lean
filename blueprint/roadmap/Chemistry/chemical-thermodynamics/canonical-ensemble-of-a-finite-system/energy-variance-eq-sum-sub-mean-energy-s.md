# Energy variance as mean square minus squared mean

For a nonempty finite system with energies `E i`, the [energy variance](energy-variance.md) at
inverse temperature `β` is the mean square energy minus the squared [mean energy](mean-energy.md),
both under the [Boltzmann probabilities](boltzmann-prob.md) `p i`:
`Var(E) = ∑ i, p i * E i ^ 2 - U ^ 2`.
