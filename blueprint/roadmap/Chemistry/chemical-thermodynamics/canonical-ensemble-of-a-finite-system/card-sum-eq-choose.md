# Stars and bars

Stars and bars for the Einstein solid: there are `C(N + q - 1, q)` ways to distribute `q`
indistinguishable quanta among `N` oscillators, that is, `N`-tuples of natural numbers with sum `q`.
Such a tuple is a multiset of size `q` of oscillators, and there are `C(N + q - 1, q)` of these.
