# Helmholtz free energy from the partition function

The Helmholtz free energy `A = -(log Z) / β`; it does not involve the Boltzmann constant. For
`kB > 0` and `β > 0` it equals `-kB T log Z`, since `kB T = 1 / β` ([free energy A = −kT log
Z](helmholtz-free-energy-eq-neg-mul-tempera.md)). By the conventions `x / 0 = 0` and `log 0 = 0`,
`A = 0` at `β = 0` and for an empty state space.
