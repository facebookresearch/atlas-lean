# Energy variance of the Boltzmann distribution

The energy variance `Var(E) = ∑ i, p i * (E i - U) ^ 2` under the
[Boltzmann probabilities](boltzmann-prob.md) `p i`, with `U` the [mean energy](mean-energy.md). For
a nonempty finite state space it is the variance of the energy under the Boltzmann distribution; for
an empty one it is `0`.
