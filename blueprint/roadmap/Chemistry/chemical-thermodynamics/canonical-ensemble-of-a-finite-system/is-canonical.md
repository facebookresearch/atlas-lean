# Canonical distribution at inverse temperature β

The canonical postulate as a predicate: `p` is canonical for the energies `E` at inverse temperature
`β` if `p` equals the [Boltzmann weights](boltzmann-prob.md) `p i = exp (-β * E i) / Z` at every
state. On a nonempty finite state space these weights form the Boltzmann distribution; for an empty
one the predicate holds trivially and the weights sum to `0`. The postulate (equal a priori
probabilities for a system and its reservoir) is a modelling assumption; results about canonical
distributions take it as a hypothesis.
