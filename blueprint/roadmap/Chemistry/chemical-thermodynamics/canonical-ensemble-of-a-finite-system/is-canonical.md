# Canonical distribution at inverse temperature β

The canonical postulate as a predicate: `p` is canonical for the energies `E` at inverse temperature
`β` if `p` is the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-prob.md) `p i = exp (-β * E i) / Z`. The
postulate (equal a priori probabilities for a system and its reservoir) is a modelling assumption;
results about canonical distributions take it as a hypothesis.
