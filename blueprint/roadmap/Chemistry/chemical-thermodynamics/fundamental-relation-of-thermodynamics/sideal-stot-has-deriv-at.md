# Total entropy rate for two exchanging ideal gases

Two ideal-gas subsystems exchange energy at fixed total energy `U_tot`. For `U* > 0`,
`U_tot - U* > 0`, `N₁ > 0` and `N₂ > 0` (and any volumes), the total entropy
`S(U₁, V₁, N₁) + S(U_tot - U₁, V₂, N₂)` has derivative
`3 * N₁ * kB / (2 * U*) - 3 * N₂ * kB / (2 * (U_tot - U*))` in `U₁` at `U₁ = U*`: the difference of
the two values of `∂S/∂U`.
