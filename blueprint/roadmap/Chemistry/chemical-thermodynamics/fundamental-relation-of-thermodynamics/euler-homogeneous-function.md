# Euler's homogeneous-function identity for entropy

For a fundamental relation `S` and a state `(U, V, N)` in the domain, differentiating
`S(tU, tV, tN) = t * S(U, V, N)` at `t = 1` gives `SU*U + SV*V + ∑ SNᵢNᵢ = S`, where `SU`, `SV` and
`SNᵢ` are the partial derivatives of `S` at the state. It uses homogeneity and the [chain
rule](has-deriv-at-comp-curve.md) along the ray `t ↦ (tU, tV, tN)`.
