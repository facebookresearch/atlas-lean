# Gibbs–Duhem relation along a curve

Gibbs–Duhem relation along a curve: let `S` be a fundamental relation and `t ↦ (U(t), V(t), N(t))` a
curve of states differentiable at `t0`, in the domain at `t0` and staying in the domain near `t0`.
If `T`, `p` and the `μᵢ` along the curve have derivatives `T'`, `p'` and `μ'ᵢ` at `t0`, then
`S*T' - V*p' + ∑ Nᵢ*μ'ᵢ = 0`. It follows by differentiating the Euler relation along the curve and
subtracting the first law. The differentiability of `T`, `p` and `μ` along the curve is a
hypothesis, not a consequence of the postulates.
