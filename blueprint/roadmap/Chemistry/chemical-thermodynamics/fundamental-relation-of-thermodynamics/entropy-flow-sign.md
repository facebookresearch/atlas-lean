# Hot-to-cold transfer raises entropy to first order

For a fundamental relation `S`, let two subsystems exchange energy at fixed total energy `U_tot`,
with states `(U*, V₁, N₁)` and `(U_tot - U*, V₂, N₂)` in the domain. If subsystem 1 is hotter than
subsystem 2 (`T₁ > T₂`), then `dS_tot/dU₁ < 0` at `U₁ = U*`, where
`S_tot(U₁) = S(U₁, V₁, N₁) + S(U_tot - U₁, V₂, N₂)`: transferring energy from 1 to 2 (decreasing
`U₁`) increases the total entropy to first order.
