# Equilibrium postulates for the entropy

The equilibrium postulates for the entropy `S` of a simple system with `k` species: on the open
domain `U > 0`, `V > 0`, `Nᵢ > 0`, `S` is Fréchet differentiable, concave, positively homogeneous of
degree 1, and has `∂S/∂U > 0`; nothing is required of `S` outside the domain. The chain rule along
curves, the Euler relation, the Gibbs–Duhem relation and the equations of state follow from these
postulates.
