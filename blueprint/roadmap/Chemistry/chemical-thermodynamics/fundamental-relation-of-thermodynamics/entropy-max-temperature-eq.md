# Temperatures agree at a local entropy maximum

For a fundamental relation `S`, let two subsystems exchange energy at fixed total energy `U_tot`. If
`U*` is a local maximizer of the total entropy `S(U₁, V₁, N₁) + S(U_tot - U₁, V₂, N₂)` with both
subsystem states in the domain, the two subsystem temperatures agree at `U*`. A local maximum
suffices; no global maximum is needed.
