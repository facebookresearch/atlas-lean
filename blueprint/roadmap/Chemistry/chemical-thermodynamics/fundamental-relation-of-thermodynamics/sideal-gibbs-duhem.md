# Gibbs–Duhem relation for the ideal gas

For `kB > 0` and a curve of states `t ↦ (U(t), V(t), N(t))` that is differentiable at `t0` and stays
in `U, V, N > 0` near `t0`, the ideal gas satisfies the Gibbs–Duhem relation
`S * dT/dt - V * dp/dt + N * dμ/dt = 0` at `t0`, where `T`, `p` and `μ` are its temperature,
pressure and chemical potential along the curve. It follows from the explicit formulas for `T`, `p`
and `μ`.
