# Total entropy is differentiable under energy exchange

For a fundamental relation `S`, let two subsystems exchange energy at fixed total energy `U_tot`,
with states `(U*, V₁, N₁)` and `(U_tot - U*, V₂, N₂)` in the domain. The total entropy
`S_tot(U₁) = S(U₁, V₁, N₁) + S(U_tot - U₁, V₂, N₂)` is differentiable at `U₁ = U*`, with derivative
`∂S/∂U (U*, V₁, N₁) - ∂S/∂U (U_tot - U*, V₂, N₂)`, by the [chain rule](has-deriv-at-comp-curve.md)
on each subsystem.
