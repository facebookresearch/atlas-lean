# First law along a differentiable curve of states

For a fundamental relation `S` and a curve of states `t ↦ (U(t), V(t), N(t))` differentiable at
`t0`, with its state at `t0` in the domain: `dU/dt = T dS/dt - p dV/dt + ∑ μᵢ dNᵢ/dt` at `t0`, where
`dS/dt` is the derivative of `S` along the curve, given by the [chain
rule](has-deriv-at-comp-curve.md) as the total differential.
