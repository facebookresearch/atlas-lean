# Differential of the Gibbs energy

For real numbers `dU`, `dS`, `dV`, `dT`, `dp`, `dG`, `T`, `p`, `S` and `V`, suppose

- `dU = T dS - p dV` (the fundamental relation for the internal energy), and
- `dG = dU - T dS - S dT + p dV + V dp` (the product rule applied to `G = U - T S + p V`).

Then `dG = -S dT + V dp`. The statement is the algebraic substitution of the first relation into
the second: the variables are numbers standing for differentials, and nothing is claimed about
integrability, Maxwell relations or an equation of state.
