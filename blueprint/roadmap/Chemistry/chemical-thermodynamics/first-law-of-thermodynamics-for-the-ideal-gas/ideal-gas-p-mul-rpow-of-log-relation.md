# Pressure–volume invariant from a logarithmic state relation

The ideal-gas `p * V ^ γ`, with `p = n * R * T / V`, takes equal values at two states related by a
logarithmic relation: for `n > 0`, `R > 0`, positive temperatures and volumes, `Cv ≠ 0`,
`γ - 1 = k / Cv` and `Cv * log (T₂ / T₁) + k * log (V₂ / V₁) = 0` with an arbitrary coefficient `k`.
This is an algebraic identity: `k` need not be `n * R`. The physical statement, with `k = n * R`, is
the [pressure–volume invariant of a reversible adiabat](adiabatic-invariant-p-v.md).
