# Adiabatic pressure ratio from the volume ratio

Reversible adiabatic invariant in ratio form: for `n > 0`, `R > 0`, positive temperatures and
volumes, `Cv ≠ 0`, `γ - 1 = n * R / Cv` and the adiabatic relation
`Cv * log (T₂ / T₁) + n * R * log (V₂ / V₁) = 0` (the [integrated adiabatic
relation](adiabatic-path-integrated.md) with `nR = n * R`), the pressures `p = n * R * T / V` of the
ideal gas satisfy `p₂ / p₁ = (V₁ / V₂) ^ γ`. Only `Cv ≠ 0` is needed; the physical case is `0 < Cv`.
