# The heat 1-form has a nonzero loop integral

For `n > 0`, `R > 0`, `T₁ ≠ T₂` and `0 < V₁ < V₂`, the integral of the heat 1-form
`n * R * T / V dV + Cv dT` around the rectangle `[V₁, V₂] × [T₁, T₂]` is nonzero: heat is path
dependent. Nothing is assumed about `Cv` or the signs of the temperatures, so it also holds outside
the physical domain, where it is a fact about the 1-form rather than about a gas; the physical case
is `0 < T₁`, `0 < T₂`, `0 < Cv`.
