# Temperature–volume invariant of a reversible adiabat

Reversible adiabatic invariant: for positive temperatures `T₁`, `T₂` and volumes `V₁`, `V₂`,
`Cv ≠ 0`, `γ - 1 = nR / Cv` and the adiabatic relation
`Cv * log (T₂ / T₁) + nR * log (V₂ / V₁) = 0`, `T₂ * V₂ ^ (γ - 1) = T₁ * V₁ ^ (γ - 1)`, so
`T * V ^ (γ - 1)` takes the same value at both states.
