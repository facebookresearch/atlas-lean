# Exact differentials agree along the right-up and up-right paths

Let `U(V, T)` have derivative `P(V, T)` in `V` at every `V` between `V₁` and `V₂` on the edges
`T = T₁` and `T = T₂`, and derivative `Q(V, T)` in `T` at every `T` between `T₁` and `T₂` on the
edges `V = V₁` and `V = V₂`, with `P` and `Q` integrable along these edges. Then the integrals of
the exact differential `dU = P dV + Q dT` from `(V₁, T₁)` to `(V₂, T₂)` along the
[right-then-up](path-right-up.md) and the [up-then-right](path-up-right.md) paths agree.
