# Heat differs along the right-up and up-right paths

For `n > 0`, `R > 0`, `T₁ ≠ T₂` and `0 < V₁ < V₂`, heat is path dependent: the integrals of the heat
1-form `n * R * T / V dV + Cv dT` from `(V₁, T₁)` to `(V₂, T₂)` along the
[right-then-up](path-right-up.md) and the [up-then-right](path-up-right.md) paths differ. This holds
for every `Cv` and all real `T₁ ≠ T₂`, nonpositive temperatures included, where it describes the
1-form rather than a gas; the physical case is `0 < T₁`, `0 < T₂`, `0 < Cv`.
