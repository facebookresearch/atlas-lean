# Reversible isothermal work on an ideal gas

Reversible isothermal work done on `n` moles of ideal gas at temperature `T` taken from volume `V₁`
to `V₂`: `w = -(n * R * T * log (V₂ / V₁))`. Physical volumes are positive; for `V₁ ≤ 0` or `V₂ ≤ 0`
the conventions `log x = log |x|` and `log 0 = 0` give a meaningless value.
