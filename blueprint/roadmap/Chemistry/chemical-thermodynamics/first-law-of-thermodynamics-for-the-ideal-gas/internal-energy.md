# Ideal-gas internal energy with constant Cv

Internal energy of an ideal gas with constant heat capacity `Cv`, relative to a reference state:
`U(T) = U₀ + Cv * (T - T₀)`, where `U₀` is the energy at the reference temperature `T₀`. A constant
`Cv` determines only energy differences, so the reference state is a parameter; in the ideal-gas
model `U` does not depend on `V`.
