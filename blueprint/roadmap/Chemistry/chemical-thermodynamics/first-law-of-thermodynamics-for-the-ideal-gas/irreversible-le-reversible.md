# Irreversible expansion work is at most the reversible work

For an isothermal expansion of an ideal gas with `n, R, T > 0` and `0 < V₁ ≤ V₂`, against a constant
external pressure `p_ext` with `0 ≤ p_ext ≤ n * R * T / V₂` (at most the final pressure), the
irreversible work `w_irr = -p_ext * (V₂ - V₁)` and the reversible work
`w_rev = -n * R * T * log (V₂ / V₁)` satisfy `|w_irr| ≤ |w_rev|`. The hypotheses are needed: without
`0 ≤ p_ext` the bound fails.
