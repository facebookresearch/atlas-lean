# Pressure–volume invariant of a reversible adiabat

Reversible adiabatic invariant: for `n > 0`, `R > 0`, positive temperatures and volumes, `Cv ≠ 0`,
`γ - 1 = n * R / Cv` and the adiabatic relation `Cv * log (T₂ / T₁) + n * R * log (V₂ / V₁) = 0`
(the [integrated adiabatic relation](adiabatic-path-integrated.md) with `nR = n * R`), `p * V ^ γ`
takes the same value at both states of the ideal gas `p = n * R * T / V`. Only `Cv ≠ 0` is needed;
the physical case is `0 < Cv`.
