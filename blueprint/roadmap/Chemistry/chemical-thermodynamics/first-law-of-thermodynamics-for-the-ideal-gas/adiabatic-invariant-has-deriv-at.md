# The adiabatic log-invariant has zero derivative

Let `T` and `V` have derivatives `T'(t)` and `V'(t)` at `t`, with `T(t) > 0`, `V(t) > 0`, `Cv ≠ 0`
and `γ - 1 = nR / Cv`. If the separated adiabatic equation
`Cv * T'(t) / T(t) + nR * V'(t) / V(t) = 0` holds at `t`, then the log-invariant
`log T + (γ - 1) * log V` has derivative `0` at `t`.
