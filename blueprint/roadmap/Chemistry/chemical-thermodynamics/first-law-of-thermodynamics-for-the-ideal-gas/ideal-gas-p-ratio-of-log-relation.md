# Pressure ratio from a logarithmic state relation

Pressure ratio of the ideal gas `p = n * R * T / V` between two states related by a logarithmic
relation: for `n > 0`, `R > 0`, positive temperatures and volumes, `Cv ≠ 0`, `γ - 1 = k / Cv` and
`Cv * log (T₂ / T₁) + k * log (V₂ / V₁) = 0` with an arbitrary coefficient `k`,
`p₂ / p₁ = (V₁ / V₂) ^ γ`. This is an algebraic identity, not the physical adiabat: `n * R` cancels
from the pressure ratio, so `k` need not be `n * R`. The physical statement, with `k = n * R`, is
the [adiabatic pressure ratio](adiabatic-invariant-ratio-p.md).
