# Isothermal work as the integral of −p dV

For `V₁ > 0` and `V₂ > 0`, the integral from `V₁` to `V₂` of `-p dV` with `p = n * R * T / V` equals
the reversible isothermal work `-(n * R * T * log (V₂ / V₁))` done on the gas. The ideal-gas
equation and reversibility enter as the explicit integrand `-p dV` at constant `T`. The identity
holds for all real `n`, `R` and `T`; its physical case is `n, R, T > 0`.
