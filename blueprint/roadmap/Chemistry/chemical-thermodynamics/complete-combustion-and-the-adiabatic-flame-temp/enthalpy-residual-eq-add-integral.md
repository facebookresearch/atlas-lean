# Kirchhoff form of the enthalpy residual

Kirchhoff form of the residual: if every `H j` has derivative `cp j` at every temperature between
`T₁` and `T₂` and the total heat capacity `∑ j, nP j * cp j T` is integrable on that interval, the
residual grows from `T₁` to `T₂` by the integral of the total heat capacity:
`R(T₂) = R(T₁) + ∫ T in T₁..T₂, ∑ j, nP j * cp j T`.
