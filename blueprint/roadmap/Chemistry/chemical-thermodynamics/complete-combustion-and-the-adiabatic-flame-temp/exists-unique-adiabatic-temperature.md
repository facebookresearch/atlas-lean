# Existence and uniqueness of the adiabatic temperature

The unique adiabatic temperature: with `Tlo < Thi`, every molar enthalpy `H j` having temperature
derivative `cp j` at every temperature of `[Tlo, Thi]`, positive total heat capacity
`∑ j, nP j * cp j T` on `[Tlo, Thi]` and the sign change `R(Tlo) ≤ 0 ≤ R(Thi)`, the residual `R` has
exactly one root in `[Tlo, Thi]`.
