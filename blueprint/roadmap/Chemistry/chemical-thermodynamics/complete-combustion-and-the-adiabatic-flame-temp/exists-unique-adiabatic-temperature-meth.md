# Existence and uniqueness of the methane flame temperature

The adiabatic temperature of the methane product model: from nonnegative reactant amounts `n₀` and a
feasible positive extent `ξ` of complete methane combustion, with `Tlo < Thi`, every molar enthalpy
`H j` having derivative `cp j > 0` at every temperature of `[Tlo, Thi]`, and `R(Tlo) ≤ 0 ≤ R(Thi)`
for the residual `R` of the products, the products `n₀ + ξ ν` (with `ν` the [complete combustion of
methane](methane-combustion.md)) have exactly one adiabatic temperature in `[Tlo, Thi]`.
