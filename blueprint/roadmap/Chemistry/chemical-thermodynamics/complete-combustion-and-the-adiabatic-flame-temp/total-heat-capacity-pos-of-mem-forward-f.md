# Feasible products have positive total heat capacity

Feasible products have positive total heat capacity: from nonnegative initial amounts `n₀`, a
feasible positive extent `ξ` of a reaction `ν` that produces some species `j₀` (`ν j₀ > 0`) leaves
nonnegative amounts with a positive amount of `j₀`; so, with positive molar heat capacities
`cp j T > 0`, the total heat capacity of the products is positive: `∑ j, (n₀ + ξ ν) j * cp j T > 0`.
