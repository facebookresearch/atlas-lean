# For μ₁ < μ₂ and n₁ < N, the all-phase-1 split is strictly lower

In the linear two-phase model `G(n₁) = n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂` at fixed temperature and pressure, with
chemical potentials `μ₁`, `μ₂` independent of composition (no mixing entropy) and `n₁` the amount in
phase 1 out of the total `N`: let `0 ≤ n₁ < N` and `μ₁ < μ₂`. Then `N μ₁ < n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂`:
every split with some amount left in phase 2 has strictly higher Gibbs energy than the all-phase-1
split.
