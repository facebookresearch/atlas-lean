# For equal chemical potentials, every split has the same Gibbs energy

In the linear two-phase model `G(n₁) = n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂` at fixed temperature and pressure, with
chemical potentials `μ₁`, `μ₂` independent of composition (no mixing entropy) and `n₁` the amount in
phase 1 out of the total `N`: if `μ₁ = μ₂`, then `n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂ = N μ₁` for all real `n₁` and
`N`. Every split has the same Gibbs energy, so every split in `[0, N]` is a minimiser: the
equilibrium is degenerate.
