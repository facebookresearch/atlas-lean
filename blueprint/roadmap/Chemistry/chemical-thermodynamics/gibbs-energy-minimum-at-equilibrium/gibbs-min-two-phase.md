# For μ₁ ≤ μ₂, putting everything in phase 1 minimises the Gibbs energy

In the linear two-phase model `G(n₁) = n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂` at fixed temperature and pressure,
with chemical potentials `μ₁`, `μ₂` independent of composition (no mixing entropy) and `n₁` the
amount in phase 1 out of the total `N`: let `N ≥ 0`, `0 ≤ n₁ ≤ N` and `μ₁ ≤ μ₂`. Then

`N μ₁ ≤ n₁ μ₁ + (N - n₁) μ₂`,

so the all-phase-1 split `n₁ = N` has the least Gibbs energy over `[0, N]`.
