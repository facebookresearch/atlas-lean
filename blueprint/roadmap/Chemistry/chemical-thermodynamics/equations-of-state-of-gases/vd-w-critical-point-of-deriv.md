# Van der Waals critical constants from vanishing derivatives

Van der Waals model, critical point stated with the derivatives themselves: for `a, b, R, T > 0` and
`V_m > b`, if the first and second derivatives of the van der Waals pressure `p` in the molar volume
vanish at `V_m` (`∂p/∂V_m = 0` and `∂²p/∂V_m² = 0`), then `V_m = 3 b`, `T = 8 a / (27 R b)`,
`p = a / (27 b ^ 2)` and `Z = 3 / 8`.
