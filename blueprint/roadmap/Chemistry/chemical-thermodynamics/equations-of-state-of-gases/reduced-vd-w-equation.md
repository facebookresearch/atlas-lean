# Reduced van der Waals equation

Van der Waals model: for `a > 0`, `b > 0` and `V_m > b`, with `p` the van der Waals pressure at
`(V_m, T)` and the reduced variables `P_r = p / p_c`, `V_r = V_m / V_{m,c}` and `T_r = T / T_c`, the
reduced equation `(P_r + 3 / V_r ^ 2) (3 V_r - 1) = 8 T_r` holds, in which `a, b, R` no longer
appear. This is exact within the van der Waals model; for real gases the law of corresponding states
is an empirical regularity. `R > 0` and `T > 0` only record the domain of the model; the identity
holds for every `R` and `T`.
