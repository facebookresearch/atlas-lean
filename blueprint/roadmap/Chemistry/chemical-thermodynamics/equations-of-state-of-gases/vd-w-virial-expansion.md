# Exact virial identity for the van der Waals gas

Van der Waals model, exact virial identity: for `b > 0`, `R > 0`, `T > 0`, `V_m > b` and `p` the van
der Waals pressure at `(V_m, T)`, `Z = 1 + B / V_m + b ^ 2 / (V_m (V_m - b))` with
`B = b - a / (R T)`. So `B` is the coefficient of `1 / V_m` in the expansion of `Z`, with the exact
remainder `b ^ 2 / (V_m (V_m - b))`, which is of order `1 / V_m ^ 2` as `V_m → ∞` (a remark beyond
the statement); the truncation `Z ≈ 1 + B / V_m` is not an equality. `a > 0` only records the domain
of the model; the identity holds for every `a`.
