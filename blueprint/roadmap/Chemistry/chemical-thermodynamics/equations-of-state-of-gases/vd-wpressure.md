# Van der Waals pressure

The van der Waals pressure `p = R T / (V_m - b) - a / V_m ^ 2` as a function of the molar
volume `Vm`. The model is used on `Vm > b` with fitted parameters `a, b > 0` and with
`R, T > 0`; outside `Vm > b` the van der Waals model does not apply. It is defined for every real
`Vm`: by the convention `x / 0 = 0`, the term `R T / (V_m - b)` is `0` at `Vm = b` and the term
`a / V_m ^ 2` is `0` at `Vm = 0`.
