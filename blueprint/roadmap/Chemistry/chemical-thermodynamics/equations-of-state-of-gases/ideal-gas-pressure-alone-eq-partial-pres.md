# Ideal-gas partial pressure is the pressure exerted alone

Ideal-gas model: in a mixture with amounts `n i ≥ 0` of positive total `∑ i, n i` at volume `V > 0`
and temperature `T`, the pressure `n i * R * T / V` that component `i` would exert alone at the same
`T` and `V` equals its partial pressure `x_i p`, with mole fraction `x_i = n i / ∑ j, n j` and total
pressure `p = (∑ j, n j) * R * T / V`. The identity needs only `0 < ∑ i, n i` and `0 < V`;
`0 ≤ n i`, `0 < T` and `0 < R` record the domain of the model.
