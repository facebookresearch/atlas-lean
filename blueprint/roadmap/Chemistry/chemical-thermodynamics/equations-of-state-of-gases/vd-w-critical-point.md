# Van der Waals critical constants from explicit equations

Van der Waals model, critical point. If `a, b, R, T > 0`, `V_m > b`, and both critical equations
hold at `(V_m, T)` in explicit form, `-R T / (V_m - b) ^ 2 + 2 a / V_m ^ 3 = 0` (`∂p/∂V_m = 0`) and
`2 R T / (V_m - b) ^ 3 - 6 a / V_m ^ 4 = 0` (`∂²p/∂V_m² = 0`), then the critical molar volume is
`V_m = 3 b`, the critical temperature is `T = 8 a / (27 R b)`, the critical pressure is
`a / (27 b ^ 2)` and the critical compressibility factor is `3 / 8`.
