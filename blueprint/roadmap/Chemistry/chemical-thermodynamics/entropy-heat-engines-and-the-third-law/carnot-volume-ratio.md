# Equal volume ratios in a Carnot cycle

The adiabatic legs of a reversible ideal-gas Carnot cycle force equal volume ratios: for
`0 < Tc < Th`, `R > 0`, `cv > 0` and positive volumes, the model relations
`Th * V₂ ^ k = Tc * V₃ ^ k` and `Tc * V₄ ^ k = Th * V₁ ^ k` with `k = R / cv` give
`V₂ / V₁ = V₃ / V₄`. The adiabatic relations are model inputs. Taking logarithms of both relations,
adding them and cancelling the factor `k ≠ 0` gives `log (V₂ / V₁) = log (V₃ / V₄)`.
