# Carnot efficiency of a reversible engine

The Carnot efficiency `1 - Tc / Th` of a reversible engine operating between a cold reservoir at
absolute temperature `Tc` and a hot reservoir at absolute temperature `Th`. Physical domain:
`0 ≤ Tc ≤ Th` with `0 < Th`, where the value lies in `[0, 1]`. Other values are nonphysical:
`Tc > Th` gives a negative value, a negative `Tc` a value above `1`, and at `Th = 0` the convention
`x / 0 = 0` gives `1`.
