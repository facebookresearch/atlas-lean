# Kirchhoff's law

Kirchhoff's law: when `ΔH` has derivative `ΔCp` at every temperature between `T1` and `T2` and `ΔCp`
is integrable on that interval, `ΔH T2 = ΔH T1 + ∫ T in T1..T2, ΔCp T`. The temperatures may be
ordered either way: the hypotheses are stated on the closed interval between `T1` and `T2`, and the
integral changes sign when the endpoints are swapped. `ΔCp` is any integrable function; no
particular heat-capacity fit is assumed.
