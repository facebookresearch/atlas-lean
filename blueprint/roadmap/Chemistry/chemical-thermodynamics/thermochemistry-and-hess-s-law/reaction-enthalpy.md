# Reaction enthalpy as a linear functional

Reaction enthalpy as a linear functional on the reaction space: for fixed molar enthalpies `H i` of
the species, the enthalpy of the reaction `ν` is `∑ i, ν i * H i`, with products positive and
reactants negative. `ΔH` is per mole of reaction as written: scaling the written equation scales
`ΔH` (see [scaling](hess-smul.md) and [doubling](hess-double.md)).
