# The first visit is finite exactly when the chain ever visits the state

Let $S$ be a set, $x_0, x \in S$ and $\omega$ a step sequence. The first visit
$V_0(x_0, x, \omega)$ to $x_0$ (the [visit](visit.md) with index $0$) of the jump chain $Y$ started
at $x$ is finite if and only if the chain is at $x_0$ at some step, step $0$ included:

$$V_0(x_0, x, \omega) < \infty \iff Y_n(x, \omega) = x_0 \text{ for some } n \in \mathbb{N} .$$
