# At most one visit has exactly $k$ earlier visits

Let $S$ be a set, $x_0, x \in S$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\omega$ a step sequence, let $Y$ be the
jump chain started at $x$, and write $N_n$ for the [number of visits](n-visits.md) to $x_0$ before
step $n$. If two steps $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$ are both visits to $x_0$ with exactly $k$ earlier
visits,

$$Y_m = x_0,\ N_m = k \qquad \text{and} \qquad Y_n = x_0,\ N_n = k ,$$

then $m = n$.
