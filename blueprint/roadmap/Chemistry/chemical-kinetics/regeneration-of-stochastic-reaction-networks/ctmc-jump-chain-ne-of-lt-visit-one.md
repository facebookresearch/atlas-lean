# Between step 0 and a finite first return, the chain avoids $x_0$

Let $S$ be a set, $x_0 \in S$ and $\omega$ a step sequence, and consider the jump chain $Y$ started
at $x_0$, whose [visits](visit.md) to $x_0$ start with $V_0 = 0$. If its first return is finite,
$V_1(x_0, x_0, \omega) = m$ for a natural number $m$, then the chain is not at $x_0$ in between:
$Y_i(x_0, \omega) \ne x_0$ for every $i$ with $0 < i < m$.
