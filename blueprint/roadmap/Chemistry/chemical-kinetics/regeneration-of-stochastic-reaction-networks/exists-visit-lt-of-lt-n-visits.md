# More than $k$ visits before step $n$ put the $k$-th visit before $n$

Let $S$ be a set, $x_0, x \in S$, $\omega$ a step sequence and $k, n \in \mathbb{N}$. Write $N_n$
for the [number of visits](n-visits.md) to $x_0$ before step $n$ of the jump chain started at $x$,
and $V_k$ for its [$k$-th visit](visit.md) to $x_0$, counting from $0$. If $k < N_n$, then
$V_k = m$ for some natural number $m < n$.
