# The first $n$ steps generate every measurable set they determine

Let $S$ be a measurable space, and give step sequences $\omega = (\omega_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$,
$\omega_k = (\xi_k, E_k) \in S^S \times \mathbb{R}$, the product σ-algebra built from the product
σ-algebra on maps $S \to S$ and the Borel σ-algebra on $\mathbb{R}$. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let
$A$ be a measurable set of step sequences whose membership depends only on the first $n$ steps: if
$\omega \in A$ and $\omega'_i = \omega_i$ for all $i < n$, then $\omega' \in A$. Then $A$ belongs to
the [σ-algebra generated by the first $n$ steps](past-sigma.md),
$\mathcal{F}_{<n} = \sigma(\omega_0, \dots, \omega_{n-1})$.
