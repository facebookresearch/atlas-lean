# The shift of the step sequence by $n$ steps is measurable

Let $S$ be a measurable space, and give step sequences $\omega = (\omega_m)_{m \in \mathbb{N}}$,
$\omega_m = (\xi_m, E_m) \in S^S \times \mathbb{R}$, the product σ-algebra built from the product
σ-algebra on maps $S \to S$ and the Borel σ-algebra on $\mathbb{R}$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
the
[shift](../../../Probability/markov-chains/finite-continuous-time-markov-chains/finite-continuous-time-markov-chains-as-path-pro/ctmc-shift.md)
$\theta_n$, $(\theta_n \omega)_m = \omega_{n+m}$, is a measurable map from the space of step
sequences to itself, with this σ-algebra on both sides.
