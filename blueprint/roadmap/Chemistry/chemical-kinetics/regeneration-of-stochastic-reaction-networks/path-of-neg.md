# Before time 0 the path is at its initial state

Let $\sigma$ be a set, $q : \sigma \to \mathbb{R}$ any rates, $x \in \sigma$ a starting state and
$\omega$ a sequence of steps. The [path](../non-explosion-of-stochastic-reaction-networks/path.md)
$X$ of the jump–hold chain started at $x$ satisfies $X_s = x$ for every time $s < 0$. No condition
is imposed on the rates or the clocks: $X_s$ is the state at time $s$ of the chain stopped after
$n$ jumps, for the least $n$ whose jump time $T_n$ exceeds $s$, and $T_0 = 0 > s$ gives $n = 0$.
