# For $a > 0$, $\int_t^\infty a^2 e^{-a r}\,dr = a e^{-a t}$

For every $a > 0$ and every real $t$,

$$\int_t^\infty a^2 e^{-a r}\, dr = a e^{-a t} ,$$

where the left side is the lower Lebesgue integral over $[t, \infty)$ of the positive function
$r \mapsto a^2 e^{-a r}$. It is finite, so it is also the ordinary integral.
