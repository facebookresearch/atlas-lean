# The σ-algebra of the first $n$ steps

Let $S$ be a measurable space. Give the maps $S \to S$ the product σ-algebra (generated by the
evaluations $\xi \mapsto \xi(y)$, $y \in S$), $\mathbb{R}$ its Borel σ-algebra, and the steps
$(\xi, E) \in S^S \times \mathbb{R}$ the product of these two σ-algebras. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
the σ-algebra of the first $n$ steps is the σ-algebra on step sequences
$\omega = (\omega_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ generated by the coordinates $\omega \mapsto \omega_k$ with
$k < n$:

$$\mathcal{F}_{<n} = \sigma(\omega_0, \dots, \omega_{n-1}) .$$

For $n = 0$ no coordinate is used, and $\mathcal{F}_{<0}$ is the trivial σ-algebra, whose only sets
are the empty set and the set of all step sequences.
