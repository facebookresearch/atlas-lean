# If the $k$-th visit is at step $n$, every earlier visit is by step $n$

Let $S$ be a set, $x_0, x \in S$ and $\omega$ a step sequence, and write $V_j$ for the
[$j$-th visit](visit.md) to $x_0$, counting from $0$, of the jump chain started at $x$. If
$V_k = n$ for natural numbers $k$ and $n$, then for every $j \le k$ the $j$-th visit is finite and
no later than step $n$: $V_j = m$ for some natural number $m \le n$.
