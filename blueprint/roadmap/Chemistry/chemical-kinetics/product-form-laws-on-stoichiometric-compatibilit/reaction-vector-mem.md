# Each reaction vector lies in the stoichiometric subspace

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set `S` and reaction set `ρ`. For every reaction `r`, the reaction vector
`prod(r) - src(r) ∈ ℝ^S` lies in the
[stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md) `𝒮` of `N`,
the linear span of all reaction vectors.
