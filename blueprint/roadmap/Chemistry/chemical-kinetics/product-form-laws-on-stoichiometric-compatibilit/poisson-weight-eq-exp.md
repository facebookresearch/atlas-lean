# Exponential form of the Poisson weight for positive means

Let `S` be a finite set and `c ∈ ℝ^S` with `c_s > 0` for every `s`. For every state `x ∈ ℕ^S` the
[Poisson weight](../../../Biology/systems-biology/stochastic-mass-action-metabolic-control-and-flu/poisson-weight.md)
is `π_c(x) = exp(∑_s (x_s log c_s - c_s)) / ∏_s x_s!`. Positivity gives
`exp(x_s log c_s) = c_s^{x_s}`.
