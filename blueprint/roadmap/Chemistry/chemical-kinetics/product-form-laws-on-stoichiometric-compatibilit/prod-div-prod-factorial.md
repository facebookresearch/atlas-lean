# Monomial over factorials in terms of the multinomial coefficient

Let `S` be a finite set, `c ∈ ℝ^S` and `x ∈ ℕ^S`. Then
`∏_s c_s^{x_s} / ∏_s x_s! = multinomial(x) · ∏_s c_s^{x_s} / (∑_s x_s)!`, where
`multinomial(x) = (∑_s x_s)! / ∏_s x_s!` is the multinomial coefficient of `x`. This holds for every
real `c`.
