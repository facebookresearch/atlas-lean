# States with a given total molecule count

Let `S` be a finite set of species and `n ∈ ℕ`. The states with total molecule count `n` are the
`x ∈ ℕ^S` with `∑_s x_s = n`. They form a finite set, which is empty exactly when `S` is empty and
`n ≥ 1`: for empty `S` the only state is the empty vector, of total `0`.
