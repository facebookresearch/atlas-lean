# Energy identity for the acoustic mode

Energy identity for the acoustic mode: let `γ ≠ 0`, `pbar ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`. If `A` and `B` have
derivatives `A'` and `B'` at `t` with `A' = -γ * pbar * (π / L) * B(t) + (γ - 1) * H` and
`ρ * B' = (π / L) * A(t) - r * B(t)`, then the energy
`E(t) = L * (A(t)^2 / (γ * pbar) + ρ * B(t)^2) / 4` has derivative
`L * (γ - 1) * A(t) * H / (2 * γ * pbar) - r * L * B(t)^2 / 2` at `t`: the heat-release source term
minus the damping term `r * L * B(t)^2 / 2`, a dissipation for `r ≥ 0` and `L > 0` (see [without
heat release the derivative is nonpositive](acoustic-mode-energy-deriv-nonpos-of-no.md)). Here `H`
is a constant heat-release amplitude.
