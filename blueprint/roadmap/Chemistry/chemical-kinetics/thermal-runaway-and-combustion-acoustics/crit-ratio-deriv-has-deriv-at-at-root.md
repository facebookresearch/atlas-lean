# Second derivative of the critical ratio at a stationary point

At a stationary point `T > 0`, a root of `T^2 - B * T + B * Tₐ`, the function
`s ↦ exp (B / s) * (s^2 - B * s + B * Tₐ) / s^2`, which equals the derivative of the critical ratio
at every `s > 0`, has derivative `exp (B / T) * (2 * T - B) / T^2` at `T`: the terms containing the
vanishing polynomial drop out. So this is the second derivative of the critical ratio at `T`.
