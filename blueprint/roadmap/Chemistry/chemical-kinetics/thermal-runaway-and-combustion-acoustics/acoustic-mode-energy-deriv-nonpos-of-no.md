# Without heat release the modal energy derivative is nonpositive

Signed dissipation with no heat release: let `γ ≠ 0`, `pbar ≠ 0`, `L > 0` and `r ≥ 0`, and let `A`
and `B` have derivatives `A'` and `B'` at `t` satisfying the modal equations
`A' = -γ * pbar * (π / L) * B(t)` (no heat release) and `ρ * B' = (π / L) * A(t) - r * B(t)`. Then
the modal energy has derivative `-(r * L * B(t)^2 / 2)` at `t`, which is nonpositive.
