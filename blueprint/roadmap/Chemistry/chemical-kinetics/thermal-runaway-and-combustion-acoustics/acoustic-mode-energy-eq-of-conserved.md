# Conservation of modal energy without heat release or damping

Genuine conservation: for `γ > 0`, `pbar > 0` and `L > 0`, if the modal equations with no heat
release and no damping, `A' = -γ * pbar * (π / L) * B` and `ρ * B' = (π / L) * A`, hold at every
time, the modal energy takes the same value at any two times.
