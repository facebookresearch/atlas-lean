# Zero energy derivative without heat release or damping

For `γ ≠ 0`, `pbar ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`, with no heat release and no damping, the modal energy has
derivative `0` at any time `t` at which `A` and `B` have derivatives `A'` and `B'` with
`A' = -γ * pbar * (π / L) * B(t)` and `ρ * B' = (π / L) * A(t)`. Conservation between two times is
[a separate result](acoustic-mode-energy-eq-of-conserved.md).
