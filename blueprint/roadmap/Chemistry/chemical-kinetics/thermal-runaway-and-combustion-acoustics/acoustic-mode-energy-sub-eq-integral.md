# Integrated energy balance of the acoustic mode

Integrated energy balance between the times `t0` and `t1`: for `γ > 0`, `pbar > 0` and `L > 0`, if
`A' = -γ * pbar * (π / L) * B + (γ - 1) * H` and `ρ * B' = (π / L) * A - r * B` at every time
between `t0` and `t1`, then
`E(t1) - E(t0) = ∫ t in t0..t1, (L * (γ - 1) * A(t) * H / (2 * γ * pbar) - r * L * B(t)^2 / 2)`: the
heat-release source minus the damping term.
