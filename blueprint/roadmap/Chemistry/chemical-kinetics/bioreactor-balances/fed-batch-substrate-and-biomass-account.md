# Fed-batch substrate and biomass account

In a fed-batch bioreactor with initial volume $V_0 > 0$, continuous feed rate $F \ge 0$, feed density equal to the broth density (so
$V' = F$), no outflow, death or maintenance, substrate feed concentration $S_{\mathrm{in}} \ge 0$, yield $Y > 0$ and Monod growth rate
$\mu(S)$ with positive parameters, let

$$S' = \frac{F}{V}(S_{\mathrm{in}} - S) - \frac{\mu(S)X}{Y}, \qquad X' = \mu(S)X - \frac{F}{V}X, \qquad S(0), X(0) \ge 0 .$$

Then $V \ge V_0$, the stocks $m_S = VS$ and $m_X = VX$ satisfy $(m_S + m_X/Y)' = F S_{\mathrm{in}}$, and $S$ and $X$ stay nonnegative.
