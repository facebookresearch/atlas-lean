# Box invariance for any bounded rate law

Comparison on a time interval `[0, τ]`, `τ ≥ 0`: for `a > 0`, `b, h, cin, c0 ≥ 0`, `T0 > 0` and any
rate law `κ` with `0 ≤ κ T` for every `T` and `κ T ≤ A` for `T > 0`, a solution of the reactor
balances on `[0, τ]` starting at `(c0, T0)` is in the box `[0, cmax] × [Tmin, Tmax]` ([concentration
ceiling](concentration-ceiling.md), [temperature floor](temperature-floor.md) and
[ceiling](temperature-ceiling.md)) at time `τ`. The concentration bounds and the two temperature
bounds come from two orthant comparison arguments (lower and upper corner).
