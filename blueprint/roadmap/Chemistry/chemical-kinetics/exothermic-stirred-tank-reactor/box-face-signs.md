# Inward signs on the faces of the box

Inward signs on the four faces of the box: for `a > 0`, `b ≥ 0`, `h ≥ 0`, `cin ≥ 0`, any rate value
`0 ≤ k ≤ A` and a state with `0 ≤ c ≤ cmax`, the [concentration balance](concentration-rhs.md) `c'`
and the [temperature balance](temperature-rhs.md) `T'` satisfy `c' ≥ 0` at `c = 0`, `c' ≤ 0` at
`c = cmax`, `T' ≥ 0` at `T = Tmin` and `T' ≤ 0` at `T = Tmax`, where `cmax`, `Tmin` and `Tmax` are
the bounds of the box.
