# Global dynamics of nonisothermal stirred tanks

This node collects two open directions for the nonisothermal stirred-tank reactor beyond the steady-state, invariant-set
and uniqueness results in this folder:

- runaway and recovery, exact counts of steady states, and the bifurcation structure of the adiabatic reactor;
- cooled reactors ($UA > 0$), whose thermal and material relaxation coefficients differ, so that they are not
  instances of the dimensionless adiabatic family.

## Expansion

A good expansion selects a precisely stated result from the literature (for example a condition for exactly three
steady states, or for a Hopf bifurcation of the cooled reactor), states it with its parameter domain, and links the
dimensionless reactor pages it builds on. Until then these are questions, not stated results.
