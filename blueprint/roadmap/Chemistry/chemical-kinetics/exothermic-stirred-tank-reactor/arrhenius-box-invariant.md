# Box invariance for the Arrhenius reactor

Invariance for the physical CSTR: for `a > 0`, `B > 0`, `T0 > 0` and `A, b, h, cin, c0 ≥ 0`, every
forward solution of the reactor balances with the Arrhenius rate, from `(c0, T0)`, stays in the box
`[0, cmax] × [Tmin, Tmax]` ([concentration ceiling](concentration-ceiling.md), [temperature
floor](temperature-floor.md) and [ceiling](temperature-ceiling.md)) for all `t ≥ 0`.
