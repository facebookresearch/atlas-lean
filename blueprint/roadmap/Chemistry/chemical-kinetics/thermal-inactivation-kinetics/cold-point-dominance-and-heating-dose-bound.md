# Cold-point dominance and heating dose bound

Under log-linear inactivation, if $T(x, s) \ge T_{\mathrm{cold}}(s)$ at every point and time considered, the decimal reduction at each
point is at least that at the cold point. For Newton heating $T(t) = T_m - (T_m - T_0)e^{-ht}$ with $h > 0$ and $T_m \ge T_0$, the
temperature is nondecreasing and, for $t \ge 0$,

$$F(t) \ge \frac{t}{2}\,10^{(T(t/2) - T_{\mathrm{ref}})/z},$$

by integrating over $[t/2, t]$ only. The bound uses monotone heating and does not apply unchanged to cooling. Conduction problems are
outside this result.
