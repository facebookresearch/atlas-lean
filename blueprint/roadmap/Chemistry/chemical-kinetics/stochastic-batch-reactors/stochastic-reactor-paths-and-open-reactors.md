# Stochastic reactor paths and open reactors

This node records the obligations and open directions around stochastic models of reacting mixtures:

- **Paths of a jump-hold simulator.** For a concrete simulator of a closed stochastic reaction network (exponential holding times
  with propensity-dependent rates, jumps by reaction vectors), construct its path law and show that it does not explode on the
  horizon considered, so that the simulated paths are paths of the continuous-time Markov chain to which the law of large numbers for
  closed networks on a finite horizon applies. Naming a continuous-time Markov chain is not this construction.
- **Open and fed reactors, fluctuations and stationary limits.** Stochastic reactors with inflow and outflow, central limit theorems
  for the fluctuations around the deterministic limit, and stationary or long-time limits are research directions; the law of large
  numbers for closed networks on a finite horizon does not cover them.

## Expansion

A good expansion constructs the jump-hold path law with a nonexplosion condition from a checked source and links the closed-network law
of large numbers, then states any open-reactor or fluctuation result precisely with its hypotheses.
