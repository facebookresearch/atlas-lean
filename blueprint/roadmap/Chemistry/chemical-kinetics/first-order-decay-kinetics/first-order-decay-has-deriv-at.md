# Exponential decay solves the first-order rate law

For all real `k`, `c₀` and `t`, the profile `c(s) = c₀ exp(-k s)` has derivative
`c'(t) = -k c(t)` at `t`: it solves the first-order rate law `c' = -k c`. It describes
nonnegative decay for `k ≥ 0` and `c₀ ≥ 0`; for `k < 0` and `c₀ ≠ 0` its magnitude grows
exponentially instead.
