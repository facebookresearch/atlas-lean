# The reactor starts at its initial concentration

For all real $V$, $Q$, $k$, $c_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $c_0$, the concentration
$c(t) = c_{\mathrm{ss}} + (c_0 - c_{\mathrm{ss}}) e^{-(Q / V + k) t}$ of the continuous stirred-tank
reactor, where $c_{\mathrm{ss}} = Q c_{\mathrm{in}} / (Q + kV)$ and a quotient by zero is read as
$0$, satisfies $c(0) = c_0$.
