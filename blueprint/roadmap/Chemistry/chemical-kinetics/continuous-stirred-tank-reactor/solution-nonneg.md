# The reactor concentration stays nonnegative

If $V > 0$, $Q > 0$, $k \ge 0$, $c_{\mathrm{in}} \ge 0$, $c_0 \ge 0$ and $t \ge 0$, then the
concentration $c(t) = c_{\mathrm{ss}} + (c_0 - c_{\mathrm{ss}}) e^{-(Q / V + k) t}$ of the continuous
stirred-tank reactor is nonnegative: it is a convex combination of $c_{\mathrm{ss}}$ and $c_0$.
