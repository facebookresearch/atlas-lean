# The reactor concentration solves the reactor equation

If $V > 0$, $Q > 0$ and $k \ge 0$, then for all $c_{\mathrm{in}}$, $c_0$ and $t$ the concentration
$c(t) = c_{\mathrm{ss}} + (c_0 - c_{\mathrm{ss}}) e^{-(Q / V + k) t}$ of the continuous stirred-tank
reactor is differentiable at $t$ with

$$c'(t) = \frac{Q}{V} \bigl(c_{\mathrm{in}} - c(t)\bigr) - k\, c(t) .$$
