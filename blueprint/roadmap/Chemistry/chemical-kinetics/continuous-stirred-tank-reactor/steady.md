# Steady concentration of a continuous stirred-tank reactor

For real numbers $V$, $Q$, $k$ and $c_{\mathrm{in}}$, let

$$c_{\mathrm{ss}} = \frac{Q\, c_{\mathrm{in}}}{Q + k V} ,$$

read as $0$ when $Q + kV = 0$. When $V > 0$, $Q > 0$ and $k \ge 0$, it is the steady concentration
of a well-mixed reactor of volume $V$ with volumetric flow $Q$, first-order rate constant $k$ and
inlet concentration $c_{\mathrm{in}}$.
