# The reactor concentration in explicit form

For all real $V$, $Q$, $k$, $c_{\mathrm{in}}$, $c_0$ and $t$, the concentration of the continuous
stirred-tank reactor is

$$c(t) = \frac{Q c_{\mathrm{in}}}{Q + kV} + \Bigl(c_0 - \frac{Q c_{\mathrm{in}}}{Q + kV}\Bigr) e^{-(Q / V + k) t} ,$$

with a quotient by zero read as $0$, as in its definition.
