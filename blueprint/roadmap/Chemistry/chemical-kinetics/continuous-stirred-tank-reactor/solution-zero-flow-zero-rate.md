# A reactor without flow or reaction keeps its concentration

For all real $V$, $c_{\mathrm{in}}$, $c_0$ and $t$, with zero flow $Q = 0$ and zero rate constant
$k = 0$, the concentration of the continuous stirred-tank reactor is $c(t) = c_0$. Here a quotient
by zero is read as $0$, so the steady concentration $Q c_{\mathrm{in}} / (Q + kV)$ is $0$.
