# Exponential law for the deviation from steady state

For all real $V$, $Q$, $k$, $c_{\mathrm{in}}$, $c_0$ and $t$, the concentration $c(t)$ of the
continuous stirred-tank reactor and its steady concentration $c_{\mathrm{ss}}$ satisfy

$$|c(t) - c_{\mathrm{ss}}| = |c_0 - c_{\mathrm{ss}}|\, e^{-(Q / V + k) t} ,$$

with a quotient by zero read as $0$. When $V > 0$, $Q > 0$ and $k \ge 0$, the rate $Q / V + k$ is
positive, so the deviation from steady state decays exponentially as $t$ grows.
