# The reactor equation's right-hand side vanishes at the steady state

For a reactor volume `V > 0`, a flow `Q > 0`, a rate constant `k ≥ 0` and every inlet
concentration `c_in`, the steady concentration `c* = Q c_in / (Q + k V)` makes the right-hand side
of the reactor equation `c' = (Q/V) (c_in - c) - k c` vanish: `(Q/V) (c_in - c*) - k c* = 0`.
