# Reaction vectors of a linkage class lie in its stoichiometric subspace

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set `S`. For every reaction `r` with [linkage class](linkage-class-of.md) `θ`, the reaction
vector `prod(r) - src(r) ∈ ℝ^S` lies in the
[stoichiometric subspace `𝒮_θ` of `θ`](linkage-class-stoichiometric-subspace.md).
