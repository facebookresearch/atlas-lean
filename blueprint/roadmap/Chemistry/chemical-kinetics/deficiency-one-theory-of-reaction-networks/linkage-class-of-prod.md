# The product complex of a reaction lies in its linkage class

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md). For
every reaction `r`, the product complex `prod(r)` lies in the [linkage class](linkage-class-of.md)
of `r`, the connected component of the source complex `src(r)` in the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md).
