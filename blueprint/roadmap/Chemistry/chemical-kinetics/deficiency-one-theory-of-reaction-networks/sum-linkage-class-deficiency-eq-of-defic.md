# In a deficiency-zero network the linkage-class deficiencies sum to δ

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
finite species set `S` and finite reaction set `ρ`. If the
[deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) of `N` is `δ = 0`, then the
[deficiencies `δ_θ` of its linkage classes](linkage-class-deficiency.md) satisfy `∑_θ δ_θ = δ`. As
[every `δ_θ` is then zero](linkage-class-deficiency-eq-zero-of-defi.md), `N` satisfies both
deficiency conditions of Feinberg's deficiency-one theorem: `δ_θ ≤ 1` for every linkage class `θ`,
and `∑_θ δ_θ = δ`.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal
  reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987)
  2229–2268.
