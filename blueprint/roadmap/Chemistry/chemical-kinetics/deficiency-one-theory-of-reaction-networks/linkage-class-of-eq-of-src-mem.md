# Reactions with source complex in a linkage class belong to it

Let `θ` be a linkage class of a
[chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md), a connected
component of its [linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md). Every reaction
`r` whose source complex `src(r)` lies in `θ` has [linkage class](linkage-class-of.md) `θ`.
