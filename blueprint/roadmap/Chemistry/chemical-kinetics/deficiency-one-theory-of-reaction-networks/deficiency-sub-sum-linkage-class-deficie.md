# Deficiency minus the sum of the linkage-class deficiencies

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
finite species set `S` and finite reaction set `ρ`. Its
[deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) `δ` and the
[deficiencies `δ_θ` of its linkage classes](linkage-class-deficiency.md) satisfy, in the integers,

`δ - ∑_θ δ_θ = ∑_θ s_θ - s`,

where `s` is the dimension of the
[stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md) of `N` and
`s_θ` that of the [stoichiometric subspace of `θ`](linkage-class-stoichiometric-subspace.md), both
over `ℝ`.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal
  reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987)
  2229–2268.
