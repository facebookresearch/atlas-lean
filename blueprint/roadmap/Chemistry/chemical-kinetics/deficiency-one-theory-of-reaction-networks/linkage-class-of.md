# Linkage class of a reaction

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set `S` and reaction set `ρ`. The linkage class of a reaction `r ∈ ρ` is the connected
component of its source complex `src(r)` in the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md) of `N`. For a linkage class `θ`,
the reactions whose linkage class is `θ` are called the reactions of `θ`. The product complex
`prod(r)` [lies in the same linkage class](linkage-class-of-prod.md).
