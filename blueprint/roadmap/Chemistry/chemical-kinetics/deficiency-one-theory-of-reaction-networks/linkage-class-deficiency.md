# Deficiency of a linkage class

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set `S`, reaction set `ρ` and finitely many
[complexes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md), and let `θ` be a linkage class of `N`,
a connected component of its [linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md).
The deficiency of `θ` is the integer `δ_θ = n_θ - 1 - s_θ`, where `n_θ` is the number of complexes
in `θ` and `s_θ` is the dimension over `ℝ` of the
[stoichiometric subspace `𝒮_θ` of `θ`](linkage-class-stoichiometric-subspace.md). This dimension is
finite, since `𝒮_θ` is spanned by differences of the finitely many complexes.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal
  reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987)
  2229–2268.
- M. Feinberg, *The existence and uniqueness of steady states for a class of chemical reaction
  networks*, Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 132 (1995) 311–370.
