# The stoichiometric subspace is the sum of the linkage-class subspaces

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set `S` and reaction set `ρ`; no finiteness is assumed. Its
[stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md) `𝒮 ⊆ ℝ^S` is
the sum of the
[stoichiometric subspaces `𝒮_θ` of its linkage classes](linkage-class-stoichiometric-subspace.md),

`𝒮 = ∑_θ 𝒮_θ`,

that is, the smallest subspace containing every `𝒮_θ`, which consists of the finite sums of vectors
of the `𝒮_θ`.
