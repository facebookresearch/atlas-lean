# The number of complexes is the sum of the linkage-class sizes

Let `N` be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
finitely many [complexes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md). The number `n` of
complexes is the sum, over the linkage classes `θ` (the connected components of the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md)), of the number `n_θ` of
complexes in `θ`:

`n = ∑_θ n_θ`.
