# Difference of two products with factors bounded by M ≥ 1

Let $s$ be a finite subset of an index set $\iota$, let $a, b \colon \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, and let
$M \ge 1$ be such that $|a_i| \le M$ and $|b_i| \le M$ for every $i \in s$. Then

$$\left|\prod_{i \in s} a_i - \prod_{i \in s} b_i\right| \le M^{|s|} \sum_{i \in s} |a_i - b_i|,$$

where $|s|$ is the number of elements of $s$. For $s$ empty both sides are $0$.
