# Positive recurrence: integrable first cycle length

For a measure `P` on `Ω` and random times `R_0, R_1, … : Ω → ℝ`, positive recurrence means that
the first [cycle length](cycle-length.md) `R_1 - R_0` is `P`-integrable: measurable up to a
`P`-null set, with `∫ |R_1 - R_0| dP < ∞`. When `P` is a probability measure and `R_0 < R_1`, it
says that the mean length `E[R_1 - R_0]` of the first cycle is finite. In a
[regeneration structure](is-regenerative.md) every cycle length has the law of the first, and
when the `R_k` are the successive entrance times into a state `x₀`, positive recurrence is the
finiteness of the mean return time to `x₀`.
