# Length of a regeneration cycle

For random times `R_0, R_1, R_2, … : Ω → ℝ`, the length of the `k`-th cycle is the random
variable

`L_k(ω) = R_{k+1}(ω) - R_k(ω)`.

The definition assumes no ordering of the times; for the strictly increasing times of a
[regeneration structure](is-regenerative.md) every `L_k` is positive.
