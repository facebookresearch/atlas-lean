# Differential equation of a zero-order rate law

Zero-order rate law from the extent convention (`ν = -1`), in a reactor of volume `V > 0`: if the
concentration of `A` is `c(s) = [A]₀ - (ξ(s) - ξ(0)) / V` for every `s`, the extent `ξ` is
differentiable at `t` and the rate of reaction is `v(t) = k`, then `c` has derivative `-k` at `t`.
