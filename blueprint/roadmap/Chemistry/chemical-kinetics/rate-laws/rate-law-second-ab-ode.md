# Differential equations of the second-order law A + B → P

Second-order rate law for `A + B → P` from the extent convention (`ν = -1` for both), in a reactor
of volume `V > 0`: if the concentrations are `a(s) = [A]₀ - (ξ(s) - ξ(0)) / V` and
`b(s) = [B]₀ - (ξ(s) - ξ(0)) / V` for every `s`, the extent `ξ` is differentiable at `t` and the
rate of reaction is `v(t) = k a(t) b(t)`, then both `a` and `b` have derivative `-k a(t) b(t)` at
`t`.
