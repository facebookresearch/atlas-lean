# Differential equation of the second-order law 2A → P

Second-order rate law for `2A → P` from the extent convention (`ν = -2`), in a reactor of volume
`V > 0`: if the concentration of `A` is `c(s) = [A]₀ - 2 (ξ(s) - ξ(0)) / V` for every `s`, the
extent `ξ` is differentiable at `t` and the rate of reaction is `v(t) = k c(t)²`, then `c` has
derivative `-(2k) c(t)²` at `t`.
