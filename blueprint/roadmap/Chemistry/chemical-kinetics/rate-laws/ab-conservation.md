# Every A + B solution conserves the concentration difference

If `gA` and `gB` satisfy the `A + B → P` rate equations `gA' = gB' = -k gA gB` at every `t ≥ 0`,
with `gA(0) = [A]₀` and `gB(0) = [B]₀`, then `gB(t) - gA(t) = [B]₀ - [A]₀` for every `t ≥ 0`: the
difference of the two concentrations keeps its initial value.
