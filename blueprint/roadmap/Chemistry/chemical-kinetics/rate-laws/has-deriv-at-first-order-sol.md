# The exponential A₀ e^(−kt) satisfies f′ = −k f at every t

The first-order solution `f(t) = [A]₀ exp(-kt)` satisfies `f' = -k f` at every `t`, for all real
`[A]₀` and `k` (the one-compartment model at infusion rate `0`). With `k = -K` and `[A]₀ = a₀` it is
the [logarithmic-branch Paris crack](../../../Physics/elasticity-and-structures/fracture-fatigue-crack-growth-and-failure-margin/paris-crack-exp.md)
`a(N) = a₀ exp(K N)`, which therefore solves `da/dN = K a` at every cycle count `N`: the reduced
Paris law for the growth exponent `m = 2`, with `K` the aggregate growth coefficient, not a stress
intensity.
