# Scalar rate group of an order-n rate law

The scalar `θ = k * t * [A]₀ ^ (n - 1)` through which the integrated laws of order `n` depend on
`k`, `t` and `[A]₀` (see the [zero-order](zero-order-sol-rate-group.md),
[first-order](first-order-sol-rate-group.md) and [2A → P](second-order-aasol-rate-group.md)
solutions in terms of the rate group). This is a normalization identity in real arithmetic, without
dimensions or units. At order `0` it divides by `[A]₀`, so it is meaningful for `[A]₀ > 0`; at
order `0` and `[A]₀ = 0` the convention `0⁻¹ = 0` makes it `0`.
