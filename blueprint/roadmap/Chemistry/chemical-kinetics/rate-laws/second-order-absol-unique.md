# Uniqueness of the A + B solution for unequal amounts

For `k > 0`, `[A]₀ > 0`, `[B]₀ > 0` and `[A]₀ ≠ [B]₀`, if `g_A' = g_B' = -k g_A g_B` on `t ≥ 0`,
`g_A` and `g_B` stay positive there and `g_A(0) = [A]₀`, `g_B(0) = [B]₀`, then `g_A` is the
[solution for A of the A + B → P law](second-order-absol-a.md) with unequal amounts on `t ≥ 0`; the
proof goes through the ratio `g_A / g_B`.
