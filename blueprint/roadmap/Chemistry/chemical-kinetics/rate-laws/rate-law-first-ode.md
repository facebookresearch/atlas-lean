# Differential equation of a first-order rate law

First-order rate law from the extent convention (`ν = -1`): if the concentration of `A` is
`c(s) = [A]₀ - (ξ(s) - ξ(0)) / V` for every `s`, the extent `ξ` is differentiable at `t` and the
rate of reaction is `v(t) = k c(t)`, then `c` has derivative `-k c(t)` at `t`.
