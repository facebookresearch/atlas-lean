# Laplace transform of the rate-one exponential law at $a \ge 0$

Let $\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ be the exponential law of rate $1$ on $\mathbb{R}$, with density
$e^{-t}$ for $t \ge 0$ and $0$ for $t < 0$. For every real $a \ge 0$,

$$\int e^{-a t} \, d\operatorname{Exp}(1)(t) = \frac{1}{1 + a},$$

a lower Lebesgue integral of a positive function, with values in $[0, \infty]$, here finite. So a
rate-$1$ exponential clock $E$ has $\mathbb{E}\bigl[e^{-a E}\bigr] = 1 / (1 + a)$ for $a \ge 0$.
