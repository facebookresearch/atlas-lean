# Total propensity of a stochastic reaction network

Let $N$ be a [stochastic chemical reaction network](stochastic-crn.md) on a set $S$ of species with
a finite set $\rho$ of reactions and propensities $\lambda_r$. Its total propensity at a state
$x \in \mathbb{N}^S$ is

$$\lambda(x) = \sum_{r \in \rho} \lambda_r(x).$$
