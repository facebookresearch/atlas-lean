# Each clock of the step sequence has the rate-one exponential law

Let $S$ be a set with a σ-algebra, $P$ a jump kernel on $S$ (a probability mass function
$P(x, \cdot)$ on $S$ for each $x \in S$), and $\mathbb{P}_P$ the [input law](input-measure.md) on
step sequences $\omega = ((\xi_n, E_n))_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the image
of $\mathbb{P}_P$ under $\omega \mapsto E_k$ is the exponential law $\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ of rate
$1$, with density $e^{-t}$ for $t \ge 0$ and $0$ for $t < 0$: under the input law every clock $E_k$
is a rate-$1$ exponential clock.
