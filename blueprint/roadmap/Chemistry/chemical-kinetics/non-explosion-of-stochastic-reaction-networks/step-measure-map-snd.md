# The clock of one step has the rate-one exponential law

Let $S$ be a set with a σ-algebra, $P$ a jump kernel on $S$ (a probability mass function
$P(x, \cdot)$ on $S$ for each $x \in S$), and $\mu_P$ the [law of one step](step-measure.md)
$(\xi, E)$. The image of $\mu_P$ under the clock coordinate $(\xi, E) \mapsto E$ is the exponential
law $\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ of rate $1$, with density $e^{-t}$ for $t \ge 0$ and $0$ for $t < 0$.
