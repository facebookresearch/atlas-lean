# Positive rates and nonnegative clocks give nonnegative jump times

Let $S$ be a set, $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$ with $q(x) > 0$ for every $x \in S$, and
$\omega = ((\xi_k, E_k))_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ a step sequence with $E_k \ge 0$ for every $k$. Then
for every $x \in S$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the [jump time](jump-time.md) satisfies
$T_n(x, \omega) \ge 0$, as every holding time $E_k / q(Y_k)$ is nonnegative. Under the
[input law](input-measure.md) the clocks are [almost surely nonnegative](ae-clock-nonneg.md).
