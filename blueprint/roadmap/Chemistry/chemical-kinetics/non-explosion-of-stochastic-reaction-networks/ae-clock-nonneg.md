# Almost surely every clock of the step sequence is nonnegative

Let $S$ be a set with a σ-algebra, $P$ a jump kernel on $S$ (a probability mass function
$P(x, \cdot)$ on $S$ for each $x \in S$), and $\mathbb{P}_P$ the [input law](input-measure.md) on
step sequences $\omega = ((\xi_k, E_k))_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$. For $\mathbb{P}_P$-almost every
$\omega$, $E_k \ge 0$ for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Indeed each clock $E_k$ has the
[rate-one exponential law](input-measure-map-clock.md), which gives $(-\infty, 0)$ mass $0$, and
there are countably many clocks.
