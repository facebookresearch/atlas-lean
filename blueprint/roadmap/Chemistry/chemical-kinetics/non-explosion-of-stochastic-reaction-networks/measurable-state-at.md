# On a countable space the state at time $s$ is jointly measurable

Let $S$ be a countable set with a σ-algebra in which every singleton, hence every subset, is
measurable, $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$ any function, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in S$. The map
$(s, \omega) \mapsto X^{(n)}_s(x, \omega)$, the
[state at time $s$ of the chain stopped after $n$ jumps](state-at.md), is measurable on
$\mathbb{R} \times \Omega$, where $\Omega$ is the space of step sequences
$\omega = ((\xi_k, E_k))_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ with $\xi_k : S \to S$ and $E_k \in \mathbb{R}$, for
the product of the Borel σ-algebra on $\mathbb{R}$ and the product σ-algebra on $\Omega$.
