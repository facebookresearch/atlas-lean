# For $0 \le s < T_n$, the path at time $s$ is the state after $n$ jumps

Let $S$ be a set, $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$, $x \in S$, $\omega$ a step sequence and
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, with [jump times](jump-time.md) $T_k = T_k(x, \omega)$. If $0 \le s < T_n$, the
[state at time $s$ of the chain stopped after $n$ jumps](state-at.md) is the [path](path.md) at time
$s$:

$$X^{(n)}_s(x, \omega) = X_s(x, \omega).$$

No sign condition on $q$ or on the clocks is needed.
