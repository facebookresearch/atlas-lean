# On a countable space the stopped discounted process is measurable

Let $S$ be a countable set with a σ-algebra in which every singleton, hence every subset, is
measurable. Let $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $V : S \to [0, \infty]$ be any functions,
$c \in \mathbb{R}$ and $A \subseteq S$, and let $D_n$ be the
[stopped discounted process](discounted-stop.md). For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in S$, the
map $\omega \mapsto D_n(x, \omega)$ is measurable, for the product σ-algebra on step sequences
$\omega = ((\xi_k, E_k))_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$, with $\xi_k : S \to S$ and $E_k \in \mathbb{R}$, and
the Borel σ-algebra on $[0, \infty]$.
