# At constant volume $V_0$ with no flows, the balance is $V_0 c_i' = G$

Let $V_0$ be a real number (the constant volume) and let $c_i$ have derivative $c_i'$ at the instant
$t$. For every real $G$ (the net generation rate of species $i$), the amount $n_i = V_0 c_i$ has
derivative $G$ at $t$, which is the species balance of a closed reactor of constant volume, with no
inflow or outflow, if and only if

$$V_0\, c_i' = G .$$

It is the special case of the [balance in concentration form](stock-iff-concentration.md) with
constant volume and zero flows. In the physical reading $V_0 > 0$; the equivalence holds for every
real $V_0$, and at $V_0 = 0$ both sides say $G = 0$.
