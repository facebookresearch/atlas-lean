# At nonzero volume, the balance gives the concentration derivative

Let $V$ have derivative $V'$ and $c_i$ derivative $c_i'$ at the instant $t$, with $V(t) \ne 0$, and
let $F_{\mathrm{in}}$, $F_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $G$ be real numbers (the inflow, outflow and net
generation rates of species $i$). If the amount $n_i = V c_i$ has derivative
$F_{\mathrm{in}} - F_{\mathrm{out}} + G$ at $t$, then

$$c_i' = \frac{F_{\mathrm{in}} - F_{\mathrm{out}} + G - V' c_i(t)}{V(t)} .$$

The hypothesis $V(t) \ne 0$ is needed: at $V(t) = 0$ the balance reduces to
$V' c_i(t) = F_{\mathrm{in}} - F_{\mathrm{out}} + G$ and puts no condition on $c_i'$. In the
physical reading the volume is positive.
