# Product rule for the amount $V c_i$ of a species at one instant

Let $V$ (the volume) and $c_i$ (the concentration of species $i$, from a family indexed by any set)
be real functions of time. If $V$ has derivative $V'$ and $c_i$ has derivative $c_i'$ at the instant
$t$, then the amount $n_i = V c_i$ has derivative

$$n_i'(t) = V' c_i(t) + V(t)\, c_i'$$

at $t$. In the physical reading $V > 0$ and $c_i \ge 0$; the rule holds for all real functions.
