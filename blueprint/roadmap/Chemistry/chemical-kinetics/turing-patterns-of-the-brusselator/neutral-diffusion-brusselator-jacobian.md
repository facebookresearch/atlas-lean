# The neutral diffusion of the Brusselator Jacobian in closed form

For all real $a$, $b$, $d_u$ and $q$, the
[neutral diffusion](../../../Analysis/partial-differential-equations/bifurcation-of-turing-pattern-branches/neutral-diffusion.md)
$(\det J - d_u g_v q) / (f_u q - d_u q^2)$ of the [Brusselator Jacobian](brusselator-jacobian.md)
$J$, where $f_u = b - 1$ and $g_v = -a^2$ are its diagonal entries and $\det J = a^2$, equals

$$\frac{a^2 (1 + d_u q)}{(b - 1) q - d_u q^2},$$

the [neutral inhibitor diffusion of the Brusselator](brusselator-neutral-diffusion.md). Both
quotients use the convention $x / 0 = 0$ and have the same denominator, so the identity also holds
where the denominator vanishes.
