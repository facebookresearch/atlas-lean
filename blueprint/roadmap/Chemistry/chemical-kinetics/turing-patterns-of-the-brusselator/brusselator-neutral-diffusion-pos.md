# With a ≠ 0, d_u ≥ 0, the neutral diffusion is positive when admissible

Let $a \ne 0$ and $b$ be real, $d_u \ge 0$ and $q \ge 0$, with $(b - 1) q - d_u q^2 > 0$. Then the
[neutral inhibitor diffusion](brusselator-neutral-diffusion.md)
$d_n(q) = a^2 (1 + d_u q) / ((b - 1) q - d_u q^2)$ is positive. The condition $a \ne 0$ is needed:
at $a = 0$ the numerator, and hence $d_n(q)$, is $0$.
