# For q > 0, a squared wavenumber is admissible iff d_u q < b − 1

For real numbers $b$ and $d_u$ and every $q > 0$, $(b - 1) q - d_u q^2 > 0$ if and only if
$d_u q < b - 1$. A squared wavenumber $q$ with $(b - 1) q - d_u q^2 > 0$ is admissible: the
denominator of the [neutral inhibitor diffusion](brusselator-neutral-diffusion.md) is then positive.
The condition $q > 0$ is needed: at $q = 0$ the first inequality fails, while the second holds
whenever $b > 1$.
