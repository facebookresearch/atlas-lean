# Jacobian of the Brusselator at its steady state

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the real $2 \times 2$ matrix

$$J = \begin{pmatrix} b - 1 & a^2 \\ -b & -a^2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

It is defined for all real $a$ and $b$. For $a \ne 0$ it is the Jacobian matrix of the
[Brusselator kinetics](brusselator.md) at the [steady state](brusselator-steady-state.md)
$(a, b / a)$ ([Jacobian at the steady state](jacobian-matrix-brusselator.md)).
