# Homogeneous steady state of the Brusselator

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the point $(u_0, v_0) = (a, b / a)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$. For $a \ne 0$
it is the homogeneous steady state of the [Brusselator kinetics](brusselator.md), and the only zero
of the kinetics: adding the two components gives $a - u = 0$, and then $g = 0$ forces $v = b / a$.
With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, at $a = 0$ the point is $(0, 0)$. In the model $a > 0$.
