# Turing patterns of the Brusselator

The Brusselator of Prigogine and Lefever, with kinetics $f(u, v) = a - (b + 1) u + u^2 v$ and
$g(u, v) = b u - u^2 v$ for the activator $u$ and the inhibitor $v$ at fixed pool concentrations $a$
and $b$: its steady state and Jacobian, its Turing threshold, and a pattern branch obtained from the
Lyapunov–Schmidt reduction. Each page states the conditions on $a$, $b$ and the diffusion
coefficients that its result needs.
