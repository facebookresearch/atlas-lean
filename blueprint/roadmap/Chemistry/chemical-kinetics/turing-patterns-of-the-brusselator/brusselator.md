# Brusselator kinetics

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the Brusselator kinetics is the map
$(u, v) \mapsto (f(u, v), g(u, v))$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with

$$f(u, v) = a - (b + 1) u + u^2 v, \qquad g(u, v) = b u - u^2 v.$$

It is the kinetics of the Prigogine–Lefever reaction scheme $A \to X$, $B + X \to Y + D$,
$2X + Y \to 3X$, $X \to E$, with $u$ and $v$ the concentrations of the activator $X$ and the
inhibitor $Y$ and with the pool concentrations $a$ and $b$ of $A$ and $B$ held fixed. In the model
$a, b > 0$ and $u, v \ge 0$; the map is defined for all real $a$, $b$, $u$ and $v$.
