# The Brusselator kinetics vanish at the steady state

For all real $a$ and $b$, the [Brusselator kinetics](brusselator.md) vanish at the
[steady state](brusselator-steady-state.md) $(a, b / a)$: $f(a, b / a) = 0$ and $g(a, b / a) = 0$.
No condition on $a$ is needed: at $a = 0$ the steady state is $(0, 0)$ by the convention
$x / 0 = 0$, and both components of the kinetics vanish there as well.
