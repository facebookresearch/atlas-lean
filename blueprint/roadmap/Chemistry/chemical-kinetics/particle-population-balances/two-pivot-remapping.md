# Two-pivot remapping

For pivots $0 < m_1 < m_2$ and a particle mass $m \in [m_1, m_2]$, assigning the weights $w_1 = (m_2 - m)/(m_2 - m_1)$ and
$w_2 = (m - m_1)/(m_2 - m_1)$ to the two pivots preserves number and mass: $w_1, w_2 \ge 0$, $w_1 + w_2 = 1$ and
$w_1 m_1 + w_2 m_2 = m$. The second moment increases, $w_1 m_1^2 + w_2 m_2^2 - m^2 = (m - m_1)(m_2 - m) \ge 0$, strictly for
$m_1 < m < m_2$. These are moments of the remapping, not conservation of particle number under aggregation; for $m$ outside the
pivot interval one weight is negative.
