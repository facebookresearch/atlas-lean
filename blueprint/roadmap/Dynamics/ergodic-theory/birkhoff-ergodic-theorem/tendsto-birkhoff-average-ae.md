# Birkhoff averages converge almost everywhere to the Birkhoff limit

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $X$, $T : X \to X$ a measurable map preserving $\mu$, and $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$
an integrable function. Then for $\mu$-almost every $x$,

$$\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} f(T^k x) \longrightarrow \bar f(x) \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\bar f$ is the [Birkhoff limit](birkhoff-limit.md) of $f$.
