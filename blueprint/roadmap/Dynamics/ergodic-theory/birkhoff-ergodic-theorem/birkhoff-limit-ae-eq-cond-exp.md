# Birkhoff limit is a conditional expectation on invariant sets

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $X$, $T : X \to X$ a measurable map preserving $\mu$, and $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$
an integrable function. Then the [Birkhoff limit](birkhoff-limit.md) $\bar f$ of $f$ is $\mu$-almost everywhere
equal to the conditional expectation $\mathbb{E}_\mu[f \mid \mathcal{I}]$ of $f$ given the σ-algebra $\mathcal{I}$
of measurable sets $E$ with $T^{-1} E = E$.
