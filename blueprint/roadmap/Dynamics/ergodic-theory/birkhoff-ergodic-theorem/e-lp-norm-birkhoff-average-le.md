# Birkhoff averages do not increase the $L^1$ norm

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $X$, $T : X \to X$ a measurable map preserving $\mu$, and $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$
an integrable function. For every $n \ge 0$, the Birkhoff average $A_n f(x) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} f(T^k x)$ ($A_0 f = 0$) satisfies

$$\int |A_n f| \, d\mu \le \int |f| \, d\mu.$$

The same bound for differences of averages is
[Birkhoff averages are $L^1$-contractions](integral-norm-birkhoff-average-sub-le.md).
