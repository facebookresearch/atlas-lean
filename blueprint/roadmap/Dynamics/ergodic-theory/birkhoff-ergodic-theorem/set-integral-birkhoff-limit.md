# Birkhoff limit preserves integrals over invariant sets

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $X$, $T : X \to X$ a measurable map preserving $\mu$, and $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$
an integrable function. For every measurable [invariant set](is-invariant-set.md) $E$,

$$\int_E f \, d\mu = \int_E \bar f \, d\mu,$$

where $\bar f$ is the [Birkhoff limit](birkhoff-limit.md) of $f$.
