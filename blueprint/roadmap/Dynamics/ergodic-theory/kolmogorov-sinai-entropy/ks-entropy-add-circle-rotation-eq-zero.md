# The irrational rotation has entropy zero

Let $\alpha$ be irrational. The rotation $x \mapsto x + \alpha$ of the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$, with its Haar
probability measure (Lebesgue measure), has Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy $0$.

Irrational rotations are ergodic but not mixing, and their entropy is zero: entropy is blind to this kind of
recurrent, "deterministic" behaviour, in contrast with the [Bernoulli shifts](of-real-le-ks-entropy-shift.md).
In the source the proof goes through generators: the irrational rotation is an invertible transformation
with a strong generator, and such a transformation has entropy zero.

## Informally uses

- [Sinai's generator theorem](ks-entropy-eq-entropy-rel-of-is-generato.md): the source's proof computes the
