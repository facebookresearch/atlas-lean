# Relative entropy is bounded by the Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy

Let $T : X \to X$ be a map of a probability space $(X, \mu)$. For every
[measurable partition modulo null sets](is-measurable-partition.md) $\xi = (C_1, \dots, C_r)$ with finitely many
atoms,

$$h_\mu(T, \xi) \le h_\mu(T),$$

since the [Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy](ks-entropy.md) is the supremum of the
[relative entropies](entropy-rel.md) over such partitions.
