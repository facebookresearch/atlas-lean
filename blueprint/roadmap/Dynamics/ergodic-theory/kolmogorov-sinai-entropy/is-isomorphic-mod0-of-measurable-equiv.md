# A conjugacy by a measure-preserving measurable equivalence is an isomorphism modulo null sets

Let $(X, \mu)$ and $(Y, \nu)$ be measure spaces, $T : X \to X$ and $S : Y \to Y$ maps, and $e : X \to Y$ a
measurable bijection with measurable inverse that is measure-preserving ($\nu$ is the image of $\mu$ under $e$)
and conjugates the dynamics everywhere: $e \circ T = S \circ e$. Then $(X, \mu, T)$ and $(Y, \nu, S)$ are
[isomorphic modulo null sets](is-isomorphic-mod0.md), with $\pi = e$ and $\rho = e^{-1}$.
