# Refinements of the time-zero partition are cylinders

Let $\sigma$ be the [left shift](../mixing/shift.md) on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ and $\xi$ the
[time-zero partition](time-zero-partition.md). For every $n \ge 0$ and every word $w = (w_0, \dots, w_{n-1}) \in S^n$,
the atom of $w$ in the [dynamical refinement](iterate-join.md) $\xi \vee \sigma^{-1}\xi \vee \dots \vee
\sigma^{-(n-1)}\xi$ is the cylinder

$$\{x \in S^{\mathbb{N}} : x_k = w_k \text{ for } 0 \le k < n\}.$$
