# Dynamical refinement of a partition

Let $T : X \to X$ be a map, $\xi = (C_i)_{i \in I}$ a family of subsets of $X$ and $n \ge 0$. The dynamical
refinement $\xi \vee T^{-1}\xi \vee \dots \vee T^{-(n-1)}\xi$ is the family indexed by words
$w = (w_0, \dots, w_{n-1}) \in I^n$ whose atom of $w$ is

$$C_w = \bigcap_{k=0}^{n-1} T^{-k} C_{w_k} = \{x \in X : T^k x \in C_{w_k} \text{ for } 0 \le k < n\},$$

the set of points whose $\xi$-itinerary begins with $w$. For $n = 0$ the only word is the empty word, whose atom
is $X$.
