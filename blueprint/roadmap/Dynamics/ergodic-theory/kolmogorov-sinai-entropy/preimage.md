# Pullback of a measurable partition

Let $\pi : (X, \mu) \to (Y, \nu)$ be a measure-preserving map and $\eta = (D_i)_{i \in I}$, with $I$ countable, a
[measurable partition of $(Y, \nu)$ modulo null sets](is-measurable-partition.md). Then the preimages
$(\pi^{-1} D_i)_{i \in I}$ form a measurable partition of $(X, \mu)$ modulo null sets.
