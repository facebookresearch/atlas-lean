# The time-zero partition is a measurable partition

Let $S$ be a measurable space in which singletons are measurable. For every measure $\mu$ on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ (with
the product σ-algebra), the [time-zero partition](time-zero-partition.md) $([s])_{s \in S}$,
$[s] = \{x : x_0 = s\}$, is a [measurable partition of $(S^{\mathbb{N}}, \mu)$ modulo null sets](is-measurable-partition.md):
its atoms are measurable, pairwise disjoint, and cover $S^{\mathbb{N}}$.
