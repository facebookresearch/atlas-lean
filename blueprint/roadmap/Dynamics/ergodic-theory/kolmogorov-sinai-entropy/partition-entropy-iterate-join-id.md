# Refinements under the identity

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $X$ and $\xi = (C_1, \dots, C_r)$ a
[measurable partition of $(X, \mu)$ modulo null sets](is-measurable-partition.md). For every $n \ge 1$, the
[dynamical refinement](iterate-join.md) $\xi \vee \xi \vee \dots \vee \xi$ ($n$ copies) of $\xi$ under the identity
map has the same [entropy](partition-entropy.md) as $\xi$:

$$H_\mu(\xi \vee \dots \vee \xi) = H_\mu(\xi).$$

The atom of a constant word $(i, \dots, i)$ is $C_i$, and the atom of a non-constant word is contained in the
intersection of two distinct atoms, a null set.
