# Entropy is invariant under conjugacy by a measure-preserving measurable equivalence

Let $(X, \mu, T)$ and $(Y, \nu, S)$ be dynamical systems on probability spaces with $T$ and $S$ measurable, and
let $e : X \to Y$ be a
measurable bijection with measurable inverse that carries $\mu$ to $\nu$ and conjugates the dynamics
everywhere, $e \circ T = S \circ e$. Then $h_\mu(T) = h_\nu(S)$.

Each inequality is the statement that [a factor has at most the entropy of the system](ks-entropy-le-of-factor.md),
applied to $e$ and to $e^{-1}$. An everywhere conjugacy is much more than measure-theoretic isomorphism: it
must, for instance, match fixed points, which form null sets for Bernoulli measures. Sinai's statement that
entropy is a metric invariant concerns isomorphism modulo null sets, and is
[Entropy is an invariant of isomorphism modulo null sets](ks-entropy-eq-of-is-isomorphic-mod0.md).
