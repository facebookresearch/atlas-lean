# Measure of a cylinder under a product measure

Let $S$ be a measurable space in which singletons are measurable, and $p$ a probability measure on $S$. For every
$n \ge 0$ and every word $w = (w_0, \dots, w_{n-1}) \in S^n$, the cylinder of $w$ has product measure

$$p^{\mathbb{N}}\bigl(\{x \in S^{\mathbb{N}} : x_k = w_k \text{ for } 0 \le k < n\}\bigr) = p(\{w_0\}) \cdots p(\{w_{n-1}\}).$$

For $n = 0$ the cylinder is the whole space and the product is empty, equal to $1$.
