# Atoms of the dynamical refinement are measurable

Let $T : X \to X$ be a measurable map and $\xi = (C_i)_{i \in I}$ a family of measurable subsets of $X$. Then for
every $n \ge 0$ and every word $w \in I^n$, the atom $C_w = \bigcap_{k < n} T^{-k} C_{w_k}$ of the
[dynamical refinement](iterate-join.md) $\xi \vee T^{-1}\xi \vee \dots \vee T^{-(n-1)}\xi$ is measurable.
