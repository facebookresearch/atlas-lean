# Dynamical refinement commutes with pullback, almost everywhere

Let $(X, \mu)$ be a measure space, $T : X \to X$ a quasi-measure-preserving map ($T$ is measurable and
$\mu(T^{-1} A) = 0$ whenever $\mu(A) = 0$), $S : Y \to Y$ a map of a set $Y$, and $\pi : X \to Y$ a map with
$\pi \circ T = S \circ \pi$ $\mu$-almost everywhere. Let $\eta = (D_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of subsets of $Y$,
$n \ge 0$ and $w \in I^n$ a word. Then the atom of $w$ in the [dynamical refinement](iterate-join.md) of
$\pi^{-1}\eta = (\pi^{-1} D_i)_{i \in I}$ under $T$ agrees $\mu$-almost everywhere with the preimage under $\pi$ of
the atom of $w$ in the dynamical refinement of $\eta$ under $S$.

The semiconjugacy [passes to the iterates](iterate-comp-ae-eq.md) $T^k$, $k < n$, almost everywhere.
