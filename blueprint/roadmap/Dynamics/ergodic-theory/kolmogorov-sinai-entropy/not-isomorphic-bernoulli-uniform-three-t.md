# Kolmogorov's non-isomorphism of Bernoulli schemes

The one-sided Bernoulli schemes with probability vectors $(\tfrac13, \tfrac13, \tfrac13)$ on $\{0, 1, 2\}^{\mathbb{N}}$
and $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ on $\{0, 1\}^{\mathbb{N}}$ are not measure-theoretically isomorphic, even [modulo null sets](is-isomorphic-mod0.md): no pair of
measure-preserving maps, inverse to each other almost everywhere, intertwines the two shifts almost everywhere.
Their entropies are $\log 3$ and $\log 2$.

Fixed and periodic points form null sets for these measures, so counting them gives nothing; entropy is the
invariant that separates the two schemes. Before entropy, all Bernoulli schemes were known to be spectrally isomorphic, and whether they are all
isomorphic was open; entropy settled the question. Ornstein later showed that entropy is a complete
invariant among Bernoulli schemes.
