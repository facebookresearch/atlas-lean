# Koopman operator

Let $T$ be a measurable map preserving a measure $\mu$ on $X$. The Koopman operator of $T$ is the operator

$$U_T : L^2(\mu) \to L^2(\mu), \qquad U_T f = f \circ T,$$

on the real space $L^2(\mu)$ of square-integrable functions modulo null functions. It is well defined because $T$
preserves $\mu$, and it is a linear isometry, in particular a continuous linear operator.
