# The two-shift factors onto the three-shift over every countable non-amenable group

Bowen proved that for every countable non-amenable group all Bernoulli shifts are
weakly isomorphic (each factors onto the other); in particular the uniform 2-shift factors onto the
uniform 3-shift.

## Sources

- M. Abért, Some questions (2010), Question 34, https://www.renyi.hu/~abert/questions.pdf; resolution: L. Bowen, Finitary random interlacements and the Gaboriau-Lyons problem, Geom. Funct. Anal. 29 (2019), 659-689, arXiv:1707.09573, Corollary 1.3: "Let Γ be any countable non-amenable group. Then all Bernoulli shifts over Γ are weakly isomorphic", i.e. factor onto each other (Ornstein–Weiss had the 2-shift onto the 4-shift for the free group of rank 2), https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.09573.
