# Frantzikinakis’s Problem 17 on syndetic multiple recurrence

The statement that for every $\ell$, every probability space, all commuting measure-preserving $\mathbb{Z}$-actions $T_1, \dots, T_\ell$ and all rationally independent $p_1, \dots, p_\ell \in \mathbb{Z}[t]$ with zero constant terms, every measurable $A$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ admit a syndetic set of $n \ge 1$ with $\mu(A \cap T_1^{-p_1(n)} A \cap \dots \cap T_\ell^{-p_\ell(n)} A) \ge \mu(A)^{\ell + 1} - \varepsilon$.

## Sources

- N. Frantzikinakis, Some open problems on multiple ergodic averages, Bull. Hellenic Math. Soc. 60 (2016), 41-90, arXiv:1103.3808, Problem 17.
