# Syndetic multiple recurrence along rationally independent polynomials

Let $\ell \ge 0$ and let $p_1, \dots, p_\ell \in \mathbb{Z}[t]$ be rationally independent (if $\sum_i c_i p_i$ is constant for integers $c_i$, then all $c_i = 0$) with zero constant terms. Let $(X, \mu)$ be a probability space and $T_1, \dots, T_\ell$ commuting measure-preserving actions of $\mathbb{Z}$ on $X$ ($T_i^0 = \mathrm{id}$, $T_i^{a+b} = T_i^a T_i^b$, and $T_i^a T_j^b = T_j^b T_i^a$). Then for every measurable $A \subseteq X$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, the set of $n \ge 1$ with

$$\mu\bigl(A \cap T_1^{-p_1(n)} A \cap \dots \cap T_\ell^{-p_\ell(n)} A\bigr) \ge \mu(A)^{\ell + 1} - \varepsilon$$

is syndetic (has bounded gaps). For $\ell = 0$ this is trivial: the intersection is $A$ and every $n \ge 1$ qualifies. For $\ell \ge 1$, Frantzikinakis and Kuca proved it, answering Problem 17.

- N. Frantzikinakis, Some open problems on multiple ergodic averages, Bull. Hellenic Math. Soc. 60 (2016), 41-90, arXiv:1103.3808, Problem 17.
- N. Frantzikinakis and B. Kuca, Joint ergodicity for commuting transformations and applications to polynomial sequences, Invent. Math. 239 (2025), 621-706, arXiv:2207.12288, Corollary 2.11.
