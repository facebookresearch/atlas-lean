# Logarithmic separation from polynomial multiples

Functions $a_1, \dots, a_\ell : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ are separated from polynomial multiples when every $f \in \operatorname{span}^*(a_1, \dots, a_\ell)$ (a linear combination that is not eventually zero) satisfies $|f(t) - c\, p(t)| / \log t \to \infty$ as $t \to \infty$ for every $c \in \mathbb{R}$ and every $p \in \mathbb{Z}[t]$.
