# Families good for multiple recurrence of a single transformation

Functions $a_1, \dots, a_\ell : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ are good for $\ell$-recurrence when for every invertible measure-preserving transformation $T$ of a probability space $(X,\mu)$ (with $T$ and $T^{-1}$ measurable) and every measurable $A$ with $\mu(A) > 0$ there is $n \ge 1$ with

$$\mu\Bigl(A \cap \bigcap_{i=1}^\ell T^{-\lfloor a_i(n) \rfloor} A\Bigr) > 0.$$
