# Multiple recurrence along separated Hardy-field sequences of polynomial growth

Every family $a_1, \dots, a_\ell$ of functions in a common Hardy field, each of polynomial growth, with every nonzero linear combination logarithmically separated from all real multiples of integer polynomials, is good for $\ell$-recurrence: for every invertible measure-preserving $T$ and every $A$ of positive measure some $n \ge 1$ has $\mu(A \cap \bigcap_i T^{-\lfloor a_i(n) \rfloor} A) > 0$. Bergelson, Moreira and Richter proved this under the weaker hypothesis $|f(t) - c\,p(t)| \to \infty$.
