# Rational Kronecker factors are characteristic for averages along polynomials independent modulo constants

Frantzikinakis and Kuca proved that the rational Kronecker factors `K_rat(Tⱼ)` are
characteristic for these averages for every system of commuting invertible measure-preserving
transformations, and present it as the resolution of Problem 16 (Theorem 2.10 of arXiv:2207.12288,
stated there for linearly independent polynomials with zero constant terms); Kuca's survey states
the theorem (Theorem 3.13, citing [69, Theorem 2.9]) for all affinely independent integer
polynomials, which are the rationally independent ones.

## Sources

- N. Frantzikinakis, Some open problems on multiple ergodic averages, Bull. Hellenic Math. Soc. 60 (2016), 41-90, arXiv:1103.3808, Problem 16; resolution: N. Frantzikinakis and B. Kuca, Joint ergodicity for commuting transformations and applications to polynomial sequences, Invent. Math. 239 (2025), 621-706, arXiv:2207.12288; stated in B. Kuca, Joint ergodicity - 40 years on, arXiv:2603.18974 (2026), Frantzikinakis-Kuca, Theorem 2.10 of the arXiv version (Theorem 2.9 of the published version, as cited by Kuca): for linearly independent p₁, …, p_ℓ ∈ ℤ[n] with zero constant terms (the standing assumption of Section 2.2) and every system, the rational Kronecker factor is characteristic, i.e. lim_N ‖E_\{n∈[N]\} ∏ⱼ Tⱼ^\{pⱼ(n)\} fⱼ - E_\{n∈[N]\} ∏ⱼ Tⱼ^\{pⱼ(n)\} E(fⱼ | K_rat(Tⱼ))‖_\{L²(μ)\} = 0 for all fⱼ ∈ L^∞(μ), which the paper says resolves the conjecture of [8, Section 1.3] (see also [14, Problem 16]); Kuca, Theorem 3.13 ([69, Theorem 2.9]): 'Any affinely independent polynomials a₁, …, a_ℓ ∈ ℤ[t] are controlled by the rational Kronecker factor', where a₁, …, a_ℓ are affinely independent if c₁a₁ + ⋯ + c_ℓa_ℓ is constant only for c₁ = ⋯ = c_ℓ = 0 (c_i ∈ ℤ, Section 3.1.1) and controlled means that for every system lim_N ‖E_\{n∈[N]\} ∏ⱼ Tⱼ^\{aⱼ(n)\} fⱼ‖_\{L²(μ)\} = 0 whenever E(fⱼ | K_rat(Tⱼ)) = 0 for some j; systems are invertible commuting measure-preserving transformations of a standard probability space, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-024-01313-w.
