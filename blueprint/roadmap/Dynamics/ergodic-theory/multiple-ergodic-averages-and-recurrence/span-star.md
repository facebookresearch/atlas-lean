# Nonzero linear combinations of a family of functions

For functions $a_1, \dots, a_\ell : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $\operatorname{span}^*(a_1, \dots, a_\ell)$ is the set of functions $t \mapsto \sum_i c_i a_i(t)$, $c \in \mathbb{R}^\ell$, that are not eventually zero.
