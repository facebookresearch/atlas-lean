# Multiple recurrence holds along integer parts of fractional prime powers

Koutsogiannis and Tsinas (Theorem 1.6(a) with `a(t) = t^c`, which satisfies their
condition (11) for positive non-integer `c`) prove that the averages over the primes `p ≤ N` of `μ(A
∩ T^\{-⌊p^c⌋\} A ∩ ⋯ ∩ T^\{-k⌊p^c⌋\} A)` have a positive limit, so some prime `p_n` gives a set of
positive measure, and `⌊p_n^c⌋ ≥ 1` since `p_n ≥ 2`; they state that this, with Theorem 1.3, answers
Problem 27.

Dyn. 22 (2026), 1-71; arXiv:2309.04939, Theorem 1.3 (for a Hardy field function a of polynomial growth satisfying (11), and any system, k and f₁, …, f_k ∈ L^∞(μ), the averages (1/π(N)) ∑_\{p ≤ N prime\} T^\{⌊a(p)⌋\} f₁ ⋯ T^\{k⌊a(p)⌋\} f_k converge in L²(μ)) and Theorem 1.6(a) (under (11), lim_N (1/π(N)) ∑_\{p ≤ N prime\} μ(A ∩ T^\{-⌊a(p)⌋\} A ∩ ⋯ ∩ T^\{-k⌊a(p)⌋\} A) > 0 for every A of positive measure); after Corollary 1.7: 'Specializing to the case where a(n) = n^c where c is a positive non-integer, Theorem 1.3 and part (a) of Theorem 1.6 provide an affirmative answer to [12, Problem 27]', [12] being Frantzikinakis's survey; a(t) = t^c satisfies their condition (11), |a(t) - c' q(t)| / log t -> infinity for all c' in R and q in Z[t], https://doi.org/10.3934/jmd.2026001.
