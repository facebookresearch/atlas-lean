# Real functions of polynomial growth

A function $a : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ has polynomial growth when there are $C > 0$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t_0$ with $|a(t)| \le C |t|^k$ for all $t \ge t_0$.
