# Frantzikinakis’s Problem 17 for one commuting system

The statement, for given $\ell$, $(X,\mu)$, $T_1, \dots, T_\ell$ and $p_1, \dots, p_\ell$, that the following implication holds. Suppose $p_1, \dots, p_\ell \in \mathbb{Z}[t]$ are rationally independent (if $\sum_i c_i p_i$ is constant for integers $c_i$, then all $c_i = 0$) with zero constant terms, $(X,\mu)$ is a probability space and $T_1, \dots, T_\ell$ are commuting measure-preserving actions of $\mathbb{Z}$ on $X$ ($T_i^0 = \mathrm{id}$, $T_i^{a+b} = T_i^a T_i^b$, and $T_i^a T_j^b = T_j^b T_i^a$). Then for every measurable $A \subseteq X$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, the set of $n \ge 1$ with

$$\mu\bigl(A \cap T_1^{-p_1(n)} A \cap \dots \cap T_\ell^{-p_\ell(n)} A\bigr) \ge \mu(A)^{\ell + 1} - \varepsilon$$

is syndetic (has bounded gaps).

## Sources

- N. Frantzikinakis, Some open problems on multiple ergodic averages, Bull. Hellenic Math. Soc. 60 (2016), 41-90, arXiv:1103.3808, Problem 17.
