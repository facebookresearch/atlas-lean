# Joint ergodicity of separated Hardy-field sequences of polynomial growth

Let $a_1, \dots, a_\ell : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ lie in a common set $H$ of functions that contains the constants, is closed under sums and products, contains an eventual derivative of each member and the eventual reciprocal of each eventually nonzero member, and whose members are eventually zero, eventually positive or eventually negative; suppose each $|a_i(t)| \le C t^n$ for large $t$. Suppose that for every nonzero $w \in \mathbb{R}^\ell$, every $c \in \mathbb{R}$ and every $p \in \mathbb{Z}[t]$, $|\sum_i w_i a_i(t) - c\, p(t)| / \log t \to \infty$. Then for every ergodic invertible measure-preserving transformation $T$ of a probability space $(X,\mu)$ and all $f_1, \dots, f_\ell \in L^\infty(\mu)$,

$$\frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^N \prod_{i=1}^\ell f_i\bigl(T^{\lfloor a_i(n) \rfloor} x\bigr) \longrightarrow \prod_{i=1}^\ell \int f_i\, d\mu$$

in $L^2(\mu)$ as $N \to \infty$. Tsinas proved this, answering Problem 23.
