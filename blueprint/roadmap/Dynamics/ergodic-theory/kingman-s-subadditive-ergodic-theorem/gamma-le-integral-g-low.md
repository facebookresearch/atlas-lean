# Time constant is at most the integral of the lower limit

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$, with $X_n \ge 0$ $\mu$-almost everywhere for every $n$. Then the
[time constant](gamma.md) is at most the integral of the [real lower limit](g-low.md) $g$ of $X_n/n$:

$$\gamma \le \int g \, d\mu.$$
