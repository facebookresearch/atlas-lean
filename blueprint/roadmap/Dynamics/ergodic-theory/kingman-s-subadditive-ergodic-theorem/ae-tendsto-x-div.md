# Kingman's theorem: normalized cocycle converges almost everywhere

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$, and suppose that the numbers $\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu$ ($n \ge 1$) are bounded below (so that the
infimum defining the [time constant](gamma.md) is finite). Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$ the sequence $X_n(\omega)/n$ converges to
a real number.
