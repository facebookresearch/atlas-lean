# Cost of a bad step in the greedy decomposition

For a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$, functions $X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, a real number $\varepsilon$ and an
integer $N$, the cost of a bad step is the function $\mathbf{1}_B X_1$, equal to $X_1(\omega)$ for $\omega$ in the
[bad set](bad-set.md) $B$ of $\varepsilon$ and $N$, and to $0$ elsewhere.
