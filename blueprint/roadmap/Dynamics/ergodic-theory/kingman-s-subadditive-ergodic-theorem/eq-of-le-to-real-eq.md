# Ordered extended reals with equal real parts are equal

Let $x \le y$ be elements of $[-\infty, \infty]$ with $x \ne -\infty$ and $y \ne +\infty$. If $x$ and $y$ have the
same real part (the extended real read as a real number, with $\pm\infty$ read as $0$), then $x = y$: both are then
real numbers.
