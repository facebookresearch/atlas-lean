# Fekete's lemma for the expectations of a subadditive cocycle

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$, and suppose that the numbers $\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu$ ($n \ge 1$) are bounded below. Then
(Fekete's lemma)

$$\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu \longrightarrow \gamma \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\gamma$ is the [time constant](gamma.md).
