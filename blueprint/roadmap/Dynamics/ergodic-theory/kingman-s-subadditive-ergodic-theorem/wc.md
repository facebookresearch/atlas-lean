# Negative subadditivity defect of a cocycle

For a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ and functions $X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ ($n \ge 0$), the negative defect is

$$W_0 = 0, \qquad W_n = X_n - A_n \quad (n \ge 1),$$

where $A_n$ is the [additive cocycle](add-coc.md) of Birkhoff sums of $X_1$. For a
[subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) it is nonpositive almost everywhere, and it is again a subadditive
cocycle.
