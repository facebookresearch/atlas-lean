# Real lower limit of a normalized cocycle

For functions $X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ ($n \ge 0$), the real lower limit of $X_n/n$ is the function

$$g(\omega) = \liminf_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n},$$

computed in $[-\infty, \infty]$ (it is the [lower limit](g-star-low.md) $g_*$) and read as a real number, with
$\pm\infty$ replaced by $0$.
