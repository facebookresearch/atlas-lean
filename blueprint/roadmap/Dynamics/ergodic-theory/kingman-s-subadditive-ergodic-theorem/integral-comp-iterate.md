# Integral is invariant under iterates of a measure-preserving map

Let $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ be a measurable map preserving a measure $\mu$, and $g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ a function
that is $\mu$-almost everywhere equal to a measurable function. Then for every $m \ge 0$,

$$\int g(T^m \omega) \, d\mu(\omega) = \int g \, d\mu,$$

where both sides are $0$ when $g$ is not integrable. Compare
[invariance of the integral under iterates](../birkhoff-ergodic-theorem/integral-comp-iterate-of-measure-preserv.md),
stated for integrable $g$.
