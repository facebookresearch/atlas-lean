# Subadditive cocycle over a measure-preserving map

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$ and $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ a map. A sequence of functions
$X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ ($n \ge 0$) is a subadditive cocycle over $T$ for $\mu$ if

- $T$ is measurable and preserves $\mu$;
- every $X_n$ is integrable;
- for all $m, n \ge 0$, $X_{m+n}(\omega) \le X_m(\omega) + X_n(T^m \omega)$ for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$.
