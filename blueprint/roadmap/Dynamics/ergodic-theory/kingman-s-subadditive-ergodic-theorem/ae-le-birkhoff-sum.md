# Subadditive cocycle bounded by Birkhoff sums of its first term

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$. Iterating the cocycle inequality, for
$\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$X_n(\omega) \le \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} X_1(T^k \omega) \qquad \text{for every } n \ge 1.$$
