# Lower limit of the normalized defect is finite almost everywhere

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$, and suppose that the numbers $\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu$ ($n \ge 1$) are bounded below (so that the
infimum defining the [time constant](gamma.md) is finite). Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\liminf_{n \to \infty} \max\Bigl(\frac{Z_n(\omega)}{n}, 0\Bigr) < \infty,$$

where $Z_n$ is the [subadditivity defect](zc.md). The proof uses Fatou's lemma.
