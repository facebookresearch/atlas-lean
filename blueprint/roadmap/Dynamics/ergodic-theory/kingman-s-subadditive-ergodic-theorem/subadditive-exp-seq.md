# Expectations of a subadditive cocycle form a subadditive sequence

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$. Then the expectations $a_n = \int X_n \, d\mu$
form a subadditive sequence: $a_{m+n} \le a_m + a_n$ for all $m, n \ge 0$.
