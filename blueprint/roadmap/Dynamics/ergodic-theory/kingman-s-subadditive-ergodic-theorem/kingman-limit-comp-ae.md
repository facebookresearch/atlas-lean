# Invariance of the Kingman limit

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$. Suppose that for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \quad \text{and} \quad
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} > -\infty,$$

and that the [Kingman limit](kingman-limit.md) $\bar X$ is integrable. Then $\bar X(T\omega) = \bar X(\omega)$ for
$\mu$-almost every $\omega$.
