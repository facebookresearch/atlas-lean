# Integrable function along an orbit divided by time tends to zero

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ a measurable map preserving $\mu$, and
$g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ an integrable function. Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\frac{g(T^m \omega)}{m} \longrightarrow 0 \qquad (m \to \infty).$$
