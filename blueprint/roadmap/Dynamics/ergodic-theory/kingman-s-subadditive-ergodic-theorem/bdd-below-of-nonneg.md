# Normalized expectations of a nonnegative cocycle are bounded below

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$, with $X_n \ge 0$ $\mu$-almost everywhere for every $n$. Then the numbers
$\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu$ ($n \ge 1$) are bounded below by $0$.
