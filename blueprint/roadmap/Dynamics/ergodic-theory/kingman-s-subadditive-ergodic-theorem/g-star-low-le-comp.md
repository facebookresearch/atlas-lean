# Lower limit of a cocycle is nondecreasing along the map

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$. Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$g_*(\omega) \le g_*(T\omega), \qquad g_*(\omega) = \liminf_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \in [-\infty, \infty].$$
