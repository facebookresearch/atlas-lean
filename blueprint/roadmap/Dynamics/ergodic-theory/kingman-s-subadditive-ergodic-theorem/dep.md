# Per-step budget of the greedy decomposition

For a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$, functions $X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, a real number $\varepsilon$ and an
integer $N$, the per-step budget of the greedy decomposition is the function

$$d(\omega) = g(\omega) + \varepsilon + \mathbf{1}_B(\omega)\, X_1(\omega),$$

where $g$ is the [real lower limit](g-low.md) of $X_n/n$ and $B$ the [bad set](bad-set.md) of $\varepsilon$ and $N$.
