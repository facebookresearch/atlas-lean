# Almost-everywhere convergence in Kingman's theorem for nonnegative cocycles

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$, with $X_n \ge 0$ $\mu$-almost everywhere for every $n$. Then for
$\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \longrightarrow \bar X(\omega) \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\bar X$ is the [Kingman limit](kingman-limit.md).

## Sources

- J. F. C. Kingman, *The ergodic theory of subadditive stochastic processes*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 30
  (1968), 499–510.
- J. M. Steele, *Kingman's subadditive ergodic theorem*, Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist. 25 (1989),
  93–98 (the proof).
