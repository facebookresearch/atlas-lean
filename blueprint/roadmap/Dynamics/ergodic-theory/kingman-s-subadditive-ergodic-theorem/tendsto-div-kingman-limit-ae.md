# Almost-everywhere convergence to the Kingman limit

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$. Suppose that for $\mu$-almost every
$\omega$,

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \quad \text{and} \quad
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} > -\infty.$$

Then $X_n(\omega)/n \to \bar X(\omega)$ for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$, where $\bar X$ is the
[Kingman limit](kingman-limit.md).
