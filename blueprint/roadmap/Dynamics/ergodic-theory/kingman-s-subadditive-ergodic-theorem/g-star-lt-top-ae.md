# Upper limit of a normalized cocycle is finite almost everywhere

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$. Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} < +\infty.$$
