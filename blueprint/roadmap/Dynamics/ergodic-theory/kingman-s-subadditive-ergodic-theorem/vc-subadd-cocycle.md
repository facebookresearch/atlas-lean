# Truncated defect is a nonnegative subadditive cocycle

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$. For every real number $M$, the
[truncated defect](vc.md) $V^M$ is a nonnegative subadditive cocycle over $T$ for $\mu$.
