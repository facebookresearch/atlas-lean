# Time constant is a lower bound for normalized expectations

Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for a measure $\mu$, and suppose that the numbers $\frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu$ ($n \ge 1$) are bounded below. Then the
[time constant](gamma.md) $\gamma$ satisfies

$$\gamma \le \frac{1}{n} \int X_n \, d\mu \qquad \text{for every } n \ge 1.$$
