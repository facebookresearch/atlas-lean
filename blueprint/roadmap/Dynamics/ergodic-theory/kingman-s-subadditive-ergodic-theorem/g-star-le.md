# Upper limit of a cocycle bounded by Birkhoff limits of blocks

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(X_n)_{n \ge 0}$ a [subadditive cocycle](subadditive-cocycle.md) over a map $T : \Omega \to \Omega$ for $\mu$, and let $k \ge 1$. Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{X_n(\omega)}{n} \le \frac{\bar Y_k(\omega)}{k},$$

where $\bar Y_k$ is the [Birkhoff limit](../birkhoff-ergodic-theorem/birkhoff-limit.md) of $X_k$ under the map
$T^k$.
