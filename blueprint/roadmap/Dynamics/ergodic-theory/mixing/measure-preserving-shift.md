# The shift preserves the product measure

Let $p$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $S$. The [left shift](shift.md) $\sigma$ on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$
is measurable and preserves the product measure $p^{\mathbb{N}}$: $p^{\mathbb{N}}(\sigma^{-1} B) = p^{\mathbb{N}}(B)$
for every measurable $B \subseteq S^{\mathbb{N}}$.
