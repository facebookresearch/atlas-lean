# Powers of a mixing map are mixing

If $T$ is a [mixing](is-mixing.md) transformation of a probability space $(X, \mu)$, then so is $T^k$ for every
integer $k \ge 1$. The power $T^k$ preserves $\mu$, and for measurable sets $A$ and $B$ the sequence
$\mu(A \cap T^{-kn} B)$, $n \ge 0$, is a subsequence of $\mu(A \cap T^{-n} B)$, so it also converges to
$\mu(A)\,\mu(B)$.
