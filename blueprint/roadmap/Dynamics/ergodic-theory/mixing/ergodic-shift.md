# Bernoulli shifts are ergodic

Let $(S, p)$ be a probability space. The [left shift](shift.md) $\sigma$ on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ is ergodic for the
product measure $p^{\mathbb{N}}$: it preserves $p^{\mathbb{N}}$, and every measurable set $E \subseteq S^{\mathbb{N}}$
with $\sigma^{-1} E = E$ has measure $0$ or $1$.

Indeed, [Bernoulli shifts are mixing](is-mixing-shift.md), and
[mixing transformations of a probability space are ergodic](ergodic.md).
