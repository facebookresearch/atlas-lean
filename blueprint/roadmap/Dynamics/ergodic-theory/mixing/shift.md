# The one-sided left shift

The left shift on one-sided sequences, $\sigma(x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots) = (x_1, x_2, \dots)$ on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$:
$(\sigma x)_n = x_{n+1}$ ([the shift reads the next symbol](shift-apply.md)). It is the full shift by $1$ on
configurations indexed by $\mathbb{N}$; with a product measure $p^{\mathbb{N}}$ it is the Bernoulli scheme.
