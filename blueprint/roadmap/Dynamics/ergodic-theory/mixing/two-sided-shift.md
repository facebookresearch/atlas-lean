# The two-sided left shift

The left shift on bi-infinite sequences, $(\sigma x)_n = x_{n+1}$ on $S^{\mathbb{Z}}$: the full shift by $1$ on
configurations indexed by $\mathbb{Z}$. It is invertible, and with a product measure $p^{\mathbb{Z}}$ it is an
invertible Bernoulli scheme, the setting of
[Ornstein's isomorphism theorem](../kolmogorov-sinai-entropy/ornstein-isomorphism.md). When $S$ has $N$
elements it is the unit shift on $\{0, \dots, N - 1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$.
