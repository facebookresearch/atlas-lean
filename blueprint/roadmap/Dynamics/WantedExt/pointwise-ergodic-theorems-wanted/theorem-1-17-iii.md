# Theorem 1 17 iii

Statement of Theorem 1.17(iii) (Maximal ergodic theorem):

There exists a constant `C ≥ 0` such that for any `f ∈ Lᵖ₁(X)` and `g ∈ Lᵖ₂(X)`,
`‖A T (N : ℕ+) X P f g‖_{Lᵖ(X;ℓ∞)} ≤ C ‖f‖_{Lᵖ₁(X)} ‖g‖_{Lᵖ₂(X)}`.
