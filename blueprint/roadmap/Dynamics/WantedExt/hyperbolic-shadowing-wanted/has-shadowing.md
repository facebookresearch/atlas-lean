# Has Shadowing

`K ⊆ ℝᵈ` has the **shadowing property** for `T`: some neighbourhood
`U ⊇ K` (open in `ℝᵈ`) admits, for every accuracy `δ > 0`, a tolerance
`ε > 0` such that every `ε`-pseudo orbit inside `U` stays within `δ` of
a real forward `T`-orbit.
