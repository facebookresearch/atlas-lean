# Curves with infinitely many simultaneously postcritically finite pairs are special

Let $F \in \mathbb{C}[x, y]$ be irreducible. If the curve $\{F = 0\}$ contains infinitely many points $(c_1, c_2)$ such that $z^2 + c_1$ and $z^2 + c_2$ are both postcritically finite (the forward orbit of $0$ is finite), then the curve is a vertical line $\{x = a\}$, a horizontal line $\{y = b\}$ or the diagonal $\{x = y\}$; that is, $F$ is associated to $x - a$, $y - b$ or $x - y$. This is the case $d = 2$ of the 'only if' direction of the theorem of Ghioca, Krieger, Nguyen and Ye.
