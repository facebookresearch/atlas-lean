# Critical points of the cubic family

For $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$, a point
$c \in \mathbb{C}$ is a critical point of $f_\lambda(z) = \lambda z^2 + z^3$ when $f_\lambda$ has
complex derivative $0$ at $c$, that is, $f_\lambda'(c) = 0$.
