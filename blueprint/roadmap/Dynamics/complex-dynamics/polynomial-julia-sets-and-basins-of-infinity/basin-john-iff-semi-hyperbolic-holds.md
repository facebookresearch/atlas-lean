# For polynomials of degree at least two, the basin of infinity is a John domain exactly in the semi-hyperbolic case

Carleson, Jones and Yoccoz proved that for every polynomial of degree at least `2`
the basin of infinity is a John domain if and only if the polynomial has no parabolic periodic
points and no critical point in the Julia set is recurrent (semi-hyperbolicity), extending the
real-quadratic equivalence to all complex polynomials once parabolic points are excluded.
