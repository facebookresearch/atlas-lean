# Composite of two tuples of polynomials

For a natural number $N$ and two tuples $F = (F_0, \dots, F_N)$ and $G = (G_0, \dots, G_N)$ of polynomials with rational coefficients in the variables $X_0, \dots, X_N$, the composite $F \circ G$ is the tuple whose $i$-th component is $F_i(G_0, \dots, G_N)$, obtained by substituting $G_j$ for $X_j$ in $F_i$. No homogeneity is assumed; for tuples of forms of a common degree it is a lift of the composite of the rational self-maps of projective space $\mathbb{P}^N$ they represent.
