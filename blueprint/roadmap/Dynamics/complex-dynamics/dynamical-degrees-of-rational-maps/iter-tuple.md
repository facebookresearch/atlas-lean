# Iterates of a tuple of polynomials

For a natural number $N$, a tuple $F = (F_0, \dots, F_N)$ of polynomials with rational coefficients in the variables $X_0, \dots, X_N$, and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th iterate of $F$ is the tuple obtained from the identity tuple $(X_0, \dots, X_N)$ by applying $G \mapsto F \circ G$, the substitution of $G$ into each component of $F$, $n$ times. For $n = 0$ it is the identity tuple, and for $n \ge 1$ it is $F \circ \cdots \circ F$ with $n$ factors.
