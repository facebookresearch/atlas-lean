# Degree of a rational self-map of projective space

The degree of the rational map of `ℙᴺ` represented by the tuple `G`: the least `e` such that a
nonzero tuple of degree-`e` forms is proportional to `G` (`Hᵢ Gⱼ = Hⱼ Gᵢ`). For a nonzero tuple of
forms of a common degree this is the common degree of the components of `G` after removing their
common factor. When no such `e` exists, as for `G = (X₀ + 1, 1)` with `N = 1`, the value is `0`,
the infimum of the empty set in `ℕ`.
