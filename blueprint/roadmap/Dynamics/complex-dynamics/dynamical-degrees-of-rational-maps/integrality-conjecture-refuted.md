# The integrality conjecture for dynamical degrees fails over ℚ

Bell, Diller and Jonsson give a dominant rational self-map of ℙ² (over any field of
characteristic not two, in particular over ℚ) whose dynamical degree is transcendental, hence not an
algebraic integer.
