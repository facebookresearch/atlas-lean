# Polynomial lift map $\mathbb{C}^{k+1} \to \mathbb{C}^{k+1}$

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $P = (P_0, \dots, P_k)$ be a tuple of polynomials in $\mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_k]$. The associated polynomial map $\mathbb{C}^{k+1} \to \mathbb{C}^{k+1}$ is

$$x \longmapsto \bigl(P_0(x), \dots, P_k(x)\bigr).$$

When the $P_i$ are homogeneous of a common degree with no common zero other than $0$, this map is a lift of a holomorphic self-map of $\mathbb{P}^k$.
