# Every endomorphism of ℙᵏ of degree at least two has a repelling periodic point

Briend and Duval proved that the repelling periodic points of period n of a
holomorphic endomorphism of ℙᵏ of degree d ≥ 2 in the support of its equilibrium measure, weighted
by d^(-kn), converge to that measure; in particular such points exist.
