# Chordal distance on complex projective space

The chordal (Fubini–Study) distance between the points `[u]` and `[v]` of `ℙᵏ`, computed on
nonzero representatives: `√(1 - |⟨u, v⟩|² / (|u|² |v|²))`, the sine of the angle between the
lines `ℂ u` and `ℂ v`. The formula takes any vectors, with division by zero read as `0`: when
`u = 0` or `v = 0` it gives `1`, which is not a distance between points of `ℙᵏ`.
