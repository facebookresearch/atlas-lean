# A holomorphic endomorphism of projective space of degree at least two with a wandering Fatou component

Astorg, Buff, Dujardin, Peters and Raissy construct polynomial skew products of ℂ²
that extend to holomorphic endomorphisms of ℙ² with a wandering Fatou component.

## Sources

- G. Buzzard, J. E. Fornæss, E. A. Gavosto, S. G. Krantz (eds.), The Several Complex Variables Problem List, arXiv:math/9509202 (1995), Problem 70; resolution: M. Astorg, X. Buff, R. Dujardin, H. Peters, J. Raissy, A two-dimensional polynomial mapping with a wandering Fatou component, Ann. of Math. 184 (2016), 263-313, arXiv:1411.1188, Main Theorem (an endomorphism of ℙ²(ℂ) induced by a polynomial skew-product ℂ² → ℂ² possesses a wandering Fatou component, with bounded orbits), the Fatou set being defined in §1 as the largest open subset of ℙᵏ(ℂ) where the iterates are locally equicontinuous; P. Berger, S. Biebler, arXiv:2001.08649, §1.1, record it as answering the question of Fornæss and Sibony, https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1188.
