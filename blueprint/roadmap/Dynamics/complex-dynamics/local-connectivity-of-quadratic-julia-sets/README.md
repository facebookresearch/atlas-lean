# Local connectivity of quadratic Julia sets

Milnor's question whether the critical point of a quadratic polynomial
$P_\alpha(z) = z^2 + e^{2\pi i \alpha} z$ with a Cremer fixed point at $0$ can be accessible from the
complement of its Julia set, answered in the negative by Kiwi.
