# The critical point of a quadratic polynomial with a Cremer fixed point is not accessible

Kiwi proves that the critical point of a quadratic polynomial with a
Cremer periodic point is not accessible from the complement of its Julia set; in particular for
every Cremer parameter `α` the critical point of `P_α` is not accessible.
