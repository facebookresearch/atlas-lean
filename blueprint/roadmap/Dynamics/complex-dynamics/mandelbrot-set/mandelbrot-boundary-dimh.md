# Hausdorff dimension of the Mandelbrot boundary (Shishikura)

The boundary of the
Mandelbrot set $M \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ has Hausdorff dimension $2$.

## Sources

- Mitsuhiro Shishikura, *The Hausdorff dimension of the boundary of the Mandelbrot set and Julia sets*, Ann. of
  Math. (2) 147 (1998), no. 2, 225–267, DOI 10.2307/121009.
- Oliver Knill, *Some Fundamental Theorems in Mathematics* (2023), §260 (Mandelbrot set), <https://people.math.harvard.edu/~knill/graphgeometry/papers/fundamental.pdf>.
- Problem `mandelbrot_boundary_dimh` of the lean-eval benchmark.
