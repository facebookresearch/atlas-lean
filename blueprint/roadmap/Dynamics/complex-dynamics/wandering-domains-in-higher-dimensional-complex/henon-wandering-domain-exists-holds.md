# A complex Hénon map with a wandering domain

Berger and Biebler construct real Hénon maps `(z, w) ↦ (z⁶ + p₄ z⁴ + ⋯ + p₀ - w, b
z)` with a wandering Fatou component, a component of the interior of `K⁺`, solving the problem
reported by Bedford and Smillie in 1991.

## Sources

- G. Buzzard, J. E. Fornæss, E. A. Gavosto, S. G. Krantz (eds.), The Several Complex Variables Problem List, arXiv:math/9509202 (1995), Problem 64; resolution: P. Berger, S. Biebler, Emergence of wandering stable components, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 36 (2023), arXiv:2001.08649, Theorem A: for every sufficiently small real b ≠ 0 there is a nonempty open P_b ⊂ ℝ⁵ such that for a dense set of p ∈ P_b the polynomial automorphism f_p(z, w) = (z⁶ + Σ_\{i≤4\} pᵢ zⁱ - w, b z) has a wandering Fatou component in ℂ² (whose compact subsets have iterates of diameter tending to 0); §1.1: for polynomial automorphisms of ℂ² the Fatou components other than the escaping set are the components of int K⁺, https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08649.
