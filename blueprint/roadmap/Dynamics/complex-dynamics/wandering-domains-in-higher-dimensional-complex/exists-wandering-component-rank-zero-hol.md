# A polynomial automorphism of ℂ² with a rank-zero wandering component

There is a polynomial automorphism $f$ of $\mathbb{C}^2$ (a polynomial map with a polynomial inverse) and a wandering component $U$ of the interior of $K^+(f)$, the set of points with bounded forward orbit, such that $\operatorname{diam} f^n(K) \to 0$ for every compact $K \subseteq U$; hence every limit of a convergent subsequence of iterates on $U$ is constant (rank zero). Here a wandering component is a connected component of the interior of $K^+(f)$ whose forward images are pairwise disjoint. Berger and Biebler's automorphisms $(z, w) \mapsto (z^6 + \sum_{i \le 4} p_i z^i - w,\ bz)$ have this property.
