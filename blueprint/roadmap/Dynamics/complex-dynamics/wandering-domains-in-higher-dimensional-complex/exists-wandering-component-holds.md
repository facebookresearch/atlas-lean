# A polynomial automorphism of ℂ² with a wandering component

Berger and Biebler (Theorem A) construct polynomial automorphisms `(z, w) ↦ (z⁶ +
Σ_\{i≤4\} p_i z^i - w, b z)` of `ℂ²` with a wandering Fatou component, which is a wandering component
of the interior of `K⁺`.

## Sources

- B. Bielefeld (ed.), Conformal Dynamics Problem List (Stony Brook, 1990), arXiv:math/9201271, §4 Stable regions for complex Hénon type maps (J. Milnor), Question 1, https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/open-problems-dynamical-systems; resolution: P. Berger and S. Biebler, Emergence of wandering stable components, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 36 (2023), 397-482; arXiv:2001.08649, Theorem A: for every sufficiently small real b ≠ 0 there is a nonempty open P_b ⊂ ℝ⁵ such that for a dense set of p ∈ P_b the polynomial automorphism f_p(z, w) = (z⁶ + Σ_\{i≤4\} p_i z^i - w, b z) of ℂ² has a wandering Fatou component C, and (2) diam f_p^n(C') → 0 for every compact C' ⋐ C; §0.1 notes that such a Fatou component is a component of the interior of K⁺ and that Theorem A answers the existence problem of Bedford and Smillie (1991), https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1005.
