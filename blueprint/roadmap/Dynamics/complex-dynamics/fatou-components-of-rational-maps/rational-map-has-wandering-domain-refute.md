# Rational maps of degree at least two have no wandering domains

Sullivan's no-wandering-domains theorem: a rational map of degree at least `2` has
no wandering domain, so the answer to Jakobson's question is no (Hayman–Lingham Update 2.62).

## Sources

Solution of the Fatou-Julia problem on wandering domains, Ann. of Math. 122 (1985), 401-418; recorded in Hayman–Lingham, Update 2.62, Hayman–Lingham Update 2.62: "Sullivan has proved that rational functions have no wandering domains, so that the answer to Jakobson's question is 'no'", https://doi.org/10.2307/1971308.
