# The full 3-shift

The space of all two-sided sequences $y = (y_i)_{i \in \mathbb{Z}}$
with every $y_i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, carrying the product topology. It is the ambient space of the
proper 3-colouring shift.

## Sources

- Michael Hochman, *Isomorphism and embedding of Borel systems on full sets*, Acta Appl. Math. 126 (2013), no. 1,
  187–201, DOI 10.1007/s10440-013-9813-8, Problem 1.9; the special case of the full 2-shift and the proper
  3-colouring shift was posed by Hochman at the 2010 Pingree Park Dynamical Systems Workshop (Ville Salo,
  *Conjugacy of transitive SFTs minus periodic points*, Introduction) (catalogue key AMR-043-0001).
