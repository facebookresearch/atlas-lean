# Sofic subshifts on a finitely generated group

A sofic subshift on a finitely generated group is the image of a subshift of finite type under a factor map given by a local rule; it is given to an algorithm by a sofic presentation, an SFT presentation together with the local rule.

Let $G$, its generating set $S$, subshifts, factor maps and SFT presentations $(A, \mathcal{F})$ be as in [subshifts of finite type on a finitely generated group](subshifts-of-finite-type-on-groups.md). A *sofic presentation* is an SFT presentation $(A, \mathcal{F})$ together with a local rule $\mu\colon A^W \to B$, where $W \subset S^*$ and $B \subset \mathbb{N}$ are finite. It presents the image of $X_{(A,\mathcal{F})}$ under the map

$$
x \mapsto \bigl(g \mapsto \mu(w \mapsto x(g\underline{w}))\bigr),
$$

which is a factor map onto its image. A subshift of this form is a *sofic subshift*; the image of the empty SFT, the empty set, is a sofic subshift.
