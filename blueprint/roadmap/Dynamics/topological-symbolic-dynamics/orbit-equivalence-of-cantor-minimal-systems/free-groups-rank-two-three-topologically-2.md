# Free groups of ranks two and three have orbit equivalent free minimal Cantor actions

Medynets, Sauer and Thom proved that non-amenable quasi-isometric
finitely generated groups admit topologically orbit equivalent free Cantor minimal systems, and that non-abelian free groups of different rank do, unlike in
the measurable setting.
