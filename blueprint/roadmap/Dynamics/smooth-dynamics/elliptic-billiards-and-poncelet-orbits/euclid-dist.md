# Euclidean distance between two points of the plane

For points $p = (p_1, p_2)$ and $q = (q_1, q_2)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$, the Euclidean distance between them is $\sqrt{(p_1 - q_1)^2 + (p_2 - q_2)^2}$. It is written out explicitly because the product metric on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ is the maximum of the coordinate distances, not the Euclidean one.
