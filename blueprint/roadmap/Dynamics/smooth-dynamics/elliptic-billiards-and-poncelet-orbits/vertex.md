# Vertices of a periodic billiard orbit in an ellipse

Let $a, b, \lambda$ be real numbers, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $O$ be an orbit record for these parameters: a strictly increasing sequence $(t_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of eccentric anomalies with $t_{i+N} = t_i + 2\pi$, together with contact points satisfying the caustic and tangency conditions (read with division by zero as $0$). For $i \in \mathbb{N}$, its vertex $P_i$ is the point $(a \cos t_i, b \sin t_i)$; the vertices are $N$-periodic in $i$. No condition on $a$, $b$ or $\lambda$ enters this definition. When $0 < b < a$ and $0 < \lambda < b^2$, the record is an $N$-periodic billiard orbit with turning number one in the ellipse $x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1$ whose sides are tangent to the confocal caustic with parameter $\lambda$, and $P_i$ is its vertex on that ellipse with eccentric anomaly $t_i$.
