# Vertices of a periodic billiard orbit in an ellipse

Let $O$ be an $N$-periodic billiard orbit with turning number one in the ellipse $x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1$ whose sides are tangent to the confocal caustic with parameter $\lambda$, given by a strictly increasing sequence $(t_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of eccentric anomalies with $t_{i+N} = t_i + 2\pi$. For $i \in \mathbb{N}$, its vertex $P_i$ is the point $(a \cos t_i, b \sin t_i)$ of the ellipse with eccentric anomaly $t_i$; the vertices are $N$-periodic in $i$.
