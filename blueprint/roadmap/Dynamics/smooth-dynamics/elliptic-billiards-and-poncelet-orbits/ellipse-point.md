# Point of an ellipse with a given eccentric anomaly

For real numbers $a$, $b$ and $t$, the point of the ellipse $x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1$ with eccentric anomaly $t$ is $(a \cos t, b \sin t)$. The definition takes arbitrary real $a$ and $b$; for $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$ the point lies on that ellipse.
