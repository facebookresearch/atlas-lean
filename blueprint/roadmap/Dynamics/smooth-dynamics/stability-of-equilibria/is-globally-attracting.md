# Globally attracting equilibrium of real solutions

Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a class of functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Then $a$ attracts $\mathcal{S}$ on $D$ when every $x \in \mathcal{S}$ with $x(0) \in D$ satisfies $x(t) \to a$ as $t \to \infty$.
