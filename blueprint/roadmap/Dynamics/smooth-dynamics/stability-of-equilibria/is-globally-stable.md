# Globally asymptotically stable equilibrium of real solutions

Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a class of functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Then $a$ is globally (asymptotically) stable for $\mathcal{S}$ on $D$ when it is Lyapunov stable (solutions starting in $D$ close to $a$ stay close to $a$ for all $t \ge 0$) and every solution starting in $D$ converges to $a$ as $t \to \infty$.
