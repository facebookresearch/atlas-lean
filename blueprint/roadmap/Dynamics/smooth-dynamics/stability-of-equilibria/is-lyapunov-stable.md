# Lyapunov stability of an equilibrium of real solutions

Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a class of functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (the solutions considered), $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Then $a$ is Lyapunov stable for $\mathcal{S}$ on $D$ when for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that every $x \in \mathcal{S}$ with $x(0) \in D$ and $|x(0) - a| < \delta$ satisfies $|x(t) - a| < \varepsilon$ for all $t \ge 0$.
