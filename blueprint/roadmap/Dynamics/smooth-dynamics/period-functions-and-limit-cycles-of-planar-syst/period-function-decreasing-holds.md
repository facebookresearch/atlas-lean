# The period function of the equivariant center is monotonically decreasing

Gasull and Rojas (Theorem A) prove that for all positive integers `n` and `k` the
period function of the center at the origin of `ż = i z + a (z z̄)ⁿ z^{k+1}`, `a ≠ 0`, is
monotonically decreasing (here `a = 1`); the decrease is strict, since their proof shows that the
derivative of the period function has no zeros.

## Sources

- A. Gasull, Some open problems in low dimensional dynamical systems, SeMA J. 78 (2021), arXiv:2012.02524, Problem 16; resolution: A. Gasull and D. Rojas, Monotonous period function for equivariant differential equations with homogeneous nonlinearities, Mediterr. J. Math. 22 (2025), article 112; arXiv:2411.12408, Theorem A: the period function of the center at the origin of ż = iz + a(zz̄)ⁿz^\{k+1\}, with n and k positive integers and 0 ≠ a ∈ ℂ, is monotonous decreasing; it starts at 2π at the origin and tends to 2(k + n)π/(k + 2n) at the boundary of the period annulus; proof of Theorem B, Proposition 2.1 and Lemma 3.1: the derivative of the period function has no zeros, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00009-025-02879-2.
