# Trigonometric Abel equations with linear coefficients have at most three limit cycles

Yu, Huang and Liu prove that the equation has at most three limit
cycles, `x = 0` included, for all real parameters, and that the bound three is attained.

## Sources

- A. Gasull, Some open problems in low dimensional dynamical systems, SeMA J. 78 (2021), arXiv:2012.02524, Problem 6; resolution: X. Yu, J. Huang and C. Liu, Maximum number of limit cycles for Abel equation having coefficients with linear trigonometric functions, J. Differential Equations 410 (2024); arXiv:2309.00510, Theorem 1.3: equation (3) with arbitrary real parameters a₀, a₁, a₂, b₀, b₁, b₂ has at most three limit cycles (including x = 0), and this upper bound is sharp, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2024.07.030.
