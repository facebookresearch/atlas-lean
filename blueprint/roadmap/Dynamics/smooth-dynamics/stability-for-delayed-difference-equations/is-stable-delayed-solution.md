# Stable solutions of a delayed difference equation

Let X be a metric space and let r be a solution of a delayed difference equation with delay m. Relative to a specified class of admissible trajectories, r is stable if for every ε>0 there exists
δ>0 such that every admissible solution whose initial history

x₋ₘ,…,x₀

is within δ of

r₋ₘ,…,r₀

remains within ε of rₙ for every n≥0.
