# Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $M$ and let
$T : M \to M$ be a map. The Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy $h_\mu(T)$ is the supremum of the
entropies $h_\mu(T, P)$ of $T$ relative to $P$ over all finite measurable partitions $P$ of $M$
modulo $\mu$-null sets, taken to be $0$ when these entropies are not bounded above.

## Background

- [Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy](../../ergodic-theory/kolmogorov-sinai-entropy/ks-entropy.md): the general definition
  in ergodic theory, with values in $[0, \infty]$, its invariance under isomorphism and its computation for
  Bernoulli shifts. For a measure-preserving map of a probability space, this real-valued entropy, used by the
  planar formulas of this topic, agrees with it whenever that entropy is finite, as it is under the hypotheses of
  those formulas (by the Margulis–Ruelle inequality).
