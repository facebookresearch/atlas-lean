# Linkage graph of a reaction network

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with species set $S$ and reaction set $\rho$. The linkage graph is the simple graph on the [complexes](complexes.md) of $N$ in
which two distinct complexes $y \ne y'$ are adjacent when some reaction goes from $y$ to $y'$ or from
$y'$ to $y$.
