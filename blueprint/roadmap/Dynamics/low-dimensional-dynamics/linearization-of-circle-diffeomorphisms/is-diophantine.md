# Diophantine numbers

A real number $\alpha$ is Diophantine when there are real numbers
$\gamma > 0$ and $\tau \ge 0$ such that $|\alpha - p/q| \ge \gamma / q^{2 + \tau}$ for every
integer $p$ and every integer $q \ge 1$.
