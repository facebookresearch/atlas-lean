# Herman's analytic linearization problem

The statement that there is an irrational real
number $\alpha$ that is not Diophantine such that, for every real-analytic circle diffeomorphism
whose lift $F$ has rotation number $\alpha$, $F$ is analytically conjugate to the rotation by
$\alpha$.

M. R. Herman asked whether such an $\alpha$ exists; this is Problem 2.90 in the list of Hayman and Lingham.

## Sources

- Walter K. Hayman and Eleanor F. Lingham, *Research Problems in Function Theory*, Problem Books in Mathematics,
  Springer (2019), Problem 2.90 (M. R. Herman); arXiv:1809.07200, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200>.
