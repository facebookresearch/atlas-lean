# Orbits of the horocycle flow on $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z}) \backslash \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{R})$ are closed or dense

Let $\Gamma = \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$, viewed inside $G = \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{R})$, and let
$u_t = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ t & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. For every $x \in G$, the orbit
$\{\Gamma x u_t : t \in \mathbb{R}\}$ of the horocycle flow on the space $\Gamma \backslash G$ of right cosets, with the
quotient topology, is either closed or dense.

Morris deduces this from Ratner's orbit closure theorem: the closure of the orbit is the image of $xS$ for a connected
closed subgroup $S \ni u_t$ such that $(x^{-1} \Gamma x) \cap S$ is a lattice in $S$, and the only such subgroups are
$\{u_t\}$ and $G$ (the lower-triangular group has no lattice). For cocompact $\Gamma$ every horocycle orbit is dense
(Hedlund, 1930s).
