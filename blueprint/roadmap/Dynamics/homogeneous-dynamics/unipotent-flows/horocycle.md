# Horocycle one-parameter subgroup

The horocycle one-parameter subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{R})$ is
$u_t = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ t & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$; it satisfies $u_{s+t} = u_s u_t$ and every $u_t$ is
unipotent. On $\Gamma \backslash \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{R})$ the flow $\Gamma x \mapsto \Gamma x u_t$ is the horocycle flow.
