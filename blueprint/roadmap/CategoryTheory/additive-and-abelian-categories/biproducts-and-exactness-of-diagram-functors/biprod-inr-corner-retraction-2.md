# Retraction equation for a split left-factor corner

Retraction equation for a split left-factor corner.

Let x : X → X' and r : X' → X satisfy x ≫ r = id_X. For the corner c_x : P → X' ⊞ B' of a
pushout of the right biproduct injection, the map (r ⊞ id_B') ≫ e.inv satisfies c_x ≫ ((r ⊞
id_B') ≫ e.inv) = id_P.
