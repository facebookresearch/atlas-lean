# Terminating paths of a feedback loop

In the category of sets and relations with the disjoint union as tensor, let $f : X \sqcup U \to Y \sqcup U$ be a relation, with $U$ the summand fed back. The terminating-path predicate $\mathrm{Path}_f(s, y)$, for every $s \in X \sqcup U$ (the start may lie inside the loop) and $y \in Y$, is generated by two rules: if $f$ relates $s$ to $\mathrm{inl}(y)$ then $\mathrm{Path}_f(s, y)$; and if $f$ relates $s$ to $\mathrm{inr}(a)$ and $\mathrm{Path}_f(\mathrm{inr}(a), y)$, then $\mathrm{Path}_f(s, y)$.
