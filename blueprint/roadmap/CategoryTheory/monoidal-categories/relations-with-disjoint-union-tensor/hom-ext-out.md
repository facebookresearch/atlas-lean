# Relations out of a sum agree if their restrictions agree

In the category of sets and relations with the disjoint union as tensor, let $R, S : X \sqcup Y \to Z$ be relations. If $\mathrm{inl} ; R = \mathrm{inl} ; S$ and $\mathrm{inr} ; R = \mathrm{inr} ; S$, where $\mathrm{inl}$ and $\mathrm{inr}$ are the injection relations and $;$ is composition in forward order, then $R = S$: a relation out of a sum is determined by its restrictions along the two injections.
