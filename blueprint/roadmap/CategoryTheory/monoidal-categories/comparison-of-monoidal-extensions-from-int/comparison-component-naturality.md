# Comparison Component naturality

Let `K, L` be braided functors from the Int construction on `T` to `D` and `i` a monoidal isomorphism between their restrictions along the embedding of `C`. For every Int morphism `f : X ⟶ Y`, `K.map f` followed by the comparison component at `Y` equals the comparison component at `X` followed by `L.map f`; the comparison components form a natural transformation `K ⟶ L`.
