# Comparison Component

Let `K, L` be braided functors out of the Int construction on `T` and `i` a monoidal isomorphism between their restrictions along the embedding of `C`. For each Int object `X = (X⁺, X⁻)`, the comparison component is the isomorphism `K.obj X ≅ L.obj X` built from `i` on the positive part `X⁺` and from the canonical comparison of mapped pairings on the negative part `X⁻`.
