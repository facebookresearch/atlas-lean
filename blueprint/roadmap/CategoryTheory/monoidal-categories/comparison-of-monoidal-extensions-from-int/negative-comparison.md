# Negative Comparison

Let `K, L` be braided functors out of the Int construction on `T` with a monoidal isomorphism `i` between their restrictions to `C`. For an object `A` of `C`, the negative comparison is the isomorphism `K (𝟙, A) ≅ L (𝟙, A)` obtained as the canonical comparison of right duals of the mapped pairings of `(A, 𝟙)` and `(𝟙, A)`.
