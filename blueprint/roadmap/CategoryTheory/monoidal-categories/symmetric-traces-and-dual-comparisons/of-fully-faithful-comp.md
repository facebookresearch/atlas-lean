# Pullback of traces along a composite

Let $C$ and $D$ be symmetric monoidal categories and $F : C \to D$ a fully faithful braided strong monoidal functor, with tensor comparison $\mu$ and inverse $\delta$. For a symmetric trace $S$ on $D$, write $F^* S$ for the trace on $C$ pulled back along $F$: the trace of $f : X \otimes U \to Y \otimes U$ is the preimage under $F$ of $S(\mu_{X,U} ; F(f) ; \delta_{Y,U})$. This is the symmetric case of Joyal–Street–Verity, Proposition 2.4. For a further such functor $G : D \to E$ and a symmetric trace $S$ on $E$, $F^*(G^* S) = (F ; G)^* S$.
