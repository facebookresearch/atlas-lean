# Uniqueness of the dual comparison

Let $C$ and $D$ be monoidal categories and $F : C \to D$ a strong monoidal functor, with unit map $\varepsilon$ and tensor comparison $\mu$ with inverse $\delta$. For an object $X$ of $C$ with a chosen right dual $X^\vee$ such that $FX$ also has a chosen right dual $(FX)^\vee$ (rigidity of the whole categories is not assumed), the image of the exact pairing of $X$ and $X^\vee$ is an exact pairing of $FX$ and $F(X^\vee)$, with coevaluation $\mathrm{coev}_{\mathrm{mapped}} = \varepsilon ; F(\mathrm{coev}_X) ; \delta_{X, X^\vee}$. If a morphism $k : F(X^\vee) \to (FX)^\vee$ satisfies $\mathrm{coev}_{\mathrm{mapped}} ; (1_{FX} \otimes k) = \mathrm{coev}_{FX}$, then $k = c_X.\mathrm{hom}$, the forward map of the [dual comparison](map-right-dual-iso.md). This is the image-pairing construction of Etingof–Gelaki–Nikshych–Ostrik, *Tensor Categories*, Example 2.10.6 (the book calls this evaluation order a left dual).
