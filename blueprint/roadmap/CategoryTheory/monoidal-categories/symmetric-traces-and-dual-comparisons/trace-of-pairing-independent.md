# The trace along an exact pairing does not depend on the pairing

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with symmetry $\beta$. If $D$ and $E$ are both right duals of $U$ through exact pairings, then for every $f : X \otimes U \to Y \otimes U$ the [traces along the two pairings](trace-of-pairing.md) agree: $\operatorname{tr}_D(f) = \operatorname{tr}_E(f)$.
