# Map Right Dual Iso coevaluation

Let `F : C ⥤ D` be a monoidal functor and `X` an object of `C` with a right dual `Xᘁ`, such that `F.obj X` also has a chosen right dual. Applying `F` to the coevaluation `η_ X Xᘁ` (conjugated by the unit and tensor comparison isomorphisms of `F`) and then whiskering on the left by `F.obj X` with the canonical comparison `F.obj Xᘁ ≅ (F.obj X)ᘁ` gives exactly the coevaluation `η_ (F.obj X) (F.obj X)ᘁ` of `F.obj X`.
