# Compose

Given a symmetric trace `T` on a symmetric monoidal category `C` and morphisms `f : A ⊗ V ⟶ B ⊗ U` and `g : B ⊗ W ⟶ D ⊗ V` of `C`, their Int composite is the morphism `A ⊗ W ⟶ D ⊗ U` obtained by running `f` and then `g` and feeding the shared object `V` back with the trace `T` (with the symmetry used to rearrange the tensor factors). This is the composition of morphisms in the Int construction.
