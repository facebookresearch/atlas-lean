# Fusion category over a field

Let $k$ be a field. A fusion category over $k$ is a category $C$ equipped with the structure of an abelian, $k$-linear, monoidal category whose tensor product is additive and $k$-linear in each variable, with left and right duals for every object (rigid), and such that:

- $C$ is semisimple (every object is a biproduct of simple objects);
- there are $n \in \mathbb N$ and simple objects $S_0, \dots, S_{n-1}$ such that every simple object is isomorphic to exactly one $S_i$ (finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects);
- every space $\operatorname{Hom}(X, Y)$ is finite-dimensional over $k$;
- the monoidal unit is simple.

The unit is only required to be simple; over an algebraically closed field this is equivalent to $\operatorname{End}(\mathbf 1) = k$.
