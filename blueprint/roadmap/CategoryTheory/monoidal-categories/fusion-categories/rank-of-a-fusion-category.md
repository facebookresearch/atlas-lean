# Rank of a fusion category

For a fusion category $C$ over a field $k$, its rank is the number $n$ of isomorphism classes of simple objects: the length of a list $S_0, \dots, S_{n-1}$ of simple objects such that every simple object is isomorphic to exactly one $S_i$ (any such list has the same length). Since the unit is simple, the rank is at least $1$.

- Bruillard, Ng, Rowell and Wang, "Rank-finiteness for modular categories", arXiv:1310.7050, first page.
