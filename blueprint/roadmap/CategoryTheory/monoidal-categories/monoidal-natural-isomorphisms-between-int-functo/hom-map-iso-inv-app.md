# Hom map Iso inv app

Let `i : F ≅ G` be a monoidal natural isomorphism between braided trace-preserving functors. For every object `X = (X⁺, X⁻)` of the Int construction, the inverse of the lifted isomorphism has component at `X` whose underlying crossed morphism is `i.inv.app X⁺ ⊗ i.hom.app X⁻`: the inverse is used on the positive part and the forward map on the negative part.
