# Extension of a monoidal natural isomorphism

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-category-underlying-the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a right rigid symmetric monoidal category, and let $F, G : C \to D$ be braided strong monoidal functors that preserve $T$, sending it to the [canonical trace](../canonical-trace-in-symmetric-right-rigid-monoida/symmetric-trace-of-rigid.md) of $D$, with [canonical extensions](../extending-traced-functors-to-compact-targets/extend.md) $\operatorname{ext}(F)$ and $\operatorname{ext}(G)$ and [factorization isomorphisms](../extending-traced-functors-to-compact-targets/extension-iso.md) $\varphi_F : \operatorname{ext}(F) \circ J \cong F$ and $\varphi_G : \operatorname{ext}(G) \circ J \cong G$. Let $i : F \cong G$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism. The extension $\operatorname{ext}(i) : \operatorname{ext}(F) \cong \operatorname{ext}(G)$ is obtained by applying the inverse equivalence $\mathrm{Int}(T_D) \to D$, where $T_D$ is the canonical trace of $D$, to the [isomorphism of Int functors](../monoidal-natural-isomorphisms-between-int-functo/map-iso.md) induced by $i$. It is a monoidal natural isomorphism; on an object $X$ it uses $i$ on $X^+$ and $i^{-1}$ on $X^-$ ([components](extend-iso-hom-app.md)).
