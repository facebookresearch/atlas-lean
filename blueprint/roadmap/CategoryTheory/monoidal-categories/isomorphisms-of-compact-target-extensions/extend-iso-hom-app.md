# Components of the extension of a monoidal isomorphism

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-category-underlying-the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a right rigid symmetric monoidal category, and let $F, G : C \to D$ be braided strong monoidal functors that preserve $T$, sending it to the [canonical trace](../canonical-trace-in-symmetric-right-rigid-monoida/symmetric-trace-of-rigid.md) of $D$, with [canonical extensions](../extending-traced-functors-to-compact-targets/extend.md) $\operatorname{ext}(F)$ and $\operatorname{ext}(G)$ and [factorization isomorphisms](../extending-traced-functors-to-compact-targets/extension-iso.md) $\varphi_F : \operatorname{ext}(F) \circ J \cong F$ and $\varphi_G : \operatorname{ext}(G) \circ J \cong G$. Let $i : F \cong G$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism. For every object $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, the component of the [extension](extend-iso.md) $\operatorname{ext}(i)$ at $X$ is the image under the inverse equivalence $\mathrm{Int}(T_D) \to D$ of the [separated morphism](../tensor-structure-on-the-int-construction/map-pair.md) $(F X^+, F X^-) \to (G X^+, G X^-)$ with components $i_{X^+} : F X^+ \to G X^+$ and $i_{X^-}^{-1} : G X^- \to F X^-$. Here $T_D$ is the canonical trace of $D$.
