# Existential trace on monotone relations

In the category of preorders and monotone relations, a morphism $X \to Y$ is a relation that is downward closed in its input and upward closed in its output. With the product tensor, feedback is existential: for a monotone relation $R : X \otimes U \to Y \otimes U$, the trace $\operatorname{Tr}^U(R) : X \to Y$ relates $x$ to $y$ exactly when $R$ relates $(x, u)$ to $(y, u)$ for some $u \in U$, with the same $u$ at input and output. This is a [symmetric trace](../symmetric-traces-and-dual-comparisons/symmetric-trace.md).
