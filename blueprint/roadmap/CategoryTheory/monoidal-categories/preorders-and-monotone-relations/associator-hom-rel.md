# Associator of the product tensor of preorders

In the category of preorders and monotone relations, a morphism $X \to Y$ is a relation that is downward closed in its input and upward closed in its output. For preorders $X$, $Y$ and $Z$, the associator $(X \times Y) \times Z \to X \times (Y \times Z)$ relates $s = ((x, y), z)$ to $t = (x', (y', z'))$ exactly when $x \le x'$, $y \le y'$ and $z \le z'$. Equivalently, as a set of pairs, it consists of the $(s, t)$ such that the rebracketing $(x, (y, z))$ of $s$ is below $t$ in the coordinatewise order.
