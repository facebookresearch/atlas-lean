# Map Pair

For objects `X`, `Y` of the Int construction on a symmetric trace and morphisms `f : X⁺ ⟶ Y⁺` and `g : Y⁻ ⟶ X⁻` of `C`, the separated Int morphism `X ⟶ Y` is the crossed morphism `X⁺ ⊗ Y⁻ ⟶ Y⁺ ⊗ X⁻` given by `f` on the positive wire and `g` on the negative wire, with the factors swapped by the symmetry.
