# Extend

Let `T` be a symmetric trace on `C`, `D` a rigid symmetric monoidal category and `F : C ⥤ D` a braided functor that preserves `T`. The extension of `F` is the functor from the Int construction on `T` to `D` sending a pair `(X⁺, X⁻)` to `F X⁺ ⊗ (F X⁻)ᘁ` and an Int morphism to the corresponding morphism obtained with the duality of `D`.
