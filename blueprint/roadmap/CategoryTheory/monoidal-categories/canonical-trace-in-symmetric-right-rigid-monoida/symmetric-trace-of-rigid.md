# Canonical symmetric trace of a right rigid category

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with symmetry $\beta$. Suppose that every object $U$ of $C$ has a chosen right dual $U^\vee$. Then $\operatorname{Tr}^U(f) = \operatorname{tr}_{U^\vee}(f)$, the [trace along the chosen pairing](trace-of-pairing.md), is a [symmetric trace](symmetric-trace.md) on $C$. The vanishing axioms use [independence of the pairing](trace-of-pairing-independent.md), since the chosen duals of $\mathbf{1}$ and of $U \otimes V$ need not be the unit pairing and the tensor pairing.
