# Trace Of Pairing slide

Let `U, D` and `V, E` be exact pairings in a symmetric monoidal category, and `f : X ⊗ U ⟶ Y ⊗ V`, `g : V ⟶ U`, `k : D ⟶ E` such that `k` is dual to `g` (that is, `η_ U D ≫ U ◁ k = η_ V E ≫ g ▷ E` and `k ▷ V ≫ ε_ V E = D ◁ g ≫ ε_ U D`). Then the trace along `(U, D)` of `f ≫ Y ◁ g` equals the trace along `(V, E)` of `X ◁ g ≫ f`.
