# Tensor Triple

For a functor `F` to monotone relations whose source is monoidal, a morphism `f : X ⊗ U ⟶ Y ⊗ V` and pairs of assertions `P` and `Q`, the tensor triple `{P} f {Q}` holds if some assertions `P'` on `X ⊗ U` and `Q'` on `Y ⊗ V`, related to `P` and `Q` by the tensor comparison maps of `F`, satisfy the Hoare triple `{P'} f {Q'}`.
