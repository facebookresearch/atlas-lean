# Positive and negative copies of an object are dual in Int

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, and let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](README.md): its objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and a morphism $X \to Y$ is a morphism $X^+ \otimes Y^- \to Y^+ \otimes X^-$ of $C$. For an object $A$ of $C$, the object $(A, \mathbf{1})$ of $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$, through the exact pairing whose coevaluation $\mathbf{1} \to (A, \mathbf{1}) \otimes (\mathbf{1}, A)$ and evaluation $(\mathbf{1}, A) \otimes (A, \mathbf{1}) \to \mathbf{1}$ are given by the unitor isomorphisms $\mathbf{1} \otimes (A \otimes \mathbf{1}) \cong (A \otimes \mathbf{1}) \otimes \mathbf{1}$ and $(\mathbf{1} \otimes A) \otimes \mathbf{1} \cong \mathbf{1} \otimes (\mathbf{1} \otimes A)$ of $C$.
