# Formal negatives are embedded source duals

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding; the negative component of $X \otimes Y$ is $Y^- \otimes X^-$. Suppose further that $C$ is right rigid, with a chosen right dual $U^\vee$ of each object $U$ of $C$. A traced category need not have such duals: in the discrete monoidal category $(\mathbb{N}, +)$ cancellation gives a trace, but $1$ has no dual. Then for every object $U$ of $C$, the formal negative object is represented by the embedded source dual: $(\mathbf{1}, U) \cong J(U^\vee)$, comparing two right duals of $J(U) = (U, \mathbf{1})$.
