# The functor induced on Int by a trace-preserving functor

Let $C$ and $D$ be symmetric monoidal categories with symmetric traces $T$ and $S$, and let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be the [Int construction](README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ and whose morphisms $X \to Y$ are crossed morphisms $X^+ \otimes Y^- \to Y^+ \otimes X^-$. For a braided strong monoidal functor $F : C \to D$, with tensor comparison $\mu$ and its inverse $\delta$, write $F^\times(f) = \mu_{A,V} ; F(f) ; \delta_{B,U}$ for its action on a crossed arrow $f : A \otimes V \to B \otimes U$, where $;$ is composition in forward order. If moreover $F$ preserves $T$ relative to $S$, the induced functor $L(F) : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to \mathrm{Int}(S)$ sends $(A, U)$ to $(FA, FU)$ and acts on morphisms by $F^\times$. It is a functor because $F^\times$ preserves identities and [Int composition](map-crossed-compose.md).
