# The parallel Hoare rule on pairs of assertions

Let $C$ be a monoidal category and $F : C \to \mathbf{MonRel}$ a strong monoidal functor to the [category of preorders and monotone relations](../preorders-and-monotone-relations/README.md), with tensor comparisons $\mu_{X,U} : FX \otimes FU \to F(X \otimes U)$ and their inverses $\delta_{X,U}$; in $\mathbf{MonRel}$, $FX \otimes FU$ is the product preorder $FX \times FU$. A [Hoare triple](triple.md) $\{P\}\, f\, \{Q\}$ for $f : X \to Y$ means that $F(f)$ relates $P$ to $Q$. For $f : X \to Y$, $g : U \to V$ and assertions $p \in FX$, $u \in FU$, $q \in FY$, $v \in FV$, the [tensor triple](tensor-triple.md) $\{(p, u)\}\, f \otimes g\, \{(q, v)\}$ holds if and only if $\{p\}\, f\, \{q\}$ and $\{u\}\, g\, \{v\}$ both hold. On pairs of assertions the comparison maps cancel, and the condition is the conjunction of the two component triples.
