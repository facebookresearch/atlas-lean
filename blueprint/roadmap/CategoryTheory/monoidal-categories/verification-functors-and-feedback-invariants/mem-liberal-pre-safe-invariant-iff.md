# One safe step into the safe invariant

In the category of sets and relations with the disjoint union as tensor, a relation $f : X \to Y$ acts on assertions $P \subseteq X$ by direct image $f[P]$, and its liberal precondition sends $Q \subseteq Y$ to $\operatorname{wlp}_f(Q) = \{x : \text{every } y \text{ related to } x \text{ by } f \text{ lies in } Q\}$. For $f : X \sqcup U \to Y \sqcup U$ and $Q \subseteq Y$, a state $s \in X \sqcup U$ is one-step safe for a hidden assertion $I \subseteq U$ when every successor of $s$ under $f$ is either $\mathrm{inl}(y)$ with $y \in Q$ or $\mathrm{inr}(u)$ with $u \in I$, that is, when $s \in \operatorname{wlp}_f(Q \sqcup I)$; no successor is required. Let $I_Q \subseteq U$ be the [safe hidden states](safe-invariant.md), those $u$ from which every [terminating execution](../relations-with-disjoint-union-tensor/feedback-path.md) exits in $Q$. Then $s$ is one-step safe for $I_Q$ if and only if every terminating execution from $s$ ends at some $y \in Q$. A direct edge from $s$ to $\mathrm{inl}(y)$ counts as a terminating execution.
