# Hoare triples for a tensor product of morphisms

Let $C$ be a monoidal category and $F : C \to \mathbf{MonRel}$ a strong monoidal functor to the [category of preorders and monotone relations](../preorders-and-monotone-relations/README.md), with tensor comparisons $\mu_{X,U} : FX \otimes FU \to F(X \otimes U)$ and their inverses $\delta_{X,U}$; in $\mathbf{MonRel}$, $FX \otimes FU$ is the product preorder $FX \times FU$. A [Hoare triple](triple.md) $\{P\}\, f\, \{Q\}$ for $f : X \to Y$ means that $F(f)$ relates $P$ to $Q$. For $f : X \to Y$, $g : U \to V$ and arbitrary assertions $P \in F(X \otimes U)$ and $Q \in F(Y \otimes V)$, the triple $\{P\}\, f \otimes g\, \{Q\}$ holds if and only if there are $p \in FX$, $u \in FU$, $q \in FY$ and $v \in FV$ such that $\delta_{X,U}$ relates $P$ to $(p, u)$, $\{p\}\, f\, \{q\}$ and $\{u\}\, g\, \{v\}$ hold, and $\mu_{Y,V}$ relates $(q, v)$ to $Q$. An assertion on a tensor object need not be a pair, so the witnesses and both comparison relations are part of the condition.
