# Monoidal natural isomorphisms commute with crossed maps

Let $C$ and $D$ be symmetric monoidal categories, $F, G : C \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors and $i : F \cong G$ a monoidal natural isomorphism. For a morphism $f : A \otimes V \to B \otimes U$ of $C$, let $F^\times(f) = \delta^F_{B,U} \circ F(f) \circ \mu^F_{A,V} : FA \otimes FV \to FB \otimes FU$, where $\mu^F$ is the tensor comparison of $F$ and $\delta^F$ its inverse, and define $G^\times(f)$ in the same way. Then

$$(i_B \otimes i_U) \circ F^\times(f) = G^\times(f) \circ (i_A \otimes i_V).$$
