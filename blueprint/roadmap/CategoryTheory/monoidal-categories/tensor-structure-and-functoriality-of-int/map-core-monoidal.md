# Strong monoidal structure of an Int lift

Let $C$ and $D$ be symmetric monoidal categories with symmetric traces $T$ and $S$, and let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be the [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ and whose morphisms $X \to Y$ are crossed morphisms $X^+ \otimes Y^- \to Y^+ \otimes X^-$. Write $\mathrm{pair}(a, b) = a \otimes b$ for the [separated morphism](map-pair.md) $X \to Y$ given by $a : X^+ \to Y^+$ and $b : Y^- \to X^-$ (the negative argument points backwards), and $\mathrm{pairIso}(a, b)$ for the isomorphism with forward map $\mathrm{pair}(a.\mathrm{hom}, b.\mathrm{hom})$ and inverse $\mathrm{pair}(a.\mathrm{inv}, b.\mathrm{inv})$. Let $F : C \to D$ be a braided strong monoidal functor preserving $T$ relative to $S$, with [induced functor](../the-int-construction/map.md) $L(F)$, unit map $\varepsilon_F$ with inverse $\eta_F$, and tensor comparison $\mu_F$ with inverse $\delta_F$. $L(F)$ is strong monoidal with exactly these two comparisons: the unit isomorphism of $L(F)$ is $\mathrm{pairIso}(\varepsilon\mathrm{Iso}_F, \varepsilon\mathrm{Iso}_F^{-1}) : \mathbf{1} \cong L(F)(\mathbf{1})$, with forward map $\mathrm{pair}(\varepsilon_F, \eta_F)$. The tensor isomorphism $L(F)X \otimes L(F)Y \cong L(F)(X \otimes Y)$ is $\mathrm{pairIso}(\mu\mathrm{Iso}_F(X^+, Y^+), \mu\mathrm{Iso}_F(Y^-, X^-)^{-1})$, with forward map $\mathrm{pair}(\mu_F(X^+, Y^+), \delta_F(Y^-, X^-))$; the negative component of $X \otimes Y$ is $Y^- \otimes X^-$, and no braiding is added.
