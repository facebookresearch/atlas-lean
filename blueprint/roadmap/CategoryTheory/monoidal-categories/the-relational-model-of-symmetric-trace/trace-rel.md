# Trace of a relation

In the category of sets and relations with the cartesian product as tensor and its [symmetric trace](symmetric-trace.md), let $R : X \times U \to Y \times U$ be a relation. Then $\operatorname{Tr}^U(R)$ relates $x \in X$ to $y \in Y$ exactly when $R$ relates $(x, u)$ to $(y, u)$ for some $u \in U$, with the same $u$ at both ends.
