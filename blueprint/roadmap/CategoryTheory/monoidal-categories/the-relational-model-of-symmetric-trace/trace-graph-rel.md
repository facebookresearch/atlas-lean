# Trace of the graph of a function

In the category of sets and relations with the cartesian product as tensor and its [symmetric trace](symmetric-trace.md), let $f : X \times U \to Y \times U$ be a function. The trace of the graph of $f$ relates $x \in X$ to $y \in Y$ exactly when $f(x, u) = (y, u)$ for some $u \in U$: the visible output is $y$ and the hidden coordinate returns to the same $u$.
