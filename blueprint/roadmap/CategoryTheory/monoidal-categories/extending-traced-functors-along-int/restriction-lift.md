# Lifting a monoidal isomorphism of restrictions to Int

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $K, L : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors. In $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $(A, \mathbf{1})$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$ through the [positive pairing](../the-int-construction/positive-pairing.md). Let $i : K \circ J \cong L \circ J$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism, with components $i_A : K(A, \mathbf{1}) \to L(A, \mathbf{1})$. The restriction lift of $i$ is the natural isomorphism $K \cong L$ whose component at $X$ is the [comparison component](comparison-component.md) $c_X$; it is natural by [naturality of the comparison components](comparison-component-naturality.md), and it is monoidal. The target $D$ need not be rigid.
