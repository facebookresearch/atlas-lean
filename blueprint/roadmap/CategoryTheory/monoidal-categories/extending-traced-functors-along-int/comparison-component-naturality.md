# Naturality of the comparison components

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $K, L : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors. In $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $(A, \mathbf{1})$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$ through the [positive pairing](../the-int-construction/positive-pairing.md). Let $i : K \circ J \cong L \circ J$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism, with components $i_A : K(A, \mathbf{1}) \to L(A, \mathbf{1})$. Then the [comparison components](comparison-component.md) $c_X : K(X) \to L(X)$ are natural: for every morphism $f : X \to Y$ of $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $c_Y \circ K(f) = L(f) \circ c_X$.
