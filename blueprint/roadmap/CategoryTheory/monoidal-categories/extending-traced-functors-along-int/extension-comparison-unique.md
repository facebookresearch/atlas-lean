# Uniqueness of the extension comparison

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $K, L : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors. In $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $(A, \mathbf{1})$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$ through the [positive pairing](../the-int-construction/positive-pairing.md). Suppose that $D$ is right rigid, and let $F : C \to D$ be a braided strong monoidal functor that preserves $T$, sending it to the [canonical trace](../symmetric-traces-and-dual-comparisons/symmetric-trace-of-rigid.md) of $D$. Let $\operatorname{ext}(F) : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ be the [canonical extension](extend.md) of $F$, with its [factorization isomorphism](extension-iso.md) $\varphi_F : \operatorname{ext}(F) \circ J \cong F$, and let $\sigma : K \circ J \cong F$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism. If $e : K \cong \operatorname{ext}(F)$ is a monoidal natural isomorphism with $\varphi_{F,A} \circ e_{J(A)} = \sigma_A$ for every object $A$ of $C$, that is, whose restriction along $J$ composed with $\varphi_F$ is $\sigma$, then $e$ is the [extension comparison](extension-comparison.md).
