# Canonical extension of a traced functor to Int

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a right rigid symmetric monoidal category, with chosen right duals $Y^\vee$ and canonical trace $T_D$ ([trace along the chosen duals](../symmetric-traces-and-dual-comparisons/symmetric-trace-of-rigid.md)), and let $F : C \to D$ be a braided strong monoidal functor that preserves $T$, sending it to $T_D$. The canonical extension $\operatorname{ext}(F) : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ is the composite of the functor $\mathrm{Int}(T) \to \mathrm{Int}(T_D)$ induced by $F$, which sends $(X^+, X^-)$ to $(F X^+, F X^-)$, with the inverse of the equivalence between $D$ and $\mathrm{Int}(T_D)$ given by the duals of $D$. It is a braided strong monoidal functor, and $\operatorname{ext}(F)(X)$ is canonically isomorphic to $F(X^+) \otimes F(X^-)^\vee$ ([object formula](extend-obj-iso.md)).
