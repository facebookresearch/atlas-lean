# Extension preserves composition of monoidal isomorphisms

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a right rigid symmetric monoidal category, and let $F, G : C \to D$ be braided strong monoidal functors that preserve $T$, sending it to the [canonical trace](../symmetric-traces-and-dual-comparisons/symmetric-trace-of-rigid.md) of $D$, with [canonical extensions](extend.md) $\operatorname{ext}(F)$ and $\operatorname{ext}(G)$ and [factorization isomorphisms](extension-iso.md) $\varphi_F : \operatorname{ext}(F) \circ J \cong F$ and $\varphi_G : \operatorname{ext}(G) \circ J \cong G$. Let $i : F \cong G$ be a monoidal natural isomorphism. Let $H : C \to D$ be a third braided strong monoidal functor preserving $T$ in the same way, and $j : G \cong H$ a monoidal natural isomorphism. Then the [extension](extend-iso.md) of the composite $j \circ i : F \cong H$ is $\operatorname{ext}(j) \circ \operatorname{ext}(i)$.
