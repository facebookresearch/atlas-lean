# Image of the positive pairing under a monoidal functor

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $K, L : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors. In $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $(A, \mathbf{1})$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$ through the [positive pairing](../the-int-construction/positive-pairing.md). For an object $A$ of $C$, $K(\mathbf{1}, A)$ is a right dual of $K(A, \mathbf{1})$ through the image under $K$ of the positive pairing, whose coevaluation and evaluation are those of the positive pairing transported by $K$ and its tensor and unit comparisons.
