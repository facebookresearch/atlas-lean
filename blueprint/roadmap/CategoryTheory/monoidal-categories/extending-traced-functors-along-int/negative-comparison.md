# Negative comparison of two functors out of Int

Let $C$ be a symmetric monoidal category with a symmetric trace $T$, let $\mathrm{Int}(T)$ be its [Int construction](../the-int-construction/README.md), whose objects are pairs $X = (X^+, X^-)$ of objects of $C$, and let $J : C \to \mathrm{Int}(T)$, $J(A) = (A, \mathbf{1})$, be the embedding. Let $D$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $K, L : \mathrm{Int}(T) \to D$ braided strong monoidal functors. In $\mathrm{Int}(T)$, $(A, \mathbf{1})$ has the right dual $(\mathbf{1}, A)$ through the [positive pairing](../the-int-construction/positive-pairing.md). Let $i : K \circ J \cong L \circ J$ be a natural isomorphism, with components $i_A : K(A, \mathbf{1}) \to L(A, \mathbf{1})$. For an object $A$ of $C$, $K(\mathbf{1}, A)$ is a right dual of $K(A, \mathbf{1})$ through the [image of the positive pairing](mapped-positive-pairing.md) under $K$, and $L(\mathbf{1}, A)$ is a right dual of $K(A, \mathbf{1})$ through the image of the positive pairing under $L$ transported along $i_A$. The negative comparison $n_A : K(\mathbf{1}, A) \to L(\mathbf{1}, A)$ is the canonical isomorphism between these two right duals of $K(A, \mathbf{1})$.
