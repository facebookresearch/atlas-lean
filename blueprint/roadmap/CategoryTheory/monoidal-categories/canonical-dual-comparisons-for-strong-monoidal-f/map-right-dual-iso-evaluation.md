# Map Right Dual Iso evaluation

Let `F : C ⥤ D` be a monoidal functor and `X` an object of `C` with a right dual `Xᘁ`, such that `F.obj X` also has a chosen right dual. Whiskering the canonical comparison `F.obj Xᘁ ≅ (F.obj X)ᘁ` on the right by `F.obj X` and then applying the evaluation `ε_ (F.obj X) (F.obj X)ᘁ` equals the image under `F` of the evaluation `ε_ X Xᘁ`, conjugated by the tensor and unit comparison morphisms of `F`.
