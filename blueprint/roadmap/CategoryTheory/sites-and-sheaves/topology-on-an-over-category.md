# The topology induced on an over category

Let $J$ be a Grothendieck topology on a category $\mathcal{C}$ and $X$ an object of $\mathcal{C}$.
The induced topology on the category $\mathcal{C}/X$ of objects over $X$ is the Grothendieck
topology in which a sieve $S$ on an object $Y \to X$ covers if and only if the sieve on $Y$ of the
morphisms $g \colon Z \to Y$ of $\mathcal{C}$ that, as morphisms over $X$ from $Z \to Y \to X$, lie
in $S$ is a covering sieve for $J$.
