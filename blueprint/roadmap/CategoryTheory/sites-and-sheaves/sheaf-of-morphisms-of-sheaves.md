# The sheaf of morphisms between two sheaves

Let $J$ be a Grothendieck topology on a category $\mathcal{C}$, and let $F$ and $G$ be sheaves on
$(\mathcal{C}, J)$ with values in a category $\mathcal{A}$. The presheaf of sets that sends an
object $X$ to the set of morphisms of sheaves $F|_{\mathcal{C}/X} \to G|_{\mathcal{C}/X}$ between
the restrictions of $F$ and $G$ to $\mathcal{C}/X$, with the induced topology, and a morphism $X'
\to X$ to the restriction along $\mathcal{C}/X' \to \mathcal{C}/X$, is a sheaf $\mathcal{H}om(F, G)$
for $J$. For sheaves of groups, its sections are homomorphisms of sheaves of groups.
