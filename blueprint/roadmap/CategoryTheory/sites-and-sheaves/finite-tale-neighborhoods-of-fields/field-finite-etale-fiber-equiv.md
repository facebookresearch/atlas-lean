# Geometric lifts to a finite étale spectrum are algebra maps into Ω

Let k and Ω be fields of universe u, with a k-algebra structure on Ω and Ω separably closed.
For a finite étale k-algebra A, geometric points Spec(Ω) → Spec(A) over Spec(k) are equivalent
to k-algebra maps A → Ω. The equivalence is the spectrum–algebra Hom correspondence, with
inverse given by taking the spectrum map.
