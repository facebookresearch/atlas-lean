# Lifted basis restrictions are lifts of the same spectrum restrictions

For a field k, a small-étale additive-group sheaf F of universe w, and f : A → B in the
opposite finite étale basis, the coefficient-lifted basis restriction sends up(x) at Spec(B)
to up(F(Spec(f).op)(x)) at Spec(A). The coefficient lift is ULift^u.
