# The finite-étale basis and finite-cover identities

For a field k of universe u, this proposition asserts the following conjunction for the
spectrum functor S and induced topology J on (FiniteEtale k)ᵒᵖ: S is an equivalence and equals
the forward functor of fieldFiniteEtaleEquivalence; S(A) is represented by A with structure
map Spec(k→A); each arrow is the opposite underlying ring map; an arbitrary family of universe
v covers exactly when its spectra are jointly surjective; every such cover has a finite
subfamily; every covering sieve contains a covering family indexed by a finite type of
universe u+1; the finite-family coverage generates J; and the empty family covers the
zero-algebra object.
