# The prescribed fiber coordinate is the spectrum–algebra Hom map

For a field k, a prescribed separable closure Ω, finite étale k-algebra A, and a geometric
lift z : Spec(Ω) → Spec(A) over k, the forward fiber comparison is Spec.homEquivAlgHom applied
to z with its commuting triangle. It yields the k-algebra map A → Ω represented by z.
