# The prescribed separable closure gives the geometric algebra-Hom fiber

Let k,Ω be fields of universe u with IsSepClosure k Ω. The geometric fiber of each finite
étale spectrum over Spec(Ω) is naturally isomorphic to the finite set Hom_k-alg(A,Ω). This is
the specialization of the separably closed fiber comparison to the chosen separable closure.
