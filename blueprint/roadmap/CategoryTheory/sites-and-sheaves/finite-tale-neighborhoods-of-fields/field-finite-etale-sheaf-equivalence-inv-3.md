# Basis restriction retains the presheaf restriction arrows

Let Q be an additive-group sheaf of height max(u,w) on the small étale site of Spec(k). For an
arrow in the opposite of the finite étale basis, the restriction map of its basis sheaf equals
Q's restriction along the image arrow under fieldFiniteEtaleToEtale. This is equality of the
full presheaf restriction maps.
