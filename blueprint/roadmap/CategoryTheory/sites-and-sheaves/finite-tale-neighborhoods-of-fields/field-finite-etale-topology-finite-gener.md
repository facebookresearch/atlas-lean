# A covering sieve contains a finite covering family

Let k be a field and S a sieve on A in (FiniteEtale k)ᵒᵖ. Then S covers if and only if there
is a finite index type I of universe u+1 and arrows f_i : B_i → A whose generated sieve is
contained in S and itself covers A. The finite family may be empty when A has empty spectrum.
