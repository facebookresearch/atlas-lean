affine étale basis, finite covering-family topology including the empty cover, additive-group
sheaf extension/restriction with coefficient lifts and natural comparisons, and the natural
geometric-fiber/algebra-Hom correspondence all satisfy their specified equations. Formally,
FieldFiniteEtaleSiteLaws k, FieldFiniteEtaleSheafLaws k, and FieldFiniteEtaleFiberLaws k Ω
