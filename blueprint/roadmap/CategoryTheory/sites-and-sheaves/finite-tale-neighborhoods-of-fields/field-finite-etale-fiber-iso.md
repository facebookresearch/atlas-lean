# The geometric fiber functor is the finite algebra-Hom functor

For a field k and a separably closed k-field Ω in universe u, the functor taking finite étale
spectra to the geometric fiber over Spec(Ω) is naturally isomorphic to A ↦ Hom_k-alg(A,Ω),
viewed as finite sets. Its maps are precomposition by the corresponding opposite algebra
morphisms.
