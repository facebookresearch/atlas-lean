# Spectra of finite étale algebras form the affine field basis

For a field k of universe u, define a functor from (FiniteEtale k)ᵒᵖ to the affine étale
category over Spec(k). It assigns to A the affine object represented by Spec(A) → Spec(k), and
an opposite algebra map induces the corresponding spectrum arrow. The algebra carriers remain
those of CommAlgCat.FiniteEtale.{u} k.
