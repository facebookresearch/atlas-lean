# The field-basis sheaf equivalence and coefficient-lift equations

Let k have universe u, E=(Ext,Res) be the additive-group sheaf equivalence between its finite
étale basis and full small site at height max(u,w), with unit η and counit ε, and L the
ULift^u coefficient functor. This proposition asserts that Res evaluates objects, morphisms,
and restrictions on the same spectrum arrows; η and ε are natural and satisfy both triangle
identities; every ε component is invertible and commutes with full-site restrictions and sheaf
morphisms; Res(L(F))(A)=ULift^u(F(Spec A)); and the lifted morphism and restriction values are
respectively up(τ(x)) and up(ρ(x)).
