# Geometric fiber coordinates are natural under algebra maps

Let k,Ω be fields in universe u with Ω a separably closed k-algebra. For f : A → B in the
opposite finite étale category and a geometric lift z to Spec(A), the fiber map to Spec(B) has
algebra coordinate h_z∘f.unop : B → Ω. Thus composition of spectrum arrows corresponds to
precomposition of the k-algebra embedding.
