# A coefficient lift restricts to the lifted spectrum-section group

Let k be a field of universe u and F an additive-group small-étale sheaf with coefficient
universe w. Lift coefficients by ULift^u to height max(u,w), then restrict to the finite étale
algebra basis. Its value at A is AddCommGrpCat.of(ULift^u(F(Spec(A)))), with exactly this one
coefficient lift.
