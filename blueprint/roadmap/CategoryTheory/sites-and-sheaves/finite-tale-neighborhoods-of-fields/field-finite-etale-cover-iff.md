# An algebra family covers exactly when its spectra are jointly surjective

For a field k, an object A of the opposite finite étale algebra category, and a family f_i :
B_i → A indexed by an arbitrary type, the generated sieve covers A if and only if the union of
the point-set ranges of Spec(B_i) → Spec(A) is all Spec(A). The covering arrows are the same
family f_i.
