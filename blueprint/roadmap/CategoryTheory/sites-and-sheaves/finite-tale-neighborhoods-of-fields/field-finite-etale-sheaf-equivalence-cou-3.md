# The sheaf-equivalence counit commutes with full-site restrictions

Let Q be an additive-group sheaf on the small étale site of Spec(k), Ext and Res the
finite-basis equivalence, and ε its counit. For f : U → V in the full étale category, ρ_f^{Ext
Res Q}≫ε_{Q,U}=ε_{Q,V}≫ρ_f^Q. Both maps go from Ext Res Q(V) to Q(U).
