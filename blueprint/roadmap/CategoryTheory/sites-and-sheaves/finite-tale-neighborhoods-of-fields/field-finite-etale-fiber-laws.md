# The prescribed fiber correspondence and its naturality equations

For a field k and a chosen separable closure Ω, this proposition asserts that the fiber
comparison at every finite étale algebra A sends a geometric lift z to its spectrum–algebra
Hom coordinate, sends an algebra map h : A → Ω back to Spec(h), and sends the fiber image
under any basis arrow f : A → B to the algebra coordinate h_z∘f.unop. The point is Spec(Ω) →
Spec(k) throughout.
