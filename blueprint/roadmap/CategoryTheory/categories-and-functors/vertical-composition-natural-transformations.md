# Vertical composite of natural transformations

Let $\mathsf{C}$ and $\mathsf{D}$ be categories, let $F, G, H\colon \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{D}$ be functors, and let $\alpha\colon F \Rightarrow G$ and $\beta\colon G \Rightarrow H$ be natural transformations. The *vertical composite* of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ is the natural transformation $\beta \cdot \alpha\colon F \Rightarrow H$ whose component at each object $c$ of $\mathsf{C}$ is the composite $(\beta \cdot \alpha)_c = \beta_c \circ \alpha_c\colon Fc \to Hc$ in $\mathsf{D}$.
