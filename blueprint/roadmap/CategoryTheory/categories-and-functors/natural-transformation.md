# Natural transformation

Let $F, G\colon \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{D}$ be functors between categories $\mathsf{C}$ and $\mathsf{D}$. A *natural transformation* $\alpha\colon F \Rightarrow G$ is a family of morphisms $\alpha_c\colon Fc \to Gc$ of $\mathsf{D}$, one for each object $c$ of $\mathsf{C}$ (the *components* of $\alpha$), such that $\alpha_{c'} \circ Ff = Gf \circ \alpha_c$ for every morphism $f\colon c \to c'$ of $\mathsf{C}$.
