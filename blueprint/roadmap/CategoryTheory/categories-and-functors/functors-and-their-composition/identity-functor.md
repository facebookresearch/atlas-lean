# Identity functor

Let $\mathsf{C}$ be a category. The *identity functor* $1_{\mathsf{C}}\colon \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{C}$ sends each object $c$ of $\mathsf{C}$ to $c$ and each morphism $f\colon c \to d$ of $\mathsf{C}$ to $f$ itself.

## Sources

- Emily Riehl, Category Theory in Context, Dover Publications, 2016.
- John C. Baez, *Applied Category Theory Course*, Lecture 41, "Composing Functors", 2018, https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/act_course/lecture_41.html: the identity functor of a category sends each object x to x and each morphism f to f.
