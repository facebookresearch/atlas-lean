# A category with all small products and equalizers has all small limits

Fix a universe of small sets. Let $\mathsf{C}$ be a category in which every family of objects indexed by a small set has a product, and every parallel pair of morphisms has an equalizer. Then every diagram $F\colon \mathsf{J} \to \mathsf{C}$ whose shape $\mathsf{J}$ is a small category (its objects and its morphisms form small sets) has a limit.
