# Right adjoints preserve limits

Let $F\colon \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{D}$ and $G\colon \mathsf{D} \to \mathsf{C}$ be functors with $F$ left adjoint to $G$. Then $G$ preserves limits: for every category $\mathsf{J}$ and every diagram $K\colon \mathsf{J} \to \mathsf{D}$, $G$ sends each limit cone over $K$ to a limit cone over $G \circ K$.
