# Adjunction between functors

An adjunction $F \dashv G$ between functors $F\colon \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{D}$ and $G\colon \mathsf{D} \to \mathsf{C}$ of locally small categories is a family of bijections $\varphi_{c,d}\colon \mathsf{D}(Fc, d) \to \mathsf{C}(c, Gd)$, one for each object $c$ of $\mathsf{C}$ and $d$ of $\mathsf{D}$, natural contravariantly in $c$ and covariantly in $d$. Mathlib defines an adjunction $F \dashv G$ between functors of arbitrary categories as data given by its unit and counit: natural transformations $\eta\colon 1_{\mathsf{C}} \to GF$ and $\varepsilon\colon FG \to 1_{\mathsf{D}}$ satisfying the triangle identities $\varepsilon_{Fc} \circ F(\eta_c) = 1_{Fc}$ for every object $c$ and $G(\varepsilon_d) \circ \eta_{Gd} = 1_{Gd}$ for every object $d$; it is a structure of chosen data, not the mere existence of an adjoint. Every adjunction gives bijections $\mathsf{D}(Fc, d) \to \mathsf{C}(c, Gd)$ sending $h$ to $G(h) \circ \eta_c$, with inverse $k \mapsto \varepsilon_d \circ F(k)$, and Mathlib proves that they are natural in $c$ and in $d$. Conversely, Mathlib constructs an adjunction from any family of bijections $\varphi_{c,d}$ natural in both variables, with unit $\eta_c = \varphi_{c,Fc}(1_{Fc})$ and counit $\varepsilon_d = \varphi_{Gd,d}^{-1}(1_{Gd})$, and proves that the bijections of the constructed adjunction are the given $\varphi_{c,d}$.
