# A triangulated subcategory closed under isomorphisms and containing the objects of `C` contains every twisted object with zero twist and at most one summand

**A triangulated subcategory closed under isomorphisms and containing the objects of `C`
contains every twisted object with zero twist and at most one summand**: with no summand it is a
zero object, and with one summand `X[r]` it is isomorphic to the shift by `r` of the object `X` of
`C`.
