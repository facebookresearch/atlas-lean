# Every one-sided twisted complex lies in any triangulated subcategory closed under isomorphisms and containing the objects of `C`

**Every one-sided twisted complex lies in any triangulated subcategory closed under
isomorphisms and containing the objects of `C`**.  By induction on the number of summands: a
summand `i₀` of maximal level receives no entry of the twist, so the twisted complex is the
extension of its summand `i₀`, which has zero twist, by the one-sided twisted complex formed by
the other summands.
