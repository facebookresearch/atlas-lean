# Theorem 2.11.6

(i) Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a finite module category over $\mathcal{C}$. Then there exists an algebra $A \in \mathcal{C}$ and a module equivalence $\mathcal{M} \simeq \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$. (ii) Let $\mathcal{M}$ be an exact module category over $\mathcal{C}$ and let $M \in \mathcal{M}$ be an object such that $[M]$ generates $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{M})$ as $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-module over $\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal{C})$. Then there is a module equivalence $\mathcal{M} \simeq \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$ where $A = \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M, M)$.
