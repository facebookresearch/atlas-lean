# Theorem 2.11.2

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a module category over $\mathcal{C}$, and assume that $M \in \mathcal{M}$ satisfies two conditions: 1. The functor $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M, \bullet)$ is right exact (note that it is automatically left exact). 2. For any $N \in \mathcal{M}$ there exists $X \in \mathcal{C}$ and a surjection $X \otimes M \rightarrow N$. Let $A = \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M, M)$. Then the functor $F := \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M, \bullet): \mathcal{M} \rightarrow \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$ is an equivalence of module categories.
