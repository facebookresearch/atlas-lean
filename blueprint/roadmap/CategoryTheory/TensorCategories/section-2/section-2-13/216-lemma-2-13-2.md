# Lemma 2.13.2

Let $\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2}, \mathcal{M}_{3}$ be exact module categories over $\mathcal{C}$. The bifunctor of composition $Fun_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{2}, \mathcal{M}_{3}) \times \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2}) \rightarrow Fun_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{3})$ is biexact.
