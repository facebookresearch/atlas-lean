# Corollary 2.13.4

Let $\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2}$ be exact module categories over $\mathcal{C}$. Any functor $F \in Fun_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2})$ maps projective objects to projectives.
