# Proposition 2.7.8

Let $\mathcal{M}_1$ and $\mathcal{M}_2$ be two module categories over $\mathcal{C}$. Assume that $\mathcal{M}_1$ is exact. Then any additive module functor $F \colon \mathcal{M}_1 \to \mathcal{M}_2$ is exact.
