# Lemma 2.7.6

The relation on $\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal{M})$ defined by: two objects $X, Y \in \operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal{M})$ are related if $Y$ appears as a subquotient of $L \otimes X$ for some $L \in \mathcal{C}$, is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.
