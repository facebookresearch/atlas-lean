# Theorem 2.14.11

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be an exact module category over $\mathcal{C}$. The maps $\mathcal{M}_{1} \mapsto \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M})$ and $\mathcal{M}_{2} \mapsto \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{M}}^{*}}(\mathcal{M}_{2}, \mathcal{M})$ are mutually inverse bijections of the sets of equivalence classes of exact module categories over $\mathcal{C}$ and over $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{M}}^{*}$.
