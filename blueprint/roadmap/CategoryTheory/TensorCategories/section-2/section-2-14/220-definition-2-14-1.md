# Definition 2.14.1

We denote the category $\operatorname{Fun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{M})$ as $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{M}}^{*}$ and call it the dual category to $\mathcal{C}$ with respect to $\mathcal{M}$.
