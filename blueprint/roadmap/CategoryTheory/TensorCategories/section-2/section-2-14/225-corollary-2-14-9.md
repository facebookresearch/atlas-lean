# Corollary 2.14.9

Assume that $\mathcal{C}$ is a finite tensor (not only multitensor) category. Then an exact module category $\mathcal{M}$ over $\mathcal{C}$ is indecomposable over $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{M}}^{*}$.
