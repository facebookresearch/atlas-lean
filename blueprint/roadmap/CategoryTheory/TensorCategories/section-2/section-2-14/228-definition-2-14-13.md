# Definition 2.14.13

The corresponding dual category $\mathcal{Z}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{C} \boxtimes \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}^{*}$ is called the Drinfeld center of $\mathcal{C}$.
