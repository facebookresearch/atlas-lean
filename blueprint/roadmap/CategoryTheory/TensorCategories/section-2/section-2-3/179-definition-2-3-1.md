# Definition 2.3.1

A (left or right) abelian module category over a multitensor category $\mathcal{C}$ is a locally finite abelian category $\mathcal{M}$ over $k$ which is equipped with a structure of a (left or right) $\mathcal{C}$-module category, such that the functor $\otimes^{\mathcal{M}}$ is bilinear on morphisms and exact in the first variable.
