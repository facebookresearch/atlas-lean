# Definition 2.9.18

We say that two algebras $A$ and $B$ in $\mathcal{C}$ are Morita equivalent if the module categories $\operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$ and $\operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(B)$ are module equivalent.
