# Definition 2.9.1

An algebra in a multitensor category $\mathcal{C}$ is a triple $(A, m, u)$ where $A$ is an object of $\mathcal{C}$, and $m, u$ are morphisms (called multiplication and unit morphisms) $m \colon A \otimes A \to A$, $u \colon \mathbf{1} \to A$ such that the following axioms are satisfied: 1. Associativity: the appropriate diagram commutes. 2. Unit: The morphisms $A \to \mathbf{1} \otimes A \to A \otimes A \to A$ and $A \to A \otimes \mathbf{1} \to A \otimes A \to A$ are both equal to $\operatorname{Id}_A$.
