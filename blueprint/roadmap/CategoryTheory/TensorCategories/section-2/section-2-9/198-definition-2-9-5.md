# Definition 2.9.5

A (right) module over an algebra $(A, m, u)$ (or just an $A$-module) is a pair $(M, p)$, where $M \in \mathcal{C}$ and $p$ is a morphism $M \otimes A \to M$ such that the following axioms are satisfied: 1. The appropriate diagram commutes. 2. The composition $M \to M \otimes \mathbf{1} \to M \otimes A \to M$ is the identity.
