# Proposition 2.9.10

The category $\operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$ together with functor $\tilde{\otimes}$ and associativity and unit constraints $\tilde{a}, \tilde{l}$ is a left module category over $\mathcal{C}$.
