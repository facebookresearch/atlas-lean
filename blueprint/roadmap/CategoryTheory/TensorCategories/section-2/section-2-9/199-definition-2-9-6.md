# Definition 2.9.6

The homomorphism between two $A$-modules $(M_1, p_1)$ and $(M_2, p_2)$ is a morphism $l \in \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M_1, M_2)$ such that the appropriate diagram commutes.
