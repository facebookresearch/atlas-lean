# Proposition 2.4.1

Let $\mathcal{M}_1, \mathcal{M}_2$ be two module categories over $\mathcal{C}$. Then the category $\mathcal{M} = \mathcal{M}_1 \oplus \mathcal{M}_2$ with $\otimes^{\mathcal{M}} = \otimes^{\mathcal{M}_1} \oplus \otimes^{\mathcal{M}_2}$, $a^{\mathcal{M}} = a^{\mathcal{M}_1} \oplus a^{\mathcal{M}_2}$, $l^{\mathcal{M}} = l^{\mathcal{M}_1} \oplus l^{\mathcal{M}_2}$ is a module category over $\mathcal{C}$.
