# Definition 2.5.4

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be multitensor categories. A $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$-bimodule category is a module category over $\mathcal{C} \boxtimes \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}$.
