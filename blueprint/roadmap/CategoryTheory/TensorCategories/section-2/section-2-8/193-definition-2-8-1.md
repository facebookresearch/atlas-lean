# Definition 2.8.1

Let $K$ be a $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-ring with basis $\{b_i\}$. A $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-module over $K$ is a $K$-module $M$ with fixed $\mathbb{Z}$-basis $\{m_l\}$ such that all the structure constants $a_{il}^k$ (defined by the equality $b_i m_l = \sum_k a_{il}^k m_k$) are nonnegative integers.
