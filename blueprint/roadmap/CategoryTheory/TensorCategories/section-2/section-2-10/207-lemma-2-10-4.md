# Lemma 2.10.4

There are canonical isomorphisms: (1) $\operatorname{Hom}(X \otimes M_1, M_2) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X, \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_1, M_2))$, (2) $\operatorname{Hom}(M_1, X \otimes M_2) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, X \otimes \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_1, M_2))$, (3) $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(X \otimes M_1, M_2) \cong \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_1, M_2) \otimes X^*$, (4) $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_1, X \otimes M_2) \cong X \otimes \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_1, M_2)$.
