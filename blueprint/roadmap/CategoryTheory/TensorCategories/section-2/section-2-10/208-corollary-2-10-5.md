# Corollary 2.10.5

(1) For a fixed $M_{1}$, the assignment $M_{2} \mapsto \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_{1}, M_{2})$ is a module functor $\mathcal{M} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$; (2) For a fixed $M_{2}$, the assignment $M_{1} \mapsto \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(M_{1}, M_{2})$ is a module functor $\mathcal{M} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$.
