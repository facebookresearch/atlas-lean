# Proposition 2.12.2

Assume that $\mathcal{M}_{1} \simeq \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(A)$ and $\mathcal{M}_{2} \simeq \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(B)$ for some algebras $A, B \in \mathcal{C}$. The category $Fun_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{M}_{1}, \mathcal{M}_{2})$ is equivalent to the category of $A$-$B$-bimodules via the functor which sends a bimodule $M$ to the functor $\bullet \otimes_{A} M$.
