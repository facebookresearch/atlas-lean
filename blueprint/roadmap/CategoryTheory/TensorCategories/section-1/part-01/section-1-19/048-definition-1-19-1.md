# Definition 1.19.1

A quasi-fiber functor on $\mathcal{C}$ is an exact faithful functor $F \colon \mathcal{C} \to \operatorname{Vec}$ from $\mathcal{C}$ to the category of finite dimensional $k$-vector spaces, such that $F(\mathbf{1}) = k$, equipped with an isomorphism $J \colon F(\bullet) \otimes F(\bullet) \to F(\bullet \otimes \bullet)$. If in addition $J$ is a monoidal structure (i.e., satisfies the monoidal structure axiom), one says that $F$ is a fiber functor.
