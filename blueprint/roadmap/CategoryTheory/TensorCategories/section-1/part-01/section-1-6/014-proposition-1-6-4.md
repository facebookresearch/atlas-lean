# Proposition 1.6.4

The functor $F \colon A\text{-bimod} \to \operatorname{End}(\mathcal{C})$ takes values in the full monoidal subcategory $\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{re}}(\mathcal{C})$ of right exact endofunctors of $\mathcal{C}$, and defines an equivalence between monoidal categories $A\text{-bimod}$ and $\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{re}}(\mathcal{C})$.
