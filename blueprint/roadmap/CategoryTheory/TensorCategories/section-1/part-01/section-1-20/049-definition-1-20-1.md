# Definition 1.20.1

A coalgebra (with counit) over a field $k$ is a $k$-vector space $C$ together with a comultiplication (or coproduct) $\Delta \colon C \to C \otimes C$ and counit $\varepsilon \colon C \to k$ such that (i) $\Delta$ is coassociative, i.e., $(\Delta \otimes \operatorname{Id}) \circ \Delta = (\operatorname{Id} \otimes \Delta) \circ \Delta$ as maps $C \to C^{\otimes 3}$; (ii) one has $(\varepsilon \otimes \operatorname{Id}) \circ \Delta = (\operatorname{Id} \otimes \varepsilon) \circ \Delta = \operatorname{Id}$ as maps $C \to C$ (the counit axiom).
