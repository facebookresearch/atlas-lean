# Proposition 1.15.5

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a multiring category. (1) $\mathcal{C} = \bigoplus_{i,j \in I} \mathcal{C}_{ij}$. Thus every indecomposable object of $\mathcal{C}$ belongs to some $\mathcal{C}_{ij}$. (2) The tensor product maps $\mathcal{C}_{ij} \times \mathcal{C}_{kl}$ to $\mathcal{C}_{il}$, and it is zero unless $j = k$. (3) The categories $\mathcal{C}_{ii}$ are ring categories with unit objects $\mathbf{1}_{i}$ (which are tensor categories if $\mathcal{C}$ is rigid). (4) The functors of left and right duals, if they are defined, map $\mathcal{C}_{ij}$ to $\mathcal{C}_{ji}$.
