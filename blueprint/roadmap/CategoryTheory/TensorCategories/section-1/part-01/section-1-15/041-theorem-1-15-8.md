# Theorem 1.15.8

(i) In a ring category with right duals, the unit object $\mathbf{1}$ is simple. (ii) In a multiring category with right duals, the unit object $\mathbf{1}$ is semisimple, and is a direct sum of pairwise non-isomorphic simple objects $\mathbf{1}_{i}$.
