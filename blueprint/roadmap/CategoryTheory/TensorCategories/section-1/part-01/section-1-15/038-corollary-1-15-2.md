# Corollary 1.15.2

In any multiring category $\mathcal{C}$ the unit object $\mathbf{1}$ is isomorphic to a direct sum of pairwise non-isomorphic indecomposable objects: $\mathbf{1} \cong \bigoplus_{i} \mathbf{1}_{i}$.
