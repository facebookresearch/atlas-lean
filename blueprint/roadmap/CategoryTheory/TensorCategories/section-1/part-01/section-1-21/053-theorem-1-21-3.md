# Theorem 1.21.3

The assignments $(C, F) \mapsto H = \operatorname{End}(F)$, $H \mapsto (\operatorname{Rep}(H), \operatorname{Forget})$ are mutually inverse bijections between 1) finite abelian $k$-linear monoidal categories $C$ with a fiber functor $F$, up to monoidal equivalence and isomorphism of monoidal functors; 2) finite dimensional bialgebras $H$ over $k$ up to isomorphism.
