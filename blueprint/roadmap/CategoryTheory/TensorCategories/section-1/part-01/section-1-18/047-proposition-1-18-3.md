# Proposition 1.18.3

There is a canonical algebra isomorphism $\alpha_{F_1, F_2} \colon \operatorname{End}(F_1) \otimes \operatorname{End}(F_2) \cong \operatorname{End}(F_1 \otimes F_2)$ given by $\alpha_{F_1, F_2}(\eta_1 \otimes \eta_2)|_{F_1(X) \otimes F_2(Y)} \coloneqq \eta_1|_{F_1(X)} \otimes \eta_2|_{F_2(Y)}$ where $\eta_i \in \operatorname{End}(F_i)$, $i = 1, 2$.
