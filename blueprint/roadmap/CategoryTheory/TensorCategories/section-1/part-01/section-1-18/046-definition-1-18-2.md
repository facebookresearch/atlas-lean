# Definition 1.18.2

A $k$-linear abelian category $\mathcal{C}$ is finite if (i) $\mathcal{C}$ has finite dimensional spaces of morphisms; (ii) every object of $\mathcal{C}$ has finite length; (iii) $\mathcal{C}$ has enough projectives, i.e., every simple object of $\mathcal{C}$ has a projective cover; and (iv) there are finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects.
