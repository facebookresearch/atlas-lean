# Proposition 1.22.1

(The antipode axiom) Let $\mu \colon H \otimes H \to H$ and $i \colon k \to H$ be the multiplication and the unit maps of $H$. Then $\mu \circ (\operatorname{Id} \otimes S) \circ \Delta = i \circ \varepsilon = \mu \circ (S \otimes \operatorname{Id}) \circ \Delta$ as maps $H \to H$.
