# Definition 1.12.3

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally finite $k$-linear abelian rigid monoidal category. We will call $\mathcal{C}$ a multitensor category over $k$ if the bifunctor $\otimes$ is bilinear on morphisms. If in addition $\operatorname{End}(\mathbf{1}) \cong k$ then we will call $\mathcal{C}$ a tensor category. A multifusion category is a semisimple multitensor category with finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects. A fusion category is a semisimple tensor category with finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects.
