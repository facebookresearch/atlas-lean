# Proposition 1.12.2

In a locally finite abelian category $\mathcal{C}$, $\operatorname{Hom}(X, Y)=0$ if $X, Y$ are simple and non-isomorphic, and $\operatorname{Hom}(X, X)=k$ for any simple object $X$.
