# Definition 1.12.1

A $k$-linear abelian category is said to be locally finite if it is essentially small, and the following two conditions are satisfied: (i) for any two objects $X, Y$ in $\mathcal{C}$, the space $\operatorname{Hom}(X, Y)$ is finite dimensional; (ii) every object in $\mathcal{C}$ has finite length.
