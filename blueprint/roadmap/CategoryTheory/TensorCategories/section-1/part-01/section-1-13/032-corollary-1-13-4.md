# Corollary 1.13.4

For any pair of morphisms $f_{1}, f_{2}$ in a multiring category $\mathcal{C}$ one has $\operatorname{Im}(f_{1} \otimes f_{2})=\operatorname{Im}(f_{1}) \otimes \operatorname{Im}(f_{2})$.
