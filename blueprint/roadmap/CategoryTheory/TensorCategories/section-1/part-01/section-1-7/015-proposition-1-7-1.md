# Proposition 1.7.1

(i) The monoidal isomorphisms $F_{f,\mu} \to F_{f,\mu'}$ of monoidal functors $F_{f,\mu_i} \colon \mathcal{C}_{G_1}^{\omega_1} \to \mathcal{C}_{G_2}^{\omega_2}$ form a torsor over the group $H^1(G_1, k^{\times}) = \operatorname{Hom}(G_1, k^{\times})$ of characters of $G_1$; (ii) Given $f$, the set of $\mu$ parametrizing isomorphism classes of $F_{f,\mu}$ is a torsor over $H^2(G_1, k^{\times})$; (iii) The structures of a monoidal category on $(\mathcal{C}_G, \otimes)$ are parametrized by $H^3(G, k^{\times})/\operatorname{Out}(G)$, where $\operatorname{Out}(G)$ is the group of outer automorphisms of $G$.
