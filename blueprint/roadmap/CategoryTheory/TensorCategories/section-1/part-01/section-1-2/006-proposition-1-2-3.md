# Proposition 1.2.3

For any object $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$ one has the equalities $l_{\mathbf{1} \otimes X} = \operatorname{Id} \otimes l_X$ and $r_{X \otimes \mathbf{1}} = r_X \otimes \operatorname{Id}$.
