# Proposition 1.2.1

The triangle diagram $(X \otimes \mathbf{1}) \otimes Y \to X \otimes (\mathbf{1} \otimes Y) \to X \otimes Y$ (with maps $a_{X,\mathbf{1},Y}$ and $\operatorname{Id}_X \otimes l_Y$, and $r_X \otimes \operatorname{Id}_Y$) is commutative for all $X, Y \in \mathcal{C}$. In particular, one has $r_{\mathbf{1}} = l_{\mathbf{1}} = \iota$.
