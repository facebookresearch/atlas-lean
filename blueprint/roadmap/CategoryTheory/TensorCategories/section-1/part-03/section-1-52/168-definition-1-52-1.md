# Definition 1.52.1

A left integral in an algebra $H$ with a counit $\varepsilon \colon H \to k$ is an element $I \in H$ such that $xI = \varepsilon(x) I$ for all $x \in H$. Similarly, a right integral in $H$ is an element $I \in H$ such that $Ix = \varepsilon(x) I$ for all $x \in H$.
