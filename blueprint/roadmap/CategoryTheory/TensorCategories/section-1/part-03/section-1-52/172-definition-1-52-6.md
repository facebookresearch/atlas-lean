# Definition 1.52.6

A finite tensor category $\mathcal{C}$ is unimodular if $L_\rho = \mathbf{1}$. A finite dimensional quasi-Hopf algebra $H$ is unimodular if $\operatorname{Rep}(H)$ is a unimodular category, i.e., if left and right integrals in $H$ coincide.
