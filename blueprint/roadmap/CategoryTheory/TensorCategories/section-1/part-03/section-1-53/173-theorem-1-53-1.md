# Theorem 1.53.1

Suppose that $\mathcal{C}$ is not semisimple, and admits an isomorphism of additive functors $u \colon \operatorname{Id} \to {}^{**}$. Then the Cartan matrix $C$ is degenerate over the ground field $k$.
