# Corollary 1.50.2

In the above situation, one has $\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{C}) \geq \operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{D})$, and $\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{D})$ divides $\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{C})$ in the ring of algebraic integers. In fact, $\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{C})/\operatorname{FPdim}(\mathcal{D}) = \sum \operatorname{FPdim}(X_i) \dim \operatorname{Hom}(F(P_i), \mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{D}})$, where $X_i$ runs over simple objects of $\mathcal{C}$.
