# Proposition 1.47.2

For any object $Z$ of $\mathcal{C}$, $P_{i} \otimes Z \cong \oplus_{j,k} N_{kj^{*}}^{i}[Z: X_{j}] P_{k}$ and $Z \otimes P_{i} \cong \oplus_{j,k} N_{{}^{*}jk}^{i}[Z: X_{j}] P_{k}$.
