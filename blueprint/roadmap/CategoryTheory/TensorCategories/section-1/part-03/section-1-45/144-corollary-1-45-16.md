# Corollary 1.45.16

Let $A$ be a fusion ring, and $X \in A$ a basis element. Then if $\mathrm{FPdim}(X)<2$ then $\mathrm{FPdim}(X)=2\cos(\pi/n)$, for some integer $n \geq 3$.
