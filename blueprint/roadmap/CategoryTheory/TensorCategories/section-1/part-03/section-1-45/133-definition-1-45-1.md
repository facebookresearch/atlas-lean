# Definition 1.45.1

We will say that a $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-ring $A$ with $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-basis $I$ is transitive if for any $X, Z \in I$ there exist $Y_{1}, Y_{2} \in I$ such that $X Y_{1}$ and $Y_{2} X$ involve $Z$ with a nonzero coefficient.
