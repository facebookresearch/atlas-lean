# Proposition 1.45.15

(Kronecker) Let $B$ be a matrix with nonnegative integer entries, such that $\lambda(B B^{T}) = \lambda(B)^{2}$. If $\lambda(B) < 2$ then $\lambda(B) = 2 \cos(\pi/n)$ for some integer $n \geq 2$.
