# Theorem 1.23.1

Let $C$ be a $k$-linear abelian category with an exact faithful functor $F \colon C \to \operatorname{Vec}$. Then $F$ defines an equivalence between $C$ and the category of finite dimensional right comodules over $C \coloneqq \operatorname{Coend}(F)$ (or, equivalently, with the category of continuous finite dimensional left $\operatorname{End}(F)$-modules).
