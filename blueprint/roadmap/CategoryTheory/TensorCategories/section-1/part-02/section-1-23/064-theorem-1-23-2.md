# Theorem 1.23.2

The assignments $(C, F) \mapsto H = \operatorname{Coend}(F)$, $H \mapsto (H\text{-Comod}, \operatorname{Forget})$ are mutually inverse bijections between 1) $k$-linear abelian monoidal categories $C$ with a fiber functor $F$, up to monoidal equivalence and isomorphism of monoidal functors, and bialgebras over $k$, up to isomorphism; 2) $k$-linear abelian monoidal categories $C$ with right duals with a fiber functor $F$, up to monoidal equivalence and isomorphism of monoidal functors, and bialgebras over $k$ with an antipode, up to isomorphism; 3) tensor categories $C$ over $k$ with a fiber functor $F$, up to monoidal equivalence and isomorphism of monoidal functors, and Hopf algebras over $k$, up to isomorphism.
