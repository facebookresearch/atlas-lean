# Definition 1.25.1

Let $q \in k$, $q \neq \pm 1$. The quantum group $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ is generated by elements $E$, $F$ and an invertible element $K$ with defining relations $K E K^{-1} = q^2 E$, $K F K^{-1} = q^{-2} F$, $[E, F] = (K - K^{-1})/(q - q^{-1})$.
