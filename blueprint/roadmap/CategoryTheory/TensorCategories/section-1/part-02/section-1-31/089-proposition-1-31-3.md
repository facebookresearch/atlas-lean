# Proposition 1.31.3

In a tensor category with the Chevalley property, $\operatorname{Lw}(X \otimes Y) \leq \operatorname{Lw}(X) + \operatorname{Lw}(Y) - 1$. Thus $C_i \otimes C_j \subset C_{i+j}$.
