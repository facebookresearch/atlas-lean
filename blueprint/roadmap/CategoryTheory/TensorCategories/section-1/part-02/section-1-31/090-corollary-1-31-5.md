# Corollary 1.31.5

In a pointed Hopf algebra $H$, the coradical filtration is a Hopf algebra filtration, i.e., $H_i H_j \subset H_{i+j}$ and $S(H_i) = H_i$, so $\operatorname{gr}(H)$ is a Hopf algebra.
