# Theorem 1.39.2

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a spherical category and $V$ be an object of $\mathcal{C}$. Then for any $x \in \operatorname{Hom}(V, V)$ one has $\operatorname{Tr}_{V}^{L}(a_{V} x)=\operatorname{Tr}_{V}^{R}(x a_{V}^{-1})$.
