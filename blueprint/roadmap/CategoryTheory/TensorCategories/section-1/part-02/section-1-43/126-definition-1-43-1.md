# Definition 1.43.1

A $*$-algebra is an associative algebra $B$ over $\mathbb{C}$ with an antilinear anti-involution $*: B \rightarrow B$ and a linear functional $\tau: B \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ such that $\tau(ab)=\tau(ba)$, and the Hermitian form $\tau(ab^{*})$ is positive definite.
