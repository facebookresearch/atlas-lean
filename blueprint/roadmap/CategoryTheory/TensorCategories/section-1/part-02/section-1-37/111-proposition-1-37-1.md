# Proposition 1.37.1

If $a \in \operatorname{Hom}(V, V^{**})$, $b \in \operatorname{Hom}(W, W^{**})$ then (1) $\operatorname{Tr}_V^L(a) = \operatorname{Tr}_{V^*}^R(a^*)$; (2) $\operatorname{Tr}_{V \oplus W}^L(a \oplus b) = \operatorname{Tr}_V^L(a) + \operatorname{Tr}_W^L(b)$ (in additive categories); (3) $\operatorname{Tr}_{V \otimes W}^L(a \otimes b) = \operatorname{Tr}_V^L(a) \operatorname{Tr}_W^L(b)$; (4) If $c \in \operatorname{Hom}(V, V)$ then $\operatorname{Tr}_V^L(ac) = \operatorname{Tr}_V^L(c^{**} a)$, $\operatorname{Tr}_V^R(ac) = \operatorname{Tr}_V^R({}^{**}c \cdot a)$. Similar equalities to (2), (3) also hold for right quantum traces.
