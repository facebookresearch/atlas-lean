# Definition 1.26.1

The $q$-analog of $n$ is $[n]_q = (q^n - q^{-n})/(q - q^{-1})$. The $q$-analog of the factorial is $[n]_q! = \prod_{l=1}^{n} [l]_q = ((q - q^{-1}) \cdots (q^n - q^{-n}))/(q - q^{-1})^n$.
