# Definition 1.26.2

The quantum group $U_q(\mathfrak{g})$ is generated by elements $E_i$, $F_i$ and invertible elements $K_i$, with defining relations $K_i K_j = K_j K_i$, $K_i E_j K_i^{-1} = q^{a_{ij}} E_j$, $K_i F_j K_i^{-1} = q^{-a_{ij}} F_j$, $[E_i, F_j] = \delta_{ij}(K_i^{d_i} - K_i^{-d_i})/(q^{d_i} - q^{-d_i})$, and the $q$-Serre relations (1.26.1) and (1.26.2).
