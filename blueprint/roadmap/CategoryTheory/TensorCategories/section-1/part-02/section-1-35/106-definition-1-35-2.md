# Definition 1.35.2

An antipode on a quasi-bialgebra $H$ is a triple $(S, \alpha, \beta)$, where $S \colon H \to H$ is a unital antihomomorphism and $\alpha, \beta \in H$, satisfying identities (1.35.1) and (1.35.2). A quasi-Hopf algebra is a quasi-bialgebra $(H, \Delta, \varepsilon, \Phi)$ for which there exists an antipode $(S, \alpha, \beta)$ such that $S$ is bijective.
