# Definition 1.42.1

(i) A $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-basis of an algebra free as a module over $\mathbb{Z}$ is a basis $B=\{b_{i}\}$ such that $b_{i} b_{j}=\sum_{k} c_{ij}^{k} b_{k}$, $c_{ij}^{k} \in \mathbb{Z}_{+}$. (ii) A $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-ring is an algebra over $\mathbb{Z}$ with unit equipped with a fixed $\mathbb{Z}_{+}$-basis.
