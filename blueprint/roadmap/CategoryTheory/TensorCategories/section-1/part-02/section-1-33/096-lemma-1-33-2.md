# Lemma 1.33.2

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $k$ of characteristic zero. Let $u \in SV \otimes SV$ be a symmetric element satisfying equation (1.33.2). Then $u = \Delta(w) - w \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes w$ for some $w \in SV$.
