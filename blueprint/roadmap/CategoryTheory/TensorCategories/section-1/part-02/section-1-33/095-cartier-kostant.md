# Theorem 1.33.1 (Cartier-Kostant)

Any cocommutative Hopf algebra $H$ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero is of the form $k[G] \ltimes U(\mathfrak{g})$, where $\mathfrak{g}$ is a Lie algebra, and $G$ is a group acting on $\mathfrak{g}$.
