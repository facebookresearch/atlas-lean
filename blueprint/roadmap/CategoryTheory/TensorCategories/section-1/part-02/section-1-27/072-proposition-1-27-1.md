# Proposition 1.27.1

Let $g, h$ be grouplike elements of a coalgebra $C$, and $\operatorname{Prim}_{h,g}(C)$ be the space of skew-primitive elements of type $h, g$. Then the space $\operatorname{Prim}_{h,g}(H)/k(h - g)$ is naturally isomorphic to $\operatorname{Ext}^1(g, h)$, where $g, h$ are regarded as $1$-dimensional right $C$-comodules.
