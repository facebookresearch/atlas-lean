# Algebraic K-theory

K-groups of rings and exact or Waldhausen categories: Grothendieck groups, stable general linear groups and their abelianization, higher K-theory via plus and Q constructions, Milnor symbols and Steinberg relations, and functoriality. Topological K-theory belongs to algebraic topology.
