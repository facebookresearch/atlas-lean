# Upper Unipotent le normalizer complement

The full upper-unipotent subgroup normalizes the complement.
Proved by closure induction on upper generators. Purpose: Lemma 16
normality `Xs ▷ X_I` for `S = P`, `I = P - {a}`.
Orientation: `upper * complement * upper⁻¹ ⊆ complement`.
