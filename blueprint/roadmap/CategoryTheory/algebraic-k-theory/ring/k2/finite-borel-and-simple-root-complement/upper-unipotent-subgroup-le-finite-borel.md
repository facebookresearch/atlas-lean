The finite upper-unipotent subgroup lies in the finite Borel subgroup.
Purpose: left join inclusion for Lemma-24 two-cell arguments.
Hypotheses: none beyond the rank `n`. Orientation: `upper ≤ Borel`.
Nonclaim: no quotient, Bruhat, or Matsumoto claim.
