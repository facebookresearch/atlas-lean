# Weyl conj complement mem upper

Conjugation by `weyl m (m + 1) 1` sends the complement into the upper
subgroup. Proved by closure induction on complement words using the
generator case above; the target is a subgroup so conjugation distributes
over products and inverses. Hypothesis `hm : m + 1 < n`.
Orientation: `weyl * complement * weyl⁻¹ ⊆ upper`.
Nonclaim: no complement stability, quotient, or Lemma 24 claim.
