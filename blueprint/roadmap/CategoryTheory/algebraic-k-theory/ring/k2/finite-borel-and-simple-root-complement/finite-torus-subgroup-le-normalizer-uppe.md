# Finite Torus Subgroup le normalizer upper Unipotent

Every finite-torus element normalizes the upper-unipotent subgroup.
Proved by closure induction on torus generators. Purpose: Lemma 21(a) torus
part. Orientation: `torus * upper * torus⁻¹ ⊆ upper`.
Nonclaim: no Borel product, quotient, or Matsumoto claim.
