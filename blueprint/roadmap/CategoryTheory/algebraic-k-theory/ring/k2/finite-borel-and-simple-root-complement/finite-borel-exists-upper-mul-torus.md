# Finite Borel exists upper mul torus

Borel product characterization: every `b ∈ finiteBorelSubgroup n` factors
as `b = u * h` with `u` upper-unipotent and `h` finite-torus. Proved by
closure induction on the join (as closure of the union), moving torus factors
right with torus normalization. Orientation: upper factor on the left.
Nonclaim: no uniqueness, quotient, or Matsumoto claim.
