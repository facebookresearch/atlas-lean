# The forward normalized coefficient-lift components

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The degree-n forward map of the normalized coefficient-lift
isomorphism is θ_n:m^↑ ⊗ tprod(a)↦ULift(m ⊗ tprod(a)),
for a:Fin n→Ā.
