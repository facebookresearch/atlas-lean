# The normalized level map in a common carrier universe

Let k be commutative, A,B k-modules with distinguished elements
1_A,1_B, and M,N k-modules, in independent carrier universes.
Let g : A → B and f : M → N be k-linear, with g(1_A)=1_B.
Write Ā=A/span_k{1_A} and B̄=B/span_k{1_B}.
Lifting the source by max{u_B,u_N} and the target by max{u_A,u_M}
places the normalized tensor map in their common k-module universe
max{u_k,u_A,u_B,u_M,u_N}.
