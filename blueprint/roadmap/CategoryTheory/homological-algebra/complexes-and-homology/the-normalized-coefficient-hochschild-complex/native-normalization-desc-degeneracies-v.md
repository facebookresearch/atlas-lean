# Degeneracy vanishing in bar and tensor coordinates

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
Let L be a k-module chain complex and f:C_*(A,M)→L a
chain map with f_{n+1}σ_i=0 for every unit degeneracy.
In the coefficient simplicial-bar coordinates, each f_n also
annihilates all degree-n simplicial degeneracies.
