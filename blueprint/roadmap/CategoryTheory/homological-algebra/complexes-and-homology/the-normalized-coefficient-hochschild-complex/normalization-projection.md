# The coefficient Hochschild normalization projection

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The normalization chain projection π:C_*(A,M)→N_*(A,M)
fixes M and takes each algebra tail factor to its ground-module class.
