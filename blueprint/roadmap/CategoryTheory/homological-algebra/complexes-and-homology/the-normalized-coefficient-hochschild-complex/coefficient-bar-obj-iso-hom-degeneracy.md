# The unit degeneracies in coefficient tensor coordinates

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
For the coordinate maps κ_n:B_n(A,M)≃C_n, the simplicial
degeneracy and unit-insertion map satisfy
κ_{n+1} σ_i^{bar} = σ_i^{tensor} κ_n.
