# The universal descent from a normalized degree

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
For a k-module T in the common universe and a linear map
f:C_n→T annihilating all degree-n unit degeneracies, there is
an induced map f̄:N_n→T through the normalized tensor quotient.
