# The boundary square of the normalization projection

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The full Hochschild boundary descends through the quotient:
b̄_\{n+1\} π_\{n+1\}=π_n b_\{n+1\}.
The normalized differential is taken on the whole alternating face sum.
