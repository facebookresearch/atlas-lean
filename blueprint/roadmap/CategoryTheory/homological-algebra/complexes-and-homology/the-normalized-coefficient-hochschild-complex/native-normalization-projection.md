# The raw projection to the degeneracy quotient

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The raw Hochschild chain complex maps to its degeneracy quotient
𝒩 by the projection of the coefficient simplicial bar, transported
through the tensor coordinates of each bar term.
