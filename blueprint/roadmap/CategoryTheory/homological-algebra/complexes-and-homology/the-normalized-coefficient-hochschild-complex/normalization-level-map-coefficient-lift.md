# Normalization commutes with coefficient and tensor lifting

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
At any lift height ℓ, let θ_C and θ_N identify coefficient-only
lifts with whole-tensor lifts. The tensor quotient square is
θ_N π_{U^ℓ M}=U^ℓ(π_M) θ_C.
