# The normalization square after whole-complex lifting

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The coefficient-lift isomorphisms of raw and normalized complexes
satisfy θ_N π_{U^ℓ M}=U^ℓ(π_M) θ_C as a square of
chain maps.
