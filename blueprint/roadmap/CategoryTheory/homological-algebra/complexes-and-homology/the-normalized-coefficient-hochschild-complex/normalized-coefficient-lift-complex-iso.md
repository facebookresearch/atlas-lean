# Lifting the coefficient normalized complex

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
At arbitrary additional lift height ℓ, the normalized complex
with coefficient U^ℓ M is chain isomorphic to U^ℓ N_*(A,M).
In each degree this compares U^ℓ M ⊗ Ā^{⊗n} with the whole
lift of M ⊗ Ā^{⊗n}.
