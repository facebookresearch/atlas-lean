# The projection in coefficient bar coordinates

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
Let κ_n:B_n(A,M)≃C_n be the coefficient bar coordinates.
The component of the raw projection to 𝒩 is the bar degeneracy
quotient after κ_n⁻¹.
