# The lifted algebra map kills normalized source degeneracies

Let k be commutative, A,B associative unital k-algebras,
and M,N compatible bimodules over A,B. Let g:A→B be a unital
k-algebra homomorphism and f:M→N k-linear with
f(am)=g(a)f(m) and f(ma)=f(m)g(a).
At any additional lift height ℓ, set T=max{u_k,u_A,u_B,u_M,u_N,ℓ}
and lift both coefficient modules to T.
The composite of the lifted raw chain map with target normalization
annihilates every source unit-insertion degeneracy:
π_B F(g,f) σ_i=0.
