# The degeneracy-quotient Hochschild complex

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
The degeneracy quotient 𝒩 is the chain complex obtained from
the coefficient simplicial bar by quotienting its degenerate chains.
