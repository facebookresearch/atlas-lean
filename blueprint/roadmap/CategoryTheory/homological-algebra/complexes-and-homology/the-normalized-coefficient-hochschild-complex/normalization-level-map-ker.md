# The unit-span relations in the normalization kernel

Let k be a commutative ring and A a k-module with a distinguished
element 1. Put S_A = span_k{1} and Ā = A/S_A as k-modules.
Let M be a k-module and set C_n = M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n},
N_n = M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}, with π_n = id_M ⊗ q_A^{⊗n}.
The kernel of π_n is the k-linear span of elementary tensors
m ⊗ tprod(a) for which some tail entry a_i belongs to S_A.
