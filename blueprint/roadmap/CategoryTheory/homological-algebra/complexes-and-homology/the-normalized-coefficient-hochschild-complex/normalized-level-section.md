# The inverse normalized degree-coordinate map

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
Let π_n:C_n→N_n be the factorwise quotient map and let
𝒩 be the degeneracy quotient of the coefficient simplicial bar.
The quotient universal property gives r_n:N_n→𝒩_n
satisfying r_n π_n=p_n. It is inverse to the degree-coordinate map e_n.
