# The complete normalized boundary on tensor representatives

Let k be commutative, A an associative unital k-algebra,
and M a compatible A-bimodule with commuting k-linear actions.
Put C_n=M ⊗[k] A^{⊗n}, Ā=A/span_k{1}, and N_n=M ⊗[k] Ā^{⊗n}.
On representatives m ⊗ [a_1] ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ [a_{n+1}], the normalized
boundary is
(ma_1) ⊗ [a_2] ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ [a_{n+1}]
+ ∑_\{i=1\}^\{n\} (−1)^i m ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ [a_i a_\{i+1\}] ⊗ ⋯
+ (−1)^{n+1}(a_{n+1}m) ⊗ [a_1] ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ [a_n].
Each bracketed product is computed in A before the quotient.
