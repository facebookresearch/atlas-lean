# The coefficient coinvariants quotient by the Lie action

Let R : Type u be a commutative ring and L : Type v an R-Lie algebra with its Lie ring
structure. For X : LieModuleCat.{w} R L, coefficientCoinvariants R L X is the quotient of X by
⁅⊤,⊤⁆, the Lie submodule generated by all action values ⁅l, m⁆ with l : L and m : X.
