# A coefficient chain map fixes the exterior tensor factor

Let R : Type u be a commutative ring and L : Type v an R-Lie algebra with its Lie ring
structure. Let X and Y be Lie modules over L with carrier universe w, and let f : X ⟶ Y be an
R-linear Lie-module morphism. For every n : ℕ, a : ⋀[R]^n L and m : X, the degree-n component
of coefficientComplexFunctor.map f sends a ⊗ₜ[R] m to a ⊗ₜ[R] f(m).
