# The coinvariant functor maps a lifted class by the coefficient morphism

Let R : Type u be a commutative ring and L : Type v an R-Lie algebra with its Lie ring
structure. Let X and Y be Lie modules over L with carrier universe w, and let f : X ⟶ Y be an
R-linear Lie-module morphism. For m : X, coefficientCoinvariantsFunctor.map f sends ULift.up
[m] in X/[L,X] to ULift.up [f(m)] in Y/[L,Y]. The brackets denote the corresponding
Lie-submodule quotient classes.
