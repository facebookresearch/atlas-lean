# Coefficient modules define an R-linear functor of CE complexes

Let R : Type u be a commutative ring and L : Type v an R-Lie algebra with its Lie ring
structure. The functor LieModuleCat.{w} R L ⥤ ChainComplex (ModuleCat.{max u v w} R) ℕ sends X
to the CE coefficient complex with degree-n term ⋀[R]^n L ⊗[R] X. A Lie-module morphism f acts
by identity on the exterior factor and f on the coefficient factor.
