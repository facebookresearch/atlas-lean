# The coinvariant map into the augmentation tensor

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let Q =
N/[L,N] for [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and T be the augmentation left U-module. Since unit insertion
kills the action-relation submodule, it induces an R-linear map Q → N ⊗_U T with q(m) ↦
[m,up(1)]. The map is well defined on the coinvariant quotient.
