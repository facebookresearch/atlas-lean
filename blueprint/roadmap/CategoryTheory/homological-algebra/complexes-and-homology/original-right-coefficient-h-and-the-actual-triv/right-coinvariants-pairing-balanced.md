# The coinvariant pairing respects enveloping balancing

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let Q be
its coinvariants for [x,m] = −m·ι(x), T the augmentation left U-module, and β(m,up(r)) =
r·q(m) the scalar pairing. For u ∈ U, m ∈ N, and a ∈ T, β(m·u,a) = β(m,u·a). This is the
balancing relation needed to descend β to N ⊗_U T, and follows from q(m·u) = ε(u)·q(m).
