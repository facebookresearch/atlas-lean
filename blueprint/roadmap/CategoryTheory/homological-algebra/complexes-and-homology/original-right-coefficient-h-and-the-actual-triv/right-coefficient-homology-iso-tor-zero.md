# The right-coefficient Tor comparison has the canonical zero-degree map

Let R be a commutative ring, L a free R-Lie module, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let C
be the Chevalley–Eilenberg coefficient complex with [x,m] = −m·ι(x), F(X) = N ⊗_U X, and T the
augmentation module. If Φ : H₀(C) ≅ (L₀F)(T) is the homology-to-Tor comparison and e : H₀(C) ≅
F(T) the coinvariant tensor identification, then Φ.hom ≫ F.fromLeftDerivedZero at T = e.hom.
This is an equality of R-module morphisms.
