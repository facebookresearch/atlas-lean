# The derived-zero comparison sends coefficient classes to units

Let R be a commutative ring, L a free R-Lie module, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Write
F(X) = N ⊗_U X, T for the augmentation module, and cl(m) for the H₀ class of the
empty-exterior tensor 1 ⊗ m in the converted coefficient complex. The homology-to-Tor
comparison followed by F.fromLeftDerivedZero at T sends cl(m) to [m,up(1)]. Thus its value in
F(T) agrees with the canonical unit tensor.
