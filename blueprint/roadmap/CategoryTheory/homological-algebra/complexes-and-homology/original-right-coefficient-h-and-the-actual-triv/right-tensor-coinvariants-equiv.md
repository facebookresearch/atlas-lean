# The augmentation tensor is the coinvariant quotient

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let Q be
the coinvariants for the left Lie action [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and let T be the augmentation left
U-module. The R-linear maps [m,up(r)] ↦ r·q(m) and q(m) ↦ [m,up(1)] are inverse, giving N ⊗_U
T ≃ₗ[R] Q. Neither freeness of L nor projectivity of N is required.
