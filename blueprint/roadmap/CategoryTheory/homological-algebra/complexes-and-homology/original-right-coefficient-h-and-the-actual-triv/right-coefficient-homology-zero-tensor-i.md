# Degree-zero right-coefficient homology is the augmentation tensor

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Form the
exterior-first Chevalley–Eilenberg complex using the converted action [x,m] = −m·ι(x). Its
degree-zero homology is isomorphic to N ⊗_U T, where T is the augmentation left U-module. The
isomorphism identifies H₀ with the Lie coinvariant quotient and then applies q(m) ↦ [m,up(1)].
It requires no freeness of L.
