# Right enveloping actions equal augmentation modulo relations

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let J ⊆ N
be generated by the converted left Lie-action values [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and let ε : U → R be the
augmentation. For every u ∈ U and m ∈ N, m·u − ε(u)·m belongs to J. The formula compares the
full right enveloping action with the scalar action, beyond the Lie generators.
