# The augmentation tensor map to coinvariants

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let Q =
N/[L,N] for the converted left action [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and let T be the augmentation left
U-module. The balanced pairing β(m,up(r)) = r·q(m) induces an R-linear map N ⊗_U T → Q. It
sends the balanced class [m,up(r)] to r·q(m), where q is the coinvariant quotient map.
