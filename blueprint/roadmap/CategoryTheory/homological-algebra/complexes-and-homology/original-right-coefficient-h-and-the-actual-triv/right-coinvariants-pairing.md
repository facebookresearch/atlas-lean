# The scalar pairing with right coinvariants

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Let q : N
→ Q be the coinvariant quotient for [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and let T be the augmentation left
U-module. Define the R-bilinear pairing N →ₗ[R] T →ₗ[R] Q by β(m,up(r)) = r·q(m). The second
factor is viewed as an R-module by restriction along R → U.
