# The zero-degree Tor isomorphism uses the derived-zero morphism

Let R be a commutative ring, L a free R-Lie module, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Put
F(X) = N ⊗_U X and let T be the augmentation module. The forward morphism of the isomorphism
(L₀F)(T) ≅ N ⊗_U T obtained through coefficient H₀ is exactly F.fromLeftDerivedZero at T. Thus
the constructed Tor coordinates agree with the canonical derived-zero transformation as
R-module morphisms.
