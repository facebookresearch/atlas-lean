# Right Lie-generator actions are coinvariant relations

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. Give N
the left Lie action [x,m] = −m·ι(x), and let J be the underlying R-submodule of [⊤_L,⊤_N],
generated by all these Lie-action values. For x ∈ L and m ∈ N, the right generator action
m·ι(x), written op(ι(x)) • m, belongs to J. Thus every right Lie-generator action vanishes in
N/J.
