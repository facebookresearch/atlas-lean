# Inserting the unit in the augmentation tensor

Let R be a commutative ring, L an R-Lie algebra, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. For the
augmentation left U-module T with scalar carrier ULift(R), define the R-linear map N → N ⊗_U T
by m ↦ [m,up(1)]. Here the bracket denotes the balanced tensor class, with N as the first,
right-module factor.
