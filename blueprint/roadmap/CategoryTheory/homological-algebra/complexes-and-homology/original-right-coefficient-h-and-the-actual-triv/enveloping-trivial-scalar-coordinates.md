# Scalar coordinates of the augmentation module

For a commutative ring R and an R-Lie algebra L, let U = U_R(L) and T be the trivial left
U-module with action u·up(r) = up(ε(u)r), where ε is augmentation. Restricting T along R → U
gives an R-module R-linearly equivalent to R. The coordinate equivalence sends up(r) to r; its
inverse is r ↦ up(r). The scalar ring and Lie algebra may lie in distinct universes.
