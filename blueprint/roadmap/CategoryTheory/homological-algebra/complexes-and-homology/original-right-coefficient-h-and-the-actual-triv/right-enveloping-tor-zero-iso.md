# Degree-zero right enveloping Tor is the augmentation tensor

Let R be a commutative ring, L a free R-Lie module, U = U_R(L), and N a right U-module. With
F(X) = N ⊗_U X and T the augmentation left module, define (L₀F)(T) ≅ N ⊗_U T by inverting the
coefficient homology-to-Tor comparison and then applying the H₀ coinvariant tensor
isomorphism. The forward morphism is the canonical map F.fromLeftDerivedZero at T.
