# The degree quotient of a first-positive cokernel

If `K₁.d 1 0 = 0`, the degree-`n` object of the first-positive cokernel
is canonically isomorphic to `K₃.X n` modulo the sum of the ranges of all
components of `P` with `p > 0`, `q ≥ 0` and `p + q = n`. The isomorphism
comes from evaluation preserving cokernels and the component range equality.
