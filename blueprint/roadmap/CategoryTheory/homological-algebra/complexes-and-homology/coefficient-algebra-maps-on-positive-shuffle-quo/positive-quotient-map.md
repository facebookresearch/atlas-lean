# Chain map between both-positive shuffle quotients

Let `k` be a commutative ring and `A,B` commutative `k`-algebras,
with `A`-module `M` and `B`-module `N` carrying compatible `k`-actions.
Let `g : A →ₐ[k] B` and `f : M →ₗ[k] N` satisfy
`f(a • m)=g(a) • f(m)` for all `a,m`. Work at carrier height
`T=max(u,v,v′,z)`, with `M,N` in `T` and the algebra factors lifted
there from their respective carrier heights. The compatible coefficient chain map descends to a chain map
`Q(A,M) → Q(B,N)` of quotients by the both-positive pairing images,
taking a chain class `[z]` to `[C(g,f)(z)]`.
