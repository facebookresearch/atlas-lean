# The bracket-and-action formula for the enveloping differential

The bracket-and-action formula for the enveloping differential.

On `(v₀ ∧ ⋯ ∧ vₙ) ⊗ u`, the differential is `d(v₀ ∧ ⋯ ∧ vₙ) ⊗ u` plus the signed deletion sum
`∑ᵢ (-1)^i (v₀ ∧ ⋯ ∧ v̂ᵢ ∧ ⋯ ∧ vₙ) ⊗ uι(vᵢ)`. The enveloping multiplication occurs on the
right of `u`.
