# Every higher Eulerian coefficient component is a proper shuffle relation

Every higher Eulerian coefficient component is a proper shuffle relation.

For a commutative ℚ-algebra k, a commutative k-algebra A and a compatible A-module M, for
2≤i≤n, eₙ⁽ⁱ⁾(x) lies in rawPositiveRelations(n) for every coefficient tensor x.
