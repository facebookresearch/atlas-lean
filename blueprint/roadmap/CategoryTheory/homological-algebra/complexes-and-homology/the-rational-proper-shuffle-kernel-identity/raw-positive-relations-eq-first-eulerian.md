# The proper-shuffle submodule is the kernel of the coefficient lowest projector

The proper-shuffle submodule is the kernel of the coefficient lowest projector.

For a commutative ℚ-algebra k, a commutative k-algebra A and a compatible A-module M,
rawPositiveRelations(n)=ker(eₙ⁽⁰⁾+eₙ⁽¹⁾), using strictly positive degrees in both shuffle
inputs.
