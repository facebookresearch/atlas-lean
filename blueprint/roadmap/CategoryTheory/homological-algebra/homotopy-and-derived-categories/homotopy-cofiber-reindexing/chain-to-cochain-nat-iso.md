# Natural chain-to-cochain comparison of homotopy cofibers

Let `C` be preadditive with binary biproducts. Reindex integer
chains to integer cochains by degree negation `i ↦ -i`. There is a natural isomorphism from reindexing the chain
homotopy cofiber of an arrow to the cochain homotopy cofiber of
its reindexed arrow.
