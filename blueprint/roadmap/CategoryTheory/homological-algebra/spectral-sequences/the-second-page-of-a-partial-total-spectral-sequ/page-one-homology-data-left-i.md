Cycle inclusion in the chosen page-transition homology data.

object's `mapFourδ₁Toδ₀'` on the lower five cuts, followed by the inverse page-one interval
coordinate. The three degrees are `η.n₀`, `η.n₁`, and `η.n₂`.
