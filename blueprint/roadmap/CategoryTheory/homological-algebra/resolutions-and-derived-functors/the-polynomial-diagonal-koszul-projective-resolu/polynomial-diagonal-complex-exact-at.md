# The polynomial diagonal complex is exact in every positive degree

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). For every n : ℕ, polynomialDiagonalComplex k σ is
exact at degree n + 1. Finite ordered subfamilies of distinct polynomial differences are
weakly regular, and finite exterior support gives boundaries for all positive-degree cycles.
