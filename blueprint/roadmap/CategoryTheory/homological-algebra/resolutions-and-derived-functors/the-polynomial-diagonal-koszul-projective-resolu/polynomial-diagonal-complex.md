# The Koszul complex on polynomial left-minus-right differences

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). Define polynomialDiagonalComplex as finsuppComplex E
δ. Its degree-n object is ⋀[E]^n V, and its boundary contracts with the E-linear functional
sending the unit coordinate at i to δ_i.
