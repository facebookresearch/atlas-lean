# Every polynomial diagonal Koszul term is projective

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). For each n : ℕ, the object
(polynomialDiagonalComplex k σ).X n is projective in ModuleCat.{max u v} E, since it is an
exterior power of the free coordinate module V.
