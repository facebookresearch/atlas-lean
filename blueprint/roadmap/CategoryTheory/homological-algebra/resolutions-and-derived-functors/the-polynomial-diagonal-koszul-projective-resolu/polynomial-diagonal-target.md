# The polynomial ring with its regular enveloping-module action

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). View the canonical lifted carrier ULift A as an
E-module by (l ⊗ op r) • a = l * a * r. The object polynomialDiagonalTarget k σ is this
regular E-module in ModuleCat.{max u v} E.
