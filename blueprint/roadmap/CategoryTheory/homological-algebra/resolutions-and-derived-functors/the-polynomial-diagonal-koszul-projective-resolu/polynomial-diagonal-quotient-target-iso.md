# The lifted quotient augmentation target is the regular polynomial module

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). Let I be the ideal generated by the δ_i and μ : E
→ₐ[k] A the multiplication map, with μ(l ⊗ op r) = l * r. View the canonical lifted carrier
ULift A as an E-module by (l ⊗ op r) • a = l * a * r. The canonical up/down equivalence
followed by polynomialDiagonalQuotientEquiv gives an E-module isomorphism from ModuleCat.of E
(ULift.{max u v} (E ⧸ I)) to polynomialDiagonalTarget k σ.
