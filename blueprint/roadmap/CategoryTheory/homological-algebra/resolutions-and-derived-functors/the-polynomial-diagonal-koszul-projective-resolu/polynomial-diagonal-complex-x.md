# The diagonal complex has exterior powers of the free enveloping module

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). For every n : ℕ, (polynomialDiagonalComplex k σ).X n
= ModuleCat.of E (⋀[E]^n V). The complex lies in ModuleCat.{max u v} E for k : Type u and σ :
Type v.
