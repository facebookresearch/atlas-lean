# The exterior-coordinate augmentation component evaluates by multiplication

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). Let I be the ideal generated by the δ_i and μ : E
→ₐ[k] A the multiplication map, with μ(l ⊗ op r) = l * r. View the canonical lifted carrier
ULift A as an E-module by (l ⊗ op r) • a = l * a * r. For z : ⋀[E]^0 V, write e₀(z) =
exteriorPower.zeroEquiv E V z : E. The value of
polynomialDiagonalAugmentationZeroComponent.hom on z is ULift.up (μ(e₀(z))).
