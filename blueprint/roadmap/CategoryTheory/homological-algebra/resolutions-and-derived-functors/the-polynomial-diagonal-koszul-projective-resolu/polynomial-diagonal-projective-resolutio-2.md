# The polynomial resolution has the diagonal exterior Koszul complex

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). The underlying complex of
polynomialDiagonalProjectiveResolution k σ equals polynomialDiagonalComplex k σ, with degree-n
term ⋀[E]^n V and signed deletion coefficients δ_i.
