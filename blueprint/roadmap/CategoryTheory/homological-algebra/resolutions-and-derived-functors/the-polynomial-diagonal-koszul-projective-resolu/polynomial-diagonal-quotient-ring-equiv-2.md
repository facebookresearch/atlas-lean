# The diagonal quotient equivalence evaluates a representative by multiplication

Let k be a commutative ring and σ any index type. Put A = MvPolynomial σ k, E = A ⊗[k] Aᵐᵒᵖ, V
= σ →₀ E and δ_i = X_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ op(X_i). Let I be the ideal generated by the δ_i and μ : E
→ₐ[k] A the multiplication map, with μ(l ⊗ op r) = l * r. For z : E,
polynomialDiagonalQuotientRingEquiv k σ (Ideal.Quotient.mk I z) = μ(z).
