# Wu's conjecture: maps omitting $n+2$ hyperplanes are degenerate

This result proves exactly the proposition stated as [Wu's conjecture: no nondegenerate maps omitting $n+2$ hyperplanes](no-nondegenerate-map-omitting-hyperplane.md). Let $n \ge 1$, let $H_0, \dots, H_{n+1}$ be linear forms on $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}$ any $n + 1$ of which are linearly independent, and let $F : \mathbb{C}^n \to \mathbb{C}^{n+1}$ be an entire holomorphic map with $H_i(F(z)) \ne 0$ for all $z$ and all $i$. Then for every $z \in \mathbb{C}^n$ the matrix with columns $F(z), \partial_1 F(z), \dots, \partial_n F(z)$ has determinant $0$. In the projective reading, every holomorphic map $\mathbb{C}^n \to \mathbb{P}^n(\mathbb{C})$ omitting $n + 2$ hyperplanes in general position has Jacobian of rank less than $n$ everywhere.
