# A complete negatively pinched Kähler threefold without bounded holomorphic coordinates

There exist a nonempty open contractible $M\subset\mathbb C^2\times\mathbb C$ and a smooth positive Hermitian Kähler metric on $M$, geodesically complete for all initial tangent data, together with $0<A\le B$ with real sectional curvature in $[-B,-A]$ on every real two-plane. No coordinatewise bounded holomorphic map $M\to\mathbb C^3$ has everywhere nonzero complex Jacobian, and there is no biholomorphism between $M$ and a bounded open subset of $\mathbb C^3$. The coordinates need not have been globally injective to be ruled out.

## Expansion

Reconcile the sectional numerator from the complex tensor with the intrinsic real two-plane formula. Use one domain throughout, and prove both holomorphic nonrealization conclusions.
