# Wente's shadow problem: connected shadows imply convexity

Question (Wente's shadow problem, in $\mathbb{R}^3$). Let $M$ be a compact, connected, Hausdorff smooth surface, let $f : M \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a $C^\infty$ immersion (its differential $df_p$ is injective at every $p \in M$), and let $n : M \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a continuous unit normal field: $\lvert n(p) \rvert = 1$ and $\langle n(p), df_p(v) \rangle = 0$ for every $p \in M$ and every tangent vector $v$ at $p$. For a unit vector $u \in S^2$ the shadow is

$$S_u = \{p \in M : \langle n(p), u \rangle > 0\}.$$

Statement: if every shadow $S_u$, $u \in S^2$, is connected (in particular nonempty), then $f$ is a convex embedding, that is, $f$ is injective and $f(M)$ is the boundary of a compact convex set with nonempty interior. It is false; the refutation is [Wente's shadow problem: connected shadows do not imply convexity](connected-shadows-imply-convex-refuted.md).
