# Wente's shadow problem: connected shadows do not imply convexity

This result refutes the proposition stated as [Wente's shadow problem: connected shadows imply convexity](connected-shadows-imply-convex.md). The statement is false: there are a compact, connected, Hausdorff smooth surface $M$, a $C^\infty$ immersion $f : M \to \mathbb{R}^3$ and a continuous unit normal field $n : M \to \mathbb{R}^3$ (with $\langle n(p), df_p(v) \rangle = 0$ for all tangent vectors $v$ at $p$) such that every shadow $S_u = \{p \in M : \langle n(p), u \rangle > 0\}$, $u \in S^2$, is connected, but $f$ is not a convex embedding: $f$ is not injective onto the boundary of any compact convex set with nonempty interior.
