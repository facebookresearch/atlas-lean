# Schwartz's optimal paper Moebius band theorem

Let $a > 0$. If
$f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ is a Moebius embedding of aspect ratio $a$, then
$a > \sqrt{3}$. That is, a smooth embedded paper Moebius band has aspect ratio greater
than $\sqrt{3}$.
