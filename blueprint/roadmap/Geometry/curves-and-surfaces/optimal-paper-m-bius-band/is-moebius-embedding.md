# Moebius embedding

Let $a$ be a real number, let $S = [0, 1] \times \mathbb{R}$ be the
unit strip and let $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$. Then $f$ is a Moebius embedding of
aspect ratio $a$ if $f(1 - x, y + a) = f(x, y)$ for every $(x, y) \in S$, $f$ is $C^\infty$ on
$S$, at every point of $S$ the derivative of $f$ within $S$ is a linear isometry
$\mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, and $f$ is injective on $[0, 1] \times [0, a)$.

For $a > 0$ this is the paper's notion of a smooth isometric embedding of a Moebius band of aspect
ratio $a$: the strip is glued into a band by the first condition, and injectivity on the fundamental
domain $[0, 1] \times [0, a)$ makes the glued map an embedding. For $a \le 0$ that domain is empty and the
injectivity condition is vacuous.
