# Apollonian orbit of a quadruple

The Apollonian orbit of a quadruple $v$ is the set of quadruples obtained from $v$ by finitely many flips; since every
flip is an involution, this is the orbit of $v$ under the Apollonian group $\langle S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4 \rangle$
(Kontorovich and Oh, arXiv:0811.2236v5, Section 2.1).
