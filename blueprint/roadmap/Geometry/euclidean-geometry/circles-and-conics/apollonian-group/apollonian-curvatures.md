# Curvatures of the Apollonian packing of a quadruple

For a quadruple $v$, this is the set of all entries of all quadruples in the Apollonian orbit of $v$; when $v$ is the
curvature quadruple of a Descartes configuration these are the curvatures of the circles of the packing it generates
(H. Oh, arXiv:1312.1383v2, Section 4; Kontorovich and Oh, arXiv:0811.2236v5, Theorem 2.3).
