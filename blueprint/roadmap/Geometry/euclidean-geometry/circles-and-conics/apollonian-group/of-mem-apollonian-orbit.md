# Integrality propagates through Apollonian orbits

If the four entries of $v$ are integers, then so are the entries of every quadruple in the Apollonian orbit of $v$:
each flip $S_i$ has integer matrix entries.

## Sources

- H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings: dynamics and number theory*, Japan. J. Math. 9 (2014), arXiv:1312.1383v2, Theorem 4.2 (Soddy, 1937).
- A. Kontorovich and H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings and closed horospheres on hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 24 (2011) 603–648, arXiv:0811.2236v5, remark after equation (1.3).
