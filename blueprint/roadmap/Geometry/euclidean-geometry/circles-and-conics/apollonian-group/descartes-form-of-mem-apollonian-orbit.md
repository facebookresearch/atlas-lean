# Apollonian orbits stay on a level set of the Descartes form

Every quadruple $w$ in the Apollonian orbit of $v$ satisfies $Q(w) = Q(v)$; in particular the orbit of a Descartes
quadruple consists of Descartes quadruples, which lie on the cone $Q = 0$ (Kontorovich and Oh, arXiv:0811.2236v5,
Section 2.1).
