# Curvatures of tangent circles form a Descartes quadruple

Let four circles in the Euclidean plane have centers $c_i$ and radii $r_i > 0$, in either configuration of
Descartes' circle theorem: (a) pairwise externally tangent, $|c_i - c_j| = r_i + r_j$, with $k_i = 1/r_i$; or (b)
one circle $e$ internally tangent to the three others, $|c_e - c_j| = r_e - r_j$, which are pairwise externally
tangent, with $k_e = -1/r_e$ and $k_j = 1/r_j$ for $j \ne e$. Then the signed curvatures satisfy $Q(k) = 0$
(Lagarias, Mallows and Wilks, arXiv:math/0101066v1, Theorem 1.1).
