# Integral Apollonian packings

If the four entries of a quadruple $v$ over a commutative ring are integers, then so is every entry of every quadruple
in the Apollonian orbit of $v$; in particular four integral initial curvatures generate a packing all of whose
curvatures are integers. For example, the packing generated by $(-1, 2, 2, 3)$, a bounding circle of radius $1$ with
circles of radii $\tfrac12, \tfrac12, \tfrac13$ inside it, contains the curvatures $15$ (flip the bounding circle) and
$35$ (flip the last circle of $(15, 2, 2, 3)$); the strip packing $(0, 0, 1, 1)$, which contains two lines, is
integral as well.
