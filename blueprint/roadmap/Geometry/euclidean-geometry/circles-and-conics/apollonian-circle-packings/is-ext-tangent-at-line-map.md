# The external tangency point of two spheres

If the centers $c_1, c_2$ of two spheres of positive radii $r_1, r_2$ in a Euclidean affine space are at distance
$r_1 + r_2$, then the spheres are externally tangent at the point $c_1 + \frac{r_1}{r_1 + r_2}(c_2 - c_1)$ of the
segment of centers: this point lies on both spheres and on the segment joining their centers.
