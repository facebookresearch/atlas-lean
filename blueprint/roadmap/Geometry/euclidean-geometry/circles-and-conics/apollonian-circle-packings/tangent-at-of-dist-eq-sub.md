# Internally tangent circles touch at one point

If the second of two plane circles has the smaller radius and their centers are at distance $r_1 - r_2$, the circles
meet exactly at the point $c_1 + \frac{r_1}{r_1 - r_2}(c_2 - c_1)$ of the ray from the first center through the second.
It combines the explicit internal tangency point with the uniqueness of the common point.
