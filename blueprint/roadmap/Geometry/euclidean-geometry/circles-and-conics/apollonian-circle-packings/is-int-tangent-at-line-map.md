# The internal tangency point of two spheres

If a sphere of radius $r_1 > 0$ has its center $c_1$ at distance $r_2 - r_1$ from the center $c_2$ of a sphere of larger
radius $r_2$, in a Euclidean affine space, then it is internally tangent to that sphere at the point
$c_2 + \frac{r_2}{r_2 - r_1}(c_1 - c_2)$: this point lies on both spheres, and $c_1$ lies between $c_2$ and it.
