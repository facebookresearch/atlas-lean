# Externally tangent spheres meet at exactly one point

Let $s_1, s_2$ be spheres of positive radii $r_1, r_2$ and centers $c_1, c_2$ in a Euclidean affine space that are
externally tangent at $p$: $p$ lies on both spheres and on the segment joining their centers. Then
$s_1 \cap s_2 = \{p\}$. Indeed, any common point $q$ satisfies $d(c_1, q) + d(q, c_2) = r_1 + r_2 = d(c_1, c_2)$, so
by strict convexity $q$ is the point of the segment at distance $r_1$ from $c_1$, which is $p$.
