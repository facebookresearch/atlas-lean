# Circle-counting function

For a bounded packing $P$, $N_P(T)$ is the number of circles of $P$ whose curvature is at most $T$ (taken to be $0$
if there are infinitely many). The bounding circle is counted with its unsigned curvature $1/r$ rather than its
signed curvature $-1/r$; this changes $N_P(T)$ by at most one and does not affect asymptotics.

## Sources

- A. Kontorovich and H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings and closed horospheres on hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 24 (2011) 603–648, arXiv:0811.2236v5, Definition 2.1(1).
