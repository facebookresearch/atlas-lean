# Internally tangent spheres meet at exactly one point

Let $s_1$ be a sphere of positive radius $r_1$ internally tangent at $p$ to a sphere $s_2$ of larger radius $r_2$, in
a Euclidean affine space: $p$ lies on both spheres and the center of $s_1$ lies between the center of $s_2$ and $p$.
Then $s_1 \cap s_2 = \{p\}$. The radii must differ: two equal spheres are internally tangent at each of their points.
