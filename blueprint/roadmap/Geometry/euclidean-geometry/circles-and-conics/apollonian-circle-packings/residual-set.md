# Residual set of an Apollonian packing

The residual set $\operatorname{Res}(P)$ of a packing $P$ is the closure in $\mathbb{C}$ of the union of its
circles. For an Apollonian packing it is the limit set of the Apollonian group, a fractal of Hausdorff dimension
$\alpha \approx 1.30568$, strictly between $1$ and $2$; $\alpha$ does not depend on the packing, since any two
Apollonian packings are related by a Möbius transformation.

## Sources

- H. Oh, *Apollonian circle packings: dynamics and number theory*, Japan. J. Math. 9 (2014), arXiv:1312.1383v2, Section 1 (definition of $\operatorname{Res}(P)$ and the residual dimension) and Section 2.
