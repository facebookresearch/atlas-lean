# Externally tangent circles touch at one point

If the centers of two plane circles of radii $r_1, r_2$ are at distance $r_1 + r_2$, the circles meet exactly at the
point $c_1 + \frac{r_1}{r_1 + r_2}(c_2 - c_1)$ of the segment of centers. It combines the explicit external tangency
point with the uniqueness of the common point.
