# Point before a reflection

For a light path with $n$ reflection points, the points are indexed $0, 1, \dots, n + 1$: index $0$ is the source, indices $1, \dots, n$ are the reflection points in order, and index $n + 1$ is the target. For a reflection numbered $i \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the point immediately before it has index $i$.
