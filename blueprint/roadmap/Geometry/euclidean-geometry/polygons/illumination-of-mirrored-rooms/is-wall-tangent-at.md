# Wall tangents of a mirrored room

Let $R$ be a polygonal room with open floor $R^\circ$, and let $p, d \in \mathbb{R}^2$. The vector $d$ is a wall tangent at $p$ when $d \ne 0$, $p$ lies on the frontier of $R^\circ$, and there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every $q$ with $\lVert q - p \rVert < \varepsilon$,

$$q \in \partial R^\circ \iff q \in \{p + t d : t \in \mathbb{R}\}.$$

So near $p$ the boundary is exactly a straight line through $p$ in direction $d$; corners of the room have no wall tangent, and subdividing a straight wall does not change which points have one.
