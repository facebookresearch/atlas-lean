# End point of a leg of a light path

For a light path with $n$ reflection points, the points are indexed $0, 1, \dots, n + 1$: index $0$ is the source, indices $1, \dots, n$ are the reflection points in order, and index $n + 1$ is the target. The legs are indexed $i = 0, \dots, n$, and leg $i$ ends at point index $i + 1$.
