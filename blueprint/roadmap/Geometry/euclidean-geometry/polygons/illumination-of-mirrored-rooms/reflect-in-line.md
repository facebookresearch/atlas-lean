# Reflection of a vector in a line

For vectors $w, v \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the reflection of $v$ in the line spanned by $w$ is

$$\rho_w(v) = 2\,\frac{\langle v, w\rangle}{\langle w, w\rangle}\, w - v.$$

It is the orthogonal reflection in $\mathbb{R} w$ when $w \ne 0$; for $w = 0$ the convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives $\rho_0(v) = -v$.
