# Euler's quadrilateral theorem

For any four points $A, B, C, D$ of the Euclidean plane,
write $XY$ for the distance between points $X$ and $Y$, and let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of
$AC$ and $BD$. Then

$$AB^2 + BC^2 + CD^2 + DA^2 = AC^2 + BD^2 + 4 MN^2.$$
