# Opposite vertices of a labelled octahedron

The opposite-vertex relation on the labels $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ of an octahedron: $i$ and $j$ are opposite exactly when $\{i, j\}$ is one of the pairs $\{0, 1\}$, $\{2, 3\}$ or $\{4, 5\}$. The relation is symmetric, and each label is opposite exactly one other label.
