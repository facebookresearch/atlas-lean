# Regular octahedra from six labelled points

Points $p_0, \dots, p_5 \in \mathbb{R}^3$ span a regular octahedron if there is an edge length $s > 0$ such that for all distinct labels $i \ne j$:

- if $\{i, j\}$ is one of the opposite pairs $\{0,1\}$, $\{2,3\}$, $\{4,5\}$, then $\lVert p_i - p_j \rVert = s\sqrt{2}$;
- otherwise $\lVert p_i - p_j \rVert = s$.

In particular the six points are distinct.
