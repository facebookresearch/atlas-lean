# Regular octahedra inscribed in smoothly embedded spheres

This result proves the proposition stated as [Statement of regular octahedra inscribed in smoothly embedded spheres](conjecture.md). Precisely, for every $C^\infty$ embedding $f : S^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ of the unit two-sphere, the image $f(S^2)$ contains six points spanning a regular octahedron: six points $p_0, \dots, p_5 \in \mathbb{R}^3$ such that for some $s > 0$, the three opposite pairs $\{p_0, p_1\}$, $\{p_2, p_3\}$, $\{p_4, p_5\}$ are at distance $s\sqrt{2}$ and every other pair of distinct indices $i \ne j$ has $\lVert p_i - p_j \rVert = s$.
