# Orthogonal 3 × 3 matrices preserve the squared radius

For every real $3 \times 3$ matrix $Q$ with $Q Q^{\mathsf T} = I$ (an element of $O(3)$, including reflections such as $\operatorname{diag}(-1, 1, 1)$, not only rotations) and every $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$, the squared radius is preserved: $\sum_i (Qx)_i^2 = \sum_i x_i^2$.
