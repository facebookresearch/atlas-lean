# Crystallographic restriction in space

Let $R$ be a real $3 \times 3$ orthogonal matrix ($R^{\mathsf T} R = RR^{\mathsf T} = I$) with $\det R = 1$ that is a lattice endomorphism (conjugate over $\mathbb{R}$ to an integer matrix), and let the natural number $n > 0$ be its order: $R^n = I$ and $R^m \ne I$ for every natural number $m$ with $0 < m < n$. Suppose also that $R$ fixes a unit vector $u \in \mathbb{R}^3$. Then $n \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$.
