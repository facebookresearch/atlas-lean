# Shear entry of a cubic matrix rotated by forty-five degrees

A $6 \times 6$ Voigt matrix $C$ encodes the rank-four tensor $c_{ijkl} = C_{v(i,j)\,v(k,l)}$, where $v$ maps the pairs $xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy$ to $0, \dots, 5$; a $3 \times 3$ matrix $R$ acts by $c'_{ijkl} = \sum R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}\,c_{abcd}$, read back in Voigt form as $R \cdot C$. Let $R_{45}$ be the rotation by $45°$ about the $z$-axis, with rows $(\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}, -\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}, 0)$, $(\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1)$. For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. For a cubic Voigt matrix $C$, the $xy$ shear entry of $R_{45} \cdot C$ is $(c_{11} - c_{12})/2$.
