# A lattice endomorphism of finite order is a lattice symmetry

A real $n \times n$ matrix $R$ is a lattice endomorphism when $R = P M P^{-1}$ for some integer matrix $M$ and invertible real matrix $P$. For an invertible real matrix $P$, the lattice $\Lambda_P = \{P z : z \in \mathbb{Z}^n\}$ is spanned by the columns of $P$. A real matrix $R$ is a lattice symmetry when $R \Lambda_P = \Lambda_P$ for some invertible $P$. If $R$ is a lattice endomorphism with $R^k = I$ for some $k > 0$, then $R$ is a lattice symmetry.
