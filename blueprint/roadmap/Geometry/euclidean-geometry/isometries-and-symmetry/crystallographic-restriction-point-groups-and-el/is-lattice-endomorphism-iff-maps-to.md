# Lattice endomorphisms are the maps of a full-rank lattice into itself

A real $n \times n$ matrix $R$ is a lattice endomorphism when $R = P M P^{-1}$ for some integer matrix $M$ and invertible real matrix $P$. For an invertible real matrix $P$, the lattice $\Lambda_P = \{P z : z \in \mathbb{Z}^n\}$ is spanned by the columns of $P$. Then $R$ is a lattice endomorphism if and only if there is an invertible $P$ with $R\Lambda_P \subseteq \Lambda_P$.
