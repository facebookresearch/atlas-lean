# The two Lamé constants are independent

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. The isotropic (Lamé) Voigt matrix with constants $\lambda, \mu$ is the cubic Voigt matrix with $c_{11} = \lambda + 2\mu$, $c_{12} = \lambda$ and $c_{44} = \mu$. If the isotropic Voigt matrices of $(\lambda, \mu)$ and $(\lambda', \mu')$ are equal, then $\lambda = \lambda'$ and $\mu = \mu'$.
