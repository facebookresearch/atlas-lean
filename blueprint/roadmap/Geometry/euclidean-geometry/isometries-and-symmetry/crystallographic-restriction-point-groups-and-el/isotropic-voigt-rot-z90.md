# Isotropic Voigt matrices are invariant under the quarter-turn about z

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. The isotropic (Lamé) Voigt matrix with constants $\lambda, \mu$ is the cubic Voigt matrix with $c_{11} = \lambda + 2\mu$, $c_{12} = \lambda$ and $c_{44} = \mu$. Voigt indices $0, \dots, 5$ encode the symmetric index pairs $xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy$. The axis swaps act on Voigt indices by the permutations $s_{xy} = (1, 0, 2, 4, 3, 5)$, $s_{xz} = (2, 1, 0, 5, 4, 3)$ and $s_{yz} = (0, 2, 1, 3, 5, 4)$, and the quarter-turn $R_z^{\mathsf T}$ about $z$ acts by the permutation $q = (1, 0, 2, 4, 3, 5)$ with signs $\varepsilon = (1, 1, 1, -1, 1, -1)$. Every isotropic Voigt matrix $C$ satisfies $C_{ij} = \varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j C_{q(i)q(j)}$.
