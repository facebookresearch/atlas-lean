# Cubic Voigt stiffness matrix

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt stiffness matrix is

$$\begin{pmatrix} c_{11} & c_{12} & c_{12} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ c_{12} & c_{11} & c_{12} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ c_{12} & c_{12} & c_{11} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & c_{44} & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & c_{44} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & c_{44} \end{pmatrix}.$$

Voigt indices $0, \dots, 5$ encode the symmetric index pairs $xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy$.
