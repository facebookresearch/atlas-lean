# The three cubic constants are independent

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. If the cubic Voigt matrices of $(c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44})$ and $(d_{11}, d_{12}, d_{44})$ are equal, then $c_{11} = d_{11}$, $c_{12} = d_{12}$ and $c_{44} = d_{44}$.
