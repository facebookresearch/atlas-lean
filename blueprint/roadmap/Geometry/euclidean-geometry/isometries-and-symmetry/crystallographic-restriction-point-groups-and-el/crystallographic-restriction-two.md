# Crystallographic restriction in the plane

Let $R$ be a real $2 \times 2$ orthogonal matrix ($R^{\mathsf T} R = I$) with $\det R = 1$ that is a lattice endomorphism (conjugate over $\mathbb{R}$ to an integer matrix), and let $n > 0$ be its order: $R^n = I$ and $R^m \ne I$ for $0 < m < n$. Then $n \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$.
