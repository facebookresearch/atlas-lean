# Lattice endomorphism

A real $n \times n$ matrix $R$ is a lattice endomorphism when $R = P M P^{-1}$ for some integer
matrix $M$ and invertible real matrix $P$. Equivalently, $R$ maps some full-rank lattice into itself
([criterion](is-lattice-endomorphism-iff-maps-to.md)), possibly onto a proper sublattice: for
$n = 1$, $R = M = 2$ and $P = 1$ map $\mathbb{Z}$ onto $2\mathbb{Z}$.
