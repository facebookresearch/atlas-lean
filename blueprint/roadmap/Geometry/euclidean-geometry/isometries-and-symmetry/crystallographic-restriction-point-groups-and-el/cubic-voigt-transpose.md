# Cubic Voigt matrices are symmetric

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. Every cubic Voigt matrix is symmetric.
