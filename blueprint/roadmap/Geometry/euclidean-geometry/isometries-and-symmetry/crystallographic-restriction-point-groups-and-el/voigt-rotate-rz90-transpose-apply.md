# Action of the quarter-turn about z on Voigt matrices

A $6 \times 6$ Voigt matrix $C$ encodes the rank-four tensor $c_{ijkl} = C_{v(i,j)\,v(k,l)}$, where $v$ maps the pairs $xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy$ to $0, \dots, 5$; a $3 \times 3$ matrix $R$ acts by $c'_{ijkl} = \sum R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}R_{ld}\,c_{abcd}$, read back in Voigt form as $R \cdot C$. Voigt indices $0, \dots, 5$ encode the symmetric index pairs $xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy$. The axis swaps act on Voigt indices by the permutations $s_{xy} = (1, 0, 2, 4, 3, 5)$, $s_{xz} = (2, 1, 0, 5, 4, 3)$ and $s_{yz} = (0, 2, 1, 3, 5, 4)$, and the quarter-turn $R_z^{\mathsf T}$ about $z$ acts by the permutation $q = (1, 0, 2, 4, 3, 5)$ with signs $\varepsilon = (1, 1, 1, -1, 1, -1)$. For every $C$ and all $I, J$, $(R_z^{\mathsf T} \cdot C)_{IJ} = \varepsilon_I \varepsilon_J C_{q(I)q(J)}$.
