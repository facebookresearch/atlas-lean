# Isotropic Voigt matrices are cubic

For reals $c_{11}, c_{12}, c_{44}$, the cubic Voigt matrix has $c_{11}$ on the diagonal and $c_{12}$ off the diagonal of its upper-left $3 \times 3$ normal block, the shear block $c_{44} I_3$, and zero normal-shear couplings. The isotropic Voigt matrix of $(\lambda, \mu)$ is the cubic Voigt matrix of $(\lambda + 2\mu, \lambda, \mu)$.
