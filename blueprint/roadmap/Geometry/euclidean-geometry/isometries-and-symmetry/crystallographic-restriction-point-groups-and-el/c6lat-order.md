# Order of the hexagonal-basis sixfold rotation

Let $C_6 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ be the order-six rotation written in the hexagonal lattice basis (an integer matrix, not the Cartesian rotation). Then $C_6^6 = I$ and $C_6^m \ne I$ for every natural number $m$ with $0 < m < 6$.
