# Pompeiu general

Pompeiu's theorem for an arbitrary point: for an equilateral triangle `ABC` and any point
`P` in its plane, `PA`, `PB`, `PC` form the sides of a (possibly degenerate) triangle.
`pompeiu` is the source-shaped form.
