# Euler OI

Euler's theorem in geometry (OI formula): `OI ^ 2 = R * (R - 2 * r)` and `R ≥ 2 * r`.
See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler%27s_theorem_in_geometry (euler-oi-s1).
