# Lattice Enumerator Pos

Source-domain enumerator `L_P(t)` for bounded `P` and positive `t`, as in
Rocha-Neves, lines 24-31. The `hb` proof rules out unbounded `P`, where
`ncard` would silently return junk `0`.
