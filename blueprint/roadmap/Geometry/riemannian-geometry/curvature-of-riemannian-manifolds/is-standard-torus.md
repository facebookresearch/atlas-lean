# Manifolds diffeomorphic to the standard torus

Let $M$ be a topological space charted on $\mathbb{R}^n$. Then $M$ is diffeomorphic to the standard torus $\mathbb{R}^n / \mathbb{Z}^n$ in the following sense: there is a surjective $C^\infty$ local diffeomorphism $p : \mathbb{R}^n \to M$ such that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n$,

$$p(x) = p(y) \iff x - y \in \mathbb{Z}^n.$$

The smoothness here is a condition on the map $p$, not an assumption on the atlas of $M$. When $M$ is a smooth manifold, such a map descends to a diffeomorphism $\mathbb{R}^n / \mathbb{Z}^n \to M$.
