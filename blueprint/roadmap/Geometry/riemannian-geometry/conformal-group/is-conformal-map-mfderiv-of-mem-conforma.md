# The differential of a conformal diffeomorphism is conformal

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold with a smooth Riemannian metric $g$, and let $\varphi$ be a smooth self-diffeomorphism of $M$ in the conformal group of $g$. Then for every point $x$, the differential $d\varphi_x : T_x M \to T_{\varphi(x)} M$ is a conformal linear map for the inner products that $g$ induces on the two tangent spaces. The statement is about self-diffeomorphisms of $M$ in the conformal group, not about maps between arbitrary manifolds.
