# Of Real dirichlet Laplacian variational Eigenvalue le

For an open set `Ω` of Euclidean space `ℝⁿ` and an index `j`, the `j`-th real min-max value of the Dirichlet Laplacian on `Ω`, viewed in `[0, ∞]`, is at most the `j`-th variational eigenvalue on the manifold `ℝⁿ` over the smooth functions with compact support in `Ω`, with respect to Lebesgue measure.
