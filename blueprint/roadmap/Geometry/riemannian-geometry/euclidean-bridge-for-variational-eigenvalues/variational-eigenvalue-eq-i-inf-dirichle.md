# Variational Eigenvalue eq i Inf dirichlet Laplacian

For an open set `Ω` of `ℝⁿ` and an index `j`, the `j`-th variational eigenvalue on the manifold `ℝⁿ` over smooth functions with compact support in `Ω`, with Lebesgue measure, equals the infimum over `(j + 1)`-dimensional spaces `V` of test functions of the supremum over nonzero `f ∈ V` of the Euclidean Rayleigh quotient, viewed in `[0, ∞]`.
