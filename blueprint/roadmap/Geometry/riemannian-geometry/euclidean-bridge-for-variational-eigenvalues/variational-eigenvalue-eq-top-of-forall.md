# Variational Eigenvalue eq top of forall finrank ne

For an open set `Ω` of `ℝⁿ` and an index `j` such that no space of smooth test functions on `Ω` has dimension `j + 1`, the `j`-th variational eigenvalue on the manifold side is `∞`, while the `j`-th real min-max value of the Dirichlet Laplacian on `Ω` is `0` by convention.
