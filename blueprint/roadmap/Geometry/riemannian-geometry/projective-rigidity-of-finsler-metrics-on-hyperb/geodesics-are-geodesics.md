# Every geodesic of one Finsler metric is a reparametrized geodesic of another

Let $F_1$ and $F_2$ be Finsler metrics on a smooth surface $M$. Every unparameterized geodesic of $F_1$ is a geodesic of $F_2$ if for every geodesic $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to M$ of $F_1$ there is an orientation-preserving reparametrization $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (an order isomorphism, that is, an increasing bijection) such that $\gamma \circ \varphi$ is a geodesic of $F_2$.

A Finsler metric on $M$ is a continuous function $F : TM \to \mathbb{R}$ that is $C^\infty$ off the zero section, positive on nonzero vectors, positively homogeneous of degree one on each fiber and strongly convex; it need not be reversible. A geodesic of $F$ is a curve $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to M$ for which there is a speed $c \ge 0$ such that, locally around every time, $d_F(\gamma(t_1), \gamma(t_2)) = c\,(t_2 - t_1)$ for $t_1 \le t_2$, where $d_F$ is the forward distance of $F$.
