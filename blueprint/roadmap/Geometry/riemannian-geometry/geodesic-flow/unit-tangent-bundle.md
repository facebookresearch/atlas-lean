# Unit tangent bundle of a Riemannian manifold

The unit tangent bundle $SM$ of a Riemannian manifold $(M, g)$ is the set of tangent vectors whose norm for the
metric on their fibre is $1$, a subset of the tangent bundle $TM$ with the subspace topology.
