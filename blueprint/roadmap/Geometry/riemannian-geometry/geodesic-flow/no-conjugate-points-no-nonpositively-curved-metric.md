# A three-manifold without conjugate points and without a nonpositively curved metric

There is a compact connected Hausdorff second-countable smooth three-manifold $M$ without boundary with an atlas all of whose transition Jacobians have positive determinant. Some smooth Riemannian metric on $M$ has no conjugate points: on every open interval $U\subseteq\mathbb R$ (bounded or unbounded), along every smooth geodesic on $U$, a smooth tangent Jacobi field with two distinct zeros on $U$ vanishes everywhere on $U$. Constant geodesics are included. But no smooth Riemannian metric on this same smooth manifold has sectional curvature everywhere $\le0$; equivalently its curvature numerator $\langle R(u,v)v,u\rangle$ cannot be everywhere nonpositive in all chart tangent pairs.
