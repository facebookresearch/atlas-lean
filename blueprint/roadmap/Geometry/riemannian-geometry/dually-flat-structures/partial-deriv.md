# Partial derivatives on $\mathbb{R}^n$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, an index $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, a function $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and a point $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the partial derivative $\partial_i f(x)$ is the Fréchet derivative of $f$ at $x$ applied to the $i$-th standard basis vector $e_i$:

$$\partial_i f(x) = Df(x)\, e_i.$$

By convention $\partial_i f(x) = 0$ when $f$ is not differentiable at $x$.
