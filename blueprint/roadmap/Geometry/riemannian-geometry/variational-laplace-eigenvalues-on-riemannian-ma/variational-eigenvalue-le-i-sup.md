# Variational Eigenvalue le i Sup

Let $V \subseteq W$ be subspaces of smooth real functions on $M$, with $V$ of dimension $k + 1$ and every nonzero $u \in V$ of positive finite mass $\int |u|^2\, d\mu$. Then the $k$-th [variational value](variational-eigenvalue.md) for $W$ and $\mu$ is at most the supremum of the Rayleigh quotient $E(u) / \int |u|^2\, d\mu$ over the nonzero $u \in V$, where $E(u) = \int \|du\|^2\, d\mu$ is the lower integral of the squared operator norm of the differential.
