# Variational Eigenvalue le i Sup

Let `V ⊆ W` be subspaces of smooth real functions on `M`, with `V` of dimension `k + 1` and every nonzero `u ∈ V` of positive finite mass `∫ |u|² dμ`. Then the `k`-th variational eigenvalue for `W` and `μ` is at most the supremum of the Rayleigh quotient `∫ |∇u|² dμ / ∫ |u|² dμ` over the nonzero `u ∈ V`.
