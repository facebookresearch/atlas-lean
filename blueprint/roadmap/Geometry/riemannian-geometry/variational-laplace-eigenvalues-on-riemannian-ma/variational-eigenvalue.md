# Variational Eigenvalue

For a manifold `M` with a Riemannian bundle, a subspace `W` of smooth real functions on `M`, a measure `μ` and an index `k ≥ 0`, the `k`-th variational eigenvalue is the min-max value `inf_V sup_{u ∈ V, u ≠ 0} R(u)`, a value in `[0, ∞]`. The infimum runs over the `(k + 1)`-dimensional subspaces `V ⊆ W` whose nonzero elements have positive finite mass `∫ |u|² dμ`, and `R(u) = ∫ |∇u|² dμ / ∫ |u|² dμ` is the Rayleigh quotient. It is `∞` when no such subspace exists.
