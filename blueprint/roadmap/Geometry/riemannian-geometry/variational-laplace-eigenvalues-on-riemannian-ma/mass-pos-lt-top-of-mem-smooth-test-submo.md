Let $\mu$ be a measure on a manifold $M$ whose $\sigma$-algebra contains the open sets, which is positive on nonempty open sets and finite on compact sets. Every nonzero smooth function $u$ with compact support in an open set $U$ has positive finite mass: $0 < \int |u|^2\, d\mu < \infty$. The measurability of open sets is needed: on $\mathbb{R}$ with the trivial $\sigma$-algebra and a probability measure, nonempty open sets have positive outer measure, yet the lower integral of a nonzero bump squared is $0$.
