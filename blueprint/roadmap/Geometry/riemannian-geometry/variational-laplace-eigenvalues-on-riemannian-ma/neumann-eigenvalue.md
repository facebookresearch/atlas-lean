# Neumann Eigenvalue

For a finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold `M` and an index `k ≥ 0`, the `k`-th Neumann eigenvalue of `M` is the `k`-th variational (min-max) eigenvalue over all smooth real functions on `M`, with respect to the Riemannian volume. For a compact surface it is the `k`-th Laplace eigenvalue, with Neumann boundary conditions when the boundary is nonempty.
