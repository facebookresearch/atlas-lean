# Neumann variational values of a manifold

For a finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold $M$ and an index $k \ge 0$, the $k$-th Neumann variational value of $M$ (zero-based) is the $k$-th [variational value](variational-eigenvalue.md) over all smooth real functions on $M$, for the Riemannian volume. In general it is a min-max value only. For a compact surface it is the $k$-th Laplace eigenvalue, with Neumann boundary conditions when the boundary is nonempty (Kokarev, eq. (1.2)); no identification is claimed in other dimensions.
