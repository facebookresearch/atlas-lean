# Rayleigh quotient

For a manifold $M$ with a Riemannian bundle, a measure $\mu$ and any function $u : M \to \mathbb{R}$, the Rayleigh quotient is the [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md) divided by the squared $L^2(\mu)$ mass, $R(u) = E(u) \,/\, \int |u|^2\, d\mu$, computed in $[0, \infty]$ with the conventions $0/0 = 0$, $a/0 = \infty$ for $a > 0$, and $a/\infty = 0$. It has its usual meaning when the mass is positive and finite.
