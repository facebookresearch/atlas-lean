# Dirichlet energy on an inner product space

On a real inner product space $F$, viewed as a manifold, with any measure $\mu$ and any function $u : F \to \mathbb{R}$, the Dirichlet energy equals $\int \|Du(x)\|^2\, d\mu(x)$, the lower Lebesgue integral of the squared operator norm of the Fréchet derivative, taken as $0$ where $u$ is not differentiable. No finite dimension or completeness is assumed; when $F$ is complete, $\|Du(x)\| = |\nabla u(x)|$ for the gradient.
