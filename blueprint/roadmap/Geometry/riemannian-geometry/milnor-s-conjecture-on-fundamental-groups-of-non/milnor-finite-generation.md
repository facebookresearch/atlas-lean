# Milnor's finite-generation property in dimension $n$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, Milnor's finite-generation property in dimension $n$ is the statement: for every connected smooth ($C^\infty$) manifold $M$ without boundary, modeled on $\mathbb{R}^n$, with a $C^\infty$ Riemannian metric whose Riemannian distance is the metric of $M$, complete for that distance, and with nonnegative Ricci curvature $\operatorname{Ric} \ge 0$, the fundamental group $\pi_1(M, x)$ is finitely generated for every base point $x \in M$.
