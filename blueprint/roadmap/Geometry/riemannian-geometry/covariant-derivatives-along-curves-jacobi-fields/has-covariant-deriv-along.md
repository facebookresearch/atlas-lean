# Has Covariant Deriv Along

Let `M` be a finite-dimensional `C²` manifold with a `C¹` Riemannian metric `g`, `u` the velocity curve of a curve `γ`, and `V`, `V'` vector fields along `γ`. Then `V'` is the covariant derivative `D_t V` of `V` at time `t` if, for every vector field `W` differentiable at `γ(t)`, the metric-compatibility identity `d/dt g(V, W ∘ γ) = g(V', W) + g(V, ∇_{γ'} W)` holds at `t`, where `∇` is the Levi-Civita connection. This characterizes `D_t V` at `t` uniquely.
