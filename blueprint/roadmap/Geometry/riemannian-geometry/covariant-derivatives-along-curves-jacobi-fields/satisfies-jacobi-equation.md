# Satisfies Jacobi Equation

Let `M` be a finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold and `u` a curve in its tangent bundle, the velocity curve of a curve `γ`. A vector field `J` along `γ` satisfies the Jacobi equation if there is a vector field `J'` along `γ` such that, at every time, `J'` is the covariant derivative of `J` and `-R(J, γ')γ'` is the covariant derivative of `J'`; that is, `D_t² J + R(J, γ')γ' = 0`. Here `R` is the Riemann curvature tensor with the sign convention of Lee, under which do Carmo's `R(γ', J)γ'` is `R(J, γ')γ'`. The curve is not required to be a geodesic.
