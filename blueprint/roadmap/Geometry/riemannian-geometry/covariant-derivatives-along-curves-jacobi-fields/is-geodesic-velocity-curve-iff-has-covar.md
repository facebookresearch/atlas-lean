# Is Geodesic Velocity Curve iff has Covariant Deriv Along

On a finite-dimensional `C²` manifold with a `C¹` Riemannian metric, a curve `u` in the tangent bundle is the velocity curve of a geodesic if and only if its velocity is parallel along it: at every time `t` the covariant derivative `D_t γ'` of the velocity field is `0`.
