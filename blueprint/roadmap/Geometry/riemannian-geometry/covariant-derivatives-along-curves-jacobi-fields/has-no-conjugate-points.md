# Has No Conjugate Points

A finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold `M` has no conjugate points if no geodesic velocity curve defined on all of `ℝ` has a pair of conjugate times; equivalently, no nontrivial Jacobi field along such a geodesic vanishes at two different times. On a complete manifold without boundary this is the usual notion; this is not the condition that every geodesic is minimizing.
