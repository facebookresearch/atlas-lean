# Conjugate times along a geodesic

Let $u$ be a curve in the tangent bundle of a finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold, defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$. Times $a$ and $b$ are conjugate along $u$ when $u$ is a geodesic velocity curve, $a \ne b$, and some vector field $J$ along it, defined at every real time, satisfies the Jacobi equation, vanishes at $a$ and $b$, and is not identically zero (do Carmo, Ch. 5, Def. 3.1; Lee, Ch. 10). Then $\gamma(a)$ and $\gamma(b)$ are conjugate along $\gamma$; the times may be negative and in either order.
