# Prime closed geodesics of a Finsler sphere

Let $F$ be a Finsler metric on $S^2$. The distinct prime closed geodesics of $F$ are the classes of unit-speed closed geodesics of $F$ modulo time shifts: the image of the set of unit-speed closed geodesics $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to S^2$ of $F$ in the quotient of all curves $\mathbb{R} \to S^2$ by the relation $\gamma \sim \delta$ when $\delta(t) = \gamma(t + s)$ for some $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and all $t$.

A closed geodesic and its iterates give the same unit-speed curve, so each class is one prime closed geodesic; a closed geodesic and its reversal, when both are geodesics, give different classes.
