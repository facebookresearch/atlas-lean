# Unit-speed closed geodesics of a Finsler sphere

Let $F$ be a Finsler metric on $S^2$ (possibly irreversible). A unit-speed closed geodesic of $F$ is a curve $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to S^2$ that is a closed geodesic of $F$ (a non-constant periodic geodesic: $\gamma(t + T) = \gamma(t)$ for some $T > 0$ and all $t$) and has unit speed: locally around every time, $d_F(\gamma(t_1), \gamma(t_2)) = t_2 - t_1$ for $t_1 \le t_2$, where $d_F$ is the forward distance of $F$. This page denotes by $\mathcal{G}(F)$ the set of all such curves.
