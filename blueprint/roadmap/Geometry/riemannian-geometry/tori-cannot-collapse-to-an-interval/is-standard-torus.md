# Manifolds diffeomorphic to the standard torus

A smooth $n$-manifold $M$ (modelled on $\mathbb{R}^n$) is diffeomorphic to the standard torus $\mathbb{R}^n / \mathbb{Z}^n$ in the following sense: there is a surjective $C^\infty$ local diffeomorphism $p : \mathbb{R}^n \to M$ such that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n$,

$$p(x) = p(y) \iff x - y \in \mathbb{Z}^n.$$

Such a map descends to a diffeomorphism $\mathbb{R}^n / \mathbb{Z}^n \to M$.
