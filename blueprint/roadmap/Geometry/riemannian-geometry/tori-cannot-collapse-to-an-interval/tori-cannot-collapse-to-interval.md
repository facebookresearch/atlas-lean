# Statement of tori cannot collapse to an interval

Question (AimPL, manifolds with non-negative sectional curvature, Problem 2.8): can an $n$-dimensional torus collapse to an interval? The statement considered is the negative answer.

Statement: let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $L > 0$. Let $(M_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of nonempty compact $C^\infty$ Riemannian $n$-manifolds, each carrying the distance induced by its Riemannian metric, such that each $M_i$ is diffeomorphic to the standard torus $\mathbb{R}^n / \mathbb{Z}^n$ and has sectional curvature at least $-1$. Then

$$d_{GH}\bigl(M_i, [0, L]\bigr) \not\to 0 \quad (i \to \infty),$$

where $d_{GH}$ is the Gromov-Hausdorff distance and $[0, L]$ carries its standard metric. It holds; the resolution is [Tori cannot collapse to an interval](tori-cannot-collapse-to-interval-holds.md).
