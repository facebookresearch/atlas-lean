# Surfaces carrying a hyperbolic metric

A smooth ($C^\infty$) surface $M$ carries a hyperbolic metric if there is a $C^\infty$ Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ of constant sectional curvature $-1$: for every $x \in M$ and every pair of linearly independent tangent vectors $u, v \in T_xM$, the sectional curvature of $g$ at $x$ on the plane spanned by $u$ and $v$ equals $-1$. The sign convention is that the round unit sphere has curvature $1$.
