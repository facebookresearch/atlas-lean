# Has Covariant Deriv Along vector Space of has Deriv At

In a finite-dimensional real inner product space $F$ with its flat metric, let $u(s) = (\gamma(s), v(s))$ be a curve in the tangent bundle and $V$, $V'$ vector fields along it. If the base curve has derivative $\gamma'(t) = v(t)$ at $t$ (the fibre component, not the pair $u(t)$), and $V$, viewed as an $F$-valued function, has derivative $V'(t)$ at $t$, then $V'(t)$ is the covariant derivative of $V$ along the curve at $t$. This is a sufficient criterion only.
