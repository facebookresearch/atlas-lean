# Has Covariant Deriv Along vector Space of has Deriv At

In a real inner product space with its flat metric, let `u` be a curve in the tangent bundle and `V`, `V'` vector fields along it. If the base curve has derivative the velocity `u(t)` at `t` and `V` has derivative `V'(t)` at `t` as a vector-valued function, then `V'(t)` is the covariant derivative of `V` along the curve at `t`.
