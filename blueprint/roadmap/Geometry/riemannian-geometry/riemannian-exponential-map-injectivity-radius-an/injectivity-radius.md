# Injectivity Radius

For a Riemannian manifold $M$ and a supplied family of maps $e_x : T_x M \to M$, the injectivity radius of $M$ is the infimum over all points $x$ of the [injectivity radius at $x$](injectivity-radius-at.md) for that family, a value in $[0, \infty]$. When the family is the Riemannian exponential map, this is the usual injectivity radius of $M$.
