# Injectivity Radius At

For a Riemannian manifold `M` with an exponential map `exp` and a point `x`, the injectivity radius at `x` is the supremum, in `[0, ∞]`, of the radii `a` such that `exp_x` restricted to the open ball of radius `a` in the tangent space `T_x M` is injective and a smooth local diffeomorphism, hence a diffeomorphism onto its image. It is `∞` when there is no bound, as for Euclidean space.
