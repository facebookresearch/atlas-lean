# Injectivity Domain

For a Riemannian manifold $M$ with Riemannian distance $d$, a supplied family of maps $e_x : T_x M \to M$ and a point $x$, the injectivity domain $I(x)$ is the set of tangent vectors $v \in T_x M$ for which some $t > 1$ satisfies $d(x, e_x(t v)) = \|t v\|$. When the family is the Riemannian exponential map, this says that the geodesic $s \mapsto \exp_x(s v)$ still minimizes distance slightly beyond time $1$.
