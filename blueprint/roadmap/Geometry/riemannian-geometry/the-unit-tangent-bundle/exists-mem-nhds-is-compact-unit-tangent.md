# Compact sets of unit tangent vectors over neighbourhoods

Let $M$ be a manifold modelled on a finite-dimensional real normed space, with a continuous Riemannian metric, and let $SM$ be its unit tangent bundle and $\pi$ the bundle projection. Every point $x$ has a neighbourhood $K$ such that $SM \cap \pi^{-1}(K)$, the set of unit tangent vectors over $K$, is compact. Finite dimension is needed: in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space the unit vectors over a single point form a closed noncompact sphere.
