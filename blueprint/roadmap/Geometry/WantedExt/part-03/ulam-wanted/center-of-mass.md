# Center Of Mass

The center of mass of a `d`-dimensional compact convex set `K ⊆ ℝᵈ` is defined to be
`(1 / vol(K)) * ∫_K x dx`, where `dx` is the usual Lebesgue measure in `ℝᵈ`.
