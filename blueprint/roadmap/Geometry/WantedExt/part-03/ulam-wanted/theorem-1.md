# Theorem 1

Statement of Theorem 1:

Let `d ≥ 3`. There exists a strictly convex non-centrally-symmetric body of revolution `K ⊂ ℝᵈ`
that floats in equilibrium in every orientation at the level `vol(K)/2`.
