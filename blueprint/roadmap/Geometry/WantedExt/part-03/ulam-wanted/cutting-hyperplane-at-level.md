# Cutting Hyperplane At Level

A hyperplane `H(ξ) = { p ∈ ℝᵈ | ξ ∙ p = t }` is called the cutting hyperplane of `K` at
level `δ ∈ ℝ`, if `vol(K ∩ H⁻(ξ)) = δ`.
