# Definition 40.8

Let $X$ be a geodesic metric space. $X$ is called nonpositively curved in the sense of Cartan--Alexandrov--Topogonov (or a CAT space) if for all $\Delta, \Delta^{\prime}$ and all points $x, y$ on the boundary of $\Delta$, with comparison points $x^{\prime}, y^{\prime}$, we have $\operatorname{dist}(x,y) \leq \operatorname{dist}(x^{\prime}, y^{\prime})$.
