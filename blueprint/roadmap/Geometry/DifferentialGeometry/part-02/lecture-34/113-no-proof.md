# Theorem 34.4 (no proof)

Moving frames with singularities always exist. Moreover, for any choice of such frame, the sum $\sum_{i} m(p_{i})$ is the same. It agrees with a topological invariant of $M$, called the Euler characteristic $\chi(M)$.
