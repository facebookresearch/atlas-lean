# Corollary 34.5 (Gauss-Bonnet theorem; sketch proof)

For any compact surface $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$, $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}^{\text{tot}} = 2\pi \cdot \chi(M)$.
