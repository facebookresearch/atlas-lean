# Theorem 35.1 (Hopf; no proof)

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be a closed hypersurface of even dimension $n$, and $\nu \colon M \rightarrow S^{n}$ a Gauss map. Then $\operatorname{deg}(\nu) = \chi(M) / 2$.
