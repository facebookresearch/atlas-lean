# Definition 35.3

A compact combinatorial surface consists of a finite collection $\{P_{i}\}$ of flat convex polygons in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$, with the following properties: any two $P_{i}$ are either disjoint or share a common edge; (ii) any edge of any given $P_{i}$ belongs to precisely one other $P_{j}$, $j \neq i$.
