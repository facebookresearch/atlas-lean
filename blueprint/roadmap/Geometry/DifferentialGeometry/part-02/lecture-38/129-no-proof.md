# Theorem 38.3 (no proof)

Suppose that $M$ is closed and connected. Then, for any given $p, q$ and any interval $[a,b]$, there is a geodesic $\gamma \colon [a,b] \rightarrow M$ which is an absolute minimizer of the energy.
