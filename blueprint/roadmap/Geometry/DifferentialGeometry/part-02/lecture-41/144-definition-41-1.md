# Definition 41.1

The geodesic curvature of a regular curve $\gamma$ on a surface $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$ with Gauss map $\nu$ is defined by $\kappa_{\text{geod}}(t) = \frac{\operatorname{det}(\gamma^{\prime}(t), \gamma^{\prime\prime}(t), \nu(\gamma(t)))}{\|\gamma^{\prime}(t)\|^{3}}$.
