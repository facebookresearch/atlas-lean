# Lemma 41.2

Take a partial parametrization $f \colon U \rightarrow M \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$ which is compatible with our choice of orientation, and let $\gamma = f(c)$. Suppose that we have a moving frame $(X_{1}(x), X_{2}(x))$ which is positively oriented, and such that $X_{1}(c(t)) = c^{\prime}(t) / I_{c(t)}(c^{\prime}(t), c^{\prime}(t))^{1/2}$. Then, in terms of the associated connection matrices, $\kappa_{\text{geod}}(t) = \frac{(A_{1})_{12} c_{1}^{\prime}(t) + (A_{2})_{12} c_{2}^{\prime}(t)}{I_{c(t)}(c^{\prime}(t), c^{\prime}(t))^{1/2}}$.
