# Corollary 41.4

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$ be a surface, $f \colon U \rightarrow M$ a partial parametrization, and $T \subset U$ a curvilinear triangle, whose sides map to geodesics in $M$. Let $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ be the angles at the corners of the triangle, measured with respect to the first fundamental form. Then $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3} = \pi + \int_{T} \kappa_{\text{gauss}} \sqrt{\operatorname{det} G} \, dx$.
