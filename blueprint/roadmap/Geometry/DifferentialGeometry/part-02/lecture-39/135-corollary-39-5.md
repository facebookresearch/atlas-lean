# Corollary 39.5

Let $M$ be a closed connected hypersurface. Then, for any two points $p, q$ there is a path $\gamma$ connecting them, such that $L(\gamma) = \operatorname{dist}(p,q)$. In other words, the infimum in the definition of distance is always attained.
