# Theorem 39.7 (Schwarz-Pick)

Let $h \colon U \rightarrow U$ be a holomorphic (complex differentiable) function. Then at every point $z \in U$, $|h^{\prime}(z)| \leq \frac{1 - |h(z)|^{2}}{1 - |z|^{2}}$.
