# Proposition 39.2 (part of the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality; no proof)

Let $f \colon [a,b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function. Then $\int_{a}^{b} f(t) \, dt \leq \sqrt{b-a} \sqrt{\int_{a}^{b} f(t)^{2} \, dt}$ with equality if and only if $f$ is constant.
