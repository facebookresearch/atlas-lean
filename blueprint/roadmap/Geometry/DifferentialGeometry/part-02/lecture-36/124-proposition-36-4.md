# Proposition 36.4

Let $f \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be a partial parametrization of $M$, and $c \colon I \rightarrow U$ a smooth curve on its domain. Then $\gamma = f(c)$ is a geodesic iff $c$ itself satisfies the geodesic equation $\frac{d^{2} c_{k}}{dt^{2}} + \sum_{ij} \Gamma_{ij}^{k} \frac{dc_{i}}{dt} \frac{dc_{j}}{dt} = 0$.
