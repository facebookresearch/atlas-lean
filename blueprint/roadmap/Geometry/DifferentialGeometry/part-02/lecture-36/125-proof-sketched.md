# Corollary 36.5 (proof sketched)

Two geodesics $\gamma, \tilde{\gamma} \colon I \rightarrow M$ with $\gamma(0) = \tilde{\gamma}(0)$ and $\gamma^{\prime}(0) = \tilde{\gamma}^{\prime}(0)$ agree.
