# Lemma 36.1

Let $\gamma \colon [a,b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a smooth path, with $\gamma(a) = p$ and $\gamma(b) = q$. Its length $L(\gamma) = \int_{a}^{b} \|\gamma^{\prime}(t)\| \, dt$ is $\geq \|q - p\|$, and equality holds iff $\gamma^{\prime}(t)$ is always a nonnegative multiple of $q - p$.
