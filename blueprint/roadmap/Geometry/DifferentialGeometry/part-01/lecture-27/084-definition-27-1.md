# Definition 27.1

Let $U, V$ be open subsets of $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. A smooth map $\phi \colon V \rightarrow U$ is a diffeomorphism if it is one-to-one and the inverse $\phi^{-1}$ is also smooth (it is in fact enough to check that $\phi$ is one-to-one and that $D\phi_{y}$ is invertible for all $y$, since that ensures smoothness of the inverse).
