# Proposition 6.2

Let $c$ be a Frenet curve in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Then $\frac{\operatorname{det}\left(c^{\prime}, c^{\prime\prime}, \ldots, c^{(n)}\right)}{\left\|c^{\prime}\right\|^{n(n+1)/2}} = \prod_{i=1}^{n-1} \kappa_{i}^{n-i}$.
