# Definition 9.2

Let $c$ be a simple closed curve. We say that $c$ is convex if the following holds. Whenever $c$ is tangent to some line $\{a_{1} x_{1} + a_{2} x_{2} = b\}$ in the plane, it is entirely contained in one of the two half-planes $\{a_{1} x_{1} + a_{2} x_{2} \leq b\}$, $\{a_{1} x_{1} + a_{2} x_{2} \geq b\}$.
