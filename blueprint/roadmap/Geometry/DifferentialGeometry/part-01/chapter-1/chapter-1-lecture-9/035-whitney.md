# Theorem 9.5 (Whitney)

Let $c$ be a closed curve with normal self-intersections. Assume that it is parametrized in such a way that $c_{2}(t)$ reaches a global minimum at $t = 0$. Then $\kappa^{\text{tot}}(c) / 2\pi = \operatorname{sign} c_{1}^{\prime}(0) - \sum_{(s,t)} \operatorname{sign} \operatorname{det}\left(c^{\prime}(s), c^{\prime}(t)\right)$ where the sum is over all $0 \leq s < t < T$ with $c(s) = c(t)$.
