# Corollary 8.6

Let $c$ be a closed curve of period $T$. Suppose that there are only finitely many points $0 \leq t_{1} < t_{2} < \cdots < t_{m} < T$ where $c_{2}^{\prime}(t_{k}) = 0$, $c_{1}^{\prime}(t_{k}) > 0$, and that any such point satisfies $\kappa(t_{k}) \neq 0$. Then, the rotation number is $\kappa^{\text{tot}}(c) / 2\pi = \sum_{k=1}^{m} \operatorname{sign}\left(\kappa(t_{k})\right)$.
