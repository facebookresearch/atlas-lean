# Proposition 8.5

$\kappa^{\text{tot}}(c) / 2\pi$ is the degree of $f(t) = c^{\prime}(t) / \left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|$. In particular, it is always an integer. We call it the rotation number of the curve.
