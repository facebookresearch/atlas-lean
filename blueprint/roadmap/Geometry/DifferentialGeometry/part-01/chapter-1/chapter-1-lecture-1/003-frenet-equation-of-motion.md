# Proposition 1.3 (Frenet equation of motion)

For a regular curve $c$, $\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{c^{\prime}(t)}{\left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|}\right) = \left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\| \kappa(t) J \frac{c^{\prime}(t)}{\left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|} = \kappa(t) J c^{\prime}(t)$.
