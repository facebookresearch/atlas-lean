# Lemma 1.1

Let $I \subset \mathbb{R}$ be an interval, and $f \colon I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$ a smooth map such that $\|f(t)\| = 1$ for all $t$. Then $f^{\prime}(t) = \operatorname{det}\left(f(t), f^{\prime}(t)\right) J f(t)$.
