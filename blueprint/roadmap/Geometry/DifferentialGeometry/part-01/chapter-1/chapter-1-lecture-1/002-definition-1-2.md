# Definition 1.2

A regular curve is a smooth map $c \colon I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$, where $I \subset \mathbb{R}$ is an interval, satisfying $c^{\prime}(t) \neq 0$ for all $t$. The curvature of $c$ at $t$ is $\kappa(t) = \frac{\operatorname{det}\left(c^{\prime}(t), c^{\prime\prime}(t)\right)}{\left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|^{3}}$.
