# Corollary 1.5

Suppose that $\kappa(t) = 1/R$ is a nonzero constant. Then $c + R J \frac{c^{\prime}}{\left\|c^{\prime}\right\|}$ is constant, and therefore $c$ is part of a circle of radius $|R|$.
