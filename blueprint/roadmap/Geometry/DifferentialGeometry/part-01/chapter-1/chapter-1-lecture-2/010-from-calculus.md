# Lemma 2.5 (from calculus)

Let $\tilde{I} \subset \mathbb{R}$ be an interval, and $\psi \colon \tilde{I} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ a smooth function such that $\psi^{\prime}(t) > 0$ for all $t$. Then $\psi(\tilde{I}) = I$ is an interval, and $\psi$ is a one-to-one map from $I$ to $\tilde{I}$. Moreover, its inverse map $\phi = \psi^{-1}$ is again smooth, and by the chain rule $\phi^{\prime}(t) = 1 / \psi^{\prime}(\phi(t))$.
