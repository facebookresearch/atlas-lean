# Lemma 2.2

The curvature of a unit speed curve is $\kappa(t) = \operatorname{det}\left(c^{\prime}(t), c^{\prime\prime}(t)\right)$. Moreover, we have $c^{\prime\prime}(t) = \kappa(t) J c^{\prime}(t)$ and in particular $|\kappa(t)| = \left\|c^{\prime\prime}(t)\right\|$.
