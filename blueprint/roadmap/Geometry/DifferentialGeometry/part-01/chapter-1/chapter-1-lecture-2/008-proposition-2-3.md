# Proposition 2.3

For every $\kappa \colon I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ there is a unit speed curve $c \colon I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ whose curvature is $\kappa$. Moreover, $c$ is unique up to translations and rotations.
