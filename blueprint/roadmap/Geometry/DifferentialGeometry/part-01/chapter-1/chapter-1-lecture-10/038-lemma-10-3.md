# Lemma 10.3

Take a simple closed curve whose curvature is everywhere positive. By reparametrizing in a suitable way, one can achieve that the curve has period $2\pi$ and satisfies $\frac{c^{\prime}(t)}{\left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|} = (\cos(t), \sin(t))$. In that case, $\kappa(t) = \frac{1}{\left\|c^{\prime}(t)\right\|}$.
