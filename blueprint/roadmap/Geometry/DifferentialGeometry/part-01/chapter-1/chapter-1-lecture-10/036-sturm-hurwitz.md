# Lemma 10.1 (Sturm-Hurwitz)

Let $f \colon \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous $2\pi$-periodic function such that $\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(t) \, dt = 0$, $\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(t) \cos(t) \, dt = 0$, $\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(t) \sin(t) \, dt = 0$. Then $f$ has at least four zeros in the region $[0, 2\pi)$.
