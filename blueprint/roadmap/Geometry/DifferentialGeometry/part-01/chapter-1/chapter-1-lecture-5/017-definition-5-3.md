# Definition 5.3

$c \colon I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a Frenet curve if for all $t$, the vectors $(c^{\prime}(t), c^{\prime\prime}(t), \ldots, c^{(n-1)}(t))$ are linearly independent.
