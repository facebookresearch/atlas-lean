# Lemma 5.4

Frenet frames are reparametrization invariant. Explicitly, if $c$ is a Frenet curve and $d(t) = c(\phi(t))$ a reparametrization, then $d$ is again Frenet, and its Frenet frame is related to that of $c$ by $f_{i}(t) = e_{i}(\phi(t))$.
