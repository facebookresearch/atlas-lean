# Lemma 5.2

Let $E(t)$ be a family of orthogonal matrices, depending differentiably on $t$. Write $\frac{d}{dt} E(t) = E(t) A(t)$. Then the matrices $A(t)$ are skewsymmetric, $A(t)^{\mathrm{tr}} = -A(t)$.
