# Definition 7.2

The degree of $f$ is $\operatorname{deg}(f) = \frac{1}{2\pi}(\theta(T) - \theta(0)) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{0}^{T} \operatorname{det}\left(f(\tau), f^{\prime}(\tau)\right) d\tau \in \mathbb{Z}$. Instead of $[0, T]$, one can take any other interval $[t_{0}, t_{0}+T]$.
