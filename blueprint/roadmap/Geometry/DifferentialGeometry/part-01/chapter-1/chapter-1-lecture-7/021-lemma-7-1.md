# Lemma 7.1

One can write $f(t) = (\cos \theta(t), \sin \theta(t))$, where $\theta \colon \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is a smooth function, unique up to adding constant integer multiples of $2\pi$. Specifically, all such functions are of the form $\theta(t) = \theta_{0} + \int_{t_{0}}^{t} \operatorname{det}\left(f(\tau), f^{\prime}(\tau)\right) d\tau$ where $(\cos \theta_{0}, \sin \theta_{0}) = f(t_{0})$.
