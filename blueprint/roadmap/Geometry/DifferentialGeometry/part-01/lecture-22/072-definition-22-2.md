# Definition 22.2

The Gauss normal vector of a spacelike hypersurface is the unique $\nu = \nu(x)$ such that $\langle \nu, \nu \rangle_{\text{Min}} = -1$, $\langle \nu, \partial_{x_{i}} f \rangle_{\text{Min}} = 0$, and $\operatorname{det}(\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f, \nu) > 0$.
