# Lemma 22.3

If $X \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ has $\langle X, X \rangle_{\text{Min}} < 0$, then its Minkowski orthogonal complement $X^{\perp} = \{Y \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \mid \langle X, Y \rangle_{\text{Min}} = 0\}$ has the property that $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle_{\text{Min}}$ restricted to $X^{\perp}$ is positive definite.
