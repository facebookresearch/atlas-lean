# Proposition 24.3 (L'Hopital's rule; proof postponed)

Let $\psi \colon V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a local defining function for $M$, and $\phi \colon V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ another smooth function which vanishes along $V \cap M$. Then there is a unique smooth function $q \colon V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ such that $\phi = q\psi$.
