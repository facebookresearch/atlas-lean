# Lemma 19.1

If $(v_{i})_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ is any basis of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, then $(v_{i} \wedge v_{j})_{1 \leq i < j \leq n}$ is a basis of the space of antisymmetric matrices.
