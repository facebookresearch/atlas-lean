# Lemma 19.3

We have $\operatorname{trace}(\Lambda^{2} L) = \frac{1}{2}(\operatorname{trace}(L)^{2} - \operatorname{trace}(L^{2}))$ and $\operatorname{det}(\Lambda^{2} L) = \operatorname{det}(L)^{n-1}$.
