# Lemma 19.4

Suppose that $L, \tilde{L} \colon \mathbb{R}^{n} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n}$ are two linear maps, with $\operatorname{rank}(L) \geq 3$. Then, if $\Lambda^{2} L = \Lambda^{2} \tilde{L}$, it also follows that $L = \pm \tilde{L}$.
