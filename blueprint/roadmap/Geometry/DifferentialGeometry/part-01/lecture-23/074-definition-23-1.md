# Definition 23.1

A set of Gauss normal vectors for $f$ consists of maps $\nu^{1}, \ldots, \nu^{m} \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+m}$ satisfying $\langle \nu^{w}, \nu^{w} \rangle = 1$, $\langle \nu^{v}, \nu^{w} \rangle = 0$ for $u \neq w$, $\langle \nu^{w}, \partial_{x_{i}} f \rangle = 0$, and $\operatorname{det}(\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f, \nu^{1}, \ldots, \nu^{m}) > 0$.
