# Proposition 29.5

Let $f$ be a partial parametrization. Denote by $I^{f}$ its first fundamental form, and by $S^{f}$ its shape operator. Under the identification $Df \colon \mathbb{R}^{n} \rightarrow TM_{f(x)}$, $I^{f}$ turns into the ordinary scalar product, and $S^{f}$ into the shape operator $S$ of $M$. Explicitly, the second part of this says that $S \colon TM_{f(x)} \rightarrow TM_{f(x)}$ and $S^{f} \colon \mathbb{R}^{n} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n}$ are related by $S^{f} = Df^{-1} \cdot S \circ Df$.
