# Lemma 29.2

Let $U \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be an open subset containing the origin, and $\psi \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ a smooth function which vanishes at all points $x \in U$ whose last coordinate $x_{n+1}$ is zero. Then there is a unique smooth function $q$ such that $\psi = q x_{n+1}$.
