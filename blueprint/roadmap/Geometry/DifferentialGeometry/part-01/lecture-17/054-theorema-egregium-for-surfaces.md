# Corollary 17.1 (Theorema egregium for surfaces)

The Gauss curvature of a surface patch is given in terms of the first fundamental form by $\kappa_{\text{gauss}} = \frac{\sum_{u} G_{2u} R_{121}^{u}}{\operatorname{det}(G)}$.
