# Theorem 30.5 (from topology; no proof)

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be a connected compact hypersurface, with $n \geq 2$, and $\phi \colon M \rightarrow S^{n}$ a smooth map such that $D\phi_{y} \colon TM_{y} \rightarrow TS_{\phi(y)}^{n}$ is an isomorphism for all $y$. Then $\phi$ is bijective (one-to-one and onto).
