# Proposition 13.1

The coordinate changes for the main associated data are $\tilde{\nu}(x) = \nu(\phi(x))$, $\tilde{G}(x) = D\phi(x)^{\mathrm{tr}} \cdot G(\phi(x)) \cdot D\phi(x)$, $\tilde{H}(x) = D\phi(x)^{\mathrm{tr}} \cdot H(\phi(x)) \cdot D\phi(x)$, $\tilde{L}(x) = D\phi(x)^{-1} \cdot L(\phi(x)) \cdot D\phi(x)$.
