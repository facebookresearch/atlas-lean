# Lemma 14.5

The Gauss curvature is $\kappa_{\text{gauss}} = (-1)^{n} \frac{\operatorname{det}\left(\partial_{x_{1}} \nu, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} \nu, \nu\right)}{\sqrt{\operatorname{det} G}}$.
