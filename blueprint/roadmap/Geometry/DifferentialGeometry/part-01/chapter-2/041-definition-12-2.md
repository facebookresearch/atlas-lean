# Definition 12.2

Let $f$ be a hypersurface patch. There is a unique $\nu \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ such that $\nu(x)$ is of length one, is orthogonal to $\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f$, and satisfies $\operatorname{det}\left(\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f, \nu(x)\right) > 0$. It is automatically smooth. We call $\nu(x)$ the Gauss normal vector of $f$ at the point $x$.
