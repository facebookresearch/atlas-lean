# Definition 12.3

Define $G_{ij}(x) = \langle \partial_{x_{i}} f, \partial_{x_{j}} f \rangle$. Equivalently, the matrix with entries $G_{ij}(x)$ is $G_{x} = Df_{x}^{\mathrm{tr}} \cdot Df_{x}$. The associated inner product, $I_{x}(X, Y) = \langle X, G_{x} Y \rangle = \langle Df_{x}(X), Df_{x}(Y) \rangle$, is called the first fundamental form.
