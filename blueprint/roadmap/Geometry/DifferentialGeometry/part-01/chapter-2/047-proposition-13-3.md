# Proposition 13.3

Let $f, \tilde{f} \colon U \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ be two hypersurface patches, defined on the same connected set $U \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$. Suppose that their first and second fundamental forms coincide. Then $\tilde{f}(x) = Af(x) + c$, where $A$ is an orthogonal matrix with determinant $+1$, and $c$ some constant.
