# Lemma 12.6

$D\nu = -Df \cdot L$ (matrix multiplication). More explicitly, each partial derivative $\partial_{x_{i}} \nu$ lies in the linear span of $\{\partial_{x_{1}} f, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} f\}$, and the shape operator allows us to express it as a linear combination of these vectors: $\partial_{x_{i}} \nu = -\sum_{j} L_{ji}(x) \partial_{x_{j}} f$.
