# Definition 14.1

The eigenvalues $(\lambda_{1}, \ldots, \lambda_{n})$ of $L_{x}$ are called the principal curvatures of the hypersurface patch $f$ at $x$. The corresponding eigenvectors $(X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n})$ are called the principal curvature directions.
