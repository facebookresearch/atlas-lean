# Theorem 16.2

Let $g^{ij}$ be the coefficients of the inverse matrix $G^{-1}$. Then $\Gamma_{ij}^{l} = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{k} g^{kl} (\partial_{x_{j}} G_{ik} - \partial_{x_{k}} G_{ij} + \partial_{x_{i}} G_{jk})$.
