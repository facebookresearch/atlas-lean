# Theorem 16.3

The Gauss equation holds: $H_{ij} L_{sk} - H_{ik} L_{sj} = \partial_{k} \Gamma_{ij}^{s} - \partial_{j} \Gamma_{ik}^{s} + \sum_{t} \Gamma_{ij}^{t} \Gamma_{kt}^{s} - \Gamma_{ik}^{t} \Gamma_{jt}^{s}$.
