# Corollary 18.5 (Gauss-Bonnet for tori)

Let $f \colon \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{3}$ be a doubly-periodic surface patch, which means that $f(x_{1}+T_{1}, x_{2}) = f(x_{1}, x_{2}) = f(x_{1}, x_{2}+T_{2})$ for some $T_{1}, T_{2} > 0$. Then $\kappa_{\text{gauss}}^{\text{tot}} \coloneqq \int_{[0, T_{1}] \times [0, T_{2}]} \kappa_{\text{gauss}} \sqrt{\operatorname{det}(G)} \, dx_{1} \, dx_{2} = 0$.
