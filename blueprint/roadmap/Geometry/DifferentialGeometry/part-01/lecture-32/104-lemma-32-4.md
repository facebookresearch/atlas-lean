# Lemma 32.4

Suppose that $\phi$ is bijective (one-to-one and onto), and that $\operatorname{det}(D\phi)$ is everywhere positive (or everywhere negative). Then $\operatorname{deg}(\phi) = 1$ (or $-1$).
