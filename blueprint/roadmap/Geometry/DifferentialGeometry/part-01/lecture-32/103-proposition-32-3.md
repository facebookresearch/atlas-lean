# Proposition 32.3

Suppose that $\tilde{M}$ is decomposed into $f_{i}(P_{i})$ as in the previous lecture, where $f_{i} \colon U_{i} \rightarrow M$ are partial parametrizations, and $P_{i} \subset U_{i}$ polytopes. Then $\operatorname{deg}(\phi) = \frac{1}{\operatorname{vol}(\tilde{M})} \left(\sum_{i} \int_{P_{i}} \operatorname{det}(\partial_{x_{1}} \phi^{f_{i}}, \ldots, \partial_{x_{n}} \phi^{f_{i}}, \tilde{\nu}(\phi(f_{i}(x)))) \, dx \right)$ where $\phi^{f_{i}} = \phi \circ f_{i}$.
