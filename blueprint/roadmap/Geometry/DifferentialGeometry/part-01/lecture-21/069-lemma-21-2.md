# Lemma 21.2

Suppose that we have numbers $S_{ijk}$ (the indices $i, j, k$ run from 1 to $n$) such that $S_{ijk} = S_{jik}$. Then there are numbers $T_{ijk}$ with $T_{ijk} = T_{kji}$ such that $S_{ijk} = T_{ijk} + T_{jik}$.
