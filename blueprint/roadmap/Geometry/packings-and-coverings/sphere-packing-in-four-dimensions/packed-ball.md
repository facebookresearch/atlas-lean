# Packed ball

For a packing $P$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$ with radius $r$ and a point
$c \in \mathbb{R}^4$, the packed ball of $P$ at $c$ is the closed ball
$\{x \in \mathbb{R}^4 : \|x - c\| \le r\}$, boundary included.

## Sources

- H. Cohn and N. Elkies, *New upper bounds on sphere packings I*, Ann. of Math. 157 (2003), 689–714, arXiv:math/0110009: the packed closed ball at a center, including its boundary.
