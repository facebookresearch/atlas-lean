# Closed rotated square in the plane

For real numbers $a, b, \theta, s$, the closed square with corner $(a, b)$, side $s$ and rotation angle $\theta$ is the subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$

$$\bigl\{(a + x\cos\theta - y\sin\theta,\ b + x\sin\theta + y\cos\theta) : 0 \le x \le s,\ 0 \le y \le s\bigr\},$$

that is, the image of $[0, s]^2$ under rotation by $\theta$ followed by translation by $(a, b)$. For $s < 0$ the set is empty and for $s = 0$ it is the single point $(a, b)$.
