# Coverings of the plane by translates

Let $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, let $(t_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and let $s \subseteq I$. The translates $P + t_i$ with $i \in s$ cover the plane when for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^2$ there is $i \in s$ with $x \in P + t_i$, that is, $x - t_i \in P$.
