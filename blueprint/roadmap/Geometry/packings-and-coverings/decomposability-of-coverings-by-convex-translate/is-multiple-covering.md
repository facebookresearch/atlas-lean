# $k$-fold coverings by translates

Let $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $(t_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ indexed by an arbitrary set $I$ (repetitions allowed). The translates $P + t_i$ form a $k$-fold covering of the plane when every point lies in at least $k$ of them, counted by index:

$$\#\{\, i \in I : x \in P + t_i \,\} \ge k \quad \text{for every } x \in \mathbb{R}^2,$$

where the count may be infinite.
