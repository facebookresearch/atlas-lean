# Atomic packing factor expression

For $n$ atoms of radius $R$ in a cell of volume $V_c$, the packing-factor expression is
$n\cdot\tfrac43\pi R^3/V_c$ (with division by $0$ giving $0$). It is a packing factor only for
$R > 0$ and $V_c > 0$, the domain of the [atomic packing factor](atomic-packing-factor.md).
