# Theoretical density expression

For $n$ atoms per cell, molar mass $A$, cell volume $V_c$ and Avogadro constant $N_A$, the density
expression is $nA/(V_cN_A)$ (with division by $0$ giving $0$). It is a density only for
$A, V_c, N_A > 0$, the domain of the [theoretical density](theoretical-density.md).
