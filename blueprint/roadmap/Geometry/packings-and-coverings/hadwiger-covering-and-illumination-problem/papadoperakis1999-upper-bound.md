# Papadoperakis's bound for Hadwiger's covering problem in dimension 3

Every convex body
$K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ is covered by $16$ translates of its interior $\operatorname{int} K$,
repetitions allowed.

## Sources

- Ioannis Papadoperakis, An estimate for the problem of illumination of the boundary of a convex body in $E^3$, Geometriae Dedicata 75 (1999), 275–285, DOI 10.1023/A:1005056207406; reported in Andrii Arman, Andriy Bondarenko and Andriy Prymak, On Hadwiger's covering problem in small dimensions, arXiv:2404.00547v1 (2024), introduction.
