# Prymak's bound for Hadwiger's covering problem in dimension 3

Every convex body
$K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ is covered by $14$ translates of its interior $\operatorname{int} K$,
repetitions allowed.

## Sources

- Andriy Prymak, Every 3-dimensional convex body can be covered by 14 smaller homothetic copies, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 37 (2023), 17–24, arXiv:2112.10698; reported in Andrii Arman, Andriy Bondarenko and Andriy Prymak, On Hadwiger's covering problem in small dimensions, arXiv:2404.00547v1 (2024), introduction.
