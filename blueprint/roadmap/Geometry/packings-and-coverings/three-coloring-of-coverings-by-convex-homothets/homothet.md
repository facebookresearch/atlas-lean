# Homothets of a planar set

For a set $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, a point $a \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and a real number $r$, the homothet of $D$ with displacement $a$ and ratio $r$ is the image of $D$ under the map $x \mapsto a + r x$:

$$a + rD = \{\, a + r x : x \in D \,\}.$$

Only translations and scalings are allowed, not rotations. The definition places no restriction on $r$; in particular $r = 0$ gives $\{a\}$ for nonempty $D$ and $r < 0$ reflects $D$ through the origin and scales it by $|r|$ before translating.
