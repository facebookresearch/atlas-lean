# Jung's bound for the Lebesgue covering problem

The Lebesgue universal covering constant
is at most $\pi/3$.

## Sources

- Jung's theorem, quoted in György Elekes, Generalized breadths, circular Cantor sets, and the least area UCC, Discrete Comput. Geom. 12 (1994), 439–449, DOI 10.1007/BF02574391. Listed in the optimization constants repository, constant 13b, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/13b.md.
