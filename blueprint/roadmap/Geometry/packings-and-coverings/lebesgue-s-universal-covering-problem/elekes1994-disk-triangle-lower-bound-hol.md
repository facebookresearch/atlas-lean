# Elekes's disk-and-triangle lower bound

The Lebesgue universal covering constant (the
infimum of the areas of universal convex covers) is at least $0.8257$.

## Sources

- György Elekes, Generalized breadths, circular Cantor sets, and the least area UCC, Discrete Comput. Geom. 12 (1994), 439–449, DOI 10.1007/BF02574391: disk and equilateral triangle lower bound.
