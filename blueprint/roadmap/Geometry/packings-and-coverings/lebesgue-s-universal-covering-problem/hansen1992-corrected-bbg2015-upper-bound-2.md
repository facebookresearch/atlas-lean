# Hansen's upper bound for Lebesgue's covering problem, as corrected

The Lebesgue universal
covering constant, the infimum of the areas of the nonempty convex sets in the Euclidean plane that
contain a congruent copy of every nonempty convex planar set of diameter $1$, is at most
$0.844137708398$.

## Sources

- H. C. Hansen, Small universal covers for sets of unit diameter, Geometriae Dedicata 42 (1992), DOI 10.1007/BF00147549; corrected in John C. Baez, Karine Bagdasaryan and Philip Gibbs, The Lebesgue universal covering problem, Journal of Computational Geometry 6 (2015), 288–299, arXiv:1502.01251.
