# Geometry

Geometry studies shapes, spaces, transformations, curvature, and the invariants preserved by geometric equivalence.

[Discrete geometry](discrete-geometry/README.md), the largest field with about a third of the subject's articles,
studies point configurations, lattice points, polyhedra and extremal shapes. Its largest topic,
[continuous Turán numbers](discrete-geometry/part-01/continuous-tur-n-numbers-of-planar-patterns/README.md), concerns
the supremum of the areas of open subsets of $[0, n]^2$ that avoid a planar pattern $P$, which for finite $P$ agrees
up to constant factors with the 0-1 matrix extremal function of $P$. The field also has
[lattice points in dilated regions](discrete-geometry/part-01/lattice-points-in-dilated-regions/README.md), the
[moving sofa](discrete-geometry/part-01/moving-sofa-problem/README.md) constant, between $2.2195$ and $2.37$, the
[Dehn invariant](discrete-geometry/part-01/dehn-invariant/README.md),
[distinct and unit distances](discrete-geometry/part-01/distinct-and-unit-distances/README.md), Monsky's theorem and
the Sylvester–Gallai theorem.

[Packings and coverings](packings-and-coverings/README.md), a fifth of the subject, has the
[Kepler conjecture](packings-and-coverings/kepler-conjecture/README.md) that the supremum of the densities of unit
sphere packings in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is $\pi/\sqrt{18}$, and covering problems such as
[Moser's worm problem](packings-and-coverings/moser-s-worm-problem/README.md),
[Lebesgue's universal covering problem](packings-and-coverings/lebesgue-s-universal-covering-problem/README.md) and
[Hadwiger's covering problem](packings-and-coverings/hadwiger-covering-and-illumination-problem/README.md), with every
convex body in $\mathbb{R}^3$ covered by $14$ translates of its interior. [Convex geometry](convex-geometry/README.md)
includes the
[Brunn–Minkowski and Prékopa–Leindler inequalities](convex-geometry/brunn-minkowski-and-pr-kopa-leindler-inequalitie/README.md)
and [Hadwiger's theorem](convex-geometry/hadwiger-s-theorem-on-valuations/README.md) that the continuous
isometry-invariant valuations on convex bodies in $\mathbb{R}^n$ form a space of dimension $n + 1$.

[Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry/README.md) runs from
[triangle geometry](euclidean-geometry/triangle-geometry/README.md) to
[isometries and crystallographic groups](euclidean-geometry/isometries-and-symmetry/README.md), with the 17 wallpaper
groups and the 230 space groups. On the smooth side, [Riemannian and Finsler geometry](riemannian-geometry/README.md)
has the [Hopf–Rinow theorem](riemannian-geometry/hopf-rinow-theorem/README.md), the
[sphere theorem](riemannian-geometry/sphere-theorem/README.md) for compact simply connected strictly quarter-pinched
manifolds and [Mostow rigidity](riemannian-geometry/mostow-rigidity-theorem/README.md) for lattices in
$\mathrm{PO}(n, 1)$ with $n \ge 3$, and [curves and surfaces](curves-and-surfaces/README.md) include the
[Fáry–Milnor theorem](curves-and-surfaces/f-ry-milnor-theorem/README.md) that a knot of total curvature at most $4\pi$
is unknotted.
