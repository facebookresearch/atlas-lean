# CAT(0) spaces

A metric space $(X, d)$ is a CAT(0) space when it is nonempty, geodesic, and satisfies the four-point condition of Berg and Nikolaev: for all $x, y, z, w \in X$,

$$d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2.$$

Among geodesic metric spaces this condition characterizes CAT(0) spaces in the comparison-triangle sense.
