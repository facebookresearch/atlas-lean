# The four-point inequality from one base point

Fix a base point $o$ and write $(x \cdot y)$ for the Gromov product at $o$. If $(x \cdot y) \ge \min((x \cdot z), (y \cdot z)) - \delta$ for all $x, y, z$, then for all $x, y, z, t$

$$(t \cdot y) + (z \cdot x) \ge \min\big((t \cdot z) + (y \cdot x),\ (x \cdot t) + (y \cdot z)\big) - 2\delta.$$

Each of the two products on the left is bounded below by two instances of the $\delta$-inequality, and an elementary case analysis on the four remaining products concludes. This is Gromov's Lemma 1.1.A (stated there without proof as "obvious").

- M. Gromov, *Hyperbolic groups*, in *Essays in Group Theory* (S. M. Gersten, ed.), Math. Sci. Res. Inst. Publ. 8, Springer, 1987, 75–263, Lemma 1.1.A.
