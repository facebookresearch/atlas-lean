# Four-point inequality of a $\delta$-hyperbolic space

If a pseudometric space $X$ is $\delta$-hyperbolic, then for all $w, x, y, z \in X$,
$(x \mid z)_w \ge \min\bigl((x \mid y)_w, (y \mid z)_w\bigr) - \delta$, where $(x \mid y)_w = \tfrac12\bigl(d(w, x) + d(w, y) - d(x, y)\bigr)$ is the Gromov
product.
