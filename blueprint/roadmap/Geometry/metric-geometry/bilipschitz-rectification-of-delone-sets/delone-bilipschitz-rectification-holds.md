# Navas' problem: Delone sets bi-Lipschitz to $\mathbb{Z}^2$

This result proves the proposition stated as [Navas' problem: bi-Lipschitz rectification of Delone sets](delone-bilipschitz-rectification.md). Let $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ be a Delone set (uniformly discrete and relatively dense) admitting a bijection $f : D \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ and constants $K_1 > 0$, $K_2 \ge 0$ with $\lVert x - y \rVert \le K_1 \lVert f(x) - f(y) \rVert$ and $\lVert f(x) - f(y) \rVert \le K_2 \lVert x - y \rVert$ for all $x, y \in D$. Then there are a map $L : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ and constants $K_1' > 0$, $K_2' \ge 0$ with

$$\lVert x - y \rVert \le K_1' \lVert L(x) - L(y) \rVert \quad\text{and}\quad \lVert L(x) - L(y) \rVert \le K_2' \lVert x - y \rVert \qquad (x, y \in \mathbb{R}^2),$$

such that $L(D) = \mathbb{Z}^2$.
