# Navas' problem: bi-Lipschitz rectification of Delone sets

Navas' problem asks whether the following holds. Let $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ be a Delone set, that is, a uniformly discrete and relatively dense subset of the Euclidean plane, and suppose $D$ is bi-Lipschitz equivalent to $\mathbb{Z}^2$: there is a bijection $f : D \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ and constants $K_1 > 0$, $K_2 \ge 0$ with

$$\lVert x - y \rVert \le K_1 \lVert f(x) - f(y) \rVert \quad\text{and}\quad \lVert f(x) - f(y) \rVert \le K_2 \lVert x - y \rVert \qquad (x, y \in D).$$

Then there is a map $L : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ and constants $K_1' > 0$, $K_2' \ge 0$ with

$$\lVert x - y \rVert \le K_1' \lVert L(x) - L(y) \rVert \quad\text{and}\quad \lVert L(x) - L(y) \rVert \le K_2' \lVert x - y \rVert \qquad (x, y \in \mathbb{R}^2),$$

such that $L(D) = \mathbb{Z}^2$. Surjectivity of $L$ onto $\mathbb{R}^2$ is not required. It holds; the resolution is [Navas' problem: Delone sets bi-Lipschitz to $\mathbb{Z}^2$](delone-bilipschitz-rectification-holds.md).
