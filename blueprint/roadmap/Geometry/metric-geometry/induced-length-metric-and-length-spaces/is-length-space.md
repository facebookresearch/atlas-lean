# Length space

A pseudo-extended-metric space `X` is a length space if for all `x, y ∈ X`
the induced length distance, the infimum of the lengths (total variations)
of all paths from `x` to `y`, equals the extended distance `edist x y`. This
is the definition of length spaces in Alexander, Kapovitch and Petrunin,
*Alexandrov geometry: foundations* (AMS GSM 236, 2024).
