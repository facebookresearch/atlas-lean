# Distance coded by a function

For $g \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $d_g(s, t) = g(s) + g(t) - 2 m_g(s, t)$, where
$m_g(s, t)$ is the minimum of $g$ between $s$ and $t$. For continuous $g$ this is the
pseudometric of the real tree coded by $g$ (Le Gall, *Random trees and applications*,
Section 2.2).
