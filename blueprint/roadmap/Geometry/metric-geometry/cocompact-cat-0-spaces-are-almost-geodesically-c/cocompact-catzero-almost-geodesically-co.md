# Noncompact proper cocompact CAT(0) spaces are almost geodesically complete

Geoghegan's question asks whether cocompact CAT(0) spaces are almost geodesically complete. The proposition stated here, the form Geoghegan and Ontaneda prove, adds the hypotheses that the space is proper and not compact (a single point is proper, CAT(0) and cocompact, but carries no geodesic ray): every proper CAT(0) space $X$ that is not compact and whose isometry group acts cocompactly is almost geodesically complete. Here CAT(0) means nonempty, geodesic and satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$; cocompact means some compact $K \subseteq X$ has $\bigcup_{g \in \mathrm{Isom}(X)} g(K) = X$; and almost geodesically complete means there is $R \ge 0$ such that for all $a, b \in X$ some geodesic ray from $a$ passes within distance $R$ of $b$. It holds; the resolution is [Geoghegan–Ontaneda: almost geodesic completeness](cocompact-catzero-almost-geodesically-co-2.md).
