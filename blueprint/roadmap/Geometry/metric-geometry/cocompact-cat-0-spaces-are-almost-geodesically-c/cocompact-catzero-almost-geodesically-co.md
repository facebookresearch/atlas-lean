# Cocompact CAT(0) spaces are almost geodesically complete

Geoghegan's question asks whether cocompact CAT(0) spaces are almost geodesically complete. The statement considered: every proper CAT(0) space $X$ that is not compact and whose isometry group acts cocompactly is almost geodesically complete. Here CAT(0) means nonempty, geodesic and satisfying the four-point condition $d(x, z)^2 + d(y, w)^2 \le d(x, y)^2 + d(y, z)^2 + d(z, w)^2 + d(w, x)^2$; cocompact means some compact $K \subseteq X$ has $\bigcup_{g \in \mathrm{Isom}(X)} g(K) = X$; and almost geodesically complete means there is $R \ge 0$ such that for all $a, b \in X$ some geodesic ray from $a$ passes within distance $R$ of $b$.
