# The complex hyperbolic plane (ball model)

The complex hyperbolic plane $\mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}}$ in the ball model is the open unit ball of $\mathbb{C}^2$ with its standard Hermitian norm:

$$\mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}} = \{\, z \in \mathbb{C}^2 : \lVert z \rVert < 1 \,\}, \qquad \lVert z \rVert^2 = \lvert z_1 \rvert^2 + \lvert z_2 \rvert^2.$$

Its distance is the complex hyperbolic distance of the ball model.
