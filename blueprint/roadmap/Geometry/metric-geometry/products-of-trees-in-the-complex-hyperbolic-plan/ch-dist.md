# Distance of the complex hyperbolic plane

For points $z, w$ of the complex hyperbolic plane $\mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}} = \{z \in \mathbb{C}^2 : \lVert z \rVert < 1\}$, the distance $d(z, w)$ is defined by

$$d(z, w) = \operatorname{arcosh} \frac{\lvert 1 - \langle z, w \rangle \rvert}{\sqrt{(1 - \lVert z \rVert^2)(1 - \lVert w \rVert^2)}},$$

where $\langle z, w \rangle = \overline{z_1} w_1 + \overline{z_2} w_2$ is the standard Hermitian inner product (its modulus does not depend on which argument is conjugated). This is the Riemannian distance of the normalization with sectional curvature between $-4$ and $-1$, equivalently half the Bergman distance of holomorphic sectional curvature $-1$.
