# Complex hyperbolic plane in a product of four trees

Buyalo's question asks whether the following holds: the complex hyperbolic plane $\mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}}$ (the open unit ball of $\mathbb{C}^2$ with distance $d$ given by $\cosh d(z, w) = \lvert 1 - \langle z, w \rangle \rvert / \sqrt{(1 - \lVert z \rVert^2)(1 - \lVert w \rVert^2)}$) admits a quasi-isometric embedding into a product $T_1 \times T_2 \times T_3 \times T_4$ of four metric trees, where the product carries the maximum metric $d\bigl((t_i), (t'_i)\bigr) = \max_i d_{T_i}(t_i, t'_i)$. That is, there are metric trees $T_1, \dots, T_4$, a map $f : \mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}} \to T_1 \times \cdots \times T_4$ and constants $a \ge 1$, $b \ge 0$ such that for all $z, w$,

$$\frac{d(z, w)}{a} - b \le d\bigl(f(z), f(w)\bigr) \le a\, d(z, w) + b.$$

It holds; the resolution is [$\mathbf{H}^2_{\mathbb{C}}$ embeds quasi-isometrically in four trees](complex-hyperbolic-plane-embeds-in-four-2.md).
