# Is Local Defining Function

Let `M` be a manifold modelled on a boundaryless model. `ρ` is a local defining function of `Ω ⊆ M` at `p` on `U`: `U` is an open neighborhood of `p`
on which `ρ` is manifold-smooth, `ρ p = 0`, the manifold differential of `ρ` at `p` is nonzero,
and a point of `U` lies in `Ω` exactly when `ρ` is negative there.

The boundaryless model matters: in the closed half-plane, $\rho(x, y) = -(x + y^2)$ cuts out the complement of the origin as $\{\rho < 0\}$ and has nonzero differential at the origin, yet the relative frontier is a single point, not a hypersurface.
