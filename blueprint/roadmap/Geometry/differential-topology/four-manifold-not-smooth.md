# A closed simply connected topological 4-manifold with no smooth structure

There
exists a compact, Hausdorff, simply connected topological space $M$ that admits an atlas of
charts modelled on $\mathbb{R}^4$, such that no atlas of charts on $M$ modelled on $\mathbb{R}^4$
makes $M$ a smooth ($C^\infty$) manifold.

## Sources

- M. H. Freedman, *The topology of four-dimensional manifolds*, J. Differential Geom. 17 (1982), 357–453 (the $E_8$ manifold); V. A. Rokhlin, *New results in the theory of four-dimensional manifolds*, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 84 (1952), 221–224 (a smooth closed simply connected 4-manifold with even intersection form has signature divisible by 16). Problem `four_manifold_not_smooth` of the lean-eval benchmark.
