# Lie group

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $I$ a model with corners on a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$, and $n$ a
smoothness degree ($n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\infty$ or analytic). A Lie group of class $C^n$ modelled on $I$ is a group
$G$ that is also a $C^n$ manifold modelled on $I$ such that multiplication $G \times G \to G$ and inversion
$G \to G$ are $C^n$ maps. The classical real Lie groups are those with $\mathbb{k} = \mathbb{R}$, $I$ the model of
$\mathbb{R}^d$ without boundary and $n = \infty$.
