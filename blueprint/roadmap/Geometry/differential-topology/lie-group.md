# Lie group

Let $\mathbb{k}$ be a nontrivially normed field, $I$ a model with corners on a normed space over $\mathbb{k}$, and $n$ a
smoothness degree ($n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\infty$ or analytic). A Lie group of class $C^n$ modelled on $I$ is a group
$G$ that is also a $C^n$ manifold modelled on $I$ such that multiplication $G \times G \to G$ and inversion
$G \to G$ are $C^n$ maps. Every classical real Lie group, a Hausdorff and second countable smooth manifold with
smooth group operations, is a Lie group in this sense, with $\mathbb{k} = \mathbb{R}$, $I$ the model of $\mathbb{R}^d$
without boundary and $n = \infty$. The definition itself requires neither the Hausdorff property nor second
countability.
