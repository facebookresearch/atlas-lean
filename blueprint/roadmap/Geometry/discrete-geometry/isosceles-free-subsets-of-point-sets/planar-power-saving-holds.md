# Erdős problem 1207: planar isosceles-free power saving

The following holds: there is $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$,

$$P_2(n) < n^{1 - c},$$

where $P_d(n)$ is the least, over all sets $S \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ of exactly $n$ points, of the largest size of an isosceles-free subset $A \subseteq S$ (no three distinct points $x, y, z \in A$ with two of $\lVert x - y \rVert$, $\lVert y - z \rVert$, $\lVert z - x \rVert$ equal) in the plane ($d = 2$). Equivalently, for large $n$ some $n$-point planar set has no isosceles-free subset of size $n^{1-c}$ or more.
