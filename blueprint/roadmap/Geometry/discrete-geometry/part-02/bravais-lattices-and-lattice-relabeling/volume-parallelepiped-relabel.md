# Relabeling preserves the cell volume

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ is given by a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$; its primitive vectors $a_j$ are the columns of $B$, and the lattice vector of an integer cell index $n$ is $Bn = \sum_j n_j a_j$. For an integer matrix $P$ with $\det P = \pm 1$, the relabeled lattice has basis matrix $BP$. The primitive cells of the relabeled and original lattices have the same volume.
