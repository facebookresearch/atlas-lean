# Coordinate-free crystal of a motif

For a finite nonempty motif $F$, the coordinate-free crystal has translation group $\mathbb{Z}^3$
and unit cell $F$: its sites are the pairs $(n, f)$ of a cell index $n \in \mathbb{Z}^3$ and a motif
label $f \in F$, and a translation $m \in \mathbb{Z}^3$ acts on the cell only, sending $(n, f)$ to
$(n + m, f)$ ([translation](vadd-mk.md)). It carries no coordinates and depends only on $F$; a
[crystal with motif](bravais-crystal.md) $F$ places its sites at [positions](site-position.md).
