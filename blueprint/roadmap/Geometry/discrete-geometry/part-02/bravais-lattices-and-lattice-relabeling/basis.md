# Primitive basis of a Bravais lattice

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ is given by a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$;
its primitive vectors $a_j$ are the columns of $B$, and the lattice vector of an integer cell index
$n$ is $Bn = \sum_j n_j a_j$. Its primitive basis is the basis $a_0, a_1, a_2$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$
formed by the primitive vectors, which are
[linearly independent](linear-independent-primitive-vector.md) and three in number.
