# Each atomic position has a unique cell and label

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. Assume the offsets are pairwise distinct modulo the lattice: $u_f - u_g$ lies in the lattice only if $f = g$. Then every atomic position equals $Bn + u_f$ for exactly one pair $(n, f)$.
