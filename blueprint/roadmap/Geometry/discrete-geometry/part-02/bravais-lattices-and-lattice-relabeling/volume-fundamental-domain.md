# Volume of the fundamental cell

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ is given by a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$; its primitive vectors $a_j$ are the columns of $B$, and the lattice vector of an integer cell index $n$ is $Bn = \sum_j n_j a_j$. The half-open fundamental cell, the points whose primitive coordinates lie in $[0, 1)$, has volume $|\det B|$.
