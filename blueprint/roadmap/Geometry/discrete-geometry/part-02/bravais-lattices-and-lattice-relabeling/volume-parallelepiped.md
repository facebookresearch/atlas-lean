# Volume of a parallelepiped

For vectors $v_0, v_1, v_2 \in \mathbb{R}^3$, the basis matrix $[v]$ has $j$-th column the Cartesian
coordinates of $v_j$. The parallelepiped $\{t_0 v_0 + t_1 v_1 + t_2 v_2 : 0 \le t_i \le 1\}$ spanned
by $v$ has Lebesgue volume $|\det [v]|$. For linearly dependent vectors both sides are $0$.
