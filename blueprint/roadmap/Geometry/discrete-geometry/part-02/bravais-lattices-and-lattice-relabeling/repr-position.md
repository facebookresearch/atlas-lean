# Coordinates of an atomic position

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. The primitive-basis coordinates of the atom $(n, f)$ are $n$ plus the coordinates of $u_f$.
