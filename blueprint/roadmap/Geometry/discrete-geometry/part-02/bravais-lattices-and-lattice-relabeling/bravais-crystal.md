# Crystal with a motif

A three-dimensional crystal is a Bravais lattice (basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$) together
with a finite nonempty motif $F$, each atom $f$ of which sits at an offset $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$
from the origin of its cell, so that its copy in the cell with index $n$ is at $Bn + u_f$
([position](position.md)). The offsets need not be distinct modulo the lattice; results that need
this [say so](position-injective-iff.md).
