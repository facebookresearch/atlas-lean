# Positions determine atoms exactly when offsets are distinct modulo the lattice

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. The map $(n, f) \mapsto Bn + u_f$ is injective if and only if $u_f - u_g$ lies in the lattice only when $f = g$.
