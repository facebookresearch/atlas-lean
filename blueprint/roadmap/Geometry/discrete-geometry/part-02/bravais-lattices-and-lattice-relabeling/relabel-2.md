# Relabeling the cells of a crystal

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. For a unimodular integer matrix $P$, the relabeled crystal has basis matrix $BP$ and the same offsets.
