# Sites correspond to atomic positions

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. Assume the offsets are pairwise distinct modulo the lattice: $u_f - u_g$ lies in the lattice only if $f = g$. The associated coordinate-free crystal has translation group $\mathbb{Z}^3$ and sites $(n, f)$; the site $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$, and a translation $m$ sends the site $(n, f)$ to $(n + m, f)$. Then sending a site to its position is a bijection from the sites onto the atomic positions.
