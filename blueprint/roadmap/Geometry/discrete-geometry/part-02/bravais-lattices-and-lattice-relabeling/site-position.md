# Position of a crystal site

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. The position of the site with cell $n$ and label $f$ is $Bn + u_f$.
