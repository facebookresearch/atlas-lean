# The site correspondence sends a site to its position

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. Assume the offsets are pairwise distinct modulo the lattice: $u_f - u_g$ lies in the lattice only if $f = g$. The associated coordinate-free crystal has translation group $\mathbb{Z}^3$ and sites $(n, f)$; the site $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$, and a translation $m$ sends the site $(n, f)$ to $(n + m, f)$. The bijection from sites to atomic positions sends each site to its position.
