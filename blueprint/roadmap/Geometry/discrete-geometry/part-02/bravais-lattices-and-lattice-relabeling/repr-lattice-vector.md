# Coordinates of a lattice vector

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ is given by a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$; its primitive vectors $a_j$ are the columns of $B$, and the lattice vector of an integer cell index $n$ is $Bn = \sum_j n_j a_j$. The coordinates of the lattice vector $Bn$ in the primitive basis are the entries of $n$.
