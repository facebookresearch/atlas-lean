# Lattice translations move atoms between cells

A crystal with a finite nonempty motif $F$ is a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ together with offsets $u_f \in \mathbb{R}^3$ for $f \in F$; the atom $(n, f)$ sits at $Bn + u_f$ for integer cell indices $n$. For all $m, n$ and $f$, $Bm + (Bn + u_f) = B(n + m) + u_f$.
