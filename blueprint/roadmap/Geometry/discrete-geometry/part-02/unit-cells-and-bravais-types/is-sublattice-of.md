# Sublattice of a Bravais lattice

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive
vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. $\Lambda_c$ is a
sublattice of $\Lambda_f$ when $L(\Lambda_c) \subseteq L(\Lambda_f)$; its cell is then called the
conventional cell. For such a pair, $|\det B_c| = N\,|\det B_f|$, with $N$ the number of
[points per cell](points-per-cell.md) ([volume ratio](points-per-cell-eq-volume-ratio.md)), where
$|\det B|$ is the
[volume](../bravais-lattices-and-lattice-relabeling/volume-parallelepiped-primitive-vector.md) of
the cell spanned by the primitive vectors.
