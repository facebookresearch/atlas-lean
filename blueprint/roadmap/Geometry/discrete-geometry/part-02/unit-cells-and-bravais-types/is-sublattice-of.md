# Sublattice of a Bravais lattice

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. $\Lambda_c$ is a sublattice of $\Lambda_f$ when $L(\Lambda_c) \subseteq L(\Lambda_f)$; its cell is then the larger, conventional cell.
