# Monoclinic lattices of types mP and mC are different

For $a, b, c > 0$ and an angle $\beta$ with $\sin\beta \ne 0$, the monoclinic lattice mP has primitive vectors $(a, 0, 0)$, $(0, b, 0)$, $(c\cos\beta, 0, c\sin\beta)$, and mC has primitive vectors $(\tfrac a2, -\tfrac b2, 0)$, $(\tfrac a2, \tfrac b2, 0)$, $(c\cos\beta, 0, c\sin\beta)$. Then the mP and mC lattices with these parameters have different sets of lattice points.
