# The integer indexing sends an index to its lattice vector

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. The isomorphism $\mathbb{Z}^3 \to L(\Lambda)$ sends $n$ to $Bn$.
