# Tetragonal lattices of types tP and tI are different

For $a, c > 0$, the primitive tetragonal lattice tP has basis matrix $\operatorname{diag}(a, a, c)$ and the body-centered tetragonal lattice tI has primitive vectors $(-\tfrac a2, \tfrac a2, \tfrac c2)$, $(\tfrac a2, -\tfrac a2, \tfrac c2)$, $(\tfrac a2, \tfrac a2, -\tfrac c2)$. Then the tP and tI lattices with these parameters have different sets of lattice points.
