# Lattice vectors of integer cell indices

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with nonsingular basis matrix $B$, whose columns are the primitive
vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, and let $L = \{B n : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ be its set of lattice vectors.
The map $\mathbb{Z}^3 \to L$, $n \mapsto B n = \sum_j n_j a_j$, sends an integer cell index to its lattice
vector in $\Lambda$; it is an additive group homomorphism.
