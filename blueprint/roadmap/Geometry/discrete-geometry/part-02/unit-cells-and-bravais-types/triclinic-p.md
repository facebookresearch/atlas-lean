# Triclinic lattice

For $a, b, c > 0$ and angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ with $\sin\gamma \ne 0$ and $\Omega = 1 - \cos^2\alpha - \cos^2\beta - \cos^2\gamma + 2\cos\alpha\cos\beta\cos\gamma > 0$, the triclinic lattice (aP) has basis matrix with rows $(a,\ b\cos\gamma,\ c\cos\beta)$, $(0,\ b\sin\gamma,\ c(\cos\alpha - \cos\beta\cos\gamma)/\sin\gamma)$ and $(0,\ 0,\ c\sqrt{\Omega}/\sin\gamma)$.
