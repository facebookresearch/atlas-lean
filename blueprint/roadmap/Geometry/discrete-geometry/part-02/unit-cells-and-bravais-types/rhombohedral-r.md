# Rhombohedral lattice

For $a > 0$ and an angle $\alpha$ with $\sin\alpha \ne 0$ and $\Omega = 1 - 3\cos^2\alpha + 2\cos^3\alpha > 0$, the rhombohedral lattice (hR) has basis matrix with rows $(a,\ a\cos\alpha,\ a\cos\alpha)$, $(0,\ a\sin\alpha,\ a(\cos\alpha - \cos^2\alpha)/\sin\alpha)$ and $(0,\ 0,\ a\sqrt{\Omega}/\sin\alpha)$.
