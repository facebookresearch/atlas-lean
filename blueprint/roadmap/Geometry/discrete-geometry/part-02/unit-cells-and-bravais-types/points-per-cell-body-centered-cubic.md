# Body-centered cubic has two points per cubic cell

For $a > 0$, let $sc$, $fcc$ and $bcc$ be the simple cubic lattice (primitive vectors $a e_j$), the face-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(0,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,0,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,0)$) and the body-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(-1,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,-1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,-1)$). For Bravais lattices $\Lambda_c, \Lambda_f$, the number of points per cell is the index of $L(\Lambda_c) \cap L(\Lambda_f)$ in $L(\Lambda_f)$, the cardinality of the quotient group (not the number of lattice points in a closed cell). The number of points of $bcc$ per cell of $sc$ is $2$, and $|\det B_{sc}| = 2\,|\det B_{bcc}|$, so $|\det B_{sc}|/|\det B_{bcc}| = 2$.
