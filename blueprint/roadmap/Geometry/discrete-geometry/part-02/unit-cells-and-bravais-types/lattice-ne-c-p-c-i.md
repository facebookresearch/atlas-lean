# Cubic lattices of types simple cubic and body-centered cubic are different

For $a > 0$, let $sc$, $fcc$ and $bcc$ be the simple cubic lattice (primitive vectors $a e_j$), the face-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(0,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,0,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,0)$) and the body-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(-1,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,-1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,-1)$). Then the simple cubic and body-centered cubic lattices with these parameters have different sets of lattice points.
