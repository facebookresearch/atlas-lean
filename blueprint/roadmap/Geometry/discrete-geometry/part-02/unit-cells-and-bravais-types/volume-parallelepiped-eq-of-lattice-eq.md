# The primitive cell volume depends only on the lattice

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. If $L(\Lambda) = L(\Lambda')$, the parallelepipeds spanned by their primitive vectors have the same volume.
