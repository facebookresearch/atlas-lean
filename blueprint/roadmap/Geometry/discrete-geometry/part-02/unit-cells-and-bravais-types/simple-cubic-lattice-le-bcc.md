# Simple cubic is a sublattice of body-centered cubic

For $a > 0$, let $sc$, $fcc$ and $bcc$ be the simple cubic lattice (primitive vectors $a e_j$), the face-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(0,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,0,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,0)$) and the body-centered cubic lattice (primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(-1,1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,-1,1)$, $\tfrac a2(1,1,-1)$). Then $L(sc) \subseteq L(bcc)$.
