# Conventional cell

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. The closed conventional cell of $\Lambda_c$ is the parallelepiped spanned by its primitive vectors, the points with all primitive coordinates in $[0, 1]$.
