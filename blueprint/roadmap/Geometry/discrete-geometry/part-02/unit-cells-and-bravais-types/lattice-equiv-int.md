# Integer indexing of lattice vectors

A Bravais lattice $\Lambda$ has a real $3 \times 3$ basis matrix $B$ with $\det B \ne 0$, primitive vectors the columns of $B$, and lattice $L(\Lambda) = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. The map $n \mapsto Bn$ is an additive group isomorphism from $\mathbb{Z}^3$ onto $L(\Lambda)$.
