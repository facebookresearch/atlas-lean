# Four Point Comparison

In a metric space, points $p, x_1, x_2, x_3$ satisfy the four-point comparison for curvature $\kappa$ when, if the three comparison angles at $p$ of the pairs $(x_1, x_2)$, $(x_2, x_3)$ and $(x_3, x_1)$ are all [admissible](model-angle-defined.md) (their sides are admissible for comparison and all distances from $p$ are positive), their sum is at most $2\pi$. Admissibility here is the strict perimeter cutoff for $\kappa > 0$, not mere existence of model triangles.
