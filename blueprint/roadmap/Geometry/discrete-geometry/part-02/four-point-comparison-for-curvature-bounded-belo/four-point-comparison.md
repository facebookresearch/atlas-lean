# Four Point Comparison

In a metric space, points `p, x₁, x₂, x₃` satisfy the four-point comparison for curvature `κ` if, whenever the three comparison angles at `p` of the pairs `(x₁, x₂)`, `(x₂, x₃)` and `(x₃, x₁)` are defined (model triangles exist and all distances from `p` are positive), their sum is at most `2π`.
