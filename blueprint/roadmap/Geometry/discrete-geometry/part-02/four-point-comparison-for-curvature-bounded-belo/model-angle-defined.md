# Admissible comparison angles

In a metric space, for a curvature $\kappa$ and points $p, x, y$, the comparison angle at $p$ is admissible when the sides $d(x, y)$, $d(p, x)$, $d(p, y)$ are [admissible for comparison](model-triangle-defined.md) (weak triangle inequalities and, for $\kappa > 0$, perimeter strictly below $2\pi/\sqrt{\kappa}$) and both adjacent distances are positive, $d(p, x) > 0$ and $d(p, y) > 0$.
