# Admissibility is symmetric in the two endpoints

In a metric space, for a curvature $\kappa$ and points $p, x, y$, the comparison angle at the vertex $p$ is [admissible](model-angle-defined.md) for the pair $(x, y)$ if and only if it is admissible for $(y, x)$. Only the two endpoints are swapped; the vertex $p$ is fixed and cannot be interchanged with an endpoint (on the real line at $\kappa = 0$, $(p, x, y) = (0, 1, 1)$ is admissible at $0$, but swapping $p$ and $x$ gives a zero adjacent distance).
