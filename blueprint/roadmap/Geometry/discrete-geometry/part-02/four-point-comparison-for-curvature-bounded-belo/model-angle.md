# Model angle

For a curvature $\kappa$ and reals $a, b, c$, the model angle opposite $a$ with adjacent sides $b, c$ is $\arccos\big((b^2 + c^2 - a^2)/(2bc)\big)$ for $\kappa = 0$ and $\arccos\big((\operatorname{cs}_\kappa a - \operatorname{cs}_\kappa b \operatorname{cs}_\kappa c)/(\kappa \operatorname{sn}_\kappa b \operatorname{sn}_\kappa c)\big)$ for $\kappa \ne 0$, with $\operatorname{cs}_\kappa, \operatorname{sn}_\kappa$ the model cosine and sine. The cosine-law formula returns a real number for every input: division by zero gives $0$ (so when an adjacent side is $0$ the argument of $\arccos$ is $0$ and the angle is $\pi/2$), and the real $\arccos$ returns $\pi$ below $-1$ and $0$ above $1$. It is the angle of a model triangle when the sides are [admissible for comparison](model-triangle-defined.md) and $b, c > 0$.
