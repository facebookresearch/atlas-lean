# Volume of the first Brillouin zone

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ ($\det B \ne 0$), primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$ (the columns of $B$) and lattice $L = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. Its first Brillouin zone, the Wigner–Seitz cell of the reciprocal lattice with basis matrix $2\pi(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$, has volume $(2\pi)^3/|\det B|$.
