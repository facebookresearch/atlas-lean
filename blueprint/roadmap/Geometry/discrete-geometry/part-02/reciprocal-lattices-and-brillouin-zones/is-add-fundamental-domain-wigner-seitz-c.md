# The Wigner–Seitz cell is a fundamental domain

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ ($\det B \ne 0$), primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$ (the columns of $B$) and lattice $L = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. The Wigner–Seitz cell of $\Lambda$ is $\{x : \lVert x \rVert \le \lVert x - R\rVert \text{ for all } R \in L\}$. Its translates by the vectors of $L$ cover $\mathbb{R}^3$, and distinct translates overlap only in sets of Lebesgue measure zero.
