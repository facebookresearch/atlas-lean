# First Brillouin zone

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ ($\det B \ne 0$), primitive vectors
$a_0, a_1, a_2$ (the columns of $B$) and lattice $L = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. Its first
Brillouin zone is the [Wigner–Seitz cell](wigner-seitz-cell.md) of its
[reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md): the wave vectors at least as close to the origin as to any
reciprocal lattice vector, boundary included. The reciprocal lattice has basis matrix
$2\pi(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ ([inverse transpose](reciprocal-basis-matrix-eq-inv-transpose.md)).
