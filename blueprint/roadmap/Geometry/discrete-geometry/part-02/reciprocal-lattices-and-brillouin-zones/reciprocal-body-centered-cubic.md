# The reciprocal of body-centered cubic is face-centered cubic

For $a > 0$, the simple cubic lattice has primitive vectors $a e_0, a e_1, a e_2$; the face-centered cubic lattice has primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(0, 1, 1)$, $\tfrac a2(1, 0, 1)$, $\tfrac a2(1, 1, 0)$; the body-centered cubic lattice has primitive vectors $\tfrac a2(-1, 1, 1)$, $\tfrac a2(1, -1, 1)$, $\tfrac a2(1, 1, -1)$. The reciprocal of the body-centered cubic lattice of side $a$ is the face-centered cubic lattice of side $4\pi/a$, with the listed primitive vectors.
