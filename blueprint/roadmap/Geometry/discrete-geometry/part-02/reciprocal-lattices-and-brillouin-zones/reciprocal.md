# Reciprocal lattice

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ ($\det B \ne 0$), primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$ (the columns of $B$) and lattice $L = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. Its reciprocal lattice, in the physics convention $a_i \cdot b_j = 2\pi\delta_{ij}$, has primitive vectors $b_0 = \tfrac{2\pi}{V}(a_1 \times a_2)$, $b_1 = \tfrac{2\pi}{V}(a_2 \times a_0)$, $b_2 = \tfrac{2\pi}{V}(a_0 \times a_1)$ with $V = \det B$.
