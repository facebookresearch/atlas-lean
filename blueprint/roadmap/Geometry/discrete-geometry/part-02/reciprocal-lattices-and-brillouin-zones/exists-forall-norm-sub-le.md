# Every point has a nearest lattice point

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice with basis matrix $B$ ($\det B \ne 0$), primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$ (the columns of $B$) and lattice $L = \{Bn : n \in \mathbb{Z}^3\}$. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$ there is $R_0 \in L$ with $\lVert x - R_0\rVert \le \lVert x - R\rVert$ for all $R \in L$.
