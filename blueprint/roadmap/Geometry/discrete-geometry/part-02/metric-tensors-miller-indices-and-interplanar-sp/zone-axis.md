# Zone axis of two plane families

For integer index triples $h, h'$, the zone axis is their cross product $h \times h'$, an integer
triple. When it is nonzero, in every Bravais lattice the lattice direction $[h \times h']$ is
nonzero, lies in both plane families, and every lattice direction lying in both is a real multiple
of it ([non-parallel families](zone-axis-unique.md)); for parallel index triples, such as $h = h'$,
it is $0$, which is not a direction.
