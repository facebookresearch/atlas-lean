# Crystallographic reciprocal basis

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its crystallographic reciprocal basis vector $b_j$ is the $j$-th column of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$, with no factor $2\pi$.
