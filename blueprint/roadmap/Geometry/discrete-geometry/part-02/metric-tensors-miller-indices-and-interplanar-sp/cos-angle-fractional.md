# Angles from the metric tensor

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its metric tensor is $G_{ij} = a_i \cdot a_j$. For fractional coordinates $v, w$, the angle $\theta$ between $\sum_i v_i a_i$ and $\sum_j w_j a_j$ satisfies

$$\cos\theta = \frac{\sum_{i,j} v_i G_{ij} w_j}{\sqrt{\sum_{i,j} v_i G_{ij} v_j}\,\sqrt{\sum_{i,j} w_i G_{ij} w_j}},$$

where the angle with a zero vector is $\pi/2$ and both sides are then $0$.
