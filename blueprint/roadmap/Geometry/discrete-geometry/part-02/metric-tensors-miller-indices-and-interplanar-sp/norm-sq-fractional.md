# Lengths from the metric tensor

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its metric tensor is $G_{ij} = a_i \cdot a_j$. For fractional coordinates $v \in \mathbb{R}^3$, $\bigl\lVert \sum_i v_i a_i \bigr\rVert^2 = \sum_{i,j} v_i G_{ij} v_j$.
