# Pairing a lattice direction with a primitive vector

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its metric tensor is $G_{ij} = a_i \cdot a_j$. The lattice direction $[u]$ for integer $u$ is $\sum_j u_j a_j$. Then $[u] \cdot a_i = \sum_j u_j G_{ji}$.
