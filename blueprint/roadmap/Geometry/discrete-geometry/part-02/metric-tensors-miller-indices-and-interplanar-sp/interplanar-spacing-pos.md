# Interplanar spacings are positive

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its crystallographic reciprocal basis $b_0, b_1, b_2$ consists of the columns of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ (no factor $2\pi$). For integer indices $h = (h_0, h_1, h_2)$, the reciprocal vector is $G_h = \sum_j h_j b_j$. The interplanar spacing of the family $h$ is $d_h = 1/\lVert G_h \rVert$. If $h \ne 0$, then $d_h > 0$.
