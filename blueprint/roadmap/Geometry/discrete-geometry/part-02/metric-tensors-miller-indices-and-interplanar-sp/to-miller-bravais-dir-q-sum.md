# Converted directions satisfy the hexagonal constraint

For rational direction indices $u = (u_0, u_1, u_2)$, the Miller–Bravais conversion is $(U, V, T, W) = \bigl((2u_0 - u_1)/3,\ (2u_1 - u_0)/3,\ -(u_0 + u_1)/3,\ u_2\bigr)$, with inverse $(U, V, T, W) \mapsto (U - T,\ V - T,\ W)$. For every $u$, the converted indices satisfy $U + V + T = 0$.
