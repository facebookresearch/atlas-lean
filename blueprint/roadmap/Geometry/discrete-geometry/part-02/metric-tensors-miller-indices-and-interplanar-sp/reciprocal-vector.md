# Reciprocal-lattice vector of Miller indices

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its crystallographic reciprocal basis $b_0, b_1, b_2$ consists of the columns of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ (no factor $2\pi$). For integer Miller indices $h$, the reciprocal vector is $G_h = \sum_j h_j b_j$.
