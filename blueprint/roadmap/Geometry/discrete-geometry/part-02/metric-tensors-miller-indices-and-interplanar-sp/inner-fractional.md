# Inner products from the metric tensor

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its metric tensor is $G_{ij} = a_i \cdot a_j$. For fractional coordinates $v, w$, $\bigl(\sum_i v_i a_i\bigr) \cdot \bigl(\sum_j w_j a_j\bigr) = \sum_{i,j} v_i G_{ij} w_j$.
