# If U + V + T = 0, converting to three indices and back is the identity

For rational direction indices $u = (u_0, u_1, u_2)$, the Miller–Bravais conversion is
$(U, V, T, W) = \bigl((2u_0 - u_1)/3,\ (2u_1 - u_0)/3,\ -(u_0 + u_1)/3,\ u_2\bigr)$, with inverse
$(U, V, T, W) \mapsto (U - T,\ V - T,\ W)$. If $U + V + T = 0$, converting $(U, V, T, W)$ to three
indices and back returns $(U, V, T, W)$.
