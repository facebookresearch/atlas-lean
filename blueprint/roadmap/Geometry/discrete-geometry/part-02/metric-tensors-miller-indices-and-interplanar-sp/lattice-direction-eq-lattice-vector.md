# A lattice direction is a lattice vector

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. The lattice direction $[u]$ for integer $u$ is $\sum_j u_j a_j$. It equals the lattice vector of $\Lambda$ with cell index $u$.
