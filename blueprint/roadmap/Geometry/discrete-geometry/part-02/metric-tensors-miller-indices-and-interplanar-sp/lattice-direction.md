# Lattice direction of an index triple

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. The lattice direction $[u]$ for integer indices $u$ is the lattice vector $\sum_j u_j a_j$ (the zero vector for $u = 0$).
