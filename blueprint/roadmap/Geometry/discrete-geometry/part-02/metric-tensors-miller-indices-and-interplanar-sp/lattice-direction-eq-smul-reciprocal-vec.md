# In a cubic lattice a direction is normal to the planes with the same indices

Let $\Lambda$ be the simple cubic lattice of side $a > 0$ (primitive vectors $a e_0, a e_1, a e_2$). Its crystallographic reciprocal basis $b_0, b_1, b_2$ consists of the columns of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ (no factor $2\pi$). For integer indices $h = (h_0, h_1, h_2)$, the reciprocal vector is $G_h = \sum_j h_j b_j$. The lattice direction $[u]$ for integer $u$ is $\sum_j u_j a_j$. Then $[h] = a^2 G_h$.
