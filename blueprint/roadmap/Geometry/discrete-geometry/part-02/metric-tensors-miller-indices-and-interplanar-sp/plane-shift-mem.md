# Shifting to the next lattice plane

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its crystallographic reciprocal basis $b_0, b_1, b_2$ consists of the columns of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ (no factor $2\pi$). For integer indices $h = (h_0, h_1, h_2)$, the reciprocal vector is $G_h = \sum_j h_j b_j$. The $n$-th plane of the family $h$ is $\{x : G_h \cdot x = n\}$. If $G_h \ne 0$ and $x$ lies in the $n$-th plane, then $x + G_h/\lVert G_h\rVert^2$ lies in the $(n+1)$-st plane.
