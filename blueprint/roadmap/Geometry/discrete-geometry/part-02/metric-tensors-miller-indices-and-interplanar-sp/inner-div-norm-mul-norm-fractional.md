# Normalized inner product from the metric tensor

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its metric tensor is $G_{ij} = a_i \cdot a_j$. If $x = \sum_i v_i a_i$ and $y = \sum_j w_j a_j$, then $\dfrac{x \cdot y}{\lVert x\rVert\lVert y\rVert} = \dfrac{\sum_{i,j} v_i G_{ij} w_j}{\lVert x\rVert\lVert y\rVert}$ (both sides $0$ when a vector is zero).
