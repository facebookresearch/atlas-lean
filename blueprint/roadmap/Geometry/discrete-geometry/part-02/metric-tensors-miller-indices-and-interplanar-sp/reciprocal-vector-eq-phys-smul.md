# Crystallographic and physical reciprocal vectors

Let $\Lambda$ be a Bravais lattice in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with primitive vectors $a_0, a_1, a_2$, the columns of its invertible basis matrix $B$. Its crystallographic reciprocal basis $b_0, b_1, b_2$ consists of the columns of $(B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$ (no factor $2\pi$). For integer indices $h = (h_0, h_1, h_2)$, the reciprocal vector is $G_h = \sum_j h_j b_j$. The physics-convention reciprocal lattice of $\Lambda$, with basis matrix $2\pi (B^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$, has lattice vector $2\pi\, G_h$ at the index $h$.
