# Crystallization for the Lennard-Jones potential in the plane

Prove crystalline optimality for an explicit realistic potential, rather than assuming a lattice and optimizing only within lattices.

## What would settle it

One precise form: for the two-dimensional Lennard-Jones potential, prove that the thermodynamic minimum energy per particle over all configurations equals the energy of the optimally scaled triangular lattice. A full structural crystallization theorem is stronger; equality of bulk energies alone does not classify defects or local limits.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Blanc and Lewin (review, §2.3) describe the crystallization problem in dimension two and higher as far from understood, with proofs available for potentials close to hard-sphere interactions; their Figure 1 shows a numerically computed planar Lennard-Jones minimizer arranged on a hexagonal lattice.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
