# Erdős problem 232: unit-distance-avoiding sets have density at most $1/4$

Conjecture (Erdős, Moser): every Lebesgue-measurable set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ with no two points at distance exactly $1$ has upper density at most $1/4$:

$$\limsup_{R \to \infty} \frac{\lambda(A \cap B_R)}{\lambda(B_R)} \le \frac{1}{4},$$

where $\lambda$ is Lebesgue measure and $B_R$ is the open disc of radius $R$ about the origin. It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 232: the density bound $1/4$](unit-distance-avoiding-density-at-most-q-2.md).
