# Convex fair partitions of convex polygons

The question of Nandakumar and Ramana Rao asks whether the following holds: for every integer $n \ge 1$ and every convex polygon $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ (the convex hull of finitely many points, with nonempty interior) there are closed convex sets $Q_1, \dots, Q_n$ with nonempty interiors and pairwise disjoint interiors, with $Q_1 \cup \cdots \cup Q_n = P$, all of the same area (Lebesgue measure) and all of the same perimeter (one-dimensional Hausdorff measure of the boundary). It holds; the resolution is [Every convex polygon has convex fair partitions](convex-fair-partition-exists-holds.md).
