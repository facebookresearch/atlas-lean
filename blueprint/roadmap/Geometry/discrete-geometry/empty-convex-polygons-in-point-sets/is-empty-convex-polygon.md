# Empty convex $k$-gons of a planar point set

Let $S, T \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be finite sets and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $T$ is an empty convex $k$-gon of $S$ if

- $T \subseteq S$ and $T$ has exactly $k$ points;
- $T$ is in convex position: no $p \in T$ lies in the convex hull of $T \setminus \{p\}$; and
- no point of $S$ lies in the interior of the convex hull of $T$.
