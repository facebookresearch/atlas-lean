# Erdős problem 216: empty convex $k$-gons for some $k \ge 7$

Erdős Problem 216 for $k \ge 7$, in the form of a positive claim: there is some $k \ge 7$ for which there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that every finite set $S \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with at least $N$ points and no three distinct points collinear contains an empty convex $k$-gon: a subset $T \subseteq S$ of exactly $k$ points, none of which lies in the convex hull of the other points of $T$, such that no point of $S$ lies in the interior of the convex hull of $T$. It is false; the refutation is [Erdős problem 216 fails for $k \ge 7$](empty-convex-polygon-number-exists-from-2.md).
