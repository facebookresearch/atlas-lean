# Existence of the empty convex $k$-gon number $g(k)$

For $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the number $g(k)$ exists if there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that every finite set $S \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with at least $N$ points and no three distinct points collinear contains an empty convex $k$-gon: a subset $T \subseteq S$ of exactly $k$ points, none of which lies in the convex hull of the other points of $T$, such that no point of $S$ lies in the interior of the convex hull of $T$.
