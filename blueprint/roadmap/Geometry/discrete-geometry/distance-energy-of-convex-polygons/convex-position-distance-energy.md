# Statement of Erdős problem 94: distance energy of convex polygons

Erdős Problem 94 asks for the following statement. There is a constant $C$ such that for every finite set $A \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ of $n$ points in convex position (no point of $A$ lies in the convex hull of the others),

$$\sum_{u \in D(A)} f_A(u)^2 \le C\, n^3,$$

where $D(A) = \{\, \lVert x - y \rVert : x, y \in A,\ x \ne y \,\}$ is the set of distances determined by $A$ and $f_A(u)$ is the number of unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of distinct points of $A$ with $\lVert x - y \rVert = u$. The constant $C$ is a single real number, independent of $A$. It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 94: distance energy of convex polygons](convex-position-distance-energy-holds.md).
