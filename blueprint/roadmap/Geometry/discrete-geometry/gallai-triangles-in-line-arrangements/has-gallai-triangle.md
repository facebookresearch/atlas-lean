# Gallai triangles in line arrangements

A finite family $\mathcal{A}$ of affine subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^2$ has a Gallai triangle if there are three distinct members $L_1, L_2, L_3 \in \mathcal{A}$ and three distinct points $p_1, p_2, p_3 \in \mathbb{R}^2$ with $p_1 \in L_2 \cap L_3$, $p_2 \in L_1 \cap L_3$, $p_3 \in L_1 \cap L_2$, such that each of $p_1, p_2, p_3$ lies on exactly two members of $\mathcal{A}$.
