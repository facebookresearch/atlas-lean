# The inverse Ackermann function at 2

The inverse Ackermann function $\alpha$ satisfies
$\alpha(2) = 1$, since $A(0, 0) = 1 < 2 \le 3 = A(1, 1)$, where $A$ is the two-argument
Ackermann function.
