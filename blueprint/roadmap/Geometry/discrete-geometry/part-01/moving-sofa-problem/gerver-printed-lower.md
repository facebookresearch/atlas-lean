# Gerver's lower bound for the moving sofa constant

The moving sofa constant, the
supremum of the areas of connected Lebesgue-measurable planar shapes that a continuous rigid motion
can carry through the right-angled corridor of width $1$, is at least $2.2195$.

## Sources

- Joseph L. Gerver, On moving a sofa around a corner, Geometriae Dedicata 42 (1992), 267–283, DOI 10.1007/BF02414066 (Gerver's sofa has area $2.21953\ldots$). The bound $2.2195$ is the value printed in the optimization constants repository, constant 41a, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/41a.md.
