# Hammersley upper bound for the ambidextrous sofa

The supremum of the areas of
ambidextrous sofas, taken in $[0, \infty]$, is at most $2\sqrt{2}$.

## Sources

- John M. Hammersley, On the enfeeblement of mathematical skills by "Modern Mathematics" and by similar soft intellectual trash in schools and universities, Bull. Inst. Math. Appl. 4 (1968), 66–85; every ambidextrous sofa is a sofa, so his bound for sofas applies. Listed in the optimization constants repository, constant 41b, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/41b.md.
