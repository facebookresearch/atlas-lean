# Romik lower bound for the ambidextrous sofa

The supremum of the areas of
ambidextrous sofas, taken in $[0, \infty]$, is at least
$164495521/100000000 = 1.64495521$.

## Sources

- Dan Romik, Differential equations and exact solutions in the moving sofa problem, Experimental Mathematics 27 (2018), 316–330, arXiv:1606.08111 (Romik's ambidextrous sofa, of area $1.64495521\ldots$). Listed, with the decimal endpoint $164495521/100000000$, in the optimization constants repository, constant 41b, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/41b.md.
