# Gibbs lower bound for the ambidextrous sofa

The supremum of the areas of
ambidextrous sofas, taken in $[0, \infty]$, is at least $164495/100000 = 1.64495$.

## Sources

- Philip Gibbs, A computational study of sofas and cars (2014), numerical construction. Listed, with the decimal endpoint $164495/100000$, in the optimization constants repository, constant 41b, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/41b.md.
