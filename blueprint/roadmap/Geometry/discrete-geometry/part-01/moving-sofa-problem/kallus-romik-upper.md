# Kallus–Romik upper bound for the moving sofa constant

The moving sofa constant, the
supremum of the areas of connected Lebesgue-measurable planar shapes that a continuous rigid motion
can carry through the right-angled corridor of width $1$, is at most $2.37$.

## Sources

- Yoav Kallus and Dan Romik, Improved upper bounds in the moving sofa problem, Advances in Mathematics 340 (2018), 960–982, arXiv:1706.06630. Listed in the optimization constants repository, constant 41a, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/41a.md.
