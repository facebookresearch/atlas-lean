# Chung–Graham lower bound for the Steiner ratio

The Steiner ratio constant, the infimum
over finite sets $V$ of at least two points in the Euclidean plane of the length of a Steiner
minimal tree for $V$ divided by the length of a minimum spanning tree of $V$, is at least $0.824$.

## Sources

- Fan R. K. Chung and Ronald L. Graham, A new bound for Euclidean Steiner minimal trees, Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences 440 (1985), 328–346, DOI 10.1111/j.1749-6632.1985.tb14564.x: the Steiner ratio is at least $0.824$. Listed as the best unconditional published lower bound in the optimization constants repository, constant 43a, https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/43a.md.
