# An acute triangulation of the cube exists

The following holds: for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there are tetrahedra $T_1, \dots, T_n$ with vertices in $[0, 1]^3$ that triangulate the unit cube $[0, 1]^3$ (non-degenerate, covering the cube, with pairwise disjoint interiors and meeting pairwise in common, possibly empty, faces) and such that every dihedral angle of every $T_i$ is strictly less than $\pi/2$.

The constructions of Kopczyński, Pak and Przytycki, and of VanderZee, Hirani, Guoy and Ramos, give such triangulations.

## Sources

- E. Kopczyński, I. Pak and P. Przytycki, "Acute triangulations of polyhedra and $\mathbb{R}^n$", Combinatorica 32 (2012), arXiv:0909.3706, https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.3706.
- E. VanderZee, A. N. Hirani, D. Guoy and E. Ramos, "A dihedral acute triangulation of the cube", arXiv:0905.3715.
