# Acute triangulations of the cube

Conjecture: the unit cube $[0, 1]^3$ has an acute triangulation. That is, for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there are tetrahedra $T_1, \dots, T_n$ forming a triangulation of $[0, 1]^3$ (non-degenerate, vertices in the cube, covering it, with disjoint interiors and meeting pairwise in common faces) such that every $T_i$ is an acute tetrahedron: affinely independent with all dihedral angles strictly less than $\pi/2$. It holds; the resolution is [An acute triangulation of the cube exists](conjecture-holds.md).
