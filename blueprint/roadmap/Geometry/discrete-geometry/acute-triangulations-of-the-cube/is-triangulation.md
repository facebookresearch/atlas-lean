# Triangulations of the unit cube

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a family of $n$ tetrahedra $T_1, \dots, T_n$, each given by four vertices in $\mathbb{R}^3$, is a triangulation of the unit cube $[0, 1]^3$ when:

- the four vertices of each $T_i$ are affinely independent;
- every vertex of every $T_i$ lies in $[0, 1]^3$;
- the closed tetrahedra cover the cube exactly: $\bigcup_i \mathrm{conv}(T_i) = [0, 1]^3$;
- for $i \ne j$ the interiors of $\mathrm{conv}(T_i)$ and $\mathrm{conv}(T_j)$ are disjoint;
- for $i \ne j$ the intersection $\mathrm{conv}(T_i) \cap \mathrm{conv}(T_j)$ is the convex hull of a subset of the vertices of $T_i$ and also the convex hull of a subset of the vertices of $T_j$, that is, a common face, possibly empty.
