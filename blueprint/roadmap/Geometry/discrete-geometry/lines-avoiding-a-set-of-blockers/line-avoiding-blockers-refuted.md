# Erdős problem 105 is false: every line through two points is blocked

The conjecture is false: there exist disjoint finite sets $A, B \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ with $|A| = |B| + 3$ and $A$ not contained in a line, such that for all distinct $p, q \in A$ the line through $p$ and $q$ contains a point of $B$.
