# Equally spaced points along a circular arc

A finite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ consists of equally spaced points along an arc of a circle if there are a centre $c \in \mathbb{R}^2$, a radius $r > 0$, an initial angle $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$ and a step $\alpha \ne 0$ such that

$$A = \bigl\{c + r(\cos(\theta + i\alpha), \sin(\theta + i\alpha)) : i \in \mathbb{N},\ 0 \le i < |A|\bigr\}.$$

The step need not close up around the circle: the points are consecutive points at angular spacing $\alpha$, and $(|A| - 1)\alpha$ may be smaller than a full turn.
