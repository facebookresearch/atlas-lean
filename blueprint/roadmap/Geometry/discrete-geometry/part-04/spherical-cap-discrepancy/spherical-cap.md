# Closed spherical caps on $S^2$

For a pole $v \in S^2$ and a real height parameter $t$, the closed spherical cap is

$$C(v, t) = \{x \in S^2 : \langle x, v \rangle \ge t\},$$

where $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ is the Euclidean inner product of $\mathbb{R}^3$. Every $t \in \mathbb{R}$ is allowed: for $t \le -1$ the cap is all of $S^2$, and for $t > 1$ it is empty.
