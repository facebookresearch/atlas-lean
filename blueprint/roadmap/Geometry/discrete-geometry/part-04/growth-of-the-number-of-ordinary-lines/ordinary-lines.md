# Ordinary lines of a finite planar point set

For a finite set $s \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, the ordinary lines of $s$ are the lines $L$ for which there are distinct $p, q \in s$ with $L$ the line through $p$ and $q$ and every point $r \in s$ on $L$ equal to $p$ or $q$. In other words, they are the lines containing exactly two points of $s$.
