# Erdős problem 210: at least $n/2$ ordinary lines

Conjecture: there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that every finite set $s \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ with $|s| \ge N$, not contained in a line, has at least $|s|/2$ ordinary lines:

$$|s| \le 2\, o(s).$$

An ordinary line of a finite set $s \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ is a line through two distinct points of $s$ that contains no other point of $s$, and $o(s)$ denotes the number of ordinary lines of $s$. It holds; the resolution is [Green-Tao: at least $n/2$ ordinary lines for large $n$](ordinary-lines-at-least-half-holds.md).
