# Partitioning complete geometric graphs into plane spanning trees

Conjecture (Bose, Hurtado, Rivera-Campo and Wood): for every $n \ge 1$ and every $2n$ points $p_1, \dots, p_{2n} \in \mathbb{R}^2$ in general position, the edge set of the complete geometric graph on these points can be partitioned into $n$ plane spanning trees.

Here the vertices are $2n$ points $p_1, \dots, p_{2n} \in \mathbb{R}^2$ in general position (pairwise distinct, no three collinear), the edges are all pairs $\{i, j\}$ with $i \ne j$, and a partition into $n$ plane spanning trees is a symmetric colouring $c(i, j) = c(j, i) \in \{1, \dots, n\}$ of the edges such that each colour class is a spanning tree on all $2n$ vertices and any two edges $\{i, j\}, \{i', j'\}$ of the same colour with four distinct endpoints have disjoint segments $[p_i, p_j] \cap [p_{i'}, p_{j'}] = \emptyset$. It is false; the refutation is [Complete geometric graphs not partitionable into plane spanning trees](plane-spanning-tree-partition-refuted.md).
