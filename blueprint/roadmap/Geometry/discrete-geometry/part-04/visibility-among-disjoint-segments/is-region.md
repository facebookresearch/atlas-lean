# The region bounded by a polygon

For points $v_0, \dots, v_{n-1} \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$, $K$ is the closed region of the polygon $v$ when $K$ is compact, has nonempty interior, and its frontier is the union of the edges:

$$\partial K = \bigcup_{i} [v_i, v_{(i+1) \bmod n}].$$
