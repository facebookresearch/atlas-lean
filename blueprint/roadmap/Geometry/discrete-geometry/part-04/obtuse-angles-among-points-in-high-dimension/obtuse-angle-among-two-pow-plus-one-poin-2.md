# Danzer-Grünbaum: $2^d + 1$ points in $\mathbb{R}^d$ span an obtuse angle

This result proves the proposition stated as [Erdős problem 224: an obtuse angle among $2^d + 1$ points](obtuse-angle-among-two-pow-plus-one-poin.md). Precisely, for every $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and every set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ of exactly $2^d + 1$ points, there are three distinct points $x, y, z \in A$ with

$$\angle y x z > \frac{\pi}{2},$$

where $\angle y x z \in [0, \pi]$ is the unoriented angle at $x$ between $y - x$ and $z - x$.
