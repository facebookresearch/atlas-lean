# Schur's conjecture on simplices in diameter graphs

Conjecture (Schur): for every $d \ge 1$ and every $n \ge d + 1$:

- every set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ of $n$ points with diameter $1$ satisfies $s(S) \le n$, and
- some set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ of $n$ points with diameter $1$ has $s(S) = n$.

Here, for a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$, a $(d - 1)$-dimensional simplex of the diameter graph of $S$ is a $d$-element subset $T \subseteq S$ whose points are pairwise at distance exactly $1$, and $s(S)$ is the number of such subsets. It holds; the resolution is [Schur's diameter-simplex bound in every dimension](schur-diameter-simplex-max-holds.md).
