# Unit simplices of a finite point set

For a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$, the unit simplices of $S$ are the $d$-element subsets $T \subseteq S$ whose points are pairwise at distance exactly $1$:

$$\{T \subseteq S : |T| = d,\ \lVert x - y \rVert = 1 \text{ for all distinct } x, y \in T\}.$$

When $S$ has diameter $1$ these are the $(d - 1)$-dimensional simplices, equivalently the $d$-cliques, of the diameter graph of $S$, which joins two points of $S$ at distance $1$.
