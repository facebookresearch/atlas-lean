# Erdős problem 189: monochromatic rectangles of every area

Conjecture: for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every colouring $\chi : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \{1, \dots, n\}$ there is a colour $i$ such that for every $A > 0$ there are points $a, b, c, d$ with $\chi(a) = \chi(b) = \chi(c) = \chi(d) = i$ that are, in order, the vertices of a rectangle of area $A$: $\langle b - a, c - b\rangle = 0$, $d = a + (c - b)$ and $\lVert a - b \rVert \, \lVert b - c \rVert = A$. It is false; the refutation is [Erdős problem 189 is false: no colour has rectangles of every area](colour-class-contains-rectangles-of-ever-2.md).
