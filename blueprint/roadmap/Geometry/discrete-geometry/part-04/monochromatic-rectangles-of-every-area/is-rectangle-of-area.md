# Rectangles of a given area in the plane

For points $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and $A \in \mathbb{R}$, the points $a, b, c, d$ are, in this order, the vertices of a rectangle of area $A$ when

$$\langle b - a, c - b\rangle = 0, \qquad d = a + (c - b), \qquad \lVert a - b \rVert \, \lVert b - c \rVert = A.$$

Rotated rectangles are included, and for $A = 0$ degenerate rectangles are allowed.
