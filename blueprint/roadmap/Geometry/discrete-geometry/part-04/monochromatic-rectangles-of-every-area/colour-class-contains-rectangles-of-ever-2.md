# Erdős problem 189 is false: no colour has rectangles of every area

This result refutes the proposition stated as [Erdős problem 189: monochromatic rectangles of every area](colour-class-contains-rectangles-of-ever.md). The conjecture is false: there are $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a colouring $\chi : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \{1, \dots, n\}$ such that for every colour $i$ some area $A > 0$ is not realized by any rectangle with all four vertices of colour $i$. Here $a, b, c, d$ are, in order, the vertices of a rectangle of area $A$ when $\langle b - a, c - b\rangle = 0$, $d = a + (c - b)$ and $\lVert a - b \rVert \, \lVert b - c \rVert = A$.
