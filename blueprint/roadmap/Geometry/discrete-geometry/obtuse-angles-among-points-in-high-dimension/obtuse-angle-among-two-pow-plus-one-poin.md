# Erdős problem 224: an obtuse angle among $2^d + 1$ points

Conjecture (Danzer and Grünbaum): for every $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and every set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ of exactly $2^d + 1$ points, there are three distinct points $x, y, z \in A$ with

$$\angle y x z > \frac{\pi}{2},$$

where $\angle y x z \in [0, \pi]$ is the unoriented angle at $x$ between $y - x$ and $z - x$.
