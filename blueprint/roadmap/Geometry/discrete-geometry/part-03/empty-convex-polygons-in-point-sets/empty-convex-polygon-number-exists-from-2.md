# Erdős problem 216 fails for $k \ge 7$

This result refutes the proposition stated as [Erdős problem 216: empty convex $k$-gons for some $k \ge 7$](empty-convex-polygon-number-exists-from.md). The claim is false: for no $k \ge 7$ is there an $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that every finite set $S \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with at least $N$ points and no three distinct points collinear contains an empty convex $k$-gon (a $k$-point subset $T \subseteq S$ in convex position with no point of $S$ in the interior of the convex hull of $T$).
