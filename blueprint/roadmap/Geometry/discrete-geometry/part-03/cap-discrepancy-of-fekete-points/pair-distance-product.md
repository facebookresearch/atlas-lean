# Product of pairwise distances on the sphere

For a finite set $A$ of points on the unit sphere $S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$, the pair distance product is the product of the Euclidean distances over all ordered pairs of distinct points of $A$:

$$\prod_{\substack{(w, w') \in A \times A \\ w \ne w'}} \lVert w - w' \rVert = \Bigl( \prod_{\{w, w'\} \subseteq A,\ w \ne w'} \lVert w - w' \rVert \Bigr)^2.$$

It is the square of the product over unordered pairs, so both have the same maximizers among $n$-point sets. For $\lvert A \rvert \le 1$ the product is empty and equals $1$.
