# Statement of Erdős problem 353: right triangles of area one

A variant of Erdős Problem 353 asks for the following statement: for every Lebesgue measurable set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ of infinite Lebesgue measure, there are pairwise distinct points $a, b, c \in A$ such that one of the angles $\angle bac$, $\angle abc$, $\angle bca$ equals $\pi/2$ and the convex hull of $\{a, b, c\}$ (the triangle $abc$) has area $1$. It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 353: right triangles of area one](right-angled-triangle-holds.md).
