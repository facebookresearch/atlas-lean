# Statement of Erdős problem 353: isosceles triangles of area one

A variant of Erdős Problem 353 asks for the following statement: for every Lebesgue measurable set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ of infinite Lebesgue measure, there are points $a, b, c \in A$ such that two of the distances $\lVert a - b \rVert$, $\lVert b - c \rVert$, $\lVert c - a \rVert$ are equal and the convex hull of $\{a, b, c\}$ (the triangle $abc$) has area $1$. It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 353: isosceles triangles of area one](isosceles-triangle-holds.md).
