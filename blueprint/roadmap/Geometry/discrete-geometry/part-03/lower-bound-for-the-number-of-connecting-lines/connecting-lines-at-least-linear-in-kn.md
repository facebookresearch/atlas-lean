# Erdős problem 211: lines determined by $n$ points

Erdős problem 211 asks whether the following holds: there is an absolute constant $c > 0$ such that for all natural numbers $n, k$ with $1 \le k < n$ and every set $s$ of exactly $n$ points in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with at most $n - k$ points of $s$ on any line, the number of lines containing at least two points of $s$ is at least

$$c\, k\, n.$$

It holds; the resolution is [Beck's theorem on connecting lines](connecting-lines-at-least-linear-in-kn-h.md).
