# Statement of Erdős problem 95: energy of distance multiplicities

Erdős Problem 95 asks for the following statement: for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a constant $C$ such that for every finite set $A \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ of $n$ points,

$$\sum_{u \in D(A)} f_A(u)^2 \le C\, n^{3 + \varepsilon},$$

where $D(A) = \{\, \lVert x - y \rVert : x, y \in A,\ x \ne y \,\}$ is the set of distances determined by $A$ and $f_A(u)$ is the number of unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of distinct points of $A$ with $\lVert x - y \rVert = u$. The constant $C$ depends only on $\varepsilon$. It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 95: energy of distance multiplicities](distance-energy-bound-holds.md).
