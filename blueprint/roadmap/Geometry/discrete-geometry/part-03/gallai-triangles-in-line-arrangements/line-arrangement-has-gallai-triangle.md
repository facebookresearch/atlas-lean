# Erdős problem 209: Gallai triangles

Erdős Problem 209, in existential form, asks for the following statement: there is $d \ge 4$ such that every family $\mathcal{A}$ of exactly $d$ lines in $\mathbb{R}^2$, pairwise non-parallel (no two distinct members of $\mathcal{A}$ are parallel) and with no point lying on four or more of them, has a Gallai triangle: three distinct members $L_1, L_2, L_3 \in \mathcal{A}$ and three distinct points $p_1, p_2, p_3 \in \mathbb{R}^2$ with $p_1 \in L_2 \cap L_3$, $p_2 \in L_1 \cap L_3$, $p_3 \in L_1 \cap L_2$, such that each of $p_1, p_2, p_3$ lies on exactly two members of $\mathcal{A}$. It is false; the refutation is [Erdős problem 209 fails: line arrangements without Gallai triangles](line-arrangement-has-gallai-triangle-ref.md).
