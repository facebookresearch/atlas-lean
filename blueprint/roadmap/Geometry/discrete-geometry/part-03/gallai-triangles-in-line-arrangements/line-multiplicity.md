# Multiplicity of a point in a line arrangement

For a finite family $\mathcal{A}$ of affine subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^2$ and a point $p \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the multiplicity of $p$ in $\mathcal{A}$ is the number of members of $\mathcal{A}$ that contain $p$:

$$m_{\mathcal{A}}(p) = \#\{\, L \in \mathcal{A} : p \in L \,\}.$$
