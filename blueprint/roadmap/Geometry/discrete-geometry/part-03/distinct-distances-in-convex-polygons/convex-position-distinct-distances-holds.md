# Erdős problem 93: distinct distances in convex polygons

This result proves the proposition stated as [Statement of Erdős problem 93: distinct distances in convex polygons](convex-position-distinct-distances.md). Precisely, for every finite set $A \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ of $n$ points in convex position (no point of $A$ lies in the convex hull of the others), the number of distinct values $\lVert x - y \rVert$ with $x, y \in A$, $x \ne y$, is at least $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor$.
