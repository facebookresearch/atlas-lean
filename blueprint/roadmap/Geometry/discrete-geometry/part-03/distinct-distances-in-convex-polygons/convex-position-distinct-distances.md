# Statement of Erdős problem 93: distinct distances in convex polygons

Erdős Problem 93 asks for the following statement. For every finite set $A \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ of $n$ points in convex position (no point of $A$ lies in the convex hull of the others), the number of distinct distances determined by $A$ is at least $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor$:

$$\#\{\, \lVert x - y \rVert : x, y \in A,\ x \ne y \,\} \ge \left\lfloor \frac{n}{2} \right\rfloor.$$

It holds; the resolution is [Erdős problem 93: distinct distances in convex polygons](convex-position-distinct-distances-holds.md).
