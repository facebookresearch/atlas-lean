# Acute tetrahedra

Four points $v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3 \in \mathbb{R}^3$ form an acute tetrahedron when they are affinely independent and all dihedral angles are acute: for all pairwise distinct indices $a, b, c, d \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$,

$$\bigl\langle (v_c - v_b)^{\perp (v_a - v_b)},\ (v_d - v_b)^{\perp (v_a - v_b)} \bigr\rangle > 0,$$

where $w^{\perp e} = w - \frac{\langle w, e \rangle}{\langle e, e \rangle} e$ is the component of $w$ orthogonal to $e$. Geometrically, the two faces through the edge $v_a v_b$ meet at an angle less than $\pi/2$.
