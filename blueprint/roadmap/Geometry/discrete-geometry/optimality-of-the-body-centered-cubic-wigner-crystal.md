# Optimality of the body-centered cubic Wigner crystal

Determine the minimum energy of classical Coulomb charges in a neutralizing background. This also informs the uniform-electron-gas constants appearing in density-functional bounds.

## What would settle it

Define the jellium thermodynamic energy with the source's normalization. Prove a lower bound matching the body-centered-cubic lattice energy against arbitrary configurations, not just Bravais lattices.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Muratov and Novaga (preprint) prove that the body-centered cubic lattice uniquely minimizes the renormalized Coulomb energy among three-dimensional Bravais lattices of fixed covolume, and describe the question of a crystalline ground state as a fundamental unsolved problem.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
