# Proposition 1 (Lecture 25, Section 2 (Seiberg-Witten Equations))

The gauge group action $(A, \psi) \mapsto (A - 2df \cdot f^{-1},\, f\psi)$ preserves the solution space of the Seiberg-Witten equations, and the action of $\mathcal{G}$ is free unless $\psi \equiv 0$ (reducible solutions), where $\operatorname{Stab}((A, 0)) \cong S^{1}$ is the space of constant maps.
