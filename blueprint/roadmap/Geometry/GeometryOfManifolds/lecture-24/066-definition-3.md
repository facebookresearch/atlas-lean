# Definition 3 (Lecture 24, Section 3 (Dirac Operator))

Given a spin$^{c}$ structure and a connection, the Dirac operator is $D_{A} \colon \Gamma(S^{\pm}) \rightarrow \Gamma(S^{\pm}),\; D_{A}\psi = \sum_{i} \gamma(e^{i})\nabla_{e_{i}}^{A}\psi$ for $\{e_{i}\}$ an orthonormal basis (though it is independent of choice of basis).
