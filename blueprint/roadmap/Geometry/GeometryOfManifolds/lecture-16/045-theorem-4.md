# Theorem 4

$L$ elliptic, self-adjoint $\Longrightarrow \exists H_{L}, G_{L} \colon C^{\infty}(E) \rightarrow C^{\infty}(E)$ such that (1) $H_{L}$ maps $C^{\infty}(E) \rightarrow \operatorname{Ker}(L)$, (2) $L \circ G_{L} = G_{L} \circ L = \mathrm{id} - H_{L}$, (3) $G_{L}, H_{L}$ extend to bounded operators $W^{s} \rightarrow W^{s}$, and (4) $C^{\infty}(E) = \operatorname{Ker} L \oplus_{\perp L^{2}} \operatorname{Im}(L \circ G_{L})$.
