# Theorem 3 (Lecture 5, Section 1 (Tubular Neighborhoods))

Let $M^{n} \supset X^{k}$ be a submanifold. Then $\exists U_{0}$ a neighborhood of $X$ in $NX$ (via the 0-section) and $U_{1}$ a neighborhood of $X$ in $M$ such that $\exists \phi \colon U_{0} \xrightarrow{\sim} U_{1}$ a diffeomorphism.
