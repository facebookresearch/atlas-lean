# Definition 3 (Lecture 2, Section 2 (Symplectic Vector Spaces))

A standard (symplectic) basis of $(V^{2n}, \Omega)$ is a basis $(\{e_{i}\}, \{f_{i}\})$ satisfying $\Omega(e_{i}, e_{j}) = \Omega(f_{i}, f_{j}) = 0$ for all $i, j$, and $\Omega(e_{i}, f_{j}) = \delta_{i,j} = -\Omega(f_{j}, e_{i})$.
