# Definition 4 (Lecture 2, Section 2 (Symplectic Vector Spaces))

$E \subset V$ is a symplectic subspace if $\Omega|_{E}$ is nondegenerate, e.g.\ in a standard basis $E$ is the span of $(e_{1}, f_{1}, \ldots, e_{k}, f_{k})$.
