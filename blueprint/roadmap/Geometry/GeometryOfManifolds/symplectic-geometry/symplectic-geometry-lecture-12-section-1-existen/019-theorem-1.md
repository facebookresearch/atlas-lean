# Theorem 1 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 12, Section 1 (Existence of Almost-Complex Structures))

$p_{1}(TM) \cdot [M] = 3\sigma(M)$, where $\sigma(M)$ is the signature of $M$ (the difference between the number of positive and negative eigenvalues of the intersection product $Q \colon H_{2}(M) \otimes H_{2}(M) \rightarrow \mathbb{Z},\; [A] \otimes [B] \mapsto [A \cap B]$ dual to the cup product on $H^{2}$).
