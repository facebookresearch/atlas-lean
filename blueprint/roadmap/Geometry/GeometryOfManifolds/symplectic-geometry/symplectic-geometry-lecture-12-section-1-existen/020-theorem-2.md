# Theorem 2 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 12, Section 1 (Existence of Almost-Complex Structures))

$\exists$ an almost complex structure $J$ on $M^{4}$ such that $\alpha = c_{1}(TM, J) \in H^{2}(M, \mathbb{Z})$ iff $\alpha$ satisfies $\alpha^{2} \cdot [M] = 2\chi + 3\sigma$ and $\alpha \cdot [A] \equiv Q([A],[A]) \pmod{2}$ for all $[A] \in H_{2}(M, \mathbb{Z})$.
