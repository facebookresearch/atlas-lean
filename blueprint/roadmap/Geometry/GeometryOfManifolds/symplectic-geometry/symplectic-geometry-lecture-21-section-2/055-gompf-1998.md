# Theorem 2 (Gompf, 1998)

If $f \colon M^{4} \rightarrow \Sigma^{2}$ is a Lefschetz fibration with $[F] \neq 0 \in H_{2}(M, \mathbb{R})$, then $M$ carries a symplectic form such that the fibers are symplectic.
