# Theorem 1 (Thurston)

Let $f \colon M \rightarrow B$ be a compact, locally trivial symplectic fibration with symplectic fiber $(F, \omega_{F})$ and symplectic base $(B, \omega_{B})$. If $\exists c \in H^{2}(M, \mathbb{R})$ such that $c|_{F} = [\omega_{F}] \in H^{2}(F, \mathbb{R})$, then for all $k \gg 0$, $\exists$ a symplectic form on $M$ in the class $c + k \cdot f^{*}[\omega_{B}]$ for which the fibers of $f$ are symplectic submanifolds.
