# Theorem 3 (Donaldson)

For $(M^{4}, \omega)$ symplectic, after blowing up points in $M$, we get $\hat{M}$ which admits a Lefschetz fibration to $S^{2}$. Here, the blowup is locally given by $\hat{\mathbb{C}^{2}} = \{(x, \ell) \in \mathbb{C}^{2} \times \mathbb{CP}^{1} \mid x \in \ell\}$.
