# Proposition 4 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 8, Section 1 (Almost-complex Structures))

If $X$ is an almost-complex submanifold in compatible $(M, \omega, J)$, then $X$ is symplectic (i.e.\ $\omega|_{X}$ is nondegenerate).
