# Definition 4 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 8, Section 2 (Vector Bundles and Connections))

For $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ a Euclidean or Hermitian metric on $E$, $\nabla$ is compatible with the metric if $d\langle \sigma, \sigma^{\prime} \rangle = \langle \nabla\sigma, \sigma^{\prime} \rangle + \langle \sigma, \nabla\sigma^{\prime} \rangle$.
