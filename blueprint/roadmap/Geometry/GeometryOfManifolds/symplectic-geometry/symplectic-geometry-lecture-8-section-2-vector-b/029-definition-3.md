# Definition 3 (Symplectic Geometry, Lecture 8, Section 2 (Vector Bundles and Connections))

A connection $\nabla$ on $E$ is an $\mathbb{R}$- or $\mathbb{C}$-linear mapping $C^{\infty}(M, E) \rightarrow C^{\infty}(M, T^{*}M \otimes E) = \Omega^{1}(M, E)$ such that $\nabla(f\sigma) = df \cdot \sigma + f\nabla\sigma$. For $v \in T_{x}M$, we let $\nabla_{v}$ denote the mapping $\sigma \mapsto \nabla\sigma(v)$.
