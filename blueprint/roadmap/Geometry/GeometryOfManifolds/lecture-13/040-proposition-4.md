# Proposition 4 (Lecture 13, Section 1, Integrability of Almost-Complex Structures)

The dual map $N^{*} \colon \bigwedge^{0,1} T^{*}M \rightarrow \bigwedge^{2,0} T^{*}M$ is precisely the map $N^{*}\alpha = (d\alpha)^{(2,0)}$.
