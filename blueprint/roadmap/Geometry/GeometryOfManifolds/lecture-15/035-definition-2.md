# Definition 2 (Lecture 15, Section 1, Hodge Theory)

The codifferential is the map $d^{*} = (-1)^{n(k-1)+1} \ast d \ast \colon \Omega^{k}(M) \rightarrow \Omega^{k-1}(M)$.
