# Congruent central hyperplane sections

Let $K, L \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$. The central hyperplane sections of $K$ and $L$ are congruent when for every linear subspace $H \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ of dimension $n - 1$ there is a linear isometry $\varphi : H \to H$ (an orthogonal transformation of $H$, with no translation) such that

$$\varphi(K \cap H) = L \cap H.$$
