# Convex fair partitions

Let $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. A family $Q_1, \dots, Q_n$ of subsets of $\mathbb{R}^2$ is a convex fair partition of $P$ into $n$ pieces when

- each $Q_i$ is closed and convex with nonempty interior;
- $Q_1 \cup \cdots \cup Q_n = P$;
- the interiors of $Q_i$ and $Q_j$ are disjoint for $i \ne j$;
- all pieces have the same area: $\lambda(Q_i) = \lambda(Q_j)$ for all $i, j$, with $\lambda$ Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$;
- all pieces have the same perimeter: $\mathcal{H}^1(\partial Q_i) = \mathcal{H}^1(\partial Q_j)$ for all $i, j$, with $\mathcal{H}^1$ the one-dimensional Hausdorff measure and $\partial Q_i$ the boundary.
