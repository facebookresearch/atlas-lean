# Combinatorial equivalence of polytopes

Let $V, W \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be finite sets and $f : \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^d$. Then $f$ is a combinatorial equivalence from $V$ to $W$ when $f$ restricts to a bijection from $V$ onto $W$ and, for every $F \subseteq V$, $F$ is the vertex set of a facet of $V$ if and only if $f(F)$ is the vertex set of a facet of $W$. Since vertex-facet incidences determine the face lattice, this is the usual notion of combinatorial equivalence of polytopes.
