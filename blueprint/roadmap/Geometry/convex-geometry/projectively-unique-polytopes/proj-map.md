# Projective maps from matrices

For a $(d + 1) \times (d + 1)$ real matrix $M$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$, let $y = M (x, 1) \in \mathbb{R}^{d+1}$, where $(x, 1)$ are the homogeneous coordinates of $x$. The projective map of $M$ sends $x$ to

$$\Bigl(\frac{y_1}{y_{d+1}}, \dots, \frac{y_d}{y_{d+1}}\Bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^d,$$

with the convention $t / 0 = 0$. For invertible $M$ this is the projective transformation of $\mathbb{R}^d$ given by $M$ wherever $y_{d+1} \ne 0$.
