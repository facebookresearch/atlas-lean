# Facets of a polytope given by its vertices

Let $V, F \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be finite sets. $F$ is the vertex set of a facet of the polytope with vertex set $V$ when $F \subseteq V$, there are a nonzero linear functional $\ell : \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\ell(v) \le c$ for all $v \in V$ and

$$F = \{v \in V : \ell(v) = c\},$$

and the vector span of $F$ (equivalently, the direction of its affine span) has dimension $d - 1$. For $d = 0$ there are no facets.
