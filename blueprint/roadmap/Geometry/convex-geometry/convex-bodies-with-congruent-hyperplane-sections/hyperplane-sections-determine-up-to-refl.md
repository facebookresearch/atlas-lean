# Do congruent hyperplane sections determine a body up to $\pm$?

This is the case of central hyperplane sections in the question whether congruent sections determine a star body up to reflection in the origin. Conjecture: there is a dimension $n \ge 3$ for which the following holds. Let $K, L \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be convex bodies (compact, convex, with nonempty interior), each containing the origin in its interior. If for every linear hyperplane $H \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ (a linear subspace of dimension $n - 1$) there is a linear isometry $\varphi_H$ of $H$ with $\varphi_H(K \cap H) = L \cap H$, then $K = L$ or $K = -L$.
