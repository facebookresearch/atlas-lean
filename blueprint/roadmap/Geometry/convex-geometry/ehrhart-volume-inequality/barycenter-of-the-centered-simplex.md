# Barycenter of the centered simplex

For every natural dimension $n$, the barycenter of $K_n$ is zero. This includes the zero-dimensional singleton; the claim does not require $n>0$.

## Expansion

Compute the coordinate integrals of the standard simplex from symmetry of its n+1 vertices and use translation and scaling of the barycenter. Treat the zero-dimensional case in the coordinate model.
