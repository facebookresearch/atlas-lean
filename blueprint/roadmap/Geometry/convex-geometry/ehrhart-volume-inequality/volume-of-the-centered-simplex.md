# Lattice-normalized volume of the centered simplex

For each $n>0$, the normalized real volume of $K_n$ is $(n+1)^n/n!$. Here the standard simplex has volume $1/n!$ before dilation.

## Expansion

Compute volume of the standard simplex with the unit-cube convention and apply the affine change-of-variables factor $(n+1)^n$.
