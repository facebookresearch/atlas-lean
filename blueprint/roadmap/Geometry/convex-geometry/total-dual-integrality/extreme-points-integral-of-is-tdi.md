# Edmonds–Giles theorem

If $A x \le b$ is a totally dual integral system with $A$ rational and $b$ integral, then every extreme point of the polyhedron $\{x : A x \le b\}$ is integral. Goemans states the conclusion as "$\{A x \le b\}$ is integral, i.e. all its extreme points are integral"; for a polyhedron without vertices the statement is vacuous.

## Sources

- M. X. Goemans, *Advanced Combinatorial Optimization*, MIT 18.438, Lecture 6 (2009), <https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/18438F09/lec6.pdf>, Theorem 2 (proof at the end of the lecture).
- J. Edmonds and R. Giles, *A min-max relation for submodular functions on graphs*, Ann. Discrete Math. 1 (1977), 185–204.
