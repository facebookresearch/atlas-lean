# Termwise comparison of finite sums

Let $N$ be an additive commutative monoid with a preorder $\le$ such that $b \le c$ implies $a + b \le a + c$ for all
$a, b, c \in N$ (such as $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{N}$). Let $s$ be a finite set of elements of a type $\iota$ and
$f, g \colon \iota \to N$ with $f(i) \le g(i)$ for every $i \in s$. Then

$$
\sum_{i \in s} f(i) \le \sum_{i \in s} g(i).
$$
