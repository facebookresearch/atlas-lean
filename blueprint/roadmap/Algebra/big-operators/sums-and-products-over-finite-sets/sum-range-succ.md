# Last term of a sum over a range

Let $M$ be an additive commutative monoid, $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to M$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

$$
\sum_{x \in \operatorname{range} (n + 1)} f(x) = \sum_{x \in \operatorname{range} n} f(x) + f(n),
$$

where $\operatorname{range} n = \{0, 1, \dots, n - 1\}$; that is, $\sum_{x=0}^{n} f(x) = \sum_{x=0}^{n-1} f(x) + f(n)$.
