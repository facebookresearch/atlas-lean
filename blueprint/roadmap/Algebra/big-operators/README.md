# Finite sums and products

Sums and products over finite sets, multisets, lists and finitely supported families in commutative monoids: reindexing, splitting and interchange of sums, sums over intervals and antidiagonals, averages, sums in rings and groups with zero, inequalities for finite sums in ordered structures and congruences of sums; infinite sums are under Topology.
