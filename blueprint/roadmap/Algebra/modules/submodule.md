# Submodule

A submodule of a module $M$ over a semiring $R$ is a subset of $M$ that contains $0$ and is closed under addition and under multiplication by scalars.

Let $R$ be a semiring and $M$ a module over $R$. A *submodule* of $M$ is a subset $N \subseteq M$ such that $0 \in N$, $x + y \in N$ for all $x, y \in N$, and $r x \in N$ for all $r \in R$ and $x \in N$. For a vector space $V$ over a division ring $F$ the submodules are the subspaces of $V$. Ordered by inclusion, the submodules of $M$ form a complete lattice, with $N \wedge N' = N \cap N'$ and $N \vee N' = N + N'$, which is modular when $R$ is a ring. For a vector space $V$ this is the *subspace lattice* $\mathrm L(V)$, a bounded modular lattice with least element $\{0\}$ and greatest element $V$; if $V$ has finite dimension $d$, then $\mathrm L(V)$ has height $d$, and it is finite if moreover $F$ is finite.
