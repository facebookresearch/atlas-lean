# Modules over rings

Modules over rings and semirings: linear maps and equivalences, submodules and their lattice, kernels and images, torsion and torsion-free modules, projective, injective and finitely presented modules, presentations, localization of modules, direct limits and ℤ-lattices. Vector spaces, bases, dimension and matrices are under linear algebra; module-theoretic results of commutative algebra are under ring theory.
