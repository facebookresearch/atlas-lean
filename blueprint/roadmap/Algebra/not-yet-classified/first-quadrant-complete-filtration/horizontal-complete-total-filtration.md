# Horizontal Complete Total Filtration

Let $C$ be a preadditive category with a zero object and finite coproducts,
and let $K$ be a double chain complex in $C$ indexed by $\mathbb N$ in both
directions. The horizontal filtration of its total complex extends to a
filtration indexed by $\mathrm{EInt}=\mathbb Z\cup\{-\infty,+\infty\}$.

Its bottom stage and every negative integer stage are the zero complex. At a
nonnegative integer $a$, its stage is the ordinary horizontal filtration
stage $a$. Its top stage is the full total complex, included by the identity.
The transition along every $i\leq j$ commutes with the structural inclusions
into the total complex.
