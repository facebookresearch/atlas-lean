# Horizontal Stable Page Graded Iso

Let $C$ be an abelian category and let $K$ be a double chain complex in $C$
indexed by $\mathbb N$ in both directions. For $p,q\in\mathbb N$ and
$r\in\mathbb Z$ with $r\geq1$, $r\geq p+1$, and $r\geq q+2$, there is a
canonical isomorphism

$$
E^r_{p,q}(K)\cong\operatorname{gr}_p H_{p+q}(\operatorname{Tot}K).
$$

The graded piece is `horizontalTotalHomologyGradedPiece K p q`, formed from
the filtration induced on total homology. The comparison transports the
stable term through the canonical abutment image quotient to that graded
piece. The filtration retains its extension data.
