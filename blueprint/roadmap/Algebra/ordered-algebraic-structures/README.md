# Ordered algebraic structures

Monoids, groups, rings, fields and modules with compatible orders: monotone operations, positive cones, lattice-ordered groups and their order-induced absolute values, ring orderings, Archimedean properties, floor and ceiling functions, and order-preserving maps. Orders and lattices as such are under order theory, orderable groups under group theory, and general absolute values and their completions under analysis.
