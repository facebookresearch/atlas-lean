# A value bounded by an average plus a slack

Let $K$ be a linearly ordered field and $m, M, p, T \in K$ with $m > 0$. If $m(M - p) \le T$, then

$$M \le \frac{T}{m} + p.$$

(This is the averaging step in Graham's analysis of list scheduling, read with $m$ machines, makespan $M$, last job length $p$ and total work $T$.)
