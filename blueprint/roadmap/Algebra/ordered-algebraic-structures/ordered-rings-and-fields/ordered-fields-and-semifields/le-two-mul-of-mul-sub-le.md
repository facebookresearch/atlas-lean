# Twice a common bound from an average plus a slack

Let $K$ be a linearly ordered field and $m, M, p, T, B \in K$ with $m > 0$. If $m(M - p) \le T$, $T/m \le B$ and $p \le B$, then $M \le 2B$.

(This is the final arithmetic step of Graham's factor-$2$ bound for list scheduling, with $B$ the optimal makespan.)
