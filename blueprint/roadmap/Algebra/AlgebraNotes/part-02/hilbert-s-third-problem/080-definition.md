# Definition (Hilbert's Third Problem, Back to Polytopes)

The **Dehn invariant** of a polytope $P$ is $d(P) = \sum_i \ell_i \otimes \theta_i \in \mathbb{R} \otimes \mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$, where the sum is taken over all edges of $P$.
