# Proposition (Lecture 4, Section: More on Characters)

If $\rho: G \to \operatorname{GL}(V)$ is a complex representation, then (a) $\chi_\rho(g)$ is a sum of roots of unity; (b) $\chi_\rho(g^{-1}) = \overline{\chi_\rho(g)}$; (c) $\overline{\chi_\rho}$ is the character of another representation of the same dimension, denoted $\rho^*$ and called the dual representation.
