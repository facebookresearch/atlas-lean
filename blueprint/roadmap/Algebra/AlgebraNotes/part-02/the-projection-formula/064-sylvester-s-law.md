# Claim (Sylvester's Law)

Given $V$ and $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle,$ the number of 1s, the number of -1s, and the number of 0s that occur in the diagonal form are determined by $V$ and $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle,$ and not by the choice of orthogonal basis. This is called Sylvester's Law, and the number of 1s, -1s, and 0s is called the signature of the form.
