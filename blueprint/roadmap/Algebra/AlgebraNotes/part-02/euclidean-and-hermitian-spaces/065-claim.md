# Claim (Section: Euclidean and Hermitian Spaces, Subsection: Complex Linear Operators)

For $v, w \in V,$ $\langle Tv, w \rangle = \langle v, T^*w\rangle.$ This property means that $T^*$ is uniquely determined, by putting $v = u_i$ and $w = u_j.$
