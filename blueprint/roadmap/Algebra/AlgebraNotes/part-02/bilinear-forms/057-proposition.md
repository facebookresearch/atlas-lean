# Proposition (Section: Bilinear Forms)

Every bilinear form $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$ arises from a matrix $A.$ That is, there exists some $A$ such that $\langle x, y \rangle = x^{T} Ay.$ Moreover, the form $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ is symmetric if and only if $A$ is symmetric.
