# Theorem (Sylow Theorems)

Let $G$ be a finite group where $|G| = n = p^em$ and $\gcd(p, m) = 1$. The three parts of the theorem follow: (1) There always exists a Sylow $p$-subgroup (a subgroup $H \leq G$ such that $|H| = p^e$). (2) Given any $K \leq G$ where $|K| = p^f$, there exists some $g\in G$ such that $gKg^{-1} \leq H$. (3) The number of Sylow $p$-subgroups is a factor of $m$ and congruent to 1 mod $p$.
