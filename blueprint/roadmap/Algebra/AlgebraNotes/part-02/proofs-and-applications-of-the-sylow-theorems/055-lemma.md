# Lemma (Proofs and Applications of the Sylow Theorems, Proof of Sylow Theorems)

Suppose we have a subset $U \in S$, which is a subset of $G$. Also, let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ that stabilizes $U$. Then, $|H|$ divides $|U|.$
