# Theorem (Section: The Special Unitary Group $SU_2$, Subsection: Longitudes)

For each $x \in \mathbb{E},$ $\operatorname{Long}_x$ is a subgroup of $SU_2$. In fact, given $\theta \in \mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$, the map $\theta \mapsto \cos \theta I + \sin \theta x$ is an isomorphism between $\mathbb{R}/2\pi \mathbb{Z}$ and $\operatorname{Long}_x$.
