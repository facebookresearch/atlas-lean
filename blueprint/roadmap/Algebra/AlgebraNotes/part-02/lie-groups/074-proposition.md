# Proposition (Section: Lie Groups, Subsection: Lie Groups)

For $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$:
- All three definitions are actually equivalent.
- For a group $G$, $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$ is actually a vector subspace of $\operatorname{Mat}_{n \times n}(\mathbb{R})$.
