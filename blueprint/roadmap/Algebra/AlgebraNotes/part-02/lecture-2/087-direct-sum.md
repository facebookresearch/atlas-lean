# Definition (Direct Sum)

Let $\psi: G \to \operatorname{GL}(V)$ and $\eta: G \to \operatorname{GL}(W)$ be two representations of the same group. Then their **direct sum** $\rho = \psi \oplus \eta$ is the representation $\rho: G \to \operatorname{GL}(U \oplus W)$ where for each $g \in G$, $\rho_g(u, w) = (\psi_g(u), \eta_g(w))$.
