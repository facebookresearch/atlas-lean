# Proposition (Section on One-Parameter Subgroups)

Every one-parameter group in $GL_n(\mathbb{C})$ is of the form $\varphi(t) = e^{tA}$ for a unique matrix $A \in \operatorname{Mat}_{n \times n}(\mathbb{C}).$
