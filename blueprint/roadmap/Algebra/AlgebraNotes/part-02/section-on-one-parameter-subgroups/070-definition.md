# Definition (Section on One-Parameter Subgroups)

For $G \leq GL_n(\mathbb{C}),$ a **one-parameter group in $G$** is a one-parameter group $\varphi(t)$ in $GL_n(\mathbb{C})$ such that $\varphi(t) \in G$ for all $t \in \mathbb{R}.$
