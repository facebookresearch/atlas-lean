# Theorem (Lecture 14: Number Fields, Section: The Gaussian Integers)

The complete list of all primes in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, up to association, consists of: for each integer prime $p = 4k + 3 \in \mathbb{Z}$, the Gaussian prime $p$ itself; for each integer prime $p = 4k + 1 \in \mathbb{Z}$, the two Gaussian primes $a \pm bi$ where $a^2 + b^2 = p$; the prime $1 + i$.
