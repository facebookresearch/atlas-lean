# Corollary (Section: Smith Normal Form, Subsection: Applications)

Every finitely presented abelian group is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/d_1\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/d_2\mathbb{Z} \times \cdots \times \mathbb{Z}/d_n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}^a$, for some positive integers $d_i$ with $d_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_n$.
