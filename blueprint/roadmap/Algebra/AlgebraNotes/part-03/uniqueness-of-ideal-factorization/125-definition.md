# Definition (Section: Uniqueness of Ideal Factorization, Subsection: Properties of Ideal Multiplication)

The **norm** of an ideal $I$, denoted $\operatorname{N}(I)$, is the positive integer $n$ with $I\overline{I} = (n)$.
