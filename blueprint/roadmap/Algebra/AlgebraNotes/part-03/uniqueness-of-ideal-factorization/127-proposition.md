# Proposition (Section: Uniqueness of Ideal Factorization, Subsection: Properties of Ideal Multiplication)

Multiplication of ideals has the following two properties: (a) Cancellation: if $IJ = I'J$ and $J \neq 0$, then $I = I'$. (b) If $I \subset J$, then $I = JJ'$ for some $J'$.
