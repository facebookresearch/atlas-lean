# Lemma (Lecture 13: More Factorization, Section: Gaussian Primes)

Let $p \in \mathbb{Z}$ be a prime number. Then $p$ is not prime in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ if and only if $p = 2$ or $p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$.
