# Theorem (Section: Decomposition of Modules, Subsection: Classification of Abelian Groups)

Any finitely presented abelian group $A$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/d_1\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/d_2\mathbb{Z} \times \cdots \times \mathbb{Z}/d_n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}^a$, where $d_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_n$.
