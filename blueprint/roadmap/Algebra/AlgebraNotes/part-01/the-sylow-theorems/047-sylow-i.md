# Theorem (Sylow I)

Given $G$ such that $|G| = n = p^e \cdot m,$ where $p^e$ is the largest power of $p$ (that is, $\gcd(p, m) = 1$), then there exists a subgroup $H \leq G$ such that $|H| = p^e.$
