# Definition (Group)

A group is a set $G$ with a composition (or product) law $G \times G \to G$, $(a, b) \mapsto a\cdot b$ fulfilling the following conditions: (1) Identity: There exists some element $e \in G$ such that $a \cdot e = e \cdot a = a$. (2) Inverse: For all $a \in G,$ there exists $b \in G$, denoted $a^{-1}$, such that $a \cdot b = b \cdot a = e$. (3) Associative: For $a, b, c \in G,$ $(ab)c = a(bc).$
