# Lemma (Section on spanning, linear independence, and bases)

If $S = \{\vec{v}_1, \cdots, \vec{v}_r\}$ spans $V,$ and $L = \{\vec{w}_1, \cdots, \vec{w}_s\}$ is linearly independent, then (1) Removing elements of $S$ gets a basis of $V.$ (2) Adding elements of $S$ to $L$ gets another basis of $V.$ (3) $|S| \geq |L|$.
