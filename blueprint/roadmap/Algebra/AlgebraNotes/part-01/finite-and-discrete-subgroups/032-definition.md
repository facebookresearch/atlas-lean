# Definition (Section: Finite and Discrete Subgroups, Continued, Subsection: Discrete Subgroups of M2)

A group $G \subseteq M_2$ is discrete if there exists some $\varepsilon > 0$ such that any translation in $G$ has distance $\geq \varepsilon$ and any rotation in $G$ has angle $\geq \varepsilon.$
