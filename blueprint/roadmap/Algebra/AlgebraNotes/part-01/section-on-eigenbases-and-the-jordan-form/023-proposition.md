# Proposition (Section on Eigenbases and the Jordan Form, subsection on The Characteristic Polynomial)

Given an $n \times n$ matrix $A$, eigenvectors $\vec{v}_1, \cdots, \vec{v}_k,$ and distinct eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \cdots, \lambda_k,$ the vectors $\vec{v}_i$ are all linearly independent.
