# Corollary (Section on Eigenbases and the Jordan Form, subsection on The Characteristic Polynomial)

Consider a matrix $A$. If the characteristic polynomial is $p_A(t) = (t - \lambda_1) \cdots (t - \lambda_n)$ where each $\lambda_i$ is distinct, $A$ will have an eigenbasis and will thus be diagonalizable.
