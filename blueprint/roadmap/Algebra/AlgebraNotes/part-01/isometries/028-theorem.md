# Theorem (Section: Isometries, Subsection: Isometries in 2-space)

Every isometry on $\mathbb{R}^2$ is (1) Translation, (2) Rotation around a point $p$, (3) Reflection across a line $L$, or (4) Glide reflection --- first, reflect across a line $L,$ then translate by some vector $b$ parallel to $L$.
