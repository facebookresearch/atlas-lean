# Theorem (Correspondence Theorem)

Where $f: G \rightarrow G'$ is a surjective homomorphism, and $K = \ker(f),$ there is a correspondence $\{\text{subgroups } H: K \subseteq H \subseteq G\} \leftrightsquigarrow \{\text{subgroups } H' : \{e_{G'}\} \subseteq H' \subseteq G'\},$ which states that subgroups of $G$ containing the kernel are in bijection with subgroups of $H'$ in the image of $f.$ The correspondence comes from taking $H \mapsto f(H) \subseteq G'$ and $f^{-1}(H') \mapsfrom H'.$
