# Theorem (Section on The Jordan Decomposition, subsection on Proof of Jordan Decomposition Theorem)

If $\dim W + \dim W' = \dim V,$ and $W \cap W' = \{\vec{0}\},$ then it must be the case that $V = W \oplus W'.$
