# Theorem (Fundamental Theorem of Symmetric Polynomials)

We have $R_n = \mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1^{(n)}, \sigma_2^{(n)}, \ldots, \sigma_n^{(n)}]$, where $\sigma_1^{(n)} = x_1 + \cdots + x_n$, $\sigma_2^{(n)} = x_1x_2 + \cdots + x_{n-1}x_n$, \ldots, $\sigma_n^{(n)} = x_1\cdots x_n$.
