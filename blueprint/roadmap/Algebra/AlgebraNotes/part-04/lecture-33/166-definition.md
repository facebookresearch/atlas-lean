# Definition (Lecture 33, Subsection: The Discriminant)

The **discriminant** of a polynomial $P(z) = z^n + a_{n-1}z^{n-1} + \cdots + a_0$ is $D = \Delta_n(-a_{n-1}, a_{n-2}, \ldots, (-1)^n a_0)$.
