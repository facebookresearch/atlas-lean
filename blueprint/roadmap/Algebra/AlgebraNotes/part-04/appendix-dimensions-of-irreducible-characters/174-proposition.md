# Proposition (Appendix: Dimensions of Irreducible Characters)

Let $\rho : G \to \operatorname{GL}(V)$ be any representation of $G$. Then if $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$ is a function such that $f(g)$ is an algebraic integer for every $g$, and $\rho(f) = r\cdot \operatorname{Id}$ for a rational number $r$, then $r$ must be an integer.
