# Lemma (Lecture 34, Subsection: Main Theorem of Algebra)

If $G = \operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{R})$ for a finite Galois extension $E/\mathbb{R}$, then $\lvert G \rvert$ is a power of $2$.
