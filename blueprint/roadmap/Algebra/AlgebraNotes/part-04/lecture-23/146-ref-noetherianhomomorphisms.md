# Lemma (\ref{noetherianhomomorphisms})

If we have a surjective homomorphism $\varphi : M \to N$ of $R$-modules, then: (1) If $M$ is finitely generated, then $N$ is also finitely generated. (2) If $N$ is finitely generated, and $K = \ker(\varphi)$ is also finitely generated, then $M$ is also finitely generated.
