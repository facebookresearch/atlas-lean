# Primitives of the Cartier dual

Let `k` be a field and `A` a commutative Hopf algebra over `k` with counit
`ε`. This is the `k`-subspace of the dual space `Hom_k(A, k)` consisting of
the linear functionals `φ` that are derivations at the counit: `φ (a b) = φ
a · ε b + ε a · φ b` for all `a, b ∈ A`. When `A` is finite-dimensional, these are the primitive elements of the
Cartier dual of `A`, whose underlying space is `Hom_k(A, k)` with the
coalgebra structure dual to the multiplication of `A`; for infinite-dimensional
`A` the full dual need not carry that coalgebra structure.
