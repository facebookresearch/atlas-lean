# Dual primitives and the cotangent space have the same dimension

Let `k` be a field and `A` a commutative and cocommutative Hopf algebra over
`k` that is finite-dimensional, with counit `ε`. The dimension over `k` of
the space of primitives of the Cartier dual (the linear functionals `φ : A →
k` with `φ (a b) = φ a · ε b + ε a · φ b`) equals the dimension over `k` of
the cotangent space `I ⧸ I ^ 2` of the augmentation ideal `I = ker ε`.
