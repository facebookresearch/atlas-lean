# A finite-dimensional complex algebra of infinite little finitistic dimension

There is a single finite-dimensional complex algebra $A$ whose little finitistic dimension is $+\infty$, meaning the supremum in $\{-\infty\}\cup\mathbb N\cup\{+\infty\}$ of the projective dimensions of the finite left $A$-modules of finite projective dimension. For every $m>0$ there is such a finite left $A$-module $N$ with $2m-2\le\operatorname{pd}_A N<+\infty$. All $m$ use the same algebra.

## Depends on

- Projective dimension in the left module category and its supremum over finite modules.

## Proof depends on

- The fixed algebra and the sequence of finite modules with finite but growing projective dimensions.

## Expansion

Port the selected orbit modules, prove a finite projective resolution bound and an independent lower bound for each $m$, and then take the supremum.
