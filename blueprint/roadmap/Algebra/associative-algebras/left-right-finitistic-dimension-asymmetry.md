# Extreme left and right finitistic-dimension asymmetry

There is a finite-dimensional complex algebra $A$ whose little and big left finitistic dimensions are both infinite, while those for $A^{\mathrm{op}}$ are both zero. The little supremum uses finite modules of finite projective dimension; the big supremum allows arbitrary modules in the stated universe. In the unbounded derived category of left $A$-modules, the injective modules in degree zero do not generate: there exists an isomorphism-closed triangulated object class closed under all small coproducts containing them but not every object. Over $A^{\mathrm{op}}$ every such class is the whole derived category.

## Depends on

- Left module categories of an algebra and its opposite, projective dimensions, and injectives generating the derived category under small coproducts.

## Proof depends on

- [Infinite little finitistic dimension](infinite-little-finitistic-dimension.md), opposite-side zero bounds and the derived-category generation comparison.

## Expansion

Identify the same split-basic algebra on both sides, carry the unbounded family across transport, and prove both opposite-side bounds and both generation polarities without conflating the big and little suprema.
