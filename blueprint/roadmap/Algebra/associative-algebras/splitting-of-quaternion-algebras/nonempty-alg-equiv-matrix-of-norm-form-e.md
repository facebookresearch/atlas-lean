# Quaternion algebras with isotropic norm form split

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic not two and $a, b \in K$ nonzero. If the norm form
$x_0^2 - a x_1^2 - b x_2^2 + a b x_3^2$ of the quaternion algebra $(a, b)_K$ is isotropic, that is,
vanishes at some nonzero $(x_0, x_1, x_2, x_3) \in K^4$, then $(a, b)_K$ splits: it is isomorphic
to the algebra $M_2(K)$ of $2 \times 2$ matrices.
