# Tate–Oort normal form in rank two

Let `R` be a commutative ring and `H` a commutative Hopf algebra over `R`
that is free of rank two as an `R`-module. Then there are an `R`-basis `1,
x` of `H` and scalars `a, b ∈ R` with `a b = 2` such that `ε(x) = 0`, `x ^ 2
= a x`, `Δ(x) = x ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ x - b (x ⊗ x)`, and the antipode sends `x` to
`x`.
