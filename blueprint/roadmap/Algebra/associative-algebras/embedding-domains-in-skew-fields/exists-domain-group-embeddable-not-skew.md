# Kourovka Problem 1.4 (domains not embeddable in skew fields)

This is the proposition that there is a ring $R$ without zero divisors (an associative unital ring with $1 \ne 0$ and no zero divisors) that admits no injective ring homomorphism into a skew field, while the multiplicative monoid of its nonzero elements admits an injective monoid homomorphism into a group.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 1.4 (A. I. Mal'cev; L. A. Bokut').
