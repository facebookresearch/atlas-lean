# A domain whose nonzero elements embed in a group but which does not embed in a skew field

There is a ring $R$ without zero divisors (an associative unital ring with $1 \ne 0$ and no zero divisors) that admits no injective ring homomorphism into a skew field, while the multiplicative monoid of its nonzero elements admits an injective monoid homomorphism into a group. Kourovka Problem 1.4 has a positive answer.
