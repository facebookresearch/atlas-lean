# Convolution-power criterion for bialgebras

Let $H$ be a commutative bialgebra over a commutative semiring $R$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. For a
commutative $R$-algebra $T$, the $R$-algebra maps $H \to T$ form a monoid under convolution
$f \star g = \mu_T \circ (f \otimes g) \circ \Delta$, with unit $h \mapsto \varepsilon(h) 1$. Then
$f^{\star m}$ is the unit for every commutative $R$-algebra $T$ and every algebra map $f : H \to T$
if and only if $\mathrm{id}_H^{\star m}$ is the unit: the identity of $H$ is the universal case.
