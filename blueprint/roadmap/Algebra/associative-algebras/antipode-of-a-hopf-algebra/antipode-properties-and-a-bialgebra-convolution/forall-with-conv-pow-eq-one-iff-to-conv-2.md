# Convolution-power criterion for Hopf algebras

The same criterion for a commutative Hopf algebra $H$ over a commutative ring $R$: for
$m \in \mathbb{N}$, the convolution power $f^{\star m}$ is the unit for every commutative
$R$-algebra $T$ and every $R$-algebra map $f : H \to T$ if and only if $\mathrm{id}_H^{\star m}$ is
the unit.
