# Comultiplication of the antipode

For a Hopf algebra $A$ over a commutative semiring $R$, with antipode $S$ and comultiplication
$\Delta$, and every $a \in A$,

$$\Delta(S(a)) = \tau\bigl((S \otimes S)(\Delta(a))\bigr),$$

where $\tau$ swaps the two tensor factors: the antipode reverses comultiplication.
