# Bracket relations of the general linear Lie algebra in dimension three

Let $A$ be a (not necessarily commutative) ring and $E = (E_{ij})_{i,j \in \{0,1,2\}}$ a family of elements of $A$. The family is a $\mathfrak{gl}_3$ bracket family when, for all $a, b, c, d \in \{0,1,2\}$,

$$E_{ab}E_{cd} - E_{cd}E_{ab} = \delta_{bc}\,E_{ad} - \delta_{da}\,E_{cb},$$

where $\delta$ is the Kronecker delta (the term is $0$ when the indices differ).
