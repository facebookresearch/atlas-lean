# The first and third Casimir elements commute

Let $A$ be a (not necessarily commutative) ring and $E = (E_{ij})_{i,j \in \{0,1,2\}}$ a family of elements of $A$ satisfying the $\mathfrak{gl}_3$ bracket relations $E_{ab}E_{cd} - E_{cd}E_{ab} = \delta_{bc}E_{ad} - \delta_{da}E_{cb}$ for all $a,b,c,d$, and let $C_1(E) = \sum_i E_{ii}$, $C_2(E) = \sum_{i,j} E_{ij}E_{ji}$, $C_3(E) = \sum_{i,j,k} E_{ij}E_{jk}E_{ki}$. Then $C_1(E)\,C_3(E) = C_3(E)\,C_1(E)$.
