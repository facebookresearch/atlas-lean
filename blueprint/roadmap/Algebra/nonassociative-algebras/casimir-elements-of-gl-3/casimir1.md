# First Casimir element of a three-by-three family

Let $A$ be a (not necessarily commutative) ring and $E = (E_{ij})_{i,j \in \{0,1,2\}}$ a family of elements of $A$. Its first Casimir element is the trace

$$C_1(E) = \sum_{i=0}^{2} E_{ii}.$$

In the motivating example the $E_{ij}$ are the archimedean derivative operators of $\mathrm{GL}_3$ acting on smooth vectors.
