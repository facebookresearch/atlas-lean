# The three Casimir elements commute with the generators and with one another

Let $A$ be a (not necessarily commutative) ring and $E = (E_{ij})_{i,j \in \{0,1,2\}}$ a family of elements of $A$ satisfying the $\mathfrak{gl}_3$ bracket relations $E_{ab}E_{cd} - E_{cd}E_{ab} = \delta_{bc}E_{ad} - \delta_{da}E_{cb}$ for all $a,b,c,d$, and let

$$C_1(E) = \sum_i E_{ii},\qquad C_2(E) = \sum_{i,j} E_{ij}E_{ji},\qquad C_3(E) = \sum_{i,j,k} E_{ij}E_{jk}E_{ki}.$$

Then each $C_m(E)$ commutes with every generator, $C_m(E)\,E_{ij} = E_{ij}\,C_m(E)$ for $m = 1,2,3$ and all $i,j$, and the three Casimir elements commute pairwise:

$$C_1C_2 = C_2C_1,\qquad C_1C_3 = C_3C_1,\qquad C_2C_3 = C_3C_2.$$

In the motivating example the generators are the archimedean derivative operators of $\mathrm{GL}_3$ acting on smooth vectors.
