# Kourovka Problem 20.109 (skew braces with perfect additive and almost simple multiplicative group)

This is the proposition that there is a finite skew brace whose additive group is perfect (generated by the commutators $x + y - x - y$) and whose multiplicative group is almost simple (isomorphic to a subgroup of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $\mathrm{Inn}(S)$ for some non-abelian finite simple group $S$) and not perfect. A skew brace is a set with a group operation $+$ (not necessarily commutative) and a group operation $\cdot$ such that $a(b + c) = ab - a + ac$ for all $a, b, c$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Problem 20.109 (C. Tsang).
