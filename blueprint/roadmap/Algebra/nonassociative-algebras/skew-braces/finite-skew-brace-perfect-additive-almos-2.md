# A finite skew brace with perfect additive group and non-perfect almost simple multiplicative group

There is a finite skew brace whose additive group is perfect (generated by the commutators $x + y - x - y$) and whose multiplicative group is almost simple (isomorphic to a subgroup of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $\mathrm{Inn}(S)$ for some non-abelian finite simple group $S$) and not perfect. A skew brace is a set with a group operation $+$ (not necessarily commutative) and a group operation $\cdot$ such that $a(b + c) = ab - a + ac$ for all $a, b, c$. Kourovka Problem 20.109 has a positive answer: Di Matteo and Ferrara construct one with additive group $\mathrm{PSU}_5(64) \times A_5$ and multiplicative group $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{PSU}_5(64))$.
