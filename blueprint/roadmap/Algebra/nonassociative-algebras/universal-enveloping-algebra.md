# Universal enveloping algebra of a Lie algebra

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $L$ a Lie algebra over $R$. Its universal enveloping algebra $U(L)$ is the
associative $R$-algebra obtained as the quotient of the tensor algebra $T(L)$ by the two-sided ideal generated by
the elements $x \otimes y - y \otimes x - [x, y]$ for $x, y \in L$.
