# Bessel polynomial

For a natural number $n$, the $n$-th classical Bessel polynomial
$y_n$ is the polynomial with rational coefficients
$y_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{(n+k)!}{2^k \, k! \, (n-k)!} \, x^k$.

## Sources

- H. L. Krall and O. Frink, "A new class of orthogonal polynomials: the Bessel polynomials", *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 65 (1949), 100–115.
