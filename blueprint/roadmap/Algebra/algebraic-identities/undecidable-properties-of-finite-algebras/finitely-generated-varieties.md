# Finitely generated varieties

A variety is finitely generated when it is the variety generated by a single finite algebra.

A [variety](varieties-of-algebras.md) $\mathcal{V}$ of a finite type is *finitely generated* when $\mathcal{V} = \mathcal{V}(A)$, the [variety generated](variety-generated-by-an-algebra.md) by $A$, for some finite algebra $A$ of that type.
