# Principal congruences

The principal congruence generated by a pair of elements of an algebra is the least congruence that identifies them.

Let $A$ be an [algebra](algebras-of-a-finite-type.md) of a finite type and $a, b \in A$. The *principal congruence* $\operatorname{Cg}^A(a, b)$ is the least [congruence](congruences-and-subdirectly-irreducible-algebras.md) of $A$ that contains the pair $(a, b)$, the intersection of all congruences of $A$ containing it.
