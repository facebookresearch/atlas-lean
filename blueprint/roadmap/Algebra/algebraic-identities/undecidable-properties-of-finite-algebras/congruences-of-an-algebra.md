# Congruences of an algebra

A congruence of an algebra is an equivalence relation on it that is compatible with all of its operations.

Let $A$ be an [algebra](algebras-of-a-finite-type.md) of a finite type. A *congruence* of $A$ is an [equivalence relation](../../../Order/ordered-sets/equivalence-relation.md) $\theta$ on $A$ such that, for every operation symbol $\omega$ of arity $n$ and all $(a_1, b_1), \dots, (a_n, b_n) \in \theta$, the pair $(\omega^A(a_1, \dots, a_n), \omega^A(b_1, \dots, b_n))$ lies in $\theta$.
