# Fixed-point form of an affine recurrence with slope different from one

Let $K$ be a field and $a, b \in K$ with $a \ne 1$. If $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to K$ satisfies $x_{n+1} = a x_n + b$ for every $n$, then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$

$$x_n = \frac{b}{1-a} + a^n\Bigl(x_0 - \frac{b}{1-a}\Bigr),$$

where $b/(1-a)$ is the unique fixed point of $t \mapsto at + b$.

Source: K. Sydsæter and P. Hammond, *Essential Mathematics for Economic Analysis*, chapter on difference equations.
