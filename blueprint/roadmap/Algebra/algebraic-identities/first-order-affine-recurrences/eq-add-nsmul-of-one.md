# Affine recurrence with slope one

Let $K$ be a field and $b \in K$. If $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to K$ satisfies $x_{n+1} = x_n + b$ for every $n$, then $x_n = x_0 + n\,b$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, where $n$ denotes the image of the natural number $n$ in $K$ (so in characteristic $p$ it is read modulo $p$).

Source: K. Sydsæter and P. Hammond, *Essential Mathematics for Economic Analysis*, chapter on difference equations.
