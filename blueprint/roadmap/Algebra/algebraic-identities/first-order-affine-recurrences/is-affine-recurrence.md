# First-order affine recurrence

For a field $K$, elements $a, b \in K$ and a sequence $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to K$, the proposition that $x$ satisfies the first-order affine recurrence with slope $a$ and intercept $b$:

$$x_{n+1} = a\,x_n + b \quad \text{for every } n \in \mathbb{N}.$$

The initial value $x_0$ is unconstrained.

Source: K. Sydsæter and P. Hammond, *Essential Mathematics for Economic Analysis*, chapter on difference equations.
