# Closed form of a first-order affine recurrence

Let $K$ be a field and $a, b \in K$. If $x \colon \mathbb{N} \to K$ satisfies $x_{n+1} = a x_n + b$ for every $n$, then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$

$$x_n = a^n x_0 + b \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a^i,$$

with no condition on $a$ (for $n = 0$ the sum is empty and $a^0 = 1$).

Source: K. Sydsæter and P. Hammond, *Essential Mathematics for Economic Analysis*, chapter on difference equations.
