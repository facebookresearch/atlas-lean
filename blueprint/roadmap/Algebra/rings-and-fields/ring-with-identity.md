# Ring with identity

A *ring with identity* is a set $R$ with an addition $+$, a multiplication $\cdot$, a negation $a \mapsto -a$ and two elements $0$ and $1$, such that:

- $R$ is an abelian group under $+$, with identity $0$ and inverse $-a$ of each $a$;
- multiplication is associative, and $1 \cdot a = a \cdot 1 = a$ for every $a \in R$;
- multiplication distributes over addition on both sides: $a(b + c) = ab + ac$ and $(a + b)c = ac + bc$;
- $0 \cdot a = a \cdot 0 = 0$ for every $a \in R$ (this follows from the other conditions).

Multiplication need not be commutative, and the ring with a single element, in which $1 = 0$, is allowed.
