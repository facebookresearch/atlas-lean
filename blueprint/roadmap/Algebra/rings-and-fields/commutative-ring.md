# Commutative ring, not necessarily with an identity element

A *commutative ring*, not necessarily with an identity element, is a set $R$ with an addition $+$, a multiplication $\cdot$, a negation $a \mapsto -a$ and an element $0$, such that:

- $R$ is an abelian group under $+$, with identity $0$ and inverse $-a$ of each $a$;
- multiplication is associative and commutative: $(ab)c = a(bc)$ and $ab = ba$ for all $a, b, c \in R$;
- multiplication distributes over addition on both sides: $a(b + c) = ab + ac$ and $(a + b)c = ac + bc$;
- $0 \cdot a = a \cdot 0 = 0$ for every $a \in R$ (this follows from the other conditions).

No multiplicative identity is required.
