# Rings and fields

Elementary theory of semirings, rings, division rings and fields: homomorphisms and isomorphisms, subrings and subfields, units, idempotents, characteristic and Frobenius, Boolean rings, formally real rings and sums of squares. Divisibility has its own field, ideals and local rings are under ring theory, and extensions of fields are under field theory.
