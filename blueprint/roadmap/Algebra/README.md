# Algebra

Algebra studies structures defined by operations and the laws, morphisms, and symmetries that organize them.

Over two fifths of the subject's articles are in [polynomials and power series](polynomials/README.md). Its largest
topic, [deep power-sum congruences](polynomials/deep-power-sum-congruences/README.md), shows that for monic
polynomials over a torsion-free $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$-algebra, congruences of the first $N$ elementary symmetric
functions modulo a divided-power ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ are equivalent to congruences of the $n$-th power sums modulo
$n\mathfrak{a}$ for $n \le N$. [Orthogonal polynomials](orthogonal-polynomials/README.md) include
[Favard's theorem](orthogonal-polynomials/favard-s-theorem/README.md) on three-term recurrences, real
[zeros of polynomials](zeros-of-polynomials/README.md) are counted by Sturm's theorem,
[Lie algebras](nonassociative-algebras/README.md) have
[Ado's theorem](nonassociative-algebras/ado-s-theorem/README.md) and, in characteristic zero, the Levi decomposition,
and [associative algebras](associative-algebras/README.md) include Frobenius's classification of the
finite-dimensional real division algebras and Jacobson's commutativity theorem.
