# The tensor-algebra lift is a graded algebra homomorphism

For a commutative semiring R, R-module M, and ℕ-graded R-algebra A with semiring carrier, let
f : M →ₗ[R] A land in degree one. The universal algebra lift T_R(M) → A defines a graded
R-algebra homomorphism from the degree family (range ι)^n to the grading of A.
