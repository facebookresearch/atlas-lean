# Descent of a unique common root

Let $L/F$ be a field extension and let $A, B \in F[X]$ with $A \neq 0$. Assume $A$ splits over $L$ with pairwise distinct roots in $L$. If $x \in L$ is a common root of $A$ and $B$, and $x$ is the only element of $L$ that is a root of both, then $x$ lies in (the image of) $F$.

The polynomial $B$ may be zero, and neither polynomial needs to be monic.
