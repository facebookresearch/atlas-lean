# Shapiro's Conjecture 10 (Forsgård–Shapiro unweighted parity bound)

This is the proposition that every real polynomial $f = \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k x^k$ of degree $n$ with $a_0, \dots, a_n > 0$ has at most $v(f)$ real zeros counted with multiplicity, where $v(f)$ is the number of parity changes between consecutive terms of the increasing list $k_1 < \dots < k_m$ of indices $0 \le k \le n$ with $c_k = a_k^2 - a_{k-1}a_{k+1}$ (with $a_{-1} = a_{n+1} = 0$) $\ge 0$.

Source: B. Shapiro, *Problems around polynomials: the good, the bad and the ugly...*, Arnold Math. J. 1 (2015), arXiv:1503.05295, Conjecture 10 (J. Forsgård, B. Shapiro).
