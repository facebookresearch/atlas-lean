# The Forsgård–Shapiro unweighted parity bound is false

It is not the case that every real polynomial $f = \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k x^k$ of degree $n$ with $a_0, \dots, a_n > 0$ has at most $v(f)$ real zeros counted with multiplicity, where $v(f)$ is the number of parity changes between consecutive terms of the increasing list $k_1 < \dots < k_m$ of indices $0 \le k \le n$ with $c_k = a_k^2 - a_{k-1}a_{k+1}$ (with $a_{-1} = a_{n+1} = 0$) $\ge 0$. The polynomial $Q_{15} = (x+1)^{13}\bigl(\tfrac{19}{360360}x^2 - \tfrac{89}{720720}x + \tfrac{83}{720720}\bigr)$ has positive coefficients and $13$ real zeros counted with multiplicity, while $c_8, c_9, c_{10} < 0$ give $v(Q_{15}) \le 11$.
