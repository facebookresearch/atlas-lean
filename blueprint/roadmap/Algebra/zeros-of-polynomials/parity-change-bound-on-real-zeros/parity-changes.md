# Parity changes of the nonnegative-difference indices

For a real polynomial $f = \sum_k a_k x^k$ of degree $n$, let $k_1 < \dots < k_m$ be the indices $0 \le k \le n$ with $c_k = a_k^2 - a_{k-1}a_{k+1}$ (with $a_{-1} = a_{n+1} = 0$) $\ge 0$. Then $v(f)$ is the number of $i < m$ such that $k_i$ and $k_{i+1}$ have different parities.

Source: B. Shapiro, *Problems around polynomials: the good, the bad and the ugly...*, Arnold Math. J. 1 (2015), arXiv:1503.05295, Conjecture 10.
