# Indices with nonnegative unweighted difference

For a real polynomial $f = \sum_k a_k x^k$ of degree $n$, the increasing list of the indices $k \in \{0, \dots, n\}$ with $c_k = a_k^2 - a_{k-1}a_{k+1}$ (with $a_{-1} = a_{n+1} = 0$) $\ge 0$.

Source: B. Shapiro, *Problems around polynomials: the good, the bad and the ugly...*, Arnold Math. J. 1 (2015), arXiv:1503.05295, Conjecture 10.
