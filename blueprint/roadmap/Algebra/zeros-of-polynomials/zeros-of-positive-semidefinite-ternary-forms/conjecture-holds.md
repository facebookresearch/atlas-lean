# Psd ternary forms of degree 2k with k^2 + 1 zeros and no indefinite square factor

For every integer $k \ge 3$ there is a real ternary form $p \in \mathbb{R}[x_0, x_1, x_2]$, homogeneous of degree $2k$ and nonnegative at every real point, which has $k^2 + 1$ pairwise non-proportional nonzero real zeros and which cannot be written as $p = h^2 q$ with $h, q \in \mathbb{R}[x_0, x_1, x_2]$ and $h$ taking both a positive and a negative value. Brugallé, Degtyarev, Itenberg and Mangolte construct, for every $k \ge 3$, a real plane curve of degree $2k$ with exactly $k^2 + 1$ real points; its equation has constant sign, and its finitely many real zeros exclude a factor $h^2$ with $h$ indefinite.
