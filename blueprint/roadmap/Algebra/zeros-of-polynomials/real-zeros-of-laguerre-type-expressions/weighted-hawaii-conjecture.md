# Shapiro's Conjecture 11 (the Hawaii conjecture for G1)

This is the proposition that for every real polynomial $p$ of degree $k \ge 2$ all of whose real zeros are simple (its non-real zeros arbitrary), the number of real zeros of $G_1 = (k-1)(p')^2 - k\,p\,p''$ (where $k = \deg p$) is at most the number of non-real zeros of $p$, both counted with multiplicity.

Source: B. Shapiro, *Problems around polynomials: the good, the bad and the ugly...*, Arnold Math. J. 1 (2015), arXiv:1503.05295, Conjecture 11.
