# The Hawaii conjecture for G1 is false

It is not the case that for every real polynomial $p$ of degree $k \ge 2$ all of whose real zeros are simple (its non-real zeros arbitrary), the number of real zeros of $G_1 = (k-1)(p')^2 - k\,p\,p''$ (where $k = \deg p$) is at most the number of non-real zeros of $p$, both counted with multiplicity. For $p(x) = (x^2 + a^2)(x + a^2)(x - 1)$ with $a \notin \{-1, 0, 1\}$ (degree $4$, simple real zeros $-a^2$ and $1$, non-real zeros $\pm ia$), $G_1$ is a positive multiple of $[(a-1)x - a(a+1)]^2\,[(a+1)x + a(a-1)]^2$, which has four real zeros counted with multiplicity.
