# Number of sign changes of a sign pattern

A sign pattern of degree $d$ is a tuple $\sigma = (\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_d) \in \{+, -\}^{d+1}$, $\sigma_k$ prescribing the sign of the coefficient of $x^k$. Its number of sign changes is the number of indices $k < d$ with $\sigma_k \ne \sigma_{k+1}$ (independent of the reading direction).

Source: B. Shapiro, *Problems around polynomials: the good, the bad and the ugly...*, Arnold Math. J. 1 (2015), arXiv:1503.05295, Conjecture 7.
