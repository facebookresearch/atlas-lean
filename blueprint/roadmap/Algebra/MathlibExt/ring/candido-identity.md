# Candido Identity

Candido polynomial identity:
`2 * (x ^ 4 + y ^ 4 + (x + y) ^ 4) = (x ^ 2 + y ^ 2 + (x + y) ^ 2) ^ 2`,
valid over any commutative semiring.
