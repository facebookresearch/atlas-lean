# Is Prime Ideal

A two-sided ideal is prime if it is proper and `a * R * b ⊆ P` forces
`a ∈ P` or `b ∈ P`. This is the noncommutative notion; Mathlib's
`Ideal.IsPrime` is the (stronger) completely prime notion.
