# Free Lie Multihomogeneous Component

Multihomogeneous component of a finitely generated free Lie algebra: the
`K`-submodule of `FreeLieAlgebra K (Fin r)` generated by homogeneous Lie
monomials in the `r` canonical generators where generator `i` occurs exactly
`degree i` times. It uses the actual `FreeLieAlgebra K (Fin r)` and
distinguishes multidegrees, not only total bracket length.
