# Bessel Polynomial

The `n`-th classical Bessel polynomial `y_n` over the rationals
(concept `jis_sem_0d5596f0982681bcb579a894`; Schork source lines 285-288):
`y_n(x) = ∑ k = 0 ^ n, ((n + k)! / (2 ^ k * k! * (n - k)!)) * x ^ k`.
