# Inverse of an equivariant equivalence is equivariant

Inverse of an equivariant equivalence is equivariant.

For types with actions of a type `T`, let `e : X ≃ Y` satisfy `e (t • x) = t • e x`. Then
`e.symm (t • y) = t • e.symm y` for all `t` and `y`, with independent action and carrier
universes.
