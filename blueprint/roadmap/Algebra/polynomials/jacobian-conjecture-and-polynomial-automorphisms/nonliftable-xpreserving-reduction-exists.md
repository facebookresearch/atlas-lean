# Vénéreau's question on x-preserving automorphisms modulo t squared

This is the proposition that there are $k \ge 2$, a reduced commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra $R$ and an automorphism $\gamma$ of the $R[t]$-algebra $R[t][x, y_1, \dots, y_k]$ whose reduction modulo $t^2$ fixes $x$ ($\gamma(x) \equiv x \bmod t^2$), such that no automorphism $\delta$ of $R[t][x, y_1, \dots, y_k]$ with $\delta(x) = x$ has the same reduction modulo $t^2$ as $\gamma$ on every variable (with $d = k + 1 \ge 3$ variables).

Source: S. Vénéreau (with A. van den Essen and S. Maubach), *The special automorphism group of R[t]/(t^m)[x_1,...,x_n] and coordinates of a subring of R[t][x_1,...,x_n]*, in Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, p. 25.
