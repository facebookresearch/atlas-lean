# Laplacian on complex polynomials

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $\mathbb{C}$-linear map $\Delta = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \partial^2/\partial X_i^2$ on $\mathbb{C}[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ (formal partial derivatives).

Source: Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, abstract on the Jacobian conjecture, Conjecture 5.1 (W. Zhao).
