# Truncated polynomial ring

For a commutative ring $R$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the quotient ring $R[t]/(t^m)$.

Source: S. Vénéreau (with A. van den Essen and S. Maubach), *The special automorphism group of R[t]/(t^m)[x_1,...,x_n] and coordinates of a subring of R[t][x_1,...,x_n]*, in Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, p. 26.
