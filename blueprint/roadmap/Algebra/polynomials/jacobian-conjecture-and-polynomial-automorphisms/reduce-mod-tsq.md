# Reduction of polynomial coefficients modulo t squared

For a commutative ring $R$ and a set of variables $\sigma$, the ring homomorphism $R[t][x_s : s \in \sigma] \to (R[t]/(t^2))[x_s : s \in \sigma]$ reducing every coefficient modulo $t^2$.

Source: S. Vénéreau (with A. van den Essen and S. Maubach), *The special automorphism group of R[t]/(t^m)[x_1,...,x_n] and coordinates of a subring of R[t][x_1,...,x_n]*, in Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, p. 25.
