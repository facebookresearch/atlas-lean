# Polynomial expansion is finite and flat of rank a power

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\sigma$ a finite set of variables and $p \in \mathbb{N}$ with $0 < p$. Let $\mathrm{expand}_p : R[X_i : i \in \sigma] \to R[X_i : i \in \sigma]$ be the $R$-algebra endomorphism $X_i \mapsto X_i^p$. Then:

- $\mathrm{expand}_p$ is finite (the target is a finitely generated module over the source);
- $\mathrm{expand}_p$ is flat;
- for every prime ideal $\mathfrak{q}$ of the source, the rank of the target localized at $\mathfrak{q}$, as a module over the localization of the source at $\mathfrak{q}$, equals $p^{|\sigma|}$.

The monomials $X^r$ with $0 \le r_i < p$ for every $i$ form a free basis of the target over the image of $\mathrm{expand}_p$.
