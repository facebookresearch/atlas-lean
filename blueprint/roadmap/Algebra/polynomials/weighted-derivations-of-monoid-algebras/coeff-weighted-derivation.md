# Coefficients of a weighted derivation

Let $w : \Gamma \to R$ be an additive map, the weight, and $D_w$ the weighted derivation of the
additive monoid algebra $R[\Gamma]$. For every $f \in R[\Gamma]$ and $g \in \Gamma$, the
coefficient of $D_w(f)$ at $g$ is $w(g)$ times the coefficient of $f$ at $g$.
