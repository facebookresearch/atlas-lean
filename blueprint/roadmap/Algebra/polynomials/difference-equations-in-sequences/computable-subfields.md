# Computable subfields

A computable subfield of a field is a subfield whose elements are coded by finite strings so that equality and the field operations are computable; decision problems about difference equations take their coefficients from one.

A *computable subfield* $k_0$ of a field $k$ is a subfield whose elements are encoded by finite strings so that equality and the field operations are computable. The field $k$ itself may be uncountable.

## Expansion

The rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$, with fractions in lowest terms, form a computable subfield of every field of characteristic zero; the undecidability results for difference equations hold for every computable subfield, so already for $\mathbb{Q}$.
