# Computable subfields

A computable subfield of a field is a subfield that is a computable field: its elements are associated with natural numbers so that the field operations become computable functions; decision problems about difference equations take their coefficients from one.

A field $F$ is *computable* when its elements are associated with natural numbers in such a way that the field operations become computable functions on these numbers. A *computable subfield* $k_0$ of a field $k$ is a subfield of $k$ that is a computable field. The field $k$ itself may be uncountable.

## Expansion

The rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$, with fractions in lowest terms, form a computable subfield of every field of characteristic zero; the undecidability results for difference equations hold for every computable subfield, so already for $\mathbb{Q}$.
