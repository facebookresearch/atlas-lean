# Jacobian determinant of a polynomial endomorphism

For a commutative ring $A$ and an $A$-algebra endomorphism $\varphi$ of $A[y_1, \dots, y_n]$, the polynomial $\det\bigl(\partial \varphi(y_i)/\partial y_j\bigr)$ (formal partial derivatives).

Source: S. Vénéreau (with A. van den Essen and S. Maubach), *The special automorphism group of R[t]/(t^m)[x_1,...,x_n] and coordinates of a subring of R[t][x_1,...,x_n]*, in Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, p. 26.
