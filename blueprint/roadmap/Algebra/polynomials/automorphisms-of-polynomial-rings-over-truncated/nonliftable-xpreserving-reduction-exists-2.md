# x-preserving reductions modulo t squared lift to x-preserving automorphisms

It is not the case that there are $k \ge 2$, a reduced commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra $R$ and an automorphism $\gamma$ of the $R[t]$-algebra $R[t][x, y_1, \dots, y_k]$ whose reduction modulo $t^2$ fixes $x$ ($\gamma(x) \equiv x \bmod t^2$), such that no automorphism $\delta$ of $R[t][x, y_1, \dots, y_k]$ with $\delta(x) = x$ has the same reduction modulo $t^2$ as $\gamma$ on every variable. The answer to Vénéreau's question is negative: by the reduction to Question 1 (the Jacobian of a lift lies in $R^\times$ for reduced $R$, and $x$ can be absorbed into the coefficient ring), every such reduction comes from an $x$-fixing automorphism.
