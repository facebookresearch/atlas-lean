# Vénéreau's Question 1 (reduction of special automorphisms modulo t^m)

This is the proposition that for every commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra $R$, every $m \ge 1$ and every $n$, each automorphism $\psi$ of the $R[t]/(t^m)$-algebra $(R[t]/(t^m))[y_1, \dots, y_n]$ with Jacobian determinant $1$ is the coefficientwise reduction modulo $t^m$ of an automorphism $\varphi$ of the $R[t]$-algebra $R[t][y_1, \dots, y_n]$ with Jacobian determinant $1$ (that is, $\varphi(y_i) \bmod t^m = \psi(y_i)$ for every $i$).

Source: S. Vénéreau (with A. van den Essen and S. Maubach), *The special automorphism group of R[t]/(t^m)[x_1,...,x_n] and coordinates of a subring of R[t][x_1,...,x_n]*, in Affine Algebraic Geometry, Oberwolfach Reports 4 (2007), no. 1, p. 26, Question 1.
