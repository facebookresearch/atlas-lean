# Coefficient L1 norm as a sum over a larger index set

Let $p$ be a multivariate polynomial with rational coefficients and $S$ a finite set of monomials
containing the support of $p$. Then the coefficient $L^1$ norm of $p$, the sum of the absolute
values of its coefficients, equals $\sum_{m \in S} |p_m|$, where $p_m$ is the coefficient of $m$.
