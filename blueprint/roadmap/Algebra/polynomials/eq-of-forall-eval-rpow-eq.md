# Bivariate complex polynomials agreeing on infinitely many real-power fibres are equal

Let $N \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N > 1$, let $u_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, let $S \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be infinite, and let $P, Q \in \mathbb{C}[X_0, X_1]$. If

$$P(x, N^u) = Q(x, N^u)$$

for every $x \in S$ and every real $u > u_0$ (with $N^u$ the real power, viewed in $\mathbb{C}$), then $P = Q$.
