# Twisted telescoping for an iterated ring endomorphism

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $s : R \to R$ a ring endomorphism and $\ell \in \mathbb{N}$ with $s^{\ell}(r) = r$ for all $r \in R$, where $s^{i}$ is the $i$-fold iterate. If $x, \lambda, u \in R$ satisfy $s(x) = \lambda x + u$, then

$$
\Bigl(1 - \prod_{i=0}^{\ell-1} s^{i}(\lambda)\Bigr)\, x = \sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1} \Bigl(\prod_{i=j+1}^{\ell-1} s^{i}(\lambda)\Bigr)\, s^{j}(u).
$$
