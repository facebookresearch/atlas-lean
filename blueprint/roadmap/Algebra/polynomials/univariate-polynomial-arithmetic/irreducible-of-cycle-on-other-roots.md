# Irreducibility from a cycle on all but one root

Let $L/F$ be a field extension and let $P \in F[X]$ be monic and split over $L$. Let $\sigma$ be an $F$-algebra automorphism of $L$, $y_0 \in L$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $r_0, \dots, r_{n-1} \in L$. Assume the roots of $P$ in $L$, counted with multiplicity, are exactly $y_0, r_0, \dots, r_{n-1}$, and that they are pairwise distinct. If $\sigma(r_i) = r_{(i+1) \bmod n}$ for every $i < n$ and $y_0 \notin F$, then $P$ is irreducible over $F$.
