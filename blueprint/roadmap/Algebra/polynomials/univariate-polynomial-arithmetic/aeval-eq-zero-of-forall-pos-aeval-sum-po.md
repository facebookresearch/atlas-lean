# A polynomial annihilating a matrix family for all positive parameters annihilates its constant term

Let $R, D \in \mathbb{N}$, let $N_0, \dots, N_D$ be complex $R \times R$ matrices and let $q \in \mathbb{C}[X]$. If

$$
q\Bigl(\sum_{a=0}^{D} y^{a} N_a\Bigr) = 0 \quad \text{for every real } y > 0,
$$

then $q(N_0) = 0$.
