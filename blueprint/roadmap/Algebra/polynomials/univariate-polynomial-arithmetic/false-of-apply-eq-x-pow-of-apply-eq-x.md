# No pair of polynomial-ring identifications swaps a variable and its power

Let $p \ge 2$, let $k$ be a nontrivial commutative ring and $R$ a commutative ring. There are no ring isomorphisms $e_0, e_1 : R \to k[X]$ and elements $a, b \in R$ with

$$
e_0(a) = X,\quad e_0(b) = X^p,\quad e_1(b) = X,\quad e_1(a) = X^p .
$$
