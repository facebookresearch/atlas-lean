# Univariate polynomial arithmetic

Arithmetic of polynomials in one indeterminate: coefficients and monomials, degree and leading terms, evaluation and composition, formal differentiation and Taylor expansion, and division with remainder. Multivariate polynomials, special irreducibility criteria and formal power series remain in the parent field; zeros and their multiplicities have their own field.
