# Wronskian of a finite family of polynomials

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $P_0, \dots, P_{r-1} \in R[X]$. The Wronskian of the family is the polynomial

$$\operatorname{Wr}[P_0, \dots, P_{r-1}] = \det\bigl(P_i^{(j)}\bigr)_{0 \le i, j < r},$$

where $P_i^{(j)}$ is the $j$-th formal derivative of $P_i$. For the empty family ($r = 0$) it is the determinant of the empty matrix, $1$; for $r = 2$ it is $P_0 P_1' - P_0' P_1$.
