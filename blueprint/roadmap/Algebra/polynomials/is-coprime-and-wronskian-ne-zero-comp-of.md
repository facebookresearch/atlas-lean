# Composition of rational functions preserves coprimality and a nonzero Wronskian

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and write $W(f, g) = f g' - f' g$ for the Wronskian. Let $u, v \in k[X]$ be coprime with $\max(\deg u, \deg v) > 0$ and $W(u, v) \neq 0$, and let $s, t \in k[X]$ be coprime with $d = \max(\deg s, \deg t) > 0$ and $W(s, t) \neq 0$. Let $U, V \in k[X]$ satisfy, for every $x \in k$ with $v(x) \neq 0$,

$$
U(x) = v(x)^{d}\, s\!\left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}\right), \qquad V(x) = v(x)^{d}\, t\!\left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}\right).
$$

Then $U$ and $V$ are coprime, $\max(\deg U, \deg V) > 0$, and $W(U, V) \neq 0$.
