# Multiplicative functions given by resultants

Let `K` be a field, `n` an even natural number, and `D` a function from
integer polynomials to `ℚ` with `D(1) = 1`. Call a monic integer polynomial
admissible if its constant coefficient is nonzero in `K`. Suppose that `D(G
H) = D(G) D(H)` for all admissible `G` and `H`, and that for every `b` there
is a polynomial `N` over `ℚ` in the variables `x_0, …, x_b`, of total degree
at most `n` and with coefficient `1` on `x_0 ^ n`, such that `D(G) = N(g_0,
…, g_b)` for every admissible `G` of degree `b + 1` with coefficients `g_i`.
Then there is a monic rational polynomial `P` of degree `n` such that `D(G)
= Res(G, P)` for every admissible `G`.
