# Gauss norm log eval

Let `K` be an algebraically closed field with a nonarchimedean absolute
value `v`, and let `p` be a nonzero polynomial over `K`. For every `z ∈ K`
with `v z ≤ 1` and `p(z) ≠ 0`, `log v(p(z)) = log ‖p‖ + ∑ log v(z - a)`,
where `‖p‖` is the Gauss norm of `p` (the maximum of `v` over its
coefficients) and the sum runs over the roots `a` of `p` with `v a ≤ 1`,
counted with multiplicity.
