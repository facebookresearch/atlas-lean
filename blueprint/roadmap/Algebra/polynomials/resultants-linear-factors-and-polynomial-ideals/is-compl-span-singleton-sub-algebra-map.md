# Complementary ideals from a root of a cyclotomic polynomial

If `ξ` in a commutative `A`-algebra `B` is a root of the `ℓ`-th cyclotomic polynomial,
then for a nontrivial `ℓ`-th root of unity `a` in the domain `A` (with `ℓ`
prime and a unit in `A`; then `a` itself is a primitive `ℓ`-th root), the
principal ideal generated by `ξ - a` is complementary to the principal ideal
generated by the product of `ξ - μ` over the remaining primitive `ℓ`-th
roots `μ`.
