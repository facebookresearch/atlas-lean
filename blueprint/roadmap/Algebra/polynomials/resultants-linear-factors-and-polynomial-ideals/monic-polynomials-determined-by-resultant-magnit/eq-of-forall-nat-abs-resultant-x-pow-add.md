# Monic integer polynomials determined by resultant magnitudes

Let `P` and `Q` be monic polynomials with integer coefficients of the same
degree, and let `S` be a set of integers that is unbounded above and
unbounded below. Suppose that for every positive integer `n` and every `c ∈
S` with `Res(X ^ n + c, P) ≠ 0`, the absolute value of the integer `Res(X ^
n + c, Q)` is at most that of `Res(X ^ n + c, P)`. Then `P = Q`.
