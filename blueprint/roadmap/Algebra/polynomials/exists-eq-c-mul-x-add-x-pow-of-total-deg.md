# Rigidity of a binary rational polynomial under a dilation

Let $g, n, \alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \ge 2$, $\alpha + \beta = n^2$ and $\alpha = \beta + n$. Let $P \in \mathbb{Q}[X_0, X_1]$ have total degree at most $g$, and suppose that for all natural numbers $a, b$,

$$P(\alpha a + \beta b,\ \beta a + \alpha b) = n^{2g}\, P(a, b)$$

(evaluation in $\mathbb{Q}$ at the images of these natural numbers). Then there is $c \in \mathbb{Q}$ with

$$P = c\,(X_0 + X_1)^g.$$
