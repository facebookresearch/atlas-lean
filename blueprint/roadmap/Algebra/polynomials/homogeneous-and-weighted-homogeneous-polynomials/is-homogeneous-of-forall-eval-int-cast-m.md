# Homogeneity from scaling on the integer grid

Let $R$ be an integral domain of characteristic zero, $\iota$ any set of variables (not necessarily finite or nonempty), $P \in R[X_i : i \in \iota]$ and $d \in \mathbb{N}$. If for every integer $n$ and every integer point $c \colon \iota \to \mathbb{Z}$

$$P(n c) = n^d\, P(c)$$

(with integers mapped into $R$), then $P$ is homogeneous of degree $d$. In particular $P = 0$ is allowed and is homogeneous of every degree.
