# A multiset is determined by its size and positive power sums

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $s, t$ be finite multisets of elements of $K$ with the same number of elements (counted with multiplicity). If

$$\sum_{x \in s} x^n = \sum_{x \in t} x^n \quad \text{for every integer } n \ge 1,$$

then $s = t$. Repeated entries and zero entries are allowed; the size hypothesis is what fixes the multiplicity of $0$.
