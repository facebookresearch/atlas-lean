# Split monic polynomials are determined by the power sums of their roots

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $P, Q \in K[X]$ be monic polynomials of the same degree, each splitting into linear factors over $K$. If for every integer $n \ge 1$ the sum of the $n$-th powers of the roots of $P$ (with multiplicity) equals that of $Q$, then $P = Q$.
