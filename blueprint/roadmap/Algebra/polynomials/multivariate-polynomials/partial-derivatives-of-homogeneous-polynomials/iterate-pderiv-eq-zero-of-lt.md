# High-order partial derivatives of a homogeneous polynomial vanish

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $\sigma$ a set of variables, $k \in \sigma$ and $\varphi \in R[X_i : i \in \sigma]$ homogeneous of degree $n$. If $i \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfies $n < i$, then the $i$-fold partial derivative vanishes:

$$\partial_k^{\,i}\varphi = 0.$$
