# Vertices of a Minkowski sum of Newton polygons are exponents of the product

Let `f` and `g` be bivariate polynomials over a ring without zero divisors. If `a + b`, with `a` an exponent of `f` and `b` an exponent of `g`, is an extreme point of `New(f) + New(g)`, then `a + b` is an exponent of `f g`: any other decomposition `a + b = x + y` would make `a + b` the midpoint of `x + b` and `a + y`, so the coefficient of `x^{a+b}` in `f g` is the single nonzero product `f_a g_b`.
