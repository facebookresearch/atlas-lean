# Values of a rational polynomial at multiples of high powers

Let $p \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ and let $N$ be a nonzero integer such that every prime dividing the denominator of some coefficient of $p$ divides $N$. Then there is $K_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $K \ge K_0$ and all $m \in \mathbb{Z}$ there is $z \in \mathbb{Z}$ with

$$
p(N^K m) = p(0) + N^{K - K_0}\, z .
$$
