# Distinct linear factors

Let `K` be a field and `I` an ideal of `K[X]` containing a separable
polynomial. Let `S` be a finite subset of `K`, let `n : K → ℕ` satisfy `n a
> 0` for every `a ∈ S`, let `c ∈ K` be nonzero, and put `f = c ∏_{a ∈ S} (X
- a) ^ (n a)`. If `f ∈ I` and `I` is contained in the radical of the ideal
`(f)`, then `I` is generated by the product of distinct linear factors `∏_{a
∈ S} (X - a)`.
