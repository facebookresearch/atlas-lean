# Rational representations of a cyclic group via cyclotomic torsion

Let `n > 0`, and let `M` and `N` be finite-dimensional `ℚ`-vector spaces
carrying compatible `ℚ[X]`-module structures, both annihilated by `X ^ n -
1`. If, for every divisor `d` of `n`, the `ℚ`-dimensions of the
`Φ_d`-torsion submodules of `M` and `N` agree, where `Φ_d` is the `d`-th
cyclotomic polynomial, then `M` and `N` are isomorphic as `ℚ[X]`-modules.
Equivalently, rational representations of a cyclic group of order `n` are
determined by these dimensions.
