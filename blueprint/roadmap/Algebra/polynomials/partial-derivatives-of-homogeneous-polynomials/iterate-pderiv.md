# Iterated partial derivatives of a homogeneous polynomial

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $\sigma$ a set of variables, $k \in \sigma$ and $\varphi \in R[X_i : i \in \sigma]$ homogeneous of degree $n$. Then for every $j \in \mathbb{N}$ the $j$-fold partial derivative $\partial_k^{\,j}\varphi$ is homogeneous of degree $n - j$, where the subtraction is truncated at $0$ (so the degree is $0$ when $j \ge n$).
