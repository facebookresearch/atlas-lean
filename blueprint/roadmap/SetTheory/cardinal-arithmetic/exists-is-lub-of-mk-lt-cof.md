# Small subsets of a well-order have least upper bounds

Let $\alpha$ be a well-ordered set (a linear order whose strict order is well-founded) and let $\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)$ be its cofinality, the least cardinality of a cofinal subset of $\alpha$. If $t \subseteq \alpha$ has cardinality $|t| < \operatorname{cf}(\alpha)$, then $t$ has a least upper bound in $\alpha$. (If $\alpha$ has a greatest element then $\operatorname{cf}(\alpha) = 1$ and $t$ must be empty, whose least upper bound is the least element of $\alpha$; if $\alpha$ is empty there is no such $t$.)
