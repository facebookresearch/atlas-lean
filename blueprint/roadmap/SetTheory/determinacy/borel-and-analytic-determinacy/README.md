# Borel and analytic determinacy

ZFC proves that all Borel games are determined. It does not prove that all analytic games are determined. Martin proved Borel determinacy, with a proof that needs the axiom of replacement, and derived analytic determinacy from a measurable cardinal. Projective determinacy follows from infinitely many Woodin cardinals (Martin and Steel, 1989).
