# Martin's analytic determinacy theorem

If there is a measurable cardinal, every analytic
subset of Baire space `ℕ^ℕ` is determined (D. A. Martin, *Measurable cardinals and analytic
games*, Fund. Math. 66 (1970) 287–291; Moschovakis, *Descriptive Set Theory*, 2nd ed., AMS 2009,
Theorem 6G.7, from the partition property that a measurable cardinal has by 6G.2 and 6G.5;
Woodin, *The continuum hypothesis, Part I*, Notices AMS 48 (2001)). The measurable cardinal may
live in any universe.

The hypothesis cannot be removed: ZFC does not prove the determinacy of all analytic sets. Projective determinacy needs still stronger hypotheses: Martin and Steel (*A proof of projective determinacy*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 2 (1989)) proved that infinitely many Woodin cardinals make every projective set determined (as quoted in Woodin, *The continuum hypothesis, Part I*, Notices AMS 48 (2001)).

## Informally uses

- [Gale–Stewart theorem, open case](../gale-stewart-games/determined-of-is-open.md): in Martin's proof player II makes auxiliary moves in the measurable cardinal (Moschovakis, proof of 6G.7), and the analytic set is replaced by an open set of plays on this larger set of moves, determined by the Gale–Stewart theorem (Woodin, *The continuum hypothesis, Part I*, Notices AMS 48 (2001)).
