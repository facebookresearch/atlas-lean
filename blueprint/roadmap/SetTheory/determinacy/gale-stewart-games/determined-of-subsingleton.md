# Games with a single move are determined

If `A` has exactly one element, there is a
single play, and every payoff set is determined: player I wins if it contains that play and
player II wins otherwise. This degenerate case shows why the existence of non-determined sets
needs at least two moves.

## Background

- [Existence of a non-determined game](../non-determined-games/exists-not-determined.md): its hypothesis of at least two moves is needed, as this case shows.
