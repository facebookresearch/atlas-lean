# Infinite games and determinacy

Infinite two-player games of perfect information on A^ℕ: the Gale–Stewart theorem for open and closed games, Martin's Borel determinacy, analytic determinacy from a measurable cardinal, the failure of the axiom of determinacy under the axiom of choice, and the regularity properties (perfect set property, property of Baire, measurability) that determinacy gives to sets of reals. The Banach–Mazur game on the reals is under Topology/descriptive-set-theory.

## Related topics
