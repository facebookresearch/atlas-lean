# Closure points and clubs of countable subsets

Let $F$ map the finite subsets of a set $\alpha$ to elements of $\alpha$. A closure point of $F$ is
a countable set $x \subseteq \alpha$ with $F(s) \in x$ for every finite $s \subseteq x$, and a
closure point of a sequence $(F_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of such maps is a countable set that is a
closure point of every $F_n$. The closure points of the constant sequence $F_n = F$ are exactly
the closure points of $F$.
