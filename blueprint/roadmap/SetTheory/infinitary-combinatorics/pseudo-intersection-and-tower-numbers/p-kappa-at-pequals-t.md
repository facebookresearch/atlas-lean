# Tsaban Problem 11.4 (p_p equals t)

The proposition that $\mathfrak{p}_{\mathfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{t}$, where $\mathfrak{p}$ is the pseudo-intersection number, $\mathfrak{t}$ the tower number, and $\mathfrak{p}_\kappa$ the [infimum](p-kappa.md) of the sizes of the centered families of subsets of $\mathbb{N}$ that cannot be split into fewer than $\kappa$ pieces each having a pseudo-intersection ($0$ when there is none).

Source: B. Tsaban, Selection principles and special sets of reals, in Open Problems in Topology II (E. Pearl, ed.), Elsevier 2007, 91–108; revised version arXiv:math/0609601, Problem 11.4.
