# The cardinal p_p equals t

$\mathfrak{p}_{\mathfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{t}$: the least size of a centered family of subsets of $\mathbb{N}$ that cannot be split into fewer than $\mathfrak{p}$ pieces each having a pseudo-intersection equals the tower number. This resolves Tsaban's Problem 11.4 positively: by the theorem of Malliaris and Shelah, $\mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{t}$, and $\mathfrak{p}_{\mathfrak{t}} = \mathfrak{t}$, so $\mathfrak{p}_{\mathfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{p}_{\mathfrak{t}} = \mathfrak{t}$.
