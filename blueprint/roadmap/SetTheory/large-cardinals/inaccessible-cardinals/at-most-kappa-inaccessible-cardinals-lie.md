# At most $\kappa$ inaccessible cardinals lie below $\kappa$

For every cardinal $\kappa$, there are at most $\kappa$ inaccessible cardinals below $\kappa$.

Proof: identifying each cardinal with its initial ordinal, the inaccessible cardinals below
$\kappa$ form a subset of the set of ordinals below $\kappa$, which has cardinality $\kappa$.
