# Under GCH, weakly inaccessible cardinals are inaccessible

Let $\kappa$ be a weakly inaccessible cardinal, and assume the generalized continuum hypothesis
below $\kappa$: $2^\mu = \mu^+$ for every infinite cardinal $\mu < \kappa$. Then $\kappa$ is
inaccessible.

Proof: for infinite $\mu < \kappa$, $2^\mu = \mu^+ < \kappa$ because $\kappa$ is a limit cardinal;
for finite $\mu$, $2^\mu$ is finite. So $\kappa$ is a strong limit, and it is uncountable and
regular by assumption.
