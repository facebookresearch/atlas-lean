# Least cardinal carrying a nontrivial measure

For a cardinal $\kappa$, the proposition that $\kappa$ is the least cardinal carrying a nontrivial measure: $\kappa$ carries a nontrivial measure (some set of cardinality $\kappa$ has a countably additive probability measure on all its subsets vanishing on singletons), and no cardinal $c < \kappa$ does.

Source: Jech.
