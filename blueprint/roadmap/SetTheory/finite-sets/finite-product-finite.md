# The Cartesian product of two finite sets is finite

Let $s$ be a subset of a set $X$ and $t$ a subset of a set $Y$. If $s$ and $t$ are finite, then their Cartesian product $s \times t = \{(x, y) \in X \times Y : x \in s,\ y \in t\}$ is finite.
