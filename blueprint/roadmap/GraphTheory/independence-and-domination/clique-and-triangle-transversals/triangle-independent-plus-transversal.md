# Erdős Problem 621 (triangle-independent sets and triangle transversals)

The statement that for every natural number $n$ and every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $\alpha_1(G) + \tau_1(G) \le n^2/4$, where $\alpha_1(G)$ is the largest size of a set of edges containing at most one edge of every triangle and $\tau_1(G)$ the least size of a set of edges containing at least one edge of every triangle. Stated here as a proposition.
