# Erdős Problem 610, first question

The statement that there is a function $\omega$ from the natural numbers to the reals with $\omega(n) \to \infty$ such that $\tau(G) \le n - \omega(n)\sqrt{n}$ for every natural number $n$ and every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$. Here $\tau(G)$ is the least size of a set of vertices meeting every maximal clique of $G$ with at least two vertices. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 610, first question](clique-transversal-weak-bound-holds.md).
