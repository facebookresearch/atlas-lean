# Erdős Problem 610, second question

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ such that $\tau(G) \le n - c\sqrt{n \log n}$ (natural logarithm) for every natural number $n$ and every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$. Here $\tau(G)$ is the least size of a set of vertices meeting every maximal clique of $G$ with at least two vertices. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 610, second question](clique-transversal-strong-bound-holds.md).

The statement quantifies over every $n$, with the convention $\log 0 = 0$.
