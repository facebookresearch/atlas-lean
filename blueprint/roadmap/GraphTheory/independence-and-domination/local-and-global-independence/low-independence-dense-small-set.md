# Alon's theorem on dense small sets, as a statement (Erdős Problem 801 variant)

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ whose independent sets all have fewer than $\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor$ vertices has a set $S$ of at most $\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor$ vertices inducing at least $c\sqrt{n}\log n$ edges (natural logarithm). This is the strict-boundary form of Erdős Problem 801: independence number below $\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$ rather than at most $\sqrt n$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Alon's theorem on dense small sets in graphs of small independence number](low-independence-dense-small-set-holds.md).
