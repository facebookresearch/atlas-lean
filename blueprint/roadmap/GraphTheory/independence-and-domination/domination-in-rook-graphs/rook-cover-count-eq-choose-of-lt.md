# Every dense placement covers all rows and columns

Let $a, b, k$ be natural numbers with $(a - 1)b < k$ and $a(b - 1) < k$, where $a - 1$ and $b - 1$ are truncated subtraction (read as $0$ when $a = 0$, respectively $b = 0$). Then the number $E_{a,b}(k)$ of $k$-element sets of cells of the $a \times b$ board that contain a cell in every row and a cell in every column equals $\binom{ab}{k}$: every $k$-element set of cells meets every row and every column.
