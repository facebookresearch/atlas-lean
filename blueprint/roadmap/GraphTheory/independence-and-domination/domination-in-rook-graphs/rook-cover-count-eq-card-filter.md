# Covering rook placements by their row and column projections

For all natural numbers $n, m, k$, the number $E_{n,m}(k)$ of $k$-element sets of cells of the $n \times m$ board that contain a cell in every row and a cell in every column equals the number of $k$-element sets $s$ of cells whose projection to the rows, the set of first coordinates of its cells, is all of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and whose projection to the columns, the set of second coordinates, is all of $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$.
