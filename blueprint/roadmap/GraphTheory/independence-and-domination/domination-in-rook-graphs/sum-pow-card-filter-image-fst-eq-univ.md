# Generating polynomial of placements meeting every row

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite types, viewed as the rows and the columns of the board $\alpha \times \beta$, let $R$ be a commutative ring and $x \in R$. Summing over the finite sets $s \subseteq \alpha \times \beta$ that meet every row (for every $a \in \alpha$ there is $b$ with $(a, b) \in s$),

$$\sum_{s} x^{|s|} = \bigl((1 + x)^{|\beta|} - 1\bigr)^{|\alpha|}.$$
