# Generating polynomial of placements meeting every column

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite types, viewed as the rows and the columns of the board $\alpha \times \beta$, let $R$ be a commutative ring and $x \in R$. Summing over the finite sets $s \subseteq \alpha \times \beta$ that meet every column (for every $b \in \beta$ there is $a$ with $(a, b) \in s$),

$$\sum_{s} x^{|s|} = \bigl((1 + x)^{|\alpha|} - 1\bigr)^{|\beta|}.$$
