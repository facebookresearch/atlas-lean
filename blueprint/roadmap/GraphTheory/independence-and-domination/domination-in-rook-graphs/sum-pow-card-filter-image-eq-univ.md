# Generating polynomial of placements meeting every row and every column

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite types, viewed as the rows and the columns of the board $\alpha \times \beta$, let $R$ be a commutative ring and $x \in R$. Summing over the finite sets $s \subseteq \alpha \times \beta$ that meet every row and every column,

$$\sum_{s} x^{|s|} = (-1)^{|\alpha| + |\beta|} \sum_{\ell=0}^{|\alpha|} \sum_{k=0}^{|\beta|} \binom{|\alpha|}{\ell} \binom{|\beta|}{k} (-1)^{k+\ell} (1 + x)^{k\ell}.$$
