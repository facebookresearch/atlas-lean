# Iterated logarithm

For a real number $x$, $\log^* x$ is the least number of times the natural logarithm must be applied to $x$ to obtain a value below $1$: the least natural number $k$ with $\log^{(k)} x < 1$, where $\log^{(k)}$ is the $k$-fold iterate of the natural logarithm (as a real function, with $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log |x|$). It is $0$ if no such $k$ exists.
