# Resolution of Erdős Problem 927

It is not the case that there is a real $C$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, $|g(n) - (n - \log_2 n - \log^* n)| \le C$, where $g(n)$ is the largest number of distinct sizes of maximal cliques of a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ (a maximal clique being a finite clique contained in no larger finite clique) and $\log^* x$ is the least number of times the natural logarithm must be applied to $x$ to obtain a value below $1$. Equivalently, for every real $C$ there are infinitely many $n$ with $|g(n) - (n - \log_2 n - \log^* n)| > C$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 927 (number of sizes of maximal cliques)](clique-size-count-asymptotic.md) is false.
