# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1**: the sequence `g(n) = n! * C(n-a+b, b)`
(`n ≥ a`) has exponential generating function
`x^a * (1 - x)^{-(b+1)}` (stated in the multiplied-out
form `(1 - x)^{b+1} * A(x) = x^a` over `ℚ`).
