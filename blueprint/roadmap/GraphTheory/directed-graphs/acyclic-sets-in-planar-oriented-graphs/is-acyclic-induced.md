# Acyclic vertex sets of a digraph

For a digraph $D$ on a vertex type $V$, a finite set $S \subseteq V$ is acyclic if the subdigraph induced on $S$ has no directed closed walk of positive length: for no vertex $v$ is $v$ related to itself by the transitive closure of the induced arc relation ($u$ related to $w$ when $u, w \in S$ and $u \to w$). For a loopless digraph this says that $S$ contains no directed cycle.
