# Arc relation induced on a vertex subset

For a digraph $D$ on a vertex type $V$ and a finite set $S \subseteq V$, the induced arc relation on $S$ relates $u$ to $v$ exactly when $u \in S$, $v \in S$ and $u \to v$ is an arc of $D$. It is a relation on all of $V$ that relates no vertex outside $S$.
