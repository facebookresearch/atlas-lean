# Oriented graphs

A digraph $D$ on a vertex type $V$, given by its arc relation $u \to v$, is oriented if it has no loop ($v \to v$ holds for no $v$) and never contains both orientations of a pair: for all $u, v \in V$, $u \to v$ implies that $v \to u$ fails. Since the arcs form a relation, there are no parallel arcs.
