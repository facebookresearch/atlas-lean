# The tournament $\Delta(T_2, T_2, T_2)$

The tournament $\Delta(T_2, T_2, T_2)$ has vertices $(i, j)$ with $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ and $j \in \{0, 1\}$, and $(i, j) \to (i', j')$ exactly when $i = i'$ and $j < j'$, or $i' = i + 1$ modulo $3$: three parts, each inducing the transitive tournament $T_2$ on two vertices, with every vertex of part $i$ beating every vertex of part $i + 1$.
