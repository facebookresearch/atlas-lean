# Alternating arc of a vertex sequence

For a sequence $v_0, v_1, \dots$ of vertices and a Boolean $b$, the $j$-th alternating arc is the pair $(v_j, v_{j+1})$ when $j$ is even and $b$ is true or $j$ is odd and $b$ is false, and the reversed pair $(v_{j+1}, v_j)$ otherwise: arcs traversed alternately forwards and backwards, starting forwards when $b$ is true.
