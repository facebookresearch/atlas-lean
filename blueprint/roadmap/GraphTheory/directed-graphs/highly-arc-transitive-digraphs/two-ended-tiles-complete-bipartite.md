# Cameron–Praeger–Wormald conjecture on tiles of two-ended highly arc transitive digraphs

The statement that for every digraph $D$ whose underlying undirected graph is connected and has exactly two ends, if $D$ is highly arc transitive (for every $s \ge 0$ the automorphisms act transitively on $s$-arcs, sequences $x_0 \to x_1 \to \dots \to x_s$ with $x_i \ne x_{i+2}$), then for every map $b : V \to \mathbb{Z}$ with finite level sets $\{v : b(v) = i\}$ and $b(v) = b(u) + 1$ for every arc $u \to v$, every integer $i$ and all vertices $u, u'$ at level $i$ and $v, v'$ at level $i + 1$: if $u \to v$, $u' \to v$ and $u \to v'$, then $u' \to v'$. That is, every tile, the bipartite digraph of arcs from one level to the next, is a disjoint union of complete bipartite graphs. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Tiles of two-ended highly arc transitive digraphs need not be unions of complete bipartite graphs](two-ended-tiles-complete-bipartite-refut.md).
