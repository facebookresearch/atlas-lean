# Highly arc transitive digraph

Let $D$ be a digraph on a vertex set $V$, with arcs $u \to v$. $D$ is highly arc transitive when for every $s \ge 0$ and any two $s$-arcs $(x_0, \dots, x_s)$ and $(y_0, \dots, y_s)$ (consecutive arcs $x_i \to x_{i+1}$, with $x_i \ne x_{i+2}$) some automorphism $\sigma$ of $D$ satisfies $\sigma(x_i) = y_i$ for every $i \le s$; for $s = 0$ this is transitivity on vertices.
