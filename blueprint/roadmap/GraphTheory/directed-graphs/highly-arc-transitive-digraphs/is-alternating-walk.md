# Alternating walk in a digraph

Let $D$ be a digraph on a vertex set $V$, with arcs $u \to v$. For $s \ge 1$, a sequence $v_0, \dots, v_s$ and a Boolean $b$ form an alternating walk when, for every $j < s$, the $j$-th alternating arc ($(v_j, v_{j+1})$ or $(v_{j+1}, v_j)$, alternately, starting forwards when $b$ is true) is an arc of $D$.
