# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1006

It is not the case that every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with girth greater than $4$ (no cycle of length at most $4$; forests included) has an [orientation](../dichromatic-number/is-orientation.md) with no directed cycle ([acyclic](../directed-acyclic-graphs-and-topological-orders/is-acyclic.md)) such that reversing any single arc $u \to v$ of it again gives a relation with no directed cycle. Equivalently, some finite graph of girth greater than $4$ has no acyclic orientation all of whose single-arc reversals are acyclic. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1006 (acyclic orientations robust under one reversal)](girth-five-reversible-acyclic-orientatio.md) is false.
