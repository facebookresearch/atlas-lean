# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1006

It is not the case that every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with girth greater than $4$ (no cycle of length at most $4$; forests included) has an orientation with no directed cycle such that reversing any single arc $u \to v$ of it again gives a relation with no directed cycle. Equivalently, some finite graph of girth greater than $4$ has no acyclic orientation all of whose single-arc reversals are acyclic.
