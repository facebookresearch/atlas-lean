# One-arc walks

Let $V$ and $E$ be sets and let each arc $e \in E$ run from its tail $t(e) \in V$ to its head $h(e) \in V$. A list of arcs is a walk from $o$ to $d$ if it is empty only when $o = d$, its first arc leaves $o$, the head of each arc is the tail of the next, and its last arc enters $d$. If an arc $a$ has $t(a) = o$ and $h(a) = d$, then the one-arc list $[a]$ is a walk from $o$ to $d$.
