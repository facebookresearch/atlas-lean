# Directed cycle with a given number of vertices

A digraph $G$ (loops allowed) has a directed cycle through $m + 1$ distinct vertices when there are distinct vertices $c_0, \dots, c_m$ with an arc from $c_i$ to $c_{i+1}$ for every $i$, indices modulo $m + 1$; for $m = 0$ this is a loop and for $m = 1$ a pair of opposite arcs.
