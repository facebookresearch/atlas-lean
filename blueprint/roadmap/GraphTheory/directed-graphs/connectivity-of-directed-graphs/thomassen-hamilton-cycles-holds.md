# Highly strongly connected tournaments have many edge-disjoint Hamilton cycles

For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ there is $f(k)$ such that every strongly $f(k)$-connected tournament has $k$ pairwise arc-disjoint Hamilton cycles; here a tournament on $\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ is strongly $f$-connected when $n > f$ and it remains strongly connected after deleting any fewer than $f$ vertices. Kühn, Lapinskas, Osthus and Patel proved this with $f(k) = C k^2 \log^2 k$ for some constant $C$ and all $k \ge 2$; for $k = 1$ it is Camion's theorem.
