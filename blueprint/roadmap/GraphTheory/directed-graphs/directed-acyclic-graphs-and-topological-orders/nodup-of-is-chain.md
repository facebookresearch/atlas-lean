# In a finite acyclic digraph every directed walk is a path

Let $G$ be an acyclic digraph on a finite vertex type. Every directed walk of $G$, that is, every finite list of vertices $(v_1, \dots, v_k)$ with an edge $v_i \to v_{i+1}$ for every $i < k$, has no repeated vertex, so it is a directed path.
