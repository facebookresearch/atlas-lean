# The directed 3-cycle has no topological order

Let $C_3$ be the directed 3-cycle, the digraph on $\{0, 1, 2\}$ with edges $0 \to 1$, $1 \to 2$ and $2 \to 0$. No bijection $\sigma : \{0, 1, 2\} \to \{0, 1, 2\}$ is a topological order of $C_3$: for every such $\sigma$ some edge $u \to v$ of $C_3$ has $\sigma(u) \ge \sigma(v)$.
