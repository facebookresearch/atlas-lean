# Topological order of a finite digraph

Let $G$ be a digraph on a finite vertex type $V$ with $N = |V|$. A topological order of $G$ is a bijection $\sigma : V \to \{0, 1, \dots, N-1\}$ such that $\sigma(u) < \sigma(v)$ for every edge $u \to v$ of $G$. A digraph here is an arbitrary adjacency relation on a vertex type $V$; loops $v \to v$ are allowed. A digraph with a loop has no topological order.
