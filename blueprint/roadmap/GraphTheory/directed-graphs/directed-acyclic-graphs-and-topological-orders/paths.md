# Set of directed paths of a digraph

For a digraph $G$ on a vertex type $V$, the directed paths of $G$ are the finite lists $(v_1, \dots, v_k)$ of vertices with no repeated vertex such that $v_i \to v_{i+1}$ is an edge of $G$ for every $i < k$. The empty list and every one-vertex list are directed paths.
