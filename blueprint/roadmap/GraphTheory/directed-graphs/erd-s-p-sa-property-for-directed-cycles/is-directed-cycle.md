# Directed cycle of a digraph

For a digraph $D$ and a natural number $m$, a map $c$ from $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ to the vertices lists a directed cycle of length $m$ when the vertices $c(0), \dots, c(m-1)$ are distinct and $c(i) \to c(i+1)$ is an arc for every $i$, indices taken modulo $m$ (for $m = 1$ this is a loop). The ordinary reading as a directed cycle is for positive $m$: for $m = 0$ the empty map satisfies the condition vacuously, in every digraph, including the empty one.
