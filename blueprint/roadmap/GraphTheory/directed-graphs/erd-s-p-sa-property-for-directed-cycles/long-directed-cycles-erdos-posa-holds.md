# Long directed cycles have the Erdős–Pósa property

There is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for all natural numbers $\ell \ge 2$ and $k$, every digraph $D$ on a finite vertex set either has $k$ pairwise vertex-disjoint directed cycles, each of length at least $\ell$, or has a set $S$ of at most $f(k + \ell)$ vertices meeting every directed cycle of length at least $\ell$. Here a directed cycle of length $m$ is a sequence of $m$ distinct vertices with an arc from each to the next, the last to the first. That is, the proposition [Erdős–Pósa property for long directed cycles (Havet–Maia question)](long-directed-cycles-erdos-posa.md) holds.
