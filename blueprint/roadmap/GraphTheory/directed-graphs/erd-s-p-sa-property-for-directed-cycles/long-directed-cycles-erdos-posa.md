# Erdős–Pósa property for long directed cycles (Havet–Maia question)

The statement that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for all natural numbers $\ell \ge 2$ and $k$, every digraph $D$ on a finite vertex set either has $k$ pairwise vertex-disjoint directed cycles, each of length at least $\ell$, or has a set $S$ of at most $f(k + \ell)$ vertices meeting every directed cycle of length at least $\ell$. Here a directed cycle of length $m$ is a sequence of $m$ distinct vertices with an arc from each to the next, the last to the first. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Long directed cycles have the Erdős–Pósa property](long-directed-cycles-erdos-posa-holds.md).
