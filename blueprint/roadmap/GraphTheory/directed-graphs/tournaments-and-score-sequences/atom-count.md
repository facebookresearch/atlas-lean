# Number of irreducible tournaments with a given number of circular triads

For natural numbers $n$ and $d$, $N(n, d)$ is the number of irreducible tournaments on $n \ge 2$ labelled players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $d$ circular triads (and $0$ for $n \le 1$); a tournament is irreducible when no nonempty proper set of players loses all its games against the complement, and a circular triad of a tournament is a set of three players each of whom beats exactly one of the other two.

## Sources

- Ian R. Harris and Ryan P. A. McShane, "Counting Tournaments with a Specified Number of Circular Triads", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.7, Section 3, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/McShane/mcshane1.html.
