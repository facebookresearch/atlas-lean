# Number of tournaments with a given number of circular triads

For natural numbers $n$ and $d$, $g_d(n)$ is the number of tournaments on the labelled players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $d$ circular triads, a circular triad of a tournament is a set of three players each of whom beats exactly one of the other two. A tournament on the players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is a relation in which no player beats itself and, for any two distinct players, exactly one beats the other.

## Sources

- Ian R. Harris and Ryan P. A. McShane, "Counting Tournaments with a Specified Number of Circular Triads", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.7, Section 1, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/McShane/mcshane1.html.
