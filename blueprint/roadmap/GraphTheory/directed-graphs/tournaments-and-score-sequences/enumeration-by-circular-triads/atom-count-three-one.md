# Irreducible tournaments on three players

$N(3, 1) = 2$: exactly two irreducible tournaments on three labelled players have one circular triad, the two directed $3$-cycles. Here $N(n, d)$ is the number of irreducible tournaments on $n \ge 2$ labelled players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $d$ circular triads (and $0$ for $n \le 1$); a tournament is irreducible when no nonempty proper set of players loses all its games against the complement, and a circular triad of a tournament is a set of three players each of whom beats exactly one of the other two.
