# Range of sizes of irreducible tournaments with $d$ circular triads

Let $d \ge 1$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $N(k, d) \ne 0$, where $N(n, d)$ is the number of irreducible tournaments on $n \ge 2$ labelled players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $d$ circular triads (and $0$ for $n \le 1$); a tournament is irreducible when no nonempty proper set of players loses all its games against the complement, and a circular triad of a tournament is a set of three players each of whom beats exactly one of the other two. Then $k \le d + 2$, and there is an integer $j$ with $0 < j \le k$ such that $j$ is odd and $j^3 - j \ge 24d$, or $j$ is even and $j^3 - 4j \ge 24d$.

## Sources

- Ian R. Harris and Ryan P. A. McShane, "Counting Tournaments with a Specified Number of Circular Triads", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.7, Theorem 8, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/McShane/mcshane1.html.
