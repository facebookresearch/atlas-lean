# Tournaments on three players by circular triads

On three labelled players there are $g_0(3) = 6$ tournaments with no circular triad (the transitive tournaments) and $g_1(3) = 2$ tournaments with one circular triad (the two directed $3$-cycles). Here $g_d(n)$ is the number of tournaments on the labelled players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $d$ circular triads, a circular triad of a tournament is a set of three players each of whom beats exactly one of the other two.

## Sources

- Ian R. Harris and Ryan P. A. McShane, "Counting Tournaments with a Specified Number of Circular Triads", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.7, Section 1, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/McShane/mcshane1.html.
