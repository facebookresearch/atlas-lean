# Number of circular triads of a tournament

For a relation $T$ on the players $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ (written $T(i, j)$ for "$i$ beats $j$"), the number of circular triads is the number of three-element sets $S$ of players such that every player of $S$ beats exactly one player of $S$.

## Sources

- Ian R. Harris and Ryan P. A. McShane, "Counting Tournaments with a Specified Number of Circular Triads", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.7, Section 2, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/McShane/mcshane1.html.
