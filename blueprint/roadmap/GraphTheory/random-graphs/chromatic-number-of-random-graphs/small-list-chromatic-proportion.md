# Proportion of graphs with small list chromatic number

For a real $\varepsilon$ and a natural number $n$, the proportion of the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, all simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ being equally likely (the model $G(n, 1/2)$), that are $q$-list-colourable for some natural number $q \le \varepsilon n$. Here a graph is $q$-list-colourable when for every type of colours and every assignment of lists of at least $q$ colours to its vertices it has a proper colouring from the lists.
