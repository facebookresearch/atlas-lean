# Erdős Problem 799 (list chromatic number of almost all graphs)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$, the proportion of the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ that are $q$-list-colourable for some natural number $q \le \varepsilon n$ tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$; that is, almost all graphs have list chromatic number $o(n)$. Here a graph is $q$-list-colourable when for every type of colours and every assignment of lists of at least $q$ colours to its vertices it has a proper colouring from the lists. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 799](list-chromatic-number-sublinear-almost-a-2.md).
