# Vertex triples

The three-element subsets of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$; there are $\binom n3$ of them. Each is the vertex set of one possible triangle of a graph on $n$ vertices, and its three [pairs](../../graph-ramsey-theory/graph-ramsey-numbers/pairs-in.md) are the triangle's edges.
