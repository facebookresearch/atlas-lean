# Dependency graph of the triangle events

Two distinct [vertex triples](triple.md) are adjacent when they share a pair of vertices, that is, when the corresponding triangles of $K_n$ share an edge. Triangles that share no edge are determined by disjoint sets of edges of $G(n, p)$ and are independent, so this is a dependency graph for the events "$T$ spans a triangle" in the sense of the [second moment method](../../../Combinatorics/probabilistic-combinatorics/first-and-second-moment-methods/variance-sum-indicator-le.md).
