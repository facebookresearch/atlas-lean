# Transient graph

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a vertex set $V$. $G$ is transient when $V$ is infinite and for some vertex $o$ the expected number of returns $\sum_{n \ge 0} p_n(o, o)$ of simple random walk is finite, where $p_n(o, o)$ is the sum over closed walks $w_0 = o, w_1, \dots, w_n = o$ in $G$ of $\prod_{i < n} 1/\deg w_i$.
