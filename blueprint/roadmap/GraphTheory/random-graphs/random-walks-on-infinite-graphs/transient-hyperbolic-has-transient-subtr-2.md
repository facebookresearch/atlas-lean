# A transient hyperbolic graph without transient subtrees

It is not the case that every connected simple graph $G$ (on a vertex set of any cardinality) of bounded degree that is Gromov hyperbolic for its graph distance (some $\delta$ with $(x \mid z)_w \ge \min\{(x \mid y)_w, (y \mid z)_w\} - \delta$ for all vertices, where $(x \mid y)_w = \tfrac12(d(x, w) + d(y, w) - d(x, y))$) and transient (infinite, with $\sum_n p_n(o, o) < \infty$ for some vertex $o$, $p_n(o, o)$ the sum over closed walks of length $n$ at $o$ of the products of $1/\deg$ over the vertices left) has a subgraph that is a tree and is itself transient for its own degrees. Equivalently, some connected, bounded-degree, Gromov hyperbolic, transient graph has no transient subtree. That is, the proposition [Benjamini's question on transient subtrees](transient-hyperbolic-has-transient-subtr.md) is false.
