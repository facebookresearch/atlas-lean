# Refinement by two sets

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\mathcal P$ a partition of $V$ and $S, T \subseteq V$. The refinement of $\mathcal P$ by $S$ and $T$ is the common refinement of $\mathcal P$ with $\{S, V \setminus S\}$ and $\{T, V \setminus T\}$: two elements are in the same class when they lie in the same class of $\mathcal P$, on the same side of $S$ and on the same side of $T$.
