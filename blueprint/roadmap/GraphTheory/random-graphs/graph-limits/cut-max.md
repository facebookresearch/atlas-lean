# Unnormalized cut norm

Let $V$ be a finite set and $u$ a real function on the unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ included), written $u(x, y) = u(y, x)$. Its unnormalized cut norm is

$$\lVert u\rVert_{\max} = \max_{S, T \subseteq V} \Big\lvert \sum_{x \in S} \sum_{y \in T} u(x, y) \Big\rvert,$$

the matrix cut norm $\lVert A\rVert_\square$ of Lovász–Szegedy, §4.1 (it is $0$ when $V$ is empty).
