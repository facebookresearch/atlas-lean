# Cut distance of graphons

For graphons $U$ and $W$,

$$\delta_\square(U, W) = \inf_{\varphi, \psi} \|U^\varphi - W^\psi\|_\square, \qquad U^\varphi(x, y) = U(\varphi(x), \varphi(y)),$$

the infimum over pairs of measure-preserving maps $\varphi, \psi$ of $[0, 1]$. It is only a pseudometric: $\delta_\square(W, W^\varphi) = 0$, and the step functions of a graph and of its blow-ups are at distance $0$. The infimum can equivalently be taken over couplings of the uniform distribution with itself, or over measure-preserving bijections.
