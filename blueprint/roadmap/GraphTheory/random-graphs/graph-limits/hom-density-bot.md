# Homomorphism densities are normalized

Let $A$ and $V$ be finite sets with $V$ nonempty, and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$. The [homomorphism density](hom-density.md) of the edgeless graph on $A$ in $w$ is $1$: homomorphism densities are normalized (Lovász–Szegedy, *Limits of dense graph sequences*, §2.2).
