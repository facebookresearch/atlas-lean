# Nonnegativity of the cut norm

Let $V$ be a finite set and $u$ a real function on the unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ included), written $u(x, y) = u(y, x)$. Its [cut norm](cut-norm.md) $\lVert u\rVert_\square = \lvert V\rvert^{-2} \max_{S, T \subseteq V} \lvert\sum_{x \in S, y \in T} u(x, y)\rvert$ (read as $0$ when $V$ is empty) is nonnegative.
