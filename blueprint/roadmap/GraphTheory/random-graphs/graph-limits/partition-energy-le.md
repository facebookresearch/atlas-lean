# The energy is bounded by the total mass

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\mathcal P$ a partition of $V$ and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$. The [energy](partition-energy.md) of $\mathcal P$ satisfies

$$\sum_{x, y \in V} w_{\mathcal P}(x, y)^2 \le \sum_{x, y \in V} w(x, y)^2,$$

where $w_{\mathcal P}$ is the [partition average](partition-average.md).
