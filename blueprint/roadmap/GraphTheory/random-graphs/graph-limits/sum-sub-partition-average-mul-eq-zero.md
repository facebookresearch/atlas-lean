# Orthogonality of the partition average

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\mathcal P$ a partition of $V$ and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$. For every $g : V \times V \to \mathbb{R}$ that is [constant on the blocks](is-block-constant.md) of $\mathcal P$,

$$\sum_{x, y \in V} \big(w(x, y) - w_{\mathcal P}(x, y)\big)\, g(x, y) = 0,$$

where $w_{\mathcal P}$ is the [partition average](partition-average.md): $w - w_{\mathcal P}$ is orthogonal to the block-constant functions.
