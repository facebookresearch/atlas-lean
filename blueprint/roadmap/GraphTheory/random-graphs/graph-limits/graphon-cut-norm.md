# Cut norm of a kernel

For a bounded measurable kernel $W$ on $[0,1]^2$,

$$\|W\|_\square = \sup_{S, T \subseteq [0,1]} \Big| \int_{S \times T} W(x, y) \, dx \, dy \Big|,$$

the supremum over measurable $S, T$. It is within a factor $4$ of the $L^\infty \to L^1$ operator norm of $W$, and at most $\|W\|_1$. The supremum is taken in $\mathbb R$, with the convention that an unbounded set of reals has supremum $0$; it is meaningful for bounded kernels, for which it is finite.
