# One refinement step

If the partition average $w_{\mathcal P}$ is not $\varepsilon$-close to $w$ in the cut norm, i.e. $\|w - w_{\mathcal P}\|_\square > \varepsilon \|w\|_2$, then some partition $\mathcal Q$ with at most four times as many classes as $\mathcal P$ (the refinement by the two sets of a witnessing rectangle) has energy larger than $E(\mathcal P) + \varepsilon^2 \sum_{x,y} w(x,y)^2$.
