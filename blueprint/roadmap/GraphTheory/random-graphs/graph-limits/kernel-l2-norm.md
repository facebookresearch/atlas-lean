# L² norm of a weighted graph

Let $V$ be a finite set and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$ (a weighted graph with unit node weights). Its normalized $L^2$ norm is

$$\lVert w\rVert_2 = \Big(\lvert V\rvert^{-2} \sum_{x, y \in V} w(x, y)^2\Big)^{1/2},$$

read as $0$ when $V$ is empty (Borgs–Chayes–Lovász–Sós–Vesztergombi, *Convergent sequences of dense graphs I*, §2.1).
