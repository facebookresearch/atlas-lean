# The partition average is block-constant

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\mathcal P$ a partition of $V$ and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$. The [partition average](partition-average.md) $(x, y) \mapsto w_{\mathcal P}(x, y)$ is [constant on the blocks](is-block-constant.md) $V_i \times V_j$ of $\mathcal P$.
