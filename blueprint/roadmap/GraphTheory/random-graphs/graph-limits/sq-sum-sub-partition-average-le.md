# Energy increment

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\mathcal P$ a partition of $V$, $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of $V$ and $S, T \subseteq V$. Then

$$\Big(\sum_{x \in S} \sum_{y \in T} (w - w_{\mathcal P})(x, y)\Big)^2 \le \lvert V\rvert^2 \big(\mathcal E(\mathcal P') - \mathcal E(\mathcal P)\big),$$

where $w_{\mathcal P}$ is the [partition average](partition-average.md), $\mathcal P'$ is the [refinement](refine-by.md) of $\mathcal P$ by $S$ and $T$, and $\mathcal E$ is the [energy](partition-energy.md).
