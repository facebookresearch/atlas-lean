# Erdős Problem 807 (bipartition number of random graphs)

The statement that the proportion of the simple graphs $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with $\tau(G) = n - \alpha(G)$ tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$, where the bipartition number $\tau(G)$ of a simple graph $G$ is the least $k$ such that $G$ is the union of $k$ pairwise edge-disjoint complete bipartite subgraphs, each with two nonempty sides and $\alpha(G)$ is the independence number. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 807](bipartition-number-almost-surely-eq-refu.md).
