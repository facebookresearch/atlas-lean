# Proportion of graphs with bipartition number n minus independence number

For a natural number $n$, the proportion of the simple graphs $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, all simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ being equally likely (the model $G(n, 1/2)$), with $\tau(G) = n - \alpha(G)$, where the bipartition number $\tau(G)$ of a simple graph $G$ is the least $k$ such that $G$ is the union of $k$ pairwise edge-disjoint complete bipartite subgraphs, each with two nonempty sides and $\alpha(G)$ is the independence number.
