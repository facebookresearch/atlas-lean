# Erdős Problem 578 (spanning hypercubes in random graphs)

The statement that the probability that the random graph $G(2^d, 1/2)$, all simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, 2^d - 1\}$ being equally likely, contains a copy of $Q_d$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced, tends to $1$ as $d \to \infty$. Here $Q_d$ is the $d$-dimensional hypercube, the graph on the vectors in $\{0, 1\}^d$ (written as Boolean vectors) joining two vectors that differ in exactly one coordinate. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 578](random-graph-contains-hypercube-holds.md).
