# The comb graph

For a natural number $k$, the comb on the $k^2$ vertices $(i, j)$, $0 \le i, j < k$, is the simple graph whose edges join $(i, 0)$ and $(i+1, 0)$ (the spine) and $(i, j)$ and $(i, j+1)$ (the teeth), for all admissible $i$ and $j$: $k$ disjoint $k$-vertex paths hanging from the $k$ vertices of a $k$-vertex path.
