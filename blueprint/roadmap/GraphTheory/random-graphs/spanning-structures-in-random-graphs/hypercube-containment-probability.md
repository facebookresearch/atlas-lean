# Probability that a random graph contains the hypercube

For a natural number $d$, the proportion of the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, 2^d - 1\}$ that contain a copy of $Q_d$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced: the probability for the random graph $G(2^d, 1/2)$. Here $Q_d$ is the $d$-dimensional hypercube, the graph on the vectors in $\{0, 1\}^d$ (written as Boolean vectors) joining two vectors that differ in exactly one coordinate.
