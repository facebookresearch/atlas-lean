# Packing Hamilton cycles in random graphs (Frieze–Krivelevich conjecture)

The statement that for every function $p$ from the natural numbers to $[0, 1]$, the probability that the binomial random graph $G(n, p(n))$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, each pair being an edge independently with probability $p(n)$, contains $\lfloor \delta(G)/2 \rfloor$ pairwise edge-disjoint Hamilton cycles, where $\delta(G)$ is its minimum degree, tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Random graphs contain $\lfloor \delta/2 \rfloor$ edge-disjoint Hamilton cycles](hamilton-cycle-packing-holds.md).
