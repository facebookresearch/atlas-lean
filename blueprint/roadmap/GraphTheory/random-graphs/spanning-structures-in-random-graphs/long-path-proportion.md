# Proportion of graphs with a long path

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$ and a real $x$, the proportion of the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $m$ edges that contain a path of length (number of edges) at least $x$: the probability that the uniform random graph $G(n, m)$ has such a path. It is $0$ when no graph has $m$ edges.
