# Proportion of graphs with a long path

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$ and a real $x$, this is the proportion of the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $m$ edges that contain a path of length (number of edges) at least $x$. For $m \le \binom{n}{2}$ it is the probability that the uniform random graph $G(n, m)$ has such a path. For $m > \binom{n}{2}$ there is no such graph and no such random graph, and the value $0$ is a convention.
