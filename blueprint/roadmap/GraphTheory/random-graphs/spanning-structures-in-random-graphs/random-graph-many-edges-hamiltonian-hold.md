# Resolution of Erdős Problem 746

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and every function $M : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that $(1/2 + \varepsilon)\, n \log n \le M(n) \le \binom{n}{2}$ for all sufficiently large $n$, with $\log$ the natural logarithm, the probability that the uniform random graph $G(n, M(n))$, all simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $M(n)$ edges being equally likely, is Hamiltonian tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 746 (Hamiltonicity of random graphs with many edges)](random-graph-many-edges-hamiltonian.md) holds.
