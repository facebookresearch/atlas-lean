# Erdős Problem 900 (long paths in sparse random graphs)

The statement that there is a function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f(c) > 0$ for every $c > 1/2$, $f(c) \to 0$ as $c \to 1/2$ from above and $f(c) \to 1$ as $c \to \infty$, such that for every real $c > 1/2$ the probability that the uniform random graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $\lfloor c n \rfloor$ edges contains a path of length (number of edges) at least $f(c)\, n$ tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 900](random-graph-linear-path-holds.md).
