# Proportion of Hamiltonian graphs with a given number of edges

For natural numbers $n$ and $M$, this is the proportion of Hamiltonian graphs among the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $M$ edges. For $M \le \binom{n}{2}$ it is the probability that the uniform random graph $G(n, M)$ is Hamiltonian. For $M > \binom{n}{2}$ there is no such graph and no such random graph, and the value $0$ is a convention. The single-vertex graph counts as Hamiltonian without containing a cycle, so the value at $n = 1$, $M = 0$ is $1$.
