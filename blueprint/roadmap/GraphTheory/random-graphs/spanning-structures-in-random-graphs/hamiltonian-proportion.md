# Proportion of Hamiltonian graphs with a given number of edges

For natural numbers $n$ and $M$, the proportion of Hamiltonian graphs among the simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $M$ edges, the probability that the uniform random graph $G(n, M)$, all simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $M$ edges being equally likely, is Hamiltonian. It is $0$ when no graph has $M$ edges.
