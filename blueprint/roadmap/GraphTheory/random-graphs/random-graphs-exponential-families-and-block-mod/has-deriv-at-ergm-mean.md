# Derivative of the exponential random graph mean is its covariance

For a finite vertex set $V$, a statistic $t$ of simple graphs on $V$ with values in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ for a finite index set $\iota$, $\theta, v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $j \in \iota$, the function $s \mapsto \mu_j(\theta + s v)$ is differentiable at $s = 0$ with derivative $\sum_k \Sigma_{jk}(\theta)\, v_k$: the derivative of the mean is the covariance matrix (Snijders 2002, equation (26)).
