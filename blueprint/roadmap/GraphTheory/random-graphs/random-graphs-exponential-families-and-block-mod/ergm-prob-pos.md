# Exponential random graph probabilities are positive

For a finite vertex set $V$, a statistic $t$ of simple graphs on $V$ with values in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ for a finite index set $\iota$, and every $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, every graph $G$ on $V$ has positive probability $P_\theta(G) = e^{\langle\theta, t(G)\rangle}/\sum_H e^{\langle\theta, t(H)\rangle} > 0$ under the exponential random graph law.
