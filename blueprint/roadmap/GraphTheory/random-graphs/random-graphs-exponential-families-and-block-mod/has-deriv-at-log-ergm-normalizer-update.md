# Coordinate derivative of the log-normalizer is the mean

For a finite vertex set $V$, a statistic $t$ of simple graphs on $V$ with values in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ for a finite index set $\iota$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and a coordinate $k$, the log-normalizer $\psi = \log \kappa$, as a function of the $k$-th coordinate of $\theta$ alone, is differentiable at $\theta_k$ with derivative $\mu_k(\theta)$, the mean of the $k$-th statistic under $P_\theta$: $\partial\psi/\partial\theta_k = \mu_k(\theta)$.

## Sources

- T. A. B. Snijders, "Markov chain Monte Carlo estimation of exponential random graph models", Journal of Social Structure 3 (2002), equation (25).
