# Relabeling the class prior permutes the labeling

Let $V$ be a finite vertex set, $K$ a finite set of classes, $p \in \mathbb{R}^K$, $z : V \to K$ a labeling and $\sigma$ a permutation of $K$. The prior weight $\prod_v p_{\sigma(z(v))}$ of $z$ under the relabeled class probabilities $p \circ \sigma$ equals the prior weight of the labeling $\sigma \circ z$ under $p$.
