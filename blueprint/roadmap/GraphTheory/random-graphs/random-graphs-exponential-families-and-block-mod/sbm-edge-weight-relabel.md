# Relabeling the block affinities permutes the labeling

Let $V$ be a finite linearly ordered vertex set, $K$ a finite set of classes, $W \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times K}$, $z : V \to K$ a labeling, $G$ a simple graph on $V$, and $\sigma$ a permutation of $K$. The edge factor of $z$ for the relabeled affinities $(W_{\sigma(i)\sigma(j)})_{i,j}$ equals the edge factor of the labeling $\sigma \circ z$ for $W$.
