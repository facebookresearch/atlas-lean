# Directional derivative of the log-normalizer is the mean

For a finite vertex set $V$, a statistic $t$ of simple graphs on $V$ with values in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ for a finite index set $\iota$, and $\theta, v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, the function $s \mapsto \psi(\theta + s v)$ with $\psi = \log \kappa$ the log-normalizer is differentiable at $s = 0$ with derivative $\langle v, \mu(\theta)\rangle$, where $\mu(\theta)$ is the mean statistic under $P_\theta$.
