# Label swapping breaks injectivity of the SBM law

Let $(p, W)$ be admissible stochastic block model parameters on a finite class set ($p_k > 0$, $\sum_k p_k = 1$, $0 \le W_{ij} \le 1$, $W$ symmetric), and let $\sigma$ be a permutation of the classes with $(p \circ \sigma, W_{\sigma(\cdot)\sigma(\cdot)}) \ne (p, W)$. Then on any finite linearly ordered vertex set, the map from admissible parameters to the law of the random graph is not injective: parameters are identified at most up to permutation of the latent classes.
