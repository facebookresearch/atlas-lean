# Unordered pairs under an equivalence

For sets $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and a bijection $e : \alpha \to \beta$, the induced bijection from unordered pairs of elements of $\alpha$ to unordered pairs of elements of $\beta$, $\{x, y\} \mapsto \{e(x), e(y)\}$, with inverse $\{u, v\} \mapsto \{e^{-1}(u), e^{-1}(v)\}$.
