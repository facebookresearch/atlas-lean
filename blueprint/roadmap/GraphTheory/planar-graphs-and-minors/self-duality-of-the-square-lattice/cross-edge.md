# Quarter-turn rotation of unordered site pairs

The bijection on unordered pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ induced by the quarter-turn rotation $\rho(a, b) = (-b, a)$: $\{x, y\} \mapsto \{\rho(x), \rho(y)\}$. It maps lattice edges to lattice edges, but not to the dual edge crossing them: $\{(0, 0), (1, 0)\}$ is sent to $\{(0, 0), (0, 1)\}$.
