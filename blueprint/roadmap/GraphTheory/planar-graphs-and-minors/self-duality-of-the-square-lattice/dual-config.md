# Rotated dual configuration

For a bond configuration $\omega$, assigning to every unordered pair $e$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ the value open or closed, the rotated dual configuration declares $e$ open exactly when $\{\rho^{-1}(x), \rho^{-1}(y)\}$ is closed in $\omega$, where $e = \{x, y\}$ and $\rho(a, b) = (-b, a)$. Applying it twice gives the half-turn of $\omega$, not $\omega$ itself.
