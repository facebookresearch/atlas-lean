# Side of a tree edge

Deleting an edge $ij$ of the decomposition tree $T$ splits the leaves into two parts. The side of
$(i, j)$ is the set of vertices whose leaves lie in the component containing $i$; the side of
$(j, i)$ is the rest of the vertex set.
