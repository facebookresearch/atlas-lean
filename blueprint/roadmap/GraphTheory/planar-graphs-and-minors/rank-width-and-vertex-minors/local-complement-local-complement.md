# Local complementation is an involution

Local complementation at a vertex is an involution: $G * v * v = G$.

## Sources

- S. Oum, *Rank-width and vertex-minors*, J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 95 (2005) 79–100; read in the author's revised preprint of 18 April 2005 (https://sangil.dimag.kr/pdf/vertexminor-revision.pdf), whose numbering is cited here, Section 2.
- S. Oum, *Rank-width: algorithmic and structural results*, Discrete Appl. Math. 231 (2017) 15–24; read as arXiv:1601.03800v2 (27 July 2016), whose numbering is cited here, Section 4.1 ("Clearly $G * v * v = G$").
