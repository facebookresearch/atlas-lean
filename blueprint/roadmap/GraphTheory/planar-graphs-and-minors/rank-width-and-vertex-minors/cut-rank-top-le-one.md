# Complete graphs have cut-rank at most one

In a complete graph every cut matrix is an all-ones matrix, of rank at most one. Hence
$\rho_{K_V}(X) \le 1$ for every vertex set $X$, and complete graphs have rank-width at most one,
although $K_n$ has tree-width $n - 1$.
