# Rank-width at most $k$

A finite graph $G$ has rank-width at most $k$ if it has at most one vertex or it has a
rank-decomposition of width at most $k$. The rank-width $\mathrm{rw}(G)$ is the least such $k$,
with $\mathrm{rw}(G) = 0$ for graphs with at most one vertex as in the sources.
