# Rank-decomposition

A rank-decomposition of a finite vertex set $V$ is a pair $(T, L)$ of a finite tree $T$ in which every
node has degree one or three and a bijection $L$ from $V$ onto the leaves of $T$. It does not
depend on a graph; a graph enters only through the width of the decomposition.

A rank-decomposition exists only when $|V| \ge 2$; for graphs with at most one vertex the
rank-width is defined to be $0$.
