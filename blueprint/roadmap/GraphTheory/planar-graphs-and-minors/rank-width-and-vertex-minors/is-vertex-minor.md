# Vertex-minor

A graph $H$ is a vertex-minor of a graph $G$ if $H$ is isomorphic to an induced subgraph of a graph
locally equivalent to $G$. Equivalently, $H$ is obtained from $G$ by a sequence of local
complementations and vertex deletions; the source introduces the relation in this second form.

Vertex-minors stand to rank-width as minors stand to tree-width; every pivot-minor is a
vertex-minor.
