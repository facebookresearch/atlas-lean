# Rank-width does not increase under vertex-minors

If $H$ is a vertex-minor of a finite graph $G$ and $G$ has rank-width at most $k$, then so does $H$.
The source's proof: local complementation does not change the cut-rank function, and deleting a
vertex does not increase cut-rank, so a rank-decomposition of $G$ restricts to one of $H$ of no
larger width.
