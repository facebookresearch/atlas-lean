# Clique-width at most $k$

A graph has clique-width at most $k$ if it has no vertices or some vertex labelling of it is
generated by a $k$-expression. The clique-width $\mathrm{cw}(G)$ is the least such $k$. Complete
graphs on at least two vertices have clique-width $2$, and every graph on $n$ vertices has
clique-width at most $n$.
