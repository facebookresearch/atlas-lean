# Failure of the cell Gauss–Bonnet formula without a pinch term

It is not true that every finite set $S$ of unit cells satisfies $c_1(S) - c_3(S) = 4\,\chi(S)$, where $c_k(S)$ is the number of corners of cells of $S$ with exactly $k$ incident cells of $S$ and $\chi(S) = V - E + F$ is the Euler characteristic. For instance, the two diagonally touching cells $(0, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$ have corner defect $6$ but Euler characteristic $1$. The correct formula adds twice the number of pinch points.
