# Corner count at the top-right corner of a new cell

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ a cell with $(x, y) \notin S$. Then the number of cells of $S$ having the lattice point $(x+1, y+1)$ as a corner is

$$\mathbf{1}[(x+1, y+1) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(x, y+1) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(x+1, y) \in S].$$
