# Change of the pinch indicator at the top-left corner of a new cell

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells and $(x, y) \notin S$ a cell, and let $\pi_T(v) \in \{0, 1\}$ indicate that $v$ is a pinch point of $T$. Then

$$\pi_{S \cup \{(x, y)\}}(x, y+1) - \pi_S(x, y+1) = \mathbf{1}\big[(x-1, y+1) \in S,\ (x, y+1) \notin S,\ (x-1, y) \notin S\big] - \mathbf{1}\big[(x, y+1) \in S,\ (x-1, y) \in S,\ (x-1, y+1) \notin S\big].$$
