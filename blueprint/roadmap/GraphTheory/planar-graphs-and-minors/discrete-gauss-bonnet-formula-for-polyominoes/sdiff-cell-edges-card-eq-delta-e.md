# New edges of an added cell match the local count

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and $(x, y) \notin S$ a cell. Then the number of sides of $(x, y)$ that are not sides of any cell of $S$ equals

$$\big(1 - \mathbf{1}[(x, y-1) \in S]\big) + \big(1 - \mathbf{1}[(x, y+1) \in S]\big) + \big(1 - \mathbf{1}[(x-1, y) \in S]\big) + \big(1 - \mathbf{1}[(x+1, y) \in S]\big).$$
