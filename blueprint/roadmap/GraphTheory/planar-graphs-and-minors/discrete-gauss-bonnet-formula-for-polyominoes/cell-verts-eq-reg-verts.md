# Agreement of the two vertex sets of a cell complex

For a finite set $S$ of unit cells, each labelled by its lower left corner, the two descriptions of its vertex set coincide: the union over cells $(a, b) \in S$ of the corner sets $\{(a, b), (a+1, b), (a, b+1), (a+1, b+1)\}$, computed in either of the two ways, gives the same set of lattice points.
