# Distinct cells have distinct top edges

Let $S$ be a finite set of unit cells, each labelled by its lower left corner. The top edge of the cell $(a, b)$ is the side $\{(a, b+1), (a+1, b+1)\}$. The set of top edges of the cells of $S$ has exactly $|S|$ elements, that is, distinct cells have distinct top edges.
