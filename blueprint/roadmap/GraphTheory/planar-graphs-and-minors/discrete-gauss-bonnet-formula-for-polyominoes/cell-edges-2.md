# Edges of a cell complex

For a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of unit cells, each labelled by its lower left corner, the set of sides of the cells of $S$, each side encoded by twice its midpoint: the cell $(a, b)$ contributes

$$(2a+1, 2b),\quad (2a+1, 2b+2),\quad (2a, 2b+1),\quad (2a+2, 2b+1),$$

its bottom, top, left and right sides.
