# Membership of a cell's bottom edge in the cell complex

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$. The horizontal side with doubled midpoint $(2x+1, 2y)$, the bottom side of the cell $(x, y)$, is a side of some cell of $S$ if and only if $(x, y) \in S$ or $(x, y-1) \in S$.
