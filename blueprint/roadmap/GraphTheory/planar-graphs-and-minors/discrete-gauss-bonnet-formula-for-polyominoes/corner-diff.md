# Corner defect of a set of unit cells

For a finite set $S$ of unit cells of the plane, the corner defect of $S$ is the integer

$$c_1(S) - c_3(S),$$

where $c_k(S)$ is the number of vertices of $S$ (corners of its cells) having exactly $k$ incident cells of $S$; vertices with one incident cell are convex corners and those with three are reflex corners.
