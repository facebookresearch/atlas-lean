# Agreement of the two edge counts of a cell complex

Let $S$ be a finite set of unit cells of the plane, each labelled by its lower left corner $(a, b) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. The number of distinct sides of cells of $S$ counted through their doubled midpoints (the side of the cell $(a, b)$ between corners $p$ and $q$ is encoded by $p + q$) equals the number of distinct sides of cells of $S$ counted as unordered pairs $\{p, q\}$ of corners.
