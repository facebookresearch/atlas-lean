# Euler characteristic bound via the open-top skeleton

Let $S$ be a finite set of unit cells. If the graph on the corners of cells of $S$ whose edges are the sides of cells of $S$ other than top sides (the top side of the cell $(a, b)$ being $\{(a, b+1), (a+1, b+1)\}$) is connected (in particular nonempty), then the Euler characteristic $V - E + F$ of $S$ is at most $1$.
