# Corner count at the bottom-left corner of a new cell

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ a cell with $(x, y) \notin S$. Then the number of cells of $S$ having the lattice point $(x, y)$ as a corner is

$$\mathbf{1}[(x-1, y) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(x, y-1) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(x-1, y-1) \in S].$$
