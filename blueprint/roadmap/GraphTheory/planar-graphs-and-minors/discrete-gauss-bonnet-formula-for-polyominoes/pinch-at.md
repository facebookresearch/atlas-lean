# Pinch point of a set of unit cells

For a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners), a lattice point $v = (x, y)$ is a pinch point of $S$ when, of the four cells $(x-1, y-1)$, $(x, y-1)$, $(x-1, y)$, $(x, y)$ around $v$, exactly two diagonally opposite ones belong to $S$: either $(x, y), (x-1, y-1) \in S$ and $(x-1, y), (x, y-1) \notin S$, or $(x-1, y), (x, y-1) \in S$ and $(x, y), (x-1, y-1) \notin S$.
