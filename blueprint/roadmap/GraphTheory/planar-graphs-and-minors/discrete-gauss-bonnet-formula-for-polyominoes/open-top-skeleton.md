# Open-top skeleton of a cell complex

For a finite set $S$ of unit cells, the open-top skeleton of $S$ is the simple graph whose vertices are the corners of cells of $S$, two vertices $u, v$ being adjacent when $\{u, v\}$ is a side of some cell of $S$ but not the top side $\{(a, b+1), (a+1, b+1)\}$ of any cell $(a, b) \in S$.
