# Angle defect at a lattice vertex

For a finite set $K$ of unit cells and a lattice point $v$, the angle defect at $v$, in quarter turns, is the integer

$$4 - 2\,d_E(v) + d_C(v),$$

where $d_E(v)$ is the number of distinct sides of cells of $K$ having $v$ as an endpoint and $d_C(v)$ is the number of cells of $K$ having $v$ as a corner.
