# Corner count after adding a cell

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells, $c \notin S$ a cell and $v$ a lattice point, and let $n_S(v)$ be the number of cells of $S$ having $v$ as a corner. Then $n_{S \cup \{c\}}(v) = n_S(v) + 1$ if $v$ is one of the four corners of $c$, and $n_{S \cup \{c\}}(v) = n_S(v)$ otherwise.
