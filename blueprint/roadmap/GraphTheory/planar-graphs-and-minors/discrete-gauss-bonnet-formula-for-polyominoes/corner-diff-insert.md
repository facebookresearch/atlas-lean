# Change of the corner defect when a cell is added

Let $S$ be a finite set of unit cells and $c \notin S$ a cell. With $n_S(v)$ the number of cells of $S$ with corner $v$ and $w(k) = \mathbf{1}[k = 1] - \mathbf{1}[k = 3]$, the corner defect $D$ satisfies

$$D(S \cup \{c\}) = D(S) + \sum_{v \text{ corner of } c} \Big(w\big(n_S(v) + 1\big) - w\big(n_S(v)\big)\Big).$$
