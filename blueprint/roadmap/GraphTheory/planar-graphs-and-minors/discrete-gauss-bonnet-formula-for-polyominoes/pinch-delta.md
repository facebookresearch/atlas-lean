# Local change of the number of pinch points

For Boolean values $e, w, n, s, ne, nw, se, sw$, read as indicators in $\{0, 1\}$ of the presence of the eight neighbouring cells of a new cell, and with $\bar x = 1 - x$, the integer

$$\big(sw\,\bar w\,\bar s - w\,s\,\overline{sw}\big) + \big(se\,\bar e\,\bar s - e\,s\,\overline{se}\big) + \big(nw\,\bar n\,\bar w - n\,w\,\overline{nw}\big) + \big(ne\,\bar n\,\bar e - n\,e\,\overline{ne}\big),$$

the change of the number of pinch points at the four corners of the new cell.
