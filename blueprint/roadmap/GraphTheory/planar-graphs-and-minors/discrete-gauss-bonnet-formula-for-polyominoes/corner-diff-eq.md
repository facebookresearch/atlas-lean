# Cell Gauss–Bonnet formula

For every finite set $S$ of unit cells of the plane,

$$c_1(S) - c_3(S) = 4\,\chi(S) + 2\,\pi(S),$$

where $c_k(S)$ is the number of vertices of $S$ (corners of its cells) with exactly $k$ incident cells of $S$, $\chi(S) = V - E + F$ is the Euler characteristic with $V$ the number of vertices, $E$ the number of distinct cell sides and $F = |S|$, and $\pi(S)$ is the number of pinch points: vertices $v = (x, y)$ such that, of the four cells $(x-1, y-1), (x, y-1), (x-1, y), (x, y)$ around $v$, exactly two diagonally opposite ones belong to $S$.
