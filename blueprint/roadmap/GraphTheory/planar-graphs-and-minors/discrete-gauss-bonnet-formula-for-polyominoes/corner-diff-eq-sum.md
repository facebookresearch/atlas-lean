# Corner defect as a sum of vertex weights

For a finite set $S$ of unit cells, let $n_S(v)$ be the number of cells of $S$ with corner $v$ and let $w(k) = \mathbf{1}[k = 1] - \mathbf{1}[k = 3]$. Then the corner defect of $S$ is

$$\sum_{v \in V(S)} w\big(n_S(v)\big),$$

where $V(S)$ is the set of corners of cells of $S$.
