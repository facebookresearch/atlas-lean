# Edge count bound for a connected open-top skeleton

Let $S$ be a finite set of unit cells whose open-top skeleton (the graph on the corners of cells of $S$ whose edges are the sides of cells of $S$ other than top sides) is connected (in particular nonempty). Then the number of corners of cells of $S$ is at most one more than the number of sides of cells of $S$ that are not top sides of cells of $S$.
