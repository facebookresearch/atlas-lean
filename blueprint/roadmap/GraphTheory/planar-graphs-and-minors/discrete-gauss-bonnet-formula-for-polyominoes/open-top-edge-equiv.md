# Edges of the open-top skeleton

For a finite set $S$ of unit cells, the bijection between the edges of the open-top skeleton of $S$ (the graph on the corners of cells of $S$ whose edges are the sides of cells of $S$ that are not top sides) and the set of sides of cells of $S$ that are not top sides of cells of $S$, sending an edge $\{u, v\}$ of the graph to the side $\{u, v\}$.
