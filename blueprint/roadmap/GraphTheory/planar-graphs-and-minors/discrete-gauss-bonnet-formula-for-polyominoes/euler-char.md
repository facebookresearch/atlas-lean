# Euler characteristic of a cell complex

For a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of unit cells, each labelled by its lower left corner, the Euler characteristic of the closed cell complex they form is the integer

$$\chi(S) = V - E + F,$$

where $V$ is the number of distinct corners of cells of $S$, $E$ the number of distinct sides of cells of $S$ and $F = |S|$.
