# Four times the local change of the Euler characteristic

For Boolean values $e, w, n, s, ne, nw, se, sw$, read as indicators in $\{0, 1\}$ of the presence of the eight neighbouring cells of a new cell, the integer $4\,(\Delta V - \Delta E + 1)$, where

$$\Delta V = \mathbf{1}[w + s + sw = 0] + \mathbf{1}[e + se + s = 0] + \mathbf{1}[n + nw + w = 0] + \mathbf{1}[ne + n + e = 0]$$

counts the new corners and $\Delta E = (1 - s) + (1 - n) + (1 - w) + (1 - e)$ the new sides. It is four times the change of the Euler characteristic when the cell is added.
