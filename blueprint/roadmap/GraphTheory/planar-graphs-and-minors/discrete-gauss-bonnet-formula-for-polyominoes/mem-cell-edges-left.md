# Membership of a cell's left edge in the cell complex

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$. The vertical side with doubled midpoint $(2x, 2y+1)$, the left side of the cell $(x, y)$, is a side of some cell of $S$ if and only if $(x, y) \in S$ or $(x-1, y) \in S$.
