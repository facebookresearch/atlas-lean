# Number of pinch points of a lattice region

For a finite set $K$ of unit cells, the number (as an integer) of corners $v$ of cells of $K$ whose angle defect $4 - 2\,d_E(v) + d_C(v)$ equals $-2$, where $d_E(v)$ is the number of sides of cells of $K$ with endpoint $v$ and $d_C(v)$ the number of cells of $K$ with corner $v$. These are the pinch points, where two cells of $K$ meet only at a corner.
