# Change of the Euler characteristic when a cell is added

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a finite set of unit cells and $c \notin S$ a cell. Then

$$\chi(S \cup \{c\}) = \chi(S) + \big(V_{\mathrm{new}} - E_{\mathrm{new}} + 1\big),$$

where $\chi$ is the Euler characteristic $V - E + F$, $V_{\mathrm{new}}$ is the number of corners of $c$ that are not corners of cells of $S$, and $E_{\mathrm{new}}$ is the number of sides of $c$ that are not sides of cells of $S$.
