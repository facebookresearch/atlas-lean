# Edge codes of a unit cell are its doubled edge midpoints

For every unit cell $c = (a, b)$, labelled by its lower left corner, applying the map $\{p, q\} \mapsto p + q$ (twice the midpoint) to its four sides $\{(a, b), (a+1, b)\}$, $\{(a, b+1), (a+1, b+1)\}$, $\{(a, b), (a, b+1)\}$, $\{(a+1, b), (a+1, b+1)\}$ gives exactly the set

$$\{(2a+1, 2b),\ (2a+1, 2b+2),\ (2a, 2b+1),\ (2a+2, 2b+1)\}$$

of doubled midpoints of the sides of $c$.
