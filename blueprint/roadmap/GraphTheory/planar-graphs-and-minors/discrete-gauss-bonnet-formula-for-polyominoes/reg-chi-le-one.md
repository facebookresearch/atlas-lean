# Regular Euler characteristic bound via the open-top skeleton

Let $K$ be a finite set of unit cells. If the open-top skeleton of $K$ (the graph on the corners of cells of $K$ whose edges are the sides of cells of $K$ other than top sides) is connected (in particular nonempty), then $V - E + F \le 1$, where $V$ and $E$ are the numbers of distinct corners and sides of cells of $K$ and $F = |K|$.
