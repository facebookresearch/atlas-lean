# Corner count as a count of the four surrounding cells

For a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and a lattice point $v = (x, y)$, the number of cells of $S$ having $v$ as a corner equals

$$\big|S \cap \{(x, y), (x-1, y), (x, y-1), (x-1, y-1)\}\big|.$$
