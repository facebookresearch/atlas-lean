# Prism

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$, with darts (ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of adjacent vertices) and a permutation $\rho$ of the darts. The pair $(G, \rho)$ is a prism when for some natural number $N$ it has with exactly $2N$ vertices, for every size $k$ exactly $[k = N] \cdot 2 + [k = 4] \cdot N$ faces of size $k$, and face vector $\{4, 4, N\}$ at every vertex (so two faces of size $N$ and $N$ quadrilaterals, counted together when $N = 4$).
