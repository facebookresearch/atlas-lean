# Face permutation of a rotation system

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$, with darts (ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of adjacent vertices) and a permutation $\rho$ of the darts. The face permutation sends the dart $(u, v)$ to $\rho(v, u)$: the facial walk traversing $(u, v)$ continues with $\rho$ applied to the reversed dart.

The words face, corner and facial walk describe an embedding only when $\rho$ is a [rotation system](is-rotation-system.md); for an arbitrary permutation $\rho$ the quantities here are orbit counts of the face permutation.
