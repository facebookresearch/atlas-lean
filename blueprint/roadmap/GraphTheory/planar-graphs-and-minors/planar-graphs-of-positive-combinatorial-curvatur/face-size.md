# Size of a face

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$, with darts (ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of adjacent vertices) and a permutation $\rho$ of the darts. The size of the face through a dart $d$ is the least period of $d$ under the face permutation $\varphi(u, v) = \rho(v, u)$ (the length of the boundary walk, edges counted with multiplicity; $0$ if $d$ is not periodic).
