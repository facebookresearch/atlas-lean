# Number of faces

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$, with darts (ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of adjacent vertices) and a permutation $\rho$ of the darts. The number of faces is the rational number $\sum_d 1/|f(d)|$, the sum over all darts $d$ of the reciprocal of the size $|f(d)|$ of the face through $d$ (each face of size $s$ has $s$ darts and contributes $1$; a term with $|f(d)| = 0$ is $0$).

The words face, corner and facial walk describe an embedding only when $\rho$ is a [rotation system](is-rotation-system.md); for an arbitrary permutation $\rho$ the quantities here are orbit counts of the face permutation.
