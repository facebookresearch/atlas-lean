# Face vector at a vertex

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$, with darts (ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of adjacent vertices) and a permutation $\rho$ of the darts. The face vector at a vertex $v$ is the multiset of the sizes of the faces through the darts at $v$, one entry per corner, the size of a face being the least period under the face permutation $\varphi(u, v) = \rho(v, u)$.

The words face, corner and facial walk describe an embedding only when $\rho$ is a [rotation system](is-rotation-system.md); for an arbitrary permutation $\rho$ the quantities here are orbit counts of the face permutation.
