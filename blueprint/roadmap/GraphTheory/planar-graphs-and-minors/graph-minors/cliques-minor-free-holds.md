# $K_t$-minor-free graphs have at most $c^t n$ cliques

There is a real constant $c$ such that for every positive integer $t$, every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with $n$ vertices that does not have the complete graph $K_t$ as a minor has at most $c^t n$ nonempty cliques (sets of pairwise adjacent vertices, maximal or not). Minors are obtained by deleting vertices or edges and contracting edges, up to isomorphism. That is, the proposition [Wood's question on cliques in $K_t$-minor-free graphs](cliques-minor-free.md) holds.
