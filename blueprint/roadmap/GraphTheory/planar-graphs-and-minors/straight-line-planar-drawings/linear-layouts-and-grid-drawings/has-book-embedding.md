# Book embedding

A simple graph $G$ has a $k$-page book embedding when there are an injective map $\mathrm{pos}$ from its vertices to the natural numbers (the order along the spine) and an assignment of the edges to pages $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ such that any two edges that cross with respect to $\mathrm{pos}$ are on different pages; two edges $vw$ and $xy$ cross when $\mathrm{pos}(v) < \mathrm{pos}(x) < \mathrm{pos}(w) < \mathrm{pos}(y)$ for some labelling of their ends.
