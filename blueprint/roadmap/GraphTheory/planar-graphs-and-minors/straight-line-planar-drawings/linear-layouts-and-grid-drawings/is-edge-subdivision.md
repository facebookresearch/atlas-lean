# Once-subdivided graph

For simple graphs $G$ on $V$ and $G'$ on $V'$ and maps $\mathrm{old} : V \to V'$ and $\mathrm{mid}$ from the edges of $G$ to $V'$, $G'$ is the subdivision of $G$ in which every edge is subdivided exactly once when $\mathrm{old}$ and $\mathrm{mid}$ are injective with disjoint images covering $V'$, $\mathrm{old}(a)$ is adjacent to $\mathrm{mid}(e)$ for every edge $e$ and every end $a$ of $e$, every neighbour of $\mathrm{mid}(e)$ is $\mathrm{old}(a)$ for an end $a$ of $e$, and every neighbour of $\mathrm{old}(a)$ is $\mathrm{mid}(e)$ for an edge $e$ containing $a$.
