# Heath–Leighton–Rosenberg conjecture on queue-number

The statement that there is a natural number $Q$ such that every planar simple graph on a finite vertex set, planar meaning that it has a straight-line drawing in the plane with distinct points for distinct vertices and no two edges meeting except at common endpoints (for finite graphs, equivalent to planarity by Fáry's theorem), has a $Q$-queue layout: a linear order of the vertices and an assignment of the edges to $Q$ queues with no two nested edges in the same queue. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Planar graphs have bounded queue-number](planar-graphs-have-bounded-queue-number-2.md).
