# Obstacle representation

A simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ has an obstacle representation with $h$ obstacles when there are distinct points $p_i$ in the plane for its vertices and $h$ connected (in particular nonempty) sets $O_1, \dots, O_h$ in the plane such that no obstacle contains a vertex point, no obstacle meets the segment of an edge, and the segment between the points of every pair of distinct non-adjacent vertices meets some obstacle.
