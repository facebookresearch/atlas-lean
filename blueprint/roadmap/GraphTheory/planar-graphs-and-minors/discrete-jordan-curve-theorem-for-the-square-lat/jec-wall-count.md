# Wall-crossing count of a walk

For a site $z \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ and a walk $w$ between two sites of the square lattice, the wall count of $z$ with respect to $w$ is the number of steps of $w$, counted with multiplicity, along horizontal edges $\{(z_0 - 1, r), (z_0, r)\}$ with $r \le z_1 - 1$, the edges crossed by the vertical ray going down from $(z_0 - \tfrac12, z_1 - \tfrac12)$.
