# Adjacency relation of two corner-sharing squares

The adjacency relation of the graph on $\{0, 1, \dots, 6\}$ with edges $\{0, 1\}, \{1, 2\}, \{2, 3\}, \{3, 0\}, \{2, 4\}, \{4, 5\}, \{5, 6\}, \{6, 2\}$ (two $4$-cycles sharing the vertex $2$), as a Boolean-valued function true exactly on the ordered pairs $(i, j)$ with $\{i, j\}$ one of these edges.
