# Ray-crossing count of a walk

For a site $z \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ and a walk $w$ between two sites of the square lattice, the ray count of $z$ with respect to $w$ is the number of steps of $w$, counted with multiplicity, along vertical edges $\{(c, z_1 - 1), (c, z_1)\}$ with $c \le z_0 - 1$, the edges crossed by the horizontal ray going left from $(z_0 - \tfrac12, z_1 - \tfrac12)$.
