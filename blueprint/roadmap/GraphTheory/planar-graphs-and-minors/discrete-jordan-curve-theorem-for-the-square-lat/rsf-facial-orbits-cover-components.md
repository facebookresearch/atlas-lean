# Facial orbits cover the components of the complement

For a closed walk $w$ at a site $a$ of the square lattice and a family $\mathcal{F}$ of sets of darts, this property holds when $\mathcal{F}$ is exactly the family of sets $\partial C$, where $C$ ranges over the connected components of the subgraph of the square lattice induced on the sites not visited by $w$ and $\partial C$ is the set of darts with tail in $C$ and head outside $C$.
