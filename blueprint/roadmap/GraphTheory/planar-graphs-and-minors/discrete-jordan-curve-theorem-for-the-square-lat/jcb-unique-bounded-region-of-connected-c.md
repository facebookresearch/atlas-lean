# Jordan curve theorem

Let $P$ be a finite connected graph $G = (V, E)$ (in particular $V \neq \varnothing$) with an injective map into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, and suppose $|E| = |V|$. Then $P$ has exactly one bounded region: the face-region graph of its image (two adjacent unit faces joined unless the primal edge separating them is an edge of the image) has exactly one connected component with finitely many faces.
