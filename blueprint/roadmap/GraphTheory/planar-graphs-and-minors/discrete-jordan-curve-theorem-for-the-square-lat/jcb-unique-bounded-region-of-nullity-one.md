# Cycle rank one gives a unique bounded region

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, and suppose its cycle rank $|E| + c(G) - |V|$ equals $1$, where $c(G)$ is the number of connected components. Then $P$ has exactly one bounded region: exactly one connected component with finitely many faces in the face-region graph of its image.
