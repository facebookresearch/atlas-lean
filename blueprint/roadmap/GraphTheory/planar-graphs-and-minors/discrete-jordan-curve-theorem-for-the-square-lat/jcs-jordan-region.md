# Jordan region of the cut lattice

A set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ has the Jordan region property when $S$ is nonempty, $\mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus S$ is nonempty, and, in the square lattice with every edge having exactly one endpoint in $S$ removed, any two sites of $S$ are joined by a walk and any two sites of $\mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus S$ are joined by a walk.
