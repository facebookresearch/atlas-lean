# Bounded regions of a planar lattice subgraph

Let $P$ be a finite graph with an injective map into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, with image graph $H$. Label unit faces by their lower left corners and let the face-region graph of $H$ join two adjacent faces unless the primal edge separating them is an edge of $H$. The bounded regions of $P$ are the connected components of the face-region graph with finitely many faces.
