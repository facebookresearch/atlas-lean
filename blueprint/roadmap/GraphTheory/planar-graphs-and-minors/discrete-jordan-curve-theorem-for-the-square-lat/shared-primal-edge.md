# Primal edge shared by adjacent faces

For sites $f, g \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, read as unit faces labelled by their lower left corners, the unordered pair of sites

$$\begin{cases} \{(f_0, m), (f_0 + 1, m)\}, & m = \max(f_1, g_1), \text{ if } f_0 = g_0, \\ \{(m', f_1), (m', f_1 + 1)\}, & m' = \max(f_0, g_0), \text{ otherwise}. \end{cases}$$

When $f$ and $g$ are nearest neighbours, it is the primal edge of the square lattice separating the two faces.
