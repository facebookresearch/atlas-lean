# Adjacency relation of two disjoint 4-cycles

The adjacency relation of two disjoint $4$-cycles on $\{0, 1, \dots, 7\}$, with edges $\{0, 1\}, \{1, 2\}, \{2, 3\}, \{3, 0\}, \{4, 5\}, \{5, 6\}, \{6, 7\}, \{7, 4\}$, as a Boolean-valued function true exactly on the ordered pairs $(i, j)$ with $\{i, j\}$ one of these edges.
