# Even-peelable graph

Even-peelable graphs on a vertex set $V$ are defined inductively:

- the graph with no edges is even-peelable;
- if $K$ is even (every vertex has even degree), $c$ is a cycle in $K$ based at a vertex $v$, every vertex of $c$ other than $v$ has no neighbours in $K$ after the edges of $c$ are deleted, and the graph obtained from $K$ by deleting the edges of $c$ is even-peelable, then $K$ is even-peelable.
