# Vertices of two unit squares sharing a corner

The map from $\{0, 1, \dots, 6\}$ to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending

$$0 \mapsto (0, 0),\ 1 \mapsto (1, 0),\ 2 \mapsto (1, 1),\ 3 \mapsto (0, 1),\ 4 \mapsto (2, 1),\ 5 \mapsto (2, 2),\ 6 \mapsto (1, 2),$$

the corners of the two unit squares $[0, 1]^2$ and $[1, 2]^2$, which share the corner $(1, 1)$.
