# Rotated region graph of a lattice subgraph

For a simple graph $H$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, the region graph of $H$ is the simple graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ in which $f$ and $g$ are adjacent when they are nearest neighbours and $\{\rho^{-1}(f), \rho^{-1}(g)\}$ is not an edge of $H$, where $\rho(a, b) = (-b, a)$ is the quarter-turn rotation. Since $\rho$ is a global rotation rather than the map sending a primal edge to the dual edge crossing it, this is the rotated image of the square lattice minus the edges of $H$, not the dual complement of $H$.
