# 8-cycle around the L-tromino as a planar lattice subgraph

The $8$-cycle around the L-shaped tromino as a finite graph in the square lattice: the cycle on $\{0, 1, \dots, 7\}$ with $i \sim i + 1 \pmod 8$, embedded by sending $0, \dots, 7$ to $(0, 0), (1, 0), (2, 0), (2, 1), (1, 1), (1, 2), (0, 2), (0, 1)$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
