# Two corner-sharing unit squares as a planar lattice subgraph

Two unit squares sharing a corner, as a finite graph in the square lattice: the graph on $\{0, 1, \dots, 6\}$ formed by the $4$-cycles $0 - 1 - 2 - 3 - 0$ and $2 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 2$, embedded by $0 \mapsto (0, 0)$, $1 \mapsto (1, 0)$, $2 \mapsto (1, 1)$, $3 \mapsto (0, 1)$, $4 \mapsto (2, 1)$, $5 \mapsto (2, 2)$, $6 \mapsto (1, 2)$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
