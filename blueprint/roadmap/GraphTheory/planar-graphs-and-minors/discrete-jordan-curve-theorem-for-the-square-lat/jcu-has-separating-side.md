# Separating side between inside and outside sites

For sets $D, B, I, O \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, this property holds when there is a set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ with $I \subseteq S$ and $O \cap S = \varnothing$ such that every pair of nearest-neighbour sites $u, v \in D$ with $u \in S$ and $v \notin S$ has $u \in B$ or $v \in B$.
