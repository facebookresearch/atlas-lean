# Facial orbit of a boundary dart

For a set $C \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and a boundary dart $e$ of $C$ (tail in $C$, head outside $C$), the facial orbit of $e$ is the connected component containing $e$ of the successor graph on boundary darts of $C$ (distinct boundary darts $a, b$ adjacent when one is the boundary successor of the other).
