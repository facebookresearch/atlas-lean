# Planar lattice subgraph of the square lattice

A finite graph in the square lattice consists of a finite vertex set $V$ (with decidable equality), a simple graph $G$ on $V$, and an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ such that $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ are nearest neighbours whenever $\{x, y\}$ is an edge of $G$.
