# Nine sites of a 3 by 3 grid

The map from $\{0, 1, \dots, 8\}$ to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending $i$ to $(i \bmod 3, \lfloor i/3 \rfloor)$, that is, $0, 1, 2 \mapsto (0, 0), (1, 0), (2, 0)$, $3, 4, 5 \mapsto (0, 1), (1, 1), (2, 1)$ and $6, 7, 8 \mapsto (0, 2), (1, 2), (2, 2)$: the nine sites of $\{0, 1, 2\}^2$ indexed row by row.
