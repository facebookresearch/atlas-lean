# Ray edges left of a site

For a site $z \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, an unordered pair of sites is a ray edge of $z$ when it is a vertical lattice edge $\{(c, z_1 - 1), (c, z_1)\}$ with $c \le z_0 - 1$; these are the edges crossed by the horizontal ray going left from the point $(z_0 - \tfrac12, z_1 - \tfrac12)$.
