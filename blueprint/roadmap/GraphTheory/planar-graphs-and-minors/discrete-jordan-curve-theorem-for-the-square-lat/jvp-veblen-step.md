# Veblen step property for even-degree subgraphs

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, and let $F(K) = \beta(K) + 1$ be the face count of a subgraph $K$ ($\beta$ the cycle rank). $P$ has this property when every subgraph $K \le G$ with at least one edge whose image $\iota(K)$ has even degree at every site has a subgraph $K' \le K$ whose image also has even degree at every site, with $|E(K')| < |E(K)|$ and $F(K') + 1 = F(K)$.
