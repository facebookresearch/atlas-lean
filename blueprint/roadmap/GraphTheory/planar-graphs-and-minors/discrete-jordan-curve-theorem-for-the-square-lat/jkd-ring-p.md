# 3 by 3 grid as a planar lattice subgraph

The $3 \times 3$ grid as a finite graph in the square lattice: the grid graph on $\{0, 1, \dots, 8\}$ (vertices $i$ and $j$ adjacent when their positions $(i \bmod 3, \lfloor i/3 \rfloor)$ and $(j \bmod 3, \lfloor j/3 \rfloor)$ are nearest neighbours), embedded by $i \mapsto (i \bmod 3, \lfloor i/3 \rfloor)$ onto $\{0, 1, 2\}^2$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
