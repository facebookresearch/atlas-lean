# Bounded regions are the components other than the outer one

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ together with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, and let $H$ be its image in $\mathbb{Z}^2$. Let the region graph of $H$ be the square lattice with every edge $\{f, g\}$ removed for which $\{\rho^{-1}(f), \rho^{-1}(g)\}$ is an edge of $H$, where $\rho(a, b) = (-b, a)$. Fix $R$ with $\iota(V) \subseteq \{-R, \dots, R\}^2$ and let the outer region be the connected component of the region graph containing $(R+1, R+1)$. The bijection between the connected components of the region graph with finitely many vertices and the components other than the outer region, which is the identity on components: a component is finite if and only if it is not the outer region.
