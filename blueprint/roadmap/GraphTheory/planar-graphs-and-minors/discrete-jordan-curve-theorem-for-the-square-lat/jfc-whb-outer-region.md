# Outer face region of a planar lattice subgraph

Let $P$ be a finite graph with an injective map $\iota$ into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, and fix $R$ with all $\iota(v)$ in $\{-R, \dots, R\}^2$. The outer region of $P$ is the connected component of the face $(R + 2, R + 2)$ in the face-region graph of the image of $P$ (two adjacent unit faces joined unless the primal edge separating them is an edge of the image).
