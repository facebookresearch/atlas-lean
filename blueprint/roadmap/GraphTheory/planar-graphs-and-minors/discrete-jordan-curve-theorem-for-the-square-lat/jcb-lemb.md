# Lattice points around the L-shaped tromino

The map from $\{0, 1, \dots, 7\}$ to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending $0, 1, \dots, 7$ to

$$(0, 0),\ (1, 0),\ (2, 0),\ (2, 1),\ (1, 1),\ (1, 2),\ (0, 2),\ (0, 1),$$

the eight lattice points on the boundary of the L-shaped tromino $[0, 2] \times [0, 1] \cup [0, 1] \times [1, 2]$, in cyclic order.
