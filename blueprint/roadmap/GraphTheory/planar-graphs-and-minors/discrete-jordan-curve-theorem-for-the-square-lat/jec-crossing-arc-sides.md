# Open vertical crossings separate the sides of a rectangle

Consider a bond configuration $\omega$ on the square lattice and integers $\alpha < \beta$, $c \le d$, $m$, $m'$ with $\alpha \le m \le m' \le \beta$. Let $R = [\alpha, \beta] \times [c, d]$ and $R' = [m, m'] \times [c, d]$ (sets of lattice sites). For every site $x_B$ on the bottom side $[m, m'] \times \{c\}$ of $R'$ and every site $y_T$ on its top side $[m, m'] \times \{d\}$ such that $x_B$ and $y_T$ are joined by an open path inside $R'$, there is a set $L \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ containing every site of $R$ with first coordinate $\alpha$, containing no site of $R$ with first coordinate $\beta$, and such that every lattice edge $\{u, v\}$ with $u, v \in R$ and exactly one endpoint in $L$ has an endpoint in the open cluster of $x_B$ inside $R'$.
