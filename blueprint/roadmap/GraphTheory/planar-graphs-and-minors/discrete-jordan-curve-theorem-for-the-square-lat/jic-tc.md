# Number of face regions of a subgraph's image

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, and let $K$ be a simple graph on $V$. The number of connected components of the face-region graph of $\iota(K)$ (unit faces, adjacent faces joined unless their separating primal edge is an edge of $\iota(K)$), as a natural number (with the convention that an infinite number of components counts as $0$).
