# Unit square with a pendant edge as a planar lattice subgraph

The unit square with a pendant edge, as a finite graph in the square lattice: the graph on $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ with edges $\{0, 1\}, \{1, 2\}, \{2, 3\}, \{3, 0\}, \{0, 4\}$, embedded by $0 \mapsto (0, 0)$, $1 \mapsto (1, 0)$, $2 \mapsto (1, 1)$, $3 \mapsto (0, 1)$, $4 \mapsto (-1, 0)$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
