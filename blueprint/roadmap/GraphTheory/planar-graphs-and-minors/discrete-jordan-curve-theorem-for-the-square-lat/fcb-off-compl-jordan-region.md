# Jordan region property off a closed walk

Let $w$ be a closed walk at a site $a$ of the square lattice and $I$ its inside by the even–odd rule (sites with odd ray count with respect to $w$). The walk has this property when there is a site of $I$ not visited by $w$, there is a site outside $I$ not visited by $w$, any two sites of $I$ not visited by $w$ are joined by a lattice walk visiting no site of $w$, and any two sites outside $I$ not visited by $w$ are joined by a lattice walk visiting no site of $w$.
