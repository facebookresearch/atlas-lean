# Two disjoint unit squares as a planar lattice subgraph

Two disjoint unit squares as a finite graph in the square lattice: the two disjoint $4$-cycles $0 - 1 - 2 - 3 - 0$ and $4 - 5 - 6 - 7 - 4$, embedded by sending $0, 1, 2, 3$ to $(0, 0), (1, 0), (1, 1), (0, 1)$ and $4, 5, 6, 7$ to $(10, 0), (11, 0), (11, 1), (10, 1)$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
