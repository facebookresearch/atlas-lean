# Inside of a closed walk by the even-odd rule

For a closed walk $w$ at a site $a$ of the square lattice, its inside by the even–odd rule is the set of sites $z \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ whose ray count with respect to $w$ is odd, the ray count being the number of steps of $w$ along vertical edges $\{(c, z_1 - 1), (c, z_1)\}$ with $c \le z_0 - 1$ (the edges crossed by the horizontal ray going left from $(z_0 - \tfrac12, z_1 - \tfrac12)$).
