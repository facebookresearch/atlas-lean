# Radius of a box containing a planar lattice subgraph

For a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, a natural number $R$ such that $\iota(v) \in \{-R, \dots, R\}^2$ for every $v \in V$ (one such $R$, chosen once and for all; it exists since $V$ is finite).
