# Lower 4-cycle left after deleting the upper one

The simple graph on $\{0, 1, \dots, 6\}$ whose edges are $\{0, 1\}, \{1, 2\}, \{2, 3\}, \{3, 0\}$ (vertices $4, 5, 6$ are isolated). It is what remains of the graph formed by the two $4$-cycles $0 - 1 - 2 - 3 - 0$ and $2 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 2$ after deleting the edges of the second one.
