# Pushed-forward subgraphs are locally finite

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, and let $K \le G$. Then the image $\iota(K)$, a graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, is locally finite: every site has finitely many neighbours (at most four, since $\iota(K)$ is a subgraph of the square lattice).
