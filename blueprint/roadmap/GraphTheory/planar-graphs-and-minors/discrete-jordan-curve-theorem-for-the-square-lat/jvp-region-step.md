# Region step property for even-degree subgraphs

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges. For a subgraph $K \le G$ let $F(K) = \beta(K) + 1$ be its face count ($\beta$ the cycle rank) and $r(K)$ the number of connected components of the face-region graph of $\iota(K)$. $P$ has this property when, for all subgraphs $K' \le K \le G$ whose images both have even degree at every site, with $|E(K')| < |E(K)|$ and $F(K') + 1 = F(K)$, one has $r(K) = r(K') + 1$.
