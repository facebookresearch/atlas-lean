# Disconnected sites are joined in the lattice minus a bridged graph

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice. $P$ has this property when, for every subgraph $K \le G$ and every edge $\{x, y\}$ of $G$ that is not an edge of $K$ and whose endpoints are not joined by any walk in $K$, the sites $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ are joined by a walk in the square lattice from which the edges of the image under $\iota$ of $K$ together with the edge $\{x, y\}$ are deleted.
