# Image of a planar lattice subgraph in the square lattice

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ together with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges of $G$ to edges of the square lattice. Its image graph is the simple graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ in which $a$ and $b$ are adjacent when $a = \iota(x)$ and $b = \iota(y)$ for some edge $\{x, y\}$ of $G$; it is a subgraph of the square lattice.
