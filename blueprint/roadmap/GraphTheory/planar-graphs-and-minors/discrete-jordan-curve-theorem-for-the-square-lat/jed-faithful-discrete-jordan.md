# Bounded faces of a finite lattice graph are counted by its cycle rank

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, with image $H$. Label unit faces by their lower left corners and let the face-region graph of $H$ join two adjacent faces unless the primal edge separating them is an edge of $H$. Then the connected components of the face-region graph with finitely many faces are in bijection with $\{0, 1, \dots, \beta(G) - 1\}$, where $\beta(G) = |E| + c(G) - |V|$ is the cycle rank and $c(G)$ the number of connected components of $G$. So the number of bounded faces is the cycle rank; no particular bounded face is matched with a particular cycle.
