# Jordan curve theorem for cycles: face count two

Let $G$ be a simple graph and $c$ a cycle in $G$. The graph $C$ formed by the vertices and edges of $c$ has face count $\beta(C) + 1 = 2$, where $\beta(C) = |E(C)| + c(C) - |V(C)|$ is the cycle rank of $C$ and $c(C)$ its number of connected components; that is, $\beta(C) = 1$.

## Sources

- Camille Jordan, *Cours d'analyse de l'École polytechnique*, tome III, Gauthier-Villars, Paris, 1887, Note sur quelques points de la théorie des fonctions, §§39–45, pp. 587–594 (the separation statement in §44, p. 593): the Jordan curve theorem.
