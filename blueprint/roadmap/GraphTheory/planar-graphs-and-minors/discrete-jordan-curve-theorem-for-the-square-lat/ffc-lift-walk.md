# Lattice walk avoiding a set, lifted to the reduced lattice

Let $B \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and let $p$ be a walk from $x$ to $y$ in the square lattice none of whose vertices lies in $B$. The same sequence of vertices is a walk from $x$ to $y$ in the square lattice with every edge having an endpoint in $B$ deleted; this is the lifted walk.
