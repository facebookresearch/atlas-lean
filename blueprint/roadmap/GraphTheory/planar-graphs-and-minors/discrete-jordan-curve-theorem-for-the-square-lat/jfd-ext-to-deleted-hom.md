# Exterior of a box maps into the face-region graph

Let $H$ be a simple graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $R$ a natural number and $f_0, g_0 \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, and suppose every non-isolated vertex of $H$ and both $f_0$ and $g_0$ lie in $\{-R, \dots, R\}^2$. Then the inclusion is a graph homomorphism from the subgraph of the square lattice induced on the exterior $\{x : |x_0| > R + 1 \text{ or } |x_1| > R + 1\}$ to the face-region graph of $H$ (adjacent unit faces joined unless their separating primal edge is an edge of $H$) with the edge $\{f_0, g_0\}$ deleted.
