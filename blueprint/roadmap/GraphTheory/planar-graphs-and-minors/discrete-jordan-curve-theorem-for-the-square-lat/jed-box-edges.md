# Twelve boundary edges of a 3 by 3 rectangle

The set of the twelve lattice edges on the boundary of the rectangle $[-1, 2] \times [-2, 1]$: the horizontal edges $\{(i, -2), (i+1, -2)\}$ and $\{(i, 1), (i+1, 1)\}$ for $i = -1, 0, 1$, and the vertical edges $\{(-1, j), (-1, j+1)\}$ and $\{(2, j), (2, j+1)\}$ for $j = -2, -1, 0$.
