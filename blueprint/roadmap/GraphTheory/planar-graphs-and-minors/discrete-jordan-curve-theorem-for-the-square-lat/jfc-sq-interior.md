# Interior region of the unit square

Let $H$ be the boundary of the unit square $[0, 1]^2$, the $4$-cycle on $(0, 0), (1, 0), (1, 1), (0, 1)$ in the square lattice. The interior region is the connected component of the face $(0, 0)$ (the face $[0, 1]^2$) in the face-region graph of $H$, in which two adjacent unit faces are joined unless the primal edge separating them is an edge of $H$.
