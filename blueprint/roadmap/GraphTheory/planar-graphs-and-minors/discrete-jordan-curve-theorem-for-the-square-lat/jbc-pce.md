# Unit square as a planar lattice subgraph

The unit square as a finite graph in the square lattice: the $4$-cycle on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ with edges $\{0, 1\}, \{1, 2\}, \{2, 3\}, \{3, 0\}$, embedded by $0 \mapsto (0, 0)$, $1 \mapsto (1, 0)$, $2 \mapsto (1, 1)$, $3 \mapsto (0, 1)$, which is injective and sends edges to lattice edges.
