# Pushforward of a subgraph into the square lattice

Let $P$ be a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map $\iota : V \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to edges of the square lattice, and let $K$ be a simple graph on $V$. The pushforward of $K$ is the simple graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ in which $a$ and $b$ are adjacent when $a = \iota(x)$ and $b = \iota(y)$ for some edge $\{x, y\}$ of $K$.
