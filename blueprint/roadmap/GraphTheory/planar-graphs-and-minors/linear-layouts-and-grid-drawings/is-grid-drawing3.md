# Three-dimensional grid drawing

A $3$-dimensional grid drawing of a simple graph $G$ is an injective map $p$ from its vertices to $\mathbb{Z}^3$ such that, drawing each edge $uv$ as the straight segment from $p(u)$ to $p(v)$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$, no edge segment contains the point of a vertex other than its ends, and the segments of two distinct edges meet only in points that are common ends of both.
