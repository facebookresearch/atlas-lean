# Three-step detour avoiding an edge

For a simple graph $A$, a list $L$ of unordered pairs of vertices and an unordered pair $e$, this property holds when $e = \{a, b\}$ is an edge of $A$ and there are vertices $a', b'$ with $a \sim a'$, $a' \sim b'$, $b' \sim b$ in $A$ such that none of the three edges $\{a, a'\}, \{a', b'\}, \{b', b\}$ equals $e$ or belongs to $L$: a three-step detour from $a$ to $b$ avoiding $e$ and the edges of $L$.
