# Graph stays connected with a given bridge after deleting edges

For a simple graph $A$ on a vertex set $V$, a set $B'$ of unordered pairs of vertices and vertices $a, b$, this property holds when the graph $A \setminus B'$ obtained by deleting the edges in $B'$ is connected, $\{a, b\}$ is an edge of $A \setminus B'$, and $a$ and $b$ are not joined by any walk in $A \setminus B'$ after the edge $\{a, b\}$ is also deleted; that is, $\{a, b\}$ is a bridge of the connected graph $A \setminus B'$.
