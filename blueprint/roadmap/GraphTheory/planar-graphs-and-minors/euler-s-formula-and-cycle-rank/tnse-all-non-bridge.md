# Successive deletion of non-bridge edges

For a simple graph $A$ and a list $L = (e_1, \dots, e_k)$ of unordered pairs of vertices, this property holds when, for each $j = 1, \dots, k$, the pair $e_j$ is a non-bridge edge of the graph obtained from $A$ by deleting $e_{j+1}, \dots, e_k$: it is an edge $\{a, b\}$ of that graph and $a, b$ remain joined by a walk after it is deleted. Thus, deleting the edges of $L$ from the last to the first, each is a non-bridge at the moment it is deleted. The empty list has the property.
