# Euler's formula for the grid graph $\{-n, \dots, n\}^2$

For every natural number $n$, the grid graph on $\{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ satisfies

$$V + F = E + 2,$$

where $V = (2n+1)^2$ is the number of its vertices, $E$ the number of its horizontal and vertical unit edges, and $F = (2n)^2 + 1$ the number of its unit squares plus one for the unbounded face.
