# Closed walk around a boundary dart orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The orbit walk of $a$ is the closed walk

$$a,\ \sigma_K(a),\ \sigma_K^2(a),\ \dots,\ \sigma_K^p(a) = a$$

of length $p$ in the successor graph on the boundary darts of $K$ (the trivial walk at $a$ when $p = 0$).
