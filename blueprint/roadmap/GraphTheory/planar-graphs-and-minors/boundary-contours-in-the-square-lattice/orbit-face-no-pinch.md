# No-pinch condition for a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The orbit of $a$ satisfies the no-pinch condition when, for all $i, j \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$, if the unit faces to the left of $\sigma_K^i(a)$ and of $\sigma_K^j(a)$ coincide, then these two darts have the same direction.
