# Minimal-period orbit loop as a lattice walk

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). Label each unit face by its lower left corner and let $F(e)$ be the face to the left of a dart $e$. The closed walk $F(a), F(\sigma_K(a)), \dots, F(\sigma_K^p(a)) = F(a)$ of face labels, regarded as a closed walk in the square lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$ (consecutive faces share a side, so their labels are nearest neighbours).
