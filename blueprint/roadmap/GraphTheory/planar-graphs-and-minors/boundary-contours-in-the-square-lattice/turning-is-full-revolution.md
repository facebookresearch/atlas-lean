# Boundary orbit making exactly one full revolution

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The orbit of $a$ makes exactly one full revolution when its total signed turning $\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_K(\sigma_K^i(a))$ equals $4$ or $-4$, where $\tau_K \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ is the signed quarter turn.
