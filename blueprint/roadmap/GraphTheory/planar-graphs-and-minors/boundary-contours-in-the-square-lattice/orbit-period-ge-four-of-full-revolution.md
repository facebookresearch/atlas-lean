# Boundary orbit with a full revolution has length at least four

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). If the total signed turning $\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_K(\sigma_K^i(a))$ equals $4$ or $-4$, where $\tau_K \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ is the signed quarter turn, then $p \ge 4$.
