# Total turning over the minimal period is divisible by four

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ (tail in $K$, head outside $K$), and let $p$ be the minimal period of $a$ under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ restricted to boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). Let $\tau_K(e) \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ be the signed quarter turn from a dart $e$ to $\sigma_K(e)$. Then

$$4 \ \Big|\ \sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_K\big(\sigma_K^i(a)\big).$$
