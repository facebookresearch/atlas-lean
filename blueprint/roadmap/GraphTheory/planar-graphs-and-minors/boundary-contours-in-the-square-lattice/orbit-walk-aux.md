# Walk along the first iterates of a boundary dart

For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, a boundary dart $a$ of $K$ and a natural number $n$, the walk

$$a,\ \sigma_K(a),\ \sigma_K^2(a),\ \dots,\ \sigma_K^n(a)$$

of length $n$ in the successor graph on boundary darts of $K$, where $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map; consecutive darts are adjacent because $\sigma_K$ fixes no boundary dart.
