# Number of straight steps in a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $e$ be a dart and $p$ a natural number, and let $\sigma_K$ be the boundary successor map of $K$. The straight step count is the number of indices $i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ for which the signed quarter turn $\tau_K(\sigma_K^i(e))$ is $0$, as an integer; here, for a dart $f$ with tail $t$, head $h$ and direction $u$ and with $r = (u_1, -u_0)$, $\tau_K(f) = 0$ means $h + r \notin K$ and $t + r \in K$.
