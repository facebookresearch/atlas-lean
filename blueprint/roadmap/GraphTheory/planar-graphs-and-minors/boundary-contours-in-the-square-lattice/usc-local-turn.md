# Local turn of a boundary orbit at a face

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic), and let $f$ be a unit face, labelled by its lower left corner. The local turn of the orbit of $a$ at $f$ is the integer

$$\sum_{i \in V_f} \tau_K\big(\sigma_K^i(a)\big), \qquad V_f = \{i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\} : \text{the face to the left of } \sigma_K^i(a) \text{ is } f\},$$

where $\tau_K \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ is the signed quarter turn from a dart to its boundary successor.
