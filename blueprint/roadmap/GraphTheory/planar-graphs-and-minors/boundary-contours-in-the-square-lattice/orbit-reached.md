# Site reached by a boundary orbit

For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, a dart $e$ and a site $v \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the orbit of $e$ reaches $v$ when some boundary dart $d$ of $K$ with head $v$ satisfies $d = \sigma_K^n(e)$ for some natural number $n$, where $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map of $K$.
