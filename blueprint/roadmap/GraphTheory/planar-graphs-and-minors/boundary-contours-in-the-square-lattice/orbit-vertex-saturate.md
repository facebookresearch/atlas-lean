# Orbit saturation at reached sites

A set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ has this property when, for every boundary dart $e$ of $K$ and every site $v$ reached by the orbit of $e$ (some $\sigma_K^n(e)$ is a boundary dart with head $v$), every boundary dart $d$ of $K$ with head $v$ is of the form $d = \sigma_K^m(e)$ for some natural number $m$. Here $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map of $K$.
