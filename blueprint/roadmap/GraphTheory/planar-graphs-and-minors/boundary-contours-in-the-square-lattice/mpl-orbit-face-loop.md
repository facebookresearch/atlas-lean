# Face loop left of one period of a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). Label each unit face of the square lattice by its lower left corner, and for a dart $e$ let $F(e)$ be the face to its left. The face loop of $a$ is the closed walk

$$F(a),\ F(\sigma_K(a)),\ \dots,\ F(\sigma_K^p(a)) = F(a)$$

in the face-boundary graph of $K$, in which two faces are adjacent when they share a side that is a primal edge with exactly one endpoint in $K$.
