# No diagonal touch along a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The orbit of $a$ has no diagonal touch when, for every $k \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$, $K$ has no diagonal touch at the unit face to the left of $\sigma_K^k(a)$ (that is, $K$ does not contain exactly two opposite corners of that face).
