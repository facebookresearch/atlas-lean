# Inner corner of a boundary dart

For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, a dart $e$ with head $h$ and direction $u = (u_0, u_1)$ has an inner corner at $K$ when $h + r \in K$, where $r = (u_1, -u_0)$ is $u$ rotated clockwise by a quarter turn. In that case the boundary successor of $e$ is the dart from $h + r$ to $h$, so it has the same head as $e$.
