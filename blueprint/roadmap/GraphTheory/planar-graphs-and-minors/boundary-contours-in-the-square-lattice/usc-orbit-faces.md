# Faces visited by a boundary orbit in one period

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The finite set of unit faces (labelled by their lower left corners) to the left of the darts $\sigma_K^i(a)$, $0 \le i \le p - 1$; it is empty when $p = 0$.
