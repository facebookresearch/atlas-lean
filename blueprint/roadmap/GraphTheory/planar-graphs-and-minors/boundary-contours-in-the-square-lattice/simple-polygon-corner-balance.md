# Corner balance of a simple lattice polygon

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). Let $R$ and $L$ be the numbers of indices $i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ for which the signed quarter turn $\tau_K(\sigma_K^i(a))$ equals $1$ (reflex corners) and $-1$ (convex corners) respectively. The orbit of $a$ satisfies the corner balance of a simple lattice polygon when $R - L = 4$ or $R - L = -4$.
