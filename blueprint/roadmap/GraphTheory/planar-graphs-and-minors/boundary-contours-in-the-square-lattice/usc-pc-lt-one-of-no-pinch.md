# $p_c(\mathbb{Z}^2) < 1$ under a no-pinch hypothesis

Consider Bernoulli bond percolation on the square lattice, and for a configuration $\omega$ let $C(\omega)$ be the open cluster of the origin. When $C(\omega)$ is finite, let $j$ be the least natural number with $(j+1, 0) \notin C(\omega)$ and $k$ the least natural number with $(-(k+1), 0) \notin C(\omega)$; the exit dart is the dart from $(j, 0)$ to $(j+1, 0)$ and the left exit dart the dart from $(-k, 0)$ to $(-k-1, 0)$, both boundary darts of $C(\omega)$. Suppose that for every configuration $\omega$ with $C(\omega)$ finite:

- the minimal period $p$ of the exit dart under the boundary successor map $\sigma$ of $C(\omega)$ satisfies $p \ge 3$;
- the orbit of the exit dart satisfies the no-pinch condition: two darts in one period of the orbit whose left faces coincide have the same direction;
- the left exit dart equals $\sigma^n$ of the exit dart for some natural number $n$.

Then the critical probability $p_c(\mathbb{Z}^2) = \sup\{p \in [0, 1] : \mathbb{P}_p(C \text{ is infinite}) = 0\}$ satisfies $p_c(\mathbb{Z}^2) < 1$.
