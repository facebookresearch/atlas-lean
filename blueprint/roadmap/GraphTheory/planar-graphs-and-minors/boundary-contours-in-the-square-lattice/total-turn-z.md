# Total signed turning along a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $e$ be a dart and $p$ a natural number, and let $\sigma_K$ be the boundary successor map of $K$. The total signed turning over the first $p$ steps of the orbit of $e$ is the integer

$$\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_K\big(\sigma_K^i(e)\big),$$

where $\tau_K(f) \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ is the signed quarter turn from $f$ to $\sigma_K(f)$.
