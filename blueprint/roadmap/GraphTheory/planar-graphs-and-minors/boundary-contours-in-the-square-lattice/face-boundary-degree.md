# Boundary degree of a unit face

For a set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and integers $a, b$, the boundary degree of the unit face $[a, a+1] \times [b, b+1]$ is the number of its four sides, the edges

$$\{(a, b), (a+1, b)\},\quad \{(a+1, b), (a+1, b+1)\},\quad \{(a+1, b+1), (a, b+1)\},\quad \{(a, b+1), (a, b)\},$$

that have exactly one endpoint in $S$. It is a natural number between $0$ and $4$.
