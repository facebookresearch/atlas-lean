# Successor graph on boundary darts

For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, the successor graph of $K$ is the simple graph whose vertices are the boundary darts of $K$ (darts with tail in $K$ and head outside $K$), two distinct boundary darts $a$ and $b$ being adjacent when $\sigma_K(a) = b$ or $\sigma_K(b) = a$, where $\sigma_K$ is the successor map along the boundary of $K$.
