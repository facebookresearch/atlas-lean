# Number of reflex corners in a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $e$ be a dart and $p$ a natural number, and let $\sigma_K$ be the boundary successor map of $K$. For a dart $f$ with tail $t$, head $h$ and direction $u$, let $r = (u_1, -u_0)$ be $u$ rotated clockwise by a quarter turn and let the signed turn $\tau_K(f)$ be $1$ if $h + r \in K$, $0$ if $h + r \notin K$ and $t + r \in K$, and $-1$ otherwise. The reflex corner count is the number of indices $i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ with $\tau_K(\sigma_K^i(e)) = 1$, as an integer.
