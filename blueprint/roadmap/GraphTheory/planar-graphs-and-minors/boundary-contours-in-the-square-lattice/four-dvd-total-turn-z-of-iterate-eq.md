# Total turning of a closed orbit segment is divisible by four

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $e$ be any dart of the square lattice and $p$ a natural number with $\sigma_K^p(e) = e$, where $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map of $K$. Let $\tau_K(f) \in \{1, 0, -1\}$ be the signed quarter turn from a dart $f$ to $\sigma_K(f)$. Then the total signed turning $\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_K(\sigma_K^i(e))$ is divisible by $4$.
