# Each face visited once per period by a boundary orbit

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic). The orbit of $a$ has face multiplicity one when, for every unit face $f$ (labelled by its lower left corner), the set of times $i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ at which the face to the left of $\sigma_K^i(a)$ is $f$ has at most one element.
