# Visit times of a boundary orbit to a face

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $a$ be a boundary dart of $K$ and $p$ its minimal period under the boundary successor map $\sigma_K$ on boundary darts ($p = 0$ if $a$ is not periodic), and let $f$ be a unit face, labelled by its lower left corner. The visit set of $f$ is the finite set of times $i \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ at which the face to the left of $\sigma_K^i(a)$ is $f$.
