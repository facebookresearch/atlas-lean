# Quarter-turn rotations form an action of $\mathbb{Z}/4$

Let $\rho(a, b) = (-b, a)$ be the counterclockwise quarter-turn rotation of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, and for $s \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ let $\rho^s$ be $\rho$ iterated $s$ times, with $s$ represented in $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$. Then for all $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ and $x \in \mathbb{Z}^2$,

$$\rho^{a+b}(x) = \rho^a\big(\rho^b(x)\big),$$

so $s \mapsto \rho^s$ is an action of the additive group $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$.
