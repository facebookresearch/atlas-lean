# Lattice connectivity in the complement implies king connectivity

A set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ has this property when any two sites $u, w \notin K$ that are joined by a walk in the subgraph of the square lattice induced on $\mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus K$ are also joined by a walk in the king-move graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus K$, in which distinct sites are adjacent when both coordinates differ by at most one. Since nearest neighbours are king neighbours, every set $K$ has this property.
