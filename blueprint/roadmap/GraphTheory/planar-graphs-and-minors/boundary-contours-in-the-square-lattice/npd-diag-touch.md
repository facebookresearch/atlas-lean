# Diagonal touch at a face

For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and a unit face $f$ with lower left corner $(a, b)$, $K$ has a diagonal touch at $f$ when $K$ contains exactly two opposite corners of $f$: either $(a, b), (a+1, b+1) \in K$ and $(a+1, b), (a, b+1) \notin K$, or $(a+1, b), (a, b+1) \in K$ and $(a, b), (a+1, b+1) \notin K$.
