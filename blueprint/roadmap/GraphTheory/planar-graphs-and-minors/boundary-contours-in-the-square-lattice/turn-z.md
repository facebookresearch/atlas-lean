# Signed quarter turn between consecutive boundary darts

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and let $e$ be a dart of the square lattice with tail $t$, head $h$ and direction $u = (u_0, u_1)$; let $r = (u_1, -u_0)$ be $u$ rotated clockwise by a quarter turn. The signed quarter turn of $e$ is the integer $1$ if $h + r \in K$, $0$ if $h + r \notin K$ and $t + r \in K$, and $-1$ otherwise: $1$ for a counterclockwise turn from $e$ to its boundary successor $\sigma_K(e)$, $0$ for going straight, $-1$ for a clockwise turn. Its reduction modulo $4$ is the turn of $e$ in $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$.
