# Boundary darts with heads in one component share an orbit

A set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ is interface connected when, for any two boundary darts $e$ and $f$ of $K$ (darts with tail in $K$ and head outside $K$) whose heads are joined by a walk in the subgraph of the square lattice induced on $\mathbb{Z}^2 \setminus K$, there is a natural number $n$ with $\sigma_K^n(e) = f$, where $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map of $K$.
