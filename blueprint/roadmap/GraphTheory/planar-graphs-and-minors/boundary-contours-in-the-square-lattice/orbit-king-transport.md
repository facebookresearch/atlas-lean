# Boundary orbits spread along king moves

A set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ has this property when, for every boundary dart $e$ of $K$ and all king-adjacent sites $u, v \notin K$ (distinct, with both coordinates differing by at most one): if some boundary dart $\sigma_K^n(e)$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, has head $u$, then some boundary dart $\sigma_K^m(e)$, $m \in \mathbb{N}$, has head $v$. Here $\sigma_K$ is the boundary successor map of $K$.
