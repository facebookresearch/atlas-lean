# Face-region graph of a square-lattice subgraph

For a simple graph $G$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, the face-region graph of $G$ is the simple graph on unit faces (labelled by lower left corners) in which two nearest-neighbour faces $f$ and $g$ are adjacent when the primal edge separating them is not an edge of $G$. Edges of $G$ that are not lattice edges play no role; for a subgraph $G$ of the square lattice it is the planar dual complement of $G$.
