# Faithful discrete Jordan property for even-degree subgraphs

The statement that every finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with an injective map into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ sending edges to lattice edges, whose image has even degree at every site, has exactly $\beta(G) = |E| + c(G) - |V|$ bounded faces: the connected components with finitely many faces of the face-region graph of its image are in bijection with $\{0, 1, \dots, \beta(G) - 1\}$.
