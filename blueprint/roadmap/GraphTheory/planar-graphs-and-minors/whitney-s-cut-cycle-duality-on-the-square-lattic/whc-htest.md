# Unit square with its bottom edge removed

The simple graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ obtained from the boundary of the unit square, the $4$-cycle on $(0, 0), (1, 0), (1, 1), (0, 1)$, by deleting the bottom edge $\{(0, 0), (1, 0)\}$: its edges are $\{(1, 0), (1, 1)\}$, $\{(1, 1), (0, 1)\}$ and $\{(0, 1), (0, 0)\}$.
