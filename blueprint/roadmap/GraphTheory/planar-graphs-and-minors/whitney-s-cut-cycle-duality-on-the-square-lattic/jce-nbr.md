# Four nearest neighbours of a site

For a site $v = (v_x, v_y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the map from $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ listing its four nearest neighbours: $0 \mapsto (v_x + 1, v_y)$ (right), $1 \mapsto (v_x - 1, v_y)$ (left), $2 \mapsto (v_x, v_y + 1)$ (up), $3 \mapsto (v_x, v_y - 1)$ (down).
