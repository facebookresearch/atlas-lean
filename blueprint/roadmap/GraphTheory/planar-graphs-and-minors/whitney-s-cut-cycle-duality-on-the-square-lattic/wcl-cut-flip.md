# Cut flip relation of a lattice graph and a dual edge

For a simple graph $H$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and sites $f_0, g_0$, the cut flip relation holds for faces $a, b$ when $S(a, b)$ or $S(b, a)$ is an edge of $H$, or $\{a, b\} = \{f_0, g_0\}$. Here $S(a, b)$ is the [total primal-edge map](whc-is-cross.md): the edge $\{(a_0, m_1), (a_0 + 1, m_1)\}$ if $a_0 = b_0$ and $\{(m_0, a_1), (m_0, a_1 + 1)\}$ otherwise, with $m_i = \max(a_i, b_i)$, which for adjacent faces is the primal edge separating them.
