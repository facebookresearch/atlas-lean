# Cut coloring of a subgraph with an extra dual edge

For a simple graph $H$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and faces $f_0, g_0$, a colouring $\varphi$ of faces is a cut colouring when, for all nearest-neighbour faces $a, b$: the primal edge separating $a$ and $b$ is an edge of $H$ or $\{a, b\} = \{f_0, g_0\}$ if and only if $\varphi(a) \neq \varphi(b)$.
