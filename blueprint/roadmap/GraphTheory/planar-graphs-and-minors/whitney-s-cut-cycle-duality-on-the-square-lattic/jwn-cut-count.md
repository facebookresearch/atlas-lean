# Cut-flip count along a walk

For a simple graph $H$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, sites $f_0, g_0$ and a walk $w$ in the square lattice (read as a walk of faces), the cut count of $w$ is the number of steps $\{a, b\}$ of $w$, counted with multiplicity, such that the primal edge separating $a$ and $b$ is an edge of $H$ or $\{a, b\} = \{f_0, g_0\}$.
