# Parity coloring of faces by an edge set

For a finite set $E$ of unordered pairs of sites, the parity colouring of faces: the face $f = (f_0, f_1)$ (labelled by its lower left corner) is coloured when an odd number of the horizontal edges $\{(f_0, h), (f_0 + 1, h)\}$ with $h \le f_1$, lying directly below $f$ in its column, belong to $E$.
