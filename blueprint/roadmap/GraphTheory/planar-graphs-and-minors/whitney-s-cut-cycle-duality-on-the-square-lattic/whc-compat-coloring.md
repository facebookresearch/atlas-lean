# Face coloring compatible with a subgraph

For a simple graph $H$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, a colouring $\varphi$ of faces (a predicate on $\mathbb{Z}^2$) is compatible with $H$ when, for all nearest-neighbour faces $a, b$, the primal edge separating $a$ and $b$ is an edge of $H$ if and only if $\varphi(a) \neq \varphi(b)$.
