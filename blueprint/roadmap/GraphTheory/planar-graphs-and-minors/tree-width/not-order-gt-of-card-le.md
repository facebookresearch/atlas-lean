# Small graphs have no large brambles

In a graph with at most $k$ vertices no bramble has order greater than $k$: the whole vertex set
covers every bramble, because the members of a bramble are nonempty.

## Background

- [Tree-width duality theorem (Seymour–Thomas)](has-treewidth-at-most-iff-forall-bramble.md):
  the theorem whose bramble side this bounds trivially.
