# The one-bag decomposition

A graph with at most $k + 1$ vertices has tree-width at most $k$: a tree with one node whose bag is
the whole vertex set is a tree-decomposition.

## Sources

- Reinhard Diestel, *Graph Theory*, 6th edition, author's online preview of Chapter 12 (PDF dated 28 June 2024), https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/diestel/books/graph.theory/preview/Ch12.pdf, Section 12.4 (definition of tree-width).
