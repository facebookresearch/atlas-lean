# Width at most $k$

A tree-decomposition $(T, (V_t))$ has width at most $k$ if every bag has at most $k + 1$
vertices. The width of a tree-decomposition is $\max\{|V_t| - 1 : t \in T\}$, so this says
that the width is at most $k$.
