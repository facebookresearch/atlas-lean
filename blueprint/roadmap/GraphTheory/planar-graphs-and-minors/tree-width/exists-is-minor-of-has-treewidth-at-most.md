# Graphs of bounded tree-width are well-quasi-ordered by minors (Robertson–Seymour)

For every natural number $k$, every infinite sequence $G_0, G_1, \dots$ of finite graphs of
tree-width at most $k$ has two terms $G_i, G_j$ with $i < j$ such that $G_i$ is a minor of $G_j$:
the graphs of bounded tree-width are well-quasi-ordered by the minor relation.

The source states this for tree-width $< k$ with $k > 0$, the same family of statements.
