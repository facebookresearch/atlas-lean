# Unit cell to the left of a lattice step

For a direction $w \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ ($0$ east, $1$ north, $2$ west, $3$ south) and a point $p = (p_x, p_y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the unit cell to the left of the unit step in direction $w$ starting at $p$, labelled by its lower left corner:

$$\lambda(0, p) = (p_x, p_y), \quad \lambda(1, p) = (p_x - 1, p_y), \quad \lambda(2, p) = (p_x - 1, p_y - 1), \quad \lambda(3, p) = (p_x, p_y - 1).$$
