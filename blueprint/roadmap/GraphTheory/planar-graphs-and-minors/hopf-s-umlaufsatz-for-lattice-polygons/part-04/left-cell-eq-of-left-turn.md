# Steps around a left turn share their left cell

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). For a direction $w$ and a point $p = (p_x, p_y)$, let $\lambda(w, p)$ be the unit cell (labelled by its lower left corner) to the left of the step in direction $w$ from $p$: $\lambda(0, p) = p$, $\lambda(1, p) = (p_x - 1, p_y)$, $\lambda(2, p) = (p_x - 1, p_y - 1)$, $\lambda(3, p) = (p_x, p_y - 1)$. If the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, and $k$ is an index with $d_{k+1} - d_k = 1$ (a left turn), then $\lambda(d_k, p_k) = \lambda(d_{k+1}, p_{k+1})$.
