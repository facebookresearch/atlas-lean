# Index of a vertical step below a walk vertex

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, and its vertices are pairwise distinct. Let $c, h$ be integers and $m$ an index with $p_m = (c, h + 1)$. If $j$ is an index with $d_j$ vertical, $x(p_j) = c$ and $\min(y(p_j), y(p_{j+1})) \le h < \max(y(p_j), y(p_{j+1}))$ (a step over the segment $\{c\} \times [h, h+1]$ below $p_m$), then $j = m$ or $j + 1 = m$.
