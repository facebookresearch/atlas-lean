# No vertical step through a non-vertex point

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, and let $c, h$ be integers such that $(c, h)$ is not a vertex of the walk. Then no index $k$ with $d_k$ vertical and $x(p_k) = c$ satisfies $\min(y(p_k), y(p_{k+1})) \le h < \max(y(p_k), y(p_{k+1}))$: no vertical step on the line $x = c$ starts its unit segment at height $h$.
