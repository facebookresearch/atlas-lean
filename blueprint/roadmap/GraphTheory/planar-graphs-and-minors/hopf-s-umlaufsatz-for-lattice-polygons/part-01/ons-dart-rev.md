# Reverse of a dart

For a natural number $L$, consider the discrete torus $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ (with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), whose darts are pairs $(s, \mu)$ of a site $s$ and a direction $\mu \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ ($0$ east, $1$ north, $2$ west, $3$ south). The reverse of the dart $(s, \mu)$ is the dart $(s + u_\mu, \mu + 2)$, where $u_\mu$ is the unit step in direction $\mu$: it starts at the head of $(s, \mu)$ and points in the opposite direction.
