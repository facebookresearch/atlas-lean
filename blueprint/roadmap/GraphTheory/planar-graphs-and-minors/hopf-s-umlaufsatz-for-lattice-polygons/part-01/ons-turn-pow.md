# Integer turn exponent between two directions

For directions $\mu, \nu \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, the integer turn exponent from $\mu$ to $\nu$ is

$$\begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } \nu = \mu, \\ 1 & \text{if } \nu = \mu + 1, \\ 0 & \text{if } \nu = \mu + 2, \\ -1 & \text{if } \nu = \mu + 3: \end{cases}$$

$1$ for a left turn, $-1$ for a right turn, $0$ for going straight and, by convention, for a U-turn.
