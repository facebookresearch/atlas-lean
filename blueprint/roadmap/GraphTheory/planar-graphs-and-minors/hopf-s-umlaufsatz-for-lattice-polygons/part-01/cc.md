# Number of left turns of a direction sequence

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. The number of left turns of $d$ is the number of indices $i \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ with $d_{i+1} - d_i = 1$ in $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$ (counterclockwise quarter turns).
