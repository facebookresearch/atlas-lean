# Turns of a cyclic direction sequence sum to zero

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Then

$$\sum_{i \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}} (d_{i+1} - d_i) = 0 \quad \text{in } \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}.$$
