# Coordinates of the four lattice steps

For every direction $w \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, with the unit steps $s(0) = (1, 0)$, $s(1) = (0, 1)$, $s(2) = (-1, 0)$, $s(3) = (0, -1)$: either $w = 0$ and $s(w) = (1, 0)$, or $w = 1$ and $s(w) = (0, 1)$, or $w = 2$ and $s(w) = (-1, 0)$, or $w = 3$ and $s(w) = (0, -1)$.
