# Vertices of a lattice walk from its step directions

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. With the unit steps $s(0) = (1, 0)$, $s(1) = (0, 1)$, $s(2) = (-1, 0)$, $s(3) = (0, -1)$, the $k$-th vertex of the lattice walk with step directions $d$, for $k \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, is

$$p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i) \in \mathbb{Z}^2,$$

so that $p_0 = 0$.
