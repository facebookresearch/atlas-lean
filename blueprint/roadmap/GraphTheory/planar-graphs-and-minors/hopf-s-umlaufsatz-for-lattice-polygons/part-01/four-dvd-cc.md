# Without right turns, the number of left turns is divisible by four

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. If every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$ (no right turns and no reversals), then the number of indices $i$ with $d_{i+1} - d_i = 1$ is divisible by $4$. No closedness of the walk is needed.
