# Number of straight continuations in a given direction

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. For a direction $c \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, the number of indices $i$ with $d_i = c$ and $d_{i+1} = c$: the straight continuations of $d$ in direction $c$.
