# Left turns are four times the north-to-west turns

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. If every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$, then the number of indices $i$ with $d_{i+1} - d_i = 1$ (left turns) equals four times the number of indices $i$ with $d_i = 1$ and $d_{i+1} = 2$ (north-to-west turns).
