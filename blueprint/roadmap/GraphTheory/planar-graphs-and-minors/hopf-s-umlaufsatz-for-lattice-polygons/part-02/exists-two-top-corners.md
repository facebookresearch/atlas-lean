# Eight left turns give two north-then-west corners

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Suppose every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$ and at least $8$ indices $i$ have $d_{i+1} - d_i = 1$. Then there are indices $i \neq j$ with $d_i = d_j = 1$ and $d_{i+1} = d_{j+1} = 2$.
