# A unique top corner forces fewer than eight left turns

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Suppose every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$, and there is at most one index $i$ with $d_i = 1$ and $d_{i+1} = 2$. Then the number of indices $i$ with $d_{i+1} - d_i = 1$ is less than $8$.
