# Agreement of the two definitions of the left cell

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Then for every index $k$ the two descriptions of the cell to the left of step $k$ agree: the cell to the left of the unit step in direction $d_k$ from the point $p_k$ (namely $p_k$, $p_k - (1, 0)$, $p_k - (1, 1)$ or $p_k - (0, 1)$ for $d_k = 0, 1, 2, 3$) equals the left cell of step $k$ of the walk defined by the same case distinction on $d_k$.
