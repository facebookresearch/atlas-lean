# Corner count as a sum of four indicators

For every finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of unit cells (labelled by lower left corners) and every point $v = (v_x, v_y)$, the number of cells of $S$ having $v$ as a corner is

$$\mathbf{1}[(v_x, v_y) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(v_x - 1, v_y) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(v_x, v_y - 1) \in S] + \mathbf{1}[(v_x - 1, v_y - 1) \in S].$$
