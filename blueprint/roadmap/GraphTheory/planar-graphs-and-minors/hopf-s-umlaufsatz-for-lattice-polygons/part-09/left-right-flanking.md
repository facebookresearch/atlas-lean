# Left and right cells of a step share that step

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$. For an index $k$ let $\lambda_k$ and $\rho_k$ be the cells to the left and to the right of step $k$. Then $\lambda_k$ and $\rho_k$ are nearest neighbours, and the primal edge separating the faces $\lambda_k$ and $\rho_k$ is $\{p_k, p_{k+1}\}$.
