# Corner count of the bounded face at a walk vertex

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, its vertices are pairwise distinct, and every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$. Let $B$ be the bounded face of the walk: the finite set of cells (unit squares labelled by lower left corners) whose faces lie in the connected component, in the face graph of the walk (adjacent faces joined unless separated by a step $\{p_i, p_{i+1}\}$), of the cell below and to the left of a fixed north-then-west corner $p_M$ ($d_{M-1} = 1$, $d_M = 2$) whose below-left cell has an odd number of horizontal steps of the walk above it in its column. Then for every index $k$, the number of cells of $B$ having $p_{k+1}$ as a corner is $1$ if $d_{k+1} - d_k = 1$ (a left turn) and $2$ otherwise.
