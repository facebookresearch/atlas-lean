# Face below-left of a chosen north-then-west corner

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, its vertices are pairwise distinct, and every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$. Let $M$ be the fixed chosen index with $d_{M-1} = 1$ (north), $d_M = 2$ (west) and such that the cell $(x(p_M) - 1, y(p_M) - 1)$ has an odd number of horizontal steps of the walk above it in its column (upward ray parity $1$), and let $f_*$ be the face labelled $(x(p_M) - 1, y(p_M) - 1)$. The face $f_*$ is the face labelled by $(x(p_M) - 1, y(p_M) - 1)$, the cell below and to the left of $p_M$.
