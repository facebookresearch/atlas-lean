# Cells of the bounded face of a simple closed walk

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, and its vertices are pairwise distinct. Its face graph is the graph on unit faces (labelled by lower left corners) in which two adjacent faces are joined unless the primal edge separating them is a step $\{p_i, p_{i+1}\}$ of the walk. Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, its vertices are pairwise distinct, and every turn $d_{i+1} - d_i$ is $0$ or $1$. Let $M$ be the fixed chosen index with $d_{M-1} = 1$ (north), $d_M = 2$ (west) and such that the cell $(x(p_M) - 1, y(p_M) - 1)$ has an odd number of horizontal steps of the walk above it in its column (upward ray parity $1$), and let $f_*$ be the face labelled $(x(p_M) - 1, y(p_M) - 1)$. The bounded face of the walk is the finite set of cells $(a, b)$ whose faces lie in the connected component of $f_*$ in the face graph. It agrees with the set of cells of upward ray parity $1$.
