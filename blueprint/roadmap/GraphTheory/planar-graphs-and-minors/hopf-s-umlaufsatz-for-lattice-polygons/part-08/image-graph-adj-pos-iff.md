# Neighbours in the image graph are walk predecessor and successor

Let $m$ be a natural number, $n = m + 3$, and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). Suppose the walk is closed, $\sum_{i} s(d_i) = 0$, and its vertices are pairwise distinct, and let $H$ be the image in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ of the cycle graph $C_n$ embedded by $i \mapsto p_i$. Then for every index $k$ and every site $q$, $p_k$ and $q$ are adjacent in $H$ if and only if $q = p_{k-1}$ or $q = p_{k+1}$.
