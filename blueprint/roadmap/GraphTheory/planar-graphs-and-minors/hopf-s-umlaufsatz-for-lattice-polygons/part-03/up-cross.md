# Upward ray crossings above a cell

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ be a cyclic sequence of directions $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, indices taken modulo $n$. Direction $k$ is the unit step $s(k)$, with $s(0) = (1, 0)$ (east), $s(1) = (0, 1)$ (north), $s(2) = (-1, 0)$ (west), $s(3) = (0, -1)$ (south), and the walk with step directions $d$ has vertices $p_k = \sum_{0 \le i < k} s(d_i)$ for $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ (so $p_0 = 0$; vertex indices are also taken modulo $n$). For integers $a, b$, the upward crossing count of the cell $[a, a+1] \times [b, b+1]$ is the number of indices $k$ such that $d_k$ is horizontal (east or west), $\min(x(p_k), x(p_{k+1})) = a$ and $y(p_k) > b$: the horizontal steps over the column $[a, a+1]$ strictly above the cell, which cross the upward vertical ray from its centre.
