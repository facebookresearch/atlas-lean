# Finitely many abelian spoke graphs with at least four arms (AimPL question)

The statement that for every natural number $N \ge 4$ there are only finitely many $N$-tuples of positive integers $(\ell_0, \dots, \ell_{N-1})$ for which the spoke graph with $N$ arms of these lengths (paths attached at a common centre) has norm $\lambda$, its largest adjacency eigenvalue, with $\mathbb{Q}(\lambda^2)$ an abelian Galois extension of $\mathbb{Q}$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Only finitely many spoke graphs with four or more arms have abelian norm squared](abelian-spoke-tuples-finite-many-arms-ho.md).
