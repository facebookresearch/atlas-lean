# Adjacency matrix of a spoke graph

For a natural number $N$ and arm lengths $\ell_0, \dots, \ell_{N-1}$, the adjacency matrix of the spoke graph is the real matrix on its vertices with entry $1$ between the centre and each vertex $(i, 0)$, and between $(i, j)$ and $(i, j')$ when $|j - j'| = 1$, and $0$ otherwise: $N$ paths of lengths $\ell_i$ attached at a common centre.
