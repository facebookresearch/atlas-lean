# Arm lengths with abelian norm squared

For a natural number $N$, the abelian spoke tuples are the tuples $(\ell_0, \dots, \ell_{N-1})$ of positive integers for which, with $\lambda$ the norm (largest adjacency eigenvalue) of the spoke graph with arms of these lengths, the subfield $\mathbb{Q}(\lambda^2)$ of $\mathbb{R}$ is an abelian Galois extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ (equivalently, by Kronecker–Weber, $\lambda^2$ lies in a cyclotomic field).
