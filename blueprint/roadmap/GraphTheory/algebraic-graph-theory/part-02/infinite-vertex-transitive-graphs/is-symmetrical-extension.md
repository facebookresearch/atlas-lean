# Symmetrical extension of a graph

A simple graph $\Sigma$ is a symmetrical extension of a simple graph $\Gamma$ by a simple graph $\Delta$ when $\Sigma$ is connected and there are a group $G$ of automorphisms of $\Sigma$ acting transitively on its vertices and a $G$-invariant partition $\sigma$ of its vertices (an imprimitivity system) such that the quotient graph $\Sigma/\sigma$ (distinct blocks adjacent when an edge joins them) is isomorphic to $\Gamma$ and every block induces a subgraph of $\Sigma$ isomorphic to $\Delta$.
