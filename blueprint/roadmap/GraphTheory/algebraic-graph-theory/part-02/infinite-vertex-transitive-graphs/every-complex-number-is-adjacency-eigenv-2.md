# Resolution of Kourovka Problem 15.89

It is not the case that for every infinite, connected, locally finite simple graph $\Gamma$ whose automorphism group acts transitively on the vertices, and every complex number $z$, there is a nonzero function $f$ from the vertices to $\mathbb{C}$ with $\sum_{w \sim v} f(w) = z\, f(v)$ for every vertex $v$: every complex number is an eigenvalue of the adjacency operator acting on all complex-valued functions on the vertices. Equivalently, some infinite, connected, locally finite, vertex-transitive simple graph has a complex number $z$ for which no nonzero $f$ satisfies $\sum_{w \sim v} f(w) = z\, f(v)$ at every vertex.
