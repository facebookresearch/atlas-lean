# Grone–Merris conjecture

The statement that for every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with $n$ vertices, the eigenvalues $\lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n$ of its Laplacian matrix $L = D - A$ ($D$ the diagonal degree matrix, $A$ the adjacency matrix) are majorized by the conjugate degree sequence: $\lambda_1 + \dots + \lambda_k \le d^*_1 + \dots + d^*_k$ for every $k \le n$, and $\lambda_1 + \dots + \lambda_n = d^*_1 + \dots + d^*_n$, where $d^*_k$ is the number of vertices of degree at least $k$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Grone–Merris theorem (Bai)](grone-merris-majorization-holds.md).
