# Square energy conjecture (Elphick, Farber, Goldberg and Wocjan)

The statement that every connected simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with $n$ vertices satisfies $\min\{S^+(G), S^-(G)\} \ge n - 1$, where $S^+(G)$ and $S^-(G)$ are the sums of the squares of the nonnegative and of the negative adjacency eigenvalues of $G$, counted with multiplicity. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Both square energies of a connected graph are at least $n - 1$](conjecture-holds.md).
