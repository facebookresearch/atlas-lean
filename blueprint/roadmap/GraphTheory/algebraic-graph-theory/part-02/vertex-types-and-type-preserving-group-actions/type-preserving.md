# Type-preserving subgroup

Let $G$ be a group acting on a set $W$, let $\mathcal{T}$ be a simple graph on $W$ and let $w_0 \in W$. The type-preserving subgroup of $G$ is

$$\{g \in G : \operatorname{type}_{w_0}(g \cdot w) = \operatorname{type}_{w_0}(w) \text{ for every } w \in W\},$$

where $\operatorname{type}_{w_0}(w) \in \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ is the parity of the graph distance from $w_0$ to $w$ (taken as $0$ when $w$ is unreachable). It is a subgroup of $G$; the action is not required to preserve the graph.
