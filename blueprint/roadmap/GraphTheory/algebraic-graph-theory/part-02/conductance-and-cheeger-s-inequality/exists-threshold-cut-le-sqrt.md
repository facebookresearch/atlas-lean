# Cheeger's inequality, hard direction

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with weights $w : E \to [0, \infty)$, [Laplacian](../../part-01/graph-laplacians-and-cheeger-s-inequality/laplacian.md) $L$, [weighted degrees](weighted-degree.md) $d(a)$ and [volumes](volume.md) $d(S) = \sum_{a \in S} d(a)$. Let $z : V \to \mathbb{R}$ be centred with respect to $d$, that is, $d(\{a : z(a) > 0\}) \le d(V)/2$ and $d(\{a : z(a) < 0\}) \le d(V)/2$, with $z^{\mathsf T} D z = \sum_a d(a)\, z(a)^2 > 0$. Then there is a threshold $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ such that the set $S_\tau = \{a : z(a) < \tau\}$ satisfies $m_\tau = \min\big(d(S_\tau), d(V \setminus S_\tau)\big) > 0$ and

$$w(\partial S_\tau) \le \sqrt{\frac{2\, z^{\mathsf T} L z}{z^{\mathsf T} D z}}\; m_\tau,$$

where $w(\partial S_\tau)$ is the total weight of the [cut](../../../graph-families-and-operations/finite-multigraphs/cut.md) of $S_\tau$. Thus the conductance $\varphi(S_\tau) = w(\partial S_\tau)/m_\tau$ is at most $\sqrt{2 z^{\mathsf T} L z / z^{\mathsf T} D z}$ (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Theorem 21.1.3 with Definition 21.1.1, stated there for positive weights). The proof, after Trevisan, rescales $z$ so that $(\min_a z(a))^2 + (\max_a z(a))^2 = 1$ and draws the threshold $\tau$ between $\min_a z(a)$ and $\max_a z(a)$ with density $2\lvert\tau\rvert$, a probability density on that interval since $\min_a z(a) \le 0 \le \max_a z(a)$ for centred $z$: then $\mathbb{E}[w(\partial S_\tau)] \le \sum_e w_e \lvert z(e^-) - z(e^+)\rvert (\lvert z(e^-)\rvert + \lvert z(e^+)\rvert) \le \sqrt{z^{\mathsf T} L z}\sqrt{2 z^{\mathsf T} D z}$ by Cauchy–Schwarz (inequality (21.2)) and $\mathbb{E}[m_\tau] = z^{\mathsf T} D z$ by centring.
