# Volume of a vertex set

Let $V$ be a set, $E$ a finite set, and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with weights $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$. The volume of a finite set $S \subseteq V$ is $d(S) = \sum_{a \in S} d(a)$, the sum of the [weighted degrees](weighted-degree.md) of its vertices.
