# Weighted degrees are nonnegative

Let $V$ be a set, $E$ a finite set, and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$. For weights $w : E \to [0, \infty)$, every [weighted degree](weighted-degree.md) $d(a) = \sum_e w_e([e^- = a] + [e^+ = a])$ is nonnegative. For positive conductances on a loopless multigraph, $d(a)$ is the weight $\pi(a)$ of R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.1: the sum of the weights of the edges incident to $a$.
