# Propagation gap bound for the path on T + 1 vertices

For every natural number $T \ge 1$,

$$\frac{1}{2T^2} \le 1 - \cos\frac{\pi}{T + 1}.$$

The right-hand side is the smallest nonzero eigenvalue of one half of the Laplacian of the path graph on $n = T + 1$ vertices, so this is a gap of order $1/T^2$.

## Sources

- Kitaev, Shen and Vyalyi, *Classical and Quantum Computation*, Sections 14.4.2 and 14.4.4.
