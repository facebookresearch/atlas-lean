# Cosine values lie in the spectrum of the half path Laplacian

Let $A_n = \tfrac12 L(P_n)$ be one half of the real Laplacian matrix of the path graph $P_n$ on the vertices $0, 1, \dots, n-1$ (with $j$ adjacent to $j + 1$). For $n \ge 1$ and every natural number $k < n$, the number $1 - \cos(\pi k / n)$ lies in the spectrum of $A_n$ in the algebra of real $n \times n$ matrices: $A_n - (1 - \cos(\pi k/n)) I_n$ is not invertible.

- Kitaev, Shen and Vyalyi, *Classical and Quantum Computation*, Sections 14.4.2 and 14.4.4.
