# Spectral gap of the half path Laplacian on zero-sum vectors

Let $A_n = \tfrac12 L(P_n)$ be one half of the real Laplacian matrix of the path graph $P_n$ on the vertices $0, 1, \dots, n-1$ (with $j$ adjacent to $j + 1$). For $n \ge 1$ and every $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\sum_{j} v_j = 0$,

$$\bigl(1 - \cos(\pi / n)\bigr)\, v \cdot v \le v \cdot (A_n v).$$
