# Batson–Spielman–Srivastava, vector form

Let $d > 1$ be real and let $v_1, \dots, v_m \in \mathbb{R}^n$ satisfy $\sum_i v_i v_i^{\mathsf T} = I$. Then there are scalars $s_1, \dots, s_m \ge 0$, at most $d n$ of them nonzero, with

$$I \preccurlyeq \sum_{i} s_i\, v_i v_i^{\mathsf T} \preccurlyeq \kappa\, I, \qquad \kappa = \frac{d + 1 + 2\sqrt d}{d + 1 - 2\sqrt d}$$

(Batson, Spielman and Srivastava, *Twice-Ramanujan sparsifiers*, arXiv:0808.0163, Theorem 3.1).
