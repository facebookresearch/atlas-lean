# Arbitrarily large hyper-regular graphs with connected links exist

There are natural numbers $a, b, c, d, e$ such that for every $N$ there are $n \ge N$ and a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ that is $(a, b, c, d, e)$-regular and has connected links up to order $5$. Here $G$ is $(d_1, \dots, d_r)$-regular when every vertex has exactly $d_1$ neighbours and the subgraph induced on every neighbourhood is $(d_2, \dots, d_r)$-regular, recursively; and it has connected links up to order $5$ when the common neighbourhood of every clique with at most $5$ vertices induces a connected graph ($G$ itself for the empty clique). That is, the proposition [Friedgut's question on regular graphs with connected links](conjecture.md) holds.
