# Tuple-regular graph on a vertex set

For a simple graph $G$, a set $W$ of vertices and a finite list $(d_1, \dots, d_r)$ of natural numbers, $G$ is $(d_1, \dots, d_r)$-regular on $W$ when, for $r = 0$, always; and otherwise every $v \in W$ has exactly $d_1$ neighbours in $W$ (a vertex with infinitely many counts as having $0$) and $G$ is $(d_2, \dots, d_r)$-regular on the set $N(v) \cap W$ of those neighbours.
