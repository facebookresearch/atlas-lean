# Ramanujan complexes give bounded-degree local spectral expanders

For every $d \ge 2$ and $\gamma > 0$ there are explicit infinite families of bounded-degree pure $d$-dimensional simplicial complexes (finite quotients of Bruhat-Tits buildings of type $\tilde A_d$ over local fields of large residue characteristic $q$, the Ramanujan complexes of Lubotzky-Samuels-Vishne) that are $\gamma$-local spectral expanders, with link parameters $O(1/\sqrt q)$.

## Proof sketch

The links of vertices in a Bruhat-Tits building of type $\tilde A_d$ are spherical buildings (flag complexes of $\mathbb{F}_q^{d+1}$), whose $1$-skeleta have explicitly computable spectra with nontrivial eigenvalues $O(1/\sqrt q)$. Links are preserved under quotients by torsion-free lattices whose injectivity radius exceeds the link size, and explicit arithmetic lattices produce bounded-degree finite quotients.
