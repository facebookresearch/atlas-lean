# Up-down and down-up random walks on simplicial complexes

For a pure $d$-dimensional weighted complex $X$ and $0 \le k < d$, the *up-down walk* on $k$-faces moves from $\sigma$ to a random $(k+1)$-face $\tau \supset \sigma$ (chosen proportionally to weight) and then to a uniformly random $k$-face $\sigma' \subset \tau$. The *down-up walk* on $(k+1)$-faces moves from $\tau$ to a uniformly random $k$-face $\sigma \subset \tau$ and then up to a random $(k+1)$-face containing $\sigma$. Both are reversible Markov chains with stationary law the induced weights, and the down-up walk on top faces of the matroid basis complex is the basis-exchange walk.
