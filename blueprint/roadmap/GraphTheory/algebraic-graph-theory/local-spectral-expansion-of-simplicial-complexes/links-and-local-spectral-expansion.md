# Links and local spectral expanders

Let $X$ be a finite pure $d$-dimensional simplicial complex with a weight function on top faces inducing weights on all faces. The *link* of a face $\sigma$ is $X_\sigma = \{\tau \setminus \sigma : \tau \in X,\ \tau \supseteq \sigma\}$. The *$1$-skeleton* of a link is the weighted graph on its vertices with edge weights induced from $X$. $X$ is a *$\gamma$-local spectral expander* if for every face $\sigma$ of dimension at most $d - 2$ (including the empty face), the $1$-skeleton of $X_\sigma$ is connected and its random walk matrix has second largest eigenvalue at most $\gamma$.
