# Cycle-continuous maps

Let $G$ and $H$ be multigraphs. A map $f$ from the edges of $G$
to the edges of $H$ is cycle-continuous when, for every binary cycle $C$ of $H$, the preimage
$f^{-1}(C)$ is a binary cycle of $G$.
