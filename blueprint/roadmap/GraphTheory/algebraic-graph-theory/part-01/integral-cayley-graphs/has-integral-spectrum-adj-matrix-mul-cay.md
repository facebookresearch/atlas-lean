# Cayley graphs on normal sets of involutions are integral

Let `R` be a set of involutions
of a finite group `G` that is closed under conjugation. Then every eigenvalue of the Cayley graph
`Cay(G, R)` is an integer. Unlike `involutionCayleyGraphIntegral_holds`, this does not assume that
`R` generates `G`, and it holds for every decidability instance of the adjacency relation.
