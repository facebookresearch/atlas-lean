# Rank-three graph

A simple graph $G$ has rank $3$ when its automorphism group is transitive on the vertices, on the ordered pairs of adjacent vertices and on the ordered pairs of distinct non-adjacent vertices: for any two ordered pairs $(u, v)$, $(u', v')$ of the same kind (both diagonal, both adjacent, or both distinct and non-adjacent) some automorphism sends $u$ to $u'$ and $v$ to $v'$.
