# Left kernel of the oriented incidence matrix

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a set, and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ with $e^- \ne e^+$ (no loops), with [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence.md) $B$. A vector $x : V \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $x^{\mathsf T} B = 0$ if and only if $x(u) = x(v)$ whenever $u$ and $v$ lie in the same connected component of the [underlying simple graph](underlying-graph.md).
