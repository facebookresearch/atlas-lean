# Reachability in the underlying graph

Let $V$ be a set, $E$ a finite set, and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$. A vertex $v$ is reachable from $u$ in the [underlying simple graph](underlying-graph.md) if and only if there is a finite sequence $u = x_0, x_1, \dots, x_k = v$ ($k \ge 0$) in which consecutive vertices are joined by an edge of the multigraph, in either orientation.
