# Incidence rank plus number of components

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with no loops. The rank of its real [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence.md) plus the number of connected components of the [underlying graph](underlying-graph.md) equals $\lvert V\rvert$.
