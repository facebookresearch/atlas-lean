# Underlying simple graph of a multigraph

Let $V$ and $E$ be sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$. The underlying simple graph is the simple graph on $V$ in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when some edge joins them, in either orientation. It forgets multiplicities, orientations and loops, and records the connectivity of the multigraph.
