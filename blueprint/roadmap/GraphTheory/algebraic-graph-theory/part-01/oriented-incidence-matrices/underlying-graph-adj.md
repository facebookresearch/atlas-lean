# Adjacency in the underlying graph

Let $V$ and $E$ be sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$. Two vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in the [underlying simple graph](underlying-graph.md) if and only if $u \ne v$ and some edge $e$ joins them in either orientation: $(e^-, e^+) = (u, v)$ or $(e^-, e^+) = (v, u)$.
