# Current of a potential

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). For conductances $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$ and a potential $v : V \to \mathbb{R}$, the current of $v$ is the edge function

$$i(e) = w_e \big(v(e^-) - v(e^+)\big),$$

the current that Ohm's law assigns to the potential $v$ (R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.1, $i(x, y) := c(x, y)(v(x) - v(y))$).
