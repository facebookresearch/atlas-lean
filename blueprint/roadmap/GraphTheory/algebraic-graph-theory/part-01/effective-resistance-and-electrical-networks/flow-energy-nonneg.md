# Energy is nonnegative

Let $E$ be a finite set and $w : E \to (0, \infty)$ conductances. For every $\theta : E \to \mathbb{R}$, the [energy](flow-energy.md) satisfies $\sum_{e \in E} \theta_e^2 / w_e \ge 0$ (R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.4, $\mathcal{E}(\theta) = \lVert\theta\rVert_r^2$).
