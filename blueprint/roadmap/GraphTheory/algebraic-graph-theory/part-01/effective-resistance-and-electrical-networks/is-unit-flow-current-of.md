# The current of a solution of $L v = \delta_s - \delta_t$ is a unit flow

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with conductances $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$ and [Laplacian](../graph-laplacians-and-cheeger-s-inequality/laplacian.md) $L$. For $s, t \in V$ and every $v : V \to \mathbb{R}$ with $L v = \delta_s - \delta_t$, the [current](current-of.md) $i(e) = w_e(v(e^-) - v(e^+))$ of $v$ satisfies $B i = \delta_s - \delta_t$, that is, it is a [unit flow](is-unit-flow.md) from $s$ to $t$ (Kirchhoff's node law for the unit current; R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.1).
