# Uniqueness of hitting times

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with no loops and with connected [underlying graph](../oriented-incidence-matrices/underlying-graph.md), and let $w : E \to (0, \infty)$ be conductances. For every vertex $z$, the [first-step equations](is-hitting-time-solution.md) of the expected hitting times of $z$ have at most one solution (R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.1, Uniqueness Principle, applied to the difference of two solutions, which is harmonic off $z$ and vanishes at $z$).
