# Effective conductance is nonnegative

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with conductances $w : E \to [0, \infty)$. For all $s, t \in V$, the [effective conductance](effective-conductance.md) satisfies $C_{\mathrm{eff}}(s, t) \ge 0$.
