# Energy of an edge function

For a finite set $E$ of edges with conductances $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$ (resistances $1/w_e$), the energy of $\theta : E \to \mathbb{R}$ is

$$\mathcal{E}(\theta) = \sum_{e \in E} \frac{\theta_e^2}{w_e},$$

the sum of resistance times squared flow, where a term with $w_e = 0$ is read as $0$ (R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.4, definition of energy $\mathcal{E}(\theta) := \lVert\theta\rVert_r^2$).
