# Existence of hitting times

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with no loops and with connected [underlying graph](../oriented-incidence-matrices/underlying-graph.md), and let $w : E \to (0, \infty)$ be conductances. For every vertex $z$, the [first-step equations](is-hitting-time-solution.md) of the expected hitting times of $z$ have a solution. In R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, the solution is the vector of expected hitting times, finite by the hitting-time identity (Proposition 2.20); a solution can also be found by linear algebra, solving $L h = \pi - \gamma \delta_z$ with $\pi$ the weighted degree and $\gamma = \sum_x \pi(x)$.
