# Range of the weighted Laplacian

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with no loops and with conductances $w : E \to (0, \infty)$. Then the weighted [Laplacian](../graph-laplacians-and-cheeger-s-inequality/laplacian.md) $L = B\, \mathrm{diag}(w)\, B^{\mathsf T}$ and the oriented incidence matrix $B$ have the same column space: every net outflow $B\theta$ of an edge function $\theta$ is the net outflow $L v = B(w \cdot B^{\mathsf T} v)$ of the current of some potential $v$. This is the existence part of R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, corrected online version of 19 August 2026, Section 2.4, equation (2.9) and the note after Thomson's principle: for every flow $\theta$ the current $i = P_\star \theta$ satisfies $d^* i = d^* \theta$. It also follows by comparing ranks, $\operatorname{rank} L = \operatorname{rank} B = \lvert V\rvert - \#\{\text{components}\}$.
