# Euclidean chain function when the first argument divides the second

For natural numbers
$x, y \ge 1$ with $x \mid y$, the Euclidean chain function $F$ satisfies $F(x, y) = 1 + y / x$.

## Sources

- Diaz-Lopez, Ha, Harris, Rogers, Koss and Smith, *Arithmetical Structures on Coconut Trees*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A82, Corollary 4 (`integers-2025-A82-cor-4`).
