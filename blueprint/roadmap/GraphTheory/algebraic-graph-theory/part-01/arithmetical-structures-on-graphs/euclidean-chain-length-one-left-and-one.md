# Euclidean chain function at one

For every natural number $y$, the Euclidean chain
function $F$ satisfies $F(1, y) = y + 1$, and for every natural number $x \ge 1$ it satisfies
$F(x, 1) = 2$.

## Sources

- Diaz-Lopez, Ha, Harris, Rogers, Koss and Smith, *Arithmetical Structures on Coconut Trees*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A82, Corollary 5 (`integers-2025-A82-cor-5`).
