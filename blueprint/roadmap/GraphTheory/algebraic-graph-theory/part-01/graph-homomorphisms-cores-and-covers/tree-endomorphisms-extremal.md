# Lin's conjecture on endomorphisms of trees

The statement that for every natural number $n$ and every tree $T$ on $\{0, \dots, n\}$, the path $P_{n+1}$ on $\{0, \dots, n\}$ has at most as many endomorphisms as $T$, and $T$ has at most as many endomorphisms as the star $S_{n+1}$ on $\{0, \dots, n\}$ centred at $0$; an endomorphism is a map of the vertex set sending adjacent vertices to adjacent vertices. Stated here as a proposition; Csikvári and Lin proved it: see [Paths and stars are the extremal trees for endomorphisms](tree-endomorphisms-extremal-holds.md).
