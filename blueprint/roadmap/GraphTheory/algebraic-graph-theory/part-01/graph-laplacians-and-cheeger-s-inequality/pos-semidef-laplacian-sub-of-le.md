# Loewner monotonicity in the weights

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). If $w, w' : E \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $w_e \le w'_e$ for every edge $e$, then $L_{w'} - L_w$ is positive semidefinite: increasing edge weights (in particular adding edges of nonnegative weight) can only increase the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) in the Loewner order (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Section 6.2).
