# The weighted Laplacian is additive in the weights

Let $V$ be a set, $E$ a finite set, and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). For all weights $w, w' : E \to \mathbb{R}$, the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) satisfies $L_{w + w'} = L_w + L_{w'}$.
