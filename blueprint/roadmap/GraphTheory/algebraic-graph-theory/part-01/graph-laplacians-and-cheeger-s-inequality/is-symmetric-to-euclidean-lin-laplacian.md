# The Laplacian as a symmetric operator

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with weights $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$. The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) $L_w$, acting on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^V$, is a symmetric (self-adjoint) operator: $\langle L_w x, y\rangle = \langle x, L_w y\rangle$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^V$.
