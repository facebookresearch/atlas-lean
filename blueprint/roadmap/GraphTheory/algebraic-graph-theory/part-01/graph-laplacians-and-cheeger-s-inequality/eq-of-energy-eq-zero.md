# Zero energy forces equal potentials along positive-weight walks

Let $V$ be an arbitrary set of nodes, possibly infinite, and $E$ a finite set of arcs, each arc $e$ running from its tail $t(e)$ to its head $h(e)$, with nonnegative weights $w_e \ge 0$. Let $G_w$ be the simple graph on $V$ in which distinct nodes $u \ne v$ are adjacent when some arc $e$ with $w_e > 0$ runs from $u$ to $v$ or from $v$ to $u$. Let $x : V \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy

$$\sum_{e \in E} w_e \big(x(h(e)) - x(t(e))\big)^2 = 0.$$

Then $x(u) = x(v)$ for all nodes $u$ and $v$ joined by a walk in $G_w$.
