# Algebraic connectivity is monotone in the weights

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). If $w, w' : E \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $w_e \le w'_e$ for every edge $e$, then the [algebraic connectivity](algebraic-connectivity.md) satisfies $\lambda_2(L_w) \le \lambda_2(L_{w'})$ (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Corollary 6.2.2 for $k = 2$).
