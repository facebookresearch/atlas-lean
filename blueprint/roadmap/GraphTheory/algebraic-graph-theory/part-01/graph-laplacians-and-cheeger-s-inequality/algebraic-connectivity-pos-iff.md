# $\lambda_2 > 0$ exactly for connected multigraphs

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed), with no loops, with weights $w : E \to (0, \infty)$ and with $\lvert V\rvert \ge 2$. Then the [algebraic connectivity](algebraic-connectivity.md) of $L_w$ is positive if and only if the [underlying graph](../oriented-incidence-matrices/underlying-graph.md) is connected (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Lemma 3.1.1).
