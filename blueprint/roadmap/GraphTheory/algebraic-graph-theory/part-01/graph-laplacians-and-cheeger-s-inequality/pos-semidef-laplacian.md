# The Laplacian of nonnegative weights is positive semidefinite

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). For weights $w : E \to [0, \infty)$, the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) $L_w$ is positive semidefinite (D. A. Spielman, *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory*, draft of 2 April 2025, Section 3.1: the quadratic form (3.1) is a sum of nonnegative terms).
