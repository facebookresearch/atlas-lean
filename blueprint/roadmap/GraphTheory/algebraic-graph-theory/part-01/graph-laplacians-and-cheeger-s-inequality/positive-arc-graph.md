# Graph of positive-weight arcs

Let $V$ and $E$ be arbitrary sets of nodes and arcs, possibly infinite, each arc $e$ running from its tail $t(e)$ to its head $h(e)$, and let $w \in \mathbb{R}^E$ be real arc weights. The graph of positive-weight arcs $G_w$ is the simple graph on $V$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent exactly when $u \ne v$ and some arc $e$ with $w_e > 0$ runs from $u$ to $v$ or from $v$ to $u$.

Orientation is forgotten, loops and parallel arcs contribute no extra edges, and arcs of weight zero or of negative weight are ignored.
