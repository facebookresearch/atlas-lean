# Rayleigh form of a real matrix as a quadratic form

Let $V$ be a finite index set and $M$ a real $V \times V$ matrix, acting on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^V$ with its standard inner product. For every $v \in \mathbb{R}^V$, the Rayleigh form of $M$ at $v$, the inner product $\langle M v, v \rangle$, equals $v^{\mathsf T} M v$. No symmetry of $M$ is assumed.
