# Constants are in the kernel of the Laplacian

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each $e \in E$ be an edge from $e^-$ to $e^+$ in $V$ (a finite multigraph; loops $e^- = e^+$ are allowed). For all weights $w : E \to \mathbb{R}$, the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) kills the constant vector: $L_w \mathbf 1 = 0$.
