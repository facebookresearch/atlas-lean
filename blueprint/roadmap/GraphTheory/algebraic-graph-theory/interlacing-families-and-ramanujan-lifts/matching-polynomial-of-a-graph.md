# The matching polynomial of a graph

For a graph $G$ on $N$ vertices, let $m_i$ be the number of matchings with $i$ edges ($m_0 = 1$). The *matching polynomial* is

$$
\mu_G(x) = \sum_{i \ge 0} (-1)^{i} m_i\, x^{N - 2i} .
$$

For forests it coincides with the characteristic polynomial of the adjacency matrix.
