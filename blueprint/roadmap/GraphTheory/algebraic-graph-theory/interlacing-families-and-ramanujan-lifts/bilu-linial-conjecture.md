# The Bilu-Linial conjecture on Ramanujan two-lifts

The *Bilu-Linial conjecture* asserts that every $d$-regular graph $G$ has a signing $s$ whose signed adjacency matrix satisfies $\|A_s\| = \max_i |\lambda_i(A_s)| \le 2\sqrt{d-1}$. A positive answer implies, by iterating $2$-lifts starting from the complete graph $K_{d+1}$, that $d$-regular Ramanujan graphs exist for every $d$ and infinitely many sizes. The one-sided version, $\lambda_{\max}(A_s) \le 2\sqrt{d-1}$, suffices for bipartite graphs whose spectra are symmetric.
