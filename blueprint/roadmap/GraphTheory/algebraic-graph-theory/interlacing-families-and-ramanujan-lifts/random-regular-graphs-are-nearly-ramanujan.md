# Friedman: random regular graphs are nearly Ramanujan

For every $d \ge 3$ and $\varepsilon > 0$, a uniformly random $d$-regular graph on $n$ vertices satisfies $\max_{i \ge 2}|\lambda_i| \le 2\sqrt{d-1} + \varepsilon$ with probability $1 - o(1)$ as $n \to \infty$ (Alon's second eigenvalue conjecture).

## Proof sketch

Friedman uses the trace method with a refined count of non-backtracking closed walks, isolating "tangles" (small dense subgraphs) that produce the only obstruction and showing they occur with vanishing probability.
