# Erdős Problem 1007 (fewest edges in dimension four)

The statement that the least number of edges of a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for some $n$, of dimension $4$ is $9$: some such graph has exactly $9$ edges and every such graph has at least $9$. Here the dimension of a graph is the least $d$ such that its vertices can be mapped injectively into $\mathbb{R}^d$ with every edge of length $1$.
