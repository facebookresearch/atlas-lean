# Leaf pairs of an unrooted binary tree with path lengths

The leaf pairs of an [unrooted binary tree](utree.md) with sides $l, r$ and root edge length $c$ form the list of the [pairs](pairs.md) of $l$, the pairs of $r$, and for each [leaf](leaves.md) $(x, d, k)$ of $l$ and $(y, e, m)$ of $r$ the quadruple $(x, y, d + c + e, k + m + 1)$: two labels, the length $d_T(x, y)$ of the path between the leaves and its number of edges $\tau_{xy}$.
