# Adjacency matrix of the square

Let $V$ and $L$ be finite sets and $\mathrm{rot}$ a [rotation map](rotation-map.md) on $V \times L$ with [adjacency matrix](adj-matrix.md) $A$. The adjacency matrix of its [square](square.md) $G^2$ is $A^2$: the number of two-step walks from $v$ to $u$ is $\sum_w A(v, w)\, A(w, u)$.
