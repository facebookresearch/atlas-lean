# Relabelling preserves the adjacency matrix

Let $\mathrm{rot}$ be a [rotation map](rotation-map.md) on $V \times L$, with $V$ a set and $L$, $L'$ finite sets, and let $e : L \to L'$ be a bijection. The [relabelled](relabel.md) rotation map has the same [adjacency matrix](adj-matrix.md) as $\mathrm{rot}$.
