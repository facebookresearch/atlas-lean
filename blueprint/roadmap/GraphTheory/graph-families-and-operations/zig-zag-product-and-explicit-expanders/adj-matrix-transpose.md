# The adjacency matrix of a rotation map is symmetric

Let $V$ be a set, $L$ a finite set and $\mathrm{rot}$ a [rotation map](rotation-map.md) on $V \times L$. Its [adjacency matrix](adj-matrix.md) is symmetric, $A^{\mathsf T} = A$: the rotation map itself matches the labels at $v$ of edges to $w$ with the labels at $w$ of edges to $v$.
