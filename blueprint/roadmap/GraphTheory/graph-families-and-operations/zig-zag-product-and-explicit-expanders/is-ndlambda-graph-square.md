# Squaring squares the spectral parameter

Let $V$ and $L$ be finite sets and $\mathrm{rot}$ a [rotation map](rotation-map.md) on $V \times L$. If $\mathrm{rot}$ describes an $(N, D, \lambda)$-graph, then its [square](square.md) describes an $(N, D^2, \lambda^2)$-graph (O. Reingold, S. Vadhan and A. Wigderson, Ann. of Math. 155 (2002), Proposition 2.3 with $t = 2$). The proof applies the bound twice, using that the adjacency matrix maps vectors orthogonal to the constants to such vectors because its columns also sum to $D$.
