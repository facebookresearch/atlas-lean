# The shear-translation (Margulis-Gabber-Galil type) expander on Z_n x Z_n

For $n \ge 1$, the shear-translation graph is the $8$-regular multigraph on $\mathbb{Z}_n \times \mathbb{Z}_n$ generated by the four moves $(x, y) \mapsto (x + y, y)$, $(x, y) \mapsto (x, y + x)$, $(x, y) \mapsto (x + 1, y)$, $(x, y) \mapsto (x, y + 1)$ and their inverses. Uniformly in $n$, its second eigenvalue satisfies $\lambda_2 \le 1 - 1/4096$, and its lazy graph has spectral expansion at most $1 - 1/8192$. Equivalently, it satisfies a Poincare inequality: for every $f$ with $\sum f = 0$, the total squared change of $f$ along the four moves is at least a fixed positive constant times $\sum |f|^2$.

## Proof sketch

Pass to the discrete Fourier transform $F$ of $f$, so $F(0) = 0$ and squared changes are preserved by Parseval. A translation multiplies $F(\xi)$ by a root of unity, which is far from $1$ unless the corresponding frequency coordinate is small, and a shear permutes frequencies by a dual shear; frequencies on which both translations act nearly trivially are moved by the shears into a region where they do not, which yields the constant.
