# Hereditary graph family

A family of labelled graphs $(F_n)$ is hereditary when for every injection $\sigma : \{0, \dots, m-1\} \to \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every $G \in F_n$, the graph on $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ with $i \sim j$ exactly when $\sigma(i) \sim \sigma(j)$ in $G$ belongs to $F_m$ (for $m = n$ this includes closure under isomorphism).
