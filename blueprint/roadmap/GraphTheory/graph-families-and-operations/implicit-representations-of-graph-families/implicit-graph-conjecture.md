# Implicit graph conjecture (Kannan–Naor–Rudich; Spinrad)

The statement that every hereditary family of labelled graphs with at most factorial speed ($|F_n| \le 2^{c n (\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + 1)}$ for some $c$ and all $n$) has an efficient implicit representation: for some $c$, codes of $c(\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + 1)$ bits on the vertices of each $n$-vertex member and a decoder depending only on the family and $n$ that recovers adjacency of distinct vertices from their codes. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [The implicit graph conjecture is false](implicit-graph-conjecture-refuted.md).

Here $\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$ is the integer logarithm, taken as $0$ for $n = 0$.
