# Graph family of at most factorial speed

A family of labelled graphs $(F_n)$ has at most factorial speed when there is a natural number $c$ with $|F_n| \le 2^{c\, n\, (\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + 1)}$ for every $n$.

Here $\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$ is the integer logarithm, taken as $0$ for $n = 0$.
