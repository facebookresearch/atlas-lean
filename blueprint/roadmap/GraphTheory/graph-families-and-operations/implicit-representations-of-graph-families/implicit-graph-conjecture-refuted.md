# The implicit graph conjecture is false

It is not the case that every hereditary family of labelled graphs with at most factorial speed ($|F_n| \le 2^{c n (\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + 1)}$ for some $c$ and all $n$) has an efficient implicit representation: for some $c$, codes of $c(\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + 1)$ bits on the vertices of each $n$-vertex member and a decoder depending only on the family and $n$ that recovers adjacency of distinct vertices from their codes. Equivalently, some hereditary family of labelled graphs of at most factorial speed has no efficient implicit representation in this sense. That is, the proposition [Implicit graph conjecture (Kannan–Naor–Rudich; Spinrad)](implicit-graph-conjecture.md) is false.

Here $\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$ is the integer logarithm, taken as $0$ for $n = 0$.
