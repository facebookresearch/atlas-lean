# Inner vertex boundary of a box

For natural numbers $d$ and $n$, the inner vertex boundary of the box $B_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subset \mathbb{Z}^d$ is the set $B_n \setminus B_{n-1}$, where $n - 1$ is truncated subtraction of natural numbers. It is empty for $n = 0$; for $n \ge 1$ it consists of the sites of $B_n$ having some coordinate equal to $\pm n$.
