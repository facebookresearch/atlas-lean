# Box sites with a forward neighbour in the box

For natural numbers $d \ge 1$ and $n$, the finite set of sites $x$ of the box $\{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ such that $x_0 + 1 \in \{-n, \dots, n\}$, where $x_0$ is the first coordinate; equivalently, the sites $x \in \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with $x_0 \le n - 1$, whose neighbour $x + e_0$ in the first coordinate direction still lies in the box.
