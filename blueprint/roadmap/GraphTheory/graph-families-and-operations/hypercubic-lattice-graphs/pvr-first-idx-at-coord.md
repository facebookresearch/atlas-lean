# First index where a walk reaches a coordinate value

Let $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $w = (w_0, w_1, \dots, w_\ell)$ be a walk from $a$ to $b$ in the square lattice, extended by $w_i = b$ for $i > \ell$, let $k \in \{0, 1\}$ be a coordinate index and $r \in \mathbb{Z}$, and suppose that $(w_i)_k = r$ for some $i$. The first index at which $w$ reaches the value $r$ in coordinate $k$ is the least natural number $i$ with $(w_i)_k = r$.
