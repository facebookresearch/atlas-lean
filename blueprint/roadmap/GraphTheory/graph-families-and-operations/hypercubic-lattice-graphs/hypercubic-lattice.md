# Hypercubic lattice $\mathbb{Z}^d$

For a natural number $d$, the hypercubic lattice $\mathbb{Z}^d$ is the simple graph whose vertices are the sites $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, two sites $x$ and $y$ being adjacent when they are nearest neighbours, that is, when

$$\sum_{i=0}^{d-1} |x_i - y_i| = 1.$$

Equivalently, $x$ and $y$ differ by $\pm 1$ in exactly one coordinate. For $d = 2$ it is the square lattice.
