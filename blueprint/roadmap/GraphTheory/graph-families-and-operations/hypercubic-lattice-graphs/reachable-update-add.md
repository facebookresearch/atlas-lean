# Increasing one coordinate stays in the same component

Let $x \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, $j \in \{0, 1\}$, $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Let $y$ and $z$ be the sites obtained from $x$ by replacing its $j$-th coordinate by $a$ and by $a + n$ respectively. Then $y$ and $z$ are joined by a walk in the square lattice.
