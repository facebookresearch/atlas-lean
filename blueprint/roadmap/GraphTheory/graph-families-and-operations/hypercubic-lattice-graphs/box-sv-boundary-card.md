# Size of the vertex boundary of the box $\{-n, \dots, n\}^d$

For natural numbers $d$ and $n$, the number of sites in the inner vertex boundary $B_n \setminus B_{n-1}$ of the box $B_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subset \mathbb{Z}^d$, where $n - 1$ is truncated subtraction of natural numbers. For $n = 0$ this boundary is $B_0 \setminus B_0 = \varnothing$, so the number is $0$; for $n \ge 1$ it counts the sites of $B_n$ having some coordinate equal to $\pm n$.
