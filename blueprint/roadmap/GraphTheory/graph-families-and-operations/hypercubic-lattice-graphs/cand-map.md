# Neighbour of a site in a coordinate direction

For a natural number $d$ and a site $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, the map from pairs $(j, \varepsilon)$, with $j \in \{0, \dots, d-1\}$ a coordinate index and $\varepsilon$ a sign flag (true or false), to $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that sends $(j, \text{true})$ to $x + e_j$ and $(j, \text{false})$ to $x - e_j$, where $e_j$ is the $j$-th standard unit vector.
