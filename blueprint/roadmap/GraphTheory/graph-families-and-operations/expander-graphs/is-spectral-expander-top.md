# The complete graph is a spectral expander

Let $V$ be a finite set with $N \ge 2$ elements. The complete graph on $V$ is a [spectral expander](is-spectral-expander.md) of degree $N - 1$ with parameter $\lambda = 1/(N - 1)$: its adjacency matrix acts as $-1$ on the vectors orthogonal to the constants, since $(A x)_i = \sum_{j \ne i} x_j = -x_i$ when $\sum_j x_j = 0$. For it the [elementary lower bound on the spectral parameter](elementary-spectral-lower-bound.md) is an equality.
