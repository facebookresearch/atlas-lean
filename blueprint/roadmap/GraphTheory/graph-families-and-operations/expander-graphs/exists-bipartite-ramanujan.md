# Bipartite Ramanujan graphs of every degree

For every $d \ge 3$ and every $N$ there are $n \ge N$ and a $d$-regular bipartite graph $G$ on $n$ vertices whose adjacency eigenvalues $\mu_1 \ge \mu_2 \ge \dots \ge \mu_n$ (with multiplicity) satisfy $\lvert\mu_i\rvert \le 2\sqrt{d-1}$ for all $1 < i < n$: arbitrarily large $d$-regular bipartite Ramanujan graphs, whose eigenvalues other than the largest ($d$) and the smallest ($-d$) all lie in $[-2\sqrt{d-1}, 2\sqrt{d-1}]$ (Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava, *Interlacing families I: bipartite Ramanujan graphs of all degrees*, arXiv:1304.4132, Theorem 5.5, with the definition of Section 2.1).
