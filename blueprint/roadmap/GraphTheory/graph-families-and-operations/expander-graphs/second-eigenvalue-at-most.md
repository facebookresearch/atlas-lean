# Second lazy-walk eigenvalue at most rho

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$. For a real $\rho$, the second eigenvalue of the lazy walk $P(x, y)$, equal to $1/2$ if $x = y$, to $1/(2\deg x)$ if $y$ is adjacent to $x$, and to $0$ otherwise is at most $\rho$, in Rayleigh-quotient form for the degree measure, when for every $f : V \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\sum_x \deg(x) f(x) = 0$, $\sum_x \deg(x) f(x) \sum_y P(x, y) f(y) \le \rho \sum_x \deg(x) f(x)^2$.
