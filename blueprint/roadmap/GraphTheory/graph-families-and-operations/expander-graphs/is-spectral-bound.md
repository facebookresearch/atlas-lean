# Spectral bound on the vectors orthogonal to the constants

Let $A$ be a real square matrix indexed by a finite set $V$ and $c$ a real number. $A$ satisfies the spectral bound $c$ on the vectors orthogonal to the constants if

$$(A x) \cdot (A x) \le c^2\, (x \cdot x) \quad \text{for every } x \in \mathbb{R}^V \text{ with } \sum_{i} x_i = 0,$$

that is, $\lVert A x\rVert \le \lvert c\rvert\, \lVert x\rVert$ for Euclidean norms. For the adjacency matrix of a $d$-regular graph and $c = d\lambda$ with $d > 0$ and $\lambda \ge 0$ this is spectral expansion $\lambda(G) \le \lambda$ in the sense of S. Vadhan, *Pseudorandomness* (2012), Section 4.1.2 and Definition 4.5.
