# Albertson–Berman conjecture

The statement that every planar simple graph on a finite vertex set with $n$ vertices has a set $S$ of vertices with $2|S| \ge n$ whose induced subgraph is a forest (has no cycle), planarity meaning a straight-line drawing in the plane with distinct points for distinct vertices and no two edges meeting except at common endpoints (for finite graphs equivalent to planarity by Fáry's theorem). Stated here as a proposition.
