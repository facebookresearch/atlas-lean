# Tandon's lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle

Let $M^*$ be Tandon's
dimension-500 code size. The Shannon capacity of the $7$-cycle is at least $(M^*)^{1/500}$.

## Sources

- Ravi Tandon, *Strengthening Recursive Constructions for Zero-Error Shannon Capacity*, arXiv:2608.30273v1 (2026), exact dimension-500 root rate; its disclosure of AI use states that ChatGPT and Claude assisted with exploration, proof development and checking, search and verification code, and manuscript refinement.
