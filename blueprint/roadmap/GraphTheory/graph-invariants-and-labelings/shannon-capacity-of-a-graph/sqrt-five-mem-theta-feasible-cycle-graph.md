# The umbrella certifies $\sqrt 5$

$\sqrt 5$ is a [feasible value](../theta-function-of-a-graph/theta-feasible.md) of the theta program for the pentagon $C_5$. The [umbrella](umbrella.md) is an orthogonal labeling: for non-adjacent $u \ne v$, [$u - v \in \{\pm 2, \pm 3\}$](cycle-graph-five-nonadj-diff.md), so [$\langle u_u, u_v\rangle$](inner-umbrella.md) $= \frac{1 + \sqrt 5}{4} + \cos(2\pi(u - v)/5) = 0$ by [the cosines of the long diagonals](cos-two-pi-mul-div-five.md). Against the handle $e_0$, every rib has cost $h^2/(h^2 + 1) = \frac{1 + \sqrt 5}{5 + \sqrt 5} = 1/\sqrt 5$.
