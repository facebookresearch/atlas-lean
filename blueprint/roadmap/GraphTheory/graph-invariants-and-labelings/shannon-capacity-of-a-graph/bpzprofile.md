# BPZ profiles

A BPZ profile is an arbitrary quadruple of integers, called its code,
pairs, auxiliary and neutral coordinates: the four integer coordinates propagated by the
valid-tuple product of Buys, Polak and Zuiddam. No validity condition is imposed on the
quadruple.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), valid-tuple profiles; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
