# Buys–Polak–Zuiddam one-block profile for the 7-cycle

This is the Buys–Polak–Zuiddam
(BPZ) profile whose code, pairs, auxiliary and neutral coordinates are $367$, $8$, $367$ and $322$.

The profile consists of these four integers alone; BPZ obtain them from a five-dimensional construction.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), seven-cycle construction; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
