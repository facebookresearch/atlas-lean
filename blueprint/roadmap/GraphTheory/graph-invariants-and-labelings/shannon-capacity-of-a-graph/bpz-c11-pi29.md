# Twenty-nine-block BPZ profile for the 11-cycle

The twenty-nine-block eleven-cycle
profile of Buys, Polak and Zuiddam is the profile product $\pi_{14} \star \pi_{15}$, where
$\pi_{14}$ and $\pi_{15}$ are their fourteen-block and fifteen-block eleven-cycle profiles.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), eleven-cycle construction; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
