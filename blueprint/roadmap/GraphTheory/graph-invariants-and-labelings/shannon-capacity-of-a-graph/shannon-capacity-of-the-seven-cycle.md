# Shannon capacity of the seven-cycle

Determine the Shannon capacity of the channel whose confusability graph is C₇. This is a small, concrete problem exposing the difficulty of zero-error communication.

## What would settle it

Define $\Theta(G) = \sup_{m \ge 1} \alpha(G^{\boxtimes m})^{1/m}$. Determine Θ(C₇) by matching constructions and universal upper bounds. The communication rate in bits is $\log_2 \Theta(C_7)$. Larger certified independent sets improve a lower bound but do not alone give exact capacity.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Buys, Polak and Zuiddam (preprint) improve lower bounds for small odd cycles and state that the capacity of every odd cycle of length at least seven has remained open since Lovász determined that of the five-cycle.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
