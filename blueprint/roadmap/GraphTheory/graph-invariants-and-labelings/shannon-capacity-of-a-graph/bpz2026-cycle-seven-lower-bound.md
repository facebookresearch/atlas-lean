# Buys–Polak–Zuiddam lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle

Let $c$ be the code
coordinate of BPZ's final forty-block seven-cycle profile, and let $M = \max(c, 0)$. Then
$M^{1/200}$ is at most the Shannon capacity of the $7$-cycle, as an inequality in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), the exact dimension-200 root rate; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
