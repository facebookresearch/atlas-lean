# Buys–Polak–Zuiddam lower bound for the capacity of the 19-cycle

Let $c$ be the code
coordinate of BPZ's final nineteen-cycle profile after twelve squarings, and let
$M = \max(c, 0)$. Then $M^{1/16384}$ is at most the Shannon capacity of the $19$-cycle, as an
inequality in $[0, \infty]$.

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), exact nineteen-cycle root rate; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
