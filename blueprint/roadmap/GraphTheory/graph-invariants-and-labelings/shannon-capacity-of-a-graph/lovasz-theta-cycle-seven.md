# Lovász's theta value for the 7-cycle

This is the real number
$\frac{7 \cos(\pi/7)}{1 + \cos(\pi/7)}$, Lovász's closed form for the theta number of the
$7$-cycle, viewed as an element of $[0, \infty]$.

Approximate decimal displays of this number in the source are not asserted as equalities.

## Sources

- L. Lovász, *On the Shannon capacity of a graph*, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 25 (1979), 1–7, as printed in Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026); the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
