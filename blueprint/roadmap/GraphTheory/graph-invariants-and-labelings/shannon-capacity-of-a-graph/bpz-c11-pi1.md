# One-block BPZ profile for the 11-cycle

The one-block eleven-cycle profile of Buys,
Polak and Zuiddam is the profile with code coordinate $148$, pairs coordinate $3$, auxiliary
coordinate $148$ and neutral coordinate $142$.

The profile consists of these four integers alone; in the source they come from a
construction in dimension $3$.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), eleven-cycle construction; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
