# Gao's lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle

Let $M_{40}$ be Gao's printed
dimension-200 cardinality, a natural number with 103 decimal digits. The Shannon capacity of the
$7$-cycle is at least $M_{40}^{1/200}$, as an inequality in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

- Yu Gao, *A Recursive Construction Improving the Lower Bound on the Shannon Capacity of C7*, arXiv:2607.27869v1 (2026), exact dimension-200 root rate; the paper states that ChatGPT 5.6 Sol implemented all the code and expanded the proofs.
