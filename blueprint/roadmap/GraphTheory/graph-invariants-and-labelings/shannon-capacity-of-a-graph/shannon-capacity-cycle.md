# Shannon capacity of the modular cycle

For a natural number $q$, the Shannon capacity modulo $q$ is the supremum, in $[0, \infty]$, of the rates $|A|^{1/n}$ over all positive integers $n$ and all [cycle codes](cycle-code.md) $A$ of length $n$ modulo $q$. For $q \ge 3$ it is the Shannon capacity of the cycle graph $C_q$. For $q = 0$ the alphabet is $\mathbb{Z}$ and the supremum is $\infty$: the words $0, 2, \dots, 2(M - 1)$ of length one form a code of size $M$.
