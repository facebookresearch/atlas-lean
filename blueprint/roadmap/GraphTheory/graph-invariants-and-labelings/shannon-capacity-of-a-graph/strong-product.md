# Strong product of finite graphs

For finite simple graphs $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $H$ on $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, the strong product $G \boxtimes H$ is the graph on the $nm$ pairs $(u, v)$ (relabelled as $\{0, \dots, nm-1\}$) in which distinct pairs $(u, v)$ and $(u', v')$ are adjacent when $u = u'$ or $uu'$ is an edge of $G$, and $v = v'$ or $vv'$ is an edge of $H$.
