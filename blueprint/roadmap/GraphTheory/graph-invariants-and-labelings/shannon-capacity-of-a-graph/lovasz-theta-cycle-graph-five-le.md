# Theta of the pentagon is at most $\sqrt 5$

$\vartheta(C_5) \le \sqrt 5$, because [the umbrella certifies $\sqrt 5$](sqrt-five-mem-theta-feasible-cycle-graph.md). Lovász proves equality, $\vartheta(C_5) = \sqrt 5$; the lower bound needs the dual characterisation of $\vartheta$.

## Sources

- L. Lovász, *On the Shannon capacity of a graph*, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 25 (1979), 1–7.
