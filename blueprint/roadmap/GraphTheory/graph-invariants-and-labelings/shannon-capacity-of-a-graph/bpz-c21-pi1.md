# Buys–Polak–Zuiddam one-block profile for the 21-cycle

This is the Buys–Polak–Zuiddam
(BPZ) profile whose code, pairs, auxiliary and neutral coordinates are $11441$, $10$,
$11441$ and $11398$.

The profile consists of these four integers alone; BPZ obtain them from a four-dimensional construction.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), twenty-one-cycle construction; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
