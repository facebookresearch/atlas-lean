# Buys–Polak–Zuiddam lower bound for the capacity of the fifteen-cycle

Let $c$ be the
code coordinate of the $1024$-block fifteen-cycle profile $\pi_{1024}$ of Buys–Polak–Zuiddam.
Then $\max(c, 0)^{1/4096}$ is at most the Shannon capacity of the $15$-cycle, as an inequality
in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

- Pjotr Buys, Sven Polak and Jeroen Zuiddam, *Lean-verified lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, arXiv:2607.29681v1 (2026), exact fifteen-cycle root rate; the authors credit ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro and Claude Opus 5 for finding the improved bounds.
