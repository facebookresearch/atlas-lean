# Cycle codes

For natural numbers $q$ and $n$, a cycle code of length $n$ modulo $q$ is a finite set $A$ of words of length $n$ over $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$ (over $\mathbb{Z}$ when $q = 0$) satisfying the [independent-code condition](is-cycle-code.md): every difference of two words of $A$ whose coordinates all lie in $\{-1, 0, 1\}$ is the zero word. For $q \ge 3$ these are the independent sets of the $n$-th strong power of the cycle graph $C_q$.
