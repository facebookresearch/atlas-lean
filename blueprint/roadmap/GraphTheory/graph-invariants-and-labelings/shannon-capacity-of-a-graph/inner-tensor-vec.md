# Inner products of tensor products factor

For $f_i, g_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$,

$$
\langle f_0 \otimes \dots \otimes f_{k-1},\ g_0 \otimes \dots \otimes g_{k-1} \rangle = \prod_{i < k} \langle f_i, g_i \rangle ,
$$

by expanding the sum over words $j$ as a product of sums. In particular norms and costs of [tensor products](tensor-vec.md) are products of those of the factors.

## Sources

- D. E. Knuth, *The Sandwich Theorem*, Electron. J. Combin. 1 (1994), #A1, arXiv:math/9312214v1, §20, eqs. (20.7)-(20.8).
