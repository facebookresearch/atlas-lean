# Lovász's umbrella

Lovász's umbrella labels the vertex $k \in \mathbb{Z}/5$ of the pentagon $C_5$ by the rib

$$
u_k = \bigl(h,\ \cos(2\pi k/5),\ \sin(2\pi k/5)\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^3 ,
$$

where $h$ is the [umbrella height](umbrella-height.md). Ribs of vertices two steps apart are orthogonal, so this is an [orthogonal labeling](../theta-function-of-a-graph/is-orthogonal-labeling.md) of $C_5$, and every rib makes the same angle with the handle $e_0$.

## Sources

- L. Lovász, *On the Shannon capacity of a graph*, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 25 (1979), 1–7 (the umbrella).
