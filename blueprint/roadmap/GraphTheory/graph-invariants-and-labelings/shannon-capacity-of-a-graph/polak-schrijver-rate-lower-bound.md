# Polak–Schrijver lower bound for the capacity of the 7-cycle

The Shannon capacity of the
$7$-cycle is at least $367^{1/5}$, as an inequality in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

- Sven Polak and Alexander Schrijver, *New lower bound on the Shannon capacity of C7 from circular graphs*, Information Processing Letters 143 (2019), 37–40, DOI 10.1016/j.ipl.2018.11.006, arXiv:1808.07438, exact fifth-root rate.
