# Knuth's sandwich of convex sets

For every finite graph $G$,

$$
\mathrm{STAB}(G) \subseteq \mathrm{TH}(G) \subseteq \mathrm{QSTAB}(G).
$$

Maximising $\sum_v x_v$ over the three sets gives $\alpha(G) \le \vartheta(G) \le \kappa(G)$, a sharper form of the [sandwich theorem](sandwich.md); the middle equality needs the [characterisation of theta as a maximum over the theta body](lovasz-theta-eq-s-sup-th.md).

## Sources

- D. E. Knuth, *The Sandwich Theorem*, Electron. J. Combin. 1 (1994), #A1, arXiv:math/9312214v1, Lemma 2, eq. (2.1).
