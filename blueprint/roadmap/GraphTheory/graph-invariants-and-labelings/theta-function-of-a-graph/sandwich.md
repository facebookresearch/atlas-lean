# Lovász's sandwich theorem

For every finite graph $G$,

$$
\alpha(G) \le \vartheta(G) \le \chi(\bar G).
$$

Both $\alpha(G)$ and $\chi(\bar G)$ are NP-hard to compute, while $\vartheta(G)$ can be computed to any precision in polynomial time (Grötschel, Lovász and Schrijver). Knuth states the same theorem for the complement, as $\omega(G) \le \vartheta(\bar G) \le \chi(G)$.
