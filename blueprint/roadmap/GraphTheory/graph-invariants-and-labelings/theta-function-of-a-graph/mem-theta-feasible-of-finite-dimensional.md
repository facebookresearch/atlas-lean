# Labelings in any finite-dimensional space give feasible values

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $a \colon V \to E$ an [orthogonal labeling](is-orthogonal-labeling.md) of $G$, $c \in E$ nonzero and $t \ge 0$ with $1 \le t \cdot \mathrm{cost}_c(a_v)$ for every vertex $v$. Then $t$ is a [feasible value](theta-feasible.md) of the theta program. An orthonormal basis identifies $E$ isometrically with $\mathbb{R}^d$, and isometries preserve inner products, norms and hence costs.
