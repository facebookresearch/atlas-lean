# The theta body lies in the clique-constrained polytope

$\mathrm{TH}(G) \subseteq \mathrm{QSTAB}(G)$. For a clique $Q$, label the vertices of $Q$ by $1 \in \mathbb{R}^1$ and the others by $0$, with handle $1$. Two distinct non-adjacent vertices are not both in the clique, so one of them gets $0$: this is an orthogonal labeling, whose costs form the characteristic vector of $Q$. The defining inequality of $\mathrm{TH}(G)$ for it is $\sum_{v \in Q} x_v \le 1$.

## Sources

- D. E. Knuth, *The Sandwich Theorem*, Electron. J. Combin. 1 (1994), #A1, arXiv:math/9312214v1, proof of Lemma 2 ("every clique labeling is an orthogonal labeling of dimension 1").
