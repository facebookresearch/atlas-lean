# Šoltés's problem for the Wiener index

Is the 11-cycle the only connected graph whose Wiener index is unchanged by deleting any vertex, with the deletion remaining connected? The Wiener index is the sum of pairwise graph distances and originated in mathematical chemistry.

## What would settle it

Prove that every graph satisfying the exact deletion property is isomorphic to C₁₁, or exhibit a different graph and verify all distance sums. A classification of a named unresolved graph family can also be a meaningful intermediate result.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Dobrynin records that the problem was posed by Šoltés in 1991, that the 11-cycle is the only known example, and that the problem remains open.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
