# Erdős Problem 1079 (a vertex with a dense neighbourhood)

The statement that for every natural number $r \ge 4$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 2$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $\mathrm{ex}(n; K_r)$ edges has a vertex $v$ of degree $d \ge c\,n$ whose neighbourhood spans at least $\mathrm{ex}(d; K_{r-1})$ edges, counting the edges of the subgraph induced by the neighbours of $v$. Here $\mathrm{ex}(m; K_s)$ is the largest number of edges of a $K_s$-free simple graph on $m$ vertices. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 1079](dense-neighbourhood-turan-holds.md).
