# Resolution of Erdős Problem 803

It is not the case that there are real constants $D$ and $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $m \ge 1$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $n \log n$ edges (natural logarithm) has a subgraph $H$, not necessarily induced, with exactly $m$ vertices and at least $c\, m \log m$ edges that is $D$-balanced as a graph on its own vertex set: its maximum degree is at most $D$ times its minimum degree. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 803 (balanced subgraphs of graphs with $n \log n$ edges)](balanced-dense-subgraphs.md) is false.
