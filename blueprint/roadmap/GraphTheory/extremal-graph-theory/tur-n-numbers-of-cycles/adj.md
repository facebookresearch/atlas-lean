# Adjacency in the Wenger graph

Over any field $K$, points $p$ and lines $\ell$ are
triples in $K^3$. A point and a line are adjacent exactly when $p_2+\ell_2=p_1\ell_1$ and
$p_3+\ell_3=p_1\ell_1^2$.

## Sources

- Cioabă, Lazebnik and Li, *On the Spectrum of Wenger Graphs*, arXiv:1402.3341, equation (1), with the two vertex classes interchanged and the equations taken over $K$.
