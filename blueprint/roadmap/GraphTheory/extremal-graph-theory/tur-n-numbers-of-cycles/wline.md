# Wenger line with prescribed first coordinate through a point

Over any field $K$,
points and lines are triples in $K^3$. Given a point $p=(p_1,p_2,p_3)$ and $b\in K$, the unique
line adjacent to $p$ with first coordinate $b$ is $(b,p_1b-p_2,p_1b^2-p_3)$.
The two incidence equations determine the remaining line coordinates.

## Sources

- Cioabă, Lazebnik and Li, *On the Spectrum of Wenger Graphs*, arXiv:1402.3341, equation (1) over finite fields, with the point and line classes interchanged.
