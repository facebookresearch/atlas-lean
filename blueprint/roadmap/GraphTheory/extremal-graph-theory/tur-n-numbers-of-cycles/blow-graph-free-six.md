# The blow-up of the Wenger graph has no 6-cycle

Over any field $K$, take points and
lines in $K^3$ and replace each line of the Wenger graph by two copies with the same neighbors.
The resulting graph contains no copy of $C_6$.

## Sources

- The one-sided doubling argument of Lazebnik, Ustimenko and Woldar, described in Füredi and Simonovits, *The history of degenerate (bipartite) extremal graph problems*, Construction 4.28.
