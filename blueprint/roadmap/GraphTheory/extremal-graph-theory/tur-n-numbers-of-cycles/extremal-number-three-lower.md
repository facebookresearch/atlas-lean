# Lower bound for the extremal number at $k = 3$

For every natural number $m$, put
$q=2^{m+1}$. Then $\mathrm{ex}(3q^3;\{C_5,C_6\}) \ge 2q^4$. Doubling each line of the Wenger
graph over the field of order $q$ gives a graph on $3q^3$ vertices with $2q^4$ edges and no copy
of either cycle.

## Sources

- Füredi and Simonovits, *The history of degenerate (bipartite) extremal graph problems*, Construction 4.28 and the bipartite lower bound following Theorem 4.31.
