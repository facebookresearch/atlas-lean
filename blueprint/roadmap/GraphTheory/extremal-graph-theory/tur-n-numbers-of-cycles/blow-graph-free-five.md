# The blow-up of the Wenger graph has no 5-cycle

Over any field $K$, take points and
lines in $K^3$ and replace each line of the Wenger graph by two copies with the same neighbors.
The resulting graph contains no copy of $C_5$, since every edge joins a point to a line copy.

## Sources

- Cioabă, Lazebnik and Li, *On the Spectrum of Wenger Graphs*, arXiv:1402.3341, §1 (the point-line bipartition of the finite Wenger graph).
