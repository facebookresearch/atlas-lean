# Erdős Problem 113 (Turán exponent 3/2 and 2-degeneracy)

The statement that for every finite set $V$ and every bipartite (properly $2$-colourable) simple graph $G$ on $V$, $\mathrm{ex}(n; G) = O(n^{3/2})$ as $n \to \infty$ if and only if $G$ is $2$-degenerate, that is, every nonempty set $W$ of vertices contains a vertex with at most two neighbours in $W$. Here $\mathrm{ex}(n; G)$ is the extremal number, the largest number of edges of a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ containing no copy of $G$ (not necessarily induced), and $0$ if every such graph contains $G$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 113](bipartite-turan-three-halves-iff-degener-2.md).
