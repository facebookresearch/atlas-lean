# The 1-subdivision of a complete graph

For a natural number $k$, the $1$-subdivision $G_k$ of $K_k$ is the bipartite simple graph whose vertices are the elements $y_i$ of $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ and the two-element subsets $p$ of $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$, with $y_i$ adjacent to $p$ exactly when $i \in p$; there are no other edges.
