# Erdős Problem 926 (Turán numbers of the graphs $H_k$)

The statement that for every natural number $k \ge 4$ there is a real $C$ such that $\mathrm{ex}(n; H_k) \le C\, n^{3/2}$ for every natural number $n$. For a natural number $k$, $H_k$ is the simple graph on the vertices $x$, $y_0, \dots, y_{k-1}$ and $z_{ij}$ for $0 \le i < j < k$, in which $x$ is adjacent to every $y_i$ and $z_{ij}$ is adjacent to exactly $y_i$ and $y_j$; there are no other edges. Here $\mathrm{ex}(n; H)$ is the extremal number, the largest number of edges of a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ containing no copy of $H$ (not necessarily induced), and $0$ if every such graph contains $H$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 926](extremal-number-three-halves-holds.md).
