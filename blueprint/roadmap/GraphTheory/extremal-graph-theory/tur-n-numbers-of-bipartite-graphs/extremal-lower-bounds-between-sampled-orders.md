# Extremal lower bounds between sampled orders

Let $H$ be a fixed finite simple graph with no isolated vertex. Suppose natural orders $N_j$ tend to infinity and $N_{j+1}\le 2N_j$ for every sufficiently large $j$. If for some $\gamma>0$ one has $\operatorname{ex}(N_j;H)\ge N_j^\gamma$ eventually, then $\operatorname{ex}(n;H)\ge2^{-\gamma}n^\gamma$ for every sufficiently large natural $n$. Real powers are used. There is no requirement that the sequence be strictly increasing: first crossing provides consecutive bracketing orders.
