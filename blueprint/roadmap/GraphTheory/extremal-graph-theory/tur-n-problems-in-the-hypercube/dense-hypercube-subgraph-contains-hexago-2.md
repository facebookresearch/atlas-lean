# Resolution of Erdős Problem 666

It is not the case that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every subgraph $H$ of the hypercube $Q_n$ (a graph on the same vertex set with $H \le Q_n$) with at least $\varepsilon\, n\, 2^{n-1}$ edges contains a cycle of length $6$. Here $Q_n$ is the graph on the Boolean vectors of length $n$ joining two vectors that differ in exactly one coordinate. Equivalently, for some $\varepsilon > 0$ there are infinitely many $n$ for which some subgraph of $Q_n$ with at least $\varepsilon\, n\, 2^{n-1}$ edges has no cycle of length $6$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 666 (hexagons in dense subgraphs of the hypercube)](dense-hypercube-subgraph-contains-hexago.md) is false.
