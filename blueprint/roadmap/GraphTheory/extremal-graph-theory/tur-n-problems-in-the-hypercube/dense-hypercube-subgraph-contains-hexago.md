# Erdős Problem 666 (hexagons in dense subgraphs of the hypercube)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every subgraph $H$ of the hypercube $Q_n$ (a graph on the same vertex set with $H \le Q_n$) with at least $\varepsilon\, n\, 2^{n-1}$ edges contains a cycle of length $6$. Here $Q_n$ is the graph on the Boolean vectors of length $n$ joining two vectors that differ in exactly one coordinate. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 666](dense-hypercube-subgraph-contains-hexago-2.md).
