# Resolution of Erdős Problem 127

For every real $K$ there is a natural number $m$ such that every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with exactly $m$ edges has a bipartite subgraph $H \le G$ with at least $m/2 + (\sqrt{8m+1} - 1)/8 + K$ edges; that is, the excess over Edwards' bound is unbounded. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 127 (excess over Edwards' bound)](edwards-excess-unbounded.md) holds.
