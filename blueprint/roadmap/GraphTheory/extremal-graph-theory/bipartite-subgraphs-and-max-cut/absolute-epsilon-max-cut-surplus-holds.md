# $K_r$-free graphs have max-cut surplus $\Omega_r(m^{1/2+\varepsilon})$

There is a real $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every natural number $r \ge 3$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that every $K_r$-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, with $m$ edges has a vertex set $S$ with at least $m/2 + c\, m^{1/2 + \varepsilon}$ edges between $S$ and its complement (each such edge counted once): the max-cut surplus of $G$ is $\Omega_r(m^{1/2 + \varepsilon})$.
