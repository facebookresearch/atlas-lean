# Resolution of Erdős Problem 581

There are real constants $c_1, c_2 > 0$ such that for every natural number $m$, $m/2 + c_1 m^{4/5} \le f(m) \le m/2 + c_2 m^{4/5}$, where $f(m)$ is the largest $k$ such that every triangle-free simple graph on a finite vertex set with exactly $m$ edges has a bipartite subgraph with at least $k$ edges. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 581 (bipartite subgraphs of triangle-free graphs)](triangle-free-max-cut-asymptotics.md) holds.
