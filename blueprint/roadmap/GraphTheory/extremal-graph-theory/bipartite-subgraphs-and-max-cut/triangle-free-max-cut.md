# Largest bipartite subgraph guaranteed in triangle-free graphs

For a natural number $m$, $f(m)$ is the largest $k$ such that every triangle-free simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with exactly $m$ edges has a bipartite subgraph $H \le G$ with at least $k$ edges. Such $k$ are at most $m$, since a matching with $m$ edges is triangle-free.
