# Erdős Problem 581 (bipartite subgraphs of triangle-free graphs)

The statement that there are real constants $c_1, c_2 > 0$ such that for every natural number $m$, $m/2 + c_1 m^{4/5} \le f(m) \le m/2 + c_2 m^{4/5}$, where $f(m)$ is the largest $k$ such that every triangle-free simple graph on a finite vertex set with exactly $m$ edges has a bipartite subgraph with at least $k$ edges. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 581](triangle-free-max-cut-asymptotics-holds.md).
