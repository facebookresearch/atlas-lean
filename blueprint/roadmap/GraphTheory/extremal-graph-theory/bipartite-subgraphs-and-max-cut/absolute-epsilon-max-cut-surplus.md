# Max-cut surplus of $K_r$-free graphs with an absolute exponent (Oberwolfach question)

The statement that there is a real $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every natural number $r \ge 3$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that every $K_r$-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, with $m$ edges has a vertex set $S$ with at least $m/2 + c\, m^{1/2 + \varepsilon}$ edges between $S$ and its complement (each such edge counted once): the max-cut surplus of $G$ is $\Omega_r(m^{1/2 + \varepsilon})$. Stated here as a proposition.

## Sources

- A. Milojević (with Z. Jin, I. Tomon and S. Zhang), MaxCut in H-free graphs: recent results and connections, in Combinatorics, Oberwolfach Reports 23 (2026), no. 1, pp. 13–14, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2026/1.
