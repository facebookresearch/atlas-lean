# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1078

There is a function $\varepsilon$ from the natural numbers to the positive reals with $\varepsilon(r) \to 0$ as $r \to \infty$ such that for all natural numbers $r \ge 2$ and $n \ge 1$, every $r$-partite simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, r-1\} \times \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, with parts $\{i\} \times \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and no edge inside a part, in which every vertex has at least $(r - 3/2 - \varepsilon(r))\, n$ neighbours contains a clique $K_r$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1078 (cliques in multipartite graphs of large minimum degree)](multipartite-min-degree-clique-with-slac.md) holds.
