# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1009

For every real $c > 0$ there is a natural number $f$ such that for all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ and every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor + k$ edges and $k < c\,n$, there is a family $T$ of triangles of $G$, any two sharing at most one vertex (so pairwise edge-disjoint), with $k \le |T| + f$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1009 (edge-disjoint triangles above the Turán number)](edge-disjoint-triangles-above-turan.md) holds.
