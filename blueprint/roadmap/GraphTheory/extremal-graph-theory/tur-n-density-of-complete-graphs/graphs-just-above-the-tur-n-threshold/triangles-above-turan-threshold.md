# Erdős Problem 1010 (triangles just above the Turán number)

The statement that for all natural numbers $n$ and $t$ with $t < \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor + t$ edges has at least $t \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ triangles. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 1010](triangles-above-turan-threshold-holds.md).
