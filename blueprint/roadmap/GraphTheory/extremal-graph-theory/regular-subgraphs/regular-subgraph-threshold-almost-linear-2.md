# Resolution of Erdős Problem 182

For every natural number $k \ge 3$ and every real $\varepsilon > 0$, for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $n^{1+\varepsilon}$ edges has a $k$-regular subgraph: a subgraph with a nonempty vertex set in which every vertex has exactly $k$ neighbours in the subgraph. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 182 (Erdős–Sauer problem on regular subgraphs)](regular-subgraph-threshold-almost-linear.md) holds.
