# A stronger form of Erdős Problem 715, second question

A stronger form of the second question of Erdős Problem 715, which asks whether there is **some** $r \ge 4$ with this property: the statement that for **every** natural number $r \ge 4$, every $r$-regular simple graph on a finite nonempty vertex set contains a $3$-regular subgraph: a subgraph, not necessarily spanning or induced, with a nonempty vertex set in which every vertex has exactly $3$ neighbours in the subgraph. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Every r-regular graph with r at least 4 contains a 3-regular subgraph](regular-degree-at-least-four-contains-cu-2.md).
