# Every r-regular graph with r at least 4 contains a 3-regular subgraph

For every natural number $r \ge 4$, every $r$-regular simple graph on a finite nonempty vertex set contains a $3$-regular subgraph: a subgraph, not necessarily spanning or induced, with a nonempty vertex set in which every vertex has exactly $3$ neighbours in the subgraph.
