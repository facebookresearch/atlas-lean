# Erdős Problem 182 (Erdős–Sauer problem on regular subgraphs)

The statement that for every natural number $k \ge 3$ and every real $\varepsilon > 0$, for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $n^{1+\varepsilon}$ edges has a $k$-regular subgraph: a subgraph with a nonempty vertex set in which every vertex has exactly $k$ neighbours in the subgraph. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 182](regular-subgraph-threshold-almost-linear-2.md).
