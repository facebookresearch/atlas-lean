# Erdős Problem 814 (small subgraphs of large minimum degree)

The statement that for every natural number $k \ge 2$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge k - 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with at least $(k-1)(n-k+2) + \binom{k-2}{2} + 1$ edges has a nonempty set $S$ of at most $(1 - c)\,n$ vertices whose induced subgraph has minimum degree at least $k$: every vertex of $S$ has at least $k$ neighbours in $S$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 814](small-min-degree-subgraph-holds.md).
