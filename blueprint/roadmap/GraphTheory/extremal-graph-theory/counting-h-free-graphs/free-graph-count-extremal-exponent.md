# Erdős Problem 59 (the number of $H$-free graphs)

The statement that for every natural number $k$, every simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ that contains a cycle, and every real $\varepsilon > 0$, for all sufficiently large $n$ the number of $H$-free simple graphs on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is at most $2^{(1 + \varepsilon)\, \mathrm{ex}(n; H)}$, where $\mathrm{ex}(n; H)$ is the largest number of edges of an $H$-free simple graph on $n$ vertices. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 59](free-graph-count-extremal-exponent-refut.md).
