# Lower bound on the Turán density, chromatic form

If $\chi(H) = r + 1$ with $r \ge 1$, then $\pi(H) \ge 1 - 1/r$. A graph with chromatic number $r + 1$ is not $r$-colourable, so this follows from the [lower bound on the Turán density](one-sub-inv-le-turan-density.md). Here $H$ may have any vertex type.
