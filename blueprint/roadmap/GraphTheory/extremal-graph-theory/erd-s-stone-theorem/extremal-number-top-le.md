# Extremal numbers dominate those of cliques

Let $H$ be a simple graph and $r \ge 1$ a natural number such that $H$ is not $r$-colourable. Then $\mathrm{ex}(n, K_{r+1}) \le \mathrm{ex}(n, H)$ for every $n$. Indeed $\mathrm{ex}(n, K_{r+1})$ is the number of edges of the Turán graph $T(n, r)$ (Turán's theorem), and that graph is $r$-colourable, so it contains no copy of $H$.
