# Lower bound on the Turán density

If a graph $H$ is not $r$-colourable, where $r \ge 1$, then

$$\pi(H) \ge 1 - \frac{1}{r}.$$

For every $n$, $\mathrm{ex}(n, H) \ge \mathrm{ex}(n, K_{r+1})$. Dividing by $\binom{n}{2}$ and passing to the limit gives $\pi(H) \ge \pi(K_{r+1}) = 1 - 1/r$.

- [Extremal numbers dominate those of cliques](extremal-number-top-le.md): the comparison $\mathrm{ex}(n, K_{r+1}) \le \mathrm{ex}(n, H)$.
