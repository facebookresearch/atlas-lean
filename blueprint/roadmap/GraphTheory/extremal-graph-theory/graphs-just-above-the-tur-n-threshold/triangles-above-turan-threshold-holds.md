# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1010

For all natural numbers $n$ and $t$ with $t < \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor + t$ edges has at least $t \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ triangles. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1010 (triangles just above the Turán number)](triangles-above-turan-threshold.md) holds.
