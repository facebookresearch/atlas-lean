# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1010

For all natural numbers $n$ and $t$ with $t < \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor + t$ edges has at least $t \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ triangles.

- V. S. Nikiforov and N. G. Khadzhiivanov, Solution of the problem of P. Erdős on the number of triangles in graphs with n vertices and [n²/4] + l edges, C. R. Acad. Bulgare Sci. (1981), 969–970.
