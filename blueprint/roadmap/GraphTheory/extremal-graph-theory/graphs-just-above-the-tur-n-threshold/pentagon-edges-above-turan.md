# Erdős Problem 608 (edges on pentagons above the Turán threshold)

The statement that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with more than $n^2/4$ edges has at least $(2/9)\, n^2$ edges that lie on a cycle of length $5$.
