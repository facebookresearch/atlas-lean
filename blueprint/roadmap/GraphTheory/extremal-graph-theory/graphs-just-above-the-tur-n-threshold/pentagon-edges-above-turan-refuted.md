# Resolution of Erdős Problem 608

It is not the case that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with more than $n^2/4$ edges has at least $(2/9)\, n^2$ edges that lie on a cycle of length $5$. Equivalently, for infinitely many $n$ some simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with more than $n^2/4$ edges has fewer than $(2/9)\, n^2$ edges on cycles of length $5$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 608 (edges on pentagons above the Turán threshold)](pentagon-edges-above-turan.md) is false.
