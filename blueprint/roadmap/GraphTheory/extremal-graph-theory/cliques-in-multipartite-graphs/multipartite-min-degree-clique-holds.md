# Haxell's theorem on cliques in multipartite graphs

For all natural numbers $r \ge 2$ and $n \ge 1$, every $r$-partite simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, r-1\} \times \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, with parts $\{i\} \times \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and no edge inside a part, in which every vertex has at least $(r - 3/2)\, n$ neighbours contains a clique $K_r$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1078 without slack](multipartite-min-degree-clique.md) holds.
