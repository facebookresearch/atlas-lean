# Induced density

For a graph $H$ on $k$ vertices and a finite graph $G$, the induced density $p(H, G)$ is the probability that a uniformly random $k$-subset of the vertices of $G$ induces a copy of $H$. It is $0$ when $G$ has fewer than $k$ vertices. These densities, written $p(M, N)$ for finite models of a universal theory, are the basic quantities of Razborov's flag algebras; the [averaging identity](induced-density-eq-average.md) is their basic relation.
