# Ordered triangles of a graph

The ordered triples $(u, v, w)$ of vertices of a finite simple graph $G$ that are pairwise adjacent. They are the homomorphisms from the triangle $K_3$ to $G$, so their number is [six times the number of triangles](card-ordered-triangles-eq-six-mul.md), and dividing it by $n^3$ gives the homomorphism density $t(K_3, G)$ of graph-limit theory.
