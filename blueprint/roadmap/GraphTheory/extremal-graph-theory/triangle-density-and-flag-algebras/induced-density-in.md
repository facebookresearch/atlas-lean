# Induced density of H inside a vertex set

For a graph $H$ on $k$ vertices, a graph $G$ and a finite set $L$ of vertices, $p(H, G[L])$ is the proportion of the $k$-subsets of $L$ that [induce a copy of $H$](induced-count-in.md) in $G$: their number divided by $\binom{|L|}{k}$, read as $0$ when $|L| < k$. It is the [induced density](induced-density.md) of $H$ in the subgraph of $G$ induced on $L$.
