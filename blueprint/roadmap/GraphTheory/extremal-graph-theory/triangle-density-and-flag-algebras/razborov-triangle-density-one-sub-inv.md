# Razborov's function at the Turán densities

For every natural number $t \ge 2$, at the edge density $a = 1 - 1/(t+1)$ of the balanced complete $(t+1)$-partite graph, [Razborov's function](razborov-triangle-density.md) takes the value

$$h(a) = \frac{t(t-1)}{(t+1)^2},$$

the triangle density of that graph. Here $c = 1/(t+1)$, all parts are equal, and Goodman's bound $a(2a - 1)$ has the same value, so the two bounds agree at these densities.
