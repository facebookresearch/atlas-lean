# Goodman's bound in homomorphism-count form

For a finite simple graph with $n$ vertices, $m$ edges and $T_0$ [ordered triangles](ordered-triangles.md),

$$2m(4m - n^2) \le nT_0$$

as an inequality of integers. Dividing by $n^4$ gives $t(K_3) \ge t(K_2)(2t(K_2) - 1)$ for the homomorphism densities $t(K_2) = 2m/n^2$ and $t(K_3) = T_0/n^3$: a finite form, with no error term, of the flag-algebra inequality $K_3 \ge \rho(2\rho - 1)$.

## Sources

- A. A. Razborov, Flag algebras, J. Symbolic Logic 72 (2007), 1239-1282; Example 11 in the author's version, people.cs.uchicago.edu/~razborov/files/flag.pdf.
