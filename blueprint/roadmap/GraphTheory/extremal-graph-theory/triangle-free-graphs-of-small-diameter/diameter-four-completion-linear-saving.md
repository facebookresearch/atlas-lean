# Erdős Problem 619 (completing connected triangle-free graphs to diameter four)

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ such that every connected triangle-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, satisfies $h_4(G) < (1 - c)\, n$, where $h_4(G)$ is the least number of edges whose addition to $G$ gives a triangle-free graph of diameter at most $4$. Stated here as a proposition.
