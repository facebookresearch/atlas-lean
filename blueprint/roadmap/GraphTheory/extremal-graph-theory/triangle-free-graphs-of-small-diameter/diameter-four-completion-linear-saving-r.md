# Resolution of Erdős Problem 619

It is not the case that there is a real $c > 0$ such that every connected triangle-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, satisfies $h_4(G) < (1 - c)\, n$, where $h_4(G)$ is the least number of edges whose addition to $G$ gives a triangle-free graph of diameter at most $4$. Equivalently, for every real $c > 0$ some connected triangle-free graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for some $n$, has $h_4(G) \ge (1 - c)\, n$.
