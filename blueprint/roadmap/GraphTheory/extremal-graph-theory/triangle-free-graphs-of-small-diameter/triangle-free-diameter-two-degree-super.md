# Erdős Problem 133, second question

The statement that for every real $K$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every triangle-free simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$) has a vertex of degree at least $K\sqrt{n}$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 133, second question](triangle-free-diameter-two-degree-super-2.md).
