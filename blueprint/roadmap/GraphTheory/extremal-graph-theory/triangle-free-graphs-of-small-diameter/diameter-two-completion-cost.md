# Cost of completing a graph to a triangle-free graph of diameter two

For a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $h_2(G)$ is the least number of edges that can be added to $G$ to obtain a triangle-free graph in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$); it is $0$ if no such graph exists.
