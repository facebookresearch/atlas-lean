# Erdős Problem 133, first question (order of the least maximum degree)

The statement that there are real numbers $c, C > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$: every triangle-free simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$) has a vertex of degree at least $c\sqrt{n}$, and some such graph has all degrees at most $C\sqrt{n}$.
