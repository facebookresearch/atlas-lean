# Resolution of Erdős Problem 134

For all real $\varepsilon, \delta > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every triangle-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which every vertex has fewer than $n^{1/2 - \varepsilon}$ neighbours is contained in a triangle-free graph $H$ on the same vertices in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$), with at most $\delta n^2$ edges of $H$ not in $G$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 134 (completing sparse triangle-free graphs to diameter two)](triangle-free-diameter-two-completion.md) holds.
