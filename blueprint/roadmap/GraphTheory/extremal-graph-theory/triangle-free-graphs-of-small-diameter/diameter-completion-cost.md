# Cost of completing a graph to a triangle-free graph of diameter r

For a natural number $r$ and a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $h_r(G)$ is the least number of edges that can be added to $G$ to obtain a triangle-free graph $H \supseteq G$ of diameter at most $r$, where diameter is extended diameter: a nonempty disconnected graph has infinite diameter, while the graph with no vertices has diameter $0$ and is its own completion, with cost $0$. The value is $0$ if no such $H$ exists.
