# Triangle-free graphs of diameter two have least maximum degree of order $\sqrt n$

There are real numbers $c, C > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$: every triangle-free simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$) has a vertex of degree at least $c\sqrt{n}$, and some such graph has all degrees at most $C\sqrt{n}$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 133, first question (order of the least maximum degree)](triangle-free-diameter-two-degree-order.md) holds.
