# Erdős Problem 618 (subquadratic completion cost)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a real $\delta > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every triangle-free simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with maximum degree at most $\delta\sqrt{n}$ satisfies $h_2(G) \le \varepsilon n^2$, where $h_2(G)$ is the least number of edges whose addition to $G$ gives a triangle-free graph in which any two distinct vertices are adjacent or have a common neighbour (diameter at most $2$). Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 618](diameter-two-completion-subquadratic-hol.md).
