# Four-cycle density as a sum

Let $V$ be a finite set and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. The [homomorphism density](../../random-graphs/graph-limits/hom-density.md) of the four-cycle is

$$t(C_4, w) = \lvert V\rvert^{-4} \sum_{a, b, c, d \in V} w(a, b)\, w(b, c)\, w(c, d)\, w(d, a),$$

with both sides $0$ when $V$ is empty.
