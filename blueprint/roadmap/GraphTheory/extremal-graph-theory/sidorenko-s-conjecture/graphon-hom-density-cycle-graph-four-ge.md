# Sidorenko's inequality for the four-cycle, graphon form

For every graphon $W$, $t(C_4, W) \ge t(K_2, W)^4$: the same two Cauchy–Schwarz steps as in the finite case, with the codegree function $M(x, z) = \int W(x, y) W(y, z) \, dy$ and the degree function $d(y) = \int W(x, y) \, dx$.

## Background

- [Sidorenko's inequality for the four-cycle](hom-density-cycle-graph-four-ge.md): the finite weighted-graph case, proved.

## Sources

- L. Lovász, "Subgraph densities in signed graphons and the local Sidorenko conjecture", arXiv:1004.3026v1 (2010); published in *Electron. J. Combin.* 18 (2011), §1 (Sidorenko's proof covers bigraphs with a colour class of at most four nodes, hence $C_4 = K_{2,2}$).
