# Edge density as a sum

Let $V$ be a finite set and $w$ a real weight on the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. The [homomorphism density](../../random-graphs/graph-limits/hom-density.md) of an edge is

$$t(K_2, w) = \lvert V\rvert^{-2} \sum_{a, b \in V} w(a, b),$$

with both sides $0$ when $V$ is empty.
