# Do common graphs have bounded chromatic number?

The statement that there is a natural number $C$ such that every common simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$, for every $k$, has chromatic number at most $C$. Here $H$ with $e(H)$ edges is common when $2^{1 - e(H)} \le t(H, W) + t(H, 1 - W)$ for every graphon $W$, $t(H, W)$ being the homomorphism density of $H$ in $W$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Common graphs have unbounded chromatic number](common-graphs-bounded-chromatic-refuted.md).
