# Erdős Problem 1008 (large four-cycle-free subgraphs)

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ such that every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set, with $m$ edges, has a subgraph $H \le G$ containing no cycle of length $4$ with at least $c\, m^{2/3}$ edges.
