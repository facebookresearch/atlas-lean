# Erdős Problem 765 (asymptotics of $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4)$)

The statement that $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4) \sim n^{3/2}/2$ as $n \to \infty$: the ratio of $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4)$ to $n^{3/2}/2$ tends to $1$. Here $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4)$ is the largest number of edges of a simple graph on $n$ vertices containing no cycle of length $4$ (not necessarily induced). Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Asymptotic formula for $\mathrm{ex}(n; C_4)$](four-cycle-extremal-asymptotic-holds.md).
