# Nowhere-zero real flow

For a loopless multigraph $M$ with vertex set $V$ and edge set $E$, each edge having an unordered pair of distinct ends and parallel edges allowed, $E$ finite, and a real number $r$, a nowhere-zero $r$-flow on $M$ consists of an orientation, a choice of a head $h(e)$ among the ends of every edge $e$, and real values $\varphi(e)$ with $1 \le |\varphi(e)| \le r - 1$, such that at every vertex $v$ the sum of $\varphi(e)$ over the edges with head $v$ equals the sum of $\varphi(e)$ over the edges with $v$ as their other end.
