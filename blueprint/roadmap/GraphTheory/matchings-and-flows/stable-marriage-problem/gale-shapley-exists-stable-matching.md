# Gale–Shapley theorem

Let $M$ and $W$ be finite sets with the same number of elements. Suppose that each $m \in M$ has a strict total order of preference on $W$ and each $w \in W$ has a strict total order of preference on $M$. Then some perfect matching between $M$ and $W$ (a bijection $M \to W$) is stable for these preferences: no $m$ and $w$ prefer each other to their partners.
