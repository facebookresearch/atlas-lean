# Perfect matching

A perfect matching between sets $M$ and $W$ is a bijection
$\mu \colon M \to W$, which matches each $m \in M$ with $\mu(m) \in W$.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "The economics of matching: stability and incentives", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 7(4) (1982), 617–628, §2: an outcome of the marriage problem is a one-to-one matching, an invertible function `x : M → W`.
