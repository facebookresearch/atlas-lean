# Stock-level arc balance

On a finite network with incidence matrix $B$, if node inventories update over one interval by $m' = m + B F + s - r$, where $F$ is a signed arc transfer and $s$, $r$ are external source and retirement vectors, then $\sum_v m'_v = \sum_v m_v + \sum_v s_v - \sum_v r_v$. No sign conditions are needed.
