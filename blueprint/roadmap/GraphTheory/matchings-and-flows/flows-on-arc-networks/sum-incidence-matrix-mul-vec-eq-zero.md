# Node sum of an incidence transfer is zero

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets and let each arc $e \in E$ run from its tail $t(e) \in V$ to its head $h(e) \in V$; parallel arcs and loops are allowed. Let $B$ be the signed incidence matrix of the network, $B_{ve} = [h(e) = v] - [t(e) = v]$, where $[P]$ is $1$ if $P$ holds and $0$ otherwise. For every $F : E \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\sum_{v \in V} (BF)_v = 0.$$

No sign condition on $F$ is assumed.
