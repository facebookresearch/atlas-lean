# Augmenting along a path (Hopcroft–Karp, Lemma 1)

Let $M$ be a matching of a simple graph $G$ and $p$ an [$M$-augmenting walk](is-augmenting-walk.md). Then the symmetric difference $M \oplus E(p)$ of $M$ with the edge set of $p$ is a matching of $G$ with $|M| + 1$ edges.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft and R. M. Karp, "An $n^{5/2}$ algorithm for maximum matchings in bipartite graphs", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 2(4) (1973), 225–231, §2. Lemma 1.
