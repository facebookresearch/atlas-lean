# Berge's lemma with augmenting walks

A matching $M$ of a simple graph on a finite vertex set is maximum if and only if no walk is [$M$-augmenting](is-augmenting-walk.md). This restates [Berge's lemma](berge.md) with the named predicate.

Hopcroft and Karp derive it as Corollary 1 of their Theorem 1 (the [disjoint augmenting paths in a symmetric difference](exists-disjoint-augmenting-walks.md)); Berge's lemma also has a direct proof.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft and R. M. Karp, "An $n^{5/2}$ algorithm for maximum matchings in bipartite graphs", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 2(4) (1973), 225–231, §2. Corollary 1, attributed there to Berge.
- C. Berge, "Two theorems in graph theory", *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the USA* 43 (1957), 842–844.
