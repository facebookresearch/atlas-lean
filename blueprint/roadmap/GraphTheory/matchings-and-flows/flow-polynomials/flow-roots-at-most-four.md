# Welsh's conjecture on real roots of flow polynomials

The statement that for every finite multigraph, given by finite sets $V$ of vertices and $E$ of edges and an end map assigning to each edge an unordered pair of vertices (loops and parallel edges allowed), whose flow polynomial $\Phi(x) = \sum_{A \subseteq E} (-1)^{|E| - |A|} x^{|A| + c(A) - |V|}$ is not identically zero on $\mathbb{R}$, every real root of $\Phi$ is at most $4$; here $c(A)$ is the number of connected components of the graph $(V, A)$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Flow polynomials can have real roots above four](flow-roots-at-most-four-refuted.md).
