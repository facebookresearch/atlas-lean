# TTC is the unique competitive allocation

A **competitive allocation** of a housing market is an allocation $\mu$ together with prices $p$ on houses such that every agent receives a house it likes best among those with price at most the price of its own endowment. Every competitive allocation lies in the strict core; the top trading cycles allocation with prices decreasing by round is competitive; and every competitive allocation equals the TTC allocation.

## Proof sketch

In a weakly blocking coalition no member trades down in price and some member trades up to an unaffordable house, which is impossible around its cycle. Uniqueness then follows from uniqueness of the strict-core allocation.
