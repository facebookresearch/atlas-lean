# Serial dictatorship is Pareto efficient and group strategy-proof

For a fixed order of the agents, the **serial dictatorship** lets each agent in turn take its favourite remaining house, ignoring endowments. It is Pareto efficient and group strategy-proof, but, with at least two agents, not individually rational; consequently individual rationality is the axiom that singles out top trading cycles in Ma's characterization.
