# Group strategy-proofness is strategy-proofness plus non-bossiness

A housing mechanism is group strategy-proof if and only if it is strategy-proof and non-bossy.

## Proof sketch

Group strategy-proofness with singleton coalitions is strategy-proofness, and non-bossiness follows by letting a coalition of one agent and the affected others deviate. Conversely, replace the coalition's reports one at a time; strategy-proofness keeps each deviator's house and non-bossiness keeps everyone else's.
