# Top trading cycles is group strategy-proof and non-bossy

The top trading cycles mechanism on Shapley-Scarf housing markets with strict preferences is non-bossy and group strategy-proof (hence also weakly group strategy-proof).

## Proof sketch

Strategy-proofness of TTC is known; for non-bossiness, if a misreport leaves an agent's house unchanged, the agent leaves in the same cycle relative to the others, and the rounds of the two runs coincide. Combine with the characterization.
