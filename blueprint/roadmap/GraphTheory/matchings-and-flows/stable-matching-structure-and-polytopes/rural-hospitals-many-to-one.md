# Roth's rural hospitals theorem

In every Hospitals-Residents market, resident-proposing deferred acceptance produces a stable matching that every resident weakly prefers to any other stable matching. Across all stable matchings, the same residents are matched, every hospital is assigned the same number of residents, and every hospital that does not fill its quota in some stable matching is assigned exactly the same set of residents in every stable matching.

## Proof sketch

Counting: the resident-optimal matching matches a superset of residents and the hospital side matches at most as many, forcing equal counts; for an under-subscribed hospital, a resident it gains in one stable matching but not another would form a blocking pair with it.
