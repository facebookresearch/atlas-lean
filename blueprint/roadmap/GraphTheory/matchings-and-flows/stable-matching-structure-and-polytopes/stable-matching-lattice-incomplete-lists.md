# The lattice of stable matchings with incomplete lists

In a one-to-one market with strict, possibly incomplete preference lists and possibly unequal sides, the stable matchings match the same agents, and for any two stable matchings $\mu, \nu$, giving every proposer the better of its two partners (and every receiver the worse) yields a stable matching $\mu \vee \nu$, and dually $\mu \wedge \nu$; with these operations the stable matchings form a distributive lattice whose top is the proposer-optimal and whose bottom is the receiver-optimal stable matching.
