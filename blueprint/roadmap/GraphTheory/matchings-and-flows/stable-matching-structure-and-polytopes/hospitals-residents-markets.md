# Many-to-one Hospitals-Residents markets

A **Hospitals-Residents** market has residents $r$ and hospitals $h$ with quotas $q_h \ge 1$; each agent has a strict, possibly incomplete preference list over acceptable agents of the other side. A matching assigns each resident to at most one acceptable hospital and each hospital at most $q_h$ acceptable residents. It is **stable** if no resident-hospital pair $(r, h)$ is mutually acceptable such that $r$ prefers $h$ to its assignment and $h$ either has a free seat or prefers $r$ to one of its assigned residents.
