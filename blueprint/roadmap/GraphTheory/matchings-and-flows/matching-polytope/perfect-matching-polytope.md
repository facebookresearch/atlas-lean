# Perfect matching polytope

The perfect matching polytope of a finite graph $G = (V, E)$ is

$$
P_{\mathrm{perfect\ matching}}(G) = \operatorname{conv}\{\chi^M : M \text{ a perfect matching in } G\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^E ,
$$

where a perfect matching is a matching containing every vertex of $G$ and $\chi^M$ is the [incidence vector](edge-incidence.md) of its edges. The polytope is empty when $G$ has no perfect matching.
