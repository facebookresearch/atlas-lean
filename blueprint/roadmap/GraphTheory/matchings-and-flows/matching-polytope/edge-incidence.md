# Incidence vector of a subgraph's edges

For a subgraph $M$ of $G = (V, E)$, its incidence vector $\chi^M \in \mathbb{R}^E$ has $\chi^M_e = 1$ if $e$ is an edge of $M$ and $\chi^M_e = 0$ otherwise. Coordinates are indexed by the edges of $G$.
