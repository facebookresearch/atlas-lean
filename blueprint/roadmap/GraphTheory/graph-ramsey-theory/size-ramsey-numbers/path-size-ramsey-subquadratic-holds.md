# Resolution of Erdős Problem 720 (b)

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, some simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$, for some $N$, has at most $\varepsilon n^2$ edges and is such that every red/blue colouring of its edges (a red subgraph $R \le H$, with blue graph $H \setminus R$) has a monochromatic copy of the path $P_n$ of length $n$ (with $n + 1$ vertices), a copy, not necessarily induced, in $R$ or in $H \setminus R$.
