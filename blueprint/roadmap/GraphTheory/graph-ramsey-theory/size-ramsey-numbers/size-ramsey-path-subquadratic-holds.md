# Size Ramsey numbers of paths are subquadratic

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, some simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, for some $m$, arrows $P_n$ and has at most $\varepsilon n^2$ edges. Here a simple graph $G$ arrows $P_n$, the path on $n$ vertices, when for every colouring of the unordered pairs of its vertices with two colours, the edges of $G$ of some colour form a graph containing a copy of $P_n$, not necessarily induced.
