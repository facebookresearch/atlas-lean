# Resolution of the cycle part of Erdős Problem 1105

For every natural number $k \ge 3$ there is a real constant $C$ such that for every natural number $n$, $\left|\mathrm{AR}(n, C_k) - \left(\frac{k-2}{2} + \frac{1}{k-1}\right) n\right| \le C$. Here $C_k$ is the cycle on $k$ vertices and $\mathrm{AR}(n, H)$ is the anti-Ramsey number: the largest $m$ for which some colouring of the edges of $K_n$ with colours $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, every colour used, has no rainbow copy of $H$ (a copy whose edges have pairwise distinct colours), and $0$ if there is no such $m$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1105, cycle part (anti-Ramsey numbers of cycles)](anti-ramsey-cycle-linear.md) holds.
