# Exponential lower bound for online Ramsey numbers (AIM question)

The statement that there are a real $\varepsilon > 0$ and a natural number $n_0$ such that for every $n \ge n_0$ and every natural number $k$, if Builder can force a monochromatic $K_n$ from the empty position within $k$ moves, then $(\sqrt{2} + \varepsilon)^n \le k$; that is, the diagonal online Ramsey number satisfies $\tilde r(n, n) \ge (\sqrt 2 + \varepsilon)^n$ for all large $n$. Builder can force a monochromatic $K_n$ from a position $P$ within $k$ moves when: for $k = 0$, $P$ already has a monochromatic $K_n$; for $k + 1$, either $P$ already has one, or there are distinct $x, y$ such that the edge $\{x, y\}$ does not occur in $P$ in either colour and, for both colours $b$, Builder can force a monochromatic $K_n$ within $k$ moves from the position obtained by adding $(\{x, y\}, b)$ to $P$. A position has a monochromatic $K_n$ when for some colour $b$ some $n$ natural numbers have all their pairs recorded in $P$ with colour $b$.
