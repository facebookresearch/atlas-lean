# Builder forcing a monochromatic clique within a number of moves

A position of the online Ramsey game is a finite list of pairs $(e, b)$, where $e$ is an unordered pair of natural numbers (an edge Builder has drawn) and $b$ is the colour Painter gave it, red (true) or blue (false). Builder can force a monochromatic $K_n$ from a position $P$ within $k$ moves when: for $k = 0$, $P$ already has a monochromatic $K_n$; for $k + 1$, either $P$ already has one, or there are distinct $x, y$ such that the edge $\{x, y\}$ does not occur in $P$ in either colour and, for both colours $b$, Builder can force a monochromatic $K_n$ within $k$ moves from the position obtained by adding $(\{x, y\}, b)$ to $P$. A position has a monochromatic $K_n$ when for some colour $b$ some $n$ natural numbers have all their pairs recorded in $P$ with colour $b$. Painter's colour is chosen adversarially at every move.
