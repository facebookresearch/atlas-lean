# Erdős Problem 1014 (ratios of consecutive Ramsey numbers)

The statement that for every natural number $k \ge 3$, the ratio $R(k, l+1) / R(k, l)$ of real numbers tends to $1$ as $l \to \infty$. Here $R(s, t)$ is the classical Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that every simple graph on $N$ vertices contains $K_s$ or has a complement containing $K_t$. Stated here as a proposition.
