# Resolution of Erdős Problem 986

For every natural number $s \ge 3$ there are real constants $c > 0$ and $C > 0$ (depending on $s$) such that $R(s, k) \ge C\, k^{s-1} / (\log k)^c$ for all sufficiently large natural numbers $k$, with $\log$ the natural logarithm. Here $R(s, t)$ is the classical Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that every simple graph on $N$ vertices contains $K_s$ or has a complement containing $K_t$.
