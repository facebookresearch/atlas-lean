# Erdős Problem 166 (cubic lower bound for $R(4, k)$)

The statement that there are real constants $c > 0$ and $C > 0$ such that $R(4, k) \ge c\, k^3 / (\log k)^C$ for all sufficiently large natural numbers $k$, with $\log$ the natural logarithm. Here $R(s, t)$ is the classical Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that every simple graph on $N$ vertices contains $K_s$ or has a complement containing $K_t$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 166](ramsey-four-cubic-lower-bound-holds.md).
