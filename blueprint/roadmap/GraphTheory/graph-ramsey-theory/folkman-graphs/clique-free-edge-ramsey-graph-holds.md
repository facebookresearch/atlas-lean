# Resolution of Erdős Problem 924

For all natural numbers $k \ge 2$ and $l \ge 3$ there are a natural number $N$ and a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$ with no $K_{l+1}$ such that for every map $c$ from the unordered pairs of vertices to $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ (a colouring of the edges of $G$ with $k$ colours; its values off the edges are irrelevant) there are an $l$-clique $s$ of $G$ and a colour $i$ with $c(\{x, y\}) = i$ for all distinct $x, y \in s$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 924 ($K_{l+1}$-free graphs that are edge-Ramsey for $K_l$)](clique-free-edge-ramsey-graph.md) holds.
