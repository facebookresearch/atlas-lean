# Resolution of Erdős Problem 582

There is a natural number $n$ and a $K_4$-free simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ such that for every subgraph $R \le G$ (the red edges, with the remaining edges of $G$ blue), $R$ or $G \setminus R$ contains a triangle: every red/blue colouring of the edges of $G$ has a monochromatic triangle. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 582 ($K_4$-free graphs that arrow the triangle)](k-four-free-edge-ramsey-for-triangles.md) holds.
