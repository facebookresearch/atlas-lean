# Resolution of Erdős Problem 636

For every real $C > 0$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ whose clique number and independence number are both less than $C \log n$ (with $\log$ the natural logarithm) has at least $c\, n^{5/2}$ distinct pairs $(|U|, e(G[U]))$, where $U$ ranges over the vertex sets of $G$ and $e(G[U])$ counts the edges of $G$ with both ends in $U$; equivalently, at least $c\,n^{5/2}$ induced subgraphs pairwise differing in their number of vertices or of edges. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 636 (induced subgraphs of distinct sizes)](ramsey-graph-many-induced-sizes.md) holds.
