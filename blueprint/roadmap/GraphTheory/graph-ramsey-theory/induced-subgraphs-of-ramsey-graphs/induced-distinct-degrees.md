# Number of distinct degrees in an induced subgraph

For a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and a set $U$ of its vertices, the number of distinct degrees of $G[U]$ is the number of distinct values of $|\{w \in U : w \text{ adjacent to } v\}|$ as $v$ ranges over $U$, that is, the number of distinct vertex degrees in the subgraph induced by $U$.
