# Number of distinct induced sizes of a graph

For a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the number of distinct induced sizes of $G$ is the number of distinct pairs $(|U|, e(G[U]))$ as $U$ ranges over all vertex sets of $G$, where $e(G[U])$ is the number of edges of $G$ with both ends in $U$. It is the largest number of induced subgraphs of $G$ that pairwise differ in their number of vertices or their number of edges.
