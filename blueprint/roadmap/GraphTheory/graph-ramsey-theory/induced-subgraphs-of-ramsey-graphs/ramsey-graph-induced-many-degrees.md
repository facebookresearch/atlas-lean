# Erdős Problem 637 (induced subgraphs with many distinct degrees)

The statement that for every real $C > 0$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ whose clique number $\omega(G)$ and independence number $\alpha(G)$ are both less than $C \log n$ (with $\log$ the natural logarithm) has a vertex set $U$ with $|U| \ge c\,n$ such that the subgraph induced by $U$ has at least $c\sqrt{n}$ distinct vertex degrees, degrees being taken inside the induced subgraph. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 637](ramsey-graph-induced-many-degrees-holds.md).
