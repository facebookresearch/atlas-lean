# Erdős Problem 518 (covering by monochromatic paths of one colour)

The statement that there is a natural number $n_0$ such that for every $n \ge n_0$ and every simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the vertices can be covered by at most $\sqrt{n}$ paths of $G$ or by at most $\sqrt{n}$ paths of its complement: in every red/blue colouring of the edges of $K_n$, at most $\sqrt{n}$ monochromatic paths of one colour cover all vertices. The paths need not be disjoint, and single vertices count as paths.
