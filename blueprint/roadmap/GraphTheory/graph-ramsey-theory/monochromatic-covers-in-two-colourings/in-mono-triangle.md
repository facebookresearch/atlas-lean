# Edge in a monochromatic triangle

For a red/blue colouring $c$ of the edges of $K_n$ (a map from the unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ to $\{0, 1\}$) and vertices $x, y$, the pair $\{x, y\}$ lies in a monochromatic triangle when some vertex $z \ne x, y$ has $c(\{x, z\}) = c(\{y, z\}) = c(\{x, y\})$.
