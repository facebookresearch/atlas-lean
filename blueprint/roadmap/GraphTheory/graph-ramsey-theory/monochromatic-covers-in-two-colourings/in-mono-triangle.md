# Edge in a monochromatic triangle

For a red/blue colouring $c$ of the edges of $K_n$ (a map from the unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ to $\{0, 1\}$) and vertices $x, y$, the pair $\{x, y\}$ lies in a monochromatic triangle when some vertex $z \ne x, y$ has $c(\{x, z\}) = c(\{y, z\}) = c(\{x, y\})$. The test is literal on all pairs, including $x = y$: with two vertices and a constant colouring, $x = y = 0$ and $z = 1$ pass it although $K_2$ has no triangle. For the reading as an edge lying in a monochromatic triangle, require $x \ne y$.
