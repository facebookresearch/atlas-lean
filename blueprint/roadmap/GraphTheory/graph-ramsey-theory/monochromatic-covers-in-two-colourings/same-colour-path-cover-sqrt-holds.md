# Resolution of Erdős Problem 518 for sufficiently large n

There is a natural number $n_0$ such that for every $n \ge n_0$ and every simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the vertices can be covered by at most $\sqrt{n}$ paths of $G$ or by at most $\sqrt{n}$ paths of its complement: in every red/blue colouring of the edges of $K_n$, at most $\sqrt{n}$ monochromatic paths of one colour cover all vertices. The paths need not be disjoint, and single vertices count as paths. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 518 for sufficiently large n (covering by monochromatic paths of one colour)](same-colour-path-cover-sqrt.md) holds.

Problem 518 asks the question for every $n$; this statement, with a threshold $n_0$, is its form for sufficiently large $n$.
