# Cover of a graph by few paths

For a simple graph $H$ and a real number $m$, the vertices of $H$ are covered by at most $m$ paths of $H$ when there are a natural number $k \le m$ and paths $P_1, \dots, P_k$ of $H$ whose vertices together include every vertex of $H$. The paths need not be disjoint, and a single vertex is a path of length $0$.
