# Erdős Problem 639 (edges in no monochromatic triangle)

The statement that for every natural number $n \ge 7$ and every red/blue colouring of the edges of $K_n$, at most $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor$ edges $\{x, y\}$, $x \ne y$, lie in no monochromatic triangle. Here a colouring is a map from the unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ to $\{0, 1\}$, and $\{x, y\}$ lies in a monochromatic triangle when some vertex $z \ne x, y$ has $c(\{x, z\}) = c(\{y, z\}) = c(\{x, y\})$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 639](uncovered-edges-at-most-quarter-holds.md).
