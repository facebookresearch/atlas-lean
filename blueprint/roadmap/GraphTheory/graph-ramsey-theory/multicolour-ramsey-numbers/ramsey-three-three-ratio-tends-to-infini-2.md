# Resolution of Erdős Problem 553

The ratio $R(3, 3, n) / R(3, n)$ of real numbers tends to $+\infty$ as $n \to \infty$. Here $R(3, 3, n)$ is the least $m$ such that every colouring of the edges of $K_m$ with colours $0, 1, 2$ has a triangle all of whose edges have colour $0$, or one of colour $1$, or $n$ vertices all of whose pairs have colour $2$; and $R(3, n)$ is the classical Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that every simple graph on $N$ vertices contains a triangle or has $n$ pairwise non-adjacent vertices.
