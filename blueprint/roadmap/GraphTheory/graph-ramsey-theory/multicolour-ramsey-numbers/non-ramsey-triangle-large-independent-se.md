# Erdős Problem 925 (independent sets in graphs not Ramsey for the triangle)

The statement that there are real numbers $\delta > 0$ and $c > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every simple graph $G$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ that is the union $G = G_1 \cup G_2$ of two triangle-free graphs on the same vertex set (equivalently, whose edges can be coloured with two colours with no monochromatic triangle) has an independent set of at least $c\, n^{1/3 + \delta}$ vertices. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 925](non-ramsey-triangle-large-independent-se-2.md).
