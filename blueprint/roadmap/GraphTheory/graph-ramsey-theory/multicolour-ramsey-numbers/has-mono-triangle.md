# Monochromatic triangle in an edge colouring

A colouring of the edges of $K_m$ with $k$ colours is a map $c$ from the unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ to $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$; only pairs of distinct vertices matter. The colouring $c$ has a monochromatic triangle of colour $i$ when there are pairwise distinct vertices $x, y, z$ with $c(\{x, y\}) = c(\{y, z\}) = c(\{x, z\}) = i$.
