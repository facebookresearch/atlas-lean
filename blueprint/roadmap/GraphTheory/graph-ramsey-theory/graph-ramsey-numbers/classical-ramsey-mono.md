# Monotonicity of the Ramsey property

If every graph on $N$ vertices contains $K_s$ or has $K_t$ in its complement, then the same holds for every graph on $M \ge N$ vertices: restrict the graph to its first $N$ vertices, find $K_s$ or a complementary $K_t$ there, and include it back. Hence the set of $N$ with the [Ramsey property](classical-ramsey.md) is upward closed, and $R(s, t) > n$ as soon as the property fails on $n$ vertices.
