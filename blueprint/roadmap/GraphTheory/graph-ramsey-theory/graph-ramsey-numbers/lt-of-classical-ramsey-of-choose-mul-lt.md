# Erdős's lower bound for diagonal Ramsey numbers

If $\binom nk 2^{1 - \binom k2} < 1$, then every $N$ such that every graph on $N$ vertices contains $K_k$ or has $K_k$ in its complement satisfies $N > n$; that is, $R(k, k) > n$. Optimising $n$ (with Stirling's formula) gives $R(k,k) > (1/(e\sqrt2) + o(1))\, k\, 2^{k/2}$ (Zhao, Fact 1.5); the notes record that improving the exponential base $\sqrt 2$ is open.
