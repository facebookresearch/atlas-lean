# Upper bound for R(3, t) (Ajtai–Komlós–Szemerédi, Shearer)

For every $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $t$,

$$R(3, t) \le (1 + \varepsilon) \frac{t^2}{\log t}.$$

Ajtai, Komlós and Szemerédi proved $R(3,t) = O(t^2 / \log t)$, removing the $\log \log t$ factor of Graver and Yackel; Shearer reduced the constant to $1 + o(1)$ and simplified the proof.

## Sources

- J. B. Shearer, "A note on the independence number of triangle-free graphs", *Discrete Math.* 46 (1983).
- J. H. Kim, "The Ramsey number R(3,t) has order of magnitude t²/log t", *Random Structures & Algorithms* 7 (1995), 173–207, equation (1), where the bound is stated with these attributions.
