# Monotonicity of the Ramsey property for a pair of graphs

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on arbitrary vertex sets, and let $N \le M$ be natural numbers. Suppose that every simple graph $C$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$ contains a copy of $G$ as a subgraph or has a complement $\overline{C}$ containing a copy of $H$ as a subgraph. Then every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, M-1\}$ has the same property.

Here a copy is an injective map of vertices that sends edges to edges, so the subgraph need not be induced.
