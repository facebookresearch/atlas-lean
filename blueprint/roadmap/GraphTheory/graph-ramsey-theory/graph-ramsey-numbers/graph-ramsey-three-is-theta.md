# R(3, t) has order of magnitude t² / log t

As $t \to \infty$,

$$R(3, t) = \Theta\Big(\frac{t^2}{\log t}\Big),$$

the combination of [Kim's lower bound](graph-ramsey-three-lower.md) and the [upper bound of Ajtai–Komlós–Szemerédi and Shearer](graph-ramsey-three-upper.md). This settled the order of magnitude of $R(3,t)$, open since Erdős's 1961 bound; Kim received the 1997 Fulkerson Prize for it.
