# Graph with a given set of pairs as edges

For a set $\omega$ of [pairs of vertices](pair.md), the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct $i$ and $j$ are adjacent exactly when $\{i, j\} \in \omega$. Read as a red–blue colouring of the edges of $K_n$, its edges are the red ones and the edges of its complement the blue ones. With $\omega$ the [random subset of pairs](../../../Combinatorics/probabilistic-combinatorics/janson-s-inequalities/random-subset-law.md) with $p = 1/2$ it is the uniformly random graph $G(n, 1/2)$, and with constant $p$ the binomial random graph $G(n, p)$.
