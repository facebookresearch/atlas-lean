# Symmetry of the Ramsey number of a pair of graphs

For finite simple graphs $G$ and $H$, the Ramsey numbers $R(G, H)$ and $R(H, G)$ are equal, where $R(G, H)$ is the least $n$ such that every simple graph on $n$ vertices contains a copy of $G$ as a subgraph or has a complement containing a copy of $H$ as a subgraph, and $0$ if there is no such $n$.
