# Erdős Problem 549 (trees with classes of sizes $k$ and $2k$)

The statement that for every natural number $k$, every tree $T$ whose vertex set is the disjoint union of a $k$-element set $A$ and a $2k$-element set $B$, with no edge inside $A$ and no edge inside $B$, satisfies $R(T) = 4k - 1$. Here, for a finite simple graph $F$, $R(F)$ is its diagonal Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that for every simple graph $H$ on $N$ vertices, $H$ or its complement contains a copy of $F$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced, and $0$ if there is no such $N$. For $k = 0$ there is no such tree, since a tree has a vertex. Stated here as a proposition.
