# Resolution of Erdős Problem 163

For every natural number $d \ge 1$ there is a real constant $C$ such that for every natural number $n$, every $d$-degenerate simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ (every nonempty set $W$ of vertices contains a vertex with at most $d$ neighbours in $W$) satisfies $R(H) \le C n$. Here, for a finite simple graph $F$, $R(F)$ is its diagonal Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that for every simple graph $H$ on $N$ vertices, $H$ or its complement contains a copy of $F$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced, and $0$ if there is no such $N$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 163 (Burr–Erdős conjecture on degenerate graphs)](degenerate-graphs-linear-ramsey.md) holds.
