# Erdős Problem 547 (Ramsey numbers of trees)

The statement that for every natural number $n \ge 2$, every tree $T$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ satisfies $R(T) \le 2n - 2$. Here, for a finite simple graph $F$, $R(F)$ is its diagonal Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that for every simple graph $H$ on $N$ vertices, $H$ or its complement contains a copy of $F$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced, and $0$ if there is no such $N$. Stated here as a proposition.
