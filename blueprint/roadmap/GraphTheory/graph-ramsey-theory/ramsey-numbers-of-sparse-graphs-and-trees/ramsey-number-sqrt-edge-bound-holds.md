# Resolution of Erdős Problem 546

There is a real constant $C$ such that every simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with no isolated vertices and $m$ edges satisfies $R(G) \le 2^{C\sqrt{m}}$. Here, for a finite simple graph $F$, $R(F)$ is its diagonal Ramsey number, the least $N$ such that for every simple graph $H$ on $N$ vertices, $H$ or its complement contains a copy of $F$ as a subgraph, not necessarily induced, and $0$ if there is no such $N$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 546 (Ramsey numbers of graphs with $m$ edges)](ramsey-number-sqrt-edge-bound.md) holds.
