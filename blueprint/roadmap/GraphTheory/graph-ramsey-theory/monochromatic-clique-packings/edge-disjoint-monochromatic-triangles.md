# Erdős Problem 76 (edge-disjoint monochromatic triangles)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every red/blue colouring of the edges of $K_n$ (red graph $R$ on the vertex set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, blue graph its complement) has a family $T$ of at least $(1 - \varepsilon) n^2/12$ monochromatic triangles, each a set of three vertices whose three pairs are all red or all blue, any two of which share at most one vertex (so they are edge-disjoint). Stated here as a proposition.
