# 1-tree within an edge set

For a vertex $v_0$ and finite sets $A$ and $T$ of unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $T$ is a 1-tree with special vertex $v_0$ within $A$ when $T \subseteq A$, the edges of $T$ not containing $v_0$ form a tree on the vertices other than $v_0$, and exactly two members of $T$ contain $v_0$.
