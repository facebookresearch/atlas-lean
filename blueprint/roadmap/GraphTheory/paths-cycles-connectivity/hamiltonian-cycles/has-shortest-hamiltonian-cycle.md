# Graph containing a shortest Hamiltonian cycle

Let $K_n$ be the complete graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, whose edges are the unordered pairs of distinct vertices, and let $w$ assign a real weight to every unordered pair. A simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ contains a shortest Hamiltonian cycle of $K_n$ when it has a Hamiltonian cycle whose weight is at most the weight of every Hamiltonian cycle of $K_n$.
