# Successive shortest members of a class of edge sets

Let $K_n$ be the complete graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, whose edges are the unordered pairs of distinct vertices, and let $w$ assign a real weight to every unordered pair. For a class $P$ of edge sets relative to an allowed set (such as spanning trees or 1-trees within it), sets $T_0, \dots, T_{k-1}$ are successive shortest members when for each $i$, $T_i$ is a member of $P$ within the edges of $K_n$ not in $T_0, \dots, T_{i-1}$, and its total weight is at most that of every member of $P$ within those edges.
