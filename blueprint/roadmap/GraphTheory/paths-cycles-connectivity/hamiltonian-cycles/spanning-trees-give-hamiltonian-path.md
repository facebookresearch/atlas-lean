# Goldengorin's question 1 (Hamiltonian paths from shortest spanning trees)

The statement that there is a natural number $k$ such that for every $n$ and every assignment $w$ of positive real weights to the unordered pairs of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, whenever $T_0, \dots, T_{k-1}$ are successive shortest spanning trees of the complete graph $K_n$ (each $T_i$ a spanning tree of $K_n$ avoiding the edges of $T_0, \dots, T_{i-1}$, of total weight at most that of every such spanning tree, chosen arbitrarily among the lightest), the graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ whose edges are those of $T_0, \dots, T_{k-1}$ has a Hamiltonian path. Stated here as a proposition.
