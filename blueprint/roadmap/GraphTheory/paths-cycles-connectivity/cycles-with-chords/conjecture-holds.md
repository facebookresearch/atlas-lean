# Resolution of Erdős Problem 1091

Every $K_4$-free simple graph on a finite vertex set with chromatic number $4$ (properly $4$-colourable but not $3$-colourable) has an odd cycle with at least two diagonals, where a graph has an odd cycle with two diagonals when for some odd $m \ge 3$ there are distinct vertices $v_i$, indexed by $i \in \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$, with $v_i$ adjacent to $v_{i+1}$ for every $i$, and two different unordered pairs $\{a, b\}$ and $\{c, d\}$ of distinct, non-consecutive indices with $v_a v_b$ and $v_c v_d$ edges. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 1091 (odd cycles with two diagonals)](conjecture.md) holds.
