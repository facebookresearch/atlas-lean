# Erdős Problem 916 (a cycle and a vertex adjacent to three of its vertices)

The statement that for every natural number $n \ge 2$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $2n - 2$ edges contains a cycle $C$ and a vertex not on $C$ adjacent to at least three distinct vertices of $C$. Stated here as a proposition.
