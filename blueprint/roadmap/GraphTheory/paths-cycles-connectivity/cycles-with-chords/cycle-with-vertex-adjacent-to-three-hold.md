# Resolution of Erdős Problem 916

For every natural number $n \ge 2$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with exactly $2n - 2$ edges contains a cycle $C$ and a vertex not on $C$ adjacent to at least three distinct vertices of $C$.
