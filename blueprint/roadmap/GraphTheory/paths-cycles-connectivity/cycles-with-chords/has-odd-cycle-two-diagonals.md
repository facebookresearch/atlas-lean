# Odd cycle with two diagonals

A simple graph has an odd cycle with two diagonals when for some odd $m \ge 3$ there are distinct vertices $v_i$, $i \in \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$, with $v_i$ adjacent to $v_{i+1}$ for every $i$, together with two different unordered pairs $\{a, b\}$ and $\{c, d\}$ of indices, each of two distinct non-consecutive indices ($b \ne a \pm 1$, $d \ne c \pm 1$), such that $v_a v_b$ and $v_c v_d$ are edges.
