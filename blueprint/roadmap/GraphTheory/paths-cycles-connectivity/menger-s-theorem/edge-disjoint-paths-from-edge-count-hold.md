# Resolution of Erdős Problem 915, edge-disjoint reading

For all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and $n \ge 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n(m-1)\}$ (that is, with $1 + n(m-1)$ vertices) with exactly $1 + n\binom{m}{2}$ edges has two distinct vertices $u, v$ joined by $m$ paths, no two of which share an edge.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #915, https://www.erdosproblems.com/915.
- W. Mader, Ein Extremalproblem des Zusammenhangs von Graphen, Math. Z. (1973), 223–231.
