# Erdős Problem 915, internally disjoint reading

The statement that for all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and $n \ge 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n(m-1)\}$ (that is, with $1 + n(m-1)$ vertices) with exactly $1 + n\binom{m}{2}$ edges has two distinct vertices $u, v$ joined by $m$ distinct paths, no two of which share a vertex other than $u$ and $v$. Stated here as a proposition.
