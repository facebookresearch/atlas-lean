# Menger's theorem for infinite graphs (Aharoni–Berger)

For every simple graph $G$, possibly infinite, and all disjoint independent sets $A$ and $B$ of vertices, there are a family of pairwise vertex-disjoint paths, each from a vertex of $A$ to a vertex of $B$, and a set $S$ of vertices lying on these paths and containing exactly one vertex of each of them, such that every path from a vertex of $A$ to a vertex of $B$ contains a vertex of $S$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 599 (the Erdős–Menger conjecture)](erdos-menger-conjecture.md) holds.
