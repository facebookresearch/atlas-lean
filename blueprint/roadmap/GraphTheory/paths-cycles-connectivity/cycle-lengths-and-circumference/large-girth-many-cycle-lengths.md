# Erdős Problem 752 (cycle lengths in graphs of large girth)

The statement that for every natural number $s$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that every simple graph $G$ on a finite nonempty vertex set with minimum degree at least $k \ge 2$ and with no cycle of length at most $2s$ has at least $c\, k^s$ distinct cycle lengths. Stated here as a proposition.
