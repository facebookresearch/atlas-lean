# Resolution of Erdős Problem 752

For every natural number $s$ there is a real $c > 0$ such that every simple graph $G$ on a finite nonempty vertex set with minimum degree at least $k \ge 2$ and with no cycle of length at most $2s$ has at least $c\, k^s$ distinct cycle lengths.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #752, https://www.erdosproblems.com/752.
- B. Sudakov and J. Verstraëte, Cycle lengths in sparse graphs, Combinatorica 28 (2008), 357–372, arXiv:0707.2117, https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2117.
