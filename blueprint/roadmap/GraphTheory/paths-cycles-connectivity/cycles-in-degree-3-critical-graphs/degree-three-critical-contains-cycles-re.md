# Resolution of Erdős Problem 815

It is not the case that for every natural number $k \ge 3$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every degree $3$-critical simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ contains a cycle of length $k$. Here a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is degree $3$-critical when it has exactly $2n - 2$ edges (read as $0$ for $n = 0$) and every nonempty proper set $S$ of vertices contains a vertex with at most two neighbours in $S$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 815 (cycles in degree 3-critical graphs)](degree-three-critical-contains-cycles.md) is false.
