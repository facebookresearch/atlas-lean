# Weighted cactus graphs with tree edges and cycle edges

A *cactus* is a connected multigraph $H$ in which every edge lies on at most one cycle (cycles of length at least $2$; a $2$-cycle is a pair of parallel edges). An edge on no cycle is a *tree edge* and an edge on a cycle is a *cycle edge*. For a parameter $\lambda > 0$, the *$\lambda$-weighting* of $H$ assigns weight $\lambda$ to every tree edge and weight $\lambda/2$ to every cycle edge.

## Sources

- Efim A. Dinits, Alexander V. Karzanov, Michael V. Lomonosov, "On the Structure of a System of Minimum Edge Cuts of a Graph", Studies in Discrete Optimization (1976).
