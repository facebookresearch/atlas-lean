# Crossing pairs of vertex bipartitions

Let $V$ be a finite set. Two subsets $S, T \subseteq V$ (identified with the bipartitions $\{S, V \setminus S\}$ and $\{T, V \setminus T\}$) *cross* if all four *corners*

$$
S \cap T, \quad S \setminus T, \quad T \setminus S, \quad V \setminus (S \cup T)
$$

are nonempty. A family of subsets is *crossing-free* (laminar up to complementation) if no two of its members cross. For a graph $G = (V, E, w)$ and disjoint $A, B \subseteq V$ write $w(A, B)$ for the total weight of edges with one endpoint in $A$ and the other in $B$.

## Sources

- Efim A. Dinits, Alexander V. Karzanov, Michael V. Lomonosov, "On the Structure of a System of Minimum Edge Cuts of a Graph", Studies in Discrete Optimization (1976).
