# Erdős Problem 816 for $n \ge 600$

The statement that for every natural number $n \ge 600$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, 2n\}$ (with $2n + 1$ vertices) with at least $n^2 + n + 1$ edges has two distinct vertices of the same degree joined by a path of length $3$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 816 for $n \ge 600$](equal-degree-path-three-holds.md).
