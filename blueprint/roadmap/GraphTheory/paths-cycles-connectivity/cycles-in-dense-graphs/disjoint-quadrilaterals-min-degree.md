# Erdős Problem 577 (vertex-disjoint quadrilaterals)

The statement that for every natural number $k$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, 4k-1\}$ in which every vertex has at least $2k$ neighbours contains $k$ vertex-disjoint cycles of length $4$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 577](disjoint-quadrilaterals-min-degree-holds.md).
