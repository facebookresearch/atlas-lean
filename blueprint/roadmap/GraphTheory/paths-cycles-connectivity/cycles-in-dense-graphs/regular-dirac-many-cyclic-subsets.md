# Erdős Problem 622 (cyclic vertex sets in regular Dirac graphs)

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, 2n-1\}$ in which every vertex has exactly $n + 1$ neighbours has at least $c\, 2^{2n}$ cyclic vertex sets, sets of vertices that are exactly the vertex set of some cycle. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 622](regular-dirac-many-cyclic-subsets-holds.md).
