# Resolution of Erdős Problem 577

For every natural number $k$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, 4k-1\}$ in which every vertex has at least $2k$ neighbours contains $k$ vertex-disjoint cycles of length $4$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 577 (vertex-disjoint quadrilaterals)](disjoint-quadrilaterals-min-degree.md) holds.
