# Resolution of Erdős Problem 622

There is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, 2n-1\}$ in which every vertex has exactly $n + 1$ neighbours has at least $c\, 2^{2n}$ cyclic vertex sets, sets of vertices that are exactly the vertex set of some cycle. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 622 (cyclic vertex sets in regular Dirac graphs)](regular-dirac-many-cyclic-subsets.md) holds.
