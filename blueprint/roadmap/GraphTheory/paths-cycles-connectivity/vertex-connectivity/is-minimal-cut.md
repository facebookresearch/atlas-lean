# Minimal vertex cut

A set $T$ of vertices of a simple graph $G$ is a minimal cut when the subgraph induced on the complement of $T$ is not connected (it has two vertices joined by no path; a graph with at most one vertex counts as connected) while for every proper subset $S \subsetneq T$ the subgraph induced on the complement of $S$ is connected. A disconnected graph has the empty set as its only minimal cut.
