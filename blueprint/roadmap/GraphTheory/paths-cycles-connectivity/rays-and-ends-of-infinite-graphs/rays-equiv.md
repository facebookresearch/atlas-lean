# Equivalent rays

Two sequences $r$ and $s$ of vertices of a simple graph $G$ are equivalent when for every finite set $S$ of vertices there are $m$ and $n$ such that $r(i) \notin S$ for $i \ge m$, $s(j) \notin S$ for $j \ge n$, and $r(m)$ and $s(n)$ are joined by a path avoiding $S$: no finite vertex set separates their tails.
