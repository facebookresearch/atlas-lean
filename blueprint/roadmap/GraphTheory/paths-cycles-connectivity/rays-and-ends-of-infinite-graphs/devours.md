# Set of rays devouring an end

For a simple graph $G$, an arbitrary sequence $r_0$ of vertices and a set $R$ of vertex sequences, $R$ devours $r_0$ when every ray $s$ of $G$ [equivalent](rays-equiv.md) to $r_0$ meets some member of $R$: they share a vertex. The sequence $r_0$ need not be a ray; when it is, this says that $R$ devours the end of $r_0$, every ray of that end meeting some member of $R$.
