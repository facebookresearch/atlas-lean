# Local clustering coefficient

For a finite simple graph and a vertex $v$ of degree $d_v$, the local clustering coefficient is $C(v) = |\{\{x, y\} \subseteq N(v) : xy \in E\}|/\binom{d_v}{2}$ when $d_v \ge 2$, and $C(v) = 0$ by convention when $d_v \le 1$. Leaving $C(v)$ undefined for $d_v \le 1$ is another convention and must be stated when used.
