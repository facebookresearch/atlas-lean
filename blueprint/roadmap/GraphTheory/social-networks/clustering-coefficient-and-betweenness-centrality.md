# Clustering coefficient and betweenness centrality

For a finite simple graph and a vertex $v$ of degree $d_v$, the local clustering coefficient is $C(v) = |\{\{x, y\} \subseteq N(v) : xy \in E\}|/\binom{d_v}{2}$ when $d_v \ge 2$, and $C(v) = 0$ by convention when $d_v \le 1$. The betweenness of $v$ is $B(v) = \sum \sigma_{st}(v)/\sigma_{st}$, summed over unordered pairs $\{s, t\}$ of distinct vertices other than $v$ with $\sigma_{st} > 0$, where $\sigma_{st}$ is the number of shortest $s$–$t$ paths and $\sigma_{st}(v)$ the number passing through $v$; pairs in different components contribute $0$. Other conventions (ordered pairs, which double $B$; normalization by $\binom{n-1}{2}$; leaving $C(v)$ undefined for $d_v \le 1$) give different numbers and must be stated when used.
