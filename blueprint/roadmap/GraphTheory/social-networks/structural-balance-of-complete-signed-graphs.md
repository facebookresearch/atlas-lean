# Structural balance of complete signed graphs

Let $K_n$ ($n \ge 1$) have each edge labeled $+$ or $-$. If every triangle has an even number of $-$ edges, then the vertices split into at most two groups with all $+$ edges inside the groups and all $-$ edges between them: fix a vertex $v$, put $v$ and its $+$-neighbours in one group and its $-$-neighbours in the other, and check each edge $uw$ with the triangle $vuw$. Conversely such a split makes every triangle balanced. For $n \le 2$ there are no triangles and the conclusion holds directly. For signed graphs that are not complete, the corresponding criterion involves all cycles, not only triangles, and is a separate statement.
