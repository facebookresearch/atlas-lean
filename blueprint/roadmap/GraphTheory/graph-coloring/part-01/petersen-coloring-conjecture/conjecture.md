# Petersen coloring conjecture

For every finite set $V$ and every simple graph $G$ on $V$, if every vertex of $G$ has exactly three neighbours and $G$ is bridgeless, then $G$ admits a [Petersen colouring](is-petersen-coloring.md): a map $f$ from the edges of $G$ to the edges of the [Petersen graph](petersen-graph.md) such that for every vertex $v$ of $G$ there is a vertex $w$ of the Petersen graph onto whose three edges $f$ maps the three edges at $v$ bijectively.
