# Perfect graphs have large pure pairs

For every real $c > 0$ there is a real $\varepsilon > 0$ such that every perfect simple graph $G$ on a finite vertex set with $n > 1$ vertices has disjoint vertex sets $A$ and $B$ with $|A|, |B| \ge \varepsilon\, n^{1 - c}$ such that every vertex of $A$ is adjacent to every vertex of $B$, or no vertex of $A$ is adjacent to any vertex of $B$ (a complete bipartite subgraph of $G$ or of its complement). Here $G$ is perfect when every induced subgraph has chromatic number equal to its clique number. That is, the proposition [Fox's conjecture on pure pairs in perfect graphs](perfect-graphs-have-large-pure-pairs.md) holds.
