# Edge-critical graphs of given chromatic number

A simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is $k$-critical when its chromatic number is $k$ and every subgraph of $G$ other than $G$ itself, viewed as a graph on its own vertex set, has chromatic number less than $k$. This is the subgraph (edge) notion of criticality, stronger than the vertex-critical notion in which only deleting a vertex must lower the chromatic number.
