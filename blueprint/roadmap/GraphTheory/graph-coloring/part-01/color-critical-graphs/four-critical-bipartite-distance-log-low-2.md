# Resolution of Erdős Problem 744 for 4-critical graphs

It is not the case that there is a real $C > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, every $4$-critical simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ needs at least $C \log n$ edge deletions to become bipartite (natural logarithm): every $2$-colouring of its vertices has at least $C \log n$ edges inside its colour classes. Here a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is $4$-critical when its chromatic number is $4$ and every subgraph of $G$ other than $G$ itself, viewed as a graph on its own vertex set, has chromatic number less than $4$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 744 for 4-critical graphs](four-critical-bipartite-distance-log-low.md) is false.
