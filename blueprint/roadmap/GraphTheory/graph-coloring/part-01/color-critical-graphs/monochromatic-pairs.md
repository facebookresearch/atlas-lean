# Monochromatic adjacent pairs of a 2-colouring

For a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and for a $2$-colouring $c$ of the vertices, the number of ordered pairs $(u, v)$ of distinct adjacent vertices with $c(u) = c(v)$, twice the number of edges inside the colour classes; $G$ becomes bipartite after deleting $m$ edges exactly when some $c$ makes this at most $2m$.
