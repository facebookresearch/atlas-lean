# Erdős Problem 641 (edge-disjoint cycles on the same vertices)

The statement that there is a function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every $k \ge 1$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, with chromatic number at least $f(k)$ has $k$ pairwise edge-disjoint cycles all having the same vertex set. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 641](chromatic-forces-common-vertex-cycles-re.md).
