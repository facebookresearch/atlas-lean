# Seymour's $r$-graph conjecture

The statement that for every natural number $r \ge 1$, every $r$-graph has a proper edge colouring with $r + 1$ colours (chromatic index at most $r + 1$). Here an $r$-graph is a finite multigraph given by finite sets $V$ of vertices and $E$ of edges, each edge $e$ joining $s(e)$ and $t(e)$; parallel edges are allowed, such that it has no loops ($s(e) \ne t(e)$), every vertex has degree $r$ (counting endpoint incidences), and for every set $X$ of vertices of odd size at least $r$ edges have exactly one end in $X$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Every $r$-graph has chromatic index at most $r + 1$](r-graph-chromatic-index-le-succ-holds.md).
