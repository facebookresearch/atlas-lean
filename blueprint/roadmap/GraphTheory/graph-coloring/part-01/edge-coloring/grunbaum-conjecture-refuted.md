# Grünbaum's conjecture is false

It is not the case that for every [simple loopless triangulation](is-simple-triangulation.md) of a closed orientable surface, given as an oriented combinatorial map $(\alpha, \sigma)$ on a finite set $D$ of darts (vertices are the $\sigma$-orbits, edges the pairs $\{d, \alpha(d)\}$ and faces the cycles of $\sigma\alpha$), the dual is $3$-edge-colourable: some colouring of the edges with three colours gives the three edges around every face distinct colours. Equivalently, some simple loopless triangulation of a closed orientable surface has a dual that is not $3$-edge-colourable. That is, the proposition [Grünbaum's conjecture](grunbaum-conjecture.md) is false.
