# 3-edge-colourable dual of an oriented map

For permutations $\alpha$ and $\sigma$ of a set $D$ of darts, as in an oriented combinatorial map with edges $\{d, \alpha(d)\}$ and faces the cycles of $\sigma\alpha$: the dual of such a map is $3$-edge-colourable when there is a map $c : D \to \{0, 1, 2\}$ with $c(\alpha(d)) = c(d)$ for every dart $d$ (a colouring of the edges) and $c(\sigma(\alpha(d))) \ne c(d)$ for every dart $d$, so that the three edges around every face have distinct colours. A face containing both darts of one edge (a loop of the dual) admits no such colouring.
