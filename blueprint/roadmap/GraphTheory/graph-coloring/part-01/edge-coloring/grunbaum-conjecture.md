# Grünbaum's conjecture

The statement that for every [simple loopless triangulation](is-simple-triangulation.md) of a closed orientable surface, given as an oriented combinatorial map $(\alpha, \sigma)$ on a finite set $D$ of darts (vertices are the $\sigma$-orbits, edges the pairs $\{d, \alpha(d)\}$ and faces the cycles of $\sigma\alpha$), the dual is $3$-edge-colourable: some colouring of the edges with three colours gives the three edges around every face distinct colours. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Grünbaum's conjecture is false](grunbaum-conjecture-refuted.md).
