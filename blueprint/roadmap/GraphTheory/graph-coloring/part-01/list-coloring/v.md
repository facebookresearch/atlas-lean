# Vertices of Noel's graph

The vertex set of Noel's graph is the fourteen-element set consisting of two terminals $s_1, s_2$ and, for each $i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, three vertices $u_i, v_i, p_i$, the vertices of the $i$-th triangle, of which $p_i$ is the private one. Index $i$ corresponds to the triangle $T_{i+1}$ of the source.

## Sources

- J. A. Noel, *The Partial List Colouring Conjecture is False*, arXiv:2609.23291, Theorem 1.1.
