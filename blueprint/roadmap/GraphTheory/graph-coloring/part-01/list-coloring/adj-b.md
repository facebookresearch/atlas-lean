# Adjacency function of Noel's graph

On the fourteen vertices $s_1, s_2$ and $u_i, v_i, p_i$ ($i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) of Noel's graph, the Boolean adjacency function is true on exactly these ordered pairs: $(s_k, u_i)$, $(s_k, v_i)$, $(u_i, s_k)$, $(v_i, s_k)$ for $k = 1, 2$ and every $i$; and, for each $i$, the ordered pairs of distinct vertices of $\{u_i, v_i, p_i\}$. It is false on every other pair, in particular on $(s_1, s_2)$, on every pair with equal entries, on every pair joining a terminal to some $p_i$, and on every pair of vertices from different triangles.
