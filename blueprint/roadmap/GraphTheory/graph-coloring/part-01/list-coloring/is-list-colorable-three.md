# Noel's graph is 3-choosable

Noel's graph $N$ has fourteen vertices: two terminals $s_1, s_2$ and, for each $i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, vertices $u_i, v_i, p_i$. Its edges are those of the four triangles $\{u_i, v_i, p_i\}$ and the edges from each terminal to every $u_i$ and every $v_i$; there are no other edges, so $s_1$ and $s_2$ are not adjacent and each $p_i$ is adjacent only to $u_i$ and $v_i$. A simple graph is $q$-list-colourable when, for every type of colours and every assignment of a finite list of at least $q$ colours to each vertex, it has a proper colouring giving each vertex a colour from its list. Then $N$ is $3$-list-colourable.

## Sources

- J. A. Noel, *The Partial List Colouring Conjecture is False*, arXiv:2609.23291, Theorem 1.1.
