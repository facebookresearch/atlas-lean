# Hedetniemi's conjecture

The statement that $\chi(G \times H) = \min(\chi(G), \chi(H))$ for all simple graphs $G$ and $H$ on finite vertex sets, where $G \times H$ is the tensor product (adjacent when adjacent in both coordinates) and $\chi$ is the chromatic number. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Hedetniemi's conjecture is false](hedetniemi-conjecture-refuted.md).
