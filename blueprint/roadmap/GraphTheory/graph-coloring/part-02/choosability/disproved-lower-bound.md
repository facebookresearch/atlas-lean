# Negation of the lower bound in Erdős Problem 753

The statement that it is not the case that there is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 1$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ satisfies $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G}) > n^{1/2 + c}$, where $\chi_L$ is the list chromatic number and $\overline{G}$ the complement of $G$; equivalently, for every real $c > 0$ some $n \ge 1$ and some simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ have $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G}) \le n^{1/2 + c}$.
