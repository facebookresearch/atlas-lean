# Alon's upper bound for $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G})$, as a statement

The statement that there is a real $C > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 2$ some simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ satisfies $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G}) \le C\,(n \log n)^{1/2}$ (natural logarithm), where $\chi_L$ is the list chromatic number and $\overline{G}$ the complement of $G$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Alon's upper bound for the list chromatic numbers of a graph and its complement](alon-upper-bound-holds.md).
