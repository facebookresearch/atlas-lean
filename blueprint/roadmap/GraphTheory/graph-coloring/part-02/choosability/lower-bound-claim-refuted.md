# Resolution of Erdős Problem 753

It is not the case that there is a real $c > 0$ such that for every natural number $n \ge 1$, every simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ satisfies $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G}) > n^{1/2 + c}$, where $\chi_L$ is the list chromatic number and $\overline{G}$ the complement of $G$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 753 (lower bound for $\chi_L(G) + \chi_L(\overline{G})$)](lower-bound-claim.md) is false.
