# Erdős Problem 630 (list colouring planar bipartite graphs)

The statement that every planar bipartite simple graph on a finite vertex set is $3$-list-colourable, planar meaning that it has a straight-line drawing in the plane with distinct points for distinct vertices and no two edges meeting except at common endpoints, which for finite graphs is equivalent to planarity (Fáry's theorem), and a graph is $q$-list-colourable when for every type of colours and every assignment of lists of at least $q$ colours to its vertices it has a proper colouring from the lists. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Planar bipartite graphs are 3-choosable](planar-bipartite-three-choosable-holds.md).
