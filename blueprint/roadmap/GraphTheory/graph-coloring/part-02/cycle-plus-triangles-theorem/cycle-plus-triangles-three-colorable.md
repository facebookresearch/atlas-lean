# Erdős Problem 842 (cycle plus triangles)

The statement that for every natural number $n$ and all simple graphs $T$ and $C$ on a common vertex set $V$ with $3n$ elements, if $T$ is the disjoint union of $n$ triangles (for some bijection of $V$ with $\{0, \dots, n-1\} \times \{0, 1, 2\}$, two vertices are adjacent in $T$ exactly when they are distinct with the same first coordinate), $C$ is a Hamiltonian cycle (the image of the cycle on $\{0, \dots, 3n-1\}$ under a bijection onto $V$), and $T$ and $C$ have no common edge, then the union $T \cup C$ is properly $3$-colourable.
