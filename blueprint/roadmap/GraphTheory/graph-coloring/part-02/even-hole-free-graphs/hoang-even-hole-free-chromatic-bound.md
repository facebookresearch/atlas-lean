# Hoàng's conjecture on even-hole-free graphs

The statement that every simple graph $G$ on a finite nonempty vertex set that is even-hole-free, even-hole-free meaning that for no $k \ge 2$ does it contain the cycle of length $2k$ as an induced subgraph, satisfies $\chi(G) \le 2\omega(G) - 1$, where $\chi$ is the chromatic number and $\omega$ the clique number. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Even-hole-free graphs satisfy $\chi \le 2\omega - 1$](hoang-even-hole-free-chromatic-bound-hol.md).
