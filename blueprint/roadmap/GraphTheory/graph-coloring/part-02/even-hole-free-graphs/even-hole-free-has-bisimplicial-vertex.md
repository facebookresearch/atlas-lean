# Hayward–Reed conjecture on bisimplicial vertices

The statement that every simple graph $G$ on a finite nonempty vertex set that is even-hole-free, even-hole-free meaning that for no $k \ge 2$ does it contain the cycle of length $2k$ as an induced subgraph, has a vertex $v$ whose neighbourhood is the union of two disjoint cliques $C_1, C_2$ of $G$ (either may be empty). Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Even-hole-free graphs have bisimplicial vertices](even-hole-free-has-bisimplicial-vertex-h.md).
