# Resolution of Erdős Problem 737

Every simple graph $G$ that has no proper colouring with countably many colours (no proper colouring by the natural numbers) but has a proper colouring by a set of cardinality $\aleph_1$ has an edge $uv$ and a natural number $N$ such that for every $n \ge N$ some cycle of length $n$ in $G$ passes through the edge $uv$.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #737, https://www.erdosproblems.com/737.
- C. Thomassen, Cycles in graphs of uncountable chromatic number, Combinatorica 3 (1983), 133–134.
