# Erdős Problem 737 (an edge on cycles of all large lengths)

The statement that every simple graph $G$ that has no proper colouring with countably many colours (no proper colouring by the natural numbers) but has a proper colouring by a set of cardinality $\aleph_1$ has an edge $uv$ and a natural number $N$ such that for every $n \ge N$ some cycle of length $n$ in $G$ passes through the edge $uv$. Stated here as a proposition.
