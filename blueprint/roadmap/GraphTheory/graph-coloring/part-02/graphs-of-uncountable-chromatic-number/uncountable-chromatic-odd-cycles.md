# Erdős Problem 594 (odd cycles in uncountably chromatic graphs)

The statement that every simple graph with no proper colouring by countably many colours (no proper colouring by the natural numbers) has a natural number $N$ such that for every $k \ge N$ it contains a cycle of length $2k + 1$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 594](uncountable-chromatic-odd-cycles-holds.md).
