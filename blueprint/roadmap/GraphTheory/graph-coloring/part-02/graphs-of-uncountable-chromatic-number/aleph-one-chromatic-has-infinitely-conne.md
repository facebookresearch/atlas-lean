# Erdős Problem 1067 (infinitely connected subgraphs)

The statement that every simple graph $G$ with chromatic cardinal $\aleph_1$ (the least cardinality of a set of colours of a proper colouring) has a set $W$ of vertices whose induced subgraph $G[W]$ has chromatic cardinal $\aleph_1$ and is infinitely connected: any two distinct vertices of $G[W]$ are joined in $G[W]$ by infinitely many distinct paths, any two of which share only their ends. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 1067](aleph-one-chromatic-has-infinitely-conne-2.md).
