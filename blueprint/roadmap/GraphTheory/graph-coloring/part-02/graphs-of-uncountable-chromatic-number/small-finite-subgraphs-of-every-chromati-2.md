# Resolution of Erdős Problem 110

It is not the case that there is a function $F : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that every simple graph $G$ with chromatic cardinal $\aleph_1$ (the least cardinality of a set of colours of a proper colouring) has, for all sufficiently large $n$, a finite set $W$ of at most $F(n)$ vertices whose induced subgraph has chromatic number exactly $n$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 110 (small finite subgraphs of large chromatic number)](small-finite-subgraphs-of-every-chromati.md) is false.
