# Sparsity constants forcing property B are below two, as a statement

The statement that for every natural number $t$ and real number $c$, if the sparsity condition $(t, c)$ forces property B then $c < 2$, where the sparsity condition $(t, c)$ forces property B when every finite family of finite sets of natural numbers, each of size at least $t$, in which every nonempty finite set $X$ contains fewer than $c|X|$ members of the family, has a $2$-colouring of the natural numbers with no monochromatic member. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Sparsity constants forcing property B are below two](constant-below-two-holds.md).
