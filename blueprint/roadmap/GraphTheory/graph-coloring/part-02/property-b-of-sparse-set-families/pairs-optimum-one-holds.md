# The optimal sparsity constant for pairs is one

$1$ is the greatest real number $c$ for which the sparsity condition $(2, c)$ forces property B: it forces property B for $c = 1$, and for no $c > 1$, where the sparsity condition $(t, c)$ forces property B when every finite family of finite sets of natural numbers, each of size at least $t$, in which every nonempty finite set $X$ contains fewer than $c|X|$ members of the family, has a $2$-colouring of the natural numbers with no monochromatic member. That is, the proposition [The optimal sparsity constant for pairs, as a statement](pairs-optimum-one.md) holds.
