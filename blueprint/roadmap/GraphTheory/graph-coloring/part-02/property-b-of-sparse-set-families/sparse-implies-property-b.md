# Sparsity forcing property B

For a natural number $t$ and a real number $c$, the sparsity condition $(t, c)$ forces property B when every finite family $F$ of finite sets of natural numbers, each of size at least $t$, such that every nonempty finite set $X$ of natural numbers contains fewer than $c\,|X|$ members of $F$, has property B: some colouring of the natural numbers with two colours leaves no member of $F$ monochromatic (every member contains elements of both colours).
