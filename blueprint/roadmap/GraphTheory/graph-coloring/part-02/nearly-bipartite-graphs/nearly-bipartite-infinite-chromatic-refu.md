# Resolution of Erdős Problem 74

It is not the case that for every function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $f(n) \to \infty$ there is a simple graph $G$ with infinite chromatic number (no proper colouring with finitely many colours) such that for every finite set $S$ of vertices, the induced subgraph $G[S]$ can be made bipartite by deleting at most $f(|S|)$ edges: some $2$-colouring of the vertices leaves at most $f(|S|)$ edges of $G[S]$ inside its colour classes.
