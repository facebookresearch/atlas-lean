# The maximum cochromatic number on twelve vertices is four

$z(12) = 4$, where $z(n)$ is the largest cochromatic number $\zeta(G)$ of a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, where $\zeta(G)$ is the least $k$ such that the vertices of $G$ can be coloured with $k$ colours so that every colour class induces a complete graph or a graph with no edges.
