# Maximum cochromatic number of graphs on n vertices

For a natural number $n$, $z(n)$ is the largest cochromatic number $\zeta(G)$ of a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, where $\zeta(G)$ is the least $k$ such that the vertices of $G$ can be coloured with $k$ colours so that every colour class induces a complete graph or a graph with no edges.
