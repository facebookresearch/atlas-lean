# Erdős Problem 797 (acyclic chromatic number and maximum degree)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large natural numbers $d$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, with maximum degree at most $d$ has an acyclic colouring with $\lfloor \varepsilon d^2 \rfloor$ colours, a proper colouring in which no cycle uses only two colours; that is, the largest acyclic chromatic number of a graph of maximum degree $d$ is $o(d^2)$. Stated here as a proposition; its resolution: [Resolution of Erdős Problem 797](acyclic-chromatic-subquadratic-holds.md).
