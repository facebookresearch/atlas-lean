# Resolution of Erdős Problem 797

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large natural numbers $d$, every simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, for every $n$, with maximum degree at most $d$ has an acyclic colouring with $\lfloor \varepsilon d^2 \rfloor$ colours, a proper colouring in which no cycle uses only two colours; that is, the largest acyclic chromatic number of a graph of maximum degree $d$ is $o(d^2)$. That is, the proposition [Erdős Problem 797 (acyclic chromatic number and maximum degree)](acyclic-chromatic-subquadratic.md) holds.
