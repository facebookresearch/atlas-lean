# Graph Theory

Graph theory studies vertices and edges through connectivity, coloring, minors, flows, and extremal structure.

[Planar graphs and minors](planar-graphs-and-minors/README.md) is the largest field, with over two fifths of the
subject's articles, most of them on curves and regions in the square lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$. The
[discrete Jordan curve theorem](planar-graphs-and-minors/discrete-jordan-curve-theorem-for-the-square-lat/README.md)
gives a connected lattice subgraph with as many edges as vertices exactly one bounded region,
[Hopf's Umlaufsatz](planar-graphs-and-minors/hopf-s-umlaufsatz-for-lattice-polygons/README.md) says that a simple
closed lattice walk with at least three steps and no U-turns turns through $\pm 4$ quarter turns, and a
[discrete Gauss–Bonnet formula](planar-graphs-and-minors/discrete-gauss-bonnet-formula-for-polyominoes/README.md)
relates the corners of a polyomino to its Euler characteristic. Beyond the lattice, the field has the
[Robertson–Seymour theorem](planar-graphs-and-minors/graph-minors/README.md): every minor-closed family of finite
graphs is defined by finitely many forbidden minors.

[Graph coloring](graph-coloring/README.md) has the theorems of Brooks and Vizing, the
[four color theorem](graph-coloring/part-01/four-color-theorem/README.md), the
[Kneser–Lovász theorem](graph-coloring/part-01/kneser-graphs/README.md) that the Kneser graph $K(n, k)$ has chromatic
number $n - 2k + 2$, and the weak perfect graph theorem.
[Algebraic graph theory](algebraic-graph-theory/README.md) has
[Kirchhoff's matrix-tree theorem](algebraic-graph-theory/part-01/kirchhoff-s-matrix-tree-theorem/README.md), and the
pentagon has [Shannon capacity](graph-invariants-and-labelings/shannon-capacity-of-a-graph/README.md) $\sqrt{5}$.
[Matchings and flows](matchings-and-flows/README.md) has the existence of stable matchings in the
[stable marriage problem](matchings-and-flows/stable-marriage-problem/README.md), Kőnig's theorem and the
[max-flow min-cut theorem](matchings-and-flows/max-flow-min-cut-theorem/README.md), and
[paths, cycles and connectivity](paths-cycles-connectivity/README.md) has
[Menger's theorem](paths-cycles-connectivity/menger-s-theorem/README.md), the theorems of Dirac and Ore on Hamiltonian
cycles, and [Fleischner's theorem](paths-cycles-connectivity/fleischner-s-theorem/README.md) that the square of a
2-connected graph is Hamiltonian.
