# Genus of a function field

For a function field $F$ over a field $K$, that is, a finite
extension of the rational function field $K(t)$, the genus of $F/K$ is the natural number
$g = \dim_K \mathcal{A}_F / (\mathcal{A}_F(0) + F)$, where $\mathcal{A}_F$ is the adele ring and $\mathcal{A}_F(0)$ the integral adeles.
See [Stichtenoth, Corollary 1.5.5].
