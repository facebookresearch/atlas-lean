# Principal adele map

Let $F$ be a function field over a field $K$, that is, a finite
extension of the rational function field $K(t)$. The principal adele map is a ring homomorphism
from $F$ to the adele ring $\mathcal{A}_F$ of $F/K$. It is the diagonal embedding sending
$x \in F$ to the adele whose coordinate at every place $P$ of $F/K$ is $x$, which is well
defined since $x$ has only finitely many poles.
