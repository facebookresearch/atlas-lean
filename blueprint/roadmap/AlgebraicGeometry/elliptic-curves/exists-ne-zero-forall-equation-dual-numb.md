# Tangent line of a Weierstrass curve over the dual numbers

Let $k$ be a field, $W$ an affine Weierstrass curve over $k$ with discriminant $\Delta(W) \neq 0$, and $(x_0, y_0) \in k^2$ a point satisfying the Weierstrass equation of $W$. Let $k[\varepsilon] = k \oplus k\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon^2 = 0$ be the dual numbers over $k$, and let $W_{k[\varepsilon]}$ be the base change of $W$ along $k \to k[\varepsilon]$.

Then there is a nonzero vector $v = (v_1, v_2) \in k \times k$ such that for all $x = x_0 + x_1\varepsilon$ and $y = y_0 + y_1\varepsilon$ in $k[\varepsilon]$,

$$
(x, y) \text{ satisfies the equation of } W_{k[\varepsilon]} \iff \exists\, c \in k,\ x_1 = c\, v_1 \text{ and } y_1 = c\, v_2.
$$

So the infinitesimal lifts of $(x_0, y_0)$ form a one-dimensional $k$-line of directions.
