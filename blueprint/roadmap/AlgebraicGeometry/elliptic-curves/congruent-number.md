# Congruent number: a positive integer that is the area of a right triangle with rational sides

A positive integer $n$ is a *congruent number* if there exist positive rational numbers $a, b, c$ with $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ and $\tfrac12 ab = n$. That is, $n$ is the area of a right triangle with rational legs $a, b$ and rational hypotenuse $c$.
