# Peu ramifiée Weierstrass curve

Let $W$ be a Weierstrass curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ with discriminant $\Delta(W)$, and let $p, \ell$ be natural numbers. The curve $W$ is peu ramifiée at $\ell$ with respect to $p$ when

$$
p \mid v_\ell(\Delta(W)) \quad\text{in } \mathbb{Z},
$$

where $v_\ell$ is the $\ell$-adic valuation of rationals, with $v_\ell(0) = 0$. No primality of $p$ or $\ell$ is required by the definition.
