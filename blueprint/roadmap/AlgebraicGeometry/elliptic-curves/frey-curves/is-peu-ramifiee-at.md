# Peu ramifiée Weierstrass curve

Let $W$ be a Weierstrass curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ with discriminant $\Delta(W)$, and let $p, \ell$ be natural numbers. The curve $W$ is peu ramifiée at $\ell$ with respect to $p$ when

$$
p \mid v_\ell(\Delta(W)) \quad\text{in } \mathbb{Z},
$$

where, for a rational number $a/b$ in lowest terms with $b > 0$, $v_\ell(a/b)$ is the multiplicity of $\ell$ in $|a|$ minus its multiplicity in $b$, with $v_\ell(0) = 0$ and $v_\ell = 0$ for $\ell \in \{0, 1\}$. For prime $\ell$ this is the $\ell$-adic valuation of nonzero rationals; for composite $\ell$ it is not additive (for example $v_4(2) = 0$ but $v_4(4) = 1$). No primality of $p$ or $\ell$ is required by the definition.
