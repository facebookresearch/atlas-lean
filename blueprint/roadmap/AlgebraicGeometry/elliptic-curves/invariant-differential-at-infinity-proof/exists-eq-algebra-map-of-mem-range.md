# Functions on `A_W` regular at infinity are constant

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over
a field `F` and let `f ∈ A_W`. If the image of `f` in the common function field `Frac(A_W)` lies
in the image of the local ring `𝒪` at the point at infinity, then `f` is a constant `c ∈ F`.
The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.

## Sources

- J. S. Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition, World Scientific, 2021, author's version <https://jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf>, Chapter I, §4, p. 31 ("only the constant functions are without zeros and poles").
