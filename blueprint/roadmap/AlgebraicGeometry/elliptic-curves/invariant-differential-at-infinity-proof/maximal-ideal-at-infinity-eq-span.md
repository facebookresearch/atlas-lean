# The ideal of the point at infinity

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a field `F`. The
kernel `m` of evaluation `B_W → F` at `z = w = 0` is the ideal `(z, w)` of `B_W`.

## Sources

- J. S. Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition, World Scientific, 2021, author's version <https://jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf>, Chapter I, §4, p. 34 (the local ring at a point `P` of an affine curve is the localization at the maximal ideal of `P`). The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.
