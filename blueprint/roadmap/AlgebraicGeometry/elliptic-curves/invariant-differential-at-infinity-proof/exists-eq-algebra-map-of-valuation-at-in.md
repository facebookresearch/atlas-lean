# Regular functions on a projective Weierstrass cubic are constant

Let `W` be a
Weierstrass curve over a field `F` and let `f ∈ A_W`. If the valuation at the point at infinity
of `f` is at most `1`, that is `f` has no pole at `O`, then `f` is a constant `c ∈ F`.

Writing `f = p(x) + q(x) y` with `p, q ∈ F[x]`, the two terms have valuations
`exp (2 deg p)` and `exp (2 deg q + 3)` when nonzero; these differ by parity, so `q = 0` and
`deg p = 0`. The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.

## Sources

- J. S. Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition, World Scientific, 2021, author's version <https://jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf>, Chapter I, §4, p. 31 ("only the constant functions are without zeros and poles"), and Chapter II, §1, p. 47 (`x` and `y` have poles of order `2` and `3` at `O` and no other poles).
