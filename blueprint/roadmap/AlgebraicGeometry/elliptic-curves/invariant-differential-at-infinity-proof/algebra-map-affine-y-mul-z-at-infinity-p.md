# `y z³ = u`

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a field `F`. In the function field
`Frac(A_W)`,

$$y z^3 = 1 + a_1 z + a_3 w - a_2 z^2 - a_4 z w - a_6 w^2,$$

and the right side (`unitAtInfinity`) is a unit of the local ring at infinity
(`isUnit_algebraMap_unitAtInfinity`); so `y = z⁻³ · (unit of 𝒪)`, that is `ord_O y = -3`.

## Sources

- J. S. Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition, World Scientific, 2021, author's version <https://jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf>, Chapter II, §1, p. 47. The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.
