# The maximal ideal at infinity is principal

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a field
`F`. The maximal ideal of the local ring `𝒪 = (B_W)_(z, w)` at the point at infinity is
generated by `z`: it is generated by `z` and `w`, and `w = z³ v` with `v` a unit.

## Sources

- B. Conrad, *Minimal models for elliptic curves*, 2015, <http://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/minimalmodel.pdf>, proof of Theorem 2.8, p. 4. The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.
