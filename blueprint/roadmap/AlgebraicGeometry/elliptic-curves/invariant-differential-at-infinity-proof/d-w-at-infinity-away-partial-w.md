# `dw = -(h_z / h_w) dz`

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a commutative ring `R` and let
`S = B_W[1 / h_w]`. In `Ω¹_{S/R}`,

$$dw = -\frac{h_z}{h_w} \, dz,$$

with `h_z = a₁ w - 3 z² - 2 a₂ z w - a₄ w²` and
`h_w = 1 + a₁ z + 2 a₃ w - a₂ z² - 2 a₄ z w - 3 a₆ w²`.

No hypothesis on the discriminant is needed.
