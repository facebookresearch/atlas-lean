# `w = z³ v` at infinity

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a field `F`. In the local
ring `𝒪 = (B_W)_(z, w)` at the point at infinity,

$$w = z^3 v, \qquad v = (1 + a_1 z + a_3 w - a_2 z^2 - a_4 z w - a_6 w^2)^{-1},$$

where `v` (`unitInvAtInfinity`) is a unit of `𝒪`.

## Sources

- B. Conrad, *Minimal models for elliptic curves*, 2015, <http://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/minimalmodel.pdf>, proof of Theorem 2.8, p. 4. The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.
