# `z` is a uniformizer at infinity

Let `W` be a Weierstrass curve over a field `F`. The
coordinate `z = X / Y` is irreducible, that is a uniformizer, in the discrete valuation ring
`𝒪 = (B_W)_(z, w)` at the point at infinity.

## Sources

- B. Conrad, *Minimal models for elliptic curves*, 2015, <http://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/minimalmodel.pdf>, proof of Theorem 2.8, p. 4. The source assumes `Δ_W ≠ 0`, which is not used.
