# Addition laws and complete systems on a Weierstrass curve

An addition law of bidegree $(\mu,\nu)$ on a projective Weierstrass curve is a triple of bihomogeneous polynomials computing $P_1+P_2$ wherever they do not all vanish; a complete system is a set of laws such that every pair of points, over every extension field, is added by at least one of them.

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a field $k$ given by $F(X,Y,Z)=Y^2Z+a_1XYZ+a_3YZ^2-X^3-a_2X^2Z-a_4XZ^2-a_6Z^3=0$ with nonzero discriminant, and $O=(0:1:0)$. For positive integers $\mu,\nu$, an *addition law of bidegree* $(\mu,\nu)$ on $E$ is a triple $X_3,Y_3,Z_3\in k[X_1,Y_1,Z_1,X_2,Y_2,Z_2]$, each homogeneous of degree $\mu$ in $X_1,Y_1,Z_1$ and of degree $\nu$ in $X_2,Y_2,Z_2$, such that for every extension $K$ of $k$ and all $P_1=(x_1:y_1:z_1)$, $P_2=(x_2:y_2:z_2)\in E(K)$ the values $x_3,y_3,z_3$ either all vanish, or $(x_3:y_3:z_3)\in E(K)$ equals $P_1+P_2$. The pair $(P_1,P_2)$ is *exceptional* for the law in the first case. A *complete system of addition laws* is a collection of addition laws such that no pair of points of $E(K)$, for any extension $K$ of $k$, is exceptional for all of them. Two laws are *equivalent* if one is a nonzero constant multiple of the other; the *zero law* has every pair exceptional. Addition laws are considered modulo the ideal generated by $F(X_1,Y_1,Z_1)$ and $F(X_2,Y_2,Z_2)$.

## Expansion

Bosma and Lenstra translate addition laws into functions on $E\times E$: the map sending a law to $Z_3Z_1^{-\mu}Z_2^{-\nu}$ is a $k$-linear isomorphism from the laws of bidegree $(\mu,\nu)$ onto the space of $h\in k(E\times E)$ with $h=0$ or $(h)\ge3\nabla-3\mu V-3\nu H$, where $V,H,\nabla$ are the pullbacks of $O$ under the first projection, the second projection and the addition map (the Lemma of Section 2). Homogenizing the usual chord formula for addition and removing a common factor $Z_1Z_2$ gives an addition law of bidegree $(2,2)$ (Section 2, p. 233). Prerequisites still to add: divisors on the surface $E\times E$, the pullbacks $V$, $H$, $\nabla$ of $O$ under the two projections and the addition map, and their intersection numbers.
