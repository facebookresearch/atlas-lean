# Removing an unramified odd prime from a Frey level

Let a Frey package have residual prime $p$ and admissible conductor level $N$. If $q$ is prime, $q\ne2$, $q\ne p$, $q\mid N$, the $p$-torsion is irreducible and unramified at $q$, and the traces are realized at level $N$, then there is a natural $M\mid N$ with $q\nmid M$ realizing the traces at level $M$.
