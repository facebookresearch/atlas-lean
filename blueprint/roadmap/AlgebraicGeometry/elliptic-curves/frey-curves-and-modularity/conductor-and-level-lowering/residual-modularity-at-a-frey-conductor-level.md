# A trace-congruence realization at a Frey conductor level

Every Frey package admits some positive squarefree level $N$ all of whose prime divisors divide $abc$, such that its rational Frey equation has a residual trace-congruence realization at level $N$ in the stated sense. The conclusion does not specify a particular $N$ or assert isomorphism of two representations.

## Expansion

Use the integral model at exact conductor level and relate its discriminant support to the product $abc$.
