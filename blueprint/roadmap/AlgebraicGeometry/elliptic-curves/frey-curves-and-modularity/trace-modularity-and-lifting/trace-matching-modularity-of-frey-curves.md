# Trace-matching modularity of Frey curves

For any Frey package, its rational Frey equation is modular in the trace-matching sense: some integral model after rational variable change and some positive level $N$ admit a weight-two cusp form with the specified coefficient conditions matching its $a_\ell$ at every good prime not dividing $N$.
