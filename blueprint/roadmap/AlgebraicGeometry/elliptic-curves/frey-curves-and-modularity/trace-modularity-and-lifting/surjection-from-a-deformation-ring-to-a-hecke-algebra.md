# Surjection from a deformation ring to a weight-two Hecke algebra

Let $\mathcal O$ be a commutative complete discrete valuation domain, and put $k=\mathcal O/\mathfrak m_{\mathcal O}$. Take an absolutely irreducible residual representation $\bar\rho$ over $k$ and a predicate $\mathcal D$ on adic representations over local commutative $\mathcal O$-algebras. An adic representation over a commutative local ring $A$ acts by $A$-linear maps on a finite free module of rank two. For each integer $n\ge0$, some finite subextension of $\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q$ has the property that automorphisms fixing it act as the identity modulo $\mathfrak m_A^n$ on the module. The residual representation $\bar\rho$ is two-dimensional and factors through a finite subextension in the analogous sense without a congruence.

Take a deformation datum $(R,\rho_R)$ where $R$ is a commutative local Noetherian $\mathfrak m_R$-adically complete $\mathcal O$-algebra, the structure map is local, and the induced residue map $k\to k_R$ is surjective. The chosen adic $\rho_R$ satisfies $\mathcal D$, and its reduction is equivalent to $\bar\rho$ after base change along $k\to k_R$. The universal property quantifies over commutative local Noetherian $\mathfrak m_A$-adically complete $\mathcal O$-algebras $A$ with a local structure map and surjective $k\to k_A$, and only over adic representations on them satisfying $\mathcal D$ whose reduction is equivalent to the scalar extension of $\bar\rho$. Each such lift is equivalent to the base change of $\rho_R$ along a unique local $\mathcal O$-algebra map $R\to A$.

For $N>0$ and $S\subseteq\mathbb N$, let $\mathbb T_{N,S}$ be the $\mathbb Z$-subalgebra of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(S_2(\Gamma_0(N)))$ generated by $T_\ell$ for primes $\ell\nmid N$ with $\ell\notin S$ and $U_q$ for primes $q\mid N$ with $q\notin S$. Here $S_2(\Gamma_0(N))$ is the complex space of weight-two cusp forms. Assume $S$ contains every prime divisor of $N$, so none of the $U_q$ occur in the generating set for this theorem. Take a residual ring homomorphism $\theta:\mathbb T_{N,S}\to k$. Let $T$ be a commutative complete local Noetherian $\mathcal O$-algebra, local over $\mathcal O$ and finite free as an $\mathcal O$-module. A Hecke–Galois datum consists of a Hecke homomorphism to $T$ generating $T$ over $\mathcal O$, with residual character induced by $\theta$ and $k\to k_T$; for each $\mathcal O$-valued Hecke character reducing to $\theta$, an $\mathcal O$-algebra map $T\to\mathcal O$ through which that character factors; surjectivity of the composite $\mathcal O\to T\to k_T$; and an adic representation $\rho_T$ whose reduction is absolutely irreducible. At every prime $\ell\nmid N$, $\ell\notin S$, the characteristic polynomial on every Frobenius element at any valuation above $\ell$ is $X^2-T_\ell X+\ell$, with the Hecke image in $T$. Assume $\rho_T$ has type $\mathcal D$ and its reduction is equivalent to the scalar extension of $\bar\rho$ along $k\to k_T$.

Then there exists a **surjective local $\mathcal O$-algebra map** $R\to T$ along which the base change of $\rho_R$ is equivalent to $\rho_T$. No injectivity is asserted.

## Expansion

Implement the rank-two adic representation, deformation datum, and weight-two Hecke and Hecke–Galois interfaces given above. The universal property supplies the local map $R\to T$ and the base-change equivalence. For each remaining $T_\ell$, use a Frobenius element above $\ell$ and compare the linear coefficients of the characteristic polynomials under scalar extension and equivalence. This puts the Hecke image of $T_\ell$ in the range. There is no $U_q$ case because its prime belongs to $S$. The Hecke images generate $T$ over $\mathcal O$, so the range is all of $T$.
