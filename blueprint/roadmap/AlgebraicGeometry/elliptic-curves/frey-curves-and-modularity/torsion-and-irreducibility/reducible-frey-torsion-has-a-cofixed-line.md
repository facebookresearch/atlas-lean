# Reducible Frey torsion has a trivial quotient by a stable line

For every Frey package, suppose its $p$-torsion fails the nontrivial irreducibility predicate. Then it has a Galois-stable proper nonzero submodule $L$ such that $\sigma x-x\in L$ for every Galois element $\sigma$ and every $p$-torsion point $x$. The conclusion is about a trivial quotient, not the existence of a rational torsion point.

## Depends on

- [Frey torsion and the cofixed predicate](frey-torsion-and-cofixed-lines.md)

## Expansion

Separate the nontriviality of the torsion from reducibility and make explicit where absence of rational $p$-torsion rules out the opposite arrangement.
