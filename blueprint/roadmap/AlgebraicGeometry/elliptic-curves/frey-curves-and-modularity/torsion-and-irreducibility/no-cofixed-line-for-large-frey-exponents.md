# No cofixed quotient for large Frey exponents

If a Frey package has exponent $p\ge17$, there is no Galois-stable proper nonzero submodule of its $p$-torsion on whose quotient every element of $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}$ acts trivially.

## Depends on

- [Frey torsion and the cofixed predicate](frey-torsion-and-cofixed-lines.md)

## Expansion

For large exponents decompose the Eisenstein-ideal argument through the reduction of the cuspidal subgroup and its component at the bad prime.

For the input carrier and the integral-to-rational comparison, see [Frey packages and their models](../trace-modularity-and-lifting/normalized-fermat-solutions-and-frey-curves.md).
