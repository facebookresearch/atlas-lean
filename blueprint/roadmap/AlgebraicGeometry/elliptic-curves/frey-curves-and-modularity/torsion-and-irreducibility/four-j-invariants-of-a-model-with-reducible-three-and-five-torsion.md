# Four possible j-invariants when three- and five-torsion are both reducible

Let $W/\mathbb Z$ be a Weierstrass equation with $\Delta(W)\ne0$. If the torsion irreducibility predicates fail at both 3 and 5, then $c_4(W)^3/\Delta(W)$ lies in $\{-25/2,-349938025/8,-121945/32,46969655/32768\}\subset\mathbb Q$. The statement does not impose semistability or claim a classification of all rational isogenies.

## Expansion

Bridge torsion reducibility to the isogeny conditions on this nonsingular model and isolate the rational-point classification used for the four values.
