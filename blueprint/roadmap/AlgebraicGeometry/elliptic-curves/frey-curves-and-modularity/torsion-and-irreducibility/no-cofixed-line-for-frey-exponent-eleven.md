# No cofixed quotient for Frey exponent eleven

If a Frey package has exponent $p=11$, there is no Galois-stable proper nonzero submodule of its $p$-torsion on whose quotient every element of $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}$ acts trivially.

- [Frey torsion and the cofixed predicate](frey-torsion-and-cofixed-lines.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Fermat’s equation at eleven](../../../../NumberTheory/diophantine-equations/higher-degree-equations/fermat-prime-exponents/fermat-equation-at-exponent-11.md)

## Expansion

Apply the Fermat result for each indicated exponent, with the conversion from nonzero integers to naturals. The conclusion in these cases follows by excluding the package itself.
