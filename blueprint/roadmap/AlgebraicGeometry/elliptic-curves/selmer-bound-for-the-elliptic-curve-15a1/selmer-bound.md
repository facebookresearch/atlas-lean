# Selmer bound for the 15a1 descent

Let $E : y^2 = x^3 + 5x^2 - 152x - 624$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, with descent coordinates $\delta(P) = (\delta_1(P), \delta_2(P))$ and the set $V_0 = \{(1, 1), (-5, -1), (-1, -1), (5, 1)\}$. For every rational point $P$ of $E$ (the point at infinity included) there is $(d_1, d_2) \in V_0$ and nonzero rationals $c_1, c_2$ with

$$
\delta_1(P) = d_1\, c_1^2 \quad\text{and}\quad \delta_2(P) = d_2\, c_2^2.
$$
