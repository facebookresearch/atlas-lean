# Supersingular elliptic curves

Let $p$ be a prime and let $E$ be an elliptic curve over an
algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$ of $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. Then $E$ is called
supersingular when every point $P \in E(\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p)$ with $pP = 0$ is $P = 0$.

## Sources

- Sarah Arpin, James Clements, Pierrick Dartois, Jonathan Komada Eriksen, Péter Kutas and Benjamin Wesolowski, *Finding orientations of supersingular elliptic curves and quaternion orders*, arXiv:2308.11539v1 (2023), Section 2.1, which gives the equivalent condition that for every positive integer $r$, every point $P$ with $p^r P = 0$ is $P = 0$; this definition is its case $r = 1$.
- Joseph H. Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*, 2nd ed., GTM 106, Springer, 2009, Theorem V.3.1.
