# A chain without backtracking composes to a cyclic isogeny

The composite of a chain of $m$ isogenies of prime degree $\ell$ without backtracking, with $\ell$ prime to the characteristic, is a cyclic isogeny of degree $\ell^m$.

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, $\ell$ a prime different from $\operatorname{char}k$, $m\ge1$, and let $\varphi_i\colon E_{i-1}\to E_i$, $1\le i\le m$, be a chain of $\ell$-isogenies [without backtracking](isogeny-chain-without-backtracking.md). Then $\varphi_m\circ\cdots\circ\varphi_1\colon E_0\to E_m$ is a [cyclic](cyclic-isogeny.md) isogeny of degree $\ell^m$.

## Expansion

The composite has degree $\ell^m$ and is separable, being a composite of separable isogenies. Cyclicity is by induction on $m$; for $m=1$ the kernel has prime order $\ell$. For $m\ge2$, the suffix $\psi=\varphi_m\circ\cdots\circ\varphi_2$ is a chain without backtracking of length $m-1$, so by induction its kernel is cyclic of order $\ell^{m-1}$, generated by some $R_1\in E_1(k)$ whose multiple $\ell^{m-2}R_1$ generates $\ker\varphi_2$. Choose $Q\in E_1(k)$ with $\ell Q=R_1$ and put $R_0=\hat\varphi_1(Q)$. Then $\varphi_1(R_0)=\varphi_1\hat\varphi_1(Q)=\ell Q=R_1$, so $\psi\varphi_1(R_0)=O$ and $R_0$ lies in the kernel of the whole composite. Its order is $\ell^m$: $\ell^{m-1}R_0=\hat\varphi_1(\ell^{m-2}R_1)$, which is $O$ only if $\ell^{m-2}R_1$, a generator of $\ker\varphi_2$, lies in $\ker\hat\varphi_1$, that is, only if $\ker\varphi_2=\ker\hat\varphi_1$, which is backtracking at step $1$. So $R_0$ has order $\ell^m$ and generates the kernel, of order $\ell^m$. This is the argument of Costache, Feigon, Lauter, Massierer and Puskás, Lemma 4.4, run from the start of the chain; Charles, Goren and Lauter argue the contrapositive: in a composite with non-cyclic kernel, the first step where the partial kernel stops being cyclic is a backtracking step.
