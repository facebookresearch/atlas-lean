# Point ideals on a Weierstrass curve determine the point

Let $F$ be a field and $W$ an affine Weierstrass curve over $F$, with coordinate ring $F[W] = F[X, Y]/(W(X, Y))$. For $(x, y) \in F^2$ let $I(x, y) \subseteq F[W]$ be the ideal generated by the classes of $X - x$ and $Y - y$. If $(x_1, y_1)$ satisfies the Weierstrass equation of $W$, then for all $x_2, y_2 \in F$,

$$
I(x_1, y_1) = I(x_2, y_2) \iff x_1 = x_2 \text{ and } y_1 = y_2.
$$

Only the first point is assumed to lie on the curve.
