# The ideal of a point on a Weierstrass curve is nonzero

Let $R$ be a nontrivial commutative ring and let $W$ be a Weierstrass curve over $R$, with
coordinate ring $R[W] = R[X, Y]/(W(X, Y))$. For every $x \in R$ and every polynomial
$y \in R[X]$, the ideal of $R[W]$ generated by the classes of $X - x$ and $Y - y(X)$ is nonzero.
