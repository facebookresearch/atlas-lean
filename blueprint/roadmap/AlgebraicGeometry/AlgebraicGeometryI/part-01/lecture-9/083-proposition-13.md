# Proposition 13

Suppose $X$ embeds via two embeddings $i_1$, $i_2$ to $\mathbb{A}^n$ and $\mathbb{A}^m$ respectively, such that there exists some $x$ such that $i_1(x) = i_2(x) = 0$, then $\widetilde{X}_1 = \widetilde{X}_2$ for two blowups at $x$.
