# Definition 33

$x$ is a smooth point on $X$ if $\dim_x(X) = \dim(\mathfrak{m}_x/\mathfrak{m}_x^2)$, where $\mathfrak{m}_x$ is the maximal ideal in $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$.
