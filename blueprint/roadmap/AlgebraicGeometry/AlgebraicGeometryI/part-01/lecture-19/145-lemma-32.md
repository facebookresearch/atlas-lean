# Lemma 32

Let $A$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{m}$ a maximal ideal, $a \in A$. Suppose $a \in \mathfrak{m}^p$, write $\overline{a} \in \overline{A}_p = \mathfrak{m}^p/\mathfrak{m}^{p+1}$ and $\overline{A} = \bigoplus_n \mathfrak{m}^n/\mathfrak{m}^{n+1}$. Then $\overline{A/(a)} = \bigoplus_n (A/a)^n/(A/a)^{n+1} = \overline{A}/(\overline{a})$ if $\overline{a}$ is not a zero divisor.
