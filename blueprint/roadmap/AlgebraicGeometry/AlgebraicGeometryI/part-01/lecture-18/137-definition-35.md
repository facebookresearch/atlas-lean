# Definition 35

Let $X$ be a variety. The Zariski cotangent space of $X$ at $x \in X$ is defined to be the vector space $\{\xi \colon \mathcal{O}_{X,x} \to k \mid \xi \text{ is linear and } \xi(fg) = f(x)\xi(g) + g(x)\xi(f)\}$, i.e.\ it is the set of derivations at $x$, and it is denoted as $T_x^*X$.
