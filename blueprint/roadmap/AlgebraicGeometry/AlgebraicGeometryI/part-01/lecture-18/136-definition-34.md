# Definition 34

Let $A$ be a commutative $k$-algebra. $\Omega_A$ is defined to be the $A$-module generated by expressions $da$, $a \in A$, modulo the following equations: $d(a+b) = da + db$; $d(\lambda a) = \lambda\, da$; $d(ab) = (da)b + a(db)$, where $a, b \in A$, $\lambda \in k$. Then $\Omega_A$ is characterized by a universal property: $\operatorname{Hom}(\Omega_A, M) = \operatorname{Der}(A, M)$ for any $A$-module $M$, where $\operatorname{Der}(A, M)$ is the $k$-module of $k$-linear derivations from $A$ to $M$.
