# Lemma 1 (Noether Normalization)

Given $A$ a finitely-generated $k$-algebra, there exist some algebraically independent elements $X_1, \ldots, X_d$ over $k$ such that $A$ is a finitely generated $k[X_1, \ldots, X_d]$-module.
