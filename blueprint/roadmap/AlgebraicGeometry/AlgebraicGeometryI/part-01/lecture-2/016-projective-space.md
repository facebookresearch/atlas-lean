# Definition 4 (Projective Space)

Topologically, the projective space $\mathbb{P}^n$ is given by the quotient topology $\mathbb{A}^{n+1} \setminus \{0\} / (x_0, \dots, x_n) \sim (\lambda x_0, \dots, \lambda x_n)$ for all $\lambda \neq 0$. A function on $U \subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ is regular if its pullback by $\mathbb{A}^{n+1} \setminus \{0\} \xrightarrow{\pi} \mathbb{P}^n$ is regular on $\pi^{-1}(U)$.
