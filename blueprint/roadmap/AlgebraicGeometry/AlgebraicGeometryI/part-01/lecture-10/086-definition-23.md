# Definition 23

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a presheaf on $X$. Then its pushforward along $f$ is a presheaf $f_*\mathcal{F}$ on $Y$, and is defined on an open subset $V \subset Y$ as $f_*\mathcal{F}(V) \coloneqq \mathcal{F}(f^{-1}V)$.
