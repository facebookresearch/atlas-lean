# Definition 26

A locally free sheaf $\mathcal{M}$ of rank $n$ on an algebraic variety $X$ is a sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules such that for some open cover of the variety $X = \bigcup U_i$, the restrictions $\mathcal{M}|_{U_i}$ are free sheaves on $U_i$ of rank $n$, that is $\mathcal{M}|_{U_i} \cong (\mathcal{O}_X|_{U_i})^n$.
