# Definition 24

Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a presheaf on $Y$. Then its pullback along $f$ is a presheaf $f^*\mathcal{G}$ on $X$, and is defined on an open subset $U \subset X$ as $f^*\mathcal{G}(U) \coloneqq \varinjlim_{V \supset f(U)} \mathcal{G}(V)$.
