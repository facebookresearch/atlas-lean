# Lemma 19

(i) Suppose $Z$ is closed in $X$. Then $X$ is complete implies $Z$ is complete. (ii) If $f \colon X \to Z$ is a morphism with $Z$ separated and $X$ complete, then $f(X) \subseteq Z$ is a closed complete subvariety. (iii) If $X$, $Y$ are complete, then so is $X \times Y$.
