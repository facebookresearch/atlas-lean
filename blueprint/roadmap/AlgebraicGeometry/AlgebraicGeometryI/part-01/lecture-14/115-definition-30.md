# Definition 30

The Cartier divisor group $\operatorname{DC}(X) = \Gamma(\mathcal{K}^*/\mathcal{O}^*)$, where $*$ means nonzero, and $\mathcal{K}$ is the sheaf of rational functions. Another way to describe it is the set of invertible fractional ideals. It can be seen as a subsheaf realized in $\mathcal{K}^*$.
