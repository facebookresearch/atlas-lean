# Definition 13

Let $X$, $Y$ be two curves in $\mathbb{A}^2$ with no common component and $(a,b)$ be an intersection point. If $I_X$ and $I_Y$ are the ideals in $k[x,y]$ defining $X$ and $Y$, respectively, then $V = k[x,y]/(I_X + I_Y)$ is a finite dimensional vector space and multiplication by $x$, $y$ induces two commuting operators on $V$. The multiplicity of intersection of $X$ and $Y$ at $(a,b)$ is defined as the dimension of the common generalized eigenspace of the two operators, with eigenvalues $a$, $b$ respectively.
