# Definition 12

Let $X \subset \mathbb{A}^n$ be a hypersurface defined by a polynomial $g$. Write $g$ as a sum of homogeneous components $g = g_m + g_{m+1} + \cdots$ with $g_m \neq 0$. If $0 \in X$, the multiplicity of $X$ at $0$ is defined to be the natural number $m$. The multiplicity at $p \in X$ is the multiplicity at $0$ after applying a linear change of coordinates mapping $p$ to $0$.
