# Lemma 8

Let $Z_1 \subsetneq Z_2$ be irreducible closed subsets of an algebraic variety $X$. If $f \colon X \longrightarrow Y$ is a finite morphism, then $f(Z_1) \subsetneq f(Z_2)$.
