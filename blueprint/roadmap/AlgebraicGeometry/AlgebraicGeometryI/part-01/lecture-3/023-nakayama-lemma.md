# Lemma 4 (Nakayama lemma)

Let $M$ be a finitely generated module over a commutative ring $A$. If $I$ is an ideal of $A$ such that $IM = M$, then there exists $a \in A$ such that $aM = 0$ and $a \equiv 1 \pmod{I}$.
