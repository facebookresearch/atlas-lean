# Definition 31

An element of $\operatorname{Div}_C(X)$ is given by 1.\ a covering $U_i$; and 2.\ rational functions $f_i$ on $U_i$, $f_i \neq 0$, such that on $U_i \cap U_j$, $f_j = \varphi_{ij} f_i$, where $\varphi_{ij} \in \mathcal{O}^*(U_i \cap U_j)$.
