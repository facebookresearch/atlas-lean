# Lemma 14 (Yoneda)

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. For every $x \in \mathcal{C}$ define a covariant functor $h^x \colon \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}$, $c \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}(x, c)$. Then the assignment $x \mapsto h^x$ defines a functor $h \colon \mathcal{C} \to \operatorname{Functors}(\mathcal{C}, \mathbf{Set})$. $h$ is fully faithful and therefore injective on objects (up to isomorphism).
