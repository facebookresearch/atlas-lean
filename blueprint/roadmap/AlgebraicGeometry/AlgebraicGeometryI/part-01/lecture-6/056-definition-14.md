# Definition 14

Let $X$ be an irreducible variety. The function field of $X$, denoted $k(X)$, is defined as the limit $K(X) = \varinjlim_{U \subset X} k[U]$ taken over all open subsets of $X$ with the obvious restriction morphisms.
