# Corollary 22

Given $f \colon X \to Y$ where $X$, $Y$ are irreducible, if $X$ is normal, $f$ is finite, onto, then $X$ can be reconstructed from $Y$ and $f^{-1}(U)$ for some open $U \neq \emptyset \subseteq Y$.
