# Theorem 17.2

Let $g$ be the (geometric) genus of $X$ and assume it equals $1$. Then $\mathbb{C}^g/\mathbb{Z}^{2g} = \mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}^2$ has dimension $1$ and is therefore a curve. Fix $x_0 \in X$. The Abel--Jacobi map gives a map $X \to \operatorname{Pic}^{\circ}(X)$, where we send $x$ to $x - x_0$. Then this is an isomorphism.
