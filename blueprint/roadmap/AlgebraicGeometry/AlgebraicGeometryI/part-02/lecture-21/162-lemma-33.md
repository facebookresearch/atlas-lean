# Lemma 33

Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a morphism of irreducible affine varieties with $\operatorname{im}(f)$ dense in $Y$. Then there is a nonempty open subset $U \subseteq Y$ such that $f^{-1}(U) \to U$ factors as $f \colon f^{-1}(U) \xrightarrow{\text{finite, onto}} U \times \mathbb{A}^n \xrightarrow{\pi_1} U$.
