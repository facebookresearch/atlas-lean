# Corollary 29

Define the arithmetic genus $g_a = \dim(H^1(\mathcal{O}))$, and the geometric genus $g_m = \dim(\Gamma(K_X))$. Then apply Serre duality to $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{O}$ to get $g_a = g_m$.
