# Corollary 30

Riemann--Roch implies $\dim(\Gamma(\mathcal{E})) - \dim(\Gamma(K \otimes \mathcal{E}^*)) = \deg(\mathcal{E}) + \operatorname{rank}(\mathcal{E})(1 - g)$. This is Riemann's form of the theorem.
