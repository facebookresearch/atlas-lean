# Proposition 41 (Grothendieck)

A $\delta$-functor $(\mathcal{F}^i)$ for given $\mathcal{F}$ is universal provided that $\mathcal{F}^i$ for $i > 0$ is effaceable: for any $M \in \mathcal{A}$ and any $m \in F^i M$, there exists some monomorphism $\varphi \colon M \to N$ such that $\mathcal{F}^i(\varphi)(m) = 0$.
