# Proposition 40

$\delta$ is a well-defined homomorphism, where $\delta \colon K^0(\mathbf{Coh}(X)) \to \mathbb{Z}$ is defined by $\delta([\mathcal{E}]) = \deg(\det(\mathcal{E}))$ for $\mathcal{E}$ locally free, and $\delta(\mathcal{T}) = \operatorname{length}(\mathcal{T})$ for torsion sheaves.
