# Definition 42

$K^0(\mathcal{A})$, the Grothendieck group of an abelian category $\mathcal{A}$, is the free abelian group generated by isomorphism classes in $\mathcal{A}$ modulo the relation that, given $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$, we have $[B] = [A] + [C]$.
