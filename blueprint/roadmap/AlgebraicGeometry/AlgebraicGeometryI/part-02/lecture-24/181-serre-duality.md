# Theorem 24.3 (Serre Duality)

If $\mathcal{E}$ is a locally free sheaf on a complete smooth irreducible curve, then we have a canonical isomorphism $\Gamma(\mathcal{E})^* \cong H^1(\mathcal{E}^\vee \otimes K_X)$.
