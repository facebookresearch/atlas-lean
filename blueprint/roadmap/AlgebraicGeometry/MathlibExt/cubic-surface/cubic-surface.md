# Cubic Surface

A cubic surface in `ℙ³` over `k`, presented by its defining equation: a nonzero homogeneous
polynomial of degree `3` in the four homogeneous coordinates. The surface is the projective zero
locus `V(defining) ⊆ ℙ³`.
