# Derived algebraic geometry

Algebraic geometry over simplicial commutative rings: derived prestacks and stacks for the étale topology, derived Artin stacks, quasi-coherent sheaves and cotangent complexes of derived stacks, differential forms and closed forms, shifted symplectic and Lagrangian structures, moduli of objects in dg categories, Calabi–Yau structures on dg categories with the Hochschild and cyclic homology of dg algebras they rest on, and dg enhancements of quasi-coherent sheaves and perfect complexes on schemes. Derived categories of quasi-coherent and coherent sheaves on schemes are under schemes and morphisms, and dg categories and dg modules as such under homological algebra.
