# Estimates for small sections along an effective divisor

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the analytic and arithmetic estimates by which Yuan
proves his [inequality for arithmetic volumes](yuan-inequality.md): the existence of small sections
whose divisors avoid prescribed points (arithmetic Bertini theorems), the asymptotics of distortion
functions of positive hermitian line bundles, and the estimate of the change of $\chi_{\sup}$ along the
divisor of a small section.

A good expansion has pages for:

- Moriwaki's small-section Bertini theorem: for an arithmetically ample hermitian line bundle $\bar H$
  on an arithmetic variety, finitely many points $x_1, \dots, x_r$ with residue fields of positive
  characteristic, and hypersurfaces $Z_m$ of the linear systems $|H^m_\infty|$ of degree bounded by a
  polynomial in $m$, for every large $m$ there is $\varphi \in H^0(X, H^m)$ with $\varphi(x_i) \ne 0$
  for all $i$, $\operatorname{div}(\varphi_\infty) \notin Z_m$ and $\lVert \varphi \rVert_{\sup} < 1$
  (Moriwaki, "Arithmetic Bogomolov–Gieseker's inequality", Theorem 5.3, p. 17, whose proof uses Zhang's
  bases of small sections); and its use in Yuan §2.3.3, pp. 18–20, with Yuan's Lemma 2.9 (perturbing to
  positive curvature);
- the distortion function of a positive hermitian line bundle and its uniform asymptotics (Bouche,
  Tian; Yuan Theorem 2.10), Yuan's Proposition 2.13 (Gromov's inequality, see
  [Gromov's inequality](gromov-inequality.md)), and his Proposition 2.14 with Lemmas 2.15–2.16,
  pp. 25–29, which estimate the volumes along the divisor using the classical Hilbert–Samuel formula on
  the vertical components and the arithmetic one on the horizontal components.
