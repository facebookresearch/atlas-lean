# Arakelov geometry

Arithmetic varieties over the integers or over rings of integers, with hermitian vector bundles and Green currents on their complex points: arithmetic Chow groups and intersection theory, arithmetic Chern classes and degrees, the arithmetic Riemann–Roch and Hilbert–Samuel theorems, heights of cycles, and arithmetic volumes and positivity of hermitian line bundles, and Arakelov divisors of number fields. Heights of algebraic numbers and of points of projective space are under heights and Mahler measure.
