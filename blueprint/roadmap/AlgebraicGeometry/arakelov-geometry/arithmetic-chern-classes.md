# Arithmetic characteristic classes of hermitian vector bundles

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the characteristic classes that Gillet and Soulé
attach to hermitian vector bundles $\bar E = (E, h)$ on arithmetic varieties, in the setting of
their Inventiones paper (arithmetic varieties quasi-projective over an arithmetic ring, over a good
arithmetic ring where it says so): on a regular arithmetic variety $X$, the arithmetic Chern classes
$\hat c_p(\bar E) \in \widehat{CH}^p(X)$, with integral coefficients, the arithmetic Chern character
$\widehat{\mathrm{ch}}(\bar E) \in \widehat{CH}^*(X)_\mathbb{Q} = \bigoplus_{p \ge 0}
\widehat{CH}^p(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ and the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(\bar E)$;
the secondary Bott–Chern classes, which measure on a metrized exact sequence the failure of the
additivity of the Chern character and of the multiplicative (Whitney) identities of the total Chern
class, the Todd class and its inverse; and the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f)$ of a
morphism, which enters the [arithmetic Riemann–Roch theorem](arithmetic-riemann-roch-theorem.md).
The classes are constructed in Gillet and Soulé's papers in the Annals of Mathematics 131 (1990),
recalled by Bost, Gillet and Soulé (§2.1.2), and extended to homological arithmetic Chow groups of
arithmetic varieties that are not regular in Section 2 of Gillet and Soulé's Inventiones paper on
the arithmetic Riemann–Roch theorem, which this node follows.

A good expansion has pages for:

- the secondary Bott–Chern classes on a complex manifold (Inventiones, 2.4.1): for an exact sequence
  $\mathcal{E} \colon 0 \to S \to E \to Q \to 0$ of holomorphic vector bundles with arbitrary
  hermitian metrics, a class $\widetilde{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathcal{E}) \in \bigoplus_p A^{p,p}(X) /
  (\operatorname{im} \partial + \operatorname{im} \bar\partial)$ with $dd^c
  \widetilde{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathrm{ch}(\bar S) - \mathrm{ch}(\bar E) +
  \mathrm{ch}(\bar Q)$, where $\mathrm{ch}(\bar E) = \operatorname{tr} \exp(\frac{i}{2\pi}
  \nabla^2)$ is the Chern–Weil form of the Chern connection; the secondary class is functorial and
  vanishes when $\mathcal{E}$ is metrically split. For the Todd class and its inverse, which are
  multiplicative, the analogous classes satisfy $dd^c \widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(\mathcal{E}) =
  \mathrm{Td}(\bar S) \mathrm{Td}(\bar Q) - \mathrm{Td}(\bar E)$ (Inventiones, 2.4.1, p. 494). Also
  the classes of finite acyclic complexes, and the identities of Inventiones 2.6.1 (Lemma 14);
- the arithmetic Chern classes $\hat c_p(\bar E) \in \widehat{CH}^p(X)$ and the arithmetic Chern
  character of a hermitian vector bundle on a regular arithmetic variety, with $\hat c_1(\bar L) =
  [(\operatorname{div} s, -\log \lVert s \rVert^2)]$ for a hermitian line bundle on the
  quasi-projective domain of Bost–Gillet–Soulé (§2.1.2, p. 932), as for
  [arithmetic degrees](arithmetic-intersection-numbers.md), whose isomorphism
  $\widehat{\mathrm{Pic}}(X) \cong \widehat{CH}^1(X)$ is stated there only for regular $X$
  projective over $A$ (on the quasi-projective domain it is Annals 131, I, §2, as identified by
  Gillet–Soulé, IHÉS 72, §3.4.2, p. 131; see also Soulé's *Lectures*, chapter "Characteristic
  Classes");
- the arithmetic Grothendieck group $\widehat{K}_0(X)$, generated by pairs $(\bar E, \eta)$ with
  $\eta \in \bigoplus_p \widetilde{A}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$ subject to the relations given by exact
  sequences and their secondary classes (Inventiones, 2.4.2), and, on an arithmetic variety that
  need not be regular, over a good arithmetic ring, the action $\widehat{K}_0(X) \otimes
  \widehat{CH}_*(X) \to \widehat{CH}_*(X)_\mathbb{Q}$, $\alpha \otimes x \mapsto
  \widehat{\mathrm{ch}}(\alpha) \cap x$, on the arithmetic Chow groups graded by dimension, with
  rational values, and its properties 1–7 (Inventiones, Theorem 4 and its proof, pp. 495–499),
  together with the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(\bar E)$ of the Remarks on pp.
  498–499, an operator in the singular case;
- the arithmetic Chern character with supports $\widehat{\mathrm{ch}}{}_P^X(E_\bullet)$: over a good
  arithmetic ring, for a closed immersion $i \colon X \to P$ into an integral arithmetic variety and
  a chosen metric on the normal bundle $N_{X(\mathbb{C})/P(\mathbb{C})}$, the class of a bounded
  complex $E_\bullet$ of hermitian vector bundles on $P$, acyclic off $X$, whose restriction to
  $P_F$ resolves the direct image of a metrized vector bundle on $X_F$; it depends on both metrics
  (Inventiones, §3.1, Definition 6, p. 510), and for regular $P$ a resolution of $i_* \mathcal{F}$
  exists and the class does not depend on its choice (Corollary 3, pp. 510–511); with the comparison
  Lemma 21 (p. 512);
- the homological Riemann–Roch class $\tau(\mathcal{F}) \in \widehat{CH}_*(X)_\mathbb{Q}$ of a
  hermitian coherent sheaf, built from it for a closed immersion $i$ into a regular irreducible
  arithmetic variety $P$: $\tau_P(\mathcal{F}) = \widehat{\mathrm{ch}}{}_P^X(\mathcal{F}) \cdot_i
  \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(P) + a(\mathrm{ch}(\mathcal{F}_\mathbb{C}) \widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(X/P))$,
  with a Todd factor and a secondary term, which depends on the metric on $TX(\mathbb{C})$ but
  neither on the auxiliary metrics (Lemma 25) nor on the embedding (Theorem 5), and equals
  $\widehat{\mathrm{ch}} \cdot \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(X)$ when $X$ is regular (§3.2, Definition 7, p.
  516, Theorem 6 (i), (iii), p. 521); the two classes are not in general the same, even for $P = X$,
  where the Todd factor need not be trivial (for $\mathcal{O}$ on $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$
  they agree). The class $\tau$ is used in the coherent-sheaf form of the arithmetic Riemann–Roch
  theorem, for the sheaf; the symbol $\tau$ that Gillet and Soulé apply there to the graded
  determinant of cohomology is not this class of its underlying line bundle;
- the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f)$ of a local complete intersection morphism $f
  \colon X \to Y$ of arithmetic varieties, smooth on the generic fibre, with a hermitian metric on
  $Tf_\mathbb{C}$: for a factorization $f = g \circ i$ through $P = Y \times \mathbb{P}^N$ with $i$
  a regular immersion of normal bundle $N$, and the secondary class $\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(f/g) =
  \widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(\mathcal{E}) \, \mathrm{Td}(\bar N_\mathbb{C})^{-1}$ of the exact sequence
  $\mathcal{E} \colon 0 \to Tf_\mathbb{C} \to i^* Tg_\mathbb{C} \to N_\mathbb{C} \to 0$,
  $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f) = \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(i^* \overline{Tg}) \,
  \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}^{-1}(\bar N) + a(\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(f/g))$ when $X$ and $Y$ are regular
  (cap products in general), and its behaviour under composition (Inventiones, 2.6.2 and Proposition
  1, with the proof through p. 507). The class depends only on the metric on $Tf_\mathbb{C}$, not on
  the factorization and the auxiliary metrics on $Tg$ and $N$; for maps $f$, $g$ of regular
  arithmetic varieties, both smooth on the generic fibre, with metrics chosen on $Tf_\mathbb{C}$,
  $Tg_\mathbb{C}$ and $T(gf)_\mathbb{C}$, $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(g f) = \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f) \,
  f^* \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(g) - a(\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(0 \to Tf_\mathbb{C} \to T(gf)_\mathbb{C}
  \to f^* Tg_\mathbb{C} \to 0))$ (Proposition 1 (ii)).

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- the properties of the secondary Bott–Chern classes (Bismut–Gillet–Soulé, I; Gillet–Soulé,
  Inventiones, 2.4.1), and the splitting principle for hermitian vector bundles, for the arithmetic
  Chern classes
- the cap product $\widehat{CH}^p(P) \otimes \widehat{CH}_q(X) \to
  \widehat{CH}_{q-p}(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ for a map from $X$ to a regular $P$ (Inventiones, §2.3.1,
  Theorem 3), for the classes on a possibly singular $X$
- the Grassmann graph construction and its singular-current correction (Inventiones, §3.1,
  Definition 6), for the arithmetic Chern character with supports
