# Arithmetic characteristic classes of hermitian vector bundles

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the characteristic classes that Gillet and Soulé
attach to hermitian vector bundles $\bar E = (E, h)$ on arithmetic varieties: the arithmetic Chern
classes, the arithmetic Chern character $\widehat{\mathrm{ch}}(\bar E) \in \widehat{CH}^*(X)_\mathbb{Q}
= \bigoplus_{p \ge 0} \widehat{CH}^p(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ and the arithmetic Todd class
$\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(\bar E)$ on a regular arithmetic variety $X$, the secondary Bott–Chern
classes that measure their failure to be additive on exact sequences, and the arithmetic Todd class
$\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f)$ of a morphism, which enters the
[arithmetic Riemann–Roch theorem](arithmetic-riemann-roch-theorem.md). The classes are constructed
in Gillet and Soulé's papers in the Annals of Mathematics 131 (1990), recalled by Bost, Gillet and
Soulé (§2.1.2), and extended to homological arithmetic Chow groups of arithmetic varieties that are
not regular in Section 2 of Gillet and Soulé's Inventiones paper on the arithmetic Riemann–Roch
theorem, which this node follows.

A good expansion has pages for:

- the secondary Bott–Chern classes on a complex manifold (Inventiones, 2.4.1): for an exact
  sequence $\mathcal{E} \colon 0 \to S \to E \to Q \to 0$ of holomorphic vector bundles with
  arbitrary hermitian metrics, a class $\widetilde{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathcal{E}) \in
  \bigoplus_p A^{p,p}(X) / (\operatorname{im} \partial + \operatorname{im} \bar\partial)$ with
  $dd^c \widetilde{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathrm{ch}(\bar S) - \mathrm{ch}(\bar E) +
  \mathrm{ch}(\bar Q)$, where $\mathrm{ch}(\bar E) = \operatorname{tr} \exp(\frac{i}{2\pi}
  \nabla^2)$ is the Chern–Weil form, functorial and vanishing when $\mathcal{E}$ is metrically
  split, together with the analogous classes $\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(\mathcal{E})$ and
  $\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}^{-1}}(\mathcal{E})$ for the Todd class and its inverse, the classes of
  finite acyclic complexes, and the identities of Inventiones 2.6.1 (Lemma 14);
- the arithmetic Chern classes $\hat c_p(\bar E) \in \widehat{CH}^p(X)$ and the arithmetic Chern
  character of a hermitian vector bundle on a regular arithmetic variety, with $\hat c_1$ of a
  hermitian line bundle given by the isomorphism $\widehat{\mathrm{Pic}}(X) \cong \widehat{CH}^1(X)$
  of [arithmetic degrees](arithmetic-intersection-numbers.md) (Annals 131, as recalled in
  Bost–Gillet–Soulé §2.1.2 and in Soulé's *Lectures*, chapter "Characteristic Classes");
- the arithmetic Grothendieck group $\widehat{K}_0(X)$, generated by pairs $(\bar E, \eta)$ with
  $\eta \in \bigoplus_p \widetilde{A}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$ subject to the relations given by exact
  sequences and their secondary classes (Inventiones, 2.4.2), and the action
  $\alpha \otimes x \mapsto \widehat{\mathrm{ch}}(\alpha) \cap x$ of $\widehat{K}_0(X)$ on the
  homological arithmetic Chow groups with its properties 1–7 (Inventiones, Theorem 4 and its
  proof, pp. 495–499), together with the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(\bar E)$ of
  the Remarks on p. 499;
- the arithmetic Todd class $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f)$ of a local complete intersection morphism
  $f \colon X \to Y$ of arithmetic varieties, smooth on the generic fibre, with a hermitian metric
  on $Tf_\mathbb{C}$: for a factorization $f = g \circ i$ through $P = Y \times \mathbb{P}^N$ with
  $i$ a regular immersion of normal bundle $N$, and the secondary class
  $\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(f/g) = \widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(\mathcal{E}) \,
  \mathrm{Td}(\bar N_\mathbb{C})^{-1}$ of the exact sequence $\mathcal{E} \colon 0 \to Tf_\mathbb{C}
  \to i^* Tg_\mathbb{C} \to N_\mathbb{C} \to 0$,
  $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f) = \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(i^* \overline{Tg}) \,
  \widehat{\mathrm{Td}}^{-1}(\bar N) + a(\widetilde{\mathrm{Td}}(f/g))$ when $X$ and $Y$ are regular
  (cap products in general), which depends only on the metric on $Tf_\mathbb{C}$, and its behaviour
  under composition (Inventiones, 2.6.2 and Proposition 1).
