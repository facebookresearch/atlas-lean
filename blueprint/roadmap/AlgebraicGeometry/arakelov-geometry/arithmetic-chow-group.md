# Arithmetic Chow groups

Let $X$ be an arithmetic variety over an arithmetic ring $(A, \Sigma, F_\infty)$ whose generic fibre
$X_F$ is smooth and quasi-projective. For $p \ge 0$, $Z^p(X)$ is the free abelian group on the
integral closed subschemes of $X$ of codimension $p$; for an integral subscheme $W$ of codimension
$p - 1$ and $f \in k(W)^\times$, $\operatorname{div}(f) \in Z^p(X)$ is the divisor of $f$, and
$CH^p(X)$ is the quotient of $Z^p(X)$ by the subgroup generated by these divisors. An integral
subscheme $Y$ of codimension $p$ defines an analytic subspace $Y_\infty$ of $X_\infty$, empty when
$Y$ does not meet $X_F$ and stable under $F_\infty$; integration over $Y_\infty$ is a current
$\delta_Y \in \mathcal{D}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$, and $Z \mapsto \delta_Z$ extends linearly to
$Z^p(X)$.

Put $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) = \mathcal{D}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) /
(\operatorname{im} \partial + \operatorname{im} \bar\partial)$ and
$\widetilde{A}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) = A^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) / (\operatorname{im} \partial +
\operatorname{im} \bar\partial)$; the operator $dd^c$ factors through these quotients, and
$\widetilde{A}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) \to \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$ is injective. A
Green current for $Z \in Z^p(X)$ is an element $g \in
\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$ such that

$$dd^c g + \delta_Z = \omega(Z, g)$$

for a smooth form $\omega(Z, g) \in A^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$. Every cycle has a Green current: for an
irreducible cycle of positive codimension one takes the class of a
[Green form of logarithmic type](green-forms-of-logarithmic-type.md), averaged under $F_\infty$ to
lie in the real parity subspace, and for $p = 0$ the class $0$, since $\delta_Z$ is then smooth. Two
Green currents for the same cycle differ by an element of $\widetilde{A}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$
(Gillet–Soulé, Lemma 1.2.4). The basic example is a divisor: if $s$ is a meromorphic section of a
holomorphic line bundle with a smooth hermitian metric on a complex manifold, not identically zero
on any connected component, then $\log \lVert s \rVert^2$ is locally integrable and $dd^c [\log
\lVert s \rVert^2] = \delta_{\operatorname{div}(s)} - \beta$, where $\beta$ is the first Chern form
of the metric, so that $-\log \lVert s \rVert^2$ is a Green current for $\operatorname{div}(s)$.

An arithmetic cycle of codimension $p$ is a pair $(Z, g)$ of a cycle $Z \in Z^p(X)$ and a Green
current $g$ for $Z$; these pairs form a subgroup $\widehat{Z}^p(X)$ of $Z^p(X) \oplus
\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$. Let $i \colon W \to X$ be an integral subscheme of
codimension $p - 1$ and $f \in k(W)^\times$. If $W$ does not meet $X_F$, then $W_\infty$ is empty
and the current $i_*[\log |f|^2]$ below is $0$. Otherwise, for a resolution of singularities $\pi
\colon \widetilde W_\infty \to W_\infty$ with $\pi$ proper, a disjoint union of resolutions of the
irreducible components of $W_\infty$, $f$ restricts to a rational function $\tilde f$ on $\widetilde
W_\infty$, the function $\log |\tilde f|^2$ is locally integrable, and its push-forward $i_*[\log
|f|^2] \in \mathcal{D}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$ along $\widetilde W_\infty \to X_\infty$ does not
depend on the resolution. Write $i_* \log |f|^2$ for its class in
$\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$. Then $dd^c \, i_*[\log |f|^2] =
\delta_{\operatorname{div}(f)}$, so

$$\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f) = \bigl( \operatorname{div}(f), -i_* \log |f|^2 \bigr)$$

is an arithmetic cycle. The arithmetic Chow groups of $X$ are

$$\widehat{CH}^p(X) = \widehat{Z}^p(X) / \widehat{R}^p(X), \qquad p \ge 0,$$

where $\widehat{R}^p(X)$ is the subgroup generated by the $\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f)$ for all
such $W$ and $f$, and $\widehat{CH}^*(X) = \bigoplus_{p \ge 0} \widehat{CH}^p(X)$.

These groups come with three maps:

1. $\zeta \colon \widehat{CH}^p(X) \to CH^p(X)$, $(Z, g) \mapsto Z$, well defined because
   $\zeta(\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f)) = \operatorname{div}(f)$; it is surjective, since every
   cycle has a Green current;
2. $a \colon \widetilde{A}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R}) \to \widehat{CH}^p(X)$, $\eta \mapsto (0, \eta)$,
   whose image is the kernel of $\zeta$, because two Green currents for the same cycle differ by a
   smooth form (Gillet–Soulé, first paragraph of the proof of the Theorem after 3.3.5); $a$ need not
   be injective;
3. $\omega \colon \widehat{CH}^p(X) \to A^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$, $(Z, g) \mapsto \delta_Z + dd^c g$,
   well defined because $\omega(\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f)) = 0$.

Bost, Gillet and Soulé work over $S = \operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}_K$ with arithmetic varieties
flat and quasi-projective over $S$ with regular generic fibre. On a regular such $X$ they take for
$g$ the current itself, not its class modulo $\operatorname{im} \partial + \operatorname{im}
\bar\partial$, and add the pairs $(0, \partial u + \bar\partial v)$ to the relations; on their
common domain the quotient is the same group. When $X$ itself need not be regular, its singularities
lying over finite places, they use instead arithmetic Chow groups graded by dimension, with
operational Chern classes (Bost–Gillet–Soulé §2.1.2; Gillet–Soulé, *An arithmetic Riemann–Roch
theorem*, §2.1, Definition 2, and §2.3.1, Theorem 3).

## Depends on

- [Arithmetic varieties and hermitian vector bundles](arithmetic-variety.md)
- [Poincaré–Lelong formula](../birational-geometry/poincare-lelong-formula.md), for $dd^c \, i_*[\log |f|^2] = \delta_{\operatorname{div}(f)}$, which makes $\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f)$ an arithmetic cycle (Gillet–Soulé 3.3.3), and for the Green current $-\log \lVert s \rVert^2$ of $\operatorname{div}(s)$ (Gillet–Soulé 1.3.1)
- [Resolution of singularities of quasi-projective varieties](../birational-geometry/resolution-of-singularities/resolution-of-singularities-of-quasi-projective-varieties.md), for the resolution $\widetilde W_\infty \to W_\infty$ through which $i_* \log |f|^2$ is defined (Gillet–Soulé 3.3.3)
- [Currents of integration and the support theorems](../birational-geometry/currents-of-integration.md),
  for the currents of integration $\delta_Y$

## Proof depends on

- [Green forms of logarithmic type and the star product](green-forms-of-logarithmic-type.md), for
  Gillet and Soulé's Theorem 1.2.2 and Lemma 1.2.4 (item 4), which make
  $\widetilde{A}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R}) \to \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}^{p,p}(X_\mathbb{R})$ injective and
  show that two Green currents for the same cycle differ by an element of
  $\widetilde{A}^{p-1,p-1}(X_\mathbb{R})$, and for the existence of Green currents (item 1,
  Gillet–Soulé, Theorem 1.3.5), which makes $\zeta$ surjective
- [Poincaré–Lelong formula](../birational-geometry/poincare-lelong-formula.md), for $dd^c \,
  i_*[\log |f|^2] = \delta_{\operatorname{div}(f)}$, which gives
  $\omega(\widehat{\operatorname{div}}(f)) = 0$
- [Resolution of singularities of quasi-projective varieties](../birational-geometry/resolution-of-singularities/resolution-of-singularities-of-quasi-projective-varieties.md),
  for the independence of $i_*[\log |f|^2]$ from the resolution, two resolutions being dominated by
  a third
