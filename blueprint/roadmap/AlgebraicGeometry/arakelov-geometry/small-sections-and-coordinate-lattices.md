# Small sections and lattices of homogeneous coordinate rings

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the comparison between small sections of powers of a
hermitian line bundle on a projective arithmetic variety and small elements of the lattices of
homogeneous elements of a coordinate ring defining it, which turns the arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel
formula for nef hermitian line bundles and Moriwaki's lower bound for the arithmetic volume into bounds
for counts of small elements of these lattices.

Let $X$ be an integral subscheme of a product of projective spaces over the ring of integers of a number
field, flat over $\mathbb{Z}$ of absolute dimension $D + 1$, defined by a prime multihomogeneous ideal,
and $\bar L$ the restriction of a hermitian $\mathcal{O}(1, \dots, 1)$ with relatively semipositive
and conjugation-invariant metric. The targets are:

1. if $\bar L$ is nef on the fibres over the closed points of $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$ and has
   nonnegative degree on every horizontal curve, then $(\bar L^{D+1}) \ge 0$;
2. if $\bar L$ is generated by small sections and has positive geometric degree, the $\limsup$ of
   $\log \#\{\text{small elements of degree } n\} / (n^{D+1} / (D + 1)!)$ over the lattices of
   homogeneous elements of degree $n$ of the coordinate ring is at most $(\bar L^{D+1})$;
3. if $\bar L$ is nef on the generic fibre and on the closed fibres, $(\bar L^{D+1})$ is at most the
   corresponding $\limsup$, the arithmetic volume computed with these lattices.

A good expansion has pages for: the surjectivity, for large $n$, of the restriction from homogeneous
polynomials of degree $n$ with integral coefficients onto $H^0(X, L^{\otimes n})$ up to a bounded
index; the comparison of the quotient norms on the coordinate lattices with sup norms of sections, by
[Gromov's inequality](gromov-inequality.md) and the estimates for
[normed lattices](small-elements-of-normed-lattices.md); the deduction of 1 and 2 from the
[nef Hilbert–Samuel formula](arithmetic-hilbert-samuel-formula-for-nef-hermitian-line-bundles.md) and
of 3 from [Moriwaki's generalized Hodge index theorem](generalized-hodge-index-theorem.md). No single
source states these bounds for coordinate lattices.
