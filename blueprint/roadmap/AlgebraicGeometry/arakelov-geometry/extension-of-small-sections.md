# Extension of small sections and bases of small sections

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the steps of Zhang's proof of his
[arithmetic ampleness theorem](arithmetic-ampleness-theorem.md) other than the arithmetic
Hilbert–Samuel theorem: the extension of small sections from a subvariety with control of their norms,
the small sections of semiample metrized line bundles, and the passage from strictly small sections on
every horizontal subvariety to bases of small sections, through filtrations of lattices and their
successive minima.

A good expansion has pages for:

- the $L^2$ estimates for $\bar\partial$ with weights on the complex points (Zhang, (2.2)–(2.4),
  pp. 193–194, after Hörmander; the [L² estimates for the ∂̄ operator](../birational-geometry/l2-estimates-for-the-dbar-operator.md)
  node contains the underlying existence theorem) and the resulting extension of sections from a closed
  subvariety with an estimate of the sup norm (Zhang, (3.3));
- the small sections of relatively semiample metrized line bundles (Zhang, (3.5));
- the construction of bases of small sections from strictly small sections on each horizontal
  subvariety, with lattice filtrations and successive minima (Zhang, (4.2) and its proof (4.3)–(4.7),
  pp. 205–207), and Zhang's Corollary (4.8), pp. 207–208.
