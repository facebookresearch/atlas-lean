# Arithmetic intersection theory, heights and arithmetic volumes

This node covers the intersection theory of arithmetic varieties and its applications to heights and
to the counting of small sections. An arithmetic variety $X$ is a scheme that is flat and of finite
type over $\mathbb{Z}$ (or over the ring of integers of a number field) with smooth generic fibre;
below $X$ is regular and projective of dimension $d + 1$, except in the statements on the
Hilbert–Samuel formula, volumes and ampleness, where, as in Zhang, Moriwaki and Yuan, $X$ is an
integral scheme of dimension $d + 1$ that is projective and flat over $\mathbb{Z}$, its arithmetic
intersection numbers being defined through a generic resolution of singularities. A hermitian line
bundle $\bar L = (L, \lVert \cdot \rVert)$ carries a smooth metric on $L_{\mathbb{C}}$ invariant under
complex conjugation, and $d^c = \frac{i}{4\pi}(\bar\partial - \partial)$.

- **Arithmetic Chow groups** (Gillet–Soulé). An arithmetic cycle of codimension $p$ is a pair
  $(Z, g)$ of a cycle $Z$ of codimension $p$ and the class $g$, modulo
  $\operatorname{im} \partial + \operatorname{im} \bar\partial$, of a Green current for $Z$, that is,
  of a current $g$ with $dd^c g + \delta_Z$ smooth. The group $\widehat{CH}^p(X)$ is the quotient of
  the group of arithmetic cycles by the subgroup generated by the pairs
  $(\operatorname{div} f, -i_* \log \lvert f \rvert^2)$, for integral subschemes
  $i \colon W \to X$ of codimension $p - 1$ and rational functions $f$ on $W$, where
  $i_* \log \lvert f \rvert^2$ is the push-forward of the current $\log \lvert f \rvert^2$ on $W$.
  Equivalently, one may work with currents themselves and add the pairs
  $(0, \partial u + \bar\partial v)$ to the relations. For $X$ regular with quasi-projective
  generic fibre there is a product
  $\widehat{CH}^p(X) \otimes \widehat{CH}^q(X) \to \widehat{CH}^{p+q}(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ making
  $\widehat{CH}^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ a commutative associative ring; $\widehat{CH}^1(X)$ is the group of
  isomorphism classes of hermitian line bundles; and a proper morphism to the spectrum of a ring of
  integers that is smooth on generic fibres gives arithmetic degrees and intersection numbers. Hermitian
  vector bundles have arithmetic Chern classes (Gillet–Soulé).
- **Arithmetic Riemann–Roch** (Gillet–Soulé). Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be a projective morphism of
  regular arithmetic varieties whose restriction to the generic fibre is smooth, choose on the
  relative tangent bundle $T_{f_{\mathbb{C}}}$ of $X(\mathbb{C}) \to Y(\mathbb{C})$ a hermitian metric
  that is Kähler on the fibres, and let $\bar E$ be a hermitian vector bundle on $X$. The
  determinant of cohomology $\lambda(E)$ with its Quillen metric $h_Q$ satisfies
  $\hat c_1(\lambda(E), h_Q) = f_*\bigl(\widehat{\mathrm{ch}}(\bar E)\,\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f) - a(\mathrm{ch}(E_{\mathbb{C}})\,\mathrm{Td}(T_{f_{\mathbb{C}}})\,R(T_{f_{\mathbb{C}}}))\bigr)^{(1)}$
  in $\widehat{CH}^1(Y)_{\mathbb{Q}}$. Here $\widehat{\mathrm{Td}}(f)$ is the arithmetic Todd class of
  $f$, defined from its relative tangent complex (no relative tangent bundle on $X$ is needed), and
  $R$ is the additive characteristic class of Gillet and Soulé, built from the values
  $\zeta'(-m)$ and $\zeta(-m)$ at odd $m \ge 1$.
- **Arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel formula.** Suppose that $L_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is ample and that $\bar L$ is
  relatively semipositive: $L$ has nonnegative degree on every closed curve in a fibre of $X$ over
  $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$, and the curvature of the metric is semipositive. Then the Euler
  characteristic
  $\chi_{\sup}(H^0(X, L^n)) = -\log \operatorname{covol}(H^0(X, L^n)) + \log \operatorname{vol}(\text{unit ball})$
  of the lattice $H^0(X, L^n)$ for the sup norm equals
  $\frac{(\bar L^{d+1})}{(d+1)!}\, n^{d+1} + o(n^{d+1})$ (Gillet–Soulé for $L^2$ norms and positive
  curvature, Abbes–Bouche, Zhang). By Minkowski's theorem the number of sections of sup norm at most
  $1$ satisfies $\log \#\{s : \lVert s \rVert_{\sup} \le 1\} \ge \chi_{\sup}(H^0(X, L^n)) + O(n^d)$, and
  for nef $\bar L$ it equals $\frac{(\bar L^{d+1})}{(d+1)!}\, n^{d+1} + o(n^{d+1})$ (Moriwaki).
- **Heights of cycles.** For a cycle $Z$ of dimension $p$ on $X$, the dimension being that of the
  arithmetic variety (a cycle flat over $\mathbb{Z}$ has generic fibre of dimension $p - 1$), the
  height $h_{\bar L}(Z) = \widehat{\deg}(\hat c_1(\bar L)^p \mid Z)$ is a real number; on projective
  space with the Fubini–Study metric it is the height introduced by Faltings (Bost–Gillet–Soulé).
- **Arithmetic volume.** The volume
  $\widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(\bar L) = \limsup_{m \to \infty} \frac{\log \#\{s \in H^0(X, mL) : \lVert s \rVert_{\sup} \le 1\}}{m^{d+1}/(d+1)!}$
  is positive exactly when $\bar L$ is big, is continuous on hermitian $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundles in
  the sense that $\widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(\bar L + \epsilon \bar A) \to \widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(\bar L)$
  as $\epsilon \to 0$ through rational numbers, and equals $(\bar L^{d+1})$ for nef $\bar L$
  (Moriwaki). For nef $\bar L$ and $\bar M$,
  $\widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(\bar L - \bar M) \ge (\bar L^{d+1}) - (d+1)(\bar L^d \cdot \bar M)$
  (Yuan for $\bar L$, $\bar M$ ample in Zhang's sense; Moriwaki for nef ones).
- **Arithmetic ampleness** (Zhang). If $X$ has regular generic fibre, $L_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is ample,
  $\bar L$ is relatively semipositive and $(\hat c_1(\bar L)\vert_Y)^{\dim Y} > 0$ for every
  horizontal irreducible subvariety $Y$, then for every hermitian line bundle $\bar M$ and $n$ large
  the lattice $H^0(X, L^n \otimes M)$ has a basis of sections of sup norm less than $1$.

Heights of algebraic numbers and of points of projective space, and Arakelov divisors of number
fields, are outside this node.

## Expansion

A good expansion replaces this node by definition pages and statements in the following order,
each citing the source and locator below.

1. Arithmetic varieties, Green currents, arithmetic cycles and the arithmetic Chow groups, with the
   intersection product, the arithmetic degree and arithmetic intersection numbers. The
   Poincaré–Lelong formula they use is described under
   [Birational geometry and vanishing theorems](../birational-geometry/README.md); the case of
   number fields is under
   [Arakelov divisors](arakelov-divisors/README.md).
2. Hermitian vector bundles and their arithmetic Chern classes, the determinant of cohomology and
   the Quillen metric, and the arithmetic Riemann–Roch theorem.
3. Heights of cycles, and their comparison with the heights under
   [Heights and Mahler measure](../../NumberTheory/heights-mahler-measure/README.md).
4. The arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel formula, Minkowski's theorem for counting small sections, the
   arithmetic volume and its continuity, Yuan's inequality, and arithmetic ampleness, each with the
   definitions of semipositive, nef, big and ample hermitian line bundles that its source uses.
