# Covolume bound for the families of characters

This node is still to be broken down. It covers a bound for covolumes that the
[definite-side hypotheses](../../RepresentationTheory/automorphic-representations/definite-side-hypotheses-for-character-families.md)
use: for every family in a class of families of auxiliary number fields attached to scales (the
families of the
[window bounds for weighted Chebyshev sums](../../NumberTheory/distribution-of-primes/primes-in-progressions/chebyshev-window-bounds-for-cyclic-character-families.md)),
there is a constant $C_0 \ge 0$ such that, at every point of the family beyond some scale that
satisfies the point conditions and the tie condition of the argument, an inequality for the covolume
of a lattice that the argument attaches to the point holds for every constant $C \ge C_0$. Points
that fail these conditions are not constrained. Its proof is an argument in arithmetic geometry: it
is assembled from resolution of singularities and principalization in characteristic $0$, vanishing
theorems, the Poincaré–Lelong formula, arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorems and arithmetic degrees,
heights of cycles, toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli spaces and further inputs from the
geometry of abelian schemes and $p$-divisible groups.

It is not yet stated as a page because the families, the points, their conditions and the lattices
attached to them are specific to this proof and defined through many auxiliary notions; a reviewable
statement needs those notions stated and checked first.

A good expansion has definition pages for the points of the families, their conditions and their
lattices, a page for the bound, and pages for the steps of its proof, linking the published results
it uses: those of the pages below, among them Fargues's integral Hodge–Tate sequence for
$p$-divisible groups; and pages, still to be added, for the Hodge filtration of prismatic Dieudonné
modules and for Green's generic irreducible characters of $\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb{F}_p)$ attached to
polynomials, which extend the cuspidal case.

## Proof depends on

- [Principalization of ideal sheaves in characteristic zero](../birational-geometry/resolution-of-singularities/principalization-of-ideal-sheaves.md), for an empty boundary
- [Principalization relative to a simple normal crossing divisor](../birational-geometry/resolution-of-singularities/principalization-relative-to-a-simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md), for an arbitrary simple normal crossing boundary, with an isomorphism off the cosupport of the ideal
- [Functorial resolution of schemes of finite type in characteristic zero](../birational-geometry/resolution-of-singularities/functorial-resolution-of-schemes-of-finite-type.md), for reduced schemes
- [Integrated Poincaré–Lelong formula](../birational-geometry/integrated-poincare-lelong-formula.md), on compact complex manifolds
- [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](../birational-geometry/kawamata-viehweg-vanishing-theorem.md), for $D = 0$ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero
- [Nadel vanishing for pairs and separation of points by sections](../birational-geometry/nadel-vanishing-for-klt-pairs-and-point-separation.md), for Nadel vanishing on normal varieties, isolated non-klt centres and the separation of two points
- [L² extension from subvarieties defined by log canonical weights](../birational-geometry/l2-extension-from-log-canonical-subvarieties.md), for the extension from finite sets of transverse common zeros of sections, which bounds the residue measure from above
- [Intersection numbers and Riemann–Roch for nef line bundles](../birational-geometry/intersection-numbers-of-nef-line-bundles.md), for projective schemes over algebraically closed fields of any characteristic; the limit of the ranks of normalized chart lattices, also used, is unsourced
- [Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties](../birational-geometry/standard-results-of-coherent-cohomology.md), for GAGA for sections of line bundles (Serre's theorem on holomorphic sections)
- [Poincaré–Lelong formula on arithmetic cycles](poincare-lelong-formula-on-arithmetic-cycles.md), for its form on possibly singular horizontal cycles, summed over the horizontal components of the divisor with their lengths, which is unsourced
- [Arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel formula for nef hermitian line bundles](arithmetic-volumes/arithmetic-hilbert-samuel-formula-for-nef-hermitian-line-bundles.md), for the asymptotic of the logarithm of the number of small sections of $m\bar L + \bar N$, with a fixed twist $\bar N$
- [Moriwaki's generalized Hodge index theorem](arithmetic-volumes/generalized-hodge-index-theorem.md), for its corollary on hermitian line bundles that are nef on the fibres and have semipositive curvature (Moriwaki's bigness criterion)
- [Arithmetic degree and arithmetic intersection numbers](arithmetic-intersection-numbers.md), for the change of metric and the projection formula
- [Geometric degrees as integrals of first Chern forms](geometric-degrees-as-integrals-of-chern-forms.md), for the unweighted identity
- [Height of a cycle](height-of-a-cycle.md), for heights of cycles, their normalization by the geometric degree and their projection formula
- [Heights of Chow forms](heights-of-chow-forms.md), for the existence of Chow forms and the comparison of the height of a cycle with the Fubini–Study height of its Chow form
- [Small sections and lattices of homogeneous coordinate rings](arithmetic-volumes/small-sections-and-coordinate-lattices.md), for the nonnegativity and the three bounds for small elements of chart lattices, which are unsourced
- [Good hermitian metrics and Chern numbers](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/good-hermitian-metrics-and-chern-numbers.md), for the Chern integrals of good metrics and the flag push-forward identity on flag refinements (Mumford's proportionality), unsourced in the form used
- [Hodge bundle on toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli schemes](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/hodge-bundle-on-siegel-toroidal-compactifications.md), for odd level $n \ge 3$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, with one boundary divisor for all charts and for the ample twist
- [Toroidal compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/toroidal-compactifications-of-unitary-shimura-varieties.md), for the charts given by a fan and the logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer isomorphism on every affine open of the compactification
- [Homomorphisms and Lie algebras of semi-abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/homomorphisms-and-lie-algebras-of-semi-abelian-schemes.md), for the extension of homomorphisms over normal bases and the seminegativity of the Lie algebra over a curve in characteristic zero
- [Line bundles on abelian varieties](../abelian-varieties/line-bundles-on-abelian-varieties.md), for the global sections of a principal polarization over an algebraically closed field
- [Isogenies of abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/isogenies-of-abelian-schemes.md), for the number of $n$-torsion points over a separably closed field of characteristic prime to $n$
- [Dual abelian scheme](../abelian-varieties/dual-abelian-scheme.md), for the dual over an arbitrary base, with its Poincaré bundle, biduality and the duals of homomorphisms
- [Zarhin's trick for abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/zarhin-trick-for-abelian-schemes.md), over the valuation rings of real-valued valuations, with a symmetric polarizing line bundle
- [Semistable reduction theorem for abelian varieties](../abelian-varieties/semistable-reduction-theorem.md), with the identity component of the Néron model as semi-abelian model
- [Deformations of abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/deformations-and-p-divisible-groups/deformations-of-abelian-schemes.md), for Grothendieck–Messing deformation theory, over rings in which $p$ is nilpotent and, unsourced, over arbitrary rings
- [De Rham cohomology of abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/deformations-and-p-divisible-groups/de-rham-cohomology-of-abelian-schemes.md), with the annihilator filtration, unsourced over arbitrary rings
- [Grothendieck's existence theorem](../abelian-varieties/deformations-and-p-divisible-groups/grothendieck-existence-theorem.md), for the algebraization of morphisms and of proper formal schemes with a line bundle ample on the closed fibre
- [Hodge–Tate sequence of a p-divisible group](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/galois-representations/p-adic-hodge-theory/hodge-tate-sequence-of-p-divisible-groups.md), for Fargues's integral identification of the kernel of the Hodge–Tate map with the annihilator of the kernel of the dual map; the cokernel bound comes from a separate estimate
- [Green's parametrization of the cuspidal characters of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$](../../GroupTheory/characters-of-finite-groups/green-cuspidal-characters-of-gl-n.md), for the case of a prime field, from which the generic irreducible characters attached to polynomials are obtained
- [Existence theorem of global class field theory](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/existence-theorem/existence-of-class-fields.md), for the ray class fields
