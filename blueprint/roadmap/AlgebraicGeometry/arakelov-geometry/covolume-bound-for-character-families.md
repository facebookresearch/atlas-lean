# Covolume bound for the families of characters

This node is still to be broken down. It covers a bound for covolumes that the
[definite-side hypotheses](../../RepresentationTheory/automorphic-representations/definite-side-hypotheses-for-character-families.md)
use: for every family in a class of families of auxiliary number fields attached to scales (the
families of the
[window bounds for weighted Chebyshev sums](../../NumberTheory/distribution-of-primes/primes-in-progressions/chebyshev-window-bounds-for-cyclic-character-families.md)),
there is a constant $C_0 \ge 0$ such that, at every point of the family beyond some scale that
satisfies the point conditions and the tie condition of the argument, an inequality for the covolume
of a lattice that the argument attaches to the point holds for every constant $C \ge C_0$. Points
that fail these conditions are not constrained. Its proof is an argument in arithmetic geometry: it
is assembled from resolution of singularities and principalization in characteristic $0$, vanishing
theorems, the Poincaré–Lelong formula, arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theorems and arithmetic degrees,
heights of cycles, toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli spaces and further inputs from the
geometry of abelian schemes and $p$-divisible groups.

It is held as a node because the families, the points, their conditions and the lattices
attached to them are specific to this proof and defined through many auxiliary notions; a reviewable
statement needs those notions stated and checked first.

A good expansion has definition pages for the points of the families, their conditions and their
lattices, a page for the bound, and pages for the steps of its proof, linking the inputs it uses:
those of the pages below, some of them in exact variants that are still unsourced, among them
Fargues's integral Hodge–Tate sequence for $p$-divisible groups; and pages for
the Hodge filtration of prismatic Dieudonné modules and for Green's generic irreducible characters of
$\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb{F}_p)$ attached to squarefree polynomials with nonzero constant term, which
extend the cuspidal case.

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links the following, each as Alpöge's library states it at the
locators under Sources:

- the class of families of auxiliary number fields attached to scales, which are the families of the window bounds for weighted Chebyshev sums (that node uses this one through the definite-side hypotheses), for the families that the bound quantifies over
- the points of a family, with their point conditions and the tie condition, for the points at which the bound holds
- the lattice that the argument attaches to each point, for the covolume that the statement bounds

## Depends on

Its statement uses the notions listed under Expansion.

- [Moriwaki's generalized Hodge index theorem](arithmetic-volumes/generalized-hodge-index-theorem.md), for its corollary on hermitian line bundles that are nef on the fibres and have semipositive curvature (Moriwaki's bigness criterion)

- [Heights of Chow forms](heights-of-chow-forms.md), for the existence and uniqueness of Chow forms over fields and the comparison of the height of a cycle with the height of its Chow form (Bost–Gillet–Soulé); the integral variant of the existence of Chow forms and their compatibility with hyperplane sections, which the proof also uses, are separate items there, and the upper bound for the arithmetic self-intersection in the form used is recorded there as a target that no single source states
- [Small sections and lattices of homogeneous coordinate rings](arithmetic-volumes/small-sections-and-coordinate-lattices.md), for the nonnegativity and the three bounds for small elements of chart lattices, which are unsourced
- [Good hermitian metrics and Chern numbers](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/good-hermitian-metrics-and-chern-numbers.md), for the Chern integrals of good metrics and the flag push-forward identity on flag refinements (Mumford's proportionality), unsourced in the form used
- [Hodge bundle on toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli schemes](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/hodge-bundle-on-siegel-toroidal-compactifications.md), for the form of these compactifications and of the positivity of their Hodge bundle that the proof uses, which that node records as its target: odd level $n \ge 3$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, with one boundary divisor for all charts and for the ample twist; the published constructions it outlines give the compactifications, their charts and the ampleness of a twist by a boundary divisor, and no source is identified there for taking that divisor to be the one of the charts
- [Toroidal compactifications of unitary Shimura varieties](../abelian-varieties/moduli-and-compactifications/toroidal-compactifications-of-unitary-shimura-varieties.md), for the form that the proof uses, which that node records as its targets: charts given by a fan and the logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer isomorphism on every affine open of the compactification, which the constructions of Lan and of Madapusi Pera do not state in this form
- [Homomorphisms and Lie algebras of semi-abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/homomorphisms-and-lie-algebras-of-semi-abelian-schemes.md), for the extension of homomorphisms over normal bases (Faltings–Chai) and the seminegativity of the Lie algebra over a curve in characteristic zero, for which that node has identified no source in this generality
- [Dual abelian scheme](../abelian-varieties/dual-abelian-scheme.md), for the dual over an arbitrary base, with its Poincaré bundle, biduality and the duals of homomorphisms
- [Zarhin's trick for abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/zarhin-trick-for-abelian-schemes.md), for its form over the valuation rings of real-valued valuations, with a symmetric line bundle inducing the principal polarization (the second target there), which asks more than the published theorem of Moret-Bailly (the first target) and needs a bridge for which that node has identified no source
- [Deformations of abelian schemes](../abelian-varieties/deformations-and-p-divisible-groups/deformations-of-abelian-schemes.md), for Grothendieck–Messing deformation theory, over rings in which $p$ is nilpotent and, unsourced, over arbitrary rings

- [Green's parametrization of the cuspidal characters of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$](../../GroupTheory/characters-of-finite-groups/green-cuspidal-characters-of-gl-n.md), for the case of a prime field, the cuspidal characters; the extension to the generic characters attached to squarefree polynomials with nonzero constant term, which the proof also uses, is a separate statement, for which the plan above names a page
