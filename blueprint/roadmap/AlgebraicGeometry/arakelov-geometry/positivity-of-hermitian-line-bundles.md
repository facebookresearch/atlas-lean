# Positivity of hermitian line bundles

Let $X$ be an arithmetic variety of dimension $d + 1$ in the sense of Zhang, Moriwaki and Yuan (an
integral scheme, projective and flat over $\mathbb{Z}$) and $\bar L = (L, \lVert \cdot \rVert)$ a
hermitian line bundle on $X$.

*Semipositivity and Zhang's ampleness.* The metric of $\bar L$, or its curvature, is semipositive
if for every analytic map $f$ from the unit polydisc of $\mathbb{C}^d$ to $X(\mathbb{C})$ the
curvature of $f^* \bar L$ is semipositive. The hermitian line bundle $\bar L$ is relatively
semipositive if its curvature is semipositive and $\deg(L|_C) \ge 0$ for every closed curve $C$
contained in a fibre of $X$ over a closed point of $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$; the second
condition says that $L$ is nef on every such fibre. If $\bar L$ is relatively semipositive, then
$L_\mathbb{Q}$ is nef. Following Zhang, $\bar L$ is ample if

1. $L_\mathbb{Q}$ is ample;
2. $\bar L$ is relatively semipositive;
3. $\bar L$ is horizontally positive: $(\hat c_1(\bar L|_Y)^{\dim Y}) > 0$ for every horizontal
   irreducible closed subvariety $Y$ of $X$, that is, every one that dominates
   $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$.

If $L_\mathbb{Q}$ is ample and $\bar L$ is relatively semipositive, then $(L, e^{-c} \lVert \cdot
\rVert)$ is ample for every sufficiently large real number $c$. A nonzero section
$s \in H^0(X, L)$ is effective if $\lVert s \rVert_{\sup} \le 1$ and strictly effective if
$\lVert s \rVert_{\sup} < 1$; $\bar L$ is effective, or strictly effective, if it has such a
section. Yuan calls $\bar L$ big if there are $c > 0$ and $N_0$ such that
$\log \#\{ s \in H^0(X, L^{\otimes N}) : \lVert s \rVert_{\sup} < 1 \} > c N^{d+1}$ for all
$N > N_0$, and strongly big if $\chi_{\sup}(\bar L^{\otimes N}) > c N^{d+1}$ for all $N > N_0$.

*Moriwaki's ample, nef and big hermitian line bundles.* Moriwaki calls $\bar L$

- ample if $L$ is ample on $X$, the first Chern form $c_1(\bar L)$ is positive on $X(\mathbb{C})$,
  and for every sufficiently large $n$ the sheaf $L^{\otimes n}$ is generated by sections
  $s \in H^0(X, L^{\otimes n})$ with $\lVert s \rVert_{\sup} < 1$;
- nef if $c_1(\bar L)$ is semipositive and $\widehat{\deg}(\bar L|_\Gamma) \ge 0$ for every
  one-dimensional closed subscheme $\Gamma$ of $X$;
- big if $L_\mathbb{Q}$ is big on $X_\mathbb{Q}$ and some $H^0(X, L^{\otimes n})$, $n \ge 1$, has a
  nonzero section $s$ with $\lVert s \rVert_{\sup} < 1$.

If $\bar L$ is ample in this sense, then for every sufficiently large $n$ the $\mathbb{Z}$-module
$H^0(X, L^{\otimes n})$ has a basis of sections of sup norm less than $1$. An element of
$\widehat{\mathrm{Pic}}(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, a hermitian $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle, is ample, nef
or big if some positive multiple of it is represented by an ample, nef or big hermitian line
bundle. If $\bar L$ is nef and $\bar A$ is ample, then $\bar L + \varepsilon \bar A$ is ample for
every rational $\varepsilon > 0$. Moriwaki shows that his bigness is equivalent to Yuan's
(Theorem 4.5 and Remark 4.6, stated in [Arithmetic volume](arithmetic-volume.md)).
