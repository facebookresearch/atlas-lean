# Ampleness of nef plus ample hermitian line bundles

This node is still to be broken down: the statement is precise, and the plan below derives it from
published results.

Let $X$ be a projective arithmetic variety in Moriwaki's sense, an integral scheme projective and
flat over $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$ whose generic fibre need not be smooth, and let $\bar L$
and $\bar A$ be $C^\infty$-hermitian line bundles on $X$, with $\bar L$ nef and $\bar A$ ample in
Moriwaki's sense (see
[positivity of hermitian line bundles](positivity-of-hermitian-line-bundles.md)). Then $b \bar L + a
\bar A$ is ample for all positive integers $a$ and $b$; equivalently, $\bar L + \varepsilon \bar A$
is an ample hermitian $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle for every rational $\varepsilon > 0$.

Moriwaki uses this without proof in *Continuity of volumes*: in Claim 5.5.1 of the proof of his
Corollary 5.5, for $\bar L + \varepsilon \bar A$ and for $p \bar L - (k \bar L - \bar A) = (p - k)
\bar L + \bar A$ with $p \ge k$, and in the proof of his Theorem 5.6, for $\bar L + \varepsilon \bar
A$ and $\bar M + \varepsilon \bar A$ with $\bar L$ and $\bar M$ nef (pp. 38–39). The case $a = 1$,
$b = p - k$ is the second form, and $p = k$ is $\bar A$ itself.
[Zhang's arithmetic ampleness theorem](arithmetic-ampleness-theorem.md) does not give it alone: it
starts from a bundle that is already ample in Zhang's sense and produces bases of small sections.

A good expansion follows this derivation, with pages for:

- the ampleness of $bL + aA$ on $X$: $L$ is nef on every fibre of $X$ over $\operatorname{Spec}
  \mathbb{Z}$, its degree on a vertical curve over $\mathbb{F}_p$ being the arithmetic degree of
  $\bar L$ there divided by $\log p$, and $aA$ is ample, so $bL + aA$ is ample by Keeler's criterion
  over the affine noetherian base $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$ (Propositions 2.13 and 2.14);
- the positivity of the first Chern form $c_1(b \bar L + a \bar A) = b\, c_1(\bar L) + a\, c_1(\bar
  A)$, semipositive plus positive; on a singular analytic space the strictly positive term absorbs
  the small perturbations of the definition;
- horizontal positivity, $(\hat c_1(b \bar L + a \bar A)|_Y)^{\dim Y} > 0$ for every horizontal
  integral closed subscheme $Y$: in the expansion, the mixed terms are nonnegative (Moriwaki,
  *Arithmetic height functions over finitely generated fields*, Proposition 2.3 (1)) and the term
  $a^{\dim Y} (\hat c_1(\bar A)|_Y)^{\dim Y}$ is positive, $\bar A$ being ample: restrict strictly
  small sections generating a power of $A$ to $Y$ and argue by induction on the dimension, as in the
  proof of his Lemma 2.4, pp. 7–9, whose hypothesis is this positivity;
- the generation by small sections: by the first three steps $\bar B = b \bar L + a \bar A$ is nef
  on every fibre, semipositive and of positive top self-intersection on every horizontal integral
  closed subscheme, so Moriwaki's Corollary 5.1 in *Semiample invertible sheaves with semipositive
  continuous hermitian metrics*, which assumes no smoothness of the generic fibre, gives for all
  large $n$ a basis of $H^0(X, nB)$ consisting of strictly small sections; as $B$ is ample, $nB$ is
  generated by its global sections for large $n$, so that basis generates the sheaf. Moriwaki
  deduces the corollary from his Theorem 0.2 on semiample metrics and the arguments of Zhang's
  Theorem (4.2); for a generically smooth $X$ this step is Zhang's (4.8), or Moriwaki's Theorem 4.2
  in *Free basis consisting of strictly small sections*.

## Proof depends on

- [Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties](../birational-geometry/standard-results-of-coherent-cohomology.md),
  for Keeler's criterion over $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$ (item 5), which makes $bL + aA$
  ample, and for the generation of $nB$ by its global sections (item 2)
- [Arithmetic degree and arithmetic intersection numbers](arithmetic-intersection-numbers.md), for
  the arithmetic intersection numbers of the horizontal positivity
- [Zhang's arithmetic ampleness theorem](arithmetic-ampleness-theorem.md), for Zhang's theorem,
  whose arguments Moriwaki's Corollary 5.1 adapts once the metric is semiample, and which gives the
  small sections directly when the generic fibre is smooth
- [Moriwaki's generalized Hodge index theorem](arithmetic-volumes/generalized-hodge-index-theorem.md),
  for the bigness of $\bar B|_Y$ on every horizontal integral closed subscheme $Y$, which its
  corollary gives from the positive top self-intersection, $B|_Y$ being nef on every geometric fibre
  and $c_1(\bar B|_Y)$ semipositive: the first step of the proof of Corollary 5.1, for the
  $C^\infty$ metric of $\bar B$; it does not give Corollary 5.1 for continuous metrics
- [Extension of small sections and bases of small sections](extension-of-small-sections.md), for
  Zhang's Theorem (4.2), whose arguments the proof of Corollary 5.1 then follows to pass to a basis
  of strictly small sections of $H^0(X, nB)$; it does not give Corollary 5.1 itself
