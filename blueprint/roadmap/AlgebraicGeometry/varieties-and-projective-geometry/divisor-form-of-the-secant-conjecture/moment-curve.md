# Moment curve

For a natural number $N$ and a real parameter $x$, the moment curve (rational normal curve) in $\mathbb{C}^N$ is the vector

$$F_N(x) = (1, x, x^2, \dots, x^{N-1}) \in \mathbb{C}^N,$$

whose coordinate of index $i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ is $x^i$, with $x$ regarded as a complex number.
