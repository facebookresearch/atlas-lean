# Secant conjecture, divisor form

The statement (Eremenko's secant conjecture): let $m, p \ge 2$ be natural numbers and let $x_{j,k} \in \mathbb{R}$, for $j \in \{0, \dots, mp-1\}$ and $k \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$, be real parameters with $x_{j,0}, \dots, x_{j,p-1}$ pairwise distinct for each $j$. Suppose there are subsets $I_0, \dots, I_{mp-1} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, pairwise disjoint, such that each $I_j$ or its complement $\mathbb{R} \setminus I_j$ is an interval (order-connected), and $x_{j,k} \in I_j$ for all $j, k$. Let $F(x) = (1, x, \dots, x^{m+p-1}) \in \mathbb{C}^{m+p}$ be the moment curve. Then every complex linear subspace $Y \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{m+p}$ of dimension $m$ such that, for every $j$,

$$Y \cap \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{C}}\{F(x_{j,0}), \dots, F(x_{j,p-1})\} \neq 0$$

is real, i.e. stable under coordinatewise complex conjugation.

The condition on $I_j$ describes the traces on $\mathbb{R}$ of intervals of $\mathbb{RP}^1$, including arcs through $\infty$.

## Sources

A. Eremenko, Disconjugacy and the Secant Conjecture, *Arnold Math. J.* 1 (2015), https://amj.math.stonybrook.edu/html-articles/Files-2015-2024/15-23/.
