# Subspaces meeting separated secant planes are real

Let $m, p \ge 2$ and let $x_{j,k} \in \mathbb{R}$ ($0 \le j < mp$, $0 \le k < p$) be distinct within each block $j$, with $x_{j,k} \in I_j$ for pairwise disjoint sets $I_j \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ each of which is an interval or has an interval as complement in $\mathbb{R}$. With $F(x) = (1, x, \dots, x^{m+p-1}) \in \mathbb{C}^{m+p}$, every $m$-dimensional complex subspace $Y \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{m+p}$ meeting each secant plane $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{C}}\{F(x_{j,0}), \dots, F(x_{j,p-1})\}$ in a nonzero vector is stable under coordinatewise complex conjugation.
