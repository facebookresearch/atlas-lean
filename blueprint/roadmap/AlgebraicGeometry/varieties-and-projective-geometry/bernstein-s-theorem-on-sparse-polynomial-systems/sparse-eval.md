# Sparse bivariate Laurent polynomial

For a finite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of exponent vectors and coefficients $a_u \in \mathbb{C}$
for $u \in A$, the sparse Laurent polynomial with exponent set $A$ and coefficients $a$ is the
function $(x, y) \mapsto \sum_{u \in A} a_u x^{u_0} y^{u_1}$ on the torus $(\mathbb{C}^*)^2$. Its
support, the set of exponents $u \in A$ with $a_u \neq 0$, is contained in $A$, and equals $A$
when every coefficient is nonzero, as it is for generic coefficients.
