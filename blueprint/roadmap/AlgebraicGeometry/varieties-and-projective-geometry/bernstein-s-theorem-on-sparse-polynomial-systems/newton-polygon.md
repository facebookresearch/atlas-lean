# Newton polygon of a finite set of exponents

For a finite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ of exponent vectors, the Newton polygon of $A$ is
the convex hull of $A$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$ (Sturmfels, CBMS 97, Section 3.1, where it is written
$\mathrm{New}(f)$ for a Laurent polynomial $f$ with support $A$). It extends the [Newton polygon](../../../Algebra/polynomials/newton-polygon/newton-polygon.md)
of a bivariate polynomial, whose exponents lie in $\mathbb{N}^2$, to Laurent polynomials.
