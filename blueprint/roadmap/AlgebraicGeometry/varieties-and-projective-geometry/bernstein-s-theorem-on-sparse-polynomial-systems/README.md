# Bernstein's theorem on sparse polynomial systems

Bernstein's theorem counts the common zeros in the torus of generic Laurent polynomials by the mixed volume of their Newton polytopes; in the plane, two generic equations have as many solutions in $(\mathbb{C}^*)^2$ as the mixed area of their Newton polygons. Bézout's bound for dense polynomials is the special case of triangles.
