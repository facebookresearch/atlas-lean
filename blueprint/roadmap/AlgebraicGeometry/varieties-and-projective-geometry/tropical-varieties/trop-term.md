# Value of one term of a tropical polynomial

Let $K$ be a field with a valuation $\mathrm{val} : K \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ and let
$f = \sum_u c_u x^u$ be a Laurent polynomial in $n$ variables over $K$. For $w \in \mathbb{R}^n$
and $u \in \mathbb{Z}^n$, the tropical term of $f$ at $u$ is
$\mathrm{val}(c_u) + u \cdot w \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$; it is $\infty$ when $c_u = 0$
(Maclagan–Sturmfels, draft 2009, Section 3.1).
