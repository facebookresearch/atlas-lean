# Laurent polynomials

For a field $K$ and a natural number $n$, the ring of Laurent polynomials
$K[x_1^{\pm 1}, \dots, x_n^{\pm 1}]$ is the group algebra of $\mathbb{Z}^n$ over $K$: its
elements are the finite sums $f = \sum_u c_u x^u$ over exponent vectors $u \in \mathbb{Z}^n$,
with coefficients $c_u \in K$.
