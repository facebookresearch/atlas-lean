# Coordinatewise valuations of a variety

Let $K$ be a field with a valuation $\mathrm{val} : K \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$, let $I$ be
an ideal of Laurent polynomials in $n$ variables over $K$, and let $X = V(I) \subseteq (K^*)^n$ be
the set of common zeros of $I$ in the torus. The set of coordinatewise valuations of the points of
$X$ is $\mathrm{val}(X) = \{(\mathrm{val}(y_1), \dots, \mathrm{val}(y_n)) : y \in X\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$
(Maclagan–Sturmfels, draft 2009, Theorem 3.2.4, the third set, before taking the closure).
