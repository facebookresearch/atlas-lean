# Tropical hypersurface

Let $K$ be a field with a valuation $\mathrm{val} : K \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ and let
$f = \sum_u c_u x^u$ be a Laurent polynomial in $n$ variables over $K$. The tropical hypersurface
of $f$ is the set of $w \in \mathbb{R}^n$ at which the minimum
$\operatorname{trop}(f)(w) = \min_{u : c_u \neq 0} (\mathrm{val}(c_u) + u \cdot w)$ is attained at
least twice, that is, at two distinct exponents $u \neq u'$ with $c_u \neq 0$ and $c_{u'} \neq 0$
(Maclagan–Sturmfels, draft 2009, Definition 3.1.1). In particular it is empty when $f$ has at
most one nonzero term.
