# Coordinatewise valuations of a hypersurface

Let $K$ be a field with a valuation $\mathrm{val} : K \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ and let
$f$ be a Laurent polynomial in $n$ variables over $K$. The set of coordinatewise valuations of
the zeros of $f$ in the torus is
$\{(\mathrm{val}(y_1), \dots, \mathrm{val}(y_n)) : y \in (K^*)^n,\ f(y) = 0\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$
(Maclagan–Sturmfels, draft 2009, Theorem 3.1.3, the third set, before taking the closure).
