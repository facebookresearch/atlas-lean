# Noncoordinate equations with affine-space zero fibers

For every integer $n\ge4$ there is a polynomial $F\in\mathbb C[X_0,\ldots,X_{n-1}]$ such that the quotient by the principal ideal $(F)$ is isomorphic as a complex algebra to $\mathbb C[Y_0,\ldots,Y_{n-2}]$, but no complex polynomial automorphism sends any coordinate $X_i$ to $F$. The assertion concerns the zero fiber and does not require every fiber to be affine space.

## Depends on

- Principal ideal quotient and polynomial algebra automorphisms over $\mathbb C$.

## Proof depends on

- Explicit affine-fiber reconstruction for the four-variable equation and a critical point on a different fiber; both properties under adjoining unused variables.
