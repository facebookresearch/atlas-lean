# Singular locus of a hypersurface in projective 4-space

For a polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]$, the singular locus of $\{f = 0\} \subseteq \mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{C})$ is the set of points $P \in \mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{C})$ such that, for a chosen representative $a \in \mathbb{C}^5 \setminus \{0\}$ of $P$,

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(a) = 0 \quad \text{for all } i \in \{0, \dots, 4\}.$$

For homogeneous $f$ the condition does not depend on the representative, and for homogeneous $f$ of positive degree these points lie on the hypersurface by Euler's formula.
