# Nodal hypersurfaces in projective 4-space with few nodes are factorial

For every natural number $d \ge 3$ and every homogeneous polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]$ of degree $d$ whose singular locus in $\mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{C})$ is finite and consists of nodes (points where the Hessian of $f$ has rank $4$), if the number of singular points is less than $(d - 1)^2$, then $\mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]/(f)$ is an integral domain in which every nonzero element is associated to a finite product of prime elements.

## Sources

AIM workshop *Numerical invariants of singularities and higher-dimensional algebraic varieties*, problem list, Problem 18 (Cheltsov), https://aimath.org/WWN/singularvariety/problems_final.pdf. Proved by I. Cheltsov, Factorial threefold hypersurfaces, *J. Algebraic Geom.* 19 (2010), 781-791, arXiv:0803.3301, Theorem 1.4; Example 1.3 there ($xg + yh = 0$ with general $g, h$) has $(d-1)^2$ nodes and is not factorial, so the bound is sharp.
