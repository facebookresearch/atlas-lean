# Factoriality bound for nodal hypersurfaces in ℙ⁴

The statement: for every natural number $d \ge 3$ and every homogeneous polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]$ of degree $d$ whose singular locus $\operatorname{Sing}(f) \subseteq \mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{C})$ is finite with every singular point a node (the Hessian of $f$ at a representative has rank $4$), if

$$\#\operatorname{Sing}(f) < (d - 1)^2,$$

then the quotient ring $\mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]/(f)$ is a unique factorization domain: it is an integral domain, and every nonzero element is associated to a finite product of prime elements.

By Cheltsov's Remark 1.2, the quotient ring being a unique factorization domain is equivalent to the hypersurface $\{f = 0\}$ being factorial.
