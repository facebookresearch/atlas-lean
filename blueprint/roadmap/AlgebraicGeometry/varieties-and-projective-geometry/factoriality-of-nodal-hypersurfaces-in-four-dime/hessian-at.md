# Hessian matrix of a polynomial in five variables

For a polynomial $f \in \mathbb{C}[x_0, \dots, x_4]$ and a point $a \in \mathbb{C}^5$, the Hessian of $f$ at $a$ is the $5 \times 5$ complex matrix

$$H_f(a)_{ij} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_j\, \partial x_i}(a), \qquad i, j \in \{0, \dots, 4\}.$$
