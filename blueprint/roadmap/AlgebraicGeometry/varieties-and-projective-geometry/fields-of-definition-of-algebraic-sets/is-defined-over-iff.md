# Defined over $k$ as an equality of ideals

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure
$\bar k$ and let $Z \subseteq \bar k^n$. Then $Z$ is defined over $k$ if and only if the vanishing
ideal of $Z$ in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ equals the ideal generated by the image of $I_k(Z)$
under the coefficient map $k[X_1, \dots, X_n] \to \bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.
