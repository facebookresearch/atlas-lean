# Sets defined over $k$

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure $\bar k$, let
$Z \subseteq \bar k^n$, and let $I_k(Z)$ be the ideal of polynomials in $k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ whose
image in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ vanishes at every point of $Z$. The set $Z$ is defined over $k$
if the vanishing ideal of $Z$ in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ equals the ideal generated by the image
of $I_k(Z)$ under the coefficient map $k[X_1, \dots, X_n] \to \bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.
