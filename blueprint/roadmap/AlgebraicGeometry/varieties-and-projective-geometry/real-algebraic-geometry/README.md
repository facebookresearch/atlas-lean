# Real algebraic geometry

Real algebraic and semialgebraic geometry: semialgebraic sets and maps over ordered rings and real closed fields, closure under projection (Tarski–Seidenberg), Hardt's semialgebraic triviality, real algebraic varieties with their nonsingular stratifications, and real algebraic and stratified-algebraic vector bundles. Quantifier elimination for real closed fields is under model theory.
