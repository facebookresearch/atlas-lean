# Eckl's 2008 lower bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at least
$4/13$.

## Sources

- Thomas Eckl, *An asymptotic version of Dumnicki's algorithm for linear systems in $\mathbb{CP}^2$*, Geometriae Dedicata 137 (2008), 149–162, arXiv:0801.2926, abstract (the lower bound $4/13$ for ten general points).
- Optimization Problems, constant 74a (10-point multi-point Seshadri constant on $\mathbb{P}^2$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/74a.md.
