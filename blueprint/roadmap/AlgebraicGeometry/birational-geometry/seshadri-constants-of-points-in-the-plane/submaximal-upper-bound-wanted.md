# Submaximal upper bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at most
$1/\sqrt{10}$.

## Sources

- Carlos Galindo, Francisco Monserrat, Carlos-Jesús Moreno-Ávila and Julio-José Moyano-Fernández, *On the valuative Nagata conjecture*, Research in the Mathematical Sciences 12 (2025), Article 18, Section 1: the general bound $\varepsilon(S, D; x_1, \dots, x_r) \le \sqrt{D^2/r}$, here with $S = \mathbb{P}^2$, $D = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(1)$ and $r = 10$.
- Optimization Problems, constant 74a (10-point multi-point Seshadri constant on $\mathbb{P}^2$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/74a.md.
