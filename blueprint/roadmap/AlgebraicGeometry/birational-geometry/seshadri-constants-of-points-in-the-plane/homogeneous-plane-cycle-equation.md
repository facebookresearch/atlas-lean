# Positive-degree equations of homogeneous plane cycles

Put $R=\mathbb C[X_0,X_1,X_2]$. Let $D$ be a nonzero finitely supported family of natural-number weights on the ideals of $R$; each ideal of positive weight is a nonzero, homogeneous, principal prime ideal. Then there exists a nonzero homogeneous $F\in R$ of strictly positive total degree such that the prime-factor multiplicities of the associate class of $F$, pushed forward by $[g]\mapsto(g)$, give exactly $D$. Weights count repeated factors, not just distinct components.

## Depends on

- Unique factorization in the ternary polynomial ring and transport from associates to principal ideals.

## Proof depends on

None.

## Proof outline

If a nonzero principal ideal is homogeneous for total degree, then any generator $g$ is homogeneous: its nonzero top homogeneous part lies in the ideal, so it is a multiple $gh$. Additivity of total degree for nonzero products over $\mathbb C$ gives $\deg h=0$, so the multiplier is a nonzero constant. Apply this to each ideal of positive weight, take the product of the prime generators to those powers, and use additivity of factorization in associates to identify the ideal weights: the selected prime generators of distinct support ideals are nonassociated, so no two terms merge under the principal-ideal equivalence. A nonzero proper prime principal ideal cannot be generated by a nonzero constant; together with nonzero support this gives positive degree.
