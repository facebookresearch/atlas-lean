# Ciliberto–Miranda lower bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at least
$55/174$.

## Sources

- Ciro Ciliberto and Rick Miranda, *Homogeneous interpolation on ten points*, Journal of Algebraic Geometry 20 (2011), no. 4, 685–726, arXiv:0812.0032, abstract (the linear systems with $d/m \ge 174/55$ have the expected dimension, which gives the bound $55/174$).
- Optimization Problems, constant 74a (10-point multi-point Seshadri constant on $\mathbb{P}^2$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/2c1968cd520b60f1cf3cf50749f7285e800e4e15/constants/74a.md.
