# Harbourne–Roé lower bound for the ten-point Seshadri constant

The generic ten-point Seshadri constant of $\mathbb{P}^2$, in the affine-chart model, is at least
$177/560$.

## Sources

- Brian Harbourne and Joaquim Roé, *Computing multi-point Seshadri constants on $\mathbb{P}^2$*, Bulletin of the Belgian Mathematical Society – Simon Stevin 16 (2009), no. 5, 887–906, Section 3, p. 895, in the discussion following Table 1 (the divisor $(560/177)L - (E_1 + \dots + E_{10})$ is nef).
