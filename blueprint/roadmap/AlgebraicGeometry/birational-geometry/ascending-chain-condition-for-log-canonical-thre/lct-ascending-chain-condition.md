# ACC for log canonical thresholds on smooth varieties

The statement (Shokurov's ACC conjecture in the smooth case): for every natural number $n$, there is no strictly increasing sequence $a_0 < a_1 < a_2 < \cdots$ of real numbers all lying in $T_n$, the [set](lct-set.md) of [analytic log canonical thresholds at the origin](lct-at-origin.md) of nonzero ideals $(f_1, \dots, f_r) \subseteq \mathbb{C}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ with $f_i(0) = 0$ for all $i$. It is [resolved true](lct-ascending-chain-condition-holds.md).

## Sources
