# ACC for log canonical thresholds on smooth varieties

The statement (Shokurov's ACC conjecture in the smooth case): for every natural number $n$, there is no strictly increasing sequence $a_0 < a_1 < a_2 < \cdots$ of real numbers all lying in $T_n$, the set of analytic log canonical thresholds at the origin of nonzero ideals $(f_1, \dots, f_r) \subseteq \mathbb{C}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ with $f_i(0) = 0$ for all $i$.

## Sources

L. Ein (with T. de Fernex and M. Mustaţă), in *Kommutative Algebra*, Oberwolfach Reports 6 (2009), no. 22, p. 1182, Theorem 0.1, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2009/22.
