# Logarithmic Kodaira additivity for negative fibers

Let $X,Y$ be smooth integral complex projective varieties with reduced simple-normal-crossing boundaries $E,D$. Let $f:X\to Y$ be a surjective complex morphism with connected fibers over complex points and $f^{-1}|D|\subset|E|$. Over $Y\setminus|D|$, require $f$ and its restriction to every nonempty intersection of boundary components of $E$ to be smooth.

For a pair $(T,B)$ let $V_m$ consist of rational tensor powers of top Kähler forms that, on each nonempty affine where $B$ is generated by a nonzero equation $t$, belong after multiplication by $t^m$ to the regular lattice generated by tensors of wedges of differentials of regular functions. Define $\kappa(T,B)$ as the supremum of $k\ge0$ such that for some $m>0$, a nonzero $s\in V_m$ can be multiplied by $k$ algebraically independent functions over $\mathbb C$ to give forms still in $V_m$. The empty supremum is $-\infty$.

There is a countable family of proper closed subsets of $Y$ such that each complex point $y$ outside them is outside $|D|$ and has this property: for every smooth integral projective pullback model $F$ of the fiber with reduced SNC boundary $B$ having ideal equal to the pulled-back boundary ideal, if $\kappa(F,B)=-\infty$, then $\kappa(X,E)=\kappa(Y,D)+\kappa(F,B)$ and $V_m=0$ on $(X,E)$ for every $m>0$. The comparator quantifies over fiber models; it does not assert their existence as a separate conjunct.

## Depends on

- SNC Cartier boundary products, rational and regular pluriforms, and formation of complex-point fibers.

## Proof depends on

- Restriction and nonvanishing of a log pluriform at a very general fiber, adjunction for the induced boundary, and conversion of section vanishing to the bottom Kodaira dimension.

## Expansion

Build the chartwise pole and restriction lemmas for the lattice formulation. For each positive tensor power exclude the loci on which the relevant restriction fails; assemble the countable exceptional family. Do not promote the finite-dimensional or negative-base branches with this result.
