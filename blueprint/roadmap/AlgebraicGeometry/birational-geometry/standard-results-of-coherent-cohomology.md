# Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties

This node is still to be broken down. It collects the classical results of coherent cohomology and
projective geometry that the proofs of the vanishing theorems and of the asymptotic Riemann–Roch
statements use without proof. The setting is given in each item. The targets are:

1. (Projection formula and Leray.) For a morphism $f \colon X' \to X$ of schemes, a quasi-coherent
   sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $X'$ and a **finite** locally free sheaf $\mathcal{L}$ on $X$, $R^q
   f_*(\mathcal{F} \otimes f^* \mathcal{L}) \cong R^q f_* \mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}$ (for a
   locally free sheaf of infinite rank this fails: for $f \colon \coprod_{j \in \mathbb{N}}
   \operatorname{Spec} k \to \operatorname{Spec} k$ the product of copies of an infinite-dimensional
   space is not the tensor product); and, the two degenerate cases of the Leray spectral sequence,
   if $R^q f_* \mathcal{F} = 0$ for $q > 0$, then $H^i(X', \mathcal{F}) \cong H^i(X, f_*
   \mathcal{F})$, while if $H^p(X, R^q f_* \mathcal{F}) = 0$ for all $p > 0$ and all $q$, then
   $H^q(X', \mathcal{F}) \cong H^0(X, R^q f_* \mathcal{F})$ for all $q$.
2. (Serre, GAGA and base change.) Serre's theorems: for a scheme $X$ proper over a noetherian ring
   $R$, for instance projective over a field or over $\mathbb{Z}$, an ample invertible sheaf $A$ on
   $X$ and a coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F}$, $H^i(X, \mathcal{F} \otimes A^{\otimes t}) = 0$ for $i >
   0$ and all large $t$, and $\mathcal{F} \otimes A^{\otimes t}$ is generated by its global sections
   for all large $t$ (Stacks Project, Tags 0B5U and 01Q3). Finiteness: for a proper morphism $f
   \colon X \to Y$ to a locally noetherian scheme and a coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$, the
   sheaves $R^q f_* \mathcal{F}$ are coherent; in particular, for $X$ proper over a noetherian ring
   $A$, the $A$-modules $H^q(X, \mathcal{F})$ are finitely generated. Serre duality on a smooth
   projective variety $X$ of pure dimension $n$: $H^i(X, E)^\vee \cong H^{n-i}(X, E^\vee \otimes
   \omega_X)$ for a finite locally free $E$, and $H^i(X, \mathcal{F})^\vee \cong
   \operatorname{Ext}^{n-i}(\mathcal{F}, \omega_X)$ for a coherent $\mathcal{F}$ (for the skyscraper
   $k(p)$ on a smooth curve the naive formula fails, its sheaf dual being $0$). GAGA: for a complex
   projective scheme and a coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F}$, the comparison maps $H^q(X, \mathcal{F})
   \to H^q(X^{\mathrm{an}}, \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{an}})$ are isomorphisms in all degrees $q \ge 0$,
   and coherent analytic sheaves are algebraic. Flat base change: for a flat ring map $A \to A'$, a
   scheme $X$ quasi-compact and quasi-separated over $A$ (for instance projective) and a
   quasi-coherent $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$, $H^q(X_{A'}, \mathcal{F}_{A'}) = H^q(X, \mathcal{F})
   \otimes_A A'$ for all $q \ge 0$ (Stacks Project, Tag 02KH), for instance along $\mathbb{Z} \to
   \mathbb{C}$ or a field extension $k \subset k'$. Without quasi-compactness it fails already in
   degree $0$: for $X$ a disjoint union of countably many points and $k'/k$ of infinite degree,
   $(\prod k) \otimes_k k' \to \prod k'$ is not surjective.
3. (Riemann–Roch.) For a proper scheme over a field, a coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F} \ne 0$ with
   support of dimension $e$ and a line bundle $L$, $\chi(X, \mathcal{F} \otimes L^{\otimes \nu})$ is
   a polynomial in $\nu$ of degree at most $e$ whose coefficient of $\nu^e$ is $\sum_Z
   \operatorname{length}(\mathcal{F}_{\eta_Z}) (L^e \cdot Z)/e!$, over the $e$-dimensional integral
   components $Z$ of the support (for $L = \mathcal{O}$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$ and $\mathcal{F} =
   \mathcal{O}$ it is the constant $1$); and, for smooth projective $X$ only, the
   Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch formula $\chi(E) = \deg(\operatorname{ch}(E) \operatorname{td}(T_X))$.
4. (Bertini.) On a smooth variety over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, a
   general member of a linear system without base points is smooth (possibly disconnected), and
   meets every stratum of a given
   [simple normal crossing divisor](simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md) transversally: restrict the
   system to each of the finitely many strata. In characteristic $p$ it fails: the pencil $\langle
   x^p, y^p \rangle$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$ is base-point-free and every member is a multiple point.
5. (Seshadri.) A line bundle $A$ on a projective variety over an algebraically closed field is ample
   if and only if there is $\varepsilon > 0$, the same for all curves, with $\deg(A|_C) \ge
   \varepsilon \operatorname{mult}_x C$ for every integral curve $C$ and closed point $x \in C$; and
   its consequence that a line bundle $L$ on a projective manifold, nef in the sense of nonnegative
   degrees on curves, has $L^{\nu} \otimes A$ ample for every ample $A$ and every $\nu > 0$
   (Esnault–Viehweg, Lemma 5.7). More generally, for a proper morphism $\pi \colon X \to S$ to a
   noetherian scheme, a $\pi$-ample invertible sheaf $A$ and an invertible sheaf $N$ whose degree is
   nonnegative on every integral curve that $\pi$ maps to a point, $A \otimes N^{\otimes \nu}$ is
   $\pi$-ample for every $\nu \ge 0$ (Keeler, Propositions 2.13 and 2.14); over an affine $S$,
   $\pi$-ample means ample.
6. (Nakai–Moishezon.) A line bundle $L$ on a projective scheme over a field is ample if and only if
   $(L^{\dim Z} \cdot Z) > 0$ for every integral closed subscheme $Z$ of positive dimension (testing
   $X$ alone is not enough: $\mathcal{O}(-1)$ on $\mathbb{P}^2$ has positive square).
7. (Embedding criterion.) On a complete variety over an algebraically closed field, a complete
   linear system **without base points** that separates points and tangent vectors defines a closed
   immersion into projective space (Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, Definition I.6.2 and Proposition
   I.6.3).
8. (Descent of the existence of an ample divisor.) A complete variety over a field $k$ has some
   ample divisor if its extension of scalars to an algebraic closure of $k$ has one (Milne,
   Proposition I.6.6 (d)); the given class need not descend (a real conic without real points has
   $\mathcal{O}(1)$ over $\mathbb{C}$ but no real line bundle of degree $1$).

9. (Growth of sections.) For a line bundle $L$ on an integral projective variety over a field with
   $H^0(X, L^\nu) \ne 0$ for some $\nu \ge 1$, of Iitaka dimension $\kappa$, the largest dimension
   of the images of the rational maps given by the $|L^m|$, there are $a, b > 0$ with $a \mu^\kappa
   \le \dim H^0(X, L^{\nu\mu}) \le b \mu^\kappa$ for $\mu \ge 1$ (Esnault–Viehweg, Properties 5.4
   b), which refer to Mori, §1).
10. (Kodaira's lemma.) $\kappa = \dim X$ if and only if $L^\nu(-C)$ is ample for some $\nu \ge 1$
    and some effective Cartier divisor $C$ (Esnault–Viehweg, Properties 5.4 d), through the
    restriction sequence and the growth bound on an ample divisor); integrality is needed, as
    $\mathcal{O}(1) \sqcup \mathcal{O}$ on $\mathbb{P}^1 \sqcup \mathbb{P}^1$ shows.

In 7 and 8 the variety is complete, not assumed projective, since 8 is an input of the projectivity
of abelian varieties. Item 5 uses only the definition of nef by degrees on curves, so that the page
on nef and big line bundles, which uses 6, does not form a cycle with this node. A good expansion
has separate pages for the independent results: the projection formula and the Leray degeneration
(Stacks Project, Tags 01E8, 01F2 and 01F4); Serre vanishing and the generation of twists by global
sections over a noetherian base (Tags 0B5U and 01Q3; Esnault–Viehweg, Theorem 1.1); Serre duality
for vector bundles and for coherent sheaves (Tags 0FVV and 0FW0); GAGA (Serre, §12, Theorems 1–3);
the finiteness of the higher direct images of coherent sheaves under proper morphisms (EGA III,
Théorème (3.2.1)); flat base change along flat ring maps (Tag 02KH; Esnault–Viehweg, 8.1, for field
extensions); the Euler polynomial of a coherent sheaf (Tags 0BEM, 0BEN and 0BEP) and, separately,
Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch on smooth projective varieties (Tag 02UO, a statement); Bertini's theorem
(Kleiman, *Bertini and his two fundamental theorems*, Theorem 4.1 and its proof); Seshadri's and
Nakai's criteria, with the definitions of ample and nef line bundles kept on a page of their own
(Keeler, Propositions 2.10, 2.13 and 2.14); and the embedding and descent criteria (Milne; Stacks
Project, Tags 0E8S, 0BDC and 0BDG).

## Proof depends on

- [Noether normalization for nonzero finite-type commutative algebras over a field](../../RingTheory/integral-extensions/noether-normalization.md),
  for the residue fields of closed points of schemes of finite type over a field $k$, finite over
  $k$ and equal to $k$ when $k$ is algebraically closed, in the proofs of 7 and 8 (Stacks Project,
  Tags 0E8S and 0BDC): such a residue field is finite over a polynomial subalgebra, which is then
  itself a field, so has no variables
- [Finite morphisms are the proper locally quasi-finite morphisms](../schemes-and-morphisms/zariski-s-main-theorem/finite-iff-proper-and-locally-quasi-finite.md),
  for the finiteness of a proper morphism with finite fibres, in the proof of the embedding
  criterion 7 (Stacks Project, Tag 0E8S)
- [Tensoring with a flat module preserves exactness](../../RingTheory/modules/tensor-with-a-flat-module-preserves-exactness.md),
  for the exactness of $- \otimes_A B$ for a flat $A$-algebra $B$, which gives $H^q(C^\bullet)
  \otimes_A B \cong H^q(C^\bullet \otimes_A B)$ for the Čech complex $C^\bullet$ of a quasi-coherent
  sheaf, as tensoring with $B$ preserves its cycles and boundaries, in flat base change in 2 (Stacks
  Project, Lemma 30.5.2, Tag 02KH)
- [The Čech complex of a presheaf](../../CategoryTheory/sites-and-sheaves/cech-complex-of-a-presheaf.md),
  for the construction of the Čech complex of a finite affine open covering in the separated case,
  where the intersections of the covering are affine; its comparison with the cohomology of a
  quasi-coherent sheaf (Stacks Project, Lemma 30.2.6) is a further step of flat base change in 2

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- the dévissage of coherent sheaves and Chow's lemma (EGA III, proof of Théorème (3.2.1)), for the
  finiteness theorem in 2
- the comparison of the Čech complex of a finite affine open covering with the cohomology of a
  quasi-coherent sheaf on a separated scheme, where the intersections of the covering are affine,
  the base change of that complex and the reduction from quasi-separated schemes (Stacks Project,
  proof of Lemma 30.5.2, Tag 02KH, with Lemmas 30.2.6 and 30.5.1), for flat base change in 2
- Kleiman's criterion for nef line bundles (Keeler, Proposition 2.11) and the nonnegativity of the
  products $L^i \cdot N^{d-i} \cdot V$ of two nef classes on a $d$-dimensional integral $V$
  contracted by $\pi$ (Keeler, Lemma 2.12), as a page of its own, for 5 and 6; the page on
  intersection numbers of nef line bundles states the nonnegativity for any number of nef classes
  but depends on this node, so this step needs a proof independent of it
- the growth estimates that Esnault and Viehweg take from Mori (Esnault–Viehweg, Properties 5.4),
  for 9 and 10
