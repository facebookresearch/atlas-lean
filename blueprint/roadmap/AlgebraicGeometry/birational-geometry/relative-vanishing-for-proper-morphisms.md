# Relative vanishing theorem for proper morphisms

This node is still to be broken down: the statement is precise, but its proof is not.

Let $X$ be a smooth variety over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and $f \colon
X \to Z$ a proper surjective morphism to a variety $Z$. An invertible sheaf on $X$ is
*$f$-numerically effective* if its degree on every curve $C \subset X$ with $\dim f(C) = 0$ is
nonnegative. Let $\mathcal{L}$ be an invertible sheaf on $X$, $N \ge 1$ an integer and $D = \sum_j
\alpha_j D_j$ an effective divisor with [snc support](simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md) such that
$\mathcal{L}^N(-D)$ is $f$-numerically effective, and put $\mathcal{L}^{(1)} = \mathcal{L}(-\lfloor
D / N \rfloor)$, with $\kappa$ the [Iitaka dimension](nef-and-big-line-bundles.md). Then
(Esnault–Viehweg, Corollary 6.11)

1. $R^b f_*(\mathcal{L}^{(1)} \otimes \omega_X) = 0$ for $b > \dim X - \dim Z -
   \kappa(\mathcal{L}|_F)$, where $F$ is a general fibre of $f$;
2. in particular, if $f$ is birational and $D = f^* \Delta$ for an effective Cartier divisor
   $\Delta$ on $Z$, then $R^b f_*(\omega_X \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(-\lfloor D / N \rfloor)) = 0$ for
   $b > 0$.

The [local vanishing theorem for multiplier ideals](local-vanishing-for-multiplier-ideals.md) is the
case 2 for a log resolution. Esnault and Viehweg's proof: the statement is local on $Z$, so $Z$ may
be taken affine, or, after compactifying $X$ and $Z$, projective, and then $X$ projective as well,
as blowing up $X$ does not change the sheaves in question (their Claim 5.13). For an ample
$\mathcal{A}$ on $Z$ and $\nu$ large, $f^* \mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes \mathcal{L}^N(-D)$ is numerically
effective with $\kappa(f^* \mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes \mathcal{L}) \ge \kappa(\mathcal{L}|_F) + \dim
Z$, and Serre's theorems on $Z$ kill $H^c(Z, \mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes R^b f_*(\mathcal{L}^{(1)}
\otimes \omega_X))$ for $c > 0$ and make these sheaves generated by their sections. The Leray
spectral sequence then gives $H^b(X, f^* \mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes \mathcal{L}^{(1)} \otimes \omega_X)
= H^0(Z, \mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes R^b f_*(\mathcal{L}^{(1)} \otimes \omega_X))$, which vanishes in
the stated degrees by the global vanishing theorem, Corollary 5.12 d), so the generated sheaf
$\mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes R^b f_*(\cdots)$ is zero.

A good expansion has pages for the compactification of $X$ and $Z$ (Nagata's theorem), the reduction
to a projective $X$ by Chow's lemma and resolution with Esnault and Viehweg's Claim 5.13, which
rests on the rationality of quotient singularities, and the global vanishing of their Corollary 5.12
d).

## Depends on

- [Geometrically reduced variety over a field](../schemes-and-morphisms/varieties-over-a-field/is-variety.md),
  for the underlying varieties $X$ and $Z$
- [Simple normal crossing divisor](simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md), for divisors with snc support
  and the round-down $\lfloor D / N \rfloor$
- [Nef and big line bundles](nef-and-big-line-bundles.md), for the Iitaka dimension
  $\kappa(\mathcal{L}|_F)$

## Proof depends on

- [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](kawamata-viehweg-vanishing-theorem.md), for the global
  vanishing on a projective $X$, Esnault and Viehweg's Corollary 5.12 c) and d)
- [Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties](standard-results-of-coherent-cohomology.md),
  for Serre vanishing and the generation of twists by global sections on $Z$ (item 2), the second
  degenerate case of the Leray spectral sequence (item 1) and Serre duality
- [Resolution of singularities of quasi-projective varieties](resolution-of-singularities/resolution-of-singularities-of-quasi-projective-varieties.md),
  for resolving the singularities of a projective birational model of $X$ in the reduction to a
  projective $X$

The other steps of its proof are not pages yet: the compactification of $X$ and $Z$ (Nagata's
theorem), Chow's lemma, Esnault and Viehweg's Claim 5.13 with the rationality of quotient
singularities, and the comparison of the Iitaka dimensions in the proof of Corollary 6.11.
