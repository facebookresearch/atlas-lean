# Relative vanishing theorem for birational morphisms

This node is still to be broken down: the statement is precise, but its proof is not.

Let $f \colon X \to Z$ be a proper birational morphism of integral varieties over an algebraically
closed field of characteristic zero, with $X$ smooth, let $\Delta$ be an effective Cartier divisor
on $Z$ such that $D = f^* \Delta$ has [snc support](simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md), and let $N
\ge 1$ be an integer. Then (Esnault–Viehweg, Corollary 6.11 b))

$$R^b f_*\bigl(\omega_X \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(-\lfloor D / N \rfloor)\bigr) = 0 \qquad (b > 0).$$

The [local vanishing theorem for multiplier ideals](local-vanishing-for-multiplier-ideals.md)
follows from the case of a log resolution. Esnault and Viehweg obtain the statement as case b) of
their Corollary 6.11, whose case a), for a proper surjective morphism and an invertible sheaf
$\mathcal{L}$ with $\mathcal{L}^N(-D)$ relatively nef, bounds the degrees of the nonzero higher
direct images of $\mathcal{L}(-\lfloor D / N \rfloor) \otimes \omega_X$ through the Iitaka dimension
of $\mathcal{L}$ on a general fibre. Case a) is not taken over here. As printed it fails: for
$\mathbb{P}^1 \to \operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{C}$, $N = 2$, $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{O}(1)$ and $D =
2[p]$, $\mathcal{L}^2(-D)$ is trivial and $\kappa(\mathcal{L}) = 1$, but $\mathcal{L}(-\lfloor D / 2
\rfloor) \otimes \omega = \omega$ has $H^1 \ne 0$, the reduction to coefficients $0 < \alpha_j < N$
preserving $\mathcal{L}^N(-D)$ but not $\kappa(\mathcal{L})$. Its proof also uses that $f^*
\mathcal{A}^\nu \otimes \mathcal{L}^N(-D)$ is nef for an ample $\mathcal{A}$ on $Z$ and large $\nu$,
which fails for the first projection $E \times E \to E$ of an elliptic curve and the sheaf
$\mathrm{add}^* B \otimes p_1^* B^{-1} \otimes p_2^* B^{-1}$, $B = \mathcal{O}_E([0])$, of degree
$0$ on the fibres and of degree $2m$ on the graph of multiplication by $m$.

A good expansion proves the statement as follows. It is local on $Z$, so $Z$ may be taken affine.
Take a projective closure of $Z$ and blow up the closure of $\Delta$ in it; as $\Delta$ is Cartier
on $Z$, this changes nothing over $Z$, and gives a projective variety $\bar Z \supseteq Z$ with an
effective Cartier divisor $\bar\Delta$ extending $\Delta$. Compactify $f$ over $\bar Z$ (Nagata;
Stacks Project, Theorem 38.33.8, Tag 0F41), make the compactification projective by Chow's lemma
(Stacks Project, Lemma 30.18.1, Tag 0200), resolve its singularities and principalize the pull-back
of $\bar\Delta$: this gives a smooth projective $\bar X$ with a birational morphism $\bar f \colon
\bar X \to \bar Z$ such that $\bar f^* \bar\Delta$ has snc support, and over $Z$ a proper birational
morphism $\tau \colon X' = \bar f^{-1}(Z) \to X$ such that $\tau^* D$ has snc support. Esnault and
Viehweg's Claim 5.13, which is local on $X$, applied after the usual reduction to coefficients $0 <
\alpha_j < N$, gives $R\tau_* \mathcal{O}_{X'}(\lfloor \tau^* D / N \rfloor) = \mathcal{O}_X(\lfloor
D / N \rfloor)$ for these inverse eigensheaves; Grothendieck duality for the proper morphism $\tau$
(Stacks Project, Section 48.19, Tag 0AU3, items (2) and (4)(c), for the functor $\tau^!$ of Section
48.16, Tag 0A9Y), with $\tau^!(\omega_X[n]) = \omega_{X'}[n]$ for the common dimension $n$ of $X$
and $X'$, turns it into
$R\tau_*\bigl(\omega_{X'}(-\lfloor \tau^* D / N \rfloor)\bigr) = \omega_X(-\lfloor D / N \rfloor)$,
so the statement for $f$ follows from the statement for $\bar f$ over $Z$. For $\bar f$, choose an
ample $\mathcal{A}$ on $\bar Z$ and $m$ so large that $m \mathcal{A} - \bar\Delta / N$ is an ample
$\mathbb{Q}$-divisor class. Its pull-back $\bar f^*(m \mathcal{A}) - \bar f^* \bar\Delta / N$ is
nef, and big as $\bar f$ is birational, and $\bar f^* \bar\Delta / N$ has snc support, so the
Kawamata–Viehweg theorem gives $H^b(\bar X, \mathcal{G} \otimes \bar f^* \mathcal{A}^m) = 0$ for
every $b > 0$, where $\mathcal{G} = \omega_{\bar X}(-\lfloor \bar f^* \bar\Delta / N \rfloor)$. For
$m$ large, Serre vanishing on $\bar Z$ also kills $H^c(\bar Z, \mathcal{A}^m \otimes R^b \bar f_*
\mathcal{G})$ for $c > 0$ and all $b$, so the projection formula and the Leray spectral sequence
give $H^b(\bar X, \mathcal{G} \otimes \bar f^* \mathcal{A}^m) = H^0(\bar Z, \mathcal{A}^m \otimes
R^b \bar f_* \mathcal{G})$; this group vanishes for $b > 0$, and, $\mathcal{A}^m \otimes R^b \bar
f_* \mathcal{G}$ being generated by its global sections for $m$ large, $R^b \bar f_* \mathcal{G} =
0$.

## Depends on

- [Geometrically reduced variety over a field](../schemes-and-morphisms/varieties-over-a-field/is-variety.md),
  for the varieties $X$ and $Z$, which the statement takes integral, over an algebraically closed
  field of characteristic zero
- [Simple normal crossing divisor](simple-normal-crossing-divisor.md), for divisors with snc support
  and the round-down $\lfloor D / N \rfloor$

## Proof depends on

- [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](kawamata-viehweg-vanishing-theorem.md), for the vanishing on
  the projective model $\bar X$, applied to the integral divisor $\bar f^*(m \mathcal{A})$ and the
  $\mathbb{Q}$-divisor $\bar f^* \bar\Delta / N$
- [Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties](standard-results-of-coherent-cohomology.md),
  for Serre vanishing and the generation of twists by global sections on $\bar Z$ (item 2), and for
  the projection formula and the second degenerate case of the Leray spectral sequence (item 1)
- [Logarithmic de Rham complexes and E₁-degeneration](logarithmic-de-rham-complexes-and-e1-degeneration.md),
  for Esnault and Viehweg's Claim 5.13, the invariance of the inverse eigensheaves under a proper
  birational morphism along which the divisor keeps normal crossings, stated there with the inputs
  of its proof
- [Resolution of singularities of quasi-projective varieties](resolution-of-singularities/resolution-of-singularities-of-quasi-projective-varieties.md),
  for a smooth projective model of the compactification
- [Principalization of ideal sheaves in characteristic zero](resolution-of-singularities/principalization-of-ideal-sheaves.md),
  for making the pull-back of $\bar\Delta$ a divisor with snc support on that model
- [Blow-ups along smooth centres and total transforms](resolution-of-singularities/blow-ups-along-smooth-centres.md),
  for the blow-up of the closure of $\Delta$, an isomorphism over $Z$, where $\Delta$ is Cartier
  (items 3 and 4)
