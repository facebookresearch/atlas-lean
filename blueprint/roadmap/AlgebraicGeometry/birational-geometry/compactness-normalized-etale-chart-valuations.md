# Compact normalized weight families of closed-chart field valuations

Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $\sigma$ finite, and $P=k[X_i\mid i\in\sigma]$. Let $R$ be the local ring of $P$ at the ideal generated by all variables. Let $A$ be a Noetherian local domain with fraction field $K$ and compatible $P$-, $R$- and $k$-algebra structures; require $R\to A$ local, essentially of finite type and formally unramified, and $P\to A$ formally étale. The formal coordinate expansion is the map from $A$ into $k[[X_i:i\in\sigma]]$ given by the closed-chart completion isomorphism. For a nonnegative weight vector $w$, evaluate a nonzero power series by the infimum of the weighted total degrees of its nonzero monomials. On $K^\times$ use the difference of numerator and denominator values for the expansions of a fraction, and send $0$ to $+\infty$.

For coefficients $a_i>0$, the family of these additive valuations indexed by $w_i\ge0$ with $\sum_i a_iw_i=1$ is compact in the pointwise topology on functions $K\to\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$. Zero weights are allowed. This is a one-chart compactness assertion, not a uniform divisor or complement theorem.

## Depends on

- The specified local formally étale chart, formal expansion, additive monomial valuations including boundary weights, and their extension to the fraction field.

## Proof depends on

- Compactness of the normalized weight simplex and pointwise continuity of extended valuations, including at zero weights.

## Expansion

Prove faithful expansion and finiteness on nonzero regular elements. Show continuity for series by reducing the weight infimum to finitely many coordinate-minimal exponents, then extend to fractions with a separate check at the zero function argument. Take the continuous image of the compact simplex.
