# Kodaira vanishing theorem

Let $X$ be an integral smooth projective variety of dimension $n$ over a field of characteristic
zero, with canonical sheaf $\omega_X = \Omega^n_X$, and let $A$ be an ample invertible sheaf on $X$.
Then

$$H^b(X, \omega_X \otimes A) = 0 \quad \text{for } b > 0, \qquad \text{equivalently} \qquad H^b(X, A^{-1}) = 0 \quad \text{for } b < n,$$

the two forms being equivalent by Serre duality.

Esnault and Viehweg state the theorem for complex projective manifolds (Theorem 1.2) and note that,
flat base change extends it to any field $k$ of characteristic zero: $X$ and $A$ are defined over a
finitely generated subfield $k_0 \subset k$, which embeds into $\mathbb{C}$, and the cohomology over
$k$ and over $\mathbb{C}$ is obtained from that over $k_0$ by the faithfully flat extensions $k_0
\subset k$ and $k_0 \subset \mathbb{C}$; their introduction recalls that GAGA and base change for
field extensions give the algebraic statement. It is the case $D = 0$, $L$ ample, of the
[Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](kawamata-viehweg-vanishing-theorem.md) (Lazarsfeld, after
Theorem 2.2).

Esnault and Viehweg give an algebraic proof (Corollary 5.2). Choose $N \ge 2$ such that $A^N$ is
generated by its global sections and $H^b(X, A^{-N-1}) = 0$ for $b < n$, and let $D$ be the zero
divisor of a general section of $A^N$. Theorem 5.1 a), applied to $L = A$, to the divisor $D$ (with
coefficient $1 < N$) and to $D' = D$, shows that $H^b(X, A^{-1}(-D)) \to H^b(X, A^{-1})$ is
surjective for every $b$; as $A^{-1}(-D) \cong A^{-N-1}$, the target vanishes for $b < n$. Theorem
5.1 is their vanishing theorem for restriction maps, which they derive from the $E_1$-degeneration
of the logarithmic de Rham complexes of cyclic covers.

- [Geometrically reduced variety over a field](../schemes-and-morphisms/varieties-over-a-field/is-variety.md),

## Proof depends on

- [Logarithmic de Rham complexes and E₁-degeneration](logarithmic-de-rham-complexes-and-e1-degeneration.md),
  for Theorem 5.1 a) of Esnault–Viehweg
- [Standard results of coherent cohomology on projective varieties](standard-results-of-coherent-cohomology.md),
  for Serre vanishing and Serre duality, and Bertini's theorem for the general section of $A^N$, whose
  zero divisor is smooth
