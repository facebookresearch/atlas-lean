# A pulled-back function with a single simple zero makes its place principal

Let `R` be a
Dedekind domain with fraction field `K`, `μ` a ring endomorphism of `K`, and `N` a surjective
partial map on the height-one primes of `R` such that, whenever `N v = some w`, for every natural
number `k` a function with valuation at most `exp (-k)` at `w` (vanishing to order at least `k`)
is sent by `μ` to a function with valuation at most `exp (-k)` at `v`. If `g = μ h` has valuation
`exp (-1)` at every place `v` with `N v = some t₀` and valuation `1` at every place `v` with
`N v = some w` for `w ≠ t₀`, then `t₀` is principal.
