# A pulled-back function with a single simple zero makes its place principal

Let `R` be a
Dedekind domain with fraction field `K`, `μ` an endomorphism of `K`, and `N` a surjective partial
map on the height-one primes of `R` along which pulling back by `μ` does not lower vanishing
orders. If `g = μ h` has valuation `exp (-1)` at every place `v` with `N v = some t₀` and
valuation `1` at every place `v` with `N v = some w` for `w ≠ t₀`, then `t₀` is principal.
