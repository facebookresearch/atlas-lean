# Degree of a field over the image of an endomorphism

Let `L` be a field extension of `K`
generated by `X` and `Y`, where `Y ^ 2 + A * Y + B = 0` with `A` and `B` in `K⟮X⟯`. Let `μ` and
`ι` be `K`-endomorphisms of `L` such that `ι` fixes `X`, sends `Y` to a root of the same quadratic
and moves `μ Y`. If `Φ(X) = μ(X) * Ψ(X)` and `Φ - c * Ψ` has degree `d > 0` for every `c` in every
intermediate field, then `L` is finite over the image of `μ`, of degree at most `d`.
