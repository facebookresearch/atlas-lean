# A translation-invariant function with simple zeros over `T` forces `T = 0`

Let `g` be a
function on `W⁄K` that is fixed by translation by every `n`-torsion point and that, at every affine
point `P` with `n • P ≠ 0`, has a simple zero (valuation `exp (-1)`) when `n • P = T` and valuation
`1` otherwise. Then `T = 0`. Here `(n : K) ≠ 0`, the coordinate ring of `W⁄K` is a Dedekind domain,
and `hpull` says that pulling back by `mulPull` along `P ↦ n • P` does not lower vanishing
orders.
