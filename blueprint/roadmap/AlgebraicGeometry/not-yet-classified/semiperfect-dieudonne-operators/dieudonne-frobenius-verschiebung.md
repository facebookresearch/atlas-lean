# Dieudonné Frobenius and Verschiebung over a semiperfect ring

Let $p$ be a prime and $R$ a
semiperfect ring of characteristic $p$, with the given crystalline data (the universal PD hull $A$
with the identification $E$ of its reduction with $R$, divided powers $P$ on its finite stages, a
Frobenius $F_0$ of the hull, its completion $\varphi$ on $A_{\mathrm{cris}}(R)$ and their
compatibility $C$); let $G$ be a $p$-divisible group over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ with Cartier dual
tower $D$, Frobenius and Verschiebung data $Q$ and comparison data $N$. Then the Frobenius
base-change map `nativeComparison`, from $\varphi^* M(G)$ to $M(G^{(p)})$, where $M$ is the integral
Dieudonné module over $A_{\mathrm{cris}}(R)$, is an isomorphism, and with it as base change the
operators $F : \varphi^* M(G) \to M(G)$, induced by the Verschiebung of $G$, and
$V : M(G) \to \varphi^* M(G)$, induced by its Frobenius, satisfy $F \circ V = p$ and $V \circ F = p$
(`OperatorsNormalization`).
