# Native mapping tangent sources

For a noetherian `ℚ`-algebra `k`, derived stacks `X` and `F` over `k` with `X` `𝒪`-compact for
a sourced scalar family `BX` (`compact`) and `F` derived Artin and locally of finite presentation,
and a derived stack `Map` whose functor is the mapping prestack `Map(X, F)` and which is derived
Artin and locally of finite presentation: data `MappingTangentSources E` for the evaluation map
`E`. These consist of the cotangent complexes of `Map(X, F)` and of `F` with their dual tangent
complexes; quasi-coherent modules on `Map(X, F) × X`, with pullback along the projection to
`Map(X, F)` and along the evaluation map to `F`; the relative evaluation and its partial
differential; the duality pairings between tangent and cotangent complexes, pulled back to
`Map(X, F) × X`; and pushforward along the projection, right adjoint to pullback in all
degrees.
