# Stable pair obstruction theory

The Pandharipande–Thomas map of a family of stable pairs is a perfect obstruction theory. Let
$X$ be a smooth projective threefold over $\mathbb{C}$ (`Threefold`) and $F$ a family of stable
pairs on $X$ over a $\mathbb{C}$-scheme $P$ that represents its image (`RepresentsImage`: every
family over a $\mathbb{C}$-scheme $T$ whose geometric fibres are isomorphic to geometric fibres of
$F$ is, up to isomorphism, the pullback of $F$ along a unique morphism $T \to P$). Given derived
pullbacks, direct images and tensor products, and the construction data `M` (`MapConstruction`) of
the map $\varphi : \mathbb{E} \to \mathbb{L}_P$ of Pandharipande–Thomas, equation (2.13), from
$\mathbb{E} = R\pi_{P*}(R\mathcal{H}om(\mathbb{I}, \mathbb{I})_0 \otimes \omega_\pi)[2]$, where
$\omega_\pi$ is the pullback of the canonical sheaf of $X$, there are a cochain complex $E$ that is
locally quasi-isomorphic to a two-term complex of free modules of finite rank in degrees $-1$ and
$0$ and an isomorphism from $E$ to $\mathbb{E}$ in the derived category, such that the composite
$E \to \mathbb{E} \to \mathbb{L}_P$ induces an isomorphism on $H^0$ and an epimorphism on
$H^{-1}$.
