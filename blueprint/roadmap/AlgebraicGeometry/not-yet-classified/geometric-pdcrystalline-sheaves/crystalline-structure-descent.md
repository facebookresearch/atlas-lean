# Descent for the structure ring of the crystalline site

Let $p$ be a prime, $(A, J, γ)$ a
divided-power ring and $S$ a scheme over $\operatorname{Spec}(A/J)$. On the site of nilpotent
divided-power thickenings of $S$ over $A$ (thickenings
$\operatorname{Spec}(B/I) ⊆ \operatorname{Spec} B$ over $S$ whose divided powers are compatible with
$γ$ and nilpotent, with $p$ nilpotent in $B$), the presheaf of rings $(B, I) ↦ B$ is a sheaf for the
topology generated by open covers of the thickenings.
