# The loop group action on the Witt vector affine Grassmannian

Let $p$ be a prime, $k$ a
perfect field of characteristic $p$, $B$ a complete discrete valuation ring, finite and totally
ramified over $W(k)$, with uniformizer π and residue field $k$ (`CoefficientData`), and $G$ an
affine smooth group scheme over $B$ with geometrically connected fibres. Then the loop group $LG$
acts on the left on the quotient presheaf $LG/L^+G$ by multiplication of representatives; $LG$ and
the positive loop group $L^+G$ are fpqc sheaves on perfect $k$-algebras; and the sheaf $LG$ acts on
the fpqc Grassmannian sheaf, compatibly with its action on the quotient presheaf (`QuotientActions`;
Zhu, arXiv:1407.8519, Sections 1.1–1.2).
