# Formal De Rham Models

Normalized de Rham hypermodels of a formal scheme: for a prime $p$, a bounded prism $(A, I)$ and
a $p$-adic formal scheme $X$ over $A/I$, given by its levels $X_n$ over $A/(I + (p)^{n+1})$, a
`System` for $X$: for each level, a de Rham complex of $X_n$ over $A/I$ with a quasi-isomorphism to
a hypermodel that computes derived global sections (it maps to a termwise injective resolution by a
quasi-isomorphism that stays one on global sections), with exact inverse images along the
transitions, and restriction maps between consecutive levels that are fixed on the local generators
$a\,db_1 ∧ ⋯ ∧ db_i$ and compatible, up to homotopy, with the comparison maps of the hypermodels.
