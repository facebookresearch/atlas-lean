# Global Closed Pullback Of

The global closed-form pullback along $f : X \to Y$ defined by `ℓ`, for any natural
transformation `ℓ` from the replaced closed-form diagram of `MY`, reindexed along `f`, to that of
`MX` (not necessarily a lift of the augmentations): the map
`globalClosedForms MY ⟶ globalClosedForms MX` into the limit induced by the cone whose legs are the
limit projections of `MY` at the reindexed families followed by `ℓ`.
