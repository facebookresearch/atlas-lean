# Correspondence Calculus

For a chosen dualizing complex $D_X$ on the space of complex points of every complex variety
$X$, chosen proper-pullback data $P_f$ for every proper morphism $f$ of complex varieties and a
chosen cycle realization $S$ (`CycleRealization D P`), assuming that the product of two smooth
projective complex varieties is a smooth projective complex variety of the summed dimension
(`ProductGeometry`): a correspondence calculus (`CorrespondenceCalculus D P S`) on rational Chow
correspondences between smooth projective complex varieties, namely a bilinear, associative
composition with the diagonal as identity, which sends the pushed fundamental cycles of proper
maps from smooth projective varieties of the expected dimensions, whenever their middle fibre
product is a smooth variety of the expected dimension mapping properly to the product, to the
pushed fundamental cycle of that fibre product, and under which the Betti action of
correspondences is functorial.
