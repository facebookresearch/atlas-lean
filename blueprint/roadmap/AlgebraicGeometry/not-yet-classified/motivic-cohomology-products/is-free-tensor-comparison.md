# Is Free Tensor Comparison

The condition that, for all pointed spaces $X$ and $Y$, the canonical comparison map
$C(X) \otimes^L C(Y) \to C(X) \otimes C(Y)$ from the derived to the ordinary tensor product of
their reduced free complexes $C(X)$ and $C(Y)$ (`tensorComparison`, induced by the K-flat
resolutions of both factors) is an isomorphism in the derived category of Nisnevich sheaves.
