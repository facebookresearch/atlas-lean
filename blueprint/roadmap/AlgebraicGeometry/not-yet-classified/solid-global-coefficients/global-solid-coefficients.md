# Global Solid Coefficients

For a prime $p$: a local coefficient diagram $G$ over the good affine perfectoid spaces
(`LocalDiagram`: local solid coefficients, pullbacks along all maps and an assembled diagram of
simplicial sets, chosen jointly), together with a global model $M$ of its limits (`GlobalModel`:
a functor on v-sheaves identified with the categorical limits of the diagram over their affine
tests) that is affine-normalized (`IsAffineNormalized`: on each good affine it is identified with
the local coefficients by categorical equivalences) and satisfies v-hyperdescent
(`IsHypercomplete`).
