# Stack Coefficient Limit

For a prime $p$, a local coefficient diagram $G$ (`LocalDiagram`) and a perfectoid v-stack $F$
(`PerfdVStack`): a model (`DiagramModel`: an injectively fibrant replacement with a monomorphic
augmentation that is an objectwise categorical equivalence) of the diagram of $G$ over the chart
site of $F$ (`stackDiagram`).
