# Integral Sections Descent

For a prime $p$, a v-sheaf $F$ on affine perfectoid spaces (`VSheaf`) and a compatible family
$D$ of untilts of its affine tests (`UntiltingDiagram`): the presheaf $(T, S) ↦ C(S, A_T^+)$ of
continuous maps from a compact Hausdorff space $S$ to the plus ring of the untilt of the affine
test $T$ (`integralPresheaf`) is a sheaf for the coefficient topology (`coefficientTopology`),
which is generated by the v-covers of affine tests and the coherent covers of compact Hausdorff
spaces.
