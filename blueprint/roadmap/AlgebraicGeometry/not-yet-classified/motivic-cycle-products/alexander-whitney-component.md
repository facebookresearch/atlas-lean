# Alexander Whitney Component

The degree-$n$ component of the Alexander–Whitney map, for simplicial objects $F$ and $H$ in
Nisnevich sheaves of abelian groups on smooth $k$-schemes and the given tensor product `N`: the map
from $(F \otimes H)_n = F_n \otimes H_n$ to the degree-$n$ term of the total tensor complex of the
alternating face complexes of $F$ and $H$ that is the sum over $0 \le a \le n$ of the maps
$F_n \otimes H_n \to F_a \otimes H_{n-a}$ induced by the front face $[0, a] \subseteq [n]$ on $F$
and the back face $[a, n] \subseteq [n]$ on $H$, each followed by the inclusion of the summand
$F_a \otimes H_{n-a}$.
