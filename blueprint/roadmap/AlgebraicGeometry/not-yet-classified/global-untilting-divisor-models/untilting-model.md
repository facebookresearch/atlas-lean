# Untilting Model

A global untilt of $S$ over $E$, glued from untilts of the perfectoid charts of $S$ in the atlas
of `F`: an $E$-space with, for each chart $i$, an untilt of the chart's base and a map from it to
the space that is an open embedding inducing isomorphisms on stalks, these images covering the
space, and a tilt comparison of each local untilt; and an isomorphism from the diamond of the space
to the perfectoid sheaf of $S$ under which the diamond of each chart map is the local tilt
comparison followed by the chart of $S$.
