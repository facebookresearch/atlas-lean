# Representability of the formal tangent functor of a p-divisible group

For a prime $p$, a
$p$-adically complete ring $R$ and a $p$-divisible group $G$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$, a finite
projective $R$-module $\omega$ with a natural isomorphism
$\operatorname{Hom}_R(\omega, M) \cong \ker(G(C \oplus M) \to G(C))$ over all tests $(C, M)$ of a
$p$-nilpotent $R$-algebra $C$ with a square-zero module $M$ (`LieModel`).
