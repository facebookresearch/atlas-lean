# Computed global exterior quasi Iso

Let $k$ be a Noetherian commutative $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra and $X$ a derived Artin stack locally
of finite presentation over $k$ (`geometric`, `lfp`), with the given model data (`Q`, `D`, `NC`,
`N`, `S`, `M`, `U`, `R`, `L`), quasi-coherent enhancement `G`, cotangent context `CY`, monoidal
enhancement `H`, exterior square `E` of the cotangent object $L_X$, universal exterior families
`B`, exterior cone `C`, and global weight-$2$ models `FF` of $X$ and `FP` of its affine parameter
prestack, such that the coefficient weight functor preserves the limit of the ordinary diagram of
`FP` and the weight restriction along the affine parameter point is a quasi-isomorphism. Then for
every cochain representative `Eη` (`GlobalExteriorRepresentative`) of the computed map
$R\Gamma(X, \wedge^2 L_X) \to \mathrm{DR}(X)(2)[-2]$ (`globalExteriorClass`), the cochain map
`Eη.map` is a quasi-isomorphism.
