# Operators Normalization

Covariant Dieudonné operators whose Frobenius base change is the canonical comparison map $c$.
Let $G$ be a $p$-divisible group over a ring $R$ of characteristic $p$, for a prime $p$ and the
given crystalline data with Frobenius $\varphi$ of $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and let $c$
(`nativeComparison`) be the $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$-linear map $\varphi^* M(G) \to M(G^{(p)})$ adjoint
to the $\varphi$-semilinear limit of the crystalline restrictions along the PD Frobenius, where $M$
is the integral Dieudonné module and $G^{(p)}$ the Frobenius twist. The structure consists of: $c$
is an isomorphism; operators `OperatorsRealization` (a base-change isomorphism
$\varphi^* M(G) \cong M(G^{(p)})$ for which $F$, induced by the Verschiebung of $G$, and $V$,
induced by its relative Frobenius, satisfy $F \circ V = p$ and $V \circ F = p$); and their
base-change isomorphism is $c$.
