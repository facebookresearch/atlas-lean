# Cotangent Representation

A cotangent object of the derived prestack $X$ at a point $x$ of $X(A)$, for a simplicial
commutative $k$-algebra $A$ with normalization $N(A)$, represented on split square-zero
extensions of $A$ (homotopy fibres being formed with the path model $P$): a cofibrant right DG
module $L$ over $N(A)$ (`module`); for every split square-zero extension $E \to A$ and every model
$V$ of its normalized kernel as an $N(A)$-module, a map from the simplicial set of the DG Hom
complex $\mathrm{Hom}(L, V)$ to the homotopy fibre of $X(E) \to X(A)$ over $x$ (`map`), which is a
weak equivalence (`weak`) and sends the zero map to the lift of $x$ through the splitting
$A \to E$ (`zero`); and these maps are natural in every morphism of kernel models $V \to W$ over a
morphism of split extensions $E \to F$ of $A$ (`naturality`).
