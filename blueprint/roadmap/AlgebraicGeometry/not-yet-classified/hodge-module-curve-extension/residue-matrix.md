# Residue Matrix

The matrix, in the coordinates $e$, of
$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\sum_{0 < m < b} \frac{(-1)^{m+1}}{m}(T - 1)^m$, where $T$ is the complex deck
transformation of the period variation $H$: the logarithm series of $T$ truncated below degree $b$,
divided by $2\pi i$. When $(T - 1)^b = 0$ it is the matrix of $\log T / 2\pi i$; for $b \le 1$ it is
zero.
