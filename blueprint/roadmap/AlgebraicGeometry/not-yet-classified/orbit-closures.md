# The torus-orbit closures of a toric variety

For a finite fan $F$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and a
proper complex scheme $X$ identified with a realization of $F$ by affine charts $U_\tau$
(`Model F`): for every cone $\sigma$ of $F$, an integral proper complex scheme $V(\sigma)$ of
dimension $n - \dim \sigma$ with a closed immersion into $X$, such that for every cone $\tau$ the
ideal of $V(\sigma) \cap U_\tau$ in $\mathbb{C}[\tau^\vee \cap \mathbb{Z}^n]$ is generated by the
characters of $\tau^\vee \cap \mathbb{Z}^n$ not vanishing on $\sigma$ when $\sigma$ is a face of
$\tau$, and is the unit ideal otherwise.
