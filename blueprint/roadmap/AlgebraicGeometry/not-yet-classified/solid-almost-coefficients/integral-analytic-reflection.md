# Integral Analytic Reflection

Let $p$ be a prime and $(R, R^+)$ a perfectoid pair with $R \neq 0$; take the given derived
restriction data $E$ along $\mathbb{Z} \to R^+$ and along $\mathbb{Z}[T] \to R^+$, $T \mapsto a$,
for $a \in R^+$, and assume that the unit object $R^+$ is analytically complete (its restrictions
are derived $\mathbb{Z}$-solid and $\mathbb{Z}[T]$-solid). Then there is a reflection of the derived
category of condensed $R^+$-modules onto the analytically complete objects: an endofunctor with a
unit transformation whose values are analytically complete and through which every map to an
analytically complete object factors uniquely (`AnalyticReflection`).
