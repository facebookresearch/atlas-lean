# Completed Integral Compact Model

Let $p$ be a prime, $(R, R^+)$ a perfectoid pair with $R \neq 0$, $E$ the given derived
restriction data, $L$ an analytic reflection, $I$ a finitely generated ideal of $R^+$ such that
$R^+$ carries the $I$-adic topology and is $I$-adically complete, and $C$ a reflection onto derived
$I$-complete objects; assume that the unit object $R^+$ is analytically complete and $I$-complete.
Then there is a small DG model of the completed compact objects (`CompactDGModel`): a small DG
category with a quasi-fully faithful DG functor to K-injective complexes of condensed $R^+$-modules
whose images are, up to isomorphism in the derived category, exactly the objects of the thick
subcategory generated by the completed analytic generators $C(L(R^+[S]))$, $S$ profinite.
