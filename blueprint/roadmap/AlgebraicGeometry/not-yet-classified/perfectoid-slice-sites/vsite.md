# VSite

The v-site $F_v$ of a small v-sheaf $F$ (Scholze, *Étale cohomology of diamonds*,
Definition 14.1(iii)), as a category: the slice category over $F$ of small v-sheaves, whose
objects are all maps $F' \to F$ from small v-sheaves $F'$; its topology `siteTopology` is the
slice of the topology generated by jointly epimorphic families. Here $p$ is a prime, v-sheaves are
sheaves for the v-topology on affinoid perfectoid spaces of characteristic $p$, and a v-sheaf is
small when it admits a jointly epimorphic family of maps from affinoid perfectoid spaces indexed
in a fixed universe.
