# Period Generator Congruence

Let $p$ be a prime, $(P, I)$ a perfect prism and $\varepsilon \in P$ a unit with
$\varphi(\varepsilon) = \varepsilon^p$ and $\varepsilon \equiv 1 \pmod I$, such that $I$ is
generated by $\xi = \sum_{k < p} \varphi^{-1}(\varepsilon)^k$. Then
$\varphi^{n+2}(\xi) - p \in \varphi(I)\varphi^2(I) \cdots \varphi^{n+1}(I)$ for every $n \ge 0$.
