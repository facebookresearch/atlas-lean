# Native smooth pseudo perfect is perfect

Pseudo-perfect complexes on a smooth proper family are perfect. Let $D$ be a proper, flat
morphism $X \to Y$ of algebraic spaces, locally of finite presentation, and $T$ an affine test
$\operatorname{Spec} A \to Y$ with base change $X_T \to \operatorname{Spec} A$, for the given
étale-site interface (`SourceTheory`, `O`, `L`, `J`, `R`) and scalar linearity `S`. Assume that
every étale chart of $X_T$ is smooth over $\operatorname{Spec} A$ (`smooth`). Then every object $E$
of the quasi-coherent DG category of $X_T$ (K-injective complexes that are quasi-coherent on every
affine chart) that is pseudo-perfect, meaning that for every K-injective perfect complex $P$ on
$X_T$ the $A$-linear Hom complex from $P$ to $E$ is a perfect complex of $A$-modules, is perfect:
its restriction to every affine étale chart $\operatorname{Spec} B$ of $X_T$ is isomorphic to the
complex associated with a bounded complex of finitely generated projective $B$-modules.
