# Affine QCoh Normalization

Normalization of quasi-coherent modules on the affine derived prestack `affineMapping k Q A` of
a simplicial $k$-algebra $A$ (built with the replacement functor $Q$), for the module fibration $M$
and the regular unit system $U$: evaluation at the augmentation family, from the quasi-coherent
modules on this prestack to the fibre of $M$ over $A$, is a categorical (Joyal) weak equivalence;
and a map `inverse` from that fibre back to the quasi-coherent modules is given such that `inverse`
followed by evaluation is the identity in the localization at categorical weak equivalences and
`inverse` sends the regular module of $A$ (a vertex of the fibre through `M.realization A`) to the
structure section of $U$ on the prestack.
