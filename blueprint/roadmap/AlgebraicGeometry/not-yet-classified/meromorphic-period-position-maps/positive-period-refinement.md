# Positive Period Refinement

For a prime $p$ and the given marked-untilt data, the ring map between the period rings
$B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ (`DeRhamPlus`) of the period charts $V$ and $V'$ induced by a refinement $r$ of
the pole chart $Q$ by $Q'$, with coefficient refinement data `Rc`: the inverse of the positive
period isomorphism of the bridge `B`, from the completed chart ring of $Q$ to $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$
of $V$, followed by the induced map `positiveMap` of completed chart rings from $Q$ to $Q'$ and by
the positive period isomorphism of the bridge `B'`.
