# Igusa Scheme

The Igusa scheme of level `N` at the prime `ℓ`: the pushout of `fFin N ℓ` and `fInf N ℓ`. It
glues the spectra of the integral closures of `ℤ_(ℓ)[j]` and `ℤ_(ℓ)[j⁻¹]` in
`modularFunctionFieldFull N` along the spectrum of the integral closure of `ℤ_(ℓ)[j, j⁻¹]`, where
`ℤ_(ℓ)` is `Rat.localizedAt ℓ`. Despite its name, this scheme is not defined as a moduli space of
elliptic curves with an Igusa structure.
