# Integrality of the Igusa scheme

For every level `N ≠ 0` and every prime `ℓ`, the Igusa
scheme `ModularCurve.IgusaScheme N ℓ` is an integral scheme. Writing `ℤ_(ℓ)` for
`Rat.localizedAt ℓ`, the rationals whose denominator is prime to `ℓ`, this scheme glues the spectra
of the integral closures of `ℤ_(ℓ)[j]` and `ℤ_(ℓ)[j⁻¹]` in the field generated over `ℚ` by the
`q`-expansions `j(q^d)` for `d ∣ N`, along their common localization.
