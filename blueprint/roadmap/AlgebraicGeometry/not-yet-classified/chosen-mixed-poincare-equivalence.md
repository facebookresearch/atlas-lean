# Mixed Fourier–Mukai equivalence of fine compactified Jacobians

Let $k$ be an algebraically
closed field and $C$ a reduced, connected, projective, locally planar proper curve over $k$ with its
torsion-free restrictions to subcurves, where $k$ has characteristic $0$ or a prime
$p > \dim_k H^1(C, \mathcal{O}_C)$; let $q$, $q'$ be nondegenerate polarizations, $s$ a smooth
marking, and $\bar J$, $\bar J'$ fine compactified Jacobians for $(q, s)$ and $(q', s)$, with their
universal sheaves; take the given determinant data $S$, $A$, line loci $L$, $L'$, open cohomology
data $D$ and product coordinates $P$, and assume that derived pullbacks, scalar extensions,
pushforwards and tensor products exist and that the tilde functors preserve finite limits. Then the
Fourier–Mukai functors with the kernel `forwardKernel`, built from the chosen universal sheaves, and
the inverse kernel `reverseKernel` restrict to mutually quasi-inverse equivalences between the
bounded derived categories of $\bar J$ and $\bar J'$ with quasi-coherent cohomology, and likewise
with coherent cohomology (`MixedFourierMukaiEquivalence`; MRV II, Theorem A).
