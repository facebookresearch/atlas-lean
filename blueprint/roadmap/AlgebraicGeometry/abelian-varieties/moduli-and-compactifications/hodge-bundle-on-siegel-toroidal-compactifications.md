# Hodge bundle on toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli schemes

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the precise form of the toroidal compactifications
of the Siegel moduli schemes, and of the positivity of their Hodge bundle, that Alpöge's library
uses; the general theory is outlined in the node on
[moduli of principally polarized abelian varieties](moduli-of-principally-polarized-abelian-varieties.md).
The target is the following.

For every $g \ge 1$ there is a polarized admissible cone decomposition $\varphi$ such that, for
every odd $n \ge 3$, the moduli scheme $X$ of principally polarized abelian schemes of relative
dimension $g$ with level-$n$ structure over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, with its universal abelian
scheme $\mathcal{A}$, has an open immersion $j \colon X \to S$ into a scheme $S$ over
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, together with **one** effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $S$ and integers
$d, N \ge 1$, such that:

1. $S$ is projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$,
   and every point of $S$ lies in the closure of $j(X)$ within its fibre;
2. there is a semi-abelian scheme $G$ over $S$, smooth of relative dimension $g$, with $G|_X \cong
   \mathcal{A}$, whose abelian locus is exactly $j(X)$;
3. at **every** point $x$ of $S$ there is a chart for $\varphi$, $B$ and $d$: a complete regular
   local ring $R$ with a morphism $\operatorname{Spec} R \to S$ sending the closed point to $x$ that
   induces an étale completion of the local ring at $x$ (with formally unramified residue
   extension), and relative parameters $t_1, \dots, t_m$ of $R$ such that the boundary $S \setminus
   j(X)$ pulls back to $(t_1 \cdots t_m)$; a finite injective local extension $R \to R'$ to a domain
   of characteristic zero containing square roots $r_i$ of the $t_i$, over which the pull-back of
   $G$ is given by Mumford's construction from degeneration data with period forms $\gamma_1, \dots,
   \gamma_m$ in a cone of $\varphi$, the periods being monomials in the $r_i$ with exponents
   $2n\gamma_i$; and $B$ pulls back to $(\prod_i t_i^{a_i})$ with $a_i = d\, \varphi(n\gamma_i)$;
4. for the Hodge bundle $\omega = \det e^* \Omega^1_{G/S}$, $\omega^N$ is generated by global
   sections, and $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample for all large $k$, with the same $B$
   as in 3.

The quantifiers matter: $\varphi$ depends only on $g$, and the divisor $B$ and the integers $d$, $N$
are chosen once for $g$ and $n$, for all points $x$, and $B$ is the divisor of both 3 and 4.

A good expansion has pages for the existence of smooth projective admissible cone decompositions for
the Siegel group, the toroidal compactification attached to such a decomposition over
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ with its semi-abelian extension and local charts (Faltings–Chai, chapter
"Toroidal Compactification of $A_g$"; Lan, Theorem 6.1), the global generation of a power of the
Hodge bundle through the minimal compactification, and the ampleness of $\omega^{Nk} \otimes
\mathcal{O}(-D')$ for a boundary-supported effective divisor $D'$ with $\mathcal{O}(-D')$ relatively
ample over the minimal compactification (Lan, Corollary 6.7). It remains open, with no source
identified, that such a $D'$ can be taken to be the divisor $B$ of 3, whose local equations are
prescribed by the values $d\,\varphi(n\gamma_i)$ of the fan on the period forms of the charts.

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- one polarized cone decomposition serving all the levels $n$ of the statement, for its quantifiers
- the charts of 3, at every point of $S$ and in the detail stated there, beyond the local charts of
  the toroidal compactification (Lan, Theorem 6.1), for 3
