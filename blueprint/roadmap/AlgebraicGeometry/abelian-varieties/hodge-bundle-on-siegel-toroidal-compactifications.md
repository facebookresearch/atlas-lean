# Hodge bundle on toroidal compactifications of Siegel moduli schemes

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the precise form of the toroidal compactifications of
the Siegel moduli schemes, and of the positivity of their Hodge bundle, that Alpöge's library uses; the
general theory is outlined in the node on [moduli of principally polarized abelian
varieties](moduli-of-principally-polarized-abelian-varieties.md). The target is the following.

For every $g \ge 1$ there is a polarized admissible cone decomposition $\varphi$ such that, for every
odd $n \ge 3$, the moduli scheme $X$ of principally polarized abelian schemes of relative dimension $g$
with level-$n$ structure over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, with its universal abelian scheme
$\mathcal{A}$, has an open immersion $j \colon X \to S$ into a scheme $S$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$
such that:

1. $S$ is projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$, and
   $j(X)$ is dense in every fibre;
2. there is a semi-abelian scheme $G$ over $S$, smooth of relative dimension $g$, with $G|_X \cong
   \mathcal{A}$, whose abelian locus is exactly $j(X)$;
3. near every point, $S$ has charts given by $\varphi$, with an effective Cartier divisor $B$ supported on
   the boundary and an integer $d \ge 1$ controlling the charts;
4. for the Hodge bundle $\omega = \det e^* \Omega^1_{G/S}$, some power $\omega^N$, $N \ge 1$, is
   generated by global sections, and $\omega^{Nk} \otimes \mathcal{O}(-B)$ is ample for all large $k$.

A good expansion has pages for the existence of smooth projective admissible cone decompositions for
the Siegel group, the toroidal compactification attached to such a decomposition over
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_n, 1/n]$ with its semi-abelian extension and local charts (Faltings–Chai, chapter
"Toroidal Compactification of $A_g$"; Lan, Theorem 6.1), the global generation of a power of the Hodge
bundle through the minimal compactification, and the relative ampleness of $\mathcal{O}(-D')$ for a
boundary-supported divisor $D'$ over the minimal compactification (Lan, Corollary 6.7), which, with the
ampleness of $\omega$ on the minimal compactification, gives 4.
