# Deformations and p-divisible groups

cohomology of abelian schemes with its Hodge filtration, the Grothendieck–Messing theorem,
deformations of abelian schemes and of their homomorphisms, and Grothendieck's existence theorem, by
which proper formal schemes carrying an invertible sheaf ample on the closed fibre are algebraized,
and morphisms between completions of proper schemes algebraize uniquely.
