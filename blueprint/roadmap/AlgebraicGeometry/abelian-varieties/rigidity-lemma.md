# Rigidity lemma

Let $k$ be a field and $S$ a connected variety over $k$. Let $\pi \colon V \to S$ be a proper flat
morphism whose fibres are geometrically connected varieties, let $\pi' \colon V' \to S$ be a variety
over $S$, and let $\alpha \colon V \to V'$ be a morphism over $S$. If for some point $s$ of $S$ the
image $\alpha(V_s)$ of the fibre $V_s$ in $V'_s$ is a single point, then $\alpha$ factors through
$\pi$: there is a morphism $\alpha' \colon S \to V'$ with $\alpha = \alpha' \circ \pi$.

In other words, if $\alpha$ is constant on one fibre of $\pi$, it is constant on every fibre. Milne
states the lemma in his section on families of abelian varieties, refers to Mumford's *Geometric
Invariant Theory* for the proof, and uses it to show that
[morphisms of abelian schemes preserving the zero section are homomorphisms](homomorphisms-of-abelian-schemes.md).
