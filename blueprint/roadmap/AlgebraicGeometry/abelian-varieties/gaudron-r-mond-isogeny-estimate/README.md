# Gaudron–Rémond isogeny estimate

The pages after the constant follow the proof of the isogeny estimate in É. Gaudron and G. Rémond,
*Polarisations et isogénies*, Duke Math. J. 163 (2014), §§6–9: their period theorem gives an abelian
subvariety of bounded degree, which bounds the last minima of lattices of homomorphisms with their
Rosati metrics; geometry of numbers in these lattices, after Zarhin's trick, turns the bounds into
isogenies of bounded degree; and Raynaud's estimate removes the auxiliary heights.
