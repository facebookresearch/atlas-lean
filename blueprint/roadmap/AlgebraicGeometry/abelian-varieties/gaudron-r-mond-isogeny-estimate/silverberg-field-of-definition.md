# Field of definition of the endomorphisms of a product of isogenous abelian varieties

Let $k$ be a number field, $A$ an [abelian variety](../abelian-scheme.md) of dimension $g$ over $k$
and $A'$ an abelian variety over $k$ that is isogenous to $A$ over some extension of $k$. Let $k_1$
be the smallest extension of $k$, inside a fixed algebraic closure $\bar k$, over which all
endomorphisms of $A \times A'$ are defined. Then $k_1$ is a finite Galois extension of $k$, it is
contained in the field generated over $k$ by the coordinates of the $3$-torsion points of $A \times
A'$, and

$$[k_1 : k] \le 4 (9g)^{4g}.$$

This is the form in which Gaudron and Rémond use a theorem of Silverberg on fields of definition of
homomorphisms of abelian varieties, at the start of the proofs of their isogeny estimate
(*Polarisations et isogénies*, §9, p. 2097); they note that $k_1$ is Galois over $k$ by its
definition (p. 2099). The bound enters through the factor $4(9g)^{4g}$ of the constant $P$ of the
[bounds for the last minima](last-minimum-bounds.md).

## Expansion

A good expansion reads Silverberg's paper, which could not be read here, states her theorem with its
exact hypotheses and locator, and derives from it the form above: the inclusion of $k_1$ in the field
of the $3$-torsion points and the bound for $[k_1 : k]$, with the Galois property of $k_1$, which
follows from its minimality.
