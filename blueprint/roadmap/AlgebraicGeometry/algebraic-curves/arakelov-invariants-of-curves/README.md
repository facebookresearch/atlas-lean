# Arakelov invariants of curves

Arakelov theory of curves over number fields, in the form used to bound the Faltings height of a
curve: the Arakelov $(1,1)$-form and the Arakelov–Green function of a compact Riemann surface,
Merkl's bound for the Arakelov–Green function in terms of an atlas, the Arakelov invariants of a
curve over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ (Faltings' delta invariant, the self-intersection of the
dualizing sheaf, the discriminant, the height of a point and the Arakelov norm of the Wronskian
differential), Noether's formula relating them to the Faltings height, and Faltings' bounds for the
self-intersection of the dualizing sheaf.
