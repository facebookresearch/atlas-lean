# Noether's formula for curves over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$

This node is still to be broken down. Let $X$ be a smooth projective connected curve over
$\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ of genus $g \ge 1$, with Faltings height $h_{\mathrm{Fal}}(X)$ and the
[Arakelov invariants](arakelov-invariants-of-a-curve.md) $e(X)$, $\Delta(X)$ and
$\delta_{\mathrm{Fal}}(X)$. Then

$$12 \, h_{\mathrm{Fal}}(X) = e(X) + \Delta(X) + \delta_{\mathrm{Fal}}(X) - 4g \log(2\pi).$$

Its proof uses the inputs listed under Expansion.

## Expansion

Javanpeykar cites the formula as well known, from Faltings and from Moret-Bailly; neither proof
was read here. A good expansion follows one of them, with pages for the metrized determinant of
cohomology of $\omega$ on a semistable arithmetic surface and the comparison of its arithmetic
degree with $(\omega, \omega)$, the discriminant and the archimedean contribution
$\delta_{\mathrm{Fal}} - 4g \log(2\pi)$.
