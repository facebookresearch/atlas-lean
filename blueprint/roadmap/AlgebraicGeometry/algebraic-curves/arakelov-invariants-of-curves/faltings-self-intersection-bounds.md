# Faltings' bounds for the self-intersection of the dualizing sheaf

This node is still to be broken down. Let $X$ be a smooth projective connected curve over
$\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ of genus $g \ge 1$, with self-intersection of the dualizing sheaf $e(X)$
and height function $h$ on $X(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})$
([Arakelov invariants](arakelov-invariants-of-a-curve.md)). Then $e(X) \ge 0$, and for every
$b \in X(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})$,

$$e(X) \le 4g(g - 1) \, h(b).$$

## Expansion

Javanpeykar cites both inequalities from Faltings' Theorem 5, which was not read here. A good
expansion reads that theorem and splits it into the nonnegativity of $(\omega, \omega)$ and the
upper bound by the height of a point, each with the properties of admissible metrics and of
Arakelov's intersection pairing it uses.
