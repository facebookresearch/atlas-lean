# Abel–Jacobi map

Let $C$ be a proper, smooth, geometrically irreducible curve over a field
$k$. Each $k$-rational point $P$ of $C$ gives a morphism of $k$-schemes $C \to J(C)$ from $C$ to
its Jacobian, the Abel–Jacobi map based at $P$.
