# Jacobian of a smooth proper curve

For a field $k$ and a curve $C$ over $k$, that is, a
$k$-scheme that is proper, smooth of relative dimension $1$ and geometrically irreducible, the
Jacobian $J(C)$ of $C$ is a $k$-scheme.
