# Uniform Mordell–Lang constant

For natural numbers $g$ and $d$, $c(g, d)$ is a natural
number: the constant in the uniform bound on rational points of curves in Theorem 1.1 of
Dimitrov, Gao and Habegger.

## Sources

- Vesselin Dimitrov, Ziyang Gao and Philipp Habegger, *Uniformity in Mordell–Lang for curves*, Annals of Mathematics 194 (2021), no. 1, 237–298, Theorem 1.1. Statement taken from https://github.com/ImperialCollegeLondon/AnnalsChallenge. Problem `annals_uniform_mordell_lang` of the lean-eval benchmark.
