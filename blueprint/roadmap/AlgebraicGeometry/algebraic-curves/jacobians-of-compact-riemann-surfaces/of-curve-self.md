# The Abel–Jacobi map sends the base point to zero

Let $X$ be a compact Riemann surface
with Jacobian $J(X)$, an abelian group. For every point $P \in X$, the Abel–Jacobi map
$X \to J(X)$ based at $P$ sends $P$ to $0$.
