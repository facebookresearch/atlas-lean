# Genus of a compact Riemann surface

For a compact Riemann surface $X$, that is, a compact
connected Hausdorff space with a complex-analytic atlas modelled on $\mathbb{C}$, the genus is
half the dimension of the first singular homology $H_1(X;\mathbb{Q})$. The first Betti number
of a closed orientable surface of genus $g$ is $2g$, so this agrees with the topological genus.
