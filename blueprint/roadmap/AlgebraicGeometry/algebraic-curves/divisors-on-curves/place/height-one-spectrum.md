# Maximal ideal of the valuation ring of a place

Let $K \subseteq F$ be fields, more precisely a field $F$ with a $K$-algebra structure, and let $v$ be a place of $F$ over $K$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_v \subseteq F$: a discrete valuation ring containing the image of $K$ and different from $F$. The height-one prime of $v$ is the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_v$, its unique nonzero prime ideal, viewed as a point of the height-one spectrum of the Dedekind domain $\mathcal{O}_v$.
