# Nonzero elements of nonnegative order lie in the valuation ring of a place

Let $K \subseteq F$ be fields, more precisely a field $F$ with a $K$-algebra structure, and let $v$ be a place of $F$ over $K$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_v \subseteq F$: a discrete valuation ring containing the image of $K$ and different from $F$. If $f \in F$ is nonzero and $\operatorname{ord}_v(f) \ge 0$, then $f \in \mathcal{O}_v$.
