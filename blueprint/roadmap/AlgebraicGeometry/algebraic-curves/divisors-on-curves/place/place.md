# Places of a field over a subfield

Let $K$ and $F$ be fields with $F$ a $K$-algebra. A place of $F$ over $K$ is a valuation subring $\mathcal{O}$ of $F$ that contains the image of $K$, is not all of $F$, and is a principal ideal ring. Such a ring is a discrete valuation ring, and the place is determined by $\mathcal{O}$.
