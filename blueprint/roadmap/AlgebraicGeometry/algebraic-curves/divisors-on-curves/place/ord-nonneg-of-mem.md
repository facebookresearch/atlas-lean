# Elements of the valuation ring of a place have nonnegative order

Let $K \subseteq F$ be fields, more precisely a field $F$ with a $K$-algebra structure, and let $v$ be a place of $F$ over $K$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_v \subseteq F$: a discrete valuation ring containing the image of $K$ and different from $F$. For every $f \in \mathcal{O}_v$, the order of $f$ at $v$ satisfies $\operatorname{ord}_v(f) \ge 0$; this includes $f = 0$, whose order is $0$ by convention.
