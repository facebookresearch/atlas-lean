# The valuation ring of a place consists of the elements of nonnegative order

Let $K \subseteq F$ be fields, more precisely a field $F$ with a $K$-algebra structure, and let $v$ be a place of $F$ over $K$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_v \subseteq F$: a discrete valuation ring containing the image of $K$ and different from $F$. For every nonzero $f \in F$, $f \in \mathcal{O}_v$ if and only if $\operatorname{ord}_v(f) \ge 0$. Both sides also hold for $f = 0$, since $0 \in \mathcal{O}_v$ and $\operatorname{ord}_v(0) = 0$ by convention; the linked theorem states the equivalence for nonzero $f$.
