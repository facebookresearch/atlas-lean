# The valuation ring of a place consists of the elements of nonnegative order

Let $K \subseteq F$ be fields, more precisely a field $F$ with a $K$-algebra structure, and let $v$ be a place of $F$ over $K$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_v \subseteq F$: a discrete valuation ring containing the image of $K$ and different from $F$. For every nonzero $f \in F$, $f \in \mathcal{O}_v$ if and only if $\operatorname{ord}_v(f) \ge 0$. The hypothesis $f \neq 0$ matters only for the reverse direction, since $\operatorname{ord}_v(0) = 0$ by convention.
