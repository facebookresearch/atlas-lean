# Javanpeykar's bound for Belyi curves

Javanpeykar bounds the Faltings height and three further Arakelov invariants of a curve over
$\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ of genus $g \ge 1$ polynomially in its Belyi degree. The proof bounds the
invariants by the height of a non-Weierstrass point and the Arakelov norm of the Wronskian
differential at it; it bounds the Arakelov–Green function of a Belyi cover of the modular curve
$X(2)$ with Merkl's theorem, which bounds the Wronskian differential and, with a model of the cover
whose vertical branch locus has small characteristic, the height of points above small rational
numbers; and it chooses a non-Weierstrass point among them.
