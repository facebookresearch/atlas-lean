# The zeros of $q_1'$

For positive $m$ and $n$, every zero $r$ of the derivative of $q_1 = c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$ in a field
$K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is $0$, $1$ or $\frac{m}{m + n}$.
