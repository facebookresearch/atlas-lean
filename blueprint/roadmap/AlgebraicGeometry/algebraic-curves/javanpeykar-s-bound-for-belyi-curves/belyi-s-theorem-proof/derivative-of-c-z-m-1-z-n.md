# Derivative of $c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$ for any constant $c$

For positive $m$ and $n$ and any $c \in \mathbb{Q}$,

$$\bigl( c \, z^m (1 - z)^n \bigr)' = -c \, z^{m - 1} (1 - z)^{n - 1} \bigl( (m + n) z - m \bigr).$$

## Sources

- B. Köck, *Belyi's theorem revisited*, Beitr. Algebra Geom. 45 (2004), no. 1, 253–265, <https://www.emis.de/ft/11086>, proof of Lemma 3.6, p. 263, which gives the derivative "up to a constant".
