# Critical values of a rational polynomial are roots of a rational polynomial of smaller degree

Let $K$ be a field with an algebra structure over $\mathbb{Q}$. For $f \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ with $f'
\ne 0$ there is a nonzero $g \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ with $\deg g \le \deg f'$ and $g(f(r)) = 0$ for
every $r \in K$ with $f'(r) = 0$.

This is the degree count of the proof of Köck's Lemma 3.5, where the set $S_1$ of values of $p_1$ at
the zeros of $p_1'$ has at most $n - 1$ elements. Here $g$ is the minimal polynomial of the class of
$f$ in $\mathbb{Q}[X] / (f')$.

- B. Köck, *Belyi's theorem revisited*, Beitr. Algebra Geom. 45 (2004), no. 1, 253–265, <https://www.emis.de/ft/11086>, proof of Lemma 3.5, pp. 262–263.
