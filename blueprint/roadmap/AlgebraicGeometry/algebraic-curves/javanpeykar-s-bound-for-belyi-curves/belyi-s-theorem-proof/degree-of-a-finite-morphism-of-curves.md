# A finite morphism of curves has constant degree

Let $k$ be a field and $f \colon X \to Y$ a finite morphism of curves over $k$ (proper, smooth of
relative dimension $1$ and geometrically irreducible $k$-schemes). Then $f$ is flat, and there is
a natural number $d$ such that the rank of $f$ (`Scheme.Hom.finrank`) is $d$ at every point of $Y$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the flatness of a nonconstant morphism of curves with normal target (Stacks, Lemma 53.2.3);
- the local freeness of a finite flat morphism of finite presentation, and the local constancy of
  its rank, which is then constant on the connected curve $Y$.

## Sources

- The Stacks project, Lemma 53.2.3 (Tag 0CCK), <https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0CCK>.
- R. Vakil, *The Rising Sea: Foundations of Algebraic Geometry*, draft of October 21, 2025, <https://math.stanford.edu/~vakil/216blog/FOAGoct2125public.pdf>, Proposition 16.3.5 and Definition 16.3.6, p. 469: for a finite morphism
  $\pi \colon C \to C'$ with $C'$ a regular Noetherian scheme of pure dimension $1$ and $C$ a
  Noetherian scheme of pure dimension $1$ with no embedded points, $\pi_* \mathcal{O}_C$ is
  locally free of finite rank, and "If $C'$ is irreducible, the rank of this locally free sheaf is
  the degree of $\pi$".
