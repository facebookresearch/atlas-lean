# The polynomial $c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$ vanishes at $1$

For $n > 0$ and every natural number $m$, $q_1(1) = 0$, where $q_1 = c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$ with $c = \dfrac{(m + n)^{m + n}}{m^m n^n}$.
