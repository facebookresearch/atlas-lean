# Values and derivative of the polynomial $c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$

Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers, let $c = \dfrac{(m + n)^{m + n}}{m^m n^n}$, and let

$$q_1(z) = c \, z^m (1 - z)^n \in \mathbb{Q}[z].$$

Then $q_1(0) = q_1(1) = 0$, $q_1\bigl(\frac{m}{m + n}\bigr) = 1$, and

$$q_1'(z) = -c \, z^{m - 1} (1 - z)^{n - 1} \bigl( (m + n) z - m \bigr).$$

In particular the zeros of $q_1'$ lie in $\{0, 1, \frac{m}{m + n}\}$. Köck writes that $q_1'$
equals $z^{m-1} (1 - z)^{n-1} ((m + n) z - m)$ "up to a constant"; the constant $-c$ comes from
$\frac{d}{dz} z^m (1 - z)^n = z^{m-1} (1 - z)^{n-1} \bigl( m (1 - z) - n z \bigr)$.
