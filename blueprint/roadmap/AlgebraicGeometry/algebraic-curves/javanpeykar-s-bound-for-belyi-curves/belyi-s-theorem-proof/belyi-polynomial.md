# Values and derivative of the polynomial $c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$

Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers, $c = \dfrac{(m + n)^{m + n}}{m^m n^n}$ and $q_1 = c \, z^m (1 - z)^n \in
\mathbb{Q}[z]$. Then $q_1(0) = q_1(1) = 0$, $q_1\bigl(\frac{m}{m + n}\bigr) = 1$,

$$q_1' = -c \, z^{m - 1} (1 - z)^{n - 1} \bigl( (m + n) z - m \bigr),$$

and every zero of $q_1'$ in a field $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ lies in $\bigl\{0, 1, \frac{m}{m + n}\bigr\}$.

Köck gives $q_1'$ up to a constant, which is $-c$.
