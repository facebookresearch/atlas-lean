# The derivative of $q_1 = c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$

For positive $m$ and $n$,

$$q_1' = -c \, z^{m - 1} (1 - z)^{n - 1} \bigl( (m + n) z - m \bigr)$$

with $c = \dfrac{(m + n)^{m + n}}{m^m n^n}$.

Köck says that $q_1'$ "equals $z^{m-1} (1 - z)^{n-1} ((m + n) z - m)$ up to a constant"; the
constant is $-c$.
