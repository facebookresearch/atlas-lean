# The polynomial $c \, z^m (1 - z)^n$ of Köck's Lemma 3.6

For natural numbers $m$ and $n$, the polynomial $c \, X^m (1 - X)^n \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ with $c = \dfrac{(m + n)^{m + n}}{m^m n^n}$.

In Köck's proof it is $q_1$, and $m$, $n$ are positive.

## Sources

- B. Köck, *Belyi's theorem revisited*, Beitr. Algebra Geom. 45 (2004), no. 1, 253–265, <https://www.emis.de/ft/11086>, proof of Lemma 3.6, p. 263.
