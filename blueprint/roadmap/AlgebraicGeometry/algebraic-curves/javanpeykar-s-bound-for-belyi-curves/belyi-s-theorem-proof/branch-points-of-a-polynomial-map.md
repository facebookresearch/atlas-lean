# Branch points of a polynomial map of the projective line

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $0$ and $p \in k[z]$ a nonconstant
polynomial, and let $p
\colon \mathbb{P}^1_k \to \mathbb{P}^1_k$ also denote the finite morphism that is $z \mapsto p(z)$
on $\mathbb{A}^1_k = \mathbb{P}^1_k \setminus \{\infty\}$ and maps $\infty$ to $\infty$. Then
$p^{-1}(\infty) = \{\infty\}$, and $p$ is unramified over

$$\mathbb{P}^1_k \setminus \bigl( \{\infty\} \cup \{p(r) : r \in k, \ p'(r) = 0\} \bigr).$$

## Proof sketch

On $\mathbb{A}^1_k$ the morphism is $\operatorname{Spec} k[z] \to \operatorname{Spec} k[w]$, $w
\mapsto p(z)$, whose module of relative differentials is $k[z] \, dz / (p'(z) \, dz) \cong
k[z]/(p'(z))$: it vanishes exactly away from the zeros of $p'$, and $p' \ne 0$ as $p$ is nonconstant and $k$
has characteristic $0$.
