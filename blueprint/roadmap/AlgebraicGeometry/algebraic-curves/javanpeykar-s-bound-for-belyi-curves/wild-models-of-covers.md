# Models of covers of the projective line with small vertical branch locus

This node is still to be broken down. Let $K$ be a number field and $Y$ a smooth projective
geometrically connected curve over $K$. For every finite morphism $\pi_K \colon Y \to
\mathbb{P}^1_K$ there is a number field $L \supseteq K$ such that:

- the normalization $\pi \colon \mathcal{Y} \to \mathbb{P}^1_{\mathcal{O}_L}$ of
  $\mathbb{P}^1_{\mathcal{O}_L}$ in the function field of $Y_L$ is finite, flat and surjective;
- the minimal resolution of singularities $\psi \colon \mathcal{Y}' \to \mathcal{Y}$ is
  semistable over $\mathcal{O}_L$;
- each irreducible component of the vertical part of the branch locus of $\pi$ has characteristic
  at most $\deg \pi$, the characteristic of a prime divisor $D$ of $\mathbb{P}^1_{\mathcal{O}_L}$
  being the residue characteristic of the local ring at its generic point.

## Expansion

A good expansion has pages for: Proposition 4.3.1, the semistability of the minimal resolution of
the normalization of $\mathcal{Y} \times_{\mathcal{O}_K} \mathcal{O}_L$ after a finite extension,
from Liu 2006b, Corollary 2.8 (as cited on p. 118; not read here); the flatness of a finite
morphism from a normal surface to a regular one; and the tameness argument showing that $\pi$ is
unramified over the primes of residue characteristic greater than $\deg \pi$ (p. 118).
