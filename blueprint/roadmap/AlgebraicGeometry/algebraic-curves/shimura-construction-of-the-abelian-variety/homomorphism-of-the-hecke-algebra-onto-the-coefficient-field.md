# Homomorphism of the Hecke algebra onto the field of an eigenform

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $\Gamma$ a group of level $N$, $\mathfrak{T}$ the
[Hecke algebra](hecke-algebra-of-the-modular-jacobian.md) of the Jacobian $J$ of its modular curve, and
$\mu \colon S_2(\Gamma) \to D_J$ the isomorphism of
[weight-two cusp forms with holomorphic $1$-forms](cusp-forms-as-one-forms-steps/cusp-forms-as-one-forms-on-the-modular-jacobian.md). Let
$f(z) = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n e^{2 \pi i n z}$, with $a_1 = 1$, be an element of $S_2(\Gamma)$ with $f \mid T_n = a_n f$ for
all $n \ge 1$, for the [Hecke operators $T_n$](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/hecke-operators-of-level-n.md), and $K$ the subfield of
$\mathbb{C}$ generated over $\mathbb{Q}$ by the $a_n$.

There is a homomorphism $\rho$ of $\mathbb{Q}$-algebras of $\mathfrak{T}$ onto $K$ with $\rho(\xi_n) = a_n$ for all $n$; it
satisfies $\delta t(\mu(f)) = \rho(t)\,\mu(f)$ for $t \in \mathfrak{T}$, with $\delta$ extended to $\mathfrak{T}$ as on the
Hecke algebra page. In particular $K = \mathbb{Q}[a_n : n \ge 1]$ is a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the definition of $\rho(t)$ as the eigenvalue of $\delta t$ on the nonzero vector $\mu(f)$, which is multiplicative
  because $\mathfrak{T}$ is generated by the $\xi_n$ and $\delta$ reverses products;
- the finite dimensionality of $\mathfrak{T}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, which makes $\rho(\mathfrak{T}) = \mathbb{Q}[a_n : n \ge 1]$ a domain of
  finite dimension over $\mathbb{Q}$, hence a field, hence $K$.
