# Shimura's construction of the abelian variety of a weight-two eigenform

The steps of Shimura's proof (J. Math. Soc. Japan 25 (1973), §1, pp. 526–527) of
[Shimura's abelian variety attached to a weight-two eigenform](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/shimura-abelian-variety-of-an-eigenform.md):
holomorphic $1$-forms and tangent spaces of a complex abelian variety and the kernel of a quotient, the isomorphism
$\mu$ of weight-two cusp forms with the holomorphic $1$-forms on the Jacobian $J$ of a modular curve and its
comparison of Hecke operators with Hecke endomorphisms, the Hecke algebra $\mathfrak{T} = \mathfrak{R} \oplus \mathfrak{S}$ and
the homomorphism $\rho$ attached to an eigenform, the abelian subvariety $C$ and the quotient $(A, \nu, \theta)$ of $J$
by it, its nontriviality, multiplicity one with the dimension $[K : \mathbb{Q}]$, and uniqueness.
