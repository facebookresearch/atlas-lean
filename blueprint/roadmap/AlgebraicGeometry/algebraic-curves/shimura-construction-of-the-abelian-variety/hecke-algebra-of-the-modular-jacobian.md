# Hecke algebra of the Jacobian of a modular curve

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $\Gamma$ a group of level $N$ and $J$ the Jacobian of the
[modular curve $X_\Gamma$](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curves-and-hecke-operators-of-level-n/modular-curve-of-a-group-of-level-n.md), with $\mathrm{End}(J)$ the ring of
[endomorphisms](../../abelian-varieties/homomorphisms-of-abelian-schemes.md) of $J_{\mathbb{C}}$. The **Hecke algebra** $\mathfrak{T}$ is the
$\mathbb{Q}$-subalgebra of $\mathrm{End}(J) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ generated by the
[Hecke endomorphisms](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curves-and-hecke-operators-of-level-n/hecke-endomorphisms-of-the-modular-jacobian.md) $\xi_n$ for all $n \ge 1$; it contains
$\xi_1 = 1$. Its radical, the Jacobson radical, is denoted $\mathfrak{R}$, and
$\mathfrak{T}_{\mathbb{C}} = \mathfrak{T} \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{C}$. The pullback $\delta$ of
[holomorphic $1$-forms](holomorphic-one-forms-and-tangent-space.md) extends to a $\mathbb{C}$-linear map from
$\mathfrak{T}_{\mathbb{C}}$ to the endomorphisms of $D_J$ that reverses products, through which $\mathfrak{T}_{\mathbb{C}}$ acts on
$D_J$.
