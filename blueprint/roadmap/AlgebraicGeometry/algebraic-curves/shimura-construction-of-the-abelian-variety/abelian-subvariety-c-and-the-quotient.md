# Shimura's abelian subvariety $C$ and the quotient $(A, \nu, \theta)$

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $\Gamma$ a group of level $N$, $J$ the Jacobian of its
[modular curve](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curve-of-a-group-of-level-n.md), defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathfrak{T}$ its
[Hecke algebra](hecke-algebra-of-the-modular-jacobian.md), $f = \sum a_n e^{2 \pi i n z}$ a normalized common eigenfunction
of the $T_n$ in $S_2(\Gamma)$ with field $K$, and $\rho$, $\rho' \colon K \to \mathfrak{K}_1$ and $\mathfrak{U}$ as in
[the ideal $\mathfrak{U}$](ideal-of-the-hecke-algebra-attached-to-an-eigenform.md). For a subset $X$ of
$\mathrm{End}(J) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, write $X \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$ for its elements that lie in $\mathrm{End}(J)$, the endomorphisms of
$J_{\mathbb{C}}$. Let $C$ be the abelian subvariety of $J$ generated by $\alpha(J)$ for all
$\alpha \in \mathfrak{U} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$. Then:

1. $C$ is defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, and there is a [quotient](../../abelian-varieties/quotient-by-an-abelian-subvariety.md) $(A, \nu)$ of $J$
   by $C$ over $\mathbb{Q}$;
2. $C$ is stable under every element of $\mathfrak{K}_1 \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$;
3. there is a ring homomorphism $\theta \colon K \to \mathrm{End}(A) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ with
   $\nu \circ \rho'(a) = \theta(a) \circ \nu$ in $\mathrm{Hom}(J, A) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ for all $a \in K$;
4. $\nu \circ \xi_n = \theta(a_n) \circ \nu$ for all $n \ge 1$; in particular $\theta(1) = \mathrm{id}_A$, as $\xi_1 = 1$,
   $a_1 = 1$ and $\nu$ is surjective.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- that the images $\alpha(J)$, $\alpha \in \mathfrak{U} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$, generate an abelian subvariety: $C$ is the image of
  $J^k \to J$, $(x_i) \mapsto \sum_i \alpha_i(x_i)$, for finitely many $\alpha_i$ generating the finitely generated group
  $\mathfrak{U} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$;
- the rationality over $\mathbb{Q}$ of the elements of $\mathfrak{T} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$, from that of the $\xi_n$, and the
  resulting rationality of $C$;
- the stability of $C$ under $\mathfrak{K}_1 \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$, as $\beta\alpha \in \mathfrak{U} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$ for $\beta$ in it and
  $\alpha \in \mathfrak{U} \cap \mathrm{End}(J)$;
- the endomorphism of $A$ induced by an endomorphism of $J$ that maps $C$ into itself, which defines $\theta$ on the
  elements $a$ with $\rho'(a) \in \mathrm{End}(J)$, extended $\mathbb{Q}$-linearly since every element of $\mathfrak{K}_1$ has a
  nonzero integer multiple in $\mathrm{End}(J)$;
- the vanishing of $\nu \circ u$ for $u \in \mathfrak{U}$, which with $\xi_n - \rho'(a_n) \in \mathfrak{U}$ gives item 4.
