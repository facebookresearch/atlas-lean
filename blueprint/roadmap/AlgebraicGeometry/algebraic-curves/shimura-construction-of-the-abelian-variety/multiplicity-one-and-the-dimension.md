# Multiplicity one and the dimension of Shimura's quotient

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $\Gamma$ a group of level $N$, $J$ the Jacobian of its
[modular curve](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curves-and-hecke-operators-of-level-n/modular-curve-of-a-group-of-level-n.md) over $\mathbb{Q}$, $\xi_n$ its
[Hecke endomorphisms](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curves-and-hecke-operators-of-level-n/hecke-endomorphisms-of-the-modular-jacobian.md), $\mu \colon S_2(\Gamma) \to D_J$ the isomorphism
of [weight-two cusp forms with holomorphic $1$-forms](cusp-forms-as-one-forms-on-the-modular-jacobian.md),
$f = \sum a_n e^{2 \pi i n z}$, $a_1 = 1$, an element of $S_2(\Gamma)$ with $f \mid T_n = a_n f$ for all $n \ge 1$, for the
[Hecke operators $T_n$](../../../NumberTheory/modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curves-and-hecke-operators-of-level-n/hecke-operators-of-level-n.md), $K$ the subfield of $\mathbb{C}$ generated by the
$a_n$, a [finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}$](homomorphism-of-the-hecke-algebra-onto-the-coefficient-field.md), and $I$ the set of
embeddings of $K$ into $\mathbb{C}$. Let $(A', \nu')$ be a [quotient](../../abelian-varieties/quotient-by-an-abelian-subvariety.md) of $J$ by an
abelian subvariety rational over $\mathbb{Q}$, and $\theta'$ an injective ring homomorphism of $K$ into
$\mathrm{End}(A') \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ with $\nu' \circ \xi_n = \theta'(a_n) \circ \nu'$ for all $n$. Then:

1. every element of $\theta'(K) \cap \mathrm{End}(A')$ is defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, so that $D_{A'}$ has a basis
   $\{w_{\sigma j} \mid \sigma \in I,\ 1 \le j \le m\}$ as in
   [holomorphic $1$-forms with an action of a number field](number-field-action-on-holomorphic-one-forms.md);
2. the cusp forms $f_{\sigma j} = \mu^{-1}(\delta\nu'(w_{\sigma j}))$ satisfy $f_{\sigma j} \mid T_n = a_n^\sigma f_{\sigma j}$ for
   all $n$;
3. $m = 1$, hence $\dim A' = [K : \mathbb{Q}]$;
4. for every $\sigma \in I$ there is $f_\sigma \in S_2(\Gamma)$ with $f_\sigma \mid T_n = a_n^\sigma f_\sigma$ for all $n$ and
   $f_\sigma(z) = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n^\sigma e^{2 \pi i n z}$;
5. $\delta\nu'(D_{A'}) = \mu\bigl(\sum_{\sigma \in I} \mathbb{C} f_\sigma\bigr)$.

The hypotheses on $(A', \nu', \theta')$ are conditions (i) and (ii) of Shimura's Theorem 1.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- item 1: $\theta'(1) = \mathrm{id}_{A'}$, from the case $n = 1$ and the surjectivity of $\nu'$; for $a \in K$ with $\theta'(a) \in \mathrm{End}(A')$, write $a$ as a polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$ in the $a_n$; then
  $\theta'(a) \circ \nu'$ is the corresponding polynomial in the $\xi_n$ composed with $\nu'$, which is defined over
  $\mathbb{Q}$, and the surjectivity of $\nu'$ makes $\theta'(a)$ equal to its Galois conjugates;
- item 2: $\delta\xi_n(\delta\nu'(w_{\sigma j})) = \delta\nu'(\delta\theta'(a_n)(w_{\sigma j})) = a_n^\sigma \delta\nu'(w_{\sigma j})$,
  carried to $S_2(\Gamma)$ by $\mu \circ T_n = \delta\xi_n \circ \mu$;
- item 3: the $f_{\sigma j}$, $1 \le j \le m$, are linearly independent because $\delta\nu'$ is injective, and $m \ge 1$
  because $\theta'$ is injective, so $A' \neq 0$; it remains that the common eigenfunctions of all $T_n$ in $S_2(\Gamma)$
  with given eigenvalues form a space of dimension at most one, where Shimura writes only "It follows that $m = 1$";
- for that step, the action (3.6.1) of the Hecke operators $T_n$ on the $q$-expansions of forms in $M_k(\Gamma_1(N))$
  with a Dirichlet character, and the resulting identity $a_1(T_n(f)) = a_n(f)$ for the first coefficient (Stein,
  Lemma 3.22, p. 54);
- the agreement of Shimura's $T_n$ on $S_2(\Gamma) \subset S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$ with those $T_n$, together with the
  decomposition of $S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$ by characters; with the previous item it gives that a common eigenfunction $g$
  with $g \mid T_n = b_n g$ for all $n$ is $a_1(g) \sum_n b_n e^{2 \pi i n z}$;
- items 4 and 5 from item 3, scaling $f_{\sigma 1}$ to first coefficient $1$.
