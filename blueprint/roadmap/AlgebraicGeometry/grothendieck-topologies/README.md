# Grothendieck topologies and étale cohomology

Sites attached to schemes: the Zariski, étale, pro-étale, fppf, fpqc, arc and quasi-compact
topologies, their points, sheaves and torsors, Čech complexes of covers and descent along these
covers, arc descent included, representability of sheaves by schemes, and the definition of ℓ-adic
cohomology as pro-étale cohomology with ℓ-adic coefficients, with subfields for étale cohomology,
for pro-étale and ℓ-adic cohomology, and for nearby and vanishing cycles. Grothendieck topologies
and sheaves on general sites are under category theory.
