# Grothendieck topologies on schemes

Sites attached to schemes: the Zariski, étale, pro-étale, fpqc and quasi-compact topologies, their
points and sheaves, representability of sheaves by schemes, and ℓ-adic cohomology. Grothendieck
topologies and sheaves on general sites are under category theory.
