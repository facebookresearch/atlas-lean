# The fppf topology on schemes

The fppf topology is the Grothendieck topology on the category of schemes generated by the fppf
coverings: the families of morphisms $\{f_i \colon T_i \to T\}_{i \in I}$ that are jointly
surjective, $T = \bigcup_i f_i(T_i)$, with every $f_i$ flat and locally of finite presentation.
