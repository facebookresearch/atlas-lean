# Jointly surjective étale families remain covers after base change

Let f : X → Y be a scheme morphism, V ∈ Etale(Y), and
g_i : U_i → V an étale family indexed by a type in an arbitrary universe.
If the union of the images of the underlying maps g_i is all of V,
then each base-changed map X ×_Y U_i → X ×_Y V is étale and their images
cover X ×_Y V. The index universe is independent of the scheme universe.
