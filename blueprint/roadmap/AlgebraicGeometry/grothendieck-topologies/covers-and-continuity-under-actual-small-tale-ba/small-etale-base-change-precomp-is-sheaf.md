# Sheaf preservation by small étale precomposition

For a scheme morphism f : X → Y with scheme universe u and
an abelian sheaf F on Etale(X) with values in AddCommGrpCat w,
the presheaf F ∘ G_fᵒᵖ is a sheaf on Etale(Y) for its small étale topology,
where G_f(U) = X ×_Y U. No relation between u and w is assumed.
