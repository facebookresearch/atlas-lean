# Point of the Hilbert functor

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$. A point of the Hilbert functor with Hilbert function $h$ over $A$ consists of an ideal $I$ of $A[X_0, \dots, X_n]$ such that

- $I$ is homogeneous: for every $p \in I$ and every $d$, the degree-$d$ homogeneous component of $p$ lies in $I$;
- for every $d$, the degree-$d$ piece $A[X_0, \dots, X_n]_d / (A[X_0, \dots, X_n]_d \cap I)$ is a finite projective $A$-module;
- for every $d$ and every prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $A$, the rank of the degree-$d$ piece at the stalk at $\mathfrak{p}$ equals $h(d)$.
