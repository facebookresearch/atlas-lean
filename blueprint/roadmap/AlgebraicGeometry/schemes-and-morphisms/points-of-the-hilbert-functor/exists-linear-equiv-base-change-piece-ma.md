# Base change of graded pieces

Let $A \to B$ be a map of commutative rings, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and $I$ a homogeneous ideal of $A[X_0, \dots, X_n]$ (it contains every homogeneous component of each of its elements). Let $I_B$ be the ideal of $B[X_0, \dots, X_n]$ generated by the image of $I$ under coefficient extension $\varphi : A[X_0, \dots, X_n] \to B[X_0, \dots, X_n]$. Then

- $I_B$ is homogeneous; and
- for every $d \in \mathbb{N}$ there is a $B$-linear isomorphism

$$
e : B \otimes_A \bigl(A[X]_d / (A[X]_d \cap I)\bigr) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } B[X]_d / (B[X]_d \cap I_B)
$$

with $e(1 \otimes [p]) = [\varphi(p)]$ for every homogeneous polynomial $p$ of degree $d$ over $A$.
