# Separatedness from an open cover

Let $g : N \to S$ be a morphism of schemes and $(f_i : U_i \to N)_{i \in I}$ an open cover of $N$. If for every pair of indices $i, j \in I$ the canonical morphism

$$
U_i \times_N U_j \longrightarrow U_i \times_S U_j,
$$

induced by $f_i$ and $f_j$ (with $U_i$ and $U_j$ viewed over $S$ via $g \circ f_i$ and $g \circ f_j$), is a closed immersion, then $g$ is separated.
