# Morphisms of schemes over a common base

Let $B$, $Y$ and $X$ be schemes with morphisms $g : Y \to B$ and $f : X \to B$. A morphism of schemes over $B$ from $Y$ to $X$ is a morphism from $g$ to $f$ in the category of schemes over $B$: a morphism of schemes $\varphi : Y \to X$ with $f \circ \varphi = g$.
