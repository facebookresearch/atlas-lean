# Group object determined by a relative group law

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $f : A \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism of schemes, and $G$ a relative group law on $A$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$. The group object structure on $A$, viewed as an object of the category of schemes over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ via $f$, is the one obtained from the presheaf of groups of $G$ through its representation by $A$ (Yoneda).
