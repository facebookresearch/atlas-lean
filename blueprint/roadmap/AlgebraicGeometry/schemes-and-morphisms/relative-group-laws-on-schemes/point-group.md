# Group of points of a relative group law

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $f : A \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism of schemes, and $G$ a relative group law on $A$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$. For a test scheme $t : T \to \operatorname{Spec} R$, the set $A(t)$ of morphisms from $t$ to $f$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ is a group whose multiplication, identity and inverse are those of $G$ at $t$.
