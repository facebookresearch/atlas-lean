# Presheaf of groups of a relative group law

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $f : A \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism of schemes, and $G$ a relative group law on $A$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$. The presheaf of groups associated with $G$ is the functor

$$
(\mathrm{Sch}/\operatorname{Spec} R)^{\mathrm{op}} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Grp}
$$

sending a scheme $t : T \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ over the base to the group of points $A(t)$ (morphisms from $t$ to $f$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$, with the group law of $G$ at $t$), and sending a morphism $\chi : T' \to T$ over the base to the group homomorphism $A(t) \to A(t')$, $x \mapsto x \circ \chi$.
