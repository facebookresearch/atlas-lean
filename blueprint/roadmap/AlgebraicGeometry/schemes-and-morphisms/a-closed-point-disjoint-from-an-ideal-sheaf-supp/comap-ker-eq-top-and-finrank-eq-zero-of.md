# A closed point outside the closed subscheme cut out by an ideal sheaf

Let $O$ be a local ring, $X$ a scheme, $\sigma : \operatorname{Spec} O \to X$ a closed immersion and $\mathcal{J}$ a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf on $X$ such that $\sigma$ maps the closed point $s$ of $\operatorname{Spec} O$ outside the closed subscheme cut out by $\mathcal{J}$: $\sigma(s) \notin V(\mathcal{J}) = \operatorname{Supp}(\mathcal{O}_X/\mathcal{J})$, the vanishing locus of $\mathcal{J}$, not its support as a module. Let $Z = V(\mathcal{J})$ be that closed subscheme, with closed immersion $\iota : Z \to X$, let $k$ be a field and $y : Z \to \operatorname{Spec} k$ any morphism. Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the pullback along $\iota$ of the kernel ideal sheaf of $\sigma$, and $\kappa : V(\mathcal{K}) \to Z$ the corresponding closed immersion. Then:

1. $\mathcal{K}$ is the unit ideal sheaf of $Z$;
2. $\mathcal{K}$ is invertible;
3. the composite $y \circ \kappa$ is a finite morphism;
4. the rank of $y \circ \kappa$ is $0$ at every point of $\operatorname{Spec} k$.
