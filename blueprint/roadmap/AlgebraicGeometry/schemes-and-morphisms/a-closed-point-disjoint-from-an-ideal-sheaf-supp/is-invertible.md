# Invertible ideal sheaf

Let $X$ be a scheme. A quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal{I}$ on $X$ is invertible if for every point $x \in X$ there are an affine open $U$ and a section $f \in \Gamma(X, U)$ with $x \in D(f)$, together with a non-zero-divisor $g \in \Gamma(X, D(f))$ such that $\mathcal{I}(D(f)) = (g)$, where $D(f)$ is the basic open of $f$, itself affine.
