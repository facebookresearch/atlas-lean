# Spectra of commutative Hopf algebras

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Taking spectra is a fully faithful functor from the opposite of the
category of commutative Hopf algebras over $R$ to the category of group schemes over
$\operatorname{Spec} R$, that is, of group objects in schemes over $\operatorname{Spec} R$: the
multiplication, unit and inverse of $\operatorname{Spec} A$ are induced by the comultiplication,
counit and antipode of $A$, and every homomorphism of group schemes $\operatorname{Spec} A \to
\operatorname{Spec} B$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ is induced by exactly one homomorphism of Hopf
algebras $B \to A$.
