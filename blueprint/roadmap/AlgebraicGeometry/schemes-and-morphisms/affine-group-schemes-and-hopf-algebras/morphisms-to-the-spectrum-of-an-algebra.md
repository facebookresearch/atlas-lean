# Morphisms into the spectrum of an algebra

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. For a scheme $X$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ and a commutative
$R$-algebra $B$, the morphisms $X \to \operatorname{Spec} B$ over $\operatorname{Spec} R$
correspond, naturally in $X$ and $B$, to the homomorphisms of $R$-algebras $B \to \Gamma(X,
\mathcal{O}_X)$: the functor of global sections, from schemes over $\operatorname{Spec} R$ to the
opposite of the category of commutative $R$-algebras, is left adjoint to $\operatorname{Spec}$.
