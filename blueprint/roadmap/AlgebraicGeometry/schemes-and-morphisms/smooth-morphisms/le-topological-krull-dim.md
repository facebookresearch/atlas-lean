# Nonempty smooth schemes over a field have Krull dimension at least the relative dimension

Let $K$ be a field, $n$ a natural number, and $X$ a nonempty scheme with a morphism $f : X \to \operatorname{Spec} K$ that is smooth of relative dimension $n$. Then the topological Krull dimension of $X$, valued in $\{-\infty\} \cup \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, is at least $n$:

$$
n \le \dim X .
$$
