# Non-quasi-finite points lie on a positive-dimensional irreducible closed subset

Let $f : X \to Y$ be a morphism of schemes that is locally of finite type, and let $x \in X$ be a point at which $f$ is not quasi-finite. Then there is a subset $Z \subseteq X$ such that

- $Z$ is closed and irreducible,
- $x \in Z$ and $Z \neq \{x\}$,
- $Z \subseteq f^{-1}\bigl(\overline{\{f(x)\}}\bigr)$.

The subset $Z$ lies over the closure of the image point $f(x)$, not necessarily over the set-theoretic fibre $f^{-1}(f(x))$.
