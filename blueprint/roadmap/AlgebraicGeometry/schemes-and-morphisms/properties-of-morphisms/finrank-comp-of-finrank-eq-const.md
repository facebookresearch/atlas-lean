# Fibre rank of a composite of finite flat morphisms

Let $f : X \to Y$ and $g : Y \to Z$ be finite flat morphisms of schemes, and let $m \in \mathbb{N}$. If $f$ has fibre rank $m$ at every point $y \in Y$, then for every point $z \in Z$,

$$
\operatorname{rk}_z(g \circ f) = m \cdot \operatorname{rk}_z(g),
$$

where $\operatorname{rk}$ denotes the fibre rank.
