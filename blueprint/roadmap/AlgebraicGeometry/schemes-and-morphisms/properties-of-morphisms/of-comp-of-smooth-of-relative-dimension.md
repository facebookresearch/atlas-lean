# Relative dimensions subtract along a surjective smooth morphism

Let $f : X \to Y$ and $g : Y \to Z$ be morphisms of schemes with $X$ nonempty, and let $a, b$ be natural numbers. Assume that $g \circ f$ is smooth of relative dimension $a$, that $f$ is smooth of relative dimension $b$ and surjective, and that $g$ is smooth. Then $b \le a$ and $g$ is smooth of relative dimension $a - b$.
