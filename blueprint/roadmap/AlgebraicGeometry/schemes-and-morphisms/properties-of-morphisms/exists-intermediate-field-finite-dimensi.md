# Morphisms over an algebraic extension descend to a finite subextension

Let $K / k$ be an algebraic field extension, and let $X$ and $Y$ be schemes over $\operatorname{Spec} k$, with $X$ quasi-compact and quasi-separated and $Y$ locally of finite type over $k$. For an intermediate field $k \subseteq L \subseteq K$ write $X_L = X \times_{\operatorname{Spec} k} \operatorname{Spec} L$ and $Y_L = Y \times_{\operatorname{Spec} k} \operatorname{Spec} L$.

Let $f : X_K \to Y_K$ be a morphism over $\operatorname{Spec} K$, that is, compatible with the projections to $\operatorname{Spec} K$. Then there is an intermediate field $L$ with $[L : k] < \infty$ and a morphism $g : X_L \to Y_L$ over $\operatorname{Spec} L$ such that

$$
\beta_Y \circ f = g \circ \beta_X,
$$

where $\beta_X : X_K \to X_L$ and $\beta_Y : Y_K \to Y_L$ are the maps induced by $\operatorname{Spec} K \to \operatorname{Spec} L$.
