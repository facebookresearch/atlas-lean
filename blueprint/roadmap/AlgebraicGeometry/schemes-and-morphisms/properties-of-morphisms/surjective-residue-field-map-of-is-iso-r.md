# Pullback of an isomorphism on residue fields is surjective

Let

$$
\begin{array}{ccc} P & \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{fst}\ } & X \\ {\scriptstyle \mathrm{snd}}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle f} \\ T & \xrightarrow{\ g\ } & S \end{array}
$$

be a pullback square of schemes and let $t$ be a point of $P$. If the residue field map of $f$ at the point $\mathrm{fst}(t)$, $\kappa(f(\mathrm{fst}(t))) \to \kappa(\mathrm{fst}(t))$, is an isomorphism, then the residue field map of $\mathrm{snd}$ at $t$, $\kappa(\mathrm{snd}(t)) \to \kappa(t)$, is surjective.
