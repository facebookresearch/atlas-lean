# Regularity of stalks is preserved by open immersions

Let $i : U \to X$ be an open immersion of schemes and $u \in U$. The stalk $\mathcal{O}_{X,i(u)}$ is a regular local ring if and only if the stalk $\mathcal{O}_{U,u}$ is a regular local ring. This holds because the stalk map of an open immersion is an isomorphism.
