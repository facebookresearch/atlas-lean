# Čech H0 of a two-chart cover

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M_0$, $M_1$, $M_{01}$ with restriction maps $r_0, r_1$ be sections of a module over a two-chart cover over $R$. The Čech $H^0$ is the kernel of the Čech differential $d(s_0, s_1) = r_1(s_1) - r_0(s_0)$, an $R$-submodule of $M_0 \times M_1$:

$$
H^0 = \{ (s_0, s_1) \in M_0 \times M_1 : r_0(s_0) = r_1(s_1) \}.
$$
