# Sections of a module over a two-chart cover

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $(A_0, A_1, A_{01}, \rho_0, \rho_1)$ be a two-chart cover over $R$. Sections of a module over this cover consist of

- an $A_0$-module $M_0$, an $A_1$-module $M_1$ and an $A_{01}$-module $M_{01}$, each also an $R$-module with the $R$-action compatible with the algebra action (scalar tower);
- $R$-linear restriction maps $r_0 : M_0 \to M_{01}$ and $r_1 : M_1 \to M_{01}$ that are semilinear along $\rho_0$ and $\rho_1$: $r_0(a m) = \rho_0(a)\, r_0(m)$ for $a \in A_0$, $m \in M_0$, and $r_1(a m) = \rho_1(a)\, r_1(m)$ for $a \in A_1$, $m \in M_1$.
