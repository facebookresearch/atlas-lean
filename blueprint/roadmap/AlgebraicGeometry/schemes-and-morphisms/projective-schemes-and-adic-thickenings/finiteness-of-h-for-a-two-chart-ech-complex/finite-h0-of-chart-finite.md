# Finiteness of H0 for a two-chart Čech complex

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring, let $(A_0, A_1, A_{01}, \rho_0, \rho_1)$ be a two-chart cover over $R$, and let $M_0$, $M_1$, $M_{01}$ with restriction maps $r_0 : M_0 \to M_{01}$, $r_1 : M_1 \to M_{01}$ be sections of a module over it. Let $f \in A_0$ and $g \in A_1$ with $\rho_0(f)\, \rho_1(g) = 1$ in $A_{01}$. Assume:

- $M_0$ is a finite module over the $R$-subalgebra $R[f] \subseteq A_0$ generated by $f$, and $M_1$ is a finite module over the $R$-subalgebra $R[g] \subseteq A_1$ generated by $g$;
- for every $m \in M_{01}$ there is $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\rho_0(f)^n m$ in the image of $r_0$, and there is $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\rho_1(g)^n m$ in the image of $r_1$;
- every $x \in M_0$ with $r_0(x) = 0$ satisfies $f^n x = 0$ for some $n$, and every $y \in M_1$ with $r_1(y) = 0$ satisfies $g^n y = 0$ for some $n$.

Then the Čech $H^0 = \{(s_0, s_1) \in M_0 \times M_1 : r_0(s_0) = r_1(s_1)\}$ is a finitely generated $R$-module.
