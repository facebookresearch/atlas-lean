# Morphisms into Proj agree when they agree on basic opens

Let $A$ be a commutative ring with an $\mathbb{N}$-grading $\mathcal{A} = (\mathcal{A}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ by additive subgroups, let $W$ be a scheme, and let $a, b : W \to \operatorname{Proj} \mathcal{A}$ be morphisms. Suppose that for every point $w \in W$ there are $n > 0$ and $r \in \mathcal{A}_n$ such that, with $D_+(r)$ the basic open of $r$ and $A_{(r)}$ the degree-zero part of the homogeneous localization away from $r$,

- $a^{-1}(D_+(r)) = b^{-1}(D_+(r))$;
- $w \in a^{-1}(D_+(r))$;
- for every $x \in A_{(r)}$, the sections of $\mathcal{O}_W$ over $a^{-1}(D_+(r))$ obtained by pulling back the section of $D_+(r)$ defined by $x$ along $a$ and along $b$ are equal.

Then $a = b$.
