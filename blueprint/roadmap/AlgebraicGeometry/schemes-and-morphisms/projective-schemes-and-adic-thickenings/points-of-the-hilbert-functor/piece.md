# Image of a homogeneous component in a quotient of a polynomial ring

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and $I$ an ideal of $A[X_0, \dots, X_n]$. For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the image of the degree-$d$ component $S_d$ of $S = A[X_0, \dots, X_n]$ in $S/I$ is the $A$-module

$$
A[X_0, \dots, X_n]_d \,/\, \bigl(A[X_0, \dots, X_n]_d \cap I\bigr),
$$

the homogeneous polynomials of degree $d$ modulo those lying in $I$. No homogeneity of $I$ is required by the definition; when $I$ is homogeneous, this is the degree-$d$ component $(S/I)_d$ of the graded ring $S/I$.
