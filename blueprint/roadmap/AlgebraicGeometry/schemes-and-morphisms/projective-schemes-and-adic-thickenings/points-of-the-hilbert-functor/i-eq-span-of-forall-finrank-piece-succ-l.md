# A Hilbert-function criterion for generation in degree m

Let $n, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$. Write $\operatorname{piece}_d(J)$ for the degree-$d$ homogeneous polynomials modulo those lying in an ideal $J$. Assume

1. $h(d) = \binom{n + d}{n}$ for every $d < m$;
2. for every $e \ge m$, every field $K$ and every ideal $J$ of $K[X_0, \dots, X_n]$ generated by homogeneous polynomials of degree $e$, if $\dim_K \operatorname{piece}_e(J) = h(e)$ then $\dim_K \operatorname{piece}_{e+1}(J) \le h(e+1)$.

Then for every commutative ring $A$ and every point $P$ of the Hilbert functor with Hilbert function $h$ over $A$, with ideal $I_P \subseteq A[X_0, \dots, X_n]$,

$$
I_P = \bigl(\{ p \in I_P : p \text{ homogeneous of degree } m \}\bigr).
$$

The fields $K$ in hypothesis 2 range over the same universe as $A$, and $\dim_K$ is the finite rank (zero for an infinite-dimensional space).
