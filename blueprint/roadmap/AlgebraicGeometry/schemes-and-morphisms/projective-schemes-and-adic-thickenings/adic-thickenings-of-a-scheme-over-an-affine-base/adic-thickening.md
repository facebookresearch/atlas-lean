# Adic thickening of a scheme over an affine base

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal, $X$ a scheme and $f : X \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th adic thickening of $X$ along $I$ is the fiber product

$$
X_n = X \times_{\operatorname{Spec} R} \operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1})
$$

of $f$ and the closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1}) \to \operatorname{Spec} R$.
