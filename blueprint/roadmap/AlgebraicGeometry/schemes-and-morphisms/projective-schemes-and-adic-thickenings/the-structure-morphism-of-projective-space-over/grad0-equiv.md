# Degree-zero part of the polynomial ring is the base ring

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $n$ a natural number, and grade the polynomial ring $R[X_0,\dots,X_n]$ (variables indexed by $\{0,\dots,n\}$) by total degree. The degree-zero homogeneous component $\mathcal{A}_0$ is isomorphic to $R$ as a ring: the isomorphism $R \cong \mathcal{A}_0$ sends $r$ to the constant polynomial $C(r)$, and its inverse sends a homogeneous polynomial $p$ of degree $0$ to its constant coefficient.
