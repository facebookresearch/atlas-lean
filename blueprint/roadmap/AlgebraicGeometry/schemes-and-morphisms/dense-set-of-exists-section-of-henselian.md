# Sections of a smooth scheme over a Henselian local ring are dense in the closed fibre

Let $R$ be a Henselian local ring whose residue field is algebraically closed, and let $t : T \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ be a smooth morphism of schemes. Let $T_s = \{x \in T : t(x) = \mathfrak{m}\}$ be the closed fibre, where $\mathfrak{m}$ is the closed point of $\operatorname{Spec} R$, with the subspace topology. Then the set of points $x \in T_s$ for which there is a section $s : \operatorname{Spec} R \to T$ of $t$ (that is, $t \circ s = \mathrm{id}$) with $s(\mathfrak{m}) = x$ is dense in $T_s$.

The proof pulls back to the residue field, where rational points are dense, and lifts those points to sections using smoothness and the Henselian property.
