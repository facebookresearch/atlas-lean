# Fppf descent of sections along a faithfully flat algebra

Let $E$ be a sheaf of abelian groups on the fppf site of schemes. Let $R \to A$ be a map of commutative rings making $A$ a faithfully flat, finitely presented $R$-algebra. Let $e \in E(\operatorname{Spec} A)$ be a section whose two pullbacks to $E(\operatorname{Spec}(A \otimes_R A))$, along the morphisms induced by $a \mapsto a \otimes 1$ and $a \mapsto 1 \otimes a$, are equal. Then there is a unique $e_0 \in E(\operatorname{Spec} R)$ whose pullback along $\operatorname{Spec} A \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ is $e$.
