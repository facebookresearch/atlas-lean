# Pullback of ideal sheaves preserves products

Let $f : X \to Y$ be a morphism of schemes and $\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{J}$ quasi-coherent ideal sheaves on $Y$. Write $\mathcal{I}\mathcal{O}_X$ for the pullback of $\mathcal{I}$ as an ideal sheaf on $X$: the ideal of $\mathcal{O}_X$ generated by the image of $f^{-1}\mathcal{I}$, which on affine opens is the extension of the ideal along the ring map. Then

$$
(\mathcal{I} \mathcal{J})\mathcal{O}_X = (\mathcal{I}\mathcal{O}_X) \cdot (\mathcal{J}\mathcal{O}_X).
$$
