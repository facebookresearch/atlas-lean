# Base-change morphism for sheaves of modules

Consider a commutative square of schemes

$$
\begin{array}{ccc} X' & \xrightarrow{\ g'\ } & X \\ {\scriptstyle \pi'}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle \pi} \\ T' & \xrightarrow{\ \psi\ } & T \end{array} \qquad \pi \circ g' = \psi \circ \pi'.
$$

For a morphism of schemes $h$, write $h^*$ and $h_*$ for pullback and pushforward of sheaves of modules, with the adjunction $h^* \dashv h_*$.

For a sheaf of modules $F$ on $X$, the base-change morphism is the morphism of sheaves of modules on $T'$

$$
\psi^* \pi_* F \longrightarrow \pi'_* g'^* F,
$$

the component at $F$ of the base-change natural transformation, which is the mate of the pullback two-square of the square under the pullback–pushforward adjunctions for $\pi$ and $\pi'$. No claim is made that it is an isomorphism.
