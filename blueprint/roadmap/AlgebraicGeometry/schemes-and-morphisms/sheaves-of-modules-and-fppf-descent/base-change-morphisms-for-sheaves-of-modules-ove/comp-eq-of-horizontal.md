# Horizontal composite of commutative squares commutes

Let $\pi : X \to T$, $\psi : T' \to T$, $\pi' : X' \to T'$, $g' : X' \to X$, $\psi' : T'' \to T'$, $\pi'' : X'' \to T''$ and $g'' : X'' \to X'$ be morphisms of schemes with $\pi \circ g' = \psi \circ \pi'$ and $\pi' \circ g'' = \psi' \circ \pi''$. Then the outer square commutes:

$$
\pi \circ (g' \circ g'') = (\psi \circ \psi') \circ \pi''.
$$
