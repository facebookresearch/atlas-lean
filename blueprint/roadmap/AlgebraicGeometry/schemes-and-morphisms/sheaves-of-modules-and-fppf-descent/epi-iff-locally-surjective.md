# Epimorphisms of sheaves of modules are locally surjective

Let $X$ be a scheme and $\varphi : M \to N$ a morphism of sheaves of modules on $X$. Then $\varphi$ is an epimorphism if and only if it is locally surjective on sections: for every open $U \subseteq X$, every section $s \in N(U)$ and every point $x \in U$, there is an open $V \subseteq U$ with $x \in V$ such that the restriction $s|_V$ lies in the image of $\varphi_V : M(V) \to N(V)$.
