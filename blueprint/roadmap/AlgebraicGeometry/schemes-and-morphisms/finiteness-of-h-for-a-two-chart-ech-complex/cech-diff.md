# Čech differential of a two-chart cover

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M_0$, $M_1$, $M_{01}$ with restriction maps $r_0 : M_0 \to M_{01}$ and $r_1 : M_1 \to M_{01}$ be sections of a module over a two-chart cover over $R$. The Čech differential is the $R$-linear map

$$
d : M_0 \times M_1 \to M_{01}, \qquad d(s_0, s_1) = r_1(s_1) - r_0(s_0).
$$
