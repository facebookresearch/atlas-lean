# Two-chart cover

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A two-chart cover over $R$ consists of three commutative $R$-algebras $A_0$, $A_1$ and $A_{01}$ (the coordinate algebras of the two charts and of their overlap) together with $R$-algebra homomorphisms

$$
\rho_0 : A_0 \to A_{01}, \qquad \rho_1 : A_1 \to A_{01},
$$

the restrictions from each chart to the overlap.
