# Quotient datum for a tower of schemes

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a commutative ring and $\pi \in \mathcal{O}$. Write $\mathcal{O}_n = \mathcal{O}/(\pi^{n+1})$ and $S_n = \operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}_n$, so that the quotient maps $\mathcal{O}_{n+1} \to \mathcal{O}_n$ give closed immersions $S_n \to S_{n+1}$. Fix a tower of schemes $(X_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ with structure morphisms $x^b_n : X_n \to S_n$ and transition morphisms $x^t_n : X_n \to X_{n+1}$, a group $G$, and group homomorphisms $a_n : G \to \operatorname{Aut}(X_n)$. A quotient datum for these data consists of

- a tower of schemes $(Y_n)$ with structure morphisms $y^b_n : Y_n \to S_n$ and transition morphisms $y^t_n : Y_n \to Y_{n+1}$, such that each square formed by $y^t_n$, $y^b_n$, $y^b_{n+1}$ and $S_n \to S_{n+1}$ is a pullback, and each $y^b_n$ is proper and flat;
- quotient morphisms $p_n : X_n \to Y_n$ with $y^b_n \circ p_n = x^b_n$ and $p_{n+1} \circ x^t_n = y^t_n \circ p_n$, such that the square formed by $x^t_n$, $p_n$, $p_{n+1}$ and $y^t_n$ is a pullback;
- invariance: $p_n \circ a_n(g) = p_n$ for all $n$ and $g \in G$;
- each $p_n$ is finite and surjective, and for every open $U \subseteq Y_n$ the restriction $p_n^{-1}(U) \to U$ is an epimorphism of schemes;
- a local universal property: for every scheme $T$, opens $U_n \subseteq Y_n$ with $(y^t_n)^{-1}(U_{n+1}) = U_n$, and morphisms $u_n : p_n^{-1}(U_n) \to T$ that are $G$-invariant ($u_n \circ a_n(g) = u_n$ on $p_n^{-1}(U_n)$) and compatible with the transitions ($u_{n+1} \circ x^t_n = u_n$ on $p_n^{-1}(U_n)$), there are morphisms $v_n : U_n \to T$ with $v_n \circ p_n = u_n$ on $p_n^{-1}(U_n)$ for all $n$;
- fibres over algebraically closed fields: for every $n$, every algebraically closed field $k$ and every point $y : \operatorname{Spec} k \to Y_n$, there is $x : \operatorname{Spec} k \to X_n$ with $p_n \circ x = y$, and for every such $x$, a point $x' : \operatorname{Spec} k \to X_n$ satisfies $p_n \circ x' = y$ if and only if $x' = a_n(g) \circ x$ for some $g \in G$;
- fibres over adic valuation rings: let $R$ be a valuation ring (an integral domain) that is an $\mathcal{O}$-algebra, complete and separated for the $\pi$-adic topology, with algebraically closed field of fractions, and write $R_n = R/(\pi^{n+1})$. For every family of morphisms $y_n : \operatorname{Spec} R_n \to Y_n$ lying over the morphisms $\operatorname{Spec} R_n \to S_n$ induced by $\mathcal{O} \to R$ and compatible with the transitions ($y_{n+1}$ restricted to $\operatorname{Spec} R_n$ equals $y^t_n \circ y_n$), there is a family $x_n : \operatorname{Spec} R_n \to X_n$ compatible with the transitions with $p_n \circ x_n = y_n$ for all $n$; and any two such compatible families $x$, $x'$ lifting $y$ satisfy $x'_n = a_n(g) \circ x_n$ for all $n$, for a single $g \in G$.
