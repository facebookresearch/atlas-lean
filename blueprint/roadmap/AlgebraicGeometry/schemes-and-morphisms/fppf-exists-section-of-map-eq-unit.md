# Splitting a map from an fppf sheaf onto the constant sheaf

Consider the fppf site of schemes and sheaves of abelian groups on it. Let $E$ be such a sheaf and $\underline{\mathbb{Z}}$ the constant sheaf with value $\mathbb{Z}$, and let $g : E \to \underline{\mathbb{Z}}$ be a morphism of sheaves. Suppose there is a section $e \in E(\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z})$ whose image $g(e)$ is the section $1$ of $\underline{\mathbb{Z}}$ over the terminal scheme $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$, the image of $1 \in \mathbb{Z}$ under the unit of the adjunction between the constant sheaf functor and sections over $\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}$. Then there is a morphism $s : \underline{\mathbb{Z}} \to E$ with

$$
g \circ s = \mathrm{id}_{\underline{\mathbb{Z}}}.
$$
