# Étale lifting across a nilpotent closed immersion

Let $q : Y \to X$ be an étale morphism of schemes and $k : T_0 \to T$ a closed immersion whose ideal sheaf (the kernel of $k$) is nilpotent. Let $t : T \to X$ and $s_0 : T_0 \to Y$ satisfy $q \circ s_0 = t \circ k$. Then there is a unique morphism $s : T \to Y$ with

$$
q \circ s = t \quad\text{and}\quad s \circ k = s_0.
$$
