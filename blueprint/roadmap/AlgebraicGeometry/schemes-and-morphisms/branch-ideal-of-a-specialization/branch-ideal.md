# Branch ideal of a specialization

Let $X$ be a scheme and $\xi, x \in X$ with $\xi$ specializing to $x$ ($x \in \overline{\{\xi\}}$). The branch ideal of this specialization is the ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$

$$
\{\, a \in \mathcal{O}_{X,x} : \operatorname{sp}(a) \in \mathfrak{m}_{\xi} \,\},
$$

the preimage of the maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_\xi$ of $\mathcal{O}_{X,\xi}$ under the specialization map $\operatorname{sp} : \mathcal{O}_{X,x} \to \mathcal{O}_{X,\xi}$. It is a prime ideal.
