# The branch ideal is the germ of the vanishing ideal of a closure

Let $X$ be a scheme and $\xi, x \in X$ with $\xi$ specializing to $x$. Let $U$ be an affine open subscheme containing $x$, and let $\mathcal{I}$ be the ideal sheaf of the closed subset $\overline{\{\xi\}}$ (its vanishing ideal). Then the ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$ generated by the image of $\mathcal{I}(U) \subseteq \Gamma(X, U)$ under the germ map $\Gamma(X, U) \to \mathcal{O}_{X,x}$ equals the branch ideal of the specialization.
