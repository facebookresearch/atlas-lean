# Membership in the branch ideal

Let $X$ be a scheme and $\xi, x \in X$ with $\xi$ specializing to $x$. An element $a \in \mathcal{O}_{X,x}$ lies in the branch ideal of the specialization if and only if its image under the specialization map $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} \to \mathcal{O}_{X,\xi}$ lies in the maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_{X,\xi}$.
