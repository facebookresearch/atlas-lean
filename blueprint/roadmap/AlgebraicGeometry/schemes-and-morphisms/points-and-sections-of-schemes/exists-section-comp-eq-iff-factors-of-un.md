# Valuative criterion for factoring sections through an open subscheme

Let $R$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$, let $g : X \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ be
a morphism of schemes, let $i : X_f \to X$ be an open immersion such that $g \circ i$ is
universally closed, and let $j : X' \to X$ be a morphism such that the images of $i$ and $j$
cover $X$ and the image of $g \circ j$ does not contain the closed point of
$\operatorname{Spec} R$. Let $x : \operatorname{Spec} K \to X$ be a morphism with
$g \circ x$ equal to the morphism $\operatorname{Spec} K \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ induced by
$R \to K$. Then $x$ extends to a section $s : \operatorname{Spec} R \to X$ of $g$ (that is,
$g \circ s = \mathrm{id}$ and $s$ restricts to $x$ on $\operatorname{Spec} K$) if and only if
$x$ factors through $i$.
