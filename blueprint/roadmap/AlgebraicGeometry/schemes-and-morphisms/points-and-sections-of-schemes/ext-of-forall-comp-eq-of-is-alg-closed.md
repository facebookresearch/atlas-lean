# Morphisms are determined by their values on rational points

Let $\kappa$ be an algebraically closed field, let $X$ be a reduced scheme locally of finite
type over $\kappa$ and $Y$ a separated scheme over $\kappa$, and let $f, g : X \to Y$ be
morphisms over $\kappa$. If $f \circ x = g \circ x$ for every $\kappa$-rational point
$x : \operatorname{Spec} \kappa \to X$ (every section of the structure morphism of $X$), then
$f = g$.
