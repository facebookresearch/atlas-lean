# Pullback points determined by projections given a residue isomorphism

Let $f : X \to S$ and $g : Y \to S$ be morphisms of schemes, and let $t_1, t_2$ be points of
the fibre product $X \times_S Y$ with the same images $x$ in $X$ and $y$ in $Y$. If the residue
field map $\kappa(f(x)) \to \kappa(x)$ of $f$ at $x$ is an isomorphism, then $t_1 = t_2$.
