# Surjective morphisms of finite type lift rational points

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $f : X \to Y$ a surjective morphism of schemes
that is locally of finite type. Every morphism $y : \operatorname{Spec} K \to Y$ lifts to $X$:
there is a morphism $x : \operatorname{Spec} K \to X$ with $f \circ x = y$.
