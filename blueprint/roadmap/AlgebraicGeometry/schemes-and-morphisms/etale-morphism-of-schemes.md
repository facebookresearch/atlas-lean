# Étale morphisms of schemes

A morphism of schemes $f \colon X \to Y$ is étale if for all affine open subschemes $U \subseteq Y$
and $V \subseteq f^{-1}(U)$ the ring map $\mathcal{O}_Y(U) \to \mathcal{O}_X(V)$ induced by $f$ is
étale, that is, of finite presentation and formally étale.
