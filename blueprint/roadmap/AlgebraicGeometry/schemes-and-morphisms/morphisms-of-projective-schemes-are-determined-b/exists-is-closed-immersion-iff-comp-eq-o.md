# Equalizer of two morphisms over a separated morphism is a closed subscheme

Let $q : H \to S$ be a separated morphism of schemes, $t : T \to S$ a morphism, and $u, v : T \to H$ morphisms with $q \circ u = t = q \circ v$. Then there exist a scheme $Z$ and a closed immersion $j : Z \to T$ such that for every scheme $T'$ and every morphism $\psi : T' \to T$,

$$
u \circ \psi = v \circ \psi \iff \psi = j \circ \psi' \text{ for some } \psi' : T' \to Z .
$$
