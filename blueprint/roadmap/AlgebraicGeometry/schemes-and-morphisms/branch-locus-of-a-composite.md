# Branch locus of a composite

Let $f \colon X \to Y$ and $g \colon Y \to Z$ be morphisms of schemes and $U$ an open subscheme of
$Z$. If the restriction $g^{-1}(U) \to U$ of $g$ is formally unramified and the restriction $(g
\circ f)^{-1}(U) \to g^{-1}(U)$ of $f$ is formally unramified, then the restriction $(g \circ
f)^{-1}(U) \to U$ of $g \circ f$ is formally unramified.

This is the inclusion $\subseteq$ of Köck's equality $\operatorname{Crit}(g \circ f) =
\operatorname{Crit}(g) \cup g(\operatorname{Crit}(f))$ for the critical values of a composite of
morphisms between curves, in terms of the open set over which a morphism is unramified.

## Sources

- B. Köck, *Belyi's theorem revisited*, Beitr. Algebra Geom. 45 (2004), no. 1, 253–265, <https://www.emis.de/ft/11086>, proof of Theorem 3.3, p. 262: "for any composition $g \circ f$ of morphisms
  between curves, we obviously have $\operatorname{Crit}(g \circ f) = \operatorname{Crit}(g) \cup
  g(\operatorname{Crit}(f))$".
