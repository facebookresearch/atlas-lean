# Structure morphism of projective space

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $n$ a natural number. Projective $n$-space over $R$ is $\operatorname{Proj}$ of the polynomial ring $R[X_0,\dots,X_n]$ graded by total degree. Its structure morphism

$$
\pi : \operatorname{Proj} R[X_0,\dots,X_n] \longrightarrow \operatorname{Spec} R
$$

is the composite of the canonical morphism from $\operatorname{Proj}$ to the spectrum of the degree-zero part $\mathcal{A}_0$ with $\operatorname{Spec}$ of the ring isomorphism $R \cong \mathcal{A}_0$ that sends $r$ to the constant polynomial $r$.
