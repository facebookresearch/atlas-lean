# Properness descends along surjective closed immersions

Let $i : S_0 \to S$ be a surjective closed immersion, let $f : X \to S$ be a morphism of schemes, and let $X_0 = X \times_S S_0$ be the fibre product, with projections $g : X_0 \to X$ and $f_0 : X_0 \to S_0$. If $f_0$ is proper and $f$ is
locally of finite type, then $f$ is proper.
