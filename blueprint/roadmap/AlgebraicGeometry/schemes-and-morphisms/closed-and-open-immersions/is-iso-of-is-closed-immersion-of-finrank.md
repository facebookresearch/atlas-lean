# Closed immersions preserving the rank over the base are isomorphisms

Let $g : Y \to S$ be a finite, flat morphism of schemes that is locally of finite
presentation, and let $i : X \to Y$ be a closed immersion such that $f = g \circ i$ is flat and
locally of finite presentation. For a point $s \in S$ write $\operatorname{rk}_s$ for the rank at
$s$, the dimension over the residue field $\kappa(s)$ of the functions on the fibre over $s$. If
$\operatorname{rk}_s(f) = \operatorname{rk}_s(g)$ for every $s \in S$, then $i$ is an
isomorphism.
