# The defining square of an adic thickening is a pullback

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal, $X$ a scheme and $f : X \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $X_n$ be the $n$-th adic thickening, with inclusion $X_n \to X$ and structure morphism $X_n \to \operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1})$. The square formed by these two morphisms, $f$, and the closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1}) \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ is a pullback square.
