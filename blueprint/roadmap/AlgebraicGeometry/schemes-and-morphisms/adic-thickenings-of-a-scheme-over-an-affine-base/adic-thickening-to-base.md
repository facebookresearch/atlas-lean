# Structure morphism of an adic thickening

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal, $X$ a scheme and $f : X \to \operatorname{Spec} R$ a morphism. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the structure morphism of the $n$-th adic thickening $X_n = X \times_{\operatorname{Spec} R} \operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1})$ is the second projection $X_n \to \operatorname{Spec}(R / I^{n+1})$ of the fiber product.
