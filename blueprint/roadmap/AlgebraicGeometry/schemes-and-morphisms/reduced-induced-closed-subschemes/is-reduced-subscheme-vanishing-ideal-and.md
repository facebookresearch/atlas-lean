# Reducedness and kernel bound for the vanishing-ideal subscheme

Let $X$ be a scheme and $Z$ a closed subset of $X$. The closed subscheme of $X$ cut out by
the ideal sheaf of functions vanishing on $Z$ is reduced, and this ideal sheaf is contained in the
kernel of every morphism $f : T \to X$ from a reduced scheme $T$ whose image lies in $Z$.
