# Universal property of the reduced subscheme on a closed subset

Let $X$ be a scheme, $Z$ a closed subset of $X$, and $Z_{\mathrm{red}} \to X$ the closed
subscheme cut out by the ideal sheaf of functions vanishing on $Z$. Every morphism $f : T \to X$
from a reduced scheme $T$ whose image lies in $Z$ factors uniquely through
$Z_{\mathrm{red}} \to X$.
