# Corollary 35.9 (Mayer-Vietoris)

Suppose $A$ and $B$ are closed subsets of a normal space, or compact subsets of a Hausdorff space. There is a natural long exact sequence: $\cdots \rightarrow \check{H}^{p-1}(A \cup B) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p-1}(A) \oplus \check{H}^{p}(B) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p-1}(A \cap B) \rightarrow \check{H}^{p}(A \cup B) \rightarrow \cdots$.
