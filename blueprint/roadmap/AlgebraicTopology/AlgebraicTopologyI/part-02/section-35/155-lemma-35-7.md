# Lemma 35.7

If $\varphi \colon \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{I}$ is cofinal then $\varinjlim_{\mathcal{J}} A\varphi \rightarrow \varinjlim_{\mathcal{I}} A$ is an isomorphism.
