# Lemma 35.8

Assume that $X$ is a normal space and $A, B$ closed subsets, or that $X$ is a Hausdorff space and $A, B$ compact subsets. Then the order-preserving maps $\mathcal{U}_{(A \cup B, B)} \leftarrow \mathcal{U}_{A} \times \mathcal{U}_{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{U}_{(A, A \cap B)}$ given by $(W \cup Y, Y) \hookleftarrow (W, Y) \mapsto (W, W \cap Y)$ are both cofinal.
