# Definition 31.1

A continuous map $p \colon E \rightarrow B$ is a covering space if (1) every point pre-image is a discrete subspace of $E$, and (2) every $b \in B$ has a neighborhood $V$ admitting a map $p^{-1}(V) \rightarrow p^{-1}(b)$ such that the induced map $p^{-1}(V) \rightarrow V \times p^{-1}(b)$ is a homeomorphism.
