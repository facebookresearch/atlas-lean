# Proposition 34.1

The cap product enjoys the following properties. (1) $(a \cup b) \cap x = a \cap (b \cap x)$ and $1 \cap x = x$: $H_{*}(X)$ is a module for $H^{*}(X)$. (2) Given a map $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$, $b \in H^{p}(Y)$, and $x \in H_{n}(X)$, $f_{*}(f^{*}(b) \cap x) = b \cap f_{*}(x)$. (3) Let $\epsilon \colon H_{*}(X) \rightarrow R$ be the augmentation. Then $\varepsilon(b \cap x) = \langle b, x \rangle$. (4) Cap and cup are adjoint: $\langle a \cap b, x \rangle = \langle a, b \cap x \rangle$.
