# Theorem 34.6

Let $X$ be a compact subset of some Euclidean space. If there is an open neighborhood of which it is a retract, then $\check{H}^{*}(X; R)$ is canonically isomorphic to the cohomology defined using the \v{C}ech construction, and is therefore independent of the embedding into Euclidean space.
