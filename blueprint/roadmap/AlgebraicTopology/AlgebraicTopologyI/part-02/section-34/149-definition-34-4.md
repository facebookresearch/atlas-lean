# Definition 34.4

The \v{C}ech cohomology of $K$ is $\check{H}^{p}(K) = \varinjlim_{U \in \mathcal{U}_{K}} H^{p}(U)$, where $\mathcal{U}_{K}$ is the set of open neighborhoods of $K$ in $X$, partially ordered by reverse inclusion.
