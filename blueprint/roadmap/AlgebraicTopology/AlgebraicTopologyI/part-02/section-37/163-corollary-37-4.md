# Corollary 37.4

Suppose that $M$ is an $n$-manifold, and let $K$ be a compact subset. An $R$-orientation along $K$ determines (with $p + q = n$) an isomorphism $\cap[M]_{K} \colon \check{H}^{p}(K; R) \rightarrow H_{q}(M, M - K; R)$.
