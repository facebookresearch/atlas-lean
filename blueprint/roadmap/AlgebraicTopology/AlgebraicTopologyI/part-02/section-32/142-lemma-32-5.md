# Lemma 32.5

Let $A_{1} \supseteq A_{2} \supseteq \cdots$ be a decreasing sequence of compact subsets in a Hausdorff space $X$ with intersection $A$. For any open neighborhood $U$ of $A$ there exists $i$ such that $A_{i} \subseteq U$.
