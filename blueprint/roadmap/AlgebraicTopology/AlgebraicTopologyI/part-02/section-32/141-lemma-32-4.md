# Lemma 32.4

Let $A_{1} \supseteq A_{2} \supseteq \cdots$ be a decreasing sequence of compact subsets of a space $X$, with intersection $A$. Then $\varinjlim_{i} H_{q}(X, X - A_{i}) \xrightarrow{\cong} H_{q}(X, X - A)$.
