# Proposition 30.6

Any finite dimensional nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form over $\mathbb{F}_{2}$ splits as an orthogonal direct sum of forms with matrices $[1]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{ll}0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right]$.
