# Lemma 30.5

The restriction of a nondegenerate bilinear form on $V$ to a subspace $W$ is nondegenerate exactly when $W \cap W^{\perp} = 0$. In that case $W^{\perp}$ is also nondegenerate, and the splitting $V \cong W \oplus W^{\perp}$ respects the forms.
