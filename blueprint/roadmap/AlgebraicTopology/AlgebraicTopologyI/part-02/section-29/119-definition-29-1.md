# Definition 29.1

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A graded $R$-algebra is a graded $R$-module $\ldots, A_{-1}, A_{0}, A_{1}, A_{2}, \ldots$ equipped with maps $A_{p} \otimes_{R} A_{q} \rightarrow A_{p+q}$ and a map $\eta \colon R \rightarrow A_{0}$ satisfying associativity and unit diagrams. A graded $R$-algebra $A$ is commutative if $\tau(x \otimes y) = (-1)^{pq} y \otimes x$ and the appropriate diagram commutes.
