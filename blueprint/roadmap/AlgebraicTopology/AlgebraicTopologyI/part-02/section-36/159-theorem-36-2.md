# Theorem 36.2

Let $A, B$ be closed in a normal space or compact in a Hausdorff space. The \v{C}ech cohomology and singular homology Mayer-Vietoris sequences are compatible: for any $x_{A \cup B} \in H_{n}(X, X - A \cup B)$, there is a commutative ladder (where $H_{q}(X \mid A) = H_{q}(X, X - A)$ and $p + q = n$) in which the homology classes $x_{A}, x_{B}, x_{A \cap B}$ are restrictions of the class $x_{A \cup B}$.
