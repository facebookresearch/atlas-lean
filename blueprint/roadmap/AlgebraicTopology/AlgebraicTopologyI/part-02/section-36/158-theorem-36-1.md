# Theorem 36.1

Let $L \subseteq K$ be closed subspaces of a space $X$. There is a fully relative cap product $\cap \colon \check{H}^{p}(K, L) \otimes H_{n}(X, X - K) \rightarrow H_{q}(X - L, X - K)$, $p + q = n$, such that for any $x_{K} \in H_{n}(X, X - K)$ the ladder involving the long exact sequences of the pairs in \v{C}ech cohomology and singular homology commutes, where $x_{L}$ is the restriction of $x_{K}$ to $H_{n}(X, X - L)$, and for any $x \in H_{n}(X)$ the corresponding ladder commutes, where $x_{K}$ is the restriction of $x$ to $H_{n}(X, X - K)$.
