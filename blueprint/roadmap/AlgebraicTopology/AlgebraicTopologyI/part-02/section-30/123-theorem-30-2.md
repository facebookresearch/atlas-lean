# Theorem 30.2

Let $M$ be a compact manifold of dimension $n$. There exists a unique class $[M] \in H_{n}(M)$, called the fundamental class, such that for every $p, q$ with $p + q = n$ the pairing $H^{p}(M) \otimes H^{q}(M) \xrightarrow{\cup} H^{n}(M) \xrightarrow{\langle -, [M]\rangle} \mathbb{F}_{2}$ is perfect. This means that the adjoint map $H^{p}(M) \rightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(H^{q}(M), \mathbb{F}_{2})$ is an isomorphism.
