# Lemma 13.1

Let $\sigma$ be an affine $n$-simplex, and $\tau$ a simplex in $\$\sigma$. Then $\operatorname{diam}(\tau) \leq \frac{n}{n+1} \operatorname{diam}(\sigma)$.
