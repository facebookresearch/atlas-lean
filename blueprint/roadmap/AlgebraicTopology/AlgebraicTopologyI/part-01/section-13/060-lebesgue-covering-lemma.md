# Lemma 13.3 (Lebesgue covering lemma)

Let $M$ be a compact metric space, and let $\mathcal{U}$ be an open cover. Then there is $\epsilon > 0$ such that for all $x \in M$, $B_{\epsilon}(x) \subseteq U$ for some $U \in \mathcal{U}$.
