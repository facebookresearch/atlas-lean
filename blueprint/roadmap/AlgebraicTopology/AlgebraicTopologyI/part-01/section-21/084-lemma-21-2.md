# Lemma 21.2

If $M_{i}' \rightarrow M_{i} \rightarrow M_{i}''$ is exact for all $i \in I$, then so is $\bigoplus M_{i}' \rightarrow \bigoplus M_{i} \rightarrow \bigoplus M_{i}''$.
