# Definition 27.2

$\operatorname{Ext}_{R}^{n}(M, N) = H^{n}(\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(F_{*}, N))$, where $0 \leftarrow M \leftarrow F_{0} \leftarrow F_{1} \leftarrow \cdots$ is a free resolution of $M$.
