# Proposition 9.4 (Five lemma)

In a map of exact sequences $A_4 \rightarrow A_3 \rightarrow A_2 \rightarrow A_1 \rightarrow A_0$ and $B_4 \rightarrow B_3 \rightarrow B_2 \rightarrow B_1 \rightarrow B_0$ with vertical maps $f_i \colon A_i \rightarrow B_i$: (1) If $f_0$ is injective and $f_1$ and $f_3$ are surjective, then $f_2$ is surjective. (2) If $f_4$ is surjective and $f_3$ and $f_1$ are injective, then $f_2$ is injective.
