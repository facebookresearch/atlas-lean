# Proposition 9.6

Let $(A, X) \rightarrow (B, Y)$ be a map of pairs, and assume that two of $A \rightarrow B$, $X \rightarrow Y$, and $(X, A) \rightarrow (Y, B)$ induce isomorphisms in homology. Then the third one does as well.
