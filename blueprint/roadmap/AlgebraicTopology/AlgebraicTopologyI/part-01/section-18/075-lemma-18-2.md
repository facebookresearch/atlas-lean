# Lemma 18.2

Let $0 \rightarrow A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow 0$ be a short exact sequence of finitely generated abelian groups. Then $\operatorname{rank} A - \operatorname{rank} B + \operatorname{rank} C = 0$.
