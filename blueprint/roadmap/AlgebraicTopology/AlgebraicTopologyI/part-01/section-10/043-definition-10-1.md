# Definition 10.1

A triple $(X, A, U)$ where $U \subseteq A \subseteq X$, is excisive if $\bar{U} \subseteq \operatorname{Int}(A)$. The inclusion $(X - U, A - U) \subseteq (X, A)$ is then called an excision.
