# Definition 4.3

A morphism $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$ in a category $\mathcal{C}$ is a split epimorphism ("split epi" for short) if there exists $g \colon Y \rightarrow X$ (called a section or a splitting) such that the composite $Y \xrightarrow{g} X \xrightarrow{f} Y$ is the identity.
