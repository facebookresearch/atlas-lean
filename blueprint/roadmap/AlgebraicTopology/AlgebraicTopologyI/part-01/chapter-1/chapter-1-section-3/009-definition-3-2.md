# Definition 3.2

If $X, Y \in \mathcal{C}$, then $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$ is an isomorphism if there exists $g \colon Y \rightarrow X$ with $f \circ g = 1_{Y}$ and $g \circ f = 1_{X}$. We may write $f \colon X \xrightarrow{\cong} Y$ to indicate that $f$ is an isomorphism.
