# Definition 1.6

A graded abelian group is a sequence of abelian groups, indexed by the integers. A chain complex is a graded abelian group $\{A_{n}\}$ together with homomorphisms $d \colon A_{n} \rightarrow A_{n-1}$ with the property that $d^{2} = 0$.
