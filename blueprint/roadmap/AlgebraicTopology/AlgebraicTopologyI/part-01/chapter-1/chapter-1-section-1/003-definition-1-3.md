# Definition 1.3

The abelian group $S_{n}(X)$ of singular $n$-chains in $X$ is the free abelian group generated by $n$-simplices $$S_{n}(X) = \mathbb{Z}\operatorname{Sin}_{n}(X)$$ So an $n$-chain is a finite linear combination of simplices, $\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i} \sigma_{i}$, $a_{i} \in \mathbb{Z}$, $\sigma_{i} \in \operatorname{Sin}_{n}(X)$. If $n < 0$, $\operatorname{Sin}_{n}(X)$ is declared to be empty, so $S_{n}(X) = 0$.
