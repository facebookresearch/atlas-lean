# Definition 1.2

Let $X$ be any topological space. A singular $n$-simplex in $X$ is a continuous map $\sigma \colon \Delta^{n} \rightarrow X$. Denote by $\operatorname{Sin}_{n}(X)$ the set of all $n$-simplices in $X$.
