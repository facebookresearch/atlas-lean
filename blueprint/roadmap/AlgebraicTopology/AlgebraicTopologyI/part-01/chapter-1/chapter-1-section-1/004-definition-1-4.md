# Definition 1.4

An $n$-cycle in $X$ is an $n$-chain $c$ with $dc = 0$. Notation: $$Z_{n}(X) = \operatorname{ker}\left(d \colon S_{n}(X) \rightarrow S_{n-1}(X)\right)$$
