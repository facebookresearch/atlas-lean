# Definition 1.1

For $n \geq 0$, the standard $n$-simplex $\Delta^{n}$ is the convex hull of the standard basis $\{e_{0}, \ldots, e_{n}\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$: $$\Delta^{n} = \left\{ \sum t_{i} e_{i} \mid \sum t_{i} = 1,\, t_{i} \geq 0 \right\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$$
