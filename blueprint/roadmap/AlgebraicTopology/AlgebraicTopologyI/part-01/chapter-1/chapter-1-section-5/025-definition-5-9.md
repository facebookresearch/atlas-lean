# Definition 5.9

Let $C_{*}, D_{*}$ be chain complexes, and $f_{0}, f_{1} \colon C_{*} \rightarrow D_{*}$ be chain maps. A chain homotopy $h \colon f_{0} \simeq f_{1}$ is a collection of homomorphisms $h \colon C_{n} \rightarrow D_{n+1}$ such that $dh + hd = f_{1} - f_{0}$.
