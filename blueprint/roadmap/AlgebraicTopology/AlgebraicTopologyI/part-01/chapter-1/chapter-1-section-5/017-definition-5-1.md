# Definition 5.1

Let $f_{0}, f_{1} \colon X \rightarrow Y$ be two maps. A homotopy from $f_{0}$ to $f_{1}$ is a map $h \colon X \times I \rightarrow Y$ (continuous, of course) such that $h(x, 0) = f_{0}(x)$ and $h(x, 1) = f_{1}(x)$. We say that $f_{0}$ and $f_{1}$ are homotopic, and that $h$ is a homotopy between them. This relation is denoted by $f_{0} \simeq f_{1}$.
