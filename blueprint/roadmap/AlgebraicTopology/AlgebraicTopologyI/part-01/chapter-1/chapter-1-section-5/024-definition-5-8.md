# Definition 5.8

An inclusion $A \hookrightarrow X$ is a deformation retract provided that there is a map $h \colon X \times I \rightarrow X$ such that $h(x, 0) = x$ and $h(x, 1) \in A$ for all $x \in X$ and $h(a, t) = a$ for all $a \in A$ and $t \in I$.
