# Theorem 25.15 (Künneth theorem)

Take coefficients in a PID $R$. There is a short exact sequence $0 \rightarrow \bigoplus_{p+q=n} H_{p}(X) \otimes_{R} H_{q}(Y) \rightarrow H_{n}(X \times Y) \rightarrow \bigoplus_{p+q=n-1} \operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(H_{p}(X), H_{q}(Y)) \rightarrow 0$ natural in $X, Y$. It splits as $R$-modules, but not naturally.
