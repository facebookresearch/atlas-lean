# Theorem 25.2

Let $R$ be a PID and $C_{*}, D_{*}$ be chain complexes of $R$-modules. Assume that $C_{n}$ is a free $R$-module for all $n$. There is a short exact sequence $0 \rightarrow \bigoplus_{p+q=n} H_{p}(C) \otimes H_{q}(D) \rightarrow H_{n}(C_{*} \otimes D_{*}) \rightarrow \bigoplus_{p+q=n-1} \operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(H_{p}(C), H_{q}(D)) \rightarrow 0$ natural in these data, that splits (but not naturally).
