# Corollary 25.3

Let $R$ be a PID and assume $C_{n}'$ and $C_{n}$ are $R$ free for all $n$. If $C_{*}' \rightarrow C_{*}$ and $D_{*}' \rightarrow D_{*}$ are homology isomorphisms then so is $C_{*}' \otimes D_{*}' \rightarrow C_{*} \otimes D_{*}$.
