# Definition 28.2

The cup product is the map obtained by taking $X = Y$ and composing with the map induced by the diagonal $\Delta \colon X \rightarrow X \times X$: $\cup \colon H^{p}(X) \otimes H^{q}(X) \xrightarrow{\times} H^{p+q}(X \times X) \xrightarrow{\Delta^{*}} H^{p+q}(X)$.
