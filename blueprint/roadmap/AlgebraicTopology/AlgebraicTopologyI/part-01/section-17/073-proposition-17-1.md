# Proposition 17.1

The homology of real projective space is as follows. $H_{k}(\mathbf{RP}^{n}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & k = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z} & k = n \text{ odd} \\ \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} & k \text{ odd},\, 0 < k < n \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
