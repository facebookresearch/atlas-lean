# Definition 20.3

Let $M, N$ be $R$-modules. A tensor product of $M$ and $N$ is an $R$-module $P$ and a bilinear map $\beta_{0} \colon M \times N \rightarrow P$ such that for every $R$-bilinear map $\beta \colon M \times N \rightarrow Q$ there is a unique factorization through an $R$-module homomorphism $f \colon P \rightarrow Q$ with $\beta = f \circ \beta_0$.
