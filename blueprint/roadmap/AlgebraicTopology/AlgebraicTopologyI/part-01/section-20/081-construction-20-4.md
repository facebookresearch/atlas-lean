# Construction 20.4

The universal $R$-bilinear map out of $M \times N$ is constructed as follows. Let $S$ be the sub-$R$-module of the free $R$-module $R\langle M \times N\rangle$ generated by: (1) $[(x + x', y)] - [(x,y)] - [(x',y)]$, (2) $[(x, y + y')] - [(x,y)] - [(x,y')]$, (3) $[(rx, y)] - r[(x,y)]$, (4) $[(x, ry)] - r[(x,y)]$ for $x, x' \in M$, $y, y' \in N$, $r \in R$. Then the composite $M \times N \rightarrow R\langle M \times N\rangle / S$ is the universal $R$-bilinear map, and $M \otimes_R N = R\langle M \times N\rangle / S$.
