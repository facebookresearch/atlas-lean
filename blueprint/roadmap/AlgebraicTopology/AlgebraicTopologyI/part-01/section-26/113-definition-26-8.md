# Definition 26.8

The relative $n$-cochain group with coefficients in $N$ is $S^{n}(X, A; N) = \ker(S^{n}(X; N) \rightarrow S^{n}(A; N))$. This defines a sub cochain complex of $S^{*}(X; N)$, and we define $H^{n}(X, A; N) = H^{n}(S^{*}(X, A; N))$.
