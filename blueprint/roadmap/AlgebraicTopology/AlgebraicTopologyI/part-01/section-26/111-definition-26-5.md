# Definition 26.5

The $n$th singular cohomology group of $X$ with coefficients in an abelian group $N$ is $H^{n}(X; N) = \frac{\ker(S^{n}(X; N) \rightarrow S^{n+1}(X; N))}{\operatorname{im}(S^{n-1}(X; N) \rightarrow S^{n}(X; N))}$. If $N$ is an $R$-module, then $H^{n}(X; N)$ is again an $R$-module.
