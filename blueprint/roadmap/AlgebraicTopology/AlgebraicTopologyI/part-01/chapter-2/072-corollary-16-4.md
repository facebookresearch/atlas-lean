# Corollary 16.4

Suppose that the CW complex $X$ has only even cells - that is, $X_{2k} \hookrightarrow X_{2k+1}$ is an isomorphism for all $k$. Then $H_{*}(X) \cong C_{*}(X)$. That is, $H_{n}(X) = 0$ for $n$ odd, is free abelian for all $n$, and the rank of $H_{n}(X)$ for $n$ even is the number of $n$-cells.
