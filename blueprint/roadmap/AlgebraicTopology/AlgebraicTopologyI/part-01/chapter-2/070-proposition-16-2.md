# Proposition 16.2

Let $k, q \geq 0$. Then $H_{q}(X_{k}) = 0$ for $k < q$ and $H_{q}(X_{k}) \xrightarrow{\cong} H_{q}(X)$ for $k > q$. In particular, $H_{q}(X) = 0$ if $q$ exceeds the dimension of $X$.
