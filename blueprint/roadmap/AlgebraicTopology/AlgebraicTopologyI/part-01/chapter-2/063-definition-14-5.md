# Definition 14.5

A CW-complex is a space $X$ equipped with a sequence of subspaces $\varnothing = \operatorname{Sk}_{-1} X \subseteq \operatorname{Sk}_{0} X \subseteq \operatorname{Sk}_{1} X \subseteq \cdots \subseteq X$ such that $X$ is the union of the $\operatorname{Sk}_{n} X$'s, and for all $n$, there is a pushout diagram in which $\operatorname{Sk}_{n} X$ is obtained from $\operatorname{Sk}_{n-1} X$ by attaching $n$-cells. The subspace $\operatorname{Sk}_{n} X$ is the $n$-skeleton of $X$. The maps $f_{n}$ are the attaching maps and the maps $g_{n}$ are characteristic maps.
