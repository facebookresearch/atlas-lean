# Definition 15.1

Let $X$ be a CW-complex with a cell structure $\{g_{\alpha} \colon D_{\alpha}^{n} \rightarrow X_{n} \mid \alpha \in A_{n},\, n \geq 0\}$. A subcomplex is a subspace $Y \subseteq X$ such that for all $n$, there is a subset $B_{n}$ of $A_{n}$ such that $Y_{n} = Y \cap X_{n}$ provides $Y$ with a CW-structure with characteristic maps $\{g_{\beta} \mid \beta \in B_{n},\, n \geq 0\}$.
