# Definition 23.8

Let $X \colon \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a directed system. A direct limit is an object $L$ and a map $X \rightarrow c_{L}$ that is initial among maps to constant systems. This means that given any other map to a constant system, say $X \rightarrow c_{A}$, there is a unique map $f \colon L \rightarrow A$ such that the corresponding diagram commutes.
