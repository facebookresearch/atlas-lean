# Lemma 23.11

Let $X \colon \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathbf{Ab}$ (or $\mathbf{Mod}_{R}$). A map $f \colon X \rightarrow c_{L}$ (given by $f_{i} \colon X_{i} \rightarrow L$ for $i \in \mathcal{I}$) is the direct limit if and only if: 1. For every $x \in L$, there exists an $i$ and an $x_{i} \in X_{i}$ such that $f_{i}(x_{i}) = x$. 2. Let $x_{i} \in X_{i}$ be such that $f_{i}(x_{i}) = 0$ in $L$. Then there exists some $j \geq i$ such that $f_{ij}(x_{i}) = 0$ in $X_{j}$.
