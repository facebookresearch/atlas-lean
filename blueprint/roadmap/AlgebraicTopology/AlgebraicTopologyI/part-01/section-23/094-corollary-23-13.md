# Corollary 23.13

Let $i \mapsto C(i)$ be a directed system of chain complexes. Then there is a natural isomorphism $\varinjlim_{i \in \mathcal{I}} H_{*}(C(i)) \rightarrow H_{*}(\varinjlim_{i \in \mathcal{I}} C(i))$.
