# Lemma 8.4

Let $A_{*}$ be a subcomplex of the chain complex $B_{*}$. There is a unique structure of chain complex on the quotient graded abelian group $C_{*}$ with entries $C_{n} = B_{n}/A_{n}$ such that $B_{*} \rightarrow C_{*}$ is a chain map.
