# Retained earnings roll forward by net income less distributions

If the retained-earnings account `re` is a permanent account, then after posting
the closing entry into `re`, the (credit-normal) retained-earnings balance equals
the opening retained earnings plus net income minus distributions:
`-b'(re) = -b(re) + (net income) - (distributions)`, where `b` and `b'` are the
debit-positive balances before and after closing and net income and
distributions are read from `b`.
