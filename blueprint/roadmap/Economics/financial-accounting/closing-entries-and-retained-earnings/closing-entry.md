# Closing entry into retained earnings

The closing entry into the account `re` posts `-b(a)` to each temporary
(non-permanent) account `a`, and adds to the posting of `re` the total `∑ b(t)` of
the debit-positive balances of the temporary accounts `t`. When `re` is a
permanent account, such as retained earnings, posting it zeroes every temporary
account and transfers their net balance to `re`.
