# The implied nominal rate satisfies the Fisher relation

For all real `r` and `π`, the nominal rate `i = (1 + r) * (1 + π) - 1`
satisfies `1 + i = (1 + r) * (1 + π)`. This holds by construction of `i` and is
not an economic claim; for an independently given nominal rate, the relation
holds exactly when `i = r + π + r * π`.
