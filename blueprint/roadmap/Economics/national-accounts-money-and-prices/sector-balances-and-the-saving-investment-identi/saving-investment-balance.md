# National saving equals investment plus net exports

For sector accounts satisfying the five identities `Y = C + I + G + (X - M)` (GDP by
expenditure), `NX = X - M` (net exports), `Y = C + Sp + T` (private income
disposal), `Sg = T - G` (government saving) and `S = Sp + Sg` (national saving),
national saving finances gross capital formation, private and government, plus net
exports: `S = I + NX` (equivalently `S - I = NX`). This relies on the open-economy
simplification of the accounts (zero net primary income and net current transfers
from abroad); in general the right side is gross capital formation plus the
current-account balance (the negative of SNA 2008's current external balance). The
pension-entitlement simplification (zero net pension-entitlement adjustment between
the private and government sectors) fixes only how `S` splits into `Sp` and `Sg`.
