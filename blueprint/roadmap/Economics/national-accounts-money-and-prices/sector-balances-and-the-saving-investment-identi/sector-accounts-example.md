# Worked example satisfies the GDP and saving–investment identities

The worked sector accounts (`C = 70`, `I = 20`, `G = 15`, `X = 25`, `M = 20`,
`Y = 110`, `NX = 5`, `T = 20`, `Sp = 20`, `Sg = 5`, `S = 25`) satisfy the GDP
identity in net-export form, `Y = C + I + G + NX`, which combines the assumed GDP
and net-export identities, and the derived saving–investment balance
`S = I + NX`.
