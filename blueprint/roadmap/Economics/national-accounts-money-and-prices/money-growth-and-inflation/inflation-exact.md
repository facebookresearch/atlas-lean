# Exact inflation–money-growth relation

Suppose the quantity equation holds at two dates with the same velocity `V ≠ 0`,
`M₀ * V = P₀ * Y₀` and `M₁ * V = P₁ * Y₁`, and `M₀`, `P₀`, `Y₀` and `Y₁` are
nonzero. Then the price growth factor is the money growth factor divided by the
real-output growth factor: `P₁ / P₀ = (M₁ / M₀) / (Y₁ / Y₀)`. With
`1 + π = P₁ / P₀`, `1 + μ = M₁ / M₀` and `1 + g = Y₁ / Y₀`, this is
`1 + π = (1 + μ) / (1 + g)`.
