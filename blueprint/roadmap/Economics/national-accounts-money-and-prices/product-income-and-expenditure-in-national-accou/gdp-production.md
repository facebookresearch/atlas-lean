# GDP by the production approach

GDP by the production approach is gross output minus intermediate consumption,
summed over a finite set of sectors: `∑_j (X(j) - ∑_i Z(i, j))`, where `X(j)` is
the gross output of sector `j` and `Z(i, j)` the value of sector `i`'s output
bought by sector `j` as an intermediate input, so that sector `j`'s intermediate
consumption is `∑_i Z(i, j)`.
