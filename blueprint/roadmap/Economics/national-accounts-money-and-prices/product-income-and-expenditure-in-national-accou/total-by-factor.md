# Total of transactions grouped by income recipient

For finitely many transactions `t` with amounts `a(t)` and factor-income
recipients `factor(t)` in a finite set, the total grouped by recipient is
`∑_k ∑_{t : factor(t) = k} a(t)`.

This is the same definition as [Total of transactions grouped by seller](total-by-seller.md) and
[Total of transactions grouped by buyer](total-by-buyer.md). Here the labels are read as income
recipients.
