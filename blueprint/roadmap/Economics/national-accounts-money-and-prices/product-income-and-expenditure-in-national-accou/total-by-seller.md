# Total of transactions grouped by seller

For finitely many transactions `t` with amounts `a(t)` and sellers `seller(t)` in
a finite set, the total grouped by seller is
`∑_p ∑_{t : seller(t) = p} a(t)`, the sum over sellers `p` of the amounts of
their transactions.

This is the same definition as
[Total of transactions grouped by income recipient](total-by-factor.md) and
[Total of transactions grouped by buyer](total-by-buyer.md). Here the labels are read as sellers.
