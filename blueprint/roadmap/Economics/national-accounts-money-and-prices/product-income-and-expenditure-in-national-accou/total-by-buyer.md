# Total of transactions grouped by buyer, seller or recipient

For finitely many transactions `t` with amounts `a(t)` and buyers `buyer(t)` in a finite set, the
total grouped by buyer is `∑_b ∑_{t : buyer(t) = b} a(t)`. Applied to the seller or the
factor-income recipient of each transaction, the same definition gives the totals grouped by seller
and by recipient.
