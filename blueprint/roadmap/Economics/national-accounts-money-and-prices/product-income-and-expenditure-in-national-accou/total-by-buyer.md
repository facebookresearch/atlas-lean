# Total of transactions grouped by buyer

For finitely many transactions `t` with amounts `a(t)` and buyers `buyer(t)` in a
finite set, the total grouped by buyer is `∑_b ∑_{t : buyer(t) = b} a(t)`.

This is the same definition as [Total of transactions grouped by seller](total-by-seller.md) and
[Total of transactions grouped by income recipient](total-by-factor.md). Here the labels are read as
buyers.
