# Grouping one amount by seller, recipient or buyer gives one total

For finitely many transactions with amounts `a(t)`, and any labels of sellers,
factor recipients and buyers in finite sets, grouping the amounts by seller, by
factor recipient or by buyer gives the same total: each grouping equals
`∑_t a(t)`. Because one amount serves all three groupings, this is bookkeeping,
not the national-accounts identity, which relates separate production, income
and expenditure flows.
