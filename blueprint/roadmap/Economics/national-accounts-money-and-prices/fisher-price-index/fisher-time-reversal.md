# Fisher index satisfies the time-reversal test

Suppose the four basket values `∑ p0 * q0`, `∑ p1 * q0`, `∑ p0 * q1` and
`∑ p1 * q1` (sums over the finite basket `s`) are strictly positive. Then
swapping the two periods, prices and baskets both, gives the reciprocal index:
the Fisher index with base prices `p1`, current prices `p0`, base basket `q1`
and current basket `q0`, times the forward Fisher index with base prices `p0`,
current prices `p1`, base basket `q0` and current basket `q1`, equals `1`.
Positivity of individual prices or quantities is not assumed.
