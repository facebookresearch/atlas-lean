# Fisher index as the geometric mean of Laspeyres and Paasche

The Fisher price index is the geometric mean of the Laspeyres and Paasche
indices, `F = √(L * P)`. It is defined for all prices and quantities: when
`L * P < 0` the convention `√x = 0` for `x < 0` makes it `0`; the intended domain is
strictly positive basket values, where `L` and `P` are positive.
