# Chained real components need not add up to the chained total

For two goods with period-0 prices `(1, 1)` and quantities `(100, 100)`, period-1
prices `(1, 4)` and quantities `(50, 100)`, and period-2 quantities `(50, 200)`, the
chained real values of the two components, `50` and `200`, do not add up to the
chained real value of the basket: `50 + 200 = 250`, while the total is
`200 * (3/4 * 17/9) = 850/3`. One link alone is additive; the second link, weighted
by the changed period-1 prices, breaks additivity.
