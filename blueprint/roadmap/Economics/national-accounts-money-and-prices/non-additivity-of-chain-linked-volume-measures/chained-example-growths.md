# Laspeyres volume growths in the chain-linking example

For two goods with period-0 prices `(1, 1)` and quantities `(100, 100)`, period-1
prices `(1, 4)` and quantities `(50, 100)`, and period-2 quantities `(50, 200)`, the
two one-period Laspeyres volume growths are `3/4` (period-0 prices) and `17/9`
(period-1 prices weight the second link).
