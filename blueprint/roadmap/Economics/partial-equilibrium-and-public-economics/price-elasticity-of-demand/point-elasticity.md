# Point price elasticity of demand

For a demand curve `q : ℝ → ℝ` and a price `p`, the point price elasticity is
`ε(p) = p q'(p) / q(p)`, defined for every `p` with the conventions that `q'(p) = 0` when `q` is not
differentiable at `p` and that a quotient by `0` is `0`. It is the economic (percentage) elasticity
when `p > 0`, `q(p) > 0` and `q` is differentiable at `p`.
