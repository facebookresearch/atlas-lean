# Rate at which the seller price moves with the tax

Seller-price incidence. Let `τ ↦ p(τ)` be a path of seller prices that are equilibria under a buyer
tax for `τ` near `τ₀` (`D(p(τ) + τ) = S(p(τ))`), differentiable at `τ₀`. Let demand `D` have
derivative `D'` at the buyer price `p(τ₀) + τ₀` and supply `S` have derivative `S'` at `p(τ₀)`, with
`S' ≠ D'`. Then the seller price moves at rate `D' / (S' - D')` in the tax: `p` has derivative
`D' / (S' - D')` at `τ₀`.
