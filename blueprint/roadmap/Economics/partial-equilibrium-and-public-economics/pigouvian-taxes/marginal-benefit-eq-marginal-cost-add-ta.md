# Marginal benefit equals marginal cost plus marginal damage

Let `q` be a local maximum of social welfare `B - C - Dm` (an interior social optimum), and let `B`,
`C` and `Dm` have derivatives `B'(q)`, `C'(q)` and `Dm'(q)` at `q`. Then marginal benefit equals
marginal private cost plus marginal damage, `B'(q) = C'(q) + Dm'(q)`: with the tax `τ = Dm'(q)`, `q`
satisfies the taxed market condition `B'(q) = C'(q) + τ`.
