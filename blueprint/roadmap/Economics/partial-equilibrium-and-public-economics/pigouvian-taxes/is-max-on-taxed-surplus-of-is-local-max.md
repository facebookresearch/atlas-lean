# The Pigouvian tax implements the chosen social optimum

Let `q` be a local maximum of social welfare `B - C - Dm` (an interior optimum, for example a global
social optimum on the neighbourhood `S`), let `B`, `C` and `Dm` be differentiable at `q`, and set
the per-unit tax to the marginal damage `d = Dm'(q)`. If the taxed private surplus
`B(x) - C(x) - d x` is concave on a set `S` that is a neighbourhood of `q`, then `q` maximizes the
taxed private surplus on `S`.
