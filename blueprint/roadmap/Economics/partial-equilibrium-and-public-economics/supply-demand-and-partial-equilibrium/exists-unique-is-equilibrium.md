# Unique equilibrium under sign change and falling excess demand

Let `a ≤ b`, and let demand `D` and supply `S` be continuous on `[a, b]`, with excess demand `D - S`
nonnegative at `a`, nonpositive at `b` and strictly decreasing on `[a, b]`. Then there is exactly
one equilibrium price in `[a, b]`: exactly one `p ∈ [a, b]` with `D(p) = S(p)`.
