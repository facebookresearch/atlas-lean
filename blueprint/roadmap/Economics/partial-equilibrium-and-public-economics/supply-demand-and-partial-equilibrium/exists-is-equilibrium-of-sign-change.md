# Excess demand changing sign gives an equilibrium price

Let `a ≤ b`, and let demand `D` and supply `S` be continuous on `[a, b]` with nonnegative excess
demand at `a` (`D(a) - S(a) ≥ 0`) and nonpositive excess demand at `b` (`D(b) - S(b) ≤ 0`). Then
some `p ∈ [a, b]` is an equilibrium price: `D(p) = S(p)`.
