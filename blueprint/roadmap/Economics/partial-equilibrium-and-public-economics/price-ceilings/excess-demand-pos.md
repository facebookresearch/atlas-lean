# A binding price ceiling causes a shortage

For demand and supply functions `D` and `S` of price, let the ceiling `c` bind at the equilibrium
price `p*` (`D(p*) = S(p*)` and `c` is below every equilibrium price). If `D` is weakly decreasing
and `S` weakly increasing on `[c, p*]`, then there is a shortage: `D(c) - S(c) > 0`.
