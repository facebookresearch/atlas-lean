# Binding price ceiling

For demand and supply functions `D` and `S` of price, the ceiling `c` binds at the equilibrium price
`p*` when `p*` is an equilibrium price (`D(p*) = S(p*)`) and `c` lies strictly below every
equilibrium price (`c < p` whenever `D(p) = S(p)`), so there is no equilibrium at or below `c` and
the price cannot rise to any equilibrium level.
