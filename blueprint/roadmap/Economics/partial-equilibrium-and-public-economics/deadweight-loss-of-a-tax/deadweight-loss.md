# Deadweight loss as lost gains from trade

For inverse demand `Pd` and inverse supply `Ps`, the deadweight loss of moving traded quantity from
the no-tax equilibrium `q*` to `q` is the lost gains from trade `∫_q^{q*} (Pd(x) - Ps(x)) dx` (an
oriented integral, taken as `0` when the integrand is not integrable). Sign convention: a loss is
positive (for a tax, `q < q*` and `Pd > Ps` on `(q, q*)`).
