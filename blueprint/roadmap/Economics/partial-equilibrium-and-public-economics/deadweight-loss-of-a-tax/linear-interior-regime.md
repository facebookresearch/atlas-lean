# Taxes up to α − γ keep linear trade in range, with nonnegative loss

For linear inverse demand `Pd(q) = α - β q` and inverse supply `Ps(q) = γ + δ q` with `β + δ > 0`,
let `q* = (α - γ) / (β + δ)` be the no-tax equilibrium quantity and `q_τ = (α - γ - τ) / (β + δ)`
the equilibrium quantity under a per-unit tax `τ`. For a tax `0 ≤ τ ≤ α - γ`, trade stays
nonnegative and the tax reduces it, `0 ≤ q_τ ≤ q*`, and the deadweight loss
`∫_{q_τ}^{q*} (Pd(x) - Ps(x)) dx` is nonnegative.
