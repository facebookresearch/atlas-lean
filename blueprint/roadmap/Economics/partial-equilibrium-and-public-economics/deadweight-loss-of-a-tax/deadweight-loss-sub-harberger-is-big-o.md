# Harberger approximation of deadweight loss for small taxes

Harberger small-tax approximation with a controlled remainder. Let `Pd` and `Ps` be twice
continuously differentiable at `q*` with `Ps'(q*) - Pd'(q*) > 0`, and let `τ ↦ q(τ)` be an
equilibrium path through `q*`: `q(0) = q*`, `q` continuous at `0`, and `Pd(q(τ)) - Ps(q(τ)) = τ` for
`τ` near `0`. Then the deadweight loss `∫_{q(τ)}^{q*} (Pd(x) - Ps(x)) dx` equals
`τ² / (2 (Ps'(q*) - Pd'(q*)))` up to a remainder `O(τ³)` as `τ → 0`. For nonlinear schedules the
triangle formula is not exact; only the remainder is controlled.
