# Exact triangular deadweight loss for linear schedules

For linear inverse demand `Pd(q) = α - β q` and inverse supply `Ps(q) = γ + δ q` with `β + δ > 0`,
let `q* = (α - γ) / (β + δ)` be the no-tax equilibrium quantity and `q_τ = (α - γ - τ) / (β + δ)`
the equilibrium quantity under a per-unit tax `τ`. Then the lost gains from trade
`∫_{q_τ}^{q*} (Pd(x) - Ps(x)) dx` are exactly the triangle `τ (q* - q_τ) / 2 = τ² / (2 (β + δ))`.
