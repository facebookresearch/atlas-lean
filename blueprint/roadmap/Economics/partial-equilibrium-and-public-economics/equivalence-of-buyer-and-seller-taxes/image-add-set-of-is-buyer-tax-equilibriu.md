# Shifting by the tax maps buyer-tax onto seller-tax equilibrium prices

Tax equivalence. For any demand and supply functions `D`, `S` and tax `τ`, adding the wedge `τ` maps
the set of buyer-tax equilibrium prices onto the set of seller-tax equilibrium prices:
`{p + τ | D(p + τ) = S(p)} = {p | D(p) = S(p - τ)}`.
