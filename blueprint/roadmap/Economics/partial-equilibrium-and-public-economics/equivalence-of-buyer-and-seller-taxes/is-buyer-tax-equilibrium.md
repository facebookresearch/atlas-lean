# Market equilibrium with a tax on buyers

For demand and supply functions `D` and `S` of price, `p` is an equilibrium when the per-unit tax
`τ` is levied on buyers if `D(p + τ) = S(p)`: `p` is the market price sellers receive, buyers pay
`p + τ`, and quantity demanded at the buyers' price equals quantity supplied at the sellers' price.
