# Buyer-tax equilibrium as a seller-tax equilibrium

For any demand and supply functions `D`, `S`, tax `τ` and price `p`, `p` is a buyer-tax equilibrium
(`D(p + τ) = S(p)`) exactly when `p + τ` is a seller-tax equilibrium (`D(p + τ) = S((p + τ) - τ)`):
both have the same buyer price, seller price and traded quantity.
