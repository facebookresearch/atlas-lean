# Samuelson condition on the feasible domain

For a finite set of consumers, let `G > 0` be a local maximum of total surplus `∑ᵢ vᵢ - cost` among
the feasible levels `G' ≥ 0`, and let each valuation `vᵢ` and the cost have derivatives `vᵢ'(G)` and
`cost'(G)` at `G`. Then the sum of marginal benefits equals the marginal cost:
`∑ᵢ vᵢ'(G) = cost'(G)`.
