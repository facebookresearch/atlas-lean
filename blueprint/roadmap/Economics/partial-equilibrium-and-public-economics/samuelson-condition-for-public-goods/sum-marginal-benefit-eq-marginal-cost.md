# Samuelson condition at an interior optimum

For a finite set of consumers, let `G` be a local maximum of total surplus `∑ᵢ vᵢ - cost`, and let
each valuation `vᵢ` and the cost have derivatives `vᵢ'(G)` and `cost'(G)` at `G`. Then the sum of
marginal benefits equals the marginal cost: `∑ᵢ vᵢ'(G) = cost'(G)`.
