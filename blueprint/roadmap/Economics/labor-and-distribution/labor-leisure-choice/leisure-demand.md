# Marshallian leisure demand along the wage

Marshallian (uncompensated) leisure demand along the wage, holding the time
endowment `T` and nonlabor income `N` fixed, is `w ↦ ℓ(w, w * T + N)`, where
`ℓ(w, M)` is leisure demanded at wage (price of leisure) `w` and full income `M`.
Because full income `w * T + N` itself moves with the wage, the wage enters twice.
