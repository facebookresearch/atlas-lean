# Scale invariance of the Gini coefficient

Multiplying every income by the same positive factor `t` leaves the Gini
coefficient unchanged. The identity needs no positivity of total income;
positivity of `t` is necessary, since `t = -1` flips the sign.

This is the same result as
[Scale invariance of the Gini coefficient for positive total income](gini-smul-of-sum-pos.md). Here
it is stated without the hypothesis of positive total income that the linked page assumes.
