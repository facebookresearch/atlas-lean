# Scale invariance of the Gini coefficient for positive total income

For an income distribution with positive total income, multiplying every income
by the same positive factor `t` leaves the Gini coefficient unchanged. Neither
form needs nonnegative incomes, which only matter for reading the coefficient as
the usual Gini index.

This is the same result as [Scale invariance of the Gini coefficient](gini-smul.md). Here it carries
the extra hypothesis of positive total income, which the identity does not need.
