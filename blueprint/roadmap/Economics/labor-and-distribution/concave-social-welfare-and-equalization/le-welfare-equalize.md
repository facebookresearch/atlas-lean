# A symmetric concave welfare function weakly prefers equalization

Let `ι` be a finite population and let `W` be a symmetric social welfare
function on income distributions `x : ι → ℝ` that is concave on a convex set
`s` containing every relabeling `x ∘ σ` of the distribution `x`. Then
replacing every income by the mean does not lower welfare: `W(x) ≤ W(x̄)`,
where `x̄` gives every individual the mean income `(∑ᵢ xᵢ) / |ι|`. For an
empty population the only distribution is the empty one, and the inequality
holds trivially.
