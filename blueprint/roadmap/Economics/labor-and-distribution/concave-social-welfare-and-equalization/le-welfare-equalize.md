# A symmetric concave welfare function weakly prefers equalization

Let `W` be a symmetric social welfare function that is concave on a convex set
`s` of income distributions containing every relabeling `x ∘ σ` of the
distribution `x`. Then replacing every income by the mean does not lower
welfare: `W(x) ≤ W(x̄)`, where `x̄` gives every individual the mean income.
