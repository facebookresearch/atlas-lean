# Present value of a difference of streams

The special case `a = 1`, `b = -1` of
[Present value is linear in the cash flows](../../interest-theory-and-fixed-income/present-value-of-dated-cash-flows/present-value-linear.md),
for the dates `0, …, T - 1` and the discount factors `((1 + r) ^ t)⁻¹`.

Present value of the incremental stream `wE - wN` is the difference of present values.
