# Strict preference for education

For a horizon `T`, a rate `r`, wage streams `wE` (with education) and `wN` (without) and a cost
stream `c`, education is strictly preferred when its lifetime value exceeds the no-education value:
`PV(wN) < PV(wE) - PV(c)`, with `PV(f) = ∑_{t < T} f(t) / (1 + r) ^ t`. The model takes `r > -1`; at
`r = -1` the convention `x / 0 = 0` makes every term after period `0` vanish, as for the
[present value](present-value.md).
