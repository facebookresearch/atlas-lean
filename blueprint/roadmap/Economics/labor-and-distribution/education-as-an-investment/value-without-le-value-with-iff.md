# Education is weakly preferred iff wage gains cover costs

For every horizon `T` and rate `r` (no condition on the rate is needed),
education is weakly preferred, `PV(wN) ≤ PV(wE) - PV(c)`, exactly when the
present value of the incremental wage stream is at least the present value of
the costs: `PV(c) ≤ PV(wE - wN)`, with `PV(f) = ∑_{t < T} f(t) / (1 + r) ^ t`.
