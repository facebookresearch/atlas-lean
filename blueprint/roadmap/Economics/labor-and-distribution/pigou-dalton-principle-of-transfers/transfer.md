# Income transfer between two individuals

The transfer of the amount `δ` from individual `j` to individual `i` changes the
income distribution `x` by giving `i` the income `x(i) + δ` and `j` the income
`x(j) - δ`; all other incomes are unchanged. It is meant for distinct `i ≠ j`;
for `i = j` that individual's income becomes `x(j) - δ`. With
`0 ≤ δ ≤ x(j) - x(i)` both new incomes stay in `[x(i), x(j)]`; this includes the
rank-preserving Pigou–Dalton transfers `0 ≤ 2 * δ ≤ x(j) - x(i)` and also the
transfers that swap the two incomes' ranks.
