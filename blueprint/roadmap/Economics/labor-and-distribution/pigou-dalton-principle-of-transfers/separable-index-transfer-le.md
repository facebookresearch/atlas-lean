# Transfers within the income gap reduce separable convex indices

Let `φ` be convex on a convex set `s` of incomes, and let `i ≠ j` be two
individuals whose incomes `x(i)` and `x(j)` lie in `s`. A transfer of `δ`, with
`0 ≤ δ ≤ x(j) - x(i)`, from `j` to `i` weakly reduces the separable inequality
index `(∑_k φ(x(k))) / n`. Progressive (rank-preserving Pigou–Dalton) transfers,
`0 ≤ 2 * δ ≤ x(j) - x(i)`, are the special case of the Pigou–Dalton principle;
the theorem also covers transfers that swap ranks.
