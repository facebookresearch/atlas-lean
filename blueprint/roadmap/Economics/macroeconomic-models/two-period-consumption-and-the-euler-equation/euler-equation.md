# Consumption Euler equation at an interior optimum

Let `c₀` maximize lifetime utility `u(c₀) + β u(c₁)` along the budget line
`c₁ = (1 + r) * (y₀ - c₀) + y₁` over the feasible first-period consumptions (both
consumptions nonnegative), with `c₀ > 0` and `c₁ > 0`. If `u` is differentiable
at `c₀` and `c₁`, then `u'(c₀) = β (1 + r) u'(c₁)`. No sign condition on `β` or
`1 + r` is needed.
