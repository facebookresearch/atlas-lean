# Euler equation with β(1 + r) = 1 equalizes consumption

Consumption equalization from the Euler equation: let `c₀ > 0` and `c₁ > 0`, and
let `u'` denote the derivative of `u` (taken to be `0` where `u` is not
differentiable). If `u'(c₀) = β (1 + r) u'(c₁)` with `β (1 + r) = 1`, and `u'` is
strictly decreasing on `(0, ∞)`, then `c₀ = c₁`.
