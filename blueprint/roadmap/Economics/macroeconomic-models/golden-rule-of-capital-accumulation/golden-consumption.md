# Steady-state consumption objective

The steady-state consumption objective is `c(k) = f(k) - (δ + n + g + n * g) * k`
for capital per effective worker `k`, where `f` is output per effective worker,
`δ` the depreciation rate, and `n` and `g` the growth rates of labor and
technology. It is defined for every real `k`; at a steady state with saving rate
`s` it equals consumption per effective worker `(1 - s) * f(k)`.
