# Discrete Solow steady-state equation

Capital per effective worker `k` is a steady state at saving rate `s`, with
depreciation rate `δ` and labor and technology growing by the factors `1 + n`
and `1 + g` per period, when

`(1 + n) * (1 + g) * k = s * f(k) + (1 - δ) * k`,

that is, when the law of motion `(1 + n) * (1 + g) * k' = s * f(k) + (1 - δ) * k`
gives `k' = k`.
