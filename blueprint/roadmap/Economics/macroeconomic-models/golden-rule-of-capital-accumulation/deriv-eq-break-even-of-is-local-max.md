# First-order condition at a local maximum of steady-state consumption

If `k` is a local maximum over all real numbers of the steady-state consumption
objective `c(x) = f(x) - (δ + n + g + n * g) * x` and `f` is differentiable at
`k`, then the marginal product equals the exact discrete break-even rate:

`f'(k) = δ + n + g + n * g`.

No steady-state or feasibility hypothesis is made; the golden-rule condition over
feasible steady states is a separate statement.
