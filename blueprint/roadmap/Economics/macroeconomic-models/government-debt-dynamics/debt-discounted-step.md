# Discounted debt rises by the discounted primary deficit

Let government debt `B` and primary deficits `D` satisfy the debt recursion
`B(t + 1) = (1 + r) * B(t) + D(t + 1)` for every `t`, and let `1 + r ≠ 0`. Dividing
the recursion by `(1 + r) ^ (t + 1)` gives the telescoping step

`B(t + 1) / (1 + r) ^ (t + 1) = B(t) / (1 + r) ^ t + D(t + 1) / (1 + r) ^ (t + 1)`.
