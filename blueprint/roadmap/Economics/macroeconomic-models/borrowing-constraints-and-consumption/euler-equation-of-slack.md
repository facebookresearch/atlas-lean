# Euler equation when the borrowing limit is slack

Let `c₀` maximize lifetime utility `u(c₀) + β u(c₁)`, with
`c₁ = (1 + r) * (y₀ - c₀) + y₁`, over the consumptions feasible under the borrowing
limit `b` (`c₀ ≥ 0`, `c₁ ≥ 0`, `c₀ ≤ y₀ + b`), with `c₀ > 0` and `c₁ > 0`, and let
`u` be differentiable at `c₀` and `c₁`. If the borrowing limit is slack,
`c₀ < y₀ + b`, the unconstrained Euler equation `u'(c₀) = β (1 + r) u'(c₁)` holds.
