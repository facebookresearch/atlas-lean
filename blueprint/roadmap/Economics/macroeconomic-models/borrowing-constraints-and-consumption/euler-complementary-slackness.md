# Euler condition with a multiplier on the borrowing limit

Let `c₀` maximize lifetime utility `u(c₀) + β u(c₁)`, with
`c₁ = (1 + r) * (y₀ - c₀) + y₁`, over the consumptions feasible under the borrowing
limit `b` (`c₀ ≥ 0`, `c₁ ≥ 0`, `c₀ ≤ y₀ + b`), with `c₀ > 0` and `c₁ > 0`, and let
`u` be differentiable at `c₀` and `c₁`. Then there is a multiplier `μ ≥ 0` on the
borrowing limit with `μ * (c₀ - (y₀ + b)) = 0` and

`u'(c₀) = β (1 + r) u'(c₁) + μ`.
