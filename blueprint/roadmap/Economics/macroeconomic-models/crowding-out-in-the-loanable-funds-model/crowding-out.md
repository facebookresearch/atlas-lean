# A higher deficit lowers equilibrium investment

With saving `S(r) = s0 + s1 * r`, investment `I(r) = i0 - i1 * r` and the
equilibrium rate `r*(D) = (i0 + D - s0) / (s1 + i1)` at government deficit `D`:
for `s1 > 0` and `i1 > 0`, a higher government deficit lowers equilibrium
investment: `D₁ < D₂` implies `I(r*(D₂)) < I(r*(D₁))` (difference form of
`dI*/dD = −i1/(s1 + i1) < 0`). This is the crowding-out comparative static.
