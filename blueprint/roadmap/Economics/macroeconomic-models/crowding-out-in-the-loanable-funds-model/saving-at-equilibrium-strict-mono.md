# A higher deficit raises equilibrium saving

With saving `S(r) = s0 + s1 * r`, investment `I(r) = i0 - i1 * r` and the
equilibrium rate `r*(D) = (i0 + D - s0) / (s1 + i1)` at government deficit `D`:
for `s1 > 0` and `i1 > 0`, `D₁ < D₂` implies `S(r*(D₁)) < S(r*(D₂))`. Saving rises
to fund the deficit while investment is crowded out.
