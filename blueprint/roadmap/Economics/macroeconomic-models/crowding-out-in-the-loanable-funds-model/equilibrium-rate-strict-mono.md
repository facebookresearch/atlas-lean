# A higher deficit raises the equilibrium rate

With saving `S(r) = s0 + s1 * r`, investment `I(r) = i0 - i1 * r` and the
equilibrium rate `r*(D) = (i0 + D - s0) / (s1 + i1)` at government deficit `D`:
for `s1 > 0` and `i1 > 0`, `D₁ < D₂` implies `r*(D₁) < r*(D₂)` (difference form
of `dr*/dD = 1/(s1 + i1) > 0`).
