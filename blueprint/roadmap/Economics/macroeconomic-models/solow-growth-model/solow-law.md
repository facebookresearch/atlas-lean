# Capital per effective worker follows the discrete Solow law

Let effective labor grow as `E' = (1 + n) * (1 + g) * E` with `E ≠ 0` and
`(1 + n) * (1 + g) ≠ 0`, let output be `F = f(k) * E`, and let aggregate capital
follow `K' = s * F + (1 - δ) * K`. Then capital per effective worker, `k = K / E`
and `k' = K' / E'`, satisfies

`k' = (s * f(k) + (1 - δ) * k) / ((1 + n) * (1 + g))`.
