# Inflation is constant at the natural rate of unemployment

Suppose expectations are adaptive, `πe(t + 1) = π(t)`, and inflation follows the
expectations-augmented Phillips curve `π(t + 1) = πe(t + 1) - κ * (u(t + 1) - uₙ)`,
for every `t`.
If unemployment stays at the natural rate, `u(t) = uₙ` for every `t`, then
inflation is constant: `π(t + 1) = π(t)` for every `t`.
