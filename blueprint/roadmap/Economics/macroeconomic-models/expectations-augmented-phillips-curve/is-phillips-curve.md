# Phillips curve linking inflation to the unemployment gap

Inflation `π`, expected inflation `πe` and unemployment `u` satisfy the
expectations-augmented Phillips curve with slope `κ` and natural rate of
unemployment `uₙ` when, for every `t`, next period's inflation equals expected
inflation minus `κ` times the unemployment gap:
`π(t + 1) = πe(t + 1) - κ * (u(t + 1) - uₙ)`.
