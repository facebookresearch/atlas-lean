# Accelerationist form of the Phillips curve

Suppose expectations are adaptive, `πe(t + 1) = π(t)`, and inflation follows the
expectations-augmented Phillips curve `π(t + 1) = πe(t + 1) - κ * (u(t + 1) - uₙ)`,
for every `t`.
If the unemployment gap is constant, `u(t + 1) - uₙ = g` for every `t`, then
inflation falls (or rises) linearly: `π(t) = π(0) - t * (κ * g)` for every `t`.
Inflation then follows a unit-root affine recurrence with intercept `-(κ * g)`.
