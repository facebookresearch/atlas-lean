# Capital share = capital elasticity under competitive pricing

Let `Y = A * F(K, L)` with `A ≠ 0` and `F(K, L) ≠ 0`, and let `F` be
differentiable at `(K, L)` with derivative `F'`. When capital is paid its
marginal product `A * F'(1, 0)`, the capital income share equals the output
elasticity of `F` with respect to capital:

`A * F'(1, 0) * K / Y = K * F'(1, 0) / F(K, L) = ε_K`.

The price `A * F'(1, 0)` enters the statement as this expression; nothing is
concluded about markets.
