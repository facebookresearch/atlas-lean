# Labour share = labour elasticity under competitive pricing

Let `Y = A * F(K, L)` with `A ≠ 0` and `F(K, L) ≠ 0`, and let `F` be
differentiable at `(K, L)` with derivative `F'`. When labour is paid its
marginal product, the wage `A * F'(0, 1)`, the labour income share equals the
output elasticity of `F` with respect to labour:

`A * F'(0, 1) * L / Y = L * F'(0, 1) / F(K, L) = ε_L`.

The wage `A * F'(0, 1)` enters the statement as this expression; nothing is
concluded about markets.
