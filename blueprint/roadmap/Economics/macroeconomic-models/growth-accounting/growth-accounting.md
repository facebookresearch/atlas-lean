# Output growth decomposes into TFP and factor growth

Let output be `Y(s) = A(s) * F(K(s), L(s))` for all `s`, with total factor
productivity `A`, capital `K` and labour `L`. Suppose `A`, `K` and `L` are
differentiable at `t`, `F` is differentiable at `(K(t), L(t))`, and `A(t)`,
`K(t)`, `L(t)` and `F(K(t), L(t))` are all nonzero. Then

`Y'(t) / Y(t) = A'(t) / A(t) + ε_K * (K'(t) / K(t)) + ε_L * (L'(t) / L(t))`,

where `ε_K = K * F_K / F` and `ε_L = L * F_L / F` are the output elasticities of
`F` with respect to capital and labour at `(K(t), L(t))`. The growth rates
`f'(t) / f(t)` are logarithmic derivatives.
