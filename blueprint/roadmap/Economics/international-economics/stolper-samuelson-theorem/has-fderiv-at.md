# Shephard's lemma for unit cost functions

If the unit bundle `(aL, aK)` is [cost-minimizing](is-cost-minimizing-at.md) for `c` at `x₀` (its
cost bounds `c` from above near `x₀` and equals it at `x₀`) and `c` is differentiable at `x₀`, then
the derivative of `c` at `x₀` is the cost-minimizing bundle: `c` has derivative
`(w, r) ↦ aL * w + aK * r` at `x₀`, that is `∂c/∂w = aL` and `∂c/∂r = aK` (envelope argument).
