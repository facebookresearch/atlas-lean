# Potential mutual gains from Ricardian trade

For two [countries](country.md), Home and Foreign, each with positive unit labor requirements
`a₁, a₂` and a positive labor endowment `L`: if the world price `p` satisfies `pH < p < pF`, where
`pH` and `pF` are Home's and Foreign's autarky prices, Home (specializing in good 1) and Foreign
(specializing in good 2) both strictly enlarge their consumption sets. This is an opportunity-set
statement, not a utility comparison.
