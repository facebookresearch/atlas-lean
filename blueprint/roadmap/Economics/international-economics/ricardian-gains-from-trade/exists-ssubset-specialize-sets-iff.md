# Mutual gains from specialization iff comparative advantage

For two [countries](country.md), Home and Foreign, each with positive unit labor requirements
`a₁, a₂` and a positive labor endowment `L`, a world price at which both countries' consumption sets
strictly enlarge (Home specializing in good 1, Foreign in good 2) exists if and only if Home has a
comparative advantage in good 1: Home's autarky price `a₁ / a₂` is below Foreign's.
