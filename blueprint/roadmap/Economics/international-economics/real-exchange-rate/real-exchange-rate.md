# Real exchange rate from nominal rate and price levels

The real exchange rate is `q = E * P* / P`, with the convention `x / 0 = 0`,
under the quote convention: `E` is domestic currency per unit of foreign
currency, `P*` the foreign price index (foreign currency per foreign basket) and
`P` the domestic price index (domestic currency per domestic basket). So `q` is
domestic baskets per foreign basket.
