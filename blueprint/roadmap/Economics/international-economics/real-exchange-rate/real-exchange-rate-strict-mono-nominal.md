# A higher nominal rate raises the real rate at fixed prices

For `P* > 0` and `P > 0`, the real exchange rate `q(E) = E * P* / P` is strictly
increasing in the nominal rate: `E₁ < E₂` implies `q(E₁) < q(E₂)`. A higher `E`
is more domestic currency per unit of foreign currency, that is, a depreciation
of the domestic currency.
