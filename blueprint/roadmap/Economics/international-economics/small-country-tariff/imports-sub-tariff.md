# Fall in imports under a non-prohibitive tariff

With linear demand `D(P) = a - b * P` and supply `S(P) = c + d * P` (`b, d > 0`), let
`t ≥ 0` and let the tariff be non-prohibitive, `S(Pw + t) ≤ D(Pw + t)`. Then
imports fall by `(b + d) * t`:
`max(D(Pw) - S(Pw), 0) - max(D(Pw + t) - S(Pw + t), 0) = (b + d) * t`.
