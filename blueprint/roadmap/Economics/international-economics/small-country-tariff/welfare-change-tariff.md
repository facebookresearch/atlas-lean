# A non-prohibitive tariff lowers total surplus by two triangles

With linear demand `D(P) = a - b * P` and supply `S(P) = c + d * P` (`b, d > 0`), let
`t ≥ 0` and let the tariff be non-prohibitive, `S(Pw + t) ≤ D(Pw + t)`. Then the
change in total surplus (consumer surplus plus producer surplus plus tariff
revenue) when the domestic price rises from `Pw` to `Pw + t` is minus the sum of
the production and consumption deadweight triangles:
`-(d * t ^ 2 / 2 + b * t ^ 2 / 2)`.
