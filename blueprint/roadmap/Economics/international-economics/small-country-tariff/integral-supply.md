# Integral of linear supply over a price interval

In a [linear market](linear-market.md) (demand slope `-b` with `b > 0`, supply slope `d > 0`) and
for all real `u` and `v`, the integral of the supply `S(P) = c + d * P` over the price interval from
`u` to `v` is `c * (v - u) + d * (v ^ 2 - u ^ 2) / 2`.
