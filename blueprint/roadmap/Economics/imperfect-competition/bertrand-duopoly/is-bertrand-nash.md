# Nash equilibrium of the Bertrand pricing game

A price profile `p` is a (pure-strategy) Nash equilibrium of the Bertrand pricing game, with two
firms of common marginal cost `c` and market demand `D`, when no firm can raise its profit by a
unilateral price change: for each firm `i` and every price `t`, firm `i`'s profit when it charges
`t` and its rival keeps its price is at most `πᵢ(p)`. Here `πᵢ(p) = (pᵢ - c) qᵢ(p)` with
`qᵢ(p) = D(pᵢ)` if `pᵢ < pⱼ`, `D(pᵢ) / 2` if `pᵢ = pⱼ` and `0` if `pᵢ > pⱼ` (`j` the rival); this is
the no-profitable-deviation form of Nash equilibrium for the game with two players, strategy set `ℝ`
and these payoffs.
