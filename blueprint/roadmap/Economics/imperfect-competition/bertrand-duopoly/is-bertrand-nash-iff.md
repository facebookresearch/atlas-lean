# Bertrand paradox: equilibrium prices equal marginal cost

Bertrand paradox. In the Bertrand duopoly with common marginal cost `c`, the tie-splitting rule and
no capacity limits, suppose demand is positive (`D(x) > 0` for every price `x`) and nonincreasing.
Then a price profile is a Nash equilibrium exactly when both firms price at marginal cost:
`p₁ = p₂ = c`.
