# Quantity sold by a Bertrand duopolist

In the homogeneous-product Bertrand duopoly with market demand `D` and price profile `p = (p₁, p₂)`,
firm `i`, whose rival is `j`, sells the whole market demand at a strictly lower price, half of it at
a tie and nothing at a strictly higher price: `qᵢ(p) = D(pᵢ)` if `pᵢ < pⱼ`, `D(pᵢ) / 2` if
`pᵢ = pⱼ`, and `0` if `pᵢ > pⱼ`. Firms have no capacity limits.
