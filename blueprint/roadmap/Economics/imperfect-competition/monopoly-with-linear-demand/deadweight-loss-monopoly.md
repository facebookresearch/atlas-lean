# Monopoly deadweight loss

In the linear monopoly model (inverse demand `P(q) = a - b q` with `b > 0`, constant marginal cost
`c < a`), the lost gains from trade between the monopoly quantity `q_m = (a - c) / (2 b)` and the
efficient quantity `q_c = (a - c) / b` are the triangle
`∫_{q_m}^{q_c} (P(x) - c) dx = (a - c)² / (8 b)`.
