# Every profit maximizer is the monopoly quantity

In the linear monopoly model (inverse demand `P(q) = a - b q` with `b > 0`, constant marginal cost
`c < a`), if `q` maximizes the profit `π(q) = (a - b q) q - c q` over all quantities (`π(q') ≤ π(q)`
for every `q'`), then `q` is the monopoly quantity `q_m = (a - c) / (2 b)`.
