# Monopoly markup equals the inverse elasticity of demand

Lerner rule. If output `q > 0` locally maximizes the monopoly profit `x P(x) - C(x)`, the inverse
demand `P` and cost `C` have derivatives `P'(q) < 0` and `C'(q)` at `q`, and the price is positive,
`P(q) > 0`, then the markup over marginal cost satisfies `(P(q) - C'(q)) / P(q) = 1 / |ε|`, where
`ε = P(q) / (q P'(q))` is the price elasticity of demand.
