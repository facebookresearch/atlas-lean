# Price elasticity from the inverse-demand slope

The price elasticity of demand at output `q` in reciprocal-slope form, from an inverse demand `P`
and a number `P'` (a slope): `ε = P(q) / (q P')`, read as `0` when `q P' = 0`. The definition
imposes no relation between `P'` and `P`; the Lerner rule takes `P'` to be the derivative of `P` at
`q`. It equals the usual `(dq/dp) (p/q)` only when demand has a local inverse `p ↦ q(p)` near `q`,
differentiable with derivative `1 / P'` (for example, by the inverse function theorem when `P` is
continuously differentiable near `q` with `P' ≠ 0`); the definition assumes no such inverse.
