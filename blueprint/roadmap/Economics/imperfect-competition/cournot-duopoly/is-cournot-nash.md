# Cournot–Nash equilibrium of the duopoly

For the duopoly with inverse demand `a - b Q` and marginal costs `c₁, c₂`, outputs `(q₁, q₂)` form a
Cournot–Nash equilibrium when both are nonnegative and no firm gains by switching to another
nonnegative output with the rival's output held fixed:
`q (a - b (q + q₂)) - c₁ q ≤ q₁ (a - b (q₁ + q₂)) - c₁ q₁` for every `q ≥ 0`, and symmetrically for
firm 2.
