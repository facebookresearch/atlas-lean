# Cournot equilibrium outputs under linear demand

For inverse demand `a - b Q` with `b > 0` and marginal costs `c₁, c₂` such that both candidate
outputs are nonnegative (`a - 2 c₁ + c₂ ≥ 0` and `a - 2 c₂ + c₁ ≥ 0`), the outputs
`q₁ = (a - 2 c₁ + c₂) / (3 b)` and `q₂ = (a - 2 c₂ + c₁) / (3 b)` form a Cournot–Nash equilibrium.
