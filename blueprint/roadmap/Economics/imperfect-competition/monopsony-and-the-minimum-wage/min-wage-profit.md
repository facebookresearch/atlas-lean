# Monopsony profit under a minimum wage

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), the profit under a minimum wage `w` from employing `L`
workers, each paid `max(w, α + β L)`, is `π_w(L) = γ L - δ L² / 2 - max(w, α + β L) L`.
