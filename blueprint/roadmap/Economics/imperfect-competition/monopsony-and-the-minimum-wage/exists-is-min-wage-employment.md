# Optimal employment exists under a binding minimum wage

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), for every minimum wage `w` above the monopsony wage
`w_m = α + β L_m` (with `L_m = (γ - α) / (δ + 2 β)`), some `L ≥ 0` maximizes the minimum-wage profit
`π_w(L) = γ L - δ L² / 2 - max(w, α + β L) L` over all `L' ≥ 0`.
