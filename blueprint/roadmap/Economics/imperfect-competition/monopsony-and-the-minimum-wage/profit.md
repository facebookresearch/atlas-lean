# Unregulated monopsony profit

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), the unregulated monopsony profit from employing `L` workers,
each paid the supply wage `α + β L`, is `π(L) = γ L - δ L² / 2 - (α + β L) L`.
