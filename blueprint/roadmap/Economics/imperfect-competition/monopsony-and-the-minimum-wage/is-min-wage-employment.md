# Profit-maximizing employment under a minimum wage

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), an employment level `L` is profit-maximizing under the minimum
wage `w` when `L ≥ 0` and `π_w(L') ≤ π_w(L)` for every `L' ≥ 0`, where
`π_w(L) = γ L - δ L² / 2 - max(w, α + β L) L`.
