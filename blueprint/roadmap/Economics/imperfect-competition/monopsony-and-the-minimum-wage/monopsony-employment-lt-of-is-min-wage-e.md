# Minimum wage raises monopsony employment

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), let the minimum wage `w` lie strictly between the monopsony
wage and the marginal revenue product at monopsony employment: `w_m < w < γ - δ L_m`, where
`L_m = (γ - α) / (δ + 2 β)` and `w_m = α + β L_m`. Then every profit-maximizing employment level `L`
under `w` exceeds monopsony employment: `L > L_m`.
