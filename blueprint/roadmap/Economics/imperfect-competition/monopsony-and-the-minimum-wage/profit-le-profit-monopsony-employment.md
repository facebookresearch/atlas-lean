# Monopsony employment maximizes unregulated profit

In the linear monopsony model (inverse labor supply `α + β L` with `β > 0`, marginal revenue product
`γ - δ L` with `δ ≥ 0`, and `α < γ`), monopsony employment `L_m = (γ - α) / (δ + 2 β)` maximizes the
unregulated profit `π(L) = γ L - δ L² / 2 - (α + β L) L`: `π(L) ≤ π(L_m)` for every `L`.
