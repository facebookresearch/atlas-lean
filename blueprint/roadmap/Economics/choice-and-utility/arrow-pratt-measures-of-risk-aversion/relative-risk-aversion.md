# Arrow–Pratt coefficient of relative risk aversion

The Arrow–Pratt coefficient of relative risk aversion of a utility `u : ℝ → ℝ` at wealth `x` is
`R(x) = x A(x) = -x u''(x) / u'(x)`, with the conventions of the absolute coefficient `A` (a missing
derivative is `0`, and the quotient is `0` when `u'(x) = 0`).
