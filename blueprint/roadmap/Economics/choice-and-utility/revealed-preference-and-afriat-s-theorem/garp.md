# Generalized axiom of revealed preference (GARP)

The Generalized Axiom of Revealed Preference for demand observations `(pⁱ, xⁱ)`: whenever `xⁱ R xʲ`,
where `R` is the transitive closure of direct revealed preference (`pⁱ · xⁱ ≥ pⁱ · xʲ`), it is not
the case that `xʲ P⁰ xⁱ`, i.e. not `pʲ · xʲ > pʲ · xⁱ` (Varian 1982, p. 947, Definition (4);
equivalently `pʲ · xʲ ≤ pʲ · xⁱ`, Appendix I).
