# Local nonsatiation of a utility function

A utility `u` on bundles of finitely many goods `G` is locally nonsatiated when every bundle has
arbitrarily close bundles of strictly higher utility: for every `y : G → ℝ` and `ε > 0` there is `z`
with `dist(z, y) < ε` and `u(z) > u(y)`, the distance being the sup distance (the "nonsatiated"
hypothesis of Afriat's theorem, used as local nonsatiation in Varian 1982, Appendix I, proof of (1)
⇒ (2)).
