# GARP implies solvability of Afriat's inequalities

Afriat's theorem, (2) ⇒ (3). Let the demand observations `(pⁱ, xⁱ)` be finitely many, over finitely
many goods. If they satisfy GARP, there are numbers `Uⁱ` and `λⁱ > 0` solving Afriat's inequalities
`Uⁱ ≤ Uʲ + λʲ pʲ · (xⁱ - xʲ)` for all observations `i, j`. No sign condition on prices is needed.
