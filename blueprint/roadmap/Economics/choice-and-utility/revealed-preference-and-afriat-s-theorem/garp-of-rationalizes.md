# Data rationalized by a locally nonsatiated utility satisfy GARP

Afriat's theorem, (1) ⇒ (2). For demand observations `(pⁱ, xⁱ)` over finitely many goods, let `u` be
a locally nonsatiated utility (every bundle has bundles of strictly higher utility arbitrarily close
to it in the sup distance) that rationalizes the data: for every observation `i`, `u(y) ≤ u(xⁱ)` for
every bundle `y` with `pⁱ · y ≤ pⁱ · xⁱ`. Then the data satisfy GARP.
