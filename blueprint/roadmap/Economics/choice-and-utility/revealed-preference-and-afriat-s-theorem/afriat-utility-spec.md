# Afriat's inequalities yield a concave rationalizing utility

Afriat's theorem, (3) ⇒ (4). Let the demand observations `(pⁱ, xⁱ)` be indexed by a finite nonempty
set, over a finite nonempty set of goods, with strictly positive prices (`pⁱ_g > 0` for every
observation and good). Let `Uⁱ` and `λⁱ > 0` satisfy Afriat's inequalities
`Uⁱ ≤ Uʲ + λʲ pʲ · (xⁱ - xʲ)` for all `i, j`. Then Afriat's utility
`U(x) = minᵢ (Uⁱ + λⁱ pⁱ · (x - xⁱ))`

- is locally nonsatiated: every bundle has bundles of strictly higher utility arbitrarily close to
  it in the sup distance;
- is continuous, concave on all of `ℝ^G`, and monotone (`x ≤ y` entrywise implies `U(x) ≤ U(y)`);
- takes the value `Uⁱ` at `xⁱ` for every observation `i`;
- rationalizes the data: for every `i`, `U(y) ≤ U(xⁱ)` for every bundle `y` with `pⁱ · y ≤ pⁱ · xⁱ`.
