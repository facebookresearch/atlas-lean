# Costly perfect information can lower expected payoff

A decision maker faces a state $\theta \in \{0, 1\}$ with probability $1/2$ each and earns $1$ for choosing the action equal to $\theta$ and $0$ otherwise. Without information the expected payoff is $1/2$; free perfect information raises it to $1$; perfect information bought at price $c > 1/2$ lowers it to $1 - c < 1/2$.
