# Probability mixture of two lotteries

For lotteries `p`, `q` over a set of outcomes (probability vectors) and `α ∈ [0, 1]`, the
probability mixture `α p + (1 - α) q` (Gonçalves, Section 2) is the lottery with weights
`α p(x) + (1 - α) q(x)`: the pointwise convex combination of the weights, not a compound lottery.
