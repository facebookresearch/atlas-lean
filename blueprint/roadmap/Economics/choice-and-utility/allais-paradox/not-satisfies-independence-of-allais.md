# The Allais pattern violates independence

Any relation `≿` on lotteries over the three Allais prizes that strictly prefers the sure USD 1M
(`L₁ ≻ L₂`) while strictly preferring the long shot (`L₄ ≻ L₃`) fails the independence axiom: there
are lotteries `p`, `q`, `r` and `α ∈ (0, 1]` for which `p ≿ q ⟺ α p + (1 - α) r ≿ α q + (1 - α) r`
fails.
