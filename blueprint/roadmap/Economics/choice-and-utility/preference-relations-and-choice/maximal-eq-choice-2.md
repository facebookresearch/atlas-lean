# Maximal and greatest elements coincide for rational preferences

For a rational preference `≿` and every menu `B`, the maximal elements of `B` (those to which no
element of `B` is strictly preferred) are exactly its greatest elements, the choice set
`C(B; ≿) = {x ∈ B | x ≿ y for all y ∈ B}`; completeness is what makes them coincide.
