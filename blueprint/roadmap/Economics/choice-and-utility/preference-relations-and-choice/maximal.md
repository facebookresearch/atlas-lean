# Maximal menu elements under an arbitrary preference relation

For an arbitrary weak preference relation `R` and a menu `B`, the maximal elements of `B`: the
`x ∈ B` such that no `y ∈ B` is strictly preferred to `x` (that is, there is no `y ∈ B` with `R y x`
and not `R x y`). Every greatest element of `B` (one with `R x y` for every `y ∈ B`) is maximal;
without completeness the converse can fail.
