# A menu-dependent choice rule that is not rationalizable

Let $X = \{x, y, z\}$ and define a choice correspondence on the nonempty subsets by $C(\{x, y, z\}) = \{x\}$, $C(\{x, y\}) = \{y\}$, $C(\{x, z\}) = \{x\}$, $C(\{y, z\}) = \{y\}$ and $C(\{w\}) = \{w\}$ for each singleton. It violates contraction consistency, since $x \in C(\{x, y, z\}) \cap \{x, y\}$ but $x \notin C(\{x, y\})$. Hence there is no complete and transitive relation on $X$ whose maximal elements on every nonempty menu are given by $C$.
