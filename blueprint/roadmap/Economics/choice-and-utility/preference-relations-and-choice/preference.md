# Rational preference relation

A rational preference relation on a set of alternatives `α` (Levin–Milgrom, *Introduction to Choice
Theory*, §2): a relation `x ≿ y`, read "`x` is at least as good as `y`", that is complete (for all
`x`, `y`, `x ≿ y` or `y ≿ x`; Definition 1) and transitive (`x ≿ y` and `y ≿ z` imply `x ≿ z`;
Definition 2).
