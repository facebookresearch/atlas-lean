# Completeness makes maximal elements greatest

For a weak preference relation `R` that is complete (`R x y` or `R y x` for all `x`, `y`), the
maximal and greatest elements of every menu `B` coincide: no element of `B` is strictly preferred to
`x ∈ B` exactly when `R x y` for every `y ∈ B`. No transitivity is needed.
