# Choice by preference maximization satisfies contraction consistency

Let $\succsim$ be a complete and transitive relation on a set $X$, and for each nonempty finite menu $B \subseteq X$ let $C(B) = \{x \in B : x \succsim y \text{ for all } y \in B\}$. If $A \subseteq B$ are nonempty finite menus and $x \in C(B) \cap A$, then $x \in C(A)$ (contraction consistency, Sen's property $\alpha$). Choice may be set-valued: $C(B)$ can contain several mutually indifferent elements. This is one direction only; it is not a characterization of the choice correspondences that maximize some preference.

## Background

- [Rational choice from a finite nonempty menu is nonempty](choice-nonempty.md): the choice set of a nonempty finite menu is nonempty.
