# First-order stochastic dominance of finite lotteries

For lotteries `p`, `q` (probability vectors) on a finite linearly ordered set of prizes, `q`
first-order dominates `p` when at every threshold `t` it puts at most as much probability as `p` on
the prizes `≤ t`: `∑_{x ≤ t} q(x) ≤ ∑_{x ≤ t} p(x)` (the CDF of `q` lies weakly below the CDF of
`p`).
