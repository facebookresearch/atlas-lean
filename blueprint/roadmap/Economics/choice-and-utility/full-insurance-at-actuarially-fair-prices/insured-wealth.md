# Final wealth under a fairly priced insurance contract

For a finite set of states with probabilities `p(x)`, the final wealth in state `x` of an agent with
initial wealth `w` who suffers the loss `ℓ(x)` and holds the insurance contract paying the indemnity
`I(x)`, bought at the actuarially fair premium `E_p[I] = ∑ₓ p(x) I(x)`, is
`w - ℓ(x) + I(x) - E_p[I]`. Every indemnity schedule `I` is available (complete insurance market);
full insurance is `I = ℓ`.
