# Existence and uniqueness of the certainty equivalent

Consider a finite monetary lottery paying `z(x)` in state `x` with probability `p(x)`. If every
prize lies in `[a, b]` and `u` is continuous and strictly increasing on `[a, b]`, there is exactly
one `c ∈ [a, b]` with `u(c) = ∑ₓ p(x) u(z(x))`: the lottery has exactly one certainty equivalent in
`[a, b]`.
