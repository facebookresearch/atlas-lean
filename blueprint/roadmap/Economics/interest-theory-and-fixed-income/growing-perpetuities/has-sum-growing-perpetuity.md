# Discounted growing payments sum to the first payment over r − g

For real `C₁`, `r` and `g` with `1 + r > 0` and `0 ≤ (1 + g) / (1 + r) < 1`, the discounted
cash flows `C₁ (1 + g)ᵗ (1 + r)^{-(t+1)}`, `t = 0, 1, 2, …`, of a growing perpetuity are
summable, with sum `C₁ / (r - g)`. The hypotheses imply `r - g > 0`.
