# Discounted cash flow of a growing perpetuity

The `t`-th discounted cash flow (`t = 0, 1, 2, …`) of a growing perpetuity with first payment
`C₁` at date 1, growth rate `g` per period and constant discount rate `r` is the payment
`C₁ (1 + g)ᵗ`, made at date `t + 1`, discounted over `t + 1` periods:
`C₁ (1 + g)ᵗ (1 + r)^{-(t+1)}`. It is defined for all real `C₁`, `r` and `g`, with the
convention `0⁻¹ = 0` when `1 + r = 0`.
