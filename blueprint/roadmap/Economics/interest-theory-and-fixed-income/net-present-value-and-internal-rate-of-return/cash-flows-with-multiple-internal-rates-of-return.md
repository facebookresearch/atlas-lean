# Cash flows with several internal rates of return

The cash flows $(-1, 5, -6)$ at times $0, 1, 2$ have net present value $-1 + 5/(1+i) - 6/(1+i)^2$, which vanishes at both $i = 1$ and $i = 2$, so they have two internal rates of return. A single sign change is not necessary for uniqueness: $(-1, 4, -4)$ changes sign twice, but its net present value is $-(i - 1)^2/(1+i)^2$, so $i = 1$ is its only internal rate on $i > -1$. Uniqueness under a conventional pattern (one initial outflow followed by nonnegative, not all zero, inflows) is a separate sufficient condition.
