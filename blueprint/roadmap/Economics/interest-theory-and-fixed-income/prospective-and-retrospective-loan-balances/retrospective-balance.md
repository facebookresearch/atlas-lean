# Retrospective balance of a level-payment loan

The retrospective balance after `k` level payments `P`, made at the ends of periods
`1, …, k` on a loan `L` at per-period rate `r`, is the loan accumulated to date `k` minus the
accumulated payments: `L (1 + r)ᵏ - P ∑_{j=0}^{k-1} (1 + r)ʲ`, for all real `L`, `P` and `r`.
