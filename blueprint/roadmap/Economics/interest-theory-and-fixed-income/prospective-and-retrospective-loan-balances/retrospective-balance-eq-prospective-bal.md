# Retrospective and prospective loan balances agree

Let `r` be a rate with `1 + r ≠ 0`, and let the loan be the present value of its `n` level
payments `P` at the ends of periods `1, …, n`, at the same rate: `L = P ∑_{t=1}^{n} (1 + r)⁻ᵗ`.
Then for every date `k ≤ n` the retrospective balance `L (1 + r)ᵏ - P ∑_{j=0}^{k-1} (1 + r)ʲ`
equals the prospective balance `P ∑_{t=1}^{n-k} (1 + r)⁻ᵗ`.
