# Price of a cash-flow stream at a common yield

The price at a common per-period yield `r` of cash flows `c_t` paid at the dates `t` of a
finite set `s ⊆ ℕ` is their present value under the constant-rate discount factors:
`P(r) = ∑_{t ∈ s} c_t (1 + r)⁻ᵗ`. It is defined for every real `r`, with the convention
`0⁻¹ = 0` at `r = -1`.
