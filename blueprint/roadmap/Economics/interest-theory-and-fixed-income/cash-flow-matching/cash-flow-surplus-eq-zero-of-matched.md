# Exact cash-flow matching immunizes against any common discount curve

If the asset and liability flows agree at every payment date, `a_t = l_t` for every `t` in the
finite set `s` of payment dates, then for every discount curve `D` the surplus
`∑_{t ∈ s} D_t a_t - ∑_{t ∈ s} D_t l_t` is `0`. No sign or positivity condition on `D` is
needed.
