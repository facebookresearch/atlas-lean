# Present-value surplus of assets over liabilities

For asset cash flows `a_t` and liability cash flows `l_t` paid at the dates `t` of a finite set
`s ⊆ ℕ`, and a common discount curve `D_t` (any real numbers), the surplus is the asset
present value minus the liability present value: `∑_{t ∈ s} D_t a_t - ∑_{t ∈ s} D_t l_t`.
