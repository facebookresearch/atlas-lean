# Local (Redington) immunization, strict form

Let the asset and liability values `A` and `L`, real functions of the rate, be differentiable
at every rate near `r₀`, with derivatives `A′` and `L′` differentiable at `r₀`. If the values
match, `A(r₀) = L(r₀)`, the dollar durations match, `A′(r₀) = L′(r₀)`, and the asset dollar
convexity strictly exceeds the liability dollar convexity, `A″(r₀) > L″(r₀)`, then the surplus
`A(r) - L(r)` is strictly positive at every rate `r ≠ r₀` sufficiently close to `r₀`: the strict
convexity condition protects against every small nonzero parallel yield shift.
