# Constant-rate discount factor

The discount factor at a constant per-period rate `r` for a payment at integer date `t ≥ 0`
is `(1 + r)⁻ᵗ = 1 / (1 + r)ᵗ`. It is defined for every real `r`, with the convention
`0⁻¹ = 0`: at `r = -1` it is `0` for `t ≥ 1` (and `1` for `t = 0`). As a financial discount
factor it needs `r > -1`.
