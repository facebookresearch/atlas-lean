# Outstanding loan balance under amortization

A loan `L` at per-period rate `r`, repaid by payments `P_t` at the ends of periods
`t = 1, 2, …`, has outstanding balance given by the amortization recursion `B_0 = L` and
`B_{t+1} = (1 + r) B_t - P_{t+1}`. Payments need not be level, and no sign condition on `r`,
`L` or the payments is imposed.
