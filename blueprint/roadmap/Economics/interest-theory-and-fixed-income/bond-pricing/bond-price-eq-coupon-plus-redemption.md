# Bond price as coupon annuity plus discounted face value

For maturity `n ≥ 1`, so that the maturity is a payment date, the price of a bond with coupon
`c` and face value `F` at the rate `r` splits into the coupon annuity plus the discounted face
value: `∑_{t=1}^{n} (1 + r)⁻ᵗ (c + [t = n] F) = c ∑_{t=1}^{n} (1 + r)⁻ᵗ + (1 + r)⁻ⁿ F`. No sign
condition on `c`, `F` or `r` is needed.
