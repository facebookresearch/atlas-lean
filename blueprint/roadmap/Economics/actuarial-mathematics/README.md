# Actuarial mathematics

Mathematics of insurance and pensions: life tables and survival functions, life insurance and annuity present values and their identities, multiple-state and multiple-life models with reserve recursions and Thiele's equation, aggregate loss models and stop-loss premiums, credibility theory and claims reserving. Interest-only present values are under Interest theory and fixed income; survival estimation from data is under Nonparametric statistics and survival analysis in Probability.
