# Limit of the Bühlmann credibility factor

For a fixed between-group variance $a > 0$ and expected within-group variance $v \ge 0$, the Bühlmann credibility factor $Z_n = n a/(n a + v)$ tends to $1$ as the number $n \ge 1$ of observations tends to infinity. When $a = 0$ and $v > 0$, $Z_n = 0$ for every $n$. This is a statement about the coefficient, not the consistency of an estimator, and the affine credibility predictor is not in general the posterior mean.
