# Last-survivor annuity values by inclusion–exclusion

Let $T_1, T_2 \ge 0$ be lifetimes on the same probability space, possibly dependent, and fix finitely many payment dates $t$ with nonnegative benefits $c_t$ and discount factors $d_t > 0$. For an event-valued process $E_t$, let $A(E) = \sum_t c_td_tP(E_t)$. Then the last-survivor value $A(\{T_1 > t\} \cup \{T_2 > t\})$ equals $A(\{T_1 > t\}) + A(\{T_2 > t\}) - A(\{T_1 > t\} \cap \{T_2 > t\})$, by inclusion–exclusion for each $t$. No independence is assumed; the joint-life term is not the product of single-life survival probabilities in general.
