# Supporting prices for a Pareto optimum

Supporting prices for a Pareto optimum (MWG Prop. 16.D.1, exchange economy, with continuous
utilities). In a pure exchange economy with finitely many consumers and a finite, nonempty set `L`
of goods, let every utility `uᵢ` be continuous and quasiconcave on `ℝ^L_+` and locally nonsatiated
there (near every nonnegative bundle, in the sup distance, there is a nonnegative bundle of strictly
higher utility). If `x` is a Pareto optimal allocation of the aggregate endowment `ω`, there is a
price vector `p ≠ 0` such that for every consumer `i` and every nonnegative bundle `z`,
`uᵢ(z) > uᵢ(xᵢ)` implies `p · z ≥ p · xᵢ`.
