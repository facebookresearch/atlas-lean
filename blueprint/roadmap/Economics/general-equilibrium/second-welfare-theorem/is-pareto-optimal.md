# Pareto optimal allocation

In a pure exchange economy with a finite set of consumers, utilities `uᵢ` and aggregate endowment
`ω`, an allocation `x` is Pareto optimal (MWG Def. 16.B.3) when it is feasible (`xᵢ ≥ 0` for every
`i` and `∑ᵢ xᵢ = ω`) and no feasible allocation `y` has `uᵢ(yᵢ) ≥ uᵢ(xᵢ)` for every consumer and
`uᵢ(yᵢ) > uᵢ(xᵢ)` for some consumer.
