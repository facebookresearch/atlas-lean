# Local nonsatiation on the nonnegative orthant

A utility `v` on bundles over a finite set `L` of goods is locally nonsatiated on the consumption
set `ℝ^L_+` (MWG Def. 3.B.3) when, for every nonnegative bundle `z` and every `ε > 0`, there is a
nonnegative bundle `z'` with `dist(z', z) < ε` and `v(z') > v(z)`, the distance being the sup
distance `maxₗ |z' l − z l|`.
