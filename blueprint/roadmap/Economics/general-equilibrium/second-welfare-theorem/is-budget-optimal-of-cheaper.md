# Budget optimality under the cheaper-bundle condition

From the quasi-equilibrium consumer condition to budget optimality (MWG Prop. 16.D.2). Let the set
`L` of goods be finite, let every utility `uᵢ` be continuous on `ℝ^L_+`, and let every bundle `xᵢ`
be nonnegative. Suppose that for every consumer, every nonnegative bundle `z` with `uᵢ(z) > uᵢ(xᵢ)`
costs `p · z ≥ p · xᵢ`, and that every consumer has a strictly cheaper nonnegative bundle: some
`z ≥ 0` with `p · z < p · xᵢ`. Then each `xᵢ` maximizes `uᵢ` on its budget set: every `z ≥ 0` with
`p · z ≤ p · xᵢ` has `uᵢ(z) ≤ uᵢ(xᵢ)`.
