# Budget optimality of each consumer

Consumer condition of a price equilibrium with transfers (MWG Def. 16.B.4 (ii)), where the wealth of
consumer `i` is the value `p · xᵢ` of their bundle: for every consumer `i` and every nonnegative
bundle `z` with `p · z ≤ p · xᵢ`, `uᵢ(z) ≤ uᵢ(xᵢ)`. That is, `xᵢ` maximizes `uᵢ` over the
nonnegative bundles it can afford. The condition involves no feasibility.
