# Quasi-equilibrium consumer condition

Consumer condition of a price quasi-equilibrium with transfers (MWG Def. 16.D.1 (ii)), where the
wealth of consumer `i` is the value `p · xᵢ` of their bundle: for every consumer `i` and every
nonnegative bundle `z`, if `uᵢ(z) > uᵢ(xᵢ)` then `p · z ≥ p · xᵢ`. The condition involves neither
`p ≠ 0` nor feasibility.
