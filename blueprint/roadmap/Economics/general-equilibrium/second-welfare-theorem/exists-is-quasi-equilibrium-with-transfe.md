# Pareto optima are quasi-equilibria with transfers

Second fundamental welfare theorem (MWG Prop. 16.D.1, exchange economy, with continuous utilities).
In a pure exchange economy with finitely many consumers and a finite, nonempty set `L` of goods, let
every utility `uᵢ` be continuous and quasiconcave on `ℝ^L_+` and locally nonsatiated there (near
every nonnegative bundle, in the sup distance, there is a nonnegative bundle of strictly higher
utility). Then every Pareto optimal allocation `x` of the aggregate endowment `ω` is a price
quasi-equilibrium with transfers for some price vector `p`: `p ≠ 0`, and every nonnegative bundle
`z` with `uᵢ(z) > uᵢ(xᵢ)` has `p · z ≥ p · xᵢ`.
