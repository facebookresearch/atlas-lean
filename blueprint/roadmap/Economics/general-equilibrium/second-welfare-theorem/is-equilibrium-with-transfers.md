# Price equilibrium with transfers

In a pure exchange economy with finitely many consumers and goods, utilities `uᵢ` and aggregate
endowment `ω`, an allocation `x` and prices `p` form a price equilibrium with transfers (MWG Def.
16.B.4) when `p ≠ 0`, the allocation is feasible (`xᵢ ≥ 0` for every `i` and `∑ᵢ xᵢ = ω`), and for
every consumer `i` every nonnegative bundle `z` with `p · z ≤ p · xᵢ` has `uᵢ(z) ≤ uᵢ(xᵢ)`: each
`xᵢ` maximizes `uᵢ` on its budget set at wealth `p · xᵢ`.
