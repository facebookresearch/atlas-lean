# Equilibrium with transfers under the cheaper-bundle condition

From quasi-equilibrium to equilibrium (MWG Prop. 16.D.2). In a pure exchange economy with finitely
many consumers and goods, let every utility `uᵢ` be continuous on `ℝ^L_+`. If `(x, p)` is a price
quasi-equilibrium with transfers for the aggregate endowment `ω` and every consumer has a strictly
cheaper nonnegative bundle (some `z ≥ 0` with `p · z < p · xᵢ`), then `(x, p)` is a price
equilibrium with transfers.
