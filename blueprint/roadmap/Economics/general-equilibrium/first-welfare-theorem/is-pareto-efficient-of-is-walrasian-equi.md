# Walrasian equilibrium allocations are Pareto efficient

First welfare theorem. In a pure exchange economy with finitely many agents and goods, suppose every
utility `uᵢ` is locally nonsatiated on the nonnegative bundles: every neighbourhood (in the sup
distance) of every bundle `z ≥ 0` contains a bundle `z' ≥ 0` with `uᵢ(z') > uᵢ(z)`. Then for every
Walrasian equilibrium `(p, x)`, the allocation `x` is Pareto efficient.
