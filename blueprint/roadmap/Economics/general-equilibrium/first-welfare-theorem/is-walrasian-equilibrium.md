# Walrasian equilibrium of an exchange economy

In a pure exchange economy with finitely many agents and goods, endowments `ωᵢ` and utilities `uᵢ`,
a price vector `p` and an allocation `x` form a Walrasian equilibrium when

- prices are nonnegative and not all zero: `p ≥ 0`, `p ≠ 0`;
- `x` is feasible: `xᵢ ≥ 0` for every agent and `∑ᵢ xᵢ ≤ ∑ᵢ ωᵢ`;
- every good with a positive price clears: `p l > 0` implies `∑ᵢ xᵢ l = ∑ᵢ ωᵢ l`;
- each agent's bundle maximizes utility on the budget set of the endowment's value:
  `p · xᵢ ≤ p · ωᵢ`, and `uᵢ(z) ≤ uᵢ(xᵢ)` for every `z ≥ 0` with `p · z ≤ p · ωᵢ`.
