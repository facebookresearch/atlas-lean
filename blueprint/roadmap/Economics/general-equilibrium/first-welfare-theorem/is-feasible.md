# Feasible allocation with free disposal

In a pure exchange economy with a finite set `I` of agents and endowments `ωᵢ`, an allocation
`x = (xᵢ)ᵢ` is feasible when every agent's bundle is nonnegative, `xᵢ ≥ 0`, and total consumption is
at most the total endowment in every good, `∑ᵢ xᵢ ≤ ∑ᵢ ωᵢ` entrywise (free disposal).
