# Existence of a free-disposal equilibrium

Gale–Nikaido–Debreu. In an exchange economy with finite sets `I` of consumers and `L` of goods, with
at least one good, let `Δ = {p | p l ≥ 0 for every l, ∑ₗ p l = 1}` be the price simplex. Suppose
each consumer's demand `xᵢ` is continuous on `Δ` and exhausts the budget there: `p · xᵢ(p) = p · ωᵢ`
for every `p ∈ Δ`. Then a free-disposal equilibrium exists: some `p ∈ Δ` has `z(p) l ≤ 0` for every
good `l`, and `p l = 0` for every good with `z(p) l < 0`, where `z(p) = ∑ᵢ (xᵢ(p) − ωᵢ)` is
aggregate excess demand.
