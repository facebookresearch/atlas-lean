# Competitive equilibrium with free disposal

In an exchange economy with finitely many consumers and goods and aggregate excess demand `z`, a
normalized competitive equilibrium with free disposal is a price vector `p` such that

- `p` lies in the price simplex: `p l ≥ 0` for every good `l` and `∑ₗ p l = 1`;
- no good is in excess demand: `z(p) l ≤ 0` for every `l`;
- every good in excess supply is free: `z(p) l < 0` implies `p l = 0`.
