# Existence of a free-disposal equilibrium, assuming Brouwer

Gale–Nikaido–Debreu, from Brouwer's theorem. In an exchange economy with finite sets `I` of
consumers and `L` of goods, assume Brouwer's fixed-point property for the price simplex
`Δ = {p | p l ≥ 0 for every l, ∑ₗ p l = 1}`: every map `ℝ^L → ℝ^L` that is continuous on `Δ` and
maps `Δ` into `Δ` has a fixed point in `Δ`. Suppose each consumer's demand `xᵢ` is continuous on `Δ`
and exhausts the budget there: `p · xᵢ(p) = p · ωᵢ` for every `p ∈ Δ`. Then a free-disposal
equilibrium exists: some `p ∈ Δ` has `z(p) l ≤ 0` for every good `l`, and `p l = 0` for every good
with `z(p) l < 0`, where `z(p) = ∑ᵢ (xᵢ(p) − ωᵢ)` is aggregate excess demand.
