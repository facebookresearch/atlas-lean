# Exchange economy in demand form

An exchange economy in demand form, with a set `I` of consumers and a set `L` of goods: each
consumer `i` has an endowment `ωᵢ : L → ℝ` and a demand function giving, at every price vector
`p : L → ℝ`, a demanded bundle `xᵢ(p) : L → ℝ`. Bundles are arbitrary real vectors: nonnegativity is
not imposed. The existence of a free-disposal equilibrium does not need it: it holds for finitely
many consumers and finitely many goods, at least one, when demands are continuous on the price
simplex and exhaust the budget there. A pure exchange economy with nonnegative endowments and
demands is the special case.
