# Walras' law: exhausted budgets give excess demand of value zero

Walras' law (Levin, *General Equilibrium*, Proposition 4(iii)). In an exchange economy in demand
form with finitely many consumers and goods, endowments `ωᵢ` and demands `xᵢ`, let `p` be a price
vector at which every consumer's demand exhausts the budget, `p · xᵢ(p) = p · ωᵢ`. Then the value of
aggregate excess demand at `p` is zero: `p · z(p) = 0`, where `z(p) = ∑ᵢ (xᵢ(p) − ωᵢ)`.
