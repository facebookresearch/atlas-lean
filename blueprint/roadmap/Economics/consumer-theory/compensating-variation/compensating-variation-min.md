# Compensating variation for perfect complements

Worked example. For the perfect-complements utility `u(x) = min(x₀, x₁)` on the nonnegative quadrant
`{x ∈ ℝ² | x ≥ 0}`, a price change `p⁰ → p¹` with all prices positive, at wealth `w ≥ 0`, has
compensating variation `CV = w - (p¹₀ + p¹₁) w / (p⁰₀ + p⁰₁)`.
