# Perfect-complements utility is not differentiable at the kink

Let `a, b > 0`, let both prices be positive and let wealth be positive (`w > 0`). Then the
perfect-complements utility `u(y) = min(a y₀, b y₁)` is not differentiable at the demanded kink
bundle `(t / a, t / b)`, `t = w / (p₀ / a + p₁ / b)`, so no first-order condition is available
there.
