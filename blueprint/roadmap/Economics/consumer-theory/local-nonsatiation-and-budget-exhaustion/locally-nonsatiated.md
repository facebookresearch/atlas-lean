# Local nonsatiation on a consumption set

A utility `u` on bundles of finitely many goods is locally nonsatiated on a set `X` of bundles (MWG
Def. 3.B.3) when every neighbourhood of every bundle in `X` contains a bundle in `X` with strictly
higher utility: for every `x ∈ X` and `ε > 0` there is `y ∈ X` with `dist(y, x) < ε` and
`u(y) > u(x)`, the distance being the sup distance `maxᵢ |y i − x i|`.
