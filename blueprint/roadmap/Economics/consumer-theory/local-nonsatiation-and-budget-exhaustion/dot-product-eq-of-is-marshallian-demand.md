# Demand under locally nonsatiated utility exhausts the budget

Levin, *Consumer Theory*, Proposition 4. Let `L` be a finite set of goods and `u` a utility that is
locally nonsatiated on `ℝ^L_+` (near every nonnegative bundle, in the sup distance, there is a
nonnegative bundle of strictly higher utility). If `x` is a Marshallian demand on `ℝ^L_+` at prices
`p` and wealth `w` (`x ≥ 0`, `p · x ≤ w`, and `u(y) ≤ u(x)` for every `y ≥ 0` with `p · y ≤ w`),
then `p · x = w`. No sign condition on `p` or `w` is needed.
