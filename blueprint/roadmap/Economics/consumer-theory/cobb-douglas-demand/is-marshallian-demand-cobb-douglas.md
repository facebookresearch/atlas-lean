# Budget-share bundle maximizes Cobb–Douglas utility

Let `0 < α < 1`, let both prices be positive (`p₀, p₁ > 0`) and let wealth be nonnegative (`w ≥ 0`).
Then the budget-share bundle `x = (α w / p₀, (1 - α) w / p₁)` maximizes the Cobb–Douglas utility
`u(y) = y₀ ^ α * y₁ ^ (1 - α)` over the budget set `{y ∈ ℝ² | y ≥ 0, p · y ≤ w}`: it lies in that
set and `u(y) ≤ u(x)` for every `y` in it.
