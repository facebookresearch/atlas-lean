# Indirect utility equals utility at Marshallian demand

If `x` is a Marshallian demand at prices `p` and wealth `w` (it lies in the budget set
`B(p, w) = {y ∈ X | p · y ≤ w}` and `u(y) ≤ u(x)` for every `y ∈ B(p, w)`), then the indirect
utility is the utility of the demanded bundle: `v(p, w) = sup {u(y) | y ∈ B(p, w)} = u(x)`.
