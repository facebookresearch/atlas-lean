# Shephard's lemma in infimum form

For finitely many goods, a utility `u`, a consumption set `X` and a target utility `ū`, let `e` be a
function of prices that, at every price vector `p` near `p₀`, is the greatest lower bound of the
costs `{p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}`. Let `h ∈ X` reach `ū` (`u(h) ≥ ū`) at cost `p₀ · h = e(p₀)`, so
that `h` is a Hicksian demand at `p₀`. If `e` has derivative `e'` (a linear map) at `p₀`, then
`e'(v) = h · v` for every direction `v`: the price gradient of expenditure at `p₀` is `h`.
