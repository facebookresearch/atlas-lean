# Uniqueness of Hicksian demand under differentiable expenditure

For a utility `u` on bundles of finitely many goods, a consumption set `X` and a target utility `ū`,
suppose that for every price vector `p` near `p₀` the costs `{p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}` are bounded
below and that the expenditure function `e(p) = inf {p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}` is differentiable at
`p₀`. Then any two Hicksian demands `h`, `k` at `p₀` (bundles of `X` reaching `ū` at least cost) are
equal.
