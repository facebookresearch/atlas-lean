# Shephard's lemma in gradient form

For a utility `u` on bundles of finitely many goods, a consumption set `X` and a target utility `ū`,
let `e(p) = inf {p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}` be the expenditure function. Suppose that for every price
vector `p` near `p₀` these costs are bounded below, that `h` is a Hicksian demand at `p₀` (`h ∈ X`,
`u(h) ≥ ū`, and `p₀ · h ≤ p₀ · y` for every `y ∈ X` with `u(y) ≥ ū`), and that `e` has derivative
`e'` at `p₀`. Then `e'` is the linear map `v ↦ h · v`: the gradient of the expenditure function at
`p₀` is `h`.
