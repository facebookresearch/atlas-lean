# Hicksian (compensated) demand bundle

For finitely many goods, a utility `u`, a consumption set `X`, prices `p` and a target utility `ū`,
a bundle `h` is a Hicksian (compensated) demand when `h ∈ X`, `u(h) ≥ ū`, and `p · h ≤ p · y` for
every `y ∈ X` with `u(y) ≥ ū`: it reaches `ū` at least cost among the bundles of `X` reaching `ū`.
