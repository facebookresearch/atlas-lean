# Shephard's lemma for the expenditure function

Shephard's lemma for the expenditure function `e(p) = inf {p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}` of a utility
`u` on bundles of finitely many goods, a consumption set `X` and a target utility `ū`. Suppose that
for every price vector `p` near `p₀` the costs `{p · x | x ∈ X, u(x) ≥ ū}` are bounded below, that
`h` is a Hicksian demand at `p₀` (`h ∈ X`, `u(h) ≥ ū`, and `p₀ · h ≤ p₀ · y` for every `y ∈ X` with
`u(y) ≥ ū`), and that `e` has derivative `e'` (a linear map) at `p₀`. Then `e'(v) = h · v` for every
direction `v`.
