# Slutsky substitution matrix

For finitely many goods and a linear map `Dh` from price changes to demand changes (the derivative
of a compensated demand), the substitution matrix has entries `S i j = (Dh eⱼ) i`, where `eⱼ` is the
`j`-th unit vector: `S i j = ∂hᵢ/∂pⱼ` when `Dh` is the derivative of `h`.
