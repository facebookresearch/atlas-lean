# Uniqueness of Marshallian demand under strict quasiconcavity

Levin, *Consumer Theory*, Proposition 5. For finitely many goods, a consumption set `X`, prices `p`
and wealth `w`, let the utility `u` be strictly quasiconcave on the budget set
`B(p, w) = {x ∈ X | p · x ≤ w}`. Then there is at most one Marshallian demand: if `x` and `y` both
maximize `u` over `B(p, w)`, then `x = y`. Strict quasiconcavity includes convexity of the budget
set, which holds when `X` is convex.
