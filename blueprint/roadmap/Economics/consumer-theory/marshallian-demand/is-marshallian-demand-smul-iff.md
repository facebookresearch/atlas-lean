# Marshallian demand is homogeneous of degree zero

For finitely many goods, any utility `u`, consumption set `X`, prices `p`, wealth `w` and any
`t > 0`, a bundle `x` is a Marshallian demand at `(t p, t w)` exactly when it is one at `(p, w)`: it
maximizes `u` over `B(t p, t w) = {y ∈ X | t p · y ≤ t w}` if and only if it maximizes `u` over
`B(p, w)`.
