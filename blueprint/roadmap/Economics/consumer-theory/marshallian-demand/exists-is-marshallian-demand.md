# Existence of Marshallian demand for continuous utility

Levin, *Consumer Theory*, Proposition 2 (Existence). For finitely many goods, strictly positive
prices `p` and wealth `w`, suppose the budget set `B = {x ∈ ℝ^L_+ | p · x ≤ w}` is nonempty and the
utility `u` is continuous on `B`. Then `u` attains its maximum on `B`: some `x ∈ B` has
`u(y) ≤ u(x)` for every `y ∈ B`, so a Marshallian demand exists.
