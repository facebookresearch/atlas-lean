# Shifted discounted dividends form a growing perpetuity

For all real `D₁`, `r`, `g` and every `t ≥ 0`, the discounted dividend of the constant-growth
stream at date `t + 1` equals the `t`-th term of the growing perpetuity with first payment `D₁`
at date 1, growth `g` and discount rate `r`: both are `D₁ * (1 + g)^t / (1 + r)^(t + 1)` (with
the discount factor read as `0` when `r = -1`, by the convention `0⁻¹ = 0`).
