# Dividend stream growing at a constant rate

For real `D₁` and `g`, the dividend stream with first dividend `D₁` at date 1, growing at rate `g`
per period, pays at date `t` the dividend `D_t = 0` for `t = 0` (no dividend is paid today) and
`D_t = D₁ * (1 + g)^(t - 1)` for `t ≥ 1`. It is indexed by the payment date, whereas the terms of a
growing perpetuity are indexed from `0` at date 1.
