# Discounted dividends of a constant-growth stream

For real `D₁`, `r` and `g`, the discounted dividend at date `t` is `D_t / (1 + r)^t`, where `D_t`
is the dividend of the constant-growth stream (`D_0 = 0` and `D_t = D₁ * (1 + g)^(t - 1)` for
`t ≥ 1`) and `r` is the discount rate. The discount factor `((1 + r)^t)⁻¹` is read as `0` for
`t ≥ 1` when `r = -1` (convention `0⁻¹ = 0`).
