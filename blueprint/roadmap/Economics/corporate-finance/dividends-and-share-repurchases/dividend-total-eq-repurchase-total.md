# Dividends and repurchases give shareholders the same wealth

For `N > 0` shares at price `P > 0` and a feasible payout `0 ≤ C ≤ N * P`, the two payout
conventions give the same total shareholder wealth: the cash dividend total `C + N * (P - C / N)`
(ex-dividend price `P - C / N`) equals the repurchase total `C + (N - C / P) * P` (repurchase at
the pre-payout price `P`), both being `N * P`.
