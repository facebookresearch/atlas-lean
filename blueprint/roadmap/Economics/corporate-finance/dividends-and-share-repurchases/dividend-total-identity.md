# Cash dividend leaves total shareholder wealth unchanged

For `N > 0` shares at price `P` and a feasible payout `0 ≤ C ≤ N * P`, total shareholder wealth
under a cash dividend, `C + N * (P - C / N)` (the cash paid plus the ex-dividend market value at
the ex-dividend price `P - C / N`), equals `N * P` (an algebraic identity), and the ex-dividend
price `P - C / N` is nonnegative.
