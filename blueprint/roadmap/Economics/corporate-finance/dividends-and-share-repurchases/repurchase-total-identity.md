# Share repurchase leaves total shareholder wealth unchanged

For `N` shares at price `P > 0` and a feasible payout `0 ≤ C ≤ N * P`, total shareholder wealth
under a share repurchase at the pre-payout price, `C + (N - C / P) * P` (the cash paid plus the
market value of the remaining shares), equals `N * P` (an algebraic identity), and the remaining
share count `N - C / P` is nonnegative.
