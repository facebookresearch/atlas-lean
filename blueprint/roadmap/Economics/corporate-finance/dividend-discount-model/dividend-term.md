# Discounted dividend at date t + 1

For a real rate `r` and dividends `D t` at dates `t = 1, 2, …`, the discounted dividend indexed by
`t = 0, 1, 2, …` is `((1 + r)^(t+1))⁻¹ * D (t+1)`, the dividend paid at date `t + 1` discounted at
rate `r`; so the `t`-th summand of the infinite dividend sum is the dividend of date `t + 1`. At
`r = -1` the factor is `0`, by the convention `0⁻¹ = 0`.
