# Corporate finance

Valuation of firms and their securities: dividend discount and Gordon growth models, retained earnings and the value of growth, capital structure and the Modigliani–Miller theorems, the debt tax shield, payout policy, debt and equity as claims on the firm's assets, and real options. Pricing of forwards and options by absence of arbitrage, and asset and portfolio returns, are under Asset pricing and financial risk; present values of deterministic cash flows are under Interest theory and fixed income.
