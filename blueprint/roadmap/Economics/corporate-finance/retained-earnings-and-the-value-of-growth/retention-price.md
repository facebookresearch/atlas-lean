# Retention price of equity under reinvestment

For real next-year earnings `E₁`, retention share `b`, reinvestment return `ROE` and required
return `r`, the equity price under the constant-return reinvestment model is Gordon's formula with
dividend `D₁ = E₁ * (1 - b)` and growth `g = b * ROE`: `P = E₁ * (1 - b) / (r - b * ROE)`, with
the convention x / 0 = 0 when `r = b * ROE`. It is an equity price only for nonnegative earnings
`E₁ ≥ 0` and a retention share `0 ≤ b ≤ 1`, where the dividend is nonnegative; the retention share
alone does not suffice (`E₁ = -1`, `b = 1 / 2` gives the dividend `-1 / 2`). The formula is
defined for every `E₁` and `b` (an algebraic extension).
