# Nonnegative urban stocks after transfers, additions and retirements

Let $V$ be a finite set of zones and $E$ a finite set of arcs, each arc $e$ running from its tail
$t(e)$ to its head $h(e)$. Let $m_v \ge 0$ be the stocks, $F_e \ge 0$ the amounts transferred along
the arcs, $s_v \ge 0$ the additions and $r_v \ge 0$ the retirements, and suppose that at every zone
the outgoing amount does not exceed the stock: $\sum_{t(e) = v} F_e + r_v \le m_v$. If
$m'_v = m_v + \sum_{h(e) = v} F_e - \sum_{t(e) = v} F_e + s_v - r_v$ for every zone, then
$m'_v \ge 0$ for every zone $v$.
