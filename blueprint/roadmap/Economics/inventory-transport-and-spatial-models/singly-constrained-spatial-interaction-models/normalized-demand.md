# Singly constrained flow to a destination

The singly constrained demand to destination `i`: an origin total `O` distributed over a finite
destination set `s` in proportion to the weights `w`: `Tᵢ = O wᵢ / ∑_{j ∈ s} wⱼ`. The definition
imposes no sign condition on `O` or `w`; the value is `0` when `∑_{j ∈ s} wⱼ = 0`.
