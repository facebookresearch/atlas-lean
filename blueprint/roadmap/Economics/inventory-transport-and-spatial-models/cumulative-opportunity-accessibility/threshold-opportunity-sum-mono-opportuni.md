# Accessibility is monotone in opportunities

For a finite set `s` of destinations with opportunities `oᵢ ∈ ℝ` and travel times `tᵢ ≥ 0` (a
destination may have no travel time), the accessibility at threshold `T ≥ 0` is `A(T) = ∑ oᵢ` over
the destinations `i ∈ s` that have a travel time `tᵢ ≤ T`. At fixed travel times and threshold, if
`o₁ᵢ ≤ o₂ᵢ` for every `i ∈ s`, the accessibility computed with the opportunities `o₁` is at most the
one computed with `o₂`.
