# Accessibility is monotone in the travel-time threshold

For a finite set `s` of destinations with opportunities `oᵢ ∈ ℝ` and travel times `tᵢ ≥ 0` (a
destination may have no travel time), the accessibility at threshold `T ≥ 0` is `A(T) = ∑ oᵢ` over
the destinations `i ∈ s` that have a travel time `tᵢ ≤ T`. If every opportunity in `s` is
nonnegative, then `A(T₁) ≤ A(T₂)` whenever `T₁ ≤ T₂`.
