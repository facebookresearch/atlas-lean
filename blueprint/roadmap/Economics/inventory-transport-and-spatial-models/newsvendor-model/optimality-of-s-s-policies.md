# Optimality of (s, S) policies with setup costs

Consider the finite-horizon inventory problem with periods $k = 0, \dots, N-1$: the stock $x_k \in \mathbb{R}$ (negative values are backlog) is observed, the stock is raised to a level $y_k \ge x_k$ by an order received at once, i.i.d. bounded demands $w_k$ arrive, and $x_{k+1} = y_k - w_k$. Ordering $u = y_k - x_k$ costs $K\,\mathbf 1\{u > 0\} + cu$ with $K \ge 0$ and $c \ge 0$; the period cost is $L(y_k - w_k)$ with $L(x) = c_B\max(0, -x) + c_H\max(0, x)$, where $c_H \ge 0$, $c_B > c$ and $c + c_H > 0$; and a convex nonnegative terminal cost $v_N(x_N)$ is charged. A function $f$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is $K$-convex if $f(\theta y + (1-\theta)y') \le \theta f(y) + (1-\theta)(K + f(y'))$ whenever $y \le y'$ and $0 \le \theta \le 1$. Let $J_N = v_N$ and, for $k < N$, $G_k(y) = cy + E[L(y - w_k)] + E[J_{k+1}(y - w_k)]$ and $J_k(x) = \min\{G_k(x), \inf_{y > x}(K + G_k(y))\} - cx$. Then every $G_k$ is $K$-convex, continuous and tends to $+\infty$ as $|y| \to \infty$. With $S_k$ a minimizer of $G_k$ and $s_k$ the smallest $z \le S_k$ with $G_k(z) = K + G_k(S_k)$, the $(s_k, S_k)$ policy, which raises the stock to $S_k$ when $x < s_k$ and orders nothing when $x \ge s_k$, attains the minimum defining $J_k(x)$ for every $x$ and every $k$ (when $s_k < S_k$, at $x = s_k$ both actions attain it). For $K = 0$, choosing $S_k$ to be the smallest minimizer of $G_k$ gives $s_k = S_k$, so the $(s_k, S_k)$ policy is a base-stock policy; with another minimizer the $(s_k, S_k)$ policy is still optimal but need not be the base-stock rule for that $S_k$. Exact policy certificates for finite discretized models and two-echelon serial systems (the Clark–Scarf decomposition) are separate statements.
