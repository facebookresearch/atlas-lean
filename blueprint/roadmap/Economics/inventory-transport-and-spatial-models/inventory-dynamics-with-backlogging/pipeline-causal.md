# The pipeline depends only on past orders

In the backlogging inventory model with lead time `L ∈ ℕ`, initial pipeline quantities
`a_0, …, a_{L-1} ≥ 0` (arriving in periods `0, …, L - 1`), orders `q_t ≥ 0` placed in period `t`
(arriving `L` periods later), demands `d_t ≥ 0` and initial net stock `I₀ ∈ ℝ`, with `L > 0`: the
pipeline at period `t` never depends on orders placed at `t` or later. If two order streams agree in
all periods `s < t`, they give the same pipeline at period `t`.
