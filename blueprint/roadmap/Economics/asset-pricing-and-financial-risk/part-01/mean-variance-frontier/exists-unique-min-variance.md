# Existence and uniqueness of the minimum-variance portfolio

With a positive definite covariance matrix `S` and independent budget and mean constraints
(the expected returns `μ_i` are not all equal), for every target return `m` there is exactly one
portfolio `w` with `∑_i w_i = 1` and `∑_i μ_i w_i = m` whose variance `wᵀ S w` is minimal among
all such portfolios.
