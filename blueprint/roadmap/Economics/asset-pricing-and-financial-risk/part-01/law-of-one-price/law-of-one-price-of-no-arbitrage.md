# Law of one price under no arbitrage

Take finitely many states `ω`, at least one, and finitely many assets `a`, where asset `a`
costs `p(a)` today and pays `X(a, ω)` in state `ω`. Suppose that, for some gross risk-free
return `R > 0`, no portfolio `h` has funded gain `∑_a h(a) (X(a, ω) - R p(a))` nonnegative in
every state and strictly positive in some state. Then two portfolios `h` and `h'` with the
same payoff in every state, `∑_a h(a) X(a, ω) = ∑_a h'(a) X(a, ω)` for every `ω`, have the
same price: `∑_a h(a) p(a) = ∑_a h'(a) p(a)`. Both prices are the value of the common payoff
under a strictly positive state-price vector, which exists under this condition.
