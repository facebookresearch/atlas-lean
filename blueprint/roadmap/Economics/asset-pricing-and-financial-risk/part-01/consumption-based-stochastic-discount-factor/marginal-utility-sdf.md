# Marginal-utility stochastic discount factor

For a period utility `u`, a time-discount factor `β`, date-0 consumption `c0` and date-1
consumption `c1(ω)` in state `ω`, the marginal-utility stochastic discount factor is
`m(ω) = β u'(c1(ω)) / u'(c0)`. Here `u'` is the derivative of `u`, taken to be `0` at a point
where `u` is not differentiable, and `m = 0` when `u'(c0) = 0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
