# State prices from marginal utility are positive

Let `m(ω) = β u'(c1(ω)) / u'(c0)` be the marginal-utility stochastic discount factor for a
period utility `u`, date-0 consumption `c0` and date-1 consumption `c1(ω)`. If the
time-discount factor is positive, `β > 0`, every state has positive weight, `π(ω) > 0`, and
marginal utility is positive at both dates, `u'(c0) > 0` and `u'(c1(ω)) > 0` for every state,
then the implied state prices are strictly positive: `π(ω) m(ω) > 0` for every state `ω`.
