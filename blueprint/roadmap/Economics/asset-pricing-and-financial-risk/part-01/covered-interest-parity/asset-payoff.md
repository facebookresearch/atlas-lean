# Terminal payoffs of the two deposits and the forward

In a two-currency market with forward rate `F`, gross domestic return `R_d` and gross
foreign return `R_f`, let the spot rate at the end of the period be `S_T(ω)` in state `ω`
(any real function of the state). The terminal domestic payoffs of the three traded assets
are: the domestic deposit (asset 0) pays `R_d`; one unit of foreign currency deposited abroad
(asset 1) pays `R_f` foreign units, worth `R_f S_T(ω)`; a long forward on one unit of foreign
currency (asset 2) pays `S_T(ω) - F`.
