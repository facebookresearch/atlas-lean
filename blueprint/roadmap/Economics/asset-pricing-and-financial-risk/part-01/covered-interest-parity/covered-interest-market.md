# One-period two-currency market with a forward

A one-period two-currency market for covered interest parity is given by four real numbers:

* the spot exchange rate `S`, in domestic currency per unit of foreign currency;
* the forward exchange rate `F` for the end of the period, in the same quotation (domestic
  per foreign);
* the gross domestic return `R_d = 1 + r_d` over the period;
* the gross foreign return `R_f = 1 + r_f` over the period.

`S` and `F` are exchange rates in domestic currency per unit of foreign currency; `R_d` and
`R_f` are dimensionless gross returns. Any real values are allowed: no sign or positivity
condition is imposed on any of the four.
