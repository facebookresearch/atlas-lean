# Log change as a difference of logarithms

For nonzero values `x₀` and `x₁`, the log change is the difference of the logarithms:
`log (x₁ / x₀) = log x₁ - log x₀`. It is a log change of a quantity when both values are
positive; for negative values both logarithms are taken of absolute values, by the convention
`log x = log |x|`.
