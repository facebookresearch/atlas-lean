# The log change is the log of one plus the percentage change

For all real `x₀` and `x₁`, the log change is the logarithm of one plus the percentage change:
`log (x₁ / x₀) = log (1 + (x₁ - x₀) / x₀)`. At a zero base both sides are `0`, by the conventions
`x / 0 = 0` and `log 0 = 0`. It is a log change only for positive `x₀` and `x₁`.
