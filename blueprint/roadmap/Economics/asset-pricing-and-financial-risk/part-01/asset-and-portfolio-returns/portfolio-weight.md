# Initial-value weight of a holding in a portfolio

The initial-value weight of a holding `h` of an asset with initial price `P₀` in a portfolio with
initial wealth `W₀` (real numbers) is `h · P₀ / W₀`, the share of initial wealth held in this
asset. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `W₀ = 0`.
