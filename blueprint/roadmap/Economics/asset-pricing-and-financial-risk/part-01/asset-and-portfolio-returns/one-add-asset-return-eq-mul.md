# Percentage changes compound

Over two steps `x₀ → x₁ → x₂` with nonzero `x₀` and `x₁` and any `x₂`, one plus the overall
percentage change is the product of one plus the step changes:
`1 + (x₂ - x₀) / x₀ = (1 + (x₁ - x₀) / x₀) · (1 + (x₂ - x₁) / x₁)`. The overall change is
therefore the sum of the step changes plus their product, not their sum.
