# The initial-value weights sum to one when initial wealth is nonzero

For holdings `h i` and initial prices `P₀ i` of the assets `i` of a finite set `s` with nonzero
initial wealth `W₀ = ∑_{i ∈ s} h i · P₀ i`, the initial-value weights sum to one:
`∑_{i ∈ s} h i · P₀ i / W₀ = 1`. The portfolio is fully invested.
