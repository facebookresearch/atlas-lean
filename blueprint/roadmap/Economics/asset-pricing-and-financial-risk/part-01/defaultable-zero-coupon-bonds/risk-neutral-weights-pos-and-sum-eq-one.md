# Positive state prices divided by their sum form a probability vector

On a finite state space with at least one state and strictly positive state prices
`ψ(ω) > 0`, the risk-neutral weights `ψ(ω) / D`, where `D = ∑_ω ψ(ω)` is the risk-free discount
factor, are positive and sum to `1`: they form a probability distribution on the states.
