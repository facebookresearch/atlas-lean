# Reverse cash-and-carry arbitrage below the lower bound

In a forward market with spot bid price `bid ≥ 0`, gross lending rate `R_l`, income `I` per
unit of the underlying paid at maturity and forward price `F < bid · R_l - I`, the reverse
cash-and-carry strategy is an arbitrage: sell one unit of the underlying short at the bid,
lend the proceeds `bid` and buy one unit forward. Its initial cash flow is `0` and its
terminal cash flow is `bid · R_l - I - F > 0`.
