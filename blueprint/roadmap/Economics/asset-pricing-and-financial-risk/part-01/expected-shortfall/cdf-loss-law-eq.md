# Distribution function of a finitely supported loss

For weights `p(ω) ≥ 0` on a finite sample space with `∑_ω p(ω) = 1`, a loss `X` and a real
`x`, the distribution function of the loss law `∑_ω p(ω) δ_{X(ω)}` at `x` is
`F_X(x) = ∑_{ω : X(ω) ≤ x} p(ω)`, the total weight of the outcomes with loss at most `x`.
