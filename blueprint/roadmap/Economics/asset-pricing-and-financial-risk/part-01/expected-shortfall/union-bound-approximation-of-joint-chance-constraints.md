# Union-bound approximation of a joint chance constraint

For events $E_k = \{g_k(x, \xi) > 0\}$, $P(\bigcup_k E_k) \le \sum_k P(E_k)$, so requiring $P(E_k) \le \varepsilon_k$ with $\sum_k \varepsilon_k \le \varepsilon$ gives a conservative approximation of the joint chance constraint $P(\bigcup_k E_k) \le \varepsilon$.
