# Expected loss is PD × LGD × EAD for constant LGD and EAD

Let `μ` be a probability measure, `D` a measurable default event with `P(D) = μ(D) ≠ 0`, and
`EAD` (exposure at default) and `LGD` (loss given default) real functions that are constant on
`D`, equal to `ead` and `lgd` there, with `EAD · LGD` and `LGD` integrable on `D`. Then the
expected loss `∫ 1_D EAD LGD dμ` equals `P(D) · lgd · ead`.
