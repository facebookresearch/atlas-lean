# Nonnegative butterfly price under positive state prices

Take finitely many states `ω` with strictly positive state prices `ψ(ω) > 0` and terminal prices
`S(ω)`, and strikes `K1 ≤ K2 ≤ K3` with `K1 + K3 ≤ 2 K2`. Then the state-price value of the long
butterfly payoff is nonnegative:
`∑_ω ψ(ω) (max(S(ω) - K1, 0) - 2 max(S(ω) - K2, 0) + max(S(ω) - K3, 0)) ≥ 0`, since under these
strike conditions the payoff is nonnegative in every state.
