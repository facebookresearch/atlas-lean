# Long-only minimum of a quadratic form is attained

Let `S` be any real square matrix indexed by finitely many assets, `μ_i` their expected returns
and `m` a target return. If some weight vector `w` satisfies `w_i ≥ 0` for every `i`,
`∑_i w_i = 1` and `∑_i μ_i w_i = m`, then the quadratic form `wᵀ S w` attains its minimum on the
set of such weight vectors. No symmetry or definiteness of `S` is needed: the set is compact and
the objective is continuous.
