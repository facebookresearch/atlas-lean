# One-step risk-neutral price equals the replication cost

For real `S₀ ≠ 0`, `u ≠ d`, `R ≠ 0` and any payoffs `Cu` (up state) and `Cd` (down state), the
one-step risk-neutral price equals the replication cost:
`(q * Cu + (1 - q) * Cd) / R = Δ * S₀ + B`, where `q = (R - d) / (u - d)`,
`Δ = (Cu - Cd) / ((u - d) * S₀)` and `B = (u * Cd - d * Cu) / ((u - d) * R)`. The identity is
pure algebra; no sign conditions are needed.
