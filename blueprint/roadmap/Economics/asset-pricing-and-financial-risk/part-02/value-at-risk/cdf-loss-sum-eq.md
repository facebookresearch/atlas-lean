# Distribution function of the total loss

Under the product weights (each of two independent losses is 100 with probability 0.04, else 0),
the distribution function of the total loss `L₁ + L₂` is `0` below `0`, `0.9216 = 0.96²` on
`[0, 100)`, `0.9984 = 0.9216 + 2 · 0.96 · 0.04` on `[100, 200)` and `1` from `200` on. The sum
takes the values `0`, `100`, `200` with probabilities `0.9216`, `0.0768`, `0.0016`.
