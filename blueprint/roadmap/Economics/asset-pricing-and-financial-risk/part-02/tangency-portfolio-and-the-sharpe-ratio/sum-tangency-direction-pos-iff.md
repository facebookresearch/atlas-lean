# Total tangency weight is positive iff r is below the GMV mean

For a positive definite covariance matrix `S` of at least one asset and expected returns `μ`, the
tangency direction `z = S⁻¹ (μ - r 1)` has positive total weight, `1ᵀ z > 0`, iff the risk-free
return `r` is below `μ_GMV = (1ᵀ S⁻¹ μ) / (1ᵀ S⁻¹ 1)`, the expected return of the global
minimum-variance portfolio.
