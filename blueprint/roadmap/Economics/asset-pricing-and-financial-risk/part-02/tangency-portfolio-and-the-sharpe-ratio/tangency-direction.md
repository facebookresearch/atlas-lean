# Unnormalized tangency direction

For finitely many assets with covariance matrix `S`, expected returns `μ` and risk-free return `r`,
the unnormalized tangency direction is `z = S⁻¹ (μ - r 1)`, where `1` is the vector of ones. It is
defined for every square matrix `S`: for singular `S` the convention that a singular matrix has
inverse `0` makes `z = 0`; the intended domain is invertible `S`, such as a positive definite
covariance matrix.
