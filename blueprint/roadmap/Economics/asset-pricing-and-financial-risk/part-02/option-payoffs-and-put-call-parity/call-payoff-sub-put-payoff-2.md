# Call payoff minus put payoff equals S − K (restated)

For every terminal underlying price `S` and strike `K`, the call payoff minus the put payoff
equals `S - K`: `max(S - K, 0) - max(K - S, 0) = S - K`.

This is the same statement as
[Call payoff minus put payoff equals S − K](call-payoff-sub-put-payoff.md).
