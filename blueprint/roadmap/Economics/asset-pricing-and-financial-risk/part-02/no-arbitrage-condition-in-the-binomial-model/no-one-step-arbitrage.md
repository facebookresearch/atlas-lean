# Absence of one-step arbitrage in the binomial model

The one-step bond/stock model with real parameters `S₀`, `u`, `d`, `R` (no sign conditions
imposed; bond price `1` today and `R` at expiry, stock price `S₀` today and `u * S₀` in the up
state or `d * S₀` in the down state) has no one-step arbitrage when no real holdings `Δ` (shares)
and `B` (bonds) satisfy `Δ * S₀ + B ≤ 0`, `Δ * (u * S₀) + B * R ≥ 0` and
`Δ * (d * S₀) + B * R ≥ 0` with at least one of these two payoffs positive.
