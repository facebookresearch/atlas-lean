# Hotelling's lemma for the profit function, gradient form

For a production set `Y` of net-output vectors of finitely many goods, let
`π(p) = sup {p · y | y ∈ Y}` (a real supremum) be its profit function. Suppose that for every price
vector `p` near `p₀` the profits `{p · y | y ∈ Y}` are bounded above, that `y₀` maximizes profit at
`p₀` (`y₀ ∈ Y` and `p₀ · y ≤ p₀ · y₀` for every `y ∈ Y`), and that `π` has derivative `π'` at `p₀`.
Then `π'` is the linear map `v ↦ y₀ · v`: the gradient of the profit function at `p₀` is `y₀`.
