# Hotelling's lemma in supremum and gradient form

For finitely many goods and a production set `Y`, let `π` be a function of prices that, at every
price vector `p` near `p₀`, is the least upper bound of the profits `{p · y | y ∈ Y}`. Let `y₀ ∈ Y`
attain the maximal profit at `p₀` (`p₀ · y₀ = π(p₀)`), and let `π` have derivative `π'` at `p₀`.
Then `π'` is the linear map `v ↦ y₀ · v`: the gradient of profit at `p₀` is the optimal net supply
`y₀`.
