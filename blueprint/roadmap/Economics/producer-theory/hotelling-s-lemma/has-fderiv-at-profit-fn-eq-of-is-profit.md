# Hotelling's lemma for the profit function

Hotelling's lemma for the profit function `π(p) = sup {p · y | y ∈ Y}` (a real supremum) of a
production set `Y` of net-output vectors of finitely many goods. Suppose that for every price vector
`p` near `p₀` the profits `{p · y | y ∈ Y}` are bounded above, that `y₀` maximizes profit at `p₀`
(`y₀ ∈ Y` and `p₀ · y ≤ p₀ · y₀` for every `y ∈ Y`), and that `π` has derivative `π'` at `p₀`. Then
`π'(v) = y₀ · v` for every direction `v`: the derivative of profit in the price of an output is its
supply, and in the price of an input minus its demand.
