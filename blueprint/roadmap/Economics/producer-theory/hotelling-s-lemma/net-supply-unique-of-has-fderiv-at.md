# Uniqueness of optimal net supply in supremum form

For finitely many goods and a production set `Y`, let `π` be a function of prices that, at every
price vector `p` near `p₀`, is the least upper bound of the profits `{p · y | y ∈ Y}`, and suppose
`π` is differentiable at `p₀`. If `y₀, y₁ ∈ Y` both attain the maximal profit at `p₀`
(`p₀ · y₀ = p₀ · y₁ = π(p₀)`), then `y₀ = y₁`.
