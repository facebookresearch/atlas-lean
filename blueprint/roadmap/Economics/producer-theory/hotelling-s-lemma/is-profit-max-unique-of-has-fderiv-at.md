# Uniqueness of the optimal plan under differentiable profit

For a production set `Y` of net-output vectors of finitely many goods, suppose that for every price
vector `p` near `p₀` the profits `{p · y | y ∈ Y}` are bounded above and that the profit function
`π(p) = sup {p · y | y ∈ Y}` (a real supremum) is differentiable at `p₀`. If `y₀` and `y₁` both
maximize profit at `p₀` over `Y`, then `y₀ = y₁`.
