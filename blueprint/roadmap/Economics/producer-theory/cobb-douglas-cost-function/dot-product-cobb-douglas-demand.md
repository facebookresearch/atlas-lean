# Conditional factor demands attain the closed-form cost

For finitely many inputs, let the exponents satisfy `∑ᵢ aᵢ = 1` and the input prices be positive,
`wᵢ > 0`. Then for every `A` and `q` the conditional factor demands `xᵢ* = (aᵢ / wᵢ) · c(w, q)` cost
exactly the closed-form cost: `w · x* = c(w, q)`, where `c(w, q) = (q / A) ∏ᵢ (wᵢ / aᵢ) ^ aᵢ`.
