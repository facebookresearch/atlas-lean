# The closed-form Cobb–Douglas cost is the least cost

For finitely many inputs, let `A > 0`, exponents `aᵢ > 0` with `∑ᵢ aᵢ = 1` and input prices
`wᵢ > 0`, and let `q ≥ 0`. Then `c(w, q) = (q / A) ∏ᵢ (wᵢ / aᵢ) ^ aᵢ` is the least cost of producing
at least `q` with nonnegative inputs: it is the minimum of `{w · x | x ≥ 0, A ∏ᵢ xᵢ ^ aᵢ ≥ q}`.
