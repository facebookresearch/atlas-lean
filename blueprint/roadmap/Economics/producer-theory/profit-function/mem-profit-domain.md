# Attained maxima lie in the profit function's domain

For finitely many goods and a production set `Y`, if a plan `y` maximizes profit at prices `p`
(`y ∈ Y` and `p · y' ≤ p · y` for every `y' ∈ Y`), then `p` lies in the finite-valued domain
`{p | Y ≠ ∅ and {p · y | y ∈ Y} is bounded above}` of the profit function.
