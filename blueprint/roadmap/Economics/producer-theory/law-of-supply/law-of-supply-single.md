# Law of supply for a single price change

For finitely many goods, let `y` maximize profit at prices `p` and `y'` at prices `p'` over the same
production set `Y`, where `p` and `p'` differ at most in the price of good `i`. Then
`(p i - p' i) (y i - y' i) ≥ 0`: net supply of good `i` moves weakly in the same direction as its
price.
