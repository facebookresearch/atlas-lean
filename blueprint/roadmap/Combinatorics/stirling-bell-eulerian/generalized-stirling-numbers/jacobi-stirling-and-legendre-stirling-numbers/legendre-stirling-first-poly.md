# Generating polynomial of the Legendre–Stirling numbers of the first kind

For a natural
number $n$, this is the polynomial $\prod_{i=0}^{n-1} \bigl(X - i(i+1)\bigr)$ with integer
coefficients; for $n = 0$ it is the empty product $1$.
