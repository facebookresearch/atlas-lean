# Simsek numbers

For natural numbers $n$ and $k$ and a complex number $\lambda$, the Simsek
number $y_1(n, k; \lambda)$ is the complex number

$$y_1(n, k; \lambda) = \frac{1}{k!} \sum_{j=0}^{k} \binom{k}{j} j^n \lambda^j,$$

where $0^0 = 1$.
