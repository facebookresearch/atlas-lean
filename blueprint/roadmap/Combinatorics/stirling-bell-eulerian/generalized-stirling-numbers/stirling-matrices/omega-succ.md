# $\Omega(\alpha, j+1)$ as $j!$ times $\Psi_\alpha(j+1)$

For a sequence $\alpha \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Q}$ let $\Psi_\alpha(k) = \sum_{l=0}^{k-1} \frac{\alpha_l}{(k-1-l)!}$ and $\Omega(\alpha, j) = \sum_{l=0}^{j-1} (j-1)^{\underline{l}} \, \alpha_l$, where $m^{\underline{l}} = m(m-1)\cdots(m-l+1)$ is the falling factorial. Then for every natural number $j$,

$$\Omega(\alpha, j+1) = j! \, \Psi_\alpha(j+1).$$
