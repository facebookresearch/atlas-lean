# Alternating sum of reciprocal factorial products

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero. For every natural number $m$, in $K$,

$$\sum_{t=0}^{m} \frac{(-1)^t}{t!\,(m-t)!} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } m = 0, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

Replacing $t$ by $m-t$ reverses the finite sum. Thus the same identity holds with $(-1)^{m-t}$ in the numerator.
