# Diagonal entries of $U_\alpha$

Let $\alpha \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Q}$ and define rationals $U_{i,j}$ for all natural numbers $i, j$ by recursion on $i$: $U_{0,j} = 0$; $U_{1,0} = 0$ and $U_{1,j} = \alpha_{j-1}$ for $j \ge 1$; and for $i \ge 2$, $U_{i,j} = 0$ when $j \le 1$ and $U_{i,j} = U_{i-1,j-1} + (j - i + 1)\, U_{i-1,j}$ when $j \ge 2$, the coefficient $j - i + 1$ being computed in $\mathbb{Q}$. The matrix $U_\alpha$ uses the indices $i, j \ge 1$. Let $\Omega(\alpha, j) = \sum_{l=0}^{j-1} (j-1)^{\underline{l}} \, \alpha_l$, where $m^{\underline{l}} = m(m-1)\cdots(m-l+1)$ is the falling factorial. Then for every natural number $i$, $U_{i+1,i+1} = \Omega(\alpha, i+1)$.
