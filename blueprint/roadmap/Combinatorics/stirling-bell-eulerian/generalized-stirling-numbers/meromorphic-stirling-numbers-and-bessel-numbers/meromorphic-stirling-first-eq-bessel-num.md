# First identification of meromorphic Stirling and Bessel numbers

For all natural numbers
$n$ and $k$ with $1 \le k \le n$, the meromorphic Stirling number $S_{2;-1}(n,k)$ equals the
Bessel number of the first kind $b(n,k)$.

## Sources

- Toufik Mansour, Matthias Schork, and Mark Shattuck, *The Generalized Stirling and Bell Numbers Revisited*, Journal of Integer Sequences 15 (2012), Article 12.8.3, Section 3, equation (22) and Theorem 10.
