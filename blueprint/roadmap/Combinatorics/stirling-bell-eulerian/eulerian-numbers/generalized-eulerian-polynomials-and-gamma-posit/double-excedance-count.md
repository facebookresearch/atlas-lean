# Number of double excedances

For a permutation $w$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, a double
excedance of $w$ is a point $i$ with $w^{-1}(i) < i < w(i)$, and $\mathrm{dexc}(w)$ is the
number of double excedances of $w$.

## Sources
