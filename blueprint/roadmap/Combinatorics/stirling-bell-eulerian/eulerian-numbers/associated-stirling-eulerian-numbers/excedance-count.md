# Number of excedances

For a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, an excedance
of $\sigma$ is a point $i$ with $\sigma(i) > i$, and the excedance count $e(\sigma)$ is the number
of excedances of $\sigma$.
