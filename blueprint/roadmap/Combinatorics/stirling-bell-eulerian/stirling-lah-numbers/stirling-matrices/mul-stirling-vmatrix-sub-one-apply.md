# Columns of $M\,(V(n) - I_n)$

For a natural number $n$, let $V(n)$ be the $n \times n$ rational matrix, with rows and columns indexed by $1, \dots, n$, whose entries are $V(n)_{i,i} = i$, $V(n)_{i,i+1} = 1$ and $0$ elsewhere. Let $I_n$ be the identity matrix. For every $n \times n$ rational matrix $M$ and all $1 \le i, j \le n$,

$$(M\, (V(n) - I_n))_{i,j} = (j-1)\, M_{i,j} + M_{i,j-1},$$

where the term $M_{i,j-1}$ is omitted (read as $0$) when $j = 1$.
