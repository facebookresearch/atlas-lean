# Difference of consecutive Stirling numbers of the second kind

For natural numbers
$i \ge 1$ and $j \ge 1$, the Stirling numbers of the second kind $S(i, j)$ satisfy, in $\mathbb{Q}$,

$$
S(i + 1, j) - S(i, j) = \sum_{k=1}^{j-1} \frac{(-1)^{j-k+1} (k + 1)^{i-1}}{(k - 1)!\,(j - k - 1)!}.
$$

## Sources

- M. Bahrami-Taghanaki, A. R. Moghaddamfar, Navid Salehy and Nima Salehy, *On Matrices Whose Entries Are Stirling Numbers of the Second Kind (I)*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A27, Corollary 4 (`integers-2025-A27-cor-4`).
