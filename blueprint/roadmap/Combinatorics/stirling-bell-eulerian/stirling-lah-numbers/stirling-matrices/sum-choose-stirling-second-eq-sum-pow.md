# Alternating binomial sums of shifted Stirling numbers in closed form

Write $S(n,k)$ for the Stirling numbers of the second kind. For all natural numbers $i$, $j$ and $N$, in $\mathbb{Q}$ and with $0^0 = 1$,

$$\sum_{l=0}^{N} (-1)^{N-l} \binom{N}{l} S(i+l, j) = \sum_{k=0}^{j} \frac{(-1)^{j-k} \binom{j}{k} k^i (k-1)^N}{j!}.$$

Here $k - 1$ is computed in $\mathbb{Q}$, so the term $k = 0$ carries the factor $(-1)^N$.
