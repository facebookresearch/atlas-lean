# Determinant of the bidiagonal matrix $V(n)$

For a natural number $n$, let $V(n)$ be the $n \times n$ rational matrix, with rows and columns indexed by $1, \dots, n$, whose entries are $V(n)_{i,i} = i$, $V(n)_{i,i+1} = 1$ and $0$ elsewhere. Then $\det V(n) = n!$ for every natural number $n$ (for $n = 0$, the empty determinant $1 = 0!$).
