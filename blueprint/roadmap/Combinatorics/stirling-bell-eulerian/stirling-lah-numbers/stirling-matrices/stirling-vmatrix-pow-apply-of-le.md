# Lower triangle of the powers of $V(n)$

For a natural number $n$, let $V(n)$ be the $n \times n$ rational matrix, with rows and columns indexed by $1, \dots, n$, whose entries are $V(n)_{i,i} = i$, $V(n)_{i,i+1} = 1$ and $0$ elsewhere. For all natural numbers $n$ and $m$ and all $1 \le j \le i \le n$,

$$(V(n)^m)_{i,j} = \begin{cases} i^m & \text{if } i = j, \\ 0 & \text{if } j < i. \end{cases}$$
