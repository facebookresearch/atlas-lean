# Adding a fixed point adds one cycle

Count the cycles of a permutation with each fixed point counted as a cycle of length one. Let $n$ be a natural number, let $e$ be a permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, and let $\tau$ be the permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, n\}$ with $\tau(0) = 0$ and $\tau(x+1) = e(x) + 1$ for $x < n$. Then $\tau$ has exactly one more cycle than $e$.
