# Stirling numbers of the first kind and the rising factorial

Let $R$ be a semiring, let $n$ be a natural number and let $x \in R$. Writing $c(n, k)$ for the
unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind (the number of permutations of $n$ elements with
exactly $k$ cycles),

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} c(n, k)\, x^k = x (x + 1) \cdots (x + n - 1),$$

where the empty product for $n = 0$ is $1$.
