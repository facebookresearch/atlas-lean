# Engel's inequality for Bell numbers

For every integer $n \ge 1$, the Bell numbers
satisfy $B_n^2 \le B_{n-1} B_{n+1}$, where $B_n$ is the number of partitions of an $n$-element
set.

This non-strict inequality is Eq. (E2) of Horst Alzer, "On Engel's Inequality for Bell
Numbers," Journal of Integer Sequences 22 (2019); Alzer's strict refinement is a different
result.

## Sources

- Horst Alzer, *On Engel's Inequality for Bell Numbers*, Journal of Integer Sequences 22 (2019), Article 19.7.1, Section 1, inequality (2), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL22/Alzer/alzer6.pdf>, which attributes the inequality to K. Engel, "On the average rank of an element in a filter of the partition lattice," J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 65 (1994), 67–78.
