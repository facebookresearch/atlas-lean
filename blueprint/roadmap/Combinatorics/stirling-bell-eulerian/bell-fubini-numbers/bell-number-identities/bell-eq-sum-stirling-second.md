# Bell numbers are row sums of Stirling numbers of the second kind

For every
natural number $n$, the Bell number $B_n$ equals $\sum_{k=0}^{n} S(n, k)$, where $S(n, k)$ is the
Stirling number of the second kind.

## Sources

- Michael Z. Spivey, *A Generalized Recurrence for Bell Numbers*, Journal of Integer Sequences 11 (2008), Article 08.2.5, equations (1), (2), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL11/Spivey/spivey25.pdf>.
