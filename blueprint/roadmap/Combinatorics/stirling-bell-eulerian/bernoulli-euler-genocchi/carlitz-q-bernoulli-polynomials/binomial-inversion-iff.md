# Binomial inversion

For real sequences $(u_n)$ and $(v_n)$, the identities
$u_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} v_k$ hold for all natural numbers $n$ if and only if the
identities $v_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} \binom{n}{k} u_k$ hold for all natural numbers $n$.
