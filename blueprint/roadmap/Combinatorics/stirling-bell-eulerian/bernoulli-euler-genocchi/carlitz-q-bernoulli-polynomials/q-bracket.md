# The $q$-analogue of a real number

For real numbers $q$ and $x$, the $q$-analogue of $x$
is

$$[x]_q = \frac{q^x - 1}{q - 1},$$

where $q^x$ is the real power; we write $[x]$ when $q$ is fixed. When $q = 1$ the denominator is
$0$ and the quotient is taken to be $0$.
