# Claim 2 (decomposition into perfect matchings)

With the notation in the proof of Edmonds' theorem (a candidate non-integral vertex $x$ of $P$, a tight odd cut $(W, \bar{W})$, and the contracted decompositions $x' = \sum_{M'} \lambda_{M'} \chi^{M'}$ in $\operatorname{PM}(G')$ and $x'' = \sum_{M''} \alpha_{M''} \chi^{M''}$ in $\operatorname{PM}(G'')$), one has $x = \sum_{e \in (W, \bar{W})} \sum_{M : e \in M} (\lambda_{M'} \alpha_{M''} / x_e) \chi^M$, exhibiting $x$ as a convex combination of perfect matchings of $G$.
