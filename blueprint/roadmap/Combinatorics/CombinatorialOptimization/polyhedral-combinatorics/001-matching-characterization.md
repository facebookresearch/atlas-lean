# Claim 1 (matching characterization)

Any vector $x = (x_e)_{e \in E}$ satisfying $x_e \in \{0,1\}$ for all $e \in E$ and $\sum_{e \in \delta(v)} x_e \leq 1$ for all $v \in V$ is the indicator vector of a matching of $G = (V, E)$.
