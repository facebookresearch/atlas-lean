# Lemma 1 (Farkas)

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix and $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$. Exactly one of the following holds: (1) there exists $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $x \geq 0$ and $Ax = b$; or (2) there exists $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$ with $A^\top y \geq 0$ and $b^\top y < 0$.
