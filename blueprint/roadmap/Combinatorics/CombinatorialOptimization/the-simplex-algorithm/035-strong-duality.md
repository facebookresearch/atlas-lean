# Theorem 4 (Strong duality)

For the standard-form primal-dual LPs (P) and (D), exactly one of the following four cases occurs: (1) both (P) and (D) are bounded and feasible and $\operatorname{opt}(P) = \operatorname{opt}(D)$; (2) (P) is infeasible and (D) is unbounded but feasible; (3) (P) is unbounded but feasible and (D) is infeasible; (4) both (P) and (D) are infeasible.
