# Lemma 1 (success probability of contraction algorithm)

When the randomized contraction algorithm is run on an undirected multigraph on $n$ vertices, any particular minimum cut is returned as the output with probability at least $1 / \binom{n}{2}$, which is approximately $2 / n^2$.
