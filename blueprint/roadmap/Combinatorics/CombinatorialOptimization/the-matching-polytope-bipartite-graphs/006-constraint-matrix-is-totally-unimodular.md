# Lemma 4 (constraint matrix is totally unimodular)

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a bipartite graph. The constraint matrix obtained by stacking the $|V| \times |E|$ vertex-edge incidence matrix of $G$ on top of the negative of the $|E| \times |E|$ identity matrix is totally unimodular.
