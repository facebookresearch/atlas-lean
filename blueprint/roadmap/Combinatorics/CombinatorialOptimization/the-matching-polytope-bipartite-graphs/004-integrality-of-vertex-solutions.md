# Observation 2 (integrality of vertex solutions)

Let $A$ be an integer $m \times n$ matrix and $b$ an integer vector, and let $y$ be a vertex of the polyhedron $P = \{x \mid Ax \leq b\}$ given by an $n \times n$ nonsingular submatrix $A_1$ of $A$ and corresponding subvector $b_1$ of $b$ satisfying $A_1 y = b_1$. If $\det(A_1) = \pm 1$ then $y$ is integral.
