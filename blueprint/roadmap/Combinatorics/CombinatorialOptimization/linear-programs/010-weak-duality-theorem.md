# Weak Duality Theorem

For the standard primal LP $\max c^\top x$ subject to $Ax \leq b$, $x \geq 0$, and its dual $\min b^\top y$ subject to $A^\top y \geq c$, $y \geq 0$, $\max\{c^\top x \mid Ax \leq b,\; x \geq 0\} \leq \min\{b^\top y \mid A^\top y \geq c,\; y \geq 0\}$.
