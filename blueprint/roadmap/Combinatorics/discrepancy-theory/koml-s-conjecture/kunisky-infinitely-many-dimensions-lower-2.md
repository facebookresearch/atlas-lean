# Kunisky's lower bounds for the Komlós constant in the dimensions $2^k$

For every natural number $k$, the Komlós constant in dimension $2^k$ (the supremum of the
discrepancy of real $2^k \times 2^k$ matrices whose columns all have Euclidean norm at most $1$)
is at least

$$
2^{-k/2} + \sum_{a=1}^{k} 2^{-a/2} = 1 + \sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2} \cdot 2^{-k/2}.
$$

These bounds increase to $1 + \sqrt{2}$, which gives Kunisky's
[lower bound $1 + \sqrt{2}$ for the Komlós constant](kunisky2023-lower-bound-holds.md). They are
all strictly below $1 + \sqrt{2}$, so they do not show that the Komlós constant in some fixed
dimension reaches $1 + \sqrt{2}$.

## Sources

- Dmitriy Kunisky, *The discrepancy of unsatisfiable matrices and a lower bound for the Komlós conjecture constant*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 37(2) (2023), 586–603, arXiv:2111.02974, Theorem 1.1.
