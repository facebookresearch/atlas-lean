# Balanced coloring of a weighted symmetric matrix

Let $I$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets with $g = |I|$ and $K = |\Gamma| \ge 1$ elements, and suppose
that $4K^6 + K^2 < g$. Let $c = (c_{ab})_{a, b \in I}$ be a symmetric matrix of natural numbers
with zero diagonal whose row sums satisfy $\sum_{b \in I} c_{ab} \le P$ for every $a \in I$. Then
there is a colouring $\chi : I \to \Gamma$ whose colour classes all satisfy
$K \cdot |\chi^{-1}(\gamma)| < 2g$ and such that

$$
K^3 \sum_{a \in \chi^{-1}(\gamma)} \sum_{b \in \chi^{-1}(\gamma')} c_{ab} \le K \sum_{a \in I} \sum_{b \in I} c_{ab} + gP
$$

for all colours $\gamma, \gamma' \in \Gamma$, including $\gamma = \gamma'$.
