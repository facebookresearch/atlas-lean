# The zero sequence is not an extraTrib

The constant sequence $n \mapsto 0$ on
$\mathbb{Z}$ is not an extraTrib.

## Sources

- Eric Chen, Adam Ge, Andrew Kalashnikov, Ella Kim, Evin Liang, Mira Lubashev, Matthew Qian, Rohith Raghavan, Benjamin Taycher, Samuel Wang and Tanya Khovanova, *Generalizing the Wythoff Array and Other Fibonacci Facts to Tribonacci Numbers*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.5.4, Section 4, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL28/Khovanova/khova43.html
