# Kimberling's blocks form a prefix chain

Let $h$ be Kimberling's morphism on $\{0, 1\}$,
$h(1) = 110$ and $h(0) = 11$, and let $K_n = h^n(1)$. Then $K_m$ is a prefix of $K_n$ for all
natural numbers $m \le n$.
