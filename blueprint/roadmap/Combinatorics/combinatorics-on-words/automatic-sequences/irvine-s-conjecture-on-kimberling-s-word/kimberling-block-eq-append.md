# Recursive structure of Kimberling's blocks

Let $h$ be Kimberling's morphism on $\{0, 1\}$,
$h(1) = 110$ and $h(0) = 11$, and let $K_n = h^n(1)$. Then for every $n \ge 2$,
$K_n = K_{n-1} K_{n-1} K_{n-2} K_{n-2}$ (concatenation of words).
