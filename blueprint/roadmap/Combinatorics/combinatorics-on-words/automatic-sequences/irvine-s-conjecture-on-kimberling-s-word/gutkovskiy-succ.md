# Recurrence for Gutkovskiy's sequence

For every natural number $n$, Gutkovskiy's sequence
satisfies $g_{n+1} = (n + 1) - g_{\lfloor g_n/2 \rfloor}$ (subtraction of natural numbers,
truncated at $0$).
