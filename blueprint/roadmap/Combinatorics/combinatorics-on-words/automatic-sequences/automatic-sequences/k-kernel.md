# The $k$-kernel of a sequence

For a natural number $k$ and a sequence $a$ indexed by
$\mathbb{N}$, the $k$-kernel of $a$ is the set of all sequences $n \mapsto a(k^e n + r)$ with
$e, r \in \mathbb{N}$ and $r < k^e$.

## Sources

- Boris Adamczewski and Jason P. Bell, *On vanishing coefficients of algebraic power series over fields of positive characteristic*, Invent. Math. 187 (2012), 343–393; arXiv:1205.4091, Section 5, Definition 5.1 (the $k$-kernel), https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4091
