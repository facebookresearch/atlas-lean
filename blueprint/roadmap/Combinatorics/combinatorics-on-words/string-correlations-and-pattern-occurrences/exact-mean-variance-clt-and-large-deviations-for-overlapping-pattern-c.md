# Mean, variance, central limit and large deviations for overlapping pattern counts in Markov texts

Let $H$ be a nonempty pattern of length $m \ge 1$ with stationary probability $P(H) > 0$, and let $O_n(H)$ be the number of possibly overlapping occurrences of $H$ in a text of length $n$ generated by a stationary first-order Markov chain over a finite alphabet with at least two letters whose transition matrix is primitive, that is, irreducible and aperiodic, as the source assumes for its Markov model (the Bernoulli model, with all letter probabilities positive, being a special case). Régnier and Szpankowski (1998, Theorem 2.1) give the generating functions of $O_n(H)$, for exactly $r$ occurrences and bivariate, in terms of $D_H(z) = (1 - z)A_H(z) + z^mP(H)(1 + (1 - z)F(z))$, where $A_H$ is the autocorrelation polynomial of $H$ and $F$ is an explicit function of the transition matrix ($F = 0$ in the Bernoulli model). With at least two letters every letter has positive stationary probability, so $0 < P(H) < 1$. Assuming $nP(H) \to \infty$, their Theorem 2.2 gives, among other estimates: $E[O_n(H)] = P(H)(n - m + 1)$ for $n \ge m$; $\mathrm{Var}[O_n(H)] = c_1n + c_2 + O(r^{-n})$ for some $r > 1$, with explicit constants in terms of $A_H$, $P(H)$ and the fundamental matrix of the chain, where in the Bernoulli model $c_1 = P(H)(2A_H(1) - 1 - (2m - 1)P(H))$ and the formula is exact for $n \ge 2m - 1$; and, when $c_1 > 0$, the local central limit theorem $P(O_n = r) = (2\pi c_1 n)^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/2}(1 + O(n^{-1/2}))$ for integers $r = EO_n + x\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}\,O_n}$ with $x$ bounded. The expansion of $P(O_n = r)$ for fixed $r$ and the large deviation estimate, which rest on the dominant root of $D_H$ and on the root of $1 - e^tM_H(e^\rho) = 0$, are separate targets in the Expansion.

## Expansion

1. The model: the Markov text, the pattern, overlapping occurrences, and the autocorrelation polynomial (compare the [correlation-polynomial generating function for pattern avoidance](../../../Combinatorics/combinatorics-on-words/string-correlations-and-pattern-occurrences/correlation-polynomial-generating-function-for-pattern-avoidance-and-c.md)).

3. The exact mean (13) and the variance (14)–(19), with the constants $E_1, E_2$ from the fundamental matrix, in the Markov and Bernoulli models.
4. The local central limit theorem (23) when $c_1 > 0$.
5. Separate target: the regime $r = O(1)$, equations (20)–(22), with the source's conditions under which $D_H$ has a unique root $\rho_H$ of smallest modulus, real and greater than $1$, and a simple pole there.
6. Separate target: the large deviation regime, equations (24)–(27), with its conditions on the root of $1 - e^tM_H(e^\rho) = 0$.
