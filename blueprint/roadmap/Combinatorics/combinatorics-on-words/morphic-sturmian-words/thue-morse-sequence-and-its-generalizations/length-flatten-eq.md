# Length of a concatenation of words of equal length

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $u_1, \dots, u_n$ be finite
words over an alphabet, each of length $m$. Then the concatenation $u_1 u_2 \cdots u_n$ has
length $n \cdot m$.
