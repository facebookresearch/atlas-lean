# Cyclic shifting preserves word length

For every $L \in \mathbb{N}$, every letter $t$ of
the alphabet $\{0, \dots, L-1\}$ and every finite word $w$ over that alphabet, the word obtained
from $w$ by shifting each letter cyclically by $t$ has the same length as $w$.

## Sources

- Eiji Miyanohara, *Transcendence of Digital Expansions Generated by a Generalized Thue-Morse Sequence*, Journal of Integer Sequences 18 (2015), Article 15.9.2, Section 2, Definition 7 (generalized Thue–Morse sequences of type $(L, k, \kappa)$), https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL18/Miyanohara/miy5.html
