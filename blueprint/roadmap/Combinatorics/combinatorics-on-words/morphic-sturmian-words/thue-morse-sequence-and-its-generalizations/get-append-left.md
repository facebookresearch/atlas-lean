# Indexing into a concatenation

Let $u$ and $v$ be finite words over an alphabet, and let $m$
be a natural number less than the length of $u$. Then the letter at position $m$ of the
concatenation $uv$ equals the letter at position $m$ of $u$ (positions start at $0$).

## Sources
