# Thue–Morse substitution

The Thue–Morse substitution $\sigma$ on the alphabet $\{0, 1\}$
sends each letter to a word of length two: $\sigma(0) = 01$ and $\sigma(1) = 10$.

## Sources

- Scott Balchin and Dan Rust, *Computations for Symbolic Substitutions*, Journal of Integer Sequences 20 (2017), Article 17.4.1, Section 2, Example 4, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL20/Rust/rust3.html
