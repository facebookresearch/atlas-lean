# Area distribution

For a finite set $S$ of words of length $n$ with letters in the
natural numbers, the area distribution of $S$ is the polynomial
$\sum_{w \in S} q^{\operatorname{area}(w)}$ in $\mathbb{Z}[q]$, where the area of a word is the
sum of its letters.
