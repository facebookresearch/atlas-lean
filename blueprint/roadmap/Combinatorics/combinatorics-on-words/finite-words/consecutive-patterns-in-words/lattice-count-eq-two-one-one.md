# The value $b_{1,1}^{(2)} = 4$

For $m = 2$, $k = 1$ and $n = 1$, $b_{1,1}^{(2)} = 4$: the integer points $(x_1, x_2)$ with $\lvert x_i \rvert \le 1$ and $\lvert x_1 \rvert + \lvert x_2 \rvert = 1$ are exactly $(\pm 1, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 1)$.

## Sources

- Sela Fried, "Proofs of Several Conjectures From the OEIS", Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.4.3, Section 2.
