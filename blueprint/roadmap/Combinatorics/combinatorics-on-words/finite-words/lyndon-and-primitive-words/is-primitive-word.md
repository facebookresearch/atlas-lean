# Primitive word

A finite word $u$ over an alphabet $\alpha$ is primitive when $u$ is nonempty and, whenever
$u = v^n$ for some word $v$ over $\alpha$ and some natural number $n$ (the concatenation of $n$
copies of $v$), we have $n = 1$. Equivalently, $u$ is nonempty and is not a power $v^n$ with
$n \ge 2$.
