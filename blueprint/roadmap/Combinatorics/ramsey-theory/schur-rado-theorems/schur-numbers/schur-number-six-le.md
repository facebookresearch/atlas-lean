# Upper bound for the sixth Schur number

The Schur number $S(6)$ is at most $1836$.

## Sources

- the multicolour Ramsey bound $R_6(3) \le 1838$ (Stanisław P. Radziszowski, *Small Ramsey Numbers*, Electron. J. Combin. Dynamic Survey DS1) together with $S(k) \le R_k(3) - 2$; the range $536 \le S(6) \le 1836$ is stated in Shalom Eliahou and Pastora Revuelta, *The Schur degree of additive sets*, arXiv:2006.01502; listed in SqueezeBench `SqueezeBench/Problems/Schur/README.md`.
