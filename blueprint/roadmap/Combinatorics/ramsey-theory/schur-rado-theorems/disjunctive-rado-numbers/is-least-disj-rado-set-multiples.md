# Disjunctive Rado number for multiples of a + m - 3

Let $a$ and $m$ be natural numbers with $4 \le m \le a$, let $a' = a + m - 3$, and let $k_1, k_2$ be natural numbers with $1 < k_j \le a + m - 2$ for $j = 1, 2$. Put $c_j = k_j a'$. Then the two-color disjunctive Rado number $R(c_1, c_2)$, the least $R \ge 1$ such that every $2$-coloring of $\{1, \dots, R\}$ admits, for some $j \in \{1, 2\}$, a monochromatic solution in $\{1, \dots, R\}$ of

$$\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = c_j,$$

equals $\min\{k_1, k_2\}$. The case $k_1 = k_2$ is included, with a single equation.

## Sources

- Srashti Dwivedi and Amitabha Tripathi, *On the Two-Color Disjunctive Rado Number for the Equations $\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = c_j$, $j = 1, 2$*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A108, Theorem 5.
