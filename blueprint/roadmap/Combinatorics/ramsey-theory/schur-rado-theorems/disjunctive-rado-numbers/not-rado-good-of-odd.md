# Non-existence of the Rado number when a + m and c are odd

Let $a$ and $m$ be natural numbers with $4 \le m \le a$ and $a + m$ odd, and let $c$ be an odd integer. Then for every natural number $R$ there is a $2$-coloring of $\{1, \dots, R\}$ with no monochromatic solution $x_1, \dots, x_m \in \{1, \dots, R\}$ of

$$\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = c.$$

So the Rado number of this equation does not exist.

## Sources

- Srashti Dwivedi and Amitabha Tripathi, *On the Two-Color Disjunctive Rado Number for the Equations $\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = c_j$, $j = 1, 2$*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A108, Theorem 1 (quoted from the authors' earlier work).
