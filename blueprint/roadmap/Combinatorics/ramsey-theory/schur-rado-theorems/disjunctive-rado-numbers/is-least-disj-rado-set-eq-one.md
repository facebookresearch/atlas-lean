# Rado number 1 for the right-hand side a + m - 3

Let $a$ and $m$ be natural numbers with $4 \le m \le a$. The Rado number of the equation

$$\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = a + m - 3$$

is $1$: the least $R \ge 1$ such that every $2$-coloring of $\{1, \dots, R\}$ admits a monochromatic solution in $\{1, \dots, R\}$ is $R = 1$.

## Sources

- Srashti Dwivedi and Amitabha Tripathi, *On the Two-Color Disjunctive Rado Number for the Equations $\sum_{i=1}^{m-2} x_i + a x_{m-1} - x_m = c_j$, $j = 1, 2$*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A108, Proposition 2 (quoted from the authors' earlier work).
