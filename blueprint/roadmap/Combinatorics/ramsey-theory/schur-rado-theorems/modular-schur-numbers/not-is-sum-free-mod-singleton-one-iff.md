# When the singleton $\{1\}$ is sum-free

Let $m \ge 1$ and $l$ be natural
numbers. The singleton $\{1\}$ is not $l$-sum-free modulo $m$ if and only if
$l \equiv 1 \pmod{m}$.

## Sources

- Jourdan D'orville, Kai An Sim, Kok Bin Wong, and Chee Kit Ho, *Modular Generalizations of Schur Numbers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A62, Corollary 1 (`integers-2025-A62-cor-1`).
