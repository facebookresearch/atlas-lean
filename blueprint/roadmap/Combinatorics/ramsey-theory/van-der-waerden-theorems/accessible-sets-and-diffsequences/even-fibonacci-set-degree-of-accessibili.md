# The even Fibonacci numbers have degree of accessibility $1$

The even Fibonacci set
$\{2, 8, 34, \dots\}$ is $1$-accessible, and it is not $r$-accessible for any natural number
$r \ge 2$.

## Sources

- William J. Wesley, *Improved Ramsey-Type Theorems for Fibonacci Numbers and Other Sequences*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A29, Corollary 2 (`integers-2025-A29-cor-2`).
