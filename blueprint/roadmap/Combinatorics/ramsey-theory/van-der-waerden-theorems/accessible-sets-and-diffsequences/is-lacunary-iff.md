# Characterization of lacunary sequences

A sequence $(m_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of natural numbers
is lacunary if and only if $m_n > 0$ for every $n$ and there is a real number $q > 1$ such that
$m_{n+1} \ge q \, m_n$ for every $n \ge 0$.
