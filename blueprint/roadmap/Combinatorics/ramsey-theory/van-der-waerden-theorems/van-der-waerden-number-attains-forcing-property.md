# The positive van der Waerden number attains the forcing property

For any natural $r>0$ and $k>0$, the [van der Waerden forcing number](van-der-waerden-forcing-number.md) $W(r,k)$ is positive. At $N=W(r,k)$, every $c:\mathbb N\to[r]$ has a monochromatic $k$-term arithmetic progression with positive common difference satisfying $a+(k-1)d<N$. This is the realization of the infimum, not a forcing statement when the set of positive forcing sizes is empty.

## Depends on

- [Van der Waerden forcing number](van-der-waerden-forcing-number.md).

## Proof depends on

- [Van der Waerden's theorem](van-der-waerden.md), shifting a coloring of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ to the interval $[0,N)$ and taking the least positive forcing size.
