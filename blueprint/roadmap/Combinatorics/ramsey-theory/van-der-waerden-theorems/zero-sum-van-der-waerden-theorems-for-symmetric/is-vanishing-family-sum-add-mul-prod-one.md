# The sum-plus-product family is vanishing over $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$

Let $F_1$ be the
family over $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$ sending a block $(b_1, \dots, b_l)$ to
$\sum_i b_i + \prod_i b_i$. Then $F_1$ is vanishing.

## Sources

- Arie Bialostocki and Vladyslav Oles, *On Generalizing the Van Der Waerden Theorem to Some Symmetric Functions*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A25, Lemma 1 (`integers-2026-A25-lem-1`).
