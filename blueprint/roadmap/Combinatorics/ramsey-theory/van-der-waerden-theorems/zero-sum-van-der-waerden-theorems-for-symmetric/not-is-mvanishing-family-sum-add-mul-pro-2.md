# $F_{\pm 1}$ over $\mathbb{Z}/6$ is not $m$-vanishing for $m > 1$

For every $m > 1$,
neither of the sum-plus-product families $F_{-1}$ and $F_1$ over $\mathbb{Z}/6$, where
$F_c(b_1, \dots, b_l) = b_1 + \dots + b_l + c\, b_1 \cdots b_l$, is $m$-vanishing.

## Sources

- Arie Bialostocki and Vladyslav Oles, *On Generalizing the Van Der Waerden Theorem to Some Symmetric Functions*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A25, Lemma 4 (`integers-2026-A25-lem-4`).
