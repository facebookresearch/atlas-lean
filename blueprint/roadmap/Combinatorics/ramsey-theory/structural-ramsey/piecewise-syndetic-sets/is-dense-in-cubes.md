# Density in subcubes

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $C, M \subseteq \mathbb{N}^t$. The set
$C$ is $d$-dense in $M$ when every subcube of $\mathbb{N}^t$ of side $d$ that is contained in $M$
meets $C$.

## Sources

- Tom C. Brown, *Piecewise Syndetic Sets in $\mathbb{N}^t$ and $\mathbb{N}^X$*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A2, Definition 4 ($d$-dense in $M$).
