# Density one means containment

For sets $C, M \subseteq \mathbb{N}^t$, the set $C$ is
$1$-dense in $M$ if and only if $M \subseteq C$.

## Sources

- Tom C. Brown, *Piecewise Syndetic Sets in $\mathbb{N}^t$ and $\mathbb{N}^X$*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A2, Definition 4 with $d = 1$ (the case $m_0 = 1$ in the proof of Lemma 1).
