# Recurrence for the Padovan-like sequence

For natural numbers $p$, $q$ and $n$ with
$q \ge 1$ and $n \ge p + q$, the Padovan-like sequence satisfies
$a_{p,q,n} = a_{p,q,n-q} + a_{p,q,n-p-q}$.
