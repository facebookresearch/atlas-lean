# Diagonal weighted Schreier counts

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, the weighted
Schreier count satisfies $a_{n,n} = 2F_n$, where $F_n$ is the $n$-th Fibonacci number
($F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$).

## Sources

- Hùng Việt Chu and Zachary Louis Vasseur, *Weighted Schreier-type Sets and the Fibonacci Sequence*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), no. 4, Theorem 1.1 (`fq-2024-chu08302024rev-thm-1.1`).
