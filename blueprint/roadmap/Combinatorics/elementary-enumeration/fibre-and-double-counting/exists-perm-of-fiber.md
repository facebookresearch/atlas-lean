# Labelings with equal fiber sizes differ by a permutation

Let $V$ be a finite set and $\alpha, \beta : V \to C$ two maps whose fibers have equal sizes: $\lvert\alpha^{-1}(y)\rvert = \lvert\beta^{-1}(y)\rvert$ for every $y \in C$. Then there is a permutation $p$ of $V$ with $\beta(p(x)) = \alpha(x)$ for every $x \in V$.
