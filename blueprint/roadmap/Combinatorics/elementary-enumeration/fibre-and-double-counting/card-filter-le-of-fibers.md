# Density bounds transfer through equal-fiber maps

Let $\Omega_s$ and $X_s$ be finite sets and $f$ a map sending $\Omega_s$ into $X_s$ such that all fibers $\{\omega \in \Omega_s : f(\omega) = x\}$, $x \in X_s$, have the same cardinality. Let $Q$ be a property of elements of $X_s$ and $\varepsilon$ a real number. If $\lvert\{x \in X_s : Q(x)\}\rvert \le \varepsilon \lvert X_s\rvert$, then

$$\lvert\{\omega \in \Omega_s : Q(f(\omega))\}\rvert \le \varepsilon\, \lvert\Omega_s\rvert.$$
