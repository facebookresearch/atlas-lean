# Swap of the children of an internal vertex

A swap relates two [planar binary trees](plane-tree.md) when the second is obtained from the first by exchanging the two children of one internal vertex, anywhere in the tree: inductively, $(l, r)$ is related to $(r, l)$, and $(l, r)$ to $(l', r)$ or $(l, r')$ whenever $l$ is related to $l'$ or $r$ to $r'$.
