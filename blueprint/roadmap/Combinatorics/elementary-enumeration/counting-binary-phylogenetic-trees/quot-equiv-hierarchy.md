# Unrooted binary trees correspond to binary hierarchies

For every finite set $X$ and every $a \in X$, mapping a tree to its [cluster set](cluster-set.md) seen from $a$ is a bijection from the [isomorphism](iso.md) classes of [unrooted binary phylogenetic trees](unrooted-binary-tree.md) on $X$ onto the [binary hierarchies](is-binary-hierarchy.md) on $X \setminus \{a\}$.
