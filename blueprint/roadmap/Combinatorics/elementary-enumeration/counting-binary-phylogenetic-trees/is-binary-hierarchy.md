# Binary hierarchy on a finite set

A binary hierarchy on a finite set $S$ is a family $H$ of subsets of $S$ such that

- $S \in H$;
- every member is nonempty;
- $\{x\} \in H$ for every $x \in S$;
- any two members $C, D$ satisfy $C \subseteq D$, $D \subseteq C$ or $C \cap D = \emptyset$;
- every member with at least two elements is the disjoint union of two members.

These are the cluster systems of the rooted binary phylogenetic trees on $S$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
