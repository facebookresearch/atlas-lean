# The $(1,t)$-bonacci polynomials below the recurrence range

For all natural numbers $s$
and $n$ with $n < s$, the $(1,s+1)$-bonacci polynomials $f^{(s+1)}_m(x)$, $m \ge 0$, satisfy
$f^{(s+1)}_{n+1}(x) = f^{(s+1)}_n(x)$.

## Sources
