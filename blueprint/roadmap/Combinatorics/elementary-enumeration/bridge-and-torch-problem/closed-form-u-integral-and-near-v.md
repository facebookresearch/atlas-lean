# The capacity-3 closed form is an integer close to the lower bound

For every integer
$n \ge 7$, let $U(n) = n^2/6 + 2n - 181/36 + (-1)^n/4 - (2/9)\cos(2n\pi/3)$ and
$V(n) = n^2/6 + 2n - 11/2$. Then $U(n)$ is an integer and $0 \le U(n) - V(n) < 1$.
