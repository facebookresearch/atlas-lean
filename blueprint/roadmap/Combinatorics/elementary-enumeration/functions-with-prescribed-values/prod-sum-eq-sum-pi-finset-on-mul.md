# Products of factorized functions over label tuples

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $\iota$ a finite set, $P$ a property of elements of $\iota$, $(T_i)_{i \in \iota}$ finite subsets of a set $\sigma$, $d \in \sigma$ and $f, g : \iota \times \sigma \to R$. Then

$$\prod_{i:\ P(i)} \sum_{x \in T_i} f_i(x)\, g_i(x) = \sum_{t} \Big(\prod_{i:\ P(i)} f_i(t_i)\Big) \Big(\prod_{i:\ P(i)} g_i(t_i)\Big),$$

where $t$ runs over the [tuples with default $d$](pi-finset-on.md).
