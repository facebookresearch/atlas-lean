# Product of sums over default-padded tuples

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, $\iota$ a finite set, $P$ a property of elements of $\iota$, $(T_i)_{i \in \iota}$ finite subsets of a set $\sigma$, $d \in \sigma$ and $H : \iota \times \sigma \to R$. Then

$$\sum_{t} \prod_{i:\ P(i)} H_i(t_i) = \prod_{i:\ P(i)} \sum_{x \in T_i} H_i(x),$$

where $t$ runs over the [tuples with default $d$](pi-finset-on.md).
