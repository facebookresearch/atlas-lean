# Infinitely many ranks admit intersecting Ryser counterexamples

There are infinitely many positive integers $r$ for which a finite nonempty hypergraph is $r$-uniform and admits a part map to $r$ labels assigning exactly one vertex of each label to every edge, is intersecting, has a vertex cover, and has matching number one and cover number $r>(r-1)\nu$.

## Depends on

- Vertex covers, pairwise disjoint matchings and the finite part map.

## Proof depends on

- [Counterexamples in odd extension degrees](odd-extension-ryser-counterexamples.md)

## Proof outline

Fix one admissible prime and show that arbitrarily large odd degrees yield distinct ranks with the same exact witness predicate.

- A sufficiently large prime congruent to two modulo three, and strict growth of its powers along odd degrees.
