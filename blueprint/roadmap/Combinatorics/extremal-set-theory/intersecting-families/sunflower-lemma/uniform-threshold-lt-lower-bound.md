# Strict comparison of sunflower thresholds

For $r\ge3$ and $w\ge2$, the strict threshold of Kupavskii and Zakharov for uniform families
is smaller than the [Alweiss–Lovett–Wu–Zhang threshold](lower-bound.md) at the
[absolute constant](c.md) $C=2^{14}$:

$$
(2^{10}r\log_2(wr))^w < \bigl(2^{14}r^3\log_{1.9}w\,\log_{1.9}(\log_{1.9}w)\bigr)^w.
$$

## Sources

- Andrey Kupavskii and Dmitrii Zakharov, [*Spread approximations for forbidden intersections problems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13379v3), §2, equation (1), for the uniform threshold.
- Ryan Alweiss, Shachar Lovett, Kewen Wu, and Jiapeng Zhang, [*Improved bounds for the sunflower lemma*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08483v3), Theorem 1.4, for the base-$1.9$ expression.
