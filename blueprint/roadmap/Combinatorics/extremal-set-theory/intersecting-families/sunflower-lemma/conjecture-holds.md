# The Erdős–Szemerédi sunflower bound for $k \ge 4$

For every natural number $k \ge 4$ there
is a real number $c$ with $0 < c < 2$ such that, for all sufficiently large $n$, every family of
subsets of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ that contains no $k$-sunflower has at most $c^n$ members. Here a
$k$-sunflower in a family is a choice of $k$ distinct members whose pairwise intersections are
all equal.

Bucić, He and Lee (Theorem 1) proved that for every $k \ge 3$ and $n \ge 1$ a family of subsets
of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with no $k$-sunflower has at most $3\,(2 - 1/(16k^3))^n$ members; this gives
the bound $c^n$ for every $c$ with $2 - 1/(16k^3) < c < 2$ and all large $n$. The paper states
that the proof of its vector-sunflower bound (Theorem 2) was found by ChatGPT 6 Astra Pro and
that the authors verified all its results.
