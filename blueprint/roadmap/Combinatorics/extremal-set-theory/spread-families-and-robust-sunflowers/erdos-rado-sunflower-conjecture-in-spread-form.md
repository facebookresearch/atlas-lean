# Spread form of the Erdős-Rado sunflower conjecture

The *Erdős-Rado sunflower conjecture* asserts that for every $r \ge 3$ there is $C_r$ such that every family of more than $C_r^{\ell}$ sets of size $\ell$ contains an $r$-sunflower. By the spread-link lemma it would follow from the *spread form*: there is $C$ such that every $\ell$-bounded $(C r)$-spread family contains $r$ pairwise disjoint members, a statement equivalent to removing the $\log \ell$ factor from the spread lemma.
