# Projection onto the first two coordinates

A triple $(x, y, z)$ is sent to $(x, y)$. In the matrix-multiplication reading of the [Loomis–Whitney inequality](card-sq-le-card-mul-card-mul-card.md), where a triple $(i, j, k)$ is the multiply-add $C(i, j) \mathrel{+}= A(i, k)\, B(k, j)$, this is the accumulator $C(i, j)$ the operation updates.
