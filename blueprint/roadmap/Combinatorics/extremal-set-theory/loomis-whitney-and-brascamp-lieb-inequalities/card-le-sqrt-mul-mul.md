# Discrete Loomis–Whitney inequality, square-root form

For every finite set $V$ of triples, $|V| \le \sqrt{|V_{yz}| \cdot |V_{xz}| \cdot |V_{xy}|}$. This is the form in which Lemma 1 of Ballard, Demmel, Holtz and Schwartz (arXiv:0905.2485) and Lemma 2.2 of Irony, Toledo and Tiskin (J. Parallel Distrib. Comput. 64 (2004)) state it: a processor that accesses $N_A$ entries of $A$ and $N_B$ entries of $B$ and contributes to $N_C$ entries of $C = AB$ performs at most $\sqrt{N_A N_B N_C}$ elementary multiplications.
