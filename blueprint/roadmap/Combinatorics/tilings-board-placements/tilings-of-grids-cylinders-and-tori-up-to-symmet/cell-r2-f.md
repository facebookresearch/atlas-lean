# Cell map of the reflection r²f

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$, the reflection $r^2 f$
acts on the pairs $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ by $(x, y) \mapsto (x, -1 - y)$, that is, $(x, y) \mapsto (x, m - 1 - y)$ with
the second coordinate taken modulo $m$.
Here $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$; when $n, m \ge 1$, the elements of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ are the cells of the $n \times m$ grid.

## Sources

- Peter Kagey and William Keehn, *Counting Tilings of the n × m Grid, Cylinder, and Torus*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.1, Section 2.2, Definition 2 (extended to all of $D_4$ through the group operation).
