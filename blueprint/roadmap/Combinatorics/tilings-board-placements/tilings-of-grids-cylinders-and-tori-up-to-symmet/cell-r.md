# Cell map of the quarter-turn r

For a natural number $n$, the quarter-turn $r$ acts on the
pairs $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ by
$(x, y) \mapsto (-1 - y, x)$, that is, $(x, y) \mapsto (n - 1 - y, x)$ modulo $n$.
Here $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$; when $n \ge 1$, the elements of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ are the cells of the $n \times n$ grid.

## Sources
