# Cell map of the three-quarter turn r³

For a natural number $n$, the three-quarter turn
$r^3$ acts on the pairs $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ by $(x, y) \mapsto (y, -1 - x)$, that is, $(x, y) \mapsto (y, n - 1 - x)$
modulo $n$.
Here $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$; when $n \ge 1$, the elements of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ are the cells of the $n \times n$ grid.

## Sources

- Peter Kagey and William Keehn, *Counting Tilings of the n × m Grid, Cylinder, and Torus*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.1, Section 2.2, Definition 2 (extended to all of $D_8$ through the group operation).
