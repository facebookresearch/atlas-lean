# Cell map of the reflection f

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$, the reflection $f$ acts on
the pairs $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ by $(x, y) \mapsto (-1 - x, y)$, that is, $(x, y) \mapsto (n - 1 - x, y)$ with the first
coordinate taken modulo $n$.
Here $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$; when $n, m \ge 1$, the elements of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ are the cells of the $n \times m$ grid.

## Sources

- Peter Kagey and William Keehn, *Counting Tilings of the n × m Grid, Cylinder, and Torus*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.1, Section 2.2, Definition 2.
