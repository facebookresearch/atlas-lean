# The reflection $sr_0$ acts on the square grid as $f$

Let $r_i$ and $sr_i$
($i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$) denote the rotations and the reflections of the dihedral group
of order $8$. For every natural number $n$, the dihedral action on $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ (the cells of the $n \times n$ grid when $n \ge 1$; $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$) sends the
reflection $sr_0$ to the reflection $f$, $(x, y) \mapsto (-1 - x, y)$.

## Sources

- Peter Kagey and William Keehn, *Counting Tilings of the n × m Grid, Cylinder, and Torus*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.1, Section 2.2, Definition 2.
