# Rectangle-group cell action of the half-turn

Let $n, m \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $r_i$
($i \in \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$) denote the rotations of the dihedral group of order $8$. Under the
rectangle-group action on $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ (the cells of the $n \times m$ grid when $n, m \ge 1$; $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), $r_2$ acts as the half-turn $(x, y) \mapsto (-1 - x, -1 - y)$.

## Sources

- Peter Kagey and William Keehn, *Counting Tilings of the n × m Grid, Cylinder, and Torus*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.1, Section 2.2, Definition 2.
