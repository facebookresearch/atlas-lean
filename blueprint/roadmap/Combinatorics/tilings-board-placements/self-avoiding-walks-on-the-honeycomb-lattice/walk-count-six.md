# Six-step self-avoiding walks on the honeycomb lattice

There are exactly $90$
self-avoiding walks with $6$ steps on the honeycomb lattice starting at a fixed vertex.

Six is the first length at which self-avoidance excludes a walk that never immediately reverses a
step: of the $3 \cdot 2^5 = 96$ such walks, the $6$ that go once around one of the three hexagons
at the starting vertex return to it and are excluded.
