# Squared Fibonacci numbers as weighted sums of squares

Let $F_k$ denote the Fibonacci
numbers ($F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$) and let $\xi(m) = m \bmod 2$. For every natural number $n \ge 1$,

$$
F_{n+1}^2 - \xi(n+1) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (n-k+1) F_k^2 = \sum_{k=1}^{n} k F_{n-k+1}^2.
$$

## Sources

- Nassima Belaggoun and Hacène Belbachir, *Combinatorial Identities for the Bi-Periodic Fibonacci Numbers Squared*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), Article belaggoun01132024, Corollary 3.10 (`fq-2024-belaggoun01132024-cor-3.10`).
