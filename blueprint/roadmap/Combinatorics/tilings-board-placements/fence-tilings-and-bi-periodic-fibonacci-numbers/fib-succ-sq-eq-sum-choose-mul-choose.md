# Squared Fibonacci numbers as a double binomial sum

Let $F_k$ denote the Fibonacci
numbers ($F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$). For every natural number $n$,

$$
F_{n+1}^2 = \sum_{i=0}^{\lfloor 2n/3 \rfloor} \sum_{j=0}^{i} \binom{i}{j} \binom{2n-3i}{j} 2^j.
$$

## Sources

- Nassima Belaggoun and Hacène Belbachir, *Combinatorial Identities for the Bi-Periodic Fibonacci Numbers Squared*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), Article belaggoun01132024, Corollary 3.3 (`fq-2024-belaggoun01132024-cor-3.3`).
