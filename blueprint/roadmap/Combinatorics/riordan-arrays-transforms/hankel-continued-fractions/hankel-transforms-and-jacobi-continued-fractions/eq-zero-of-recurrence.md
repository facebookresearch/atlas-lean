# Hankel transform of a linearly recurrent sequence vanishes

Let $R$ be an integral domain, let $(P_n)_{n \ge 0}$ and $(c_k)_{k \ge 0}$ be sequences in $R$, and let $r \le N$ be natural numbers. Suppose that $c_k \ne 0$ for some $k \le r$ and that

$$\sum_{k=0}^{r} c_k\, P_{n-k} = 0 \quad \text{for every } n \ge r.$$

Then the $N$-th term of the Hankel transform of $P$ vanishes: the determinant of the $(N+1) \times (N+1)$ Hankel matrix $(P_{i+j})_{0 \le i, j \le N}$ is $0$.
