# Weak composition

Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers. A weak composition of $n$ into $k$ parts is a function
$a : \{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that $a(0) + a(1) + \dots + a(k - 1) = n$.
The values $a(i)$ are called the parts and may be zero.

When $k = 0$, the empty function is a weak composition of $n$ exactly when $n = 0$.
