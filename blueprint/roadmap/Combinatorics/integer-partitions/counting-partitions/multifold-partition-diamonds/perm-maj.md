# Major index of a permutation

For a permutation $\tau$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, d - 1\}$, the
major index is $\mathrm{maj}(\tau) = \sum_{p \in \mathrm{Des}(\tau)} p$, the sum of the
positions, numbered from $1$, in the descent set $\mathrm{Des}(\tau)$.
