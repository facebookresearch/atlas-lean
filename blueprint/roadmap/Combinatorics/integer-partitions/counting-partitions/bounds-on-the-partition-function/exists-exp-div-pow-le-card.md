# Exponential lower bound for the partition function

There exist a real number $H > 0$ and a natural number $B$ such that for every integer $n \ge 1$
the number $p(n)$ of integer partitions of $n$ satisfies

$$p(n) \ge \frac{H \exp\left(\pi \sqrt{2/3}\, \sqrt{n}\right)}{n^B}.$$

## Sources

- P. Erdős, *On an elementary proof of some asymptotic formulas in the theory of partitions*, Ann. of Math. (2) 43 (1942), no. 3, 437–450, formula (1), which gives the bound with $B = 1$.
