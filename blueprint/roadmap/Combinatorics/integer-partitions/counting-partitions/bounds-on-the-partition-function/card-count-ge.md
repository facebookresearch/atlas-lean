# Partitions with at least $k$ copies of a part

Let $E$, $v$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $v \ge 1$ and $kv \le E$. The number of partitions of
$E$ having at least $k$ parts equal to $v$ equals $p(E - kv)$, the number of partitions of $E - kv$.
Removing $k$ copies of the part $v$ gives a bijection between the two sets.

## Sources

- P. Erdős, *On an elementary proof of some asymptotic formulas in the theory of partitions*, Ann. of Math. (2) 43 (1942), no. 3, 437–450, the counting argument given for identity (2).
