# Each pair of positions is inverted by exactly half of the permutations

For distinct positions $i \ne j$ in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, exactly half of the $n!$ permutations
$\sigma$ satisfy $\sigma(j) < \sigma(i)$: twice their number is $n!$. Composing with the
transposition of $i$ and $j$ exchanges the permutations with $\sigma(j) < \sigma(i)$ and those with
$\sigma(i) < \sigma(j)$, and every permutation is of one kind.
