# Signed reversal distance of a signed permutation

For a natural number $n$ and a [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$, the
signed reversal distance $d^{\pm}(\pi)$ is the least natural number $k$ such that $\pi$
[sorts](ssortable-in.md) to $(1, \dots, n)$ in exactly $k$ signed reversals. Such a $k$ exists
because [every signed permutation can be sorted by signed reversals](ssortable.md); the distance
is defined only for signed permutations of $1, \dots, n$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed
setting).
