# Signed reversals remove at most two breakpoints of signed permutations

For a natural number $n$, a [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$ and natural
numbers $i \le j$ with $j$ less than the length $n$ of $\pi$, the
[signed breakpoint count](sbreaks.md) before and after the [signed reversal](srev-block.md) of
positions $i$ to $j$ satisfies
$b^{\pm}_n(\pi) \le b^{\pm}_n(\operatorname{rev}^{\pm}_{i,j}(\pi)) + 2$: a signed reversal
decreases the signed breakpoint count by at most two (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed
setting).
