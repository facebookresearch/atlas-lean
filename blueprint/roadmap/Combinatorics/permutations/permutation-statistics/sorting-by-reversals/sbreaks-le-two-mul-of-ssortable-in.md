# Sortable in k signed reversals implies at most 2k signed breakpoints

For natural numbers $k$ and $n$ and a [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$
that [sorts](ssortable-in.md) to $(1, \dots, n)$ in exactly $k$ signed reversals, the
[signed breakpoint count](sbreaks.md) satisfies $b^{\pm}_n(\pi) \le 2k$: each signed reversal of
a signed permutation removes at most two signed breakpoints, and the identity list has none
(Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting).
