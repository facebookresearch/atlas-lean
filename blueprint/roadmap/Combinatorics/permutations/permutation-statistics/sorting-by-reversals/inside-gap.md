# The two entries of a doubled pair differ by one

For every integer $x$, the entries of the [doubled pair](dbl-one.md)
$\delta(x) = (\delta_1(x), \delta_2(x))$ satisfy $|\delta_2(x) - \delta_1(x)| = 1$: their
[gap indicator](is-gap.md) is $0$, so the inside of a doubled pair is never a breakpoint (Shamir and
Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting). This includes $x = 0$, whose pair is $(-1, 0)$.
