# Doubling of a signed entry into a pair of consecutive integers

For an integer $x$, the doubled pair $\delta(x)$ is $(2x - 1, 2x)$ if $x \ge 0$ and
$(2|x|, 2|x| - 1)$ if $x < 0$. So for $a \ge 1$ the entry $+a$ becomes $(2a - 1, 2a)$ and the entry
$-a$ becomes $(2a, 2a - 1)$: two consecutive integers, increasing for a positive entry and
decreasing for a negative one (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting). Doubling is meant for
the nonzero entries of a [signed permutation](svalid.md); the entry $0$ falls in the first case and
gives $(-1, 0)$.
