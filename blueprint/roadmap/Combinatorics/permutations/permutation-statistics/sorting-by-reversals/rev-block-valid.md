# A reversal inside a permutation of 1, …, n gives another one

For a natural number $n$, a [permutation](uvalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$, written as a list
of integers, and natural numbers $i \le j$ with $j$ less than the length $n$ of $\pi$, the
[reversal](rev-block.md) $\operatorname{rev}_{i,j}(\pi)$ of positions $i$ to $j$ is again a
permutation of $1, \dots, n$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, §11.2).
