# Every signed permutation can be sorted by signed reversals

For every natural number $n$ and every [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$,
there is a natural number $k$ such that $\pi$ [sorts](ssortable-in.md) to $(1, \dots, n)$ in
exactly $k$ signed reversals (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting).
