# Unsigned reversal distance of a permutation of 1, …, n

For a natural number $n$ and a [permutation](uvalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$, written as a
list of integers, the unsigned reversal distance $d(\pi)$ is the least natural number $k$ such
that $\pi$ [sorts](usortable-in.md) to $(1, \dots, n)$ in exactly $k$ reversals. Such a $k$
exists because [every permutation of 1, …, n can be sorted by reversals](sortable.md); the
distance is defined only for permutations of $1, \dots, n$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, §11.2).
