# Signed reversals inside signed permutations add at most 2 breakpoints

For a natural number $n$, a [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$ and natural
numbers $i \le j$ with $j$ less than the length $n$ of $\pi$, the
[signed breakpoint count](sbreaks.md) after the [signed reversal](srev-block.md) of positions
$i$ to $j$ satisfies $b^{\pm}_n(\operatorname{rev}^{\pm}_{i,j}(\pi)) \le b^{\pm}_n(\pi) + 2$: a
signed reversal increases the signed breakpoint count by at most two (Shamir and Peer,
Lecture 11, signed setting).
