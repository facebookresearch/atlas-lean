# Doubling a list of signed integers

For a list of integers $\pi = (\pi_0, \dots, \pi_{m-1})$, the doubled list $\delta(\pi)$ is the
concatenation $\delta(\pi_0)\,\delta(\pi_1) \cdots \delta(\pi_{m-1})$ of the
[doubled pairs](dbl-one.md) of its entries, a list of length $2m$. For a
[signed permutation](svalid.md) of $1, \dots, n$ it is a permutation of $1, \dots, 2n$, the
unsigned permutation that represents it (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting).
