# The identity is the only permutation of 1, …, n with no breakpoints

For every natural number $n$ and every [permutation](uvalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$, written
as a list of integers, the [unsigned breakpoint count](ubreaks.md) satisfies $b_n(\pi) = 0$ if
and only if $\pi = (1, 2, \dots, n)$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, §11.2).
