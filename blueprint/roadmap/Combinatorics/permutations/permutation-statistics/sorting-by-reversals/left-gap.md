# Left-end gap is unchanged by doubling for a nonzero entry

For an integer $x \ne 0$ (that is, $|x| \ge 1$), the [gap indicator](is-gap.md) of $0$ and the first
entry $\delta_1(x)$ of the [doubled pair](dbl-one.md) of $x$ equals the
[directed gap indicator](is-dir-gap.md) of $(0, x)$: $|\delta_1(x)| = 1$ if and only if $x = 1$.
This compares the first adjacent pair of a signed list framed by $0$ with that of its doubled list
(Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting). For $x = 0$ the equality fails: $\delta_1(0) = -1$ is
one apart from $0$, but $0 - 0 \ne 1$.
