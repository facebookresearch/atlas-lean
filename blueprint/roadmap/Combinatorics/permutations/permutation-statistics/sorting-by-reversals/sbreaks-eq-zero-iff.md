# The identity is the only signed permutation with no signed breakpoints

For every natural number $n$ and every [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$,
the [signed breakpoint count](sbreaks.md) satisfies $b^{\pm}_n(\pi) = 0$ if and only if
$\pi = (1, 2, \dots, n)$, with every entry positive (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed
setting).
