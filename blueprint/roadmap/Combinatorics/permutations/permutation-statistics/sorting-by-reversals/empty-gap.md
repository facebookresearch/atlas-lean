# Directed gap from 0 to n + 1 equals the gap from 0 to 2n + 1

For every natural number $n$, the [directed gap indicator](is-dir-gap.md) of $(0, n + 1)$ equals
the [gap indicator](is-gap.md) of $(0, 2n + 1)$: both are $0$ if $n = 0$ and $1$ otherwise. They
are the signed breakpoint count of the empty list framed by $0$ and $n + 1$ and the unsigned
breakpoint count of its doubled list, the empty list framed by $0$ and $2n + 1$.
