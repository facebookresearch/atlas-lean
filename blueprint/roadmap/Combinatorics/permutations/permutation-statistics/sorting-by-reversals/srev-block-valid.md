# Signed reversals inside the list preserve signed permutations

For a natural number $n$, a [signed permutation](svalid.md) $\pi$ of $1, \dots, n$ and natural
numbers $i \le j$ with $j$ less than the length $n$ of $\pi$, the
[signed reversal](srev-block.md) $\operatorname{rev}^{\pm}_{i,j}(\pi)$ of positions $i$ to $j$
is again a signed permutation of $1, \dots, n$ (Shamir and Peer, Lecture 11, signed setting).
