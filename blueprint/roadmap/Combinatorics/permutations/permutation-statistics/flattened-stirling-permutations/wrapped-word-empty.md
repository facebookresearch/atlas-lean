# Wrapped word of the empty set

The wrapped word $g(\emptyset)$ of the empty set is the
empty word.

## Sources

- Adam Buck, Jennifer Elder, Azia A. Figueroa, Pamela E. Harris, Kimberly J. Harry, and Anthony Simpson, *Flattened Stirling Permutations*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A104, Lemma 3 (the map `g`).
