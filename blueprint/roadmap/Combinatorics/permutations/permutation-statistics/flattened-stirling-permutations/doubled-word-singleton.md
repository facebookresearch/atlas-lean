# The doubled word of a singleton

For every natural number $s$, the doubled word of the
singleton $\{s\}$ is $f(\{s\}) = s\,s$, the word of length two whose letters are both $s$.

## Sources

- Adam Buck, Jennifer Elder, Azia A. Figueroa, Pamela E. Harris, Kimberly J. Harry, and Anthony Simpson, *Flattened Stirling Permutations*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A104, Lemma 3 (the map `f`).
