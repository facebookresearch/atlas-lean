# Non-top components of the left-shift strategy

For all $n$ and $m$ with $m \ne n$, the
component on $m$ positions of the left-shift strategy $\mathrm{CSL}_n$ is the cyclic shift
$j \mapsto j + 1 \bmod m$ of $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$.
