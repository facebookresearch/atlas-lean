# Identity on two elements is decomposable

The identity permutation of $\{0, 1\}$ is not indecomposable: it maps the initial segment $\{0\}$ into itself.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 4.1, footnote 2.
