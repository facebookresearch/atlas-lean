# Indecomposable noncrossing nonnesting partitions and indecomposable permutations

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, there is a bijection between the indecomposable noncrossing nonnesting set partitions of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and the indecomposable permutations in $S_n(321, 3412)$.

$S_n(321, 3412)$ denotes the permutations of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ avoiding both classical patterns $321$ and $3412$. A set partition is indecomposable when for no $1 \le k < n$ does every block lie inside $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ or inside $\{k, \dots, n-1\}$; a permutation is indecomposable when for no $1 \le k < n$ does it map $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ into itself.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Proposition 9.
