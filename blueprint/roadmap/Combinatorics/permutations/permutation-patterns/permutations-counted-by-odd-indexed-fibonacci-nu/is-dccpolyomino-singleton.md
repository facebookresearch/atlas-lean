# Single cell is a directed column-convex polyomino

The one-cell set $\{(0, 0)\} \subseteq \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ is a directed column-convex polyomino; it has area $1$.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 5.1.
