# Identity avoids the vincular pattern 2143

For every natural number $n$, the identity permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ does not contain the vincular pattern $2143$ (positions $i, i+1, j, k$ with $i + 1 < j < k$ and $\sigma(i+1) < \sigma(i) < \sigma(k) < \sigma(j)$).

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 1.
