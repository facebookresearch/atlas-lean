# Partition into singletons is noncrossing and nonnesting

For every natural number $n$, the partition of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ into singletons is noncrossing and nonnesting: no two distinct blocks cross, and since no block has two elements there are no arcs, so no two arcs nest.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 4.1.
