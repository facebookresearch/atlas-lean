# Block fountains correspond to permutations avoiding 321 and vincular 2143

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, there is a bijection between the block $n$-fountains (lists of rows forming a block fountain above the bottom row of $n$ coins at positions $0, \dots, n-1$) and the permutations of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ that avoid the classical pattern $321$ and avoid the vincular pattern $2143$ (positions $i, i+1, j, k$ with $i + 1 < j < k$ and $\sigma(i+1) < \sigma(i) < \sigma(k) < \sigma(j)$).

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Proposition 6.
