# Dyck path of height at most three

A Dyck word $p$ (a word in up steps $U$ and down steps $D$) has height at most $3$ when for every $i$ not exceeding its length, the first $i$ steps of $p$ contain at most three more $U$ steps than $D$ steps:

$$\#_U(p_1 \cdots p_i) \le \#_D(p_1 \cdots p_i) + 3.$$

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Proposition 3 (Dyck paths of height at most $3$).
