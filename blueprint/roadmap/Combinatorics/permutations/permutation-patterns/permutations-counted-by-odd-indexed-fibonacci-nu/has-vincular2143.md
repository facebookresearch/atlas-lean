# Containing the vincular pattern 2143

A permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ contains the vincular pattern $2143$ when there are positions $i, j, k \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with $i + 1 < j < k$ such that

$$\sigma(i+1) < \sigma(i) < \sigma(k) < \sigma(j).$$

That is, $\sigma$ has an occurrence of the classical pattern $2143$ whose first two entries are adjacent.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 1 (vincular pattern $2143$).
