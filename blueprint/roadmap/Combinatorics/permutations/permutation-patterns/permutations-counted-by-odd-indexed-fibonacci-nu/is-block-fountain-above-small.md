# Smallest block fountains

For all natural numbers $a$ and $l \ge 2$, the empty list of rows is a block fountain above the row $(a, l)$, and so is the single row $(a, l - 1)$ of $l - 1$ coins starting at position $a$.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Section 3.1.
