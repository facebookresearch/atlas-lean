# Directed column-convex polyominoes correspond to 231- and 3124-avoiding permutations

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, there is a bijection between the directed column-convex polyominoes of area $n$ (finite sets of $n$ cells in $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ containing $(0,0)$, each other cell having its south or west neighbour in the set, with contiguous columns) and the permutations of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ avoiding both classical patterns $231$ and $3124$.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Proposition 12.
