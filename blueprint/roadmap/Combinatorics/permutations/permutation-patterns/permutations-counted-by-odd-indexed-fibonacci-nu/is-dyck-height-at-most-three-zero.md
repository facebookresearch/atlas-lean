# Empty Dyck path has height at most three

The empty Dyck word has height at most $3$: none of its prefixes has more than three more up steps than down steps.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Felix H. Xu, and William Y. Zhu, "Odd-Indexed Fibonacci Numbers via Pattern-Avoiding Permutations", *INTEGERS* 25 (2025), Article A79, Proposition 3.
