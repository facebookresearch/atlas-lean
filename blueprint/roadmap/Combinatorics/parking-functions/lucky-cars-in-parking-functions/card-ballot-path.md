# Number of ballot paths

For natural numbers $k \le \ell$, the number of lattice paths
from $(0,0)$ to $(k, \ell)$ with unit up and right steps that stay on or above the line $y = x$
is $\frac{\ell - k + 1}{\ell + 1} \binom{k + \ell}{k}$.

## Sources

- Steve Butler, Kimberly Hadaway, Victoria Lenius, Preston Martens, and Marshall Moats, "Lucky Cars and Lucky Spots in Parking Functions", Journal of Integer Sequences 29 (2026), Article 26.1.1, Lemma 21.
