# Displacement compositions of 1122 and 11244233

The displacement composition of a word
$w = w_1 \cdots w_{2n}$ is $\mathrm{dis}(w) = (d(1), \dots, d(2n))$, where $d(i)$ is the
displacement of car $i$. For the Stirling permutations $1122$ of order $2$ and $11244233$ of
order $4$, $\mathrm{dis}(1122) = (0, 1, 1, 2)$ and
$\mathrm{dis}(11244233) = (0, 1, 1, 0, 1, 4, 4, 5)$ (the paper's Example 40).

## Sources

- Laura Colmenarejo, Jennifer Elder, Kimberly J. Harry, Dorian Smith, Aleyah Dawkins, Pamela E. Harris, Selvi Kara, and Bridget Eileen Tenner, *On the Lucky and Displacement Statistics of Stirling Permutations*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.6.7, Definition 39 and Example 40.
