# Every $k$-Naples parking function is $(k + 1)$-Naples

For all natural numbers $k$
and $n$, every $k$-Naples parking function $\alpha$ of length $n$ is also a $(k + 1)$-Naples
parking function.

## Sources

- João Pedro Carvalho, Pamela E. Harris, Gordon Rojas Kirby, Nico Tripeny, and Andrés R. Vindas-Meléndez, *Enumerating k-Naples Parking Functions through Catalan Objects*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A44, Proposition 1.
