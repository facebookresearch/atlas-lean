# Left spine length of a binary tree

The left spine length of a binary tree $t$ is the number of nodes on its left spine: the root,
its left child, that child's left child, and so on. The empty tree has left spine length $0$, and
a tree with root, left subtree $l$ and right subtree $r$ has left spine length one more than
that of $l$.
