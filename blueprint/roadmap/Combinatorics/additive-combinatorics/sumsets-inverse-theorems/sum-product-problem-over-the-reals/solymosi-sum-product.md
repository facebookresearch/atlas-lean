# Solymosi's finite sum-product inequality

For a finite real set $A$ with at least two
elements, $|A|^4 \log 2 \le 1296 |AA| |A+A|^2 \log |A|$. Apply the positive-set bound to the
larger of the positive and negative parts of $A$, reflecting the negative part if chosen.
If the larger part is a singleton, then $|A| \le 3$ and the inequality holds directly.

## Sources

- Derived from Theorem 2.1 of József Solymosi, *Bounding multiplicative energy by the sumset*, https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.1040.
