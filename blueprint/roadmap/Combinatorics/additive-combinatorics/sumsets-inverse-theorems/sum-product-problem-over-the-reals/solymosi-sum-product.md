# Sum-product problem over the reals

For a finite real set $A$ with at least two elements,
$|A|^4 \log 2 \le 1296 |AA| |A+A|^2 \log |A|$. The absolute constant accounts for restricting
to the larger sign class and reflecting it if necessary.

## Sources

- József Solymosi, *Bounding multiplicative energy by the sumset*, Theorem 2.1, https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.1040.
