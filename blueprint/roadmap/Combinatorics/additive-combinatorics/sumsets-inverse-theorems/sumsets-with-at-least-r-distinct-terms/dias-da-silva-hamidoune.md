# Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem

Let $p$ be a prime, $A$ a nonempty subset of
$\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ with $|A| = k$, and $h$ a natural number with $1 \le h \le k$. Then the
restricted sumset $h^{\wedge}A$ of sums of $h$ distinct elements of $A$ satisfies
$|h^{\wedge}A| \ge \min(p,\ hk - h^2 + 1)$.

## Background

- [Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture (Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem)](../../combinatorial-nullstellensatz/min-le-card-restricted-sumset.md): the case $h = 2$, proved by Alon's Combinatorial Nullstellensatz argument; the same bound written in the form $\min(p, 2|A| - 2^2 + 1)$ is the [Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem for h = 2](../../combinatorial-nullstellensatz/dias-da-silva-hamidoune-two.md).
