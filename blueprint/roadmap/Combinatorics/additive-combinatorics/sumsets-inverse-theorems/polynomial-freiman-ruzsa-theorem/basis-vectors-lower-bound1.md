# Statement of the basis-vector lower bound

The proposition that every admissible
asymptotic coset-cover exponent $C$ satisfies $C \ge 1$.

The obstruction comes from sets of basis vectors.

## Sources

- *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository started by Terence Tao), constant $C_{18a}$ (Marton's conjecture constant), https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/18a.html.
