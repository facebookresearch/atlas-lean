# Polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa conjecture over $\mathbb{F}_2$

There are real polynomials $P$
and $Q$, with $P(K) > 0$ and $Q(K) > 0$ for every real $K \ge 1$, such that for every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, every nonempty finite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^n$ and every real $K \ge 1$
with $|A + A| \le K|A|$, there is a subset $A' \subseteq A$ with $|A| / P(K) \le |A'|$ and
$|\operatorname{span}(A')| \le Q(K)\,|A'|$, where $\operatorname{span}(A')$ is the
$\mathbb{F}_2$-linear span of $A'$.

It is recorded in the report of the 2012 Oberwolfach workshop on complexity theory and was proved by Gowers, Green, Manners and Tao.

## Sources

- Noga Ron-Zewi, *The Polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa Conjecture in Additive Combinatorics & Applications to Complexity*, in *Complexity Theory*, Oberwolfach Report 54/2012, pp. 3279–3280, https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2012/54.
- William Timothy Gowers, Ben Green, Freddie Manners and Terence Tao, "On a conjecture of Marton", arXiv:2311.05762, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05762.
