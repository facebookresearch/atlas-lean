# Gowers–Green–Manners–Tao exponent $7 + \sqrt{17}$

The number $7 + \sqrt{17}$ is an
admissible asymptotic coset-cover exponent.

## Sources

- William Timothy Gowers, Ben Green, Freddie Manners and Terence Tao, "On a conjecture of Marton", arXiv:2311.05762, and *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository started by Terence Tao), constant $C_{18a}$ (Marton's conjecture constant), https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/18a.html.
