# Freiman and Balog–Szemerédi–Gowers theorems

Inverse theorems describe the sets with few sums. Freiman's theorem says that a set of integers with $|A + A| \le C|A|$ is a dense subset of a generalized arithmetic progression of bounded dimension. The Balog–Szemerédi–Gowers theorem passes from the weaker hypothesis of many additive quadruples (large additive energy) to a large subset with small difference set, with polynomial bounds; Gowers proved it in this form for his proof of Szemerédi's theorem, where it finds structure in the frequencies of the derivatives of a function with large Gowers norm. The surrounding tools are additive energy, the Plünnecke–Ruzsa inequalities and Freiman homomorphisms.
