# Cover count after prepending an entry

Let $s$ be a finite indexed family in an abelian
group $G$ and let $g, x \in G$. The number of subsets of indices of $(g, s)$ that sum to $x$ is
the sum of the cover counts of $x$ and $x - g$ in $s$. These count subsets that omit and contain
the first index, respectively. No perfectness assumption is needed.

## Sources

- the subset partition in the proof of Lemma 5 of John Greene and Clayton Higgins, *Maximal Subset Sums in a Group of Order a Power of 2*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.7.8.
