# Olson's theorem for groups of rank at most two

For positive integers $h$ and $k$ with
$h \mid k$, the Davenport constant $D(G) = D_{\{1\}}(G)$ of
$G = \mathbb{Z}/h\mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z}$ is $D(G) = h + k - 1$.
