# Optimal Golomb rulers with 3 to 11 marks

The optimal Golomb ruler lengths with
$m = 3, 4, \dots, 11$ marks are, in order, $3, 6, 11, 17, 25, 34, 44, 55, 72$.

## Sources

- B. Kocuk and W.-J. van Hoeve, “A Computational Comparison of Optimization Methods for the Golomb Ruler Problem”, §2.3 and Table 1. The authors test infeasibility at one unit below the optimal length for 9–11 marks. [arXiv:1902.08660](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.08660).
- The lengths are tabulated in OEIS A003022 and in leansolving/SqueezeBench `SqueezeBench/Problems/Golomb/README.md`.
