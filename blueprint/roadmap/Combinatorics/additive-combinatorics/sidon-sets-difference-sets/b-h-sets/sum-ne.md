# Sum of a nonempty sequence

Let $G$ be a set with a binary operation $+$ (not necessarily associative, commutative or
unital). For a nonempty finite sequence $(a_1, \dots, a_k)$ of elements of $G$, its sum is
obtained by adding the entries from left to right starting from the first: it is $a_1$ when
$k = 1$, and $(\cdots((a_1 + a_2) + a_3) + \cdots) + a_k$ in general. No zero element is needed.
