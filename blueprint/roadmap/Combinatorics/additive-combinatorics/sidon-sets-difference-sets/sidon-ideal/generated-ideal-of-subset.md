# Generated ideals are closed under subsets

Let $P$ be a property of subsets of
$\mathbb{N}$. If $A$ lies in the ideal generated by $P$ and $B \subseteq A$, then $B$ also lies in
the ideal generated by $P$.

## Sources

- Andrzej Nowik, *On the Sidon Ideal*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A91, Section 1 (ideals, item 2).
