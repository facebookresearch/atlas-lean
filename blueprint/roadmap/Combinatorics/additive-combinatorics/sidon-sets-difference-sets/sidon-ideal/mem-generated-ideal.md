# Sets with a property lie in the ideal it generates

Let $P$ be a property of subsets
of $\mathbb{N}$. Every set $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ with property $P$ lies in the ideal
generated by $P$, the family of all subsets of finite unions of sets with property $P$.

## Sources

- Andrzej Nowik, *On the Sidon Ideal*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A91, Section 1 (ideals).
