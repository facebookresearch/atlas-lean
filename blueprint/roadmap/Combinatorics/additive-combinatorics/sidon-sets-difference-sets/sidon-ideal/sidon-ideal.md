# Sidon ideal

The Sidon ideal $\mathrm{Si}$ is the ideal on $\mathbb{N}$ generated by the
Sidon sets: it consists of all sets $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ contained in a finite union of
Sidon subsets of $\mathbb{N}$.

## Sources

- Andrzej Nowik, *On the Sidon Ideal*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A91, Definition 13.
