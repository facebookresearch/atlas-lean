# Mian–Chowla lower bound for an infinite Sidon set

There exist a Sidon set
$A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ and a real constant $c > 0$ such that
$|A \cap \{1, \dots, n\}| > c\, n^{1/3}$ for every natural number $n \ge 1$.

## Sources

- Andrzej Nowik, *On the Sidon Ideal*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A91, Theorem 3 (`integers-2025-A91-thm-3`), item 1, quoting A. M. Mian and S. Chowla, *On the B₂ sequences of Sidon*, Proc. Nat. Acad. Sci. India Sect. A 14 (1944).
