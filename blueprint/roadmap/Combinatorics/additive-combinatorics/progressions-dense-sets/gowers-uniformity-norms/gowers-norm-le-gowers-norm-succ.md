# Monotonicity of the Gowers norms

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For every $d \ge 0$ and $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$,

$$\|f\|_{U^d} \le \|f\|_{U^{d+1}}.$$

This is (1.2) of Green and Tao, an immediate consequence of the recursion (1.1). Gowers's Lemma 3.4 is the same statement in the language of uniformity: a function that is $\alpha$-uniform of degree $d$ is $\alpha^{1/2}$-uniform of degree $d - 1$. With the convention $\|f\|_{U^0} = |\mathbb{E} f|$ the case $d = 0$ is an equality. The proof is the induction from (1.1) in the [Cauchy–Schwarz step](sq-norm-gowers-pow-le-and-real.md).
