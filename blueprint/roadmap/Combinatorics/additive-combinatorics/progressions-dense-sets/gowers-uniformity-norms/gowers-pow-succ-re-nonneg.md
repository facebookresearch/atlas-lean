# Gowers powers are nonnegative

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For every $d \ge 0$ and $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, $\|f\|_{U^{d+1}}^{2^{d+1}}$ is a nonnegative real number (real by the [Cauchy–Schwarz step](sq-norm-gowers-pow-le-and-real.md)), so the root in Green and Tao's Definition 1.6 is the root of a nonnegative real number.

## Sources

- B. Green and T. Tao, "An inverse theorem for the Gowers $U^3(G)$ norm", Proc. Edinb. Math. Soc. 51 (2008), 73–153; numbering of arXiv:math/0503014, Definition 1.6.
