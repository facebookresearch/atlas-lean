# Multiplicative derivative

For a finite abelian group $G$, a shift $h \in G$ and $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, the multiplicative derivative of $f$ in direction $h$ is

$$\Delta_h f(x) = f(x)\,\overline{f(x + h)}.$$

For a phase $f = e(\varphi)$ it is $e(\varphi(x) - \varphi(x + h))$, a discrete derivative of the phase. This is Gowers's difference function $\Delta(f; k)(s) = f(s)\overline{f(s - k)}$ with $k = -h$, and Green and Tao's $T^h f \cdot \overline{f}$ up to complex conjugation; neither change affects the Gowers norms built from it.
