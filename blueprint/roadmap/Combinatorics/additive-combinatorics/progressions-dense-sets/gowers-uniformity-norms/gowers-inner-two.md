# Gowers $U^2$ inner product

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For four functions $f_{00}, f_{10}, f_{01}, f_{11} : G \to \mathbb{C}$ indexed by $\{0,1\}^2$,

$$\langle f_{00}, f_{10}, f_{01}, f_{11} \rangle = \mathbb{E}_{x, a, b} f_{00}(x)\,\overline{f_{10}(x + a)}\,\overline{f_{01}(x + b)}\,f_{11}(x + a + b),$$

the case $d = 2$ of the left-hand side of Gowers's Lemma 3.8, with averages in place of sums and $+$ in place of $-$ in the shifts. The general [Gowers inner product](gowers-inner.md) is the same average over $d$-dimensional cubes; this four-function form is the one with a [Fourier expansion](card-pow-four-mul-gowers-inner-two.md).
