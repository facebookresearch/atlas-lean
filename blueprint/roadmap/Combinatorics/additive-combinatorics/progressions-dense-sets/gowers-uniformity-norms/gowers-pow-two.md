# The $U^2$ power as an average over two-dimensional cubes

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For every $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$,

$$\|f\|_{U^2}^4 = \mathbb{E}_{x, a, b \in G} f(x)\,\overline{f(x + a)}\,\overline{f(x + b)}\,f(x + a + b),$$

the case $d = 2$ of Green and Tao's Definition 1.6, which follows from the recursion. For the indicator of a set $A$ this is $|G|^{-3}$ times the number of additive quadruples $(a, b, c, d) \in A^4$ with $a - b = c - d$, the quantity that Gowers relates to the Fourier coefficients of $A$ in §2 and to the Balog–Szemerédi theorem in §7.

## Background

- [Balog–Szemerédi–Gowers theorem](../../sumsets-inverse-theorems/freiman-and-balog-szemer-di-gowers-theorems/balog-szemeredi-gowers.md): the inverse theorem for sets with many additive quadruples, that is, with large additive energy.
