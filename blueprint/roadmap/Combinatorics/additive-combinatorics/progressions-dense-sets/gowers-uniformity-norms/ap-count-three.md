# Three-term progression count

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For $f_0, f_1, f_2 : G \to \mathbb{C}$,

$$\Lambda_3(f_0, f_1, f_2) = \mathbb{E}_{x, r \in G} f_0(x)\, f_1(x + r)\, f_2(x + 2r),$$

the form $\Lambda_k$ of Green and Tao for $k = 3$. For $f_0 = f_1 = f_2 = 1_A$ it is $|G|^{-2}$ times the number of progressions $(x, x + r, x + 2r)$ in $A$, including the $|A|$ trivial ones with $r = 0$. The form for every $k$ appears in the [generalized von Neumann theorem](norm-ap-count-le-gowers-norm.md).

## Sources

- B. Green and T. Tao, "An inverse theorem for the Gowers $U^3(G)$ norm", Proc. Edinb. Math. Soc. 51 (2008), 73–153; numbering of arXiv:math/0503014, §1, definition of $\Lambda_k$.
