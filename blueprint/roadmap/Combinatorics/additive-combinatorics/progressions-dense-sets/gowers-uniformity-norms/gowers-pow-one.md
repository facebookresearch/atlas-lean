# The $U^1$ power is the squared modulus of the mean

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For every $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, $\|f\|_{U^1}^2 = \mathbb{E}_{h}\mathbb{E}_x f(x)\overline{f(x + h)} = |\mathbb{E} f|^2$, the case $d = 1$ of the recursion (1.1) of Green and Tao.

## Sources

- B. Green and T. Tao, "An inverse theorem for the Gowers $U^3(G)$ norm", Proc. Edinb. Math. Soc. 51 (2008), 73–153; numbering of arXiv:math/0503014, (1.1).
