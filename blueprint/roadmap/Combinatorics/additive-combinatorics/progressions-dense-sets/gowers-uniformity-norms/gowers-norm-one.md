# The $U^1$ norm is the modulus of the mean

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and let $\mathbb{E}$ denote an average over $G$. For every $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, $\|f\|_{U^1} = |\mathbb{E} f|$. In particular, when $|G| > 1$, $U^1$ vanishes on nonzero functions of mean zero, so in general it is only a seminorm (for the one-element group it is the modulus on $\mathbb{C}$); Green and Tao call the cases $d = 0, 1$ degenerate.
