# Size of the difference set of the Chu family set

Let $k$, $l$, $t$ be positive integers
and let $A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ be the Chu family set with parameters $k$, $l$, $t$. Then
$|A - A| = 11 + 6klt + 8lt + 8t$.

## Sources

- Manish Kumar, Mohan and Ram Krishna Pandey, *A Conjecture of Chu et al. and a New Family of MSTD Sets*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A32, Theorem 6 (`integers-2024-A32-thm-6`).
