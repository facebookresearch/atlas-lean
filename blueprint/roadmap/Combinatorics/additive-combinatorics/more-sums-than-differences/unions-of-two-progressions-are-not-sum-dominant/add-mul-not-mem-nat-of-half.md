# No integer points for a half-integer start and an odd denominator

Let $s_1, s_2, p_2, q_2$
be natural numbers with $q_2$ odd, and let $x = s_1 + 1/2$ and $d = s_2 + p_2/q_2$. Then
$x + id$ is not a nonnegative integer for any natural number $i$.

## Sources

- Manish Kumar, Mohan, and Ram Krishna Pandey, *The Union of Two Arithmetic Progressions with Different Common Differences Is Not an MSTD Set*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A58, Lemma 22 (`integers-2026-A58-lem-22`).
