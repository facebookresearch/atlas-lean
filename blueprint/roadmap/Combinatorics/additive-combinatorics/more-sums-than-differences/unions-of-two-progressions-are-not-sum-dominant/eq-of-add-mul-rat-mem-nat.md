# Uniqueness of a nonnegative integer point for a rational difference

Let $x$ be a real
number, let $s, p, q$ be natural numbers with $0 < p < q$ and $\gcd(p, q) = 1$, and let
$d = s + p/q$. If $i, j \in \{1, \dots, q - 1\}$ and $x + id$ and $x + jd$ are both nonnegative
integers, then $i = j$.

## Sources

- Manish Kumar, Mohan, and Ram Krishna Pandey, *The Union of Two Arithmetic Progressions with Different Common Differences Is Not an MSTD Set*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A58, Lemma 11 (`integers-2026-A58-lem-11`).
