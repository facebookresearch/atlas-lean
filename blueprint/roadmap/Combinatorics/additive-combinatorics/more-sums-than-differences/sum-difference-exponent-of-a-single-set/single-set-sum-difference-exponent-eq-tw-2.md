# The single-set sum-difference exponent equals 2

The single-set sum-difference exponent
$C_{3d}$ is equal to $2$.

Equivalently, the exponent $2$ in the classical inequality $|A+A|/|A| \le (|A-A|/|A|)^2$ cannot be lowered. Lin and Li prove this by constructing finite sets $A_K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ with $\log(|A_K+A_K|/|A_K|) / \log(|A_K-A_K|/|A_K|) \to 2$.

## Sources

- Haowei Lin and Shanda Li, *Settling the Optimal Exponent Relating Sumsets and Difference Sets*, arXiv:2607.27199v1 (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27199v1. The abstract states that the construction and its proof were developed with the assistance of Hyra, an AI research agent based on the open-weights Hy3 model.
