# Pi z mod dual product

Let $g \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\delta_1, \dots, \delta_g$ be positive integers and let $d = \prod_{i=1}^{g} \delta_i$. Let $L$ be a finite abelian group and write $\widehat{L} = \operatorname{Hom}(L, \mathbb{Z}/d\mathbb{Z})$ for its group of homomorphisms into $\mathbb{Z}/d\mathbb{Z}$. If there is a group isomorphism

$$L \times \widehat{L} \cong \left(\prod_{i=1}^{g} \mathbb{Z}/\delta_i\mathbb{Z}\right) \times \left(\prod_{i=1}^{g} \mathbb{Z}/\delta_i\mathbb{Z}\right),$$

then $L \cong \prod_{i=1}^{g} \mathbb{Z}/\delta_i\mathbb{Z}$.
