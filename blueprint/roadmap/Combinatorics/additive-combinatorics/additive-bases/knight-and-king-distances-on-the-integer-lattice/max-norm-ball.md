# Max-norm ball

For a natural number $h$, the max-norm ball $B_h \subset \mathbb{Z}^2$ is the
product of two copies of the integer interval $\{-h, \dots, h\}$, that is, the set of lattice
points $(x, y)$ with $\max(|x|, |y|) \le h$.
