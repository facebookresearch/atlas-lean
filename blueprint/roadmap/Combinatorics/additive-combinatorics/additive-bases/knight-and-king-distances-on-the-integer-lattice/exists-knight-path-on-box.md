# Bounded reachability for generalized knights

Let $a$ and $b$ be natural numbers with
$1 \le a < b$, $\gcd(a,b)=1$, and $a+b$ odd. Every integer point $(x,y)$ with
$\max(|x|,|y|) \le a+b$ is a sum of at most $13b$ moves from the $(a,b)$-knight's move set.
A sum of zero moves is the origin.

## Sources

- Christian Táfula, *Knights Are 24/13 Times Faster than the King*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62(3) (2024), Lemma 2.1.
