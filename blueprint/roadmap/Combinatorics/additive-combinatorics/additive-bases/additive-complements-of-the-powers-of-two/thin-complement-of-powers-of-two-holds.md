# The powers of two have an additive complement of size O(N/log N)

There is a set $A$ of natural numbers and a real constant $C$ such that $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \le C N/\log N$ for all sufficiently large $N$, and every sufficiently large integer $n$ is $2^k + a$ for some $k \ge 0$ and $a \in A$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 221 (a thin additive complement of the powers of two)](thin-complement-of-powers-of-two.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Ruzsa (1972), cited under Sources.
