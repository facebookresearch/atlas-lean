# Erdős Problem 221 (a thin additive complement of the powers of two)

The statement that there is a set $A$ of natural numbers and a real constant $C$ such that $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \le C N/\log N$ for all sufficiently large $N$, and every sufficiently large integer $n$ is $2^k + a$ for some $k \ge 0$ and $a \in A$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The powers of two have an additive complement of size O(N/log N)](thin-complement-of-powers-of-two-holds.md).
