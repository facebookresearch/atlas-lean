# Quadratic recurrence sets need not be additive bases of order two

It is not the case that for every irrational $\alpha > 0$, every sufficiently large integer is the sum of two elements of the set of integers $n \ge 2$ with $\|\alpha n^2\| < 1/\log n$, where $\|y\|$ is the distance from $y$ to the nearest integer.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 1147 (quadratic recurrence sets as additive bases of order two)](quadratic-recurrence-set-is-basis-of-ord.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Konieczny (2016), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1147, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1147.
- J. Konieczny, Sets of recurrence as bases for the positive integers, Acta Arith. (2016), 309–338.
