# Erdős Problem 1147 (quadratic recurrence sets as additive bases of order two)

The statement that for every irrational $\alpha > 0$, every sufficiently large integer is the sum of two elements of the set of integers $n \ge 2$ with $\|\alpha n^2\| < 1/\log n$, where $\|y\|$ is the distance from $y$ to the nearest integer.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Quadratic recurrence sets need not be additive bases of order two](quadratic-recurrence-set-is-basis-of-ord-2.md).
