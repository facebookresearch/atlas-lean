# The excess of exact additive complements diverges

For all infinite sets $A$ and $B$ of positive integers such that every sufficiently large integer is $a + b$ with $a \in A$, $b \in B$, and $A(x)B(x)/x \to 1$, we have $A(x)B(x) - x \to \infty$ as $x \to \infty$, where for a set $A$ of natural numbers, $A(x) = |A \cap \{1, \dots, x\}|$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 785 (Erdős–Danzer problem on exact additive complements)](exact-complements-excess-diverges.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is the published work cited under Sources.
