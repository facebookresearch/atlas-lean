# Erdős Problem 880 (Burr–Erdős question on restricted sums from a basis)

The statement that for some $k \ge 3$, every set $A$ of natural numbers such that every sufficiently large integer is a sum of at most $k$ elements of $A$ (repetitions allowed) has the property that the integers which are sums of at most $k$ distinct elements of $A$ have bounded gaps: there is $C$ such that every interval $[x, x + C]$ contains one.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Restricted sums from an additive basis can have unbounded gaps](restricted-sums-bounded-gaps-high-order-2.md).
