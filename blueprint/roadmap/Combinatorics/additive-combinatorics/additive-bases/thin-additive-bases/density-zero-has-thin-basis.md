# Erdős Problem 333 (thin bases for sets of density zero)

The statement that every set $A$ of natural numbers with $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}|/N \to 0$ is contained in $B + B$ for some set $B$ of natural numbers with $|B \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| = o(N^{1/2})$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Sets of density zero need not have thin bases](density-zero-has-thin-basis-refuted.md).
