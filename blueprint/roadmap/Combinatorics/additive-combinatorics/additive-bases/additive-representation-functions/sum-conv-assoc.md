# Associativity of the sum convolution

Let $R$ be a non-unital semiring: multiplication is associative and distributes over addition, and no unit is assumed. For all $f, g, h \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$, the sum convolution $(f * g)(n) = \sum_{a + b = n} f(a)\, g(b)$ is associative:

$$(f * g) * h = f * (g * h).$$
