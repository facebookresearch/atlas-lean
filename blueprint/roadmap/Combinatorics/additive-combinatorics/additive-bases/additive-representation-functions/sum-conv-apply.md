# Range form of the sum convolution

Let $R$ be a non-unital, non-associative semiring and let $f, g \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the sum convolution $(f * g)(n) = \sum_{a + b = n} f(a)\, g(b)$ satisfies

$$(f * g)(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f(k)\, g(n - k).$$

Here $k \le n$, so $n - k$ is ordinary subtraction of natural numbers.
