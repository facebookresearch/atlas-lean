# Unit sample is a left identity for the sum convolution

Let $R$ be a non-associative semiring with unit $1$, and let $\delta \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$ be the unit sample, $\delta(0) = 1$ and $\delta(n) = 0$ for $n \ne 0$. For every $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$, the sum convolution satisfies $\delta * f = f$, where $(\delta * f)(n) = \sum_{a + b = n} \delta(a)\, f(b)$.
