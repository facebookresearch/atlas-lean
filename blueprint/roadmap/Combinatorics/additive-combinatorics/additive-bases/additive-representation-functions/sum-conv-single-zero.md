# Unit sample is a right identity for the sum convolution

Let $R$ be a non-associative semiring with unit $1$, and let $\delta \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$ be the unit sample, $\delta(0) = 1$ and $\delta(n) = 0$ for $n \ne 0$. For every $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to R$, the sum convolution satisfies $f * \delta = f$, where $(f * \delta)(n) = \sum_{a + b = n} f(a)\, \delta(b)$.
