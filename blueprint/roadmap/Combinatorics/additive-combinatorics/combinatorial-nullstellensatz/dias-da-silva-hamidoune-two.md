# Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem for h = 2

For a prime $p$ and $A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}_p$ with $|A| \ge 2$,

$$|2^{\wedge}A| \ge \min(p,\ 2|A| - 2^2 + 1).$$

This is the case $h = 2$ of the [Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem](../sumsets-inverse-theorems/sumsets-with-at-least-r-distinct-terms/dias-da-silva-hamidoune.md), written in
its form. It is the [Erdős–Heilbronn bound](min-le-card-restricted-sumset.md), since
$2|A| - 4 + 1 = 2|A| - 3$ when $|A| \ge 2$.

## Informally uses

- [Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture (Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem)](min-le-card-restricted-sumset.md): the proved bound $\min(p, 2|A| - 3)$.

## Sources

- Noga Alon, "Combinatorial Nullstellensatz", *Combin. Probab. Comput.* 8 (1999) 7–29, Theorem 4.3.
