# Combinatorial Nullstellensatz, Theorem 1.2

Let $F$ be a field, $\sigma$ a finite index set, $f \in F[x_i : i \in \sigma]$ and
$t \colon \sigma \to \mathbb{N}$. Suppose that the total degree of $f$ is $\sum_{i} t_i$ and that the
coefficient of $\prod_i x_i^{t_i}$ in $f$ is nonzero. If $S_i \subseteq F$ ($i \in \sigma$) are finite
sets with $|S_i| > t_i$, then there is a point $s$ with $s_i \in S_i$ for every $i$ and $f(s) \ne 0$.
