# Strehl Franel

Strehl's binomial identity for Franel numbers: for every `n`, the sum of
`C(n, k) ^ 2 * C(2 * k, n)` over `k = 0..n` equals the sum of `C(n, k) ^ 3`
(the Franel numbers).
