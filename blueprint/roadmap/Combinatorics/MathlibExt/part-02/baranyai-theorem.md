# Baranyai

Baranyai's theorem (statement `baranyai-s1` from
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baranyai%27s_theorem):
the complete `h`-uniform hypergraph on `n` vertices, with `h ∣ n`,
partitions into perfect matchings (1-factors).
