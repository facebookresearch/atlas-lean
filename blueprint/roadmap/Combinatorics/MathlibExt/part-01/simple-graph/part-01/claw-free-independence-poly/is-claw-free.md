# Is Claw Free

Claw-free graph: no induced `K₁,₃`, i.e. no center `c` with three
pairwise nonadjacent, mutually distinct neighbors. Unlike `MetaMathlibExt.clawFree`,
this applies to graphs on arbitrary types.
