# Thue Morse

The zero-indexed Thue–Morse sequence. Its value is the parity of the number
of `1`s in the binary expansion of its index, so evaluation takes time and
space logarithmic in the index rather than constructing an exponentially long
finite substitution block.

The source lists the initial blocks `0`, `01`, `0110`, and `01101001`, and the
limit beginning `0110100110010110`. See Eiji Miyanohara, *Transcendence of
Digital Expansions Generated by a Generalized Thue-Morse Sequence*, Journal of
Integer Sequences 18 (2015):
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL18/Miyanohara/miy5.tex.
