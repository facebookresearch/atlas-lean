# Is Sturmian

An infinite binary word `x : ℕ → Bool` is Sturmian exactly when it is
   aperiodic and balanced (concept `jis_term_4c219044135404e2d10fb16f`,
   statement `jis_a07921e69f2b6f8112afb1b1`).
