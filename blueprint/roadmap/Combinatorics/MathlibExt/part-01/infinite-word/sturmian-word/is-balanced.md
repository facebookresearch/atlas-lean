# Is Balanced

A binary word is balanced when any two factors of the same length
   have `1`-counts whose absolute difference is at most `1`
   (concept `jis_term_4c219044135404e2d10fb16f`, statement `jis_a07921e69f2b6f8112afb1b1`).
