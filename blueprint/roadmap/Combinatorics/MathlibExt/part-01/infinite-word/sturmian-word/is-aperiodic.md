# Is Aperiodic

A one-sided word is aperiodic when it is not ultimately periodic:
   no positive period `p` and preperiod `N` satisfy `x (n + p) = x n`
   for all `n ≥ N`
   (concept `jis_term_4c219044135404e2d10fb16f`, statement `jis_a07921e69f2b6f8112afb1b1`).
