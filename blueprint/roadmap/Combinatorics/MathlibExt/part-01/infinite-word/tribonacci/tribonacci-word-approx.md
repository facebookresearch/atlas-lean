# Tribonacci Word Approx

Finite Tribonacci-word approximants: `W(0) = a`, `W(1) = ab`, `W(2) = abac`,
and `W(n + 3) = W(n + 2) W(n + 1) W(n)`, where juxtaposition is concatenation.
