# Smarandache Block

The `n`-th block of the Smarandache permutation sequence
(concept `jis_sem_23e7b5e48daf56d0a358ebfc`, statement
`jis_c46e1d9809a20ee69374dac7`): the odd numbers `1, 3, ..., 2n - 1`
followed by the even numbers `2n, 2n - 2, ..., 2`.
