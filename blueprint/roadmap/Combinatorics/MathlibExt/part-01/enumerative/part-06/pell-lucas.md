# Pell Lucas Number

The Pell--Lucas sequence, initialized by `Q₀ = Q₁ = 2` and satisfying
`Qₙ₊₂ = 2 Qₙ₊₁ + Qₙ`. This is the source's specialization
`L_{1,n}(2,1)`.
