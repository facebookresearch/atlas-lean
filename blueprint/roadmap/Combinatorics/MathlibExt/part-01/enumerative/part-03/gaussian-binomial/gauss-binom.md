# Gauss Binom

Gaussian binomial coefficient `G_Q(M, k)`: `G(M, 0) = 1`, `G(0, k+1) = 0`,
and `G(M+1, k+1) = G(M, k) + Q^(k+1) * G(M, k+1)`.
