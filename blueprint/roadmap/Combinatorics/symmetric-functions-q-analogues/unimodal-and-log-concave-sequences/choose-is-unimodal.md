# Rows of Pascal's triangle are unimodal

For every $n$ the sequence $\binom{n}{0}, \dots, \binom{n}{n}$ is unimodal.

It follows from the log-concavity of the row and the criterion that a log-concave nonnegative
sequence with no internal zeros is unimodal; a row of Pascal's triangle is positive, so it has no
internal zeros.
