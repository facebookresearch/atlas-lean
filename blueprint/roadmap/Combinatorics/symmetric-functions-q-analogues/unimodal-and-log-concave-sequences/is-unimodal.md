# Unimodal sequence

A finite sequence $(a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n)$ of real numbers is unimodal if for some $0 \le j \le n$

$$a_0 \le a_1 \le \cdots \le a_j \ge a_{j+1} \ge a_{j+2} \ge \cdots \ge a_n.$$

The sequence may be given as a function $a \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ together with its last
index $n$; the values beyond $n$ play no role. A polynomial is called unimodal when its coefficient sequence
is.

## Sources

- Richard P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Chapter 1, Exercise 50.
