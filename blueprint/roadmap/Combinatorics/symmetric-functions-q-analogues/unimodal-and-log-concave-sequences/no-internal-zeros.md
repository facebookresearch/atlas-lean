# Sequence with no internal zeros

A finite sequence $(a_0, \dots, a_n)$ has no internal zeros if there do not exist $i < j < k \le n$
with $a_i \ne 0$, $a_j = 0$ and $a_k \ne 0$; that is, its nonzero terms are consecutive.

## Sources

- Richard P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Chapter 1, Exercise 50.
