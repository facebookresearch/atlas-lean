# Products of log-concave polynomials

If $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ have nonnegative coefficients whose sequences are log-concave with no internal
zeros, then so does $P(x)Q(x)$.

## Sources

- Richard P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Chapter 1, Exercise 50(d).
