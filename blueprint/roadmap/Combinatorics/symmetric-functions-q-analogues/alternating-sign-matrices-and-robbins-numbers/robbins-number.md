# Robbins numbers

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the Robbins number $A_n$ is the natural number
obtained by dividing $\prod_{k=0}^{n-1} (3k+1)!$ by $\prod_{k=0}^{n-1} (n+k)!$, where the division
is division of natural numbers rounded down.
