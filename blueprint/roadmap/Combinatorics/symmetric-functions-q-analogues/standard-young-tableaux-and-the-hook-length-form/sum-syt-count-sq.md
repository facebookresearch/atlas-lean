# Sum of the squares of the numbers of standard Young tableaux

For every $n$,

$$\sum_{\lambda \vdash n} \left(f^\lambda\right)^2 = n!.$$

Representation-theoretically, $f^\lambda$ is the dimension of the irreducible representation of
$\mathfrak S_n$ indexed by $\lambda$, and the identity is the decomposition of the regular
representation. Combinatorially, the Robinson–Schensted–Knuth algorithm is a bijection between
$\mathfrak S_n$ and the pairs $(P, Q)$ of standard Young tableaux of the same shape
$\lambda \vdash n$ (Theorem 8.13), which proves it bijectively. Stanley's proof of Corollary 8.5
counts walks in Young's lattice instead.
