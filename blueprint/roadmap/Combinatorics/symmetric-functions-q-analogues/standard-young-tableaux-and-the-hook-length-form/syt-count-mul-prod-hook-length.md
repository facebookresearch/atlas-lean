# Hook length formula

For a partition $\lambda \vdash n$,

$$f^\lambda = \frac{n!}{\prod_{u \in \lambda} h(u)},$$

where $u$ runs over the cells of the diagram of $\lambda$ and $h(u)$ is the hook length.
Equivalently, with the denominator cleared, $f^\lambda \prod_u h(u) = n!$. For $\lambda = (4, 2, 2)$ it gives
$f^\lambda = 8!/(6 \cdot 5 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1) = 56$. The formula is due to
Frame, Robinson and Thrall (1954).
