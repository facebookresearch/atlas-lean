# Tableaux are determined by their entries

Two standard Young tableaux of the same skew shape with the same entries are equal: a tableau is
its filling, a function of the cells that vanishes off the skew diagram, and the row, column and
bijectivity conditions are properties of that filling. Hence a skew shape with $n$ cells has
finitely many standard Young tableaux, since each one is determined by a function on its $n$
cells with values in $\{1, \dots, n\}$.
