# Straight shape as a skew shape

A Young diagram $\lambda$ is viewed as the skew shape $\lambda / \varnothing$, so that the standard
Young tableaux of skew shape $\lambda/\varnothing$ are the standard Young tableaux of shape
$\lambda$.
