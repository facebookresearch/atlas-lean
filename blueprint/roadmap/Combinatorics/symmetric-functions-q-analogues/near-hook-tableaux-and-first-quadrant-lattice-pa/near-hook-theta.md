# The near-hook diagram $\theta^{(n)}$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\theta^{(n)}$ is the Young
diagram of the near-hook partition $(n + 2, 2, 1^n)$ of $2n + 4$: its rows have lengths $n + 2$
and $2$, followed by $n$ rows of length $1$.

## Sources
