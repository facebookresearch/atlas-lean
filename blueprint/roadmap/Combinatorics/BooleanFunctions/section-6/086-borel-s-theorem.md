# Theorem 6.3 (Borel's theorem)

Let $\rho \in [0,1]$, and $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to [-1,1]$ with $\mathbb{E}[f] = 0$. Then $\operatorname{Stab}_{\rho}(f) \leqslant 1 - \frac{2}{\pi}\operatorname{Arccos}(\rho)$.
