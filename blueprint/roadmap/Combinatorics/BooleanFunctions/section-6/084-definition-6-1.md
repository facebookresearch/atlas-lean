# Definition 6.1

For $\rho \in [0,1]$, the operator $T_{\rho}$ acting on functions $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ is defined as $U_{\rho}(z) = \mathbb{E}_{w \sim N(0, I_n)} [f(\rho z + \sqrt{1 - \rho^2} w)]$.
