# Definition 6.2

Given $\rho \in [0,1]$ and $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, the noise stability of $f$ with parameter $\rho$ is $\operatorname{Stab}_{\rho}(f) = \langle f, U_{\rho} f \rangle$.
