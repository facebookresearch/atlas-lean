# Theorem 5.2

Suppose $f(x) = \sum_{0 < |S| \leqslant d} a_S \chi_S$ is a multi-linear polynomial such that $\sum_S a_S^2 \leqslant 1$, and $I \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is an interval of length at most $\varepsilon$. Then $\Pr_{z \sim N(0,1)}[|f(z)| \leqslant \varepsilon] \leqslant O(d \varepsilon^{1/d})$.
