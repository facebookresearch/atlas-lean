# Theorem 2.5

For all $\rho \in (0,1)$, $\delta > 0$ there exist $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\tau > 0$ such that if $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to [-1,1]$ is a function for which $\max_i I_i^{\leqslant d}[f] \leqslant \tau$, then $\operatorname{Stab}_{-\rho}(f) \geqslant \frac{2}{\pi}\operatorname{Arccos}(\rho) - 1 - \delta$.
