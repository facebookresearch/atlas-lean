# Theorem 2.1 (Lecture 16, Section 2)

Assuming the Unique-Games Conjecture, for all $\rho \in (0,1)$ and $\varepsilon > 0$, given a graph $G = (V, E)$ it is NP-hard to distinguish between the following two cases: (1) YES case: $G$ has a cut of fractional size at least $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\rho - \varepsilon$; (2) NO case: all cuts in $G$ have fractional size at most $1 - \frac{1}{\pi} \operatorname{Arccos}(\rho) + \varepsilon$.
