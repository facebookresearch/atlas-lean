# Claim 1.3

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $\rho \in [0,1]$. Then $\operatorname{Stab}_{\rho}(f) = \sum_{S} \rho^{|S|} \widehat{f}(S)^2$.
