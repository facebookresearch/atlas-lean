# Definition 1.1 (Noise Stability)

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, and $\rho \in [0,1]$. The stability of $f$ with parameter $\rho$ is defined as $\operatorname{Stab}_{\rho}(f) = \langle f, T_{\rho} f \rangle$.
