# Fact 1.1

Suppose $Y_1, \ldots, Y_n$ are independent random variables such that $|Y_i| \leqslant 1$ almost surely. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$, $\Pr\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{E}\left[Y_i\right] \geqslant \varepsilon n\right] \leqslant 2e^{-\frac{\varepsilon^2}{2+\varepsilon}n}$.
