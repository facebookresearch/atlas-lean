# Theorem 3.4

For all $f \colon \{-1, 1\}^n \to \{-1, 1\}$, $\mathbb{E}_{x}\left[\sqrt{s_{f}(x)}\right] \geqslant \Omega\left(\operatorname{var}(f)\sqrt{\log\left(\frac{1}{\operatorname{var}(f)}\right)}\right)$.
