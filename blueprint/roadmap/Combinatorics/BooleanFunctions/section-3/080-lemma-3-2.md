# Lemma 3.2

Suppose $f \colon \{-1,1\}^t \times \mathbb{R}^{n-t} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a function of degree at most $d$, and $q \geqslant 2$. Then $\|f\|_q \leqslant \sqrt{q-1}^d \|f\|_2$.
