# Lemma 3.1 (Hypercontractivity for Gaussian space)

Suppose $f \colon (\mathbb{R}^n, \mu^{\otimes n}) \to \mathbb{R}$ is a function of degree at most $d$, and $q \geqslant 2$. Then $\|f\|_q \leqslant \sqrt{q-1}^d \|f\|_2$.
