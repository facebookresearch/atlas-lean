# Theorem 4.4

There exists $\alpha > 0$ such that for all monotone $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$, $(1-\varepsilon)M(f) \leqslant \left|\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{var}(f) - \operatorname{NS}_{\varepsilon}(f)\right| \leqslant M(f)^{\alpha\varepsilon}$. Thus, $f$ is $(\varepsilon, o(1))$ noise sensitive if and only if $M(f) = o(1)$.
