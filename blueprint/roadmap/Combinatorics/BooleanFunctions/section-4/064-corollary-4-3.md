# Corollary 4.3

There exists an absolute constant $\alpha > 0$ such that for all $\varepsilon > 0$ and $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$, $\left|\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{var}(f) - \operatorname{NS}_{\varepsilon}(f)\right| \leqslant M(f)^{\alpha\varepsilon}$.
