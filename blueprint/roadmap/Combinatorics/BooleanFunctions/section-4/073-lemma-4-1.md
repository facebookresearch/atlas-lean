# Lemma 4.1

For all $\zeta, \alpha > 0$, there exists $r \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varepsilon > 0$ such that the following holds. Suppose $\zeta < p < 1/2$ and $f \colon \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}$ is monotone with $\mu_p(f) \geqslant \alpha$. If $f$ is $(r, \varepsilon)$ quasi-random, then $\mu_{p+\zeta/2}(f) \geqslant 0.9$.
