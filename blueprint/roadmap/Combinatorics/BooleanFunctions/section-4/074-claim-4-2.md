# Claim 4.2

Suppose $\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H} \subseteq \mathcal{P}([n])$ are such that $\mu_{1/2}(\mathcal{G}) + \mu_{1/2}(\mathcal{H}) > 1$. Then there are disjoint $F \in \mathcal{F}$, $G \in \mathcal{G}$.
