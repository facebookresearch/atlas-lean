# Definition 1.1

For a function $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}$ and a coordinate $i \in [n]$, the influence of $i$ is defined as $I_i[f] = \Pr_{x \in \{-1,1\}^n} [f(x) \neq f(x^{\oplus i})]$. Here, $x^{\oplus i}$ is the vector $x$ in which the $i$th coordinate has been flipped.
