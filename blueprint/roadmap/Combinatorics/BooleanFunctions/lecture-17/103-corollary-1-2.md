# Corollary 1.2 (Lecture 17, Section 1.1)

Assuming UGC, for all $\varepsilon > 0$, given a graph $G = (V, E)$ it is NP-hard to distinguish between the cases: 1. YES case: $\operatorname{VC}(G) \leqslant \left(\frac{1}{2} + \varepsilon\right) n$. 2. NO case: $\operatorname{VC}(G) \geqslant (1 - \varepsilon)n$.
