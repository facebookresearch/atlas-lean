# Definition 3.2 (Edge expansion)

Let $G = (V, E, w)$ be a weighted regular graph, and let $S \subseteq V$ be a vertex set. The expansion of $S$ is defined as $$\Phi_G(S) = \Pr_{x \in S, y \sim_w N(x)} [y \notin S].$$
