# Definition 2.2 (Lecture 5, Section 2.2)

Let $x \in \{-1,1\}^n$, and let $\rho \in [0,1]$. The distribution of $\rho$-correlated inputs with $x$, denoted as $y \sim T_{\rho} x$, is defined as: for each $i \in [n]$ independently, set $y_i = x_i$ with probability $\rho$, and otherwise resample $y_i$ according to the uniform distribution over $\{-1,1\}$.
