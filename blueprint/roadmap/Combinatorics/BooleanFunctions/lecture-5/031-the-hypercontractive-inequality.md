# Theorem 2.3 (The hypercontractive inequality)

For all $f \colon \{-1,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$, $1 \leqslant p \leqslant q$ and $0 \leqslant \rho \leqslant \sqrt{\frac{p-1}{q-1}}$ it holds that $\|T_{\rho} f\|_q \leqslant \|f\|_p$.
