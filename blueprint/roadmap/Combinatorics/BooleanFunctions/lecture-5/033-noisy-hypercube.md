# Definition 3.1 (Noisy hypercube)

For $\rho \in [0,1]$, the $\rho$-noisy hypercube graph is the graph on the vertex set $\{-1,1\}^n$, whose edges are sampled according to the $T_\rho$ process. Namely, the distribution over the neighbours of a given vertex $x \in \{-1,1\}^n$ is given by $T_\rho x$.
