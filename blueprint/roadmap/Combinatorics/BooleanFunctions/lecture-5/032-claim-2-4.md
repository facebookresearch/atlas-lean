# Claim 2.4 (Lecture 5, Section 2.2)

For all $f \colon \{-1,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\rho \in [0,1]$ we have that $$T_{\rho} f(x) = \sum_{S \subseteq [n]} \rho^{|S|} \widehat{f}(S) \chi_S(x).$$
