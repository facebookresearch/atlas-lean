# Theorem 3.6

Suppose $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ is a function of degree at most $d$, and let $t \geqslant 2^d$. Then $$\Pr_{x}[|f(x)| \geqslant t\|f\|_2] \leqslant e^{-\frac{t^{2/d}}{2}}.$$
