# Theorem 3.8

Suppose $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ is a function of degree at most $d$, and let $0 < \theta < 1$. Then $$\Pr_{x} [|f(x)| \geqslant \theta \|f\|_2] \geqslant \frac{(1 - \theta^2)^2}{9^d}.$$
