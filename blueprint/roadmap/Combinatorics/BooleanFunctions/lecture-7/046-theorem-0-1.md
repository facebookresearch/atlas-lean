# Theorem 0.1

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ be a function such that $I[f] \leqslant K \cdot \operatorname{var}(f)$. Then there is an $i \in [n]$ such that $I_i[f] \geqslant e^{-O(K)}$.
