# Theorem 1.1 (Talagrand)

There exists an absolute constant $C > 0$, such that for any $f \colon \{-1, 1\}^n \to \{-1, 1\}$ it holds that $$C\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{I_i[f]}{\log(1/I_i[f])} \geqslant \operatorname{var}(f).$$
