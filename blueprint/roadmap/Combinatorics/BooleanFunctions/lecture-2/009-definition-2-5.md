# Definition 2.5

Given a function $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and a parameter $p \in [0,1]$, a $p$-random restriction is sampled by: taking $J \subseteq [n]$ randomly by including each $i \in [n]$ in $J$ with probability $p$, and then taking $z \in \{-1,1\}^{\overline{J}}$.
