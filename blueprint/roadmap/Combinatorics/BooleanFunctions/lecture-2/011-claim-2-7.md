# Claim 2.7

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $d \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $(J,z)$ be a $p$-random restriction. Then $\mathbb{E}_{J,z}\left[W^{=d}[f_{\bar{J}\to z}]\right] = \sum_{Q} \widehat{f}(Q)^{2} \Pr\left[\operatorname{Bin}(|Q|,p) = d\right]$.
