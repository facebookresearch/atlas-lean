# Definition 2.6

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be a function, and $d \in \mathbb{N}$. The level $d$ Fourier weight of a function $f$ is defined as $W^{=d}[f] = \sum_{|S|=d} \widehat{f}(S)^2$. We also define $W^{\leqslant d}[f] = \sum_{i \leqslant d} W^{=i}[f]$ and $W^{\geqslant d}[f] = \sum_{i \geqslant d} W^{=i}[f]$.
