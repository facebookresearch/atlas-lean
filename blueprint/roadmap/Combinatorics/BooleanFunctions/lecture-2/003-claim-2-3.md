# Claim 2.3

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $J \subseteq [n]$, $z \in \{-1,1\}^{\overline{J}}$ and $S \subseteq J$. We have $\widehat{f_{\bar{J}\to z}}(S) = \sum_{T \subseteq \bar{J}} \widehat{f}(S \cup T) \chi_T(z)$.
