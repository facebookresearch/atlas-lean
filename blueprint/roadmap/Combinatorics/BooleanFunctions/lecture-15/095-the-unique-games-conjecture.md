# Conjecture 3.6 (The Unique-Games Conjecture)

For all $\varepsilon, \delta > 0$, there exists $k \in \mathbb{N}$ such that given a Unique-Games instance $\Psi$, it is NP-hard to distinguish between: (1) YES case: $\operatorname{val}(\Psi) \geqslant 1 - \varepsilon$; (2) NO case: $\operatorname{val}(\Psi) \leqslant \delta$. In other words, $\operatorname{gap-UniqueGames}_k[1 - \varepsilon, \delta]$ is NP-hard.
