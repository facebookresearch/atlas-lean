# Lemma 2.2

Let $f \colon \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,0,1\}$ be a function such that $0 < \Pr_x [f(x) \neq 0] \leqslant \delta$. Then $$\sum_{|S| \leqslant \frac{1}{20} \log(1/\delta)} \widehat{f}(S)^2 \leqslant \delta^{24/20}.$$
