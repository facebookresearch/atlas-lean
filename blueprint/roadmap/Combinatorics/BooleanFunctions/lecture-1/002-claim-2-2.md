# Claim 2.2 (Lecture 1, Section 2.1)

The following holds for any $f, g \colon \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$: 1. Plancherel's equality: $\langle f, g \rangle = \sum_{S \subseteq [n]} \widehat{f}(S) \widehat{g}(S)$. 2. Parseval's equality: $\|f\|_2^2 = \sum_{S \subseteq [n]} \widehat{f}(S)^2$.
