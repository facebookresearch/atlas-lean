# Sparse neural codes

A neural code is $k$-sparse when each of its codewords has at most $k$ neurons.

For a natural number $k$, a [neural code](neural-codes-and-their-realizations.md) $\mathcal{C}$ is *$k$-sparse* if $|\sigma| \le k$ for every $\sigma \in \mathcal{C}$; equivalently, its simplicial complex (the smallest simplicial complex containing $\mathcal{C}$) has dimension at most $k - 1$.
