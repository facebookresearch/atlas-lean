# Dimension of a polytope

The dimension of $P = \operatorname{conv} s$ is the dimension of its affine hull, that is, of the
linear subspace parallel to $\operatorname{aff} P = \operatorname{aff} s$. A $d$-polytope is a
polytope of dimension $d$.

Degenerate case: the empty polytope ($s = \emptyset$) gets dimension $0$ here, not the usual $-1$, because the direction of the empty affine subspace is the zero subspace.
