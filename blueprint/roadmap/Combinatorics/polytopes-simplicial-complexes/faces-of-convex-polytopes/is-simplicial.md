# Simplicial polytope

The polytope $P = \operatorname{conv} s$ of dimension $d$ is simplicial if every facet of $P$ (a
nonempty face of dimension $d - 1$) contains exactly $d$ vertices of $P$, that is, is a
$(d-1)$-simplex. Cyclic polytopes, and the convex hull of finitely many points in general
position, are simplicial; their boundary complexes are simplicial spheres.

Degenerate case: a polytope of dimension $0$ (a point, or the empty polytope, whose dimension is also taken to be $0$) has no facets in this sense and is simplicial vacuously.
