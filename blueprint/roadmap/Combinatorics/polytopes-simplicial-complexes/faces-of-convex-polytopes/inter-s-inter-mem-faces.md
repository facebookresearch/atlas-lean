# Intersections of faces are faces

Let $s$ be finite, $P = \operatorname{conv} s$, and let $A$ be any family of faces of $P$. Then
$P \cap \bigcap_{F \in A} F$ is a face of $P$; for the empty family it is $P$ itself. Since there
are only finitely many faces, this reduces to finite intersections, where the sum of the exposing
functionals exposes the intersection.
