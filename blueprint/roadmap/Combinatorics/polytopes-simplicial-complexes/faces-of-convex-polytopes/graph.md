# The graph of a polytope

The graph of $P = \operatorname{conv} s$ has the vertices (extreme points) of $P$ as its vertices,
and two distinct vertices $u, v$ are adjacent when the segment $[u, v]$ is a face of $P$, that is,
an edge. Every one-dimensional face of a polytope is such a segment between two vertices, so
this is the 1-skeleton of $P$. By Steinitz's theorem, the graphs of 3-polytopes are exactly the
3-connected planar graphs.
