# Faces cut out by a functional

Let $s$ be a finite set, $T \subseteq s$, and $\ell$ a continuous linear functional with
$\ell(y) \le V$ for all $y \in s$, $\ell(y) = V$ for all $y \in T$, and $\ell(y) < V$ for
$y \in s \setminus T$. Then $\operatorname{conv} T$ is a face of $\operatorname{conv} s$. (For
$T = \emptyset$ the face is empty.) This is how faces of a polytope are produced from its vertex
set alone: the face cut out by $\ell$ is the convex hull of the vertices where $\ell$ is maximal.
