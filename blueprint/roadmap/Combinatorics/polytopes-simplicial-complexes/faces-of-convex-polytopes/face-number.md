# Face numbers of a polytope

For a finite set $s$ and $k \ge 0$, the face number $f_k(P)$ of $P = \operatorname{conv} s$ is the
number of nonempty faces of $P$ whose affine hull has dimension $k$. Thus $f_0$ counts the
vertices, $f_1$ the edges and, for a $d$-polytope, $f_{d-1}$ the facets, while $f_d = 1$ counts $P$
itself. The vector $(f_0, f_1, \dots, f_{d-1})$ is the f-vector of $P$.

The empty face, of dimension $-1$ with the convention $f_{-1} = 1$, is not counted by this
function. The count is finite because a polytope has finitely many faces.
