# Faces of convex polytopes

A convex polytope is the convex hull $P = \operatorname{conv} s$ of a finite set $s$ of points. Its faces are the empty set and the sets of points of $P$ at which a linear functional attains its maximum over $P$; they include $P$ itself. Ordered by inclusion they form the face lattice of $P$, a finite lattice whose isomorphism class is the combinatorial type of $P$. Every face is the convex hull of the points of $s$ it contains, so there are finitely many. The face numbers $f_k$ count the faces of each dimension, and the vertices and edges form the graph of the polytope.
