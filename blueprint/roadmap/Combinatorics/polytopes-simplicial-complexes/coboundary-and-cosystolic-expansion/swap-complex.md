# The swap complex (faces complex) of a high-dimensional expander

Let $X$ be a $d$-dimensional complex and $k \ge 1$ with $3k - 1 \le d$. The **swap complex** (or faces complex) $F^{k}X$ is the $2$-dimensional complex whose vertices are the $(k-1)$-faces of $X$, whose edges are pairs $\{s, t\}$ of disjoint $(k-1)$-faces with $s \sqcup t \in X(2k-1)$, and whose triangles are triples $\{s, t, u\}$ of pairwise disjoint faces with $s \sqcup t \sqcup u \in X(3k-1)$, weighted by the induced face distributions of $X$. **Swap coboundary (cocycle) expansion** of $X$ means coboundary (cocycle) expansion of $F^{k}X$ in dimension $1$ with constants independent of $k$. Agreement tests that compare local functions on disjoint or intersecting faces of $X$ are naturally tests on the swap complex.
