# Cosystolic expansion and cosystolic distance

Let $X$ be a pure weighted complex and $0 \le i < d$. $X$ is an **$(\varepsilon, \mu)$-cosystolic expander** in dimension $i$ if

$$
\|\delta_i f\| \ge \varepsilon \cdot \operatorname{dist}(f, Z^i) \quad \text{for all } f \in C^i, \qquad \text{and} \qquad \|z\| \ge \mu \ \text{for all } z \in Z^i \setminus B^i.
$$

The second condition (large **cosystoles**) allows nontrivial cohomology but asks that every nontrivial cohomology class have only heavy representatives. Coboundary expansion is the special case $Z^i = B^i$.
