# Evra–Kaufman: bounded-degree cosystolic expanders of every dimension

For every $d \ge 2$ there exist $\varepsilon_d, \mu_d > 0$ and $q_0(d)$ such that for every prime power $q \ge q_0(d)$, the $d$-skeletons of $(d+1)$-dimensional Ramanujan complexes of type $\tilde A_{d+1}$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ are $(\varepsilon_d, \mu_d)$-cosystolic expanders over $\mathbb{F}_2$ in every dimension $0 \le i < d$. Consequently, bounded-degree $d$-dimensional topological expanders exist for every $d$.

## Proof sketch

One proves a local-to-global criterion: a complex whose proper links are $\beta$-coboundary expanders and which is a $\lambda$-local spectral expander with $\lambda$ small relative to $\beta$ and $d$ is a cosystolic expander. The proof shows that a locally minimal cochain with small coboundary is spread out, and uses the high-order random-walk mixing implied by local spectral expansion to show that such a cochain has small norm unless it is a cosystole. Spherical buildings supply the coboundary-expanding links.
