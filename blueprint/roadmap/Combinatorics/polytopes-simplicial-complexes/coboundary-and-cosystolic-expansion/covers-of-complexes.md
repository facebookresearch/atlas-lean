# Connected covers of a simplicial complex

Let $X$ be a connected simplicial complex. An **$m$-sheeted cover** of $X$ is a simplicial complex $Y$ with a surjective simplicial map $\rho : Y \to X$ such that for every face $\sigma \in X$, the preimage $\rho^{-1}(\sigma)$ is a disjoint union of $m$ faces each mapped bijectively onto $\sigma$. Up to isomorphism, $m$-sheeted covers of $X$ correspond bijectively to $S_m$-valued $1$-cocycles on $X$ modulo coboundaries, via the permutation of sheets along each edge. A cover is **connected** if $Y$ is connected; $X$ has a connected cover with $m \ge 2$ sheets if and only if its fundamental group has a proper subgroup of index $m$.
