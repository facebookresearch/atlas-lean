# Ehrhart numerator of the six-dimensional counterexample simplex

The full-dimensional
[weighted simplex](../weighted-projective-space-simplices/weighted-simplex.md) with weights
$(1,1,1,1,1,3)$ has Ehrhart numerator
$1 + X + 2X^2 + X^3 + 2X^4 + X^5 + X^6$.

## Sources

- S. Payne, *Ehrhart series and lattice triangulations*, Discrete Comput. Geom. 40 (2008), 365–376, Example 4.2 with $b = 3$, $k = 2$, $r = 0$ (the simplex and its numerator); Benjamin Braun, Robert Davis, and Liam Solus, *Detecting the integer decomposition property and Ehrhart unimodality in reflexive simplices*, Adv. in Appl. Math. 100 (2018), 122–142, Theorem 2.2 (the general formula and its fundamental-parallelepiped proof); restated by Benjamin Braun, Robert Davis, Derek Hanely, Morgan Lane, and Liam Solus, *The Integer Decomposition Property and Weighted Projective Space Simplices*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A60, Theorem 5.
