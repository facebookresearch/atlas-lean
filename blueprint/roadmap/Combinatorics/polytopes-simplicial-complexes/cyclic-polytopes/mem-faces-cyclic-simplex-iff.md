# The faces of a cyclic simplex

For $d + 1$ distinct parameters, the faces of the simplex $C_d(d+1)$ are exactly the sets
$\operatorname{conv}\{\gamma(t_i) : i \in T\}$ for $T \subseteq \{0, \dots, d\}$, including the empty
face ($T = \emptyset$) and the simplex itself ($T$ = all indices). Distinct $T$ give distinct faces,
so the face lattice of a $d$-simplex is the Boolean lattice of subsets of its $d + 1$ vertices.
