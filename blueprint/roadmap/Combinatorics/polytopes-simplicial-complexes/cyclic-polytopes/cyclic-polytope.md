# Cyclic polytope

For parameters $t_1, \dots, t_n \in \mathbb{R}$, the cyclic polytope is

$$C_d(n) = \operatorname{conv}\{\gamma(t_1), \dots, \gamma(t_n)\} \subset \mathbb{R}^d.$$

For distinct parameters and $n > d$ it is a simplicial $d$-polytope; its combinatorial type does
not depend on the parameters, as Gale's evenness criterion shows. The definition makes sense for arbitrary parameters, not necessarily distinct.
