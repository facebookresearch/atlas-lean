# The moment curve

For $d \ge 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the moment curve in $\mathbb{R}^d$ is

$$\gamma(t) = (t, t^2, \dots, t^d).$$

A curve in $\mathbb{R}^d$ has order (degree) $d$ when no $d + 1$ of its points lie on a common
affine hyperplane; the moment curve is the standard example, and cyclic polytopes are convex hulls
of finitely many of its points.
