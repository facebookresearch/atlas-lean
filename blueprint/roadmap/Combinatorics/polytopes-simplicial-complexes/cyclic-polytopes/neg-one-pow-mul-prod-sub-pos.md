# Sign of a product of linear factors

Let $t_i$, $i \in S$, be real numbers and let $\tau$ differ from all of them. Then

$$(-1)^{m} \prod_{i \in S} (\tau - t_i) > 0, \qquad m = |\{i \in S : t_i > \tau\}|,$$

that is, the product has the sign $(-1)^m$: exactly the $m$ factors with $t_i > \tau$ are negative.
