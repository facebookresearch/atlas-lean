# The basis polynomials $b^d_j$

For natural numbers $d$ and $j$, $b^d_j$ is the complex
polynomial $b^d_j = \prod_{i=0}^{d-1} (X + d - j - i)$.



## Sources

- Max Kölbl, *Properties of Ehrhart Polynomials Whose Roots Lie on the Canonical Line*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A68, Section 2 (the Pochhammer symbols $(z + d - j)_d$ in the definition of $p^d_j$); $b^d_j = (X + d - j)_d$ is $d!$ times the binomial polynomial $\binom{X + d - j}{d}$ of Equation (1).
