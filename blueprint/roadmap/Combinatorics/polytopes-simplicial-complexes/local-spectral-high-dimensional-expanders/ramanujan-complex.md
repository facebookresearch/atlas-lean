# Ramanujan complexes of type A_d

Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field with residue field $\mathbb{F}_q$ and $\mathcal{B}_d$ the Bruhat–Tits building of $\mathrm{PGL}_{d}(F)$, a contractible infinite $(d-1)$-dimensional simplicial complex in which every link of a vertex is the spherical building of $\mathrm{PGL}_d(\mathbb{F}_q)$. A finite quotient $\Gamma \backslash \mathcal{B}_d$ by a cocompact torsion-free lattice $\Gamma$ is a **Ramanujan complex** if every nontrivial eigenvalue of each of the $d-1$ Hecke (colour) adjacency operators $A_1, \dots, A_{d-1}$ lies in the spectrum of the corresponding operator on $L^2(\mathcal{B}_d)$. For $d = 2$ this specializes to $(q+1)$-regular Ramanujan graphs.
