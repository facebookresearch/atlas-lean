# Ramanujan complexes of type Ã_{d-1}

Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field with residue field $\mathbb{F}_q$ and $\mathcal{B}_d$ the Bruhat–Tits building of $\mathrm{PGL}_{d}(F)$, a contractible infinite $(d-1)$-dimensional simplicial complex in which every link of a vertex is the spherical building of $\mathrm{PGL}_d(\mathbb{F}_q)$. The building is of type $\tilde A_{d-1}$; its vertices carry colours in $\mathbb{Z}/d$, and for $1 \le k \le d-1$ the Hecke (colour) adjacency operator $A_k$ sums over the neighbours of a vertex whose colour exceeds its own by $k$. These operators commute; let $S_d \subset \mathbb{C}^{d-1}$ be the joint spectrum of $(A_1, \dots, A_{d-1})$ on $L^2$ of the vertices of $\mathcal{B}_d$, which is contained in the product $S_{d,1} \times \dots \times S_{d,d-1}$ of the individual spectra (and, for $d \ge 3$, not equal to it). A finite quotient $\Gamma \backslash \mathcal{B}_d$ by a cocompact torsion-free lattice $\Gamma$ is a **Ramanujan complex** if for every nontrivial simultaneous eigenvector $v$ of the induced operators on $L^2$ of its vertices, with $A_k v = \lambda_k v$, the tuple $(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_{d-1})$ lies in $S_d$. The weaker requirement that each $\lambda_k$ lie in $S_{d,k}$ (pseudo-Ramanujan, Lubotzky–Samuels–Vishne Definition 2.6) follows from this condition and is equivalent to it for $d = 2, 3$ (Remark 2.9). For $d = 2$ this specializes to $(q+1)$-regular Ramanujan graphs.
