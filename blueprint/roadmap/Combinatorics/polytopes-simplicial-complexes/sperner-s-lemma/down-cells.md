# Down cells of the grid triangulation

The down cells are the index pairs $(i, j)$ of natural numbers with $i + j + 2 \le n$; the pair
$(i, j)$ stands for the unit triangle with vertices $(i + 1, j)$, $(i, j + 1)$ and
$(i + 1, j + 1)$. Together with the up cells they triangulate the triangle with corners $(0, 0)$,
$(n, 0)$ and $(0, n)$ into $n^2$ unit triangles.
