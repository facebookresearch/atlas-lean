# Sperner's lemma in dimension one

Colour the points $0, 1, \dots, n$ with two colours $0$ and $1$ so that $c(0) = 0$ and
$c(n) = 1$. Then the number of indices $i < n$ with $c(i) \ne c(i + 1)$ (bichromatic unit
segments) is odd; in particular there is at least one.

## Sources

- E. Sperner, "Neuer Beweis für die Invarianz der Dimensionszahl und des Gebietes", Abh. Math. Sem. Univ. Hamburg 6 (1928), 265–272 (the one-dimensional case of his lemma).
