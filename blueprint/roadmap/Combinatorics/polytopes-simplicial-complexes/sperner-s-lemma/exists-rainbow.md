# Sperner's lemma in dimension two

Every Sperner colouring of the grid triangle with corners $(0, 0)$, $(n, 0)$ and $(0, n)$ has a
rainbow cell: an up cell $\{(i, j), (i + 1, j), (i, j + 1)\}$ or a down cell
$\{(i + 1, j), (i, j + 1), (i + 1, j + 1)\}$ whose three vertices carry the three colours. This
follows from the parity form of the lemma, since an odd number is not zero (Sperner 1928,
two-dimensional case, for the grid triangulation).
