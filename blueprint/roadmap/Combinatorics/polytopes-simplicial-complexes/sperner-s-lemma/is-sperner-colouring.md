# Sperner colouring of the grid triangle

A colouring $c$ that assigns a colour $c(i, j) \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ to each grid point $(i, j)$ is a
Sperner colouring of the triangle with corners $(0, 0)$, $(n, 0)$ and $(0, n)$ when the points
$(i, 0)$, $i \le n$, of the bottom side avoid colour $2$, the points $(0, j)$, $j \le n$, of the
left side avoid colour $1$, and the points $(i, n - i)$, $i \le n$, of the hypotenuse avoid colour
$0$. The corners then have colours $0$, $1$ and $2$ (for $n \ge 1$; for $n = 0$ no colouring
qualifies).
