# Gale diagrams

For $n$ points spanning a $d$-dimensional affine space, the affine dependences form a vector space of dimension $n - d - 1$. Writing a basis of it as the columns of an $n \times (n - d - 1)$ matrix, the $n$ rows are the Gale transform of the configuration. Gale diagrams turn questions about polytopes with few vertices, and about projections of polytopes, into questions about low-dimensional vector configurations.
