# The affine dependence map of a point configuration

For points $v_1, \dots, v_n$ in a real vector space $E$, the linear map

$$\mathbb{R}^n \to E \times \mathbb{R}, \qquad \lambda \mapsto \Bigl(\sum_i \lambda_i v_i,\ \sum_i \lambda_i\Bigr).$$

Its kernel is the space of affine dependences of the configuration.
