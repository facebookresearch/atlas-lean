# Space of affine dependences

For points $v_1, \dots, v_n$ in a real vector space $E$, the affine dependences are the vectors
$\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with

$$\sum_i \lambda_i v_i = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \sum_i \lambda_i = 0.$$

They form a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$, which is zero exactly when the points are affinely
independent. A Gale transform of the configuration is read off from a basis of this subspace.
