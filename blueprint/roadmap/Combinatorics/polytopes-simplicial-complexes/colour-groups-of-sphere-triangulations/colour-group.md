# Colour group of a 2-coloured sphere triangulation

Let $X$ be a finite simplicial
2-sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$, let a colouring assign white or black to every finite set of points of
$\mathbb{R}^3$, and let $c$ be one of the two colours. The colour group of $X$ for $c$ is the
abelian group generated by the vertices of $X$ with the relations $u + v + w = 0$ for each
triangle $\{u, v, w\}$ of $X$ (a face with exactly three vertices) of colour $c$; that is, the
free abelian group $\mathbb{Z}^{(V)}$ on the vertex set $V$ of $X$ modulo the subgroup generated by
the indicator functions of the triangles of $X$ of colour $c$.
