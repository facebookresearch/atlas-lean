# Proper colourings of the triangles of a sphere triangulation

Let $X$ be a finite
simplicial 2-sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and let a colouring assign white or black to every finite
set of points of $\mathbb{R}^3$. The colouring is proper on $X$ if any two distinct triangles of
$X$ (faces with exactly three vertices) that have exactly two vertices in common receive different
colours.
