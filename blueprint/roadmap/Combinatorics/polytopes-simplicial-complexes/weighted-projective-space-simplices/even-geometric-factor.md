# Even geometric factor

For a natural number $n$, the even geometric factor is the
polynomial $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} z^{2k} = 1 + z^2 + \dots + z^{2n-2}$ with integer coefficients;
for $n = 0$ it is the zero polynomial.
