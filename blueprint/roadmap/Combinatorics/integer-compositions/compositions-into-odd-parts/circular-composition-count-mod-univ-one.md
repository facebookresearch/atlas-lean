# Number of circular compositions of 3

Let $\mathbf{1}_{\mathbb{N}}$ be the multiplicity function of $\mathbb{N}$ (every part allowed, in one color) and let $c_{\mathbb{N}}(m, q; n)$ count the circular compositions of $n$ (nonempty sets of removed edges of the cycle $C_n$) with a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. Then $c_{\mathbb{N}}(1, 0; 3) = 7 = 2^3 - 1$.

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Section 4.3.
