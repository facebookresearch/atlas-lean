# Circular compositions of 4 into an even number of odd parts

Let $O$ be the set of odd natural numbers and let $c_O(m, q; n)$ count the circular compositions of $n$ with all parts odd and a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. Then $c_O(2, 0; 4) = 5$: they are the four rotations of $1 + 3$ around the cycle $C_4$ and the single composition $1 + 1 + 1 + 1$.
