# Colored cyclic compositions with a congruence condition on the number of parts

Let $\mu \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and let $m, q, n$ be natural numbers. A colored composition of $n$ with $k$ parts is a list $((a_1, \gamma_1), \dots, (a_k, \gamma_k))$ of pairs of natural numbers with every $a_i \ge 1$, $a_1 + \dots + a_k = n$ and $\gamma_i < \mu(a_i)$. The number $d_\mu(m, q; n)$ is the number of classes of colored compositions of $n$ with $k$ parts, $1 \le k \le n$ and $k \bmod m = q \bmod m$ (with $k \bmod 0 = k$), where two lists lie in the same class when one is a cyclic rotation of the other. These classes are the cyclic compositions of $n$ with parts colored by $\mu$ and a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. The empty composition is excluded, so $d_\mu(m, q; 0) = 0$. For $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, write $d_A = d_{\mathbf{1}_A}$.
