# Compositions of n and circular compositions of n − 1

Let $b(m, q; n)$ and $c(m, q; n)$ be the numbers of compositions and of circular compositions of $n$ (all positive parts allowed) whose number of parts is congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. For all natural numbers $m$, $q$ and $n$ with $m > 1$, $q < m$ and $n \ge 1$,

$$b(m, q; n) = \begin{cases} c(m, m - 1; n - 1) & \text{if } q = 0, \\ c(m, 0; n - 1) + 1 & \text{if } q = 1, \\ c(m, q - 1; n - 1) & \text{if } 2 \le q < m. \end{cases}$$

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Lemma 9.
