# Number of cyclic compositions of 4

Let $\mathbf{1}_{\mathbb{N}}$ be the multiplicity function of $\mathbb{N}$ (every part allowed, in one color) and let $d_{\mathbb{N}}(m, q; n)$ count the cyclic compositions of $n$ (nonempty compositions up to cyclic rotation) with a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. Then $d_{\mathbb{N}}(1, 0; 4) = 5$: the cyclic compositions of $4$ are $4$, $3+1$, $2+2$, $2+1+1$ and $1+1+1+1$.

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Section 4.3.
