# Compositions as differences of circular composition counts

Let $b(m, q; n)$ and $c(m, q; n)$ be the numbers of compositions and of circular compositions of $n$ (all positive parts allowed) whose number of parts is congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. For all natural numbers $m$, $q$ and $n$ with $q < m$ and $n \ge 1$, in $\mathbb{Z}$,

$$b(m, q; n) = c(m, q; n) - c(m, q; n - 1).$$

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Lemma 9.
