# Number of compositions of 4

Let $\mathbf{1}_{\mathbb{N}}$ be the multiplicity function of $\mathbb{N}$ (every part allowed, in one color) and let $b_{\mathbb{N}}(m, q; n)$ count the compositions of $n$ with a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. Then $b_{\mathbb{N}}(1, 0; 4) = 8$: the number $4$ has $2^3 = 8$ compositions.

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Section 4.3.
