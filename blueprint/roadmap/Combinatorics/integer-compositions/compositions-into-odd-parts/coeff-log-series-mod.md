# Coefficients of the residue-class logarithmic series

Let $L_{m,s}(y) = \sum_{r \ge 1,\ r \bmod m = s \bmod m} y^r / r$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. For all natural numbers $m$, $s$ and $r$, the coefficient of $y^r$ in $L_{m,s}(y)$ is

$$[y^r]\, L_{m,s}(y) = \begin{cases} 1/r & \text{if } r \ne 0 \text{ and } r \bmod m = s \bmod m, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Theorem 3.
