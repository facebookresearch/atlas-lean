# Cyclic compositions of 3 with a colors on a part a

Let $\nu(a) = a$, so that a part $a$ carries one of $a$ colors, and let $d_\nu(m, q; n)$ count the cyclic compositions of $n$ with parts colored by $\nu$ and a number of parts congruent to $q$ modulo $m$. Then $d_\nu(1, 0; 3) = 6$: writing the color as a subscript, they are $3_1$, $3_2$, $3_3$, $2_1 + 1_1$, $2_2 + 1_1$ and $1_1 + 1_1 + 1_1$.

## Sources

- Joshua P. Bowman, *Compositions with an Odd Number of Parts, and Other Congruences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.6, Section 5.3.
