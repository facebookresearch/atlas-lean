# Diagonals of a convex polygon

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the diagonal set is the set of pairs $(a, b)$ of natural numbers with
$a + 2 \le b \le n + 1$ and $(a, b) \ne (0, n+1)$; it is empty for $n = 0$. For $n \ge 1$, label
the vertices of the convex $(n+2)$-gon $0, 1, \dots, n+1$ in cyclic order: these pairs are its
diagonals, the excluded pair being the side joining the vertices $0$ and $n+1$.

## Sources

- Juan B. Gil, Emma G. Hoover, and Jessica A. Shearer, *Bijections Between Colored Compositions, Dyck Paths, and Polygon Partitions*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.5.2, Section 1.3 (Polygon partitions).
