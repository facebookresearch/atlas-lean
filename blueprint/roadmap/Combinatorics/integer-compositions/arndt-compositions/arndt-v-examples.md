# Values of the map $V$

The map $V$ sends $(2, 3)$ to $(1, 1, 3)$, $(1, 4)$ to $(5)$,
$(4, 1)$ to $(1, 1, 1, 2)$, $(1, 3, 1)$ to $(1, 3, 1)$ and $(2, 2, 1)$ to $(4, 1)$.
