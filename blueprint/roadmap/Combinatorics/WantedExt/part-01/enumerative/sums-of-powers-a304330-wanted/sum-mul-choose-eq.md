# Lemma 7

**Lemma 7**: `Σ_{i=1}^n i·C(i + k - 1, 2k - 1) =
(k(2n + 1)/(2k + 1))·C(n + k, 2k)` for `n ≥ 1` and fixed
positive `k`.
