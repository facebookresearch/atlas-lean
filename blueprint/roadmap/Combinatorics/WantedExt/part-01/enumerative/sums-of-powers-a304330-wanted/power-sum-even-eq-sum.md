# Proposition 3 (Knuth, 1993), even case

**Proposition 3 (Knuth, 1993), even case**:
`S_{2k}(n) = Σ_{m=1}^k R(k, m)·((2n + 1)/(2m + 1))·C(n + m, 2m)`
for `k ≥ 1`.
