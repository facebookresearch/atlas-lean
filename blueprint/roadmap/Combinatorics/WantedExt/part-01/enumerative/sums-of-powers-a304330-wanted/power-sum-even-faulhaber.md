# Proposition 12, even case

**Proposition 12, even case**: the Faulhaber form
`S_{2k}(n) = S_2(n)·Σ_{r=1}^k b_{k,r}·S_1(n)^{r-1}` with
`b_{k,r}` as in `faulhaberB`, for `k ≥ 1`.
