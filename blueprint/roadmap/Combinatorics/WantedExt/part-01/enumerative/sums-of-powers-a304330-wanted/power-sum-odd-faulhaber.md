# Proposition 12, odd case

**Proposition 12, odd case**: the Faulhaber form
`S_{2k+1}(n) = S_1(n)^2·Σ_{r=1}^k c_{k,r}·S_1(n)^{r-1}` with
`c_{k,r}` as in `faulhaberC`, for `k ≥ 1`.
