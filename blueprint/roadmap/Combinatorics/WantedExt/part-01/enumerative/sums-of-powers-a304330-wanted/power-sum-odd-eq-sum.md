# Proposition 3 (Knuth, 1993), odd case

**Proposition 3 (Knuth, 1993), odd case**:
`S_{2k-1}(n) = Σ_{m=1}^k R(k, m)·(1/m)·C(n + m, 2m)` for `k ≥ 1`.
