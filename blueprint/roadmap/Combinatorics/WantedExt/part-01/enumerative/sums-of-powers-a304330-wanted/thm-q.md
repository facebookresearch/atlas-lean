# Thm Q

The polynomial values `Q_{k,m}(n)` of Theorem 10:
`n^{2k-1}` for `m = 1`, and
`2·Σ_{j=m}^k C(2k-1, 2j-2)·R(j-1, m-1)·n^{2k-2j+1}` for
`m ≥ 2`, evaluated at `n`.
