# Theorem 18

**Theorem 18** (generalized Fleron's recurrence):
`R^{(j)}_{n,k} = R^{(j)}_{n-1,k} + R^{(j)}_{n-1,k-1} -
R^{(j)}_{n-2,k-1} + R^{(j-1)}_{n-2,k-1}` for `2 ≤ n`,
`0 ≤ k ≤ n`, and `1 ≤ j`, with the `ℤ`-extension by `0` out
of range handling the boundary values.
