# Corollary 15

**Corollary 15**:
`(1/(m!)²) ∏_{k=1}^m (R_{n,k} - 1) = \binom{n-1}{m}` for
`1 ≤ m ≤ n`, written cross-multiplied to stay in `ℤ`.
