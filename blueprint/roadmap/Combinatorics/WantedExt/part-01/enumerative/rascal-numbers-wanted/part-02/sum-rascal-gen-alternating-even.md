# Theorem 23

**Theorem 23** (generalized alternating row sum, even
case): `∑_{k=0}^{2m} (-1)^k R^{(j)}_{2m,k} =
(-1)^j \binom{m-1}{j}`, with the paper's convention
`\binom{-1}{j} = (-1)^j` at `m = 0`.
