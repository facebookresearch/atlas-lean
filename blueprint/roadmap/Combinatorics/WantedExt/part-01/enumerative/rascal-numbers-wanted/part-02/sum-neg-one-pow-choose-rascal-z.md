# Theorem 12

**Theorem 12**:
`∑_{j=0}^r (-1)^{r-j} \binom{r}{j} R_{n+j,k} = 0` for
`0 ≤ k ≤ n` and `r ≥ 2` (the `r`-th finite difference of a
linear function of `n`).
