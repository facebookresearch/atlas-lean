# Theorem 14

**Theorem 14**, product as subset sum:
`∏_{k=1}^m (R_{n,k} - 1) = ∑_{S ⊆ [m]} (-1)^{m-|S|}
∏_{i ∈ S} R_{n,i}` for `1 ≤ m ≤ n`, with `[m] = {1, …, m}`.
