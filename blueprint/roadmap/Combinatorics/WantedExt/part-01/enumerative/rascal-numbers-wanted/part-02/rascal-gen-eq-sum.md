# Theorem 19

**Theorem 19**: `R^{(j)}_{n,k} = ∑_{i=0}^j \binom{k}{i}
\binom{n-k}{i}` for `k ≤ n` (the printed summation binder is
`r` with summand in `i`; without `k ≤ n` the sum is `1` while
`R^{(j)}_{n,k} = 0`).
