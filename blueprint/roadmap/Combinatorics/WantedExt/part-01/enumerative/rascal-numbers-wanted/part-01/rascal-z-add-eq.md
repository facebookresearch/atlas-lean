# Theorem 6

**Theorem 6**: `R_{n+m,k} = R_{n,k} + R_{m+k,k} - 1`,
with the hypothesis `k ≤ n` that the printed statement
omits (without it, `R_{0+1,2} = 0 ≠ 0 + 3 - 1`).
