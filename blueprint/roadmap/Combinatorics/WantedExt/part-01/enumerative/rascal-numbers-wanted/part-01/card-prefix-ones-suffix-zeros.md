# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3**: for `ℓ ≤ k` and `k + u ≤ n`,
`R_{n-u-ℓ,k-ℓ}` counts the words in `B_k(n)` that start with
at least `ℓ` ones and end with at least `u` zeros.
