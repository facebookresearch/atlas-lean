# Theorem 5

**Theorem 5** (generalization of the diamond recurrence):
`R_{n,k} = (R_{n-u,k} R_{n-ℓ,k-ℓ} + uℓ) / R_{n-u-ℓ,k-ℓ}`,
written cross-multiplied to stay in `ℤ`.
