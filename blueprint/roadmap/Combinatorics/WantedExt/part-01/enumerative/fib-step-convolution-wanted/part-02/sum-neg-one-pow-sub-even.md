# Theorem 15

**Theorem 15**: for `m ≥ 2` (so that `F^{(2m-2)}` is
defined) and `n ≥ 2m - 1`,
`∑_{j=0}^{n-1} (-1)^j (F^{(2m)}_j - F^{(2m-2)}_j) =
(-1)^{n+1} ∑_{j=0}^{n-2m+1} F^{(2m)}_j F^{(2m-2)}_{n-2m+1-j}`.
