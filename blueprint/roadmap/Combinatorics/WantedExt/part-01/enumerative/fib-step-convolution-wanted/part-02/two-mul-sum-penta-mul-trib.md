# Corollary 18

**Corollary 18**, second identity:
`2 ∑_{j=0}^n P_j T_{n-j} = -P_{n+7} + 2P_{n+6} - P_{n+5}
+ 2P_{n+4} + P_{n+3} - T_{n+4} + T_{n+2}` (printed with a
factor `1/2`).
