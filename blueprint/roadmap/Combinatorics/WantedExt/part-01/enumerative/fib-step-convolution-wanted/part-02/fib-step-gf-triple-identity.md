# Theorem 24

**Theorem 24** (generating functions): for
`m, p, q ≥ 1`,
`X^{2m+p} (1-X^p)(1-X^q) F^{(m)} F^{(m+p)} F^{(m+p+q)} =
X^m (1-X)(1-X^p) F^{(m)} F^{(m+p+q)} -
(1-X)^2 (F^{(m+p)} - F^{(m)})` in `ℚ⟦X⟧`.
