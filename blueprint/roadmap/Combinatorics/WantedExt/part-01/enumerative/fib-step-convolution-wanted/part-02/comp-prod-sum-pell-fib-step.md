# Theorem 21

**Theorem 21** (Pell–Fibonacci `m`-step relation):
for `n ≥ 1`, `m ≥ 2`,
`∑_{K(3,n-1)} 𝒫_{k₁} F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾_{k₂} F⁽ᵐ⁾_{k₃} =
∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾ⱼ (𝒫_{n-j} - F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-j}) -
∑_{j=0}^{n-1} 𝒫_j F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-1-j}`.
