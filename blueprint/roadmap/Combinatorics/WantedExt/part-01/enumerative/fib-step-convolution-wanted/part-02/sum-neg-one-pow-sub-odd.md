# Theorem 17

**Theorem 17**: for `n ≥ 2m`,
`∑_{j=0}^{n-1} (-1)^{j+1} (F^{(2m+1)}_j - F^{(2m-1)}_j) =
(-1)^n ∑_{j=0}^{n-2m} F^{(2m+1)}_j F^{(2m-1)}_{n-2m-j}`.
