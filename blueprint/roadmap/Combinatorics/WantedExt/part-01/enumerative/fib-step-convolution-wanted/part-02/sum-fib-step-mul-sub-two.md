# Theorem 19

**Theorem 19**: for `m ≥ 3`, `n ≥ 2`, with `J` the
Jacobsthal numbers, `∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵐ⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁻²⁾_{n-j} =
J_{n-1} + ∑_{j=0}^{n-2} J_j (F⁽ᵐ⁻²⁾_{n-j} - F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-2-j})`.
