# Theorem 12

**Theorem 12**: for `m, n ≥ 2`,
`∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-j} =
∑_{j=0}^{n-2} 2^j (F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾_{n-1-j} - F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-2-j})`.
