# Theorem 27

**Theorem 27**: for `n ≥ 7`,
`2 ∑_{K(3,n-7)} T_{k₁} Q_{k₂} P_{k₃} =
P_n + P_{n-2} + P_{n-4} + T_n - T_{n-2} - 2Q_n` (printed
with a factor `1/2`).
