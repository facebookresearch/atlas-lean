# Theorem 25

**Theorem 25**, coefficient form: for `n ≥ m`,
`∑_{K(3,n-m)} F⁽ᵐ⁾_{k₁} F⁽ᵐ⁺¹⁾_{k₂} F⁽ᵖ⁾_{k₃} =
∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵖ⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁺¹⁾_{n-j} -
∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵖ⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-j}`.
