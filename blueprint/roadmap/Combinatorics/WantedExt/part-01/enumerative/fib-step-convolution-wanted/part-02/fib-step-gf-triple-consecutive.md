# Theorem 24

**Theorem 24**, particular case (`p = q = 1`):
`X^{2m+1} F^{(m)} F^{(m+1)} F^{(m+2)} =
X^m F^{(m)} F^{(m+2)} - F^{(m+1)} + F^{(m)}`.
