# Corollary 23

**Corollary 23**, first form:
`∑_{K(3,n)} 𝒫_{k₁} F_{k₂} T_{k₃} = 𝒫_{n+1} + F_{n+2} -
T_{n+3} - ∑_{j=0}^n 𝒫_j T_{n-j}`.
