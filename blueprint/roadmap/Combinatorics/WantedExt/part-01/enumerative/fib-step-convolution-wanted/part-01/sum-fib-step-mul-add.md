# Corollary 4

**Corollary 4**: for `n ≥ m`,
`∑_{j=0}^{n-m-1} F⁽ᵐ⁾ⱼ (F⁽ᵐ⁺²⁾_{n-m-j} +
F⁽ᵐ⁺²⁾_{n-m-j-1}) = F⁽ᵐ⁺²⁾ₙ - F⁽ᵐ⁾ₙ`.
