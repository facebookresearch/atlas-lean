# Theorem 10

**Theorem 10**: for `n ≥ 1`, `m ≥ 3`,
`∑_{j=0}^n F⁽ᵐ⁻²⁾ⱼ (F⁽ᵐ⁾_{n-j} - F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾_{n-j}) =
∑_{j=0}^{n-1} F⁽ᵐ⁾ⱼ (F⁽ᵐ⁻¹⁾_{n-1-j} - F⁽ᵐ⁻²⁾_{n-1-j})`.
