# Theorem 3

**Theorem 3**: for `n ≥ m`,
`∑_{k=0}^{p-1} ∑_{j=0}^{n-m-k} F⁽ᵐ⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁺ᵖ⁾_{n-m-k-j}
= F⁽ᵐ⁺ᵖ⁾ₙ - F⁽ᵐ⁾ₙ`.
