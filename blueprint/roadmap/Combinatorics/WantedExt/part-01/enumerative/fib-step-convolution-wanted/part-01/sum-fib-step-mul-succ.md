# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1**: for `n ≥ m`,
`∑_{j=0}^{n-m} F⁽ᵐ⁾ⱼ F⁽ᵐ⁺¹⁾_{n-m-j} = F⁽ᵐ⁺¹⁾ₙ - F⁽ᵐ⁾ₙ`.
