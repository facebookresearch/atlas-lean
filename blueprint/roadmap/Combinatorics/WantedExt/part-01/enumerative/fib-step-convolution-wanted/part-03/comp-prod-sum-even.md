# Theorem 35

**Theorem 35**, even case: for `ℓ ≥ 1`,
`∑_{K(2ℓ, n-ℓ(m+ℓ-1))} ∏_{j=0}^{2ℓ-1} F^{(m+j)}_{k_{j+1}}
= ∑_{K(ℓ, n)} ∏_{j=0}^{ℓ-1}
(F^{(m+2j+1)}_{k_{j+1}} - F^{(m+2j)}_{k_{j+1}})`.
