# Theorem 35

**Theorem 35**, odd case: for `ℓ ≥ 1`,
`∑_{K(2ℓ+1, n-ℓ(m+ℓ-1))} ∏_{j=0}^{2ℓ} F^{(m+j)}_{k_{j+1}}
= ∑_{K(ℓ+1, n)} F^{(m+2ℓ)}_{k_{ℓ+1}}
∏_{j=0}^{ℓ-1} (F^{(m+2j+1)}_{k_{j+1}} -
F^{(m+2j)}_{k_{j+1}})`.
