# Corollary 36

**Corollary 36**:
`∑_{K(3,n-2)} F_{k₁} T_{k₂} Q_{k₃} =
∑_{j=0}^n (T_j - F_j) Q_{n-j} =
∑_{j=0}^{n+1} F_{n+1-j} (Q_j - T_j)`.
