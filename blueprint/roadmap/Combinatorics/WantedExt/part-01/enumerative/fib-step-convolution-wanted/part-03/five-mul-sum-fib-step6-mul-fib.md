# Theorem 41

**Theorem 41**: with `s = F⁽⁶⁾`, for `n ≥ 3`,
`5 ∑_{j=0}^n s_j F_{n-j} =
s_{n+3} + s_{n+1} - s_n + 3s_{n-1} + s_{n-3} - F_{n+3} -
F_{n+1}` (printed with a factor `1/5`).
