# Compositions Odd Parts Wanted

The partial-fraction (roots-of-unity filter) identity
`u ^ α / (1 - u ^ β) = (1 / β) ∑_{k < β} ζ ^ (-k α) / (1 - ζ ^ k u)`
with `ζ = e ^ (2πi / β)`, for `0 ≤ α < β` and `1 - u ^ β ≠ 0`.
