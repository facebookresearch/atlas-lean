# Theorem 6, strongly sparse case

**Theorem 6, strongly sparse case**:
`|A^s_{n,ℓ}| = Σ_{i=0}^{n-1} |E^d_{i,ℓ+1}|` for `n ≥ 1` and
`ℓ ≥ 0`.
