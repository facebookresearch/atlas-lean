# Theorem 7, sparse case

**Theorem 7, sparse case**: `|A_{n,ℓ}| = |G_{n-1,ℓ}|` for
positive `n` and `ℓ` (the paper's `ℕ` is the positive integers;
the identity fails at `ℓ = 0`).
