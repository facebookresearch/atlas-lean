# Partition Min Part Gap Count

`|E_{n,ℓ,k,q}|`: partitions in `E_{n,ℓ,k}` where the
difference between the largest and second largest parts is `q`,
encoded by removing the two largest parts `b + q ≥ b` from the
parts multiset.
