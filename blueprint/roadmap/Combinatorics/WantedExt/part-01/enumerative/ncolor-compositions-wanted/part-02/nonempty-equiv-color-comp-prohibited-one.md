# Theorem 12

**Theorem 12**: there is a bijection between `n`-color
compositions of `n+2` with colors `1, 2` prohibited and
compositions of `n` with parts `≡ 1, 2 (mod 4)` (for
`n ≥ 1`; at `n = 0` the counts are `0` and `1`).
