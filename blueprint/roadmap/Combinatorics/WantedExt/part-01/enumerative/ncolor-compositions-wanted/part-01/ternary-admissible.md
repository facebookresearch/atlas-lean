# Ternary Admissible

The ternary strings of Theorem 2: length `n - 1`, and
every zero-free segment is all `1`s of length `≥ b - 1` or
all `2`s of length `≥ b` (the paper's no-`12`/`21`, run-length,
and zero-separation conditions in segment form).
