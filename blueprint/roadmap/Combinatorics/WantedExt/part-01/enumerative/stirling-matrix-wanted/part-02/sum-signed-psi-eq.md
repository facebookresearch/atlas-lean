# Proposition 9

**Proposition 9**: for `i, j ≥ 1`,
`∑_{k₁=1}^i ∑_{k₂=1}^j s(i,k₁) k₂^{k₁-1}
(-1)^{j-k₂}/(j-k₂)! Ψ_α(k₂)` equals `Λ(α,i,j)` if
`i ≤ j` and `0` if `i > j`.
