# Corollary 10

**Corollary 10**: `∑_{k₁=1}^i ∑_{k₂=1}^j
(-1)^{j-k₂} s(i,k₁) k₂^{k₁-1} / ((j-k₂)! (k₂-1)!) =
δ_{i,j}`.
