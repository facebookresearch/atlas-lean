# Proposition 5

**Proposition 5**: for `i, j ≥ 1`,
`∑_{k=1}^{min(i,j)} S(i,k) Λ(α,k,j) =
∑_{k=1}^j k^{i-1} (-1)^{j-k}/(j-k)! Ψ_α(k)`.
