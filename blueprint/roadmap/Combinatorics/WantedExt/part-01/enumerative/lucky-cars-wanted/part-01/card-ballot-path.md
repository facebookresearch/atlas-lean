# Lemma 21

**Lemma 21**: for `ℓ ≥ k`, the number of lattice paths
from `(0,0)` to `(k, ℓ)` staying on or above `y = x` is
`((ℓ-k+1)/(ℓ+1)) \binom{k+ℓ}{k}`.
