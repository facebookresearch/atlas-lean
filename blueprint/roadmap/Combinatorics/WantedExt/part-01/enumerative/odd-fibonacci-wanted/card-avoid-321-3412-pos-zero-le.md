# Proposition 8

**Proposition 8**, first clause: for `n > 1`, the
numbers of `(321, 3412)`-avoiding permutations of `[n]`
with `σ(1) = 1` and with `σ(2) = 1` (1-based) both equal
`F_{2n-3}`.
