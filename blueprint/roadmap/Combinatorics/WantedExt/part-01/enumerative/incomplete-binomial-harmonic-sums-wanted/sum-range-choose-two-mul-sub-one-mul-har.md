# Sum range choose two mul sub one mul harmonic

The odd half-range case of Kollár's Theorem 3:
`∑ i ∈ [0,n-1], (2n - 1).choose i * Hᵢ` in terms of harmonic
numbers and the auxiliary sums `C` and `D`.
