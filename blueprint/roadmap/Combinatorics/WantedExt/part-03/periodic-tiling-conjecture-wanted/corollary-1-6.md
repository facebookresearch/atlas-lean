# Corollary 1 6

Statement of Corollary 1.6 (Counterexample to Conjecture 1.2, II):

For all sufficiently large `d`, there exists a finite non-empty `F ⊆ ℤᵈ` such that
`F` forms an aperiodic tiling equation.
