# Theorem 1 4

Statement of Theorem 1.4 (Counterexample to Conjecture 1.2, I):

There exists a finite abelian group `G₀` and a finite non-empty `F ⊆ ℤ² × G₀` such that
`F` forms an aperiodic tiling equation.
