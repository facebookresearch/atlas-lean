# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3**: for `n ≥ 1`,
`m_{4n} = 2n - 1 + m_{n+1}`, `M_{4n} = 2n + M_{n+1}`,
`m_{4n+2} = 2n + m_{n+1}`, and `M_{4n+2} = 2n + 1 + M_{n+1}`.
