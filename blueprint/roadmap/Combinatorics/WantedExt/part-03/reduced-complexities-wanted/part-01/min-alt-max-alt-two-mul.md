# Lemma 2

**Lemma 2**: for `n ≥ 2`,
`m_{2n} = 2n - 1 - M_{n+1}`, `M_{2n} = 2n - 1 - m_n`,
`m_{2n+1} = 2n - M_{n+1}`, and `M_{2n+1} = 2n - m_{n+1}`.
