# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4**: any binary word of length `2n+1` whose even
positions alternate (`a?b?a?b? ⋯` or `b?a?b?a? ⋯`, the `?`
arbitrary) has `n + 1` runs; the paper applies this to
factors of the paperfolding word.
