# Knuth Old Sum Weight

The weight `(2^m + 1)·2^{m-1} = (4^m + 2^m)/2` in Knuth's old
sum; by Lemma 1 below it counts the words of length `m` over a
four-letter alphabet with an even number of `a`'s.
