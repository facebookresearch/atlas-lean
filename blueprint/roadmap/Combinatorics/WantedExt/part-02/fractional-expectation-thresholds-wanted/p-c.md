# P c

Given an increasing collection `𝓕` of subsets of `X` such that `𝓕 ≠ ∅` and  `𝓕 ≠ 𝓟 X`, we
define the `threshold`, `p_c(𝓕)` of `𝓕` as the (unique) `p` such that the Bernoulli `p` product
measure of `𝓕` equals `1 / 2`.
