# Morph Conjugate

Conjugate morphisms (Labbé 2014, Section 2; Lothaire): `ψ` and `θ` are
conjugate when `ψ a ++ w = w ++ θ a` for every letter `a` (or the
symmetric condition) for some word `w`.
