# Conjecture holds

Resolved true: Cassaigne, Currie, Schaeffer and Shallit (J. ACM 61 (2014), arXiv:1106.5204,
Theorem 18) prove, via a finite-graph reachability reduction checked by computer, that the fixed
point of 0->03, 1->43, 3->1, 4->01 contains no additive cubes. Source: Julien Cassaigne, James
D. Currie, Luke Schaeffer and Jeffrey Shallit, Avoiding three consecutive blocks of the same
size and same sum, J. ACM 61(2) (2014), Article 10, arXiv:1106.5204,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5204. Moved from `OpenConjectures/Combinatorics/PVHHCubeMorphicWord`.
