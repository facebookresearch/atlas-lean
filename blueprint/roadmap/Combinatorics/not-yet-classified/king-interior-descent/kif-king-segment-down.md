# Kif King Segment Down

Every off-support interior site `z ≠ seed` whose left neighbour is on `Vc` starts a run `z 0 ≤ t
≤ cx` in its row avoiding `Vc` such that either `(cx, z 1 - 1)` is off `Vc`, or both `(cx + 1, z 1)`
and `(cx + 1, z 1 - 1)` are off `Vc`. When `seed` is not the lexicographically least off-support
interior site, this fails at the least site (`kif_not_forall_kingSegmentDown`).
