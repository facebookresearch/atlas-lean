# Kollár's auxiliary sum $C_n$

For a natural number $n$, $C_n$ is the rational number
$C_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k \, 2^k}$.

## Sources

- Richard Kollár, *Incomplete Finite Binomial Sums of Harmonic Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.2.1, equation (6), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Kollar/kollar6.html>.
