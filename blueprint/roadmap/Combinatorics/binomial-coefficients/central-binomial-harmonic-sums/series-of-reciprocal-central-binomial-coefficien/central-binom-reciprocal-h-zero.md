# The closed form $H$ equals $1$ at $0$

The function
$H(x) = \frac{4\sqrt{x}\,\arcsin(\sqrt{x}/2)}{(4 - x)\sqrt{4 - x}} + \frac{4}{4 - x}$ of a real
variable satisfies $H(0) = 1$.
