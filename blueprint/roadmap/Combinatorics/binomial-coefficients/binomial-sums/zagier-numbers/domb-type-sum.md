# Domb-type sums

For natural numbers $n$, $r$, $s$ and $t$, the Domb-type sum is the
natural number

$$D(n, r, s, t) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}^r \binom{2k}{k}^s \binom{2(n-k)}{n-k}^t.$$

The source takes $r \ge 1$; the same finite formula is meaningful at $r = 0$.

## Sources

- Geng-Rui Zhang, *Realizability of Some Combinatorial Sequences*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.3.3, Definition 7, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Zhang/zhang9.html>.
