# Strehl's identity for the Franel numbers

For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}^2 \binom{2k}{n} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}^3,$$

where the right-hand side is the $n$-th Franel number.
