# Hockey-stick convolution

For all natural numbers $n$, $p$ and $q$, $\sum_{a + b = n} \binom{a}{p}\binom{b}{q} = \binom{n+1}{p+q+1}$, the sum over the pairs $(a, b)$ of natural numbers with $a + b = n$. This is the upper-index form of the Chu–Vandermonde identity; for $q = 0$ it is the hockey-stick identity.
