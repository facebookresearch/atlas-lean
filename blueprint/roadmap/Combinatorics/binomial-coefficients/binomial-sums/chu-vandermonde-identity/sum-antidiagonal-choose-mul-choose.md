# Hockey-stick convolution

For all natural numbers $n$, $p$ and $q$,

$$\sum_{a + b = n} \binom{a}{p}\binom{b}{q} = \sum_{i=0}^{n} \binom{i}{p}\binom{n-i}{q} = \binom{n+1}{p+q+1},$$

where the first sum runs over the pairs $(a, b)$ of natural numbers with $a + b = n$ and the second is the same sum indexed by $i = a$. This is the upper-index form of the Chu–Vandermonde identity; for $q = 0$ it is the hockey-stick identity.
