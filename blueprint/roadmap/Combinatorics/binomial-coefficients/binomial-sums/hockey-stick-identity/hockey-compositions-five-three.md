# Compositions with parts between 2 and 4 of size at most 5

The compositions of size at most $5$ with every part in $\{2, 3, 4\}$ are

$$\mathcal{C}(5, 3) = \{\emptyset, (2), (3), (4), (2, 2), (2, 3), (3, 2)\}.$$
