# Signed count of labeled lattice sequences

For all natural numbers $k, n, r$, the sum of $\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha) = (-1)^{|\alpha| - \ell(\alpha)}$ over all labeled lattice sequences $(\lambda, L)$ of type $(k, n, r)$, that is, over all $\alpha \in \mathcal{C}(k, r)$ and all pairs $(\lambda, L) \in \Lambda(\alpha, r) \times \mathcal{L}(rk + n + 1 + \ell(\alpha), k - |\alpha|)$, is

$$\sum_{\alpha \in \mathcal{C}(k, r)} \sum_{(\lambda, L)} \operatorname{sgn}(\alpha) = \sum_{i=0}^{k} \binom{(r+1)i + n}{i},$$

computed in the integers.
