# Composition operations reverse the sign

Let $a \ge 2$ be a natural number and let $t = (t_1, \dots, t_m)$ be a finite list of positive natural numbers. Write $(a, t)$ for the composition $(a, t_1, \dots, t_m)$, where $\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha) = (-1)^{|\alpha| - \ell(\alpha)}$. Each of the compositions

- $(a + 1, t)$,
- $(2, a, t)$, and
- $(a - 1, t)$ if $a > 2$, or $t$ itself if $a = 2$,

has sign $-\operatorname{sgn}((a, t))$.

## Sources
