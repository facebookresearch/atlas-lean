# Statistic mu of a lattice sequence

For a sequence $L = (L_0, \dots, L_{m+1})$ with values in $\{0, \dots, h\}$ and a natural number $\eta$, the integer $\mu_\eta(L)$ is

$$\mu_\eta(L) = \eta - 1 - \gamma_\eta(L),$$

where $\gamma_\eta(L)$ is the largest $i < \eta$ with $L_i < L_{i+1}$, or $-1$ if there is none.
