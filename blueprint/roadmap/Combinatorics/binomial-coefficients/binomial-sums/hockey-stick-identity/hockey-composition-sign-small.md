# Signs of the compositions (2) and (3, 1)

The composition $(2)$ has sign $\operatorname{sgn}((2)) = (-1)^{2-1} = -1$, and the composition $(3, 1)$ has sign $\operatorname{sgn}((3, 1)) = (-1)^{4-2} = 1$.
