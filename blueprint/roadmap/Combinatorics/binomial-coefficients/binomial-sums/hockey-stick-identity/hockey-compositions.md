# Compositions with parts between 2 and r + 1

For natural numbers $k$ and $r$, $\mathcal{C}(k, r)$ is the finite set of finite sequences $\alpha = (\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_\ell)$ of natural numbers, of any length $\ell \ge 0$, with every part in $\{2, 3, \dots, r + 1\}$ and $\alpha_1 + \dots + \alpha_\ell \le k$: the compositions of the integers $j \le k$ with parts in $[2, r + 1]$. It always contains the empty composition, and for $r = 0$ it contains nothing else.
