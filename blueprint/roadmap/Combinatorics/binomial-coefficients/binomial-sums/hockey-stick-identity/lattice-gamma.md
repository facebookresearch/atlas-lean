# Last ascent statistic of a lattice sequence

For a sequence $L = (L_0, \dots, L_{m+1})$ with values in $\{0, \dots, h\}$ and a natural number $\eta$, the integer $\gamma_\eta(L)$ is the largest $i \in \{0, 1, \dots, \eta - 1\}$ with $L_i < L_{i+1}$ (an ascent of $L_0, \dots, L_\eta$), entries past the end read as $0$, and is $-1$ when there is no such $i$.
