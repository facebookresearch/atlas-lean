# Sign of a composition

For a finite list $\alpha = (\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_\ell)$ of natural numbers, the parts of a composition, with size $|\alpha| = \alpha_1 + \dots + \alpha_\ell$ and length $\ell(\alpha) = \ell$, the sign of $\alpha$ is the integer

$$\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha) = (-1)^{|\alpha| - \ell(\alpha)}.$$

The exponent is truncated subtraction, read as $0$ when $|\alpha| < \ell(\alpha)$, which can happen only when some part is $0$.
