# Step statistic of a lattice sequence

For a sequence $L = (L_0, \dots, L_{m+1})$ with values in $\{0, \dots, h\}$ and a natural number $\eta$, the statistic $\omega_\eta(L)$ is the natural number $L_{\eta+1} - L_\eta$, with truncated subtraction and entries past the end read as $0$. In particular $\omega_\eta(L) = 0$ for $\eta \ge m + 1$.
