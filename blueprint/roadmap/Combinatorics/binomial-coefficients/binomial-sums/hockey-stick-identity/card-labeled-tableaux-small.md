# Numbers of labeled tableaux of shapes (2, 2) and the empty shape

There are $|\Lambda((2, 2), 2)| = 4$ labeled tableaux of shape $(2, 2)$ with labels in $\{0, 1\}$, and $|\Lambda(\emptyset, 3)| = 1$ labeled tableau of empty shape with labels in $\{0, 1, 2\}$.

## Sources

- Molly Lynch and Michael Weselcouch, *A Generalized Hockey Stick Theorem*, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.7.7, Section 2.3 (labeled tableaux), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL28/Weselcouch/wesel8.html>.
