# Numbers of lattice sequences of sizes (1, 1) and (2, 2)

There are $|\mathcal{L}(1, 1)| = 2$ lattice sequences of width $1$ and height $1$, and $|\mathcal{L}(2, 2)| = 6 = \binom{4}{2}$ lattice sequences of width $2$ and height $2$.
