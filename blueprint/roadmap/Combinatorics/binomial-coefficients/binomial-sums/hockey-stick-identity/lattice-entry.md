# Entry of a lattice sequence

For natural numbers $m, h$, a sequence $L = (L_0, \dots, L_{m+1})$ with values in $\{0, \dots, h\}$ and a natural number $j$, the entry $L_j$ is the $j$-th term of $L$, as a natural number, when $j \le m + 1$, and is $0$ when $j \ge m + 2$.
