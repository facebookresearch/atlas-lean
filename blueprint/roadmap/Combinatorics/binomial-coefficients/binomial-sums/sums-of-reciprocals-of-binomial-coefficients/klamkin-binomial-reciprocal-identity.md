# Klamkin's binomial reciprocal identity

For natural numbers $n$, $b$ and $x$ with
$n + b \le x$, the following equality of rational numbers holds:

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\binom{n}{k}}{\binom{x}{k+b}} = \frac{x+1}{(x-n+1)\binom{x-n}{b}}.$$

## Sources

- Henry W. Gould and Jocelyn Quaintance, *On the Binomial Identities of Frisch and Klamkin*, J. Integer Seq. 19 (2016), Article 16.7.7, equation (3), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL19/Gould/gould8.tex>.
