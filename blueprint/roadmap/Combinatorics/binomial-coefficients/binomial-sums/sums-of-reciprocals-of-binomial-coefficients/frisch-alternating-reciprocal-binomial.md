# Frisch's alternating reciprocal-binomial identity

For natural numbers $n$, $b$ and $c$
with $0 < c \le b$, the rational numbers
$\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k \binom{n}{k} \binom{b+k}{c}^{-1}$ and
$\frac{c}{n+c} \binom{n+b}{b-c}^{-1}$ are equal.
