# Exponential bound on a weighted partial binomial sum

For natural numbers $r \le D$ and every real $x$ with $0 \le x \le 1$,

$$x^r \sum_{j=0}^{r} \binom{D}{j} \le e^{D x},$$

with $x^0 = 1$, so that at $x = 0$ the case $r = 0$ reads $1 \le 1$. It follows from the
[bound by the full expansion](pow-mul-sum-choose-le.md) and $1 + x \le e^x$.
