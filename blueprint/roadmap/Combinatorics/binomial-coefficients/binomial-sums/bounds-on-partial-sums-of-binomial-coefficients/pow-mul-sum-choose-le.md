# Weighted partial binomial sum bounded by the full expansion

For natural numbers $r \le D$ and every real $x$ with $0 \le x \le 1$,

$$x^r \sum_{j=0}^{r} \binom{D}{j} \le (1 + x)^D.$$

Since $x \le 1$, $x^r \le x^j$ for $j \le r$, so the left side is at most
$\sum_{j=0}^{D} \binom{D}{j} x^j = (1 + x)^D$.
