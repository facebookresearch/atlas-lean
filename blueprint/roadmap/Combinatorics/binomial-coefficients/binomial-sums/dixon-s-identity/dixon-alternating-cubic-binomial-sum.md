# Dixon's alternating cubic binomial sum

For every natural number $n$,
$\sum_{k=0}^{2n} (-1)^k \binom{2n}{k}^3 = (-1)^n \binom{2n}{n} \binom{3n}{2n}$.

Mikić attributes the identity to Dixon (1891). This is the specialization of Dixon's three-parameter
identity in which all three parameters equal $n$ ([Dixon's summation theorem](dixon-summation.md)).

## Sources

- Jovan Mikić, *A Method For Examining Divisibility Properties Of Some Binomial Sums*, J. Integer Seq. 21 (2018), Article 18.8.7, equation (4), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL21/Mikic/mikic25.tex>.
- A. C. Dixon, *On the sum of the cubes of the coefficients in a certain expansion by the binomial theorem*, Messenger of Mathematics 20 (1891), 79–80.
