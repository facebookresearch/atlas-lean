# Central binomial polynomial identity, Bernstein form

For every natural number $n$ and
every complex $x$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} 2^{-k} \binom{2k}{k} (1-x)^k x^{n-k} =
\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{2k} 2^{-2k} \binom{2k}{k} (1-x)^{2k}.$$
