# Odd-index binomial sum with odd reciprocal weights

For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\lceil n/2 \rceil} \frac{1}{2k+1} \binom{n}{2k-1} =
\frac{2^{n+1}}{n+2} - \frac{2^n}{n+1}.$$

## Sources

- Kunle Adegoke, *A Short Proof of Knuth's Old Sum, Generalizations and Related Sums*, [arXiv:2412.00040, equation (4.5)](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.00040#S4.E5). The identity follows from the even-index reciprocal sum in Proposition 4 of the journal version, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A19.
