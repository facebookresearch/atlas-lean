# Odd-index binomial sum with odd reciprocal weights

For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\lceil n/2 \rceil} \frac{1}{2k+1} \binom{n}{2k-1} =
\frac{2^{n+1}}{n+2} - \frac{2^n}{n+1}.$$
