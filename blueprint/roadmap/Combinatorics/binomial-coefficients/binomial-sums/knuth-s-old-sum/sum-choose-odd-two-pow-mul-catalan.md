# Odd-index binomial sum of Catalan numbers

Write $C_m = \binom{2m}{m}/(m+1)$ for the
$m$-th Catalan number. For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\lceil n/2 \rceil} \binom{n}{2k-1} \frac{2^n}{2^{2k}} C_k =
\frac{1}{2} C_{n+2} - C_{n+1}.$$
