# Reflection symmetry of the central binomial polynomial sum

For every natural number $n$
and every complex $x$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} \binom{2k}{k} 2^{-2k} \binom{n}{k} (1-x)^{n-k} =
\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k \binom{2k}{k} 2^{-2k} \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k}.$$
