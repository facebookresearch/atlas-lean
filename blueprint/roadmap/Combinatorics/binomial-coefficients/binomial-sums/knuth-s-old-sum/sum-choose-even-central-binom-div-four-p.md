# Even-index binomial sum equal to a scaled central binomial coefficient

For every
natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{2k} \frac{1}{2^{2k}} \binom{2k}{k} =
\frac{1}{2^n} \binom{2n}{n}.$$
