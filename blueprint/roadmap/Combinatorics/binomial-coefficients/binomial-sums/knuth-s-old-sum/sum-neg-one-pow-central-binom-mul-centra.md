# Alternating convolution of central binomial coefficients

For every natural number
$n$, the integer $\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k \binom{2k}{k} \binom{2(n-k)}{n-k}$ equals
$2^n \binom{n}{n/2}$ when $n$ is even and $0$ when $n$ is odd.
