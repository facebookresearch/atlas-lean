# Even-index expansion of a weighted central binomial sum

For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} 2^{-k} \binom{2k}{k} =
\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{2k} 2^{n-4k} \binom{2k}{k}.$$
