# Alternating sum of $\binom{n}{k} \binom{2k}{k}$

For every natural number $n$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} \binom{n}{k} \binom{2k}{k}
= \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{k} \binom{n-k}{k}$$

as integers.

Both sides are the central trinomial coefficient.
