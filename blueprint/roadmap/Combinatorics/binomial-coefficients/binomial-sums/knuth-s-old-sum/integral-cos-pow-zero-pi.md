# Integral of $\cos^m x$ over $(0, \pi)$

For every natural number $m$,
$\int_0^\pi \cos^m x \, dx$ equals $\pi \binom{m}{m/2} / 2^m$ if $m$ is even and $0$ if $m$ is
odd.

This is equation (9) of the source.
