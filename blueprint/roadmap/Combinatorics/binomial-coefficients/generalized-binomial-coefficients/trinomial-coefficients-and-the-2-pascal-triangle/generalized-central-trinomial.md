# Sun's generalized central trinomial coefficient

For integers $b, c$ and a natural number $n$, the generalized central trinomial coefficient $T_n(b, c)$ is the coefficient of $x^n$ in the integer polynomial $(x^2 + bx + c)^n$. For $b = c = 1$ it is the central trinomial coefficient, the coefficient of $x^n$ in $(1 + x + x^2)^n$.

## Sources

- Zhi-Wei Sun, *Congruences involving generalized central trinomial coefficients*, Sci. China Math. 57 (2014), introduction.
- Chahinaze Djadi and Hacène Belbachir, *Congruence Properties for the Delannoy Triangle Coefficients*, J. Integer Seq. 29 (2026), Article 26.5.2, Section 4.
