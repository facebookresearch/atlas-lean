# Unfolded binomial-sum formula for generalized central trinomial coefficients

Let $T_n(b, c)$ be the coefficient of $x^n$ in $(x^2 + bx + c)^n$. For all integers $b, c$ and every natural number $n$,

$$T_n(b, c) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} \binom{n-k}{k} b^{\,n-2k} c^k,$$

computed in the integers with $0^0 = 1$. The exponent $n - 2k$ is truncated subtraction, read as $0$ when $2k > n$; those terms vanish in any case, since then $n - k < k$ and $\binom{n-k}{k} = 0$.
