# Generalized central trinomial coefficient at n = 1

For all integers $b$ and $c$, the generalized central trinomial coefficient $T_1(b, c)$, the coefficient of $x$ in $x^2 + bx + c$, equals $b$.

## Sources

- Chahinaze Djadi and Hacène Belbachir, *Congruence Properties for the Delannoy Triangle Coefficients*, J. Integer Seq. 29 (2026), Article 26.5.2, Section 4.
