# Rank-$l$ base-$b$ digits of a list

For natural numbers $b$ and $l$ and a list
$(k_1, \dots, k_m)$ of natural numbers, this is the list $((k_1)_l, \dots, (k_m)_l)$, where
$(k_i)_l$ is the rank-$l$ base-$b$ digit of $k_i$.
