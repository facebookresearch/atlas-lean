# Alternating row sums of Rascal numbers on even rows

For every natural number $m$, the
Rascal numbers satisfy $\sum_{k=0}^{2m} (-1)^k R_{2m,k} = 1 - m$.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 13, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
