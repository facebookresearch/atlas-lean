# Column sums of the Rascal triangle

For all natural numbers
$k$ and $r$, the Rascal numbers $R_{n,k} = k(n-k) + 1$
($0 \le k \le n$) satisfy
$\sum_{i=0}^{r} R_{k+i,k} = k \binom{r+1}{2} + r + 1$.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 9, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
