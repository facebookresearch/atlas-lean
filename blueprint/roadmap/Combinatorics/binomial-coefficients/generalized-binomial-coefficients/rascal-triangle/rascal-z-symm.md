# Symmetry of Rascal numbers

For integers $n$ and $k$ with $0 \le k \le n$, the Rascal
numbers satisfy $R_{n,k} = R_{n,n-k}$.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 2, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
