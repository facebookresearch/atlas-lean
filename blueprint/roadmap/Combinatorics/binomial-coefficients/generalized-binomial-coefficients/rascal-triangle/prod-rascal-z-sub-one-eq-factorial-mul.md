# Closed form for the product of the shifted Rascal numbers

For natural numbers $m$ and
$n$ with $1 \le m \le n$,

$$\prod_{k=1}^{m} (R_{n,k} - 1) = m! \, (n-1)^{\underline{m}},$$

where $R_{n,k}$ denotes the Rascal numbers and $x^{\underline{m}} = x(x-1)\cdots(x-m+1)$ is the
falling factorial.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 14, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
