# Subset-sum expansion of the product of the shifted Rascal numbers

For natural numbers
$m$ and $n$ with $1 \le m \le n$, writing $[m] = \{1, \dots, m\}$,

$$\prod_{k=1}^{m} (R_{n,k} - 1) = \sum_{S \subseteq [m]} (-1)^{m-|S|} \prod_{i \in S} R_{n,i},$$

where $R_{n,k}$ denotes the Rascal numbers.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 14, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
