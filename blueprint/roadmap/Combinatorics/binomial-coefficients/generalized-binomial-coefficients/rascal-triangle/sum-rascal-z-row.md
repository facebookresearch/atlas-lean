# Row sums of the Rascal triangle

For every natural number
$n$, the Rascal numbers $R_{n,k} = k(n-k) + 1$ ($0 \le k \le n$)
satisfy $\sum_{k=0}^{n} R_{n,k} = \binom{n+1}{3} + n + 1$.

## Sources

- Amelia Gibbs and Brian K. Miceli, *Two Combinatorial Interpretations of Rascal Numbers*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.8.2, Theorem 8, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Miceli/miceli9.html>.
