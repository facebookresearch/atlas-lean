# Quadrinomial numbers as a specialization of Zhang's V-sum

For every natural number $n$,
the quadrinomial number $Q(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} \binom{n}{2k}$ equals Zhang's
six-parameter binomial sum $V(n, 1, 1, 0, 0, 0)$.

## Sources

- Geng-Rui Zhang, *Realizability of Some Combinatorial Sequences*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.3.3, Remark 34, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Zhang/zhang9.html>.
