# Zhang's six-parameter binomial sum

For natural numbers $n$, $r_1$, $r_2$, $s$, $t$, $u$,
Zhang's binomial sum is

$$
V(n, r_1, r_2, s, t, u) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}^{r_1} \binom{n}{2k}^{r_2}
\binom{n+k}{k}^{s} \binom{2k}{k}^{t} \binom{2(n-k)}{n-k}^{u}.
$$

with the convention $0^0 = 1$. The source takes $r_1 + r_2 \ge 1$; the finite formula is meaningful for all parameters.

## Sources

- Geng-Rui Zhang, *Realizability of Some Combinatorial Sequences*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.3.3, Remark 34, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Zhang/zhang9.html>.
