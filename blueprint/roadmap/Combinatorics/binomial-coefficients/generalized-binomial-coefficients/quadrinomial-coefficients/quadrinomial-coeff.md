# Quadrinomial sequence A005725

For a natural number $n$, the quadrinomial number is
$Q(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} \binom{n}{2k}$. This is OEIS sequence A005725.

## Sources

- Geng-Rui Zhang, *Realizability of Some Combinatorial Sequences*, J. Integer Seq. 27 (2024), Article 24.3.3, Remark 34, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Zhang/zhang9.html>.
