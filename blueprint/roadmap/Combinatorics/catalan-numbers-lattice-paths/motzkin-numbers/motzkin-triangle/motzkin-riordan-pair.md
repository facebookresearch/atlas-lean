# The set of Motzkin Riordan pairs

The Motzkin Riordan pairs are the pairs $(g, h)$ of formal power series in $\mathbb{Q}[[X]]$ that form a
[Motzkin Riordan pair](is-motzkin-riordan-pair.md): there is $s \in \mathbb{Q}[[X]]$ with $s^2 = 1 - 2X - 3X^2$ and
constant coefficient $1$ such that $g s = 1$ and $2 X h = 1 - X - s$.
