# Recurrence for alternating Motzkin sums

Let $M_k$ denote the $k$-th Motzkin number and
let $T_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} M_k$. For every natural number $n$, $T_{n+1} = M_{n+1} - T_n$.
