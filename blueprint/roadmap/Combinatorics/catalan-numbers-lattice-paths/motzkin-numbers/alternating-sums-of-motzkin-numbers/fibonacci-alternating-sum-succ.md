# Recurrence for alternating Fibonacci sums

Let $F_k$ denote the $k$-th Fibonacci
number, with $F_0 = 0$ and $F_1 = 1$, and let $W_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} F_k$. For every
natural number $n$, $W_{n+1} = F_{n+1} - W_n$.
