# The alternating Fibonacci sum at 0

Let $F_k$ denote the $k$-th Fibonacci number, with
$F_0 = 0$ and $F_1 = 1$, and let $W_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} F_k$. Then $W_0 = 0$.
