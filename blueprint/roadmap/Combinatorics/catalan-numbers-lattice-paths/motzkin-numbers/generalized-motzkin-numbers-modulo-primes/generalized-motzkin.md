# Generalized Motzkin numbers

For integers $a, b$ and a natural number $n$, the generalized
Motzkin number $M^{(a,b)}_n$ is the constant term of the Laurent polynomial
$(a x^{-1} + b + a x)^n (1 - x^2)$ with integer coefficients.
