# Functional equation for the augmented Dyck generating function

Let $k \ge 1$ and let
$G_{k+1}(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[[x]]$ be the generating function of augmented $(k+1)$-Dyck paths
by size. Then

$$G_{k+1} = 1 + x \, G_{k+1}^{k+2}.$$

## Sources

- Zhang and Zhuang, *A Subfamily of Skew Dyck Paths Related to `k`-ary Trees*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.2.4, Section 2.
