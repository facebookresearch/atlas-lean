# Path weights split at a vertex

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $V$ a set and $w : V \times V \to R$ a weight. For lists of vertices
$a = (a_1, \dots, a_r)$ and $b = (b_1, \dots, b_s)$ and a vertex $v$, the [weight](lgv-path-weight.md) of the list
$(a_1, \dots, a_r, v, b_1, \dots, b_s)$ is the weight of $(a_1, \dots, a_r, v)$ times the weight of
$(v, b_1, \dots, b_s)$.
