# Weight of a vertex-list path

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $V$ a set and $w : V \times V \to R$ a weight on ordered pairs of vertices. The
weight of a path $v_0 v_1 \cdots v_m$, given as the list of its vertices, is the product
$w(v_0, v_1)\, w(v_1, v_2) \cdots w(v_{m-1}, v_m)$ of the weights of its edges; a list with at most one vertex has
weight $1$ (Fomin and Zelevinsky,
*Total positivity: tests and parametrizations*, Math. Intelligencer 22 (2000), no. 1, 23–33,
arXiv:math/9912128v1, page 2: "the weight of a directed path in Γ is defined as the product of the weights of
its edges").
