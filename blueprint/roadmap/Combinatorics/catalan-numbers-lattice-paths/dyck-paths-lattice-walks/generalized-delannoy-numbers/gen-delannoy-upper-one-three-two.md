# An upper generalized Delannoy number equal to $44$

For natural numbers $k$, $m$ and $n$, write
$D^k(m, n) = \sum_{j=0}^{n} \binom{m + n + k - j}{n + k} \binom{n}{j}$ for the upper
generalized Delannoy numbers. Then $D^1(3, 2) = 44$.
