# Valley heights of UUDUDDUD

The Dyck path $UUDUDDUD$ has exactly two valleys, of heights
$1$ and $0$ from left to right.

In particular this path is not non-decreasing.

## Sources

- Rigoberto Flórez, Toufik Mansour, José L. Ramírez, Fabio A. Velandia, and Diego Villamizar, *Restricted Dyck Paths on Valleys Sequence*, arXiv:2108.08299, Section 1.
