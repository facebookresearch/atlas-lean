# Non-decreasing Dyck paths

A Dyck path is non-decreasing when its valley heights (the
heights reached after the $D$ of each factor $DU$), read from left to right, form a
non-decreasing sequence.

## Sources

- Rigoberto Flórez, Toufik Mansour, José L. Ramírez, Fabio A. Velandia, and Diego Villamizar, *Restricted Dyck Paths on Valleys Sequence*, arXiv:2108.08299, Section 1 (the $0$-Dyck paths), as used by Rigoberto Flórez, Leandro Junes, Luisa M. Montoya, and José L. Ramírez, *Counting Subwords in Non-Decreasing Dyck Paths*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.1.6.
