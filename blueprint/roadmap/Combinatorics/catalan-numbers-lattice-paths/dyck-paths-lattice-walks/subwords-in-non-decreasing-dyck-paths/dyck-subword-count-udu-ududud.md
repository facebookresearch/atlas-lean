# Occurrences of UDU in UDUDUD

The contiguous subword $UDU$ occurs exactly $2$ times in
$UDUDUD$; the two occurrences overlap.

## Sources

- Rigoberto Flórez, Leandro Junes, Luisa M. Montoya, and José L. Ramírez, *Counting Subwords in Non-Decreasing Dyck Paths*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.1.6, Section 1.
