# Riordan numbers

For a natural number $n$, the $n$-th Riordan number is the integer

$$r_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-k} \binom{n}{k} C_k,$$

where $C_k$ is the $k$-th Catalan number.
