# Parity of Catalan numbers

For every natural number $n$, the $n$th Catalan number $C_n$
is odd if and only if $n = 2^a - 1$ for some natural number $a$.

## Sources

- Alon Regev, *The Central Component of a Triangulation*, Journal of Integer Sequences 16 (2013), Article 13.4.1,
  Theorem 2, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL16/Regev/regev4.tex>.
