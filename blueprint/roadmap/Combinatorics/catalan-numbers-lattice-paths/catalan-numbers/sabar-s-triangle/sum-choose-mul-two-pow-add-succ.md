# Consecutive binomial sums weighted by powers of 2

For a natural number $m$, let
$V_m(2) = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{m+k}{k} 2^k$. For every natural number $n \ge 1$,
$V_n(2) + V_{n+1}(2) = 3 \cdot 2^n \binom{2n+2}{n+1}$.
