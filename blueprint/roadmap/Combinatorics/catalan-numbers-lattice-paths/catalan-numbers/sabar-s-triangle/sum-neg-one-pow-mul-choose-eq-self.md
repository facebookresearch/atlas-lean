# Sabar's alternating binomial sum equal to $n$

For every natural number $n \ge 2$, in the
rational numbers,

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^{k+1} \left(1 - \frac{2k}{n+1} + \frac{n}{k+1}\right) \binom{n+k}{k} = n.$$

## Sources

- Sabar Taoufik, *A New Triangle of Numbers: Properties and Observations*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A27, Corollary 2 (`integers-2026-A27-cor-2`).
