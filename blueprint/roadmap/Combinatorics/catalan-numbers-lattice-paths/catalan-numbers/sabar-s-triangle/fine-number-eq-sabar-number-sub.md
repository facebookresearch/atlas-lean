# Fine numbers from Sabar's triangle

For every natural number $n \ge 2$, the Fine number
$F(n)$ is the difference of two entries of row $2n$ of Sabar's triangle $s$:
$F(n) = s(2n, n+1) - s(2n, n-1)$.

## Sources

- Sabar Taoufik, *A New Triangle of Numbers: Properties and Observations*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A27, Theorem 4 (`integers-2026-A27-thm-4`).
