# Surjective order-preserving maps

$e_P(s)$ is the number of surjective order-preserving maps $P \to [s]$. For $s = |P|$ these are the
linear extensions of $P$. Choosing first the image of an order-preserving map $P \to [m]$ gives
$\Omega_P(m) = \sum_s e_P(s) \binom{m}{s}$.

## Sources

- R. P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Section 3.15.3, $e_{P,\omega}(s)$ for a natural labelling $\omega$, after reversing $[s]$.
