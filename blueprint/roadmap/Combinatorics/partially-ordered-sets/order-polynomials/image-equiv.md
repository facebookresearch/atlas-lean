# Order-preserving maps with a given image

For $S \subseteq [m]$, composing with the increasing enumeration $[|S|] \to S$ is a bijection from
the surjective order-preserving maps $P \to [|S|]$ to the order-preserving maps $P \to [m]$ with
image exactly $S$.

## Sources

- R. P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Section 3.15.3 ("by first choosing the image").
