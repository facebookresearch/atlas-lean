# Reciprocity theorem for order polynomials

The order polynomial $\Omega_P$, which counts order-preserving maps $P \to [m]$, and the count
$\bar\Omega_P(m)$ of strictly order-preserving maps $P \to [m]$ are related by

$$\bar\Omega_P(m) = (-1)^{p}\, \Omega_P(-m) \qquad (m \in \mathbb{N}),$$

with $p = |P|$. For the two-element chain, $\Omega_P(m) = \binom{m+1}{2}$ and
$\bar\Omega_P(m) = \binom{m}{2} = \binom{-m+1}{2}$. Stanley proves it for every labelling $\omega$,
relating $(P, \omega)$- and $(P, \bar\omega)$-partitions; this is the case of a natural labelling.

## Sources

- R. P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Corollary 3.15.12.
