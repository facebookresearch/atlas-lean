# Neggers' conjecture

For every $n$ and every relation $R$ on the labels
$\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$ that is naturally labeled, meaning that $R(i, j)$ implies $i < j$, every
complex zero of the $W$-polynomial $W(P, x)$ of the poset $P$ generated by $R$ is real.

This is Stanley, Positivity Problems, Problem 20, for naturally labeled posets.
