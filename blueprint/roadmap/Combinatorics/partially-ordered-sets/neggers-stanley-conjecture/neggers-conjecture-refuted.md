# Neggers' conjecture is false

There exist $n$ and a naturally labeled relation $R$ on the
labels $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, one in which $R(i, j)$ implies $i < j$, such that the
$W$-polynomial $W(P, x)$ of the poset $P$ generated by $R$ has a complex zero that is not real.

Stembridge found naturally labeled posets on $17$ elements whose $W$-polynomials have non-real
zeros, disproving Neggers' conjecture and hence the Neggers–Stanley conjecture (Brändén had
earlier given counterexamples with non-natural labelings).
