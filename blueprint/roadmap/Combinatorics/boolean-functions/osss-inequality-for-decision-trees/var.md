# Variance under a product weight

For a finite set $E$, weights $\nu = (\nu_e)_{e \in E}$ with $\nu_e : \{0, 1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $g : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$, the variance of $g$ is $\mathrm{Var}_\nu(g) = \mathrm{Cov}_\nu(g, g) = \mathbb{E}_\nu[g^2] - \mathbb{E}_\nu[g]^2$, with the [covariance](cov.md) and [expectation](expect.md) taken under the product weight $\nu$.
