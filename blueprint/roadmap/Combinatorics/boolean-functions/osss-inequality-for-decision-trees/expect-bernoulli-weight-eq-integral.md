# Bernoulli product weight computes product-measure integrals

Let $E$ be a finite set and $0 \le p \le 1$. For every $g : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$, the [expectation](expect.md) of $g$ under the [Bernoulli($p$) product weight](bernoulli-weight.md) equals $\int g\, d\mu_p$, where $\mu_p$ is the product probability measure on $\{0, 1\}^E$ whose coordinates are independent and equal to $1$ with probability $p$.
