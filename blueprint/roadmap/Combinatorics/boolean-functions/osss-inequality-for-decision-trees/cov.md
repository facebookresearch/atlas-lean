# Covariance under a product weight

For a finite set $E$, weights $\nu = (\nu_e)_{e \in E}$ with $\nu_e : \{0, 1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $f, g : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$, the covariance of $f$ and $g$ is

$$\mathrm{Cov}_\nu(f, g) = \mathbb{E}_\nu[f g] - \mathbb{E}_\nu[f]\, \mathbb{E}_\nu[g],$$

with $\mathbb{E}_\nu$ the [expectation](expect.md) under the product weight $\nu$.
