# Poincaré inequality for the Bernoulli product measure

Let $E$ be a finite set, $0 \le p \le 1$ and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$. Under the $p$-biased product measure,

$$\mathrm{Var}(f) \le \sum_{e \in E} \mathrm{Inf}_e(f),$$

where $\mathrm{Inf}_e(f)$ is the probability that $e$ is pivotal for $A$.
