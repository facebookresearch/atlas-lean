# Point-mass weight on the Booleans

For $b \in \{0, 1\}$, the point-mass weight at $b$ is the weight on $\{0, 1\}$ equal to $1$ at $b$ and $0$ at $1 - b$. Replacing the weight $\nu_e$ of a product weight by the point mass at $b$ is defined for all weights; when the weights are probability weights and $\nu_e(b) > 0$, it gives the conditional law of the product measure given $\omega_e = b$.
