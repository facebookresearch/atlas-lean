# Weight of a configuration under a product weight

For a finite set $E$, weights $\nu = (\nu_e)_{e \in E}$ with $\nu_e : \{0, 1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$, the weight of $\omega$ is $\prod_{e \in E} \nu_e(\omega_e)$. When $\nu$ is a [product probability weight](is-prob-weight.md) it is the probability of $\omega$ under the product measure.
