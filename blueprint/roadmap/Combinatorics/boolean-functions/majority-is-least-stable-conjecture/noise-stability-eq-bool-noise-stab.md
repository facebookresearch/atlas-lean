# The two noise-stability definitions agree

For every $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ and every real $\rho$, the Fourier sum $\sum_{S \subseteq [n]} \rho^{|S|} \hat f(S)^2$ equals the correlation $\mathbb{E}[f \cdot T_\rho f]$, the [noise stability of $f$ on the Boolean cube](../../../Analysis/harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/influences-and-the-noise-operator-on-the-boolean/bool-noise-stab.md). The proof is the [Fourier formula for noise stability](../../../Analysis/harmonic-analysis/discrete-fourier-analysis/influences-and-the-noise-operator-on-the-boolean/bool-noise-stab-eq-sum.md).
