# At most half of field pairs have dot product one

Let $F$ be a finite field that is an algebra over $\mathbb{F}_2$, let $s$ and $m$ be natural numbers, and let $\beta : F \to \mathbb{F}_2^s$ be an $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear isomorphism. For every natural number $E < m$,

$$2\, \#\{(a, b) \in F^2 : \beta(a^E) \cdot \beta(b) = 1\} \le \lvert F\rvert^2,$$

where $u \cdot v = \sum_i u_i v_i \in \mathbb{F}_2$ and $a^0 = 1$: at most half of all pairs have dot product $1$.
