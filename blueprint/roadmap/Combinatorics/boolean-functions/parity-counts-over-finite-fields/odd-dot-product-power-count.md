# Lower bound on odd-parity pairs in a finite field

Let $F$ be a finite field that is an algebra over $\mathbb{F}_2$, let $s$ and $m$ be natural numbers, and let $\beta : F \to \mathbb{F}_2^s$ be an $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear isomorphism. For every nonempty set $V \subseteq \{0, 1, \dots, m-1\}$ of exponents, the number $N$ of pairs $(a, b) \in F^2$ for which an odd number of the dot products $\beta(a^E) \cdot \beta(b)$, $E \in V$, equal $1$ satisfies

$$\lvert F\rvert^2 \le 2N + (m - 1)\lvert F\rvert,$$

where $u \cdot v = \sum_i u_i v_i \in \mathbb{F}_2$.
