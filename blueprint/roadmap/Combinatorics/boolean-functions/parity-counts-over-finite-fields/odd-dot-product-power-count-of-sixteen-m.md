# At least 15/32 of field pairs have odd parity

Let $F$ be a finite field that is an algebra over $\mathbb{F}_2$, let $s$ and $m$ be natural numbers with $16m \le 2^s$, and let $\beta : F \to \mathbb{F}_2^s$ be an $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear isomorphism. For every nonempty set $V \subseteq \{0, 1, \dots, m-1\}$ of exponents,

$$15\, \lvert F\rvert^2 \le 32\, \#\big\{(a, b) \in F^2 : \#\{E \in V : \beta(a^E) \cdot \beta(b) = 1\} \text{ is odd}\big\},$$

where $u \cdot v = \sum_i u_i v_i \in \mathbb{F}_2$: at least $15/32$ of the pairs have odd parity across $V$.
