# Number of unate $k$-SAT functions

For natural numbers $k$ and $n$, the number of Boolean
functions $f : \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^n \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$ that are
$k$-SAT functions on $n$ variables and are unate.

## Sources

- Abstract *Enumerating k-SAT functions*, in M. Krivelevich, R. Morris, O. Riordan and A. Steger (organisers), *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing*, Oberwolfach Rep. 19 (2022), no. 2, 1165–1237, p. 1222, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2022/22; D. Dong, N. Mani and Y. Zhao, *Enumerating k-SAT functions*, arXiv:2107.09233: typical structure (unate).
