# Counting self-dual monotone functions on n + 2 variables

For every natural number $n$, the number $\lambda_{n+2}$ of self-dual monotone Boolean functions on $\{0,1\}^{n+2}$ (monotone and equal to their dual) equals

$$\sum_{b} \bigl|\{z : z \text{ monotone},\ b \lor b^* \le z\}\bigr|,$$

the sum over all monotone Boolean functions $b$ on $\{0,1\}^n$, with $z$ ranging over Boolean functions on $\{0,1\}^n$ and $\lor$ the pointwise disjunction. Order $\{0,1\}$ by $0 < 1$, inputs in $\{0,1\}^n$ coordinatewise and Boolean functions pointwise; $\bar x$ is the coordinatewise complement of $x$ and $f^*(x) = \lnot f(\bar x)$ is the dual of a Boolean function $f$ on $\{0,1\}^n$.

## Sources

- Bartłomiej Pawelski and Andrzej Szepietowski, *Counting Self-Dual Monotone Boolean Functions*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.6.5. Theorem 10.
