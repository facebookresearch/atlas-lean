# Self-dual monotone concatenations

Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers. Order $\{0,1\}$ by $0 < 1$, inputs coordinatewise and Boolean functions pointwise; let $f^*(x) = \lnot f(\bar x)$ be the dual of $f$, where $\bar x$ is the coordinatewise complement of $x$, and say that a Boolean function is self-dual monotone when it is monotone and equals its dual. For a finite list $W = (W_0, \dots, W_{m-1})$ of Boolean functions on $\{0,1\}^n$, its concatenation $W_0 \cdot W_1 \cdots W_{m-1}$ on $n + k$ variables sends $(x, y)$, with $x \in \{0,1\}^n$ and $y \in \{0,1\}^k$, to $W_{\iota(y)}(x)$, where $\iota(y) = \sum_{j < k} y_j 2^j$; here and below $W_i$ denotes the constant function $0$ when $i \ge m$. Then the concatenation of $W$ on $n + k$ variables is self-dual monotone if and only if: (a) $W_i$ is monotone for every $i < 2^k$; (b) $W_i \le W_{i + 2^j}$ for all $i < 2^k$ and $j < k$ such that bit $j$ of $i$ is $0$; and (c) $W_{2^k - 1 - i} = (W_i)^*$ for every $i < 2^k$.
