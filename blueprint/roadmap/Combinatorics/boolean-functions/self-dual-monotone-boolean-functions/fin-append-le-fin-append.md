# Appending tuples is monotone

Let $\alpha$ be a preordered type, let $x, x' \in \alpha^m$ with $x \le x'$ and $y, y' \in \alpha^n$ with $y \le y'$, coordinatewise. Then $(x_0, \dots, x_{m-1}, y_0, \dots, y_{n-1}) \le (x'_0, \dots, x'_{m-1}, y'_0, \dots, y'_{n-1})$ coordinatewise in $\alpha^{m+n}$.
