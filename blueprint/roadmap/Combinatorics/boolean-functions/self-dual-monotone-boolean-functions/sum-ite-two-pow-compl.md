# Binary index of the complement

Let $k$ be a natural number and $y \in \{0,1\}^k$, with coordinatewise complement $\bar y$. Then $\iota(\bar y) = 2^k - 1 - \iota(y)$, where the binary index of $y \in \{0,1\}^k$ is $\iota(y) = \sum_{j < k} y_j 2^j$, with $y_j \in \{0, 1\}$. The subtraction is in the natural numbers; it is exact since $\iota(y) \le 2^k - 1$.
