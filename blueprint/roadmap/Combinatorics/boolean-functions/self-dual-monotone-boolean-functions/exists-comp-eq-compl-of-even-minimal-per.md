# Permutations with even orbits admit an alternating Boolean labelling

Let $\alpha$ be a finite type and $\pi$ a permutation of $\alpha$ such that every point has even minimal period under $\pi$, that is, every orbit of $\pi$ has even size. Then there is a function $x \colon \alpha \to \{0,1\}$ with $x(\pi(i)) = \lnot x(i)$ for every $i \in \alpha$.
