# Complement of an appended tuple

Let $\alpha$ be a type with a unary complement operation $a \mapsto a^c$, applied coordinatewise to tuples. For $x \in \alpha^m$ and $y \in \alpha^n$, the complement of the appended tuple $(x_0, \dots, x_{m-1}, y_0, \dots, y_{n-1})$ is the appended tuple $(x_0^c, \dots, x_{m-1}^c, y_0^c, \dots, y_{n-1}^c)$.
