# Bits of the complement index

Let $k$ and $i$ be natural numbers with $i < 2^k$. Then for every $m < k$, bit $m$ of $2^k - 1 - i$ is the negation of bit $m$ of $i$, where bit $j$ of a natural number $i$ is the coefficient of $2^j$ in the binary expansion of $i$. The subtraction is in the natural numbers; it is exact since $i \le 2^k - 1$.
