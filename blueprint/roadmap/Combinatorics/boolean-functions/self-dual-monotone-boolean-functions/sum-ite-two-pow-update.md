# Switching on a coordinate adds a power of two to the binary index

Let $k$ be a natural number, $y \in \{0,1\}^k$ and $j < k$ with $y_j = 0$, and let $y'$ be $y$ with coordinate $j$ set to $1$. Then $\iota(y') = \iota(y) + 2^j$, where the binary index of $y \in \{0,1\}^k$ is $\iota(y) = \sum_{j < k} y_j 2^j$, with $y_j \in \{0, 1\}$.
