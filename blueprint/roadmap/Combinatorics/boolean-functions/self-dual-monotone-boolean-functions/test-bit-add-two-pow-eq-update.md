# Bits after adding a power of two at a clear bit

Let $k$ and $i$ be natural numbers with $i < 2^k$, and let $j < k$ be such that bit $j$ of $i$ is $0$, where bit $j$ of a natural number $i$ is the coefficient of $2^j$ in the binary expansion of $i$. Then for every $m < k$, bit $m$ of $i + 2^j$ is $1$ if $m = j$ and equals bit $m$ of $i$ otherwise.
