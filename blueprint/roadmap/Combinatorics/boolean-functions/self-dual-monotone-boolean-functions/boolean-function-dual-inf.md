# Dual of a conjunction of Boolean functions

For all Boolean functions $f$ and $g$ on $\{0,1\}^n$, not necessarily monotone, $(f \land g)^* = f^* \lor g^*$, where $\lor$ and $\land$ are pointwise disjunction and conjunction and $f^*(x) = \lnot f(\bar x)$ is the dual of $f$, with $\bar x$ the coordinatewise complement of $x$.
