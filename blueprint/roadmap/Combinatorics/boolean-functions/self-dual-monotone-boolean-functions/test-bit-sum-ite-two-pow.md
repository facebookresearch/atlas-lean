# Bits of a binary index

Let $k$ be a natural number, $y \in \{0,1\}^k$ and $j < k$, where the binary index of $y \in \{0,1\}^k$ is $\iota(y) = \sum_{j < k} y_j 2^j$, with $y_j \in \{0, 1\}$. Then bit $j$ of $\iota(y)$ is $y_j$, where bit $j$ of a natural number $i$ is the coefficient of $2^j$ in the binary expansion of $i$.
