# Total influence at $p = 1/2$ via $p$-biased Fourier coefficients

Let $E$ be a finite set and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, with [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](../../../../Probability/concentration-inequalities/bonami-beckner-hypercontractivity/pbiased-tensorize/ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$ at $p = 1/2$. Its total [influence](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/infl.md) under the uniform measure is

$$\sum_{e \in E} \mathrm{Inf}_e = \sum_{S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, \hat f(S)^2.$$
