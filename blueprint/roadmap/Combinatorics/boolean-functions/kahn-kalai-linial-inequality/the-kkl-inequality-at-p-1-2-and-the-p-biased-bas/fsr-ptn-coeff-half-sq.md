# Squared $p$-biased and Walsh coefficients agree at $p = 1/2$

Let $E$ be a finite set, $f : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$ and $S \subseteq E$. The square of the [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](../../../../Probability/concentration-inequalities/bonami-beckner-hypercontractivity/pbiased-tensorize/ptn-coeff.md) of $f$ at $p = 1/2$ equals the square of its Walsh–Fourier coefficient $2^{-\lvert E\rvert} \sum_x f(x) \prod_{i \in S} (-1)^{x_i}$.
