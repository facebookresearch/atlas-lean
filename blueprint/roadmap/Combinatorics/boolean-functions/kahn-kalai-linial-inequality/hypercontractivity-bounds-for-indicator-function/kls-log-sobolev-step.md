# Indicator-bound form of KKL implies the KKL inequality at $p = 1/2$

For every real number $c$: if the [Fourier-form KKL inequality conditional on the indicator Bonami–Beckner bound](kls-log-optimisation.md) holds with constant $c$, then the [KKL inequality with constant $c$](../the-kkl-inequality-statement-and-elementary-boun/kklhypercontractive.md) holds for the uniform measure ($p = 1/2$).
