# Poincaré inequality in Fourier form

Let $E$ be a finite set and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, with Walsh–Fourier coefficients $\hat f(S)$ under the uniform measure. Then

$$\sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq E} \hat f(S)^2 \le \sum_{S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, \hat f(S)^2.$$

The left side is the variance of $f$ and the right side its total influence, so this is the Poincaré inequality on the cube.
