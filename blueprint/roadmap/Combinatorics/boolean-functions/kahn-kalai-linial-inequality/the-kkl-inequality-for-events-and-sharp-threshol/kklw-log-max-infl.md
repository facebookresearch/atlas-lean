# Logarithm of the reciprocal maximal influence of an event

For a finite nonempty set $E$, a real number $p$ and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, this is

$$\log \frac{1}{\max_{e \in E} \mathrm{Inf}_e(1_A)},$$

where $\mathrm{Inf}_e(1_A)$ is the [influence](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/infl.md) of the indicator of $A$ under the product weight $w_p(1) = p$, $w_p(0) = 1 - p$, and the logarithm is extended by $\log(1/0) = 0$ and $\log x = \log \lvert x\rvert$ for $x < 0$ (for $p \notin [0, 1]$ the weights, and so the influences, can be negative).
