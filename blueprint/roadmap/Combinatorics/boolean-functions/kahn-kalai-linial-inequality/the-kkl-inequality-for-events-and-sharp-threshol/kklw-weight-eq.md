# Weight of a configuration under the $p$-biased product weights

For a finite set $E$, a real number $p$ and $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$, the [product weight](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/weight.md) of $\omega$ for the coordinate weights $w_p(1) = p$, $w_p(0) = 1 - p$ is

$$\prod_{e \in E} w_p(\omega_e) = p^{k} (1 - p)^{\lvert E\rvert - k},$$

where $k$ is the number of coordinates of $\omega$ equal to $1$; for $0 \le p \le 1$ it is the probability of $\omega$ under independent Bernoulli($p$) coordinates.
