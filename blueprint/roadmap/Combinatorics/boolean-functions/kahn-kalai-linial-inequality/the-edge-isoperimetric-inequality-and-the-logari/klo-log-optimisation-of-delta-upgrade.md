# Measure–influence form of KKL implies the indicator-bound form

For every real number $c$: if the [Fourier-form KKL inequality conditional on the measure–influence bound](klo-delta-upgrade.md) holds with constant $c$, then so does the [Fourier-form KKL inequality conditional on the indicator Bonami–Beckner bound](../hypercontractivity-bounds-for-indicator-function/kls-log-optimisation.md).
