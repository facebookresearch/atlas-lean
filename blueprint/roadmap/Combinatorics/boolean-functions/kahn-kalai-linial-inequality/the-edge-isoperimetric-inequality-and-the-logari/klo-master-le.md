# Bound on $m - m^{2/(1+\rho^2)}$ by total influence

Let $E$ be a finite set, $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, $m = \mathbb{E} f$ its mean under the uniform measure and $\hat f(S)$ its Walsh–Fourier coefficients. For every $\rho \in [0, 1]$,

$$m - m^{2/(1 + \rho^2)} \le \frac{1 - \rho^2}{4} \sum_{S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, \hat f(S)^2,$$

where the sum is the total influence of $A$.
