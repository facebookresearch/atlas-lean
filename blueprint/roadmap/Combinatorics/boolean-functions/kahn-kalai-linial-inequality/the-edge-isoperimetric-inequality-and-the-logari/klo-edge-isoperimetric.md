# Edge-isoperimetric inequality

Let $E$ be a finite set, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ and $f = 1_A$, with mean $m = \lvert A\rvert / 2^{\lvert E\rvert}$ under the uniform measure and Walsh–Fourier coefficients $\hat f(S)$. If $m > 0$, then

$$2 m \log \frac{1}{m} \le \sum_{S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, \hat f(S)^2,$$

and the right side is the total influence of $A$ (S. Hart, *A note on the edges of the n-cube*, Discrete Math. 1976). Here $\log$ is the natural logarithm, so this is weaker by the factor $\log 2$ than the sharp bound $2 m \log_2(1/m)$.
