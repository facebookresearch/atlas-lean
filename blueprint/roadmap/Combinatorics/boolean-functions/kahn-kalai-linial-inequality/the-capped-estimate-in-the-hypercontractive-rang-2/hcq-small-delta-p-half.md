# Capped estimate at $p = 1/2$ for all $u \in (0, 1)$

Let $E$ be a finite nonempty set and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, with [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](../../../../Probability/concentration-inequalities/bonami-beckner-hypercontractivity/pbiased-tensorize/ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$ at $p = 1/2$, and let $\delta$ and $I$ be the maximal and total [influence](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/infl.md) under the uniform measure. For every $u \in (0, 1)$,

$$\sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, (1 - u)^{\lvert S\rvert - 1}\, \hat f(S)^2 \le \delta^{u/(2-u)}\, I.$$
