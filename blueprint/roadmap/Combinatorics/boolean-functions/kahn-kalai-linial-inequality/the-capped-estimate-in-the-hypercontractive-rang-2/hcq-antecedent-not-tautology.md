# Low-range case condition fails for some $u$

Let $1/2 < p < 1$ and $0 < \delta < 1$, and put $q^*(u) = 1 + (1 - u)/(4p(1-p))$. Then there is $u \in (0, 1)$ with $u \ge 1 - 4p(1-p)$ and

$$\delta^{\frac{u}{2-u} - \left(\frac{2}{q^*(u)} - 1\right)} \ge 4p(1-p),$$

so the case condition of the [low-range residual case](hcq-low-range-residue.md) fails at this $u$.
