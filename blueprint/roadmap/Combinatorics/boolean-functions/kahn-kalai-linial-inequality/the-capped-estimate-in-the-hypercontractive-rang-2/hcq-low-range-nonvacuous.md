# Right side of the capped estimate is nonnegative

Let $0 \le p \le 1$, let $E$ be a finite nonempty set and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, and let $\delta$ and $I$ be the maximal and total [influence](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/infl.md) of $f$ under the $p$-biased product measure. For every $u \in (0, 1)$, $\delta^{u/(2-u)}\, I \ge 0$.
