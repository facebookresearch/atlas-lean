# Under the gap condition the hypercontractive bound is dominated

Let $p$, $\delta$, $q'$ and $u$ be real numbers with $\delta > 0$, let $\sigma = \sqrt{p(1-p)}$ (so $\sigma = 0$ if $p(1-p) < 0$), and read quotients by $0$ as $0$. If

$$4\sigma^2 \le \delta^{\frac{u}{2-u} - \left(\frac{2}{q'} - 1\right)},$$

then $4\sigma^2\, \delta^{2/q' - 1} \le \delta^{u/(2-u)}$.
