# Gap condition holds at some $u < 1$ in the hypercontractive range

Let $1/2 < p < 1$ and $0 < \delta < 1$, and put $q^*(u) = 1 + (1 - u)/(4p(1-p))$. Then there is $u \in (0, 1)$ in the hypercontractive range $u \ge 1 - 4p(1-p)$ satisfying the gap condition

$$4p(1-p) \le \delta^{\frac{u}{2-u} - \left(\frac{2}{q^*(u)} - 1\right)}.$$
