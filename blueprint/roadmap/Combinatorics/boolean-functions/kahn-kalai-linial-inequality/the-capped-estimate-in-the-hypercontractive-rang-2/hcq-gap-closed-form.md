# Closed form of the exponent gap

For real numbers $s$ and $u$ with $s > 0$, $u < 1$ and $s + 1 - u > 0$,

$$\frac{u}{2 - u} - \left(\frac{2}{1 + (1 - u)/s} - 1\right) = \frac{2(1 - u)(1 - s)}{(s + 1 - u)(2 - u)}.$$

With $s = 4p(1-p)$ the left side is the gap between the target exponent $u/(2-u)$ and the hypercontractive exponent $2/q' - 1$.
