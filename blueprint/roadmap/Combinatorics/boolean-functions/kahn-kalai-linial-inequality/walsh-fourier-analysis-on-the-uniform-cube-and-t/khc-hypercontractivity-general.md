# Bonami–Beckner hypercontractive inequality

Let $E$ be a finite set and $0 \le \rho \le 1$. For every $g : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\lVert T_\rho g\rVert_2 \le \lVert g\rVert_{1 + \rho^2},$$

where $\lVert h\rVert_r = (\mathbb{E}\lvert h\rvert^r)^{1/r}$ under the uniform measure and $T_\rho g(x) = \sum_y \prod_{i \in E} \kappa_i(x, y)\, g(y)$ with $\kappa_i = (1 + \rho)/2$ if $x_i = y_i$ and $(1 - \rho)/2$ otherwise (A. Bonami, *Étude des coefficients de Fourier des fonctions de $L^p(G)$*, Ann. Inst. Fourier 1970; W. Beckner, *Inequalities in Fourier analysis*, Ann. of Math. 1975).
