# Reindexing a Walsh character reindexes its coordinate set

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be sets, $e : \iota \to \kappa$ a bijection and $S$ a finite subset of $\iota$. Transporting the [Walsh character](khc-walsh-char.md) $\chi_S$ to $\{0, 1\}^\kappa$ along $e$, that is, taking $y \mapsto \chi_S(y \circ e)$, gives $\chi_{e(S)}$.
