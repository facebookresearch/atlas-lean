# Fourier–Walsh coefficient under the uniform measure

For a finite set $E$, $f : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$ and $S \subseteq E$, the Walsh–Fourier coefficient of $f$ at $S$ is

$$\hat f(S) = \mathbb{E}[f \chi_S] = 2^{-\lvert E\rvert} \sum_{x \in \{0, 1\}^E} f(x)\, \chi_S(x),$$

where $\chi_S(x) = \prod_{i \in S} (-1)^{x_i}$ is the [Walsh character](khc-walsh-char.md) and $\mathbb{E}$ is the expectation under the uniform measure.
