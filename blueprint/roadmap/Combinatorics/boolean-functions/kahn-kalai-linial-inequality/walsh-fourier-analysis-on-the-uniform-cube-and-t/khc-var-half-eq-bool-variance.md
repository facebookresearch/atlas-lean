# Two expressions of the uniform variance agree

For every natural number $n$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, the [variance](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/var.md) of $f$ for the product weights $w(0) = w(1) = 1/2$ equals $\mathbb{E}\big[(f - \mathbb{E} f)^2\big]$, with $\mathbb{E}$ the uniform average over $\{0, 1\}^n$.
