# Fourier coefficients are invariant under reindexing

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets, $e : \iota \to \kappa$ a bijection, $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and $S \subseteq \iota$, and let $f^e(y) = f(y \circ e)$ for $y \in \{0, 1\}^\kappa$. Then the [Walsh–Fourier coefficients](khc-fourier-coeff.md) satisfy $\widehat{f^e}(e(S)) = \hat f(S)$.
