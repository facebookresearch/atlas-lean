# KKL inequality at $p = 1/2$ from the conditional statement

For every real number $c$: if the [Fourier form of the KKL inequality](bph-rho-optimise.md) holds with constant $c$, then the [KKL inequality with constant $c$](../the-kkl-inequality-statement-and-elementary-boun/kklhypercontractive.md) holds for the uniform measure on the cube ($p = 1/2$).
