# Convexity of the damped total weight

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p$ a real number, and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, with [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](../../../../Probability/concentration-inequalities/bonami-beckner-hypercontractivity/pbiased-tensorize/ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$. The damped total weight

$$u \mapsto \sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, (1 - u)^{\lvert S\rvert - 1}\, \hat f(S)^2$$

is convex on $[0, 1]$.
