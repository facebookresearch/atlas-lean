# Maximal influence of a Boolean function is at most one

Let $E$ be a finite nonempty set, $0 \le p \le 1$ and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$. Its maximal [influence](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/infl.md) under the $p$-biased product measure satisfies $\max_{e \in E} \mathrm{Inf}_e \le 1$.
