# Integral of damped total-influence weights over the noise parameter

Let $E$ be a finite set and $c$ a real-valued function on the subsets of $E$. Then

$$\int_0^1 \sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq E} 4 \lvert S\rvert\, (1 - u)^{\lvert S\rvert - 1}\, c(S)\, du = 4 \sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq E} c(S).$$
