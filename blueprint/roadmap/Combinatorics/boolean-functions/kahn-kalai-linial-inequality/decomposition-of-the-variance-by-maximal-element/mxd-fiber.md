# Nonempty subsets with a given maximal element

For a finite nonempty linearly ordered set $E$ and $e \in E$, the fiber $\mathcal{F}_e$ of $e$ is the family of nonempty subsets $S \subseteq E$ whose largest element is $e$. The fibers $\mathcal{F}_e$, $e \in E$, partition the nonempty subsets of $E$.
