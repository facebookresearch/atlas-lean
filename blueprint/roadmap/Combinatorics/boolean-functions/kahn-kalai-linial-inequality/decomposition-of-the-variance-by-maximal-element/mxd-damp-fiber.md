# Noise-damped fiber weight

Let $E$ be a finite nonempty linearly ordered set, $p$ and $\rho$ real numbers, and $f = 1_A$ the indicator of a set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, with [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](../../../../Probability/concentration-inequalities/bonami-beckner-hypercontractivity/pbiased-tensorize/ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$. The noise-damped fiber weight of $e \in E$ is

$$D_e(\rho) = \sum_{S \in \mathcal{F}_e} \rho^{2\lvert S\rvert}\, \hat f(S)^2,$$

where $\mathcal{F}_e$ is the [fiber](mxd-fiber.md) of $e$: the nonempty subsets of $E$ whose largest element is $e$.
