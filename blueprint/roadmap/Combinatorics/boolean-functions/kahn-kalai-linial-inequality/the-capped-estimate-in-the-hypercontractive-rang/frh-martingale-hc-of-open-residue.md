# Open-interval case implies the full capped estimate on $(1/2, q)$

For every real number $q$, the [open-interval residual case](frh-open-residue.md) for $q$ implies the [capped estimate on $(1/2, q)$](../decomposition-of-the-variance-by-maximal-element/mxd-martingale-hc-statement.md): the endpoints $u = 0$ and $u = 1$ always satisfy the estimate.
