# Some truncation level gives a nonnegative budget

Let $0 < p < 1$, $0 < u < 1$ and let $\delta$ be a real number with $\delta^{u/(2-u)} > 0$ ($x^s$ is the real power; for $x < 0$ it is $\lvert x\rvert^s \cos(\pi s)$). Then there is a natural number $k$ with

$$4p(1-p)(1 - u)^k \le \delta^{u/(2-u)}.$$
