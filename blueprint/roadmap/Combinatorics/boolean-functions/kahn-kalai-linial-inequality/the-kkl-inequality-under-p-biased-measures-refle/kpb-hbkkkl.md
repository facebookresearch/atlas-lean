# KKL hypothesis on $(1/2, q)$ restated on the interior of the closed interval

Let $q$ be a real number. If the [KKL inequality with constant $2$](../the-kkl-inequality-statement-and-elementary-boun/kklhypercontractive.md) holds at every $p \in (1/2, q)$, then it holds at every $p$ in the interior of the closed interval $\{x : 1/2 \le x \le q\}$.
