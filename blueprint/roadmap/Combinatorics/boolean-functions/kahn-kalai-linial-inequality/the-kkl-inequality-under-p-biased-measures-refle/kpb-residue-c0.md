# KKL inequality with constant 0 holds trivially on $(1/2, q)$

For every real number $q \le 1$, the [KKL inequality](../the-kkl-inequality-statement-and-elementary-boun/kklhypercontractive.md) with constant $0$ holds at every $p \in (1/2, q)$: it then states that the total influence, a sum of probabilities, is nonnegative.
