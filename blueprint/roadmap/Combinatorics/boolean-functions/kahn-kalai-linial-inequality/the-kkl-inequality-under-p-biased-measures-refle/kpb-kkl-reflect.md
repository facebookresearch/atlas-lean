# Reflection of the KKL inequality from $p$ to $1 - p$

For every real number $p$: if the [KKL inequality with constant $2$](../the-kkl-inequality-statement-and-elementary-boun/kklhypercontractive.md) holds at $p$, then it holds at $1 - p$. Complementing all coordinates exchanges the product weights for $p$ and $1 - p$ and preserves variances and influences.
