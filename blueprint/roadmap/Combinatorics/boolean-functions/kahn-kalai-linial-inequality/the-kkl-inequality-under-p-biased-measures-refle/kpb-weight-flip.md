# Complement swaps the Bernoulli $p$ and $1 - p$ weights

For a finite set $E$, a real number $p$ and $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$, the [product weight](../../osss-inequality-for-decision-trees/weight.md) of the complement $\bar\omega$ for the coordinate weights $w_{1-p}$ equals that of $\omega$ for $w_p$:

$$\prod_{e \in E} w_{1-p}(\bar\omega_e) = \prod_{e \in E} w_p(\omega_e), \qquad w_p(1) = p,\ w_p(0) = 1 - p.$$
