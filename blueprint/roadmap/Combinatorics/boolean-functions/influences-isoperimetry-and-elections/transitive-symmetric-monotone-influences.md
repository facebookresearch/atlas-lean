# Monotone transitive-symmetric functions have influences at most 1/√n

Let $f : \{-1,1\}^n \to \{-1,1\}$ be monotone and transitive-symmetric (its automorphism group, the coordinate permutations $\pi$ with $f^\pi = f$, acts transitively on $[n]$). Then every coordinate has $\mathbf{Inf}_i[f] \le 1/\sqrt n$. Majority shows the bound is tight up to the constant $\sqrt{2/\pi}$.

## Proof sketch

Transitivity makes all influences equal to $\mathbf{I}[f]/n$, and monotone functions have $\mathbf{I}[f] \le \sqrt n$.
