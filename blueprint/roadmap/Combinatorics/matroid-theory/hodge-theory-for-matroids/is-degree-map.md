# Degree map of a matroid of rank $r + 1$

A linear map $\deg : A^*(M)_\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a degree map for $r$ if it
vanishes on every graded piece $A^q(M)_\mathbb{R}$ with $q \ne r$ and satisfies

$$\deg(x_{F_1} x_{F_2} \cdots x_{F_r}) = 1$$

for every flag $F_1 \subsetneq F_2 \subsetneq \cdots \subsetneq F_r$ of nonempty proper
flats. For a loopless matroid of rank $r + 1$ these flags are the maximal ones, and the
monomials $x_{F_1} \cdots x_{F_r}$ correspond to the $r$-dimensional cones of the Bergman
fan, so on $A^r(M)_\mathbb{R}$ this is the normalisation by which Adiprasito, Huh and Katz
characterise their degree map. The condition off degree $r$ only extends it by zero, so that
it is a map on the whole ring.

- K. Adiprasito, J. Huh and E. Katz, *Hodge theory for combinatorial geometries*, Ann. of
