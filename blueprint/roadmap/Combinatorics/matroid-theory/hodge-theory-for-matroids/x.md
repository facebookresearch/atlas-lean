# The generator $x_F$ of the Chow ring

For a nonempty proper flat $F$ of $M$, $x_F$ is the class of the variable $x_F$ in the
Chow ring $A^*(M)_\mathbb{R}$.

## Sources

- K. Adiprasito, J. Huh and E. Katz, *Hodge theory for combinatorial geometries*, Ann. of
  Math. 188 (2018), 381–452, Definition 1.3, https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.02888.
