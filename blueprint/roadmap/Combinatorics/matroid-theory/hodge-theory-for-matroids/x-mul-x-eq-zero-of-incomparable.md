# Incomparable flats multiply to zero

If $F$ and $G$ are nonempty proper flats with $F \not\subseteq G$ and
$G \not\subseteq F$, then $x_F \, x_G = 0$ in the Chow ring. Consequently a nonzero
monomial in the $x_F$ is supported on a chain of flats.
