# Graded piece $A^q(M)_\mathbb{R}$ of the Chow ring

$A^q(M)_\mathbb{R}$ is the image in the Chow ring of the homogeneous polynomials of degree
$q$ in the variables $x_F$. Since $I_M + J_M$ is generated by homogeneous elements, these
subspaces form a grading of $A^*(M)_\mathbb{R}$. For a loopless matroid of rank $r + 1$,
$A^r(M)_\mathbb{R} \cong \mathbb{R}$ through the degree map.
