# The Chow ring $A^*(M)_\mathbb{R}$ of a matroid

The Chow ring of a matroid $M$ is

$$A^*(M)_\mathbb{R} = \mathbb{R}[x_F]_{F} \,/\, (I_M + J_M),$$

the polynomial ring with one variable for each nonempty proper flat, modulo the
incomparability relations $x_{F_1} x_{F_2} = 0$ and the linear relations
$\sum_{F \ni i_1} x_F = \sum_{F \ni i_2} x_F$.

The presentation is due to Feichtner and Yuzvinsky. When $M$ is realised by a hyperplane
arrangement, $A^*(M)_\mathbb{R}$ is the cohomology ring of the wonderful compactification of
De Concini and Procesi; in general it is the Chow ring of the smooth, non-compact toric variety
of the Bergman fan of $M$.
