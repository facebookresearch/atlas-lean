# Nonempty proper flats of a matroid

For a matroid $M$ on $E$, the nonempty proper flats are the flats $F$ of $M$ with $F \ne \emptyset$ and $F \ne E$. They index the variables $x_F$
of the Chow ring. For a loopless matroid $\emptyset$ is a flat, and these are the flats
strictly between the bottom and the top of the lattice of flats.
