# Hard Lefschetz theorem for matroids

Let $M$ be a finite loopless matroid of rank $r + 1$ and let $\ell = \ell(c)$ be the
class of a strictly submodular function $c$. For every integer $q$ with $2q \le r$,
multiplication by $\ell^{r-2q}$ is a bijection

$$A^q(M)_\mathbb{R} \longrightarrow A^{r-q}(M)_\mathbb{R}, \qquad a \longmapsto \ell^{r-2q} a.$$

Equivalently, every $b \in A^{r-q}(M)_\mathbb{R}$ equals $\ell^{r-2q} a$ for exactly one
$a \in A^q(M)_\mathbb{R}$.

This is part (1) of the main theorem of Adiprasito, Huh and Katz. The proof follows McMullen's
inductive proof of the hard Lefschetz theorem for simple polytopes: it moves from the
Bergman fan of a smaller order filter to a larger one by "matroidal flips", and proves the hard
Lefschetz theorem and the Hodge–Riemann relations together (Theorem 8.8), deducing the former
from local Hodge–Riemann relations at each step.
