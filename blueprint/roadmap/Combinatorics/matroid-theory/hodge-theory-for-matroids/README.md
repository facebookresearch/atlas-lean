# Hodge theory for matroids

Adiprasito, Huh and Katz attach to every matroid $M$ a graded ring $A^*(M)_\mathbb{R}$, the Chow
ring, which for a matroid realised by a hyperplane arrangement is the cohomology ring of the
wonderful compactification of the arrangement complement. They prove that it satisfies Poincaré
duality, the hard Lefschetz theorem and the Hodge–Riemann relations, although for a
non-realizable matroid there is no variety behind it. The coefficients of the reduced
characteristic polynomial are mixed degrees in this ring, and the Hodge–Riemann relations in
degree at most one make them log-concave: this is the proof of the Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture.
