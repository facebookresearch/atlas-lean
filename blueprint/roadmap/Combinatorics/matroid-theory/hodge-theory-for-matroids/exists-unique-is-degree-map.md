# The degree map of a matroid

For a finite loopless matroid $M$ of rank $r + 1$ there is exactly one linear map
$\deg : A^*(M)_\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ that vanishes off degree $r$ and takes the
value $1$ on $x_{F_1} \cdots x_{F_r}$ for every flag of nonempty proper flats
$F_1 \subsetneq \cdots \subsetneq F_r$.

Adiprasito, Huh and Katz define $\deg$ as the cap product with the constant Minkowski weight
on the Bergman fan (Definition 5.9), observe that it is determined by the normalisation, and
prove that it is an isomorphism (Proposition 5.10): all maximal flag monomials are equal to
$\alpha_M^r$ in $A^r(M)$ (Proposition 5.8).
