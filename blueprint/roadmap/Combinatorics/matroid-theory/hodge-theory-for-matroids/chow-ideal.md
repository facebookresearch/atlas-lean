# The Chow ideal $I_M + J_M$ of a matroid

In the polynomial ring $S_M = \mathbb{R}[x_F]$, with one variable for each nonempty proper
flat $F$ of $M$, the ideal $I_M$ is generated by the monomials $x_{F_1} x_{F_2}$ for
incomparable flats $F_1, F_2$, and the ideal $J_M$ by the linear forms

$$\sum_{F \ni i_1} x_F - \sum_{F \ni i_2} x_F$$

for distinct elements $i_1, i_2$ of the ground set. This is the ideal $I_M + J_M$.

For a finite matroid these sums are finite; for an infinite matroid a sum with infinitely many nonzero terms is taken to be $0$.
