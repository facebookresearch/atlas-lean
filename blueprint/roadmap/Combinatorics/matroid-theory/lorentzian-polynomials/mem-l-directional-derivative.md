# Directional derivatives in nonnegative directions

If $f$ lies in $L^{d+1}_n$ and $a_1, \dots, a_n \ge 0$, then
$\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i \, \partial_i f$ lies in $L^{d}_n$.

This is Brändén and Huh's Corollary 2.11.
