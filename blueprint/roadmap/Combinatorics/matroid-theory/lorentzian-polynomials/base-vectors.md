# Base vectors of a matroid

For a matroid $M$ on $\iota$, this is the set
$\{e_B : B \text{ a finite base of } M\} \subseteq \mathbb{N}^\iota$ of indicator vectors of
its bases. For a matroid on $[n]$ it is the support of the basis generating polynomial
$\sum_{B} w^B$, the polynomial that Brändén and Huh show to be Lorentzian.

## Sources

- P. Brändén and J. Huh, *Lorentzian polynomials*, Ann. of Math. 192 (2020), 821–891,
  Section 3.2 and Theorem 3.10 (8), https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03719.
