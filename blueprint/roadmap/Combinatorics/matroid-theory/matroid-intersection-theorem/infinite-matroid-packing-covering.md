# Packing and covering on complementary parts of a matroid ground set

For matroids $M_0,M_1$ on the same ground set $E$, a packing-covering partition is a disjoint partition $E=P\sqcup C$. Require disjoint $S_0,S_1\subseteq P$ with each $S_i$ spanning the restriction $M_i|P$. Require $I_0,I_1\subseteq C$ independent in their respective contractions onto $C$, with $I_0\cup I_1=C$. Contraction onto $C$ is $(M_i^*|C)^*$. The covering sets need not be disjoint. This predicate also applies when $E$ is infinite; it asserts no existence theorem.
