# Lemma 8.1

Suppose $w=D^{s_{k}} U^{r_{k}} \cdots D^{s_{2}} U^{r_{2}} D^{s_{1}} U^{r_{1}}$, where $r_{i} \geq 0$ and $s_{i} \geq 0$. Let $\lambda \vdash n$. Then there exists a Hasse walk of type $w$ from $\varnothing$ to $\lambda$ if and only if: $\sum_{i=1}^{k}(r_{i}-s_{i})=n$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{j}(r_{i}-s_{i}) \geq 0$ for $1 \leq j \leq k$.
