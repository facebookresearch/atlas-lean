# Corollary 8.7

The total number of Hasse walks in $Y$ of length $2m$ from $\varnothing$ to $\varnothing$ is given by $$\beta(2m, \varnothing) = 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2m-1).$$
