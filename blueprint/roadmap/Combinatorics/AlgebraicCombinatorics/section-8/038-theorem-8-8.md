# Theorem 8.8

The eigenvalues of $Y_{j-1,j}$ are given as follows: 0 is an eigenvalue of multiplicity $p(j) - p(j-1)$; and for $1 \leq s \leq j$, the numbers $\pm\sqrt{s}$ are eigenvalues of multiplicity $p(j-s) - p(j-s-1)$.
