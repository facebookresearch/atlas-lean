# Theorem 8.6

Let $\ell \geq n$ and $\lambda \vdash n$, with $\ell - n$ even. Then $$\beta(\ell, \lambda) = \binom{\ell}{n}(1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (\ell - n - 1)) f^\lambda.$$
