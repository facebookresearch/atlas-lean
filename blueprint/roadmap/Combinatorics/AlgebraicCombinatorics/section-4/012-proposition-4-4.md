# Proposition 4.4

Let $P$ be a graded poset of rank $n$. Suppose there exists an integer $0 \leq j \leq n$ and order-matchings $$P_{0} \rightarrow P_{1} \rightarrow P_{2} \rightarrow \cdots \rightarrow P_{j} \leftarrow P_{j+1} \leftarrow P_{j+2} \leftarrow \cdots \leftarrow P_{n}.$$ Then $P$ is rank-unimodal and Sperner.
