# Definition 4.1

A poset (short for partially ordered set) $P$ is a finite set, also denoted $P$, together with a binary relation denoted $\leq$ satisfying the following axioms: (P1) (reflexivity) $x \leq x$ for all $x \in P$; (P2) (antisymmetry) If $x \leq y$ and $y \leq x$, then $x=y$; (P3) (transitivity) If $x \leq y$ and $y \leq z$, then $x \leq z$.
