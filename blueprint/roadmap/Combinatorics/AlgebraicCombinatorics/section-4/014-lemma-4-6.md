# Lemma 4.6

Let $0 \leq i \leq n$. Then $D_{i+1} U_{i} - U_{i-1} D_{i} = (n-2i) I_{i}$, where $U_i$ and $D_i$ are the order-raising and order-lowering operators on $B_n$ defined by $U_i(x) = \sum_{\substack{y \in (B_n)_{i+1} \\ y > x}} y$ and $D_i(y) = \sum_{\substack{x \in (B_n)_{i-1} \\ x < y}} x$, with $U_n = 0$ and $D_0 = 0$, and $I_i$ is the identity transformation on $\mathbb{R}(B_n)_i$.
