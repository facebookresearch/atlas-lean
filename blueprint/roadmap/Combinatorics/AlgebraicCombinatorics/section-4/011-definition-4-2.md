# Definition 4.2

Let $P$ be a graded poset of rank $n$. We say that $P$ has the Sperner property or is a Sperner poset if $$\max \{|A| \mid A \text{ is an antichain of } P\}=\max \{|P_{i}| \mid 0 \leq i \leq n\}.$$ In other words, no antichain is larger than the largest level $P_{i}$.
