# Lemma 5.7

A basis for $\mathbb{R}(B_{n})_{i}^{G}$ consists of the elements $v_{\mathcal{O}} \coloneqq \sum_{x \in \mathcal{O}} x$ where $\mathcal{O} \in (B_{n})_{i} / G$, the set of $G$-orbits for the action of $G$ on $(B_{n})_{i}$.
