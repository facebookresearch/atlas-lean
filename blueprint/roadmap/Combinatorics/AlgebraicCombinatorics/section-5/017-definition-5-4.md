# Definition 5.4

Let $G$ be a subgroup of $\mathfrak{S}_{n}$. Define the quotient poset $B_{n} / G$ as follows: The elements of $B_{n} / G$ are the orbits of $G$. If $\mathcal{O}$ and $\mathcal{O}^{\prime}$ are two orbits, then define $\mathcal{O} \leq \mathcal{O}^{\prime}$ in $B_{n} / G$ if there exist $x \in \mathcal{O}$ and $y \in \mathcal{O}^{\prime}$ such that $x \leq y$ in $B_{n}$. (It's easy to check that this relation $\leq$ is indeed a partial order.)
