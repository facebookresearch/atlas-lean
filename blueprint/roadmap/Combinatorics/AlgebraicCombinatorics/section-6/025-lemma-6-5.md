# Lemma 6.5

We have $$\left[\begin{array}{c} k \\ j \end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{array}{c} k-1 \\ j \end{array}\right] + q^{k-j}\left[\begin{array}{c} k-1 \\ j-1 \end{array}\right]$$ whenever $k \geq 1$, with the initial conditions $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]=1$, $\left[\begin{array}{c} k \\ j \end{array}\right]=0$ if $j < 0$ or $j > k$ (the same initial conditions satisfied by the binomial coefficients $\binom{k}{j}$).
