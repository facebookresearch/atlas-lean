# Theorem 6.6

Let $p_{i}(m, n)$ denote the number of elements of $L(m, n)$ of rank $i$. Then $$\sum_{i \geq 0} p_{i}(m, n) q^{i}=\left[\begin{array}{c}m+n \\ m\end{array}\right].$$ (Note. The sum on the left-hand side is really a finite sum, since $p_{i}(m, n) = 0$ if $i > mn$.)
