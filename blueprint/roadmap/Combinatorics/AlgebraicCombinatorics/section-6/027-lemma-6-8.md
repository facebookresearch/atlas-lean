# Lemma 6.8

Every orbit $\mathcal{O}$ of the action of $G_{mn}$ on $B_{R}$ contains exactly one Young diagram $D$ (i.e., exactly one subset $D \subseteq R$ such that $D$ is left-justified, and if $\lambda_{i}$ is the number of elements of $D$ in row $i$ of $R$, then $\lambda_{1} \geq \lambda_{2} \geq \cdots \geq \lambda_{m}$).
