# Corollary 7

Either $\gamma_{0}/\gamma_{1} \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$ or $\gamma_{1}/\gamma_{0} \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$. (In fact, both $\gamma_{0}/\gamma_{1} \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$ and $\gamma_{1}/\gamma_{0} \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$, as will soon become apparent, but we don't need this fact here.)
