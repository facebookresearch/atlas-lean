# The integral expectation-threshold is at most the fractional one

For every family $\mathcal F$ of subsets of a finite set, $q_c(\mathcal F)\le q_f(\mathcal F)$.
Indeed, if an integral cover $G$ witnesses that $q$ is admissible for $q_c$, its indicator
$g(S)=\mathbf 1_{S\in G}$ gives each $T\in\mathcal F$ weight at least one and has the same cost
$\sum_{S\in G}q^{|S|}\le 1/2$. Thus $q$ is also admissible for $q_f$; take suprema.
