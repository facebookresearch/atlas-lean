# The integral expectation-threshold

For a family $\mathcal F$ of subsets of a finite ground set $X$, its integral expectation-threshold
$q_c(\mathcal F)$ is the supremum in $[0,1]$ of the parameters $q\in(0,1)$ for which there is a
family $G\subseteq 2^X$ satisfying both of the following:

- Every $T\in\mathcal F$ contains some $S\in G$.
- The cost $\sum_{S\in G}q^{|S|}$ is at most $1/2$.

If no parameter is admissible, the supremum in $[0,1]$ is zero.
