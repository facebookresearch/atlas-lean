# Sum-of-squares count bound over the whole type

For a finite type $\Omega$, $f : \Omega \to \mathbb Z$ and an integer $s \ge 0$,

$$\#\{x \in \Omega : f(x) \ge s\} \cdot s^2 \le \sum_{x \in \Omega} f(x)^2,$$

the case $S = \Omega$ of the [count bound from the sum of squares](card-filter-mul-sq-le-sum-sq.md).
