# Bound on the weight of pairs sharing an endpoint with a fixed pair

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $R$ a linearly ordered commutative semiring (for instance $\mathbb{N}$,
$\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$). Let $c = (c_{ab})_{a, b \in \iota}$ be a symmetric
matrix with entries $c_{ab} \ge 0$ in $R$ whose row sums satisfy $\sum_{b} c_{ab} \le P$ for every
$a$. Then for every pair $p = (p_1, p_2) \in \iota \times \iota$,

$$
\sum_{q} c_{q_1 q_2} \le 4P,
$$

the sum running over the pairs $q = (q_1, q_2)$ that share an endpoint with $p$, that is, with
$q_1 \in \{p_1, p_2\}$ or $q_2 \in \{p_1, p_2\}$. Pairs with $p_1 = p_2$ or $q_1 = q_2$ are allowed:
by symmetry each of the four conditions contributes at most one row sum.
