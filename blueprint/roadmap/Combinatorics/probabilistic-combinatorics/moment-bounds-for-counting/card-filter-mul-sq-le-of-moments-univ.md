# Moment count bound over the whole type

For a finite type $\Omega$, $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb N$ and natural numbers $A, m, V, t, s$ with $A \sum_x Y(x) = |\Omega|\, m$ and $A^2 \sum_x Y(x)^2 \le |\Omega|\, m^2 + V$,

$$\#\{x \in \Omega : t m + s \le t A\, Y(x)\} \cdot s^2 \le t^2 V,$$

the case $S = \Omega$ of the [count bound from first and second moments](card-filter-mul-sq-le-of-moments.md).
