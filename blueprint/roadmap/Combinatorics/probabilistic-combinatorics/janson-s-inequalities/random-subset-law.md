# Law of a random subset with independent elements

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and $p : \alpha \to [0, 1]$. The random subset $R \subseteq \alpha$
contains each element $a$ independently with probability $p(a)$. Identifying a subset with its
indicator function $\omega : \alpha \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$, the law of $R$ is the
product of the Bernoulli laws $\mathrm{Ber}(p(a))$ on the coordinates. The binomial random graph
$G(n, p)$ is the case where $\alpha$ is the set of pairs of vertices and $p$ is constant.
