# Second Janson inequality

In the setting of the [first Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md), if $\Delta \ge \mu$ then

$$
\Pr[\text{no } A_i \text{ occurs}] \le e^{-\mu^2/(2\Delta)}.
$$

If $\Delta = 0$ then also $\mu = 0$, the quotient $\mu^2/(2\Delta)$ is read as $0$, and the bound
is the trivial bound $1$. For $\Delta > 0$ it is proved by applying the first inequality to a
random subfamily of the events, each kept with probability $q = \mu/\Delta$.

## Informally uses

- [First Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md): applied to the random subfamily.
