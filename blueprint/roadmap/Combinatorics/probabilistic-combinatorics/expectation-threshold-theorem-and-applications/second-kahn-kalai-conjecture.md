# The second Kahn-Kalai conjecture for subgraph containment

For a graph $H$ and the property $\mathcal{F}_H = \{G : H \subseteq G\}$ of containing a copy of $H$ in $G(n, p)$, the *second Kahn-Kalai conjecture* asserts that $p_c(\mathcal{F}_H) \le K \log(e(H))\, p_E(H)$ for a universal constant $K$, where $p_E(H)$ is the expectation threshold restricted to the first-moment obstruction: the least $p$ such that every subgraph $H' \subseteq H$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\#\text{copies of } H' \text{ in } G(n,p)] \ge 1/2$. Mossel, Niles-Weed, Sun and Zadik established it in several regimes; the general case remains open.
