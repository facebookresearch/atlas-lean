# The Kahn-Kalai expectation-threshold conjecture

The *Kahn-Kalai conjecture* (2006) asserts that there is a universal constant $K$ such that for every finite set $X$ and every nontrivial increasing family $\mathcal{F} \subseteq 2^X$,

$$
p_c(\mathcal{F}) \le K\, q(\mathcal{F}) \log \ell(\mathcal{F}),
$$

where $\ell(\mathcal{F})$ is the maximum of $2$ and the largest size of a minimal member of $\mathcal{F}$. *Talagrand's fractional version* replaces $q$ by the larger fractional expectation threshold $q_f$.
