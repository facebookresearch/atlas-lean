# Lueker's upper bound for the binary Chvátal–Sankoff constant

The binary Chvátal–Sankoff
constant $C_{31a}$ is at most $0.826280$.

## Sources

- George S. Lueker, *Improved bounds on the average length of longest common subsequences*, J. ACM 56(3) (2009), 1–38 (computer assisted).
- *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository started by Terence Tao), constant $C_{31a}$, Chvátal–Sankoff constant for a binary alphabet, https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/31a.html.
