# Heineman et al. lower bound for the binary Chvátal–Sankoff constant

For each $n$, let
$E_n$ be the expected length of a longest common subsequence of two independent uniformly random
binary strings of length $n$, and let $C$, the binary Chvátal–Sankoff constant, be the limit
inferior of $E_n / n$ as $n \to \infty$. Then $0.792665992 \le C$.

## Sources

- George T. Heineman, Chase Miller, Daniel Reichman, Andrew Salls, Gábor Sárközy and Duncan Soiffer, *Improved lower bounds on the expected length of longest common subsequences*, arXiv:2407.10925 (2024) (computer assisted).
- *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository started by Terence Tao), constant $C_{31a}$, Chvátal–Sankoff constant for a binary alphabet, https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/31a.html.
