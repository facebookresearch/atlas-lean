# Statement of the reported 2026 lower bound $0.79970$

The proposition that the binary
Chvátal–Sankoff constant $C_{31a}$ is at least $0.79970$.

This bound was reported in 2026 from an AI- and computer-assisted Monte Carlo computation; the catalogue lists it as unverified, since the estimate holds only with probability $1 - 10^{-12}$ over the sampled strings.

## Sources

- *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository started by Terence Tao), constant $C_{31a}$, Chvátal–Sankoff constant for a binary alphabet, https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/31a.html. Lower bound $0.79970$, marked unverified.
