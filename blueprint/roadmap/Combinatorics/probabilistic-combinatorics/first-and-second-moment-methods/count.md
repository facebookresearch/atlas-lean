# Number of events that occur

For events $A_i$ indexed by a finite set, the count of those that occur at $\omega$ is

$$X(\omega) = \sum_i 1_{A_i}(\omega).$$

Counts of copies of a fixed subgraph in $G(n,p)$, of monochromatic cliques, or of sets contained in a random subset are all of this form.
