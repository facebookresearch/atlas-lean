# Factorial product of a sequence

For a natural number $n$ and a sequence
$g \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, this is the natural number
$\prod_{i=0}^{n-1} g(i)!\,\bigl((i+1)!\bigr)^{g(i)}$.
