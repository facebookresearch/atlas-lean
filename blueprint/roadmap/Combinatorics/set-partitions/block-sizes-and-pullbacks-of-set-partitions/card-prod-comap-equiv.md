# Pull-back along an equivalence preserves block-size products

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite sets, let $e : \alpha \to \beta$ be a bijection, let $P$ be a
partition of $\beta$, let $R$ be a commutative monoid and let $g : \mathbb{N} \to R$. The
[block-size product](card-prod.md) of $g$ over the [pull-back](comap.md) $e^{*}P$ of $P$ along
$e$ equals the one over $P$:

$$\prod_{C \in e^{*}P} g(|C|) = \prod_{B \in P} g(|B|).$$
