# Pulling back a partition along a composite

Let $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ be finite sets, let $f : \alpha \to \beta$ and
$g : \beta \to \gamma$ be maps and let $P$ be a partition of $\gamma$. Pulling $P$ back along $g$
and then along $f$ gives the [pull-back](comap.md) of $P$ along $g \circ f$.
