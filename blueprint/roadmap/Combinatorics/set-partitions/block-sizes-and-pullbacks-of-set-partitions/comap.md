# Pull-back of a finite partition along a map

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite sets, let $f : \alpha \to \beta$ be a map and let $P$ be a
partition of $\beta$. The pull-back $f^{*}P$ of $P$ along $f$ is the partition of $\alpha$ whose
blocks are the nonempty preimages $f^{-1}(B)$ of the blocks $B$ of $P$.
