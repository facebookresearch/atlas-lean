# Probability of a given merger in the pair-merger walk

Let $P$ be a partition of a finite set $s$ with $k \ge 2$ blocks, and let $Q$ be a partition of $s$ obtained from $P$ by merging two blocks ($P$ refines $Q$ and $Q$ has $k - 1$ blocks). Then the entry $T(P, Q)$ of the transition matrix of the uniform pair-merger walk equals $1/\binom{k}{2}$.
