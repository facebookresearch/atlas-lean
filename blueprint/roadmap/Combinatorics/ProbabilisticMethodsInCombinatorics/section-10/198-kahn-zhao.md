# Theorem 10.4.12 (Kahn, Zhao)

Let $G$ be an $n$-vertex $d$-regular graph. Then $i(G) \leq i(K_{d,d})^{n/(2d)} = (2^{d+1} - 1)^{n/(2d)}$ where $i(G)$ is the number of independent sets of $G$.
