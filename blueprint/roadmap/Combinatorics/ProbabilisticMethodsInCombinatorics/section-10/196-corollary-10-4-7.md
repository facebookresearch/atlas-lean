# Corollary 10.4.7

Let $A_1, \ldots, A_s \subseteq \Omega$ where each $i \in \Omega$ appears in at least $k$ sets $A_j$. Then for every family $\mathcal{F}$ of subsets of $\Omega$, $|\mathcal{F}|^k \leq \prod_{j \in [s]} |\mathcal{F}|_{A_j}|$ where $\mathcal{F}|_A \coloneqq \{F \cap A : F \in \mathcal{F}\}$.
