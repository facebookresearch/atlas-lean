# Corollary 10.4.6 (Loomis-Whitney inequality)

Writing $\pi_i$ for the projection from $\mathbb{R}^n$ onto the hyperplane $x_i = 0$, we have for every $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, $|S|^{n-1} \leq \prod_{i=1}^n |\pi_i(S)|$.
