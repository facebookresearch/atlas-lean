# Corollary 10.4.4

Let $S$ be a body in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Then $\operatorname{vol}(S)^2 \leq \operatorname{area}(\pi_{xy}(S)) \cdot \operatorname{area}(\pi_{xz}(S)) \cdot \operatorname{area}(\pi_{yz}(S))$.
