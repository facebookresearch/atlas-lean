# Theorem 10.4.15 (Sah, Sawhney, Stoner, and Zhao 2020)

Let $G$ be an $n$-vertex $d$-regular graph. Then $c_q(G) \leq c_q(K_{d,d})^{n/(2d)}$ where $c_q(G)$ is the number of $q$-colorings of $G$.
