# Theorem 10.4.5 (Shearer's lemma)

Let $A_1, \ldots, A_s \subseteq [n]$ where each $i \in [n]$ appears in at least $k$ sets $A_j$. Writing $X_A \coloneqq (X_i)_{i \in A}$, then $k H(X_1, \ldots, X_n) \leq \sum_{j \in [s]} H(X_{A_j})$.
