# Theorem 10.4.14 (Galvin and Tetali 2004)

Let $G$ be an $n$-vertex $d$-regular bipartite graph. Let $H$ be any graph allowing loops. Then $\operatorname{hom}(G, H) \leq \operatorname{hom}(K_{d,d}, H)^{n/(2d)}$.
