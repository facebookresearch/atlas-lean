# Definition 4.1.3 (Variance)

The variance of a random variable $X$ is $\operatorname{Var}[X] \coloneqq \mathbb{E}[(X - \mathbb{E}X)^2] = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - \mathbb{E}[X]^2$. The covariance of two random variables $X$ and $Y$ (jointly distributed) is $\operatorname{Cov}[X, Y] \coloneqq \mathbb{E}[(X - \mathbb{E}X)(Y - \mathbb{E}Y)] = \mathbb{E}[XY] - \mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y]$.
