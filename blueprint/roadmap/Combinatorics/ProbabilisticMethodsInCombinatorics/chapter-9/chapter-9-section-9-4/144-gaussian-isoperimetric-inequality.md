# Theorem 9.4.15 (Gaussian isoperimetric inequality)

If $A, H \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, $H$ a half-space, and $\gamma(A) = \gamma(H)$, then $\gamma(A_t) \geq \gamma(H_t)$ for all $t \geq 0$, where $\gamma$ is the Gauss measure.
