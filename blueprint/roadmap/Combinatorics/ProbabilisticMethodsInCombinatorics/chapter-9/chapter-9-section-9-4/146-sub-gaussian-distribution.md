# Definition 9.4.17 (Sub-Gaussian distribution)

We say that a random variable $X$ is $K$-subGaussian about its mean if $\mathbb{P}(|X - \mathbb{E}X| \geq t) \leq 2e^{-t^2/K^2}$ for all $t \geq 0$.
