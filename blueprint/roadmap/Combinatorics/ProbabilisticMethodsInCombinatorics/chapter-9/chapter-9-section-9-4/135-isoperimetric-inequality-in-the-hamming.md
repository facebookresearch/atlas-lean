# Theorem 9.4.3 (Isoperimetric inequality in the Hamming cube; Harper 1966)

Let $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^n$. Let $B \subseteq \{0,1\}^n$ be a Hamming ball with $|A| \geq |B|$. Then for all $t > 0$, $|A_t| \geq |B_t|$.
