# Theorem 9.4.22 (Johnson and Lindenstrauss 1982)

There exists a constant $C > 0$ so that the following holds. Let $\varepsilon > 0$. Let $X$ be a set of $N$ points in $\mathbb{R}^m$. Then for any $d > C\varepsilon^{-2} \log N$, there exists $f \colon X \to \mathbb{R}^d$ so that $(1 - \varepsilon)|x - y| \leq |f(x) - f(y)| \leq (1 + \varepsilon)|x - y|$ for all $x, y \in X$.
