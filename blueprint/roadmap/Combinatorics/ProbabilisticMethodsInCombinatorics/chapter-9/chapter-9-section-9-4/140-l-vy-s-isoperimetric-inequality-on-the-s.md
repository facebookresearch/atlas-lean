# Theorem 9.4.10 (Lévy's isoperimetric inequality on the sphere)

On a sphere in $\mathbb{R}^n$, let $A$ be a measurable subset and $B$ a spherical cap with $\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(A) = \operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(B)$. Then for all $t \geq 0$, $\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(A_t) \geq \operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(B_t)$.
