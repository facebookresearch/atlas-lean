# Theorem 9.4.1 (Isoperimetric inequality in Euclidean space)

Let $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be a measurable set, and let $B \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be a ball with $\operatorname{vol}(A) = \operatorname{vol}(B)$. Then, for all $t \geq 0$, $\operatorname{vol}(A_t) \geq \operatorname{vol}(B_t)$.
