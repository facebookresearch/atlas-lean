# Theorem 9.4.11 (Upper bound on spherical cap size)

Let $x = (x_1, \ldots, x_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be a uniform random unit vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Then for any $\varepsilon \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(x_1 \geq \varepsilon) \leq e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$.
