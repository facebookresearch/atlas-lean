# Corollary 9.4.25

There is a constant $c > 0$ so that for every positive integer $d$, there is a set of $e^{c\varepsilon^2 d}$ points in $\mathbb{R}^d$ whose pairwise distances are in $[1 - \varepsilon, 1 + \varepsilon]$.
