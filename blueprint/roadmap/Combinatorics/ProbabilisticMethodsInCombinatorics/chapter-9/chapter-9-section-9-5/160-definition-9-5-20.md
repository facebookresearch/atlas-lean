# Definition 9.5.20

Let $\Omega = \Omega_1 \times \cdots \times \Omega_n$. Let $A \subseteq \Omega$. We say that $A$ is **$s$-certifiable** if for every $x \in A$, there exists a set $I(x) \subseteq [n]$ with $|I| \leq s$ such that for every $y \in \Omega$ with $x_i = y_i$ for all $i \in I(x)$, one has $y \in A$.
