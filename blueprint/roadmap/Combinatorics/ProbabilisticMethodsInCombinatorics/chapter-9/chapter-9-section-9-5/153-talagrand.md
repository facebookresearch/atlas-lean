# Corollary 9.5.6 (Talagrand)

Let $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be a convex and $1$-Lipschitz function (with respect to Euclidean distance on $\mathbb{R}^n$). Let $x \sim \operatorname{Unif}\{0,1\}^n$. Then for any $r \in \mathbb{R}$ and $t \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(f(x) \leq r) \cdot \mathbb{P}(f(x) \geq r + t) \leq e^{-t^2/4}$.
