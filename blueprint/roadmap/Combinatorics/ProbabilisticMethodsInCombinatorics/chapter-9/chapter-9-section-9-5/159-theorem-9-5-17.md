# Theorem 9.5.17

Let $A = (a_{ij})$ be an $n \times n$ symmetric random matrix with independent entries in $[-1, 1]$. Let $\lambda_1(A)$ denote the largest eigenvalue of $A$. Then $\mathbb{P}(|\lambda_1(A) - M\lambda_1(A)| \geq t) \leq 4e^{-t^2/32}$.
