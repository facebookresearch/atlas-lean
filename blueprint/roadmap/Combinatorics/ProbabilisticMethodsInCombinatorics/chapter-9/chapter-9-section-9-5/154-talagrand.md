# Corollary 9.5.8 (Talagrand)

Let $f \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be a convex and $1$-Lipschitz function (with respect to Euclidean distance on $\mathbb{R}^n$). Let $x \sim \operatorname{Unif}(\{0,1\}^n)$. Then $\mathbb{P}(|f(x) - Mf(x)| \geq t) \leq 4e^{-t^2/4}$.
