# Theorem 9.5.11 (Talagrand's inequality: general form)

Let $A \subseteq \Omega = \Omega_1 \times \cdots \times \Omega_n$, with $\Omega$ equipped with a product probability measure. Let $x \in \Omega$ be chosen randomly with independent coordinates. Let $t \geq 0$. Then $\mathbb{P}(x \in A) \cdot \mathbb{P}(d_T(x, A) \geq t) \leq e^{-t^2/4}$.
