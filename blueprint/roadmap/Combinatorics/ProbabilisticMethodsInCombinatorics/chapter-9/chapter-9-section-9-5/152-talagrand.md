# Theorem 9.5.3 (Talagrand)

Let $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be convex. Let $x \sim \operatorname{Unif}\{0,1\}^n$. Then for any $t \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(x \in A) \cdot \mathbb{P}(\operatorname{dist}(x, A) \geq t) \leq e^{-t^2/4}$.
