# Lemma 9.5.12 (Convex distance upper bounds Euclidean distance)

Let $A \subseteq [0,1]^n$ and $x \in [0,1]^n$. Then $\operatorname{dist}(x, \operatorname{ConvexHull} A) \leq d_T(x, A)$.
