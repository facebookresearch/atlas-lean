# Corollary 9.5.22 (Talagrand's inequality for certifiable functions)

Let $\Omega = \Omega_1 \times \cdots \times \Omega_n$ be equipped with a product measure. Let $f \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be $1$-Lipschitz with respect to Hamming distance on $\Omega$. Suppose $\{f \geq r\}$ is $r$-certifiable for every $r$. Then for every $t \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(f \leq Mf - t) \leq 2 \exp\!\left(\frac{-t^2}{4 Mf}\right)$ and $\mathbb{P}(f \geq Mf + t) \leq 2 \exp\!\left(\frac{-t^2}{4(Mf + t)}\right)$.
