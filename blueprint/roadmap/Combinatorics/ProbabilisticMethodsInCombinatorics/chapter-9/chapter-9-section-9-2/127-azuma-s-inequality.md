# Theorem 9.2.7 (Azuma's inequality)

Let $Z_0, Z_1, \ldots, Z_n$ be a martingale satisfying $|Z_i - Z_{i-1}| \leq 1$ for each $i \in [n]$. Then for every $\lambda > 0$, $$\mathbb{P}(Z_n - Z_0 \geq \lambda \sqrt{n}) \leq e^{-\lambda^2/2}.$$
