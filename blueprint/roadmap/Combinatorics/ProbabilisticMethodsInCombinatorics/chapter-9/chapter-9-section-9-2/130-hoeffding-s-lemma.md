# Lemma 9.2.12 (Hoeffding's lemma)

Let $X$ be a real random variable contained in an interval of length $\ell$. Suppose $\mathbb{E}X = 0$. Then $\mathbb{E}[e^X] \leq e^{\ell^2/8}$.
