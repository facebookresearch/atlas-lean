# Definition 9.2.1

A martingale is a random real sequence $Z_0, Z_1, \ldots$ such that for every $Z_n$, $\mathbb{E}|Z_n| < \infty$ and $\mathbb{E}[Z_{n+1} \mid Z_0, \ldots, Z_n] = Z_n$.
