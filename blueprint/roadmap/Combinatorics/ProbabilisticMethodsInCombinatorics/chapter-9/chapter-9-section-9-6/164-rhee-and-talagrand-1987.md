# Theorem 9.6.1 (Rhee and Talagrand 1987)

$L_n$ is $O(\sqrt{\log n})$-subGaussian about its mean. That is, $\mathbb{P}(|L_n - \mathbb{E}L_n| \geq t) \leq \exp\!\left(\frac{-ct^2}{\log n}\right)$ for all $t > 0$, where $c > 0$ is some constant.
