# Lemma 9.6.11

Let $\Omega = ([0,1]^2)^n$ be the space of $n$-tuples of points in $[0,1]^2$. There exists a map $\alpha \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n_{\geq 0}$ so that for all $x \in \Omega$, $\alpha(x) = (\alpha_1(x), \ldots, \alpha_n(x)) \in \mathbb{R}^n_{\geq 0}$ satisfies $L(x) \leq L(y) + \sum_{i \colon x_i \neq y_i} \alpha_i(x)$ for all $x, y \in \Omega$ and $\sup_{x \in \Omega} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \alpha_i(x)^2 = O(1)$.
