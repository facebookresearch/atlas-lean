# Definition 9.6.5 (Hölder continuity)

Given two metric spaces $(X, d_X)$ and $(Y, d_Y)$, we say that a map $f \colon X \to Y$ is Holder continuous with exponent $\alpha$ if there is some constant $C$ (depending on $f$) so that $d_Y(f(x), f(x')) \leq C \, d_X(x, x')^{\alpha}$ for all $x, x' \in X$.
