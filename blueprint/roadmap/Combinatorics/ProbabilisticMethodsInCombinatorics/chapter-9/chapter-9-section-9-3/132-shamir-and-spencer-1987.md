# Theorem 9.3.4 (Shamir and Spencer 1987)

Let $\alpha > 5/6$ be fixed. Then for $p < n^{-\alpha}$, $\chi(G(n, p))$ is concentrated on four values with probability $1 - o(1)$. That is, there exists $u = u(n, p)$ such that, as $n \to \infty$, $\mathbb{P}(u \leq \chi(G(n, p)) \leq u + 3) = 1 - o(1)$.
