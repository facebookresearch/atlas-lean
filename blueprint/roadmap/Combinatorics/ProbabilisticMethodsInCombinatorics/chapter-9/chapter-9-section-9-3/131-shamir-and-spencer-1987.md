# Theorem 9.3.1 (Shamir and Spencer 1987)

For every $\lambda \geq 0$, the chromatic number of a random graph $Z = \chi(G(n, p))$ satisfies $\mathbb{P}(|Z - \mathbb{E}Z| \geq \lambda \sqrt{n-1}) \leq 2e^{-2\lambda^2}$.
