# Lemma 9.3.5

Fix $\alpha > 5/6$ and $C$. Let $p \leq n^{-\alpha}$. Then with probability $1 - o(1)$ every subset of at most $C\sqrt{n}$ vertices of $G(n, p)$ can be properly $3$-colored.
