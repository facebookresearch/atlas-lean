# Theorem 5.0.1 (Chernoff bound)

Let $S_n = X_1 + \cdots + X_n$ where $X_i \in \{-1, 1\}$ uniformly i.i.d. Let $\lambda > 0$. Then $\mathbb{P}(S_n \geq \lambda\sqrt{n}) \leq e^{-\lambda^2/2}$.
