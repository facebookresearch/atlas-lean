# Lemma 5.1.6

Let $v_1, \ldots, v_m \in \mathbb{R}^n$. Then there exists $a_1, \ldots, a_m \in [-1, 1]^m$ with $|\{i : a_i \notin \{-1, 1\}\}| \leq n$ such that $a_1 v_1 + \cdots + a_m v_m = 0$.
