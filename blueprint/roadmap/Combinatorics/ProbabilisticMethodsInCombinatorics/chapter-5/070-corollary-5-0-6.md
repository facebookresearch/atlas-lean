# Corollary 5.0.6

Let $X$ be a sum of $n$ independent Bernoulli random variables (with not necessarily identical probability). Let $\mu = \mathbb{E}X$ and $\lambda > 0$. Then $\mathbb{P}(X \geq \mu + \lambda\sqrt{n}) \leq e^{-\lambda^2/2}$ and $\mathbb{P}(X \leq \mu - \lambda\sqrt{n}) \leq e^{-\lambda^2/2}$.
