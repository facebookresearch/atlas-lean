# Theorem 5.0.7

Let $X$ be the sum of independent Bernoulli random variables (not necessarily the same probability). Let $\mu = \mathbb{E}X$. For all $\varepsilon > 0$, $\mathbb{P}(X \geq (1+\varepsilon)\mu) \leq e^{-((1+\varepsilon)\log(1+\varepsilon)-\varepsilon)\mu} \leq e^{-\varepsilon^2 \mu/(1+\varepsilon)}$ and $\mathbb{P}(X \leq (1-\varepsilon)\mu) \leq e^{-\varepsilon^2 \mu/2}$.
