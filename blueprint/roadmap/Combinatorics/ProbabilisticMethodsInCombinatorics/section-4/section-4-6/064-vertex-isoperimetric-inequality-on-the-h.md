# Theorem 4.6.4 (Vertex-isoperimetric inequality on the hypercube: Harper 1966)

Every $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^k$ with $|A| = 2^{k-1}$ has $|\partial A| \geq \binom{k}{\lfloor k/2 \rfloor}$.
