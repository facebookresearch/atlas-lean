# Definition 4.2.7

Define the edge-vertex ratio of a graph $H$ by $\rho(H) \coloneqq e_H / v_H$. (This is the same as half the average degree.) Define the maximum edge-vertex ratio of a subgraph of $H$: $m(H) \coloneqq \max_{H' \subseteq H} \rho(H')$.
