# Lemma 4.2.4

In the above setup (Setup 4.2.2), if $\mathbb{E}[X] \to \infty$ and $\Delta^* = o(\mathbb{E}[X])$, then $X > 0$ and $X \sim \mathbb{E}[X]$ whp.
