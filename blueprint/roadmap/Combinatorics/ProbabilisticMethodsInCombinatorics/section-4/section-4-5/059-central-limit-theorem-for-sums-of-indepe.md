# Theorem 4.5.2 (Central limit theorem for sums of independent Bernoullis)

If $X_n$ is a sum of independent Bernoulli random variables, and $\operatorname{Var} X_n \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$, then $(X_n - \mathbb{E}X_n) / \sqrt{\operatorname{Var} X_n}$ converges to the normal distribution.
