# Theorem 4.5.1 (Hardy and Ramanujan 1917)

For every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists $C$ such that for all sufficiently large $n$, all but $\varepsilon$-fraction of $x \in [n]$ satisfy $|\nu(x) - \log \log n| \leq C\sqrt{\log \log n}$.
