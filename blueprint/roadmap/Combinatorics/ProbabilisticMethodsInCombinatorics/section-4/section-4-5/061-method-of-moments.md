# Theorem 4.5.4 (Method of moments)

Let $X_n$ be a sequence of real-valued random variables such that for every positive integer $k$, $\lim_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{E}[X_n^k]$ equals the $k$-th moment of the standard normal distribution. Then $X_n$ converges in distribution to the standard normal, i.e., $\lim_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{P}(X_n \leq a) = \mathbb{P}(Z \leq a)$ for every $a \in \mathbb{R}$, where $Z$ is a standard normal.
