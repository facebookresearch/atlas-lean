# Definition 4.3.1 (Threshold)

Let $\Omega = \Omega^{(n)}$ be a finite set and $F = F^{(n)}$ a monotone property of subsets of $\Omega$. We say that $q_n$ is a threshold for $F$ if $\mathbb{P}(\Omega_{p_n} \in F) \to 0$ when $p_n / q_n \to 0$, and $\mathbb{P}(\Omega_{p_n} \in F) \to 1$ when $p_n / q_n \to \infty$.
