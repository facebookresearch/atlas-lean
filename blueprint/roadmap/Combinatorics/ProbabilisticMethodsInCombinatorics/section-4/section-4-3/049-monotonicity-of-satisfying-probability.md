# Theorem 4.3.5 (Monotonicity of satisfying probability)

Let $\Omega$ be a finite set and $F$ a non-trivial monotone property of subsets of $\Omega$. Then $p \mapsto \mathbb{P}(\Omega_p \in F)$ is a strictly increasing function of $p \in [0, 1]$.
