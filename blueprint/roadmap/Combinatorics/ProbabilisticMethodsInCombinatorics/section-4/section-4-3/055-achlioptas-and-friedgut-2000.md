# Theorem 4.3.17 (Achlioptas and Friedgut 2000)

For every $k \geq 3$, there exists a function $d_k(n)$ such that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ and sequence $d(n) > 0$, $\mathbb{P}(G(n, d(n)/n) \text{ is } k\text{-colorable}) \to 1$ if $d(n) < d_k(n) - \varepsilon$, and $\mathbb{P}(G(n, d(n)/n) \text{ is } k\text{-colorable}) \to 0$ if $d(n) > d_k(n) + \varepsilon$.
