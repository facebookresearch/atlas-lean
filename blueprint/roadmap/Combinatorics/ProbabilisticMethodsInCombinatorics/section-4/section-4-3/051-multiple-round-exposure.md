# Lemma 4.3.7 (Multiple round exposure)

Let $\Omega$ be a finite set and $F$ some non-trivial monotone property. If $p \in [0, 1]$ and $m$ is a nonnegative integer, then $\mathbb{P}(\Omega_p \notin F) \leq \mathbb{P}(\Omega_{p/m} \notin F)^m$.
