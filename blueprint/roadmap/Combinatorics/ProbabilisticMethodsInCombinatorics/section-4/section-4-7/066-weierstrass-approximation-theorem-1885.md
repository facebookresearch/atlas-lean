# Theorem 4.7.1 (Weierstrass approximation theorem 1885)

Let $f \colon [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Let $\varepsilon > 0$. Then there is a polynomial $p(x)$ such that $|p(x) - f(x)| \leq \varepsilon$ for all $x \in [0,1]$.
