# Corollary 10.2.2 (Kahn and Lovász)

Let $G$ be a graph. Let $d_v$ denote the degree of $v$. Then the number $\operatorname{pm}(G)$ of perfect matchings of $G$ satisfies $\operatorname{pm}(G) \leq \prod_{v \in V(G)} (d_v!)^{1/(2d_v)} = \prod_{v \in V(G)} \operatorname{pm}(K_{d_v, d_v})^{1/(2d_v)}$.
