# Theorem 8.1.10

Suppose $p = p_n \leq 0.99$. Then $$\mathbb{P}(G(n, p) \text{ is triangle-free}) = \begin{cases} \exp\left(-\Theta(n^2 p)\right) & \text{if } p \gtrsim n^{-1/2} \\ \exp\left(-\Theta(n^3 p^3)\right) & \text{if } p \lesssim n^{-1/2} \end{cases}$$
