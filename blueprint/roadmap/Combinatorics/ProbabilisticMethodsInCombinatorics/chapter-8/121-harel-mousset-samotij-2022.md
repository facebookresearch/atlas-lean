# Theorem 8.2.5 (Harel, Mousset, Samotij 2022)

Let $X$ be the number of triangles in $G(n, p)$ with $p = p_n$ satisfying $n^{-1/2} \ll p \ll 1$, $$-\log \mathbb{P}(X \geq (1+\delta)\mathbb{E}X) \sim \min\!\left\{\frac{\delta}{3}, \frac{\delta^{2/3}}{2}\right\} n^2 p^2 \log(1/p),$$ and for $n^{-1} \log n \ll p \ll n^{-1/2}$, $$-\log \mathbb{P}(X \geq (1+\delta)\mathbb{E}X) \sim \frac{\delta^{2/3}}{2} n^2 p^2 \log(1/p).$$
