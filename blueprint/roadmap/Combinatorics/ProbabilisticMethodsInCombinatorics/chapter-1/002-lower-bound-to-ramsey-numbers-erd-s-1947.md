# Theorem 1.1.2 (Lower bound to Ramsey numbers; Erdős 1947)

If $\binom{n}{k} 2^{1 - \binom{k}{2}} < 1$, then $R(k, k) > n$. In other words, there exists a red-blue edge-coloring of $K_n$ with no monochromatic $K_k$.
