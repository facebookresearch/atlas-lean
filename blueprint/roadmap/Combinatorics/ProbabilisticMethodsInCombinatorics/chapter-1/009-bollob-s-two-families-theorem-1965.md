# Theorem 1.2.6 (Bollobás' two families theorem 1965)

Let $A_1, \ldots, A_m$ and $B_1, \ldots, B_m$ be finite sets such that $A_i \cap B_i = \emptyset$ for all $i$ and $A_i \cap B_j \neq \emptyset$ for all $i \neq j$. Then $\sum_{i=1}^{m} \binom{|A_i| + |B_i|}{|A_i|}^{-1} \leq 1$.
