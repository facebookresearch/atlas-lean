# Theorem 1.2.2 (Sperner's theorem, 1928)

If $\mathcal{F}$ is an antichain of subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$, then $|\mathcal{F}| \leq \binom{n}{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor}$.
