# Theorem 1.2.3 (LYM inequality; Bollobás 1965, Lubell 1966, Meshalkin 1963, and Yamamoto 1954)

If $\mathcal{F}$ is an antichain of subsets of $[n]$, then $\sum_{A \in \mathcal{F}} \binom{n}{|A|}^{-1} \leq 1$.
