# Theorem 7.1.1 (Harris 1960)

If $A$ and $B$ are increasing events of independent boolean random variables, then $\mathbb{P}(A \cap B) \geq \mathbb{P}(A)\mathbb{P}(B)$. Equivalently, we can write $\mathbb{P}(A \mid B) \geq \mathbb{P}(A)$.
