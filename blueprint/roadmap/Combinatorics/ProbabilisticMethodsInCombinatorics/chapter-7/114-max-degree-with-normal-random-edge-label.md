# Proposition 7.2.6 (Max degree with normal random edge labels)

Assign an independent standard normal random variable $Z_{uv}$ to each edge of $K_n$. Let $W_v = \sum_{u \neq v} Z_{uv}$ be the sum of the labels of the edges incident to a vertex $v$. Then $\mathbb{P}(W_v \leq 0 \; \forall v) = (0.6102\ldots + o(1))^n$.
