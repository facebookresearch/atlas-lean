# Theorem 2.5.1

Let $a_{ij} \in \{-1, 1\}$ for all $i, j \in [n]$. There exists $x_i, y_j \in \{-1, 1\}$ for all $i, j \in [n]$ such that $\sum_{i,j=1}^{n} a_{ij} x_i y_j \geq \left(\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}} + o(1)\right) n^{3/2}$.
