# Theorem 2.5.2

Let $k \geq 2$. Let $V = V_1 \cup \cdots \cup V_k$, where $V_1, \ldots, V_k$ are disjoint sets of size $n$. The edges of the complete $k$-uniform hypergraph on $V$ are colored with red/blue. Suppose that every edge formed by taking one vertex from each $V_1, \ldots, V_k$ is colored blue. Then there exists $S \subseteq V$ such that the number of red edges and blue edges in $S$ differ by more than $c_k n^k$, where $c_k > 0$ is a constant.
