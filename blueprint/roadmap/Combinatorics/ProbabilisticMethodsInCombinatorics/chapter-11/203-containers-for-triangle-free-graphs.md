# Theorem 11.1.1 (Containers for triangle-free graphs)

For every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists $C > 0$ such that the following holds. For every $n$, there is a collection $\mathcal{C}$ of graphs on $n$ vertices, with $|\mathcal{C}| \leq n^{Cn^{3/2}}$ such that (a) every $G \in \mathcal{C}$ has at most $(\frac{1}{4} + \varepsilon)n^2$ edges, and (b) every triangle-free graph is contained in some $G \in \mathcal{C}$.
