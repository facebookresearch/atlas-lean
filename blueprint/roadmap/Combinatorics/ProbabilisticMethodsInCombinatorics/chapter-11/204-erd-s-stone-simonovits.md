# Theorem 11.1.2 (Erdős-Stone-Simonovits)

Fix a non-bipartite graph $H$. Then $\operatorname{ex}(n, H) = \left(1 - \frac{1}{\chi(H) - 1} + o(1)\right) \binom{n}{2}$.
