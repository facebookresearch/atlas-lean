# Theorem 11.3.1 (Container theorem for 3-uniform hypergraph)

For every $c > 0$ there exists $\delta > 0$ such that the following holds. Let $H$ be a $3$-uniform hypergraph with average degree $d \geq \delta^{-1}$ and $\Delta_1(H) \leq cd$ and $\Delta_2(H) \leq c\sqrt{d}$. Then there exists a collection $\mathcal{C}$ of subsets of $V(H)$ with $|\mathcal{C}| \leq \binom{v(H)}{\leq v(H)/\sqrt{d}}$ such that (1) Every independent set of $H$ is contained in some $C \in \mathcal{C}$, and (2) $|C| \leq (1 - \delta) v(H)$ for every $C \in \mathcal{C}$.
