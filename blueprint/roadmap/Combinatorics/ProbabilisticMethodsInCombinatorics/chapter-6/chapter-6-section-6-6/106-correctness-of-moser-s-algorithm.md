# Theorem 6.6.6 (Correctness of Moser's algorithm)

Given a $k$-CNF where every clause shares variables with at most $2^{k-3}$ other clauses, Algorithm 6.6.5 outputs a satisfying assignment with expected running time at most polynomial in the number of variables and clauses.
