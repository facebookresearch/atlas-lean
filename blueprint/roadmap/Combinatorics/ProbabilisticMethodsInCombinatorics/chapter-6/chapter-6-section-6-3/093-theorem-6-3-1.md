# Theorem 6.3.1

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a graph with maximum degree $\Delta$ and let $V = V_1 \cup \cdots \cup V_r$ be a partition, where each $|V_i| \geq 2e\Delta$. Then there is an independent set in $G$ containing one vertex from each $V_i$.
