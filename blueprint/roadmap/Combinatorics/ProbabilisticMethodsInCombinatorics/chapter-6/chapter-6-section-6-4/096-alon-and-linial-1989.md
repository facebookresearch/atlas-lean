# Theorem 6.4.3 (Alon and Linial 1989)

Every directed graph with min out-degree $\delta$ and max in-degree $\Delta$ contains a cycle of length divisible by $k \in \mathbb{N}$ as long as $k \leq \delta / (1 + \log(1 + \delta\Delta))$.
