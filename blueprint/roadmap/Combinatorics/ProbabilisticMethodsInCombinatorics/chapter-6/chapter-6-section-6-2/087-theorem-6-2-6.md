# Theorem 6.2.6

Let $H$ be a (non-uniform) hypergraph on a possibly infinite vertex set, such that each edge is finite, has at least $k$ vertices, and intersects at most $d$ other edges. If $e \cdot 2^{-k+1}(d+1) \leq 1$, then $H$ has a proper $2$-coloring.
