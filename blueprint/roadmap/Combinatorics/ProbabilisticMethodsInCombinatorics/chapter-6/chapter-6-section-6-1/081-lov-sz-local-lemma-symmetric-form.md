# Theorem 6.1.7 (Lovász local lemma; symmetric form)

Let $A_1, \ldots, A_n$ be events, with $\mathbb{P}[A_i] \leq p$ for all $i$. Suppose that each $A_i$ is independent from a set of all other $A_j$ except for at most $d$ of them. If $ep(d+1) \leq 1$, then with some positive probability, none of the events $A_i$ occur.
