# Definition 6.1.2 (Dependency (di)graph)

Let $A_1, \ldots, A_n$ be events (the "bad events" we wish to avoid). Let $G$ be a (directed) graph with vertex set $[n]$. We say that $G$ is a dependency (di)graph for the events $A_1, \ldots, A_n$ if, for every $i$, $A_i$ is independent from all $\{A_j : j \notin N(i) \cup \{i\}\}$ ($N(i)$ is the set of (out)neighbors of $i$ in $G$).
