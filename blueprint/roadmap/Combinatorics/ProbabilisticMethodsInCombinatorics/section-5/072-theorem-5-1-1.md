# Theorem 5.1.1

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a collection of $m$ subsets of $[n]$. Then there exists some assignment $[n] \to \{-1, 1\}$ so that the sum on every set in $\mathcal{F}$ is $O(\sqrt{n \log m})$ in absolute value.
