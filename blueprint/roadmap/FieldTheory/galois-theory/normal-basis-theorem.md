# Normal basis theorem

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of fields with Galois group $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Then there is a
basis $b = (b_\sigma)_{\sigma \in G}$ of the $K$-vector space $L$, indexed by $G$, such that $b_\sigma = \sigma(b_1)$
for every $\sigma \in G$, where $1$ is the identity of $G$. Mathlib constructs such a basis as
`IsGalois.normalBasis K L`: take $b$ to be that basis and $x = b_1$; then the family $(\sigma(x))_{\sigma \in G}$ is a
basis of $L$ over $K$, a normal basis generated by $x$. Nothing is asserted about rings of integers.
