# Separability from the degree over the subfield of $p$-th powers

Let $F/E$ be a finite field extension with $F$ of prime characteristic $p$, and let $F^p = \{a^p : a \in F\}$ be the subfield of $p$-th powers, the image of the Frobenius map. If $[F : F^p] = p$ and some $y \in E$ has image in $F$ outside $F^p$, then $F/E$ is separable.

The Kähler differentials of $F/E$ vanish on $F^p$ and on the image of $E$, hence on the intermediate field they generate. Since $[F : F^p]$ is prime, that intermediate field is either $F^p$ or all of $F$; the element $y$ rules out the first case, so every differential vanishes and $F/E$ is formally unramified, hence separable.
