# Injectivity of the Bogomolov–Pop reconstruction correspondence

Let $\ell$ be prime, and let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be essentially finite-type fields over algebraically closed fields with $\ell$ nonzero in each constant field and both transcendence degrees at least two. For a field $F$, take the maximal pro-$\ell$ quotient $G$ of its absolute Galois group, obtained by intersecting the open normal subgroups with finite $\ell$-group quotient. Put $A_F=G/\overline{[G,G]}$, $C_F=G/\overline{[\overline{[G,G]},G]}$, and $\Delta_F=\ker(C_F\to A_F)$. The bracket on $A_F$ takes values in $\Delta_F$ by lifting to $G$ and taking the commutator $x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$.

Consider isomorphisms $K^{\mathrm{perf}}\to L^{\mathrm{perf}}$ sending the image of $k$ onto that of $l$, modulo the equivalence generated by postcomposition by target Frobenius in positive characteristic. Also consider continuous group isomorphisms $A_L\to A_K$ preserving the bracket through some continuous isomorphism $\Delta_L\to\Delta_K$, modulo the equivalence generated by $\ell$-adic unit powering (limits of integer-approximation powers). A field class and an abelian class are related if representatives $\alpha,\phi$ admit an algebraic-closure isomorphism $\beta$ extending $\alpha$ such that for every $\sigma\in G_L^{\rm abs}$ a $\tau\in G_K^{\rm abs}$ satisfies $\beta\tau=\sigma\beta$ and $\phi(\bar\sigma)=\bar\tau$. Two field classes related to the same abelian class are equal. No surjectivity of the correspondence is asserted.

## Depends on

- Pro-$\ell$ and closed lower-central quotients with the commutator pairing; perfect closures and the two equivalence closures.

## Proof depends on

- Compare the induced Kummer data for representatives of a common abelian class and recover the field map up to Frobenius.

## Expansion

Prove independence of lifts in the bracket, transport the Kummer and valuation data under closure extensions and unit scaling, and isolate the step identifying field classes modulo the generated Frobenius relation.
