# Galois groups over the reals have order a power of 2

If $E$ is a finite Galois extension
of $\mathbb{R}$, then the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{R})$ has order $2^n$ for some natural
number $n$.

In fact the order is $1$ or $2$.
