# Norm in a tensor product with a Galois extension

Let $M/F$ be a finite Galois extension of fields, let $R$ be a commutative $F$-algebra, and let
$z \in R \otimes_F M$. The norm $N(z) \in R$ of $z$ over $R$ is the determinant of multiplication by
$z$ on the free $R$-module $R \otimes_F M$. Its image under the canonical embedding
$R \to R \otimes_F M$, $r \mapsto r \otimes 1$, is the product of the Galois conjugates of $z$:

$$N(z) \otimes 1 = \prod_{g \in \operatorname{Gal}(M/F)} (\mathrm{id}_R \otimes g)(z).$$
