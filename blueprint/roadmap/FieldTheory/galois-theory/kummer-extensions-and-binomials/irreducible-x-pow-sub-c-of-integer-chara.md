# Irreducibility of binomials from an integer-valued multiplicative function

Let $K$ be a field and $v : K \to \mathbb{Z}$ a function with $v(xy) = v(x) + v(y)$ for all nonzero $x, y \in K$ (the value $v(0)$ is arbitrary). Let $n \ge 1$ be a natural number and $a \in K$ with $a \neq 0$. If $v(a)$ and $n$ are coprime in $\mathbb{Z}$ (that is, $u\,v(a) + w\,n = 1$ for some integers $u, w$), then $X^n - a$ is irreducible in $K[X]$.
