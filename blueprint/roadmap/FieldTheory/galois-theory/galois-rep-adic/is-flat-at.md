# Is Flat At

Let $\rho$ be an adic representation of $\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ on a free module $V$ of rank $2$ over a local ring $A$. It is flat at $p$ when two conditions hold.

- The residue field of $A$ is finite.
- For every ideal $I$ of $A$ with $A/I$ finite, there are a finite, flat, commutative and cocommutative Hopf algebra $H$ over $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ (the rationals localized at $p$) and a bijection $e$ from the algebra maps $H \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ to $V/IV$ that sends the convolution product of two such maps to the sum of their images in $V/IV$ and respects the Galois actions: if $g = \sigma \circ f$ for $\sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$, then $e(g)$ is the image of $e(f)$ under the action of $\rho(\sigma)$ on $V/IV$.
