# Hilbert's Theorem 90 for the multiplicative group

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of fields with Galois group $G$, acting on the multiplicative group
$L^\times$. Then $H^1(G, L^\times) = 0$: for every map $f \colon G \to L^\times$ with
$f(\sigma\tau) = f(\sigma) \, \sigma(f(\tau))$ for all $\sigma, \tau \in G$, there is $\beta \in L^\times$ with
$f(\sigma) = \sigma(\beta) / \beta$ for all $\sigma \in G$.

Milne's proof: by Dedekind's theorem on the independence of characters, the map
$z \mapsto \sum_{\tau \in G} f(\tau) \, \tau(z)$ is not zero; for $z$ with
$\gamma = \sum_{\tau \in G} f(\tau) \, \tau(z) \ne 0$, the cocycle relation gives
$\sigma(\gamma) = f(\sigma)^{-1} \gamma$, so $\beta = \gamma^{-1}$ works. Milne deduces the classical form:
if $G$ is cyclic of order $m$, generated by $\sigma$, every $\alpha \in L^\times$ with
$\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\alpha) = \alpha \, \sigma(\alpha) \cdots \sigma^{m-1}(\alpha) = 1$ is of the form
$\beta / \sigma(\beta)$, since such an $\alpha$ defines a map $f$ as above with $f(\sigma) = \alpha$.

The additive analogue, for the trace, is the page
[additive Hilbert 90 for cyclic extensions](additive-hilbert-s-theorem-90/ker-trace-eq-range-alg-equiv-sub-id.md).
