# Extending a multiplicative assignment on generators to an algebra map

Let `G ⊆ L`
contain `1` and be closed under multiplication, and let `θ : L → L'` be multiplicative on `G`
and preserve the finite `K`-linear relations among elements of `G`: whenever `∑ cᵢ gᵢ = 0`
for finitely many `gᵢ ∈ G` and `cᵢ ∈ K`, also `∑ cᵢ θ gᵢ = 0`. If the field generated by `G`
is algebraic over the field generated by one element of `G` whose image under `θ` is
transcendental, then some `K`-algebra map on the field generated by `G` agrees with `θ` on
`G`. It need not agree with `θ` off `G`: for `G` the powers of `X` in the rational function
field `K(X)` and `θ` squaring, the map is the substitution `X ↦ X ^ 2`, which sends `1 + X`
to `1 + X ^ 2`, not `(1 + X) ^ 2`, when `2 ≠ 0` in `K`.
