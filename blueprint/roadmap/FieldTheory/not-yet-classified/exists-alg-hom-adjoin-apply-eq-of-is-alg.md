# Adjoin Alg Hom Transcendental

If `G` is multiplicative, `θ` is multiplicative on `G` and preserves
its base-field linear relations, and the field generated by `G` is
algebraic over the field generated by one element of `G` whose image
under `θ` is transcendental, then some `K`-algebra map on that generated
field agrees with `θ` on `G`. It need not agree with `θ` off `G`: for `G`
the powers of `X` in the rational function field `K(X)` and `θ` squaring,
the map is the substitution `X ↦ X ^ 2`, which sends `1 + X` to
`1 + X ^ 2`, not `(1 + X) ^ 2`, when `2 ≠ 0` in `K`.
