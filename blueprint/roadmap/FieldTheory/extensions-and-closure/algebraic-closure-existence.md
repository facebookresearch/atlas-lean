# Every field has an algebraic closure

Every field $k$ has an algebraic closure: an extension field of $k$ that is algebraically closed and algebraic over $k$. More precisely, Mathlib gives an explicit witness: for every field $k$ it constructs a field $\overline{k}$, written $\mathrm{AlgebraicClosure}\,k$, together with a $k$-algebra structure, and proves that $\overline{k}$ is an algebraic closure of $k$, that is, $\overline{k}$ is algebraically closed, every polynomial over $\overline{k}$ splitting into linear factors, and every element of $\overline{k}$ is algebraic over $k$. Mathlib defines being an algebraic closure for a field that is a torsion-free algebra over a commutative ring; over a field $k$ the torsion-freeness is automatic. The statement certifies this particular extension; it asserts no uniqueness up to isomorphism.
