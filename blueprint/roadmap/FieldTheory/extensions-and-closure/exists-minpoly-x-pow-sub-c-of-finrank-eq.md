# Purely inseparable extensions of prime degree

Let $p$ be a prime, let $K$ be a field of
characteristic $p$, and let $L/K$ be a finite purely inseparable field extension of degree
$[L : K] = p$. Then there are $\alpha \in L$ and $a \in K$ such that the minimal polynomial of
$\alpha$ over $K$ is $X^p - a$, $a$ is not a $p$-th power in $K$ (that is, $b^p \neq a$ for every
$b \in K$), and $L = K(\alpha)$.
