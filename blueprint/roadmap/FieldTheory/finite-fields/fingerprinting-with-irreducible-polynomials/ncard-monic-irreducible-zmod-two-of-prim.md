# Monic irreducible polynomials of prime degree over Z2

For a prime $k$, the number of monic irreducible polynomials
$x^k + a_{k-1} x^{k-1} + \dots + a_0 \in \mathbb{Z}_2[x]$ is $(2^k - 2)/k$. For example, $k = 2$ gives
the single polynomial $x^2 + x + 1$ and $k = 3$ gives $x^3 + x + 1$ and $x^3 + x^2 + 1$; for
$k = 31$ there are $(2^{31} - 2)/31 = 69273666$ moduli to choose from.
