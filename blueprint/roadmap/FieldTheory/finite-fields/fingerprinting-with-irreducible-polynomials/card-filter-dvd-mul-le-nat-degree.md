# At most deg H / k irreducible factors of degree k

Let $K$ be a field, $k$ a natural number, $S$ a finite set of monic irreducible polynomials of
degree $k$ over $K$, and $H \ne 0$ a polynomial over $K$. Then

$$\#\{f \in S : f \mid H\} \cdot k \le \deg H.$$

Distinct monic irreducible polynomials are coprime, so the product of those dividing $H$ divides
$H$, and its degree is $k$ times their number.

Rabin uses this over $\mathbb{Z}_2$ with $k$ prime; the statement here holds over every field and
for every $k$.
