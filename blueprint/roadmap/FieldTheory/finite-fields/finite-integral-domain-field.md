# Every finite integral domain is a field

Let $R$ be a finite integral domain, that is, a finite commutative ring with identity in which $1 \neq 0$ and $ab = 0$ implies $a = 0$ or $b = 0$. Then $R$ is a field: every nonzero $a \in R$ has an element $b \in R$ with $ab = 1$.
