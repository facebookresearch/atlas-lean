# Alg equiv of card eq

Let `K` be a field and `L₁`, `L₂` finite fields that are `K`-algebras. If `L₁` and `L₂` have the same number of elements, then they are isomorphic as `K`-algebras. Both are splitting fields over `K` of `X ^ q - X`, where `q` is their common cardinality, since every element of a finite field with `q` elements is a root of this polynomial.
