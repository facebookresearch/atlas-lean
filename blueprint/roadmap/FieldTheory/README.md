# Field Theory

Field theory studies extensions of fields, algebraic elements, automorphisms, and Galois-theoretic structure.

[Galois theory](galois-theory/README.md), with nearly half of the subject's articles, includes the Abel–Ruffini
theorem that every complex root of every rational polynomial of degree $n$ is expressible by
[radicals](galois-theory/solvability-by-radicals/README.md) exactly when $n \le 4$, the
[Gauss–Wantzel theorem](galois-theory/gauss-wantzel-theorem-on-constructible-polygons/README.md) on constructible
regular polygons, and the [essential dimension](galois-theory/essential-dimension-of-symmetric-groups/README.md) of
$S_n$ over $\mathbb{C}$, which lies between $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ and $n - 3$ for $n \ge 5$ (Buhler and Reichstein).
[Finite fields](finite-fields/README.md) appear mostly through polynomials over them, such as the
[permutation polynomials](finite-fields/permutation-polynomials/README.md), where $X^d$ permutes $\mathbb{F}_q$
exactly when $\gcd(d, q - 1) = 1$, and [field extensions and algebraic closure](extensions-and-closure/README.md)
covers [Puiseux's theorem](extensions-and-closure/puiseux-s-theorem/README.md) on the algebraic closure of $K((t))$
for $K$ algebraically closed of characteristic zero.
