# Theorem 2.1

Let $S$ be a multiplicative subset of an integral domain $A$. There is a one-to-one correspondence between the prime ideals of $S^{-1}A$ and the prime ideals of $A$ that do not intersect $S$ given by the inverse maps $\mathfrak{q} \mapsto \mathfrak{q} \cap A$ and $\mathfrak{p} \mapsto \mathfrak{p} S^{-1}A$.
