# Definition 2.23

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain. The quotient $\operatorname{cl}(A)\coloneqq \mathcal{I}_A/\mathcal{P}_A$ is the ideal class group of $A$; it is also called the Picard group of $A$ and denoted $\operatorname{Pic}(A)$.
