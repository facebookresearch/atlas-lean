# Proposition 2.6

Let $A$ be a subring of a field $K$, and let $M$ be an $A$-module contained in a $K$-vector space $V$ (equivalently, for which the map $M \rightarrow M \otimes_{A} K$ is injective). Then $M=\bigcap_{\mathfrak{m}} M_{\mathfrak{m}}=\bigcap_{\mathfrak{p}} M_{\mathfrak{p}}$, where $\mathfrak{m}$ ranges over the maximal ideals of $A$, $\mathfrak{p}$ ranges over the prime ideals of $A$, and the intersections take place in $V$.
