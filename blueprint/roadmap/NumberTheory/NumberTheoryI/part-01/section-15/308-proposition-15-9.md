# Proposition 15.9

Let $K$ be a number field with $s$ complex places, define $B_K \coloneqq \left(\frac{2}{\pi}\right)^s \sqrt{|D_K|}$. If $c$ is an Arakelov divisor of size $\|c\| > B_K$ then $L(c)$ contains an element of $K^\times$.
