# Definition 15.1

Let $K$ be a global field. A (multiplicative) Arakelov divisor is a sequence of positive real numbers $c = (c_v)$ indexed by $v \in M_K$ with all but finitely many $c_v = 1$ and $c_v \in \|K_v^\times\| \coloneqq \{\|x\|_v : x \in K_v^\times\}$. The set of Arakelov divisors $\operatorname{Div} K$ forms an abelian group under pointwise multiplication $(c_v)(d_v) \coloneqq (c_v d_v)$. The multiplicative group $K^\times$ is canonically embedded in $\operatorname{Div} K$ via the map $x \mapsto (\|x\|_v)$, where it forms the subgroup $\operatorname{Princ} K$ of principal Arakelov divisors.
