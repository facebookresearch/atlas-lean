# Definition 15.16

The regulator of a number field $K$ is $R_{K}\coloneqq \operatorname{covol}(\pi(\operatorname{Log}(\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}))) \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ where $\pi: \mathbb{R}^{r+s} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{r+s-1}$ is any coordinate projection; the value of $R_{K}$ does not depend on the choice of $\pi$, since we use $\pi$ to normalize the Haar measure on $\mathbb{R}_{0}^{r+s} \simeq \mathbb{R}^{r+s-1}$. If $\epsilon_{1}, \ldots, \epsilon_{r+s-1}$ is a fundamental system of units (a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis for the free part of $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}$), then $R_{K}$ can be computed as the absolute value of the determinant of any $(r+s-1) \times (r+s-1)$ minor of the $(r+s) \times (r+s-1)$ matrix whose columns are the vectors $\operatorname{Log}(\epsilon_{i}) \in \mathbb{R}^{r+s}$.
