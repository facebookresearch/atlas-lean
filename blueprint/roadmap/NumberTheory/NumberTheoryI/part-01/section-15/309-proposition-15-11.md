# Proposition 15.11

Let $K$ be a number field with $r$ real and $s$ complex places, and let $\Lambda_{K}$ be the image of the unit group $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}$ in $\mathbb{R}_{0}^{r+s}$ under the $\operatorname{Log}$ map. The following hold: (1) We have a split exact sequence of finitely generated abelian groups $1 \rightarrow \mu_{K} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \xrightarrow{\operatorname{Log}} \Lambda_{K} \rightarrow 0$. (2) $\Lambda_{K}$ is a lattice in the trace zero hyperplane $\mathbb{R}_{0}^{r+s}$.
