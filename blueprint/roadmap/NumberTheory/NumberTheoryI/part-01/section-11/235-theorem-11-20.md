# Theorem 11.20

Let $K$ be a global field with a nontrivial absolute value $|\;|$, and let $\hat{K}$ be the completion of $K$ with respect to $|\;|$. Every finite separable extension $\hat{L}$ of $\hat{K}$ is the completion of a finite separable extension $L$ of $K$ with respect to an absolute value that restricts to $|\;|$. One can choose $L$ so that $[L:K]=[\hat{L}:\hat{K}]$, in which case $\hat{L}=\hat{K} \cdot L$.
