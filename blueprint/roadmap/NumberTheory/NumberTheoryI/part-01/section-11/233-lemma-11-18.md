# Lemma 11.18

Let $K$ be a field with absolute value $|\;|$ and let $f\coloneqq \prod_{i=1}^{n}(x-\alpha_i) \in K[x]$ be a monic polynomial with roots $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n \in L$, where $L/K$ is a field with an absolute value that extends $|\;|$. Then $|\alpha|\lt \|f\|_1$ for every root $\alpha$ of $f$.
