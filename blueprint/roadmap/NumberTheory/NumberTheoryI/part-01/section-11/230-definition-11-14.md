# Definition 11.14

Let $K$ be the fraction field of a complete DVR with absolute value $|\;|$ extended to an algebraic closure $\bar{K}$. For $\alpha, \beta \in \bar{K}$, we say $\beta$ belongs to $\alpha$ if $|\beta-\alpha|<|\beta-\sigma(\alpha)|$ for all $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_{K}(\bar{K})$ with $\sigma(\alpha) \neq \alpha$, that is, $\beta$ is strictly closer to $\alpha$ than it is to any of its conjugates. This is equivalent to requiring that $|\beta-\alpha|<|\alpha-\sigma(\alpha)|$ for all $\sigma(\alpha) \neq \alpha$, since every nonarchimedean triangle is isosceles (if one side is shorter than another, it is the shortest of all three sides).
