# Definition 11.1

Let $A$ be a DVR with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$. A monic polynomial $f(x)=x^{n}+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{1}x+a_{0} \in A[x]$ is Eisenstein (or an Eisenstein polynomial) if $a_{i} \in \mathfrak{p}$ for $0 \leq i\lt n$ and $a_{0} \notin \mathfrak{p}^{2}$; equivalently, $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(a_{i}) \geq 1$ for $0 \leq i\lt n$ and $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(a_{0})=1$. Note that this means $a_{0}$ is a uniformizer.
