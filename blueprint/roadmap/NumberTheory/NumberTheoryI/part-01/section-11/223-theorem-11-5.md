# Theorem 11.5

Assume $AKLB$ with $A$ a complete DVR and $\pi$ a uniformizer for $B$. The extension $L/K$ is totally ramified if and only if $B=A[\pi]$ and the minimal polynomial of $\pi$ is Eisenstein.
