# Lemma 11.15 (Krasner's lemma)

Let $K$ be the fraction field of a complete DVR and let $\alpha, \beta \in \bar{K}$, with $\alpha$ separable over $K$. If $\beta$ belongs to $\alpha$ then $K(\alpha) \subseteq K(\beta)$.
