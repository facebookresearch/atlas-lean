# Definition 1.7

For a prime $p$ the $p$-adic valuation $v_p: \mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ is defined by $v_p(\pm \prod_q q^{e_q}) \coloneqq e_p$, and we define $v_p(0) \coloneqq \infty$. The $p$-adic absolute value on $\mathbb{Q}$ is defined by $|x|_p \coloneqq p^{-v_p(x)}$, where $|0|_p = p^{-\infty}$ is understood to be 0.
