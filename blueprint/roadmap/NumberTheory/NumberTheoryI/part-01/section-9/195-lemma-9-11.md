# Lemma 9.11

Let $R$ be a ring, let $f = \sum f_i x^i \in R[x]$ be a polynomial, and let $a \in R$. Then $f(x) = f(a) + f'(a)(x - a) + g(x)(x - a)^2$ for a unique $g \in R[x]$.
