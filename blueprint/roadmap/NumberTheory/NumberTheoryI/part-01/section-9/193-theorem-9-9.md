# Theorem 9.9

Let $L$ be a local field. If $L$ is archimedean then it is isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$; otherwise, $L$ is isomorphic to a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ or $\mathbb{F}_q((t))$.
