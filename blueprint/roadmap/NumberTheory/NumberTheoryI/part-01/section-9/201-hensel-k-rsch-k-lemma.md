# Lemma 9.20 (Hensel-Kürschák lemma)

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$, and let $f \in K[x]$ be an irreducible polynomial whose leading and constant coefficients lie in $A$. Then $f \in A[x]$.
