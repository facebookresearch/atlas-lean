# Theorem 9.22

Assume AKLB and that $A$ is a complete DVR with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$. Then $B$ is a DVR whose maximal ideal $\mathfrak{q}$ is necessarily the unique prime above $\mathfrak{p}$.
