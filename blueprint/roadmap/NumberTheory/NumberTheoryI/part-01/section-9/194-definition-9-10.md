# Definition 9.10

Let $R$ be a (commutative) ring, and let $f(x) = \sum f_i x^i \in R[x]$ be a polynomial. The (formal) derivative $f'$ of $f$ is the polynomial $f'(x) \coloneqq \sum i f_i x^{i-1} \in R[x]$.
