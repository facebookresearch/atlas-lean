# Lemma 9.15 (Hensel's Lemma I)

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ and residue field $k\coloneqq A/\mathfrak{p}$. Suppose $f \in A[x]$ is a monic polynomial whose reduction to $k[x]$ has a simple root $\bar{a} \in k$. Then $\bar{a}$ can be lifted to a root of $f$ in $A$.
