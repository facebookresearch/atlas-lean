# Definition 7.10

Assume $AKLBG$, and let $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $B$. The kernel of the surjective homomorphism $\pi_{\mathfrak{q}}: D_{\mathfrak{q}} \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}_{A/\mathfrak{p}}(B/\mathfrak{q})$ is the inertia group $I_{\mathfrak{q}}$ of $\mathfrak{q}$.
