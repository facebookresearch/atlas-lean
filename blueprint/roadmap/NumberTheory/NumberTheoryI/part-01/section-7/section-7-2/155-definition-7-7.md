# Definition 7.7

Assume $AKLBG$. For each prime $\mathfrak{q}$ of $B$ the decomposition group $D_{\mathfrak{q}}$ (also denoted $D_{\mathfrak{q}}(L/K)$) is the stabilizer of $\mathfrak{q}$ in $G$.
