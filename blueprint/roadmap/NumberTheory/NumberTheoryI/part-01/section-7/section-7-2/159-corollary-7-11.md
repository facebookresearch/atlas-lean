# Corollary 7.11

Assume $AKLBG$ and let $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $B$. We have an exact sequence $1 \longrightarrow I_{\mathfrak{q}} \longrightarrow D_{\mathfrak{q}} \longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}_{A/\mathfrak{p}}(B/\mathfrak{q}) \longrightarrow 1$, and $\# I_{\mathfrak{q}}=e_{\mathfrak{p}}[B/\mathfrak{q}: A/\mathfrak{p}]_{i}$.
