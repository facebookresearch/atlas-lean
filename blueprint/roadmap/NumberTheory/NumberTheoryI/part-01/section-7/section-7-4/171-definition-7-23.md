# Definition 7.23

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with finite residue fields. Let $L$ be a finite abelian extension of $K = \operatorname{Frac} A$, and let $S$ be the set of primes of $A$ that ramify in $L$. The Artin map is the homomorphism $\left(\frac{L/K}{\cdot}\right) \colon \mathcal{I}_A^S \to \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ defined by $\prod_{i=1}^m \mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i} \mapsto \prod_{i=1}^m \left(\frac{L/K}{\mathfrak{p}_i}\right)^{e_i}$.
