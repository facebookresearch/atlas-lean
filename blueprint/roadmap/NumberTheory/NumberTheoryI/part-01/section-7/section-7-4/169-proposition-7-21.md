# Proposition 7.21

Assume $AKLBG$ with finite residue fields and $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ unramified. Then $\mathfrak{p}$ splits completely if and only if $\left(\frac{L/K}{\mathfrak{q}}\right) = 1$.
