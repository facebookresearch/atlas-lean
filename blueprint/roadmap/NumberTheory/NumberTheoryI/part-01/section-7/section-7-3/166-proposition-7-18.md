# Proposition 7.18

Assume $AKLBG$ with finite residue fields and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be an unramified prime of $A$. The set of Frobenius elements $\{\sigma_{\mathfrak{q}} : \mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}\}$ is a conjugacy class of $G$.
