# Definition 7.16

Assume $AKLBG$ with finite residue fields and $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ unramified. The inverse image of the Frobenius automorphism of $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{q}}/\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}})$ under $\pi_{\mathfrak{q}} \colon D_{\mathfrak{q}} \to \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{q}}/\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}})$ is the Frobenius element $\sigma_{\mathfrak{q}} \in D_{\mathfrak{q}} \subseteq G$ (also called the Frobenius substitution).
