# Proposition 7.17

Assume $AKLBG$ with finite residue fields and $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ unramified. The Frobenius element $\sigma_{\mathfrak{q}}$ is the unique $\sigma \in G$ such that for all $x \in B$ we have $\sigma(x) \equiv x^{\#\mathbb{F}_{\mathfrak{p}}} \pmod{\mathfrak{q}}$.
