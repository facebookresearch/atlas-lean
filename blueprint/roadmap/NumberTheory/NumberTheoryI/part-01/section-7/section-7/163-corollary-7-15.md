# Corollary 7.15

Assume $AKLBG$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $K$. The fields $L^{I_{\mathfrak{p}}}$ and $L^{D_{\mathfrak{p}}}$ are Galois extensions of $K$, and for any intermediate field $E$ we have $e_{\mathfrak{q}_E/\mathfrak{p}} = 1$ for all $\mathfrak{q}_E \mid \mathfrak{p}$ if and only if $E \subseteq L^{I_{\mathfrak{p}}}$, and $e_{\mathfrak{q}_E/\mathfrak{p}} = f_{\mathfrak{q}_E/\mathfrak{p}} = 1$ for all $\mathfrak{q}_E \mid \mathfrak{p}$ if and only if $E \subseteq L^{D_{\mathfrak{p}}}$.
