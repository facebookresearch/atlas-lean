# Corollary 7.4

Assume $AKLBG$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. The residue field degrees $f_{\mathfrak{q}}\coloneqq [B/\mathfrak{q}: A/\mathfrak{p}]$ are the same for every $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$, as are the ramification indices $e_{\mathfrak{q}}\coloneqq v_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{p} B)$.
