# Theorem 7.2

Assume $AKLBG$. For each fractional ideal $I$ of $B$ and $\sigma \in G$ define $\sigma(I)\coloneqq \{\sigma(x): x \in I\}$. The set $\sigma(I)$ is a fractional ideal of $B$, and this defines a group action on $\mathcal{I}_{B}$ that makes it a left $G$-module. Moreover, the restriction of this action to $\operatorname{Spec} B$ makes it a $G$-set.
