# Definition 7.1

Let $G$ be a group. A left $G$-module is an abelian group $M$ equipped with a left $G$-action that commutes with its group operation; in additive notation we have $\sigma(a+b)=\sigma(a)+\sigma(b)$ for all $\sigma \in G$ and $a, b \in M$. One similarly defines a right $G$-module as an abelian group with a right $G$-action that commutes with the group operation.
