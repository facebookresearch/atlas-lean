# Corollary 7.3

Assume $AKLBG$. For each prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $A$ the group $G$ acts transitively on the set $\{\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}\}$; in other words, the orbits of the $G$-action on $\operatorname{Spec} B$ are the fibers of the contraction map $\operatorname{Spec} B \rightarrow \operatorname{Spec} A$.
