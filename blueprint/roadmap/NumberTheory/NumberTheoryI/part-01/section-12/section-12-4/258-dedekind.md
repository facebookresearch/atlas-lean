# Theorem 12.23 (Dedekind)

Let $K=\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ be a number field, let $f$ be the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$, let $p$ be a prime, let $\bar{f}=\prod_{i=1}^{r} \bar{f}_{i}^{e_{i}}$ be the factorization of $\bar{f}\coloneqq f \bmod p$ in $\mathbb{F}_{p}[x]$. Let $f_{i}$ be any monic lift of $\bar{f}_{i}$ to $\mathbb{Z}[x]$, let $u\coloneqq \prod f_{i}$, let $v$ be any monic lift of $\bar{f}/\bar{g}$ to $\mathbb{Z}[x]$, and let $w=(uv-f)/p \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$. Then $p$ divides $[\mathcal{O}_{K}:\mathbb{Z}[\alpha]]$ if and only if $\bar{u}, \bar{v}, \bar{w}$ have no common factor in $\mathbb{F}_{p}[x]$.
