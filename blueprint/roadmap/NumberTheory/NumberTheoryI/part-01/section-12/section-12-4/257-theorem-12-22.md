# Theorem 12.22

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order with integral closure $B$ and conductor $\mathfrak{c}$. Then $D_{\mathcal{O}/A} = \mathrm{N}_{B/A}(\mathfrak{c}) D_{B/A}$.
