# Theorem 12.19

Assume $AKLB$, let $\mathfrak{q}$ be a prime of $B$ lying above a prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $A$ such that $B/\mathfrak{q}$ is a separable extension of $A/\mathfrak{p}$. The extension $L/K$ is unramified at $\mathfrak{q}$ if and only if $\mathfrak{q}$ does not divide $\mathcal{D}_{B/A}$, and it is unramified at $\mathfrak{p}$ if and only if $\mathfrak{p}$ does not divide $D_{B/A}$.
