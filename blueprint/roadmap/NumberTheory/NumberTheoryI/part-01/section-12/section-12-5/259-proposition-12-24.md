# Proposition 12.24

Assume $AKLB$. If $B=A[\alpha]$ for some $\alpha \in L$ and $f \in A[x]$ is the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$, then $\mathcal{D}_{B/A}=(f'(\alpha))$ is the $B$-ideal generated by $f'(\alpha)$.
