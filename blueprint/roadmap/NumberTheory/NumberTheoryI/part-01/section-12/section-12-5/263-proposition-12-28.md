# Proposition 12.28

Assume $AKLB$ and let $M/L$ be a finite separable extension and let $C$ be the integral closure of $A$ in $M$. Then $\mathcal{D}_{C/A}=\mathcal{D}_{C/B} \cdot \mathcal{D}_{B/A}$ (where the product on the right is taken in $C$), and $D_{C/A}=(D_{B/A})^{[M:L]} N_{B/A}(D_{C/B})$.
