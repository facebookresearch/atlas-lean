# Theorem 12.27

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{q}$ be a prime of $L$ lying above $\mathfrak{p}=\mathfrak{q} \cap A$ for which the residue field extension $(B/\mathfrak{q})/(A/\mathfrak{p})$ is separable. Then $e_{\mathfrak{q}}-1 \leq v_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathcal{D}_{B/A}) \leq e_{\mathfrak{q}}-1+v_{\mathfrak{q}}(e_{\mathfrak{q}})$, and the lower bound is an equality if and only if $\mathfrak{q}$ is tamely ramified.
