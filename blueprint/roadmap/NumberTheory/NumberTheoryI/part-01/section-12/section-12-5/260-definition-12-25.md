# Definition 12.25

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\alpha \in B$ have minimal polynomial $f \in A[x]$. The different of $\alpha$ is defined by $\delta_{B/A}(\alpha) \coloneqq f'(\alpha)$ if $L=K(\alpha)$, and $\delta_{B/A}(\alpha) \coloneqq 0$ otherwise.
