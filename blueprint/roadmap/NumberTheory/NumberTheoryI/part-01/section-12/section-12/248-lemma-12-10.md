# Lemma 12.10

Assume $AKLB$ and let $M' \subseteq M$ be free $A$-lattices in $L$. The discriminants $D(M') \subseteq D(M)$ are nonzero principal fractional ideals. If $D(M') = D(M)$ then $M' = M$.
