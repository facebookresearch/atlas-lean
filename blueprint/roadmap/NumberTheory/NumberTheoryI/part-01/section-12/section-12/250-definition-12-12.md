# Definition 12.12

Assume $AKLB$. The discriminant $D_{L/K}$ of $L/K$ (and the discriminant $D_{B/A}$ of $B/A$) is the discriminant of $B$ as an $A$-module: $$D_{L/K} \coloneqq D_{B/A} \coloneqq D(B) \in \mathcal{I}_A$$ which is an $A$-ideal, since $\operatorname{disc}(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = \det[T_{B/A}(x_i x_j)]_{i,j} \in A$ for all $x_1, \ldots, x_n \in B$.
