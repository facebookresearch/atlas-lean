# Definition 12.9

Assume $AKLB$, let $M$ be an $A$-lattice in $L$, and let $n \coloneqq [L:K]$. The discriminant $D(M)$ of $M$ is the $A$-module generated by $\{\operatorname{disc}(x_1, \ldots, x_n) : x_1, \ldots, x_n \in M\}$.
