# Definition 12.2

Assume $AKLB$. The different $\mathcal{D}_{L/K}$ of $L/K$ (and the different $\mathcal{D}_{B/A}$ of $B/A$), is the inverse of $B^*$ in $\mathcal{I}_B$. Explicitly, we have $B^* \coloneqq \{x \in L : \mathrm{T}_{L/K}(xb) \in A \text{ for all } b \in B\}$ and we define $\mathcal{D}_{L/K} \coloneqq \mathcal{D}_{B/A} \coloneqq (B^*)^{-1} = (B:B^*) = \{x \in L : xB^* \subseteq B\}$.
