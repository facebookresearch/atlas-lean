# Theorem 16.17

A sequence or series of holomorphic functions $f_n$ that converges locally uniformly on an open set $U$ converges to a holomorphic function $f$ on $U$, and the sequence or series of derivatives $f_n'$ then converges locally uniformly to $f'$ (and if none of the $f_n$ has a zero in $U$ and $f \neq 0$, then $f$ has no zeros in $U$).
