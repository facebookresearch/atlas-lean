# Definition 16.9

Let $h: \mathbb{R}_{>0} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a piecewise continuous function. The Laplace transform $\mathcal{L} h$ of $h$ is the complex function defined by $\mathcal{L} h(s) \coloneqq \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} h(t) dt$, which is holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re}(s) > c$ for any $c \in \mathbb{R}$ for which $h(t) = O(e^{ct})$.
