# Lemma 16.8

Let $f: \mathbb{R}_{\geq 1} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a nondecreasing function. If the integral $\int_{1}^{\infty} \frac{f(t)-t}{t^{2}} dt$ converges then $f(x) \sim x$.
