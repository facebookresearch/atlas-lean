# Theorem 16.2 (Euler product)

For $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$ we have $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n \geq 1} n^{-s}=\prod_{p}\left(1-p^{-s}\right)^{-1}$ where the product converges absolutely. In particular, $\zeta(s) \neq 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$.
