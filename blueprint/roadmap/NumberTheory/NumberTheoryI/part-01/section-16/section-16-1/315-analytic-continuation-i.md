# Theorem 16.3 (Analytic continuation I)

For $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$ we have $\zeta(s)=\frac{1}{s-1}+\phi(s)$ where $\phi(s)$ is a holomorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}(s)>0$. Thus $\zeta(s)$ extends to a meromorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}(s)>0$ that has a simple pole at $s=1$ with residue 1 and no other poles.
