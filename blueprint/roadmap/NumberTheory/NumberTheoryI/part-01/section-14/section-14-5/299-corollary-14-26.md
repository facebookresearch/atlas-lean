# Corollary 14.26

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$ with $s$ complex places. Then $|D_{K}| \geq \left(\frac{n^{n}}{n!}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^{2s} > \frac{1}{e^{2} n}\left(\frac{\pi e^{2}}{4}\right)^{n}$.
