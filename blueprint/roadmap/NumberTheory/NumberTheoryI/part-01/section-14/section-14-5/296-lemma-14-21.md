# Lemma 14.21

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$ and let $M \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$. The number of $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideals of norm $\mathrm{N}(I) \leq M$ is bounded by $(nM)^{\log_{2} M}$ (and in particular, finite).
