# Theorem 14.20

Let $K$ be a number field. Every ideal class in $\mathrm{cl}\, \mathcal{O}_{K}$ contains an ideal $I \subseteq \mathcal{O}_{K}$ of absolute norm $\mathrm{N}(I) \leq m_{K}$, where $m_{K}$ is the Minkowski constant for $K$.
