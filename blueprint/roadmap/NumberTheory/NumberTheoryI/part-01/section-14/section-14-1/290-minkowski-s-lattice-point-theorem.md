# Theorem 14.13 (Minkowski's Lattice Point Theorem)

Let $\Lambda$ be a lattice in $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and $\mu$ a Haar measure on $V$. If $S \subseteq V$ is a symmetric convex measurable set that satisfies $\mu(S)>2^{n} \operatorname{covol}(\Lambda)$, then $S$ contains a nonzero element of $\Lambda$.
