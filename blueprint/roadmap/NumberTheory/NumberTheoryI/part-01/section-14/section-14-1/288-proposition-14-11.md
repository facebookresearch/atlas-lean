# Proposition 14.11

If $\Lambda' \subseteq \Lambda$ are lattices in $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$, then $\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda')=[\Lambda:\Lambda'] \operatorname{covol}(\Lambda)$.
