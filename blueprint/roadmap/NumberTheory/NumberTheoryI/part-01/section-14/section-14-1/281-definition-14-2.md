# Definition 14.2

A subgroup $H$ of a topological group $G$ is discrete if the subspace topology on $H$ is the discrete topology (every point is open), and cocompact if $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$ and the quotient $G/H$ is compact (here $G/H$ denotes the group $G/H$ with the quotient topology given by identifying elements of $G$ that lie in the same coset of $H$).
