# Proposition 14.9

Let $\Lambda$ be a lattice in $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and let $\mu$ be a Haar measure on $V$. Every fundamental domain for $\Lambda$ has the same measure, and this measure is finite and nonzero.
