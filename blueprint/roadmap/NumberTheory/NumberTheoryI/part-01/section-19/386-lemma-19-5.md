# Lemma 19.5

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a measurable set whose boundary $\partial S\coloneqq \bar{S}-S^{0}$ is $(n-1)$-Lipschitz parametrizable. Then $$\#\left(t S \cap \mathbb{Z}^{n}\right)=\mu(S) t^{n}+O\left(t^{n-1}\right)$$ as $t \rightarrow \infty$, where $\mu$ is the standard Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$.
