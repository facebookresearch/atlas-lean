# Theorem 19.8

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$. As $t \rightarrow \infty$, the number of nonzero $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideals $\mathfrak{a}$ of absolute norm $\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{a}) \leq t$ is $$\left(\frac{2^{r}(2\pi)^{s} h_{K} R_{K}}{w_{K}\left|D_{K}\right|^{1 / 2}}\right) t+O\left(t^{1-1 / n}\right)$$ where $r$ and $s$ are the number of real and complex places of $K$, respectively, $h_{K}=\# \operatorname{cl} \mathcal{O}_{K}$ is the class number, $R_{K}$ is the regulator, $w_{K}\coloneqq \# \mu_{K}$ is the number of roots of unity, and $D_{K}\coloneqq \operatorname{disc} \mathcal{O}_{K}$ is the absolute discriminant.
