# Theorem (Analytic Class Number Formula)

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n$. The Dedekind zeta function $\zeta_{K}(z)$ extends to a meromorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 1 - \frac{1}{n}$ that is holomorphic except for a simple pole at $z = 1$ with residue $\lim_{z \to 1^{+}}(z-1)\zeta_{K}(z) = \frac{2^{r}(2\pi)^{s} h_{K} R_{K}}{w_{K} |D_{K}|^{1/2}}$ where $r$ and $s$ are the number of real and complex places of $K$, respectively, $h_{K} \coloneqq \# \operatorname{cl} \mathcal{O}_{K}$ is the class number, $R_{K}$ is the regulator, $w_{K} \coloneqq \# \mu_{K}$ is the number of roots of unity, and $D_{K} \coloneqq \operatorname{disc} \mathcal{O}_{K}$ is the absolute discriminant.
