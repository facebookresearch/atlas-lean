# Corollary 19.6

Let $\Lambda$ be a lattice in an $\mathbb{R}$-vector space $V \simeq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and let $S \subseteq V$ be a measurable set whose boundary is $(n-1)$-Lipschitz parametrizable. Then $$\#(t S \cap \Lambda)=\frac{\mu(S)}{\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda)} t^{n}+O\left(t^{n-1}\right).$$
