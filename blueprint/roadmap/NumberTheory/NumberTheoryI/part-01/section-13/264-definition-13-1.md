# Definition 13.1

Let $K$ be a field. A place of $K$ is an equivalence class of nontrivial absolute values on $K$. We may use $M_K$ to denote the set of places of $K$, and for each place $v$ we use $|\,|_v$ to denote any representative absolute value and $K_v$ to denote the completion of $K$ with respect to $|\,|_v$. We call a place $v$ archimedean when $K_v$ is archimedean and nonarchimedean otherwise.
