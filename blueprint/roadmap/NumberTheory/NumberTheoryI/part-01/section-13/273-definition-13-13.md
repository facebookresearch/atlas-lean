# Definition 13.13

A locally compact group is a topological group that is Hausdorff and locally compact. A (left) Haar measure $\mu$ on a locally compact group $G$ is a nonzero Radon measure that is translation invariant, meaning that $\mu(S)=\mu(x+S)$ for all $x \in G$ and measurable $S \subseteq G$ (we write the group operation additively because we have in mind the additive group of a local field). A compact group is a locally compact group that is compact; in compact groups every measurable set has finite measure.
