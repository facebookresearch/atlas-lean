# Lemma 13.19

Let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension of global fields, let $v$ be a place of $K$ and let $w \mid v$ be a place of $L$. Then $\|x\|_{w}=\left\|\mathrm{N}_{L_{w}/K_{v}}(x)\right\|_{v}$.
