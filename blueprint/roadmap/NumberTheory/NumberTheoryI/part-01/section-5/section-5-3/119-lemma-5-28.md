# Lemma 5.28

Let $A$ be a ring of dimension one contained in a Dedekind domain $B$. Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$ and let $\mathfrak{q}$ be a prime of $B$. Then $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$ if and only if $\mathfrak{q} \cap A = \mathfrak{p}$.
