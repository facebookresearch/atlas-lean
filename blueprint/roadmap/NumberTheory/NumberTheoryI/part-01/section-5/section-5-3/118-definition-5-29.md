# Definition 5.29

Assume $AKLB$, and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. The exponent $e_{\mathfrak{q}}$ in the factorization $\mathfrak{p}B = \prod_{\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}} \mathfrak{q}^{e_{\mathfrak{q}}}$ is the ramification index of $\mathfrak{q}$, and the degree $f_{\mathfrak{q}} = [B/\mathfrak{q} : A/\mathfrak{p}]$ of the corresponding residue field extension is the residue degree (or inertia degree) of $\mathfrak{q}$. In situations where more than one extension of Dedekind domains is under consideration, we may write $e_{\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak{p}}$ for $e_{\mathfrak{q}}$ and $f_{\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak{p}}$ for $f_{\mathfrak{q}}$.
