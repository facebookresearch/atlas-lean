# Theorem 5.5

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ be a free $A$-module of rank $n$. Then $M^{\vee}$ is also a free $A$-module of rank $n$, and each basis $(e_1, \ldots, e_n)$ of $M$ uniquely determines a dual basis $(e_1^{\vee}, \ldots, e_n^{\vee})$ of $M^{\vee}$ with the property $e_i^{\vee}(e_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
