# Proposition 5.7

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ be a free $A$-module of rank $n$ with a perfect pairing $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$. For each $A$-basis $(e_1, \ldots, e_n)$ of $M$ there is a unique basis $(e_1', \ldots, e_n')$ for $M$ such that $\langle e_i', e_j\rangle = \delta_{ij}$.
