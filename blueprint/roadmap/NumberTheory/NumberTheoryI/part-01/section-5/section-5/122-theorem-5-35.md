# Theorem 5.35

Assume $AKLB$. For each prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $A$ we have $$\sum_{\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}} e_{\mathfrak{q}} f_{\mathfrak{q}} = [L:K].$$
