# Lemma 5.33

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. The dimension of $B/\mathfrak{p}B$ as an $A/\mathfrak{p}$-vector space is equal to the dimension of $L$ as a $K$-vector space.
