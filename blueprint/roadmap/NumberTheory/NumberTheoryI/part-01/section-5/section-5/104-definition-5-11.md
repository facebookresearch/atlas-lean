# Definition 5.11

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, and let $V$ be a $K$-vector space of finite dimension with a perfect pairing $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle$. If $M$ is an $A$-lattice in $V$, its dual lattice (with respect to the perfect pairing $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle$ on $V$) is the $A$-module $M^* \coloneqq \{x \in V : \langle x, m\rangle \in A \text{ for all } m \in M\}$.
