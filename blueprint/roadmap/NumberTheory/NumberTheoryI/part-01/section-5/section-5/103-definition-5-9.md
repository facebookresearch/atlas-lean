# Definition 5.9

Let $A$ be an integral domain with fraction field $K$ and let $V$ be a $K$-vector space of finite dimension. A (full) $A$-lattice in $V$ is a finitely generated $A$-submodule $M$ of $V$ that spans $V$ as a $K$-vector space.
