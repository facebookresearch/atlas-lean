# Corollary 5.36

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $A$. Then $g_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is an integer in the interval $[1, n]$, where $n = [L:K]$, as are $e_{\mathfrak{q}}$ and $f_{\mathfrak{q}}$ for each $\mathfrak{q} \mid \mathfrak{p}$.
