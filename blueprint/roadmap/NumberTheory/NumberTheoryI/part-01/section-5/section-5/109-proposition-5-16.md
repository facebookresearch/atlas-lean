# Proposition 5.16

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, let $V$ be a $K$-vector space of finite dimension with a symmetric perfect pairing $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle$, and let $M$ be an $A$-lattice in $V$. Then $M^{**} = M$.
