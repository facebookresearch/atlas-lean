# Lemma 5.15

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain with fraction field $K$, let $V$ be a $K$-vector space of finite dimension with a perfect pairing $\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle$, let $M$ be an $A$-lattice in $V$, and let $S$ be a multiplicative subset of $A$. Then $S^{-1}M$ and $S^{-1}M^*$ are $(S^{-1}A)$-lattices in $V$ satisfying $(S^{-1}M)^* = S^{-1}M^*$.
