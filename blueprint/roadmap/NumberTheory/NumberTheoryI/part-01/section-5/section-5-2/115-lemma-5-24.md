# Lemma 5.24

Let $B/A$ be an extension of domains with $B$ integral over $A$, and let $\mathfrak{q}_0 \subsetneq \mathfrak{q}_1$ be primes of $B$. Then $\mathfrak{q}_0 \cap A \subsetneq \mathfrak{q}_1 \cap A$ and $\dim A \geq \dim B$.
