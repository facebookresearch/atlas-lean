# Proposition 5.22

Assume $AKLB$ (i.e., $A$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, $L/K$ is a finite separable extension, and $B$ is the integral closure of $A$ in $L$). Then $B$ is an $A$-lattice in $L$, and in particular, $B$ is finitely generated as an $A$-module.
