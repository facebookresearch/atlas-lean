# Proposition 5.17

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite extension, and let $B$ be the integral closure of $A$ in $L$. Every element of $L$ can be written as $b/a$ with $a \in A$ and $b \in B$. In particular, $B$ spans $L$ as a $K$-vector space and $L$ is the fraction field of $B$.
