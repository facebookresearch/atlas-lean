# Corollary 18.16

Let $M(m)$ denote the set of Dirichlet characters of modulus $m$, let $X(m)$ denote the set of primitive Dirichlet characters of conductor dividing $m$, and let $\widehat{G}(m)$ denote the character group of $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$. We have canonical bijections $M(m) \to X(m) \to \widehat{G}(m)$ given by $\chi \mapsto \widetilde{\chi} \mapsto (n \mapsto \widetilde{\chi}(n))$.
