# Definition 18.9

Let $\chi_1$ and $\chi_2$ be Dirichlet characters of modulus $m_1$ and $m_2$, respectively, with $m_1 \mid m_2$. If $\chi_2(n) = \chi_1(n)$ for $n \in (\mathbb{Z}/m_2\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$ then $\chi_2$ is induced by $\chi_1$. A Dirichlet character that is not induced by any character other than itself is primitive.
