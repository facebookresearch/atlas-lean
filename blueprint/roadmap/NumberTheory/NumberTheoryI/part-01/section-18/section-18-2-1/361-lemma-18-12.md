# Lemma 18.12

Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character of modulus $m$. Then $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}} \chi(n) \neq 0$ if and only if $\chi = \mathbb{1}_m$.
