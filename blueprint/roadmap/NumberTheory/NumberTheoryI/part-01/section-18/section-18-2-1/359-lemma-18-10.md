# Lemma 18.10

A Dirichlet character $\chi_2$ of modulus $m_2$ is induced by a Dirichlet character of modulus $m_1 \mid m_2$ if and only if $\chi_2$ is constant on residue classes in $(\mathbb{Z}/m_2\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$ that are congruent modulo $m_1$. When this holds, the Dirichlet character $\chi_1$ of modulus $m_1$ that induces $\chi_2$ is uniquely determined.
