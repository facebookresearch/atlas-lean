# Definition 18.11

A Dirichlet character $\chi$ induced by $\mathbb{1}$ is called principal (and is primitive if and only if $\chi = \mathbb{1}$). For $m \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ we use $\mathbb{1}_m$ to denote the principal Dirichlet character of modulus $m$; it corresponds to the trivial character of $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$.
