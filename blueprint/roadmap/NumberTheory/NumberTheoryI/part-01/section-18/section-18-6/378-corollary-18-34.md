# Corollary 18.34

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group. Then $g \in G$ is the identity if and only if $\chi(g) = 1$ for all $\chi \in \widehat{G}$ and $\chi \in \widehat{G}$ is the identity if and only if $\chi(g) = 1$ for all $g \in G$.
