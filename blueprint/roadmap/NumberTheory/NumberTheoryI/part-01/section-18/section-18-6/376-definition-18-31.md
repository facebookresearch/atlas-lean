# Definition 18.31

A character of a group $G$ is a homomorphism $\chi: G \to \mathrm{U}(1)$. The character group (or dual group) of $G$ is the abelian group $\widehat{G} \coloneqq \operatorname{Hom}(G, \mathrm{U}(1))$ under pointwise multiplication: $(\chi_{1}\chi_{2})(g) \coloneqq \chi_{1}(g)\chi_{2}(g)$. The inverse of $\chi$ is given by complex conjugation: $\chi^{-1}(g) = \bar{\chi}(g) \coloneqq \overline{\chi(g)}$. The identity element of $\widehat{G}$ is the trivial character $g \mapsto 1$.
