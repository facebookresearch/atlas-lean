# Corollary 18.38

For $\chi \in \widehat{G}$ we have $\sum_{g \in G} \chi(g) \neq 0$ if and only if $\chi$ is the trivial character. For $g \in G$ we have $\sum_{\chi \in \widehat{G}} \chi(g) \neq 0$ if and only if $g$ is the trivial element.
