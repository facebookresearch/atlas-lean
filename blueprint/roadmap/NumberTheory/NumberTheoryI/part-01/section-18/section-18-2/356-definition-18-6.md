# Definition 18.6

A Dirichlet character of modulus $m$ is an $m$-periodic Dirichlet character $\chi$ that is the extension by zero of a group character on $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$; equivalently, an $m$-periodic Dirichlet character for which $\chi(n) \neq 0 \iff m \perp n$.
