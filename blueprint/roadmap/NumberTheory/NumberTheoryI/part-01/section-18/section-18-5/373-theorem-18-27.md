# Theorem 18.27

Let $f$ and $g$ be functions on $[a, b]$ of bounded variation such that for every $c \in [a, b]$ the function $f$ is continuous from the left at $c$ and the function $g$ is continuous from the right at $c$. Then $\int_{a}^{b} f\,dg$ and $\int_{a}^{b} g\,df$ both exist.
