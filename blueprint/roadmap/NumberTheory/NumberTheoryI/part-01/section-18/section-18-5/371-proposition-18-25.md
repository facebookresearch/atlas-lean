# Proposition 18.25

Let $f$ and $g$ be real-valued functions on $[a, b]$ and suppose $g$ has a continuous derivative $g'$ on $[a, b]$. Then $$\int_a^b f\,dg = \int_a^b f(x) g'(x)\,dx.$$
