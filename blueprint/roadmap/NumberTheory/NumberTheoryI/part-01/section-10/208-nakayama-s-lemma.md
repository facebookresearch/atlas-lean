# Lemma 10.9 (Nakayama's Lemma)

Let $A$ be a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, and let $M$ be a finitely generated $A$-module. If the images of $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n} \in M$ generate $M/\mathfrak{p} M$ as an $(A/\mathfrak{p})$-vector space then $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ generate $M$ as an $A$-module.
