# Definition 10.1

Let $K$ be a field with absolute value $|\;|$ and let $V$ be a $K$-vector space. A norm on $V$ is a function $\|\;\|: V \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ such that: (1) $\|v\|=0$ if and only if $v=0$; (2) $\|\lambda v\|=|\lambda|\|v\|$ for all $\lambda \in K$ and $v \in V$; (3) $\|v+w\| \leq \|v\|+\|w\|$ for all $v, w \in V$.
