# Corollary 10.18

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$ and finite residue field of characteristic $p$, and suppose that $K$ does not contain a primitive $p$th root of unity. The extension $K(\zeta_m)/K$ is ramified if and only if $p$ divides $m$.
