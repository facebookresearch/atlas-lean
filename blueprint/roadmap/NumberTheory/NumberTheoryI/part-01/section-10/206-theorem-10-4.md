# Theorem 10.4

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$, maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, discrete valuation $v_{\mathfrak{p}}$, and absolute value $|x|_{\mathfrak{p}}\coloneqq c^{v_{\mathfrak{p}}(x)}$, with $0\lt c<1$. Let $L/K$ be a finite extension of degree $n$. The following hold: (i) There is a unique absolute value $|x|\coloneqq |\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(x)|_{\mathfrak{p}}^{1/n}$ on $L$ that extends $|\;|_{\mathfrak{p}}$; (ii) The field $L$ is complete with respect to $|\;|$, and its valuation ring $\{x \in L:|x| \leq 1\}$ is equal to the integral closure $B$ of $A$ in $L$; (iii) If $L/K$ is separable then $B$ is a complete DVR whose maximal ideal $\mathfrak{q}$ induces $|x|=|x|_{\mathfrak{q}}\coloneqq c^{\frac{1}{e_{\mathfrak{q}}} v_{\mathfrak{q}}(x)}$, where $e_{\mathfrak{q}}$ is the ramification index of $\mathfrak{q}$, that is, $\mathfrak{p} B=\mathfrak{q}^{e_{\mathfrak{q}}}$.
