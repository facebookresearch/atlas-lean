# Corollary 10.16

Let $A$ be a complete DVR with fraction field $K$ and residue field $k$, and let $\zeta_n$ be a primitive $n$th root of unity in some algebraic closure of $K$, with $n$ prime to the characteristic of $k$. The extension $K(\zeta_n)/K$ is unramified.
