# Corollary 10.15

Assume $AKLB$ with $A$ a complete DVR with residue field $k$. Then $L/K$ is unramified if and only if $B=A[\alpha]$ for some $\alpha \in L$ whose minimal polynomial $g \in A[x]$ has separable image $\bar{g}$ in $k[x]$.
