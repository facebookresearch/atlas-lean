# Corollary 10.11

Let $A$ be a local noetherian ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, let $g \in A[x]$ be a polynomial with reduction $\bar{g} \in (A/\mathfrak{p})[x]$, and let $\alpha$ be the image of $x$ in the ring $B\coloneqq A[x]/(g(x))=A[\alpha]$. The maximal ideals of $B$ are $(\mathfrak{p}, g_i(\alpha))$, where $g_1, \ldots, g_m \in A[x]$ are lifts of the distinct irreducible polynomials $\bar{g}_i \in (A/\mathfrak{p})[x]$ that divide $\bar{g}$.
