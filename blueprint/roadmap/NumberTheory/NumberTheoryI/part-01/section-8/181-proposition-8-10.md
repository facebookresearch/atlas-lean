# Proposition 8.10

Let $(X_i, f_{ij})$ be an inverse system of Hausdorff topological spaces. Then $X \coloneqq \varprojlim X_i$ is a closed subset of $\prod X_i$, and if the $X_i$ are compact then $X$ is compact.
