# Proposition 8.17

Every element of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ has a unique $p$-adic expansion and every sequence $(b_0, b_1, b_2, \ldots)$ of integers in $[0, p-1]$ is the $p$-adic expansion of an element of $\mathbb{Z}_p$.
