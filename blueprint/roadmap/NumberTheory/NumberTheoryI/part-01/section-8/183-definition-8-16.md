# Definition 8.16

Let $a = (a_n)$ be a $p$-adic integer with each $a_n$ uniquely represented by an integer in $[0, p^n - 1]$. The sequence $(b_0, b_1, b_2, \ldots)$ with $b_0 = a_1$ and $b_n = (a_{n+1} - a_n)/p^n$ is called the $p$-adic expansion of $a$.
