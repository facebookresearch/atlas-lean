# Theorem 8.5 (Weak Approximation)

Let $K$ be a field and let $|\cdot|_1, \ldots, |\cdot|_n$ be pairwise inequivalent nontrivial absolute values on $K$. Let $a_1, \ldots, a_n \in K$ and let $\epsilon_1, \ldots, \epsilon_n$ be positive real numbers. Then there exists an $x \in K$ such that $|x - a_i|_i < \epsilon_i$ for $1 \leq i \leq n$.
