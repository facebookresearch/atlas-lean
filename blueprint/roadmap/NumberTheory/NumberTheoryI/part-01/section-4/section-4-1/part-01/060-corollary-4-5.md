# Corollary 4.5

Let $f \in K[x]$ be irreducible and let $p \geq 0$ be the characteristic of $K$. We have $f(x) = g(x^{p^n})$ for some irreducible separable $g \in K[x]$ and integer $n \geq 0$ that are uniquely determined by $f$.
