# Lemma 4.7

Let $L = K(\alpha)$ be an algebraic field extension contained in an algebraic closure $\bar{K}$ of $K$ and let $f \in K[x]$ be the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $K$. Then
$$\# \operatorname{Hom}_K(L, \bar{K}) = \#\{\beta \in \bar{K} : f(\beta) = 0\} \leq [L:K]$$
with equality if and only if $\alpha$ is separable over $K$.
