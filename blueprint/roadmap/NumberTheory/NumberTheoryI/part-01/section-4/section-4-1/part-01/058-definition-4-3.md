# Definition 4.3

Let $L/K$ be an algebraic field extension. An element $\alpha \in L$ is separable over $K$ if it is the root of a separable polynomial in $K[x]$ (in which case its minimal polynomial is necessarily separable). The extension $L/K$ is separable if every $\alpha \in L$ is separable over $K$; otherwise it is inseparable.
