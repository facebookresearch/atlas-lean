# Definition 4.8

Let $L/K$ be a finite extension of fields. The separable degree of $L/K$ is $[L:K]_s \coloneqq \# \operatorname{Hom}_K(L, \bar{K})$. The inseparable degree of $L/K$ is $[L:K]_i \coloneqq [L:K]/[L:K]_s$.
