# Lemma 4.10

Let $L/F/K$ be a tower of finite extensions of fields and $\bar{K}$ be an algebraic closure of $K$ that contains $L$. Then
$$\# \operatorname{Hom}_K(L, \bar{K}) = \# \operatorname{Hom}_K(F, \bar{K}) \cdot \# \operatorname{Hom}_F(L, \bar{K}).$$
