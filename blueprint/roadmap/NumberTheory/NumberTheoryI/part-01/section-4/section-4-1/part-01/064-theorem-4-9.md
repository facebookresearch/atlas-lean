# Theorem 4.9

Let $L/K$ be an algebraic field extension and let $\phi_K : K \to \Omega$ be a homomorphism to an algebraically closed field $\Omega$. Then $\phi_K$ extends to a homomorphism $\phi_L : L \to \Omega$.
