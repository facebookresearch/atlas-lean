# Corollary 4.39

Let $L \simeq K[x] /(f)$ be a finite separable extension of a field $K$ defined by an irreducible separable polynomial $f \in K[x]$. Let $K^{\prime} / K$ be any field extension, and let $f=f_{1} \cdots f_{m}$ be the factorization of $f$ into distinct irreducible polynomials $f_{i} \in K^{\prime}[x]$. We have an isomorphism of finite étale $K^{\prime}$-algebras $L \otimes_{K} K^{\prime} \simeq \prod_{i} K^{\prime}[x] /\left(f_{i}\right)$ where each $K^{\prime}[x] /\left(f_{i}\right)$ is a finite separable field extension of $K^{\prime}$.
