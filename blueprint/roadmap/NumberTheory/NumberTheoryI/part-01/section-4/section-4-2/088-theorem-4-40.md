# Theorem 4.40

Let $L$ be a commutative $K$-algebra of finite dimension and assume that the dimension of $L$ is less than the cardinality of $K$. The following are equivalent: (a) $L$ is a finite étale $K$-algebra. (b) Every element of $L$ is separable over $K$. (c) $L \otimes_{K} K^{\prime}$ is reduced for every extension $K^{\prime} / K$. (d) $L \otimes_{K} K^{\prime}$ is semisimple for every extension $K^{\prime} / K$. (e) $L=K[x] /(f)$ for some separable $f \in K[x]$. The implications $(a) \Leftrightarrow(b) \Leftrightarrow(c) \Leftrightarrow(d) \Leftarrow(e)$ hold regardless of the dimension of $L$.
