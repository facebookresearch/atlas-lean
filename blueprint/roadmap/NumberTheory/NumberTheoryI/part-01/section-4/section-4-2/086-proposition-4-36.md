# Proposition 4.36

Suppose $L$ is a finite étale $K$-algebra and $K^{\prime} / K$ is any field extension. Then $L \otimes_{K} K^{\prime}$ is a finite étale $K^{\prime}$-algebra of the same dimension as $L$.
