# Proposition 4.43

Suppose $L$ is a finite étale $K$-algebra and $\Omega$ is a separably closed field extension of $K$. There is an isomorphism of finite étale $\Omega$-algebras $L \otimes_{K} \Omega \xrightarrow{\sim} \prod_{\sigma \in \operatorname{Hom}_{K}(L, \Omega)} \Omega$ that sends $\beta \otimes 1$ to the vector $(\sigma(\beta))_{\sigma}$ for each $\beta \in L$.
