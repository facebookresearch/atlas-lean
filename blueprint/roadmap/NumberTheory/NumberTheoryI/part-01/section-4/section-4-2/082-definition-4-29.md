# Definition 4.29

Let $K$ be a field. An étale $K$-algebra is a $K$-algebra $L$ that is isomorphic to a finite product of separable field extensions of $K$. The dimension of an étale $K$-algebra is its dimension as a $K$-vector space. When this dimension is finite we say that $L$ is a finite étale $K$-algebra. A homomorphism of étale $K$-algebras is a homomorphism of $K$-algebras (which means a ring homomorphism that commutes with scalar multiplication).
