# Definition 4.34

Let $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a homomorphism of rings (so $B$ is an $A$-module), and let $M$ be any $A$-module. The tensor product of $A$-modules $M \otimes_{A} B$ is a $B$-module (with multiplication defined by $b\left(m \otimes b^{\prime}\right)\coloneqq m \otimes b b^{\prime}$) called the base change (or extension of scalars) of $M$ from $A$ to $B$. If $M$ is an $A$-algebra then its base change to $B$ is a $B$-algebra.
