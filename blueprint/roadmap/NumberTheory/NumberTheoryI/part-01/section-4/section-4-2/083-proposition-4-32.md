# Proposition 4.32

Let $A=\prod K_{i}$ be a $K$-algebra written that is a product of field extensions $K_{i} / K$. Every surjective homomorphism $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ of $K$-algebras corresponds to the projection of $A$ on to a subproduct of its factors.
