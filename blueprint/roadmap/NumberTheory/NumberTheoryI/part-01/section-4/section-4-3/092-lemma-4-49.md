# Lemma 4.49

Let $B/A$ be a ring extension with $B$ free of rank $n$ over $A$, and let $\varphi: A \rightarrow A'$ be a ring homomorphism. The base change $B' = B \otimes_A A'$ of $B$ to $A'$ is a free $A'$-module of rank $n$, and for every $b \in B$ we have $\varphi(\mathrm{N}_{B/A}(b)) = \mathrm{N}_{B'/A'}(b \otimes 1)$ and $\varphi(\mathrm{T}_{B/A}(b)) = \mathrm{T}_{B'/A'}(b \otimes 1)$.
