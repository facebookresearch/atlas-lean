# Definition 4.46

Let $B/A$ be a (commutative) ring extension in which $B$ is a free $A$-module of finite rank. The (relative) norm $\mathrm{N}_{B/A}(b)$ and trace $\mathrm{T}_{B/A}(b)$ of $b$ (down to $A$) are the determinant and trace of the $A$-linear multiplication-by-$b$ map $B \rightarrow B$ defined by $x \mapsto bx$.
