# Theorem 4.50

Let $K$ be a field with separable closure $\Omega$ and let $L$ be a finite étale $K$-algebra. For all $\alpha \in L$ we have $\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(\alpha) = \prod_{\sigma \in \operatorname{Hom}_K(L,\Omega)} \sigma(\alpha)$ and $\mathrm{T}_{L/K}(\alpha) = \sum_{\sigma \in \operatorname{Hom}_K(L,\Omega)} \sigma(\alpha)$.
