# Theorem 4.53 (Transitivity of Norm and Trace)

Let $A \subseteq B \subseteq C$ be rings with $C$ free of finite rank over $B$ and $B$ free of finite rank over $A$. Then $C$ is free of finite rank over $A$ and $\mathrm{N}_{C/A} = \mathrm{N}_{B/A} \circ \mathrm{N}_{C/B}$ and $\mathrm{T}_{C/A} = \mathrm{T}_{B/A} \circ \mathrm{T}_{C/B}$.
