# Theorem 3.11

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain. The ideal group $\mathcal{I}_{A}$ of $A$ is the free abelian group generated by its nonzero prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}$. The isomorphism $\mathcal{I}_{A} \simeq \bigoplus_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathbb{Z}$ is given by the inverse maps $I \mapsto (\ldots, v_{\mathfrak{p}}(I), \ldots)$ and $\prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathfrak{p}^{e_{\mathfrak{p}}} \leftarrow (\ldots, e_{\mathfrak{p}}, \ldots)$.
