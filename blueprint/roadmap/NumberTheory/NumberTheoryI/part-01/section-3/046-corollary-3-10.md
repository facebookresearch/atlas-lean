# Corollary 3.10

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$. For each nonzero fractional ideal $I$ we have $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(I)=0$ for all but finitely many prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}$. In particular, if $x \in K^{\times}$ then $v_{\mathfrak{p}}(x)=0$ for all but finitely many $\mathfrak{p}$.
