# Definition 6.30

Let $A$ be a noetherian domain and let $J$ be an ideal of $A$. A fractional ideal $I$ of $A$ is prime to $J$ if $I A_{\mathfrak{p}}=A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ for all prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}$ that contain $J$. The set of invertible fractional ideals prime to $J$ is denoted $\mathcal{I}_{A}^{J}$; it is a subgroup of the ideal group $\mathcal{I}_{A}$.
