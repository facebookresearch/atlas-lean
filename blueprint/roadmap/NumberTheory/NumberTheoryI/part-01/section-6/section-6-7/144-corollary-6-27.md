# Corollary 6.27

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order with integral closure $B$ and conductor $\mathfrak{c}$. The restriction of the map $\operatorname{Spec} B \rightarrow \operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}$ defined by $\mathfrak{q} \mapsto \mathfrak{q} \cap \mathcal{O}$ to prime ideals not containing $\mathfrak{c}$ is a bijection with inverse $\mathfrak{p} \mapsto \mathfrak{p} B$.
