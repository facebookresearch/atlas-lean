# Lemma 6.26

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order with integral closure $B$ and conductor $\mathfrak{c}$ and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime of $\mathcal{O}$ not containing $\mathfrak{c}$. Then $\mathfrak{p} B$ is a prime of $B$.
