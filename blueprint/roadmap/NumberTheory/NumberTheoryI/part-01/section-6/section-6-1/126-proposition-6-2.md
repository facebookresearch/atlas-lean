# Proposition 6.2

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$ and let $M$ and $N$ be $A$-lattices in a $K$-vector space of finite dimension. The module index $[M:N]_A$ is a nonzero fractional ideal of $A$ whose localization at each prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $A$ is equal to the local module index $[M_{\mathfrak{p}} : N_{\mathfrak{p}}]_{A_{\mathfrak{p}}}$.
