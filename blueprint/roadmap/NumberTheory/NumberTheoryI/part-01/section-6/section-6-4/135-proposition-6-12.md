# Proposition 6.12

Let $L$ be a number field with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_{L}$. For any nonzero $\mathcal{O}_{L}$-ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ we have $\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{a})=\left(\left[\mathcal{O}_{L}: \mathfrak{a}\right]\right)$. If $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{a}$ are nonzero fractional ideals of $\mathcal{O}_{L}$, then $[\mathfrak{a}: \mathfrak{b}]_{\mathbb{Z}}=([\mathfrak{a}: \mathfrak{b}])$.
