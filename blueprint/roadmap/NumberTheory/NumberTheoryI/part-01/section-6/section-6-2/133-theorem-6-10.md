# Theorem 6.10

Assume $AKLB$ and let $\mathfrak{q}$ be a prime lying above $\mathfrak{p}$. Then $N_{B/A}(\mathfrak{q})=\mathfrak{p}^{f_{\mathfrak{q}}}$, where $f_{\mathfrak{q}}=[B/\mathfrak{q}: A/\mathfrak{p}]$ is the residue field degree of $\mathfrak{q}$.
