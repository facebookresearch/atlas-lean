# Definition 6.5

Assume $AKLB$. The ideal norm $N_{B/A}: \mathcal{I}_{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{I}_{A}$ is the map $I \mapsto [B: I]_{A}$. We extend $N_{B/A}$ to the zero ideal by defining $N_{B/A}((0))=(0)$.
