# Corollary 6.9

Assume $AKLB$ and let $I$ be a fractional ideal of $B$. The ideal norm of $I$ is the fractional ideal of $A$ generated by the image of $I$ under the field norm $N_{L/K}$, that is, $N_{B/A}(I)=\left(\mathrm{N}_{L/K}(\alpha): \alpha \in I\right)$.
