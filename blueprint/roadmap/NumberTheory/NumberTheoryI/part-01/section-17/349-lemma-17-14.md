# Lemma 17.14

Let $f(s)$ be a holomorphic function on $\mathbb{C}$ such that $f(s+1)=f(s)$ and $|f(s)|=O\left(e^{\operatorname{Im}(s)}\right)$ as $\operatorname{Im}(s) \rightarrow \infty$ in the vertical strip $\operatorname{Re}(s) \in[0,1]$. Then $f$ is constant.
