# Theorem 17.13 (Euler's Reflection Formula)

We have $$\Gamma(s) \Gamma(1-s)=\frac{\pi}{\sin (\pi s)}$$ as meromorphic functions on $\mathbb{C}$ with simple poles at each integer $s \in \mathbb{Z}$.
