# Theorem 17.17 (Analytic Continuation II)

The function $$\xi(s)\coloneqq \binom{s}{2} \Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(s) \zeta(s)$$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{C}$ and satisfies the functional equation $$\xi(s)=\xi(1-s).$$ The zeros of $\xi(s)$ all lie in the critical strip $0\lt \operatorname{Re}(s)<1$.
