# Lemma 17.6

For all $a \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ and $f \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$, we have $\widehat{f(a x)}(y)=\frac{1}{a} \hat{f}\left(\frac{y}{a}\right)$.
