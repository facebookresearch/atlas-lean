# Theorem 17.8 (Poisson Summation Formula)

For all $f \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ we have the identity $$\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} f(n)=\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \hat{f}(n).$$
