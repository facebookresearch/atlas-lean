# Lemma 17.7

For $f \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ we have $\frac{d}{d y} \hat{f}(y)=-2 \pi i \widehat{x f(x)}(y)$ and $\widehat{\frac{d}{d x} f(x)}(y)=2 \pi i y \hat{f}(y)$.
