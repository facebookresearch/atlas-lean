# Definition 23.59

A sequence of morphisms $0 \rightarrow A \xrightarrow{f} B \xrightarrow{g} C \rightarrow 0$ is left exact if it is exact at $A$ and $B$ ($\ker f=0$ and $\operatorname{im} f=\ker g$), and right exact if it is exact at $B$ and $C$ ($\operatorname{im} f=\ker g$ and $\operatorname{im} g=C$). A functor that takes exact sequences to left (resp. right) exact sequences is said to be left exact (resp. right exact).
