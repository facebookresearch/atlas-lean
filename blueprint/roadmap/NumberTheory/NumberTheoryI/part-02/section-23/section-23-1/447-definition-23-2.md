# Definition 23.2

Let $A$ be a $G$-module. The $G$-invariants of $A$ constitute the $G$-module $A^{G}\coloneqq \{a \in A: ga=a \text{ for all } g \in G\}$ consisting of elements fixed by $G$. It is the largest trivial $G$-submodule of $A$.
