# Lemma 23.6

Let $\alpha: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of $G$-modules. We have induced group homomorphisms $\alpha^{n}: C^{n}(G, A) \rightarrow C^{n}(G, B)$ defined by $f \mapsto \alpha \circ f$ that commute with the coboundary maps. In particular, $\alpha^{n}$ maps cocycles to cocycles and coboundaries to coboundaries and thus induces homomorphisms $\alpha^{n}: H^{n}(G, A) \rightarrow H^{n}(G, B)$ of cohomology groups, and we have a morphism of cochain complexes $\alpha: \mathcal{C}_{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{B}$.
