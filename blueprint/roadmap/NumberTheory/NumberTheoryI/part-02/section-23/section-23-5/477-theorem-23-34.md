# Theorem 23.34

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $B$ be an induced or co-induced $G$-module associated to some abelian group $A$. Then $\hat{H}^{n}(G, B)=\hat{H}_{n}(G, B)=0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
