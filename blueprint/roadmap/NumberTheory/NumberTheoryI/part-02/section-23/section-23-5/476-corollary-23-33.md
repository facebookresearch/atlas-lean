# Corollary 23.33

Let $G$ be a finite group. For any $G$-modules $A$ and $B$ we have $\hat{H}^{n}(G, A \oplus B) \simeq \hat{H}^{n}(G, A) \oplus \hat{H}^{n}(G, B)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, and the isomorphisms commute with the natural inclusion and projection maps for the direct sums on both sides.
