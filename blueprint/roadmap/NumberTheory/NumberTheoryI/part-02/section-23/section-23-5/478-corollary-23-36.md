# Corollary 23.36

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $A$ be a free $\mathbb{Z}[G]$-module. Then we have $\hat{H}_{n}(G, A)=\hat{H}^{n}(G, A)=0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
