# Lemma 23.30

Let $A$ be a $G$-module and let $N_{G}: A \rightarrow A$ be the $G$-module endomorphism $a \mapsto N_{G} a$. We then have $I_{G} A \subseteq \ker N_{G}$ and $\operatorname{im} N_{G} \subseteq A^{G}$, thus $N_{G}$ induces a morphism $\hat{N}_{G}: A_{G} \rightarrow A^{G}$ of trivial $G$-modules.
