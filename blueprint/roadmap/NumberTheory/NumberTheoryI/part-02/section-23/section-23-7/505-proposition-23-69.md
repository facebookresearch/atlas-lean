# Proposition 23.69

Let $A$ be a left $R$-module and let $\bullet_*$ denote the application of the functor $\bullet \otimes_{R} A$. Let $f, g: C \rightarrow D$ be homotopic morphisms of chain complexes of right $R$-modules. Then $f_{*}, g_{*}: C_{*} \rightarrow D_{*}$ are homotopic morphisms of chain complexes of $\mathbb{Z}$-modules.
