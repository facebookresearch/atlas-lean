# Corollary 23.28

Let $G$ be a finite group, $A$ be an abelian group, and let $B$ be $\operatorname{Ind}^{G}(A)$ or $\operatorname{CoInd}^{G}(A)$. Then $H_{0}(G, B) \simeq H^{0}(G, B) \simeq A$ and $H_{n}(G, B)=H^{n}(G, B)=0$ for all $n \geq 1$.
