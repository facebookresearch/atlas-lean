# Definition 23.23

Let $G$ be a group. The augmentation map $\varepsilon: \mathbb{Z}[G] \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ is the ring homomorphism $\sum a_{g} g \mapsto \sum a_{g}$. The augmentation ideal $I_{G}$ is the kernel of the augmentation map; it is a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module with basis $\{g-1: g \in G\}$.
