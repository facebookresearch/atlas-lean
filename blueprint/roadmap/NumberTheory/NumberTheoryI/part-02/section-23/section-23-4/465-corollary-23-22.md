# Corollary 23.22

For any $G$-modules $A$ and $B$ we have $H_{n}(G, A \oplus B) \simeq H_{n}(G, A) \oplus H_{n}(G, B)$ for all $n \geq 0$, and the isomorphism commutes with the natural inclusion and projection maps for the direct sums on both sides.
