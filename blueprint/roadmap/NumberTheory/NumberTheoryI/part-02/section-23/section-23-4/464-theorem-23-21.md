# Theorem 23.21

Every short exact sequence of $G$-modules $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$ induces a long exact sequence of homology groups $\cdots \longrightarrow H_{1}(G, C) \xrightarrow{\delta_{0}} H_{0}(G, A) \xrightarrow{\alpha_{0}} H_{0}(G, B) \xrightarrow{\beta_{0}} H_{0}(G, C) \longrightarrow 0$, and commutative diagrams of short exact sequences of $G$-modules induce corresponding commutative diagrams of long exact sequences of homology groups.
