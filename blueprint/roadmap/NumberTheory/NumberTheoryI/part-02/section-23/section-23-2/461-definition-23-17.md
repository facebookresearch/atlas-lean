# Definition 23.17

Let $G$ be a group and let $A$ be an abelian group. The abelian group $\operatorname{CoInd}^{G}(A)\coloneqq \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[G], A)$ with $G$-action defined by $(g \varphi)(z)\coloneqq \varphi(zg)$ is the coinduced $G$-module associated to $A$.
