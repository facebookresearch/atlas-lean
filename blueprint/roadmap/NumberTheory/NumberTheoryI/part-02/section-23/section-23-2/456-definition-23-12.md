# Definition 23.12

Let $R$ be a (not necessarily commutative) ring. A free resolution $P$ of a (left) $R$-module $M$ is an exact sequence of free (left) $R$-modules $P_{n}$: $\cdots \xrightarrow{d_{n+1}} P_{n+1} \xrightarrow{d_{n}} P_{n} \xrightarrow{d_{n-1}} \cdots \xrightarrow{d_{1}} P_{1} \xrightarrow{d_{0}} M \longrightarrow 0$.
