# Lemma 23.19

Let $G$ be a group and $A$ an abelian group. Then $H^{0}(G, \operatorname{CoInd}^{G}(A)) \simeq A$ and $H^{n}(G, \operatorname{CoInd}^{G}(A))=0$ for all $n \geq 1$.
