# Corollary 23.14

Let $A$ be a $G$-module. The cochain complexes $\mathcal{S}_{A}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{A}$ have the same cohomology groups, in other words, $H^{n}(G, A) \simeq \operatorname{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}[G]}^{n}(\mathbb{Z}, A)$ for all $n \geq 0$, and we can compute $H^{n}(G, A)$ using any free resolution of $\mathbb{Z}$ by $G$-modules.
