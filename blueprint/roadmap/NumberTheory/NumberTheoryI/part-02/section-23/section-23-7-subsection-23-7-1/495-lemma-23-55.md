# Lemma 23.55

Homotopic morphisms of cochain complexes $f, g: C \rightarrow D$ induce the same morphisms of cohomology groups $H^{n}(C) \rightarrow H^{n}(D)$; we have $H^{n}(f)=H^{n}(g)$ for all $n \geq 0$.
