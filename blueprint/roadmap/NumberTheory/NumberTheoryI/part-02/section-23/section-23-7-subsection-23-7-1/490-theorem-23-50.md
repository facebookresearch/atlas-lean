# Theorem 23.50

Associated to each short exact sequence of chain complexes $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$ is a long exact sequence of homology groups $\cdots \longrightarrow H_{n+1}(A) \xrightarrow{H_{n+1}(\alpha)} H_{n+1}(B) \xrightarrow{H_{n+1}(\beta)} H_{n+1}(C) \xrightarrow{\delta_{n}} H_{n}(A) \xrightarrow{H_{n}(\alpha)} H_{n}(B) \xrightarrow{H_{n}(\beta)} H_{n}(C) \longrightarrow \cdots$ and this association maps morphisms of short exact sequences to morphisms of long exact sequences. In other words, the family of functors $H_{n}(\bullet)$ is a homological $\delta$-functor. For $n<0$ we have $H_{n}(\bullet)=0$, by definition, so this sequence ends at $H_{0}(C) \rightarrow 0$.
