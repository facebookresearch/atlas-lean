# Lemma 23.52

Homotopic morphisms of chain complexes $f, g: C \rightarrow D$ induce the same morphisms of homology groups $H_{n}(C) \rightarrow H_{n}(D)$; we have $H_{n}(f)=H_{n}(g)$ for all $n \geq 0$.
