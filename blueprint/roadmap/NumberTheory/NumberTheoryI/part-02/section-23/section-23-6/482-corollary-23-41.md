# Corollary 23.41

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group. For any exact sequence of $G$-modules $0 \longrightarrow A \xrightarrow{\alpha} B \xrightarrow{\beta} C \longrightarrow 0$, if any two of $h(A), h(B), h(C)$ are defined then so is the third and $h(B)=h(A) h(C)$.
