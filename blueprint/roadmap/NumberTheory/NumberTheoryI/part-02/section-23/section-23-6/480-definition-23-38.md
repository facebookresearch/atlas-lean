# Definition 23.38

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group and let $A$ be a $G$-module. We define $h^{n}(A)\coloneqq h^{n}(G, A)\coloneqq \# \hat{H}^{n}(G, A)$ and $h_{n}(A)\coloneqq h_{n}(G, A)\coloneqq \# \hat{H}_{n}(G, A)$. Whenever $h^{0}(A)$ and $h_{0}(A)$ are both finite, we also define the Herbrand quotient $h(A)\coloneqq h^{0}(A) / h_{0}(A) \in \mathbb{Q}$.
