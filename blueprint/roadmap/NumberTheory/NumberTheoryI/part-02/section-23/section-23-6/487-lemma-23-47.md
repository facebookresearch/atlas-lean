# Lemma 23.47

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group and let $\alpha: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of $G$-modules with finite kernel and cokernel. If either $h(A)$ or $h(B)$ is defined then $h(A)=h(B)$.
