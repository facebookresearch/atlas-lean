# Theorem 23.37

Let $G=\langle g\rangle$ be a finite cyclic group and let $A$ be a $G$-module. For all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ we have $\hat{H}^{2n}(G, A) \simeq \hat{H}_{2n-1}(G, A) \simeq \hat{H}^{0}(G, A)$ and $\hat{H}_{2n}(G, A) \simeq \hat{H}^{2n-1}(G, A) \simeq \hat{H}_{0}(G, A)$.
