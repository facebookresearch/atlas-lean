# Theorem 23.75

Let $P, Q$ be projective resolutions of a right $R$-module $M$. Let $A$ be a left $R$-module, and let $\bullet_{*}^{A}$ denote $\bullet \otimes_{R} A$. Then $H_{n}(P_{*}^{A}) \simeq H_{n}(Q_{*}^{A})$ for $n \geq 0$.
