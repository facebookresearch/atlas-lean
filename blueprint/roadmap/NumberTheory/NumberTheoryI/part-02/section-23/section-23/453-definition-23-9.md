# Definition 23.9

A family of functors $F^{n}$ from the category of $G$-modules to the category of abelian groups that associates to each short exact sequence of $G$-modules a long exact sequence of abelian groups such that commutative diagrams of short exact sequences yield commutative diagrams of long exact sequences is called a $\delta$-functor. A $\delta$-functor is said to be cohomological if the connecting homomorphisms in long exact sequences are of the form $\delta^{n}: F^{n}(G, C) \rightarrow F^{n+1}(G, A)$. If we instead have $\delta_{n}: F_{n+1}(G, C) \rightarrow F_{n}(G, A)$ then the $\delta$-functor is homological.
