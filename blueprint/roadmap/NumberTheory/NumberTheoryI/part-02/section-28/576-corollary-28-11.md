# Corollary 28.11

Let $L / K$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields with Galois group $G$. For every $\sigma \in G$ the Dirichlet density of the set $S$ of primes $\mathfrak{p}$ of $K$ unramified in $L$ for which $\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak{p}}=\{\sigma\}$ is $1 / \# G$.
