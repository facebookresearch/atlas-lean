# Proposition 28.3

Let $K$ be a global field. The global Artin homomorphism $\theta_{K}$ is the unique continuous homomorphism $\mathbb{I}_{K} \rightarrow \operatorname{Gal}\left(K^{\mathrm{ab}} / K\right)$ with the property that for every finite abelian extension $L / K$ in $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ and every place $w$ of $L$ lying over a place $v$ of $K$ the diagram $K_{v}^{\times} \xrightarrow{\theta_{L_{w} / K_{v}}} \operatorname{Gal}\left(L_{w} / K_{v}\right)$, $K_{v}^{\times} \xrightarrow{\iota_v} \mathbb{I}_{K}$, $\operatorname{Gal}(L_{w}/K_v) \xrightarrow{\varphi_w} \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, $\mathbb{I}_{K} \xrightarrow{\theta_{L/K}} \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ commutes, where the homomorphism $\theta_{L / K}$ is defined by $\theta_{L / K}(a)\coloneqq \theta_{K}(a)_{|_{L}}$.
