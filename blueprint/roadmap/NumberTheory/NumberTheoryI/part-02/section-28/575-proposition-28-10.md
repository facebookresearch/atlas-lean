# Proposition 28.10

Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for a number field $K$ and let $\mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ be the corresponding ray class group. For every ray class $c \in \mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ the Dirichlet density of the set of primes $\mathfrak{p}$ of $K$ that lie in $c$ is $1 / \# \mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$.
