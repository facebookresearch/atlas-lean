# Theorem 24.8

Let $L / K$ be an extension of number fields with cyclic Galois group $G = \langle\sigma\rangle$. The Herbrand quotient of the $G$-module $\mathcal{O}_{L}^{\times}$ is $h(\mathcal{O}_{L}^{\times}) = \frac{e_{\infty}(L / K)}{[L : K]}$.
