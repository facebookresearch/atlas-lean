# Theorem 24.15

Let $L/K$ be a totally unramified cyclic extension of number fields. Then $\left[\mathcal{I}_{K}: N\left(\mathcal{I}_{L}\right) \mathcal{P}_{K}\right] \geq [L: K]$.
