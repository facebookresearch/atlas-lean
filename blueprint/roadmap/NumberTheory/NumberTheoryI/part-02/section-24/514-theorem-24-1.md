# Theorem 24.1

Let $L / K$ be a cyclic Galois extension with Galois group $G \coloneqq \operatorname{Gal}(L / K)$. (i) $\hat{H}^{0}(L)$ and $\hat{H}_{0}(L)$ are both trivial. (ii) $\hat{H}^{0}(L^{\times}) \simeq K^{\times} / N(L^{\times})$ and $\hat{H}_{0}(L^{\times})$ is trivial.
