# Corollary 24.16

Let $L/K$ be a totally unramified cyclic extension of number fields. Then $\# \mathrm{Cl}_{L}^{G} = \# \mathrm{Cl}_{K} / [L:K]$ and the Tate cohomology groups of $\mathrm{Cl}_{L}$ are all trivial.
