# Corollary 24.10

Let $L / K$ be a cyclic extension of global fields and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$ divisible by all the primes that ramify in $L$. Then $h(\mathcal{I}_{L}^{\mathfrak{m}}) = [\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} : N(\mathcal{I}_{L}^{\mathfrak{m}})]$.
