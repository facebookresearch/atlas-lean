# Lemma 24.7

Let $K$ be a number field with archimedean places $v_{1}, \ldots, v_{r+s}$. Any units $u_{1}, \ldots, u_{r+s} \in \mathcal{O}_{L}^{\times}$ that satisfy $\|u_{i}\|_{v_{j}} < 1$ for $j \neq i$ generate a finite index subgroup of $\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}$.
