# Corollary 21.17

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields with Galois group $G\coloneqq \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ and let $H$ be a normal subgroup of $G$. The set $S$ of primes for which $\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak{p}} \subseteq H$ has polar density $\rho(S)=\# H / \# G$.
