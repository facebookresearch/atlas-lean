# Theorem 21.8

Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for a number field $K$. We have an exact sequence $1 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \cap K^{\mathfrak{m}, 1} \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \longrightarrow K^{\mathfrak{m}} / K^{\mathfrak{m}, 1} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Cl}_{K} \longrightarrow 1$ and a canonical isomorphism $K^{\mathfrak{m}} / K^{\mathfrak{m}, 1} \simeq \{ \pm 1\}^{\# \mathfrak{m}_{\infty}} \times (\mathcal{O}_{K} / \mathfrak{m}_{0})^{\times}$.
