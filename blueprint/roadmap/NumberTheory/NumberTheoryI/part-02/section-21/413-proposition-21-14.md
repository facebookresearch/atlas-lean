# Proposition 21.14

Let $S$ and $T$ denote sets of primes in a number field $K$, let $\mathcal{P}$ be the set of all primes of $K$, and let $\mathcal{P}_{1}$ be the set of degree-1 primes of $K$. The following hold: (a) If $S$ is finite then $\rho(S)=0$; if $\mathcal{P}-S$ is finite then $\rho(S)=1$. (b) If $S \subseteq T$ both have polar densities, then $\rho(S) \leq \rho(T)$. (c) If two sets $S$ and $T$ have finite intersection and any two of the sets $S$, $T$, and $S \cup T$ have polar densities then so does the third and $\rho(S \cup T)=\rho(S)+\rho(T)$. (d) We have $\rho(\mathcal{P}_{1})=1$, and $\rho(S \cap \mathcal{P}_{1})=\rho(S)$ whenever $S$ has a polar density.
