# Corollary 21.9

Let $K$ be a number field and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$. The ray class group $\mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ is a finite abelian group whose cardinality $h_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}\coloneqq \# \mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ is given by $h_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}=\frac{\phi(\mathfrak{m}) h_{K}}{[\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}: \mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times} \cap K^{\mathfrak{m}, 1}]}$, where $h_{K}\coloneqq \# \mathrm{Cl}_{K}$ and $\phi(\mathfrak{m})\coloneqq \#(K^{\mathfrak{m}} / K^{\mathfrak{m}, 1})=\phi(\mathfrak{m}_{\infty}) \phi(\mathfrak{m}_{0})$, with $\phi(\mathfrak{m}_{\infty})=2^{\# \mathfrak{m}_{\infty}}$, $\phi(\mathfrak{m}_{0})=\#(\mathcal{O}_{K} / \mathfrak{m}_{0})^{\times}=\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{m}_{0}) \prod_{\mathfrak{p} \mid \mathfrak{m}_{0}}(1-\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{p})^{-1})$. In particular, $h_{K}$ divides $h_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ and $h_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ divides $h_{K} \phi(\mathfrak{m})$.
