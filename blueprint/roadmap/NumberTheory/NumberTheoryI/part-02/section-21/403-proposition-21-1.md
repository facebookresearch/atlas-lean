# Proposition 21.1

Let $K \subseteq L \subseteq M$ be a tower of finite abelian extensions of global fields and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be an $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideal divisible by all primes $\mathfrak{p}$ of $K$ that ramify in $M$. We have a commutative diagram where the Artin maps $\psi_{M/K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: \mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} \to \operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$ and $\psi_{L/K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: \mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} \to \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ are related by the vertical map $\sigma \mapsto \sigma|_{L}$ induced by restriction.
