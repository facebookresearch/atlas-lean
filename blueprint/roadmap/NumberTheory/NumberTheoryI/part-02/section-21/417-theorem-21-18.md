# Theorem 21.18

If $L/K$ and $M/K$ are two finite Galois extensions of number fields then $L \subseteq M \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Spl}(M) \precsim \operatorname{Spl}(L) \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Spl}(M) \subseteq \operatorname{Spl}(L)$, $L=M \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Spl}(M) \sim \operatorname{Spl}(L) \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Spl}(M)=\operatorname{Spl}(L)$, and the map $L \mapsto \operatorname{Spl}(L)$ is an injection from the set of finite Galois extensions of $K$ (inside some fixed algebraic closure) to sets of primes of $K$ that have a positive polar density.
