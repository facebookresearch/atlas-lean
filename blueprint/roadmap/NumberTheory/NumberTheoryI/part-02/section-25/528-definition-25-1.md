# Definition 25.1

Let $(X_i)$ be a family of topological spaces indexed by $i \in I$, and let $(U_i)$ be a family of open sets $U_i \subseteq X_i$. The restricted product $\prod(X_i, U_i)$ is the topological space $\prod(X_i, U_i) \coloneqq \{(x_i): x_i \in U_i \text{ for almost all } i \in I\} \subseteq \prod X_i$ with the basis of open sets $\mathcal{B} \coloneqq \{\prod V_i: V_i \subseteq X_i \text{ is open for all } i \in I \text{ and } V_i = U_i \text{ for almost all } i \in I\}$ where almost all means all but finitely many.
