# Proposition 25.5

Let $(X_i)$ be a family of topological spaces indexed by $i \in I$, let $(U_i)$ be a family of open sets $U_i \subseteq X_i$, and let $X \coloneqq \prod(X_i, U_i)$ be the corresponding restricted product. For each finite $S \subseteq I$ define $X_S \coloneqq \prod_{i \in S} X_i \times \prod_{i \notin S} U_i$. Then $\prod(X_i, U_i) \simeq \varinjlim_S X_S$.
