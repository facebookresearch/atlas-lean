# Definition 25.7

Let $K$ be a global field. The adele ring of $K$ is the restricted product $\mathbb{A}_{K} \coloneqq \prod(K_{v}, \mathcal{O}_{v})_{v \in M_{K}}$ which we may view as a subset (but not a subspace!) of $\prod_{v} K_{v}$; indeed $\mathbb{A}_{K} = \{(a_{v}) \in \prod K_{v}: a_{v} \in \mathcal{O}_{v} \text{ for almost all } v\}$.
