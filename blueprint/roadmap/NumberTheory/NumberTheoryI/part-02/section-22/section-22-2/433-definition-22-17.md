# Definition 22.17

The Weber $L$-function $L(s, \chi)$ of a ray class character $\chi$ for a number field $K$ is the complex function $L(s, \chi)\coloneqq \prod_{\mathfrak{p}}(1-\chi(\mathfrak{p})\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{p})^{-s})^{-1}=\sum_{\mathfrak{a}} \chi(\mathfrak{a})\mathrm{N}(\mathfrak{a})^{-s}$ where the product is over prime ideals of $\mathcal{O}_{K}$ and the sum is over nonzero $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideals; the product and sum both converge to a non-vanishing holomorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$.
