# Corollary 22.23

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields and let $\mathcal{C}$ be a congruence subgroup for a modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ of $K$. If $\operatorname{Spl}(L) \precsim \{\mathfrak{p} \in \mathcal{C}\}$ then $\left[\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}: \mathcal{C}\right] \leq [L:K]$.
