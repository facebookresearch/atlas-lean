# Definition 22.27

Let $L / K$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$ divisible by the conductor of $L / K$. The norm group (or Takagi group) associated to $\mathfrak{m}$ is the congruence subgroup $T_{L / K}^{\mathfrak{m}}\coloneqq \mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} N_{L / K}(\mathcal{I}_{L}^{\mathfrak{m}})$, where $\mathcal{I}_{L}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ denotes the subgroup of fractional ideals in $\mathcal{I}_{L}$ that are coprime to $\mathfrak{m} \mathcal{O}_{L}$.
