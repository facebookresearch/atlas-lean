# Proposition 22.28

Let $L / K$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$ divisible by the conductor of $L / K$. Then $\operatorname{Spl}(L / K) \precsim T_{L / K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$.
