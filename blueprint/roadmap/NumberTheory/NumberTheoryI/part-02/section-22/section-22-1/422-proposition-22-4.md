# Proposition 22.4

Let $K$ be a number field. The relation $(\mathcal{C}_{1}, \mathfrak{m}_{1}) \sim (\mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathfrak{m}_{2})$ is an equivalence relation. If $(\mathcal{C}_{1}, \mathfrak{m}_{1}) \sim (\mathcal{C}_{2}, \mathfrak{m}_{2})$ then $\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}_{1}} / \mathcal{C}_{1} \simeq \mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}_{2}} / \mathcal{C}_{2}$ are related by a canonical isomorphism that preserves cosets of fractional ideals prime to both $\mathfrak{m}_{1}$ and $\mathfrak{m}_{2}$.
