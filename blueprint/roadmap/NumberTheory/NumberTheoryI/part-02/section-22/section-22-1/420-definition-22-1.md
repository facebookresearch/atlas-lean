# Definition 22.1

Let $K$ be a number field and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a modulus for $K$. A congruence subgroup for the modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ is a subgroup $\mathcal{C}$ of $\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ that contains $\mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$. We use $\overline{\mathcal{C}}$ to denote the image of $\mathcal{C}$ in $\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}} / \mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}=\mathrm{Cl}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$ under the quotient map.
