# Corollary 22.7

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ for a number field $K$. There is a unique congruence subgroup in the equivalence class of $\mathcal{C}$ whose modulus $\mathfrak{c}$ divides the modulus of every congruence subgroup equivalent to $\mathcal{C}$.
