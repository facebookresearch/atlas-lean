# Proposition 22.9

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a primitive congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ for a number field $K$. Then $\mathfrak{m}$ is the conductor of every congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{m}$ contained in $\mathcal{C}$; in particular, $\mathfrak{m}$ is the conductor of $\mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}}$.
