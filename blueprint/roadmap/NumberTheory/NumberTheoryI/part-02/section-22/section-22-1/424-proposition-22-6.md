# Proposition 22.6

Let $\mathcal{C}_{1} \sim \mathcal{C}_{2}$ be congruence subgroups of modulus $\mathfrak{m}_{1}$ and $\mathfrak{m}_{2}$, respectively. There exists a congruence subgroup $\mathcal{C} \sim \mathcal{C}_{1} \sim \mathcal{C}_{2}$ with modulus $\mathfrak{n}\coloneqq \operatorname{gcd}(\mathfrak{m}_{1}, \mathfrak{m}_{2})$.
