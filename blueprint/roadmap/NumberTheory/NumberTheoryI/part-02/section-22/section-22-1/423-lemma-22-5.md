# Lemma 22.5

Let $\mathcal{C}_{1}$ be a congruence subgroup of modulus $\mathfrak{m}_{1}$ for a number field $K$. There exists a congruence subgroup $\mathcal{C}_{2}$ of modulus $\mathfrak{m}_{2} \mid \mathfrak{m}_{1}$ equivalent to $\mathcal{C}_{1}$ if and only if $\mathcal{I}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}_{1}} \cap \mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}_{2}} \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{1}$, in which case $\mathcal{C}_{2}=\mathcal{C}_{1} \mathcal{R}_{K}^{\mathfrak{m}_{2}}$.
