# Definition 26.7

Let $K$ be a global field. The group of 1-ideles is the topological group $\mathbb{I}_{K}^{1}\coloneqq \ker\|\cdot\|=\{a \in \mathbb{I}_{K}:\|a\|=1\}$ which contains $K^{\times}$, by the product formula (Theorem 13.21).
