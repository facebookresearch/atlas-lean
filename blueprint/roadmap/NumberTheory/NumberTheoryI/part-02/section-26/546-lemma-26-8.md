# Lemma 26.8

The group of 1-ideles $\mathbb{I}_{K}^{1}$ is a closed subset of $\mathbb{A}_{K}$ and $\mathbb{I}_{K}$, and the two subspace topologies on $\mathbb{I}_{K}^{1}$ coincide.
