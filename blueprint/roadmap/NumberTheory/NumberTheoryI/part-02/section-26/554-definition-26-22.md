# Definition 26.22

Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension with Galois group $G\coloneqq \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. The Krull topology on $G$ has the basis consisting of all cosets of subgroups $H_{F}\coloneqq \operatorname{Gal}(L/F)$, where $F$ ranges over finite normal extensions of $K$ in $L$.
