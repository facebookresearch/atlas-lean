# Corollary 26.20

Let $G$ be a profinite group. Then $G$ is naturally isomorphic to its profinite completion. In fact, $G \simeq \varprojlim G / U$, where $U$ ranges over open normal subgroups (ordered by containment). However, $G$ is isomorphic to its profinite completion as a group (in other words, strongly complete) if and only if every finite index subgroup of $G$ is open.
