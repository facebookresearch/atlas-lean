# Membership in $M_n$

Let $F$ be a finite field and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. A polynomial $f$ over
$F$ lies in the set $M_n$ of monic polynomials of degree $n$ over $F$ if and only if there are
$c_0, \dots, c_{n-1} \in F$ with $f = X^n + \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} c_i X^i$.
