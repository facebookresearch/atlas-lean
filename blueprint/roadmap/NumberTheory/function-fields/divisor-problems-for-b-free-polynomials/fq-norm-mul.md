# Multiplicativity of the norm

Let $F$ be a finite field with $q$ elements, and let
$|f| = q^{\deg f}$ be the norm of a nonzero $f \in F[T]$. For all nonzero $f, g \in F[T]$,
$|fg| = |f| \, |g|$.
