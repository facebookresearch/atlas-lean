# Indicator of a statement

For a proposition $P$, the indicator $\mathbf{1}_P$ is the real
number $1$ if $P$ holds and $0$ otherwise.

## Sources

- Will Sawin and Mark Shusterman, *On the Chowla and twin primes conjectures over $\mathbb{F}_q[T]$*, Annals of Mathematics 196 (2022), no. 2, 457–506, Equation (1.4).
