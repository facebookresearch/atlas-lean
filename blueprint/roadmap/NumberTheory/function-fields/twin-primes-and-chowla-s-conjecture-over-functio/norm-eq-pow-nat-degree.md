# Norm of a nonzero polynomial

Let $F$ be a finite field and give $F[T]$ its ring norm.
For every nonzero $f \in F[T]$, the norm of $f$ is $|f| = |F|^{\deg f}$, where $|F|$ is the number
of elements of $F$.

## Sources

- Will Sawin and Mark Shusterman, *On the Chowla and twin primes conjectures over $\mathbb{F}_q[T]$*, Annals of Mathematics 196 (2022), no. 2, 457–506, Equation (1.1).
