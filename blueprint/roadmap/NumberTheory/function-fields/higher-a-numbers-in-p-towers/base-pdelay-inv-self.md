# The delay of $1/p$ is $1$

For every prime $p$, the delay of the base-$p$ expansion of
$1/p$ is $D_{1/p} = -v_p(1/p) = 1$, where $v_p$ is the $p$-adic valuation.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Lemma 2 ($D_{1/p} = 1$).
