# The denominator $\tau_D$

For natural numbers $p$ and $d$, $\tau_D$ is the denominator in
lowest terms of the rational number $\tau = (p + 1)/d$ (when $d = 0$, $\tau$ is taken to be $0$,
so $\tau_D = 1$).

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Notation 2 ($\tau_D$).
