# Two values of $\delta$ for $p = 5$ and $d = 4$

For $p = 5$ and $d = 4$, so that
$\tau = (p + 1)/d = 3/2$, the rounding indicator satisfies $\delta(1) = 1$ and $\delta(2) = 0$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Example 5 and Table 2 ($\delta(1) = 1$, $\delta(2) = 0$ for $p = 5$, $d = 4$).
