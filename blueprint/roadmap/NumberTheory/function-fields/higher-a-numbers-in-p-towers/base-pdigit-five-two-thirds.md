# Base-5 digits of 2/3

Writing $[5^j](x)$ for the $j$-th base-$5$ digit of a rational
number $x$, we have $[5^{-1}](2/3) = 3$, $[5^{-2}](2/3) = 1$ and $[5^0](2/3) = 0$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Example 4 (base-$5$ example $2/3 = (0.3131\ldots)_5$).
