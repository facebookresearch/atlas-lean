# The base-$p$ expansion of $1$ has period $1$

For every natural number $p$, the period
$L_1$ of the base-$p$ expansion of $1$ equals $1$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Lemma 2 ($L_x = 1$ when $x_D = 1$).
