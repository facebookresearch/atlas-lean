# $\tau_D = 1$ when $d = 1$

For every natural number $p$, the denominator $\tau_D$ of
$\tau = (p + 1)/d$ in lowest terms equals $1$ when $d = 1$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Notation 2 ($\tau_D = 1$ when $d = 1$).
