# $\mu(2) = 3$ for $p = 5$ and $d = 4$

For $p = 5$ and $d = 4$ the lattice boundary
$\mu(2)$ equals $3$; here $\tau = (p + 1)/d = 3/2$, so $\tau \cdot 2 = 3$ is an integer and
its floor and ceiling agree.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Definition 2 ($\mu(2) = 3$ for $p = 5$, $d = 4$, where $\tau \cdot 2 = 3$).
