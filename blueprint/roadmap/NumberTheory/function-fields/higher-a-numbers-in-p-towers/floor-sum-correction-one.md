# The correction $A_{x^{-1}}$ vanishes at $x = 1$

For all natural numbers $p$ and $n$, the
periodic correction $A_{x^{-1}}(n)$ of Proposition 4 satisfies $A_1(n) = 0$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Proposition 4 ($A_1 = 0$).
