# The constant $\gamma$

For natural numbers $p$, $d$ and $r$,
$\gamma = ((p - 1) r + (p + 1))/d$, a rational number (taken to be $0$ when $d = 0$).

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Notation 1 (ii).
