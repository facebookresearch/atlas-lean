# The variant $\delta_0$ of the rounding indicator

For natural numbers $p$, $d$ and $i$, with
$\delta$ the rounding indicator for $p$ and $d$, the value $\delta_0(i)$ is $0$ if $p \mid i$, and
$\delta(i)$ otherwise.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Definition 7.
