# The correction $F_y$ vanishes at $y = 0$

For all natural numbers $p$, $d$ and $e$, the
correction $F_y(e)$ of Lemma 9, taken at $y = 0$, is $0$.

## Sources

- Jeremy Booher, Jack Hsieh, Rakesh Rivera, Vincent Tran, James Upton, and Carol Wu, *Higher a-Numbers in $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Towers via Counting Lattice Points*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A78, Lemma 9 ($F_0 = 0$).
