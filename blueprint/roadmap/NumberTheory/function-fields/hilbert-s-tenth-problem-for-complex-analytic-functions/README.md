# Hilbert's tenth problem for rings of complex analytic functions

Undecidability of positive existential theories of rings of complex entire and meromorphic functions in one or several variables. These rings are not function fields; they are placed here beside the function-field cases as further rings of functions, and the one-variable proofs adapt Denef's Pell-equation method for polynomial rings.
