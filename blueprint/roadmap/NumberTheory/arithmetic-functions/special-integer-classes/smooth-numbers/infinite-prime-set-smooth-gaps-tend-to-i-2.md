# Integers composed of a sparse infinite set of primes have growing gaps

There is an infinite set $P$ of primes such that, if $a_1 < a_2 < \cdots$ are the positive integers all of whose prime factors lie in $P$, then $a_{i+1} - a_i \to \infty$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 240 (Wintner's question on gaps between smooth numbers)](infinite-prime-set-smooth-gaps-tend-to-i.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Tijdeman (1973), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- R. Tijdeman, On integers with many small prime factors, Compositio Math. 26 (1973), 319–330.
