# Some infinite sets have no Behrend translate

It is not the case that for every infinite set $A$ of positive integers there is $k \ge 1$ such that the positive integers having a divisor of the form $a + k$ with $a \in A$ have natural density $1$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 26 (Behrend translates of infinite sets)](exists-shift-with-density-one-multiples.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Davenport and Erdős (1951), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #26, https://www.erdosproblems.com/26.
- H. Davenport and P. Erdős, On sequences of positive integers, J. Indian Math. Soc. (N.S.) (1951), 19–24.
