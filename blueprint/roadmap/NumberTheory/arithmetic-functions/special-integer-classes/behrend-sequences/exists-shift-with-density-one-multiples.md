# Erdős Problem 26 (Behrend translates of infinite sets)

The statement that for every infinite set $A$ of positive integers there is $k \ge 1$ such that the positive integers having a divisor of the form $a + k$ with $a \in A$ have natural density $1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Some infinite sets have no Behrend translate](exists-shift-with-density-one-multiples-2.md).
