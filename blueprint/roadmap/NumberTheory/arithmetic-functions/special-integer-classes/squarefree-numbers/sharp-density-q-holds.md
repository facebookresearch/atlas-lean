# A squarefree sequence with property Q of density 6/π²

There is a strictly increasing sequence $a_0 < a_1 < \cdots$ of squarefree natural numbers whose set of values has property Q and $j/a_j \to 6/\pi^2$; here a set $A$ of natural numbers has property Q when there are infinitely many $n$ such that $n + a$ is squarefree for every $a \in A$ with $a < n$.

This resolves [A squarefree sequence with property Q of density 6/π², as a statement](sharp-density-q.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to van Doorn and Tao (2025), cited under Sources.

## Sources
