# Sequences with property P have density zero

For every strictly increasing sequence $a_0 < a_1 < \cdots$ of natural numbers whose set of values has property P, $a_j/j \to \infty$ as $j \to \infty$; here a set $A$ of natural numbers has property P when for every $n \ge 1$ only finitely many $a \in A$ make $n + a$ squarefree.

This resolves [Sequences with property P have density zero, as a statement](density-zero-of-p.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to van Doorn and Tao (2025), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1102, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1102.
- W. van Doorn and T. Tao, Growth rates of sequences governed by the squarefree properties of its translates, arXiv:2512.01087 (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01087; published in Acta Arithmetica (2026), https://doi.org/10.4064/aa251207-28-5.
