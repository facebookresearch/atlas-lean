# Weighted perfect numbers of the first kind

Fix a set $S$ of integers. An integer $n$ is $S$-perfect of the first kind if $|n|>1$ and each positive divisor $d$ of $|n|$ with $1<d<|n|$ can be assigned a weight $\lambda_d\in S$ so that

$$
n=1+\sum_{1<d<|n|,\;d\mid |n|}\lambda_d d.
$$

The integer on the left is $n$, not its absolute value.

- Ross, *A Perfect Number Generalization and Some Euclid-Euler Type Results*, JIS 27 (2024), Article 24.7.5, Section 1 (definition of `S`-perfect numbers of the first kind).
