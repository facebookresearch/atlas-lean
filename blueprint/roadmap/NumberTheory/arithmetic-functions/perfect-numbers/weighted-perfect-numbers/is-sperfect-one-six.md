# Six with unit divisor weights

The integer $6$ is $\{1\}$-perfect of the first kind: its positive divisors strictly between $1$ and $6$ are $2$ and $3$, and $6=1+2+3$.

## Sources

- Ross, *A Perfect Number Generalization and Some Euclid-Euler Type Results*, JIS 27 (2024), Article 24.7.5, Example 2 (`S = {1}`).
