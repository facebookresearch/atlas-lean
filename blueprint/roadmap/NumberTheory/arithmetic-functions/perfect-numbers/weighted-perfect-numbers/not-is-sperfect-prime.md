# A positive prime is never weighted perfect

For every set $S$ of integers and every positive prime $p$, the integer $p$ is not $S$-perfect of the first kind. There is no positive divisor strictly between $1$ and $p$, so the right-hand side of the defining equation is $1$.

## Sources

- Ross, *A Perfect Number Generalization and Some Euclid-Euler Type Results*, JIS 27 (2024), Article 24.7.5, Proposition 3 (the prime-power case with exponent one).
