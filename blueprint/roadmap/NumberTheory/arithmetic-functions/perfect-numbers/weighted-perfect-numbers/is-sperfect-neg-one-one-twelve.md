# Twelve with signed divisor weights

The integer $12$ is $\{-1,1\}$-perfect of the first kind. Assign weight $-1$ to $2$ and $+1$ to $3,4,6$. Then $12=1-2+3+4+6$.

## Sources

- Ross, *A Perfect Number Generalization and Some Euclid-Euler Type Results*, JIS 27 (2024), Article 24.7.5, Example 2 (`S = {-1, 1}`).
