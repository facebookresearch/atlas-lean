# Grün's bound for non-deficient numbers

Let $n$ be a non-deficient number with $k$
distinct prime factors, the least of which is $p_1$. Then $\tfrac{3}{2} p_1 - 2 < k$, and if
$p_1 \ge 5$ then $p_1 + 2 \le k$.
