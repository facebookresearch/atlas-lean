# Primes are not $(k,\ell)$-near-perfect

Let $k$ and $\ell$ be natural numbers with
$\ell \ge 1$ and let $p$ be a prime. Then $p$ is not $[k,\ell]$-near-perfect.

## Sources

- Flora Jeba S, Anirban Roy, Pankaj Jyoti Mahanta, and Manjil P. Saikia, "On near $F_k$-perfect and deficient $F_k$-perfect numbers", INTEGERS 25 (2025), #A86, https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/z86/z86.pdf, Proposition 2.
