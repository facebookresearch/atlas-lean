# No $(2,\ell)$-near-perfect number of the form $2^a p$

Let $\ell$ and $a$ be natural
numbers with $\ell \ge 1$, and let $p$ be an odd prime. Then $2^a p$ is not
$[2,\ell]$-near-perfect.

## Sources

- Flora Jeba S, Anirban Roy, Pankaj Jyoti Mahanta, and Manjil P. Saikia, "On near $F_k$-perfect and deficient $F_k$-perfect numbers", INTEGERS 25 (2025), #A86, https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/z86/z86.pdf, Theorem 10.
