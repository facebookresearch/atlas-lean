# Odd primes are not $(k,\ell)$-deficient-perfect

Let $p$ be an odd prime. Then for all
natural numbers $k$ and $\ell$, $p$ is not $[k,\ell]$-deficient-perfect.

## Sources

- Flora Jeba S, Anirban Roy, Pankaj Jyoti Mahanta, and Manjil P. Saikia, "On near $F_k$-perfect and deficient $F_k$-perfect numbers", INTEGERS 25 (2025), #A86, https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/z86/z86.pdf, Proposition 1.
