# Square values of the discriminant $2^{2k+2} + 2^{k+2} - 2^{a+2} - 7$

Let $a$ and $k$ be
natural numbers with $1 \le a \le k$. If $2^{2k+2} + 2^{k+2} - 2^{a+2} - 7$ is the square of an
integer, then $k = a = 1$.

## Sources

- Vedant Aryan, Dev Madhavani, Savan Parikh, Ingrid Slattery and Joshua Zelinsky, *On 2-Near Perfect Numbers*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A61, Lemma 2 (`integers-2024-A61-lem-2`).
