# Lower bound for the prime-counting function at primes $p \ge 19$

For every prime
$p \ge 19$, the number $\pi(p)$ of primes at most $p$ satisfies $\pi(p) \ge p/\log p + 1$,
where $\log$ is the natural logarithm.

## Sources

- Joshua Zelinsky, *The Sum of the Reciprocals of the Prime Divisors of an Odd Perfect or Odd Primitive Non-deficient Number*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A59, Lemma 4 (`integers-2025-A59-lem-4`).
