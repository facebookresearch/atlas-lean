# Zelinsky's bound on the smallest prime divisor of an odd perfect number

If $N$ is an odd
perfect number with $m$ distinct prime divisors, then the smallest prime divisor $p$ of $N$
satisfies $p < (m - 1)/2$.

## Sources

- Andrew Stone, *Improved Upper Bounds for Odd Perfect Numbers -- Part I*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A114, Lemma 1 (viii) (`integers-2024-A114-lem-1`).
