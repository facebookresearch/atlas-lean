# Multiplicativity of the biunitary divisor sum

The biunitary divisor sum satisfies
$\sigma^{**}(1) = 1$. For coprime natural numbers $m$ and $n$, it satisfies
$\sigma^{**}(mn) = \sigma^{**}(m)\sigma^{**}(n)$.

## Sources

- Tomohiro Yamada, *Determining all Biunitary Triperfect Numbers of a Certain Form*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.4.5, Section 2 (after Wall).
