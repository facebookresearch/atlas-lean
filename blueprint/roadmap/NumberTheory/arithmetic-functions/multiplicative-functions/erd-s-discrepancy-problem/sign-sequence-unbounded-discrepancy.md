# Erdős Problem 67 (the Erdős discrepancy problem)

The statement that for every $f : \mathbb{N} \to \{-1, 1\}$ and every real $C > 0$ there are integers $d, m \ge 1$ with $\bigl|\sum_{k=1}^{m} f(kd)\bigr| > C$.
