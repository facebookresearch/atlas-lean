# Tao's solution of the Erdős discrepancy problem

For every $f : \mathbb{N} \to \{-1, 1\}$ and every real $C > 0$ there are integers $d, m \ge 1$ with $\bigl|\sum_{k=1}^{m} f(kd)\bigr| > C$.

## Sources

- T. Tao, The Erdős discrepancy problem, Discrete Anal. 2016, Paper No. 1, arXiv:1509.05363.
