# Sums of six and of eight nonzero squares

Every integer $n \ge 20$ is a sum of $6$
squares of positive integers, and every integer $n \ge 22$ is a sum of $8$ squares of positive
integers.

## Sources

- Jewel Mahajan, *Multiplicative Functions Additive on Partitions of 2k Nonzero Squares*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A121, Corollary 1, as extracted inside the Lemma 1 record (`integers-2026-A121-lem-1`).
