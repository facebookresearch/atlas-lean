# Erdős Problem 239 (mean values of multiplicative ±1 functions)

The statement that for every $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f(1) = 1$, $f(mn) = f(m) f(n)$ for coprime $m, n$, and $f(n) \in \{-1, 1\}$ for every $n \ge 1$, the mean $\frac1N \sum_{n=1}^{N} f(n)$ converges as $N \to \infty$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Multiplicative ±1 functions have mean values](multiplicative-sign-mean-converges-holds.md).
