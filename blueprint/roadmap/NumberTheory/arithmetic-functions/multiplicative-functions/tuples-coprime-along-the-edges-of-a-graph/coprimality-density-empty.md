# The coprimality density of the empty graph is $1$

For every natural number $k$, the
coprimality density of the graph on $\{1, \dots, k\}$ with no edges equals $1$.

## Sources

- László Tóth, *On the Asymptotic Density of k-tuples of Positive Integers with Pairwise Non-Coprime Components*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.5, Theorem 1, the display labelled `A_G_version` in the TeX source, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Toth/toth27.tex.
