# Coprimality indicator of a graph

Let $E$ be a set of ordered pairs $(i, j)$ of indices in
$\{0, \dots, k-1\}$, the edge set of a graph $G$ on these $k$ vertices. For
$n = (n_0, \dots, n_{k-1}) \in \mathbb{N}^k$, the coprimality indicator $\delta_G(n)$ is the
natural number $1$ if $\gcd(n_i, n_j) = 1$ for every $(i, j) \in E$, and $0$ otherwise.

## Sources

- László Tóth, *On the Asymptotic Density of k-tuples of Positive Integers with Pairwise Non-Coprime Components*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.5, display (def_delta_G).
