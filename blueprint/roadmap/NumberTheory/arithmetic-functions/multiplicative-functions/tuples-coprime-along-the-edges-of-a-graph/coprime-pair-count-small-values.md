# Number of coprime pairs: examples

The triple $(2, 3, 4)$ has exactly two coprime pairs
of components, and the triple $(6, 10, 15)$ has none, that is, its components are pairwise
non-coprime.

## Sources

- László Tóth, *On the Asymptotic Density of k-tuples of Positive Integers with Pairwise Non-Coprime Components*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.5, the displays labelled `def_beta` and `def_beta_exact` in the TeX source, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Toth/toth27.tex.
