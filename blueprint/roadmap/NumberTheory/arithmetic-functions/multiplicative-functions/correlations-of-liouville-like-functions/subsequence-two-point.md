# AimPL question on Elliott's conjecture along a subsequence

The statement that for every multiplicative $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $|f(n)| \le 1$ that is non-pretentious, there is a sequence of natural numbers $X_j \to \infty$ with $\frac{1}{X_j} \sum_{1 \le n \le X_j} f(n)\overline{f(n+1)} \to 0$; here an arithmetic function $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}$ is non-pretentious when for every modulus $q \ge 1$, every Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $q$ and every real $t$, the series $\sum_p \bigl(1 - \operatorname{Re}(f(p)\overline{\chi(p)} p^{it})\bigr)/p$ over the primes diverges.
