# The square-root part of $1$ is $1$

We have $v(1) = 1$, where for $n \ge 1$ the
square-root part $v(n)$ is the product of $p^{\lfloor e/2 \rfloor}$ over the prime powers $p^e$
exactly dividing $n$.
