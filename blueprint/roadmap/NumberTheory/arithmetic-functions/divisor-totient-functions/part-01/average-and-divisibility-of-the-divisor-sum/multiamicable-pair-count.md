# Counting multiamicable pairs

For natural numbers $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $x$, $M(x)$ is the number of pairs of natural numbers $0 < m < n$ with $m \le x$ and $\sigma(m) = \sigma(n) = \alpha m + \beta n$, where $\sigma$ is the sum-of-divisors function.

## Sources

- S. Dimitrov, Generalizations of amicable numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.4.2, Section 3 and Theorem 5.
