# Multiamicable pairs have density zero

For positive integers $\alpha$ and $\beta$, $M(x) = o(x)$ as $x \to \infty$, where $M(x)$ is the number of pairs of natural numbers $0 < m < n$ with $m \le x$ and $\sigma(m) = \sigma(n) = \alpha m + \beta n$, $\sigma$ being the sum-of-divisors function.

## Sources

- S. Dimitrov, Generalizations of amicable numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.4.2, Theorem 5.
