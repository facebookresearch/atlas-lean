# Dimitrov's construction of multiamicable tuples

Let $k$ be a natural number, $a > 0$, and let $N_1, \dots, N_k$ and $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_k$ be positive integers with $\gcd(a, N_i) = 1$ and $\sigma(a)/a = (\alpha_1 N_1 + \dots + \alpha_k N_k)/\sigma(N_i)$ for every $i$, where $\sigma$ is the sum-of-divisors function. Then $\sigma(aN_i) = \alpha_1 aN_1 + \dots + \alpha_k aN_k$ for every $i$.

## Sources

- S. Dimitrov, Generalizations of amicable numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.4.2, Theorem 4.
