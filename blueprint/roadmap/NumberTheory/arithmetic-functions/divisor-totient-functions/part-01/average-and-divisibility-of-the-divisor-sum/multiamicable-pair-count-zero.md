# No multiamicable pairs below one

For all natural numbers $\alpha$ and $\beta$, $M(0) = 0$, where $M(x)$ is the number of pairs $0 < m < n$ with $m \le x$ and $\sigma(m) = \sigma(n) = \alpha m + \beta n$.

## Sources

- S. Dimitrov, Generalizations of amicable numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.4.2, Section 4, equation (2), and Theorem 5. The source defines these tuples for positive coefficients $\alpha, \beta$; zero coefficients are an extension of its definition.
