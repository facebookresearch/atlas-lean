# Erdős Problem 446, integers with exactly one such divisor

The statement that whenever $\delta(n)$ and $\delta_1(n)$ are the natural densities of the integers with a divisor in $(n, 2n)$ and of the integers with exactly one divisor in $(n, 2n)$, $\delta_1(n) = o(\delta(n))$ as $n \to \infty$.
