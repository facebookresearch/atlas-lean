# Almost all integers have two divisors within a factor of two

The natural numbers $n$ having two divisors $d_1 < d_2 < 2d_1$ have natural density $1$: the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $1$ as $b \to \infty$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 144 (integers with two divisors within a factor of two)](close-divisor-pair-density-one.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Maier and Tenenbaum (1984), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #144, https://www.erdosproblems.com/144.
- H. Maier and G. Tenenbaum, On the set of divisors of an integer, Invent. Math. 76 (1984), 121–128.
