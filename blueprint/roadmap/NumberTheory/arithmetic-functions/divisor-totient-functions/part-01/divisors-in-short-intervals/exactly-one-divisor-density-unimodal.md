# Erdős Problem 692 (unimodality of the density of integers with one divisor in an interval)

The statement that for every $n \ge 1$, the sequence $m \mapsto \delta_1(n, m)$ is unimodal from $m = n + 2$, where for natural numbers $n$ and $m$, $\delta_1(n, m)$ is the natural density of the positive integers with exactly one divisor among $n + 1, \dots, m - 1$ (the limit of the proportion of such $x < N$, which exists since the set is periodic), and a sequence $f$ is unimodal from $a$ when there is $M$ with $f(m) \le f(m+1)$ for $a \le m < M$ and $f(m+1) \le f(m)$ for $m \ge \max(a, M)$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [The density of integers with one divisor in an interval need not be unimodal](exactly-one-divisor-density-unimodal-ref.md).
