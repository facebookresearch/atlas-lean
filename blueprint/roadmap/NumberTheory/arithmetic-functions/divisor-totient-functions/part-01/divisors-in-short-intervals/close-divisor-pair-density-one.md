# Erdős Problem 144 (integers with two divisors within a factor of two)

The statement that the natural numbers $n$ having two divisors $d_1 < d_2 < 2d_1$ have natural density $1$: the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $1$ as $b \to \infty$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Almost all integers have two divisors within a factor of two](close-divisor-pair-density-one-holds.md).
