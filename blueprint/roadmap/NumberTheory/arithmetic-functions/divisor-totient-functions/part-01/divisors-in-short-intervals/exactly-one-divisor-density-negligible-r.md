# Integers with exactly one divisor in a dyadic interval are not negligible

It is not the case that whenever $\delta(n)$ and $\delta_1(n)$ are the natural densities of the integers with a divisor in $(n, 2n)$ and of the integers with exactly one divisor in $(n, 2n)$, $\delta_1(n) = o(\delta(n))$ as $n \to \infty$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 446, integers with exactly one such divisor](exactly-one-divisor-density-negligible.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Ford (2008), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #446, https://www.erdosproblems.com/446.
- K. Ford, The distribution of integers with a divisor in a given interval, Ann. of Math. (2) 168 (2008), 367–433.
