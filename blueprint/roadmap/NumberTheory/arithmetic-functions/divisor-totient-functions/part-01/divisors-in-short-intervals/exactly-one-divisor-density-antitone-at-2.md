# Density of integers with exactly one divisor in (1, m) is non-increasing

$\delta_1(1, m+1) \le \delta_1(1, m)$ for every $m \ge 3$, where for natural numbers $n$ and $m$, $\delta_1(n, m)$ is the natural density of the positive integers with exactly one divisor among $n + 1, \dots, m - 1$ (the limit of the proportion of such $x < N$, which exists since the set is periodic).

This resolves [Cambie's monotonicity of the density for n = 1, as a statement](exactly-one-divisor-density-antitone-at.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Cambie (2025), cited under Sources.
