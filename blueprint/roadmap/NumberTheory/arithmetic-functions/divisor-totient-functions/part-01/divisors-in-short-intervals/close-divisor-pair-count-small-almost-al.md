# Erdős Problem 449 (pairs of close divisors)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$, the natural numbers $n$ with $r(n) < \varepsilon\, \tau(n)$ have natural density $1$: the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $1$ as $b \to \infty$; here $r(n)$ is the number of pairs $(d_1, d_2)$ of divisors of $n$ with $d_1 < d_2 < 2 d_1$ and $\tau(n)$ is the number of divisors of $n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Pairs of close divisors are not rare among divisors](close-divisor-pair-count-small-almost-al-2.md).
