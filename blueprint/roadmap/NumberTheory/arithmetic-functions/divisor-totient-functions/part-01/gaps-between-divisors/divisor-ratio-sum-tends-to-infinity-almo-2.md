# Sums of ratios of consecutive divisors tend to infinity for almost all integers

For every real $K$, the natural numbers $n$ with $G(n) > K$ have natural density $1$ (the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $1$ as $b \to \infty$), where for a natural number $n$ with divisors $1 = d_1 < \dots < d_{\tau(n)} = n$, $G(n) = \sum_{i < \tau(n)} d_i/d_{i+1}$.
