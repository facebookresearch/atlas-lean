# Absolute summability from bounds at prime powers

Let $F$ be a multiplicative complex
arithmetic function. Suppose $b,d$ are nonnegative summable sequences and
$|F(p^{k+1})| \le b(p)d(k)$ for every prime $p$ and every $k \ge 0$. Then $\sum_n |F(n)|$ is
finite.

## Sources

- Unique prime factorization embeds positive integers into finite sets of prime-exponent pairs. Mathlib's `summable_finsetProd_of_summable_nonneg` bounds the sum over those sets.
