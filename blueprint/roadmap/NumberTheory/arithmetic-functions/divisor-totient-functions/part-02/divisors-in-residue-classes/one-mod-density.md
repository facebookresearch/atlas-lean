# Density of integers with a divisor congruent to one

For a natural number $m$ and a real $\alpha$, $\delta(m, \alpha)$ is the natural density of the natural numbers divisible by some $d \equiv 1 \pmod m$ with $1 < d < \exp(m^\alpha)$ (the limit of the proportion among $0, \dots, N-1$, which exists since the set is periodic).
