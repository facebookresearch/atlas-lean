# Erdős Problem 697 (density of multiples of divisors congruent to one)

The statement that for every real $\alpha < 1/\log 2$, $\delta(m, \alpha) \to 0$ as $m \to \infty$, and for every real $\alpha > 1/\log 2$, $\delta(m, \alpha) \to 1$, where $\delta(m, \alpha)$ is the natural density of the natural numbers divisible by some $d \equiv 1 \pmod m$ with $1 < d < \exp(m^\alpha)$ (the limit of the proportion among $0, \dots, N-1$, which exists since the set is periodic).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Threshold 1/log 2 for divisors congruent to one modulo m](one-mod-divisor-density-threshold-holds.md).
