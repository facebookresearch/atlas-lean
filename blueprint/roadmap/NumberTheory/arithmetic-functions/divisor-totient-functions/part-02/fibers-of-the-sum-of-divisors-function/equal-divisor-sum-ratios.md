# Erdős Problem 823 (ratios of integers with equal divisor sums)

The statement that for every real $\alpha > 1$ there are sequences $(n_k)$ and $(m_k)$ of positive integers with $\sigma(n_k) = \sigma(m_k)$ for every $k$ and $n_k/m_k \to \alpha$, where $\sigma$ is the sum-of-divisors function.
