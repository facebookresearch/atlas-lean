# Ratios of integers with equal divisor sums are dense

For every real $\alpha > 1$ there are sequences $(n_k)$ and $(m_k)$ of positive integers with $\sigma(n_k) = \sigma(m_k)$ for every $k$ and $n_k/m_k \to \alpha$, where $\sigma$ is the sum-of-divisors function.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 823 (ratios of integers with equal divisor sums)](equal-divisor-sum-ratios.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Pollack (2015), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- P. Pollack, Remarks on fibers of the sum-of-divisors function, in Analytic Number Theory, in honor of Helmut Maier's 60th birthday, Springer (2015), 305–320, Theorem 1.
