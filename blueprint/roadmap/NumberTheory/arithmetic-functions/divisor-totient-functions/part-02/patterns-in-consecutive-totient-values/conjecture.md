# Erdős Problem 415 (patterns in consecutive values of the totient function)

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ with $F(n)/\log\log\log n \to c$ as $n \to \infty$, where $F(n)$ is the largest $k \le n$ such that every permutation of $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ appears among $k$ consecutive totient values $\varphi(m+1), \dots, \varphi(m+k)$ with $m + k \le n$, a permutation $\sigma$ appearing in $x_0, \dots, x_{k-1}$ when $\sigma(i) < \sigma(j)$ implies $x_i < x_j$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [The totient pattern-length ratio has no positive limit](conjecture-refuted.md).
