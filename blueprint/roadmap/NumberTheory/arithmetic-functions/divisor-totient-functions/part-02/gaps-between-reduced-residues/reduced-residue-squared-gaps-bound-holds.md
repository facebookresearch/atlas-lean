# Montgomery–Vaughan bound for squared gaps between reduced residues

There is a real $C$ such that for every $n \ge 1$, $\sum_{1 \le k < \varphi(n)} (a_{k+1} - a_k)^2 \le C n^2/\varphi(n)$, where $a_1 < \dots < a_{\varphi(n)}$ are the integers $1 \le a < n$ coprime to $n$.

- H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, On the distribution of reduced residues, Ann. of Math. (2) 123 (1986), 311–333.
