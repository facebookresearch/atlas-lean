# Proportion of small gaps between reduced residues of primorials

For a natural number $k$ and a real $c$, the proportion $\#\{2 \le i \le \varphi(N_k) : a_i - a_{i-1} \le c N_k/\varphi(N_k)\}/\varphi(N_k)$, where $N_k$ is the product of the first $k$ primes and $a_1 < \dots < a_{\varphi(N_k)}$ are the integers below $N_k$ coprime to $N_k$.
