# Proportion of small gaps between reduced residues of primorials

For a natural number $k$ and a real $c$, the proportion $\#\{2 \le i \le r : a_i - a_{i-1} \le c N_k/\varphi(N_k)\}/\varphi(N_k)$, where $N_k$ is the product of the first $k$ primes and $a_1 < \dots < a_r$ is the increasing list of the integers $1 \le a < N_k$ coprime to $N_k$. For $k \ge 1$, $N_k \ge 2$ and $r = \varphi(N_k)$; at $k = 0$, $N_0 = 1$, the list is empty and the proportion is $0$.
