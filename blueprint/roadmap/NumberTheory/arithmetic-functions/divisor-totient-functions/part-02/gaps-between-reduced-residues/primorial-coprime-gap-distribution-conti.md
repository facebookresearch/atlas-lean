# Erdős Problem 235 (gaps between reduced residues of primorials)

The statement that there is a function $F$, continuous on $[0, \infty)$, such that for every real $c \ge 0$ the proportion of indices $2 \le i \le \varphi(N_k)$ with $a_i - a_{i-1} \le c N_k/\varphi(N_k)$, among $\varphi(N_k)$, tends to $F(c)$ as $k \to \infty$; here $N_k$ is the product of the first $k$ primes and $a_1 < \dots < a_{\varphi(N_k)}$ are the integers $1 \le a < N_k$ coprime to $N_k$.
