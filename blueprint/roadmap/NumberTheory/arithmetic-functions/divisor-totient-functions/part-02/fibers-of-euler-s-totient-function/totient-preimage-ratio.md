# Ratio of the largest and smallest totient preimages

For $n$ in the image of Euler's totient $\varphi$, $f_{\max}(n)/f_{\min}(n)$, the ratio of the largest and the smallest $m$ with $\varphi(m) = n$. With the convention $\varphi(0) = 0$, the only preimage of $0$ is $0$, so the value at $n = 0$ is $0/0 = 0$.
