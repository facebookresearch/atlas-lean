# Erdős Problem 694 (largest and smallest totient preimages)

The statement that $\max_{n \le x} f_{\max}(n)/f_{\min}(n) = (e^\gamma + o(1))\log\log x$ as $x \to \infty$, where the maximum is over the $n \le x$ in the image of Euler's totient $\varphi$, $f_{\max}(n)$ and $f_{\min}(n)$ are the largest and smallest $m$ with $\varphi(m) = n$, and $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant.
