# Maximal ratio of totient preimages up to x

For a natural number $x$, $\max_{n \le x} f_{\max}(n)/f_{\min}(n)$ over the $n$ with $1 \le n \le x$ in the image of $\varphi$, where $f_{\max}(n)$ and $f_{\min}(n)$ are the largest and smallest $m$ with $\varphi(m) = n$ ($0$ for $x = 0$, where there is no such $n$).
