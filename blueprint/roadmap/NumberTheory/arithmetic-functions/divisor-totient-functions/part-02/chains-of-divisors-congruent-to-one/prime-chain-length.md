# Longest chains of prime divisors congruent to one

For a natural number $n$, $h(n)$ is the largest $\ell$ for which there are primes $p_1 < \dots < p_\ell$ dividing $n$ with $p_{i+1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p_i}$ for every $i < \ell$ (for $n \ge 1$ these lengths are bounded; for $n = 0$ every prime divides $n$, and every prime $p$ has larger primes $\equiv 1 \pmod p$, so chains of every length exist and $h(0)$ is $0$ by the convention for the supremum of an unbounded set of natural numbers).
