# Longest chains of divisors congruent to one

For a natural number $n$, $H(n)$ is the largest $u$ for which there are positive integers $d_1 < \dots < d_u$ dividing $n$ with $d_{i+1} \equiv 1 \pmod{d_i}$ for every $i < u$ (for $n \ge 1$ these lengths are bounded; for $n = 0$ every natural number divides $n$, so chains of every length exist and $H(0)$ is $0$ by the convention for the supremum of an unbounded set of natural numbers).
