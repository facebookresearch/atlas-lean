# Erdős Problem 964 (ratios of consecutive divisor counts)

The statement that for all reals $0 < a < b$ there is a natural number $n \ge 1$ with $a < \tau(n+1)/\tau(n) < b$, where $\tau(m)$ is the number of divisors of $m$: the ratios $\tau(n+1)/\tau(n)$ are dense in $(0, \infty)$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Ratios of consecutive divisor counts are dense](divisor-count-ratio-dense-holds.md).
