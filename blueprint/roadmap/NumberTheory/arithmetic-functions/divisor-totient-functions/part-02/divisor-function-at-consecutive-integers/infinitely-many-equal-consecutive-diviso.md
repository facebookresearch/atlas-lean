# Erdős Problem 946 (consecutive integers with equally many divisors)

The statement that there are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ with $\tau(n) = \tau(n + 1)$, where $\tau(m)$ is the number of divisors of $m$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Infinitely many consecutive integers with equally many divisors](infinitely-many-equal-consecutive-diviso-2.md).
