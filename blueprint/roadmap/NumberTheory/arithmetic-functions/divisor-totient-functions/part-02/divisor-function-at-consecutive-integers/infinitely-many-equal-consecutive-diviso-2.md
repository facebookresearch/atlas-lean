# Infinitely many consecutive integers with equally many divisors

There are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ with $\tau(n) = \tau(n + 1)$, where $\tau(m)$ is the number of divisors of $m$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 946 (consecutive integers with equally many divisors)](infinitely-many-equal-consecutive-diviso.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Heath-Brown (1984), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #946, https://www.erdosproblems.com/946.
- D. R. Heath-Brown, The divisor function at consecutive integers, Mathematika (1984), 141–149.
