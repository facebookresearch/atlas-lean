# Erdős Problem 1054 (sums of smallest divisors)

The statement that all of the following hold: (1) $2$ is not the sum of the $k$ smallest divisors of any $m$, for any $1 \le k \le \tau(m)$; (2) neither is $5$; (3) every $n \ge 6$ is such a sum, so that $f(n)$ is defined; and (4) either $f(n) = o(n)$ as $n \to \infty$ ($n \ge 6$), or $f(n) = o(n)$ along a set of $n$ of natural density $1$ while $\limsup_n f(n)/n = \infty$. Here $f(n)$ is the least $m$ such that $n$ is the sum of the $k$ smallest divisors of $m$ for some $1 \le k \le \tau(m)$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
