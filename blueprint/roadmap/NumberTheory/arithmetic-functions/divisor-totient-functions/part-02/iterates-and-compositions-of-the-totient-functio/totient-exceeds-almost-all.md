# Erdős Problem 1064, first part

The statement that the set of $n$ with $\varphi(n) > \varphi(n - \varphi(n))$ has natural density $1$, where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [φ(n) exceeds φ(n − φ(n)) for almost all n](totient-exceeds-almost-all-holds.md).
