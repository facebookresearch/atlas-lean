# Erdős Problem 1064, second part

The statement that there are infinitely many $n$ with $\varphi(n) < \varphi(n - \varphi(n))$, where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [φ(n) is below φ(n − φ(n)) infinitely often](totient-deficit-infinitely-often-holds.md).
