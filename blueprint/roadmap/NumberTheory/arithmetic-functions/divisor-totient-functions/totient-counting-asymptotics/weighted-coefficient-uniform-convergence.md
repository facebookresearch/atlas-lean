# Uniform convergence of the weighted coefficient

For each fixed natural $k\ge1$, the approximants $A_H(f_k;s)$ converge to $A(f_k;s)$ uniformly for
real $s\in[0,1)$ as the natural cutoff $H\to\infty$. No seed condition is imposed.
