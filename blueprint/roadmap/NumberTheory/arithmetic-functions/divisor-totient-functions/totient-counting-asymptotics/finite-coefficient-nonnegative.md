# Nonnegativity of the unweighted finite coefficient

For every natural stage $H$ and every real phase $s\in[0,1)$, the finite arithmetic coefficient
with constant weight one satisfies $A_H(1;s)\ge0$.

## Expansion

Show nonnegativity from the union-mass interpretation of the inner signed inclusion–exclusion sum, including the empty-support case; do not drop the signs or identify nonnegativity with positivity of the limit.
