# Uniform positive bounds for the unweighted coefficient

There exist real constants $c_->0$ and $c_+$ such that $c_-\le A(1;s)\le c_+$ for all phases
$0\le s<1$.

## Depends on

- [Arithmetic approximants](finite-coefficients.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Uniform convergence](unweighted-coefficient-uniform-convergence.md)
- [Nonnegativity of the finite coefficient](finite-coefficient-nonnegative.md)

## Expansion

Prove the positive lower bound furnished by a nonzero seed and a uniform upper bound. The bounds must be uniform even along phases approaching 1 from below.
