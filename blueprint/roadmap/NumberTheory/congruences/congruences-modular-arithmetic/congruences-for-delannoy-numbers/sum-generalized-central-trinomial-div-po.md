# Sum of generalized central trinomial coefficients over powers of $m$ modulo $p$

Let $p$ be an odd prime, let $b, c, m$ be integers with $p \nmid m$, and let $T_k(b, c)$ be the
coefficient of $x^k$ in $(x^2 + b x + c)^k$. Then in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$

$$\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} T_k(b, c)\, (m^{-1})^k = \left(\frac{(m - b)^2 - 4c}{p}\right),$$

where $m^{-1}$ is the inverse of $m$ in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ and the right side is the Legendre
symbol, read in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.

## Sources

- Chahinaze Djadi and Hacène Belbachir, "Congruence Properties for the Delannoy Triangle Coefficients", Journal of Integer Sequences 29 (2026), Article 26.5.2, Lemma 12 (quoting Z.-W. Sun, Sci. China Math. 57 (2014), Theorem 1.2(i)).
