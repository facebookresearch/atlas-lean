# Central trinomial coefficients as generalized central trinomial coefficients

For integers $a, b$ and every natural number $n$, the constant term of the Laurent polynomial
$(a x^{-1} + b + a x)^n$ equals the generalized central trinomial coefficient $T_n(b, a^2)$, where
$T_n(b, c)$ is the coefficient of $x^n$ in $(x^2 + b x + c)^n$.
