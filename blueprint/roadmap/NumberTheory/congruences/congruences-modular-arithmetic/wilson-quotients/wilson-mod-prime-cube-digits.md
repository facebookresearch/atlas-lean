# Wilson's theorem modulo $p^3$ through $p$-adic digits of Fermat quotients

Let $p$ be an odd prime. For $1 \le i \le p - 1$ let $\delta_0(i)$ be the remainder of $(i^{p-1} - 1)/p$ modulo $p$, and $\delta_1(i)$ the remainder of $\lfloor (i^{p-1} - 1)/p^2 \rfloor$ modulo $p$, the second and third $p$-adic digits of $i^{p-1}$. Then, with all sums over $1 \le i \le p - 1$, the rational number

$$(p-1)! - \left(-1 + p \sum \delta_0(i) + p^2 \sum \left(\delta_0(i) + \delta_1(i)\right) - \frac{p^2}{2}\left(\left(\sum \delta_0(i)\right)^2 + \sum \delta_0(i)^2\right)\right)$$

has $p$-adic norm at most $p^{-3}$. The hypothesis $p \neq 2$ is needed: at $p = 2$ the difference is $2$.
