# Wilson's theorem modulo $p^2$

Let $p$ be an odd prime and, for $1 \le k \le p - 1$, let
$q_p(k)$ be the Fermat quotient of $k$, the integer with $k^{p-1} = 1 + p\, q_p(k)$. Then

$$
(p - 1)! \equiv -1 + p \sum_{k=1}^{p-1} q_p(k) \pmod{p^2}.
$$
