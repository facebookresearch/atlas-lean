# Second $p$-adic digit of the Hensel roots of $X^{p-1} + (p-1)!$

Let $p$ be a prime, $k$ an integer with $1 \le k \le p - 1$, and $t$ an integer with $p^2 \mid (k + p t)^{p-1} + (p-1)!$. Let $\delta_0(k) = (k^{p-1} - 1)/p$ be the Fermat quotient of $k$. Then

$$p t \equiv k \left(1 + (p-1)! + p \, \delta_0(k)\right) \pmod{p^2}.$$

## Sources

Claire Levaillant, "Wilson's Theorem Modulo $p^2$ Derived from Faulhaber Polynomials", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A87, Lemma 1.
