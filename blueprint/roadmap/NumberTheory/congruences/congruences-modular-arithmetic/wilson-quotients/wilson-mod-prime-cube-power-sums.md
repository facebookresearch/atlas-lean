# Wilson's theorem modulo $p^3$ through power sums

Let $p$ be an odd prime, $S_1 = \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} i^{p-1}$ and $S_2 = \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} \left(i^{p-1}\right)^2$. Then the rational number

$$(p-1)! - \left(-1 - \frac{S_1^2 + S_2}{2} + (2p + 1) S_1 - (p - 1)\left(\frac{3p}{2} + 1\right)\right)$$

has $p$-adic norm at most $p^{-3}$. The hypothesis $p \neq 2$ is needed: at $p = 2$ the difference is $2$.
