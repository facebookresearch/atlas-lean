# Glaisher's congruence for $(p-1)!$ modulo $p^2$

For every prime $p$, the rational number $(p-1)! - \left(p B_{p-1} - p\right)$ has $p$-adic norm at most $p^{-2}$, that is,

$$(p-1)! \equiv p B_{p-1} - p \pmod{p^2},$$

where $B_n$ are the Bernoulli numbers with $B_1 = -\tfrac{1}{2}$.

Claire Levaillant, "Wilson's Theorem Modulo $p^2$ Derived from Faulhaber Polynomials", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A87, Corollary 1 (after Glaisher).
