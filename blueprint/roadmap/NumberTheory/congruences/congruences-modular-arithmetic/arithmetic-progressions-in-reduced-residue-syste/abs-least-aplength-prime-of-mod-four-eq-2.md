# Longest progressions of absolute least residues: primes congruent to 3 mod 4

For a
prime $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, the length $g(p)$ of the longest arithmetic progression in the
absolute least reduced residue system modulo $p$ is $(p + 1)/2$.

## Sources

- Chaninat Phothila, Natthakan Thoket and Narakorn Rompurk Kanasri, *Length of the Longest Arithmetic Progressions in a Certain Reduced Residue System*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A6, Theorem 1 (`integers-2026-A6-thm-1`).
