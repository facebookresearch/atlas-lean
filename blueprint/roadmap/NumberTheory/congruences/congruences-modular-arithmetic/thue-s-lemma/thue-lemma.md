# Thue's lemma

Let $a$ and $n$ be integers with $a > 1$, $n > 1$ and $\gcd(a, n) = 1$. Then
there exist nonzero integers $x$ and $y$ with $|x| < \sqrt{n}$, $|y| < \sqrt{n}$ and
$x \equiv a y \pmod{n}$.

The hypothesis $a > 1$ follows the source and is not needed for the conclusion.
