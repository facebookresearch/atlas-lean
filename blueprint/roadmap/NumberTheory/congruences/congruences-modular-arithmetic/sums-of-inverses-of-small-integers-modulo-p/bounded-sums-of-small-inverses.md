# Erdős Problem 1180 (residues as bounded sums of inverses of small integers)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $C$ such that for every prime $p$, every residue modulo $p$ is a sum of at most $C$ inverses $n^{-1} \bmod p$ with $1 \le n \le p^\varepsilon$ and $p \nmid n$ (repetitions allowed).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Residues are bounded sums of inverses of small integers](bounded-sums-of-small-inverses-holds.md).
