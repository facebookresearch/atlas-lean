# Residues are bounded sums of inverses of small integers

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $C$ such that for every prime $p$, every residue modulo $p$ is a sum of at most $C$ inverses $n^{-1} \bmod p$ with $1 \le n \le p^\varepsilon$ and $p \nmid n$ (repetitions allowed).
