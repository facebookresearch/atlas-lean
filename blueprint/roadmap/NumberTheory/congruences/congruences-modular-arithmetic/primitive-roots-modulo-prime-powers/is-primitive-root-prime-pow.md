# Lifting primitive roots to prime powers

Let $p$ be an odd prime and $g$ a natural number that is a primitive root modulo $p$ with $g^{p-1} \not\equiv 1 \pmod{p^2}$. Then $g$ is a primitive root modulo $p^\ell$ for every $\ell \ge 2$.
