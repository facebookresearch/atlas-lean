# Covering density of a CD congruence set by inclusion–exclusion

Let $d_1, \dots, d_t$ be positive integers and $a_1, \dots, a_t$ integers such that, for $i \ne j$,
the classes $a_i \bmod d_i$ and $a_j \bmod d_j$ share an integer only if $\gcd(d_i, d_j) = 1$. Then
the proportion of the integers $x \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ lying in at least one class $a_i \bmod d_i$
tends, as $N \to \infty$, to

$$\sum_{S} \frac{(-1)^{|S| + 1}}{\prod_{i \in S} d_i},$$

the sum running over the nonempty sets $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, t\}$ of indices whose moduli are
pairwise coprime.

- Sarosh Adenwalla, "A Question of Erdős and Graham on Covering Systems", INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A52, Lemma 2.1.
