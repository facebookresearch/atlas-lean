# Non-intersecting numbers of the form $q p^k$ and their covering density

Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct primes and $k \ge 1$. Then

1. $q p^k$ is non-intersecting if and only if $k = 1$ or $p > 2$; and
2. for every CD congruence set $\{a(d) \bmod d\}$ of $q p^k$ (one class for each divisor $d > 1$),
   the proportion of the integers $x \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ lying in at least one of the classes
   tends, as $N \to \infty$, to

    $$\frac{1}{q} + \frac{p^k - 1}{p^k (p - 1)}.$$

## Sources

- Sarosh Adenwalla, "A Question of Erdős and Graham on Covering Systems", INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A52, Proposition 4.2.
