# Non-intersecting integers have few prime factors beyond the smallest

Let $n > 1$ be non-intersecting and let $p$ be the smallest prime dividing $n$. Then $n / p$ has
fewer than $p$ distinct prime divisors.

## Sources

- Sarosh Adenwalla, "A Question of Erdős and Graham on Covering Systems", INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A52, Lemma 3.1.
