# Prime powers are non-intersecting, with covering density $(p^k - 1)/(p^k(p - 1))$

Let $p$ be a prime and $k \ge 1$. Then $p^k$ is non-intersecting, and for every CD congruence set
$\{a(d) \bmod d\}$ of $p^k$ (one class for each divisor $d > 1$), the proportion of the integers
$x \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ lying in at least one of the classes tends, as $N \to \infty$, to

$$\frac{p^k - 1}{p^k (p - 1)}.$$

## Sources

- Sarosh Adenwalla, "A Question of Erdős and Graham on Covering Systems", INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A52, Proposition 4.1.
