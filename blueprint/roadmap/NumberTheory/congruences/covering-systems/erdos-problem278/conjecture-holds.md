# Equal residues minimize the density covered by congruences

For every $r$ and all integers $1 \le n_1 < \dots < n_r$ there is a natural number $c < n_1$ such that choosing $a_i = c$ for every $i$ minimizes the covered count: no choice of residues $0 \le a_i < n_i$ covers fewer integers in one period; here for moduli $n_1, \dots, n_r$ and residues $0 \le a_i < n_i$, the covered count is the number of $x \in \{0, 1, \dots, M - 1\}$, $M = n_1 \cdots n_r$, with $x \equiv a_i \pmod{n_i}$ for some $i$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 278 (minimum density covered by congruences)](conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Simpson (1986), cited under Sources.
