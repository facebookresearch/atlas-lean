# Erdős's minimum modulus problem for covering systems

The statement that
for every natural number $N$ there are finitely many residue classes $a_1 \bmod m_1, \dots,
a_k \bmod m_k$ that cover $\mathbb{Z}$ (every integer lies in at least one of them) whose moduli
are pairwise distinct and all at least $N$.

Erdős asked whether this holds; Hough answered the question in the negative, see [Hough's minimum modulus theorem](conjecture-refuted.md).

## Sources

- Bob Hough, Solution of the minimum modulus problem for covering systems, Annals of Mathematics 181 (2015), 361-382; arXiv:1307.0874, https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.0874. The abstract states that the paper answers this question of Erdős.
