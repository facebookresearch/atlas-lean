# Erdős Problem 277 (abundant numbers whose divisors carry no covering system)

The statement that for every real $c$ there is a natural number $n$ with $\sigma(n) > c\,n$ such that no choice of one residue class $a_d \bmod d$ for each divisor $d > 1$ of $n$ covers all of $\mathbb{Z}$: some integer $x$ satisfies $x \not\equiv a_d \pmod d$ for every divisor $d > 1$ of $n$. Here $\sigma(n)$ is the sum of the divisors of $n$; equivalently, no covering system has distinct moduli all of which are divisors $d > 1$ of $n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Abundant numbers whose divisors carry no covering system](abundant-non-covering-numbers-holds.md).
