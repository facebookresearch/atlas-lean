# Some finite colouring admits no covering system with monochromatic moduli

It is not the case that for every colouring of the natural numbers with finitely many colours there is a covering system of $\mathbb{Z}$, finitely many residue classes $a \bmod m$ with distinct moduli $m \ge 2$ whose union is $\mathbb{Z}$, all of whose moduli have the same colour.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 8 (covering systems with monochromatic moduli)](monochromatic-covering-system.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Hough (2015), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #8, https://www.erdosproblems.com/8.
- B. Hough, Solution of the minimum modulus problem for covering systems, Ann. of Math. (2) 181 (2015), 361–382, arXiv:1307.0874.
