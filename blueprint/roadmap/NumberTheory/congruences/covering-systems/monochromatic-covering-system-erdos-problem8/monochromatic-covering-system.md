# Erdős Problem 8 (covering systems with monochromatic moduli)

The statement that for every colouring of the natural numbers with finitely many colours there is a covering system of $\mathbb{Z}$, finitely many residue classes $a \bmod m$ with distinct moduli $m \ge 2$ whose union is $\mathbb{Z}$, all of whose moduli have the same colour.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Some finite colouring admits no covering system with monochromatic moduli](monochromatic-covering-system-refuted.md).
