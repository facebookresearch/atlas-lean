# Eisenstein's lemma for Jacobi symbols

Let $a$ and $b$ be positive odd natural numbers that
are coprime. Then the Jacobi symbol $\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)$ equals $(-1)^{S}$, where
$S = \sum_{i=1}^{(b-1)/2} \lfloor ia/b \rfloor$.
