# Small pairs of consecutive k-th power residues

For every $k \ge 2$ there are natural numbers $B$ and $N$ such that for every prime $p \ge N$ some $r$ with $1 \le r \le B$ has both $r$ and $r + 1$ being $k$-th power residues modulo $p$, where a natural number $r$ is a $k$-th power residue modulo $p$ when $r \equiv x^k \pmod p$ for some $x \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 436, first question (consecutive power residues)](conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Hildebrand (1991), cited under Sources.
