# Average of the least k-th power non-residue over the primes p ≡ 1 (mod k)

For every $k \ge 2$ there is a real $c > 0$ with $\sum_{p} n_k(p) \sim c\, x/\log x$ as $x \to \infty$, the sum over the primes $p < x$ with $p \equiv 1 \pmod k$, where for natural numbers $k$ and $p$, $n_k(p)$ is the least positive integer that is not a $k$-th power modulo $p$ ($0$ if every residue is a $k$-th power).

Problem 980 as printed sums $n_k(p)$ over all primes $p < x$. The sum here is restricted to the primes $p \equiv 1 \pmod k$, the range of Elliott's theorem as Martin and Pollack state it. For prime $k$ the omitted primes have no $k$-th power non-residue; for composite $k$ some do, for instance the prime $3$ for $k = 4$, with $n_4(3) = 2$. This is the progression-restricted result.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 980 restricted to the primes p ≡ 1 (mod k) (sums of least power non-residues)](least-power-nonresidue-sum-asymptotic.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Elliott (1967), cited under Sources.
