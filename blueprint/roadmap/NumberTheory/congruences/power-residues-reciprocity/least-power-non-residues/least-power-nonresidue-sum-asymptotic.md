# Erdős Problem 980 restricted to the primes p ≡ 1 (mod k) (sums of least power non-residues)

The statement that for every $k \ge 2$ there is a real $c > 0$ with $\sum_{p} n_k(p) \sim c\, x/\log x$ as $x \to \infty$, the sum over the primes $p < x$ with $p \equiv 1 \pmod k$, where for natural numbers $k$ and $p$, $n_k(p)$ is the least positive integer that is not a $k$-th power modulo $p$ ($0$ if every residue is a $k$-th power).

Problem 980 as printed sums over all primes $p < x$; this is the version restricted to the primes $p \equiv 1 \pmod k$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Average of the least k-th power non-residue over the primes p ≡ 1 (mod k)](least-power-nonresidue-sum-asymptotic-ho.md).
