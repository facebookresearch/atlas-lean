# Quadratic Dirichlet character of a fundamental discriminant

Let $D$ be a fundamental
discriminant. Then there is a unique integer-valued Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $|D|$ such
that $\chi(n \bmod |D|) = \left(\frac{D}{n}\right)$ for every natural number $n$, where
$\left(\frac{D}{n}\right)$ is the Kronecker symbol.

## Sources

- Joshua Males, Andreas Mono, Larry Rolen, and Ian Wagner, *Central L-values of newforms and local polynomials*, arXiv:2306.15519v5, subsection “L-functions and L-values”: “One may also consider L-functions associated to a Dirichlet character $\chi_D = (D/\cdot)$, namely …”. The statement here is the existence and uniqueness of this character; it makes no claim about primitivity or the conductor.
