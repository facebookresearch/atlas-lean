# Kronecker symbol of $4m$ at odd numbers, for $m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$

Let $\chi_4$ be the nontrivial Dirichlet character modulo $4$, let $m$ be an integer with
$m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, and let $p$ be an odd natural number, prime or not. Then
$\chi_4(p) \cdot \left(\frac{p}{|m|}\right) = \left(\frac{4m}{p}\right)$, where
$\left(\frac{p}{|m|}\right)$ is the Jacobi symbol and $\left(\frac{4m}{p}\right)$ is the Kronecker
symbol.

At an odd $p$ the Kronecker symbol $\left(\frac{4m}{p}\right)$ is the Jacobi symbol, which equals
$\left(\frac{m}{p}\right)$ ([Jacobi symbol of $4m$ at an odd denominator](jacobi-sym-four-mul-of-odd.md)),
so this is [the case $D = m$ of the statement for integers congruent to 3 mod 4](chi4-mul-jacobi-sym-nat-abs-eq-kronecker.md).
