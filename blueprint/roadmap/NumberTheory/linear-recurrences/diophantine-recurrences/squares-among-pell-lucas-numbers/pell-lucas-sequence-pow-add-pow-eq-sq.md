# Sums of powers of consecutive Pell–Lucas numbers that are squares

Let $(Q_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Pell–Lucas numbers given by $Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$ and $Q_{n+2} = 2Q_{n+1} +
Q_n$. For natural numbers $n$, $x$ and $y$, the equation $Q_n^x + Q_{n+1}^x = y^2$ holds if and only
if $n = 1$, $x = 1$ and $y = 2$; that is, its only solution in natural numbers is $Q_1 + Q_2 = 1 +
3 = 2^2$.
