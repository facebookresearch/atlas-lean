# Square Pell–Lucas numbers

Let $(Q_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Pell–Lucas numbers given by $Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$ and $Q_{n+2} = 2Q_{n+1} +
Q_n$. For every natural number $n$, $Q_n$ is a perfect square if and only if $n = 0$ or $n = 1$;
thus the only square terms of the sequence are $Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$.
