# Consecutive Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime

Let $(Q_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Pell–Lucas numbers given by $Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$ and $Q_{n+2} = 2Q_{n+1} +
Q_n$. For every natural number $n$, $Q_n$ and $Q_{n+1}$ satisfy $\gcd(Q_n, Q_{n+1}) = 1$.
