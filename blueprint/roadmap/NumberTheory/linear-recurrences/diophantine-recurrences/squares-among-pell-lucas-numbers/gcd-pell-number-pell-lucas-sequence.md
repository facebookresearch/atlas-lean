# Pell and Pell–Lucas numbers are coprime

Let $(P_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Pell numbers, given by $P_0 = 0$, $P_1 = 1$ and $P_{n+2} = 2P_{n+1} +
P_n$, and let $(Q_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Pell–Lucas numbers given by $Q_0 = Q_1 = 1$ and $Q_{n+2} =
2Q_{n+1} + Q_n$. For every natural number $n$, $P_n$ and $Q_n$ satisfy $\gcd(P_n, Q_n) = 1$.
