# Nonzero single digits are repdigits

Every natural number $d$ with $1 \le d \le 9$ is a
repdigit.

## Sources

- Monalisa Mohapatra, Pritam Kumar Bhoi, and Gopal Krishna Panda, *Balancing and Lucas-Balancing Numbers as the Difference of Two Repdigits*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A15, Section 1 (definition of repdigit).
