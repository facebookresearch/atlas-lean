# Balancing numbers

The balancing numbers are the integers $B_n$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$
defined by $B_0 = 0$, $B_1 = 1$ and $B_{n+2} = 6 B_{n+1} - B_n$ for all $n$.

## Sources

- Standard definition, as used in Monalisa Mohapatra, Pritam Kumar Bhoi, and Gopal Krishna Panda, *Balancing and Lucas-Balancing Numbers as the Difference of Two Repdigits*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A15; the values $B_2 = 6$, $B_3 = 35$ are those displayed in its Theorem 2.
