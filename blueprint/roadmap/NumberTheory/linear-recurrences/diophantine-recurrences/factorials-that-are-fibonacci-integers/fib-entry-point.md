# Entry point in the Fibonacci sequence

The entry point (rank of apparition) $z(k)$ of a
natural number $k$ is the least $m > 0$ such that $k$ divides the Fibonacci number $F_m$, and
$z(k) = 0$ if there is no such $m$.
