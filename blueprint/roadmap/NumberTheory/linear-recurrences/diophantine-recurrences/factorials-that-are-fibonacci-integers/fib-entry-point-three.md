# Fibonacci entry point of $3$

The least $m > 0$ such that $3$ divides the Fibonacci number
$F_m$ is $4$, that is, the Fibonacci entry point of $3$ is $z(3) = 4$.

## Sources

- direct computation ($F_1 = F_2 = 1$, $F_3 = 2$, $F_4 = 3$) from the definition of the entry point in Florian Luca, *`F_4 F_5^6 F_7 F_8^4 F_9 F_{10}^2 F_{12}^5 F_{14} F_{15}^{-1} F_{18} F_{24} F_{30} = 36!`*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A72, Section 2.
