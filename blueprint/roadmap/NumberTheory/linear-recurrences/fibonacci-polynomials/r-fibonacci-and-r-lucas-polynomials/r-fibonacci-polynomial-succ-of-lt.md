# Initial values of the $r$-Fibonacci polynomials

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, let
$x, y \in R$, and let $n < r$ be natural numbers. Then the $r$-Fibonacci polynomial
$U^{(r)}_{n+1}(x, y)$ equals $x^n$.
