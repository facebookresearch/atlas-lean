# The (-r)-Fibonacci polynomials vanish at indices 2 to r

Let $A$ be a commutative ring,
$x \in A$ and $y$ a unit of $A$. For natural numbers $r$ and $n$ with $1 \le n < r$, the
$(-r)$-Fibonacci polynomial satisfies $U^{(-r)}_{n+1}(x, y) = 0$.
