# The $r$-th term of the companion sequence $V^{(-r)}$

Let $A$ be a commutative ring,
$r \ge 1$ a natural number, $x \in A$ and $y$ a unit of $A$. The companion sequence of the
$(-r)$-Fibonacci polynomials satisfies $V^{(-r)}_r(x, y) = r y^{-1}$.
