# Binomial coefficients of odd lower index as an alternating sum

For natural numbers $n$
and $k$ with $k < \lfloor (n-1)/2 \rfloor$,

$$\binom{n}{2k+1} = \sum_{i=k}^{\lfloor (n-1)/2 \rfloor} (-1)^{i-k} \, 2^{n-2i-1}
  \binom{n-i-1}{i} \binom{i}{k}.$$
