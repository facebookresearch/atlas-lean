# The row-one front value is nonsingular

For every natural number $k$, the element
$1 - X_1$ of the field of rational functions over $\mathbb{Q}$ in the variables $X_0, X_1, \dots$
is not equal to $3 \cdot 9^k$.

## Sources

- Emily J. Evans and Russell Jay Hendel, *Recursions and Characteristic Polynomials of the Rows of the Circuit Array*, Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), Article 62-4/hendel08232024rev2, Definition 2.5.
