# Right edge on the left front

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let
$x, q \in K$ with $q \ne 0$ and $x + 3q - 1 \ne 0$. Then the wye-delta value
`circuitY` of the delta-wye values $\Delta(1, 1, 1)$, $\Delta(1, 1, 1 - (1 - x)/q)$ and
$\Delta(1, 1, 1)$ is $(9q - 1 + x)/(3(x + 3q - 1))$.

## Sources

- Emily J. Evans and Russell Jay Hendel, *Recursions and Characteristic Polynomials of the Rows of the Circuit Array*, Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), Article 62-4/hendel08232024rev2, Section 2 and the proof of Corollary 4.10.
