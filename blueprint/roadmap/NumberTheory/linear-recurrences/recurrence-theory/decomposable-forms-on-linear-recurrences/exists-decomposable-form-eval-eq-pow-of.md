# Decomposable forms taking the values of a power on recurrence sequences

Let
$\gamma_0, \dots, \gamma_{k-1} \in \mathbb{C}$, let $\chi(x) = x^k - \sum_{i\lt k} \gamma_i x^i$,
and let $G^{(1)}, \dots, G^{(k)} \colon \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$ be $\mathbb{C}$-linearly
independent sequences, each satisfying $G_{n+k} = \sum_{i\lt k} \gamma_i G_{n+i}$ for every
$n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then there are linear forms $L_1, \dots, L_k$ in $k$ variables with complex
coefficients whose product $F = L_1 \cdots L_k$ satisfies
$F(G^{(1)}_n, \dots, G^{(k)}_n) = ((-1)^k \chi(0))^n$ for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

For $k \ge 1$ we have $\chi(0) = -\gamma_0$, so the right side is $((-1)^{k+1} \gamma_0)^n$.

- Kálmán Győry, Attila Pethő and László Szalay, *Decomposable Forms Generated by Linear Recurrences*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.5, Theorem 2 (`jis-2024-24.3.5-thm-2`).
