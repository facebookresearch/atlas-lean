# Symmetric weighted sums of Lucas sequence products

Let $T_{i,j}$ be real numbers with $T_{i,j} = T_{j,i}$ and $p_n = \sum_{i=0}^{n} T_{n-i,i}$. Then for all natural numbers $k, m, n$, $p_n U_{kn+2m} = \sum_{i=0}^{n} T_{n-i,i} V_{ki+m} U_{k(n-i)+m}$, where for integers $P$ and $Q \ne 0$, $U_n$ and $V_n$ are the Lucas sequences with $U_0 = 0$, $U_1 = 1$, $V_0 = 2$, $V_1 = P$ and $X_{n+2} = PX_{n+1} - QX_n$.

## Sources

- V. V. Kruchinin and M. Y. Perminova, Identities and Hadamard product of the generalized Fibonacci, Lucas, Catalan, and harmonic numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.8.8, Theorem 2.
