# Generating function of Catalan numbers times a Lucas sequence

Let $P$ and $Q \ne 0$ be integers and suppose $S$ is a power series over $\mathbb{Q}$ with constant term $1$ and $S^2 = 1 - 4Px + 16Qx^2$. Then $(P^2 - 4Q)\bigl(1 + 2Qx\sum_{n \ge 0} C_n U_n x^n\bigr)^2 = -2QS - 4PQx + (P^2 - 2Q)$ as power series, where $C_n$ is the $n$-th Catalan number and for integers $P$ and $Q \ne 0$, $U_n$ and $V_n$ are the Lucas sequences with $U_0 = 0$, $U_1 = 1$, $V_0 = 2$, $V_1 = P$ and $X_{n+2} = PX_{n+1} - QX_n$.

## Sources

- V. V. Kruchinin and M. Y. Perminova, Identities and Hadamard product of the generalized Fibonacci, Lucas, Catalan, and harmonic numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.8.8, Corollary 5.
