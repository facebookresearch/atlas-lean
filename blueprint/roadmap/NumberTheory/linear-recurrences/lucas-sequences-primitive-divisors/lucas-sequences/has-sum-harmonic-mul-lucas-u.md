# Generating function of harmonic numbers times a Lucas sequence

Let $P$ and $Q \ne 0$ be integers with $D = P^2 - 4Q > 0$. Then there is $r > 0$ such that for every real $|x| < r$, $\sum_{n \ge 0} H_n U_n x^n = -\dfrac{\frac{2 - Px}{\sqrt D}\log\frac{2 - \sqrt D x - Px}{2 + \sqrt D x - Px} + x\log(1 - Px + Qx^2)}{2(1 - Px + Qx^2)}$, where $H_n$ is the $n$-th harmonic number and for integers $P$ and $Q \ne 0$, $U_n$ and $V_n$ are the Lucas sequences with $U_0 = 0$, $U_1 = 1$, $V_0 = 2$, $V_1 = P$ and $X_{n+2} = PX_{n+1} - QX_n$.

## Sources

- V. V. Kruchinin and M. Y. Perminova, Identities and Hadamard product of the generalized Fibonacci, Lucas, Catalan, and harmonic numbers, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.8.8, Theorem 3.
