# Fibonacci numbers shifted by $14475$ are never prime

Let $F_n$ be the $n$-th
Fibonacci number, with $F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$ and $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_n$. For every
natural number $n$, the number $F_n + 14475$ is not prime.

## Sources

- Dan Ismailescu, Daniel Hyunwoo Jeon, Aaron Kim, Joonbeom Kwon and Austin Lee, *Shifting Lucas Sequences Away from Primes*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A42, Theorem 5 (`integers-2025-A42-thm-5`).
