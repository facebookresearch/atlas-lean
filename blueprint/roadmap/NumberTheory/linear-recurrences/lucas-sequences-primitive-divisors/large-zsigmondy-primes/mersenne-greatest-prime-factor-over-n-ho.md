# The greatest prime factor of 2^n − 1 grows faster than n

$P(2^n - 1)/n \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$, where $P(m)$ is the greatest prime factor of $m$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 977 (the greatest prime factor of 2^n − 1)](mersenne-greatest-prime-factor-over-n.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Stewart (2013), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- C. L. Stewart, On divisors of Lucas and Lehmer numbers, Acta Math. 211 (2013), 291–314, arXiv:1008.1274.
