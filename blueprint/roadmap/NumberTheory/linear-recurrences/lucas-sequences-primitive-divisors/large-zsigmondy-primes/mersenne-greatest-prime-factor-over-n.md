# Erdős Problem 977 (the greatest prime factor of 2^n − 1)

The statement that $P(2^n - 1)/n \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$, where $P(m)$ is the greatest prime factor of $m$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The greatest prime factor of 2^n − 1 grows faster than n](mersenne-greatest-prime-factor-over-n-ho.md).
