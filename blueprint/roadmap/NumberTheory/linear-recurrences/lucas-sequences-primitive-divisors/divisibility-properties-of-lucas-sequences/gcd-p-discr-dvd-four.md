# Common divisors of $P$ and the discriminant

Let $P$ and $Q$ be coprime integers and let
$D = P^2 - 4Q$. Then $\gcd(P, D)$ divides $4$, and $\gcd(P, D') = 1$ for every odd integer $D'$
dividing $D$.

## Sources

- Lawrence Somer and Michal Křížek, *On Lucas and Frobenius Pseudoprimes*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A107, Lemma 4.9 (`integers-2025-A107-lem-4-9`).
