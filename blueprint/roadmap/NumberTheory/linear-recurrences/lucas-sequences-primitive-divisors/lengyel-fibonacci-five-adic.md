# The $5$-adic valuation of Fibonacci numbers

Writing $F_n$ for the $n$-th Fibonacci number
and $v_5$ for the $5$-adic valuation, $v_5(F_n) = v_5(n)$ for every integer $n \ge 1$.

The source states the formula for every $n \ge 0$; at $n = 0$ both sides are infinite, since
$F_0 = 0$.

## Sources

- Amirali Fatehizadeh and Daniel Yaqubi, *Average of the Fibonacci Numbers*, Journal of Integer Sequences 25 (2022), Article 22.2.6, Lemma 6 ($5$-adic clause; the lemma also gives the $2$-adic and $3$-adic valuations of $F_n$), citing T. Lengyel, *The Order of the Fibonacci and Lucas Numbers*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 33 (1995), 234–239, Lemmas 1 and 2.
