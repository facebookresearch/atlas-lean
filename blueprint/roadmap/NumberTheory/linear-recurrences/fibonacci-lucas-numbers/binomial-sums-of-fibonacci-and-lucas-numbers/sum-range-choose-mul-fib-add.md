# Binomial sums of shifted Fibonacci numbers

For all natural numbers $n$ and $m$, $\sum_{i=0}^{n} \binom{n}{i} F_{i+m} = F_{2n+m}$, where $F_k$ is the $k$-th Fibonacci number.
