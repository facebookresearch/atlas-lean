# Genocchi numbers via Stirling numbers of the second kind

For every natural number
$n \ge 1$, the Genocchi number $G_n$, given by $2t/(e^t + 1) = \sum_{k \ge 0} G_k t^k / k!$,
satisfies

$$
\frac{G_n}{n} = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (-1)^j \frac{j!}{2^j} S(n-1, j),
$$

where $S(n-1, j)$ are the Stirling numbers of the second kind.

## Sources

- Fatima Zohra Bensaci, Rachid Boumahdi, and Laala Khaldi, "Finite Sums Involving Fibonacci and Lucas Numbers", Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.7.2, Theorem 22.
