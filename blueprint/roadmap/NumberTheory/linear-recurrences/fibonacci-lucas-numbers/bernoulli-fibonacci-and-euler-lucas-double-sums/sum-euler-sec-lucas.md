# A double sum of Euler and Lucas numbers equal to two

Let $L_n$ be the Lucas numbers and
let $E_k$ be the Euler (secant) numbers, given by $2/(e^t + e^{-t}) = \sum_{k \ge 0} E_k t^k / k!$.
For every natural number $m$,

$$\sum_{n=0}^{m} \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\sqrt{5}^{\,k}}{2^{k+m-n}} \binom{n}{k} \binom{m}{n}
E_k L_{n-k} = 2.$$

## Sources

- Fatima Zohra Bensaci, Rachid Boumahdi, and Laala Khaldi, "Finite Sums Involving Fibonacci and Lucas Numbers", Corollary 7.
