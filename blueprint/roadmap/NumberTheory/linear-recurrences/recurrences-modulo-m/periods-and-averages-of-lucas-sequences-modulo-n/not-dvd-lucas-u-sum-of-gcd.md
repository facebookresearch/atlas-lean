# Non-integer period average of a Lucas sequence when $\gcd(n, a, b) > 1$

Theorem 2.13: for nondegenerate `U(a, b)` with `d = gcd(a, b) > 1` and
`gcd(n, d) > 1`, `A_U(n)` is not an integer.

## Sources

- Michal Křížek and Lawrence Somer, *Period Lengths Modulo n and Average of Terms of Second Order Linear Recurrences*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A36, Theorem 2.13 (`integers-2024-A36-thm-2-13`).
