# Irregular recurrences when the discriminant is a residue

Let $a, b$ be integers and $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ an integer sequence with $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$, and write $D = a^2 + 4b$. Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p \nmid b$, $p$ not dividing both $w_0$ and $w_1$, and $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right) = 1$. Then there are $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ with $\alpha + \beta = a$ and $\alpha\beta = -b$, and for any such $\alpha, \beta$: $\alpha\beta \ne 0$; $w$ is not $p$-regular if and only if $w_1 \equiv \alpha w_0$ or $w_1 \equiv \beta w_0 \pmod p$; and $w_1 \equiv \alpha w_0$ implies $w_n \equiv \alpha^n w_0 \pmod p$ for all $n$. Here $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$.
