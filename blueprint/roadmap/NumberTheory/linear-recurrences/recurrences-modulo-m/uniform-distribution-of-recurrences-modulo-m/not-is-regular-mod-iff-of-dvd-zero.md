# Irregular recurrences with first term divisible by p

Let $a, b$ be integers and $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ an integer sequence with $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$, and write $D = a^2 + 4b$. Let $p$ be a prime with $p \nmid b$ and $p \mid w_0$. Then $w$ is not $p$-regular if and only if $p \mid w_1$; here $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$.

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Lemma 2.2 (i).
