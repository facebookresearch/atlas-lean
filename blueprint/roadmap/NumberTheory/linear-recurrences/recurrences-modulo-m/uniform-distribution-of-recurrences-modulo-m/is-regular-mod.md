# Regular recurrences modulo p

For an integer sequence $w$ and a natural number $p$, $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$.

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Section 2.
