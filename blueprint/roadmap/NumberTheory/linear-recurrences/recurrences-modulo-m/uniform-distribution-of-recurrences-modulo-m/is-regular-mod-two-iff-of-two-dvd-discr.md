# 2-regularity of recurrences with even discriminant

Let $a, b$ be integers and $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ an integer sequence with $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$, and write $D = a^2 + 4b$. If $b$ is odd and $2 \mid D$, then $a$ is even, and $w$ is $2$-regular if and only if $w_0 \not\equiv w_1 \pmod 2$; here $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$ (with $p = 2$).

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Theorem 2.3 (v).
