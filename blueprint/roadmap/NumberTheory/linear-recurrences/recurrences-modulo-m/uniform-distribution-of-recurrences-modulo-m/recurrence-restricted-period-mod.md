# Restricted period of a sequence modulo m

For an integer sequence $w$ and a natural number $m$, $h_w(m)$ is the least $f > 0$ such that $w_{n+f} \equiv M w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ for some integer $M$ coprime to $m$ ($0$ if none).

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Section 1.
