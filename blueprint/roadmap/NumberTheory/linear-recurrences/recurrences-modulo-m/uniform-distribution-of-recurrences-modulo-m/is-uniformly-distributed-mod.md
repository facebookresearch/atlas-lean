# Uniform distribution of a sequence modulo m

For an integer sequence $w$ and a natural number $m$, $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $m$ when $\lambda_w(m) > 0$ and, for every integer $x$, $m$ times the number of $n < \lambda_w(m)$ with $w_n \equiv x \pmod m$ equals $\lambda_w(m)$. For $m \ge 1$ this says that every residue modulo $m$ occurs exactly $\lambda_w(m)/m$ times among $w_0, \dots, w_{\lambda_w(m)-1}$; for $m = 0$ the condition is false, as the left side is $0$. Here $\lambda_w(m)$ is the least $\ell > 0$ with $w_{n+\ell} \equiv w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ ($0$ if none).
