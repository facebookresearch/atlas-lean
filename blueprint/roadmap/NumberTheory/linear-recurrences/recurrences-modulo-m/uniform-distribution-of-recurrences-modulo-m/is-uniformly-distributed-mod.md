# Uniform distribution of a sequence modulo m

For an integer sequence $w$ and a natural number $m$, $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $m$ when $\lambda_w(m) > 0$ and every residue modulo $m$ occurs exactly $\lambda_w(m)/m$ times among $w_0, \dots, w_{\lambda_w(m)-1}$, where $\lambda_w(m)$ is the least $\ell > 0$ with $w_{n+\ell} \equiv w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ ($0$ if none).

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Section 1.
