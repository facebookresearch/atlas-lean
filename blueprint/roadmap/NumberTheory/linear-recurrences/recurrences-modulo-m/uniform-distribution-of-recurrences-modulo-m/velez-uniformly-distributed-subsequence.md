# Vélez's theorem on uniform distribution of recurrences

Let $w$ satisfy $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$ for integers $a, b$, let $p$ be a prime with $p \nmid b$ and $e \ge 1$, and suppose $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $p^e$. Then for every $s \ge 0$ every residue modulo $p^e$ occurs exactly once among $w_{s+nE}$, $0 \le n < p^e$. Moreover, if $p \ge 3$ then $p \nmid a$ and $E$ is the multiplicative order of $a/2$ modulo $p$, and if $p = 2$ then $E = 1$. Here, for $m \ge 1$, $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $m$ when $\lambda_w(m) > 0$ and every residue modulo $m$ occurs exactly $\lambda_w(m)/m$ times among $w_0, \dots, w_{\lambda_w(m)-1}$, $\lambda_w(m)$ is the least $\ell > 0$ with $w_{n+\ell} \equiv w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ ($0$ if none), and $E = \lambda_w(p^e)/p^e$.
