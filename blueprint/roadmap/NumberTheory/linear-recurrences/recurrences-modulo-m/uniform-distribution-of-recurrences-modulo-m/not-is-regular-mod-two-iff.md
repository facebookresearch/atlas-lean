# Irregular recurrences modulo two

Let $a, b$ be integers and $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ an integer sequence with $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$, and write $D = a^2 + 4b$. Let $b$ be odd and suppose $w_0, w_1$ are not both even. Then $w$ is not $2$-regular if and only if $a$ is even and $w_0, w_1$ are odd; in that case $2 \mid D$ and every $w_n$ is odd. Here $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$ (with $p = 2$).
