# Period length of a constant sequence

For every integer $c$ and natural number $m$, the constant sequence $w_n = c$ has $\lambda_w(m) = 1$, where $\lambda_w(m)$ is the least $\ell > 0$ with $w_{n+\ell} \equiv w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ ($0$ if none).

## Sources

- L. Somer and M. Křížek, Generalization of a theorem of Vélez on uniform distribution in second-order linear recurrences, Integers 25 (2025), Article A1, Section 1.
