# Uniform distribution of recurrences modulo powers of three

Let $a, b$ be integers and $(w_n)_{n \ge 0}$ an integer sequence with $w_{n+2} = aw_{n+1} + bw_n$, and write $D = a^2 + 4b$. Let $3 \nmid b$, $e \ge 2$ and $E = \lambda_w(3)/3$. Then $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $3^e$ if and only if $3 \mid D$, $D \not\equiv 6 \pmod 9$ and $w$ is $3$-regular; in that case $3 \nmid a$, $h_w(3^e) = 3^e$, $E$ is the multiplicative order of $a/2$ modulo $3$, $\lambda_w(3^e) = 3^e E$ and $E \mid 2$. Here, for $m \ge 1$, $w$ is uniformly distributed modulo $m$ when $\lambda_w(m) > 0$ and every residue modulo $m$ occurs exactly $\lambda_w(m)/m$ times among $w_0, \dots, w_{\lambda_w(m)-1}$, $w$ is $p$-regular when $w_0 w_2 - w_1^2 \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$ (with $p = 3$), $\lambda_w(m)$ is the least $\ell > 0$ with $w_{n+\ell} \equiv w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ ($0$ if none), and $h_w(m)$ is the least $f > 0$ such that $w_{n+f} \equiv M w_n \pmod m$ for all $n$ for some integer $M$ coprime to $m$ ($0$ if none).
