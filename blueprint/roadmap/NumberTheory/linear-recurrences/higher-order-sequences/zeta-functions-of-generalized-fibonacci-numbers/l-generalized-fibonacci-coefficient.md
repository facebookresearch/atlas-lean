# Dresden–Du coefficient

For a natural number $\ell$ and a complex number $\beta$, the
Dresden–Du coefficient is the complex number
$c(\beta) = \frac{\beta - 1}{2 + (\ell + 1)(\beta - 2)}$, taken to be $0$ when the denominator
vanishes.
