# Perrin sequence

The Perrin sequence $E_n$, for natural numbers $n$, is defined by
$E_0 = 3$, $E_1 = 0$, $E_2 = 2$ and $E_n = E_{n-2} + E_{n-3}$ for every $n \ge 3$.
