# Shifted Padovan sequence

The shifted Padovan sequence is the sequence of natural numbers
$(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ with $a_0 = 1$, $a_1 = 0$, $a_2 = 1$ and $a_{n+3} = a_{n+1} + a_n$ for every
$n \ge 0$.

## Sources

- Daniel Birmajer, Juan B. Gil, and Michael D. Weiner, *Linear Recurrence Sequences and Their Convolutions via Bell Polynomials*, Journal of Integer Sequences 18 (2015), Article 15.1.2, Example 17, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL18/Gil/gil3.html>, where this shifted version is used.
