# Bounds on the dominant root of the k-generalized Fibonacci polynomial

Let $k$ be a natural
number and let $\alpha > 1$ be a real number with $\alpha^k = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} \alpha^i$. Then
$2 - 1/k < \alpha < 2$, and if $k \ge 4$, moreover $2 - 1/(3k) < \alpha < 2$.
