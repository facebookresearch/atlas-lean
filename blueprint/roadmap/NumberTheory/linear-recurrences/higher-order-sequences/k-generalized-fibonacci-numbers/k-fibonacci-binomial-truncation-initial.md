# Initial values of the truncated binomial sums

Let $k \ge 2$ be a natural number and let
$G_n^{(k)}$ be the truncated binomial sum. Then $G_n^{(k)} = 2^{n-2}$ for every natural number $n$
with $2 \le n \le k + 1$, and $G_{k+2}^{(k)} = 2^k - 1$.

## Sources
