# Recurrence for the N-step Fibonacci numbers

For all natural numbers $N$ and $n$, the
$N$-step Fibonacci numbers $F^{(N)}$ satisfy
$F^{(N)}_{n+2} = \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} F^{(N)}_{\max(n+1-i,\,0)}$.
