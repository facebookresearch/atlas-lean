# Fibonacci p-numbers

For a natural number $p$, the Fibonacci $p$-numbers are the
natural numbers $F_p(r)$, $r \in \mathbb{N}$, given by $F_p(0) = 0$, $F_p(r) = 1$ for
$1 \le r \le p$, and $F_p(r) = F_p(r-1) + F_p(r-p-1)$ for $r > p$.
