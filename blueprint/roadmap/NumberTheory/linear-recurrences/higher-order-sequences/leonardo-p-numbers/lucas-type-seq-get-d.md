# Entries of a Lucas-type list do not depend on its length

Let $\alpha$ be a set with
elements $0$ and $1$ and a binary operation $+$, let $p$ be a natural number and let $a_0, c \in \alpha$. For natural
numbers $i \le n$, the entry at index $i$ of the Lucas-type list with parameters $p$, $a_0$, $c$
ending at index $n$ equals the entry at index $i$ of the Lucas-type list ending at index $i$.

## Sources

- Amine Belkhir and Elif Tan, *Combinatorial Insights into Leonardo $p$-Numbers and Lucas-Leonardo $p$-Numbers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A57, Section 1.
