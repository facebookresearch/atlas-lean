# Relation between the sequences $A_p$ and $B_p$

For natural numbers $p \ge 2$ and $n$,
the auxiliary sequences $A_p$ and $B_p$ satisfy $A_p(n) = B_p(n+1) - 1$ if $p$ divides $n$, and
$A_p(n) = B_p(n+1)$ otherwise.
