# Hyper Leonardo $p$-numbers

For natural numbers $p$, $n$ and $k$, the hyper Leonardo
$p$-number is the integer

$$\mathcal{L}^{(k)}_{p,n} = (p + 1) \sum_{i=0}^{\lfloor n/(p+1) \rfloor}
\binom{n + k - pi}{i + k} - p \binom{n + k}{k}.$$
