# The sequence $A_p$ as a sum of Fibonacci $p$-numbers

For natural numbers $p$, $j$ and $m$
with $j < p$, the auxiliary sequence $A_p$ and the Fibonacci $p$-numbers $F_p$ satisfy
$A_p(mp + j) = \sum_{i=0}^{m} F_p(ip + j)$.
