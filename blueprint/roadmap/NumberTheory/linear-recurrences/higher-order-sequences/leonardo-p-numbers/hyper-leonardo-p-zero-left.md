# Hyper Leonardo $p$-numbers at $n = 0$

For all natural numbers $p$ and $k$, the hyper
Leonardo $p$-number $\mathcal{L}^{(k)}_{p,0}$ equals $1$.

## Sources

- Amine Belkhir and Elif Tan, *Combinatorial Insights into Leonardo $p$-Numbers and Lucas-Leonardo $p$-Numbers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A57, display (12) and Proposition 10.
