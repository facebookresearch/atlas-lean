# Recurrence for the (a, b)-Fibonacci numbers

For all complex numbers $a$ and $b$ and every
natural number $n$, the $(a,b)$-Fibonacci numbers satisfy
$F^{a,b}_{n+2} = a F^{a,b}_{n+1} + b F^{a,b}_n$.
