# (a, b)-Lucas numbers

For complex numbers $a$ and $b$, the $(a, b)$-Lucas numbers are the
sequence $(L_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of complex numbers defined by $L_0 = 2$, $L_1 = a$ and
$L_{n+2} = a L_{n+1} + b L_n$ for all $n \ge 0$.
