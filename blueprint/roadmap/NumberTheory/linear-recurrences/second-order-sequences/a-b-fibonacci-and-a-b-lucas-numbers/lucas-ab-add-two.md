# Recurrence for the (a, b)-Lucas numbers

For all complex numbers $a$ and $b$ and every
natural number $n$, the $(a,b)$-Lucas numbers satisfy
$L^{a,b}_{n+2} = a L^{a,b}_{n+1} + b L^{a,b}_n$.
