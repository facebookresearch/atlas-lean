# Generalized Leonardo numbers

For an integer $k$, the generalized Leonardo numbers
$L_{k,0}, L_{k,1}, L_{k,2}, \ldots$ are the integers defined by $L_{k,0} = L_{k,1} = 1$ and
$L_{k,n+2} = L_{k,n+1} + L_{k,n} + k$ for all $n \ge 0$.

The paper takes $k$ positive; the definition above allows every integer $k$.

## Sources

- Kantaphon Kuhapatanakul, *Note on the Generalized Leonardo Numbers*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), no. 3, 201–207, introduction.
