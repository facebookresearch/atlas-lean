# Powers of two times Lucas numbers modulo 5

Let $L_m$ denote the Lucas numbers. For every
natural number $n$, $2^n L_{n+1} \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$.

## Sources

- Michael R. Bacon and Charles K. Cook, *Some Properties of Oresme Numbers and Convolutions with Various Second Order Sequences*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), no. 3, Article cookbacon061124, Corollary 4.4 (`fq-2024-cookbacon061124-cor-4.4`).
