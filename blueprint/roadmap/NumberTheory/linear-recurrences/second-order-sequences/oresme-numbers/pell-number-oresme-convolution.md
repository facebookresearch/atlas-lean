# Pell–Oresme convolution

Let $P_m$ denote the Pell numbers and let $O_m = m/2^m$ denote
the Oresme numbers. For every natural number $n$,
$\sum_{k=0}^{n} P_k O_{n-k} = \frac{2}{49}(5 P_{n+1} - 4 P_n) - \frac{2(7n + 5)}{49 \cdot 2^n}$.

## Sources

- Michael R. Bacon and Charles K. Cook, *Some Properties of Oresme Numbers and Convolutions with Various Second Order Sequences*, The Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), no. 3, Article cookbacon061124, Proposition 5.2 (`fq-2024-cookbacon061124-prop-5.2`).
