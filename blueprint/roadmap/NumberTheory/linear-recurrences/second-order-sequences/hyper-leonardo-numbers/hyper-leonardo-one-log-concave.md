# Eventual log-concavity of the hyper-Leonardo numbers of order one

For every natural
number $n \ge 2$, the hyper-Leonardo numbers $Le^{(1)}_n$ of order $1$ satisfy
$Le^{(1)}_{n-1} Le^{(1)}_{n+1} \le (Le^{(1)}_n)^2$.
