# Recurrence across consecutive orders of hyper-Leonardo numbers

For all natural numbers
$r \ge 1$ and $n \ge 1$, the hyper-Leonardo numbers $Le^{(r)}_n$ satisfy
$Le^{(r)}_n = Le^{(r)}_{n-1} + Le^{(r-1)}_n$.
