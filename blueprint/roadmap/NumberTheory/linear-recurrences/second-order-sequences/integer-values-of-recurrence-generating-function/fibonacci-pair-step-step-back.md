# The forward step undoes the backward step

For every pair $v$ of integers, applying the backward step and then the forward step returns $v$; here the forward step is $(H_n, H_{n+1}) \mapsto (H_{n+1}, H_n + H_{n+1})$ and the backward step is $(H_n, H_{n+1}) \mapsto (H_{n+1} - H_n, H_n)$.
