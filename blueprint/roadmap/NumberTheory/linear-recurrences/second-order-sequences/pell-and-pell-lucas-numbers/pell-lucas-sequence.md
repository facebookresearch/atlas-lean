# Pell–Lucas sequence

The Pell–Lucas (associated Pell) numbers $Q_n$, for natural numbers
$n$, are defined by $Q_0 = 1$, $Q_1 = 1$ and $Q_{n+2} = 2Q_{n+1} + Q_n$ for every $n$. They begin
$1, 1, 3, 7, 17, \dots$ (OEIS A001333).

## Sources
