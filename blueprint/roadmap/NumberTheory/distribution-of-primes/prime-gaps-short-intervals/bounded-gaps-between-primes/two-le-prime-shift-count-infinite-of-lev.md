# GPY: level $0.971$ gives two primes in every admissible 6-tuple

If the primes have level of distribution $\vartheta \ge 0.971$, then every admissible set $H$ of at least $6$ non-negative integers has infinitely many $n$ with at least two of the $n + h$, $h \in H$, prime (Goldston, Pintz and Yıldırım, Theorem 1, second sentence). The level hypothesis is stated as on [the general GPY page](exists-card-two-le-prime-shift-count-of.md).

Applied to the admissible $6$-tuple $\{0, 4, 6, 10, 12, 16\}$, this gives $\liminf_n (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 16$ (GPY (1.7)). That consequence, under the full Elliott–Halberstam conjecture, is recorded as [the GPY bound under Elliott–Halberstam](gpy-elliott-halberstam-upper-bound.md); the statement here needs only level $0.971$ and is the stronger one.
