# OpenAI's bound 186 on small prime gaps

Let $p_0 < p_1 < p_2 < \cdots$ be the primes in
increasing order. Then $\liminf_{n \to \infty} (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 186$, where the liminf is
taken in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$.
