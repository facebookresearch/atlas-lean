# Maynard's bound under Elliott–Halberstam

Let $p_0 < p_1 < p_2 < \cdots$ be the primes in
increasing order. If the Elliott–Halberstam conjecture holds at every level $\theta$ with
$0 < \theta < 1$, in Polymath8b's dyadic form, then $\liminf_{n \to \infty} (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 12$,
where the liminf is taken in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$.

This is the first inequality of Theorem 1.4 of Maynard's paper (arXiv:1311.4600v3 numbering), proved from [$M_5 > 2$](../../sieve-methods/gpy-maynard-sieve/two-lt-maynard-m-five.md) with the admissible $5$-tuple $\{0, 2, 6, 8, 12\}$. The second inequality is [three primes in intervals of length 600](elliott-halberstam-frequently-nth-prime.md).
