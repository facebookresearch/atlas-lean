# Maynard's bound 600 on small prime gaps

Let $p_0 < p_1 < p_2 < \cdots$ be the primes in
increasing order. Then $\liminf_{n \to \infty} (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 600$, where the liminf is
taken in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$.

The constant 600 is Maynard's. Tao independently and at the same time developed the same multidimensional sieve, obtaining the finiteness of $\liminf_n (p_{n+m} - p_n)$ for every $m$ with the weaker bound $\ll e^{Cm}$ (Maynard (2015), remark after Theorem 1.1; Tao, blog post of 19 November 2013; Polymath8b v4, Section 1).

The bound is Theorem 1.3 of Maynard's paper (arXiv:1311.4600v3 numbering), proved with $M_{105} > 4$ and an admissible $105$-tuple of diameter $600$; see [$M_{105} > 4$](../../sieve-methods/gpy-maynard-sieve/four-lt-maynard-m-105.md).
