# Goldston–Pintz–Yıldırım bound under Elliott–Halberstam

Let $p_0 < p_1 < p_2 < \cdots$
be the primes in increasing order. If the Elliott–Halberstam conjecture holds at every level
$\theta$ with $0 < \theta < 1$, in Polymath8b's dyadic form, then
$\liminf_{n \to \infty} (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 16$, where the liminf is taken in
$\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$.

In GPY's paper (arXiv:math/0508185v1 numbering) this is display (1.7), deduced from Theorem 1 with the admissible $6$-tuple $\{0, 4, 6, 10, 12, 16\}$. Theorem 1 needs only level of distribution $0.971$, so the source proves more than this statement; see [GPY: level $0.971$ gives two primes in every admissible 6-tuple](two-le-prime-shift-count-infinite-of-lev.md).
