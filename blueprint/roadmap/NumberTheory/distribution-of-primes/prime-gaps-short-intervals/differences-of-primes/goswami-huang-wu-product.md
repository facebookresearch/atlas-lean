# Multiples as products of Kronecker and Maillet numbers

There are a natural number $k > 0$ and a set $D$ of at most $720$ Maillet numbers such that for every $n > 0$, $kn = d k'$ for some $d \in D$ and some Kronecker number $k'$. Here an even natural number $n$ is a Maillet number when $n = p - q$ for some primes $p$ and $q$, and an even natural number $n$ is a Kronecker number when $n = p - q$ for infinitely many pairs of primes $(p, q)$.

## Sources

- S. Goswami, W. Huang and X. Wu, On the set of Kronecker numbers, Bull. Aust. Math. Soc. 110 (2024), 262–270, arXiv:2303.05767, Theorem 1.4.
