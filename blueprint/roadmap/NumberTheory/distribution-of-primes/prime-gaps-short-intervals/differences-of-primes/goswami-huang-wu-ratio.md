# Positive rationals as ratios of Kronecker numbers

Every positive rational number $q$ equals $k_1/k_2$ for some Kronecker numbers $k_1$ and $k_2 > 0$, where an even natural number $n$ is a Kronecker number when $n = p - q$ for infinitely many pairs of primes $(p, q)$.

## Sources

- S. Goswami, W. Huang and X. Wu, On the set of Kronecker numbers, Bull. Aust. Math. Soc. 110 (2024), 262–270, arXiv:2303.05767, Theorem 1.5.
