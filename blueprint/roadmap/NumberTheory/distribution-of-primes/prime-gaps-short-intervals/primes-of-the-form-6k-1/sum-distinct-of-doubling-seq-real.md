# Sums of distinct elements from a doubling sequence, real direction

Let $H$ be a set of pairs of natural numbers, where elements $a + b\omega$ of $E = \{a + b\omega : a, b \in \mathbb{N}\}$, with $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$, are written as pairs $(a, b)$. Suppose $H$ contains $(x_n, 0)$ for distinct positive integers $x_0, x_1, \dots$ with $x_{n+1} \le 2x_n$. Let $a, b$ be natural numbers such that for every $m$ with $a < m \le a + x_0$, the pair $(m, b)$ is the sum of a finite set of elements of $H$ none of which is one of the $(x_n, 0)$. Then for every $m > a$, $(m, b)$ is the sum of a finite set of distinct elements of $H$.

## Sources
