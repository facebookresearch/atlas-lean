# Small selected Eisenstein primes in the additive set E

$2$, $5\omega$ and $2 + \omega$ are selected primes of $E$, while $3$ and $1 + \omega$ are not; here $E = \{a + b\omega : a, b \in \mathbb{N}\}$ with $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$ is an additive set of Eisenstein integers, and a pair $(a, b)$ of natural numbers, standing for $a + b\omega$, is a selected prime of $E$ when $b = 0$ and $a$ is a prime $\equiv 2 \pmod 3$, or $a = 0$ and $b$ is a prime $\equiv 2 \pmod 3$, or $a, b > 0$ and $a^2 - ab + b^2$ is prime.
