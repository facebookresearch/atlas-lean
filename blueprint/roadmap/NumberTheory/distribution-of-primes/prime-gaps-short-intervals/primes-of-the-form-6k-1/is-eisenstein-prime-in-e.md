# Primes of the Eisenstein semiring E

A pair $(a, b)$ of natural numbers, standing for $a + b\omega$ with $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$, is a prime of $E$ when $b = 0$ and $a$ is a prime $\equiv 2 \pmod 3$, or $a = 0$ and $b$ is a prime $\equiv 2 \pmod 3$, or $a, b > 0$ and $a^2 - ab + b^2$ is prime.

## Sources

- E. Hasanalizade, Sums of distinct Eisenstein primes, Integers 24 (2024), Article A57, Section 2.
