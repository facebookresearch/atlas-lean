# Sums of distinct elements from a doubling sequence, omega direction

Let $H$ be a set of pairs of natural numbers, where elements $a + b\omega$ of $E = \{a + b\omega : a, b \in \mathbb{N}\}$, with $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$, are written as pairs $(a, b)$. Suppose $H$ contains $(0, y_n)$ for distinct positive integers $y_0, y_1, \dots$ with $y_{n+1} \le 2y_n$. Let $a, b$ be natural numbers such that for every $m$ with $b < m \le b + y_0$, the pair $(a, m)$ is the sum of a finite set of elements of $H$ none of which is one of the $(0, y_n)$. Then for every $m > b$, $(a, m)$ is the sum of a finite set of distinct elements of $H$.

## Sources

- E. Hasanalizade, Sums of distinct Eisenstein primes, Integers 24 (2024), Article A57, Corollary 1.
