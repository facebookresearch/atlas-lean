# Laishram's upper bound for Ramanujan primes

Let $p_k$ denote the $k$-th prime, so that
$p_1 = 2$. For all natural numbers $n$ and $R$, if $R$ is the $n$-th Ramanujan prime (which
entails $n \ge 1$), then $R < p_{3n}$.
