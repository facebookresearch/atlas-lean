# Erdős Problem 682 (rough numbers between consecutive primes)

The statement that the set of indices $n$ for which some integer $m$ with $p_n < m < p_{n+1}$ has least prime factor at least $p_{n+1} - p_n$ has natural density $1$, where $p_0 = 2 < p_1 = 3 < \cdots$ are the primes.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
