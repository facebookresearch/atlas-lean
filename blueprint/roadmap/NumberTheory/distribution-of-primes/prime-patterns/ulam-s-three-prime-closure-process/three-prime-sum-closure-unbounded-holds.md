# Ulam's three-prime process can grow without bound

There is a finite set $Q_0$ of primes such that the sets $Q_{i+1}$, obtained from $Q_i$ by adding every prime that is a sum of three distinct elements of $Q_i$, have unbounded size.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 471 (Ulam's three-prime closure process)](three-prime-sum-closure-unbounded.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #471, is an observation of R. Mrazović and V. Kovač, and independently N. Alon, from Vinogradov's three-primes theorem.
