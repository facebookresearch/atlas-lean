# Erdős Problem 471 (Ulam's three-prime closure process)

The statement that there is a finite set $Q_0$ of primes such that the sets $Q_{i+1}$, obtained from $Q_i$ by adding every prime that is a sum of three distinct elements of $Q_i$, have unbounded size.
