# Every finite colouring has monochromatic progressions of primes

For every $k \ge 3$ and every colouring of the integers with finitely many colours there is a monochromatic set of $k$ primes in arithmetic progression.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 1187 (monochromatic progressions of primes)](monochromatic-prime-progressions.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Green and Tao (2008), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #1187, https://www.erdosproblems.com/1187.
- B. Green and T. Tao, The primes contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions, Ann. of Math. (2) 167 (2008), 481–547, Theorem 1.2.
