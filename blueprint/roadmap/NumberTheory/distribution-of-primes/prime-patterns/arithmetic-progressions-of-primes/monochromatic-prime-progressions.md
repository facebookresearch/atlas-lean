# Erdős Problem 1187 (monochromatic progressions of primes)

The statement that for every $k \ge 3$ and every colouring of the integers with finitely many colours there is a monochromatic set of $k$ primes in arithmetic progression.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Every finite colouring has monochromatic progressions of primes](monochromatic-prime-progressions-holds.md).
