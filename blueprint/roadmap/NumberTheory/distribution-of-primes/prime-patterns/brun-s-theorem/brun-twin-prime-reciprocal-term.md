# Twin-prime reciprocal term

For a natural number $p$, the twin-prime reciprocal term
$t(p)$ is the real number $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{p + 2}$ when $p$ and $p + 2$ are both prime,
and $0$ otherwise.
