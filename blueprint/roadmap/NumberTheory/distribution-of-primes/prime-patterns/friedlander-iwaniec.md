# Friedlander–Iwaniec theorem

There are infinitely many primes $p$ of the form
$p = a^2 + b^4$ with $a$ and $b$ natural numbers.

## Sources

- Friedlander, John, and Henryk Iwaniec. “The Polynomial X² + Y⁴ Captures Its Primes.” Annals of Mathematics, vol. 148, no. 3, 1998, pp. 945–1040. JSTOR, https://doi.org/10.2307/121034. Via leanprover/lean-eval@38361be, problem `friedlander_iwaniec`.
