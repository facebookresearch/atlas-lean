# A permutation of the positive integers with prime adjacent sums

There is a permutation $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of the positive integers (a bijection from $\mathbb{N}$ onto the positive integers) such that $a_k + a_{k+1}$ is prime for every $k$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 473 (permutations of the positive integers with prime adjacent sums)](prime-adjacent-sum-permutation.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #473, is due to A. M. Odlyzko.
