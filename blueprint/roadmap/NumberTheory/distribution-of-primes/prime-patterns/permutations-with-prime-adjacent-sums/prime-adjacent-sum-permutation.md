# Erdős Problem 473 (permutations of the positive integers with prime adjacent sums)

The statement that there is a permutation $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of the positive integers (a bijection from $\mathbb{N}$ onto the positive integers) such that $a_k + a_{k+1}$ is prime for every $k$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [A permutation of the positive integers with prime adjacent sums](prime-adjacent-sum-permutation-holds.md).
