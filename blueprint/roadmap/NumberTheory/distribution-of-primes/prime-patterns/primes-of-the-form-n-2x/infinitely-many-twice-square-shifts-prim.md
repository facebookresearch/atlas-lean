# Erdős Problem 1140 (integers n with n − 2x² prime for all small x)

The statement that there are infinitely many natural numbers $n \ge 1$ such that $n - 2x^2$ is prime for every integer $x \ge 0$ with $2x^2 < n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Finitely many n have n − 2x² prime for all small x](infinitely-many-twice-square-shifts-prim-2.md).
