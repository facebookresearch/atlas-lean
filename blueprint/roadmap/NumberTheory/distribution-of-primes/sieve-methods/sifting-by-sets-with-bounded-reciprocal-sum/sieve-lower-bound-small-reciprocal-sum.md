# Erdős Problem 784 for reciprocal sums at most one

The statement that the sieve bound holds at every level $0 < C \le 1$, where for a real $C$, the sieve bound at level $C$ holds when there are $c > 0$ and $\kappa > 0$ such that for all sufficiently large $x$ and every $A \subseteq \{2, \dots, x\}$ with $\sum_{a \in A} 1/a \le C$, at least $\kappa x/(\log x)^c$ integers $m \in \{1, \dots, x\}$ are divisible by no element of $A$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sifting by a set with reciprocal sum at most one leaves many integers](sieve-lower-bound-small-reciprocal-sum-h.md).
