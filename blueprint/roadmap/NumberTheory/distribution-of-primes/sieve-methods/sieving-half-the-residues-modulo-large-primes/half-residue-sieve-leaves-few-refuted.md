# Sieving half the residues modulo large primes can leave many survivors

It is not the case that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $k$ such that for every $n$, all primes $p_1 < \dots < p_k < n^{1-\varepsilon}$ and all sets $A_i$ of $\lfloor (p_i - 1)/2 \rfloor$ residue classes modulo $p_i$ (none for $p_i = 2$), at most $\varepsilon n$ integers $m \in \{1, \dots, n\}$ avoid $A_i$ modulo $p_i$ for every $i$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 1202 (sieving half the residues modulo large primes)](half-residue-sieve-leaves-few.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is the one recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #1202.
