# Maynard's simplex

For $k \in \mathbb N$,

$$\mathcal R_k = \Bigl\{(x_1, \dots, x_k) \in [0, \infty)^k : \sum_{i=1}^{k} x_i \le 1\Bigr\},$$

the set on which Maynard's profiles $F$ are supported (Maynard (2015), Proposition 4.1). The constraint $\sum_i x_i \le 1$ reflects the support condition $\prod_i d_i < R$ on the sieve weights $\lambda_{d_1, \dots, d_k}$.

## Sources

- J. Maynard, "Small gaps between primes", *Ann. of Math.* 181 (2015), 383–413; theorem and display numbers are those of arXiv:1311.4600v3 (28 Oct 2019).
