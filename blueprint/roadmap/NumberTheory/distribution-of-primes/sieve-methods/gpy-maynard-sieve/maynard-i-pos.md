# $I_k$ of an admissible profile is positive

For an [admissible profile](is-maynard-profile.md) $F$, $I_k(F) > 0$: the integral of $F^2$ is non-negative, and it is non-zero by assumption.

## Sources

- J. Maynard, "Small gaps between primes", *Ann. of Math.* 181 (2015), 383–413; theorem and display numbers are those of arXiv:1311.4600v3 (28 Oct 2019).
