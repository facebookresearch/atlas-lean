# GPY detection principle over dyadic ranges

Let $w_N(n) \ge 0$ for all $N, n \in \mathbb N$, let $H$ be a finite set of shifts and $\rho \in \mathbb R$. If, for all sufficiently large $N$,

$$S(N, \rho) = \sum_{N \le n < 2N} \Bigl(\sum_{h \in H} \mathbf 1_{\mathbb P}(n + h) - \rho\Bigr) w_N(n) > 0,$$

then infinitely many $n$ have more than $\rho$ of the $n + h$ prime. This is the conclusion "if $S(N, \rho) > 0$ for all large $N$, there are infinitely many integers $n$ for which at least $\lfloor \rho + 1 \rfloor$ of the $n + h_i$ are prime" in Section 2 of Maynard (2015).
