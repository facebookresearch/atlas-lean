# Growth of $M_k$

For all sufficiently large $k$,

$$M_k > \log k - 2 \log \log k - 2$$

(Maynard (2015), Proposition 4.3(3)). In particular $M_k \to \infty$, which, with the Bombieri–Vinogradov level, gives any number of primes in infinitely many translates of a long enough admissible tuple; this is the source of [Maynard's explicit bound for gaps between $m + 1$ primes](../../prime-gaps-short-intervals/bounded-gaps-between-primes/frequently-nth-prime-add-sub-le-cube-mul.md).
