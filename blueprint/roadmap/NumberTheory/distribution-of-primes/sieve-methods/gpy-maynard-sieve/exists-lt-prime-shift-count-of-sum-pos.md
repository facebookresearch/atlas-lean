# GPY detection principle, finite form

Let $s$ be a finite set of natural numbers, $w : \mathbb N \to \mathbb R$ with $w_n \ge 0$ for $n \in s$, $H$ a finite set of shifts and $\rho \in \mathbb R$. If

$$\sum_{n \in s} \Bigl(\sum_{h \in H} \mathbf 1_{\mathbb P}(n + h) - \rho\Bigr) w_n > 0,$$

then some $n \in s$ has more than $\rho$ of the numbers $n + h$, $h \in H$, prime, that is at least $\lfloor \rho + 1 \rfloor$ of them.

This is the positivity argument stated after display (2.1) of Maynard (2015): a positive sum of terms $(\cdot)\, w_n$ with $w_n \ge 0$ has a term with a positive first factor.

## Sources

- J. Maynard, "Small gaps between primes", *Ann. of Math.* 181 (2015), 383–413; theorem and display numbers are those of arXiv:1311.4600v3 (28 Oct 2019).
