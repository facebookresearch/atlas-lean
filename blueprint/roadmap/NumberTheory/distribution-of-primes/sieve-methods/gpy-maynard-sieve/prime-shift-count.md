# Number of prime translates

For a finite set $H$ of natural numbers and $n \in \mathbb N$, the number of prime translates is

$$\#\{h \in H : n + h \text{ is prime}\} = \sum_{h \in H} \mathbf 1_{\mathbb P}(n + h),$$

the inner sum in the Goldston–Pintz–Yıldırım detecting sum $S(N, \rho)$ of Maynard (2015), display (2.1). Shifts are natural numbers, as in Maynard's and GPY's definition of a $k$-tuple $\{h_1, \dots, h_k\}$ of distinct non-negative integers.
