# Maynard's ratio

For $F : \mathbb R^k \to \mathbb R$, the ratio

$$\frac{\sum_{m=1}^{k} J_k^{(m)}(F)}{I_k(F)}$$

whose supremum over admissible profiles is $M_k$ (Maynard (2015), Proposition 4.2), built from [$I_k$](maynard-i.md) and [$J_k^{(m)}$](maynard-j.md).

## Sources

- J. Maynard, "Small gaps between primes", *Ann. of Math.* 181 (2015), 383–413; theorem and display numbers are those of arXiv:1311.4600v3 (28 Oct 2019).
