# $M_5 > 2$

[Maynard's variational quantity](maynard-m.md) for $k = 5$ satisfies $M_5 > 2$ (Maynard (2015), Proposition 4.3(1)). With the admissible $5$-tuple $\{0, 2, 6, 8, 12\}$ and the Elliott–Halberstam level $\theta = 1 - \varepsilon$, [the reduction](infinite-lt-prime-shift-count-of-lt-mayn.md) gives two primes in infinitely many translates, hence $\liminf_n (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 12$ under Elliott–Halberstam (Maynard (2015), proof of Theorem 1.4).
