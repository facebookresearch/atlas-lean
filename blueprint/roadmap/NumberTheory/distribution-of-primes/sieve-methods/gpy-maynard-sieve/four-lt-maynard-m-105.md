# $M_{105} > 4$

[Maynard's variational quantity](maynard-m.md) for $k = 105$ satisfies $M_{105} > 4$ (Maynard (2015), Proposition 4.3(2)). With the Bombieri–Vinogradov level $\theta = \tfrac12 - \varepsilon$ this gives $\theta M_{105}/2 > 1$, so every admissible $105$-tuple has infinitely many translates with two primes; with an admissible $105$-tuple of diameter $600$ this is $\liminf_n (p_{n+1} - p_n) \le 600$ (Theorem 1.3). Under Elliott–Halberstam, $\theta M_{105}/2 > 2$ and the same tuple gives $\liminf_n (p_{n+2} - p_n) \le 600$ (Theorem 1.4).
