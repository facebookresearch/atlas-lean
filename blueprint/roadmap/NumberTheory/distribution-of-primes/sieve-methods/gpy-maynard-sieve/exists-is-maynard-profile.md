# Admissible profiles exist

For every $k \in \mathbb N$, the indicator of the box $[0, \tfrac{1}{k+1}]^k$ is an [admissible profile](is-maynard-profile.md): it is bounded and measurable, it vanishes outside [Maynard's simplex](maynard-simplex.md) because $k \cdot \tfrac{1}{k+1} \le 1$, and $I_k$ and every $J_k^{(m)}$ are positive on it. Hence the set of ratios whose supremum is [$M_k$](maynard-m.md) is nonempty, including for $k = 0$, where the cube $[0,1]^0$ is a point.

Concretely $I_k = (k+1)^{-k}$ and $J_k^{(m)} = (k+1)^{-(k+1)}$, so this profile only shows $M_k \ge k/(k+1)$.
