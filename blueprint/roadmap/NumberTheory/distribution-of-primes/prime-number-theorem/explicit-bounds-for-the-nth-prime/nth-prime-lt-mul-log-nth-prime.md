# For $n \ge 7$, the $n$th prime is less than $n$ times its logarithm

For every integer
$n \ge 7$, the $n$th prime $p_n$ (counting from $p_1 = 2$) satisfies $p_n < n \log p_n$.

## Sources

- Qianxi Zhang and Xinhua Xiong, *New Refinements of Mandl's Inequality*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A58, Lemma 2, inequality (2) (`integers-2025-A58-lem-2`).
