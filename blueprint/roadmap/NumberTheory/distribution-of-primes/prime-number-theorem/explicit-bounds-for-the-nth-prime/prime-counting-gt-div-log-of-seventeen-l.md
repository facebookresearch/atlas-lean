# The Rosser–Schoenfeld lower bound $x / \log x$ for the prime-counting function

For every
real number $x \ge 17$, the number $\pi(x)$ of primes at most $x$ satisfies
$x / \log x < \pi(x)$.

## Sources

- Qianxi Zhang and Xinhua Xiong, *New Refinements of Mandl's Inequality*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A58, Lemma 2 (`integers-2025-A58-lem-2`).
