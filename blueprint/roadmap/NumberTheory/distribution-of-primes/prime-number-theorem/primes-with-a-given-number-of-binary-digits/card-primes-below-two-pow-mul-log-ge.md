# Density of primes below a power of two

For $n \ge 2$, the number $\pi'$ of primes less than $2^n$ satisfies $2^n\log 2 - \log(2^n + 1) \le \pi' \cdot n\log 2$; so a uniformly random integer below $2^n$ is prime with probability at least $1/n - \log(2^n + 1)/(n 2^n\log 2)$.

## Sources

- J. Katz and Y. Lindell, Introduction to Modern Cryptography, 2nd ed., Section 8.2.1.
