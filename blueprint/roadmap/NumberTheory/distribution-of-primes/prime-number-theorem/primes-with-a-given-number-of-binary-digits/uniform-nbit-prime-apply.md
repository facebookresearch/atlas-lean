# Probabilities of uniformly random n-bit primes

For $n \ge 2$ and a natural number $p$, the probability of $p$ under the uniform distribution on the $n$-bit primes (the primes $p$ with $2^{n-1} \le p < 2^n$) is $1/P_n$ if $p$ is an $n$-bit prime and $0$ otherwise, where $P_n$ is the number of $n$-bit primes.
