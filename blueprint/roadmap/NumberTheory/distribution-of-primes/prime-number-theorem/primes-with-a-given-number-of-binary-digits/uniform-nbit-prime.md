# Uniformly random n-bit primes

For a natural number $n \ge 2$, the uniform distribution on the $n$-bit primes, the primes $p$ with $2^{n-1} \le p < 2^n$; this is the output distribution of the prime generation algorithm conditioned on success.

## Sources

- J. Katz and Y. Lindell, Introduction to Modern Cryptography, 2nd ed., Section 8.2.1.
