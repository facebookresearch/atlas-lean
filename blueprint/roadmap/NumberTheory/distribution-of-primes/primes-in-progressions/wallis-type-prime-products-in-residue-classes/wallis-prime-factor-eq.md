# Factor identity for Wallis-type prime products

For every odd prime $p$,

$$\frac{p - \chi_4(p)}{p + \chi_4(p)} = \frac{(1 - \chi_4(p)/p)^2}{1 - 1/p^2},$$

where $\chi_4$ is the nontrivial Dirichlet character modulo $4$.
