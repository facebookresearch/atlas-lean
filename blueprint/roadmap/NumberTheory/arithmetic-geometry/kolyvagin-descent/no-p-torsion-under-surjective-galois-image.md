# No rational $p$-torsion under surjective mod-$p$ Galois image

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$, $p$ an odd prime such that the Galois group of the field
$\mathbb{Q}(E_p)$ generated by the points of order $p$ is isomorphic, through its action on $E_p \cong (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^2$,
to $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$, and $K$ a quadratic field. Then $E(K)$ has no point of order $p$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the observation that a nonzero $K$-rational point of $E_p$ is fixed by $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/K)$, whose image
  in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ has index at most $2$;
- the group-theoretic fact that no subgroup of index at most $2$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$, $p$ odd, fixes a
  nonzero vector of $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^2$, with its source.
