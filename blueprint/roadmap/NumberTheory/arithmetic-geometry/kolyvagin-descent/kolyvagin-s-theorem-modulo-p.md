# Kolyvagin's theorem modulo $p$

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ of conductor $N$ without complex multiplication, let $K$ be an imaginary
quadratic field of discriminant $D_K \ne -3, -4$ in which every prime factor of $N$ splits, and let $y_K \in E(K)$ be
[the Heegner point](../heegner-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). Let $p$ be an odd prime such that
$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(E_p)/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ and $y_K \notin pE(K)$. Then the
$p$-Selmer group $S^{(p)}(K, E)$ is cyclic, generated by the image $\delta(y_K)$ of $y_K$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the Heegner points $y_n$ of squarefree conductor $n$, built from primes $\ell$ with $\mathrm{Frob}(\ell) = \mathrm{Frob}(\infty)$
  in $\operatorname{Gal}(K(E_p)/\mathbb{Q})$, and their norm relations (Gross, §3);
- Kolyvagin's derivative classes $c(n) \in H^1(K, E_p)$ and $d(n)$, with their local behaviour (Gross, §4) and their
  eigenvalue under complex conjugation (Gross, §5);
- the bound on the Selmer group from local Tate duality at the primes $\lambda$ above the Kolyvagin primes, which exist
  by the Chebotarev density theorem (Gross, §§6–9).

- [Selmer groups and the torsion of the Shafarevich–Tate group of an abelian variety](../cassels-tate-pairing/selmer-groups-of-abelian-varieties.md), for $S^{(p)}(K, E)$ and $\delta$

## Proof depends on

- [Complex conjugation on the Heegner point](../complex-conjugation-on-the-heegner-point.md), for the case $n = 1$ of the eigenvalue of complex conjugation
- [Local Tate duality](../../algebraic-number-theory/galois-representations/galois-cohomology-of-local-fields/local-tate-duality.md), for the duality of $H^1(K_\lambda, E_p)$ with itself through the Weil pairing
- [Chebotarev density theorem](../../algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/chebotarev-density-theorem.md), for the Kolyvagin primes
- [No rational $p$-torsion under surjective mod-$p$ Galois image](no-p-torsion-under-surjective-galois-image.md), for $E(K)_p = 0$

The rest of its proof is that of the sources below, as the Expansion describes.

## Sources

- B. H. Gross, *Kolyvagin's work on modular elliptic curves*, in *L-functions and arithmetic* (Durham, 1989),
  London Math. Soc. Lecture Note Ser. 153, Cambridge Univ. Press, 1991, 235–256: Proposition 2.3, p. 238 ("Let $p$ be an odd prime such that the extension $\mathbb{Q}(E_p)$ has Galois group
  $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$, and assume that $p$ does not divide $y_K$ in $E(K)$. Then the group
  $\operatorname{Sel}(E/K)_p$ is cyclic, generated by $\delta y_K$."), under the standing assumption of §2 that $E$ has no
  complex multiplication, p. 237, with the outline of its proof, p. 238, and §§3–9.
