# An ideal of index $N$ in a quadratic field

Let $K$ be a quadratic field with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$ and let $N \ge 1$ be an integer such that every prime
$p \mid N$ splits in $K$, that is, $p\mathcal{O}_K = PQ$ for distinct prime ideals $P \ne Q$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$. Then there
is an ideal $\mathcal{N}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ with $\mathcal{O}_K/\mathcal{N} \cong \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ as rings.

The field may be real or imaginary. The statement contains
[an ideal of index $N$ under the Heegner hypothesis](ideal-of-index-n-under-the-heegner-hypothesis.md), which takes $K$
imaginary and records that the additive group $\mathcal{O}_K/\mathcal{N}$ is cyclic of order $N$.

## Sources

- B. H. Gross, *Kolyvagin's work on modular elliptic curves*, in *L-functions and arithmetic* (Durham, 1989),

  imaginary quadratic field in which the prime factors of $N$ split.
