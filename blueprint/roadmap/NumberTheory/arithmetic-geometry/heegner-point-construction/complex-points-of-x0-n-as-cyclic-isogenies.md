# Complex points of $X_0(N)$ as cyclic $N$-isogenies

Let $N \ge 1$ and let $X_0(N)$ be the [modular curve](../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/modular-curve-of-a-group-of-level-n.md)
$X_\Gamma$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ for $\Gamma = \Gamma_0(N)$, whose complex points outside the cusps are
$\mathfrak{H}/\Gamma_0(N)$. For $\tau$ in the upper half-plane $\mathfrak{H}$, let $A_\tau = \mathbb{C}/\langle 1, \tau\rangle$
and let $C_\tau \subset A_\tau$ be the cyclic subgroup of order $N$ generated by the image of $1/N$. Then
$\tau \mapsto (A_\tau, C_\tau)$ induces a bijection from $\mathfrak{H}/\Gamma_0(N)$ onto the set of isomorphism classes of
pairs $(A, C)$ of a complex elliptic curve $A$ and a cyclic subgroup $C \subset A$ of order $N$; equivalently, of
cyclic $N$-isogenies $A \to A/C$.

In particular the cyclic $N$-isogeny $\mathbb{C}/\mathcal{O}_K \to \mathbb{C}/\mathcal{N}^{-1}$ of the
[Heegner isogeny page](kernel-of-the-heegner-isogeny.md), with kernel $\mathcal{N}^{-1}/\mathcal{O}_K$, defines a point
$x_1 \in X_0(N)(\mathbb{C})$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the computation that $\gamma \in \Gamma_0(N)$ sends $(A_\tau, C_\tau)$ to an isomorphic pair, and that every pair is
  isomorphic to some $(A_\tau, C_\tau)$, with $\tau$ unique modulo $\Gamma_0(N)$ (Darmon: "it can be checked that this
  correspondence is well-defined and sets up a bijection");
- the identification of $\mathfrak{H}/\Gamma_0(N)$ with the noncuspidal complex points of the modular curve, from the
  modular curve page.
