# Colliot-Thélène's question on odd-degree points of Skorobogatov's surface

The statement that there are a number field $k$ of odd degree over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $x, y, z, t \in k$ with $y^2 = p(t)(x^2 + 1)$ and $z^2 = p(t)(x^2 + 2)$, where $p(t) = 3(t^4 - 54t^2 - 117t - 243)$: Skorobogatov's bielliptic surface has a point over a number field of odd degree.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Skorobogatov's bielliptic surface has a point of odd degree](skorobogatov-surface-odd-degree-point-ho.md).
