# At most $16$ rational torsion points on an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$. The torsion
subgroup of the group $E(\mathbb{Q})$ of rational points of $E$ is finite and has at most $16$
elements.

The bound follows from Mazur's list of the fifteen possible torsion subgroups, the largest of which,
$\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$, has $16$ elements.

## Sources

- Barry Mazur, *Modular curves and the Eisenstein ideal*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 47 (1977), 33–186, doi:10.1007/BF02684339, <https://www.numdam.org/item/?id=PMIHES_1977__47__33_0>.
