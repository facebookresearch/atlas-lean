# The Shafarevich–Tate group of an elliptic curve over a number field

Let $E$ be a
Weierstrass curve over a number field $K$ with algebraic closure $\bar K$, and let $E(\bar K)$ be
the group of nonsingular points of $E$ with coordinates in $\bar K$. This is the subgroup of the
continuous cohomology group $H^1(G_K, E(\bar K))$ of classes whose restriction to the absolute
Galois group $G_{K_v}$ of the completion $K_v$ is zero for every place $v$ of $K$, finite or
infinite.

Here $G_{K_v}$ acts on $\bar K$ through a $K$-embedding of $\bar K$ into an algebraic closure
$\bar K_v$ of $K_v$, and the restriction lies in $H^1(G_{K_v}, E(\bar K))$, with the coefficients
$E(\bar K)$ fixed. When $E$ is elliptic (equivalently, $\Delta(E) \ne 0$), this subgroup is the
Shafarevich–Tate group $\text{Ш}(E/K)$: the map from $H^1(G_{K_v}, E(\bar K))$ to
$H^1(G_{K_v}, E(\bar K_v))$ is injective at every place, so the subgroup is the same as the one
defined with the local groups $H^1(G_{K_v}, E(\bar K_v))$. At a finite place the map is an
isomorphism (Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, 2nd ed., Remark I.3.10 (a)(ii), p. 47, for the
abelian variety $E$ over the henselization of $K$ at $v$), at a real place it is injective by
Tarski's transfer principle for real closed fields, and at a complex place both groups are
zero.
