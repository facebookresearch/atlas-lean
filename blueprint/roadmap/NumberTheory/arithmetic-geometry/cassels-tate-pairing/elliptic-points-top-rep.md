# The Galois module of points of an elliptic curve

Let $E$ be a Weierstrass curve over a
field $K$ with algebraic closure $\bar K$. This is the group $E(\bar K)$ of nonsingular points
of $E$ with coordinates in $\bar K$ (the point at infinity and the nonsingular affine points, so
all points when $E$ is elliptic), with the discrete topology, as a topological
$\mathbb{Z}$-representation of the absolute Galois group $G_K = \mathrm{Gal}(\bar K/K)$, which
acts on the coordinates of points.
