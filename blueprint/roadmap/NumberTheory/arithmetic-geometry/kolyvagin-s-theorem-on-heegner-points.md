# Kolyvagin's theorem on Heegner points

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ of conductor $N$, let $K$ be an
imaginary quadratic field of discriminant $D_K \ne -3, -4$ in which every prime factor of $N$ splits, and let
$y_K \in E(K)$ be [the Heegner point](heegner-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) for a modular parametrization of $E$.
Assume that $y_K$ has infinite order in $E(K)$. Then

1. the Mordell–Weil group $E(K)$ has rank $1$, so that $y_K$ generates a subgroup of finite index;
2. the [Shafarevich–Tate group](cassels-tate-pairing/elliptic-tate-shafarevich.md) $\text{Ш}(E/K)$ is finite.

Gross also records Kolyvagin's bound on the order of $\text{Ш}(E/K)$ (his Theorem 1.3 (2)); this page does not state
it.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the Heegner points $y_n$ of squarefree conductor $n$ over the ring class fields $K_n$, their norm relations, and
  Kolyvagin's derivative classes $c(n) \in H^1(K, E_p)$ built from them, with their local behaviour and their
  eigenvalue under complex conjugation (Gross, §§3–5; for $n = 1$,
  [complex conjugation on the Heegner point](complex-conjugation-on-the-heegner-point.md));
- the partial result that Gross proves and Darmon proves as Theorem 10.2, for a single prime $p$: if $E$ has no complex
  multiplication (Gross's standing assumption from his §2 on), $p$ is odd,
  $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(E_p)/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ and $p$ does not divide
  $y_K$ in $E(K)$, then $\operatorname{Sel}(E/K)_p$ is cyclic, generated by the image of $y_K$, so that $E(K)$ has
  rank $1$ and $\text{Ш}(E/K)_p = 0$ (Gross, Propositions 2.1 and 2.3, through the Kummer sequence
  $0 \to E(K)/pE(K) \to \operatorname{Sel}(E/K)_p \to \text{Ш}(E/K)_p \to 0$, Gross (2.2); Darmon adds
  $p \nmid 6N$ and the triviality of $E(K_\lambda)/pE(K_\lambda)$ for $\lambda \mid N$). Its proof bounds the
  Selmer group with local Tate duality at the primes $\lambda$ of $K$ above Kolyvagin primes, chosen by the Chebotarev
  density theorem;
- what the partial result leaves to the full theorem. For $E$ without complex multiplication it applies to all but
  finitely many $p$, by Serre's theorem (Darmon, Remark 10.3 and Theorem 10.4), and one such $p$ already gives
  item 1 (Darmon, Remark 10.5). It gives $\text{Ш}(E/K)_p = 0$ only for those $p$; and the vanishing of the
  $p$-torsion does not bound the $p$-primary part, which may be divisible. Item 2 needs Kolyvagin's refinement at the
  remaining primes: at the primes dividing $y_K$, an argument with powers $p^n$, using that $p^n$ does not divide $y_K$
  for large $n$; at the primes where the Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}(E_p)$ is smaller, Serre's theorem that the image of
  $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/p^n\mathbb{Z})$ has bounded
  index; the bound on the $p$-primary part of $\text{Ш}(E/K)$ by Kolyvagin's techniques on ideal class groups (Gross,
  p. 237); and the prime $p = 2$, which the partial result excludes. For $E$ with complex multiplication, both items, item 1 included, rest on the full theorem (Kolyvagin,
  Theorem A), which this page cites for everything the partial result does not prove.

## Proof depends on

- [Complex conjugation on the Heegner point](complex-conjugation-on-the-heegner-point.md), for the eigenvalue of complex conjugation on $y_K$, the case $n = 1$ of the classes $c(n)$
- [Local Tate duality](../algebraic-number-theory/galois-representations/galois-cohomology-of-local-fields/local-tate-duality.md), for the perfect pairing between $H^1(K_\lambda, A)$ and $H^1(K_\lambda, A')$ for the finite module $A = E_p$ over the completions $K_\lambda$, whose dual $A' = \operatorname{Hom}(E_p, \mu)$ the Weil pairing identifies with $E_p$; Gross uses this duality to control the local components of Selmer classes (p. 238)
- [Chebotarev density theorem](../algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/chebotarev-density-theorem.md), for the infinitely many Kolyvagin primes $\ell$ with $\mathrm{Frob}(\ell) = \mathrm{Frob}(\infty)$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(K(E_p)/\mathbb{Q})$

The rest of its proof is that of the sources below, as the Expansion describes.
