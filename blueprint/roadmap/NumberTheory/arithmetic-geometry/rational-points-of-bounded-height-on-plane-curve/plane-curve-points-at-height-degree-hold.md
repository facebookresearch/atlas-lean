# Points of height at most d on plane curves of degree d

There are absolute real constants $c$ and $\kappa$ such that for every $d \ge 3$, every irreducible polynomial $P \in \mathbb{Q}[x, y]$ of total degree $d$ has at most $c\, d^2\, d^{2/d} (\log d)^\kappa$ zeros $(x, y) \in \mathbb{Q}^2$ whose coordinates both have height at most $d$; here a rational number has height at most $H$ when, in lowest terms $a/b$ with $b \ge 1$, both $|a| \le H$ and $b \le H$.

This resolves [Heath-Brown's question on points of height at most d on curves of degree d](plane-curve-points-at-height-degree.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Binyamini, Cluckers and Novikov (2024), cited under Sources.
