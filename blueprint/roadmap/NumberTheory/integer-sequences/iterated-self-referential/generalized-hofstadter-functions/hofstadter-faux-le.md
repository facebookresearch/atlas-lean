# The depth-bounded Hofstadter approximations are bounded by their argument

For all natural numbers
$k$, $p$ and $n$, the depth-bounded approximation $A_k(p, n)$ of the generalized Hofstadter
function satisfies $A_k(p, n) \le n$.

## Sources

- Pierre Letouzey, Shuo Li, and Wolfgang Steiner, *Pointwise Order of Generalized Hofstadter Functions G, H, and Beyond*, Journal of Integer Sequences 29 (2026), Article 26.3.3, Section 1.1 ($0 \le F_k(n) \le n$).
