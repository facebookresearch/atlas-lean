# Stern's diatomic sequence

The Stern sequence is the function
$s : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ defined by $s(0) = 0$, $s(1) = 1$, and, for every $n \ge 1$,
$s(2n) = s(n)$ and $s(2n + 1) = s(n) + s(n + 1)$.

## Sources

- Stern sequence ("Stern diatomic sequence", OEIS A002487); Jennifer Lansing, *Largest Values for the Stern Sequence*, Journal of Integer Sequences 17 (2014), Article 14.7.5, Section 1; Melissa Dennison, *On Properties of the General Bow Sequence*, Journal of Integer Sequences 22 (2019), Article 19.2.7, Section 1.
