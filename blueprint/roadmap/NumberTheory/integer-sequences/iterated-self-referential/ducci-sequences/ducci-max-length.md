# Length $L_m(n)$ of the basic Ducci sequence

For integers $m \ge 1$ and $n \ge 1$, let $D$ be the Ducci map on $\mathbb{Z}_m^n$, $D(x_1, \dots, x_n) = (x_1 + x_2, x_2 + x_3, \dots, x_n + x_1)$, and let $u = (0, \dots, 0, 1)$ be the basic tuple. The length $L_m(n)$ is the least integer $\alpha \ge 0$ such that $D^{\alpha + \beta}(u) = D^{\alpha}(u)$ for some $\beta \ge 1$: the number of steps before the basic Ducci sequence enters its cycle. Since $m \ge 1$, the set $\mathbb{Z}_m^n$ is finite and such an $\alpha$ exists.

## Sources

- Mark L. Lewis and Shannon M. Tefft, *The Maximum Length for Ducci Sequences on $\mathbb{Z}_m^n$ when $n$ is Even*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A17, Definition 1 and Section 1.
