# The basic Ducci tuple

For integers $m \ge 0$ and $n \ge 1$, the basic tuple in $\mathbb{Z}_m^n$, where $\mathbb{Z}_m = \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$, is $u = (0, \dots, 0, 1)$: its last entry is $1$ and all other entries are $0$. For $m = 1$ it is the zero tuple. Its orbit $u, D(u), D^2(u), \dots$ under the Ducci map $D$ is the basic Ducci sequence.

## Sources

- Mark L. Lewis and Shannon M. Tefft, *The Maximum Length for Ducci Sequences on $\mathbb{Z}_m^n$ when $n$ is Even*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A17, Section 1.
