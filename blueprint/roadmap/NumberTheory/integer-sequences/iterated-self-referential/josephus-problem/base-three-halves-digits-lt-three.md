# Base-3/2 digits are at most two

For every natural number $N$, every digit $d$ in the
base-$3/2$ digit list of $N$ satisfies $d < 3$, that is, $d \in \{0, 1, 2\}$.

## Sources

- Yunier Bello-Cruz and Roy Quintero-Contreras, *Fixed Points of the Josephus Function via Fractional Base Expansions*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A54, Section 2.2, Equation (8) (digits in $\{0, 1, 2\}$) and Steps 1 and 2 of the digit algorithm.
