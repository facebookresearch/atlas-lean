# The Josephus function has infinitely many fixed points

For every natural number
$k \ge 2$, the set of natural numbers $n$ with $J_k(n) = n$ is infinite, where $J_k$ is the
Josephus function.

## Sources

- Yunier Bello-Cruz and Roy Quintero-Contreras, *Analytical Study and Efficient Evaluation of the Josephus Function*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.8, Theorem 13.
