# A density-one sequence with distinct products of consecutive terms

There is a strictly increasing sequence $1 \le d_0 < d_1 < \cdots$ of natural numbers whose set of terms has natural density $1$ (the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $1$ as $b \to \infty$), such that the products $d_u d_{u+1} \cdots d_v$ ($u \le v$) of consecutive terms are pairwise distinct: equal products come from equal index intervals.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 421 (density-one sequences with distinct consecutive products)](density-one-distinct-consecutive-product.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #421, is an approach building on ideas of T. Tao, developed in the AI-assisted proofs of Chojecki and of Sneiderman, the latter improving quantitative aspects of the former; K. Pratt gives a digested exposition following both (cited under Sources).
