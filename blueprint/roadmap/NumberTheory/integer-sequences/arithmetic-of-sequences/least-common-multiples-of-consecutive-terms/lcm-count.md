# Counting consecutive terms with small least common multiple

For a sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of natural numbers and $x \in \mathbb{N}$, $A(x)$ is the number of indices $i$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a_i, a_{i+1}) \le x$, where the size of an infinite set is taken to be $0$. For a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers, the case the bounds on $A(x)$ consider, the set of such indices is finite. Otherwise it may be infinite: for the constant sequence $1$ and $x = 1$ every index qualifies, and $A(1) = 0$.
