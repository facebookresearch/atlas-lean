# Consecutive terms with small least common multiple are at most of order the square root

For every strictly increasing sequence $a_0 < a_1 < \cdots$ of positive integers there is a real $C$ with $A(x) \le C\sqrt{x}$ for every $x \in \mathbb{N}$, where $A(x)$ is the number of indices $i$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a_i, a_{i+1}) \le x$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 440, first question](lcm-count-sqrt-bound.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Erdős and Szemerédi (1980), cited under Sources.
