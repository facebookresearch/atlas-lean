# The value $a_{1,1}^{(3)} = 7$

For $m = 3$, $k = 1$ and $n = 1$, $a_{1,1}^{(3)} = 7$: the integer points $(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ with $\lvert x_i \rvert \le 1$ and $\lvert x_1 \rvert + \lvert x_2 \rvert + \lvert x_3 \rvert \le 1$ are the origin and the six vectors $\pm e_1, \pm e_2, \pm e_3$.
