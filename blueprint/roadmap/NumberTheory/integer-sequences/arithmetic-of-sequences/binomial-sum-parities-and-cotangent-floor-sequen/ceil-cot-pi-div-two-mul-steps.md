# The ceiling of $\cot(\pi/(2m))$ rises in steps of $0$ or $1$

For $m \ge 1$ let
$g(m) = \lceil \cot(\pi/(2m)) \rceil$. Then for every $m \ge 1$, $g(m) \le g(m+1)$ and
$g(m+1) - g(m) \in \{0, 1\}$; and for every integer $n \ge 1$, the number of $m \ge 1$ with
$g(m) = n$ is $1$ or $2$.
