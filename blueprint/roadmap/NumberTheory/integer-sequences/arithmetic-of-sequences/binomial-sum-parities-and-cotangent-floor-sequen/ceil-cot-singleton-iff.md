# Values taken once by the ceiling of $\cot(\pi/(2m))$

For $m \ge 1$ let
$g(m) = \lceil \cot(\pi/(2m)) \rceil$. Let $r \ge 1$ and $m \ge 2$ be integers. Then $m$ is the
only integer $m' \ge 1$ with $g(m') = r$ if and only if $g(m-1) = r - 1$, $g(m) = r$ and
$g(m+1) = r + 1$.
