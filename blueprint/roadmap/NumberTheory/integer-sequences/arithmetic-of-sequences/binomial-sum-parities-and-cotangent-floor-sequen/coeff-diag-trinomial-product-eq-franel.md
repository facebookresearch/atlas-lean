# A diagonal coefficient equal to a signed Franel number

For every $n \ge 0$, the
coefficient of $x^n y^n z^n$ in the integer polynomial
$(1 + x + y - z)^n (1 + x - y + z)^n (1 - x + y + z)^n$ equals
$(-1)^n \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}^3$ (A000172).
