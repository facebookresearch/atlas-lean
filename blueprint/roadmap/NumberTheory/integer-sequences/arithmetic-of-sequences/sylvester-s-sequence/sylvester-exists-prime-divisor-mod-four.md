# Prime divisors congruent to 3 modulo 4 of Sylvester numbers

Let $(S_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the
Sylvester sequence, $S_0 = 2$ and $S_{n+1} = S_n^2 - S_n + 1$. For every $n \ge 1$, $S_n$ has a
prime divisor $p$ with $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$.
