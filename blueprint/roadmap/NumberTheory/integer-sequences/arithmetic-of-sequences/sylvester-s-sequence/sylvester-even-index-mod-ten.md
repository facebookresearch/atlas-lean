# Sylvester numbers of even index modulo 10

Let $(S_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be the Sylvester
sequence, $S_0 = 2$ and $S_{n+1} = S_n^2 - S_n + 1$. For every $i \ge 1$,
$S_{2i} \equiv 7 \pmod{10}$.
