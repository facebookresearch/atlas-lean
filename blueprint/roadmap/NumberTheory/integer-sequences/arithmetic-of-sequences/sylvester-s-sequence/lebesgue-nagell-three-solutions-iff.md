# Coprime solutions of $x^2 + 3 = 4y^n$

For natural numbers $n$, $x$ and $y$, the
conditions $n \ge 3$, $x \ge 1$, $y \ge 1$, $\gcd(x, y) = 1$ and $x^2 + 3 = 4y^n$ hold if and only
if $n \ge 3$ and either $x = y = 1$ or $(n, x, y) = (3, 37, 7)$ (Luca–Tengely–Togbé).

## Sources

- Florian Luca, Szabolcs Tengely, and Alain Togbé, *On the Diophantine equation $x^2 + C = 4y^n$*, Annales des sciences mathématiques du Québec 33 (2009), no. 2, 171–184, Theorem 1.1, as quoted in Abderrahim Makki Naciri, *A Note on Sylvester's Sequence*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A117, Theorem 1, equation with $C = 3$.
