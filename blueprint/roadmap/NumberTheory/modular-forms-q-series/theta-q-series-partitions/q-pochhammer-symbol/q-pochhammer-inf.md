# Infinite $q$-Pochhammer symbol

For complex numbers $a$ and $q$ with $|q| < 1$, the infinite
$q$-Pochhammer symbol is $(a;q)_\infty = \prod_{n=0}^{\infty} (1 - a q^n)$, the unconditional
infinite product of the factors $1 - a q^n$.

## Sources

- Aritram Dhar, Ankush Goswami, and Mohit Tripathi, *Sign Patterns in a Two Colored Partition Companion series*, arXiv:2607.10576 (2026) (`2607.10576:D01`), and Chuanan Wei and Qin Wang, *Some Dwork-type $q$-supercongruences from a $_6\phi_5$ summation formula*, arXiv:2608.13818 (2026) (`2608.13818:D02`), which use $(a;q)_\infty = \prod_{j \ge 0} (1 - a q^j)$ for complex $a$, $q$ with $|q| < 1$.
