# Modular transformation of the Jacobi theta function

For $\tau$ in the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H} = \{\tau \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Im}(\tau) > 0\}$, let $\vartheta(\tau) = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} e^{\pi i n^2 \tau}$. Then for every $\tau \in \mathbb{H}$,

$$\vartheta(-1/\tau) = (-i\tau)^{1/2}\, \vartheta(\tau),$$

where $(-i\tau)^{1/2} = \exp\bigl(\tfrac{1}{2} \log(-i\tau)\bigr)$ is taken with the principal branch of the logarithm. Here $-1/\tau$ is the image of $\tau$ under the Möbius action of the matrix $S \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ with rows $(0, -1)$ and $(1, 0)$.
