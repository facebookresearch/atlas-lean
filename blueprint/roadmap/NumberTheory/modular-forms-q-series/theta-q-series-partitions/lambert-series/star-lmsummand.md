# Terms of the Lambert-type series $L_m^*$

For a complex number $q$ with $|q| < 1$, an
integer $m \ge 1$ and a natural number $k$, the term of index $k$ is
$q^{(2k+1)m} / (1 - q^{2k+1})^{2m}$, that is, $q^{(2n-1)m} / (1 - q^{2n-1})^{2m}$ with $n = k + 1$.

## Sources

- Živorad Tomovski and Stefan Gerhold, *Mathieu-Fibonacci Series*, Journal of Integer Sequences 25 (2022), Article 22.6.3, Section 3 (definition of $L_m^*(q)$), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL25/Gerhold/gerhold6.html>.
