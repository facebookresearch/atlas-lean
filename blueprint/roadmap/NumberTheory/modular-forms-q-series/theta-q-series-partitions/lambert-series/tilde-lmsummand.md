# Terms of the Lambert-type series $\tilde L_m$

For a complex number $q$ with $|q| < 1$,
an integer $m \ge 1$ and a natural number $k$, the term of index $k$ is
$q^{(2k+1)m} / (1 + q^{2k+1})^{2m}$, that is, $q^{(2n-1)m} / (1 + q^{2n-1})^{2m}$ with $n = k + 1$.
