# Summability of the Lambert-type series $\hat L_m$

For a complex number $q$ with
$|q| < 1$ and an integer $m \ge 1$, the summability condition for $\hat L_m(q)$ is that
the series
$\sum_{n \ge 1} q^{mn} / (1 + q^{2n})^m$ is summable in $\mathbb{C}$.

## Sources

- Živorad Tomovski and Stefan Gerhold, *Mathieu-Fibonacci Series*, Journal of Integer Sequences 25 (2022), Article 22.6.3, Section 3 (definition of $\hat L_m(q)$), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL25/Gerhold/gerhold6.html>.
