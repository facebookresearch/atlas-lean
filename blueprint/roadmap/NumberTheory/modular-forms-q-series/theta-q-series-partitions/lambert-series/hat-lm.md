# The Lambert-type series $\hat L_m$

For a complex number $q$ with $|q| < 1$ and an
integer $m \ge 1$, $\hat L_m(q) = \sum_{n \ge 1} q^{mn} / (1 + q^{2n})^m$, where the sum is
taken to be $0$ if the series is not summable.
