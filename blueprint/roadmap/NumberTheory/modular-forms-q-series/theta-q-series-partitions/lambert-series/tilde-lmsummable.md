# Summability of the Lambert-type series $\tilde L_m$

For a complex number $q$ with
$|q| < 1$ and an integer $m \ge 1$, this predicate holds when the series
$\sum_{n \ge 1} q^{(2n-1)m} / (1 + q^{2n-1})^{2m}$ is summable in $\mathbb{C}$.
