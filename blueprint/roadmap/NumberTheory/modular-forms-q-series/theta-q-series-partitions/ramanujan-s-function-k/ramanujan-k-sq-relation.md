# Modular equation of degree 2 for Ramanujan's function $k$

For every complex number $q$
with $|q| < 1$, the values $X = k(q)$ and $Y = k(q^2)$ of Ramanujan's function satisfy
$X^2 - Y + 2XY + X^2 Y + Y^2 = 0$.
