# Coefficients of Saito's eta products

For natural numbers $h$ and $j$, $c_h(j)$ is the coefficient of $q^j$ in $\prod_{m \ge 1}(1 - q^{hm})^{\varphi(h)} \prod_{d \mid h}(1 - q^{dm})^{-\mu(d)}$, the product side of the eta product $\eta(h\tau)^{\varphi(h)}\prod_{d \mid h}\eta(d\tau)^{-\mu(d)}$ without its power of $q$.
