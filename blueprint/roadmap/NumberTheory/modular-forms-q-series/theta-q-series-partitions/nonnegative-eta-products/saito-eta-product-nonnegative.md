# K. Saito's conjecture on nonnegative eta products

The statement that for every $h \ge 1$ the eta product $\eta(h\tau)^{\varphi(h)}/\prod_{d \mid h}\eta(d\tau)^{\mu(d)}$ has nonnegative Fourier coefficients: every coefficient of $\prod_{m \ge 1}\big[(1 - q^{hm})^{\varphi(h)} \prod_{d \mid h}(1 - q^{dm})^{-\mu(d)}\big]$ is nonnegative, where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function and $\mu$ the Möbius function.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Saito's eta products have nonnegative coefficients](saito-eta-product-nonnegative-holds.md).
