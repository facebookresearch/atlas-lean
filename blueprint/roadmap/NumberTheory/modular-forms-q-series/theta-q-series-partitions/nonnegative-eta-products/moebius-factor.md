# Möbius-weighted factors of eta products

For natural numbers $d$ and $m$, with $\mu$ the Möbius function, the power series over $\mathbb{Q}$ that is $(1 - q^{dm})^{-1}$ when $\mu(d) = 1$, $1 - q^{dm}$ when $\mu(d) = -1$ and $1$ when $\mu(d) = 0$: for $dm > 0$ it is $(1 - q^{dm})^{-\mu(d)}$. For $dm = 0$ the series $1 - q^0$ is $0$ and its inverse is read as $0$, so the factor is $1$ if $\mu(d) = 0$ and $0$ otherwise. In the eta products, $m \ge 1$ and the divisors are positive, so $dm > 0$.
