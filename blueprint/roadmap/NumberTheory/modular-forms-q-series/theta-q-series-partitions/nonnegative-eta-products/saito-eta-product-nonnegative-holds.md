# Saito's eta products have nonnegative coefficients

For every $h \ge 1$ the eta product $\eta(h\tau)^{\varphi(h)}/\prod_{d \mid h}\eta(d\tau)^{\mu(d)}$ has nonnegative Fourier coefficients: every coefficient of $\prod_{m \ge 1}\big[(1 - q^{hm})^{\varphi(h)} \prod_{d \mid h}(1 - q^{dm})^{-\mu(d)}\big]$ is nonnegative, where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function and $\mu$ the Möbius function.

This resolves [K. Saito's conjecture on nonnegative eta products](saito-eta-product-nonnegative.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Berkovich and Garvan (2008), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. Ibukiyama, in Gitter und Anwendungen, Oberwolfach Reports 2 (2005), no. 1, 54–55, https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2005/01.
- A. Berkovich and F. G. Garvan, K. Saito's conjecture for nonnegative eta products and analogous results for other infinite products, J. Number Theory 128 (2008), arXiv:math/0702027, Section 3, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0702027.
