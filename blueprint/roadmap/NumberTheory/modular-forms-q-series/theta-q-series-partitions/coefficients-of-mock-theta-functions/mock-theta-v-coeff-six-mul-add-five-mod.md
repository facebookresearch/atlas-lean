# The coefficients of $q^{6n+5}$ in $v(q)$ are divisible by 3

Let $P_v(n)$ be the
coefficient of $q^n$ in the eighth-order mock theta function $v(q)$. Then
$P_v(6n+5) \equiv 0 \pmod 3$ for every $n \ge 0$.

## Sources

- Sabi Biswas and Nipen Saikia, *Arithmetic Properties of the Coefficients of Some Mock Theta Functions*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A72, Theorem 2 (i) (`integers-2025-A72-thm-2`).
