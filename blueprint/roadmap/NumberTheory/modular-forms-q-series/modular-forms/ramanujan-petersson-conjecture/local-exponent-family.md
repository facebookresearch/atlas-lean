# Family of local exponents

A local-exponent family indexed by a set $\iota$ is a function
$m : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, the local Langlands exponents, with $0 \le m(i) \le 1/2$ for every
$i \in \iota$.

The upper bound $1/2$ is the Jacquet–Shalika range for cuspidal automorphic representations. The
index set is part of the data: the Ramanujan exponent of such a family depends on the family. For
the family of all local exponents of cuspidal automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_2$ it is
the Ramanujan exponent $\delta_2$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2$.
