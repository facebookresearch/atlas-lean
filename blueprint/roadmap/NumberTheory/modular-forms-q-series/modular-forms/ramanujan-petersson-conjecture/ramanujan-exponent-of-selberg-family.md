# The Selberg family's exponent is Selberg's constant

The Ramanujan exponent of the
Selberg family of archimedean exponents, whose local exponents are the numbers
$\sqrt{1/4 - \lambda}$ for the Laplace eigenvalues $\lambda \in (0, 1/4)$ of the congruence subgroups
of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, equals Selberg's congruence spectral-gap constant $C_{61}$, the
supremum of the gap parameters $\theta(\Gamma)$ over all congruence subgroups $\Gamma$.

This concerns only the archimedean exponents coming from exceptional eigenvalues; it does not
identify Selberg's constant with the Ramanujan exponent $\delta_2$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2$.
