# $-1$ lies in $\Gamma_1(N)$ exactly when $N \le 2$

For every positive integer $N$, the matrix $-1 \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$ lies in
$\Gamma_1(N) = \left\{\gamma \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z}) : \gamma \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & * \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \pmod N\right\}$
if and only if $N \le 2$.
