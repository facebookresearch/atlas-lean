# Second vertex map for Ihara's amalgam

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$, the homomorphism from $\Gamma_0(N) \subseteq \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ into $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ given by the entrywise base-change homomorphism $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ (an inclusion for $q \ne 0$; for $q = 0$ the ring $\mathbb{Z}[1/0]$ is the zero ring and every matrix has the same image) followed by $B \mapsto D B D^{-1}$ with $D = \operatorname{diag}(1, q)$. For $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ the image is $\begin{pmatrix} a & b/q \\ qc & d \end{pmatrix}$; for instance, at $q = 2$ the upper unitriangular generator's off-diagonal entry $1$ goes to $1/2$. This is the inverse of the conjugation $A \mapsto D^{-1} A D$ of [the automorphism](w-conj.md).
