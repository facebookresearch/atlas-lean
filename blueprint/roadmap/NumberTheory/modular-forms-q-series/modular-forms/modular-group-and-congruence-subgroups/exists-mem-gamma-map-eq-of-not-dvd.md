# Reduction mod $p$ maps $\Gamma(N)$ onto $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ when $p$ does not divide $N$

Let $p$ be a prime and $N$ a natural number with $p \nmid N$. Then every
$g \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ is the reduction modulo $p$ of some element of the
principal congruence subgroup $\Gamma(N) = \{\gamma \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z}) : \gamma \equiv I \pmod N\}$.
