# Conjugation by diag(1, q)⁻¹ on the left

For a natural number $q$, the group automorphism $A \mapsto D^{-1} A D$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$, with $D = \operatorname{diag}(1, q)$. For $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ the image is $\begin{pmatrix} a & qb \\ c/q & d \end{pmatrix}$; at $q = 2$ the upper unitriangular generator's off-diagonal entry $1$ goes to $2$. Its inverse is $A \mapsto D A D^{-1}$, used by [the second vertex map](vertex-one.md).
