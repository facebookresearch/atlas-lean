# First vertex map for Ihara's amalgam

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$, the restriction to $\Gamma_0(N) \subseteq \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ of the entrywise base-change homomorphism $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ (an inclusion for $q \ne 0$; for $q = 0$ the ring $\mathbb{Z}[1/0]$ is the zero ring and every matrix has the same image).
