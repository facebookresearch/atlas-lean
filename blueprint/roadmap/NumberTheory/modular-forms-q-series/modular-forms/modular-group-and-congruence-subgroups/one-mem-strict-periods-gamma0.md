# $1$ is a strict period of $\Gamma_0(N)$

For every natural number $N$, the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ lies in the
image of $\Gamma_0(N)$ in $\mathrm{GL}(2, \mathbb{R})$; that is, $1$ is a strict period of $\Gamma_0(N)$,
where a real number $x$ is a strict period of a subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}(2, \mathbb{R})$ when
$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & x \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ belongs to it.
