# First vertex map into Γ₀(N) over Z[1/q]

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$, the inclusion of $\Gamma_0(N) \subseteq \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ into $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$, where $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$ is the subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ of matrices whose lower-left entry is divisible by $N$.
