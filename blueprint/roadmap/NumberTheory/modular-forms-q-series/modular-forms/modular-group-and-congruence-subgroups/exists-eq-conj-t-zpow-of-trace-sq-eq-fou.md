# Parabolic elements of $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$ are conjugate to $\pm$ powers of $T$

Let $T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. For every
$\gamma \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$ with $(\operatorname{tr} \gamma)^2 = 4$ there are
$\varepsilon \in \{1, -1\}$, $\delta \in \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$ and $h \in \mathbb{Z}$ with
$\gamma = \varepsilon\, \delta T^h \delta^{-1}$.
