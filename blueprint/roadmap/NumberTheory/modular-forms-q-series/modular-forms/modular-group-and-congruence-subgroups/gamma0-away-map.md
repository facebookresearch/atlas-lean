# Lower-right entry map of Γ₀(N) over Z[1/q]

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$ with $\gcd(N, q) = 1$, the monoid homomorphism from $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$ to $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ sending a matrix to the reduction of its lower-right entry, where $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$ is the subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ of matrices whose lower-left entry is divisible by $N$.
