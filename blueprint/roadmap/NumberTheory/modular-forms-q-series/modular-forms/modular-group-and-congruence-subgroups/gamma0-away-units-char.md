# Lower-right entry character of Γ₀(N) over Z[1/q]

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$ with $\gcd(N, q) = 1$, the character of $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$ with values in $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$ sending a matrix to the reduction of its lower-right entry; it extends the corresponding character of $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}$. Here $\Gamma_0(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/q]$ is the subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}[1/q])$ of matrices whose lower-left entry is divisible by $N$.
