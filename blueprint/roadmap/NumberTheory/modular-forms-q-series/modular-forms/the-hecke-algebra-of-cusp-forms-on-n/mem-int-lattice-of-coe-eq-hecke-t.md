# Mem int Lattice of coe eq hecke T

In weight `k ≥ 1` and for `p ≠ 0`, if `f` lies in the integral lattice and the Hecke sum
`heckeT k p ⇑f` is the underlying function of a cusp form `g` on `Γ₀(N)`, then `g` lies in the
integral lattice. For a prime `p ∤ N`, the bundled operator `heckeTLin` gives such a `g`.
