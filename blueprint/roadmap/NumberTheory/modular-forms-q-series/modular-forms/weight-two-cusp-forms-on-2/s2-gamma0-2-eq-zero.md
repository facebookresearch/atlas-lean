# No nonzero weight-two cusp forms on Γ₀(2)

Every cusp form of weight $2$ for $\Gamma_0(2)$ is zero.

Proof: The norm of such a form $f$ to $\Gamma_0(1) = \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is a cusp form of
level one and weight $2 \cdot [\Gamma_0(1) : \Gamma_0(2)] = 6$, hence zero by the dimension formula
for level one, and the norm vanishes only when $f$ does.
