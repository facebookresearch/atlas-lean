# Norm of a cusp form vanishes only for the zero form

Let $\mathcal{G}$ and $\mathcal{H}$ be subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that
$\mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H}$ has finite index in $\mathcal{H}$ and $\det h = \pm 1$ for every
$h \in \mathcal{H}$; neither group need contain the other. Let $k$ be an integer, write
$F|_k g$ for the weight-$k$ slash action of $g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ on functions $F$ on
the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$, and let $d = [\mathcal{H} : \mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H}]$.
Let $f$ be a cusp form of weight $k$ for $\mathcal{G}$, and let
$N_{\mathcal{H}}(f) = \prod_{q \in \mathcal{H}/(\mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H})} f|_k h_q^{-1}$ be its
norm to $\mathcal{H}$, a cusp form of weight $kd$ for $\mathcal{H}$. Then $N_{\mathcal{H}}(f) = 0$ if
and only if $f = 0$.

The norm is a finite product of translates $f|_k h^{-1}$, each of which is nonzero when $f$ is
nonzero, and a product of nonzero holomorphic functions on the connected set $\mathbb{H}$ is
nonzero.
