# Cusp form norm agrees with the modular form norm

Let $\mathcal{G}$ and $\mathcal{H}$ be subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that
$\mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H}$ has finite index in $\mathcal{H}$ and $\det h = \pm 1$ for every
$h \in \mathcal{H}$; neither group need contain the other. Let $k$ be an integer, write
$F|_k g$ for the weight-$k$ slash action of $g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ on functions $F$ on
the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$, and let $d = [\mathcal{H} : \mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H}]$.
Let $f$ be a cusp form of weight $k$ for $\mathcal{G}$. Then the norm of $f$ to $\mathcal{H}$ as a
cusp form, as a function on $\mathbb{H}$, equals the norm of $f$ to $\mathcal{H}$ as a modular form:
both are the product
$\prod_{q \in \mathcal{H}/(\mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H})} f|_k h_q^{-1}$ over representatives
$h_q \in \mathcal{H}$ of the left cosets of $\mathcal{G} \cap \mathcal{H}$, a form of weight $kd$
for $\mathcal{H}$.
