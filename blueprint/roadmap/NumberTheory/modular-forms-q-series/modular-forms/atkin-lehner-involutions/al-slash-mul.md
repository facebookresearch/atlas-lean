# Product rule for the Atkin–Lehner slash operator

For $M \ne 0$, an Atkin–Lehner datum for $M$ and $q$, integers $k_1, k_2$ and functions $F, G$ on the upper half-plane, $(FG)|_{k_1+k_2} W = q \cdot (F|_{k_1} W)(G|_{k_2} W)$, where the Atkin–Lehner matrix of the datum is $W = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} qa & b \\ qR & q \end{smallmatrix}\right)$, of determinant $q$ and $f|_k\gamma(\tau) = \det(\gamma)^{k-1}(c\tau + d)^{-k} f(\gamma\tau)$ is the weight-$k$ slash action of $\gamma = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
