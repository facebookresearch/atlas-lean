# Atkin–Lehner matrices

For an Atkin–Lehner datum $(R, a, b)$ for $M$ and $q$, the integer matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix} qa & b \\ qR & q \end{smallmatrix}\right)$; here for natural numbers $M$ and $q$, an Atkin–Lehner datum consists of $R \in \mathbb{N}$ with $M = qR$ and integers $a, b$ with $qa - Rb = 1$.
