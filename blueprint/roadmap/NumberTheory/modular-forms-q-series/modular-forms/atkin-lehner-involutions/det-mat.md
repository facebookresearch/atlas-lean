# Determinant of an Atkin–Lehner matrix

For every Atkin–Lehner datum for $M$ and $q$, the Atkin–Lehner matrix has determinant $q$, where for natural numbers $M$ and $q$, an Atkin–Lehner datum consists of $R \in \mathbb{N}$ with $M = qR$ and integers $a, b$ with $qa - Rb = 1$ and the Atkin–Lehner matrix of the datum is $W = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} qa & b \\ qR & q \end{smallmatrix}\right)$, of determinant $q$.
