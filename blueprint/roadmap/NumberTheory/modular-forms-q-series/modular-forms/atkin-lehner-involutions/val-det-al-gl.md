# Determinant of the real Atkin–Lehner matrix

For $M \ne 0$ and an Atkin–Lehner datum for $M$ and $q$, the Atkin–Lehner matrix in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ has determinant $q$, where the Atkin–Lehner matrix of the datum is $W = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} qa & b \\ qR & q \end{smallmatrix}\right)$, of determinant $q$.
