# Atkin–Lehner slash operator

For $M \ne 0$, an Atkin–Lehner datum for $M$ and $q$ and an integer $k$, the operator $f \mapsto f|_k W$ on functions on the upper half-plane, where the Atkin–Lehner matrix of the datum is $W = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} qa & b \\ qR & q \end{smallmatrix}\right)$, of determinant $q$ and $f|_k\gamma(\tau) = \det(\gamma)^{k-1}(c\tau + d)^{-k} f(\gamma\tau)$ is the weight-$k$ slash action of $\gamma = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
