# Modular forms for cocompact groups

Let $\Gamma$ be a discrete subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ whose elements have determinant $1$, and suppose some compact set $K$ in the upper half-plane meets the $\Gamma$-orbit of every point. Then every modular form of weight $0$ for $\Gamma$ is constant, and every modular form of negative weight for $\Gamma$ is zero.
