# p-integrality of Eisenstein series coefficients

Let $p$ be a prime and $k \ge 3$ an even integer with $p - 1 \mid k$, and let $E_k$ be the normalized Eisenstein series of weight $k$ (constant term $1$). Then for every $m \ge 1$ there are an integer $x$ and a natural number $s$ with $p \nmid s$, $x = s\,a_m(E_k)$ and $p \mid x$, where $a_m(E_k)$ is the $m$-th $q$-expansion coefficient.
