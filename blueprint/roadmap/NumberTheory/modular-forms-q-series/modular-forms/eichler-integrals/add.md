# Sums of Eichler integrals

For every $n$, if $F$ is an Eichler integral of degree $n$ of $f$ and $G$ is an Eichler integral of degree $n$ of $g$, then $F + G$ is an Eichler integral of $f + g$; here for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a function $F$ from the upper half-plane to the binary forms of degree $n$ is an Eichler integral of $f$ when each coefficient of $F$ is holomorphic with derivative $f(\tau)$ times the corresponding coefficient of $(\tau X_0 + X_1)^n$.
