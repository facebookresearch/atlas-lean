# The zero Eichler integral

For every $n$, the zero function is an Eichler integral of degree $n$ of the zero function, where for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a function $F$ from the upper half-plane to the binary forms of degree $n$ is an Eichler integral of $f$ when each coefficient of $F$ is holomorphic with derivative $f(\tau)$ times the corresponding coefficient of $(\tau X_0 + X_1)^n$.
