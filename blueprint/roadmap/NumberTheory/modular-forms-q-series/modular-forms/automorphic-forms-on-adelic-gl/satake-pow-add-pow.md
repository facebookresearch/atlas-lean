# Satake power sums are power sums of the parameters

For a commutative ring $R$, $\alpha, \beta \in R$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p_n(\alpha + \beta, \alpha\beta) = \alpha^n + \beta^n$, where for a commutative ring $R$, $s, e \in R$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p_n(s, e)$ is defined by $p_0 = 2$, $p_1 = s$ and $p_{n+2} = s\,p_{n+1} - e\,p_n$.
