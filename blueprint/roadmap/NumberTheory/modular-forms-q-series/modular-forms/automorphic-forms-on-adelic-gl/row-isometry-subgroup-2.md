# Row isometries of determinant one

For a normed field $K$, the subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_2(K)$ of row isometries of determinant $1$, where for a normed field $K$, $k \in \mathrm{GL}_2(K)$ is a row isometry when $|\det k| = 1$ and $|xk_{00} + yk_{10}|^2 + |xk_{01} + yk_{11}|^2 = |x|^2 + |y|^2$ for all $x, y \in K$.
