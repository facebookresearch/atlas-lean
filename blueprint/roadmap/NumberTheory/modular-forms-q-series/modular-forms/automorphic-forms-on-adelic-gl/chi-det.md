# Determinant characters on adelic GL₂

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here, and let $\eta : \mathbb{A}_{R,K}^\times \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ be a character. The function $g \mapsto \eta(\det g)$ on $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_{R,K})$, viewed in $\mathbb{C}$.
