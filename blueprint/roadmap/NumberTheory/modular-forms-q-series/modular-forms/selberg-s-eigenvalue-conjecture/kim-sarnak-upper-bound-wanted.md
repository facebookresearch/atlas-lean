# Kim–Sarnak bound on the spectral-gap constant

Selberg's congruence spectral-gap constant
$C_{61}$ is at most $7/64$.

## Sources

- Henry H. Kim and Peter Sarnak, Appendix 2 to Henry H. Kim, *Functoriality for the exterior square of $\mathrm{GL}_4$ and the symmetric fourth of $\mathrm{GL}_2$*, Journal of the American Mathematical Society 16 (2003), 139–183: the bound $\lambda_1(\Gamma) \ge 975/4096 = 1/4 - (7/64)^2$, which gives $C_{61} \le 7/64$.
