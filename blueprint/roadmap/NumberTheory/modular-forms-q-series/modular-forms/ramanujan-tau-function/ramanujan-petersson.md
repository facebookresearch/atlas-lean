# Ramanujan–Petersson conjecture for τ (Deligne's theorem)

For every prime $p$, the
Ramanujan τ-function satisfies $|\tau(p)| \le 2p^{11/2}$, where $|\cdot|$ is the absolute value
of a complex number.
