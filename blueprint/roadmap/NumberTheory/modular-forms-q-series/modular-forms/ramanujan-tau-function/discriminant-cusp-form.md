# The modular discriminant is a cusp form of weight 12

For $z$ in the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$ let $q = e^{2\pi i z}$, let
$\eta(z) = e^{2\pi i z / 24} \prod_{n \ge 1} (1 - q^n)$ be the Dedekind eta function, and let

$$\Delta(z) = \eta(z)^{24} = q \prod_{n \ge 1} (1 - q^n)^{24}$$

be the modular discriminant. Then $\Delta$ is a cusp form of weight $12$ and level one: it is
holomorphic on $\mathbb{H}$; for all integers $a, b, c, d$ with $ad - bc = 1$ and all $z \in \mathbb{H}$,

$$\Delta\Bigl( \frac{az + b}{cz + d} \Bigr) = (cz + d)^{12} \, \Delta(z),$$

that is, $\Delta$ is invariant under the weight-$12$ slash action of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$; and it
vanishes at every cusp.
