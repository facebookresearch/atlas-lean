# Taylor–Wiles patching datum

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a commutative ring, $\ell$ and $r$ natural numbers, $R$ a commutative
$\mathcal{O}$-algebra, $M$ an $R$-module and $S = \mathcal{O}[[X_1, \dots, X_r]]$. A Taylor–Wiles
patching datum consists of, for every natural number $n$, a level of a Taylor–Wiles patching
datum for $\mathcal{O}$, $r$, $R$ and $M$ whose relation ideal is

$$J_n = \bigl((1 + X_1)^{\ell^n} - 1, \dots, (1 + X_r)^{\ell^n} - 1\bigr) \subseteq S.$$
