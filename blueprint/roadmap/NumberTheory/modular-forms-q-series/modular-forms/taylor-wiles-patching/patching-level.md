# Level of a Taylor–Wiles patching datum

One level of a Taylor–Wiles patching datum: a finite module `N` over the
power series ring `𝒪⟦X₁, …, Xᵣ⟧`, with a surjective algebra map `ψ` to `R`
killing the `φ`-images of the variables, a surjective `ψ`-semilinear map
`π : N → M` whose kernel is the span of the `φ (Xᵢ)`, and a finite family
`b` spanning `N` with relations exactly `J`.
