# Hecke operator as a function on fixed points

Let $G$ be a group acting on an additive commutative monoid $A$ by additive maps, let $U$ and
$V$ be subgroups of $G$, and let $g \in G$ be such that the double coset $UgV$ is the union of
finitely many left cosets $g_1 V, \dots, g_r V$ (pairwise distinct). Write $A^V$ and $A^U$ for the
elements of $A$ fixed by $V$ and by $U$. The Hecke operator
$T(g) \colon A^V \to A^U$ is $T(g)(a) = \sum_{i=1}^{r} g_i \cdot a$. The sum is fixed by $U$ and does
not depend on the choice of the representatives $g_i$, since $a$ is fixed by $V$.
