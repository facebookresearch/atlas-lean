# Hecke operator

Let $G$ be a group acting on an additive commutative monoid $A$ by additive maps, let $U$ and
$V$ be subgroups of $G$, and let $g \in G$ be such that the double coset $UgV$ is the union of
finitely many left cosets $g_1 V, \dots, g_r V$ (pairwise distinct). Write $A^V$ and $A^U$ for the
elements of $A$ fixed by $V$ and by $U$. Suppose moreover that $A$ is a module over a
semiring $R$ whose scalar multiplication commutes with the action of $G$, so that $A^V$ and $A^U$
are $R$-modules. Then the Hecke operator $T(g) \colon A^V \to A^U$,
$T(g)(a) = \sum_{i=1}^{r} g_i \cdot a$, is an $R$-linear map.
