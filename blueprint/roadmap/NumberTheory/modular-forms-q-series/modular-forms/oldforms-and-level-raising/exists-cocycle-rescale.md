# The rescaling identity $D\gamma = \delta D$ for $\Gamma_0(M)$

For natural numbers $d, R, M$ with $dR \mid M$ and every $\gamma \in \Gamma_0(M)$, there is $\delta \in \Gamma_0(R)$ with $\operatorname{diag}(d, 1)\,\gamma = \delta\,\operatorname{diag}(d, 1)$ as integer matrices, where $D = \operatorname{diag}(d, 1)$. For $d \ne 0$ this says that conjugation by $D$ carries $\gamma$ to $\delta = D\gamma D^{-1}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$; for $d = 0$ the matrix $D$ is not invertible and the identity is not a conjugation.
