# Slash action of the rescaling matrix

For an integer $k$, a natural number $d \ne 0$ and a function $f$ on the upper half-plane, $(f|_k\operatorname{diag}(d, 1))(\tau) = d^{k-1}f(d\tau)$, where $f|_k\gamma(\tau) = |\det\gamma|^{k-1}(c_\gamma\tau + d_\gamma)^{-k}\,\sigma_\gamma\bigl(f(\gamma\tau)\bigr)$ is the weight-$k$ slash action of $\gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ with bottom row $(c_\gamma, d_\gamma)$, with $\sigma_\gamma$ the identity when $\det\gamma > 0$ and complex conjugation when $\det\gamma < 0$; here $\det\operatorname{diag}(d, 1) = d > 0$, so no conjugation occurs.
