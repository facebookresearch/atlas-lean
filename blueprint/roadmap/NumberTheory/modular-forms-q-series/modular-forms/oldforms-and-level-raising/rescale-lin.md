# Rescaling cusp forms to a higher level

For natural numbers $d, R, M$ with $M \ne 0$ and $dR \mid M$ and an integer $k$, the linear map from cusp forms of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(R)$ to cusp forms of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(M)$ sending $f$ to $f|_k\operatorname{diag}(d, 1)$, that is, $\tau \mapsto d^{k-1}f(d\tau)$.
