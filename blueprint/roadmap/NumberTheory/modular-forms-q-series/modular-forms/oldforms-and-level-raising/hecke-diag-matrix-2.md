# The rescaling matrix acts without complex conjugation

For every natural number $d$, the matrix $\delta_d$ acts on the upper half-plane without complex conjugation (its determinant is positive), where for a natural number $d \ne 0$, $\delta_d = \operatorname{diag}(d, 1) \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ (the identity when $d = 0$).
