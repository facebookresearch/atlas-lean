# Partial zeta functions of an imaginary quadratic field as Epstein zeta functions

Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field of discriminant $D < 0$, with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$ and
$w$ roots of unity. Write $N$ for the [absolute norm](../../../RingTheory/dedekind-domains/ideal-absolute-norm.md) of ideals,
$N(\mathfrak{a}) = \#(\mathcal{O}_K / \mathfrak{a})$ for a nonzero ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$, extended to fractional ideals
by $N(\mathfrak{b}) = N(d \mathfrak{b}) / d^2$ for the least positive integer $d$ with $d \mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$. For an
ideal class $A$ of $K$ let

$$\zeta_K(s, A) = \sum_{\mathfrak{a} \in A} N(\mathfrak{a})^{-s} \qquad (\operatorname{Re} s > 1),$$

the sum running over the nonzero integral ideals $\mathfrak{a}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ in $A$, so that the
Dedekind zeta function of $K$ is $\zeta_K(s) = \sum_A \zeta_K(s, A)$, a sum over the $h$ ideal classes, finitely many by the
[finiteness of the class group](../../algebraic-number-theory/number-fields-embeddings/class-group-finite.md). Every
ideal class contains fractional ideals of the form $\mathfrak{b} = \mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z} z$ with
$\operatorname{Im} z > 0$, as shown below. If
$\mathfrak{b} = \mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z} z$, with $\operatorname{Im} z > 0$, lies in the inverse class $A^{-1}$,
then $2 \operatorname{Im} z = N(\mathfrak{b}) \sqrt{|D|}$ and

$$\zeta_K(s, A) = \frac{1}{w} \left( \frac{2}{\sqrt{|D|}} \right)^{s} Z(z, s), \qquad Z(z, s) = \sum_{(m, n) \neq (0, 0)} \frac{(\operatorname{Im} z)^s}{|m + n z|^{2s}} \qquad (\operatorname{Re} s > 1).$$

Choosing such a $\mathfrak{b}_A = \mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z} z_A$ in $A^{-1}$ for every class $A$ therefore gives
$\zeta_K(s) = \frac{1}{w} \bigl(2/\sqrt{|D|}\bigr)^{s} \sum_A Z(z_A, s)$ for $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$.

For the representatives, let $\mathfrak{c}$ be a fractional ideal of the class, $d$ a positive integer with
$d \mathfrak{c} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$ and $0 \neq x \in d \mathfrak{c}$. Then $\mathfrak{c}$ is a finitely generated torsion-free
$\mathbb{Z}$-module, hence [free](../../../LinearAlgebra/eigenvalues-and-canonical-forms/finite-torsion-free-module-pid-free.md), of rank $2$, because
$x \mathcal{O}_K \subseteq d \mathfrak{c} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$, where $d \mathfrak{c} \cong \mathfrak{c}$, and $x \mathcal{O}_K \cong \mathcal{O}_K$ has rank
$[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2$. For a
$\mathbb{Z}$-basis $(\alpha, \beta)$ of $\mathfrak{c}$, the quotient $\beta / \alpha$ is not rational, hence not real, as
$K \cap \mathbb{R} = \mathbb{Q}$; ordering the basis so that $\operatorname{Im}(\beta / \alpha) > 0$ and dividing by $\alpha$ gives
$\mathfrak{b} = \mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z} z$ in the class, with $z = \beta / \alpha$.

Next, $\mathfrak{a} \mapsto \mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{b}$ maps the integral ideals of $A$ bijectively onto the principal fractional ideals
$(\gamma) = \gamma \mathcal{O}_K$ with $0 \neq \gamma \in \mathfrak{b}$: as $\mathfrak{b} \in A^{-1}$, $\mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{b}$ is principal, generated by
an element of $\mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{b}$, and the inverse map is $(\gamma) \mapsto (\gamma) \mathfrak{b}^{-1}$, whose
values are integral, because $\gamma \in \mathfrak{b}$, and lie in $A$, the class of $\mathfrak{b}^{-1}$. Here $\mathfrak{b}$ is invertible, with
$\mathfrak{b}^{-1} = a^{-1} d' J$ if $d' \mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$ and $J$ is an ideal with $(d' \mathfrak{b}) J = (a) \neq 0$, which
exists as $\mathcal{O}_K$ is a [Dedekind domain](../../../RingTheory/dedekind-domains/characterizations-of-dedekind-domains/is-dedekind-domain-iff-principal-product.md). With $d$ the least positive integer such that
$d \mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K$, $\mathfrak{a} \cdot d \mathfrak{b} = (d \gamma)$, so
$N(\mathfrak{a}) N(d \mathfrak{b}) = N((d \gamma)) = |N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(d \gamma)| = d^2 |\gamma|^2$, by the multiplicativity of $N$ and the
[norm of a principal ideal](../../../RingTheory/dedekind-domains/ideal-absolute-norm-principal.md), that is, $N(\mathfrak{a}) = |\gamma|^2 / N(\mathfrak{b})$ with
$\gamma = m + n z$. Each such ideal has exactly $w$ generators: its generators are the $\gamma u$ with $u$ a unit of
$\mathcal{O}_K$, and the units are the elements of $\mathcal{O}_K$ of
[norm $\pm 1$](../../algebraic-number-theory/discriminants-ramification/unit-iff-norm-plus-minus-one.md), that is, as
$N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(u) = |u|^2$, the points of the lattice $\mathcal{O}_K \subset \mathbb{C}$ on the unit circle, a finite group,
hence the group of the $w$ roots of unity of $K$. For the relation $2 \operatorname{Im} z = N(\mathfrak{b}) \sqrt{|D|}$, take this $d$ and a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis $(\omega_1, \omega_2)$ of
$\mathcal{O}_K$, whose
discriminant is $D$, and let $T$ be the matrix of the coordinates of the basis $(d, d z)$ of $d \mathfrak{b}$ in it. Then
[$|\det T| = N(d \mathfrak{b})$](../../../RingTheory/dedekind-domains/ideal-absolute-norm-det-basis-change.md) $= d^2 N(\mathfrak{b})$, so
by the [change of basis for discriminants](../../../RingTheory/algebras-and-differentials/discriminant-change-of-basis.md) the
discriminant of $(d, d z)$, which is $d^4 (z - \bar z)^2 = -4 d^4 (\operatorname{Im} z)^2$, equals
$(\det T)^2 D = -d^4 N(\mathfrak{b})^2 |D|$; dividing by $-d^4$ gives $4 (\operatorname{Im} z)^2 = N(\mathfrak{b})^2 |D|$.
