# Cusp form with conjugated coefficients

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$ and a cusp form $f$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$, the cusp form $f|_k J$ of the same weight and level obtained by slashing with the coefficient-conjugation matrix $J$; its $q$-expansion coefficients are the complex conjugates of those of $f$.
