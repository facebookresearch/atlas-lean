# Cauchy product of q-expansions

Let $h > 0$ and let $A, B$ be complex power series and $F, G$ functions on the upper half-plane such that $\sum_m a_m q_h(\tau)^m = F(\tau)$ and $\sum_m b_m q_h(\tau)^m = G(\tau)$ for every $\tau$, where $q_h(\tau) = e^{2\pi i\tau/h}$ and $a_m, b_m$ are the coefficients. Then the coefficients of $AB$ satisfy $\sum_m (AB)_m q_h(\tau)^m = F(\tau)G(\tau)$ for every $\tau$.
