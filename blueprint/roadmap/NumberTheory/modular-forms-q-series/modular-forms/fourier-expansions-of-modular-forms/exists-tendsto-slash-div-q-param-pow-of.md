# Nonzero modular forms have a nonzero leading coefficient at each cusp

Let $\Gamma$ be a subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, $k$ an integer and $f \ne 0$ a modular form of weight $k$ for $\Gamma$. Let $\sigma \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ and $h > 0$ with $\sigma T^h \sigma^{-1} \in \Gamma$, where $T = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Then there are $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a \ne 0$ with $(f|_k\sigma)(\tau)/q_h(\tau)^n \to a$ as $\operatorname{Im}\tau \to \infty$, where $q_h(\tau) = e^{2\pi i\tau/h}$.
