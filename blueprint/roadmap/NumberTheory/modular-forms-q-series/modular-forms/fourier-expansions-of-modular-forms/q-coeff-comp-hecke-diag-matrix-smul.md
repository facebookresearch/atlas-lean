# q-coefficients of a modular form composed with τ ↦ dτ

Let $f$ be a modular form of weight $k$ for a subgroup $\Gamma$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})$ having $1$ as a strict period, and let $d \ge 1$. Then the $n$-th $q$-expansion coefficient of $\tau \mapsto f(d\tau)$ is $a_{n/d}(f)$ if $d \mid n$ and $0$ otherwise, where $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th [$q$-expansion coefficient](q-coeff.md); for the forms here it is the coefficient of $q^n$, $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$, in their $q$-expansion.
