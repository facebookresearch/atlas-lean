# Complex conjugation of q-coefficients preserves cusp forms

For $N \ge 1$ and an integer $k$, there is a map $\rho$ from the cusp forms of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$ to themselves with $a_n(\rho f) = \overline{a_n(f)}$ for every $f$ and $n$, where for a function $f$ on the upper half-plane and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th coefficient of the $q$-expansion of $f$ with $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$.
