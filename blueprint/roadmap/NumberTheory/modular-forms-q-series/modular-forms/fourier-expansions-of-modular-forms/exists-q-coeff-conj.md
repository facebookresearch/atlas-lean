# Complex conjugation of q-coefficients preserves cusp forms

For $N \ge 1$ and an integer $k$, there is a map $\rho$ from the cusp forms of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$ to themselves with $a_n(\rho f) = \overline{a_n(f)}$ for every $f$ and $n$, where $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th [$q$-expansion coefficient](q-coeff.md); for the forms here it is the coefficient of $q^n$, $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$, in their $q$-expansion.
