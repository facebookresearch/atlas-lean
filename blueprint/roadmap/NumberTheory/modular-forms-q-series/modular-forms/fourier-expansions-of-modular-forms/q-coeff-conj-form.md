# Coefficients of the conjugated cusp form

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$, a cusp form $f$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th $q$-expansion coefficient of the conjugated cusp form $f|_k J$ is $\overline{a_n(f)}$, where for a function $f$ on the upper half-plane and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th coefficient of the $q$-expansion of $f$ with $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$.
