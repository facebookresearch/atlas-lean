# Coefficients of the conjugated cusp form

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$, a cusp form $f$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th $q$-expansion coefficient of the conjugated cusp form $f|_k J$ is $\overline{a_n(f)}$, where $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th [$q$-expansion coefficient](q-coeff.md); for the forms here it is the coefficient of $q^n$, $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$, in their $q$-expansion.
