# q-coefficients of a rescaled cusp form

Let $d, R, M$ be natural numbers with $M \ne 0$ and $dR \mid M$, let $k$ be an integer and $f$ a cusp form of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(R)$, and let $g$ be the cusp form for $\Gamma_0(M)$ obtained by slashing $f$ with $\operatorname{diag}(d, 1)$. Then $a_n(g) = d^{k-1}a_{n/d}(f)$ if $d \mid n$ and $a_n(g) = 0$ otherwise, where for a function $f$ on the upper half-plane and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $a_n(f)$ is the $n$-th coefficient of the $q$-expansion of $f$ with $q = e^{2\pi i\tau}$.
