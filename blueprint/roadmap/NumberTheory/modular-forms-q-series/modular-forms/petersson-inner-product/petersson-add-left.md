# Additivity of the Petersson pairing

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$ and cusp forms $f_1, f_2, g$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$, $\langle f_1 + f_2, g\rangle = \langle f_1, g\rangle + \langle f_2, g\rangle$ for the Petersson pairing, the integral of the Petersson integrand over the standard fundamental domain with respect to the hyperbolic area measure $dx\,dy / y^2$.
