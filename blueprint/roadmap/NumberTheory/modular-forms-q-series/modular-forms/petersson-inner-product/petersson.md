# Petersson pairing of cusp forms for Γ₀(N)

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$ and cusp forms $f, g$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$, the Petersson pairing is

$$\langle f, g \rangle = \int_{\mathcal{F}} \sum_{\gamma} \overline{(f|_k\gamma^{-1})(\tau)}\,(g|_k\gamma^{-1})(\tau)\,(\operatorname{Im}\tau)^k\, \frac{dx\,dy}{y^2}, \qquad \tau = x + iy,$$

where $\mathcal{F}$ is the standard fundamental domain of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ in the upper half-plane, the sum runs over the chosen coset representatives $\gamma$ of $\Gamma_0(N)$ in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, and the integral is taken with respect to the hyperbolic area measure $dx\,dy / y^2$ of the upper half-plane, not Euclidean area. The integrand is integrable on $\mathcal{F}$.
