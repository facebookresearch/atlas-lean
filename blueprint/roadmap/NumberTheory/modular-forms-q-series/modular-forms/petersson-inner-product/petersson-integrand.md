# Integrand of the Petersson pairing

For $N \ge 1$, an integer $k$ and cusp forms $f, g$ of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$, the Petersson integrand at $\tau$ is $\sum_{\gamma}\overline{(f|_k\gamma^{-1})(\tau)}\,(g|_k\gamma^{-1})(\tau)\,(\operatorname{Im}\tau)^k$, the sum over the chosen coset representatives $\gamma$ of $\Gamma_0(N)$ in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$.
