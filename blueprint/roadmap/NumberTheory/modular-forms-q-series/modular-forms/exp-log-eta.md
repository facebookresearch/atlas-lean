# Exponential of the logarithm series of the Dedekind eta function

For every $z$ in the upper half-plane, $\exp\bigl(\pi i z/12 + \sum_{n \ge 0}\log(1 - e^{2\pi i(n+1)z})\bigr) = \eta(z)$, where $\eta(z) = e^{\pi i z/12}\prod_{n \ge 1}(1 - e^{2\pi i n z})$ is the Dedekind eta function and $\log$ is the principal branch.
