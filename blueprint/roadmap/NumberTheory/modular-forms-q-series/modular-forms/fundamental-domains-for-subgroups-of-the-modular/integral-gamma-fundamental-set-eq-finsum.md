# Unfolding integrals over a fundamental set

Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-index subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ containing $-1$, $E$ a real normed space and $f$ an $E$-valued function on the upper half-plane integrable on the fundamental set of $\Gamma$. Then the integral of $f$ over the fundamental set equals $\sum_{\gamma} \int_{\mathcal{D}} f(\gamma^{-1}\tau)\,d\tau$, the sum over the chosen coset representatives $\gamma$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})/\Gamma$ and $\mathcal{D}$ the standard fundamental domain. Integrability and all integrals are with respect to the hyperbolic area measure $dx\,dy / y^2$ of the upper half-plane ($\tau = x + iy$).

The space $E$ need not be complete: the integrals are Bochner integrals, which are defined as $0$ when $E$ is not complete, in which case both sides are $0$.
