# Unfolding integrals over a fundamental set

Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-index subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ containing $-1$, $E$ a real normed space and $f$ an $E$-valued function on the upper half-plane integrable on the fundamental set of $\Gamma$. Then the integral of $f$ over the fundamental set equals $\sum_{\gamma} \int_{\mathcal{D}} f(\gamma^{-1}\tau)\,d\tau$, the sum over the chosen coset representatives $\gamma$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})/\Gamma$ and $\mathcal{D}$ the standard fundamental domain.
