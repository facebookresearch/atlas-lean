# Hecke endomorphisms $\xi_n$ of the Jacobian of a modular curve

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $\Gamma$ a group of level $N$, and $J$ the Jacobian of the
[modular curve $X_\Gamma$](modular-curve-of-a-group-of-level-n.md), with $J(\mathbb{C})$ the
[Jacobian](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/algebraic-curves/jacobians-of-compact-riemann-surfaces/jacobian.md)
of the compact Riemann surface $\mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma$.

For $\alpha \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q})$ with $\det \alpha > 0$, let $\Gamma_\alpha = \Gamma \cap \alpha^{-1} \Gamma
\alpha$, a subgroup of finite index in $\Gamma$, and let $\pi_1, \pi_2 \colon \mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma_\alpha
\to \mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma$ be the holomorphic maps induced by $z \mapsto z$ and $z \mapsto \alpha z$. The
image of $(\pi_1, \pi_2)$ is Shimura's correspondence $X(\Gamma \alpha \Gamma)$ on
$\mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma \times \mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma$, the locus of $z \times \alpha(z)$ modulo
$\Gamma \times \Gamma$, and the endomorphism of $J$ it defines is

$$\{\Gamma \alpha \Gamma\} = (\pi_2)_* \circ \pi_1^* \colon J \to J,$$

with $\pi_1^*$ the [pullback](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/algebraic-curves/jacobians-of-compact-riemann-surfaces/pullback.md)
and $(\pi_2)_*$ the [pushforward](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/algebraic-curves/jacobians-of-compact-riemann-surfaces/pushforward.md)
on Jacobians. On divisors, it sends the point $[z]$ of $\mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma$, for $z \in \mathfrak{H}$
whose image is not a branch point of $\pi_1$, to $\sum_\nu [\alpha_\nu z]$, where
$\Gamma \alpha \Gamma = \bigsqcup_\nu \Gamma \alpha_\nu$.

For a positive integer $n$, let $q$ be the largest divisor of $n$ prime to $N$ and $m = n / q$. The
**Hecke endomorphism** $\xi_n$ of $J$ is

$$\xi_n = \xi_m \xi_q, \qquad \xi_m = \{\Gamma \alpha \Gamma\} \text{ with } \alpha = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & m \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \xi_q = \sum_\beta \{\Gamma \beta \Gamma\},$$

the last sum over the same double cosets $\Gamma \beta \Gamma$ as in the definition of the
[Hecke operators $T_n$](hecke-operators-of-level-n.md): $\beta \in M_2(\mathbb{Z})$ with $\det \beta = q$ and
$\beta \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & q \end{pmatrix} \pmod N$. The $\xi_n$ are endomorphisms of
the abelian variety $J$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, that is, they are defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, and $\xi_1$ is the
identity.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the compact Riemann surface $\mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma'$ of a finite-index subgroup $\Gamma'$ of
  $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ and the holomorphic maps $\pi_1$, $\pi_2$, with the finiteness of
  $\Gamma_\alpha$ in $\Gamma$;
- the identification of $J(\mathbb{C})$, for the standard model of $X_\Gamma$, with the Jacobian of
  $\mathfrak{H}^*/\Gamma$, through which an endomorphism of the complex Jacobian is an endomorphism of
  $J_{\mathbb{C}}$;
- Weil's homomorphism of Jacobians attached to a correspondence, and its agreement with
  $(\pi_2)_* \circ \pi_1^*$;
- the rationality of the $\{\Gamma \alpha \Gamma\}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ for the standard model, which
  makes each $\xi_n$ an endomorphism of $J$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ and which Shimura (1973) uses in the
  proof of Theorem 1 (p. 526) without a locator.
