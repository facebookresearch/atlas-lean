# Legendre form of the j-invariant

For an element $t$ of a field $K$, $j(t) = 2^8(t^2 - t + 1)^3/(t^2(t - 1)^2)$, with division by zero giving $0$, so $j(0) = j(1) = 0$. When the characteristic of $K$ is not $2$ and $t \notin \{0, 1\}$, this is the $j$-invariant of the Legendre elliptic curve $y^2 = x(x - 1)(x - t)$. For $t \in \{0, 1\}$ that cubic is singular and the formula has no such interpretation.
