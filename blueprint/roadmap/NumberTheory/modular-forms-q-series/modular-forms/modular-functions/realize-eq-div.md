# Realization of a Laurent series is a quotient of forms

Let $N$ be a natural number, $k$ an integer, $g, h$ modular forms of weight $k$ for $\Gamma_0(N)$ and $x$ a complex Laurent series with $x \cdot \tilde h = \tilde g$, where $\tilde g, \tilde h$ are the $q$-expansions. If $h(\tau) \ne 0$, then the realization of $x$ at $\tau$ is $g(\tau)/h(\tau)$.
