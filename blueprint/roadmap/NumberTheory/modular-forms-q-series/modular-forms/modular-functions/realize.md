# Realizing a Laurent series as a quotient of modular forms

For a natural number $N$, a complex Laurent series $x$ and $\tau$ in the upper half-plane: if there are modular forms $g, h$ of the same weight for $\Gamma_0(N)$ whose $q$-expansions have quotient $x$, the value is $g(\tau)/h(\tau)$ for such a pair, and $0$ when no such pair exists.
