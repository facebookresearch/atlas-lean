# $c_8(N)$ is even when $15N + 1$ is not a square

Let $c_8$ be the initial-repetitions
partition function of Nyirenda and Mugwangwavari. For every integer $N \ge 0$ such that $15N + 1$
is not a perfect square, $c_8(N)$ is even.

## Sources

- John Greene and James A. Sellers, *Extending Recent Parity Results of Nyirenda and Mugwangwavari for Partitions with Initial Repetitions*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A32, Theorem 11 (`integers-2025-A32-thm-11`).
