# Generating function of $[j, k]$-overpartition pairs with even parts distinct

For natural
numbers $j$ and $k$, let $f_a = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{am})$ be the Euler products. The
generating function of $[j, k]$-overpartition pairs with even parts distinct is the integer power
series $f_4^2 \bigl(\prod_{i \ge 0} (1 + q^{j+ki})\bigr)^2 \, g$, where $g$ is the inverse of
$f_1^2$ (whose constant coefficient is $1$), and the infinite product is the coefficientwise limit
of its finite partial products when that limit exists, and $1$ otherwise. The limit exists exactly
when $k \ge 1$; for $k = 0$ the product is $1$ and the generating function is $f_4^2/f_1^2$. In
$q$-Pochhammer notation it is $(q^4; q^4)_\infty^2 (-q^j; q^k)_\infty^2 / (q; q)_\infty^2$. It is
used for $k \ge j \ge 1$.
