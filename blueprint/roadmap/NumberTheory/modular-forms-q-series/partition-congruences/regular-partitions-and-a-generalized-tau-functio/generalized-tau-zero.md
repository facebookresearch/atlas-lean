# Generalized tau function at zero

For every integer $k$, $\tau_k(0) = 0$, where $\tau_k$
is the generalized tau function: the series $q \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^m)^k$ has no constant term.

## Sources

- S. Sriram and A. David Christopher, *Congruences Relating Regular Partition Functions, a Generalized Tau Function, and Partition Function Weighted Composition Sums*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A39, Definition 1.
