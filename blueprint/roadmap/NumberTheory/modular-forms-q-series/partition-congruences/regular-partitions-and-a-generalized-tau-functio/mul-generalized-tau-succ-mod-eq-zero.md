# Divisibility of $n\,\tau_k(n + 1)$ by $|k|$

Let $\tau_k$ be the generalized tau function.
For every positive integer $n$ and every integer $k$ with $|k| \ge 2$,
$n\,\tau_k(n + 1) \equiv 0 \pmod{|k|}$.

## Sources

- S. Sriram and A. David Christopher, *Congruences Relating Regular Partition Functions, a Generalized Tau Function, and Partition Function Weighted Composition Sums*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A39, Proposition 2 (`integers-2024-A39-prop-2`).
