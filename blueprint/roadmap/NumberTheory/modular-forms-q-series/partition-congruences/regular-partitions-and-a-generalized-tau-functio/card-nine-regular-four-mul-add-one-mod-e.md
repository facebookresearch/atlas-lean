# A congruence for 9-regular partitions at $4n + 1$

Let $R_9(m)$ denote the number of
partitions of $m$ in which no part is divisible by $9$. For every positive integer $n$,
$R_9(4n + 1) \equiv R_9(n) \pmod 3$.
