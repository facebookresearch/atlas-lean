# $PD_2(8n+6)$ is divisible by 4

Let $PD_2(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ with
designated summands in which every part is odd. For every $n \ge 0$,
$PD_2(8n + 6) \equiv 0 \pmod 4$.

## Sources

- James A. Sellers, *New Infinite Families of Congruences Modulo Powers of 2 for 2-Regular Partitions with Designated Summands*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A16, Corollary 2 (`integers-2024-A16-cor-2`).
