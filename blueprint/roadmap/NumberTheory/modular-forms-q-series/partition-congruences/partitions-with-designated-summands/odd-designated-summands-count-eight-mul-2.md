# $PD_2(8n+7)$ is divisible by 8

Let $PD_2(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ with
designated summands in which every part is odd. For every $n \ge 0$,
$PD_2(8n + 7) \equiv 0 \pmod 8$.

## Sources

- James A. Sellers, *New Infinite Families of Congruences Modulo Powers of 2 for 2-Regular Partitions with Designated Summands*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A16, Theorem 9 (`integers-2024-A16-thm-9`).
