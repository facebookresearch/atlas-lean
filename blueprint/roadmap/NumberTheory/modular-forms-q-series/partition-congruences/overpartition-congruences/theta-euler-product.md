# Gauss's product formula for the signed square theta series

Let
$t(q)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}q^{j^2}$ and let $E(q)=\prod_{m\ge 1}(1-q^m)$, written as the
pentagonal series $\sum_{j\in\mathbb{Z}}(-1)^j q^{j(3j-1)/2}$. As integer power series,
$t(-q)E(q^2)=E(q)^2$.

## Sources
