# Euler quotient for regular cubic partitions with odd parts overlined

For a natural number $\ell$, Buragohain and Saikia's Eq. (2) gives the integer power series

$$\frac{f_2 f_\ell^2 f_{4\ell}}{f_1^2 f_4 f_{2\ell}}, \qquad f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km}).$$

For $\ell \ge 1$, the denominator has constant term $1$ and is invertible.
At $\ell=0$ the factor $f_0^2$ makes the series zero.
At $\ell=4$, the quotient equals the generating function obtained by forbidding all parts divisible by four
in cubic partitions with odd parts overlined, multiplied by
$f_{16}/f_8=\prod_{m\ge1}(1+q^{8m})$.

## Sources

- Pujashree Buragohain and Nipen Saikia, *Some New Congruences for $\ell$-Regular Cubic Partitions with Odd Parts Overlined*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A101, Section 1, eq. (2).
