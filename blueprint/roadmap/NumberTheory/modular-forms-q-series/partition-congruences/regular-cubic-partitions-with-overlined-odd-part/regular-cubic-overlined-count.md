# Coefficients of the regular cubic Euler quotient

For natural numbers $\ell$ and $n$,
$a_\ell(n)$ is the coefficient of $q^n$ in Buragohain and Saikia's Eq. (2),

$$\frac{f_2 f_\ell^2 f_{4\ell}}{f_1^2 f_4 f_{2\ell}}, \qquad f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km}).$$

For $\ell \ge 1$ the denominator has constant term $1$.
At $\ell=0$ the factor $f_0^2$ gives $a_0(n)=0$ for all $n$.
For example, $a_3(5)=18$ and $a_4(9)=130$.

## Sources

- Pujashree Buragohain and Nipen Saikia, *Some New Congruences for $\ell$-Regular Cubic Partitions with Odd Parts Overlined*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A101, Section 1, eq. (2).
