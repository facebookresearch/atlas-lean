# Ramanujan's congruence modulo 5

For every natural number $m$, the number of partitions
of $5m + 4$ is divisible by $5$.

## Sources

- Hartosh Singh Bal and Gaurav Bhatnagar, *Glaisher's Divisors and Infinite Products*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.1.6, Proposition 29 (Ramanujan), https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Bhatnagar/bhat4.tex
