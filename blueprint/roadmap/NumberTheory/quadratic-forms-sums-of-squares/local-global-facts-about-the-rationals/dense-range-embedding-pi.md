# The rationals are dense in a finite product of their completions

Let $S$ be a finite set
of places of $\mathbb{Q}$. The image of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\prod_{v \in S} \mathbb{Q}_v$ under the
diagonal map $q \mapsto (q)_{v \in S}$ is dense in the product topology.

- A. V. Sutherland, *18.782 Introduction to Arithmetic Geometry*, MIT, Fall 2013, lecture notes, Lecture 11, Theorem 11.7, p. 2; J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, course notes, version 3.08 (2020), Theorem 7.20 and the remark after it, p. 114.
