# Erdős Problem 496 for positive α (small values of x² + y² − α z²)

The statement that for every irrational $\alpha > 0$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there are positive integers $x, y, z$ with $|x^2 + y^2 - \alpha z^2| < \varepsilon$.

Problem 496 as printed asks this for every irrational $\alpha$; for negative $\alpha$ the form is positive definite and the statement fails, so this is the version for positive irrational $\alpha$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Small values of x² + y² − α z² for positive irrational α](ternary-form-small-positive-value-holds.md).
