# Lagrange's four-square theorem

Every natural number is a sum of four squares: for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there are
$a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{N}$ with $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2 = n$.

## Sources

- G. H. Hardy and E. M. Wright, *An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers*, 6th ed., Oxford University
  Press, 2008: Theorem 369.
