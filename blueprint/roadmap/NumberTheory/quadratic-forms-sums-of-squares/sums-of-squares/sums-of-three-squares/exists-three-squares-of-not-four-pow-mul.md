# Legendre's three-square theorem

Every natural number $n$ that is not of the form
$4^a (8b + 7)$ with natural numbers $a, b$ is a sum of three squares: there are natural numbers
$x, y, z$ with $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = n$.
