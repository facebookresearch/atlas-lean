# Strict coefficient bound for fundamental discriminants

For a Gauss-reduced form
$ax^2 + bxy + cy^2$ such that $-d = b^2 - 4ac$ is a fundamental discriminant and $d \ne 4$,
$c > \sqrt d / 2$. Indeed a fundamental discriminant $-4k^2$ is $4m$ with $m = -k^2$ squarefree,
so $k^2 = 1$ and $d = 4$.

## Sources

- J. Oesterlé, *Nombres de classes des corps quadratiques imaginaires*, Séminaire Bourbaki, vol. 1983/84, exposé 631, Astérisque 121–122 (1985), 309–323, §1.3 (p. 312), "(et même $c > \frac{\sqrt d}{2}$ si $d \ne 4$)".
