# Strict coefficient bound

For a Gauss-reduced form $ax^2 + bxy + cy^2$ with
$d = 4ac - b^2$ not of the form $4k^2$ with $k$ an integer, $c > \sqrt d / 2$.

## Sources

- J. Oesterlé, *Nombres de classes des corps quadratiques imaginaires*, Séminaire Bourbaki, vol. 1983/84, exposé 631, Astérisque 121–122 (1985), 309–323, §1.3 (p. 312), "(et même $c > \frac{\sqrt d}{2}$ si $d \ne 4$)", stated there for $-d$ the discriminant of an imaginary quadratic field.
