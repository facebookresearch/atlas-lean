# Homothetic lattices have the same endomorphism ring

If the lattice of a period pair $L'$
is $c\Lambda$ for the lattice $\Lambda$ of $L$ and a nonzero $c \in \mathbb{C}$, then
$\{a \in \mathbb{C} : a\Lambda' \subseteq \Lambda'\} =
\{a \in \mathbb{C} : a\Lambda \subseteq \Lambda\}$.

## Sources

- Andrew V. Sutherland, *18.783 Elliptic Curves*, lecture notes, MIT OpenCourseWare, Fall 2025, Lecture 17, Lemma 17.9 (properness depends only on the homothety class).
