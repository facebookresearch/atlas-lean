# Proper equivalence of binary quadratic forms

Two integer triples $f$ and $g$, standing for
binary quadratic forms, are properly equivalent if $g = f \cdot \gamma$, that is
$g(x, y) = f(px + qy, rx + sy)$, for some
$\gamma = \begin{pmatrix} p & q \\ r & s \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$; the proper
equivalence classes are the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-orbits of forms.

## Sources

- David Cox, John McKay and Peter Stevenhagen, *Principal moduli and class fields*, Bull. London Math. Soc. 36 (2004), 3–12, §2, which works with the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-orbits of primitive positive definite binary quadratic forms.
