# Substitution of a matrix into a binary quadratic form

For an integer triple $(a, b, c)$,
standing for $f(x, y) = ax^2 + bxy + cy^2$, and
$\gamma = \begin{pmatrix} p & q \\ r & s \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, the triple of
coefficients of the substituted form $(f \cdot \gamma)(x, y) = f(px + qy, rx + sy)$, namely
$(ap^2 + bpr + cr^2,\ 2apq + b(ps + qr) + 2crs,\ aq^2 + bqs + cs^2)$.

## Sources

- David Cox, John McKay and Peter Stevenhagen, *Principal moduli and class fields*, Bull. London Math. Soc. 36 (2004), 3–12, §2, which works with the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$-orbits of forms; the substitution is written out here.
