# Homothety of the lattices of two points of the upper half-plane

For $\tau$ and $\tau'$ in
the upper half-plane $\mathbb{H}$, the lattices $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\tau$ and
$\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\tau'$ are homothetic if and only if
$\tau' = \gamma\tau = \frac{p\tau + q}{r\tau + s}$ for some
$\gamma = \begin{pmatrix} p & q \\ r & s \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$.

## Sources

- Andrew V. Sutherland, *18.783 Elliptic Curves*, lecture notes, MIT OpenCourseWare, Fall 2025, Lecture 15, Lemma 15.9 and its proof, which derives $\tau' = \gamma\tau$ with $\det\gamma = 1$ from the homothety; the converse, by a change of basis of $[1, \tau]$, is written out here.
