# Form classes and proper ideal classes of an imaginary quadratic order

Let $D$ be an [imaginary quadratic discriminant](../../../algebraic-number-theory/quadratic-fields/imaginary-quadratic-discriminants/is-imaginary-quadratic-discriminant.md),
let $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D}) \subset \mathbb{C}$ with $\sqrt{D} = i\sqrt{|D|}$, and let
$\mathcal{O} = \mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\,\frac{D + \sqrt{D}}{2}$ be the order of discriminant $D$ in $K$.
Let $C(\mathcal{O})$ be the class group of $\mathcal{O}$: the proper fractional $\mathcal{O}$-ideals,
those lattices $\mathfrak{a} \subset K$ in $\mathbb{C}$ that are [proper ideals](../../../../Analysis/special-functions/elliptic-functions/complex-multiplication-of-period-lattices/is-proper-ideal.md)
of $\mathcal{O}$, modulo the principal ones. Then:

1. for every primitive positive definite form $f(x, y) = ax^2 + bxy + cy^2$ of discriminant
   $b^2 - 4ac = D$ (so $a > 0$ and $\gcd(a, b, c) = 1$), the lattice
   $\mathfrak{a}_f = \mathbb{Z}a + \mathbb{Z}\,\frac{-b + \sqrt{D}}{2}$ is a proper ideal of $\mathcal{O}$;
2. $f \mapsto [\mathfrak{a}_f]$ induces a bijection from the proper equivalence classes of primitive
   positive definite forms of discriminant $D$ (equivalence under $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$) onto
   $C(\mathcal{O})$.

Since every such class contains exactly one reduced form, the class number $h(\mathcal{O}) =
|C(\mathcal{O})|$ equals the [Gauss class number](../kronecker-s-class-number-sums/gauss-class-number.md)
$h(D)$, the number of reduced primitive positive definite forms of discriminant $D$.

If $\tau$ in the upper half-plane is the root of $a\tau^2 + b\tau + c = 0$ with $a > 0$, then
$\tau = \frac{-b + \sqrt{D}}{2a}$ and the lattice $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\tau = a^{-1}\mathfrak{a}_f$ is
homothetic to $\mathfrak{a}_f$; so its endomorphism ring is $\mathcal{O}$, and $j(\tau) = j(\mathfrak{a}_f)$. Here
$j(\tau) = E_4(\tau)^3/\Delta(\tau)$ is the [modular $j$-function](../../../not-yet-classified/level-nalgebra/j-function.md) and
$j(\Lambda) = 1728\, g_2(\Lambda)^3/(g_2(\Lambda)^3 - 27\, g_3(\Lambda)^2)$ the $j$-invariant of a lattice $\Lambda$, with
$g_2$ and $g_3$ its Eisenstein invariants. The two agree on $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\tau$: by
[Jacobi's product formula](../../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/lattice-discriminant-eta-product.md), $g_2 = \frac{4\pi^4}{3} E_4$ and $g_2^3 - 27 g_3^2 = (2\pi)^{12} \Delta$ there, so
$1728\, g_2^3/(g_2^3 - 27 g_3^2) = 1728 \cdot \frac{64\pi^{12}}{27} E_4^3 / \bigl((2\pi)^{12} \Delta\bigr) = E_4^3/\Delta$; and
$j(\Lambda)$ is unchanged when $\Lambda$ is replaced by a homothetic lattice, as $g_2(\lambda\Lambda) = \lambda^{-4} g_2(\Lambda)$ and
$g_3(\lambda\Lambda) = \lambda^{-6} g_3(\Lambda)$. The related [ideal class of a binary quadratic form](class-of-form.md), whose natural number $D$ is
$|D|$ here, is the class, in the class group of the ring of integers of $K$, of the ideal generated by
$a$ and $\frac{b + i\sqrt{|D|}}{2} = \frac{b + \sqrt{D}}{2}$; for a fundamental discriminant $D$ that
ideal is the complex conjugate $\overline{\mathfrak{a}_f} = \mathbb{Z}a + \mathbb{Z}\,\frac{b + \sqrt{D}}{2}$
of $\mathfrak{a}_f$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the endomorphism ring of the lattice $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\tau$ is the order
  $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}a\tau$ of discriminant $b^2 - 4ac$, for $\tau$ a root of the primitive equation
  $a\tau^2 + b\tau + c = 0$;
- the equivalence of proper and invertible ideals of an imaginary quadratic order;
- the bijection itself, through the action of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ on the root $\tau$;
- the uniqueness of the reduced form in each proper equivalence class.

Each page states its result precisely, with its locator in the source it follows.

Number Theory 158 (2016), 397–410, [arXiv:1410.1806](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.1806), proof of Proposition 2.5 (numbered as in the arXiv version): for $(a, b, c)$ in the set $T_\Delta$ of reduced primitive triples of discriminant $\Delta$ ($-a < b \le a < c$ or $0 \le b \le a = c$), the lattice $\langle 1, \tau(a, b, c) \rangle$ with $\tau(a, b, c) = \frac{b + \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}$ is an invertible fractional ideal of the order $\mathcal{O}$ of discriminant $\Delta$, and $(a, b, c) \mapsto$ its class is a bijection from $T_\Delta$ onto $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal{O})$, by the joint application of David A. Cox, *Primes of the form* $x^2 + ny^2$, Wiley, New York, 1989, Theorems 2.8 and 7.7. Their $\tau(a, b, c)$ is the root $\frac{-b' + \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}$ of the form $(a, b', c)$ with $b' = -b$, so their bijection is the one above composed with $(a, b, c) \mapsto (a, -b, c)$.
