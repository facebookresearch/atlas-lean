# The substitution of a translate

For integers $a, b, c, l$, the substitution
$(x, y) \mapsto (x + ly, y)$ carries $(a, b, c)$ to the properly equivalent form
$(a, b + 2al, al^2 + bl + c)$.

## Sources

- Anthony W. Knapp, *Advanced Algebra*, digital second edition, 2016, Chapter I, §4, p. 26, the paragraph before Lemma 1.10: the computation with $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & l \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, "valid for any integer $l$".
