# The first coefficient of a form of nonsquare discriminant is not zero

If the discriminant
$b^2 - 4ac$ of $(a, b, c)$ is not the square of an integer, then $a \ne 0$.

## Sources

- Anthony W. Knapp, *Advanced Algebra*, digital second edition, 2016, Chapter I, §3, p. 14: "Since $D = b^2 - 4ac$ is assumed not to be the square of an integer, neither $a$ nor $c$ can be $0$."
