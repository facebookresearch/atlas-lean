# Fowler's lower bound for class numbers of fundamental discriminants

For every fundamental discriminant $D < 0$, the Gauss class number $h(D)$ satisfies

$$h(D)^2 \ge \frac{\log |D|}{42000}, \qquad \text{that is} \qquad h(D) \ge \frac{1}{\sqrt{42000}} (\log |D|)^{1/2},$$

where $\log$ is the natural logarithm.

## Proof depends on

- [Oesterlé's effective lower bound for class numbers](oesterle-class-number-lower-bound.md), for $h(D) \ge \vartheta(|D|) \log |D| / 7000$; as $\vartheta(|D|) > 0$ and $|D| \ge 3$, it also gives $h(D) \ge 1$
- [Genus theory bound on the prime divisors of a fundamental discriminant](genus-two-power-dvd-class-number.md), for at most $v_2(h(D))$ factors in $\vartheta(|D|)$
- [Lower bound for Oesterlé's product](oesterle-product-lower-bound.md), with $v = v_2(h(D))$, for $\vartheta(|D|) \ge \tfrac16\, 2^{-v_2(h(D))} \ge 1 / (6\, h(D))$
