# Twenty-three is not a sum of eight cubes

The number $23$ is not a sum of $8$ cubes of
natural numbers: the only cubes at most $23$ are $0$, $1$ and $8$, and a sum of $a$ eights and
$b$ ones equal to $23$ has $a \le 2$, hence $a + b = 23 - 7a \ge 9$ terms.

## Sources

- direct check; this is the sharpness half of Wieferich's theorem `wieferich_g_three`.
