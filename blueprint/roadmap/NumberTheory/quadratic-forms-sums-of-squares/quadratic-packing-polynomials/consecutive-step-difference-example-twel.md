# Step difference of the slope-$12/7$ example

Let
$P(x, y) = 6(x - y/2)(x - y/2 - 3/2) + x + y + 2$. For $m = 7$ and $n = 12$ (the sector of
slope $12/7$), the step difference of $P$ is $k = 3$: with $l = \gcd(6, 12) = 6$, one has
$P(1, 2) - P(0, 0) = 3$.
