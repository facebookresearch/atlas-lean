# The Dedekind zeta coefficient at $1$

For every number field $K$, $a_K(1) = 1$, where
$a_K(n)$ is the number of ideals of the ring of integers of $K$ of absolute norm $n$: the unit
ideal is the only ideal of norm $1$.
