# Fourth moment of the $L$-function modulo $4$

Let $\chi$ be a nontrivial Dirichlet
character modulo $4$ and let $\varepsilon > 0$. Then there is a real constant $C$ such that for
every real $T \ge 1$, $\int_1^T |L(1/2 + it, \chi)|^4 \, dt \le C\, T^{1 + \varepsilon}$.

## Sources

- Naveen K. Godara and Prashant Tiwari, *The Higher Power Moments of the Coefficients of the Dedekind Zeta Function over a Polynomial in Six Variables*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A101, Lemma 5, third display (`integers-2025-A101-lem-5`).
