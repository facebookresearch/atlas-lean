# Summability of Dedekind zeta terms for σ > 1

Let $F$ be a number field with ring of integers
$\mathcal{O}_F$, and let $\sigma > 1$ be a real number. Then the family
$N(\mathfrak{p})^{-\sigma}$, indexed by the nonzero prime ideals $\mathfrak{p}$ of
$\mathcal{O}_F$, is summable, where $N(\mathfrak{p}) = |\mathcal{O}_F / \mathfrak{p}|$ is the
absolute norm.
