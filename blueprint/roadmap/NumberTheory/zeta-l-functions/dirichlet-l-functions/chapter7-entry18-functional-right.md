# Right side of the functional equation of the Dirichlet beta function

For $r \in \mathbb{C}$, $\cos(\pi r/2)(\pi/2)^{r-1}\Gamma(1 - r)\beta(1 - r)$, where $\beta(s) = L(s, \chi_4)$ is the Dirichlet beta function, the L-function of the nontrivial character $\chi_4$ modulo $4$ (so $\beta(s) = \sum_{k \ge 0} (-1)^k/(2k+1)^s$ for $\operatorname{Re} s > 0$).
