# Right member of Ramanujan's Entry 18

For $r \in \mathbb{C}$, $(\pi/2)^r E^*(r)/\Gamma(r)$, where $E^*(s) = 2\Gamma(s)\beta(s)/(\pi/2)^s$ and $\beta(s) = L(s, \chi_4)$ is the Dirichlet beta function, the L-function of the nontrivial character $\chi_4$ modulo $4$ (so $\beta(s) = \sum_{k \ge 0} (-1)^k/(2k+1)^s$ for $\operatorname{Re} s > 0$).
