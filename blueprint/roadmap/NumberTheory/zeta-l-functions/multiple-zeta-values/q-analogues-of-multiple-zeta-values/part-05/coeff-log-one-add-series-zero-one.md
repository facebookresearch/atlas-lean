# Low-order coefficients of the logarithm series

The power series
$\ln(1 + T) = \sum_{n \ge 1} (-1)^{n+1} T^n / n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ has constant coefficient $0$
and coefficient $1$ at $T$.
