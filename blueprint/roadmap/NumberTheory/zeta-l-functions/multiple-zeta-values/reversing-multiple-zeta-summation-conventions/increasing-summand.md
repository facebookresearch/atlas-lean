# Summand of the increasing multiple-zeta series

Let $k = (k_0, \dots, k_{d-1})$ be an
index of depth $d$. To a strictly increasing tuple $m = (m_0, \dots, m_{d-1})$ of positive
natural numbers it attaches the real number

$$\Bigl(\prod_{i=0}^{d-1} m_i^{k_i}\Bigr)^{-1}.$$

This is the convention of the papers listed below.

## Sources

- Kwang-Wu Chen, Minking Eie and Yao Lin Ong, *Some Symmetry and Duality Theorems on Multiple Zeta(-star) Values*, arXiv:2304.08722.
- Z. Li, Z. Wang and L. Zhang, *A proof of a weighted sum conjecture for finite multiple zeta values of level two*, arXiv:2608.10675.
