# Regrouping Euler products along an extension of number fields

Let $K/F$ be an extension of number fields with $\mathcal{O}_K$ integral over $\mathcal{O}_F$, let $c$ be a function from the nonzero primes of $\mathcal{O}_K$ to $\mathbb{C}$, and let $s, Z \in \mathbb{C}$. If the product over the nonzero primes $\mathfrak{P}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ of $(1 - c(\mathfrak{P}) N\mathfrak{P}^{-s})^{-1}$ converges (unconditionally) to $Z$, then so does the product over the nonzero primes $\mathfrak{p}$ of $\mathcal{O}_F$ of $P_{\mathfrak{p}}(N\mathfrak{p}^{-s})^{-1}$, where $P_{\mathfrak{p}}(X) = \prod_{\mathfrak{P} \mid \mathfrak{p}} (1 - c(\mathfrak{P}) X^{f(\mathfrak{P})})$ is the [induced Euler polynomial](induced-euler-poly.md), $f(\mathfrak{P}) = [\kappa(\mathfrak{P}) : \kappa(\mathfrak{p})]$ is the [residue degree of $\mathfrak{P}$ relative to $\mathfrak{p}$](induced-factor.md) in $K/F$ (not the absolute residue degree), and $N$ the absolute norm.

Inversion is in $\mathbb{C}$ with $0^{-1} = 0$, so a zero local factor contributes $0$ rather than a pole; for example $c(\mathfrak{P}) = 1$ and $s = 0$ give the local factor $1 - 1 = 0$. This is separate from the convention for unconditional products.
