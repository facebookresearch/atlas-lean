# Induced Euler polynomial

Let $K/F$ be an extension of number fields with $\mathcal{O}_K$ integral over $\mathcal{O}_F$, let $R$ be a commutative ring and $c$ a function from the nonzero primes of $\mathcal{O}_K$ to $R$. For a nonzero prime $\mathfrak{p}$ of $\mathcal{O}_F$, the polynomial $\prod_{\mathfrak{P} \mid \mathfrak{p}} \bigl(1 - c(\mathfrak{P}) X^{f(\mathfrak{P})}\bigr) \in R[X]$, the finite product over the primes $\mathfrak{P}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ above $\mathfrak{p}$, where $f(\mathfrak{P})$ is the inertia degree.
