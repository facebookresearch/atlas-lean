# Local factor of an induced Euler polynomial

Let $K/F$ be an extension of number fields with $\mathcal{O}_K$ integral over $\mathcal{O}_F$, let $R$ be a commutative ring and $c$ a function from the nonzero primes of $\mathcal{O}_K$ to $R$. For a nonzero prime $\mathfrak{P}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$, the polynomial $1 - c(\mathfrak{P}) X^{f(\mathfrak{P})} \in R[X]$, where $f(\mathfrak{P})$ is the inertia degree of $\mathfrak{P}$ over the prime of $\mathcal{O}_F$ below it.
