# Recurrence for the signed stride sum

Let $q \ge 1$, $n \ge 1$ and $t \ge 0$ be integers
and let $T_{q,t}$ denote the signed stride sum. Then
$T_{q,t}(n + q) = 1/(n + q)^t + (-1)^{q+1} T_{q,t}(n)$.
