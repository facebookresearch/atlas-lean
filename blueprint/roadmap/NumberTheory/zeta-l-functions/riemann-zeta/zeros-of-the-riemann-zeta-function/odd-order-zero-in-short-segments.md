# First Hardy–Littlewood zeta function conjecture

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $T_0$ such that for every $T > T_0$ some $t$ with $T < t \le T + T^{1/4 + \varepsilon}$ is the ordinate of a zero of odd order of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line (the order of vanishing of $\zeta$ at $1/2 + it$ is odd).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Zeros of odd order of zeta in short segments of the critical line](odd-order-zero-in-short-segments-holds.md).

## Sources

- G. H. Hardy and J. E. Littlewood, Contributions to the theory of the Riemann zeta-function and the theory of the distribution of primes, Acta Math. 41 (1916), 119–196, Theorem 4.41.
