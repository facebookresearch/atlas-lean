# Montgomery–Taylor window constant

This is $c_{\mathrm{MT}}$, not the window functional
$R(\psi_{\mathrm{MT}})$. For $\psi_{\mathrm{MT}}(s)=\cos(\sqrt{2}\,s)\mathbf{1}_{[-1/2,1/2]}(s)$,
$R(\psi_{\mathrm{MT}})=c_{\mathrm{MT}}^{-1}=1/2+\cot(1/\sqrt{2})/\sqrt{2}$.
The closed form for $c_{\mathrm{MT}}$ is
$\sqrt{2}\tan(1/\sqrt{2})/(1+\tan(1/\sqrt{2})/\sqrt{2})$.

## Sources

- Alpöge and Furman, [arXiv:2608.13637v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13637v2), Theorem A, (2.6), and Lemma 5.6 (5.13); Appendix A gives the closed form.
