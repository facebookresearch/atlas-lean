# Logarithmic bound for $\zeta'/\zeta$ in the classical zero-free region

There are constants $c_1 > 0$, $t_0 \ge 1$ and $C$ such that

$$
\left| \frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)} \right| \le C \log(2 + |t|)
$$

for every $s = \sigma + it$ with $|t| \ge t_0$ and $\sigma \ge 1 - c_1 / \log(2 + |t|)$.

The constant $c_1$ is smaller than the constant of the classical zero-free region, so that the
region stays at a distance of order $1/\log(2 + |t|)$ from the zeros of $\zeta$. The proof bounds
the real part of $-\zeta'/\zeta$ from above, using the approximate formula for $\zeta'/\zeta$ in
terms of nearby zeros together with the classical zero-free region, and then bounds
$\zeta'/\zeta$ itself with the Borel–Carathéodory theorem.
