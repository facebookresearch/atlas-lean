# Even zeta values and Bernoulli numbers

For every integer $k \ge 1$, the series $\sum_{n \ge 1} 1/n^{2k}$ converges, and

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2k}} = (-1)^{k+1}\, \frac{2^{2k-1}\, \pi^{2k}\, B_{2k}}{(2k)!},$$

where $B_{2k}$ is the $2k$-th Bernoulli number.
