# Stirling–Ramanujan constants

For an integer $n$, a real number $S$ is the $n$-th Stirling–Ramanujan constant when $S$ is the Ramanujan sum of $k \mapsto k^n\log k$ if $n \ge 0$, and of $k \mapsto k^{n}$ if $n < 0$; here a real number $S$ is the Ramanujan sum of $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ when there are polynomials $A, B \in \mathbb{Q}[s]$ with $B(0) = 0$ and $\sum_{k=1}^{s} a(k) = A(s)\log s + B(s) + S + O(1/s)$ as $s \to \infty$.

## Sources

- V. Muñoz and R. Pérez-Marco, Stirling–Ramanujan constants are exponential periods, Commun. Number Theory Phys. 18 (2024), 485–507, where the constants are also evaluated, e.g. $S_{-1} = \gamma$ and $S_n = \zeta(-n)$ for $n \le -2$.
