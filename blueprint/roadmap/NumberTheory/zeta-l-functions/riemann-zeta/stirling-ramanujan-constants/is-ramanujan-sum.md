# Ramanujan sums in the Euler–Maclaurin setting

A real number $S$ is the Ramanujan sum of $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ when there are polynomials $A, B \in \mathbb{Q}[s]$ with $B(0) = 0$ and $\sum_{k=1}^{s} a(k) = A(s)\log s + B(s) + S + O(1/s)$ as $s \to \infty$.

## Sources
