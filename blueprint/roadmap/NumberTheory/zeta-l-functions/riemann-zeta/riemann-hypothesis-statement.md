# Statement of the Riemann hypothesis

The Riemann hypothesis is the proposition that every zero of the Riemann zeta function other than
the trivial zeros and the point $1$ has real part $1/2$: for every complex number $s$ with
$\zeta(s) = 0$, $s \ne -2(n + 1)$ for every natural number $n$, and $s \ne 1$,

$$\operatorname{Re} s = \tfrac{1}{2}.$$

Here $\zeta$ is the meromorphic continuation of $\sum_{n \ge 1} n^{-s}$; at its pole $s = 1$ it is
given a finite value, so the condition $s \ne 1$ is part of the statement.
