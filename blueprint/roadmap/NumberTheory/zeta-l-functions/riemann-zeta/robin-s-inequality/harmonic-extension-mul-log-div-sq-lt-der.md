# Lower bound for the derivative $H'(x)$

With $H$ the real extension of the harmonic numbers
and $\log$ the natural logarithm, every real $x \ge 4$ satisfies

$$H'(x) > \frac{H(x) \log H(x)}{x^2}.$$
