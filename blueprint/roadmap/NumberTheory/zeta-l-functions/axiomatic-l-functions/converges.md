# Convergent L-data

For an L-datum, the datum converges when for every $s$ with $\operatorname{Re} s > \sigma_0$ the sums $\sum_i |E_i(N_i^{-s}) - 1|$ and $\sum_i |E^\vee_i(N_i^{-s}) - 1|$ converge and $L(s) \ne 0$, $L^\vee(s) \ne 0$, where $L(s) = \prod_i E_i(N_i^{-s})^{-1}$ and $L^\vee(s) = \prod_i E^\vee_i(N_i^{-s})^{-1}$.
