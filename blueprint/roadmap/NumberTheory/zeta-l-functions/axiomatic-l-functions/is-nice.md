# Nice L-data

An L-datum is nice when it is well formed, it converges, and there are bad data $B$, $B^\vee$, a constant $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{C}$, a real $N > 0$, and entire functions $\Lambda$, $\Lambda^\vee$ bounded on every vertical strip $a \le \operatorname{Re} s \le b$, such that for $\operatorname{Re} s > \sigma_0$, $\Lambda(s) B(s) = \gamma(s) L(s)$ and $\Lambda^\vee(s) B^\vee(s) = \gamma^\vee(s) L^\vee(s)$, and for all $s$, $\Lambda(s) = \varepsilon N^{c - s} \Lambda^\vee(2c - s)$. Here the datum is well formed when every $N_i \ge 2$, every $E_i$ and $E^\vee_i$ has constant term $1$ and degree at most $d$, and every shift $\mu$ in the four multisets has $-\operatorname{Re}\mu \le \sigma_0$; the datum converges when for every $s$ with $\operatorname{Re} s > \sigma_0$ the sums $\sum_i |E_i(N_i^{-s}) - 1|$ and $\sum_i |E^\vee_i(N_i^{-s}) - 1|$ converge and $L(s) \ne 0$, $L^\vee(s) \ne 0$; $L(s) = \prod_i E_i(N_i^{-s})^{-1}$, $L^\vee(s) = \prod_i E^\vee_i(N_i^{-s})^{-1}$, $\gamma(s) = \prod_{\mu \in \Gamma_\mathbb{R}} \Gamma_\mathbb{R}(s + \mu) \prod_{\mu \in \Gamma_\mathbb{C}} \Gamma_\mathbb{C}(s + \mu)$, $\gamma^\vee(s) = \prod_{\mu \in \Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{R}} \Gamma_\mathbb{R}(s + \mu) \prod_{\mu \in \Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{C}} \Gamma_\mathbb{C}(s + \mu)$, $\Gamma_\mathbb{R}(s) = \pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)$ and $\Gamma_\mathbb{C}(s) = 2(2\pi)^{-s}\Gamma(s)$; for a multiset $B$ of pairs $(n, Q)$ with $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $Q \in \mathbb{C}[X]$, $B(s) = \prod_{(n, Q) \in B} Q(n^{-s})$; and a multiset of pairs $(n, Q)$ is bad data when every $n \ge 2$ and every $Q$ has constant term $1$.
