# L-data

An L-datum indexed by a type $\iota$ consists of norms $N_i \in \mathbb{N}$, Euler polynomials $E_i$ and dual Euler polynomials $E^\vee_i$ in $\mathbb{C}[X]$ for $i \in \iota$, four multisets of complex shifts $\Gamma_\mathbb{R}$, $\Gamma_\mathbb{C}$, $\Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{R}$, $\Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{C}$, a real abscissa $\sigma_0$, a real centre $c$, and a degree $d \in \mathbb{N}$.
