# Well-formed L-data

For an L-datum, the datum is well formed when every $N_i \ge 2$, every $E_i$ and $E^\vee_i$ has constant term $1$ and degree at most $d$, and every shift $\mu$ in the four multisets has $-\operatorname{Re}\mu \le \sigma_0$; here an L-datum indexed by a type $\iota$ consists of norms $N_i \in \mathbb{N}$, Euler polynomials $E_i$ and dual Euler polynomials $E^\vee_i$ in $\mathbb{C}[X]$ for $i \in \iota$, four multisets of complex shifts $\Gamma_\mathbb{R}$, $\Gamma_\mathbb{C}$, $\Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{R}$, $\Gamma^\vee_\mathbb{C}$, a real abscissa $\sigma_0$, a real centre $c$, and a degree $d \in \mathbb{N}$.
