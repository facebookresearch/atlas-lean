# Bad Euler factor from local polynomials

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$, for a multiset $B$ of pairs $(n, Q)$ with $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $Q \in \mathbb{C}[X]$, $B(s) = \prod_{(n, Q) \in B} Q(n^{-s})$.

The norms are natural numbers and $n^{-s}$ is the complex power, so for $n = 0$ it is $0^0 = 1$ when $s = 0$ and $0^{-s} = 0$ otherwise, unlike exponentiation of a positive norm.
