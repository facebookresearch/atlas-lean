# The p-adic valuation of a product of nonzero integers is the sum of their p-adic valuations

Let $p$ be a prime number. For a nonzero integer $n$, let $v_p(n)$ be the largest natural number $k$ such that $p^k$ divides $n$. Then $v_p(ab) = v_p(a) + v_p(b)$ for all nonzero integers $a$ and $b$.
