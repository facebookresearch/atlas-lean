# The ring of p-adic integers

Let $p$ be a prime number and let $\mathbb{Q}_p$ be the field of $p$-adic numbers, with its $p$-adic absolute value $|\cdot|_p$. The *ring of $p$-adic integers* is the subring $\mathbb{Z}_p = \{x \in \mathbb{Q}_p : |x|_p \le 1\}$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, the closed unit ball.
