# p-adic integrality is equivalent to norm at most one

Let $p$ be a prime. An element $x$ of the algebraic closure
$\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is integral over $\mathbb{Z}_p$ if and only if
$\lVert x \rVert \le 1$, where $\lVert \cdot \rVert$ is the norm on $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p$
extending the $p$-adic norm.
