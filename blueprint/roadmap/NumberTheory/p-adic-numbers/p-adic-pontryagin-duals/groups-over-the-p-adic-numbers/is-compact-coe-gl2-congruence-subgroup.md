# Principal congruence subgroups of GL₂(ℚ_p) are compact

For every prime $p$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the principal congruence subgroup of level $p^n$ in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ is compact, where for a prime $p$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the principal congruence subgroup of level $p^n$ is the subgroup of $g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ such that every entry of $g - I$ and of $g^{-1} - I$ has $p$-adic norm at most $p^{-n}$.
