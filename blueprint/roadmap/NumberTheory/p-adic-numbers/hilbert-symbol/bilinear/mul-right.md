# The $p$-adic Hilbert symbol is multiplicative in its second argument

For every prime $p$
and all nonzero $a, b, b' \in \mathbb{Q}_p$, the $p$-adic Hilbert symbol satisfies

$$(a, b b')_p = (a, b)_p \, (a, b')_p.$$

## Sources

- J.-P. Serre, *A Course in Arithmetic*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 7, Springer, 1973, Chapter III, §1.2, Theorem 2, p. 20 (bilinearity, the case $k = \mathbb{Q}_p$), from formula v) of §1.1 and the symmetry $(a, b) = (b, a)$ of Proposition 2 i), p. 19.
