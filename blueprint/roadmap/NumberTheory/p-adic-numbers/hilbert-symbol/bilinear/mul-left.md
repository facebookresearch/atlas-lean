# The $p$-adic Hilbert symbol is multiplicative in its first argument

For every prime $p$
and all nonzero $a, a', b \in \mathbb{Q}_p$, the $p$-adic Hilbert symbol satisfies

$$(a a', b)_p = (a, b)_p \, (a', b)_p.$$

## Sources

- J.-P. Serre, *A Course in Arithmetic*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 7, Springer, 1973, Chapter III, §1.1, formula v), p. 20, which Theorem 2 of §1.2, p. 20, states as bilinearity (the case $k = \mathbb{Q}_p$).
