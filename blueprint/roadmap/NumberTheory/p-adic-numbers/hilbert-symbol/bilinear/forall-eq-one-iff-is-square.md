# The $p$-adic Hilbert symbol is nondegenerate

Let $p$ be a prime and let
$b \in \mathbb{Q}_p$ be nonzero. Then $(a, b)_p = 1$ for every nonzero $a \in \mathbb{Q}_p$ if and
only if $b$ is a square in $\mathbb{Q}_p^\times$.

## Sources

- J.-P. Serre, *A Course in Arithmetic*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 7, Springer, 1973, Chapter III, §1.2, Theorem 2, p. 20: "if $b \in k^*$ is such that $(a, b) = 1$ for all $a \in k^*$, one has $b \in k^{*2}$", in the case $k = \mathbb{Q}_p$. The converse is Proposition 2 i), p. 19.
