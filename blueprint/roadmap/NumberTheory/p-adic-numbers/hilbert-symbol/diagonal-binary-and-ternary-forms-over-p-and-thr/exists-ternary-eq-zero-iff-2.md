# When a diagonal ternary form over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ has a nonzero zero

Let $p$ be a prime
($p = 2$ included) and let $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Q}_p$ be nonzero. Then
$a x^2 + b y^2 + c z^2 = 0$ for some $(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{Q}_p^3$ other than $(0, 0, 0)$ if and
only if the $p$-adic Hilbert symbols satisfy $(a, b)_p = (-c, -ab)_p$.

## Sources

- A. V. Sutherland, *18.782 Introduction to Arithmetic Geometry*, MIT, Fall 2013, Lecture 11, Corollary 11.4, p. 1. The proof here goes through `hilbertSymbol.exists_ternary_eq_zero_iff` and bilinearity instead of Sutherland's reduction to Lemma 11.3.
