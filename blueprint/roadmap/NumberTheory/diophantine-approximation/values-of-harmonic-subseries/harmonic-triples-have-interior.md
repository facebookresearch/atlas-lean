# Erdős Problem 268 (open sets of harmonic subseries triples)

The statement that the set of points $\bigl(\sum_{n \in A} 1/n, \sum_{n \in A} 1/(n+1), \sum_{n \in A} 1/(n+2)\bigr)$, as $A$ ranges over the infinite sets of positive integers with $\sum_{n \in A} 1/n < \infty$, has nonempty interior in $\mathbb{R}^3$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Harmonic subseries triples fill an open set](harmonic-triples-have-interior-holds.md).
