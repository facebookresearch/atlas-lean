# Harmonic triple of a set of integers

For a set $A$ of natural numbers, the harmonic triple of $A$ is the point $\bigl(\sum_{n \in A} \frac1n, \sum_{n \in A} \frac1{n+1}, \sum_{n \in A} \frac1{n+2}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ (unconditional sums, $0$ when not summable), where $1/0 = 0$ when $0 \in A$.
