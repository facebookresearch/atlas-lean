# Rational snapping to the circle is within 3δ

For a unit vector $z \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and a rational vector $w$ with $\lvert w_j - z_j\rvert \le \delta$ for each coordinate, where $\delta \le 1/4$, the stereographic snap of $w$ to a rational point of the unit circle lies within distance $3\delta$ of $z$.
