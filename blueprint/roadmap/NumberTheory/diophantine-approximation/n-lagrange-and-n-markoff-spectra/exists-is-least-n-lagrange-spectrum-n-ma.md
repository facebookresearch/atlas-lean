# Common minimum of the $n$-Lagrange and $n$-Markoff spectra

For every integer $n \ge 1$
there is a real number $\alpha$ that is the least element both of the $n$-Lagrange spectrum $L_n$
and of the $n$-Markoff spectrum $M_n$.

## Sources

- Brandon Dong, Soren Dupont, Evan M. O'Dorney and W. Theo Waitkus, *Raney Transducers and the Lowest Point of the n-Lagrange Spectrum*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A47, Proposition 12 (`integers-2025-A47-prop-12`).
