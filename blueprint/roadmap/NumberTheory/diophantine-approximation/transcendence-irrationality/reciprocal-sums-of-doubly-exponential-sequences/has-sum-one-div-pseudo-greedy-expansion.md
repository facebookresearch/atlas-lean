# Convergence of the pseudo-greedy expansion

For every real number $r > 0$, the
pseudo-greedy expansion $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of $r$ satisfies $\sum_n 1 / a_n = r$.

## Sources

- Junnosuke Koizumi, *Irrationality of the Reciprocal Sum of Doubly Exponential Sequences*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A28, Lemma 1 (`integers-2026-A28-lem-1`).
