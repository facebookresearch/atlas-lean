# Erdős Problem 250 (irrationality of the sum of sigma(n)/2^n)

The statement that the real number $\sum_{n \ge 1} \sigma(n)/2^n$ is irrational, where $\sigma(n)$ is the sum of the divisors of $n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The sum of sigma(n)/2^n is irrational](divisor-sum-binary-series-irrational-hol.md).
