# Erdős Problem 69 (irrationality of the sum of omega(n)/2^n)

The statement that the real number $\sum_{n \ge 2} \omega(n)/2^n$ is irrational, where $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
