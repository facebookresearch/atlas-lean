# The squarefree binary series is irrational

The real number $\sum_{n \ge 1} \mu(n)^2 n/2^n$ is irrational, where $\mu$ is the Möbius function (so the sum runs over the squarefree $n$).

This resolves [Erdős Problem 259 (irrationality of the squarefree binary series)](squarefree-binary-series-irrational.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Chen and Ruzsa (1999), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- Y.-G. Chen and I. Z. Ruzsa, On the irrationality of certain series, Period. Math. Hungar. (1999), 31–37.
