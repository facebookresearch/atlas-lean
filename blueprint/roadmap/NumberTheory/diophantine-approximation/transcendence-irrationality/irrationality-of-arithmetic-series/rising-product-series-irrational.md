# Erdős Problem 270 (irrationality of rising-product series)

The statement that for every $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $f(n) \to \infty$, the sum $\sum_{n \ge 1} \frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)\cdots(n+f(n))}$ is irrational.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Rising-product series can be rational](rising-product-series-irrational-refuted.md).
