# All shifted reciprocal sums of a sequence can be rational

It is not the case that for every sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of positive integers with $\sum_n 1/a_n$ convergent there is an integer $t \ge 1$ such that $\sum_n 1/(a_n + t)$ is irrational.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 266 (Stolarsky's conjecture on shifted reciprocal sums)](exists-shift-with-irrational-reciprocal.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Kovač and Tao (2024), cited under Sources.
