# Erdős Problem 266 (Stolarsky's conjecture on shifted reciprocal sums)

The statement that for every sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of positive integers with $\sum_n 1/a_n$ convergent there is an integer $t \ge 1$ such that $\sum_n 1/(a_n + t)$ is irrational.
