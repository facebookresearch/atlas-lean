# Erdős Problem 259 (irrationality of the squarefree binary series)

The statement that the real number $\sum_{n \ge 1} \mu(n)^2 n/2^n$ is irrational, where $\mu$ is the Möbius function (so the sum runs over the squarefree $n$).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The squarefree binary series is irrational](squarefree-binary-series-irrational-hold.md).
