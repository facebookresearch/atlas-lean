# The square root of 2 is irrational

The real number $\sqrt{2}$, the nonnegative square root of $2$, is irrational: it is not equal to any rational number.

## Sources

- G. H. Hardy and E. M. Wright, An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers, 4th ed., Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1960, §4.3, Theorem 43, p. 39.
