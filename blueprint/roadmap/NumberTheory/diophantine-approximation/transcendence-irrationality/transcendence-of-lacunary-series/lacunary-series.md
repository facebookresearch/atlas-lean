# Lacunary power series with coefficients modulo p

For a natural number $p$ and a sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of natural numbers, the power series $\sum_k u_k z^k$ with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, where $u_k = 1$ when $k$ is one of the $n_i$ and $u_k = 0$ otherwise. The modulus $p$ need not be prime (for $p = 4$ the coefficients lie in $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$, and for $p = 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}$); for a prime $p$, the case the conjecture uses, it is a power series over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_p$.
