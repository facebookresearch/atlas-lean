# Loxton–van der Poorten conjecture on lacunary decimal numbers

The statement that for every prime $p$ and every strictly increasing sequence $0 < n_0 < n_1 < \cdots$ of integers, if the power series $\sum_i z^{n_i}$ is algebraic over $\mathbb{F}_p(z)$ in the field $\mathbb{F}_p((z))$ of Laurent series but is not a rational function, then $\sum_i 10^{-n_i}$ is transcendental.

## Sources

- M. Waldschmidt, Open Diophantine Problems, Moscow Math. J. 4 (2004), arXiv:math/0312440, Conjecture 5.4 (Loxton and van der Poorten).
