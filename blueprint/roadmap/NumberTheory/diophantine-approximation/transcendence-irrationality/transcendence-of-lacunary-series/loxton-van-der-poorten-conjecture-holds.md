# Lacunary decimal numbers with algebraic characteristic-p series are transcendental

For every prime $p$ and every strictly increasing sequence $0 < n_0 < n_1 < \cdots$ of integers, if the power series $\sum_i z^{n_i}$ is algebraic over $\mathbb{F}_p(z)$ in the field $\mathbb{F}_p((z))$ of Laurent series but is not a rational function, then $\sum_i 10^{-n_i}$ is transcendental.

This resolves [Loxton–van der Poorten conjecture on lacunary decimal numbers](loxton-van-der-poorten-conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Adamczewski and Bugeaud (2007), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- B. Adamczewski and Y. Bugeaud, On the complexity of algebraic numbers I. Expansions in integer bases, Ann. of Math. (2) 165 (2007), 547–565, arXiv:math/0511674.
