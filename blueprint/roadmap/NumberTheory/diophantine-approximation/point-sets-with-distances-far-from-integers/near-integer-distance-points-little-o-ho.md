# Point sets with distances far from integers have o(X) points

For every $0 < \delta < 1/2$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $X_0$ such that for every $X \ge X_0$, every finite set of points in a closed disc of radius $X$ whose pairwise distances are all at distance at least $\delta$ from the nearest integer has at most $\varepsilon X$ points.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 465, first question](near-integer-distance-points-little-o.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Sárközy (1976), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- A. Sárközy, On distances near integers. I, II, Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. (1976), 37–50, 105–111.
