# Erdős Problem 465, first question

The statement that for every $0 < \delta < 1/2$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $X_0$ such that for every $X \ge X_0$, every finite set of points in a closed disc of radius $X$ whose pairwise distances are all at distance at least $\delta$ from the nearest integer has at most $\varepsilon X$ points.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Point sets with distances far from integers have o(X) points](near-integer-distance-points-little-o-ho.md).
