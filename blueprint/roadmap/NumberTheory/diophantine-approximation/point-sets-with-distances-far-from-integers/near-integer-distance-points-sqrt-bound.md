# Erdős Problem 465, second question

The statement that for every $\delta > 0$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $X_0$ such that for every $X \ge X_0$, every finite set of points in a closed disc of radius $X$ whose pairwise distances are all at distance at least $\delta$ from the nearest integer has fewer than $X^{1/2 + \varepsilon}$ points.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Point sets with distances far from integers have at most X^(1/2 + o(1)) points](near-integer-distance-points-sqrt-bound-2.md).
