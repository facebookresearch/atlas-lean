# Planar point sets with distances far from integers

For reals $X$ and $\delta$, a finite set $P$ of points of the plane is admissible when it lies in some closed disc of radius $X$ and $\|\,|p - q|\,\| \ge \delta$ for all distinct $p, q \in P$, where $\|t\|$ is the distance from a real $t$ to the nearest integer.
