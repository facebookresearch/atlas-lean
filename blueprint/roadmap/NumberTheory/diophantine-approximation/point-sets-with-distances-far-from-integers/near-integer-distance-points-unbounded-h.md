# Large point sets with all distances far from integers

There is $\delta > 0$ such that for every $M$ there is $X_0$ with the following property: for every $X \ge X_0$ some set of at least $M$ points in a closed disc of radius $X$ has all pairwise distances at distance at least $\delta$ from the nearest integer.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 466 (many points with distances far from integers)](near-integer-distance-points-unbounded.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Sárközy (1976), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #466, https://www.erdosproblems.com/466.
- A. Sárközy, On distances near integers. I, II, Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. (1976), 37–50, 105–111.
