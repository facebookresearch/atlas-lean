# Erdős Problem 466 (many points with distances far from integers)

The statement that there is $\delta > 0$ such that for every $M$ there is $X_0$ with the following property: for every $X \ge X_0$ some set of at least $M$ points in a closed disc of radius $X$ has all pairwise distances at distance at least $\delta$ from the nearest integer.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Large point sets with all distances far from integers](near-integer-distance-points-unbounded-h.md).
