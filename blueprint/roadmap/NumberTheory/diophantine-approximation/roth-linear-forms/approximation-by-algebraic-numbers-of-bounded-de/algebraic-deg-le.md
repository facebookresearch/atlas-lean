# Real algebraic numbers of bounded degree

For a natural number $n$, this is the set of
real numbers $\alpha$ that are algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}$ and whose minimal polynomial over
$\mathbb{Q}$ has degree at most $n$.

## Sources

- A. Poëls, *On approximation to a real number by algebraic numbers of bounded degree*, Annals of Math. 201 (1) (2025); its approximants are real algebraic numbers.
