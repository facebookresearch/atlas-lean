# Erdős Problem 1149 (density of n coprime to the integer part of a power)

The statement that for every real $\alpha > 0$ that is not an integer, the natural numbers $n \ge 1$ with $\gcd(n, \lfloor n^\alpha \rfloor) = 1$ have natural density $6/\pi^2$ (the proportion of them among $0, 1, \dots, b-1$ tends to $6/\pi^2$ as $b \to \infty$).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Density of n coprime to the integer part of n to a non-integer power](coprime-floor-power-density-holds.md).
