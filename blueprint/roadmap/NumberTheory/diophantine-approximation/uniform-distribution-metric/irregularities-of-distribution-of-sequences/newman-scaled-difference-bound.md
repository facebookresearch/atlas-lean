# Erdős Problem 480 (Newman's conjecture on scaled differences)

The statement that for every sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in $[0, 1]$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $n \ge 1$ with $n\,|x_{m+n} - x_m| < 5^{-1/2} + \varepsilon$ for infinitely many $m$; that is, $\inf_{n \ge 1} \liminf_{m \to \infty} n\,|x_{m+n} - x_m| \le 5^{-1/2}$.
