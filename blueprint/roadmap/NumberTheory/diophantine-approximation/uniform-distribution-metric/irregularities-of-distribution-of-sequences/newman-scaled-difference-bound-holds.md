# Newman's bound on scaled differences of sequences in [0, 1]

For every sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in $[0, 1]$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $n \ge 1$ with $n\,|x_{m+n} - x_m| < 5^{-1/2} + \varepsilon$ for infinitely many $m$; that is, $\inf_{n \ge 1} \liminf_{m \to \infty} n\,|x_{m+n} - x_m| \le 5^{-1/2}$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 480 (Newman's conjecture on scaled differences)](newman-scaled-difference-bound.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Chung and Graham (1984), cited under Sources.
