# Erdős Problem 997 holds

For every real number $\alpha$, the sequence
$n \mapsto \{\alpha p_n\}$ of fractional parts of $\alpha$ times the primes
$p_0 = 2, p_1 = 3, p_2 = 5, \dots$ is not well-distributed.

## Sources

- Boris Alexeev, Mark Putterman, Mehtaab Sawhney, Mark Sellke, and Gregory Valiant, Short proofs in combinatorics and number theory, arXiv:2603.29961 (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29961. Moved from `OpenConjectures/NumberTheory/ErdosProblem997`. Resolved true: Alexeev, Putterman, Sawhney, Sellke, and Valiant proved that the prime-indexed fractional-part sequence is never well-distributed.
