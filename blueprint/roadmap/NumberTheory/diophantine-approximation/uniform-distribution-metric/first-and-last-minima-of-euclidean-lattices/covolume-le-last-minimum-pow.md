# The covolume of a lattice is at most a power of its last minimum

Let $L$ be a lattice in
a Euclidean space $E$ of dimension $n$: a discrete $\mathbb{Z}$-submodule that spans $E$. Let
$\operatorname{vol}(L)$ be its covolume, for the Haar measure given by an orthonormal basis, and
$\Lambda(L)$ its last minimum. Then $\operatorname{vol}(L) \le \Lambda(L)^n$.

## Sources

- É. Gaudron and G. Rémond, *Polarisations et isogénies*, Duke Math. J. 163 (2014), 2057–2108, §3, p. 2069 (the bound $\operatorname{vol}(\Omega) \le \Lambda(\Omega)^n$, from Hadamard's inequality).
