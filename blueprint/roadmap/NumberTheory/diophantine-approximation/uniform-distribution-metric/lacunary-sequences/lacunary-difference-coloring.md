# Erdős Problem 894 (chromatic number of lacunary Cayley graphs)

The statement that for every lacunary sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of positive integers (some $\varepsilon > 0$ has $a_{k+1} \ge (1 + \varepsilon) a_k$ for every $k$) there is a colouring of $\mathbb{N}$ with finitely many colours such that $b$ and $b + a_k$ have different colours for all $b$ and $k$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Lacunary Cayley graphs on the integers have finite chromatic number](lacunary-difference-coloring-holds.md).
