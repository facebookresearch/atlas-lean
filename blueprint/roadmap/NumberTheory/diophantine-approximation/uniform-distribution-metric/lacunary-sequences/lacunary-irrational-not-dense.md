# Erdős Problem 464 (irrational multiples of lacunary sequences)

The statement that for every lacunary sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of positive integers (some $\varepsilon > 0$ has $n_{k+1} \ge (1 + \varepsilon) n_k$ for every $k$) there is an irrational $\theta$ such that the distances $\|\theta n_k\|$ to the nearest integer are not dense in $[0, 1/2]$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Irrational multiples of a lacunary sequence avoiding density](lacunary-irrational-not-dense-holds.md).
