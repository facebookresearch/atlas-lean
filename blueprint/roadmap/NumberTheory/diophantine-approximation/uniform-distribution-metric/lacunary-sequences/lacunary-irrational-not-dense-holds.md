# Irrational multiples of a lacunary sequence avoiding density

For every lacunary sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of positive integers (some $\varepsilon > 0$ has $n_{k+1} \ge (1 + \varepsilon) n_k$ for every $k$) there is an irrational $\theta$ such that the distances $\|\theta n_k\|$ to the nearest integer are not dense in $[0, 1/2]$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 464 (irrational multiples of lacunary sequences)](lacunary-irrational-not-dense.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is the published work cited under Sources.

## Sources

- B. de Mathan, Numbers contravening a condition in density modulo 1, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. (1980), 237–241.
