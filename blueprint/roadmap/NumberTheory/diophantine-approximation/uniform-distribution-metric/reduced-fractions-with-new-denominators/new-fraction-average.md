# Average proportion of fractions with a new denominator

For a sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of natural numbers and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the average $\frac1N \sum_{k < N} \varphi_A(k)/n_k$, where $\varphi_A(k)$ is the number of $m \in \{1, \dots, n_k\}$ such that $n_k/\gcd(m, n_k)$ divides no $n_j$ with $j < k$ (for positive terms, the number of fractions $m/n_k$ that cannot be written with an earlier denominator). Divisions by zero are read as $0$: the average is $0$ for $N = 0$, a term with $n_k = 0$ contributes $0$, and after a zero term every later count is $0$.
