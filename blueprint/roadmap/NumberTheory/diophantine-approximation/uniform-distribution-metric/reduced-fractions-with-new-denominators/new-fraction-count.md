# Fractions with a new denominator

For a sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of positive integers and a natural number $k$, $\varphi_A(k)$ is the number of $m \in \{1, \dots, n_k\}$ such that $m/n_k$ cannot be written with denominator $n_j$ for any $j < k$: the reduced denominator $n_k/\gcd(m, n_k)$ divides no $n_j$ with $j < k$.
