# Erdős Problem 1000 (averages of new-denominator fraction counts)

The statement that some strictly increasing sequence $n_0 < n_1 < \cdots$ of positive integers has $\frac1N \sum_{k < N} \varphi_A(k)/n_k \to 0$ as $N \to \infty$, where for a sequence $n_0, n_1, \dots$ of positive integers and a natural number $k$, $\varphi_A(k)$ is the number of $m \in \{1, \dots, n_k\}$ such that $m/n_k$ cannot be written with denominator $n_j$ for any $j < k$: the reduced denominator $n_k/\gcd(m, n_k)$ divides no $n_j$ with $j < k$.
