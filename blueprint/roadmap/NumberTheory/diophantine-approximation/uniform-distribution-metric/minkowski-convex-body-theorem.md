# Minkowski's convex body theorem

Let $E$ be a real normed space of finite dimension $n$, with an additive Haar measure $\mu$ on its
Borel $\sigma$-algebra, let $L \subseteq E$ be a countable additive subgroup and let $F$ be a
fundamental domain of $L$ for $\mu$: a null-measurable set whose translates $x + F$, $x \in L$, are
pairwise disjoint up to null sets and cover $E$ up to a null set. If $S \subseteq E$ is convex and
symmetric, $-x \in S$ for $x \in S$, and $\mu(S) > 2^n \mu(F)$, then $S$ contains a nonzero element
of $L$. For a lattice $L$, $\mu(F)$ is its covolume for $\mu$.
