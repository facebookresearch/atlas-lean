# Erdős Problem 1001, existence of the limit (limit measure of well-approximable numbers)

The statement that for all reals $A > 0$ and $c > 1$, the Lebesgue measure of $S(N, A, c)$ converges as $N \to \infty$, where for a natural number $N \ge 1$ and reals $A$ and $c$, $S(N, A, c)$ is the set of $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ with $|\alpha - x/y| < A/y^2$ for some coprime integers $x$ and $y$ with $N \le y \le cN$, and $S(0, A, c) = \emptyset$.

This is the existence part of Problem 1001. The problem also asks for the explicit form of the limit, which this statement does not give.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The measure of well-approximable numbers in a dyadic range converges](approximable-measure-limit-exists-holds.md).
