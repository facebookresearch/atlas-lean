# Well-approximable numbers with denominators in a dyadic range

For a natural number $N \ge 1$ and reals $A$ and $c$, $S(N, A, c)$ is the set of $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ with $|\alpha - x/y| < A/y^2$ for some coprime integers $x$ and $y$ with $N \le y \le cN$. For $N = 0$ the set is empty: the only candidate is $y = 0$, both divisions by zero are read as $0$, and $|\alpha| < 0$ is impossible.
