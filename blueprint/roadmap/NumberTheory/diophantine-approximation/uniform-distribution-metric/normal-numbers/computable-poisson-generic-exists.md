# Becher's question on computable Poisson generic sequences

The statement that there is a computable binary sequence that is Poisson generic, where a binary sequence $x$ is Poisson generic when for every real $\lambda > 0$ and every $k \ge 0$, the proportion of the $2^n$ binary words $w$ of length $n$ occurring exactly $k$ times in $x$ at positions $i < \lfloor \lambda 2^n \rfloor$ tends to $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ as $n \to \infty$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Computable Poisson generic sequences exist](computable-poisson-generic-exists-holds.md).

## Sources

- V. Becher, open problems, Computability Theory, Oberwolfach Report 21/2021, p. 1179.
