# Martin-Löf random binary sequences

A binary sequence $x$ is Martin-Löf random when for every Martin-Löf test $U$, a uniformly computably enumerable sequence $U_0, U_1, \dots$ of sets of finite binary strings such that the sequences with a prefix in $U_m$ have fair-coin measure at most $2^{-m}$, some $U_m$ contains no prefix of $x$.
