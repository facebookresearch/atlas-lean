# Computable Poisson generic sequences exist

There is a computable binary sequence that is Poisson generic, where a binary sequence $x$ is Poisson generic when for every real $\lambda > 0$ and every $k \ge 0$, the proportion of the $2^n$ binary words $w$ of length $n$ occurring exactly $k$ times in $x$ at positions $i < \lfloor \lambda 2^n \rfloor$ tends to $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ as $n \to \infty$.

This resolves [Becher's question on computable Poisson generic sequences](computable-poisson-generic-exists.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Álvarez, Becher and Mereb (2023), cited under Sources.
