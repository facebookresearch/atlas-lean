# Poisson generic binary sequences

A binary sequence $x$ is Poisson generic when for every real $\lambda > 0$ and every $k \ge 0$, the proportion of the $2^n$ binary words $w$ of length $n$ occurring exactly $k$ times in $x$ at positions $i < \lfloor \lambda 2^n \rfloor$ tends to $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ as $n \to \infty$.
