# Schmidt's conjecture on weighted badly approximable pairs

The statement that for any two pairs of weights $(i_1, j_1)$ and $(i_2, j_2)$ with $i_k, j_k \ge 0$ and $i_k + j_k = 1$, the sets $\mathrm{Bad}(i_1, j_1)$ and $\mathrm{Bad}(i_2, j_2)$ intersect; here for weights $i, j$, a point $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ lies in $\mathrm{Bad}(i, j)$ when there is $c > 0$ with $\max\{\|qx\|^{1/i}, \|qy\|^{1/j}\} > c/q$ for every integer $q \ge 1$, where $\|\cdot\|$ is the distance to the nearest integer and $t^{1/0} = 0$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Weighted badly approximable sets intersect](schmidt-weighted-bad-intersect-holds.md).
