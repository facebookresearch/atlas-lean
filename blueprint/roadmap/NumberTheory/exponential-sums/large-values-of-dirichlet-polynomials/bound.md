# The Guth–Maynard large values bound

For a natural number $N$ and real numbers $V$ and
$T$, this is the real number $N^2 V^{-2} + N^{18/5} V^{-4} + T N^{12/5} V^{-4}$, the factor
multiplying $T^{o(1)}$ (as $T \to \infty$) on the right-hand side of the Guth–Maynard large values
estimate for Dirichlet polynomials. Here $V^{-2}$ and $V^{-4}$ are read as $0$ when $V = 0$.

## Sources

- L. Guth and J. Maynard, *New large value estimates for Dirichlet polynomials*, Annals of Math. 203 (2) (2026), Theorem 1.1.
