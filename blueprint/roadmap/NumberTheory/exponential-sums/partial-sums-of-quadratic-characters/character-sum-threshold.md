# Eventual threshold of quadratic character sums

For a real $\varepsilon > 0$ and a prime $p$, $f_\varepsilon(p)$ is the least integer $m \ge 1$ such that $\sum_{n=1}^{N} \left(\frac{n}{p}\right) < \varepsilon N$ for every $N \ge m$, where $\left(\frac{n}{p}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol ($0$ if no such $m$ exists).
