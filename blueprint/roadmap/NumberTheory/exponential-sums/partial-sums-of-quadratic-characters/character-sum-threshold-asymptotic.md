# Erdős Problem 981 (eventual threshold of quadratic character sums)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $c > 0$ such that $\sum_{p} f_\varepsilon(p) \sim c\, x/\log x$ as $x \to \infty$, the sum over the odd primes $p < x$; here for a real $\varepsilon > 0$ and a prime $p$, $f_\varepsilon(p)$ is the least integer $m \ge 1$ such that $\sum_{n=1}^{N} \left(\frac{n}{p}\right) < \varepsilon N$ for every $N \ge m$, where $\left(\frac{n}{p}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol ($0$ if no such $m$ exists).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Average eventual threshold of quadratic character sums](character-sum-threshold-asymptotic-holds.md).
