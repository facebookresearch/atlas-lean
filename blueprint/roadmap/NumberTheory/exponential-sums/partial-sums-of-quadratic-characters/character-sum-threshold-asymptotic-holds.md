# Average eventual threshold of quadratic character sums

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $c > 0$ such that $\sum_{p} f_\varepsilon(p) \sim c\, x/\log x$ as $x \to \infty$, the sum over the odd primes $p < x$; here for a real $\varepsilon > 0$ and a prime $p$, $f_\varepsilon(p)$ is the least integer $m \ge 1$ such that $\sum_{n=1}^{N} \left(\frac{n}{p}\right) < \varepsilon N$ for every $N \ge m$, where $\left(\frac{n}{p}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol ($0$ if no such $m$ exists).

This resolves [Erdős Problem 981 (eventual threshold of quadratic character sums)](character-sum-threshold-asymptotic.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Elliott (1969), cited under Sources.
