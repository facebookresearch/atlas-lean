# Erdős Problem 256 (maxima of products of 1 − z^a on the unit circle)

The statement that there are reals $c > 0$ and $C > 0$ and a natural number $N$ with $\log f(n) \ge C n^c$ for every $n \ge N$, where for $n \ge 1$, $f(n)$ is the infimum over all integers $1 \le a_1 \le \dots \le a_n$ of $\max_{|z| = 1} \bigl|\prod_{i=1}^{n} (1 - z^{a_i})\bigr|$, the maximum taken over the complex unit circle, and $f(0) = 0$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [The least circle maximum of products of 1 − z^a grows slowly](conjecture-refuted.md).
