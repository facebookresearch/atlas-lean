# The least circle maximum of products of 1 − z^a grows slowly

It is not the case that there are reals $c > 0$ and $C > 0$ and a natural number $N$ with $\log f(n) \ge C n^c$ for every $n \ge N$, where for $n \ge 1$, $f(n)$ is the infimum over all integers $1 \le a_1 \le \dots \le a_n$ of $\max_{|z| = 1} \bigl|\prod_{i=1}^{n} (1 - z^{a_i})\bigr|$, the maximum taken over the complex unit circle, and $f(0) = 0$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 256 (maxima of products of 1 − z^a on the unit circle)](conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Belov and Konyagin (1996), cited under Sources.
