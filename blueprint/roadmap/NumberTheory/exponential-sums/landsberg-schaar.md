# Landsberg–Schaar relation

For all positive integers $p$ and $q$,

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}} \sum_{n=0}^{p-1} \exp\left(\frac{2 \pi i n^2 q}{p}\right) =
\frac{e^{\pi i / 4}}{\sqrt{2q}} \sum_{n=0}^{2q-1} \exp\left(-\frac{\pi i n^2 p}{2q}\right).
$$
