# Erdős Problem 987, second question (exponential sums of a sequence)

The statement that some sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ of reals in $(0, 1)$ has $A_k = o(k)$: for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $K$ with $A_k \le \varepsilon k$ for all $k \ge K$, where for a sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ of reals and a natural number $k$, $A_k = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \bigl|\sum_{j < n} e(k x_j)\bigr|$, a value in $[0, \infty]$, where $e(t) = e^{2\pi i t}$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
