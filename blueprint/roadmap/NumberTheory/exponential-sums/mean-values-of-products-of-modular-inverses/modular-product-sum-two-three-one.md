# The modular product sum $S_2(3, 1)$

The modular product sum satisfies $S_2(3, 1) = 5$:
the pairs $(a_1, a_2) \in \{1, 2, 3\}^2$ of numbers coprime to $3$ with $a_1 a_2 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$
are $(1, 1)$ and $(2, 2)$, and $1 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 2 = 5$.
