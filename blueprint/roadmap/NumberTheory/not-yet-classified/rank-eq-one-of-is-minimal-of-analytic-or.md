# Gross–Zagier–Kolyvagin, rank-one case, for a global minimal equation

Let $E$ be an
elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ given by a global minimal Weierstrass equation: its coefficients
are integers and it is minimal at every prime. If its Hasse–Weil $L$-function vanishes to order
exactly one at $s = 1$, then the Mordell–Weil group $E(\mathbb{Q})$ has rank one. The $L$-function
is given as an entire function $F$ that agrees with the $L$-series on the half-plane
$\operatorname{Re}(s) > 3/2$, where the series converges absolutely.

## Sources

- Robert L. Miller, *Proving the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture for specific elliptic curves of analytic rank zero and one*, LMS J. Comput. Math. 14 (2011), 327–350, [Theorem 4.4, p. 336](https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157011000180), in the case $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E) = 1$: "If $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E) \le 1$, then there exists a quadratic field $K$ satisfying the Heegner hypothesis such that $y_K$ is non-torsion. In particular, $r(E) = r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)$, …". The conventions are those of §1, p. 327: "Let $E$ be an elliptic curve defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, given by a global minimal Weierstrass equation. We denote … the rank of the Mordell–Weil group $E(\mathbb{Q})$ by $r$ … Let $L(E/\mathbb{Q}, s)$ be the Hasse–Weil $L$-function of $E$, and denote its order of vanishing at $s = 1$ by $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E/\mathbb{Q})$."
