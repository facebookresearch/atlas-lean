# Degree-one primes carry the logarithmic singularity

For every number field `K` and every
finite set `S₀` of natural numbers, `∑ ℓ ∉ S₀, degOneCount K ℓ * ℓ ^ (-s) = -log (s - 1) + O(1)` as
`s → 1⁺`. The sum runs over the primes of `𝓞 K` of residue degree one that lie over primes outside
`S₀`; the primes over `S₀` and the primes of residue degree at least two contribute `O(1)` to the
prime ideal sum of `FrobeniusDensity.primeSum_toReal_add_log_isBigO`.
