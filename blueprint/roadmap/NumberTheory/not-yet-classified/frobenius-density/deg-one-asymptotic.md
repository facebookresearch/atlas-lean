# Degree-one asymptotic over the fixed fields of a Galois extension

For every subgroup `H`
of `Gal(L/ℚ)` and every finite set `S₀` of natural numbers, with `K = L^H` the fixed field of `H`,
the series `∑ ℓ ∉ S₀, degOneCount K ℓ * ℓ ^ (-s)` converges for every `s > 1`, and its sum plus
`log (s - 1)` is bounded as `s → 1⁺`.
