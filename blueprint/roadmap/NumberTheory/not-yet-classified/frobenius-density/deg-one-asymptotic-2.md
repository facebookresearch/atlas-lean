# Degree-one asymptotic for the fixed fields of a Galois extension

`DegOneAsymptotic L` holds
for every Galois number field `L`: it is `FrobeniusDensity.summable_degOne_term` and
`FrobeniusDensity.degOneSum_add_log_isBigO` applied to each fixed field `L^H`. The hypothesis
`IsGalois ℚ L` is part of the source's statement and is not used by the proof.
