# Bala's supercongruence for A211417

For every prime `p ≥ 5` and every `k ≥ 1`,
`a (p ^ k) ≡ a (p ^ (k - 1))` modulo `p ^ (3 k)`. Conjectured by Peter Bala
(Jan 24 2020) in [A211417](https://oeis.org/A211417).
