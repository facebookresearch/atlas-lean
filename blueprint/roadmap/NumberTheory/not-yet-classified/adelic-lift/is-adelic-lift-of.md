# Is Adelic Lift Of

A function `φ` on adelic `GL(2)` over `ℚ` is an adelic lift of a weight-two cusp form `g` on
`Γ₀(M)` when it is left invariant under `GL(2)` of `ℚ`, right invariant under the finite
level-one subgroup at level `M`, and, at each `h` with trivial finite part whose archimedean
component `ratArchGL2 h` has positive determinant, takes the value `(g ∣[2] ratArchGL2 h) I`.
