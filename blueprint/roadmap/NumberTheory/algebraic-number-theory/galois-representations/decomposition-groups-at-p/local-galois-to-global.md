# Restriction from the local to the global Galois group

For a prime $p$, the group homomorphism $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p) \to \operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ obtained by restricting automorphisms along $\iota_p$, where $\iota_p : \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ is a chosen embedding of $\mathbb{Q}$-algebras.
