# Decomposition group at p and the local Galois group

For a prime $p$, the decomposition group in $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ of the $p$-adic place $\{x : |\iota_p(x)|_p \le 1\}$ is contained in the closure of the image of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p)$ under restriction along $\iota_p$, where $\iota_p : \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ is a chosen embedding of $\mathbb{Q}$-algebras.
