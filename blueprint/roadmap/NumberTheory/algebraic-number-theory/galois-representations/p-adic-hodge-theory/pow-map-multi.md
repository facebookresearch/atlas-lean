# Pow Mapₛₗ ιMulti

For a `(φ, N)`-module `M` on `D` and a basis `b` of `D` indexed by `Fin r`, where
`r = finrank K₀ D`, the map induced by `φ` on `⋀[K₀]^r D` sends `b 0 ∧ ⋯ ∧ b (r - 1)` to its
multiple by the determinant `b.det (M.φ ∘ b)` of the matrix of `φ` in `b` (the linear algebra
behind Fontaine–Ouyang, Proposition 8.21).
