# Crystalline comparison for prismatic cohomology

Let $(A, (p))$ be a bounded crystalline prism, that is, $A$ is a $p$-torsion-free $p$-complete ring with a
Frobenius lift $\varphi_A$, and let $X$ be a smooth $p$-adic formal scheme over $A/p$. Let
$R\Gamma_\Delta(X/A)$ be the prismatic cohomology of $X$ relative to $A$, a commutative algebra in the derived
category $D(A)$ with a $\varphi_A$-linear endomorphism $\varphi$, and $R\Gamma_{\mathrm{crys}}(X/A)$ the crystalline
cohomology of $X$ over $A$. Then there is a canonical $\varphi$-equivariant isomorphism of commutative algebras in
$D(A)$

$$R\Gamma_{\mathrm{crys}}(X/A) \cong R\Gamma_\Delta(X/A) \mathbin{\widehat{\otimes}}^{L}_{A, \varphi_A} A$$

with the derived $p$-completed base change of prismatic cohomology along $\varphi_A$. Thus prismatic cohomology
gives a canonical Frobenius descent of crystalline cohomology.
