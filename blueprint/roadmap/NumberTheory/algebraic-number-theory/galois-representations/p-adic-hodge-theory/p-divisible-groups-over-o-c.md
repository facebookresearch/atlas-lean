# p-divisible groups over the ring of integers of C

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the structure theory of $p$-divisible groups over the ring of
integers $\mathcal{O}_C$ of a complete algebraically closed extension $C$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ that the proofs of the
[Hodge–Tate sequence of a p-divisible group](hodge-tate-sequence-of-p-divisible-groups.md) use, following
Scholze–Weinstein, §§3–5, and Fargues–Fontaine.

A good expansion has pages for:

1. the Dieudonné module of a $p$-divisible group over a semiperfect ring and its evaluation on
   $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and the full faithfulness of the Dieudonné functor over $\mathcal{O}_C/p$ (Scholze–Weinstein,
   §4.1, Theorem 4.1.4), with the comparison of §4.3 (Theorem 4.3.1, Proposition 4.3.3);
2. the Fargues–Fontaine curve $X$ attached to $C$ and the classification of vector bundles on it by
   isocrystals over $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$ (Fargues–Fontaine, Theorem 7.14, as cited by Scholze–Weinstein), with the
   criterion for modifications of vector bundles (Scholze–Weinstein, Proposition 5.1.1);
3. the classification of $p$-divisible groups over $\mathcal{O}_C$ by pairs $(T, W)$ of a finite free
   $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module and a $C$-subspace of $T \otimes C(-1)$ (Scholze–Weinstein, Theorem 5.1.4 and Remark 5.1.5,
   resting on Fargues–Fontaine, Theorems 7.15, 7.24 and Proposition 7.17);
4. Fargues's proof of the integral Hodge–Tate sequence ([FGL08, Théorème II.1.1]).
