# p-divisible groups over the ring of integers of C

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the structure theory of $p$-divisible groups over the ring of
integers $\mathcal{O}_C$ of a complete algebraically closed extension $C$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and the vector bundles on the
Fargues–Fontaine curve, that the proof of the
[Hodge–Tate sequence of a p-divisible group](hodge-tate-sequence-of-p-divisible-groups.md) and the proof of the
[classification of crystalline lattices by prismatic F-crystals](prismatic-f-crystals-and-crystalline-lattices.md)
use, following Scholze–Weinstein, §§4–5, Fargues–Fontaine and Fargues.

A good expansion has pages for:

1. the Dieudonné module of a $p$-divisible group over a semiperfect ring, evaluated on $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$: over an
   f-semiperfect ring the Dieudonné functor is fully faithful on $p$-divisible groups **up to isogeny**
   (Scholze–Weinstein, Theorem 4.1.4); over a quotient $S/J$ of a perfect ring by an ideal generated by a regular
   sequence it is fully faithful integrally (Corollary 4.1.12), which applies to $\mathcal{O}_C/p$ (Remark 5.1.3); with
   the comparison of §4.3 (Theorem 4.3.1, Proposition 4.3.3);
2. the Fargues–Fontaine curve $X$ attached to $C$ and its vector bundles: the classification by isocrystals over
   $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$ ([FF12], Theorem 7.14, as cited by Scholze–Weinstein), the statement that an injective map of
   coherent sheaves of the same rank and degree is an isomorphism (Scholze–Weinstein, Proposition 5.1.1), and the
   results used by Bhatt–Scholze 2021, §6.4: $\varphi$-modules over the Robba ring are vector bundles on the curve
   ([FF19], Corollary 11.2.22), the bundle attached to a weakly admissible filtered $\varphi$-module is semistable of
   slope $0$ ([FF19], Proposition 10.5.6), and semistable bundles of slope $0$ are trivial ([FF19], Theorem 8.2.10 (1));
3. the vector bundle $\mathcal{E}(G_0)$ of a $p$-divisible group over $\mathcal{O}_C/p$, the isotriviality of $p$-divisible groups
   over $\mathcal{O}_C$ and the full faithfulness, up to isogeny, of $G_0 \mapsto \mathcal{E}(G_0)$ with essential image the
   bundles with slopes in $[0, 1]$ (Scholze–Weinstein, Theorem 5.1.4 and Remark 5.1.5, resting on [FF12],
   Theorems 7.15, 7.24 and Proposition 7.17);
4. the modification of vector bundles attached to a $p$-divisible group $G$ over $\mathcal{O}_C$ with Tate module $T$,
   writing $\mathcal{E}_H = \mathcal{E}(H_0)$ and $\mathcal{F}_H = \mathcal{O}_X \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} T(H)$ for the bundles attached to a
   $p$-divisible group $H$ over $\mathcal{O}_C$: the exact sequence
   $0 \to \mathcal{F}_G \to \mathcal{E}_G \to i_{\infty *}(\operatorname{Lie} G \otimes C) \to 0$ of coherent sheaves on $X$
   (Scholze–Weinstein, Proposition 5.1.6 (i), proved through Proposition 5.1.1 and the case of $\mu_{p^\infty}$); the
   adjunction morphism $\mathcal{E}_G \to i_{\infty *} i_\infty^* \mathcal{E}_G$, which induces the quasi-logarithm on global
   sections (Proposition 5.1.6 (ii)); and, after a trivialisation of $\mathbb{Z}_p(1)$, the identification of the composite
   $(\mathcal{E}_{G^\vee})^\vee \to (\mathcal{F}_{G^\vee})^\vee \cong \mathcal{F}_G \to \mathcal{E}_G \cong (\mathcal{E}_{G^\vee})^\vee \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{O}_X(1)$
   with multiplication by $t = \log([\epsilon]) \in H^0(X, \mathcal{O}_X(1))$, which vanishes at $\infty$ (proof of
   Proposition 5.1.6, p. 51, by reduction to $T \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p$). From these Scholze–Weinstein
   reprove the exactness of the Hodge–Tate sequence after inverting $p$, which they take from [FGL08], Chapter II,
   Appendix C. That exact sequence over $C$ and its integral form ([FGL08], Théorème II.1.1, the case
   $(C, \mathcal{O}_C)$ of Scholze–Weinstein, Proposition 4.3.6) are items 1 and 2 of the
   [Hodge–Tate sequence of a p-divisible group](hodge-tate-sequence-of-p-divisible-groups.md) and are not part of this
   node;
5. the classification of $p$-divisible groups over $\mathcal{O}_C$ by pairs $(T, W)$ of a finite free $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module $T$ and
   a $C$-subspace $W$ of $T \otimes C(-1)$ (Scholze–Weinstein, Theorem 5.2.1). Its functor sends $G$ to its Tate module and
   $W = \operatorname{Lie} G \otimes C$ embedded by the Hodge–Tate map $\alpha_{G^\vee}^\vee$, so it comes after the Hodge–Tate
   sequence and is not an input to it.

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- the extended Robba ring attached to $A_{\mathrm{inf}}$ (Fargues–Fontaine 2019, Definition 1.8.1, as cited by Bhatt–Scholze 2021, §6.4, n. 13), for the $\varphi$-modules over it in item 2
- the realisation of every vector bundle on the Fargues–Fontaine curve as the bundle attached to an isocrystal over $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$ ([FF12], Theorem 7.14), for the classification of item 2 and the essential image in item 3 (Scholze–Weinstein, proof of Theorem 5.1.4)
- the isotriviality of Dieudonné modules over $B_{\mathrm{cris}}^+$ ([FF12], Theorem 7.24) and the full faithfulness of their functor to vector bundles ([FF12], Theorem 7.15 with Proposition 7.17), to which Scholze–Weinstein's Theorem 4.1.4 reduces the isotriviality and the full faithfulness of item 3
- the crystalline Dieudonné module as a crystal on the full crystalline site, and its evaluation on $A_{\mathrm{cris}}(R)$ for a semiperfect ring $R$, a divided power thickening that is not nilpotent (Messing; Berthelot–Breen–Messing; Scholze–Weinstein, §4.1), for the Dieudonné modules of items 1 and 3
- perfectoid spaces and tilting (Scholze–Weinstein, §2.3, citing [Sch12]), for the perfectoid affinoid algebras of Scholze–Weinstein, §4.3, and the curve attached to $C$ in item 2
