# (φ, Γ)-modules and p-adic differential equations

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the theory through which Berger proves the
[p-adic monodromy theorem](p-adic-monodromy-theorem.md): Fontaine's $(\varphi, \Gamma)$-modules, their
overconvergence, and the $p$-adic differential equations with Frobenius structure attached to de Rham
representations, with the quasi-unipotence theorem (Crew's conjecture).

A good expansion has pages for:

1. the field of norms $E$ of the cyclotomic tower, a field of characteristic $p$ whose absolute Galois group is
   identified with $H_K$, and Fontaine's equivalence between $p$-adic representations of $G_K$ and étale
   $(\varphi, \Gamma)$-modules over the characteristic-zero field $\operatorname{Frac} C(E)$, for a Cohen ring $C(E)$ of $E$
   (Fontaine–Ouyang, Chapter 5, Theorem 5.13, Definition 5.21, Theorem 5.22);
2. the overconvergence of $(\varphi, \Gamma)$-modules (Cherbonnier–Colmez) and the Robba ring, as used by Berger;
3. Berger's module $N_{\mathrm{dR}}(V)$ over $B^\dagger_{\mathrm{rig}, K}$ with its connection and Frobenius for a de Rham
   representation $V$, and the equivalence of its quasi-unipotence with the potential semistability of $V$
   (Berger 2002, introduction and §5, Corollaire 5.22);
4. the $p$-adic local monodromy theorem (Crew's conjecture): every $(\varphi, \partial)$-module over the Robba ring
   is quasi-unipotent, proved independently by André, Kedlaya and Mebkhout.

## Depends on

- [de Rham representations](de-rham-representations.md)
- [Semistable and potentially semistable representations](semistable-representations.md), for the potential semistability of item 3

## Proof depends on

- [Structure of the rings of periods](structure-of-the-period-rings.md), for the ring $R$, the field $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ with its subrings and Sen's theory (its items 1 and 2), from which Berger builds the rings and the modules $D_{\mathrm{Sen}}(V)$ and $D_{\mathrm{dif}}(V)$ of items 2 and 3 (Berger 2002, §§1–2 and 5.3)

## Expansion

A good expansion also states and links:

- Fontaine's equivalence for the absolute Galois group of a field $E$ of characteristic $p$, between its $\mathbb{Z}_p$-representations and the étale $\varphi$-modules over the Cohen ring $C(E)$ (Fontaine–Ouyang, Theorem 3.34) and between its $p$-adic representations and the étale $\varphi$-modules over the characteristic-zero field $\operatorname{Frac} C(E)$ (Theorem 3.35), which item 1 applies to $H_K$, identified with the absolute Galois group of the field of norms, before adding the action of $\Gamma_K$ (Fontaine–Ouyang, (5.26)–(5.29) and Theorem 5.22)
