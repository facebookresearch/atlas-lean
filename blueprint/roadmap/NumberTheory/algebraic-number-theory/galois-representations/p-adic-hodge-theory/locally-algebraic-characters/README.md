# Locally algebraic characters

Continuous characters of the Galois group of a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ that agree near $1$, through the
local Artin map, with an algebraic homomorphism; Tate's theorem that these are exactly the Hodge–Tate characters,
with the two inputs of the theory: Sen's theorem on $C$-admissible representations, and the Hodge–Tate weights of
the characters given by one embedding.
