# p-adic Hodge theory

Fontaine's period rings and the Hodge–Tate, de Rham, semistable and crystalline $p$-adic representations
of the Galois group of a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$; weakly admissible filtered $(\varphi, N)$-modules and
the theorems of Colmez–Fontaine and Berger; the Weil–Deligne representation of a de Rham representation;
the Bloch–Kato subgroups of local Galois cohomology; the Hodge–Tate sequence of $p$-divisible groups; and the
prismatic description of lattices in crystalline representations. Hodge–Tate weights are given with their sign
convention on each page that uses them. The main source is Fontaine–Ouyang's book draft.
