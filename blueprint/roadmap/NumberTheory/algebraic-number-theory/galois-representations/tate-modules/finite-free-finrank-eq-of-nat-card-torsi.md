# Rank of the Tate module from torsion counts

Let $\ell$ be a prime, $M$ an abelian group and $d \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|M[\ell^n]| = (\ell^n)^d$ for every $n$, where $M[m]$ is the $m$-torsion subgroup. Then the Tate module $T_\ell M$ is a finitely generated free $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-module of rank $d$, and $V_\ell M = T_\ell M \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ has dimension $d$ over $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. Here for a natural number $\ell$ and an abelian group $M$, the Tate module $T_\ell M$ is the group of sequences $(x_n)_{n \ge 0}$ in $M$ with $\ell^n x_n = 0$ and $\ell\,x_{n+1} = x_n$ for all $n$.
