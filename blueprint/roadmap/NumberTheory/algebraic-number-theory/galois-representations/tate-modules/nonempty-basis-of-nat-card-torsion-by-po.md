# Bases of the Tate module from torsion counts

Let $\ell$ be a prime, $M$ an abelian group and $d \in \mathbb{N}$ with $|M[\ell^n]| = (\ell^n)^d$ for every $n$, where $M[m]$ is the $m$-torsion subgroup. Then the Tate module $T_\ell M$ has a $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-basis indexed by $\{0, \dots, d - 1\}$.
