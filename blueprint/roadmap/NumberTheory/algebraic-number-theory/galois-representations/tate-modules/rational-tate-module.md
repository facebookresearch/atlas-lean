# Rational Tate modules

For a prime $p$ and an abelian group $M$, $V_p M = T_p M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathbb{Q}_p$, where for a natural number $p$ and an abelian group $M$, the Tate module $T_p M$ is the group of sequences $(x_n)_{n \ge 0}$ in $M$ with $p^n x_n = 0$ and $p\,x_{n+1} = x_n$ for all $n$.
