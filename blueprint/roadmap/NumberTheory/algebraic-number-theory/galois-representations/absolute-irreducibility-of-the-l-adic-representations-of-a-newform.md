# Absolute irreducibility of the ℓ-adic representations of a newform

Let $f = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n q^n$ be a newform of weight $2$ and level $N$ (Darmon–Diamond–Taylor, Definition 1.21:
a normalized eigenform in the new subspace of $S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$), $\psi$ its character, sending
$d \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$ to the eigenvalue of $\langle d \rangle$ on $f$, and $K_f \subset \mathbb{C}$ the number field
generated by the $a_n$. Let $A_f$ be the abelian variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ that
[Shimura attaches to $f$](../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/shimura-abelian-variety-of-an-eigenform.md), a
quotient of the Jacobian $J_1(N)$ of $X_1(N)$, with $K_f$ embedded in $\mathrm{End}(A_f) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ through the Hecke
endomorphisms. Let $\ell$ be a prime. The rational Tate module $V_\ell(A_f) = T_\ell(A_f) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_\ell} \mathbb{Q}_\ell$,
where $T_\ell(A_f)$ is the inverse limit of the groups $A_f[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb{Q}})$, is a free module of rank two over
$K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$, and $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ acts on it $K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$-linearly, so that a basis gives a
representation $G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{GL}_2(K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell)$. Let $\lambda$ be a prime of $K_f$ above $\ell$, and fix
embeddings of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ into $\mathbb{C}$ and into $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ such that, with $K_f \subset \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$
through the first, the second induces $\lambda$ on $K_f$. The $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-algebra $K_{f,\lambda} \subset \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$
generated by the $a_n$ is then the completion of $K_f$ at $\lambda$. Let $\rho_f \colon G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{GL}_2(K_{f,\lambda})$
be the pushforward of that representation along the natural map $K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell \to K_{f,\lambda}$. Then:

1. $\rho_f$ is unramified at every prime $p \nmid \ell N$, and $\rho_f(\mathrm{Frob}_p)$ has characteristic polynomial
   $X^2 - a_p X + p\,\psi(p)$;
2. $\rho_f$ is **absolutely irreducible**: it stays irreducible after extension of scalars to any field extension of
   $K_{f,\lambda}$.

The conventions for unramified representations and arithmetic Frobenius elements are those of
[Galois representations attached to normalized eigenforms](../../not-yet-classified/exists-galois-rep-charpoly-frob.md).

These are parts (a) and (c) of Darmon, Diamond and Taylor's Theorem 3.1, for their representation $\rho_f$ with values in
$\mathrm{GL}_2(K'_f)$, where $K'_f$ is the $K$-algebra in $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ generated by the $a_n$ over a fixed finite
extension $K$ of $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$. Here $K = \mathbb{Q}_\ell$: then $K'_f$ is $K_{f,\lambda}$, since it contains the completion of
$K_f$ at the prime over $\ell$ determined by the embeddings (p. 85) and lies in it.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

1. the rational Tate module of $A_f$ as a free module of rank two over $K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ on which $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ acts
   $K_f$-linearly (§3.1, p. 85, citing their Lemma 1.48), the representation (3.1.1) and its pushforward $\rho_f$;
2. part 1, due to Shimura through the Eichler–Shimura congruence relation (their Theorem 1.29) and the good reduction
   of $J_1(N)$ at the primes not dividing $N$, by which $\mathrm{Frob}_p$ acts on the Tate module through the Frobenius
   endomorphism of the reduction (p. 86);
3. part 2, proved by Ribet (p. 86, citing §2 of his paper [R3]; not read here), who, assuming reducibility, applies
   algebraicity results of Lang and Serre to contradict the estimate on the Fourier coefficients of their Theorem 1.24.
