# Absolute irreducibility of the ℓ-adic representations of a newform

Let $f = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n q^n$ be a newform of weight $2$ and level $N$ (Darmon–Diamond–Taylor, Definition 1.21:
a normalized eigenform in the new subspace of $S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$), $\psi$ its character, sending
$d \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$ to the eigenvalue of $\langle d \rangle$ on $f$, $K_f \subset \mathbb{C}$ the number field
generated by the $a_n$, $\ell$ a prime, $\lambda$ a prime of $K_f$ above $\ell$ and $K_{f,\lambda}$ the completion. Then
every continuous representation $\rho \colon G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{GL}_2(K_{f,\lambda})$ that is unramified at every
prime $p \nmid \ell N$, with every arithmetic Frobenius element at a prime above such a $p$ acting with characteristic
polynomial $X^2 - a_p X + \psi(p)\,p$, is **absolutely irreducible**: it stays irreducible after extension of
scalars to any field extension of $K_{f,\lambda}$. The conventions for unramified representations and Frobenius
elements are those of
[Galois representations attached to normalized eigenforms](../../not-yet-classified/exists-galois-rep-charpoly-frob.md).

This combines parts (a) and (c) of Darmon, Diamond and Taylor's Theorem 3.1, for their representation $\rho_f$, with
their Proposition 2.6 (c): the semisimplification of $\rho$, after any extension of scalars, has the Frobenius traces
of $\rho_f$, hence is isomorphic to it, and a two-dimensional representation whose semisimplification is irreducible
is irreducible.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

1. Darmon–Diamond–Taylor's representation $\rho_f \colon G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{GL}_2(K'_f)$, the pushforward along
   $K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell \to K'_f$ of the action of $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ on the rational Tate module of
   [Shimura's abelian variety](../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/shimura-abelian-variety-of-an-eigenform.md)
   $A_f$, free of rank two over $K_f \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ (§3.1, p. 85, citing their Lemma 1.48), with $K'_f$ the
   completion $K_{f,\lambda}$ when the embedding of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ into $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ induces $\lambda$ and
   $K = \mathbb{Q}_\ell$;
2. part (a) of their Theorem 3.1: $\rho_f$ is unramified at $p \nmid N\ell$ and $\rho_f(\mathrm{Frob}_p)$ has characteristic
   polynomial $X^2 - a_p X + p\psi(p)$, due to Shimura through the Eichler–Shimura congruence relation (p. 86);
3. part (c): $\rho_f$ is absolutely irreducible, proved by Ribet (p. 86, citing §2 of his paper [R3]; not read here),
   who, assuming reducibility, applies algebraicity results of Lang and Serre to contradict the estimate on the
   Fourier coefficients;
4. the deduction above, through
   [the determination of semisimple ℓ-adic representations by their Frobenius traces](deligne-serre-theorem-on-weight-one-forms/semisimple-galois-representations-with-equal-frobenius-polynomials.md),
   applied after extension of scalars to a finite extension of $K_{f,\lambda}$.
