# Toric scalar exclusion in Mazur's principle

Let $G$ be a group, $k$ a field and $V$ a two-dimensional $k$-vector space with a $G$-action commuting with scalars, and let $J$ be a module over a commutative ring $R$ with a $G$-action, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varphi \in G$ and $\mathcal{T} \subseteq J$ a submodule satisfying the toric Frobenius-square condition. Suppose $\iota : V \to J$ is an injective additive $G$-equivariant map with image in $\mathcal{T}$, that $\varphi$ acts on $V$ as a scalar $\lambda \in k$, and that the determinant of $\varphi$ on $V$ is $q$. Then $q = 0$ or $q = 1$ in $k$. Here for a group $G$ acting distributively on a module $J$ over a commutative ring $R$, a natural number $q$, $\varphi \in G$ and a submodule $\mathcal{T} \subseteq J$, the toric Frobenius-square condition holds when $\varphi\cdot\varphi\cdot x = q^2 x$ for every $x \in \mathcal{T}$.
