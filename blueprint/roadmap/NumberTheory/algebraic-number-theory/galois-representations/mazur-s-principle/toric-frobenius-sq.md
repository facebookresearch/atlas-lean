# Toric Frobenius-square condition

For a group $G$ acting distributively on a module $J$ over a commutative ring $R$, a natural number $q$, $\varphi \in G$ and a submodule $\mathcal{T} \subseteq J$, the toric Frobenius-square condition holds when $\varphi\cdot\varphi\cdot x = q^2 x$ for every $x \in \mathcal{T}$.
