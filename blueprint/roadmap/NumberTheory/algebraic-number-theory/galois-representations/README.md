# Galois representations and p-adic Hodge theory

ℓ-adic and p-adic representations of the Galois groups of local and global fields and their Galois cohomology: Galois cohomology of local and global fields (local Tate duality, Euler characteristic formulas, the Poitou–Tate sequence, Selmer and Bloch–Kato groups), Weil–Deligne representations and the ℓ-adic monodromy theorem, Fontaine's period rings with crystalline, semistable, de Rham and Hodge–Tate representations and (φ, Γ)-modules, p-adic comparison theorems for varieties and p-divisible groups, and prismatic cohomology. Class field theory is under Adeles, ideles and class field theory, cohomology of abstract groups under group cohomology, ℓ-adic cohomology of schemes under Grothendieck topologies on schemes, and Galois representations of elliptic curves under elliptic curves.
