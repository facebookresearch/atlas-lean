# Galois representations and p-adic Hodge theory

ℓ-adic and p-adic representations of the Galois groups of local and global fields and their Galois cohomology: Galois cohomology of local and global fields (local Tate duality, Euler characteristic formulas, the Poitou–Tate sequence, Selmer and Bloch–Kato groups), Weil–Deligne representations and the ℓ-adic monodromy theorem, Fontaine's period rings with crystalline, semistable, de Rham and Hodge–Tate representations and (φ, Γ)-modules, the Hodge–Tate sequence of p-divisible groups and p-divisible groups over O_C, and the prismatic F-crystals that classify lattices in crystalline representations, with the prisms, prismatic cohomology and crystalline comparison they rest on. Class field theory is under Adeles, ideles and class field theory, cohomology of abstract groups under group cohomology, ℓ-adic cohomology of schemes under Grothendieck topologies and étale cohomology, other prismatic, crystalline and syntomic cohomology of schemes and formal schemes, and the comparison theorems for the cohomology of varieties, under p-adic cohomology, and Galois representations of elliptic curves under elliptic curves.
