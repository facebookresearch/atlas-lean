# Galois representations attached to modular eigenforms

The two-dimensional $\ell$-adic representations of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ attached to normalized
eigenforms on $\Gamma_1(N)$, following the proof of
[Galois representations attached to normalized eigenforms](../../../not-yet-classified/exists-galois-rep-charpoly-frob.md)
by weight: Shimura's construction in weight $2$ from the Jacobian of $X_1(N)$, Deligne's in weight at least $3$ from
$\ell$-adic cohomology, and Deligne and Serre's in weight $1$. In weight $2$ the steps are the good reduction of
$X_1(N)$ away from $N$, the Hecke operators on its Jacobian, the Eichler–Shimura relation, the freeness of the
rational Tate module over the Hecke algebra, the characteristic polynomial of Frobenius on it, and the passage to the
abelian variety $A_f$ and to the completions of the coefficient field.
