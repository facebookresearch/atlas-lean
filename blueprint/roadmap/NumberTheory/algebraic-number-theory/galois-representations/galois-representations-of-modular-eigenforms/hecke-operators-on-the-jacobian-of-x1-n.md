# Hecke operators on the Jacobian of X₁(N) over Z[1/N]

This node is still to be broken down. It defines the action of the weight-two Hecke algebra of level $N$ on
the Jacobian of the [modular curve $X_1(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$](modular-curve-x1-n-over-z-1-over-n.md), in
the normalization ("$(\ )_*$-action") in which it induces the classical Hecke operators on cusp forms.

Let $N \ge 5$, let $X_1(N) \supset Y_1(N)$ be the curves over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ of
[that page](modular-curve-x1-n-over-z-1-over-n.md), and let

$$J = \operatorname{Pic}^0_{X_1(N)/\mathbb{Z}[1/N]},$$

an abelian scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$, hence the [Néron model](../../../../AlgebraicGeometry/abelian-varieties/neron-model.md)
over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ of its generic fibre $J_{\mathbb{Q}}$, the
[Jacobian](../../../../AlgebraicGeometry/algebraic-curves/jacobians-of-curves/jacobian.md) of $X_1(N)_{\mathbb{Q}}$.
For a finite flat map $f$ of proper smooth curves with geometrically connected fibres, write
$\operatorname{Pic}^0(f)$ for pullback and $\operatorname{Alb}(f)$ for pushforward of degree-zero line bundles.

- For $n \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^\times$, $I_n$ is the automorphism of $X_1(N)$ extending
  $(E, P) \mapsto (E, nP)$, and $\langle n \rangle_* = \operatorname{Alb}(I_n)$.
- For a prime $p$, $X_1(N, p)$ is the proper smooth curve over $\mathbb{Z}[1/Np]$ containing the moduli scheme
  $Y_1(N, p)$ of triples $(E, P, C)$, with $C \subset E$ a finite locally free subgroup scheme of order $p$
  not contained in the subgroup generated by $P$ on geometric fibres, and
  $\pi_1(E, P, C) = (E, P)$, $\pi_2(E, P, C) = (E/C, P \bmod C)$ extend to maps
  $X_1(N, p) \to X_1(N)_{\mathbb{Z}[1/Np]}$. Then
  $(T_p)_* = \operatorname{Alb}(\pi_2) \circ \operatorname{Pic}^0(\pi_1)$ is an endomorphism of
  $J_{\mathbb{Z}[1/Np]}$, and by the Néron mapping property it extends uniquely to an endomorphism
  $(T_p)_*$ of $J$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$; in particular it acts on the abelian variety $J_{\mathbb{F}_p}$ for
  $p \nmid N$.
- For a primitive $N$th root of unity $\zeta$, $w_\zeta$ is the automorphism of $X_1(N)$ over
  $\mathbb{Z}[1/N, \zeta_N]$ extending $(E, P) \mapsto (E/\langle P \rangle, P')$, where $\langle P \rangle$ is the
  subgroup scheme generated by $P$ and $P' \in (E[N]/\langle P \rangle)(S)$ is determined by
  $\langle P, P' \rangle_N = \zeta$ for the Weil pairing on $E[N]$, with Conrad's sign convention (p. 60); it satisfies $w_{\zeta^j} w_\zeta = I_j$, so
  $w_\zeta^2 = 1$, and $w_\zeta$ also denotes the endomorphism
  $\operatorname{Alb}(w_\zeta) = \operatorname{Pic}^0(w_\zeta)$ of $J$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N, \zeta_N]$.

The **Hecke algebra** $\mathbb{T}_1(N) \subset \operatorname{End}(J)$ is the subring generated by the $(T_p)_*$
for all primes $p$ and the $\langle n \rangle_*$. It is commutative, it is a finite flat $\mathbb{Z}$-algebra, and
under the isomorphism of the cotangent space at the origin of $J(\mathbb{C})$ with the space
$S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$ of weight-two [cusp forms](../../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/cusp-form.md) for
[$\Gamma_1(N)$](../../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/congruence-subgroup-gamma1.md) (through
$H^0(X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}, \Omega^1)$), $(T_p)_*$ and $\langle n \rangle_*$ induce the classical
[Hecke operator $T_p$](../../../modular-forms-q-series/modular-forms/hecke-operators-of-level-n.md) and the
diamond operator $\langle n \rangle$, $f \mapsto f \mid_2 \gamma$ for any
$\gamma = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{smallmatrix}\right) \in \Gamma_0(N)$ with $d \equiv n \pmod N$.
So $\mathbb{T}_1(N)$ is identified with the ring generated by the $T_p$ and $\langle n \rangle$ acting on
$S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- Albanese and $\operatorname{Pic}^0$ functoriality for finite flat maps of proper smooth curves over any base,
  their duality for the canonical principal polarizations (Conrad, Lemma 5.4, in the analytic setting), and
  the fact that an abelian scheme over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$ is the Néron model of its generic fibre (Conrad, p. 67);
- the weight-two Shimura isomorphism
  $S_2(\Gamma_1(N)) \oplus \overline{S_2(\Gamma_1(N))} \cong H^1(X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathbb{C}$ and
  its compatibility with $T_p$, $\langle n \rangle$ and $w_N$ (Conrad, Theorem 5.1);
- the $(\ )^*$-operators $T_p^* = \operatorname{Alb}(\pi_1) \circ \operatorname{Pic}^0(\pi_2)$, $\langle n \rangle^* = \operatorname{Pic}^0(I_n)$
  on $H^1$ and on $\operatorname{Pic}^0$, the anticommutativity of cup product with the Weil pairing (Conrad,
  Lemma 5.7), and Conrad's claim after Definition 5.6 that the $(\ )_*$-operators are the duals of the
  $(\ )^*$-operators and induce the classical action on $S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$;
- the identification of the algebraic operators with the analytic ones after base change to $\mathbb{C}$, and
  the faithfulness of the Tate module functor on complex tori (Conrad, pp. 64 and 68).
