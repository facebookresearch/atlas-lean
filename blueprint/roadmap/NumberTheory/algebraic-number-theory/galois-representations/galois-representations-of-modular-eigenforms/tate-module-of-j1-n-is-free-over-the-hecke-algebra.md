# The rational Tate module of J₁(N) is free of rank two over the Hecke algebra

This node is still to be broken down. Let $N \ge 5$, let $J$ be the Jacobian of the
[modular curve $X_1(N)$ over $\mathbb{Z}[1/N]$](modular-curve-x1-n-over-z-1-over-n.md) and
$\mathbb{T}_1(N) \subset \operatorname{End}(J)$ the Hecke algebra generated by the $(T_p)_*$ and $\langle n \rangle_*$
of [Hecke operators on the Jacobian of X₁(N)](hecke-operators-on-the-jacobian-of-x1-n.md). Let $\ell$ be a
prime, $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\mathbb{C}$,

$$T_\ell(J) = \varprojlim_n J[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb{Q}}), \qquad V_\ell(N) = \mathbb{Q}_\ell \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_\ell} T_\ell(J),$$

the limit taken along multiplication by $\ell$, with the induced action of $\mathbb{T}_1(N)$, and let
$(\ ,\ )_\ell \colon V_\ell(N) \times V_\ell(N) \to \mathbb{Q}_\ell(1)$ be the perfect alternating Weil pairing of the
canonical principal [polarization](../../../../AlgebraicGeometry/abelian-varieties/polarization.md) of the
Jacobian. Then:

1. $V_\ell(N)$ is a free $\mathbb{Q}_\ell \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{T}_1(N)$-module of rank $2$;
2. $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\mathbb{Q} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{T}_1(N), \mathbb{Q})$ is a free
   $\mathbb{Q} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{T}_1(N)$-module of rank $1$, and likewise with $\mathbb{Q}$ replaced by any field
   of characteristic $0$, such as $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$;
3. for $(\ ,\ )_\ell$, the adjoint of $(T_p)_*$ for a prime $p \nmid N$ is $\langle p \rangle_*^{-1} (T_p)_*$ and the
   adjoint of $\langle n \rangle_*$ is $\langle n \rangle_*^{-1}$; for a primitive $N$th root of unity
   $\zeta \in \mathbb{C}$ and $[x, y]_\ell = (x, w_\zeta y)_\ell$, every element of $\mathbb{T}_1(N)$ is self-adjoint.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the weight-two Shimura isomorphism
  $S_2(\Gamma_1(N)) \oplus \overline{S_2(\Gamma_1(N))} \cong H^1(X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathbb{C}$ with its Hecke
  compatibility (Conrad, Theorem 5.1), and its compatibility with cup product and the Petersson product
  (Theorem 5.2), which with the classical adjointness of the Hecke operators for the Petersson product gives the
  adjointness on $H^1(X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}, \mathbb{Z})$ (Corollary 5.3);
- the isomorphism $T_\ell(\operatorname{Pic}^0_{X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}}) \cong H^1(X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ from the
  exponential sequence (Conrad, (5.3) and (5.4)), the anticommutativity of cup product with the Weil pairing
  (Lemma 5.7), and the comparison $\varprojlim_n J[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb{Q}}) \cong T_\ell(\operatorname{Pic}^0_{X_1(N)^{\mathrm{an}}})$
  (Conrad, (5.6));
- the perfect $\mathbb{C} \otimes \mathbb{T}_1(N)$-equivariant pairing
  $S_2(\Gamma_1(N)) \times (\mathbb{C} \otimes \mathbb{T}_1(N)) \to \mathbb{C}$, $(f, T) \mapsto a_1(Tf)$, which makes
  $\operatorname{Hom}(S_2(\Gamma_1(N)), \mathbb{C})$ free of rank $1$ over $\mathbb{C} \otimes \mathbb{T}_1(N)$, and the descent of
  freeness from $\mathbb{C}$ to $\mathbb{Q}$ for finite modules over the semilocal ring $\mathbb{Q} \otimes \mathbb{T}_1(N)$
  (Conrad, proof of Corollary 5.9; Darmon–Diamond–Taylor, Lemmas 1.37–1.39 and Corollary 1.40).
