# Representations modulo λ attached to eigenforms modulo λ

Let $K \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a number field, $\lambda$ a finite place of $K$, $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$ the corresponding valuation
ring, $\mathfrak{m}_\lambda$ its maximal ideal, $k_\lambda = \mathcal{O}_\lambda/\mathfrak{m}_\lambda$ the residue field and $\ell$
its characteristic. A [modular form $f$ of type $(k, \varepsilon)$ on $\Gamma_0(N)$](modular-forms-of-type-k-epsilon.md)
is $\lambda$-integral, resp. $\equiv 0$ (mod $\lambda$), if the coefficients of its $q$-expansion lie in
$\mathcal{O}_\lambda$, resp. in $\mathfrak{m}_\lambda$. A $\lambda$-integral $f$ is an eigenvector of $T_p$ modulo
$\lambda$ with eigenvalue $a_p \in k_\lambda$ if

$$f|T_p - a_p f \equiv 0 \pmod{\lambda},$$

that is, if the reduction modulo $\mathfrak{m}_\lambda$ of the $q$-expansion of $f|T_p$ is $a_p$ times that of $f$.

Let $N \ge 1$ and $k \ge 1$, let $\varepsilon$ be a Dirichlet character mod $N$ with $\varepsilon(-1) = (-1)^k$ and values in
$K$, and let $f$ be a modular form of type $(k, \varepsilon)$ on $\Gamma_0(N)$ with coefficients in $K$ that is
$\lambda$-integral, with $f \not\equiv 0$ (mod $\lambda$), and an eigenvector of the $T_p$ modulo $\lambda$ for every prime
$p \nmid N\ell$, with eigenvalues $a_p \in k_\lambda$. Let $k_f$ be the subfield of $k_\lambda$ generated by the $a_p$ and
the reductions modulo $\lambda$ of the $\varepsilon(p)$. Then there is a semisimple representation

$$\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}_2(k_f), \qquad G = \operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q}),$$

continuous for the discrete topology of $k_f$ and unramified outside $N\ell$, such that for every prime $p \nmid N\ell$

$$\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\rho,p}) = a_p \qquad \text{and} \qquad \det(F_{\rho,p}) \equiv \varepsilon(p)\, p^{k-1} \pmod{\lambda},$$

with unramified and $F_{\rho,p}$ as on
[Semisimple Galois representations with the same Frobenius polynomials](semisimple-galois-representations-with-equal-frobenius-polynomials.md).

This is Théorème 6.7 of Deligne–Serre. That $\varepsilon$ takes values in $K$ is implicit there, as the statement
reduces the $\varepsilon(p)$ modulo $\lambda$.

Their proof (pp. 522–523) has four steps. (6.8) If $K'$ contains $K$, $\lambda'$ extends $\lambda$ and $f'$ is a form of
type $(k', \varepsilon')$ as above with eigenvalues $a'_p$, $a_p \equiv a'_p$ and
$\varepsilon(p)p^{k-1} \equiv \varepsilon'(p)p^{k'-1}$ (mod $\lambda'$) for all $p \nmid N\ell$, then the theorem for $f$ is
equivalent to the theorem for $f'$; the second condition holds when $\varepsilon = \varepsilon'$ and
$k \equiv k' \pmod{\ell - 1}$, and it implies the first when $f \equiv f'$ (mod $\lambda'$). (6.9) Multiplying by an
[Eisenstein series congruent to 1 modulo ℓ](eisenstein-series-congruent-to-one-modulo-l.md) reduces to $k \ge 2$.
(6.10) [Lifting the eigenvalues](lifting-common-eigenvalues-over-a-discrete-valuation-ring.md), applied to the $T_p$ on
the $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$-module of forms of type $(k, \varepsilon)$ with coefficients in $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$, reduces to an
$f$ that is an eigenvector of the $T_p$, $p \nmid N\ell$; as $T_\ell$ commutes with them, $f$ may also be taken to be an
eigenvector of $T_\ell$ if $\ell \nmid N$ (6.12). (6.12) Then the
[λ-adic representation](lambda-adic-representations-of-eigenforms-of-weight-at-least-two.md) $\rho_\lambda$ of $f$ can be
conjugated into $\mathrm{GL}_2(\hat{\mathcal{O}}_\lambda)$, $\hat{\mathcal{O}}_\lambda$ the ring of integers of $K_\lambda$; its
reduction modulo $\lambda$ has a semisimplification $\varphi$, unramified outside $N\ell$ and satisfying the congruences,
with finite image; by the [Chebotarev density theorem](../../adeles-class-field-theory/chebotarev-density-theorem.md)
every element of $\varphi(G)$ is a Frobenius element $F_{\varphi,p}$ with $p \nmid N\ell$, so the coefficients of every
$\det(1 - sT)$, $s \in \varphi(G)$, lie in $k_f$ (6.12.1), and $\varphi$ is
[realizable over $k_f$](realizability-of-a-semisimple-representation-over-a-finite-subfield.md).

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the reduction principle 6.8, comparing the conditions of the theorem for two congruent forms;
- the freeness of finite type of the $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$-module of forms of type $(k, \varepsilon)$ with coefficients in
  $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$, to which the lifting lemma is applied, and its stability under the $T_p$;
- the existence of a $G$-stable $\hat{\mathcal{O}}_\lambda$-lattice for a continuous representation of the compact group $G$
  over $K_\lambda$, and the semisimplification of its reduction, with its characteristic polynomials.
