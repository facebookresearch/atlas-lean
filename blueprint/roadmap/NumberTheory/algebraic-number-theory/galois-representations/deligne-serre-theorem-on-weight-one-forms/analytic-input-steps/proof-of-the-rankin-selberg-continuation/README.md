# Proof of the continuation of the Rankin–Selberg series

The steps of Rankin's method behind
[Rankin–Selberg series of a primitive form](rankin-selberg-series-of-a-primitive-form.md), which P. Deligne and
J.-P. Serre, *Formes modulaires de poids 1*, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (4) 7 (1974), 507–530, p. 518, quote from Rankin,
in the form A. P. Ogg (Invent. Math. 7 (1969), §2) and W.-C. W. Li (Math. Ann. 212 (1975), §4) give it on $\Gamma_0(N)$. The
integral of $f \overline{g} y^k$ against the Eisenstein series of $\Gamma_0(N)$ at the cusp $\infty$ unfolds into the Mellin
transform of $\sum_n a_n \overline{b_n} e^{-4\pi n y}$. Multiplied by $\zeta_N(2s)$, the Eisenstein series is a combination of
Epstein zeta functions at the points $Nz/d$, whose continuation and functional equation continue the convolution, with a
simple pole at $s = 1$ whose residue is a positive constant times the Petersson product $\langle f, g \rangle$.
