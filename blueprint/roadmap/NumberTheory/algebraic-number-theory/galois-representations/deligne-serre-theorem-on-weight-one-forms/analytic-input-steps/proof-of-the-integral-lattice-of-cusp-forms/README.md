# Proof of the integral lattice of cusp forms and the algebraicity of their Hecke eigenvalues

The steps of the proof of
[Integral lattice of cusp forms on Γ₁(N) and algebraicity of their Hecke eigenvalues](integral-lattice-of-cusp-forms-and-algebraicity-of-hecke-eigenvalues.md),
Proposition 2.7 of P. Deligne and J.-P. Serre, *Formes modulaires de poids 1*, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (4) 7 (1974),
507–530, p. 512. The cusp forms of weight $k$ on $\Gamma_1(N)$ with rational $q$-expansions span the whole space; each of
them, with its transforms $f|R_d$, has bounded denominators, so that a nonzero multiple of it has integral expansions, and
the forms with integral expansions form a lattice $L$. The Hecke operators preserve $L$, by their action on
$q$-expansions, so they act on it by integer matrices: their eigenvalues are algebraic integers, and they commute with the
action of the automorphisms of $\mathbb{C}$ on the coefficients.
