# Proof of Rankin's bound for the mean square of Hecke eigenvalues

The steps of the proof of
[Rankin's bound for the mean square of Hecke eigenvalues](rankin-bound-for-the-mean-square-of-hecke-eigenvalues.md),
Proposition 5.1 of P. Deligne and J.-P. Serre, *Formes modulaires de poids 1*, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (4) 7 (1974),
507–530, pp. 518–519. An eigenvector of the $T_p$, $p \nmid N$, has the eigenvalues of a primitive form of a level $M$
dividing $N$, at which the rest of the proof works. The coefficients of a primitive form satisfy the Hecke relations, so
they form an Euler product, and Rankin's identity compares the Euler product $L(s)$ of the
$\varphi_p \otimes \overline{\varphi}_p$ with the
[Rankin–Selberg series](../proof-of-the-rankin-selberg-continuation/rankin-selberg-series-of-a-primitive-form.md); the
absolute values of the coefficients $a_p$ at the primes dividing $M$ keep the correcting factor $H(s)$ holomorphic and
nonzero on $\operatorname{Re}(s) \ge k$.
