# Lifting common eigenvalues from the residue field of a discrete valuation ring

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, residue field $k$ and field of fractions
$K$, and let $M$ be a free $\mathcal{O}$-module of finite type. Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a set of pairwise commuting
endomorphisms of $M$. Let $f \in M/\mathfrak{m}M$ be a nonzero common eigenvector of the $T \in \mathcal{T}$, acting on
$M/\mathfrak{m}M$, and let $a_T \in k$ be the corresponding eigenvalues. Then there are a discrete valuation ring
$\mathcal{O}'$ containing $\mathcal{O}$, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}'$ such that $\mathcal{O} \cap \mathfrak{m}' = \mathfrak{m}$
and with field of fractions $K'$ finite over $K$, and a nonzero element $f'$ of
$M' = \mathcal{O}' \otimes_{\mathcal{O}} M$ that is an eigenvector of every $T \in \mathcal{T}$, with eigenvalues
$a'_T \in \mathcal{O}'$ such that

$$a'_T \equiv a_T \pmod{\mathfrak{m}'} \qquad \text{for every } T \in \mathcal{T},$$

where $k$ is viewed inside $\mathcal{O}'/\mathfrak{m}'$ through $\mathcal{O} \cap \mathfrak{m}' = \mathfrak{m}$. The eigenvector $f$
itself need not lift: only the eigenvalues do.

This is Lemme 6.11 of Deligne–Serre. In their proof (p. 522) $\mathcal{H}$ is the subalgebra of $\operatorname{End}(M)$
generated by $\mathcal{T}$ and $\chi \colon \mathcal{H} \to k$ the homomorphism with $h f = \chi(h) f$. After a finite
extension of scalars $K \otimes \mathcal{H}$ is a product of artinian rings with residue field $K$; since $\mathcal{H}$ is free
over $\mathcal{O}$ there is a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ of $\mathcal{H}$ inside $\operatorname{Ker}(\chi)$ with
$\mathfrak{p} \cap \mathcal{O} = 0$, the kernel of a homomorphism $\chi' \colon \mathcal{H} \to \mathcal{O}$ that reduces to
$\chi$ modulo $\mathfrak{m}$; the ideal of $K \otimes \mathcal{H}$ generated by $\mathfrak{p}$ lies in the support of
$K \otimes M$, so some nonzero $f'' \in K \otimes M$ has $h f'' = \chi'(h) f''$ for all $h \in \mathcal{H}$, and $f'$ is a
nonzero multiple of $f''$ in $M$. A variant puts the $T$ in upper triangular form on an
$\mathcal{T}$-indecomposable $M$ and takes $f' = e_1$.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the extension of $\mathcal{O}$ to a discrete valuation ring $\mathcal{O}'$ of a finite extension $K'$ of $K$, with
  $\mathcal{O} \cap \mathfrak{m}' = \mathfrak{m}$, over which $K' \otimes \mathcal{H}$ is a product of artinian local rings with
  residue field $K'$;
- the existence of a prime ideal of the finite free $\mathcal{O}$-algebra $\mathcal{H}$ that is contained in a given
  maximal ideal and meets $\mathcal{O}$ in $0$;
- for an artinian ring acting faithfully on a finite-dimensional vector space, that each maximal ideal annihilates a
  nonzero vector.
