# The Deligne–Serre theorem on modular forms of weight one

The proof of
[Galois representations attached to weight-one eigenforms (Deligne–Serre)](../../../not-yet-classified/exists-galois-rep-trace-det-frob-of-weig.md),
following §§5–8 of P. Deligne and J.-P. Serre, *Formes modulaires de poids 1*, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (4) 7 (1974),
507–530 ([doi:10.24033/asens.1277](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1277)). For a cusp form $f$ of type $(1, \varepsilon)$
on $\Gamma_0(N)$ that is an eigenvector of the $T_p$, $p \nmid N$, a bound of Rankin for the mean square of the
eigenvalues shows that, outside a set of primes of small upper density, the eigenvalues $a_p$ take finitely many values
(§5). Multiplying $f$ by an Eisenstein series congruent to $1$ modulo $\ell$ moves it to weight at least $2$, where
Deligne's $\lambda$-adic representations exist, and reduction modulo $\lambda$ gives representations of
$\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ in characteristic $\ell$ attached to $f$ (§6). For the primes $\ell$ that split
completely in the field of the eigenvalues, a bound on the orders of semisimple subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_\ell)$
with few characteristic polynomials (§7) makes the images of these representations uniformly bounded; so the $a_p$
are sums of two roots of unity, and for large $\ell$ the representation in characteristic $\ell$ lifts to a complex
representation with finite image, which Rankin's bound makes irreducible (§8). An Eisenstein series gives a sum of
two characters (§8.1).
