# Surjective arboreal Galois representations

For a field $K$, a polynomial $f \in K[X]$ and $P \in K$, the index condition $\iota_K(f, P) = 1$ holds when for every $n$ the polynomial $f^{\circ n}(X) - P$ is separable and its splitting field has degree $(d!)^{1 + d + \dots + d^{n-1}}$ over $K$, where $d = \deg f$ and $f^{\circ n}$ is the $n$-fold composite.
