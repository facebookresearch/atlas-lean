# Odoni's conjecture fails over some Hilbertian field

It is not the case that for every Hilbertian field $K$ of characteristic zero and every $d \ge 2$ there are a monic $f \in K[X]$ of degree $d$ and $P \in K$ such that for every $n$ the polynomial $f^{\circ n}(X) - P$ is separable with splitting field of degree $(d!)^{1 + d + \dots + d^{n-1}}$ over $K$, the full automorphism group of the $d$-ary rooted tree of depth $n$; here a field $K$ is Hilbertian (in Serre's thin-set form) when for every finite set of polynomials $f(T, X) \in K[T][X]$, each irreducible and of degree at least $2$ in $X$, and every finite set $S \subseteq K$, there is $a \in K \setminus S$ such that no $f(a, X)$ has a root in $K$.

This refutes [Odoni's conjecture on arboreal Galois representations](odoni-conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Dittmann and Kadets (2022), cited under Sources.
