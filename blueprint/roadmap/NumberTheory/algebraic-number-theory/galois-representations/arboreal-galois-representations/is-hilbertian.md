# The thin-set Hilbertian condition on a field

A field $K$ satisfies this condition when for every finite set of polynomials $f(T, X) \in K[T][X]$, each irreducible in $K[T][X]$ and of degree at least $2$ in $X$, and every finite set $S \subseteq K$, there is $a \in K \setminus S$ such that no $f(a, X)$ has a root in $K$.

The condition places no separability requirement on $f$ in $X$. In characteristic zero every irreducible $f$ is separable in $X$, and the condition is Serre's thin-set form of Hilbertianity. In characteristic $p > 0$ it is stronger than the usual notion, which tests only polynomials separable in $X$: for instance, over a perfect field of characteristic $p$ the polynomial $X^p - T$ is irreducible of degree $p$ in $X$, yet every specialisation $X^p - a$ has a root, so no perfect field of characteristic $p$ satisfies the condition. [Odoni's conjecture](odoni-conjecture.md) applies the condition only to fields of characteristic zero.
