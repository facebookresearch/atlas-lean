# Hilbertian fields

A field $K$ is Hilbertian (in Serre's thin-set form) when for every finite set of polynomials $f(T, X) \in K[T][X]$, each irreducible and of degree at least $2$ in $X$, and every finite set $S \subseteq K$, there is $a \in K \setminus S$ such that no $f(a, X)$ has a root in $K$.
