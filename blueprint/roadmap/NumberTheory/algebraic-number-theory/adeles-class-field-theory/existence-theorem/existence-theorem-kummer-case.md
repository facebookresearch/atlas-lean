# Key case of the existence theorem

Let $p$ be a prime and $K$ a number field containing a primitive $p$-th root of unity, with idèle class
group $C_K$. Then every open subgroup $V$ of $C_K$ such that $C_K / V$ is finite and killed by $p$ is a norm
group: $V = \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(C_L)$ for some finite abelian extension $L/K$, with
$\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K} \colon C_L \to C_K$ induced by the idèle norm.

Milne's proof. Choose a finite set $S$ of places of $K$ containing the infinite places and the places
above $p$, and large enough that $\mathbb{I}_K = K^\times \mathbb{I}_{K,S}$ with
$\mathbb{I}_{K,S} = \prod_{v \in S} K_v^\times \times \prod_{v \notin S} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$ (possible as
the class group is finite, by [Minkowski's bound](../../number-fields-embeddings/minkowski-bound.md)), and also, given $V$, such that the preimage $U$ of $V$ in $\mathbb{I}_K$
contains $\prod_{v \notin S} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$. Let $s = |S|$, let $L = K[U(S)^{1/p}]$ be the
[Kummer extension of the S-units](../cohomology-of-idele-classes/kummer-extension-of-s-units.md), of degree
$p^s$ and unramified outside $S$, and let
$E = \prod_{v \in S} K_v^{\times p} \times \prod_{v \notin S} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$. Then
$E \subseteq \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L)$: at $v \in S$ because
$K_v^\times / \mathrm{Nm}_{L_w/K_v}(L_w^\times) \cong \mathrm{Gal}(L_w/K_v)$ is killed by $p$, by the
[local Artin map](../../../p-adic-numbers/local-class-field-theory.md) (at a real place,
$\mathbb{R}^{\times 2} = \mathbb{R}_{>0} = \mathrm{Nm}_{\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}}(\mathbb{C}^\times)$); at
$v \notin S$ because $L_w/K_v$ is unramified. So $K^\times E \subseteq K^\times \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L)$,
and the second group has index $[L : K] = p^s$ by the
[global reciprocity law](../reciprocity-law/global-reciprocity-law.md). For the reverse inclusion, since
$\mathbb{I}_K = K^\times \mathbb{I}_{K,S}$ and $K^\times \cap \mathbb{I}_{K,S} = U(S)$,

$$(\mathbb{I}_K : K^\times E) = (\mathbb{I}_{K,S} : E) \, / \, (U(S) : K^\times \cap E)$$

(Milne's Lemma VII.6.5). Here $(\mathbb{I}_{K,S} : E) = \prod_{v \in S} (K_v^\times : K_v^{\times p}) = p^{2s}$,
by the [local index of $p$-th powers](../../../p-adic-numbers/index-of-n-th-powers.md) and the
[product formula](../adeles-and-ideles/adelic-norm-principal-embedding.md) applied to $p$; and
$K^\times \cap E = U(S)^p$, because an $S$-unit that is a $p$-th power in $K_v$ for every $v \in S$ is a
$p$-th power in $K$ ([S-units that are local p-th powers are global p-th powers](../cohomology-of-idele-classes/local-powers-are-global-powers.md),
with $T = \emptyset$; Milne's Proposition VII.9.2), so $(U(S) : K^\times \cap E) = p^s$ by
[Dirichlet's S-unit theorem](../../discriminants-ramification/s-unit-theorem.md). Hence
$(\mathbb{I}_K : K^\times E) = p^s$ and $K^\times E = K^\times \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L)$. Finally $U$
contains $\mathbb{I}_K^p$, since $C_K / V$ is killed by $p$, and $\prod_{v \notin S} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$, so
$U \supseteq K^\times E$, and $V$ contains the norm group $\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(C_L)$, the image of $K^\times E$; it
is a norm group because [subgroups containing a norm group are norm groups](overgroups-of-norm-groups.md).

Milne's text computes only the numerator $(\mathbb{I}_{K,S} : E)$ of this index before concluding; the
equality $K^\times \cap E = U(S)^p$ that the conclusion also needs is his Proposition VII.9.2.
