# Inertia group at a finite place

For an extension $M/K$ of number fields and a finite place $v$ of $K$, $G_0(v)$ is the inertia group of $\mathfrak{P}_v$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$, the automorphisms acting trivially on $\mathcal{O}_M/\mathfrak{P}_v$, where $\mathfrak{P}_v$ is a chosen maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_M$ lying over the finite place $v$ of $K$.
