# Swan conductor of a Galois character

For an extension $M/K$ of number fields, a character $\psi : \operatorname{Gal}(M/K) \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ and a finite place $v$ of $K$, the rational number $\operatorname{sw}_v(\psi) = \sum_{i \ge 1} \frac{|G_i(v)|}{|G_0(v)|}[\psi|_{G_i(v)} \ne 1]$, where $G_i(v)$ is the subgroup of $\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$ acting trivially on $\mathcal{O}_M/\mathfrak{P}_v^{i+1}$ and $\mathfrak{P}_v$ is a chosen maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_M$ lying over the finite place $v$ of $K$.
