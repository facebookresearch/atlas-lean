# Primes dividing the Artin conductor are the ramified ones

Let $M/K$ be a Galois extension of number fields, a character $\psi : \operatorname{Gal}(M/K) \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ and $v$ a finite place of $K$. Then $v$ divides the Artin conductor $\mathfrak{f}(\psi)$ if and only if $\psi$ is ramified at $v$, that is, nontrivial on the inertia group at $v$.
