# Unramified Galois characters

For an extension $M/K$ of number fields, a character $\psi : \operatorname{Gal}(M/K) \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ and a finite place $v$ of $K$, $\psi$ is unramified at $v$ when $\psi$ is trivial on $G_0(v)$, where $G_0(v)$ is the inertia group of $\mathfrak{P}_v$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$, the automorphisms acting trivially on $\mathcal{O}_M/\mathfrak{P}_v$ and $\mathfrak{P}_v$ is a chosen maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_M$ lying over the finite place $v$ of $K$.
