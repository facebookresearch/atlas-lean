# Higher ramification groups

For an extension $M/K$ of number fields, a finite place $v$ of $K$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, $G_i(v)$ is the subgroup of $\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$ acting trivially on $\mathcal{O}_M/\mathfrak{P}_v^{i+1}$, where $\mathfrak{P}_v$ is a chosen maximal ideal of $\mathcal{O}_M$ lying over the finite place $v$ of $K$.
