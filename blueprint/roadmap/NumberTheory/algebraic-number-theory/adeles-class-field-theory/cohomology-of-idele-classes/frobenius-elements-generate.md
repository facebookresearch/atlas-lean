# Frobenius elements generate the Galois group of a solvable extension

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields with solvable Galois group $G$. For a prime
$\mathfrak{P}$ of $\mathcal{O}_L$ unramified over $K$, lying over $\mathfrak{p}$, let
$(\mathfrak{P}, L/K) \in G$ be its Frobenius element, the unique $\sigma \in G$ with
$\sigma(x) \equiv x^{N\mathfrak{p}} \pmod{\mathfrak{P}}$ for all $x \in \mathcal{O}_L$.

1. If $L \neq K$, then infinitely many primes of $K$ do not split completely in $L$.
2. For every finite set $T$ of primes of $\mathcal{O}_L$ containing the primes that ramify over $K$, the
   Frobenius elements $(\mathfrak{P}, L/K)$ of the primes $\mathfrak{P} \notin T$ generate $G$.
3. If $L/K$ is abelian and $\mathfrak{m}$ is a modulus of $K$ such that every prime of $K$ not dividing
   its finite part $\mathfrak{m}_0$ is unramified in $L$, then the
   [Artin map](../artin-map/artin-map.md) from the group of fractional ideals of $K$ coprime to
   $\mathfrak{m}_0$ to $\mathrm{Gal}(L/K)$, which sends such a prime $\mathfrak{p}$ to the Frobenius
   element of the primes above it, is surjective.

Milne's proof. For part 1, let $S$ be a finite set of places of $K$ containing the infinite places and
every prime that does not split completely, and let $D$ be the group of idèles whose components at the
places of $S$ are $1$. For $v \notin S$ every $L_w$ with $w \mid v$ equals $K_v$, so
$D \subseteq \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L)$, and $K^\times D$ is dense in $\mathbb{I}_K$ by the weak
approximation theorem; the [density criterion](norm-density-criterion.md) gives $L = K$. For part 2,
enlarge $T$ to be stable under $G$; the subgroup $H$ generated by the Frobenius elements of the primes
outside $T$ is then normal, since $\tau (\mathfrak{P}, L/K) \tau^{-1} = (\tau \mathfrak{P}, L/K)$. The
[restriction](../../../../RingTheory/ramification/frobenius-elements/restrict-normal-hom-eq-of-is-arith-frob.md)
of each such Frobenius element to the fixed field $E = L^H$ is the Frobenius element in $E/K$, which is
trivial, so every prime of $K$ below no prime of $T$
[splits completely](../../../../RingTheory/ramification/frobenius-elements/frobenius-elements-and-complete-splitting/arith-frob-at-eq-one-iff-ncard-primes-ov-2.md)
in $E$. As $E/K$ is solvable, part 1 gives $E = K$, so $H = G$. Part 3 follows from part 2, since the
[Artin map sends a prime to the Frobenius element](../artin-map/artin-map-prime-coprime-unit.md) at the
primes above it.
