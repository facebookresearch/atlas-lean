# Second inequality for Kummer extensions of prime exponent

Let $p$ be a prime, let $K$ be a number field containing a primitive $p$-th root of unity, and let $L/K$
be a finite abelian extension whose Galois group is killed by $p$, of degree $[L : K] = p^r$. Let
$\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K} \colon \mathbb{I}_L \to \mathbb{I}_K$ be the idèle norm,
$\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(b)_v = \prod_{w \mid v} \mathrm{Nm}_{L_w/K_v}(b_w)$. Then the index
$(\mathbb{I}_K : K^\times \mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L))$ is finite and divides $p^r$.

Milne's proof. By [Kummer theory](../../../../FieldTheory/galois-theory/kummer-theory.md),
$L = K[a_1^{1/p}, \dots, a_r^{1/p}]$ for some $a_i \in K^\times$. Choose a finite set $S$ of places of $K$
containing the infinite places, the places above $p$ and the places where some $a_i$ is not a unit, and
large enough that $\mathbb{I}_K = K^\times \prod_{v \in S} K_v^\times \prod_{v \notin S} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$
(possible because the class group of $K$ is finite, by [Minkowski's bound](../../number-fields-embeddings/minkowski-bound.md)), and let
$s = |S|$. The
[Kummer extension of the S-units](kummer-extension-of-s-units.md) $M = K[U(S)^{1/p}]$ contains $L$, has
degree $p^s$ and is unramified outside $S$; write $[M : L] = p^t$, so $r + t = s$. Next comes a finite set
$T$ of places of $K$, disjoint from $S$, at which $L_w = K_v$ and whose Frobenius elements in $M/K$ form a
basis of $\mathrm{Gal}(M/L)$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ (Lemma VII.6.2). For a prime $w$ of $M$ above a place
$v \notin S$, the extension $M_w/K_v$ is unramified, hence cyclic, and its group, a subgroup of
$\mathrm{Gal}(M/K)$, is killed by $p$, so $[M_w : K_v]$ is $1$ or $p$; hence if $M_w \ne L_w$, then
$L_w = K_v$ and the Frobenius element of $w$ over $L$ is its Frobenius element over $K$. Since the
[Frobenius elements generate](frobenius-elements-generate.md) $\mathrm{Gal}(M/L)$, applied to the abelian
extension $M/L$, there are primes $w_1, \dots, w_t$ of $M$, above places of $K$ outside $S$, whose Frobenius
elements over $L$ form a basis of $\mathrm{Gal}(M/L)$; these are nontrivial, so $M_{w_i} \ne L_{w_i}$, and
the places of $K$ below the $w_i$ form the set $T$. The group

$$E = \prod_{v \in S} K_v^{\times p} \times \prod_{v \in T} K_v^\times \times \prod_{v \notin S \cup T} \mathcal{O}_v^\times$$

is contained in $\mathrm{Nm}_{L/K}(\mathbb{I}_L)$ (Lemma VII.6.4): at $v \in S$ because
$K_v^\times / \mathrm{Nm}_{L_w/K_v}(L_w^\times) \cong \mathrm{Gal}(L_w/K_v)$ is killed by $p$, by the
[local Artin map](../../../p-adic-numbers/local-class-field-theory.md) (at a real place,
$\mathbb{R}^{\times 2} = \mathbb{R}_{>0} = \mathrm{Nm}_{\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}}(\mathbb{C}^\times)$); at $v \in T$ because $L_w = K_v$;
elsewhere because $L_w/K_v$ is unramified. It remains to show $(\mathbb{I}_K : K^\times E) = p^r$, which
Milne computes as $p^{2s} / p^{s+t}$: the factor $p^{2s} = \prod_{v \in S} (K_v^\times : K_v^{\times p})$
comes from the [local index of $p$-th powers](../../../p-adic-numbers/index-of-n-th-powers.md) and the
[product formula](../adeles-and-ideles/adelic-norm-principal-embedding.md) applied to $p$, and the factor
$p^{s+t} = (U(S \cup T) : U(S \cup T)^p)$ from
[Dirichlet's S-unit theorem](../../discriminants-ramification/s-unit-theorem.md), after showing that
$K^\times \cap E = U(S \cup T)^p$: Lemma VII.6.9 shows that $U(S)$ maps onto
$\prod_{v \in T} \mathcal{O}_v^\times / \mathcal{O}_v^{\times p}$, and then
[S-units that are local p-th powers are global p-th powers](local-powers-are-global-powers.md)
(Proposition VII.6.10).
