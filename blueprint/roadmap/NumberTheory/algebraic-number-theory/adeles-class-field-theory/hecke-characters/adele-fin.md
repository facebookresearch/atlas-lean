# Finite part of an adele

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here, with finite part $\mathbb{A}_{R,K,f}$ (the restricted product of the completions $K_v$ over the height-one primes $v$ of $R$). The ring homomorphism $\mathbb{A}_{R,K} \to \mathbb{A}_{R,K,f}$ sending an adele to its finite part.
