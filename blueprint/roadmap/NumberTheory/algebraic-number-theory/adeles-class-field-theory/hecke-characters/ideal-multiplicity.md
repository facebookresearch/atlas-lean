# Multiplicity of a prime in an ideal

For a number field $K$, a finite place $v$ and an ideal $\mathfrak{f}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$, the exponent of $v$ in the prime factorization of $\mathfrak{f}$ when $\mathfrak{f} \ne 0$. For the zero ideal $(0)$ the value is the natural number $0$, not an infinite order.
