# Hecke characters with a given modulus

For a number field $K$ and an ideal $\mathfrak{f}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$, a character $\chi : \mathbb{A}_K^\times \to \mathbb{C}^\times$ admits the modulus $\mathfrak{f}$ when $\mathfrak{f} \ne 0$ and $\chi(u) = 1$ for every idele $u$ with infinite part $1$ such that, at every finite place $v$, $|u_v|_v = 1$ and $\operatorname{ord}_v(u_v - 1) \ge \operatorname{ord}_v(\mathfrak{f})$.
