# Ideles supported at one place

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here, with finite part $\mathbb{A}_{R,K,f}$ (the restricted product of the completions $K_v$ over the height-one primes $v$ of $R$), and let $v$ be a height-one prime of $R$. The group homomorphism $K_v^\times \to \mathbb{A}_{R,K,f}^\times$ sending $t$ to the finite idele with component $t$ at $v$ and $1$ at every other place.
