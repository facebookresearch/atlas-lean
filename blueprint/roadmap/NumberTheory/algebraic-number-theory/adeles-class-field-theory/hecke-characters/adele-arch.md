# Infinite part of an adele

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here. The ring homomorphism $\mathbb{A}_{R,K} \to \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty}$ sending an adele to its infinite part.
