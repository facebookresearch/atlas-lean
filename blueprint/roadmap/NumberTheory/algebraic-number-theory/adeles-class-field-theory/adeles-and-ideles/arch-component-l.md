# Component of the archimedean base change map over L

Let $L/K$ be a field extension and $w$ an infinite place of $L$. The map $L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty} \to L_w$, $x \otimes a \mapsto x \cdot a_v$, as an $L$-algebra homomorphism for the $L$-algebra structure through the left factor, where for an infinite place $w$ of $L$, $v$ is the infinite place of $K$ below $w$ and $K_v \to L_w$ the induced map of completions.
