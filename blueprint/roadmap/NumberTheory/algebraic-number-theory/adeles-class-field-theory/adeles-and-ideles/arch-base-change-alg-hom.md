# Archimedean base change as an L-algebra map

Let $L/K$ be an extension of number fields. The archimedean base change map as an $L$-algebra homomorphism for the $L$-algebra structure on $L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty}$ through the left factor; here the archimedean base change map $L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty} \to \mathbb{A}_{L,\infty}$ sends $x \otimes a$ to the infinite adele with component $x \cdot a_v$ at each infinite place $w$ of $L$, where for an infinite place $w$ of $L$, $v$ is the infinite place of $K$ below $w$ and $K_v \to L_w$ the induced map of completions.
