# Diagonal homomorphism from GL₂(K) to adelic GL₂

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here. The group homomorphism $\mathrm{GL}_2(K) \to \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_{R,K})$ induced entrywise by the diagonal homomorphism $K \to \mathbb{A}_{R,K}$. At this generality it need not be injective: for $R = K = \mathbb{F}_3$ there are no places, the adele ring is the one-element ring, and the target group is trivial. For a number field $K$ with $R = \mathcal{O}_K$ it is the usual diagonal embedding into $\mathrm{GL}_2$ of the adeles of $K$; for other choices of $R$ the finite part has one factor for each height-one prime of $R$ only (for $K = \mathbb{Q}$ and $R = \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$, just $\mathbb{Q}_p$).
