# Diagonal homomorphism from GL₂(K) to adelic GL₂

For a Dedekind domain $R$ with fraction field $K$, the group homomorphism $\mathrm{GL}_2(K) \to \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_K)$ induced entrywise by the diagonal homomorphism $K \to \mathbb{A}_K$. At this generality it need not be injective: for $R = K = \mathbb{F}_3$ there are no places, the adele ring is the one-element ring, and the target group is trivial. For a global field it is the usual diagonal embedding.
