# Archimedean base change on pure tensors

Let $L/K$ be an extension of number fields. For $x \in L$, an infinite adele $a$ of $K$ and an infinite place $w$ of $L$, the component at $w$ of the base change of $x \otimes a$ is the product of the image of $a_v$ in $L_w$ and the image of $x$ in $L_w$, where for an infinite place $w$ of $L$, $v$ is the infinite place of $K$ below $w$ and $K_v \to L_w$ the induced map of completions.
