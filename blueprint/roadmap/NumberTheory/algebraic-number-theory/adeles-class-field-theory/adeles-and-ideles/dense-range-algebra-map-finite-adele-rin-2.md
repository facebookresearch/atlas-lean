# Density of a number field in its finite adeles and its consequence

For every number field $K$, the diagonal image of $K$ is dense in the finite adele ring $\mathbb{A}_{K,f}$, and for every Hausdorff topological ring $R$, two continuous ring homomorphisms $\mathbb{A}_{K,f} \to R$ that agree on $K$ are equal.
