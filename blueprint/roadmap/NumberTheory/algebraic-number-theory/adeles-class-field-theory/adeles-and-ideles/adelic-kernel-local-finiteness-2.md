# Rational points of GL₂ meet adelic compact sets finitely often

For every number field $F$, for every compact set $C \subseteq \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_F)$ and all $x, y \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_F)$, only finitely many $\gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_2(F)$ satisfy $x^{-1}\gamma y \in C$, with $\gamma$ embedded diagonally. This proves the proposition [Local finiteness of the adelic kernel](adelic-kernel-local-finiteness.md) for every number field.
