# Component of the archimedean base change map

Let $L/K$ be a field extension and $w$ an infinite place of $L$. The $K$-algebra homomorphism $L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty} \to L_w$ sending $x \otimes a$ to $x \cdot a_v$ in $L_w$, where for an infinite place $w$ of $L$, $v$ is the infinite place of $K$ below $w$ and $K_v \to L_w$ the induced map of completions.
