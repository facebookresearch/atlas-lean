# Archimedean base change of infinite adeles

Let $L/K$ be an extension of number fields. The ring homomorphism $L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}_{K,\infty} \to \mathbb{A}_{L,\infty}$ sending $x \otimes a$ to the infinite adele whose component at each infinite place $w$ of $L$ is $x \cdot a_v$, where for an infinite place $w$ of $L$, $v$ is the infinite place of $K$ below $w$ and $K_v \to L_w$ the induced map of completions.
