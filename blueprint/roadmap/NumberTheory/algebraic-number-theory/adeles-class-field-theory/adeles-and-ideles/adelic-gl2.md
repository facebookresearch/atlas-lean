# Adelic GL₂

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$ be the adele ring built from the infinite places of $K$ and the height-one primes of $R$, the finite places here. The group $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_{R,K})$ of invertible $2 \times 2$ matrices over $\mathbb{A}_{R,K}$.
