# Every imaginary quadratic discriminant has a conductor decomposition

Every imaginary quadratic discriminant $D$, that is, every integer $D < 0$ with $D \equiv 0$ or
$1 \pmod 4$, has a conductor decomposition: there are an integer $f \ge 1$ and a fundamental
imaginary quadratic discriminant $D_K$ with $D = f^2 D_K$.

Here $D_K$ is the discriminant of $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$ and $f$ is the conductor of the
order of discriminant $D$ in $K$.
