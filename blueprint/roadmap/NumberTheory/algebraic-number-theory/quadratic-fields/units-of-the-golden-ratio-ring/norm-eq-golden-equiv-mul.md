# Norm in the golden order

For integers $a, b$, the norm of $a + b\omega$ in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ equals $(a + b\varphi)(a + b(1 - \varphi)) = a^2 + ab - b^2$, where $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ with $\omega^2 = \omega + 1$ is the quadratic algebra over $\mathbb{Z}$ with elements $a + b\omega$, and $\varphi = (1 + \sqrt5)/2$ is the golden ratio.
