# Minkowski's bound: every ideal class of a number field contains an integral ideal of small norm

Let $K$ be a number field of degree $n = [K : \mathbb{Q}]$, with ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$, discriminant $d_K$ and $r_2$ complex places, that is, $r_2$ pairs of complex conjugate non-real embeddings of $K$ into $\mathbb{C}$. Then every class of the ideal class group of $\mathcal{O}_K$ is the class of a nonzero ideal $I$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ whose absolute norm $N(I) = |\mathcal{O}_K / I|$ satisfies

$$N(I) \le \left(\frac{4}{\pi}\right)^{r_2} \frac{n!}{n^n} \sqrt{|d_K|}.$$
