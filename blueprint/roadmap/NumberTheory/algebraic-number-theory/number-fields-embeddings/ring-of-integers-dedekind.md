# The ring of integers of a number field is a Dedekind domain

The ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$ of a number field $K$, the set of elements of $K$ that are integral over $\mathbb{Z}$, is a Dedekind domain. Mathlib proves exactly this for every number field $K$, a field of characteristic zero that is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb{Q}$, with $\mathcal{O}_K$ defined as the integral closure of $\mathbb{Z}$ in $K$. Here a Dedekind domain is an integral domain that is Noetherian, integrally closed in its field of fractions, and in which every nonzero prime ideal is maximal.
