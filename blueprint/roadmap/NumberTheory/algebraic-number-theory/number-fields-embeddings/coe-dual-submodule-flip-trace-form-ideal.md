# Dual of an ideal lattice under the trace form

Let $K$ be a number field and $I$ a nonzero fractional ideal of $K$. The dual lattice, with respect to the trace form on the mixed space $\mathbb{R}^{r_1} \times \mathbb{C}^{r_2}$, of the image of $I$ under the mixed embedding is the image under the mixed embedding of the trace dual $I^\vee = \{x \in K : \operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(xI) \subseteq \mathbb{Z}\}$.
