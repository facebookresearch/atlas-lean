# Covolume of the ring of integers in the mixed space

Let $K$ be a number field with $r_1$ real and $r_2$ complex places, and let $\Lambda \subset \mathbb{R}^{r_1} \times \mathbb{C}^{r_2}$
be the image of its ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$ under the mixed embedding, which sends $x$ to its images under the
$r_1$ real embeddings and under one complex embedding for each complex place. Then $\Lambda$ is a lattice whose
covolume, for Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^{r_1} \times \mathbb{C}^{r_2}$ with $\mathbb{C}$ identified with
$\mathbb{R}^2$, is

$$\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda) = 2^{-r_2} \sqrt{\lvert d_K \rvert},$$

where $d_K$ is the discriminant of $K$.
