# Dirichlet's S-unit theorem

Let $K$ be a number field with $r$ real and $s$ complex places, let $S$ be a finite set of places of $K$
containing all the infinite places, and let

$$U(S) = \{ a \in K^\times : \mathrm{ord}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a) = 0 \text{ for every finite place } \mathfrak{p} \notin S \}$$

be the group of $S$-units of $K$. Then $U(S)$ is finitely generated, its torsion subgroup is the finite
cyclic group $\mu(K)$ of roots of unity in $K$, and its rank is $|S| - 1$, so that
$U(S) \cong \mu(K) \times \mathbb{Z}^{|S| - 1}$. Moreover, the homomorphism

$$\lambda \colon U(S) \to \mathbb{R}^S, \qquad a \mapsto (\log |a|_v)_{v \in S},$$

where $|\cdot|_v$ is the normalized absolute value at $v$ (the square of the usual one at a complex
place), has kernel $\mu(K)$, and its image is a full lattice in the hyperplane
$\{ x \in \mathbb{R}^S : \sum_{v \in S} x_v = 0 \}$: a subgroup generated by a basis of that
$(|S| - 1)$-dimensional space.

For $S$ the set of infinite places, $U(S)$ is the unit group of the ring of integers and this is
[Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-unit-theorem.md): the logarithmic image of the units of the ring of
integers is a full lattice in the trace-zero hyperplane of $\mathbb{R}^{S_\infty}$, which leaving out one
coordinate identifies with $\mathbb{R}^{r+s-1}$ (Milne, Proposition 5.8 and Theorem 5.9). Milne states the
rank for $S$ given by the infinite places together with a finite set of $t$ prime ideals, as
$r + s + t - 1$, and proves it from that case and the finiteness of the class group: if $h$ is the class
number, $\mathfrak{p}^h$ is principal for every prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, so the valuations at the
finite places of $S$ map $U(S)$ onto a subgroup of finite index of $\mathbb{Z}^t$, with kernel the units of
the ring of integers (Milne, Theorem 5.11). The image of $\lambda$ lies in the hyperplane by the
[product formula](../adeles-class-field-theory/adeles-and-ideles/adelic-norm-principal-embedding.md),
since $|a|_v = 1$ at every place $v \notin S$.

## Sources

- J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, version 3.08 (2020),
  <https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf>, Chapter 5: Theorem 5.11 (the $S$-units are finitely
  generated of rank $r + s + \#S - 1$, for a finite set $S$ of prime ideals, with torsion subgroup
  $\mu(K)$) and its proof, Proposition 5.8 and Theorem 5.9 (the logarithmic image of the units is a full
  lattice in the trace-zero hyperplane).
- J. S. Milne, *Class Field Theory*, version 4.03 (2020),
  <https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf>, Section VII.3, proof of Proposition 3.1 ("the proof
  of the $T$-unit theorem shows $M_0$ is a lattice in $V_0$", with $M_0$ the logarithmic image of the
  $T$-units and $V_0$ the trace-zero hyperplane) and Section VII.6 (the form
  $U(S) \approx \mathbb{Z}^{s-1} \times U(S)_{\mathrm{tors}}$, $s = |S|$).
