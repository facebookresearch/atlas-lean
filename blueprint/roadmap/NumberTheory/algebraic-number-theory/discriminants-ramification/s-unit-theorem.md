# Dirichlet's S-unit theorem

Let $K$ be a number field with $r$ real and $s$ complex places, let $S$ be a finite set of places of $K$
containing all the infinite places, and let

$$U(S) = \{ a \in K^\times : \mathrm{ord}_{\mathfrak{p}}(a) = 0 \text{ for every finite place } \mathfrak{p} \notin S \}$$

be the group of $S$-units of $K$. Then $U(S)$ is finitely generated, its torsion subgroup is the finite
cyclic group $\mu(K)$ of roots of unity in $K$, and its rank is $|S| - 1$, so that
$U(S) \cong \mu(K) \times \mathbb{Z}^{|S| - 1}$. Moreover, the homomorphism

$$\lambda \colon U(S) \to \mathbb{R}^S, \qquad a \mapsto (\log |a|_v)_{v \in S},$$

where $|\cdot|_v$ is the normalized absolute value at $v$ (the square of the usual one at a complex
place), has kernel $\mu(K)$, and its image is a full lattice in the hyperplane
$\{ x \in \mathbb{R}^S : \sum_{v \in S} x_v = 0 \}$: a subgroup generated by a basis of that
$(|S| - 1)$-dimensional space.

For $S$ the set of infinite places, $U(S)$ is the unit group of the ring of integers and this is
Dirichlet's unit theorem. Milne states the rank for $S$ given by the infinite places together with a
finite set of $t$ prime ideals, as $r + s + t - 1$. The image of $\lambda$ lies in the hyperplane by
the [product formula](../adeles-class-field-theory/adeles-and-ideles/adelic-norm-principal-embedding.md),
since $|a|_v = 1$ at every place $v \notin S$.

  $U(S) \approx \mathbb{Z}^{s-1} \times U(S)_{\mathrm{tors}}$, $s = |S|$).
