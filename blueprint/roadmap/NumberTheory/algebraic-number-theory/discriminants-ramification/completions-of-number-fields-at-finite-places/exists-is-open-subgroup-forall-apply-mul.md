# Open subgroups of units fixing compactly supported locally constant functions

Let $K$ be a number field, $v$ a finite place of $K$ and $\Phi : K_v \times K_v \to \mathbb{C}$ locally constant with compact support. Then there is an open subgroup $U$ of $K_v^\times$ with $\Phi(bt, z) = \Phi(b, z)$ for all $t \in U$ and $b, z \in K_v$.
