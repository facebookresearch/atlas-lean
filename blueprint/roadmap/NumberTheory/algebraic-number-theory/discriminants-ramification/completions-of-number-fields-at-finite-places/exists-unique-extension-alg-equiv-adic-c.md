# Unique extension of a place when the completed tensor product is a field

Let $M/F$ be an extension of number fields and $v$ a finite place of $F$. If $M \otimes_F F_v$ is a field, then $v$ has exactly one extension $w$ to $M$, and $M \otimes_F F_v \cong M_w$ as $F_v$-algebras by an isomorphism sending $x \otimes 1$ to $x$, with $[M_w : F_v] = [M : F]$.
