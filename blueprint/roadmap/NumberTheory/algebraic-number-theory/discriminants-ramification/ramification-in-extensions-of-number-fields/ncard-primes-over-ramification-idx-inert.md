# Primes in Galois extensions of prime degree split, are inert, or ramify totally

Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension of number fields of prime degree $\ell$, $\mathfrak{p}$ a nonzero prime of $\mathcal{O}_K$ and $\mathfrak{P}$ a prime of $\mathcal{O}_L$ over $\mathfrak{p}$. Then one of the following holds, with $g$ the number of primes over $\mathfrak{p}$ and $e, f$ the ramification index and inertia degree of $\mathfrak{P}$: $g = \ell$, $e = f = 1$; or $e = \ell$, $g = f = 1$; or $f = \ell$, $g = e = 1$.
