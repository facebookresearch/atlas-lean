# Lenstra's bound for the different

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete valuation rings with maximal ideals $\mathfrak{m}_A$ and
$\mathfrak{m}_B$, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an $A$-module,
such that the extension $L/K$ of fraction fields is separable; let $n$ be its degree. Let $e$ and
$r$ be natural numbers with $\mathfrak{m}_A B = \mathfrak{m}_B^e$ and such that the different ideal
of $B$ over $A$ is $\mathfrak{m}_B^r$. Then

$$r \le e - 1 + e \cdot \operatorname{ord}_A(n)$$

in $\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, where $\operatorname{ord}_A$ is the normalized valuation of $A$
($\operatorname{ord}_A(n) = \infty$ when $n = 0$ in $A$). No separability of the residue field
extension is assumed.

Javanpeykar states it for a discrete valuation ring $A$ of characteristic $0$ and the integral
closure $B$ of $A$ in $L$; then $L/K$ is separable and $B$ is finite over $A$, and the bound is the
case of characteristic $0$, where $\operatorname{ord}_A(n)$ is finite. When the residue field
extension is separable, the bound follows from the remark after Serre's Proposition III.6.13
(Dedekind's discriminant bound), as Javanpeykar notes; the general case is needed for imperfect
residue fields.
