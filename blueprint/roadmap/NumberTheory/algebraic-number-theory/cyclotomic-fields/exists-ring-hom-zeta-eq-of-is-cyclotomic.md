# Embedding a cyclotomic extension of the rationals

Let $n \ge 1$ be a natural number, let $K$ be an $n$-th cyclotomic
extension of $\mathbb{Q}$, and let $\zeta \in K$ be its distinguished primitive $n$-th root of
unity. If $L$ is a field of characteristic zero and $\xi \in L$ is a primitive $n$-th root of
unity, then there is a ring homomorphism $\varphi : K \to L$ with $\varphi(\zeta) = \xi$.
