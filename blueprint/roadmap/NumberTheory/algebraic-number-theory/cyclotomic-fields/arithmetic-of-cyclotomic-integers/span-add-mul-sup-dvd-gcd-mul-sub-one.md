# Common ideal factor of two cyclotomic linear forms

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain, $p$ a prime, $\zeta \in R$ a primitive $p$-th root of unity, $\eta_1 \ne \eta_2$ two $p$-th roots of unity in $R$, and $x, y \in R$. Then the ideal $(x + y\eta_1) + (x + y\eta_2)$ divides $\gcd((x), (y)) \cdot (\zeta - 1)$.
