# Integer divisibility of prime-order cyclotomic sums

Let $p$ be a prime, $L$ a field of characteristic zero that is a $p$-th cyclotomic extension of $\mathbb{Q}$, $\zeta$ a primitive $p$-th root of unity in the ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_L$, and $f_0, \dots, f_{p-1}$ integers. If an integer $m$ divides $\sum_{j < p} f_j\zeta^j$ in $\mathcal{O}_L$, then $m \mid f_i - f_j$ for all $i, j$.
