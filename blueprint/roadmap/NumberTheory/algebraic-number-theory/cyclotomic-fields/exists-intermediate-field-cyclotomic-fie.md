# Cyclic subextensions of prime-power cyclotomic extensions

For every number field $K$, prime $p$ and natural number $a$, there are $k$ and an intermediate field $K \subseteq L \subseteq K(\zeta_{p^k})$ of the $p^k$-th cyclotomic extension such that $L/K$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group and $[L : K] = p^a$.
