# Uniqueness of the generalized Frobenius number

For natural numbers $a_1, \dots, a_k$ and
$s$, if the integers $g$ and $g'$ are both the generalized Frobenius number $g(a_1, \dots, a_k; s)$,
then $g = g'$.
