# $2$-Sylvester number of three consecutive star numbers

Write $S_m$ for the $m$-th star
number $6m(m-1)+1$. For every natural number $n \ge 6$, the number of natural numbers $N$ for
which at most two triples $(x, y, z)$ of natural numbers satisfy
$x S_n + y S_{n+1} + z S_{n+2} = N$ is $33n^3 + 35n^2 + 20n + 2$.
