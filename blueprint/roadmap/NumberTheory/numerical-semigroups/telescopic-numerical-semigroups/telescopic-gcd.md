# Prefix gcds of a sequence

For a finite sequence $G = (g_1, \dots, g_k)$ of natural numbers
and a natural number $j$, $d_j$ is the greatest common divisor of the first $j$ entries of $G$
(of all of $G$ when $j > k$), with $d_0 = 0$.
