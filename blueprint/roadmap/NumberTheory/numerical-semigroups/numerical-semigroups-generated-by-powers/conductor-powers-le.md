# Dutch–Rickett conductor recursion for semigroups of $k$-th powers

Fix an integer
$k \ge 1$. For $m \ge 1$ let $S_m$ be the additive submonoid of $\mathbb{N}$ generated by
$m^k, (m+1)^k, (m+2)^k, \ldots$, and let $\tau(S_m)$ be its conductor, the least natural number
$c$ such that every $j \ge c$ lies in $S_m$. Then for every integer $n \ge 1$,

$$\tau(S_n) \le 2^k \, \tau(S_{\lceil n/2 \rceil}) + (2^k - 1)(2n)^k.$$

## Sources

- Fabián Arias, Jerson Borja and Jaider Terán, *A Note on Numerical Semigroups Generated by k-th Powers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A11, Proposition 2, inequality (3), quoting K. Dutch and C. Rickett (`integers-2025-A11-prop-2`).
