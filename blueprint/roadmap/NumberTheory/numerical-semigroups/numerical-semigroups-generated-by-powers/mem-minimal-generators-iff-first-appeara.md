# Minimal generators of the tail semigroups of an increasing sequence

Let
$x_0 < x_1 < x_2 < \cdots$ be a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers. For $m \ge 1$
let $S_m$ be the additive submonoid of $\mathbb{N}$ generated by $x_m, x_{m+1}, \ldots$, and let
$\beta(S_m)$ be its set of minimal generators, the nonzero elements of $S_m$ that are not the sum
of two nonzero elements of $S_m$. For $k \ge 1$ let $r_k$ be the least $m \ge 1$ with
$x_k \in \beta(S_m)$. Then for all integers $k, n \ge 1$, $x_k \in \beta(S_n)$ if and only if
$r_k \le n \le k$; and the embedding dimension $e(S_n) = |\beta(S_n)|$ equals the number of
integers $j \ge 1$ with $r_j \le n \le j$.

The paper's standing assumption $\gcd(x_n, x_{n+1}, \ldots) = 1$ is not needed and is dropped.

## Sources

- Fabián Arias, Jerson Borja and Jaider Terán, *A Note on Numerical Semigroups Generated by k-th Powers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A11, Lemma 1.
