# Embedding dimension bound from linear growth of first appearances

Let
$x_0 < x_1 < x_2 < \cdots$ be a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers. For $m \ge 1$
let $S_m$ be the additive submonoid of $\mathbb{N}$ generated by $x_m, x_{m+1}, \ldots$; call a
nonzero element of $S_m$ that is not the sum of two nonzero elements of $S_m$ a minimal generator
of $S_m$, and let the embedding dimension $e(S_m)$ be the number of minimal generators of $S_m$.
For $k \ge 1$ let $r_k$ be the least $m \ge 1$ such that $x_k$ is a minimal generator of $S_m$.
If $\alpha > 0$ is a real number with $r_k \ge \alpha k$ for all $k \ge 1$, then
$e(S_n) \le \lfloor n / \alpha \rfloor - n + 1$ for all $n \ge 1$.

The paper's standing assumption $\gcd(x_n, x_{n+1}, \ldots) = 1$ is not needed and is dropped.

## Sources

- Fabián Arias, Jerson Borja and Jaider Terán, *A Note on Numerical Semigroups Generated by k-th Powers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A11, Proposition 1.
