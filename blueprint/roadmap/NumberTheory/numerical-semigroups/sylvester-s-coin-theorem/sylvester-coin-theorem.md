# Sylvester's coin theorem

Let $a$ and $b$ be coprime natural numbers. Then the number of
natural numbers $n$ that cannot be written as $n = ax + by$ with natural numbers $x, y$ is
$(a - 1)(b - 1)/2$.

If $a = 0$ or $b = 0$, coprimality forces the other number to be $1$, every natural number is
representable, and both sides are $0$.

## Sources

- James Joseph Sylvester, *On Subinvariants, i.e. Semi-Invariants to Binary Quantics of an Unlimited Order*, American Journal of Mathematics 5 (1882), no. 1, 79–136; the count $(a - 1)(b - 1)/2$ of gaps for coprime $a$ and $b$ is on p. 134.
