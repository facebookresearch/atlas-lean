# Landen form series of the identity matrix

For the identity matrix $g$, with rows $(1, 0)$
and $(0, 1)$, the Landen form series $u \cdot G_g(u)$ is the constant power series $1$ in
$\mathbb{R}[[u]]$; that is, $G_g(t) = 1/t$.
