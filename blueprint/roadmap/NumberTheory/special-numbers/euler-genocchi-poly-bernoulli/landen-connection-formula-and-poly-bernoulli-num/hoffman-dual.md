# Hoffman dual index

For an index $k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)$ of positive integers write
$k\uparrow = (k_1, \dots, k_r + 1)$ and $k\rightarrow = (k_1, \dots, k_r, 1)$. The Hoffman dual
index $k^\vee$ is defined recursively by $(1)^\vee = (1)$, $(k\uparrow)^\vee = (k^\vee)\rightarrow$
and $(k\rightarrow)^\vee = (k^\vee)\uparrow$. For an arbitrary finite list $k$ of natural numbers, $k^\vee$ is the reverse of $R$ applied to the
reverse of $k$, where $R$ is the map of the reversed-list recursion for the Hoffman dual; in
particular the empty list is sent to the empty list.
