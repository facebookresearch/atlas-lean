# Genocchi numbers

For a natural number $n$, the Genocchi number $G_n \in \mathbb{Q}$ is
$n!$ times the coefficient of $x^n$ in the Genocchi power series
$\frac{2x}{e^x + 1} \in \mathbb{Q}[[x]]$, so that
$\frac{2x}{e^x + 1} = \sum_{n \ge 0} G_n \frac{x^n}{n!}$.
