# A composition operator applied to the constant $1$

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $S$ a formal power series over $R$. Its composition operator
sends the constant polynomial $1$ to the constant polynomial $S(0)$, the constant term of $S$.

## Sources

- Nouara Mokhtari, Rachid Boumahdi, and Farid Bencherif, "Generalizations of Several Explicit Formulas for Euler Polynomials", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A65, Section 1 (composition operators).
