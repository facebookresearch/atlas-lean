# Identities for the Appell sequence of a composition operator

Let $S$ be a formal power series over $\mathbb{C}$, let $\lambda \ne 0$ be a complex number, and let
$A_n(x) = S(D)(x^n)$, where $S(D)$ is the composition operator of $S$. Then for every $n \ge 0$

1. $A_{n+1}(x) - x A_n(x) = S'(D)(x^n)$, where $S'$ is the formal derivative of $S$;
2. $A_n(\lambda x) = \lambda^n\, \tilde S(D)(x^n)$, where $\tilde S(z) = S(z/\lambda)$; and
3. $(-1)^n A_n(\lambda - x) = T(D)(x^n)$, where $T(z) = e^{-\lambda z} S(-z)$.

## Sources

- Nouara Mokhtari, Rachid Boumahdi, and Farid Bencherif, "Generalizations of Several Explicit Formulas for Euler Polynomials", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A65, Lemma 2.1.
