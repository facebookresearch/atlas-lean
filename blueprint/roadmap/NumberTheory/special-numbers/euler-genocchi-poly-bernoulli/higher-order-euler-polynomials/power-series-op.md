# The composition operator of a formal power series

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $S = \sum_k s_k z^k$ a formal power series over $R$. The
composition operator $S(D)$ sends a polynomial $p$ over $R$ to

$$S(D)(p) = \sum_{k=0}^{\deg p} s_k\, D^k p,$$

where $D$ is differentiation and $\deg p$ is the degree of $p$ (taken as $0$ for the zero
polynomial); the terms with $k > \deg p$ vanish, so the sum is the full operator $\sum_k s_k D^k$.

- Nouara Mokhtari, Rachid Boumahdi, and Farid Bencherif, "Generalizations of Several Explicit Formulas for Euler Polynomials", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A65, Section 1 (composition operators).
