# Explicit formula for Euler polynomials of order $\alpha$

For every complex number $\alpha$ and natural numbers $n$ and $m$ with $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor \le m$,
the Euler polynomial of order $\alpha$ satisfies

$$E_n^{(\alpha)}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \frac{(-1)^k}{2^{n+k}} \binom{\alpha + k - 1}{k}
\sum_{j=0}^{2k} (-1)^j \binom{2k}{j} (2x - \alpha - k + j)^n,$$

where $\binom{\alpha + k - 1}{k} = \alpha(\alpha + 1)\cdots(\alpha + k - 1)/k!$.

## Sources

- Nouara Mokhtari, Rachid Boumahdi, and Farid Bencherif, "Generalizations of Several Explicit Formulas for Euler Polynomials", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A65, Theorem 4.4.
