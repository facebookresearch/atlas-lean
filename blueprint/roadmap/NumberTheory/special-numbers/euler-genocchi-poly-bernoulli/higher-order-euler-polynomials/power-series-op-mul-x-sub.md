# Commutator of a composition operator with multiplication by $x$

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $S$ a formal power series over $R$. For every polynomial $p$
over $R$,

$$S(D)(x\,p) - x\, S(D)(p) = S'(D)(p),$$

where $S(D)$ is the composition operator of $S$ and $S'$ is the formal derivative of $S$.

## Sources

- Nouara Mokhtari, Rachid Boumahdi, and Farid Bencherif, "Generalizations of Several Explicit Formulas for Euler Polynomials", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A65, Lemma 2.4.
