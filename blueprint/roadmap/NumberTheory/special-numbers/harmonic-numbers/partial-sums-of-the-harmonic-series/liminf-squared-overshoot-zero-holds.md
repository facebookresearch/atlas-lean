# Harmonic blocks can exceed one by o(1/n²)

For every real $\delta > 0$ there are infinitely many $n$ with $n^2 \varepsilon(n) < \delta$, where $\varepsilon(n) = \sum_{k=n}^{m(n)} 1/k - 1$, where $m(n)$ is the least $m$ with $\sum_{k=n}^{m} 1/k \ge 1$; that is, $\liminf_n n^2 \varepsilon(n) = 0$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 314 (small overshoots of harmonic blocks over one)](liminf-squared-overshoot-zero.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Lim and Steinerberger (2024), cited under Sources.
