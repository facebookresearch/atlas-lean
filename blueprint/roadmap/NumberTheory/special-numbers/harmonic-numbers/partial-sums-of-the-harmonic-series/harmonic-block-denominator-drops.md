# Erdős Problem 290 (decreasing denominators of harmonic blocks)

The statement that for every $a \ge 1$ there is $b > a$ such that the reduced denominator of $\sum_{n=a}^{b+1} 1/n$ is smaller than the reduced denominator of $\sum_{n=a}^{b} 1/n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
