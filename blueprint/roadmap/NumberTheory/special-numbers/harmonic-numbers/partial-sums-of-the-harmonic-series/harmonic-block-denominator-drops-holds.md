# The denominators of harmonic blocks eventually drop

For every $a \ge 1$ there is $b > a$ such that the reduced denominator of $\sum_{n=a}^{b+1} 1/n$ is smaller than the reduced denominator of $\sum_{n=a}^{b} 1/n$.
