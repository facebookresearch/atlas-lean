# The value $H_1^{(r)}(1) = 1$

For every complex number $r$, the generalized
hyperharmonic number $H_1^{[r]}(1)$ equals $1$.

## Sources

- Paul Thomas Young, *Parametric Euler Sums of Generalized Hyperharmonic Numbers*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A81, Section 2, (2.4) (`integers-2026-A81-cor-1`).
