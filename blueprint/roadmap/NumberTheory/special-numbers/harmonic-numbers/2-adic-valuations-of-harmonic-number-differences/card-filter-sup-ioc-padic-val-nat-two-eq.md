# Counting intervals with a given largest 2-adic valuation

For natural numbers $n$ and $a$, the number of $m < n$ with $\mu_2(m, n) = a$ is $2^a b_a$, where $b_a$ is the binary digit of $n$ at position $a$ and $\mu_p(m, n) = \max_{m < k \le n} \nu_p(k)$ is the largest $p$-adic valuation of an element of $\{m + 1, \dots, n\}$ (with $p = 2$).
