# Lower bound for the 2-adic valuation of elementary symmetric functions of reciprocals

For natural numbers $1 \le k \le n$, $\nu_2(E_k(n, n)) \ge \sum_{i=1}^{k} \bigl(-\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + \lfloor \log_2 i \rfloor\bigr) = -k\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + (k + 1)\lfloor \log_2 k \rfloor - 2\bigl(2^{\lfloor \log_2 k \rfloor} - 1\bigr)$, where for natural numbers $n$, $N$ and $k$, $E_k(n, N)$ is the $k$-th elementary symmetric function of the numbers $1/i$ with $\max(n - N, 0) < i \le n$, and $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Lemma 2.
