# 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers

For natural numbers $m \le n$, $\nu_2(H_n - H_m) = -\max_{m < k \le n} \nu_2(k)$, where $H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 1/k$ and $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation on $\mathbb{Q}$ with the convention $\nu_2(0) = 0$; when $m = n$ both sides are $0$, the maximum over the empty range being $0$.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27: the unique-most-negative-term argument following Theorem 2 gives the formula for $m < n$; Lemma 1 is the special case $H_{2n} - H_n$. The convention at $m = n$ is this page's.
