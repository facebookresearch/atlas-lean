# 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers

For natural numbers $m \le n$, $\nu_2(H_n - H_m) = -\max_{m < k \le n} \nu_2(k)$, where $H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 1/k$ and $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation (the maximum is $0$ when $m = n$).

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Lemma 1.
