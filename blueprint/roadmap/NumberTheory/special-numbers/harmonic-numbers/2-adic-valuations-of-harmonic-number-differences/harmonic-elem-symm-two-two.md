# Elementary symmetric functions of 1 and 1/2

$E_1(2, 2) = 3/2$ and $E_2(2, 2) = 1/2$, where for natural numbers $n$, $N$ and $k$, $E_k(n, N)$ is the $k$-th elementary symmetric function of the numbers $1/i$ with $\max(n - N, 0) < i \le n$.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Section 4.
