# Equality in the 2-adic lower bound for elementary symmetric functions

For every $k \ge 1$ and every $m$ there is $n \ge \max(m, k)$ with $\nu_2(E_k(n, n)) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \bigl(-\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + \lfloor \log_2 i \rfloor\bigr)$, where for natural numbers $n$, $N$ and $k$, $E_k(n, N)$ is the $k$-th elementary symmetric function of the numbers $1/i$ with $\max(n - N, 0) < i \le n$, and $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Corollary 3.
