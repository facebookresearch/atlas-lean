# Elementary symmetric functions of reciprocals

For natural numbers $n$, $N$ and $k$, $E_k(n, N)$ is the $k$-th elementary symmetric function of the numbers $1/i$ with $\max(n - N, 0) < i \le n$; $E_1(n, N) = H_n - H_{n-N}$ for $N \le n$.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Section 4.
