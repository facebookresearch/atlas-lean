# Weighted binary digit sum over a range of positions

For natural numbers $n < 2^K$, $a(n) = \sum_{i < K} b_i\, i\, 2^i$, where $b_i$ is the binary digit of $n$ at position $i$ and the weighted binary digit sum of $n$ is $a(n) = \sum_i b_i\, i\, 2^i$, where $n = \sum_i b_i 2^i$ is the binary expansion of $n$.
