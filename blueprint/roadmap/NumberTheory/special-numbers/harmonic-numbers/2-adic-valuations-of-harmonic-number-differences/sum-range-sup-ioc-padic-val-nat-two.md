# Sum of largest 2-adic valuations over intervals

For every natural number $n$, $\sum_{m < n} \mu_2(m, n) = a(n)$, where $\mu_p(m, n) = \max_{m < k \le n} \nu_p(k)$ is the largest $p$-adic valuation of an element of $\{m + 1, \dots, n\}$ (with $p = 2$) and the weighted binary digit sum of $n$ is $a(n) = \sum_i b_i\, i\, 2^i$, where $n = \sum_i b_i 2^i$ is the binary expansion of $n$.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Corollary 2 and Theorem 4.
