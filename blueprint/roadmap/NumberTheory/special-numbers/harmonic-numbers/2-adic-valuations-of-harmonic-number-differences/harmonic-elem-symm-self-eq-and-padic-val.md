# Elementary symmetric functions of 1, 1/2, ..., 1/n and Stirling numbers

For natural numbers $1 \le k \le n$, $E_k(n, n) = |s(n + 1, k + 1)|/n!$, where $|s(\cdot, \cdot)|$ are the unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind, and $\nu_2(E_k(n, n)) \ge -k\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$; here for natural numbers $n$, $N$ and $k$, $E_k(n, N)$ is the $k$-th elementary symmetric function of the numbers $1/i$ with $\max(n - N, 0) < i \le n$, and $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Section 4.
