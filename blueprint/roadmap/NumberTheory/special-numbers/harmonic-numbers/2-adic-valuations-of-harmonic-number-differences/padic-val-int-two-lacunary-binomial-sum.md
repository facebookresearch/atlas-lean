# 2-adic valuation of differences of lacunary binomial sums

For positive integers $a \le n$, $b$ and $N$ with $\nu_2(N) \ge \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$, $\nu_2\Bigl(\sum_{m=1}^{\lfloor n/a \rfloor} \bigl(\binom{n + N}{am} - \binom{n}{am}\bigr) 2^{bm}\Bigr) \ge \nu_2(N) - \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + b$, where $\nu_2$ is the $2$-adic valuation.

## Sources

- T. Lengyel, On the 2-adic valuation of differences of harmonic numbers, Integers 24 (2024), Article A27, Theorem 7.
