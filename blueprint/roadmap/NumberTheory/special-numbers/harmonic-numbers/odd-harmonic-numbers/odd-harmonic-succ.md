# Recurrence for the odd harmonic numbers

For every natural number $n$, the odd harmonic
numbers satisfy $O_{n+1} = O_n + \frac{1}{2n+1}$.

## Sources

- Kunle Adegoke and Robert Frontczak, *Three Classes of Combinatorial Sums Involving Central Binomial Coefficients and Harmonic Numbers*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A109, Section 1 (definition of $O_n$); the recurrence is used in the proof of Corollary 1.
