# Alternating form of Ramanujan's sum identity for $1/((4k)^3 - 4k)$

For every positive
integer $n$,

$$
1 + 2 \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{(4k)^3 - 4k} =
\sum_{k=1}^{4n+1} \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{k} + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{2n} \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{k}.
$$
