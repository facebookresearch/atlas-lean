# Ramanujan's series $\sum_{k \ge 1} 1/((2k)^3 - 2k) = \log 2 - 1/2$

The series

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2k)^3 - 2k}$$

converges, and its sum is $\log 2 - \frac{1}{2}$.
