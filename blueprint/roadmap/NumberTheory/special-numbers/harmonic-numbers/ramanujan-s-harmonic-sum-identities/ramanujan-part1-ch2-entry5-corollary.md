# Ramanujan's series for $\frac{1}{2}\log 3 + \frac{1}{3}\log 4 - 1$

The numbers
$\frac{2}{(6k)^3 - 6k}$ for $k = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ are summable with sum
$\frac{1}{2}\log 3 + \frac{1}{3}\log 4 - 1$, where $\log$ is the natural logarithm.
