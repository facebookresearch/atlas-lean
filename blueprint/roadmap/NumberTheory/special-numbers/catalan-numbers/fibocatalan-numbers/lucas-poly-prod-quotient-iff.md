# Divisibility criterion for quotients of products of Lucas polynomials

Let $\{n\}$ denote the Lucas polynomials in $s$ and $t$ with integer coefficients, $\{0\} = 0$, $\{1\} = 1$ and $\{n\} = s\{n-1\} + t\{n-2\}$. Let $n_1, \dots, n_a$ and $k_1, \dots, k_b$ be lists of positive integers, $f = \prod_i \{n_i\}$ and $g = \prod_j \{k_j\}$. Then:

1. there is a polynomial $q$ in $s$ and $t$ with integer coefficients with $q \, g = f$ if and only if, for every integer $d \ge 2$, the number of $k_j$ divisible by $d$ is at most the number of $n_i$ divisible by $d$;
2. every polynomial $q$ with integer coefficients and $q \, g = f$ has non-negative coefficients.

## Sources

Kendra Killpatrick, "Super FiboCatalan Numbers and Their Lucas Analogues", Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.5.8, Theorem 1 (quoted from Sagan and Tirrell, Theorem 1.1).
