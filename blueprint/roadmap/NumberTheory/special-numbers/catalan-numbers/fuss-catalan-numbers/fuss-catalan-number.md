# Fuss–Catalan numbers

For natural numbers $k \ge 1$ (the chord arity) and $m$, the
Fuss–Catalan number is the natural-number quotient of $\binom{km}{m}$ by $(k-1)m+1$, that is
$\lfloor \binom{km}{m} / ((k-1)m+1) \rfloor$.
