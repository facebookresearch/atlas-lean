# Prime divisibility of generalized Catalan numbers

Let $p$ be a prime and $n$ a natural
number. Then $p$ divides the generalized Catalan number $\frac{1}{(p-1)n+1}\binom{pn}{n}$ (an
integer) if and only if $n$ is not of the form $\frac{p^k-1}{p-1}$ for any natural number $k$.

For $p = 2$ this is the classical criterion, also stated in the source, that the Catalan number
$C_n = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}$ is odd if and only if $n = 2^k - 1$ for some $k$.

## Sources

- B. Sury, *Generalized Catalan Numbers: Linear Recursion and Divisibility*, Journal of Integer Sequences 12 (2009), Article 09.7.5, Theorem 2, <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL12/Sury/sury31.html>.
