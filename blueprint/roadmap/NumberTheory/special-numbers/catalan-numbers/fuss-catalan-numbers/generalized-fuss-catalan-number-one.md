# Generalized Fuss–Catalan numbers at $n = 1$

For all natural numbers $m$ and $k$, the
generalized Fuss–Catalan number $F_m(1, k)$ equals $k$.

## Sources

- Wun-Seng Chou, Tian-Xiao He, and Peter J.-S. Shiue, *On the Primality of the Generalized Fuss-Catalan Numbers*, Journal of Integer Sequences 21 (2018), Article 18.2.1, Theorem 1 (c), <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL21/He/he61.html>.
