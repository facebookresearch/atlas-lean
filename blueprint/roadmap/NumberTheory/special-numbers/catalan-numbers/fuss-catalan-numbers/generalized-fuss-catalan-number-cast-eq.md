# Closed form of the Fuss–Catalan (Raney) numbers

For natural numbers $p$, $n$ and $r$, the generalized Fuss–Catalan number $A_n(p, r)$ equals $\frac{r}{pn + r}\binom{pn + r}{n}$ as a rational number (with $x/0 = 0$, so $A_0(p, 0) = 0$).
