# Closed form of the Fuss–Catalan (Raney) numbers

For natural numbers $p$, $n$ and $r$, the generalized Fuss–Catalan number $A_n(p, r)$ equals $\frac{r}{pn + r}\binom{pn + r}{n}$ as a rational number, with $x/0 = 0$. When $pn + r \ne 0$ this is the paper's formula. At $pn + r = 0$ the total quotient is $0$ (so $A_0(p, 0) = 0$), which differs from the paper's coefficient convention $A_n(p, r) = [x^n] M^r$: for $p = 2$, $r = 0$, $n = 0$ that coefficient is $1$. The paper also assumes $(p, r) \ne (0, 0)$.

## Sources

- T. Goy and M. Shattuck, Determinants of Toeplitz–Hessenberg matrices with Fuss–Catalan entries, J. Integer Seq. 29 (2026), Article 26.1.2, Section 1 (formula for $pn + r \ne 0$; coefficient definition $A_n(p, r) = [x^n]M^r$ for $(p, r) \ne (0, 0)$).
