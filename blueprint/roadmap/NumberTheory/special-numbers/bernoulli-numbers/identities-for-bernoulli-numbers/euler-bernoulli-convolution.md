# Euler's convolution identity for Bernoulli numbers

Let $B_j$ denote the $j$-th
Bernoulli number, with $B_1 = -1/2$. For every natural number $n \ge 1$,

$$\sum_{i=0}^{n} \binom{n}{i} B_i B_{n-i} = -n B_{n-1} - (n-1) B_n.$$
