# Carlitz's Bernoulli identity

Let $B_j$ denote the $j$-th Bernoulli number, with
$B_1 = -1/2$. For all natural numbers $m$ and $n$,

$$(-1)^m \sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{m}{k} B_{n+k} = (-1)^n \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} B_{m+k}.$$

## Sources
