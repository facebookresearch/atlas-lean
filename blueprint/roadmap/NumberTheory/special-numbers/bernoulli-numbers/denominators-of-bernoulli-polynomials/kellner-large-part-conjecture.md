# Kellner's large-part conjecture

For a natural number $n$, let $\mathbb{D}^+_n$ be the
product of the primes $p > \sqrt{n}$ with $s_p(n) \ge p$, where $s_p(n)$ is the sum of the
base-$p$ digits of $n$. Kellner's large-part conjecture asserts that $\mathbb{D}^+_n > 1$ for every
natural number $n > 192$.

## Sources

- Bernd C. Kellner, *On the Finiteness of Bernoulli Polynomials Whose Derivative Has Only Integral Coefficients*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.2.8, Conjecture 2 (i), quoting Bernd C. Kellner, *On a product of certain primes*, Journal of Number Theory 179 (2017), 126–141, Conjectures 1, 2.
