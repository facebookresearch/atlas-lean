# Logarithm of the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant

The Glaisher–Kinkelin constant $A$ satisfies
$\log A = \frac{1}{12} - \operatorname{Re} \zeta'(-1)$, where $\zeta'$ is the complex derivative
of the Riemann zeta function.
