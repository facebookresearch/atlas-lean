# Erdős Problem 384 with the non-strict bound p ≤ n/2 (small prime factors of binomial coefficients)

The statement that for all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $1 < k < n - 1$ and $(n, k) \ne (7, 3), (7, 4)$, the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has a prime factor $p$ with $2p \le n$.

Problem 384 as printed asks for $p < n/2$, which fails for $\binom{4}{2} = 6$; this is the corrected statement with the non-strict bound.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Binomial coefficients have a prime factor at most n/2 (non-strict bound)](binomial-has-prime-factor-at-most-half-h.md).
