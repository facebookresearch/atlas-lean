# Erdős Problem 384 (small prime factors of binomial coefficients)

The statement that for all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $1 < k < n - 1$ and $(n, k) \ne (7, 3), (7, 4)$, the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has a prime factor $p$ with $2p \le n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Binomial coefficients have a prime factor at most n/2](binomial-has-prime-factor-at-most-half-h.md).
