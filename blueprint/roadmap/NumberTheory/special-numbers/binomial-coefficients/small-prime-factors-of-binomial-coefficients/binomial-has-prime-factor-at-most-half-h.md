# Binomial coefficients have a prime factor at most n/2

For all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $1 < k < n - 1$ and $(n, k) \ne (7, 3), (7, 4)$, the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has a prime factor $p$ with $2p \le n$.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #384, https://www.erdosproblems.com/384.
- E. F. Ecklund, Jr., On prime divisors of the binomial coefficient, Pacific J. Math. (1969), 267–270.
