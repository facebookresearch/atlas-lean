# Infinitely many pairs of central binomial coefficients with the same prime divisors

There are infinitely many pairs $0 < n < m$ of natural numbers such that $\binom{2n}{n}$ and $\binom{2m}{m}$ have the same prime divisors.

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #730, https://www.erdosproblems.com/730.
