# Factoring n! into n large factors: the deficit below n/e

There is a real $c > 0$ with $t(n)/n \le 1/e - c/\log n$ for infinitely many $n$, where for a natural number $n$, $t(n)$ is the largest $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_n$ with $n$ natural numbers $a_i \ge t$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 391, second question](largest-least-factor-deficit.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Alexeev, Conway, Rosenfeld, Sutherland, Tao, Uhr and Ventullo (2025), cited under Sources.
