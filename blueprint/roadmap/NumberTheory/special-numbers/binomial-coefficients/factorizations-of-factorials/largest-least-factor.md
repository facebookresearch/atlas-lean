# Largest least factor in a factorization of n! into n factors

For a natural number $n$, $t(n)$ is the largest $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_n$ with $n$ natural numbers $a_i \ge t$ (for $n = 0$ the empty product is $0! = 1$, so every $t$ qualifies, and $t(0)$ is $0$ by the convention for the supremum of an unbounded set of natural numbers).
