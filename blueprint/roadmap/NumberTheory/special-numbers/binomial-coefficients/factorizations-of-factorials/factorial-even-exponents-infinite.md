# Erdős Problem 646 (even prime exponents in n!)

The statement that for every finite set $S$ of primes there are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ such that the exponent of every $p \in S$ in the prime factorization of $n!$ is even.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Infinitely many factorials with prescribed prime exponents all even](factorial-even-exponents-infinite-holds.md).
