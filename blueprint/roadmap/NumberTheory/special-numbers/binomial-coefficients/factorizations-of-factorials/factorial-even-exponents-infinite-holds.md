# Infinitely many factorials with prescribed prime exponents all even

For every finite set $S$ of primes there are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ such that the exponent of every $p \in S$ in the prime factorization of $n!$ is even.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #646, https://www.erdosproblems.com/646.
- D. Berend, On the parity of exponents in the factorization of n!, J. Number Theory (1997), 13–19.
