# Erdős Problem 401 (factorials dividing n! times powers of small primes)

The statement that there is $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f(r) \to \infty$ such that for every $r \ge 1$ there are infinitely many $n$ admitting positive integers $a_1, a_2$ with $a_1 + a_2 > n + f(r)\log n$ and $a_1!\, a_2! \mid n!\, (p_1 p_2 \cdots p_r)^n$, where $p_1 = 2 < p_2 = 3 < \cdots$ are the primes.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
