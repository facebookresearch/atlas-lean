# Factoring n! into n large factors: the limit 1/e

$t(n)/n \to 1/e$ as $n \to \infty$, where for a natural number $n$, $t(n)$ is the largest $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_n$ with $n$ natural numbers $a_i \ge t$.
