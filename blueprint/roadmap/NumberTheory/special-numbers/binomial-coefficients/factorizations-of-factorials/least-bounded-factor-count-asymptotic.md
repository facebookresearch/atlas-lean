# Erdős Problem 392 (factoring n! into factors at most n²)

The statement that $A(n) = n/2 - n/(2\log n) + o(n/\log n)$ as $n \to \infty$, where for a natural number $n$, $A(n)$ is the least $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_t$ with natural numbers $a_i \le n^2$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
