# Erdős Problem 391, second question

The statement that there is a real $c > 0$ with $t(n)/n \le 1/e - c/\log n$ for infinitely many $n$, where for a natural number $n$, $t(n)$ is the largest $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_n$ with $n$ natural numbers $a_i \ge t$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Factoring n! into n large factors: the deficit below n/e](largest-least-factor-deficit-holds.md).
