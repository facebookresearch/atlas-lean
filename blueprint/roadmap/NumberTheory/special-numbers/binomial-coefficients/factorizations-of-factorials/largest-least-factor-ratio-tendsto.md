# Erdős Problem 391, first question

The statement that $t(n)/n \to 1/e$ as $n \to \infty$, where for a natural number $n$, $t(n)$ is the largest $t$ such that $n! = a_1 \cdots a_n$ with $n$ natural numbers $a_i \ge t$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Factoring n! into n large factors: the limit 1/e](largest-least-factor-ratio-tendsto-holds.md).
