# The sequence A265233

The sequence $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ of rational numbers is given by
$a_0 = 1$, $a_1 = 1$, $a_2 = 7$, $a_3 = 56$ and, for every integer $N \ge 4$,

$$N^2 a_N = (19N^2 - 19N + 6)\, a_{N-1} - 96 (N-1)^2 a_{N-2} + 144 (N-1)(N-2)\, a_{N-3}.$$
