# Density of rows with many squarefree binomial coefficients exists

For every $r$, the set of natural numbers $n$ such that $\binom{n}{k}$ is squarefree for at least $r$ values $1 \le k < n$ has a natural density.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 378, first question](squarefree-binomial-density-exists.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Granville and Ramaré (1996), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #378, https://www.erdosproblems.com/378.
- A. Granville and O. Ramaré, Explicit bounds on exponential sums and the scarcity of squarefree binomial coefficients, Mathematika (1996), 73–107.
