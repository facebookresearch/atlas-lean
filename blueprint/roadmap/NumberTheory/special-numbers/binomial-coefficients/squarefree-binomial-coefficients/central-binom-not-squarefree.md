# Erdős Problem 175 (non-squarefree central binomial coefficients)

The statement that for every natural number $n \ge 5$, the central binomial coefficient $\binom{2n}{n}$ is not squarefree.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Central binomial coefficients are not squarefree from n = 5 on](central-binom-not-squarefree-holds.md).
