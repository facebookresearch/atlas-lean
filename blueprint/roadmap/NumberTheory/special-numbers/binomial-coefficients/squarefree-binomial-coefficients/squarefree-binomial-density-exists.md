# Erdős Problem 378, first question

The statement that for every $r$, the set of natural numbers $n$ such that $\binom{n}{k}$ is squarefree for at least $r$ values $1 \le k < n$ has a natural density.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Density of rows with many squarefree binomial coefficients exists](squarefree-binomial-density-exists-holds.md).
