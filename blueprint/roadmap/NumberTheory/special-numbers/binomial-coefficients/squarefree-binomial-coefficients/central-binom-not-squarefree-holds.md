# Central binomial coefficients are not squarefree from n = 5 on

For every natural number $n \ge 5$, the central binomial coefficient $\binom{2n}{n}$ is not squarefree.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 175 (non-squarefree central binomial coefficients)](central-binom-not-squarefree.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is the published work cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #175, https://www.erdosproblems.com/175.
- A. Granville and O. Ramaré, Explicit bounds on exponential sums and the scarcity of squarefree binomial coefficients, Mathematika 43 (1996), 73–107.
- G. Velammal, Is the binomial coefficient $\binom{2n}{n}$ squarefree?, Hardy-Ramanujan J. 18 (1995), 23–45.
