# Erdős Problem 378, second question

The statement that for every $r$, the set of natural numbers $n$ such that $\binom{n}{k}$ is squarefree for at least $r$ values $1 \le k < n$ has a positive natural density.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Rows with many squarefree binomial coefficients have positive density](squarefree-binomial-density-pos-holds.md).
