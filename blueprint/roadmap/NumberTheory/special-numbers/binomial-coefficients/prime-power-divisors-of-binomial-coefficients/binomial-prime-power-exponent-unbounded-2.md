# Resolution of Erdős Problem 379

For every natural number $s$ there are infinitely many $n \ge 2$ such that every binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ with $1 \le k < n$ is divisible by $p^s$ for some prime $p$ (depending on $k$).

The prime may differ from one coefficient to the next; no prime power need divide all of them. In row $6$, for instance, every interior coefficient has a prime factor, yet $\gcd(6, 15, 20) = 1$.

This resolves [Unbounded prime-power exponent in every interior coefficient](binomial-prime-power-exponent-unbounded.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is the one recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #379.
