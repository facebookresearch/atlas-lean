# The constant sequence $1$ is atomic

The constant sequence $(1, 1, 1, \dots)$ is a
nonzero atomic binomid index: it is a nonzero binomid index that is not the sum of two nonzero
binomid indices.

## Sources

- Jonathan Caalim and Yu-ichi Tanaka, *Generalized Binomial Coefficients and the Monoid of Lexicographically Minimal Extensions*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A113, Example 1 (the sequence $(E_m)^\infty$ with $m = 1$).
