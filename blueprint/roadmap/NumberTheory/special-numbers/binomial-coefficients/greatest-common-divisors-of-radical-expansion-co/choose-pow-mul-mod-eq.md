# Binomial coefficients $\binom{p^r a}{p^r b}$ modulo $p$

Let $p$ be a prime, let $r \ge 1$,
and let $a, b$ be natural numbers not divisible by $p$. Then
$\binom{p^r a}{p^r b} \equiv \binom{a}{b} \pmod{p}$.

## Sources
