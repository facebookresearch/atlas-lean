# Erdős Problem 387 is false: divisors of $\binom{n}{k}$ in $(cn, n]$

There is no real number $c > 0$ such that for all natural
numbers $n$ and $k$ with $1 \le k < n$ the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ has a divisor
$d$ with $cn < d \le n$.

This follows from Theorem 1.4 of Hung M. Bui, Slava Naprienko, Kyle Pratt and Alexandru
Zaharescu: for every sufficiently large $k_0$ there are infinitely many binomial coefficients
$\binom{n}{k}$ with $k > k_0$ that have no divisor in the interval
$(241\, n \log\log k / \log k,\ n]$, and $\log\log k / \log k \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$.
