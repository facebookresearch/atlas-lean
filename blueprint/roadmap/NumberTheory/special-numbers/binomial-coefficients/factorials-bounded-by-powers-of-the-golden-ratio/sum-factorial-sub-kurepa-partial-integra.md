# The remainder of Kurepa's partial integral, explicitly

For a natural number $n$ and a real
number $x$, $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} k! - K_n(x) = e^{-x} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} k! \sum_{j=0}^{k} x^j / j!$.

## Sources
