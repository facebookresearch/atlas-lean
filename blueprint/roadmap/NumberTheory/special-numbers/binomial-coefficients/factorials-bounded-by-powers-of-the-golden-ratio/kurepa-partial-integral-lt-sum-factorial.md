# Kurepa's partial integral stays below the left factorial

For a natural number $n \ge 1$ and
a real number $x \ge 0$, $K_n(x) < \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} k!$, where the sum is the left factorial $!n$.

## Sources

- Petojević, Gorjanac Ranitović, Rastovac and Mandić, *The Golden Ratio, Factorials, and the Lambert W Function*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.5.7, Sections 3.1-3.3.
