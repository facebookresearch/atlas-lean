# Erdős Problem 698 (common divisors of binomial coefficients)

The statement that there is $h : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $h(n) \to \infty$ such that $\gcd\bigl(\binom{n}{i}, \binom{n}{j}\bigr) \ge h(n)$ for all natural numbers $n, i, j$ with $2 \le i < j$ and $2j \le n$.
