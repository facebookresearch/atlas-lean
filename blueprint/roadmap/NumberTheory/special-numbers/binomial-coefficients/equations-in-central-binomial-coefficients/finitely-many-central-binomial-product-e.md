# Erdős Problem 397 (equal products of central binomial coefficients)

The statement that only finitely many pairs $(M, N)$ of disjoint nonempty finite sets of positive integers satisfy $\prod_{m \in M} \binom{2m}{m} = \prod_{n \in N} \binom{2n}{n}$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Infinitely many equal products of central binomial coefficients](finitely-many-central-binomial-product-e-2.md).
