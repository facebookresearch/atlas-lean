# Monier's bound n_k at most k!, as a statement

The statement that $n_k \le k!$ for every $k \ge 3$, where for a natural number $k$, $n_k$ is the least $n \ge 2k$ such that all but one of the numbers $n - i$, $0 \le i < k$, divide $\binom{n}{k}$: exactly one $i_0 < k$ has $n - i_0 \nmid \binom{n}{k}$ ($0$ if there is no such $n$).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Monier's bound n_k at most k!](monier-bound-holds.md).
