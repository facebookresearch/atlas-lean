# Cambie's bound for n_k, as a statement

The statement that $n_k \le k \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(1, 2, \dots, k-1)$ for every $k \ge 3$, where for a natural number $k$, $n_k$ is the least $n \ge 2k$ such that all but one of the numbers $n - i$, $0 \le i < k$, divide $\binom{n}{k}$: exactly one $i_0 < k$ has $n - i_0 \nmid \binom{n}{k}$ ($0$ if there is no such $n$).
