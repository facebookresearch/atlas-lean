# Some factor fails to divide the binomial coefficient, as a statement

The statement that for all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $k \ge 2$ and $2k \le n$, some $i < k$ has $n - i \nmid \binom{n}{k}$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Some factor fails to divide the binomial coefficient](exists-exception-holds.md).
