# Some factor fails to divide the binomial coefficient

For all natural numbers $n$ and $k$ with $k \ge 2$ and $2k \le n$, some $i < k$ has $n - i \nmid \binom{n}{k}$.

This resolves [Some factor fails to divide the binomial coefficient, as a statement](exists-exception.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #1063, is due to Erdős and Selfridge, with a proof by Monier (1985).
