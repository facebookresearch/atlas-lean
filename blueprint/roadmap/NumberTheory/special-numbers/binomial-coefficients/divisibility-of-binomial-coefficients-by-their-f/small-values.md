# Small values of n_k, as a statement

The statement that $n_2 = 4$, $n_3 = 6$, $n_4 = 9$ and $n_5 = 12$, where for a natural number $k$, $n_k$ is the least $n \ge 2k$ such that all but one of the numbers $n - i$, $0 \le i < k$, divide $\binom{n}{k}$: exactly one $i_0 < k$ has $n - i_0 \nmid \binom{n}{k}$ ($0$ if there is no such $n$).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Small values of n_k](small-values-holds.md).
