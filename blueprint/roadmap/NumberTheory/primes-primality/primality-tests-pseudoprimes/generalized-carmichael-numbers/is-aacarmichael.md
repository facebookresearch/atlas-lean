# $(a, a)$-Carmichael numbers

Let $a$ be an integer. A natural number $n$ is an
$(a, a)$-Carmichael number if $n$ is squarefree and $p - a$ divides $n - a$ in $\mathbb{Z}$ for
every prime $p$ dividing $n$.
