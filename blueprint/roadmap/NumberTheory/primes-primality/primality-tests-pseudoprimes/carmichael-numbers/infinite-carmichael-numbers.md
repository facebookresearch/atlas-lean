# There are infinitely many Carmichael numbers

There are infinitely many Carmichael numbers: natural numbers $n > 1$ that are not prime and satisfy $a^n \equiv a \pmod n$ for every natural number $a$.

## Sources

- W. R. Alford, A. Granville and C. Pomerance, There are infinitely many Carmichael numbers, Ann. of Math. 139 (1994), 703–722.
