# There are no even Fibonacci pseudoprimes of the first kind

It is not the case that there is an even Fibonacci pseudoprime of the first kind: an even composite number $n > 1$ with $L_n \equiv 1 \pmod n$, where $L_n$ is the $n$-th Lucas number ($L_0 = 2$, $L_1 = 1$, $L_{k+2} = L_{k+1} + L_k$). Equivalently, every even $n > 1$ with $L_n \equiv 1 \pmod n$ is prime.

This refutes [Guy's question on even Fibonacci pseudoprimes](exists-even-fibonacci-pseudoprime.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is the published work cited under Sources.

## Sources

- R. K. Guy, Unsolved Problems in Number Theory, Section A12.
- A. Di Porto, Nonexistence of even Fibonacci pseudoprimes of the 1st kind, Fibonacci Quart. 31 (1993), 173–177.
