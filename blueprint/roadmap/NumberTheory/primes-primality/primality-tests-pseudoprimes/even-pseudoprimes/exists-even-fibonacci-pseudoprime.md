# Guy's question on even Fibonacci pseudoprimes

The statement that there is an even Fibonacci pseudoprime of the first kind: an even composite number $n > 1$ with $L_n \equiv 1 \pmod n$, where $L_n$ is the $n$-th Lucas number ($L_0 = 2$, $L_1 = 1$, $L_{k+2} = L_{k+1} + L_k$).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [There are no even Fibonacci pseudoprimes of the first kind](exists-even-fibonacci-pseudoprime-refute.md).

## Sources

- R. K. Guy, Unsolved Problems in Number Theory, Section A12.
