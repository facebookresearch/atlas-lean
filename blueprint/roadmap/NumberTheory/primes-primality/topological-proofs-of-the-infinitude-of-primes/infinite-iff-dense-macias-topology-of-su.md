# A nonempty set of primes is dense in the comaximal space iff it is infinite

Equip the
positive integers with the comaximal topology, the topology generated by the sets
$\{m : \gcd(n, m) = 1\}$ for positive integers $n$. A nonempty set $A$ of primes is infinite if
and only if it is dense in this topology.

## Sources

- Jhixon Macías, *A Topological Proof of the Infinitude of Prime Numbers in Certain Arithmetic Progressions*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A39, Lemma 1 (quoting Macías, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A47).
