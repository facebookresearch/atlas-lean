# Macías's coprime sets form a basis

For a positive integer $n$ let $\Omega_n$ be the set
of positive integers coprime to $n$. The family $\beta = \{\Omega_n : n \ge 1\}$ is a basis of
the topology on the positive integers that it generates (Macías's coprime topology).

## Sources

- Jhixon Macías, "Another Topological Proof of the Infinitude of Prime Numbers", Theorem 1.
