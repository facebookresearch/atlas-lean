# Erdős Problem 248 (few prime factors in all shifts)

The statement that there are a real $C$ and infinitely many natural numbers $n$ such that $\omega(n + k) \le C k$ for every $k \ge 1$, where $\omega(m)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $m$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
