# Erdős Problem 372 (descending largest prime factors of three consecutive integers)

The statement that there are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ with $P(n) > P(n + 1) > P(n + 2)$, where $P(m)$ is the largest prime factor of $m$ ($0$ if $m \le 1$).
