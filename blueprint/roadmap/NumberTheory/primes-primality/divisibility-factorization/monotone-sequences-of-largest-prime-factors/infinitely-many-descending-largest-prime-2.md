# Infinitely many triples of consecutive integers with descending largest prime factors

There are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ with $P(n) > P(n + 1) > P(n + 2)$, where $P(m)$ is the largest prime factor of $m$ ($0$ if $m \le 1$).

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #372, https://www.erdosproblems.com/372.
- A. Balog, On triplets with descending largest prime factors, Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. (2001), 45–50.
