# Order of the longest chain with decreasing largest prime factors

$g(n) \asymp (n/\log n)^{1/2}$: there are constants $0 < c_1 \le c_2$ with $c_1 (n/\log n)^{1/2} \le g(n) \le c_2 (n/\log n)^{1/2}$ for all sufficiently large $n$, where for a natural number $n$, $g(n)$ is the largest $t$ for which there are integers $2 \le a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_t < n$ with $P(a_1) > P(a_2) > \dots > P(a_t)$, where $P(m)$ is the largest prime factor of $m$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 648 (increasing integers with decreasing largest prime factors)](decreasing-prime-factor-chain-order.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Cambie (2025), cited under Sources.
