# Graham's gcd conjecture

Every nonempty finite set $A$ of positive integers contains $a$ and $b$ (possibly equal) with $\gcd(a, b)\,|A| \le a$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 402 (Graham's gcd problem)](exists-pair-with-small-gcd.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Balasubramanian and Soundararajan (1996), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #402, https://www.erdosproblems.com/402.
- R. Balasubramanian and K. Soundararajan, On a conjecture of R. L. Graham, Acta Arith. (1996), 1–38.
