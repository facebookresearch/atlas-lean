# Erdős Problem 402 (Graham's gcd problem)

The statement that every nonempty finite set $A$ of positive integers contains $a$ and $b$ (possibly equal) with $\gcd(a, b)\,|A| \le a$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Graham's gcd conjecture](exists-pair-with-small-gcd-holds.md).
