# Erdős Problem 649 (prescribed largest prime factors of n and n + 1)

The statement that for any two distinct primes $p$ and $q$ there is a natural number $n$ with $P(n) = p$ and $P(n + 1) = q$, where $P(m)$ denotes the largest prime factor of $m$ (and $0$ if $m$ has none).

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Largest prime factors of n and n + 1 cannot be prescribed](consecutive-largest-prime-factors-refute.md).
