# Erdős Problem 649 for large primes

The statement that there is $N$ such that for any two distinct primes $p, q \ge N$ there is a natural number $n$ with $P(n) = p$ and $P(n + 1) = q$, where $P(m)$ denotes the largest prime factor of $m$ (and $0$ if $m$ has none).

This page records the statement as a proposition.
