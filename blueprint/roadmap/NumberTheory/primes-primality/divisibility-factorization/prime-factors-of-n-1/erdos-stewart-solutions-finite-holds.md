# Finitely many n with n! + 1 divisible only by the next two primes

Only finitely many positive integers $n$ are Erdős–Stewart solutions, where a positive integer $n$ is an Erdős–Stewart solution when, with $p_0 = 2 < p_1 = 3 < \cdots$ the primes, there is $k$ with $p_{k-1} \le n < p_k$ (just $n < 2$ when $k = 0$) such that every prime factor of $n! + 1$ is $p_k$ or $p_{k+1}$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 1058 (n! + 1 divisible only by the next two primes)](erdos-stewart-solutions-finite.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Luca (2001), cited under Sources.
