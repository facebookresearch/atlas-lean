# Reversals of least common multiples by arbitrary ratios

For every natural number $C$ and every sufficiently large $k$ there are natural numbers $m, n$ with $m > n + k$ and $M(n, k) > C\, M(m, k+1)$, where $M(n, k) = \operatorname{lcm}(n+1, n+2, \dots, n+k)$.

This resolves [Cambie's strong form of Erdős Problem 678, as a statement](lcm-reversal-arbitrary-ratio.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Cambie (2024), cited under Sources.
