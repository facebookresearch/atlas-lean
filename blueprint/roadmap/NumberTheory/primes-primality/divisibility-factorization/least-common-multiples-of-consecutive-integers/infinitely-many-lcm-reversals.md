# Erdős Problem 678 (least common multiples of consecutive integers out of order)

The statement that there are infinitely many triples $(m, n, k)$ of natural numbers with $k \ge 3$, $m \ge n + k$ and $M(n, k) > M(m, k+1)$, where $M(n, k) = \operatorname{lcm}(n+1, n+2, \dots, n+k)$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
