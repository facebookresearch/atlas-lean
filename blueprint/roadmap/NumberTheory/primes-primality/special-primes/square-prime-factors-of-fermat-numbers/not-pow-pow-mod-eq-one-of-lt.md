# Smaller iterated powers are not congruent to one

Let $a, b, m \ge 2$ and $n \ge 1$ be
natural numbers with $a^{b^n} \not\equiv 1 \pmod m$. Then $a^{b^t} \not\equiv 1 \pmod m$ for every
natural number $t < n$.
