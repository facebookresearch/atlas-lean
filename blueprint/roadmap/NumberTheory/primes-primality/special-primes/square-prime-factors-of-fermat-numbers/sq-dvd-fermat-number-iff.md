# Squared prime factors of Fermat numbers

Let $F_n = 2^{2^n} + 1$ be the $n$-th Fermat
number, and let $p$ be a prime dividing $F_n$. Then $p^2$ divides $F_n$ if and only if
$2^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p^2}$.

## Sources
