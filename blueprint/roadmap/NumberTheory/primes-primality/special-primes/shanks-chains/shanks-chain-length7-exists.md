# Guy's question on Shanks chains of length seven

The statement that there is a Shanks chain of length $7$: primes $p_0, \dots, p_6$ with $p_{i+1} = 4p_i^2 - 17$ for $i = 0, \dots, 5$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [A Shanks chain of length seven exists](shanks-chain-length7-exists-holds.md).

## Sources

- R. K. Guy, Unsolved Problems in Number Theory, Section A7.
