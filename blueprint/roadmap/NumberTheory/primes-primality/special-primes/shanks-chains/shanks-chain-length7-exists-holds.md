# A Shanks chain of length seven exists

There is a Shanks chain of length $7$: primes $p_0, \dots, p_6$ with $p_{i+1} = 4p_i^2 - 17$ for $i = 0, \dots, 5$.

This resolves [Guy's question on Shanks chains of length seven](shanks-chain-length7-exists.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Leyland (2000), cited under Sources, who announced the chain with initial prime $p_0 = 176996212597$ and proved all seven terms prime.

## Sources

- R. K. Guy, Unsolved Problems in Number Theory, Section A7.
- P. Leyland, A Shanks (4,17) chain of length 7, NMBRTHRY mailing list, 22 November 2000, https://listserv.nodak.edu/cgi-bin/wa.exe?A2=NMBRTHRY;696450a6.0011.
