# Erdős Problem 795 (sets with distinct subset products)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ whose subsets have pairwise distinct products satisfies $|A| \le \pi(n) + \pi(\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor) + \varepsilon \sqrt n/\log n$, where $\pi$ is the prime-counting function.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Size of sets with distinct subset products](distinct-subset-products-bound-holds.md).
