# Reciprocal sums of sets with large pairwise least common multiples

For every $n$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) > n$ for all distinct $a, b \in A$ satisfies $\sum_{a \in A} 1/a \le 31/30$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 542, first question](large-lcm-reciprocal-sum-bound.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Schinzel and Szekeres (1959), cited under Sources.
