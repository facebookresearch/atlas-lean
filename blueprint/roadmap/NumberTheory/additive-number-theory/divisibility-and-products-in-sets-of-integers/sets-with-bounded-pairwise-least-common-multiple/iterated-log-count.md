# Number of logarithms needed to fall below one

For a real $x$, $\operatorname{loc} x$ is the least $r$ such that the $r$-fold iterate $L^r(x)$ lies in $[0, 1)$ ($0$ if there is none), where $L(0) = 0$ and $L(y) = \log|y|$ otherwise. For $x \ge 1$ this is the number of natural logarithms needed to fall below $1$; on other inputs the convention matters: at $x = -1$, $L^0(-1) = -1 \notin [0, 1)$ and $L(-1) = 0$, so $\operatorname{loc}(-1) = 1$.
