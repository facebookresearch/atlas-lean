# Erdős Problem 537 (equal prime multiples in dense sets)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N_0$ such that for every $N \ge N_0$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ with $|A| \ge \varepsilon N$ contains $a_1, a_2, a_3$ with $a_1 p_1 = a_2 p_2 = a_3 p_3$ for some distinct primes $p_1, p_2, p_3$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Dense sets need not contain three equal prime multiples](dense-set-has-prime-multiple-triple-refu.md).
