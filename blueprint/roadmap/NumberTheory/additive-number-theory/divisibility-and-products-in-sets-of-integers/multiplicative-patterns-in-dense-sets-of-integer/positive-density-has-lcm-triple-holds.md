# Sets of positive density contain a least common multiple of two of their elements

Every set $A$ of natural numbers having a positive natural density (the proportion of $A$ among $0, 1, \dots, N-1$ converges to some $\alpha > 0$) contains distinct $a, b, c$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) = c$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 487 (least common multiples in sets of positive density)](positive-density-has-lcm-triple.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Kleitman (1971), cited under Sources.
