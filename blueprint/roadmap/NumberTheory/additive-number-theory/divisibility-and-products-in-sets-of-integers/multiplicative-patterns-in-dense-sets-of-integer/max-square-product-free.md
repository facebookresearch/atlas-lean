# Largest sets with no square product of k distinct elements

For natural numbers $k$ and $N$, $F_k(N)$ is the largest size of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ such that no product of $k$ distinct elements of $A$ is a perfect square ($F_0(N) = 0$: for $k = 0$ the empty product $1$ is a square, so no set qualifies).
