# Tao's bound for sets with no square product of an odd number of elements, as a statement

The statement that for every $k \ge 2$ there is $c > 0$ with $F_{2k+1}(N) \le (1 - c)N$ for all sufficiently large $N$, where $F_k(N)$ is the largest size of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ such that no product of $k$ distinct elements of $A$ is a perfect square.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sets with no square product of 2k + 1 distinct elements have density below one](odd-product-free-bounded-away-from-full-2.md).
