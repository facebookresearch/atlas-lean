# Pairs of sets with distinct products are small

There is a real $C$ such that for every $N \ge 2$ and all sets $A, B \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ whose products $ab$ ($a \in A$, $b \in B$) are pairwise distinct (each product determines $a$ and $b$), $|A|\,|B| \le C N^2/\log N$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 490 (pairs of sets with distinct products)](distinct-products-card-bound.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Szemerédi (1976), cited under Sources.
