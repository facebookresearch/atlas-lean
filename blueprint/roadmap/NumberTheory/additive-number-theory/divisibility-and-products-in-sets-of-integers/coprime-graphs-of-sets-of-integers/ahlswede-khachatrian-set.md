# Ahlswede–Khachatrian extremal sets

For natural numbers $N$ and $q$, with $q_1 < \dots < q_j$ the prime factors of $N$ that are at most $q$, the set of integers in $\{1, \dots, N\}$ divisible by $q_1 q_2 \cdots q_j$ or by one of $2q_1, \dots, 2q_j$. When $N$ has no prime factor at most $q$ (for instance $N = 1$), the empty product is $1$ and the set is all of $\{1, \dots, N\}$.
