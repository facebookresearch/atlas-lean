# Dense sets of integers contain complete tripartite coprime configurations

For every $\ell \ge 1$ there is $N_0$ such that for every $n \ge N_0$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ with more than $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor + \lfloor n/3 \rfloor - \lfloor n/6 \rfloor$ elements contains a complete tripartite coprime configuration with parts of sizes $(1, \ell, \ell)$; here a finite set $A$ of natural numbers contains a complete tripartite coprime configuration with parts of sizes $(1, \ell, \ell)$ when there are $v_0 \in A$ and disjoint sets $L_1, L_2 \subseteq A$ of size $\ell$, neither containing $v_0$, such that $v_0$ is coprime to every element of $L_1 \cup L_2$ and every element of $L_1$ is coprime to every element of $L_2$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 883, second question (complete tripartite coprime configurations)](statement.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Sárközy (1999), cited under Sources.
