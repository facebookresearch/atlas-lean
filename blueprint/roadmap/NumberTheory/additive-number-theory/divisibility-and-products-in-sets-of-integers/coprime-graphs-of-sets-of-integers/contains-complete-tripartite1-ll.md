# Complete tripartite coprime configurations

For a natural number $\ell$, a finite set $A$ of natural numbers contains a complete tripartite coprime configuration with parts of sizes $(1, \ell, \ell)$ when there are $v_0 \in A$ and disjoint sets $L_1, L_2 \subseteq A$ of size $\ell$, neither containing $v_0$, such that $v_0$ is coprime to every element of $L_1 \cup L_2$ and every element of $L_1$ is coprime to every element of $L_2$.
