# Erdős Problem 534 (largest sets with pairwise common factors containing N)

The statement that for every $N \ge 2$ there is a prime factor $q$ of $N$ such that every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ containing $N$ with $\gcd(a, b) > 1$ for all distinct $a, b \in A$ has at most as many elements as the set of integers in $\{1, \dots, N\}$ divisible by $q_1 \cdots q_j$ or by one of $2q_1, \dots, 2q_j$, where $q_1 < \dots < q_j$ are the prime factors of $N$ that are at most $q$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Largest sets with pairwise common factors containing N](pairwise-common-factor-max-size-holds.md).
