# Erdős Problem 587 (largest sets with no square subset sum)

The statement that there are reals $c > 0$ and $C$ such that for all sufficiently large $N$ the largest size $f(N)$ of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ no nonempty subset of which sums to a perfect square satisfies $c N^{1/3} \le f(N) \le N^{1/3} (\log N)^C$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Order of the largest sets with no square subset sum](max-no-square-subset-sum-order-holds.md).
