# Order of the largest sets with no square subset sum

There are reals $c > 0$ and $C$ such that for all sufficiently large $N$ the largest size $f(N)$ of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ no nonempty subset of which sums to a perfect square satisfies $c N^{1/3} \le f(N) \le N^{1/3} (\log N)^C$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 587 (largest sets with no square subset sum)](max-no-square-subset-sum-order.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Nguyen and Vu (2010), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #587, https://www.erdosproblems.com/587.
- H. H. Nguyen and V. H. Vu, Squares in sumsets, in An Irregular Mind (2010), 491–524, arXiv:0811.1311.
