# Erdős Problem 205, first question

The statement that every sufficiently large natural number $n$ is $2^k + m$ for some $k \ge 0$ and positive integer $m$ with $\Omega(m) < \log\log m$, where $\Omega(m)$ is the number of prime factors of $m$ counted with multiplicity.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Not every large integer is 2^k + m with Ω(m) < log log m](eventually-representable-log-log-refuted.md).
