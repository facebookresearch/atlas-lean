# Erdős Problem 205, first question

The statement that every sufficiently large natural number $n$ is $2^k + m$ for some $k \ge 0$ and positive integer $m$ with $\Omega(m) < \log\log m$ ($m = 1$ is never a witness, since $\Omega(1)$ and the bound are both $0$), where $\Omega(m)$ is the number of prime factors of $m$ counted with multiplicity.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
