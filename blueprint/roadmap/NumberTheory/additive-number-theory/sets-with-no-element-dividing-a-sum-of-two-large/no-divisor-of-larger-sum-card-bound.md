# Erdős Problem 13 (sets with no element dividing a sum of two larger ones)

The statement that there is a real $C$ such that for every $N$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ containing no $a, b, c$ ($b = c$ allowed) with $a < b$, $a < c$ and $a \mid b + c$ has $|A| \le N/3 + C$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sets with no element dividing a sum of two larger ones have at most N/3 + O(1) elements](no-divisor-of-larger-sum-card-bound-hold.md).
