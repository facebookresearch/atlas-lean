# Odd integers not of the form 2^k + p are not a progression plus a null set

It is not the case that the set of odd natural numbers not of the form $2^k + p$ ($k \ge 0$, $p$ prime) is the union of an infinite arithmetic progression $\{a + md : m \in \mathbb{N}\}$ ($d > 0$) and a set of natural density $0$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 16 (odd integers not of the form 2^k + p)](odd-not-prime-plus-power-of-two-is-progr.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Chen (2023), cited under Sources.
