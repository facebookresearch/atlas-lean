# Erdős Problem 123 (sums of smooth numbers forming a divisibility antichain)

The statement that for all pairwise coprime natural numbers $a, b, c \ge 2$, every sufficiently large natural number is the sum of a finite set of distinct numbers of the form $a^k b^l c^m$ ($k, l, m \ge 0$), no one of which divides another.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
