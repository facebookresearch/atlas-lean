# Large integers are antichain sums of numbers a^k b^l c^m

For all pairwise coprime natural numbers $a, b, c \ge 2$, every sufficiently large natural number is the sum of a finite set of distinct numbers of the form $a^k b^l c^m$ ($k, l, m \ge 0$), no one of which divides another.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 123 (sums of smooth numbers forming a divisibility antichain)](conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #123, is an AI-generated proof, prompted by C. Snyder and accepted by the site, which T. F. Bloom summarizes (cited under Sources).
