# Large integers are antichain sums of numbers a^k b^l c^m

For all pairwise coprime natural numbers $a, b, c \ge 2$, every sufficiently large natural number is the sum of a finite set of distinct numbers of the form $a^k b^l c^m$ ($k, l, m \ge 0$), no one of which divides another.
