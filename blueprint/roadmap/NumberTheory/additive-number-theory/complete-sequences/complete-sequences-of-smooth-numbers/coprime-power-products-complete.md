# Erdős Problem 246 (completeness of the products a^k b^l)

The statement that for all coprime natural numbers $a, b \ge 2$, every sufficiently large natural number is a sum of distinct integers of the form $a^k b^l$ with $k, l \ge 0$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The products a^k b^l of coprime a and b form a complete sequence](coprime-power-products-complete-holds.md).
