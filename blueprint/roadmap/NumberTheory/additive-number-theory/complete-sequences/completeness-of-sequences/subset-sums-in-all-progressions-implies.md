# Erdős Problem 253 (subset sums in every progression)

The statement that for every strictly increasing sequence $1 \le a_0 < a_1 < \cdots$ of integers with $a_{i+1}/a_i \to 1$ such that every infinite arithmetic progression $r, r + d, r + 2d, \dots$ ($d \ge 1$) contains infinitely many sums of distinct terms, every sufficiently large integer is a sum of distinct terms.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Subset sums in every progression without completeness](subset-sums-in-all-progressions-implies-2.md).
