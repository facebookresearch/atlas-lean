# Erdős Problem 1136 (dense sets with no sum a power of two)

The statement that some set $A$ of natural numbers with lower density greater than $1/3$ ($\liminf_N |A \cap [0, N)|/N > 1/3$) has $a + b \ne 2^k$ for all $a, b \in A$ (equal elements allowed) and all $k \ge 0$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Dense sets with no two elements summing to a power of two](power-of-two-sum-free-lower-density-abov-2.md).
