# Erdős Problem 350 (reciprocal sums of sets with distinct subset sums)

The statement that every finite set $A$ of natural numbers whose distinct subsets have distinct sums satisfies $\sum_{n \in A} 1/n < 2$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sets with distinct subset sums have reciprocal sum below two](reciprocal-sum-lt-two-of-distinct-subset-2.md).
