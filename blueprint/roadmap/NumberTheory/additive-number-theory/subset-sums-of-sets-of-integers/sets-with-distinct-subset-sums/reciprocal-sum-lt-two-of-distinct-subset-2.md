# Sets with distinct subset sums have reciprocal sum below two

Every finite set $A$ of natural numbers whose distinct subsets have distinct sums satisfies $\sum_{n \in A} 1/n < 2$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 350 (reciprocal sums of sets with distinct subset sums)](reciprocal-sum-lt-two-of-distinct-subset.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Benkoski and Erdős (1974), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #350, https://www.erdosproblems.com/350.
- S. J. Benkoski and P. Erdős, On weird and pseudoperfect numbers, Math. Comp. (1974), 617–623.
