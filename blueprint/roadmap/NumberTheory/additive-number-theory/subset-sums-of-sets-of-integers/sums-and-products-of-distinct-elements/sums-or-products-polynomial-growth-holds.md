# Many sums or products of distinct elements

For every $k$ there is $N$ such that every finite set $A$ of integers with $|A| \ge N$ has at least $|A|^k$ integers that are the sum or the product of the elements of a nonempty subset of $A$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 53 (Erdős–Szemerédi question on sums or products of distinct elements)](sums-or-products-polynomial-growth.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Chang (2003), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- M.-C. Chang, The Erdős–Szemerédi problem on sum set and product set, Ann. of Math. (2003), 939–957.
