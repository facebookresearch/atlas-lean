# Erdős Problem 53 (Erdős–Szemerédi question on sums or products of distinct elements)

The statement that for every $k$ there is $N$ such that every finite set $A$ of integers with $|A| \ge N$ has at least $|A|^k$ integers that are the sum or the product of the elements of a nonempty subset of $A$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Many sums or products of distinct elements](sums-or-products-polynomial-growth-holds.md).
