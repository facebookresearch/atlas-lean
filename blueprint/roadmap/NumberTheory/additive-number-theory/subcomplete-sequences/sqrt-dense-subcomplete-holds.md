# Square-root dense sets are subcomplete

There is an absolute constant $C > 0$ such that every set $A$ of natural numbers with $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \ge C\sqrt N$ for all sufficiently large $N$ is subcomplete: the set of sums of finite subsets of $A$ contains an infinite arithmetic progression.

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #344, https://www.erdosproblems.com/344.
- E. Szemerédi and V. H. Vu, Finite and infinite arithmetic progressions in sumsets, Ann. of Math. (2006), 1–35.
