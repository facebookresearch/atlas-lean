# The powers of two have an additive complement of size O(N/log N)

There is a set $A$ of natural numbers with $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \le C N/\log N$ for some $C$ and all sufficiently large $N$, such that every sufficiently large integer is $2^k + a$ with $k \ge 0$ and $a \in A$.
