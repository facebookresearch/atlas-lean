# Erdős Problem 221 (a thin additive complement of the powers of two)

The statement that there is a set $A$ of natural numbers with $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \le C N/\log N$ for some $C$ and all sufficiently large $N$, such that every sufficiently large integer is $2^k + a$ with $k \ge 0$ and $a \in A$.
