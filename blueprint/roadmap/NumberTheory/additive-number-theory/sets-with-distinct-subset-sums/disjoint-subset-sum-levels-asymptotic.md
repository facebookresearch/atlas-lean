# Erdős Problem 874 (Erdős–Straus problem on disjoint subset sum levels)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $N$, some subset of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ with at least $(2 - \varepsilon)\sqrt N$ elements has disjoint subset sum levels, and no subset of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ with more than $(2 + \varepsilon)\sqrt N$ elements has them; here a finite set $A$ of natural numbers has disjoint subset sum levels when any two nonempty subsets of $A$ of different sizes have different sums. That is, the largest size $k(N)$ of such a set satisfies $k(N) \sim 2\sqrt N$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Largest sets whose subset sums of different sizes are disjoint](disjoint-subset-sum-levels-asymptotic-ho.md).
