# Motzkin–Ralston–Selfridge upper bound $0.4$ for the minimum-overlap constant

The
continuous Erdős minimum-overlap constant $C_{1b}$ satisfies $C_{1b} \le 0.4$.

## Sources

- Theodore S. Motzkin, K. E. Ralston, and John L. Selfridge, *Minimal overlap under translation*, abstract, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society 62 (1956), 558; *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository curated by Terence Tao), Constant 1b, https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/1b.html.
