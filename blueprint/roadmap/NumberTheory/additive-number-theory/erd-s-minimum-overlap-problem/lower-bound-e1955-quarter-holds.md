# Erdős's lower bound $C_{1b} \ge 1/4$

The continuous Erdős minimum-overlap constant
$C_{1b}$ satisfies $1/4 \le C_{1b}$.

## Sources

- Paul Erdős, *Problems and results in additive number theory*, Colloque sur la Théorie des Nombres, Bruxelles, 1955, 127–137 (1956); *Optimization Constants in Mathematics* (crowdsourced repository curated by Terence Tao), Constant 1b, https://teorth.github.io/optimizationproblems/constants/1b.html.
