# Overlaps of Haugland's 15-step pair at whole-cell translations

Write $h_i$ for
`weight i / 10⁸`. For a natural number $k$, the overlap of Haugland's 15-step function and its
complement at the translation $x = 2k/15$ by $k$ cells is
$\frac{2}{15} \sum_{i < 15 - k} h_i (1 - h_{i + k})$.

## Sources

- the integral (1) in Jan Kristian Haugland, *The minimum overlap problem revisited* (2016), arXiv:1609.08000, computed cell by cell.
