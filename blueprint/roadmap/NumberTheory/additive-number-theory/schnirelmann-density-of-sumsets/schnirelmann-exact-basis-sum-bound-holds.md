# Plünnecke's bound for Schnirelmann density of sums with an exact basis

For every set $B$ of natural numbers with $0 \in B$ such that every natural number is the sum of exactly $k$ elements of $B$, and every set $A$ of natural numbers, $\sigma(A + B) \ge \alpha + \alpha(1 - \alpha)/k$, where $\alpha = \sigma(A)$ and $\sigma$ is the Schnirelmann density.

This resolves [Exact-basis variant of Erdős Problem 35](schnirelmann-exact-basis-sum-bound.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Plünnecke (1970), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #35, https://www.erdosproblems.com/35.
- H. Plünnecke, Eine zahlentheoretische Anwendung der Graphentheorie, J. Reine Angew. Math. (1970), 171–183.
