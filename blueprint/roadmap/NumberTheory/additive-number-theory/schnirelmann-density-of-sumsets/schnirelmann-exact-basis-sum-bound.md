# Exact-basis variant of Erdős Problem 35

The statement that for every set $B$ of natural numbers with $0 \in B$ such that every natural number is the sum of exactly $k$ elements of $B$, and every set $A$ of natural numbers, $\sigma(A + B) \ge \alpha + \alpha(1 - \alpha)/k$, where $\alpha = \sigma(A)$ and $\sigma$ is the Schnirelmann density.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Plünnecke's bound for Schnirelmann density of sums with an exact basis](schnirelmann-exact-basis-sum-bound-holds.md).
