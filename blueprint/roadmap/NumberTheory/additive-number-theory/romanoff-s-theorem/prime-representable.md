# Sums of a power of two and a prime

A natural number $m$ is of this form when
$m = 2^k + p$ for some natural number $k$ and some prime $p$.

## Sources

- Romanoff's theorem, as recorded in T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #851, https://www.erdosproblems.com/851.
- N. P. Romanoff, *Über einige Sätze der additiven Zahlentheorie*, Mathematische Annalen 109 (1934), 668–678.
