# Powers of two plus integers with few prime factors

For $g : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$, a natural number $n$ is representable when $n = 2^k + m$ for some $k \ge 0$ and some positive integer $m$ with $\Omega(m) < g(m)$, where $\Omega(m)$ is the number of prime factors of $m$ counted with multiplicity.
