# Erdős Problem 16 (odd integers not of the form 2^k + p)

The statement that the set of odd natural numbers not of the form $2^k + p$ ($k \ge 0$, $p$ prime) is the union of an infinite arithmetic progression $\{a + md : m \in \mathbb{N}\}$ ($d > 0$) and a set of natural density $0$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
