# Sets with no square product of an odd number of elements are not almost full

It is not the case that $F_{2k+1}(N) \sim N$ as $N \to \infty$ for every $k \ge 2$, where $F_k(N)$ is the largest size of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ such that no product of $k$ distinct elements of $A$ is a perfect square.
