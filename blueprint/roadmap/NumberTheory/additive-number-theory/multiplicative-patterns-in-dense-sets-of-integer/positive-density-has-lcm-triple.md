# Erdős Problem 487 (least common multiples in sets of positive density)

The statement that every set $A$ of natural numbers having a positive natural density (the proportion of $A$ among $0, 1, \dots, N-1$ converges to some $\alpha > 0$) contains distinct $a, b, c$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) = c$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sets of positive density contain a least common multiple of two of their elements](positive-density-has-lcm-triple-holds.md).
