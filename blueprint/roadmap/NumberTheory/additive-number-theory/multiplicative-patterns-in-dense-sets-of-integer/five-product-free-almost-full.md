# Erdős Problem 121, first question

The statement that $F_5(N) \sim N$ as $N \to \infty$, where $F_k(N)$ is the largest size of a set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ such that no product of $k$ distinct elements of $A$ is a perfect square.
