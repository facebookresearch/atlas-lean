# Erdős Problem 164 (primes maximize the Erdős sum of primitive sets)

The statement that for every set $A$ of integers at least $2$ in which no element divides another, the sum $\sum_{a \in A} 1/(a \log a)$ converges and is at most $\sum_{p} 1/(p \log p)$, the sum over all primes.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Erdős primitive set conjecture](primes-maximize-erdos-sum-holds.md).
