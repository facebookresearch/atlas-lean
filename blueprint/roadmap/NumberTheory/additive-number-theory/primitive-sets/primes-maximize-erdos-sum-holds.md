# Erdős primitive set conjecture

For every set $A$ of integers at least $2$ in which no element divides another, the sum $\sum_{a \in A} 1/(a \log a)$ converges and is at most $\sum_{p} 1/(p \log p)$, the sum over all primes.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 164 (primes maximize the Erdős sum of primitive sets)](primes-maximize-erdos-sum.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Lichtman (2022), cited under Sources.
