# Erdős Problem 54 (sparse Ramsey complete sets)

The statement that there are a real $C$ and a Ramsey $2$-complete set $A$ of positive integers with $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}| \le C(\log N)^2$ for every $N \ge 2$, where a set $A$ of natural numbers is Ramsey $2$-complete when for every colouring of $A$ with two colours, every sufficiently large integer is the sum of a finite set of distinct elements of $A$ of one colour.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
