# Quadratic recurrence sets need not be additive bases of order two

It is not the case that for every irrational $\alpha > 0$, every sufficiently large integer is the sum of two elements of the set of integers $n \ge 2$ with $\|\alpha n^2\| < 1/\log n$, where $\|y\|$ is the distance from $y$ to the nearest integer.
