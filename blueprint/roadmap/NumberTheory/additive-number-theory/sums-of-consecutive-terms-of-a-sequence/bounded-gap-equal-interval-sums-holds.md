# Bounded-gap sequences have equal interval sums

For all natural numbers $a_1, K \ge 1$ there is $f$ such that every strictly increasing sequence $b_0 < b_1 < \dots < b_{s-1}$ of natural numbers with $s \ge 1$, $b_0 = a_1$, consecutive gaps $b_{t+1} - b_t \le K$ and $b_{s-1} > f$ has two distinct intervals of consecutive indices with equal sums.

- N. Hegyvári, On consecutive sums in sequences, Acta Math. Hungar. 48 (1986), 193–200, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01949064.
