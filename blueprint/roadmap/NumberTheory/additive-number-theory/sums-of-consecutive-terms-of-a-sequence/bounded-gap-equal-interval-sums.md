# Erdős Problem 1213 (equal interval sums in sequences with bounded gaps)

The statement that for all natural numbers $a_1, K \ge 1$ there is $f$ such that every strictly increasing sequence $b_0 < b_1 < \dots < b_{s-1}$ of natural numbers with $s \ge 1$, $b_0 = a_1$, consecutive gaps $b_{t+1} - b_t \le K$ and $b_{s-1} > f$ has two distinct intervals of consecutive indices with equal sums.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Bounded-gap sequences have equal interval sums](bounded-gap-equal-interval-sums-holds.md).
