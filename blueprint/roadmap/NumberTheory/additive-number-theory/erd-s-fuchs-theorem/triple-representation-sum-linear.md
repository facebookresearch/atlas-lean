# Erdős Problem 764 (three-fold Erdős–Fuchs question)

The statement that there are a set $A$ of natural numbers and reals $c > 0$ and $C$ with $\bigl|\sum_{n=0}^{N} r_3(n) - cN\bigr| \le C$ for every $N$, where $r_3(n)$ is the number of ordered triples of elements of $A$ with sum $n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Three-fold Erdős–Fuchs theorem](triple-representation-sum-linear-refuted.md).
