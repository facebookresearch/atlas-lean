# Theorem 9.3

Let $V$ be a finite set. For any $v_{0} \in V$, the expected value of $\rho$
 for a walk from $v_{0}$ defined by a random function $f: V \to V$ is
 $\mathrm{E}[\rho] \sim \sqrt{\pi N / 2}$ as the cardinality $N$ of $V$ tends
 to infinity.
