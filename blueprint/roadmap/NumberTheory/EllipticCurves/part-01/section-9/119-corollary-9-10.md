# Corollary 9.10

Let $G$ be a cyclic group of prime order $N$. Every generic Las Vegas algorithm for the discrete logarithm problem in $G$ that generates an expected $o(\sqrt{N} / \log N)$ random group elements uses at least $(2\sqrt{2}/3 + o(1))\sqrt{N}$ expected group operations.
