# Corollary 9.9

Let $G$ be a cyclic group of prime order $N$. Every generic Monte Carlo algorithm for the discrete logarithm problem in $G$ that uses $o(\sqrt{N} / \log N)$ random group elements uses at least $(1 + o(1))\sqrt{N}$ group operations.
