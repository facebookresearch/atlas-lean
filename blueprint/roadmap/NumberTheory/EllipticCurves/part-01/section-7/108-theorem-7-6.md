# Theorem 7.6

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group with exponent $\lambda(G)$. Let $\alpha$
 and $\beta$ be uniformly distributed random elements of $G$. Then
 $\operatorname{Pr}[\operatorname{lcm}(|\alpha|,|\beta|)=\lambda(G)]>\frac{6}{\pi^{2}}$.
