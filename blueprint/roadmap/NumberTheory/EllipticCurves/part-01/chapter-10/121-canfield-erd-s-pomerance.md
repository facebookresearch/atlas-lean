# Theorem 10.6 (Canfield-Erdős-Pomerance)

The asymptotic bound $\frac{1}{x} \psi(x, x^{1/u}) = u^{-u+o(u)}$ holds
 uniformly as $u, x \rightarrow \infty$, provided that $u < (1-\epsilon) \log
 x / \log \log x$ for some $\epsilon > 0$.
