# Corollary 3.31

Let $R$ be a commutative ring containing a primitive $n$th root of unity,
 with $n = 2^k$, and assume $2 \in R^{\times}$. We can multiply two
 polynomials in $R[x]$ of degree less than $n/2$ using $O(n \log n)$
 operations in $R$.
