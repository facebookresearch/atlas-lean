# Theorem 3.6

Let $q=p^{n}$ be a prime power. The field $\mathbb{F}_{q}$ has cardinality
 $q$ and every field of cardinality $q$ is (non-canonically) isomorphic to
 $\mathbb{F}_{q}$.
