# Theorem 3.3

The ring $\mathbb{F}_{p}$ is a field, and every field of characteristic $p$
 contains a canonical subfield isomorphic to $\mathbb{F}_{p}$. In particular,
 all fields of cardinality $p$ are isomorphic.
