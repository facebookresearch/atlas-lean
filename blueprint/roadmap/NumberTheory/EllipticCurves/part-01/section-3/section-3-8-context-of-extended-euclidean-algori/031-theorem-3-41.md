# Theorem 3.41

Let $q=p^{e}$ be a prime power and assume $\log e=O(\log p)$. The time to
 invert an element of $\mathbb{F}_{q}^{\times}$ is $O(\operatorname{M}(n)
 \log n)=O(n \log^{2} n)$, where $n=\lg q$.
