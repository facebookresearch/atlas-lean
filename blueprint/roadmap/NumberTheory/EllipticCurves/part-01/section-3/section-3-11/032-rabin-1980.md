# Theorem 3.44 (Rabin 1980)

For every pair of distinct $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{F}_{q}$ we have
 $$\#\left\{\delta \in \mathbb{F}_{q} \mid \alpha+\delta \text{ and }
 \beta+\delta \text{ are of different type}\right\}=\frac{q-1}{2}.$$
