# Theorem 15.1

Let $L \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be a lattice and let $E_{L}: y^{2}=4
 x^{3}-g_{2}(L) x-g_{3}(L)$ be the corresponding elliptic curve. The map
 $\Phi: \mathbb{C} / L \rightarrow E_{L}(\mathbb{C})$ is a group isomorphism.
