# Lemma 6.6

Let $\delta: E_{0} \rightarrow E_{1}$, $\alpha, \beta: E_{1} \rightarrow
 E_{2}$, and $\gamma: E_{2} \rightarrow E_{3}$ be isogenies. Then $\delta
 \circ \alpha = \delta \circ \beta \Longrightarrow \alpha = \beta$ and
 $\alpha \circ \gamma = \beta \circ \gamma \Longrightarrow \alpha = \beta$.
