# Theorem 6.7

For any isogeny $\alpha: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ of elliptic curves over a
 field $k$ there exists a unique isogeny $\hat{\alpha}: E_{2} \rightarrow
 E_{1}$ for which $\hat{\alpha} \circ \alpha = [n]$, where $n = \deg \alpha$.
