# Proposition 6.5

Let $E_{1}, E_{2}$ be elliptic curves over a field $k$. For all $n \in
 \mathbb{Z}$ and all $\alpha \in \operatorname{Hom}(E_{1}, E_{2})$ we have
 $[n] \circ \alpha = n\alpha = \alpha \circ [n]$, where the map $[n]$ on the
 LHS is multiplication-by-$n$ on $E_{2}$ and the map $[n]$ on the RHS is
 multiplication-by-$n$ on $E_{1}$.
