# Definition 6.2

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve defined over a field of characteristic $p>0$. If
 $E[p] \simeq \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ then $E$ is said to be ordinary, and if
 $E[p] \simeq \{0\}$, we say that $E$ is supersingular.
