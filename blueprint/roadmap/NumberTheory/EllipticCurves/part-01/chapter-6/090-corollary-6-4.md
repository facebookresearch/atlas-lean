# Corollary 6.4

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. Every finite subgroup of $E(\bar{k})$ can be
 written as the direct sum of two (possibly trivial) cyclic groups, at most
 one of which has order divisible by the characteristic of $k$. If
 $k=\mathbb{F}_{q}$ is a finite field of characteristic $p$ we have
 $E(\mathbb{F}_{q}) \simeq \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z} \oplus
 \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ for some positive integers $m, n$ with $m \mid n$
 and $p \nmid m$.
