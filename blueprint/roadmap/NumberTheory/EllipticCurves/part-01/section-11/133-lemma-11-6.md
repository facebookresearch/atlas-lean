# Lemma 11.6

Let $p = 2^s t + 1$ be prime, with $t$ odd, and let $a$ be an integer that is
 nonzero modulo $p$. Exactly one of the following holds: (i) $a^t \equiv 1
 \bmod p$; (ii) $a^{2^i t} \equiv -1 \bmod p$, for some $0 \leq i < s$.
