# Theorem 11.13 (Goldwasser-Kilian)

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve, and let $M, N > 1$ be integers with
 $M > (N^{1/4}+1)^2$ and $N \perp \Delta(E)$, and let $P \in E(\mathbb{Q})$.
 If $MP$ is zero $\bmod N$ and $(M/\ell)P$ is strongly nonzero $\bmod N$ for
 every prime $\ell \mid M$ then $N$ is prime.
