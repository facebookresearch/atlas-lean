# Definition 1.3

A polynomial $f \in k[x, y]$ is nondegenerate with respect to an edge
 $\gamma$ of $\partial \Delta(f)$ if the polynomials $f_{\gamma}, x
 \frac{\partial f_{\gamma}}{\partial x}, y \frac{\partial
 f_{\gamma}}{\partial y}$ have no common zero in $(\bar{k}^{\times})^{2}$.
 The polynomial $f$ is nondegenerate with respect to $\Delta(f)$ if it is
 nondegenerate with respect to every edge of $\partial \Delta(f)$ and not
 divisible by $x$ or $y$.
