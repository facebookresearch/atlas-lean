# Proposition 1.5

Let $f(x, y) \in k[x, y]$ be an irreducible nondegenerate polynomial in
 $\bar{k}[x, y]$, and suppose $f^{*}(x, y, z)$ has no singularities outside
 $\{(0: 0: 1),(0: 1: 0),(1: 0: 0)\}$. Then $g(F)=\#\{\Delta^{\circ}(F) \cap
 \mathbb{Z}^{2}\}$.
