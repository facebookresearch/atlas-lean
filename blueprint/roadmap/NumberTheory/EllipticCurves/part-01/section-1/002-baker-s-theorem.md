# Theorem 1.2 (Baker's Theorem)

Let $f(x, y) \in k[x, y]$ be irreducible in $\bar{k}[x, y]$, and let $F
 \coloneqq \operatorname{Frac}(k[x, y]/(f))$ denote the corresponding
 function field, with genus $g(F)$. Then $g(F) \leq \#\{\Delta^{\circ}(F)
 \cap \mathbb{Z}^{2}\}$.
