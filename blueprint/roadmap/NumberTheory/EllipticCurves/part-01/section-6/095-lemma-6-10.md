# Lemma 6.10

For an isogeny $\alpha$ of degree $n$ we have $\alpha \circ
 \hat{\alpha}=[n]$, meaning that $\hat{\hat{\alpha}}=\alpha$. For any $n \in
 \mathbb{Z}$ the endomorphism $[n]$ is self-dual, that is, $[\hat{n}]=[n]$.
