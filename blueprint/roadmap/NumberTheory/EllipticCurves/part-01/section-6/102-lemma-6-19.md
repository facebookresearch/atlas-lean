# Lemma 6.19

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be endomorphisms of an elliptic curve $E/k$ and let
 $m$ be the maximum of $\operatorname{deg} \alpha$ and $\operatorname{deg}
 \beta$. Let $n \geq 2\sqrt{m}+1$ be an integer prime to the characteristic
 of $k$, and also relatively prime to the integers $\operatorname{deg}
 \alpha$ and $\operatorname{deg} \beta$. If $\alpha_n=\beta_n$ then
 $\alpha=\beta$.
