# Definition 6.13

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. The endomorphism ring of $E$ is the additive
 group $\operatorname{End}(E)\coloneqq\operatorname{Hom}(E, E)$ with
 multiplication given by composition: $\alpha \beta\coloneqq\alpha \circ
 \beta$.
