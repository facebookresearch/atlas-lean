# Lemma 6.12

For any $\alpha \in \operatorname{Hom}(E_2, E_3)$ and $\beta \in
 \operatorname{Hom}(E_1, E_2)$ we have $\widehat{\alpha \circ
 \beta}=\hat{\beta} \circ \hat{\alpha}$.
