# Theorem 6.18

Let $\alpha$ be an endomorphism of an elliptic curve. Both $\alpha$ and its
 dual $\hat{\alpha}$ are solutions to $\lambda^{2}-(\operatorname{tr} \alpha)
 \lambda+\operatorname{deg} \alpha=0$.
