# Definition 14.9

The order of an elliptic function is the number of poles it has in any
 fundamental parallelogram, where each pole is counted with multiplicity
 equal to its order (this is a finite number because the poles in a
 fundamental parallelogram are a discrete subset of its closure, which is
 compact).
