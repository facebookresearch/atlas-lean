# Definition 14.1

A lattice $L=\left[\omega_{1}, \omega_{2}\right]$ is an additive subgroup
 $\omega_{1} \mathbb{Z}+\omega_{2} \mathbb{Z}$ of $\mathbb{C}$ generated by
 complex numbers $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ that are linearly independent
 over $\mathbb{R}$.
