# Definition 14.8

An elliptic function for a lattice $L$ is a complex function $f(z)$ such that
 1. $f$ is meromorphic on $\mathbb{C}$. 2. $f$ is periodic with respect to
 $L$; this means that $f(z+\omega)=f(z)$ for all $\omega \in L$.
