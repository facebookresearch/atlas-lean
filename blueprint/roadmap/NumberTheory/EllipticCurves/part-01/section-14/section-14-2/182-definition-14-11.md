# Definition 14.11

A smooth curve in $\mathbb{C}$ is a continuously differentiable function
 $\gamma:[a, b] \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ where $[a, b]$ is a closed interval
 in $\mathbb{R}$. A piecewise smooth curve $\gamma:[a, b] \rightarrow
 \mathbb{C}$ is defined by a finite sequence of $n$ smooth curves
 $\gamma_{i}:[a_{i}, b_{i}] \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ with $a_{0}=a,
 a_{i+1}=b_{i}$, and $b_{n}=b$. We will simply use the term curve to refer to
 a piecewise smooth curve. A curve is simple if its restriction to the open
 interval $(a, b)$ is injective, and it is closed if $\gamma(a)=\gamma(b)$.
