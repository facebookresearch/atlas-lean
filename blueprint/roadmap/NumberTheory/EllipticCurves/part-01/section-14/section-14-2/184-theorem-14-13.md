# Theorem 14.13

Let $\Omega$ be an open set containing a curve $\gamma:[a, b] \rightarrow
 \mathbb{C}$, and let $F(z)$ be a holomorphic function on $\Omega$ and let
 $f(z)=F'(z)$. Then $$\int_{\gamma} f(z) dz=F(\gamma(b))-F(\gamma(a))$$
