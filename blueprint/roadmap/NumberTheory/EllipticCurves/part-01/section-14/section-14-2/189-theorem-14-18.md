# Theorem 14.18

Let $f(z)$ be a nonzero elliptic function for a lattice $L$. When counted
 with multiplicity, the number of zeros of $f(z)$ in any fundamental
 parallelogram $F_{\alpha}$ for $L$ is equal to the number of poles of $f(z)$
 in $F_{\alpha}$.
