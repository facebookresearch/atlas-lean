# Theorem 14.16 (Residue formula)

Let $\gamma$ be a simple closed curve with positive orientation and let
 $f(z)$ be a function that is meromorphic on an open set containing $\gamma$
 and its interior with no poles on $\gamma$. Let $z_{1}, \ldots, z_{N}$ be
 the poles of $f(z)$ that lie in the interior of $\gamma$. Then
 $$\int_{\gamma} f(z) dz=2 \pi i \sum_{k=1}^{N}
 \operatorname{res}_{z_{k}}(f)$$
