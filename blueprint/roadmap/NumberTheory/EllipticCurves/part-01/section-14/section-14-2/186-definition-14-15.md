# Definition 14.15

The residue at $z_{0}$ of a function $f(z)=\sum_{n \geq n_{0}}
 a_{n}(z-z_{0})^{n}$ that is meromorphic on an open neighborhood of $z_{0}$
 is $$\operatorname{res}_{z_{0}}(f)\coloneqqa_{-1}$$
