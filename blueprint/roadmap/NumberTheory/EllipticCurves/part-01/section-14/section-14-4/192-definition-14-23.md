# Definition 14.23

The Weierstrass $\wp$-function of a lattice $L$ is defined by
 $$\wp(z)\coloneqq\wp(z ; L)\coloneqq\frac{1}{z^{2}}+\sum_{\omega \in
 L^{*}}\left(\frac{1}{(z-\omega)^{2}}-\frac{1}{\omega^{2}}\right).$$
