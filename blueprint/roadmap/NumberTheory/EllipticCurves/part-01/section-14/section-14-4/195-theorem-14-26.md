# Theorem 14.26

For any lattice $L$, the function $\wp(z)=\wp(z ; L)$ and its derivative
 $$\wp^{\prime}(z)=-2 \sum_{\omega \in L} \frac{1}{(z-\omega)^{3}}$$ satisfy
 the following: (i) $\wp(z)$ is a meromorphic even function whose poles
 consist of double poles at each $z \in L$. (ii) $\wp^{\prime}(z)$ is a
 meromorphic odd function whose poles consist of triple poles at each $z \in
 L$.
