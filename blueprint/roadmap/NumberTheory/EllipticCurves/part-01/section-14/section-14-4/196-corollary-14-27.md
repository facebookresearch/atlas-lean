# Corollary 14.27

Let $L$ be a lattice. The function $\wp(z)=\wp(z ; L)$ is an elliptic
 function of order 2 for $L$, and its derivative $\wp^{\prime}(z)$ is an
 elliptic function of order 3 for $L$.
