# Theorem 14.29

Let $L$ be a lattice. The function $\wp(z)=\wp(z ; L)$ satisfies the
 differential equation $$\wp^{\prime}(z)^{2}=4 \wp(z)^{3}-g_{2}(L)
 \wp(z)-g_{3}(L)$$ where $g_{2}(L)=60 G_{4}(L)$ and $g_{3}(L)=140 G_{6}(L)$.
