# Definition 14.19

Let $L$ be a lattice and let $k>2$ be an integer. The weight-$k$ Eisenstein
 series for $L$ is the sum $$G_{k}(L)=\sum_{\omega \in L^{*}}
 \frac{1}{\omega^{k}}$$ where $L^{*}=L-\{0\}$.
