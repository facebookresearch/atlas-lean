# Lemma 8.2

Let $E/\mathbb{F}_{q}$ be an elliptic curve with Frobenius endomorphism
 $\pi$, let $\ell$ be a prime not dividing $q$, and let $P \in E[\ell]$ be
 nonzero. Suppose that for some integer $c$ the equation $\pi_{\ell}^{2}(P)-c
 \pi_{\ell}(P)+q_{\ell}(P)=0$ holds. Then $c \equiv
 t_{\ell}=\operatorname{tr} \pi \bmod \ell$.
