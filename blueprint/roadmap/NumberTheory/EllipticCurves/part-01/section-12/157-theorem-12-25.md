# Theorem 12.25

The set of algebraic integers $\mathcal{O}_{K}$ in a number field $K$ form a
 ring that is a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module of rank $r$, where
 $r=[K:\mathbb{Q}]$ is the dimension of $K$ as a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space.
