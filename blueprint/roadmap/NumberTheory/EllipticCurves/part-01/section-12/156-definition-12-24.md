# Definition 12.24

An algebraic number $\alpha$ is a complex number that is the root of a
 polynomial with coefficients in $\mathbb{Q}$. An algebraic integer is a
 complex number that is the root of a monic polynomial with coefficients in
 $\mathbb{Z}$.
