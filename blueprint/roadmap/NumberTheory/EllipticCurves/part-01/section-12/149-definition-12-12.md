# Definition 12.12

A quaternion algebra over a field $k$ is a $k$-algebra that has a $k$-basis
 of the form $\{1, \alpha, \beta, \alpha \beta\}$, with $\alpha^{2},
 \beta^{2} \in k^{\times}$ and $\alpha \beta=-\beta \alpha$.
