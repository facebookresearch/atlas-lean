# Lemma 12.10

Let $\alpha \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$. Then $\alpha$ and $\hat{\alpha}$
 are roots of the polynomial $x^{2}-(\mathrm{T} \alpha) x+\mathrm{N} \alpha
 \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$.
