# Theorem 12.17

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. Then $\operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ is
 isomorphic to one of: (i) the field of rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$; (ii)
 an imaginary quadratic field $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ with $\alpha^{2}<0$; (iii)
 a quaternion algebra $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \beta)$ with $\alpha^{2},
 \beta^{2}<0$.
