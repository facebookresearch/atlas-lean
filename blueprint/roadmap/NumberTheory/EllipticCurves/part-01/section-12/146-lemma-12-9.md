# Lemma 12.9

For all $\alpha \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ we have $\mathrm{T}
 \hat{\alpha}=\mathrm{T} \alpha \in \mathbb{Q}$. For any $r \in \mathbb{Q},
 \alpha, \beta \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ we have
 $\mathrm{T}(\alpha+\beta)=\mathrm{T} \alpha+\mathrm{T} \beta$, and
 $\mathrm{T}(r \alpha)=r \mathrm{T} \alpha$.
