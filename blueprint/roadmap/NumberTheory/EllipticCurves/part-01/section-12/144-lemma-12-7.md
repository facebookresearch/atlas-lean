# Lemma 12.7

For all $\alpha \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ we have $\mathrm{N} \alpha \in
 \mathbb{Q}_{\geq 0}$, with $\mathrm{N} \alpha=0$ if and only if $\alpha=0$.
 We also have $\mathrm{N} \hat{\alpha}=\mathrm{N} \alpha$ and
 $\mathrm{N}(\alpha \beta)=\mathrm{N} \alpha \mathrm{N} \beta$ for all
 $\alpha, \beta \in \operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$.
