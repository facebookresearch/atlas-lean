# Corollary 12.20

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. The endomorphism ring $\operatorname{End}(E)$
 is a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module of rank $r$, where $r=1,2,4$ is the dimension
 of $\operatorname{End}^{0}(E)$ as a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space.
