# Theorem 12.27

Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field with ring of integers
 $\mathcal{O}_{K}$. The orders $\mathcal{O}$ in $K$ are precisely the
 subrings $\mathbb{Z}+f\mathcal{O}_{K}$, where $f$ is any positive integer.
