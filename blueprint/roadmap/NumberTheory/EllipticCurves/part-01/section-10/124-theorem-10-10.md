# Theorem 10.10

Let $p$ and $q$ be prime divisors of $N$, and let $\ell_p$ and $\ell_q$ be
 the largest prime divisors of $p-1$ and $q-1$, respectively. If $\ell_p \leq
 B$ and $\ell_p < \ell_q$ then Algorithm 10.8 succeeds with probability at
 least $1 - 1/\ell_q$.
