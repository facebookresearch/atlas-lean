# Lemma 17.5

Let $\alpha$ be a nonzero element of an order $\mathcal{O}$ in a number field
 $K$. Then $\mathrm{N}(\alpha) = |\mathrm{N} \alpha|$, where $(\alpha)$
 denotes the principal $\mathcal{O}$-ideal generated by $\alpha$.
