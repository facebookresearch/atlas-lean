# Theorem 17.14

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order, let $E / \mathbb{C}$ be an
 elliptic curve with endomorphism ring $\mathcal{O}$, let $\mathfrak{a}$ be
 an invertible $\mathcal{O}$-ideal, and let $\phi_{\mathfrak{a}}$ be the
 corresponding isogeny from $E$ to $\mathfrak{a} E$. The following hold: (i)
 $\operatorname{ker} \phi_{\mathfrak{a}}=E[\mathfrak{a}]$; (ii)
 $\operatorname{deg} \phi_{\mathfrak{a}}=\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{a}$.
