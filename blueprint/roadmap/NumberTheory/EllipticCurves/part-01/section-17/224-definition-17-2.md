# Definition 17.2

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an integral domain with fraction field $K$. For any
 $\lambda \in K^{\times}$ and $\mathcal{O}$-ideal $\mathfrak{a}$, the
 $\mathcal{O}$-module $\mathfrak{b}=\lambda \mathfrak{a}\coloneqq\{\lambda
 \alpha \mid \alpha \in \mathfrak{a}\}$ is called a fractional
 $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. Multiplication of fractional ideals
 $\mathfrak{b}=\lambda \mathfrak{a}$ and
 $\mathfrak{b}^{\prime}=\lambda^{\prime} \mathfrak{a}^{\prime}$ is defined by
 $\mathfrak{b} \mathfrak{b}^{\prime}\coloneqq(\lambda \lambda^{\prime})
 \mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{a}^{\prime}$, where $\mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{a}'$ is
 the product of the $\mathcal{O}$-ideals $\mathfrak{a}$ and
 $\mathfrak{a}^{\prime}$.
