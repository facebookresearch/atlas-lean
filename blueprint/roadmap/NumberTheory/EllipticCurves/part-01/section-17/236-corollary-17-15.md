# Corollary 17.15

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order and let $\mathfrak{a}$ be
 an invertible $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. For every elliptic curve $E / \mathbb{C}$
 with CM by $\mathcal{O}$ the elliptic curves $E$ and $\mathfrak{a} E$ are
 related by an isogeny $\phi_{\mathfrak{a}}: E \rightarrow \mathfrak{a} E$ of
 degree $\mathrm{N}\mathfrak{a}$.
