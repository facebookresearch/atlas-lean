# Definition 17.3

Let $K/k$ be a field extension and let $\lambda \in K^{\times}$. The
 multiplication-by-$\lambda$ map $K \rightarrow K$ is an invertible linear
 transformation $M_{\lambda} \in \mathrm{GL}(K)$ of $K$ as a $k$-vector
 space. The (field) norm and trace of $\lambda$ are defined by
 $\mathrm{N}_{K/k} \lambda \coloneqq \det M_{\lambda} \in k^{\times}$ and
 $\mathrm{T}_{K/k} \lambda \coloneqq \operatorname{tr} M_{\lambda} \in k$.
