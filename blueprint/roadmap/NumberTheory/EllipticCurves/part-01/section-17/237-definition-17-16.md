# Definition 17.16

Let $\mathcal{O}=[1, \tau]$ be an imaginary quadratic order. The discriminant
 of $\mathcal{O}$ is the discriminant of the minimal polynomial of $\tau$,
 which we can compute as
 $\operatorname{disc}(\mathcal{O})=(\tau+\bar{\tau})^{2}-4\tau\bar{\tau}=(\tau-\bar{\tau})^{2}=\operatorname{det}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}
 1 & \tau \\ 1 & \bar{\tau} \end{smallmatrix}\right)^{2}$.
