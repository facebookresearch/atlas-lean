# Theorem 17.18

Let $D$ be an imaginary quadratic discriminant. There is a unique imaginary
 quadratic order $\mathcal{O}$ with $\operatorname{disc}(\mathcal{O})=D=u^{2}
 D_{K}$, where $D_{K}$ is the fundamental discriminant of the maximal order
 $\mathcal{O}_{K}$ in $K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D_{K}})$, and
 $u=[\mathcal{O}_{K}:\mathcal{O}]$.
