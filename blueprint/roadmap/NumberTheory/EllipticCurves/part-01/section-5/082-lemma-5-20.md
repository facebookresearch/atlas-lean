# Lemma 5.20

For every integer $n$,

$\psi_n$ lies in $\mathbb{Z}[x, A, B]$ if $n$ is odd, and in $2y\mathbb{Z}[x, A, B]$ if $n$ is even;

$\phi_n$ lies in $\mathbb{Z}[x, A, B]$ for all $n$;

$\omega_n$ lies in $\mathbb{Z}[x, A, B]$ if $n$ is even, and in $y\mathbb{Z}[x, A, B]$ if $n$ is odd.
