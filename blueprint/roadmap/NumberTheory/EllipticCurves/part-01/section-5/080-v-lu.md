# Theorem 5.13 (Vélu)

Let $E: y^2 = x^3 + Ax + B$ be an elliptic curve over $k$ and let $x_0 \in
 \bar{k}$ be a root of $x^3 + Ax + B$. Define $t \coloneqq 3x_0^2 + A$ and $w
 \coloneqq x_0 t$. The rational map $\phi(x, y) \coloneqq \left(\frac{x^2 -
 x_0 x + t}{x - x_0}, \frac{(x - x_0)^2 - t}{(x - x_0)^2} y\right)$ is a
 separable isogeny from $E$ to $E': y^2 = x^3 + A'x + B'$, where $A'
 \coloneqq A - 5t$ and $B' \coloneqq B - 7w$. The kernel of $\phi$ is the
 group of order 2 generated by $(x_0, 0)$.
