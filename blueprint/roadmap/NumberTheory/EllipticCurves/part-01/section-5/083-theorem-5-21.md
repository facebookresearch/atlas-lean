# Theorem 5.21

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve defined by the equation $y^2 = x^3 + Ax + B$ and let $n$ be a nonzero integer. The rational map
$$[n](x,y) = \left(\frac{\phi_n(x)}{\psi_n^2(x)}, \frac{\omega_n(x,y)}{\psi_n^3(x,y)}\right)$$
sends each point $P \in E(\bar{k})$ to $nP$.
