# Definition 4.18

An isogeny $\phi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ of elliptic curves defined over
 $k$ is a surjective morphism of curves that induces a group homomorphism
 $E_{1}(\bar{k}) \rightarrow E_{2}(\bar{k})$. The elliptic curves $E_{1}$ and
 $E_{2}$ are then said to be isogenous.
