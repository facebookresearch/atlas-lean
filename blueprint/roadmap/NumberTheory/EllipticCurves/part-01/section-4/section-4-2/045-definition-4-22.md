# Definition 4.22

Elliptic curves $E_{1}$ and $E_{2}$ defined over a field $k$ are isomorphic
 if there exist isogenies $\phi_{1}: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ and $\phi_{2}:
 E_{2} \rightarrow E_{1}$ whose composition is the identity; the isogenies
 $\phi_{1}$ and $\phi_{2}$ are then isomorphisms.
