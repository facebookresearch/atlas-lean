# Definition 4.31

Let $\alpha(x, y)=\left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}, \frac{s(x)}{t(x)} y\right)$ be an
 isogeny in standard form. The degree of $\alpha$ is $\deg
 \alpha\coloneqq\max \{\deg u, \deg v\}$, and we say that $\alpha$ is
 separable if the derivative of $\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}$ is nonzero; otherwise we
 say that $\alpha$ is inseparable.
