# Definition 4.7

Let $C / k$ be a projective curve with $\alpha \in k(C)$. We say that
 $\alpha$ is defined (or regular) at a point $P \in C(\bar{k})$ if $\alpha$
 can be represented as $g / h$ for some $g, h \in k[x, y, z]$ with $h(P) \neq
 0$.
