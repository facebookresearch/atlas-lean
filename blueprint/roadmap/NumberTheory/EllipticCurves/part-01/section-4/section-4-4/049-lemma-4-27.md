# Lemma 4.27

Let $E_{1}: y^{2}=f_{1}(x)$ and $E_{2}: y^{2}=f_{2}(x)$ be elliptic curves
 over $k$ and let $\alpha(x, y)=\left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}, \frac{s(x)}{t(x)}
 y\right)$ be an isogeny from $E_{1}$ to $E_{2}$ in standard form. Then
 $v^{3}$ divides $t^{2}$ and $t^{2}$ divides $v^{3} f_{1}$. Moreover, $v(x)$
 and $t(x)$ have the same set of roots in $\bar{k}$.
