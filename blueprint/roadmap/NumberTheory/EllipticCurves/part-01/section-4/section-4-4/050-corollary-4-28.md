# Corollary 4.28

Let $\alpha(x, y)=\left(\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}, \frac{s(x)}{t(x)} y\right)$ be an
 isogeny $E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ in standard form. The affine points
 $\left(x_{0}: y_{0}: 1\right) \in E_{1}(\bar{k})$ in the kernel of $\alpha$
 are precisely those for which $v\left(x_{0}\right)=0$.
