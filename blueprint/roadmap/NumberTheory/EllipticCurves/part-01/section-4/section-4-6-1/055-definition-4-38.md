# Definition 4.38

A field $k$ is perfect if any of the following equivalent conditions hold:
 (1) $\operatorname{char}(k)=0$ or $\operatorname{char}(k)=p>0$ and $k=\{x^p
 \mid x \in k\}$ ($k$ is fixed by Frobenius); (2) every finite extension of
 $k$ is separable over $k$; (3) every algebraic extension of $k$ is separable
 over $k$.
