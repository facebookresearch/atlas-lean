# Corollary 4.51

The maximal ideals of the ring $\bar{k}[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ are all of the
 form $m_P = (x_1 - P_1, \ldots, x_n - P_n)$ for some point $P = (P_1,
 \ldots, P_n)$ in $\mathbb{A}^n(\bar{k})$.
