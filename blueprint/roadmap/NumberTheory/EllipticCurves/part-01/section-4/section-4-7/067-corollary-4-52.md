# Corollary 4.52

There is a one-to-one inclusion-reversing correspondence between radical
 ideals $I \subseteq \bar{k}[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ and algebraic sets $Z
 \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n(\bar{k})$ in which $I = I(Z)$ and $Z = Z_I$.
