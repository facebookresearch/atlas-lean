# Theorem 13.6

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_{q}$ and suppose
 $\pi_{E} \notin \mathbb{Z}$. Then
 $\operatorname{End}^{0}(E)=\mathbb{Q}(\pi_{E}) \simeq \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$
 is an imaginary quadratic field with $D=(\operatorname{tr} \pi_{E})^{2}-4q$.
 This applies in particular whenever $q$ is prime, and also whenever $E$ is
 ordinary.
