# Theorem 13.14

Let $E$ and $E^{\prime}$ be elliptic curves over $k$. Then $E$ and
 $E^{\prime}$ are isomorphic over $\bar{k}$ if and only if
 $j(E)=j\left(E^{\prime}\right)$. If $j(E)=j\left(E^{\prime}\right)$ and the
 characteristic of $k$ is not 2 or 3 then there is a field extension $K / k$
 of degree at most 6, 4, or 2, depending on whether $j(E)=0$, $j(E)=1728$, or
 $j(E) \neq 0,1728$, such that $E$ and $E^{\prime}$ are isomorphic over $K$.
