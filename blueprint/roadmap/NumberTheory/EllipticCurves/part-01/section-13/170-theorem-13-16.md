# Theorem 13.16

Let $E$ be a supersingular elliptic curve over a field $k$ of characteristic
 $p>0$. Then $j(E)$ lies in $\mathbb{F}_{p^{2}}$ (and possibly in
 $\mathbb{F}_{p}$).
