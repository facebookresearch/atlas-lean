# Corollary 13.7

Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{F}_{q}$ with $q=p^{n}$. If $n$ is
 odd or $E$ is ordinary, then $\operatorname{End}^{0}(E)=\mathbb{Q}(\pi_{E})
 \simeq \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$ is an imaginary quadratic field with
 $D=(\operatorname{tr} \pi_{E})^{2}-4q$.
