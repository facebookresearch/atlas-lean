# Corollary 5.4

Let $\alpha$ be an isogeny of elliptic curves over a field $k$ of
 characteristic $p > 0$. Then $\alpha = \alpha_{\mathrm{sep}} \circ \pi^n$
 for some separable isogeny $\alpha_{\text{sep}}$ and integer $n \geq 0$,
 where $\pi$ is the $p$-power Frobenius morphism $(x:y:z) \mapsto
 (x^p:y^p:z^p)$. We then have $\deg \alpha = p^n \deg \alpha_{\text{sep}}$.
