# Theorem 5.11

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve and let $G$ be a finite subgroup of
 $E(\bar{k})$. There exists an elliptic curve $E'$ and a separable isogeny
 $\phi: E \rightarrow E'$ with $\ker \phi = G$. The curve $E'$ and the
 isogeny $\phi$ are defined over a finite extension of $k$ and unique up to
 isomorphism.
