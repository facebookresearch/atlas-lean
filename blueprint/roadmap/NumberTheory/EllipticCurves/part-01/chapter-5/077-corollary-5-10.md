# Corollary 5.10

For any composition of isogenies $\alpha = \beta \circ \gamma$ we have $\deg
 \alpha = (\deg \beta)(\deg \gamma)$, $\deg_s \alpha = (\deg_s \beta)(\deg_s
 \gamma)$, $\deg_i \alpha = (\deg_i \beta)(\deg_i \gamma)$.
