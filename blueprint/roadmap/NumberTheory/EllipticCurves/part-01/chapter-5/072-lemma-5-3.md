# Lemma 5.3

Let $\alpha: E_1 \rightarrow E_2$ be an inseparable isogeny of elliptic
 curves $E_1: y^2 = f_1(x)$ and $E_2: y^2 = f_2(x)$ over a field $k$ of
 characteristic $p > 0$. Then $\alpha$ can be written in the form $\alpha =
 (a(x^p), b(x^p) y^p)$ for some rational functions $a, b \in k(x)$.
