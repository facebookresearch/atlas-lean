# Definition 5.6

For an isogeny $\alpha = \alpha_{\text{sep}} \circ \pi^n$ decomposed as in
 Corollary 5.4, we define the separable degree $\deg_s \alpha$ and
 inseparable degree $\deg_i \alpha$ of $\alpha$ as $\deg_s \alpha \coloneqq
 \deg \alpha_{\text{sep}}$, $\deg_i \alpha = p^n$, and we always have $\deg
 \alpha = (\deg_s \alpha)(\deg_i \alpha)$.
