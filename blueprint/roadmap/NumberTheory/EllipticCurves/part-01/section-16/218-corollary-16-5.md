# Corollary 16.5

Let $L \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be a lattice and let $E \coloneqq E_L$. The maps
 $\alpha \mapsto \phi_\alpha$ and $\phi \mapsto \alpha_\phi$ are inverse ring
 isomorphisms between $\{\alpha \in \mathbb{C} \mid \alpha L \subseteq L\}$
 and $\operatorname{End}(E)$, the involution $\phi \mapsto \hat{\phi}$ of
 $\operatorname{End}(E)$ corresponds to complex conjugation $\alpha \mapsto
 \bar{\alpha}$ in $\{\alpha \in \mathbb{C} \mid \alpha L \subseteq L\}$, and
 we have $\mathrm{T}(\alpha) \coloneqq \alpha + \bar{\alpha} =
 \operatorname{tr} \phi_\alpha$ and $\mathrm{N}(\alpha) \coloneqq
 \alpha\bar{\alpha} = \operatorname{deg} \phi_\alpha = \operatorname{deg} u =
 \operatorname{deg} v + 1$, where $u, v \in \mathbb{C}[x]$ are as in (2) of
 Theorem 16.4.
