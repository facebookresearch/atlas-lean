# Theorem 16.1

Let $\varphi: \mathbb{C}/L_1 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}/L_2$ be a holomorphic map
 with $\varphi(0)=0$. There is a unique $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$ for which
 $\varphi=\varphi_\alpha$.
