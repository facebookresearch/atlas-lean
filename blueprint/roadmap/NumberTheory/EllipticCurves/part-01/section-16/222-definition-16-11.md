# Definition 16.11

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order in an imaginary quadratic field. The ideal
 class group $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ is the multiplicative group of
 equivalence classes of proper $\mathcal{O}$-ideals.
