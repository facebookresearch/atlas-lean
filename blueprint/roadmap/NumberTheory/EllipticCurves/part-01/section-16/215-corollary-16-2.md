# Corollary 16.2

For any two lattices $L_1, L_2 \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ the map $$\{\alpha \in
 \mathbb{C} \mid \alpha L_1 \subseteq L_2\} \rightarrow \{\text{morphisms }
 \varphi: \mathbb{C}/L_1 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}/L_2\}, \quad \alpha \mapsto
 \varphi_\alpha$$ is an isomorphism of additive groups. If $L_1=L_2$ it is an
 isomorphism of commutative rings.
