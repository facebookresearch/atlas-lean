# Theorem 16.12

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order in an imaginary quadratic field. There is a
 one-to-one correspondence between elements of the ideal class group
 $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{O})$ and homothety classes of lattices $L
 \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ for which $\operatorname{End}(E_L) \simeq
 \mathcal{O}$.
