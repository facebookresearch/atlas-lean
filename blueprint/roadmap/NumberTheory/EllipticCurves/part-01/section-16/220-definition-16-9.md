# Definition 16.9

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order in an imaginary quadratic field $K$, and let
 $L$ be an $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. We say that $L$ is a proper
 $\mathcal{O}$-ideal if $\mathcal{O}(L) = \mathcal{O}$.
