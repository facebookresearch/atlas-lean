# Theorem 24.11

The operators $T_{n}$ and $R_{\lambda}$ satisfy the following: (i) $T_{n}
 R_{\lambda}=R_{\lambda} T_{n}$ and $R_{\lambda} R_{\mu}=R_{\lambda\mu}$.
 (ii) $T_{mn}=T_{m} T_{n}$ for all $m \perp n$. (iii) $T_{p^{r+1}}=T_{p^{r}}
 T_{p}-p T_{p^{r-1}} R_{p}$ for all primes $p$ and integers $r \geq 1$.
