# Corollary 24.12

The subring of $\operatorname{End}(\operatorname{Div}(\mathcal{L}))$
 generated by $\{R_{p}, T_{p} \mid p \text{ prime}\}$ is commutative and
 contains all the Hecke operators $T_{n}$.
