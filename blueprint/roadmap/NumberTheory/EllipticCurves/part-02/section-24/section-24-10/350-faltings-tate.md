# Theorem 24.38 (Faltings-Tate)

Let $E$ and $E'$ be elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$ with $L$-functions
 $L(E, s) = \sum a_n n^{-s}$ and $L(E', s) = \sum a_n' n^{-s}$, respectively.
 If $a_p = a_p'$ for sufficiently many primes $p$ of good reduction for $E$
 and $E'$, then $E$ and $E'$ are isogenous.
