# Corollary 24.13

The Hecke operators $T_{n}$ for $M_{k}(\Gamma_{0}(1))$ satisfy $T_{mn}=T_{m}
 T_{n}$ for $m \perp n$ and $T_{p^{r+1}}=T_{p^{r}} T_{p}-p^{k-1} T_{p^{r-1}}$
 for $p$ prime.
