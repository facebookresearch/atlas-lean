# Theorem 24.14

For any $f \in S_{k}(\Gamma_{0}(1))$ and prime $p$ we have $$a_{n}(T_{p} f)=
 \begin{cases}a_{np}(f) & \text{if } p \nmid n, \\ a_{np}(f)+p^{k-1}
 a_{n/p}(f) & \text{if } p \mid n.\end{cases}$$
