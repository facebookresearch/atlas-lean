# Theorem 24.25 (Hecke)

Let $f \in S_{k}(\Gamma_{0}(N))$. The $L$-function $L(f, s)$ extends
 analytically to a holomorphic function on $\mathbb{C}$, and the normalized
 $L$-function $\tilde{L}_{f}(s)=N^{s/2}(2\pi)^{-s} \Gamma(s) L(f, s)$
 satisfies the functional equation $\tilde{L}_{f}(s)= \pm
 \tilde{L}_{f}(k-s)$.
