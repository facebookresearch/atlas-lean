# Theorem 24.27

Let $f \in S_{k}^{\text{new}}(\Gamma_{0}(N))$. The $L$-function $L(f, s)$ has
 the Euler product $L(f, s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}
 n^{-s}=\prod_{p}(1-a_{p} p^{-s}+\chi(p) p^{k-1} p^{-2s})^{-1}$ where
 $\chi(p)=0$ for $p \mid N$ and $\chi(p)=1$ otherwise.
