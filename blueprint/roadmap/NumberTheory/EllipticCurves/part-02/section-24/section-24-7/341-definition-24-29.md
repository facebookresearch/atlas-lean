# Definition 24.29

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve. A (global) minimal model for $E$ is
 an integral Weierstrass equation $y^{2}+a_{1} x y+a_{3} y=x^{3}+a_{2}
 x^{2}+a_{4} x+a_{6}$ with $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, a_{4}, a_{6} \in \mathbb{Z}$
 that defines an elliptic curve that is isomorphic to $E$ and whose
 discriminant $\Delta_{\min}(E)$ divides the discriminant of every integral
 Weierstrass equation for $E$.
