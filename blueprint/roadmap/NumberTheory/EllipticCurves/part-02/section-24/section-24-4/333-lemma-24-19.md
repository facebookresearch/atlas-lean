# Lemma 24.19

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{C}$-vector space equipped with a
 positive definite Hermitian form, and let $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \ldots$
 be a sequence of commuting Hermitian operators. Then $V=\bigoplus_{i}
 V_{i}$, where each $V_{i}$ is an eigenspace of every $\alpha_{n}$.
