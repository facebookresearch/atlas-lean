# Definition 24.18

The Petersson inner product on $S_{k}(\Gamma)$ is defined by $$\langle f,
 g\rangle=\int_{\mathcal{F}} f(\tau) \overline{g(\tau)} y^{k-2} dx\, dy$$
 where the integral ranges over points $\tau=x+yi$ in a fundamental region
 $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathbb{H}$ for $\Gamma$. It is a positive definite
 Hermitian form: it is bilinear in $f$ and $g$, it satisfies $\langle f,
 g\rangle=\overline{\langle g, f\rangle}$, and $\langle f, f\rangle \geq 0$
 with equality only when $f=0$. It thus defines an inner product on the
 $\mathbb{C}$-vector space $S_{k}(\Gamma)$.
