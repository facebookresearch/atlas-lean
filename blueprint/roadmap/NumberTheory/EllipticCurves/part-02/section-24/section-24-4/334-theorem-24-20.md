# Theorem 24.20

The space of cusp forms $S_{k}(\Gamma_{0}(1))$ is a direct sum of
 one-dimensional eigenspaces for the Hecke operators $T_{n}$ and has a unique
 basis of eigenforms $f(\tau)=\sum a_{n} q^{n}$, where each $a_{n}$ is the
 eigenvalue of $T_{n}$ on the one-dimensional subspace spanned by $f$.
