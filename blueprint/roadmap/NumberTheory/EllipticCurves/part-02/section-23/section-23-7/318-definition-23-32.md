# Definition 23.32

Let $E / K$ be an elliptic curve over a field $K$ and let $n$ be a positive
 integer. The embedding degree of $E$ with respect to $n$ is the degree of
 the minimal extension $L / K$ for which $E[n] \subseteq E(L)$.
