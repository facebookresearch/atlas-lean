# Corollary 23.27

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve with distinct points $P, Q \in E(k)[n]$, where
 $n > 1$ is prime to the characteristic of $k$. Then $$e_n(P, Q) = (-1)^n
 \frac{f_{n,P}(Q)}{f_{n,Q}(P)}.$$
