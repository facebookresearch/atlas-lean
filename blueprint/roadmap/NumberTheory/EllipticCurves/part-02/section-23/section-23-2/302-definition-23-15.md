# Definition 23.15

Let $C / k$ be a smooth projective curve with a rational point $0 \in C(k)$.
 The Abel-Jacobi map is the map $C(k) \rightarrow \operatorname{Pic}^{0} C$
 defined by $P \mapsto [P]-[0]$.
