# Lemma 23.21

The value of the Weil pairing $e_{n}(D_{1}, D_{2}) \in k^{\times}$ depends
 only on the divisor classes of $D_{1}$ and $D_{2}$ and is an element of
 $\mu_{n} \subseteq k^{\times}$.
