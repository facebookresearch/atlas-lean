# Theorem 23.22

Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $k$ be an algebraically closed field whose characteristic does not divide $n$, and let $C/k$ be a smooth projective curve. Let $D_1, D_2, D_3$ denote divisors with disjoint support that represent $n$-torsion elements of $\operatorname{Pic}^0 C$. The Weil pairing $e_n: (\operatorname{Pic}^0 C)[n] \times (\operatorname{Pic}^0 C)[n] \to \mu_n$ satisfies:
- Bilinear: $e_n(D_1 + D_2, D_3) = e_n(D_1, D_3) e_n(D_2, D_3)$;
- Alternating: $e_n(D_1, D_2) = e_n(D_2, D_1)^{-1}$.
