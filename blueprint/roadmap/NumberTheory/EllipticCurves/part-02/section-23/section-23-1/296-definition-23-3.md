# Definition 23.3

For any point $P \in C(\bar{k})$, we define the local ring at $P$ (or the
 ring of regular functions at $P$) by $\mathcal{O}_{P}\coloneqq\{f \in k(C)
 \mid f(P) \neq \infty\} \subseteq k(C)$ where $\infty=(1:0) \in
 \mathbb{P}^{1}$. It is a principal ideal domain (PID) with a unique maximal
 ideal $\mathfrak{m}_{P}\coloneqq\{f \in \mathcal{O}_{P} \mid f(P)=0\}$. Any
 generator $u_{P}$ for the principal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_{P}=(u_{P})$ is
 called a uniformizer at $P$.
