# Theorem 23.9

Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field $k$
 and let $f \in k(C)^{\times}$ be an element of its function field (viewed as
 a morphism $f: C \rightarrow \mathbb{P}^{1}$). For every point $Q \in
 \mathbb{P}^{1}(k)$ we have $\operatorname{deg} f = \sum_{f(P)=Q} v_{P}(u_{Q}
 \circ f)$ where $u_{Q} \in k(\mathbb{P}^{1})$ denotes any uniformizer for
 $m_{Q}$.
