# Definition 22.10

An $\ell$-volcano $V$ is a connected undirected graph whose vertices are
 partitioned into one or more levels $V_0, \ldots, V_d$ such that the
 following hold: 1. The subgraph on $V_0$ (the surface) is a regular graph of
 degree at most 2. 2. For $i > 0$, each vertex in $V_i$ has exactly one
 neighbor in level $V_{i-1}$, and this accounts for every edge not on the
 surface. 3. For $i < d$, each vertex in $V_i$ has degree $\ell + 1$. Level
 $V_d$ is called the floor of the volcano; the floor and surface coincide
 when $d = 0$.
