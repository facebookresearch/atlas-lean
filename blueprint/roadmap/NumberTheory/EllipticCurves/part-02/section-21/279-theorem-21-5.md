# Theorem 21.5

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an imaginary quadratic order with discriminant $D$ and
 ring class field $L$. Let $p \nmid D$ be an odd prime unramified in $L$. The
 following are equivalent: (i) $p$ is the norm of a principal
 $\mathcal{O}$-ideal; (ii) $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=1$ and $H_{D}(X)$ splits
 into linear factors in $\mathbb{F}_{p}[X]$; (iii) $p$ splits completely in
 $L$; (iv) $4p=t^{2}-v^{2}D$ for some integers $t$ and $v$ with $t \not\equiv
 0 \bmod p$.
