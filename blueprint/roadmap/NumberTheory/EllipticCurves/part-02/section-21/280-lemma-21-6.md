# Lemma 21.6

Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field of discriminant $D$ with ring of
 integers $\mathcal{O}_{K}=[1, \omega]$ and let $p$ be prime. Every
 $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideal of norm $p$ is of the form $\mathfrak{p}=[p,
 \omega-r]$, where $r \in \mathbb{Z}$ is a root of the minimal polynomial of
 $\omega$ modulo $p$. The number of such ideals $\mathfrak{p}$ is
 $1+\left(\frac{D}{p}\right) \in \{0,1,2\}$ and the factorization of the
 principal $\mathcal{O}_{K}$-ideal into prime ideals is
 $(p)=\mathfrak{p}\overline{\mathfrak{p}}$ if $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=1$,
 $(p)=\mathfrak{p}^{2}$ if $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=0$, $(p)=(p)$ if
 $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=-1$.
