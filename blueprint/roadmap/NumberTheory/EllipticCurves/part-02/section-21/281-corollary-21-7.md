# Corollary 21.7

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an order of discriminant $D$ in an imaginary quadratic
 field $K$, and let $p$ be a prime. When $p$ divides the conductor
 $[\mathcal{O}: \mathcal{O}_{K}]$ there are no proper $\mathcal{O}$-ideals of
 norm $p$ and otherwise there are $1+\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=0,1,2$,
 depending on whether $p$ is inert, ramified, or split in $K$, respectively.
