# Definition 21.4

Let $p$ be a prime and $D$ an integer. For $p>2$ the Kronecker symbol is
 $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)\coloneqq\#\{x \in \mathbb{F}_{p} \mid
 x^{2}=D\}-1$. For $p=2$, we define $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)$ to be 1 for $D
 \equiv \pm 1 \bmod 8$, zero if $p \mid D$, and -1 for $D \equiv \pm 3 \bmod
 8$.
