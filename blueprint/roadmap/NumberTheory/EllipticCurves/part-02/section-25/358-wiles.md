# Theorem 25.7 (Wiles)

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be a semistable elliptic curve for which
 $\bar{\rho}_{E,5}$ is irreducible. There exists a semistable elliptic curve
 $E'/\mathbb{Q}$ such that $\bar{\rho}_{E',3}$ is irreducible and
 $\bar{\rho}_{E',5} \simeq \bar{\rho}_{E,5}$.
