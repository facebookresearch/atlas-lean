# Theorem 25.2 (Ribet)

Let $\ell$ be prime, let $E$ be an elliptic curve of conductor $N=mN'$, where
 $m$ is the product of all primes $p \mid N$ such that $v_p(N)=1$ and
 $v_p(\Delta_{\min}(E)) \equiv 0 \bmod \ell$. If $E$ is modular and
 $\bar{\rho}_{E,\ell}$ is irreducible, then $\bar{\rho}_{E,\ell}$ is modular
 of weight 2 and level $N'$.
