# Conjecture 25.1 (Serre's modularity conjecture)

Every odd irreducible Galois representation $\bar{\rho}: G_{\mathbb{Q}}
 \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z} / \ell \mathbb{Z})$ is modular.
