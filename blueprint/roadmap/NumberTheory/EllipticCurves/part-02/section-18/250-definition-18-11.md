# Definition 18.11

The principal congruence subgroup $\Gamma(N)$ is defined by
 $\Gamma(N)=\left\{\left(\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right)
 \in \operatorname{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}) \mid\left(\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ c
 & d\end{array}\right) \equiv\left(\begin{array}{ll}1 & 0 \\ 0 &
 1\end{array}\right) \bmod N\right\}$. A congruence subgroup (of level $N$)
 is any subgroup of $\operatorname{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})$ that contains
 $\Gamma(N)$. A modular curve is a quotient of $\mathbb{H}^{*}$ or
 $\mathbb{H}$ by a congruence subgroup.
