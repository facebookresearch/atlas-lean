# Lemma 18.2

For any $\tau_{1}, \tau_{2} \in \mathbb{H}^{*}$ there exist open
 neighborhoods $U_{1}$, $U_{2}$ of $\tau_{1}, \tau_{2}$ such that $\gamma
 U_{1} \cap U_{2} \neq \emptyset \Longleftrightarrow \gamma
 \tau_{1}=\tau_{2}$, for all $\gamma \in \Gamma$. In particular, each $\tau
 \in \mathbb{H}^{*}$ has an open neighborhood in which it is the sole
 representative of its $\Gamma$-orbit.
