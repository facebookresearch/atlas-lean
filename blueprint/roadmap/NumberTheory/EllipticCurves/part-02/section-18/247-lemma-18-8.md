# Lemma 18.8

Let $\tau_{x} \in \mathbb{H}$, with $\delta_{x}(\tau)$ as in (1), and let
 $\varphi: \mathbb{H} \rightarrow \mathbb{H}$ be a holomorphic function
 fixing $\tau_{x}$ whose $n$-fold composition with itself is the identity,
 with $n$ minimal. Then for some primitive $n$th root of unity $\zeta$, we
 have $\delta_{x}(\varphi(\tau))=\zeta \delta_{x}(\tau)$ for all $\tau \in
 \mathbb{H}$.
