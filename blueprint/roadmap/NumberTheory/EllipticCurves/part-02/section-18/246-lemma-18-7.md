# Lemma 18.7

For $\tau \in \mathcal{F}^{*}$, let $G_{\tau}$ denote the stabilizer of
 $\tau$ in $\Gamma=\operatorname{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})$. Let
 $S=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and
 $T=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Then
 $G_{\tau}=\{\pm I\} \simeq \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ if $\tau \notin \{i,
 \rho, \infty\}$; $G_{\tau}=\langle S\rangle \simeq \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$
 if $\tau=i$; $G_{\tau}=\langle ST\rangle \simeq \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ if
 $\tau=\rho$; $G_{\tau}=\langle \pm T\rangle \simeq \mathbb{Z}$ if
 $\tau=\infty$.
