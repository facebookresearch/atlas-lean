# Lemma 19.16

For prime $N$ we can write the right cosets of $\Gamma_0(N)$ in $\Gamma(1)$
 as $\{\Gamma_0(N)\} \cup \{\Gamma_0(N) S T^k \mid 0 \leq k < N\}$ where $S =
 \left(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $T =
 \left(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
