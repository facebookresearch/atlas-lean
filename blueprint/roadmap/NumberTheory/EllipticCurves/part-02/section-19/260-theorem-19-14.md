# Theorem 19.14

The field of modular functions for $\Gamma_{0}(N)$ is an extension of
 $\mathbb{C}(j)$ of degree $n\coloneqq[\Gamma(1): \Gamma_{0}(N)]$ generated
 by $j_{N}(\tau)$.
