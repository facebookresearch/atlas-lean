# Theorem 19.11

Let $\Gamma$ be a congruence subgroup. The field $\mathbb{C}(\Gamma)$ of
 modular functions for $\Gamma$ is a finite extension of $\mathbb{C}(j)$ of
 degree at most $n\coloneqq[\Gamma(1): \Gamma]$.
