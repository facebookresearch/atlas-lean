# Lemma 19.5

Let $\sigma_{k}(n)=\sum_{d \mid n} d^{k}$, and let $q=e^{2 \pi i \tau}$. We
 have $$g_{2}(\tau)=\frac{4 \pi^{4}}{3}\left(1+240 \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 \sigma_{3}(n) q^{n}\right)$$ $$g_{3}(\tau)=\frac{8 \pi^{6}}{27}\left(1-504
 \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sigma_{5}(n) q^{n}\right)$$
 $$\Delta(\tau)=g_{2}(\tau)^{3}-27 g_{3}(\tau)^{2}=(2 \pi)^{12} q
 \prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1-q^{n}\right)^{24}$$
