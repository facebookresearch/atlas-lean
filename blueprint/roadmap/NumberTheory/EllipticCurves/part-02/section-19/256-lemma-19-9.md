# Lemma 19.9

Every meromorphic function $f: \mathcal{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ on the
 Riemann sphere $\mathcal{S}\coloneqq\mathbb{P}^{1}(\mathbb{C})$ is a
 rational function.
