# Theorem 20.4

Let $N>1$ be an integer and let $k$ be a field of characteristic not dividing
 $N$. For all $j_{1}, j_{2} \in k$ we have $\Phi_{N}(j_{1}, j_{2})=0$ if and
 only if $j_{1}$ and $j_{2}$ are the $j$-invariants of elliptic curves over
 $k$ that are related by a cyclic isogeny of degree $N$ defined over $k$.
