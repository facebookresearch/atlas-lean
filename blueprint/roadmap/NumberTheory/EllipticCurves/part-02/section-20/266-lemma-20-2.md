# Lemma 20.2

Let $L=[1, \tau]$ be a lattice with $\tau \in \mathbb{H}$ and let $N$ be
 prime. The cyclic sublattices of $L$ of index $N$ are the lattice $[1,
 N\tau]$ and the lattices $[N, \tau+k]$, for $0 \leq k < N$.
