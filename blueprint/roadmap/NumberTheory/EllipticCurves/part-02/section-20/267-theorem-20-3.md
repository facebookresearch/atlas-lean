# Theorem 20.3

For all $j_{1}, j_{2} \in \mathbb{C}$, we have $\Phi_{N}(j_{1}, j_{2})=0$ if
 and only if $j_{1}$ and $j_{2}$ are the $j$-invariants of elliptic curves
 over $\mathbb{C}$ that are related by a cyclic isogeny of degree $N$.
