# Markoff graph modulo an integer

Two distinct nonzero Markoff triples over `ZMod p`
are adjacent when one is obtained from the other by replacing a single coordinate $x_i$
with $3x_jx_k-x_i$, where $j$ and $k$ are the other two indices.

## Sources

- Daniel E. Martin, *A new proof of Chen's theorem for Markoff graphs*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15960.
