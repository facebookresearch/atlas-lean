# Amato's product of $(3, 4, 5)$ with itself

For integer triples,
$(3, 4, 5) \star (3, 4, 5) = (9, 40, 41)$, where $\star$ is Amato's product of triples.

## Sources

- Roberto Amato, *Groups and Monoid in the Set of Pythagorean Triples*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A5, Lemma 1, (4) (`integers-2024-A5-lem-1`).
