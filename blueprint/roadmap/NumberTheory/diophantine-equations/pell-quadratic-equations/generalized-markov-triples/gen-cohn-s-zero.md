# The matrix $S$ vanishes for $k = 0$

For $k = 0$, the matrix $S$ of the generalized
Cohn triple relation is the zero $2 \times 2$ matrix, so the relation $Q = PR - S$ reduces to
the classical Cohn relation $Q = PR$.
