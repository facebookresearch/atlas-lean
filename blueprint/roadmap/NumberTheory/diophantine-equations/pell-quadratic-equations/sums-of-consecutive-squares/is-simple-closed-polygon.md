# Non-self-intersecting closed polygons

The closed polygon with vertices $v_0, \dots, v_{N-1}$, side $i$ joining $v_i$ to $v_{i+1}$, is
non-self-intersecting when any two distinct sides $i \ne j$ (with $i, j < N$) meet at most in a
common endpoint: every point of both segments is an endpoint of each of them.

## Sources

- Anderson, Woodall and Zaharescu, *Arithmetic Polygons and Sums of Consecutive Squares*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A1, Section 3 (Remark 3 and Theorem 6).
