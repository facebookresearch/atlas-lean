# Non-self-intersecting closed polygons

For any sequence $v_0, v_1, \dots$ of points in the plane, the closed polygon with vertices
$v_0, \dots, v_{N-1}$, side $i$ joining $v_i$ to $v_{i+1}$ (the last side, $i = N - 1$, ends at $v_N$,
which is $v_0$ for the periodic sequences used here), is non-self-intersecting when any two distinct
sides $i \ne j$ (with $i, j < N$) meet at most in a common endpoint: every point of both segments is
an endpoint of each of them.

## Sources

- Anderson, Woodall and Zaharescu, *Arithmetic Polygons and Sums of Consecutive Squares*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A1, Section 3 (Remark 3 and Theorem 6).
