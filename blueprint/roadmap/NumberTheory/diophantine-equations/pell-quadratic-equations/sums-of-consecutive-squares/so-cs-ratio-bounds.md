# Bounds on the ratio of the two spans

For every solution $(a, b, c)$ of the sum of
consecutive squares problem, $1 < (b - a)/(c - b) < 1 + 2^{1/3} + 2^{2/3}$.

## Sources

- Anderson, Woodall and Zaharescu, *Arithmetic Polygons and Sums of Consecutive Squares*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A1, Lemma 1 (`integers-2026-A1-lem-1`).
