# The Pell-type parameter is a positive nonsquare

For every solution $(a, b, c)$ of the
sum of consecutive squares problem, the integer
$A = 3(b - c)^2\bigl(12(b - a)^2(c - b)^2 - (a - 2b + c)^4\bigr)$ is positive and is not a
perfect square.
