# $|x^2 - d y^2|$ is never $2 \bmod 4$ when $d \equiv 1 \pmod 4$

Let $d$ and $z$ be integers
with $d \equiv 1 \pmod 4$ and $z \equiv 2 \pmod 4$. Then $|x^2 - d y^2| \ne z$ for all integers $x$
and $y$.
