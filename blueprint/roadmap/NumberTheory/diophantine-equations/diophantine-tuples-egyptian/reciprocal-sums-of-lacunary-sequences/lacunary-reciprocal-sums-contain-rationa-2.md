# Lacunary sequences whose reciprocal sums represent all rationals in an interval

There are a lacunary sequence $a$ of positive integers and reals $u < v$ such that every rational $q$ with $u < q < v$ is $\sum_{m \in S} 1/m$ for some finite set $S$ of terms of $a$; here a sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots$ of natural numbers is lacunary when all terms are positive and $a_{n+1} \ge c\, a_n$ for all $n$, for some $c > 1$.
