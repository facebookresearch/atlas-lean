# Worst least largest denominator for a given denominator

For a natural number $b$, $D(b) = \max_{1 \le a < b} D(a, b)$ ($0$ for $b \le 1$), where for natural numbers $a$ and $b$, $D(a, b)$ is the least $N$ such that $a/b = 1/n_1 + \dots + 1/n_k$ for distinct positive integers $n_1 < \dots < n_k \le N$ (the least possible largest denominator; $0$ if there is no such $N$).
