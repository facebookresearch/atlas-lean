# Erdős Problem 292 (largest denominators of Egyptian fractions for one)

The statement that $|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}|/N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$, where $A$ is the set of largest unit denominators: natural numbers $n$ with $1 = 1/m_1 + \dots + 1/m_k$ for some integers $1 \le m_1 < \dots < m_k = n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Almost every integer is the largest denominator of an Egyptian fraction for one](largest-unit-denominators-density-one-ho.md).
