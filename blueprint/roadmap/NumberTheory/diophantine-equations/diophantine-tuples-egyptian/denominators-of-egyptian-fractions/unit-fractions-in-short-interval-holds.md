# Egyptian fractions for one with denominators in a short interval

For every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $k$ there are integers $0 < n_1 < \dots < n_k$ with $1 = 1/n_1 + \dots + 1/n_k$ and $n_k - n_1 \le (e - 1 + \varepsilon) k$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 286 (Egyptian fractions for one in short intervals)](unit-fractions-in-short-interval.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Croot III (2001), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- E. S. Croot III, On unit fractions with denominators in short intervals, Acta Arith. (2001), 99–114.
