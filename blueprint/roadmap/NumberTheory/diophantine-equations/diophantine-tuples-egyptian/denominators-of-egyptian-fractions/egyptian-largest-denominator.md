# Least largest denominator of an Egyptian fraction

For natural numbers $a$ and $b$, $D(a, b)$ is the least $N$ such that $a/b = 1/n_1 + \dots + 1/n_k$ for distinct positive integers $n_1 < \dots < n_k \le N$ (the least possible largest denominator; $0$ if there is no such $N$). When $a = 0$ or $b = 0$ the quotient $a/b$ is $0$ (with $a/0 = 0$), the empty sum represents it, and $D(a, b) = 0$.
