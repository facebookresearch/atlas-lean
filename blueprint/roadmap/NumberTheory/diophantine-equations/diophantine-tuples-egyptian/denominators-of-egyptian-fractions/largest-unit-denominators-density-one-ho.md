# Almost every integer is the largest denominator of an Egyptian fraction for one

$|A \cap \{1, \dots, N\}|/N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$, where $A$ is the set of largest unit denominators: natural numbers $n$ with $1 = 1/m_1 + \dots + 1/m_k$ for some integers $1 \le m_1 < \dots < m_k = n$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 292 (largest denominators of Egyptian fractions for one)](largest-unit-denominators-density-one.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Martin (2000), cited under Sources.
