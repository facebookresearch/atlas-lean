# Asymptotics of the least largest denominator of a k-term Egyptian fraction for one

$f(k) \big/ \bigl(\tfrac{e}{e-1} k\bigr) \to 1$ as $k \to \infty$, where for a natural number $k$, $f(k)$ is the least $n$ such that $1 = 1/n_1 + \dots + 1/n_k$ with integers $0 < n_1 < \dots < n_k = n$ ($0$ if $1$ has no such $k$-term representation).

This resolves [Erdős Problem 285 (least largest denominator of an Egyptian fraction for one)](min-largest-denominator-asymptotic.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Martin (2000), cited under Sources.
