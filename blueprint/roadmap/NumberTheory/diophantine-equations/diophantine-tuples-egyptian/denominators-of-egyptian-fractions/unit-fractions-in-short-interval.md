# Erdős Problem 286 (Egyptian fractions for one in short intervals)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $k$ there are integers $0 < n_1 < \dots < n_k$ with $1 = 1/n_1 + \dots + 1/n_k$ and $n_k - n_1 \le (e - 1 + \varepsilon) k$.
