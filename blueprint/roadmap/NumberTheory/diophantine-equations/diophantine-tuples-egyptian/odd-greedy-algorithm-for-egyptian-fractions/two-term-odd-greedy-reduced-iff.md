# Reduced fractions with a two-term odd greedy expansion

For coprime natural numbers $a$ and $b$, the fraction $a/b$ has a two-term odd greedy expansion if and only if $a$ is even and $b = a\,(\prod_{p \mid r} p^{\lceil \nu_p(r)/2 \rceil})(1 + 2t) - r$ for some positive integer $r < 2a$ coprime to $2a$ and some natural number $t$, where $\nu_p$ is the $p$-adic valuation; here the odd greedy choice at a positive rational $r$ is the least positive odd integer $d$ with $1/d \le r$, and $q$ has a two-term odd greedy expansion when, with $x_1$ the odd greedy choice at $q$ and $x_2$ the odd greedy choice at $q - 1/x_1$, $q = 1/x_1 + 1/x_2$.
