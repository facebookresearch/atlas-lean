# The least integer not a sum of distinct unit fractions is near the harmonic sum

For all sufficiently large $N$, the least positive integer that is not a sum of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ is $m_N$ or $m_N + 1$, where $m_N = \lfloor \sum_{n \le N} 1/n \rfloor$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 308, first question](least-non-representable-near-harmonic.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Croot III (1999), cited under Sources.
