# Integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions form an initial segment

For all sufficiently large $N$, the positive integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ form an initial segment $\{1, \dots, m\}$.

Problem 308 is posed for every $N \ge 1$; this is the version for all sufficiently large $N$, which Croot's bounds give. The version for every $N$ is not part of this statement.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 308, second question, for all large N](representable-integers-initial-segment.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Croot III (1999), cited under Sources.
