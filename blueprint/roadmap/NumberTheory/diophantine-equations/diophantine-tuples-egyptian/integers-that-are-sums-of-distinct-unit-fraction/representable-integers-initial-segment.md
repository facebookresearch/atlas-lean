# Erdős Problem 308, second question, for all large N

The statement that for all sufficiently large $N$, the positive integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ form an initial segment $\{1, \dots, m\}$.

Problem 308 is posed for every $N \ge 1$; this is the version for all sufficiently large $N$, which Croot's bounds give. The version for every $N$ is not part of this statement.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions form an initial segment](representable-integers-initial-segment-h.md).
