# Erdős Problem 309, first question

The statement that $F(N)/\log N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$, where $F(N)$ is the number of positive integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Asymptotic count of integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions](representable-count-asymptotic-holds.md).
