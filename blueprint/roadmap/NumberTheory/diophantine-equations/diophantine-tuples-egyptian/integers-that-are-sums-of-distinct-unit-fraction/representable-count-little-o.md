# Erdős Problem 309, second question

The statement that $F(N) = o(\log N)$ as $N \to \infty$, where $F(N)$ is the number of positive integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Integers that are sums of distinct unit fractions are not o(log N) in number](representable-count-little-o-refuted.md).
