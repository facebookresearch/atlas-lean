# Erdős Problem 308, first question, for all large N

The statement that for all sufficiently large $N$, the least positive integer that is not a sum of distinct unit fractions $1/n$ with $n \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ is $m_N$ or $m_N + 1$, where $m_N = \lfloor \sum_{n \le N} 1/n \rfloor$.

Problem 308 is posed for every $N \ge 1$; this is the version for all sufficiently large $N$, which Croot's bounds give. The version for every $N$ is not part of this statement.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [The least integer not a sum of distinct unit fractions is near the harmonic sum](least-non-representable-near-harmonic-ho.md).
