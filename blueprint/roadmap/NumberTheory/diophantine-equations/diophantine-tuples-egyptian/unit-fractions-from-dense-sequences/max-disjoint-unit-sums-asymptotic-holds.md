# Number of disjoint unit sums in an initial segment

$k(N)/\log N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$, where $k(N)$ is the largest $k$ for which there are $k$ pairwise disjoint subsets of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ each with reciprocal sum $1$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 296, estimate](max-disjoint-unit-sums-asymptotic.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #296, follows from T. F. Bloom's density theorem (2021), applied greedily as Hunter and Sawhney observed.
