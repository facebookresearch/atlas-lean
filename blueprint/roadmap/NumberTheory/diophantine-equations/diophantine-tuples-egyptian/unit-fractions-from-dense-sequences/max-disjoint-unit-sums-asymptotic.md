# Erdős Problem 296, estimate

The statement that $k(N)/\log N \to 1$ as $N \to \infty$, where $k(N)$ is the largest $k$ for which there are $k$ pairwise disjoint subsets of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ each with reciprocal sum $1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Number of disjoint unit sums in an initial segment](max-disjoint-unit-sums-asymptotic-holds.md).
