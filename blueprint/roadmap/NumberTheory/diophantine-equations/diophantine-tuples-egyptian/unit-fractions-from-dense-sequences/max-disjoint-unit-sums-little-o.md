# Erdős Problem 296, question

The statement that $k(N) = o(\log N)$, where $k(N)$ is the largest $k$ for which there are $k$ pairwise disjoint subsets of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ each with reciprocal sum $1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [The number of disjoint unit sums is not o(log N)](max-disjoint-unit-sums-little-o-refuted.md).
