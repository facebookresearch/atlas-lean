# Erdős Problem 206 (eventually greedy best Egyptian underapproximations)

The statement that the set of reals $x > 0$ with eventually greedy best Egyptian underapproximations has positive Lebesgue measure, where a real $x$ has eventually greedy best underapproximations when there are positive integers $m_0 < m_1 < \cdots$ and $n_0$ such that $\sum_{k<n} 1/m_k$ is the best $n$-term Egyptian underapproximation of $x$ for every $n \ge n_0$ and the best $n$-term Egyptian underapproximation of $x$ is the largest sum of $n$ distinct unit fractions below $x$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Eventually greedy best Egyptian underapproximations are exceptional](eventually-greedy-on-positive-measure-re.md).
