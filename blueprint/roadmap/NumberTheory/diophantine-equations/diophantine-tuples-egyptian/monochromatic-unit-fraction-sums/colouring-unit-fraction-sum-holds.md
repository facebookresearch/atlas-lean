# Every finite colouring has a monochromatic Egyptian fraction for one

For every colouring of the natural numbers with finitely many colours there is a finite set $S$ of integers $n \ge 2$, all of one colour, with $\sum_{n \in S} 1/n = 1$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 46 (monochromatic Egyptian fractions for one)](colouring-unit-fraction-sum.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Croot III (2003), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #46, https://www.erdosproblems.com/46.
- E. S. Croot III, On a coloring conjecture about unit fractions, Ann. of Math. (2) (2003), 545–556.
