# Monochromatic unit fraction sums among the divisors of a single integer

For every $k \ge 2$ there is a natural number $n$ such that for every colouring of the divisors $d$ of $n$ with $1 < d < n$ by $k$ colours, some set $D$ of such divisors, all of one colour, has $\sum_{d \in D} 1/d = 1$.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #45, https://www.erdosproblems.com/45.
- E. S. Croot III, On a coloring conjecture about unit fractions, Ann. of Math. (2) (2003), 545–556.
