# Erdős Problem 45 (monochromatic unit fraction sums among divisors)

The statement that for every $k \ge 2$ there is a natural number $n$ such that for every colouring of the divisors $d$ of $n$ with $1 < d < n$ by $k$ colours, some set $D$ of such divisors, all of one colour, has $\sum_{d \in D} 1/d = 1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Monochromatic unit fraction sums among the divisors of a single integer](divisor-colouring-unit-fraction-sum-hold.md).
