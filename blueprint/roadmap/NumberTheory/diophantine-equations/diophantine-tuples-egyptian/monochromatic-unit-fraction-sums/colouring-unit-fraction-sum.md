# Erdős Problem 46 (monochromatic Egyptian fractions for one)

The statement that for every colouring of the natural numbers with finitely many colours there is a finite set $S$ of integers $n \ge 2$, all of one colour, with $\sum_{n \in S} 1/n = 1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Every finite colouring has a monochromatic Egyptian fraction for one](colouring-unit-fraction-sum-holds.md).
