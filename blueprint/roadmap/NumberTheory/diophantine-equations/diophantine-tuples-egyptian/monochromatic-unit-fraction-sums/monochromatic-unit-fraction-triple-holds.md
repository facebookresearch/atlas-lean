# Monochromatic solutions of 1/a = 1/b + 1/c

For every colouring of the positive integers with finitely many colours there are distinct positive integers $a, b, c$ of the same colour with $1/a = 1/b + 1/c$.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #303, https://www.erdosproblems.com/303.
- T. C. Brown and V. Rödl, Monochromatic solutions to equations with unit fractions, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. (1991), 387–392.
