# Iterated logarithm scale

For natural numbers $k$ and $N$, the scale is $\dfrac{N}{\log N} \prod_{j=3}^{k} \log_j N$, where $\log_j$ is the $j$-fold iterated natural logarithm, taken as the real logarithm with $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$. For $N \le 1$, $\log N = 0$ and the value is $0$ by $x/0 = 0$.
