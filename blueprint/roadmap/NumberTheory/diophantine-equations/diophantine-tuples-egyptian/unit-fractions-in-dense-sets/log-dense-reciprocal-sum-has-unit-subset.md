# Erdős Problem 47 (unit fractions in sets with large reciprocal sum)

The statement that for every $\delta > 0$ there is $N_0$ such that for every $N \ge N_0$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ with $\sum_{a \in A} 1/a > \delta\log N$ has a subset $S$ with $\sum_{n \in S} 1/n = 1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Sets with large reciprocal sum contain an Egyptian fraction for one](log-dense-reciprocal-sum-has-unit-subset-2.md).
