# Sets with large reciprocal sum contain an Egyptian fraction for one

For every $\delta > 0$ there is $N_0$ such that for every $N \ge N_0$, every set $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ with $\sum_{a \in A} 1/a > \delta\log N$ has a subset $S$ with $\sum_{n \in S} 1/n = 1$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 47 (unit fractions in sets with large reciprocal sum)](log-dense-reciprocal-sum-has-unit-subset.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Bloom (2021), cited under Sources.
