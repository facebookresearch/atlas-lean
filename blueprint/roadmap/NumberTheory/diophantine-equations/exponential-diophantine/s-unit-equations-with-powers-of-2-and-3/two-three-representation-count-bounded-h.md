# Bounded number of representations n = 2^a + 3^b + 2^c 3^d

There is a constant $C$ such that for every $n$, the equation $n = 2^a + 3^b + 2^c 3^d$ has at most $C$ solutions $(a, b, c, d)$ in natural numbers.
