# Erdős Problem 407 (representations n = 2^a + 3^b + 2^c 3^d)

The statement that there is a constant $C$ such that for every $n$, the equation $n = 2^a + 3^b + 2^c 3^d$ has at most $C$ solutions $(a, b, c, d)$ in natural numbers.
