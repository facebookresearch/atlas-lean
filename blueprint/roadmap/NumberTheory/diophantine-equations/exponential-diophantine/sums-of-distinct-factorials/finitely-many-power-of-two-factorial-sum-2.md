# Finitely many powers of two are sums of distinct factorials

Only finitely many pairs $(m, S)$, with $m$ a natural number and $S$ a finite set of positive integers, satisfy $2^m = \sum_{a \in S} a!$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 403 (powers of two that are sums of distinct factorials)](finitely-many-power-of-two-factorial-sum.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #403, is due to S. Lin and, independently, P. Frankl.
