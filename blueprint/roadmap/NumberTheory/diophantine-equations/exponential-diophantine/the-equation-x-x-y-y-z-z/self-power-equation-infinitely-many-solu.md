# Erdős Problem 674 (the equation x^x y^y = z^z)

The statement that the equation $x^x y^y = z^z$ has infinitely many solutions in integers $x, y, z > 1$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Infinitely many solutions of x^x y^y = z^z](self-power-equation-infinitely-many-solu-2.md).
