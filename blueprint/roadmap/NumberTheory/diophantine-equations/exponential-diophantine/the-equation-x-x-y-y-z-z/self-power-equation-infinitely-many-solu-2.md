# Infinitely many solutions of x^x y^y = z^z

The equation $x^x y^y = z^z$ has infinitely many solutions in integers $x, y, z > 1$. For example $x = 2^{12}3^6$, $y = 2^8 3^8$, $z = 2^{11}3^7$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 674 (the equation x^x y^y = z^z)](self-power-equation-infinitely-many-solu.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Ko (1940), cited under Sources.
