# Solutions of $2x^2 + 1 = 3^y$

For natural numbers $x, y$, we have $2x^2 + 1 = 3^y$ if and
only if $(x, y)$ is $(0, 0)$, $(1, 1)$, $(2, 2)$ or $(11, 5)$.
