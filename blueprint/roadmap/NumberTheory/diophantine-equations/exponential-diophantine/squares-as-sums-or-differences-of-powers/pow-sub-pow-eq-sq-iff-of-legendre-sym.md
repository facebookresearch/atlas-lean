# Solutions of (p + a)^x − p^y = z² under a Legendre symbol condition

Let $p \ne 3$ be a prime with $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, and $a$ a natural number with $p + a$ prime, $a \equiv 2 \pmod 4$, $a \equiv \pm 1 \pmod p$ and $\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) = -1$. Then for natural numbers $x, y, z$, $(p + a)^x = p^y + z^2$ if and only if $(x, y, z) = (0, 0, 0)$, or $x = 1$, $y = 0$ and $z^2 = p + a - 1$.

## Sources

- S. Choudhury and N. K. Sarma, On the Diophantine equations $(p+a)^x + p^y = z^2$ and $(p+a)^x - p^y = z^2$ where $p$ and $p+a$ are odd primes, Integers 26 (2026), Article A87, Theorem 8.
