# No solutions of p^x + (p + a)^y = z² under a Legendre symbol condition

Let $p > 3$ be a prime and $a$ a natural number with $p + a$ prime, $a \equiv 2 \pmod 4$, $p \mid a + 1$ and $\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) = -1$. Then $p^x + (p + a)^y \ne z^2$ for all natural numbers $x, y \ge 1$ and $z$.

## Sources

- S. Choudhury and N. K. Sarma, On the Diophantine equations $(p+a)^x + p^y = z^2$ and $(p+a)^x - p^y = z^2$ where $p$ and $p+a$ are odd primes, Integers 26 (2026), Article A87, Theorem 7.
