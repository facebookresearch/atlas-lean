# Prime powers one less than a square

Let $p$ be a prime. Then non-negative integers $x$
and $y$ satisfy $p^x + 1 = y^2$ if and only if $(p, x, y) = (2, 3, 3)$ or $(p, x, y) = (3, 1, 2)$.
