# Finitely many solutions of (p − 1)! + a^(p − 1) = p^k

Only finitely many triples $(a, k, p)$ of natural numbers with $p$ an odd prime satisfy $(p - 1)! + a^{p-1} = p^k$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 405 (the equation (p − 1)! + a^(p − 1) = p^k)](finitely-many-factorial-power-solutions.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is the published work cited under Sources.

## Sources

- B. Brindza and P. Erdős, On some Diophantine problems involving powers and factorials, J. Austral. Math. Soc. Ser. A (1991), 1–7.
- K. Yu and D. Liu, A complete resolution of a problem of Erdős and Graham, Rocky Mountain J. Math. (1996), 1235–1244.
