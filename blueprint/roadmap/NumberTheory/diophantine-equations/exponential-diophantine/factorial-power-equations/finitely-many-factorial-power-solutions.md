# Erdős Problem 405 (the equation (p − 1)! + a^(p − 1) = p^k)

The statement that only finitely many triples $(a, k, p)$ of natural numbers with $p$ an odd prime satisfy $(p - 1)! + a^{p-1} = p^k$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Finitely many solutions of (p − 1)! + a^(p − 1) = p^k](finitely-many-factorial-power-solutions-2.md).
