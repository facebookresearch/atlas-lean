# Erdős Problem 941 (sums of at most three powerful numbers)

The statement that every sufficiently large integer is the sum of at most three powerful numbers, a positive integer $m$ being powerful when $p^2 \mid m$ for every prime $p \mid m$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Large integers are sums of three powerful numbers](sum-of-at-most-three-powerful-holds.md).
