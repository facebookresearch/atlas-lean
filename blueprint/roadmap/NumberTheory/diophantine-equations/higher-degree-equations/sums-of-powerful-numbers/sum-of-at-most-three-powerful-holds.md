# Large integers are sums of three powerful numbers

Every sufficiently large integer is the sum of at most three powerful numbers, a positive integer $m$ being powerful when $p^2 \mid m$ for every prime $p \mid m$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 941 (sums of at most three powerful numbers)](sum-of-at-most-three-powerful.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Heath-Brown (1988), cited under Sources.

## Sources
