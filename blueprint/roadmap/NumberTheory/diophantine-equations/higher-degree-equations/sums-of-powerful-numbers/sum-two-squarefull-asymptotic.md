# Erdős Problem 1081 (counting sums of two squarefull numbers)

The statement that there is $c > 0$ with $A(x) \sim c\,x/(\log x)^{1/2}$ as $x \to \infty$, where for a natural number $x$, $A(x)$ is the number of $n \le x$ that are the sum of two squarefull numbers, a positive integer $m$ being squarefull when $p^2 \mid m$ for every prime $p \mid m$.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Sums of two squarefull numbers are not counted by c x / (log x)^(1/2)](sum-two-squarefull-asymptotic-refuted.md).
