# Infinitely many coprime 3-powerful solutions of a + b = c

There are infinitely many triples $(a, b, c)$ of pairwise coprime $3$-powerful numbers with $a + b = c$, where a natural number $n$ is $3$-powerful when $n > 0$ and $p^3 \mid n$ for every prime $p \mid n$.

This resolves [Erdős Problem 939, third question (sums of two 3-powerful numbers)](conjecture-2.md), the statement recorded as a proposition, in the affirmative; the resolution is due to Nitaj (1995), cited under Sources.
