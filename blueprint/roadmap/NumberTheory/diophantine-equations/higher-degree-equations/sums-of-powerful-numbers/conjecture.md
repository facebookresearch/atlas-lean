# Erdős Problem 939, second question (finiteness of powerful sums)

The statement that for every $r \ge 4$ there are only finitely many finite sets $S$ of $r - 2$ distinct positive $r$-powerful integers with greatest common divisor $1$ and $r$-powerful sum, where a positive integer $n$ is $r$-powerful when $p^r \mid n$ for every prime $p \mid n$.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
