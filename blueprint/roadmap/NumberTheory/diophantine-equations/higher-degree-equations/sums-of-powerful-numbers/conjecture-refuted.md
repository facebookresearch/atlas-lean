# Infinitely many coprime powerful sets with powerful sum

It is not the case that for every $r \ge 4$ there are only finitely many finite sets $S$ of $r - 2$ distinct positive $r$-powerful integers with greatest common divisor $1$ and $r$-powerful sum, where a positive integer $n$ is $r$-powerful when $p^r \mid n$ for every prime $p \mid n$.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 939, second question (finiteness of powerful sums)](conjecture.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation, recorded in Erdős Problems, Problem #939, is a construction of L. Price for every $r \ge 6$.
