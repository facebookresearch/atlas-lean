# Infinitely many square products of blocks of fixed sizes

It is not the case that for all sizes $k_1, \dots, k_n \ge 4$, only finitely many lists of pairwise disjoint intervals $I_1, \dots, I_n$ of positive integers with $|I_i| = k_i$ have $\prod_i \prod_{m \in I_i} m$ a perfect square.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 363 with fixed block sizes](finitely-many-square-interval-products-o.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is the published work cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #363, https://www.erdosproblems.com/363.
- M. Ulas, On products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers, Enseign. Math. (2) (2005), 331–334.
- M. Bauer and M. A. Bennett, On a question of Erdős and Graham, Enseign. Math. (2) (2007), 259–264.
