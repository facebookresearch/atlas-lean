# Erdős Problem 363 (square products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers)

The statement that for every $n$, only finitely many lists of $n$ pairwise disjoint intervals of positive integers, each with at least $4$ elements, have $\prod_i \prod_{m \in I_i} m$ a perfect square.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its refutation: [Infinitely many square products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers](finitely-many-square-interval-products-r.md).
