# Disjoint blocks of consecutive integers with square product

For a list $k_1, \dots, k_n$ of sizes, a list of sets $I_1, \dots, I_n$ is a square interval collection when each $I_i$ is an interval $\{a, \dots, b\}$ of positive integers with $k_i$ elements, the $I_i$ are pairwise disjoint, and $\prod_i \prod_{m \in I_i} m$ is a perfect square.
