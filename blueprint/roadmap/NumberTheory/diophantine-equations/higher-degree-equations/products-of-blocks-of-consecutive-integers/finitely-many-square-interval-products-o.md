# Erdős Problem 363 with fixed block sizes

The statement that for all sizes $k_1, \dots, k_n \ge 4$, only finitely many lists of pairwise disjoint intervals $I_1, \dots, I_n$ of positive integers with $|I_i| = k_i$ have $\prod_i \prod_{m \in I_i} m$ a perfect square.
