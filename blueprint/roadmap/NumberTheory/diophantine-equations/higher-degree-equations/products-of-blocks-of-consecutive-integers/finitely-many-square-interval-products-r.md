# Infinitely many square products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers

It is not the case that for every $n$, only finitely many lists of $n$ pairwise disjoint intervals of positive integers, each with at least $4$ elements, have $\prod_i \prod_{m \in I_i} m$ a perfect square.

This refutes [Erdős Problem 363 (square products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers)](finitely-many-square-interval-products.md), the statement recorded as a proposition; the refutation is due to Ulas (2005), cited under Sources.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #363, https://www.erdosproblems.com/363.
- M. Ulas, On products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers, Enseign. Math. (2) (2005), 331–334.
