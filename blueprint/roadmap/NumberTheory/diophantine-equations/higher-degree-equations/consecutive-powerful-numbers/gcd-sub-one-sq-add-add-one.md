# The gcd of $x \mp 1$ and $x^2 \pm x + 1$

For every integer $x$,
$\gcd(x - 1, x^2 + x + 1) = \gcd(x - 1, 3)$ and $\gcd(x + 1, x^2 - x + 1) = \gcd(x + 1, 3)$.

## Sources

- Jialai She, *Nonexistence of Consecutive Powerful Triplets Around Cubes with Prime-Square Factors*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A103, Lemma 4 (`integers-2025-A103-lem-4`).
