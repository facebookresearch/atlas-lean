# Walker's theorem on consecutive powerful numbers

There are infinitely many natural numbers $n$ such that $n$ and $n + 1$ are both powerful (every prime factor appears at least squared) and neither $n$ nor $n + 1$ is a perfect square.

## Sources

- D. T. Walker, Consecutive integer pairs of powerful numbers and related Diophantine equations, Fibonacci Quart. 14 (1976), 111–116.
