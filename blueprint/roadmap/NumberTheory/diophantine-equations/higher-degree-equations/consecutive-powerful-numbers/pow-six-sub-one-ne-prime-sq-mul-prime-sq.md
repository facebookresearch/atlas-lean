# $x^6 - 1$ is not $p^2 q^2$ times a nonzero cube

For all primes $p$, $q$ and all integers
$x$ and $a$ with $a \ne 0$, $x^6 - 1 \ne p^2 q^2 a^3$.

## Sources

- Jialai She, *Nonexistence of Consecutive Powerful Triplets Around Cubes with Prime-Square Factors*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A103, Corollary 1 (`integers-2025-A103-cor-1`).
