# Squares of polynomials with many terms have many terms

For every $M$ there is $K$ such that the square of every polynomial with rational coefficients and at least $K$ nonzero terms has at least $M$ nonzero terms.

## Sources

- T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #485, https://www.erdosproblems.com/485.
- A. Schinzel, On the number of terms of a power of a polynomial, Acta Arith. (1987), 55–70.
