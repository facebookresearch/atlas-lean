# Erdős Problem 485 (terms of the square of a sparse polynomial)

The statement that for every $M$ there is $K$ such that the square of every polynomial with rational coefficients and at least $K$ nonzero terms has at least $M$ nonzero terms.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Squares of polynomials with many terms have many terms](square-term-count-tends-to-infinity-hold.md).
