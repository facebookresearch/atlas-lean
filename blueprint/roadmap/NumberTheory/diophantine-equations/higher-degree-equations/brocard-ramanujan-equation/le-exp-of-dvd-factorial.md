# Divisors of a factorial bounded by their largest prime exponent

Let $n \ge 1$ and let
$x$ be a positive integer dividing $n!$. Write $K(x)$ for the largest exponent in the prime
factorization of $x$, with $K(1) = 0$. Then $x \le \exp(3 n K(x) / 2)$.

## Sources
