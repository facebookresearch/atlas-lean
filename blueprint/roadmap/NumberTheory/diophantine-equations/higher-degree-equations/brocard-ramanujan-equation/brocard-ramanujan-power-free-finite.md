# Brocard–Ramanujan solutions with $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ power-free are finite

For every
natural number $k \ge 2$, there are only finitely many pairs of natural numbers $(n, x)$ with
$n! + 1 = x^2$ such that $x - 1$ or $x + 1$ is $k$-free.

## Sources

- Abderrahim Makki Naciri, *On the Brocard-Ramanujan Equation with 7-Free Integers and Prime Powers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A71, Theorem 1(i), first sentence (`integers-2025-A71-thm-1`).
