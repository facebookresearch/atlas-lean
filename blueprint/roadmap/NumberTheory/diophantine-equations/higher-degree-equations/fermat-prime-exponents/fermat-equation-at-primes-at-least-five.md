# Fermat’s equation has no nonzero natural solution at primes at least five

For every natural prime $p\ge5$, and all nonzero natural numbers $a,b,c$, $a^p+b^p\ne c^p$. The threshold $5$ is a restriction on the prime exponent, not on the variables.
