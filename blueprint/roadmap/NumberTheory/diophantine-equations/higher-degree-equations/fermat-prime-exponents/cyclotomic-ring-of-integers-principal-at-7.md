# The ring of integers of a 7th cyclotomic field is principal

For any number field $K$ that is a $7$th cyclotomic extension of $\mathbb Q$, every ideal of $\mathcal O_K$ is principal. The field is arbitrary subject to the cyclotomic hypothesis, not a particular presentation of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{7})$.
