# A principal prime-ideal certificate in a cyclotomic field

Let $n>0$, $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ with $\zeta$ a primitive $n$th root, and write $\theta$ for the corresponding algebraic integer. Let $q$ be a natural prime, $r\in\mathbb Z$, and $A\in\mathbb Z[X]$. Suppose $q$ divides both $\Phi_n(r)$ and $A(r)$, and there are $b,c\in\mathcal O_K$ with

$$
A(\theta)b=q,\qquad A(\theta)c=\theta-r.
$$

Then the ideal generated by $A(\theta)$ is a prime ideal of $\mathcal O_K$ above the prime ideal $(q)$ of $\mathbb Z$.

## Expansion

Construct the map from the cyclotomic integers to $\mathbb F_q$ by evaluating at $r$. Show that its kernel is the ideal $(q,\theta-r)$ by division by $X-r$; the two multiplication certificates and $A(r)\equiv0$ then identify this kernel with $(A(\theta))$. Keep the argument independent of the cyclotomic exponent and the prime, so the same lemma certifies all of the explicit ideal computations.
