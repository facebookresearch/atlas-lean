# Fermat’s equation at exponent 11

For all nonzero natural numbers $a,b,c$, $a^{11}+b^{11}\ne c^{11}$.

## Proof depends on

- [Regular-prime Fermat theorem](../../../zeta-l-functions/multiple-zeta-values/finite-multiple-zeta-values-of-level-n/fermat-last-theorem-for-of-regular.md)

## Proof outline

Use the regular-prime theorem after proving that principality gives class number one. Retain the nonzero hypotheses and the exact exponent.
