# Nagell–Lutz integrality

Let $E : y^2 = x^3 + A x + B$ be an elliptic curve over
$\mathbb{Q}$ with $A, B \in \mathbb{Z}$. Every point $P = (x, y) \in E(\mathbb{Q})$ of finite order
$m \ge 2$ has $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$.

## Sources
