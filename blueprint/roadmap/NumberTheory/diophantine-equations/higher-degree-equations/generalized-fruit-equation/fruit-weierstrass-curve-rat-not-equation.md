# No rational affine points of $E_\alpha$ with even $x$-numerator

Let $\alpha$ be an integer
and let $x, y$ be rational. If the numerator of $x$ in lowest terms is even, then $(x, y)$ does not
satisfy $y^2 - \alpha x y = x^3 - (\alpha^2 + 5)$. No discriminant hypothesis is needed.

## Sources

- The reduction modulo four behind the integral obstruction in Satyabrat Sahoo and Shanta Laishram, *Generalized Fruit Diophantine Equation over Number Fields*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A21, Theorems 1 and 4, adapted to rational coordinates by clearing denominators and showing that both reduced denominators are odd.
