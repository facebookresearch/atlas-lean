# Erdős Problem 437 (square partial products)

The statement that for every real $\varepsilon > 0$ and all sufficiently large $x$ there are integers $1 \le a_1 < \dots < a_k \le x$ with more than $x^{1-\varepsilon}$ partial products $a_1 \cdots a_j$ that are perfect squares.

This page records the statement as a proposition. Its resolution: [Increasing sequences with many square partial products](many-square-partial-products-holds.md).
