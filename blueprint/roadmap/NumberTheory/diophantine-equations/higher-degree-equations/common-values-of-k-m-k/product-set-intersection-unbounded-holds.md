# Values k(m − k) and l(n − l) can share arbitrarily many elements

For every natural number $s$ there are natural numbers $n < m$ such that the sets $\{k(m - k) : 1 \le k \le \lfloor m/2 \rfloor\}$ and $\{l(n - l) : 1 \le l \le \lfloor n/2 \rfloor\}$ have at least $s$ common elements.
