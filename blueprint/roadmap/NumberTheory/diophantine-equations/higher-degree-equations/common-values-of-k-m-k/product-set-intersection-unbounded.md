# Erdős Problem 443, first question

The statement that for every natural number $s$ there are natural numbers $n < m$ such that the sets $\{k(m - k) : 1 \le k \le \lfloor m/2 \rfloor\}$ and $\{l(n - l) : 1 \le l \le \lfloor n/2 \rfloor\}$ have at least $s$ common elements.

This page records the statement as a proposition.
