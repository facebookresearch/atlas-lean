# Swapping the first two of three digits changes the characteristic number

For positive
integers $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a \ne b$, the strings $(a, b, c)$ and $(b, a, c)$ have different
characteristic numbers: $\chi(a, b, c) \ne \chi(b, a, c)$.

## Sources
