# Stern–Brocot labels of the Mallows representation vertices

Let $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ be irrational. Let
$I_0(\alpha), I_1(\alpha), \dots$ be the gaps along its Stern–Brocot path and
$J_0(\alpha), J_1(\alpha), \dots$ the gaps along its Mallows path, and for $i \ge 0$ let $o_i$ be
the $i$-th odd position (counted from $0$) of the parity sequence $Q(\mathrm{SB}(\alpha))$ of its
Stern–Brocot representation. Then for every $i \ge 0$, $\varphi_{SBT}$ of the Stern–Brocot vertex
with gap $I_{o_i}(\alpha)$ is the pair $\langle p, q \rangle$ that Mallows' insertion rule inserts
in the gap $J_i(\alpha)$.

In the source's notation, which numbers from $1$, this is
$\varphi_{SBT}(f_{o(i)}(\alpha)) = \mathrm{pf}_i(\alpha)$ for every $i \ge 1$.

## Sources

- Takanori Hida, *The Mallows Representation of the Irrational Numbers*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A78, Lemma 1.
