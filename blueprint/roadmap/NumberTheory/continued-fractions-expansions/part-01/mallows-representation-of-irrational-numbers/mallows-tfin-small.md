# Two values of $\dot T$

The finite version $\dot T$ of $T$ satisfies
$\dot T(\langle O_L, E_R, O_R \rangle) = \langle M \rangle$ and
$\dot T(\langle O_L, O_L \rangle) = \langle L \rangle$.
