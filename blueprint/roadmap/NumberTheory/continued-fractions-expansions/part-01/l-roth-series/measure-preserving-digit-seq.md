# Joint distribution of Lüroth digits

Under Lebesgue measure on $(0,1]$, the digit
sequence is distributed as a product of identical discrete measures, each giving mass
$1/(d(d-1))$ to digit $d \ge 2$ and zero mass to digits $0$ and $1$.

## Sources

- ATLAS, *Measure of Lüroth cylinders*. The finite cylinder probabilities determine the joint distribution.
