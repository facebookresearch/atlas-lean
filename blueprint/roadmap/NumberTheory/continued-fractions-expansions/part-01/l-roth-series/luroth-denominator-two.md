# Lüroth denominators of the constant digit sequence 2

If $d_j = 2$ for every $j \ge 0$,
then the Lüroth denominators are $Q_n = 2^{n+1}$ for every $n \ge 0$ (digits numbered from $0$;
this is the paper's $Q_{n+1} = 2^{n+1}$).
