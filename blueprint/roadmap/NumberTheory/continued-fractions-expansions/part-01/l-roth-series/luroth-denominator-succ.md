# Recurrence for Lüroth denominators

For every sequence of natural numbers
$d_0, d_1, d_2, \dots$ with Lüroth denominators $Q_0, Q_1, Q_2, \dots$ and every $n \ge 0$,

$$Q_{n+1} = Q_n (d_n - 1) d_{n+1}.$$
