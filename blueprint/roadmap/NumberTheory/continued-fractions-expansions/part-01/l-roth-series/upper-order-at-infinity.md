# Upper order at infinity

For a function $\psi$ from the natural numbers to the reals, the
upper order at infinity of $\psi$ is the element

$$\bar\tau_\psi = \limsup_{q \to \infty} \max\left(0, \frac{-\log \psi(q)}{\log q}\right)$$

of $[0, \infty]$.
