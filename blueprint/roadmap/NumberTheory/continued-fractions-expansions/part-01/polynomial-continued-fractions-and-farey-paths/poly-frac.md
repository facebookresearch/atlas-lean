# The fraction of a pair of polynomials

Let $F$ be a field. The value of a pair $(u, v)$ of
polynomials in $F[x]$ is the rational function $u / v$ in the field $F(x)$ of rational functions
over $F$ (it is $0$ when $v = 0$).

## Sources
