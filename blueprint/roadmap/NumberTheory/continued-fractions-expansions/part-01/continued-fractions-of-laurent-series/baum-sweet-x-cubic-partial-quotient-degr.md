# Baum–Sweet cubic: partial quotients of degree at most 2

If
$\alpha \in \mathbb{F}_2((x^{-1}))$ satisfies $x\alpha^3 + \alpha + x = 0$, then every partial
quotient $b_n$ of $\alpha$, $n \ge 0$, has degree at most $2$ in $x$.

## Sources

- Tuangrat Chaichana, Natthaphong Chitmung, and Vichian Laohakosol, *Certain Algebraic Formal Laurent Series with Bounded Partial Quotients*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A57, Corollary 2 (`integers-2026-A57-cor-2`).
