# Baum–Sweet: unbounded partial quotients for higher degrees

Let $n > 1$ and let
$g \in \mathbb{F}_2[x]$ have degree at least $1$. If $f \in \mathbb{F}_2((x^{-1}))$ satisfies
$f^{2^n+1} + g^{-1} f + 1 = 0$, then $f$ does not have bounded partial quotients.

## Sources

- Tuangrat Chaichana, Natthaphong Chitmung, and Vichian Laohakosol, *Certain Algebraic Formal Laurent Series with Bounded Partial Quotients*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A57, Theorem 2 (quoting Baum and Sweet, Theorem 5) (`integers-2026-A57-thm-2`).
