# Limit of the rescaled Farey staircase increment

Let $A_n$ be the Farey staircase of order
$n$ and $D_n = A_n - A_{n-1}$. For every real $x \ge 0$,
$n^2 D_n(x/n) \to \sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor x \rfloor} (1 - \log(x/k))$ as $n \to \infty$.
