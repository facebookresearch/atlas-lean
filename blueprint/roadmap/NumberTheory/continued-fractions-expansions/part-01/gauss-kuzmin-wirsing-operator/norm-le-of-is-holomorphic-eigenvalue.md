# Mayer–Roepstorff bound for each holomorphic eigenvalue

Every holomorphic eigenvalue
$\lambda \ne 1$ of the Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing operator on the Mayer disk
$\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - 1| < 3/2\}$ satisfies $|\lambda| \le 0.30366327$.

## Sources

- Mayer and Roepstorff (1987), eq. (5.20), catalogue upper bound `0.30366327`.
