# Example: $N_5^{1/2}(1) = 3$

In the Farey sequence of order $5$ the fractions with numerator $1$ and value below $1/2$ are $1/5$, $1/4$ and $1/3$, so $N_5^{1/2}(1) = 3$, where $N_n^x(h)$ counts the fractions of order $n$ with numerator $h$ below $x$.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Section 1 (definitions) and Section 2, p. 6 (the listing of the Farey sequence of order 5).
