# Farey fractions whose numerator is a prime power

Let $p$ be a prime, $m \ge 1$ and $n$ natural numbers with $p^m \le n$. Then

$$N_n(p^m) = \left\lceil (n - p^m)\left(1 - \frac{1}{p}\right) \right\rceil,$$

where $N_n(h)$ denotes the number of fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$, that is, of denominators $b$ with $h \le b \le n$ and $\gcd(h, b) = 1$.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 1.
