# Ranks of unit fractions under shifts by multiples of a least common multiple

Let $c$, $k$ and $p$ be positive integers, $L = \operatorname{lcm}(1, \dots, c)$, $k^\prime = k + pL$ and $A(c) = c \sum_{j=1}^{c} \varphi(j)/j - \sum_{j=1}^{c} \varphi(j)$, with $\varphi$ Euler's totient function, and let $I_n(x)$ count the fractions $r$ of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with $0 < r < x$. Then

1. $I_{c k^\prime}(1/k^\prime) = I_{c k}(1/k) + (k^\prime - k) A(c)$;
2. $I_{c k^\prime}(1/k^\prime) - I_{c k}(1/k) = I_{c (k^\prime - k)}\left(1/(k^\prime - k)\right)$;
3. $I_{c (k^\prime - k)}\left(1/(k^\prime - k)\right) / (k^\prime - k) = A(c)$.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 6.
