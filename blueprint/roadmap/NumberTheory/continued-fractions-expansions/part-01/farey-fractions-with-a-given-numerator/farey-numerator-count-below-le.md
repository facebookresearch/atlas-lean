# Counting Farey fractions below a bound gives at most the full count

For all natural numbers $n$ and $h$ and every rational $x$, $N_n^x(h) \le N_n(h)$, where $N_n^x(h)$ counts the fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$ and value below $x$, and $N_n(h)$ denotes the number of fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$, that is, of denominators $b$ with $h \le b \le n$ and $\gcd(h, b) = 1$.
