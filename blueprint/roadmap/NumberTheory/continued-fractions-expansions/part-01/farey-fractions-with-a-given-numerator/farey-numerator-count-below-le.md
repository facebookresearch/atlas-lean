# Counting Farey fractions below a bound gives at most the full count

For all natural numbers $n$ and $h$ and every rational $x$, $N_n^x(h) \le N_n(h)$, where $N_n(h)$ is the number of denominators $b$ with $h \le b \le n$ and $\gcd(h, b) = 1$, and $N_n^x(h)$ the number of those with moreover $h/b < x$. For $h \ge 1$ these count the fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$, respectively those below $x$; the inequality also holds at $h = 0$, where both counts are defined the same way.
