# Farey fractions with a given numerator below a bound

For natural numbers $n$ and $h$ and a rational number $x$, $N_n^x(h)$ is the number of natural numbers $b$ with $h \le b \le n$, $\gcd(h, b) = 1$ and $h/b < x$: the number of fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$ and value strictly below $x$. For $h = 0$ the only candidate is $b = 0$, which counts when $\gcd(0, 0) = 1$ fails, so $N_n^x(0) = 0$.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Section 1.
