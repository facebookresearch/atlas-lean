# Farey fractions with a given numerator below a bound

For natural numbers $n$ and $h$ and a rational number $x$, $N_n^x(h)$ is the number of natural numbers $b$ with $h \le b \le n$, $\gcd(h, b) = 1$ and $h/b < x$. For $h \ge 1$ this is the number of fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$ and value strictly below $x$, the quantity of the cited article, which considers positive numerators. The count is defined for every $h$: at $h = 0$ the only denominator coprime to $0$ is $b = 1$, so $N_n^x(0) = 1$ when $n \ge 1$ and $x > 0$, and $N_n^x(0) = 0$ otherwise.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Section 1.
