# Farey fractions with a given numerator below two over an odd number

Let $h$, $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $h > 2$, $k$ odd, $k > 1$ and $(k+1) h < 2n$, and let $\{n/d\} = (n \bmod d)/d$ denotes the fractional part. Then

$$N_n^{2/k}(h) = \frac{n \, \varphi(h)}{h} - \frac{k}{2} \varphi(h) - \sum_{d \mid h} \mu(d) \left\{\frac{n}{d}\right\},$$

where $N_n^x(h)$ counts the fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$ and value below $x$, $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function and $\mu$ the Möbius function. The hypotheses $k > 1$ and $h > 2$ are needed: for $n = 3$, $h = 1$ and $k = 3$ the left side is $2$ and the right side $3/2$.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 7, with the hypotheses of the lemma its proof uses.
