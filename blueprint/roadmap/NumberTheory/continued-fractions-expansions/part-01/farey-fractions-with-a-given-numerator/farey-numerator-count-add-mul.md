# Shifting the order of a Farey sequence by multiples of the numerator

Let $h$, $n$ and $m$ be natural numbers with $0 < h < n$. Then $N_{n + m h}(h) = N_n(h) + m \, \varphi(h)$, where $N_n(h)$ denotes the number of fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$, that is, of denominators $b$ with $h \le b \le n$ and $\gcd(h, b) = 1$ and $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 2.
