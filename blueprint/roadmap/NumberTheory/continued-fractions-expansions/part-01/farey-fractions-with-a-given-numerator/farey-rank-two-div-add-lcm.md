# Ranks of two over odd numbers under shifts by multiples of a least common multiple

Let $k$, $c$ and $p$ be natural numbers with $k$ odd and $k > 2$, $c$ even and positive, and $p > 0$. Let $L = \operatorname{lcm}(1, \dots, c)$, $k^\prime = k + pL$, $n = ck/2$ and $n^\prime = c k^\prime/2$, and let $I_m(x)$ count the fractions $r$ of the Farey sequence of order $m$ with $0 < r < x$. Then

$$I_{n^\prime}(2/k^\prime) = I_n(2/k) + \frac{k^\prime - k}{2} \left(c \sum_{j=1}^{c} \frac{\varphi(j)}{j} - \sum_{j=1}^{c} \varphi(j)\right),$$

where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 8, second part.
