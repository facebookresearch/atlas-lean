# Number of Farey fractions below a rational number

For a natural number $n$ and a rational number $x$, $I_n(x)$ is the number of fractions $q$ of the Farey sequence of order $n$, the reduced fractions in $[0, 1]$ with denominator at most $n$, with $0 < q < x$; the fraction $0/1$ is excluded. For $x = 1/k$ this is the rank of $1/k$ in the sequence.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Section 1.
