# Farey fractions with a given numerator below a unit fraction

Let $h$, $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $0 < h < n$, $k > 0$ and $k h < n$, and let $\{n/d\} = (n \bmod d)/d$ denotes the fractional part. Then

$$N_n^{1/k}(h) = \frac{n \, \varphi(h)}{h} - k \, \varphi(h) - \sum_{d \mid h} \mu(d) \left\{\frac{n}{d}\right\},$$

where $N_n^x(h)$ counts the fractions of the Farey sequence of order $n$ with numerator $h$ and value below $x$, $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function and $\mu$ the Möbius function.

## Sources

Rogelio Tomás García, "Farey Fractions with Equal Numerators and the Rank of Unit Fractions", INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article A63, Corollary 5.
