# Recursion for the left-most node

Let $T$ be a TRIP-Stern tree with left transition
$L$. For every natural number $n$, the left-most node of $T$ at depth $n + 1$ equals $L$ applied
to the left-most node of $T$ at depth $n$.

## Sources
