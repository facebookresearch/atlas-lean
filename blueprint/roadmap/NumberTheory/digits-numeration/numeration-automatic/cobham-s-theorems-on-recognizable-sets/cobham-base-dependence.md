# Cobham's theorem

Let $k$ and $l$ be multiplicatively independent natural numbers with
$l \ge 2$, and let $S$ be a set of natural numbers that is recognizable in base $k$ and is not
ultimately periodic. Then $S$ is not recognizable in base $l$.

## Sources

- Proof following Thijmen J. P. Krebs, *A more reasonable proof of Cobham's theorem*, arXiv:1801.06704, Section 3.
- Tomi Kärki, Anne Lacroix, and Michel Rigo, *On the Recognizability of Self-Generating Sets*, Journal of Integer Sequences 13 (2010), Article 10.2.2, especially the remark invoking Cobham's theorem: <https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL13/Rigo/rigo6.tex>.
