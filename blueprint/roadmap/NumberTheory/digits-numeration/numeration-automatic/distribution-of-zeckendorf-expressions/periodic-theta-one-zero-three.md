# The maximal block $\theta^4$ in the Fibonacci case

For $L = (1, 0)$, the maximal $L$-block
$\theta^4$ is $(1, 0, 1)$: its entries at indices $0$, $1$, $2$ are $1$, $0$, $1$, and all its
entries from index $3$ on are $0$.
