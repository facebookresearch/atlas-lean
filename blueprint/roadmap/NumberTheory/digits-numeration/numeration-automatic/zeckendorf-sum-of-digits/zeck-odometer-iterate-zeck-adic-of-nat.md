# The odometer adds on the natural numbers

Let $T$ be the Zeckendorf odometer and, for
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $i(n)$ be the Zeckendorf digit sequence of $n$, whose digit $k$ is $1$
exactly when $F_k$ is a term of the Zeckendorf representation of $n$. Then
$T^r(i(v)) = i(v + r)$ for all $v, r \in \mathbb{N}$: on the copy $i(\mathbb{N})$ of the natural
numbers, $x + r = T^r(x)$ is the usual sum.

## Sources

- Yohan Hosten, *On the Variation of the Sum of Digits in the Zeckendorf Representation: An Algorithm to Compute the Distribution and Mixing Properties*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.3.2, Section 2.2 (`T` restricted to `ℕ` is `n ↦ n + 1`).
