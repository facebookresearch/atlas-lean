# S-perfect numbers and prime powers

Every integer $n$ with at least two distinct prime factors is $S$-perfect for some finite set $S$ with $|S| \le \tau(|n|) - 2$, where $\tau$ counts divisors; and no integer $n$ with $|n|$ a prime power is $S$-perfect for any $S$. Here for a set $S$ of integers, an integer $n$ with $|n| > 1$ is $S$-perfect when $n = 1 + \sum_d \lambda_d d$, the sum over the divisors $d$ of $|n|$ with $1 < d < |n|$, for some weights $\lambda_d \in S$.
