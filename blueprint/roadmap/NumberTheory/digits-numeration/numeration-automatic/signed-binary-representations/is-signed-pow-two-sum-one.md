# Example of a signed power-of-two sum

The integer $1$ can be written as
$\sum_{j=0}^{1} \lambda_j 2^j$ with every $\lambda_j \in \{-1, 3\}$, namely
$1 = 3 \cdot 2^0 + (-1) \cdot 2^1$.
