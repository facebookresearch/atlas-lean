# Uniformly recurrent sequences are recurrent

Every uniformly recurrent sequence
$s : \mathbb{N} \to \alpha$, with values in any set $\alpha$, is recurrent.

## Sources

- Nadav Kohen, *A Linear Representation for Constant Term Sequences mod p^a with Applications to Uniform Recurrence*, Journal of Integer Sequences 29 (2026), Article 26.4.2, Definitions 1 and 18.
