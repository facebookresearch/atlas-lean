# Knott expansions of Lucas numbers

For every $n \ge 1$, each of the Lucas numbers $L_{2n}$
and $L_{2n+1}$ (with $L_0 = 2$, $L_1 = 1$) has exactly $2n + 1$ Knott expansions.

## Sources

- Michel Dekking and Ad van Loon, *Counting Base Phi Representations*, Fibonacci Quarterly 62 (2024), Article 62-2/dekking11102023, Theorem 3.3 (`fq-2024-dekking11102023-thm-3.3`).
