# Termination of comma sequences from cycles through phase zero

For every base $b \ge 2$, if no vertex $(d, u, 0)$ of the reduced comma digraph $G_b'$ lies on a directed cycle, then the base-$b$ comma sequence starting at every positive integer terminates.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
