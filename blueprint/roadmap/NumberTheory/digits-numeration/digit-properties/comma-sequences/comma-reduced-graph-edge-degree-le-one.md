# The reduced comma digraph has degrees at most one

For every base $b \ge 2$, every vertex of the reduced comma digraph $G_b'$ has at most one outgoing edge and at most one incoming edge.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
