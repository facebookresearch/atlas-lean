# Final values of comma sequences in danger intervals

For natural numbers $b$ and $d$, $U(b, d)$ is the set of numbers $u = rb + s$ with digits $r, s < b$ such that, when $d = 1$, $s > 0$ and $r + s \le b$, and when $d \ne 1$, either $s > 0$ and $r + s < b$, or $r + s = b$ and $s < d$.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
