# Counting initial values that never leave a danger interval

For natural numbers $b \ge 2$ and $m \ge 3$, the number of $v$ with $b^m - b^2 \le v < b^m$ such that every term reachable from $v$ by base-$b$ comma successor steps is less than $b^m$, plus one, equals the number of pairs $(i, j)$ with $1 \le i \le b$, $0 \le j \le b$ and $\lfloor i(i+3)/2 \rfloor + ij \le b$.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
