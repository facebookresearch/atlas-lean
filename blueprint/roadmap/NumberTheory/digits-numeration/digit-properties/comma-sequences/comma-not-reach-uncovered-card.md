# Uncovered non-escaping values and triangular numbers

For natural numbers $b \ge 3$ and $k \ge 3$, the number of $w \in I_k(b)$ that are not of the form $b^k - b^2 + (r+1)b + s$ with $r, s < b - 1$ and $(b-1)^k - (b-1)^2 + r(b-1) + s \in I_k(b-1)$ equals the number of pairs $1 \le j < i \le b$ with $i(i+1)/2 - j(j+1)/2 = b$; here $I_k(c)$ is the set of $v$ with $c^k - c^2 \le v < c^k$ such that every term reachable from $v$ by base-$c$ comma successor steps is less than $c^k$.
