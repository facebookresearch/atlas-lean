# The comma map between danger intervals

For natural numbers $b, d, u, k, u'$, let $T = (d+1)b^k$ if $d < b - 1$ and $T = b^{k+1}$ otherwise; the comma map sends $(d, u, k)$ to $u'$ when $0 < u' \le b^2$, the base-$b$ comma sequence from $d b^k - u$ reaches $T - u'$ after zero or more comma successor steps, and $T - u'$ either has no comma successor in base $b$ or has only comma successors at least $T$.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
