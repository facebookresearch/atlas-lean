# Cycle sum of comma differences

For natural numbers $b > 0$, $d$ and $u$, $S_b(d, u) = \sum_{0 \le m < b/\gcd(b,d)} \bigl(((md - u) \bmod b)\, b + d\bigr)$, with $(md - u) \bmod b \in \{0, \dots, b-1\}$; it is the sum of the cycle of comma differences. For $b = 0$ the range is empty (natural division by zero gives $0$) and $S_0(d, u) = 0$.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
