# Cycle sum of comma differences

For natural numbers $b$, $d$ and $u$, $S_b(d, u) = \sum_{m=0}^{b/\gcd(b,d) - 1} \bigl(((md - u) \bmod b)\, b + d\bigr)$, with $(md - u) \bmod b \in \{0, \dots, b-1\}$; it is the sum of the cycle of comma differences.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
