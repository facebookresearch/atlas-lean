# Comma successors in consecutive bases

For natural numbers $b \ge 3$, $k \ge 3$, $x < b - 1$, $0 < y < b - 1$, $r < b - 1$ and $s < b - 1$: in base $b - 1$, $(b-1)^k - (b-1)^2 + x(b-1) + y$ has comma successor $(b-1)^k - (b-1)^2 + r(b-1) + s$ if and only if, in base $b$, $b^k - b^2 + (x+1)b + y$ has comma successor $b^k - b^2 + (r+1)b + s$.

## Sources

- R. Dougherty-Bliss and N. Ter-Saakov, The comma sequence is finite in other bases, J. Integer Seq. 28 (2025), Article 25.5.5.
