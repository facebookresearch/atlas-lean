# The number of units of order $L$ is a multiple of $\varphi(L)$

For natural numbers
$n \ge 1$ and $L$, the number $E(L, n)$ of elements of order $L$ in the unit group
$(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times$ is divisible by Euler's totient $\varphi(L)$.
