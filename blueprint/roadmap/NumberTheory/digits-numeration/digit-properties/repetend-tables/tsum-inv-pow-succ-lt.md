# Tail bound for sums of inverse powers

For natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and $s \ge 1$,
$\sum_{n = s+1}^{\infty} n^{-m} < \dfrac{1}{(m-1)\, s^{m-1}}$.

## Sources

- R. J. Booth and D. J. Horton, *Matched Column Sets in Repetend Tables*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A26, Lemma 42 (`integers-2025-A26-lem-42`).
