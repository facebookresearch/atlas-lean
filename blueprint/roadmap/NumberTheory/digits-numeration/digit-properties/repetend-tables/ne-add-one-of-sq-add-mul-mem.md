# A constraint on the quotient of the square of a small base

Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $b$ be a small base: a positive integer coprime to $n$ and less than $n$.
Let $u$ and $b_2$ be integers with $b^2 = b_2 + un$ and $2 \le b_2 \le n - 2$. Then $b \ne u + 1$.
