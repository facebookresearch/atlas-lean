# Complementary moment

For natural numbers $b$, $d$, $k$ and $m$ with $d < b$, let $\mu_k$
be the Irwin measure for base $b$, digit $d$ and digit count $k$. The complementary moment is

$$
v_{k;m} = \int_{[0,1)} (1 - x)^m \, d\mu_k(x).
$$
