# Summability of the digit-string weights with a fixed count of one digit

Let $b > 1$ and
$d < b$ be natural numbers, and let $k$ be a natural number. Over the base-$b$ digit strings in
which the digit $d$ occurs exactly $k$ times, the weights $b^{-|X|}$ (read as real numbers) are
summable.

## Sources

- Jean-François Burnol, *Measures for the Summation of Irwin Series*, INTEGERS 26 (2026), Article A11, definition of `μ_k`.
