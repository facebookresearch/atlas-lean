# Triangular repdigits

The positive triangular numbers $n(n+1)/2$ with $n \ge 1$ whose
decimal representation consists of a single repeated digit are exactly $1$, $3$, $6$, $55$, $66$
and $666$.

## Sources

- Christian Hercher and Karl Fegert, *Triangular Numbers With a Single Repeated Digit*, Journal of Integer Sequences 28 (2025), Article 25.2.1, Theorem 1, arXiv:2407.21487.
