# Blocks of non-basic reverse divisors with quotient nine

Let $n > 0$ be a natural number whose decimal reversal is $9n$ and which is not of the form $S_r = 11(10^{r+2} - 1)$ for any $r$. Say that a number $X$ begins with a block $B$ when $X = B \cdot 10^t + m$ for some $t$ and $m < 10^t$. Then (1) for some $r$, $n$ begins with $S_r$ and $n \equiv S_r \pmod{10^{r+4}}$; and (2) for every $r$ such that $n$ begins with $S_r$ and $n \equiv S_r \pmod{10^{r+4}}$: if $r = 0$, the decimal reversal of $11n$ begins with $97$ and is congruent to $11$ modulo $100$; if $r \ge 1$, the decimal reversal of $(10^{r+1} + 1)n$ begins with $99 \cdot 10^r - 2$ and is congruent to $11$ modulo $10^{r+2}$.

## Sources
