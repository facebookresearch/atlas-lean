# Reverse divisors with quotient $4$ lack property $P^*$

If $n > 0$ is a natural number
whose decimal reversal equals $4n$, then $n$ does not have the property $P^*$.

## Sources

- Mohamed Ayad and Rachid Bouchenna, *Which Integer Divides the Reverse of Any of Its Multiples?*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A37, Lemma 1.
