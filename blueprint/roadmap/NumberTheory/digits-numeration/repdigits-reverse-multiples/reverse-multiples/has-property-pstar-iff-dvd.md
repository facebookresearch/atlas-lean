# The positive integers with property $P^*$ are the divisors of $99$

A natural number
$n \ge 1$ has property $P^*$ if and only if $n$ divides $99$.

## Sources

- Mohamed Ayad and Rachid Bouchenna, *Which Integer Divides the Reverse of Any of Its Multiples?*, INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A37, Theorem 3.
