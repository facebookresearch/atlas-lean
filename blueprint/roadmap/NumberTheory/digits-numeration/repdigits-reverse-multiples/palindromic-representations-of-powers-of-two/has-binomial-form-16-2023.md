# The binomial form of 2023 in base 16

The number $2023$ has a base-$16$ representation in
binomial form: its base-$16$ digits are $7, 14, 7$, that is
$7\binom{2}{2}, 7\binom{2}{1}, 7\binom{2}{0}$.
