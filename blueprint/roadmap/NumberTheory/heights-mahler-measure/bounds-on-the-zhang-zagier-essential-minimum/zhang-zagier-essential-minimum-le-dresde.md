# Dresden's upper bound for the Zhang–Zagier essential minimum

$m_{ZZ} \le 0.39679$, where $m_{ZZ}$ is the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha)$ on the algebraic numbers $\alpha$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height): the supremum of the reals $H$ for which only finitely many $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$; decimals denote exact rational numbers.

## Sources

- G. P. Dresden, Sums of heights of algebraic numbers, Math. Comp. 72 (2003), 1487–1499, Section 7.
