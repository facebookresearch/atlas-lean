# Positivity of the Zhang–Zagier essential minimum

$m_{ZZ} > 0$, where $m_{ZZ}$ is the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha)$ on the algebraic numbers $\alpha$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height): the supremum of the reals $H$ for which only finitely many $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$; decimals denote exact rational numbers.

## Sources

- S. Zhang, Positive line bundles on arithmetic varieties, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 8 (1995), 187–221.
