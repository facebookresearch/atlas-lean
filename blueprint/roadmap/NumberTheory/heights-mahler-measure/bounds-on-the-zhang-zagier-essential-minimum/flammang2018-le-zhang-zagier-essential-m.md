# Flammang's lower bound for the Zhang–Zagier essential minimum

$m_{ZZ} \ge 0.24874$, where $m_{ZZ}$ is the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha)$ on the algebraic numbers $\alpha$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height): the supremum of the reals $H$ for which only finitely many $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$; decimals denote exact rational numbers.

## Sources

- V. Flammang, On the Zhang–Zagier measure, Int. J. Number Theory 14 (2018), 2663–2671.
