# Doche's upper bound for the Zhang–Zagier essential minimum

$m_{ZZ} \le 0.25594$, where $m_{ZZ}$ is the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha)$ on the algebraic numbers $\alpha$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height): the supremum of the reals $H$ for which only finitely many $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$; decimals denote exact rational numbers.

## Sources

- C. Doche, On the spectrum of the Zhang–Zagier height, Math. Comp. 70 (2001), 419–430.
