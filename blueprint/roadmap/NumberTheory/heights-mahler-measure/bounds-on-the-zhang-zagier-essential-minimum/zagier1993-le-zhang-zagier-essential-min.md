# Zagier's lower bound for the Zhang–Zagier essential minimum

$m_{ZZ} \ge \frac12 \log\frac{1 + \sqrt5}{2}$, where $m_{ZZ}$ is the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha)$ on the algebraic numbers $\alpha$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height): the supremum of the reals $H$ for which only finitely many $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$; decimals denote exact rational numbers.

## Sources

- D. Zagier, Algebraic numbers close to both 0 and 1, Math. Comp. 61 (1993), 485–491.
