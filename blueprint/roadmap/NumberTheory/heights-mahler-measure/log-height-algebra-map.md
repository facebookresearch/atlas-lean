# Logarithmic height under extension of number fields

Let $L/K$ be an extension of number fields and $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a finite tuple of elements of $K$. Then the field-relative logarithmic height of the tuple $x$ viewed in $L$ (the logarithmic height over $L$, not normalized by the degree) is $[L : K]$ times the field-relative logarithmic height of $x$ over $K$. The height of an all-zero tuple, including the empty tuple, is $0$, so there the identity reads $0 = [L : K] \cdot 0$.
