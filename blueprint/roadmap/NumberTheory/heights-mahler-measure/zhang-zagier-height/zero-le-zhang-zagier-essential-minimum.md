# The Zhang–Zagier essential minimum is nonnegative

The essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height is at least $0$, where the essential minimum of the Zhang–Zagier height is the supremum of the finite Zhang–Zagier thresholds, the reals $H$ for which only finitely many algebraic numbers $\alpha$ have $h(\alpha) + h(1 - \alpha) \le H$ ($h$ the absolute logarithmic Weil height).
