# Real cyclotomic integers with house between 2 and 76/33

Let $n \ge 1$, let $K$ be a number
field that is a cyclotomic extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ generated by the $n$-th roots of unity, and
let $\beta \in K$ be an algebraic integer lying in the maximal real subfield of $K$. Write
$\operatorname{house}(\beta)$ for the largest absolute value of the images of $\beta$ under the
complex embeddings of $K$. If $2 < \operatorname{house}(\beta) < 76/33$, then
$\operatorname{house}(\beta)$ is one of $(\sqrt{7} + \sqrt{3})/2$, $\sqrt{5}$,
$1 + 2\cos(2\pi/7)$, $(1 + \sqrt{5})/\sqrt{2}$ and $(1 + \sqrt{13})/2$.
