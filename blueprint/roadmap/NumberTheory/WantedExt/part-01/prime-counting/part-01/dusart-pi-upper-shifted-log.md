# Dusart Pi Upper Shifted Log Wanted

Dusart shifted-log upper bound for `π(x)`: for `x > exp 1.112`,
`π(x) ≤ x / (log x - 1.112)`, with `π` as `Nat.primeCounting ⌊x⌋₊`.
