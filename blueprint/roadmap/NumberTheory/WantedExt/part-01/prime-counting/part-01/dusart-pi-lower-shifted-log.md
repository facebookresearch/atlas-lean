# Dusart Pi Lower Shifted Log Wanted

Dusart shifted-log lower bound for `π(x)`: for `x ≥ 5393`,
`π(x) ≥ x / (log x - 1)`, with `π` as `Nat.primeCounting ⌊x⌋₊`.
