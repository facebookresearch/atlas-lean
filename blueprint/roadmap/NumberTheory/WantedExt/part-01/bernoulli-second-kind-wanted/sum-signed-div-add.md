# Theorem 19

**Theorem 19**: for `r ≥ 1`, `∑_{k=0}^n s(n,k)/(k+r)
= ∑_{k=0}^{r-1} λ_{r,n,k} B_{n+k}^*` where
`λ_{r,n,k} = ∑_{ℓ=0}^{r-1-k} C(r-1,ℓ) S(r-1-ℓ,k) n^ℓ`.
