# Theorem 17

**Theorem 17**: for `r ≥ 1`, `∑_{k=0}^n (-1)^k
(k!/(k+r)) S(n,k) = (1/(r-1)!) ∑_{k=0}^{r-1}
|s(r,k+1)| B_{n+k}`, with `|s|` the unsigned Stirling
numbers of the first kind.
