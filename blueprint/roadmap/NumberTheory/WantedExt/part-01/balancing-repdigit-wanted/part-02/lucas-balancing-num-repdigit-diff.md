# Lucas Balancing Num repdigit Diff

If `C_k` is a difference of two repdigits then `C_k ∈ {3, 17}`; the paper displays
`C_2 = 3 = 11 - 8` and `C_3 = 17 = 22 - 5`.

The hypothesis `2 ≤ k` excludes `C_1 = 1 = 2 - 1`, as in Theorem 2.
