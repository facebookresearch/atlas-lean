# Is Concat Three Repdigits

Concatenation of three repdigits: the decimal expansion has exactly three
digit runs, of digits `d₁ ∈ [1, 9]`, `d₂, d₃ ∈ [0, 9]` with `d₁ ≠ d₂` and
`d₂ ≠ d₃`.
