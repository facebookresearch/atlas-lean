# Is Repdigit

A (decimal) repdigit: a positive integer all of whose decimal digits are
equal to some `d` with `1 ≤ d ≤ 9`, with `m ≥ 1` digits.

The condition is written without division as `9 * n = d * (10^m - 1)`.
