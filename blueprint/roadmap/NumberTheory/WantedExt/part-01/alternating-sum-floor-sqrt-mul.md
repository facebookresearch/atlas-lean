# Alternating Floor Sqrt Sum Wanted

Chamberland and Dilcher's Theorem 1: for odd `n ≥ 1`, the alternating
sum of `⌊√(jn)⌋` over `1 ≤ j ≤ n` equals `(n + 1) / 2`.
