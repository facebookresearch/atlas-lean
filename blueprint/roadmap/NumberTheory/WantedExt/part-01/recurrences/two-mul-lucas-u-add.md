# Proposition 1

**Proposition 1** (integer specialization): for the
generalized Fibonacci numbers `F_n` and Lucas numbers
`L_n` with integer parameters `a` and nonzero integer
`b` (the paper allows real `a, b`),
`2 F_{m + n} = F_m L_n + L_m F_n`.
