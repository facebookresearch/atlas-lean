# Four mul sum tribonacci mul sub two

Gryszka's Lemma 1, cleared of the denominator `4`: for an
integer-indexed Tribonacci recurrence, four times
`∑ k ∈ [1,n], T k * T (k - 2)` is the displayed difference of
squares.
