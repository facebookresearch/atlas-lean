# Theorem 1, `C = 7`

**Theorem 1, `C = 7`** (Luca--Tengely--Togbé, quoted by Naciri):
the positive coprime solutions of `x² + 7 = 4yⁿ` with `n ≥ 3` are
exactly `(n, x, y) = (3, 5, 2)`, `(5, 11, 2)`, and `(13, 181, 2)`.
