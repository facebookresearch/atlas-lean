# Theorem 1, `C = 3`

**Theorem 1, `C = 3`** (Luca--Tengely--Togbé, quoted by Naciri):
the positive coprime solutions of `x² + 3 = 4yⁿ` with `n ≥ 3` are
exactly `x = y = 1` (any `n ≥ 3`) and `(n, x, y) = (3, 37, 7)`.
