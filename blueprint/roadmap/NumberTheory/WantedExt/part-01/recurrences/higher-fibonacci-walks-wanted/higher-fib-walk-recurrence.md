# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1**: for the standard order-`m` Fibonacci sequence,
the shifted self-convolution `wₙ⁽ᵐ⁾` satisfies
`(m+1) wₙ₊₁ - 2m wₙ = (n+m+2)Fₙ₊ₘ - (n+m)Fₙ₊ₘ₋₁`.
