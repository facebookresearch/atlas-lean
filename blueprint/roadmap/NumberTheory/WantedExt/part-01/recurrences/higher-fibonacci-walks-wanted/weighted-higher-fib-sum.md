# Weighted Higher Fib Sum

The weighted sum `S(m,N,l,q) = ∑_{k=0}^{N} q^k F_{k+l}`.
An upper limit `N < 0` gives the empty sum.  The order `m` is carried
explicitly to match the paper notation, although the sum itself only
uses `F`, `N`, `l`, and `q`.
