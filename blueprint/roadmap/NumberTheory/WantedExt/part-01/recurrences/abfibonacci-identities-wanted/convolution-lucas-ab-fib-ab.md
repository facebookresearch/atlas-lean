# Corollary 14(a)

**Corollary 14(a)**: if `b ≠ d` then
`∑_{i=1}^n L_{n-i}^{a,b} F_i^{a,d}
  = (L_{n+1}^{a,b} - L_{n+1}^{a,d}) / (b - d)
  = ∑_{i=1}^n L_{n-i}^{a,d} F_i^{a,b}`
(first equality in multiplied-out form).
