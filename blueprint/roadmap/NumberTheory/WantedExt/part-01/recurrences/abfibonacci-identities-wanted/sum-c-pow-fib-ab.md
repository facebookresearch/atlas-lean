# Theorem 12

**Theorem 12**: for nonzero `c` with
`c^2 - a*c - b ≠ 0` and positive `n`,
`∑_{i=1}^n c^(n-i) F_i
  = (c^(n+1) - c F_{n+1} - b F_n) / (c^2 - a*c - b)`.
