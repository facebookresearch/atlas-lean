# Theorem 5(a)

**Theorem 5(a)**: for `b ≠ 0`,
(i) `∑_{i=0}^n (-b)^(n-i) L_{2i+1} = F_{2n+2}` and
(ii) `∑_{i=0}^n (-b)^(n-i) L_{2i} = F_{2n+1} + (-b)^n`.
