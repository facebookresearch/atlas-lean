# Corollary 4

**Corollary 4**: for `(a, b)`-Fibonacci and
`(a, b)`-Lucas numbers, and `Δ = a^2 + 4b ≠ 0`,
`Δ F_{m+1} = L_{m+2} + b L_m`.
