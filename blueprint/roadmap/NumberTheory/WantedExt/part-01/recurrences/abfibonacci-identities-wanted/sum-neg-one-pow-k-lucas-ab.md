# Corollary 9

**Corollary 9**: the `k`-Fibonacci case:
`∑_{i=0}^n (-1)^i L_{k, 2(i+m)}
  = (-1)^n F_{k, 2(n+m)+1} + F_{k, 2m-1}` (for
`m ≥ 1`; `F_{k, j}`, `L_{k, j}` are `fibAB k 1`,
`lucasAB k 1`).
