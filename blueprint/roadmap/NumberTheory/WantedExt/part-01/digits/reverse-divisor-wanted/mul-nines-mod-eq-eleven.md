# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4**: the product of the integer consisting
of `r` digits `9` followed by `89` with the integer
consisting of `r + 2` digits `9` ends with `r` digits
`0` followed by `11`; that is, it is congruent to `11`
modulo `10^{r+2}`.
