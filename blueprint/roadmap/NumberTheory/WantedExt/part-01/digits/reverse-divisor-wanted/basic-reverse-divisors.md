# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1**, basic examples: for `k ≥ 2`, the
`(k + 2)`-digit numbers `11 * (10^k - 1)` and
`22 * (10^k - 1)` are reverse divisors with respective
quotients `9` and `4`.
