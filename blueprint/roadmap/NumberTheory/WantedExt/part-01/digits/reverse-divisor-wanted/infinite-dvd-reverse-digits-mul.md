# Proposition 6

**Proposition 6**: if `10 ∤ n` and there exists
`k₀ ≥ 1` with `n ∣ reverse(k₀ * n)`, then there are
infinitely many `k ≥ 1` with `n ∣ reverse(k * n)`.
