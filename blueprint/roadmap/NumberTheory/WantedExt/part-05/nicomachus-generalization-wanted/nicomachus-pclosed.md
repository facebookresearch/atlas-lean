# Nicomachus PClosed

The polynomial expansion of `P_{m,o}(x)` in `m`, obtained from
the defining sum by the Faulhaber formulas for `Σ_{j=0}^m j^p`,
`p ≤ 3`; it agrees with `nicomachusPOdd` at natural `m`.
