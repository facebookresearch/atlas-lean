# Nicomachus Alpha

The numbers `α_k` of Equation (12): the continued-fraction
convergents `[1; 6, 3, …, 6, 3]` with `2k - 2` partial quotients
after the semicolon.  Defined by the paper's recurrence (13),
with `α₁ = 1`; the value at `0` is a dummy.
