# Theorem 1 1

Statement of Theorem 1.1:

Let `d ≥ 2` and `n ≥ d` with `(d, n) ≠ (3, 3)`. Then we have `lim_{A → ∞} ρ_{d,n}(A) = ρˡᵒᶜ_{d,n}`.
