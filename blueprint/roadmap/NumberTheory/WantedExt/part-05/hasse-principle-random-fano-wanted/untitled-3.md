# Ρ

`ρ_{d,n}(A)` is the proportion of degree-`d` hypersurfaces in `ℙⁿ` of height at most `A`
that have a rational point:
`ρ_{d,n}(A) = #{V ∈ 𝕍_{d,n}(A) | V(ℚ) ≠ ∅} / #𝕍_{d,n}(A)`.
