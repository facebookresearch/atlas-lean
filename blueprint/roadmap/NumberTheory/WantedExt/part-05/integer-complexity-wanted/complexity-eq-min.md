# Proposition 1

**Proposition 1**: for `n > 1`, `‖n‖` is the minimum of
`‖d‖ + ‖n / d‖` over divisors `d` of `n` with `d ≥ 2` and
of `‖j‖ + ‖n - j‖` over `1 ≤ j ≤ n / 2`.
