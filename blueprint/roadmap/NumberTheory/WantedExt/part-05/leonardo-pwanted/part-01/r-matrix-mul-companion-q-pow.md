# Theorem 10

**Theorem 10**: `R_{p,m} Q_p^n = R_{p,m+n}` for every
`ℋ ∈ H(p)` (here: every `H : ℤ → ℤ` satisfying the
`H(p)` recurrence at all integers) and positive `m`.
