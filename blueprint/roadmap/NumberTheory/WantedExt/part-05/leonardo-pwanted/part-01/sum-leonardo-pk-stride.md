# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4**: stride sums of `ℒ_{p,k}`:
`∑_{i=0}^n ℒ_{p,k}((p+1)i + m)` equals
`ℒ_{p,k}((p+1)n + m + 1) - kn` for `m < p` and
`ℒ_{p,k}((p+1)(n+1)) - k(n+1) - 1` for `m = p`.
