# Lemma 1

**Lemma 1**: if `m, n` are coprime positive integers and
`(x, y)` is a pair of Bézout coefficients for `(m, n)`, i.e.
`xm + yn = 1`, then `x` is an inverse of `m` modulo `n` and `y`
is an inverse of `n` modulo `m`.
