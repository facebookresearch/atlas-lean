# Lemma 6

**Lemma 6**: if `r₀` and `r₁` are both odd, then
`|s₁| = (r₀ - 1)/2` and `|s₂| = (r₁ - 1)/2` hold simultaneously
if and only if `r₀ - r₁ = 2`.
