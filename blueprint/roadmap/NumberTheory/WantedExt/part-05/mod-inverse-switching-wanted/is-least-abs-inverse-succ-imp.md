# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3**: if for some `j ∈ {1, …, N}` the inverse
`s_{j+1}` is the least absolute inverse of `r_{j+1}` modulo
`r_j`, then `s_j` is the least absolute inverse of `r_j` modulo
`r_{j-1}`.
