# Theorem 1

Statement of Theorem 1 (Dirichlet's principle in Grassmann varieties):

For all `l`-dimensional subspaces `x ≤ ℝᵈ` and all `k ∈ {1,...,l}`, we have
`βₖ(x) ≥ d / (k * (d - l))`, and this lower bound is optimal.
