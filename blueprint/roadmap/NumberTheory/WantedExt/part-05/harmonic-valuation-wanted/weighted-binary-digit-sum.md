# Weighted Binary Digit Sum

The weighted binary digit sum `a(n) = ∑ aᵢ * 2^{aᵢ}`, summed over the
exponents `aᵢ` of the powers of `2` in the binary expansion of `n` (that is,
over the indices of the `1` bits of `n`).
