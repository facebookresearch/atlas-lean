# Theorem B

Statement of Theorem B:

Let `w ∈ ℕ`, where `ℕ := {1,2,...}`. MZVs of weight `w` in `𝒯0 w` are all linearly independent over
`K`. In particular, `dim 𝒵 w ≥ |𝒯0 w|`. Note that `K` in the paper is `RatFunc F`.
