# Corollary 4

**Corollary 4**: if a positive even `n`, with
`2^m < n ≤ 2^(m+1)`, is neither a sum of one or two
distinct powers of `2` nor of one of the four forms in
`twoComplexityAddTwoForm`, then `log₂ n + 2 ≤ ‖n‖₂`.
