# Proposition 2

**Proposition 2**: for `l > 1`, writing a positive
multiple `n` of `l` uniquely as `n = b + a * l` with `b` a
positive multiple of `l^2` and `0 ≤ a ≤ l - 1`, one has
`‖n‖_l = ‖b‖_l + a`.
