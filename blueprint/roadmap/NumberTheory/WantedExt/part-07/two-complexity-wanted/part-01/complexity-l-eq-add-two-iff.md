# Proposition 4

**Proposition 4** (= **Theorem 1**(2)): for `m ≥ 3` and
positive even `n` with `2^m < n ≤ 2^(m+1)`, `‖n‖₂ = m + 2`
if and only if `n` is of one of the four forms in
`twoComplexityAddTwoForm`.
