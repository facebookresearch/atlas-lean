# Corollary 3

**Corollary 3**: for `m ≥ 3` and positive even `n` with
`2^m < n ≤ 2^(m+1)`, if `n` is of one of the four forms in
`twoComplexityAddTwoForm`, then `‖n‖₂ = m + 2`.
