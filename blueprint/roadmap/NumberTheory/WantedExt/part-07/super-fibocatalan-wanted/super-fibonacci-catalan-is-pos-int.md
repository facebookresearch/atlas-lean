# Corollary 5

**Corollary 5** (super FiboCatalan half): the super
FiboCatalan numbers
`S(m,n)_F = F_{2m}! F_{2n}!/(F_m! F_n! F_{m+n}!)` are positive
integers (the generalized half is
`MetaMathlibExt.generalizedFibonacciCatalan_isPosInt`).
