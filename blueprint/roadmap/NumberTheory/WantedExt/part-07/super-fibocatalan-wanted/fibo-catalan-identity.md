# Theorem 7

**Theorem 7**:
`F_{2n+1} F_{2n} C_{n,F} - F_{n+1} F_n C_{n+1,F}
= (-1)^n F_n F_{2n+1} F_{2n}!/(F_{n+2}! F_n!)`.
