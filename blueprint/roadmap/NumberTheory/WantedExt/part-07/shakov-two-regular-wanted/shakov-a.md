# Shakov A

The auxiliary sequence of Proposition 9: `a(1) = 1`
and `a(n)` is `2 a(n-1)`, `a(n-1) + 1`,
`2 a(n-1) + 1`, `a(n-1) - 1` according as
`n ≡ 0, 1, 2, 3 (mod 4)`. The value at `0` is a dummy.
