# Theorem 4

**Theorem 4**:
`∑_{m ≥ 0} τ(m^2 + 1) x^m = ∑_{n ≥ 1} x^{s(n)}`, stated
coefficientwise: for every `m`, the fiber
`{n ≥ 1 : s(n) = m}` is finite with exactly
`τ(m^2 + 1)` elements (in particular `s(n) ≥ 0`).
