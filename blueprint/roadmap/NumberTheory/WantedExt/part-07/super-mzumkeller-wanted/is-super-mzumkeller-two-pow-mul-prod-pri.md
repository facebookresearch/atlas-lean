# Corollary 2 (ii)

**Corollary 2 (ii)**: `2^k·(product of `r ≥ 3` distinct
odd primes)` is super-m-Zumkeller for every `k` (the proof's
`τ(n) = (k+1)2^r` forces the `p_i` to be odd).
