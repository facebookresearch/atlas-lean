# Theorem 2

Statement of Theorem 2:

There is an effectively computable absolute constant `k₀` such
that if `k ≥ k₀` is a positive integer, then any `Solution` with prime exponent `l` satisfies
either `y = 0` or `d = 0` or `l ≤ exp(10 ^ k)`.
