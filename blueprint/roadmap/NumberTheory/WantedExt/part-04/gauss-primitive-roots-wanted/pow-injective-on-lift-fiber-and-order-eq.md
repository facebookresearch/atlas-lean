# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4**: for `n` coprime to both the odd prime `p` and
`ord_{p²}(a)`, the power map `x ↦ x^n` is injective on `P_a`,
maps `P_a` onto `P_{a^n}`, and preserves the order of every
element of `P_a`.
