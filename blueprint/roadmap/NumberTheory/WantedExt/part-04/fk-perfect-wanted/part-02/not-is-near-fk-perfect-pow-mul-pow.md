# Theorem 20

**Theorem 20**: there is no near `F_k`-perfect number
of the form `p₁^a p₂^b` (distinct odd primes, `k > 1`,
`a, b ≥ 1`) when `a + b` is odd or both `a` and `b` are
odd.
