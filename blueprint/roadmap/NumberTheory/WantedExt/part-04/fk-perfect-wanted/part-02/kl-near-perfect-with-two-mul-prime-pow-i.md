# Theorem 14

**Theorem 14**, part 2: if `2p^a` (`p` an odd prime)
is `[k,ℓ]`-near-perfect with divisor `d` and `k > 1`, then
`p² ∣ 1 + 2^k`, and `a` is even if and only if `d` is odd.
