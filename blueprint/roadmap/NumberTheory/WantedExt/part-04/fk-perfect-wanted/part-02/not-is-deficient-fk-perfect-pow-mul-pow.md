# Theorem 19

**Theorem 19**: there is no deficient `F_k`-perfect
number of the form `p₁^a p₂^b` (distinct odd primes,
`k > 1`, `a, b ≥ 1`) when `a + b` is odd or both `a` and
`b` are odd.
