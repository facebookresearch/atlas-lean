# Theorem 10

**Theorem 10**: let `n` be a positive odd integer
and `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` a prime. Then the geometric sum
`1 + p^n + p^{2n} + ... + p^{(p-1)n}` is the product of
Pythagorean primes, equivalently the sum of two squares.
