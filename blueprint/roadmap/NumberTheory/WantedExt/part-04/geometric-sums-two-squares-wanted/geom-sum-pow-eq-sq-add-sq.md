# Corollary 11

**Corollary 11**: let `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` be a prime,
`n` a positive odd integer, and `q = p^k` for some
positive integer `k`. Then there are integers `a, b`
with `1 + p^n + p^{2n} + ... + p^{(q-1)n} = a^2 + b^2`.
