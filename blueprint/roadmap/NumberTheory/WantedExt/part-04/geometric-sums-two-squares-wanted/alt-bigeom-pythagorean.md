# Theorem 6

**Theorem 6**: let `x, y` be integers with
`gcd(x, y) = 1` and `n > 0`. Then
`x^{4n} - x^{4n-2} y^2 + ... - x^2 y^{4n-2} + y^{4n}` is
the product of Pythagorean primes, equivalently the sum
of the squares of two relatively prime natural numbers.
