# Corollary 8

**Corollary 8**: let `p ≡ 1 (mod 4)` be a prime, `n`
a positive odd integer, and `q = p^k` for some positive
integer `k`. Then there are integers `a, b` with
`1 - p^n + p^{2n} - ... + p^{(q-1)n} = a^2 + b^2`.
