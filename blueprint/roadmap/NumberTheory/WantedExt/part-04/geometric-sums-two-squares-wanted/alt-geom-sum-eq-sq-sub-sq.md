# Proposition 9

**Proposition 9**: for each positive odd integer
`n` and each prime `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, there are integers
`a, b` with
`1 - p^n + p^{2n} - ... + p^{(p-1)n} = a^2 - b^2`.
