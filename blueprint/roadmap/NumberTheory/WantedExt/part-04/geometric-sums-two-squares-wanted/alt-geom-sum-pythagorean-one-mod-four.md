# Theorem 7

**Theorem 7**: let `n` be a natural number and
`p ≡ 1 (mod 4)` a prime. Then the alternating geometric
sum `1 - p^n + p^{2n} - ... + p^{(p-1)n}` is the product
of Pythagorean primes, equivalently the sum of two
squares.
