# Cheng–Kinlaw–Nguyen reciprocal-sum bounds

**Cheng–Kinlaw–Nguyen reciprocal-sum bounds.** The sum of the
reciprocals of all even pseudoprimes lies strictly between `0.000011`
and `0.0059`.
