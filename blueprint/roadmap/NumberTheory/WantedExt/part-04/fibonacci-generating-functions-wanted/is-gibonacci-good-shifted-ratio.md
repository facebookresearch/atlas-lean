# Is Gibonacci Good shifted ratio

Ratios of the Gibonacci sequence `H` with `H_0 = -b` and `H_1 = a`
are good for `(a, b)` whenever the denominator is nonzero. The paper
indexes `H` by all integers; this statement covers `n ∈ ℕ`.
