# Is Gibonacci Good fib div

Consecutive Fibonacci ratios are good: `F_n / F_{n+1}` is good for
every `(a, b)`. The paper allows every integer `n ≠ -1` with the
two-sided Fibonacci extension; this statement covers `n ∈ ℕ`.
