# Lemma 21

**Lemma 21**: the `k`-th forward difference with step
`r` of `y ↦ yⁿ` is `∑_{j=0}^k (-1)^{k-j} C(k,j) (x + rj)ⁿ`
(paper: nonzero complex `r`, positive `k`; the identity
holds for all real `r` and all `k`).
