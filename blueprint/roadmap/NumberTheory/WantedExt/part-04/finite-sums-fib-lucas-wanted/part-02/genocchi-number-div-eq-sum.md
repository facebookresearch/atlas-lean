# Theorem 22

**Theorem 22**: `Gₙ/n = ∑_{j=0}^{n-1} (-1)ʲ j!/2ʲ
S(n-1, j)` for `n ≥ 1`, with `S` the Stirling numbers of
the second kind, as a rational identity.
