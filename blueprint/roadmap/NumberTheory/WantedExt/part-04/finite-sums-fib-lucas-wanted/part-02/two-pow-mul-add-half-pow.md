# Theorem 18

**Theorem 18** (corrected multinomial): for `q ≥ 1`
and every real `x`, `2^q (x + q/2)ᵐ` is the sum over
`n ≤ m` and `j₁+⋯+j_q+ℓ₁+⋯+ℓ_q = n` of
`(√5/2)^{ℓ₁+⋯+ℓ_q} · (n!/(∏jᵢ!∏ℓᵢ!)) · C(m,n) ·
E_{ℓ₁}⋯E_{ℓ_q} · L_{j₁}⋯L_{j_q} · x^{m-n}`.
