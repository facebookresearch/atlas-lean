# Corollary 17

**Corollary 17** (corrected multinomial): the `q = 2`,
`x = √5` case of Theorem 15, whose sum is `1` since
`√5 + 2β = 1`; with the paper's displayed multinomial the
sum at `m = 2` is `8/3`, not `1`.
