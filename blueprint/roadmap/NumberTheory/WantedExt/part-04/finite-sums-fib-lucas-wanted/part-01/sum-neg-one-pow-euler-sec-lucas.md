# Corollary 6

**Corollary 6**: `∑_{n=0}^m ∑_{k=0}^n (-1)ⁿ √5ᵏ /
2^{k+m-n} C(n,k) C(m,n) Eₖ L_{n-k} = 0`, stated for
`m ≥ 1` (at `m = 0` the sum is `2`).
