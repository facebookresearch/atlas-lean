# Theorem 5

**Theorem 5**: `(x + 1/2)ᵐ = (1/2) ∑_{n=0}^m
∑_{k=0}^n (√5/2)ᵏ C(n,k) C(m,n) x^{m-n} Eₖ L_{n-k}` for
every real `x`, with `Eₖ` the Euler (secant) numbers.
