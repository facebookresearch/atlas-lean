# Corollary 12

**Corollary 12** (corrected): the `x = -β`
specialization of corrected Theorem 11,
`∑_{n=0}^m C(m,n) cₙ (-β)^{m-n} = 0` for `m ≥ 1`; as
printed (with `β^{m-n}` and without the correction term in
`cₙ`) the sum at `m = 2` is `-10 + 4√5`, not `0`.
