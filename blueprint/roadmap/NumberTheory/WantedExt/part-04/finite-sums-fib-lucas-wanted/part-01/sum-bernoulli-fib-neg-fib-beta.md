# Corollary 2

**Corollary 2** (corrected): with the factor
`(-β)^{m-n}` of the `x = -β` specialization of Theorem 1,
the double sum is `0` for `m ≥ 1`; as printed (factor
`β⁻ⁿ`) the sum equals `2ᵐ`, not `0`.
