# Gen Lucas Coeff

The corrected inner coefficient of Theorem 11:
`∑_{k=0}^n √5ᵏ C(n,k) Gₖ L_{n-k+1}/(n-k+1) +
2√5^{n+1} G_{n+1}/(n+1)`, where the last term is the
contribution of the `2/D` term that the printed proof drops
from `(e^{αD} + e^{βD})/D`.
