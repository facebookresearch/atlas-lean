# Proposition 3.1

**Proposition 3.1**: for every `n`,
`2^n E_n((x + 1) / 2) = ∑_{k=0}^n k! α_k S(n, k, x)`,
where `α_k` are the coefficients of Lemma 2.2 and
`S(n, k, x)` is the generalized Stirling polynomial.
