# Dirichlet Weyl Bound Wanted

Statement of Corollary 1.3:

Suppose `χ` has cubefree conductor `q`. Then
`|L(1/2 + it, χ)| ≪ q ^ (1/6 + ε) * (1 + |t|) ^ (1/6 + ε)`.
