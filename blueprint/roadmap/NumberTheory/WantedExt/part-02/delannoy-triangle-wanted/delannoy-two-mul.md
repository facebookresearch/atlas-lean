# Theorem 1

**Theorem 1**: for every prime `p ≥ 5`,
`C(2p, p)_{[2]} ≡ 3 + 2p q_p(2) - p^2 q_p(2)^2` and
`C(2p-1, p-1)_{[2]}
  ≡ 1 + 2p q_p(2) - p^2 q_p(2)^2 (mod p^3)`, where
`q_p(2) = (2^{p-1} - 1) / p` is the Fermat quotient.
