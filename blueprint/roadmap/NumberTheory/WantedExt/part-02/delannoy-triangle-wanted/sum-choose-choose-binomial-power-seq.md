# Lemma 13

**Lemma 13**: for every integer `k ≥ 0`,
`∑_{j=0}^k C(k, j) C(k+j, k+1) = (D_{k+1} - 3 D_k) / 4`,
where `D_k` is the central Delannoy number (stated in
the multiplied-out form over `ℤ`).
