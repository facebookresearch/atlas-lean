# Theorem 2 b

Statement of Theorem 2(b):

Let `ψ : ℕ → ℝ≥0`, `𝒦` and `ψ⋆` be as above. Then, if `∑ ψ⋆ (q)` diverges, `𝒦` has Lebesgue
measure `1`.
