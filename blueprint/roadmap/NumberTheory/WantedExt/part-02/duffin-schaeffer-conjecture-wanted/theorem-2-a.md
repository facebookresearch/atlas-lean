# Theorem 2 a

Statement of Theorem 2(a):

Let `ψ : ℕ → ℝ≥0`, `𝒦` and `ψ⋆` be as above. Then, if `∑ ψ⋆ (q)` converges, `𝒦` has Lebesgue
measure `0`.
