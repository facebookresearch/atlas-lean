# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3**: let `n = p^k * n₁` with `p` prime and
`n₁ > 1`. For `c ≥ 1` and `s` not of the form
`b * p^k + 1` with `0 ≤ b < n₁`, `a_{c * p^k, s} ≡ 0
(mod p)`.
