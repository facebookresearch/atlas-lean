# Ducci Coeff

The coefficient `a_{r,s}` of `x_s` (`1 ≤ s ≤ n`) in
the first entry of the `r`-th iterate of the Ducci map
on `ℤ_m^n`, as an integer count:
`∑_{j ≡ s - 1 (mod n)} C(r, j)`.
