# Exists is PFrobenius Number star of ten le

The `n ≥ 10` case of Theorem 1: for `p = 0, 1, 2`, the
`p`-Frobenius number of the three consecutive star numbers is
`(2n - 11) * S_{n+1} + (p + 3) * n * S_{n+2} - S_n` (written
over `ℤ`).
