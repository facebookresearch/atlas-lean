# Theorem 1 1

Statement of Theorem 1.1:

Let `n` be an integer such that `n ≥ 2`. For any transcendental real number `ξ`, we have
`ω⋆_n(ξ) ≥ a * n`, where `a = 1 / (2 - log 2)`.
