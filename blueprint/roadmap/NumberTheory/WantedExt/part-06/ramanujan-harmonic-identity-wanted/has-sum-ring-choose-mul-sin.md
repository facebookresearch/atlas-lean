# Lemma 1

**Lemma 1**: Ramanujan's generalized-binomial sine series.
For real `a`, `N ≥ 0`, and `|θ| ≤ π / 2`, the series
`∑' ν, (N choose ν) * sin((a + 2ν)θ)` converges to
`2^N * cos(θ)^N * sin((a + N)θ)`, with real powers.
