# Lemma 4

**Lemma 4**: Gould's reciprocal generalized-binomial identity.
For real `x` different from `0, 1, …, N`,
`∑_{ν=0}^{N} (-1)^ν / (x - ν) * (N choose ν)` equals
`(-1)^N / ((x - N) * (x choose N))`.
