# Int sum four polynomial cubes of cube form

Choudhry's Theorem 1: every integer of the form `p ^ 3 + q ^ 3` or
`2 * (p ^ 6 - q ^ 6)` is a sum of four cubes of nonconstant integer
polynomials.
