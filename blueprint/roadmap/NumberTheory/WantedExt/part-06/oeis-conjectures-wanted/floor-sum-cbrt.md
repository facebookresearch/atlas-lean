# Theorem 7

**Theorem 7**: for `n` divisible by `3`, writing
`n = 3m`,
`⌊∑_{i=1}^{10^n} i^{1/3}⌋
  = 3 * 10^{4m} / 4 + 5 * 10^{m-1} - 1`.
