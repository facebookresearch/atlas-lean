# Lemma 3

**Lemma 3**: for every `m ≥ 0`, the coefficient of
`x^m y^m z^m` in `((x + y)(y + z)(z + x))^m` is the
Franel number `f_m`
(`MetaMathlibExt.binomialPowerSeq 3 0 m`).
