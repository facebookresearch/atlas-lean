# A265233

OEIS A265233, defined over `ℚ` by the recurrence of
the paper's Theorem 1(2) with its initial values
`a_0 = 1`, `a_1 = 1`, `a_2 = 7`, `a_3 = 56`: for
`N ≥ 4`, `N^2 a_N = (19N^2 - 19N + 6) a_{N-1}
- 96(N-1)^2 a_{N-2} + 144(N-1)(N-2) a_{N-3}`.
