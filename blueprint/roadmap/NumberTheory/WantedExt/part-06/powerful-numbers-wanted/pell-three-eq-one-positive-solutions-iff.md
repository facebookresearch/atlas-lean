# Lemma 5

**Lemma 5**: the positive integer solutions of
`x^2 - 3*y^2 = 1` are exactly the coordinates of
`(2 + √3)^k` for `k ≥ 1`.  The coordinates begin
`(x₁, y₁) = (2, 1)` and `(x₂, y₂) = (7, 4)` and satisfy the
second-order recursion `uₖ₊₂ = 4*uₖ₊₁ - uₖ`.
