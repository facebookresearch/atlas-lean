# Lemma 2

**Lemma 2**: for coprime `m n > 1` and a prime divisor `q` of
`λ(m*n)`, one has `h_q(m*n) ≥ h_q(m) * h_q(n)`, with strict
inequality exactly under the three displayed `Δ_q` conditions.
