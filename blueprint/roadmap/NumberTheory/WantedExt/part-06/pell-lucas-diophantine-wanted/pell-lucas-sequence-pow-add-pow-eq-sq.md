# Theorem 2

**Theorem 2**: the Diophantine equation
`Q_n^x + Q_{n+1}^x = y^2`, where `Q_n` is the `n`-th
Pell–Lucas number, has as its only solution in
nonnegative integers `(n, x, y) = (1, 1, 2)`.
