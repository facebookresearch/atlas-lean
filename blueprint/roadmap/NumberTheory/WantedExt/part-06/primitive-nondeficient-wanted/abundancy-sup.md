# Abundancy Sup

The paper's `H(n) = ∏_{p ∣ n} p/(p-1)`, in `ℚ`. The paper
defines `H` for positive `n`; at `n = 0` the empty product makes
this `1`, and every use below assumes `n ≠ 0`.
