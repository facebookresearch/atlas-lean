# Lemma

**Lemma** (Grün): if `n` is non-deficient with least prime
factor `p₁` and `k` distinct prime factors, then
`(3/2) p₁ - 2 < k`, and if `p₁ ≥ 5` then `p₁ + 2 ≤ k`.
