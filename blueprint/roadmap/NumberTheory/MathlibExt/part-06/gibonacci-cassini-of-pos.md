# Gibonacci Cassini

Generalized Cassini identity for Gibonacci numbers: for `n ≥ 1`,
`G (n+1) * G (n-1) - G n ^ 2 = (-1)^n * (G₁^2 - G₀*G₁ - G₀^2)`.
