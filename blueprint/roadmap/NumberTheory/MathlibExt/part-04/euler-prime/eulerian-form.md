# Eulerian Form

Full witnessed Eulerian form of an odd perfect number:
perfectness, oddness, primality of `q`, the factorization
`N = q ^ k * n ^ 2`, both congruences `q ≡ 1 (mod 4)`,
`k ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, and coprimality of `q` and `n`.
