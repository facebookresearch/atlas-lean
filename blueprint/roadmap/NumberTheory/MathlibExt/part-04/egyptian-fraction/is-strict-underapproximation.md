# Is Strict Underapproximation

A strict underapproximation by distinct unit fractions, presented with
strictly increasing denominators. This is the condition
`(a₁,…,aₙ) ∈ 𝓔ₙ^<` and `∑ 1/aᵢ < λ` from lines 113–120, with `n ≥ 1`
and `λ > 0`.
