# Ramanujan part1 ch2 entry4 general

Ramanujan's Entry 4 for every `n`: `∑ k ∈ [1, n], 1 / (n + k)` plus
`∑ k ∈ [0, n], 1 / (2n + 2k + 1)` equals `1 + 2 ∑ k ∈ [1, n], 1 / ((4k)³ - 4k)`. At `n = 0` both
sides are `1`. `ramanujan_part1_ch2_entry4` is the source-shaped form.
