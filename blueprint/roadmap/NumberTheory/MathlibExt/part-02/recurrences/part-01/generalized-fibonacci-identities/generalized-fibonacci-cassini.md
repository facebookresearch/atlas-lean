# Generalized Fibonacci cassini

Cassini-type identity `W n W (n + 2) - W (n + 1) ^ 2 = (-1) ^ n Δ` with
`Δ = W 0 ^ 2 + p W 0 W 1 - W 1 ^ 2`, a specialization of
`horadam_generalized_catalan_identity`.
