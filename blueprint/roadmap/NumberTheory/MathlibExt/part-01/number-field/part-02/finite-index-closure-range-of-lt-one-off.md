# Units

A family of units that is `< 1` off the diagonal generates a subgroup of finite
index: if `w (u v) < 1` whenever `w ≠ v`, then the subgroup generated by the range
of `u` has finite index in `(𝓞 F)ˣ`.

The logarithmic images form a column strictly diagonally dominant system — the
off-diagonal components are negative while each column sum is positive — hence
are linearly independent by `det_ne_zero_of_sum_col_lt_diag`. Maximal rank is
transported along `equivFinRank` and concluded with
`isMaxRank_iff_closure_finiteIndex` and `Subgroup.finiteIndex_of_le`.
