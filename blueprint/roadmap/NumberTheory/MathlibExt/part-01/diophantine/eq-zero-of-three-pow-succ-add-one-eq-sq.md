# Power Square

If `3 ^ (y + 1) + 1` is a square, then `y = 0`.

This is Lemma 2.1 of M. Buosi, G. S. Ferreira, and A. L. P. Porto,
arXiv:2308.10043v1, `main.tex`, lines 128–131. The paper uses natural numbers including zero.
