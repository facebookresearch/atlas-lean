# Cassels Embedding

Cassels' embedding theorem: the subring generated by a finite subset of a
characteristic-zero field embeds injectively into `ℤ_[p]` for some prime `p ≥ 3`.

J. W. S. Cassels, "An embedding theorem for fields", Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 14
(1976), 193–198.
