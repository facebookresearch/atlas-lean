# Arakawa kaneko duality

Arakawa–Kaneko duality for poly-Bernoulli numbers:
`Bₙ⁽⁻ᵏ⁾ = Bₖ⁽⁻ⁿ⁾` for `n, k : ℕ`, with `polyBernoulli` as above.
