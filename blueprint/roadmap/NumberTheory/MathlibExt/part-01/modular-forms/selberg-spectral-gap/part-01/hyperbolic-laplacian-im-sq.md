# Hyperbolic Laplacian im sq

Non-holomorphic sanity check: for `f(z) = (im z)^2`, the hyperbolic
Laplacian is `-2 (im z)^2`, not zero. With complex derivatives this
computation would collapse by Cauchy--Riemann.
