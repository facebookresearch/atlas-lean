# Isobaric Weight

Weighted isobaric sum `∑ j, (j + 1) * d j` of an exponent vector.

This is the condition `∑_{j=1}^k j α_j = n` from the source, with `Fin k`
indices shifted by one. Source statement: `jis_975c9f8263707d64d52bd0a3`.
