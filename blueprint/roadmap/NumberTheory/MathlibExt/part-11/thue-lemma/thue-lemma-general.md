# Thue lemma general

Thue's lemma without a lower bound on `a`: for `n > 1` and `a` coprime to `n` there are
nonzero `x`, `y` with `|x|, |y| < √n` and `x ≡ a * y [ZMOD n]`. `thue_lemma` is the
source-shaped form.
