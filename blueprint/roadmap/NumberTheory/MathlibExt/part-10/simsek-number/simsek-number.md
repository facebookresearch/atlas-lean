# Simsek Number

Simsek numbers `y_1(n, k; lam)` in explicit finite-sum form:
   `(1 / k !) * ∑ j ∈ Finset.range (k + 1), C(k, j) * j ^ n * lam ^ j`.
   Source: equation (snmf1) of https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL26/Oussi/oussi9.tex;
