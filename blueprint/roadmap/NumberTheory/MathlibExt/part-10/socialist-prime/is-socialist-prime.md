# Is Socialist Prime

A natural number `p` is a socialist prime iff `p` is prime, `5 < p`,
and the factorials `k! % p` for `k ∈ [2, p-1]` are pairwise distinct.
Trudgian, arXiv:1310.6403v2, line 24.
