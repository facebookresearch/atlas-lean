# Quadratic Gauss Sum5

Quadratic Gauss sum `g(k; 5)` for `k : ℤ`: the sum over `n = 0, ..., 4` of
`Complex.exp (2 * Real.pi * Complex.I * k * n ^ 2 / 5)`.
