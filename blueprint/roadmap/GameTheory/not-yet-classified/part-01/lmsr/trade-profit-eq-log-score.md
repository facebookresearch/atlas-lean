# LMSR trade profit equals a log-score difference

For a finite nonempty set of outcomes, a liquidity parameter $b > 0$ and share vectors $q, q'$, the profit of moving from $q$ to $q'$ when outcome $i$ occurs, $(q'_i - q_i) - (C_b(q') - C_b(q))$, equals $b(\log p_i(q') - \log p_i(q))$, where $p_i$ is the LMSR price of outcome $i$.
