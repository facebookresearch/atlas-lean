# Existence of EF1 allocations

Let finitely many agents, at least one, have nonnegative valuations $v_i(g) \ge 0$ of finitely many goods, with bundles valued additively. For every set $S$ of goods there is an allocation of $S$ that is envy-free up to one good: for all agents $i$ and $j$ with $j$'s bundle nonempty, some good can be removed from $j$'s bundle so that $i$ values the remainder at most as much as $i$'s own bundle.
