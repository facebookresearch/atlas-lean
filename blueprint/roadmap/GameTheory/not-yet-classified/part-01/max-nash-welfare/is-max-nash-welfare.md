# Maximum Nash welfare allocation

For finitely many agents with valuations $v_i$ over finitely many goods, call an agent positive under an allocation when its bundle has positive value. An allocation $A$ has maximum Nash welfare when, for every allocation $B$, $B$ has at most as many positive agents as $A$, and if $B$ has the same number, then the product of the positive agents' bundle values under $B$ is at most that under $A$.
