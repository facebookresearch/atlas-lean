# Nisan-Ronen lower bound (factor 2)

For every $c < 2$ there is a number of tasks $m$ such that, on two machines with $m$ tasks, no truthful mechanism is a $c$-approximation for the makespan. Here a mechanism is an allocation rule $A$ with payments $P$; it is truthful when, for every nonnegative processing-time profile, no machine gains (payment minus true load) by unilaterally reporting another nonnegative row; and $A$ is a $c$-approximation when on every nonnegative profile its makespan is at most $c$ times the optimal makespan.
