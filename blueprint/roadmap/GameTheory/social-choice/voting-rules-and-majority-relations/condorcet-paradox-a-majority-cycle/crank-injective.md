# Crank injective

For each of the three voters `i` of the cyclic profile on alternatives `0, 1, 2`, the rank function `a ↦ (a - i) mod 3` is injective.

Equivalently, for every voter `i ∈ Fin 3` and alternatives `a, b ∈ Fin 3`, if the cyclic ranks `(a - i) mod 3` and `(b - i) mod 3` are equal then `a = b`.
