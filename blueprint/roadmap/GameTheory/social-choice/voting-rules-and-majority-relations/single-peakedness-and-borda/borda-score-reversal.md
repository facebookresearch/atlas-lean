# Borda score reversal

There are two profiles `P` and `Q` of five voters on three alternatives `0, 1, 2` in which every voter compares `0` and `1` the same way in `P` as in `Q`, yet `1` has a lower Borda score than `0` in `P` while `0` has a lower Borda score than `1` in `Q`. The Borda ranking of `0` and `1` thus depends on the position of the irrelevant alternative `2`.
