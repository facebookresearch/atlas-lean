# Price envy-freeness up to one good

Let $X$ be an allocation of goods and $p$ a price vector. The pair $(X, p)$ is price envy-free up to one good (pEF1) if for all agents $i, j$ with $X_j \ne \emptyset$ there is a good $g \in X_j$ with

$$
p(X_i) \ge p(X_j \setminus \{g\}).
$$

Equivalently, the poorest agent in terms of spending, after removing the most expensive good from every other bundle, spends at least as much as everyone else.
