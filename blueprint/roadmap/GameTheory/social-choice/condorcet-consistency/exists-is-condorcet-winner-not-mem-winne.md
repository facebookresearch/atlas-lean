# Borda is not Condorcet-consistent

The Borda rule can fail to elect the Condorcet winner. In a profile of five voters on alternatives `0, 1, 2`, three voters rank `0` above `1` above `2` and two voters rank `1` above `2` above `0`. Then `0` is the Condorcet winner, beating each other alternative by `3` votes to `2`, but the Borda scores are `6` for `0` and `7` for `1`, so `0` is not a Borda winner.
