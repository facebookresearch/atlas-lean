# Optimal metric distortion of randomized voting

How much welfare can a voting rule lose when it sees only rankings, assuming voters and candidates lie in a common metric space?

## What would settle it

Define a randomized rule on ordinal profiles and prove a sharp worst-case bound on expected total distance relative to the best candidate, with a matching lower bound against every such rule. Account for zero optimum and tied rankings explicitly.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Shah (preprint) proves distortion at most 2.1441 for a family of randomized rules and records the universal lower bound of about 2.1126 for all randomized rules, which refuted the earlier conjecture that 2 is optimal.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
