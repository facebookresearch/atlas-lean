# Maximum size of Condorcet domains

Determine the maximum size of a Condorcet domain on n alternatives: a set of rankings for which every odd-voter profile drawn from it has a transitive majority relation.

## What would settle it

Construct a domain and verify its defining property, then prove a matching upper bound. Exact small-n maximality requires a sound exhaustive certificate or a structural theorem, not just a large example found by search.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Karpov, Markström, Riis and Zhou improve the lower bounds and record that the exact maximum is known only for n ≤ 8.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
