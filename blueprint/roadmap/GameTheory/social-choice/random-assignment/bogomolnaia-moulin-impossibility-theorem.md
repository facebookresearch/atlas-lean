# Bogomolnaia–Moulin impossibility theorem

For $n \ge 4$ agents and objects, no random assignment rule is simultaneously sd-efficient, satisfies equal treatment of equals, and is sd-strategy-proof.

## Proof sketch

For four agents, follow two chains of single-agent adjacent swaps among three rankings: strategy-proofness keeps certain entries fixed along each chain and equal treatment plus efficiency pin them down at both ends, forcing one probability to equal both $1/3$ and $1/4$. Larger $n$ reduces to $n = 4$ by appending objects ranked last by everyone.
