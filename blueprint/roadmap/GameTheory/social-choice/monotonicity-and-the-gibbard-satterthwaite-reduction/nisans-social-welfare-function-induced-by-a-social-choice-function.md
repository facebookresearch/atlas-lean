# Nisan's social welfare function induced by a social choice function

Given a strategy-proof social choice function $f$ onto $A$ with finitely many voters, define the social ranking at a profile $P$ by declaring $a$ socially at least as good as $b$ when $f(P^{\{a,b\}}) = a$, where $P^{\{a,b\}}$ is obtained from $P$ by moving $a$ and $b$ to the top of every voter's ranking while preserving their relative order and the order of all other alternatives.
