# Nisan's induced social welfare function is Pareto, IIA, and inherits dictators

For a strategy-proof social choice function $f$ onto $A$ with finitely many voters (and at least one voter or at least two alternatives), the induced social welfare function satisfies unanimity (weak Pareto) and independence of irrelevant alternatives, and a voter is a dictator of the induced social welfare function if and only if it is a dictator of $f$. Combined with Arrow's theorem this yields the Gibbard–Satterthwaite theorem for at least three alternatives.

## Proof sketch

If all voters rank $a$ over $b$, then $a$ is top in every lifted ranking and ontoness plus monotonicity give $f = a$. The lifted profile depends only on the relative order of $a$ and $b$, giving IIA. Dictatorships correspond under the construction.
