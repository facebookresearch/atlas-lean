# Nisan's induced relation is a strict social ranking

For a strategy-proof social choice function onto $A$ with finitely many voters, the induced relation is total, antisymmetric and transitive at every profile, so it defines a strict ranking of $A$.

## Proof sketch

By Maskin monotonicity and ontoness, $f(P^{\{a,b\}}) \in \{a, b\}$. For transitivity, lift $\{a, b, c\}$ to the top and use monotonicity to compare the outcome there with the pairwise outcomes.
