# Ordinal single-peaked preferences on an interval

A weak order on a closed interval $[a, b]$ is *single-peaked* with peak $p$ if $p$ is its unique most-preferred point and preference strictly decreases as one moves away from $p$ on either side (comparisons across the peak are unrestricted). A *rule* maps profiles of single-peaked preferences of finitely many agents to points of $[a, b]$; it is *strategy-proof* if no agent obtains a strictly preferred outcome by misreporting, *unanimous* if a common peak is chosen, and *Pareto optimal* if no point is weakly preferred by all and strictly by some.
