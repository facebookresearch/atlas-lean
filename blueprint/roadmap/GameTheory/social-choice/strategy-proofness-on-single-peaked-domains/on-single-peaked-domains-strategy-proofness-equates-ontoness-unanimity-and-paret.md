# On single-peaked domains, strategy-proofness equates ontoness, unanimity and Pareto optimality

For a strategy-proof rule on single-peaked preferences over $[a, b]$ with finitely many agents (nonempty for the Pareto statements), the rule is onto if and only if it is unanimous, if and only if it is Pareto optimal. Weak and strong Pareto optimality coincide on this domain and are characterized by the outcome lying between the smallest and largest reported peaks.

## Proof sketch

If $x$ is in the range, an agent can force $x$ from the unanimous profile at $x$ by strategy-proofness, giving unanimity. If the outcome lay outside the hull of the peaks, all agents would prefer the nearest extreme peak, and an agent could manipulate towards it.
