# Anonymous strategy-proof rules are phantom medians

A rule on ordinal single-peaked preferences over $[a, b]$ with $n \ge 1$ agents is strategy-proof, onto and anonymous if and only if there are $n - 1$ fixed phantom points $y_1, \dots, y_{n-1} \in [a, b]$ such that the outcome is the median of the $n$ reported peaks together with the $n - 1$ phantoms.

## Proof sketch

Anonymity makes the coalition parameters depend only on coalition size; setting $y_k = \beta_S$ for $|S| = k$ turns the generalized median formula into the median with phantoms.
