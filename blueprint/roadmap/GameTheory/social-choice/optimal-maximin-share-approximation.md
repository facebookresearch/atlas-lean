# Optimal maximin-share approximation

Determine the best α such that every additive-goods instance admits an allocation giving each agent at least α times their maximin share.

## What would settle it

Define $\mathrm{MMS}_i = \max_{\text{partitions}} \min_{\text{bundles } B} v_i(B)$. Prove a universal α guarantee with a matching impossibility family. A finite counterexample requires certifying the MMS values as well as failure of all candidate allocations.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Exact maximin-share allocations need not exist under additive valuations. Ezra and Garbuz (preprint) improve the impossibility bounds and record that the best existence guarantee is 7/9 of the maximin share; the optimal α is not determined there.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
