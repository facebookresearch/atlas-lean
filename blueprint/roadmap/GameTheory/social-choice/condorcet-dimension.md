# Condorcet dimension

Determine the least k such that every election with strict rankings has a Condorcet winning set of at most k candidates.

## What would settle it

A Condorcet winning set is a set S of candidates such that for every candidate x outside S, fewer than half the voters rank x above every member of S; the same voters must prefer x to all of S, not separate majorities for each member. Find the least such universal k and prove a matching lower bound by an election in which every set of k − 1 candidates fails.

## Status

The cited sources, in the versions cited, record the partial results below; they do not pose the question in this form, and this page does not certify its present status.

Charikar, Ramakrishnan and Wang expose a recent proof that every election has a Condorcet winning set of at most five candidates. Lower bounds on the least number needed are not verified from the sources cited here and belong to the expansion.

This page records the research target above and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
