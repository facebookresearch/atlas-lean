# GreedyAV satisfies justified representation

In an approval-based committee election with $n$ voters and committee size $k$, a committee $W$ satisfies
justified representation if for every group $N^*$ of at least $n/k$ voters whose approval sets have a common
candidate, some voter in $N^*$ approves some member of $W$. The greedy approval voting rule, which repeatedly adds
a candidate approved by the largest number of voters none of whose approved candidates is yet in the committee,
always outputs a committee satisfying justified representation, in polynomial time.
