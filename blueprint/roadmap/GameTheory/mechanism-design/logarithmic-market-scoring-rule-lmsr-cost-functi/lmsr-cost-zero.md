# Lmsr Cost zero

For a finite set of `n` outcomes and every real `b`, the LMSR cost `b · log ∑ⱼ exp(q j / b)` of the zero share vector is `b · log n`. No outcome is required: for `n = 0` the sum is empty and both sides are `0`, with the convention `log 0 = 0`.

At zero liquidity, $b = 0$, both sides are $0$: by the convention of the [LMSR cost function](lmsr-cost.md), every quotient $q_j/b$ is $0$ and the cost is $0$.
