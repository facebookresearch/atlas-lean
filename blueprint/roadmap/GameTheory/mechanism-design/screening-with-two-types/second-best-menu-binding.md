# Low-type participation and high-type incentive constraints bind

For all real parameters `θ_L`, `θ_H`, `p_L`, `p_H`, the second-best menu
`q_L = θ_L - (p_H / p_L)(θ_H - θ_L)`, `t_L = θ_L q_L`, `q_H = θ_H`,
`t_H = θ_H q_H - (θ_H - θ_L) q_L` (with `p_H / p_L` read as `0` when `p_L = 0`) makes the low type's
participation constraint and the high type's incentive constraint bind: `θ_L q_L - t_L = 0` and
`θ_H q_L - t_L = θ_H q_H - t_H`.
