# Roberts' theorem for neutral rules

With finitely many agents, at least three outcomes and unrestricted valuations, every neutral weakly monotone social choice rule (one commuting with permutations of the outcomes) maximizes a pure weighted welfare $\sum_i w_i b_i(a)$ with nonnegative weights summing to $1$; the additive constants vanish. Zero weights must be allowed, to cover dictatorships.

## Proof sketch

Neutrality forces the affine constants $c(a)$ to be equal across outcomes, and a neutral rule is surjective, so Roberts' theorem applies on the full range.
