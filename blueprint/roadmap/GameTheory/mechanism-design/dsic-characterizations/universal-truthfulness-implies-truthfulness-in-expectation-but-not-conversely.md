# Universal truthfulness implies truthfulness in expectation, but not conversely

Every universally truthful randomized mechanism with summable utilities (for instance finite support or bounded utilities) is truthful in expectation. Conversely, a fair coin flip between two deterministic mechanisms, one of which is not DSIC, can be truthful in expectation without being universally truthful.

## Proof sketch

Average the incentive inequalities over the support. For the separation, combine a mechanism with an incentive defect and one with a compensating strict incentive, so the expected utility is maximized by the truth.
