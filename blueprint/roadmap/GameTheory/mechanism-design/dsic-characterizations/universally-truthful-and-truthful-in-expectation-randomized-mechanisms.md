# Universally truthful and truthful-in-expectation randomized mechanisms

A *randomized mechanism* is a probability distribution over deterministic direct mechanisms with common agents and outcome space. It is *universally truthful* on domains $V$ if every mechanism in its support is DSIC on $V$, and *truthful in expectation* if for every agent truthful reporting maximizes expected quasilinear utility over the distribution, for all reports of the others.
