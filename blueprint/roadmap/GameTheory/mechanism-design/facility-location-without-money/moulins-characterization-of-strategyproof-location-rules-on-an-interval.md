# Moulin's characterization of strategyproof location rules on an interval

On a compact interval $[a, b]$ with $a < b$ (or any metric space isometric to one) and finitely many agents with distance costs, a deterministic facility-location mechanism is strategyproof and onto if and only if it is a generalized median voter scheme; every such mechanism is group strategyproof in the strong sense. It is moreover anonymous if and only if it is a phantom median rule with $n - 1$ phantoms.

## Proof sketch

Strategyproof onto rules are peaks-only and their option sets are intervals; recording, for each coalition, the outcome when exactly that coalition reports $b$ and the rest report $a$ yields the phantom parameters, and strategyproofness forces the min-max formula. Anonymity makes $\alpha_S$ depend only on $|S|$.
