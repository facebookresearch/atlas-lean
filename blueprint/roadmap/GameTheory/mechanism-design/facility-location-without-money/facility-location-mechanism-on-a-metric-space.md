# Facility-location mechanism on a metric space

A (randomized) *facility-location mechanism* maps reported locations $x \in G^\iota$ of finitely many agents in a pseudometric space $G$ to a probability distribution over facility locations; agent $i$'s cost is the expected distance from $x_i$ to the facility. It is *strategyproof* if no agent lowers its cost by misreporting, *group strategyproof* if no coalition can misreport so that all members weakly gain and some strictly gain, and it has *approximation ratio* $\beta$ for social cost (sum of distances) or maximum cost if its expected cost is at most $\beta$ times the optimum on every profile.
