# Extended median voter scheme on a tree

For a finite tree metric graph, an *extended median voter scheme* records, at every point $z$ of the tree and in every direction away from $z$, which coalitions are winning: when the agents located in a branch at $z$ form a winning coalition for that direction, the facility moves into that branch. The schemes are required to be consistent along paths, so that their restriction to every leaf-to-leaf path is a generalized median voter scheme.
