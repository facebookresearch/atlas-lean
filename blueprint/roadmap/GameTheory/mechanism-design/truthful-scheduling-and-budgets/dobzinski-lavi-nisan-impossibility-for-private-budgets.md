# Dobzinski–Lavi–Nisan impossibility for private budgets

For two bidders and positive divisible supply, no deterministic anonymous auction (permuting the two bidders' reports permutes their allocations and payments) is simultaneously feasible, nonnegatively paying, budget feasible, individually rational, Pareto optimal and dominant-strategy incentive compatible when both values and budgets are private information.

## Proof sketch

For equal public budgets, anonymity, individual rationality, incentive compatibility and Pareto optimality determine the mechanism uniquely (a scaled version of Dobzinski, Lavi and Nisan's mechanism M3). A mechanism for private budgets must coincide with it at every pair of equal budgets, and comparing its outcomes for different reported budgets at fixed values exhibits a profitable budget misreport.
