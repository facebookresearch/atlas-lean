# Dobzinski–Lavi–Nisan impossibility for private budgets

For at least two bidders and positive divisible supply, no deterministic auction is simultaneously feasible, nonnegatively paying, budget feasible, individually rational, Pareto optimal and dominant-strategy incentive compatible when both values and budgets are private information.

## Proof sketch

Pareto optimality pins down the allocation when a budget-constrained bidder faces an unconstrained one: the constrained bidder must spend its whole budget. Varying the reported budget then changes the payment without changing the bidder's marginal incentives in a way incompatible with truthfulness.
