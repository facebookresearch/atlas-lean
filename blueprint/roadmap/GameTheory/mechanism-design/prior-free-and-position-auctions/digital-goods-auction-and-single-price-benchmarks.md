# Digital goods auction and single-price benchmarks

In a *digital goods auction* an unlimited supply of a zero-cost good is sold to unit-demand bidders with bids $b_i \ge 0$; a mechanism is a $0$-$1$ single-parameter mechanism and its profit is its total payment. The *optimal single-price profit* is $\mathcal F(b) = \max_k k\, b_{(k)}$, where $b_{(k)}$ is the $k$-th highest bid, and $\mathcal F^{(2)}(b) = \max_{k \ge 2} k\, b_{(k)}$ requires at least two winners. A mechanism is *$\beta$-competitive* if its expected profit is at least $\mathcal F^{(2)}(b)/\beta$ on every bid vector.
