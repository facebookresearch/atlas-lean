# The spherical scoring rule is strictly proper

On a finite outcome set, the spherical scoring rule $S(p, \omega) = p(\omega) / \|p\|_2$ is strictly proper on the whole probability simplex.

## Proof sketch

The expected score is $\langle q, p\rangle / \|p\|$, which by Cauchy–Schwarz is at most $\|q\|$ with equality iff $p$ is a positive multiple of $q$, i.e. $p = q$ on the simplex.
