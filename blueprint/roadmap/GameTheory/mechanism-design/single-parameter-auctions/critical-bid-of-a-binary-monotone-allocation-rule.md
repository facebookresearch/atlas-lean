# Critical bid of a binary monotone allocation rule

In a single-parameter environment with $0$-$1$ allocations, the *critical bid* of bidder $i$ at the others' bids $b_{-i}$ is $\inf\{z \ge 0 : x_i(z, b_{-i}) = 1\}$. The *critical-bid mechanism* charges every winner its critical bid and every loser $0$.
