# Gross-substitutes valuation

A valuation $v$ on bundles of items satisfies *gross substitutes* if for any price vectors $p \le q$ and any bundle $S$ demanded at $p$, there is a bundle demanded at $q$ containing every item $j \in S$ with $p_j = q_j$: raising some prices does not reduce demand for items whose prices are unchanged. Additive, unit-demand and downward-sloping valuations are gross substitutes.
