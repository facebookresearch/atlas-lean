# Single-minded bidders and combinatorial auction mechanisms

A bidder is *single-minded* if it desires a single bundle $S^*$ and has value $v^* \ge 0$ for every bundle containing $S^*$ and $0$ otherwise. A mechanism for single-minded bidders receives declared pairs $(S_i, v_i)$, selects a set of winners with pairwise disjoint bundles, gives each winner exactly its declared bundle and charges payments, losers paying $0$. It is *monotone* if a winner declaring $(S, v)$ also wins with any $(S', v')$ with $S' \subseteq S$ and $v' > v$; its *critical value* for bidder $i$ is the infimum of values with which $i$ wins on its bundle.
