# Characterization of truthful mechanisms for single-minded bidders

A mechanism for single-minded bidders with nonnegative payments and individual rationality is dominant-strategy incentive compatible if and only if it is monotone (in the strict-value sense) and charges every winner its critical value.

## Proof sketch

For a fixed bundle the problem is single-parameter, so Myerson-type arguments apply; declaring a superset bundle never helps under monotonicity because critical values only increase, and declaring a smaller bundle gives zero value.
