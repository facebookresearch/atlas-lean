# Revenue equivalence (in expectation)

Let $Q, P_1, P_2 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$. If $(Q, P_1)$ and $(Q, P_2)$ are
both incentive compatible on $[a, b]$ and $P_1(a) = P_2(a)$, then for every measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$
(for instance a distribution of values)

$$\int_{[a, b]} P_1\,d\mu = \int_{[a, b]} P_2\,d\mu$$

(the one-bidder form of Myerson 1981, Corollary: the seller's expected revenue is determined by the
allocation rule and the utilities of the lowest types).
