# Allocations of a dominant-strategy incentive-compatible auction are monotone

If a single-parameter auction with allocation rule $x$ and payment rule $\mathrm{pay}$ is [dominant-strategy incentive compatible](dsic.md), then for every bidder $i$, every profile $b_{-i}$ of nonnegative bids and all $0 \le z \le z'$,

$$x_i(z, b_{-i}) \le x_i(z', b_{-i}).$$

This is the dominant-strategy form of the monotonicity half of [Myerson's lemma](is-incentive-compatible-on-iff.md).
