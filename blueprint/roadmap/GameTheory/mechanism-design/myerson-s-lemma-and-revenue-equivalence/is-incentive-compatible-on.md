# Incentive compatibility on an interval

A single-parameter mechanism is given by an allocation rule $Q : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a payment rule $P : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$: an agent of value $t$ who reports $s$ gets the utility $t\,Q(s) - P(s)$, and $U(t) = t\,Q(t) - P(t)$ is its truthful utility.
For reals $a, b$, the mechanism $(Q, P)$ is incentive compatible on $[a, b]$ if for all values
$s, t \in [a, b]$ an agent of value $t$ does not gain by reporting $s$:
$t\,Q(s) - P(s) \le t\,Q(t) - P(t)$ (Myerson 1981, (3.5) without revision effects; Hartline and Karlin
2007, Definition 13.5).

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, (3.5).
- J. D. Hartline and A. R. Karlin, "Profit maximization in mechanism design", Chapter 13 of the same volume, Definition 13.5.
