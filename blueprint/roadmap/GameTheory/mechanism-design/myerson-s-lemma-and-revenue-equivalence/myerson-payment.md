# Myerson payment of an allocation rule

For an allocation rule $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ of one bidder, the other bids held fixed, the Myerson payment of the bid $z$ is

$$p(z) = z\, x(z) - \int_0^z x(t)\, dt,$$

with the oriented interval integral (zero when $x$ is not integrable on the interval). It is the [payment formula](payment-formula.md) with zero payment at the lowest type.
