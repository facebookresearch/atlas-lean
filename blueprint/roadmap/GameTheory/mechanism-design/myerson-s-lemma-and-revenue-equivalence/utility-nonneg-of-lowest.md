# Individual rationality from the lowest type

A single-parameter mechanism is given by an allocation rule $Q : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a payment rule $P : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$: an agent of value $t$ who reports $s$ gets the utility $t\,Q(s) - P(s)$, and $U(t) = t\,Q(t) - P(t)$ is its truthful utility.
If $(Q, P)$ is incentive compatible on $[a, b]$, $Q \ge 0$ on $[a, b]$ and the lowest value gets
$U(a) \ge 0$, then $U(t) \ge 0$ for every $t \in [a, b]$ (Myerson 1981, proof of Lemma 2: (3.4) follows
from (4.3) and (4.4)).

## Informally uses

- [Envelope formula](envelope-formula.md): the utility exceeds that of the lowest value by the integral of $Q \ge 0$.

## Sources

- R. B. Myerson, "Optimal auction design", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6(1) (1981), 58–73, proof of Lemma 2.
