# The Myerson payment makes truthful bidding optimal

Let $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be nondecreasing on $[0, \infty)$ and let $p$ be its [Myerson payment](myerson-payment.md). For every valuation $v \ge 0$ and every bid $b \ge 0$,

$$v\, x(b) - p(b) \le v\, x(v) - p(v),$$

so a bidder with a nonnegative valuation maximizes quasilinear utility over nonnegative bids by bidding truthfully.
