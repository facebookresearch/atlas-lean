# Optimal prophet-secretary ratio

Determine the best fraction of a prophet's expected reward obtainable by a stopping rule when independent, known-distribution values arrive in random order. This underpins online selection and posted-price mechanisms.

## What would settle it

Define the minimax ratio over instance-dependent rules that know the distributions and over all admissible independent-value instances. Construct a rule with a matching universal guarantee, and prove a matching upper bound against every rule. Specify information revealed at arrival.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Feng, Jin, Wu and Xiang (preprint) prove a competitive ratio of 0.6902 using only the mean of the maximum, above the previous ratio of 0.688 with full distribution information that they record; they do not determine the optimal ratio.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
