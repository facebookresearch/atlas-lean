# Truthful bidding is weakly dominant in a second-price auction

Let $n \ge 2$. For every bidder $i$, every valuation $v \in \mathbb{R}$ and every bid profile $b \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the [utility](vickrey-utility.md) of $i$ under $b$ is at most its utility under the profile obtained from $b$ by replacing $b_i$ with $v$. Ties are broken toward the least index.
