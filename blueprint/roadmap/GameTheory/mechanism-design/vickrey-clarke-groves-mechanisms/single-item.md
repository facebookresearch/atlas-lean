# Single-item valuations

Let $I$ be a finite set of agents bidding for one item. The alternatives are the possible winners
$a \in I$, and a bid vector $t = (t_i)_{i \in I} \in \mathbb{R}^I$ gives the valuation profile $v^t$ in
which agent $i$ values the alternative $a$ at

$$v^t_i(a) = \begin{cases} t_i & \text{if } a = i, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

(Nisan 2007, §9.3.1 and §9.3.5.1).

## Sources

- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, §9.3.1.
