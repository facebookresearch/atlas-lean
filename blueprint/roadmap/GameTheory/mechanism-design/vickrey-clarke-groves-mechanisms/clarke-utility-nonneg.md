# Individual rationality

Let $I$ be a finite set of agents and $A$ a finite nonempty set of alternatives. Let $f$ be an alternative
rule and $v$ a valuation profile such that $f(v)$ maximizes the welfare at $v$ ($W(v, a) \le W(v, f(v))$ for
every $a$) and all valuations are nonnegative ($v_i(a) \ge 0$ for all $i$ and $a$). Then with the Clarke
pivot payments $p_i$ every truthful agent gets a nonnegative utility: $v_i(f(v)) - p_i(v) \ge 0$ for every
agent $i$ (Nisan 2007, Lemma 9.20, second part).

## Sources

- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, Lemma 9.20.
