# Clarke pivot term

Let $I$ be a finite set of agents and $A$ a finite nonempty set of alternatives. For a valuation profile
$v = (v_i)_{i \in I}$ with $v_i : A \to \mathbb{R}$ and an agent $i$, the Clarke pivot term is the best
welfare the other agents could get, over all alternatives:

$$h_i(v) = \max_{b \in A} \sum_{j \neq i} v_j(b)$$

(Nisan 2007, Definition 9.19).

## Sources

- N. Nisan, "Introduction to mechanism design (for computer scientists)", Chapter 9 of N. Nisan, T. Roughgarden, É. Tardos and V. V. Vazirani (eds.), *Algorithmic Game Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2007, Definition 9.19.
