# Changing one bid changes one valuation

Let $t$ be a bid vector of a set $I$ of agents, $i$ an agent and $s \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $t'$ be $t$ with
$t_i$ replaced by $s$. Replacing, in the single-item profile $v^t$, the valuation of agent $i$ by its
valuation $v^{t'}_i$ gives the single-item profile $v^{t'}$.
