# Is Truthful min Work

For finitely many machines, at least two of them, and finitely many tasks, the MinWork mechanism, which assigns each task to a fastest machine for it and pays that machine the second-lowest reported processing time of the task, is truthful on nonnegative processing times: for every true profile of nonnegative processing times and every machine, reporting any other nonnegative row of processing times does not increase that machine's utility, its payment minus its true load. The statement says nothing about negative reports or negative true times.
