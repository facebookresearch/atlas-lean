# Reflections of finite sets of rationals

Let $A$ and $B$ be finite sets of rational
numbers. $B$ is a reflection of $A$ if there are rationals $a < 0$ and $b$ such that
$B = aA + b = \{ax + b : x \in A\}$.

## Sources
