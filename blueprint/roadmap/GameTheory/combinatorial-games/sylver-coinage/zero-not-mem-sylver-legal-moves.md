# Zero is never a legal move

For every set $M$ of natural numbers, $0$ is not a legal move
of the Sylver Coinage position $M$.

## Sources

- Morgan Boyers, Natalie Burton, Jeff Rushall, James Warner and Tara Zurick, *On Winning Strategies in Sylver Coinage When 4 Has Been Chosen*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article G2, Section 1 (rules of Sylver Coinage, `L(M)`).
