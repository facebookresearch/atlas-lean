# Even replies to 4 and an odd number at least 5 lose

Let $x \ge 5$ be an odd natural
number. For every even legal move $b$ of the position $\{4, x\}$, the position $\{4, x, b\}$ is
an N-position.

## Sources

- Morgan Boyers, Natalie Burton, Jeff Rushall, James Warner and Tara Zurick, *On Winning Strategies in Sylver Coinage When 4 Has Been Chosen*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article G2, Proposition 5.
