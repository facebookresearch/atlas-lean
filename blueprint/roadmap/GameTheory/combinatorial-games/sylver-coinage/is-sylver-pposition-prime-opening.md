# Hutchings' theorem

For every prime $p > 3$, the position $\{p\}$ of Sylver Coinage is a
P-position: a first player who opens with $p$ wins.

## Sources

- Morgan Boyers, Natalie Burton, Jeff Rushall, James Warner and Tara Zurick, *On Winning Strategies in Sylver Coinage When 4 Has Been Chosen*, INTEGERS 24 (2024), Article G2, Theorem 1.
