# The pass is unavailable from the empty position

Let $s \in \mathbb{N}$ and
$m \in \mathbb{N}^s$ with $m_i > 0$ for every $i < s$. Then the pass indicator of $m$ takes the
value $0$ at the zero tuple $(0, \dots, 0)$.
