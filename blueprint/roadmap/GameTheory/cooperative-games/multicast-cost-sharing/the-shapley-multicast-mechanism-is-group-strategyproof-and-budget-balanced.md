# The Shapley multicast mechanism is group-strategyproof and budget-balanced

Moulin's mechanism driven by the Shapley-value scheme of the multicast cost function (each link's cost split equally among served receivers below it) is group-strategyproof, budget-balanced, satisfies voluntary participation and consumer sovereignty.

## Proof sketch

Submodularity makes the Shapley scheme cross-monotonic, and Moulin's theorem applies.
