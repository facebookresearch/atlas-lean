# Multicast cost-sharing problem

A *multicast instance* is a rooted tree $T$ with nonnegative link costs and strategic receivers located at nodes, each with private value $u_j \ge 0$ for receiving the transmission. Serving a set $R$ of receivers costs $c(R)$, the total cost of links on the union of root-to-receiver paths. A mechanism selects a set of receivers and charges them; net welfare is $\sum_{j \in R} u_j - c(R)$.
