# Gross substitutability of an excess-demand function

Let $Z : \mathbb{R}^G_{>0} \to \mathbb{R}^G$ be an aggregate excess-demand function, homogeneous of degree $0$ and satisfying Walras's law $p \cdot Z(p) = 0$. It satisfies *weak gross substitutability* (WGS) if whenever $p' \ge p$ with $p'_g = p_g$ for some good $g$, then $Z_g(p') \ge Z_g(p)$; it satisfies *gross substitutability* (GS) if the inequality is strict whenever $p' \ne p$ on the coordinates raised. A positive price vector $p$ is a Walrasian equilibrium if $Z(p) \le 0$ (equivalently $Z(p) = 0$ under Walras's law with positive prices).
