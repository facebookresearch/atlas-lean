# Uniqueness and convexity of equilibria under gross substitutability

If an excess-demand function satisfies GS, positive Walrasian equilibrium prices are unique up to positive scaling. If it satisfies WGS, then for every equilibrium $p^*$ and every non-equilibrium positive price vector $p$, $p^* \cdot Z(p) > 0$ (the Arrow–Block–Hurwicz separating hyperplane), and the set of positive equilibrium price vectors is convex.

## Proof sketch

For uniqueness, scale a second equilibrium so it touches the first from above in some coordinate; raising the other prices strictly increases that good's excess demand, contradicting $Z = 0$. The separating inequality follows by moving from $p^*$ to $p$ through hybrid price vectors that raise one sorted price ratio at a time.
