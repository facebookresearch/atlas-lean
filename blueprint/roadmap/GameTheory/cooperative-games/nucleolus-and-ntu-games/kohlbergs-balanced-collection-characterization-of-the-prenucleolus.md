# Kohlberg's balanced-collection characterization of the prenucleolus

An efficient payoff vector $x$ is the prenucleolus of a TU game if and only if, for every real $t$, the collection of coalitions with excess at least $t$ at $x$ is either empty or balanced (admits positive weights summing to $1$ at every agent, together with the grand coalition).

## Proof sketch

If some level collection is unbalanced, a separating direction lowers all its excesses simultaneously without raising lower ones too much, contradicting minimality; conversely balancedness of all level sets forces any improving direction to raise some top excess.
