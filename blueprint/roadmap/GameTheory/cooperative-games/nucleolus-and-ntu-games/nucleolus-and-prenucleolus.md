# Nucleolus and prenucleolus

For a TU game $v$ on agents $A$ and a payoff vector $x$, the *excess* of a coalition $S$ is $e(S, x) = v(S) - x(S)$. Let $\theta(x)$ list the excesses of all proper nonempty coalitions in nonincreasing order. The *prenucleolus* is the set of efficient vectors ($x(A) = v(A)$) minimizing $\theta(x)$ lexicographically; the *nucleolus* is the lexicographic minimizer among efficient individually rational vectors ($x_i \ge v(\{i\})$).
