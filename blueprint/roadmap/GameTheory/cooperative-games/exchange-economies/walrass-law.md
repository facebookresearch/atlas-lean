# Walras's law

If every trader's utility is locally nonsatiated on nonnegative bundles, then every optimal demand exhausts the budget: $p \cdot x_i = p \cdot e_i$ for every $x_i$ in $i$'s demand set at prices $p$. Consequently the value of aggregate excess demand vanishes, $p \cdot \sum_i (x_i - e_i) = 0$.

## Proof sketch

If $p \cdot x_i < p \cdot e_i$, local nonsatiation provides a nearby affordable bundle strictly preferred to $x_i$, contradicting optimality.
