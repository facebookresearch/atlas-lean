# Fisher market

A *Fisher market* has buyers $i$ with money budgets $m_i \ge 0$ and utility functions $u_i$ over bundles of finitely many divisible goods with supplies $q_g \ge 0$. An *equilibrium* is a price vector $p \ge 0$ and an allocation $x$ such that each $x_i$ maximizes $u_i$ subject to $p \cdot x_i \le m_i$, and every good with positive price is fully sold while no good is oversold.
