# Fisher markets embed into exchange markets

A Fisher market with positive budgets corresponds to an exchange market in which each buyer $i$ is endowed with the fraction $m_i / \sum_k m_k$ of every good's supply. Under this proportional-endowment embedding, $(x, p)$ is a Fisher equilibrium if and only if it is a competitive equilibrium of the exchange market with $\sum_g p_g q_g = \sum_k m_k$, where $q_g$ is the supply of good $g$; since exchange equilibrium prices can be multiplied by any positive factor, every competitive equilibrium of the exchange market rescales to a Fisher equilibrium.

## Proof sketch

With proportional endowments each trader's income is proportional to its budget, and normalizing prices so that total value equals total budget makes the budget sets coincide.
