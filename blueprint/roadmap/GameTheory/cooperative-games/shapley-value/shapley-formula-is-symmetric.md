# Shapley's formula is symmetric

If a permutation $\sigma$ of the players leaves the game invariant, $v(\sigma S) = v(S)$ for every
coalition $S$, then Shapley's formula pays $\sigma(i)$ what it pays $i$. The proof reindexes the
sum over the coalitions containing $\sigma(i)$ by their preimages under $\sigma$.
