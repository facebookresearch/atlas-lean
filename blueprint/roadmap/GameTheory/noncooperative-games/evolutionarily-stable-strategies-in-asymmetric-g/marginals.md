# Marginal behavior strategy of a strategy on pairs

For finite sets `ι` and `κ` and a vector `x` on `ι × κ`, the marginals are the pair of vectors
`(i ↦ ∑_k x(i, k), k ↦ ∑_i x(i, k))` on `ι` and `κ`. For a [mixed strategy](is-mixed.md) `x` they
form a behavior strategy, a pair of mixed strategies.
