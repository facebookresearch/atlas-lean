# If role II has at least two strategies, no ESS is fully mixed there

For finite sets `ι` and `κ` with at least two role-II strategies, payoff matrices `A` and `B` and a
role probability `0 < ρ < 1`, no [evolutionarily stable behavior strategy](is-role-ess.md) `(p, q)`
of the role game has `q_k > 0` for every `k ∈ κ`.
