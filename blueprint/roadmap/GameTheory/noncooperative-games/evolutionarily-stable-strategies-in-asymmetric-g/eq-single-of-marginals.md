# A mixed strategy with pure marginals is a point mass

For finite sets `ι` and `κ`, a [mixed strategy](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/expectation-maximization-algorithm/is-dist.md) `x` on `ι × κ` and strategies `i ∈ ι`
and `k ∈ κ`, if the [marginals](marginals.md) of `x` are the point masses at `i` and at `k`, then
`x` is the point mass at `(i, k)`.
