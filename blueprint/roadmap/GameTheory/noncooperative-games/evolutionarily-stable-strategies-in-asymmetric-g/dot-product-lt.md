# A unique best pure reply strictly beats every other mixed strategy

For a finite set `ι`, a payoff vector `v`, a strategy `i` with `v_j < v_i` for every `j ≠ i`, and a
[mixed strategy](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/expectation-maximization-algorithm/is-dist.md) `p` different from the point mass at `i`, `p · v < v_i`.
