# Selten's theorem for role probabilities strictly between 0 and 1

For finite sets `ι` and `κ`, payoff matrices `A : ι × κ → ℝ` and `B : κ × ι → ℝ`, a role probability
`0 < ρ < 1` and vectors `p` on `ι` and `q` on `κ`, the pair `(p, q)` is an
[evolutionarily stable behavior strategy](is-role-ess.md) of the role game if and only if it is a
[strict Nash equilibrium](is-strict-nash.md) of the bimatrix game `(A, B)` (Selten 1980; Hofbauer
and Sigmund 1998, Chapter 10).
