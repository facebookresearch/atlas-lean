# Evolutionarily stable states of the symmetrized role game, 0 < ρ < 1

For finite sets `ι` and `κ`, payoff matrices `A : ι × κ → ℝ` and `B : κ × ι → ℝ`, a role probability
`0 < ρ < 1` and a vector `x` on `ι × κ`, `x` is an
[evolutionarily stable state](../../../Biology/population-dynamics/stability-of-evolutionarily-stable-states/is-ess.md)
of the [symmetrized matrix game](symmetrize.md) if and only if `x` is the point mass at a pair
`(i, k)` such that the point masses at `i` and `k` form a
[strict Nash equilibrium](is-strict-nash.md) of the bimatrix game `(A, B)` (Hofbauer and Sigmund
1998, Chapter 10).
