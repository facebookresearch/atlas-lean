# If role I has at least two strategies, no ESS is fully mixed there

For finite sets `ι` and `κ` with at least two role-I strategies, payoff matrices `A` and `B` and a
role probability `0 < ρ < 1`, no [evolutionarily stable behavior strategy](is-role-ess.md) `(p, q)`
of the role game has `p_i > 0` for every `i ∈ ι`, in contrast with the
[interior evolutionarily stable states](../../../Biology/population-dynamics/stability-of-evolutionarily-stable-states/asymptotically-stable.md)
of a symmetric game.
