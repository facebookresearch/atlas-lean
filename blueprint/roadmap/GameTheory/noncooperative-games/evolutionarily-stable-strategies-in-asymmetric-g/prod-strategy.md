# Product strategy on pairs of role strategies

For finite sets `ι` and `κ` and vectors `p` on `ι` and `q` on `κ`, the product strategy is the
vector `(i, k) ↦ p_i q_k` on `ι × κ`. For [mixed strategies](is-mixed.md) `p` and `q` it is a mixed
strategy whose [marginals](marginals.md) are `p` and `q`.
