# A mixed strategy earns at most a common bound on pure payoffs

For a finite set `ι`, a [mixed strategy](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/expectation-maximization-algorithm/is-dist.md) `p` on `ι`, a payoff vector `v` and a real
`c` with `v_j ≤ c` for every `j`, `p · v ≤ c`.
