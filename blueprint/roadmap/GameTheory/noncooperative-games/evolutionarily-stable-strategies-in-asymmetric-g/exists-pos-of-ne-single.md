# A mixed strategy other than a pure one weights another strategy

For a finite set `ι`, a [mixed strategy](../../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/bayesian-inference-and-sampling/expectation-maximization-algorithm/is-dist.md) `p` on `ι` and a strategy `i` with `p`
different from the point mass at `i`, there is `j ≠ i` with `p_j > 0`.
