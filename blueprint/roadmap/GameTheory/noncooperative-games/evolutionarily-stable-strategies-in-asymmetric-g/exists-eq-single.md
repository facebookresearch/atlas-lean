# For 0 < ρ < 1 every evolutionarily stable behavior strategy is pure

For finite sets `ι` and `κ`, payoff matrices `A` and `B` and a role probability `0 < ρ < 1`, every
[evolutionarily stable behavior strategy](is-role-ess.md) `(p, q)` of the role game is pure in both
roles: `p` and `q` are the point masses at some `i ∈ ι` and `k ∈ κ`.
