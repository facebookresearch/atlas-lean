# Golden-ratio pure price of anarchy on two related machines

For every set of jobs on two uniformly related machines, every pure Nash equilibrium $A$ satisfies $\max_j \ell_j(A) \le \frac{1 + \sqrt5}{2}\, \mathrm{OPT}$, and a two-job instance attains equality.

## Proof sketch

Case analysis on which machine is the bottleneck, combining the equilibrium inequality with the volume bound; the extremal speed ratio is the golden ratio.
