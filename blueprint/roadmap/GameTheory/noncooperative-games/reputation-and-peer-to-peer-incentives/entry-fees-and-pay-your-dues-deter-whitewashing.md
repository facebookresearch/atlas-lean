# Entry fees and pay-your-dues deter whitewashing

In a population game where players can freely change pseudonyms (whitewash) to escape bad reputations, an entry fee deters one-time and perpetual whitewashing if and only if it is at least the gain from defecting and re-entering, given by explicit thresholds in $\delta$. Under the pay-your-dues scheme, in which newcomers are defected against for one round by veterans, cooperation among veterans is sustainable iff the discount factor exceeds an explicit threshold (strictly so when it exceeds $1/2$), and the welfare loss from treating newcomers badly is bounded explicitly.

## Proof sketch

Compare the discounted payoff of cooperating forever with that of defecting once and re-entering under a fresh pseudonym, net of the fee or of the one-round newcomer penalty.
