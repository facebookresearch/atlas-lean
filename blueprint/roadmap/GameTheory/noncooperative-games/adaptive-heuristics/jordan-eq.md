# Nash equilibrium of Jordan's games

In Jordan's family of three-player two-action games with parameters $a_1, a_2, a_3$ close to $1$,
where each player wants to mismatch the next, the unique Nash equilibrium has player $i$ play its
first action with probability $a_{i-1}/(a_{i-1} + 1)$ (indices mod 3).

## Sources

- S. Hart and A. Mas-Colell, "Uncoupled dynamics do not lead to Nash equilibrium", Amer. Econ. Rev. 93(5) (2003), 1830–1836, §III and footnote 14.
