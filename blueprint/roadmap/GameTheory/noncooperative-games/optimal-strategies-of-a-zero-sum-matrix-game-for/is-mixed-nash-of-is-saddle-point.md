# A saddle point is a mixed Nash equilibrium

Let `A` be an `m × n` real matrix and `p`, `q` mixed strategies such that no mixed row strategy does better than `p` against `q` and no mixed column strategy concedes less than `q` against `p`. Then `(p, q)` is a mixed Nash equilibrium of the two-player game with payoffs `A` to the row player and `-A` to the column player.
