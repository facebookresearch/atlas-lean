# Exchange le

Let $A$ be a maximum Nash welfare allocation of finitely many goods for valuations $v$ with every single-good value $v_i(g) \ge 0$, and write $V_k$ for agent $k$'s value of its bundle under $A$. Let $g$ be a good in the bundle of an agent $j$, and let $i \ne j$ be an agent with $V_i > 0$ such that $j$ keeps positive value without $g$, $V_j - v_j(g) > 0$. Then moving $g$ from $j$ to $i$ cannot increase the product of the two bundle values:

$$(V_i + v_i(g))\,(V_j - v_j(g)) \le V_i\, V_j.$$
