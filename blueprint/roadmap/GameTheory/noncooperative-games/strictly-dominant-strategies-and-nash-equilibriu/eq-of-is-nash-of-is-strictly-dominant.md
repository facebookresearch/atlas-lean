# A strictly dominant profile is the only Nash equilibrium

In a game with nonempty strategy sets and real payoffs, suppose $\sigma_i$ is a strictly dominant strategy for every player $i$ (against every profile of the others, $\sigma_i$ gives $i$ a strictly higher payoff than any other strategy). Then every pure Nash equilibrium $\tau$ equals $\sigma$.

The same holds for a normal-form game, applied to its payoffs and its nonempty strategy sets.
