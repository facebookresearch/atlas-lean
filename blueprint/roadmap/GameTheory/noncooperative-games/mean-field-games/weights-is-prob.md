# Points of the standard simplex are probability vectors

Let $Q$ be a finite set. Every point of the standard simplex of $\mathbb{R}^Q$ is a probability vector: its
weights are nonnegative and sum to $1$. Mean field equilibria are stated for probability vectors
$m : Q \to \mathbb{R}$, while the mixed strategies of [Nash's theorem](../nash-s-theorem/exists-is-mixed-nash.md)
are points of the simplex.
