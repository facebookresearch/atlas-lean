# Existence of mean field equilibria

If $Q$ is a nonempty finite set and the cost is Lipschitz in the distribution,
$|F(y, m_1) - F(y, m_2)| \le K \sum_z |m_1(z) - m_2(z)|$ for probability vectors $m_1, m_2$, then a
[mean field equilibrium](is-mean-field-equilibrium.md) exists.

Cardaliaguet proves this for a compact metric space $Q$ and $F$ Lipschitz for the
Kantorovich–Rubinstein distance (on a finite set equivalent to the $\ell^1$ distance), as the limit of
symmetric mixed Nash equilibria of the $N$-player games, whose existence comes from Nash's theorem
(his Theorems 8.3–8.4 and Remark 2.6).

## Background

- [Nash's theorem](../nash-s-theorem/exists-is-mixed-nash.md): existence of mixed equilibria of
  finite games, the input of the limiting argument (in the symmetric form of Cardaliaguet's
  Theorem 8.4).
