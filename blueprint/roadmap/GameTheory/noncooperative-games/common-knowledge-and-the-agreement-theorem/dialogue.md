# Dialogue of posteriors

Starting from information partitions $\mathcal P_i^{(0)}$, the partitions after $n$ rounds of public
announcement of posteriors of the event $A$:

$$\mathcal P^{(n+1)} = \text{(one round applied to) } \mathcal P^{(n)},$$

where in each round every agent announces its current posterior of $A$ and refines its partition
by the level sets of the announced profile.

## Background

- [One round of public announcement of posteriors](dialogue-step.md): the round that is iterated.
