# Values of commonly known functions are common knowledge

Let $f$ be a function on $\Omega$ that every agent knows, that is, $f$ is constant on every cell of
every agent's partition. Then $f$ is constant on the cells of the meet, and the event
$\{\omega' : f(\omega') = f(\omega)\}$ is common knowledge at $\omega$.

The meet is the finest partition coarser than all the agents' partitions, and the level sets of
$f$ form such a coarser partition, so the meet is finer than it.
