# Summing over the cells (inequality)

On a finite set with a partition, if the sum of $f$ over every cell is nonpositive, then the total
sum of $f$ is nonpositive. This is the step "multiplying by $\mathrm{Prob}\,P$ and summing over all
$P$ in $\mathcal P^i$" in Aumann's proof of the Main Theorem of 1987.

## Sources

- R. J. Aumann, "Correlated equilibrium as an expression of Bayesian rationality", Econometrica 55(1) (1987), 1–18, p. 8 (proof of the Main Theorem).
