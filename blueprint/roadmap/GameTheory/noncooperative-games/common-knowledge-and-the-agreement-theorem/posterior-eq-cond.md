# Posteriors are conditional probabilities

Let $\Omega$ be a finite set of states in which every singleton is measurable, $\mu$ a measure on $\Omega$
and $\mathcal P$ a partition. The posterior $\mu(A \cap P(\omega))/\mu(P(\omega))$ of the event $A$ at
$\omega$ (with $x/0 = 0$) equals, as a real number, the conditional probability $\mu[A \mid P(\omega)]$,
where $\mu[\,\cdot \mid P] = \mu(P)^{-1}\,\mu(P \cap \cdot)$ is the conditional probability measure; both are
$0$ on a null cell.
