# One round of public announcement of posteriors

Given information partitions $\mathcal P_i$ and an event $A$, every agent announces its posterior
$q_i$ of $A$. Agent $i$'s new partition is the common refinement of $\mathcal P_i$ and the partition
of $\Omega$ into the level sets of the profile $\omega \mapsto (q_j(\omega))_j$.

This is a simultaneous-announcement form of the communication process of Geanakoplos and
Polemarchakis, in which the two agents communicate back and forth.
