# Common knowledge

Let agents $i$ have information partitions $\mathcal P_i$ of $\Omega$, and let $\mathcal M$ be their
meet, the finest partition that is coarser than every $\mathcal P_i$. An event $E$ is common
knowledge at $\omega$ when $E$ contains the cell $M(\omega)$ of the meet that contains $\omega$.

Ordering equivalence relations by inclusion ($r \le s$ when $r$ is finer than $s$), the meet in
Aumann's sense is the supremum $\bigsqcup_i r_i$ of the agents' equivalence relations, the equivalence
relation generated by their union. The name "meet" refers to partitions ordered by refinement, where the
order is reversed.
