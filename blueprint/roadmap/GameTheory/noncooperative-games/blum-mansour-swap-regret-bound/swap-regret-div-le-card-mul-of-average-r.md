# Blum–Mansour bound, averaged form

Blum–Mansour, averaged form: for a positive horizon `T` and a finite nonempty action set `K`, let `p` be the stationary play of the master algorithm built from the copies' plays `q t j`. If the average external regret of every copy `j` against its scaled loss sequence is at most `R`, then the master's swap regret divided by `T` is at most `|K| · R`.
