# Blum–Mansour bound, uniform form

For a finite nonempty action set `K`, let `p` be the stationary play of the master algorithm built from the copies' plays `q t j` (`IsStationaryPlay q p`: every `q t j` and every `p t` is a probability distribution on `K`, and each `p t` is a stationary distribution of the chain with transition probabilities `q t j i`). If every copy's external regret against its scaled loss sequence is at most `R`, then the master's swap regret is at most `|K| · R`.
