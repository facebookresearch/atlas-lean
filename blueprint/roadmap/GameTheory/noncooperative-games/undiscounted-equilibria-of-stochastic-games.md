# Undiscounted equilibria of multiplayer stochastic games

Does every finite multiplayer stochastic game admit an undiscounted equilibrium payoff? This is a central existence question beyond the two-player cases.

## What would settle it

Fix the exact long-run payoff convention, then prove existence of approximate equilibria at every ε>0, with the required payoff-limit property. That is, unilateral deviations must not improve any player's payoff by more than ε. Do not silently replace undiscounted equilibrium by the stronger uniform-equilibrium notion; equivalence holds in certain subclasses, not by definition.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Solan and Vieille describe this as one of the main open problems in game theory, record its solution for two-player games, and solve the subclass of positive recursive absorbing games without rectangular components.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
