# Shapley's index of a Lemke–Howson half-edge

In the Lemke–Howson graph of two nondegenerate bounded systems with missing label $k_0$, each active half-edge leaving a node $(x, y)$ on one side receives a sign in $\{-1, +1\}$: the product of the sign of the permutation matching the combined tight bases (with the leaving label replaced by $k_0$ on the active side) and the signs of the determinants of the two basis matrices. For a completely labelled pair this sign is *Shapley's index* of the corresponding equilibrium.
