# Shapley's orientation of Lemke–Howson paths

In the Lemke–Howson graph of two nondegenerate bounded systems, the two half-edges at every degree-two node carry opposite signs, so the signs orient every path consistently. Hence the two endpoints of every Lemke–Howson path have opposite Shapley indices. For a nondegenerate bimatrix game with positive payoffs, the artificial equilibrium $(0, 0)$ is the endpoint of one path, the remaining equilibria are matched by paths in pairs of opposite index, and the number of equilibria of index $+1$ exceeds those of index $-1$ by one.

## Proof sketch

Pivoting in or out of a basis changes one column of one basis matrix; a determinant expansion shows that the sign flips exactly compensate the change of the leaving label, so consecutive half-edges along a path have opposite signs.
