# Lemke–Howson graph of two labelled polyhedra

Let $A_1 x \le b_1$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n_1}$ and $A_2 y \le b_2$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n_2}$ be two systems whose rows are indexed by a common label set $K$ with $|K| = n_1 + n_2$; a label of a point is a tight row. Fix $k \in K$. The *Lemke–Howson graph* for missing label $k$ has as nodes the pairs $(x, y)$ of vertices that are *$k$-almost completely labelled* (every label other than $k$ is a label of $x$ or $y$), and joins two nodes when they share one vertex and the other vertices are adjacent along an edge of the corresponding polyhedron, the pair remaining $k$-almost completely labelled along that edge.
