# Pure best responses of player 1

Let $M$ and $N$ be finite sets of pure strategies of players 1 and 2, and let $\Delta(M)$ and $\Delta(N)$ be their sets of mixed strategies (probability vectors).
For a payoff matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times N}$ and a mixed strategy $y \in \Delta(N)$ of player 2, the
pure best responses of player 1 are the rows $i \in M$ that maximise $(Ay)_i = \sum_{j} A_{ij}\, y_j$.
