# Nondegenerate bimatrix game

Let $M$ and $N$ be finite sets of pure strategies of players 1 and 2, and let $\Delta(M)$ and $\Delta(N)$ be their sets of mixed strategies (probability vectors).
The game $(A, B)$, with $A, B \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times N}$, is nondegenerate if no mixed strategy has more
pure best responses than the size of its support, for either player: every $x \in \Delta(M)$ has at most
$|\operatorname{supp} x|$ pure best responses of player 2, and every $y \in \Delta(N)$ has at most
$|\operatorname{supp} y|$ pure best responses of player 1 (von Stengel, *Algorithmic Game Theory*,
Definition 3.2).
