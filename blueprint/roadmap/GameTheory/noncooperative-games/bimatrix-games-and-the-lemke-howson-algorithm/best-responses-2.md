# Pure best responses of player 2

Let $M$ and $N$ be finite sets of pure strategies of players 1 and 2, and let $\Delta(M)$ and $\Delta(N)$ be their sets of mixed strategies (probability vectors).
For a payoff matrix $B \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times N}$ and a mixed strategy $x \in \Delta(M)$ of player 1, the
pure best responses of player 2 are the columns $j \in N$ that maximise
$(x^{\mathsf T} B)_j = \sum_{i} x_i\, B_{ij}$.
