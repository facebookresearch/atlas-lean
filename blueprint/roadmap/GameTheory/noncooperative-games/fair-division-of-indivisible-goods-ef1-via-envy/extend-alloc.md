# Extend Alloc

For a finite set `S` of goods, an allocation `A` of the goods in `S`, a good `g` and an agent `i₀`, the extended allocation of the goods in `insert g S` gives `g` to `i₀` and every other good its holder under `A`. If `g` already lies in `S`, it is reassigned to `i₀`; `g` needs to be a new good only in the EF1 extension theorem.
