# Rotate Alloc

For any map `c : ZMod m → ι` with `m ≥ 2` and an allocation `A` of finitely many goods, the rotated allocation gives a good `g` whose owner `A g` lies in the image of `c` to `c (t - 1)`, where `t` is a chosen preimage of `A g` under `c`, and leaves every good whose owner is outside the image of `c` with that owner. When `c` is injective, for example an envy cycle, each agent `c t` receives the former bundle of its successor `c (t + 1)` ([Bundle rotate on Cycle](bundle-rotate-on-cycle.md)).
