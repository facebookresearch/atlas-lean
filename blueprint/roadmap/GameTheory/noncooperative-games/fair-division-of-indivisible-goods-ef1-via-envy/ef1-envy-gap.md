# Ef1 envy gap

Let `v` be nonnegative valuations of finitely many goods and `A` an EF1 allocation. If agent `i` envies agent `j`, then some good `g` in `j`'s bundle satisfies: `i` values `j`'s bundle without `g` at most as much as its own bundle, and the envy `vᵢ(bundle j) - vᵢ(bundle i)` is at most `v i g`.
