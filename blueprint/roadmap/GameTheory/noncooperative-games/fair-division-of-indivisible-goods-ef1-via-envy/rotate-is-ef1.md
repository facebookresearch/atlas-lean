# Rotate is EF1

Let `v` be valuations of finitely many goods and `A` an EF1 allocation (envy-free up to one good), and let `c : ZMod m → ι` with `m ≥ 2` be an injective envy cycle of `A`. Then the allocation obtained by rotating goods along the cycle is again EF1: bundles are only permuted among agents and no agent's own bundle loses value.
