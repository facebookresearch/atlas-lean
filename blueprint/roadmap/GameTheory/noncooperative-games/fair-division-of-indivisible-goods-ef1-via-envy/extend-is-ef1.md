# Extend is EF1

Let `v` be nonnegative valuations of finitely many goods, `S` a set of goods, `g ∉ S` a new good and `A` an EF1 allocation of the goods in `S`. If agent `i₀` is a source of the envy graph of `A` (nobody envies `i₀`), then the allocation of `S ∪ {g}` that gives `g` to `i₀` and keeps every other good with its holder is again EF1.
