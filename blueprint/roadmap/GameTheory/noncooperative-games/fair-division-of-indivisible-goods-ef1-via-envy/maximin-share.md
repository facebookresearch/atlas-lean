# Maximin share

For a nonempty finite set of agents, a finite set of goods and additive valuations $v_i(g) \in \mathbb{R}$, the maximin share of agent $i$ is

$$\max_{A} \min_{j} \sum_{g \in A^{-1}(j)} v_i(g),$$

the maximum over all allocations $A$ of goods to agents of the smallest value, to $i$, of any agent's bundle.
