# Pure Cost eq sum filter

For finitely many players with finite strategy sets, cost functions and arbitrary real weights `σ j : S j → ℝ`, the pure cost of player `i` playing `a` equals the sum over the profiles `s` with `s i = a` of `cost i s · ∏_{j ≠ i} σ j (s j)`. No simplex hypothesis is needed. When each `σ j` is a probability distribution, the sum is the expected cost of `a` against the opponents' independent mixed play.
