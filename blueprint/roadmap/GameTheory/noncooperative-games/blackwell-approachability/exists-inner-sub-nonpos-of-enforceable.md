# Blackwell's half-space step

Let $A$ be a finite set, $B$ a set, $E$ a real inner product space, $v : A \times B \to E$ a vector payoff
and $C \subseteq E$ convex. Suppose every half-space $\{x : \langle\lambda, x\rangle \le \rho\}$ containing
$C$ is enforceable: some mixed action $q$ gives $\langle \lambda, v(q, b)\rangle \le \rho$ against every
opponent action $b$. If $c \in C$ is a nearest point of $C$ to $x$, then some mixed action $q$ satisfies
$\langle x - c,\, v(q, b) - c\rangle \le 0$ for every $b$: the half-space through $c$ with outer normal
$x - c$ contains $C$, by the variational characterisation of the projection, and is enforceable.
