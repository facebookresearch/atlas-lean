# The M/√t rate of approachability

Under the hypotheses of [Blackwell's approachability recursion](sq-mul-inf-dist-average-le.md),
$\operatorname{dist}(\bar x_n, C) \le M/\sqrt n$ for every $n \ge 1$. In the game this is
Hart's bound $\mathbb E[\operatorname{dist}(\bar a_t, C)] \le M/\sqrt t$ before the passage to
realised payoffs.

## Informally uses

- [Blackwell's approachability recursion](sq-mul-inf-dist-average-le.md): take square roots in
  $n^2\delta_n \le nM^2$.

## Sources

- S. Hart, "Adaptive heuristics", Econometrica 73(5) (2005), 1401–1430, Appendix, proof of Theorem 5 (ii) and §10.8 (rate $1/\sqrt T$).
