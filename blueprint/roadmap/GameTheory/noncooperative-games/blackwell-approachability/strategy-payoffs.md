# Stage payoffs of a strategy

In the setting of [running sums](strategy-sums.md), the expected vector payoff at stage $n$ when the
decision maker plays $\sigma$ against the opponent sequence $\beta$ is $v\bigl(\sigma(S_n / n), \beta_n\bigr)$,
where $S_n$ is the running sum and $S_n / n = 0$ for $n = 0$.
