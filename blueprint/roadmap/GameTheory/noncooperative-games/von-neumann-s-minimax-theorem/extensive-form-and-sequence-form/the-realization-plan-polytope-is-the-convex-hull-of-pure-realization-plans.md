# The realization plan polytope is the convex hull of pure realization plans

Under perfect recall, the polytope $\{x : Ex = e,\ x \ge 0\}$ of realization plans of player $i$ equals the convex hull of the realization plans of pure strategies, and its vertices are exactly the $0$-$1$ realization plans of pure strategies.

## Proof sketch

Every realization plan comes from a behavior strategy, and a behavior strategy's realization plan is a convex combination of pure realization plans. A basic feasible solution of the flow system is integral, hence pure.
