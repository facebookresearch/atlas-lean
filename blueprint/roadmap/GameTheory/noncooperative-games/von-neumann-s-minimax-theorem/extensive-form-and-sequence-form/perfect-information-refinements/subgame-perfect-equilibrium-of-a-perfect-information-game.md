# Subgame-perfect equilibrium of a perfect-information game

Let $T$ be a finite perfect-information game tree with leaves carrying payoff vectors, decision nodes owned by players with nonempty finite choice sets, and chance nodes with finite-support distributions. A pure strategy of player $i$ selects a choice at every decision node of $i$, including nodes in unreachable subtrees. A pure profile is a *subgame-perfect equilibrium* if for every node $u$ its restriction to the subtree rooted at $u$ is a pure Nash equilibrium of that subtree.
