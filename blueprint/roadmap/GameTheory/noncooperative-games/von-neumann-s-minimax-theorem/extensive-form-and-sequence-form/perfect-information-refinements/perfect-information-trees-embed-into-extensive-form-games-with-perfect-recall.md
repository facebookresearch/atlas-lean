# Perfect-information trees embed into extensive-form games with perfect recall

Every finite perfect-information game tree $T$ embeds into an extensive-form game in which each decision node forms its own singleton information set. The embedded game has perfect recall for every player, its pure strategies are in canonical bijection with those of $T$ preserving expected payoffs, and the pure and mixed Nash equilibria of the two strategic forms correspond. Consequently Kuhn's theorem applies, and the backward-induction profile of $T$ maps to a pure Nash equilibrium of the embedded game.

## Proof sketch

With singleton information sets the sequence leading to a node is determined by the node, so perfect recall is automatic; the strategy spaces coincide by construction.
