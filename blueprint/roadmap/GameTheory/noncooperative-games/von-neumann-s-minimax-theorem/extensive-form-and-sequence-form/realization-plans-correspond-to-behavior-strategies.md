# Realization plans correspond to behavior strategies

Assume player $i$ has perfect recall. The realization plan of every behavior strategy satisfies $Ex = e$, $x \ge 0$; conversely every realization plan $x$ is the realization plan of the behavior strategy $\beta(h)(c) = x(\sigma_h c) / x(\sigma_h)$ (arbitrary when $x(\sigma_h) = 0$). This correspondence is bijective modulo the choices at information sets reached with probability zero.

## Proof sketch

The realization probability of $\sigma_h c$ is that of $\sigma_h$ times the behavior probability of $c$, which gives flow conservation; dividing recovers the behavior strategy.
