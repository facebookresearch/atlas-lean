# Kuhn's theorem on behavior strategies

In a finite extensive-form game in which player $i$ has perfect recall, every mixed strategy of player $i$ is realization equivalent to a behavior strategy: for every profile of the other players, both induce the same distribution over leaves. Equivalently the realization plan of every mixed strategy is a realization plan in the sense of the sequence form.

## Proof sketch

The realization plan of a mixed strategy satisfies flow conservation by perfect recall, since reaching $h$ depends only on $i$'s choices in $\sigma_h$. The behavior strategy obtained from it has the same realization probabilities, and leaf probabilities factor through these.
