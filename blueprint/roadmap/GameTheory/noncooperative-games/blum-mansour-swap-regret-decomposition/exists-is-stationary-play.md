# Existence of a stationary play

Let $K$ be a finite nonempty set and let $q_t(j, \cdot)$ be a probability distribution on $K$ for every time $t < T$ and every $j \in K$. Then there are probability distributions $p_t$ on $K$ with $p_t(i) = \sum_j p_t(j)\,q_t(j, i)$ for all $t$ and $i$: each $p_t$ is stationary for the chain with transition probabilities $q_t$.
