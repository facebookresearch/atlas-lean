# Pigou's network

Pigou's network is the two-edge network of Pigou: one commodity, two parallel edges from $s$ to $t$, each forming a path, with latencies $\ell_0(x) = 1$ and $\ell_1(x) = x$.

## Sources

- T. Roughgarden and É. Tardos, "How bad is selfish routing?", J. ACM 49(2) (2002), 236–259, §2.5, Figure 3 (with $p = 1$).
- A. C. Pigou, *The Economics of Welfare*, Macmillan, 1920.
