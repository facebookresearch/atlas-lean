# Latencies of Braess's network

Braess's network has vertices $s, v, w, t$ and five edges: $0 : s \to v$ and $3 : w \to t$ with
latency $\ell(x) = x$, $1 : v \to t$ and $2 : s \to w$ with constant latency $1$, and the shortcut
$4 : v \to w$ with latency $0$.

## Sources

- T. Roughgarden and É. Tardos, "How bad is selfish routing?", J. ACM 49(2) (2002), 236–259, §1.2, Figure 1.
- D. Braess, "Über ein Paradoxon aus der Verkehrsplanung", Unternehmensforschung 12 (1968), 258–268.
