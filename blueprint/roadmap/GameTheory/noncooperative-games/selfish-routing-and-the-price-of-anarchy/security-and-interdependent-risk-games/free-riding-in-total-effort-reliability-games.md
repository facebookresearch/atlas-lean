# Free riding in total-effort reliability games

In a total-effort system reliability game with strictly concave reliability, a profile is a pure Nash equilibrium iff the aggregate effort equals the largest stand-alone optimum $\max_i \bar x_i$ and only agents attaining the largest benefit-cost ratio $v_i / c_i$ exert effort; generically a single agent provides all effort. Equilibrium aggregate effort is at most, and under positive marginal benefit strictly less than, the socially optimal effort, and the agents providing effort in equilibrium need not be those with the lowest cost of effort. Adding agents weakly increases equilibrium reliability under total effort (the largest stand-alone optimum can only grow), whereas under weakest link it weakly decreases.

## Proof sketch

Each agent's best response sets the aggregate to its stand-alone level if that exceeds the others' effort and contributes nothing otherwise; the social optimum equates the sum of marginal benefits with the minimum cost, exceeding every individual first-order condition.
