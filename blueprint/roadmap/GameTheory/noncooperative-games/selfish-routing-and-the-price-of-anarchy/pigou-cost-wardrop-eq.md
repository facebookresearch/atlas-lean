# Pigou's network attains the bound 4/3

In Pigou's network the Wardrop equilibrium costs exactly $\tfrac43$ times the optimal flow:
$C(0, 1) = 1 = \tfrac43 \cdot \tfrac34$. So the constant $4/3$ in the price-of-anarchy bound for
affine latencies cannot be improved.
