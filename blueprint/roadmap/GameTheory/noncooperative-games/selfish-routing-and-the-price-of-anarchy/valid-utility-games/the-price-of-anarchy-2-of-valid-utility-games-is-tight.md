# The price of anarchy 2 of valid utility games is tight

The closed-neighbourhood location game on a finite graph, in which each player selects a vertex and the value of a vertex is shared among players whose closed neighbourhoods contain it, is a monotone valid utility game. For every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a graph and a pure Nash equilibrium $s$ of positive welfare with a profile $o$ satisfying $(2 - \varepsilon) V(s) < V(o)$.

## Proof sketch

Use a star-like graph where all players crowd a central vertex in equilibrium while the optimum spreads them over many leaves of equal value.
