# Regret vector

For a decision maker with finite action set $A$ and costs $c(a, b)$ against the opponent's action
$b$, the regret vector of having played $a$ against $b$ has coordinates $c(a, b) - c(k, b)$,
$k \in A$: the cost saved by playing $k$ instead. Its average over the rounds is the vector of
signed unconditional regrets $\tilde V(k) - U$ of Hart (2005), in cost form.

## Sources

- S. Hart, "Adaptive heuristics", Econometrica 73(5) (2005), 1401–1430, §10.1, (10)–(11).
