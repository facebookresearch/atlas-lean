# Smooth game

A cost-minimisation game is $(\lambda, \mu)$-smooth if

$$\sum_i c_i(t_i, s_{-i}) \le \lambda\, C(t) + \mu\, C(s)$$

for every two profiles $s$ and $t$. For $\mu < 1$ this implies that every pure Nash equilibrium
costs at most $\lambda / (1 - \mu)$ times every profile, the robust price-of-anarchy bound.

## Sources

- T. Roughgarden, "Intrinsic robustness of the price of anarchy", J. ACM 62(5) (2015), Article 32, Definition 2.1.
