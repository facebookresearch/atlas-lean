# Congestion poa

Price of anarchy `5/2` for singleton atomic congestion games with affine delays: let `R` be a finite set of resources with delays `a r · k + b r`, where `a r ≥ 0` and `b r ≥ 0`, and let `s` and `opt` be strategy profiles of `n` players. Suppose that no player `i` lowers their cost by switching from `s i` to `opt i` alone. Then the social cost of `s` is at most `5/2` times the social cost of `opt`. In particular this holds for every pure Nash equilibrium `s` and every profile `opt`, including a social optimum.
