# Aumann's correlated equilibrium of Chicken

The distribution with probability $1/3$ on each profile of Chicken except (stay, stay) is a
correlated equilibrium. A player told to leave believes the other leaves or stays with equal
probability, and leaving gives it $\frac12 5 + \frac12 3 = 4$ against $\frac12 6 = 3$ for
staying; a player told to stay knows the other leaves, and staying gives $6 > 5$.
