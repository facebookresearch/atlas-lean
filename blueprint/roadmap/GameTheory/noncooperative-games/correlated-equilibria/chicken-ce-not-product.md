# Chicken's correlated equilibrium is not a product

Aumann's correlated equilibrium of Chicken gives probability $0$ to (stay, stay), while each
player stays with probability $1/3$; so it is not the product of its marginals and is not a mixed
Nash equilibrium. (It is not even a mixture of Nash equilibria: Hart and Mas-Colell 2000, §2.)

## Sources

- S. Hart, "Adaptive heuristics", Econometrica 73(5) (2005), 1401–1430, §6.
- S. Hart and A. Mas-Colell, "A simple adaptive procedure leading to correlated equilibrium", Econometrica 68(5) (2000), 1127–1150, §2.
