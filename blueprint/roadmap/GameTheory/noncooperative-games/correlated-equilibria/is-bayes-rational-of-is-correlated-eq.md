# Every correlated equilibrium is the distribution of Bayes-rational play

Let $\psi$ be a correlated equilibrium of a finite game. Take the action profiles themselves as
the states of the world, $\psi$ as the common prior and the identity as the action profile, and
let player $i$'s information partition be generated by its own action, so that each player is told
only its own recommendation. Then every player is Bayes rational at every state.

Bayes rationality at a state $t$ is the inequality on the cell $\{s : s_i = t_i\}$, which is
exactly the correlated-equilibrium inequality for the recommendation $t_i$.
