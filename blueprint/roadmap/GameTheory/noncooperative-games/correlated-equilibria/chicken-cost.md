# The game of Chicken

Chicken in cost form: each of two players chooses *leave* (strategy $0$) or *stay* (strategy
$1$), with payoffs $(5,5)$ for (leave, leave), $(3,6)$ for (leave, stay), $(6,3)$ for
(stay, leave) and $(0,0)$ for (stay, stay); costs are their negatives.

## Sources

- S. Hart, "Adaptive heuristics", Econometrica 73(5) (2005), 1401–1430, §6, Figure 3.
