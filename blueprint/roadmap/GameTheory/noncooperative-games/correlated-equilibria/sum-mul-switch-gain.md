# Correlated-equilibrium sums over a recommendation

For every real function $\psi$ on profiles (a distribution in the intended use),
$\sum_s \psi(s)\, g_i^{j\to k}(s) = \sum_{s :\, s_i = j} \psi(s)\,\bigl(c_i(s) - c_i(k, s_{-i})\bigr)$:
the sum used in the definition of correlated equilibria is the sum over the event
$\{s_i = j\}$ of Hart and Mas-Colell's Definition.

## Sources

- S. Hart and A. Mas-Colell, "A simple adaptive procedure leading to correlated equilibrium", Econometrica 68(5) (2000), 1127–1150, §2, Definition and footnote 3.
