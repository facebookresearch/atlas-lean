# Nash equilibria are correlated equilibria

If no player can lower its expected cost by switching from its mixed strategy $\sigma_i$ to a pure
strategy, the product distribution $\prod_i \sigma_i(s_i)$ is a correlated equilibrium. Indeed
every pure strategy in the support of $\sigma_i$ then has the minimal expected cost, so each
correlated equilibrium sum $\sigma_i(j)(\bar c_i(j) - \bar c_i(k))$ is nonpositive.
