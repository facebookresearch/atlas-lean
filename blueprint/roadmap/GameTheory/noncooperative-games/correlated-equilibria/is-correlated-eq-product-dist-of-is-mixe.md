# Mixed Nash equilibria are correlated equilibria

If the mixed profile $\sigma$ is a Nash equilibrium of the game with payoffs $u_i$ (no player can
raise its expected payoff by changing its mixed strategy), then the product distribution
$\prod_i \sigma_i(s_i)$ is a correlated equilibrium of the game with costs $-u_i$. This applies
[Nash equilibria are correlated equilibria](is-correlated-eq-product-dist.md) to the deviations
to pure strategies.
