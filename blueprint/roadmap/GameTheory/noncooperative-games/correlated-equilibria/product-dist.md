# Product distribution of mixed strategies

For real weights $\sigma_i : S_i \to \mathbb{R}$ (mixed strategies in the intended use), the product distribution is
$\psi(s) = \prod_i \sigma_i(s_i)$: the law of the profile when the players randomise
independently.

## Sources

- S. Hart and A. Mas-Colell, "A simple adaptive procedure leading to correlated equilibrium", Econometrica 68(5) (2000), 1127–1150, §2 ("Nash equilibria correspond to the special case where $\psi$ is a product
  measure").
