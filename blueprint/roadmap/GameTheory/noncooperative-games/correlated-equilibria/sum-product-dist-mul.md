# Expected cost under a product distribution

For real weights $\sigma_l : S_l \to \mathbb{R}$ (mixed strategies in the intended use), under their product $\psi$, player $i$'s expected cost is the
$\sigma_i$-average of the expected costs of its pure strategies:
$\mathbb E_\psi[c_i] = \sum_a \sigma_i(a)\, \bar c_i(a)$.
