# Iterated deletion of strictly dominated strategies

Given a finite game with strategy sets $S_i$, set $R^0_i = S_i$ and let $R^{k+1}_i$ be obtained from $R^k_i$ by deleting every strategy that is strictly dominated relative to $R^k = \prod_j R^k_j$. The sets decrease and stabilize after finitely many rounds at the *iterated-deletion limit* $R^\infty$. Deleting instead, at each round $k$, every strategy that is weakly dominated relative to $R^k$ defines *iterated deletion of weakly dominated strategies*.
