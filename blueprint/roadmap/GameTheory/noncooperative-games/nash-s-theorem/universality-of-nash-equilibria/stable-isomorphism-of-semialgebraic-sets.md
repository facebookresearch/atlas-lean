# Stable isomorphism of semialgebraic sets

Two semialgebraic sets $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ and $W \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$ are *stably isomorphic* if they are related by the equivalence relation generated by (a) homeomorphisms whose graph is semialgebraic, and (b) the projections $V \times \mathbb{R}^k \to V$ for $k \ge 0$.
