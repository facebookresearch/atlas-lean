# Totally mixed Nash equilibrium

Let $G$ be a finite strategic game with player set $I$, finite nonempty pure strategy sets $S_i$ and costs $c_i : \prod_j S_j \to \mathbb{R}$. A mixed profile $x = (x_i)_{i \in I}$, with $x_i$ a probability vector on $S_i$, is a *totally mixed Nash equilibrium* if it is a mixed Nash equilibrium of $G$ and $x_i(a) > 0$ for every player $i$ and every pure strategy $a \in S_i$.
