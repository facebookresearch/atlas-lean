# Local neighbourhood equivalence of joint distributions

Two finitely supported distributions $\sigma, \tau$ on action profiles of players $I$ are *locally neighbourhood equivalent* with respect to a graph $G$ if for every player $i$ their marginals on the closed neighbourhood $N[i]$ coincide.
