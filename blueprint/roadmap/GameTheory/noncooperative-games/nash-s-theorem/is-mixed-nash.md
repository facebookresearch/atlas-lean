# Mixed Nash equilibrium

A profile $\sigma = (\sigma_j)_j$ of mixed strategies is a Nash equilibrium (Nash's equilibrium
point) if no player can raise its expected payoff by switching to another mixed strategy while
the others keep theirs: $U_i(\tau, \sigma_{-i}) \le U_i(\sigma)$ for every player $i$ and every
mixed strategy $\tau$ of $i$.

## Sources

- J. Nash, "Non-cooperative games", Ann. of Math. 54 (1951), 286–295, p. 287 (section "Formal Definitions and Terminology").
