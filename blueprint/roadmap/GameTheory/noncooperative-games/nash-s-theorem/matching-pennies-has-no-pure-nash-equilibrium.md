# Matching pennies has no pure Nash equilibrium

In the game with payoffs $(1, -1)$ when the two players' choices match and $(-1, 1)$ when they differ, every pure profile gives one player a profitable deviation, so there is no pure Nash equilibrium; the unique Nash equilibrium has each player mix with probability $1/2$.
