# Expected payoff of a mixed profile

In a finite game with players $I$, finite strategy sets $S_i$ and payoffs
$u_i : \prod_j S_j \to \mathbb R$, let each player $j$ play a mixed strategy $\sigma_j$, a
probability vector on $S_j$, independently of the others. The expected payoff of player $i$ is

$$U_i(\sigma) = \sum_{s \in \prod_j S_j} \Big(\prod_j \sigma_j(s_j)\Big)\, u_i(s).$$

## Sources

- J. Nash, "Non-cooperative games", Ann. of Math. 54 (1951), 286–295, p. 287 (section "Formal Definitions and Terminology").
