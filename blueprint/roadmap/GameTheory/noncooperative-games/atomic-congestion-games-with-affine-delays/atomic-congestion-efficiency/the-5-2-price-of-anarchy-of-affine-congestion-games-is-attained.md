# The 5/2 price of anarchy of affine congestion games is attained

There is a three-player atomic congestion game with latencies $\ell_e(k) = k$ and a pure Nash equilibrium whose social cost is exactly $5/2$ times the optimal social cost. Hence the bound $5/2$ for pure Nash equilibria of congestion games with nonnegative affine latencies is tight.

## Proof sketch

Use six resources arranged so that each player's two strategies share resources cyclically; in the bad equilibrium each player pays $5$ versus $2$ at the optimum, and a direct check shows no unilateral deviation helps.
