# The best Nash equilibrium need not minimize the potential

There is a three-player single-sink fair connection game (shared trunk of cost $2$, bypasses of cost $6/5$) in which the socially optimal profile is a pure Nash equilibrium of cost $2$ with Rosenthal potential $11/3$, while the all-bypass profile is a pure Nash equilibrium of cost $18/5$ that is the unique minimizer of the potential. Hence the potential-minimization argument cannot certify the best equilibrium.

## Proof sketch

Direct computation: the potential of the shared profile is $2 H_3 = 11/3$, which exceeds $3 \cdot 6/5 = 18/5$; both profiles are checked to be equilibria.
