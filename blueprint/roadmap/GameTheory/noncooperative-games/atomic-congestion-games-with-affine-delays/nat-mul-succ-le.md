# Christodoulou–Koutsoupias inequality

For all natural numbers $y$ and $z$,

$$y\,(z + 1) \le \tfrac53\, y^2 + \tfrac13\, z^2 .$$

Equality holds at $(y, z) = (1, 1)$ and $(1, 2)$. The inequality fails for real numbers, e.g. at
$y = 1/2$, $z = 1$: integrality of the loads is what makes the atomic bound $5/2$ differ from the
nonatomic $4/3$.

## Sources

- G. Christodoulou and E. Koutsoupias, "The price of anarchy of finite congestion games", STOC 2005, 67–73, Lemma 1.
- T. Roughgarden, "Intrinsic robustness of the price of anarchy", J. ACM 62(5) (2015), Article 32, inequality (6) in Example 2.5.
