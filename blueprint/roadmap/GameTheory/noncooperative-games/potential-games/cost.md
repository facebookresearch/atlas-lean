# Cost of a player in a congestion game

In a congestion game, the cost of player $i$ at the profile $s$ is
$c_i(s) = \sum_{e \in R_i(s_i)} d_e(n_e(s))$, the sum of the delays of the resources it uses.

## Sources

- R. W. Rosenthal, "A class of games possessing pure-strategy Nash equilibria", Int. J. Game Theory 2 (1973), 65–67.
