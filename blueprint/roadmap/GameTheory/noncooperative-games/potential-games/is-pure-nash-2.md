# Pure Nash equilibrium of a finite game

Let finitely many players $i$ have finite nonempty strategy sets $S_i$ and costs
$c_i : \prod_j S_j \to \mathbb{R}$. A strategy profile $s$ is a pure Nash equilibrium when no
unilateral deviation lowers the deviating player's cost: $c_i(s) \le c_i(a, s_{-i})$ for every player
$i$ and every $a \in S_i$. The definition is that of [pure Nash equilibrium](is-pure-nash.md),
restricted to finitely many players with finite nonempty strategy sets.
