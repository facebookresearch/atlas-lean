# Rosenthal's theorem

Every congestion game is an exact potential game, with Rosenthal's potential
$\Phi(s) = \sum_e \sum_{k=1}^{n_e(s)} d_e(k)$ as potential. No assumption on the delays is needed.

## Informally uses

- [Rosenthal's potential splits over one player](potential-eq.md): when player $i$ deviates, only
  the term $c_i$ changes.
