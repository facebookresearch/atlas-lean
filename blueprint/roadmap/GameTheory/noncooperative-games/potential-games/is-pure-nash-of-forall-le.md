# Potential minimisers are Nash equilibria

If $\Phi$ is an exact potential and the profile $s$ minimises $\Phi$ over all profiles, then $s$ is
a pure Nash equilibrium.

The same statement for the exact potentials and pure Nash equilibria of the improvement-path module, that a minimiser of an exact potential is a pure Nash equilibrium, is `pureNash_of_min_potential`: with the quantifiers of the potential predicate reordered, the minimum hypothesis and the conclusion are identical.

## Sources

- D. Monderer and L. S. Shapley, "Potential games", Games Econom. Behav. 14 (1996), 124–143, Lemma 2.1 (stated there for payoff maximisation and ordinal potentials, which include exact
  potentials).
