# Strong equilibrium, strong Nash equilibrium and k-resilience

Let $G$ be a cost-minimization game. A pure profile $s$ is a *strong equilibrium* if no nonempty coalition $C$ has a joint deviation $s'_C$ under which every member of $C$ strictly lowers its cost. It is a *strong Nash equilibrium* if no nonempty coalition has a joint deviation under which no member's cost increases and some member's cost strictly decreases. It is *$k$-resilient* (a *$k$-strong equilibrium*) if no nonempty coalition $C$ of size at most $k$ has a joint deviation $s'_C$ under which every member of $C$ strictly lowers its cost. Every strong Nash equilibrium is a strong equilibrium, and both are pure Nash equilibria; a strong equilibrium is $k$-resilient for every $k$, and $1$-resilience is the pure Nash condition.
