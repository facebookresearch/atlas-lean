# Potential is at most H(k) times the network cost

In the fair cost-sharing game with $k$ players, Rosenthal's potential satisfies
$\Phi(s) \le H(k) \cdot \sum_{e : n_e(s) > 0} c_e$, because every load is at most $k$.

## Sources
