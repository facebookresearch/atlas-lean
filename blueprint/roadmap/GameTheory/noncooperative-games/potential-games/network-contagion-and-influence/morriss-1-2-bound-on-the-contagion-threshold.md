# Morris's 1/2 bound on the contagion threshold

If $G$ is an infinite locally finite graph without isolated vertices, then for every $q > 1/2$ no finite set is contagious under either dynamics. Hence the contagion threshold of every such graph is at most $1/2$.

## Proof sketch

Track the number of edges between adopters and non-adopters under progressive dynamics: when $q > 1/2$ a new adopter has more than half its edges into the adopter set, so the boundary strictly decreases at every step, which is impossible for infinitely many steps.
