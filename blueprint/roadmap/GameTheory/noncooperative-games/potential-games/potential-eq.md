# Rosenthal's potential splits over one player

For every player $i$ and profile $s$, Rosenthal's potential is

$$\Phi(s) = \sum_{e \in R} \sum_{k=1}^{m_e} d_e(k) + c_i(s),$$

where $m_e$ is the number of players other than $i$ using $e$. The first term does not depend on
$s_i$.

## Informally uses

- [Load splits over one player](load-eq.md): the load of $e$ is $m_e$ or $m_e + 1$.
