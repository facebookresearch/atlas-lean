# Cost of the built network

The cost of the network built at the profile $s$ of a fair cost-sharing game is the total cost
$\sum_{e : n_e(s) > 0} c_e$ of the edges used by at least one player. It is the sum of the
players' payments.

## Sources

- E. Anshelevich, A. Dasgupta, J. Kleinberg, É. Tardos, T. Wexler and T. Roughgarden, "The price of stability for network design with fair cost allocation", SIAM J. Comput. 38(4) (2008), 1602–1623, §2.
