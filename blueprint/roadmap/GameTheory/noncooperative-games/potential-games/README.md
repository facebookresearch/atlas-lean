# Potential games

A game has an exact potential when one function on strategy profiles tracks every player's unilateral change of cost. Its minimisers are pure Nash equilibria and improvement paths are finite. Rosenthal's theorem makes every congestion game a potential game, and in fair cost-sharing network design the potential sandwiches the network cost within a factor $H(k)$, so the price of stability is at most $H(k)$.
