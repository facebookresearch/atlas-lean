# Finite potential games have pure Nash equilibria

Every game with finitely many (and at least one) strategy profiles that has an exact potential
has a pure Nash equilibrium.

## Informally uses

- [Potential minimisers are Nash equilibria](is-pure-nash-of-forall-le.md): a minimiser of the
  potential over the finite set of profiles is an equilibrium.

## Sources

- D. Monderer and L. S. Shapley, "Potential games", Games Econom. Behav. 14 (1996), 124–143, Corollary 2.2.
