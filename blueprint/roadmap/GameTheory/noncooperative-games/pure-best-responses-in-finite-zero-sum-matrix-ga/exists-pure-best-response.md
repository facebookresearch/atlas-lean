# Pure best responses exist

Let `A` be an `m × n` real matrix game with `m ≥ 1` rows and `q` a mixed column strategy. Then some row `i` is a best response to `q` among all mixed row strategies: for every mixed row strategy `p`, the payoff `pᵀ A q` is at most the payoff `eᵢᵀ A q` of the pure strategy `i`.
