# Remaining single ranked last

For a strict total order $T$ of preference over a set of partners, the order "$T$ with remaining single
last" is the strict total order on the partners and the option $\varnothing$ of remaining single that agrees
with $T$ on partners and ranks $\varnothing$ below every partner. It describes an agent for whom every
partner is acceptable, as in the complete preference lists of the stable marriage problem.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "On the allocation of residents to rural hospitals: a general property of two-sided matching markets", *Econometrica* 54(2) (1986), 425–427, p. 426 and footnote 9: a student is acceptable to a hospital when the hospital prefers the student to leaving a position unmatched; ranking remaining single last makes every partner acceptable.
