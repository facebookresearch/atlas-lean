# Bridge to the stable marriage model

Let $M$ and $W$ be sets, every man with a strict total order of preference on $W$ and every woman with one on
$M$, and let every agent rank remaining single last. A perfect matching $\mu : M \to W$ (a bijection), viewed
as a matching with singles in which everybody has a partner, is stable as a matching with singles if and
only if it is stable in the sense of the stable marriage problem (no blocking pair). Individual rationality
holds automatically, since every agent is matched to an acceptable partner.
