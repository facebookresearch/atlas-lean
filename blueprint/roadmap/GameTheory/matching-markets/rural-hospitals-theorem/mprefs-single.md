# Men's preferences with the option of remaining single

For sets $M$ of men and $W$ of women, each man has a strict total order on $W \cup \{\varnothing\}$, where
$\varnothing$ is remaining single and $a$ before $b$ means that $a$ is preferred (Roth, *Econometrica* 54
(1986), p. 425: a strict preference ordering over the set $H \cup \{u\}$, with $u$ the possibility of
remaining unmatched).
