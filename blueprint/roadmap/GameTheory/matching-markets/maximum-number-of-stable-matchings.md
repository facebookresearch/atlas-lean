# Maximum number of stable matchings

Determine the asymptotic maximum number of stable matchings in an n-by-n two-sided market with complete strict preferences. This describes the possible multiplicity of stable outcomes.

## What would settle it

Define blocking pairs and stable matchings, then prove a sharp bound on their number over all profiles, together with a matching construction. An improved exponential base is a meaningful partial result, not a full solution.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Ghorbani and Mnich (preprint) prove that the number of stable matchings is less than 2.81^n and record a lower-bound construction of base about 2.28; the exact exponential base is not determined there.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
