# Strategy-proofness of deferred acceptance with unequal sides and unacceptable partners

Consider a marriage market with finite, possibly unequal, sets of men and women, in which each agent has a strict ranking of the acceptable agents on the other side and of remaining single, and stated preferences are of the same form. Under the men-proposing deferred-acceptance mechanism, stating his true preferences is a weakly dominant strategy for every man. This extends the case of equal sides and complete lists. It is distinct from the impossibility theorem, which says that no stable mechanism makes truth-telling dominant for every agent; that theorem needs a domain with at least two agents on each side whose preferences can vary, and is false for a market with one man and one woman. Preferences with ties need a tie-breaking rule fixed first.

## Proof depends on

- [Strategy-proofness of the man-optimal stable mechanism for the men](man-optimal-mechanism-strategy-proof-for-men.md): the case of equal sides and complete preferences.

## Expansion

A sound expansion specifies the following prerequisites and links their statements.

- Padding with dummy agents that maps the men-proposing deferred-acceptance outcome of a market with unacceptable partners and unequal sides, and every misreport of a man, to the corresponding objects of a padded market with complete lists.
