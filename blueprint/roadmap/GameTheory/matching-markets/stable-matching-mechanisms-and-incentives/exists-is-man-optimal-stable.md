# Every market with sides of equal size has a man-optimal stable matching

For finite sets of men and women of the same size and any profile of strict preferences, there is a
man-optimal stable matching. With the uniqueness statement, this makes "the" man-optimal stable mechanism
of Roth's Theorem 5 well defined.
