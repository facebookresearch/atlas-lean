# Stability read off ranks on three agents

For three men and three women with preferences given by rank functions $r_m$ (man $m$'s ranks of the
women) and $s_w$ (woman $w$'s ranks of the men), a map $f$ from the men to the women satisfies the
stability condition read off ranks if there are no man $m$ and woman $w$ with $r_m(w) < r_m(f(m))$ and
$s_w(m) < s_w(m')$ for every man $m'$ with $f(m') = w$. It is the condition of
[stability without the inverse matching](is-stable-iff-forall-partner.md), written with ranks; being a
finite check, it decides stability in Roth's market.
