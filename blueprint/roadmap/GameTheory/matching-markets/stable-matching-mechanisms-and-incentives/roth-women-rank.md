# Women's ranks in Roth's market

Roth's market has three men $m_1, m_2, m_3$ and three women $w_1, w_2, w_3$. The women's preferences, given by ranks $0, 1, 2$ from the most to the least
preferred man, are $w_1 : m_1 \succ m_3 \succ m_2$, $w_2 : m_3 \succ m_1 \succ m_2$ and
$w_3 : m_1 \succ m_2 \succ m_3$ (Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982), proof of Theorem 3).
