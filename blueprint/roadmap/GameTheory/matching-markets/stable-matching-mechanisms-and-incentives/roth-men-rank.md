# Men's ranks in Roth's market

Roth's market has three men $m_1, m_2, m_3$ and three women $w_1, w_2, w_3$. The men's preferences, given by ranks $0, 1, 2$ from the most to the least
preferred woman, are $m_1 : w_2 \succ w_1 \succ w_3$, $m_2 : w_1 \succ w_2 \succ w_3$ and
$m_3 : w_1 \succ w_2 \succ w_3$ (Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982), proof of Theorem 3).
