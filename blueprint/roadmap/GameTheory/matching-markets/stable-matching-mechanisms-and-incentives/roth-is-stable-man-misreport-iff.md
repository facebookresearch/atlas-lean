# After $m_1$'s misreport, $x$ is the unique stable matching

In Roth's market, when man $m_1$ reports $w_2 \succ w_3 \succ w_1$ instead of his true preference and every
other agent states the true preference, the matching $x = \{(m_1, w_2), (m_2, w_3), (m_3, w_1)\}$ is the
only stable perfect matching (Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982), proof of Theorem 3: $C(P'') = \{x\}$).
