# Matching mechanism

A matching mechanism for a set $M$ of men and a set $W$ of women assigns a perfect matching between $M$ and
$W$ (a bijection $M \to W$) to every stated profile of men's strict preferences over $W$ and women's strict
preferences over $M$. Roth calls it a matching procedure and identifies procedures that yield the same
outcomes (Roth 1982, §4, remark after Corollary 5.1), which is why a mechanism is a function of the stated
profile.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "The economics of matching: stability and incentives", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 7(4) (1982), 617–628, §4.
