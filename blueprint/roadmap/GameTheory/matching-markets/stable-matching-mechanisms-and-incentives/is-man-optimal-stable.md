# Man-optimal stable matching

Let $M$ (the men) and $W$ (the women) be sets. A preference profile gives every man a strict total order of preference on $W$ and every woman one on $M$; a perfect matching is a bijection $\mu : M \to W$, and it is stable for a profile if no man $m$ and woman $w$ prefer each other to their partners.
A perfect matching $\mu$ is man-optimal stable for a profile if it is stable and no man prefers his partner
in any other stable matching: for every stable $\nu$ and every man $m$, $m$ does not prefer $\nu(m)$ to
$\mu(m)$ (Gale and Shapley, *Amer. Math. Monthly* 69 (1962), Theorem 2; Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982),
Theorem 2).
