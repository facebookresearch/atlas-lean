# Truthful revelation is dominant for a man

Let $M$ (the men) and $W$ (the women) be sets. A preference profile gives every man a strict total order of preference on $W$ and every woman one on $M$; a perfect matching is a bijection $\mu : M \to W$, and it is stable for a profile if no man $m$ and woman $w$ prefer each other to their partners.
For a matching mechanism $h$, truthful revelation is a dominant strategy for the man $m$ if, for every
profile $P$ and every strict order $T$ on $W$, the partner $m$ gets when he reports $T$ (the others stating
$P$) is not one he prefers, under his true preference $P(m)$, to the partner he gets by reporting $P(m)$
(Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982), §4, the paragraph before Theorem 3).
