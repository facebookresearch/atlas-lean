# Weak Pareto optimality of the man-optimal stable matching for the men

Let $M$ and $W$ be finite sets of men and women with $M$ nonempty, and let $\mu$ be a man-optimal stable
matching for a profile of strict preferences. Then no perfect matching, stable or not, gives every man a
partner he strictly prefers to his partner in $\mu$. The assumption of a nonempty set of men excludes the
empty market, where the condition "every man strictly prefers" holds vacuously. Roth's example after
Corollary 5.1 shows that strong Pareto optimality fails: a matching can make some men better off and none
worse off.

Roth's proof uses the last step of the men-proposing algorithm.
