# Uniqueness of the man-optimal stable matching

Let $M$ (the men) and $W$ (the women) be sets. A preference profile gives every man a strict total order of preference on $W$ and every woman one on $M$; a perfect matching is a bijection $\mu : M \to W$, and it is stable for a profile if no man $m$ and woman $w$ prefer each other to their partners.
Two man-optimal stable matchings of the same profile are equal: if they gave some man different partners,
his strict preference would rank one above the other, and the other matching would not be man-optimal.

## Sources

- A. E. Roth, "The economics of matching: stability and incentives", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 7(4) (1982), 617–628, §4, the paragraph before Theorem 5 ("a unique optimal stable outcome" for each set of agents).
