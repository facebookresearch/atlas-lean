# After $w_1$'s misreport, $y$ is the unique stable matching

In Roth's market, when woman $w_1$ reports $m_1 \succ m_2 \succ m_3$ instead of her true preference and every
other agent states the true preference, the matching $y = \{(m_1, w_1), (m_2, w_3), (m_3, w_2)\}$ is the
only stable perfect matching (Roth, *Math. Oper. Res.* 7 (1982), proof of Theorem 3: $C(P') = \{y\}$).
