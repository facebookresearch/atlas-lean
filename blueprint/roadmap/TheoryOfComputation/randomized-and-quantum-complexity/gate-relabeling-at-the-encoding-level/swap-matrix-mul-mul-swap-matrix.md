# Conjugating by the swap matrix exchanges the two qubits

For a pair of bits $p = (a, b)$ write $\bar p = (b, a)$. Let $S$ be the complex $4 \times 4$ swap
matrix, indexed by pairs of bits. For every $4 \times 4$ complex matrix $M$ indexed by pairs of
bits, $S M S$ has entry $M(\bar p, \bar q)$ at $(p, q)$.
