# Exchanging the qubits of a controlled gate moves the control

For a pair of bits $p = (a, b)$ write $\bar p = (b, a)$. Matrices below have Gaussian-rational
entries (complex numbers with rational real and imaginary parts, each part encoded by a sign, a
numerator and a denominator); $U_{\mathbb{C}}$ is the complex matrix that $U$ represents. For every
$2 \times 2$ Gaussian-rational matrix $U$, the matrix $(p, q) \mapsto C(\bar p, \bar q)$, where $C$
is controlled-$U$ with the first qubit as control, is controlled-$U$ with the second qubit as
control.
