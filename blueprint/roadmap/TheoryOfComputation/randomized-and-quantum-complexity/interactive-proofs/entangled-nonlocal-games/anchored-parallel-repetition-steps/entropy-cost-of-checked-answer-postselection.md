# Entropy cost of postselecting on checked answers

Let $|\psi\rangle$ be a unit vector in a tensor product $\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$ of finite-dimensional spaces, with Alice's reduced state $\rho_A = \operatorname{tr}_B |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$. Let $L$ be a finite set of labels with a probability law $P_L$, and for each $\ell \in L$ let $\{A_\ell(a)\}_{a \in \mathcal{A}}$ be a POVM on $\mathcal{H}_A$ and $\{B_\ell(b)\}_{b \in \mathcal{B}}$ a POVM on $\mathcal{H}_B$ (the label may carry Bob's question, so Bob's measurement may depend on it), with finite checked-answer sets and $\mathcal{Z} = \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B}$. The *Alice branch* of $(\ell, a, b)$ is the positive operator $\tau_{\ell, a, b} = \operatorname{tr}_B\bigl[(A_\ell(a)^{1/2} \otimes B_\ell(b)^{1/2})\, |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\, (A_\ell(a)^{1/2} \otimes B_\ell(b)^{1/2})\bigr]$, whose trace is the Born weight $\langle\psi| A_\ell(a) \otimes B_\ell(b) |\psi\rangle$, and the *reference state* of $\ell$ is $\sigma_\ell = \sum_{a} A_\ell(a)^{1/2} \rho_A A_\ell(a)^{1/2}$, which has trace one and does not depend on Bob's measurements. Relative entropy is Umegaki's, $D(\rho \,\|\, \sigma) = \operatorname{tr} \rho (\ln \rho - \ln \sigma)$. Then: (1) forgetting Bob's checked answer leaves Alice's post-measurement branch, $\sum_b \tau_{\ell, a, b} = A_\ell(a)^{1/2} \rho_A A_\ell(a)^{1/2}$, so every branch lies below $\sigma_\ell$; consequently, for every set $S \subseteq \mathcal{Z}$ of checked answers of Born weight $w = \sum_{(a, b) \in S} \operatorname{tr} \tau_{\ell, a, b} > 0$, the normalized branch $\rho_S = \frac{1}{w} \sum_{(a, b) \in S} \tau_{\ell, a, b}$ satisfies $D(\rho_S \,\|\, \sigma_\ell) \le \ln(1/w)$; (2) for every event $E \subseteq L \times \mathcal{Z}$, the *restricted Born state* $\xi_E = \sum_{(\ell, a, b) \in E} P_L(\ell)\, |\ell\rangle\langle\ell| \otimes \tau_{\ell, a, b} \otimes |a, b\rangle\langle a, b|$ has trace equal to the event probability $P = \sum_{(\ell, a, b) \in E} P_L(\ell) \langle\psi| A_\ell(a) \otimes B_\ell(b) |\psi\rangle$, and for $P > 0$ its normalization satisfies $D\bigl(\xi_E / P \,\big\|\, R\bigr) \le \ln|\mathcal{Z}| - \ln P$ for the reference $R = \sum_{\ell} P_L(\ell)\, |\ell\rangle\langle\ell| \otimes \sigma_\ell \otimes \frac{1}{|\mathcal{Z}|} I_{\mathcal{Z}}$ with uniform checked answers and the original label law (Bavarian, Vidick and Yuen, Section 5.2, proof of Claim 5.13: the bound $P(W_C)\, \xi \le \Xi$ and eqs. (38)–(40)).

## Proof sketch

For (1), $\sum_b B_\ell(b) = I$ and cyclicity of the partial trace on $\mathcal{H}_B$ give $\sum_b \tau_{\ell, a, b} = A_\ell(a)^{1/2} \rho_A A_\ell(a)^{1/2}$, and summing over $a$ gives $\sigma_\ell$; all branches are positive, so $w \rho_S \le \sigma_\ell$. If $0 \le \rho \le \lambda \sigma$ for a state $\rho$, then $D(\rho \,\|\, \sigma) \le \ln \lambda$ by operator monotonicity of the logarithm; take $\lambda = 1/w$. For (2), each $\tau_{\ell, a, b} \le \sigma_\ell$ by (1), so $\xi_E \le |\mathcal{Z}|\, R$, and the same bound with $\lambda = |\mathcal{Z}|/P$ applies.
