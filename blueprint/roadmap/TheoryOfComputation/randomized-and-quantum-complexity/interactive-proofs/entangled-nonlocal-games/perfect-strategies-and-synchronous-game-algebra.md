# Perfect commuting strategies and the synchronous game algebra

For a synchronous game $G$ (one whose predicate rejects different answers to equal questions), let $\mathcal{A}(G)$ be the universal unital $*$-algebra generated by projections $e_{x,a}$ with $\sum_a e_{x,a} = 1$ for each $x$ and $e_{x,a} e_{y,b} = 0$ whenever $V(x, y, a, b) = 0$. Then $G$ has a perfect commuting-operator strategy if and only if $\mathcal{A}(G)$ has a tracial state, equivalently a $*$-representation in a C*-algebra with a faithful trace. When the question distribution has full support, winning with probability $1$ is equivalent to assigning zero probability to every rejected tuple.

## Proof sketch

A perfect strategy is synchronous, so its correlation is tracial on Alice's algebra and the relations of $\mathcal{A}(G)$ hold there. Conversely, given a trace on $\mathcal{A}(G)$, the GNS construction with commuting left and right actions gives a commuting strategy whose correlation vanishes on rejected tuples.
