# The class NC(poly) of polynomial-depth bounded fan-in circuit families

$\mathrm{NC}(\mathrm{poly})$ is the class of promise problems decided by families of bounded fan-in Boolean circuits $(C_n)$ of depth $\mathrm{poly}(n)$ (and hence size $2^{\mathrm{poly}(n)}$) that are uniformly generated in the sense that a description of any gate of $C_n$ and its inputs can be computed in space $\mathrm{poly}(n)$. It is the polynomial-depth analogue of $\mathrm{NC} = \bigcup_k \mathrm{NC}^k$ (depth $\log^k n$, polynomial size).
