# Controlled phase-shifted Hadamard with control on the first qubit

Let $B = e^{i\pi/4} H$, where $H$ is the Hadamard matrix. Controlled-$B$ with the first qubit as
control equals $D C$. Here $D$ is the diagonal matrix with entry $e^{i\pi/4}$ at the basis states
whose first bit is $1$ and $1$ elsewhere, and $C$ is controlled-$H$: it is the identity on the
states whose first bit is $0$ and applies $H$ to the second qubit on the states whose first bit is
$1$.
