# Controlled phase-shifted Hadamard with control on the second qubit

Let $B = e^{i\pi/4} H$, where $H$ is the Hadamard matrix. Controlled-$B$ with the second qubit as
control equals $D C$. Here $D$ is the diagonal matrix with entry $e^{i\pi/4}$ at the basis states
whose second bit is $1$ and $1$ elsewhere, and $C$ is controlled-$H$: it is the identity on the
states whose second bit is $0$ and applies $H$ to the first qubit on the states whose second bit is
$1$.
