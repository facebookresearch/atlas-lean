# Quantum PCP conjecture

Is approximating a local Hamiltonian's ground energy within a constant additive error per local term QMA-hard? This concerns the complexity and entanglement of low-energy quantum matter.

## What would settle it

Fix constant local dimension/locality and bounded local terms. Construct a polynomial-time reduction with a constant normalized energy gap, and prove completeness and soundness. An inverse-polynomial promise gap or an NLTS theorem is not the same result.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Aharonov, Arad and Vidick survey the conjecture; Gharibian and Hecht state that the question of a quantum PCP theorem remains wide open. An inverse-polynomial promise gap is not the same statement.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
