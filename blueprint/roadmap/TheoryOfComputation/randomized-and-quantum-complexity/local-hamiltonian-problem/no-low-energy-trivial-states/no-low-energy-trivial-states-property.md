# The NLTS property and the Freedman-Hastings conjecture

A family $(H^{(n)})_n$ of $(k, \ell)$-local Hamiltonians with $k, \ell = O(1)$ has the *NLTS property* (no low-energy trivial states) if there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every constant $d$ and all sufficiently large $n$, no state of relative energy $\varepsilon$ for $H^{(n)}$ has circuit depth at most $d$; equivalently, the minimum circuit depth of $\varepsilon$-low-energy states tends to infinity. The *NLTS conjecture* of Freedman and Hastings asserts that such families exist.
