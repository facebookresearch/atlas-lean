# Block operator of k matrices

For $2^m \times 2^m$ complex matrices $A_0, \dots, A_{k-1}$, the block operator $\bigotimes_i A_i$
is the $2^{km} \times 2^{km}$ matrix with entries $\prod_{i < k} A_i(s_i, t_i)$, where $s_i$ and
$t_i$ are the $i$-th blocks of $m$ binary digits of $s$ and $t$, block $0$ lowest.
