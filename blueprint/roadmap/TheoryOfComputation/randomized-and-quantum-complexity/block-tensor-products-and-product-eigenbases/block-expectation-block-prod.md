# The expectation of a block operator in a product state factors

For $k$ complex $2^m \times 2^m$ matrices $A_i$ and $k$ vectors $\psi_i \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}$, the expectation $\langle \Psi, A \Psi \rangle$ of the block operator $A = A_0 \otimes \dots \otimes A_{k-1}$ in the product state $\Psi = \psi_0 \otimes \dots \otimes \psi_{k-1}$ equals $\prod_i \langle \psi_i, A_i \psi_i \rangle$, where $\langle x, y \rangle = \sum_s \overline{x_s} y_s$.
