# A product over k + 1 blocks splits off the first block

For $\psi_0 \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}$ and vectors $\psi_1, \dots, \psi_k \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}$, the
product state of $\psi_0, \psi_1, \dots, \psi_k$ equals the binary tensor product of $\psi_0$ (in
the low $m$ digits) with the product state of $\psi_1, \dots, \psi_k$, after identifying indices
along $(k + 1) m = m + k m$.
