# BQP is contained in PSPACE

Every decision problem in BQP, the class of bounded-error
quantum polynomial-time decision problems, lies in PSPACE, the class of decision problems
decidable in polynomial space.

## Sources

- E. Bernstein and U. Vazirani, "Quantum complexity theory", SIAM Journal on Computing 26 (1997), 1411-1473 (BQP ⊆ PSPACE; Theorem 8.2.3 in the author-hosted version dated September 8, 1997, https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~vazirani/pubs/bv.pdf, proved there for quantum Turing machines); S. Arora and B. Barak, *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach*, Cambridge University Press, 2009, Theorem 10.2.
