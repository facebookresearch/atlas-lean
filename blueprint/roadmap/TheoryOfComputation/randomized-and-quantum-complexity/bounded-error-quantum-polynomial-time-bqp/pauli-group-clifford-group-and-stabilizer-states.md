# Pauli group, Clifford group and stabilizer states

The $n$-qubit Pauli group $\mathcal{P}_n$ consists of the operators $i^k P_1 \otimes \dots \otimes P_n$ with $k \in \{0,1,2,3\}$ and
$P_j \in \{I, X, Y, Z\}$. The Clifford group is the set of unitaries $U$ with $U \mathcal{P}_n U^* = \mathcal{P}_n$, taken
modulo global phases; it is generated, up to phases, by $H$, $S$ and CNOT. A stabilizer group is an abelian subgroup of
$\mathcal{P}_n$ not containing $-I$. Each element of $\mathcal{P}_n$ can be written as $c\, X^{a} Z^{b}$ with $c \in \{\pm 1, \pm i\}$ and
$a, b \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$ recording the positions of the $X$ and $Z$ factors. If the group is generated by
$M_j = c_j X^{a_j} Z^{b_j}$, $j = 1, \dots, n$, with $M_j^2 = I$, pairwise commuting, and with the vectors $(a_j, b_j)$
linearly independent over $\mathbb{F}_2$, its joint $+1$ eigenspace has dimension $2^{n - n} = 1$, and
the stabilizer state it defines is the pure state spanned by that eigenspace, determined as a unit vector only up to a
global phase. Stabilizer states are represented by binary tableaux of the generators with their signs.

## Expansion

A good expansion adds a page for each pending prerequisite below.

- Statement prerequisite: the binary representation with phases: $c\, X^a Z^b$ corresponds to $(a, b) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$
  together with its phase, and a tableau lists the generators' vectors with their signs.
- Proof prerequisite: two elements of $\mathcal{P}_n$ commute exactly when the symplectic form of their vectors vanishes.
- Proof prerequisite: conjugation by $H$, $S$ and CNOT maps Pauli operators to Pauli operators by the standard tableau
  update rules, these rules agree with conjugation by the corresponding matrices, and $H$, $S$ and CNOT generate the
  Clifford group up to phases.
