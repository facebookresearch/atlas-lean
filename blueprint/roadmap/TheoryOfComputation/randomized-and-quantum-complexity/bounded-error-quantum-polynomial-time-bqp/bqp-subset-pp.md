# BQP is contained in PP

Every decision problem in BQP lies in PP.

## Sources

- L. M. Adleman, J. DeMarrais and M.-D. A. Huang, "Quantum computability", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 26 (1997), 1524–1540 (BQP ⊆ PP).
- E. Bernstein and U. Vazirani, "Quantum complexity theory", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 26 (1997), 1411–1473 (the class BQP).
- S. Arora and B. Barak, *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach*, Cambridge University Press, 2009, Chapter 10.
