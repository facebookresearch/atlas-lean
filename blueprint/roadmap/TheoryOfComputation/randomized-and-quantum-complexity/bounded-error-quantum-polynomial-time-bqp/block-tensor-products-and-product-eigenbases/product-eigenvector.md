# Product eigenvectors

For a Hermitian complex $2^m \times 2^m$ matrix $Q$ with its chosen orthonormal eigenvector basis $(e_j)$ and a tuple $(j_0, \dots, j_{k-1})$ of indices, the product eigenvector is the product state $e_{j_0} \otimes \dots \otimes e_{j_{k-1}} \in \mathbb{C}^{2^{km}}$, whose entry at a global index $s$ is $\prod_i e_{j_i}(s_i)$ with $s_i$ the $i$-th block digit of $s$.
