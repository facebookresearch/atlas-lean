# Product eigenvectors are orthonormal

For a Hermitian complex $2^m \times 2^m$ matrix $Q$ with its chosen orthonormal eigenvector basis and any number $k$ of blocks, the product eigenvectors, indexed by tuples $(j_0, \dots, j_{k-1})$ of eigenvector indices, form an orthonormal family in $\mathbb{C}^{2^{km}}$ with its standard inner product.
