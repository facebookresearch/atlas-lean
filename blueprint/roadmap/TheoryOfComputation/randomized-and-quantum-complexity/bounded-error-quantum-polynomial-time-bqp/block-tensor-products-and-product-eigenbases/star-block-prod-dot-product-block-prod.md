# The inner product of product states factors

For vectors $\varphi_i, \psi_i \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}$, $i = 0, \dots, k-1$, the inner product $\sum_s \overline{\Phi_s} \Psi_s$ of the product states $\Phi = \varphi_0 \otimes \dots \otimes \varphi_{k-1}$ and $\Psi = \psi_0 \otimes \dots \otimes \psi_{k-1}$ equals $\prod_i \sum_t \overline{\varphi_i(t)} \psi_i(t)$.
