# The quadratic form of a block operator factors on product vectors

For $k$ complex $2^m \times 2^m$ matrices $A_i$ and $k$ vectors $\psi_i \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}$, the quadratic form $\langle \Psi, A \Psi \rangle$ of the block operator $A = A_0 \otimes \dots \otimes A_{k-1}$ at the product vector $\Psi = \psi_0 \otimes \dots \otimes \psi_{k-1}$ equals $\prod_i \langle \psi_i, A_i \psi_i \rangle$, where $\langle x, y \rangle = \sum_s \overline{x_s} y_s$. The matrices and vectors are arbitrary; only when each $\psi_i$ is a unit vector and each $A_i$ is Hermitian is $\langle \psi_i, A_i \psi_i \rangle$ the expectation of the observable $A_i$ in the state $\psi_i$, and then the identity says that the expectation of $A$ in the product state $\Psi$ is the product of these expectations.
