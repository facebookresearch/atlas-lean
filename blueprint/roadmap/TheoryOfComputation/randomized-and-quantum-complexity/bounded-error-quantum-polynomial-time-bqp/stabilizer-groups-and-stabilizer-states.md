# Stabilizer groups and stabilizer states

A *stabilizer group* is an abelian subgroup $S$ of the [Pauli group](pauli-group-on-n-qubits.md) $\mathcal{P}_n$ with
$-I \notin S$. Every element of $S$ then squares to $I$ (an element squaring to $-I$ would put $-I$ in $S$), and $S$ is
generated by elements $M_1, \dots, M_r$ whose binary vectors $(a_j, b_j) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$ are linearly independent,
so $|S| = 2^r$. The joint $+1$ eigenspace $V_S = \{ \psi : M\psi = \psi \text{ for all } M \in S \}$ has dimension
$2^{n - r}$. When $r = n$ it is one-dimensional, and a unit vector spanning it, determined up to a global phase, is the
*stabilizer state* of $S$. A stabilizer state is recorded by the $n \times 2n$ tableau of $n$ independent generators with
their signs. Examples are the computational basis states ($|x\rangle$ is stabilized by the $(-1)^{x_j} Z_j$), the state
$|+\rangle$ (stabilized by $X$) and the Bell state $(|00\rangle + |11\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ (stabilized by $XX$ and $ZZ$).
