# Basis change from Z to Y

Let $B = e^{i\pi/4} H$, where $H$ is the Hadamard matrix, and let $S^\dagger$ be the diagonal matrix
with diagonal entries $1$ and $-i$. Then $(B S^\dagger)^\dagger Z (B S^\dagger) = Y$, where $Z$ is
the Pauli $Z$ matrix and $Y$ is the matrix with rows $(0, -i)$ and $(i, 0)$.
