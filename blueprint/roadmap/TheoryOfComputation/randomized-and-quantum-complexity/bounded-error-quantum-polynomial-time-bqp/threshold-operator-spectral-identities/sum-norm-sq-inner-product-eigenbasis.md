# Parseval identity for the product eigenbasis

Let $Q$ be Hermitian with eigenvalues $\lambda_0, \dots, \lambda_{2^m - 1}$ and an orthonormal basis
of eigenvectors $v_0, \dots, v_{2^m - 1}$. For $j = (j_0, \dots, j_{k-1})$ write
$v_j = v_{j_0} \otimes \cdots \otimes v_{j_{k-1}}$ for the product eigenvector and
$\lambda_j = (\lambda_{j_0}, \dots, \lambda_{j_{k-1}})$. For every vector
$\Psi \in \mathbb{C}^{2^{km}}$, $\sum_j |\langle v_j, \Psi \rangle|^2 = \sum_s |\Psi_s|^2$.
