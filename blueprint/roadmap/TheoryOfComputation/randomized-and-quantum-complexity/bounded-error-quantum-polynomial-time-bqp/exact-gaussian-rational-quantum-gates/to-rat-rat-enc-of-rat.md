# Decoding a rational encoding

A rational encoding is a triple $(s, p, q)$ of a Boolean sign and natural numbers, standing for $-p/q$ if $s$ is true and $p/q$ otherwise. The encoding of a rational $r$ is (true if $r < 0$, the absolute value of its reduced numerator, its reduced denominator). For every rational $r$, the rational number its encoding stands for is $r$.
