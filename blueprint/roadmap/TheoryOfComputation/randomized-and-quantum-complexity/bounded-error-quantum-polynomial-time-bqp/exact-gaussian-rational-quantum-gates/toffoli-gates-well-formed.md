# The five Toffoli gates are well formed

A rational encoding is a triple $(s, p, q)$ of a Boolean sign $s$ and natural numbers $p, q$, standing for $-p/q$ if $s$ is true and $p/q$ otherwise (with value $0$ when $q = 0$). A Gaussian-rational encoding is a pair of rational encodings, standing for the complex number whose real and imaginary parts they encode. The encoding of a rational $r$ is (true if $r < 0$, the absolute value of its reduced numerator, its reduced denominator); the encoding of $a + bi$ with $a, b$ rational is the pair of the encodings of $a$ and $b$. A two-qubit gate is two positions $i, j$ and an entry list of encodings; it is well formed on $N$ qubits when $i < j < N$ and the complex matrix of the entries is unitary. For distinct qubit positions $a, b, c$, each less than $N$ and in any order, every gate in the five-gate Toffoli list with controls $a, b$ and target $c$ is well formed.
