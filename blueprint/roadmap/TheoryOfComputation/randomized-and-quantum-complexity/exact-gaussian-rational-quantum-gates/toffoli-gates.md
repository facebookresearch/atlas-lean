# The five-gate Toffoli construction

A rational encoding is a triple $(s, p, q)$ of a Boolean sign $s$ and natural numbers $p, q$, standing for $-p/q$ if $s$ is true and $p/q$ otherwise (with value $0$ when $q = 0$). A Gaussian-rational encoding is a pair of rational encodings, standing for the complex number whose real and imaginary parts they encode. The encoding of a rational $r$ is (true if $r < 0$, the absolute value of its reduced numerator, its reduced denominator); the encoding of $a + bi$ with $a, b$ rational is the pair of the encodings of $a$ and $b$. For qubit positions $a, b$ (controls) and $c$ (target), the Toffoli list is the sequence of five controlled gates of A. Barenco et al., *Elementary gates for quantum computation*, Phys. Rev. A 52 (1995), 3457, Lemma 6.1: controlled-$V$ from $b$ to $c$, controlled-$X$ from $a$ to $b$, controlled-$V^\dagger$ from $b$ to $c$, controlled-$X$ from $a$ to $b$, and controlled-$V$ from $a$ to $c$. Here $V$ is the square root of $X$ with entries $\tfrac12 \pm \tfrac12 i$, and a controlled gate from control $u$ to target $t$ is stored on the pair of positions in increasing order, with the control on the lower or higher qubit accordingly.
