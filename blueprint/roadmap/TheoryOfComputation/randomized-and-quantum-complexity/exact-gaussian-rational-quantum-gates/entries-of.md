# Entry list of a two-qubit matrix

Matrices below have Gaussian-rational entries (complex numbers with rational real and imaginary
parts, each part encoded by a sign, a numerator and a denominator); $U_{\mathbb{C}}$ is the complex
matrix that $U$ represents. For a $4 \times 4$ matrix $M$ with rows and columns indexed by pairs of
bits, the entry list consists of the sixteen entries of $M$ in row-major order, rows and columns
both in the order $(0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 1)$.
