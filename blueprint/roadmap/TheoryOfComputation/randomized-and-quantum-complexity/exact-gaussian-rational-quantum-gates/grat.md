# Gaussian-rational encodings

A rational encoding is a triple $(s, p, q)$ of a Boolean sign $s$ and natural numbers $p, q$, standing for $-p/q$ if $s$ is true and $p/q$ otherwise (with value $0$ when $q = 0$). A Gaussian-rational encoding is a pair of rational encodings: the first encodes the real part and the second the imaginary part.
