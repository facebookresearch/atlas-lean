# Controlled gates on distinct in-range qubits are well-formed

Let $U$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix with Gaussian-rational entries whose complex form $U_{\mathbb{C}}$
satisfies $U_{\mathbb{C}}^\dagger U_{\mathbb{C}} = I$. For qubit positions $c \ne t$ with $c < N$
and $t < N$, the controlled-$U$ gate with control $c$ and target $t$, listed with the smaller
position first, is a well-formed two-qubit gate on $N$ qubits: its positions are increasing and
below $N$, and its decoded $4 \times 4$ matrix is unitary.
