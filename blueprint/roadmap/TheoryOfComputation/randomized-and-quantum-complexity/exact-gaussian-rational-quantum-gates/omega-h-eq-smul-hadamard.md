# The encoded phase-shifted Hadamard is a global phase times Hadamard

A rational encoding is a triple $(s, p, q)$ of a Boolean sign $s$ and natural numbers $p, q$, standing for $-p/q$ if $s$ is true and $p/q$ otherwise (with value $0$ when $q = 0$). A Gaussian-rational encoding is a pair of rational encodings, standing for the complex number whose real and imaginary parts they encode. The encoding of a rational $r$ is (true if $r < 0$, the absolute value of its reduced numerator, its reduced denominator); the encoding of $a + bi$ with $a, b$ rational is the pair of the encodings of $a$ and $b$. The complex matrix of the encoded phase-shifted Hadamard gate, whose entries are $\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 i$ except the bottom-right entry $-\tfrac12 - \tfrac12 i$, equals $e^{i\pi/4} H$, where $H = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ is the Hadamard matrix.
