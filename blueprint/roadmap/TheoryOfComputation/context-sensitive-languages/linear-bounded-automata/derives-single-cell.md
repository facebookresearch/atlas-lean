# Replacing a single symbol is a derivation step

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets, let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$, and let $G_M$ be the grammar of $M$. Let $u, p, q$ be sentential forms and $x, y$ symbols of $G_M$ such that $u = pxq$ and $x \to y$ is a
production of $G_M$. Then $u$ derives $pyq$ in $G_M$.
