# Every cell of a configuration encoding is active

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be sets, let $w$ be a word over $\Gamma$, let $x$ be a finite list of tape cells
(letters of $\Gamma$ or blanks), let $h$ be a natural number or undefined, let $q \in Q$, and let $t$ and
$N$ be natural numbers. Every cell tuple in the cell-tuple encoding of $(w, x, h, q, t, N)$ has activity
flag $1$.
