# The grammar of an LBA is context-sensitive

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets and let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$, with grammar $G_M$.
Then $G_M$ is context-sensitive: every production of $G_M$ has a nonterminal on its left side and a
right side at least as long as its left side.
