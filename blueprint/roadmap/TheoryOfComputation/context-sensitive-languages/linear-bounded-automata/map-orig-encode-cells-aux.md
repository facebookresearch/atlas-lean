# Original symbols of an encoding are the input word

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be sets, let $w$ be a word over $\Gamma$ and $x$ a list of tape cells (letters of
$\Gamma$ or blanks) with $|w| = |x|$, let $h$ be a natural number or undefined, let $q \in Q$ and let $t, N$
be natural numbers. The list of original symbols of the cell tuples in the cell-tuple encoding of
$(w, x, h, q, t, N)$ is $w$ (as a word of terminal symbols).
