# Reachable LBA configurations keep the tape length and the head on the tape

Let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$. Let $w$ be a nonempty word over $\Gamma$ and let $c$ be a configuration reachable from the initial
configuration of $M$ on $w$ by finitely many (possibly zero) steps of $M$. Then the tape of $c$ has length
$|w|$ and the head position of $c$ is less than $|w|$.
