# The grammar of an LBA

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets and let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape
alphabet $\Gamma$. The grammar $G_M$ of $M$ has terminals $\Gamma$, the nonterminals of the grammar of a
linear bounded automaton (cells, the generator and the start symbol), initial nonterminal the start
symbol, and as productions the generation rules, the activation rules, the simulation rules for stay,
right and left moves, and the conversion rules of $M$.
