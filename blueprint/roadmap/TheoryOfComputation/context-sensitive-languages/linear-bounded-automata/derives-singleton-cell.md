# Singleton-to-singleton productions are derivation steps

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets, let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$, and let $G_M$ be the grammar of $M$. Let $x$ and $y$ be symbols of $G_M$ such that $x \to y$ is a production of $G_M$. Then the
one-letter sentential form $x$ derives the one-letter sentential form $y$ in $G_M$.
