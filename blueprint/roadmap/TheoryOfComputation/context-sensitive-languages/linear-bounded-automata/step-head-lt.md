# LBA steps keep the head on the tape

Let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$. Let $c$ and $c'$ be configurations of $M$ such that $c'$ is obtained from $c$ by one step of $M$, and
suppose the head position of $c$ is less than the length of its tape. Then the head position of $c'$ is
less than the length of the tape of $c'$.
