# Cell tuple as a grammar symbol

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be sets. A cell tuple $(a, x, s, f, \beta)$, with $a \in \Gamma$, $x$ a letter of
$\Gamma$ or the blank, $s$ a state of $Q$ or $\bot$, $f \in \{0, 1\}$ and $\beta$ a boundary tag, is
sent to the grammar symbol given by the cell nonterminal $\langle a, x, s, f, \beta\rangle$ with the same
components.
