# Two-cell replacement at indices $i$ and $j = i + 1$

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets, let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$, and let $G_M$ be the grammar of $M$. Let $u = u_0 \cdots u_{m-1}$ be a sentential form of $G_M$, let $i$ and $j$ be natural numbers with
$i + 1 < m$ and $j = i + 1$, and let $y_1, y_2$ be symbols of $G_M$ such that $u_i u_j \to y_1 y_2$ is a
production of $G_M$. Then $u$ derives in $G_M$ the sentential form obtained from $u$ by replacing $u_i$
by $y_1$ and then $u_j$ by $y_2$.
