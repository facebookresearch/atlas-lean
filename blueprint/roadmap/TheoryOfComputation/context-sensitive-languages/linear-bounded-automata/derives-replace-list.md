# Replacing a block by a rule is a derivation step

Let $Q$ and $\Gamma$ be finite sets, let $M$ be a linear bounded automaton with state set $Q$ and tape alphabet $\Gamma$, and let $G_M$ be the grammar of $M$. Let $u, p, q, \ell, r$ be sentential forms of $G_M$ such that $u = p \ell q$ and $\ell \to r$ is a
production of $G_M$. Then $u$ derives $prq$ in $G_M$ (in finitely many rewriting steps; here one step
suffices).
