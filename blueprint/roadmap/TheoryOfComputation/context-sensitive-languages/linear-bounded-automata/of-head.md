# Boundary tag of a head position

For natural numbers $h$ (a head position) and $n$ (a tape length), the boundary tag $\beta(h, n)$ is: both
if $h = 0$ and $n = 1$; left if $h = 0$ and $n \ne 1$; right if $h \ne 0$ and $h + 1 = n$; and middle
otherwise. In particular $\beta(0, 0)$ is left and $\beta(h, n)$ is middle when $h \ge n$ and $h \ne 0$.
