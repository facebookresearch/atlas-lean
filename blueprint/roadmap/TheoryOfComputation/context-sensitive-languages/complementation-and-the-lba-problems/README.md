# Complementation and the LBA problems

Kuroda asked whether the context-sensitive languages are closed under complementation and whether deterministic linear
bounded automata are as powerful as nondeterministic ones. The first question was settled positively by the theorem of
Immerman and Szelepcsényi that nondeterministic space is closed under complementation; the second remains open.
