# Derivations preserve balancedness

Let $u$ and $v$ be sentential forms of the grammar $G_{abc}$ for $\{a^n b^n c^n : n \ge 1\}$ such that $v$
is derived from $u$ in finitely many (possibly zero) rewriting steps. If $u$ is balanced, that is, has
equal $a$-, $b$- and $c$-counts (counting $B$ as $b$ and $C$ as $c$), then $v$ is balanced.
