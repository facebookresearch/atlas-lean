# Count of b's in a sentential form

The $b$-count of a sentential form $u$ of the grammar $G_{abc}$ (a finite word over the symbols
$a, b, c, S, B, C$) is the number of occurrences of the terminal $b$ in $u$ plus the number of
occurrences of the nonterminal $B$ in $u$.

Each $B$ eventually becomes a $b$, so it is counted as a $b$ not yet produced.
