# Derivation in an unrestricted grammar

Let $G$ be an unrestricted grammar with terminal set $T$ and nonterminal set $N$, and let $u$ and $v$ be
finite words over the disjoint union of $T$ and $N$. Then $v$ is derived from $u$ in $G$ if $v$ is
obtained from $u$ by finitely many (possibly zero) rewriting steps of $G$; that is, derivation is the
reflexive-transitive closure of the one-step rewriting relation of $G$.
