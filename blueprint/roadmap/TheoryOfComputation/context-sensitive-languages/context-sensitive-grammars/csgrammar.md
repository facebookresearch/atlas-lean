# Unrestricted grammar

Let $T$ and $N$ be sets, of terminal and nonterminal symbols. An unrestricted grammar over $T$ and $N$
consists of a finite list of productions $\ell \to r$, where $\ell$ and $r$ are finite words over the
disjoint union of $T$ and $N$ (either may be empty), together with an initial nonterminal in $N$.
