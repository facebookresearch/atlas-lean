# Balanced sentential form

A sentential form $u$ of the grammar $G_{abc}$ (a finite word over the symbols $a, b, c, S, B, C$) is
balanced if its $a$-count, $b$-count and $c$-count are equal, where the $a$-count is the number of
occurrences of $a$ in $u$, the $b$-count is the number of occurrences of $b$ or $B$, and the $c$-count is
the number of occurrences of $c$ or $C$.
