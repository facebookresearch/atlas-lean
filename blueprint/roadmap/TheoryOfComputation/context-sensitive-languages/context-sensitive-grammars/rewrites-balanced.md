# Rewriting steps preserve balancedness

Let $u$ and $v$ be sentential forms of the grammar $G_{abc}$ for $\{a^n b^n c^n : n \ge 1\}$ such that $v$
is obtained from $u$ by one rewriting step. If $u$ is balanced, that is, has equal $a$-, $b$- and
$c$-counts (counting $B$ as $b$ and $C$ as $c$), then $v$ is balanced.

Each production changes the three counts by the same amount, and the surrounding context contributes
equally to the counts of $u$ and of $v$.
