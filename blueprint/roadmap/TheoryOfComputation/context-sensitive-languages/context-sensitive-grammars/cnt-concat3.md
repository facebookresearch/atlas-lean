# Symbol counts are additive over concatenation

Let $p$, $l$, $q$ be sentential forms of the grammar $G_{abc}$ (finite words over the symbols
$a, b, c, S, B, C$) and let $s$ be one of these symbols. The number of occurrences of $s$ in the
concatenation $plq$ is the number of occurrences of $s$ in $p$ plus that in $l$ plus that in $q$.
