# Language of an unrestricted grammar

Let $G$ be an unrestricted grammar with terminal set $T$, nonterminal set $N$ and initial nonterminal
$S$. The language of $G$ is the set of finite words $w$ over $T$ such that $w$, viewed as a word over
the disjoint union of $T$ and $N$, is derived from the one-letter word $S$ in finitely many (possibly
zero) rewriting steps of $G$.
