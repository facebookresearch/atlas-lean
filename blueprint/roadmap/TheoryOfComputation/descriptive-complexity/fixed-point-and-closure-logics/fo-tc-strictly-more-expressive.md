# FO(TC) is strictly more expressive than FO on finite structures

The FO(TC) formula $[\mathrm{tc}_{x,y}\, E(x,y)](x_0, x_1)$ defines the reflexive–transitive closure of $E$ on every finite structure over one binary relation, but no first-order formula defines it on all finite structures. Hence first-order logic is a proper fragment of FO(TC) on finite structures, even without order.

## Proof sketch

The closure formula has the stated meaning by definition; non-definability in first-order logic is Gaifman locality applied to long directed paths.
