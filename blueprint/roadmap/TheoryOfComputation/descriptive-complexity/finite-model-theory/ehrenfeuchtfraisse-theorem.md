# Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé theorem

Let $\mathfrak{A}$ and $\mathfrak{B}$ be structures over a finite relational signature and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $\mathfrak{A}$ and $\mathfrak{B}$ satisfy the same first-order sentences of quantifier rank at most $k$ if and only if Duplicator has a winning strategy in the $k$-round Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game on $\mathfrak{A}$ and $\mathfrak{B}$. As consequences, the class of finite sets of even cardinality is not first-order definable over the empty signature or over finite linear orders, and the class of finite connected graphs is not first-order definable.
