# Pebble games with counting and the Sherali–Adams hierarchy

This node covers the $k$-pebble games on finite structures, with and without counting, and their
correspondence with relaxations of graph isomorphism. The cited source treats
equivalence in the counting pebble game as higher-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman color refinement. Grohe and Otto give a precise
characterization of the level-$k$ Sherali–Adams relaxation of graph isomorphism by a modified counting
pebble game, a variant of the Sherali–Adams levels that matches the $k$-pebble counting game exactly,
and a proof that the interleaving of the two hierarchies is strict; for plain $k$-pebble equivalence,
without counting, they obtain analogous correspondences and separations with Boolean instead of real or
rational arithmetic. At the base level, color refinement corresponds to fractional isomorphism
(Tinhofer; Ramana, Scheinerman and Ullman).

## Expansion

A sound expansion fixes finite relational structures, the $k$-pebble game and the counting $k$-pebble
game as games between two players with explicit rules, the $k$-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman
refinement, and the Sherali–Adams relaxation of the graph-isomorphism linear program. Each
correspondence and separation of the cited source is then a separate page; the logical
characterizations of pebble-game equivalence by finite-variable logics, with and without counting
quantifiers, need their own source.
