# Immerman-Vardi: FO + least fixed point is P on ordered structures

For every class $K$ of finite ordered graphs, the decision problem of the
[encodings of $K$](og-encodings.md) (the bitstrings that decode to a member $(n, t)$ of $K$ whose
edge table $t$ has length exactly $n^2$) is in P if and only if $K$ is
[definable in first-order logic with least fixed points](og-lfpdefinable.md).

## Sources

- Neil Immerman, "Relational queries computable in polynomial time", Information and Control 68 (1986), 86-104 (extended abstract in Proceedings of the Fourteenth Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing, STOC 1982, 147-152).
- Moshe Y. Vardi, "The complexity of relational query languages", Proceedings of the Fourteenth Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1982), 137-146.
