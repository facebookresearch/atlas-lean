# Finite satisfiability and finite validity

A first-order sentence $\varphi$ over a relational vocabulary $\sigma$ is *finitely satisfiable* if it has a finite model, and *finitely valid* if it holds in every finite $\sigma$-structure (with nonempty universe). $\varphi$ is finitely valid iff $\neg\varphi$ is not finitely satisfiable. For a fixed size $n$, satisfiability of $\varphi$ in some structure with universe $\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ is decidable by enumerating the finitely many interpretations of the symbols of $\varphi$, primitive recursively in $\varphi$ and $n$.
