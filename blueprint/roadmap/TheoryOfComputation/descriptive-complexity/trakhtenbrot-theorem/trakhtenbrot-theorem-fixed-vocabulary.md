# Trakhtenbrot's theorem for any vocabulary with a relation of arity at least two

Let $\sigma$ be a relational vocabulary containing at least one relation symbol of arity $\ge 2$. Then finite satisfiability of first-order sentences over $\sigma$ is undecidable, and the finitely valid sentences over $\sigma$ do not form a recursively enumerable set; in particular a single binary relation symbol suffices.

## Proof sketch

Compose the general undecidability with the reductions to one relation symbol and then to a single binary relation; pad arguments to reach arity $l \ge 2$.
