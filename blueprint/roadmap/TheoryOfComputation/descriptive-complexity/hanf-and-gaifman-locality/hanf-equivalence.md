# Hanf equivalence

Two pointed finite structures $(\mathfrak{A},\bar a)$ and $(\mathfrak{B},\bar b)$ are *Hanf-equivalent at radius $d$*, written $(\mathfrak{A},\bar a) \leftrightarrows_d (\mathfrak{B},\bar b)$, if there is a bijection $f : A \to B$ such that for every $c \in A$ the $d$-neighborhoods of $\bar a c$ in $\mathfrak{A}$ and of $\bar b f(c)$ in $\mathfrak{B}$ are isomorphic. For $d = 0$ this says $|A| = |B|$ and the atomic types of single extensions can be matched bijectively.
