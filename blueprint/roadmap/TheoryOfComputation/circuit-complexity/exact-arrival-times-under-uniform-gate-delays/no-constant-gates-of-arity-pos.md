# Over a signature without constants, no gate is constant

A straight-line circuit program has input wires and a sequence of gates, each applying an operation of a gate signature to wires that are inputs or earlier gates. The depth of an input wire is $0$, and the depth of a gate is one more than the largest depth among the wires it reads. A gate arrives at its delay plus the maximum of $0$ and the arrival times of the wires it reads. If every operation of the signature takes at least one argument, then every program over that signature, with any number of inputs and gates, has no constant gates: every gate reads at least one wire.
