# Layouts of multigraphs, cycle rank and the layout hypothesis

For a finite multigraph $G=(V,E)$ (parallel edges allowed), a *layout* is a linear order of $V$; its *width* is the maximum over prefixes $P$ of the number of edges with exactly one endpoint in $P$ (the cutwidth of the order). The *cycle rank* of $G$ is $\beta_1(G)=|E|-|V|+c(G)$, where $c(G)$ is the number of connected components. For $\Delta\ge2$ and $A>0$, the *layout hypothesis* $\mathrm{LB}(\Delta,A)$ states that every multigraph of maximum degree at most $\Delta$ has a layout of width at most $(A+o(1))\beta_1(G)+o(|V|)$.
