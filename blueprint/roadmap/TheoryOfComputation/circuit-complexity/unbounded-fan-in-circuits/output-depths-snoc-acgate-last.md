# Depth of an appended nonconstant flat gate

Let $c$ be a straight-line circuit with $k$ outputs and $g$ a flat gate that is not a constant. If, for
every $t < k$, output $t$ of $c$ has depth $u_t$ (a list $u$ read with default $0$), then the new output
of $c$ with $g$ appended has the flat depth of $g$ computed from $u$.
