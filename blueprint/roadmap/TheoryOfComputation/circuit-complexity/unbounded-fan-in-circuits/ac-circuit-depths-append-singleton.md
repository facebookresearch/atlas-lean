# Measuring one more gate with constants at depth one

The gate depths, with constants at depth one, of the flat circuit $C$ followed by a gate $g$ are those of
$C$ followed by the depth of $g$, computed from the depths of the gates of $C$. No well-formedness is
assumed: a reference of $g$ to a position with no gate of $C$ reads depth $0$, and the largest depth among
no references is $0$.
