# Soundness of earliest changes at the outputs

Let a circuit follow the causal gate-delay model for its contamination delays, and let every input signal be stable on $[0, e_i)$, where $e_i \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ is the input's stated earliest change. Then the signal of every output $o$ is stable on $[0, e_o)$, where $e_o$ is the output's computed earliest change.
