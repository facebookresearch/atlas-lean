# A common lower bound on the inputs bounds a gate's earliest change

For a gate of a straight-line circuit with contamination delay $d \in \mathbb{R}$, earliest change times of its input wires (in $\mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$), and a time $t \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ with $t$ at most the earliest change of every wire the gate reads, $d + t$ is at most the gate's earliest change. Here the gate's earliest change is $d$ plus the minimum of the wires' earliest changes, the minimum taken from $\infty$.
