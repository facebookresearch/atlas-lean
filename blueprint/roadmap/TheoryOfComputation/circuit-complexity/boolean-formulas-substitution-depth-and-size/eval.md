# Evaluation of a Boolean formula

Under $\sigma : \{0, \dots, n-1\} \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$, an input leaf $x_i$ of a [formula](boolean-formula.md) takes $\sigma(i)$, a constant leaf its value, and AND, OR and NOT gates the conjunction, disjunction and negation of their children's values.
