# Evaluation of a substituted formula

For a [formula](boolean-formula.md) $g$ over $n$ inputs, formulas $w_0, \dots, w_{n-1}$ over $m$ inputs and an assignment $\tau$ of the $m$ inputs, the [substituted formula](subst.md) takes under $\tau$ the value $g$ takes under $i \mapsto$ (the value of $w_i$ under $\tau$).
