# Depth of a substituted formula

For a [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) $g$ over $n$ inputs, formulas $w_0, \dots, w_{n-1}$ over $m$ inputs and any natural number $D$ with $\mathrm{depth}(w_i) \le D$ for every $i$ (no maximum need be attained; for $n = 0$ every $D$ qualifies), the [substituted formula](subst.md) has depth at most $\mathrm{depth}(g) + D$: substitution adds depth, it does not multiply it.
