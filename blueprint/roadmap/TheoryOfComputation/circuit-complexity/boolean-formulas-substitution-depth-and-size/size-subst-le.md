# Size of a substituted formula, weaker form

Let $g$ be a Boolean formula over $n$ inputs and substitute for each input $i$ a formula $w_i$
over $m$ inputs, each with at most $D$ gates. Then the substituted formula has at most
$\mathrm{size}(g)(D + 1) + (D + 1)$ gates, one more than the tight bound
$\mathrm{size}(g)(D + 1) + D$.
