# The multiplexer output is false during the glitch interval

This example models a two-path multiplexer with data inputs $b = c = \mathrm{true}$ under transport delay: at time $0$ the select signal switches, so that the positive path becomes true after its delay $d_1 \ge 0$ and the complemented path becomes false after its delay $d_2 \ge 0$, and the two paths are combined by an output OR gate with delay $d_{\mathrm{or}} \ge 0$. Signals are Boolean functions of real time. Let $d_1, d_2, d_{\mathrm{or}} \ge 0$ with $d_2 < d_1$. Then the output signal of the unrepaired multiplexer is false at every time $t$ with $d_2 + d_{\mathrm{or}} \le t < d_1 + d_{\mathrm{or}}$.
