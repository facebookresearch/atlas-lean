# A gate with no inputs arrives at its delay

A straight-line circuit program over a gate signature has a fixed number of input wires and a sequence of gates; each gate applies an operation of the signature to a list of wires (inputs or earlier gates), one for each argument position of the operation. If the operation of a gate takes no arguments, then for any arrival times of the wires and any real delay $d$, the arrival time of the gate's output is exactly $d$.
