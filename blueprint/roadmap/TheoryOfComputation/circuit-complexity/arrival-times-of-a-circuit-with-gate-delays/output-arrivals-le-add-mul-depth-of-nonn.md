# Output arrival times are bounded by the circuit depth for any nonnegative delay bound

Let a circuit have gate delays at most $D$, where $D \ge 0$ (delays may be negative), and input
arrival times at most $a_0$, where $a_0 \ge 0$. Then every designated output arrives no later than
$a_0 + D \cdot d$, where $d$ is the depth of the circuit.
