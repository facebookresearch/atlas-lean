# Affine dispersers

A Boolean function $f:\mathbb{F}_2^n\to\{0,1\}$ is an *affine disperser for dimension $d$* if $f$ is nonconstant on every affine subspace of $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ of dimension at least $d$ (equivalently, of dimension exactly $d$). A family $(f_n)$ is *explicit* if it is computable in time polynomial in $n$.
