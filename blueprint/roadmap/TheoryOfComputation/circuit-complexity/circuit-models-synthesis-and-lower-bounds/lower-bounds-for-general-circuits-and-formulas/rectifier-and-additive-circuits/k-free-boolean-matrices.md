# k-free (rectangle-free) Boolean matrices

A Boolean matrix $A$ is *$(k,\ell)$-free* if it contains no $k\times\ell$ all-ones submatrix (rectangle), and *$k$-free* if it is $(k,k)$-free. A $2$-free matrix is the bipartite adjacency matrix of a graph without $4$-cycles $K_{2,2}$.

## Sources

- Stasys Jukna, Igor Sergeev, "Complexity of Linear Boolean Operators", Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science 9(1), 1–123, 2013.
- William G. Brown, "On Graphs That Do Not Contain a Thomsen Graph", Canadian Mathematical Bulletin 9(3), 281–285, 1966.
