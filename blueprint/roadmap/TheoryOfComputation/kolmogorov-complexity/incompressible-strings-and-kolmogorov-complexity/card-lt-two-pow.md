# Few strings have small complexity

Let $m$ be a natural number and let $S$ be a finite set of binary strings, each of Kolmogorov
complexity less than $m$. Then $S$ has fewer than $2^m$ elements.

Padding the shortest descriptions of the strings of $S$ to length $m$ maps $S$ injectively to the
binary strings of length $m$ other than $0^m$.
