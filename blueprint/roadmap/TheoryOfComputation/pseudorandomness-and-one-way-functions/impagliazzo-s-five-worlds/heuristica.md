# Heuristica

Heuristica is the statement that $\mathrm{P} \ne \mathrm{NP}$ and that every decision problem $L$ in
NP is easy on average with respect to every samplable distribution: for every polynomial $p$ with
natural-number coefficients and every family of samplers $S = (S_n)_n$, where $S_n$ maps $p(n)$
random bits to a bitstring, if $S$ is samplable then $L$ is easy on average with respect to $S$.
