# Equivalence of Grassmann, shortcode and bilinear small-set expansion

Let $\mathrm{Bil}(n, \ell)$ be the bilinear scheme graph on matrices $M \in \mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ in which $M \sim M'$ iff $\operatorname{rank}(M - M') = 1$, and let the **shortcode graph** be the Cayley graph on $\mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ generated by rank-one matrices. Then, up to changes of the parameters $r$ and $\varepsilon$ by constant factors, the following statements are equivalent: (i) pseudorandom sets in $\mathrm{Gr}(n, \ell)$ have expansion $1 - o(1)$; (ii) every set in $\mathrm{Bil}(n, \ell)$ of expansion at most $1 - \delta$ has density $\Omega_\delta(1)$ in some affine sub-scheme of bounded codimension (a zoom of bounded order); (iii) the same for the shortcode graph. Moreover each of these implies the soundness of the $2$-to-$2$ reduction from the Grassmann test.

## Proof sketch

The bilinear scheme is the set of $\ell$-dimensional subspaces of $\mathbb{F}_2^{n+\ell}$ that are complementary to a fixed $n$-dimensional subspace, an open cell of $\mathrm{Gr}(n+\ell, \ell)$ in which zoom-ins and zoom-outs correspond to affine sub-schemes; the inclusion is measure-preserving up to constants and maps edges to edges, so expansion statements transfer in both directions. The shortcode graph is the Cayley-graph form of the same scheme.
