# Equivalence of Grassmann, shortcode and bilinear small-set expansion

Let $\mathrm{Bil}(n, \ell)$ be the bilinear scheme graph on matrices $M \in \mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ in which $M \sim M'$ iff $\operatorname{rank}(M - M') = 1$; equivalently, the (degree-two) **shortcode graph**, the Cayley graph on $\mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ generated by the rank-one matrices. A set $T \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ is **$r$-nice** (a zoom of order $r$) if $T = \{M : Mx_i = y_i\ (i \le a),\ z_j^{\top} M = w_j^{\top}\ (j \le b)\}$ for some $x_i \in \mathbb{F}_2^{\ell}$, $y_i, z_j \in \mathbb{F}_2^{n}$, $w_j \in \mathbb{F}_2^{\ell}$ with $a + b \le r$. Then the following are equivalent:

1. (Grassmann expansion) for every $\eta > 0$ there are $\delta, r$ such that every set $S$ of vertices of $\mathrm{Gr}(n, \ell)$ with $\Pr_{L \sim S,\ L' \sim L}[L' \in S] \ge \eta$ satisfies $\Pr_{L : Q \subseteq L \subseteq W}[L \in S] \ge \delta$ for some $Q \subseteq W$ with $\dim Q + \operatorname{codim} W \le r$;
2. (inverse shortcode hypothesis) for every $\eta > 0$ there are $\delta, r$ such that every $S \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^{n \times \ell}$ with $\Pr_{M \sim S,\ a \sim \mathbb{F}_2^{\ell},\ b \sim \mathbb{F}_2^{n}}[M + b a^{\top} \in S] \ge \eta$ satisfies $|S \cap T| \ge \delta |T|$ for some $r$-nice $T$.

Moreover the second statement implies the soundness of the shortcode consistency test and hence of the Grassmann test, the $2$-to-$2$ inner test of the $2$-to-$2$ Games Theorem.

## Proof sketch

The bilinear scheme is the set of $\ell$-dimensional subspaces of $\mathbb{F}_2^{n+\ell}$ that are complementary to a fixed $n$-dimensional subspace, an open cell of $\mathrm{Gr}(n+\ell, \ell)$ in which zoom-ins and zoom-outs correspond to nice sets; the inclusion is measure-preserving up to constants and maps edges to edges, so expansion statements transfer in both directions. The shortcode graph is the Cayley-graph form of the same scheme.
