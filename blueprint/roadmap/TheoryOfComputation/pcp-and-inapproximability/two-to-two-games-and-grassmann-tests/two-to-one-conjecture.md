# Khot's 2-to-1 games conjecture with perfect completeness

The **$2$-to-$1$ games conjecture** asserts: for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $R = R(\varepsilon)$ such that, given a $2$-to-$1$ label-cover game (each $\Phi_e$ is the graph of a $2$-to-$1$ map $[2R] \to [R]$), it is NP-hard to distinguish whether its value is $1$ or at most $\varepsilon$. The analogous **$2$-to-$2$ games conjecture** with perfect completeness asks the same for $2$-to-$2$ constraints. With completeness $1 - \varepsilon$ in place of $1$, the statement is a theorem.
