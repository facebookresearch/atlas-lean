# Characters are $\pm 1$-valued

Every character $\chi_S(x) = \prod_{i \in S} x_i$ takes only the values $1$ and $-1$ on
$\{-1, 1\}^n$, since $\chi_S(x)^2 = 1$. The characters are the Boolean-valued linear functions
of the BLR test.

## Sources

- Ryan O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Section 1.3 (Theorem 1.5) and Definition 1.28.
