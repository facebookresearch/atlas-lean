# Monochromatic-1 rectangle

For $f : X \times Y \to \{\mathrm{false}, \mathrm{true}\}$ and finite sets $A \subseteq X$ and $B \subseteq Y$, the rectangle $A \times B$ is monochromatic-1 for $f$ when $f(x, y) = \mathrm{true}$ for all $x \in A$ and $y \in B$. The types $X$ and $Y$ need not be finite.
