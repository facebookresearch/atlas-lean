# A monochromatic-1 rectangle meets a fooling set at most once

Let $S$ be a finite [fooling set](../deterministic-communication-protocols/is-fooling-set.md) for $f$ with value true, and let $A \times B$ be a [monochromatic-1 rectangle](rect-mono.md) with finite sides. If $p, q \in S$ both lie in $A \times B$, then $p = q$: otherwise one of the crossed inputs $(p_1, q_2)$ and $(q_1, p_2)$, which also lie in $A \times B$, would have value false.
