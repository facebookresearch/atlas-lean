# Untyped lambda terms with de Bruijn indices

Untyped lambda terms with de Bruijn indices are generated by the grammar

$$t ::= \underline{k} \mid t\, t \mid \lambda.\, t \qquad (k \in \mathbb{N}),$$

where $\underline{k}$ is the variable with index $k$, referring to the $k$-th enclosing binder counted from the inside starting at $0$ (or, if there are fewer binders, to a free variable), $t\,u$ is application and $\lambda.\,t$ is abstraction.

## Sources

- H. P. Barendregt, *The Lambda Calculus: Its Syntax and Semantics*, North-Holland, 1984, §2.1.
