# Garden-of-Eden theorem

Let $G$ be a $d$-dimensional [cellular automaton](cellular-automata-on-z-d.md) with a quiescent state, and let $G_F$ be its
restriction to [finite configurations](finite-periodic-and-garden-of-eden-configurations.md). Then $G_F$ is injective if and
only if $G$ is surjective. Equivalently, $G$ has a Garden-of-Eden configuration if and only if two different finite
configurations have the same image. The direction from surjectivity to injectivity on finite configurations is Moore's
(1962), and the converse is Myhill's (1963).
