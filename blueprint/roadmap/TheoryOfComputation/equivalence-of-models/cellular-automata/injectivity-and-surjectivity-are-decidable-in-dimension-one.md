# Injectivity and surjectivity of one-dimensional cellular automata are decidable

There are algorithms that decide, given a one-dimensional [cellular automaton](cellular-automata-on-z-d.md) by its state set,
neighborhood and local rule, whether its global transition function is injective, and whether it is surjective (Amoroso
and Patt 1972). Hence reversibility of one-dimensional cellular automata is decidable. Later decision procedures work on de
Bruijn graphs (Sutner).
