# A predicate recognized by a single-tape NTM is recognized by a deterministic single-tape machine

Let $\alpha$ be a type with a primitive recursive encoding into $\mathbb{N}$. Every predicate on
$\alpha$ that is recognizable by a single-tape nondeterministic machine (in the model of this
topic) is recognizable by a single-tape deterministic Turing machine.

## Sources

- M. Sipser, *Introduction to the Theory of Computation*, 3rd ed., Cengage Learning, 2013,
  Theorem 3.16 (here through recursion theory rather than a breadth-first simulation).
