# A contiguous marked tape

For a head cell content $a$ (a source symbol or blank) and lists $l$ and $r$ of source symbols or blanks, the marked tape has under its head the visited cell holding $a$, to its left the visited cells holding the entries of $l$ (nearest first), and to its right the visited cells holding the entries of $r$ (nearest first); visited source blanks are marked cells, and every cell beyond these lists is unvisited.
