# The weak call-by-value λ-calculus and its time and space measures

Terms of the weak call-by-value λ-calculus $\mathsf{L}$ are de Bruijn terms $s, t ::= n \mid s\,t \mid \lambda s$ with $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Reduction $s \succ t$ is the deterministic relation generated by

- $(\lambda s)(\lambda t) \succ s^0_{\lambda t}$, where $s^0_{\lambda t}$ substitutes $\lambda t$ for index $0$ in $s$;
- $s\,t \succ s'\,t$ whenever $s \succ s'$;
- $(\lambda s)\,t \succ (\lambda s)\,t'$ whenever $t \succ t'$.

No reduction takes place under an abstraction. Substitution is the source's capturing, non-shifting operation: $n^k_u$ is $u$ when $n=k$ and $n$ otherwise, $(s\,t)^k_u=s^k_u\,t^k_u$, and $(\lambda s)^k_u=\lambda(s^{k+1}_u)$. On the closed terms used for computation, the substituted abstraction is closed, so this operation agrees with the usual substitution there. The only closed irreducible terms are abstractions. The size of a term is $\|n\| = 1 + n$, $\|\lambda s\| = 1 + \|s\|$ and $\|s\,t\| = 1 + \|s\| + \|t\|$ (indices counted in unary). For a closed term $s$ with a reduction $s = s_0 \succ s_1 \succ \dots \succ s_k$ ending in an abstraction, the **time** of $s$ is $\|s\|_T = k$, the number of β-steps, and the **space** of $s$ is $\|s\|_S = \max_{0 \le i \le k} \|s_i\|$, the size of the largest intermediate term.

A function $f : \Sigma^* \to \Sigma^*$ is **computable by $\mathsf{L}$ in time $T$ and space $S$** if there is a closed term $s_f$ with $s_f\,\ulcorner x \urcorner \succ^* \ulcorner f(x) \urcorner$, $\|s_f\,\ulcorner x \urcorner\|_T \le T(|x|)$ and $\|s_f\,\ulcorner x \urcorner\|_S \le S(|x|)$ for every $x \in \Sigma^*$, where $\ulcorner \cdot \urcorner$ is a fixed encoding of strings over $\Sigma$ as terms (the source uses Scott encodings for its simulation of Turing machines).
