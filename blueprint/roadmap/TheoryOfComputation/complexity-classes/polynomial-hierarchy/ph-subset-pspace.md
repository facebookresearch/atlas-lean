# The polynomial hierarchy is contained in PSPACE

Every decision problem in the polynomial
hierarchy PH belongs to PSPACE.

## Sources

- L. J. Stockmeyer, "The polynomial-time hierarchy", *Theoretical Computer Science* 3 (1976), 1–22; S. Arora and B. Barak, *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach*, Cambridge University Press, 2009, Theorem 5.4.
