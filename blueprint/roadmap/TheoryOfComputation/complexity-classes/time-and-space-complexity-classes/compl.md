# Polynomial many-one reductions also reduce complements

Decision problems are maps from bitstrings to $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$, and
polynomial-time maps are computed in polynomial time with the canonical bitstring encodings.
If a decision problem $L$ reduces to a decision problem $K$ by a polynomial-time many-one reduction,
then the complement of $L$ reduces to the complement of $K$ by a polynomial-time many-one reduction
(the same reduction map works).
