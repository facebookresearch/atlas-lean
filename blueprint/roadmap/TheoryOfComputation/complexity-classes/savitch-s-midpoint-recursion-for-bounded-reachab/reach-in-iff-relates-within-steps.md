# The Savitch predicate decides bounded reachability

For a decidable relation $R$ on a finite type with decidable equality, the [Savitch midpoint predicate](reach-in.md), configurations $x, y$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the
Savitch midpoint predicate at level $k$ accepts $(x, y)$ exactly when $y$ is reachable from $x$ in
at most $2^k$ steps of $R$.
