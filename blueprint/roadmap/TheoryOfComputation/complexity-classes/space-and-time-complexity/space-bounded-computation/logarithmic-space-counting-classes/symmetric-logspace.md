# Symmetric logarithmic space SL

$\mathsf{SL}$ is the class of languages logarithmic-space many-one reducible to undirected $s$–$t$ connectivity $\mathrm{USTCON}$ (equivalently, by Lewis and Papadimitriou, decided by symmetric nondeterministic logspace machines whose transition relation is closed under reversal). $\mathrm{USTCON}$ is $\mathsf{SL}$-complete and $\mathsf{L}\subseteq\mathsf{SL}\subseteq\mathsf{NL}$.
