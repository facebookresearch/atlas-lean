# Completeness of the Savitch predicate

Let $C$ be a finite type with decidable equality and $R$ a decidable relation on $C$, and let reachIn be the [Savitch midpoint predicate](reach-in.md). If there is a path from $x$ to $y$ of at most $2^k$ steps of $R$, then the predicate accepts $(x, y)$ at level $k$.
