# The Boolean hierarchy lies in Σ₂ ∩ Π₂

$\mathsf{BH}\subseteq\Sigma_2^p\cap\Pi_2^p$; in particular $\mathsf{DP}\cup\mathsf{coDP}\subseteq\Sigma_2^p\cap\Pi_2^p$.

## Proof sketch

$\Sigma_2^p\cap\Pi_2^p$ is closed under union, intersection and complement and contains $\mathsf{NP}$.
