# Definition (PSPACE and NPSPACE)

$\mathsf{PSPACE} = \bigcup_k \mathsf{SPACE}(n^k)$ (polynomial space). $\mathsf{NPSPACE} = \bigcup_k \mathsf{NSPACE}(n^k)$ (nondeterministic polynomial space).
