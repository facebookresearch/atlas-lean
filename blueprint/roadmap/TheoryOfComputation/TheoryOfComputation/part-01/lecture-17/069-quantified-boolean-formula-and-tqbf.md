# Definition (Quantified Boolean Formula and TQBF)

A quantified Boolean formula (QBF) is a Boolean formula with leading $\exists x$ and $\forall x$ quantifiers, where all variables must lie within the scope of a quantifier. A QBF is True or False. $\mathrm{TQBF} = \{\langle \phi \rangle \mid \phi \text{ is a QBF that is TRUE}\}$.
