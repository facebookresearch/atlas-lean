# Definition (SPACE complexity)

Let $f \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ where $f(n) \geq n$. Say TM $M$ runs in space $f(n)$ if $M$ always halts and uses at most $f(n)$ tape cells on all inputs of length $n$.
