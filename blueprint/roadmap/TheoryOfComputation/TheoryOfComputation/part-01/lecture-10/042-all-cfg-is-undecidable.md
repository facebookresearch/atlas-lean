# Theorem (ALL_CFG is undecidable)

$\mathrm{ALL}_{\mathrm{CFG}}$ is undecidable, where $\mathrm{ALL}_{\mathrm{CFG}} = \{\langle G \rangle \mid G \text{ is a CFG and } L(G) = \Sigma^*\}$.
