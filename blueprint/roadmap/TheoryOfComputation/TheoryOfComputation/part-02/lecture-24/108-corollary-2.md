# Corollary 2 (Lecture 24, Roots of Polynomials)

If $p(x) \neq 0$ has degree $\leq d$ and we pick a random $r \in \mathbb{F}_q$, then $\Pr[p(r) = 0] \leq \frac{d}{q}$.
