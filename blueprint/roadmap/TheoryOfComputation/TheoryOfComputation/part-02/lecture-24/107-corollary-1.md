# Corollary 1 (Lecture 24, Roots of Polynomials)

If $p_1(x)$ and $p_2(x)$ are both degree $\leq d$ and $p_1 \neq p_2$ then $p_1(a) = p_2(a)$ for $\leq d$ values $a$.
