# LST lower bound for set-multilinear formulas computing IMM

In the setting of the relative-rank upper bound, every set-multilinear formula of product-depth at most $\Delta$ computing a word polynomial $P_w$ (in particular, after projection, $\mathrm{IMM}_{n,d}$ with $2^k\le n$) has at least $2^{k(d^{1/(4^\Delta-1)}/20-1/2)}$ nodes.

## Proof sketch

$P_w$ has relative rank one, while small set-multilinear formulas have relative rank at most $s\,2^{-k(d^{1/(4^\Delta-1)}/20-1/2)}$.
