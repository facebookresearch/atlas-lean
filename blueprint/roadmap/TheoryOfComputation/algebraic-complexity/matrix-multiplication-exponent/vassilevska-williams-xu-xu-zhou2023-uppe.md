# Vassilevska Williams–Xu–Xu–Zhou upper bound for matrix multiplication

For every field
$F$, the square matrix multiplication exponent of $F$ in the arithmetic-circuit model is at
most $2.371552$.

## Sources

- V. Vassilevska Williams, Y. Xu, Z. Xu and R. Zhou, "New bounds for matrix multiplication: from alpha to omega", *Proceedings of the 2024 Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2024)*, 3792–3835; arXiv:2307.07970.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15a}$ (the matrix multiplication exponent $\omega$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15a.md.
