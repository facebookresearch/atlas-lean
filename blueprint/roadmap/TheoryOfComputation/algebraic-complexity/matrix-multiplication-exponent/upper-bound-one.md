# Dual exponent at most one

For every field $F$, the dual matrix multiplication
exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies $\alpha(F) \le 1$.

## Sources

- M. Christandl, F. Le Gall, V. Lysikov and J. Zuiddam, "Barriers for rectangular matrix multiplication", *Computational Complexity* 34 (2025), article 4; arXiv:2003.03019. Figure 2, the general upper bound $\alpha \le 1$.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15b}$ (the dual matrix multiplication exponent $\alpha$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15b.md.
