# Stothers–Davie bound on the matrix multiplication exponent

For every field $F$, the
square matrix multiplication exponent $\omega(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\omega(F) \le 2.373689703$.

## Sources

- A. J. Stothers, *On the complexity of matrix multiplication*, PhD thesis, University of Edinburgh, 2010.
- A. M. Davie and A. J. Stothers, "Improved bound for complexity of matrix multiplication", *Proceedings of the Royal Society of Edinburgh, Section A* 143 (2013), 351–369.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15a}$ (the matrix multiplication exponent $\omega$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15a.md.
