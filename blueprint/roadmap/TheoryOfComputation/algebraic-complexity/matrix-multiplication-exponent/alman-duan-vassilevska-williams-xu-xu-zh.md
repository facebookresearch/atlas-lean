# Alman–Duan–Vassilevska Williams–Xu–Xu–Zhou upper bound for matrix multiplication

For
every field $F$, the square matrix multiplication exponent of $F$ in the arithmetic-circuit
model is at most $2.371339$.

## Sources

- J. Alman, R. Duan, V. Vassilevska Williams, Y. Xu, Z. Xu and R. Zhou, "More asymmetry yields faster matrix multiplication", *Proceedings of the 2025 Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2025)*, 2005–2039; arXiv:2404.16349.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15a}$ (the matrix multiplication exponent $\omega$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15a.md.
