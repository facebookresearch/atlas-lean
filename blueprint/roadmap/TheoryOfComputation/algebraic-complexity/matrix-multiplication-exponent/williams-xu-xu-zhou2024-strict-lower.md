# Vassilevska Williams–Xu–Xu–Zhou bound on the dual exponent

For every field $F$, the dual matrix
multiplication exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\alpha(F) > 0.321334$.

## Sources

- V. Vassilevska Williams, Y. Xu, Z. Xu and R. Zhou, "New bounds for matrix multiplication: from alpha to omega", *Proceedings of the 2024 Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2024)*, 3792–3835; arXiv:2307.07970. Introduction, the bound $\alpha > 0.321334$.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15b}$ (the dual matrix multiplication exponent $\alpha$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15b.md.
