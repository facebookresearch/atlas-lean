# The rectangular exponent depends only on the characteristic

If two infinite fields $F$ and $K$ have the same characteristic, then for every real $k \ge 0$ the
rectangular matrix multiplication exponents of $F$ and $K$ at aspect $k$ in the arithmetic-circuit
model are equal.

## Sources

- F. Le Gall, "Faster algorithms for rectangular matrix multiplication", *2012 IEEE 53rd Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 2012)*, 514–523; arXiv:1204.1111. Section 1, on the dependence of the exponents on the characteristic of the field.
