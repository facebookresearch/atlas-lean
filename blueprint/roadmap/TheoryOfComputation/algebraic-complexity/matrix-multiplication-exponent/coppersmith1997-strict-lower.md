# Coppersmith's 1997 bound on the dual exponent

For every field $F$, the dual matrix
multiplication exponent $\alpha(F)$ of the arithmetic-circuit model satisfies
$\alpha(F) > 0.29462$.

## Sources

- F. Le Gall, "Faster algorithms for rectangular matrix multiplication", *2012 IEEE 53rd Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 2012)*, 514–523; arXiv:1204.1111. Abstract, which quotes Coppersmith's bound $\alpha > 0.29462$ (*Journal of Complexity*, 1997).
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15b}$ (the dual matrix multiplication exponent $\alpha$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15b.md.
