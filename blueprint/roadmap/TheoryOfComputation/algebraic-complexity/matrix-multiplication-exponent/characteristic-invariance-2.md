# The square exponent depends only on the characteristic

If two infinite fields $F$ and $K$ have the same characteristic, then the square matrix
multiplication exponents of $F$ and $K$ in the arithmetic-circuit model are equal.

## Sources

- A. Schönhage, "Partial and total matrix multiplication", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 10 (1981), 434–455. The exponent depends only on the characteristic of the field.
- Optimization Constants in Mathematics, constant $C_{15a}$ (the matrix multiplication exponent $\omega$), https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/blob/87b4ebbd426ff8db597a2004cc45da17903bdcf2/constants/15a.md.
