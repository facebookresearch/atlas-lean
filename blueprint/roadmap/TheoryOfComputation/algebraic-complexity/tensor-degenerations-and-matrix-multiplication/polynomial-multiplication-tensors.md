# Polynomial multiplication tensors

For $a,b\ge1$ the *full polynomial multiplication tensor* $\mathrm{Full}_{a,b}$ is the tensor of the bilinear map sending a polynomial of length $a$ (degree below $a$) and a polynomial of length $b$ to all $a+b-1$ coefficients of their product; its rank over an infinite field is $a+b-1$.
