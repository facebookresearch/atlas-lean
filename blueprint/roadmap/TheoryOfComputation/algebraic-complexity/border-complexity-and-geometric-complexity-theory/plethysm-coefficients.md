# Plethysm coefficients

For partitions $\lambda$ of $nD$ and integers $n, D$, the **plethysm coefficient** $a_\lambda(D[n])$ is the multiplicity of the irreducible $\mathrm{GL}_m$-representation $V_\lambda$ in the representation $\mathrm{Sym}^{D}(\mathrm{Sym}^{n}\mathbb{C}^m)$ of polynomials of degree $D$ on the space of degree-$n$ forms. These multiplicities bound the multiplicities in coordinate rings of orbit closures from above: for every form $f$ of degree $n$ in $m$ variables, the multiplicity of $V_\lambda$ in the degree-$D$ part of $\mathbb{C}[\overline{\mathrm{GL}_m \cdot f}]$ is at most $a_\lambda(D[n])$.
