# Bürgisser–Ikenmeyer–Panova: no occurrence obstructions for permanent versus determinant

Let $n \ge m^{25}$ and let $\lambda$ be any partition of $nD$ with at most $n^2$ parts. If $\gamma_\lambda(m, n) > 0$, i.e. $V_\lambda$ occurs in the coordinate ring of $\overline{\mathrm{GL}_{n^2}\cdot \ell^{n-m}\mathrm{perm}_m}$, then $\delta_\lambda(n) > 0$, i.e. $V_\lambda$ also occurs in the coordinate ring of $\overline{\mathrm{GL}_{n^2}\cdot\det_n}$. Consequently occurrence obstructions cannot prove superpolynomial lower bounds on $\underline{\mathrm{dc}}(\mathrm{perm}_m)$ (for $n$ polynomially larger than $m$). Independently, Ikenmeyer and Panova showed that rectangular Kronecker coefficients cannot vanish in the required pattern either.

## Proof sketch

Representations occurring in the padded permanent orbit closure must have a long first row because of the padding, by a theorem of Kadish-Landsberg. The authors show that for every such $\lambda$ the corresponding multiplicity in the coordinate ring of the determinant orbit, which is bounded below by a rectangular Kronecker coefficient times a plethysm coefficient, is positive; positivity is shown by explicit constructions with tableaux and semigroup properties of Kronecker coefficients.
