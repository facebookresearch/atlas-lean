# The partial-derivative measure

For $f\in K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ and $k\ge0$, let $\partial^{=k}f$ be the set of all $k$-th order partial derivatives of $f$, and let $\mu_k(f)=\dim_K\operatorname{span}\partial^{=k}f$. The measure is subadditive, $\mu_k(f+g)\le\mu_k(f)+\mu_k(g)$, and for a product of $d$ affine forms $\mu_k(\ell_1\cdots\ell_d)\le\binom dk$.
