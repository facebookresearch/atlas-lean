# Matrix rigidity

For a matrix $A$ over a field $K$ and $r\ge0$, the *rigidity* $R_A(r)$ is the least number of entries of $A$ that must be changed to obtain a matrix of rank at most $r$: $R_A(r)=\min\{|\mathrm{supp}(A-B)|:\operatorname{rank}B\le r\}$.
