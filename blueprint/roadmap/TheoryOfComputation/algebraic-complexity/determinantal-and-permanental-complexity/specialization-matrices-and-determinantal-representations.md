# Specialization matrices and determinantal representations

A *specialization matrix* over $K[x_1,\dots,x_N]$ is a square matrix each of whose entries is a variable or a constant. A polynomial $f$ is a projection of the generic $m\times m$ determinant iff $f=\det M$ for an $m\times m$ specialization matrix $M$ (similarly for the permanent). More generally, $f$ has a *determinantal representation of size $m$* if $f=\det M$ for an $m\times m$ matrix $M$ with affine-linear entries; the *determinantal complexity* $\mathrm{dc}(f)\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty\}$ is the least such $m$.
