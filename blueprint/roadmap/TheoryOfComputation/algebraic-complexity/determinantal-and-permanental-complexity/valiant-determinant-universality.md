# Valiant's universality: every polynomial is a projection of a generic determinant

Over every commutative ring $K$ and for an arbitrary set of variables, every polynomial $f$ is a projection of the generic $m\times m$ determinant for some $m$. In particular every polynomial has finite determinantal complexity, and $\mathrm{dc}(f)$ is attained by some finite matrix.

## Proof sketch

Every polynomial is computed by some arithmetic formula (a sum of monomials), and formulas are projections of determinants of size one more than their number of operation gates.
