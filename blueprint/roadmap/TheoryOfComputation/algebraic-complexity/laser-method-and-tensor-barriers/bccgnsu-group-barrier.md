# Blasiak et al.: slice rank obstructs the group-theoretic approach over bounded-exponent abelian groups

The **slice rank** of a tensor $T \in \mathbb{F}^{X \times Y \times Z}$ is the least $r$ such that $T$ is a sum of $r$ tensors each of the form $f(x) g(y, z)$, $f(y) g(x, z)$ or $f(z) g(x, y)$. For every finite abelian group $H$ generated by elements of order at most $m$, every tricolored sum-free set in $H$ has size at most $3\,|H|^{1 - \varepsilon/m}$, where $\varepsilon = 0.02831\ldots$ is an absolute constant; the proof bounds the slice rank, over a finite field, of the tensor $\sum_{a + b + c = 0} x_a y_b z_c$. Consequently, for every $\ell$ there is $\varepsilon_\ell > 0$ such that no construction satisfying the simultaneous triple product property in an abelian group of exponent at most $\ell$ yields a bound better than $\omega \le 2 + \varepsilon_\ell$: abelian groups of bounded exponent do not yield $\omega = 2$ via the Cohn-Kleinberg-Szegedy-Umans approach.

## Proof sketch

The tensor $\sum_{a + b + c = 0} x_a y_b z_c$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$, indexed by $H = \mathbb{Z}_p^n$, has slice rank $O(\Gamma_p^n)$ with $\Gamma_p < p$, by the Croot-Lev-Pach / Ellenberg-Gijswijt polynomial method as reformulated by Tao; a reduction extends the bound to all abelian groups of bounded exponent. The slice rank upper-bounds the size of every tricolored sum-free set, every construction satisfying the simultaneous triple product property yields a large tricolored sum-free set, and such a construction proving $\omega = 2$ would require one of size $|H|^{1 - o(1)}$.
