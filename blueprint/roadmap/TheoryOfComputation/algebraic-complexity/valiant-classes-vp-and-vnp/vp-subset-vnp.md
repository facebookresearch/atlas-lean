# VP is contained in VNP, and VNP is closed under p-projections

$\mathsf{VP}_K\subseteq\mathsf{VNP}_K$, every family in $\mathsf{VNP}_K$ is a p-family, and $\mathsf{VNP}_K$ is closed under p-projections. Consequently, if $h$ is $\mathsf{VNP}$-complete then $h\in\mathsf{VP}_K$ if and only if $\mathsf{VP}_K=\mathsf{VNP}_K$.

## Proof sketch

Take $m=0$ for the inclusion. Boolean summation of a family of polynomial degree yields polynomial degree; substituting a projection inside $g$ commutes with the Boolean sum. If a complete family is in $\mathsf{VP}$, every $\mathsf{VNP}$ family is a p-projection of a $\mathsf{VP}$ family.
