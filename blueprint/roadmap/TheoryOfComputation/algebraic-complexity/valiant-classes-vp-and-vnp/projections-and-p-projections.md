# Projections and p-projections

A polynomial $g\in K[y_1,\dots,y_m]$ is a *projection* of $f\in K[x_1,\dots,x_N]$ if $g=f(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_N)$ where each $\sigma_i$ is a variable $y_j$ or a constant of $K$. A family $g$ is a *p-projection* of a family $f$ if there is a polynomially bounded $t(n)$ such that $g_n$ is a projection of $f_{t(n)}$ for every $n$. p-projections are transitive.
