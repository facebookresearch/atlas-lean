# Homogeneous depth-five (ΣΠΣΠΣ) circuits

A *syntactically homogeneous $\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\Sigma$ circuit* computes a sum of products of sums of products of linear forms in which every gate computes a homogeneous polynomial of its formal degree. Its size is measured by the number of lower product gates $S_1$, middle sum gates and upper product gates $S_3$ (bottom linear forms are unrestricted and gates may be shared).
