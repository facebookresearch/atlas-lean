# A rank measure from differentiation and multiplication

Split the variables into a set $s$ and its complement. For a polynomial $g$ and a bidegree $(k,m)$, let $\Phi_{k,m}(g)$ be the linear map sending a pair (a $k$-th order partial derivative $\partial^\alpha$ in the variables of $s$, a monomial $x^\beta$ of degree $m$ in the complementary variables) to the component in a fixed bihomogeneous slice of $x^\beta\cdot\partial^\alpha g$ (shifted partial derivatives). The *rank measure* $\rho_{k,m}(g)$ is the rank of the matrix of $\Phi_{k,m}(g)$ in the monomial bases; $D$ denotes the dimension of the slice on which it acts. The measure is subadditive and satisfies a product rule bounding $\rho$ of a product of homogeneous polynomials of degrees $e_j$ by $2D\prod_jH(e_j)$ with explicit weights $H(e)=\sum_{i\le e}q^{|i-\lambda e|}$.
