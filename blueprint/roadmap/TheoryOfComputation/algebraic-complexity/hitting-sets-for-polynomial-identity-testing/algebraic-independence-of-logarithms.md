# Algebraic independence of the logarithmic curve

Over a field $F$ of characteristic zero, the power series $\ln(1+(i+1)t)\in F[[t]]$, $i=0,\dots,n-1$, are algebraically independent over $F[t]$.

## Proof sketch

Take a nonzero relation $g$ of least degree $d$ in the series variables with fewest top-degree monomials; differentiating along the curve with the chain rule (the derivative of $\ln(1+(i+1)t)$ is the rational function $(i+1)/(1+(i+1)t)$) produces another relation with fewer top-degree monomials, hence zero, which forces the top-degree coefficients to satisfy incompatible rational identities.
