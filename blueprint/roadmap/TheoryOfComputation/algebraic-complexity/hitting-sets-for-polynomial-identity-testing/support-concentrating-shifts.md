# Support-concentrating shifts for multilinear polynomials of small split rank

Over a field of characteristic zero, with $\ell=\lfloor\log_2w\rfloor+1$, every nonzero multilinear polynomial $f$ in $n$ variables of split rank at most $w$ across every set of $\ell$ variables has, after a generic shift $x\mapsto x+u$ (for all $u\in F^n$ outside a proper Zariski-closed set), a monomial involving at most $\lfloor\log_2w\rfloor$ variables.

## Proof sketch

For a set $Y$ of $\ell$ variables with $w<2^\ell$, low split rank means the $2^\ell$ partial evaluations on $Y$ are linearly dependent, so after a generic shift some monomial of small support survives.
