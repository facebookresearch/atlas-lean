# Sorting by reversals as a decision problem

SORTING BY REVERSALS as a decision problem over bitstrings: a bitstring is a yes-instance when it decodes to a pair $(\pi, K)$, where $\pi$ is a list of length $n$ that is a permutation of $1, \dots, n$ and at most $K$ reversals of intervals $[i, j]$ with $i < j < n$, applied in order, turn $\pi$ into $[1, 2, \dots, n]$. Bitstrings that do not decode are no-instances.
