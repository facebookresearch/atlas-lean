# Missing mass in the Chi-Geman example

Let $S_0 = S_1 = 0$ and $S_{n+1} = \tfrac13 + \tfrac23 S_n^2$ for $n \ge 1$, so $S_2 = 1/3$. With this index shift, $S_{h+1}$ is the total weight captured at cutoff $h$, the summed weight of the Chi-Geman derivation trees of depth at most $h$. Then $1 - S_h \ge 1/2$ for every $h \in \mathbb N$.
