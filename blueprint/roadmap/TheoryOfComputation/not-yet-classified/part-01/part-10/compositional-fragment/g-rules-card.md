# The compositional fragment grammar has seven rules

The context-free grammar of the compositional fragment, with start symbol $S$ and rules $S \to \text{Subj}\ \text{VP}$, $\text{VP} \to \text{TV}\ \text{Obj}$, $S \to \text{SP}\ \text{Obj}$, $\text{SP} \to \text{Subj}\ \text{TV}$, $\text{Subj} \to \textit{everybody}$, $\text{Obj} \to \textit{someone}$, $\text{TV} \to \textit{admires}$, has exactly seven distinct rules.
