# Wang tilings of finitely generated groups and the domino problem

A Wang tile set on a finitely generated group colors the edges of its Cayley graph under local matching rules; the domino
problem of the group asks whether a given finite tile set admits a valid tiling.

Let $G$ be a group and $\mathcal{A} \subseteq G$ a finite set with $n$ elements, and fix a finite set $\mathcal{C}$ of colors. A
*Wang tile on $\mathcal{A}$* is a map $t : \mathcal{A} \times \{+, -\} \to \mathcal{C}$: $t(g, +)$ is the color of the outgoing
edge labeled $g$ and $t(g, -)$ that of the incoming edge labeled $g$. For a finite set $\tau$ of Wang tiles on $\mathcal{A}$, a
*valid tiling of $G$ by $\tau$* is a map $x : G \to \tau$ such that

$$
x(h)(g, +) = x(hg)(g, -) \quad \text{for all } h \in G \text{ and } g \in \mathcal{A} ,
$$

that is, adjacent tiles of the Cayley graph agree on the color of their common edge.

For a finitely generated group $G$ with a fixed finite set $\mathcal{A}$ of generators, the *domino problem on $G$* is the
decision problem: given a finite set $\tau$ of Wang tiles on $\mathcal{A}$, is there a valid tiling of $G$ by $\tau$? Whether
it is decidable does not depend on the finite generating set: if $\mathcal{B}$ lies in the subgroup generated by $\mathcal{A}$
and tileability for tile sets on $\mathcal{A}$ is decidable, then so is tileability for tile sets on $\mathcal{B}$. In
particular a finitely generated subgroup of a group with decidable domino problem has a decidable domino problem.

For $G = \mathbb{Z}^2 = \langle a, b \mid ab = ba \rangle$ and $\mathcal{A} = \{a, b\}$ these are the classical
[Wang tiles and valid tilings](../wang-tiles-and-valid-tilings.md), and the domino problem is the
[tiling problem](../the-tiling-problem-is-undecidable.md). Virtually free groups have a decidable domino problem,
and a group with undecidable word problem has an undecidable one.
