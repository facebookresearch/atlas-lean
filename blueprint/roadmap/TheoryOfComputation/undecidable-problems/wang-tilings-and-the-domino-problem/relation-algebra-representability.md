# Relation algebras and representability

A **relation algebra** is a Boolean algebra $(A,+,\cdot,-,0,1)$ with a constant $1'$, a unary operation $\breve{\ }$ and a binary operation $;$ satisfying Tarski's axioms (associativity of $;$, $1'$ a unit, involution laws, distributivity, and the cycle law $(a;b)\cdot c=0\iff(\breve a;c)\cdot b=0$). It is **representable** if it is isomorphic to a subalgebra of a product of algebras of binary relations $\mathcal{P}(U\times U)$ with union, intersection, complement, identity, converse and relational composition. Every finite relation algebra is the complex algebra of its atom structure, which is presented by a finite table of forbidden and allowed atom triangles.
