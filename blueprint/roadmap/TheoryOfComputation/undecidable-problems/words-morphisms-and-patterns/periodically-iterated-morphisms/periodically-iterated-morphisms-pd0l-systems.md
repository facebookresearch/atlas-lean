# Periodically iterated morphisms (PD0L systems)

A PD0L system iterates a cyclic list of morphisms, applying the $i$-th morphism to the letters at positions congruent to $i$ modulo the
period; in the limit it generates a finite or infinite word, infinite when the system is productive, extending the purely morphic words generated by a single morphism.

Let $H = (h_0, \dots, h_{p-1})$ be a tuple of morphisms $h_i : \Sigma^* \to \Sigma^*$. The map $H : \Sigma^* \to \Sigma^*$ is

$$
H(a_0 a_1 \cdots a_n) = h_{0 \bmod p}(a_0)\, h_{1 \bmod p}(a_1) \cdots h_{n \bmod p}(a_n),
$$

that is, the letter at position $i$ is mapped by $h_k$ with $k \equiv i \pmod p$. If $s \in \Sigma^*$ is a prefix of $H(s)$, the triple $\mathcal{H} = (\Sigma, H, s)$ is a *PD0L system*, and
the limit $H^\omega(s) = \lim_{i \to \infty} H^i(s)$, a finite or infinite word, is the *PD0L word* it generates. The system is *productive* if $H^\omega(s)$ is infinite and
*erasing* if some $h_i$ maps a letter to the empty word. D0L systems are the case $p = 1$; images of D0L words under letter-to-letter morphisms are the morphic words.
