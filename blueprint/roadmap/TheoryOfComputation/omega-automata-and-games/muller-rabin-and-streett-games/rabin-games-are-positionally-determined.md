# Rabin games are positionally determined for the Rabin player

In every game whose condition is a Rabin objective, as in [Muller, Rabin and Streett objectives](muller-rabin-and-streett-objectives.md),
Eve, whose condition is the Rabin objective, has a positional strategy winning from every vertex of her winning region,
and every vertex lies in the winning region of one of the players. Adam, whose condition is a Streett objective, may need
memory.

## Depends on

- [Muller, Rabin and Streett objectives](muller-rabin-and-streett-objectives.md)
- [Arenas, strategies and memory structures](arenas-strategies-and-memory-structures.md)
