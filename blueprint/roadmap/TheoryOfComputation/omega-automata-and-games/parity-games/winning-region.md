# Winning region of a parity game

Let $G$ be a parity game: positions owned by two players, a move relation in which every position
has at least one move, and a priority in $\mathbb{N}$ for each position.
The winning region of a player $p$ is the set of positions $v$ from which $p$ has a winning
positional strategy: some positional strategy $\sigma$ for $p$ such that every play starting at
$v$ that conforms to $\sigma$ is won by $p$.
