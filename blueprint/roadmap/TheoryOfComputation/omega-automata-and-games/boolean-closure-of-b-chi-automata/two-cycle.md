# Two-state cyclic Büchi automaton

Over a one-letter alphabet, this Büchi automaton has states $\mathsf{false}$ (initial) and
$\mathsf{true}$ and moves from each to the other at every step; its accepting set is a
parameter. Vardi's automata $A_1$ and $A_2$ are the cases of accepting sets
$\{\mathsf{false}\}$ and $\{\mathsf{true}\}$.

## Sources

- M. Y. Vardi, "An automata-theoretic approach to linear temporal logic", in *Logics for Concurrency: Structure versus Automata*, LNCS 1043, Springer 1996, pp. 238-266.
