# Polynomial-time solvability of mean-payoff games

Can the winner of a finite turn-based mean-payoff game be computed in polynomial time? It is important for synthesis, verification, and dynamic optimization.

## What would settle it

Give an algorithm on a finite directed game graph with signed binary-encoded weights. Prove that its winning region matches the strategy/liminf-average definition and that its bit complexity is polynomial in the complete input length.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Asadi, Chatterjee and Luo (preprint) state that a polynomial-time algorithm is a major open question, note that all known deterministic algorithms take exponential time in the worst case, and give algorithms with smoothed polynomial complexity.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
