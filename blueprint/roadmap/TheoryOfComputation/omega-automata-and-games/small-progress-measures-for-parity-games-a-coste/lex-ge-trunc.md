# Non-strict truncated lexicographic order

For a threshold $p$ and tuples $a, b$ of $d + 1$ natural numbers, $a \ge_p b$ holds when $a >_p b$ or $a_i = b_i$ for every index $i \ge p$.

## Sources

- M. Jurdziński, "Small progress measures for solving parity games", Proceedings of STACS 2000, LNCS 1770, 290-301.
