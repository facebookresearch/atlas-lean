# The costed verifier gives the right answer

For a parity game on $n$ positions and a partial assignment $\mu$ of tuples of $d + 1$ natural numbers to positions, the result of the costed progress-measure verifier equals the result of the progress-measure verifier on $\mu$.

## Sources

- M. Jurdziński, "Small progress measures for solving parity games", Proceedings of STACS 2000, LNCS 1770, 290-301 (background: the paper defines progress measures and the lifting algorithm; the verifier and its comparison counts here are not the paper's algorithm or bound).
