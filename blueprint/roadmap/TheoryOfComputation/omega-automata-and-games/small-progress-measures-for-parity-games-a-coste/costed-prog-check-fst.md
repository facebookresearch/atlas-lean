# The costed edge check gives the right answer

For a parity game on $n$ positions, a partial assignment $\mu$ of tuples of $d + 1$ natural numbers and positions $v, w$, the result of the costed progress check for the ordered pair $(v, w)$ equals the progress check for that pair; neither inspects the moves of the game.

## Sources

- M. Jurdziński, "Small progress measures for solving parity games", Proceedings of STACS 2000, LNCS 1770, 290-301 (background: the paper defines progress measures and the lifting algorithm; the verifier and its comparison counts here are not the paper's algorithm or bound).
