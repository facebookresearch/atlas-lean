# Cost bound for the costed edge check

For a parity game on $n$ positions with priorities, a partial assignment $\mu$ of tuples of $d + 1$ natural numbers to positions, and positions $v$ and $w$, the costed progress check for the edge from $v$ to $w$ costs at most $(d+1)(2 + 2(d+1)) + 2(d+1)$.

## Sources

- M. Jurdziński, "Small progress measures for solving parity games", Proceedings of STACS 2000, LNCS 1770, 290-301.
