# Stochastic reachability games are positionally determined

Every [stochastic reachability game](stochastic-arenas-and-games.md), on a finite arena, is determined, and both players have uniform pure positional optimal strategies.
The values are the least fixed point of the monotone operator on $V \to [0, 1]$ that is $1$ at $\mathrm{Win}$, the maximum (respectively
minimum) of the successors' values at vertices of Max (respectively Min), and the $\delta$-weighted average at random vertices; every
pure positional strategy of Min that moves to a successor of least value is optimal.
