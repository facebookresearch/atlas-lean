# Timed Büchi and timed Muller automata accept the same timed languages

A timed language is accepted by some [timed Büchi automaton](timed-buchi-automata.md) if and only if it is accepted by
some [timed Muller automaton](timed-muller-automata.md). Hence the timed languages of timed Muller automata are exactly
the timed regular languages.

## Sources
