# Alternating automata

Alternating automata combine existential and universal choice: a transition is a positive Boolean formula over states, and
a run is a tree. On finite words they recognize exactly the regular languages, exponentially more succinctly than
nondeterministic automata, are complemented by dualizing their transitions, and have a PSPACE-complete emptiness problem. On
infinite words, alternating Büchi automata are translated into nondeterministic Büchi automata with an exponential blow-up
(Miyano and Hayashi).
