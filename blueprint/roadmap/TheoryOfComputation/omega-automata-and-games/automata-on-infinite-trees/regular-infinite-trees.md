# Regular infinite trees

An infinite binary tree $t : \{0, 1\}^* \to A$, as in [tree automata on infinite trees](tree-automata-on-infinite-trees.md), is *regular* if it is
generated by a deterministic finite automaton $B$ over the alphabet $\{0, 1\}$ with an output function $f_B$ from its states to $A$: the
label $t(w)$ is $f_B$ of the state that $B$ reaches after reading $w$ from its initial state.
