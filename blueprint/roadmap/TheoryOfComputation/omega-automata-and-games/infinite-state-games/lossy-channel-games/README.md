# Lossy channel games

Games on lossy channel systems, unbounded FIFO channels that may lose messages, with two players or several concurrent stochastic players; reachability and safety are decidable for two-player games, while weak parity, parity, Büchi and co-Büchi objectives, and verification of the core under almost-sure LTL objectives, are undecidable.
