# Lower bounds for complementing Büchi automata

Complementing a nondeterministic Büchi automaton with $n + 2$ states can require $n!$ states, by Michel's example, and in
fact $(0.76n)^n$ states up to a polynomial factor, by the full-automata technique; the same examples show that no
determinization of Büchi automata into Rabin automata with $2^{O(n)}$ states and $O(n)$ pairs exists. That Büchi automata can
be complemented at all is in [Boolean closure of Büchi automata](../boolean-closure-of-b-chi-automata/README.md).
