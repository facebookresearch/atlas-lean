# The evaluator is primitive recursive

Fix $k \in \mathbb{N}$. The map sending a code $c$ for $k$ work tapes, a bit list $x$ and a clock
$T \in \mathbb{N}$ to the result of the
[clocked evaluator](eval-steps.md)
on $c$, $x$ and $T$ (an output bit list, or no result) is primitive recursive, for the standard
primitive recursive codings of codes (through their tables), bit lists, natural numbers and
possibly absent results.
