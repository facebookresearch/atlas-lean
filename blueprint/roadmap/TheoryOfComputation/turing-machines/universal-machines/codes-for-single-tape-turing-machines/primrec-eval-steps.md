# The clocked evaluator is primitive recursive

The map sending a code $c$, a bit list $x$ and a clock $T \in \mathbb{N}$ to the result of the
[clocked evaluator](eval-steps.md)
on $c$, $x$ and $T$ (an output list of natural numbers, or no result) is primitive recursive, for
the standard primitive recursive codings of codes (through their tables), bit lists, natural
numbers and possibly absent results.
