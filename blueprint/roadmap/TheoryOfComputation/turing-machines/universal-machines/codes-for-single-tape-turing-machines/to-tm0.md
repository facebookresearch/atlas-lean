# Machine configuration of a simulation configuration

The machine configuration of a configuration
$k$ of the simulation is the configuration of a single-tape Turing machine with states and symbols in $\mathbb{N}$ (the blank being $0$) that has the state of $k$, the cell under the head of $k$ as its head cell, and
as the halves of its tape left and right of the head the cells of $k$ left and right of the head,
nearest first, followed by infinitely many blanks.
