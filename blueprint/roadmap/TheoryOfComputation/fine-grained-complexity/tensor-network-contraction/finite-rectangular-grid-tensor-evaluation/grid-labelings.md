# Directed labelings of a finite grid

For finite sets $\rho$ and $\gamma$ and finite sets $\Lambda$ (edge labels) and $\Delta$ (diagonal
labels) in a type $A$, the directed grid labelings are the matrices $\ell$ indexed by
$(\rho \times \gamma) \times (\rho \times \gamma)$ with entries in $A$ such that $\ell(w, w) \in
\Delta$ for every cell $w$ and $\ell(w, w') \in \Lambda$ for all cells $w' \ne w$. The entry
$\ell(w, w')$ is the label of the half-edge from $w$ to $w'$.
