# Transpose of a rectangle kernel

For a family of kernels $G_\omega$ of four labels indexed by $\omega \in (\rho \times \rho) \times
(\gamma \times \gamma)$, the transposed family is indexed by $(\gamma \times \gamma) \times
(\rho \times \rho)$ and given by $G^{\top}_{(k, p)}(x, y, u, v) = G_{(p, k)}(x, u, y, v)$: the
second and third diagonal ends are exchanged.
