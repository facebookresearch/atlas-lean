# Permanent equals determinant in characteristic two

Over a commutative ring of characteristic $2$, the permanent of every square matrix equals its
determinant, since every sign $\pm 1$ equals $1$.

## Sources

- L. G. Valiant, *The complexity of computing the permanent*, Theoretical Computer Science 8 (1979), 189-201, Theorem 3 ("the permanent and determinant are equal mod 2").
