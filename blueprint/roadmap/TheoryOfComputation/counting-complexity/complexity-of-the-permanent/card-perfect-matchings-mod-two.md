# Parity of the number of bipartite perfect matchings

For a bipartite graph with $n$ rows and $n$ columns given by a relation $B$, the number of perfect
matchings, taken modulo $2$, is the determinant over $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ of the 0-1
biadjacency matrix. Its parity is thus computable in polynomial time, although counting the perfect
matchings exactly is $\#P$-complete.

## Sources

- L. G. Valiant, *The complexity of computing the permanent*, Theoretical Computer Science 8 (1979), 189-201, Theorem 1 and Theorem 3.
