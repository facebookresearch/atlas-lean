# PP is contained in PSPACE

Every decision problem in PP, the class of decision problems
decided with unbounded error in probabilistic polynomial time, belongs to PSPACE.

## Sources

- J. Gill, "Computational complexity of probabilistic Turing machines", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 6 (1977), 675–695; S. Arora and B. Barak, *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach*, Cambridge University Press, 2009 (PP ⊆ PSPACE).
