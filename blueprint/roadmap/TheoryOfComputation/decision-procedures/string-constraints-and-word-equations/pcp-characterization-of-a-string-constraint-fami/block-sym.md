# Alphabet for the PCP encoding

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. A domino over an alphabet $\alpha$ is a pair $d = (d^{\uparrow}, d^{\downarrow})$ of words over $\alpha$ (its top and bottom words). The block alphabet $\hat\alpha$ is the disjoint union of the set of all dominoes over $\alpha$ (whose elements, written $[d]$, are called domino letters) and $\alpha$ (plain letters).
