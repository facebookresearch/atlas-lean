# Bottom morphism of the PCP encoding

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. A domino over an alphabet $\alpha$ is a pair $d = (d^{\uparrow}, d^{\downarrow})$ of words over $\alpha$ (its top and bottom words). The block alphabet $\hat\alpha$ is the disjoint union of the set of all dominoes over $\alpha$ (whose elements, written $[d]$, are called domino letters) and $\alpha$ (plain letters). The bottom morphism sends a domino letter $[d]$ to the bottom word
$d^{\downarrow}$, written with plain letters, and sends every plain letter to the empty word; it is
applied to words letter by letter.
