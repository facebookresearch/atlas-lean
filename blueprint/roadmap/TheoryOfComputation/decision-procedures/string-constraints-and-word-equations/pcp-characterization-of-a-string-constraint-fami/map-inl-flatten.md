# Flattening a list of one-letter blocks

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. A domino over an alphabet $\alpha$ is a pair $d = (d^{\uparrow}, d^{\downarrow})$ of words over $\alpha$ (its top and bottom words). The block alphabet $\hat\alpha$ is the disjoint union of the set of all dominoes over $\alpha$ (whose elements, written $[d]$, are called domino letters) and $\alpha$ (plain letters). For every finite sequence $d_1, \dots, d_k$ of dominoes, the word
$[d_1] \cdots [d_k]$ over $\hat\alpha$ equals the concatenation of the one-letter words $[d_1], \dots,
[d_k]$.
