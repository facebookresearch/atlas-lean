# Words of length at most $B$ over a finite alphabet

Let $\alpha$ be a finite alphabet. For $B \in \mathbb{N}$, the enumeration of words of length at most $B$ over $\alpha$ is the
concatenation of the enumerations of words of length exactly $0, 1, \dots, B$, in this order. It lists
every word of length at most $B$ exactly once.
