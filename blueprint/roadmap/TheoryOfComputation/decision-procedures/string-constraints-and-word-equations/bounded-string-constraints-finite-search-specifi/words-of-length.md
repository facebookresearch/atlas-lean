# Words of a given length over a finite alphabet

Let $\alpha$ be a finite alphabet. The enumeration of words of length $n$ over $\alpha$ is defined recursively: for $n = 0$ it is the
list whose only entry is the empty word; for $n + 1$ it consists of the words $wa$, for each $w$ in the
enumeration for $n$ and each letter $a \in \alpha$ (in a fixed enumeration of $\alpha$). It lists every
word of length $n$ exactly once.
