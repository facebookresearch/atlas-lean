# Fixed-length word-equation solver via DPLL

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $E$ be a finite list of word equations $L = R$ over the alphabet $\{0, 1\}$ with variables $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$ (each side a finite word over $\{0, 1\} \cup \{x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}\}$). A substitution $\sigma$ assigns a binary word $\sigma(x_i)$ to each variable, and it satisfies $L = R$ if the words obtained from $L$ and $R$ by replacing each $x_i$ with $\sigma(x_i)$ are equal. A length profile is a map $\ell : \{0, \dots, n-1\} \to \mathbb{N}$. The bit-blasting of a word $u$ over $\{0, 1\} \cup \{x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}\}$ at a length profile $\ell$ is the list of literals obtained by replacing each constant $b \in \{0, 1\}$ by the literal $(0, b)$ (variable $0$, polarity true for $1$ and false for $0$) and each variable $x_i$ by the $\ell(i)$ positive literals $(\langle i, 0\rangle + 1, \mathrm{true}), \dots, (\langle i, \ell(i) - 1\rangle + 1, \mathrm{true})$, where $\langle i, p\rangle$ denotes the pairing bijection $\mathbb{N}^2 \to \mathbb{N}$ given by $\langle i, p\rangle = p^2 + i$ if $i < p$ and $\langle i, p\rangle = i^2 + i + p$ otherwise. The CNF encoding of $E$ at $\ell$ consists of the unit clause $(0, \mathrm{true})$ followed, for each equation $L = R$ of $E$ in order, by the clauses that force the bit-blastings of $L$ and $R$ to agree position by position if they have the same length (two clauses $\{\neg a, b\}$ and $\{\neg b, a\}$ for each pair of literals $a, b$ in the same position), and by one empty clause if their lengths differ.

The fixed-length solver at $\ell$ returns the answer (true for satisfiable) of the DPLL satisfiability
procedure run on the CNF encoding of $E$ at $\ell$.
