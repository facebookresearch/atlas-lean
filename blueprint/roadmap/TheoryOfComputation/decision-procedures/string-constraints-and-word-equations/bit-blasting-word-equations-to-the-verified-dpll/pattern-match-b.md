# Pattern-matching decision against a concrete target

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $u$ be a finite word over $\{0, 1\} \cup \{x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}\}$ (a pattern)
and let $t$ be a binary word. The pattern-matching procedure returns the answer of the bounded
word-equation solver with bound $|t|$ on the system consisting of the single equation $u = t$, where $t$ is
read as a word of constants.
