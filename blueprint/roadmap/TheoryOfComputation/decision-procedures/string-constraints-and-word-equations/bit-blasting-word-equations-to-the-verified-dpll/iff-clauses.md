# Clauses forcing two literals to be equal

For literals $a = (v, s)$ and $b = (w, t)$ (pairs of a variable in $\mathbb{N}$ and a polarity), the
equivalence clauses of $a$ and $b$ are the two clauses $\{\neg a, b\}$ and $\{\neg b, a\}$, where
$\neg(v, s) = (v, \lnot s)$. An assignment satisfies both exactly when it gives $a$ and $b$ the same truth
value.
