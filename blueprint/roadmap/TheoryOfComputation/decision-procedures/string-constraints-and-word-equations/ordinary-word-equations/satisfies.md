# Solution of a word equation

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet of constants and $V$ a set of variables, disjoint from $\alpha$. A pattern is a finite word over $\alpha \cup V$, and for a substitution $\sigma : V \to \alpha^*$ the evaluation $\sigma(w)$ of a pattern $w$ is the word over $\alpha$ obtained by replacing each variable $x$ in $w$ by $\sigma(x)$. A substitution $\sigma : V \to \alpha^*$ solves a word equation $L = R$ (a pair of patterns) if
$\sigma(L) = \sigma(R)$.
