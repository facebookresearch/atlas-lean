# Ground test agrees with satisfaction under any substitution

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet and let $L = R$ be a ground word equation over $\alpha$, that is, $L$ and $R$ are words over $\alpha$ with no variables. The ground test (true if and only if $L$ and $R$ are equal words) returns true if and only if $L = R$
is solved by the (unique, empty) substitution.
