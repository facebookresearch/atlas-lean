# Satisfiability of a single word equation at a finite length bound

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and let the variables be $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$; a pattern is a finite word over $\alpha \cup \{x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}\}$, and for a substitution $\sigma$ of words over $\alpha$ for the variables, $\sigma(w)$ is the word obtained from a pattern $w$ by replacing each $x_i$ by $\sigma(x_i)$. A word equation $L = R$ between patterns has a solution if and only if there are $B \in \mathbb{N}$
and a solution $\sigma$ with $|\sigma(x_i)| \le B$ for all $i < n$.

The bound depends on the solution; no bound computable from the equation is asserted.
