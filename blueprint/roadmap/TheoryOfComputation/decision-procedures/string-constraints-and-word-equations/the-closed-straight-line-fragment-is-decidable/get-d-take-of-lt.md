# Lookup in a taken prefix agrees with the whole list

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. For a list $l$ of words over $\alpha$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, $l[j]$ denotes the $j$-th entry of $l$, or the empty word if $j \ge |l|$. Let $l$ be a list of words over $\alpha$ and let $n < m$ and $n < |l|$. Then
the $n$-th entry, with default the empty word, of the list of the first $m$ entries of $l$ equals $l[n]$.
