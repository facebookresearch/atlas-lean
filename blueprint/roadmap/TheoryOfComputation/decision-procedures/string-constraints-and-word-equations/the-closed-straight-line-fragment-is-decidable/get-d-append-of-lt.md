# Lookup in an appended list below the prefix length

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. For a list $l$ of words over $\alpha$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, $l[j]$ denotes the $j$-th entry of $l$, or the empty word if $j \ge |l|$. Let $a$ be a list of words over $\alpha$, $x$ a word and $j < |a|$. Then the
$j$-th entry of the list $a$ followed by $x$, with default the empty word, equals $a[j]$.
