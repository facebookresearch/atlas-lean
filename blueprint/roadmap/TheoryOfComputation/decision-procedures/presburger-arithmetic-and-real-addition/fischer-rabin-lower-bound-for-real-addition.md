# Fischer–Rabin lower bound for real addition

Let $\mathrm{Th}(\mathbb R, +)$ be the set of sentences in the language with $+$ and $=$ that are true in the real numbers under addition. There is a constant $d > 0$ such that for every decision procedure for $\mathrm{Th}(\mathbb R, +)$, deterministic or nondeterministic, as in the [Fischer–Rabin lower bound for Presburger arithmetic](fischer-rabin-lower-bound-for-presburger-arithmetic.md), there is an $n_0$ such that for every $n > n_0$ some sentence of length $n$ requires more than $2^{dn}$ computation steps of the procedure.
