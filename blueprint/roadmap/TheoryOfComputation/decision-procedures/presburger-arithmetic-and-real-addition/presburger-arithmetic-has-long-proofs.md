# Proofs in Presburger arithmetic are doubly exponentially long

Let PA be as in the [Fischer–Rabin lower bound](fischer-rabin-lower-bound-for-presburger-arithmetic.md). There is a constant $c > 0$ such that for every system of axioms AX, in the language of PA or an extension of it, such that a sentence of the language of PA is provable from AX exactly when it belongs to PA, and such that whether a sentence is an axiom can be decided in time polynomial in its length, there is an $n_0$ such that for every $n > n_0$ some sentence of length $n$ in PA has no proof from AX with at most $2^{2^{cn}}$ symbols.
