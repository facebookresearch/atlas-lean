# Proofs in the theory of real addition are exponentially long

There is a constant $d > 0$ such that for every system of axioms AX for $\mathrm{Th}(\mathbb R, +)$ with the properties required in [Proofs in Presburger arithmetic are doubly exponentially long](presburger-arithmetic-has-long-proofs.md), there is an $n_0$ such that for every $n > n_0$ some true sentence of length $n$ has no proof from AX with at most $2^{dn}$ symbols.
