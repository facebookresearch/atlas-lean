# Integer polynomial formulas over the reals

A *term* is an expression built from integer constants, variables $x_1,\dots,x_n$, addition and multiplication; it denotes a polynomial in $\mathbb{Z}[x_1,\dots,x_n]$. An *atomic formula* compares a term with zero ($t = 0$, $t > 0$, $t \ge 0$), and a (quantifier-free) *formula* is a Boolean combination of atomic formulas. A formula $\Phi$ defines its *solution set* $\{a \in \mathbb{R}^n : \Phi(a)\}$, a semialgebraic set. Terms are encoded in binary with integer constants in binary and variable indices in unary.
