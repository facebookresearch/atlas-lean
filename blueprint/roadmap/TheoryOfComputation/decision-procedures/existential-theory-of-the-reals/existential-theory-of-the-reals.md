# The existential theory of the reals

The *existential theory of the reals* (ETR) is the decision problem: given a quantifier-free integer polynomial formula $\Phi(x_1,\dots,x_n)$, decide whether its solution set is nonempty, i.e. whether $\exists x \in \mathbb{R}^n\, \Phi(x)$ holds. The complexity class $\exists\mathbb{R}$ consists of the problems polynomial-time reducible to ETR. More generally the first-order theory of the reals decides sentences with alternating real quantifiers.
