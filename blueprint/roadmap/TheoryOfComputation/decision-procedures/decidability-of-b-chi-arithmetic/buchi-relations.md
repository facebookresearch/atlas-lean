# Relation symbol of Büchi arithmetic

Büchi arithmetic has one relation symbol, the binary symbol $V_2$, and no others. In the standard model $\mathbb{N}$, $V_2(x, y)$ holds when $y$ is the largest power of $2$ dividing $x$: $y = 2^k$ for some $k$, $y \mid x$, and every $k'$ with $2^{k'+1} \mid x$ satisfies $k' < k$. In particular $V_2(0, y)$ is false for every $y$.
