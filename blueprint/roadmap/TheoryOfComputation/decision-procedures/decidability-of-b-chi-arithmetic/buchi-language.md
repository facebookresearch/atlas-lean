# The language of Büchi arithmetic

The first-order language of Büchi arithmetic has function symbols $0$ (a constant), $S$ (unary) and $+$ (binary), and a single binary relation symbol $V_2$. Its standard structure is $\mathbb{N}$ with $0$, successor $S(x) = x + 1$ and addition, and $V_2(x, y)$ holding when $y$ is the largest power of $2$ dividing $x$ (false when $x = 0$).
