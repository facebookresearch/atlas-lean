# Correctness of the infiniteness test for encoded DFAs

For every encoded DFA $D$ over a
finite alphabet, the infiniteness test returns true on $D$ if and only if the language of $D$ is
infinite.

## Sources

- M. O. Rabin and D. Scott, *Finite automata and their decision problems*, IBM Journal of Research and Development 3(2) (April 1959), 114–125, Corollary 9.1 (p. 120): infiniteness of $T(\mathfrak{A})$ is decidable.
