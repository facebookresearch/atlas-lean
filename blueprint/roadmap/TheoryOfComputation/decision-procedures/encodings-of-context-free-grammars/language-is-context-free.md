# Encoded grammars generate context-free languages

Let $T$ be an alphabet and let $g$ be an encoded context-free grammar over $T$: a start nonterminal
$n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite list of productions $A \to \gamma$ with $A \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\gamma$
a finite word over the disjoint union of $\mathbb{N}$ (nonterminals) and $T$ (terminals). Then the
language of $g$, the set of words over $T$ derivable from $n_0$, is context-free.
