# Encoded DFAs as triples

Sending an encoded DFA over $\alpha$ to the triple of its start
state, its list of accepting states and its list of transitions is an equivalence (a bijection
with explicit inverse) between encoded DFAs over $\alpha$ and the set $\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}^* \times ((\mathbb{N} \times \alpha) \times \mathbb{N})^*$ of triples of a natural number, a finite list of natural numbers and a finite list of transitions $((q, a), q')$.
