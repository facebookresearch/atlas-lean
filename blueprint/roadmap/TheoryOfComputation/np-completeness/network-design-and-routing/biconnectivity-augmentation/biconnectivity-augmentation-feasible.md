# Feasible BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION instances

An instance $(n, E, w, B)$ of BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION is feasible when some list $E'$ of pairs of distinct vertices below $n$ has total weight at most $B$ (each listed pair counted with its weight, repetitions counted again) and adding the pairs of $E'$ to $G$ gives a $2$-vertex-connected graph.
