# BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION is NP-complete

The BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND18] BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION.
- K. P. Eswaran and R. E. Tarjan, "Augmentation problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 653-665, doi:10.1137/0205044.
