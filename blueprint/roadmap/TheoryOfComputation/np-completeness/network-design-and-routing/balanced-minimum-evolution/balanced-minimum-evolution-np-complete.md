# BALANCED MINIMUM EVOLUTION is NP-complete

The BALANCED MINIMUM EVOLUTION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Fiorini and G. Joret, "Approximating the balanced minimum evolution problem", Operations Research Letters 40 (2012), 31-35, doi:10.1016/j.orl.2011.10.003 (arXiv:1104.1080), Theorem 3.1.
