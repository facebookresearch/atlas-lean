# Well-formed BALANCED MINIMUM EVOLUTION instances

An instance $(n, D, L)$ of BALANCED MINIMUM EVOLUTION is well-formed when $D$ has $n$ rows of $n$ entries, every entry has a positive denominator, every diagonal entry has numerator $0$, the matrix is symmetric ($\delta_{ij} = \delta_{ji}$, compared by cross-multiplication), and $L$ has a positive denominator.
