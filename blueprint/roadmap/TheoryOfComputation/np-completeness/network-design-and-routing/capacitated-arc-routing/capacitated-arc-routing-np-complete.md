# CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING is NP-complete

The CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. L. Golden and R. T. Wong, "Capacitated arc routing problems", Networks 11 (1981), 305-315, doi:10.1002/net.3230110308 (introduces the problem and shows it NP-hard).
- R. van Bevern, S. Hartung, A. Nichterlein, and M. Sorge, "Constant-factor approximations for Capacitated Arc Routing without triangle inequality", Operations Research Letters 42 (2014), 290-292, doi:10.1016/j.orl.2014.05.002, arXiv:1404.3660 (the problem statement used here).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND27] RURAL POSTMAN (the special case with unbounded capacity; J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, Networks 6 (1976), 273-280).
