# Feasible CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING instances

An instance $(n, E, v_0, W, B)$ of CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING is feasible when, for some $m$, there are $m$ closed walks at the depot $v_0$ in the graph and an assignment of every edge entry to one of the walks such that every edge of positive demand is traversed by the walk it is assigned to, the entries assigned to each walk have total demand at most $W$, and the walks have total cost at most $B$, counting the cost of an edge once for every traversal.
