# DIAMETER SPANNING SUBGRAPH is NP-complete

The DIAMETER SPANNING SUBGRAPH decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, web edition (2000), problem MINIMUM DIAMETER SPANNING SUBGRAPH (decision version).
- J. Plesník, "The complexity of designing a network with minimum diameter", Networks 11(1) (1981), 77-85, doi:10.1002/net.3230110110.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND4] BOUNDED DIAMETER SPANNING TREE (the spanning-tree analogue).
