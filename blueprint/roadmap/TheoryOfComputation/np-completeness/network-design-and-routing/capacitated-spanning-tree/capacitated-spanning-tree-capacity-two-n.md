# CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with capacities 2 and 0-1 requirements and lengths is NP-complete

The CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with capacities 2 and 0-1 requirements and lengths decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND5] CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE, comment (p. 207): "remains NP-complete if all capacities are 2, all requirements 0 or 1, and all edge lengths 0 or 1 [Even and Johnson, 1977]" (S. Even and D. S. Johnson, unpublished results, 1977, as listed in Garey and Johnson's references).
