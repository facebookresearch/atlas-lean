# CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE is NP-complete

The CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND5] CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE.
- C. H. Papadimitriou, "The complexity of the capacitated tree problem", Networks 8 (1978), 217-230, doi:10.1002/net.3230080306.
