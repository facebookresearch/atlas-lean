# DIRECTED RURAL POSTMAN is NP-complete

The DIRECTED RURAL POSTMAN decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND27] RURAL POSTMAN, comment on p. 213 ("as does the corresponding problem for directed graphs").
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "On general routing problems", Networks 6 (1976), 273-280, doi:10.1002/net.3230060305, Theorem (p. 275: the rural postman problem is NP-complete "both in the case that G is undirected and in the case that G is directed").
