# STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS with unit weights on bipartite graphs is NP-complete

The STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS with unit weights on bipartite graphs decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a bipartite graph `G = (V, E)`, a subset `R ⊆ V` of terminals, and a positive integer `K`. Question: is there a subtree of `G` that includes all the vertices of `R` and has at most `K` edges?
