# STEINER TREE IN CHORDAL BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The STEINER TREE IN CHORDAL BIPARTITE GRAPHS decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a chordal bipartite graph `G = (V, E)` (bipartite, and every cycle of length at least `6` has a chord), a terminal set `R ⊆ V`, and a positive integer `K`. Question: is there a subtree of `G` containing every vertex of `R` with at most `K` vertices?

## Sources
