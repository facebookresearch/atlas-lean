# Well-formed instances of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS with unit weights on chordal graphs

An instance of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS with unit weights on chordal graphs is well formed when it is a well-formed STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS instance, every edge has weight `1`, its graph is chordal (no induced cycle of length at least `4`), it has at least one vertex, and `K` is positive.
