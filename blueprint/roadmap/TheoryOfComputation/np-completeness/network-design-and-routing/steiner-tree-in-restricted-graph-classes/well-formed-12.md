# Well-formed instances of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most k

For a natural number $k$, an instance $(n, E, R, B)$ of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most $k$ is well formed when it is a [well-formed STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS instance](../../paths-cycles-and-subgraphs/steiner-tree/well-formed.md), every edge in $E$ has weight $1$, its graph has thickness at most $k$ (its [GRAPH THICKNESS instance](with-thickness.md) for $k$ is feasible: each entry of $E$ can be assigned one of $k$ classes so that, for every class, the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$ of the edges in that class is planar), $n \ge 1$, and $0 < B$. The definition applies to every natural number $k$; for $k = 0$ no class is available, so the condition holds exactly when $E$ is empty.
