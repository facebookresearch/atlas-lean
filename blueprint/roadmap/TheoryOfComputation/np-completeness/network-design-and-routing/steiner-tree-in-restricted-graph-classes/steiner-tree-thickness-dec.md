# The STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most k decision problem

For each natural number $k$, the STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most $k$ decision problem with parameter $k$ accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is [well formed](well-formed-12.md) for $k$ and [feasible](../../paths-cycles-and-subgraphs/steiner-tree/steiner-tree-feasible.md) as a STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS instance. Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected. Each value of $k$, including $k = 0$, gives a separate decision problem.
