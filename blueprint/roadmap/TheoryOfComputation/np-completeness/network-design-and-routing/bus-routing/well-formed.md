# Well-formed BUS ROUTING instances

An instance $(n, A, s, t, W, B)$ of BUS ROUTING is well-formed when the arcs, $s$, $t$ and the weighted pairs use vertices below $n$; the digraph of $A$ is acyclic; every weighted pair lies in the transitive closure $A^*$ of the arcs; the weighted pairs are distinct; every pair of $A^*$ is weighted; and $B > 0$.
