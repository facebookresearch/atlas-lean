# BUS ROUTING is NP-complete

The BUS ROUTING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide" (fifth edition), Journal of Algorithms 3 (1982), 381-395, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(82)90032-3, problem [1] BUS ROUTING (reference: Rock; transformation from HAMILTONIAN PATH).
