# Well-formed BUS ROUTING (minimization, grid) instances

An instance $(n, A, s, t, W, B)$ (the instance format of BUS ROUTING) is well-formed for the grid minimization version when it is a well-formed BUS ROUTING instance and, for some $h, w \ge 1$ with $n = h w$, its arcs are exactly the south and east arcs of the $h \times w$ grid, $s = 0$ is the northwest corner and $t = h w - 1$ is the southeast corner.
