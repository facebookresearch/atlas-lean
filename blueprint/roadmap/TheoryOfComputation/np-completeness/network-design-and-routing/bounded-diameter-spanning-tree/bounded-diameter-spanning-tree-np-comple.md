# BOUNDED DIAMETER SPANNING TREE is NP-complete

The BOUNDED DIAMETER SPANNING TREE decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND4] BOUNDED DIAMETER SPANNING TREE, p. 206 (reference: [Garey and Johnson, unpublished], transformation from EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS).
