# Feasible instances of SUBGRAPH WITH GIVEN UNLABELLED DEGREE SEQUENCE

An instance of SUBGRAPH WITH GIVEN UNLABELLED DEGREE SEQUENCE is feasible when some sublist `E'` of the edge list spans a subgraph `(V, E')` whose multiset of vertex degrees equals the multiset of desired degrees. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
