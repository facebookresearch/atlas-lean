# Well-formed instances of UNICONNECTED SUBGRAPH for acyclic directed graphs

An instance of UNICONNECTED SUBGRAPH for acyclic directed graphs is well formed when it is a well-formed UNICONNECTED SUBGRAPH instance (a loop-free arc list without duplicates on `Fin n`, and `0 < K ≤ |A|`) whose digraph is acyclic: no vertex reaches itself by a nonempty sequence of arcs.
