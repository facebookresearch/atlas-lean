# Normalizing framed number payloads at most triples the length

For every list $(p^1, \ldots, p^k)$ of bit strings, the concatenation of the framed forms of the
[normalizations](../normalizing-the-total-binary-natural-number-deco/normalize-nat.md) of $p^1, \ldots, p^k$ has
length at most three times the length of the concatenation of the framed forms of $p^1, \ldots, p^k$.
