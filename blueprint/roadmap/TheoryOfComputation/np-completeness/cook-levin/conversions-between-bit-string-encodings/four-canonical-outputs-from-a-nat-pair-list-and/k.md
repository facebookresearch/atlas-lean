# Stacks of the four-output pair-list decoder

The four-output decoder has seventeen stacks: a copy of the eleven stacks of the [pair-list
projector](../canonical-projection-of-lists-of-binary-nat-pair/k.md) (the item workspace) and a separate copy of
the six stacks of the [number-pair projector](../projecting-and-normalizing-a-pair-of-binary-natu/k.md) (the final
pair workspace). The buffer of the final pair workspace also serves as scratch for the initial split of the input
and is empty again before the final pair is normalized.
