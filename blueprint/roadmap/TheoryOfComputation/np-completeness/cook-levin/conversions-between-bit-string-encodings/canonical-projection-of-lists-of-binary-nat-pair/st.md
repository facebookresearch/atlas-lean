# Eleven-stack invariant of the pair-list projector

For bit strings $x$, $a_1$, $a_2$, $o_1$, $o_2$ and stack contents $P$ of the [number-pair
projector](../projecting-and-normalizing-a-pair-of-binary-natu/k.md), the stack contents
$\mathrm{st}(x, a_1, a_2, o_1, o_2, P)$ of the [pair-list projector](prog.md) put $x$ on the input, $a_1$ and $a_2$
on the first and second accumulators, $o_1$ and $o_2$ on the first and second outputs, and $P$ on the workspace.
