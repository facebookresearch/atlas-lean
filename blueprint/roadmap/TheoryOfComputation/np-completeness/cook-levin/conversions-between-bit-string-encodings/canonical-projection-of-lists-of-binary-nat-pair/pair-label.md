# Pair-normalizer halt redirection in the pair-list projector

The label map from the [number-pair projector](../projecting-and-normalizing-a-pair-of-binary-natu/lab.md) to the
[pair-list projector](lab.md) sends good to $\mathrm{emit}(\text{first})$, bad to bad, and every other label $\ell$
to $\mathrm{pair}(\ell)$.
