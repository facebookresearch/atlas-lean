# Control labels of the pair-list projector

The labels of the pair-list projector are loop, block, back, $\mathrm{pair}(\ell)$ for each label $\ell$ of the
[number-pair projector](../projecting-and-normalizing-a-pair-of-binary-natu/lab.md), $\mathrm{emit}(s)$ and
$\mathrm{finish}(s)$ for $s \in \{\text{first}, \text{second}\}$, good and bad: it reads one framed block of the
input, normalizes the pair it codes, emits both fields, and repeats.
