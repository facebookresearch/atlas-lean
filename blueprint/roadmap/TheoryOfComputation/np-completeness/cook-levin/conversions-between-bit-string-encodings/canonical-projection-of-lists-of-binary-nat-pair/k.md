# Stacks of the pair-list projector

The pair-list projector has eleven stacks: the input, two accumulators (first components and second components)
holding outputs reversed, two outputs (first components and second components), and a copy of the six stacks of the
[number-pair projector](../projecting-and-normalizing-a-pair-of-binary-natu/k.md), used as the workspace for one
pair.
