# Workspace layout of the list-of-naturals canonicalizer

For bit strings $x$, $a$, $o$ and stack contents $s$ of the [payload normalizer
machine](../a-linear-time-machine-for-total-binary-payload-n/k.md), the stack contents $\mathrm{st}(x, a, o, s)$ of
the [list canonicalizer](prog.md) put $x$ on the input, $a$ on the accumulator, $o$ on the output and $s$ on the
three workspace stacks.
