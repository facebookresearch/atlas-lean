# Pair-frame splitter halt redirection in the number-pair projector

The label map from the [pair-frame splitter](../raw-projection-of-the-canonical-pair-frame/lab.md) to the
[number-pair projector](lab.md) sends the splitter's label good to
$\mathrm{norm}(\text{first}, \mathrm{read}(\text{none}))$, its label bad to bad, and every other label $\ell$ to
$\mathrm{pair}(\ell)$.
