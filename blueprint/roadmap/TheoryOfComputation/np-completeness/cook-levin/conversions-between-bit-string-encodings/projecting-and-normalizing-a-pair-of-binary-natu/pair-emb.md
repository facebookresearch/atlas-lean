# Pair-frame splitter stack embedding in the number-pair projector

The stack embedding of the [pair-frame splitter](../raw-projection-of-the-canonical-pair-frame/prog.md) into the
[number-pair projector](prog.md) sends each of the splitter's three stacks (input, left, scratch) to the
corresponding copy in the projector, with the identity on the bit alphabets. The splitter acts on no other stack.
