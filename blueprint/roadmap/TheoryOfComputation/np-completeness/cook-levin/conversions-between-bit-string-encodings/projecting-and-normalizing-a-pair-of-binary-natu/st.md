# Six-stack invariant of the number-pair projector

For bit strings $x$, $q$, $u$, $w$, $\ell$, $\rho$, the stack contents $\mathrm{st}(x, q, u, w, \ell, \rho)$ of the
[number-pair projector](prog.md) put $x$ on the splitter's input stack, $q$ on the splitter's left stack, $u$ on the
splitter's scratch stack, $w$ on the buffer, $\ell$ on the left output and $\rho$ on the right output.
