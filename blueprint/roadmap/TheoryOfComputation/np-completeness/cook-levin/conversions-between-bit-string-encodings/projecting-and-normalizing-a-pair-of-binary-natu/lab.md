# Control labels of the number-pair projector

The labels of the number-pair projector are $\mathrm{pair}(\ell)$ for each label $\ell$ of the [pair-frame
splitter](../raw-projection-of-the-canonical-pair-frame/lab.md), $\mathrm{norm}(s, \ell)$ for each field
$s \in \{\text{first}, \text{second}\}$ and each label $\ell$ of the [payload normalizer
machine](../a-linear-time-machine-for-total-binary-payload-n/lab.md), good and bad: the pair is split, then its
two fields are normalized one after the other.
