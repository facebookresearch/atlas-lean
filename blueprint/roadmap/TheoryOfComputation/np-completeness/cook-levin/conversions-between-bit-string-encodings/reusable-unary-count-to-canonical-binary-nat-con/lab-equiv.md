# Label enumeration of the unary-to-binary converter

The [labels](lab.md) of the unary-to-binary converter are in bijection with the disjoint union of the labels of the
vertex-cover to subset-sum reduction machine and two copies of the Booleans: $\mathrm{emit}(\ell)$ corresponds to
$\ell$, clean and read to false and true in the first copy, and back and done to false and true in the second copy.
In particular the converter has finitely many labels.
