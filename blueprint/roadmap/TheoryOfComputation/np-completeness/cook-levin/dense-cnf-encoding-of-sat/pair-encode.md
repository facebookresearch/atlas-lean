# Pair encoding $w \# y$

For bit strings $w$ and $y$, the word over the pair alphabet
$G = \{0,1\} \sqcup (\{0,1\} \cup \{\#\})$ consisting of the bits $b$ of $w$ in
order, each tagged on the left as $\mathrm{inl}\,b$, then the separator
$\#$, then the bits $b$ of $y$ in order, each tagged on the right as
$\mathrm{inr}(b)$. This is the input of the dense CNF-SAT verifier.
