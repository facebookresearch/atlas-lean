# ZERO FORCING NUMBER is NP-complete

The decision problem ZERO FORCING NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

Yang, "On the complexity of the positive semidefinite zero forcing number", Linear Algebra and its Applications 491 (2016), 101-122, doi:10.1016/j.laa.2015.03.011, Corollary 3.4 (via B. - A. Aazami, *Hardness results and approximation algorithms for some problems on graphs*, PhD thesis, University of Waterloo (2008), Theorem 2.3.1 (NP-hardness with node weights 0 and 1, on planar graphs).
