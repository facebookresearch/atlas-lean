# The decision problem MINIMUM MAXIMAL MATCHING on planar graphs of maximum degree 3

MINIMUM MAXIMAL MATCHING on planar graphs of maximum degree 3 as a decision problem on
bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring
encoding, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM MAXIMAL MATCHING. A
bitstring that does not decode to an instance is rejected.
