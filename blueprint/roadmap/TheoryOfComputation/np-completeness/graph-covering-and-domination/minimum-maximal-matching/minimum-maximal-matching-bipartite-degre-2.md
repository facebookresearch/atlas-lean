# MINIMUM MAXIMAL MATCHING on bipartite graphs of maximum degree 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM MAXIMAL MATCHING on bipartite graphs of maximum degree 3 is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT10]; Yannakakis and Gavril (1980).
