# DOMINATING SET ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMINATING SET ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT2] DOMINATING SET, p. 190, comment: "Remains NP-complete for planar graphs with maximum vertex degree 3" (credited there to [Garey and Johnson, unpublished]).
- I. E. Zverovich and V. E. Zverovich, "An induced subgraph characterization of domination perfect graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 20 (1995), 375-395, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190200313 (NP-completeness on planar bipartite graphs of maximum degree 3 and girth at least 9, a subclass, as recorded by ISGCI for class `gc_1226`).
