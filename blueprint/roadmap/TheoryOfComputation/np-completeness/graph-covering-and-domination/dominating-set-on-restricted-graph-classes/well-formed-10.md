# Well-formed instances of DOMINATING SET ON (C_3, \dots, C_8, H, X_{85}, K_{1,4})-FREE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for DOMINATING SET ON $(C_3, \dots, C_8, H, X_{85}, K_{1,4})$-FREE GRAPHS when it is a well-formed DOMINATING SET instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $0 < K \le n$, and none of the cycles $C_3, \dots, C_8$, the graph $H$ (two adjacent vertices of degree $3$ with two further neighbors each), the graph $X_{85}$ (a path on five vertices with pendant vertices at its second and fourth vertices) and the star $K_{1,4}$ is an induced subgraph of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$). Here a graph $F$ is an induced subgraph of a graph $G$ when some injective map from the vertices of $F$ to those of $G$ preserves both adjacency and non-adjacency.
