# Feasible instances of DOMINATING CYCLE ON 2-CONNECTED PLANAR GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for DOMINATING CYCLE ON 2-CONNECTED PLANAR GRAPHS when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a cycle of length at most $K$ whose vertices form a dominating set: every vertex lies on the cycle or is adjacent to a vertex of it.
