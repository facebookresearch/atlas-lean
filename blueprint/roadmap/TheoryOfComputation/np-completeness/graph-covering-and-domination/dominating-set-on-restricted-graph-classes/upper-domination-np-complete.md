# UPPER DOMINATION is NP-complete

The decision problem UPPER DOMINATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. A. Cheston, G. Fricke, S. T. Hedetniemi and D. P. Jacobs, "On the computational complexity of upper fractional domination", Discrete Applied Mathematics 27(3) (1990), 195-207, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(90)90065-K.
