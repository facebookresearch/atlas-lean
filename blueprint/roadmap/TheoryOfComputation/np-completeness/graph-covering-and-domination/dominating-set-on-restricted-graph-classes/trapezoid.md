# Trapezoid between two horizontal lines

For real numbers $a, b, c, d$, the trapezoid is the convex hull of the four points $(a, 1)$, $(b, 1)$, $(c, 0)$ and $(d, 0)$: the region between the lines $y = 1$ and $y = 0$ whose top side is the segment joining $(a, 1)$ and $(b, 1)$ and whose bottom side is the segment joining $(c, 0)$ and $(d, 0)$. No order of the endpoints is assumed, so the top side runs from $\min(a, b)$ to $\max(a, b)$; the ordering $a \le b$, $c \le d$ is imposed only in the definition of trapezoid graphs.
