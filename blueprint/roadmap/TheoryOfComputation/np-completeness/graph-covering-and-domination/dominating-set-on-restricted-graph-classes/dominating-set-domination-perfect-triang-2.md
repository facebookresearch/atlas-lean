# DOMINATING SET ON TRIANGLE-FREE DOMINATION PERFECT GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMINATING SET ON TRIANGLE-FREE DOMINATION PERFECT GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 190, problem [GT2] DOMINATING SET, comment: "Remains NP-complete for planar graphs with maximum vertex degree 3" [Garey and Johnson, unpublished].
- I. E. Zverovich and V. E. Zverovich, "An induced subgraph characterization of domination perfect graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 20 (1995), 375-395, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190200313 (definition of domination perfect graphs; NP-completeness of DOMINATING SET on classes of them).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_553` (domination perfect ∩ triangle-free), problem Domination: NP-complete (Zverovich and Zverovich 1995; and on its subclass `gc_529`, Zverovich and Zverovich 2006).
