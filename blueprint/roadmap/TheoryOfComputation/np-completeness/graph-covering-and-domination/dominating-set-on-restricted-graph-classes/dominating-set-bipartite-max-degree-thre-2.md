# DOMINATING SET ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMINATING SET ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 190, problem [GT2] DOMINATING SET, comment: "Remains NP-complete for planar graphs with maximum vertex degree 3" [Garey and Johnson, unpublished].
- I. E. Zverovich and V. E. Zverovich, "An induced subgraph characterization of domination perfect graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 20 (1995), 375-395, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190200313.
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_941` (bipartite ∩ maximum degree 3, https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_941.html), problem Domination: NP-complete, from the subclass bipartite ∩ girth >= 9 ∩ maximum degree 3 ∩ planar (Zverovich and Zverovich 1995).
