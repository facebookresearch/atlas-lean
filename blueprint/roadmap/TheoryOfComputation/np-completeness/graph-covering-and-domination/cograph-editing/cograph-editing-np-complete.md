# COGRAPH EDITING is NP-complete

The COGRAPH EDITING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Y. Liu, J. Wang, J. Guo and J. Chen, "Complexity and parameterized algorithms for Cograph Editing", Theoretical Computer Science 461 (2012), 45-54, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2011.11.040.
