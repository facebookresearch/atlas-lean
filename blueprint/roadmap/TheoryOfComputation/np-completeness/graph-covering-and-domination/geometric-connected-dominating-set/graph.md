# Graph

The distance-$B$ graph of a GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET instance has the positions $0, \dots, m-1$ of its point list as vertices; two distinct positions $a \ne b$ are adjacent when their points $p_a, p_b$ satisfy $\|p_a - p_b\|_2^2 \le B^2$, that is, when their Euclidean distance is at most $B$. Two positions holding the same point are adjacent. For a [well-formed](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/distance-d-partition-of-points-in-the-plane/well-formed.md) instance the listed points are distinct, so the vertices are the points of $P$.
