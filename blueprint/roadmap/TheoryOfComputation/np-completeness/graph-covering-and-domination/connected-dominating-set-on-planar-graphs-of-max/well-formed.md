# Well-formed CONNECTED DOMINATING SET on planar graphs of maximum degree 4 instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of CONNECTED DOMINATING SET on planar graphs of maximum degree 4 is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < K \le n$, its graph is planar, and every vertex has degree at most $4$.
