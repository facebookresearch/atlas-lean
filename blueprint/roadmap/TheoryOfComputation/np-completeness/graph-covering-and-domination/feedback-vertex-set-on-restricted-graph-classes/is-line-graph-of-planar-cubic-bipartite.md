# Line graphs of planar cubic bipartite graphs

A simple graph $G$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ is the line graph of a planar cubic bipartite graph when, for some $m$, there is a simple graph $H$ on $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ that has a straight-line planar drawing (an injective placement of the vertices in $\mathbb{R}^2$ in which the segments of two distinct edges meet at most in common endpoints), is bipartite and has every vertex of degree exactly $3$, such that $G$ is isomorphic to the line graph of $H$.
