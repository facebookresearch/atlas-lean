# Star convex bipartite graphs

A simple graph is star convex bipartite when its vertices split into a set $X$ and its complement with every edge joining the two sides, and for some center $c \in X$, for every vertex $y$ outside $X$, the neighbors of $y$ induce a preconnected subgraph of the star on $X$ with center $c$ (any two of them are joined by a walk inside that set; an empty or one-vertex set qualifies). In a star this says that every vertex outside $X$ has at most one neighbor or is adjacent to $c$. The center makes $X$ nonempty.
