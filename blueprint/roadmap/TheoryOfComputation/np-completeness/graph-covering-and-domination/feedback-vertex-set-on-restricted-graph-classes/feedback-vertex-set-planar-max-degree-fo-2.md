# FEEDBACK VERTEX SET ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 is NP-complete

The decision problem FEEDBACK VERTEX SET ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Speckenmeyer, "On feedback vertex sets and nonseparating independent sets in cubic graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 12(3) (1988), 405-412, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190120311.
- T. Bai, Y. Cao and M. Xiao, "Feedback set problems on bounded-degree (planar) graphs", arXiv:2605.11407 (2026), Section 1 (records that Speckenmeyer, and independently two other groups, settled the undirected problem on planar graphs of maximum degree four in the 1980s).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT7] FEEDBACK VERTEX SET (undirected version in the comment).
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_909.html), problem "Feedback vertex set" on planar graphs of maximum degree 4: NP-complete, with the attribution to Speckenmeyer (1988).
