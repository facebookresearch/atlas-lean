# FEEDBACK VERTEX SET ON STAR CONVEX BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem FEEDBACK VERTEX SET ON STAR CONVEX BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- W. Jiang, T. Liu, T. Ren and K. Xu, "Two hardness results on feedback vertex sets", Frontiers in Algorithmics and Algorithmic Aspects in Information and Management (FAW-AAIM 2011), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 6681 (2011), 233-243, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-21204-8_26.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT7] FEEDBACK VERTEX SET (undirected version in the comment).
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_1151.html), problem "Feedback vertex set" on star convex graphs: NP-complete, with the attribution to Jiang, Liu, Ren and Xu.
