# Perfect edge elimination orderings

A list $e_0, \dots, e_{m-1}$ of vertex pairs is a perfect edge elimination ordering of a simple graph $G$ when each $e_i$ is a bisimplicial edge of the subgraph induced by the vertices that are not endpoints of $e_0, \dots, e_{i-1}$, and no edge of $G$ joins two vertices that are endpoints of no $e_j$.
