# K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs is NP-complete

The K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. G. Corneil and Y. Perl, "Clustering and domination in perfect graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 9(1) (1984), 27-39, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(84)90088-X (abstract: "it is shown that the k-cluster problem is NP-complete for both bipartite and chordal graphs").
