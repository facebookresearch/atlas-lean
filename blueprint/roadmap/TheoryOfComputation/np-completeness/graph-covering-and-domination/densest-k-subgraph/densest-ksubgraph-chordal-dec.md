# K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs as a decision problem

The K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to a well-formed, feasible instance of K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
