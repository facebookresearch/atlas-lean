# INDEPENDENT SEQUENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem INDEPENDENT SEQUENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. M. Halldórsson, "Approximations of weighted independent set and hereditary subset problems", Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications 4(1) (2000), 1-16, doi:10.7155/jgaa.00020, Section 2.1, problem Max Independent Sequence (introduced by Blundo) and Proposition 2.3 (a size-preserving reduction from INDEPENDENT SET).
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM INDEPENDENT SEQUENCE.
