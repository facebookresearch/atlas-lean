# Feasible DIRECTED ELIMINATION ORDERING instances

An instance $(n, A, K)$ of DIRECTED ELIMINATION ORDERING is feasible when some elimination ordering, a one-to-one map $f$ from the vertices to $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, has fill-in at most $K$: at most $K$ ordered pairs $(u, v)$ that are not arcs are joined by a fill path.
