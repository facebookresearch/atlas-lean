# DIRECTED ELIMINATION ORDERING is NP-complete

The DIRECTED ELIMINATION ORDERING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT46] DIRECTED ELIMINATION ORDERING.
- D. J. Rose and R. E. Tarjan, "Algorithmic aspects of vertex elimination on directed graphs", SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 34 (1978), 176-197, doi:10.1137/0134014.
