# Well-formed COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, and $0 < K \le |E|$, the length of the edge list.
