# COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS is NP-complete

The COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT18] COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS.
- J. Orlin, "Contentment in graph theory: covering graphs with cliques", Indagationes Mathematicae (Proceedings) 80 (1977), 406-424, doi:10.1016/1385-7258(77)90055-5 (NP-complete already for bipartite graphs).
