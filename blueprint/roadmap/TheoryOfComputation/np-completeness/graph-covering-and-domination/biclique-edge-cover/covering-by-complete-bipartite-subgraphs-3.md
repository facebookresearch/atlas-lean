# COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS as a decision problem

The COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to a well-formed, feasible instance of COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
