# Feasible COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of COVERING BY COMPLETE BIPARTITE SUBGRAPHS is feasible when, for some $k \le K$, there are vertex sets $A_j$ and $B_j$ ($j \le k$), every vertex of $A_j$ adjacent to every vertex of $B_j$, such that every edge has one endpoint in $A_j$ and the other in $B_j$ for some $j$.
