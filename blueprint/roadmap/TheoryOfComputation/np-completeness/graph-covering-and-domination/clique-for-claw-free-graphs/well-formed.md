# Well-formed CLIQUE for claw-free graphs instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of CLIQUE for claw-free graphs is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, its graph is claw-free (has no induced $K_{1,3}$), and $0 < K \le n$.
