# Well-formed K-CLUSTER (DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH) on chordal graphs instances

An instance $(n, E, k, J)$ of DENSEST k-SUBGRAPH is well-formed for chordal graphs when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, its graph is chordal (no induced cycle of length $4$ or more), $k > 0$, and $J > 0$.
