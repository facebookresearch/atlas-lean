# L1 Graph

The $L_1$ distance-$B$ graph has the positions of the point list as vertices; two distinct positions are adjacent when their points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ satisfy $|x_1 - x_2| + |y_1 - y_2| \le B$. Two positions holding the same point are adjacent; for a well-formed instance the listed points are distinct, so the vertices are the points of $P$.
