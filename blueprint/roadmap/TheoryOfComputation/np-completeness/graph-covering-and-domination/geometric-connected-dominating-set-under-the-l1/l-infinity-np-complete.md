# GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET under the $L_\infty$ metric is NP-complete

The decision problem [GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET under the $L_\infty$
metric](l-infinity-dec.md) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time
many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND48] GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET, comment (p. 219): "Remains NP-complete if the Euclidean metric is replaced by the `L₁` rectilinear metric or the `L∞` metric [Garey and Johnson, ——]" (unpublished).
