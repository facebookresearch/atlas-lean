# GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET under the $L_1$ metric as a decision problem

The decision problem GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET under the $L_1$ metric accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/distance-d-partition-of-points-in-the-plane/well-formed.md) for
GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET and [feasible under the $L_1$ metric](l1-feasible.md). It
rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
