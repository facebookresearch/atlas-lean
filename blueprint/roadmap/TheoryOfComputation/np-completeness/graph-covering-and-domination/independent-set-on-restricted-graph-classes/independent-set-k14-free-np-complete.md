# INDEPENDENT SET ON K_{1,4}-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem INDEPENDENT SET ON $K_{1,4}$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 195, problem [GT20] INDEPENDENT SET, comment: "Remains NP-complete for cubic planar graphs [Garey, Johnson, and Stockmeyer, 1976], [Garey and Johnson, 1977a], [Maier and Storer, 1977]".
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and L. Stockmeyer, "Some simplified NP-complete graph problems", Theoretical Computer Science 1(3) (1976), 237-267, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1.
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_388` (K_1,4-free), problem Independent set: NP-complete (with the subclasses "cubic ∩ hamiltonian ∩ planar" and "2-connected ∩ cubic ∩ planar" also recorded as NP-complete).
