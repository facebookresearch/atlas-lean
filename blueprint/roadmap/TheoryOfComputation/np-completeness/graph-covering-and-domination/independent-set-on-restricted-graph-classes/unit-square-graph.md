# Intersection graph of unit squares

In INDEPENDENT SET OF UNIT SQUARES, the intersection graph of the closed axis-parallel unit squares centered at the given points: two distinct squares are adjacent when both coordinates of their centers differ by at most $1$, that is, $|x_a - x_b| \le 1$ and $|y_a - y_b| \le 1$, with the differences taken in $\mathbb{Q}$.
