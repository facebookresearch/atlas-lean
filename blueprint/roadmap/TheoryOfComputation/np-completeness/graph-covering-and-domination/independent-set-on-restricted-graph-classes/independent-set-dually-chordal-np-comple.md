# INDEPENDENT SET ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem INDEPENDENT SET ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT20] INDEPENDENT SET (the parent problem).
- A. Brandstädt, V. D. Chepoi and F. F. Dragan, "The algorithmic use of hypertree structure and maximum neighbourhood orderings", Discrete Applied Mathematics 82(1-3) (1998), 43-77, doi:10.1016/s0166-218x(97)00125-x (cited by ISGCI and Wikipedia for this result).
- A. Brandstädt, F. F. Dragan, V. D. Chepoi and V. I. Voloshin, "Dually chordal graphs", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 11(3) (1998), 437-455, doi:10.1137/S0895480193253415 (the class).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_185` (dually chordal), problem Independent set: NP-complete (Brandstädt, Chepoi and Dragan 1998).
