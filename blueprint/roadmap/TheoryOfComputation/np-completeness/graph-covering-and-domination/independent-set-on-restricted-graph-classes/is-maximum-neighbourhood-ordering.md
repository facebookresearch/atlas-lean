# Maximum neighbourhood orderings

An ordering $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ of the vertices of a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is a maximum neighbourhood ordering when, for every position $i$, writing $N_i[x]$ for the closed neighbourhood of $x$ in the subgraph induced by the vertices at positions $\ge i$, there is $j \ge i$ with $\sigma_j \in N_i[\sigma_i]$ such that $N_i[\sigma_k] \subseteq N_i[\sigma_j]$ for every $\sigma_k \in N_i[\sigma_i]$ (with $k \ge i$).
