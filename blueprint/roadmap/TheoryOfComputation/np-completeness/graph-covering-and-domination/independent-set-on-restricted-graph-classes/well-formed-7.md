# Well-formed instances of INDEPENDENT SET ON PURE-3-DIR GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for INDEPENDENT SET ON PURE-3-DIR GRAPHS when its edge list $E$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is a PURE-3-DIR graph (there are three real slopes, not necessarily distinct, and for each vertex a non-degenerate closed segment in the plane whose left endpoint has smaller first coordinate than its right endpoint and whose slope is one of the three, such that segments of distinct vertices with the same slope are disjoint and two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their segments meet; so at most three directions are used), and $0 < K \le n$.
