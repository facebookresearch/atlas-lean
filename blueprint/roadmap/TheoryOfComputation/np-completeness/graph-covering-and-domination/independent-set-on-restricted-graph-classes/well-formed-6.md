# Well-formed instances of INDEPENDENT SET ON PLANAR k-REGULAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS

Fix a natural number $k$. An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for INDEPENDENT SET ON PLANAR $k$-REGULAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS when its edge list $E$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, $0 < K \le n$, and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a straight-line planar drawing (an injective placement of the vertices in $\mathbb{R}^2$ in which the segments of two distinct edges meet at most in common endpoints), has every vertex of degree exactly $k$, and is Hamiltonian in Mathlib's sense (if $n \ne 1$ it has a Hamiltonian cycle).
