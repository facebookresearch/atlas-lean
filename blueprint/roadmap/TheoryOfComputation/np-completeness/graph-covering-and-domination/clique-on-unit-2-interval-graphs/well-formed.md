# Well-formed CLIQUE on unit 2-interval graphs instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of CLIQUE on unit 2-interval graphs is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < K \le n$, and its graph is a unit $2$-interval graph: each vertex can be assigned two disjoint closed intervals of length $1$ so that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when the unions of their intervals intersect.
