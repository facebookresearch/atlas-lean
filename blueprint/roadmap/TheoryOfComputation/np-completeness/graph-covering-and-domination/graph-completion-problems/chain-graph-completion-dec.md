# CHAIN GRAPH COMPLETION as a decision problem

The decision problem CHAIN GRAPH COMPLETION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(p, q, E, K)$ (two natural numbers, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for CHAIN GRAPH COMPLETION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
