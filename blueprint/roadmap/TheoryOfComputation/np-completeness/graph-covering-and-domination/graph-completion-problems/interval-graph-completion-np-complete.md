# INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION is NP-complete

The decision problem INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT35] INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION (credited there to unpublished work of Garey, Gavril, and Johnson).
- K. Yamaguchi, T. Araki, and T. Kashiwabara, "NP-completeness of interval graph completion from a path graph", Electronics and Communications in Japan, Part III 78(6) (1995), 95-102, doi:10.1002/ecjc.4430780610 (NP-complete already when the input graph is a path graph, that is, a vertex intersection graph of paths in a tree).
- Y. Villanger, P. Heggernes, C. Paul, and J. A. Telle, "Interval completion is fixed parameter tractable", SIAM Journal on Computing 38(5) (2009), 2007-2020, doi:10.1137/070710913 (records the problem as NP-complete, citing [GT35]).
