# INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION ON LINE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION ON LINE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT35] INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION, p. 198, comment: "Remains NP-complete when G is restricted to be an edge graph" (the entry is credited there to Garey, Gavril, and Johnson, 1977, unpublished).
