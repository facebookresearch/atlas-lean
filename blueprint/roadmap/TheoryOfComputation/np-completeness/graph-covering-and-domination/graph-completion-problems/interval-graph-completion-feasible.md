# Feasible instances of INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for INTERVAL GRAPH COMPLETION when some interval graph $H$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ contains every edge of the graph $G$ on those vertices in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$, and has at most $K$ edges that are not edges of $G$.
