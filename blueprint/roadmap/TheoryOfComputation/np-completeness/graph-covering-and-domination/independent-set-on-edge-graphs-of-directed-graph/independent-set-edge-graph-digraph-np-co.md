# INDEPENDENT SET on edge graphs of directed graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem INDEPENDENT SET on edge graphs of directed graphs belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT20] INDEPENDENT SET, comment: "Remains NP-complete … for edge graphs of directed graphs [Gavril, 1977a]".
- F. Gavril, "Some NP-complete problems on graphs", Proceedings of the 11th Conference on Information Sciences and Systems, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore (1977), 91-95 (not available; the result and its attribution are those recorded by Garey and Johnson).
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and L. Stockmeyer, "Some simplified NP-complete graph problems", Theoretical Computer Science 1(3) (1976), 237-267, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1.
