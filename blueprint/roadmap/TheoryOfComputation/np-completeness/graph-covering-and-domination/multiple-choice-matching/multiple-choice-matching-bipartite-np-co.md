# MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING (bipartite, classes of at most 2 edges, K = |V|/2) is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING (bipartite, classes of at most 2 edges, K = |V|/2)
is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time
many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT55], comment, which states this restriction for a partition of
the edges into classes; Itai, Rodeh and Tanimoto (1978), Section 2, whose related formulation lets
one edge occur in several conflict pairs rather than in one class; Itai and Rodeh (1977).
