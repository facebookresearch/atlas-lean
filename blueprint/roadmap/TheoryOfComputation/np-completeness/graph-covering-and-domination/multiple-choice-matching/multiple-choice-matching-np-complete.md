# MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT55], which states the problem for a partition of the edges
into classes; Itai, Rodeh and Tanimoto (1978), Section 2, whose related formulation lets one edge
occur in several conflict pairs rather than in one class; Itai and Rodeh (1977).
