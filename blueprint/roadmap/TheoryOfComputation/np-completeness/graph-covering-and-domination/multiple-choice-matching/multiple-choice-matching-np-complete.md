# MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPLE CHOICE MATCHING is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT55]; Itai, Rodeh and Tanimoto (1978); Itai and Rodeh
(1977).
