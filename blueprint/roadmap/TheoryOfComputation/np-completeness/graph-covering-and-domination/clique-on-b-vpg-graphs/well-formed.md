# Well-formed CLIQUE on B1-VPG graphs instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of CLIQUE on $B_1$-VPG graphs is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < K \le n$, and its graph is a $B_1$-VPG graph.
