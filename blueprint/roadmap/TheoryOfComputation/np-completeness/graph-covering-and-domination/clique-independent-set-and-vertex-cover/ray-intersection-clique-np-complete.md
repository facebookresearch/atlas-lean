# CLIQUE in ray intersection graphs is NP-complete

CLIQUE in ray intersection graphs is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT19]; NP-hardness, with the rays given by integer coordinates: Cabello, Cardinal and Langerman (2013), Lemma 2.8 and Theorem 2.10; membership: a clique is a polynomial-time checkable certificate.
