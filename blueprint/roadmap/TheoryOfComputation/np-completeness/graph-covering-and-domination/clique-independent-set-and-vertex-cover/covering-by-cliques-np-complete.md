# COVERING BY CLIQUES is NP-complete

The COVERING BY CLIQUES decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Since INTERSECTION GRAPH BASIS ([GT59]) is the same language, this is also its NP-completeness.
