# COVERING BY CLIQUES is NP-complete

The COVERING BY CLIQUES decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT17] COVERING BY CLIQUES.
- L. T. Kou, L. J. Stockmeyer, and C. K. Wong, "Covering edges by cliques with regard to keyword conflicts and intersection graphs", Communications of the ACM 21 (1978), 135-139, doi:10.1145/359340.359346.
- J. Orlin, "Contentment in graph theory: covering graphs with cliques", Indagationes Mathematicae (Proceedings) 80 (1977), 406-424, doi:10.1016/1385-7258(77)90055-5.
