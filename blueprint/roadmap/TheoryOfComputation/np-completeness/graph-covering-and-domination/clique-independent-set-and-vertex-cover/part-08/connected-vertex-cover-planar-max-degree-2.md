# Feasible instances of CONNECTED VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for CONNECTED VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a set $S$ of at most $K$ vertices that is a vertex cover (every edge has an endpoint in $S$) and induces a connected subgraph. Connectedness includes nonemptiness, so $S$ has at least one vertex.
