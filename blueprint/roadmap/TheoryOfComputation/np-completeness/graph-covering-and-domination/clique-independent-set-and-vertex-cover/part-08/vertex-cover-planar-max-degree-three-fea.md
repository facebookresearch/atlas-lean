# Feasible instances of VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a vertex cover with at most $K$ vertices: a set $S$ of vertices such that every edge has an endpoint in $S$.
