# CONNECTED VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 is NP-complete

The decision problem CONNECTED VERTEX COVER ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT1] VERTEX COVER, p. 190, comment: "Variation in which the subgraph induced by V' is required to be connected is also NP-complete, even for planar graphs with no vertex degree exceeding 4 [Garey and Johnson, 1977a]".
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, "The rectilinear Steiner tree problem is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 32(4) (1977), 826-834, doi:10.1137/0132071.
