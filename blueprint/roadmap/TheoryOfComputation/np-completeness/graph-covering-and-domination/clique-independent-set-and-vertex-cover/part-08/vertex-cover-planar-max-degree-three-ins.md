# Instances of planar max-degree-3 VERTEX COVER

An instance of VERTEX COVER on planar graphs of maximum degree $3$ is a triple $(n, E, K)$ consisting of a natural number $n$ (the vertices are $0, \dots, n-1$), a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers (the edges) and a natural number $K$ (the bound on the size of the cover).
