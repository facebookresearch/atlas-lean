# CLIQUE of linear size is NP-complete

For all natural numbers $p$ and $q$ with $0 < p < q$, the CLIQUE of linear size decision problem with fraction $p/q$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT19] CLIQUE, comment.
