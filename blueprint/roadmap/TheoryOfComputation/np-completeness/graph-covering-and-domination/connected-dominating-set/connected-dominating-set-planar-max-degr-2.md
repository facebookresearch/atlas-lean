# CONNECTED DOMINATING SET on planar graphs of maximum degree 4 is NP-complete

The CONNECTED DOMINATING SET on planar graphs of maximum degree 4 decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT2] DOMINATING SET, p. 190, comment ("Variation in which the subgraph induced by V' is required to be connected is also NP-complete", credited there to [Garey and Johnson, unpublished]).
- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 5(1) (1984), 147-160, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(84)90045-2, Section 3 (records that the unpublished 1978 result of Garey and Johnson covers CONNECTED DOMINATING SET on "planar graphs with maximum degree 4"; the original proof was not available).
