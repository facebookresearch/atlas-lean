# FEEDBACK ARC SET with total degree at most 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem FEEDBACK ARC SET with total degree at most 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT8] FEEDBACK ARC SET, comment on p. 192 ("Remains NP-complete for digraphs in which no vertex has total in-degree and out-degree more than 3, and for edge digraphs [Gavril, 1977a]").
- F. Gavril, "Some NP-complete problems on graphs", Proc. 11th Conference on Information Sciences and Systems, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore, MD (1977), 91-95.
