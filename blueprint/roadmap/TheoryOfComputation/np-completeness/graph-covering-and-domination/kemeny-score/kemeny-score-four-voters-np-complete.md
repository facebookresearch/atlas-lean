# KEMENY SCORE with four voters is NP-complete

The decision problem KEMENY SCORE with four voters is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Dwork, Kumar, Naor and Sivakumar (2001), Theorem 11; Madarasi (2026), Lemma 30.
