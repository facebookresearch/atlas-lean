# The decision problem KEMENY SCORE with four voters

KEMENY SCORE with four voters as a decision problem on bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is [well-formed for KEMENY SCORE with four voters](well-formed.md) (a well-formed KEMENY SCORE instance with exactly four ballots) and feasible for KEMENY SCORE. The feasibility test is the unrestricted problem's; the restriction to KEMENY SCORE with four voters comes from the well-formedness test alone. A bitstring that does not decode to an instance is rejected.
