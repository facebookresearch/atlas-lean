# Well-formed INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs instances

A INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs instance is well-formed when the edge list describes a simple graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, the graph is isomorphic to the total graph of a bipartite graph `H` (on the vertices $0, \dots, m-1$): the disjoint sum of `H` and its line graph, together with the edges joining each vertex `a` of `H` to each edge `e` of `H` containing it; and $0 < K \le n$. The edge-list encoding checks are those of a [simple edge list](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/partition-into-triangles/well-formed.md): no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ has $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, and no pair is listed together with its reversal. These checks concern the encoding: adding the reversed copy of a listed edge makes the instance malformed although it does not change the simple graph.
