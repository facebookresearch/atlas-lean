# Well-formed INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs instances

A INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs instance is well-formed when the edge list
describes a simple graph on `Fin n`, the graph is isomorphic to the total graph of a bipartite
graph `H` (on vertices `Fin m`): the disjoint sum of `H` and its line graph, together with the
edges joining each vertex `a` of `H` to each edge `e` of `H` containing it; and `0 < K ≤ |V|`.
