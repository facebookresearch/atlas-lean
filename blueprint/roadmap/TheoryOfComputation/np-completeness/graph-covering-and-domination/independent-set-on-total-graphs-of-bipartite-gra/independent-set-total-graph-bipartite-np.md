# INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem INDEPENDENT SET on total graphs of bipartite graphs belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT20] INDEPENDENT SET, comment: "Remains NP-complete … for total graphs of bipartite graphs [Yannakakis and Gavril, 1978]".
- M. Yannakakis and F. Gavril, "Edge dominating sets in graphs", SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 38(3) (1980), 364-372, doi:10.1137/0138030.
