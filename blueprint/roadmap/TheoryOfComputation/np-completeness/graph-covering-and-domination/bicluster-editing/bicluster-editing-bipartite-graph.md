# Bipartite graph of a set of pairs

For a set $F \subseteq \{0, \dots, p - 1\} \times \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$, the bipartite graph of $F$ has the disjoint union of the two sides as its vertices, and $u$ in the first side is adjacent to $v$ in the second side exactly when $(u, v) \in F$; there are no edges within a side.
