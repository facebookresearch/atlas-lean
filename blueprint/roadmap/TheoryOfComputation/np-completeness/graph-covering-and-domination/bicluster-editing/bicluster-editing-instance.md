# Instances of BICLUSTER EDITING

An instance of BICLUSTER EDITING is a quadruple $(p, q, E, K)$: the sizes $p = |U|$ and $q = |V|$ of the two sides $U = \{0, \dots, p - 1\}$ and $V = \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ of a bipartite graph, its edge list $E$ of pairs $(u, v)$ with $u \in U$ and $v \in V$, and the bound $K$ on the number of edits.
