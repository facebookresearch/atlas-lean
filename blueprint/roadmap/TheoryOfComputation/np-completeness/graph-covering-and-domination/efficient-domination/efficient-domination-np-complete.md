# EFFICIENT DOMINATION (PERFECT CODE) is NP-complete

The decision problem EFFICIENT
DOMINATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP reduces to it by a polynomial-time
many-one reduction.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), EFFICIENT DOMINATION; Derek G. Corneil and Yehoshua Perl, "Clustering and domination in perfect graphs" (1984) and the perfect-code literature.
