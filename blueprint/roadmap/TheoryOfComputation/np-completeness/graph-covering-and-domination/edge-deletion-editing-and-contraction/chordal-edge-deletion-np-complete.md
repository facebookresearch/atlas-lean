# CHORDAL EDGE DELETION is NP-complete

The CHORDAL EDGE DELETION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Natanzon, R. Shamir and R. Sharan, "Complexity classification of some edge modification problems", Discrete Applied Mathematics 113 (2001), 109-128, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00391-7, Theorem 3.6.
