# COGRAPH EDGE DELETION is NP-complete

The COGRAPH EDGE DELETION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- E. S. El-Mallah and C. J. Colbourn, "The complexity of some edge deletion problems", IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems 35 (1988), 354-362, doi:10.1109/31.1748.
- A. Natanzon, R. Shamir and R. Sharan, "Complexity classification of some edge modification problems", Discrete Applied Mathematics 113 (2001), 109-128, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00391-7.
