# BIPARTITE CONTRACTION is NP-complete

The BIPARTITE CONTRACTION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Heggernes, P. van 't Hof, D. Lokshtanov and C. Paul, "Obtaining a bipartite graph by contracting few edges", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 27(4) (2013), 2143-2156, doi:10.1137/130907392 (arXiv:1102.5441), Sections 1, 2 and 5.
