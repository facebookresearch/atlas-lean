# Well-formed CONNECTED EDGE-SUBGRAPH on graphs of maximum degree 3 instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ (the instance format of EDGE-SUBGRAPH) is well-formed for this variant when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < K \le |E|$, and every vertex of its graph has degree at most $3$.
