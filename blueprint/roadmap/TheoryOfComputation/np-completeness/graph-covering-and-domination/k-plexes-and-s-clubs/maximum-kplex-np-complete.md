# MAXIMUM K-PLEX is NP-complete for every fixed k ≥ 1

For every natural number $k \ge 1$, the following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, and a positive integer $c$, decide whether some set $S$ of exactly $c$ vertices has every vertex of $S$ adjacent to at least $|S| - k$ vertices of $S$.

## Sources

- B. Balasundaram, S. Butenko and I. V. Hicks, "Clique relaxations in social network analysis: the maximum k-plex problem", Operations Research 59(1) (2011), 133-142, doi:10.1287/opre.1100.0851.
