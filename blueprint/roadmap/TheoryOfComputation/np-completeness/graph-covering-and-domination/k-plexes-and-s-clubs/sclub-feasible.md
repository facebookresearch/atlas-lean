# Feasible instances of s-CLUB

For a fixed natural number $s$, an instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for s-CLUB when some set $S$ of at least $K$ vertices induces a subgraph of diameter at most $s$, where the diameter is the largest distance between two vertices of the subgraph, infinite when two of them lie in different components and $0$ for the empty subgraph. For nonempty $S$ the induced subgraph is then connected; when $K = 0$ the empty set always qualifies, so connectedness is not part of the condition.
