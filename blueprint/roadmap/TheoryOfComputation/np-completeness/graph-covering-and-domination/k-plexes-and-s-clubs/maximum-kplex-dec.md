# MAXIMUM K-PLEX as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem MAXIMUM K-PLEX accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, c)$, to an instance that is well-formed for MAXIMUM K-PLEX and whose graph has a $k$-plex with exactly $c$ vertices. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
