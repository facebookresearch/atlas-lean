# EFFICIENT EDGE DOMINATION is NP-complete

The decision problem EFFICIENT EDGE DOMINATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP
has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. L. Grinstead, P. J. Slater, N. A. Sherwani and N. D. Holmes, "Efficient edge domination problems in graphs", Information Processing Letters 48 (1993), 221-228, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(93)90084-M.
