# CLIQUE on 3-interval graphs is NP-complete

The CLIQUE on 3-interval graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Butman, D. Hermelin, M. Lewenstein and D. Rawitz, "Optimization problems in multiple-interval graphs", ACM Transactions on Algorithms 6(2) (2010), Article 40, doi:10.1145/1721837.1721856 (SODA 2007, 268-277).
- recorded in M. C. Francis, D. Gonçalves and P. Ochem, "The maximum clique problem in multiple interval graphs", Algorithmica 71 (2015), 812-836, doi:10.1007/s00453-013-9828-6, Section 1 ("Butman et al. showed that MAXIMUM CLIQUE is NP-complete for t-interval graphs when t ≥ 3").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT19] CLIQUE.
