# Well-formed CLIQUE on 3-interval graphs instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of CLIQUE on 3-interval graphs is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < K \le n$, and its graph is a $3$-interval graph: each vertex can be assigned three closed real intervals (possibly equal) so that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when the unions of their intervals intersect.
