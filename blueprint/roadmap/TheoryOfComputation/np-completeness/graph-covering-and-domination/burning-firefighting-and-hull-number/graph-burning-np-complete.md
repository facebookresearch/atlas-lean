# GRAPH BURNING is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH BURNING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Bessy, A. Bonato, J. Janssen, D. Rautenbach and E. Roshanbin, "Burning a graph is hard", Discrete Applied Mathematics 232 (2017), 73-87, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2017.07.016.
