# Feasible instances of FIREFIGHTER on trees of maximum degree three

An instance $(n, E, r, k)$ of FIREFIGHTER on trees of maximum degree three is feasible when the root $r$ is a vertex ($r < n$) and, with the fire starting at $r$, there is a strategy, defending at each time a vertex not on fire and running until the fire can no longer spread, under which at most $k$ vertices burn.
