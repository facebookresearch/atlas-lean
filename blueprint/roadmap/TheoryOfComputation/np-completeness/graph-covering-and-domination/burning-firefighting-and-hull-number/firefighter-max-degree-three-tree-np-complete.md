# FIREFIGHTER on trees of maximum degree three is NP-complete

The decision problem FIREFIGHTER on trees of maximum degree three belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Finbow, A. King, G. MacGillivray and R. Rizzi, "The firefighter problem for graphs of maximum degree three", Discrete Mathematics 307 (2007), 2094-2105, doi:10.1016/j.disc.2005.12.053.
