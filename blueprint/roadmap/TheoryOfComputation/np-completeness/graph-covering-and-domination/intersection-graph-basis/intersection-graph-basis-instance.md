# Instances of INTERSECTION GRAPH BASIS

An instance of INTERSECTION GRAPH BASIS consists of a vertex count $n$, an edge list and the bound $K$. A raw edge may have an endpoint outside $0, \dots, n-1$; that the edges are in range and simple is checked separately by [well-formedness](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/connected-bipartite-subgraph/well-formed.md).
