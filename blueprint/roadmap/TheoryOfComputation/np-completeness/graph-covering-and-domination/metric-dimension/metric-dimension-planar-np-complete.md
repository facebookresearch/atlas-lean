# METRIC DIMENSION on planar graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem METRIC DIMENSION on planar graphs is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT61]; Díaz, Pottonen, Serna and van Leeuwen (2017), Theorem
3.6.
