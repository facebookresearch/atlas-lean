# IRREDUNDANCE NUMBER ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem IRREDUNDANCE NUMBER ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for IRREDUNDANCE NUMBER ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
