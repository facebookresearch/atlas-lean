# IRREDUNDANCE NUMBER ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem IRREDUNDANCE NUMBER ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
