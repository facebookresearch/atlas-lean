# Redundant vertices of a set

A vertex $v$ of a set $S$ of vertices of a simple graph is redundant in $S$ when $v$ is adjacent to some other member of $S$ and every neighbor of $v$ lies in $S$ or is adjacent to some vertex of $S$ other than $v$. Equivalently, every vertex of the closed neighborhood of $v$ lies in or is adjacent to $S \setminus \{v\}$, that is, $v$ has no private neighbor with respect to $S$.
