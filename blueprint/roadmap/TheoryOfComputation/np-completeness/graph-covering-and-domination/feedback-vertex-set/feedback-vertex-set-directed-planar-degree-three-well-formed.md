# Well-formed instances of FEEDBACK VERTEX SET on planar digraphs with in- and out-degree at most 3

An instance of FEEDBACK VERTEX SET on planar digraphs with in- and out-degree at most 3 is well-formed when the digraph has no loops or multiple arcs, its underlying graph is planar, every vertex has in-degree and out-degree at most 3, and $0 < K \le |V|$.
