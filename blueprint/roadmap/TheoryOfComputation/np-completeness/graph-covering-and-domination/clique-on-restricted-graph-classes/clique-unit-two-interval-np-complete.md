# CLIQUE on unit 2-interval graphs is NP-complete

The CLIQUE on unit 2-interval graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. C. Francis, D. Gonçalves and P. Ochem, "The maximum clique problem in multiple interval graphs", Algorithmica 71 (2015), 812-836, doi:10.1007/s00453-013-9828-6, Section 5 (Theorem 10 of arXiv:1201.0043v2: MAXIMUM CLIQUE is NP-complete for unit 2-interval graphs).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT19] CLIQUE.
