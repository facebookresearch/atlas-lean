# CLIQUE on dually chordal graphs is NP-complete

The CLIQUE on dually chordal graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT19] CLIQUE (the parent problem).
- A. Brandstädt, V. D. Chepoi and F. F. Dragan, "The algorithmic use of hypertree structure and maximum neighbourhood orderings", Discrete Applied Mathematics 82(1-3) (1998), 43-77, doi:10.1016/s0166-218x(97)00125-x (cited by Wikipedia, "Dually chordal graph", for this result).
- A. Leitert, "3-Colourability of dually chordal graphs in linear time", arXiv:1206.0233 (2012), Section 3 (adding a universal vertex makes any graph dually chordal, which reduces several NP-complete problems to dually chordal graphs; the idea is credited to Brandstädt et al.).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class gc_185 (dually chordal), problem Clique: NP-complete (note ref_1504: the universal-vertex reduction, communicated by V. Santos).
