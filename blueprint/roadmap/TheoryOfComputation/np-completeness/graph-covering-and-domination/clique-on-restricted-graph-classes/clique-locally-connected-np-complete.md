# CLIQUE on locally connected graphs is NP-complete

The CLIQUE on locally connected graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT19] CLIQUE (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class gc_932 (locally connected), problem Clique: NP-complete, reference ref_1506 (ISGCI note: adding a universal vertex to a connected graph yields a locally connected graph and raises the size of a largest clique by exactly one; communicated by P. Irzhavski).
