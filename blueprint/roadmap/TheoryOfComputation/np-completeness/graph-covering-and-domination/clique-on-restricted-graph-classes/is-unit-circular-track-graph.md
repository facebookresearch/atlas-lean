# Unit circular track graphs

A simple graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ is a unit $d$-circular track graph when, for each track $j < d$, there is a circle of positive circumference $c_j$ (the reals modulo $c_j$) and each vertex $v$ is assigned on it the closed unit arc from $l_{v,j}$ to $l_{v,j} + 1$, such that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their arcs on some track meet.
