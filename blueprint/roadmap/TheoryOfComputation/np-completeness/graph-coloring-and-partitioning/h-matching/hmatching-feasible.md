# Feasible H-MATCHING instances

For a fixed pattern graph $H$ (on the vertices $0, \dots, m-1$, with the pattern's edge list), an H-MATCHING instance with graph $G$ and bound $K$ is feasible when $G$ contains $K$ copies of $H$ (injective homomorphisms from $H$ into $G$) whose vertex images are pairwise disjoint, that is, $K$ pairwise vertex-disjoint subgraphs of $G$ isomorphic to $H$.
