# GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM onto a triangle is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM onto a triangle belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT52] GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM, p. 203, comment: "Remains NP-complete for H fixed to be a triangle" (no separate attribution; the entry cites L. A. Levin, "Universal sequential search problems", Problemy Peredachi Informatsii 9(3) (1973), 115-116, for the problem with $H$ part of the instance).
