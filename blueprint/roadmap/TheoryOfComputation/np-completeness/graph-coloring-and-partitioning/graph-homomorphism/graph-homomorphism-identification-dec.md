# GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices) as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
