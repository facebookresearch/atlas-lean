# GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices) is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT52] GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM.
- L. A. Levin, "Universal sequential search problems", Problemy Peredachi Informatsii 9(3) (1973), 115-116; English translation in Problems of Information Transmission 9 (1973), 265-266.
