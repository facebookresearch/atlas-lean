# Feasible instances of GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices)

An instance of GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices) is feasible when $H$ is obtained from $G$ by identifying nonadjacent vertices: some surjective map $f : V_1 \to V_2$ has fibers that are independent sets of $G$, and the quotient of $G$ by its fibers is $H$.
