# GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM onto a triangle as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM onto a triangle accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance whose graph $G$, paired with the triangle $H$ on $\{0, 1, 2\}$, is a well-formed and feasible instance of GRAPH HOMOMORPHISM (identification of nonadjacent vertices). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
