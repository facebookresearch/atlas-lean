# CHROMATIC NUMBER OF H-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

Let $H$ be a simple graph on the vertices $0, \dots, h-1$ that is not an induced subgraph of the path $P_4$ on four vertices, nor of the disjoint union $P_1 + P_3$ of a single vertex and a path on three vertices. Then the decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $H$-FREE GRAPHS for this $H$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Here a graph is an induced subgraph of another when some injective map between their vertex sets preserves both adjacency and non-adjacency.
