# Well-formed instances of GRAPH COLORING ON (K4, claw, diamond)-FREE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for GRAPH COLORING ON $(K_4, \text{claw}, \text{diamond})$-FREE GRAPHS when it is a well-formed GRAPH COLORING instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $K > 0$, the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has no four pairwise adjacent vertices, and neither the claw $K_{1,3}$ (the complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes $1$ and $3$) nor the diamond is an induced subgraph of it. Here a graph $H$ is an induced subgraph of $G$ when some injective map from the vertices of $H$ to those of $G$ preserves both adjacency and non-adjacency.
