# 3-COLORABILITY OF (K4, claw, diamond)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF $(K_4, \text{claw}, \text{diamond})$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
