# 3-COLORABILITY OF (K1,5, K3)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF $(K_{1,5}, K_3)$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY.
- F. Maffray and M. Preissmann, "On the NP-completeness of the k-colorability problem for triangle-free graphs", Discrete Mathematics 162 (1996), 313-317, doi:10.1016/S0012-365X(97)89267-9.
- ISGCI (Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions), class $(K_{1,5}, \text{triangle})$-free (gc_921), problem 3-Colourability: NP-complete [Maffray and Preissmann 1996].
