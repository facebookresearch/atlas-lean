# GRAPH COLORING ON (C4, C5)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $(C_4, C_5)$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
