# GRAPH COLORING ON (K1,5, K3)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $(K_{1,5}, K_3)$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
