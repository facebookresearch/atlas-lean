# 3-COLORABILITY OF GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9 is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
