# 5-COLORABILITY OF P6-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 5-COLORABILITY OF $P_6$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY.
- S. Huang, "Improved complexity results on k-coloring P_t-free graphs", European Journal of Combinatorics 51 (2016), 336-346, doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2015.06.005.
