# GRAPH COLORING ON (2K2, net)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $(2K_2, \text{net})$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), the problem restricted here.
- D. Schindl, "Some new hereditary classes where graph coloring remains NP-hard", Discrete Mathematics 295 (2005), 197-202, doi:10.1016/j.disc.2005.03.003.
- P. A. Golovach, M. Johnson, D. Paulusma, and J. Song, "A survey on the computational complexity of coloring graphs with forbidden subgraphs", Journal of Graph Theory 84 (2017), 331-363, doi:10.1002/jgt.22028, arXiv:1407.1482, Theorem 17 (Schindl's theorem).
