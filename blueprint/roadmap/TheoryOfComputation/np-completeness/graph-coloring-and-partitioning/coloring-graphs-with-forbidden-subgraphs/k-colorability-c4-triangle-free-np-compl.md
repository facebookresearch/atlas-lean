# GRAPH COLORING ON (C4, K3)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $(C_4, K_3)$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), the problem restricted here.
- D. Král', J. Kratochvíl, Zs. Tuza and G. J. Woeginger, "Complexity of coloring graphs without forbidden induced subgraphs", Graph-Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science (WG 2001), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2204 (2001), 254-262, doi:10.1007/3-540-45477-2_23.
