# Well-formed instances of GRAPH COLORING ON (C4, K3)-FREE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for GRAPH COLORING ON $(C_4, K_3)$-FREE GRAPHS when it is a well-formed GRAPH COLORING instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $K > 0$, the cycle $C_4$ on 4 vertices is not an induced subgraph of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$), and that graph has no triangle (no three pairwise adjacent vertices). Here a graph $H$ is an induced subgraph of $G$ when some injective map from the vertices of $H$ to those of $G$ preserves both adjacency and non-adjacency.
