# Well-formed instances of 3-COLORABILITY OF LINEAR DOMINO GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for 3-COLORABILITY OF LINEAR DOMINO GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 when it is a well-formed GRAPH COLORING instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $K = 3$, neither the claw $K_{1,3}$ (a center joined to three pairwise non-adjacent leaves) nor the diamond $K_4 - e$ (four vertices, all pairs adjacent except one) is an induced subgraph of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$), and every vertex of that graph has at most $4$ neighbors. Here a graph $H$ is an induced subgraph of $G$ when some injective map from the vertices of $H$ to those of $G$ preserves both adjacency and non-adjacency.
