# MAXIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs as a decision problem

The decision problem MAXIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(P, E, K)$ (a list of points with rational coordinates, an edge list and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for MAX CUT on unit disk graphs (distinct points with positive denominators, a simple edge list on $\{0, \dots, |P| - 1\}$ whose graph is exactly the unit disk graph of the points, and $K > 0$) and feasible for MAXIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
