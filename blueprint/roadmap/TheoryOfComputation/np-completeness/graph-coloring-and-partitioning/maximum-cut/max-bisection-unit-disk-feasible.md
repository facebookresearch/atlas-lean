# Feasible instances of MAXIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs

An instance $(P, E, K)$ of MAX CUT on unit disk graphs is feasible for MAXIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs when some set $S$ of $\lfloor |P| / 2 \rfloor$ vertices, all numbered below $|P|$, and some sublist of at least $K$ entries of $E$ are such that every chosen entry has exactly one endpoint in $S$. For a simple edge list this says that the vertices split into parts of sizes $\lfloor |P| / 2 \rfloor$ and $\lceil |P| / 2 \rceil$ with at least $K$ edges between them.
