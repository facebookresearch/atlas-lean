# Feasible instances of CUT COVER

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for CUT COVER when, for some $m \le K$, there are vertex sets $V_0, \dots, V_{m-1}$ such that every edge of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has exactly one endpoint in some $V_j$.
