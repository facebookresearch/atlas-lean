# CUT COVER is NP-complete

The decision problem CUT COVER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Motwani and J. Naor, "On exact and approximate cut covers of graphs", Technical Note STAN-CS-TN-94-11, Department of Computer Science, Stanford University (1994), Theorem 2.1.
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), MINIMUM CUT COVER (citing Motwani and Naor).
- R. Loulou, "Minimal cut cover of a graph with an application to the testing of electronic boards", Operations Research Letters 12 (1992), 301-305, doi:10.1016/0167-6377(92)90089-L (introduces the problem).
