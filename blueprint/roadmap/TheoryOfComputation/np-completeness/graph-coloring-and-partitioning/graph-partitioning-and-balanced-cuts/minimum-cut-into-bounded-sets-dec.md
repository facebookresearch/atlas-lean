# MINIMUM CUT INTO BOUNDED SETS as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT INTO BOUNDED SETS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, E, s, t, B, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM CUT INTO BOUNDED SETS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
