# Instances of ACYCLIC PARTITION

An instance of ACYCLIC PARTITION is a quintuple $(n, w, A, B, K)$: a natural number $n$, the number of vertices $0, \dots, n-1$; a list $w$ of natural numbers, whose $v$-th entry is the weight $w(v)$ of vertex $v$; a list $A$ of triples $(u, v, c)$ of natural numbers, each an arc from $u$ to $v$ with cost $c$; a bound $B$ on the total weight of a block; and a bound $K$ on the total cost of the arcs between blocks.
