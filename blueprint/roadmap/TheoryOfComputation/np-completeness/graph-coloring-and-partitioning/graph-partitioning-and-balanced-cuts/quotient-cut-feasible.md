# Feasible instances of QUOTIENT CUT

An instance $(n, w, E, K)$ is feasible for QUOTIENT CUT when some set $C \subseteq V = \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ has $m = \min(w(C), w(V \setminus C)) > 0$ and the total cost of the edges with exactly one end in $C$ is at most $K \cdot m$, that is, the quotient of the cut cost by $m$ is at most the natural number $K$.
