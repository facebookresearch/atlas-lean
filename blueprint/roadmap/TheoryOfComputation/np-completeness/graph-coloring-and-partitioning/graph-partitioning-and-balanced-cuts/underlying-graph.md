# Underlying undirected graph of an ACYCLIC PARTITION instance

The underlying undirected graph of an instance $(n, w, A, B, K)$ of ACYCLIC PARTITION is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when some entry of $A$ is an arc from $u$ to $v$ or from $v$ to $u$.
