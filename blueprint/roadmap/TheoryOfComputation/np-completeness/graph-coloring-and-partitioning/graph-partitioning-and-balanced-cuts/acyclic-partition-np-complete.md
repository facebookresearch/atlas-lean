# ACYCLIC PARTITION is NP-complete

The decision problem ACYCLIC PARTITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND15] ACYCLIC PARTITION (credited there to Garey and Johnson, unpublished; transformation from X3C).
