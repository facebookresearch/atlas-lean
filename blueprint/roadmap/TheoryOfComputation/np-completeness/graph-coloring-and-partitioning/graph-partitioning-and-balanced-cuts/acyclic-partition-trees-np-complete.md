# ACYCLIC PARTITION FOR TREES is NP-complete

The decision problem ACYCLIC PARTITION FOR TREES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Weights and costs are encoded in binary.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND15] ACYCLIC PARTITION, comment.
- J. A. Lukes, "Efficient algorithm for the partitioning of trees", IBM Journal of Research and Development 18 (1974), 217-224, doi:10.1147/rd.183.0217 (the pseudo-polynomial algorithm cited in that comment).
