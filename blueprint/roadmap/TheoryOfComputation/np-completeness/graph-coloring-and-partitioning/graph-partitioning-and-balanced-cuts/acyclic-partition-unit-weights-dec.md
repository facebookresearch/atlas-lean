# ACYCLIC PARTITION WITH UNIT WEIGHTS AND COSTS as a decision problem

The decision problem ACYCLIC PARTITION WITH UNIT WEIGHTS AND COSTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, w, A, B, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed for ACYCLIC PARTITION WITH UNIT WEIGHTS AND COSTS and feasible for ACYCLIC PARTITION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
