# QUOTIENT CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem QUOTIENT CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, MINIMUM QUOTIENT CUT.
- J. K. Park and C. A. Phillips, "Finding minimum-quotient cuts in planar graphs", Proceedings of the 25th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1993), 766-775, doi:10.1145/167088.167284.
