# Well-formed instances of b-BALANCED CUT

An instance $(n, w, E, (p, q), K)$ of b-BALANCED CUT is well-formed when the weight list $w$ has exactly $n$ entries, the edge list $E$ of triples $(u, v, c)$ has no repeated entries, every edge satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, no two different edges join the same unordered pair of vertices, the balance $b = p/q$ satisfies $0 < p$ and $2p \le q$ (so $0 < b \le 1/2$), and $K > 0$. Vertex weights and edge costs are natural numbers and may be $0$.
