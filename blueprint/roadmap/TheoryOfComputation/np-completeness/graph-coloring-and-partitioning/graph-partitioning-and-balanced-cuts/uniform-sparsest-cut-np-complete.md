# UNIFORM SPARSEST CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIFORM SPARSEST CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. W. Matula and F. Shahrokhi, "Sparsest cuts and bottlenecks in graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 27 (1990), 113-123, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(90)90133-W.
- A. Gupta, K. Talwar and D. Witmer, "Sparsest cut on bounded treewidth graphs: algorithms and hardness results", STOC 2013, doi:10.1145/2488608.2488644 (Section 1, recording the result).
