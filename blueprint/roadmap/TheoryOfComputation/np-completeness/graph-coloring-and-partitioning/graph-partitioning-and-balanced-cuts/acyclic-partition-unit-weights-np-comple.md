# ACYCLIC PARTITION WITH UNIT WEIGHTS AND COSTS is NP-complete

The decision problem ACYCLIC PARTITION WITH UNIT WEIGHTS AND COSTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND15] ACYCLIC PARTITION, p. 209, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if all v ∈ V have w(v) = 1 and all a ∈ A have c(a) = 1" (the entry's reference is [Garey and Johnson, ——], unpublished; transformation from X3C).
