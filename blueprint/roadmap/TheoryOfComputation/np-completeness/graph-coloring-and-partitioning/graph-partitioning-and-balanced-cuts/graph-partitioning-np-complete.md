# GRAPH PARTITIONING is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH PARTITIONING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND14] GRAPH PARTITIONING.
- L. Hyafil and R. L. Rivest, "Graph partitioning and constructing optimal decision trees are polynomial complete problems", Rapport de Recherche 33, IRIA-Laboria, Rocquencourt (1973).
