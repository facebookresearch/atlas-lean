# RATIO CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem RATIO CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, MINIMUM RATIO-CUT.
