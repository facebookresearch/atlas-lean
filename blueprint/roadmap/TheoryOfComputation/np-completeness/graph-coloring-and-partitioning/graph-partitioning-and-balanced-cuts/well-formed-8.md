# Well-formed instances of QUOTIENT CUT

An instance $(n, w, E, K)$ of QUOTIENT CUT is well-formed when the weight list $w$ has exactly $n$ entries, the edge list $E$ of triples $(u, v, c)$ has no repeated entries, every edge satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, no two different edges join the same unordered pair of vertices, and $K > 0$.
