# Well-formed instances of ACYCLIC PARTITION

An instance $(n, w, A, B, K)$ of ACYCLIC PARTITION is well-formed when $w$ has exactly $n$ entries, all positive; no pair $(u, v)$ occurs as the endpoints of two entries of $A$; every entry $(u, v, c)$ of $A$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $c > 0$; and $B > 0$ and $K > 0$. Arcs in both directions between the same two vertices are allowed.
