# Arcs of the quotient digraph of a block labelling

Let $(n, w, A, B, K)$ be an instance of ACYCLIC PARTITION and let $p$ assign a natural number (a block label) to every natural number. The quotient digraph of $p$ has an arc from label $b_1$ to label $b_2$ when $b_1 \ne b_2$ and some entry $(u, v, c)$ of $A$ has $p(u) = b_1$ and $p(v) = b_2$. Arcs inside one block give no arc.
