# Feasible instances of VERTEX RANKING

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for VERTEX RANKING when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a vertex ranking with the colors $0, \dots, K-1$: a map $c$ to those colors such that every path between two distinct vertices of the same color contains a vertex of larger color.
