# HIERARCHICAL CLUSTERING with Dasgupta's cost is NP-complete

The HIERARCHICAL CLUSTERING with Dasgupta's cost decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Dasgupta, "A cost function for similarity-based hierarchical clustering", Proceedings of the 48th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 2016), 118-127, doi:10.1145/2897518.2897527 (arXiv:1510.05043), Section 2 (the cost function) and Section 4 (NP-hardness of finding a tree of minimum cost).
