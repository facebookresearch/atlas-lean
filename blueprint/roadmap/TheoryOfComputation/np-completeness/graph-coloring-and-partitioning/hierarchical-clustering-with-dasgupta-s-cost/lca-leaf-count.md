# Leaves below the lowest common ancestor

For a rooted binary tree $T$ with labeled leaves and leaves $i$, $j$, the count $|\mathrm{leaves}(T[i \vee j])|$ is the number of leaves of the smallest subtree of $T$ containing both $i$ and $j$ among its leaves; it is the number of leaves of $T$ when no proper subtree contains both, and $1$ for a single leaf.
