# GRAPH k-COLORABILITY is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 4$, the decision problem GRAPH $k$-COLORABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), comment "remains NP-complete for all fixed K ≥ 3".
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and L. Stockmeyer, "Some simplified NP-complete graph problems", Theoretical Computer Science 1 (1976), 237-267, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1.
