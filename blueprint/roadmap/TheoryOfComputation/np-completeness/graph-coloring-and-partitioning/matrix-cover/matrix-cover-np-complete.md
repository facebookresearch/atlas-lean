# MATRIX COVER is NP-complete

The decision problem MATRIX COVER is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem
in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), p. 282, [MS13], which prints `≥ K`; the `≤ K` question is the
one its cited MAX CUT transformation proves NP-hard, as in McCulloch's discussion of MS13.
