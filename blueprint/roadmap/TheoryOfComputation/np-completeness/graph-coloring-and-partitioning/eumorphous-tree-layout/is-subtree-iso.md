# Is Subtree Iso

`φ` maps the subtree rooted at `u` isomorphically onto the subtree rooted at `v`, as a binary
tree: `φ u = v`, and for every descendant `w` of `u` and each side, the child of `w` on that
side is sent to the child of `φ w` on the same side, and `φ w` has a child on that side only if
`w` has one.
