# Well-formed DIRECTED BANDWIDTH instances for rooted trees

An instance $(n, A, K)$ is well-formed for rooted directed trees when it is a well-formed DIRECTED BANDWIDTH instance (distinct, loopless arcs between vertices below $n$, no directed cycle, $0 < K \le n$), its underlying undirected graph is a tree, and every vertex has at most one in-neighbor and at most two out-neighbors.
