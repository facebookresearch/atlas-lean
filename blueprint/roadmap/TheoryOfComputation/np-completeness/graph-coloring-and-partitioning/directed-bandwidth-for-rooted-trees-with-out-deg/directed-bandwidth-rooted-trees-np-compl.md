# DIRECTED BANDWIDTH is NP-complete for rooted directed trees with maximum in-degree 1 and maximum out-degree at most 2

The DIRECTED BANDWIDTH for rooted directed trees with out-degree at most 2 decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT41] DIRECTED BANDWIDTH, p. 200 (comment: "Remains NP-complete for rooted directed trees with maximum in-degree 1 and maximum out-degree at most 2.").
