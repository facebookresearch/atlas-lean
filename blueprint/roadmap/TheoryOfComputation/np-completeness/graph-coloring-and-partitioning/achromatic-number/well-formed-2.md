# Well-formed ACHROMATIC NUMBER instances on complements of bipartite graphs

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for ACHROMATIC NUMBER on complements of bipartite graphs when it is a well-formed instance $(n, E, K)$ of ACHROMATIC NUMBER (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), the complement of the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is bipartite, and $0 < K \le n$.
