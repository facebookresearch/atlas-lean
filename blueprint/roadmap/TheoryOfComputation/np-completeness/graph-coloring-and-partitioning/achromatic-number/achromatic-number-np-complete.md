# ACHROMATIC NUMBER is NP-complete

The ACHROMATIC NUMBER decision problem belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), ACHROMATIC NUMBER; Martin Farber, Geňa Hahn, Pavol Hell and Donald J. Miller, "Concerning the achromatic number of graphs", Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 40 (1986), 21-39.
