# ACHROMATIC NUMBER OF TREES is NP-complete

The ACHROMATIC NUMBER OF TREES decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT5] ACHROMATIC NUMBER.
