# ACHROMATIC NUMBER OF INTERVAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The ACHROMATIC NUMBER OF INTERVAL GRAPHS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT5] ACHROMATIC NUMBER.
- H. L. Bodlaender, "Achromatic number is NP-complete for cographs and interval graphs", *Information Processing Letters* 31 (1989), 135-138, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(89)90221-4.
