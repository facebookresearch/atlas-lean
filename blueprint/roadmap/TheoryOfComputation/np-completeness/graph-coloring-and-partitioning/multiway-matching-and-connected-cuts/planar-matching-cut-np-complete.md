# MATCHING CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MATCHING CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Bonsma, "The complexity of the matching-cut problem for planar graphs and other graph classes", Journal of Graph Theory 62 (2009), 109-126, doi:10.1002/jgt.20390.
