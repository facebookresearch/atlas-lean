# Feasible instances of MATCHING CUT

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for MATCHING CUT when the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ has a decomposition: a two-coloring of its vertices that uses both colors and gives every vertex at most one neighbor of the other color, so the edges between the color classes form a matching.
