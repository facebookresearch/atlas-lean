# EDGE DELETION k-PARTITION as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem EDGE DELETION k-PARTITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for EDGE DELETION k-PARTITION with $k$ colors. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
