# Well-formed instances of MINIMUM k-CUT

An instance $(n, E, k, B)$ of MINIMUM k-CUT is well-formed when the endpoint pairs $(u, v)$ of the weighted edges $(u, v, w)$ in $E$ have no repeats, satisfy $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, and no edge's reversed pair $(v, u)$ also occurs; and $2 \le k \le n$. Edge weights are natural numbers and may be $0$.
