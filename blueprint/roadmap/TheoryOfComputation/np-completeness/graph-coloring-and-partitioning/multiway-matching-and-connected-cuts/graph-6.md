# Graph of a VERTEX K-CUT instance

For an instance $(n, E, P, w, K)$ of VERTEX K-CUT, its graph is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct vertices $i$ and $j$ are adjacent exactly when $(i, j)$ or $(j, i)$ belongs to the edge list $E$. Loops are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$ contribute no edge.
