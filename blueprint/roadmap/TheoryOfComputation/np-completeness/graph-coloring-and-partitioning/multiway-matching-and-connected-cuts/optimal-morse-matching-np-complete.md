# OPTIMAL MORSE MATCHING is NP-complete

OPTIMAL MORSE MATCHING is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Joswig and Pfetsch (2006), Section 4; Eğecioğlu and Gonzalez (1996).
