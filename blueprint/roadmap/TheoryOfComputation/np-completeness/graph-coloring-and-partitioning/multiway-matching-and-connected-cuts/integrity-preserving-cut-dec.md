# INTEGRITY PRESERVING CUT as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGRITY PRESERVING CUT accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, U_1, U_2)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for INTEGRITY PRESERVING CUT. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
