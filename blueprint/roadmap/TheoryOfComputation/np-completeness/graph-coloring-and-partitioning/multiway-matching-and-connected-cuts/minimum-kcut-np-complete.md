# MINIMUM k-CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM k-CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- O. Goldschmidt and D. S. Hochbaum, "A polynomial algorithm for the k-cut problem for fixed k", Mathematics of Operations Research 19 (1994), 24-37, doi:10.1287/moor.19.1.24.
