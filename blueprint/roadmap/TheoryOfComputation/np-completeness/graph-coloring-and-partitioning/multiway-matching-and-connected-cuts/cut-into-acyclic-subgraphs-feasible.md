# Feasible instances of CUT INTO ACYCLIC SUBGRAPHS

An instance $(n, A)$ is feasible for CUT INTO ACYCLIC SUBGRAPHS when the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ can be colored with two colors so that no closed directed walk of positive length, along arcs $u \to v$ with $(u, v)$ listed in $A$, has all its vertices of one color; equivalently, both color classes induce acyclic subdigraphs. A pair of opposite arcs $u \to v$, $v \to u$ counts as a directed cycle.
