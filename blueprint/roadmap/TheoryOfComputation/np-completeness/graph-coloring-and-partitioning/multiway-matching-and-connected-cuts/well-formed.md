# Well-formed instances of BALANCED CONNECTED PARTITION

An instance $(n, E, w, K)$ is well-formed for BALANCED CONNECTED PARTITION when the list $E$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is connected, the weight list $w$ has exactly $n$ entries, and $K > 0$.
