# MULTIWAY CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIWAY CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Dahlhaus, D. S. Johnson, C. H. Papadimitriou, P. D. Seymour and M. Yannakakis, "The complexity of multiterminal cuts", SIAM Journal on Computing 23(4) (1994), 864-894, doi:10.1137/S0097539792225297 (the planar problem is NP-hard when the number of terminals is not fixed).
- M. Johnson, B. Martin, S. Pandey, D. Paulusma, S. Smith and E. J. van Leeuwen, "Edge multiway cut and node multiway cut are hard for planar subcubic graphs", 19th Scandinavian Symposium on Algorithm Theory (SWAT 2024), LIPIcs 294 (2024), 29:1-29:17, doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.SWAT.2024.29 (Theorem 1.1: unweighted EDGE MULTIWAY CUT is NP-complete for planar subcubic graphs).
