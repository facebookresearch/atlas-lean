# VERTEX K-CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem VERTEX K-CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MINIMUM VERTEX K-CUT, citing N. Garg, V. V. Vazirani and M. Yannakakis, "Multiway cuts in directed and node weighted graphs", Proc. 21st ICALP, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 820 (1994), 487-498, doi:10.1007/3-540-58201-0_92; journal version: "Multiway cuts in node weighted graphs", Journal of Algorithms 50 (2004), 49-61, doi:10.1016/S0196-6774(03)00111-1.
- M. Johnson, B. Martin, S. Pandey, D. Paulusma, S. Smith and E. J. van Leeuwen, "Edge Multiway Cut and Node Multiway Cut are hard for planar subcubic graphs", Algorithmica 88 (2026), article 66, doi:10.1007/s00453-026-01412-5 (NODE MULTIWAY CUT, the special case in which the pairs are all pairs of a terminal set `T`, every weight is `1` and `C ⊆ V \ T`, is NP-complete, even for planar graphs of maximum degree 3; NP-completeness for `|T| = 3` is credited there to Garg, Vazirani and Yannakakis).
