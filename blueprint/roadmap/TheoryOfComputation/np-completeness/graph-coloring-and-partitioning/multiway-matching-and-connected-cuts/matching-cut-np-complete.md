# MATCHING CUT is NP-complete

The decision problem MATCHING CUT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. Chvátal, "Recognizing decomposable graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 8(1) (1984), 51-53, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190080106 (recognizing decomposable graphs is NP-complete, already for graphs of maximum degree 4).
