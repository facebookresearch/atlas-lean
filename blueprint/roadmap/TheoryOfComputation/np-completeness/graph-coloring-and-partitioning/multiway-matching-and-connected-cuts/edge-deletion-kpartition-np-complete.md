# EDGE DELETION k-PARTITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 2$, the decision problem EDGE DELETION k-PARTITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MINIMUM EDGE DELETION K-PARTITION.
- V. Kann, S. Khanna, J. Lagergren and A. Panconesi, "On the hardness of approximating Max k-Cut and its dual", Chicago J. Theoret. Comput. Sci. 1997, Article 2 (the Min k-Partition problem).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND16] MAX CUT (the case `k = 2`, by complementing the bound).
