# MULTIWAY CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTIWAY CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of MULTIWAY CUT instances, to an instance that is well-formed for MULTIWAY CUT FOR PLANAR GRAPHS and feasible for MULTIWAY CUT. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
