# TOPOLOGICAL BANDWIDTH is NP-complete

The decision problem TOPOLOGICAL BANDWIDTH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- F. S. Makedon, C. H. Papadimitriou and I. H. Sudborough, "Topological bandwidth", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 6 (1985), 418-444, doi:10.1137/0606044 (conference version: CAAP '83, Lecture Notes in Computer Science (1983), 317-331, doi:10.1007/3-540-12727-5_19).
