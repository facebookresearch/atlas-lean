# Feasible instances of 2-DIMENSIONAL BANDWIDTH

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for 2-DIMENSIONAL BANDWIDTH when some injective map $f$ from the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ places the ends of every edge of the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ at $\ell^1$ distance at most $K$: $|f(u)_1 - f(v)_1| + |f(u)_2 - f(v)_2| \le K$.
