# 2-DIMENSIONAL BANDWIDTH is NP-complete

The decision problem 2-DIMENSIONAL BANDWIDTH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Z. Miller and J. B. Orlin, "NP-completeness for minimizing maximum edge length in grid embeddings", Journal of Algorithms 6(1) (1985), 10-16, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(85)90016-1 (problem EDGE LENGTH).
- S. N. Bhatt and S. S. Cosmadakis, "The complexity of minimizing wire lengths in VLSI layouts", Information Processing Letters 25 (1987), 263-267, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(87)90173-6 (the case `K = 1`, `G` a tree).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT40] BANDWIDTH (the one-dimensional problem).
