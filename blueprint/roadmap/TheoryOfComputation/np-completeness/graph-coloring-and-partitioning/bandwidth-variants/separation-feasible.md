# Feasible instances of SEPARATION

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for SEPARATION when there is an ordering of the numbers $0, \dots, n-1$ such that $|p(u) - p(v)| > K$ for every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$, where $p(m)$ is the position of $m$ in the ordering, counting from $0$, and $p(m) = n$ when $m$ is not among $0, \dots, n-1$. Thus with $n = 1$ and $K = 0$, the list $E = [(0, 1)]$ is feasible: $p(0) = 0$ and $p(1) = 1$.
