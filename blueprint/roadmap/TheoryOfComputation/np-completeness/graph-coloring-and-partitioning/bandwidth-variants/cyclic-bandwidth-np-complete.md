# CYCLIC BANDWIDTH is NP-complete

The decision problem CYCLIC BANDWIDTH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Y.-T. Leung, O. Vornberger and J. D. Witthoff, "On some variants of the bandwidth minimization problem", SIAM Journal on Computing 13 (1984), 650-667, doi:10.1137/0213040 (abstract: "the cycle-bandwidth problem which for a given graph G and positive integer k, asks if there is a circular layout such that every pair of adjacent vertexes have a distance at most k ... We show that the cycle-bandwidth problem is NP-complete for each fixed k ≥ 2").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT40] BANDWIDTH (the linear version).
