# The consecutive ones property

A $0$-$1$ matrix with $m$ rows and $n$ columns has the consecutive ones property when some permutation of its columns makes the $1$'s of every row consecutive: whenever positions $j_1 \le j_2 \le j_3$ of a row of the permuted matrix hold $1$ at $j_1$ and $j_3$, they hold $1$ at $j_2$.
