# CONSECUTIVE ONES MATRIX AUGMENTATION is NP-complete

The CONSECUTIVE ONES MATRIX AUGMENTATION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR16] CONSECUTIVE ONES MATRIX AUGMENTATION, p. 229 (references: [Booth, 1975], [Papadimitriou, 1976a]; transformation from OPTIMAL LINEAR ARRANGEMENT).
- K. S. Booth, *PQ-Tree Algorithms*, Ph.D. thesis, University of California, Berkeley (1975).
- W. Lipski, Jr., "Generalizations of the consecutive ones property and related NP-complete problems", Coordinated Science Laboratory Report T-67, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (1978), hdl:2142/74046 (records the result as Booth's, Problem 3).
