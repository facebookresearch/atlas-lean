# DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN THE PLANE is NP-complete

The decision problem DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN THE PLANE belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
