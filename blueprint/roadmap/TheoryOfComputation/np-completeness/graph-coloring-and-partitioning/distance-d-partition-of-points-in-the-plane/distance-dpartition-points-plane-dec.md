# DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN THE PLANE as a decision problem

The decision problem DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN THE PLANE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly
when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN
THE PLANE instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](distance-dpartition-points-plane-feasibl.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
