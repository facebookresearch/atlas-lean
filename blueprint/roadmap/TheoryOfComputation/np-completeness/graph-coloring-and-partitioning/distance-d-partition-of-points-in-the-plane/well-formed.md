# Well-formed point-list instances with two positive parameters

An instance consisting of a list of points (each coordinate an integer $p - q$ given as the pair $(p, q)$) and two natural numbers is well-formed when the decoded points are pairwise distinct and both numbers are positive. This test is shared by DISTANCE-d PARTITION OF POINTS IN THE PLANE (the numbers are $d$ and $K$) and GEOMETRIC CONNECTED DOMINATING SET (the numbers are $B$ and $K$).
