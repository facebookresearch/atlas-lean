# DOMATIC NUMBER is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMATIC NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT3] DOMATIC NUMBER (transformation from 3SAT, credited there to unpublished work of Garey, Johnson, and Tarjan).
- H. Kaplan and R. Shamir, "The domatic number problem on some perfect graph families", Information Processing Letters 49 (1994), 51-56, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(94)90054-X (a published proof, already for split graphs and for bipartite graphs).
