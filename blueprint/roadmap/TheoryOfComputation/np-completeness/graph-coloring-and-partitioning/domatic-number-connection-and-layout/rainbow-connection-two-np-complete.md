# RAINBOW CONNECTION NUMBER AT MOST 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem RAINBOW CONNECTION NUMBER AT MOST 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Chakraborty, E. Fischer, A. Matsliah, and R. Yuster, "Hardness and algorithms for rainbow connection", Journal of Combinatorial Optimization 21(3) (2011), 330-347, doi:10.1007/s10878-009-9250-9.
