# RAINBOW CONNECTION NUMBER AT MOST 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem RAINBOW CONNECTION NUMBER AT MOST 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ of a vertex count and an edge list, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](rainbow-connection-two-feasible.md) for RAINBOW CONNECTION NUMBER AT MOST 2. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
