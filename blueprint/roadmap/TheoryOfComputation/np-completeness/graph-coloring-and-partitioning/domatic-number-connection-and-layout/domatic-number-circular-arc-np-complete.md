# DOMATIC NUMBER FOR CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMATIC NUMBER FOR CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT3] DOMATIC NUMBER.
- M. A. Bonuccelli, "Dominating sets and domatic number of circular arc graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 12 (1985), 203-213, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(85)90025-3.
- A. Sprague, "NP-completeness of the domatic number problem on circular arc graphs", Proceedings of the 37th Annual ACM Southeast Regional Conference (1999), doi:10.1145/306363.306416.
