# PATHWIDTH ON CHORDAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON CHORDAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Gustedt, "On the pathwidth of chordal graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 45(3) (1993), 233-248, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(93)90012-D.
