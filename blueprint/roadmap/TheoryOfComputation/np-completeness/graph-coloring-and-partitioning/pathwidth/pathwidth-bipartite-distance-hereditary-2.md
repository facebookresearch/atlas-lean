# PATHWIDTH ON BIPARTITE DISTANCE-HEREDITARY GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON BIPARTITE DISTANCE-HEREDITARY GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Kloks, H. Bodlaender, H. Müller and D. Kratsch, "Computing treewidth and minimum fill-in: all you need are the minimal separators", Proc. 1st European Symposium on Algorithms (ESA '93), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 726, Springer (1993), 260-271, doi:10.1007/3-540-57273-2_61 (the result and its attribution are also recorded by Wikipedia, "Pathwidth": determining whether the pathwidth is at most `k` "remains NP-complete when `G` is restricted to ... bipartite distance-hereditary graphs", citing this paper; R. Mihai and I. Todinca, "Pathwidth is NP-hard for weighted trees", FAW 2009, LNCS 5598, 181-195, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-02270-8_20, also credit it with NP-hardness for distance-hereditary graphs).
