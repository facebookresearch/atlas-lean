# Well-formed instances of PATHWIDTH OF CHORDAL DOMINOES

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for PATHWIDTH OF CHORDAL DOMINOES when it is well-formed for PATHWIDTH ON CHORDAL GRAPHS (its edge list $E$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, no cycle $C_k$ with $k \ge 4$ is an induced subgraph of the graph, and $K > 0$) and every vertex lies in at most two maximal cliques of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$).
