# PATHWIDTH ON COCOMPARABILITY GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON COCOMPARABILITY GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Habib and R. H. Möhring, "Treewidth of cocomparability graphs and a new order-theoretic parameter", Order 11 (1994), 47-60, doi:10.1007/BF01462229 (treewidth and pathwidth coincide on cocomparability graphs).
- S. Arnborg, D. G. Corneil, and A. Proskurowski, "Complexity of finding embeddings in a k-tree", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 8 (1987), 277-284, doi:10.1137/0608024 (NP-complete on complements of bipartite graphs).
- T. Kloks, D. Kratsch, and J. Spinrad, "Treewidth and pathwidth of cocomparability graphs of bounded dimension", Computing Science Notes 93/46, Eindhoven University of Technology (1993) (records both results).
