# PATHWIDTH OF CHORDAL DOMINOES is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH OF CHORDAL DOMINOES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- T. Kloks, D. Kratsch and H. Müller, "Dominoes", Graph-Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science (WG '94), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 903 (1995), 106-120, doi:10.1007/3-540-59071-4_41 (technical report version: Computing Science Note 94/12, Eindhoven University of Technology (1994)).
