# PATHWIDTH is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Ohtsuki, H. Mori, E. S. Kuh, T. Kashiwabara and T. Fujisawa, "One-dimensional logic gate assignment and interval graphs", IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems 26 (1979), 675-684, doi:10.1109/TCS.1979.1084695 (minimum clique number interval augmentation is NP-complete).
- T. Kashiwabara and T. Fujisawa, "NP-completeness of the problem of finding a minimum-clique-number interval graph containing a given graph as a subgraph", Proc. IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (1979), 657-660.
