# PATHWIDTH ON CIRCLE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON CIRCLE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Kloks, H. L. Bodlaender, H. Müller, and D. Kratsch, "Computing treewidth and minimum fill-in: all you need are the minimal separators", Algorithms - ESA '93, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 726 (1993), 260-271, doi:10.1007/3-540-57273-2_61 (pathwidth is NP-complete on bipartite distance-hereditary graphs).
- R. Mihai and I. Todinca, "Pathwidth is NP-hard for weighted trees", Frontiers in Algorithmics (FAW 2009), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 5598 (2009), 181-195, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-02270-8_20 (records that pathwidth remains NP-hard for distance-hereditary graphs and circle graphs, and that circle graphs contain all distance-hereditary graphs).
