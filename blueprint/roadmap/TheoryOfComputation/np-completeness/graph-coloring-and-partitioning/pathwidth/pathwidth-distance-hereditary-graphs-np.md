# PATHWIDTH ON DISTANCE-HEREDITARY GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON DISTANCE-HEREDITARY GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Kloks, H. L. Bodlaender, H. Müller, and D. Kratsch, "Computing treewidth and minimum fill-in: all you need are the minimal separators", Algorithms - ESA '93, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 726 (1993), 260-271, doi:10.1007/3-540-57273-2_61 (pathwidth is NP-complete on bipartite distance-hereditary graphs).
- I. Adler, M. M. Kanté, and O. Kwon, "Linear rank-width of distance-hereditary graphs I. A polynomial-time algorithm", Algorithmica 78 (2017), 342-377, doi:10.1007/s00453-016-0164-5, arXiv:1403.1081 (records that computing the pathwidth of distance-hereditary graphs is NP-hard).
- E. Howorka, "A characterization of distance-hereditary graphs", The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics 28 (1977), 417-420, doi:10.1093/qmath/28.4.417 (the definition).
