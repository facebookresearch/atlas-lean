# Graphs of pathwidth at most K

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of a pathwidth problem is feasible when, for some $r$, the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ has a path decomposition $X_0, \dots, X_{r-1}$ with every bag of at most $K + 1$ vertices, that is, the graph has pathwidth at most $K$.
