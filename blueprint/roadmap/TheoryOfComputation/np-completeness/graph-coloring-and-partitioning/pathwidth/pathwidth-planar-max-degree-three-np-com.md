# PATHWIDTH ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. Monien and I. H. Sudborough, "Min cut is NP-complete for edge weighted trees", Theoretical Computer Science 58 (1988), 209-229, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(88)90028-X (Vertex Separation for planar graphs of maximum vertex degree 3).
- N. G. Kinnersley, "The vertex separation number of a graph equals its path-width", Information Processing Letters 42 (1992), 345-350, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(92)90234-M.
