# Circle graphs

A simple graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ is a circle graph when it is isomorphic to the circle graph of some list of chords $(a, b)$ of natural numbers with $a < b$ and all endpoints distinct: the graph on the positions of the list in which two distinct chords $(a, b)$, $(a', b')$ are adjacent when their endpoints interleave, $a < a' < b < b'$ or $a' < a < b' < b$.
