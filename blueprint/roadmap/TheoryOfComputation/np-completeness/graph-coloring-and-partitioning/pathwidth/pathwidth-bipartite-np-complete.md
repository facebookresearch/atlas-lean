# PATHWIDTH ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PATHWIDTH ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Kloks, H. Bodlaender, H. Müller and D. Kratsch, "Computing treewidth and minimum fill-in: all you need are the minimal separators", Algorithms (ESA '93), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 726 (1993), 260-271, doi:10.1007/3-540-57273-2_61.
- Wikipedia, "Pathwidth" (section "Computing path-decompositions"), which records this result.
