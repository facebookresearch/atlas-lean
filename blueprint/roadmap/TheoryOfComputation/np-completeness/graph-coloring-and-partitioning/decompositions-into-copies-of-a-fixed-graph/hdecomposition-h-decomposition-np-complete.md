# H-DECOMPOSITION is NP-complete for every fixed H with a connected component of at least three edges

For every graph $H$ given by a simple edge list on $\{0, \dots, m - 1\}$ such that some connected component of $H$ has at least three edges, the following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether the edge set of $G$ can be partitioned into the edge sets of copies of $H$.

## Sources

- D. Dor and M. Tarsi, "Graph decomposition is NP-complete: a complete proof of Holyer's conjecture", SIAM Journal on Computing 26 (1997), 1166-1187, doi:10.1137/S0097539792229507.
