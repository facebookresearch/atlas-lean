# Feasible instances of H-DECOMPOSITION

For a fixed pattern graph $H$ given by $(m, hs)$, an instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for H-DECOMPOSITION when its graph $G$ has an $H$-decomposition: a finite family of copies of $H$ in $G$ (injective homomorphisms of $H$ into $G$) such that every edge of $G$ is the image of an edge of $H$ under exactly one copy.
