# DIGRAPH D-MORPHISM is NP-complete

The DIGRAPH D-MORPHISM decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT53] DIGRAPH D-MORPHISM.
- A. S. Fraenkel and Y. Yesha, "Complexity of problems in games, graphs and algebraic equations", Discrete Applied Mathematics 1 (1979), 15-30, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(79)90012-X.
