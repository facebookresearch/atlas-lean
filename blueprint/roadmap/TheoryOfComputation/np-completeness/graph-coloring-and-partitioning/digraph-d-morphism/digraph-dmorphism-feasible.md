# Feasible DIGRAPH D-MORPHISM instances

An instance is feasible when there is a D-morphism from $G$ to $H$: a map $f$ from the vertices of $G$ to those of $H$ sending every arc $(u, v)$ of $G$ to an arc of $H$ in one of the two directions ($(f(u), f(v))$ or $(f(v), f(u))$), such that every arc of $H$ leaving $f(u)$ ends at $f(v)$ for some out-neighbor $v$ of $u$ in $G$.
