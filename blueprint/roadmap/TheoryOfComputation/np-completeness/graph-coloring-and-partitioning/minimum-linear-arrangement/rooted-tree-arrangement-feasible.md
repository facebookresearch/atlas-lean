# Feasible instances of ROOTED TREE ARRANGEMENT

In ROOTED TREE ARRANGEMENT: a tree `T` on `Fin n` with root `r` and a one-to-one `f` such that for each edge of the graph there is a simple path from `r` in `T` containing the images of both of its ends (the path may differ from edge to edge), and the total tree distance is at most `K`.
