# Clique cover

Problem 13 of Karp's 1972 list: decide whether the vertices of a graph are covered by at most $\ell$ cliques. A partition of the vertices of $G$ into $\ell$ cliques is a proper colouring of the complement of $G$ with $\ell$ colours, which gives Karp's reduction from CHROMATIC NUMBER.
