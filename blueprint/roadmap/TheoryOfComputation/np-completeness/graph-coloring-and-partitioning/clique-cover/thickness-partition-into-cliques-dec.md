# The PARTITION INTO CLIQUES for graphs of thickness at most k decision problem

The PARTITION INTO CLIQUES for graphs of thickness at most k decision problem accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is well formed and feasible as an instance of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES. Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected. For each value of the fixed parameter `k` this is a separate decision problem.
