# The PARTITION INTO CLIQUES for graphs of thickness at most k decision problem

For a natural number $k$, the PARTITION INTO CLIQUES for graphs of thickness at most k decision problem accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is [well formed for thickness at most $k$](../coloring-restricted-graph-classes/well-formed-10.md) (that is, a well-formed PARTITION INTO CLIQUES instance whose graph has thickness at most $k$) and feasible as an instance of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES (the [clique-cover feasibility test](clique-cover-feasible.md): the vertices split into at most the given bound of cliques). Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected. A yes-instance of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES whose graph has thickness greater than $k$ is rejected. For each value of the fixed parameter $k$ this is a separate decision problem.
