# Well-formed clique-cover instances

A clique-cover instance $(n, E, \ell)$ is well formed when $E$ has no repeated entry and every listed pair $(u, v)$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, with $(v, u)$ not also listed. These are the same conditions as for [GRAPH COLORING instances](../graph-coloring/well-formed.md).
