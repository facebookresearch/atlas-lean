# ELIMINATION DEGREE SEQUENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem ELIMINATION DEGREE SEQUENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP
has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT47] ELIMINATION DEGREE SEQUENCE (attributed there to Garey, Johnson, and Papadimitriou, 1977, unpublished; transformation from EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS).
