# MAXIMUM CUT ON CLAW-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON CLAW-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. L. Bodlaender and K. Jansen, "On the complexity of the maximum cut problem", Nordic Journal of Computing 7(1) (2000), 14-31 (conference version: STACS 94, LNCS 775 (1994), 769-780, doi:10.1007/3-540-57785-8_189) (NP-complete for "graphs that are the complement of a bipartite graph", which are claw-free).
- C. M. H. de Figueiredo, A. A. de Melo, D. Sasaki and A. Silva, "Revising Johnson's table for the 21st century", Discrete Applied Mathematics 323 (2022), 184-200, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2021.05.021, Table 1 (MAX CUT on CLAW-FREE: N [20], [20] = Bodlaender and Jansen).
