# Energy in GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD

In GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD: the spin energy of the assignment `s` (`true` is spin `+1`): `- ∑_e J(e) s(u) s(v) - ∑_v h(v) s(v)`. A list entry that is not present (an index beyond the end of its list) reads as $(0, 0)$.
