# MAXIMUM CUT ON CO-BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON CO-BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. L. Bodlaender and K. Jansen, "On the complexity of the maximum cut problem", Nordic Journal of Computing 7 (2000), 14-31 (STACS 1994, LNCS 775, 769-780, doi:10.1007/3-540-57785-8_189).
- R. Adhikary, K. Bose, S. Mukherjee and B. Roy, "Complexity of maximum cut on interval graphs", Discrete & Computational Geometry 70 (2023), 307-322, doi:10.1007/s00454-022-00472-y (records "Max Cut remains NP-hard even for ... co-bipartite graphs [Bodlaender and Jansen]").
- ISGCI, class "co-bipartite" (gc_486), problem Maximum cut, NP-complete.
