# Instances of MAXIMUM CUT ON UNIT DISK GRAPHS

An instance is a triple $(P, E, K)$ of a list $P$ of disk centers, each a pair of coordinates, each coordinate a rational number coded as $((a, b), d)$ with natural numbers $a, b, d$, standing for $(a - b)/d$; a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers (the edges); and a natural number $K$ (the bound). The vertices are the positions $0, \dots, |P| - 1$ of the list $P$.
