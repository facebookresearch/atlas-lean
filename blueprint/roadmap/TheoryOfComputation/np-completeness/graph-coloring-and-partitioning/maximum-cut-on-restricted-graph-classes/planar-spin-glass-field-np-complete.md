# GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD is NP-complete

GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Barahona (1982); Johnson (1983).
