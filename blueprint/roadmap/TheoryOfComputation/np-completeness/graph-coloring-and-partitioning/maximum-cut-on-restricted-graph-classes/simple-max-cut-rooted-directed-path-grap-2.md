# MAXIMUM CUT ON ROOTED DIRECTED PATH GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON ROOTED DIRECTED PATH GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
