# WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of triples of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
