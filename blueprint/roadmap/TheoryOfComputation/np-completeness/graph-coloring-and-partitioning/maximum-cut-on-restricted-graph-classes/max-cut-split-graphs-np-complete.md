# MAXIMUM CUT ON SPLIT GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON SPLIT GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. L. Bodlaender and K. Jansen, "On the complexity of the maximum cut problem", Nordic Journal of Computing 7(1) (2000), 14-31 (conference version: STACS 94, LNCS 775 (1994), 769-780, doi:10.1007/3-540-57785-8_189).
