# MAXIMUM CUT ON CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON CUBIC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Berman and M. Karpinski, "On some tighter inapproximability results (extended abstract)", Automata, Languages and Programming (ICALP 1999), LNCS 1644 (1999), 200-209, doi:10.1007/3-540-48523-6_17 (approximating MAX CUT on 3-regular graphs within some constant factor below 1 is NP-hard).
- J. Díaz and G. B. Yannakakis, "Node- and edge-deletion NP-complete problems", STOC 1978, 253-264, doi:10.1145/800133.804355).
