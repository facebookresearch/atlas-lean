# Feasible instances of WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2 when some set $S$ of vertices, all less than $n$, and some sublist $C$ of $E$ of total weight at least $K$ have exactly one endpoint of every entry of $C$ in $S$.
