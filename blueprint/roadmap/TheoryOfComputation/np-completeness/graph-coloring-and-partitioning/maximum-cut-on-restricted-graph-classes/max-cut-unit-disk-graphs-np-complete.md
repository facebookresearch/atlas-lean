# MAXIMUM CUT ON UNIT DISK GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON UNIT DISK GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Díaz and M. Kamiński, "MAX-CUT and MAX-BISECTION are NP-hard on unit disk graphs", Theoretical Computer Science 377 (2007), 271-276, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2007.02.013 (preprint arXiv:cs/0609128).
