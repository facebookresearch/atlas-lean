# MAXIMUM CUT ON 2-SUBDIVISIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON 2-SUBDIVISIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND16] MAX CUT, comment ("Remains NP-complete if `w(e) = 1` for all `e ∈ E` (the SIMPLE MAX CUT problem)").
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and L. Stockmeyer, "Some simplified NP-complete graph problems", Theoretical Computer Science 1(3) (1976), 237-267, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1 (SIMPLE MAX CUT).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "2-subdivision" (gc_472, https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_472.html), problem Maximum cut: NP-complete, reference [1648] (a note communicated by P. Irzhavski, section "Maximum cut").
