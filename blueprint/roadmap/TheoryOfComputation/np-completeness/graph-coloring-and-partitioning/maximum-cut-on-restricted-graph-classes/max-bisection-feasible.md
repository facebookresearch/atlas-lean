# Feasible instances of MAXIMUM BISECTION ON PLANAR GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for MAXIMUM BISECTION when some set $S$ of exactly $\lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ vertices, all less than $n$, and some sublist $C$ of $E$ with at least $K$ entries have exactly one endpoint of every pair in $C$ in $S$. The other side, the vertices of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ outside $S$, then has $\lceil n/2 \rceil$ vertices.
