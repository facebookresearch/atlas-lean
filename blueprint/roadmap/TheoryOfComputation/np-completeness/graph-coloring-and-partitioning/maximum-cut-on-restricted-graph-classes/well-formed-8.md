# Well-formed instances of MAXIMUM CUT ON CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for MAXIMUM CUT ON CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPHS when it is a well-formed MAXIMUM CUT instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is a circular-arc graph (for some $L$, each vertex carries an arc $(a, b)$ with $a, b < L$, read clockwise on the points $0, \dots, L-1$ of a circle, and two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when some point lies on both arcs), and $K > 0$.
