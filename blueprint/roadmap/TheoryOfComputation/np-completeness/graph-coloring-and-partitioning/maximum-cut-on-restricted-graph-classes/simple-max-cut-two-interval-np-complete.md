# MAXIMUM CUT ON 2-INTERVAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON 2-INTERVAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Adhikary, K. Bose, S. Mukherjee and B. Roy, "Complexity of maximum cut on interval graphs", Discrete & Computational Geometry 70 (2023), 307-322, doi:10.1007/s00454-022-00472-y, Theorem 1 (MAX CUT is NP-complete on interval graphs).
- H. L. Bodlaender and K. Jansen, "On the complexity of the maximum cut problem", Nordic Journal of Computing 7 (2000), 14-31 (the reference ISGCI gives for this class).
- ISGCI, class "2-interval" (gc_40), problem Maximum cut, NP-complete.
