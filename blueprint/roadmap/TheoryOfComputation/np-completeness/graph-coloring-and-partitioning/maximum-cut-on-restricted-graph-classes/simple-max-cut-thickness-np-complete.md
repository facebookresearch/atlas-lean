# MAXIMUM CUT ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST k is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 2$, the decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST $k$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
