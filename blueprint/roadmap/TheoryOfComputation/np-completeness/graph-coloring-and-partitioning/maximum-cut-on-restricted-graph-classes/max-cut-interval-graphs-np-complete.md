# MAXIMUM CUT ON INTERVAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON INTERVAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Adhikary, K. Bose, S. Mukherjee and B. Roy, "Complexity of maximum cut on interval graphs", Discrete & Computational Geometry 70(2) (2023), 307-322, doi:10.1007/s00454-022-00472-y (SoCG 2021; preprint arXiv:2006.00061).
