# WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem WEIGHTED MAXIMUM CUT ON COGRAPHS WITH WEIGHTS 1 AND 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND16] MAX CUT, p. 210 (the problem).
- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", J. Algorithms 8 (1987), 438-448, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(87)90021-6 (records Bodlaender's result: MAX CUT is in P for cographs in the unweighted version, and NP-complete for cographs "if even just weights 1 and 2 are allowed", citing H. L. Bodlaender, "The maximum cut and minimum cut into bounded sets problems of cographs", manuscript, June 1987).
