# Feasible instances of MAXIMUM CUT ON UNIT DISK GRAPHS

An instance $(P, E, K)$ is feasible for MAXIMUM CUT ON UNIT DISK GRAPHS when $(|P|, E, K)$ is feasible for MAXIMUM CUT: some set $S$ of vertices, all less than $|P|$, and some sublist $C$ of $E$ with at least $K$ entries have exactly one endpoint of every pair in $C$ in $S$.
