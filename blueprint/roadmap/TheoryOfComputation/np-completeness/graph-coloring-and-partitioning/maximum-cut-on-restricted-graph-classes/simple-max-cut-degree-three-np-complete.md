# MAXIMUM CUT ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM CUT ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND16] MAX CUT, comment ("Remains NP-complete if `w(e) = 1` for all `e ∈ E` (the SIMPLE MAX CUT problem) [...], and if, in addition, no vertex has degree exceeding 3").
- M. Yannakakis, "Node- and edge-deletion NP-complete problems", Proc. 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1978), 253-264, doi:10.1145/800133.804355.
