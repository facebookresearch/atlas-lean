# MAXIMUM BISECTION ON PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM BISECTION ON PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- K. Jansen, M. Karpinski, A. Lingas, and E. Seidel, "Polynomial time approximation schemes for MAX-BISECTION on planar and geometric graphs", SIAM Journal on Computing 35 (2005), 110-119, doi:10.1137/S009753970139567X (NP-hardness of exact MAX-BISECTION on planar graphs, credited there to M. Jerrum, personal communication).
- J. Díaz and M. Kamiński, "MAX-CUT and MAX-BISECTION are NP-hard on unit disk graphs", Theoretical Computer Science 377 (2007), 271-276, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2007.02.013 (records the result: "max-bisection remains NP-hard on planar graphs (result of Jerrum presented in [JKLS])").
- ISGCI, class "planar" (gc_43), problem Maximum bisection, NP-complete.
