# Graph of an instance of GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD

The graph of an instance of GROUND STATE OF A PLANAR SPIN GLASS IN A MAGNETIC FIELD is the [graph of its edge list](../graph-3-coloring/part-01/edge-graph.md) formed by the endpoint pairs of the listed weighted edges, on the vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$: distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is a listed edge.
