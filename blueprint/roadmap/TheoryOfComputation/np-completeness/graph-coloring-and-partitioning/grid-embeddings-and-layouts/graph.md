# Graph of an instance of RECTILINEAR PLANARITY TESTING

The graph of an instance of RECTILINEAR PLANARITY TESTING is the [graph of its edge list](../graph-3-coloring/part-01/edge-graph.md) on its vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$: distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is a listed edge.
