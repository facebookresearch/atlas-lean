# PLANAR 4-REGULAR GRAPH 3-COLORABILITY is NP-complete

PLANAR 4-REGULAR GRAPH 3-COLORABILITY is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Dailey (1980).
