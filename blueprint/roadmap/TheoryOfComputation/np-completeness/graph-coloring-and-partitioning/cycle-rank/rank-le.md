# Bounded cycle rank

The subdigraph induced by a vertex set $S$ has cycle rank at most $0$ when every strongly connected component inside $S$ is trivial, and cycle rank at most $k + 1$ when every nontrivial strongly connected component $C$ inside $S$ has a vertex $x$ such that the subdigraph induced by $C \setminus \{x\}$ has cycle rank at most $k$.
