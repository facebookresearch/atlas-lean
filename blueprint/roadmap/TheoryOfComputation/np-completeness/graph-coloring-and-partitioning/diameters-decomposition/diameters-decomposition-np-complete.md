# DIAMETERS DECOMPOSITION is NP-complete

The DIAMETERS DECOMPOSITION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Plesník, "Complexity of decomposing graphs into factors with given diameters or radii", Mathematica Slovaca 32 (1982), 379-388, Theorem 1 and Section 5 (concluding remarks).
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), MINIMUM DIAMETERS DECOMPOSITION (citing Plesník).
