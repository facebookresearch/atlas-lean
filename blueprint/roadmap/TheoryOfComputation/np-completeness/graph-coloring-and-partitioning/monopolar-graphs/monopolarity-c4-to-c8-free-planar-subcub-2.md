# MONOPOLARITY on (C₄, …, C₈)-free planar graphs of maximum degree 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem MONOPOLARITY on (C₄, …, C₈)-free planar graphs of maximum degree 3 is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Le and Nevries (2011, 2014).
