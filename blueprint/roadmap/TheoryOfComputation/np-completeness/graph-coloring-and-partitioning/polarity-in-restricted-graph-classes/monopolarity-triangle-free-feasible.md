# Feasible instances of MONOPOLARITY OF TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E)$ of MONOPOLARITY OF TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS is feasible when its graph is monopolar: its vertex set splits into an independent set and a set inducing a disjoint union of cliques. The same predicate on $(n, E)$ is the feasibility test of [MONOPOLAR GRAPH RECOGNITION](../monopolar-graphs/README.md) and of MONOPOLARITY on $(C_4, \dots, C_8)$-free and on triangle-free planar graphs of maximum degree $3$; those problems differ only in their well-formedness tests.
