# POLARITY OF CLAW-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a claw-free simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether its vertex set can be split into a part inducing a complete multipartite graph and a part inducing a disjoint union of cliques.

## Sources

- R. Churchley and J. Huang, "On the polarity and monopolarity of graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 76 (2014), 138-148, doi:10.1002/jgt.21755 (recognizing polarity is NP-complete for claw-free graphs).
