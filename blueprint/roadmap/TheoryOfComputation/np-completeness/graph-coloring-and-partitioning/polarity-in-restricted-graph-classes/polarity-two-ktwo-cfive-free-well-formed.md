# Well-formed instances of POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E)$ of POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edges (in either orientation) and no loops, and every endpoint is below $n$, so that it describes a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, and this graph has no induced subgraph isomorphic to $2K_2$ (two disjoint edges) and none isomorphic to the cycle $C_5$.
