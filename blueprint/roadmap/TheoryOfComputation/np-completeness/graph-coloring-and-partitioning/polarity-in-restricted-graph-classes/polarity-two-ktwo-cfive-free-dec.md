# POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
