# POLARITY OF (2K₂, C₅)-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ with no induced $2K_2$ and no induced $C_5$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether its vertex set can be split into a part inducing a complete multipartite graph and a part inducing a disjoint union of cliques.

## Sources

- V. B. Le and R. Nevries, "Complexity and algorithms for recognizing polar and monopolar graphs", Theoretical Computer Science 528 (2014), 1-11, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2014.01.032 (recognizing polar graphs is NP-complete on {2K₂, C₅}-free co-planar graphs).
