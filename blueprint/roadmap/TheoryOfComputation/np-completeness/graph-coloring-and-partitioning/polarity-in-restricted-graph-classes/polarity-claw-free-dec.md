# POLARITY OF CLAW-FREE GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem POLARITY OF CLAW-FREE GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$, to an instance that is well-formed for POLARITY OF CLAW-FREE GRAPHS and whose graph is polar. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
