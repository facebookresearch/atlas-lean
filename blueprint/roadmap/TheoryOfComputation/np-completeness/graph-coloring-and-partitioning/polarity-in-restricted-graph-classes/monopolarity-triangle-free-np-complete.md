# MONOPOLARITY OF TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a triangle-free simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether its vertex set can be split into an independent set and a set inducing a disjoint union of cliques.

## Sources

- R. Churchley and J. Huang, "On the polarity and monopolarity of graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 76 (2014), 138-148, doi:10.1002/jgt.21755.
- ISGCI (Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions), class triangle-free (gc_371), problem Monopolarity: NP-complete [Churchley and Huang 2014].
