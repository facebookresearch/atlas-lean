# Interior points of a grid segment on the refined grid

In PARTITION INTO CLIQUES on B₀-CPG graphs, for grid points $p, q \in \mathbb{N}^2$, the interior points of the pair $(p, q)$ on the grid refined by a factor of $2$ are

$$\{x \in \mathbb{N}^2 : 2p \le x \le 2q \text{ coordinatewise}\} \setminus \{2p, 2q\},$$

the closed coordinatewise box between the doubled endpoints with those two endpoints removed. No order or alignment of $p$ and $q$ is assumed: for $p = (0, 0)$ and $q = (1, 1)$ the set contains $(0, 1)$, off the diagonal. When $p < q$ lie on a common horizontal or vertical line, as in the definition of B₀-CPG graphs, it is the set of interior points of the segment from $p$ to $q$ on the refined grid.
