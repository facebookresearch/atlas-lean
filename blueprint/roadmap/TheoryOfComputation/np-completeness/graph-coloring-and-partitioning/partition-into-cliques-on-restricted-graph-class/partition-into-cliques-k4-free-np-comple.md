# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON K_4-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON $K_4$-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES, comment: remains NP-complete for graphs containing no complete subgraphs on 4 vertices (see the construction for PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES in Chapter 3, Theorem 3.7).
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations*, Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9 (the unrestricted problem, there called CLIQUE COVER).
