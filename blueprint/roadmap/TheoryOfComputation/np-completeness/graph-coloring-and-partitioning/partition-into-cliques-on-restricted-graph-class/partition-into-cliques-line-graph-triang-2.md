# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON LINE GRAPHS OF TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON LINE GRAPHS OF TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Munaro, "Bounded clique cover of some sparse graphs", Discrete Mathematics 340(9) (2017), 2208-2216, doi:10.1016/j.disc.2017.04.004.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES and problem [GT1] VERTEX COVER.
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_708.html), problem "Clique cover" on line graphs of triangle-free graphs: NP-complete, with the attribution to Munaro (2017).
