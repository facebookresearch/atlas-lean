# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CUBIC PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CUBIC PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Cerioli, L. Faria, T. O. Ferreira, C. A. J. Martinhon, F. Protti and B. Reed, "Partition into cliques for cubic graphs: Planar case, complexity and approximation", Discrete Applied Mathematics 156(12) (2008), 2270-2278, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2007.10.015.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES.
