# Well-formed instances of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS when it is a well-formed PARTITION INTO CLIQUES instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, and $0 < K \le n$), and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is dually chordal (it has an ordering $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ of its vertices such that, for every position $i$, writing $N_i[x]$ for the closed neighbourhood of $x$ in the subgraph induced by the vertices at positions $\ge i$, some $\sigma_j \in N_i[\sigma_i]$ with $j \ge i$ satisfies $N_i[\sigma_k] \subseteq N_i[\sigma_j]$ for every $\sigma_k \in N_i[\sigma_i]$).
