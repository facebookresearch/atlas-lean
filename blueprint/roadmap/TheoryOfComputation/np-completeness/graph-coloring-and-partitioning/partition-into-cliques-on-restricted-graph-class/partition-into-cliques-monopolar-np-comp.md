# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON MONOPOLAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON MONOPOLAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Barbato and D. Bezzi, "Monopolar graphs: Complexity of computing classical graph parameters", Discrete Applied Mathematics 291 (2021), 277-285, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2020.12.023.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES.
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_1272.html), problem "Clique cover" on monopolar graphs: NP-complete, with the attribution to Barbato and Bezzi.
