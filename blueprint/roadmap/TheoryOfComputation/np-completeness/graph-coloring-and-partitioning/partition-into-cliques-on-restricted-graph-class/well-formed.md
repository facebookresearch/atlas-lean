# Well-formed instances of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON B_0-CPG GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON $B_0$-CPG GRAPHS when it is a well-formed PARTITION INTO CLIQUES instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, and $0 < K \le n$), and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is a $B_0$-CPG graph in the grid-segment sense (horizontal or vertical segments between grid points of $\mathbb{N}^2$, without common interior points, adjacent exactly when they share a point).
