# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CIRCLE GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CIRCLE GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(C, K)$ (a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CIRCLE GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
