# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES (the parent problem).
- A. Brandstädt, F. F. Dragan, V. D. Chepoi and V. I. Voloshin, "Dually chordal graphs", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 11(3) (1998), 437-455, doi:10.1137/S0895480193253415 (the class).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_185` (dually chordal), problem Clique cover: NP-complete (ISGCI reference note 1504: adding a universal vertex to any graph gives a dually chordal graph with the same clique cover number).
