# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE AT MOST k is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 3$, the decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE AT MOST $k$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
