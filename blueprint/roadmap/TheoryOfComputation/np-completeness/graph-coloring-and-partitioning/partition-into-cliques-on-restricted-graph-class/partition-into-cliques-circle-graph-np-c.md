# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CIRCLE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CIRCLE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. M. Keil and L. Stewart, "Approximating the minimum clique cover and other hard problems in subtree filament graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 154(14) (2006), 1983-1995, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2006.03.003, Theorem 2.1.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT15] PARTITION INTO CLIQUES.
