# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON B_0-CPG GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON $B_0$-CPG GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
