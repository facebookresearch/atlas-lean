# Well-formed instances of PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE AT MOST k

Fix a natural number $k$. An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE AT MOST $k$ when it is a well-formed PARTITION INTO CLIQUES instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, and $0 < K \le n$), and every vertex of the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has degree at most $k$.
