# PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CLAW-FREE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO CLIQUES ON CLAW-FREE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
