# B₀-CPG graphs

In PARTITION INTO CLIQUES on B₀-CPG graphs: `G` is a `B₀-CPG` graph: there are horizontal or vertical grid segments `P v` from `(P v).1` to `(P v).2 > (P v).1`, one per vertex, pairwise without common interior points, such that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their segments have a common point.
