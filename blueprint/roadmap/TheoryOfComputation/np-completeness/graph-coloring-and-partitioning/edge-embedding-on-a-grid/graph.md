# Graph of a EDGE EMBEDDING ON A GRID instance

The graph of a EDGE EMBEDDING ON A GRID instance with $n$ vertices and edge list $E$ is the
simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when
$(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ belongs to $E$. Loops are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$
contribute no edge.
