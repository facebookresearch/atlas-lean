# EDGE RANKING is NP-complete

The decision problem EDGE RANKING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. W. Lam and F. L. Yue, "Edge ranking of graphs is hard", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85(1) (1998), 71-86, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00029-8 (computing an optimal edge ranking of a general graph is NP-hard).
