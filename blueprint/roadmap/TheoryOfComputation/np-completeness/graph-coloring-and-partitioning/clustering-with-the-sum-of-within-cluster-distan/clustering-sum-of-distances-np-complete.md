# CLUSTERING with the sum of within-cluster distances is NP-complete

The CLUSTERING with the sum of within-cluster distances decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS9] CLUSTERING (p. 281), comment "Variants in which we ask that the sum, over all Xᵢ, of ... Σ_{x,y ∈ Xᵢ} d(x,y) be at most B, are similarly NP-complete (with the last one NP-complete even for K = 2)".
- P. Brucker, "On the complexity of clustering problems", in *Optimization and Operations Research* (R. Henn, B. Korte, and W. Oettli, eds.), Lecture Notes in Economics and Mathematical Systems 157, Springer (1978), 45-54, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-95322-4_5.
