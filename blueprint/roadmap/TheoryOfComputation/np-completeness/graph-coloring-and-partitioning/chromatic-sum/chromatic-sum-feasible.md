# Feasible instances of CHROMATIC SUM

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for CHROMATIC SUM when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has a proper coloring $c$ by positive integers (adjacent vertices receive different colors) with $\sum_{v=0}^{n-1} c(v) \le K$.
