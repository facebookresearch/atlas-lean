# GRAPH THICKNESS 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH THICKNESS 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), open problem [OPEN3].
- A. Mansfield, "Determining the thickness of graphs is NP-hard", Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society 93 (1983), 9-23, doi:10.1017/S030500410006028X.
