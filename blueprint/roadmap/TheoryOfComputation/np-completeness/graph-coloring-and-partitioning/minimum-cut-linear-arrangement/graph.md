# Graph

The graph of an instance of MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT on edge-weighted trees: the simple
graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$, where $n$ is the vertex count of the instance, in which
two distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent exactly when the weighted edge list $E$ of the
instance contains an entry $((u, v), w)$ or $((v, u), w)$, whatever its weight $w$. Loops
$(u, u)$ add no edge, and an entry with an endpoint outside $0, \dots, n - 1$ contributes
nothing.
