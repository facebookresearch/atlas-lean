# MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for split graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for split graphs is NP-complete:
it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to
it.

Source: Heggernes, Lokshtanov, Mihai and Papadopoulos (2008); Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT44].
