# The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for planar graphs of maximum degree 3

MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT for planar graphs of maximum degree 3 as a decision problem on
bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring
encoding, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible. A bitstring that does not decode to
an instance is rejected.
