# Feasible MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT on edge-weighted trees instances

A one-to-one numbering of the vertices, given by the positions in a permutation `perm` of $0, \dots, n - 1$, such that for every cut position $j$ with $j + 1 < n$ the entries of the edge list with one endpoint at a position $\le j$ and the other at a position $> j$ have total weight at most $K$. Every such entry is summed, so on a list that is not simple an edge can count more than once; for a well-formed instance the list is simple and this is the weight of the edges across the cut.
