# MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) on unit disk graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) on unit disk graphs is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Díaz, Penrose, Petit and Serna (1999), Theorem 1.
