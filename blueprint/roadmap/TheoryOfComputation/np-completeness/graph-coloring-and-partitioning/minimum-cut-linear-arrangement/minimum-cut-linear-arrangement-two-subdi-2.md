# MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for planar 2-subdivision graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for planar 2-subdivision graphs
is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time
many-one reduction to it.

Source: Monien and Sudborough (1988); ISGCI (gc_675); Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT44].
