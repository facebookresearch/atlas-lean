# MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for planar graphs of maximum degree 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (CUTWIDTH) for planar graphs of maximum
degree 3 is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Monien and Sudborough (1988); Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT44].
