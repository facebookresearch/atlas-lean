# MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT for grid graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM CUT LINEAR ARRANGEMENT for grid graphs is NP-complete: it belongs
to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Díaz, Penrose, Petit and Serna (1999), Theorem 1; Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT44].
