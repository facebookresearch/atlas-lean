# CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPH COLORING is NP-complete

The decision problem CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPH COLORING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (comment: NP-complete for circular arc graphs, even given their representation as families of arcs).
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson, G. L. Miller and C. H. Papadimitriou, "The complexity of coloring circular arcs and chords", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 1(2) (1980), 216-227, doi:10.1137/0601025.
