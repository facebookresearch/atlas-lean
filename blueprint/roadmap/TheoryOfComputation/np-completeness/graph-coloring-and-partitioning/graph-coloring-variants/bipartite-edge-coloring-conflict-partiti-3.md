# BIPARTITE EDGE COLORING WITH CONFLICT PARTITION as a decision problem

The decision problem BIPARTITE EDGE COLORING WITH CONFLICT PARTITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(n, E, K)$ and a list of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for BIPARTITE EDGE COLORING WITH CONFLICT PARTITION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
