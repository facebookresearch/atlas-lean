# Feasible instances of CIRCLE GRAPH K-COLORABILITY FOR FIXED K

Fix a natural number $K$. A list $C$ of chords is feasible for CIRCLE GRAPH $K$-COLORABILITY when the circle graph of $C$ (one vertex per chord, two distinct chords $(a, b)$ and $(a', b')$ adjacent when $a < a' < b < b'$ or $a' < a < b' < b$) is $K$-colorable.
