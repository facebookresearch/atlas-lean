# GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON CIRCLE GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON CIRCLE GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs (a list of chord pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON CIRCLE GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
