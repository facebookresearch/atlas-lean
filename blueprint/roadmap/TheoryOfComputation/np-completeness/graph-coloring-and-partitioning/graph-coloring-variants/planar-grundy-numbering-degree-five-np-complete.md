# GRUNDY NUMBERING FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 5 is NP-complete

The decision problem GRUNDY NUMBERING FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 5 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT56] GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING, comment: "Remains NP-complete when restricted to planar graphs in which no vertex has in- or out-degree exceeding 5 [Fraenkel and Yesha, 1977]".
- A. S. Fraenkel, "Planar kernel and Grundy with d ≤ 3, dout ≤ 2, din ≤ 2 are NP-complete", Discrete Applied Mathematics 3 (1981), 257-262, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(81)90003-2 (a subclass).
