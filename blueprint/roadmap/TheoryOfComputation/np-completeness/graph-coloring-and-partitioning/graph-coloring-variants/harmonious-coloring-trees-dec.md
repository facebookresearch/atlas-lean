# HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES as a decision problem

The decision problem HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES and feasible for HARMONIOUS COLORING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
