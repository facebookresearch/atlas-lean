# Well-formed instances of GRUNDY NUMBERING FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 5

A GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING instance is well-formed for GRUNDY NUMBERING FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 5 when it is a well-formed GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING instance (no repeated arcs, endpoints are vertices, no loops), its underlying graph is planar, and every vertex has in-degree and out-degree at most $5$.
