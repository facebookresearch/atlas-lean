# CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPH COLORING as a decision problem

The decision problem CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPH COLORING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(L, A, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for CIRCULAR-ARC GRAPH COLORING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
