# TOTAL COLORING OF REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem TOTAL COLORING OF $k$-REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS WITH $k+1$ COLORS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for this problem with degree $k$ and feasible for TOTAL COLORING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
