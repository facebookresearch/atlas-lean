# TOTAL COLORING OF REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 3$, the decision problem TOTAL COLORING OF REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. J. H. McDiarmid and A. Sánchez-Arroyo, "Total colouring regular bipartite graphs is NP-hard", Discrete Mathematics 124 (1994), 155-162, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(92)00058-Y.
- A. Sánchez-Arroyo, "Determining the total colouring number is NP-hard", Discrete Mathematics 78 (1989), 315-319, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(89)90187-8.
