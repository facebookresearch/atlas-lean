# CIRCLE GRAPH K-COLORABILITY as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem CIRCLE GRAPH K-COLORABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of pairs of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for circle graph $K$-colorability. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
