# BIPARTITE EDGE COLORING WITH CONFLICT PARTITION is NP-complete

The decision problem BIPARTITE EDGE COLORING WITH CONFLICT PARTITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
