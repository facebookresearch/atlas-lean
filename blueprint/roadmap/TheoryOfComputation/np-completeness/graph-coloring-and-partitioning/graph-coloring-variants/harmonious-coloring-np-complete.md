# HARMONIOUS COLORING is NP-complete

The decision problem HARMONIOUS COLORING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft and M. S. Krishnamoorthy, "On the harmonious coloring of graphs", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 4 (1983), 306-311, doi:10.1137/0604032.
