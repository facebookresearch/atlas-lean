# CHROMATIC INDEX 3 OF CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem CHROMATIC INDEX 3 OF CUBIC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (comment: CHROMATIC INDEX, coloring the line graph, NP-complete for $K = 3$ and cubic graphs, citing Holyer's 1979 preprint) and open problem [OPEN5] CHROMATIC INDEX.
- I. Holyer, "The NP-completeness of edge-coloring", SIAM Journal on Computing 10(4) (1981), 718-720, doi:10.1137/0210055.
