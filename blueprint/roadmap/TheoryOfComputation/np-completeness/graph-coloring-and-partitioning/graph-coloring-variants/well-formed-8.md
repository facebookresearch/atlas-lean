# Well-formed instances of HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES when the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, $K > 0$, and the graph of the instance is a tree (connected and acyclic, so $n \ge 1$).
