# GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON CIRCLE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON CIRCLE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), p. 191 (comment: "For arbitrary K, the problem is NP-complete for circle graphs and circular arc graphs (even given their representation as families of arcs)").
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson, G. L. Miller and C. H. Papadimitriou, "The complexity of coloring circular arcs and chords", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 1 (1980), 216-227, doi:10.1137/0601025.
