# HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES is NP-complete

The decision problem HARMONIOUS COLORING OF TREES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- K. Edwards and C. McDiarmid, "The complexity of harmonious colouring for trees", Discrete Applied Mathematics 57 (1995), 133-144, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)00100-R.
