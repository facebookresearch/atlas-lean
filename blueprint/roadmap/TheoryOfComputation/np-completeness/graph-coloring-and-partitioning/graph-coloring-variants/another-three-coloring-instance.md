# Instances of ANOTHER 3-COLORING

An instance of ANOTHER 3-COLORING is a triple $(n, E, c)$: a natural number $n$, the number of vertices $0, \dots, n-1$; a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers, the edges; and a list $c$ of natural numbers, whose $v$-th entry $c(v)$ is the color given to vertex $v$.
