# (2, 1)-DEFECTIVE COLORING OF PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem (2, 1)-DEFECTIVE COLORING OF PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. J. Cowen, W. Goddard and C. E. Jesurum, "Defective coloring revisited", Journal of Graph Theory 24(3) (1997), 205-219, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1097-0118(199703)24:3<205::AID-JGT2>3.0.CO;2-T (abstract: "the (2, 1)-coloring and the (3, 1)-coloring problems are NP-complete even for planar graphs").
