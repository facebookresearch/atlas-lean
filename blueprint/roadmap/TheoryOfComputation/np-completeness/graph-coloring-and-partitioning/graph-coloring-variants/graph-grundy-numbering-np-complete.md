# GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT56] GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING.
- J. van Leeuwen, "Having a Grundy-numbering is NP-complete", Report No. 207, Computer Science Department, Pennsylvania State University (1976).
- A. S. Fraenkel, "Planar kernel and Grundy with d ≤ 3, d_out ≤ 2, d_in ≤ 2 are NP-complete", Discrete Applied Mathematics 3 (1981), 257-262, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(81)90003-2.
