# Well-formed instances of GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING for planar digraphs with degree bounds

An instance of GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING for planar digraphs with degree bounds is well-formed when it is a well-formed GRAPH GRUNDY NUMBERING instance (no repeated arcs, endpoints are vertices, no loops); the underlying undirected graph is planar; every vertex has total degree at most $3$, in-degree at most $2$ and out-degree at most $2$.
