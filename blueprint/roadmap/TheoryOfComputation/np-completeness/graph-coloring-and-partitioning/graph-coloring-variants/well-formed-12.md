# Well-formed instances of TOTAL COLORING OF k-REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS WITH k+1 COLORS

Fix a natural number $k$. An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for TOTAL COLORING OF k-REGULAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS WITH k+1 COLORS when the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, $K = k + 1$, $n > 0$, and the graph of the instance is $k$-regular (every vertex has exactly $k$ neighbors) and bipartite.
