# ORIENTED k-COLORING as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem ORIENTED $k$-COLORING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, A)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs of natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for ORIENTED $k$-COLORING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
