# Feasible instances of ANOTHER 3-COLORING

An instance $(n,E,c)$ is feasible when its graph has a proper coloring $f$ with colors in $\{0,1,2\}$ that is not a renaming of the given coloring. For a vertex $v<n$, the given color is the entry at position $v$ in $c$ modulo $3$; a missing entry gives color zero. No permutation of the three colors may send the given color to $f(v)$ for every vertex $v$.
