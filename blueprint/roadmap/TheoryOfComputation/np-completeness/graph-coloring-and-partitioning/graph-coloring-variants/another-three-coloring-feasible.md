# Feasible instances of ANOTHER 3-COLORING

An instance $(n, E, c)$ of ANOTHER 3-COLORING is feasible when the graph of the instance has a proper coloring $f$ with colors in $\{0, 1, 2\}$ that is not obtained from the given coloring by renaming colors: there is no permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, 1, 2\}$ with $f(v) = \sigma(\text{given color of } v)$ for every vertex $v$.
