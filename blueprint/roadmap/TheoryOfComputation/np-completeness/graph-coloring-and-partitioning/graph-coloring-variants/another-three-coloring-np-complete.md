# ANOTHER 3-COLORING is NP-complete

The decision problem ANOTHER 3-COLORING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. P. Dailey, "Uniqueness of colorability and colorability of planar 4-regular graphs are NP-complete", Discrete Mathematics 30(3) (1980), 289-293, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(80)90236-8.
- Shalu M. A. and C. Antony, "Hardness transitions and uniqueness of acyclic colouring", Discrete Applied Mathematics 345 (2024), 77-98, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2023.11.030, Section 1.4.
