# INTERVAL EDGE-COLORABILITY OF BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem INTERVAL EDGE-COLORABILITY OF BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources
