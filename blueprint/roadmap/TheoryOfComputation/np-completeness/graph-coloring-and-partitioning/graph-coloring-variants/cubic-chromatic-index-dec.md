# CHROMATIC INDEX 3 OF CUBIC GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem CHROMATIC INDEX 3 OF CUBIC GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for CHROMATIC INDEX 3 OF CUBIC GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
