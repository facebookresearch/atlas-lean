# Harmonious colorings

Let $G$ be a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $c$ a proper coloring of $G$ with colors in $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$. Then $c$ is harmonious when any two edges $\{u, v\}$ and $\{x, y\}$ of $G$ with $\{c(u), c(v)\} = \{c(x), c(y)\}$ are the same edge: each unordered pair of colors occurs on at most one edge.
