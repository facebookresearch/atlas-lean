# Feasible instances of PARTITION INTO INDUCED ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS

Let $G$ be the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ given by $E$ and $H$ the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, m-1$ given by $F$ (in each, $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is listed). An instance $(n, E, m, F)$ is feasible for PARTITION INTO INDUCED ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS when, for some positive $q$, the vertices of $G$ split into $q$ blocks such that the subgraph of $G$ induced by each block is isomorphic to $H$.
