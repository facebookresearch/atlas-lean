# Feasible instances of PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS when, for some $k \le K$, the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ can be colored with colors $0, \dots, k-1$ so that, in the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$, every vertex has exactly one neighbor of its own color; that is, the subgraph induced by each color class is a perfect matching of that class.
