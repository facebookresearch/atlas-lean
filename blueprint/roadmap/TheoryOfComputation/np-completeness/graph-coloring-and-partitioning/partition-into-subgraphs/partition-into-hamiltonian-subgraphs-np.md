# PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT13] PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS (credited there to L. G. Valiant, unpublished).
- B. Manthey, "On approximating restricted cycle covers", SIAM Journal on Computing 38 (2008), 181-206, doi:10.1137/060676003 (Section 1: deciding whether a directed graph has a cycle cover all of whose cycles have length at least three is NP-complete, citing Garey and Johnson).
