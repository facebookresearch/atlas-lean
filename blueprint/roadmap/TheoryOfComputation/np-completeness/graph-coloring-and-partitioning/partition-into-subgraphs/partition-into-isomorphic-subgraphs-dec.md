# PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, m, F)$ (two vertex counts and two edge lists), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
