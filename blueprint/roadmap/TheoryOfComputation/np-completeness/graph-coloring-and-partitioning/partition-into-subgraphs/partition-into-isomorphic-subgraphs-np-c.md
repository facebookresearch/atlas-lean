# PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. G. Kirkpatrick and P. Hell, "On the completeness of a generalized matching problem", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1978), 240-245, doi:10.1145/800133.804353.
- D. G. Kirkpatrick and P. Hell, "On the complexity of general graph factor problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 12 (1983), 601-609, doi:10.1137/0212040.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 193, the comment to problem [GT12] PARTITION INTO ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS ("the analogous problem in which the subgraph induced by `V_i` need only have the same number of vertices as `H` and contain a subgraph isomorphic to `H`"). This is the problem on this page (the $H$-factor problem); Garey and Johnson's main [GT12] entry asks instead that the subgraph induced by each $V_i$ be isomorphic to $H$, which is PARTITION INTO INDUCED ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS.
