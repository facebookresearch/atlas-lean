# PARTITION INTO INDUCED ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO INDUCED ISOMORPHIC SUBGRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
