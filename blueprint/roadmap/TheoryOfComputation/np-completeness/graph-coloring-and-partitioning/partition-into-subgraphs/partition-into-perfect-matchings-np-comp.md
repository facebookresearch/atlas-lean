# PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT16] PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS.
- T. J. Schaefer, "The complexity of satisfiability problems", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 216-226, doi:10.1145/800133.804350 (already for `K = 2`, by a transformation from NOT-ALL-EQUAL 3SAT).
- See also E. D. Demaine, K. Karntikoon, and N. Pitimanaaree, "2-Colorable perfect matching is NP-complete in 2-connected 3-regular planar graphs", Theory of Computing Systems (2025), doi:10.1007/s00224-025-10221-2.
