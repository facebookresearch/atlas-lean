# PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS OF AT LEAST SIX VERTICES is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS OF AT LEAST SIX VERTICES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT13] PARTITION INTO HAMILTONIAN SUBGRAPHS, comment (p. 193: for undirected graphs "it becomes NP-complete if we require that |V_i| ≥ 6", credited to C. H. Papadimitriou, private communication, 1978).
- P. Hell, D. G. Kirkpatrick, J. Kratochvíl and I. Kříž, "On restricted two-factors", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 1 (1988), 472-484, doi:10.1137/0401046 (deciding whether a graph has a two-factor with all cycle lengths in `L` is NP-complete unless every excluded length is 3 or 4; here the excluded lengths are 3, 4, 5), as recorded by B. Manthey, "On approximating restricted cycle covers", SIAM Journal on Computing 38 (2008), 181-206, doi:10.1137/060676003, Section 1.
