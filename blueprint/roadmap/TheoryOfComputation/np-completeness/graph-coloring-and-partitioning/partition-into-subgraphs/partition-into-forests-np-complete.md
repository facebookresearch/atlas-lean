# PARTITION INTO FORESTS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO FORESTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT14] PARTITION INTO FORESTS (unpublished result; NP-complete already for `K = 2`).
- S. L. Hakimi and E. F. Schmeichel, "A note on the vertex arboricity of a graph", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 2 (1989), 64-67, doi:10.1137/0402007 (NP-complete for `K = 2` already on planar graphs).
