# PARTITION INTO 4-CYCLES FOR PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO 4-CYCLES FOR PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- F. Berman, D. Johnson, T. Leighton, P. W. Shor and L. Snyder, "Generalized planar matching", Journal of Algorithms 11 (1990), 153-184, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(90)90001-U.
- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 6 (1985), 434-451, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(85)90012-4 (PARTITION INTO 4-CYCLES).
