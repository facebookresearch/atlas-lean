# PARTITION INTO FORESTS as a decision problem

The decision problem PARTITION INTO FORESTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for PARTITION INTO FORESTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
