# PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES FOR PLANAR GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES FOR PLANAR GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES instances, to an instance that is well-formed for PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES FOR PLANAR GRAPHS and feasible for PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
