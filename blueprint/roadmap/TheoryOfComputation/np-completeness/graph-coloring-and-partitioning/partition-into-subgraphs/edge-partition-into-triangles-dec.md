# EDGE PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES as a decision problem

The decision problem EDGE PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for EDGE PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
