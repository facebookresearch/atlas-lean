# PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS FOR PLANAR CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS FOR PLANAR CUBIC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT16] PARTITION INTO PERFECT MATCHINGS, comment: remains NP-complete for planar cubic graphs.
- T. J. Schaefer, "The complexity of satisfiability problems", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 216-226, doi:10.1145/800133.804350.
- E. D. Demaine, K. Karntikoon and N. Pitimanaaree, "2-Colorable perfect matching is NP-complete in 2-connected 3-regular planar graphs", Theory of Computing Systems 69 (2025), doi:10.1007/s00224-025-10221-2.
