# GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3 as a decision problem

The decision problem GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when
$x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3
instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](generalized-hypertree-width-three-feasib.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
