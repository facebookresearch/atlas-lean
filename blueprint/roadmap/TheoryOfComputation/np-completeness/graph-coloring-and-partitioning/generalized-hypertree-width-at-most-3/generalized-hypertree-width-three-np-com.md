# GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3 belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. Gottlob, Z. Miklós and T. Schwentick, "Generalized hypertree decompositions: NP-hardness and tractable variants", Journal of the ACM 56 (2009), no. 6, 1-32, doi:10.1145/1568318.1568320.
- W. Fischl, G. Gottlob and R. Pichler, "General and fractional hypertree decompositions: hard and easy cases", Proceedings of the 37th ACM SIGMOD-SIGACT-SIGAI Symposium on Principles of Database Systems (PODS 2018), 17-32, doi:10.1145/3196959.3196962 (arXiv:1611.01090), Section 1 (the attribution of NP-completeness for `k = 3` to Gottlob, Miklós and Schwentick) and Section 2.3 (Definition 2.3 and the text following Definition 2.5: the definition of generalized hypertree decompositions and their width).
