# Instances of GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3

An instance of GENERALIZED HYPERTREE WIDTH AT MOST 3 consists of the number of vertices $n$ (vertices $0, \dots, n-1$) and a list of hyperedges, each a list of vertices. Hyperedges are identified by their position in the list, so repeated entries are distinct hyperedges; see the [width condition](is-ghdwidth-at-most-three.md).
