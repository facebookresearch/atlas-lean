# Feasible instances of BOOK THICKNESS AT MOST 2

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible when the graph with vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$ and edge list $E$ has a $2$-page book embedding: an injective placement of the vertices at distinct positions on a line (the spine) and an assignment of each edge to one of two pages such that no two edges on the same page cross. Two edges $\{v, w\}$ and $\{x, y\}$ cross when their endpoints interleave along the spine, that is, $v, x, w, y$ appear in this order for some labelling of their endpoints.
