# BOOK THICKNESS AT MOST 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem BOOK THICKNESS AT MOST 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](book-thickness-two-feasible.md) for BOOK THICKNESS AT MOST 2. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
