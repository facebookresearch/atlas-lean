# ONE-SIDED CROSSING MINIMIZATION as a decision problem

The decision problem ONE-SIDED CROSSING MINIMIZATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(U, W, E, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](one-sided-crossing-minimization-feasible.md) for ONE-SIDED CROSSING MINIMIZATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
