# CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE as a decision problem

The decision problem CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE and feasible for CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
