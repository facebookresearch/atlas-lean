# CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE is NP-complete

The decision problem CUT INTO CONNECTED COMPONENTS OF BOUNDED SIZE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
