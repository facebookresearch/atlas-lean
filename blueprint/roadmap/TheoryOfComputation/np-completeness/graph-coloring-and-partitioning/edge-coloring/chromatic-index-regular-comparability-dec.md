# CHROMATIC INDEX of k-regular comparability graphs as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem CHROMATIC INDEX of k-regular comparability graphs accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed for CHROMATIC INDEX of $k$-regular comparability graphs and whose graph has an edge coloring with $k$ colors. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
