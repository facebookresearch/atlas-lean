# Feasible instances of DISTANCE-d CHROMATIC NUMBER

Fix a natural number $d$. An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for DISTANCE-$d$ CHROMATIC NUMBER when there is a map $c$ from the vertices to $\{0, \dots, K-1\}$ such that for any two distinct vertices $u, v$ with $c(u) = c(v)$, every path in the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) from $u$ to $v$ has length greater than $d$. Blocks of the partition may be empty, and vertices in different components may share a block.
