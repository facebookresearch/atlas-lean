# DISTANCE-d CHROMATIC NUMBER is NP-complete

For every natural number $d \ge 2$, the decision problem DISTANCE-$d$ CHROMATIC NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
