# L(2,1)-LABELING WITH FIXED SPAN K as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem L(2,1)-LABELING WITH FIXED SPAN K accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$, to an instance whose edge list is a simple edge list on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ and whose graph has an $L(2,1)$-labeling with labels in $\{0, \dots, K\}$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
