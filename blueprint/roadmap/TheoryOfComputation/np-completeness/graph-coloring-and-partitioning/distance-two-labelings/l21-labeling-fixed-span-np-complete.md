# L(2,1)-LABELING WITH FIXED SPAN K is NP-complete for every fixed K ≥ 4

For every natural number $K \ge 4$, the following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether its vertices can be labeled with $\{0, \dots, K\}$ so that adjacent vertices get labels at least $2$ apart and distinct vertices with a common neighbor get different labels.

## Sources

- J. Fiala, T. Kloks and J. Kratochvíl, "Fixed-parameter complexity of λ-labelings", Discrete Applied Mathematics 113 (2001), 59-72, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00387-5.
