# TREEWIDTH ON CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON CUBIC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. L. Bodlaender, É. Bonnet, L. Jaffke, D. Knop, P. T. Lima, M. Milanič, S. Ordyniak, S. Pandey and O. Suchý, "Treewidth is NP-complete on cubic graphs", Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 32(3) (2025), #P3.36, doi:10.37236/13205 (arXiv:2301.10031, "Treewidth is NP-complete on cubic graphs (and related results)", Theorem 4.1: "Treewidth is NP-complete on regular graphs of degree 3").
