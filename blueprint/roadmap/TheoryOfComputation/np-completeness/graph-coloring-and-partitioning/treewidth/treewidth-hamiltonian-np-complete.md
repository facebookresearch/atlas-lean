# TREEWIDTH ON HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Cavallaro, *Hamiltonicity and the computational complexity of graph problems*, Bachelor thesis, Technische Universität Berlin (2019), https://fpt.akt.tu-berlin.de/publications/theses/BA-Cavallaro.pdf, Theorem 7.3.
- S. Arnborg, D. G. Corneil and A. Proskurowski, "Complexity of finding embeddings in a k-tree", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 8 (1987), 277-284, doi:10.1137/0608024 (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "hamiltonian" (gc_1092), problem treewidth: NP-complete.
