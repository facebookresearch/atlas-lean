# TREEWIDTH ON PERFECT ELIMINATION BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON PERFECT ELIMINATION BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Liu, "Restricted bipartite graphs: comparison and hardness results", in Algorithmic Aspects in Information and Management (AAIM 2014), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 8546, Springer (2014), 241-252, doi:10.1007/978-3-319-07956-1_22 (abstract: "We also show the NP-completeness of treewidth and feedback vertex set for perfect elimination bipartite graphs.").
