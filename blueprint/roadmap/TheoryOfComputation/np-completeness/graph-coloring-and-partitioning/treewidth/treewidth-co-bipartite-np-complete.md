# TREEWIDTH ON CO-BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON CO-BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Arnborg, D. G. Corneil and A. Proskurowski, "Complexity of finding embeddings in a k-tree", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 8 (1987), 277-284, doi:10.1137/0608024.
- H. L. Bodlaender, É. Bonnet, L. Jaffke, D. Knop, P. T. Lima, M. Milanič, S. Ordyniak, S. Pandey and O. Suchý, "Treewidth is NP-complete on cubic graphs", Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 32(3) (2025), #P3.36, doi:10.37236/13205 (arXiv:2301.10031, "Treewidth is NP-complete on cubic graphs (and related results)": Section 2 defines co-bipartite graphs, Corollary 3.2 states "Treewidth is NP-complete on co-bipartite graphs", and the introduction attributes this to Arnborg, Corneil and Proskurowski: "their proof also gives NP-completeness on co-bipartite graphs").
