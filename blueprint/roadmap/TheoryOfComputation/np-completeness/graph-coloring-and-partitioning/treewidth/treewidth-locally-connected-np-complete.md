# TREEWIDTH ON LOCALLY CONNECTED GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON LOCALLY CONNECTED GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Arnborg, D. G. Corneil and A. Proskurowski, "Complexity of finding embeddings in a k-tree", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 8 (1987), 277-284, doi:10.1137/0608024 (NP-completeness of TREEWIDTH on all graphs, the source problem of the reduction).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "locally connected" (gc_932, https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_932.html), problem treewidth decomposition: NP-complete, reference [1506] (a note: adding a universal vertex to a connected graph gives a locally connected graph; "Treewidth is reduced analogously to clique (Communicated by Arne Leitert)").
