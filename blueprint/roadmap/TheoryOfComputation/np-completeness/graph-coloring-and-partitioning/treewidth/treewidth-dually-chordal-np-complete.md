# TREEWIDTH ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem TREEWIDTH ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Brandstädt, V. D. Chepoi and F. F. Dragan, "The algorithmic use of hypertree structure and maximum neighborhood orderings", Discrete Applied Mathematics 82 (1998), 43-77, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(97)00125-X (Section 9, Theorem 9.2: "The problem of computing the treewidth of a dually chordal graph is NP-complete.").
- S. Arnborg, D. G. Corneil and A. Proskurowski, "Complexity of finding embeddings in a k-tree", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 8 (1987), 277-284, doi:10.1137/0608024 (NP-completeness of TREEWIDTH on all graphs, the source problem of the reduction).
