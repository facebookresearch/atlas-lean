# MINIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Díaz and G. B. Mertzios, "Minimum bisection is NP-hard on unit disk graphs", Information and Computation 256 (2017), 83-92, doi:10.1016/j.ic.2017.04.010 (conference version MFCS 2014, LNCS 8635, 251-262, doi:10.1007/978-3-662-44465-8_22; preprint arXiv:1404.0117).
