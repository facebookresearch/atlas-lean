# MINIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM BISECTION on unit disk graphs accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed for MAX CUT on unit disk graphs and whose graph, with the bound $K$, is a feasible MINIMUM BISECTION instance. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
