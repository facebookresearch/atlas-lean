# Graph of a MINIMUM BISECTION on 3-regular graphs instance

The graph of an instance of MINIMUM BISECTION on 3-regular graphs is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $n$ the vertex count of the instance, in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ belongs to the edge list of the instance.
