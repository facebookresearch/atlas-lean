# Well-formed instances of MINIMUM BISECTION on 3-regular graphs

An instance $(n, E, K)$ of MINIMUM BISECTION is well-formed for MINIMUM BISECTION on 3-regular graphs when it is well-formed for MINIMUM BISECTION ($n$ is even and $E$ is a simple edge list on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$) and every vertex of its graph has degree exactly $3$.
