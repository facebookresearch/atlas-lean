# B-CHROMATIC NUMBER is NP-complete

The B-CHROMATIC NUMBER decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. W. Irving and D. F. Manlove, "The b-chromatic number of a graph", Discrete Applied Mathematics 91 (1999), 127-141, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00146-2 (Theorem 3.3).
