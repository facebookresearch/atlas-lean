# Caterpillars with hairs of bounded length

For a natural number $h$, a simple graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ is a caterpillar with hairs of length at most $h$ when it is a tree and has a body, a list of distinct vertices with consecutive entries adjacent, such that every vertex off the body has degree at most $2$ and lies within distance $h$ of some body vertex.
