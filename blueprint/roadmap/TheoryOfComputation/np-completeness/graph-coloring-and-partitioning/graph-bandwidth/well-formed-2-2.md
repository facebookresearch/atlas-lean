# Well-formed instances of BANDWIDTH ON TREES OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for BANDWIDTH ON TREES OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 when it is a well-formed BANDWIDTH instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is a tree with every vertex of degree at most $3$, and $0 < K \le n$.
