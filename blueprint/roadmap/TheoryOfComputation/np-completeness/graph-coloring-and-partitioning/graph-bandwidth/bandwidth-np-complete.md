# BANDWIDTH is NP-complete

The BANDWIDTH decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), BANDWIDTH; Christos H. Papadimitriou, "The NP-completeness of the bandwidth minimization problem" (1976).
