# GRAPH COLORING ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST k is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 1$, the decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST $k$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (comment: NP-complete for $K = 3$ and planar graphs with no vertex degree exceeding 4).
- L. Stockmeyer, "Planar 3-colorability is polynomial complete", ACM SIGACT News 5(3) (1973), 19-25, doi:10.1145/1008293.1008294.
- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 6(3) (1985), 434-451, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(85)90012-4, Table 1 (CHROMATIC NUMBER on graphs of thickness $k$).
