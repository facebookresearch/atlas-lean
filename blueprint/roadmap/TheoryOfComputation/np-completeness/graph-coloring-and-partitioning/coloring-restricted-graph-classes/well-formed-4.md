# Well-formed instances of GRAPH COLORING ON B0-VPG GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for GRAPH COLORING ON $B_0$-VPG GRAPHS when it is a well-formed GRAPH COLORING instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $3 \le K \le n$, and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is a $B_0$-VPG graph: there are points $\ell_v \le h_v$ of $\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$ (in the componentwise order) for each vertex $v$, with $\ell_v$ and $h_v$ agreeing in at least one coordinate, such that two distinct vertices $u, v$ are adjacent exactly when the grid boxes $[\ell_u, h_u]$ and $[\ell_v, h_v]$ share a point; since the endpoints agree in a coordinate, each box is a horizontal or vertical grid segment.
