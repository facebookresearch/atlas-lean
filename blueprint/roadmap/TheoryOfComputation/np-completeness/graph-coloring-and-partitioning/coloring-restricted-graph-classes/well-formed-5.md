# Well-formed instances of GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON GRAPHS OF BOXICITY AT MOST 2

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed for GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON GRAPHS OF BOXICITY AT MOST 2 when it is a well-formed GRAPH COLORING instance (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), $0 < K \le n$, and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is an intersection graph of closed axis-parallel rectangles: there are points $\ell_v \le h_v$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ (componentwise) for each vertex $v$ such that two distinct vertices $u, v$ are adjacent exactly when the rectangles $[\ell_u, h_u]$ and $[\ell_v, h_v]$ intersect.

The same test is the well-formedness condition of INDEPENDENT SET on boxicity-2 graphs.
