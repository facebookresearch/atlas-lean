# 3-COLORABILITY OF k-REGULAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 4$, the decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF $k$-REGULAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (the parent problem).
- H. Fleischner and G. Sabidussi, "3-colorability of 4-regular Hamiltonian graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 42(2) (2003), 125-140, doi:10.1002/jgt.10079 (the case $k = 4$).
- D. Cavallaro, *Hamiltonicity and the computational complexity of graph problems*, Bachelor thesis, Technische Universität Berlin (2019), Theorem 4.9 (the case $k = 4$, attributed to Fleischner and Sabidussi) and Theorem 4.10 (every fixed $k \ge 4$).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, classes 4-regular ∩ hamiltonian (gc_1318) and 5-regular ∩ hamiltonian (gc_1317), problem 3-Colourability: NP-complete.
