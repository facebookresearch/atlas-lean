# GRAPH COLORING ON GRAPHS WITHOUT A SUBDIVIDED K_{3,3} is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON GRAPHS WITHOUT A SUBDIVIDED $K_{3,3}$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (comment: remains NP-complete for $K = 3$ and planar graphs).
- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide" (sixteenth edition), Journal of Algorithms 6(3) (1985), 434-451, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(85)90012-4, Table 1 (row K3,3-free, column chromatic number) and Section 3 (K3,3-free graphs contain no subgraph homeomorphic to $K_{3,3}$ but may contain one homeomorphic to $K_5$).
