# GRAPH COLORING ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON DUALLY CHORDAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER) (the parent problem).
- A. Leitert, "3-Colourability of dually chordal graphs in linear time", arXiv:1206.0233 (2012), Theorem 1 (4-COLOURABILITY is NP-complete for dually chordal graphs).
- A. Brandstädt, V. D. Chepoi and F. F. Dragan, "The algorithmic use of hypertree structure and maximum neighbourhood orderings", Discrete Applied Mathematics 82(1-3) (1998), 43-77, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(97)00125-X (dually chordal graphs are recognized in linear time).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class gc_185 (dually chordal), problem Colourability: NP-complete.
