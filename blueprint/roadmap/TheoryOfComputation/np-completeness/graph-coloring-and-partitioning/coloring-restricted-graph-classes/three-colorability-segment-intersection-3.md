# 3-COLORABILITY OF SEGMENT INTERSECTION GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF SEGMENT INTERSECTION GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of segments, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for 3-COLORABILITY OF SEGMENT INTERSECTION GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
