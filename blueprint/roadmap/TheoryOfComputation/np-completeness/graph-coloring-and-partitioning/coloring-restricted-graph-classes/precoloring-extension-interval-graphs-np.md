# PRECOLORING EXTENSION ON INTERVAL GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem PRECOLORING EXTENSION ON INTERVAL GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Biró, M. Hujter, and Zs. Tuza, "Precoloring extension. I. Interval graphs", Discrete Mathematics 100(1-3) (1992), 267-279, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(92)90646-W.
- D. Marx, *Graph Coloring with Local and Global Constraints*, Ph.D. thesis, Budapest University of Technology and Economics (2004), Chapter 3 (records the result of Biró, Hujter, and Tuza).
