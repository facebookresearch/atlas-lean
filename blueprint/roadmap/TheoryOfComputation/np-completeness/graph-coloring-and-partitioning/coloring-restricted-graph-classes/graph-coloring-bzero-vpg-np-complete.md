# GRAPH COLORING ON B0-VPG GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON $B_0$-VPG GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), the problem restricted here.
- A. Asinowski, E. Cohen, M. C. Golumbic, V. Limouzy, M. Lipshteyn, and M. Stern, "Vertex intersection graphs of paths on a grid", Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications 16 (2012), 129-150, doi:10.7155/jgaa.00253, Theorem 8.
