# 3-COLORABILITY OF MONOPOLAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF MONOPOLAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Barbato and D. Bezzi, "Monopolar graphs: complexity of computing classical graph parameters", Discrete Applied Mathematics 291 (2021), 277-285, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2020.12.023 (Section 2.1, Proposition 2.8 in the authors' version: "The 3-Colorability problem on monopolar graphs is NP-complete.").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "monopolar" (gc_1272), problem 3-Colourability: NP-complete, with the reference to Barbato and Bezzi (2021).
