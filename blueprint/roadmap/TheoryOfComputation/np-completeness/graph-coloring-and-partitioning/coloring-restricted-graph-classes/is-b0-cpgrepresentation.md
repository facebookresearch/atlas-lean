# B0-CPG representations

Grid points $A_v, B_v \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ for the vertices $v$ of a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ form a $B_0$-CPG representation of $G$ when every path is a horizontal or vertical segment between two distinct grid points ($A_v \ne B_v$, and they agree in one coordinate), the interiors of the paths of distinct vertices are disjoint, and two distinct vertices $u, v$ are adjacent exactly when some grid point lies on both of their paths and is one of the four endpoints $A_u, B_u, A_v, B_v$.
