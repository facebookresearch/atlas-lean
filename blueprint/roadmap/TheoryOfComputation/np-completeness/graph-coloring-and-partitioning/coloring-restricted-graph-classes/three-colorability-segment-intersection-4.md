# 3-COLORABILITY OF SEGMENT INTERSECTION GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF SEGMENT INTERSECTION GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources
