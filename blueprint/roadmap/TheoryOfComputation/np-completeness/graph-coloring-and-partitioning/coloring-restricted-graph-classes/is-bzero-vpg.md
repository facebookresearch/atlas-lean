# B0-VPG graphs

A simple graph $G$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ is a $B_0$-VPG graph when there are points $\ell_v \le h_v$ of $\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}$ (in the componentwise order) for each vertex $v$, with $\ell_v$ and $h_v$ agreeing in at least one coordinate, such that two distinct vertices $u, v$ are adjacent exactly when the grid boxes $[\ell_u, h_u]$ and $[\ell_v, h_v]$ share a point; since the endpoints agree in a coordinate, each box is a horizontal or vertical grid segment.
