# k-COLORABLE INDUCED SUBGRAPH is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 1$, the decision problem $k$-COLORABLE INDUCED SUBGRAPH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT21] INDUCED SUBGRAPH WITH PROPERTY Π (comment).
- J. M. Lewis and M. Yannakakis, "The node-deletion problem for hereditary properties is NP-complete", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 20 (1980), 219-230, doi:10.1016/0022-0000(80)90060-4.
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann, *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM K-COLORABLE INDUCED SUBGRAPH.
