# GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON GRAPHS OF BOXICITY AT MOST 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH K-COLORABILITY ON GRAPHS OF BOXICITY AT MOST 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), the problem restricted here.
- H. Imai and T. Asano, "Finding the connected components and a maximum clique of an intersection graph of rectangles in the plane", Journal of Algorithms 4 (1983), 310-323, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(83)90012-3 (coloring rectangle intersection graphs is NP-complete).
- D. Catanzaro, S. Chaplick, S. Felsner, B. V. Halldórsson, M. M. Halldórsson, T. Hixon, and J. Stacho, "Max point-tolerance graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 216 (2017), 84-97, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2015.08.019, arXiv:1508.03810 (records the result for boxicity-2 graphs).
