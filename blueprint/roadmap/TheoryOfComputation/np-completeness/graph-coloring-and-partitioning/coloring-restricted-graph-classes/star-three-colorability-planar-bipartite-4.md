# STAR 3-COLORABILITY OF PLANAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem STAR 3-COLORABILITY OF PLANAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. O. Albertson, G. G. Chappell, H. A. Kierstead, A. Kündgen and R. Ramamurthi, "Coloring with no 2-colored $P_4$'s", Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 11(1) (2004), #R26, doi:10.37236/1779 (Definition 2.1; Theorem 7.2).
