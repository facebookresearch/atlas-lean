# 3-COLORABILITY OF GRAPHS OF DIAMETER AT MOST 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF GRAPHS OF DIAMETER AT MOST 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. B. Mertzios and P. G. Spirakis, "Algorithms and almost tight results for 3-colorability of small diameter graphs", Algorithmica 74(1) (2016), 385-414, doi:10.1007/s00453-014-9949-6 (Theorem 6: 3-coloring is NP-complete on triangle-free graphs with diameter 3 and radius 2).
