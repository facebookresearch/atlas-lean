# 3-COLORABILITY OF B0-CPG GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF $B_0$-CPG GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY, the problem restricted here.
