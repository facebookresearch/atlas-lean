# 3-COLORABILITY OF PLANAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF PLANAR HAMILTONIAN GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (the parent problem).
- D. Cavallaro, *Hamiltonicity and the computational complexity of graph problems*, Bachelor thesis, Technische Universität Berlin (2019), Theorem 4.5 and Lemma 4.6.
- D. P. Dailey, "Uniqueness of colorability and colorability of planar 4-regular graphs are NP-complete", Discrete Mathematics 30(3) (1980), 289-293, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(80)90236-8 (the source problem of Cavallaro's reduction).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class hamiltonian ∩ planar (gc_1294), problem 3-Colourability: NP-complete.
