# 3-COLORABILITY OF LINE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem 3-COLORABILITY OF LINE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- I. Holyer, "The NP-completeness of edge-coloring", SIAM Journal on Computing 10(4) (1981), 718-720, doi:10.1137/0210055 (deciding whether a cubic graph has chromatic index 3 is NP-complete; a proper 3-edge-coloring of a graph $H$ is a proper 3-coloring of its line graph).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (the parent problem), and [OPEN5] CHROMATIC INDEX.
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "line" (gc_249), problem 3-Colourability: NP-complete, with the reference to Holyer (1981).
