# GRAPH COLORING ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH COLORING ON PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY (CHROMATIC NUMBER), p. 191, comment ("remains NP-complete ... for K = 3, for planar graphs having no vertex degree exceeding 4").
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and L. Stockmeyer, "Some simplified NP-complete graph problems", Theoretical Computer Science 1(3) (1976), 237-267, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1.
