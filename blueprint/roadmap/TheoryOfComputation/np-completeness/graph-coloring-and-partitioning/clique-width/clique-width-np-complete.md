# CLIQUE-WIDTH is NP-complete

The CLIQUE-WIDTH decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Fellows, F. A. Rosamond, U. Rotics and S. Szeider, "Clique-width is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 23(2) (2009), 909-939, doi:10.1137/070687256.
- definition of $k$-expressions and clique-width: B. Courcelle and S. Olariu, "Upper bounds to the clique width of graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 101(1-3) (2000), 77-114, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(99)00184-5.
