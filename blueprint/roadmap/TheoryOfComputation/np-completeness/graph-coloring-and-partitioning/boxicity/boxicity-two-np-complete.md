# BOXICITY AT MOST 2 is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether it is the intersection graph of a family of closed axis-parallel rectangles in the plane, one per vertex.

## Sources

- J. Kratochvíl, "A special planar satisfiability problem and a consequence of its NP-completeness", Discrete Applied Mathematics 52 (1994), 233-252, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90143-0 (abstract: NP-completeness "of recognizing several classes of intersection graphs of geometric objects in the plane, including grid intersection graphs and graphs of boxicity two").
