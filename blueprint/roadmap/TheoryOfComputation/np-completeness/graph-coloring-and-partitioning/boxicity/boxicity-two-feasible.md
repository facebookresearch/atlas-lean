# Feasible instances of BOXICITY AT MOST 2

An instance $(n, E)$ of BOXICITY AT MOST 2 is feasible when its graph has boxicity at most $2$: every vertex $v$ can be assigned a nonempty closed axis-parallel rectangle $[a_v, b_v] \times [c_v, d_v]$ in the real plane so that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their rectangles intersect. Complete graphs and interval graphs, of boxicity $0$ and $1$, are included.
