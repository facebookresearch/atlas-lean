# FREQUENCY ALLOCATION is NP-complete

The decision problem FREQUENCY ALLOCATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MAXIMUM FREQUENCY ALLOCATION.
- E. Malesińska and A. Panconesi, "On the hardness of allocating frequencies for hybrid networks", Theoret. Comput. Sci. 209 (1998), 347-363, doi:10.1016/S0304-3975(98)00156-X (the channel stability number; Section 1: the problem generalizes list coloring).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT4] GRAPH K-COLORABILITY.
