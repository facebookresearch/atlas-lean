# HIERARCHICAL-TREE CLUSTERING (ℓ₁ ultrametric fitting) is NP-complete

The decision problem HIERARCHICAL-TREE CLUSTERING (ℓ₁ ultrametric fitting) belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Křivánek and J. Morávek, "NP-hard problems in hierarchical-tree clustering", Acta Informatica 23 (1986), 311-323, doi:10.1007/BF00289116.
- M. Křivánek, "The complexity of ultrametric partitions on graphs", Information Processing Letters 27 (1988), 265-270, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(88)90090-7, problem (P6).
