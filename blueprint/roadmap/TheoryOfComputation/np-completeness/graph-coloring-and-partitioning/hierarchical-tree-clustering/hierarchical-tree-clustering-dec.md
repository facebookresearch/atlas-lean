# HIERARCHICAL-TREE CLUSTERING (ℓ₁ ultrametric fitting) as a decision problem

The decision problem HIERARCHICAL-TREE CLUSTERING (ℓ₁ ultrametric fitting) accepts a bitstring
$x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of HIERARCHICAL-TREE
CLUSTERING (ℓ₁ ultrametric fitting) instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](hierarchical-tree-clustering-feasible.md). It
rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
