# Feasible instances of CATERPILLAR ARBORICITY AT MOST 2

An instance $(n, E)$ of CATERPILLAR ARBORICITY AT MOST 2 is feasible when its graph $G$ is the union of two spanning subgraphs $H_1$ and $H_2$ with disjoint edge sets that are both caterpillar forests ($H_2$ may have no edges, so caterpillar forests themselves are included).
