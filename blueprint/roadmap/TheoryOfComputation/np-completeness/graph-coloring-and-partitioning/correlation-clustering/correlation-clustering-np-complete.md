# CORRELATION CLUSTERING is NP-complete

The CORRELATION CLUSTERING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Bansal, A. Blum and S. Chawla, "Correlation clustering", Machine Learning 56 (2004), 89-113, doi:10.1023/B:MACH.0000033116.57574.95, Section 3 (decision version of minimizing disagreements; reduction from PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES).
