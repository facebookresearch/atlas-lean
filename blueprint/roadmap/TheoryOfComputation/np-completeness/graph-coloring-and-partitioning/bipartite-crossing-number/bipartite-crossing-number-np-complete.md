# BIPARTITE CROSSING NUMBER is NP-complete

The BIPARTITE CROSSING NUMBER decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, "Crossing number is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 4(3) (1983), 312-316, doi:10.1137/0604033 (the bipartite crossing number problem for bipartite multigraphs, the intermediate step of the proof, by a transformation from OPTIMAL LINEAR ARRANGEMENT [GT42]).
- C. Buchheim, M. Chimani, C. Gutwenger, M. Jünger and P. Mutzel, "Crossings and planarization", in R. Tamassia (ed.), *Handbook of Graph Drawing and Visualization*, CRC Press (2013), chapter 2, Section 2.3.1, Lemma 2.1 (statement of the problem and a reproduction of the proof).
