# Feasible BIPARTITE CROSSING NUMBER instances

An instance $(V_1, V_2, E, K)$ of BIPARTITE CROSSING NUMBER is feasible when some ordering of $V_1$ and some ordering of $V_2$ give a two-layer drawing with at most $K$ crossings, where the crossings are counted as the ordered pairs of listed edges $((u_1, w_1), (u_2, w_2))$, taken over all pairs of positions in $E$, with $u_1$ before $u_2$ and $w_2$ before $w_1$.
