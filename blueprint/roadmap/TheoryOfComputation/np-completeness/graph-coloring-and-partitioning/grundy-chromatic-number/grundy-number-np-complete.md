# GRUNDY NUMBER is NP-complete

The decision problem GRUNDY NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Zaker, "Results on the Grundy chromatic number of graphs", Discrete Mathematics 306 (2006), 3166-3173, doi:10.1016/j.disc.2005.06.044 (Grundy colorings and `Γ(G)`; deciding `Γ(G) ≥ k` is NP-complete, even for complements of bipartite graphs).
