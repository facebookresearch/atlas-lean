# CHROMATIC INDEX ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST k as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem CHROMATIC INDEX ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST $k$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for CHROMATIC INDEX ON GRAPHS OF THICKNESS AT MOST $k$ and feasible for EDGE COLORING: the entries of $E$ can be given colors below $K$ so that any two distinct entries sharing an endpoint get different colors. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
