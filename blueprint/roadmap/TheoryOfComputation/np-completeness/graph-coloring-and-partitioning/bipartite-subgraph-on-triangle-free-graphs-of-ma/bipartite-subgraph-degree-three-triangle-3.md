# BIPARTITE SUBGRAPH on triangle-free graphs of maximum degree 3 as a decision problem

The BIPARTITE SUBGRAPH on triangle-free graphs of maximum degree 3 decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to a well-formed, feasible instance of BIPARTITE SUBGRAPH on triangle-free graphs of maximum degree 3; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
