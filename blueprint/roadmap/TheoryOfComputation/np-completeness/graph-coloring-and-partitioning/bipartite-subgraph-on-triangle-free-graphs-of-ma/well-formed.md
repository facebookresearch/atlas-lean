# Well-formed BIPARTITE SUBGRAPH on triangle-free graphs of maximum degree 3 instances

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, no vertex of its graph has degree greater than $3$, the graph has no triangle, and $0 < K \le |E|$, the length of the edge list.
