# ACYCLIC COLORING is NP-complete

The decision problem ACYCLIC COLORING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- T. F. Coleman and J.-Y. Cai, "The cyclic coloring problem and estimation of sparse Hessian matrices", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 7(2) (1986), 221-235, doi:10.1137/0607026, Theorem 3.1.
- A. V. Kostochka, *Upper bounds of chromatic functions of graphs* (in Russian), doctoral thesis, Novosibirsk (1978).
