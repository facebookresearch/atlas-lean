# ACYCLIC COLORING as a decision problem

The decision problem ACYCLIC COLORING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for ACYCLIC COLORING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
