# Feasible instances of ACYCLIC COLORING

An instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for ACYCLIC COLORING when the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has an acyclic coloring with the colors $0, \dots, K-1$: a proper coloring in which, for every two colors $i, j$ (possibly equal), the vertices colored $i$ or $j$ induce an acyclic subgraph.
