# MAX CARDINALITY SMTI is NP-complete

The decision problem MAX CARDINALITY SMTI is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Manlove, Irving, Iwama, Miyazaki, and Morita (2002), Lemma 2.1; Iwama, Manlove,
Miyazaki, and Morita (1999).
