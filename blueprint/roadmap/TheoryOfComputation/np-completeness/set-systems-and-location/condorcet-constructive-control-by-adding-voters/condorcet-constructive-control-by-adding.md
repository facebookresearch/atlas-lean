# Instances of CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY ADDING VOTERS

An instance of CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY ADDING VOTERS is a tuple $(m, V, W, c, k)$ of a number $m$ of candidates $0, \dots, m-1$, a list $V$ of registered votes, a list $W$ of unregistered votes (each vote a ranking, most preferred first), a distinguished candidate $c$ and a bound $k$.
