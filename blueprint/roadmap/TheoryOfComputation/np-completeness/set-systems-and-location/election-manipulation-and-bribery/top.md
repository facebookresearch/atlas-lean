# Candidate a ballot counts for while the candidates $R$ remain in SINGLE TRANSFERABLE VOTE MANIPULATION

In SINGLE TRANSFERABLE VOTE MANIPULATION, the candidate a ballot counts for while the candidates in the list $R$ remain is optional: it is the first entry of the ballot that belongs to $R$, the ballot's most preferred remaining candidate, if there is one, and there is no such candidate (the result is none) when no entry of the ballot belongs to $R$. A ballot with no remaining candidate counts for nobody.
