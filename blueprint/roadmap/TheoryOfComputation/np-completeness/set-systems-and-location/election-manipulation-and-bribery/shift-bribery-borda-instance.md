# Instances of SHIFT BRIBERY for Borda

An instance of SHIFT BRIBERY for Borda is a value of type `ℕ × ℕ × List (List ℕ × List ℕ) × ℕ`: the number `m` of candidates, the preferred candidate `p`, one pair per voter (its vote over `0, …, m - 1`, most preferred first, and its finite prices `(ρ(1), …, ρ(j))`, `j` the index of `p` in the vote), and the budget `B`.
