# SECOND-ORDER COPELAND MANIPULATION (unique winner): `p` is the unique second-order Copeland winner

For a profile `P` of the preferences of finitely many voters over finitely many alternatives, the alternative `p` is the unique second-order Copeland winner when every other alternative `q` has a smaller Copeland score than `p`, or the same Copeland score and a smaller second-order Copeland score.
