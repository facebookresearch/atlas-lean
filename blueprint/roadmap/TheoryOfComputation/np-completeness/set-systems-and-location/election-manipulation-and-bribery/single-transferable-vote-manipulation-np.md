# SINGLE TRANSFERABLE VOTE MANIPULATION is NP-complete

The SINGLE TRANSFERABLE VOTE MANIPULATION decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. J. Bartholdi III and J. B. Orlin, "Single transferable vote resists strategic voting", Social Choice and Welfare 8 (1991), 341-354, doi:10.1007/BF00183045 (Theorem 1: EFFECTIVE PREFERENCE is NP-complete under Successive Elimination).
