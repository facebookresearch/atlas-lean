# EXACT COVER is NP-complete

The decision problem EXACT COVER is in NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), EXACT COVER; Richard M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems" (1972).
