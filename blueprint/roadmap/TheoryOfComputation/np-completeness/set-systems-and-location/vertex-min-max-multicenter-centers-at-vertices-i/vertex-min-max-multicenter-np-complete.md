# VERTEX MIN-MAX MULTICENTER (centers at vertices) is NP-complete

The following holds: the decision problem VERTEX MIN-MAX MULTICENTER, in which the centers must be vertices, belongs to the class NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND50] MIN-MAX MULTICENTER, comment, p. 220.
- O. Kariv and S. L. Hakimi, "An algorithmic approach to network location problems. I: The p-centers", *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* 37 (1979), 513-538, doi:10.1137/0137040.
