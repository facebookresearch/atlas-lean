# IWD-STRATEGY-ELIMINATION (eliminability by iterated weak dominance) is NP-complete

The decision problem IWD-STRATEGY-ELIMINATION (eliminability by iterated weak dominance) is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Conitzer and Sandholm (2005), Theorem 1.
