# The threshold decision version of OPTIMAL MIXED STRATEGY TO COMMIT TO in two-player Bayesian games is NP-complete

The OPTIMAL MIXED STRATEGY TO COMMIT TO in two-player Bayesian games decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. Conitzer and T. Sandholm, "Computing the optimal strategy to commit to", Proceedings of the 7th ACM Conference on Electronic Commerce (EC 2006), 82-90, doi:10.1145/1134707.1134717, Sections 2.1, 3.1, Theorem 7 (NP-hardness), and the linear programs in the proofs of Theorems 2 and 8 (adapted below for membership in NP).
