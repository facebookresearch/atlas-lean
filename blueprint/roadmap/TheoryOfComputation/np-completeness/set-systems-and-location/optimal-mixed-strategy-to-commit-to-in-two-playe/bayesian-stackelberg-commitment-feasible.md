# Feasible OPTIMAL MIXED STRATEGY TO COMMIT TO instances

An instance is feasible when some mixed strategy $p$ of the leader (nonnegative weights on the leader actions summing to $1$), together with a choice of follower action $t_\theta$ for every type $\theta$ that is a best response of type $\theta$ to $p$ (no follower action gives type $\theta$ a larger expected utility $\sum_s p_s\, u_f^\theta(s, t)$), gives the leader expected utility $\sum_\theta \pi_\theta \sum_s p_s\, u_l(s, t_\theta)$ at least the threshold, where $\pi_\theta$ is the prior probability of $\theta$.
