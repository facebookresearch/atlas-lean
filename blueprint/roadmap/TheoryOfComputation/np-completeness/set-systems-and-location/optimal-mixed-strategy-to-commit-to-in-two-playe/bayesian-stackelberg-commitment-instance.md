# Instances of OPTIMAL MIXED STRATEGY TO COMMIT TO in Bayesian games

An instance of OPTIMAL MIXED STRATEGY TO COMMIT TO in two-player Bayesian games is a tuple $(m, n, w, U, (U_\theta)_\theta, k)$: the number $m$ of leader actions, the number $n$ of follower actions, a list $w$ of prior weights of the follower types, the leader's utility table $U$ ($m$ rows of $n$ entries), one utility table $U_\theta$ of the follower per type, and a threshold $k$. Utilities are integers, each given by any two natural numbers $(a, b)$ representing $a - b$ (not a canonical split into positive and negative parts: $(2, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$ both represent $1$), and the threshold is a pair $((a, b), d)$ standing for $(a - b)/d$.
