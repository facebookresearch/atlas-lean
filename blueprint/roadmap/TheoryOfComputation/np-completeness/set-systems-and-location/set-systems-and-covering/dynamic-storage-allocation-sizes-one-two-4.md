# DYNAMIC STORAGE ALLOCATION WITH SIZES 1 AND 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem DYNAMIC STORAGE ALLOCATION WITH SIZES 1 AND 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR2] DYNAMIC STORAGE ALLOCATION, p. 226 (comment: NP-complete in the strong sense, even if `s(a) ∈ {1, 2}` for all `a ∈ A`.
- the entry's reference is [Stockmeyer, 1976b], listed by Garey and Johnson as a private communication, which was not available).
