# ENSEMBLE COMPUTATION is NP-complete

The decision problem ENSEMBLE COMPUTATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO9] ENSEMBLE COMPUTATION; the proof is Theorem 3.6 in Section 3.2.2 of the book (local replacement, a transformation from VERTEX COVER).
