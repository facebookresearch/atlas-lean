# ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS as a decision problem

The decision problem ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a good count and a utility matrix, to an instance that is [well-formed](../part-09/well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](envy-free-allocation-feasible.md) for ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
