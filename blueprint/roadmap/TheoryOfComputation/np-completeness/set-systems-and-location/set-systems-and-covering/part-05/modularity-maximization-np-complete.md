# MODULARITY MAXIMIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MODULARITY MAXIMIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- U. Brandes, D. Delling, M. Gaertler, R. Görke, M. Hoefer, Z. Nikoloski and D. Wagner, "On modularity clustering", IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering 20(2) (2008), 172-188, doi:10.1109/TKDE.2007.190689 (arXiv:physics/0608255: Problem 1 (Modularity) and Theorem 3, "Modularity is strongly NP-complete", by a transformation from 3-PARTITION).
