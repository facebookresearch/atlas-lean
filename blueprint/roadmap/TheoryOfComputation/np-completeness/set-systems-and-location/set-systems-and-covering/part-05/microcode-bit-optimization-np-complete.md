# MICROCODE BIT OPTIMIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MICROCODE BIT OPTIMIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO10] MICROCODE BIT OPTIMIZATION.
- E. L. Robertson, "Microcode bit optimization is NP-complete", IEEE Transactions on Computers C-28 (1979), 316-319, doi:10.1109/TC.1979.1675353.
