# 3D ISING SPIN GLASS GROUND STATE is NP-complete

The decision problem 3D ISING SPIN GLASS GROUND STATE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- F. Barahona, "On the computational complexity of Ising spin glass models", Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and General 15 (1982), 3241-3253, doi:10.1088/0305-4470/15/10/028.
