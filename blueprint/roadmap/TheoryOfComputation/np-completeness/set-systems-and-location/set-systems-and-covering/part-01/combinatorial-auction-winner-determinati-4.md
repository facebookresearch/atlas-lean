# COMBINATORIAL AUCTION WINNER DETERMINATION is NP-complete

The decision problem COMBINATORIAL AUCTION WINNER DETERMINATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. H. Rothkopf, A. Pekeč and R. M. Harstad, "Computationally manageable combinational auctions", Management Science 44(8) (1998), 1131-1147, doi:10.1287/mnsc.44.8.1131.
- T. Sandholm, "Algorithm for optimal winner determination in combinatorial auctions", Artificial Intelligence 135(1-2) (2002), 1-54, doi:10.1016/S0004-3702(01)00159-X.
