# BALANCED KNOCKOUT TOURNAMENT FIXING is NP-complete

The decision problem BALANCED KNOCKOUT TOURNAMENT FIXING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Aziz, S. Gaspers, S. Mackenzie, N. Mattei, P. Stursberg and T. Walsh, "Fixing a balanced knockout tournament", Proceedings of the Twenty-Eighth AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI 2014), 552-558, doi:10.1609/aaai.v28i1.8805, Theorem 1.
