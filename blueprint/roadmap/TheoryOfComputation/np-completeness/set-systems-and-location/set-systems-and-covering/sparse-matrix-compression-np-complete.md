# SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION is NP-complete

The decision problem SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR13] SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION (reference: [Even, Lichtenstein, and Shiloach, 1977]; transformation from GRAPH 3-COLORABILITY).
- S. Even, D. I. Lichtenstein and Y. Shiloach, "Remarks on Ziegler's method for matrix compression", unpublished manuscript (1977).
- V. Jugé, D. Köppl, V. Limouzy, A. Marino, J. Olblich, G. Punzi and T. Uno, "Revisiting the sparse matrix compression problem", arXiv:2602.15314 (2026), Section 1 (records the result).
