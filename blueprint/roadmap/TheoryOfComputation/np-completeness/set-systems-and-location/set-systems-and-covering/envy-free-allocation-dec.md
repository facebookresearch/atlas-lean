# ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS as a decision problem

The decision problem ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a good count and a utility matrix, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for ENVY-FREE ALLOCATION OF INDIVISIBLE GOODS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
