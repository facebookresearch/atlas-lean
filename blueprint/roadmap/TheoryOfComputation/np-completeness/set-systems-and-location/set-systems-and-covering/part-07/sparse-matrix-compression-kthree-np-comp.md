# SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION WITH K = 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION WITH K = 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 229, problem [SR13] SPARSE MATRIX COMPRESSION (comment: remains NP-complete for fixed `K = 3`; reference: [Even, Lichtenstein, and Shiloach, 1977]).
- S. Even, D. I. Lichtenstein and Y. Shiloach, "Remarks on Ziegler's method for matrix compression", unpublished manuscript (1977).
- V. Jugé, D. Köppl, V. Limouzy, A. Marino, J. Olblich, G. Punzi and T. Uno, "Revisiting the sparse matrix compression problem", arXiv:2602.15314 (2026), Section 1 (records that the problem is NP-complete even if the maximum needed shift is at most two).
