# 3D ISING SPIN GLASS GROUND STATE as a decision problem

The decision problem 3D ISING SPIN GLASS GROUND STATE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of dimensions, couplings and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](../part-08/well-formed-2-9.md) and [feasible](three-dimensional-ising-spin-glass-feasi.md) for 3D ISING SPIN GLASS GROUND STATE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
