# Feasible instances of COMPONENT SUM AT MOST B WITH THREE PARTS

An instance is feasible when some assignment of the list positions of $C$ onto the parts $0, \dots, K - 1$ (every part receiving at least one position; a vector listed twice occupies two positions) has [component sum](../part-01/component-sum.md) at most $B$. For example, $C = [[1], [1]]$ with $m = 1$ and $K = 2$ splits into two nonempty parts.
