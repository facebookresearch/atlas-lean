# MAXIMUM LIKELIHOOD RANKING is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM LIKELIHOOD RANKING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS11] MAXIMUM LIKELIHOOD RANKING, p. 281, crediting L. Rafsky (1977, private communication) (transformation from FEEDBACK ARC SET.
- NP-complete in the strong sense).
