# Feasible MIN-MAX MULTICENTER instances

A MIN-MAX MULTICENTER instance is feasible when some set $P$ of at most $K$ points of the graph
(vertices or points on edges) satisfies $w(v) \cdot d(v) \le B$ for every vertex $v$, where
$d(v)$ is the distance from $v$ to a closest point of $P$ along the graph and $B = p / q$ is
computed by real division (well-formedness requires $p, q > 0$).
