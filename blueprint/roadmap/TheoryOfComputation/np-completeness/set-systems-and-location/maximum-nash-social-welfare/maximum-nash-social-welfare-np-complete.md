# MAXIMUM NASH SOCIAL WELFARE with additive valuations is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM NASH SOCIAL WELFARE with additive valuations is NP-complete: it
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Ramezani and Endriss (2010) (problem); hardness from PARTITION as in the module
docstring.
