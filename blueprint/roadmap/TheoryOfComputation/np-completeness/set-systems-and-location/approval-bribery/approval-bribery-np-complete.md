# APPROVAL BRIBERY is NP-complete

The APPROVAL BRIBERY decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Faliszewski, E. Hemaspaandra and L. A. Hemaspaandra, "How hard is bribery in elections?", Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research 35 (2009), 485-532, doi:10.1613/jair.2676, Theorem 4.2 (approval-bribery is NP-complete).
