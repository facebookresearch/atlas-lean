# GENERALIZED 0-1 ASSIGNMENT is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED 0-1 ASSIGNMENT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP
has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MINIMUM GENERALIZED 0-1 ASSIGNMENT, citing S. Sahni and T. Gonzalez, "P-complete approximation problems", Journal of the ACM 23(3) (1976), 555-565, doi:10.1145/321958.321975.
- S. Martello and P. Toth, *Knapsack Problems: Algorithms and Computer Implementations*, Wiley (1990), Section 7.1, p. 190 (even the feasibility question of the generalized assignment problem is NP-complete, by a reduction from PARTITION with two knapsacks).
