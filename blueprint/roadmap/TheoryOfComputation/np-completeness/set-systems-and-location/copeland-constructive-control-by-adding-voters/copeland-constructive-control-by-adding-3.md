# COPELAND^α CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY ADDING VOTERS is NP-complete for every rational $0 \le \alpha \le 1$

For every rational number $\alpha$ with $0 \le \alpha \le 1$, the COPELAND^α CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY ADDING VOTERS decision problem with tie value $\alpha$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Faliszewski, E. Hemaspaandra, L. A. Hemaspaandra and J. Rothe, "Llull and Copeland voting computationally resist bribery and constructive control", Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research 35 (2009), 275-341, doi:10.1613/jair.2697 (Definition 2.1; problem E-CCAV, Section 2.3.
- Theorem 4.17: for each rational $\alpha$ with $0 \le \alpha \le 1$, Copeland^α is resistant to constructive control via adding voters in the unique-winner model).
