# QUADRATIC ASSIGNMENT is NP-complete

The decision problem QUADRATIC ASSIGNMENT lies in NP,
and every decision problem in NP reduces to it by a polynomial-time many-one reduction.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), QUADRATIC ASSIGNMENT PROBLEM; Sartaj Sahni and Teofilo F. Gonzalez, "P-complete approximation problems" (1976).
