# EUCLIDEAN LINE COVER (covering lines by points) as a decision problem

The decision problem EUCLIDEAN LINE COVER (covering lines by points) accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of EUCLIDEAN LINE COVER
(covering lines by points) instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](euclidean-line-cover-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to
an instance.
