# Edge `vw` blocks the matching with partner function `μ` in STABLE ROOMMATES WITH TIES (weak stability, complete lists)

For an instance `i` of STABLE ROOMMATES WITH TIES (weak stability, complete lists), the edge `vw` blocks the matching with partner function `μ`: `v ≠ w`, they are not matched to each other, `v` is unmatched or strictly prefers `w` to its partner, and `w` is unmatched or strictly prefers `v` to its partner.
