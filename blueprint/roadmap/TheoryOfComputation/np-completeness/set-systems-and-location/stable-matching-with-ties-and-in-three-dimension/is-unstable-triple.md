# Unstable triples in THREE-DIMENSIONAL STABLE MATCHING

For an instance `i` of THREE-DIMENSIONAL STABLE MATCHING, the triple `abd` is unstable for the marriage given by `μ` and `ν` (woman `a'` is in the family `(a', μ a', ν a')`): the woman `a` prefers `(b, d)` to her partners, the man `b` prefers `(a, d)` to his partners, and the dog `d` prefers `(a, b)` to its partners. A position beyond the end of a list reads as the default entry `[]`.
