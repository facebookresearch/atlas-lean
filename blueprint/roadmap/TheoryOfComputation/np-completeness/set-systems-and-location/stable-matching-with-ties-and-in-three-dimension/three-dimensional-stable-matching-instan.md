# Instances of THREE-DIMENSIONAL STABLE MATCHING

An instance of THREE-DIMENSIONAL STABLE MATCHING is a value of type `ℕ × List (List (ℕ × ℕ)) × List (List (ℕ × ℕ)) × List (List (ℕ × ℕ))`: the number `n` of agents of each gender, then the preference lists of the women (over man-dog pairs `(b, d)`), of the men (over woman-dog pairs `(a, d)`), and of the dogs (over woman-man pairs `(a, b)`), the `k`-th list being agent `k`'s.
