# STABLE ROOMMATES WITH TIES (weak stability, complete lists) is NP-complete

The STABLE ROOMMATES WITH TIES (weak stability, complete lists) decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Ronn, "NP-complete stable matching problems", Journal of Algorithms 11 (1990), 285-304, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(90)90007-2.
