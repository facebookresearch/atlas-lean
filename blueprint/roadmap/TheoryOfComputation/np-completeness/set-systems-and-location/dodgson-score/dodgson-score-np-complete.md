# DODGSON SCORE is NP-complete

The decision problem DODGSON SCORE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. J. Bartholdi III, C. A. Tovey and M. A. Trick, "Voting schemes for which it can be difficult to tell who won the election", Social Choice and Welfare 6 (1989), 157-165, doi:10.1007/BF00303169.
- E. Hemaspaandra, L. A. Hemaspaandra and J. Rothe, "Exact analysis of Dodgson elections: Lewis Carroll's 1876 voting system is complete for parallel access to NP", Journal of the ACM 44 (1997), 806-825, doi:10.1145/268999.269002 (arXiv:cs/9907036), Section 3 (the decision problem DodgsonScore and its NP-completeness, due to Bartholdi, Tovey and Trick).
