# Instances of DODGSON SCORE

An instance of DODGSON SCORE consists of a natural number $m$ of candidates, a natural candidate label $c$, the ballots as arbitrary lists of natural numbers (most preferred first in the usual reading) and a natural bound $k$. At $m = 1$, $c = 1$ and the ballot `[9]` decode; that each ballot is a permutation of the candidates and that $c < m$ are later checks ([validation](well-formed.md)).
