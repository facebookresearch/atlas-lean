# Feasible DODGSON SCORE instances

There is a list of at most $k$ moves $(v, p)$, with $v$ below the number of ballots and $p + 1 < m$ (not $p + 1$ below the length of the ballot), such that applying them left to right by [apply-switch](apply-switch.md) makes $c$ a [Condorcet winner](is-condorcet-winner.md). At $m = 2$, the ballot $[1]$, $c = 0$ and one allowed move turn the ballot into $[0]$; this is not an exchange of two present candidates.
