# PLANAR SUPPORT OF A HYPERGRAPH (PLANAR VENN DIAGRAM) as a decision problem

The decision problem PLANAR SUPPORT OF A HYPERGRAPH (PLANAR VENN DIAGRAM) accepts a bitstring
$x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of PLANAR SUPPORT OF A
HYPERGRAPH (PLANAR VENN DIAGRAM) instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md)
and [feasible](hypergraph-planar-support-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not
decode to an instance.
