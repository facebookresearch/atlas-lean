# Feasible instances of MINIMUM WEIGHT AND/OR GRAPH SOLUTION

An instance of MINIMUM WEIGHT AND/OR GRAPH SOLUTION is feasible when there is a solution subgraph of weight at most $K$: vertices $V'$ and arc positions $A'$ with $s \in V'$, every arc of $A'$ joining vertices of $V'$, all arcs leaving an and-vertex of $V'$ in $A'$, some arc leaving each or-vertex of $V'$ of nonzero out-degree in $A'$, and total weight at most $K$. A vertex with no listed label is treated as an or-vertex.
