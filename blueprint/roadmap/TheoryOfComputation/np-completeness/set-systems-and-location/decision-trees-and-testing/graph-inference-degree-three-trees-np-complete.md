# GRAPH INFERENCE FROM A WALK FOR TREES OF DEGREE AT MOST 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH INFERENCE FROM A WALK FOR TREES OF DEGREE AT MOST 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
