# DECISION TREE (tests with at most three positives) as a decision problem

The decision problem DECISION TREE (tests with at most three positives) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed for DECISION TREE (tests with at most three positives) and feasible for DECISION TREE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
