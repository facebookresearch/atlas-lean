# Instances of PURE NASH EQUILIBRIUM in graphical games (3-bounded neighbourhood)

An instance of PURE NASH EQUILIBRIUM in graphical games (3-bounded neighbourhood) consists of the following data: for each player $p$, the number $|\operatorname{Act}(p)|$ of actions, the list $\operatorname{Neigh}(p)$ of neighbours, and the utility table (combined strategy of $p$ and its neighbours, utility), where a key lists the action of $p$, then those of its neighbours in the listed order.
