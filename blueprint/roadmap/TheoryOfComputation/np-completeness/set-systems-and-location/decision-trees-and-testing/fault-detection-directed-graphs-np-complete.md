# FAULT DETECTION IN DIRECTED GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem FAULT DETECTION IN DIRECTED GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS18] FAULT DETECTION IN DIRECTED GRAPHS, p. 283, crediting T. Ibaraki, T. Kameda and S. Toida, "NP-complete diagnosis problems on systems graphs", unpublished manuscript (1977) (transformation from X3C).
