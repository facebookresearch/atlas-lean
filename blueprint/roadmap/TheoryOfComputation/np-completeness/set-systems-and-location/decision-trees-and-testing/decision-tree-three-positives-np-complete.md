# DECISION TREE (tests with at most three positives) is NP-complete

The decision problem DECISION TREE (tests with at most three positives) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS15] DECISION TREE (comment: remains NP-complete even if each test is $1$ on at most three objects).
- L. Hyafil and R. L. Rivest, "Constructing optimal binary decision trees is NP-complete", Information Processing Letters 5 (1976), 15-17, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(76)90095-8.
