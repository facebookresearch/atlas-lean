# PURE NASH EQUILIBRIUM in graphical games (3-bounded neighbourhood) is NP-complete

The decision problem PURE NASH EQUILIBRIUM in graphical games (3-bounded neighbourhood) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. Gottlob, G. Greco and F. Scarcello, "Pure Nash equilibria: hard and easy games", Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research 24 (2005), 357-406, doi:10.1613/jair.1683, Theorem 3.1.
