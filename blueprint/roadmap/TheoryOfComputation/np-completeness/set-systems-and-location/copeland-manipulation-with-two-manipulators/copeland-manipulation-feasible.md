# Feasible COPELAND^α MANIPULATION instances with two manipulators

For a rational $\alpha$, an instance $(m, V, p)$ (the instance format of UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators) is feasible when two votes $w_1, w_2$, each a permutation of $0, \dots, m-1$, make $p$ a Copeland$^\alpha$ winner of the election with votes $V$ followed by $w_1$ and $w_2$.
