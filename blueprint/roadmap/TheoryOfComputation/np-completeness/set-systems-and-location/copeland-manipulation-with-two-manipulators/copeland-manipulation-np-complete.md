# COPELAND^α MANIPULATION with two manipulators is NP-complete for every rational $\alpha$ with $0 < \alpha < 1$ and $\alpha \ne 1/2$

For every rational number $\alpha$ with $0 < \alpha < 1$ and $\alpha \ne 1/2$, the COPELAND^α MANIPULATION with two manipulators decision problem with tie value $\alpha$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Faliszewski, E. Hemaspaandra and H. Schnoor, "Copeland voting: ties matter", Proceedings of the 7th International Joint Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems (AAMAS 2008), 983-990 (Definition 2.1; the problem E-manipulation, Section 2; Theorem 3.1, proved by Lemmas 3.2 and 3.3 with two manipulative voters).
