# On Path

$v$ lies between $u_1$ and $u_2$: $u_1$ reaches $v$ and $v$ reaches $u_2$ in the directed graph, so $v$ is on a directed walk from $u_1$ to $u_2$. On a well-formed instance the graph is acyclic, and such a walk is a directed path; in general it need not be (with arcs $0 \to 1$, $1 \to 0$, $0 \to 2$, vertex $1$ lies on a walk from $0$ to $2$ but on no path).
