# HOSPITALS/RESIDENTS WITH COUPLES as a decision problem

The decision problem HOSPITALS/RESIDENTS WITH COUPLES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$
decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of HOSPITALS/RESIDENTS WITH COUPLES instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](hospitals-residents-with-couples-feasibl.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
