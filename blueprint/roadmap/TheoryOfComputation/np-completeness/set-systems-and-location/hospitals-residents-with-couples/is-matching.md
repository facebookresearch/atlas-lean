# Is Matching

$M$ is a matching when every single resident is unassigned or assigned to a hospital on its own list, every couple is jointly unassigned or jointly assigned to a pair on its list, and every listed hospital $h$ has the length of its [assigned](assigned.md) list at most its capacity. That length counts repeated resident labels: listing resident $7$ twice and assigning $7$ to hospital $0$ gives length two.
