# Instances of HOSPITALS/RESIDENTS WITH COUPLES

An instance of HOSPITALS/RESIDENTS WITH COUPLES consists of a list of hospital records (a natural capacity $c_j$ and a list of natural resident labels, the preference list), a list of single-resident records (a natural label $r_i$ and a list of natural hospital labels) and a list of couple records (a pair of natural labels and a list of natural pairs of hospital labels). The labels are raw: a single may list hospital $7$ when no hospitals are recorded. Membership of the labels is checked separately by [validation](well-formed.md).
