# CIRCULAR BLOCK MINIMIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem CIRCULAR BLOCK MINIMIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR17] CONSECUTIVE BLOCK MINIMIZATION, p. 230, comment: "Remains NP-complete if "j = n" is replaced by "j = n and b_i1 = 0" [Booth, 1975]".
- K. S. Booth, *PQ-tree algorithms*, Ph.D. thesis, University of California, Berkeley (1975), as cited by Garey and Johnson (the thesis itself was not available).
