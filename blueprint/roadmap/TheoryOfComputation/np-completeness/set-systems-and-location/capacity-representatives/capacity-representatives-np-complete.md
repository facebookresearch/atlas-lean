# CAPACITY REPRESENTATIVES is NP-complete

The CAPACITY REPRESENTATIVES decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, MAXIMUM CAPACITY REPRESENTATIVES.
- M. Bellare, "Interactive proofs and approximation: reductions from two provers in one round", Proceedings of the 2nd Israel Symposium on Theory and Computing Systems (ISTCS 1993), 266-274, doi:10.1109/ISTCS.1993.253462.
