# MONROE MULTIWINNER (approval misrepresentation) is NP-complete

The decision problem MONROE MULTIWINNER (approval misrepresentation) is NP-complete: it belongs
to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Procaccia, Rosenschein and Zohar (2008); Betzler, Slinko and Uhlmann (2013), Theorem 1.
