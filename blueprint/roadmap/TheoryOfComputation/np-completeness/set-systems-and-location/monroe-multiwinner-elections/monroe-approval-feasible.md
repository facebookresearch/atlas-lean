# Feasible MONROE MULTIWINNER (approval misrepresentation) instances

A MONROE MULTIWINNER instance $(m, V, k, R)$ is feasible when some committee $C'$ of exactly $k$
candidates and some assignment $w$ of the voters onto $C'$ (every voter assigned to a member of
$C'$, every member receiving at least one voter) respect the Monroe criterion and have total
misrepresentation at most $R$. The Monroe criterion asks every member of $C'$ to receive at
least $\lfloor |V| / k \rfloor$ and at most
$\lfloor (|V| + k - 1) / k \rfloor = \lceil |V| / k \rceil$ voters, in natural-number division.
