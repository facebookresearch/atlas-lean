# CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY DELETING VOTERS is NP-complete

The CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY DELETING VOTERS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. J. Bartholdi III, C. A. Tovey, and M. A. Trick, "How hard is it to control an election?", Mathematical and Computer Modelling 16 (1992), 27-40, doi:10.1016/0895-7177(92)90085-Y.
- E. Hemaspaandra, L. A. Hemaspaandra, and J. Rothe, "Anyone but him: The complexity of precluding an alternative", Proceedings of the 20th National Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI 2005), 95-101 (Table 1 records the Bartholdi-Tovey-Trick result).
