# Instances of CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY DELETING VOTERS

An instance of CONDORCET CONSTRUCTIVE CONTROL BY DELETING VOTERS is a tuple $(m, V, c, k)$ of a number $m$ of candidates $0, \dots, m-1$, a list $V$ of votes (each a ranking, most preferred first), a distinguished candidate $c$ and a bound $k$.
