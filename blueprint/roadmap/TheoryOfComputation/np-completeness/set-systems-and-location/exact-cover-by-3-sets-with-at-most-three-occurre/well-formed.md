# Well-formed EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element instances

A EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element instance is well-formed when
it is a well-formed X3C instance, every member of `C` is listed in increasing order, no member
is listed twice, and every element of `X` occurs in at most three members of `C`.
