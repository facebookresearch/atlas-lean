# EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element as a decision problem

The decision problem EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](../exact-cover-by-3-sets/exact-cover-by3-sets-feasible.md). It rejects every
bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
