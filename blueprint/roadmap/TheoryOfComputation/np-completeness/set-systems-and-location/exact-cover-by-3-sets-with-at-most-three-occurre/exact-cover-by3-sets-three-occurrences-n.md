# EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element is NP-complete

The decision problem EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS with at most three occurrences per element belongs to
NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 221, problem [SP2] EXACT COVER BY 3-SETS (X3C) (comment: remains NP-complete if no element occurs in more than three subsets [Garey and Johnson, unpublished]).
- T. F. Gonzalez, "Clustering to minimize the maximum intercluster distance", Theoretical Computer Science 38 (1985), 293-306, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(85)90224-5 (NP-completeness when every element occurs in exactly three subsets).
