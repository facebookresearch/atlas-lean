# Feasible instances of MAXIMUM COVERAGE LOCATION ON A NETWORK

An instance of MAXIMUM COVERAGE LOCATION ON A NETWORK is feasible when some set $P$ of at most $p$ points of $G$ covers customers of total gain at least $W$, a customer $v$ being covered when some point of $P$ is at distance less than $r(v)$ from $v$. An edge's length is read from the first entry of the edge list with the same endpoints, and is $0$ when no entry has them. A vertex with no listed customer data has gain $0$ and radius $0$, and a listed customer $(w, a, 0)$ has radius $0$.
