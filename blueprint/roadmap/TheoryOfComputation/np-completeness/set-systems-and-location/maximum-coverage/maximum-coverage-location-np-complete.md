# MAXIMUM COVERAGE LOCATION ON A NETWORK is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM COVERAGE LOCATION ON A NETWORK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Megiddo, E. Zemel, and S. L. Hakimi, "The maximum coverage location problem", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 4 (1983), 253-261, doi:10.1137/0604028.
