# MIN-SUM MULTICENTER with unit weights and lengths on planar graphs of maximum degree 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem MIN-SUM MULTICENTER with unit weights and lengths on planar graphs of
maximum degree 3 is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND51], comment; Kariv and Hakimi (1979).
