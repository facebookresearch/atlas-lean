# MIN-SUM MULTICENTER is NP-complete

The decision problem MIN-SUM MULTICENTER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND51] MIN-SUM MULTICENTER.
- O. Kariv and S. L. Hakimi, "An algorithmic approach to network location problems. II: The p-medians", SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 37 (1979), 539-560, doi:10.1137/0137041.
