# K-FACILITY LOCATION is NP-complete

The decision problem K-FACILITY LOCATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann, *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM K-FACILITY LOCATION.
