# EUCLIDEAN p-CENTER is NP-complete

The decision problem EUCLIDEAN p-CENTER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Megiddo and K. J. Supowit, "On the complexity of some common geometric location problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 13 (1984), 182-196, doi:10.1137/0213014.
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM GEOMETRIC DISK COVER.
