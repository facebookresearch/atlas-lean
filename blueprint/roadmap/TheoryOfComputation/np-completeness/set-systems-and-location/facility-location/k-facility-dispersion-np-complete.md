# K-FACILITY DISPERSION is NP-complete

The decision problem K-FACILITY DISPERSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann, *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM K-FACILITY DISPERSION.
- S. S. Ravi, D. J. Rosenkrantz, and G. K. Tayi, "Heuristic and special case algorithms for dispersion problems", Operations Research 42 (1994), 299-310, doi:10.1287/opre.42.2.299.
