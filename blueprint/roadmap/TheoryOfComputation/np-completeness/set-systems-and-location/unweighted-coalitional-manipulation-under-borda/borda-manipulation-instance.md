# Instances of UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators

An instance of UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators is a triple $(m, V, c)$ of a number $m$ of candidates $0, \dots, m-1$, a list $V$ of nonmanipulative votes (each a ranking, most preferred first) and a distinguished candidate $c$.
