# UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators is NP-complete

The UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
