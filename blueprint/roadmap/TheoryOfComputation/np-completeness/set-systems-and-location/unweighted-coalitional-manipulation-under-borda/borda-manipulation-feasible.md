# Feasible UNWEIGHTED COALITIONAL MANIPULATION under Borda with two manipulators instances

An instance $(m, V, c)$ is feasible when two votes $w_1, w_2$ over the $m$ candidates make $c$ a Borda winner of the election with votes $V$ together with $w_1$ and $w_2$: no candidate $d < m$ has a larger Borda score than $c$.
