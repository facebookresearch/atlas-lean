# CONSTRUCTIVE COALITIONAL WEIGHTED MANIPULATION for Borda with three candidates is NP-complete

The CONSTRUCTIVE COALITIONAL WEIGHTED MANIPULATION for Borda with three candidates decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. Conitzer, T. Sandholm and J. Lang, "When are elections with few candidates hard to manipulate?", Journal of the ACM 54(3) (2007), Article 14, doi:10.1145/1236457.1236461, Definition 1, Theorem 6 and Corollary 1 (reduction from PARTITION).
