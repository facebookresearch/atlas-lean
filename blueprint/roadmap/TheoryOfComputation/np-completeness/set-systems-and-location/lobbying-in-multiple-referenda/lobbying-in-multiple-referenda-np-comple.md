# LOBBYING IN MULTIPLE REFERENDA is NP-complete

The decision problem LOBBYING IN MULTIPLE REFERENDA is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Christian, Fellows, Rosamond, and Slinko (2007); Erdélyi, Fernau, Goldsmith, Mattei,
Raible, and Rothe (arXiv:0906.4431).
