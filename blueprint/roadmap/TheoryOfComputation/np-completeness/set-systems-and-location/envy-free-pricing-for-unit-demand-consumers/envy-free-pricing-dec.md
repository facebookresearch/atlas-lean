# ENVY-FREE PRICING for unit-demand consumers with unlimited supply as a decision problem

The decision problem ENVY-FREE PRICING for unit-demand consumers with unlimited supply accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of ENVY-FREE
PRICING for unit-demand consumers with unlimited supply instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](envy-free-pricing-feasible.md). It rejects every
bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
