# Envy Free Pricing Instance

An instance consists of a natural number $m$ of items, one arbitrary list of natural valuations per consumer (read as $[v_i(0), \dots, v_i(m-1)]$) and a natural revenue bound $R$. Even at $m = 1$ an empty row is representable; exactly $m$ entries per row is a condition of [validation](well-formed.md).
