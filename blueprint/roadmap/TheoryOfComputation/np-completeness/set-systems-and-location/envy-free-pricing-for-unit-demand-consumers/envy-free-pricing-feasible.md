# Envy Free Pricing Feasible

There is a natural price for each item and, independently for each consumer, a choice from that consumer's [demand set](in-demand-set.md): no purchase, or one item. Several consumers may choose the same item; the seller's revenue sums the price of the chosen item once per purchasing consumer (zero for no purchase), and it must be at least $R$. Two buyers who value the only item at $3$ can both buy it at price $3$, for a revenue of $6$; a one-copy interpretation cannot reach that.
