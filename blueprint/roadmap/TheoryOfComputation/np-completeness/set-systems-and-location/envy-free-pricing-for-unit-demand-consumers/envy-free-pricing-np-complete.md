# ENVY-FREE PRICING for unit-demand consumers with unlimited supply is NP-complete

The decision problem ENVY-FREE PRICING for unit-demand consumers with unlimited supply belongs
to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. Guruswami, J. D. Hartline, A. R. Karlin, D. Kempe, C. Kenyon and F. McSherry, "On profit-maximizing envy-free pricing", Proceedings of the Sixteenth Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2005), 1164-1173, doi:10.5555/1070432.1070598 (Section 2, Definitions 2.1 and 2.2; Section 1: the problems are "not only NP-complete, but APX-hard"; Theorem 3.1: the unit-demand problem is APX-hard even with unlimited supply, by a reduction from VERTEX COVER).
