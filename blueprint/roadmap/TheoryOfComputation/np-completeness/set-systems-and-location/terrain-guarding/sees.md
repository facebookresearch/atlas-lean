# Visibility between two points in TERRAIN GUARDING

For an instance of TERRAIN GUARDING, a point $p$ of the real plane sees a point $q$ when the closed segment $pq$ is never strictly below the [terrain](terrain.md): for every point $z$ of the segment $pq$ and every point $t$ of the terrain with the same $x$-coordinate as $z$, the $y$-coordinate of $t$ is at most that of $z$.
