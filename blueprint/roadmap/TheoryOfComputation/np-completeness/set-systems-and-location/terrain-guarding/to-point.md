# Point of the plane given by a pair of rational codes in TERRAIN GUARDING

In TERRAIN GUARDING, a pair $(p_1, p_2)$ of [rational codes](rat-code.md) gives the point $(\operatorname{val}(p_1), \operatorname{val}(p_2))$ of the real plane $\mathbb{R}^2$, where $\operatorname{val}$ is the [value of a rational code](val.md). A coordinate whose code has denominator $0$ becomes $0$; positive denominators for the vertices, guards and points to be guarded are required separately by [well-formedness](well-formed.md).
