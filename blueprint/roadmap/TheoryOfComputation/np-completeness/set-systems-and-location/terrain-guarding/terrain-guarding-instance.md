# Instances of TERRAIN GUARDING

An instance of TERRAIN GUARDING is a quadruple $(V, G, N, K)$ of three lists of pairs of [rational codes](rat-code.md) and a natural number $K$, the bound. Each pair gives the $x$- and $y$-coordinates of a [point](to-point.md); the list $V$ gives the terrain vertices $v_1, \dots, v_n$ in order, $G$ the candidate guards, and $N$ the points to be guarded.
