# Well-formed instances of 3-DIMENSIONAL MATCHING with at most three occurrences per element

An instance of 3-DIMENSIONAL MATCHING with at most three occurrences per element is well formed when it is a well-formed 3-DIMENSIONAL MATCHING instance (every coordinate below `q`) whose triples are distinct, and in which every element of `W`, of `X` and of `Y` occurs in at most three triples.
