# COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT with unit weights is NP-complete

The COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT with unit weights decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SP10] COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT, p. 223, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if all subsets in R and S have weight 1 [Garey and Johnson, ——]" (unpublished; the result and its attribution are those recorded by Garey and Johnson).
