# BANKS WINNER is NP-complete

The BANKS WINNER decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. J. Woeginger, "Banks winners in tournaments are difficult to recognize", Social Choice and Welfare 20 (2003), 523-528, doi:10.1007/s003550200197 (deciding whether a given vertex is a Banks winner is NP-complete; reduction from graph 3-colorability).
- F. Brandt, F. Fischer and P. Harrenstein, "Recognizing members of the tournament equilibrium set is NP-hard", arXiv:0711.2961 (2007), Definition 1 (the Banks set) and Section 4 (an alternative proof of Woeginger's result, by a reduction from 3SAT).
