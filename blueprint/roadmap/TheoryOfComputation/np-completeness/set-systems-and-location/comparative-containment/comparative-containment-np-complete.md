# COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT is NP-complete

The COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SP10] COMPARATIVE CONTAINMENT, p. 223 ("Reference: [Plaisted, 1976]. Transformation from VERTEX COVER.").
- D. A. Plaisted, "Some polynomial and integer divisibility problems are NP-hard", Proc. 17th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 1976), 264-267, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1976.29.
