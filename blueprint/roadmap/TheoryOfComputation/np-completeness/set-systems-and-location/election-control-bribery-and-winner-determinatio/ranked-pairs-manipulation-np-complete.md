# RANKED PAIRS MANIPULATION (single manipulator, co-winner) is NP-complete

RANKED PAIRS MANIPULATION (single manipulator, co-winner) is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Xia, Zuckerman, Procaccia, Conitzer and Rosenschein (2009), Theorem 4.1; Xia, Conitzer, Procaccia and Rosenschein (2008), Theorem 2.
