# PLURALITY-WEIGHTED-PRICED BRIBERY is NP-complete

PLURALITY-WEIGHTED-PRICED BRIBERY is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Faliszewski, Hemaspaandra and Hemaspaandra (2009), Theorem 3.2.
