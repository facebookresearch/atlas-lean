# Feasible instances of RANKED PAIRS MANIPULATION (single manipulator, co-winner)

In RANKED PAIRS MANIPULATION: some vote of the manipulator (the last voter) makes `p` a ranked-pairs winner, the other voters voting their sincere orders (voter `k` prefers `a` to `b` exactly when `a` comes before `b` in the `k`-th vote): `p` is at the top for some allowed order of consideration.
