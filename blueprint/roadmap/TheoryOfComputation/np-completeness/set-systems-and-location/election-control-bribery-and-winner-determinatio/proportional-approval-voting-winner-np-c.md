# PROPORTIONAL APPROVAL VOTING WINNER DETERMINATION is NP-complete

PROPORTIONAL APPROVAL VOTING WINNER DETERMINATION is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Aziz, Gaspers, Gudmundsson, Mackenzie, Mattei and Walsh (2015), Theorem 2 and Corollary 1.
