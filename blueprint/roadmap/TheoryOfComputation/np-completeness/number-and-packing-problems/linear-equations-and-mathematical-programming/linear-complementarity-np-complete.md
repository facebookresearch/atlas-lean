# LINEAR COMPLEMENTARITY is NP-complete

The decision problem LINEAR COMPLEMENTARITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. J. Chung, "NP-completeness of the linear complementarity problem", Journal of Optimization Theory and Applications 60 (1989), 393-399, doi:10.1007/BF00940344.
