# UNSATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM is NP-complete

The decision problem UNSATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM UNSATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM (the optimization problem whose decision version is stated here).
- E. Amaldi and V. Kann, "The complexity and approximability of finding maximum feasible subsystems of linear relations", Theoretical Computer Science 147 (1995), 181-210, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)00254-G (MAX FLS with equations is NP-hard).
- E. Amaldi and V. Kann, "On the approximability of minimizing nonzero variables or unsatisfied relations in linear systems", Theoretical Computer Science 209 (1998), 237-260, doi:10.1016/S0304-3975(97)00115-1 (approximability of Min ULR).
