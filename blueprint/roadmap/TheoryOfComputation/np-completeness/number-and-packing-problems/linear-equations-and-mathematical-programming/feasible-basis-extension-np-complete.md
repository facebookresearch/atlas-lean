# FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION is NP-complete

The decision problem FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP4] FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION.
- K. G. Murty, "A fundamental problem in linear inequalities with applications to the travelling salesman problem", Mathematical Programming 2 (1972), 296-308, doi:10.1007/BF01584550.
