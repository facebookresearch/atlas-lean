# MAXIMUM SATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM SATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM SATISFYING LINEAR SUBSYSTEM (decision version: at least `K` equations satisfied).
- E. Amaldi and V. Kann, "The complexity and approximability of finding maximum feasible subsystems of linear relations", Theoretical Computer Science 147 (1995), 181-210, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)00254-G.
