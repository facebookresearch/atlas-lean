# MINIMUM WEIGHT SOLUTION TO LINEAR EQUATIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM WEIGHT SOLUTION TO LINEAR EQUATIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP5] MINIMUM WEIGHT SOLUTION TO LINEAR EQUATIONS (a result of Garey and Johnson, first published there).
- E. Amaldi and V. Kann, "On the approximability of minimizing nonzero variables or unsatisfied relations in linear systems", Theoretical Computer Science 209 (1998), 237-260, doi:10.1016/S0304-3975(97)00115-1 (records the NP-hardness of this problem, Min RVLS with equations, citing Garey and Johnson 1979).
