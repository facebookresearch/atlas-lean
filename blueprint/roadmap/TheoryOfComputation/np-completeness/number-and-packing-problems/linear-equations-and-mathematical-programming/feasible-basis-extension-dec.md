# FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION as a decision problem

The decision problem FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples (two dimensions, a matrix, a vector and a column list), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) and [feasible](feasible-basis-extension-feasible.md) for FEASIBLE BASIS EXTENSION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
