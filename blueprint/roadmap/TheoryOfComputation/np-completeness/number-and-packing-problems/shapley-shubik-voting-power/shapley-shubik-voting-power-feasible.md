# Feasible instances of SHAPLEY-SHUBIK VOTING POWER

An instance of SHAPLEY-SHUBIK VOTING POWER is feasible when some ordering `π` of the voters has `v₁` (voter `0`) at a position `j` where the votes of the first `j` voters total less than `q` and the votes of the first `j + 1` voters total at least `q`. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both. A position beyond the end of a list reads as the default entry `0`.
