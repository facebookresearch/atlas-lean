# EXACT-WEIGHT CODEWORD is NP-complete

The decision problem EXACT-WEIGHT CODEWORD belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS7] DECODING OF LINEAR CODES (comment: the variant asking for exactly `K` 1's is NP-complete, even for fixed `y = (0, ..., 0)`).
- E. R. Berlekamp, R. J. McEliece, and H. C. A. van Tilborg, "On the inherent intractability of certain coding problems", IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 24 (1978), 384-386, doi:10.1109/TIT.1978.1055873.
