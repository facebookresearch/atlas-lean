# Well-formed instances of FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM

A DECODING OF LINEAR CODES instance $(\alpha, \beta, K)$ is well-formed for FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM when it is well-formed for DECODING OF LINEAR CODES, $\beta$ has $m > 0$ coordinates, and $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n, \beta$ are pairwise distinct.
