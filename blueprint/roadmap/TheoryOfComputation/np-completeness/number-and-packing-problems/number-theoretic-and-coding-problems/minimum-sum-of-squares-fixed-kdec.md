# MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES WITH K SETS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES WITH K SETS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a size list and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](minimum-sum-of-squares-fixed-kfeasible.md) for $K$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
