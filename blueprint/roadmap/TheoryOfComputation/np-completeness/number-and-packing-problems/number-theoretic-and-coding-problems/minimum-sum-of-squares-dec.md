# MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(s, K, J)$ of a size list and two natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-10.md) and [feasible](minimum-sum-of-squares-feasible.md) for MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
