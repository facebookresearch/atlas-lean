# SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q) is NP-complete

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF[Q] (decision version).
- J. Håstad, S. Phillips and S. Safra, "A well-characterized approximation problem", Information Processing Letters 47 (1993), 301-305, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(93)90076-L, Theorem 4.2 (for every fixed prime `p`, deciding whether all equations of an instance can be satisfied simultaneously is NP-complete).
