# MINIMUM SUM OF α-TH POWERS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $\alpha$. The decision problem MINIMUM SUM OF α-TH POWERS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(s, K, J)$ of a size list and two natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and [feasible](minimum-sum-of-alpha-powers-feasible.md) for $\alpha$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
