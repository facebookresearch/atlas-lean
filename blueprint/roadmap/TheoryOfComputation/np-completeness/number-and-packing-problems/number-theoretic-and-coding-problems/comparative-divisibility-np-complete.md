# COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN4] COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY.
- D. A. Plaisted, "Some polynomial and integer divisibility problems are NP-hard", Proc. 17th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (1976), 264-267, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1976.29; journal version SIAM Journal on Computing 7 (1978), 458-464, doi:10.1137/0207036.
