# FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM as a decision problem

The decision problem FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(A, y, K)$ of the rows of a binary matrix, a binary vector and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) for FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM and feasible for DECODING OF LINEAR CODES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
