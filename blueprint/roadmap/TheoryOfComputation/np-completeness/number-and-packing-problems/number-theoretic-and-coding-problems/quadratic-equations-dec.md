# SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q) as a decision problem

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(q, n, P, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-17.md) and [feasible](quadratic-equations-feasible.md) for SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
