# Feasible instances of MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES

An instance is feasible when some assignment of the elements of $A$ to the bins $0, \dots, K - 1$, not necessarily onto (bins may be empty, an empty bin contributing $0$), has sum of squares $\sum_j (\sum_{a \in A_j} s(a))^2$ at most $J$. For example, one element of size $1$, two bins and $J = 1$ form a feasible instance. Well-formedness separately requires $K \le |A|$.
