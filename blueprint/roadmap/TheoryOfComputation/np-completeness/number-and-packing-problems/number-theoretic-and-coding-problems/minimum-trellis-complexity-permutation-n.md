# MINIMUM TRELLIS COMPLEXITY UNDER COORDINATE PERMUTATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM TRELLIS COMPLEXITY UNDER COORDINATE PERMUTATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Kashyap, "Matroid pathwidth and code trellis complexity", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 22 (2008), 256-272, doi:10.1137/070691152 (Corollary 3.3 and Section 4; the problem is "Trellis State-Complexity" of Vardy and "Maximum Partition Rank Permutation" of G. B. Horn and F. R. Kschischang, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 42 (1996), 2042-2048, doi:10.1109/18.556701, who conjectured it NP-hard).
