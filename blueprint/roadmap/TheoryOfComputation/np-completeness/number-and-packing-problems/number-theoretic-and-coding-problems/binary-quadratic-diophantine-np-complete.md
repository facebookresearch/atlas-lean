# BINARY QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem BINARY QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. C. Lagarias, "Succinct certificates for the solvability of binary quadratic Diophantine equations", Proceedings of the 20th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 1979), 47-54, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1979.40 (full version arXiv:math/0611209, Theorem 1.2(ii): the set of `a, b, c, d, e, f ∈ ℤ` such that `a x₁² + b x₁ x₂ + c x₂² + d x₁ + e x₂ + f = 0` has a solution in nonnegative integers is in NP, and with Manders and Adleman's result "testing membership in the sets Σ₁ and Σ₂ in Theorem 1.2 are both NP-complete").
- K. L. Manders and L. Adleman, "NP-complete decision problems for binary quadratics", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 16 (1978), 168-184, doi:10.1016/0022-0000(78)90044-2 (the special form `α x² + β y - γ = 0` with natural-number solutions is NP-complete).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN8] QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS (the special form).
