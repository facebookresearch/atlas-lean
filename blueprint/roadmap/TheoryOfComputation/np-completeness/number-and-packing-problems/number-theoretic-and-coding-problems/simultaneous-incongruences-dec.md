# SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES as a decision problem

The decision problem SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of pairs, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-20.md) and [feasible](simultaneous-incongruences-feasible.md) for SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
