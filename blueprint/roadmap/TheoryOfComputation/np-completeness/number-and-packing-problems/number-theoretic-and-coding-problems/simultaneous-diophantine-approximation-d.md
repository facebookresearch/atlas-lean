# GOOD SIMULTANEOUS DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATION as a decision problem

The decision problem GOOD SIMULTANEOUS DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a list of rational codes, a bound and a pair, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-18.md) and [feasible](simultaneous-diophantine-approximation-f.md) for GOOD SIMULTANEOUS DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
