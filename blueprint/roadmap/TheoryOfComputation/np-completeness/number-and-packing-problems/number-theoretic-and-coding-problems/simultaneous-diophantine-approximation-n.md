# GOOD SIMULTANEOUS DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATION is NP-complete

The decision problem GOOD SIMULTANEOUS DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. C. Lagarias, "The computational complexity of simultaneous Diophantine approximation problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 14 (1985), 196-209, doi:10.1137/0214016.
- F. Eisenbrand and T. Rothvoß, "Recent hardness results for periodic uni-processor scheduling", Dagstuhl Seminar Proceedings 10071, Scheduling (2010), Section 2, problem (1).
