# MINIMUM SUM OF α-TH POWERS is NP-complete

For every natural number $\alpha \ge 2$, the decision problem MINIMUM SUM OF α-TH POWERS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SP19] MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES, p. 225, comment: NP-completeness is preserved if the exponent `2` is replaced by any fixed rational `α > 1` (stated here for the integer exponents `α ≥ 2`).
