# BINARY QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem BINARY QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples of encoded integers, to an instance that is [feasible](binary-quadratic-diophantine-feasible.md) for BINARY QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS (no well-formedness condition is imposed). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
