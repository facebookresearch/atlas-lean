# DECODING OF LINEAR CODES is NP-complete

The decision problem DECODING OF LINEAR CODES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS7] DECODING OF LINEAR CODES.
- E. R. Berlekamp, R. J. McEliece, and H. C. A. van Tilborg, "On the inherent intractability of certain coding problems", IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 24 (1978), 384-386, doi:10.1109/TIT.1978.1055873 (COSET WEIGHTS).
