# QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES is NP-complete

The decision problem QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN1] QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES.
- K. L. Manders and L. M. Adleman, "NP-complete decision problems for binary quadratics", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 16 (1978), 168-184, doi:10.1016/0022-0000(78)90044-2.
