# NON-DIVISIBILITY OF A PRODUCT POLYNOMIAL as a decision problem

The decision problem NON-DIVISIBILITY OF A PRODUCT POLYNOMIAL accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of sparse polynomials and a natural number, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for NON-DIVISIBILITY OF A PRODUCT POLYNOMIAL. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
