# COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of two lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](comparative-divisibility-feasible.md) for COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
