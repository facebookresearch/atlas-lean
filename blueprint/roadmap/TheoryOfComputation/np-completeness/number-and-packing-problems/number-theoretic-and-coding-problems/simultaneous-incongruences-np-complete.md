# SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES is NP-complete

The decision problem SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN2] SIMULTANEOUS INCONGRUENCES.
- L. J. Stockmeyer and A. R. Meyer, "Word problems requiring exponential time (preliminary report)", Proceedings of the 5th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1973), 1-9, doi:10.1145/800125.804029.
