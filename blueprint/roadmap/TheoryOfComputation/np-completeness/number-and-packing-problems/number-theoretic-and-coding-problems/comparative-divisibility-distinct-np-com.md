# COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY WITH DISTINCT ENTRIES is NP-complete

The decision problem COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY WITH DISTINCT ENTRIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN4] COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY, p. 249, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if all `aᵢ` are different and all `bⱼ` are different", credited there to Garey and Johnson (unpublished; no published proof was available). The general problem is due to D. A. Plaisted, "Some polynomial and integer divisibility problems are NP-hard", SIAM Journal on Computing 7 (1978), 458-464, doi:10.1137/0207036.
