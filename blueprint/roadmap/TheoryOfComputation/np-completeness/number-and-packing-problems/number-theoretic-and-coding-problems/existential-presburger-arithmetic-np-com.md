# EXISTENTIAL PRESBURGER ARITHMETIC is NP-complete

The decision problem EXISTENTIAL PRESBURGER ARITHMETIC belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. Scarpellini, "Complexity of subcases of Presburger arithmetic", Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 284 (1984), 203-218, doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-1984-0742421-9 (Section 7, Corollary 1, and Fact 1 of Section 5).
