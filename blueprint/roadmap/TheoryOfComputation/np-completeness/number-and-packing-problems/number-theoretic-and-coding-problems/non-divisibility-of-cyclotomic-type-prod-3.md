# NON-DIVISIBILITY OF CYCLOTOMIC-TYPE PRODUCTS as a decision problem

The decision problem NON-DIVISIBILITY OF CYCLOTOMIC-TYPE PRODUCTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of two lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and [feasible](non-divisibility-of-cyclotomic-type-prod-2.md) for NON-DIVISIBILITY OF CYCLOTOMIC-TYPE PRODUCTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
