# Instances of SATISFIABILITY OF QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF(q)

An instance is a quadruple of a natural number $q$ (primality is a separate test of [well-formedness](well-formed-17.md); coefficients are read in $\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}$), a number $n$ of variables, polynomials given as lists of terms (coefficient, variable-index list), and a bound $K$.
