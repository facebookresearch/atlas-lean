# FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM is NP-complete

The decision problem FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column", ACM Transactions on Algorithms 1 (2005), 160-176, doi:10.1145/1077464.1077476 (statement of FINITE-FIELD SUBSET SUM).
- A. Vardy, "The intractability of computing the minimum distance of a code", IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 43 (1997), 1757-1766, doi:10.1109/18.641542.
