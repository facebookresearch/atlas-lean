# NON-DIVISIBILITY OF CYCLOTOMIC-TYPE PRODUCTS is NP-complete

The decision problem NON-DIVISIBILITY OF CYCLOTOMIC-TYPE PRODUCTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
