# QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES WITH A GIVEN FACTORIZATION as a decision problem

The decision problem QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES WITH A GIVEN FACTORIZATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple and a factorization list, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-14.md) for QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES WITH A GIVEN FACTORIZATION and whose $(a, b, c)$ is feasible for QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
