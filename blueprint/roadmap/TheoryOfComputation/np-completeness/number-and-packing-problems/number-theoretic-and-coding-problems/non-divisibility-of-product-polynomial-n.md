# NON-DIVISIBILITY OF A PRODUCT POLYNOMIAL is NP-complete

The decision problem NON-DIVISIBILITY OF A PRODUCT POLYNOMIAL belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
