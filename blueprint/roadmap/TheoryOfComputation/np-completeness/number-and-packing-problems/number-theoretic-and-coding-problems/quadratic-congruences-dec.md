# QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES as a decision problem

The decision problem QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(a, b, c)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-15.md) and [feasible](quadratic-congruences-feasible.md) for QUADRATIC CONGRUENCES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
