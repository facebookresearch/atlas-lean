# MINIMUM TRELLIS COMPLEXITY UNDER COORDINATE PERMUTATION as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM TRELLIS COMPLEXITY UNDER COORDINATE PERMUTATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, G, w)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-11.md) and [feasible](minimum-trellis-complexity-permutation-f.md) for MINIMUM TRELLIS COMPLEXITY UNDER COORDINATE PERMUTATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
