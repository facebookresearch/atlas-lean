# QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(a, b, c)$, to an instance that is well-formed and [feasible](quadratic-diophantine-equations-feasible.md) for QUADRATIC DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
