# COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY WITH DISTINCT ENTRIES as a decision problem

The decision problem COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY WITH DISTINCT ENTRIES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of two lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) for COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY WITH DISTINCT ENTRIES and feasible for COMPARATIVE DIVISIBILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
