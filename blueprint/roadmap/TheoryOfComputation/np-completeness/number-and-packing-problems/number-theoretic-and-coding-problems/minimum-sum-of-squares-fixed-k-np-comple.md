# MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES WITH K SETS is NP-complete

For every natural number $K \ge 2$, the decision problem MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES WITH K SETS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SP19] MINIMUM SUM OF SQUARES, p. 225 ("Reference: Transformation from PARTITION or 3-PARTITION. Comment: [...] NP-complete in the ordinary sense and solvable in pseudo-polynomial time for any fixed K.").
