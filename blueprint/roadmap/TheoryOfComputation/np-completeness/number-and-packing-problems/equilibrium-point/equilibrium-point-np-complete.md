# EQUILIBRIUM POINT is NP-complete

The decision problem EQUILIBRIUM POINT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN15] EQUILIBRIUM POINT.
- S. Sahni, "Computationally related problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 3 (1974), 262-279, doi:10.1137/0203021, Section 2.4, Theorem 2.4.1 (existence of an equilibrium point of an n-person game with polynomial payoffs is NP-complete).
