# Control labels of the canonical PARTITION membership transducer

The labels of the membership transducer are $\mathsf{copy}$ and $\mathsf{distribute}$, a copy of
each label of the SUBSET-SUM summation machine, a copy of each
[label of the doubling machine](lab.md), then $\mathsf{prefix}$, $\mathsf{bits}$ (write the block
of the sum in front of the output) and $\mathsf{done}$.
