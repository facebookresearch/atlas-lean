# Stacks of the raw-input SUBSET-SUM to PARTITION machine

The raw-input machine has a copy of each stack of the
[list normalizer](../../cook-levin/conversions-between-bit-string-encodings/a-finite-list-nat-canonicalizer/README.md) and a copy of each [stack of the padding machine](k-3.md),
kept apart, and four further stacks of bits: $\mathit{raw}$ (the input, preserved),
$\mathit{buffer}$, and the scratch stacks $\mathit{left}$ and $\mathit{right}$ of the comparison.
