# Padding reduction has linear output length

For every list $a$ of natural numbers and every natural number $t$, the canonical encoding of the
[padded instance](from-subset-sum.md) of $a$ and $t$ has length at most $3\ell + 1$, where $\ell$
is the length of the encoding of the SUBSET-SUM instance with numbers $a$ and target $t$.
