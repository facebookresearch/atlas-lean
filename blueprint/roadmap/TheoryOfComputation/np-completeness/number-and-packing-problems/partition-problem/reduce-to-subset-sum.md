# Encoded reduction from PARTITION to SUBSET-SUM

The map on bitstrings that sends $x$, when it decodes under the canonical bitstring encoding of
lists of natural numbers to a list $(a_1, \dots, a_n)$, to the encoding of the SUBSET-SUM
instance with numbers $2a_1, \dots, 2a_n$ and target $a_1 + \dots + a_n$, and that sends every
other bitstring to the encoding of the SUBSET-SUM instance with no numbers and target $1$, a
no-instance.
