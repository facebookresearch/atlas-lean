# PARTITION is NP-complete

The decision problem [PARTITION](partition-dec.md) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in
NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman, 1979, problem PARTITION.
- Richard M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations*, Plenum Press, 1972, 85-103.
