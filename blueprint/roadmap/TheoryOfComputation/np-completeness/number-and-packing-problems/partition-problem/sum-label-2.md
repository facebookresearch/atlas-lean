# Label map from summation halts to the doubling pass

The map from the labels of the SUBSET-SUM summation machine to the
[labels of the membership transducer](lab-5.md) that sends both halting labels, $\mathsf{yes}$ and
$\mathsf{no}$, to the doubling machine's label $\mathsf{scan}(\text{start})$ and every other label
to its copy.
