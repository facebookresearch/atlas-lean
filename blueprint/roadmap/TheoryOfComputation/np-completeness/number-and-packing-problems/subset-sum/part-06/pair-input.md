# Framed input of two natural numbers

For natural numbers $m$ and $n$, the framed input of $m$ and $n$ is the block of $m$ followed by
the block of $n$, where the block of a natural number $m$ is its binary digits, least significant first (none for $m = 0$), each digit $c$ written as the two bits true, $c$, followed by a final false.
