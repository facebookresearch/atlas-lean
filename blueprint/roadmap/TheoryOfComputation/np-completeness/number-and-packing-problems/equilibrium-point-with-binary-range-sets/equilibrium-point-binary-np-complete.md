# EQUILIBRIUM POINT with binary range-sets is NP-complete

The decision problem EQUILIBRIUM POINT with binary range-sets belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN15] EQUILIBRIUM POINT, p. 252 (comment: "Remains NP-complete even if M_i = {0,1} for 1 ≤ i ≤ n.").
- S. Sahni, "Computationally related problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 3 (1974), 262-279, doi:10.1137/0203021, Section 2.4, Theorem 2.4.1.
