# COLLAPSE OF A 3-COMPLEX TO A 1-COMPLEX is NP-complete

The decision problem COLLAPSE OF A 3-COMPLEX TO A 1-COMPLEX belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Malgouyres and A. R. Francés, "Determining whether a simplicial 3-complex collapses to a 1-complex is NP-complete", Discrete Geometry for Computer Imagery (DGCI 2008), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 4992, Springer (2008), 177-188, doi:10.1007/978-3-540-79126-3_17.
- M. Tancer, "Recognition of collapsible complexes is NP-complete", Discrete & Computational Geometry 55 (2016), 21-38, doi:10.1007/s00454-015-9747-1 (definitions, Section 2).
