# TENSOR RANK OVER GF(p) is NP-complete

For every fixed prime $p$, the decision problem TENSOR RANK OVER GF(p) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Håstad, "Tensor rank is NP-complete", Journal of Algorithms 11(4) (1990), 644-654, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(90)90014-6 (the rank of a three-dimensional tensor over any finite field is NP-complete to decide; over the rationals it is NP-hard).
- C. J. Hillar and L.-H. Lim, "Most tensor problems are NP-hard", Journal of the ACM 60(6) (2013), 1-39, doi:10.1145/2512329, Section 8 ("tensor rank is NP-hard over Q and NP-complete over finite fields" [Håstad 1990]).
