# d-COLLAPSIBILITY is NP-complete

For every fixed natural number $d \ge 4$, the decision problem d-COLLAPSIBILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Tancer, "d-collapsibility is NP-complete for d ≥ 4", Chicago Journal of Theoretical Computer Science 2010, Article 3, doi:10.4086/cjtcs.2010.003 (Theorem 1.1(ii)).
