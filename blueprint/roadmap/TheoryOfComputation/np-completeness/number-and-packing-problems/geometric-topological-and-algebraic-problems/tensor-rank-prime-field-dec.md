# TENSOR RANK OVER GF(p) as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $p$ (the source studies prime $p$, where $\mathbb{Z}/p$ is the field GF(p)). The decision problem TENSOR RANK OVER GF(p) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a nested list and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for $p$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
