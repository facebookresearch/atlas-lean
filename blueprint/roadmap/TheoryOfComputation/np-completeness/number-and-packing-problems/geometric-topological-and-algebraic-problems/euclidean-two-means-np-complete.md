# EUCLIDEAN 2-MEANS is NP-complete

The decision problem EUCLIDEAN 2-MEANS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Aloise, A. Deshpande, P. Hansen and P. Popat, "NP-hardness of Euclidean sum-of-squares clustering", Machine Learning 75 (2009), 245-248, doi:10.1007/s10994-009-5103-0 (sum-of-squares clustering into two clusters is NP-hard when the dimension is part of the input).
