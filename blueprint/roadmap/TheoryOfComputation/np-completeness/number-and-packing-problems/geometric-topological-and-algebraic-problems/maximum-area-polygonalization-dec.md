# MAXIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION as a decision problem

The decision problem MAXIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a point list and a rational, to an instance that is well-formed for MINIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION (the listed points are pairwise distinct and $A$ has a positive denominator) and feasible for MAXIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
