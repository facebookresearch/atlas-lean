# SHELLABILITY OF d-DIMENSIONAL COMPLEXES is NP-complete

For every fixed natural number $d \ge 2$, the decision problem SHELLABILITY OF d-DIMENSIONAL COMPLEXES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- X. Goaoc, P. Paták, Z. Patáková, M. Tancer and U. Wagner, "Shellability is NP-complete", Journal of the ACM 66 (2019), Article 21, doi:10.1145/3314024 (conference version: SoCG 2018; preprint arXiv:1711.08436).
