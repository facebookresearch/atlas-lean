# MINIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. P. Fekete, "On simple polygonalizations with optimal area", Discrete and Computational Geometry 23(1) (2000), 73-110, doi:10.1007/PL00009492.
