# MAXIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM AREA POLYGONALIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. P. Fekete, "On simple polygonalizations with optimal area", Discrete & Computational Geometry 23 (2000), 73-110, doi:10.1007/PL00009492, Theorem 4.1.
