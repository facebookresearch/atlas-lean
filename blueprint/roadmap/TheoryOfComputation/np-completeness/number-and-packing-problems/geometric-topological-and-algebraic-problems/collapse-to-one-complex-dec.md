# COLLAPSE OF A 3-COMPLEX TO A 1-COMPLEX as a decision problem

The decision problem COLLAPSE OF A 3-COMPLEX TO A 1-COMPLEX accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of faces, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for COLLAPSE OF A 3-COMPLEX TO A 1-COMPLEX. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
