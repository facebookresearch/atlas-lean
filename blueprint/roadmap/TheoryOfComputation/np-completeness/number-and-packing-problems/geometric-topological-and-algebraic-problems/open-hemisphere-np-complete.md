# OPEN HEMISPHERE is NP-complete

The decision problem OPEN HEMISPHERE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP6] OPEN HEMISPHERE.
- D. S. Johnson and F. P. Preparata, "The densest hemisphere problem", Theoretical Computer Science 6 (1978), 93-107, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(78)90006-3.
