# HOMOLOGY LOCALIZATION is NP-complete

The decision problem HOMOLOGY LOCALIZATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Chen and D. Freedman, "Hardness results for homology localization", Discrete and Computational Geometry 45 (2011), 425-448, doi:10.1007/s00454-010-9322-8.
