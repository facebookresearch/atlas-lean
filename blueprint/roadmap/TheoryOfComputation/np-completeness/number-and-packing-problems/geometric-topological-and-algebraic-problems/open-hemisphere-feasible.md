# Feasible instances of OPEN HEMISPHERE

An instance is feasible when some rational $m$-tuple $y$ has $x \cdot y > 0$ for at least $K$ entries $x$ of the point list $X$. Entries are counted with repetition, so an $m$-tuple listed twice counts twice, and coordinates missing from an entry read as $0$. That the entries are distinct $m$-tuples and that $0 < K \le |X|$ is the separate well-formedness condition of the encoded problem, not part of this predicate.
