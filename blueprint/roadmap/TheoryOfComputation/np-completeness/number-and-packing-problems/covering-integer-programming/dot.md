# Scalar product with an encoded rational vector

The scalar product of an encoded rational vector $a = ((p_1, q_1), (p_2, q_2), \dots)$ with a list $x$ of natural numbers is $\sum_k (p_k / q_k)\, x_k$, taken over the positions present in both lists (it stops at the shorter list), where an entry with $q_k = 0$ contributes $0$.
