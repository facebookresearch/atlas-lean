# CARPENTER'S RULER FOLDING is NP-complete

The CARPENTER'S RULER FOLDING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Hopcroft, D. Joseph and S. Whitesides, "On the movement of robot arms in 2-dimensional bounded regions", SIAM Journal on Computing 14 (1985), 315-333, doi:10.1137/0214025 (conference version: Proc. 23rd Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 1982), 280-289, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1982.29).
- G. Călinescu and A. Dumitrescu, "The carpenter's ruler folding problem", in *Combinatorial and Computational Geometry*, MSRI Publications 52, Cambridge University Press (2005), 155-166 (introduction: the problem "was first posed in [Hopcroft et al. 1985], where the authors proved that the problem is NP-complete using a reduction from the NP-complete problem PARTITION").
