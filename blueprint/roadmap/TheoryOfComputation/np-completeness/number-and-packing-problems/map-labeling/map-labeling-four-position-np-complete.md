# MAP LABELING WITH FOUR POSITIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAP LABELING WITH FOUR POSITIONS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

The labels here may touch: two labels conflict only when their interiors overlap. Formann and
Wagner distinguish open and closed labels.

Source: Formann and Wagner (1991).
