# Instances of CAPACITY ASSIGNMENT

An instance of CAPACITY ASSIGNMENT is a tuple $(M, T, K, J)$: a list $M$ of capacities; for every communication link $c$, a list of pairs $(g(c, x), d(c, x))$ of cost and delay penalty, one for each capacity $x$ of $M$ in order; a cost bound $K$; and a delay bound $J$.
