# Well-formed CAPACITY ASSIGNMENT instances

An instance $(M, T, K, J)$ of CAPACITY ASSIGNMENT is well-formed when the capacities of $M$ are distinct and positive; every link lists exactly one pair per capacity, each with positive cost and positive delay penalty; for every link $c$ and capacities $x < y$ of $M$, $g(c, x) \le g(c, y)$ and $d(c, x) \ge d(c, y)$; and $K$ and $J$ are positive.
