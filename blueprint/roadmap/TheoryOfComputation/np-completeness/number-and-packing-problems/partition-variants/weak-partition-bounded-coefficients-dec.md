# WEAK PARTITION WITH BOUNDED COEFFICIENTS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem WEAK PARTITION WITH BOUNDED COEFFICIENTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed for WEAK PARTITION (positive sizes) and feasible for weak partition with coefficients bounded by $K$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
