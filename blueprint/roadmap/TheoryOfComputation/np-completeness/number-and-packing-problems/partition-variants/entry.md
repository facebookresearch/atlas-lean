# Entry of an array given by its rows

The entry $A[r, c]$ of an array $A$ of natural numbers given as a list of rows: entry $c$ of row $r$, with rows and columns numbered from $0$. A missing row or a missing entry of a row reads as $0$. The same lookup serves ARRAY PARTITION, SYMMETRIC LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION and ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE.
