# DOUBLE DIGEST is NP-complete

The decision problem DOUBLE DIGEST belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. Goldstein and M. S. Waterman, "Mapping DNA by stochastic relaxation", Advances in Applied Mathematics 8 (1987), 194-207, doi:10.1016/0196-8858(87)90013-3.
- M.-Y. Kao, J. Samet and W.-K. Sung, "The enhanced double digest problem for DNA physical mapping", arXiv:cs/0102010 (2001), Section 1 (records the problem and Goldstein and Waterman's NP-hardness proof by a reduction from set partition).
- M. Cieliebak, S. Eidenbenz and G. J. Woeginger, "Double digest revisited: complexity and approximability in the presence of noisy data", COCOON 2003, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2697 (2003), 519-527, doi:10.1007/3-540-45071-8_52.
