# PARTIAL DIGEST SUPERSET is NP-complete

PARTIAL DIGEST SUPERSET is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Cieliebak, Eidenbenz and Penna (2003), Theorem 1.
