# RING LOADING is NP-complete

RING LOADING is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Schrijver, Seymour and Winkler (1998), Sections 2-3; Cosares and Saniee (1994); Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM RING LOADING.
