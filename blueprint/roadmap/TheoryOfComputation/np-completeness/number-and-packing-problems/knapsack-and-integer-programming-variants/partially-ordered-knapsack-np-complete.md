# PARTIALLY ORDERED KNAPSACK is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTIALLY ORDERED KNAPSACK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP12] PARTIALLY ORDERED KNAPSACK.
- D. S. Johnson and K. A. Niemi, "On knapsacks, partitions, and a new dynamic programming technique for trees", Mathematics of Operations Research 8 (1983), 1-14, doi:10.1287/moor.8.1.1.
