# QUADRATIC KNAPSACK as a decision problem

The decision problem QUADRATIC KNAPSACK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a weight list, a capacity, a matrix and a bound), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-12.md) and [feasible](quadratic-knapsack-feasible.md) for QUADRATIC KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
