# CLASS-CONSTRAINED KNAPSACK is NP-complete

The decision problem CLASS-CONSTRAINED KNAPSACK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MAXIMUM CLASS-CONSTRAINED KNAPSACK.
- H. Shachnai and T. Tamir, "On two class-constrained versions of the multiple knapsack problem", Algorithmica 29 (2001), 442-467, doi:10.1007/s004530010057 (the class-constrained multiple knapsack problem, CMKP, is NP-hard).
