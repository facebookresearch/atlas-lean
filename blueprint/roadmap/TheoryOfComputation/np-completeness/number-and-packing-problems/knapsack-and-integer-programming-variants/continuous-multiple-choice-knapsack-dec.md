# CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK as a decision problem

The decision problem CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a list of blocks of (size, value) pairs and two natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4.md) and [feasible](continuous-multiple-choice-knapsack-feas.md) for CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
