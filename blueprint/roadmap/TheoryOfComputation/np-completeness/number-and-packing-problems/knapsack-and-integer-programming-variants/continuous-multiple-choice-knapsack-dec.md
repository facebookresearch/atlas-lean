# CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK as a decision problem

The decision problem CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a list of blocks of (size, value) pairs and two natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
