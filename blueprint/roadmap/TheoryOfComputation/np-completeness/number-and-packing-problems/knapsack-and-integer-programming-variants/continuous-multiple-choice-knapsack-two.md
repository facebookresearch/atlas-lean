# CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK WITH BLOCKS OF AT MOST TWO ELEMENTS as a decision problem

The decision problem CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK WITH BLOCKS OF AT MOST TWO ELEMENTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a list of blocks of (size, value) pairs and two natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) for CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK WITH BLOCKS OF AT MOST TWO ELEMENTS and feasible for CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
