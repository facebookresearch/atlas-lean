# PARTIALLY ORDERED KNAPSACK WITH EQUAL SIZES AND VALUES is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTIALLY ORDERED KNAPSACK WITH EQUAL SIZES AND VALUES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
