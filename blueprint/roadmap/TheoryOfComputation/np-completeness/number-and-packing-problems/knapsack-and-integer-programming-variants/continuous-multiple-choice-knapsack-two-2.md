# CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK WITH BLOCKS OF AT MOST TWO ELEMENTS is NP-complete

The decision problem CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK WITH BLOCKS OF AT MOST TWO ELEMENTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP11] CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK, comment (p. 247): "Solvable in pseudo-polynomial time, but remains NP-complete even if `|Uᵢ| ≤ 2`, `1 ≤ i ≤ m`" (reference of the entry: [Ibaraki, 1978]; transformation from PARTITION).
