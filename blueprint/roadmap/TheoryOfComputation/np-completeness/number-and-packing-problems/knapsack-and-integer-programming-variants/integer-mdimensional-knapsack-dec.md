# INTEGER m-DIMENSIONAL KNAPSACK as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $m$. The decision problem INTEGER m-DIMENSIONAL KNAPSACK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples (a list of columns, a vector and a natural number), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-7.md) for dimension $m$ and [feasible](integer-mdimensional-knapsack-feasible.md) for INTEGER m-DIMENSIONAL KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
