# CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK is NP-complete

The decision problem CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MP11] CONTINUOUS MULTIPLE CHOICE KNAPSACK.
- T. Ibaraki, "Approximate algorithms for the multiple-choice continuous knapsack problems", Journal of the Operations Research Society of Japan 23 (1980), 28-63, doi:10.15807/jorsj.23.28.
