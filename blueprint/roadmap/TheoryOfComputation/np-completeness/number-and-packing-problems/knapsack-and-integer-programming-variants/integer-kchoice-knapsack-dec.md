# INTEGER K-CHOICE KNAPSACK as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGER K-CHOICE KNAPSACK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a natural number, a list of rows of pairs, and two natural numbers), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-5.md) and [feasible](integer-kchoice-knapsack-feasible.md) for INTEGER K-CHOICE KNAPSACK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
