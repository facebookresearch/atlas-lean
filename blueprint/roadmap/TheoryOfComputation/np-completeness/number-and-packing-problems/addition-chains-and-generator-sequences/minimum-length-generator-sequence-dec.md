# MINIMUM LENGTH GENERATOR SEQUENCE as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM LENGTH GENERATOR SEQUENCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a natural number, a list of lists, a list and a list of Booleans), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM LENGTH GENERATOR SEQUENCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
