# Lookup in a list of values with a default

For a list $p$ of natural numbers and a natural number $x$, the value is entry $x$ of $p$ when $x$ is less than the length of $p$, and $x$ itself otherwise. The list is arbitrary: for $p = [0, 0]$ both $0$ and $1$ map to $0$, so the map is not a permutation. When $p$ lists the values of a permutation of $\{0, \dots, |p| - 1\}$, as for the generators and target of a well-formed instance, this is the image of $x$ under that permutation, extended by fixing every point beyond its degree.
