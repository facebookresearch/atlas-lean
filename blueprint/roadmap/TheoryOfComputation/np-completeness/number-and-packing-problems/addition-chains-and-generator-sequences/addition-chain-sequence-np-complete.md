# ADDITION CHAIN FOR A SEQUENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem ADDITION CHAIN FOR A SEQUENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Downey, B. Leong and R. Sethi, "Computing sequences with addition chains", SIAM Journal on Computing 10 (1981), 638-646, doi:10.1137/0210047.
