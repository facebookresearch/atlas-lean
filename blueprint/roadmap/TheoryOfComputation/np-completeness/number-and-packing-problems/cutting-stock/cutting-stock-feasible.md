# Feasible CUTTING STOCK instances

An instance $(T, K)$ of CUTTING STOCK is feasible when the items can be packed into $K$ bins of capacity $1$: bin $b$ receives $c_{bj}$ copies of item type $j$, every bin has total size $\sum_j c_{bj} s_j \le 1$, and every item type $j$ is packed exactly $a_j$ times in total.
