# Unit-time open-shop schedules

In OPEN SHOP O|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax, `σ k j` (start of job `j`'s unit operation on machine `k`) is an open-shop schedule for the precedence arcs `A`: each machine starts its operations at pairwise different times, each job starts its operations at pairwise different times, and if job `i` precedes job `j` then every operation of `j` starts after every operation of `i` is completed.
