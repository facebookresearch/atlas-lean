# Instances of OPEN SHOP O|p_ij=1;outtree;r_i|Cmax

An instance of OPEN SHOP O|p_ij=1;outtree;r_i|Cmax is a quadruple $(m, J, A, K)$: a natural number $m$ of machines; a list $J$ of jobs, the $j$-th entry a pair of a stored list of processing times (intended as $(p_{j1}, \dots, p_{jm})$) and a release date $r_j \in \mathbb{N}$; a list $A$ of precedence arcs $(i, j)$ of natural numbers (job $i$ precedes job $j$); and a bound $K \in \mathbb{N}$. The raw instance places no condition on these lists: a stored processing-time list may have any length and any entries, the job list may be empty, and an arc may name a number that is not a job. That $m \ge 1$, that there is at least one job, that every job stores exactly $m$ processing times, all equal to $1$, and that the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic out-tree relation are part of [well-formedness](well-formed.md).
