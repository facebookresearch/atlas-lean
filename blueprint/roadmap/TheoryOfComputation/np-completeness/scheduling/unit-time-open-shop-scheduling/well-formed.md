# Well-formed instances of OPEN SHOP O|p_ij=1;outtree;r_i|Cmax

An instance of OPEN SHOP O|p_ij=1;outtree;r_i|Cmax is well formed when it is well-formed for the unit-time out-tree flow shop once the release dates are set aside (`m ≥ 1`, every job has `m` unit operations, the arcs name jobs and are acyclic, and each job has at most one immediate predecessor), and there is at least one job.
