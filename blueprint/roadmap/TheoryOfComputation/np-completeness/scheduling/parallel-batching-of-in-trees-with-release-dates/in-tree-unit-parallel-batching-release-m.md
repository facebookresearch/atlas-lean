# PARALLEL BATCHING of in-trees with release dates as a decision problem

The decision problem PARALLEL BATCHING of in-trees with release dates accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and admits a schedule of its unit-time jobs on a single batching machine, processing at most $b$ jobs per unit-time batch, that respects the release dates and the precedence constraints and has makespan at most $K$. The encoding reuses the parallel-machine schedule with $m = b$ machines: jobs with equal integral start times form one batch. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
