# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149 (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1 (the full text was not available to us).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Table 5.3 (`P2|pᵢ = 1; chains|∑wᵢCᵢ`, NP-hard in the strong sense, Timkovsky).
