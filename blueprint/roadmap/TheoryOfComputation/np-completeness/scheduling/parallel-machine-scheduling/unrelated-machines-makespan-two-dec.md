# UNRELATED PARALLEL MACHINES WITH MAKESPAN 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem UNRELATED PARALLEL MACHINES WITH MAKESPAN 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(m, P)$ (a natural number and a list of lists of natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for UNRELATED PARALLEL MACHINES WITH MAKESPAN 2. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
