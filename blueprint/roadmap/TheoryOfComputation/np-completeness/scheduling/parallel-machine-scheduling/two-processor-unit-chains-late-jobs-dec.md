# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(d, P, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
