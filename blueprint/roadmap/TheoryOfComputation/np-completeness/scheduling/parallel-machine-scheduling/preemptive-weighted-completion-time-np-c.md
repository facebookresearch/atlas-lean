# PREEMPTIVE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem PREEMPTIVE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources
