# TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING (comment: remains NP-complete for `m = 2`) and Section 3.2.1, p. 65 (proof by restriction to PARTITION, allowing only instances with `m = 2`).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorem 3(a) (PARTITION reduces to `P2 || Cmax`).
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations*, Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9 (PARTITION).
