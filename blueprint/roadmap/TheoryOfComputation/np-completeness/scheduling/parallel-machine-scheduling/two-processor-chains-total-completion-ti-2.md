# TWO-PROCESSOR CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Du, J. Y.-T. Leung and G. H. Young, "Scheduling chain-structured tasks to minimize makespan and mean flow time", Information and Computation 92 (1991), 219-236, doi:10.1016/0890-5401(91)90009-Q.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Table 5.3 (`P2|chains|∑Cᵢ`, NP-hard in the strong sense, Du et al.).
