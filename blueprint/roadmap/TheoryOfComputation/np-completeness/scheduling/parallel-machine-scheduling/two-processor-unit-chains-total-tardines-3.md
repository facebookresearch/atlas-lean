# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `pqr_nop` (parallel machine problems without preemption), entry `P2|chains;p_i=1|ΣU_i, ΣT_i` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, justified by the single-machine problem).
- J. Y.-T. Leung and G. H. Young, "Minimizing total tardiness on a single machine with precedence constraints", ORSA Journal on Computing 2 (1990), 346-352, doi:10.1287/ijoc.2.4.346 (`1|chains, p_j = 1|ΣT_j` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
