# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a weighted-tardiness instance $(T, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
