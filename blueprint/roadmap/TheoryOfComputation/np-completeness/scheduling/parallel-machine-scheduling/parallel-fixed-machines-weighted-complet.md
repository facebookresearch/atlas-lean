# WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME ON m PARALLEL MACHINES as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $m_0$. The decision problem WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME ON $m$ PARALLEL MACHINES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, T, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME ON $m$ PARALLEL MACHINES and feasible for SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
