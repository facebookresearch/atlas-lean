# `P|intree; r_i; p_i = 1|C_max` is NP-complete

`P|intree; r_i; p_i = 1|C_max` is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Brucker, Garey and Johnson (1977); Brucker and Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*.
