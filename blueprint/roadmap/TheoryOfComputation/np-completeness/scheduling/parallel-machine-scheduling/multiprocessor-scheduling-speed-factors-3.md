# MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(l, S, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
