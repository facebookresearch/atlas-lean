# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `pqr_nop` (parallel machine problems without preemption), entry `P2|chains;p_i=1|ΣU_i, ΣT_i` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, justified by the single-machine problem).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity results for scheduling chains on a single machine", European Journal of Operational Research 4 (1980), 270-275, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(80)90111-3, Theorem 1 (`1|chains, p_j = 1|ΣU_j`).
