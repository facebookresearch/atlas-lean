# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, T, K, P)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs, a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME CHAINS SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME and feasible for weighted completion time with precedence (some start times have at most $m$ tasks running at every time, respect the precedence ($\sigma(t) + l(t) \le \sigma(t')$), and have weighted completion time at most $K$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
