# MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH SPEED FACTORS.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, ""Strong" NP-completeness results: motivation, examples, and implications", Journal of the ACM 25 (1978), 499-508, doi:10.1145/322077.322090 (MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING is NP-complete in the strong sense).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING.
