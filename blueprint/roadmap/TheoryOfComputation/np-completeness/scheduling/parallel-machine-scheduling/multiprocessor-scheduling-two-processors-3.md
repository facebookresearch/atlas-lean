# TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(l, m, D)$ (a list of natural numbers and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING and feasible for MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
