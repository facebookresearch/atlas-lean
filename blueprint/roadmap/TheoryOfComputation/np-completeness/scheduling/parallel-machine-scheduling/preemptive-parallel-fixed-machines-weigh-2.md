# PREEMPTIVE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME ON m PARALLEL MACHINES is NP-complete

For every natural number $m_0 \ge 2$, the decision problem PREEMPTIVE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME ON $m$ PARALLEL MACHINES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
