# SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, T, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
