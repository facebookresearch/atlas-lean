# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE, TOTAL TARDINESS when every task has length $1$ and weight $1$, every pair in $P$ names two tasks, the listed pairs form a strict partial order, and the order is chain-like. Deadlines and $K$ are arbitrary natural numbers.
