# PRODUCTION PLANNING remains NP-complete when all demands are equal, all set-up costs are equal, and all inventory costs are `0`

PRODUCTION PLANNING remains NP-complete when all demands are equal, all set-up costs are equal, and all inventory costs are `0`: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
