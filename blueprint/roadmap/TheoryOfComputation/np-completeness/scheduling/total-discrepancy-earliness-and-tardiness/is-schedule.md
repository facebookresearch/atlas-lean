# Real starting times forming a one-processor schedule in TOTAL DISCREPANCY

For $N$ tasks with natural-number lengths $l_a$, real starting times $s_a$ form a one-processor schedule when, for any two distinct tasks $a \ne b$, $s_a + l_a \le s_b$ or $s_b + l_b \le s_a$: one task finishes at or before the other starts, so consecutive tasks may touch. The starting times range over all real numbers, negative ones included. The lengths are not required to be positive here; positive lengths are part of [well-formedness](well-formed.md).
