# Feasible instances of TOTAL DISCREPANCY

An instance of TOTAL DISCREPANCY is feasible when some schedule has total discrepancy at most `k`: the sum over the tasks of the distance between the actual midtime `sᵢ + lᵢ / 2` and the preferred midtime `Mᵢ` is at most `k`. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
