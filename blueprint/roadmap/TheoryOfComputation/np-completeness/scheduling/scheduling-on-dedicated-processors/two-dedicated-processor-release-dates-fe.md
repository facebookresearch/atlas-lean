# Feasible instances of TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax`

An instance of TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax` is feasible when there are start times `s` such that every task starts no earlier than its release date and completes by `K`, and any two different tasks with a common processor are processed in disjoint time intervals. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
