# The TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax` decision problem

The TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax` decision problem accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is well formed and feasible. Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected.
