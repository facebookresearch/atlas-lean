# Well-formed instances of TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax`

An instance of TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH RELEASE DATES `P2|r_i;fix_i|Cmax` is well formed when every task has a positive processing time and uses a nonempty set of processors, listed without repetitions, all among the two processors `0, 1`.
