# Instances of TWO-PROCESSOR TREE-PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR TREE-PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is a quadruple $(m, J, K, P)$: a number of processors $m \in \mathbb{N}$, a list $J$ of pairs $(l(t), w(t)) \in \mathbb{N}^2$, one per task $t$ (its length and weight), a bound $K \in \mathbb{N}$, and a list $P$ of pairs $(t, t')$ of task indices, read as the precedence relation $t < t'$.
