# Well-formed instances of SINGLE-MACHINE PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING WITH UNIT WEIGHTS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of precedence-constrained weighted completion is well-formed for SINGLE-MACHINE PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING WITH UNIT WEIGHTS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME when it is well-formed for precedence-constrained weighted completion, every job has positive processing time and weight $1$, and $K > 0$.
