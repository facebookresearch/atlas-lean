# SINGLE-MACHINE PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING WITH UNIT WEIGHTS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING WITH UNIT WEIGHTS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS4] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME (comment: NP-complete in the strong sense and remains so even if all task weights are 1).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22.
- E. L. Lawler, "Sequencing jobs to minimize total weighted completion time subject to precedence constraints", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 2 (1978), 75-90, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70323-6.
