# TWO-PROCESSOR TREE-PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR TREE-PRECEDENCE SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, J, K, P)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs, a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode, or decodes to an instance that is not well-formed.
