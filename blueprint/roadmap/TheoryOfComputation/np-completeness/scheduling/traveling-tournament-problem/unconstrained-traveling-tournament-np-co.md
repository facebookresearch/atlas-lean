# UNCONSTRAINED TRAVELING TOURNAMENT PROBLEM is NP-complete

The decision problem UNCONSTRAINED TRAVELING TOURNAMENT PROBLEM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Bhattacharyya, "Complexity of the unconstrained traveling tournament problem", Operations Research Letters 44(5) (2016), 649-654, doi:10.1016/j.orl.2016.07.011, Theorem 1.1.
