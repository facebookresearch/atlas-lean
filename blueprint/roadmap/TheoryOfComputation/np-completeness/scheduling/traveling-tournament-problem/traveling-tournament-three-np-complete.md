# TRAVELING TOURNAMENT WITH AT MOST THREE CONSECUTIVE HOME OR AWAY GAMES is NP-complete

The decision problem TRAVELING TOURNAMENT WITH AT MOST THREE CONSECUTIVE HOME OR AWAY GAMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Thielen and S. Westphal, "Complexity of the traveling tournament problem", Theoretical Computer Science 412(4-5) (2011), 345-351, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2010.10.001.
- decision version (TTP-k) as restated in D. Chatterjee, "Complexity of traveling tournament problem with trip length more than three", arXiv:2110.02300 (2021), Definition 1.1.
