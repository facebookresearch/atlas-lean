# Storage-time product of a processing order

For a processing order, the storage-time product sums, over the edges $(t_1, t_2, w)$, $w$ times the total length of the tasks in the positions from $\min(p_1, p_2)$ to $\max(p_1, p_2)$, where $p_1, p_2$ are the positions of $t_1, t_2$ (entries missing from the lists read as $0$).
