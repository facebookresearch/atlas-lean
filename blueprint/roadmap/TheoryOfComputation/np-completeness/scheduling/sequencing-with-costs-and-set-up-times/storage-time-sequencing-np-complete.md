# STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING is NP-complete

The decision problem STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, entry MINIMUM STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING (the instance, schedule and storage-time measure).
- S. Rao and A. W. Richa, "New approximation techniques for some linear ordering problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 34(2) (2005), 388-404, doi:10.1137/S0097539702413197 (records minimum storage-time product as NP-hard).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT43] DIRECTED OPTIMAL LINEAR ARRANGEMENT (the special case of unit lengths and weights, up to the additive constant `|E|`).
