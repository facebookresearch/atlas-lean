# SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND EQUAL SET-UP TIMES as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND EQUAL SET-UP TIMES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(L, T)$ (a list of natural numbers and a list of triples), to an instance that is well-formed for SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND EQUAL SET-UP TIMES and feasible for SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND SET-UP TIMES (some one-processor schedule meets every deadline and, whenever $t'$ directly follows $t$ with a different compiler, leaves at least the set-up time of $t'$'s compiler between them). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
