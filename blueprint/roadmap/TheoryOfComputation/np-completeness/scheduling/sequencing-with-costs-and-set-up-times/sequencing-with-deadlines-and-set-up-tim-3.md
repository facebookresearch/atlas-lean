# SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND SET-UP TIMES as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND SET-UP TIMES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(L, T)$ (a list of natural numbers and a list of triples), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND SET-UP TIMES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
