# SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND EQUAL SET-UP TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND EQUAL SET-UP TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS6] SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND SET-UP TIMES (comment: remains NP-complete even if all set-up times are equal).
- J. Bruno and P. Downey, "Complexity of task sequencing with deadlines, set-up times and changeover costs", SIAM Journal on Computing 7 (1978), 393-404, doi:10.1137/0207031.
