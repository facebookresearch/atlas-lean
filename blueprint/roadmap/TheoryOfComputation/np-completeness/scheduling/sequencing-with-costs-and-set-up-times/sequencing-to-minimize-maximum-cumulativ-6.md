# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS7] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST (reference: [Abdel-Wahab, 1976], transformation from REGISTER SUFFICIENCY).
- H. M. Abdel-Wahab, *Scheduling with Applications to Register Allocation and Deadlock Problems*, Ph.D. thesis, University of Waterloo (1976) (not available to this formalization).
- H. M. Abdel-Wahab and T. Kameda, "Scheduling to minimize maximum cumulative cost subject to series-parallel precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 141-158, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.141.
