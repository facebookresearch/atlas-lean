# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST WITH COSTS IN −1, 0 AND 1 is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST WITH COSTS IN −1, 0 AND 1 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS7] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM CUMULATIVE COST, p. 238 (comment: "Remains NP-complete even if `c(t) ∈ {-1, 0, 1}` for all `t ∈ T`"; reference: [Abdel-Wahab, 1976], transformation from REGISTER SUFFICIENCY).
