# SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND CHANGEOVER COSTS as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND CHANGEOVER COSTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a pair $(L, T)$ and a natural number, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SEQUENCING WITH DEADLINES AND CHANGEOVER COSTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
