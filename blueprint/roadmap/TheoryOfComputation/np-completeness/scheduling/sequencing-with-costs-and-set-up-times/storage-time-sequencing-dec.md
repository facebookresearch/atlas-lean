# STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING as a decision problem

The decision problem STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(l, E, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of triples and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for STORAGE-TIME SEQUENCING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
