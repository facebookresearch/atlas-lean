# PERIODIC EVENT SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem PERIODIC EVENT SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Serafini and W. Ukovich, "A mathematical model for periodic scheduling problems", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 2 (1989), 550-581, doi:10.1137/0402049.
