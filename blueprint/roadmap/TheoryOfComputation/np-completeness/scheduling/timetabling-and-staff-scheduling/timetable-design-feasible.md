# Feasible instances of TIMETABLE DESIGN

An instance is feasible when some $f : C \times T \times H \to \{0, 1\}$ has $f(c, t, h) = 1$ only when $h \in A(c) \cap A(t)$, gives each craftsman at most one task per hour and each task at most one craftsman per hour, and assigns craftsman $c$ to task $t$ in exactly $R(c, t)$ hours (availabilities and requirements missing from the lists read as empty and $0$).
