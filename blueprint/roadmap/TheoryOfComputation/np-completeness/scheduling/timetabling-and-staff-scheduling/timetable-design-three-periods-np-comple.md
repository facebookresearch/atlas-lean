# TIMETABLE DESIGN WITH THREE PERIODS is NP-complete

The decision problem TIMETABLE DESIGN WITH THREE PERIODS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 243, problem [SS19] TIMETABLE DESIGN, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if `|H| = 3`, `A(t) = H` for all `t ∈ T`, and each `R(c, t) ∈ {0, 1}`".
- S. Even, A. Itai, and A. Shamir, "On the complexity of timetable and multicommodity flow problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 691-703, doi:10.1137/0205048 (the reference Garey and Johnson give for [SS19]).
