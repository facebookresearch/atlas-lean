# Well-formed instances of TIMETABLE DESIGN WITH THREE PERIODS

An instance is well-formed for TIMETABLE DESIGN WITH THREE PERIODS when there is one list of available hours per craftsman and per task, every available hour lies in $H$, and $R$ is a $|C| \times |T|$ matrix, $|H| = 3$, every task is available in every period, and every requirement $R(c, t)$ is $0$ or $1$.
