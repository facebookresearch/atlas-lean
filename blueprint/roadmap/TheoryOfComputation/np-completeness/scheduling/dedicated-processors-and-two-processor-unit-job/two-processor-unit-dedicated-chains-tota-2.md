# TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(m, F, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed for two dedicated processors with chains and feasible for TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
