# TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
