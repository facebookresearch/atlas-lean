# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
