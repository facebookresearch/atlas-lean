# Instances of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS

An instance is a pair of a JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING instance $(m, J, K)$, its last component read as the bound $K$, and a list of arcs $(j, j')$ (job $j$ before job $j'$); that the arcs form chains is part of well-formedness.
