# THREE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING OF PARALLEL TASKS, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING OF PARALLEL TASKS, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(T, K)$ (a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for THREE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING OF PARALLEL TASKS, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
