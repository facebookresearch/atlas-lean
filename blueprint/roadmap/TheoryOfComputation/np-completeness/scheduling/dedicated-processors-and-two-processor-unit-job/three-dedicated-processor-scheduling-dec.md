# 3-DEDICATED PROCESSOR SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem 3-DEDICATED PROCESSOR SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(T, K)$ (a list of pairs of a natural number and a list, and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for 3-DEDICATED PROCESSOR SCHEDULING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
