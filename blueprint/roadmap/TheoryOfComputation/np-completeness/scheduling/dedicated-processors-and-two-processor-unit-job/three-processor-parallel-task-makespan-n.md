# THREE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING OF PARALLEL TASKS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING OF PARALLEL TASKS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING (the case in which every task has size `1`).
- J. Du and J. Y.-T. Leung, "Complexity of scheduling parallel task systems", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 2 (1989), 473-487, doi:10.1137/0402042 (pseudo-polynomial algorithm for three processors).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Section 11.4 (tasks of type `sizeᵢ`).
