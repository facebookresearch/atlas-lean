# UNIT-TIME SCHEDULING WITH DEDICATED PROCESSOR SETS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME SCHEDULING WITH DEDICATED PROCESSOR SETS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. A. Hoogeveen, S. L. van de Velde and B. Veltman, "Complexity of scheduling multiprocessor tasks with prespecified processor allocations", Discrete Applied Mathematics 55 (1994), 259-272, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90012-4, Theorem 2.5 (deciding whether an instance of `P|fixⱼ, pⱼ = 1|C_max` has a schedule of length at most 3 is NP-hard in the strong sense).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Multiprocessor task problems with dedicated processors", which lists the problem as minimal NP-hard with this attribution.
