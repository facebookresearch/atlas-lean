# UNIT-TIME PARALLEL TASKS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIT-TIME PARALLEL TASKS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, s, K)$ (a natural number, a list of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for unit-time parallel tasks, total completion time. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
