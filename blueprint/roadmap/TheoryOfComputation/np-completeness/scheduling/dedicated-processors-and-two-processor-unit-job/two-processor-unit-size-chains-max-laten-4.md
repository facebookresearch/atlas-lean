# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH SIZES AND CHAINS, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT TASKS WITH SIZES AND CHAINS, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, S. Knust, D. Roper and Y. Zinder, "Scheduling UET task systems with concurrency on two parallel identical processors", Mathematical Methods of Operations Research 52 (2000), 369-387, doi:10.1007/s001860000089.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Multiprocessor task problems with parallel processors" (`P2|chains;p_i=1;size_i|L_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Brucker et al. 2000; the class page defines the model: each task `i` requires `size_i` of the `m` identical processors during its processing).
