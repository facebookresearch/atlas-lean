# UNIT-TIME SCHEDULING WITH DEDICATED PROCESSOR SETS, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIT-TIME SCHEDULING WITH DEDICATED PROCESSOR SETS, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, F, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for UNIT-TIME SCHEDULING WITH DEDICATED PROCESSOR SETS, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
