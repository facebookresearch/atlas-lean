# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
