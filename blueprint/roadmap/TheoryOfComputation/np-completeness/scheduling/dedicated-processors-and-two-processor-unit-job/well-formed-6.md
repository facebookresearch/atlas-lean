# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS

An instance is well-formed when the job-shop instance is well-formed ($m > 0$, every job has at least one task, processors in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job on different processors, last component positive), $m = 2$, every task has length $1$, every arc names two jobs, no two distinct arcs share a tail or a head, and the arcs have no directed cycle.
