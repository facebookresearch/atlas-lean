# FIVE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem FIVE-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Du and J. Y.-T. Leung, "Complexity of scheduling parallel task systems", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 2 (1989), 473-487, doi:10.1137/0402042 (only the abstract was available).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `pmpt_nop` (multiprocessor tasks on parallel processors, without preemption), entry `P5|size_i|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Du and Leung 1989).
