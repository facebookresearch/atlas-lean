# TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TASKS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TASKS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING, p. 239 (comment: "If each task `t` can only be executed by a specified processor `p(t)`, the problem is NP-complete for `m = 2` and `<` arbitrary, and for `m` arbitrary and `<` a forest, but can be solved in polynomial time for `m` arbitrary if `<` is a 'cyclic forest' [Goyal, 1976]"), citing D. K. Goyal, "Scheduling processor bound systems", Technical Report, Computer Science Department, Washington State University (1976) (not available to this formalization).
