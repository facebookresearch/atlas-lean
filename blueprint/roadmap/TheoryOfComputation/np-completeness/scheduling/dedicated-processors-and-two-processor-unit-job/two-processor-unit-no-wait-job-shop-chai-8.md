# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85 (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8 (`J2|chains, pᵢⱼ = 1, no-wait|C_max` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Job-shop problems with no-wait" (`J2|chains; pᵢⱼ = 1; no-wait|C_max`, minimal NP-hard, credited to Timkovsky 1998).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING (the parent problem).
