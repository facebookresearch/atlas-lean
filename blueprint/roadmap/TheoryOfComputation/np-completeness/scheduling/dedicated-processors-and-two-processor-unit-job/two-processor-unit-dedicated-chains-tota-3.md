# TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. A. Hoogeveen, S. L. van de Velde and B. Veltman, "Complexity of scheduling multiprocessor tasks with prespecified processor allocations", Discrete Applied Mathematics 55 (1994), 259-272, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90012-4, Theorem 3.6.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Multiprocessor task problems with dedicated processors" (`P2|chains;p_i=1;fix_i|ΣC_i`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense).
