# TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(m, F, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO DEDICATED PROCESSORS WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
