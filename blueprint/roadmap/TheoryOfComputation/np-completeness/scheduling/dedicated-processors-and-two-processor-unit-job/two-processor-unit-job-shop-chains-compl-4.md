# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH CHAINS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85 (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8 (`J2|chains, pᵢⱼ = 1|∑ Cⱼ` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
