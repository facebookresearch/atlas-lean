# MPT FLOW SHOP WITH TREE PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES (FMPT|tree; p_ij = 1|L_max) is NP-complete

The decision problem MPT FLOW SHOP WITH TREE PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES (FMPT|tree; p_ij =
1|L_max) is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Brucker and Knust (1999); Brucker and Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling
problems*; Brucker (2004), Chapter 11.
