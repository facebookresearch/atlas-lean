# Two-machine unit-time robot flow-shop schedules

Start times $\sigma$ and transport starts $\rho$ form two-machine unit-time robot flow-shop schedule (start times $\sigma_1(j), \sigma_2(j)$ on $M_1, M_2$ forming a unit-time flow-shop schedule with delays $t_j$, transport start times $\rho_j$ with $\sigma_1(j) + 1 \le \rho_j$ and $\rho_j + t_j \le \sigma_2(j)$, and transports of distinct jobs not overlapping).
