# Transport time from the second to the third machine in THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is a triple $(L, p, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of triples $(t_{i1}, t_{i2}, d_i)$ of natural numbers, one per job, a processing time $p$, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. The transport time $t_{i2}$ of job $i$ from machine $M_2$ to machine $M_3$ is the second component of the triple at position $i$ of $L$.
