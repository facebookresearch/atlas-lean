# Jobs of an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is a pair $(L, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of triples $(r_j, t_j, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the release date, transport time and due date of job $j$, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal triples at different positions are different jobs.
