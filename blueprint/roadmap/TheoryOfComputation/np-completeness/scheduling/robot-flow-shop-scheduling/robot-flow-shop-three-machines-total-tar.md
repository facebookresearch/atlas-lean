# THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of quadruples of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed (all weights $1$) and feasible for three-machine unit-time robot flow-shop weighted tardiness. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
