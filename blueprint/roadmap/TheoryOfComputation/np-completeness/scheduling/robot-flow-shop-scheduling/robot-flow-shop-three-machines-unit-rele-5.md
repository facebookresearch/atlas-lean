# Feasible instances of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance is feasible when there is a single-robot three-machine schedule (start times $a, c, f$ of the unit operations on $M_1, M_2, M_3$ and $b, e$ of the transports $M_1 \to M_2$, $M_2 \to M_3$ of lengths $t_{j1}, t_{j2}$; each job's steps in order, pairwise different start times on each machine, and all transports pairwise disjoint in time) (with the transport times read from the first two entries) starting every job on $M_1$ at or after its release date, with $f_j + 1 + y^- \le d_j + y^+$ for every job.
