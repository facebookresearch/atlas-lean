# Jobs of an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of pairs $(t_j, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the transport time and due date of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total tardiness. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs.
