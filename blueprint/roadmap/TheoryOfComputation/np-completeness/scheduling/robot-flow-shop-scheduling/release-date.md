# Release date of a job in THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES AND RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN

An instance of THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES AND RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN is a triple $(L, p, K)$ of a list $L$ of triples $(t_{i1}, t_{i2}, r_i)$ of natural numbers, one per job, a processing time $p$, and a bound $K$ on the makespan. The release date $r_i$ of job $i$ is the third component of the triple at position $i$ of $L$.
