# Jobs of an instance of THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is a triple $(L, p, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of triples $(t_{i1}, t_{i2}, d_i)$ of natural numbers, one per job, a processing time $p$, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal triples at different positions are different jobs.
