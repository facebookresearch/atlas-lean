# Due date of a job in THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is a pair $(L, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of tuples $(t_{i1}, t_{i2}, r_i, d_i)$ of natural numbers, one per job, the transport times from $M_1$ to $M_2$ and from $M_2$ to $M_3$, the release date and the due date, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. The due date $d_i$ of job $i$ is the fourth entry of the tuple at position $i$ of $L$.
