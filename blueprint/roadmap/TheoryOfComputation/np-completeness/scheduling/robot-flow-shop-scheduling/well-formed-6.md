# Well-formed instances of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME has the data of an instance of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS: a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of tuples $(t_{i1}, t_{i2}, r_i, w_i)$ of natural numbers, one per job, read here as the two transport times, a release date and a weight, and a bound $K$. It is well formed when every job has weight $w_i = 1$, so that the weighted objective is the total completion time.
