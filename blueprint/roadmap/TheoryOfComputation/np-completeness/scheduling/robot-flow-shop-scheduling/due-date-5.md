# Due date of a job in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of triples $(t_j, d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the transport time, due date and weight of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total weighted tardiness. The due date $d_j$ of job $j$ is the second component of the triple at position $j$ of $L$.
