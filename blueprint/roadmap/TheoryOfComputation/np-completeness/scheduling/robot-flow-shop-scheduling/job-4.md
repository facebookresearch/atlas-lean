# Jobs of a three-machine robot flow shop

An instance of a three-machine robot flow shop, as in THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS, is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of tuples of four natural numbers, one per job, and a bound $K$. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal tuples at different positions are different jobs.
