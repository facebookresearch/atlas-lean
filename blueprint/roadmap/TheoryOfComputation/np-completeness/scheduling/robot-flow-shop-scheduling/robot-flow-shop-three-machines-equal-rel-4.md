# THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES AND RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH EQUAL PROCESSING TIMES AND RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Knust, *Shop-scheduling problems with transportation*, PhD thesis, Universität Osnabrück (1999) (not available; the result is recorded by Brucker and Knust).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `f_1ro` (flow-shop problems with transportation times and a single robot), entry `F3;R1|p_ij=p;t_ik;r_i|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Knust 1999).
