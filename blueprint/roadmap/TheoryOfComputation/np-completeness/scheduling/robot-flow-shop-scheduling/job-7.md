# Jobs of an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of triples $(t_j, d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the transport time, due date and weight of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total weighted tardiness. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal triples at different positions are different jobs.
