# Transport time of a job in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP, TOTAL TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of pairs $(t_j, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the transport time and due date of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total tardiness. The transport time $t_j$ of job $j$, the time the robot needs to carry it from the first machine to the second, is the first component of the pair at position $j$ of $L$.
