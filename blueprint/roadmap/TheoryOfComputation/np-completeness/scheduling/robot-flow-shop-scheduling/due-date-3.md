# Due date of a job in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is a pair $(L, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of triples $(r_j, t_j, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the release date, transport time and due date of job $j$, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. The due date $d_j$ of job $j$ is the third component of the triple at position $j$ of $L$.
