# TWO-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH CONSTANT TRANSPORT TIME, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE ROBOT FLOW SHOP WITH CONSTANT TRANSPORT TIME, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Hurink and S. Knust, "Makespan minimization for flow-shop problems with transportation times and a single robot", Discrete Applied Mathematics 112 (2001), 199-216, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00316-4 (Theorem 1: strongly NP-hard).
- H. Kise, "On an automated two-machine flowshop scheduling problem with infinite buffer", Journal of the Operations Research Society of Japan 34(3) (1991), 354-361, doi:10.15807/jorsj.34.354 (NP-hard in the ordinary sense).
