# FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS15] FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING.
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. Sethi, "The complexity of flowshop and jobshop scheduling", Mathematics of Operations Research 1 (1976), 117-129, doi:10.1287/moor.1.2.117.
