# TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP, MEAN FLOW TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP, MEAN FLOW TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. Sethi, "The complexity of flowshop and jobshop scheduling", Mathematics of Operations Research 1 (1976), 117-129, doi:10.1287/moor.1.2.117 (the two-machine flow shop `F2||∑ Cᵢ`, of which this problem is a generalization).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 10 (the MPM flow shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (shop problems with multipurpose machines: `FMPM2||∑ Cᵢ`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, Garey et al. (1976)).
