# Operations of a multi-purpose machine job shop

An instance of a multi-purpose machine job shop is a triple $(m, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $J$ of jobs, and a bound $K$; job $j$, at position $j$ of $J$, is the list of its operations in order, each operation given as a pair $(p_{jk}, \mu_{jk})$ of a natural-number processing time and a list $\mu_{jk}$ of eligible machines. An operation of the instance is a pair $(j, k)$ of a job $j$, a position of $J$, and a position $k$ in the operation list of job $j$, numbered from $0$.
