# Chain arcs of an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

For an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME with jobs $0, \dots, n-1$ and arc list $A$, job $j$ is the immediate predecessor of job $j^\prime$ when the pair $(j, j^\prime)$ occurs in $A$. That arcs name jobs, that no two arcs share a tail or a head, and that the arcs have no cycle are conditions of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
