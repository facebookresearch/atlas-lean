# Well-formed instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance $((m, J, K), D)$ of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is well formed when it is a well-formed multi-purpose machine job shop with two machines, that is, $m = 2$, every job has at least one operation, and every operation has a nonempty list of eligible machines, all in $\{1, 2\}$; every operation has processing time $p_{jk} = 1$; and the list $D$ has exactly one pair per job, so its length equals the length of $J$.
