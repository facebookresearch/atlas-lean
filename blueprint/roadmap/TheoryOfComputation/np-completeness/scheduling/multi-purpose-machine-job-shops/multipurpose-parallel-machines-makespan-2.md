# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING (comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense for `m` arbitrary (3-PARTITION is a special case)").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, ""Strong" NP-completeness results: motivation, examples, and implications", Journal of the ACM 25 (1978), 499-508, doi:10.1145/322077.322090.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Multipurpose machine problems" (`PMPM||C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Garey and Johnson (1978)).
