# THREE-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Yu. N. Sotskov and N. V. Shakhlevich, "NP-hardness of shop-scheduling problems with three jobs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 59 (1995), 237-266, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(95)80004-N.
- recorded in P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (shop problems with multipurpose machines: `JMPM3|n=3|ΣC_i`, minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense, Sotskov and Shakhlevich (1995)).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 10 (the MPM job shop model).
