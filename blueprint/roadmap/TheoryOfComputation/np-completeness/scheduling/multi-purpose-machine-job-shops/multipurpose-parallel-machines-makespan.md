# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of pairs of a natural number and a list of natural numbers, and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, MAKESPAN and feasible for multi-purpose parallel machine makespan (a multi-purpose machine schedule completing every operation by $K$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
