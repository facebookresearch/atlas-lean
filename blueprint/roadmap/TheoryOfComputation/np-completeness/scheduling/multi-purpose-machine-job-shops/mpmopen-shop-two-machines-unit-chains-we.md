# Instances of two-machine unit-time stage shops with weights and chains

An instance is a quadruple $(\mu, w, A, K)$ of stage machine sets $\mu$, a list $w$ of weights (its entries are the jobs, job $i$ having weight $w_i$), a list of arcs $(i, k)$ (job $i$ before job $k$), and a bound $K$. That the arcs form chains is part of well-formedness.
