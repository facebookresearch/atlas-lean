# Feasible instances of THREE-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when there is a multi-purpose machine job-shop schedule (each operation on an eligible machine, operations of a job in order, no overlap on a machine) whose job completion times $C_j$ (completion of the last operation of job $j$) satisfy $\sum_j C_j \le K$.
