# MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of pairs of a natural number and a list of natural numbers, and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
