# MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 10 (the MPM job shop model, and the statement that the MPM job shop problem with two machines and three jobs is NP-hard, attributed to W. - P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (shop problems with multipurpose machines: `JMPM2|n=3|C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense and maximal pseudopolynomially solvable, citing B. Jurisch, Discrete Applied Mathematics 58 (1995), 145-156, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(93)E0124-H, and Meyer (1992)).
