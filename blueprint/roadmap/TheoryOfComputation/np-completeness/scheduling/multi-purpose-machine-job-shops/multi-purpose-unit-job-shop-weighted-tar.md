# Instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a pair of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$, with $m$ the number of machines, $J$ the list of jobs, each a list of operations given as pairs $(p_{jk}, \mu_{jk})$ of a processing time and a list of eligible machines, and $K$ the bound on the total weighted tardiness, and a list of pairs $(d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the due date and the weight of job $j$, read by position.
