# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when there is a multi-purpose machine job-shop schedule (each operation on an eligible machine, operations of a job in order, no overlap on a machine) in which, for every listed arc $(j, j')$, the first operation of job $j'$ starts no earlier than the last operation of job $j$ completes, and the job completion times sum to at most $K$.
