# Processing time of an operation in a multi-purpose machine job shop

An instance of a multi-purpose machine job shop is a triple $(m, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $J$ of jobs, and a bound $K$; job $j$, at position $j$ of $J$, is the list of its operations in order, each operation given as a pair $(p_{jk}, \mu_{jk})$ of a natural-number processing time and a list $\mu_{jk}$ of eligible machines. The processing time of an operation $(j, k)$ is the first component $p_{jk}$ of the pair at position $k$ of the operation list of job $j$.
