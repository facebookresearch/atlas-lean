# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, TWO MACHINES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, TWO MACHINES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING (comment: remains NP-complete for `m = 2`).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Multipurpose machine problems" (`PMPM2||C_max`, minimal NP-hard, credited to Lenstra et al. (1977)).
