# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 10 (the MPM flow-shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `FMPM2|chains;p_ij=1|Σw_iC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Tanaev et al. (1994), and class "Flow-shop problems without preemption", which lists the special case `F2|p_ij=1;chains|Σw_iC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Tanaev et al. (1994).
