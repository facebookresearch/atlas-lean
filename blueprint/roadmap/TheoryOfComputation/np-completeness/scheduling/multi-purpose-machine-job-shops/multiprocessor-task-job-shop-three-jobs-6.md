# MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH THREE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 11 (shop problems with multiprocessor tasks: the MPT job-shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", entry `JMPT3|n=3|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense, citing Sotskov and Shakhlevich 1995).
- Yu. N. Sotskov and N. V. Shakhlevich, "NP-hardness of shop-scheduling problems with three jobs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 59 (1995), 237-266, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(95)80004-N (`J3|n=3|C_max` is NP-hard).
