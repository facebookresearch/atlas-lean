# Well-formed instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is well-formed when the job-shop instance is well-formed with $2$ machines (nonempty jobs, nonempty eligible lists in $\{1, 2\}$) and every operation has length $1$, every arc names two jobs, no two distinct arcs share a tail or a head, and the arcs have no directed cycle.
