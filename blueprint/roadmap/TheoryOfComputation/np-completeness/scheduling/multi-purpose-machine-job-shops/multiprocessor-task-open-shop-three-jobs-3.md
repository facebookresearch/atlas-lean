# MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH THREE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH THREE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS14] OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment: remains NP-complete if `m = 3`; T. Gonzalez and S. Sahni, "Open shop scheduling to minimize finish time", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 665-679, doi:10.1145/321978.321985; P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 11 (shop problems with multiprocessor tasks); P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", entry `OMPT|n=3|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense, citing Gonzalez and Sahni 1976).
