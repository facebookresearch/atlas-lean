# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, WEIGHTED TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85 (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8.
