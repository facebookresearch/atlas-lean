# THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for single-machine problems with positive finish-start time-lags", Computing 63 (1999), 299-316, doi:10.1007/s006070050036; recorded as strongly NP-hard (a minimal NP-hard problem) in P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Flow-shop problems without preemption" (University of Osnabrück, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/), problem `F3|p_ij=1;chains|ΣU_i`, credited to Brucker and Knust (1999).
