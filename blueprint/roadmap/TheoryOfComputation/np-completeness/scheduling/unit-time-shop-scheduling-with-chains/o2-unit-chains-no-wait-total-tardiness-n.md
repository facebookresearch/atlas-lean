# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149(2) (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1; recorded as strongly NP-hard (a minimal NP-hard problem) in P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Open-shop problems with nowait" (University of Osnabrück, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/), problem `O2|p_ij=1;chains;no-wait|ΣT_i`, credited to Timkovsky (2003).
