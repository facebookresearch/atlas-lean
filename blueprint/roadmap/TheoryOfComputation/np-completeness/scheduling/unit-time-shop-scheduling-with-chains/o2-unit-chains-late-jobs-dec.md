# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(d, A, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed (the arcs form chains on the jobs) and feasible for TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
