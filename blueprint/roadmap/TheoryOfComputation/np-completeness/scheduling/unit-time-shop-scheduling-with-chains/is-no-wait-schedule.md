# No-wait unit-time open-shop schedules

For $m$ machines, $n$ jobs, precedence arcs $A$ and job start times $S_j$, start times $\sigma_k(j)$ form a no-wait schedule when they form a unit-time open-shop schedule for $A$ (each machine and each job start their operations at pairwise different times, and every operation of $j$ starts after every operation of $i$ completes for each arc $(i, j)$) and every operation of job $j$ starts in $[S_j, S_j + m)$, so the $m$ operations of job $j$ run back to back from $S_j$.
