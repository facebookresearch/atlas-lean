# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines) and Table 5.3 (`P|pᵢ = 1; prec|∑Cᵢ`, NP-hard in the strong sense, Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan).
