# TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Y.-T. Leung and G. H. Young, "Minimizing total tardiness on a single machine with precedence constraints", ORSA Journal on Computing 2 (1990), 346-352, doi:10.1287/ijoc.2.4.346 (`1|chains, pⱼ = 1|∑ Tⱼ`).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Multi-purpose machine problems" (`PMPM2|chains; p_i=1|ΣT_i`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, as a single-machine problem).
