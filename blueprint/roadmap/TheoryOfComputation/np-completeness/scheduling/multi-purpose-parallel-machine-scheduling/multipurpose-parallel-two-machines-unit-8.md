# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. A. Hoogeveen, S. L. van de Velde and B. Veltman, "Complexity of scheduling multiprocessor tasks with prespecified processor allocations", Discrete Applied Mathematics 55 (1994), 259-272, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90012-4, Theorem 2.7.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines), Section 10.1 (`PMPM2|pᵢ = 1; chains|C_max`).
