# MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
