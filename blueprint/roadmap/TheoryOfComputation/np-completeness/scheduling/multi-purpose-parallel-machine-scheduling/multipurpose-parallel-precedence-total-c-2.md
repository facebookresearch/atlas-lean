# Feasible instances of MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when a multi-purpose machine schedule (every operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) respects the arcs (for every arc $(j, j')$, every operation of $j$ completes before every operation of $j'$ starts) and has $\sum_t (\sigma(t) + p_t) \le K$, the sum over all operations $t$.
