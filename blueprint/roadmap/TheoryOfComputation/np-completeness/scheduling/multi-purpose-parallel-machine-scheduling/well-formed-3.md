# Well-formed instances of MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES

An instance is well-formed when $m > 0$, the job-shop instance is well-formed with its own $m$ (nonempty jobs, nonempty eligible lists in $\{1, \dots, m\}$), every job has exactly one operation, of processing time $1$, and the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic digraph.
