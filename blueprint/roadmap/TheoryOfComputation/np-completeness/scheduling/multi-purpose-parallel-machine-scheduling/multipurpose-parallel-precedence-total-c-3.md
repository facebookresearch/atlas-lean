# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list, to an instance that is well-formed ($m > 0$, the job-shop instance is well-formed with its own $m$ (nonempty jobs, nonempty eligible lists in $\{1, \dots, m\}$), every job has exactly one operation, of processing time $1$, and the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic digraph) and feasible for MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
