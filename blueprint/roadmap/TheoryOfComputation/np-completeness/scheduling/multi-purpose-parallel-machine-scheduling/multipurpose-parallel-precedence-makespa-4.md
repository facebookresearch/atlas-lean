# MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (the special case in which every machine set contains all machines).
- J. D. Ullman, "NP-complete scheduling problems", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 10 (1975), 384-393, doi:10.1016/S0022-0000(75)80008-0.
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
