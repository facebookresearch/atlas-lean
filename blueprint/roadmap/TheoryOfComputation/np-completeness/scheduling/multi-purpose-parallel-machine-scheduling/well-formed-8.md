# Well-formed instances of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES

An instance is well-formed when the job-shop instance is well-formed with $2$ machines (nonempty jobs, nonempty eligible lists in $\{1, 2\}$), every job has exactly one operation, of processing time $1$, the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic digraph, and they form chains.
