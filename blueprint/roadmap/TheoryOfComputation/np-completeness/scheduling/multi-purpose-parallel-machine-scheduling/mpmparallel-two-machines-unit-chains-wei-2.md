# Feasible instances of TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when a multi-purpose machine schedule (each operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) respects the arcs and $\sum_t w_j (\sigma(t) + p_t) \le K$, the sum running over all operations $t$, $j$ being the job of $t$ and $w_j$ the $j$-th listed weight ($0$ if the weight list is shorter).
