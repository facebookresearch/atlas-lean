# UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines" (`OMPM|p_ij=1;r_i|ΣU_i`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Kravchenko 1999).
- S. A. Kravchenko, "On the complexity of minimizing the number of late jobs in unit time open shop", Discrete Applied Mathematics 100 (2000), 127-132, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(99)00202-4 (the open shop `O|p_ij=1;r_i|ΣU_i`, the case `μ_j = {M_j}`; the full text was not available).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (the MPM open-shop model).
