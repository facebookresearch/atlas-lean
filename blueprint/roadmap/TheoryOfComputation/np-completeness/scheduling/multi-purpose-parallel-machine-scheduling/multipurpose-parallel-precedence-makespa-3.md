# MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
