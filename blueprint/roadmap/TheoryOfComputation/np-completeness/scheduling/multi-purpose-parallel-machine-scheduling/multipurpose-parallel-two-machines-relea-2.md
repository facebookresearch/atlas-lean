# Feasible instances of TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when a multi-purpose machine schedule (each operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) starts every operation at or after the release date of its job (missing dates read as $0$) and has $\sum_t (\sigma(t) + p_t) \le K$, the sum running over all operations $t$.
