# MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose parallel instance with precedence and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
