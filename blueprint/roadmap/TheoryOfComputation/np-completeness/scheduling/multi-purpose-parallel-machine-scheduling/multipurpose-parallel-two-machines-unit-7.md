# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
