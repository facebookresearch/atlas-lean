# Instances of TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance is a pair of an instance of two multi-purpose parallel machines with chains and unit times and a list of due dates, job $j$ having the $j$-th listed due date $d_j$. That the list has one entry per job is part of well-formedness, not of the instance type.
