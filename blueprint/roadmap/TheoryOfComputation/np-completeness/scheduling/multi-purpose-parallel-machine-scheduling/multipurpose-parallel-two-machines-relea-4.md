# TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Parallel machine problems with multi-purpose machines", which lists `PMPM2|r_i|ΣC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense ("Single-machine problem").
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X (the single-machine problem `1|r_j|ΣC_j`).
