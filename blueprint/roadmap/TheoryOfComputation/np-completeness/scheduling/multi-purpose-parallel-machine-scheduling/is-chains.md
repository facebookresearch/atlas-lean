# Chain precedence in TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS is a pair of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$, with $m$ the number of machines, $J$ the list of jobs, each a list of operations given as pairs $(p, \mu)$ of a processing time and a list of eligible machines, and $K$ the bound on the makespan, and a list $A$ of precedence arcs $(i, k)$. Job $j$ precedes job $k$ when $k$ is reachable from $j$ along the arcs, and $j$ is an immediate predecessor of $k$ when $j$ precedes $k$ and no job $c$ has $j$ preceding $c$ and $c$ preceding $k$. The precedence relation of the arcs is a set of chains when every job has at most one immediate successor and at most one immediate predecessor.
