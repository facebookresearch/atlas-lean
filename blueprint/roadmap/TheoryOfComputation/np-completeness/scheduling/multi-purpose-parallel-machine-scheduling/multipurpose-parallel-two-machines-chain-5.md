# TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list, to an instance that is well-formed for two multi-purpose parallel machines with chains ($m = 2$, every job has exactly one operation, of positive length, with a nonempty list of eligible machines in $\{1, 2\}$, and the arcs name jobs, form no directed cycle and give every job at most one immediate predecessor and at most one immediate successor) and feasible for multi-purpose parallel machines with precedence, total completion time (a schedule respecting the arcs with $\sum_t (\sigma(t) + p_t) \le K$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
