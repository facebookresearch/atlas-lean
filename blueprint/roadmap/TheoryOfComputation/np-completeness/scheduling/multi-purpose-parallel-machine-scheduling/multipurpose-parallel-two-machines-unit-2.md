# Feasible instances of TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, LATE JOBS

An instance is feasible when a multi-purpose machine schedule (every operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) respects the arcs (for every arc $(j, k)$, every operation of $j$ completes before every operation of $k$ starts) and at most $K$ operations complete after the due date of their job (missing due dates read as $0$).
