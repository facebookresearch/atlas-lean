# Feasible instances of TWO MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance is feasible when a multi-purpose machine schedule (every operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) respects the arcs (for every arc $(j, k)$, every operation of $j$ completes before every operation of $k$ starts) and $\sum_t (\sigma(t) + p_t - d_j) \le K$ with truncated subtraction, $j$ being the job of $t$ and $d_j$ the $j$-th listed due date ($0$ if the due-date list is shorter).
