# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a multi-purpose machine job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$ and a list, to an instance that is well-formed for MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME and feasible for two-machine weighted completion as stated (a multi-purpose machine schedule (every operation on one of its eligible machines, no overlap of positive-length operations on a machine, the operations of each job in order) with $\sum_t w_{j(t)} (\sigma(t) + p_t) \le K$, the sum over all operations $t$ with $j(t)$ the job of $t$, no machine-count restriction in the feasibility). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
