# Well-formed instances of MULTI-PURPOSE UNIFORM MACHINES WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, MAKESPAN

An instance is well-formed when the inner instance is well-formed for multi-purpose parallel machines with precedence ($m > 0$, the job-shop instance is well-formed with its own $m$ (nonempty jobs, nonempty eligible lists in $\{1, \dots, m\}$), every job has exactly one operation, of processing time $1$, and the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic digraph), the arcs form chains, there are exactly $m$ speeds, and every speed pair $(a, b)$ has $a > 0$ and $b > 0$.
