# MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE PARALLEL MACHINES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS13] SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, p. 240 (comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense for m arbitrary [Lageweg and Lenstra, 1977]", a private communication; the original was not available).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (multi-purpose machines).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Multipurpose machine problems", which lists `PMPM||Σw_iC_i` as minimal NP-hard (strongly), crediting an unpublished result of J. K. Lenstra.
