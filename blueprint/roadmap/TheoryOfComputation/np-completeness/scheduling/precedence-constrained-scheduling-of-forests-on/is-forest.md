# Is Forest

Join two distinct task indices when either immediately precedes the other by the listed-pair test, forget the direction, and require the resulting undirected graph to be acyclic. The test is not conditional on a partial order: two tasks listing both $(0, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$ give one undirected edge and pass, although the relation is not a strict order. Only after order validation is this graph the Hasse diagram of the precedence order.
