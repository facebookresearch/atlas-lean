# GRAPH BALANCING (makespan 2, weights 1 and 2) is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH BALANCING (makespan 2, weights 1 and 2) belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Ebenlendr, M. Krčál, and J. Sgall, "Graph balancing: a special case of scheduling unrelated parallel machines", Algorithmica 68 (2014), 62-80, doi:10.1007/s00453-012-9668-9 (the problem).
- Y. Asahiro, E. Miyano, and H. Ono, "Graph classes and the complexity of the graph orientation minimizing the maximum weighted outdegree", Discrete Applied Mathematics 159 (2011), 498-508, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2010.11.003, Theorem 13 with `k = 2`.
