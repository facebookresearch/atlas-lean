# GRAPH BALANCING (makespan 2, weights 1 and 2) as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH BALANCING (makespan 2, weights 1 and 2) accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of GRAPH BALANCING (makespan 2,
weights 1 and 2) instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](graph-balancing-makespan-two-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not
decode to an instance.
