# UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. J. Rayward-Smith and D. Rebaine, "Open shop scheduling with delays", RAIRO Informatique théorique et Applications 26 (1992), 439-447, doi:10.1051/ita/1992260504391, Section 2, problem OmDU and Theorem 2 ("OmDU is NP-complete even if the delays are symmetric").
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Open-shop problems with transportation delays" (University of Osnabrück, http://www.mathematik.uni-osnabrueck.de/research/OR/class/), problem `O|p_ij=1;t_kl=t_lk|C_max`, minimal strongly NP-hard, credited to Rayward-Smith and Rebaine (1992).
