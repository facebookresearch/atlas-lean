# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment on p. 242 ("The analogous "no-wait" versions of OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING and JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING are NP-complete in the strong sense for m = 2 [Sahni and Cho, 1977b]").
- S. Sahni and Y. Cho, "Complexity of scheduling shops with no wait in process", Mathematics of Operations Research 4 (1979), 448-457, doi:10.1287/moor.4.4.448.
