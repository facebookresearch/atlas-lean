# THREE-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS14] OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment: remains NP-complete if `m = 3`.
- T. Gonzalez and S. Sahni, "Open shop scheduling to minimize finish time", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 665-679, doi:10.1145/321978.321985.
