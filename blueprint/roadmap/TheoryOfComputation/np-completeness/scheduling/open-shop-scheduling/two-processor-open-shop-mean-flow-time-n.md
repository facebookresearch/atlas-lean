# TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. O. Achugbue and F. Y. Chin, "Scheduling the open shop to minimize mean flow time", SIAM Journal on Computing 11 (1982), 709-720, doi:10.1137/0211058.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `o_nop` (open-shop problems without preemption), Universität Osnabrück (last update 2006).
