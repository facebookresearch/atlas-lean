# THREE-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for THREE-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING and feasible for OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING (an open-shop schedule meeting the bound: start times $\sigma_p(j)$ of job $j$'s task on processor $p$, pairwise different on each processor, such that a task starting after another task $k$ on the same processor starts no earlier than $k$ finishes, the tasks of each job occupy pairwise disjoint time intervals, and every task finishes by the last component). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
