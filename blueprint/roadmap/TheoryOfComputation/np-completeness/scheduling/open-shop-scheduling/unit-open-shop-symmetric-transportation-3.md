# UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(m, J, K)$ and a list of lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
