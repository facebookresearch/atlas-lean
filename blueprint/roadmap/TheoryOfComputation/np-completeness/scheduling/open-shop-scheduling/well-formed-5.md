# Well-formed instances of UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS

An instance is well-formed for UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH SYMMETRIC TRANSPORTATION DELAYS when the open-shop instance is well-formed ($m > 0$, every job has exactly $m$ tasks, and the last component is positive), every task has length $1$, the delay matrix is $m \times m$, symmetric, and zero on the diagonal.
