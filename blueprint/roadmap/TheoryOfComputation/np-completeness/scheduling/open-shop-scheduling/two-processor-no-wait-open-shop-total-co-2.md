# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
