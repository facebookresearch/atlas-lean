# Chain arcs of a two-machine unit-time shop with chains

An instance of a two-machine unit-time shop with chains, as in TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME FLOW-SHOP TOTAL TARDINESS WITH CHAINS, is a triple $(d, A, K)$ of a list $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, a list $A$ of pairs of natural numbers, the arcs of the chains, and a bound $K$. Job $j$ is the immediate predecessor of job $k$ when the pair $(j, k)$ occurs in $A$. That arcs name jobs, that no two arcs share a tail or a head, and that the arcs have no cycle are conditions of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
