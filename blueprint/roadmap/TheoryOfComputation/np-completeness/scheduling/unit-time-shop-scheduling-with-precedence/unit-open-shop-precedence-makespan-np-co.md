# UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, classification tables (class "Open-shop problems without preemption", minimal NP-hard entry `O | p_ij = 1; prec | C_max`, NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Timkovsky (2003)).
- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149(2) (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1.
