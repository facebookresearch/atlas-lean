# Due date of a job in UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS

An instance of UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS is a triple $(m, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $J$ of jobs, job $j$ given as a triple $(p, r_j, d_j)$ of a list $p$ of processing times and two natural numbers, and a bound $K$ on the number of late jobs. The due date of job $j$, a position of $J$, is the entry $d_j$ of the triple at position $j$.
