# THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `o_now` (open-shop problems with no-wait), entry `O3|p_ij=1;chains;no-wait|Σw_iC_i` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Timkovsky 2003).
- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149 (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1 (only the abstract and introduction were available).
