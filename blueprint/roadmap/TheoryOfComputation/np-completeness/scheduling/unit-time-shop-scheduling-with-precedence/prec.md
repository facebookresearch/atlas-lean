# Precedence relation of a list of arcs on jobs

Given a list $A$ of pairs of natural numbers and jobs $0, \dots, n-1$, job $i$ precedes job $j$ when the arc $(i, j)$ occurs in $A$. Only the listed arcs are related, not their transitive closure, and arcs whose entries are not below $n$ relate no jobs; that the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic graph is the separate well-formedness of the precedence arcs.
