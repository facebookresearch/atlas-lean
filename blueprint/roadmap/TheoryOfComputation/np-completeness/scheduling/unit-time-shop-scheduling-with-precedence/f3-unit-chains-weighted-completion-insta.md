# Instances of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance of THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is a triple $(w, A, K)$ of a list $w = (w_0, \dots, w_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, the weights of the jobs $0, \dots, n-1$, where $n$ is the length of $w$; a list $A$ of pairs of natural numbers, the chain arcs, where an arc $(i, j)$ says that job $i$ immediately precedes job $j$; and a natural number $K$, the bound on the total weighted completion time.
