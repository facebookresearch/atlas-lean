# UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. A. Kravchenko, "On the complexity of minimizing the number of late jobs in unit time open shop", Discrete Applied Mathematics 100 (2000), 127-132, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(99)00202-4 (the full text was not available); recorded as strongly NP-hard (a minimal NP-hard problem) in P.
