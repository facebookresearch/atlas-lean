# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME FLOW-SHOP TOTAL TARDINESS WITH CHAINS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME FLOW-SHOP TOTAL TARDINESS WITH CHAINS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for single-machine problems with positive finish-start time-lags", Computing 63 (1999), 299-316, doi:10.1007/s006070050036 (`F2|pᵢⱼ = 1, chains|∑ Tⱼ` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Flow-shop problems without preemption" (`F2|pᵢⱼ = 1; chains|∑ Tᵢ`, minimal NP-hard, credited to Brucker and Knust 1999).
