# Instances of unit-time shops with precedence

An instance is a quadruple $(m, P, A, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $P$ of jobs (each given by a list of processing times, its $k$-th entry for machine $k$), a list $A$ of precedence arcs $(i, j)$ (job $i$ precedes job $j$), and a bound $K$. That every job lists $m$ processing times is part of well-formedness, not of the instance type.
