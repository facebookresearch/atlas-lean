# Jobs of a two-machine unit-time shop with chains

An instance of a two-machine unit-time shop with chains, as in TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME FLOW-SHOP TOTAL TARDINESS WITH CHAINS, is a triple $(d, A, K)$ of a list $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, a list $A$ of pairs of natural numbers, the arcs of the chains, and a bound $K$. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $d$, where $n$ is the length of $d$, one per listed due date.
