# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for single-machine problems with positive finish-start time-lags", Computing 63 (1999), 299-316, doi:10.1007/s006070050036.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `f_nop` (flow-shop problems without preemption), Universität Osnabrück (last update 2006).
