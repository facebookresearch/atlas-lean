# Unit-time flow-shop schedules with precedence

Start times $\sigma_k(j)$ of job $j$ on machine $k$ form a unit-time flow-shop schedule for the arcs $A$ when each machine starts its operations at pairwise different times, each operation $O_{j,k+1}$ starts after $O_{j,k}$ completes ($\sigma_k(j) + 1 \le \sigma_{k+1}(j)$), and whenever $(i, j)$ is an arc every operation of $j$ starts at or after every operation of $i$ completes.
