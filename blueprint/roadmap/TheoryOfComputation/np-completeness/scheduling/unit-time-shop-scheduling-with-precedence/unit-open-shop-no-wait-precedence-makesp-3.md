# UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, classification tables (class "Open-shop problems with no-wait", minimal NP-hard entry `O | p_ij = 1; prec; no-wait | C_max`, NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Ullman (1975)).
- J. D. Ullman, "NP-complete scheduling problems", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 10 (1975), 384-393, doi:10.1016/S0022-0000(75)80008-0.
