# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS

An instance $(d, A, K)$ is feasible when there is a two-machine unit-time flow-shop schedule (each machine starts its unit operations at pairwise different times, each job's second operation starts after its first completes) obeying the chains (for every listed arc $(a, b)$, job $b$ starts on $M_1$ only after job $a$ completes on $M_2$) with at most $K$ late jobs ($d_j < \sigma_2(j) + 1$).
