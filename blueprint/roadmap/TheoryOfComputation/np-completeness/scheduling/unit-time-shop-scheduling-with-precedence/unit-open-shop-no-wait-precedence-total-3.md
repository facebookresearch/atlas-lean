# UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, classification tables (class "Open-shop problems with no-wait", minimal NP-hard entry `O | p_ij = 1; prec; no-wait | Σ C_i`, NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan (1978)).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26(1) (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22.
