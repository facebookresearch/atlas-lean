# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH CHAINS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `f_nop` (flow-shop problems without preemption), Universität Osnabrück (last update 2006).
