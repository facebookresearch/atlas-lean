# Feasible instances of UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS

An instance is feasible when some unit-time open-shop schedule without precedence (each machine and each job start operations at pairwise different times) starts no operation of a job before its release date and has at most $K$ late jobs, a job being late when one of its operations completes after $d_j$. Every operation takes one unit of time, whatever processing times the jobs list.
