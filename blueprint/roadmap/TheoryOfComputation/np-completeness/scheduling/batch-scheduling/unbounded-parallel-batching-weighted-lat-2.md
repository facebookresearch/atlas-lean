# UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, A. Gladky, H. Hoogeveen, M. Y. Kovalyov, C. N. Potts, T. Tautenhahn and S. L. van de Velde, "Scheduling a batching machine", Journal of Scheduling 1 (1998), 31-54, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-1425(199806)1:1<31::AID-JOS4>3.0.CO;2-R (`1|p-batch|Σw_jU_j` NP-hard).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class `bat_p` (parallel batching), entry `1|p-batch|Σw_iU_i`: minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense.
