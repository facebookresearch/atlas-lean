# Due date of a job in SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS is a pair of an instance $(s, L, K)$ of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, whose jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $L$ of pairs $(p_j, w_j)$, and a list $d$ of natural numbers. The due date $d_j$ of job $j$ is the entry of $d$ at position $j$, and $0$ when $d$ has no entry at that position. That $d$ has exactly one entry per job is a condition of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
