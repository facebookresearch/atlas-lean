# SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(p, P, s, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
