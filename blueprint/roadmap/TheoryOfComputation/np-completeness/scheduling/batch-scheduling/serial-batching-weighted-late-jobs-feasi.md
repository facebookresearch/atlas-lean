# Feasible instances of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS

An instance is feasible when there is a serial-batch schedule (batches in index order, each a setup $s$ followed by its jobs) in which the late jobs ($d_j < C_j$) have total weight at most $K$, with $d_j$ the $j$-th listed due date, $0$ when the due-date list has no such entry.
