# Jobs of an instance of UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of quadruples $(p_j, r_j, d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the processing time, release date, due date and weight of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total weighted tardiness. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal quadruples at different positions are different jobs.
