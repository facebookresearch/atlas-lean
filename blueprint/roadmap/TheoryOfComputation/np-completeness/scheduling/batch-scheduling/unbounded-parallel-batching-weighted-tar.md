# UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, WEIGHTED TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, WEIGHTED TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of quadruples of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed (positive processing times, all release dates $0$) and feasible for unbounded parallel batching with release dates and weighted tardiness. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
