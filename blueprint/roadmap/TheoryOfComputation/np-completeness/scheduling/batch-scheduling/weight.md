# Weight of a job in SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is a pair of an instance $(p, R, s, K)$ of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME, whose jobs are the indices $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $p$, and a list $w$ of natural numbers. The weight $w_j$ of job $j$ is the entry of $w$ at index $j$, and $0$ when $w$ has no entry at that index. That $w$ has exactly one entry per job is a condition of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
