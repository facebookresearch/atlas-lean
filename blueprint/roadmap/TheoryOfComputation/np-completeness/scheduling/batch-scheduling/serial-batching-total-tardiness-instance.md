# Instances of SERIAL BATCHING, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING, TOTAL TARDINESS is a triple $(s, L, K)$: a natural number $s$, the setup time; a list $L$ of pairs $(p_j, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the processing time and the due date of the job at position $j$ of the list; and a natural number $K$, the bound on the total tardiness. The jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs.
