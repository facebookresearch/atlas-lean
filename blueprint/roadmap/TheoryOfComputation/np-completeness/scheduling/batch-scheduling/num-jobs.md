# Number of jobs in a bounded parallel batching instance

An instance $(b, L, K)$ of the bounded parallel batching problems consists of a batch capacity $b$, a list $L$ of pairs of natural numbers, one per job, and a bound $K$; the jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs. The number of jobs is the length $n$ of $L$.
