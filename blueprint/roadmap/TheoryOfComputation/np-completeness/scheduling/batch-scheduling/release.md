# Release date of a job in BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance $(b, L, K)$ of the bounded parallel batching problems consists of a batch capacity $b$, a list $L$ of pairs of natural numbers, one per job, and a bound $K$; the jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs. In BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME the pair at position $j$ is $(p_j, r_j)$, and the release date $r_j$ of job $j$ is its second component.
