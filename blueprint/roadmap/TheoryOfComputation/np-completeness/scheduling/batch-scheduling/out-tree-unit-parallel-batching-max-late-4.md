# PARALLEL BATCHING `1|outtree;p_i=1;p-batch;b<n|L_max` is NP-complete

PARALLEL BATCHING `1|outtree;p_i=1;p-batch;b<n|L_max` is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Brucker and Knust's classification (reduction from `P|outtree;p_i=1|L_max`, Brucker, Garey and Johnson 1977).
