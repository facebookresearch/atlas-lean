# Instances of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS

An instance is a pair of a serial-batching instance $(s, J, K)$ with jobs $(p_j, w_j)$ and a list of due dates, job $j$ having the $j$-th listed due date $d_j$. That the list has one entry per job is part of well-formedness, not of the instance type.
