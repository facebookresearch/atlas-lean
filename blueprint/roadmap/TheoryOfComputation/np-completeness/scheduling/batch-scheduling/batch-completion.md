# Completion time of a serial batch

With jobs assigned to batches by $b$ and batch $k$ starting its setup at $S(k)$, batch $k$ completes at $S(k) + s$ plus the sum of the processing times of its jobs: after the setup the batch runs for that sum, and all its jobs complete together at this time. For an index $k$ that no job is assigned to, the value is $S(k) + s$.
