# Instances of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is a triple $(s, L, K)$: a natural number $s$, the setup time; a list $L$ of pairs $(p_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the processing time and the weight of the job at position $j$ of the list; and a natural number $K$, the bound on the total weighted completion time. The jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs.
