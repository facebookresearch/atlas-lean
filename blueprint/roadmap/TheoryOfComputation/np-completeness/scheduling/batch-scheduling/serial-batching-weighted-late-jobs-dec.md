# SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS as a decision problem

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(s, J, K)$ and a list of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
