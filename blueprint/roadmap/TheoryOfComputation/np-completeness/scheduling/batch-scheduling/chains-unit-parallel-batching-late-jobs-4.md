# UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, A. Gladky, H. Hoogeveen, M. Y. Kovalyov, C. N. Potts, T. Tautenhahn and S. L. van de Velde, "Scheduling a batching machine", Journal of Scheduling 1 (1998), 31-54, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-1425(199806)1:1<31::AID-JOS4>3.0.CO;2-R (the parallel batching model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Parallel batching problems" (`1|chains;p_i=1;p-batch;b<n|ΣU_i`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, by reduction from the single machine problem `1|chains;p_i=1|ΣU_i`).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity results for scheduling chains on a single machine", European Journal of Operational Research 4 (1980), 270-275, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(80)90111-3, Theorem 1 (`1|chains, p_j = 1|ΣU_j`; Garey and Johnson, [SS2], comment).
