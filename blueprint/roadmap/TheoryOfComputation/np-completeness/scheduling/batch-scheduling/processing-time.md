# Processing time of a job in SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is a tuple $(p, R, s, K)$ of a list $p = (p_0, \dots, p_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, a list $R$ of pairs of natural numbers, a set-up time $s$ and a bound $K$. The processing time of job $j \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ is the entry $p_j$ of $p$ at index $j$.
