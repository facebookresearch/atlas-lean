# UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of quadruples of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
