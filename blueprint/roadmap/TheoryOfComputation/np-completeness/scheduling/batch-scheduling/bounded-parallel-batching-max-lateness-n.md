# BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, A. Gladky, H. Hoogeveen, M. Y. Kovalyov, C. N. Potts, T. Tautenhahn and S. L. van de Velde, "Scheduling a batching machine", Journal of Scheduling 1 (1998), 31-54, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-1425(199806)1:1<31::AID-JOS4>3.0.CO;2-R (`1|p-batch;b<n|L_max` strongly NP-hard, even for `b = 2`).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class `bat_p` (parallel batching), entry `1|p-batch;b<n|L_max`: minimal NP-hard in the strong sense.
