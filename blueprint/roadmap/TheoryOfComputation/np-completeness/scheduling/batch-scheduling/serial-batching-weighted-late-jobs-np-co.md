# SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING, WEIGHTED NUMBER OF LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and M. Y. Kovalyov, "Single machine batch scheduling to minimize the weighted number of late jobs", Mathematical Methods of Operations Research 43 (1996), 1-8, doi:10.1007/BF01303431.
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations*, Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9.
