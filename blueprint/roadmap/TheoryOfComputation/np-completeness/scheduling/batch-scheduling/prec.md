# Precedence relation of an instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is a tuple $(p, R, s, K)$ of a list $p = (p_0, \dots, p_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, a list $R$ of pairs of natural numbers, a set-up time $s$ and a bound $K$. Job $j$ precedes job $j^\prime$, written $j \to j^\prime$, when the pair $(j, j^\prime)$ occurs in the list $R$. Pairs of $R$ whose entries are not job indices relate no jobs; that every pair names two jobs, that the relation is a strict order, and that it consists of chains are conditions of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
