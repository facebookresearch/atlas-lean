# BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, MAXIMUM LATENESS as a decision problem

The decision problem BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, MAXIMUM LATENESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(c, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for BOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING, MAXIMUM LATENESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
