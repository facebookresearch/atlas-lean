# SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a quadruple $(p, P, s, K)$ and a list of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
