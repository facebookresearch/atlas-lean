# Release date of a job in UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of UNBOUNDED PARALLEL BATCHING WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of quadruples $(p_j, r_j, d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers, the processing time, release date, due date and weight of job $j$, and a bound $K$ on the total weighted tardiness. The release date $r_j$ of job $j$ is the second entry of the quadruple at position $j$ of $L$.
