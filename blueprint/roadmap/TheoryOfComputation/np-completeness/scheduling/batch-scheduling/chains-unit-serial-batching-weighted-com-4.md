# SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS AND UNIT TIMES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Albers and P. Brucker, "The complexity of one-machine batching problems", Discrete Applied Mathematics 47 (1993), 87-107, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(93)90085-3 (`1|chains; pⱼ = 1; s-batch|∑ wⱼCⱼ`).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Sections 2.7 and 8.1 (the s-batching model) and the table of NP-hard single machine s-batching problems, which lists `1|chains; pⱼ = 1; s-batch|∑ wⱼCⱼ` as NP-hard in the strong sense (Albers and Brucker).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (serial batching problems).
