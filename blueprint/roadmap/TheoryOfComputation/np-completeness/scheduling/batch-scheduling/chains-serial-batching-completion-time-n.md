# SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Albers and P. Brucker, "The complexity of one-machine batching problems", Discrete Applied Mathematics 47 (1993), 87-107, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(93)90085-3 (`1|chains; s-batch|∑ Cᵢ`).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th edition, Springer (2007), Sections 2.7 and 8.1 (the single machine s-batching model) and Table 8.2 (NP-hard serial batching problems), which lists `1|chains; s-batch|∑ Cᵢ` as NP-hard with the attribution to Albers and Brucker.
