# Jobs of an instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of SERIAL BATCHING WITH CHAINS TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is a tuple $(p, R, s, K)$ of a list $p = (p_0, \dots, p_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, a list $R$ of pairs of natural numbers, a set-up time $s$ and a bound $K$. Its jobs are the indices $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $p$, one for each entry, where $n$ is the length of $p$.
