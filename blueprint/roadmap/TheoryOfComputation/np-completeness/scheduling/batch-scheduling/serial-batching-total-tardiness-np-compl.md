# SERIAL BATCHING, TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem SERIAL BATCHING, TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Baptiste and A. Jouglet, "On minimizing total tardiness in a serial batching problem", RAIRO Operations Research 35(1) (2001), 107-115, doi:10.1051/ro:2001105 (the problem and its model; "This problem 1|s-batch|ΣT_i is known to be NP-Hard in the ordinary sense", citing Du and Leung).
- J. Du and J. Y.-T. Leung, "Minimizing total tardiness on one machine is NP-hard", Mathematics of Operations Research 15(3) (1990), 483-495, doi:10.1287/moor.15.3.483.
