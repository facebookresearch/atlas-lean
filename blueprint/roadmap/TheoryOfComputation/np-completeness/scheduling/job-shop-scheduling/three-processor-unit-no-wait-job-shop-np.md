# THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-LENGTH NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-LENGTH NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING (the parent problem) and problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING (the no-wait condition `σᵢ₊₁(j) = σᵢ(j) + l(tᵢ[j])`).
- C. Sriskandarajah and P. Ladet, "Some no-wait shops scheduling problems: complexity aspect", European Journal of Operational Research 24 (1986), 424-438, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(86)90036-6.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `j_now`, entry `J3|p_ij=1;no-wait|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Sriskandarajah and Ladet 1986).
