# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING is an instance $(m, J, D)$ of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING. It is well formed when it is a well-formed JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING instance, that is, $m > 0$, every job has at least one task, every task processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job use different processors, and $D > 0$; and when $m = 2$.
