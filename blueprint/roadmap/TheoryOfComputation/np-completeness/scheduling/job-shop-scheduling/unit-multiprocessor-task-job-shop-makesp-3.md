# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 11 (the multiprocessor-task model) and Section 11.2, "Job Shop Problems" (records that `JMPT2|p_ij = 1|C_max` is NP-hard, credited to Brucker and Krämer).
- P. Brucker and A. Krämer, "Shop scheduling problems with multiprocessor tasks on dedicated processors", Annals of Operations Research 57 (1995), 13-27, doi:10.1007/BF02099688 (not available; Brucker's book records the result and its attribution).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `mpt_shop` (shop problems with multiprocessor tasks), Universität Osnabrück, which lists `JMPT2|p_ij = 1|C_max` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Brucker and Krämer (1995).
