# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85 (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8 (the dedicated case `J2|r_j, p_ij = 1|∑ w_j C_j`, recorded by Brucker and Knust as strongly NP-hard).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `mpt_shop` (shop problems with multiprocessor tasks), Universität Osnabrück, which lists `JMPT2|r_i; p_ij = 1|∑ w_i C_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Timkovsky (1998).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 11 (the multiprocessor-task model).
