# TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of lists and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
