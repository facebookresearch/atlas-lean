# Chain arcs of an instance of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is a pair of a unit-time multi-purpose job-shop instance $(m, J, K)$, whose jobs are the positions of $J$ and whose last component is read as the bound $K$ on the total completion time, and a list $A$ of pairs of natural numbers. The job data reuse the multi-purpose job-shop encoding, but in the multiprocessor task reading each task requires all of its listed processors at the same time, not a choice of one of them. Job $j$ is the immediate predecessor of job $j^\prime$ when the pair $(j, j^\prime)$ occurs in $A$. That arcs name jobs, that no two arcs share a tail or a head, and that the arcs have no cycle are conditions of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
