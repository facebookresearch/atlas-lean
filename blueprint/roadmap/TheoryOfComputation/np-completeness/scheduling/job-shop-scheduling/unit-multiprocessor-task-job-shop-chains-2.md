# Feasible instances of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when some unit-time multiprocessor task job-shop schedule starts, for every listed arc $(j, j')$, the first task of job $j'$ no earlier than the last task of job $j$ completes, and the job completion times sum to at most $K$.
