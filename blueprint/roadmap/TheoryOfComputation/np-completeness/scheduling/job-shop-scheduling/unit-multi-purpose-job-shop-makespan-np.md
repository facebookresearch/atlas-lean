# THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 242, problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment (equal task lengths, `m = 3`).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Computational complexity of discrete optimization problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 4 (1979), 121-140, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70821-5.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, online classification, class `mpm_shop` (shop problems with multi-purpose machines), Universität Osnabrück.
