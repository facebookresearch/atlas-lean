# THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
