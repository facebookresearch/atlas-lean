# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Röck, "Some new results in flow shop scheduling", Zeitschrift für Operations Research 28 (1984), 1-16, doi:10.1007/BF01919082 (the two-processor no-wait flow shop with total completion time, `F2|no-wait|∑ C_i`, is NP-hard in the strong sense; it is the special case of this problem in which every job has a task on processor 1 followed by a task on processor 2).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class table "Job-shop problems with nowait" (`J2|no-wait|∑ C_i`: minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, Röck (1984)).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problems [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING and [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING (definitions; the comment to [SS16] states the sum bound `K` and the no-wait versions of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING).
