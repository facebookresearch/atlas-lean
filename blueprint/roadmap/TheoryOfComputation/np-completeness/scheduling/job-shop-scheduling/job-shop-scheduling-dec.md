# JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
