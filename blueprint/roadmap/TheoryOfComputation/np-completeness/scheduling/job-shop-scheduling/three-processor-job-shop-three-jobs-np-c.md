# THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH THREE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH THREE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING (the parent problem).
- Yu. N. Sotskov and N. V. Shakhlevich, "NP-hardness of shop-scheduling problems with three jobs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 59 (1995), 237-266, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(95)80004-N.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `j_nop`, entry `J3|n=3|C_max` (minimal NP-hard in the ordinary sense, citing Sotskov and Shakhlevich 1995).
