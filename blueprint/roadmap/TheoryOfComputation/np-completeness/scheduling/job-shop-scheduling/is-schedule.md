# Job-shop schedules with release dates and job precedence

Start times $\sigma$ and job completion times $C$ form a schedule when there are job-shop start times one-to-one on the tasks of each processor, a later task on a processor starting no earlier than an earlier one ends, and the tasks of each job in order, the first operation of each job starts at or after $r_j$, $C_j$ is the end of job $j$'s last operation, and every operation of $b$ starts no earlier than $C_a$ whenever $a$ precedes $b$.
