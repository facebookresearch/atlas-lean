# Job-shop schedules with release dates and job precedence

Start times $\sigma$ and job completion times $C$ form a schedule when there are job-shop start times one-to-one on the tasks of each processor, a later task on a processor starting no earlier than an earlier one ends, and the tasks of each job in order, the first operation of each job with operations starts at or after $r_j$, $C_j$ is the end of the last operation of each job $j$ with operations, and every operation of $b$ starts no earlier than $C_a$ whenever $a$ precedes $b$. A job with no operations has no first or last operation, so $r_j$ does not constrain it and its $C_j$ is bounded only by the start times of the operations of the jobs it precedes. A job with no listed triple $(r_j, d_j, w_j)$ has $r_j = 0$.
