# Well-formed instances of THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH THREE JOBS

An instance $(m, J, D)$ is well-formed for THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH THREE JOBS when it is well-formed for JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING ($m > 0$, every job has at least one task, every task's processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job use different processors, and the last component is positive) and $m = 3$ and there are exactly three jobs.
