# THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of lists and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for THREE-MACHINE UNIT-TIME MULTI-PURPOSE JOB SHOP, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
