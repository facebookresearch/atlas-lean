# JOB SHOP WITH A FIXED NUMBER OF JOBS, WEIGHTED TARDINESS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem JOB SHOP WITH A FIXED NUMBER OF JOBS, WEIGHTED TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a job-shop instance, a list of triples and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed for $k$ jobs and feasible for the weighted-tardiness job shop. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
