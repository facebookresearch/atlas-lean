# THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH AT MOST TWO TASKS PER JOB as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH AT MOST TWO TASKS PER JOB accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH AT MOST TWO TASKS PER JOB and feasible for JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING (a schedule respecting processors and job order in which every job finishes by $D$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
