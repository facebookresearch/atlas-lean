# Well-formed instances of THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance $(m, J, K)$ is well-formed for THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME when it is well-formed for JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING ($m > 0$, every job has at least one task, every task's processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job use different processors, and the last component is positive), $m = 3$, and every task has length $1$.
