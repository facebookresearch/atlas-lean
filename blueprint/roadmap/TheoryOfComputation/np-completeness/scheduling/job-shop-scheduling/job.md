# Jobs of an instance of JOB SHOP WITH A FIXED NUMBER OF JOBS, WEIGHTED TARDINESS

An instance of JOB SHOP WITH A FIXED NUMBER OF JOBS, WEIGHTED TARDINESS is a triple of a JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING instance $(m, J, K)$, whose list $J$ has one list of operations per job and whose last component is the bound $K$ on the total weighted tardiness; a list $T$ of triples $(r_j, d_j, w_j)$ of natural numbers; and a list of precedence pairs. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $J$, where $n$ is the length of $J$, one for each listed job.
