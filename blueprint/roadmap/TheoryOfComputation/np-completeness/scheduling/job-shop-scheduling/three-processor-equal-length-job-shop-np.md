# THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH EQUAL TASK LENGTHS is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH EQUAL TASK LENGTHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 242, problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment: "If in the nonpreemptive case all tasks have the same length, the problem is NP-complete for `m = 3`".
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Computational complexity of discrete optimization problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 4 (1979), 121-140, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70821-5 (cited by Garey and Johnson as [Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan, 1978b]).
