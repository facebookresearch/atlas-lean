# Operations of a unit-time multi-purpose job shop

An instance of a unit-time multi-purpose job shop is a triple $(m, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $J$ of jobs, job $j$ being the list of its operations in order, each operation given by the list of its eligible machines, and a bound $K$. An operation of the instance is a pair $(j, k)$ of a job $j$, a position of $J$, and a position $k$ in the operation list of job $j$, numbered from $0$.
