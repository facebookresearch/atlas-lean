# Processor of a task in JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING

An instance of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING is a triple $(m, J, D)$ of a number $m$ of processors, a list $J$ of jobs, and a deadline $D$; job $j$, at position $j$ of $J$, is the list of its tasks $t_1[j], \dots, t_{n_j}[j]$ in order, each task given as the pair $(l(t), p(t))$ of its length and its processor, all natural numbers. The processor of a task $(j, k)$ is the second component $p(t)$ of the pair at position $k$ of the task list of job $j$. That every processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$ is a condition of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
