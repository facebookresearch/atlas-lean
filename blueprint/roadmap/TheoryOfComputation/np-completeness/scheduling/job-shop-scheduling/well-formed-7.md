# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is an instance $(m, J, K)$ of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING whose last component is read as the bound $K$ on the total completion time. It is well formed when it is a well-formed JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING instance, that is, $m > 0$, every job has at least one task, every task processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job use different processors, and $K > 0$; and when $m = 2$.
