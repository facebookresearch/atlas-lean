# THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-LENGTH NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-LENGTH NO-WAIT JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for THREE-PROCESSOR UNIT-LENGTH JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING ($m > 0$, every job has at least one task, every task's processor lies in $\{1, \dots, m\}$, consecutive tasks of a job use different processors, and the last component is positive, $m = 3$, all tasks of length $1$) and feasible for no-wait scheduling with deadline $D$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
