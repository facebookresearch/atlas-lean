# Instances of job shops with job data and precedence

An instance is a triple of a JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING instance $(m, J, K)$, a list of triples $(r_j, d_j, w_j)$, job $j$ having the $j$-th listed triple, and a list of precedence pairs $(i, j)$. That the triple list has one entry per job, and that the pairs name jobs, is part of well-formedness, not of the instance type.
