# Eligible machines of an operation in a unit-time multi-purpose job shop

An instance of a unit-time multi-purpose job shop is a triple $(m, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $J$ of jobs, job $j$ being the list of its operations in order, each operation given by the list of its eligible machines, and a bound $K$. The eligible machines $\mu_{jk}$ of an operation $(j, k)$ are the list at position $k$ of the operation list of job $j$. That this list is nonempty and lists machines among $1, \dots, m$ is a condition of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
