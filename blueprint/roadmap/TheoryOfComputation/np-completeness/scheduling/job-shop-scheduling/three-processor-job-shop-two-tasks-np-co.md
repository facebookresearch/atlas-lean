# THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH AT MOST TWO TASKS PER JOB is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH AT MOST TWO TASKS PER JOB belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 242, problem [SS18] JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING, comment: "NP-complete (in the ordinary sense) if ... `m = 3` and `n_j ≤ 2` for all `j ∈ J`".
- T. Gonzalez and S. Sahni, "Flowshop and jobshop schedules: complexity and approximation", Operations Research 26 (1978), 36-52, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.36.
