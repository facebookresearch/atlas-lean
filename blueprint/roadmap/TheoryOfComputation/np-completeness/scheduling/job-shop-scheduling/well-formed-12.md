# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR UNIT-TIME MULTIPROCESSOR TASK JOB SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is well-formed when the job-shop instance is well-formed ($m = 2$, every job has at least one task, and every task has a nonempty processor set in $\{1, \dots, m\}$), every arc names two jobs, no two distinct arcs share a tail or a head, and the arcs have no directed cycle.
