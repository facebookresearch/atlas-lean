# Instances of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING

An instance of JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING is a triple $(m, J, D)$ of a natural number $m$ (the number of processors), a list $J$ of jobs, each job the list of its tasks in order, each task a pair $(l, p)$ of its length $l$ and its processor $p$, and a natural number $D$ (the deadline).
