# FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT as a decision problem

The decision problem FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, A, K, f)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) and [feasible](feasible-register-assignment-feasible.md) for FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
