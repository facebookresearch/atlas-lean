# DISCRETE TIME-COST TRADEOFF as a decision problem

The decision problem DISCRETE TIME-COST TRADEOFF accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, A, T, d, b)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for DISCRETE TIME-COST TRADEOFF. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
