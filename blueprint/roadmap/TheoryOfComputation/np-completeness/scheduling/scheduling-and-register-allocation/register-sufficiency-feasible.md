# Feasible instances of REGISTER SUFFICIENCY

An instance is feasible when some computation uses at most $K$ registers: an ordering of the vertices and register sets $S_0 = \emptyset, \dots, S_n$ of size at most $K$, every in-degree-zero vertex in $S_n$, and at each step the computed vertex held afterwards, no other value newly loaded, and all its operands held before.
