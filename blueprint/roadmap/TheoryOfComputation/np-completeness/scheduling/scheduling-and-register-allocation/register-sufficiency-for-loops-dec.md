# REGISTER SUFFICIENCY FOR LOOPS as a decision problem

The decision problem REGISTER SUFFICIENCY FOR LOOPS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(N, V, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](register-sufficiency-for-loops-feasible.md) for REGISTER SUFFICIENCY FOR LOOPS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
