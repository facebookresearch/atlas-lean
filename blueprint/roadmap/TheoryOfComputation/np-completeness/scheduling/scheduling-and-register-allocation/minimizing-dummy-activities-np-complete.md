# MINIMIZING DUMMY ACTIVITIES IN PERT NETWORKS is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMIZING DUMMY ACTIVITIES IN PERT NETWORKS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND44] MINIMIZING DUMMY ACTIVITIES IN PERT NETWORKS, p. 218.
- M. S. Krishnamoorthy and N. Deo, "Complexity of the minimum-dummy-activities problem in a PERT network", Networks 9 (1979), 189-194, doi:10.1002/net.3230090302.
