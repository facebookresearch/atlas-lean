# REGISTER SUFFICIENCY as a decision problem

The decision problem REGISTER SUFFICIENCY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, A, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-7.md) and [feasible](register-sufficiency-feasible.md) for REGISTER SUFFICIENCY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
