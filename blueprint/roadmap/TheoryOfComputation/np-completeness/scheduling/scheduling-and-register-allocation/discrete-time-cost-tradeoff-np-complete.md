# DISCRETE TIME-COST TRADEOFF is NP-complete

The decision problem DISCRETE TIME-COST TRADEOFF belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. De, E. J. Dunne, J. B. Ghosh, and C. E. Wells, "Complexity of the discrete time-cost tradeoff problem for project networks", Operations Research 45 (1997), 302-306, doi:10.1287/opre.45.2.302 (Section 2: the DTCT decision problem is strongly NP-hard, by a reduction from EXACTLY-ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT).
