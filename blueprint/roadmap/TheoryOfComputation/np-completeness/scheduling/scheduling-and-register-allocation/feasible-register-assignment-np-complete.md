# FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT is NP-complete

The decision problem FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 272, problem [PO2] FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT.
- R. Sethi, "Complete register allocation problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 4 (1975), 226-248, doi:10.1137/0204020.
