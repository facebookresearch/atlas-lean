# UET-UCT SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem UET-UCT SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. J. Rayward-Smith, "UET scheduling with unit interprocessor communication delays", Discrete Applied Mathematics 18 (1987), 55-71, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(87)90042-4.
- J. A. Hoogeveen, J. K. Lenstra, and B. Veltman, "Three, four, five, six, or the complexity of scheduling with communication delays", Operations Research Letters 16 (1994), 129-137, doi:10.1016/0167-6377(94)90024-8, Theorem 2 (NP-complete already for the bound `4`).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (the problem without communication delays).
