# SCHEDULING WITH INDIVIDUAL DEADLINES as a decision problem

The decision problem SCHEDULING WITH INDIVIDUAL DEADLINES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, P, d)$ (a natural number, a list of pairs and a list of natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SCHEDULING WITH INDIVIDUAL DEADLINES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
