# PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH DEADLINE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH DEADLINE 3 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (comment: remains NP-complete for `D = 3`).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22.
