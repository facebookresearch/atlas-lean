# PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING ON HIERARCHIC PARALLEL ORDERS is NP-complete

The decision problem PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING ON HIERARCHIC PARALLEL ORDERS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
