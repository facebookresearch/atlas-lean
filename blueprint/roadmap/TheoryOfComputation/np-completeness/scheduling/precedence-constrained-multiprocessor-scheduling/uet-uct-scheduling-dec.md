# UET-UCT SCHEDULING as a decision problem

The decision problem UET-UCT SCHEDULING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, p, A, d)$ (a natural number, a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for UET-UCT SCHEDULING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
