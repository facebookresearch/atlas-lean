# UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, A. Gladky, H. Hoogeveen, M. Y. Kovalyov, C. N. Potts, T. Tautenhahn and S. L. van de Velde, "Scheduling a batching machine", Journal of Scheduling 1 (1998), 31-54, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-1425(199806)1:1<31::AID-JOS4>3.0.CO;2-R (the parallel batching model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Parallel batching problems" (`1|prec;p_i=1;p-batch;b<n|C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, by reduction from the parallel machine problem `P|prec;p_i=1|C_max`).
- J. D. Ullman, "NP-complete scheduling problems", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 10 (1975), 384-393, doi:10.1016/S0022-0000(75)80008-0 (`P|prec;p_i=1|C_max`; Garey and Johnson, [SS9]).
