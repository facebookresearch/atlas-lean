# TWO-PROCESSOR PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH TASK LENGTHS 1 AND 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH TASK LENGTHS 1 AND 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (comment: the `m = 2` case becomes NP-complete if both task lengths 1 and 2 are allowed).
- J. D. Ullman, "NP-complete scheduling problems", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 10 (1975), 384-393, doi:10.1016/S0022-0000(75)80008-0.
