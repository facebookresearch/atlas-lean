# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE

An instance $(l, m, P, D)$ is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE when $m = 2$, every task length is positive, the pairs in $P$ form chains on the tasks (no repeated pairs, both ends name tasks, no task has two immediate successors or two immediate predecessors, no directed cycle), and $D > 0$. Lengths need not be $1$.
