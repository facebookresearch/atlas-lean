# Deadline $d(t)$ of a task in SCHEDULING WITH INDIVIDUAL DEADLINES

For an instance of SCHEDULING WITH INDIVIDUAL DEADLINES with deadline list $d(0), \dots, d(n - 1)$, the tasks are $0, \dots, n - 1$, one for each listed deadline, and the deadline of task $t$ is the entry $d(t)$. Every task has an entry, since the tasks are indexed by the positions of the list.
