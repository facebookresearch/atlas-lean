# Order of a hierarchic parallel graph

The order of a derivation on its nodes $0, \dots, \mathrm{size}-1$ is empty for a single node. For a series composition of $a$ and $b$, the nodes of $a$ come first (numbered from $0$), those of $b$ follow, each part keeps its own order, and every node of $a$ is before every node of $b$. For a diamond with top $a$, middle parts and bottom $c$, numbered in that order, each block keeps its own order, every top node is before every middle and bottom node, and every middle node is before every bottom node.
