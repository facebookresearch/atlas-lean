# TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Du, J. Y.-T. Leung, and G. H. Young, "Scheduling chain-structured tasks to minimize makespan and mean flow time", Information and Computation 92 (1991), 219-236, doi:10.1016/0890-5401(91)90009-Q (minimizing the makespan of a nonpreemptive schedule of chains on `m` identical processors is NP-hard in the strong sense for each fixed `m > 1`).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Parallel machine problems without preemption" (`P2|chains|C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Du, Leung, and Young 1991).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problems [SS8] MULTIPROCESSOR SCHEDULING and [SS9] PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (the `m`-processor schedule and the parent problem).
