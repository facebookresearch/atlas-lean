# UET-UCT SCHEDULING ON UNBOUNDEDLY MANY PROCESSORS WITH MAKESPAN AT MOST 6 is NP-complete

The decision problem UET-UCT SCHEDULING ON UNBOUNDEDLY MANY PROCESSORS WITH MAKESPAN AT MOST 6 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. A. Hoogeveen, J. K. Lenstra and B. Veltman, "Three, four, five, six, or the complexity of scheduling with communication delays", Operations Research Letters 16 (1994), 129-137, doi:10.1016/0167-6377(94)90024-8 (problem `P̄|prec,c=1,p_j=1|C_max`, schedule of length at most 6; Theorem 4 of the preprint, CWI Report BS-R9229, 1992).
