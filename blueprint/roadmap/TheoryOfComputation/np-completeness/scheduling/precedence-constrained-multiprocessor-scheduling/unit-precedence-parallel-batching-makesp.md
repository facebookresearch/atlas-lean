# UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(l, m, P, D)$ (a list of natural numbers, a natural number, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for UNIT-TIME PARALLEL BATCHING WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN and feasible for PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING with $b$ processors and deadline $K$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
