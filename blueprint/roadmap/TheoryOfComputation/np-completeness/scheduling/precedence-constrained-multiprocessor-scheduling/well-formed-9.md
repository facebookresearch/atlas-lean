# Well-formed instances of UET-UCT SCHEDULING ON UNBOUNDEDLY MANY PROCESSORS WITH MAKESPAN AT MOST 6

An instance $(m, p, A, d)$ is well-formed when it is well-formed for UET-UCT SCHEDULING ($m > 0$, every processing time is $1$, every arc names two tasks, and the precedence graph is acyclic), there are at least as many processors as tasks ($n \le m$), and $d = 6$.
