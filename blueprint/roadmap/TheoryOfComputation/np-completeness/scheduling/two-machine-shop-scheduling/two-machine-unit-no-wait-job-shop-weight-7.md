# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP, TOTAL WEIGHTED TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP, TOTAL WEIGHTED TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of quadruples and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP, TOTAL WEIGHTED TARDINESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
