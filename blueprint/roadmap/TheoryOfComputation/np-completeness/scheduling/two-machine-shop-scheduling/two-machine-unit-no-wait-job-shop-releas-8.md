# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85(2) (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Job-shop problems with nowait", which lists `J2|p_ij = 1; r_i; no-wait|ΣC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense with the attribution to Timkovsky (1998).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, Springer, Section 6.4 (the job-shop model).
