# TWO-MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X.
- recorded in E. L. Lawler, J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and D. B. Shmoys, "Sequencing and scheduling: algorithms and complexity" (1989/1993), and in P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for scheduling problems" (minimal NP-hard `F2|r_i|C_max`).
