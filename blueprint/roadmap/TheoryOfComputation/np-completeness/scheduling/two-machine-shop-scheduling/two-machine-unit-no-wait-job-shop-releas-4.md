# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Is a unit-time job shop not easier than identical parallel machines?", Discrete Applied Mathematics 85 (1998), 149-162, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00032-8.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class table "Job-shop problems with nowait" (`J2|p_ij=1;r_i;no-wait|L_max`: minimal NP-hard, in the strong sense, Timkovsky (1998)).
