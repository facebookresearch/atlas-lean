# Operations of an instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP, TOTAL WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP, TOTAL WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is a pair $(J, K)$ of a list $J$ of jobs, job $j$ given as a triple $(\mu_{j1}, n_j, w_j)$ of the machine of its first operation, its number $n_j$ of operations and its weight, and a bound $K$. An operation of the instance is a pair $(j, k)$ of a job $j$, a position of $J$, and a position $k \in \{0, \dots, n_j - 1\}$ among its operations, numbered from $0$.
