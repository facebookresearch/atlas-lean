# TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH EQUAL DELAYS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH EQUAL DELAYS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- W. Yu, *The two-machine flow shop problem with delays and the one-machine total tardiness problem*, PhD thesis, Technische Universiteit Eindhoven (1996), Chapter 2, Appendix "The Open Shop Problem with Equal Delays", Theorem 5 (pp. 17-18).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück (online tables), class "Open-shop problems with transportation delays" (records the problem `O2|t_ikl = T|Cmax` as minimal NP-hard, citing Yu (1996)).
