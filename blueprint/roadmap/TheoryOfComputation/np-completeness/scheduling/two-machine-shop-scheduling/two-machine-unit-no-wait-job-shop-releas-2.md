# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME NO-WAIT JOB SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance is feasible when there are start times in which distinct operations on the same machine occupy disjoint unit intervals and each later operation of a job starts exactly one time unit after the previous one, the first operation of every job with operations starts at or after $r_j$, and the last operation of every job $j$ with operations completes at $C_j$ with $C_j - d_j \le K$ in the integers. A job with no operations has no first or last operation, so $r_j$, $d_j$ and $K$ do not constrain it.
