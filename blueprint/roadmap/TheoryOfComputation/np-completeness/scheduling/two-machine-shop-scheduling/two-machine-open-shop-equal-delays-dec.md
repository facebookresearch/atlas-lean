# TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH EQUAL DELAYS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH EQUAL DELAYS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(T_1, T_2, J, K)$ (two natural numbers, a list of triples and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH EQUAL DELAYS and feasible for the two-machine open shop with two delay values. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
