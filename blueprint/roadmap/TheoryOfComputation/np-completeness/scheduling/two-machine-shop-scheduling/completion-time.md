# Completion time of a job (unit no-wait job shop)

Under start times $\sigma$, job $j$ completes at $C_j = \max_k (\sigma(j, k) + p_{jk})$, the latest completion time of its operations. The maximum is taken in $\mathbb{N}$, where a maximum over no elements is $0$, so a job with no operations has $C_j = 0$.
