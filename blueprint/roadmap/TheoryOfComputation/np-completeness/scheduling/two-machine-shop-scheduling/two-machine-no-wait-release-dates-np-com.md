# TWO-MACHINE NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Röck, "Some new results in flow shop scheduling", Zeitschrift für Operations Research 28 (1984), 1-16, doi:10.1007/BF01919082; recorded in P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for scheduling problems" (minimal NP-hard `F2|r_i;no-wait|C_max`, Röck 1984) and in E. L. Lawler, J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and D. B. Shmoys, "Sequencing and scheduling: algorithms and complexity" (1989/1993) (`F2|no wait|L_max` strongly NP-hard, Röck 1984).
