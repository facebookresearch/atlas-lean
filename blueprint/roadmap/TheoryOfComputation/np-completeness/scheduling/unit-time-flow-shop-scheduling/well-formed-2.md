# Well-formed instances of FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax

An instance of FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax is well formed when `m ≥ 1`, every job has `m` unit operations, the arcs are well formed (they name jobs and are acyclic), and the precedence relation is an out-tree: each job has at most one immediate predecessor.
