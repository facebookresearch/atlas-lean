# FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax is NP-complete

The FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, classification tables (class "Flow-shop problems without preemption", minimal NP-hard entry `F | p_ij = 1; outtree | L_max`).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for single-machine problems with positive finish-start time-lags", Computing 63 (1999), 299-316, doi:10.1007/s006070050036.
