# Instances of FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax

An instance of FLOW SHOP F|p_ij=1;outtree|Lmax is a quadruple $(m, J, A, K)$: a natural number $m$ of machines; a list $J$ of jobs, the $j$-th entry a pair of a stored list of processing times (intended as $(p_{j1}, \dots, p_{jm})$) and a due date $d_j$; a list $A$ of precedence arcs $(i, j)$ of natural numbers (job $i$ precedes job $j$); and a bound $K$. The due dates and the bound are integers, each given by any pair $(a, b)$ of natural numbers standing for $a - b$. The raw instance places no condition on these lists: a stored processing-time list may have any length and any entries, the job list may be empty, and an arc may name a number that is not a job. That $m \ge 1$, that every job stores exactly $m$ processing times, all equal to $1$, and that the arcs name jobs and form an acyclic out-tree relation are part of [well-formedness](well-formed-2.md).
