# MULTI-MODE RCPSP FEASIBILITY (NONRENEWABLE RESOURCES) is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-MODE RCPSP FEASIBILITY (NONRENEWABLE RESOURCES) is NP-complete: it
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Kolisch and Drexl (1997); Hartmann (2001).
