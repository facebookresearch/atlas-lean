# RESOURCE-CONSTRAINED PROJECT SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem
RESOURCE-CONSTRAINED PROJECT SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), BIN PACKING; Jacek Błażewicz, Jan Karel Lenstra, and Alexander H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Scheduling subject to resource constraints: classification and complexity", Discrete Applied Mathematics 5 (1983), 11-24 (classification).
