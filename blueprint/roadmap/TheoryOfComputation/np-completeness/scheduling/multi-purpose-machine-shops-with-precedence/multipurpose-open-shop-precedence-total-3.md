# MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5: Chapter 10 (the MPM open-shop model), Section 1.3 (precedence relations) and Table 5.3 (`P|p_i = 1; prec|ΣC_i` is NP-hard in the strong sense, Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `OMPM|prec; p_ij = 1|ΣC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense with the attribution to Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan (1978).
