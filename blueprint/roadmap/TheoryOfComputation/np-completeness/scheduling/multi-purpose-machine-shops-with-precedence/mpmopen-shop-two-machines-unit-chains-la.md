# Instances of two-machine stage shops with chains and due dates

An instance is a quadruple $(\mu, d, A, K)$ of stage machine sets $\mu = (\mu_1, \dots, \mu_r)$, due dates $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ of the jobs, chain arcs $A$, and a bound $K$. All processing times are $1$ and there are two machines.
