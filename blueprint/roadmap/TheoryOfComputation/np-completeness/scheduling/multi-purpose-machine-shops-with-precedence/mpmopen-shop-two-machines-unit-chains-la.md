# Instances of two-machine stage shops with chains and due dates

An instance is a quadruple $(\mu, d, A, K)$ of stage machine sets $\mu = (\mu_1, \dots, \mu_r)$, a list $d$ of due dates (its entries are the jobs, job $i$ having due date $d_i$), a list $A$ of arcs $(i, k)$ (job $i$ before job $k$), and a bound $K$; that the arcs form chains is part of well-formedness. All processing times are $1$ and there are two machines.
