# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance $(\mu, d, A, K)$ is feasible when there is a unit-time multi-purpose flow-shop schedule on the two machines respecting the arcs of $A$ (each operation on a machine of its stage, stages in order, no two operations on one machine at once, and successor jobs start after predecessors complete) in which, with $C_j$ the completion time of job $j$ ($0$ when there are no stages), the total tardiness $\sum_j (C_j - d_j)$, with truncated subtraction, is at most $K$.
