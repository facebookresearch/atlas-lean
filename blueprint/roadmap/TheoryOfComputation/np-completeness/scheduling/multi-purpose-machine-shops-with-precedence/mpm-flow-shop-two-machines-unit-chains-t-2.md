# TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 10 (the MPM flow-shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `FMPM2|chains;p_ij=1|ΣT_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense ("Single-machine problem").
- J. Y.-T. Leung and G. H. Young, "Minimizing total tardiness on a single machine with precedence constraints", ORSA Journal on Computing 2 (1990), 346-352, doi:10.1287/ijoc.2.4.346 (the single-machine problem `1|chains;p_j=1|ΣT_j`).
