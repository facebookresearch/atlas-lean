# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149 (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", which lists `OMPT2|chains; p_ij = 1|∑ T_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense with the attribution to Timkovsky (2003).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 11 (the MPT open-shop model) and Section 1.3 (precedence relations).
