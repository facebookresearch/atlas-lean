# TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE OPEN SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE OPEN SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
