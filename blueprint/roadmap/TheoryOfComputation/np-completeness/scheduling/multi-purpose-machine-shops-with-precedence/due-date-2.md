# Due date of a job in MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS

An instance of MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH RELEASE DATES, LATE JOBS is a tuple $(m, M, J, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $M$ of stage machine sets, a list $J$ of jobs, each given as a pair $(p, (r, d))$ of a list $p$ of processing times and a pair of natural numbers, and a bound $K$. The due date $d_j$ of job $j$, a position of $J$, is the second component $d$ of the pair $(r, d)$ listed with it.
