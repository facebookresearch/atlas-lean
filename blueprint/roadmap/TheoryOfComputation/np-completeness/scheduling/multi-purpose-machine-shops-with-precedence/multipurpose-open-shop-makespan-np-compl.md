# MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (the MPM open-shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines" (`OMPM||C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to an unpublished result of J. K. Lenstra).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS14] OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING (the case `μ_j = {M_j}`; comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense for m arbitrary [Lenstra, 1977]").
- T. Gonzalez and S. Sahni, "Open shop scheduling to minimize finish time", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 665-679, doi:10.1145/321978.321985.
