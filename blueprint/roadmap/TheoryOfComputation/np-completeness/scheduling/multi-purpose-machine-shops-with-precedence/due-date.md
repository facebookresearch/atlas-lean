# Due date of a job in TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL TARDINESS is a tuple $(m, M, J, A, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, a list $M$ of stage machine sets, a list $J$ of jobs, each given as a pair $(p, d)$ of a list $p$ of processing times and a natural number $d$, a list $A$ of chain arcs, and a bound $K$. The due date $d_j$ of job $j$, a position of $J$, is the second component of the pair at position $j$ of $J$.
