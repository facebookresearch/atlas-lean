# Instances of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance is a quintuple $(m, \mu, J, A, K)$ of a number $m$ of machines, stage machine sets $\mu$, a list $J$ of jobs, each a pair of its processing times and its due date $d_j$, a list $A$ of arcs (job $i$ before job $k$ for an arc $(i, k)$), and a bound $K$. That the arcs form chains is part of well-formedness.
