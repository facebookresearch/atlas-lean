# MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH PRECEDENCE, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 4th ed., Springer (2004), Chapter 11 (shop problems with multiprocessor tasks: the MPT open-shop model) and Section 1.3 (precedence relations).
- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149 (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", which lists `OMPT|prec; p_ij = 1|C_max` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense with the attribution to Timkovsky (2003).
