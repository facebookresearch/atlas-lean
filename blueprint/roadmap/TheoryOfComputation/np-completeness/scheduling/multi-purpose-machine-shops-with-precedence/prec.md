# Precedence relation of a list of job pairs

Given a list $R$ of pairs of natural numbers and a number $n$ of jobs $0, \dots, n-1$, job $i$ precedes job $k$, written $i \to k$, when the pair $(i, k)$ occurs in $R$. Pairs whose entries are not below $n$ relate no jobs. That the listed pairs name jobs and form a strict partial order is a separate condition, the well-formedness of the precedence pairs.
