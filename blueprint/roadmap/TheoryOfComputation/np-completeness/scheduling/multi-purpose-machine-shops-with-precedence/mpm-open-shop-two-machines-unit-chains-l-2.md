# TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE OPEN SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE OPEN SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149(2) (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1; P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 10 (the MPM open-shop model); P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `OMPM2|chains;p_ij=1|ΣU_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Timkovsky (2003), and class "Open-shop problems without preemption", which lists the special case `O2|p_ij=1;chains|ΣU_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Timkovsky (2003).
