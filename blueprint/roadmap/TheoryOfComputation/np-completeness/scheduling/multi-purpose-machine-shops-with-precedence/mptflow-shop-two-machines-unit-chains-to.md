# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING TOTAL TARDINESS

An instance $(\mu, d, A, K)$ is feasible when there is a unit-time multiprocessor task flow-shop schedule respecting the arcs of $A$ (stages in order, each operation occupying all machines of its stage, no overlap of operations sharing a machine, and successor jobs start after predecessors complete) in which, with $C_j$ the completion time of job $j$ ($0$ when there are no stages), the total tardiness $\sum_j (C_j - d_j)$, with truncated subtraction, is at most $K$.
