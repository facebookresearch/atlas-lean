# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK FLOW SHOP WITH UNIT TIMES AND CHAINS, MINIMIZING LATE JOBS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), doi:10.1007/978-3-540-69516-5, Chapter 11 (the MPT flow-shop model).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", which lists `FMPT2|chains;p_ij=1|ΣU_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense ("Single-machine problem").
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity results for scheduling chains on a single machine", European Journal of Operational Research 4 (1980), 270-275, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(80)90111-3, Theorem 1 (the single-machine problem `1|chains;p_j=1|ΣU_j`).
