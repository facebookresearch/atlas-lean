# Feasible instances of SIMPLE ASSEMBLY LINE BALANCING (SALBP-1)

An instance of SIMPLE ASSEMBLY LINE BALANCING (SALBP-1) is feasible when there is an assignment `f` of the tasks to the stations `0, …, K - 1` such that `f i ≤ f j` for every arc `(i, j)` and the task times at every station sum to at most `c`. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
