# THREE-PROCESSOR RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH UNIT BOUNDS is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING WITH UNIT BOUNDS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Błażewicz, J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Scheduling subject to resource constraints: classification and complexity", Discrete Applied Mathematics 5 (1983), 11-24, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(83)90012-4 (the problem `P3 | res·11, p_j = 1 | C_max`).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problems [SS10] RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING (parent problem) and [GT11] PARTITION INTO TRIANGLES (source problem of the reduction below).
