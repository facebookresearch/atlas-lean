# Tasks of an instance of RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING

An instance of RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING is a tuple $(m, r, B, T, D)$ of a number $m$ of processors, a number $r$ of resources, a list $B = (B_0, \dots, B_{r-1})$ of resource bounds, a list $T$ of rows, the row of task $t$ being $(R_0(t), \dots, R_{r-1}(t))$, and a deadline $D$. Its tasks are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $T$, one per row of the requirement table, where $n$ is the length of $T$.
