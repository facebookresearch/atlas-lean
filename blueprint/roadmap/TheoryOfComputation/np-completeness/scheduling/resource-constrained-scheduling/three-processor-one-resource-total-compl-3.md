# THREE-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Błażewicz, J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Scheduling subject to resource constraints: classification and complexity", Discrete Applied Mathematics 5 (1983), 11-24, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(83)90012-4 (Section 4.1: the strong NP-hardness of `P3 | res1··, p_j = 1 | C_max`, their Theorem 4, carries over to `ΣC_j`).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS13] SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, pp. 240-241, comment: "If resources are allowed, the same subcases mentioned under RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING are NP-complete, even for equal weights [Blazewicz, 1977a]", and [SS10] RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING, p. 239, comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense, even if `r = 1` and `m = 3`".
