# TWO-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND ALL-OR-NOTHING REQUIREMENTS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND ALL-OR-NOTHING REQUIREMENTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. D. Ullman, "Complexity of sequencing problems", in E. G. Coffman, Jr. (ed.), *Computer and Job/Shop Scheduling Theory*, John Wiley & Sons, New York (1976), 139-164.
