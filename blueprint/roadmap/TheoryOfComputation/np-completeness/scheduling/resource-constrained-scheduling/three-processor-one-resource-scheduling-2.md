# THREE-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS10] RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING, comment: NP-complete in the strong sense, even if `r = 1` and `m = 3`.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, "Complexity results for multiprocessor scheduling under resource constraints", SIAM Journal on Computing 4 (1975), 397-411, doi:10.1137/0204035.
