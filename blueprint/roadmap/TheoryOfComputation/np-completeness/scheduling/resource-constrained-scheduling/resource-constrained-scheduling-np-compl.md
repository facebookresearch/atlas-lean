# RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS10] RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, "Complexity results for multiprocessor scheduling under resource constraints", SIAM Journal on Computing 4 (1975), 397-411, doi:10.1137/0204035.
