# TWO-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND ALL-OR-NOTHING REQUIREMENTS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND ALL-OR-NOTHING REQUIREMENTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a quintuple $(m, r, B, R, D)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR ONE-RESOURCE SCHEDULING WITH PRECEDENCE AND ALL-OR-NOTHING REQUIREMENTS and feasible for resource-constrained scheduling with a partial order. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
