# TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND ONE UNIT RESOURCE is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND ONE UNIT RESOURCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
