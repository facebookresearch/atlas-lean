# Well-formed resource-constrained scheduling with a partial order

An instance is well-formed with its partial order when the RESOURCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING instance is well-formed ($m > 0$, $r > 0$, there are $r$ positive bounds, every task row has $r$ requirements with $R_k(t) \le B_k$, and $D > 0$), every pair names two tasks, and the pairs form a strict partial order.
