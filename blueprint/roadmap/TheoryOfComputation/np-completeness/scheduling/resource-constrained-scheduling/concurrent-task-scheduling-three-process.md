# CONCURRENT TASK SCHEDULING ON THREE PROCESSORS as a decision problem

The decision problem CONCURRENT TASK SCHEDULING ON THREE PROCESSORS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a quintuple $(m, r, B, R, D)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed for CONCURRENT TASK SCHEDULING ON THREE PROCESSORS and feasible for resource-constrained scheduling with a partial order. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
