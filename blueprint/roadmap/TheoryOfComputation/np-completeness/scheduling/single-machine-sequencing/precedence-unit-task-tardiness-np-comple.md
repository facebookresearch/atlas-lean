# SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS5] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS (comment: if precedence constraints are added, the problem is NP-complete even with equal lengths and equal weights, credited to Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan 1978).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity of scheduling under precedence constraints", Operations Research 26 (1978), 22-35, doi:10.1287/opre.26.1.22 (`1|prec, pⱼ = 1|∑ Tⱼ`).
