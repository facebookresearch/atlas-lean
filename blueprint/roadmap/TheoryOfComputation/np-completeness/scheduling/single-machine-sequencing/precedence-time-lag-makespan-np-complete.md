# SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Y.-T. Leung, O. Vornberger, and J. D. Witthoff, "On some variants of the bandwidth minimization problem", SIAM Journal on Computing 13 (1984), 650-667, doi:10.1137/0213040 (the directed separation problem is NP-complete for arbitrary `k`, with reductions to scheduling problems).
- V. G. Timkovsky, "Identical parallel machines vs. unit-time shops and preemptions vs. chains in scheduling complexity", European Journal of Operational Research 149 (2003), 355-376, doi:10.1016/S0377-2217(02)00767-1.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Single machine problems with non-negative time-lags", which lists `1|prec(l); pᵢ = 1|C_max` as strongly NP-hard with these two references.
