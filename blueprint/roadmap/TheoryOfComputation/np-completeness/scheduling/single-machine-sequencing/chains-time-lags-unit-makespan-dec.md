# SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND TIME LAGS, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND TIME LAGS, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(p, L, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of triples and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND TIME LAGS, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
