# PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS as a decision problem

The decision problem PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(d, E, D, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
