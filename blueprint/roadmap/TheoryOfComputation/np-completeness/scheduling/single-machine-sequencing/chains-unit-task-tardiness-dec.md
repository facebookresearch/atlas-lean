# SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a weighted-tardiness instance $(T, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed for SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS and feasible for unit-task tardiness with precedence (a one-processor schedule respecting the pairs with weighted tardiness at most $K$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
