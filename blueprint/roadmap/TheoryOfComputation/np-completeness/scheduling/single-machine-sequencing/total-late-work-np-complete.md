# SINGLE-MACHINE TOTAL LATE WORK is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE TOTAL LATE WORK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. N. Potts and L. N. Van Wassenhove, "Single machine scheduling to minimize total late work", Operations Research 40 (1992), 586-595, doi:10.1287/opre.40.3.586.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS1] (the definition of a one-processor schedule).
