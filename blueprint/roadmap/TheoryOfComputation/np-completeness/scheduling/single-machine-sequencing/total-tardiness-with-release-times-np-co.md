# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS WITH RELEASE TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS WITH RELEASE TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS5] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS, p. 237, comment ("If release times are added instead, the problem is NP-complete in the strong sense for equal task weights (see SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES)").
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorems 4(a) and 1(j) (`n|1|rⱼ ≥ 0, wⱼ = 1|∑ wⱼCⱼ` reduces to `n|1|rⱼ ≥ 0, wⱼ = 1|∑ wⱼTⱼ`).
