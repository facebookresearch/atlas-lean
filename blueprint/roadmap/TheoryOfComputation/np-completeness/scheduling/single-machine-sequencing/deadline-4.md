# Deadline of a task given by a triple

For a single-machine sequencing instance given by a list of triples of natural numbers and a bound, the deadline, or due date, $d(t)$ of task $t$ is the third coordinate of the $t$-th triple. The same projection serves SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS WITH RELEASE TIMES, SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS WITH RELEASE TIMES and SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS.
