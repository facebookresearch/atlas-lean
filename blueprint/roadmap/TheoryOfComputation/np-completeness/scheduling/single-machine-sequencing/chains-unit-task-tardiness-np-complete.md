# SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TOTAL TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Y.-T. Leung and G. H. Young, "Minimizing total tardiness on a single machine with precedence constraints", ORSA Journal on Computing 2 (1990), 346-352, doi:10.1287/ijoc.2.4.346 (`1|chains, pⱼ = 1|∑ Tⱼ`).
- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity results for scheduling chains on a single machine", European Journal of Operational Research 4 (1980), 270-275, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(80)90111-3 (the scheduling model and the chain-like precedence relation).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (single machine problems: `1|chains; pᵢ = 1|∑ Tᵢ`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, Leung and Young (1990)).
