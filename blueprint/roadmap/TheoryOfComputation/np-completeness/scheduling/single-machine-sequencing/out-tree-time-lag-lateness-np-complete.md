# `1|outtree(l); pᵢ = 1|L_max` is NP-complete

`1|outtree(l); pᵢ = 1|L_max` is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Brucker and Knust (1999); Brucker and Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*.
