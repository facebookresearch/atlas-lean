# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS WITH RELEASE TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS WITH RELEASE TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS2] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS, p. 236, comment (with `<` empty, NP-complete "for arbitrary release times (see previous problem)", i.e. [SS1] SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, NP-complete in the strong sense).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorem 4(f) (KNAPSACK reduces to `n|1|rⱼ ≥ 0, wⱼ = 1|∑ wⱼUⱼ`).
