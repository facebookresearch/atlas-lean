# Listed precedence pairs of a single-machine instance with lengths

For a single-machine instance given by a list of job entries, a list of precedence pairs and two further natural numbers, job $j$ is joined to job $j'$ when the ordered pair $(j, j')$ occurs in the list of precedence pairs. The same relation is the precedence relation of SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN and the edge relation of PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS.
