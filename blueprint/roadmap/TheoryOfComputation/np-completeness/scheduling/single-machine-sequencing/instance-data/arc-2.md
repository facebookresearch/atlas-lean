# Listed precedence pairs of a single-machine instance with pairs

For a single-machine instance given by a list of pairs (one per job), a list of precedence pairs and a bound, job $j$ is joined to job $k$ when the ordered pair $(j, k)$ occurs in the list of precedence pairs. The same relation gives the arcs of the precedence digraph in SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, and the precedence relation of SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS.
