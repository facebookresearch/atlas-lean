# SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(p, P, l, K)$ (a list of natural numbers, a list of pairs and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH PRECEDENCE AND A COMMON TIME LAG, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
