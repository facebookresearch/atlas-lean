# Well-formed instances of OUT-TREE TIME LAGS, MAXIMUM LATENESS (1|outtree(l); pᵢ = 1|L_max)

Well-formedness of an instance of `1|outtree(l); pᵢ = 1|L_max`: every listed pair names two jobs, the listed pairs form a strict partial order, and the order is an out-tree (every job has at most one immediate predecessor).
