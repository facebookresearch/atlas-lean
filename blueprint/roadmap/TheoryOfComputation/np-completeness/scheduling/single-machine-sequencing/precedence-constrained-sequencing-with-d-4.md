# PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS is NP-complete

The decision problem PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING WITH DELAYS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
