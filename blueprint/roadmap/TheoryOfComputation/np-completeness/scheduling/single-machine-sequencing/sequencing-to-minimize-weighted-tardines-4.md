# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
