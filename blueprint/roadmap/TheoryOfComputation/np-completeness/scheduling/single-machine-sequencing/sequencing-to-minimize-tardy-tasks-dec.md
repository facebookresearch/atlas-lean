# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(T, P, K)$ (a list of pairs, a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
