# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH DEADLINES is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH DEADLINES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS4] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, p. 237, comment ("If the partial order < is replaced by individual task deadlines, the resulting problem is NP-complete in the strong sense [Lenstra, 1977]").
