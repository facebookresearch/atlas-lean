# SINGLE-MACHINE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH REJECTION is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH REJECTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- D. W. Engels, D. R. Karger, S. G. Kolliopoulos, S. Sengupta, R. N. Uma and J. Wein, "Techniques for scheduling with rejection", Journal of Algorithms 49(1) (2003), 175-191, doi:10.1016/S0196-6774(03)00078-6 (conference version: ESA 1998, LNCS 1461, 490-501, doi:10.1007/3-540-68530-8_41).
