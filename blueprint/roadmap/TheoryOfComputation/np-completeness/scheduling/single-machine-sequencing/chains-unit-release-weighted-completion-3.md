# SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND RELEASE DATES, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Complexity results for scheduling chains on a single machine", European Journal of Operational Research 4 (1980), 270-275, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(80)90111-3, Theorem 3 (`1|chain, rⱼ, pⱼ = 1|∑ wⱼCⱼ`).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (single machine problems: `1|chains; pᵢ = 1; rᵢ|∑ wᵢCᵢ`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan (1980)).
