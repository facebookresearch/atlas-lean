# Feasible instances of SINGLE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND A TIME LAG, MAKESPAN

An instance is feasible when some start times $S$ complete every job by $K$, never run two jobs at once, and start each entry of a chain at least $l$ after its immediate predecessor in the chain completes. Start times are given to all natural numbers, and an entry of a chain that is not a job index has processing time $0$ and is otherwise unconstrained.
