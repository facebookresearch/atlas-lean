# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH RELEASE TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TOTAL COMPLETION TIME WITH RELEASE TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS4] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME (comment: with individual task release times instead of the partial order, NP-complete in the strong sense even if all task weights are 1).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorem 4(a) (KNAPSACK reduces to `1|rⱼ|∑ Cⱼ`).
