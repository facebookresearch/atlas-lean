# SINGLE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND A TIME LAG, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND A TIME LAG, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Wikum, D. C. Llewellyn, and G. L. Nemhauser, "One-machine generalized precedence constrained scheduling problems", Operations Research Letters 16(2) (1994), 87-99, doi:10.1016/0167-6377(94)90064-7; recorded as strongly NP-hard in P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, class "Single machine problems with non-negative time-lags" (University of Osnabrück), minimal NP-hard problem `1|chains(l)|Cmax`.
