# SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(T, K)$ (a list of triples of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED TARDINESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
