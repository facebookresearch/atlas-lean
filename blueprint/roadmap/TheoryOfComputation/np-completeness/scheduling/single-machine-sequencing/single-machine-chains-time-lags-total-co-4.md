# SINGLE-MACHINE CHAINS WITH CONSTANT TIME LAGS AND TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE CHAINS WITH CONSTANT TIME LAGS AND TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, "Complexity results for single-machine problems with positive finish-start time-lags", Computing 63 (1999), 299-316, doi:10.1007/s006070050036.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück (online tables), class "Single machine problems with non-negative time-lags" (lists `1|chains(l)|ΣCᵢ` as minimal strongly NP-hard, citing Brucker and Knust (1999)).
