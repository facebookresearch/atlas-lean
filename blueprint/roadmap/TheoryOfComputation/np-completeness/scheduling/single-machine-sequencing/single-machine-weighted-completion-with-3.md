# SINGLE-MACHINE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH REJECTION as a decision problem

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH REJECTION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of triples and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SINGLE-MACHINE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH REJECTION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
