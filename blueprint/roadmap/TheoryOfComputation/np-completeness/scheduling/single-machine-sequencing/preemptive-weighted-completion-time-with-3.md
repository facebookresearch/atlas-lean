# PREEMPTIVE SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH RELEASE TIMES is NP-complete

The decision problem PREEMPTIVE SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME WITH RELEASE TIMES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS4] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME, p. 237, comment ("If there are individual task release times instead of deadline, ...
