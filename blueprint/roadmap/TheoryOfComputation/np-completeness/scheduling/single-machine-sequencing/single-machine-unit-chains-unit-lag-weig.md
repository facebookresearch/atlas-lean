# Feasible instances of SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND UNIT LAG, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when some start times $S$ form a one-processor schedule of the unit jobs, start each entry of a chain at least $l$ after its immediate predecessor completes (start times are given to all natural numbers, and an entry that is not a job index takes part only in these chain constraints), and give $\sum_j w_j (S(j) + 1) \le K$.
