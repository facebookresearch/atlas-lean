# SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES TO MINIMIZE WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, web edition (2000), problem MINIMUM SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES.
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorem 4(a) (`1|rⱼ|∑ Cⱼ` is NP-hard).
