# SINGLE-MACHINE TOTAL LATE WORK as a decision problem

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE TOTAL LATE WORK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(T, K)$ (a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is feasible for SINGLE-MACHINE TOTAL LATE WORK (no well-formedness condition is imposed). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
