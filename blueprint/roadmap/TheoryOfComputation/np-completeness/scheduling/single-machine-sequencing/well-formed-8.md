# Well-formed instances of SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS

An instance $(d, P, K)$ is well-formed when every deadline is positive, $0 < K \le n$, every pair in $P$ names two tasks, the listed pairs form a strict partial order, and the order consists of chains: every task has at most one immediate predecessor and at most one immediate successor.
