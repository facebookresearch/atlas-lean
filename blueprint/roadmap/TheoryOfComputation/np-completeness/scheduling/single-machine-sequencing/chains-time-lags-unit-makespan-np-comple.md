# SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND TIME LAGS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem SINGLE-MACHINE UNIT JOBS WITH CHAINS AND TIME LAGS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- W. Yu, H. Hoogeveen and J. K. Lenstra, "Minimizing makespan in a two-machine flow shop with delays and unit-time operations is NP-hard", Journal of Scheduling 7 (2004), 333-348, doi:10.1023/B:JOSH.0000036858.59787.c2, Theorem 24.
- W. Yu, *The two-machine flow shop problem with delays and the one-machine total tardiness problem*, PhD thesis, Technische Universiteit Eindhoven (1996).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/ (single machine problems with non-negative time-lags: `1|chains(lᵢⱼ); pᵢ = 1|C_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, Yu (1996), Yu et al. (2004)).
