# SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING UNIT TASKS WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS2] SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASKS (comment: remains NP-complete even if all task lengths are 1 and `<` consists only of "chains", credited to Lenstra 1977).
