# Feasible instances of TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP

An instance is feasible when some stage-1 machine $\mu_j \in \{0, 1\}$ and start times $s_1, s_2$ keep distinct jobs on the same stage-1 machine, and distinct jobs at stage 2, in disjoint intervals, start each job's stage 2 after its stage 1 finishes, and finish every job by $K$.
