# EARLINESS-TARDINESS WITH A RESTRICTIVE COMMON DUE DATE as a decision problem

The decision problem EARLINESS-TARDINESS WITH A RESTRICTIVE COMMON DUE DATE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(p, d, y)$ (a list of natural numbers and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for EARLINESS-TARDINESS WITH A RESTRICTIVE COMMON DUE DATE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
