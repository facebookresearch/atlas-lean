# COUPLED TASK SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem COUPLED TASK SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. J. Orman and C. N. Potts, "On the complexity of coupled-task scheduling", Discrete Applied Mathematics 72 (1997), 141-154, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(96)00041-8.
