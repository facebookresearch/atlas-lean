# MULTIPLE-CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPLE-CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", J. Algorithms 4 (1983), 189-203, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(83)90045-7, problem [1] MULTIPLE CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS (the problem, credited to Simons and Sipser, transformation from 3SAT).
