# MULTIPLE-CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS as a decision problem

The decision problem MULTIPLE-CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, l, I)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MULTIPLE-CHOICE SCHEDULING WITHIN INTERVALS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
