# WEIGHTED EARLINESS-TARDINESS ABOUT A COMMON DUE DATE is NP-complete

The decision problem WEIGHTED EARLINESS-TARDINESS ABOUT A COMMON DUE DATE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. G. Hall and M. E. Posner, "Earliness-tardiness scheduling problems, I: weighted deviation of completion times about a common due date", Operations Research 39 (1991), 836-846, doi:10.1287/opre.39.5.836.
