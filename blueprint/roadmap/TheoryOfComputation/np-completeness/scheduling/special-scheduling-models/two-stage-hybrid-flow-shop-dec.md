# TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of pairs and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
