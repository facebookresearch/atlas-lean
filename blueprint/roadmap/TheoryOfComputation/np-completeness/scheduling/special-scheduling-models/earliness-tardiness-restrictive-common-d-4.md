# EARLINESS-TARDINESS WITH A RESTRICTIVE COMMON DUE DATE is NP-complete

The decision problem EARLINESS-TARDINESS WITH A RESTRICTIVE COMMON DUE DATE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. G. Hall, W. Kubiak, and S. P. Sethi, "Earliness-tardiness scheduling problems, II: deviation of completion times about a restrictive common due date", Operations Research 39 (1991), 847-856, doi:10.1287/opre.39.5.847 (the recognition version is NP-complete in the ordinary sense).
- J. A. Hoogeveen and S. L. van de Velde, "Scheduling around a small common due date", European Journal of Operational Research 55 (1991), 237-242, doi:10.1016/0377-2217(91)90228-N (Theorem 3, with all weights equal to 1; independent proof).
