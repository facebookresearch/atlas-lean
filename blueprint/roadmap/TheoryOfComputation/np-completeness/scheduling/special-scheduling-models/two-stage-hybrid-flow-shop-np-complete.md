# TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-STAGE HYBRID FLOW SHOP belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. N. D. Gupta, "Two-stage, hybrid flowshop scheduling problem", Journal of the Operational Research Society 39 (1988), 359-364, doi:10.1057/jors.1988.63 (the model; NP-completeness of the two-stage problem with identical parallel machines at each stage).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SP12] PARTITION (NP-hardness of the restriction stated here, by the reduction in the module docstring).
