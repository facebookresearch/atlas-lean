# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS15] FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 241 (comment: with a bound `K` on the sum of the completion times in place of the deadline, the problem is NP-complete in the strong sense even if `m = 2`).
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. Sethi, "The complexity of flowshop and jobshop scheduling", Mathematics of Operations Research 1 (1976), 117-129, doi:10.1287/moor.1.2.117.
