# FIXED-INTERVAL SCHEDULING ON ELIGIBLE MACHINES as a decision problem

The decision problem FIXED-INTERVAL SCHEDULING ON ELIGIBLE MACHINES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, W)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for FIXED-INTERVAL SCHEDULING ON ELIGIBLE MACHINES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
