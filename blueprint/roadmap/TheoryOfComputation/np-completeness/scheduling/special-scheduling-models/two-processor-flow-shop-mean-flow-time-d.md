# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, K)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
