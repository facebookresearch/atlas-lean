# FIXED-INTERVAL SCHEDULING ON ELIGIBLE MACHINES is NP-complete

The decision problem FIXED-INTERVAL SCHEDULING ON ELIGIBLE MACHINES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. M. Arkin and E. B. Silverberg, "Scheduling jobs with fixed start and end times", Discrete Applied Mathematics 18 (1987), 1-8, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(87)90037-0.
- D. Hermelin, Y. Itzhaki, H. Molter, and D. Shabtay, "Hardness of interval scheduling on unrelated machines", arXiv:2206.12825 (2022), Sections 1-2 (problem definition, and the statement that Arkin and Silverberg proved the problem strongly NP-hard).
