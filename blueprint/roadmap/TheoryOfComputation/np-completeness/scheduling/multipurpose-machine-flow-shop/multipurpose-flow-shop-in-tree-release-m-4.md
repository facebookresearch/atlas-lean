# MPM FLOW SHOP, IN-TREE, RELEASE DATES (FMPM|intree; r_i; p_ij = 1|C_max) is NP-complete

The decision problem MPM FLOW SHOP, IN-TREE, RELEASE DATES (FMPM|intree; r_i; p_ij = 1|C_max) is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Brucker (2007), Chapter 10 and Table 5.3; Brucker, Garey and Johnson (1977); Brucker and
Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*.
