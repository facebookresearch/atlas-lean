# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, K)$ (a list of pairs of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is feasible for TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME (the well-formedness condition is always true). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
