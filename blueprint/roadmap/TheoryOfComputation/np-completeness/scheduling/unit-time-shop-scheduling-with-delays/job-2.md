# Jobs of a unit-time two-machine shop with delays

An instance of a unit-time two-machine shop with delays, as in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN, is a pair $(t, K)$ of a list $t = (t_0, \dots, t_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, one delay per job, and a bound $K$. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of the list $t$, where $n$ is its length.
