# TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- W. Yu, *The two-machine flow shop problem with delays and the one-machine total tardiness problem*, PhD thesis, Technische Universiteit Eindhoven (1996), Section 3.8, Theorem 2.
- W. Yu, H. Hoogeveen and J. K. Lenstra, "Minimizing makespan in a two-machine flow shop with delays and unit-time operations is NP-hard", Journal of Scheduling 7 (2004), 333-348, doi:10.1023/B:JOSH.0000036858.59787.c2.
