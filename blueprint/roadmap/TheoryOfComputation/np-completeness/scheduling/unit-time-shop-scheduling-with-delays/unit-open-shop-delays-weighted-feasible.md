# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH DELAYS, WEIGHTED COMPLETION TIME

An instance $(J, K)$ is feasible when there is a two-machine unit-time open-shop schedule with delays: each machine and each job start their operations at pairwise different times, and the two operations of job $j$, in either order, are separated by at least the delay $t_j$ (the later starts at least the delay $t_j$ after the earlier completes) with $\sum_j w_j (\max(\sigma_0(j), \sigma_1(j)) + 1) \le K$.
