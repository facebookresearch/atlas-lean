# Two-machine unit-time flow-shop schedules with delays

For jobs with delays $t_j$, start times $\sigma_1(j), \sigma_2(j)$ form a schedule when each machine runs a one-processor schedule of its unit-time operations (pairwise different start times) and $\sigma_1(j) + 1 + t_j \le \sigma_2(j)$ for every job $j$.
