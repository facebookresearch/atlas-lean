# Delay of a job in a unit-time two-machine shop with delays

An instance of a unit-time two-machine shop with delays, as in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN, is a pair $(t, K)$ of a list $t = (t_0, \dots, t_{n-1})$ of natural numbers, one delay per job, and a bound $K$. The delay of job $j$, the least time between the completion of its operation on the first machine and the start of its operation on the second, is the entry $t_j$ of the list at position $j$.
