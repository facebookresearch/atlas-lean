# Two-machine unit-time open-shop schedules with delays

For jobs with delays $t_j$, start times $\sigma_0(j), \sigma_1(j)$ form a schedule when they form a unit-time open-shop schedule on two machines without precedence (each machine and each job start their operations at pairwise different times) and, for every job $j$, $\sigma_0(j) + 1 + t_j \le \sigma_1(j)$ or $\sigma_1(j) + 1 + t_j \le \sigma_0(j)$.
