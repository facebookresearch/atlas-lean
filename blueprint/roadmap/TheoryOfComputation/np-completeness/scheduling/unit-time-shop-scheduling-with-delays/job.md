# Jobs of a unit-time shop with delays and release dates

An instance of a unit-time shop with delays and release dates, as in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME, is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of pairs $(r_j, t_j)$ of natural numbers, the release date and the delay of job $j$, and a bound $K$. Its jobs are the positions $0, \dots, n-1$ of $L$, where $n$ is the length of $L$, so equal pairs at different positions are different jobs.
