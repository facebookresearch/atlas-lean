# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN

An instance is feasible when there is a two-machine unit-time schedule with delays: start times $\sigma_1(j), \sigma_2(j)$ on machines $M_1, M_2$ such that each machine runs a one-processor schedule of its unit operations and job $j$'s second operation starts at least $t_j$ after its first completes ($\sigma_1(j) + 1 + t_j \le \sigma_2(j)$) in which every second operation completes by $K$.
