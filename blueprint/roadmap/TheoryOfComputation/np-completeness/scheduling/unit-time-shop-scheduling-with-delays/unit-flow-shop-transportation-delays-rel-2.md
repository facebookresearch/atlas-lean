# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME

An instance is feasible when there is a two-machine unit-time schedule with delays: start times $\sigma_1(j), \sigma_2(j)$ on machines $M_1, M_2$ such that each machine runs a one-processor schedule of its unit operations and job $j$'s second operation starts at least $t_j$ after its first completes ($\sigma_1(j) + 1 + t_j \le \sigma_2(j)$), starting each job at or after its release date on $M_1$, with $\sum_j (\sigma_2(j) + 1) \le K$.
