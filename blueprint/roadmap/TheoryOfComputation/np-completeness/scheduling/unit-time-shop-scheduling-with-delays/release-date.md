# Release date of a job in a unit-time shop with delays and release dates

An instance of a unit-time shop with delays and release dates, as in TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME FLOW SHOP WITH DELAYS AND RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME, is a pair $(L, K)$ of a list $L$ of pairs $(r_j, t_j)$ of natural numbers, the release date and the delay of job $j$, and a bound $K$. The release date $r_j$ of job $j$ is the first component of the pair at position $j$ of $L$.
