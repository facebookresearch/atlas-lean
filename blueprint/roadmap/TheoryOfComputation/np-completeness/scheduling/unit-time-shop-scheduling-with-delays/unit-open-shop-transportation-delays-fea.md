# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE UNIT-TIME OPEN SHOP WITH TRANSPORTATION DELAYS, MAKESPAN

An instance $(t, K)$ is feasible when there is a two-machine unit-time open-shop schedule with delays: each machine and each job start their operations at pairwise different times, and the two operations of job $j$, in either order, are separated by at least $t_j$ (the later starts at least $t_j$ after the earlier completes) in which every operation completes by $K$.
