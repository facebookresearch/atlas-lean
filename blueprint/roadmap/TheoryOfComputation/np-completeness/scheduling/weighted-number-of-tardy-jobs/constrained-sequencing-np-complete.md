# CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASK WEIGHT is NP-complete

The decision problem CONSTRAINED SEQUENCING TO MINIMIZE TARDY TASK WEIGHT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
