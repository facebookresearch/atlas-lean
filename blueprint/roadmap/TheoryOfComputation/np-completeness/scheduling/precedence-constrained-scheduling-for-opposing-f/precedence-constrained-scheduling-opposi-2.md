# PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING is NP-complete for opposing forests

PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINED SCHEDULING is NP-complete for opposing forests: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [SS9]; Garey, Johnson, Tarjan and Yannakakis (1983).
