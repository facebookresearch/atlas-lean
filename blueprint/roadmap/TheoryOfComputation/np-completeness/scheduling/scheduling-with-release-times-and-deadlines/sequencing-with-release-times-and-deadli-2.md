# SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH is NP-complete

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS1] SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, p. 236, comment: the problem "remains NP-complete (in the ordinary sense) even when each [of `r(t)` and `d(t)`] can take on only two values" (no citation is given), and Theorem 3.8, pp. 70-71, whose transformation from PARTITION to SEQUENCING WITHIN INTERVALS uses only the release times `0` and `⌈B/2⌉` and the deadlines `⌈(B+1)/2⌉` and `B + 1`.
