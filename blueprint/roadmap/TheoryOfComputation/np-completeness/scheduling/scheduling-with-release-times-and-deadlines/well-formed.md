# Well-formed instances of SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH

An instance of SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES (tasks $(l(t), r(t), d(t))$) is well-formed for SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH when every length and deadline is positive, the release times take at most two distinct values, and the deadlines take at most two distinct values.
