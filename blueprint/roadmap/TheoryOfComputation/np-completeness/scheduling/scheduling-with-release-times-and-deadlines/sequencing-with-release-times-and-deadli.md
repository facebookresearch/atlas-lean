# SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH as a decision problem

The decision problem SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of triples $(l(t), r(t), d(t))$, to an instance that is well-formed for SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES, TWO VALUES EACH and feasible for SEQUENCING WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
