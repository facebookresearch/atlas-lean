# TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C.-Y. Lee and X. Cai, "Scheduling one and two-processor tasks on two parallel processors", IIE Transactions 31 (1999), 445-455, doi:10.1080/07408179908969847 (Appendix).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, class "Multiprocessor tasks on parallel processors" (`P2|size_i|L_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, citing Lee and Cai 1999).
