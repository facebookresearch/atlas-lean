# Well-formed instances of TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINTS

An instance is well-formed when the FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING instance is well-formed ($m > 0$, every job has $m$ tasks, $D > 0$), $m = 2$, every arc names two jobs, no two distinct arcs share a tail or a head, and the arcs have no directed cycle.
