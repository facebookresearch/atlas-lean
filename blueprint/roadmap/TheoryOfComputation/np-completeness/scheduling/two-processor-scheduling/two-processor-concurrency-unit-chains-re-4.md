# TWO-PROCESSOR UET CONCURRENT TASK SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND RELEASE DATES is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR UET CONCURRENT TASK SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS AND RELEASE DATES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, S. Knust, D. Roper and Y. Zinder, "Scheduling UET task systems with concurrency on two parallel identical processors", Mathematical Methods of Operations Research 52 (2000), 369-387, doi:10.1007/s001860000089 (the full text was not available to us).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Table 11.5 (`MPT2|chains; rᵢ; pᵢ = 1; sizeᵢ|C_max`, NP-hard in the strong sense, Brucker et al.).
