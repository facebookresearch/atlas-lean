# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of triples and a pair of natural numbers, to an instance that is feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS (the well-formedness condition is always true). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
