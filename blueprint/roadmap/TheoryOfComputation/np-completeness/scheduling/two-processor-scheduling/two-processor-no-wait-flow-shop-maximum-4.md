# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Röck, "Some new results in flow shop scheduling", Zeitschrift für Operations Research 28 (1984), 1-16, doi:10.1007/BF01919082 (`F2|no-wait|Lmax` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Flow-shop problems with no-wait" (`F2|no-wait|Lmax`, minimal NP-hard, credited to Röck 1984).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 242 (the deadline version, solvable in polynomial time for `m = 2`, Gilmore and Gomory 1964).
