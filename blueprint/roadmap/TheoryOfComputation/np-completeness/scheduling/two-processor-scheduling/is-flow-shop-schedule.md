# Two-processor flow-shop schedules meeting a deadline

Start times $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$ form a two-processor flow-shop schedule meeting the deadline when each $\sigma_p$ is a one-processor schedule (one-to-one, and a task starting after task $k$ starts no earlier than $k$ finishes), the two tasks of each job occupy disjoint intervals, every task finishes by $D$, and each job starts on processor 2 no earlier than it finishes on processor 1.
