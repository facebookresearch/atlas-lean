# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS17] TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER (with the definitions of [SS1], [SS14] and [SS15]).
- C. H. Papadimitriou and P. C. Kanellakis, "Flowshop scheduling with limited temporary storage", Journal of the ACM 27 (1980), 533-549, doi:10.1145/322203.322213.
