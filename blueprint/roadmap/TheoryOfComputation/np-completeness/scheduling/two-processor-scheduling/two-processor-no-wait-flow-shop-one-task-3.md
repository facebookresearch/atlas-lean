# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(J, D)$ (a list of triples of a Boolean and two natural numbers, and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
