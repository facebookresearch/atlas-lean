# TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. A. Hoogeveen, S. L. van de Velde and B. Veltman, "Complexity of scheduling multiprocessor tasks with prespecified processor allocations", Discrete Applied Mathematics 55 (1994), 259-272, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90012-4, Theorem 2.8 (`P2|fixⱼ, rⱼ|C_max`).
- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Table 11.2 (`MPT2||L_max`, NP-hard in the strong sense, Hoogeveen et al.).
