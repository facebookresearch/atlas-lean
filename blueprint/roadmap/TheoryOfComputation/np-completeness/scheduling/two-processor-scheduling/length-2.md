# Length of the second task of a job in TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS is a pair $(L, D)$ of a list $L$ of triples $(b, l_1, l_2)$, one per job, with $b$ a boolean and $l_1, l_2$ natural numbers, and a deadline $D$; the jobs are the positions of $L$. The length $l(t_2[j])$ of the task of job $j$ on the second processor is the entry $l_2$ of the triple at position $j$.
