# TWO PARALLEL MACHINES WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO PARALLEL MACHINES WITH RELEASE DATES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `pqr_nop` (parallel machine problems without preemption), entry `P2|r_i|ΣC_i` (minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, justified by the single-machine problem).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X (`1|r_j|ΣC_j` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
