# TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of triples and a pair of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
