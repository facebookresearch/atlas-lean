# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X, Theorem 1(k) and Theorem 4(c) in the preprint, Mathematisch Centrum report BW 43/75 (1975), https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/9725 (KNAPSACK reduces to `n|1|r_n ≥ 0|Lmax`, which reduces to `n|2|F|Lmax`: `F2||Lmax`).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Flow-shop problems without preemption" (`F2||Lmax`, minimal NP-hard, credited to Lenstra, Rinnooy Kan, and Brucker 1977).
