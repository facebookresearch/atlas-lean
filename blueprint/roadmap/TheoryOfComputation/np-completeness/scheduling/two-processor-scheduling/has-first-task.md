# Jobs with a task on the first processor in TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW SHOP WITH ONE-TASK JOBS is a pair $(L, D)$ of a list $L$ of triples $(b, l_1, l_2)$, one per job, with $b$ a boolean and $l_1, l_2$ natural numbers, and a deadline $D$; the jobs are the positions of $L$. Job $j$ has a task on the first processor when the boolean $b$ of the triple at position $j$ is true; otherwise the job consists of the single task $t_2[j]$ on the second processor.
