# Instances of TWO-PROCESSOR MULTIPROCESSOR-TASK SCHEDULING TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance is a pair of a list of tasks, each a triple $(p_i, \mathrm{fix}_i, d_i)$ of processing time, list of required processors and due date, and a bound $y$ given as a pair $(y^+, y^-)$ with $y = y^+ - y^-$.
