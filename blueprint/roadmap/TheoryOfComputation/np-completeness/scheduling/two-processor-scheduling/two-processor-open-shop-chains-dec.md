# TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(m, J, K)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
