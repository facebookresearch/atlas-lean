# TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. L. Lawler, J. K. Lenstra, and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "Minimizing maximum lateness in a two-machine open shop", Mathematics of Operations Research 6 (1981), 153-158, doi:10.1287/moor.6.1.153; Erratum, Mathematics of Operations Research 7 (1982), 635, doi:10.1287/moor.7.4.635 (the nonpreemptive problem is NP-hard).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, Universität Osnabrück, table "Open-shop problems without preemption" (`O2||L_max`, minimal NP-hard in the strong sense, credited to Lawler, Lenstra, and Rinnooy Kan 1981, 1982).
