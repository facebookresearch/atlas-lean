# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(J, D, B)$ (a list of pairs of natural numbers and two natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP WITH BOUNDED BUFFER. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
