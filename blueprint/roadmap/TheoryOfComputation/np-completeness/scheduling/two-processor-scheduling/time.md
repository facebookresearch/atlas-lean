# Processing time on the first machine in TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance of TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP MAXIMUM LATENESS is a pair $(L, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $L$ of triples $(p_{j1}, p_{j2}, d_j)$ of natural numbers, the processing times of job $j$ on machines $M_1$ and $M_2$ and its due date, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. The processing time $p_{j1}$ of job $j$ on machine $M_1$ is the first component of the triple at position $j$ of $L$.
