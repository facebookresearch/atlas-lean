# TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINTS as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a triple $(m, J, D)$ and a list of pairs, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-PROCESSOR FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAIN PRECEDENCE CONSTRAINTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
