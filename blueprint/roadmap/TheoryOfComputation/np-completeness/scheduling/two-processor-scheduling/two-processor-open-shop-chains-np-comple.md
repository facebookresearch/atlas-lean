# TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH CHAINS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. S. Tanaev, Y. N. Sotskov and V. A. Strusevich, *Scheduling Theory. Multi-Stage Systems*, Mathematics and its Applications 285, Kluwer Academic Publishers, Dordrecht (1994), doi:10.1007/978-94-011-1192-8 (not available here); recorded as strongly NP-hard (a minimal NP-hard problem) in P.
