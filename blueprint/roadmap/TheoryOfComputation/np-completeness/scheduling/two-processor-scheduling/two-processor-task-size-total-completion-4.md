# TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR SCHEDULING WITH TASK SIZES, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C.-Y. Lee and X. Cai, "Scheduling one and two-processor tasks on two parallel processors", IIE Transactions 31 (1999), 445-455, doi:10.1080/07408179908969847 (only the abstract was available).
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class `pmpt_nop` (multiprocessor tasks on parallel processors, without preemption), entry `P2|size_i|ΣC_i` (minimal NP-hard, citing Lee and Cai 1999).
- K. Jansen and K. Kahler, "On the complexity of scheduling problems with a fixed number of parallel identical machines", Lecture Notes in Computer Science, Springer, 192-206, doi:10.1007/978-3-031-23101-8_13 (records that Lee and Cai proved `P2|size|ΣC_j` NP-hard and that its strong NP-hardness is open).
