# MPM OPEN SHOP, OUT-TREE, MAXIMUM LATENESS (OMPM|outtree; p_ij = 1|L_max) is NP-complete

The decision problem MPM OPEN SHOP, OUT-TREE, MAXIMUM LATENESS (OMPM|outtree; p_ij = 1|L_max) is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Brucker, Garey and Johnson (1977), via the case of one stage `{M_1, …, M_m}`; Timkovsky
(2003), as recorded in the Brucker-Knust classification; Brucker (2007), Chapter 10 (model).
