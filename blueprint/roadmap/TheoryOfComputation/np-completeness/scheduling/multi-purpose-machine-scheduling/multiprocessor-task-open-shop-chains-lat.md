# Feasible instances of TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP WITH CHAINS, LATE JOBS

An instance is feasible when there is a unit-time multiprocessor task open-shop schedule respecting the arcs (no overlap within a job, no overlap of operations sharing a machine, and every operation of $k$ starting after job $i$ completes whenever $(i, k)$ is an arc) with at most $K$ late jobs ($d_j < C_j$, with $C_j = 0$ when there are no stages). Every operation takes time $1$, whatever processing times the jobs list, and the number $m$ of machines plays no role.
