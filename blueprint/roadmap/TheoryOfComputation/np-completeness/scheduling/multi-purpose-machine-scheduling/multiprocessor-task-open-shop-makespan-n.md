# MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 11 (multiprocessor tasks: the MPT open-shop model).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS14] OPEN-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 241 (comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense for m arbitrary [Lenstra, 1977]", a private communication; the original was not available).
- D. P. Williamson, L. A. Hall, J. A. Hoogeveen, C. A. J. Hurkens, J. K. Lenstra, S. V. Sevast'janov and D. B. Shmoys, "Short shop schedules", Operations Research 45 (1997), 288-294, doi:10.1287/opre.45.2.288.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", which lists `OMPT||C_max` as minimal NP-hard (strongly), crediting an unpublished result of J. K. Lenstra.
