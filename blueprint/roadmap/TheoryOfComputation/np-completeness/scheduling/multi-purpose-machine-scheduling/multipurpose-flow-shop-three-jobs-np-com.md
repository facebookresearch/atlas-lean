# MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE FLOW SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (the MPM flow-shop model; Section 10.2: "Proceeding as for problem JMPM2|n = 3|C_max, FMPM|n = 3|C_max and OMPM|n = 3|C_max can be shown to be NP-hard").
- P. Brucker, B. Jurisch and A. Krämer, "Complexity of scheduling problems with multi-purpose machines", Annals of Operations Research 70 (1997), 57-73, doi:10.1023/A:1018950911030.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `FMPM|n = 3|C_max` as NP-hard with the attribution to Brucker, Jurisch and Krämer (1997).
