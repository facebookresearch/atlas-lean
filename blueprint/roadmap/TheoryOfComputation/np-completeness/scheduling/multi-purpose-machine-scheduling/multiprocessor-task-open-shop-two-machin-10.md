# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 11 (multiprocessor tasks: the MPT open-shop model).
- J. O. Achugbue and F. Y. Chin, "Scheduling the open shop to minimize mean flow time", SIAM Journal on Computing 11(4) (1982), 709-720, doi:10.1137/0211058.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multiprocessor tasks", which lists `OMPT2||ΣC_i` as minimal NP-hard in the strong sense with the attribution to Achugbue and Chin (1982).
