# TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAXIMUM LATENESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
