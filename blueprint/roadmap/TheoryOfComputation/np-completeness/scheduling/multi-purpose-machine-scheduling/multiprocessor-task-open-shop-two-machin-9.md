# TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, \mu, P, K)$ (a natural number, two lists of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TWO-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, TOTAL COMPLETION TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
