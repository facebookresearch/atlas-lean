# MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, MAKESPAN is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP WITH THREE JOBS, MAKESPAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Brucker, *Scheduling Algorithms*, 5th ed., Springer (2007), Chapter 10 (the MPM open-shop model; Section 10.2: "Proceeding as for problem JMPM2|n = 3|C_max, FMPM|n = 3|C_max and OMPM|n = 3|C_max can be shown to be NP-hard").
- T. Gonzalez and S. Sahni, "Open shop scheduling to minimize finish time", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 665-679, doi:10.1145/321978.321985.
- P. Brucker and S. Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling problems*, http://www2.informatik.uni-osnabrueck.de/knust/class/, class "Shop problems with multipurpose machines", which lists `OMPM|n = 3|C_max` as minimal NP-hard with the attribution to Gonzalez and Sahni (1976).
