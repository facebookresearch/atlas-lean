# Processing time of an operation in a two-machine stage shop with due dates

An instance of a two-machine stage shop with due dates, as in TWO-MACHINE MULTI-PURPOSE MACHINE OPEN SHOP, MAXIMUM LATENESS, is a tuple $(M, J, d, (y^+, y^-))$ of a list $M = [\mu_1, \dots, \mu_r]$ of stage machine sets, each a list of natural numbers (in a well-formed instance, machine indices $0$ and $1$), a list $J$ of jobs, each a list of natural-number processing times, a list $d$ of due dates, and a pair of natural numbers standing for the bound $y = y^+ - y^-$ on the maximum lateness. The processing time $p_{is}$ of the operation of job $i$ in stage $s$ is the entry at position $s$ of the list at position $i$ of $J$, and $0$ when that list has no entry at position $s$. That every job lists exactly one processing time per stage is a condition of a well-formed instance, not of this definition.
