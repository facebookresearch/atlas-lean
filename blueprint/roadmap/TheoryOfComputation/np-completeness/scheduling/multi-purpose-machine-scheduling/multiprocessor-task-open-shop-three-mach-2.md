# THREE-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN as a decision problem

The decision problem THREE-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, \mu, P, K)$ (a natural number, two lists of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for THREE-MACHINE MULTIPROCESSOR TASK OPEN SHOP, MAKESPAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
