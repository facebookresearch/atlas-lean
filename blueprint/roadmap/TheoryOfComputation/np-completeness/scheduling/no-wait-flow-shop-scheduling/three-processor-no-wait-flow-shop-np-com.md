# THREE-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem THREE-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 242 (comment: "Open for fixed `m = 3`").
- H. Röck, "The three-machine no-wait flow shop is NP-complete", Journal of the ACM 31 (1984), 336-345, doi:10.1145/62.65.
