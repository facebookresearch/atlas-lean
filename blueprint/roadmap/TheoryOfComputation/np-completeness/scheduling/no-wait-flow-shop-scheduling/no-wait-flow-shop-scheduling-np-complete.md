# NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING (with the definitions of [SS1], [SS14] and [SS15]).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X.
- C. H. Papadimitriou and P. C. Kanellakis, "Flowshop scheduling with limited temporary storage", Journal of the ACM 27 (1980), 533-549, doi:10.1145/322203.322213 (already for `m = 4` processors).
