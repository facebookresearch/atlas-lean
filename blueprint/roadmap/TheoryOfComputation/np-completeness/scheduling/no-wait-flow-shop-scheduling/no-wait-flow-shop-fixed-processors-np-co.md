# NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH m PROCESSORS is NP-complete

For every natural number $m_0 \ge 4$, the decision problem NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING WITH $m$ PROCESSORS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 242 (comment: "NP-complete in the strong sense for any fixed `m ≥ 4`", credited to Papadimitriou and Kanellakis).
- C. H. Papadimitriou and P. C. Kanellakis, "Flowshop scheduling with limited temporary storage", Journal of the ACM 27 (1980), 533-549, doi:10.1145/322203.322213.
