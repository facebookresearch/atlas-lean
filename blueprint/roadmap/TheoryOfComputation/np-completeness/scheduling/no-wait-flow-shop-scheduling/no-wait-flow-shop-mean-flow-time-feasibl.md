# Feasible instances of NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME

An instance $(m, J, K)$ is feasible when some flow-shop schedule, with every task finishing by $K$ and no waiting between consecutive tasks of a job, has the completion times on the last processor summing to at most $K$. A task length $l(t_p[j])$ missing from job $j$'s list counts as $0$. With $m = 0$ there is no last processor, and the sum imposes no condition.
