# NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 242 (comment: with a bound `K` on the sum of the completion times in place of the deadline, the problem is NP-complete in the strong sense for `m` arbitrary).
- J. K. Lenstra, A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, and P. Brucker, "Complexity of machine scheduling problems", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 343-362, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70743-X.
