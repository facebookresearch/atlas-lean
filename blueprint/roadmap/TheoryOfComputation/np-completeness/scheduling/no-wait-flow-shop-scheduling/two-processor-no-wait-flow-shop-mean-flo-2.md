# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME is NP-complete

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SS16] NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP SCHEDULING, p. 242 (comment: with a bound `K` on the sum of the completion times in place of the deadline, the problem is NP-complete in the strong sense for `m` arbitrary, and open for fixed `m ≥ 2`).
- H. Röck, "Some new results in flow shop scheduling", Zeitschrift für Operations Research 28 (1984), 1-16, doi:10.1007/BF01919082 (`F2|no-wait|∑ Cⱼ` is NP-hard in the strong sense).
