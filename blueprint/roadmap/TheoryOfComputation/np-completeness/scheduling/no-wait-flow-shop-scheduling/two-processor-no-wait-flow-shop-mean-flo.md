# TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME as a decision problem

The decision problem TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, J, D)$ (a natural number, a list of lists of natural numbers and a natural number), to an instance that is well-formed for TWO-PROCESSOR NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME and feasible for NO-WAIT FLOW-SHOP MEAN FLOW TIME (a no-wait flow-shop schedule with every task finishing by $K$ and last-processor completion times summing to at most $K$). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
