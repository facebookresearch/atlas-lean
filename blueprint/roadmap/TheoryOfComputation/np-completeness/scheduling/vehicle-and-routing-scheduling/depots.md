# Depots of a multiple-depot vehicle scheduling instance

The depots of a MULTIPLE-DEPOT VEHICLE SCHEDULING instance: the listed triples
$(r_d, [o_{d0}, \dots, o_{d(n-1)}], [e_{0d}, \dots, e_{(n-1)d}])$ of a depot's vehicle count,
its pull-out costs to the trips and its pull-in costs from the trips.
