# TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE is NP-complete

The decision problem TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. N. Tsitsiklis, "Special cases of traveling salesman and repairman problems with time windows", Networks 22(3) (1992), 263-282, doi:10.1002/net.3230220305, Theorem 2.
