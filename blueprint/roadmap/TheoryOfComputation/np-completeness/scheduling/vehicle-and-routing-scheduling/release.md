# Release time `r_a` of job `a` in TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE

For an instance `i` of TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE, the release time `r_a` of job `a`. It is the second coordinate of the job's triple, and for a job index beyond the end of the list of triples the release time is the number $0$.
