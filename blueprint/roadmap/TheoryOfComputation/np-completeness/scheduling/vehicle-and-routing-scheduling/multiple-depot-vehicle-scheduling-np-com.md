# MULTIPLE-DEPOT VEHICLE SCHEDULING is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPLE-DEPOT VEHICLE SCHEDULING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. A. Bertossi, P. Carraresi and G. Gallo, "On some matching problems arising in vehicle scheduling models", Networks 17 (1987), 271-281, doi:10.1002/net.3230170303.
- E. F. Olariu and C. Frăsinaru, "Multiple-depot vehicle scheduling problem heuristics", arXiv:2004.14951 (2020), Section 1.
