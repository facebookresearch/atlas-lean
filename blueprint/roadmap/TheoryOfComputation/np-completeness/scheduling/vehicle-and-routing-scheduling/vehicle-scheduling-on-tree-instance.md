# Instances of VEHICLE SCHEDULING ON TREE

An instance of VEHICLE SCHEDULING ON TREE is a triple $(P, R, K)$. The parent list $P$ is a list of $m$ triples $(p_k, f_k, b_k)$ of natural numbers, $k = 0, \dots, m-1$: vertex $k + 1$ is a child of vertex $p_k$, the edge between them takes time $f_k$ to traverse from $p_k$ to $k + 1$ (away from the root) and time $b_k$ in the other direction. The vertices are $0, \dots, m$, and vertex $0$ is the root. These readings describe the intended tree; the triple itself is unrestricted, and the parent condition $p_k \le k$ is part of [well-formedness](well-formed-3.md). The task list $R$ is a list of pairs $(r(v), h(v))$ of natural numbers, entry $v$ giving the release time and handling time of vertex $v$, and $K$ is a natural number (the time bound).
