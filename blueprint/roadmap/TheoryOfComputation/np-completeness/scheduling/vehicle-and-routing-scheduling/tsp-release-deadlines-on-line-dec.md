# TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE as a decision problem

The decision problem TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a natural number and a list of triples, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TSP WITH RELEASE TIMES AND DEADLINES ON A LINE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
