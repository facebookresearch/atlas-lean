# MPM PARALLEL MACHINES `PMPM|intree; p_i = 1; r_i|C_max` is NP-complete

The decision problem MPM PARALLEL MACHINES `PMPM|intree; p_i = 1; r_i|C_max` is NP-complete: it
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Brucker, Garey and Johnson (1977); Brucker and Knust, *Complexity results for scheduling
problems*.
