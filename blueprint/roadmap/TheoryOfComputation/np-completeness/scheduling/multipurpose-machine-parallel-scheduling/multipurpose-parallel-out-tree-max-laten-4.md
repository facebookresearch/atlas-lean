# MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH OUT-TREE PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete

The decision problem MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH OUT-TREE PRECEDENCE AND UNIT
TIMES TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Brucker, Garey and Johnson (1977), via the case $\mu_i = \{M_1, \dots, M_m\}$; Brucker (2007), Table 5.3.
