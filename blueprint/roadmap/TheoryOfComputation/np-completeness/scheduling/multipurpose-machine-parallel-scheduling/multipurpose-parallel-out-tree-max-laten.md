# Instances of MULTIPURPOSE-MACHINE PARALLEL SCHEDULING WITH OUT-TREE PRECEDENCE AND UNIT TIMES TO MINIMIZE MAXIMUM LATENESS

An instance: an MPM job-shop instance (`m`, the jobs as lists of operations `(pᵢ, μᵢ)`, an
unused bound slot), the precedence arcs `(i, k)`, the due dates (the `i`-th for job `i`), and
the bound `y` as a pair `(y⁺, y⁻)` with `y = y⁺ - y⁻`.
