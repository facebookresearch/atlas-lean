# Instances of TWO-LAYER MULTIPLE MODULE ROUTING

An instance of TWO-LAYER MULTIPLE MODULE ROUTING is a value of type `List ((ℕ × ℕ) × (ℕ × ℕ) × (ℕ × ℕ) × (ℕ × ℕ)) × List (((ℕ × ℕ) × (ℕ × ℕ)) × ℕ) × ℕ × ℕ`: the modules, each given by the coordinates `x₁, y₁, x₂, y₂` of its lower-left and upper-right corners; the terminals, each given by its coordinates and its net label; the number `m` of nets; and the area bound `B`.
