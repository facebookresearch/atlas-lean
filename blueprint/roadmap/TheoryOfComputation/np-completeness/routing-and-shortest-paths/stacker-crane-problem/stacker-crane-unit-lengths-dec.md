# The STACKER-CRANE with unit lengths decision problem

The STACKER-CRANE with unit lengths decision problem accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is [well formed with unit lengths](well-formed.md) (that is, well formed as an instance of STACKER-CRANE and with every directed and undirected edge having length $1$) and [feasible](stacker-crane-feasible.md) as an instance of STACKER-CRANE. Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected.
