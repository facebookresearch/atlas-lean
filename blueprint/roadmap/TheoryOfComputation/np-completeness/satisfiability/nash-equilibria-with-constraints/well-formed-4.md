# Well-formed instances of NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT

Well-formedness of an instance of NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT: both players have at least one pure strategy, `R` and `C` are `m₁ × m₂` matrices of rationals, and `s` is a pure strategy of player 1 (`s < m₁`).
