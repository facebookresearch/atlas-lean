# NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH SOCIAL WELFARE AT LEAST k is NP-complete

NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH SOCIAL WELFARE AT LEAST k is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Conitzer and Sandholm (2008); Bilò and Mavronicolas (2021).
