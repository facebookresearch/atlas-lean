# Utility in NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT

In NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT: the payoff of player `p` at the pure profile `a`: the entry of `R` (for player 1) or `C` (for player 2) in row `a 0` and column `a 1`. Each payoff is stored as `((a, b), q)` with value `(a - b) / q`; a missing row or a missing entry of a row (an index beyond the end of its list) reads as `((0, 0), 1)`, that is, the payoff $0$.
