# NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT is NP-complete

NASH EQUILIBRIUM WITH A GIVEN STRATEGY IN THE SUPPORT is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Conitzer and Sandholm (2008); Gilboa and Zemel (1989); Bilò and Mavronicolas (2021).
