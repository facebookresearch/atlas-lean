# Feasible INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `sub1` instances

For INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `sub1`: there are two expression trees $f$ and $g$ whose [prefix token lists](tokens.md) are exactly the two stored lists, and one assignment $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ under which their [evaluations](eval.md) differ. An empty list parses to no tree, so it fails; and at $|X| = 0$ trees for the variables $0$ and $1$ can still be separated. The bounds on variable indices belong to the separate [validation](well-formed.md).
