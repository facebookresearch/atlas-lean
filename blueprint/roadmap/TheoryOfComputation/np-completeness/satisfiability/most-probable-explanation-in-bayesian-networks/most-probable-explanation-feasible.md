# Feasible MOST PROBABLE EXPLANATION in Bayesian networks instances

D-MPE: the domain size $d$ is positive, and some Bayesian network $b$ that [agrees with the instance](encodes.md) has an instantiation $x$ of all the variables with $\Pr(x, e) > p$, where $\Pr(x, e)$ is $\Pr(x)$ if $x$ agrees with the evidence and $0$ otherwise. For well-formed input, $b$ is the network the instance describes.
