# QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF[2] is NP-complete

The decision problem QUADRATIC EQUATIONS OVER GF[2] belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN9] ALGEBRAIC EQUATIONS OVER GF[2], p. 251, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if none of the polynomials has a term involving more than two variables", credited there to L. G. Valiant (1977, private communication; not available).
