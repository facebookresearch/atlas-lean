# Instances of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `p`

An instance of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `p` consists of the declared natural number $|X|$ of variables and two arbitrary lists of natural triples (the format of [INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS](../inequivalence-of-simple-functions/README.md) with triples), read as the prefix token lists of $f$ and $g$. A list need not parse to any expression: the empty list `[]` is an instance component. Parsing and the variable bounds are checked later.
