# Feasible INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `p` instances

An instance of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `p` is feasible when some assignment of nonnegative integers to the variables gives $f$ and $g$ different values, exactly as for [INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS](../inequivalence-of-simple-functions/README.md).
