# PLANAR 3SAT is NP-complete

PLANAR 3SAT is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [LO1], comment; Lichtenstein (1982).
