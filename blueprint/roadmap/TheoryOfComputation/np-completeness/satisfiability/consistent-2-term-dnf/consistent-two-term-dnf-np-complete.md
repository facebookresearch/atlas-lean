# CONSISTENT 2-TERM DNF is NP-complete

The CONSISTENT 2-TERM DNF decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. Pitt and L. G. Valiant, "Computational limitations on learning from examples", Journal of the ACM 35(4) (1988), 965-984, doi:10.1145/48014.63140 (disjunctions of two monomials, i.e. 2-term DNF, cannot be learned by 2-term DNF hypotheses unless RP = NP; the reduction shows that deciding whether some 2-term DNF is consistent with given examples is NP-complete).
- S. Ghoshal and R. Saket, "Hardness of learning DNFs using halfspaces", arXiv:1911.06358 (Section 1: "it is NP-hard to properly learn a t-term DNF [PV88], for any t ≥ 2").
