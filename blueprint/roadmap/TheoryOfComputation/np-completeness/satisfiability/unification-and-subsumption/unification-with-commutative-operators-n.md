# UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN16] UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS (credited there to R. Sethi, private communication, 1977).
- F. Baader and W. Snyder, "Unification theory", in *Handbook of Automated Reasoning*, vol. I, Elsevier (2001), 445-533, doi:10.1016/B978-044450813-3/50010-2, Section 3.4 (commutativity `C_f`: unification with constants is NP-complete; the hardness result is the one in Garey and Johnson, attributed to Sethi).
