# UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS FOR SHORT EXPRESSIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS FOR SHORT EXPRESSIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(|V|, |C|, P)$ of two counts and pairs of token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-7.md) for UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS FOR SHORT EXPRESSIONS and feasible for UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
