# Feasible instances of AC-UNIFICATION

An instance is feasible when its token lists spell terms $s$ and $t$ over the variables $0, \dots, v - 1$ and some substitution $\sigma$, sending each of these variables to a term whose own variables are arbitrary natural numbers, makes $\sigma(s)$ and $\sigma(t)$ equal modulo associativity and commutativity of the AC symbols. The images need not be ground terms: $x = x$ is feasible with $\sigma(x) = x$.
