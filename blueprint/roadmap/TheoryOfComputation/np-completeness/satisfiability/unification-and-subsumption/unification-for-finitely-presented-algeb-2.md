# Instances of UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS

An instance is a sextuple of a natural number $|G|$, a list of natural operator arities, a list of pairs of raw token lists (read as the defining relations $\Gamma$), a natural number $|V|$, and two raw token lists, read as the terms $x$ and $y$. A token list may be empty or fail to parse; which term or relation it represents is defined only when parsing succeeds. Generator, operator and variable bounds belong to [validation](well-formed-9.md).
