# AC-UNIFICATION is NP-complete

The decision problem AC-UNIFICATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Kapur and P. Narendran, "Complexity of unification problems with associative-commutative operators", Journal of Automated Reasoning 9 (1992), 261-288, doi:10.1007/BF00245463 (membership in NP, hence NP-completeness); D. Benanav, D. Kapur and P. Narendran, "Complexity of matching problems", Journal of Symbolic Computation 3 (1987), 203-216, doi:10.1016/S0747-7171(87)80027-5 (NP-hardness). Both results, and the definition used here, are recorded in D. Kapur and P. Narendran, "Double-exponential complexity of computing a complete set of AC-unifiers", Proc. 7th IEEE Symposium on Logic in Computer Science (LICS 1992), 11-21, doi:10.1109/LICS.1992.185515, Section 1.
