# UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(|V|, |C|, P)$ of two counts and pairs of token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-6.md) and [feasible](unification-with-commutative-operators-f.md) for UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
