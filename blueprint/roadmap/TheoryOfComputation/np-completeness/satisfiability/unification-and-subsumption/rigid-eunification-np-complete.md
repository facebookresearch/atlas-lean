# RIGID E-UNIFICATION is NP-complete

The decision problem RIGID E-UNIFICATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. H. Gallier, P. Narendran, D. Plaisted, and W. Snyder, "Rigid E-unification: NP-completeness and applications to equational matings", Information and Computation 87 (1990), 129-195, doi:10.1016/0890-5401(90)90061-L.
- B. Beckert, "A completion-based method for mixed universal and rigid E-unification", Automated Deduction (CADE-12), LNAI 814 (1994), 678-692, doi:10.1007/3-540-58156-1_49, Definitions 1 and 2 (the problem) and Section 3 ("Purely rigid E-unification is NP-complete").
