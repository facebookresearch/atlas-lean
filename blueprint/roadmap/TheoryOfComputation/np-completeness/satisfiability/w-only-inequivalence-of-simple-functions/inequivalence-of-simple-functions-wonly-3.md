# INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `w` only as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `w` only accepts a bitstring
$x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE
FUNCTIONS in terms of `w` only instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md)
and [feasible](../inequivalence-of-simple-functions/inequivalence-of-simple-functions-feasib.md). It rejects every bitstring that
does not decode to an instance.
