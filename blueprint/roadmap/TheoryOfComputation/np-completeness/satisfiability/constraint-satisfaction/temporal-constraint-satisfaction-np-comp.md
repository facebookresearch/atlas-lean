# TEMPORAL CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION is NP-complete

The decision problem TEMPORAL CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Dechter, I. Meiri, and J. Pearl, "Temporal constraint networks", Artificial Intelligence 49 (1991), 61-95, doi:10.1016/0004-3702(91)90006-6, Section 2 (TCSP) and Theorem 4.1 (deciding consistency of a TCSP is NP-hard; credited there to E. Davis, private communication, 1989).
