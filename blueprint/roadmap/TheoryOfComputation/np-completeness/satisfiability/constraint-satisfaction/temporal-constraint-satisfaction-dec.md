# TEMPORAL CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION as a decision problem

The decision problem TEMPORAL CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a variable count and two constraint lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](temporal-constraint-satisfaction-feasibl.md) for TEMPORAL CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
