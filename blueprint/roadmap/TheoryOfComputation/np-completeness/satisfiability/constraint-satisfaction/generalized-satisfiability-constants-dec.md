# GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY WITH CONSTANTS as a decision problem

Fix a sequence $S$ of Boolean relations. The decision problem GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY WITH CONSTANTS over $S$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of collections of entry tuples, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-2.md) and [feasible](generalized-satisfiability-constants-fea.md) for $S$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
