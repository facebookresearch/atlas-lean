# CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND ORDER RELATIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND ORDER RELATIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of term nodes and a list of literals, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-2.md) for CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND ORDER RELATIONS and feasible for conjunctive satisfiability with functions. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
