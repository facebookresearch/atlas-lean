# CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES as a decision problem

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of term nodes and a list of literals, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) and [feasible](conjunctive-satisfiability-feasible.md) for CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
