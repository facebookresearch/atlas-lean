# CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES is NP-complete

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO16] CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES.
- V. R. Pratt, "Two easy theories whose combination is hard", technical report, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (1977), http://boole.stanford.edu/pub/sefnp.pdf.
