# GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY WITH CONSTANTS is NP-complete

For every well-formed sequence $S$ of relations satisfying none of the alternatives (3)–(6), the decision problem GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY WITH CONSTANTS over $S$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
