# INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly
when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH
ASSIGNMENTS instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](inequivalence-programs-assignments-feasi.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
