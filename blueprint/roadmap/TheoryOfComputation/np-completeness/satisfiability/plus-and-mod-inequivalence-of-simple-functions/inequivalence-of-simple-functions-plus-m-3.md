# INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `mod` as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `mod` accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE
OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `mod` instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](../inequivalence-of-simple-functions/inequivalence-of-simple-functions-feasib.md). It
rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
