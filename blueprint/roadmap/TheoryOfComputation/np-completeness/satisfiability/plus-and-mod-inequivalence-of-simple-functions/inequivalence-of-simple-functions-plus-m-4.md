# INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `mod` is NP-complete

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS in terms of `plus` and `mod` belongs to
NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO15] INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS, p. 276 (comment: remains NP-complete even if `f` and `g` are defined only in terms of `plus` and `mod` [Lieberherr, 1977]; Garey and Johnson list [Lieberherr, 1977] as a private communication, which was not available).
