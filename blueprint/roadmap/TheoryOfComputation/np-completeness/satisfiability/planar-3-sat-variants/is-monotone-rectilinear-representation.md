# Is monotone rectilinear representation in PLANAR MONOTONE RECTILINEAR 3SAT

In PLANAR MONOTONE RECTILINEAR 3SAT: the data form a crossing-free monotone rectilinear representation: one interval per variable, one height and one leg per literal for each clause; the intervals are disjoint; the heights are positive; each leg lies in the interval of its literal's variable; legs on the same side have different `x`; and no leg of a clause `c` lies in the `x`-range of the segment of a different clause `c'` on the same side with `h_{c'} ≤ h_c`. A literal index beyond the end of its clause reads as the positive literal of variable $0$.
