# PLANAR MONOTONE RECTILINEAR 3SAT is NP-complete

PLANAR MONOTONE RECTILINEAR 3SAT is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: de Berg and Khosravi (2010, 2012).
