# PLANAR POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT is NP-complete

PLANAR POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Moore and Robson (2001), Section 3.1 (Laroche, 1992).
