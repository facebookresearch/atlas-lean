# Barto–Kozik: finite idempotent Taylor algebras have cyclic terms of every prime arity above the domain size

Let $\mathbf{A}$ be a finite idempotent algebra. The following are equivalent: (a) $\mathbf{A}$ has a Taylor term; (b) $\mathbf{A}$ has a cyclic term $c(x_1,\dots,x_p)\approx c(x_2,\dots,x_p,x_1)$ of arity $p$ for every prime $p > |A|$; (c) $\mathbf{A}$ has a cyclic term of some arity $p\ge 2$. As corollaries one obtains the Maróti–McKenzie WNU characterization and the Hell–Nešetřil dichotomy for undirected graphs: a finite core graph with a loop-free non-bipartite core has no cyclic polymorphism, hence $H$-colouring is NP-complete unless $H$ is bipartite or has a loop.

## Proof sketch

The proof goes through the Absorption Theorem: a minimal counterexample is analysed via the $p$-ary relation of tuples on which the term operations of $\mathbf{A}$ can be cyclically shifted, and absorbing subuniverses allow induction on the size of $A$; when no proper absorbing subuniverse exists the relevant linked subdirect relation must be full, which yields the cyclic tuple. Primality of $p$ and $p > |A|$ are used so that the cyclic group $\mathbb{Z}_p$ acts freely on non-constant tuples.
