# Instances of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS solely in terms of `minus`

An instance of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS solely in terms of `minus` consists of the declared natural number $|X|$ of variables and two arbitrary lists of natural pairs, read as the prefix token lists of $f$ and $g$. A list need not parse to any expression: the empty list `[]` is an instance component. Parsing and the variable bounds are checked later.
