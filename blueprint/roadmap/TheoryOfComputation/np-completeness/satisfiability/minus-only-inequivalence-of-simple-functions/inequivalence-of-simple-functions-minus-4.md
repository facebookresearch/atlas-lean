# INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS solely in terms of `minus` is NP-complete

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS solely in terms of `minus` belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
