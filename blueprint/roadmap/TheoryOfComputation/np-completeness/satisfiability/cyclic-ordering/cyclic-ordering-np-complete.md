# CYCLIC ORDERING is NP-complete

The CYCLIC ORDERING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [MS2] CYCLIC ORDERING.
- Z. Galil and N. Megiddo, "Cyclic ordering is NP-complete", Theoretical Computer Science 5 (1977), 179-182, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(77)90005-6.
