# TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES FOR TWO EXPRESSIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES FOR TWO EXPRESSIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO10] TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES, p. 261, comment: remains NP-complete even if `|C| = 2` (reference: R. Statman (1976), private communication).
