# MODAL LOGIC S5-SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem MODAL LOGIC S5-SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO13] MODAL LOGIC S5-SATISFIABILITY.
- R. E. Ladner, "The computational complexity of provability in systems of modal propositional logic", SIAM Journal on Computing 6 (1977), 467-480, doi:10.1137/0206033.
