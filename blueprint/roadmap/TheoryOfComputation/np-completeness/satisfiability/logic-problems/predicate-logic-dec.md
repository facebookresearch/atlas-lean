# VALIDITY OF NEGATION-FREE EXISTENTIAL SENTENCES WITH TWO CONSTANTS as a decision problem

The decision problem VALIDITY OF NEGATION-FREE EXISTENTIAL SENTENCES WITH TWO CONSTANTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-7.md) and [feasible](predicate-logic-feasible.md): a negation-free existential sentence over two constants that is valid. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
