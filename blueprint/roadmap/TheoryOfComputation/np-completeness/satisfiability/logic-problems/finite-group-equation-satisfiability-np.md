# EQUATION SATISFIABILITY OVER A FINITE GROUP is NP-complete

For every non-solvable group $G$ with a bijection $e$ from $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ to $G$, the decision problem EQUATION SATISFIABILITY OVER A FINITE GROUP belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Goldmann and A. Russell, "The complexity of solving equations over finite groups", Information and Computation 178 (2002), 253-262, doi:10.1006/inco.2002.3173.
