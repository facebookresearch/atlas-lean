# SATISFIABILITY OF LTL WITH EVENTUALLY ONLY as a decision problem

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY OF LTL WITH EVENTUALLY ONLY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of postfix token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-2.md) and [feasible](ltleventually-satisfiability-feasible.md) for SATISFIABILITY OF LTL WITH EVENTUALLY ONLY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
