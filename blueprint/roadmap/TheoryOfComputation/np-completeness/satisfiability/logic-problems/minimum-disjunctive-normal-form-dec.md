# MINIMUM DISJUNCTIVE NORMAL FORM as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM DISJUNCTIVE NORMAL FORM accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a variable count, a list of assignments and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-4.md) and [feasible](minimum-disjunctive-normal-form-feasible.md) for MINIMUM DISJUNCTIVE NORMAL FORM. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
