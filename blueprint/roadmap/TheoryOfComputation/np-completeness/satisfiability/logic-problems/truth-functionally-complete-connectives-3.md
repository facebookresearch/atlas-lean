# TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES as a decision problem

The decision problem TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of postfix expressions, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-5.md) and [feasible](truth-functionally-complete-connectives-2.md) for TRUTH-FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE CONNECTIVES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
