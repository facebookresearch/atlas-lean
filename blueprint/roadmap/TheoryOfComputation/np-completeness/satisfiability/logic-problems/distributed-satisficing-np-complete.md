# DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING is NP-complete

The decision problem DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. H. Papadimitriou and J. Tsitsiklis, "On the complexity of designing distributed protocols", Information and Control 53 (1982), 211-218, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(82)91034-8, Section 2 (DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING) and Theorem 1.
