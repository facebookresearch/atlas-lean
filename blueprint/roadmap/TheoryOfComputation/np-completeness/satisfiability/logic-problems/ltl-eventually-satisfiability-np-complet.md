# SATISFIABILITY OF LTL WITH EVENTUALLY ONLY is NP-complete

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY OF LTL WITH EVENTUALLY ONLY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. P. Sistla and E. M. Clarke, "The complexity of propositional linear temporal logics", Journal of the ACM 32 (1985), 733-749, doi:10.1145/3828.3837, Theorem 3.5 (ii).
