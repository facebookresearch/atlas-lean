# KD45 SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem KD45 SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Y. Halpern and Y. Moses, "A guide to completeness and complexity for modal logics of knowledge and belief", Artificial Intelligence 54 (1992), 319-379, doi:10.1016/0004-3702(92)90049-4 (preliminary version: Proc. IJCAI-85, 480-490, Theorems 14 and 15).
- J. Y. Halpern and L. C. Rêgo, "Characterizing the NP-PSPACE gap in the satisfiability problem for modal logic", Journal of Logic and Computation 17 (2007), 795-806, doi:10.1093/logcom/exm029.
