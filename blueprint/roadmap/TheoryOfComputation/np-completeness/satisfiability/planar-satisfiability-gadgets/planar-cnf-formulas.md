# Planar CNF formulas

The *variable–clause incidence graph* of a CNF formula $\varphi$ is the bipartite graph with a vertex for every variable and every clause of $\varphi$, and an edge between $x$ and $C$ whenever $x$ or $\neg x$ occurs in $C$. The formula is *planar* if this graph is planar. Planar 3-SAT asks whether a planar CNF formula with clauses of at most three literals is satisfiable.
