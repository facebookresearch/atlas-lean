# EQUIVALENCE DELETION is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given variables $0, \dots, n - 1$, a list $C$ of equivalences between literals over them and a positive integer $K$, decide whether some truth assignment leaves at most $K$ of the equivalences unsatisfied.
