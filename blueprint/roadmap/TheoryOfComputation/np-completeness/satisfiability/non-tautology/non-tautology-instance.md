# Instances of NON-TAUTOLOGY

An instance of NON-TAUTOLOGY is a pair $(n, E)$ of a natural number $n$ (the variables $u_0, \dots, u_{n-1}$) and a Boolean expression $E$ with connectives $\neg$, $\wedge$, $\vee$ and $\to$, written in postfix notation as a list of tokens: $(0, x)$ is the variable $u_x$, $(1, \cdot)$ is $\neg$, and $(2, \cdot)$, $(3, \cdot)$, $(4, \cdot)$ are $\wedge$, $\vee$, $\to$ applied to the two most recent subexpressions in their written order.
