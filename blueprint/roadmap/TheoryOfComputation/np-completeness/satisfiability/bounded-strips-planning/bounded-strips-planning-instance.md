# Instances of BOUNDED STRIPS PLANNING

An instance of BOUNDED STRIPS PLANNING is a tuple $(N, O, I, (G_+, G_-), k)$: the number $N$ of conditions $0, \dots, N-1$, a list $O$ of operators, the initial state $I$ (a list of conditions), the positive and negative goals $G_+$ and $G_-$, and the plan-length bound $k$ written in unary as a Boolean list of length $k$.
