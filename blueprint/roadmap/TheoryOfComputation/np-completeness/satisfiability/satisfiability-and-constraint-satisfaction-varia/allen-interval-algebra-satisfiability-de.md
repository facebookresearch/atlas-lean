# ALLEN INTERVAL ALGEBRA SATISFIABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem ALLEN INTERVAL ALGEBRA SATISFIABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of assertions, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](allen-interval-algebra-satisfiability-fe.md) for ALLEN INTERVAL ALGEBRA SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
