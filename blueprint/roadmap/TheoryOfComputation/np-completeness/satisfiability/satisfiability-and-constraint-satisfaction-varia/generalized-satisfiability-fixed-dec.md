# GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY OVER FIXED NON-SCHAEFER RELATIONS as a decision problem

Fix a sequence $S$ of Boolean relations. The decision problem GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY over $S$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of collections, to an instance that is well-formed for $S$ and whose paired instance is feasible for GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
