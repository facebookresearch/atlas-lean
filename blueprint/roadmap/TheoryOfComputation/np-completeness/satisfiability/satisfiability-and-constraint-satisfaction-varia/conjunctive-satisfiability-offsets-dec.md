# CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH OFFSETS as a decision problem

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH OFFSETS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a clause list, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH OFFSETS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
