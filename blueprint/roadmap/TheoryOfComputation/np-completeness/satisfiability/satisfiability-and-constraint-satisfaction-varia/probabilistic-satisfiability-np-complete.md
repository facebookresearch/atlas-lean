# PROBABILISTIC SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem PROBABILISTIC SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- M. Finger and G. De Bona, "A logic based algorithm for solving probabilistic satisfiability", Advances in Artificial Intelligence (IBERAMIA 2010), Lecture Notes in Computer Science, 453-462, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-16952-6_46, Section 2 (the problem, Proposition 1 of Georgakopoulos et al., and NP-completeness).
