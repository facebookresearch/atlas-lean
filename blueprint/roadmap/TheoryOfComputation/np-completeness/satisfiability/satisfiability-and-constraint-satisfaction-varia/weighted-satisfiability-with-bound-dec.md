# WEIGHTED SATISFIABILITY WITH BOUND as a decision problem

The decision problem WEIGHTED SATISFIABILITY WITH BOUND accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples (a variable count, a token list, a bound, a weight list and a bound), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4-3.md) and [feasible](weighted-satisfiability-with-bound-feasi.md) for WEIGHTED SATISFIABILITY WITH BOUND. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
