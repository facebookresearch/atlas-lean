# GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of (arity, relation, collection) triples, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-3.md) and [feasible](generalized-satisfiability-feasible.md) for GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
