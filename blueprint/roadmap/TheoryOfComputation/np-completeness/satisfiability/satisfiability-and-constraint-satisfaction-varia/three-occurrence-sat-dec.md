# SATISFIABILITY WITH AT MOST THREE OCCURRENCES PER VARIABLE as a decision problem

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY WITH AT MOST THREE OCCURRENCES PER VARIABLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a CNF formula, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SATISFIABILITY WITH AT MOST THREE OCCURRENCES PER VARIABLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
