# CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH OFFSETS is NP-complete

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH OFFSETS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO16] CONJUNCTIVE SATISFIABILITY WITH FUNCTIONS AND INEQUALITIES, comment (the variant with terms `u` and `u + c`, attributed to [Chan, 1977]).
