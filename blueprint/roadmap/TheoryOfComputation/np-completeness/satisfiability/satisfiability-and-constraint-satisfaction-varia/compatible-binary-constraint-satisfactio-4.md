# COMPATIBLE BINARY CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION is NP-complete

The decision problem COMPATIBLE BINARY CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), entry MAXIMUM COMPATIBLE BINARY CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION, which records it as equivalent to MAXIMUM CLIQUE under PTAS-reduction following A. Jagota, "Constraint satisfaction and maximum clique", Working Notes, AAAI Spring Symposium on AI and NP-hard Problems, Stanford University (1993), 92-97 (not available to us).
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations* (R. E. Miller and J. W. Thatcher, eds.), Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9 (CLIQUE, the special case with one-value domains).
