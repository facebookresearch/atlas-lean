# GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY OVER FIXED NON-SCHAEFER RELATIONS is NP-complete

For every well-formed sequence $S$ of relations satisfying none of Schaefer's six alternatives, the decision problem GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY over $S$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO6] GENERALIZED SATISFIABILITY (comment: for any fixed sequence `S` the problem is NP-complete unless one of six alternatives holds).
- T. J. Schaefer, "The complexity of satisfiability problems", Proceedings of the Tenth Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 216-226, doi:10.1145/800133.804350.
