# PROBABILISTIC SATISFIABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem PROBABILISTIC SATISFIABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a list of weighted clauses, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4-2.md) and [feasible](probabilistic-satisfiability-feasible.md) for PROBABILISTIC SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
