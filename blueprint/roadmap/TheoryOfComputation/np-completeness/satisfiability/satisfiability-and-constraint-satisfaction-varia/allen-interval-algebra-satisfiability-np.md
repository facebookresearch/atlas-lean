# ALLEN INTERVAL ALGEBRA SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem ALLEN INTERVAL ALGEBRA SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
