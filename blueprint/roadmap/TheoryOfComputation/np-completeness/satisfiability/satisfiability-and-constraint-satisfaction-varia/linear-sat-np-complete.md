# LINEAR SAT is NP-complete

The decision problem LINEAR SAT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Porschen, E. Speckenmeyer and X. Zhao, "Linear CNF formulas and satisfiability", Discrete Applied Mathematics 157 (2009), 1046-1068, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2008.03.031 (SAT is NP-complete for the unrestricted linear formula class).
- S. Porschen, E. Speckenmeyer and B. Randerath, "On linear CNF formulas", in *Theory and Applications of Satisfiability Testing (SAT 2006)*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 4121 (2006), 212-225, doi:10.1007/11814948_22.
