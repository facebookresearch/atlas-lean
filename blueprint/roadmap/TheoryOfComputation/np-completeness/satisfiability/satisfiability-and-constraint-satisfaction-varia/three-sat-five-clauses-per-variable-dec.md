# 3SAT WITH AT MOST FIVE CLAUSES PER VARIABLE as a decision problem

The decision problem 3SAT WITH AT MOST FIVE CLAUSES PER VARIABLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a variable count and a CNF formula, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for 3SAT WITH AT MOST FIVE CLAUSES PER VARIABLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
