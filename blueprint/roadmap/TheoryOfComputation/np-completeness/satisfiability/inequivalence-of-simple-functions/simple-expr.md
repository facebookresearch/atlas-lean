# Expressions over the simple functions

Expressions over the simple functions are built from variables $x_k$ by the operations $s(e)$,
$p(e)$, $\mathrm{plus}(e, e')$, $\mathrm{div}(e, t)$ and $\mathrm{mod}(e, t)$ for a natural
number $t$, $w(e, e')$, and $\mathrm{select}_i(e_1, \dots, e_n)$ for a natural number $i$ and a
list of $n$ expressions.
