# INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$
decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE OF SIMPLE FUNCTIONS instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](inequivalence-of-simple-functions-feasib.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
