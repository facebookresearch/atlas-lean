# Bounded-occurrence 3-SAT instances

A bounded-occurrence 3-SAT instance is a pair
$(n, \varphi)$ of a natural number $n$, the number of variables, and a CNF formula $\varphi$.

The same pairs are the instances of LINEAR SAT, of SAT with at most three literals per clause and at most three clauses per variable, and of 3-SAT with at most five clauses per variable; each problem states its restrictions in its well-formedness condition.
