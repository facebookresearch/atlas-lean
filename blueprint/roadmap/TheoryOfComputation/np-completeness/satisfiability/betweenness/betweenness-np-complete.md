# BETWEENNESS is NP-complete

The BETWEENNESS decision problem belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), BETWEENNESS; Jaroslav Opatrný, "Total ordering problem", SIAM Journal on Computing 8 (1979), 111-114.
