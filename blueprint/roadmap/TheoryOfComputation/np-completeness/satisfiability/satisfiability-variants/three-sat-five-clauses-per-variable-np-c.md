# 3SAT WITH AT MOST FIVE CLAUSES PER VARIABLE is NP-complete

The decision problem 3SAT WITH AT MOST FIVE CLAUSES PER VARIABLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO2] 3-SATISFIABILITY (3SAT), comment (no attribution given).
- C. A. Tovey, "A simplified NP-complete satisfiability problem", Discrete Applied Mathematics 8 (1984), 85-89, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(84)90081-7 (3SAT stays NP-complete when every variable occurs in at most four clauses).
- P. Berman, M. Karpinski and A. D. Scott, "Computational complexity of some restricted instances of 3-SAT", Discrete Applied Mathematics 155 (2007), 649-653, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2006.07.009 (records Tovey's result).
- J. Kratochvíl, P. Savický and Z. Tuza, "One more occurrence of variables makes satisfiability jump from trivial to NP-complete", SIAM J. Comput. 22 (1993), 203-210, doi:10.1137/0222015.
