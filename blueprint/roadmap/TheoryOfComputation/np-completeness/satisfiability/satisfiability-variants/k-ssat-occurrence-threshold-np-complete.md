# (k, s)-SAT is NP-complete above the occurrence threshold

For all natural numbers $k \ge 3$ and $s \ge f(k) + 1$, the decision problem (k, s)-SAT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Kratochvíl, P. Savický and Z. Tuza, "One more occurrence of variables makes satisfiability jump from trivial to NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Computing 22 (1993), 203-210, doi:10.1137/0222015.
