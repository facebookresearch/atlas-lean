# RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA as a decision problem

The decision problem RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a variable count, a CNF formula and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
