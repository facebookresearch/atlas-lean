# SATISFIABILITY OF BOOLEAN EXPRESSIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY OF BOOLEAN EXPRESSIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
