# UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples (a generator count, arities, relations, a variable count and two token lists), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](unification-for-finitely-presented-algeb.md) for UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
