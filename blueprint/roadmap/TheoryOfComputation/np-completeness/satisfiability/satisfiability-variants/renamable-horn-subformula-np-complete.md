# RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA is NP-complete

The decision problem RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM RENAMABLE HORN SUBFORMULA (decision version).
- E. Boros, "Maximum renamable Horn sub-CNFs", Discrete Appl. Math. 96-97 (1999), 29-40, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(99)00031-1, Sections 1-2 (definitions; NP-hard because on CNFs whose clauses all have two literals the problem is equivalent to MAX-2-SAT).
