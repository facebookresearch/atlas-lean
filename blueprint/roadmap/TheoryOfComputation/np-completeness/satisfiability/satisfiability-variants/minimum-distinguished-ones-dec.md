# MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a variable count, a variable list, a CNF formula and a bound), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-4.md) and [feasible](minimum-distinguished-ones-feasible.md) for MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
