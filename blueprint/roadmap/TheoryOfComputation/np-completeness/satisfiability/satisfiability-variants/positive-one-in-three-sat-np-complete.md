# POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT is NP-complete

The decision problem POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO4] ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT (comment: remains NP-complete if no clause contains a negated literal).
- T. J. Schaefer, "The complexity of satisfiability problems", Proc. 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 216-226, doi:10.1145/800133.804350.
