# POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT as a decision problem

The decision problem POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-5.md) for POSITIVE ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT and feasible for ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
