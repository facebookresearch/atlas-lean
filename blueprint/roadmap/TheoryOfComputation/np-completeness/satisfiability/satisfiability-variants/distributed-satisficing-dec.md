# DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING as a decision problem

The decision problem DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples of four sizes and a quadruple list, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](distributed-satisficing-feasible.md) for DISTRIBUTED SATISFICING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
