# RIGID E-UNIFICATION as a decision problem

The decision problem RIGID E-UNIFICATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples (arities, a variable count, equations and two token lists), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-8.md) and [feasible](rigid-eunification-feasible.md) for RIGID E-UNIFICATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
