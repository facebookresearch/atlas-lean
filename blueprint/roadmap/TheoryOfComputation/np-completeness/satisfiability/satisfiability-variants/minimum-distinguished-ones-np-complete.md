# MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), MINIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES (decision version).
- V. Kann, "Polynomially bounded minimization problems which are hard to approximate", Proc. 20th ICALP, LNCS 700 (1993), 52-63, doi:10.1007/3-540-56939-1_61.
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations* (R. E. Miller and J. W. Thatcher, eds.), Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9 (satisfiability with at most three literals per clause is NP-complete).
