# UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS FOR SHORT EXPRESSIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS FOR SHORT EXPRESSIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AN16] UNIFICATION WITH COMMUTATIVE OPERATORS, p. 252, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if no `eᵢ` or `fᵢ` contains more than 7 occurrences of constants and variables" (the entry credits the problem to R. Sethi, private communication, 1977; the original was not available).
