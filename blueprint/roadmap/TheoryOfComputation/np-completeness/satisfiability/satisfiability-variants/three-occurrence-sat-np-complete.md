# SATISFIABILITY WITH AT MOST THREE OCCURRENCES PER VARIABLE is NP-complete

The decision problem SATISFIABILITY WITH AT MOST THREE OCCURRENCES PER VARIABLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. A. Tovey, "A simplified NP-complete satisfiability problem", Discrete Applied Mathematics 8(1) (1984), 85-89, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(84)90081-7, Theorem 2.1.
- M. Breen-McKay, B. Lavallee and B. D. Sullivan, "Hardness of the generalized coloring numbers", arXiv:2112.10562 (2021), Section 3 (records Tovey's Theorem 2.1: "SAT remains NP-complete when restricted to instances with 2 or 3 variables per clause and at most 3 occurrences per variable").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO1] SATISFIABILITY.
