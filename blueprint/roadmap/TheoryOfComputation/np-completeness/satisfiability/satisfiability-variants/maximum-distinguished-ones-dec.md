# MAXIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES as a decision problem

The decision problem MAXIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (two sizes, a CNF formula and a bound), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-3.md) and [feasible](maximum-distinguished-ones-feasible.md) for MAXIMUM DISTINGUISHED ONES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
