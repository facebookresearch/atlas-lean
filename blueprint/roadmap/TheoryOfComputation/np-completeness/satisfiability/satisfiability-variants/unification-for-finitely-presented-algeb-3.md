# UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNIFICATION FOR FINITELY PRESENTED ALGEBRAS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
