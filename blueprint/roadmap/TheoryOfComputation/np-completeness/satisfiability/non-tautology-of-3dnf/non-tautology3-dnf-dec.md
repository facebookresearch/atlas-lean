# NON-TAUTOLOGY OF 3DNF as a decision problem

The NON-TAUTOLOGY OF 3DNF decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances $(n, E)$, to an instance of NON-TAUTOLOGY OF 3DNF that is well-formed and feasible; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
