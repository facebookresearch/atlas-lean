# NON-TAUTOLOGY OF 3DNF is NP-complete

The NON-TAUTOLOGY OF 3DNF decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [LO8] NON-TAUTOLOGY, comment: remains NP-complete for E in disjunctive normal form with three literals per disjunct.
- S. A. Cook, "The complexity of theorem-proving procedures", Proceedings of the 3rd Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1971), 151-158, doi:10.1145/800157.805047.
