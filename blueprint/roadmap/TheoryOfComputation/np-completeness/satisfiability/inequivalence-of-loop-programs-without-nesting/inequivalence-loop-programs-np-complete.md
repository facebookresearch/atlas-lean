# INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING is NP-complete

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO14] INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING.
- R. L. Constable, H. B. Hunt III, and S. Sahni, "On the computational complexity of program scheme equivalence", SIAM Journal on Computing 9 (1980), 396-416, doi:10.1137/0209031 (journal version of Cornell report TR 74-201 (1974), which Garey and Johnson cite; NP-hardness of equivalence for the Loop 1 language).
- D. Tsichritzis, "The equivalence problem of simple programs", Journal of the ACM 17 (1970), 729-738, doi:10.1145/321607.321621 (membership in NP).
