# INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE OF LOOP
PROGRAMS WITHOUT NESTING instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](inequivalence-loop-programs-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not
decode to an instance.
