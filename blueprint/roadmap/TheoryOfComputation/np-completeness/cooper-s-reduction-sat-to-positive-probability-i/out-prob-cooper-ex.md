# Output probability of Cooper's worked example

For Cooper's example
$(u_1 \vee u_2 \vee u_3) \wedge (\neg u_1 \vee \neg u_2 \vee u_3) \wedge (u_2 \vee \neg u_3 \vee u_4)$,
the output node of Cooper's network is true with probability $5/8$: ten of the sixteen assignments
of $u_1, \dots, u_4$ satisfy the formula.
