# Output probability of Cooper's example as a count of assignments

For Cooper's example
$(u_1 \vee u_2 \vee u_3) \wedge (\neg u_1 \vee \neg u_2 \vee u_3) \wedge (u_2 \vee \neg u_3 \vee u_4)$,
the probability that the output node of Cooper's network is true equals $(1/2)^4$ times the number
of assignments of $u_1, \dots, u_4$ under which the network's output node evaluates to true.
