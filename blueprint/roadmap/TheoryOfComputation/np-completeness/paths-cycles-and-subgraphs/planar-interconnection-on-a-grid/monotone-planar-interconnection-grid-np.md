# PLANAR INTERCONNECTION ON A GRID with horizontally monotone paths is NP-complete

The decision problem PLANAR INTERCONNECTION ON A GRID with horizontally monotone paths is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Johnson, NP-completeness column (tenth edition, 1984), update to PLANAR INTERCONNECTION ON A GRID, crediting Karplus (1982).
