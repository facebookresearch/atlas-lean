# Length of the edge joining two vertices

For an instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER, a list $\ell$ of natural numbers and natural numbers $a, b$, pair the $k$-th entry of $E$ with $\ell_k$ for each $k$ less than the lengths of both lists. The length of the edge joining $a$ and $b$ is the $\ell_k$ of the first such pair whose entry of $E$ is $(a, b)$ or $(b, a)$, and $0$ if there is none.
