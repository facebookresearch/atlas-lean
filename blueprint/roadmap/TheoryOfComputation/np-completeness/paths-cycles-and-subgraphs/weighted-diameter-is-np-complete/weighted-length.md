# Length of a walk under a length assignment

For an instance of WEIGHTED DIAMETER, a list $\ell$ of natural numbers and a walk $u = x_0, x_1, \dots, x_m = v$ in the graph of the instance, the length of the walk under $\ell$ is

$$\sum_{t=0}^{m-1} \lambda(x_t, x_{t+1}),$$

where $\lambda(a, b)$ is the length, under $\ell$, of the edge joining $a$ and $b$.
