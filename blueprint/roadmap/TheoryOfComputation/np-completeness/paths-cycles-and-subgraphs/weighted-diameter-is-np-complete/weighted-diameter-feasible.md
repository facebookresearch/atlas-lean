# Feasibility for WEIGHTED DIAMETER

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER is feasible when there is a rearrangement $\ell$ of the list $C$ (a list with the same entries and multiplicities) such that, when the $k$-th edge of $E$ has length $\ell_k$, every pair of vertices $u, v$ (including $u = v$) is joined by a path in the graph of the instance whose length is at most $K$.
