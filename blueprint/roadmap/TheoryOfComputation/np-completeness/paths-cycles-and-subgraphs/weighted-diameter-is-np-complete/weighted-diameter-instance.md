# Instances of WEIGHTED DIAMETER

An instance of WEIGHTED DIAMETER is a quadruple $(n, E, C, K)$ consisting of a natural number $n$ (the vertices are $0, \dots, n-1$), a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers (the edges), a list $C$ of natural numbers (the collection of edge lengths, repetitions allowed) and a natural number $K$ (the bound). The same quadruples are the instances of the variant asking for diameter $K$ or greater; only the feasibility condition differs.
