# HAMILTONIAN CYCLE (DIRECTED) is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CYCLE
(DIRECTED) belongs to NP, and every decision problem $L$ in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (directed); Richard M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems" (1972).
