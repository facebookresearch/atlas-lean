# RECTILINEAR STEINER TREE is NP-complete

RECTILINEAR STEINER TREE is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND13], comment; Garey and Johnson (1977).
