# Feasible instances of POINT-TO-POINT CONNECTION

In POINT-TO-POINT CONNECTION: some sublist `E'` of the edges has total weight at most `K` and connects every listed pair whose two endpoints are vertices: in the graph with edge set `E'` on `0, …, n - 1`, each destination `dⱼ` is reachable from its source `sⱼ` whenever `sⱼ < n` and `dⱼ < n`. A pair with an endpoint outside the range imposes no condition here; well-formedness rejects such instances.
