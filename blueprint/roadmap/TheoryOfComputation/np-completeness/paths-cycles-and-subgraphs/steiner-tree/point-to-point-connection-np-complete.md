# POINT-TO-POINT CONNECTION is NP-complete

POINT-TO-POINT CONNECTION is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM POINT-TO-POINT CONNECTION; Li, McCormick and Simchi-Levi (1992).
