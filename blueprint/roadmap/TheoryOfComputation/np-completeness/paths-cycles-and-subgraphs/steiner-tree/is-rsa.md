# Is rsa in RECTILINEAR STEINER ARBORESCENCE

In RECTILINEAR STEINER ARBORESCENCE: the edge list `T` is a rectilinear Steiner arborescence for the points `P` when its edges are rectilinear and oriented right or up, each listed once, drawn without crossings, and form a directed tree rooted at the origin whose vertices include every point of `P`.
