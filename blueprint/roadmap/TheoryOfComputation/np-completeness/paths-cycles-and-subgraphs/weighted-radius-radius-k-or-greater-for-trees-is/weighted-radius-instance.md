# Weighted radius-at-least instances

An instance of WEIGHTED DIAMETER with radius $K$ or greater for trees is a quadruple $(n, E, C, K)$ of a natural number $n$, the number of vertices (the vertices are $0, \dots, n-1$), a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers, the edges, a list $C$ of natural numbers, the available edge lengths (repetitions allowed), and a natural number $K$, the bound.
