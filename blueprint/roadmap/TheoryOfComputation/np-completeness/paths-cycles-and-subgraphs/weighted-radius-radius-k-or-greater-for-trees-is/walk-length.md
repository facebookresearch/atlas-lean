# Length of a walk under edge lengths

Let $(n, E, C, K)$ be an instance and $w$ a list of natural numbers giving the $j$-th listed edge the length $w_j$. The length of a walk $p$ in the graph of the instance is the sum, over the edges $s$ traversed by $p$ (with multiplicity), of the length of $s$ under $w$ (the length of the first listed edge with the same endpoints as $s$, or $0$ if none).
