# Edge-length assignments giving radius at least $K$

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ is feasible when there is a rearrangement $w$ of the list $C$ (a permutation of its entries, with multiplicity), giving the $j$-th listed edge the length $w_j$, such that for every vertex $v$ there is a vertex $u$ for which every path from $v$ to $u$ in the graph of the instance has length at least $K$. Here the length of a path is the sum of the lengths of its edges.

This expresses that, with these edge lengths, the graph has radius at least $K$.
