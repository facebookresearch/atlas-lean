# HAMILTONIAN PATH on 4-regular planar graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH on 4-regular planar graphs belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Picouleau, "Complexity of the hamiltonian cycle in regular graph problem", Theoretical Computer Science 131 (1994), 463-473, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)90185-6 ("When the k-regular graph is planar, deciding whether the graph has a hamiltonian cycle (or path) was proved NP-complete for k = 3 and polynomial for k ≥ 6. We prove that for k = 4 and k = 5 the problem is NP-complete").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH (the parent problem).
