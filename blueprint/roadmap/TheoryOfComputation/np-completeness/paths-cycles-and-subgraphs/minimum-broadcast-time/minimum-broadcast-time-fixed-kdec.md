# MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME FOR FIXED K as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME FOR FIXED K accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E, V_0)$, to an instance whose quadruple $(n, E, V_0, K)$ is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
