# Graph of a MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME instance

The graph of an instance $(n, E, V_0, K)$ of MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ in which distinct vertices $i$ and $j$ are adjacent when $(i, j)$ or $(j, i)$ belongs to $E$. Loops are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$ contribute no edge.
