# MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME WITH A SINGLE ORIGINATOR as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME WITH A SINGLE ORIGINATOR accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, V_0, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed for MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME WITH A SINGLE ORIGINATOR and feasible for MINIMUM BROADCAST TIME. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
