# GEOMETRIC CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with equal requirements is NP-complete

The decision problem GEOMETRIC CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with equal requirements belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND6] GEOMETRIC CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE, comment (reference [Papadimitriou, 1976c]).
- C. H. Papadimitriou, "The complexity of the capacitated tree problem", Networks 8 (1978), 217-230, doi:10.1002/net.3230080306.
