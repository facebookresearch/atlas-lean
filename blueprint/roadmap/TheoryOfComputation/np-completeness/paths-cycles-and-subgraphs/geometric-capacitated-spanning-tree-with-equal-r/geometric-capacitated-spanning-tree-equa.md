# GEOMETRIC CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with equal requirements as a decision problem

The decision problem GEOMETRIC CAPACITATED SPANNING TREE with equal requirements accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](../geometric-capacitated-spanning-tree/geometric-capacitated-spanning-tree-feas.md).
It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
