# Well-formed BOUNDED DIAMETER AUGMENTATION instances

An instance $(u, E, D, K)$ of BOUNDED DIAMETER AUGMENTATION is well-formed when every entry of $u$ is true (so $n$ is written in unary), the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$ and no edge appears in both orientations, $0 < D < n$, and $K > 0$.
