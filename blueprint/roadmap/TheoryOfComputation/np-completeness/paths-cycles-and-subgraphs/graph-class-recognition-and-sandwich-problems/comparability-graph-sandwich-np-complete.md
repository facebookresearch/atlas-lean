# COMPARABILITY GRAPH SANDWICH is NP-complete

The decision problem COMPARABILITY GRAPH SANDWICH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. C. Golumbic, H. Kaplan and R. Shamir, "Graph sandwich problems", Journal of Algorithms 19(3) (1995), 449-473, doi:10.1006/jagm.1995.1047.
