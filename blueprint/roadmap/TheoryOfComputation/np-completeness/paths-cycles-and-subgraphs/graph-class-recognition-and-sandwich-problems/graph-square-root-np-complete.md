# GRAPH SQUARE ROOT is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH SQUARE ROOT belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Motwani and M. Sudan, "Computing roots of graphs is hard", Discrete Applied Mathematics 54 (1994), 81-88, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)00023-9.
- recorded in B. Farzad and M. Karimi, "Square-root finding problem in graphs, a complete dichotomy theorem", arXiv:1210.7684 (2012), Section 1.
