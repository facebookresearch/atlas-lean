# COMPARABILITY GRAPH SANDWICH as a decision problem

The decision problem COMPARABILITY GRAPH SANDWICH accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, E_1, E_2)$, to an instance that is well-formed for INTERVAL GRAPH SANDWICH and feasible for COMPARABILITY GRAPH SANDWICH. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
