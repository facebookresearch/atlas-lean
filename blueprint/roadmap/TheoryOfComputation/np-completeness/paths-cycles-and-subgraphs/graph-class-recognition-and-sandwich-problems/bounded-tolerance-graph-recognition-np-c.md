# BOUNDED TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem BOUNDED TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- G. B. Mertzios, I. Sau and S. Zaks, "The recognition of tolerance and bounded tolerance graphs", SIAM Journal on Computing 40 (2011), 1234-1257, doi:10.1137/090780328 (arXiv:1001.3251; Section 1: the definitions of tolerance and bounded tolerance graphs, and "both recognition problems are NP-complete", membership in NP citing Hayward and Shamir).
