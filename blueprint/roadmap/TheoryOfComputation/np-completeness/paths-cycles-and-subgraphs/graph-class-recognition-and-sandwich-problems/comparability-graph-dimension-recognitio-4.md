# COMPARABILITY GRAPH DIMENSION RECOGNITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $d \ge 3$, the decision problem RECOGNITION OF COMPARABILITY GRAPHS OF POSET DIMENSION AT MOST $d$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
