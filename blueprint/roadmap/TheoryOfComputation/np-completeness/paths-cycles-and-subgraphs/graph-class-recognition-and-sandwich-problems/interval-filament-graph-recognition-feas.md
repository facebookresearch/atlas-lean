# Feasible instances of RECOGNITION OF INTERVAL FILAMENT GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for RECOGNITION OF INTERVAL FILAMENT GRAPHS when the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ has an interval filament representation.
