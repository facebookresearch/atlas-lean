# BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION as a decision problem

The decision problem BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
