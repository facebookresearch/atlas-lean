# EPT GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem EPT GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. C. Golumbic and R. E. Jamison, "Edge and vertex intersection of paths in a tree", Discrete Mathematics 55 (1985), 151-159, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(85)90043-3.
- A. Boyacı, T. Ekim, M. Shalom and S. Zaks, "Graphs of edge-intersecting non-splitting paths in a tree: representations of holes, Part I", arXiv:1309.2898, Section 1.2 ("it is shown that EPT graph recognition is NP-complete", citing Golumbic and Jamison, Discrete Mathematics 55).
- L. Alcón, M. Gutierrez and M. P. Mazzoleni, "Helly EPT graphs on bounded degree trees: forbidden induced subgraphs and efficient recognition", arXiv:1604.08775, Section 2 (the definition of an EPT representation).
