# UNIT 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The UNIT 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a finite simple undirected graph $G = (V, E)$. Question: is $G$ a unit 2-interval graph, that is, can each vertex be associated with the union of two disjoint closed real intervals of length $1$ so that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their unions intersect?
