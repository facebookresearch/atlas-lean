# RECOGNITION OF TRIANGLE GRAPHS (PI* GRAPHS): `H` is a triangle graph

In RECOGNITION OF TRIANGLE GRAPHS (PI* GRAPHS), `H` is a triangle graph, that is, each vertex `v` has a trapezoid between the lines `y = 1` and `y = 0` with top side `[a v, b v]` and bottom side `[c v, d v]`, one of which is a single point (so the trapezoid is a triangle with its apex on either line), and two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their triangles intersect.
