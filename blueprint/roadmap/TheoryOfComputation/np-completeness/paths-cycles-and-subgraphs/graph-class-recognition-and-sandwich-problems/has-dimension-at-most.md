# Orders of dimension at most d

A binary relation $r$ on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ has dimension at most $d$ when there are $k \le d$ linear orderings of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, given by permutations $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{k-1}$, such that $r(u, v)$ holds exactly when $\sigma_j(u) < \sigma_j(v)$ for every $j < k$. With $k = 0$ the condition makes $r$ hold for all pairs, so an irreflexive relation on a nonempty set needs $k \ge 1$.
