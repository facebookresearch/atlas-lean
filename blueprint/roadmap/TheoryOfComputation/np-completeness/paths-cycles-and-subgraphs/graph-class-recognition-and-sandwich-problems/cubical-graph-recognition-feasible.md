# Feasible instances of CUBICAL GRAPH RECOGNITION

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for CUBICAL GRAPH RECOGNITION when, for some $d$, the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is isomorphic to a subgraph, not necessarily induced, of the hypercube $Q_d$.
