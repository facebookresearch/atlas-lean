# k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for $k$-DIR recognition. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
