# Feasible instances of INTERVAL GRAPH SANDWICH

An instance $(n, E_1, E_2)$ is feasible for INTERVAL GRAPH SANDWICH when some interval graph $H$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ satisfies $G_1 \le H \le G_2$ (every edge of $G_1$ is an edge of $H$ and every edge of $H$ is an edge of $G_2$), where $G_k$ is the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E_k$. Here $H$ is an interval graph when its vertices can be assigned closed real intervals so that two distinct vertices are adjacent exactly when their intervals meet.
