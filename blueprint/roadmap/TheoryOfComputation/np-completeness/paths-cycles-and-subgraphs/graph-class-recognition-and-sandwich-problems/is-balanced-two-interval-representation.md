# Balanced 2-interval representations

For a simple graph $H$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, a balanced 2-interval representation assigns to each vertex $v$ two closed real intervals $[l_{v,0}, r_{v,0}]$ and $[l_{v,1}, r_{v,1}]$ with $l_{v,j} \le r_{v,j}$ (a one-point interval is allowed) such that the two intervals of each vertex are disjoint and have the same length, $r_{v,0} - l_{v,0} = r_{v,1} - l_{v,1}$, and two distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in $H$ exactly when the union of the two intervals of $u$ meets the union of the two intervals of $v$.
