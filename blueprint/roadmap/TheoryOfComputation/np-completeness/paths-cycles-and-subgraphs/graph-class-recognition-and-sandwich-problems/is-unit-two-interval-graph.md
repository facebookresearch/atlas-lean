# UNIT 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION: `H` is a unit 2-interval graph

In UNIT 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION, `H` is a unit 2-interval graph, that is, each vertex `v` is assigned two disjoint closed intervals `[l v 0, l v 0 + 1]` and `[l v 1, l v 1 + 1]` of length `1`, and two distinct vertices are adjacent in `H` exactly when the unions of their intervals intersect.
