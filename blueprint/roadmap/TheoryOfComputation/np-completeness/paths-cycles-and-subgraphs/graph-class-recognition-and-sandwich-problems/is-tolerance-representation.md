# TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION: `(l, r, t)` is a tolerance representation of `G`

In TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION, `(l, r, t)` is a tolerance representation of `G`, that is, vertex `v` has the closed interval `[l v, r v]` and the positive tolerance `t v`, and two distinct vertices are adjacent if and only if their intervals intersect in length at least the smaller of their tolerances.
