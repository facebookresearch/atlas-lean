# Feasible instances of GRAPH SQUARE ROOT

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for GRAPH SQUARE ROOT when some simple graph $H$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ has square equal to the graph $G$ on those vertices in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$; here the square of $H$ makes two distinct vertices adjacent when they are adjacent in $H$ or have a common neighbor in $H$.
