# EPT CHORDAL GRAPH RECOGNITION as a decision problem

The decision problem EPT CHORDAL GRAPH RECOGNITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed for EPT GRAPH RECOGNITION and feasible for EPT CHORDAL GRAPH RECOGNITION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
