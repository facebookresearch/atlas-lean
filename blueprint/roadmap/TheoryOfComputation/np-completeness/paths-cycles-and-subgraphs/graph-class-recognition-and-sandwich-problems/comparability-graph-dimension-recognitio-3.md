# RECOGNITION OF COMPARABILITY GRAPHS OF POSET DIMENSION AT MOST d as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $d$. The decision problem RECOGNITION OF COMPARABILITY GRAPHS OF POSET DIMENSION AT MOST $d$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ (a natural number and a list of pairs), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for dimension bound $d$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
