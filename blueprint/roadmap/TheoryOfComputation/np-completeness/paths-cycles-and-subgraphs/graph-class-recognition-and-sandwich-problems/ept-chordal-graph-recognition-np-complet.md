# EPT CHORDAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem EPT CHORDAL GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. C. Golumbic and R. E. Jamison, "Edge and vertex intersection of paths in a tree", Discrete Mathematics 55 (1985), 151-159, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(85)90043-3, Theorem 3 ("It is an NP-complete problem to decide whether a path graph is an EPT graph").
- M. M. Sysło, "Triangulated edge intersection graphs of paths in a tree", Discrete Mathematics 55 (1985), 217-220, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(85)90050-0 (the reference the ISGCI database gives for recognizing EPT ∩ chordal graphs).
