# TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The TOLERANCE GRAPH RECOGNITION decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a graph `G = (V, E)`. Question: is `G` a tolerance graph?

## Sources
