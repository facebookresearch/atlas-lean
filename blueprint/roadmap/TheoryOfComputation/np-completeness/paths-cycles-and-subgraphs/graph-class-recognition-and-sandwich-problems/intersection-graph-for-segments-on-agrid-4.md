# INTERSECTION GRAPH FOR SEGMENTS ON A GRID is NP-complete

The decision problem INTERSECTION GRAPH FOR SEGMENTS ON A GRID belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND46] INTERSECTION GRAPH FOR SEGMENTS ON A GRID, p. 219, crediting F. Gavril, "Some NP-complete problems on graphs", Proceedings of the 11th Conference on Information Sciences and Systems, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore (1977), 91-95 (transformation from 3-PARTITION).
