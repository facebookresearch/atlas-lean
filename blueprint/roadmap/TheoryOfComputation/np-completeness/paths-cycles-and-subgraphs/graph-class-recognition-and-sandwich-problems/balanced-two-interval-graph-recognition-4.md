# BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Gambette and S. Vialette, "On restrictions of balanced 2-interval graphs", Graph-Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science (WG 2007), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 4769 (2007), 55-65, doi:10.1007/978-3-540-74839-7_6 (arXiv:0704.1571; Section 1: definitions of 2-interval and balanced 2-interval graphs; Theorem 1: "Recognizing balanced 2-interval graphs is an NP-complete problem").
- ISGCI, class "balanced 2-interval" (gc_876), problem Recognition, NP-complete.
