# PROBE (2,2)-GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

PROBE (2,2)-GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Dantas, Faria, de Figueiredo and Teixeira (2016; announced 2013).
