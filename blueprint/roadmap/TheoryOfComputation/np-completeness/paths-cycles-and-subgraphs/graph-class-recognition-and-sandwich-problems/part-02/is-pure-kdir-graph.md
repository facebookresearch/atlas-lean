# Is pure kdir graph in PURE-k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION

In PURE-k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION, for the fixed number of slopes `k`: `H` is a PURE-`k`-DIR graph: there are slopes `s j` (`j : Fin k`) and, for each vertex `v`, a non-degenerate closed segment from `a v` to `b v` whose slope is `s (c v)`, such that segments of distinct vertices with the same slope are disjoint, and two distinct vertices are adjacent in `H` exactly when their segments intersect.
