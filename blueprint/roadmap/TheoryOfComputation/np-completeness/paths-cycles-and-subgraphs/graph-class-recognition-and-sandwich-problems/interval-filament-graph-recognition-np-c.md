# RECOGNITION OF INTERVAL FILAMENT GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem RECOGNITION OF INTERVAL FILAMENT GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
