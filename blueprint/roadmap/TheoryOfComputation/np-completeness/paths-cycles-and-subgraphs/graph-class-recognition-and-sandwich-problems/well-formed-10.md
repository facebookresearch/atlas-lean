# Well-formed instances of INTERVAL GRAPH SANDWICH

An instance $(n, E_1, E_2)$ of INTERVAL GRAPH SANDWICH is well-formed when, for $k = 1, 2$, the list $E_k$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E_k$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E_k$, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E_1$ has $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ in $E_2$.
