# CLIQUE GRAPH (clique graph recognition) is NP-complete

The CLIQUE GRAPH (clique graph recognition) decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. Alcón, L. Faria, C. M. H. de Figueiredo and M. Gutierrez, "The complexity of clique graph recognition", Theoretical Computer Science 410(21-23) (2009), 2072-2083, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2009.01.018.
