# k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete for every fixed k ≥ 2

For every natural number $k \ge 2$, the decision problem k-DIR GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Kratochvíl and J. Matoušek, "NP-hardness results for intersection graphs", Commentationes Mathematicae Universitatis Carolinae 30 (1989), no. 4, 761-773, http://dml.cz/dmlcz/106799 (Theorem 1 and Proposition 5).
- J. Kratochvíl, "A special planar satisfiability problem and a consequence of its NP-completeness", Discrete Applied Mathematics 52 (1994), 233-252, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90143-0 (the case $k = 2$).
- J. Kratochvíl and J. Matoušek, "Intersection graphs of segments", Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 62 (1994), 289-315, doi:10.1006/jctb.1994.1071 (membership in NP).
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_935.html), problem "Recognition" on 3-DIR graphs: NP-complete.
