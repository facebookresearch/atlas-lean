# Feasible instances of BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION

An instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for BALANCED 2-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION when the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ has a balanced 2-interval representation.
