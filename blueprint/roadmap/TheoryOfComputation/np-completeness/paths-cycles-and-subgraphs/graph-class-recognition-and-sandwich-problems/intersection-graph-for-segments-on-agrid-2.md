# Feasible instances of INTERSECTION GRAPH FOR SEGMENTS ON A GRID

An instance $(n, E, M, N)$ is feasible for INTERSECTION GRAPH FOR SEGMENTS ON A GRID when there is a one-to-one assignment of segments on the $M \times N$ grid to the vertices $v < n$ such that two distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in the graph on $0, \dots, n-1$ given by $E$ (adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) exactly when their closed segments in $\mathbb{R}^2$ have a common point.
