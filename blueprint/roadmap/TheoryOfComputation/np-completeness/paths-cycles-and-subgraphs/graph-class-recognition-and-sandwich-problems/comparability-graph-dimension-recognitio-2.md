# Feasible instances of RECOGNITION OF COMPARABILITY GRAPHS OF POSET DIMENSION AT MOST d

For a natural number $d$, an instance $(n, E)$ is feasible for RECOGNITION OF COMPARABILITY GRAPHS OF POSET DIMENSION AT MOST $d$ when the graph on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$ is the comparability graph of a strict partial order of dimension at most $d$.
