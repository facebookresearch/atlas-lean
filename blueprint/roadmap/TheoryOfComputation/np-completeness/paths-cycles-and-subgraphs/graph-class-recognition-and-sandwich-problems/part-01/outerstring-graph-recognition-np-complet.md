# OUTERSTRING GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

OUTERSTRING GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Rok and Walczak (2019), Section 1, recording Kratochvíl (1991) and Schaefer, Sedgwick and Štefankovič (2003); ISGCI, class `gc_951`.
