# POLARITY ON PLANAR TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

POLARITY ON PLANAR TRIANGLE-FREE GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Le and Nevries (2014); ISGCI, class `gc_1274`, problem Polarity.
