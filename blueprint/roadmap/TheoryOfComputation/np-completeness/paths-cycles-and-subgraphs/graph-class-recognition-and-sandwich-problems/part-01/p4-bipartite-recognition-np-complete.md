# P_4-BIPARTITE GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

P_4-BIPARTITE GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Hoàng and Le (2001), Lemma 1; Achlioptas (1997).
