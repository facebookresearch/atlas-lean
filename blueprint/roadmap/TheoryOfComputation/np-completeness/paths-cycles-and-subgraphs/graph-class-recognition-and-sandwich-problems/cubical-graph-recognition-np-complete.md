# CUBICAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem CUBICAL GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- F. Afrati, C. H. Papadimitriou and G. Papageorgiou, "The complexity of cubical graphs", Information and Control 66 (1985), 53-60, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(85)80012-7.
