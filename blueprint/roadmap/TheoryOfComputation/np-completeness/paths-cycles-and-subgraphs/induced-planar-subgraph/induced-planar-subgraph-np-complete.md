# INDUCED PLANAR SUBGRAPH is NP-complete

The decision problem INDUCED PLANAR SUBGRAPH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has
a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT27] PLANAR SUBGRAPH, p. 197 (comment: "Corresponding problem in which `G'` is the subgraph induced by a set `V'` of at least `K` vertices is also NP-complete").
- M. S. Krishnamoorthy and N. Deo, "Node-deletion NP-complete problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 8 (1979), 619-625, doi:10.1137/0208049 (property (v), planar).
- M. Yannakakis, "Node- and edge-deletion NP-complete problems", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 253-264, doi:10.1145/800133.804355.
