# DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS as a decision problem

The decision problem DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a two-commodity instance and a list of triples, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
