The decision problem DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH UNIT CAPACITIES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, A, (s_1, t_1, R_1), (s_2, t_2, R_2))$, to an instance that is well-formed for DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH UNIT CAPACITIES and feasible for DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW.
