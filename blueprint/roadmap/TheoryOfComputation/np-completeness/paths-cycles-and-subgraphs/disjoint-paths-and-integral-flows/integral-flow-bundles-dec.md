# INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, A, s, t, \mathcal{B}, R)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
