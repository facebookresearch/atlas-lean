# DIRECTED TWO DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED TWO DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND40] DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS, p. 217 (the problem; its comment records that the directed version is NP-complete in general).
- S. Fortune, J. Hopcroft, and J. Wyllie, "The directed subgraph homeomorphism problem", Theoretical Computer Science 10 (1980), 111-121, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(80)90009-2 (the directed problem is NP-complete already for two pairs).
