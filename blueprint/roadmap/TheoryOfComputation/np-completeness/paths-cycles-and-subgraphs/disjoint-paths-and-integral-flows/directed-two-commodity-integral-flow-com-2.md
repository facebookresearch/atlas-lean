# Feasible instances of DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS

An instance is feasible if there are integral flows $f_1,f_2$ for the two commodities whose sum on each ordered vertex pair is at most its capacity. Use the first matching arc's capacity; a pair with no matching arc has capacity zero. Each restriction $(u,v,1)$ requires $f_2(u,v)=0$, and each restriction $(u,v,2)$ requires $f_1(u,v)=0$, for endpoints in the vertex set. The requirement that the two commodities share terminals is a separate well-formedness condition.
