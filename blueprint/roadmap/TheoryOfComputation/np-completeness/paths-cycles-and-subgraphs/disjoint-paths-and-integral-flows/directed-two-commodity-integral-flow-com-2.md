# Feasible instances of DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS

An instance is feasible when some integral flows $f_1$ for commodity 1 and $f_2$ for commodity 2 have $f_1 + f_2$ within the capacity on every arc, an arc restricted to commodity 1 carries no $f_2$, and an arc restricted to commodity 2 carries no $f_1$.
