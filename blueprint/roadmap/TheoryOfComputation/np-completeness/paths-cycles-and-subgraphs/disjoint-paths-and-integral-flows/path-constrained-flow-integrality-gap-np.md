# Whether the best rational path-constrained flow fails to exceed the best integral one is NP-complete

Whether the best rational path-constrained flow fails to exceed the best integral one is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
