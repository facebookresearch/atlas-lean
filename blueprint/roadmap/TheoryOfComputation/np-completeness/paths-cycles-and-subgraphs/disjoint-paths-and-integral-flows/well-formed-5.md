# Well-formed instances of INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES OF UNIT CAPACITY

An instance is well-formed when the arcs form a digraph on $0, \dots, n-1$ without loops or repeats, $s, t$ are vertices, every bundle consists of arcs and has positive capacity, the bundles cover all arcs, and $R > 0$, and every bundle has capacity $1$ and exactly two distinct arcs.
