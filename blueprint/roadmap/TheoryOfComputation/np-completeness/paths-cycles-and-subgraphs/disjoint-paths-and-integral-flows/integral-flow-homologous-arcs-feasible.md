# Feasible instances of INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS

The source $s$ and sink $t$ must be vertices. An instance is feasible when some natural-number flow respects the capacities, is conserved away from $s,t$, takes equal values on the two ordered vertex pairs of each listed homologous pair with all endpoints in range, and has net flow into $t$ at least $R$. The first arc record with the specified endpoints supplies its capacity. If no record matches, the capacity is zero and the flow on that pair must vanish.
