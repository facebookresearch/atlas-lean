# INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES is NP-complete

The decision problem INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann, *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MAXIMUM INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES.
- N. Garg, V. V. Vazirani and M. Yannakakis, "Primal-dual approximation algorithms for integral flow and multicut in trees", Algorithmica 18 (1997), 3-20, doi:10.1007/BF02523685.
