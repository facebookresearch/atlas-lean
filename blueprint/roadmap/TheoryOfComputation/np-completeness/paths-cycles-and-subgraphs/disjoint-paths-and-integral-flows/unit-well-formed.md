# Well-formed instances of DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH UNIT CAPACITIES

An instance is well-formed when it is well-formed for directed two-commodity integral flow (the arcs form a digraph on $0, \dots, n-1$ without loops or repeats, every capacity is positive, and for each commodity the terminals are vertices and the requirement is positive), every capacity is $1$, and $R_1 = 1$.
