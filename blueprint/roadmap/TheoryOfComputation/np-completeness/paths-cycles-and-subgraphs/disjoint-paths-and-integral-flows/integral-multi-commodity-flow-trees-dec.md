# INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, P, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
