# Well-formed instances of DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS

An instance is well-formed when the two-commodity instance is well-formed (the arcs form a digraph on $0, \dots, n-1$ without loops or repeats, every capacity is positive, and for each commodity the terminals are vertices and the requirement is positive), $s_1 = s_2$ and $t_1 = t_2$, and the restrictions name distinct arcs, each with $k \in \{1, 2\}$.
