# INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, A, s, t, R, H)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
