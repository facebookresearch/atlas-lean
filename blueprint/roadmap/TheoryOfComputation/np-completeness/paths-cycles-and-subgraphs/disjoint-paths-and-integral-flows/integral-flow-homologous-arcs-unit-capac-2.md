# INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS AND UNIT CAPACITIES is NP-complete

The decision problem INTEGRAL FLOW WITH HOMOLOGOUS ARCS AND UNIT CAPACITIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. - S. Even, A. Itai and A. Shamir, "On the complexity of timetable and multicommodity flow problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 691-703, doi:10.1137/0205048.
