# INTEGRAL FLOW WITH MULTIPLIERS as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGRAL FLOW WITH MULTIPLIERS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, s, t, A, h, R)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for INTEGRAL FLOW WITH MULTIPLIERS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
