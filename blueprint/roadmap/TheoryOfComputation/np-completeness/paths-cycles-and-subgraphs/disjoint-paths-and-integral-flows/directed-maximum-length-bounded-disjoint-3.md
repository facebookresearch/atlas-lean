# DIRECTED MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS as a decision problem

The DIRECTED MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to a well-formed, feasible instance of DIRECTED MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
