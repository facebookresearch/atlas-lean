# DIRECTED TWO DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS as a decision problem

The decision problem DIRECTED TWO DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, A, s_1, t_1, s_2, t_2)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for DIRECTED TWO DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
