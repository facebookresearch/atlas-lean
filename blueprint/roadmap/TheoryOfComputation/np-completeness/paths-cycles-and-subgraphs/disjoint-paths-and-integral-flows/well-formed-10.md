# Well-formed instances of INTEGRAL K-MULTICOMMODITY FLOW ON TREES

An instance is well-formed when the endpoint pairs $(u, v)$ of the entries $(u, v, c)$ of $E$ have no repeated entries and each has $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u)$ not among them, the graph of the instance is a tree, every pair consists of two distinct vertices, and $K > 0$.
