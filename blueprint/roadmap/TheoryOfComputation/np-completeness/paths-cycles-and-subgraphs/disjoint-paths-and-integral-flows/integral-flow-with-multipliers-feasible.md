# Feasible instances of INTEGRAL FLOW WITH MULTIPLIERS

An instance is feasible if some natural-number flow indexed by arc position does not exceed the capacity of any listed arc and satisfies $h(v)\,\mathrm{inflow}(v)=\mathrm{outflow}(v)$ for each $v<n$ other than $s$ and $t$. A missing multiplier is zero, so conservation at such a vertex requires its outflow to be zero. The net flow into $t$, computed as inflow minus outflow in the integers, must be at least $R$.
