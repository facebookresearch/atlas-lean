# DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH COMMON TERMINALS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND38] DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW, p. 216, comment ("Variant in which s₁ = s₂, t₁ = t₂, and arcs can be restricted to carry only one specified commodity is also NP-complete (follows from [Even, Itai, and Shamir, 1976])").
- S. Even, A. Itai, and A. Shamir, "On the complexity of timetable and multicommodity flow problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 691-703, doi:10.1137/0205048.
