# INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES is NP-complete

The decision problem INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND36] INTEGRAL FLOW WITH BUNDLES.
- S. Sahni, "Computationally related problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 3 (1974), 262-279, doi:10.1137/0203021 (Problem N(iv), Theorem 2.2).
