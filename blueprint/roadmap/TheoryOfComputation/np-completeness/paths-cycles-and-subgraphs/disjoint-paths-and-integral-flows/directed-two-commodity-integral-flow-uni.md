# DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH UNIT CAPACITIES is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW WITH UNIT CAPACITIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 216, problem [ND38] DIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if `c(a) = 1` for all `a ∈ A` and `R₁ = 1`".
- S. Even, A. Itai, and A. Shamir, "On the complexity of timetable and multicommodity flow problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 691-703, doi:10.1137/0205048 (the reference Garey and Johnson give for [ND38]).
