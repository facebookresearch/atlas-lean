# Integral flows for a commodity

A natural-number flow $f$ is a flow for commodity $(s, t, R)$ when it is feasible for the arc capacities (nonnegative, at most the capacity on every ordered pair, hence zero off the arcs, and conserved at every vertex other than $s$ and $t$), $s$ and $t$ are vertices, and its net flow into $t$ is at least $R$.
