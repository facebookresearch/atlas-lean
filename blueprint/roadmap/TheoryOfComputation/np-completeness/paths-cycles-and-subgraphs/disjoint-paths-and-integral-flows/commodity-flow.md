# Integral flows for a commodity

A natural-number flow $f$ is a flow for a commodity $(s,t,R)$ if $s$ and $t$ are vertices, $f$ is within capacity on every ordered vertex pair, it is conserved at each vertex other than $s,t$, and its net flow into $t$ is at least $R$. The capacity of $(u,v)$ is the capacity in the first matching arc record, or zero if there is no match. Thus a pair not in the arc list carries no flow.
