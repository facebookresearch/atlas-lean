# Well-formed DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH instances with fixed degree bound

For a fixed degree bound $d$, an instance $(n, E, d', K)$ of DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH is well-formed when the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, $d' \le n$, $0 < K \le |E|$, and its degree bound $d'$ equals $d$.
