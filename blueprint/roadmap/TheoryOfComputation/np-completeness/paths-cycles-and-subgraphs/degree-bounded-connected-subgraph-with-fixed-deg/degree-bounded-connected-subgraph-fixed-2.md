# DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH is NP-complete for every fixed degree bound $d \ge 2$

For every natural number $d \ge 2$, the DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH decision problem with degree bound $d$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
