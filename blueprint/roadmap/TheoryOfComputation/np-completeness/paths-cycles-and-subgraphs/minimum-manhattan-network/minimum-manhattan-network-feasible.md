# Feasible MINIMUM MANHATTAN NETWORK instances

Some list $N$ of axis-parallel segments of total length at most $L$ has, for all points $p, q$ of $T$, a [rectilinear path](is-rectilinear-path-in.md) from $p$ to $q$ in the union of its segments whose length is the $L_1$ distance of $p$ and $q$. For $p = q$ the one-point path qualifies whether or not $p$ lies on a segment of $N$, so a terminal need not lie in the network; for example, with $T = \{p\}$ the empty list is a witness.
