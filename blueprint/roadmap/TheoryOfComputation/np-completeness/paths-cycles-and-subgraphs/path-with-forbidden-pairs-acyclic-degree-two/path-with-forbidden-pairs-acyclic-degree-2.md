# PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS is NP-complete on acyclic digraphs with in- and out-degree at most 2

PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS is NP-complete on acyclic digraphs with in- and out-degree at most 2: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
