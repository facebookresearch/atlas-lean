# EVEN ALTERNATING PATH (2-arc-colored digraphs) as a decision problem

The decision problem EVEN ALTERNATING PATH (2-arc-colored digraphs) accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of EVEN ALTERNATING PATH
(2-arc-colored digraphs) instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](even-alternating-path-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode
to an instance.
