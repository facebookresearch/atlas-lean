# EVEN ALTERNATING PATH (2-arc-colored digraphs) is NP-complete

The decision problem EVEN ALTERNATING PATH (2-arc-colored digraphs) belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", J. Algorithms 3 (1982), 381-395, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(82)90032-3, comment to problem [3] EVEN LENGTH PATH (reference: O. Vornberger, "Komplexität von Wegproblemen in Graphen", Doctoral Thesis, Gesamthochschule Paderborn (1980)).
