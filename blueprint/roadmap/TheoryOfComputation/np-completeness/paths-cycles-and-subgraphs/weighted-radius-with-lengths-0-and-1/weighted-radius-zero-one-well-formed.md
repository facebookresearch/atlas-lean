# Well-formed instances of WEIGHTED RADIUS WITH LENGTHS 0 AND 1

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ is well-formed for WEIGHTED RADIUS WITH LENGTHS 0 AND 1 when it is well-formed for WEIGHTED DIAMETER (the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$, the list $C$ has as many entries as $E$, and $K > 0$), every entry of $C$ is $0$ or $1$, and $K = 1$.
