# WEIGHTED RADIUS WITH LENGTHS 0 AND 1 as a decision problem

The decision problem WEIGHTED RADIUS WITH LENGTHS 0 AND 1 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, C, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed for WEIGHTED RADIUS WITH LENGTHS 0 AND 1 and feasible for WEIGHTED RADIUS (some assignment of the lengths in $C$ to the edges gives a vertex within weighted distance $K$ of every vertex). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
