# Edge lengths from a list assignment

For an instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER, a list $w$ of natural numbers and an edge $s = \{a, b\}$ of the graph, pair the $j$-th entry of $E$ with $w_j$ for each $j$ less than the lengths of both lists. The length of $s$ is the $w_j$ of the first such pair whose entry of $E$ is $(a, b)$ or $(b, a)$, and $0$ if there is none.
