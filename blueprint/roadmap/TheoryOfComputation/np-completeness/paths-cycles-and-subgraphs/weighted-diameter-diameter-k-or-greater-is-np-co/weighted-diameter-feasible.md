# Feasibility for WEIGHTED DIAMETER, diameter $K$ or greater

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER is feasible for the variant asking for diameter $K$ or greater when there are a rearrangement $w$ of the list $C$ (a list with the same entries and multiplicities) and vertices $u, v$ such that every path from $u$ to $v$ in the graph of the instance has length at least $K$, where the $j$-th edge of $E$ has length $w_j$. If no path joins $u$ and $v$, the condition holds vacuously.
