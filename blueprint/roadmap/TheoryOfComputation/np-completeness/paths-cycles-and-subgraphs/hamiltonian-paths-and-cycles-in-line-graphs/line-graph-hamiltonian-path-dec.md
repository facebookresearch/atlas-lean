# The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH IN LINE GRAPHS

HAMILTONIAN PATH IN LINE GRAPHS as a decision problem on bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted
exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to a well-formed graph $G$
whose line graph (one vertex per edge of $G$, two vertices adjacent when their edges share an
endpoint) has a Hamiltonian path. A bitstring that does not decode to an instance is rejected.
