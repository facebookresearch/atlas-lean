# DEFECTIVE SUPERCONCENTRATOR is NP-complete

The DEFECTIVE SUPERCONCENTRATOR decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 6(1) (1985), 145-159, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(85)90025-2, problem DEFECTIVE SUPERCONCENTRATOR.
