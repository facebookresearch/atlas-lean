# Feasible CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS instances

An instance $(n, A, E, B)$ of CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS is feasible when some closed walk, a list of steps with valid edge indices in which each step ends where the next starts and the last ends where the first starts, traverses every directed edge in its direction and every undirected edge at least once and has total length at most $B$.
