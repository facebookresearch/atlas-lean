# CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS is NP-complete

The CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND25] CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS.
- C. H. Papadimitriou, "On the complexity of edge traversing", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 544-554, doi:10.1145/321958.321974.
