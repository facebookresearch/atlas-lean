# Instances of CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS

An instance of CHINESE POSTMAN FOR MIXED GRAPHS is a tuple $(n, A, E, B)$ of a number $n$ of vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, a list $A$ of directed edges $(u, v, \ell)$ from $u$ to $v$ of length $\ell$, a list $E$ of undirected edges $(u, v, \ell)$ of length $\ell$, and a bound $B$.
