# Instances of EVEN HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX

An instance of EVEN HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX has the format of ODD HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX: a natural vertex count $n$, a list of natural pairs (the edge list) and a natural label $v$ for the given vertex. The label need not be a vertex below $n$: at $n = 1$ the label $1$ is allowed. Range belongs to the validation shared with ODD HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX: a simple edge list on the vertices below $n$, and $v < n$.
