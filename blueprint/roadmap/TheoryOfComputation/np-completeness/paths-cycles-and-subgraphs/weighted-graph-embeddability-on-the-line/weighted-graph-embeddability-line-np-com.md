# WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY on the line (K = 1) is NP-complete

The following holds: the decision problem WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY on the line (dimension $K = 1$) belongs to the class NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
