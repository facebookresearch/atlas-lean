# Instances of EVEN LENGTH PATH (directed)

An instance of EVEN LENGTH PATH (directed) consists of a natural vertex count $n$, a list of natural pairs (an entry `(u, v)` is read as the arc $u \to v$), and natural labels $s$ and $t$. The labels need not be vertices below $n$. Range and the other conditions of the usual problem belong to [validation](well-formed.md).
