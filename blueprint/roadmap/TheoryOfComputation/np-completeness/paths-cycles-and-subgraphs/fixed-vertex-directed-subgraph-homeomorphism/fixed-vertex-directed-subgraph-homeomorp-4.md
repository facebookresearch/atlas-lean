# FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH HOMEOMORPHISM is NP-complete

Let $H$ be a pattern digraph on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ with arc list $A_H$ that is
[well-formed](pattern-well-formed.md), such that the arcs of $A_H$ do not all have the same tail
and do not all have the same head. Then the decision problem [FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH
HOMEOMORPHISM](fixed-vertex-directed-subgraph-homeomorp-3.md) for $H$ belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
