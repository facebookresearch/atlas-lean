# FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH HOMEOMORPHISM as a decision problem

For a natural number $k$ and **any** list $A_H$ of natural pairs, the decision problem FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH HOMEOMORPHISM accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is [well-formed for $k$](well-formed.md) and [feasible for $A_H$](fixed-vertex-directed-subgraph-homeomorp-2.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance. The pattern itself is not validated: with $k = 1$, $A_H = [(1, 2)]$ and the host $(1, [\,], [0])$, both image lookups default to $0$ and the one-vertex path $[0]$ serves the arc. The hypothesis that the pattern is a well-formed digraph on $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ belongs to the hardness theorem.
