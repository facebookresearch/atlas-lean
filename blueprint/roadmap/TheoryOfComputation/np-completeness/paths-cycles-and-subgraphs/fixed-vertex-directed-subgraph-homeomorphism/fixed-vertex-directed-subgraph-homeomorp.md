# Instances of FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH HOMEOMORPHISM

An instance of FIXED-VERTEX DIRECTED SUBGRAPH HOMEOMORPHISM consists of a natural vertex count $n$ of $G$, a list of natural pairs (the arcs of $G$), and an arbitrary list of natural labels, read as the prescribed images of the pattern vertices. It is not a map with exactly $k$ entries: the pattern size $k$ is a parameter of the language, and at $k = 2$ even an empty image list decodes. The conditions on the images belong to [validation](well-formed.md).
