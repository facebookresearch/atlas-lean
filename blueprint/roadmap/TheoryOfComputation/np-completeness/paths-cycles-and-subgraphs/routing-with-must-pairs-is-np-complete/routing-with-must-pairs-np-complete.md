# ROUTING WITH MUST PAIRS is NP-complete

ROUTING WITH MUST PAIRS is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Johnson, NP-completeness column (1982), problem [2]; Ntafos and Hakimi (1979).
