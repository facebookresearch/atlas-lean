# BUS ROUTING (minimization, grid) is NP-complete

The BUS ROUTING (minimization, grid) decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 3 (1982), 381-395, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(82)90032-3, comment to problem [1] BUS ROUTING (reference: Rock).
- H. Rock, "Bus routing problems on acyclic networks", in J. R. Mühlbacher (ed.), Proc. 7th Conf. on Graph Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science, C. Hanser Verlag, Vienna (1982), 99-113, as cited by the column (Rock's paper was not available; the column records the result and its attribution).
