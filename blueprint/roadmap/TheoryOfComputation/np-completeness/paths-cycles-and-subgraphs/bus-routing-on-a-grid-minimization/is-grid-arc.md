# South and east arcs of a grid

A pair $a$ is a south or east arc of the $h \times w$ grid, whose vertex in row $r$ and column $c$ is numbered $r w + c$, when for some $r < h$ and $c < w$ either $c + 1 < w$ and $a = (r w + c, r w + c + 1)$ (an east arc) or $r + 1 < h$ and $a = (r w + c, (r + 1) w + c)$ (a south arc).
