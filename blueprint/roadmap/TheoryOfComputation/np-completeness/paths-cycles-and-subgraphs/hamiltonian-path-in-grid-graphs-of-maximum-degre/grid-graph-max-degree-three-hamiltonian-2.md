# HAMILTONIAN PATH IN GRID GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH IN GRID GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- C. H. Papadimitriou and U. V. Vazirani, "On two geometric problems related to the travelling salesman problem", Journal of Algorithms 5 (1984), 231-246, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(84)90029-4.
- A. Itai, C. H. Papadimitriou and J. L. Szwarcfiter, "Hamilton paths in grid graphs", SIAM Journal on Computing 11 (1982), 676-686, doi:10.1137/0211056.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_1114` (grid graph ∩ maximum degree 3), problem Hamiltonian path: NP-complete (Papadimitriou and Vazirani 1984).
