# HAMILTONIAN PATH for bipartite graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH for bipartite graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH, p. 199 (comment: "Remains NP-complete under restrictions (1) and (2) for HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT", where restriction (2) of [GT37] is "if G is bipartite [Krishnamoorthy, 1975]").
