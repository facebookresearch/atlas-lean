# ORIENTEERING is NP-complete

ORIENTEERING is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Golden, Levy and Vohra (1987) (problem, NP-hardness); Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT39] (reduction in the module docstring).
