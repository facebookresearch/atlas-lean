# DIRECTED MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete

The DIRECTED MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND41] MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS, p. 217, comment ("The same results hold if G is a directed graph and the paths must be directed paths").
- A. Itai, Y. Perl, and Y. Shiloach, "The complexity of finding maximum disjoint paths with length constraints", Networks 12 (1982), 277-286, doi:10.1002/net.3230120306.
