# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN GRID GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN GRID GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 3 belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
