# Well-formed 0/1 WEIGHTED DIAMETER instances

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER is well formed for the restriction to $0/1$ lengths and $K = 2$ when the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, with the reversed pair $(v, u)$ not in $E$, the list $C$ has as many entries as $E$, every entry of $C$ is $0$ or $1$, and $K = 2$.
