# UPGRADING SPANNING TREE is NP-complete

The following holds: the decision problem UPGRADING SPANNING TREE belongs to the class NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. O. Krumke, M. V. Marathe, H. Noltemeier, R. Ravi, S. S. Ravi, R. Sundaram and H.-C. Wirth, "Improving minimum cost spanning trees by upgrading nodes", *Journal of Algorithms* 33 (1999), 92-111, Definition 2 and the proof of Lemma 16, pp. 109-110 (a reduction from MINIMUM DOMINATING SET), doi:10.1006/jagm.1999.1021.
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, MINIMUM UPGRADING SPANNING TREE.
