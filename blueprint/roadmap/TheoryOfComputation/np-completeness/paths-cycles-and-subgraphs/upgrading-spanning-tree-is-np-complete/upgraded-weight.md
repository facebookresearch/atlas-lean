# Edge weight after upgrading vertices

For a finite set $W$ of natural numbers and an edge entry $e = (u, v, d_0, d_1, d_2)$, the upgraded weight is

$$d_W(e) = \begin{cases} d_2 & \text{if } u \in W \text{ and } v \in W, \\ d_1 & \text{if exactly one of } u, v \text{ lies in } W, \\ d_0 & \text{if } u \notin W \text{ and } v \notin W. \end{cases}$$
