# ISOMORPHIC SPANNING TREE for 3-stars is NP-complete

The decision problem ISOMORPHIC SPANNING TREE for 3-stars is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND8], comment (c) (Garey and Johnson, unpublished).
