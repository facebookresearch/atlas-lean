# Three Star

The 3-star with $n$ legs: the simple graph on the $3n + 1$ vertices $0, \dots, 3n$ whose edges
are, for each $k < n$, the edges $\{0, 3k + 1\}$, $\{3k + 1, 3k + 2\}$ and $\{3k + 2, 3k + 3\}$:
a center $0$ joined to the first vertex of each of $n$ disjoint paths on three vertices. It is the [graph of an edge list](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/graph-3-coloring/part-01/edge-graph.md) on these vertices and pairs.
