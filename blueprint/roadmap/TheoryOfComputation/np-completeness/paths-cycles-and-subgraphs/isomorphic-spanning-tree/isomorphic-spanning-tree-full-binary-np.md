# ISOMORPHIC SPANNING TREE for full binary trees is NP-complete

The decision problem ISOMORPHIC SPANNING TREE for full binary trees is NP-complete: it belongs
to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND8], comment (b); Papadimitriou and Yannakakis (1978;
published 1982).
