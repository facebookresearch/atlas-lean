# Feasible instances of OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE

In OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE: a spanning tree `T` with `∑_{u, v} W_T({u, v}) * r({u, v}) ≤ B`, the sum over unordered pairs `{u, v}` of distinct vertices (each once, as `u < v`), where `P u v` is the path joining `u` and `v` in `T` and `W_T({u, v})` its total weight.
