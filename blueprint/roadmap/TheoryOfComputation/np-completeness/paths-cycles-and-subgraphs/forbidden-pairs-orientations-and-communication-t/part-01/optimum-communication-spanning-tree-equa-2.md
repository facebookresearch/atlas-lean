# OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE is NP-complete with equal requirements

OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE is NP-complete with equal requirements: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND7], comment; Johnson, Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan (1978).
