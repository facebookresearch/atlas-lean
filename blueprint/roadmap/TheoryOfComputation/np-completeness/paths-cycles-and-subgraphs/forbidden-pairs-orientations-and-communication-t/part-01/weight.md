# Weight in OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE

In OPTIMUM COMMUNICATION SPANNING TREE: the weight of `u` and `v`: the entry in row `u` and column `v` of the weight matrix. This is an ordered lookup; on a raw matrix the entries for `(u, v)` and `(v, u)` need not agree, and well-formedness requires the matrix to be symmetric. A list entry that is not present (an index beyond the end of its list) reads as $0$.
