# ORIENTED DIAMETER is NP-complete for `K = 2`

ORIENTED DIAMETER is NP-complete for `K = 2`: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT64], comment; Chvátal and Thomassen (1978).
