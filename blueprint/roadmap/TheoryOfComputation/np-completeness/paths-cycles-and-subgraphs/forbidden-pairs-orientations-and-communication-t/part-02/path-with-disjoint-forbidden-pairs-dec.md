# PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS with disjoint pairs, on acyclic digraphs of in- and out-degree as a decision problem

PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS with disjoint pairs on acyclic digraphs of in- and out-degree at most 2 as a decision problem over bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes to an instance that is well formed and has a solution; every bitstring that decodes to no instance is rejected.
