# PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS OF ARCS is NP-complete

PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS OF ARCS is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT54], comment; Gabow, Maheshwari and Osterweil (1976).
