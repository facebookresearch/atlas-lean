# Well-formed instances of PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS on acyclic digraphs of in- and out-degree at most 2

Well-formedness of an instance of PATH WITH FORBIDDEN PAIRS on acyclic digraphs of in- and out-degree at most 2: the parent's (arcs, `s`, `t` and pairs use vertices below `m`), the digraph is acyclic, and every vertex has at most 2 distinct in-neighbours and at most 2 distinct out-neighbours.
