# Is routing tree of congestion in ROUTING TREE CONGESTION

In ROUTING TREE CONGESTION: `(T, τ)` is a routing tree for the graph on `0, …, n - 1` with weighted edges `es`, of congestion at most `K`: a binary phylogenetic tree with taxa `τ v`: `T` is a tree, `τ` is a bijection from the graph vertices onto the leaves (degree-one vertices) of `T`, and every other vertex of `T` has degree three; and at most `K` weight is routed along every edge of `T`.
