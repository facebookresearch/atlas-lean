# GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes,
under the canonical bitstring encoding of GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY instances, to an instance that
is [well-formed](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/graph-homomorphism/well-formed.md) and [feasible](graph-contractability-feasible.md). It rejects
every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
