# GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT51] GRAPH CONTRACTABILITY (attributed there to R. Statman, unpublished, 1976).
- A. E. Brouwer and H. J. Veldman, "Contractibility and NP-completeness", Journal of Graph Theory 11 (1987), 71-79, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190110111.
