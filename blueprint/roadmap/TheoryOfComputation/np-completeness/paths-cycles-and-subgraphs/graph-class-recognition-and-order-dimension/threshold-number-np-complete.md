# THRESHOLD NUMBER is NP-complete

The decision problem THRESHOLD NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT63] THRESHOLD NUMBER.
- V. Chvátal and P. L. Hammer, "Aggregation of inequalities in integer programming", Annals of Discrete Mathematics 1 (1977), 145-162, doi:10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70731-3 (threshold graphs and the threshold number).
- M. Yannakakis, "The complexity of the partial order dimension problem", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 3 (1982), 351-358, doi:10.1137/0603036 (NP-complete for every fixed `K ≥ 3`).
