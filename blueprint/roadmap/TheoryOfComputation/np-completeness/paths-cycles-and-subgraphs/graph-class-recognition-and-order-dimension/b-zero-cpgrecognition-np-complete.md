# B0-CPG GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem B0-CPG GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Z. Deniz, E. Galby, A. Munaro and B. Ries, "On contact graphs of paths on a grid", Graph Drawing and Network Visualization (GD 2018), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 11282 (2018), 317-330, doi:10.1007/978-3-030-04414-5_22, Theorem 2 (arXiv:1803.03468).
