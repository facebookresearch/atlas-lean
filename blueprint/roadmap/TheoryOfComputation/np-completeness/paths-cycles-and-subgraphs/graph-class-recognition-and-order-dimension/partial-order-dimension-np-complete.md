# PARTIAL ORDER DIMENSION is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTIAL ORDER DIMENSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), open problem [OPEN7] PARTIAL ORDER DIMENSION.
- M. Yannakakis, "The complexity of the partial order dimension problem", SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods 3(3) (1982), 351-358, doi:10.1137/0603036.
