# PARTIAL GRID GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem PARTIAL GRID GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- V. G. P. de Sá, G. D. da Fonseca, R. C. S. Machado and C. M. H. de Figueiredo, "Complexity dichotomy on partial grid recognition", Theoretical Computer Science 412 (2011), 2370-2379, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2011.01.018.
- S. N. Bhatt and S. S. Cosmadakis, "The complexity of minimizing wire lengths in VLSI layouts", Information Processing Letters 25 (1987), 263-267, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(87)90173-6.
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_440.html), problem "Recognition" on partial grid graphs: NP-complete.
