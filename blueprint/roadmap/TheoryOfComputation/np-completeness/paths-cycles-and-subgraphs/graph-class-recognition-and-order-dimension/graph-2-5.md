# Graph of a dimension instance

For an instance $(u, E, K)$ whose vertex count $n$ is the length of the list $u$, the graph of the instance is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ in which distinct vertices $i$ and $j$ are adjacent exactly when $(i, j)$ or $(j, i)$ belongs to $E$. Pairs in $E$ with an entry at least $n$, and pairs $(i, i)$, contribute no edge.
