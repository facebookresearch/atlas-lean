# LEAF POWER RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem LEAF POWER RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Dupré la Tour, M. Lafond and N. Ndiaye, "Recognizing leaf powers and pairwise compatibility graphs is NP-complete", Proceedings of the 2026 Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2026), 6166-6183, doi:10.1137/1.9781611978971.219 (arXiv:2510.19763; Section 2: the definition of leaf powers used below; Corollary 1.2: "Recognition of leaf powers" is NP-complete).
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_649.html), class leaf power, problem "Recognition": NP-complete.
