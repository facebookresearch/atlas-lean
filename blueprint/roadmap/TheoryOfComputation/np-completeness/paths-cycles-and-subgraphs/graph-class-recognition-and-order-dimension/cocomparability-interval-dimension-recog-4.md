# COCOMPARABILITY INTERVAL DIMENSION RECOGNITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $d \ge 3$, the decision problem COCOMPARABILITY INTERVAL DIMENSION RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
