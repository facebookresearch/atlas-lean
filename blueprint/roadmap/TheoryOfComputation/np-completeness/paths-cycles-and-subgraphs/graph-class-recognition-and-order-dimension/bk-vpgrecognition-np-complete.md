# B_k-VPG RECOGNITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $k$, the decision problem B_k-VPG RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- S. Chaplick, V. Jelínek, J. Kratochvíl and T. Vyskočil, "Bend-bounded path intersection graphs: sausages, noodles, and waffles on a grill", WG 2012, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 7551 (2012), 274-285, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-34611-8_28.
