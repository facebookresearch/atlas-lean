# BEND NUMBER is NP-complete

The decision problem BEND NUMBER belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Garg and R. Tamassia, "On the computational complexity of upward and rectilinear planarity testing", SIAM Journal on Computing 31 (2001), 601-625, doi:10.1137/S0097539794277123.
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, entry MINIMUM BEND NUMBER.
