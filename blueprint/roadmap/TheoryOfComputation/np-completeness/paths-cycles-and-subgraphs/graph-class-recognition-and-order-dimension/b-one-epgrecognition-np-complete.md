# B1-EPG RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem B1-EPG RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Heldt, K. Knauer and T. Ueckerdt, "Edge-intersection graphs of grid paths: the bend-number", Discrete Applied Mathematics 167 (2014), 144-162, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2013.10.035, Section 7 (arXiv:1009.2861).
