# B1-CPG RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem B1-CPG RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Champseix, E. Galby, A. Munaro and B. Ries, "CPG graphs: Some structural and hardness results", Discrete Applied Mathematics 290 (2021), 17-35, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2020.11.018 (arXiv:1903.01805; Section 1: the definition of CPG and `B_k`-CPG graphs; Theorem 9: "Recognition is NP-complete in `B_k`-CPG for any `k ≥ 1`").
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_1338.html), class B_1-CPG, problem "Recognition": NP-complete, citing Champseix, Galby, Munaro and Ries.
