# PARTIAL ORDER DIMENSION OF HEIGHT-ONE ORDERS FOR FIXED K is NP-complete

For every natural number $K \ge 4$, the decision problem PARTIAL ORDER DIMENSION OF HEIGHT-ONE ORDERS FOR FIXED K with bound $K$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
