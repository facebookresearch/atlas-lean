# LINEAR ARBORICITY TWO RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem LINEAR ARBORICITY TWO RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. C. Shermer, "On rectangle visibility graphs III. External visibility and complexity", Proceedings of the 8th Canadian Conference on Computational Geometry (CCCG'96), 234-239 (1996), doi:10.1515/9780773591134-041 (Section 3, Theorem 3.1: "Linear Arboricity 2 is NP-complete", for simple graphs, by modifying Péroche's proof for multigraphs).
- B. Péroche, "Complexité de l'arboricité linéaire d'un graphe", RAIRO Recherche Opérationnelle 16 (1982), 125-129, doi:10.1051/ro/1982160201251 (Théorème 4).
- ISGCI (https://www.graphclasses.org/classes/gc_733.html), class linear arboricity <= 2, problem "Recognition": NP-complete, citing Shermer.
