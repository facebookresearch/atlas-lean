# INTERVAL NUMBER AT MOST t as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $t$. The decision problem INTERVAL NUMBER AT MOST t accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ of a vertex count and an edge list, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for $t$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
