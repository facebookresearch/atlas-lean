# ALL-k-SIMPLICIAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 3$, the decision problem ALL-k-SIMPLICIAL GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Gambette and S. Vialette, "On restrictions of balanced 2-interval graphs", Graph-Theoretic Concepts in Computer Science (WG 2007), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 4769 (2007), 55-65, doi:10.1007/978-3-540-74839-7_6, Theorem 3 (arXiv:0704.1571).
