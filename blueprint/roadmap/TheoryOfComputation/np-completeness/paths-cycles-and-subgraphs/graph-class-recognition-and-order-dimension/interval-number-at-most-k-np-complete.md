# INTERVAL NUMBER AT MOST t is NP-complete

For every natural number $t \ge 2$, the decision problem INTERVAL NUMBER AT MOST t belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. B. West and D. B. Shmoys, "Recognizing graphs with fixed interval number is NP-complete", Discrete Applied Mathematics 8 (1984), 295-305, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(84)90127-6.
- W. T. Trotter and F. Harary, "On double and multiple interval graphs", Journal of Graph Theory 3 (1979), 205-211, doi:10.1002/jgt.3190030302 (definition of `t`-interval graphs).
