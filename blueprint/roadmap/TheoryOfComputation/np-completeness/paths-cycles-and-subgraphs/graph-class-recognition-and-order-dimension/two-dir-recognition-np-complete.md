# RECOGNITION OF 2-DIR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem RECOGNITION OF 2-DIR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Kratochvíl and J. Matoušek, "NP-hardness results for intersection graphs", Commentationes Mathematicae Universitatis Carolinae 30 (1989), 761-773, http://dml.cz/dmlcz/106799 (Theorem 1: "RECOG(k-DIR) and RECOG(PURE-k-DIR), for every k ≥ 2, are NP-complete"; Proposition 5, whose proof for 2-DIR is deferred to the next reference).
- J. Kratochvíl, "A special planar satisfiability problem and a consequence of its NP-completeness", Discrete Applied Mathematics 52 (1994), 233-252, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(94)90143-0.
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "2-DIR" (gc_864), problem Recognition: NP-complete.
