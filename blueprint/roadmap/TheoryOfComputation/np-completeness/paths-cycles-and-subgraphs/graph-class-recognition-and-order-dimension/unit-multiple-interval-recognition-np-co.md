# UNIT d-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

For every natural number $d \ge 2$, the decision problem UNIT d-INTERVAL GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V. Ardévol Martínez, R. Rizzi, F. Sikora and S. Vialette, "Recognizing unit multiple interval graphs is hard", Discrete Applied Mathematics 360 (2025), 258-274, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2024.09.011, Corollary 21 (arXiv:2309.11908; ISAAC 2023).
