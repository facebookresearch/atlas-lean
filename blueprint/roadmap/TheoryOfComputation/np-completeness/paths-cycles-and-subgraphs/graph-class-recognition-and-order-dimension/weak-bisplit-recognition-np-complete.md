# WEAK BISPLIT GRAPH RECOGNITION is NP-complete

The decision problem WEAK BISPLIT GRAPH RECOGNITION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Brandstädt, P. L. Hammer, V. B. Le and V. V. Lozin, "Bisplit graphs", Discrete Mathematics 299 (2005), 11-32, doi:10.1016/j.disc.2004.08.046 (Section 4: the definitions of `k`-bisplit and weak bisplit graphs; Theorem 15: "Recognizing weak bisplit graphs is NP-complete, even for 3-colorable comparability graphs"; earlier version: Rutcor Research Report RRR 28-2002).
