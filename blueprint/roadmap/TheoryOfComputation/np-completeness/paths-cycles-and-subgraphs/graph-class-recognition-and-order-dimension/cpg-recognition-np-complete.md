# RECOGNITION OF CPG GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem RECOGNITION OF CPG GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Champseix, E. Galby, A. Munaro and B. Ries, "CPG graphs: Some structural and hardness results", Discrete Applied Mathematics 290 (2021), 17-35, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2020.11.018, Theorem 10 ("Recognition is NP-complete in CPG"; membership in NP is their Theorem 4).
- Z. Deniz, E. Galby, A. Munaro and B. Ries, "On contact graphs of paths on a grid", Graph Drawing 2018, LNCS 11282 (2018), 317-330, doi:10.1007/978-3-030-04414-5_22 (the class).
