# RECOGNITION OF WEAK RECTANGLE VISIBILITY GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem RECOGNITION OF WEAK RECTANGLE VISIBILITY GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. C. Shermer, "On rectangle visibility graphs III. External visibility and complexity", in Proceedings of the 8th Canadian Conference on Computational Geometry (CCCG 1996), Carleton University Press (1996), 234-239, doi:10.1515/9780773591134-041 (Section 1: the definitions of bands of visibility, rectangle visibility graphs and weak rectangle visibility graphs used below; Section 4, Theorem 4.5, "RVG Recognition is NP-complete", and Section 5: "RVG Recognition is NP-complete, for weak, collinear, or noncollinear RVGs").
