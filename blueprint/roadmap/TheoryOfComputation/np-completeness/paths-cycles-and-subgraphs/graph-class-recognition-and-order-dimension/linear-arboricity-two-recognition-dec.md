# LINEAR ARBORICITY TWO RECOGNITION as a decision problem

The decision problem LINEAR ARBORICITY TWO RECOGNITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ of a vertex count and an edge list, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for LINEAR ARBORICITY TWO RECOGNITION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
