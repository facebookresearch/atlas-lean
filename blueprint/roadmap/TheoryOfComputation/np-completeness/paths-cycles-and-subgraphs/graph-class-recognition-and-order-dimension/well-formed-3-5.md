# Well-formed instances of TOPOLOGICAL MINOR TESTING

An instance is well-formed when the edge list $E_G$ of $G$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in it satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E_G$, and the edge list $E_H$ of $H$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in it satisfies $u < m$, $v < m$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E_H$.
