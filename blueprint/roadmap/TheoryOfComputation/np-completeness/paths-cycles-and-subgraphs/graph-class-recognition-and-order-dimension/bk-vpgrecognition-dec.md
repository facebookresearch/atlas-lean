# B_k-VPG RECOGNITION as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $k$. The decision problem B_k-VPG RECOGNITION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$ of a vertex count and an edge list, to an instance that is well-formed and whose graph is $B_k$-VPG. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
