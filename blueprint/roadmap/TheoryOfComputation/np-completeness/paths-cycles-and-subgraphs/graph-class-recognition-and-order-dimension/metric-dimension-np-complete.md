# METRIC DIMENSION is NP-complete

The decision problem METRIC DIMENSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT61] METRIC DIMENSION (an unpublished result of the authors).
- S. Khuller, B. Raghavachari, and A. Rosenfeld, "Landmarks in graphs", Discrete Applied Mathematics 70 (1996), 217-229, doi:10.1016/0166-218X(95)00106-2 (a published proof).
