# WEIGHTED DIAMETER with 0/1 lengths is NP-complete for $K = 2$

The following holds: the decision problem WEIGHTED DIAMETER with $0/1$ lengths and $K = 2$ belongs to the class NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT65] WEIGHTED DIAMETER, p. 205, comment (credited to Y. Perl and S. Zaks, 1978).
- Y. Perl and S. Zaks, "On the complexity of edge labelings for trees", *Theoretical Computer Science* 19 (1982), 1-16, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(82)90011-1.
