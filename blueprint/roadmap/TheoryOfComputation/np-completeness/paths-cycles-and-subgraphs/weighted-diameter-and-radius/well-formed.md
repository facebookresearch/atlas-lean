# Well-formed WEIGHTED DIAMETER instances

An instance $(n, E, C, K)$ of WEIGHTED DIAMETER is well formed when the list $E$ has no repeated entries, every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$ and $u \ne v$, with the reversed pair $(v, u)$ not in $E$, the list $C$ has as many entries as $E$, and $K > 0$.
