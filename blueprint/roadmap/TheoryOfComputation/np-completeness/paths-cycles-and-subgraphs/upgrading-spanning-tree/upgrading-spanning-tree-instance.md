# Instances of UPGRADING SPANNING TREE

An instance of UPGRADING SPANNING TREE is a tuple $(n, E, c, D, K)$ consisting of a natural number $n$ (the vertices are $0, \dots, n-1$), a list $E$ of quintuples $(u, v, d_0, d_1, d_2)$ of natural numbers, each describing the edge $\{u, v\}$ with its three weights, a list $c$ of natural numbers whose entry $v$ is the upgrade cost $c(v)$ of vertex $v$, a natural number $D$ (the tree-weight bound) and a natural number $K$ (the budget).
