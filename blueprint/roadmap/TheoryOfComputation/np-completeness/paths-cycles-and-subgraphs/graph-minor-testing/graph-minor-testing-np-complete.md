# GRAPH MINOR TESTING is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH MINOR TESTING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Matoušek and R. Thomas, "On the complexity of finding iso- and other morphisms for partial k-trees", Discrete Mathematics 108 (1992), 343-364, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(92)90687-B (minor containment is NP-complete, even for trees of bounded diameter).
- T. Gima, S. Kumabe, K. Kurita, Y. Okada and Y. Otachi, "Dichotomies for tree minor containment with structural parameters", in *WALCOM: Algorithms and Computation*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 14549, Springer (2024), 392-405, doi:10.1007/978-981-97-0566-5_28 (Section 1.2 records the result of Matoušek and Thomas; arXiv:2311.03225).
