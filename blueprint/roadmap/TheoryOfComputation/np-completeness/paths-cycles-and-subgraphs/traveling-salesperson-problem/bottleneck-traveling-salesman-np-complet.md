# BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN is NP-complete

The decision problem BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND24] BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN (transformation from HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT; NP-complete even with all distances in `{1, 2}`).
