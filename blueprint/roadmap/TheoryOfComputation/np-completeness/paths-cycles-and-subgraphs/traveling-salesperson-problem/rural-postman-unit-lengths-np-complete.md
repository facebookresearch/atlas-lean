# RURAL POSTMAN is NP-complete with unit lengths

RURAL POSTMAN is NP-complete with unit lengths: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND27], comment; Lenstra and Rinnooy Kan (1976).
