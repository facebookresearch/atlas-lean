# BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN as a decision problem

The decision problem BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, d, B)$ of a number of cities, a distance matrix and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
