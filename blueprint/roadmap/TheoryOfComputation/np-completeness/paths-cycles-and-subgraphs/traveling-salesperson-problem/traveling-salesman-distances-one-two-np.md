# TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
