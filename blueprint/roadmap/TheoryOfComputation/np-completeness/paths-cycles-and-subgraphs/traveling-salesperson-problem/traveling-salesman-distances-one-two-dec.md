# TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of TRAVELING SALESMAN instances $(m, d, B)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-7.md) for TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 and feasible for TRAVELING SALESMAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
