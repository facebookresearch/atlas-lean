# GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN IN THE L∞ METRIC is NP-complete

The decision problem GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN IN THE L∞ METRIC belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND23] GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN, comment.
- M. R. Garey, R. L. Graham and D. S. Johnson, "Some NP-complete geometric problems", Proceedings of the 8th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1976), 10-22, doi:10.1145/800113.803626 (the `L₁` case).
