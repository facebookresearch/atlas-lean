# GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN IN THE L∞ METRIC as a decision problem

The decision problem GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN IN THE L∞ METRIC accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(P, B)$ of a list of encoded integer points and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed for GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN (the points are nonempty and distinct and $B > 0$) and [feasible](geometric-traveling-salesman-linfinity-f.md) for GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN IN THE L∞ METRIC. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
