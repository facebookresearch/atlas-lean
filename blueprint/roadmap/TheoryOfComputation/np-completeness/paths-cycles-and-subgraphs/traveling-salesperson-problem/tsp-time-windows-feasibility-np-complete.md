# TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH TIME WINDOWS (FEASIBILITY) is NP-complete

The decision problem TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH TIME WINDOWS (FEASIBILITY) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. W. P. Savelsbergh, "Local search in routing problems with time windows", Annals of Operations Research 4 (1985), 285-305, doi:10.1007/BF02022044.
- M. W. P. Savelsbergh, *Computer Aided Routing*, CWI Tract 75, Centrum voor Wiskunde en Informatica, Amsterdam (1992), Section 4.1, pp. 19-20 (the statement and proof: "The problem of determining whether there exists a feasible tour for the TSPTW is NP-complete in the strong sense").
