# RURAL POSTMAN with unit lengths as a decision problem

RURAL POSTMAN with unit lengths as a decision problem over bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes to an instance that is well formed and has a solution: a closed walk in the graph that traverses every required edge and has total length at most the bound $B$, each traversal of an edge counting its length (so a single required edge needs $B \ge 2$); every bitstring that decodes to no instance is rejected.
