# RECTILINEAR TRAVELING SALESMAN is NP-complete

The decision problem RECTILINEAR TRAVELING SALESMAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND23] GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN, p. 212, comment: "Remains NP-complete in the strong sense if the distance measure is replaced by the L₁ "rectilinear" metric".
- M. R. Garey, R. L. Graham, and D. S. Johnson, "Some NP-complete geometric problems", Proc. 8th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1976), 10-22, doi:10.1145/800113.803626.
