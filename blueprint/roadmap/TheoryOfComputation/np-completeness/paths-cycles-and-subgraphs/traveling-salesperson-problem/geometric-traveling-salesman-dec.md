# GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN as a decision problem

The decision problem GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(P, B)$ of a list of encoded integer points and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
