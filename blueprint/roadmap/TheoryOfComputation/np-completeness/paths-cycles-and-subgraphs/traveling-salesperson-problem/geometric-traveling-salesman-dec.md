# GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN as a decision problem

The decision problem GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(P, B)$ of a list of encoded integer points and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-2.md) and [feasible](geometric-traveling-salesman-feasible.md) for GEOMETRIC TRAVELING SALESMAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
