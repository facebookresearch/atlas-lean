# BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 as a decision problem

The decision problem BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, d, B)$ of a number of cities, a distance matrix and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) for BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN WITH DISTANCES 1 AND 2 and feasible for BOTTLENECK TRAVELING SALESMAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
