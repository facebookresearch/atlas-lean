# METRIC TRAVELING SALESPERSON is NP-complete

The decision problem METRIC TRAVELING SALESPERSON belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND22] TRAVELING SALESMAN, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if d(cᵢ, cⱼ) ∈ {1, 2} for all cᵢ, cⱼ ∈ C" (transformation from HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT).
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), MINIMUM METRIC TRAVELING SALESPERSON PROBLEM.
