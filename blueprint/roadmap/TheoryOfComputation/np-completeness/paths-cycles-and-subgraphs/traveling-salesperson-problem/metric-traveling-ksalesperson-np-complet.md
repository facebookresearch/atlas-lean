# METRIC TRAVELING K-SALESPERSON is NP-complete

The decision problem METRIC TRAVELING K-SALESPERSON belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), MINIMUM METRIC TRAVELING K-SALESPERSON PROBLEM (decision version).
- G. N. Frederickson, M. S. Hecht, and C. E. Kim, "Approximation algorithms for some routing problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 7 (1978), 178-193, doi:10.1137/0207017 (the k-person min-max tour problem).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND22] TRAVELING SALESMAN (NP-complete with distances in `{1, 2}`, which satisfy the triangle inequality).
