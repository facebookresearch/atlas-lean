# INDUCED CYCLE is NP-complete

The decision problem INDUCED CYCLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT23] INDUCED PATH, comment ("The same result holds for the variant in which 'simple path' is replaced by 'simple cycle'"), attributed to M. Yannakakis, "Node- and edge-deletion NP-complete problems", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 253-264, doi:10.1145/800133.804355.
