# Instances of GENERAL ROUTING

An instance of GENERAL ROUTING consists of a RURAL POSTMAN instance $(n, E, E', B)$ (a vertex count, the edges with their lengths, the required edges $E'$ and a bound $B$) together with a list $V'$ of natural labels of required vertices. The required labels need not be vertices below $n$. Range and the other conditions of the usual problem belong to [validation](well-formed.md).
