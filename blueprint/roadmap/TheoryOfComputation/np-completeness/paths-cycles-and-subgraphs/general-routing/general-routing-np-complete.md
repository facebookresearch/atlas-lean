# GENERAL ROUTING is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERAL ROUTING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lenstra and A. H. G. Rinnooy Kan, "On general routing problems", Networks 6 (1976), 273-280, doi:10.1002/net.3230060305 (Section 2: the general routing problem, and the theorem that its special case `Q = ∅`, the rural postman problem, is polynomial complete).
- C. S. Orloff, "A fundamental problem in vehicle routing", Networks 4 (1974), 35-64, doi:10.1002/net.3230040105 (definition of the problem).
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM GENERAL ROUTING ("Generalization of ND27" of Garey and Johnson (1979)).
