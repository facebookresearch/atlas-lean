# Adding units along no stacks

For a list of (stack, symbol) pairs, adding $i$ units pushes $i$ copies of the symbol of the first
pair naming a stack onto that stack, for every stack named in the list, and leaves every other
stack unchanged. For every $i \in \mathbb{N}$ and all stack contents $S$ of the HAMPATH emitter,
adding $i$ units along the empty list of pairs leaves $S$ unchanged.
