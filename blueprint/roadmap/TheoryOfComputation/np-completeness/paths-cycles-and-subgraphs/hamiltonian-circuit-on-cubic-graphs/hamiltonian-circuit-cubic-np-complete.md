# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on $k$-regular graphs for $k = 3$, that is on cubic
graphs, belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction
to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT, the problem restricted here.
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. E. Tarjan, "The planar Hamiltonian circuit problem is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 704-714, doi:10.1137/0205049.
