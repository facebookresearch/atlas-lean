# UNDIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW as a decision problem

The decision problem UNDIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, E, (s_1, t_1, R_1), (s_2, t_2, R_2))$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) and [feasible](undirected-two-commodity-integral-flow-f.md) for UNDIRECTED TWO-COMMODITY INTEGRAL FLOW. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
