# MINIMUM QUADRATIC-COST FLOW is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM QUADRATIC-COST FLOW belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND32] MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW (p. 214), comment "However, becomes NP-complete once more if (4) is replaced by ∑_{a ∈ A} (p₁(a) f(a)² + p₂(a) f(a)) ≤ B" (credited there to P. P. Herrmann, 1973).
