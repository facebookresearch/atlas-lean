# MINIMUM QUADRATIC-COST FLOW as a decision problem

The decision problem MINIMUM QUADRATIC-COST FLOW accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, A, s, t, R, B)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](minimum-quadratic-cost-flow-feasible.md) for MINIMUM QUADRATIC-COST FLOW. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
