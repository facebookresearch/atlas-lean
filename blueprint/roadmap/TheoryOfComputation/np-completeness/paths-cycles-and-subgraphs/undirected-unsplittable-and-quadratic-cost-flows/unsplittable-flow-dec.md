# UNSPLITTABLE FLOW as a decision problem

The decision problem UNSPLITTABLE FLOW accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, E, s, T, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4.md) and [feasible](unsplittable-flow-feasible.md) for UNSPLITTABLE FLOW. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
