# UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS (NON-INTEGRAL) as a decision problem

The decision problem UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS (NON-INTEGRAL) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, E, s, t, R)$, to an instance that is well-formed for UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS and [feasible](non-integral-feasible.md) for UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS (NON-INTEGRAL). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
