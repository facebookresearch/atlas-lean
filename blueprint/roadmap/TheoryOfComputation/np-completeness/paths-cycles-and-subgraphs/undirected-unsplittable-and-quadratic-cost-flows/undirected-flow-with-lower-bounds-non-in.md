# UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS (NON-INTEGRAL) is NP-complete

The decision problem UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS (NON-INTEGRAL) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 216, problem [ND37] UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS, comment: "Problem is NP-complete in the strong sense, even if non-integral flows are allowed".
- A. Itai, "Two-commodity flow", Journal of the ACM 25 (1978), 596-611, doi:10.1145/322092.322100 (the reference Garey and Johnson give for [ND37]).
