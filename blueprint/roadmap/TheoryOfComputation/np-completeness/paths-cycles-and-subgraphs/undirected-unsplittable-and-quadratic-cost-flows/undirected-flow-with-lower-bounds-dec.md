# UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS as a decision problem

The decision problem UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, E, s, t, R)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](undirected-flow-with-lower-bounds-feasib.md) for UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
