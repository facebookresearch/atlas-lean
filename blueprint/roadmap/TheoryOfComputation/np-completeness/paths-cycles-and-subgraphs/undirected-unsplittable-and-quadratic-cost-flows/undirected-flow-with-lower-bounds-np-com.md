# UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS is NP-complete

The decision problem UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND37] UNDIRECTED FLOW WITH LOWER BOUNDS.
- A. Itai, "Two-commodity flow", Journal of the ACM 25 (1978), 596-611, doi:10.1145/322092.322100.
