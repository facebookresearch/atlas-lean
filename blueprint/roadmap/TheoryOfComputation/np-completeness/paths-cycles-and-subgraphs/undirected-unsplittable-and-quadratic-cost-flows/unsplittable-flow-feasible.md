# Feasible instances of UNSPLITTABLE FLOW

An instance is feasible when, provided the source $s$ and every sink are vertices (indices below $n$), there is one path from $s$ to each sink such that every edge carries total demand at most $K$ times its capacity. If the source or some sink is not a vertex, the predicate holds vacuously; the decision problem rejects such instances through its well-formedness test.
