# EDGE-SUBGRAPH on graphs of maximum degree 4 as a decision problem

The decision problem EDGE-SUBGRAPH on graphs of maximum degree 4 accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly
when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that
is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](../edge-subgraph/edge-subgraph-feasible.md). It
rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
