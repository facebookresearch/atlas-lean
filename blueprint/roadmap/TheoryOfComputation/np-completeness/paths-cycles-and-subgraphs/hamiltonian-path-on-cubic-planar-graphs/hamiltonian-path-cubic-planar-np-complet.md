# HAMILTONIAN PATH on cubic planar graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH on cubic planar graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 199, problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH, comment: "Remains NP-complete under restrictions (1) and (2) for HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT", where restriction (1) of [GT37] is "if `G` is planar, cubic, 3-connected, and has no face with fewer than 5 edges" [Garey, Johnson and Tarjan, 1976a].
- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. E. Tarjan, "The planar Hamiltonian circuit problem is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Computing 5 (1976), 704-714, doi:10.1137/0205049.
- C. Picouleau, "Complexity of the Hamiltonian cycle in regular graph problem", Theoretical Computer Science 131 (1994), 463-473, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)90185-6.
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_1102` (cubic ∩ planar), problem Hamiltonian path: NP-complete (Garey, Johnson and Tarjan 1976; Picouleau 1994).
