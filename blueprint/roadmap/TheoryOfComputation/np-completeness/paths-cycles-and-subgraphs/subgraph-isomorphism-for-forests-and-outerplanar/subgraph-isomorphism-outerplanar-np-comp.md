# SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM for connected outerplanar graphs is NP-complete

The SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM for connected outerplanar graphs decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: connected outerplanar graphs `G = (V₁, E₁)` and `H = (V₂, E₂)`. Question: does `G` contain a subgraph isomorphic to `H`, i.e., is there an injective map from `V₂` to `V₁` sending every edge of `H` to an edge of `G`?
