# EDGE-SUBGRAPH as a decision problem

The decision problem EDGE-SUBGRAPH accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the
canonical bitstring encoding of EDGE-SUBGRAPH instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/connected-bipartite-subgraph/well-formed.md) and [feasible](edge-subgraph-feasible.md). It rejects every
bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
