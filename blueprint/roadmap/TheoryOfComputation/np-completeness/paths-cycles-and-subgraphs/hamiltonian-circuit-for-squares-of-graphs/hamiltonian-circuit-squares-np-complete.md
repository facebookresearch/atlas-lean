# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT for squares of graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT for squares of graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT, p. 199, comment (3): "if G is the square of a graph", credited there to V. Chvátal (1976, private communication).
- P. Underground, "On graphs with Hamiltonian squares", Discrete Mathematics 21 (1978), 323, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(78)90164-4 (deciding whether the square of a given graph is Hamiltonian is NP-complete).
