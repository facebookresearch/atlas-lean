# Instances of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on circle graphs

An instance of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on circle graphs is a list of chords, one per vertex, each given by its endpoints $(a, b)$; two distinct chords $(a, b)$ and $(a', b')$ are adjacent in the circle graph when their endpoints interleave ($a < a' < b < b'$ or $a' < a < b' < b$).
