# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on circle graphs is NP-complete

The HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on circle graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT.
- P. Damaschke, "The Hamiltonian circuit problem for circle graphs is NP-complete", Information Processing Letters 32 (1989), 1-2, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(89)90059-8.
