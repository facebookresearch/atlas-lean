# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9 is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT.
- M. De Biasi, answer to "Polynomial problems in graph classes defined by forbidden induced cyclic subgraphs", Theoretical Computer Science Stack Exchange (2014), https://cstheory.stackexchange.com/a/24882 (Theorem: "for any `k ≥ 3`, the Hamiltonian cycle problem remains NP-complete even if the graph `G` does not contain cycles of length `≤ k`", by a gadget reduction from the planar directed case of Papadimitriou and Vazirani); ISGCI records the result for the class `girth >= 9` (`gc_1225`), citing this answer.
