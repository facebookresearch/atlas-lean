# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN TRIANGULAR GRID GRAPHS is NP-complete

The HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN TRIANGULAR GRID GRAPHS decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a finite set `V` of vertices of the tiling of the plane with unit-side equilateral triangles; the triangular grid graph `G` on `V` is the subgraph of the tiling induced by `V`. Question: does `G` have a Hamiltonian circuit?
