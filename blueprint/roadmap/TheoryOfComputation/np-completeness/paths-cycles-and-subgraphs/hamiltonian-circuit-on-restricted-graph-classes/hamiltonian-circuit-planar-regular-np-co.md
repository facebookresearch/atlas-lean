# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON PLANAR k-REGULAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

For $k = 4$ and for $k = 5$, the decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON PLANAR $k$-REGULAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Picouleau, "Complexity of the Hamiltonian cycle in regular graph problem", Theoretical Computer Science 131(2) (1994), 463-473, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)90185-6 (abstract: "When the k-regular graph is planar, deciding whether the graph has a hamiltonian cycle (or path) was proved NP-complete for k = 3 and polynomial for k ≥ 6. We prove that for k = 4 and k = 5 the problem is NP-complete.").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, classes "4-regular ∩ planar" (gc_1103) and "5-regular ∩ planar" (gc_1106), problem Hamiltonian cycle: NP-complete, with the reference to Picouleau (1994).
