# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on chordal bipartite graphs as a decision problem

The HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on chordal bipartite graphs decision problem accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to a well-formed, feasible instance of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on chordal bipartite graphs; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
