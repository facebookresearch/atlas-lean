# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON 2-CONNECTED CUBIC PLANAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON 2-CONNECTED CUBIC PLANAR BIPARTITE GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Akiyama, T. Nishizeki and N. Saito, "NP-completeness of the Hamiltonian cycle problem for bipartite graphs", Journal of Information Processing 3(2) (1980), 73-76 (abstract: "We show that this problem is NP-complete for two classes of bipartite graphs: 2-connected cubic bipartite planar graphs; and 3-connected cubic bipartite graphs.").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
