# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON LOCALLY CONNECTED GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 7 is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON LOCALLY CONNECTED GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 7 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT.
- V. S. Gordon, Y. L. Orlovich, C. N. Potts and V. A. Strusevich, "Hamiltonian properties of locally connected graphs with bounded vertex degree", Discrete Applied Mathematics 159 (2011), 1759-1774, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2010.10.005.
