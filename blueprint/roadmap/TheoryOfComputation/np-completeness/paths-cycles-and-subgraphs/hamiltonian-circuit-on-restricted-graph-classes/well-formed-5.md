# Well-formed instances of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9

An instance $(n, E)$ is well-formed for HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON GRAPHS OF GIRTH AT LEAST 9 when it is a well-formed HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT instance ($n > 0$, the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) has girth at least $9$ (every cycle has at least $9$ edges; a graph with no cycle qualifies).
