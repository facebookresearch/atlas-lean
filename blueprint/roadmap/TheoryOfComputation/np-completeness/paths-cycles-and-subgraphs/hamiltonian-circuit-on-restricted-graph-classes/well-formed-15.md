# Well-formed HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on chordal bipartite graphs instances

An instance $(n, E)$ of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on chordal bipartite graphs is well-formed when $n > 0$, the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, and the graph of the instance is bipartite and has no induced cycle of length $6$ or more.
