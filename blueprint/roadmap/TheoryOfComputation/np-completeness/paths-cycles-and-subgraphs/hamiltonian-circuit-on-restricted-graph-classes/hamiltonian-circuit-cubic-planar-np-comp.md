# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON PLANAR CUBIC GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON PLANAR CUBIC GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
