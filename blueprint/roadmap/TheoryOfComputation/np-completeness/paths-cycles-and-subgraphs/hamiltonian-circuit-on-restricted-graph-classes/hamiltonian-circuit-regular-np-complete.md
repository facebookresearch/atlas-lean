# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON k-REGULAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

For every natural number $k \ge 4$, the decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON $k$-REGULAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Picouleau, "Complexity of the Hamiltonian cycle in regular graph problem", Theoretical Computer Science 131(2) (1994), 463-473, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)90185-6 (abstract: "for any fixed k > 3, deciding whether a k-regular graph has a hamiltonian cycle (or path) is a NP-complete problem").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, classes "4-regular" (gc_1101) and "5-regular" (gc_1104), problem Hamiltonian cycle: NP-complete, with the reference to Picouleau (1994).
