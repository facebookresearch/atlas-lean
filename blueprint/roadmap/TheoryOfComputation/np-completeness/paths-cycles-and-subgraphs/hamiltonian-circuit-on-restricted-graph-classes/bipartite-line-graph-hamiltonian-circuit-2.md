# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on line graphs of bipartite graphs is NP-complete

The HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on line graphs of bipartite graphs decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
