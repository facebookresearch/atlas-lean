# Well-formed instances of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS

An instance $(n, E)$ is well-formed for HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON BIPARTITE GRAPHS when it is a well-formed HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT instance ($n > 0$, the list $E$ has no repeated entries, and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$), and the graph of the instance (on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with $u$ and $v$ adjacent when $u \ne v$ and $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is in $E$) is bipartite.

The same test is the well-formedness condition of HAMILTONIAN PATH for bipartite graphs.
