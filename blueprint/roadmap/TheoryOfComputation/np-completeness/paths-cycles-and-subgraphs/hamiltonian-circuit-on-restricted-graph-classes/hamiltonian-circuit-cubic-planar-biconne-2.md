# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON 2-CONNECTED CUBIC PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON 2-CONNECTED CUBIC PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey, D. S. Johnson and R. E. Tarjan, "The planar Hamiltonian circuit problem is NP-complete", SIAM Journal on Computing 5(4) (1976), 704-714, doi:10.1137/0205049 (abstract: "We consider the problem of determining whether a planar, cubic, triply-connected graph G has a Hamiltonian circuit. We show that this problem is NP-complete. Hence the Hamiltonian circuit problem for this class of graphs, or any larger class containing all such graphs, is probably computationally intractable.").
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class "2-connected ∩ cubic ∩ planar" (gc_1183), problem Hamiltonian cycle: NP-complete, with the reference to Garey, Johnson and Tarjan (1976).
