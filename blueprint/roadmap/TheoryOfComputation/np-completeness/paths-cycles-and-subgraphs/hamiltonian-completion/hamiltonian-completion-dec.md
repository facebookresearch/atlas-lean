# HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION as a decision problem

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes,
under the canonical bitstring encoding of HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION instances, to an instance that
is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](hamiltonian-completion-feasible.md). It rejects
every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
