# Graph of a HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION instance

The graph of a HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION instance with one vertex for each entry of its vertex
list, numbered $0, \dots, n-1$ and edge list $E$ is the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in
which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ belongs to $E$. Loops
are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$ contribute no edge.
