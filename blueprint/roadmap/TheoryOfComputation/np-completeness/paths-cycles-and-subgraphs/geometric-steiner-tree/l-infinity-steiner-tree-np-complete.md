# GEOMETRIC STEINER TREE in the L∞ metric is NP-complete

The decision problem GEOMETRIC STEINER TREE in the L∞ metric is NP-complete: it belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND13], comment; Garey and Johnson (1977).
