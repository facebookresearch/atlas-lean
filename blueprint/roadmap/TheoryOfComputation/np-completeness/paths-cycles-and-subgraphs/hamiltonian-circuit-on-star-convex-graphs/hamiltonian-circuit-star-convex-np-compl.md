# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on star convex graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on star convex graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class `gc_1151` (star convex), problem Hamiltonian cycle: NP-complete (reference note 1655, P. Irzhavsky: reductions from HAMILTONIAN PATH on bipartite graphs).
