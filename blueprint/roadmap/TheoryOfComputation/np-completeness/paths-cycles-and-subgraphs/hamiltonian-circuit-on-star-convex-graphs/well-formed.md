# Well-formed HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on star convex graphs instances

A HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on star convex graphs instance is well-formed when a well-formed
HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT instance (at least one vertex, simple edge list on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$) whose graph is
star convex bipartite.

The same test is the well-formedness condition of HAMILTONIAN PATH on star convex bipartite graphs.
