# ODD HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX is NP-complete

ODD HOLE THROUGH A GIVEN VERTEX is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Bienstock (1991, 1992); Chudnovsky, Scott, Seymour and Spirkl (2020), Section 1.
