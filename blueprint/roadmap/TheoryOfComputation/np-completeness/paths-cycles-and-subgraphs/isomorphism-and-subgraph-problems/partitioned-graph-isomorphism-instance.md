# Instances of PARTITIONED GRAPH ISOMORPHISM

An instance of PARTITIONED GRAPH ISOMORPHISM is a quintuple $(n, E, m, F, K)$: a natural number $n$ and a list $E$ of pairs of natural numbers (the graph $G$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$), a natural number $m$ and a list $F$ of pairs of natural numbers (the graph $H$ on the vertices $0, \dots, m-1$), and a natural number $K$ (the number of parts).
