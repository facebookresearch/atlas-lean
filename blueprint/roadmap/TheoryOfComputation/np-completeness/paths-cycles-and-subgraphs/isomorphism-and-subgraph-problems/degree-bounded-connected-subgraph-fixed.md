# DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH with a fixed degree bound as a decision problem

For a fixed natural number $d$, the DEGREE-BOUNDED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH decision problem with degree bound $d$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for that degree bound; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
