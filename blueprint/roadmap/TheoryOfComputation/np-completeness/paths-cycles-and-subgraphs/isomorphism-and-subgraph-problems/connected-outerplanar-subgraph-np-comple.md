# CONNECTED OUTERPLANAR SUBGRAPH is NP-complete

The CONNECTED OUTERPLANAR SUBGRAPH decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT27] PLANAR SUBGRAPH, comment: "The related problem in which we ask if $G$ contains a connected "outerplanar" subgraph with $K$ or more edges is also NP-complete".
- M. Yannakakis, "Node- and edge-deletion NP-complete problems", Proceedings of the 10th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1978), 253-264, doi:10.1145/800133.804355 (Theorem 10 and the closing remark of Section 4).
