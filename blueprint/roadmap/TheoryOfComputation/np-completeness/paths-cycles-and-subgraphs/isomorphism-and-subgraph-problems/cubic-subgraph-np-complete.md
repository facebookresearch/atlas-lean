# CUBIC SUBGRAPH is NP-complete

The CUBIC SUBGRAPH decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT32] CUBIC SUBGRAPH (credited there to V. Chvátal, 1976); I. A. Stewart, "Deciding whether a planar graph has a cubic subgraph is NP-complete", Discrete Mathematics 126 (1994), 349-357, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(94)90277-1.
