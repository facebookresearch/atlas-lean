# Well-formed instances of PARTITIONED GRAPH ISOMORPHISM

An instance $(n, E, m, F, K)$ of PARTITIONED GRAPH ISOMORPHISM is well-formed when the list $E$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $E$ satisfies $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin E$; the list $F$ has no repeated entries and every pair $(u, v)$ in $F$ satisfies $u < m$, $v < m$, $u \ne v$ and $(v, u) \notin F$; and $K > 0$.
