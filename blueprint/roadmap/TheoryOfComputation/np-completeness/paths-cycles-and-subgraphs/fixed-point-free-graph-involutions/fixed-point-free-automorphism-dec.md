# FIXED-POINT-FREE AUTOMORPHISM as a decision problem

The decision problem FIXED-POINT-FREE AUTOMORPHISM accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, E)$, to an instance whose edge list is a simple edge list on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ and whose graph has an automorphism fixing no vertex. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
