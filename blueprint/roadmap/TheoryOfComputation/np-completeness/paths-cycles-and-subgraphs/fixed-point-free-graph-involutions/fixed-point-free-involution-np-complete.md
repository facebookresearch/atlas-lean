# FIXED-POINT-FREE AUTOMORPHISM OF ORDER 2 is NP-complete

The following decision problem is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Given a simple graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, given by a vertex count $n$ and an edge list without repeated edges (in either orientation) or loops, decide whether $G$ has an automorphism $f$ of order exactly $2$ with $f(v) \ne v$ for every vertex $v$, that is, a fixed-point-free involution of $G$ (the graph with no vertices is a no-instance).
