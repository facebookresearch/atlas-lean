# Instances of TWO-LAYER CHANNEL ROUTING

An instance of TWO-LAYER CHANNEL ROUTING is a value of type `ℕ × ℕ × List ((ℕ × ℕ) × ℕ) × List ((ℕ × ℕ) × ℕ)`: the channel width `W`, the number `m` of nets, the upper terminals `(aᵢ, W)` and the lower terminals `(bⱼ, 0)`, each given by its `x`-coordinate (as positive part, negative part) and its net label.
