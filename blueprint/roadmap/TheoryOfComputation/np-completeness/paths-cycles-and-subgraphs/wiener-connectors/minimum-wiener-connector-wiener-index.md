# Wiener index of an induced subgraph

For a graph $G$ on $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ and a set $S$ of vertices, the Wiener index of the subgraph $G[S]$ induced by $S$ is the sum, over unordered pairs $\{u, v\} \subseteq S$ of distinct vertices, of the distance between $u$ and $v$ in $G[S]$ (a pair in different components contributes $0$).
