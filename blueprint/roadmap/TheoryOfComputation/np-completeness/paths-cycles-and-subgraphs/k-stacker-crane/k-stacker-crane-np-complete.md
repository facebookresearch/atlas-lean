# K-STACKER CRANE PROBLEM is NP-complete

The decision problem K-STACKER CRANE PROBLEM is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM K-STACKER CRANE PROBLEM; Frederickson, Hecht, and Kim
(1978); Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND26].
