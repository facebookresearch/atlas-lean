# GEOMETRIC STEINER TREE is NP-complete

The decision problem GEOMETRIC STEINER TREE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has
a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND13] GEOMETRIC STEINER TREE.
- M. R. Garey, R. L. Graham, and D. S. Johnson, "The complexity of computing Steiner minimal trees", SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 32 (1977), 835-859, doi:10.1137/0132072.
