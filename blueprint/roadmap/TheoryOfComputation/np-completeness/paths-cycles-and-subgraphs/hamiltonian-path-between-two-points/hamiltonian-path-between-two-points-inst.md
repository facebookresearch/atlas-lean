# Instances of HAMILTONIAN PATH BETWEEN TWO POINTS

An instance of HAMILTONIAN PATH BETWEEN TWO POINTS consists of a natural vertex count $n$, a list of natural pairs (the edge list) and two natural labels $u$ and $v$ for the end points. The labels need not be vertices below $n$, and the instance does not require $u \ne v$. Range and the other conditions of the usual problem belong to [validation](well-formed.md).
