# KIDNEY EXCHANGE (perfect cycle cover with cycles of length at most L) is NP-complete

The decision problem KIDNEY EXCHANGE (perfect cycle cover with cycles of length at most L) is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it. Its instances have cycle-length bound $L \ge 3$: an instance with $L < 3$ is not
well-formed, and its encoding is rejected.

Source: D. J. Abraham, A. Blum and T. Sandholm, "Clearing algorithms for barter exchange markets:
enabling nationwide kidney exchanges", Proceedings of the 8th ACM Conference on Electronic
Commerce (EC 2007), 295-304, doi:10.1145/1250910.1250954, Theorem 1.
