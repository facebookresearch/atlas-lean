# INDUCED SUBGRAPH WITH PROPERTY Π as a decision problem

For a graph property $\Pi$, the decision problem INDUCED SUBGRAPH WITH PROPERTY $\Pi$ accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances,
to an instance that is [well-formed](../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/partition-into-cliques/well-formed-2.md) and [feasible for
$\Pi$](induced-subgraph-with-property-pi-feasib.md). It rejects every bitstring that does not
decode to an instance.
