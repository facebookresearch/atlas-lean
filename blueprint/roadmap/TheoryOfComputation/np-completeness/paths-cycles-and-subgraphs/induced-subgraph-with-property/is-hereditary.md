# Is Hereditary

A graph property $\Phi$ is hereditary when, whenever a graph $G$ on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ has $\Phi$, so does every induced subgraph of $G$, in every numbering of its vertices: for every one-to-one map $f$ from $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ to the vertices of $G$, the graph on $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ in which $a$ and $b$ are adjacent exactly when $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ are adjacent in $G$ has $\Phi$.
