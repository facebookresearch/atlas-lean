# Feasible instances of MINIMUM t-SPANNER

For a fixed natural number $t$, an instance $(n, E, K)$ is feasible for MINIMUM t-SPANNER when its graph $G$ has a $t$-spanner with at most $K$ edges: a subgraph $H \le G$ on the same vertices with at most $K$ edges and $d_H(u, v) \le t \cdot d_G(u, v)$ for all vertices $u, v$ (distances in the extended natural numbers).
