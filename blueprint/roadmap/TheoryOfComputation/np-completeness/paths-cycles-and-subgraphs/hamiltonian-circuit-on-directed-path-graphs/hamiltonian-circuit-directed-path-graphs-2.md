# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON DIRECTED PATH GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT ON DIRECTED PATH GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
