# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT for claw-free graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT for claw-free graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (the parent problem).
- A. A. Bertossi, "The edge Hamiltonian path problem is NP-complete", Information Processing Letters 13 (1981), 157-159, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(81)90048-X (line graphs, which are claw-free).
- C. M. H. de Figueiredo, A. A. de Melo, D. Sasaki and A. Silva, "Revising Johnson's table for the 21st century", Discrete Applied Mathematics 323 (2022), 184-200, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2021.05.021, Table 1 (HAM CIRC on CLAW-FREE: N).
