# Well-formed HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on line graphs of bipartite graphs instances

An instance $(n, E)$ of HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on line graphs of bipartite graphs is well-formed when $n > 0$, the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations, and the graph of the instance (vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, with distinct vertices adjacent when $E$ joins them in either orientation) is isomorphic to the line graph of a bipartite graph.
