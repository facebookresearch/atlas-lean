# PLANAR INTERCONNECTION ON A GRID is NP-complete

PLANAR INTERCONNECTION ON A GRID is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Johnson, NP-completeness column (1982), problem [6]; Richards (1984); Kramer and van Leeuwen (1982).
