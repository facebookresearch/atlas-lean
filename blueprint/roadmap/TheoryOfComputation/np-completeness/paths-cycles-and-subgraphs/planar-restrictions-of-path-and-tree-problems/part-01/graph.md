# Graph of an instance of PLANAR HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION

The graph of an instance of PLANAR HAMILTONIAN COMPLETION is the [graph of its edge list](../../../graph-coloring-and-partitioning/graph-3-coloring/part-01/edge-graph.md) on the vertices $0, \dots, n - 1$, where $n$ is the length of the instance's vertex list (the values in that list are not used here): distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ is a listed edge.
