# MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE FOR PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE FOR PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND2] MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE, comment on p. 206 ("Remains NP-complete ... if G is planar with no degree exceeding 4".
