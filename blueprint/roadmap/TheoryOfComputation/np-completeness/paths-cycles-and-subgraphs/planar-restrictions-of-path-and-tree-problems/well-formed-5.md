# Well-formed instances of MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE FOR PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4

A MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE instance is well-formed for MAXIMUM LEAF SPANNING TREE FOR PLANAR GRAPHS OF MAXIMUM DEGREE 4 when it is well-formed for that problem ($n > 0$ and a simple edge list on $n$ vertices), its graph is planar with every degree at most $4$, and $0 < K \le n$.
