# DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS instances, to an instance that is well-formed for DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS and feasible for DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
