# Well-formed instances of DIRECTED HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 2

A directed Hamiltonian circuit instance is well-formed for DIRECTED HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 2 when it is well-formed for that problem (at least one vertex, distinct arcs in range, no loops), its underlying graph is planar, and every vertex has in-degree and out-degree at most $2$.
