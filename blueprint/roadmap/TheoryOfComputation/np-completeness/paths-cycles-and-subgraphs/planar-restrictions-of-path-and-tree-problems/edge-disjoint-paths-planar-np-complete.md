# EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Middendorf and F. Pfeiffer, "On the complexity of the disjoint paths problem", Combinatorica 13(1) (1993), 97-107, doi:10.1007/BF01202792 (the edge-disjoint paths problem is NP-complete for planar graphs, even when the graph together with the demand edges `sᵢtᵢ` is planar).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND40] DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS (the vertex-disjoint version).
