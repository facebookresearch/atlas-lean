# EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS as a decision problem

The decision problem EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
