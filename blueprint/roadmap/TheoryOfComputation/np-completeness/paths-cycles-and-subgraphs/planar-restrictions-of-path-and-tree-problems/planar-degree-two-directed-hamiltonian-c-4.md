# DIRECTED HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 2 is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT FOR PLANAR DIGRAPHS WITH IN- AND OUT-DEGREE AT MOST 2 belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT38] DIRECTED HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT (comment: NP-complete for planar digraphs, citing Plesník's 1978 manuscript).
- J. Plesník, "The NP-completeness of the Hamiltonian cycle problem in planar digraphs with degree bound two", Information Processing Letters 8(4) (1979), 199-201, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(79)90023-1.
