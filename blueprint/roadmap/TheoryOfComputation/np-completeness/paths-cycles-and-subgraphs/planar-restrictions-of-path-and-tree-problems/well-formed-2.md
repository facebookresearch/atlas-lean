# Well-formed instances of DIRECTED STEINER TREE FOR PLANAR ACYCLIC DIGRAPHS

An instance is well-formed when the arcs are distinct and in range, the digraph is acyclic (no vertex reaches itself along one or more arcs, so there are no loops) and its underlying undirected graph is planar (straight-line drawing), the source and the distinct sinks are vertices, and $B > 0$.
