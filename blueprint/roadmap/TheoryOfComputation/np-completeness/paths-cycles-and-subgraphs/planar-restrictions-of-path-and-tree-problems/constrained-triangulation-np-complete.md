# CONSTRAINED TRIANGULATION is NP-complete

The CONSTRAINED TRIANGULATION decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND45] CONSTRAINED TRIANGULATION.
- E. L. Lloyd, "On triangulations of a set of points in the plane", Proc. 18th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 1977), 228-240, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1977.21.
