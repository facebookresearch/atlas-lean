# DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS FOR PLANAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND40] DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS, p. 217, comment ("Remains NP-complete for planar graphs [Lynch, 1974], [Lynch, 1975]").
- J. F. Lynch, "The equivalence of theorem proving and the interconnection problem", ACM SIGDA Newsletter 5(3) (1975), 31-36, doi:10.1145/1061425.1061430.
