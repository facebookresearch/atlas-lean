# Graph of a GRAPH MOTIF instance

The graph of a GRAPH MOTIF instance with $n$ vertices and edge list $E$ is the simple graph on
$\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when $(u, v)$ or $(v,
u)$ belongs to $E$. Loops are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$ contribute no
edge.
