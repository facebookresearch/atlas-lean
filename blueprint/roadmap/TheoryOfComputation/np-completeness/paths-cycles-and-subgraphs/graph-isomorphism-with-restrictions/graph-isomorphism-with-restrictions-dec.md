# GRAPH ISOMORPHISM WITH RESTRICTIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAPH ISOMORPHISM WITH RESTRICTIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when
$x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of GRAPH ISOMORPHISM WITH RESTRICTIONS
instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](graph-isomorphism-with-restrictions-feas.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
