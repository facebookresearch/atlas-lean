# GRAPH ISOMORPHISM WITH RESTRICTIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH ISOMORPHISM WITH RESTRICTIONS belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Lubiw, "Some NP-complete problems similar to graph isomorphism", SIAM Journal on Computing 10 (1981), 11-21, doi:10.1137/0210002 (original not available here).
- V. Arvind, J. Köbler, S. Kuhnert and J. Torán, "Finding small weight isomorphisms with additional constraints is fixed-parameter tractable", IPEC 2017, LIPIcs 89 (2018), 2:1-2:13, doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.IPEC.2017.2 (abstract: the problem statement quoted below, attributed to Lubiw, "In the non-parameterized setting, all these problems are NP-complete").
