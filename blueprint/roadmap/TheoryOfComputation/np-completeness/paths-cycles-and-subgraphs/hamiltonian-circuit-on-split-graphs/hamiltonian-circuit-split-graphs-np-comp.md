# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on split graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on split graphs belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT37] HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT.
- H. Müller, "Hamiltonian circuits in chordal bipartite graphs", Discrete Mathematics 156 (1996), 291-298, doi:10.1016/0012-365X(95)00057-4 (NP-complete already for strongly chordal split graphs).
