# DIRECTED DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED DISJOINT PATHS
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP reduces to it by a polynomial-time many-one
reduction.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), DISJOINT PATHS; Steven Fortune, John Hopcroft, and James Wyllie, "The directed subgraph homeomorphism problem" (1980).
