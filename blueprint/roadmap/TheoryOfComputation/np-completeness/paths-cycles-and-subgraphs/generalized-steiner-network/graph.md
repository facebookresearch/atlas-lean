# Graph of a GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instance

The graph of a GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instance with $n$ vertices and weighted edge list is
the simple graph on $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ in which distinct vertices $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when
$(u, v)$ or $(v, u)$ belongs to $E$. Loops are dropped, and pairs with an endpoint at least $n$
contribute no edge. Here $E$ is the edge list with the weights dropped.
