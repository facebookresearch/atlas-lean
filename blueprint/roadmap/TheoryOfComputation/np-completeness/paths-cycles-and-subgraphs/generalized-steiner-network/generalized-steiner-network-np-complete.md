# GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP
has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK ("Also called Minimum Survivable Network").
- K. Jain, "A factor 2 approximation algorithm for the generalized Steiner network problem", Combinatorica 21 (2001), 39-60, doi:10.1007/s004930170004 (the problem with a bound `u_e` on the number of copies of each edge `e`).
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in *Complexity of Computer Computations* (R. E. Miller and J. W. Thatcher, eds.), Plenum Press (1972), 85-103, doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9 (STEINER TREE is NP-complete).
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND12] STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS.
