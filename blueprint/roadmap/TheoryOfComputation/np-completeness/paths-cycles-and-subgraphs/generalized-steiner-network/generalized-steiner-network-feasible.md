# Feasible GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instances

A GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instance is feasible when some purchase $f$, within the capacities and of cost at most $K$, meets every requirement: for each listed $(a, b, r)$ with both $a$ and $b$ in range, there is an indexed family of $r$ paths from $a$ to $b$ in $G$, not necessarily distinct, such that every listed edge $e$ lies on at most $f(e)$ of them. Requirements with an endpoint out of range impose nothing. Two purchased copies of one edge can serve a demand of two along the same path.
