# Feasible GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instances

A GENERALIZED STEINER NETWORK instance is feasible when some `f` within the capacities and of
cost at most `K` meets every requirement: for each listed `(a, b, r)` there are `r` paths from
`a` to `b` in `G` such that every edge `e` lies on at most `f(e)` of them.
