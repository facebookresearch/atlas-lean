# MAXIMUM FIXED-LENGTH DISJOINT PATHS with fixed `K ≥ 4` is NP-complete

For every natural number $K \ge 4$, MAXIMUM FIXED-LENGTH DISJOINT PATHS with the path length
fixed to $K$ is NP-complete: the decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in
NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND42], comment; Itai, Perl and Shiloach (1982).
