# MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Li, McCormick and Simchi-Levi (1990), whose two disjoint paths may include the edge
$\{s, t\}$, as here; Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS, whose paths
each have at least one internal vertex, so the one-edge path is excluded there.
