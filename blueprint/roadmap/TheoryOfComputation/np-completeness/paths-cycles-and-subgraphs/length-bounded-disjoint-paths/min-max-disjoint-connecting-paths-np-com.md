# MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM MAXIMUM DISJOINT CONNECTING PATHS; Li, McCormick and
Simchi-Levi (1990).
