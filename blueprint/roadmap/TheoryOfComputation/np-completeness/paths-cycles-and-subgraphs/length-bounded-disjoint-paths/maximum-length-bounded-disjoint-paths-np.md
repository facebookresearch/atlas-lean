# MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND41]; Itai, Perl, and Shiloach (1982).
