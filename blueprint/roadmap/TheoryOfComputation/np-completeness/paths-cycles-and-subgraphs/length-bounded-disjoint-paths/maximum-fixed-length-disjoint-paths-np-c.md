# MAXIMUM FIXED-LENGTH DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM FIXED-LENGTH DISJOINT PATHS is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and
every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND42]; Itai, Perl and Shiloach (1982).
