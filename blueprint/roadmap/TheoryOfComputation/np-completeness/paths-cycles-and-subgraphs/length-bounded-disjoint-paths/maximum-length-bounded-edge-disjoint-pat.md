# MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS with fixed `K ≥ 5` is NP-complete

For every natural number $K \ge 5$, MAXIMUM LENGTH-BOUNDED EDGE-DISJOINT PATHS with the length
bound fixed to $K$ is NP-complete: the decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND41], comment; Itai, Perl, and Shiloach (1982).
