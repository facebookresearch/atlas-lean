# Grid Edges

The edges of the grid graph of a list of sites are the pairs $(u, v)$ of list positions with $u < v < |V|$ whose sites are adjacent in the infinite square grid $\mathbb{Z}^2$ (they differ by $1$ in exactly one coordinate). Positions, not sites, are the vertices: two positions holding the same site are not adjacent, since a site is not adjacent to itself.
