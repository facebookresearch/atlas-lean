# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN GRID GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT IN GRID GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Itai, C. H. Papadimitriou and J. L. Szwarcfiter, "Hamilton paths in grid graphs", SIAM Journal on Computing 11 (1982), 676-686, doi:10.1137/0211056.
