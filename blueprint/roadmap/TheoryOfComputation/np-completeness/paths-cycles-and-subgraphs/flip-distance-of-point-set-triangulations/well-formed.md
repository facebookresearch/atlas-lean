# Well-formed FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS instances

A FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS instance is well-formed when one coordinate pair per
point, pairwise distinct points, no three points collinear, both edge lists describe simple
graphs on the vertices $0, \dots, n-1$ (no duplicate edges in either orientation, no self-loops, endpoints in range),
`T₁` and `T₂` are triangulations of the point set, and `k` is positive.
