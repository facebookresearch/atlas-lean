# FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly
when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET
TRIANGULATIONS instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](flip-distance-point-set-triangulation-fe.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
