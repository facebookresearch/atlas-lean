# FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem FLIP DISTANCE OF POINT-SET TRIANGULATIONS belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Lubiw and V. Pathak, "Flip distance between two triangulations of a point set is NP-complete", Computational Geometry 49 (2015), 17-23, doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2014.11.001 (Theorem 2).
- A. Pilz, "Flip distance between triangulations of a planar point set is APX-hard", Computational Geometry 47 (2014), 589-604, doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2014.01.001 (Lemmas 9 and 10, point sets in general position).
