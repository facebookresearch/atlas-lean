# METRIC BOTTLENECK WANDERING SALESPERSON as a decision problem

The decision problem METRIC BOTTLENECK WANDERING SALESPERSON accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a bottleneck instance and two cities, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](metric-bottleneck-wandering-salesperson-2.md) for METRIC BOTTLENECK WANDERING SALESPERSON. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
