# Fundamental Cycle Length

The sum of $\operatorname{dist}_T(u, v) + 1$ over the edges $\{u, v\}$ of $G$ that are not edges of $T$ (each once, as $u < v$), where $\operatorname{dist}_T$ is $0$ between vertices that $T$ does not connect. When $T$ is a spanning tree of $G$, as feasibility requires, this is the total length of the fundamental cycles of $G$ with respect to $T$.
