# GRAND TOUR as a decision problem

The decision problem GRAND TOUR accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of grids of flag pairs, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](grand-tour-feasible.md) for GRAND TOUR. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
