# INDUCED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH WITH PROPERTY Π is NP-complete

Let $\Pi$ be a graph property that is
[hereditary](../induced-subgraph-with-property/is-hereditary.md) and [nontrivial on connected
graphs](is-connected-nontrivial.md), and whose [recognition
problem](../induced-subgraph-with-property/property-dec.md) is in P. Then the decision problem
[INDUCED CONNECTED SUBGRAPH WITH PROPERTY Π](induced-connected-subgraph-with-property-2.md) for
$\Pi$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction
to it.
