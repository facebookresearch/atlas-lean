# Instances of MULTIPLE CHOICE BRANCHING

An instance of MULTIPLE CHOICE BRANCHING is a triple $(n, A, K)$ of a vertex count $n$, a list $A$ of arcs, each a quadruple $(\mathit{tail}, \mathit{head}, \mathit{weight}, \mathit{class})$ of natural numbers, and a natural number $K$, the required total weight.
