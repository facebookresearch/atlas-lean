# Edge Flow

The number of unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of vertices (each counted once, as $x < y$) that are not connected in $T$ after the edge $\{u, v\}$ is removed, including pairs that were already disconnected and whether or not $\{u, v\}$ is an edge of $T$. When $T$ is a spanning tree and $\{u, v\}$ is one of its edges, this is the flow on that edge.
