# Well-formed WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY instances

An instance $(n, E)$ of WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY on the line is well formed when the endpoint pairs $(u, v)$ of the entries of $E$ are pairwise distinct, and every entry $(u, v, w)$ of $E$ has $u < n$, $v < n$, $u \ne v$ and $w > 0$, with the reversed pair $(v, u)$ not the endpoint pair of any entry of $E$.
