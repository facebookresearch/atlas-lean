# Instances of WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY on the line

An instance of WEIGHTED GRAPH EMBEDDABILITY on the line is a pair $(n, E)$ consisting of a natural number $n$ (the vertices are $0, \dots, n-1$) and a list $E$ of triples $(u, v, w)$ of natural numbers, each the edge $\{u, v\}$ with weight $w$. The pair is unrestricted: no simplicity, positivity or bound on the weights is required. The same pairs are the instances of GRAPH BALANCING with makespan two.
