# UPWARD PLANARITY TESTING is NP-complete

The following holds: the decision problem UPWARD PLANARITY TESTING belongs to the class NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Feasibility asks for a planar straight-line upward drawing; by Di Battista and Tamassia, a digraph is upward planar exactly when it has such a drawing.

## Sources

- A. Garg and R. Tamassia, "On the computational complexity of upward and rectilinear planarity testing", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 31 (2001), 601-625, doi:10.1137/S0097539794277123.
