# MINIMUM K-CONNECTED SUBGRAPH for fixed K ≥ 2 is NP-complete

For every natural number $K$ with $2 \le K$, the decision problem MINIMUM K-CONNECTED SUBGRAPH
for fixed K ≥ 2 with that parameter is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem
in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [GT31], comment (Chung and Graham, 1977).
