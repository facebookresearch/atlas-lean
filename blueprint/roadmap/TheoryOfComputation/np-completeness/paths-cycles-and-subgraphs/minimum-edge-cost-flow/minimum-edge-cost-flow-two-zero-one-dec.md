# The decision problem MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW with capacities 2 and prices 0/1

MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW with capacities $2$ and prices $0$/$1$ as a decision problem on
bitstrings: a bitstring is accepted exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring
encoding, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW. A
bitstring that does not decode to an instance is rejected.
