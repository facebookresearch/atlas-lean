# Feasible MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW instances

A MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW instance is feasible when $s$ and $t$ are vertices and there is an
integral flow $f$ from $s$ to $t$ within the capacities, conserved at every vertex other than
$s$ and $t$, whose net flow into $t$ (inflow minus outflow, computed in the reals) is at least
$R$, and such that the arcs carrying nonzero flow have total price at most $B$.
