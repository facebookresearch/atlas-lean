# MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW with capacities 2 and prices 0/1 is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM EDGE-COST FLOW with capacities 2 and prices 0/1 is NP-complete: it
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [ND32], comment (Even and Johnson, 1977, unpublished).
