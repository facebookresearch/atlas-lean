# HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on strongly chordal graphs as a decision problem

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN CIRCUIT on strongly chordal graphs accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an
instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](../undirected-hamiltonian-cycle/hamiltonian-cycle-undirected-feasible.md). It rejects
every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
