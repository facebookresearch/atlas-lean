# Well-formed instances of DEFECTIVE CONCENTRATOR

An instance $(n, A, I, O)$, a vertex count, an arc list and lists of input and output vertices, is well-formed for DEFECTIVE CONCENTRATOR when the arcs are distinct, loopless and between vertices below $n$; the directed graph is acyclic (no vertex reaches itself by a nonempty directed path); $I$ and $O$ are lists of distinct vertices below $n$ with no vertex in common; and $|O| \le |I|$.
