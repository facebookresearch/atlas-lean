# DEFECTIVE CONCENTRATOR as a decision problem

The decision problem DEFECTIVE CONCENTRATOR accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, A, I, O)$ (a vertex count, a list of arcs and two lists of vertices), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for DEFECTIVE CONCENTRATOR. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
