# Instances of CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING

An instance of CAPACITATED ARC ROUTING is a tuple $(n, E, v_0, W, B)$ of a number $n$ of vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, an edge list $E$ (an entry $(u, v, c, d)$ is the edge $\{u, v\}$ with cost $c$ and demand $d$), a depot $v_0$, a vehicle capacity $W$ and a cost bound $B$.
