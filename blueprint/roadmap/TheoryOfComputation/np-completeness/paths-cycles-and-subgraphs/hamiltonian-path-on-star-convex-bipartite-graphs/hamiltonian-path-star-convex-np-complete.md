# HAMILTONIAN PATH on star convex bipartite graphs is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH on star convex bipartite graphs belongs to NP, and every
decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Chen, Z. Lei, T. Liu, Z. Tang, C. Wang and K. Xu, "Complexity of domination, Hamiltonicity and treewidth for tree convex bipartite graphs", Journal of Combinatorial Optimization 32 (2016), 95-110, doi:10.1007/s10878-015-9917-3.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH (the unrestricted problem).
- ISGCI, *Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions*, class star convex (gc_1151), problem Hamiltonian path: NP-complete.
