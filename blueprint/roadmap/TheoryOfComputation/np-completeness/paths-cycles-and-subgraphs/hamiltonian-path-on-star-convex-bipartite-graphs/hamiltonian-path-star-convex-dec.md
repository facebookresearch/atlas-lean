# HAMILTONIAN PATH on star convex bipartite graphs as a decision problem

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH on star convex bipartite graphs accepts a bitstring $x$
exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an
instance that is [well-formed](../hamiltonian-circuit-on-star-convex-graphs/well-formed.md) and
[feasible](../undirected-hamiltonian-path/hamiltonian-path-undirected-feasible.md). It rejects
every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
