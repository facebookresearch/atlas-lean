# K-SPANNING TREE is NP-complete

The decision problem K-SPANNING TREE is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Crescenzi and Kann, MINIMUM K-SPANNING TREE; Ravi, Sundaram, Marathe, Rosenkrantz, and
Ravi (1996), Theorem 2.1.
