# Well-formed DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) instances

A DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) instance is well-formed when both
arc lists are directed graphs on their vertex sets, `G` is acyclic (a permutation of its
vertices along which every arc goes forward), and `H` is a directed tree (no antiparallel arcs,
and the underlying undirected graph is a tree).
