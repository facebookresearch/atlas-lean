# DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) as a decision problem

The decision problem DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) accepts a
bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of DIRECTED
SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](directed-subgraph-isomorphism-acyclic-tr-2.md). It
rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
