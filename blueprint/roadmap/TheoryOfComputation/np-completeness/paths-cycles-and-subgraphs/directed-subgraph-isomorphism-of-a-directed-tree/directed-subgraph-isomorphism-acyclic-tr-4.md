# DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) is NP-complete

The decision problem DIRECTED SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM (G acyclic, H a directed tree) belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT48] SUBGRAPH ISOMORPHISM, comment: "Variant for directed graphs is also NP-complete, even if G is acyclic and H is a directed tree [Aho and Sethi, 1977]" (A. V. Aho and R. Sethi, 1977, as cited there; the original was not available).
