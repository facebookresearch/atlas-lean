# MINIMUM EQUIVALENT DIGRAPH is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM EQUIVALENT
DIGRAPH belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction
to it.

## Sources

- Michael R. Garey and David S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability* (1979), MINIMUM EQUIVALENT DIGRAPH; Alfred V. Aho, Michael R. Garey, and Jeffrey D. Ullman, "The transitive reduction of a directed graph", SIAM Journal on Computing 1 (1972), 131-137 (distinguishing transitive reduction from the minimum equivalent subgraph and the hardness connection); Dennis M. Moyles and Gerald L. Thompson, "An algorithm for finding a minimum equivalent graph of a digraph", Journal of the ACM 16 (1969), 455-460.
