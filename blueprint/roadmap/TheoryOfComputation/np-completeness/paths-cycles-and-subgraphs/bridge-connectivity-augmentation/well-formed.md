# Well-formed BRIDGE-CONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION instances

An instance $(n, E, w, B)$ of BRIDGE-CONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION (the instance format of BICONNECTIVITY AUGMENTATION) is well-formed when $n \ge 1$; the edge list has no repeated edge, every edge joins two distinct vertices below $n$, and no edge appears in both orientations; $w$ has $n$ rows of $n$ entries and is symmetric; every off-diagonal weight is positive; and $B > 0$.
