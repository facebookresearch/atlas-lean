# HAMILTONIAN PATH IN 4-REGULAR GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem HAMILTONIAN PATH IN 4-REGULAR GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GT39] HAMILTONIAN PATH.
- C. Picouleau, "Complexity of the Hamiltonian cycle in regular graph problem", Theoretical Computer Science 131 (1994), 463-473, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(94)90185-6.
- ISGCI (Information System on Graph Classes and their Inclusions), class 4-regular (gc_1101), problem Hamiltonian path: NP-complete [Picouleau 1994].
