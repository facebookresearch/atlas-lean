# MINIMUM SPANNING TREE DELETING K EDGES is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM SPANNING TREE DELETING K EDGES is NP-complete: it belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Frederickson and Solis-Oba (1999); Liang (2001); Crescenzi and Kann, MAXIMUM MINIMUM
SPANNING TREE DELETING K EDGES.
