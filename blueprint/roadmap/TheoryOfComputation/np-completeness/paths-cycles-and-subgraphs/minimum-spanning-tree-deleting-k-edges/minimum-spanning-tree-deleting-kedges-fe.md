# Feasible MINIMUM SPANNING TREE DELETING K EDGES instances

Some sublist $D$ of $k$ edge records can be chosen so that, after removing every record equal to a member of $D$ (not just one occurrence), the remaining records have a minimum spanning tree of weight at least $K$. With parallel records the removal can take more than $k$ records; on a well-formed instance, which has none, it deletes exactly $k$ edges.
