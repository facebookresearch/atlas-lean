# DEGREE CONSTRAINED SPANNING TREE is NP-complete for every fixed $K \ge 2$

For every natural number $K \ge 2$, the DEGREE CONSTRAINED SPANNING TREE for fixed K ≥ 2 decision problem with degree bound $K$ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [ND1] DEGREE CONSTRAINED SPANNING TREE, p. 206 (comment: "Remains NP-complete for any fixed K ≥ 2").
