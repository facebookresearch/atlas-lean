# DEGREE CONSTRAINED SPANNING TREE for fixed K ≥ 2 as a decision problem

For a fixed natural number $K$, the DEGREE CONSTRAINED SPANNING TREE for fixed K ≥ 2 decision problem with degree bound $K$ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for that degree bound; it rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
