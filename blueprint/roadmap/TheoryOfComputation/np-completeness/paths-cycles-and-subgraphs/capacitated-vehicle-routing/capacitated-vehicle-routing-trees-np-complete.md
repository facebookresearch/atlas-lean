# CAPACITATED VEHICLE ROUTING ON TREES is NP-complete

The decision problem CAPACITATED VEHICLE ROUTING ON TREES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Labbé, G. Laporte and H. Mercure, "Capacitated vehicle routing on trees", Operations Research 39 (1991), 616-622, doi:10.1287/opre.39.4.616.
