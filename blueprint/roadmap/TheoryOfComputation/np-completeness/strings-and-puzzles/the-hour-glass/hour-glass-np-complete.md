# THE HOUR-GLASS is NP-complete

The decision problem THE HOUR-GLASS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Petersen, "The Complexity of Some Combinatorial Puzzles", arXiv:1507.08690 (2015), Theorem 2 ("Generalized Hour-Glass is complete in NP").
