# THE HOUR-GLASS as a decision problem

The decision problem THE HOUR-GLASS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the
canonical bitstring encoding of THE HOUR-GLASS instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](hour-glass-feasible.md). It rejects every bitstring
that does not decode to an instance.
