# UNSHUFFLING A SQUARE is NP-complete

The UNSHUFFLING A SQUARE decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Buss and M. Soltys, "Unshuffling a square is NP-hard", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 80(4) (2014), 766-776, doi:10.1016/j.jcss.2013.11.002, arXiv:1211.7161 (Section 1, definition of shuffle and square; Theorem 3).
