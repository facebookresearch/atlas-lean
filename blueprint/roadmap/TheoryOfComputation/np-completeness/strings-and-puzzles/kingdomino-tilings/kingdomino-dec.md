# KINGDOMINO (K-TILING) as a decision problem

The decision problem KINGDOMINO (K-TILING) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a domino list and a bound, to an instance that is feasible for KINGDOMINO (K-TILING) (every instance is well-formed). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
