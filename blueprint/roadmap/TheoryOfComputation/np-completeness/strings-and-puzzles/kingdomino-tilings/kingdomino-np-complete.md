# KINGDOMINO (K-TILING) is NP-complete

The decision problem KINGDOMINO (K-TILING) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- V.-H. Nguyen, K. Perrot and M. Vallet, "NP-completeness of the game Kingdomino", Theoretical Computer Science 822 (2020), 23-35, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2020.04.007 (arXiv:1909.02849), Section 3 (K-tiling problem) and Theorem 1.
