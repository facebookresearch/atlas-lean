# Instances of REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE FOR BOUNDED LANGUAGES

An instance is a triple of a natural alphabet size $m$ and two raw token lists (lists of natural pairs), read as the postfix encodings of $E_1$ and $E_2$. A token list may be empty or fail to parse; which expression it represents is defined only when parsing succeeds. Successful parsing, alphabet bounds and boundedness of at least one of the two languages belong to [validation](well-formed-7.md).
