# INTEGER EXPRESSION MEMBERSHIP as a decision problem

The decision problem INTEGER EXPRESSION MEMBERSHIP accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a token list and a target, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-3.md) and [feasible](integer-expression-membership-feasible.md) for INTEGER EXPRESSION MEMBERSHIP. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
