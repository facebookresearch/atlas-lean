# Feasible instances of REGULAR EXPRESSION SUBSTITUTION

An instance $(m, R, (R_1, \dots, R_k), w)$ is feasible when the token list of $R$ [parses](parse.md) to an expression, each token list $R_i$ parses to an expression, and there are $z \in L(R)$ and words $w_i \in L(R_i)$ such that [substituting](substitute.md) them in $z$ gives $w$. In the substitution, a label below $m$ survives unchanged, a label $m + i$ with $i < k$ is replaced by the chosen word $w_{i+1}$, and a label $m + i$ with $i \ge k$ (an out-of-range replacement index) contributes the empty word. So raw substitution can erase a letter: with $m = 1$, the expression `char 7`, no replacement words and empty target, the witness $z = [7]$ gives $[\,]$, whereas ordinary substitution of no variables would leave $[7]$. Ordinary alphabet substitution is the well-formed case.
