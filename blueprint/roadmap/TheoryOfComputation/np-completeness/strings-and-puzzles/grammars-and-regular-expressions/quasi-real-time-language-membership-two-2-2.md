# QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP FOR TWO GRAMMARS as a decision problem

The decision problem QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP FOR TWO GRAMMARS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(k, G_1, G_2, m, h, w)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](quasi-real-time-language-membership-two.md) for QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP FOR TWO GRAMMARS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
