# QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP is NP-complete

The decision problem QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
