# Instances given by a terminal count and two grammars

An instance is a triple of a natural number $k$ of terminals and two grammar records $G_1$ and $G_2$. The same triples are the instances of STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET and of the REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE problems: $k = 2$ is a legitimate input, and the one-letter, linearity and regularity conditions belong to the validators and theorems.
