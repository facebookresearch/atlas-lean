# RIGHT-LINEAR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEME INEQUIVALENCE as a decision problem

The decision problem RIGHT-LINEAR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEME INEQUIVALENCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of STRONG INEQUIVALENCE FOR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEMES instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-8.md) for RIGHT-LINEAR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEME INEQUIVALENCE and whose schemes are strongly inequivalent. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
