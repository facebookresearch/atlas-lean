# INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS OVER BOOLEAN VALUES is NP-complete

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS OVER BOOLEAN VALUES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO12] INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS, p. 275 (comment: remains NP-complete for `V = {0, 1}`; reference [Downey and Sethi, 1976]).
- P. J. Downey and R. Sethi, "Assignment commands and array structures", Proceedings of the 17th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (1976), 57-66, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1976.7.
