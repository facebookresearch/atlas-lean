# Context-free grammar given by grammar data

Grammar data $(n, S, P)$ denotes the context-free grammar with initial nonterminal $S$ and with one production $A \to w$ for each listed production $(A, w)$, where each symbol of the right-hand side $w$ is flagged either as a nonterminal $B$ or as a terminal $a$, a natural number. That the nonterminals lie below $n$ and the terminals below the alphabet size is required separately, by well-formedness.
