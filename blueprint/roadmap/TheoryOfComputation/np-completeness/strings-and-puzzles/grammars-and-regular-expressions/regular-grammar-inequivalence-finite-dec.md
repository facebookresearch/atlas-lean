# REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE FOR FINITE LANGUAGES as a decision problem

The decision problem REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE FOR FINITE LANGUAGES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of REYNOLDS COVERING instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-4.md) for REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE FOR FINITE LANGUAGES and whose grammars generate different languages. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
