# STAR-FREE REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE as a decision problem

The decision problem STAR-FREE REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(m, \mathit{toks}_1, \mathit{toks}_2)$ of an alphabet size and two postfix token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-10.md) and feasible for STAR-FREE REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
