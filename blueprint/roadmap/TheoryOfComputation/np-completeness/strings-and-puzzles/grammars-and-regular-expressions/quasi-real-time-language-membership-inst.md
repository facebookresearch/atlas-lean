# Instances of QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP

An instance is a septuple of a natural number $k$, three grammar records $G_1, G_2, G_3$, a natural number $m$, a list $h$ of natural numbers (the proposed letter images) and a word $w$ (a list of natural letters). The data are raw: $k = 1$, $m = 0$ and $h = [\,]$ is representable, although there is no map from a one-letter alphabet to the empty one. That $h$ is a total map from $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$ to $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$, alphabet membership and the grammar conditions belong to [validation](well-formed-2-5.md).
