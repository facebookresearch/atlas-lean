# Membership is NP-complete for some fixed ε-free TOL grammar

Some well-formed, $\varepsilon$-free TOL grammar $G$ has a membership problem that belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL19] ETOL LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP, comment ("If `G` is fixed, the problem is in NP and there exist particular grammars for which it is NP-complete, even if `G` is a "TOL grammar" (has no nonterminals) and is `ε`-free").
- J. van Leeuwen, "The membership question for ETOL-languages is polynomially complete", Information Processing Letters 3 (1975), 138-143, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(75)90027-7 (Corollary 3.2).
