# INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS OVER BOOLEAN VALUES as a decision problem

The decision problem INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS OVER BOOLEAN VALUES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4.md) for INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS OVER BOOLEAN VALUES and feasible for INEQUIVALENCE OF PROGRAMS WITH ASSIGNMENTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
