# Feasible instances of HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS

A REYNOLDS COVERING instance $(k, G_1, G_2)$ is feasible for HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS when $G_2$ is a homomorphic image of $G_1$: some map on nonterminals (identity on terminals) sends nonterminals of $G_1$ to nonterminals of $G_2$, the initial symbol to the initial symbol, every production of $G_1$ to a production of $G_2$, and every production of $G_2$ is the image of a production of $G_1$.
