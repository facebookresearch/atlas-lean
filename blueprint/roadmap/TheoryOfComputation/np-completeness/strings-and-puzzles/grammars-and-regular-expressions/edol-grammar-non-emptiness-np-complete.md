# EDOL GRAMMAR NON-EMPTINESS is NP-complete

The decision problem EDOL GRAMMAR NON-EMPTINESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL16] ETOL GRAMMAR NON-EMPTINESS, comment ("NP-complete if `G` contains just one table and exactly one production for each symbol (`G` is an "EDOL grammar")").
