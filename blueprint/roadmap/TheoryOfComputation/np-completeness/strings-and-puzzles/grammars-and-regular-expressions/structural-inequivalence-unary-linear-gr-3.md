The decision problem STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(k, G_1, G_2)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-9.md) and [feasible](structural-inequivalence-unary-linear-gr-2.md) for STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET.
