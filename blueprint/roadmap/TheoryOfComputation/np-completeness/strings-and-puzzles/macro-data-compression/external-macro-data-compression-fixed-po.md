# Instances of macro data compression with a fixed pointer cost

With a pointer cost $h \in \mathbb{N}$ fixed separately, an instance is a triple $(k, s, B)$ of a natural number $k$ (the alphabet is $\{0, \dots, k - 1\}$), a finite string $s$ of natural numbers and a bound $B \in \mathbb{N}$; it is decided as the quadruple $(k, s, h, B)$.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR22] EXTERNAL MACRO DATA COMPRESSION, p. 231, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if $h$ is any fixed integer 2 or greater".
- J. A. Storer and T. G. Szymanski, "Data compression via textual substitution", Journal of the ACM 29 (1982), 928-951, doi:10.1145/322344.322346.
