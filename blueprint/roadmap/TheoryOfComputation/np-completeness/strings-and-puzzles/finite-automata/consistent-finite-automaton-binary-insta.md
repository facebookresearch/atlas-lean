# Instances of CONSISTENT FINITE AUTOMATON OVER A BINARY ALPHABET

An instance is a triple of two lists of lists of natural numbers, the strings $P$ to accept and $N$ to reject, and a natural-number state bound $K$. The strings are meant to be over $\{0, 1\}$, but the instance type does not require it: a string with an entry $2$ still decodes, and the alphabet condition is part of well-formedness.
