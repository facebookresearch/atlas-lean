# REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA as a decision problem

The decision problem REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(n, m, \delta, q_0, F, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-3.md) and [feasible](reduction-of-incompletely-specified-auto.md) for REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
