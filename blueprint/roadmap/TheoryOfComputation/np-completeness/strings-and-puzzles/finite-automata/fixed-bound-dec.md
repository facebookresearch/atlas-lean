# REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA FOR FIXED K as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $K$. The decision problem REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA FOR FIXED K accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, m, \delta, q_0, F)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA with bound $K$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
