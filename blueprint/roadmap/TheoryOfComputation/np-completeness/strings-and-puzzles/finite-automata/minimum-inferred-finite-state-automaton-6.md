# MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON FOR NEARLY COMPLETE SAMPLES is NP-complete

For all positive natural numbers $p$ and $q$, the decision problem MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON FOR NEARLY COMPLETE SAMPLES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 267, comment to problem [AL8] MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON ("for any fixed ε > 0, the problem remains NP-complete if restricted to instances for which (S ∪ T) ⊆ Σ^(n) and |Σ^(n) - (S ∪ T)| ≤ |Σ^(n)|^ε", credited there to Angluin, 1977).
- D. Angluin, "On the complexity of minimum inference of regular sets", Information and Control 39 (1978), 337-350, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(78)90683-6.
