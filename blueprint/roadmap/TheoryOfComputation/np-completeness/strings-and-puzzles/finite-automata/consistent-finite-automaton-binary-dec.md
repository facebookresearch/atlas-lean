# CONSISTENT FINITE AUTOMATON OVER A BINARY ALPHABET as a decision problem

The decision problem CONSISTENT FINITE AUTOMATON OVER A BINARY ALPHABET accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(P, N, K)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON with alphabet $\{0, 1\}$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
