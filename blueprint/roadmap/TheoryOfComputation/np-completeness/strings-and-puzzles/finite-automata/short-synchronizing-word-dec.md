# SHORT SYNCHRONIZING WORD as a decision problem

The decision problem SHORT SYNCHRONIZING WORD accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(n, k, \delta, m)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3-5.md) and [feasible](short-synchronizing-word-feasible.md) for SHORT SYNCHRONIZING WORD. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
