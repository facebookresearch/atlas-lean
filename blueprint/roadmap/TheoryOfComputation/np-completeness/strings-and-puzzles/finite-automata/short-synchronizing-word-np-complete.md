# SHORT SYNCHRONIZING WORD is NP-complete

The decision problem SHORT SYNCHRONIZING WORD belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Eppstein, "Reset sequences for monotonic automata", SIAM Journal on Computing 19(3) (1990), 500-510, doi:10.1137/0219033, Theorem 8 (given an automaton and an integer `m`, deciding whether the automaton has a reset sequence of length at most `m` is NP-complete).
