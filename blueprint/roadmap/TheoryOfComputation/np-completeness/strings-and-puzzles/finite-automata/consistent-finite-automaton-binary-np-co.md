# CONSISTENT FINITE AUTOMATON OVER A BINARY ALPHABET is NP-complete

The decision problem CONSISTENT FINITE AUTOMATON OVER A BINARY ALPHABET belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources
