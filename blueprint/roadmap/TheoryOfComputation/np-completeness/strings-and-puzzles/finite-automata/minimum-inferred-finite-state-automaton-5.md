# MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL8] MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON.
- E. M. Gold, "Complexity of automaton identification from given data", Information and Control 37 (1978), 302-320, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(78)90562-4.
