# FINITE STATE AUTOMATON INEQUIVALENCE OVER A UNARY ALPHABET is NP-complete

The decision problem FINITE STATE AUTOMATON INEQUIVALENCE OVER A UNARY ALPHABET belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL1] FINITE STATE AUTOMATON INEQUIVALENCE, p. 265, comment: the general problem is NP-complete if `|Σ| = 1` (no separate citation; the related [AL9] comment records that REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE is NP-complete if `|Σ| = 1`, citing L. J. Stockmeyer and A. R. Meyer, "Word problems requiring exponential time (preliminary report)", STOC 1973, 1-9, doi:10.1145/800125.804029).
