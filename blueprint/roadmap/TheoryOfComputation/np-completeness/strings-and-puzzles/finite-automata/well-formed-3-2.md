# Well-formed instances of MINIMUM INFERRED FINITE STATE AUTOMATON FROM NEARLY COMPLETE SAMPLES

For natural numbers $p, q$, an instance is well-formed when every letter of every string in $S$ and $T$ is below $m$, $K > 0$, and the sample is [nearly complete](nearly-complete.md) for $(p, q)$; for $q > 0$ this is the condition for $\varepsilon = p/q$.
