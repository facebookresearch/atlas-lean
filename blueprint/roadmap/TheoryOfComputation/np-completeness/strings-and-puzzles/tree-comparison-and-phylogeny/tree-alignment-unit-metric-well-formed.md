# Well-formed instances of TREE ALIGNMENT (given phylogeny, unit metric)

An instance of TREE ALIGNMENT (given phylogeny, unit metric) is well-formed when the alphabet has at least two letters (the source's binary or larger alphabets), the parent of vertex $v + 1$ is at most $v$ (so the parent list is a rooted tree), every vertex has zero or two children (a binary phylogeny), the leaf labels name distinct vertices, a vertex is named iff it is a leaf (has no children), and the labels are strings over $Σ$.
