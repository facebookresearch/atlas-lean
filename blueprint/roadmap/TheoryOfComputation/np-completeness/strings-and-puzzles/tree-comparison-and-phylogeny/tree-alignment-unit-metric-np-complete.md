# TREE ALIGNMENT (given phylogeny, unit metric) is NP-complete

The decision problem TREE ALIGNMENT (given phylogeny, unit metric) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- I. Elias, "Settling the intractability of multiple alignment", Journal of Computational Biology 13(7) (2006), 1323-1339, doi:10.1089/cmb.2006.13.1323.
