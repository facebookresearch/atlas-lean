# COMMON EMBEDDED SUBTREE is NP-complete

The decision problem COMMON EMBEDDED SUBTREE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), problem MAXIMUM COMMON EMBEDDED SUB-TREE.
- K. Zhang and T. Jiang, "Some MAX SNP-hard results concerning unordered labeled trees", Information Processing Letters 49 (1994), 249-254, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(94)90062-0.
- P. Kilpeläinen and H. Mannila, "Ordered and unordered tree inclusion", SIAM Journal on Computing 24(2) (1995), 340-356, doi:10.1137/S0097539791218202 (unordered tree inclusion, the case $K = |T_1|$, is NP-complete).
