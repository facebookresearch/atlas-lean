# STEINER PROBLEM IN PHYLOGENY is NP-complete

The decision problem STEINER PROBLEM IN PHYLOGENY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. R. Foulds and R. L. Graham, "The Steiner problem in phylogeny is NP-complete", Advances in Applied Mathematics 3(1) (1982), 43-49, doi:10.1016/S0196-8858(82)80004-3.
- N. Misra, G. Blelloch, R. Ravi, and R. Schwartz, "Generalized Buneman pruning for inferring the most parsimonious multi-state phylogeny", *Journal of Computational Biology* 18(3) (2011), 445–457, doi:10.1089/cmb.2010.0254; preprint arXiv:0910.1830 (maximum parsimony is NP-hard [Foulds and Graham] and, for discrete characters, equivalent to finding a minimum Steiner tree displaying the input taxa).
