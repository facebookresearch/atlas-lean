# ISOMORPHIC EDGE BISECTION OF TREES as a decision problem

The decision problem ISOMORPHIC EDGE BISECTION OF TREES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for ISOMORPHIC EDGE BISECTION OF TREES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
