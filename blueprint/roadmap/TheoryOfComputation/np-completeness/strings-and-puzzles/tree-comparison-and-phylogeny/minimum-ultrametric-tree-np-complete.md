# MINIMUM ULTRAMETRIC TREE is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM ULTRAMETRIC TREE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. Y. Wu, K.-M. Chao and C. Y. Tang, "Approximation and exact algorithms for constructing minimum ultrametric trees from distance matrices", Journal of Combinatorial Optimization 3 (1999), 199-211, doi:10.1023/A:1009885610075 (the minimum ultrametric tree problem is NP-hard, also when the distance matrix is a metric).
- M. Křivánek and J. Morávek, "NP-hard problems in hierarchical-tree clustering", Acta Informatica 23 (1986), 311-323, doi:10.1007/BF00289116.
