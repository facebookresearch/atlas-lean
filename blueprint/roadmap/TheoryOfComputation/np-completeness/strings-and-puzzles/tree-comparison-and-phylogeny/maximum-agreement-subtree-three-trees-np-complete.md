# MAXIMUM AGREEMENT SUBTREE for three trees is NP-complete

The decision problem MAXIMUM AGREEMENT SUBTREE for three trees belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Amir and D. Keselman, "Maximum agreement subtree in a set of evolutionary trees: metrics and efficient algorithms", SIAM Journal on Computing 26(6) (1997), 1656-1669, doi:10.1137/S0097539794269461 (the maximum homeomorphic agreement subtree problem is NP-complete for three trees with unbounded degrees).
- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003), Section 3.5 (rooted phylogenetic trees correspond to their cluster systems, the hierarchies).
