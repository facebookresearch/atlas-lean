# COMMON SUBTREE is NP-complete

The decision problem COMMON SUBTREE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems* (web edition, 2000), entry MAXIMUM COMMON SUBTREE.
- T. Akutsu and M. M. Halldórsson, "On the approximation of largest common subtrees and largest common point sets", Theoretical Computer Science 233 (2000), 33-50, doi:10.1016/S0304-3975(97)00278-8 (conference version: Proc. ISAAC 1994, LNCS 834, 405-413, doi:10.1007/3-540-58325-4_205).
