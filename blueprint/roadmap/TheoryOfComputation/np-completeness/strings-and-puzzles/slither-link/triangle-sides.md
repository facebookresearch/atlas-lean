# Sides of a triangle in TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK

In TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK, the three sides of the triangle at hex vertex `p = (x, y)`, whose hex neighbours are `(x - 1, y)`, `(x + 1, y)` and `(x, y + 1)` or `(x, y - 1)` as `x + y` is even or odd. Subtraction is natural-number subtraction, so `a - b = 0` whenever `a ≤ b`.
