# TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK is NP-complete

The TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: integers `w, h ≥ 1` and a clue value for some of the faces (the `wh` dodecagons and the triangles between them) of the truncated hexagonal grid with `h` rows of `w` dodecagons. Question: is there a cycle `C` in the grid such that every clue face has exactly as many of its sides in `C` as its clue value?
