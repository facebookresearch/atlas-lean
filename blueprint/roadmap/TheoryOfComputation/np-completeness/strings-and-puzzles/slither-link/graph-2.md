# Truncated hexagonal grid in TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK

For an instance of TRUNCATED HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK, the grid is the simple graph on all [vertices of the truncated grid](point-2.md) in which two vertices are adjacent when they are the ends of one of the twelve [sides](dodecagon-sides.md) of a dodecagon $(c, r)$ with $c < w$ and $r < h$. Here $h$ is the number of rows of the dodecagon clue grid and $w$ is the [width](width-2.md) of its first row. Every other vertex is isolated.
