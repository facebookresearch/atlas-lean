# GENERALIZED FREECELL is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED FREECELL belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Helmert, "Complexity results for standard benchmark domains in planning", Artificial Intelligence 143(2) (2003), 219-262, doi:10.1016/S0004-3702(02)00364-8, Section 4.3, Definitions 44 (FREECELL task) and 45 (FREECELL domain) and Theorem 46 (plan existence for FREECELL is NP-complete, even for an arbitrary fixed number of free cells).
