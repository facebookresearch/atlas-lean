# Picking up a card in GENERALIZED FREECELL

Picking up card $x$ in a state (tableau $T$, free cells $F$) leaves: $F$ without $x$, when $x$ is in a free cell; $T$ without the pile $[x]$, when $x$ is the only card of its pile; or $T$ with $x$ removed from the top of its pile, when that pile has another card below $x$.
