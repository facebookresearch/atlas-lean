# Dropping a card in GENERALIZED FREECELL

Dropping card $x$ in a state (tableau $T$, free cells $F$), for tableau width $w$ and free cell count $c$, gives: $x$ added to the free cells, when fewer than $c$ are occupied; a new one-card pile $[x]$, when $T$ has fewer than $w$ piles; $x$ on top of a pile whose top card it [matches](matches.md); or the unchanged state, when no card of $T$ or $F$ of the same suit as $x$ has a lower value (the card goes to the foundations).
