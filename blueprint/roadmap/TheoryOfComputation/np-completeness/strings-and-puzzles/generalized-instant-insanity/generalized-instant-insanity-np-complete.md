# GENERALIZED INSTANT INSANITY is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED INSTANT INSANITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in
NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GP15] GENERALIZED INSTANT INSANITY.
- E. Robertson and I. Munro, "NP-completeness, puzzles and games", Utilitas Mathematica 13 (1978), 99-116.
