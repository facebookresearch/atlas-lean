# DOSUN-FUWARI is NP-complete

The decision problem DOSUN-FUWARI belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Ibusuki, "Dosun-Fuwari is NP-complete", Journal of Information Processing 26 (2018), 358-361, doi:10.2197/ipsjjip.26.358.
