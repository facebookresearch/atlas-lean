# DOSUN-FUWARI as a decision problem

The decision problem DOSUN-FUWARI accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the
canonical bitstring encoding of DOSUN-FUWARI instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](dosun-fuwari-feasible.md). It rejects every
bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
