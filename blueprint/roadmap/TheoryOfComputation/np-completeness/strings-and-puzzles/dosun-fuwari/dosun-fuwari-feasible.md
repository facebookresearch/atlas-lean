# Feasible DOSUN-FUWARI instances

A DOSUN-FUWARI instance is feasible when some placement of balloons and iron balls satisfies the
rules: objects sit in distinct room cells, each room holds exactly one balloon and exactly one
iron ball, every balloon is in the top row or right under a black cell or a balloon, and every
iron ball is in the bottom row or right over a black cell or an iron ball.
