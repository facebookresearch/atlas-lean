# CONSECUTIVE SETS is NP-complete

The CONSECUTIVE SETS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR18] CONSECUTIVE SETS.
- L. T. Kou, "Polynomial complete consecutive information retrieval problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 6 (1977), 67-75, doi:10.1137/0206004.
