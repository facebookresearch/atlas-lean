# Moves of a BUS OUT game

A move of BUS OUT either boards the first passenger onto a bus of the same color in a spot that has at least one remaining seat, removing the passenger from the queue and lowering the bus's remaining seats by one (a bus with no seats left vacates its spot); or, only when no boarding move is applicable, dispatches a bus that is still in traffic and blocked by no bus still in traffic to an empty spot, where it arrives with its full capacity of seats.
