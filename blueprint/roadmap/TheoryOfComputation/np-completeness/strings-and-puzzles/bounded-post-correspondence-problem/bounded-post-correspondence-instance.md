# Instances of BOUNDED POST CORRESPONDENCE

An instance of BOUNDED POST CORRESPONDENCE is a triple $(s, D, K)$ of a natural number $s$ (the alphabet size, letters $0, \dots, s-1$), a finite list $D = ((a_1, b_1), \dots, (a_n, b_n))$ of pairs of words over the natural numbers (the dominoes), and a natural number $K$ (the bound).
