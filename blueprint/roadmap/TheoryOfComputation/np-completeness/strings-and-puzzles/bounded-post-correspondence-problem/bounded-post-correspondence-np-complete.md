# BOUNDED POST CORRESPONDENCE is NP-complete

The BOUNDED POST CORRESPONDENCE decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR11] BOUNDED POST CORRESPONDENCE PROBLEM (reference: [Constable, Hunt, and Sahni, 1974]; generic transformation).
