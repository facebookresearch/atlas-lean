# Attacking queens

Queen $q = (\alpha, \beta)$ attacks queen $q' = (\alpha', \beta')$: they are distinct and share
a column ($\alpha = \alpha'$), a row ($\beta = \beta'$), a sum diagonal
($\alpha + \beta = \alpha' + \beta'$) or a difference diagonal
($\alpha - \beta = \alpha' - \beta'$, computed in the integers).
