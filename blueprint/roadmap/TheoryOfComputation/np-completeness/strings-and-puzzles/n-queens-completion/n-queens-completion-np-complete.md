# N-QUEENS COMPLETION is NP-complete

The decision problem N-QUEENS COMPLETION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- I. P. Gent, C. Jefferson and P. Nightingale, "Complexity of n-Queens Completion", Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research 59 (2017), 815-848, doi:10.1613/jair.5512 (Problem 2; Theorem 37).
