# EYE-WITLESS is NP-complete

The decision problem EYE-WITLESS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Tang, "On the NP-completeness of satisfying certain path and loop puzzles", arXiv:2004.12849 (2020), Sections 1.1 and 3.1.
- puzzle rules: TheGreatEscaper, "The Ludicrous Loop: over a thousand cells of circular logic", https://puzzling.stackexchange.com/q/52899/ (2017).
