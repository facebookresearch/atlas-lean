# EYE-WITLESS as a decision problem

The decision problem EYE-WITLESS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the
canonical bitstring encoding of EYE-WITLESS instances, to an instance that is
[well-formed](../choco-banana/well-formed.md) and [feasible](eye-witless-feasible.md). It rejects every
bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
