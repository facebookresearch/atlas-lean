# PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE 3D HP MODEL is NP-complete

The decision problem [PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE HP
MODEL](../protein-folding-in-the-2d-hp-model/hp-folding-dec.md) in dimension $3$ belongs to NP,
and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. Berger and T. Leighton, "Protein folding in the hydrophobic-hydrophilic (HP) model is NP-complete", Journal of Computational Biology 5 (1998), 27-40, doi:10.1089/cmb.1998.5.27.
