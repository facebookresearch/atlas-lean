# Circle cells of a Yosenabe puzzle

In a Yosenabe puzzle, the content of a cell $x = (r, c)$ is the $c$-th entry of the $r$-th row, and $(0, 0)$ when that row or entry does not exist. A cell contains a circle when the first component $k$ of its content $(k, a)$ is $1$; the circle's number is then $a$.
