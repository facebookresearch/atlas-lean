# Cells of a Yosenabe puzzle

For a Yosenabe puzzle with $m$ rows and $n$ columns, a cell is a pair $(r, c)$ with row $r \in \{0, \dots, m-1\}$ and column $c \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$, where $m$ is the number of rows and $n$ the length of the first row. The cells of a Sashigane grid are the same pairs.
