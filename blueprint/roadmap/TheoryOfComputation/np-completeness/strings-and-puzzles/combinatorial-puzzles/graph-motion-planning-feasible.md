# Feasible instances of GRAPH MOTION PLANNING

An instance of GRAPH MOTION PLANNING is feasible when some motion scheme of at most $K$ steps moves the robot from $s$ to $t$: configurations $(R k, O k)$ for $k \le τ$, $τ \le K$, starting at $(s, W)$, with $R τ = t$, each obtained from the previous one by a $Step$.
