# Feasible instances of GRAPH MOTION PLANNING

An instance of GRAPH MOTION PLANNING is feasible when some motion scheme of at most $K$ steps moves the robot from $s$ to $t$: configurations $(R_k, O_k)$ for $k \le τ$, $τ \le K$, starting with the robot at $s$ and the obstacles on the listed obstacle vertices that lie in $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$ (listed entries $\ge n$ are dropped; [well-formed](graph-motion-planning-well-formed.md) instances have none), with $R_τ = t$, each obtained from the previous one by one step (the robot moves to an adjacent vertex free of obstacles, or one obstacle moves to an adjacent vertex occupied neither by an obstacle nor by the robot).
