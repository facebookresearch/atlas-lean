# SOLVABILITY OF PEG SOLITAIRE ON GRAPHS is NP-complete

The decision problem SOLVABILITY OF PEG SOLITAIRE ON GRAPHS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- K. Ito and Y. Takenaga, "Solvability of peg solitaire on graphs is NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E106-D (2023), 1111-1116, doi:10.1587/transinf.2022EDP7092.
