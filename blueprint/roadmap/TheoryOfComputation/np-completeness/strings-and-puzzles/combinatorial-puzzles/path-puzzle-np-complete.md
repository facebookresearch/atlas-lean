# PATH PUZZLE with complete row and column information is NP-complete

The decision problem PATH PUZZLE with complete row and column information belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Bosboom, E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, A. Hesterberg, R. Kimball and J. Kopinsky, "Path puzzles: discrete tomography with a path constraint is hard", Graphs and Combinatorics 36 (2020), 251-267, doi:10.1007/s00373-019-02092-5.
