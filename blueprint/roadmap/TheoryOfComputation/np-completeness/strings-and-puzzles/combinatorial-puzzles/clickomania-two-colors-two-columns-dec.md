# CLICKOMANIA WITH TWO COLORS AND TWO COLUMNS as a decision problem

The decision problem CLICKOMANIA WITH TWO COLORS AND TWO COLUMNS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of CLICKOMANIA boards, to an instance that is well-formed for CLICKOMANIA WITH TWO COLORS AND TWO COLUMNS and feasible for CLICKOMANIA. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
