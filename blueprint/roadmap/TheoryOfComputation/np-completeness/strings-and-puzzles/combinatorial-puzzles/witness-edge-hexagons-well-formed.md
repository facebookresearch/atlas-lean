# Well-formed instances of WITNESS PUZZLES WITH EDGE HEXAGONS

An instance of WITNESS PUZZLES WITH EDGE HEXAGONS is well-formed when the grid has at least two rows and at least two columns of vertices; every row of marks has the same length and every mark is at most $3$; there are as many rows of horizontal flags as rows of vertices, each one entry shorter than a row of marks, and one row fewer of vertical flags, each as long as a row of marks; end caps occur only at boundary vertices; and there is at least one start circle and at least one end cap.
