# Instances of WITNESS PUZZLES WITH EDGE HEXAGONS

An instance of WITNESS PUZZLES WITH EDGE HEXAGONS consists of the marks of the vertices of a rectangular grid, row by row (mark $1$: a start circle; mark $2$: an end cap leaving the grid at the vertex; mark $3$: both; mark $0$: neither), the horizontal-edge flags (the flag at vertex $(r, c)$ says that the edge from $(r, c)$ to $(r, c + 1)$ carries a hexagon) and the vertical-edge flags (the flag at vertex $(r, c)$ says that the edge from $(r, c)$ to $(r + 1, c)$ carries a hexagon).
