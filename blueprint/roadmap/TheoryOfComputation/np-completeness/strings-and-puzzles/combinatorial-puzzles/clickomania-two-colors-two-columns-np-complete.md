# CLICKOMANIA WITH TWO COLORS AND TWO COLUMNS is NP-complete

The decision problem CLICKOMANIA WITH TWO COLORS AND TWO COLUMNS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Adler, E. D. Demaine, A. Hesterberg, Q. Liu and M. Rudoy, "Clickomania is hard, even with two colors and columns", in J. Beineke and J. Rosenhouse (eds.), *The Mathematics of Various Entertaining Subjects, Vol. 2*, Princeton University Press (2017), doi:10.23943/princeton/9780691171920.003.0018, Theorem 1.
