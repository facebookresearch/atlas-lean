# 2-TRP as a decision problem

The decision problem 2-TRP (the two-color Tantrix rotation puzzle) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is a well-formed 2-TRP instance (no two tiles share a position and every tile is a tile of the two-color tile set) and that is feasible for the Tantrix rotation puzzle. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
