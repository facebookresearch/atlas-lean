# EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE is NP-complete

The decision problem EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Demaine and M. L. Demaine, "Jigsaw puzzles, edge matching, and polyomino packing: connections and complexity", Graphs and Combinatorics 23 (2007), suppl. 1, 195-208, doi:10.1007/s00373-007-0713-4 (Theorem 3).
