# 1 × n EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE as a decision problem

The decision problem 1 × n EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed for 1 × n EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE and whose tiles solve the edge-matching puzzle on the $1 \times n$ board, $n$ the number of tiles. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
