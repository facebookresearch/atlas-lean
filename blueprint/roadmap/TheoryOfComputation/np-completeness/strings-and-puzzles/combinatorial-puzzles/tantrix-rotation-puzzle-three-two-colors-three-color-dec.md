# 3-TRP as a decision problem

The decision problem 3-TRP (the three-color Tantrix rotation puzzle) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is a well-formed 3-TRP instance (a Tantrix rotation puzzle instance whose tiles use only red, yellow and blue) and that is feasible for the Tantrix rotation puzzle. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
