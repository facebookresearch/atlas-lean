# 1 × n EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE is NP-complete

The decision problem 1 × n EDGE-MATCHING PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Bosboom, E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, A. Hesterberg, P. Manurangsi, and A. Yodpinyanee, "Even 1 × n edge-matching and jigsaw puzzles are really hard", Journal of Information Processing 25 (2017), 682-694, doi:10.2197/ipsjjip.25.682 (arXiv:1701.00146), Definition 3 ($h \times w$ Edge-Matching Puzzle) and Theorem 1 (gap hardness of 1 × n placement, whose yes side is a complete assembly).
- M. Ebbesen, P. Fischer, and C. Witt, "Edge-matching problems with rotations", in *Fundamentals of Computation Theory (FCT 2011)*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 6914 (2011), 114-125, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-22953-4_10 (NP-hardness for a $1 \times n$ target, as cited by Bosboom et al.).
