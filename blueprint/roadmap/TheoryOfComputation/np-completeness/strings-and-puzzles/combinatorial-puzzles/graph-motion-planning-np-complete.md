# GRAPH MOTION PLANNING is NP-complete

The decision problem GRAPH MOTION PLANNING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. H. Papadimitriou, P. Raghavan, M. Sudan and H. Tamaki, "Motion planning on a graph", in *Proceedings 35th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science*, IEEE (1994), 511-520, doi:10.1109/SFCS.1994.365740 (the problem; NP-complete even for planar graphs).
- G. Călinescu, A. Dumitrescu and J. Pach, "Reconfigurations in graphs and grids", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 22 (2008), 124-138, doi:10.1137/060652063 (Section 1 records the result: "The problem has been shown to be NP-complete even for planar graphs").
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, problem MINIMUM GRAPH MOTION PLANNING.
