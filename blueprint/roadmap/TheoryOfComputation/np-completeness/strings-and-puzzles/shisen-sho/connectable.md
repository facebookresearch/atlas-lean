# Connectable points in GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO

In GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO, the points `a` and `b` can be connected with at most three orthogonal line segments that cross no tile: there is a path `a → p₁ → p₂ → b` of axis-parallel segments (degenerate segments allowed, so one or two segments also qualify) none of whose lattice points other than `a` and `b` is occupied.
