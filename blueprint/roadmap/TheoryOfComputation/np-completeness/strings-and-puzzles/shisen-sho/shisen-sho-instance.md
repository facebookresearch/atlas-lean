# Instances of GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO

An instance of GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO is a list of lists of natural numbers: the initial layout of tile labels, row by row. The board is the rectangle of $R$ rows, the number of stored rows, and $C$ columns, the length of the first row. The stored rows need not have length $C$: positions of a shorter row beyond its end are still [cells](cells.md) and carry label $0$, and entries of a longer row beyond column $C$ take no part in the game. That every row has length $C$ is a condition of [well-formedness](well-formed.md).
