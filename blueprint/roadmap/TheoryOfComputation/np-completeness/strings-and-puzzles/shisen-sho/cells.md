# Cells of the board in GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO

For an instance of GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO with $R$ stored rows whose first row has length $C$ ($C = 0$ when there are no rows), the cells of the board are the integer points $(r, c) \in \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ with $0 \le r < R$ and $0 \le c < C$, listed row by row. They form the $R \times C$ rectangle fixed by the first row: a position of a shorter row beyond its end is still a cell (its [label](label.md) reads as $0$), and entries of a longer row beyond column $C$ are not cells.
