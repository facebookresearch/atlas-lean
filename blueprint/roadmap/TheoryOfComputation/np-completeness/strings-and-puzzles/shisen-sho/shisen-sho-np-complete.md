# GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO is NP-complete

The GENERALIZED SHISEN-SHO decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto, Y. Wada and K. Morita, "Generalized Shisen-Sho is NP-Complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E95-D(11) (2012), 2712-2715, doi:10.1587/transinf.E95.D.2712.
