# NUMERICAL TAXONOMY is NP-complete

NUMERICAL TAXONOMY is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Agarwala, Bafna, Farach, Paterson and Thorup (1999), Theorem 5.1.
