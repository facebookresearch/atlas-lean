# Well-formed instances of POSITIONAL SEQUENCING BY HYBRIDIZATION

Well-formedness of an instance of POSITIONAL SEQUENCING BY HYBRIDIZATION: `p` is positive; every string of the spectrum has length `p` and letters among `0, 1, 2, 3`, and its allowed positions are below `|S|`; and copies of the same string have the same allowed positions (each copy's positions are allowed for every other copy, so the sets are equal).
