# PERFECT PHYLOGENY is NP-complete

PERFECT PHYLOGENY is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Bodlaender, Fellows and Warnow (1992), Section 3; Steel (1992).
