# Score in PROTEIN THREADING

In PROTEIN THREADING: `f(t)`, Lathrop's equation (4): the vertex scores (1), the edge scores (2) and the loop scores (3). Loop region `i ∈ {0, …, m}` runs from the end of segment `i - 1` (from `0` when `i = 0`) to the start of segment `i` (to `n` when `i = m`). A list entry that is not present (an index beyond the end of its list) reads as $0$.
