# STAR-FREE REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem STAR-FREE REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
