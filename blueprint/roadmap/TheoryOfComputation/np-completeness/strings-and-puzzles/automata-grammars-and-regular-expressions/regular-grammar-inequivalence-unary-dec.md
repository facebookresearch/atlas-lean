# REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET as a decision problem

The decision problem REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of REYNOLDS COVERING instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](regular-grammar-inequivalence-feasible.md) for REGULAR GRAMMAR INEQUIVALENCE OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
