# REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE WITH FIXED E₂ as a decision problem

Fix an alphabet size $m$ and a regular expression $E_2$. The decision problem REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE WITH FIXED E₂ accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of postfix token lists, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2-6.md) for $m$ and [feasible](regular-expression-inequivalence-fixed-b-2.md) for $E_2$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
