# RIGHT-LINEAR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEME INEQUIVALENCE is NP-complete

The decision problem RIGHT-LINEAR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEME INEQUIVALENCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO17] STRONG INEQUIVALENCE FOR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEMES (comment: NP-complete even if one scheme trivially sets `f₀(x) = x` and the other is right linear).
- R. L. Constable, H. B. Hunt III and S. Sahni, "On the computational complexity of program scheme equivalence", SIAM Journal on Computing 9(2) (1980), 396-416, doi:10.1137/0209031.
