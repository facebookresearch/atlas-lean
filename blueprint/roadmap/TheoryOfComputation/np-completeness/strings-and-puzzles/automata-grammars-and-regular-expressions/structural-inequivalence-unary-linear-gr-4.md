# STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET is NP-complete

The decision problem STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL13] STRUCTURAL INEQUIVALENCE FOR LINEAR GRAMMARS, comment ("NP-complete if `|Σ| = 1`").
- H. B. Hunt III, D. J. Rosenkrantz and T. G. Szymanski, "On the equivalence, containment, and covering problems for the regular and context-free languages", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 12 (1976), 222-268, doi:10.1016/S0022-0000(76)80038-4.
