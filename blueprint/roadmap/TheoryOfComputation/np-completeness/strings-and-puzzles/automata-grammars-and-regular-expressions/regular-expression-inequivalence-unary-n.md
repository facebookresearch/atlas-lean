# REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET is NP-complete

The decision problem REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE OVER A ONE-LETTER ALPHABET, which accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes to a well-formed and feasible instance, belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
