# REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE FOR BOUNDED LANGUAGES is NP-complete

The decision problem REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE FOR BOUNDED LANGUAGES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), p. 267, comment to problem [AL9] REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE ("Also NP-complete if one or both of E₁ and E₂ represent bounded languages").
