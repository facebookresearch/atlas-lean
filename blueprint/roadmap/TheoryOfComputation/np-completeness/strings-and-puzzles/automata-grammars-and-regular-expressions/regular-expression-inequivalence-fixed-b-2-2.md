# REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE WITH FIXED E₂ is NP-complete

For every alphabet size $m$ and every regular expression $E_2$ over $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ representing an infinite bounded language, the decision problem REGULAR EXPRESSION INEQUIVALENCE WITH FIXED E₂ belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
