# HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS as a decision problem

The decision problem HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of REYNOLDS COVERING instances, to an instance that is well-formed for REYNOLDS COVERING and [feasible](homomorphic-image-feasible.md) for HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
