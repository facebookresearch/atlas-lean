# MINIMUM INFERRED REGULAR EXPRESSION is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM INFERRED REGULAR EXPRESSION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL10] MINIMUM INFERRED REGULAR EXPRESSION.
- D. Angluin, "On the complexity of minimum inference of regular sets", Information and Control 39 (1978), 337-350, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(78)90683-6.
