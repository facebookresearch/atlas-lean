# HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS is NP-complete

The decision problem HOMOMORPHIC IMAGE FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL11] REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS, comment ("The same results hold for the related questions of whether ... `G₂` is a "homomorphic image" of `G₁`"; reference: [Hunt and Rosenkrantz, 1977]).
- D. J. Rosenkrantz and H. B. Hunt III, "Testing for grammatical coverings", Theoretical Computer Science 38 (1985), 323-341, doi:10.1016/0304-3975(85)90226-9, Definition 2.7(1) and Theorem 3.2(1).
