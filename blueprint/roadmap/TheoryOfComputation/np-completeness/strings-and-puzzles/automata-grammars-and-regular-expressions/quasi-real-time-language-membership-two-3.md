# QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP FOR TWO GRAMMARS is NP-complete

The decision problem QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP FOR TWO GRAMMARS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL18] QUASI-REAL-TIME LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP, comment ("remains NP-complete if `L₃ = Σ*`").
- S. A. Greibach, "Jump PDA's, deterministic context-free languages, principal AFDLs and polynomial time recognition (extended abstract)", Proc. 5th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1973), 20-28, doi:10.1145/800125.804031.
