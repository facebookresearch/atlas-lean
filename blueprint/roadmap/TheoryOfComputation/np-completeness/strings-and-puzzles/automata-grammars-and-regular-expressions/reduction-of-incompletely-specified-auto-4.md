# REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA is NP-complete

For every natural number $K \ge 6$, the decision problem REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [AL7] REDUCTION OF INCOMPLETELY SPECIFIED AUTOMATA, comment: remains NP-complete for any fixed `K ≥ 6`.
- C. P. Pfleeger, "State reduction in incompletely specified finite-state machines", IEEE Transactions on Computers C-22 (1973), 1099-1102, doi:10.1109/T-C.1973.223655 (the reference Garey and Johnson give for [AL7]).
