# REGULAR EXPRESSION SUBSTITUTION is NP-complete

The decision problem REGULAR EXPRESSION SUBSTITUTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR24] REGULAR EXPRESSION SUBSTITUTION (attributed to A. V. Aho and J. D. Ullman [1977], private communication).
