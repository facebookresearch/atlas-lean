# DISTINGUISHING SUBSTRING SELECTION is NP-complete

The decision problem DISTINGUISHING SUBSTRING SELECTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lanctot, M. Li, B. Ma, S. Wang and L. Zhang, "Distinguishing string selection problems", Information and Computation 185 (2003), 41-55, doi:10.1016/S0890-5401(03)00057-9.
- J. Gramm, J. Guo and R. Niedermeier, "Parameterized intractability of distinguishing substring selection", Theory of Computing Systems 39 (2006), 545-560, doi:10.1007/s00224-004-1185-z.
