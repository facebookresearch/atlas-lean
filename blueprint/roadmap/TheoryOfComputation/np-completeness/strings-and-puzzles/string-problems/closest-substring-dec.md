# CLOSEST SUBSTRING as a decision problem

The decision problem CLOSEST SUBSTRING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(a, S, L, d)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](closest-substring-feasible.md) for CLOSEST SUBSTRING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
