# PREFIX REVERSAL DISTANCE FOR BINARY STRINGS is NP-complete

The decision problem PREFIX REVERSAL DISTANCE FOR BINARY STRINGS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Hurkens, L. van Iersel, J. Keijsper, S. Kelk, L. Stougie and J. Tromp, "Prefix reversals on binary and ternary strings", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 21(3) (2007), 592-611, doi:10.1137/060664252, Theorem 9 (the decision problem binary-PD is NP-complete, by a reduction from 3-PARTITION); preprint arXiv:math/0602456.
