# DISTINGUISHING SUBSTRING SELECTION as a decision problem

The decision problem DISTINGUISHING SUBSTRING SELECTION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of sextuples $(q, L, \mathit{bad}, \mathit{good}, k_b, k_g)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) and [feasible](distinguishing-substring-selection-feasi.md) for DISTINGUISHING SUBSTRING SELECTION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
