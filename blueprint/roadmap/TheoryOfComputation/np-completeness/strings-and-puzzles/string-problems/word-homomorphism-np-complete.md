# WORD HOMOMORPHISM is NP-complete

The decision problem WORD HOMOMORPHISM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Ehrenfeucht and G. Rozenberg, "Finding a homomorphism between two words is NP-complete", Information Processing Letters 9 (1979), 86-88, doi:10.1016/0020-0190(79)90135-2.
- M. L. Schmid, *On the membership problem for pattern languages and related topics*, PhD thesis, Loughborough University (2012) (records that NP-completeness for nonerasing substitutions, NE-pattern languages, "has been shown by Angluin and independently by Ehrenfeucht and Rozenberg").
