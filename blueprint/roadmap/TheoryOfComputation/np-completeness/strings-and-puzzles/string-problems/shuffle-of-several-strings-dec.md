# SHUFFLE OF SEVERAL STRINGS as a decision problem

The decision problem SHUFFLE OF SEVERAL STRINGS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of strings and a string, to an instance that is [feasible](shuffle-of-several-strings-feasible.md) for SHUFFLE OF SEVERAL STRINGS (no well-formedness condition is imposed). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
