# STRING-TO-STRING CORRECTION is NP-complete

The decision problem STRING-TO-STRING CORRECTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR20] STRING-TO-STRING CORRECTION.
- R. A. Wagner, "On the complexity of the extended string-to-string correction problem", Proceedings of the 7th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (1975), 218-223, doi:10.1145/800116.803771.
