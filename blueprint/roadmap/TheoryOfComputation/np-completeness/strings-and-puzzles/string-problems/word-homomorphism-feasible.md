# Feasible instances of WORD HOMOMORPHISM

An instance $(|\Delta|, u, |\Sigma|, w)$ is feasible when some nonerasing map $h$ from all natural numbers to nonempty words, such that $h(a)$ is a word over $\Sigma = \{0, \dots, |\Sigma| - 1\}$ for every letter $a < |\Delta|$, extends to a homomorphism with $h(u) = w$. Letters of $u$ outside $\Delta$ are mapped by $h$ without the alphabet condition; well-formed instances have $u$ over $\Delta$ and $w$ over $\Sigma$.
