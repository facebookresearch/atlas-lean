# GROUPING BY SWAPPING is NP-complete

The decision problem GROUPING BY SWAPPING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR21] GROUPING BY SWAPPING (attributed to T. D. Howell, "Grouping by swapping is NP-complete", unpublished manuscript (1977)).
