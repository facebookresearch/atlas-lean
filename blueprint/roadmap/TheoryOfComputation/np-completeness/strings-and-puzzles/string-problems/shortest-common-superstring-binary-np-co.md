# SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSTRING OVER A BINARY ALPHABET is NP-complete

The decision problem SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSTRING OVER A BINARY ALPHABET belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR9] SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSTRING, p. 228, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if |Σ| = 2".
- J. Gallant, D. Maier, and J. A. Storer, "On finding minimal length superstrings", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 20 (1980), 50-58, doi:10.1016/0022-0000(80)90004-5.
