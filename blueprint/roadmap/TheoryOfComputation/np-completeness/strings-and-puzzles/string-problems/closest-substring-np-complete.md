# CLOSEST SUBSTRING is NP-complete

The decision problem CLOSEST SUBSTRING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. K. Lanctot, M. Li, B. Ma, S. Wang, and L. Zhang, "Distinguishing string selection problems", Information and Computation 185 (2003), 41-55, doi:10.1016/S0890-5401(03)00057-9.
- M. Frances and A. Litman, "On covering problems of codes", Theory of Computing Systems 30 (1997), 113-119, doi:10.1007/BF02679443 (CLOSEST STRING, the special case `L = |sᵢ|`, is NP-complete over the binary alphabet).
