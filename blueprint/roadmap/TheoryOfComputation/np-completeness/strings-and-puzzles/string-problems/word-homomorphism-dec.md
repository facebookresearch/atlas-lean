# WORD HOMOMORPHISM as a decision problem

The decision problem WORD HOMOMORPHISM accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(|\Delta|, u, |\Sigma|, w)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-19.md) and [feasible](word-homomorphism-feasible.md) for WORD HOMOMORPHISM. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
