# Feasible instances of LONGEST ARC-PRESERVING COMMON SUBSEQUENCE FOR NESTED ARCS

An instance is feasible when there is an arc-preserving common subsequence of length at least $k$: order embeddings of $\{0, \dots, \ell - 1\}$, $\ell \ge k$, into the positions of $S_1$ and $S_2$ that match equal letters and map arcs of $P_1$ exactly onto arcs of $P_2$.
