# GROUPING BY SWAPPING as a decision problem

The decision problem GROUPING BY SWAPPING accepts a bitstring $z$ exactly when $z$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(s, x, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-5.md) and [feasible](grouping-by-swapping-feasible.md) for GROUPING BY SWAPPING. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
