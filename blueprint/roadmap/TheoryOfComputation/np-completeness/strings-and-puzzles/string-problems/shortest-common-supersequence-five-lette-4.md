# SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSEQUENCE OVER FIVE LETTERS is NP-complete

The decision problem SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSEQUENCE OVER FIVE LETTERS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR8] SHORTEST COMMON SUPERSEQUENCE, p. 228, comment: "Remains NP-complete even if |Σ| = 5".
- D. Maier, "The complexity of some problems on subsequences and supersequences", Journal of the ACM 25 (1978), 322-336, doi:10.1145/322063.322075.
