# SHIKAKU is NP-complete

The decision problem SHIKAKU belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- Y. Takenaga, S. Aoyagi, S. Iwata and T. Kasai, "Shikaku and ripple effect are NP-complete", Congressus Numerantium 216 (2013), 119-127, MR3220078.
- puzzle rules: Nikoli Co., Ltd., "Shikaku", https://www.nikoli.co.jp/en/puzzles/shikaku/.
