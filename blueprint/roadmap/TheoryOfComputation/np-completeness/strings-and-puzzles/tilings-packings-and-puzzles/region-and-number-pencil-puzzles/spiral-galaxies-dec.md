# SPIRAL GALAXIES (TENTAI SHOW) as a decision problem

The decision problem SPIRAL GALAXIES (TENTAI SHOW) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of Boolean grids, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SPIRAL GALAXIES (TENTAI SHOW). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
