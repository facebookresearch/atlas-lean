# TATAMIBARI is NP-complete

The decision problem TATAMIBARI belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Adler, J. Bosboom, E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, Q. C. Liu and J. Lynch, "Tatamibari is NP-complete", 10th International Conference on Fun with Algorithms (FUN 2021), LIPIcs 157 (2020), 1:1-1:24, doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.FUN.2021.1 (arXiv:2003.08331).
