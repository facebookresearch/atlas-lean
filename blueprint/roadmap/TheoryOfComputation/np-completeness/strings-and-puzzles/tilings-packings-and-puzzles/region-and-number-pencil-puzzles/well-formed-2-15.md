# Well-formed instances of SASHIGANE

An instance is well-formed when the board is given row by row, has at least one row and one column, and its rows have equal length, and every entry is empty $(0, 0)$, a white circle $(1, v)$, unnumbered when $v = 0$ and numbered $v$ otherwise, or an arrow $(2, d)$ with $d \le 3$, pointing up for $d = 0$, down for $1$, left for $2$ and right for $3$, with rows numbered downward.
