# SPIRAL GALAXIES (TENTAI SHOW) is NP-complete

The decision problem SPIRAL GALAXIES (TENTAI SHOW) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Friedman, "Spiral Galaxies Puzzles are NP-complete", manuscript, Stetson University (2002), https://erich-friedman.github.io/papers/spiral.pdf.
- E. D. Demaine, M. Löffler and C. Schmidt, "Rectangular Spiral Galaxies are still hard", Computational Geometry 110 (2023), 101949, doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2022.101949 (Section 1.1 for the definition below).
