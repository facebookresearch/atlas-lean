# HASHIWOKAKERO (BRIDGES) is NP-complete

The decision problem HASHIWOKAKERO (BRIDGES) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Andersson, "Hashiwokakero is NP-complete", Information Processing Letters 109(19) (2009), 1145-1146, doi:10.1016/j.ipl.2009.07.017.
