# OPTIMAL DELETE-FREE PLANNING is NP-complete

The decision problem OPTIMAL DELETE-FREE PLANNING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Bylander, "The computational complexity of propositional STRIPS planning", Artificial Intelligence 69 (1994), 165-204, doi:10.1016/0004-3702(94)90081-7.
- J. Hoffmann and B. Nebel, "The FF planning system: fast plan generation through heuristic search", Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research 14 (2001), 253-302, doi:10.1613/jair.855, Section 3 (STRIPS actions, relaxed planning tasks without delete lists, plans) and Section 4 (computing the optimal length of such a plan is NP-hard, Bylander 1994).
