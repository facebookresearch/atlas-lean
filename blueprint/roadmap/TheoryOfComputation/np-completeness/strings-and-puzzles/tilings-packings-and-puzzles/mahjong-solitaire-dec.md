# MAHJONG SOLITAIRE WITH PERFECT INFORMATION as a decision problem

The decision problem MAHJONG SOLITAIRE WITH PERFECT INFORMATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of tiles, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MAHJONG SOLITAIRE WITH PERFECT INFORMATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
