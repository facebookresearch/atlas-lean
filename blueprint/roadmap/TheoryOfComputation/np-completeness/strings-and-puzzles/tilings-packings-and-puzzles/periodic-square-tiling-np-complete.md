# PERIODIC SQUARE-TILING WITH BOUNDED PERIOD is NP-complete

The decision problem PERIODIC SQUARE-TILING WITH BOUNDED PERIOD belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GP13] SQUARE-TILING, comment (p. 257: "Variant in which we ask if T can be used to tile the entire plane (Z×Z) 'periodically' with period less than N is also NP-complete"), credited there to [Garey, Johnson, and Papadimitriou, 1977], listed in the book's bibliography as unpublished results.
