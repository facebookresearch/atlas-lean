# BLACK-WHITE PEBBLE GAME WITHOUT RE-PEBBLING is NP-complete

The decision problem BLACK-WHITE PEBBLE GAME WITHOUT RE-PEBBLING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
