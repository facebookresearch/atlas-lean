# Well-formed instances of RUBIK'S SQUARE

An instance is well-formed when $n \ge 1$, the face arrays are $n \times n$, the side lists have length $n$, every sticker has a colour below $6$, and the colouring is reachable by moves from one of the listed placements of the solved puzzle. Writing a placement as the colours of its top, bottom, $+x$, $-x$, $+y$ and $-y$ faces, the list is $(0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)$ and $(0, 1, 5, 4, 2, 3)$, and for $n = 1$ also $(4, 5, 2, 3, 1, 0)$, $(4, 5, 0, 1, 2, 3)$, $(3, 2, 0, 1, 4, 5)$ and $(3, 2, 5, 4, 0, 1)$.
