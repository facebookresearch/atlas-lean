# RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC) is NP-complete

The decision problem RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Demaine, S. Eisenstat and M. Rudoy, "Solving the Rubik's Cube Optimally is NP-complete", 35th Symposium on Theoretical Aspects of Computer Science (STACS 2018), LIPIcs 96 (2018), 24:1-24:13, doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2018.24, Problem 2 (the STM Rubik's Cube problem).
