# Well-formed instances of RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC)

An instance is well-formed when $n \ge 1$, there are six faces, each an $n \times n$ array of colours below $6$, and the colouring is reachable by moves from the solved cube with colours $0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$ on the faces $+x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z$.
