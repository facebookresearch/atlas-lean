# PACKING SQUARES IN A RECTILINEAR POLYGON as a decision problem

The decision problem PACKING SQUARES IN A RECTILINEAR POLYGON accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(d, P, K)$ of a size, a list of polygon cells and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for PACKING SQUARES IN A RECTILINEAR POLYGON. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
