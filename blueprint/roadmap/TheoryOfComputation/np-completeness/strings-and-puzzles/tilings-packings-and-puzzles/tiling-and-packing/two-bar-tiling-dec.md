# TILING BY 1 × m AND k × 1 BARS as a decision problem

Fix natural numbers $m$ and $k$. The decision problem TILING BY 1 × m AND k × 1 BARS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of cells, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for bars $1 \times m$ and $k \times 1$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
