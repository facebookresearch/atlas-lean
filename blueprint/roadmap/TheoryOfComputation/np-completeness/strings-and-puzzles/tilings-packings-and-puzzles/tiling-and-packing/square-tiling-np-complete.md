# SQUARE TILING is NP-complete

The decision problem SQUARE TILING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GP13] SQUARE-TILING (credited there to unpublished 1977 work of Garey, Johnson, and Papadimitriou).
- E. D. Demaine and M. L. Demaine, "Jigsaw puzzles, edge matching, and polyomino packing: connections and complexity", Graphs and Combinatorics 23 (2007), suppl. 1, 195-208, doi:10.1007/s00373-007-0713-4 (records the attribution).
