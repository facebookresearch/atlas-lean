# TILING A REGION WITH RIGHT TROMINOES as a decision problem

The decision problem TILING A REGION WITH RIGHT TROMINOES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of cells, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for TILING A REGION WITH RIGHT TROMINOES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
