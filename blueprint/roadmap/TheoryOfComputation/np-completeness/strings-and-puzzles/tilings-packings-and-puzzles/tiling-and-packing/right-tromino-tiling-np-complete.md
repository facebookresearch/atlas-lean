# TILING A REGION WITH RIGHT TROMINOES is NP-complete

The decision problem TILING A REGION WITH RIGHT TROMINOES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Moore and J. M. Robson, "Hard tiling problems with simple tiles", Discrete and Computational Geometry 26(4) (2001), 573-590, doi:10.1007/s00454-001-0047-6, Section 3 ("the existence problem is NP-complete for the set of tiles consisting only of the right tromino (with rotations allowed)"; arXiv:math/0003039).
