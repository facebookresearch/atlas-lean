# RECTANGLE TILING is NP-complete

The decision problem RECTANGLE TILING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Khanna, S. Muthukrishnan and M. Paterson, "On approximating rectangle tiling and packing", Proceedings of the Ninth Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 1998), 384-393 (Section 1.1, problem RTILE; Theorem 2.1: RTILE is NP-hard even with element weights that are non-negative integers bounded by a constant, by a reduction from PLANAR-3SAT); research report version: Department of Computer Science Research Report CS-RR-339, University of Warwick (1998).
- P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, entry MINIMUM RECTANGLE TILING.
