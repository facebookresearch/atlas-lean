# RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC) as a decision problem

The decision problem RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a side length, faces and a budget, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
