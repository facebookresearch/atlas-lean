# CHAINED BLOCK is NP-complete

The decision problem CHAINED BLOCK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Ide, "Chained Block is NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E107-D (2024), 320-324, doi:10.1587/transinf.2023FCL0001.
