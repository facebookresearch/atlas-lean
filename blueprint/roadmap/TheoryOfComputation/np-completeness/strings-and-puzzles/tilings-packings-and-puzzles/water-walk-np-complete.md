# WATER WALK is NP-complete

The decision problem WATER WALK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Eua-anant, P. Apinyanon, T. Jirachaisri, N. Ruangsuksriwong and S. Ruangwises, "NP-completeness proofs of All or Nothing, Water Walk, and Remembered Length using the T-metacell framework", arXiv:2510.21938 (2025), Section 2.2.
