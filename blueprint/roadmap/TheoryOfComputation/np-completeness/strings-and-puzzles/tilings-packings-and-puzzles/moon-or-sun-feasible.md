# Feasible instances of MOON-OR-SUN

An instance is feasible when some cycle of the grid graph (a single loop through cell centres) visits every room and, for every room, contains at most one move from a cell of another room into it; a loop lying wholly within one room makes no such move. In addition, each room gets a type, moon or sun, such that a moon cell lies on the loop exactly when its room has type moon and a sun cell exactly when its room has type sun, and every move of the loop from one room into another changes the type. A room with neither moons nor suns may take either type.
