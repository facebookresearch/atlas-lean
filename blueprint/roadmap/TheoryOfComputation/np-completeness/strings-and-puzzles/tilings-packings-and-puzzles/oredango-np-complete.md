# OREDANGO is NP-complete

The decision problem OREDANGO belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Takahata, N. Minamikawa and T. Okuno, "Computational complexity and integer programming formulation of the Oredango puzzle", arXiv:2503.10393 (2025) (Section 1, rules (a)-(d) and Theorem 1.1).
