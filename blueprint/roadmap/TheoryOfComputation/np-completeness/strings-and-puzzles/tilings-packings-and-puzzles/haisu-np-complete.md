# HAISU is NP-complete

The decision problem HAISU belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- H. Tang, "On the NP-completeness of satisfying certain path and loop puzzles", arXiv:2004.12849 (2020), Sections 1.2, 2 and 3.
- puzzle rules: TheGreatEscaper, "HAISU (Room Count): An original grid-logic challenge", Puzzling Stack Exchange, https://puzzling.stackexchange.com/q/48029/.
