# TILING BY 1 × m AND k × 1 BARS is NP-complete

For all natural numbers $m \ge 2$ and $k \ge 3$, the decision problem TILING BY 1 × m AND k × 1 BARS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Beauquier, M. Nivat, É. Rémila and M. Robson, "Tiling figures of the plane with two bars", Computational Geometry 5(1) (1995), 1-25, doi:10.1016/0925-7721(94)00015-N.
- T. Horiyama, T. Ito, K. Nakatsuka, A. Suzuki and R. Uehara, "Complexity of tiling a polygon with trominoes or bars", Discrete and Computational Geometry 58(3) (2017), 686-704, doi:10.1007/s00454-017-9884-9, Theorem 3 (2BAR-tiling).
