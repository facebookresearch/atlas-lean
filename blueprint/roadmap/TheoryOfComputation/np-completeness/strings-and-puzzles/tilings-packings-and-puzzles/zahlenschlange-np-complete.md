# ZAHLENSCHLANGE is NP-complete

The decision problem ZAHLENSCHLANGE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Kiatchaipipat and S. Ruangwises, "NP-completeness proofs of puzzles using the T-metacell framework", arXiv:2508.11570 (2025), Section 2.3.
