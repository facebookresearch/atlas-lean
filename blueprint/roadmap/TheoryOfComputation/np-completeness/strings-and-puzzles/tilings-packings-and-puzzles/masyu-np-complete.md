# MASYU is NP-complete

The decision problem MASYU belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Friedman, "Pearl puzzles are NP-complete", manuscript, Stetson University (2002), https://erich-friedman.github.io/papers/pearl/pearl.html.
- H. Tang, "A framework for loop and path puzzle satisfiability NP-hardness results", arXiv:2202.02046 (2022), Section 3.2.
