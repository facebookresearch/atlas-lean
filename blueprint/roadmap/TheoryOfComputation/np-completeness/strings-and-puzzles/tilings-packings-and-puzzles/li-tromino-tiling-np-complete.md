# TILING A POLYGON WITH L- AND I-TROMINOES is NP-complete

The decision problem TILING A POLYGON WITH L- AND I-TROMINOES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Horiyama, T. Ito, K. Nakatsuka, A. Suzuki and R. Uehara, "Complexity of tiling a polygon with trominoes or bars", Discrete and Computational Geometry 58(3) (2017), 686-704, doi:10.1007/s00454-017-9884-9, Theorem 3 (LI-tiling).
