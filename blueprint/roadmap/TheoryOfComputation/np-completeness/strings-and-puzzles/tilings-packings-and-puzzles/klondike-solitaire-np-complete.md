# KLONDIKE SOLITAIRE is NP-complete

The decision problem KLONDIKE SOLITAIRE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. Longpré and P. McKenzie, "The complexity of Solitaire", Theoretical Computer Science 410(50) (2009), 5252-5260, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2009.08.027 (Section 2.2, Definition 1, Theorem 2).
