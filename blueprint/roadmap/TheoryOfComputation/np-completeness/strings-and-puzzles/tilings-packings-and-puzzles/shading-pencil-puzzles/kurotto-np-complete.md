# KUROTTO is NP-complete

The decision problem KUROTTO belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Ibusuki, "Polynomial-time reductions from 3SAT to Kurotto and Juosan puzzles", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E103-D(3) (2020), 500-505, doi:10.1587/transinf.2019FCP0004.
