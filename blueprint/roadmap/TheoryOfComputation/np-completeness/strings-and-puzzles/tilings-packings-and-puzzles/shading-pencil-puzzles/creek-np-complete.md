# CREEK is NP-complete

The decision problem CREEK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Uejima and K. Oe, "The computational complexity of Creek puzzles on several grids", Journal of Information Processing 28 (2020), 911-918, doi:10.2197/ipsjjip.28.911, Theorem 1.
