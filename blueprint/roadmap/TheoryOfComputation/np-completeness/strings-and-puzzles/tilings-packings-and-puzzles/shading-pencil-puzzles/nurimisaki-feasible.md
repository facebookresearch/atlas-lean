# Feasible instances of NURIMISAKI

An instance is feasible when the cells can be filled black or white so that the white cells with exactly one white neighbor (promontories) are exactly the circled cells, each number $k$ means that the promontory and the next $k - 1$ cells in the direction of its white neighbour are white and the cell after them is black or outside the grid, the white cells are connected, and no $2 \times 2$ square is all black or all white.
