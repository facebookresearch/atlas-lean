# YIN-YANG is NP-complete

The decision problem YIN-YANG belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Demaine, J. Lynch, M. Rudoy and Y. Uno, "Yin-Yang puzzles are NP-complete", Proceedings of the 33rd Canadian Conference on Computational Geometry (CCCG 2021), arXiv:2106.15585.
