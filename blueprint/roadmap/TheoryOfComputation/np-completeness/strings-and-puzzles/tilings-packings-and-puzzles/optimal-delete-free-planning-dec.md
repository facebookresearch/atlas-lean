# OPTIMAL DELETE-FREE PLANNING as a decision problem

The decision problem OPTIMAL DELETE-FREE PLANNING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (an action list, an initial state, goals and a bound), to an instance that is feasible for OPTIMAL DELETE-FREE PLANNING (no well-formedness condition is imposed). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
