# MAHJONG SOLITAIRE WITH PERFECT INFORMATION is NP-complete

The decision problem MAHJONG SOLITAIRE WITH PERFECT INFORMATION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. de Bondt, "Solving Mahjong Solitaire boards with peeking", arXiv:1203.6559 (2012), Theorems 2 and 3, https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6559 (Theorem 2 is credited there to D. Eppstein, "Computational complexity of games and puzzles", entry Shanghai, https://ics.uci.edu/~eppstein/cgt/hard.html).
