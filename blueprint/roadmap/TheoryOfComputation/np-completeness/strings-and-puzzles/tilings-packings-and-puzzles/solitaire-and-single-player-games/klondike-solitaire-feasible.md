# Feasible instances of KLONDIKE SOLITAIRE

An instance is feasible when thoughtful Klondike (all cards visible) can be won: some sequence of moves leads from the initial configuration (the top card of each build stack face up and the others face down, the suit stacks and the talon empty) to a configuration in which every suit stack holds $n$ cards. A build stack accepts a card, or a block whose bottom card is $c$, when it is empty and $c$ is a King (rank $n$), or when its top card is one rank above $c$ and of the other color. A suit stack accepts the card of its suit one rank above its top card, an Ace when it is empty. A move is one of the following: (1) move all face-up cards of a build stack together, in order, onto another build stack that accepts the block; (2) move the top face-up card of a build stack onto its suit stack; (3) move the top card of a suit stack onto a build stack that accepts it; (4) if the pile is nonempty, deal its top three cards one by one onto the talon, or all of them if fewer than three remain; if the pile is empty, turn the talon over into the pile in one move, its top card becoming the bottom of the pile; (5) move the top card of the talon onto a build stack that accepts it or onto its suit stack. Pile cards are played only as the top card of the talon. When a build stack loses its last face-up card, its top face-down card, if any, is turned face up.
