# GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and Y. Matsui, "Generalized Pyramid is NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E96-D(11) (2013), 2462-2465, doi:10.1587/transinf.E96.D.2462.
