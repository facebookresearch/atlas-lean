# PEG SOLITAIRE is NP-complete

The decision problem PEG SOLITAIRE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Uehara and S. Iwata, "Generalized Hi-Q is NP-complete", Transactions of the IEICE E73(2) (1990), 270-273 (the result is recorded by the next reference).
- E. D. Demaine and R. A. Hearn, "Playing games with algorithms: algorithmic combinatorial game theory", in Games of No Chance 3, MSRI Publications 56, Cambridge University Press (2009), 3-56, Section 5.10, arXiv:cs/0106019.
