# GENERALIZED CALCULATION SOLITAIRE is NP-complete

The decision problem GENERALIZED CALCULATION SOLITAIRE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Ide, "Calculation Solitaire is NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E106-D (2023), 328-332, doi:10.1587/transinf.2022FCL0002, Theorem 1.
