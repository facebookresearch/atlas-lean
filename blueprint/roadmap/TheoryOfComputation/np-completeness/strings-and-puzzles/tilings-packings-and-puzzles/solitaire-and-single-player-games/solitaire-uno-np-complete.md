# SOLITAIRE UNO is NP-complete

The decision problem SOLITAIRE UNO belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, N. J. A. Harvey, R. Uehara, T. Uno and Y. Uno, "UNO is hard, even for a single player", Theoretical Computer Science 521 (2014), 51-61, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2013.11.023, Theorem 3 (Uno-1); arXiv:1003.2851.
