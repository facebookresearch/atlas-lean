# Feasible instances of GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE

An instance is feasible when some sequence of moves leads from the full pyramid with no stock card used to the position in which the pyramid is empty and every stock card has been removed. Ranks $a$ and $b$ match when $a + b = k + 1$ for even $k$ and $a + b = k$ for odd $k$. A pyramid card is exposed when it lies in the first layer or neither of the two cards covering it remains. Every move needs a nonempty stock, and a move is one of the following: remove two distinct exposed pyramid cards whose ranks match; for odd $k$, remove an exposed pyramid card of rank $k$ alone; remove an exposed pyramid card together with the top stock card when their ranks match; or discard the top stock card, which is allowed whenever the stock is nonempty. Since no move is possible once the stock is used up, the pyramid must be empty by the time the last stock card is removed.
