# RECTANGULAR SPIRAL GALAXIES is NP-complete

The decision problem RECTANGULAR SPIRAL GALAXIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. D. Demaine, M. Löffler and C. Schmidt, "Rectangular Spiral Galaxies are still hard", Computational Geometry 110 (2023), 101949, doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2022.101949, Section 1 (Rectangular Galaxies) and Theorem 2.
