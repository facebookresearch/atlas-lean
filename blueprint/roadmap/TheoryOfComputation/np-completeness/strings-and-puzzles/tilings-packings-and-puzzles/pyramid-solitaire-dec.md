# GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE as a decision problem

The decision problem GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a rank count, pyramid layers and a stock, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for GENERALIZED PYRAMID SOLITAIRE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
