# Instances of RUBIK'S CUBE (n × n × n, SLICE TURN METRIC)

An instance is a triple of a side length $n$, the sticker colours of the six faces, and a move budget $k$. The faces are listed as $+x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z$, and entry $(u, v)$ of the face with normal axis $a$ is the sticker whose cubie has coordinate $u$ along axis $a + 1$ and $v$ along axis $a + 2$, with axes numbered mod $3$.
