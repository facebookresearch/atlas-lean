# MASYU is NP-complete

The decision problem MASYU belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- MIT Hardness Group, J. Brunner, L. Chung, E. D. Demaine, D. Hendrickson and A. Tockman, "ASP-Completeness of Hamiltonicity in Grid Graphs, with Applications to Loop Puzzles", Fun with Algorithms (FUN 2024), LIPIcs, doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.FUN.2024.23, Section 1 (an ASP-complete problem has an NP-complete decision version) and Section 6.3.1 (Masyu is ASP-complete).
