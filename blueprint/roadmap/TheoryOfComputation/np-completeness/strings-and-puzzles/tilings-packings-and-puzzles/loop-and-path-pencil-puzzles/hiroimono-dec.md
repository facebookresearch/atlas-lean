# HIROIMONO (GOISHI HIROI) as a decision problem

The decision problem HIROIMONO (GOISHI HIROI) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of points, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for HIROIMONO (GOISHI HIROI). It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
