# Feasible instances of NAGARERU

An instance is feasible when some directed loop through white cells passes straight through every white arrow cell in the arrows' direction, never advances against the wind (no move into or out of a windy cell goes against its wind), where wind blows from each black arrow cell in its arrow's direction through the following cells up to the next black cell or the border, and when entering a cell with crosswind is carried downwind at least one cell without hitting the border or a black cell.
