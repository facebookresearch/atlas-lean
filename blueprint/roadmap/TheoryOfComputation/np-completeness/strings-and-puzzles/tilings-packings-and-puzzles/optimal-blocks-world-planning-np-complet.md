# OPTIMAL BLOCKS-WORLD PLANNING is NP-complete

The decision problem OPTIMAL BLOCKS-WORLD PLANNING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- N. Gupta and D. S. Nau, "On the complexity of blocks-world planning", Artificial Intelligence 56 (1992), 223-254, doi:10.1016/0004-3702(92)90028-V (Section 1: the Elementary Blocks World; Section 5: PBW PLAN, the special case with a completely specified goal state; Section 7, results 2 and 5: deciding whether there is a plan of length `L` or less is NP-complete, also when the goal state is completely specified).
