# Feasible MAKARO instances

A MAKARO instance is feasible when some assignment $f$ of natural numbers to the cells satisfies
the rules: every room cell gets a number between $1$ and the size of its room; the numbers
within a room are distinct; orthogonally adjacent cells of different rooms get different
numbers; and each black cell's arrow points at an adjacent room cell whose number is larger than
the numbers of all other room cells adjacent to that black cell.
