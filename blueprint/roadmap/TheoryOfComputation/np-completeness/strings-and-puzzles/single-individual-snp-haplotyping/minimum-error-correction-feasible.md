# Feasible MINIMUM ERROR CORRECTION (single individual SNP haplotyping) instances

There are haplotypes $H_1, H_2 \in \{0, 1\}^m$ and an assignment of the rows to them whose total [mismatch count](mismatches.md) is at most $K$. For a [well-formed](well-formed.md) matrix, with entries $0$, $1$ or $2$, this says that at most $K$ entry flips make the matrix feasible.
