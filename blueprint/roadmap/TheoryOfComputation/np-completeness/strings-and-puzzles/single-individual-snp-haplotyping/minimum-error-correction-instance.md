# Instances of MINIMUM ERROR CORRECTION (single individual SNP haplotyping)

An instance: the number $m$ of columns (SNP sites), the rows (fragments) of the SNP matrix as lists of natural numbers, and the bound $K$. The intended entries are $0$, $1$, or $2$ for a hole; rows of length $m$ with entries in that alphabet are what [well-formedness](well-formed.md) requires.
