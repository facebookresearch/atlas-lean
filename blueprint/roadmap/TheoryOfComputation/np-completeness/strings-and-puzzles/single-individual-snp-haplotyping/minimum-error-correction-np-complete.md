# MINIMUM ERROR CORRECTION (single individual SNP haplotyping) is NP-complete

The decision problem MINIMUM ERROR CORRECTION (single individual SNP haplotyping) is
NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one
reduction to it.

Source: Cilibrasi, van Iersel, Kelk and Tromp (2007), Section II.
