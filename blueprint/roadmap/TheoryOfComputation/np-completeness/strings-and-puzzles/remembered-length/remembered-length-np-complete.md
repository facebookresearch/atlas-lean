# REMEMBERED LENGTH is NP-complete

REMEMBERED LENGTH is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Eua-anant, Apinyanon, Jirachaisri, Ruangsuksriwong and Ruangwises (2025).
