# Instances of INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING

An instance of INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING consists of a number $m$ of sites and a list of genotypes, each a list of natural numbers, where $3$ stands for the missing entry `?`. The raw genotypes may have any length and any entries; that each has $m$ entries in $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ is checked separately by [well-formedness](well-formed.md).
