# Feasible INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING instances

An INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING instance is feasible when some completion $c$ of the genotypes has an expansion $H$ (two haplotypes $H(j, \mathrm{false})$ and $H(j, \mathrm{true})$ explaining each completed genotype $c_j$) that admits an unrooted binary perfect phylogeny on the indexed haplotypes, not a rooted one.
