# INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING as a decision problem

The decision problem INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY HAPLOTYPING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly
when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of INCOMPLETE PERFECT PHYLOGENY
HAPLOTYPING instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and
[feasible](incomplete-perfect-phylogeny-haplotyping-2.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
