# Instances of HAPLOTYPE INFERENCE BY PURE PARSIMONY

An instance of HAPLOTYPE INFERENCE BY PURE PARSIMONY consists of a declared natural number $m$ of sites, an arbitrary list of rows of natural numbers (the genotypes in the usual reading) and a natural bound $K$: $(1, [[3, 9]], 1)$ decodes. Ternary genotypes of length $m$ are the case accepted by [validation](../single-individual-snp-haplotyping/well-formed.md).
