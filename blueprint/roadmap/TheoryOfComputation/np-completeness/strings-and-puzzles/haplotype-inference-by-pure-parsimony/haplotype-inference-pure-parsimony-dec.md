# HAPLOTYPE INFERENCE BY PURE PARSIMONY as a decision problem

The decision problem HAPLOTYPE INFERENCE BY PURE PARSIMONY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when
$x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of HAPLOTYPE INFERENCE BY PURE PARSIMONY
instances, to an instance that is [well-formed](../single-individual-snp-haplotyping/well-formed.md) and
[feasible](haplotype-inference-pure-parsimony-feasi.md). It rejects every bitstring that does
not decode to an instance.
