# ALL OR NOTHING is NP-complete

The ALL OR NOTHING decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Eua-anant, P. Apinyanon, T. Jirachaisri, N. Ruangsuksriwong and S. Ruangwises, "NP-Completeness Proofs of All or Nothing, Water Walk, and Remembered Length Using the T-Metacell Framework", arXiv:2510.21938 (2025), Section 2.1.
