# Loops satisfying the ALL OR NOTHING rules

A closed walk $c$ in the grid graph satisfies the ALL OR NOTHING rules when (1) if it visits one cell of a region it visits every cell of that region; (2) for every region $k < R$, at most one step of $c$ enters region $k$ from a cell of another region; and (3) for every two adjacent cells in different regions, $c$ visits at least one of them.
