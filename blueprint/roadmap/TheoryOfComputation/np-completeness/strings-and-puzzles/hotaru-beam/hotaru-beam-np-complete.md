# HOTARU BEAM is NP-complete

The decision problem HOTARU BEAM belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- T. Otsuji, P. Fulla and T. Fukunaga, "NP-completeness and physical zero-knowledge proof of Hotaru Beam", arXiv:2603.01393 (2026) (Section 2: the rules; Theorem 1).
