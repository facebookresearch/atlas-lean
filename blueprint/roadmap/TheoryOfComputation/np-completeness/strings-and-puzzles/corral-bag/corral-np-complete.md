# CORRAL (BAG) is NP-complete

The CORRAL (BAG) decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Friedman, "Corral puzzles are NP-complete", manuscript, Stetson University (2002), https://erich-friedman.github.io/papers/corral.pdf.
