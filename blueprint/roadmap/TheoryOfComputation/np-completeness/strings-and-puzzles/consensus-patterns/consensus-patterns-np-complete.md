# CONSENSUS PATTERNS is NP-complete

The CONSENSUS PATTERNS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Li, B. Ma and L. Wang, "Finding similar regions in many sequences", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 65 (2002), 73-96, doi:10.1006/jcss.2002.1823 (Theorem 1: Consensus Patterns is NP-hard over an alphabet of size four).
