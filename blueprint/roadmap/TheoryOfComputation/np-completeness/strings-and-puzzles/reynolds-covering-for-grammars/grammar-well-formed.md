# Grammar well formed in REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS

In REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS: a grammar is well-formed over `k` terminals when its initial symbol is one of its `n` nonterminals and every production has its left-hand side among the nonterminals, and every right-hand side symbol is a nonterminal below `n` or a terminal below `k`.
