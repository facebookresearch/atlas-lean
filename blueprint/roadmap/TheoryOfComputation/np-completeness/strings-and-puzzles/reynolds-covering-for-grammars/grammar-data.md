# Grammar data in REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS

In REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS: a context-free grammar as data: the number `n` of nonterminals (they are `0, ..., n - 1`), the initial nonterminal, and the productions. A production `(A, w)` has left-hand side `A` and right-hand side `w`, in which `(false, B)` is nonterminal `B` and `(true, a)` is terminal `a`.
