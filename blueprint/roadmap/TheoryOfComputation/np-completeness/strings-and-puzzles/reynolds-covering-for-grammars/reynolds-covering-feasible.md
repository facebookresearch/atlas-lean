# Feasible instances of REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS

In REYNOLDS COVERING FOR CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMARS: `G₂` Reynolds covers `G₁`: some map `f` on nonterminals (extended by the identity on terminals) sends every nonterminal of `G₁` to a nonterminal of `G₂` and the initial symbol of `G₁` to that of `G₂`, and sends every production of `G₁` to a production of `G₂`.
