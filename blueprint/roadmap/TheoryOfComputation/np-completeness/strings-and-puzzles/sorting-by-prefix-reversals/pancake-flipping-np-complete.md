# SORTING BY PREFIX REVERSALS (PANCAKE FLIPPING) is NP-complete

SORTING BY PREFIX REVERSALS (PANCAKE FLIPPING) is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Bulteau, Fertin and Rusu (2015), Theorem 2.
