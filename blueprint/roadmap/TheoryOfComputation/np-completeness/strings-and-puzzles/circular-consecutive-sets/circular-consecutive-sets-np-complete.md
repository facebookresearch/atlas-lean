# CIRCULAR CONSECUTIVE SETS is NP-complete

The CIRCULAR CONSECUTIVE SETS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR18] CONSECUTIVE SETS, p. 230, comment ("The variant in which we ask only that the elements of each Σᵢ occur in a consecutive block of |Σᵢ| symbols of the string ww (i.e., we allow blocks that circulate from the end of w back to its beginning) is also NP-complete [Booth, 1975]").
- K. S. Booth, *PQ-Tree Algorithms*, Ph.D. thesis, University of California, Berkeley (1975).
