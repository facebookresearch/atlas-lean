# DOMINO TATAMI COVERING as a decision problem

The decision problem DOMINO TATAMI COVERING accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes,
under the canonical bitstring encoding of DOMINO TATAMI COVERING instances, to an instance that
is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](domino-tatami-covering-feasible.md). It rejects
every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
