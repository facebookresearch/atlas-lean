# DOMINO TATAMI COVERING is NP-complete

The decision problem DOMINO TATAMI COVERING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has
a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Erickson and F. Ruskey, "Domino tatami covering is NP-complete", Combinatorial Algorithms (IWOCA 2013), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 8288 (2013), 140-149, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-45278-9_13 (arXiv:1305.6669), Definition 1.
