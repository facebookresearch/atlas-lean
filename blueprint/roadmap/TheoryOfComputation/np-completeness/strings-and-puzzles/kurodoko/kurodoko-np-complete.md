# KURODOKO (KUROMASU) is NP-complete

The decision problem KURODOKO (KUROMASU) is NP-complete: it belongs to NP, and every decision
problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Source: Kölker (2012), Theorem 2 (membership, with `w` and `h` in binary) and Theorem 3
(hardness: a reduction from one-in-three SAT).
