# Cell contents of a Yosenabe puzzle

A Yosenabe puzzle is given as a list of rows, each a list of cell contents $(k, a)$ of natural numbers: $k = 0$ is an empty white cell, $k = 1$ a white cell with a circle numbered $a$, and $k = 2$ a gray cell, labelled $a$ when $a \ne 0$. The content of a cell $x = (r, c)$ is the $c$-th entry of the $r$-th row, and $(0, 0)$ (an empty white cell) when that row or entry does not exist. The same function on the same lists of pairs gives the whole cell pair of a ZEIGER, Double Choco or Sashigane grid.
