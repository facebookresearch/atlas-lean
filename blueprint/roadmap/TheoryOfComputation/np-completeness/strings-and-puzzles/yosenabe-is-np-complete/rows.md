# Number of rows of a Yosenabe puzzle

A Yosenabe puzzle is given as a list of rows, each a list of cell contents $(k, a)$ of natural numbers: $k = 0$ is an empty white cell, $k = 1$ a white cell with a circle numbered $a$, and $k = 2$ a gray cell, labelled $a$ when $a \ne 0$. Its number of rows is the length of this list. The same function on the same lists of pairs gives the number of rows of a ZEIGER, Double Choco, Ripple Effect or Sashigane grid; only the meaning of the pairs differs.
