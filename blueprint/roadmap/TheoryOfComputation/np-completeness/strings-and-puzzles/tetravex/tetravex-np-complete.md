# TETRAVEX is NP-complete

The TETRAVEX decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a side length `n` and `n²` unit square tiles, each edge of which is labelled with a number. Question: can each tile be placed within an `n × n` square, every tile used exactly once and none rotated, such that all neighbouring edges are labelled with an identical number?
