# CHOCO BANANA is NP-complete

The CHOCO BANANA decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Tokunaga, "Choco Banana is NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Fundamentals of Electronics, Communications and Computer Sciences E107-A(9) (2024), 1488-1491, doi:10.1587/transfun.2023DML0001.
