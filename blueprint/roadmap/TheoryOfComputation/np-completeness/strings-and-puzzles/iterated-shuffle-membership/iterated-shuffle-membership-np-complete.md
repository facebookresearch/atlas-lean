# ITERATED SHUFFLE MEMBERSHIP is NP-complete

The ITERATED SHUFFLE MEMBERSHIP decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. K. Warmuth and D. Haussler, "On the complexity of iterated shuffle", Journal of Computer and System Sciences 28 (1984), 345-358, doi:10.1016/0022-0000(84)90018-7 (as recorded by M. Domaratzki, PhD thesis, Chapter 3, Theorem 3.2.1: "Let Σ = {a, b, c}. Given words u, v ∈ Σ*, it is NP-complete to determine whether u ∈ (⧢)*(v)").
