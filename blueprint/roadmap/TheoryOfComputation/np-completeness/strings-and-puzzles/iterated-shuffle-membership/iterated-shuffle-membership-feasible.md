# Feasible ITERATED SHUFFLE MEMBERSHIP instances

An instance $(w, u)$ of ITERATED SHUFFLE MEMBERSHIP is feasible when $w$ belongs to the iterated shuffle of $u$: for some $m \ge 0$, $w$ is obtained by interleaving the letters of $m$ copies of $u$, keeping the letters of each copy in order (for $m = 0$ the shuffle is $\{\varepsilon\}$).
