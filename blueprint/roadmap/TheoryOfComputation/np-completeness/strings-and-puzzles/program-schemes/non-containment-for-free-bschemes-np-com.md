# NON-CONTAINMENT FOR FREE B-SCHEMES is NP-complete

NON-CONTAINMENT FOR FREE B-SCHEMES is NP-complete: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the bitstrings that decode to a well-formed instance with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [PO18]; Fortune, Hopcroft, and Schmidt (1978).
