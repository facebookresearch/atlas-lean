# NON-FREEDOM FOR LOOP-FREE PROGRAM SCHEMES is NP-complete with two variables

NON-FREEDOM FOR LOOP-FREE PROGRAM SCHEMES is NP-complete with two variables: the decision problem over bitstrings that accepts exactly the encodings of well-formed instances with a solution lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it. Source: Garey and Johnson (1979), [PO19]; Constable, Hunt, and Sahni (1974; journal version 1980).
