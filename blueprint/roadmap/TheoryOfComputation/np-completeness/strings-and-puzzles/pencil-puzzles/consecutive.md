# Consecutive in NONDANGO

In NONDANGO: the cells `p`, `q`, `s`, in this order, are three consecutive cells along one of four directed offsets: one column to the right, one row down, one row down and one column right, or one row down and one column left. So `q = p + Δ` and `s = q + Δ` for one of `Δ = (0, 1)`, `(1, 0)`, `(1, 1)`, `(1, -1)` (row, column); the same three cells listed in the opposite order do not qualify.
