# Well-formed SINGLE-LETTER CONTEXT-FREE LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP instances

An instance $(G, K)$ of SINGLE-LETTER CONTEXT-FREE LANGUAGE MEMBERSHIP is well-formed when the initial symbol and every left-hand side of $G$ are among its $n$ nonterminals, every right-hand side symbol is a nonterminal below $n$ or the single terminal $0$, and $K > 0$.
