# Derivability in the Lambek calculus

Derivability of sequents $\Gamma \to A$ in the Lambek calculus $L$, with $\Gamma$ a finite sequence of types, is generated by the axioms $A \to A$; cut (from $\Phi \to B$ and $\Gamma\, B\, \Delta \to A$ infer $\Gamma\, \Phi\, \Delta \to A$); from $\Pi\, A \to B$ with $\Pi$ nonempty infer $\Pi \to B / A$; from $\Phi \to A$ and $\Gamma\, B\, \Delta \to C$ infer $\Gamma\, (B / A)\, \Phi\, \Delta \to C$; from $A\, \Pi \to B$ with $\Pi$ nonempty infer $\Pi \to A \backslash B$; from $\Phi \to A$ and $\Gamma\, B\, \Delta \to C$ infer $\Gamma\, \Phi\, (A \backslash B)\, \Delta \to C$; from $\Gamma \to A$ and $\Delta \to B$ infer $\Gamma\, \Delta \to A \cdot B$; and from $\Gamma\, A\, B\, \Delta \to C$ infer $\Gamma\, (A \cdot B)\, \Delta \to C$.
