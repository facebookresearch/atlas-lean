# Types of the Lambek calculus

The types of the Lambek calculus are built from primitive types $p_0, p_1, \dots$ by the product $A \cdot B$ and the divisions $B / A$ and $A \backslash B$.

## Sources

- M. Pentus, "Lambek calculus is NP-complete", Theoretical Computer Science 357 (2006), 186-201, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2006.03.018.
