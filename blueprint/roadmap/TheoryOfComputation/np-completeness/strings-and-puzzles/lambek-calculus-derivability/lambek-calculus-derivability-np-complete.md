# LAMBEK CALCULUS DERIVABILITY is NP-complete

The LAMBEK CALCULUS DERIVABILITY decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Pentus, "Lambek calculus is NP-complete", Theoretical Computer Science 357 (2006), 186-201, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2006.03.018.
