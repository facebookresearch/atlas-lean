# HEYAWAKE is NP-complete

The decision problem HEYAWAKE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Holzer and O. Ruepp, "The troubles of interior design: a complexity analysis of the game Heyawake", Fun with Algorithms (FUN 2007), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 4475 (2007), 198-212, doi:10.1007/978-3-540-72914-3_18.
