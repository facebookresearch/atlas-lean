# Equality of terms modulo associativity and commutativity

Given a list of AC symbols (indices of binary function symbols), two terms $s$ and $t$ are equal modulo associativity and commutativity when every equivalence relation on terms relates them that is compatible with every function symbol (applying a symbol to related arguments gives related terms) and that, for every AC symbol $f$ and all terms $u, v, w$, relates $f(u, f(v, w))$ with $f(f(u, v), w)$ and $f(u, v)$ with $f(v, u)$.
