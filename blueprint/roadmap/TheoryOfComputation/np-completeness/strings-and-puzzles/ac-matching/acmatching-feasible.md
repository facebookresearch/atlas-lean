# Feasible AC-MATCHING instances

An instance $(a, A, m, s, t)$ of AC-MATCHING is feasible when $s$ and $t$ spell a term $s$ with variables among $0, \dots, m-1$ and a ground term $t$, and some substitution $\sigma$ of ground terms for the variables makes $\sigma(s)$ equal to $t$ modulo associativity and commutativity of the symbols in $A$.
