# FLOOD-IT is NP-complete

For every number of colors $c \ge 3$, the decision problem [FLOOD-IT with $c$
colors](flood-it-dec.md) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time
many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- R. Clifford, M. Jalsenius, A. Montanaro, and B. Sach, "The complexity of flood filling games", Theory of Computing Systems 50(1) (2012), 72-92, doi:10.1007/s00224-011-9339-2 (arXiv:1001.4420, Theorem 1).
