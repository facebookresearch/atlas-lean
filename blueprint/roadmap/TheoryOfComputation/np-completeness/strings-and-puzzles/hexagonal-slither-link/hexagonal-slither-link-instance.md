# Instances of HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK

An instance of HEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK is a list of columns of natural numbers, from the left; entry $r$ of column $c$ is the clue of hexagon $(c, r)$, `0` for no clue and $v + 1$ for clue value $v$. The columns may have different lengths: the grid is the rectangle (number of columns) × (length of the first column). Missing entries in it are blank, and entries outside it are not clues; in `[[0], [0, 8]]` the trailing $8$ is not a clue to satisfy.
