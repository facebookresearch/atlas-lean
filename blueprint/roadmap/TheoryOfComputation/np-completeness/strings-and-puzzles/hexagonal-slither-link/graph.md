# Graph

The graph has **all** pairs of natural coordinates as vertices. Two vertices are adjacent exactly when they are the ends of a side of an in-range hexagon of the grid with `w` columns of `h` hexagons (see [sides](sides.md)). With one hexagon, the point $(20, 20)$ is also a vertex, and it is isolated: the finite drawing gives the right cycles, not the whole vertex set.
