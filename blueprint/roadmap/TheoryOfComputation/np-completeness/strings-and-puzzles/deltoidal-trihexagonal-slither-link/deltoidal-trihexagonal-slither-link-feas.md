# Feasible DELTOIDAL TRIHEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK instances

An instance of DELTOIDAL TRIHEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK is feasible when some cycle of its [grid](graph.md) (a closed walk of positive length with no repeated edge, whose only repeated vertex is its start) satisfies every nonzero clue entry at the enumerated positions: for every $r < h$, $c < w$ and $k < 6$ whose [clue entry](clue.md) is $v + 1$, exactly $v$ of the four [sides](kite-sides.md) of kite $k$ of hexagon $(c, r)$ are edges of the cycle. Here $h$ is the [number of rows](height.md) of the clue grid and $w$ the [length of its first row](width.md); entries of longer rows beyond position $w$ are not consulted, and missing entries read as $0$ (no clue). Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
