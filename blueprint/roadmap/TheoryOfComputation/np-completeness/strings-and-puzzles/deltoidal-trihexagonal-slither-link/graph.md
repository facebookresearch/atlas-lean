# Grid graph of a DELTOIDAL TRIHEXAGONAL SLITHER LINK instance

The deltoidal trihexagonal grid of an instance is the simple graph on lattice points in which two points are adjacent when they are the two ends of a [side](kite-sides.md) of one of the six [kites](kites.md) of a hexagon $(c, r)$ with $c < w$ and $r < h$, where $h$ is the [number of rows](height.md) of the clue grid and $w$ the [length of its first row](width.md). Every other lattice point is isolated.
