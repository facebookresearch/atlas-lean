# Sliding moves

One move takes a labelling $a$ of the cells of the $n \times n$ grid to $b$ when some cell $x$ with $a(x) = 0$ and a cell $y$ sharing a side with $x$ have $b$ equal to $a$ with the labels of $x$ and $y$ exchanged. The move does not require a unique zero cell or a nonzero label at $y$: for $n \ge 2$ an all-zero labelling moves to itself. A $1 \times 1$ or $0 \times 0$ board has no two side-adjacent cells and so no move. On configurations with one hole, a move slides a tile into the hole.
