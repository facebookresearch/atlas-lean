# JIGSAW PUZZLE as a decision problem

The decision problem JIGSAW PUZZLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a list of pieces and two dimensions, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-5.md) and [feasible](jigsaw-puzzle-feasible.md) for JIGSAW PUZZLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
