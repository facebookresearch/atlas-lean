# CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT BLANK SQUARES is NP-complete

The decision problem CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT BLANK SQUARES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources
