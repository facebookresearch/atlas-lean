# TANTRIX ROTATION PUZZLE as a decision problem

The decision problem TANTRIX ROTATION PUZZLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of lists of placed tiles, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-14.md) and [feasible](tantrix-rotation-puzzle-feasible.md) for TANTRIX ROTATION PUZZLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
