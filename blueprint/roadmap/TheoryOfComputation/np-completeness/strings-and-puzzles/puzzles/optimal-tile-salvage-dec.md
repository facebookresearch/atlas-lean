# OPTIMAL TILE SALVAGE as a decision problem

Fix natural numbers $x$ and $y$. The decision problem OPTIMAL TILE SALVAGE with $x \times y$ rectangles accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a list of cells and a bound, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for $x \times y$ rectangles. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
