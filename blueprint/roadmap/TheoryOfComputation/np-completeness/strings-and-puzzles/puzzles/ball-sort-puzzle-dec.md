# BALL SORT PUZZLE as a decision problem

The decision problem BALL SORT PUZZLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(h, k, F)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](ball-sort-puzzle-feasible.md) for BALL SORT PUZZLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
