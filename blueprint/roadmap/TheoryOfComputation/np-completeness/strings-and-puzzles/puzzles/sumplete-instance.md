# Instances of SUMPLETE

An instance is a triple of a list of rows of natural pairs (each $(p, q)$ read as the integer $p - q$), a list of row targets and a list of column targets, both lists of natural pairs. The three lists have unrelated lengths in the type: the rows may be ragged and the target lists need not match the grid; those shapes belong to [validation](well-formed-13.md).
