# SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE as a decision problem

The decision problem SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a side length, two configurations and a bound), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-8.md) and [feasible](npuzzle-shortest-solution-feasible.md) for SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
