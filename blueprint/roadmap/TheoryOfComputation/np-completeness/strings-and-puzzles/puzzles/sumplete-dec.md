# SUMPLETE as a decision problem

The decision problem SUMPLETE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples (a grid and two target lists of encoded integers), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-13.md) and [feasible](sumplete-feasible.md) for SUMPLETE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
