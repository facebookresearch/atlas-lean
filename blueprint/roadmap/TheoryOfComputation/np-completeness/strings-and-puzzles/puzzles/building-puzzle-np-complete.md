# BUILDING PUZZLE is NP-complete

The decision problem BUILDING PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and Y. Matsui, "Computational complexity of building puzzles", IEICE Transactions on Fundamentals of Electronics, Communications and Computer Sciences E99-A(6) (2016), 1145-1148, doi:10.1587/transfun.E99.A.1145.
