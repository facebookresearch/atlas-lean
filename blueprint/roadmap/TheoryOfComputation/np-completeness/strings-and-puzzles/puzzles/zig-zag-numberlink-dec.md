# ZIG-ZAG NUMBERLINK as a decision problem

The decision problem ZIG-ZAG NUMBERLINK accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of boards given as lists of lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-15.md) and [feasible](zig-zag-numberlink-feasible.md) for ZIG-ZAG NUMBERLINK. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
