# Instances of NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES

An instance is a pair of a Numberlink board, a list of rows of naturals ($0$ for no terminal, $c \ge 1$ for a terminal of pair $c$), and a list of natural pairs read as obstacle squares (row, column). The rows may be ragged, and obstacle coordinates may lie outside the board; rectangularity, two terminals per used label and valid obstacle coordinates belong to [validation](well-formed-9.md).
