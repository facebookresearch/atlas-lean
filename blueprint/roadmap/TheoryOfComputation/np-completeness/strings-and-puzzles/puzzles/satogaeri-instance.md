# Instances of SATOGAERI

An instance is a pair of a natural number $R$ of regions and a list of rows of natural pairs (region, clue), with clue $0$ (no circle), $1$ (a circle without a number) or $d + 2$ (a circle with number $d$). The rows may be ragged and region labels need not be below $R$; these belong to [validation](well-formed-12.md).
