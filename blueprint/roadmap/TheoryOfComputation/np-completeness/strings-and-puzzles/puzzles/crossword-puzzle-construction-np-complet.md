# CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION is NP-complete

The decision problem CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [GP14] CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION (credited there to Lewis and Papadimitriou).
- J. Engel, M. Holzer, O. Ruepp, and F. Sehnke, "On computer integrated rationalized crossword puzzle manufacturing", Fun with Algorithms (FUN 2012), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 7288 (2012), 131-141, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-30347-0_15 (records the attribution).
