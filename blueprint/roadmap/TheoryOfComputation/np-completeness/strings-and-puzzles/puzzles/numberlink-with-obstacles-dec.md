# NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES as a decision problem

The decision problem NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a board and a list of obstacle squares, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](numberlink-with-obstacles-feasible.md) for NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
