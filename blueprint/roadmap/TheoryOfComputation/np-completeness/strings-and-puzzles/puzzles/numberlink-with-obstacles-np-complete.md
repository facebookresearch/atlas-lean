# NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES is NP-complete

The decision problem NUMBERLINK WITH OBSTACLES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. F. Lynch, "The equivalence of theorem proving and the interconnection problem", ACM SIGDA Newsletter 5(3) (1975), 31-36, doi:10.1145/1061425.1061430.
- A. Adcock, E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, M. P. O'Brien, F. Reidl, F. Sánchez Villaamil, and B. D. Sullivan, "Zig-zag Numberlink is NP-complete", Journal of Information Processing 23(3) (2015), 239-245, doi:10.2197/ipsjjip.23.239 (abstract and Section 3, recording Lynch's result).
