# SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE is NP-complete

The decision problem SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Ratner and M. Warmuth, "The (n²−1)-puzzle and related relocation problems", Journal of Symbolic Computation 10(2) (1990), 111-137, doi:10.1016/S0747-7171(08)80001-6.
- E. D. Demaine and M. Rudoy, "A simple proof that the (n²−1)-puzzle is hard", Theoretical Computer Science 732 (2018), 80-84, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2018.04.031 (Problem 1).
