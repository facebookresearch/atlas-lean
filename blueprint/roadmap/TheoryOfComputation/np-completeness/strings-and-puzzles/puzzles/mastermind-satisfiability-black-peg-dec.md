# BLACK-PEG MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem BLACK-PEG MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(K, N, Q)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](mastermind-satisfiability-black-peg-feas.md) for BLACK-PEG MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
