# SATOGAERI as a decision problem

The decision problem SATOGAERI accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a number of regions and a grid, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-12.md) and [feasible](satogaeri-feasible.md) for SATOGAERI. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
