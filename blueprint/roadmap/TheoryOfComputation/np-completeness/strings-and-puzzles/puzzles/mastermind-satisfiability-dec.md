# MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY as a decision problem

The decision problem MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(\kappa, \ell, G)$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
