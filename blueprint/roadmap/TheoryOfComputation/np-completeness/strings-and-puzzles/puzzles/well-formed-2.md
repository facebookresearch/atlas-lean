# Well-formed instances of BUILDING PUZZLE

In the grid $S$ an entry $0$ represents a blank. An instance is well-formed when $S$ is a well-formed partial Latin square instance ($n \times n$, entries at most $n$, $n \ge 1$) in which no nonzero entry occurs twice in a row or in a column, and the four lists of Building numbers have length $n$ and entries at most $n$. Blanks may repeat: a blank $2 \times 2$ grid with zero clues is well-formed.
