# ZIG-ZAG NUMBERLINK is NP-complete

The decision problem ZIG-ZAG NUMBERLINK belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Adcock, E. D. Demaine, M. L. Demaine, M. P. O'Brien, F. Reidl, F. Sánchez Villaamil, and B. D. Sullivan, "Zig-zag Numberlink is NP-complete", Journal of Information Processing 23(3) (2015), 239-245, doi:10.2197/ipsjjip.23.239 (arXiv:1410.5845, Definitions 1-4 and Theorem 1).
