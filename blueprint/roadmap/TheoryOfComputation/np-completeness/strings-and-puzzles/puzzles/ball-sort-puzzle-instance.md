# Instances of BALL SORT PUZZLE

An instance is a triple $(h, k, F)$ of a natural bin capacity $h$, a natural number $k$ of empty bins, and a list $F$ of lists of natural colours, each listing a bin from bottom to top. A listed bin may be shorter or longer than the capacity: $(2, 0, [[1]])$ is representable. That every listed bin holds exactly $h$ balls belongs to [validation](well-formed.md).
