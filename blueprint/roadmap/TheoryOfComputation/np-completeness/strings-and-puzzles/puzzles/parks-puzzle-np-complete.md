# PARKS PUZZLE is NP-complete

For all natural numbers $c, r \ge 1$, the decision problem PARKS PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- I. Minevich, G. Cunningham, K. A. Karan and J. V. Gyllinsky, "Parks: A Doubly Infinite Family of NP-Complete Puzzles and Generalizations of A002464", arXiv:2411.02251 (2024), Definition 1, Section IV and Theorem 2.
