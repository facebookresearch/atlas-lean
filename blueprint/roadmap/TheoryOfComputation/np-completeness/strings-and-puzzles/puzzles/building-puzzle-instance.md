# Instances of BUILDING PUZZLE

An instance is a pair of a LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION instance (a natural size $n$ and a list of rows of naturals, $0$ for blank) and four lists of natural Building numbers (top, bottom, left, right; $0$ for none). The rows may be ragged and the four lists may have any lengths; the Latin-square conditions, the shape and the lengths of the four lists belong to [validation](well-formed-2.md).
