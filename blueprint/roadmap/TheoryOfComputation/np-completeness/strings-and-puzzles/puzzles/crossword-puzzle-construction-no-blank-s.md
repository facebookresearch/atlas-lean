# CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT BLANK SQUARES as a decision problem

The decision problem CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT BLANK SQUARES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(W, n, A)$ of a word list, a side length and a 0–1 matrix, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-3.md) for CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT BLANK SQUARES and feasible for CROSSWORD PUZZLE CONSTRUCTION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
