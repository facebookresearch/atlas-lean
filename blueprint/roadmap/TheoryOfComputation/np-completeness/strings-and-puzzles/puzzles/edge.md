# Edges of jigsaw pieces

An edge is stored as a natural pair $(k, c)$. In the intended reading it is flat ($k = 0$), a tab of shape $c$ ($k = 1$) or a pocket of shape $c$ ($k = 2$); an edge such as $(3, 7)$ is representable but has no legal shape, which is excluded by [validation](well-formed-5.md).
