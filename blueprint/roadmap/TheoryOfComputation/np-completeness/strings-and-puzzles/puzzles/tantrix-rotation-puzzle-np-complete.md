# TANTRIX ROTATION PUZZLE is NP-complete

The decision problem TANTRIX ROTATION PUZZLE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Holzer and W. Holzer, "Tantrix rotation puzzles are intractable", Discrete Applied Mathematics 144 (2004), 345-358, doi:10.1016/j.dam.2003.11.011.
- D. Baumeister and J. Rothe, "The three-color and two-color Tantrix rotation puzzle problems are NP-complete via parsimonious reductions", Information and Computation 207 (2009), 1119-1139, doi:10.1016/j.ic.2009.03.001 (arXiv:0711.1827), Section 2.2 (the formal definition of the problem, following Holzer and Holzer).
