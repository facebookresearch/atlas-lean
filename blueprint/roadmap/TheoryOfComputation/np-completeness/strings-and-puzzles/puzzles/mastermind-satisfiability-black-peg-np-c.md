# BLACK-PEG MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem BLACK-PEG MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. T. Goodrich, "On the algorithmic complexity of the Mastermind game with black-peg results", Information Processing Letters 109(13) (2009), 675-678, doi:10.1016/j.ipl.2009.02.021, arXiv:0904.4911 (Section 2, Theorem 1).
