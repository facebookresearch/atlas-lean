# Instances of SHORTEST SOLUTION OF THE (n²−1)-PUZZLE

An instance is a quadruple of a natural board side $n$, two lists of rows of naturals (the start and target configurations, $0$ for a hole) and a natural move bound $k$. The rows may be ragged and need not number $n$, and the labels need not form a permutation; the board shape and labels belong to [validation](well-formed-8.md).
