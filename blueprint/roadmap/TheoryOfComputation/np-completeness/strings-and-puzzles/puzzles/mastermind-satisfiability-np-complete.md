# MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem MASTERMIND SATISFIABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- J. Stuckman and G.-Q. Zhang, "Mastermind is NP-complete", INFOCOMP Journal of Computer Science 5(2) (2006), 25-28, arXiv:cs/0512049 (Definition 2.1, Section 2.1, Theorem 2.1).
