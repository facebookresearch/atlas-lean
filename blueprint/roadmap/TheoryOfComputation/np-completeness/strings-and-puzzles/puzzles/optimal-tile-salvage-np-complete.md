# OPTIMAL TILE SALVAGE is NP-complete

For all natural numbers $1 \le x \le y$ with $(x, y)$ different from $(1, 1)$ and $(1, 2)$, the decision problem OPTIMAL TILE SALVAGE with $x \times y$ rectangles belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
