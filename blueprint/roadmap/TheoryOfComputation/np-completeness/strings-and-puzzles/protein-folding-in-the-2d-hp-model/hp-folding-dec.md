# PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE HP MODEL as a decision problem

For a dimension $d$, the decision problem PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE $d$-dimensional HP MODEL
accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its
instances, to an instance that is [feasible in dimension $d$](hpfolding-feasible.md); no further
well-formedness is required. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to an instance.
