# PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE 2D HP MODEL is NP-complete

The decision problem [PROTEIN FOLDING IN THE HP MODEL](hp-folding-dec.md) in dimension $2$
belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- P. Crescenzi, D. Goldman, C. Papadimitriou, A. Piccolboni, and M. Yannakakis, "On the complexity of protein folding", Journal of Computational Biology 5 (1998), 423-465, doi:10.1089/cmb.1998.5.423.
