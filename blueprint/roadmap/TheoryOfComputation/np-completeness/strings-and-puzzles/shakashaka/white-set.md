# Union of the closed white parts of the white squares in SHAKASHAKA

For an instance of SHAKASHAKA and a choice of options $s(r, c) \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$, this is the union of the closed [white parts](white-part.md) under option $s(r, c)$ of the squares $(r, c)$ with $r < m$, $c < n$ and [entry](cell.md) $0$, where $m$ is the number of rows and $n$ the length of the first row. It is a closed subset of $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$. A position of a short row beyond its end reads as black and contributes nothing, and entries of a longer row beyond column $n$ do not contribute.
