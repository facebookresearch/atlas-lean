# Closed white part of square `(r, c)` under option `t` in SHAKASHAKA

In SHAKASHAKA, the closed white part of square `(r, c)` under option `t`. With local coordinates `u = x - r`, `v = y - c` in `[0, 1]`: option `0` leaves the square white; options `1, 2, 3, 4` place the black half-square with its right angle at the corner `(r, c)`, `(r, c + 1)`, `(r + 1, c)`, `(r + 1, c + 1)` respectively, leaving the opposite closed half white.
