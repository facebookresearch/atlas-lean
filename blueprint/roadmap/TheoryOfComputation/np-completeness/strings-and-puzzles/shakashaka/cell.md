# Entry of a square in SHAKASHAKA

For an instance of SHAKASHAKA, the entry of square $(r, c)$ is entry $c$ of row $r$ of the stored board, or $1$ (an unnumbered black square) when there is no row $r$ or that row has no entry $c$. It is defined for every $(r, c)$; only the squares with $r < m$ and $c < n$, where $m$ is the number of rows and $n$ the length of the first row, take part in [feasibility](shakashaka-feasible.md).
