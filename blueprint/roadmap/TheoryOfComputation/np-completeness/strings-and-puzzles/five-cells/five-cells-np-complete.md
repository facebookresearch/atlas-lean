# FIVE CELLS is NP-complete

The decision problem FIVE CELLS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a
polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- C. Iwamoto and T. Ide, "Five Cells and Tilepaint are NP-complete", IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E105-D(3) (2022), 508-516, doi:10.1587/transinf.2021FCP0001.
