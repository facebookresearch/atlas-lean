# SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS as a decision problem

The decision problem SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a permutation list and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-8.md) and [feasible](sorting-by-transpositions-feasible.md) for SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
