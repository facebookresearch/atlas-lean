# Instances of REVERSAL MEDIAN

An instance is a quintuple of a natural number $n$, three [signed gene lists](signed-perm.md) $\pi^1, \pi^2, \pi^3$ and a natural bound $K$. The lists may repeat or omit genes and use labels above $n$; that each is a signed permutation of $n$ genes belongs to [validation](well-formed-6.md).
