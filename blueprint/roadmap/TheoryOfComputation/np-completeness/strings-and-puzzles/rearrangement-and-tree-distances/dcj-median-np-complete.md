# DCJ MEDIAN is NP-complete

The decision problem DCJ MEDIAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- E. Tannier, C. Zheng and D. Sankoff, "Multichromosomal median and halving problems under different genomic distances", BMC Bioinformatics 10 (2009), 120, doi:10.1186/1471-2105-10-120 (Theorem 5: "The DCJ median problem for multichromosomal genomes is NP-hard, even for circular genomes").
