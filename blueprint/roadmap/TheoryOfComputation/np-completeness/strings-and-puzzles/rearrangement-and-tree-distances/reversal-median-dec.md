# REVERSAL MEDIAN as a decision problem

The decision problem REVERSAL MEDIAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quintuples $(n, \pi^1, \pi^2, \pi^3, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](reversal-median-feasible.md) for REVERSAL MEDIAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
