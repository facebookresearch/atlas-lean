# BREAKPOINT MEDIAN is NP-complete

The decision problem BREAKPOINT MEDIAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- I. Pe'er and R. Shamir, "The median problems for breakpoints are NP-complete", Electronic Colloquium on Computational Complexity, Report TR98-071 (1998) (Section 2, the signed median problem and breakpoints; Corollary 4.9: the signed permutations median problem is NP-complete).
