# REVERSAL MEDIAN is NP-complete

The decision problem REVERSAL MEDIAN belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Caprara, "The reversal median problem", INFORMS Journal on Computing 15 (2003), 93-113, doi:10.1287/ijoc.15.1.93.15155.
- the hardness for three genomes is recorded by M. Bader, "The transposition median problem is NP-complete", Theoretical Computer Science 412 (2011), 1099-1110, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2010.12.009.
