# Instances of TBR DISTANCE

An instance is a quadruple of a natural number $m$ of taxa, two [tree records](tree-data.md) $T_1$ and $T_2$ and a natural bound $K$. That $m \ge 3$ and that the records describe unrooted binary phylogenetic trees on the taxa $0, \dots, m-1$ belong to [validation](well-formed-10.md).
