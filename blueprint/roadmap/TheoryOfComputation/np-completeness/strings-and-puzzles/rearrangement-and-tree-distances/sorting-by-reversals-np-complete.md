# SORTING BY REVERSALS is NP-complete

The decision problem SORTING BY REVERSALS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Caprara, "Sorting permutations by reversals and Eulerian cycle decompositions", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 12 (1999), 91-110, doi:10.1137/S089548019731994X.
- problem as stated in P. Crescenzi and V. Kann (eds.), *A compendium of NP optimization problems*, entry MINIMUM SORTING BY REVERSALS.
