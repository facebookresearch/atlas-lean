# SYNTENIC DISTANCE is NP-complete

The decision problem SYNTENIC DISTANCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. DasGupta, T. Jiang, S. Kannan, M. Li and E. Sweedyk, "On the complexity and approximation of syntenic distance", Discrete Applied Mathematics 88 (1998), 59-82, doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00066-3; Definition 2.1 and Theorem 3.1 are numbered as in the conference version (RECOMB 1997).
