# NNI DISTANCE as a decision problem

The decision problem NNI DISTANCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, T_1, T_2, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-5.md) and [feasible](nnidistance-feasible.md) for NNI DISTANCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
