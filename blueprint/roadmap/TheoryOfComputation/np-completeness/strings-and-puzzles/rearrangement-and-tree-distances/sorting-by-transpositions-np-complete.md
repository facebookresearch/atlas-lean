# SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS is NP-complete

The decision problem SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- L. Bulteau, G. Fertin and I. Rusu, "Sorting by transpositions is difficult", SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 26 (2012), 1148-1180, doi:10.1137/110851390, arXiv:1011.1157.
