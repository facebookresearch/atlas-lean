# EXEMPLAR BREAKPOINT DISTANCE as a decision problem

The decision problem EXEMPLAR BREAKPOINT DISTANCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of two signed-gene lists and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-4.md) and [feasible](exemplar-breakpoint-distance-feasible.md) for EXEMPLAR BREAKPOINT DISTANCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
