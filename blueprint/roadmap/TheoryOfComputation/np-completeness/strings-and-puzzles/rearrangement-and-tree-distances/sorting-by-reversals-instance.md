# Instances given by a list of naturals and a bound

An instance is a pair of a raw list of natural numbers and a natural bound. The same pairs are the instances of SORTING BY REVERSALS and BLOCK SORTING, where the list is read as a permutation $[\pi(1), \dots, \pi(n)]$, of SORTING BY TRANSPOSITIONS, where it is read as $[\pi(0), \dots, \pi(n)]$, and of MINIMUM STRING ATTRACTOR and SMALLEST GRAMMAR, where it is a word over natural letters. No permutation, fixed-endpoint, word witness or distance is part of the pair; those are the separate well-formedness and feasibility predicates.
