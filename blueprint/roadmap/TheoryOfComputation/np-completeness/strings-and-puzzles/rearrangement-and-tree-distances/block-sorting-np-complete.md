# BLOCK SORTING is NP-complete

The decision problem BLOCK SORTING belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- W. W. Bein, L. L. Larmore, S. Latifi and I. H. Sudborough, "Block sorting is hard", Proc.
