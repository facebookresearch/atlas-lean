# Feasible instances of REVERSAL MEDIAN

An instance is feasible when some signed permutation $\sigma$ of the $n$ genes is transformed into $\pi^1$, $\pi^2$ and $\pi^3$ by three sequences of reversals of total length at most $K$, where every reversal $(a, b)$ in each sequence satisfies $a \le b < n$. The bounds are part of the condition; the reversal operator itself is defined for all pairs.
