# UNSIGNED TRANSLOCATION DISTANCE is NP-complete

The decision problem UNSIGNED TRANSLOCATION DISTANCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. Zhu and L. Wang, "On the complexity of unsigned translocation distance", Theoretical Computer Science 352 (2006), 322-328, doi:10.1016/j.tcs.2005.09.078 (Section 2: the model.
- Theorem 3: computing the unsigned translocation distance is NP-hard).
