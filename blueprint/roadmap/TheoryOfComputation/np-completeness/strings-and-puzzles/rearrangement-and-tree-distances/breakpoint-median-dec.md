# BREAKPOINT MEDIAN as a decision problem

The decision problem BREAKPOINT MEDIAN accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples of three signed permutations and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](breakpoint-median-feasible.md) for BREAKPOINT MEDIAN. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
