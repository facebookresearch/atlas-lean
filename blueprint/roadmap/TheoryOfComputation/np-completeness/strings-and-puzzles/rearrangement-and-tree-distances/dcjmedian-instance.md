# Instances of DCJ MEDIAN

An instance is a quintuple of a list of Booleans, whose length is the gene count $n$, three lists of pairs of natural numbers meant as the adjacency lists of genomes $\Pi_1, \Pi_2, \Pi_3$ over the extremities $0, \dots, 2n-1$ (gene $g$ has tail $2g$ and head $2g+1$), and a bound $K$. The gene count is the length of the Boolean list whatever its entries, so $[\mathrm{false}]$ also counts one gene; that all entries are true, and that the lists describe genomes, are parts of well-formedness.
