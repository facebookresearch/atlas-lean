# NNI DISTANCE is NP-complete

The decision problem NNI DISTANCE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- B. DasGupta, X. He, T. Jiang, M. Li, J. Tromp and L. Zhang, "On computing the nearest neighbor interchange distance", in *Discrete Mathematical Problems with Medical Applications*, DIMACS Series in Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science 55 (2000), 125-143, doi:10.1090/dimacs/055/09 (Section 1: nni operations and the nni distance; Theorem 1: "Let `T1` and `T2` be two trees with unique leaf labels and `k` be an integer. It is NP-complete to decide if `Dnni(T1, T2) ≤ k`"; conference version in the Proceedings of the Eighth Annual ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 1997), 427-436).
