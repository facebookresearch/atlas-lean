# Block moves

For arbitrary lists $\pi$ and $\pi'$ of natural numbers, $\pi'$ arises from $\pi$ by a block move when $\pi = A\,B\,C$ with $B$ a [block](is-block.md) of $\pi$, and $\pi' = X\,B\,Y$ for some split $X\,Y = A\,C$, with $\pi' \ne \pi$. On a permutation this picks up a block and places it elsewhere.
