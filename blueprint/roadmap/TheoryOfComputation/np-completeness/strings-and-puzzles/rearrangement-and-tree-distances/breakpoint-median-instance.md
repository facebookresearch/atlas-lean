# Instances of BREAKPOINT MEDIAN

An instance is a quadruple of three lists $\pi_0, \pi_1, \pi_2$ of pairs (absolute value, sign), where the sign $\mathrm{true}$ means $+$ and $\mathrm{false}$ means $-$, and a bound $k$. The lists are meant as signed permutations, but the instance type does not require it; that is part of well-formedness. The [reversal median](reversal-median-instance.md) encoding uses the opposite polarity.
