# Blocks of a list of naturals

For arbitrary lists $\pi$ and $B$ of natural numbers, not necessarily permutations, $B$ is a block of $\pi$ when $B$ is nonempty, a contiguous sublist of $\pi$ and a contiguous sublist of the identity list of length $|\pi|$, and no longer contiguous sublist of $\pi$ containing $B$ is also a contiguous sublist of that identity list. On a permutation these are the maximal runs shared with the identity.
