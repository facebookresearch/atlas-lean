# Feasible instances of TBR DISTANCE

An instance $(m, T_1, T_2, K)$ is feasible when both tree data $T_1$ and $T_2$ [describe unrooted binary phylogenetic trees](is-phylo-tree.md) on the $m$ taxa, with their supplied leaf lists, and some agreement forest for the two trees, a partition of the taxa, has at most $K + 1$ parts. The condition $m \ge 3$ of well-formedness is not part of feasibility.
