# TBR DISTANCE as a decision problem

The decision problem TBR DISTANCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples $(m, T_1, T_2, K)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-10.md) and [feasible](tbrdistance-feasible.md) for TBR DISTANCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
