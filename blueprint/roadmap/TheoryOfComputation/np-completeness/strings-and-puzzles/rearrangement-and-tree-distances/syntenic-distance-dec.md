# SYNTENIC DISTANCE as a decision problem

The decision problem SYNTENIC DISTANCE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of two genomes and a bound, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-9.md) and [feasible](syntenic-distance-feasible.md) for SYNTENIC DISTANCE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
