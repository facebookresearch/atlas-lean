# The STRONG INEQUIVALENCE OF IANOV SCHEMES (loop-free, trivial `P₂`) decision problem

The STRONG INEQUIVALENCE OF IANOV SCHEMES (loop-free, trivial `P₂`) decision problem accepts a bitstring exactly when it decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding, to an instance that is well formed and feasible as an instance of STRONG INEQUIVALENCE OF IANOV SCHEMES. Bitstrings that do not decode to an instance are rejected.
