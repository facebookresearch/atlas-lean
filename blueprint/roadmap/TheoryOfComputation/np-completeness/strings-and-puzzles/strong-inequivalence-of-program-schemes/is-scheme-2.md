# Scheme over `nF` function symbols and `nP` predicate symbols in STRONG INEQUIVALENCE OF IANOV SCHEMES

In STRONG INEQUIVALENCE OF IANOV SCHEMES, a scheme over `nF` function symbols and `nP` predicate symbols: nonempty, every instruction is `x ← f_r(x)` with `r < nF`, `if p_r(x) then goto Iⱼ else goto Iₖ` with `r < nP` and `1 ≤ j, k ≤ m`, or `halt`, and no assignment is the last instruction.
