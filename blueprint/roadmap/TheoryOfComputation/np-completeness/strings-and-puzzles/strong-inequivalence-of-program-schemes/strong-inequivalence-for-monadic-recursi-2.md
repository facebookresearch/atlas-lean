# Feasible instances of STRONG INEQUIVALENCE FOR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEMES

An instance of STRONG INEQUIVALENCE FOR MONADIC RECURSION SCHEMES is feasible when under some domain, interpretation and initial value, the values of `f₀(x₀)` defined by `S₁` and `S₂` differ, or exactly one of them is defined. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
