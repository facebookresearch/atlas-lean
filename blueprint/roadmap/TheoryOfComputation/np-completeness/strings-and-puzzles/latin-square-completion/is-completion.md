# Completions of a partial Latin square

A matrix $C$ completes $(n, M)$ when $C$ is a full $n \times n$ matrix with entries in $1, \dots, n$ forming a well-formed and feasible LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION instance (a Latin square), and $C$ agrees with $M$ on the nonzero entries of $M$. Cells missing from $M$ count as empty ($0$).
