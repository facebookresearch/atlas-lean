# SYMMETRIC LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION as a decision problem

The decision problem SYMMETRIC LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, M)$ (a natural number and a list of lists of natural numbers), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SYMMETRIC LATIN SQUARE COMPLETION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
