# ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE is NP-complete

The decision problem ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
