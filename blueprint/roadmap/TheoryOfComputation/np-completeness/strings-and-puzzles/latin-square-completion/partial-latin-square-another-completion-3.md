# ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE as a decision problem

The decision problem ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a pair $(n, M)$ and a list of lists of natural numbers, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for ANOTHER COMPLETION OF A PARTIAL LATIN SQUARE. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
