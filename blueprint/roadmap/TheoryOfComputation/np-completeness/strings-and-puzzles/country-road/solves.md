# Loops satisfying the COUNTRY ROAD rules

A closed walk $c$ in the grid graph satisfies the COUNTRY ROAD rules when it visits a cell of every region $k < R$; for every region, at most one step of $c$ enters it from a cell of another region; every region carrying a number $v$ has exactly $v$ of its cells visited by $c$; and for every two adjacent cells in different regions, $c$ visits at least one of them.
