# COUNTRY ROAD is NP-complete

The COUNTRY ROAD decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- A. Ishibashi, Y. Sato and S. Iwata, "NP-completeness of two pencil puzzles: Yajilin and Country Road", Utilitas Mathematica 88 (2012), 237-246.
- H. Tang, "A framework for loop and path puzzle satisfiability NP-hardness results", arXiv:2202.02046 (2022), Section 3.7.
