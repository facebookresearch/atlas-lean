# Feasible instances of RESILIENCE OF THE TRIANGLE QUERY

An instance of RESILIENCE OF THE TRIANGLE QUERY is feasible when `(D, k) ∈ RES(q△)`: `D ⊨ q△`, and deleting at most `k` tuples of `D` leaves a database `D'` with `D' ⊭ q△`. Feasibility does not include well-formedness; the decision problem requires both.
