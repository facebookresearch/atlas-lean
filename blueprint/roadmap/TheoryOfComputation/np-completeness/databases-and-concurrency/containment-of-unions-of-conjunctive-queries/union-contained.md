# Containment of unions of conjunctive queries, at the level of assignments in UNION OF CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES CONTAINMENT

In UNION OF CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES CONTAINMENT, containment of unions of conjunctive queries, at the level of assignments: on every database `D`, every satisfying assignment `σ` of a conjunctive query `Q` of `U₁` is matched by a satisfying assignment `τ` of some conjunctive query `Q'` of `U₂` producing the same head tuple. This reads `U₁(D) ⊆ U₂(D)` for the answer-set semantics.
