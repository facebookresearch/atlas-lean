# Instances of UNION OF CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES CONTAINMENT

An instance of UNION OF CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES CONTAINMENT is a value of type `List (List ℕ × List (ℕ × List ℕ)) × List (List ℕ × List (ℕ × List ℕ))`: the conjunctive queries of `U₁` and of `U₂`, each as its head tuple and its body atoms `(relation, argument tuple)`.
