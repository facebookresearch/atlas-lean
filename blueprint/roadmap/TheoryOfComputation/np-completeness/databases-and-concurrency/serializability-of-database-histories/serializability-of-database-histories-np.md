# SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES is NP-complete

The decision problem SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR33] SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES.
- C. H. Papadimitriou, "The serializability of concurrent database updates", Journal of the ACM 26 (1979), 631-653, doi:10.1145/322154.322158.
