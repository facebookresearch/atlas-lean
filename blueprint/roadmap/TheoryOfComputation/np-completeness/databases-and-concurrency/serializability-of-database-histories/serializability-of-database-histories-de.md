# SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES as a decision problem

The decision problem SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(k, T, H)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed.md) and [feasible](serializability-of-database-histories-fe.md) for SERIALIZABILITY OF DATABASE HISTORIES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
