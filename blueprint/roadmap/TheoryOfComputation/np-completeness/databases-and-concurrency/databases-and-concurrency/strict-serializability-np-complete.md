# STRICT SERIALIZABILITY is NP-complete

The decision problem STRICT SERIALIZABILITY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- D. S. Johnson, "The NP-completeness column: an ongoing guide", Journal of Algorithms 4 (1983), 286-300, doi:10.1016/0196-6774(83)90028-7, problem [4] STRICT SERIALIZABILITY (reference: Sethi; transformation from 3SAT).
- R. Sethi, "Useless actions make a difference: strict serializability of database updates", Journal of the ACM 29 (1982), 394-403, doi:10.1145/322307.322314.
