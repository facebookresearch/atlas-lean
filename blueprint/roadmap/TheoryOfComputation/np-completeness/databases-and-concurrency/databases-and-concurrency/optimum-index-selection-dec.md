# OPTIMUM INDEX SELECTION as a decision problem

The decision problem OPTIMUM INDEX SELECTION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(k, \text{records}, p)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-12.md) and [feasible](optimum-index-selection-feasible.md) for OPTIMUM INDEX SELECTION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
