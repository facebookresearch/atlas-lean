# CONJUNCTIVE BOOLEAN QUERY is NP-complete

The decision problem CONJUNCTIVE BOOLEAN QUERY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR31] CONJUNCTIVE BOOLEAN QUERY, p. 233 (reference [Chandra and Merlin, 1977]; transformation from CLIQUE).
- A. K. Chandra and P. M. Merlin, "Optimal implementation of conjunctive queries in relational data bases", Proceedings of the 9th Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 1977), 77-90, doi:10.1145/800105.803397.
