# ADDITIONAL KEY is NP-complete

The decision problem ADDITIONAL KEY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR27] ADDITIONAL KEY, p. 232 (reference [Beeri and Bernstein, 1978]; transformation from HITTING SET), with the closure $F^*$ as defined in [SR26] MINIMUM CARDINALITY KEY, p. 232.
