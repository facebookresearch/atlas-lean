# THIRD NORMAL FORM TEST as a decision problem

The decision problem THIRD NORMAL FORM TEST accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs $(n, F)$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-15.md) and [feasible](third-normal-form-test-feasible.md) for THIRD NORMAL FORM TEST. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
