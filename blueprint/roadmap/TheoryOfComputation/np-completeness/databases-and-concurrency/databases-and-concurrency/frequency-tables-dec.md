# CONSISTENCY OF DATABASE FREQUENCY TABLES as a decision problem

The decision problem CONSISTENCY OF DATABASE FREQUENCY TABLES accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of quadruples (a unary count, domain sizes, tables and known values), to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-6.md) and [feasible](frequency-tables-feasible.md) for CONSISTENCY OF DATABASE FREQUENCY TABLES. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
