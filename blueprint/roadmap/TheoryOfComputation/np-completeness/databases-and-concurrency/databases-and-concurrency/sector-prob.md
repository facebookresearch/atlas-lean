# Weight of a sector

The sum $p(R_i)$ of the listed weights $a/b$ of the records an allocation places in sector $i$ (with $a/0 = 0$). It is a probability only for normalised inputs, where the weights lie in $[0, 1]$ and sum to $1$, as well-formedness requires; a single record of weight $2/1$ gives $2$.
