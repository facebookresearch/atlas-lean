# EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST is NP-complete

The decision problem EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [SR4] EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST.
- R. A. Cody and E. G. Coffman, Jr., "Record allocation for minimizing expected retrieval costs on drum-like storage devices", Journal of the ACM 23 (1976), 103-115, doi:10.1145/321921.321933.
