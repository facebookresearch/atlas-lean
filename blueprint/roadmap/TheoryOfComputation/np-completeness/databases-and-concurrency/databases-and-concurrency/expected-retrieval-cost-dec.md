# EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST as a decision problem

The decision problem EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples of a fraction list and two natural numbers, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-8.md) and [feasible](expected-retrieval-cost-feasible.md) for EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
