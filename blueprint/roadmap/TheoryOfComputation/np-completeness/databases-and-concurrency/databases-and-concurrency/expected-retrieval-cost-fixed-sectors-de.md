# EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST WITH m SECTORS as a decision problem

Fix a natural number $m$. The decision problem EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST WITH m SECTORS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a fraction list and a fraction, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-7.md) and [feasible](expected-retrieval-cost-fixed-sectors-fe.md) for $m$. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
