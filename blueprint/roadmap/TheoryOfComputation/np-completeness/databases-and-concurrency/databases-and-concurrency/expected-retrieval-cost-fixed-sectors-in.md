# Instances of EXPECTED RETRIEVAL COST WITH m SECTORS

An instance is a pair of record weights $p(r)$ as fractions $(a, b)$, read as $a/b$ and $0$ when $b = 0$ (probabilities for well-formed instances), and a rational bound $K$ given as a fraction $(a, b)$ of natural numbers, which reads as $0$ when $b = 0$; well-formedness requires $a > 0$ and $b > 0$, so $K$ is positive (where the parent problem takes a positive integer bound); the number $m$ of sectors is fixed.
