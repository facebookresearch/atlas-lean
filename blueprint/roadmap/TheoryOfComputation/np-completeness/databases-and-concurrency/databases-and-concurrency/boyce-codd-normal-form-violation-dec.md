# BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION as a decision problem

The decision problem BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, F, A')$, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
