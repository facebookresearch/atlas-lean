# BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION as a decision problem

The decision problem BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples $(n, F, A')$, to an instance that is [well-formed](well-formed-2.md) and [feasible](boyce-codd-normal-form-violation-feasibl.md) for BOYCE-CODD NORMAL FORM VIOLATION. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
