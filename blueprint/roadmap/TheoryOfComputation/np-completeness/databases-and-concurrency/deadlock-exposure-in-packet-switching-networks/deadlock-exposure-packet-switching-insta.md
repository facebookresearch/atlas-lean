# Instances of DEADLOCK EXPOSURE IN PACKET SWITCHING NETWORKS

An instance of DEADLOCK EXPOSURE IN PACKET SWITCHING NETWORKS is a tuple $(n, E, b, R, W)$ of a number $n$ of vertices $0, \dots, n-1$, an edge list $E$, the buffer counts $b(0), \dots, b(n-1)$, a list $R$ of routes (each a list of vertices), and a list $W$ of windows $(u, v, w(u, v))$.
