# DEADLOCK EXPOSURE IN PACKET SWITCHING NETWORKS is NP-complete

The DEADLOCK EXPOSURE IN PACKET SWITCHING NETWORKS decision problem belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.
