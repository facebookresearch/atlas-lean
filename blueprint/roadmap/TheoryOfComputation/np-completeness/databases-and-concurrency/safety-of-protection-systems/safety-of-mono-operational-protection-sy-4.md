# SAFETY OF MONO-OPERATIONAL FILE PROTECTION SYSTEMS is NP-complete

The SAFETY OF MONO-OPERATIONAL FILE PROTECTION SYSTEMS decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a finite set $R = \{0, \dots, g - 1\}$ of generic rights, a finite set $O = \{0, \dots, |O| - 1\}$ of objects, and a set $S = \{0, \dots, |S| - 1\} \subseteq O$ of subjects (so $|S| \le |O|$); these three sets are fixed, since no operation creates or destroys a subject or an object. Further, sets $P(s, o) \subseteq R$ of rights for each subject $s \in S$ and object $o \in O$ (the access matrix); a finite set $C$ of commands, each with formal parameters $X_0, \dots, X_{k-1}$ and of the form "if $r_1 \in P(X_{a_1}, X_{b_1})$ and ... and $r_m \in P(X_{a_m}, X_{b_m})$ then $\theta$" ($m \ge 0$), where the rights $r_j$ are fixed members of $R$, every index is below $k$, and $\theta$ is a single operation "enter $r$ into $P(X_a, X_b)$" or "delete $r$ from $P(X_a, X_b)$" with $r \in R$ and $a, b < k$; and a specified right $r' \in R$. A command runs with actual parameters that are objects; it is executable when its conditions hold and the row $X_a$ of its operation is a subject. Question: is there a sequence of executable commands from $C$, with actual parameters, such that at some point in its execution the right $r'$ is entered into a set $P(s, o)$ that previously did not contain $r'$?
