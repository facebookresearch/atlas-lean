# Instances of SAFETY OF MONO-OPERATIONAL FILE PROTECTION SYSTEMS

An instance of SAFETY OF MONO-OPERATIONAL FILE PROTECTION SYSTEMS is a value of type `ℕ × ℕ × ℕ × List (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ) × List Command × ℕ`: the number `g` of rights, the number `|O|` of objects, the number `|S|` of subjects, the initial access matrix as a list of triples `(s, o, r)` (`r ∈ P(s, o)`), the commands, and the right `r'`.
