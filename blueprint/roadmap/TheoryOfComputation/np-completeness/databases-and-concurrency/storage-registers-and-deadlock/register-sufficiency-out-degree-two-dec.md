# REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (out-degree at most 2) as a decision problem

The decision problem REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (out-degree at most 2) accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed for REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (out-degree at most 2) and feasible for REGISTER SUFFICIENCY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
