# Well-formed instances of FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT for out-degree at most 2

An instance of FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT for out-degree at most 2 is well-formed when it is a well-formed FEASIBLE REGISTER ASSIGNMENT instance (a directed acyclic graph, $K \ge 1$, and a register index below $K$ for each vertex), and every vertex has out-degree at most 2.
