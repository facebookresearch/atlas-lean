# OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS as a decision problem

The decision problem OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of its instances, to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
