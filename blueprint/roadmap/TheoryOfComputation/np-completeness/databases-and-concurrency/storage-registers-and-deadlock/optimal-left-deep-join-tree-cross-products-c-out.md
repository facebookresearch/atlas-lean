# Cost of a left-deep join order

The cost $C_{\mathrm{out}}$ of the left-deep tree joining the relations in the order $π$: the sum of the output cardinalities of its joins, $\sum _{k \ge 1} |s_1, \dots, s_{k+1}|$. A pair of relations with no listed selectivity has selectivity $1$ (a cross product).
