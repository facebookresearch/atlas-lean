# REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (out-degree at most 2) is NP-complete

The decision problem REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (out-degree at most 2) belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman (1979), problem [PO1] REGISTER SUFFICIENCY (comment: remains NP-complete even if all vertices of $G$ have out-degree 2 or less).
- R. Sethi, "Complete register allocation problems", SIAM Journal on Computing 4 (1975), 226-248, doi:10.1137/0204020.
