# Output cardinality of a join prefix

The output cardinality $|s_1, \dots, s_{k+1}|$ of joining the relations at positions $0, \dots, k$ of the order $π$: $\prod _{a \le k} |R_{π a}| \cdot \prod _{a < b \le k} f_{π a, π b}$. A pair of relations with no listed selectivity has selectivity $1$ (a cross product).
