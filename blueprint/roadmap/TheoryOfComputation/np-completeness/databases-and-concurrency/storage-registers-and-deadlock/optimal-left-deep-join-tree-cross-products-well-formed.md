# Well-formed instances of OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS

An instance of OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS is well-formed when there is at least one relation, every cardinality is positive, every selectivity entry $(i, j, p, q)$ has $i < j < n$ and $0 < p \le q$, and no pair $(i, j)$ is listed twice.
