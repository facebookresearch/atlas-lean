# OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS is NP-complete

The decision problem OPTIMAL LEFT-DEEP JOIN TREE WITH CROSS PRODUCTS belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- S. Cluet and G. Moerkotte, "On the complexity of generating optimal left-deep processing trees with cross products", in *Database Theory - ICDT '95*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 893, Springer (1995), 54-67, doi:10.1007/3-540-58907-4_6 (NP-hardness for the cost function $C_{\mathrm{out}}$, by a reduction from 3-DIMENSIONAL MATCHING, already for star queries).
- G. Moerkotte, *Building Query Compilers*, Section 3.1 (cardinalities, selectivities and $C_{\mathrm{out}}$) and Section 3.1.6 (the complexity table, "general/tree/star, left-deep, cross products: yes, ASI: NP-hard", credited to Cluet and Moerkotte).
