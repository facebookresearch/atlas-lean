# Free resources after aborting processes

The free amounts after the processes in $A$ are aborted and their resources returned: $B_j - \sum_{q \notin A} r_j(q)$, computed in the natural numbers, so the value is $0$ when the allocations of the remaining processes exceed the bound $B_j$. Well-formed instances rule out such over-allocation, but this definition does not assume well-formedness. Resource bounds, allocations and requests that are not listed count as $0$.
