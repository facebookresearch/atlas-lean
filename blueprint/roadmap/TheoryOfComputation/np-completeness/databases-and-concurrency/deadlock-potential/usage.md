# Resource usage of a partial schedule

For a partial schedule $\sigma$ (a list of process indices, position $t'$ executing the next step of process $\sigma_{t'}$, namely the step numbered by how often $\sigma_{t'}$ occurs before position $t'$), the usage of resource $j$ after $t$ positions is the sum of the amounts $a_j$ of the steps executed at positions $t' < t$.
