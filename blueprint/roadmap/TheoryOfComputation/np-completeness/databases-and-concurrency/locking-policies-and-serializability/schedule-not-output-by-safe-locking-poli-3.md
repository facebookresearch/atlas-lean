# SCHEDULE NOT OUTPUT BY ANY SAFE LOCKING POLICY is NP-complete

The decision problem SCHEDULE NOT OUTPUT BY ANY SAFE LOCKING POLICY belongs to NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

## Sources

- M. Yannakakis, "Serializability by locking", J. ACM 31(2) (1984), 227-244, doi:10.1145/62.322425 (Section 2: the model; Section 5: locking policies, Corollaries 2 and 3, Theorem 6 and Corollary 4: "It is NP-complete to test whether a schedule cannot be produced by any SSR-safe locking policy").
