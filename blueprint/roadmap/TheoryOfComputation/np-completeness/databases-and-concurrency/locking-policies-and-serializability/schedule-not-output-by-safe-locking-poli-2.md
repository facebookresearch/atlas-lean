# SCHEDULE NOT OUTPUT BY ANY SAFE LOCKING POLICY as a decision problem

The decision problem SCHEDULE NOT OUTPUT BY ANY SAFE LOCKING POLICY accepts a bitstring $x$ exactly when $x$ decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of pairs of a natural number and a schedule (a list of transaction-entity-mode triples), to an instance that is well-formed and feasible for SCHEDULE NOT OUTPUT BY ANY SAFE LOCKING POLICY. It rejects every bitstring that does not decode to such an instance.
