# Instances of SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION (project-join view)

An instance of SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION (project-join view) consists of a list of pairs of natural numbers, the tuples $(a, b)$ of $R_1(A, B)$; a list of pairs of natural numbers, the tuples $(b, c)$ of $R_2(B, C)$; and a pair $(a, c)$ of natural numbers, the candidate target tuple $t$ over the attributes $(A, C)$. The candidate target need not be a tuple of the view; well-formedness requires that it is.
