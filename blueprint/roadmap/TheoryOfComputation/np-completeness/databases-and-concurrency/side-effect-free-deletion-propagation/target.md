# Candidate target tuple over $(A, C)$ in SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION (project-join view)

For an instance of SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION (project-join view) whose last component is the pair $(a, c)$, the candidate target tuple $t$ over the attributes $A = 0$ and $C = 2$: value $a$ at $A$, value $c$ at $C$, undefined at $B$. The tuple $t$ need not belong to the view of the instance; well-formedness requires that it does.
