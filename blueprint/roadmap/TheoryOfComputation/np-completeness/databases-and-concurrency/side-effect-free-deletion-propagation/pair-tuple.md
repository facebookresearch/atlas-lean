# Two-attribute tuples in SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION

In SIDE-EFFECT-FREE DELETION PROPAGATION (project-join view), for attributes $x, y$ among $A = 0$, $B = 1$, $C = 2$ and natural numbers $u, v$, the tuple that has value $u$ at attribute $x$, value $v$ at attribute $y$, and is undefined at the remaining attributes. The construction is total: when $x = y$ the first value wins, giving the tuple with value $u$ at $x$ and undefined elsewhere.
