# Step at position `p` of the schedule `σ`, with its transaction in UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY

For an instance `i` of UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY, the step at position `p` of the schedule `σ`, with its transaction: the `k`-th occurrence of `t` executes the `k`-th step of `Tₜ`. Positions beyond the end of the lists read as the default entries `0` and `[]` and `(0, 0)`.
