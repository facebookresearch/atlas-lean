# UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY for two distributed transactions: `σ` is a schedule

For an instance `i` of UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY for two distributed transactions, `σ` is a schedule, that is, it lists every step `(t, p)` of both transactions exactly once, and `(t, p)` occurs before `(t, q)` whenever `p` precedes `q` in `Tₜ`.
