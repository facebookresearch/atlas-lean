# UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY for two distributed transactions is NP-complete

The UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY for two distributed transactions decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: variables that are natural numbers, each assigned to a site, and two transactions $T_0$, $T_1$. Each transaction is a list of steps of the forms $\mathrm{lock}(x)$, $\mathrm{update}(x)$ and $\mathrm{unlock}(x)$ on variables that have a site, with a strict partial order on its steps that is consistent with the list order and totally orders the steps on variables of any one site. Read in the list order, each transaction satisfies the conditions of UNSAFE LOCKING POLICY: for each variable $x$, the transaction either has no step on $x$, or has exactly one $\mathrm{lock}(x)$ and exactly one $\mathrm{unlock}(x)$, the $\mathrm{lock}(x)$ before the $\mathrm{unlock}(x)$, with every $\mathrm{update}(x)$ of the transaction (there may be any number of them, including none) strictly between the two; so a lone $\mathrm{unlock}(x)$ is excluded. Any number of sites may be involved in each transaction. Question: is there a legal schedule that is a total order of all the steps of both transactions, consistent with the partial orders of both, and that is not serializable?
