# Feasible instances of NON-LIVENESS OF FREE CHOICE PETRI NETS

In NON-LIVENESS OF FREE CHOICE PETRI NETS: `P` is not live: some `t ∈ T` and some firing sequence `σ` of transitions of `T` such that no sequence `τ` of transitions of `T` makes `σ τ t` firable at `M₀`. A list entry that is not present (an index beyond the end of its list) reads as $0$.
