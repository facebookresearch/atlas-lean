# Fire in NON-LIVENESS OF FREE CHOICE PETRI NETS

In NON-LIVENESS OF FREE CHOICE PETRI NETS: the marking `M - a + b` after firing the transition `t = ⟨a, b⟩` at the marking `M`, computed place by place in the natural numbers with truncated subtraction; this agrees with ordinary subtraction whenever `t` is enabled at `M`. A list entry that is not present counts as false.
