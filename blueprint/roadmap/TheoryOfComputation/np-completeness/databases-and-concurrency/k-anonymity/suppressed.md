# Suppressed

The image $t(v)$ of row $v$ under the suppressor that suppresses the positions marked by
$\mathrm{sup}$: at a coordinate $a$ with $\mathrm{sup}(v, a)$ true it is the suppression symbol
$*$, and elsewhere it is the row's own symbol in coordinate $a$ (read as $0$ if the row has no
entry $a$).
