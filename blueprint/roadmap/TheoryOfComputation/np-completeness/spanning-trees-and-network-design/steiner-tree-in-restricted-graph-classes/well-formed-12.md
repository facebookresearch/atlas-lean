# Well-formed instances of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most k (fixed k ≥ 1)

An instance of STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) on graphs of thickness at most k (fixed k ≥ 1) is well formed when it is a well-formed STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS instance, every edge has weight `1`, its graph has thickness at most `k`, it has at least one vertex, and the bound `B` is positive.
