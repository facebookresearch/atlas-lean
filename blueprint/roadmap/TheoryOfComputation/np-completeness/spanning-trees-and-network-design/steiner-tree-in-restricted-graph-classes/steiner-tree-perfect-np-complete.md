# STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) for perfect graphs is NP-complete

The STEINER TREE IN GRAPHS (unit weights) for perfect graphs decision problem lies in NP, and every decision problem in NP has a polynomial-time many-one reduction to it.

Instance: a perfect graph `G = (V, E)`, a set `R ⊆ V` of terminals and a positive integer `K`. Question: is there a subtree of `G` containing every vertex of `R` and having at most `K` edges?
