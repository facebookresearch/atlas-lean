# Two identifications of the states with residuals agree

Let $M$ be an accessible DFA whose distinct states have distinct continuation languages. The bijection
from the states of $M$ onto the left quotients of its language, sending each state to the language
accepted from it, equals the composite of any bijection $\varphi$ from the states to the Nerode quotient
with $\varphi(\text{state reached on } x) = [x]$ for every word $x$, followed by the identification of
the Nerode quotient with the left quotients.
