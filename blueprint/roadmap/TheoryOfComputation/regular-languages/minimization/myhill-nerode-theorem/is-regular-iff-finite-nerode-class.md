# Myhill–Nerode theorem, quotient form

A language $L$ is regular, that is, accepted by some
deterministic finite automaton, if and only if $L$ has finitely many Nerode classes.

## Sources

- M. O. Rabin and D. Scott, *Finite automata and their decision problems*, IBM Journal of Research and Development 3(2) (April 1959), 114–125, Theorem 2 (p. 118), the equivalence of (i) and (iii), attributed there to Nerode; Theorem 1
  (p. 117) is Myhill's two-sided version with congruences.
