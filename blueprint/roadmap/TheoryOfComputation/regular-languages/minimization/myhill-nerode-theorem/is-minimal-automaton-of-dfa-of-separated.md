# Accessible separated DFAs are minimal

A DFA that is accessible, and whose distinct states have distinct continuation languages, is a minimal
automaton for its language: it accepts the language, and its number of states equals the number of
Nerode classes of the language, both counted as natural numbers (an infinite set counts as $0$). Its
states correspond one-to-one to the Nerode classes, each state to the class of the words that reach it.
