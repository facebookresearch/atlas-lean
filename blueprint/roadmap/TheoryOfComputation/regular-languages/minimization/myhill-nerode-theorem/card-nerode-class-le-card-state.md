# Nerode classes bound the number of DFA states

Let $L$ be a language with finitely many
Nerode classes, and let $M$ be a DFA with a finite set of states $Q$ that accepts exactly $L$.
Then the number of Nerode classes of $L$ is at most $|Q|$.

## Sources

- M. O. Rabin and D. Scott, *Finite automata and their decision problems*, IBM Journal of Research and Development 3(2) (April 1959), 114–125, proof of Theorem 2 and Corollary 2.1 (p. 118): the number of classes of the Nerode
  relation is the least number of states of an automaton defining the language; this page is the
  lower-bound half.
