# Transporting the isomorphism keeps its values on words

For languages $L_1 = L_2$, a deterministic automaton $M$ and a monoid isomorphism $e$ from the syntactic monoid of $L_1$ to the transition monoid of $M$, the isomorphism obtained by transporting $e$ along the equality sends the syntactic class of a word $x$ for $L_2$ to $e$ of the syntactic class of $x$ for $L_1$. No finiteness of the states is assumed.
