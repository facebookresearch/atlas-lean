# Transporting the isomorphism along an equality of languages

For languages $L_1 = L_2$, a deterministic automaton $M$ and a monoid isomorphism from the syntactic monoid of $L_1$ to the transition monoid of $M$, the transported isomorphism is the corresponding isomorphism from the syntactic monoid of $L_2$ to the transition monoid of $M$. It sends the syntactic class of a word to the same transition as the original. No finiteness of the states is assumed.
