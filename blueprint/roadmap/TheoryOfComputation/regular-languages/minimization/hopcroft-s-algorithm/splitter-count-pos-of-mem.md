# Waiting entries give positive splitter count

Let $\Sigma$ be an alphabet and $Q$ a set. For $q \in Q$, $b \in \Sigma$ and a list $W$ of splitters, the splitter count $\mathrm{cnt}(q, b, W)$ is the number of entries $(C, b')$ of $W$, counted with multiplicity, with $b' = b$ and $q \in C$. If $(D, b)$ occurs in a list $W$ of splitters and $q \in D$, then
$\mathrm{cnt}(q, b, W) > 0$.
