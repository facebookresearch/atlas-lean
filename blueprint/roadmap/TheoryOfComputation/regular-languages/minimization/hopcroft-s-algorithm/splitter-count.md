# Splitter count of a state and letter

Let $\Sigma$ be an alphabet and $Q$ a set. For $q \in Q$, $b \in \Sigma$ and a finite list $W$ of splitters $(C, b')$ with
$C \subseteq Q$ finite and $b' \in \Sigma$, the splitter count $\mathrm{cnt}(q, b, W)$ is the number of entries
$(C, b')$ of $W$ with $b' = b$ and $q \in C$, counted with multiplicity.
