# Products of aperiodic monoids are aperiodic

If monoids $M$ and $N$ are aperiodic, then so is their direct product $M \times N$.

## Sources

- S. Eilenberg, *Automata, Languages, and Machines*, Vol. B, Academic Press, 1976, Chapter V, Example 3.3 (for finite monoids: the aperiodic monoids form a variety, closed under finite direct products); the statement here also applies to infinite monoids.
