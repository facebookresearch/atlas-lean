# The aperiodic finite monoids form a pseudovariety

The class of aperiodic finite monoids, those in which every element $x$ satisfies $x^{n+1} = x^n$ for some $n$, is a pseudovariety: it contains the trivial monoid and is closed under submonoids, quotients by surjective homomorphisms, and finite direct products.

## Sources

- J.-É. Pin, *Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory*, version of 24 March 2025, Chapter XI, Proposition 4.22.
