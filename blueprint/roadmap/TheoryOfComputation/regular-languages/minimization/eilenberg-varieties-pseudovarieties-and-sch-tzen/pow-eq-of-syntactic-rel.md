# A syntactic power identity gives a power identity in the syntactic monoid

For a language $L$, a word $x$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, if $x^{n+1}$ and $x^n$ are syntactically equivalent for $L$ (for all words $u, v$, $u x^{n+1} v \in L$ if and only if $u x^n v \in L$), then the image $\bar x$ of $x$ in the syntactic monoid of $L$ satisfies $\bar x^{n+1} = \bar x^n$.
