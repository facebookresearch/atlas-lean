# Aperiodicity of a finite monoid is uniform

If a finite monoid is aperiodic, then a single exponent $k$ works for every element: $x^{k+1} = x^k$ for all $x$.

## Sources

- J.-É. Pin, *Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory*, version of 24 March 2025, Chapter VI, §2, Proposition 2.1 (a uniform exponent for a finite monoid).
