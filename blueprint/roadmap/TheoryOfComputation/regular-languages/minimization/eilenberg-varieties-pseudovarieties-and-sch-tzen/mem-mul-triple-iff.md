# Splitting a product of three words

For languages $L, K$ and words $U, X, V$, the word $UXV$ belongs to the concatenation $LK$ if and only if the cut falls inside $U$, inside $X$ or inside $V$: either $U = U_1 U_2$ with $U_1 \in L$ and $U_2 X V \in K$, or $X = X_1 X_2$ with $U X_1 \in L$ and $X_2 V \in K$, or $V = V_1 V_2$ with $U X V_1 \in L$ and $V_2 \in K$.
