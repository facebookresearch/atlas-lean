# Syntactic Rel pow of pow eq

A syntactic power identity for a prescribed repeated block: if the `k`-th and `(k+1)`-th
powers of `w` agree in the syntactic monoid of `L`, then a block of `k` copies of `w`
and a block of `k + 1` copies agree in every two-sided context.

## Sources

- Pin, "Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory", Proposition IV.3.2.
