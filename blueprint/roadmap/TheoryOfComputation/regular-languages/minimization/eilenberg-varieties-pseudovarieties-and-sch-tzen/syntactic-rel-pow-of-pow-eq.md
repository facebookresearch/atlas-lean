# Equal powers in the syntactic monoid give a syntactic identity

For a language $L$, a word $w$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, if the image $\bar w$ of $w$ in the syntactic monoid of $L$ satisfies $\bar w^{k+1} = \bar w^k$, then for all words $u$ and $v$, $u\, w^{k+1} v \in L$ if and only if $u\, w^k v \in L$.

- Pin, "Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory", Proposition IV.3.2.
