# Complement preserves aperiodic syntactic monoids

If the syntactic monoid of a language $L$ is aperiodic, then so is the syntactic monoid of its complement; the two syntactic monoids are isomorphic.

## Sources

- J.-É. Pin, *Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory*, version of 24 March 2025, Chapter XI, Proposition 4.22 (background); the statement here is a consequence for the syntactic monoids of a language and its complement.
