# Aperiodicity is preserved by isomorphism

If a monoid $M$ is aperiodic and $N$ is isomorphic to $M$ as a monoid, then $N$ is aperiodic.

## Sources

- S. Eilenberg, *Automata, Languages, and Machines*, Vol. B, Academic Press, 1976, Chapter V, Example 3.3 (for finite monoids: the aperiodic monoids form a variety, closed under isomorphic copies); the statement here also applies to infinite monoids.
