# Star-free languages are regular

Every star-free language, built from the empty language and the one-letter languages by complement, finite union and concatenation, is regular.

## Sources

- M. Sipser, *Introduction to the Theory of Computation*, 3rd ed., Theorems 1.25 and 1.26 (closure of the regular languages under union and concatenation) and Exercise 1.14(a) (closure under complement); regularity of the star-free languages follows by induction on their construction.
