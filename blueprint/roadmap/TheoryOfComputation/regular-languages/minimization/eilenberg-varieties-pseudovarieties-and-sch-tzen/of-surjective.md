# Quotients of aperiodic monoids are aperiodic

If a monoid $M$ is aperiodic and $f : M \to N$ is a surjective monoid homomorphism, then $N$ is aperiodic.

## Sources

- S. Eilenberg, *Automata, Languages, and Machines*, Vol. B, Academic Press, 1976, Chapter V, Example 3.3 (for finite monoids: the aperiodic monoids form a variety, closed under quotients); the statement here also applies to infinite monoids.
