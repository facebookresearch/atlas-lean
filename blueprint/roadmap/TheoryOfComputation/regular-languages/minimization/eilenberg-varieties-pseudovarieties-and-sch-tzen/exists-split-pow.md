# Exists split pow

A split of a repeated block `w ^ (N + 1)` cuts inside one copy: there are `i` full
copies before the cut, a prefix `p` of `w`, the complementary suffix `s` of `w`, and `j`
full copies after, with `i + j + 1 = N + 1`.
