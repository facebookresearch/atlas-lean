# Pseudovarieties of finite monoids

A pseudovariety of finite monoids is a class of finite monoids that contains the trivial monoid (the empty finite product) and is closed under submonoids, under images of surjective monoid homomorphisms, and under direct products of two members. Requiring the trivial monoid excludes the empty class, which would satisfy every closure condition vacuously.
