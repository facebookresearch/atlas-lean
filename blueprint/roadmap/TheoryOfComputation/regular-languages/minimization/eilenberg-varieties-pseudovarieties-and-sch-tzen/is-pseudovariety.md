# Is Pseudovariety

A pseudovariety of finite monoids is a class of finite monoids containing the trivial
monoid (the empty finite product, as `PUnit`) and closed under submonoids, quotients,
and finite direct products. The trivial-monoid clause is the nonemptiness condition:
without it, the constantly-false class would vacuously satisfy every closure property.
