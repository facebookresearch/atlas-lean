# Reiterman's theorem

A class of finite monoids is a [variety of finite monoids](varieties-of-finite-monoids.md) if and only if it is the class of all finite monoids that
satisfy some set of profinite identities $u = v$, where $u$ and $v$ are elements of the free profinite monoid $\widehat{A^*}$ on a finite alphabet $A$
and $M$ satisfies $u = v$ when $\hat\varphi(u) = \hat\varphi(v)$ for every morphism $\varphi : A^* \to M$, with $\hat\varphi$ its uniformly continuous
extension (Reiterman). The same holds for varieties of finite semigroups and of finite ordered monoids and semigroups. For example, the
[aperiodic monoids](aperiodic-finite-monoids.md) are defined by $x^\omega = x^{\omega + 1}$.
