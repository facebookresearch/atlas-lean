# Varieties of finite monoids

A *variety of finite monoids* is a class $\mathbf{V}$ of finite monoids closed under submonoids, quotients and finite direct products; varieties
of finite semigroups are defined in the same way with subsemigroups. A semigroup $S$ *divides* $T$ if $S$ is a quotient of a subsemigroup of $T$,
and the variety generated by a class $C$ consists of the finite semigroups dividing a finite direct product of members of $C$. The smallest variety is the trivial one and the largest is the class of all finite monoids.
