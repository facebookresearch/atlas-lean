# Varieties of finite monoids

A *variety of finite monoids* is a class $\mathbf{V}$ of finite monoids closed under submonoids, quotients and finite direct products (the
empty product being the trivial monoid); equivalently, closed under division and finite products, where $M$ *divides* $N$ if $M$ is a quotient
of a submonoid of $N$. Varieties of finite semigroups are defined in the same way. Examples are the commutative monoids, the
[aperiodic monoids](aperiodic-finite-monoids.md) and the [$\mathcal{J}$-trivial monoids](green-s-relations-and-j-trivial-monoids.md).
