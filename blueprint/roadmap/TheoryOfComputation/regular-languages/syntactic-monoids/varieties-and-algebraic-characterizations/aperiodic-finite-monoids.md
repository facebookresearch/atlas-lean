# Aperiodic finite monoids

A finite monoid $M$ is *aperiodic* if for each $x \in M$ there is an integer $n \ge 0$ with $x^n = x^{n+1}$; equivalently, every subsemigroup of $M$ that is a
group is trivial, or $M$ is $\mathcal{H}$-trivial in the sense of [Green's relations](green-s-relations-and-j-trivial-monoids.md). The finite aperiodic monoids form a
[variety of finite monoids](varieties-of-finite-monoids.md), denoted $\mathbf{A}$.
