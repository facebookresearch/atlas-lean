# Aperiodic finite monoids

A finite monoid $M$ is *aperiodic* if for each $x \in M$ there is an integer $n > 0$ with $x^n = x^{n+1}$. Equivalently, $M$ is
$\mathcal{H}$-trivial, in the sense of [Green's relations](green-s-relations-and-j-trivial-monoids.md). The finite
aperiodic monoids form a [variety of finite monoids](varieties-of-finite-monoids.md), denoted $\mathbf{A}$, defined by the profinite identity
$x^\omega = x^{\omega + 1}$.
