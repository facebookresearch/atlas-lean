# Characterization of locally testable languages

A language is [locally testable](locally-testable-languages.md) if and only if its syntactic semigroup is finite and *locally idempotent and
commutative*: for every idempotent $e$, the local subsemigroup $eSe$ is idempotent and commutative (Brzozowski and Simon; McNaughton).
Consequently it is decidable whether a regular language is locally testable.
