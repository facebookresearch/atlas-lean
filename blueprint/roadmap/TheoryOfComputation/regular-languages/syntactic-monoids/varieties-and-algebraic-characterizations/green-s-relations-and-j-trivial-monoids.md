# Green's relations and $\mathcal{J}$-trivial monoids

Let $S$ be a semigroup and $S^1$ the monoid obtained by adding an identity if $S$ has none. *Green's relations* are the equivalences
$s \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} t \iff s S^1 = t S^1$, $s \mathrel{\mathcal{L}} t \iff S^1 s = S^1 t$, $s \mathrel{\mathcal{J}} t \iff S^1 s S^1 = S^1 t S^1$, and
$s \mathrel{\mathcal{H}} t \iff s \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} t$ and $s \mathrel{\mathcal{L}} t$; that is, $s$ and $t$ generate the same right, left or two-sided ideal. A semigroup
is *$\mathcal{J}$-trivial* (respectively *$\mathcal{R}$-*, *$\mathcal{L}$-*, *$\mathcal{H}$-trivial*) if the corresponding relation is the identity.
