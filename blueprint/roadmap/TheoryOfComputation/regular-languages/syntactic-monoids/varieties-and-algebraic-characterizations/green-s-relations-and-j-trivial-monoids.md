# Green's relations and $\mathcal{J}$-trivial monoids

Let $S$ be a semigroup and $S^1$ the monoid obtained by adding an identity if $S$ has none. *Green's relations* on $S$ are the equivalences
$s \mathrel{\mathcal{L}} t$ if $s = xt$ and $t = ys$ for some $x, y \in S^1$; $s \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} t$ if $s = tx$ and $t = sy$ for some $x, y \in S^1$;
$s \mathrel{\mathcal{J}} t$ if $s = xty$ and $t = usv$ for some $x, y, u, v \in S^1$; and $s \mathrel{\mathcal{H}} t$ if $s \mathrel{\mathcal{R}} t$ and $s \mathrel{\mathcal{L}} t$. A semigroup
is *$\mathcal{J}$-trivial* (respectively *$\mathcal{R}$-*, *$\mathcal{L}$-*, *$\mathcal{H}$-trivial*) if the corresponding relation is the identity. In a finite
semigroup, an $\mathcal{H}$-class containing an idempotent $e$ is a group with identity $e$.
