# The Straubing–Thérien hierarchy is strict

The *Straubing–Thérien hierarchy* classifies the star-free languages of $A^*$ by alternating polynomial closure and Boolean closure: level $0$
consists of $\emptyset$ and $A^*$; level $n + 1/2$ consists of the finite unions of products $L_0 a_1 L_1 \cdots a_k L_k$ with letters $a_i$ and languages $L_i$ of
level $n$; and level $n + 1$ consists of the Boolean combinations of languages of level $n + 1/2$. Level $1$ consists of the
[piecewise testable languages](piecewise-testable-languages.md). If $A$ has at least two letters, the hierarchy is strict: for every $n$ there are languages of
level $n + 1$ that are not of level $n + 1/2$, and languages of level $n + 1/2$ that are not of level $n$; this was first proved for the dot-depth
hierarchy (Brzozowski and Knast).
