# The Straubing–Thérien hierarchy is infinite

The *Straubing–Thérien hierarchy* classifies the star-free languages over an alphabet $A$ by alternating Boolean operations and concatenation:
level $0$ consists of $\emptyset$ and $A^*$; level $n + 1/2$ consists of the finite unions of products $L_0 a_1 L_1 \cdots a_k L_k$ with $a_i \in A$ and
$L_i$ of level $n$; and level $n + 1$ consists of the Boolean combinations of languages of level $n + 1/2$. Level $1$ consists of the
[piecewise testable languages](piecewise-testable-languages.md). The hierarchy is infinite: no level contains all star-free languages (Brzozowski
and Knast).
