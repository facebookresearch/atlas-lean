# Membership in the levels of the Straubing–Thérien hierarchy

Given a half-integer $n$ and a star-free language $L$, by a finite automaton, can one decide whether $L$ belongs to level $n$ of the
[Straubing–Thérien hierarchy](the-straubing-therien-hierarchy-is-infinite.md)? Level $0$ is trivial and level $1$ is decidable by
[Simon's theorem](simon-s-theorem-on-piecewise-testable-languages.md).

## What would settle it

An algorithm that, for every level $n$, decides membership of a given regular language in level $n$, or a proof that some level is undecidable.

## Status

The cited notes state the problem as a major open problem; this page does not certify its present status.

## Sources

- J.-É. Pin, *Mathematical Foundations of Automata Theory*, lecture notes, version of March 24, 2025, <https://www.irif.fr/~jep/PDF/MPRI/MPRI.pdf>: Section XX.1, the problem stated after Theorem XX.1.1.
