# Membership in the levels of the Straubing–Thérien hierarchy

Given a half-integer $n$ and a regular language $L$, given by a finite automaton, can one decide whether $L$ belongs to level $n$ of the
[Straubing–Thérien hierarchy](the-straubing-therien-hierarchy-is-infinite.md)? Levels $0$, $1/2$, $1$ and $3/2$ are decidable, level $1$ by
[Simon's theorem](simon-s-theorem-on-piecewise-testable-languages.md).

## Depends on

- [Simon's theorem on piecewise testable languages](simon-s-theorem-on-piecewise-testable-languages.md)
- [The Straubing–Thérien hierarchy is strict](the-straubing-therien-hierarchy-is-infinite.md)

## What would settle it

An algorithm that, for every level $n$, decides membership of a given regular language in level $n$, or a proof that some level is undecidable.

## Status

The cited chapter calls the decidability of each level the main question and states that the decidability of level 2 is still open at the time of writing; this page does not certify the present status of any level.
