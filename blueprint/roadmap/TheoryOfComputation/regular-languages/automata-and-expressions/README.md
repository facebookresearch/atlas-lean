# Automata and regular expressions

DFA, NFA, ε-NFA and two-way automata, sequential transducers and Mealy and Moore machines, regular expressions, Thompson's construction and Kleene's theorem, star height, and closure of regular languages under homomorphism, quotient, shuffle and substitution.

Determinisation by the subset construction is Rabin and Scott's Definition 11 and Theorem 11
(M. O. Rabin and D. Scott, *Finite automata and their decision problems*, IBM Journal of Research and Development 3(2) (April 1959), 114–125, p. 121): the deterministic automaton
whose states are the sets of states of a nondeterministic automaton accepts the same language. It
is used, for example, in the closure of regular languages under homomorphic images and in the
equivalence test for regular expressions.
