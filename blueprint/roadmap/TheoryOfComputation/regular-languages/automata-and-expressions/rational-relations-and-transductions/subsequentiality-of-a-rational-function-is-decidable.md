# Subsequentiality of a rational function is decidable

Given a transducer, in the sense of [rational relations and transducers](rational-relations-and-transducers.md), one can decide whether the
transduction it realizes is a [subsequential function](sequential-and-subsequential-functions.md), and whether it is a sequential function
(Choffrut). After checking functionality and passing to an unambiguous representation, the test is the twinning property of the states.
