# From the set-valued-transition form to a relation-valued-transition automaton

A nondeterministic automaton on finite words with set-valued transitions (no finiteness of the states is assumed) becomes a relation-valued-transition automaton with the same start and accepting sets, in which there is a transition from $s$ to $s'$ with letter $a$ exactly when $s'$ belongs to the step from $s$ with $a$.
