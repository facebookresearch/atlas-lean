# From the set-valued-transition form to a relation-valued-transition automaton, with empty moves

A finite-word automaton with empty moves with set-valued transitions becomes a relation-valued-transition automaton with the same start and accepting sets, in which there is a transition from $s$ to $s'$ with label $a$ (a letter, or the empty label for an empty move) exactly when $s'$ belongs to the step from $s$ with $a$.
