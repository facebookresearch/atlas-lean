# From a relation-valued-transition automaton with empty moves to the set-valued-transition form

A finite-word automaton with empty moves with relation-valued transitions becomes a set-valued-transition automaton with the same start and accepting sets, whose step from $s$ with $a$ (a letter, or the empty label for an empty move) is the set of states $s'$ with a transition from $s$ to $s'$ labelled $a$.
