# Rational series

A series in $S\langle\langle \Sigma^* \rangle\rangle$ over a [semiring](semirings-and-formal-power-series.md) $S$ is *rational* if it can be built from the
monomials $s a$, with $s \in S$ and $a \in \Sigma \cup \{\varepsilon\}$, by finitely many applications of sum, Cauchy product and iteration, the last applied
only to proper series. Over the Boolean semiring the supports of the rational series are the rational languages.
