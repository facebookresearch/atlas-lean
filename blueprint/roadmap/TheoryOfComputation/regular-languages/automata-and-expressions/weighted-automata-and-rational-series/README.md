# Weighted automata and rational series

A weighted automaton over a semiring assigns to every word the sum, over its paths, of the products of the weights along
them; its behavior is a formal power series on the free monoid. The recognizable series are exactly the rational ones
(Kleene–Schützenberger), equivalence is decidable over fields but undecidable over the max-plus and min-plus semirings, and
probabilistic automata recognize regular languages at isolated cut-points while their emptiness and value-1 problems are
undecidable. Boolean weights give back finite automata and [Kleene's theorem](../kleene-s-theorem/README.md).
