# Rational series of bounded dimension

Let $K$ be a semiring and $\Sigma$ a finite alphabet. $\mathrm{Rat}_{\le n}$ is the set of series $f:\Sigma^*\to K$
defined by a linear representation $(\lambda,\mu,\rho)$ of dimension at most $n$, that is
$f(w)=\lambda\,\mu(w)\,\rho$ with $\lambda\in K^{1\times n}$, $\rho\in K^{n\times1}$ and a monoid morphism $\mu:\Sigma^*\to K^{n\times n}$;
equivalently, the behaviors of weighted automata with at most $n$ states. By the
Kleene–Schützenberger theorem, the union of the $\mathrm{Rat}_{\le n}$ is the set of rational series.
