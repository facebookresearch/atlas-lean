# Pumped words lie in a language containing x y* z

Words are finite lists over an alphabet, and a language is a set of words. If a language $L$ contains every word of the form $x y^k z$, that is, the concatenation of the single word $x$, any number $k \ge 0$ of copies of $y$, and the single word $z$, then $x y^i z \in L$ for every $i \ge 0$.
