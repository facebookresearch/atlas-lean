# Sharpness of the finite-alphabet hypothesis

Let $\alpha$ be an infinite alphabet. There is a regular language over $\alpha$ that is the language of no regular
expression over $\alpha$; for instance the language of all one-letter words is regular, while a regular expression
mentions only finitely many letters.

## Sources

- M. Sipser, *Introduction to the Theory of Computation*, 3rd ed., Cengage Learning, 2013, Theorem 1.54 (for a finite alphabet).
