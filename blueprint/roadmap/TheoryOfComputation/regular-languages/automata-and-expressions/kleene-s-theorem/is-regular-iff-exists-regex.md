# Kleene's theorem

Let $\alpha$ be a finite alphabet and $L$ a language over $\alpha$. Then $L$ is regular (accepted by a deterministic
automaton with finitely many states) if and only if $L$ is the language of some regular expression over $\alpha$.

## Sources

- M. Sipser, *Introduction to the Theory of Computation*, 3rd ed., Cengage Learning, 2013, Theorem 1.54.
