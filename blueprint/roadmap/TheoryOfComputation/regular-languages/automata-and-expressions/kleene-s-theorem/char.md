# Single-letter languages are regex-denoted

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet. A language over $\alpha$ is regex-denoted if it is the language matched by some regular expression over $\alpha$ (regular expressions being built from $0$, $1$ and single letters by sum, concatenation and Kleene star). For every letter $a \in \alpha$, the language $\{a\}$ consisting of the one-letter
word $a$ is regex-denoted (by the expression $a$).
