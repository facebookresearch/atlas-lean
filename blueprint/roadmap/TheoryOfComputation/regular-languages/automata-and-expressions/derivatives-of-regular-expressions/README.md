# Derivatives of regular expressions

The derivative of a regular expression with respect to a letter denotes the left quotient of its language. Up to associativity,
commutativity and idempotence of sum a regular expression has finitely many derivatives, which form a deterministic automaton for
its language; Antimirov's partial derivatives split sums into sets and give a nondeterministic automaton. Both give decision
procedures for the equivalence of regular expressions.
