# The shuffle of two regular languages is regular

Let $\alpha$ be an alphabet and $L, M$ regular languages over $\alpha$. Then their shuffle, the set of all words
$w$ that are an interleaving of some $u \in L$ and some $v \in M$ (that is, the letters of $w$ can be split into
two subsequences, one spelling $u$ and the other $v$), is regular.
