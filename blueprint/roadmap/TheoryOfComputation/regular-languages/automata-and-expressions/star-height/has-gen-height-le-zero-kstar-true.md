# The language of all-true words is star-free in the generalized sense

Over the alphabet $\{\text{true}, \text{false}\}$, the language of all words consisting only of the letter true, the star of the one-letter word true, has generalized star height $0$: it is the complement of the set of words containing the letter false, $\Sigma^* \, \text{false} \, \Sigma^*$, where $\Sigma^*$ is itself the complement of the empty language.
