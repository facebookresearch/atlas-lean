# Satisfiability of a modal μ-calculus formula is EXPTIME-complete

[Modal μ-calculus satisfiability](mu-calculus-sat-dec.md), with the formula given by its token
encoding (one token per connective), is in EXP, and every decision problem in EXP reduces to it
by a polynomial-time many-one reduction.

## Sources

- Dexter Kozen, "Results on the propositional μ-calculus", Theoretical Computer Science 27 (1983), 333-354.
- E. Allen Emerson and Charanjit S. Jutla, "Tree automata, mu-calculus and determinacy", Proceedings of the 32nd Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 1991), 368-377.
