# The END-OF-THE-LINE graph

Let $V$ be a set and let $S, P : V \to V$ be maps. The END-OF-THE-LINE graph of $S$ and $P$ is
the directed graph on $V$ with an edge from $x$ to $y$ exactly when $S(x) = y$ and $P(y) = x$
(Daskalakis, Goldberg and Papadimitriou, SIAM J. Comput. 2009). Every vertex $x$
has at most one out-neighbour, $S(x)$, and at most one in-neighbour, $P(x)$.
