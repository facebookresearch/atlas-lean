# The model checking, satisfiability and validity problems of the linear modal μ-calculus are PSPACE-complete

Each of the three decision problems of the linear μ-calculus,
[satisfiability](linear-mu-sat-dec.md), [validity](linear-mu-valid-dec.md) and
[model checking](linear-mu-model-check-dec.md), with the formula given by its token encoding
(one token per connective), is in PSPACE, and every decision problem in PSPACE reduces to it by a
polynomial-time many-one reduction, as for linear temporal logic.

## Sources

- Moshe Y. Vardi, "A temporal fixpoint calculus", Proceedings of the 15th ACM SIGPLAN-SIGACT Symposium on Principles of Programming Languages (POPL 1988), 250-259.
- A. Prasad Sistla and Edmund M. Clarke, "The complexity of propositional linear temporal logics", Journal of the ACM 32 (1985), 733-749 (the analogous result for linear temporal logic).
