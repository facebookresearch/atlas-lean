# Parikh vectors add over concatenation

For an alphabet $T$ and a word $w$ over $T$, the Parikh vector of $w$ is the vector in $\mathbb{N}^T$ whose entry at each letter $a$ is the number of occurrences of $a$ in $w$.
For all words $u$ and $v$ over $T$, the Parikh vector of the concatenation $uv$ is the sum of the
Parikh vectors of $u$ and of $v$.

## Sources

- R. J. Parikh, "On context-free languages", *Journal of the ACM* 13 (1966), 570–581.
