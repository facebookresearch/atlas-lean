# Duplicate periods get independent coefficients

Let $T$ be a set and $p : T \to \mathbb{N}$. The linear combination of the period list $p, p$ with the
coefficient list $1, 0$ is $1 \cdot p + 0 \cdot p = p$ (not $2p$): coefficients are attached to positions in
the list, not to period values.

- R. J. Parikh, "On context-free languages", *Journal of the ACM* 13 (1966), 570–581.
