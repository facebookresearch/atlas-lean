# Chomsky normal form for context-free languages

Let $T$ be an alphabet and $L$ a context-free language over $T$. Then $L$ is generated by a context-free
grammar $G$ with terminals $T$ in Chomsky normal form: every production of $G$ has the form $A \to BC$ with
$B, C$ nonterminals, $A \to a$ with $a \in T$, or $S \to \varepsilon$ with $S$ the initial nonterminal; and
either $S$ occurs on the right side of no production, or no production has empty right side.
