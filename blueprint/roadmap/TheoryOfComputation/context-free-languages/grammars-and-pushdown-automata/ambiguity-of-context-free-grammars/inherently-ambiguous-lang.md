# A classical inherently ambiguous context-free language

The example language is the language

$$\{a^i b^j c^k : i, j, k \in \mathbb{N},\ i = j \text{ or } j = k\}$$

over the three-letter alphabet $\{a, b, c\}$, a classical example of an inherently ambiguous context-free language.

## Sources

- S. Ginsburg, The Mathematical Theory of Context-Free Languages, McGraw-Hill, 1966; J. E. Hopcroft and J. D. Ullman, Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation, Addison-Wesley, 1979.
