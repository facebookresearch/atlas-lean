# End-marker encodings form a regular language

Let $T$ be an alphabet and $\dashv$ an end-marker letter not in $T$. The language $\{w\dashv : w \in T^*\}$
of the end-marker encodings of the words over $T$ is a regular language over $T \cup \{\dashv\}$.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft, R. Motwani and J. D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd ed., Addison-Wesley, 2006, Section 6.4.
