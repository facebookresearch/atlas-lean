# Injectivity of the end-marker encoding

Let $T$ be an alphabet and $\dashv$ an end-marker letter not in $T$, and let the end-marker encoding of a word $w$ over $T$ be $w\dashv$.
The end-marker encoding is injective: if $w\dashv = w'\dashv$ for words $w, w'$ over $T$, then $w = w'$.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft, R. Motwani and J. D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd ed., Addison-Wesley, 2006, Section 6.4.
