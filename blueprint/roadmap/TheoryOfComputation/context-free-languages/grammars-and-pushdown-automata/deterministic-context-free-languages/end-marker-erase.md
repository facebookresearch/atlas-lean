# End-marker erasure

Let $T$ be an alphabet and $\dashv$ an end-marker letter not in $T$, and let the end-marker encoding of a word $w$ over $T$ be $w\dashv$.
End-marker erasure sends each letter $a \in T$ to the one-letter word $a$ and the end marker $\dashv$
to the empty word.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft, R. Motwani and J. D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd ed., Addison-Wesley, 2006, Section 6.4 (deterministic pushdown automata, acceptance by empty stack with an end marker).
