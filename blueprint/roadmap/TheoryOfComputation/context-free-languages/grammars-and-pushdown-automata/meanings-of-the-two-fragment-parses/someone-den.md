# Meaning of the quantifier someone

The fragment is a small context-free grammar for sentences such as "everybody admires someone", whose parse trees are compiled into terms of a simply typed lambda calculus over two base types, entities $e$ and truth values $t$. A model consists of a set $D$ of entities, a predicate "person" on $D$ and a binary relation "admire" on $D$. In a model, the word "someone" denotes the generalized quantifier of type $(e \to t) \to t$ sending a property $P$ of entities to the proposition that some entity $y$ is a person and satisfies $P(y)$.
