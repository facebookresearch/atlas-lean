# Meaning of the verb admires

The fragment is a small context-free grammar for sentences such as "everybody admires someone", whose parse trees are compiled into terms of a simply typed lambda calculus over two base types, entities $e$ and truth values $t$. A model consists of a set $D$ of entities, a predicate "person" on $D$ and a binary relation "admire" on $D$. In a model, the word "admires" denotes the function of type $e \to e \to t$ sending entities $x$ and $y$ to the proposition that $x$ admires $y$.
