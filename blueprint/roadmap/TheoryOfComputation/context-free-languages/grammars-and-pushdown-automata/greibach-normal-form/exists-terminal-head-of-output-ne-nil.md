# Nonempty Greibach productions begin with a terminal

In Greibach normal form, every nonempty production output begins
with a terminal followed by nonterminals.

## Sources

- J. E. Hopcroft and J. D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, Addison-Wesley, 1979 (Greibach normal form); M. A. Harrison, *Introduction to Formal Language Theory*, Addison-Wesley, 1978, Chapter 4.
