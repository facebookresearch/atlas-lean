# The cylinder mass is a product of Bernoulli masses

For a real number $p$ with $0 \le p \le 1$, let $\beta_p$ be the Bernoulli($p$) measure on the two
Booleans (mass $p$ on true, $1 - p$ on false) and let $\mu_p$ be the product measure on $\Omega$
under which the coordinates $\omega(a)$ are independent with law $\beta_p$. For a finite set $s$ of
addresses and an assignment $t$ of a Boolean to every address, the cylinder mass of $s$ and $t$ with
parameter $p$ equals $\prod_{a \in s} \beta_p(\{t(a)\})$.
