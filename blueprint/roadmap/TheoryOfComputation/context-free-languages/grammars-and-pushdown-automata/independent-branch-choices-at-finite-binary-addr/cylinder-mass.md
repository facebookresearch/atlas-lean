# Product mass of a cylinder

For a real number $p$, a finite set $s$ of binary addresses and an assignment $t$ of a Boolean to
every address, the cylinder mass is the product $\prod_{a \in s} m(a)$ in $[0, \infty]$, where
$m(a)$ is $p$ when $t(a)$ is true and $1 - p$ when $t(a)$ is false, each converted to an extended
nonnegative real (a negative value becomes $0$).
