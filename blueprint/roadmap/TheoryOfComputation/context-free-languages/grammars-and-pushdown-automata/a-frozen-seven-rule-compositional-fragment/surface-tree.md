# The surface-scope parse

The surface parse of "everybody admires someone" in the seven-rule fragment is the derivation tree that combines the transitive verb "admires" with the object "someone" into a verb phrase VP, and then combines the subject "everybody" with that verb phrase into a sentence S. Its meaning is $\mathrm{everybody}(\lambda x.\ \mathrm{someone}(\lambda y.\ \mathrm{admires}\ x\ y))$.
