# Semantic action of the rule S → SP Obj

Over the base types $e$ (entities) and $t$ (truth values), write $P = e \to t$,
$Q = (e \to t) \to t$ and $R = e \to e \to t$; the categories S, Subj, Obj, TV, VP and SP have types
$t$, $Q$, $Q$, $R$, $P$ and $P$. For terms $s : P$ and $q : Q$ in any context, the action of
$S \to \mathrm{SP}\ \mathrm{Obj}$ is the application $q\, s : t$: the object's quantifier takes
scope over the predicate.
