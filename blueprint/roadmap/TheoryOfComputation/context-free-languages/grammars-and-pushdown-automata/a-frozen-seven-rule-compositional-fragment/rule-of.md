# The seven rules of the compositional fragment

The fragment's rules, numbered $0$ to $6$, are $S \to \mathrm{Subj}\ \mathrm{VP}$,
$\mathrm{VP} \to \mathrm{TV}\ \mathrm{Obj}$, $S \to \mathrm{SP}\ \mathrm{Obj}$,
$\mathrm{SP} \to \mathrm{Subj}\ \mathrm{TV}$, $\mathrm{Subj} \to$ everybody, $\mathrm{Obj} \to$
someone and $\mathrm{TV} \to$ admires. Rules $0$ to $3$ are binary and rules $4$ to $6$ are
terminal.
