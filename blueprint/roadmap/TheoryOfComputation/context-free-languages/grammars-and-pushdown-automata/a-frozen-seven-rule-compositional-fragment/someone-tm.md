# Lexical term of someone

Over the base types $e$ (entities) and $t$ (truth values), write $P = e \to t$,
$Q = (e \to t) \to t$ and $R = e \to e \to t$; the categories S, Subj, Obj, TV, VP and SP have types
$t$, $Q$, $Q$, $R$, $P$ and $P$. The lexical term of someone is the variable in slot $1$ of the
context $[Q, Q, R]$; it has type $Q$, the same type as the term of everybody, but it is a different
variable.
