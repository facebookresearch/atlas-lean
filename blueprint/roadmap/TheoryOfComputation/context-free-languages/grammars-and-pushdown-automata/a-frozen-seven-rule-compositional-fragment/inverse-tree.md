# The inverse-scope parse

The inverse parse of "everybody admires someone" in the seven-rule fragment is the derivation tree that combines the subject "everybody" with the transitive verb "admires" into a sentence missing its object (SP), and then combines that with the object "someone" into a sentence S. Its meaning is $\mathrm{someone}(\lambda y.\ \mathrm{everybody}(\lambda x.\ \mathrm{admires}\ x\ y))$. This bracketing is a deliberately artificial scope alternative, not a claim about English syntax.
