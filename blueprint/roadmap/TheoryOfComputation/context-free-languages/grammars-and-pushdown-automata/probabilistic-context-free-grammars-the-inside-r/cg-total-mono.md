# The total tree weight grows with the depth bound

A one-nonterminal tree is either a leaf or a node with two subtrees; its weight for real numbers $p$
and $q$ is $q$ for a leaf and $p$ times the weights of the two subtrees for a node. Write $W_h$ for
the sum of the weights of all one-nonterminal trees of depth at most $h$. If $p \ge 0$ and
$q \ge 0$, then $W_h \le W_{h+1}$ for every $h$.
