# Yield of a derivation tree

A derivation tree is either a leaf labelled by a nonterminal $A$ and a terminal $a$, or a node labelled by nonterminals $A, B, C$ with a left and a right subtree. Its yield is the one-letter word $a$ for a leaf, and the concatenation of the yields of the left and right subtrees, in that order, for a node.
