# Bounded-depth binary trees grow with the bound

A one-nonterminal tree is a finite binary tree: either a leaf or a node with a left and a right subtree. Its depth is $1$ for a leaf and one more than the larger depth of the subtrees for a node. For reals $p$ and $q$, its weight is $q$ for a leaf and $p$ times the weights of the two subtrees for a node. For every $h$, every one-nonterminal tree of depth at most $h$ also has depth at most $h + 1$: the finite set of trees of depth at most $h$ is contained in the set for $h + 1$.
