# Total weight of one-nonterminal trees of bounded depth

A one-nonterminal tree is either a leaf or a node with two subtrees; its weight for real numbers $p$
and $q$ is $q$ for a leaf and $p$ times the weights of the two subtrees for a node. For a natural
number $h$, the total weight is the sum of the weights of all one-nonterminal trees of depth at most
$h$.
