# Both parses yield aaa

The ambiguous example grammar has one nonterminal $A$, which is its start symbol, one terminal $a$, and the two rules $A \to A\,A$ and $A \to a$. Both the right-associated tree, grouping $a\,(a\,a)$, and the left-associated tree, grouping $(a\,a)\,a$, have yield exactly the three-letter word $a\,a\,a$.
