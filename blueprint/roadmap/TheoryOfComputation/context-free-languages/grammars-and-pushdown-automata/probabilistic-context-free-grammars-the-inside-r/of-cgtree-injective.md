# One-nonterminal trees embed injectively as derivation trees

Map each one-nonterminal binary tree to the derivation tree of the grammar $A \to A A$, $A \to a$
with the same shape: a leaf goes to the leaf $A \to a$, and a node with subtrees $l$ and $r$ goes to
an application of $A \to A A$ to the images of $l$ and $r$. This map is injective.
