# Binary tree weights are nonnegative

A one-nonterminal tree is a finite binary tree: either a leaf or a node with a left and a right subtree. Its depth is $1$ for a leaf and one more than the larger depth of the subtrees for a node. For reals $p$ and $q$, its weight is $q$ for a leaf and $p$ times the weights of the two subtrees for a node. If $p \ge 0$ and $q \ge 0$, then every one-nonterminal tree has nonnegative weight.
