# No binary tree has depth zero

A one-nonterminal tree is a finite binary tree: either a leaf or a node with a left and a right subtree. Its depth is $1$ for a leaf and one more than the larger depth of the subtrees for a node. For reals $p$ and $q$, its weight is $q$ for a leaf and $p$ times the weights of the two subtrees for a node. The set of one-nonterminal trees of depth at most $0$ is empty, since every tree has depth at least $1$.
