# Shapes and example derivation trees are in bijection

The ambiguous example grammar has one nonterminal $A$, which is its start symbol, one terminal $a$, and the two rules $A \to A\,A$ and $A \to a$. Turning a derivation tree of this grammar into its shape as a one-nonterminal binary tree and then back into a derivation tree gives the original tree. Together with the converse identity, the two maps are inverse bijections.
