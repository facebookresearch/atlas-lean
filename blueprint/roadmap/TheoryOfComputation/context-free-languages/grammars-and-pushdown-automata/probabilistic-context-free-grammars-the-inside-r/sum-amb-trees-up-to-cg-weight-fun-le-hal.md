# The Chi and Geman bound for the normalized example grammar

The ambiguous example grammar has one nonterminal $A$, which is its start symbol, one terminal $a$, and the two rules $A \to A\,A$ and $A \to a$. The depth of a leaf is $1$, and the depth of a node is one more than the larger depth of its subtrees. Give $A \to a$ the weight $1/3$ and $A \to A\,A$ the weight $2/3$; these weights are normalized. Then for every $h$, the valid derivation trees of the grammar of depth at most $h$ have total weight at most $1/2$.
