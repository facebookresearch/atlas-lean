# Cutoff sums for p = 1/2 tend to one

For reals $p, q$, the cutoff sums $S_h$ are defined by $S_0 = 0$, $S_1 = 0$ and $S_{h+1} = q + p\,S_h^2$ for $h \ge 1$; by the index shift, $S_{h+1}$ is the total weight of the one-nonterminal trees of depth at most $h$, where a leaf has weight $q$ and a node weight $p$ times the weights of its subtrees. For $p = q = 1/2$, the sequence $S_h$ converges to $1$ as $h \to \infty$.
