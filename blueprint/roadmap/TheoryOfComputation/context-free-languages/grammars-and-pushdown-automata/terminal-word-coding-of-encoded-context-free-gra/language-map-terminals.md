# The language of a coded grammar

For terminal alphabets $T$ and $T'$ with decidable equality, a map $\varphi$ from $T$ to words over $T'$, and an encoded context-free grammar $g$ over $T$, the grammar obtained by replacing every terminal $a$ by the word $\varphi(a)$ generates exactly the image of the language of $g$ under the word homomorphism extending $\varphi$.
