# Injective coding preserves and reflects a common word

For terminal alphabets $T$ and $T'$ with decidable equality, a map $\varphi$ from $T$ to words over $T'$ with injective induced word homomorphism, and encoded context-free grammars $g_1$ and $g_2$ over $T$, the coded grammars generate a common word if and only if $g_1$ and $g_2$ generate a common word.
