# Coding a sentential form

For a map $\varphi$ from terminals of $T$ to words over $T'$, the coded form of a sentential form $u$ (a list of terminals and nonterminals, the nonterminals numbered by natural numbers) is obtained by replacing each terminal $a$ by the terminals of the word $\varphi(a)$ and keeping each nonterminal, concatenating the results in order.
