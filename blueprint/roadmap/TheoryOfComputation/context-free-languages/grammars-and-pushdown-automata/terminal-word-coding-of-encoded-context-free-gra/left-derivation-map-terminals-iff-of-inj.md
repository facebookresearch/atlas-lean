# Leftmost derivations under injective coding

Let $T$ have decidable equality, let $\varphi$ send terminals of $T$ to words over $T'$ with injective induced word homomorphism $h$, and let $g$ be an encoded context-free grammar over $T$. For a sentential form $u$, a terminal word $w$ and a list of rules $\rho$, the coded grammar has a leftmost derivation from the coded form of $u$ to $h(w)$ with rule list the coded rules of $\rho$ if and only if $g$ has a leftmost derivation from $u$ to $w$ with rule list $\rho$.
