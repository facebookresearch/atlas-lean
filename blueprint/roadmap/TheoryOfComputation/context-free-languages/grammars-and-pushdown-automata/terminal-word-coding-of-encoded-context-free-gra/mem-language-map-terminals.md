# Membership in the language of a coded grammar

For terminal alphabets $T$ and $T'$ with decidable equality, a map $\varphi$ from $T$ to words over $T'$ with induced word homomorphism $h$, an encoded context-free grammar $g$ over $T$ and a word $w$ over $T'$, $w$ is generated by the coded grammar if and only if $w = h(u)$ for some word $u$ generated by $g$.
