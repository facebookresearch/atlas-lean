# Correctness of the Earley recognizer

Let $G$ be a context-free grammar with terminals $T$. For every word $w$ over $T$, the Earley recognizer
of $G$ accepts $w$ if and only if $w$ belongs to the language of $G$.

## Sources

- J. Earley, "An efficient context-free parsing algorithm", *Communications of the ACM* 13 (1970), 94–102.
