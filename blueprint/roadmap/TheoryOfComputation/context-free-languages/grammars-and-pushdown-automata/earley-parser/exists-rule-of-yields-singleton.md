# A yield from one nonterminal starts with one of its productions

Let $G$ be a context-free grammar with terminals $T$, $A$ a nonterminal of $G$ and $w$ a word over $T$.
If the one-symbol sentential form $A$ yields $w$ in $G$, then $G$ has a production $A \to \gamma$ such
that $\gamma$ yields $w$.
