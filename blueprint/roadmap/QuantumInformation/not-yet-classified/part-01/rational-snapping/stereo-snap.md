# Stereographic snap to the rational sphere

For $w \in \mathbb Q^{n+1}$ and an index $k$, let $s = 1$ if $w_k \ge 0$ and $s = -1$ otherwise, let $t_j = w_j/(1 + s w_k)$ for $j \ne k$ and $t_k = 0$, and let $r = \sum_j t_j^2$. The stereographic snap of $w$ is the vector with $k$-th coordinate $s(1 - r)/(1 + r)$ and $j$-th coordinate $2t_j/(1 + r)$ for $j \ne k$: projection from the pole $-s e_k$ followed by exact inverse projection over $\mathbb Q$.
