# Rational snapping on the three-sphere

Let $z$ be a unit vector in $\mathbb{R}^4$ and $w \in \mathbb{Q}^4$ with $|w_j - z_j| \le \delta$ for every $j$, where $\delta \le 1/8$. The stereographic snap of $w$ (project from the pole opposite in sign to a largest coordinate of $w$, then invert exactly over $\mathbb{Q}$) lies within distance $4\delta$ of $z$.
