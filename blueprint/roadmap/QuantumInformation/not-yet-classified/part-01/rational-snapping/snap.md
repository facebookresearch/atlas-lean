# Rational stereographic snapping

For a rational vector $w \in \mathbb Q^{n+1}$, let $k$ be the first index of largest $|w_k|$ and $s = 1$ if $w_k \ge 0$, else $s = -1$. Project from the pole $-s\,e_k$: $t_j = w_j/(1 + s w_k)$ for $j \ne k$, $t_k = 0$, and $r = \sum_j t_j^2$. The snapped vector has coordinates $s(1 - r)/(1 + r)$ at $k$ and $2 t_j/(1 + r)$ at $j \ne k$.
