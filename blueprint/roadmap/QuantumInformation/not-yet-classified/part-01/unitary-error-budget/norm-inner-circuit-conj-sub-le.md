# Error budget for projected measurements of unitary circuits

Let $U_1, \dots, U_T$ and $V_1, \dots, V_T$ be unitary matrices with $\|U_i - V_i\| \le \varepsilon_i$, let $U = U_T\cdots U_1$ and $V = V_T\cdots V_1$, let $P$ be an orthogonal projection, and let $\psi$ be a unit vector. Then

$$\big|\langle \psi, U^* P U\psi\rangle - \langle \psi, V^* P V\psi\rangle\big| \le 2\sum_i \varepsilon_i.$$
