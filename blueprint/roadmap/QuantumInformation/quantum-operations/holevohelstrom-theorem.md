# Holevo–Helstrom theorem

Let $\rho_0, \rho_1$ be density operators and $\lambda \in [0,1]$. The maximum, over binary measurements, of
the probability of correctly identifying which state was prepared when $\rho_0$ is prepared with probability
$\lambda$ and $\rho_1$ with probability $1-\lambda$ equals

$$\frac12 + \frac12\big\|\lambda\rho_0 - (1-\lambda)\rho_1\big\|_1,$$

and it is attained by the projective measurement onto the positive part of $\lambda\rho_0 - (1-\lambda)\rho_1$
and its complement.
