# The quantum Fourier transform matrix is symmetric

The quantum Fourier transform matrix is symmetric: $A_q^{T} = A_q$, since its $(a, c)$ entry
$q^{-1/2} e^{2\pi i a c/q}$ depends only on the product $a c$. This is why the transformation
can be described either by rows or by columns, as in Shor's equation (4.1).

## Sources

- P. W. Shor, *Polynomial-time algorithms for prime factorization and discrete logarithms on a quantum computer*, SIAM J. Comput. 26 (1997), 1484–1509, arXiv:quant-ph/9508027, Section 4, equation (4.1).
