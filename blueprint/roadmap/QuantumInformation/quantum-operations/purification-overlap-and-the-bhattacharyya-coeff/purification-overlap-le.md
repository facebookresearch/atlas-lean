# Purification overlap bound

Let `X` and `E` be finite types and let `A B : X → E → ℂ` be amplitude
arrays whose rows have squared norms `∑ e, |A x e| ^ 2 = p x` and `∑ e, |B x
e| ^ 2 = q x`, as they do when `A` and `B` purify the density states `diag
p` and `diag q` with a common environment `E`. Then the overlap `|∑ x, ∑ e,
conj (A x e) * B x e|` is at most the Bhattacharyya coefficient `∑ x, √(p x
* q x)` of `p` and `q`.
