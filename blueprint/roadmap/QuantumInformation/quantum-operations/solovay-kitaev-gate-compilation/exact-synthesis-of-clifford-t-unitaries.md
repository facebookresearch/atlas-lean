# Kliuchnikov-Maslov-Mosca: exact synthesis of single-qubit Clifford+T unitaries

A $2 \times 2$ unitary $U$ is equal to the matrix of some ancilla-free circuit over $\{H, T\}$ (equivalently, over Clifford+T) if and only if all entries of $U$ lie in the ring $\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}, i]$. This is exact matrix equality; $U$ is implementable up to a global phase if and only if some phase multiple $e^{i\varphi} U$ has all entries in the ring.

For $w \in \mathbb{Z}[\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}, i]$ let $\mathrm{sde}(w)$ be the smallest integer $k$ with $\sqrt2^{\,k} w \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$. If $U$ has entries in the ring and an entry $z$ with $\mathrm{sde}(|z|^2) \ge 4$, then over the gates $H, T, T^\dagger, S, S^\dagger, X, Y, Z$ (implementation up to global phase) the minimal number of $H$ gates is $h(U) = \mathrm{sde}(|z|^2) - 1$, and the minimal number of $T$ gates is $h(U) - 1 + (l \bmod 2) + (j \bmod 2)$, where $l, j$ are chosen with $h(H T^{l} U T^{j} H) = h(U) + 2$. A circuit attaining both minima is computed with $O(n_{\mathrm{opt}}(U))$ ring operations, where $n_{\mathrm{opt}}(U)$ is the length of a shortest circuit, and with $O(n_{\mathrm{opt}}(U)^2)$ bit operations.

## Proof sketch

Necessity is immediate since $H$ and $T$ have entries in the ring. For sufficiency, the invariant is $\mathrm{sde}(|z|^2)$, not the denominator exponent of the entries themselves (already $H$ has entry exponent one but $T$-count zero). If $\mathrm{sde}(|z|^2) \ge 4$, an analysis of residues in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ shows that $H T^{k} U$ has $\mathrm{sde}(|z|^2)$ one smaller for some $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, while one $H T^k$ step changes it by at most one. Iterating reaches a unitary with $\mathrm{sde}(|z|^2) \le 3$; these form a small finite set, whose optimal circuits are found by breadth-first search and looked up in a table (they need not be Clifford). Optimality of the counts follows from the brute-force base case, the one-step bound and a normal form with exactly one $T$ between consecutive $H$ gates.
