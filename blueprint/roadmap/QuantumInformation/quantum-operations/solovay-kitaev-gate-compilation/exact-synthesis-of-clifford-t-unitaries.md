# Kliuchnikov-Maslov-Mosca: exact synthesis of single-qubit Clifford+T unitaries

A single-qubit unitary $U$ is exactly implementable by an ancilla-free circuit over $\{H, S, T\}$ (up to a global phase) if and only if all entries of $U$ lie in the ring $\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}, i]$. Moreover, an optimal circuit (with minimal number of $H$ and $T$ gates) can be computed in time linear in the smallest denominator exponent of the entries, and the $T$-count equals that exponent up to an additive constant.

## Proof sketch

Necessity is immediate since $H, S, T$ have entries in the ring. For sufficiency, write $U$ with entries $(a, b)/\sqrt{2}^{\,k}$ for minimal $k$; one can show that some $H T^{j}$ with $j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ applied to $U$ decreases $k$ by one, by analysing the residues of $a, b$ modulo powers of $1 + \omega$ in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$. Induction on $k$ ends at a Clifford element.
