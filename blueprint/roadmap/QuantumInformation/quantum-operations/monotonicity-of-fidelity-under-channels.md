# Monotonicity of fidelity under channels

For every quantum channel $\Phi$ and all positive semidefinite operators $P, Q$ on its input space,

$$F(\Phi(P), \Phi(Q)) \ge F(P, Q).$$

In particular the fidelity of two density operators does not decrease under a channel.
