# Quantum capacity of the depolarizing channel

Determine the unassisted rate at which quantum information can be transmitted through a basic noise model—or even its exact positive-capacity threshold.

## What would settle it

Fix a parameterization, for example $N_p(\rho) = (1-p)\rho + p I/2$. Prove matching coding and converse bounds, allowing entanglement across arbitrarily many channel uses. Single-use coherent information is not generally the capacity. Do not mix p with a differently normalized Pauli-error probability.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Agarwal, Kalra, Lee and Leung, and Ahmed, Bhalerao, Lee and Leditzky (preprints) state that quantum capacities are mostly unknown and improve the noise threshold for positive capacity of the depolarizing channel.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
