# Subset products multiply by symmetric difference

Let $g_1, \dots, g_r$ be signed Pauli operators on $n$ qubits, $g_j = c_j X^{a_j} Z^{b_j}$ with $a_j, b_j \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$ and $c_j \in \mathbb{C}$ that are involutive ($g_j^2 = I$) and pairwise commuting. For subsets $T, T' \subseteq \{1, \dots, r\}$, writing $g_T$ for the product of the
$g_j$ with $j \in T$ in index order, $g_T \, g_{T'} = g_{T \,\triangle\, T'}$.
