# Signed Pauli generator

For $a, b \in (\mathbb{F}_2^n)^r$ and complex scalars $c \in \mathbb{C}^r$, the $j$-th generator is $g_j = c_j X^{a_j} Z^{b_j}$, the [Pauli operator](../css-codes/pauli.md) $X^{a_j} Z^{b_j}$ on $n$ qubits scaled by an arbitrary complex number $c_j$ (not only $\pm 1$ or $\pm i$).
