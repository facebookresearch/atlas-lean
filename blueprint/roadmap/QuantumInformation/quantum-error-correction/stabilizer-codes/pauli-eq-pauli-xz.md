# Two constructions of the Pauli operator agree

For $a, b \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$, the [Pauli operator](../css-codes/pauli.md) $X^a Z^b$ on $n$ qubits, written with the phase $\mathrm{sign}(b \cdot v)$, and the computational-basis matrix with entries given by the phase $(-1)^{b \cdot v}$ are the same $2^n \times 2^n$ matrix: the one sending $|v\rangle$ to $(-1)^{b \cdot v} |v + a\rangle$ for every $v \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$.
