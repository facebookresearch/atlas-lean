# Logical Pauli operators of a stabilizer code

Let $S$ be a stabilizer group on $n$ qubits with $n - k$ independent generators, $k \ge 1$, and let $C(S)$ be its code
space, of dimension $2^k$. Then there are Hermitian Pauli operators
$\bar X_1, \dots, \bar X_k, \bar Z_1, \dots, \bar Z_k \in N(S)$ such that

$$\bar X_i \bar X_j = \bar X_j \bar X_i, \qquad \bar Z_i \bar Z_j = \bar Z_j \bar Z_i, \qquad
\bar X_i \bar Z_j = (-1)^{\delta_{ij}} \bar Z_j \bar X_i ,$$

and $N(S)$ is generated by $S$, $iI$ and these $2k$ operators. Moreover $C(S)$ has an orthonormal basis
$|\bar c_1 \dots \bar c_k\rangle$, $c \in \mathbb{F}_2^k$, on which $\bar Z_i$ acts as $(-1)^{c_i}$ and $\bar X_i$ flips
$c_i$; that is, $\bar X_i$ and $\bar Z_i$ act on the code as $X$ and $Z$ on the $i$-th encoded qubit. Elements of $N(S)$
in the same coset of $S$ act identically on $C(S)$, and $N(S)/S$ is isomorphic to the $k$-qubit Pauli group.

Proof sketch (Preskill): the [normalizer](order-of-the-normalizer-of-a-stabilizer-group.md) corresponds to an
$(n + k)$-dimensional space $V^{\perp} \supseteq V$; extend the generators of $S$ by $\bar Z_1, \dots, \bar Z_k$ to a
maximal commuting set and choose $\bar X_i$ by symplectic Gram–Schmidt, so that the
[symplectic products](pauli-operators-commute-iff-symplectic-product-vanishes.md) give the relations above.
