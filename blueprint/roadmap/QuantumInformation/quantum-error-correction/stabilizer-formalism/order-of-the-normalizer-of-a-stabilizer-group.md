# Order of the normalizer of a stabilizer group

Let $S$ be a stabilizer group on $n$ qubits generated by $n - k$ elements $M_j = c_j X^{a_j} Z^{b_j}$ whose
vectors $(a_j, b_j) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$ are linearly independent, and let $V \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$ be their
span. Then the [normalizer](normalizer-of-a-stabilizer-group-equals-its-centralizer.md) of $S$ in the Pauli group is

$$N(S) = \{ c\, X^a Z^b : c \in \{\pm 1, \pm i\},\ (a, b) \in V^{\perp} \},$$

where $V^{\perp}$ is the orthogonal complement of $V$ for the [symplectic product](symplectic-product-of-pauli-operators.md),
a subspace of dimension $n + k$ containing $V$. Consequently $|S| = 2^{n-k}$, $|N(S)| = 4 \cdot 2^{n+k}$ and
$|N(S)/S| = 4^{k+1}$.

Proof: by the [commutation criterion](pauli-operators-commute-iff-symplectic-product-vanishes.md), $c\, X^a Z^b$
commutes with every generator exactly when $(a, b) \in V^{\perp}$; nondegeneracy of $\omega$ gives
$\dim V^{\perp} = 2n - (n - k)$, and $V \subseteq V^{\perp}$ because the generators commute. The group $S$ has
$2^{n-k}$ elements since no nonempty product of generators is a scalar.
