# Quantum LDPC codes

A quantum low-density parity-check code has checks of bounded weight, each qubit in boundedly many checks. CSS codes are
chain complexes of $\mathbb{F}_2$-vector spaces, with logical operators given by homology, and products of complexes give
new codes: the hypergraph product of two classical LDPC codes has rate bounded below and distance proportional to the
square root of the length. Codes with constant rate and linear distance, the long-open qLDPC conjecture, were constructed
by Panteleev and Kalachev and by Leverrier and Zémor (Breuckmann and Eberhardt survey the area).
