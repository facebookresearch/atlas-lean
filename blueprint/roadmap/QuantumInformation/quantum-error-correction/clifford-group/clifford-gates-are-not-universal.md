# Clifford gates are not universal

For every $n \ge 1$, the group generated by the gates $H$ and $S$ on single qubits and CNOT on pairs of qubits of an
$n$-qubit register is contained in the Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_n$, and $\mathcal{C}_n$ is finite modulo global phases:
$\mathcal{C}_n / \{ e^{i\theta} I \}$ is a finite group. Hence $\{H, S, \mathrm{CNOT}\}$ is not a
[universal gate set](../universal-gate-sets/universal-gate-set.md): finitely many unitaries, even up to phase, cannot
approximate every unitary on one qubit to arbitrary accuracy.

Proof: conjugation gives a homomorphism from $\mathcal{C}_n$ to the automorphism group of the finite group
$\mathcal{P}_n$, of order $4^{n+1}$; its kernel consists of the scalars, because a unitary commuting with every Pauli
operator is a scalar.
