# Instruction set for $SU(d)$

Let $d \ge 2$. An instruction set for $SU(d)$ is a finite set $\mathcal{G}$ of quantum gates such that

1. every $g \in \mathcal{G}$ lies in $SU(d)$: it is unitary with determinant $1$;
2. $\mathcal{G}$ is closed under inverses: $g^{*} \in \mathcal{G}$ for every $g \in \mathcal{G}$;
3. the group generated by $\mathcal{G}$ is dense in $SU(d)$: for every $U \in SU(d)$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a
   finite product $g_1 \cdots g_m$ of elements of $\mathcal{G}$ with $\| U - g_1 \cdots g_m \| < \varepsilon$.

Distances are in the operator norm $\|A\| = \sup_{\|\psi\| = 1} \|A \psi\|$. These are the hypotheses of the
Solovay–Kitaev theorem; condition 2 is used in its proof.

- C. M. Dawson and M. A. Nielsen, *The Solovay–Kitaev algorithm*, Quantum Inf. Comput. 6 (2006), 81–95, arXiv:quant-ph/0505030, Section 2 (the definition of an instruction set and of $\varepsilon$-approximation).
