# The logical unitaries in a compact Lie group form a Lie subgroup

Let $A$ be a closed subgroup of the unitary group of a finite-dimensional space, for instance the group of unitary product
operators $U_1 \otimes \dots \otimes U_n$ of a system of $n$ subsystems, and let $P$ be a code projector. The logical
elements of $A$, those $U \in A$ with $(I - P) U P = 0$, form a closed subgroup $B$ of $A$, by
[the group property of logical unitaries](logical-unitaries-of-a-code-form-a-group.md). By Cartan's closed subgroup
theorem, a closed subgroup of a Lie group is an embedded Lie subgroup, so $B$ is a compact Lie group; it is a finite union
of cosets of its identity component $B_0$, a connected Lie subgroup whose Lie algebra is contained in that of $A$.
