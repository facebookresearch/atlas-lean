# Balanced products of chain complexes over a group

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $A$, $B$ be chain complexes of free $\mathbb{F}_2[G]$-modules, with $G$ acting freely on bases, $A$ from the right and $B$ from the left. The *balanced product* $A \otimes_G B$ is the quotient of the tensor product complex $A \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_2} B$ by the relations $a g \otimes b = a \otimes g b$ for $g \in G$; equivalently it is the tensor product over the group algebra, $A \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_2[G]} B$, with differential $\partial(a \otimes b) = \partial a \otimes b + a \otimes \partial b$. When $A$ and $B$ are graphs (two-term complexes), $A \otimes_G B$ is a two-dimensional square complex whose size is $|A||B|/|G|$.
