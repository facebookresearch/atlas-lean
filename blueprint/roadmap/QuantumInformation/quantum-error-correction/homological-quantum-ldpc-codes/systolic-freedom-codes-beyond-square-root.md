# Freedman-Meyer-Luo: homological codes with distance above the square root

There is a family of quantum codes obtained from cellulations of $\mathbb{F}_2$-systolically free manifolds with $k = 2$ (constant dimension), weight-bounded checks, and distance $d = \Omega(\sqrt{n \log n})$, beating the $\sqrt n$ distance of the toric code by a logarithmic factor.

## Proof sketch

Choose a Riemannian $3$-manifold (a fibre bundle over a circle with surface fibres) whose $1$-systole and $2$-systole, measured with $\mathbb{F}_2$ coefficients, have product exceeding the volume by a $\log$ factor. A bounded-geometry triangulation turns the systoles into the $Z$- and $X$-distances of the associated homological code, with $n$ proportional to the volume.
