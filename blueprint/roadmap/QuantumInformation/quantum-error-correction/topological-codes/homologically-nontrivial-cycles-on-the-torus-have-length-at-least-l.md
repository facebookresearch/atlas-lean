# Homologically nontrivial cycles on the $L \times L$ torus have length at least $L$

On the square lattice of the $L \times L$ torus, call a set of edges a *cycle* if every vertex meets an even number of its
edges, and *trivial* if it is the boundary of a set of faces (the sum, modulo $2$, of the four-edge boundaries of those
faces). Every nontrivial cycle has at least $L$ edges: a cycle that is not a boundary winds around the torus in at least
one direction, so it crosses every horizontal circle, or every vertical circle, an odd number of times; it then contains at
least $L$ vertical edges, one in each of the $L$ rows, or at least $L$ horizontal edges, one in each column. The same holds
on the dual lattice. These cycles are the supports of the $Z$-type (respectively $X$-type) logical operators of the
[toric code](toric-code.md).
