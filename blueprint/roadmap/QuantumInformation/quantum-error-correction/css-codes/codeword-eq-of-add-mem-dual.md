# CSS codewords depend on $w$ modulo $C_1^{\perp}$

If $w_1 + w_2 \in C_1^{\perp}$, then $|c_{w_1}\rangle = |c_{w_2}\rangle$: for every $v \in C_1$,
$v \cdot w_1 = v \cdot w_2$. Hence the codewords are indexed by the cosets of $C_1^{\perp}$, and the
code $Q_{C_1, C_2}$, spanned by the $|c_w\rangle$ with $w \in C_2^{\perp}$, has one basis vector for
each coset of $C_1^{\perp}$ in $C_2^{\perp}$, that is $2^{\dim C_1 - \dim C_2}$ of them.

## Sources

- A. R. Calderbank and P. W. Shor, *Good quantum error-correcting codes exist*, Phys. Rev. A 54 (1996), 1098–1105, arXiv:quant-ph/9512032, Section III, after equation (2).
