# Sign of a binary digit

For $x \in \mathbb{F}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, $\operatorname{sign}(x) = (-1)^x$ as a complex
number: $1$ for $x = 0$ and $-1$ for $x = 1$. It is multiplicative,
$(-1)^{x + y} = (-1)^x (-1)^y$, which is what turns the dot product $v \cdot w$ over
$\mathbb{F}_2$ into the amplitudes $(-1)^{v \cdot w}$ of CSS codewords.
