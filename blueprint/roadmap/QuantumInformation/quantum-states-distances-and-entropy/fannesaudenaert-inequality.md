# Fannes–Audenaert inequality

Let $\rho_0, \rho_1$ be density operators on a complex Euclidean space of dimension $n \ge 2$ and let
$\delta = \frac12\|\rho_0 - \rho_1\|_1$. Then

$$|S(\rho_0) - S(\rho_1)| \le \delta \log(n-1) + h(\delta),$$

where $S$ is the von Neumann entropy and $h$ the binary entropy function.
