# Fidelity of positive semidefinite matrices

For positive semidefinite matrices $P, Q \in M_\iota(\mathbb{C})$ the (unsquared, root) *fidelity* is

$$
F(P, Q) = \big\|\sqrt{P}\,\sqrt{Q}\big\|_1 = \operatorname{Tr}\sqrt{\sqrt{P}\,Q\,\sqrt{P}},
$$

where $\sqrt{\cdot}$ is the positive square root and $\|\cdot\|_1$ the trace norm. For density matrices this is the fidelity appearing in the Fuchs-van de Graaf inequalities and in Uhlmann's theorem (not its square).
