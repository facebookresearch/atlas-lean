# Fuchs–van de Graaf inequalities

For all density operators $\rho, \sigma$ on a complex Euclidean space,

$$1 - F(\rho, \sigma) \le \tfrac12\|\rho - \sigma\|_1 \le \sqrt{1 - F(\rho, \sigma)^2}.$$
