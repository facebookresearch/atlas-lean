# Invariance of a PEPS under an on-site symmetry is undecidable

No algorithm decides whether the translation-invariant projected entangled pair state generated by a given tensor is invariant under a given on-site symmetry in the thermodynamic limit, or whether two given tensors describe the same state; so there is no algorithm bringing PEPS into a canonical form from which one can decide whether two tensors describe the same state.

Let $C$ be the tensor of a translation-invariant [projected entangled pair state](projected-entangled-pair-states.md) (PEPS) on the square lattice, generating states $\lvert\Psi_{N_h,N_v}\rangle$ on $N_h \times N_v$ patches with arbitrary boundary conditions; as an input convention of this page (the source fixes none), tensors and the unitaries $U_g$ of the symmetry are given by their finitely many entries, taken rational; the source's construction below gives rational instances, for example with $U_g = \sigma_x$, the non-invariant product state $\lvert 0\rangle$ and $B$ the bond-dimension-1 tensor with coefficients $(1, 1)$, which yield entries in $\{0, 1\}$. Following the source, the PEPS is *invariant in the thermodynamic limit* under an on-site symmetry $\mathcal{S}_g = U_g^{\otimes N}$ if for every finite region $R$ the reduced density matrix $\rho_R$, in the limit where the boundary is taken far away, is unique, independent of the boundary condition, and satisfies $U_g^{\otimes \lvert R\rvert} \rho_R (U_g^{\otimes \lvert R\rvert})^\dagger = \rho_R$, with $\rho_R$ the [reduced state](../density-matrices-and-fidelity/density-matrices-and-reduced-states.md) on $R$ of the normalized patch state; only boundary conditions that give a nonzero patch state are considered, since the normalized state is otherwise undefined. Two tensors $A$, $B$ *describe the same PEPS* if the reduced density matrices of their states on every finite region become indistinguishable as the boundary is taken to infinity, read here as their trace distance tending to $0$.

**Theorem (Scarpa, Molnár, Ge, García-Ripoll, Schuch, Pérez-García and Iblisdir).**

1. (Symmetries in PEPS.) Given a tensor $C$ and an on-site symmetry $\mathcal{S}_g$, it is undecidable whether the translation-invariant PEPS constructed from $C$ is invariant under $\mathcal{S}_g$ in the thermodynamic limit (Undecidable problem 2 of the source).
2. (Fundamental theorem for PEPS.) Given tensors $A$ and $B$, it is undecidable whether they describe the same PEPS for all system sizes (Undecidable problem 3).

The source notes that the result holds for any symmetry representation, and thus already for spin-$\tfrac12$ systems; it does not fix how the invariant PEPS $B$ of its construction is chosen, so this page states item 1 with $C$ and $\mathcal S_g$ both given as input.

In particular there is no algorithm bringing PEPS tensors to a canonical form from which one could decide whether two of them describe the same state, and no algorithmically checkable list of all ways a symmetry can be encoded in a PEPS tensor.

## Expansion

Take any PEPS $\lvert\Theta_{\mathrm{sym}}\rangle$, with tensor $B$, that is invariant under $\mathcal{S}_g$, and a product state that is not invariant under $\mathcal{S}_g$ for some $g$; encode the latter, following the tiling construction of the zero-testing problem, as a PEPS $\lvert\tilde\Psi_{\mathrm{tile}}\rangle$ with tensor $\tilde{A}$ whose vanishing is undecidable. The tensor $C$ is the direct sum of $\tilde{A}$ and $B$ on the direct sum of their virtual spaces, so that $C$ generates $\lvert\tilde\Psi_{\mathrm{tile}}\rangle + \lvert\Theta_{\mathrm{sym}}\rangle$. If the tile set tiles the plane, boundary conditions supported in the $\tilde{A}$ sector give reduced density matrices that depend on the boundary and are not symmetric; if it does not, $\lvert\tilde\Psi_{\mathrm{tile}}\rangle$ vanishes on large patches and only the symmetric part contributes. For item 2 choose $B^i = \sum_j (U_g)_{ij} A^j$, so that item 1 is a special case. A good expansion adds a page for the variant of zero testing used here, in which the tiling PEPS encodes a given product state that is not invariant under $\mathcal S_g$, and the argument that the reduced density matrices of the direct sum are symmetric and boundary-independent exactly when the tiling sector vanishes on large patches.
