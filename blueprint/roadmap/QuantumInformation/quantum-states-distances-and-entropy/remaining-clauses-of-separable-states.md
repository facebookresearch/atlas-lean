# Separable states: convexity, entangled mixed states and Schmidt rank one

Separability of states, entanglement of pure states, and the equivalence of separability with being a product for
pure states are separate definitions and results and are not part of this node. This node collects only the remaining
clauses.

## Expansion

A sound expansion has separate pages for:

1. **Convexity and compactness.** The set of separable density operators on $X \otimes Y$ (finite-dimensional) is the convex
   hull of the pure product states $xx^* \otimes yy^*$ and is convex and compact.
2. **Entangled mixed states.** A density operator on $X \otimes Y$ is entangled exactly when it is not separable.
3. **Schmidt rank.** A pure state is a product state if and only if its Schmidt rank is $1$.
