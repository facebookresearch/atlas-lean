# Peres PPT necessary condition

Let $X$ and $Y$ be finite-dimensional complex Euclidean spaces with fixed bases, and let $T$ be the transpose on $L(X)$ in
the basis of $X$. If $\rho \in D(X \otimes Y)$ is separable, that is, a convex combination of product states, then
$(T \otimes \mathrm{id})(\rho)$ is positive semidefinite. For the two-qubit state
$\lvert \phi^+ \rangle = (\lvert 00 \rangle + \lvert 11 \rangle)/\sqrt 2$, the partial transpose of $\lvert \phi^+ \rangle\langle \phi^+ \rvert$
has eigenvalue $-1/2$, so this state is entangled. The converse of the first statement in dimensions $2 \times 2$ and
$2 \times 3$ is a separate result, not part of this node.
