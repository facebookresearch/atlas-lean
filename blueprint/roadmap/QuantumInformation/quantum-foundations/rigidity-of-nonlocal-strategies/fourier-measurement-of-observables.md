# Fourier measurement of a family of $\pm1$ observables

Let $A(a)$, $a\in\{0,1\}^n$, be $\pm1$ observables (self-adjoint, $A(a)^2 = 1$) in a star-ordered algebra. Their *Fourier coefficients* are $\hat A(u) = \mathbb{E}_a(-1)^{u\cdot a}A(a)$ for $u\in\{0,1\}^n$; they are self-adjoint and, by Parseval, $\sum_u\hat A(u)^2 = \mathbb{E}_aA(a)^2 = 1$. The *Fourier measurement* is the measurement with outcomes $u\in\{0,1\}^n$ and effects $B_u = \hat A(u)^2$. Moreover $\sum_u\hat A(u)^3 = \mathbb{E}_{a,b}A(a)A(b)A(a+b)$, and if $A$ is exactly linear ($A(a)A(b) = A(a+b)$) then $B$ is projective and its character observables recover $A$.
