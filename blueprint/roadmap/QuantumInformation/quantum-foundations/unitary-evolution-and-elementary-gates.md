# Unitary evolution and elementary gates

A unitary operator $U$ on $\mathbb{C}^\Sigma$, that is, one with $U^*U = I$, maps a pure state $\psi$ to $U\psi$
and a density operator $\rho$ to $U\rho U^*$; it preserves inner products and maps states to states. Standard
gates are the Pauli gates $X, Y, Z$, the Hadamard gate $H$, the phase gates $S$ and $T$, the controlled-NOT gate
and the Toffoli gate; a gate acting on some registers acts on a product register by the tensor product with
the identity on the others.
