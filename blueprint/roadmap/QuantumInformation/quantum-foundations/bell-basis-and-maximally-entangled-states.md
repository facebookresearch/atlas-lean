# Bell basis and maximally entangled states

The four Bell states

$$\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|00\rangle + |11\rangle),\quad \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|00\rangle - |11\rangle),\quad
\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|01\rangle + |10\rangle),\quad \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|01\rangle - |10\rangle)$$

form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$; each is obtained from the first by a Pauli operator
applied to one qubit. Each Bell state is maximally entangled: the reduced density matrix of either qubit, obtained by the partial trace over the other, is $I/2$.
