# Equivalence of the Connes, Kirchberg and Tsirelson problems

The following are equivalent: (i) the Connes embedding conjecture; (ii) Kirchberg's conjecture; (iii) a positive answer to Tsirelson's problem, $C_{qa} = C_{qc}$ for all finite question and answer sets; (iv) a positive answer to the synchronous version of Tsirelson's problem.

## Proof sketch

(i) iff (ii) is Kirchberg's theorem. Correlations in $C_{qc}$ are states on the maximal tensor product of free products of finite-dimensional abelian C*-algebras, which embed in $C^*(\mathbb{F}_2)$-type algebras, while $C_{qa}$ corresponds to the minimal tensor product; this gives (ii) implies (iii) (Fritz; Junge et al.) and Ozawa proved the converse. Kim, Paulsen and Schafhauser show that synchronous correlations already detect the Connes embedding problem through tracial states.
