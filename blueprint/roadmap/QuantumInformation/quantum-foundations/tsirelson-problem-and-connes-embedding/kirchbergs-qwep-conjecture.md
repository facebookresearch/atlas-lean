# Kirchberg's conjecture on tensor products of free group C*-algebras

*Kirchberg's conjecture* asserts that for the full group C*-algebra $C^*(\mathbb{F}_2)$ of the free group on two generators the minimal and maximal C*-tensor norms coincide:

$$
C^*(\mathbb{F}_2) \otimes_{\min} C^*(\mathbb{F}_2) = C^*(\mathbb{F}_2) \otimes_{\max} C^*(\mathbb{F}_2).
$$

It is equivalent to the statement that every C*-algebra is a quotient of a C*-algebra with Lance's weak expectation property (QWEP).

## Sources

- Eberhard Kirchberg, "On non-semisplit extensions, tensor products and exactness of group C*-algebras", Inventiones Mathematicae 112 (1993).
- Narutaka Ozawa, "About the Connes embedding conjecture: algebraic approaches", Japanese Journal of Mathematics 8 (2013).
