# Containments C_q in C_qs in C_qa in C_qc and closedness of C_qc

For all finite $\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{A}$: $C_q \subseteq C_{qs} \subseteq C_{qa} \subseteq C_{qc}$, and $C_{qa}$, $C_{qc}$ are compact convex sets.

## Proof sketch

Finite-dimensional strategies are special infinite-dimensional ones, and tensor-product strategies are commuting by taking $A \otimes I$ and $I \otimes B$. An infinite-dimensional tensor strategy is a limit of its compressions to finite-dimensional subspaces, giving $C_{qs} \subseteq C_{qa}$. Closedness of $C_{qc}$ follows from a GNS argument: correlations in $C_{qc}$ are exactly evaluations of states on the maximal tensor product of two universal C*-algebras generated by POVMs, and the state space is weak-* compact; convexity follows from direct sums.
