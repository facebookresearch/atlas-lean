# The Connes embedding conjecture

Let $\mathcal{R}$ be the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor and $\mathcal{R}^\omega$ its ultrapower along a free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\mathbb{N}$, with trace $\tau_\omega$. The *Connes embedding conjecture* asserts that every finite von Neumann algebra $(M, \tau)$ with faithful normal tracial state acting on a separable Hilbert space admits a trace-preserving embedding into $(\mathcal{R}^\omega, \tau_\omega)$. Equivalently, for all self-adjoint $x_1, \dots, x_m \in M$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, there are $n$ and self-adjoint $X_1, \dots, X_m \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ with $\|X_i\| \le \|x_i\|$ whose normalized-trace moments of order at most $k$ agree with those of $(x_i)$ up to $\varepsilon$.
