# The Connes embedding conjecture is false

There is a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal tracial state on a separable Hilbert space that does not embed, trace-preservingly, into an ultrapower of the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor. Equivalently, Kirchberg's conjecture is false: the minimal and maximal tensor norms on $C^*(\mathbb{F}_2) \otimes C^*(\mathbb{F}_2)$ differ.

## Proof sketch

Combine the negative answer to Tsirelson's problem with the equivalence theorem. The witness algebra is the von Neumann algebra generated, in the GNS representation of an optimal commuting-operator strategy, by the measurement operators of the separating synchronous game.
