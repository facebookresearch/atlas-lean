# Reconstruction at a wet lake at rest

Let the reconstructed face state of a cell with depth $h$, velocity $u$ and bed $z$ at face bed $z_F$ be $(\bar h, \bar h u)$ with $\bar h = \max(0, h + z - z_F)$. Suppose $h_L + z_L = h_R + z_R = \eta$, $\max(z_L, z_R) < \eta$, and $h_L u_L = h_R u_R = 0$. Then at face bed $z_F = \max(z_L, z_R)$ both reconstructed states equal $(\eta - \max(z_L, z_R), 0)$.
