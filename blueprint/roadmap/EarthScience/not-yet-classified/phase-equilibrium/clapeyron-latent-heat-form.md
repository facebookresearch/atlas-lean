# Latent-heat form of the Clapeyron equation

Let the chemical potentials $\mu_1, \mu_2$ of two phases have derivatives $v_1\,dp - s_1\,dT$ and $v_2\,dp - s_2\,dT$ at $t$, and let the phases coexist near $t$, so that $\mu_1 = \mu_2$ on a neighbourhood of $t$. If $v_2 - v_1 \ne 0$, $dT \ne 0$, $T \ne 0$ and $L = T(s_2 - s_1)$ is the molar latent heat, then $dp/dT = L/(T(v_2 - v_1))$.
