# Conservative and advective forms of the advection-diffusion-reaction equation

Let $U$ and $C(t,x)$ be real functions and $D, k \in \mathbb{R}$. At a point $(t, x)$ where $U'(x) = 0$ and both $x \mapsto C(t,x)$ and $x \mapsto \partial_x C(t,x)$ are differentiable at $x$, the conservative equation $\partial_t C + \partial_x(U C - D\,\partial_x C) = -kC$ holds at $(t,x)$ if and only if the advective equation $\partial_t C + U\,\partial_x C - D\,\partial_{xx} C = -kC$ holds there.
