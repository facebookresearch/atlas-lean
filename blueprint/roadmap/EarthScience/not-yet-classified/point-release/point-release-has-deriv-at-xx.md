# Point release: the second spatial derivative exists

For all real $M$, $D$, $k$, $U$, $t$, let $c(t,x) = M e^{-kt} \exp\!\big(-(x - Ut)^2/(4Dt)\big) / \sqrt{4\pi D t}$, with each quotient equal to $0$ when its denominator $4Dt$ or $\sqrt{4\pi D t}$ is $0$ (in particular when $Dt = 0$), by the convention $x/0 = 0$. Then $x \mapsto \partial_x c(t,x)$ is differentiable at every $x$, with derivative $\partial_{xx} c(t,x)$.
