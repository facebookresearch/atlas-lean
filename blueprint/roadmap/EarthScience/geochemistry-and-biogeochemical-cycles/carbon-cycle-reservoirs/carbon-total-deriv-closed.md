# Total carbon of a closed system has zero derivative

Let `s` be a finite set of carbon reservoirs and `F i j t` the real carbon flux from reservoir
`i` to reservoir `j` at time `t`. If at time `t` every reservoir content `C i` with `i ∈ s` has
derivative `∑ j ∈ s, (F j i t - F i j t)` (no external fluxes), then the total carbon
`∑ i ∈ s, C i` has derivative `0` at `t`.
