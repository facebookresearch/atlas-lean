# Total carbon changes at the sum of the external fluxes

Let `s` be a finite set of carbon reservoirs, `F i j t` the real carbon flux from reservoir `i`
to reservoir `j` at time `t`, and `e i t` the real external flux into reservoir `i`. If at time
`t` every reservoir content `C i` with `i ∈ s` has derivative
`∑ j ∈ s, (F j i t - F i j t) + e i t`, then the total carbon `∑ i ∈ s, C i` has derivative
`∑ i ∈ s, e i t` at `t`: the internal transfers cancel in pairs.
