# Residual-solid concentration in batch melting

Let `D > 0` be the bulk solid/melt partition coefficient and `0 ≤ F ≤ 1` the melt fraction. If
the concentrations `C₀` of the source, `Cl` of the melt and `Cs` of the residual solid satisfy
the mass balance `C₀ = F * Cl + (1 - F) * Cs` and the partitioning `Cs = D * Cl`, then the
residual solid has `Cs = D * C₀ / (D + F * (1 - D))`.
