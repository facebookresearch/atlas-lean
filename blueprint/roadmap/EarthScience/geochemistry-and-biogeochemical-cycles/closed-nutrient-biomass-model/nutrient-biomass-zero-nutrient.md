# Nutrient and biomass derivatives at zero nutrient

In the closed nutrient–biomass model, suppose that at time `t` the nutrient `N` and the biomass
`B` have derivatives `-u(N t) * B t + r * B t` and `u(N t) * B t - r * B t`. If `N t = 0` and
the uptake rate vanishes at zero nutrient, `u(0) = 0`, then at `t` the derivative of biomass is
`-r * B t` (losses only) and the derivative of nutrient is `r * B t`. These are identities for
all real `r` and `B t`; no sign is asserted.
