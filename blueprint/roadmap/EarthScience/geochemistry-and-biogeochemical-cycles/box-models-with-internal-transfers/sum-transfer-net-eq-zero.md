# Net internal transfers sum to zero

For a finite set `s` of compartments and any real transfer rates `q i j` from compartment `i` to
compartment `j`, the net internal transfer summed over all compartments vanishes:
`∑ i ∈ s, ∑ j ∈ s, (q j i - q i j) = 0`, since every transfer is counted once with each sign.
