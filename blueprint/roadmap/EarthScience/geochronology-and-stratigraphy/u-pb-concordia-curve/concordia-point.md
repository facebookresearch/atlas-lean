# Point on the U–Pb concordia curve at a given age

The ideal closed-system concordia point at age `t` for decay constants `l1` and `l2`:
`(exp (l1 * t) - 1, exp (l2 * t) - 1)`, for all real `l1`, `l2` and `t`. In the U–Pb concordia
diagram, `l1 = λ₂₃₅` and the first coordinate is `²⁰⁷Pb*/²³⁵U`, and `l2 = λ₂₃₈` and the second
coordinate is `²⁰⁶Pb*/²³⁸U`.
