# The U–Pb concordia curve is injective on nonnegative ages

For two positive distinct decay constants `l1, l2 > 0`, `l1 ≠ l2` (the U–Pb setting
`l1 = λ₂₃₅`, `l2 = λ₂₃₈`), the concordia parametrization
`t ↦ (exp (l1 * t) - 1, exp (l2 * t) - 1)` is injective on `t ≥ 0`. Injectivity needs only
`l1 > 0`; the conditions `l2 > 0` and `l1 ≠ l2` state the setting.
