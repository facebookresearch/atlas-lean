# The repeated-rate daughter is the limit of the distinct-rate one

For all real `l1`, `P0`, `D0` and `t`, as `l2 → l1` with `l2 ≠ l1`, the distinct-rate daughter
`D0 * exp (-l2 * t) + P0 * l1 / (l2 - l1) * (exp (-l1 * t) - exp (-l2 * t))` tends to the
repeated-rate daughter `(D0 + P0 * l1 * t) * exp (-l1 * t)`.
