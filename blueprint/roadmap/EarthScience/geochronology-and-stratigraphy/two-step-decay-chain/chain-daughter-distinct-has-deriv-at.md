# The distinct-rate daughter solves the daughter equation

For distinct rates `l1 ≠ l2` and all real `P0`, `D0` and `t`, the daughter
`D(t) = D0 * exp (-l2 * t) + P0 * l1 / (l2 - l1) * (exp (-l1 * t) - exp (-l2 * t))` has
derivative `l1 * P(t) - l2 * D(t)` at `t`, where `P(t) = P0 * exp (-l1 * t)`: it satisfies
`D' = l1 * P - l2 * D`.
