# The repeated-rate daughter solves the daughter equation

For all real `l`, `P0`, `D0` and `t`, the repeated-rate daughter
`D(t) = (D0 + P0 * l * t) * exp (-l * t)` has derivative `l * P(t) - l * D(t)` at `t`, where
`P(t) = P0 * exp (-l * t)`: it satisfies `D' = l * P - l * D`, the daughter equation with
`l1 = l2 = l`.
