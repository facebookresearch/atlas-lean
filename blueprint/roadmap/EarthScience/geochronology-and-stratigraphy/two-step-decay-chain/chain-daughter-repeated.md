# Daughter inventory of a decay chain with a repeated rate

The daughter inventory in a two-step decay chain whose parent, with initial inventory `P0`, and
daughter, with initial inventory `D0`, decay at the same rate `l1 = l2 = l`:
`D(t) = (D0 + P0 * l * t) * exp (-l * t)`. It is defined for all reals; physically `l > 0`,
`P0, D0 ≥ 0` and `t ≥ 0`.
