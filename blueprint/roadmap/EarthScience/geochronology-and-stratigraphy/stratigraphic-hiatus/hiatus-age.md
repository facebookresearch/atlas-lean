# Sediment age across a stratigraphic hiatus

The age at depth `d` (positive downward) under a constant accumulation speed `v`, with a hiatus
at depth `h` that removes `g` of time: `d / v` above (shallower than) the hiatus, `d < h`, and
`d / v + g` at and below (deeper than) it, `h ≤ d`. It is defined for all real `v`, `h`, `g` and
`d`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, at `v = 0` it is `0` above the hiatus and `g` at and below
it. The intended domain is `v > 0` and `g > 0`.
