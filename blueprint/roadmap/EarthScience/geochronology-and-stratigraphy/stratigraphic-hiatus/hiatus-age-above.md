# Above a hiatus the age is the constant-accumulation age

Under accumulation speed `v`, with a hiatus at depth `h` that removes `g` of time, the age at a
depth `d < h` above (shallower than) the hiatus is the ordinary constant-accumulation age `d / v`.
This holds for all real `v`, `h` and `g`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0` the age is `0` when
`v = 0`. The intended domain is `v > 0`.
