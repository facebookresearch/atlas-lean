# At or below a hiatus the age is offset by the gap

Under accumulation speed `v`, with a hiatus at depth `h` that removes `g` of time, the age at a
depth `d ≥ h` at or below the hiatus is the constant-accumulation age offset by the gap:
`d / v + g`. This holds for all real `v`, `h` and `g`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0` the age is
`g` when `v = 0`. The intended domain is `v > 0`.
