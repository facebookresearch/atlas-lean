# At the hiatus depth the age jumps by the gap

For all real `v`, `h` and `g`, the age at the hiatus depth `h` is `h / v + g`: the
constant-accumulation age `h / v`, which the age `d / v` above the hiatus approaches, offset by
the gap `g`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `g` when `v = 0`; the intended domain is
`v > 0`.
