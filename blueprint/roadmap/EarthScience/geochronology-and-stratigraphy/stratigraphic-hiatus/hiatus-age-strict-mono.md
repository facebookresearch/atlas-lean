# With a hiatus, age still increases strictly with depth

The hiatus is a jump, not a negative rate: for an accumulation speed `v > 0` and a gap `g > 0`,
the age with a hiatus at depth `h` (`d / v` for `d < h`, `d / v + g` for `d ≥ h`) is strictly
increasing in the depth `d` on the whole real line, including across pairs of depths straddling
the hiatus.
