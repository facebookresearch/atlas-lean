# A synthetic isochron with half-life 10 and age 10 has slope 1

A synthetic isochron: decay constant `λ = log 2 / 10` (half-life 10 years), age `t = 10`, two
samples with parent/reference ratios `x₁ = 1` and `x₂ = 2`, any real common initial ratio `b`,
and daughter/reference ratios `yᵢ = b + xᵢ * (exp (λ * t) - 1)`. The isochron slope
`(y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)` is `1`, since it equals `exp (λ * 10) - 1` and `exp (log 2) = 2`.
