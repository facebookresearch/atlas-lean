# The isochron slope gives back the age

For a decay constant `λ > 0` and real `t` and `b`, let two samples have distinct parent/stable
ratios `x₁ ≠ x₂` and daughter/stable ratios on the isochron `yᵢ = b + xᵢ * (exp (λ * t) - 1)`.
Then their slope `m = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)` gives back the age: `log (1 + m) / λ = t`.
