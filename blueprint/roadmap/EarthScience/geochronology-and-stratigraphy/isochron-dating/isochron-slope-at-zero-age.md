# The synthetic isochron has slope 0 at age zero

In the synthetic isochron with decay constant `λ = log 2 / 10`, parent/reference ratios `x₁ = 1`
and `x₂ = 2`, any real common initial ratio `b` and daughter/reference ratios
`yᵢ = b + xᵢ * (exp (λ * t) - 1)`, at age `t = 0` the slope `(y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)` is
`exp (λ * 0) - 1 = 0`.
