# Isochron age change under a slope perturbation, with remainder

For a decay constant `λ > 0`, a slope `m > 0` and a nonnegative slope perturbation `δ ≥ 0`, the
change of the isochron age `log (1 + m) / λ` differs from the first-order term
`δ / (λ * (1 + m))` by at most `δ ^ 2 / (2 * λ * (1 + m) ^ 2)`:
`|log (1 + (m + δ)) / λ - log (1 + m) / λ - δ / (λ * (1 + m))| ≤ δ ^ 2 / (2 * λ * (1 + m) ^ 2)`.
