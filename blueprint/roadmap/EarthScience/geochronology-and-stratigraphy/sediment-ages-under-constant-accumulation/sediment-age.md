# Sediment age under constant accumulation

The age of the sediment at depth `d` (positive downward) under a constant accumulation speed `v`,
with no compaction, no hiatus and present surface age zero: `d / v`. It is defined for all real
`v` and `d`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `v = 0`. The intended domain is
`v > 0` and `d ≥ 0`.
