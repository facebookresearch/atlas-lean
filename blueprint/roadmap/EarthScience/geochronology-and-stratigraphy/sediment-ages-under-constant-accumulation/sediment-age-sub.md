# Age difference between two horizons

Sediment at depths `d₁` and `d₂` differs in age by the separation divided by the accumulation
speed: `d₂ / v - d₁ / v = (d₂ - d₁) / v`. This holds for every real `v`; with the convention
`x / 0 = 0` both sides are `0` when `v = 0`. The intended domain is `v > 0`.
