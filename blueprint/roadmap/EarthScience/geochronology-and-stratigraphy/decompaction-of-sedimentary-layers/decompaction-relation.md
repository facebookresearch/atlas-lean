# Decompaction conserves solid volume and thickens the layer

A layer of present thickness `h ≥ 0` and present porosity `φ`, with `0 ≤ φ ≤ φ0 < 1`,
decompacted to porosity `φ0`, has thickness `h0 = h * (1 - φ) / (1 - φ0)`. It conserves the solid
volume, `h0 * (1 - φ0) = h * (1 - φ)`, and is at least the present thickness, `h ≤ h0`. The
lower bound `0 ≤ φ` (hence `0 ≤ φ0`) restricts to physical porosities; both conclusions hold
without it, for `h ≥ 0` and every real `φ ≤ φ0 < 1`.
