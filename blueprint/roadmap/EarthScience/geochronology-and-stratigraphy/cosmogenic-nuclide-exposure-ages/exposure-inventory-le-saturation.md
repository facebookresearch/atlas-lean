# An inventory starting at or below saturation stays at or below it

For a production rate `P > 0`, a decay constant `λ > 0` and an inherited inventory
`0 ≤ N0 ≤ P / λ`, the inventory `N(t) = N0 * exp (-λ * t) + (P / λ) * (1 - exp (-λ * t))` is at
most the saturation value: `N(t) ≤ P / λ` for every `t ≥ 0`.
