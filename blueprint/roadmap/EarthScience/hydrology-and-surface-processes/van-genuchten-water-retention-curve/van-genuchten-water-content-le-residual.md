# A saturated content not above the residual one caps the water content

If `θs ≤ θr`, then for `α > 0`, `n > 1` and a head `h ≤ 0` the water content
`θr + (θs - θr) * S_e(h)`, with the [van Genuchten effective saturation](van-genuchten-se.md)
`S_e`, is at most `θr`; so a water content above `θr` requires `θr < θs`.
