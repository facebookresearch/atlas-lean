# Settlement of the consolidation mode as an effective-stress integral

Let $n$ be a natural number, $\sigma$ a field of $n \times n$ total stress matrices depending on the
depth $z$ and constant in time, $k$ an index and $m_v$, $u_0$, $H$, $c_v$, $t$ real numbers. Write
$\sigma'(z, t) = \sigma(z) - u(z, t) I$ for the compression-positive effective stress at pore
pressure $u(z, t)$, where $u(z, t)$ is the drained consolidation mode of a layer of thickness $H$ with consolidation coefficient $c_v$ and initial amplitude $u_0$, equal to $u_0 e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t} \sin(\pi z / H)$ when $H \neq 0$. Then

$$m_v \int_0^H \bigl(u(z, 0) - u(z, t)\bigr)\, dz = m_v \int_0^H \bigl(\sigma'(z, t)_{kk} - \sigma'(z, 0)_{kk}\bigr)\, dz ,$$

$m_v$ times the depth integral of the increase of the effective stress component $kk$.
