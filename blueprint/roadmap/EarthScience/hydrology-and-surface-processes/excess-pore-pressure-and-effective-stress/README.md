# Excess pore pressure and effective stress

Terzaghi's effective stress, the total stress minus the pore-water pressure, and the excess
pore-pressure ratio, the rise of the pore pressure divided by the initial vertical effective stress;
when that initial stress is positive, the ratio equals one exactly when the vertical effective stress
vanishes.
