# Effective-stress traction adds the pore pressure to the total traction

For an `n × n` tension-positive total stress `σ`, a pore pressure `u` and a face normal `v`, the
physical traction of the compression-positive effective stress `-σ - u I` is minus its action on
`v` (the compression-positive convention), and it equals the tension-positive total traction
`σ v` plus `u v`: `-((-σ - u I) v) = σ v + u v`.
