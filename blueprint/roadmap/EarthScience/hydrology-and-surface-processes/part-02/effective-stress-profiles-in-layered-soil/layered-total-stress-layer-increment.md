# Total-stress increment across one layer

Let `t i ≥ 0` be the thicknesses of the layers `i = 0, 1, …` (for every `i`), so that layer `i`
lies between the depths `z_i = ∑_{j<i} t j` and `z_{i+1}`, and let `γ i` be the layer unit
weights and `q` a surcharge (real numbers). If `k < n`, the increase of the total vertical
stress of the `n` layers across layer `k` is the integral of the constant `γ k` over that layer:
`σ(z_{k+1}) - σ(z_k) = ∫ γ k dz` over `[z_k, z_{k+1}]`, that is `γ k * t k`. This concerns a
constant unit weight in each layer, not an overburden integral for a varying profile.
