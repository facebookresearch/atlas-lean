# Continuous, layerwise affine effective stress with an interface table

Let `t i ≥ 0` be the thicknesses of the layers `i = 0, 1, …` (for every `i`), so that layer `i`
lies between the depths `z_i = ∑_{j<i} t j` and `z_{i+1}`, and let `γ i` be the layer unit
weights and `q` a surcharge (real numbers). Let `γw` be the unit weight of water and let the
water table lie at the top of layer `m`, at depth `z_m`. Then the effective stress `σ'` of the
`n` layers is continuous in depth, and on every layer `k < n` it is affine: there are real `a`,
`b` with `σ'(z) = a + b * z` for all `z` with `z_k ≤ z ≤ z_{k+1}`.
