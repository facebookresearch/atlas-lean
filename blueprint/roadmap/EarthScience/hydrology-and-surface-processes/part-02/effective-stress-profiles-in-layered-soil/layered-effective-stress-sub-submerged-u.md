# Submerged unit weight is the effective-stress slope below the table

Let `t i ≥ 0` be the thicknesses of the layers `i = 0, 1, …` (for every `i`), so that layer `i`
lies between the depths `z_i = ∑_{j<i} t j` and `z_{i+1}`, and let `γ i` be the layer unit
weights and `q` a surcharge (real numbers). Let the water table lie at the top of layer `m`,
at depth `z_m`, and let `γw` be the unit weight of water. Let layer `k`, with `k < n` and
`m ≤ k`, be saturated: `γ k = (Gs + e) * γw / (1 + e)` for a specific gravity `Gs` and a void
ratio `e` with `1 + e ≠ 0`. If `z_k ≤ z₁ ≤ z₂ ≤ z_{k+1}`, the effective stress of the `n` layers
changes by `σ'(z₂) - σ'(z₁) = γ_sub * (z₂ - z₁)`, where `γ_sub = (Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` is the
submerged unit weight.
