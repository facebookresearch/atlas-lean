# The layered effective-stress profile is continuous in depth

For every number of layers `n`, surcharge `q`, unit weight of water `γw`, water-table depth `zw`,
thicknesses `t i` and unit weights `γ i` (real numbers, of any sign), the effective stress
`σ'(z) = q + ∑_{i<n} γ i * d_i(z) - γw * max (z - zw) 0`
is a continuous function of the depth `z`, where `d_i(z)` is the
[thickness of layer `i` traversed](traversed-thickness.md) down to `z`.
