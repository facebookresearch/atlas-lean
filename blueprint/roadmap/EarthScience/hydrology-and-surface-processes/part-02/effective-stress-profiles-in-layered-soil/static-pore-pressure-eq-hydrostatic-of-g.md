# Below the water table the static pore pressure is hydrostatic

For all real `ρ`, `g`, a water-table depth `zw` and a depth `z ≥ zw`, the static pore pressure
with unit weight of water `γw = ρ * g` is the hydrostatic profile `p(d) = 0 + ρ * g * d`, with
gauge pressure zero at the table, at the depth `d = z - zw` below the table:
`ρ * g * max (z - zw) 0 = 0 + ρ * g * (z - zw)`. Moreover `p` has derivative `ρ * g` at
`z - zw`.
