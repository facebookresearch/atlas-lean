# Equal products $V_p c_f$ give equal linearized produced masses

For all real $V_{p,1}$, $V_{p,2}$, $c_1$, $c_2$, $\rho_0$, $p_0$ and $p$ with
$V_{p,1} c_1 = V_{p,2} c_2$, the [linearized produced masses](linear-produced-mass.md) agree:
$V_{p,1} \rho_0 c_1 (p_0 - p) = V_{p,2} \rho_0 c_2 (p_0 - p)$. A linearized history therefore cannot
separate $V_p$ from $c_f$.
