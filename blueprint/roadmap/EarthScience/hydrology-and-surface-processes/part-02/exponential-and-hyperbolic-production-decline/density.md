# Fluid density under the exponential compressibility law

For real $\rho_0$, $c_f$, $p_0$ and $p$, the density at pressure $p$ under the exponential
compressibility law with reference density $\rho_0$ at the reference pressure $p_0$ and fluid
compressibility $c_f$ is

$$\rho(p) = \rho_0\, e^{c_f (p - p_0)}.$$

It is defined for all real inputs; in the physical reading $\rho_0 > 0$ and $c_f > 0$.
