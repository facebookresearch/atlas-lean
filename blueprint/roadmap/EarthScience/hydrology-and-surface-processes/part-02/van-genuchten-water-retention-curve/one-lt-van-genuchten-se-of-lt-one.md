# For 0 < n < 1 the saturation exceeds one at every negative head

For `α > 0`, `0 < n < 1` and a head `h < 0`, the outer exponent `-(1 - 1 / n)` is positive and the
[van Genuchten effective saturation](van-genuchten-se.md) satisfies `S_e(h) > 1`; so for these
exponents the [upper bound `S_e ≤ 1`](van-genuchten-se-pos-and-le-one.md) fails at every negative
head.
