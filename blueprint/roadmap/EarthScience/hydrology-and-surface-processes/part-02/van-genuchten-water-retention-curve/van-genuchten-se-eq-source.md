# Gauge-head and source forms of the van Genuchten saturation agree

For `α > 0`, `n > 1` and `h ≤ 0`, the gauge-head form
`S_e(h) = (1 + (α |h|) ^ n) ^ (-(1 - 1 / n))` of the
[van Genuchten effective saturation](van-genuchten-se.md) equals Eq. [3] of van Genuchten (1980),
`(1 / (1 + (α h_p) ^ n)) ^ m`, with the positive head `h_p = -h` of the paper and `m = 1 - 1 / n`.
