# Slab weight resolved down the slope is the driving shear stress

For a slip-plane area `A > 0` and all real `γ`, `z` and `β`, the downslope component of the weight
of the slab of volume `A * z * cos β`, per unit slip-plane area, is the driving shear stress:
`γ * (A * z * cos β) * sin β / A = γ * z * sin β * cos β`.
