# Without friction the safety ratio does not depend on pore pressure

For all real `c`, `γ`, `z` and `β`, at friction angle `φ = 0` the safety ratio
`F(u) = (c + (σn - u) * tan 0) / τd`, with `σn = γ * z * cos β ^ 2` and
`τd = γ * z * sin β * cos β`, equals `c / τd` for every pore pressure `0 ≤ u ≤ σn`, since
`tan 0 = 0`. On a genuine slope `τd > 0`; where `τd = 0` both sides are `0` by the convention
`x / 0 = 0`.
