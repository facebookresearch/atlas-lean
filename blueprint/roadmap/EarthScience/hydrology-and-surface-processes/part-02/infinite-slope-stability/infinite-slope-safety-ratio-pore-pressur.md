# Difference of safety ratios at two pore pressures

For all real `c`, `φ`, `γ`, `z`, `β`, `u₁` and `u₂`, the safety ratios
`F(u) = (c + (σn - u) * tan φ) / τd`, with `σn = γ * z * cos β ^ 2` and
`τd = γ * z * sin β * cos β`, at the pore pressures `u₁` and `u₂` differ by
`F(u₁) - F(u₂) = (u₂ - u₁) * tan φ / τd`. This algebraic identity also holds where `τd = 0` or
`cos φ = 0`, with the convention `x / 0 = 0` on both sides; it is not a monotonicity claim.
