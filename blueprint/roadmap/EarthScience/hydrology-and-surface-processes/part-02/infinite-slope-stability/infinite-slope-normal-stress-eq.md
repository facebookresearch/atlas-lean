# Slab weight resolved normal to the slip plane is the normal stress

For a slip-plane area `A > 0` and all real `γ`, `z` and `β`, the normal component of the weight of
the slab of volume `A * z * cos β`, per unit slip-plane area, is the normal stress:
`γ * (A * z * cos β) * cos β / A = γ * z * cos β ^ 2`.
