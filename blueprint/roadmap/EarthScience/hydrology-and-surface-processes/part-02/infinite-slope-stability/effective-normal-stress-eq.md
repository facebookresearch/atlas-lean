# Normal component of the effective stress on a unit normal

For an `m × m` compression-positive total stress `σc`, a pore pressure `u` and a unit vector `v`
(`v · v = 1`), the normal component of the effective stress `σc - u I` on `v` is the total normal
component minus `u`: `v · ((σc - u I) v) = v · (σc v) - u`.
