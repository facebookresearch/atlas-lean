# The Mohr–Coulomb safety ratio is at least one exactly when τR ≥ τd

For a unit weight `γ > 0`, a vertical depth `z > 0`, a slope angle `0 < β < π / 2` and all real
`c`, `φ` and `u`, the [Mohr–Coulomb resisting stress](infinite-slope-resisting-stress.md)
`τR = c + (σn - u) * tan φ`, with `σn = γ * z * cos β ^ 2` (and `tan φ = 0` where `cos φ = 0`, by
the convention `x / 0 = 0`), and the driving stress `τd = γ * z * sin β * cos β` satisfy
`τR / τd ≥ 1` if and only if `τR ≥ τd`.
