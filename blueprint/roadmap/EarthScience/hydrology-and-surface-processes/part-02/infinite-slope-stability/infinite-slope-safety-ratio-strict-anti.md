# With friction the safety ratio strictly falls as pore pressure rises

Let `γ > 0` be the unit weight, `z > 0` the vertical depth and `0 < β < π / 2` the slope angle
(a genuine slope), let `c` be the effective cohesion and `φ` the friction angle, and let
`F(u) = (c + (σn - u) * tan φ) / τd` be the safety ratio at slip-plane pore pressure `u`, with the
normal stress `σn = γ * z * cos β ^ 2` and the driving stress `τd = γ * z * sin β * cos β`.

If `0 < φ < π / 2`, then `F` is strictly antitone on `0 ≤ u ≤ σn`: for `0 ≤ u₁ < u₂ ≤ σn`,
`F(u₂) < F(u₁)`.
