# The safety ratio is at least one exactly when resistance dominates

For a unit weight `γ > 0`, a vertical depth `z > 0`, a slope angle `0 < β < π / 2` and any real
resisting stress `τR`, the driving stress `τd = γ * z * sin β * cos β` is positive, and the safety
ratio satisfies `τR / τd ≥ 1` if and only if `τR ≥ τd`.
