# Thiem head difference between two radii

For a nonzero transmissivity `T`, a pumping rate `Q`, an additive constant `C` and radii `r1 > 0`
and `r2 > 0`, the steady confined radial-flow profile `h(r) = C + Q * log r / (2 * π * T)` rises
from `r1` to `r2` by `h(r2) - h(r1) = Q * log (r2 / r1) / (2 * π * T)`.
