# The settlement of the consolidation mode is nonnegative

If $H > 0$, $c_v \ge 0$, $m_v \ge 0$, $u_0 \ge 0$ and $t \ge 0$, then the settlement over $[0, t]$
of the drained consolidation mode $u(z, t) = u_0 e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t} \sin(\pi z / H)$,
$m_v \int_0^H \bigl(u(z, 0) - u(z, t)\bigr)\, dz$, is nonnegative.
