# Settlement of the consolidation mode

For real numbers $m_v$, $u_0$, $H$, $c_v$ and $t$, let

$$s(t) = m_v \int_0^H \bigl(u(z, 0) - u(z, t)\bigr)\, dz ,$$

which is $0$ when $H = 0$, and where for $H \neq 0$,
$u(z, t) = u_0 e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t} \sin(\pi z / H)$ is the drained consolidation mode. For a layer
of thickness $H > 0$ with consolidation coefficient $c_v > 0$ under constant total stress, coefficient
of volume compressibility $m_v$ and $t \ge 0$, it is the settlement over the time interval $[0, t]$
contributed by the mode: $m_v$ times the depth integral of the dissipated excess pore pressure.
