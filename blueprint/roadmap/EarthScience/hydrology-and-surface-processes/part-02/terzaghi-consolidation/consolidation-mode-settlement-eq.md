# Settlement of the consolidation mode in closed form

For real numbers $m_v$, $u_0$, $c_v$, $t$ and $H \neq 0$, the drained consolidation mode
$u(z, t) = u_0 e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t} \sin(\pi z / H)$ satisfies

$$m_v \int_0^H \bigl(u(z, 0) - u(z, t)\bigr)\, dz = \frac{2 m_v u_0 H}{\pi} \Bigl(1 - e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t}\Bigr) .$$
