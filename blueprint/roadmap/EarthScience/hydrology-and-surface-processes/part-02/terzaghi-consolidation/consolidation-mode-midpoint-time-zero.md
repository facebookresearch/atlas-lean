# The consolidation mode starts at its full amplitude at mid-depth

For a layer thickness `H ≠ 0` and all real `u0` and `cv`, the
[consolidation mode](consolidation-mode.md) at `t = 0` and mid-depth `z = H / 2` equals the
amplitude `u0`: `H` is the full layer thickness, so the sine factor there is `sin (π / 2) = 1`.
