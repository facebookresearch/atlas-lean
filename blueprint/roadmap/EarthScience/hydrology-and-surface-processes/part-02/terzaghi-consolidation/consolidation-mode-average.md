# Depth average of the consolidation mode

For real numbers $u_0$, $H$, $c_v$ and $t$, the depth average of the consolidation mode is

$$\bar u(t) = \frac{1}{H} \int_0^H u(z, t)\, dz ,$$

read as $0$ when $H = 0$. For $H \neq 0$, $u(z, t) = u_0 e^{-c_v (\pi / H)^2 t} \sin(\pi z / H)$ is the
drained consolidation mode of a layer of thickness $H$ with consolidation coefficient $c_v$ and
initial amplitude $u_0$, and $\bar u(t)$ is its mean over the depths between $0$ and $H$; the
physical case is $H > 0$ and $c_v > 0$.
