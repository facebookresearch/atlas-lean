# The Rankine active thrust acts at one third of the wall height

For `Ka > 0`, `γ > 0` and a wall height `H > 0`, the linear pressure `p(z) = Ka * γ * z` on a wall
of unit width over the depths `0 ≤ z ≤ H` has resultant force `F = ∫_0^H p(z) dz` and first moment
about the top `M = ∫_0^H z * p(z) dz`, and the resultant acts at height `H - M / F = H / 3` above
the base, that is, at depth `M / F = 2 * H / 3`.
