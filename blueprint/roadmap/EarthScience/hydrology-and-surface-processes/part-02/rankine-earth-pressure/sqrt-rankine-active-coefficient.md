# Square root of the Rankine active coefficient, cosine over one plus sine

For every friction angle $\varphi$ with $0 \le \varphi < \pi / 2$, the Rankine active coefficient
$K_a(\varphi) = (1 - \sin \varphi) / (1 + \sin \varphi)$ satisfies

$$\sqrt{K_a(\varphi)} = \frac{\cos \varphi}{1 + \sin \varphi} .$$
