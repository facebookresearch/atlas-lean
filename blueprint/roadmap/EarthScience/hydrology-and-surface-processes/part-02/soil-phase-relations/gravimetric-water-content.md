# Gravimetric water content, or dry-basis moisture content, Mw / Ms

For a water mass `Mw` and a solid mass `Ms`, the gravimetric water content is `w = Mw / Ms`. It is
defined for all real `Mw` and `Ms`: with the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `Ms = 0`. The
intended domain is `Ms > 0` and `Mw ≥ 0`.

In drying, the same quotient of the water mass by the dry-solid mass is the dry-basis moisture
content `X = Mw / Ms`, the water mass per unit mass of dry solid, as opposed to the
[wet-basis content](../../../../Chemistry/amounts-and-stoichiometry/moisture-content-on-wet-and-dry-bases/wet-basis.md)
`Mw / (Mw + Ms)`. With both masses in the same unit it is a pure number, nonnegative and unbounded
above on the intended domain.

The other form has its own page: [Dry-basis moisture ratio](../../../../Chemistry/amounts-and-stoichiometry/moisture-content-on-wet-and-dry-bases/dry-basis.md).
