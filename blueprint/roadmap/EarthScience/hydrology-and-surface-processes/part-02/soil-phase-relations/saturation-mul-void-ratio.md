# Saturation times void ratio is water content times specific gravity

Let a soil element have solid volume `Vs > 0`, void volume `Vv > 0`, water volume `Vw` (any real
number), solid density `ρs > 0` and water density `ρw > 0`. Then the
[degree of saturation](degree-of-saturation.md) `S = Vw / Vv`, the [void ratio](void-ratio.md)
`e = Vv / Vs`, the [gravimetric water content](gravimetric-water-content.md)
`w = (ρw * Vw) / (ρs * Vs)` (water mass over solid mass) and the
[specific gravity](specific-gravity.md) `Gs = ρs / ρw` satisfy `S * e = w * Gs`.
