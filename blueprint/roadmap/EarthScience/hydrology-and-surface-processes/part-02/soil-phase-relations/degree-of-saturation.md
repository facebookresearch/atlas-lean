# Degree of saturation of a soil element

For a water volume `Vw` and a void volume `Vv`, the degree of saturation is `S = Vw / Vv`, the
fraction of the voids filled with water. It is defined for all real `Vw` and `Vv`: with the
convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `Vv = 0`. The intended domain is `0 ≤ Vw ≤ Vv` with `Vv > 0`,
where it [lies between `0` and `1`](degree-of-saturation-mem-icc.md).
