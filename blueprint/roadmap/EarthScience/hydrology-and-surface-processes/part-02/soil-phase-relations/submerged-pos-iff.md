# Positive submerged unit weight exactly for specific gravity above one

For a positive water unit weight `γw` and `1 + e > 0`, the
[submerged unit weight](submerged-unit-weight.md) `(Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` is positive if and
only if the specific gravity satisfies `Gs > 1`; solids no denser than water (`Gs ≤ 1`) give no
positive submerged weight.
