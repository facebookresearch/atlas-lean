# Submerged unit weight is the saturated one minus that of water

For `1 + e ≠ 0` and all real `Gs` and `γw`, the [submerged unit weight](submerged-unit-weight.md)
`(Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` equals the [bulk unit weight](bulk-unit-weight.md) at full saturation,
`(Gs + 1 * e) * γw / (1 + e)` (degree of saturation `S = 1`), minus the water unit weight `γw`.
