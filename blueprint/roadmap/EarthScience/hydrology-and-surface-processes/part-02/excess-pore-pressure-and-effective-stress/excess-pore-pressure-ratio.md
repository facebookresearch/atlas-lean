# Excess pore-pressure ratio

Let $\sigma$ be a fixed $n \times n$ compression-positive total stress matrix, $k$ an index and $u$ a
pore pressure depending on time, with vertical effective stress
$\sigma'_v(t) = \bigl(\sigma - u(t) I\bigr)_{kk}$. The excess pore-pressure ratio at time $t$ is

$$r_u(t) = \frac{u(t) - u(0)}{\sigma'_v(0)} ,$$

taken to be $0$ when $\sigma'_v(0) = 0$; it has its physical meaning when $\sigma'_v(0) > 0$.
