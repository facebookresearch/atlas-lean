# Cyclic soil response and liquefaction

This node records open directions for cyclic soil response: the evolution of excess pore pressure under cyclic loading, drainage, site
amplification and calibrated liquefaction models. The existing result that the excess pore-pressure ratio equals one exactly when the
vertical effective stress vanishes holds under fixed total stress and its normalization; it is not a universal liquefaction-trigger
criterion.
