# Vertical effective stress

Let $\sigma$ be a fixed $n \times n$ compression-positive total stress matrix, $k$ an index (the
vertical direction) and $u$ a pore pressure depending on time. The vertical effective stress at time
$t$ is the $(k, k)$ entry of the effective stress $\sigma - u(t) I$, that is
$\sigma'_v(t) = \bigl(\sigma - u(t) I\bigr)_{kk}$.
