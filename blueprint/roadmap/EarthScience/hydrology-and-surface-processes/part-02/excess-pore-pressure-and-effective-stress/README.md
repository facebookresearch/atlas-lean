# Excess pore pressure and effective stress

Terzaghi's effective stress, the total stress minus the pore-water pressure, and the excess
pore-pressure ratio, the rise of the pore pressure divided by the initial vertical effective stress;
when that initial stress is positive, the ratio equals one exactly when the vertical effective
stress vanishes. For a stress tensor, the effective stress is written in the compression-positive
and the tension-positive sign conventions, with the conversion between them and the traction it
exerts on a face.
