# Sign conversion of the effective stress

The conversion between the tension-positive and compression-positive sign conventions is negation,
`σ ↦ -σ`. For every `n × n` compression-positive total stress `σc` and pore pressure `u`,
converting the compression-positive effective stress `σc - u I` gives the tension-positive
effective stress of the converted total stress `-σc`: `-(σc - u I) = (-σc) + u I`.
