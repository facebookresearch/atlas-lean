# The excess pore-pressure ratio is one exactly when the effective stress vanishes

Let $\sigma$ be an $n \times n$ total stress matrix, $k$ an index and $u$ a pore pressure depending
on time, with vertical effective stress $\sigma'_v(t) = \bigl(\sigma - u(t) I\bigr)_{kk}$ and excess
pore-pressure ratio $r_u(t) = (u(t) - u(0)) / \sigma'_v(0)$. If $\sigma'_v(0) > 0$, then for every
time $t$, $r_u(t) = 1$ if and only if $\sigma'_v(t) = 0$.
