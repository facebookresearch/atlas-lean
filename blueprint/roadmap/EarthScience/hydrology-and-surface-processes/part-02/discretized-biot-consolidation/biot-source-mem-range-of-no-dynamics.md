# Without dynamics the Biot source lies in the range of H

For finite index sets and any matrices `K` and `H`: if paths `u`, `p` satisfy the finite Biot rate
system with no coupling, no storage compliance and no load rate (`Q = 0`, `C = 0`, `df = 0`),
then the source lies in the range of `H`: `s = H w` for some vector `w` (the storage balance
reads `0 = s - H p(t)`). So a source outside the range of a singular `H` admits no solution.
