# The modified Cam-Clay yield function in translated-ellipse form

For all real `M`, `pc`, `p` and `q`,
`q ^ 2 + M ^ 2 * p * (p - pc) = M ^ 2 * (p - pc / 2) ^ 2 + q ^ 2 - (M * pc / 2) ^ 2`. So for
`M ≠ 0` and `pc ≠ 0` the yield locus `F = 0` is an ellipse centred at `(pc / 2, 0)`, with
semi-axes `|pc| / 2` and `|M * pc| / 2` (`pc / 2` and `M * pc / 2` when `M, pc > 0`); for `M ≠ 0`
and `pc = 0` it is the origin. At `M = 0` the condition `F ≤ 0` reduces to `q ^ 2 ≤ 0`: the
elastic domain is the line `q = 0`, unbounded in `p`.
