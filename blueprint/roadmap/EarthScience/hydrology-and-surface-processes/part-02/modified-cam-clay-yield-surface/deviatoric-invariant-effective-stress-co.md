# Pore pressure does not change the deviatoric invariant

For every compression-positive `3 × 3` total stress `σc` and pore pressure `u`, the deviatoric
invariant `q(σ) = √(3/2 * tr (sᵀ s))`, with `s = σ - (tr σ / 3) I`, is the same for the effective
stress `σc - u I` as for `σc`: `q(σc - u I) = q(σc)`.
