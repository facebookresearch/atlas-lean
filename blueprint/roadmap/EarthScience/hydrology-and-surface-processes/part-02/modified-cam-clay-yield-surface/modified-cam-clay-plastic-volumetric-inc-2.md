# For positive M and λ the increment vanishes exactly at p = pc/2

For `M > 0` and a plastic multiplier `λ > 0`, the plastic volumetric increment
`Δε = λ * M ^ 2 * (2 * p - pc)` is zero if and only if `p = pc / 2`. No yield-locus
hypothesis is needed; with `λ = 0` the increment is zero at every `p`.
