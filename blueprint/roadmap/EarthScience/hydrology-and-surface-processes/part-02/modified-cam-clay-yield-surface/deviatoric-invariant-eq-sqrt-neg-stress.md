# For symmetric stress q is the square root of three times J₂

For a symmetric real `3 × 3` stress `σ` (`σᵀ = σ`), with deviatoric part `s = σ - (tr σ / 3) I`,
the deviatoric invariant `q = √(3/2 * tr (sᵀ s))` equals `√(3 J₂)`, where `J₂ = -I₂(s)` and
`I₂(s) = ((tr s) ^ 2 - tr (s s)) / 2` is the second invariant of `s`.
