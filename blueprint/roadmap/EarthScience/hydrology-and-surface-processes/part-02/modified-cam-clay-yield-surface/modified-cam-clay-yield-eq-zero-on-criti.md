# The critical-state line meets the yield locus at two points

For `M > 0` and `pc > 0`, and the yield function `F(p, q) = q ^ 2 + M ^ 2 * p * (p - pc)`, a point
`(p, q)` satisfies `q ≥ 0`, `q = M * p` and `F(p, q) = 0` if and only if it is the origin `(0, 0)`
or the critical point `(pc / 2, M * pc / 2)`. This is yield-locus geometry at fixed `pc`; no flow
rule or hardening evolution is involved.
