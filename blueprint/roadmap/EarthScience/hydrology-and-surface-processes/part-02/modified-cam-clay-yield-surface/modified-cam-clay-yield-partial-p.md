# Partial derivative of the Cam-Clay yield function in p

For real `M`, `pc` and `p`, the partial derivative of the modified Cam-Clay yield function
`q ^ 2 + M ^ 2 * p * (p - pc)` with respect to the compression-positive mean stress `p`, at fixed
`M`, `pc` and `q`, is given by the explicit expression `∂F/∂p = M ^ 2 * (2 * p - pc)`, which does
not depend on `q`.
