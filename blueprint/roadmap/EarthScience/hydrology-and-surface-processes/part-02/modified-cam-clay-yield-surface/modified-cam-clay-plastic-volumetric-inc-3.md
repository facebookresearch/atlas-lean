# For p > 0 on the yield locus the increment vanishes exactly at q = M p

Let `M > 0`, `pc > 0`, `p > 0`, `q ≥ 0` and a plastic multiplier `λ > 0`, and let `(p, q)` lie on
the yield locus: `q ^ 2 + M ^ 2 * p * (p - pc) = 0`. Then the plastic volumetric increment
`Δε = λ * M ^ 2 * (2 * p - pc)` vanishes if and only if `p = pc / 2`, and on the locus
`p = pc / 2` holds if and only if `q = M * p`, the critical-state line. This is a pointwise
statement at fixed `pc`: no hardening evolution, and no claim that flow reaches or stays at a
critical state.
