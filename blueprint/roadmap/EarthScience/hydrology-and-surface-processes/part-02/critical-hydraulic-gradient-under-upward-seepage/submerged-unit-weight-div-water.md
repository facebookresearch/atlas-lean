# Submerged unit weight relative to the unit weight of water

For a unit weight of water `γw ≠ 0`, a specific gravity `Gs` and a void ratio `e` (real
numbers), the submerged unit weight `γ_sub = (Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` satisfies
`γ_sub / γw = (Gs - 1) / (1 + e)`. At `e = -1` both sides are `0` by the convention `x / 0 = 0`;
the intended domain is `e ≥ 0`.
