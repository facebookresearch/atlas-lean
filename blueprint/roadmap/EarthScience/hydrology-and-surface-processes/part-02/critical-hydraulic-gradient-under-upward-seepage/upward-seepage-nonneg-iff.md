# Nonnegative effective stress exactly up to the critical gradient

Let `γw > 0` be the unit weight of water, `i ≥ 0` the upward hydraulic gradient, `e ≥ 0` the void
ratio and `Gs` the specific gravity, and let `γ_sub = (Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` be the submerged
unit weight. Then the effective stress `z * (γ_sub - i * γw)` is nonnegative at every depth `z > 0`
if and only if the gradient is at most the critical gradient: `i ≤ (Gs - 1) / (1 + e)`.
