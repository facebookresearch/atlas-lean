# Effective stress under upward seepage

Let `e` be a void ratio with `1 + e ≠ 0`, and `Gs`, `γw`, `i` and `z` real numbers (specific
gravity, unit weight of water, gradient and depth). The total vertical stress `γ_sat * z` of the
saturated column, with `γ_sat = (Gs + e) * γw / (1 + e)`, minus the pore pressure
`γw * (i * z + z)` of the upward head profile `h(z) = i * z`, is the effective stress
`σ'(z) = z * (γ_sub - i * γw)`, where `γ_sub = (Gs - 1) * γw / (1 + e)` is the submerged unit
weight.
