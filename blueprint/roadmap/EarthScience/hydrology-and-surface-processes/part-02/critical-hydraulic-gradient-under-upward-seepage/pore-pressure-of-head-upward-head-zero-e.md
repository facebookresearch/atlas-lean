# No-flow pore pressure is the hydrostatic profile with unit weight ρ g

For all real `ρ`, `g` and `z`, the pore pressure of the zero-gradient head profile with unit
weight of water `γw = ρ * g` is the depth-coordinate hydrostatic profile `p(d) = p₀ + ρ * g * d`
with surface pressure `p₀ = 0`, evaluated at `z`: `ρ * g * (0 * z + z) = 0 + ρ * g * z`.
