# Hyperbolic cumulative production lies between zero and its asymptote

For `qi > 0`, `Di > 0`, `0 < b < 1` and `t ≥ 0`, the hyperbolic cumulative production
`Np(t) = qi * (1 - (1 + b * Di * t) ^ ((b - 1) / b)) / ((1 - b) * Di)` satisfies
`0 ≤ Np(t) ≤ qi / ((1 - b) * Di)`. The bound is the finite mathematical asymptote of the law, not
a reserve estimate.
