# Hyperbolic cumulative production in terms of the rate

For `qi > 0`, `Di > 0`, `0 < b < 1` and `t ≥ 0`, the hyperbolic cumulative production
`Np(t) = qi * (1 - (1 + b * Di * t) ^ ((b - 1) / b)) / ((1 - b) * Di)` and the hyperbolic rate
`q(t) = qi * (1 + b * Di * t) ^ (-1 / b)` satisfy
`Np(t) = qi ^ b * (qi ^ (1 - b) - q(t) ^ (1 - b)) / ((1 - b) * Di)` (real powers).
