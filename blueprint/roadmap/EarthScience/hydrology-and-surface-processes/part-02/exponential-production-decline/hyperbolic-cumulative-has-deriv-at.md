# The derivative of hyperbolic cumulative production is the rate

For `qi > 0`, `Di > 0`, `0 < b < 1` and `t ≥ 0`, the hyperbolic cumulative production
`Np(t) = qi * (1 - (1 + b * Di * t) ^ ((b - 1) / b)) / ((1 - b) * Di)` is differentiable at `t`,
with derivative the hyperbolic rate: `Np'(t) = q(t) = qi * (1 + b * Di * t) ^ (-1 / b)`.
