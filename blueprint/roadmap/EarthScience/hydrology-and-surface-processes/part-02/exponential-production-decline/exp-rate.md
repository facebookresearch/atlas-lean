# Production rate under exponential decline

For real numbers $q_i$, $D$ and $t$, let $q(t) = q_i e^{-D t}$. For $q_i > 0$, $D > 0$ and $t \ge 0$
it is the production rate under the stipulated exponential-decline law, with initial rate $q_i$ and
decline rate $D$; at $D = 0$ it is the constant rate $q_i$.
