# Series effective conductivity is at most the parallel one

For `n ≥ 1` layers with thicknesses `L i > 0` and conductivities `K i > 0` for every `i < n`, the
effective conductivity across the layering is at most the one along it:
`(∑_{i<n} L i) / (∑_{i<n} L i / K i) ≤ (∑_{i<n} L i * K i) / (∑_{i<n} L i)`.
