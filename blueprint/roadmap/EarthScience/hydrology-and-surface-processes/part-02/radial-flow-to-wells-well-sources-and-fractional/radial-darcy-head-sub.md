# Radial Darcy head difference on an annulus around a well

Let `r0 > 0` be the well radius and `Tr > 0` a constant transmissivity, and let the head `h` be
continuous on the closed annulus `r0 ≤ r ≤ r1`, with derivative `h'(r)` at every `r0 < r < r1`.
If the steady outward discharge `-2 * π * r * Tr * h'(r)` equals the same constant `Q` at every
`r0 < r < r1`, then `h(r) - h(r0) = -Q * log (r / r0) / (2 * π * Tr)` for every `r0 ≤ r ≤ r1`.
