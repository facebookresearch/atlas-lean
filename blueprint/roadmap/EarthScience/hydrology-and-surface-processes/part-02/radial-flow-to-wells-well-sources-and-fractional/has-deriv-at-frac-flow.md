# The fractional flow is differentiable for positive viscosities

For viscosities `μo > 0` and `μw > 0`, the [fractional flow](frac-flow.md)
`f(s) = μo * s ^ 2 / (μo * s ^ 2 + μw * (1 - s) ^ 2)` has derivative
`f'(s) = 2 * μo * μw * s * (1 - s) / (μo * s ^ 2 + μw * (1 - s) ^ 2) ^ 2` at every real `s`; its
denominator never vanishes.
