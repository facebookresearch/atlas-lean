# Normalized water saturation

For a connate water saturation `Swc` and a residual oil saturation `Sor`, the normalized water
saturation of `S` on the mobile range `[Swc, 1 - Sor]` is `s = (S - Swc) / ΔS`, with
`ΔS = 1 - Sor - Swc`. It is defined for all real `Swc`, `Sor` and `S`: with the convention
`x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `ΔS = 0`. The intended domain is `ΔS > 0`.
