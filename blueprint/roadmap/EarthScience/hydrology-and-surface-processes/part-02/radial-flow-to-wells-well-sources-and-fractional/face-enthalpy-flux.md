# Enthalpy flux through a face

For signed face mass fluxes `Fm j` and, at each face `j`, the specific internal energy `ef j`, the
pressure `pf j` and the density `ρf j` of the fluid crossing it, with every face density positive,
the enthalpy flux through face `j` is `Fm j * (ef j + pf j / ρf j)`: the mass flux times the
specific enthalpy `e + p / ρ`, the flow work being counted once, inside the enthalpy.
