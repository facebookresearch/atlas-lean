# Bound on the fractional-flow derivative

For viscosities `μo` and `μw`, the derivative bound is `B = (μo + μw) ^ 2 / (2 * μo * μw)`. It is
defined for all real `μo` and `μw`: with the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `μo * μw = 0`.
For positive viscosities it [bounds the fractional-flow derivative](frac-flow-deriv-mem-icc.md) on
`[0, 1]`.
