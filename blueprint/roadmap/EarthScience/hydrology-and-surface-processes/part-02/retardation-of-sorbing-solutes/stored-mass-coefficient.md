# Concentration-to-mass coefficient of a bulk cell

For a fixed bulk cell of volume `V`, porosity `phi`, dry bulk density `rho_b` and distribution
coefficient `Kd` (any real numbers), the coefficient converting aqueous concentration to
[stored mass](stored-mass.md) is `V * (phi + rho_b * Kd)`. It is
[positive](stored-mass-coefficient-pos.md) for `V > 0`, `phi > 0`, `rho_b ≥ 0` and `Kd ≥ 0`.
