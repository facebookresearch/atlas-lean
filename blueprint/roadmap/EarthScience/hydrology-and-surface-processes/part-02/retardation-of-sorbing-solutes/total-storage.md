# Total solute storage per bulk volume

For porosity `phi`, dry bulk density `rho_b`, distribution coefficient `Kd` and aqueous dissolved
concentration `c` (any real numbers), the total storage per bulk volume is
`phi * c + rho_b * (Kd * c)`: the dissolved storage `phi * c` plus the sorbed mass per bulk
volume `rho_b * S` under the linear equilibrium sorption `S = Kd * c`.
