# Stored mass is the cell coefficient times the concentration

For all real `V`, `phi`, `rho_b`, `Kd` and `c`, the [stored mass](stored-mass.md)
`V * (phi * c + rho_b * (Kd * c))` equals `V * (phi + rho_b * Kd) * c`, the
[concentration-to-mass coefficient](stored-mass-coefficient.md) times the concentration.
