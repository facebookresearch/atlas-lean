# Total storage is the retardation factor times the dissolved storage

For nonzero porosity `phi` and all real `rho_b`, `Kd` and `c`, the
[total storage](total-storage.md) `phi * c + rho_b * (Kd * c)` equals `phi * R * c`, where
`R = 1 + rho_b * Kd / phi` is the [retardation factor](retardation-factor.md): the dissolved
storage `phi * c` scaled by `R`.
