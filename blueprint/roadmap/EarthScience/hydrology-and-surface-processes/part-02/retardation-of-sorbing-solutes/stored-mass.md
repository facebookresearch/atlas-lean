# Stored solute mass in a bulk cell

For a fixed bulk cell of volume `V`, porosity `phi`, dry bulk density `rho_b`, distribution
coefficient `Kd` and aqueous concentration `c` (any real numbers), the stored solute mass is
`V * (phi * c + rho_b * (Kd * c))`, the bulk volume times the
[total storage per bulk volume](total-storage.md). The dissolved part occupies the carrier-water
volume `V * phi`; the sorbed part is mass on the solids of the same cell.
