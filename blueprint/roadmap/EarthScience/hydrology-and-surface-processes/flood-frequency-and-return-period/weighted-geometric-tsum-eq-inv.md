# Weighted geometric series for the mean recurrence time sums to 1/p

For a real number `p` with `0 < p ≤ 1`, the series `∑ (n + 1) * (p * (1 - p) ^ n)`, summed over
`n = 0, 1, 2, …`, has sum `1 / p`. This is an identity of real series: no random variable or
expectation appears. In the textbook flood-frequency model, where each year independently exceeds
a level with probability `p`, the first exceedance year `W` has `P(W = n + 1) = p * (1 - p) ^ n`,
so the series is the mean of `W`.
