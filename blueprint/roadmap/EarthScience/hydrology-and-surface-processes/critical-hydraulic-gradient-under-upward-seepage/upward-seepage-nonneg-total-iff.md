# Total stress covers pore pressure exactly up to the critical gradient

Let `γw > 0` be the unit weight of water, `i ≥ 0` the upward hydraulic gradient, `e ≥ 0` the void
ratio (so `1 + e ≠ 0`) and `Gs` the specific gravity. Then the total vertical stress
`γ_sat * z`, with `γ_sat = (Gs + e) * γw / (1 + e)`, minus the pore pressure `γw * (i * z + z)` of
the upward head profile `h(z) = i * z`, is nonnegative at every depth `z > 0` if and only if
`i ≤ (Gs - 1) / (1 + e)`.
