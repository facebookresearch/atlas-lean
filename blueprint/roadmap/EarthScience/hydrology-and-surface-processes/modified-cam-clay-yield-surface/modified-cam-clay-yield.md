# Modified Cam-Clay yield function

For a critical-state stress ratio `M`, a preconsolidation pressure `pc`, a compression-positive
effective mean stress `p` and a deviatoric stress `q` (real numbers), the modified Cam-Clay yield
function is `F(p, q) = q ^ 2 + M ^ 2 * p * (p - pc)`. The physical branch is `M > 0`, `pc > 0`
and `q ≥ 0`.
