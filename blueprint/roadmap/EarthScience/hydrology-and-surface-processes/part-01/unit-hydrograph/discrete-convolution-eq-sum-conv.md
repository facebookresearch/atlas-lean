# The unit-hydrograph outflow is the sum convolution

For all real sequences `p` and `u` and every step `k`, the
[unit-hydrograph outflow](discrete-convolution.md) `q k` is the
[sum convolution](../../../../Combinatorics/additive-combinatorics/additive-bases/additive-representation-functions/sum-conv.md) of `p` and `u` at `k`, `∑_{i + j = k} p i * u j`.
