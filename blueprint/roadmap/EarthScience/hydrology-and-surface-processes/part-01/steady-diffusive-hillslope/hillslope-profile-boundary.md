# The steady hillslope profile vanishes at both channels

For a diffusivity `D > 0`, a half-width `L > 0` and an uplift rate `U ≥ 0`, the steady diffusive
hillslope profile `z(x) = U * (L ^ 2 - x ^ 2) / (2 * D)` satisfies `z(L) = 0` and `z(-L) = 0`: it
meets the fixed base level at both ends of `[-L, L]`.
