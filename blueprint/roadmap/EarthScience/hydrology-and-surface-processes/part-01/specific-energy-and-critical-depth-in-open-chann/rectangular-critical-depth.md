# Critical depth in a rectangular channel

For a discharge per unit width `q` and gravity `g`, the critical depth is the real power
`hc = (q ^ 2 / g) ^ (1 / 3)`. It is defined for all real `q` and `g`, with the conventions
`x / 0 = 0`, `0 ^ y = 0` for `y ≠ 0` and `x ^ y = |x| ^ y * cos (π y)` for `x < 0`: it is `0` when
`q = 0` or `g = 0`, and for `g < 0` and `q ≠ 0` it is `|q ^ 2 / g| ^ (1 / 3) / 2`, not a cube root
of `q ^ 2 / g`. The intended domain is `q > 0` and `g > 0`, where it is the depth of
[least specific energy](specific-energy-unique-minimum.md).
