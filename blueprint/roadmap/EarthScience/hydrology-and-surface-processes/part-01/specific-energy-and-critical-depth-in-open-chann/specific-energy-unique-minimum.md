# Specific energy has a unique minimum over positive depths

For a discharge per unit width `q > 0` and gravity `g > 0`, the
[specific energy](rectangular-specific-energy.md) `E(h) = h + q ^ 2 / (2 * g * h ^ 2)` of a
rectangular channel has a unique minimum over the depths `h > 0`: the critical depth
`hc = (q ^ 2 / g) ^ (1 / 3)` is positive, satisfies `hc ^ 3 = q ^ 2 / g`, and every `h > 0` has
`E(h) ≥ E(hc)`, with equality if and only if `h = hc`.
