# Specific energy in a rectangular channel

For a rectangular channel with discharge per unit width `q`, gravity `g` and flow depth `h`, the
specific energy per unit weight is the depth plus the velocity head,
`E(h) = h + q ^ 2 / (2 * g * h ^ 2)`, the mean velocity being `q / h`. It is defined for all real
`q`, `g` and `h`: with the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `h` when `g = 0` or `h = 0` (so `0` at
`h = 0`). The intended domain is `g > 0` and `h > 0`.
