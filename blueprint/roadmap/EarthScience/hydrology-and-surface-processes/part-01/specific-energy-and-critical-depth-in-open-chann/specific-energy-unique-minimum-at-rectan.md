# The critical depth is the unique minimizer of the specific energy

For a discharge per unit width `q > 0` and gravity `g > 0`, the
[critical depth](rectangular-critical-depth.md) `hc = (q ^ 2 / g) ^ (1 / 3)` is positive, and every
depth `h > 0` has [specific energy](rectangular-specific-energy.md) `E(h) ≥ E(hc)`, with equality if
and only if `h = hc`.
