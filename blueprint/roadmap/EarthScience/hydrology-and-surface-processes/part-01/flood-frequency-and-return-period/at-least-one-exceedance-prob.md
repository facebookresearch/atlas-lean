# Probability of at least one exceedance in n independent years

Let `μ` be a probability measure and `p` an annual exceedance probability with `0 ≤ p ≤ 1`. If the
no-exceedance events `F i` of the years `i = 0, …, n - 1` are measurable and independent under `μ`,
and each has probability `μ(F i) = 1 - p`, then the probability of an exceedance in at least one
of the `n` years is `μ(⋃ᵢ (F i)ᶜ) = 1 - (1 - p) ^ n`. Independence and the constant annual
probability are assumptions of the model.
