# At least one exceedance from no exceedance, in terms of T = 1/p

Let `μ` be a probability measure and `F i`, for `i = 0, …, n - 1`, measurable events, the
no-exceedance events of `n` years, with `μ(⋂ᵢ F i) = (1 - p) ^ n` for a real number `p > 0`. Then,
writing `T = 1 / p`, the probability of an exceedance in at least one year is
`μ(⋃ᵢ (F i)ᶜ) = 1 - (1 - 1 / T) ^ n`. No independence is assumed.
