# Probability of no exceedance in n independent years

Let `μ` be a probability measure, `n` a natural number and `p` a real number with `0 ≤ p ≤ 1`. If
the no-exceedance events `F i` of the years `i = 0, …, n - 1` are measurable and independent under
`μ`, and each has probability `μ(F i) = 1 - p`, then the probability of no exceedance in any of the
`n` years is `μ(⋂ᵢ F i) = (1 - p) ^ n`.
