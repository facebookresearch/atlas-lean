# The equal-rate Streeter–Phelps deficit solves the deficit equation

For a positive rate `k > 0` shared by deoxygenation and reaeration (`ka = kd = k`) and all real
`L0`, `D0` and `t`, the oxygen deficit `D(t) = (D0 + k * L0 * t) * exp (-k * t)` has derivative
`k * L(t) - k * D(t)` at `t`, where `L(t) = L0 * exp (-k * t)`: it solves `D' = k * L - k * D`.
