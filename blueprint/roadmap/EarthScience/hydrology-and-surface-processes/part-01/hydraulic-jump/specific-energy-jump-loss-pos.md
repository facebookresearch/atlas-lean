# A jump to the larger conjugate depth dissipates energy

Let `g > 0` be the gravitational acceleration, `q` a discharge per unit width, and `0 < h1 < h2`
two depths with the same specific force `M(h) = h ^ 2 / 2 + q ^ 2 / (g * h)`. Then the specific
energy `E(h) = h + q ^ 2 / (2 * g * h ^ 2)` drops across the jump: `E(h1) - E(h2) > 0`.
