# Conjugate depths of a hydraulic jump

Let `g > 0` be the gravitational acceleration, `q` a discharge per unit width and
`M(h) = h ^ 2 / 2 + q ^ 2 / (g * h)` the specific force of a rectangular channel. If two unequal
positive depths `h1 ≠ h2` have the same specific force, `M(h1) = M(h2)`, then
`q ^ 2 = g * h1 * h2 * (h1 + h2) / 2`. Equal specific force is the momentum balance across a
hydraulic jump in a rectangular prismatic channel, with hydrostatic pressure away from the jump,
equal bed elevation and no friction over the jump control volume. Only `q ^ 2` enters, so `q` may
have either sign.
