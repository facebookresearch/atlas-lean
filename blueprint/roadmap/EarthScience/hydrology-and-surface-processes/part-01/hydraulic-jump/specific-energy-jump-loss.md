# Energy loss across a hydraulic jump

Let `g > 0` be the gravitational acceleration, `q` a discharge per unit width, and `h1, h2 > 0`
two depths with the same specific force `M(h) = h ^ 2 / 2 + q ^ 2 / (g * h)` (conjugate depths of
a hydraulic jump in a rectangular channel). Then their specific energies
`E(h) = h + q ^ 2 / (2 * g * h ^ 2)` differ by `E(h1) - E(h2) = (h2 - h1) ^ 3 / (4 * h1 * h2)`.
