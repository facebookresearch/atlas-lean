# Manning's law at the friction slope returns the discharge

For a roughness `n > 0`, a width `b > 0`, a depth `y > 0` and a discharge `Q ≥ 0`, the Manning
discharge at flow area `b * y`, hydraulic radius `R = (b * y) / (b + 2 * y)` and slope equal to
the friction slope `S_f(y) = (n * Q / ((b * y) * R ^ (2 / 3))) ^ 2` is exactly `Q`:
`(1 / n) * (b * y) * R ^ (2 / 3) * √(S_f(y)) = Q`.
