# The terminal deficit is the storage deficit after all n steps

For required releases `R` and inflows `Q` over `n` time steps, the
[terminal storage deficit](terminal-storage-deficit.md) equals the
[storage deficit](required-storage-at.md) `K_n` after `t = n` steps. It is not the storage capacity
needed over the horizon, which is the [worst required storage](worst-required-storage.md).
