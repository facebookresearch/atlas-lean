# The worst required storage is the largest storage deficit

For required releases `R` and inflows `Q` over `n` time steps, every
[storage deficit](required-storage-at.md) `K_t`, `t` any natural number, is at most the
[worst required storage](worst-required-storage.md) `W`, the largest net deficit over a window of
consecutive steps, and `K_t = W` for some `t ≤ n`.
