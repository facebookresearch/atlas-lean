# Some final window attains the storage deficit

For required releases `R` and inflows `Q` over `n` time steps and `t ≤ n`, some `i ≤ t` has
[net deficit](suffix-deficit-sum.md) `d_i + … + d_{t-1}` equal to the
[storage deficit](required-storage-at.md) `K_t`; the window may be empty (`i = t`, net deficit
`0`). With the [bound](suffix-deficit-sum-le-required-storage-a.md), `K_t` is the largest net
deficit over a final window.
