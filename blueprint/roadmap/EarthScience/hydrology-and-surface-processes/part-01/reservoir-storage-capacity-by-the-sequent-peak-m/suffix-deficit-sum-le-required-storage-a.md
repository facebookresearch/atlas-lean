# Every final-window net deficit is at most the storage deficit

For required releases `R` and inflows `Q` over `n` time steps and natural numbers
`0 ≤ i ≤ t ≤ n`, the [net deficit](suffix-deficit-sum.md) `d_i + … + d_{t-1}` of the steps
`i, …, t - 1` is at most the [storage deficit](required-storage-at.md) `K_t`.
