# Rectangular-channel discharge strictly increases with depth

For a prefactor `C > 0` and a width `b > 0`, the rectangular-channel discharge
`h ↦ C * (b * h) * ((b * h) / (b + 2 * h)) ^ (2 / 3)` is strictly increasing on the depths
`h > 0`. So a discharge is reached at most at one positive depth; nothing is claimed about
existence or the range.
