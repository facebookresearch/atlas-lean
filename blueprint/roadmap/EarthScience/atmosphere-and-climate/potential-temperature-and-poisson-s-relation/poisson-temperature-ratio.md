# Poisson's relation for a reversible ideal-gas adiabat

Let `0 < R < cp` and `γ = cp / (cp - R)`, and consider two states with positive pressures
`p₁`, `p₂`, positive specific volumes `v₁`, `v₂` and temperatures `T₁ > 0` and `T₂`. If both
states obey the ideal-gas law, `p₁ v₁ = R T₁` and `p₂ v₂ = R T₂`, and lie on the adiabat
`p₁ v₁ ^ γ = p₂ v₂ ^ γ` (real powers), then `T₂ = T₁ (p₂ / p₁) ^ (R / cp)`.
