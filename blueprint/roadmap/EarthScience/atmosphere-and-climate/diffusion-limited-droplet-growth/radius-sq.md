# Squared droplet radius follows the parabolic growth law

In the diffusion-limited growth model with initial radius `r0 > 0`, growth coefficient `K ≥ 0`
and supersaturation `S`, the radius is `r(t) = √(r0² + 2 K S t)`. At every time `t` with
`r0² + 2 K S t ≥ 0` it satisfies `r(t)² = r0² + 2 K S t`.
