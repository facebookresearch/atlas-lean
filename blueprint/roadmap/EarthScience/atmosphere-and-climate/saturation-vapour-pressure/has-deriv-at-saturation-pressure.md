# Clausius–Clapeyron derivative of saturation vapour pressure

For all real `es0`, `L`, `Rv` and `T0`, the saturation vapour pressure
`es(T) = es0 exp((L / Rv) (1 / T0 - 1 / T))` is differentiable at every temperature `T > 0`
with derivative `es(T) L / (Rv T²)`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, both sides are `0` when
`Rv = 0` (the function is then the constant `es0`), and `1 / T0 = 0` when `T0 = 0`. The
physical domain is `es0, L, Rv, T0 > 0`.
