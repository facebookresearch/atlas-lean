# Sign of the squared Brunt–Väisälä frequency of a dry atmosphere

For `g > 0` and `θ0 > 0`, the squared Brunt–Väisälä frequency `N² = g a / θ0` is positive if
and only if `a > 0` (stable), zero if and only if `a = 0` (neutral), and negative if and only if
`a < 0` (unstable), where `a` is the vertical gradient of potential temperature.
