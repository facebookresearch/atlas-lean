# Hemispheric integration of Lambertian intensity gives σT⁴

For a Stefan–Boltzmann coefficient `σ ≥ 0` and an absolute temperature `T ≥ 0`, integrating
the Lambertian intensity `σ T⁴ / π` over the outgoing hemisphere, with the projection factor
`cos θ` and the azimuthal factor `2π sin θ`, gives the Stefan–Boltzmann flux: the integral over
`0 ≤ θ ≤ π/2` of `(σ T⁴ / π) cos θ · 2π sin θ` is `σ T⁴`.
