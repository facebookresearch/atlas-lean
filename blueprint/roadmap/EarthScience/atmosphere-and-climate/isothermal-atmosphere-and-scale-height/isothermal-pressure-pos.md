# Isothermal pressure is positive for positive surface pressure

If the surface pressure is positive, `p0 > 0`, then for all real `g`, `R` and `T` the
isothermal profile `p(z) = p0 exp(-g z / (R T))` is positive at every height `z`. This includes
`R T = 0`, where the convention `x / 0 = 0` makes the profile the constant `p0`.
