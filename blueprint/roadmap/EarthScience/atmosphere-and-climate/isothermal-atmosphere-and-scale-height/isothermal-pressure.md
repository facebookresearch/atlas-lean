# Pressure profile of an isothermal atmosphere

The pressure profile of an isothermal ideal gas is `p(z) = p0 exp(-g z / (R T))`, with surface
pressure `p0`, constant gravity `g`, mass-specific gas constant `R`, temperature `T` and height
`z` measured upward. It is defined for all real arguments; with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, it
is the constant `p0` when `R T = 0`. The physical regime is `p0, g, R, T > 0`.
