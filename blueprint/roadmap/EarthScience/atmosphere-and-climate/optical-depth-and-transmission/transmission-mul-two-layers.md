# Beer–Lambert transmission multiplies across two adjacent layers

For `a ≤ b ≤ c` and an absorption coefficient `κ` integrable on `[a, b]` and on `[b, c]`, the
Beer–Lambert transmission through the combined layer is the product of the two layer
transmissions: `exp(-τ(a, c)) = exp(-τ(a, b)) exp(-τ(b, c))`, where `τ(a, b) = ∫ₐᵇ κ(x) dx`.
