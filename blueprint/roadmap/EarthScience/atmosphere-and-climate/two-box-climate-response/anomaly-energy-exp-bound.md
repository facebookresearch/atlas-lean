# Exponential decay bound for the two-box anomaly energy

Let `Cs, Cd, λ, γ > 0`. Suppose that at every time `t ≥ 0` the anomalies `xs` and `xd` have
derivatives within `[0, ∞)` given by `Cs xs'(t) = -λ xs(t) - γ (xs(t) - xd(t))` and
`Cd xd'(t) = γ (xs(t) - xd(t))`, so that only the forward trajectory matters and at `t = 0` a
right derivative suffices. Then the energy `E = (Cs xs² + Cd xd²) / 2` satisfies
`E(t) ≤ exp(-c t) E(0)` for every `t ≥ 0`, where `c = min(2 λ / (Cs + 2 Cd), γ / Cd) > 0`.
