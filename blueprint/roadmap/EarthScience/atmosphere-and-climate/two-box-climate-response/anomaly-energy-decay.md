# Differential inequality for the two-box anomaly energy

Let `Cs, Cd, λ, γ > 0`. If at a time `t` the anomalies `xs` and `xd` are differentiable with
`Cs xs'(t) = -λ xs(t) - γ (xs(t) - xd(t))` and `Cd xd'(t) = γ (xs(t) - xd(t))`, then the energy
`E = (Cs xs² + Cd xd²) / 2` satisfies `E'(t) ≤ -c E(t)`, where
`c = min(2 λ / (Cs + 2 Cd), γ / Cd) > 0`.
