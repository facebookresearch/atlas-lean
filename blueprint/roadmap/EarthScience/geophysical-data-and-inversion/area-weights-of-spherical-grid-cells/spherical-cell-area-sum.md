# Cell areas of a latitude partition are nonnegative and sum to 4πR²

Let `-π/2 = φ_0 < φ_1 < … < φ_n = π/2` be a strictly increasing partition of `[-π/2, π/2]` into
latitude bands, let `m ≠ 0` be a number of longitude cells and let `R` be real. Every cell area
`R² (sin φ_{i+1} - sin φ_i) (2π / m)`, for a band `i < n`, is nonnegative, and the areas of the
`n m` cells sum to the sphere area `4π R²`.
