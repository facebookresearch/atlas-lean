# Signed band weights sum to 4πR² for any sequence from -π/2 to π/2

Let `φ_0, …, φ_n` be real numbers with `φ_0 = -π/2` and `φ_n = π/2`, in any order, let `m ≠ 0`
be a number of longitude cells and let `R` be real. The weights
`R² (sin φ_{i+1} - sin φ_i) (2π / m)`, one for each band `i < n` and each of the `m` longitude
cells, sum to `4π R²`. Since no ordering of the `φ_i` is assumed, a weight need not be an area and
can be negative; for strictly increasing `φ` the weights are the
[cell areas of a latitude partition](spherical-cell-area-sum.md).
