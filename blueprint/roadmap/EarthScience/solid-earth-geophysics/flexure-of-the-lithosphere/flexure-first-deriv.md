# First derivative of the periodic flexure profile

For a flexural rigidity `D ≥ 0`, a restoring coefficient `a > 0` (so `D * k ^ 4 + a > 0`) and all
real wavenumbers `k` and load amplitudes `q₀`, the deflection profile
`w(x) = q₀ * cos (k * x) / (D * k ^ 4 + a)` has derivative
`w'(x) = -q₀ * k * sin (k * x) / (D * k ^ 4 + a)` at every `x`.
