# The periodic profile solves the linear flexure equation

For a flexural rigidity `D ≥ 0`, a restoring coefficient `a > 0` and all real `k`, `q₀` and `x`,
the profile `w(x) = q₀ * cos (k * x) / (D * k ^ 4 + a)`, whose fourth derivative is
`q₀ * k ^ 4 * cos (k * x) / (D * k ^ 4 + a)`, satisfies the flexure equation under the periodic
load `q₀ * cos (k * x)`: `D * w''''(x) + a * w(x) = q₀ * cos (k * x)`.
