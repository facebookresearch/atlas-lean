# The dispersion relation makes a plane wave satisfy the wave equation

If `ω ^ 2 = c ^ 2 * k ^ 2` (the dispersion relation), then for all real `x` and `t` the second
time derivative `-ω ^ 2 * cos (k * x - ω * t)` of the plane wave `cos (k * x - ω * t)` equals
`c ^ 2` times its second space derivative `-k ^ 2 * cos (k * x - ω * t)`.
