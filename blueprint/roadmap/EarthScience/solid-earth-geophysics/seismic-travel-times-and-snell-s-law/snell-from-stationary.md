# Snell's law from stationary travel time

Take a source at depth `a > 0` and a receiver at depth `b > 0` on either side of a planar
interface, at horizontal distance `D`, with speeds `v₁ > 0` and `v₂ > 0` in the two layers, and an
interior refraction point `0 < x < D`. If the travel time
`T(x) = √(a² + x²) / v₁ + √(b² + (D - x)²) / v₂` has derivative `0` at `x`, then
`sin θ₁ / v₁ = sin θ₂ / v₂`, where `sin θ₁ = x / √(a² + x²)` and
`sin θ₂ = (D - x) / √(b² + (D - x)²)`.
