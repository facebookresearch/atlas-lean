# Snell's law with equal speeds gives equal sines

For a nonzero common speed `v ≠ 0` and all real `a`, `b`, `D` and `x`: if
`x / (v * √(a² + x²)) = (D - x) / (v * √(b² + (D - x)²))` (Snell's law with `v₁ = v₂ = v`), then
`x / √(a² + x²) = (D - x) / √(b² + (D - x)²)`, that is `sin θ₁ = sin θ₂`. A denominator here
vanishes only together with its numerator, and the quotient is then `0` by the convention
`u / 0 = 0`.
