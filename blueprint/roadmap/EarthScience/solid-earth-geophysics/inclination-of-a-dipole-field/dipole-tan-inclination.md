# The tangent of the dipole inclination is twice that of latitude

For the dipole components `B_r = 2 * C * cos θ / r ^ 3` and `B_θ = C * sin θ / r ^ 3`, with a
moment factor `C < 0` (moment toward `θ = π`; Earth's present orientation when `θ` is the
colatitude from the geomagnetic north pole), a distance `r > 0` and a colatitude `0 < θ < π`: with
the downward vertical component `Z = -B_r` and the horizontal intensity `H = |B_θ|`,
`tan I = Z / H = 2 * tan λ`, where `λ = π / 2 - θ` is the magnetic latitude.
