# Fourier's law with conductivity 3 and flux 0.06 gives gradient 0.02

A synthetic conductive geotherm: under Fourier's law `flux = -k * grad` with conductivity
`k = 3` (in W/(m K)), a heat flux of magnitude `|flux| = 0.06` (in W/m²) forces the temperature
gradient magnitude `|grad| = 0.02` (in K/m): for every real `grad`, `|-3 * grad| = 6 / 100`
implies `|grad| = 2 / 100`.
