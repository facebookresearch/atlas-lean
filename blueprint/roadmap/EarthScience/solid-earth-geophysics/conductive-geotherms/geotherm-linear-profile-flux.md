# Derivative and flux of a synthetic linear geotherm

For every real `T₀`, the linear profile `T(z) = T₀ - (2 / 100) * z`, the steady profile of a slab
of thickness `100` with end temperatures `T₀` and `T₀ - 2`, has derivative `-(2 / 100)` at every
`z`; with conductivity `k = 3` its conductive flux is `-k * T' = 6 / 100`.
