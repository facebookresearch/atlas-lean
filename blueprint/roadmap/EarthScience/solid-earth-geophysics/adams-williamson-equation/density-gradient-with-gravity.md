# The Adams–Williamson gradient with gravity from the enclosed mass

Let `G`, `m`, `r > 0`, `ρ`, `φ ≠ 0`, `dp/dr` and `dρ/dr` be real numbers, the last two standing
for the radial derivatives of pressure and density. If `dp/dr = -ρ * (G * m / r ^ 2)`
(hydrostatic equilibrium with gravity `g = G * m / r ^ 2` at radius `r`) and
`dp/dr = φ * dρ/dr` (`φ` the seismic parameter), then `dρ/dr = -ρ * (G * m / r ^ 2) / φ`. The
centre `r = 0` is excluded.
