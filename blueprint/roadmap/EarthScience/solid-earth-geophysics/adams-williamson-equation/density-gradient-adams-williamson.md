# The Adams–Williamson density gradient

Let `ρ`, `g`, `φ ≠ 0`, `dp/dr` and `dρ/dr` be real numbers, the last two standing for the radial
derivatives of pressure and density. If `dp/dr = -ρ * g` (hydrostatic equilibrium) and
`dp/dr = φ * dρ/dr` (adiabatic compression of a homogeneous layer, `φ` the seismic parameter),
then `dρ/dr = -ρ * g / φ`.
