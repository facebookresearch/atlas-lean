# Factorisation of the Rayleigh number

For a kinematic viscosity `ν > 0`, a thermal diffusivity `κ > 0` and all real `g`, `α`, `ΔT` and
`H`, the product of `g * α * ΔT * H / ν` and `H ^ 2 / κ` is the Rayleigh number
`g * α * ΔT * H ^ 3 / (ν * κ)`. This is an identity between the two expressions; it does not
derive the Rayleigh number from the Boussinesq equations.
