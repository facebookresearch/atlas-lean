# Boussinesq nondimensionalization and the Rayleigh number

Take the Boussinesq equations $u_t + (u \cdot \nabla)u = -\nabla p/\rho_0 + \nu\nabla^2 u + g\alpha(T - T_0)e_z$, $\nabla \cdot u = 0$ and
$T_t + u \cdot \nabla T = \kappa\nabla^2 T$, with constant $\rho_0, \nu, \kappa, \alpha, g, \Delta T, H > 0$. Scaling lengths by $H$, time by
the diffusion time $H^2/\kappa$, velocity by $\kappa/H$, temperature differences by $\Delta T$ and pressure by $\rho_0\kappa\nu/H^2$ gives

$$\frac{1}{\mathrm{Pr}}\bigl(u_t + (u \cdot \nabla)u\bigr) = -\nabla p + \nabla^2 u + \mathrm{Ra}\,\theta\,e_z, \qquad \theta_t + u \cdot \nabla\theta = \nabla^2\theta,$$

with Rayleigh number $\mathrm{Ra} = g\alpha\Delta T H^3/(\nu\kappa)$ and Prandtl number $\mathrm{Pr} = \nu/\kappa$, both dimensionless. The
onset of convection depends on the boundary conditions; there is no universal critical Rayleigh number.
