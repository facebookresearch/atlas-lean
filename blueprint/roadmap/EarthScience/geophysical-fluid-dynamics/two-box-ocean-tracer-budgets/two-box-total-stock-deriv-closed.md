# Closed two-box exchange conserves the total tracer stock

For positive volumes `V1` and `V2`, equal or not, and every real exchange flux `Q`, with no
external fluxes: if at a time `t` the tracer concentrations are differentiable with
`V1 S1'(t) = Q (S2(t) - S1(t))` and `V2 S2'(t) = Q (S1(t) - S2(t))`, then the total tracer stock
`V1 S1 + V2 S2` has derivative `0` at `t`.
