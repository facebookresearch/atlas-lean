# Energy-density rate is a divergence under the QG equation

For a deformation radius `Ld ≠ 0`, real values of `ψ` and of its partial derivatives `ψ_x`,
`ψ_y`, `ψ_t`, `ψ_xt`, `ψ_yt`, `ψ_xxt`, `ψ_yyt`, and real values `q_t`, `q_x`, `q_y`, `q_xy` with
`q_xy` the mixed partial of `q`, suppose that `q_t = (ψ_xxt + ψ_yyt) - ψ_t / Ld²` and that the
quasigeostrophic equation `q_t = -J(ψ, q) = -(ψ_x q_y - ψ_y q_x)` holds. Then the time
derivative `e_t = ψ_x ψ_xt + ψ_y ψ_yt + ψ ψ_t / Ld²` of the energy density is the divergence of
`G = (ψ ψ_xt + ψ² q_y / 2, ψ ψ_yt - ψ² q_x / 2)`, written out: `e_t = ∂ₓG_x + ∂ᵧG_y` with
`∂ₓG_x = ψ_x ψ_xt + ψ ψ_xxt + ψ ψ_x q_y + ψ² q_xy / 2` and
`∂ᵧG_y = ψ_y ψ_yt + ψ ψ_yyt - ψ ψ_y q_x - ψ² q_xy / 2`. On the walls `ψ = 0`, so `G_y = 0`
there; with `x` periodic, the channel integral of `div G` vanishes by the divergence theorem,
which is energy conservation.
