# The product of ψ and the Jacobian is a divergence

For real values `ψ`, `ψ_x`, `ψ_y`, `q_x`, `q_y` and `q_xy`, the divergence of the flux
`(ψ² q_y / 2, -ψ² q_x / 2)`, written out with `q_xy` the mixed partial of `q` as
`(ψ ψ_x q_y + ψ² q_xy / 2) + (-(ψ ψ_y q_x) - ψ² q_xy / 2)`, equals
`ψ J(ψ, q) = ψ (ψ_x q_y - ψ_y q_x)`.
