# Energy and enstrophy conservation in a quasigeostrophic channel

On a channel $[0, L_x] \times [0, L_y]$ with $L_x, L_y, L_d > 0$, let the streamfunction $\psi$ have a jointly $C^3$ extension to a
neighbourhood of $I \times [0, L_x] \times [0, L_y]$ for an open time interval $I$, be $L_x$-periodic in $x$ with all derivatives, and
vanish on the walls $y = 0$ and $y = L_y$ at every time. Let $q = \nabla^2\psi - \psi/L_d^2 + \beta y$ satisfy $q_t + J(\psi, q) = 0$, with
velocity $(-\psi_y, \psi_x)$. Then the energy $\frac12\int(\lvert\nabla\psi\rvert^2 + \psi^2/L_d^2)$ and the enstrophy $\frac12\int q^2$ are
constant on $I$; the wall conditions give $\psi_t = \psi_x = 0$ there and rule out net transport along the channel. These are conditional
identities for a classical solution, not an existence result, and the model is the nondimensional beta-plane, not the rotating
Navier–Stokes system.
