# Buoyancy acceleration of a displaced Boussinesq parcel

In the background density profile `ρ(z) = ρ0 - β z` (`z` upward), a Boussinesq parcel displaced
by `ζ` keeps its density `ρ0`, while the ambient density at `ζ` is `ρ0 - β ζ`. For `ρ0 ≠ 0` and
all real `g`, `β` and `ζ`, its buoyancy acceleration `-g (ρ0 - (ρ0 - β ζ)) / ρ0` equals
`-(g β / ρ0) ζ`.
