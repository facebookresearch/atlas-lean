# Buoyancy frequency of a linear profile and its stability signs

For `g > 0`, `ρ0 > 0`, every real `β` and every height `z`, the squared Brunt–Väisälä frequency
`N² = -(g / ρ0) ρ_z` of the linear Boussinesq profile `ρ(z) = ρ0 - β z`, where `ρ_z` is the
profile's vertical gradient at `z`, equals `g β / ρ0`; and the stratification is stable
(`N² > 0`), neutral (`N² = 0`) or unstable (`N² < 0`) exactly when `β > 0`, `β = 0` or
`β < 0`.
