# Sverdrup balance gives the meridional transport

For density `ρ₀ > 0` and `β ≠ 0`, the interior Sverdrup balance `β V = curl / ρ₀`, after
Sverdrup (1947), gives `V = curl / (ρ₀ β)`. Here `x` points east and `y` north, `β = ∂f/∂y`,
`curl = ∂τʸ/∂x - ∂τˣ/∂y` is the vertical curl of the surface wind stress and `V` the northward
volume transport per unit width.
