# Shallow-water potential vorticity is conserved along a trajectory

Along a parcel trajectory, with constant Coriolis parameter `f`, relative vorticity `ζ`, depth
`h` and divergence `D` as functions of time, suppose that at every time `t` the depth is
nonzero, `h(t) ≠ 0`, and the continuity equation `h'(t) = -h(t) D(t)` and the vorticity
equation `(ζ + f)'(t) = -(ζ(t) + f) D(t)` hold, the functions being differentiable. Then the
potential vorticity `q = (ζ + f) / h` takes the same value at all times:
`(ζ(s) + f) / h(s) = (ζ(t) + f) / h(t)` for all `s` and `t`.
