# Primitive equations as a quasilinear system

For fields `h` and `u`, a point `(t, x)`, any real `g` and any real numbers `hₜ`, `hₓ`, `uₜ`,
`uₓ` (in use, the partial derivatives of `h` and `u` at that point), the two primitive
equations `hₜ + u hₓ + h uₓ = 0` and `uₜ + u uₓ + g hₓ = 0`, with `h` and `u` evaluated at
`(t, x)`, hold if and only if `(hₜ, uₜ) + A (hₓ, uₓ) = 0`, where `A` is the primitive
coefficient matrix with rows `(u, h)` and `(g, u)` at `(h(t, x), u(t, x))`.
