# Conservative and primitive shallow-water forms agree at positive depth

Let `h` be the depth and `u` the velocity of one-dimensional flat-bed frictionless shallow
water, with time and space slices differentiable at `(t, x)` with partial derivatives `hₜ`,
`hₓ`, `uₜ`, `uₓ`, let the depth be positive there, `h(t, x) > 0`, and let `g` be any real
number. Then mass and momentum conservation, `∂ₜh + ∂ₓ(h u) = 0` and
`∂ₜ(h u) + ∂ₓ(h u² + g h² / 2) = 0`, hold at `(t, x)` if and only if `hₜ + u hₓ + h uₓ = 0`
and `uₜ + u uₓ + g hₓ = 0` hold there. This is a classical pointwise statement; weak solutions
are not considered.
