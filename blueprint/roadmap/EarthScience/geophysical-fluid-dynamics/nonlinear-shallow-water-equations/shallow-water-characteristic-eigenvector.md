# Characteristic directions of the shallow-water equations

If `g h ≥ 0`, the vectors `(h, √(g h))` and `(h, -√(g h))` satisfy the right-eigenvector
relations of the primitive coefficient matrix `A`, with rows `(u, h)` and `(g, u)`, for the
speeds `u + √(g h)` and `u - √(g h)`: `A (h, √(g h)) = (u + √(g h)) (h, √(g h))` and
`A (h, -√(g h)) = (u - √(g h)) (h, -√(g h))`. The two vectors are nonzero exactly when
`h ≠ 0`; at `h = 0` both are the zero vector and the relations are trivial.
