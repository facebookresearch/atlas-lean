# Reconstruction at a wet lake at rest

Let the cells of a face be a lake at rest with free surface `η`: `hL + zL = η`, `hR + zR = η`,
`max(zL, zR) < η` (both cells strictly wet) and zero discharges `hL uL = 0`, `hR uR = 0`. Then both
[reconstructed face states](face-state.md), with the face bed `max(zL, zR)`, equal the wet rest
state `(η - max(zL, zR), 0)`: the reconstructed depths agree and the velocities vanish. This is the
first step of the source's proof of Theorem 2.1 (ii).
