# Riemann invariants of shallow water are transported at positive depth

Let `h` be the depth and `u` the velocity of one-dimensional flat-bed shallow water, with time
and space slices differentiable at `(t, x)` with partial derivatives `hₜ`, `hₓ`, `uₜ`, `uₓ`; let
gravity and the depth there be positive, `g > 0` and `h(t, x) > 0`; and let the primitive
equations `hₜ + u hₓ + h uₓ = 0` and `uₜ + u uₓ + g hₓ = 0` hold at `(t, x)`. Then, with
`c = √(g h)`, both paired transport equations `(∂ₜ + (u + c) ∂ₓ)(u + 2 c) = 0` and
`(∂ₜ + (u - c) ∂ₓ)(u - 2 c) = 0` hold at `(t, x)`.
