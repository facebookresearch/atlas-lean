# Coefficient matrix of the primitive shallow-water equations

For gravity `g`, depth `h` and velocity `u` (all real), the primitive coefficient matrix of
one-dimensional flat-bed shallow water for the state `(h, u)`, depth first, is the `2 × 2`
matrix `A(h, u)` with rows `(u, h)` and `(g, u)`; the primitive form is
`∂ₜ(h, u) + A ∂ₓ(h, u) = 0`.
