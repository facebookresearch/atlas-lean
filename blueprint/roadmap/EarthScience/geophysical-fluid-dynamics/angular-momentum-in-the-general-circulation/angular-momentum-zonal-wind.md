# Zonal wind of a parcel conserving angular momentum from the equator

An air parcel starting at rest at the equator has absolute angular momentum per unit mass
`Ω a²`. Let the planetary radius be `a > 0`, let `Ω` and `u` be real, and let the latitude
satisfy `|φ| < π/2`. If at latitude `φ` the eastward wind `u` conserves that angular momentum,
`(Ω a cos φ + u) (a cos φ) = Ω a²`, then `u = Ω a sin² φ / cos φ`.
