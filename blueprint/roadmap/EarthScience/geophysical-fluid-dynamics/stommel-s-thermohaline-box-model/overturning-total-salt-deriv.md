# Total salt of the closed overturning loop changes only by forcing

Two well-mixed boxes of positive volumes `V1` and `V2` exchange water through an overturning
loop of transport `q` (Stommel 1961): the volume flux `|q|` goes from one box to the other
along one branch and back along the other, so with upwind salinities each box receives `|q|`
times the other box's salinity and loses `|q|` times its own, whatever the sign of `q`. If at
a time `t` the salinities are differentiable with `V1 S1'(t) = |q| S2(t) - |q| S1(t) + F1` and
`V2 S2'(t) = |q| S1(t) - |q| S2(t) + F2`, then the total salt `V1 S1 + V2 S2` has derivative
`F1 + F2` at `t`: it changes only through the external forcing.
