# Sign of the Stommel overturning transport

For `k > 0` and all real `α`, `β`, `ΔT` and `ΔS`, the Stommel transport `q = k (α ΔT - β ΔS)`
is positive, zero or negative exactly when `α ΔT - β ΔS` is. With `ΔT = T1 - T2`,
`ΔS = S1 - S2` and a linear equation of state `ρ = ρ₀ (1 - α T + β S)`,
`α ΔT - β ΔS = (ρ2 - ρ1) / ρ₀`, so for `ρ₀ > 0` the transport has the sign of the density
difference `ρ2 - ρ1`.
