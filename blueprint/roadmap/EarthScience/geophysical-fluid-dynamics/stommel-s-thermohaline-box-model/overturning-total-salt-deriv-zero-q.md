# Without exchange total salt changes only by forcing

At the flow-reversal boundary `q = 0` there is no advective exchange, in the closed loop and on
a single edge alike. For positive volumes `V1` and `V2`, if at a time `t` the salinities are
differentiable with `V1 S1'(t) = F1` and `V2 S2'(t) = F2`, then the total salt `V1 S1 + V2 S2`
has derivative `F1 + F2` at `t`.
