# Zonal thermal wind from zonal geostrophic and hydrostatic balance

On a vertical line, let `ug(z)` be the zonal geostrophic wind and `py(z)` the northward
pressure gradient at height `z`, with `ρ0 ≠ 0` and `f`, `g`, `ρ_y` real. Suppose the zonal
geostrophic balance `f ug(z) = -py(z) / ρ0` holds at every height, `ug` and `py` are
differentiable at a height `t` with derivatives `A` and `Dpy`, and the hydrostatic relation
differentiated in `y` gives `Dpy = -g ρ_y`. Then `f A = (g / ρ0) ρ_y`, that is
`f ∂ug/∂z = (g / ρ0) ρ_y` at `t`. No meridional data is needed.
