# For positive f a northward density increase gives positive shear

On a vertical line, let `ug(z)` be the zonal geostrophic wind and `py(z)` the northward pressure
gradient at height `z`, with `f > 0`, `ρ0 > 0`, `g > 0` and a positive northward density
gradient `ρ_y > 0`. If the zonal geostrophic balance `f ug(z) = -py(z) / ρ0` holds at every
height, `ug` and `py` are differentiable at a height `t` with derivatives `A` and `Dpy`, and
`Dpy = -g ρ_y`, then the vertical shear of the zonal wind is positive there, `A > 0`.
