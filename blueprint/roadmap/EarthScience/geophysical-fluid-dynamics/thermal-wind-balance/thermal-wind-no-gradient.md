# No horizontal density gradient gives no thermal-wind shear

On a vertical line, let `ug(z)`, `vg(z)` be the geostrophic wind and `py(z)`, `px(z)` the
horizontal pressure gradient at height `z`, with `f ≠ 0`, `ρ0 ≠ 0` and `g` real. Suppose the
geostrophic balances `f ug(z) = -py(z) / ρ0` and `f vg(z) = px(z) / ρ0` hold at every height,
`ug`, `vg`, `py`, `px` are differentiable at a height `t` with derivatives `A`, `B`, `Dpy`,
`Dpx`, and there is no horizontal density gradient: `Dpy = -g · 0` and `Dpx = -g · 0`. Then
the geostrophic wind has no vertical shear at `t`: `A = 0` and `B = 0`.
