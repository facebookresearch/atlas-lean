# Thermal-wind relations on a vertical line

On a vertical line, let `ug(z)`, `vg(z)` be the geostrophic wind and `py(z)`, `px(z)` the
horizontal pressure gradient at height `z`, with `f ≠ 0`, `ρ0 ≠ 0` and `g`, `ρ_x`, `ρ_y` real.
Suppose the f-plane geostrophic balances `f ug(z) = -py(z) / ρ0` and `f vg(z) = px(z) / ρ0`
hold at every height; `ug`, `vg`, `py`, `px` are differentiable at a height `t` with
derivatives `A`, `B`, `Dpy`, `Dpx`; and the hydrostatic relations differentiated in the
horizontal, with mixed derivatives commuted, give `Dpy = -g ρ_y` and `Dpx = -g ρ_x`. Then
`f A = (g / ρ0) ρ_y` and `f B = -(g / ρ0) ρ_x`, the thermal-wind relations at `t`.
