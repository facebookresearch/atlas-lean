# The Ekman spiral solves the Ekman balance when 2νa² = f

For all real `a`, `ν` and `f` with `2 ν a² = f`, the Ekman spiral components
`u(ζ) = exp(-a ζ) cos(a ζ)` and `v(ζ) = -exp(-a ζ) sin(a ζ)` satisfy, at every depth `ζ`, the
Ekman equations `ν u'' = -f v` and `ν v'' = f u`. These are the component equations of the
Ekman balance with `z` upward (and `f > 0` in the northern hemisphere), written in the downward
depth `ζ = -z`, which leaves second derivatives unchanged.
