# Ekman spiral component along the surface current

In the depth coordinate `ζ`, measured downward from the surface (`ζ ≥ 0` in the ocean), the
component of the Ekman spiral along the surface current is `u(ζ) = exp(-a ζ) cos(a ζ)`, where
`a` is the inverse Ekman depth. It is defined for all real `a` and `ζ`.
