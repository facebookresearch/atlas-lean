# Surface values and surface shear of the Ekman spiral

For every real `a`, the Ekman spiral components and their depth derivatives at the surface are
`u(0) = 1`, `v(0) = 0`, `u'(0) = -a` and `v'(0) = -a`, where `u(ζ) = exp(-a ζ) cos(a ζ)`,
`v(ζ) = -exp(-a ζ) sin(a ζ)`, `u'(ζ) = -a exp(-a ζ) (cos(a ζ) + sin(a ζ))` and
`v'(ζ) = a exp(-a ζ) (sin(a ζ) - cos(a ζ))`. For `a > 0` and eddy viscosity `ν > 0`, the
surface stress points along `-(u'(0), v'(0)) = (a, a)`, at 45° to the left of the surface
current `(1, 0)`.
