# Eastward southern-hemisphere wind under a northward pressure increase

For `f < 0` (southern hemisphere) and `ρ0 > 0`, a velocity `(ux, uy)` in geostrophic balance
`f ux = -py / ρ0`, `-f uy = -px / ρ0` with a positive northward pressure gradient `py > 0` has
a positive eastward component, `ux > 0`: the sign flips with `f`.
