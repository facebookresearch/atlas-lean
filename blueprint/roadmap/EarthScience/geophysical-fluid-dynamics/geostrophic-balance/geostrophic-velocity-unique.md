# Off the equator, geostrophic balance admits at most one velocity

For `f ≠ 0` and `ρ0 ≠ 0`, two velocities `(ux1, uy1)` and `(ux2, uy2)` that both satisfy the
geostrophic balance `f ux = -py / ρ0`, `-f uy = -px / ρ0` for the same pressure gradient
`(px, py)` are equal: `ux1 = ux2` and `uy1 = uy2`.
