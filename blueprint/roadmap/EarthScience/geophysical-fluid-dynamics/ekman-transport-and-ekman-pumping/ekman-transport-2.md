# Off the equator, Ekman transport balances a horizontal wind stress

For density `ρ₀ > 0`, Coriolis parameter `f ≠ 0` and a wind stress `τ = (τ₀, τ₁, τ₂)` with no
vertical component, `τ₂ = 0`, the Ekman transport `M = (τ₁ / (ρ₀ f), -τ₀ / (ρ₀ f), 0)`
satisfies `ρ₀ f k̂ × M = τ`, where `k̂ = (0, 0, 1)` and the vertical coordinate `z` points
upward.
