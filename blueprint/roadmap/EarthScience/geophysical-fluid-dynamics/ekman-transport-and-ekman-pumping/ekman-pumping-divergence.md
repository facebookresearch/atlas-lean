# Divergence of Ekman transport is the stress curl over ρ₀ f

Let `τ(x, y) = (τ₀, τ₁, τ₂)` be a wind-stress field on the horizontal plane, `ρ₀` a density and
`f` a constant Coriolis parameter with `ρ₀ f ≠ 0`, and let
`M(x, y) = (τ₁ / (ρ₀ f), -τ₀ / (ρ₀ f), 0)` be the Ekman transport. At a point `(x, y)` where
`τ₁` is differentiable in `x` and `τ₀` is differentiable in `y`, the horizontal divergence of
the transport equals the vertical curl of the stress divided by `ρ₀ f`:
`∂ₓM₀ + ∂ᵧM₁ = (∂ₓτ₁ - ∂ᵧτ₀) / (ρ₀ f)`. By mass continuity this divergence is read as the
vertical velocity at the base of the Ekman layer, the Ekman pumping velocity.
