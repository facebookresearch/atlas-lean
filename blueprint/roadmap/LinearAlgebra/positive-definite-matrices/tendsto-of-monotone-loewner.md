# Monotone bounded sequences of symmetric matrices converge

Let $m$ be a finite index set and $(A_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ a sequence of real symmetric $m \times m$ matrices that is nondecreasing in the Löwner order ($A_j - A_i$ is positive semidefinite whenever $i \le j$) and bounded above by a real matrix $B$ ($B - A_k$ positive semidefinite for all $k$). Then there is a real symmetric matrix $L$ with $L - A_k$ positive semidefinite for every $k$ such that $A_k \to L$ entrywise as $k \to \infty$.
