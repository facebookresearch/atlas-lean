# Golden–Thompson inequality for commuting matrices

If $A$ and $B$ are commuting complex square matrices, then $\operatorname{tr} e^{A+B} \le \operatorname{tr}(e^A e^B)$, in fact with equality. This is the restriction to commuting matrices of the Golden–Thompson inequality (Forrester and Thompson, arXiv:1408.2008v1, equation (1.1)).

The content of the general [Golden–Thompson inequality](golden-thompson.md) lies in the noncommuting case.
