# The right side of Golden–Thompson is a nonnegative real

For Hermitian complex square matrices $A$ and $B$, $\operatorname{tr}(e^A e^B)$ is a nonnegative real
number. Indeed $\operatorname{tr}(e^A e^B) = \operatorname{tr}(X X^*)$ with $X = e^{A/2} e^{B/2}$. So
the Golden–Thompson inequality $\operatorname{tr} e^{A+B} \le \operatorname{tr}(e^A e^B)$
(Forrester and Thompson, arXiv:1408.2008v1, equation (1.1)) compares two real numbers even though
$e^A e^B$ need not be Hermitian.

## Background

- [Golden–Thompson inequality](golden-thompson.md): the inequality whose right side this result shows to be real.
