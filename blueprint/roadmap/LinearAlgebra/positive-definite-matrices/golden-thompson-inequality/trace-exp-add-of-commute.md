# Golden–Thompson equality for commuting matrices

If $A$ and $B$ are commuting complex square matrices, then $\operatorname{tr} e^{A+B} = \operatorname{tr}(e^A e^B)$, because $e^{A+B} = e^A e^B$ for commuting matrices. This
is the equality case of the Golden–Thompson inequality (Forrester and Thompson,
arXiv:1408.2008v1, equation (1.1)); no Hermitian hypothesis is needed.

## Background

- [Golden–Thompson inequality](golden-thompson.md): the general inequality, of which this is the equality case.
