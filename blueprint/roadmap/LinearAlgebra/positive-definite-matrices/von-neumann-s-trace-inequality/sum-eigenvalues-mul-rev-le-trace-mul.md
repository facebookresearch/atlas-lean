# Von Neumann's trace inequality, sorted lower form

Let $A$ and $B$ be real symmetric $d \times d$ matrices (indexed by a finite set) with eigenvalues $\alpha^\downarrow_0 \ge \dots \ge \alpha^\downarrow_{d-1}$ and $\beta^\downarrow_0 \ge \dots \ge \beta^\downarrow_{d-1}$ in decreasing order. Then

$$\sum_{k=0}^{d-1} \alpha^\downarrow_k \beta^\downarrow_{d-1-k} \le \operatorname{tr}(AB):$$

pairing the largest eigenvalues of $A$ with the smallest of $B$ minimizes the trace. It follows from the pairing form and the rearrangement inequality.
