# Monotonicity of the determinant for real positive semidefinite matrices: $\det S \le \det D$ when $S \succeq 0$ and $D - S \succeq 0$

Let $S$ and $D$ be real square matrices of the same size such that $S$ and $D - S$ are positive
semidefinite. Then $\det S \le \det D$: the determinant is monotone on the cone of positive
semidefinite matrices.
