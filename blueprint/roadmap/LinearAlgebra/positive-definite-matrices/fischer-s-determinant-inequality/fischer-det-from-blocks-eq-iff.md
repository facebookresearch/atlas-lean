# Equality case of Fischer's inequality

Let $A$ be a real positive definite $m \times m$ matrix, $D$ a real positive definite $n \times n$
matrix and $B$ a real $m \times n$ matrix such that the block matrix

$$M = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\mathsf{T}} & D \end{pmatrix}$$

is positive semidefinite. Then $\det M = \det A \cdot \det D$ if and only if $B = 0$.
