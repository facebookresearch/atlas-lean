# The trace of a product of positive semidefinite matrices has nonnegative real part

Let $A$ and $B$ be positive semidefinite complex $n \times n$ matrices. Then
$\operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr}(AB) \ge 0$.

## Idea

Write $B = B^{1/2} B^{1/2}$ with $B^{1/2}$ positive semidefinite. By cyclicity of the trace,
$\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(B^{1/2} A B^{1/2})$, the trace of a positive semidefinite
matrix, so $\operatorname{tr}(AB)$ is a nonnegative real number. For the trace inner product this says
that the positive semidefinite cone lies in its own dual cone.
