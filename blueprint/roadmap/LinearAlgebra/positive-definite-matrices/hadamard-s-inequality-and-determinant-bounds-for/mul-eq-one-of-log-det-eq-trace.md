# Mul eq one of log det eq trace

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n$ elements, and let $A, B \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ be positive definite. If

$$\log(\det A \cdot \det B) = \operatorname{tr}(A B) - n,$$

then $B A = I$. This is the equality case of the inequality $\log(\det A \det B) \le \operatorname{tr}(A B) - n$.
