# A channel with zero gains has determinant ratio one

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ a real matrix and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. Write $G = H^{\top} R^{-1} H$ for the channel Gram matrix, $\lambda_i$ ($i \in n$) for its eigenvalues and $V$ for the orthogonal matrix of eigenvectors chosen by the spectral theorem, so that $G = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^{\top}$. If every channel eigenvalue $\lambda_i$ is $0$, then $\det(R + H K H^{\top}) / \det R = 1$ for every positive semidefinite $K \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$.
