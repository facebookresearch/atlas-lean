# Orthogonal eigenbasis of the channel Gram matrix

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. The input basis is the real $n \times n$ matrix $V$ whose columns are orthonormal eigenvectors of $H^{\top} R^{-1} H$, the $i$-th column for the $i$-th channel eigenvalue, as chosen by the spectral theorem.
