# Determinant ratio for a covariance diagonal in the channel eigenbasis

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. Let $V \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with $V^{\top} V = I$ and $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^n$ satisfy $H^{\top} R^{-1} H = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^{\top}$. Then for every $p \in \mathbb{R}^n$ (of any sign),

$$\frac{\det(R + H V \operatorname{diag}(p) V^{\top} H^{\top})}{\det R} = \prod_{i \in n} (1 + \lambda_i p_i).$$
