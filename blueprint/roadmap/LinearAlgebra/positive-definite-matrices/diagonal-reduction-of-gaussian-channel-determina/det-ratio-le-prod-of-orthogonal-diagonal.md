# Hadamard bound for the Gaussian channel determinant ratio

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite and $K \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ positive semidefinite. Let $V \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with $V^{\top} V = I$ and $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be any data with $H^{\top} R^{-1} H = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^{\top}$. Then

$$\frac{\det(R + H K H^{\top})}{\det R} \le \prod_{i \in n} \big(1 + \lambda_i (V^{\top} K V)_{ii}\big).$$
