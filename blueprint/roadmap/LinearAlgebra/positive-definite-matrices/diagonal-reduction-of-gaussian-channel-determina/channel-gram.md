# Whitened channel Gram matrix

For finite index sets $m$, $n$ and real matrices $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, the whitened channel Gram matrix is $H^{\top} R^{-1} H \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, where $R^{-1}$ is the inverse of $R$ when $R$ is invertible and the zero matrix by convention otherwise.
