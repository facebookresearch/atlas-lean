# Channel eigenvalues

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. The channel eigenvalues are the eigenvalues $(\lambda_i)_{i \in n}$ of the symmetric matrix $H^{\top} R^{-1} H$, listed with multiplicity and indexed by $n$ as chosen by the spectral theorem for symmetric matrices.
