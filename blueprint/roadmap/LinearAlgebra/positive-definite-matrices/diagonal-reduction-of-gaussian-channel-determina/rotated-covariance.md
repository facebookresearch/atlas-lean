# Rotated input covariance

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ a real matrix and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. Write $G = H^{\top} R^{-1} H$ for the channel Gram matrix, $\lambda_i$ ($i \in n$) for its eigenvalues and $V$ for the orthogonal matrix of eigenvectors chosen by the spectral theorem, so that $G = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^{\top}$. For $K \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, the rotated input covariance is $V^{\top} K V$.
