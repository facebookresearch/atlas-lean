# Diagonalized input covariance

Let $m$, $n$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ a real matrix and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ positive definite. Write $G = H^{\top} R^{-1} H$ for the channel Gram matrix, $\lambda_i$ ($i \in n$) for its eigenvalues and $V$ for the orthogonal matrix of eigenvectors chosen by the spectral theorem, so that $G = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^{\top}$. For $K \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with power allocation $p_i = (V^{\top} K V)_{ii}$, the diagonalized covariance is $V \operatorname{diag}(p) V^{\top}$: the rotated covariance with its off-diagonal entries removed, rotated back.
