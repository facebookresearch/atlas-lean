# Equality case of the matched-filter bound

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota\times\iota}$ positive definite, and $s, w \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $s \neq 0$ and $w \neq 0$. The matched-filter quotient

$$\mathrm{SNR}(w) = \frac{(w^{\mathsf T}s)^2}{w^{\mathsf T}Rw}$$

equals $s^{\mathsf T}R^{-1}s$ if and only if $w = c\,R^{-1}s$ for some real $c \neq 0$.
