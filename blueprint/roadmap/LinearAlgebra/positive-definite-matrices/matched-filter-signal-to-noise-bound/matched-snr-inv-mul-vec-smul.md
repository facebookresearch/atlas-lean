# The matched filter attains the bound

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive definite, $s \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ nonzero and $c \in \mathbb{R}$ nonzero (of either sign). Then the weight $w = c R^{-1} s$ attains the matched-filter bound: $(w^{\top} s)^2 / (w^{\top} R w) = s^{\top} R^{-1} s$.
