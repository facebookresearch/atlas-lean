# The upper-left block of a positive definite block matrix is positive definite

Let $m$, $p$ be finite index sets and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ real matrices, and suppose the block matrix $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite (symmetric with $x^{\top} G x > 0$ for all $x \ne 0$). Then $A$ is positive definite.
