# The lower-right block of a positive definite block matrix is invertible

Let $m$, $p$ be finite index sets and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ real matrices, and suppose the block matrix $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite (symmetric with $x^{\top} G x > 0$ for all $x \ne 0$). Then $D$ is an invertible matrix, equivalently $\det D \ne 0$.
