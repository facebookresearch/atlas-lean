# The Schur complement of a positive definite block matrix is positive semidefinite

Let $m$, $p$ be finite index sets and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ real matrices, and suppose the block matrix $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite (symmetric with $x^{\top} G x > 0$ for all $x \ne 0$). Then the Schur complement $A - B D^{-1} B^{\top}$ is positive semidefinite.
