# Schur complement of the lower-right block

For finite index sets $m$, $p$ and real matrices $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$, the Schur complement of $D$ is

$$K = A - B D^{-1} B^{\top} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m},$$

where $D^{-1}$ is the inverse of $D$ when $D$ is invertible and the zero matrix by convention otherwise (in which case $K = A$).
