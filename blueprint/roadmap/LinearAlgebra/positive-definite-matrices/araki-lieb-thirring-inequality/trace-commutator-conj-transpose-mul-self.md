# Commutator identity for traces

For Hermitian complex square matrices $X$ and $Y$, with $C = XY - YX$,
$\operatorname{tr}(C^* C) = 2\bigl(\operatorname{tr}(XXYY) - \operatorname{tr}(XYXY)\bigr)$.

The proof expands $C^* C = (YX - XY)(XY - YX)$ and uses cyclicity of the trace: $\operatorname{tr}(YXXY) = \operatorname{tr}(XYYX) = \operatorname{tr}(XXYY)$ and $\operatorname{tr}(YXYX) = \operatorname{tr}(XYXY)$.
