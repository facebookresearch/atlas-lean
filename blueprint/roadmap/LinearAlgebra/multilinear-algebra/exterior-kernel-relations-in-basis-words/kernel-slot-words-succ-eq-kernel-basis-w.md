# Kernel slots can be moved to a basis-tail generator

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M` an `R`-module with basis `b : Basis I R M`, and `n : ℕ`.
For an `R`-module `N` and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`, `kernelSlotWords f (n+1) = kernelBasisWords f b n`.
Both are relation spans, generated by a kernel vector and the remaining exterior factors.
