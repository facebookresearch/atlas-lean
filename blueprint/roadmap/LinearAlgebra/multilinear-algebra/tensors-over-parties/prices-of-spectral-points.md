# Prices: limits of scaled wire exponents

Let $V$ be a finite set of parties. A *price* on $V$ is a function $w:V\times V\to\mathbb{R}_{\ge0}$ that is a limit of functions $c\cdot\mathrm{wexp}_F$, $c\ge0$, where $\mathrm{wexp}_F(a,b)$ is the wire exponent of a spectral point $F$ at the wire between parties $a$ and $b$. Prices are pseudometrics (symmetric, zero on the diagonal, satisfying the triangle inequality) and are closed under random pull-backs along maps of party sets.
