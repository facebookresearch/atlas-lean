# Upper functionals and spectral points on tensors

An *upper functional* on tensors over parties $I$ is a function $P\ge0$ with $P(\langle1\rangle)=1$, $P(s\otimes t)\le P(s)P(t)$, and $P(s)\le\sum_kP(b_k)$ whenever $s$ is *covered* by the family $(b_k)$ (that is, $s$ is a restriction of $\bigoplus_kb_k$). A *spectral point* is an upper functional that is multiplicative, $F(s\otimes t)=F(s)F(t)$; spectral points are monotone under restriction and additive on direct sums, in the spirit of Strassen's asymptotic spectrum. For a spectral point $F$ and a party map, the value at a unit tensor $\langle q\rangle$ with legs at given parties is $q^{g}$ for a *unit exponent* $g\in[0,1]$, and similarly wires have *wire exponents*.
