# Duality for tensors: covering versus spectral points

Below every upper functional $P$ on tensors over parties $I$ there is a spectral point $F\le P$ that agrees with $P$ asymptotically on a prescribed tensor, i.e. $F(t)=\lim_k P(t^{\otimes k})^{1/k}$. Consequently (duality) for tensors $s$ and $t$, $s^{\otimes k}$ is covered by $t^{\otimes(k+o(k))}$-many copies, in the sense of asymptotic covering, if and only if $F(s)\le F(t)$ for every spectral point $F$.

## Proof sketch

Work in the commutative monoid of tensor classes with its covering relation and apply a Hahn–Banach/Strassen-type separation for preordered semirings: subadditive submultiplicative functionals are dominated by multiplicative ones obtained by regularization along powers, and the asymptotic covering relation is characterized by all such monotone homomorphisms.
