# Vanishing modulo an ideal contains every positive minor

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M,N` be `R`-modules, and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`. The coefficient
ideal `c(f)` is generated by all `φ(f x)` with `x : M` and `φ : Dual R N`. For `r : ℕ`, put
`I_r(f)=c(exteriorPower.map r f)`. Let `J : Ideal R`, assume `N` finite free over `R`, and
suppose `f.baseChange (R/J)=0`. If `0<r`, then `I_r(f) ≤ J`.
