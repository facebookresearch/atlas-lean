# The ideal of ordered matrix minors

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `m,n` linearly ordered index types, `A : Matrix m n R`, and `r
: ℕ`. `Matrix.minorIdeal r A` is generated by determinants of `A.submatrix e_s e_t`, where `s`
and `t` are row and column subsets of cardinality `r` and `e_s,e_t : Fin r ↪o _` enumerate
them in order.
