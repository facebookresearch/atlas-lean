# The zero-size determinantal ideal is the unit ideal

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M,N` be `R`-modules, and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`. The coefficient
ideal `c(f)` is generated by all `φ(f x)` with `x : M` and `φ : Dual R N`. For `r : ℕ`, put
`I_r(f)=c(exteriorPower.map r f)`. `I_0(f)=⊤`, since the degree-zero exterior map is the
identity on `R` and the empty determinant is `1`.
