# Determinantal ideals are matrix-minor ideals in finite bases

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M,N` be `R`-modules, and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`. The coefficient
ideal `c(f)` is generated by all `φ(f x)` with `x : M` and `φ : Dual R N`. For `r : ℕ`, put
`I_r(f)=c(exteriorPower.map r f)`. Let `bM : Basis ι R M` and `bN : Basis κ R N`, with finite
linearly ordered index types `ι,κ` (and `Fintype ι`). Then `I_r(f)=Matrix.minorIdeal r
(LinearMap.toMatrix bM bN f)`. Rows index the target basis `κ`, and columns index the source
basis `ι`.
