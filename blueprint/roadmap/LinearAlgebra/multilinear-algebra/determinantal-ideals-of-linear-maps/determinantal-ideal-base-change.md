# Scalar extension of determinantal ideals

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M,N` be `R`-modules, and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`. The coefficient
ideal `c(f)` is generated by all `φ(f x)` with `x : M` and `φ : Dual R N`. For `r : ℕ`, put
`I_r(f)=c(exteriorPower.map r f)`. Let `S` be a commutative `R`-algebra and assume `N` is
finite free over `R`. Then `Ideal.map (algebraMap R S) I_r(f) = I_r(f.baseChange S)`.
