# Scalar extension of a coefficient ideal

Let `R` be a commutative ring, `M,N` be `R`-modules, and `f : M →ₗ[R] N`. The coefficient
ideal `c(f)` is generated by all `φ(f x)` with `x : M` and `φ : Dual R N`. Let `S` be a
commutative `R`-algebra and assume `N` is finite free over `R`. For `f_S : S ⊗[R] M → S ⊗[R]
N`, `Ideal.map (algebraMap R S) c(f) = c(f_S)`.
