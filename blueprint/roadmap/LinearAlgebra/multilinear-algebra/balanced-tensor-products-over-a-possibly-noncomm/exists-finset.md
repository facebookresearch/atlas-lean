# Finite pure-tensor expressions for balanced classes

Let k be a commutative ring, R a semiring, M a right R-module
and N a left R-module, both additive commutative groups with k-module
structures. For every x ∈ M ⊗_R N, formed as the balanced quotient
of M ⊗[k] N, there is a finite set s of pairs (m, n) with
x = ∑_(m,n)∈s [m ⊗ n]. The ring and module universes are independent.
