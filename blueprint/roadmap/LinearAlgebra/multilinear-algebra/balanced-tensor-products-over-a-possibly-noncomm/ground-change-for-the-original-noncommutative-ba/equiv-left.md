# Left-linear ground equivalence of balanced tensors

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
If a semiring A acts on Q on the left, commuting with k and the
right R-action, the ground comparison is an A-linear equivalence
between the k-ground and integer-ground quotients. Its pure value
remains [q ⊗ n]_k ↦ [q ⊗ n]_ℤ.
