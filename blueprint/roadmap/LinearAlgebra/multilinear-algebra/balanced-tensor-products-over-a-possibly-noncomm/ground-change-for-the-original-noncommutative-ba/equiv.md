# Ground-ring equivalence of balanced tensor quotients

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
This is the k-linear equivalence between the k-ground and integer-ground
balanced quotients. Its forward and inverse maps preserve the ordered
pure class [q ⊗ n].
