# Comparison from ground-balanced to integer-balanced tensors

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
This is the k-linear comparison from the k-ground balanced quotient
to the integer-ground balanced quotient, sending [q ⊗ n]_k to
[q ⊗ n]_ℤ. The target k-action is induced from Q.
