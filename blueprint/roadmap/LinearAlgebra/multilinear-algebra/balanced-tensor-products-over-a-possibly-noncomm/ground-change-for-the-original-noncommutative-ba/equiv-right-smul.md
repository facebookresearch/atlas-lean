# Right outer action preserved by the ground comparison

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
If a semiring A acts on N on the right, commuting with k and the
left R-action, the ground-ring equivalence intertwines the induced
right A-actions on the two quotients.
