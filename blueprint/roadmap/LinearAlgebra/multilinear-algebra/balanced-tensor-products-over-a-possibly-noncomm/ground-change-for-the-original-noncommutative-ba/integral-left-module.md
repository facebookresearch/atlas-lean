# Left outer module on integer balanced tensors

For a ring R, a right R-module Q and left R-module N, suppose
a semiring A acts on Q on the left and this action commutes with the
right R-action. The induced A-module structure on integral R Q N is
a • [q ⊗ n] = [(a • q) ⊗ n]. The integer ground actions are those
of the additive commutative groups.
