# Moving ground scalars across an integer-balanced tensor

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
In the integer-ground balanced quotient, scalars also act through N:
[q ⊗ (c • n)] = c • [q ⊗ n] for c ∈ k.
The balancing relation for algebraMap(k, R)(c) identifies this value
with [(c • q) ⊗ n].
