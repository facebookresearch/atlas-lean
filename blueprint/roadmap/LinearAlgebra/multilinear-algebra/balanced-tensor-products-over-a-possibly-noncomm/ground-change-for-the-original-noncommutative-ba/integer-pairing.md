# Integer-bilinear pairing into ground-balanced tensors

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
Regarded with their integer actions from addition, Q and N pair
integer-bilinearly into the k-ground balanced quotient by
(q, n) ↦ [q ⊗ n]_k.
