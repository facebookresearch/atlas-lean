# Ground-bilinear pairing into integer balanced tensors

Let k be a commutative ring, R a k-algebra which is a ring,
Q a right R-module and N a left R-module. Both modules are additive
commutative groups with k-actions compatible with their R-actions.
The pure pairing Q × N → integral R Q N is k-bilinear for the induced
k-action on the integer quotient. It sends (q, n) to [q ⊗ n]
and is linear in both variables.
