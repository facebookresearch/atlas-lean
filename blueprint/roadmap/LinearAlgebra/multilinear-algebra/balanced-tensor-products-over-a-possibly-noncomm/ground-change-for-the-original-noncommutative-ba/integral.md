# Integer-ground balanced tensor carrier

For a ring R, a right R-module Q and left R-module N with
additive commutative group structures, integral R Q N is the quotient
of Q ⊗[ℤ] N by the subgroup generated by (q r) ⊗ n - q ⊗ (r n),
using the integer actions from their additive groups.
Its carrier universe is max(q, n), independent of R's universe.
