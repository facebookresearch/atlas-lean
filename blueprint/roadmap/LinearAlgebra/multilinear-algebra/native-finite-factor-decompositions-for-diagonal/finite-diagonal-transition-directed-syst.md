# Directed-system laws for simultaneous tensor families

Let R be a commutative semiring, ι a preorder, M_a R-modules,
and V_(j,a) a family of R-modules indexed by any β, all with additive
commutative monoid structures. If f and every g_j satisfy identity and
composition, then f_ab ⊗ (⨂_j g_j,ab) satisfies those laws on the
simultaneous tensor modules. Neither finiteness of β nor directedness
of ι is needed for this directed-system structure.
