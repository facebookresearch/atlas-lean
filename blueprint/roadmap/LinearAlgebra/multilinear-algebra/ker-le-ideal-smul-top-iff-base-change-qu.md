# A surjective map becomes injective under quotient base change exactly when its kernel lies in the ideal multiple

Let R be a commutative ring in universe u, I⊆R an ideal and f:P→M a surjective R-linear map,
with source and target carrier universes v,w independent of u. Then ker(f)⊆IP if and only if the
base-changed map (R/I)⊗_R P→(R/I)⊗_R M is injective. Here IP is the submodule generated by
products i•p with i∈I. Surjectivity supplies the right-exact tensor presentation.
