# Orthogonal sum of systems of quadratic forms

Let $R$ be a commutative semiring, let $M$ and $N$ be $R$-modules, and let $(Q_i)_{i \in \iota}$ and $(Q'_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be families of quadratic forms on $M$ and on $N$ indexed by the same set $\iota$. Their orthogonal sum is the family $(Q_i \oplus Q'_i)_{i \in \iota}$ of quadratic forms on $M \times N$, where $(Q_i \oplus Q'_i)(x, y) = Q_i(x) + Q'_i(y)$.

## Idea

A system of quadratic forms is a finite or infinite family of forms on the same module, as in the study of common zeros of several quadratic equations. The orthogonal sum of two systems is taken index by index: the $i$-th form of the sum adds the $i$-th forms of the two systems on separate variables. It restricts to the original systems on each summand, so a common zero of the first system, paired with $0$, is a common zero of the sum.
