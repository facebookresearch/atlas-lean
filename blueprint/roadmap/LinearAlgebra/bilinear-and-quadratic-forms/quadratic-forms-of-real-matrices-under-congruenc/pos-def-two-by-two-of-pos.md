# Positive definiteness of a symmetric 2 × 2 matrix

Let $a, b, d \in \mathbb{R}$ with $a > 0$ and $a d - b^2 > 0$. Then the real symmetric matrix $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ b & d \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite.
