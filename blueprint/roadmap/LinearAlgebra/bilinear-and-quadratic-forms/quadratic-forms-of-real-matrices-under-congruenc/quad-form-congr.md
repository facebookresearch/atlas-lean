# Quadratic forms under congruence

For a finite index set $n$ and a real matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, write $Q_A(x) = x^{\top} A x$. For all real $n \times n$ matrices $A$, $U$ and all $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $Q_{U^{\top} A U}(x) = Q_A(U x)$. Neither orthogonality nor invertibility of $U$ is needed.
