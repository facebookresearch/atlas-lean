# Bilinear forms intertwining a matrix with its conjugated adjugate

Let $k$ be a field, let $\mu$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix over $k$ with $\det \mu \neq 0$, and let $b : k^2 \times k^2 \to k$ be a bilinear form such that

$$b(x v, w) = b\big(v, \mu^{-1} (\operatorname{adj} x)\, \mu\, w\big)$$

for every $2 \times 2$ matrix $x$ over $k$ and all $v, w \in k^2$, where $\operatorname{adj} x$ is the adjugate. Then there is $c \in k$ with

$$b(v, w) = c\, v^{\top} J \mu\, w \quad \text{for all } v, w \in k^2, \qquad J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$
