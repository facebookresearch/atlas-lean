# Eigenvector of a linear map

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an $R$-module, $f \colon M \to M$ an $R$-linear map and $\mu \in R$. A vector
$x \in M$ is an eigenvector of $f$ with eigenvalue $\mu$ if $x$ lies in the eigenspace of $f$ for $\mu$, the kernel of
$f - \mu \cdot 1$, and $x \neq 0$; equivalently, $x \neq 0$ and $f(x) = \mu x$.
