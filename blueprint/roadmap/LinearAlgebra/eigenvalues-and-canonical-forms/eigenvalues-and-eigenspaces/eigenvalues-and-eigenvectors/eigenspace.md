# Eigenspace of a linear map

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an $R$-module, $f \colon M \to M$ an $R$-linear map and $\mu \in R$. The eigenspace
of $f$ for $\mu$ is the submodule $\ker(f - \mu \cdot 1) = \{x \in M : f(x) = \mu x\}$ of $M$. It is defined for every
scalar $\mu$, and it is $\{0\}$ exactly when $\mu$ is not an eigenvalue of $f$.
