# Eigenvalues of a linear map

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an $R$-module and $f \colon M \to M$ an $R$-linear map. A scalar $\mu \in R$ is an
eigenvalue of $f$ if its eigenspace $\ker(f - \mu \cdot 1)$ is not zero, that is, if $f(x) = \mu x$ for some nonzero
$x \in M$. The eigenvalues of $f$ are the elements of the set of these scalars.
