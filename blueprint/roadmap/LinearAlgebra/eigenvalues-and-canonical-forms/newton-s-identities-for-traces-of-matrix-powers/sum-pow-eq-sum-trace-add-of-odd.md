# Sums of powers as traces of diagonal matrices, odd case

Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $n$ odd, and let $s = \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$, so $n = 2s + 1$. Then

$$1^k + 2^k + \cdots + n^k = \sum_{i=1}^{s} \operatorname{tr}\left(M_i^k\right) + n^k, \qquad M_i = \operatorname{diag}(i, 2s + 1 - i),$$

as real numbers, for every $k$, including $k = 0$.

## Sources

Peiran Ding, Yanjun Zhu and Lei Wang, "A New Formula for the Sum of Integer Powers Based on Elementary Matrix Theory", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A98, Lemma 2.
