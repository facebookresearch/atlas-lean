# Newton's identities for traces of matrix powers

Newton's identities relate the traces of the powers of a square matrix to the coefficients of its characteristic polynomial. The paper of Ding, Zhu and Wang that gives the trace formula for $2 \times 2$ matrices here also evaluates, as its Lemma 3, the integral $\int_0^1 x^r (1 - x)^r \, dx$; it is under [beta integral for equal natural powers](../../../Analysis/special-functions/gamma-function/beta-integral-for-equal-natural-powers/README.md) in Analysis.
