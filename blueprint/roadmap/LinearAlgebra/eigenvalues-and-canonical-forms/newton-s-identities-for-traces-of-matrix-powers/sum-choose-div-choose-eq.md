# An alternating sum of ratios of binomial coefficients

For every natural number $k$,

$$\sum_{r=0}^{\lfloor k/2 \rfloor} \frac{(-1)^r}{(k - r)(2r + 1)} \binom{k - r}{r} \binom{2r}{r}^{-1} = \frac{2}{k (k+1)}$$

in the rationals, where division by zero is read as $0$: at $k = 0$ the only term has denominator $0$, so both sides are $0$.

## Sources

Peiran Ding, Yanjun Zhu and Lei Wang, "A New Formula for the Sum of Integer Powers Based on Elementary Matrix Theory", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A98, Theorem 4.
