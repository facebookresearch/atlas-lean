# Sums of powers as traces of diagonal matrices, even case

Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers with $n$ even, and let $s = n/2$. Then

$$1^k + 2^k + \cdots + n^k = \sum_{i=1}^{s} \operatorname{tr}\left(M_i^k\right), \qquad M_i = \operatorname{diag}(i, 2s + 1 - i),$$

as real numbers. The statement includes $k = 0$ and $n = 0$, where both sides are $n$ and $0$.

## Sources

Peiran Ding, Yanjun Zhu and Lei Wang, "A New Formula for the Sum of Integer Powers Based on Elementary Matrix Theory", INTEGERS 25 (2025), Article A98, Lemma 1.
