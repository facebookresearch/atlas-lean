# A finitely generated torsion-free module over a principal ideal domain is free

Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain and $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module that is torsion-free: for every nonzero
$r \in R$, multiplication by $r$ is injective on $M$. Then $M$ is a free $R$-module.

## Sources

- D. S. Dummit and R. M. Foote, *Abstract Algebra*, 3rd edition, Wiley (2004), §12.1, Theorem 5(2) (a finitely generated
  module over a principal ideal domain is torsion-free if and only if it is free).
