# Existence of an eigenvalue over an algebraically closed field

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $V$ a finite-dimensional vector space over $K$ with $V \neq 0$. Then every
$K$-linear map $f \colon V \to V$ has an eigenvalue: there is $c \in K$ such that the eigenspace
$\ker(f - c \cdot 1)$ is not zero, that is, $f(v) = c v$ for some nonzero $v \in V$.
