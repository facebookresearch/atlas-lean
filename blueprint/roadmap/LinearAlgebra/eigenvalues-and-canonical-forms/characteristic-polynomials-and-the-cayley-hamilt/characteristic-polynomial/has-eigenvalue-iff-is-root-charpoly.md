# Eigenvalues are the roots of the characteristic polynomial

Let $R$ be an integral domain, $M$ a free module of finite rank over $R$, and $f \colon M \to M$ an $R$-linear map with
characteristic polynomial $\chi_f \in R[X]$, the determinant of $X \cdot 1 - A$ for the matrix $A$ of $f$ in a basis of
$M$. A scalar $\mu \in R$ is an eigenvalue of $f$, that is, $f(x) = \mu x$ for some nonzero $x \in M$, if and only if
$\chi_f(\mu) = 0$.
