# Cayley–Hamilton theorem for endomorphisms of finite free modules

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $M$ be an $R$-module that is free and finitely generated, and let $f \colon M \to M$
be an $R$-linear map. Let $\chi_f \in R[X]$ be the characteristic polynomial of $f$: the determinant of the matrix
$X \cdot 1 - A$ with entries in $R[X]$, where $A$ is the matrix of $f$ in a basis of $M$ fixed once and for all. Then
evaluating $\chi_f$ at $f$, in the $R$-algebra of $R$-linear maps $M \to M$, gives $\chi_f(f) = 0$.
