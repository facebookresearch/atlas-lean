# Spectral radius under a row-sum bound

Let $n$ be a finite nonempty index set and let $K$ be a real matrix with rows and columns indexed by
$n$, with nonnegative entries and all row sums at most $1$: $\sum_j K_{ij} \le 1$ for every $i$.
Then the spectral radius of $K$, viewed as a complex matrix, is at most $1$: every complex eigenvalue
$\lambda$ of $K$, real or not, satisfies $|\lambda| \le 1$. The proof bounds the spectral radius by
the operator norm induced by the maximum norm, which is the largest absolute row sum.
