# Observation

Let $K$ be a field, $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $A$ a $d \times d$ matrix over $K$, $x, c \in K^d$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The observation at time $n$ of the linear system with transition matrix $A$, initial state $x$ (a column vector) and output vector $c$ is

$$y_n = c^{\top} A^n x = \sum_{i=1}^{d} c_i\, (A^n x)_i .$$
