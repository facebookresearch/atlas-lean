# Charpoly pow trace

Let $L$ be a field, $V$ a finite-dimensional vector space over $L$, $T : V \to V$ a linear map, and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Let $E$ be an algebraically closed field that is an $L$-algebra. Let $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_d$ be the roots in $E$, listed with multiplicity, of the characteristic polynomial of $T$ with coefficients mapped into $E$. Then

$$\sum_{i=1}^{d} \lambda_i^{\,n} = \operatorname{tr}(T^n),$$

where the trace, an element of $L$, is mapped into $E$.
