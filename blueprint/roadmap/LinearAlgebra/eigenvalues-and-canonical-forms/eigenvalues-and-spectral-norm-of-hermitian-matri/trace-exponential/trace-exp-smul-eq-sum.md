# Trace exponential in terms of eigenvalues

For a Hermitian matrix $A$ over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_i(A)$ and a real number $\theta$,

$$\operatorname{tr} e^{\theta A} = \sum_i e^{\theta \lambda_i(A)},$$

an identity in $\mathbb{K}$. It combines the two previous facts: $e^{\theta A}$ is the standard matrix function of $a \mapsto e^{\theta a}$, and the trace of a standard matrix function is the sum of the function over the eigenvalues.
