# Real trace exponential in terms of eigenvalues

For a Hermitian matrix $A$ and a real number $\theta$, the real part of $\operatorname{tr} e^{\theta A}$ is $\sum_i e^{\theta\lambda_i(A)}$. (The trace is real; the real part is how the real-valued statements of this topic read it.)
