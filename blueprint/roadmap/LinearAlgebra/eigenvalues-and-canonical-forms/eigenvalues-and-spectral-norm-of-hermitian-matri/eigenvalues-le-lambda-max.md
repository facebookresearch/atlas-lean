# Every eigenvalue is at most the largest eigenvalue

For a Hermitian matrix $A$ indexed by a nonempty finite set, $\lambda_i(A) \le \lambda_{\max}(A)$ for every index $i$.

## Sources

- J. A. Tropp, An Introduction to Matrix Concentration Inequalities, Found. Trends Mach. Learn. 8 (2015), 1–230, arXiv:1501.01571, §2.1.6.
