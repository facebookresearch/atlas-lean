# Smallest eigenvalue

For a Hermitian matrix $A$ indexed by a nonempty finite set, $\lambda_{\min}(A) = \min_i \lambda_i(A)$ is its smallest eigenvalue; $\lambda_{\min}(A) = -\lambda_{\max}(-A)$.
