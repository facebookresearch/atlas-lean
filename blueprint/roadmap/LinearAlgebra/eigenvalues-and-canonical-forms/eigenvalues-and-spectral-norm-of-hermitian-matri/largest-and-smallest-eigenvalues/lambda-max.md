# Largest eigenvalue

For a Hermitian matrix $A$ indexed by a nonempty finite set, $\lambda_{\max}(A) = \max_i \lambda_i(A)$ is its largest eigenvalue. Its variational description $\lambda_{\max}(A) = \max_{\|u\| = 1} \langle u, A u\rangle$ is the top case of the Courant–Fischer theorem, and for a positive-semidefinite $A$ it equals the spectral norm.
