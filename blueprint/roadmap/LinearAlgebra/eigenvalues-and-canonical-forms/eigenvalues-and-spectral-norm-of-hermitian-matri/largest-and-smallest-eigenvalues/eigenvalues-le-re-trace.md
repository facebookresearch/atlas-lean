# Trace dominates each eigenvalue of a positive-semidefinite matrix

For a positive-semidefinite matrix $A$ over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and every index $i$, $\lambda_i(A) \le \operatorname{tr} A$; in particular $\lambda_{\max}(A) \le \operatorname{tr} A$. The trace is the sum of the eigenvalues, which are all nonnegative.
