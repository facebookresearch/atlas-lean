# The spectrum of the Hermitian dilation is symmetric

Let $J = \begin{bmatrix} I & 0 \\ 0 & -I \end{bmatrix}$ be the signature matrix of the block decomposition. Then $J\,\mathscr{H}(B)\,J = -\mathscr{H}(B)$. Since $J^2 = I$, the matrices $\mathscr{H}(B)$ and $-\mathscr{H}(B)$ are similar, so the eigenvalues of $\mathscr{H}(B)$ are symmetric about $0$.
