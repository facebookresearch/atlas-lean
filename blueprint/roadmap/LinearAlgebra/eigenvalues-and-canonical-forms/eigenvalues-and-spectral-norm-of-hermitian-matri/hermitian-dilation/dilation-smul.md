# The Hermitian dilation commutes with real scalars

$\mathscr{H}(rB) = r\,\mathscr{H}(B)$ for every scalar $r$ fixed by the star operation (a real scalar), so the dilation is real-linear; it is not complex-linear, since $\mathscr{H}(iB) \ne i\,\mathscr{H}(B)$ in general.
