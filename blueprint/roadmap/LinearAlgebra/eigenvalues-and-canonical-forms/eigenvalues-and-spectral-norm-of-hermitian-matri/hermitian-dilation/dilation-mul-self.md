# Square of the Hermitian dilation

For every rectangular matrix $B$,

$$\mathscr{H}(B)^2 = \begin{bmatrix} BB^* & 0 \\ 0 & B^*B \end{bmatrix}.$$

So the squared eigenvalues of $\mathscr{H}(B)$ are the squared singular values of $B$, together with zeros.
