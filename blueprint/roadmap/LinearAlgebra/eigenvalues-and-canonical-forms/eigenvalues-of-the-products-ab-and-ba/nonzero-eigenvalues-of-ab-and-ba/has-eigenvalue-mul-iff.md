# Nonzero eigenvalues of ST and TS coincide

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a nontrivially normed field, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, let
$S$ and $T$ be bounded linear operators on $E$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then $\mu$ is
an eigenvalue of $ST$ if and only if it is an eigenvalue of $TS$.
