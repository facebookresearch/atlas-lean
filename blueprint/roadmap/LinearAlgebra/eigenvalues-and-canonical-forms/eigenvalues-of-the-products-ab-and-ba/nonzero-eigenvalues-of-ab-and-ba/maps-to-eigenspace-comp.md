# Eigenspace of fg maps into the eigenspace of gf

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, zero allowed. Then $g$ maps the
$\mu$-eigenspace of $f \circ g$ into the $\mu$-eigenspace of $g \circ f$: if $f(g(x)) = \mu x$, then
$g(f(g(x))) = \mu\, g(x)$.
