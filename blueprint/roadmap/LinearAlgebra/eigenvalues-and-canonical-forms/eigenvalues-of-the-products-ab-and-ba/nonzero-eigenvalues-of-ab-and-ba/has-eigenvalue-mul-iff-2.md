# Nonzero eigenvalues of fg and gf coincide, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then $\mu$ is an eigenvalue of $AB$
if and only if it is an eigenvalue of $BA$. This is the case $W = V$ of
[the statement for $fg$ and $gf$](has-eigenvalue-comp-iff.md).
