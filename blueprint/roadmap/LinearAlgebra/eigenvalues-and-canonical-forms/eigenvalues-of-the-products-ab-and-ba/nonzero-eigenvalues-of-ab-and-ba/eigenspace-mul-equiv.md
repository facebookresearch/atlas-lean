# Eigenspaces of fg and gf are equivalent, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the $\mu$-eigenspaces of $AB$
and of $BA$ are isomorphic as $\mathbb{K}$-vector spaces, by the restriction of $B$,
$x \mapsto Bx$. This is the case $W = V$ of
[the linear equivalence of the eigenspaces of $fg$ and $gf$](eigenspace-comp-equiv.md).
