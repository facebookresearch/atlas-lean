# Linear equivalence of eigenspaces of fg and gf

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the $\mu$-eigenspaces of
$f \circ g$ and of $g \circ f$ are isomorphic as $\mathbb{K}$-vector spaces, by the restriction of
$g$, which is [injective](injective-restrict-eigenspace-comp.md) and
[surjective](surjective-restrict-eigenspace-comp.md) between them.
