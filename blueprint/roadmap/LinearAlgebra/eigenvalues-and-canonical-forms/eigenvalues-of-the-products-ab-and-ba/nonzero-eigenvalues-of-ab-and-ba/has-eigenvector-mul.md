# Eigenvectors of fg map to eigenvectors of gf, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. If $x$ is an eigenvector of $AB$
with eigenvalue $\mu$, then $Bx$ is an eigenvector of $BA$ with eigenvalue $\mu$. This is the case
$W = V$ of [the statement for $fg$ and $gf$](has-eigenvector-comp.md).
