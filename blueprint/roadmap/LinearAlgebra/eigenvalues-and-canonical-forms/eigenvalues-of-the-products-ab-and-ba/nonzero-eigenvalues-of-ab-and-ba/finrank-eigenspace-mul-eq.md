# Equal geometric multiplicities of nonzero eigenvalues of ST and TS

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a nontrivially normed field, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, let
$S$ and $T$ be bounded linear operators on $E$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the
$\mu$-eigenspaces of $ST$ and of $TS$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space
is given dimension $0$.
