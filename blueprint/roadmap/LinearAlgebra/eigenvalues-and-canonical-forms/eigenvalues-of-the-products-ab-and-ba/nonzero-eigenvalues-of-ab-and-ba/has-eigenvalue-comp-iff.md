# Nonzero eigenvalues of fg and gf coincide

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then $\mu$ is an eigenvalue
of $f \circ g$ if and only if it is an eigenvalue of $g \circ f$.
