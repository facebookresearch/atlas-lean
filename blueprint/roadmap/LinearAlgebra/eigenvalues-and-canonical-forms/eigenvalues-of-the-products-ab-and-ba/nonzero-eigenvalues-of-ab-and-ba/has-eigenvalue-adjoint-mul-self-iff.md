# Nonzero eigenvalues of an operator times its adjoint, in either order

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, let $E$ be a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K}$, let
$T$ be a bounded linear operator on $E$ with adjoint $T^*$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero.
Then $\mu$ is an eigenvalue of $T^* T$ if and only if it is an eigenvalue of $T T^*$.
