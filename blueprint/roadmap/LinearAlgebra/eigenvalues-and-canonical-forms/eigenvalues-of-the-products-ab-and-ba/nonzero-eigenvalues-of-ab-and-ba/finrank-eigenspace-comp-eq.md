# Geometric multiplicities of fg and gf agree

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the $\mu$-eigenspaces of
$f \circ g$ and of $g \circ f$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space is given
dimension $0$. When $V$ and $W$ are finite-dimensional, these dimensions are the geometric
multiplicities of $\mu$ as an eigenvalue of $f \circ g$ and of $g \circ f$.
