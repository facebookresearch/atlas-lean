# Nonzero eigenvalues of matrix products AB and BA coincide

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a field, let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ an $n \times m$ matrix over
$\mathbb{K}$, with rows and columns indexed by finite sets, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero.
Then $\mu$ is an eigenvalue of the square matrix $AB$ if and only if it is an eigenvalue of $BA$.
