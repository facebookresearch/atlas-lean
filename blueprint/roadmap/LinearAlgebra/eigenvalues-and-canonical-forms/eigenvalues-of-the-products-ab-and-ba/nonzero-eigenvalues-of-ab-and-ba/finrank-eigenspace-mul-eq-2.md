# Geometric multiplicities of fg and gf agree, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the $\mu$-eigenspaces of $AB$
and of $BA$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space is given dimension $0$.
When $V$ is finite-dimensional, these dimensions are the geometric multiplicities of $\mu$ as an
eigenvalue of $AB$ and of $BA$. This is the case $W = V$ of
[the statement for $fg$ and $gf$](finrank-eigenspace-comp-eq.md).
