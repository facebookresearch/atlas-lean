# Geometric multiplicities of matrix products AB and BA agree

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a field, let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ an $n \times m$ matrix over
$\mathbb{K}$, with rows and columns indexed by finite sets, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero.
Then the geometric multiplicities of $\mu$ as an eigenvalue of $AB$ and of $BA$ agree: the
$\mu$-eigenspaces of $AB$ in $\mathbb{K}^m$ and of $BA$ in $\mathbb{K}^n$ have the same dimension.
