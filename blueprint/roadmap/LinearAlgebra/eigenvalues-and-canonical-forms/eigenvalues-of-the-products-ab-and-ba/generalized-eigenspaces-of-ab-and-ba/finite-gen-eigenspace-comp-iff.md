# Generalized eigenspaces of fg and gf are finite-dimensional together

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of
$f \circ g$ is finite-dimensional if and only if that of $g \circ f$ is. Here the generalized
$\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of an endomorphism $T$ is $\ker (T - \mu)^k$ for finite $k$, and
the union of these kernels over all finite $k$ for $k = \infty$.
