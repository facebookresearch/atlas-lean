# Generalized eigenspaces of fg and gf have equal dimension

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspaces of exponent $k$ of
$f \circ g$ and of $g \circ f$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space is given
dimension $0$; they are finite-dimensional together. Here the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of
exponent $k$ of an endomorphism $T$ is $\ker (T - \mu)^k$ for finite $k$, and the union of these
kernels over all finite $k$ for $k = \infty$. When $V$ and $W$ are finite-dimensional, the
generalized eigenspaces of exponent $\infty$ have dimension the algebraic multiplicity of $\mu$.
