# Equal dimensions of generalized eigenspaces of ST and TS

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a nontrivially normed field, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, let
$S$ and $T$ be bounded linear operators on $E$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspaces of exponent $k$ of $ST$
and of $TS$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space is given dimension $0$.
Here the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of an operator $R$ is $\ker (R - \mu)^k$ for
finite $k$, and the union of these kernels over all finite $k$ for $k = \infty$.
