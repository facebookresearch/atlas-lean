# Finite-dimensionality of generalized eigenspaces of ST and TS

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a nontrivially normed field, let $E$ be a normed space over $\mathbb{K}$, let
$S$ and $T$ be bounded linear operators on $E$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of $ST$ is
finite-dimensional if and only if that of $TS$ is.
