# Generalized eigenspaces of AB and BA are finite-dimensional together

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a field, let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ an $n \times m$ matrix over
$\mathbb{K}$, with rows and columns indexed by finite sets, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and
let $k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of
$AB$ is finite-dimensional if and only if that of $BA$ is. For matrices both always are, being
subspaces of $\mathbb{K}^m$ and $\mathbb{K}^n$; this is the matrix case of
[the statement for linear maps](finite-gen-eigenspace-comp-iff.md).
