# Injectivity of g on generalized eigenspaces of fg

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, let $f : V \to W$ and $g : W \to V$ be
linear maps, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let $k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the
[restriction of $g$](gen-eigenspace-comp-map.md) to the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent
$k$ of $f \circ g$, as a map into that of $g \circ f$, is injective.
