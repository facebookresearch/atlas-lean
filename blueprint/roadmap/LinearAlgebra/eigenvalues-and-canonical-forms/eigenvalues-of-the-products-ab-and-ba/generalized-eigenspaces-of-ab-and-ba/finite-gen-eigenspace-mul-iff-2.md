# Generalized eigenspaces finite-dimensional together, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of $AB$
is finite-dimensional if and only if that of $BA$ is. Here the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of
exponent $k$ of an endomorphism $T$ is $\ker (T - \mu)^k$ for finite $k$, and the union of these
kernels over all finite $k$ for $k = \infty$. This is the case $W = V$ of
[the statement for $fg$ and $gf$](finite-gen-eigenspace-comp-iff.md).
