# Generalized eigenspace of fg maps into that of gf

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, let $f : V \to W$ and $g : W \to V$ be
linear maps, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$, zero allowed, and let $k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then
$g$ maps the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of $f \circ g$ into that of $g \circ f$,
because $(g \circ f) \circ g = g \circ (f \circ g)$. Here the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of
exponent $k$ of an endomorphism $T$ is $\ker (T - \mu)^k$ for finite $k$, and the union of these
kernels over all finite $k$ for $k = \infty$.
