# Generalized eigenspace dimensions agree, for endomorphisms

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $A$ and $B$ be linear
endomorphisms of $V$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let
$k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the generalized $\mu$-eigenspaces of exponent $k$ of $AB$
and of $BA$ have the same dimension, where an infinite-dimensional space is given dimension $0$.
Here the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of an endomorphism $T$ is
$\ker (T - \mu)^k$ for finite $k$, and the union of these kernels over all finite $k$ for
$k = \infty$. This is the case $W = V$ of
[the statement for $fg$ and $gf$](finrank-gen-eigenspace-comp-eq.md).
