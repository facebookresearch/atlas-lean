# Kernel of g meets a generalized eigenspace of fg trivially

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, let $f : V \to W$ and $g : W \to V$ be
linear maps, let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero, and let $k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$. If $x$
lies in the generalized $\mu$-eigenspace of exponent $k$ of $f \circ g$ and $g(x) = 0$, then
$x = 0$. Indeed, $f \circ g$ vanishes on the kernel of $g$, so $(f \circ g - \mu)^l$ acts there as
multiplication by $(-\mu)^l \ne 0$.
