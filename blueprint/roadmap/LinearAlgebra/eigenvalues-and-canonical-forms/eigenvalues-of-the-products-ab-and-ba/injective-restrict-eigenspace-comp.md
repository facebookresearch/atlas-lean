# Injectivity of g between eigenspaces of fg and gf

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over a field $\mathbb{K}$, of any dimension, let $f : V \to W$ and
$g : W \to V$ be linear maps, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{K}$ be nonzero. Then the restriction of $g$ to
the $\mu$-eigenspace of $f \circ g$, as a map into the $\mu$-eigenspace of $g \circ f$, is
injective: if $f(g(x)) = \mu x$ and $g(x) = 0$, then $\mu x = 0$, so $x = 0$.
