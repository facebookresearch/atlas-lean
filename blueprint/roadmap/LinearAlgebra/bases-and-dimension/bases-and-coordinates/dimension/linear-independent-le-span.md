# A linearly independent family is no larger than a finite spanning set

Let $R$ be a semiring with the strong rank condition: an injective linear map from $R^n$ to $R^m$ exists only when
$n \le m$. Every nonzero commutative ring has it, in particular every field. Let $M$ be a module over $R$, let
$(v_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a linearly independent family in $M$, and let $w$ be a finite set of elements of $M$ that spans
$M$. Then the cardinality of $\iota$ is at most the number of elements of $w$.
