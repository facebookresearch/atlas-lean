# Any two bases are indexed by sets of the same cardinality

Let $R$ be a semiring with the invariant basis number property, which says that $R^n$ and $R^m$ are isomorphic as
modules over $R$ only when $n = m$; every nonzero commutative ring has it, in particular every field. This hypothesis
is weaker than the strong rank condition, under which an injective linear map from $R^n$ to $R^m$ exists only when
$n \le m$. Let $M$ be a module over $R$. If $(v_i)_{i \in \iota}$ and $(v'_j)_{j \in \iota'}$ are two bases of $M$,
then the index sets $\iota$ and $\iota'$ have the same cardinality.
