# Linearly independent family

Let $R$ be a semiring, $M$ a module over $R$, and $(v_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of elements of $M$ indexed by a set
$\iota$. The family is linearly independent when the linear map that sends a family of coefficients
$(c_i)_{i \in \iota}$ in $R$, only finitely many of them nonzero, to the linear combination $\sum_i c_i v_i$ is
injective: two such linear combinations are equal only when their coefficients are equal. When $R$ is a ring, for
instance a field, two linear combinations are equal exactly when their difference, the combination with the
differences of the coefficients, is $0$; so the family is then linearly independent exactly when $\sum_i c_i v_i = 0$
only for coefficients that are all zero.
