# Every vector space has a basis

Let $K$ be a division ring, for instance a field, and $V$ a vector space over $K$. Then there are a subset
$s \subseteq V$ and a basis of $V$ indexed by $s$. Here a basis indexed by $s$ is a linear isomorphism from $V$ onto the
space of finitely supported functions $s \to K$, which sends a vector to its coordinates; its basis vectors form a
linearly independent family that spans $V$.
