# Adjoining a vector outside the span preserves linear independence

Let $K$ be a division ring, $V$ a vector space over $K$, $(v_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of vectors of $V$, and $s$ a
set of indices such that the vectors $v_i$, $i \in s$, are linearly independent. If $x$ is an index such that $v_x$
does not lie in the span of $\{v_i : i \in s\}$, then the vectors $v_i$, $i \in s \cup \{x\}$, are linearly
independent.
