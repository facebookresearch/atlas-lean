# Extending a linearly independent set within a larger set

Let $K$ be a division ring, $V$ a vector space over $K$ and $(v_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of vectors of $V$. Let
$s \subseteq t$ be sets of indices such that the vectors $v_i$, $i \in s$, are linearly independent. Then there is a
set $b$ with $s \subseteq b \subseteq t$ such that the vectors $v_j$, $j \in b$, are linearly independent and every
$v_i$ with $i \in t$ lies in their span.
