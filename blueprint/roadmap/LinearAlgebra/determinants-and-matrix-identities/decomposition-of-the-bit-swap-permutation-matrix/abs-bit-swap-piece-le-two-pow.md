# Uniform power-of-two entry bound for bit-swap pieces

For all $n, i \in \mathbb{N}$, every entry of the [piece](bit-swap-piece.md) $\Pi_{n,i}$ has
absolute value at most $2^n$. This is the uniform entry bound used in the source; it follows from
the [binomial bound](abs-bit-swap-piece-le-choose.md) since $\binom{n}{i} \le 2^n$.
