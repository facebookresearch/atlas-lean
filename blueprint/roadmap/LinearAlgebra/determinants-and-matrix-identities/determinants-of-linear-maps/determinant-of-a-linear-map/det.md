# Determinant of a linear map

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a module over $R$. The determinant of $R$-linear maps from $M$ to itself is the map
$f \mapsto \det f \in R$, multiplicative with $\det(\mathrm{id}) = 1$, defined as follows. If $M$ has a basis indexed
by a finite set of its elements, one such basis is chosen once and for all, and $\det f$ is the determinant of the
matrix of $f$ in that basis. If $M$ has no such basis, $\det f = 1$ for every $f$.
