# The determinant of a linear map is the determinant of its matrix in any basis

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ a module over $R$, and $b$ a basis of $M$ indexed by a finite set. For every
$R$-linear map $f \colon M \to M$, the determinant of the matrix of $f$ in the basis $b$ equals $\det f$, the determinant
of $f$.
