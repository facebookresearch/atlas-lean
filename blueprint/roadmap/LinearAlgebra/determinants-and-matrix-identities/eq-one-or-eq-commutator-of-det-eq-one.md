# Determinant-one elements of GL₂(ℤ/3ℤ) are the identity or commutators

Let $g \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z})$ with $\det g = 1$. Then either $g = I$, or $g$ is a commutator in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z})$: there exist $x, y \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z})$ with

$$g = x\, y\, x^{-1} y^{-1}.$$
