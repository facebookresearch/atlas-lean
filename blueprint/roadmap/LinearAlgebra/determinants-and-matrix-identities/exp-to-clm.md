# Operator and matrix exponentials agree

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $\iota$ a finite index set, $B$ a square matrix over $\mathbb{K}$ indexed by $\iota$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v \in \mathbb{K}^\iota$. The exponential of $t$ times the continuous linear map $v \mapsto B v$, applied to $v$, equals $e^{tB} v$, the matrix exponential of $tB$ times $v$.
