# Both terms of the Schur energy splitting are nonnegative

Let $m$, $p$ be finite index sets and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ real matrices, and suppose the block matrix $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite (symmetric with $x^{\top} G x > 0$ for all $x \ne 0$). Then for all $\phi_P \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $j_I \in \mathbb{R}^p$, both $\phi_P^{\top} (A - B D^{-1} B^{\top}) \phi_P \ge 0$ and $j_I^{\top} D^{-1} j_I \ge 0$.
