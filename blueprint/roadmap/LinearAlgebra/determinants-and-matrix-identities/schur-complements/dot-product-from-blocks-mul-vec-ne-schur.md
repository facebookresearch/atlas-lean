# A nonzero interior source changes the Schur energy

Let $m$, $p$ be finite index sets and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ real matrices, and suppose the block matrix $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite (symmetric with $x^{\top} G x > 0$ for all $x \ne 0$). Let $\phi_P \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $\phi_I \in \mathbb{R}^p$ with $B^{\top} \phi_P + D \phi_I \ne 0$. Then, with $\phi = (\phi_P, \phi_I)$, $\phi^{\top} G \phi$ the quadratic form of $G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{pmatrix}$ and $K = A - B D^{-1} B^{\top}$ the Schur complement, $\phi^{\top} G \phi \ne \phi_P^{\top} K \phi_P$.
