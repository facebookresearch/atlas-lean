# Symmetry criterion for matrices indexed by a product

Let $\rho$ and $\gamma$ be linearly ordered index sets and let $A$ be a matrix with rows and columns
indexed by $\rho \times \gamma$. Then $A$ is symmetric if and only if
(1) for every $a \in \rho$ the submatrix $\bigl(A_{(a,i),(a,j)}\bigr)_{i,j \in \gamma}$ is symmetric;
(2) for every $i \in \gamma$ the submatrix $\bigl(A_{(a,i),(b,i)}\bigr)_{a,b \in \rho}$ is symmetric; and
(3) for all $a < b$ in $\rho$ and $i < j$ in $\gamma$, $A_{(a,i),(b,j)} = A_{(b,j),(a,i)}$ and
$A_{(a,j),(b,i)} = A_{(b,i),(a,j)}$.
