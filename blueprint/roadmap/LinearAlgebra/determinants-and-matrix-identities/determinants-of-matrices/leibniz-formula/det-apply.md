# Leibniz formula for the determinant

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a square matrix over $R$ whose rows and columns are indexed by a finite set $\iota$.
Then

$$\det M = \sum_{\sigma} \operatorname{sign}(\sigma) \prod_{i \in \iota} M_{\sigma(i), i},$$

where the sum runs over the permutations $\sigma$ of $\iota$ and $\operatorname{sign}(\sigma) \in \{1, -1\}$ is the sign of
$\sigma$.
