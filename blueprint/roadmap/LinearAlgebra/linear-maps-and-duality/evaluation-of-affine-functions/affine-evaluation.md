# Evaluation of an affine function at two points

Let $F$ be a field and let $\theta \ne \theta'$ be elements of $F$. The map
$(a, b) \mapsto (a\theta + b,\ a\theta' + b)$, which sends the slope $a$ and the intercept $b$ of the
affine function $x \mapsto ax + b$ to its values at $\theta$ and $\theta'$, is a linear isomorphism
$F^2 \to F^2$. Its inverse sends $(y, y')$ to $\bigl(\frac{y - y'}{\theta - \theta'},\
y - \frac{y - y'}{\theta - \theta'}\, \theta\bigr)$.
