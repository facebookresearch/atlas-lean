# Partial trace over the first factor

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $m$ and $n$ be finite index sets. The
partial trace over the first factor maps a square matrix $A$ with rows and columns indexed by
$m \times n$ to the $n \times n$ matrix

$$\operatorname{tr}_1(A)_{j j'} = \sum_{i \in m} A_{(i, j), (i, j')}.$$
