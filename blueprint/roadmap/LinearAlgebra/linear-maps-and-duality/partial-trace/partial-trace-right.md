# Partial trace over the second factor

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and let $m$ and $n$ be finite index sets. The
partial trace over the second factor maps a square matrix $A$ with rows and columns indexed by
$m \times n$ to the $m \times m$ matrix

$$\operatorname{tr}_2(A)_{i i'} = \sum_{j \in n} A_{(i, j), (i', j)}.$$
