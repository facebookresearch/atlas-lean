# Trace and determinant are invariant under semiconjugacy

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ and $N$ two $R$-modules, $e : M \to N$ a linear isomorphism, and
$f$ and $g$ endomorphisms of $M$ and $N$ with $e \circ f = g \circ e$. Then $\operatorname{tr} f =
\operatorname{tr} g$ and $\det f = \det g$: since $g = e f e^{-1}$, both invariants agree. (For a
module without a finite basis the trace is taken to be $0$ and the determinant $1$.)
