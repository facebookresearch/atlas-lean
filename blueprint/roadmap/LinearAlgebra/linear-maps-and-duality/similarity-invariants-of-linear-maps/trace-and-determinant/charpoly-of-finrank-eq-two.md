# Characteristic polynomial of an endomorphism of a free module of rank two

Let $R$ be a nontrivial commutative ring and let $M$ be a finitely generated free $R$-module of rank
$2$. For every $R$-linear endomorphism $f$ of $M$, the characteristic polynomial of $f$ is

$$\chi_f(X) = X^2 - \operatorname{tr}(f)\, X + \det(f).$$

The hypothesis is on the module, which has rank two; the endomorphism $f$ itself may have any rank.
