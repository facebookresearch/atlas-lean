# Kernel of the dual map has the same dimension as the kernel

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $K$ and $f : V \to V$ a linear map. The
kernel of the dual map $f^* : V^* \to V^*$ has the same dimension as the kernel of $f$. The kernel of
$f^*$ is the annihilator of the range of $f$, so its dimension, like that of $\ker f$ by rank–nullity, is
$\dim V - \dim \operatorname{range} f$.
