# Reflection attached to a vector and a linear form

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ an $R$-module, let $x \in M$, and let $f \colon M \to R$ be an $R$-linear form
with $f(x) = 2$. The reflection attached to $x$ and $f$ is the $R$-linear bijection $M \to M$,
$y \mapsto y - f(y)\, x$, which is its own inverse.
