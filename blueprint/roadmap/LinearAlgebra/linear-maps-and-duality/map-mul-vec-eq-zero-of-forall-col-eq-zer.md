# Linear functionals vanishing on the columns of a matrix

Let $\kappa$ be any index set, $\iota$ a finite index set, $A$ a real matrix with rows indexed by $\kappa$ and columns by $\iota$, and $\varphi \colon \mathbb{R}^{\kappa} \to \mathbb{R}$ a linear functional with $\varphi(A_{\cdot s}) = 0$ for every column $A_{\cdot s}$ of $A$. Then $\varphi(A \xi) = 0$ for every $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$.
