# Singular values of a linear map

Let $\mathbb K$ be $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, let $E$ and $F$ be finite-dimensional inner product spaces over
$\mathbb K$ with $n = \dim E$, and let $T \colon E \to F$ be a $\mathbb K$-linear map with adjoint $T^*$. The singular
values of $T$ form the finitely supported sequence $(\sigma_i)_{i \in \mathbb N}$ of real numbers, indexed from $0$,
in which $\sigma_i$ for $i < n$ is the square root of the $(i+1)$-th largest eigenvalue of the self-adjoint operator
$T^* T$ on $E$, the eigenvalues being listed in decreasing order and each repeated as many times as the dimension of
its eigenspace, and $\sigma_i = 0$ for $i \geq n$.
