# Spectral condition number of a positive definite matrix

For a real symmetric positive definite $n \times n$ matrix $A$, the spectral condition number is
$\kappa(A) = \lambda_{\max} / \lambda_{\min}$, the ratio of the largest to the smallest eigenvalue
of $A$. For $n = 0$ there are no eigenvalues, and the definition gives $0$, by the convention that a
quotient with zero denominator is $0$; the [error bound](energy-norm-iterate-sub-le-cond-num.md) is
stated for $n \ge 1$.
