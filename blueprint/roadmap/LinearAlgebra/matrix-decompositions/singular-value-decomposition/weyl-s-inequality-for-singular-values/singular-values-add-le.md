# Upper half of Weyl's inequality for singular values

For linear maps $T, U : E \to F$ between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and every
$k \ge 0$, $\sigma_k(T + U) \le \sigma_k(T) + \|U\|_2$, where $\sigma_0 \ge \sigma_1 \ge \cdots$ are the
singular values, indexed from $0$ and followed by zeros, and $\|U\|_2$ is the spectral norm of $U$.

## Informally uses

- [Eckart–Young theorem in operator norm](../../../../Analysis/operator-theory/eigenvalue-inequalities/eckart-young-best-rank-approx-operator-n.md): $\sigma_k(T + U)$ and $\sigma_k(T)$ are the Eckart–Young distances from $T + U$ and from $T$ to the maps of rank at most $k$.
