# Schur complement of an invertible block

For finite index sets $m$, $n$, $p$ and real matrices $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$, $C \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times n}$ and an invertible $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$, the Schur complement of $D$ is $A - B D^{-1} C \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$.
