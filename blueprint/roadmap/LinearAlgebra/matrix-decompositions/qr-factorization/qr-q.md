# The Q factor of the Gram–Schmidt QR factorization

For a real $m \times k$ matrix $A$ with columns $a_1, \dots, a_k$, the Gram–Schmidt factor $Q$ is the $m \times k$ matrix whose $j$-th column is $q_j = v_j / \|v_j\|$, the normalized $j$-th vector of
the Gram–Schmidt process applied to $a_1, \dots, a_k$ (with the convention $q_j = 0$ when
$v_j = 0$).
