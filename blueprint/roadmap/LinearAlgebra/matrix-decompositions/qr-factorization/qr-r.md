# The R factor of the Gram–Schmidt QR factorization

For a real $m \times k$ matrix $A$, the Gram–Schmidt factor $R$ is the $k \times k$ matrix with entries
$r_{ij} = \langle q_i, a_j \rangle$, where $q_i$ is the $i$-th column of the Gram–Schmidt
factor $Q$ and $a_j$ the $j$-th column of $A$.
