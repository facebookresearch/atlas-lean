# Exists two Level prod mul diagonal eq

Call a $d \times d$ matrix $M$ two-level on a pair of distinct indices $i \ne j$ if $M_{kl} = \delta_{kl}$ whenever $k \notin \{i, j\}$ or $l \notin \{i, j\}$.

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $U \in U(d)$ be a $d \times d$ complex unitary matrix. Then there are $N \le d(d-1)/2$ unitary matrices $M_1, \dots, M_N \in U(d)$, each two-level on some pair of distinct indices, and phases $\varphi_1, \dots, \varphi_d \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\lvert \varphi_k \rvert = 1$ for all $k$, such that

$$U = M_1 M_2 \cdots M_N \, \operatorname{diag}(\varphi_1, \dots, \varphi_d).$$
