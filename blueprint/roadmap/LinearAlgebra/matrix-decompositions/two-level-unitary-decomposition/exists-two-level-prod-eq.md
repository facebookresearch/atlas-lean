# Exists two Level prod eq

Call a $d \times d$ matrix $M$ two-level on a pair of distinct indices $i \ne j$ if $M_{kl} = \delta_{kl}$ whenever $k \notin \{i, j\}$ or $l \notin \{i, j\}$, so that $M$ differs from the identity only in the $2 \times 2$ block on rows and columns $i, j$.

Let $d \ge 2$ and let $U \in U(d)$ be a $d \times d$ complex unitary matrix, with indices $0, \dots, d-1$. Then there are exactly $N = d(d-1)/2$ unitary matrices $M_1, \dots, M_N \in U(d)$, each two-level on some adjacent pair $(s, s+1)$ with $s + 1 < d$, such that

$$U = M_1 M_2 \cdots M_N.$$
