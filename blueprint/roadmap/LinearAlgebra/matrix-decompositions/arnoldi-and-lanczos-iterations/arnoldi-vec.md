# Arnoldi vectors

The Arnoldi vectors $q_0, \dots, q_{k-1}$ are the normalized Gram–Schmidt vectors of the
Krylov vectors $r, Tr, \dots, T^{k-1} r$; in exact arithmetic and without breakdown they are the
vectors produced by the Arnoldi iteration.

## Sources
