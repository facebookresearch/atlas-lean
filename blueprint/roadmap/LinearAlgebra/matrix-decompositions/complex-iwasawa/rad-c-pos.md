# Rad C pos

Let $g = \begin{pmatrix} g_{11} & g_{12} \\ g_{21} & g_{22} \end{pmatrix}$ be a complex $2 \times 2$ matrix and $z \in \mathbb{C}$. Write $P(z) = g_{11} + z\, g_{21}$, $Q(z) = g_{12} + z\, g_{22}$ and $r(z) = \sqrt{\lvert P(z) \rvert^2 + \lvert Q(z) \rvert^2}$. If $\det g \neq 0$, then

$$r(z) > 0,$$

since $P(z) = Q(z) = 0$ would make the rows of $g$ proportional.
