# Rad C

Let $g = \begin{pmatrix} g_{11} & g_{12} \\ g_{21} & g_{22} \end{pmatrix}$ be a complex $2 \times 2$ matrix and $z \in \mathbb{C}$. With $P(z) = g_{11} + z\, g_{21}$ and $Q(z) = g_{12} + z\, g_{22}$, the normalizing radius in complex Iwasawa coordinates is the nonnegative real number

$$r(z) = \sqrt{\lvert P(z) \rvert^2 + \lvert Q(z) \rvert^2}.$$
