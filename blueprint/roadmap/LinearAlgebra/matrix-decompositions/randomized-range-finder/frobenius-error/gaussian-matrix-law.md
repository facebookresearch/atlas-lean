# Standard Gaussian test matrix

For natural numbers $n$ and $\ell$, the standard Gaussian law on real $n \times \ell$ matrices is the law of an $n \times \ell$ standard Gaussian matrix: independent $N(0,1)$ entries, as a product measure on the entry functions $\{0,\dots,n-1\} \times \{0,\dots,\ell-1\} \to \mathbb{R}$.

## Sources

- N. Halko, P.-G. Martinsson and J. A. Tropp, Finding structure with randomness: probabilistic algorithms for constructing approximate matrix decompositions, SIAM Rev. 53 (2011), 217–288, arXiv:0909.4061, §4.1 and §10.
