# Frobenius error of the range finder

The Frobenius error of the range finder for $A$ and the test matrix $\Omega$ is the Frobenius norm $\|(I - P_Y)A\|_F = \big(\sum_j \|(I - P_Y)A e_j\|^2\big)^{1/2}$ of the range-finder residual, where $e_j$ are the standard basis vectors of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

## Sources

- N. Halko, P.-G. Martinsson and J. A. Tropp, Finding structure with randomness: probabilistic algorithms for constructing approximate matrix decompositions, SIAM Rev. 53 (2011), 217–288, arXiv:0909.4061, §10.2.
