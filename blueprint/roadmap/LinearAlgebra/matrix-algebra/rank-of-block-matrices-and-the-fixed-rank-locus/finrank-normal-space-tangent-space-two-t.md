# Tangent and normal dimensions for rank-one 2 × 2 matrices

Let $K$ be a field and $F$ a $2 \times 2$ matrix over $K$ of rank $1$. Then the normal space $N_F = \{Y : F^{\top} Y = 0 \text{ and } Y F^{\top} = 0\}$ has dimension $1$ and the tangent space $T_F = \{X : u^{\top} X v = 0 \text{ for all } u, v \text{ with } u^{\top} F = 0 \text{ and } F v = 0\}$ has dimension $3$.
