# Dimension of the tangent space to the rank-k locus

Let $K$ be a field and $F$ a matrix over $K$ with $n$ rows and $r$ columns ($n, r$ finite) and rank $k$. Then the tangent space $T_F = \{X : u^{\top} X v = 0 \text{ for all } u, v \text{ with } u^{\top} F = 0 \text{ and } F v = 0\}$ has dimension

$$\dim_K T_F = k(n + r - k).$$

For example, the diagonal matrix with diagonal $(1,0)$ has rank one. Its tangent space in the space of $2\times2$ matrices has dimension $3$.
