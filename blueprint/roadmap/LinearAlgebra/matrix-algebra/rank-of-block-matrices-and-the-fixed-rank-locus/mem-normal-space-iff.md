# The normal space is the trace-form orthogonal of the tangent space

Let $K$ be a field and $F$, $Y$ matrices over $K$ of the same finite shape. Then $Y$ lies in the normal space $N_F = \{Y : F^{\top} Y = 0 \text{ and } Y F^{\top} = 0\}$ if and only if $\operatorname{tr}(X^{\top} Y) = 0$ for every $X$ in the tangent space $T_F = \{X : u^{\top} X v = 0 \text{ for all } u, v \text{ with } u^{\top} F = 0 \text{ and } F v = 0\}$. Over $\mathbb{R}$ this says the normal space is the orthogonal complement of the tangent space for the Frobenius inner product.
