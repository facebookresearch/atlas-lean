# Dimension of the normal space to the rank-k locus

Let $K$ be a field and $F$ a matrix over $K$ with $n$ rows and $r$ columns ($n, r$ finite) and rank $k$. Then the normal space $N_F = \{Y : F^{\top} Y = 0 \text{ and } Y F^{\top} = 0\}$ has dimension

$$\dim_K N_F = (n - k)(r - k).$$

(Since $k \le \min(n, r)$ the subtractions are exact.)

For a rank-one $2\times2$ matrix the normal space has dimension $1$.
