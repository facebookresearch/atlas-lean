# Rank additivity for the Schur complement

Let $K$ be a field, $m$, $p$, $q$ finite index sets, and $A \in K^{m \times m}$, $B \in K^{m \times q}$, $C \in K^{p \times m}$, $D \in K^{p \times q}$ with $\det A \ne 0$. Then

$$\operatorname{rank} \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix} = |m| + \operatorname{rank}(D - C A^{-1} B).$$
