# Tangent and normal spaces of the rank locus are complementary

Let $F$ be a real matrix with finite row and column index sets. Then the tangent space $T_F = \{X : u^{\top} X v = 0 \text{ for all } u, v \text{ with } u^{\top} F = 0 \text{ and } F v = 0\}$ and the normal space $N_F = \{Y : F^{\top} Y = 0 \text{ and } Y F^{\top} = 0\}$ are complementary subspaces of the space of real matrices of that shape: $T_F \cap N_F = 0$ and $T_F + N_F$ is everything.
