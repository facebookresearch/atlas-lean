# Local manifold structure of the rank-k matrix locus

Let $F$ be a real $n \times r$ matrix (rows and columns indexed by finite sets of sizes $n$ and $r$) of rank $k$, and put $d = k(n + r - k)$, $c = (n - k)(r - k)$. Then there are a linear isomorphism $e \colon \mathbb{R}^{n \times r} \to \mathbb{R}^{d} \times \mathbb{R}^{c}$, an open set $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{d}$ containing the first component of $e(F)$, a map $g \colon \mathbb{R}^{d} \to \mathbb{R}^{c}$ that is $C^\infty$ on $U$, and a neighbourhood $V$ of $F$ such that

$$V \cap \{\operatorname{rank} M \le k\} = V \cap \{\operatorname{rank} M = k\} = V \cap e^{-1}\{(x, g(x)) : x \in U\}.$$

So near $F$ the rank-$k$ matrices form the graph of a smooth map, a smooth embedded submanifold of dimension $k(n + r - k)$ and codimension $(n - k)(r - k)$. The endpoint cases $k = 0$ and $k = \min(n, r)$ are included.
