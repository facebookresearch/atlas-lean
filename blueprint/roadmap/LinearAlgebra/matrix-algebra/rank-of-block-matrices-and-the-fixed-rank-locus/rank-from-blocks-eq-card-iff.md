# Vanishing of the Schur complement and minimal rank on a block chart

Let $K$ be a field, $m$, $p$, $q$ finite index sets, and $A \in K^{m \times m}$, $B \in K^{m \times q}$, $C \in K^{p \times m}$, $D \in K^{p \times q}$ with $\det A \ne 0$. Then $\begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$ has rank exactly $|m|$ if and only if the Schur complement vanishes: $D - C A^{-1} B = 0$.

The invertible leading block gives a lower bound $|m|$ on the rank. Thus “rank at most $|m|$” yields the same criterion. In terms of the four blocks of a single matrix $M$, its rank is $|m|$ exactly when its bottom-right block satisfies $D=C A^{-1} B$.
