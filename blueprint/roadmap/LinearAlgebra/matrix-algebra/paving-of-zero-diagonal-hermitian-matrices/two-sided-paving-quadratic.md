# Two-sided paving with O(ε⁻²) blocks

The proposition that there is a constant $C \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for every $\varepsilon \in (0, 1)$, every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every Hermitian complex $n \times n$ matrix $A$ with zero diagonal and operator norm $\|A\| \le 1$, there are $r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $r \le C / \varepsilon^2$ and a partition of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ into $r$ blocks $S_0, \dots, S_{r-1}$ (given by a colouring $c \colon \{0, \dots, n-1\} \to \{0, \dots, r-1\}$; blocks may be empty) with

$$\|P_{S_j} A P_{S_j}\| \le \varepsilon \quad \text{for every } j,$$

where $P_S$ is the diagonal $0$/$1$ projection onto the coordinates in $S$ and norms are $\ell^2$ operator norms.
