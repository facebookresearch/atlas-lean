# Two-sided paving with O(ε⁻²) blocks holds

There is a constant $C$ such that for every $\varepsilon \in (0, 1)$, every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every Hermitian complex $n \times n$ matrix $A$ with zero diagonal and $\|A\| \le 1$, the index set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ can be partitioned into at most $C/\varepsilon^2$ blocks $S$ (possibly empty) with $\|P_S A P_S\| \le \varepsilon$ for each block, $P_S$ being the coordinate projection onto $S$ and norms the $\ell^2$ operator norms. This answers Marcus's question (Oberwolfach 2015, Problem 4(4)) on obtaining both sides of the paving bound with $O(\varepsilon^{-2})$ blocks; Ravichandran and Srivastava pave $k$ zero-diagonal Hermitian contractions simultaneously with at most $18 k \varepsilon^{-2}$ blocks with top eigenvalue below $\varepsilon$ on each block, and applying this to $A$ and $-A$ gives the two-sided bound.

- M. Ravichandran and N. Srivastava, Asymptotically optimal multi-paving, *Int. Math. Res. Not. IMRN* (2021), arXiv:1706.03737, Theorem 1.
