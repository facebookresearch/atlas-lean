# Families with a uniform Gram matrix are linearly independent

Let $v_1,\dots,v_m\in K^\kappa$ over a field $K$ have Gram matrix $(d-c)I+cJ$ (inner products $d$ on the diagonal and $c$ off it). If $d\neq c$ and $d-c+mc\neq0$, then $v_1,\dots,v_m$ are linearly independent; hence $m\le|\kappa|$. This underlies the oddtown theorem and Fisher's inequality.

## Proof sketch

The Gram matrix has eigenvalues $d-c$ (multiplicity $m-1$) and $d-c+mc$, so it is nonsingular, and the rank of a Gram matrix is at most the rank of the family.
