# Block rank and cut rank of a matrix

For a matrix $M$ over a field indexed by $V\times V$ and sets $S,T\subseteq V$, the *block rank* is the rank of the submatrix with rows $S$ and columns $T$. The *cut rank* of $S$ is the block rank of $(S,V\setminus S)$; it is symmetric ($S$ and $V\setminus S$ have the same cut rank) when $M$ is symmetric, and submodular as a function of $S$.
