# Tyaglov's sign pattern is spectrally arbitrary

For every $n \ge 2$ and every monic real polynomial $q$ of degree $n$ there is a real $n \times n$ matrix $A$ with $\det(X I - A) = q$ whose entries are positive on the superdiagonal and in position $(0, 0)$, negative on the subdiagonal and in position $(n-1, n-1)$, and zero elsewhere (indices $0, \dots, n-1$). This answers Tyaglov's question (AimPL, Stability and hyperbolicity, Problem 2.4): the pattern is the negative transpose of the pattern $T_n$, which Garnett and Shader proved spectrally arbitrary for every $n \ge 2$, and spectral arbitrariness is preserved by negation and transposition (Drew, Johnson, Olesky and van den Driessche).
