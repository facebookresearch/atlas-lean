# Zeroth circulant eigenvalue is the sum of entries

Let $n \ge 1$ and $v : \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$. The
[Fourier eigenvalue](circulant-eigenvalue.md) of the circulant matrix of $v$ at $j = 0$ is the sum
of the entries of $v$: $\lambda_0(v) = \sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}} v(k)$.
