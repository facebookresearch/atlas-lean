# Exponential-sum formula for circulant eigenvalues

Let $n \ge 1$, $\zeta_n = e^{2\pi i/n}$ and $v : \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$. For every
$j \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, the [Fourier eigenvalue](circulant-eigenvalue.md) of the circulant
matrix of $v$ is

$$\lambda_j(v) = \sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}} v(k)\, \zeta_n^{-kj},$$

where $k$ and $j$ are read as integers in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$.
