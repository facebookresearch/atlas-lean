# Two-dimensional block-circulant matrix on the torus

Let $L$ and $d$ be natural numbers and let
$v : (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2 \to \mathbb{C}^{d \times d}$. The two-dimensional block-circulant
matrix of $v$ is the complex matrix with rows and columns indexed by
$(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2 \times \{0, \dots, d-1\}$ whose $(p, q)$ block is $v(p - q)$, the
difference being taken coordinatewise in $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$: its entry in row $(p, a)$
and column $(q, b)$ is $v(p - q)_{ab}$.
