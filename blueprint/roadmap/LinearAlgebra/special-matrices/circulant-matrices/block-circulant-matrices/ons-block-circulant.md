# Block-circulant matrix over ℤ_n

Let $n$ and $d$ be natural numbers and let $v : \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}^{d \times d}$
assign a complex $d \times d$ block to each residue. The block-circulant matrix of $v$ is the
complex matrix with rows and columns indexed by $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \times \{0, \dots, d-1\}$
whose $(p, q)$ block is $v(p - q)$: its entry in row $(p, a)$ and column $(q, b)$ is
$v(p - q)_{ab}$.
