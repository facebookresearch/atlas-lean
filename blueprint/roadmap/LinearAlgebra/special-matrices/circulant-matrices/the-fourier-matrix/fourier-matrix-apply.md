# Entrywise exponential formula for the Fourier matrix

Let $n \ge 1$ and $\zeta_n = e^{2\pi i/n}$. For all $k, j \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, the entry of
the [Fourier matrix](fourier-matrix.md) $F_n$ in row $k$ and column $j$ is $\zeta_n^{kj}$, where
$k$ and $j$ are read as integers in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$.
