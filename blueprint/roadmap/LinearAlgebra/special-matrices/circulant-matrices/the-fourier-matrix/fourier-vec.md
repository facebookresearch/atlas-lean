# Fourier vector on the integers modulo n

Let $n \ge 1$, let $\psi_n(k) = e^{2\pi i k/n}$ be the standard additive character of
$\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, and let $j \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$. The $j$-th Fourier vector is the
function $f_j : \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$, $f_j(k) = \psi_n(kj)$, the product $kj$
being taken in $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$.
