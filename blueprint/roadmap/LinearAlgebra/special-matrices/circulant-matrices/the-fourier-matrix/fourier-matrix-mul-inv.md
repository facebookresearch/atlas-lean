# Fourier matrix times its inverse is the identity

Let $n \ge 1$ and $\zeta_n = e^{2\pi i/n}$. The product of the [Fourier matrix](fourier-matrix.md)
$F_n = (\zeta_n^{kj})_{k,j}$ and the [inverse Fourier matrix](fourier-matrix-inv.md)
$\bigl(\frac{1}{n} \zeta_n^{-kj}\bigr)_{k,j}$, in this order, is the $n \times n$ identity matrix.
In particular $F_n$ is invertible.
