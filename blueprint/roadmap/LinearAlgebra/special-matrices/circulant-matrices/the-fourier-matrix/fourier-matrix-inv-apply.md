# Entrywise exponential formula for the inverse Fourier matrix

Let $n \ge 1$ and $\zeta_n = e^{2\pi i/n}$. For all $k, j \in \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, the entry of
the [inverse Fourier matrix](fourier-matrix-inv.md) in row $k$ and column $j$ is
$\frac{1}{n} \zeta_n^{-kj}$, where $k$ and $j$ are read as integers in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$.
