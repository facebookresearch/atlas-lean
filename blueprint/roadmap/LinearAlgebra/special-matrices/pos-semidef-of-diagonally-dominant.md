# Weak diagonal dominance and positive semidefiniteness

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and let $M \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ be symmetric. Suppose that for every $i \in \iota$ the diagonal entry dominates the absolute off-diagonal row sum,

$$\sum_{j \ne i} \lvert M_{ij} \rvert \le M_{ii},$$

with equality allowed. Then $M$ is positive semidefinite: $x^{\mathsf{T}} M x \ge 0$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$.
