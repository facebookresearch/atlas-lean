# Norm identity for a generalized Householder matrix

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set, let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and let
$H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$. For every real vector $x$ with the same index set, in the
Euclidean norm,
$\|H x\|_2^2 = \|x\|_2^2 - \beta (2 - \beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v) (v^{\mathsf{T}} x)^2$.
