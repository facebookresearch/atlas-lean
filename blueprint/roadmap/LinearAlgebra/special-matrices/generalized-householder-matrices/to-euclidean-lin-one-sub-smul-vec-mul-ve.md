# A generalized Householder matrix that is an orthogonal projection

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set, let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ with
$\beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v = 1$ and let $H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$. Acting on the Euclidean
space of the same index set, $H$ is the orthogonal projection onto the orthogonal complement
$v^{\perp}$ of $v$.
