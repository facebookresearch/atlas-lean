# Characteristic polynomial of a generalized Householder matrix

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set with $n \ge 1$ elements, let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$
and let $H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$. The characteristic polynomial of $H$ is
$(X - 1)^{n - 1} \bigl(X - (1 - \beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v)\bigr)$.
