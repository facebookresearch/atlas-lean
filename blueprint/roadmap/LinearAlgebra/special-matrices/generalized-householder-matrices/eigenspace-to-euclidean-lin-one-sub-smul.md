# Fixed subspace of a generalized Householder matrix

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set, let $\beta \ne 0$ and let
$H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$, acting on the Euclidean space of the same index set. The
eigenspace of $H$ at the scalar $1$, that is the subspace of vectors fixed by $H$, is the
orthogonal complement $v^{\perp}$ of $v$. With one index and $v \ne 0$ it is zero, and $1$ is
then not an eigenvalue of $H$.
