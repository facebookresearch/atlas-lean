# Eigenspace of a generalized Householder matrix for its other eigenvalue

Let $v \ne 0$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set, let $\beta \ne 0$ and let
$H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$, acting on the Euclidean space of the same index set. The
eigenspace of $H$ for the eigenvalue $1 - \beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v$ is the line spanned by $v$.
