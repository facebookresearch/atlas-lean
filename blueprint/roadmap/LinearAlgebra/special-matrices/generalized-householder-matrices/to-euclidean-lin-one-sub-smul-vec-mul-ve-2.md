# A generalized Householder matrix that is a reflection

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set, let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ with
$\beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v = 2$ and let $H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$. Acting on the Euclidean
space of the same index set, $H$ is the reflection across the orthogonal complement $v^{\perp}$ of
$v$ (the Householder reflection).
