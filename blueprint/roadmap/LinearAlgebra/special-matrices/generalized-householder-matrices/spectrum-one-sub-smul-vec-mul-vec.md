# Spectrum of a generalized Householder matrix

Let $v$ be a real vector indexed by a finite set with $n \ge 2$ elements, let
$\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $H = I - \beta v v^{\mathsf{T}}$. The real spectrum of $H$ is
$\{1, 1 - \beta\, v^{\mathsf{T}} v\}$.

## Sources

- R. Grazzi, J. Siems, A. Zela, J. K. H. Franke, F. Hutter and M. Pontil, "Unlocking State-Tracking in Linear RNNs Through Negative Eigenvalues", ICLR 2025, arXiv:2411.12537 (version 5), Section 4.2, for a unit vector $v$ and $\beta \in [0, 2]$.
