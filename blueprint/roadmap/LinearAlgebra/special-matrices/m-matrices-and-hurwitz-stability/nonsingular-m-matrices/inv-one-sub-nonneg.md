# Neumann series

Let $K$ be a real square matrix, with rows and columns indexed by a finite set, whose entries are
all nonnegative and whose spectral radius, as a complex matrix, is less than $1$. Then
$(I - K)^{-1}$ has nonnegative entries. The proof identifies $(I - K)^{-1}$ with the limit of the
partial sums $\sum_{n < N} K^n$ of the Neumann series.
