# Is Hurwitz transpose iff

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set. A real matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ is Hurwitz if every complex eigenvalue $\mu$ of $A$ (an element of the spectrum of $A$ viewed as a complex matrix) satisfies $\operatorname{Re} \mu < 0$.

For every $A \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$, $A^{\mathsf{T}}$ is Hurwitz if and only if $A$ is Hurwitz.
