# Collatz–Wielandt lower bound

Let $K$ be a real square matrix, with rows and columns indexed by a finite set, whose entries are
all nonnegative. If $z$ is a nonzero vector with nonnegative entries and $r \ge 0$ is a real number
with $r z \le K z$ entrywise, then $r$ is at most the spectral radius of $K$, viewed as a complex
matrix.
