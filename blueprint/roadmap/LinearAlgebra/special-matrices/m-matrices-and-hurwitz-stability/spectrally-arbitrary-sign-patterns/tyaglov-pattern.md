# Tyaglov's tridiagonal sign pattern

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the sign pattern $S_n$ is the $n \times n$ matrix of signs (indices $0, \dots, n-1$) with $+$ on the superdiagonal (positions $(i, i+1)$), $-$ on the subdiagonal (positions $(i+1, i)$), $+$ in position $(0, 0)$, $-$ in position $(n-1, n-1)$, and $0$ elsewhere. For $n \ge 2$ the two diagonal corners are distinct; for $n = 1$ the single entry is $+$ (the $(0, 0)$ rule is applied first). It is the negative transpose of the tridiagonal pattern $T_n$ of Drew, Johnson, Olesky and van den Driessche.
