# AimPL total positivity Problem 1.55 (converse of the Barrett–Johnson inequalities)

This is the proposition that for all $n, r \ge 1$ and all nonincreasing sequences $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_r)$, $\mu = (\mu_1, \dots, \mu_r)$ of nonnegative integers with sum $n$, if $F_\mu(A) \le F_\lambda(A)$ for every totally nonnegative real $n \times n$ matrix $A$ (every minor with increasing rows and columns is $\ge 0$), then $\mu$ majorizes $\lambda$: $\lambda_1 + \dots + \lambda_i \le \mu_1 + \dots + \mu_i$ for every $i$. Here $F_\lambda(A) = \lambda_1! \cdots \lambda_r! \sum_{I} \prod_{k=1}^{r} \det A[I_k]$, the sum over tuples $(I_1, \dots, I_r)$ of disjoint, possibly empty sets covering $\{1, \dots, n\}$ with $|I_k| = \lambda_k$, $A[I]$ the principal submatrix on $I$ and the determinant of an empty submatrix equal to $1$.

Source: AimPL, *Theory and applications of total positivity*, Problem 1.55 (A. Khare), http://aimpl.org/totalpos/1/.
