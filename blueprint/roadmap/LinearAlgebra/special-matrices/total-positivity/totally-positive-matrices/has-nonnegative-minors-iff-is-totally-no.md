# Nonnegative minors and total nonnegativity of square matrices

For a square real matrix indexed by a linearly ordered set, possibly infinite, [having nonnegative minors](has-nonnegative-minors.md) is the same as being totally nonnegative: both say that every finite square submatrix with strictly increasing row and column indices has nonnegative determinant (Fomin and
Zelevinsky, *Total positivity: tests and parametrizations*, Math. Intelligencer 22 (2000),
arXiv:math/9912128v1, Introduction: a matrix is totally nonnegative if all its minors are
nonnegative).

## Background

- [Matrices with nonnegative minors](has-nonnegative-minors.md): the same condition for rectangular matrices; total nonnegativity of square matrices also makes sense over any ordered commutative ring.
