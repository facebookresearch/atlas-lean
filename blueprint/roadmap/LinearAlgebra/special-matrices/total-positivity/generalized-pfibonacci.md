# Generalized $p$-Fibonacci sequence

For integers $p$, $a$ and $b$, the generalized
Fibonacci sequence $(W_{p,n})_{n \ge 0}$ of integers is defined by $W_{p,0} = a$, $W_{p,1} = b$
and $W_{p,n} = p W_{p,n-1} + W_{p,n-2}$ for $n \ge 2$.
