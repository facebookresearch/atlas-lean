# Symmetrized product of principal minors over set partitions

For a real $n \times n$ matrix $A$ and a sequence $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_r)$ of natural numbers, the number $F_\lambda(A) = \lambda_1! \cdots \lambda_r! \sum_{I} \prod_{k=1}^{r} \det A[I_k]$, the sum over tuples $(I_1, \dots, I_r)$ of disjoint, possibly empty sets covering $\{1, \dots, n\}$ with $|I_k| = \lambda_k$, $A[I]$ the principal submatrix on $I$ and the determinant of an empty submatrix equal to $1$ (equivalently, over maps $f \colon \{1, \dots, n\} \to \{1, \dots, r\}$ with $|f^{-1}(k)| = \lambda_k$). It is $0$ when $\sum_k \lambda_k \ne n$.

Source: AimPL, *Theory and applications of total positivity*, Problem 1.55 (A. Khare), http://aimpl.org/totalpos/1/.
