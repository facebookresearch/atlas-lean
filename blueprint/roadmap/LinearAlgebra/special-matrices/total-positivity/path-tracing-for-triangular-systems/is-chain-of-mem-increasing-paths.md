# Increasing paths of nonzero weight are directed paths

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $R$ a monoid with zero, $B$ an $R$-valued matrix indexed by $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, and $p$ one of the increasing paths from $j$ to $i$ with nonzero weight $B_{v_1 v_0} \cdots B_{v_k v_{k-1}} \ne 0$. Then $B_{vu} \ne 0$ for every pair of consecutive nodes $u, v$ of $p$, the first node of $p$ is $j$ and its last node is $i$.
