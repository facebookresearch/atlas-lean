# Csima–Datta theorem

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $A$ be a real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix with nonnegative entries. Then
there is a vector $d \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with positive entries such that
$\operatorname{diag}(d)\, A \operatorname{diag}(d)$ is doubly stochastic if and only if $A$ has total
support.
