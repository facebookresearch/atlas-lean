# Weighted permutation averages and four-row power means

On $I=\{0,1,2,3\}$ let $\nu$ be a nonnegative mass function on permutations with total one. The uniform law has mass $1/24$ per permutation. The TV distance is half the sum of the absolute mass differences. Uniform coordinate marginals mean $\sum_{\pi:\pi(i)=j}\nu(\pi)=1/4$ for each $i,j$. For a nonnegative four-by-four matrix, its weighted permutation average is $\sum_\pi\nu(\pi)\prod_i f(i,\pi(i))$ and the normalized $L^p$ row means are $(\tfrac14\sum_j f(i,j)^p)^{1/p}$. Only for uniform $\nu$ is this average the ordinary permanent divided by $24$.
