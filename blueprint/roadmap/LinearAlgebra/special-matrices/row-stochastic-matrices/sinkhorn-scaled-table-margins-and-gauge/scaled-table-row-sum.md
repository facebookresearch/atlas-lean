# Scaled Table row sum

Let $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $a \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $K \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $b \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $r \in \mathbb{R}^m$, and let $T_{ij} = a_i K_{ij} b_j$ be the scaled table. If for every $i$

$$a_i \sum_j K_{ij} b_j = r_i,$$

then $T$ has row sums $r$: $\sum_j T_{ij} = r_i$ for every $i$. No positivity or nonvanishing of $a_i$ is assumed.
