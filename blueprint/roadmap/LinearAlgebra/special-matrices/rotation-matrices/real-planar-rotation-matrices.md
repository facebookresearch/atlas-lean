# Real planar rotation matrices

For $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$, the rotation matrix through the angle $\theta$ is
$R(\theta) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}
\in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}$.
