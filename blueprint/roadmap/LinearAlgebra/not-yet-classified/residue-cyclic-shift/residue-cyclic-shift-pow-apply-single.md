q⁻¹x_r at block0 and x_j to block j+1 for j<r. For a∈lˣ let P_a multiply every block by a, and
P_(a,q,m)=T_q^m∘P_a. For e=r+1 and i∈Fin e, T_q^m(δ_i x)=δ_((i+m) mod e)((q⁻¹)^((i+m) div
e)*x).
