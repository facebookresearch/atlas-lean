# Load factor equality from a complementary certificate

Let $B$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix, $\sigma, y \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $f, v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. Suppose $B^\top \sigma = \lambda f$ (equilibrium), $|\sigma_e| \le y_e$ for every $e$ (yield), $f \cdot v = 1$ (normalization), and $y_e |(B v)_e| = \sigma_e (B v)_e$ for every $e$ (stress aligned with the mechanism strain). Then $\lambda = \sum_e y_e |(B v)_e|$.
