# Scalar Schur complement of a positive-definite 2 × 2 matrix

Let $a, b, d \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the $2 \times 2$ matrix $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ b & d \end{pmatrix}$, viewed as a block matrix with $1 \times 1$ blocks, is positive definite. Then the $1 \times 1$ Schur complement $\begin{pmatrix} a - b\,d^{-1}\,b \end{pmatrix}$ is positive definite.
