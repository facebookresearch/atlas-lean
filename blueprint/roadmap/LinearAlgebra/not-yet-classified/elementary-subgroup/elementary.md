# Elementary generator as an element of its subgroup

Let R be a ring and n a finite index type. Write GL(n,R) for the group of invertible n×n
matrices, and E_ij(a)=I+a*e_ij for i≠j, where e_ij is the single-entry matrix. Let
E(n,R)=elementarySubgroup n R be the subgroup generated by all transvections E_ij(a), i≠j and
a∈R. The element ElementarySubgroup.elementary i j hij a is E_ij(a), viewed in E(n,R).
