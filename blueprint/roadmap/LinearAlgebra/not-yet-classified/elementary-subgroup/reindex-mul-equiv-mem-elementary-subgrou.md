# Index reindexing preserves the elementary subgroup

matrices, and E_ij(a)=I+a*e_ij for i≠j, where e_ij is the single-entry matrix. Let
E(n,R)=elementarySubgroup n R be the subgroup generated by all transvections E_ij(a), i≠j and
a∈R. For a finite index type m, an equivalence e:n≃m and g∈E(n,R), reindexMulEquiv e g belongs
to E(m,R). Reindexing sends E_ij(a) to E_(e(i),e(j))(a).
