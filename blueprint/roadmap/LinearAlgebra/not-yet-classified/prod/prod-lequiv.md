# Linear equivalence splitting product fibers

dependent finitely supported vectors. The equivalence Φ:⊕i(M_i×N_i)≃ₗ[R]&#40;⊕iM_i)×(⊕iN_i) splits
coordinates, with inverse (m,n)↦(i↦(m_i,n_i)). The map prodLEquiv is Φ.
