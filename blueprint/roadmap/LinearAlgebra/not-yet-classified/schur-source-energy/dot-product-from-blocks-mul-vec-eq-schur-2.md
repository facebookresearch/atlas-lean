# Schur energy identity with a source term

Let $m$ and $p$ be finite index sets, $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times p}$ and $D \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times p}$ invertible, and suppose

$$G = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\mathsf T} & D \end{pmatrix}$$

is symmetric. Let $K = A - B D^{-1} B^{\mathsf T}$. If $\varphi_P \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $\varphi_I, j_I \in \mathbb{R}^p$ satisfy $B^{\mathsf T}\varphi_P + D\varphi_I = j_I$, then $\varphi = (\varphi_P, \varphi_I)$ satisfies

$$\varphi^{\mathsf T} G \varphi = \varphi_P^{\mathsf T} K \varphi_P + j_I^{\mathsf T} D^{-1} j_I.$$
