# Descent of a shrunk subspace

Let K be a field, I,J,D finite with D nonempty, B ⊆ Mat(I,J;K), and S ⊆ K^(J × D). Put B^[D] =
span{A ⊗ T : A ∈ B, T ∈ Mat(D,D;K)} and assume dim B^[D]&#40;S) + s ≤ dim S for s ∈ ℕ. Let U be
the span of projected columns of S, W = B(U), and δ = dim U − dim W. Then dim W + δ = dim U
and s ≤ δ|D|.
