# The image of a subspace under a blow-up

Let R be a commutative semiring. Let J,D be finite, B ⊆ Mat(I,J;R), and S ⊆ R^(J × D). Define
B(U) = span{Ax : A ∈ B, x ∈ U}, B^[D] = span{A ⊗ T : A ∈ B, T ∈ Mat(D,D;R)}, and P(S) as the
span of columns of S. Then B^[D]&#40;S) = B(P(S))^D, where amplification places a vector of
B(P(S)) in each column.
