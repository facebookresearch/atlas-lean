# Borel σ-algebra of a countable product of second-countable spaces

Let $\iota$ be a countable set and, for each $i \in \iota$, let $X_i$ be a second countable topological space with its
Borel σ-algebra. Then on the product $\prod_{i \in \iota} X_i$ the product σ-algebra, the smallest σ-algebra for which
every coordinate projection is measurable, is the Borel σ-algebra of the product topology.
