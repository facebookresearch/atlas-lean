# Pointwise limits of measurable functions are measurable

Let $\alpha$ be a set with a σ-algebra and let $\beta$ be a pseudometrizable topological
space with its Borel σ-algebra, such as $\mathbb{R}$, $[-\infty, \infty]$ or $\mathbb{C}$. If
$f_0, f_1, \ldots \colon \alpha \to \beta$ are measurable and $f_n(x) \to g(x)$ for every $x \in \alpha$, then
$g \colon \alpha \to \beta$ is measurable.
