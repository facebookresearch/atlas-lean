# σ-algebra

Let $\alpha$ be a set. A σ-algebra on $\alpha$, or measurable space structure, is a family of
subsets of $\alpha$, called measurable sets, such that $\emptyset$ is measurable, the complement of a measurable set
is measurable, and the union $\bigcup_n s_n$ of a sequence $s_0, s_1, \ldots$ of measurable sets is measurable.
