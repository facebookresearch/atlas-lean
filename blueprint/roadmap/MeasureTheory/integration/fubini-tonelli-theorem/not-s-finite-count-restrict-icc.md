# Counting measure on [0, 1] is not s-finite

Counting measure on $[0, 1]$ is not s-finite (not a countable sum of finite measures), and in
particular not $\sigma$-finite. Otherwise Tonelli's theorem would apply to the measurable indicator
of the diagonal, whose two iterated integrals are $1$ and $0$.
