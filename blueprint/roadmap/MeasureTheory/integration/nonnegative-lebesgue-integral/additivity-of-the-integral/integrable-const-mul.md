# A constant multiple of an integrable function is integrable

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $\mathbb{K}$ be a normed ring, such as $\mathbb{R}$ or
$\mathbb{C}$, and let $f \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{K}$ be $\mu$-integrable: $\mu$-almost everywhere strongly measurable,
with $\int_\Omega \|f\| \, d\mu < \infty$. Then for every $c \in \mathbb{K}$ the function $x \mapsto c \, f(x)$ is
$\mu$-integrable.
