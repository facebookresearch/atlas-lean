# Additivity of the integral of integrable functions

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, and let $G$ be a real normed vector space. For integrable
functions $f, g \colon \alpha \to G$,

$$\int (f + g) \, d\mu = \int f \, d\mu + \int g \, d\mu.$$

Here the integral is the Bochner integral, which for real and complex functions is the Lebesgue integral, and which is
defined to be $0$ when $G$ is not complete.
