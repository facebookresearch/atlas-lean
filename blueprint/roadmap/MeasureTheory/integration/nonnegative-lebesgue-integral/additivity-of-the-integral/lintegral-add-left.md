# Additivity of the integral when one function is measurable

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $f \colon \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ be measurable and let
$g \colon \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ be any function. Then, in $[0, \infty]$,

$$
\int_\Omega (f + g) \, d\mu = \int_\Omega f \, d\mu + \int_\Omega g \, d\mu,
$$

where the integrals are integrals of functions with values in $[0, \infty]$ and $a + \infty = \infty$.
