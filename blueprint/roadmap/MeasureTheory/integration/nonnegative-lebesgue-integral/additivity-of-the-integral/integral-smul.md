# Homogeneity of the integral

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $\mathbb{K}$ be a normed division ring, such as
$\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $G$ be a real normed vector space that is also a $\mathbb{K}$-module, with
$\|c v\| = \|c\| \|v\|$ for $c \in \mathbb{K}$ and $v \in G$ and with the actions of $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{K}$
commuting. For every $c \in \mathbb{K}$ and every function $f \colon \alpha \to G$,

$$\int c f \, d\mu = c \int f \, d\mu,$$

where the integral is the Bochner integral, which is $0$ for a function that is not integrable and for every function
when $G$ is not complete.
