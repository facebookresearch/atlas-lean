# Integral of a constant on a set

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $B \subseteq \Omega$ be measurable and let
$c \in [0, \infty]$. Let $c \, \mathbf{1}_B \colon \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ be the function equal to $c$ on $B$ and to
$0$ elsewhere. Then, in $[0, \infty]$,

$$
\int_\Omega c \, \mathbf{1}_B \, d\mu = c \, \mu(B),
$$

with $0 \cdot \infty = 0$.
