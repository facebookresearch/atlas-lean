# Monotonicity of the integral

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra. For functions
$f, g \colon \alpha \to [0, \infty]$ with $f(x) \le g(x)$ for every $x \in \alpha$,

$$\int f \, d\mu \le \int g \, d\mu.$$

Here the integral of a function $h \colon \alpha \to [0, \infty]$, measurable or not, is the supremum of
$\sum_j c_j \mu(A_j)$ over the simple functions $\sum_j c_j \chi_{A_j} \le h$ with $A_j$ disjoint measurable sets and
$c_j \in [0, \infty]$, so no measurability is assumed.
