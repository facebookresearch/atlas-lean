# Constant functions are integrable on a finite measure space

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $E$ be a normed additive commutative group, such
as $\mathbb{R}$. Then for every $c \in E$ the constant function $\Omega \to E$ with value $c$ is $\mu$-integrable: it is
$\mu$-almost everywhere strongly measurable and the integral of its norm is finite.
