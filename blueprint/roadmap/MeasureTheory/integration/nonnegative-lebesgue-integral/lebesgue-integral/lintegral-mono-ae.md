# Monotonicity of the integral almost everywhere

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $f, g \colon \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ be functions with
$f(x) \le g(x)$ for $\mu$-almost every $x \in \Omega$. Then, in $[0, \infty]$,

$$
\int_\Omega f \, d\mu \le \int_\Omega g \, d\mu.
$$

No measurability is assumed.
