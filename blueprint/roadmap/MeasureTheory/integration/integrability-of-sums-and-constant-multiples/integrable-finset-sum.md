# Integrability of a finite pointwise sum

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, and let $E$ be a
topological extended seminormed additive commutative monoid with continuous
addition. Let $I$ be an index type, $S\subseteq I$ a finite set, and
$(f_i:\Omega\to E)_{i\in I}$ a family of functions. If $f_i$ is
$\mu$-integrable for every $i\in S$, then the pointwise sum
$\omega\mapsto\sum_{i\in S} f_i(\omega)$ is $\mu$-integrable. The set $S$
may be empty.
