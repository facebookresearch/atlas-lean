# Borel sets are Carathéodory measurable for a metric outer measure

Let $X$ be an extended metric space and let $\mu$ be a metric outer measure on $X$. Then the
Borel σ-algebra of $X$ is contained in the σ-algebra of Carathéodory measurable sets of $\mu$: every Borel set
$B \subseteq X$ satisfies $\mu(E) = \mu(E \cap B) + \mu(E \setminus B)$ for every $E \subseteq X$.
