# Prekopa Leindler

Prékopa–Leindler: for `0 < t < 1` and nonnegative measurable `h((1-t)x+ty) ≥ f(x)^(1-t) g(y)^t` then
`(∫⁻ f)^(1-t)(∫⁻ g)^t ≤ ∫⁻ h`.
