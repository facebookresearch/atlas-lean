# Area of the unit square

The closed unit square $\{x \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 0 \le x_1 \le 1 \text{ and } 0 \le x_2 \le 1\}$ in the
Euclidean plane has area $1$.

## Idea

Area in the Euclidean plane is Lebesgue measure, normalised by this square: the area of a rectangle
is then the product of its side lengths, and areas of other regions are limits of areas of finite
unions of small squares.
