# Volume of the coordinate cube

For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the coordinate cube $[-1, 1]^n$ in the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$
has volume $2^n$.

## Idea

Lebesgue measure gives a box the product of its side lengths, and $[-1, 1]^n$ is a box with $n$
sides of length $2$. For $n = 0$ the space is a single point, of measure $1 = 2^0$.
