# Uniqueness of the Jordan decomposition

Let $\alpha$ be a set with a σ-algebra. Call a pair $(\mu, \nu)$ of finite measures on
$\alpha$ that are mutually singular a Jordan decomposition, with signed measure $\mu - \nu$. If two Jordan
decompositions $(\mu_1, \nu_1)$ and $(\mu_2, \nu_2)$ have the same signed measure, $\mu_1 - \nu_1 = \mu_2 - \nu_2$,
then $\mu_1 = \mu_2$ and $\nu_1 = \nu_2$.
