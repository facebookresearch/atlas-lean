# Absolute continuity of a vector measure with respect to another

Let $\alpha$ be a set with a σ-algebra and let $v$ and $w$ be vector measures on $\alpha$ with
values in additive commutative monoids $M$ and $N$ carrying topologies: countably additive functions on the measurable
sets, extended by $0$ to the sets that are not measurable. Then $v$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $w$,
written $v \ll w$, if $v(s) = 0$ for every subset $s$ of $\alpha$ with $w(s) = 0$.
