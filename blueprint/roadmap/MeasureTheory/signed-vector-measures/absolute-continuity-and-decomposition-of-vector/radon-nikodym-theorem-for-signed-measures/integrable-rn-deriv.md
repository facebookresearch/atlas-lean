# The Radon–Nikodym derivative of a finite signed measure is integrable

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra and let $s$ be a signed measure on $\alpha$, with real
values. Then the Radon–Nikodym derivative $ds/d\mu \colon \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ of $s$ with respect to $\mu$ is
integrable with respect to $\mu$.
