# Finite measures are determined by integrals of bounded continuous functions

Let $\Omega$ be a topological space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra in which every closed set is approximated from outside by bounded continuous functions (for example any pseudo-metrizable space, such as a metric space), and let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite measures on $\Omega$. If

$$\int_\Omega f\,d\mu=\int_\Omega f\,d\nu\qquad\text{for every bounded continuous }f:\Omega\to\mathbb R,$$

then $\mu=\nu$. When $\Omega$ is a compact metric space, the bounded continuous functions are all of $C(\Omega)$.

## Sources

- P. Billingsley, *Convergence of Probability Measures*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Chapter 1, Section 1.
