# Finite measures on Polish spaces are inner regular

Let $\alpha$ be a topological space that is second countable and completely pseudometrizable, such as a Polish space,
with its Borel σ-algebra, and let $P$ be a finite measure on $\alpha$. Then for every measurable set
$A \subseteq \alpha$,

$$P(A) = \sup\{P(K) : K \subseteq A,\ K \text{ compact and closed}\}.$$
