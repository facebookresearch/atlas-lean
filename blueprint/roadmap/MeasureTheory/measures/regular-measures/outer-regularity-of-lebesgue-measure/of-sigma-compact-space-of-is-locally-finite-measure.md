# Locally finite measures on σ-compact pseudometrizable spaces are regular

Let $X$ be a topological space that is pseudometrizable and σ-compact, that is, a countable union of compact sets,
with its Borel σ-algebra, and let $\mu$ be a locally finite measure on $X$: every point of $X$ has a neighbourhood of
finite measure. Then $\mu$ is regular: $\mu(K) < \infty$ for every compact set $K$; $\mu$ is outer regular, so for
every measurable set $A$ and every $r > \mu(A)$ there is an open set $U \supseteq A$ with $\mu(U) < r$; and for every
open set $U$ and every $r < \mu(U)$ there is a compact set $K \subseteq U$ with $r < \mu(K)$.
