# Mixed moments of a finite measure on the unit interval

For a finite measure $\mu$ on the unit interval $[0, 1]$ and natural numbers $a$ and $b$, the mixed moment of $\mu$ is

$$m_{a,b}(\mu) = \int_{[0,1]} x^a (1 - x)^b \, d\mu(x),$$

with $0^0 = 1$. The integrand lies in $[0, 1]$, so it is integrable for every finite $\mu$. These are the unnormalized Bernstein moments of $\mu$.
