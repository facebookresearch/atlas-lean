# Continuity from below for increasing families of sets

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $\iota$ be a preordered set whose filter of terminal
sections $\{i : i \ge i_0\}$ is countably generated, such as $\mathbb{N}$ or $\mathbb{R}$, and let $(s_i)_{i \in \iota}$
be subsets of $\alpha$, not necessarily measurable, with $s_i \subseteq s_j$ whenever $i \le j$. Then

$$\mu(s_i) \to \mu\Big(\bigcup_i s_i\Big)$$

along this filter, where a set that is not measurable has the measure
$\mu(A) = \inf\{\mu(E) : E \supseteq A \text{ measurable}\}$.
