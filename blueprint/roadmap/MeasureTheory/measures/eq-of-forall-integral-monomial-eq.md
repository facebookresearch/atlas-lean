# Moment determinacy on a compact subset of `ℝ^d`

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $E \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be compact, and let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite Borel measures on $\mathbb{R}^d$ with $\mu(\mathbb{R}^d \setminus E) = \nu(\mathbb{R}^d \setminus E) = 0$. If all mixed moments agree, that is, for every multi-index $\beta \in \mathbb{N}^d$

$$\int \prod_{i=1}^{d} x_i^{\beta_i} \, d\mu(x) = \int \prod_{i=1}^{d} x_i^{\beta_i} \, d\nu(x),$$

then $\mu = \nu$. The moment with $\beta = 0$ is the total mass.
