# Uniqueness of regular left-invariant measures up to a scalar

Let $G$ be a locally compact topological group with its Borel σ-algebra. Let $\mu$ be a
[Haar measure](../is-haar-measure.md) on $G$ and $\mu'$ a left-invariant measure on $G$, and suppose both are
[regular](../../measures/regular-measures/radon-measures/regular.md): finite on compact sets, outer regular, and
inner regular on open sets with respect to compact sets. Then $\mu' = c\,\mu$ for some real number $c \ge 0$.
