# Measurable double Coset Cutoff

Let $G$ be a topological group with its Borel σ-algebra, $U \subseteq G$ open, $T \le G$ a subgroup with a measure $\tau$, and $S \subseteq G$ finite. Then the double-coset cutoff

$$\phi(y) = \sum_{s \in S} \mathbf{1}_{sU}(y)\, \tau\bigl(\{ t \in T : s^{-1} t s \in U \}\bigr)^{-1}$$

(with the mass read as a real number, $\infty \mapsto 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$) is a measurable function $G \to \mathbb{R}$.
