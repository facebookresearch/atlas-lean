# Double Coset Cutoff nonneg

Let $G$ be a group, $U \subseteq G$, $T \le G$ a subgroup with a measure $\tau$, and $S \subseteq G$ finite. The double-coset cutoff is

$$\phi(y) = \sum_{s \in S} \mathbf{1}_{sU}(y)\, m_s^{-1}, \qquad m_s = \tau\bigl(\{ t \in T : s^{-1} t s \in U \}\bigr),$$

where $m_s$ is taken as a real number with the conventions $\infty \mapsto 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$. Then $\phi(y) \ge 0$ for every $y \in G$, with no positivity or finiteness assumption on the masses $m_s$.
