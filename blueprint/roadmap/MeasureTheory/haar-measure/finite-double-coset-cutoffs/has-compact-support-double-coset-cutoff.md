# Has Compact Support double Coset Cutoff

Let $G$ be a topological group, $U \subseteq G$ compact, $T \le G$ a subgroup with a measure $\tau$, and $S \subseteq G$ finite. Then the double-coset cutoff

$$\phi(y) = \sum_{s \in S} \mathbf{1}_{sU}(y)\, \tau\bigl(\{ t \in T : s^{-1} t s \in U \}\bigr)^{-1}$$

(with the mass read as a real number, $\infty \mapsto 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$) has compact support: the cutoff vanishes outside the compact set $\bigcup_{s \in S} sU$.
