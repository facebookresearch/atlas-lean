# Translation-invariant measures on a product are Haar times a σ-finite measure

Let $G$ be a second-countable locally compact additive topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and an additive Haar measure $\mu$, and let $Y$ be a measurable space. Let $\rho$ be a measure on $G \times Y$ invariant under every translation $(g, y) \mapsto (a + g, y)$, and suppose there are measurable sets $B_0, B_1, \dots$ covering $Y$ with $\rho(K \times B_n) < \infty$ for every compact $K \subseteq G$ and every $n$. Then $\rho = \mu \otimes \sigma$ for some $\sigma$-finite measure $\sigma$ on $Y$.
