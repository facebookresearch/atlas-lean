# Continuous cutoff for a closed-embedding homomorphism

Let $G$ be a locally compact topological group, $H$ a topological group with its Borel
$\sigma$-algebra and a right-invariant measure $\tau$ that is finite on compact sets and positive on
nonempty open sets, and $\iota : H \to G$ a continuous homomorphism that is a closed embedding. Then
for every compact $K \subseteq G$ there is a continuous, nonnegative, compactly supported
$w : G \to \mathbb{R}$ with

$$\int_H w\big(\iota(h')\, \iota(h)\, k\big)\, d\tau(h') = 1 \qquad \text{for all } h \in H,\ k \in K,$$

so $w$ is normalized on every left $\iota(H)$-orbit meeting $K$. The measure $\tau$ may have any
scale, and no Hausdorff or countability hypotheses are needed.
