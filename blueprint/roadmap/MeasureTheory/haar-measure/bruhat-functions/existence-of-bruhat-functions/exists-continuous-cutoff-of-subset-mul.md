# Continuous Bruhat cutoff for a closed subgroup

Let $G$ be a locally compact topological group, $T \le G$ a closed subgroup with its Borel
$\sigma$-algebra and a right-invariant measure $\tau$ that is finite on compact sets and positive on
nonempty open sets, $K \subseteq G$ compact and $E \subseteq TK$. Then there is a continuous,
nonnegative, compactly supported $w : G \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\int_T w(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ for every
$x \in E$.
