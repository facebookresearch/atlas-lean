# Bounded invariant functions are integrable against normalized cutoffs

Let $G$ be a locally compact, second-countable topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra
and a Haar measure $\mu$, and $T \le G$ a closed subgroup with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and an
inversion-invariant Haar measure $\tau$. Let $F : G \to \mathbb{C}$ be bounded, measurable and
$T$-invariant ($F(tx) = F(x)$), and $w : G \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative, measurable and compactly
supported with $\int_T w(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ whenever $F(x) \ne 0$. Then $F w$ is
$\mu$-integrable. The weight need not be bounded.
