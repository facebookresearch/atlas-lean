# Subgroup-normalized weights give equal integrals

Let $G$ be a group with a $\sigma$-algebra for which multiplication $G \times G \to G$ is measurable,
and $\mu$ an s-finite left-invariant measure on $G$. Let $T \le G$ be a subgroup with a
$\sigma$-algebra for which inversion and the inclusion $T \to G$ are measurable, and $\tau$ an
s-finite inversion-invariant measure on $T$. Let $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$ be measurable and
$T$-invariant ($f(tx) = f(x)$), and $w_1, w_2 : G \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative and measurable with
$\int_T w_i(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ whenever $f(x) \ne 0$. Then
$\int_G f w_1\, d\mu = \int_G f w_2\, d\mu$ (Bochner integrals, $0$ for non-integrable functions).
No closedness of $T$, compact support or boundedness is needed.
