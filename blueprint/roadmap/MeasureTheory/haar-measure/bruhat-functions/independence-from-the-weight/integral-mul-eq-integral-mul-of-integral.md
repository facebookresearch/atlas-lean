# Cutoff independence of integrals of bounded invariant functions

Let $G$ be a locally compact, second-countable topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra
and a Haar measure $\mu$, and $T \le G$ a closed subgroup with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and an
inversion-invariant Haar measure $\tau$. Let $F : G \to \mathbb{C}$ be bounded, measurable and
$T$-invariant, and let $w_1, w_2 : G \to \mathbb{R}$ be nonnegative, measurable and compactly
supported with $\int_T w_i(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ whenever $F(x) \ne 0$. Then

$$\int_G F\, w_1\, d\mu = \int_G F\, w_2\, d\mu .$$

Normalization is not required outside the support of $F$. [Subgroup-normalized weights give equal
integrals](integral-mul-eq-of-normalized-subgroup.md) needs neither boundedness nor compact support;
the hypotheses here also guarantee integrability ([bounded invariant functions are
integrable](integrable-mul-of-integral-subgroup-tran.md)).
