# Orbit-normalized weights give equal nonnegative integrals

Let a group $A$ with measurable inversion act on a measurable space $X$ with
$(a, x) \mapsto a \cdot x$ measurable, let $\tau$ be an s-finite inversion-invariant measure on $A$,
and $\mu$ an s-finite measure on $X$ preserved by every $x \mapsto a \cdot x$. Let
$f, w_1, w_2 : X \to [0, \infty]$ be measurable, with $f$ $A$-invariant and
$\int_A w_i(a \cdot x)\, d\tau(a) = 1$ whenever $f(x) \ne 0$. Then
$\int_X f w_1\, d\mu = \int_X f w_2\, d\mu$ in $[0, \infty]$; the common value may be infinite.
