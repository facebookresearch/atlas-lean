# Positive common integral of cocompact subgroup cutoffs

Let $G$ be a locally compact, second-countable topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra
and a Haar measure $\mu$, and $T \le G$ a closed subgroup with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and an
inversion-invariant Haar measure $\tau$. Suppose $G = TK$ for a compact set $K$. Then there is
$\kappa > 0$ such that

- some continuous, nonnegative, compactly supported $w : G \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies
  $\int_T w(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ for every $x \in G$, and
- every nonnegative, measurable, compactly supported $w$ with $\int_T w(tx)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ for
  every $x \in G$ is $\mu$-integrable with $\int_G w\, d\mu = \kappa$.

Such a $w$ is integrable even when it is unbounded. Neither $G$ nor $T$ needs to be Hausdorff.
