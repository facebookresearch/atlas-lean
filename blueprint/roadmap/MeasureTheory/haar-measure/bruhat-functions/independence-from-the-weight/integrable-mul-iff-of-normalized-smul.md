# Integrability is independent of the orbit-normalized weight

Let a group $A$ with measurable inversion act on a measurable space $X$ with
$(a, x) \mapsto a \cdot x$ measurable, let $\tau$ be an s-finite inversion-invariant measure on $A$,
and $\mu$ an s-finite measure on $X$ preserved by every $x \mapsto a \cdot x$. Let $f : X \to \mathbb{C}$
be measurable and $A$-invariant, and $w_1, w_2 : X \to [0, \infty)$ measurable with
$\int_A w_i(a \cdot x)\, d\tau(a) = 1$ whenever $f(x) \ne 0$. Then $f w_1$ is $\mu$-integrable if
and only if $f w_2$ is. Neither weighted function is assumed integrable.
