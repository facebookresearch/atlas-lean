# Lintegral mul eq of ae normalized smul

Let a group $A$ act on a measurable space $X$, where $A$ carries a measurable structure with measurable inversion and the action map $(a, x) \mapsto a \cdot x$ is measurable. Let $\tau$ be an S-finite measure on $A$ invariant under inversion, and let $\mu$ be an S-finite measure on $X$ preserved by every map $x \mapsto a \cdot x$. Let $f \colon X \to [0, \infty]$ be $\mu$-a.e. measurable and invariant, $f(a \cdot x) = f(x)$ for all $a, x$, and let $w_1, w_2 \colon X \to [0, \infty]$ be measurable with

$$\int_A w_i(a \cdot x)\, d\tau(a) = 1 \quad \text{for } \mu\text{-a.e. } x \text{ with } f(x) \ne 0, \qquad i = 1, 2.$$

Then

$$\int_X f\, w_1 \, d\mu = \int_X f\, w_2 \, d\mu,$$

where both lower integrals may equal $\infty$.
