# Integrals of functions on a finite coset space

Let $K$ be a topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and a left-invariant probability measure $\mu$, and let $H$ be an open subgroup with finitely many left cosets. For every function $f : K/H \to E$ to a real Banach space,

$$\int_K f(kH)\, d\mu(k) = \frac{1}{[K : H]} \sum_{q \in K/H} f(q).$$

No measurability of $f$ or normality of $H$ is required.
