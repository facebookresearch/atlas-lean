# Changing an orbit-normalized density preserves integrals of invariant functions

Let a group $A$, with measurable inversion, act measurably on a measurable space $X$ (the action map $A \times X \to X$ is measurable), let $\tau$ be an s-finite measure on $A$ that is invariant under inversion, and let $\mu$ be an s-finite measure on $X$ preserved by every map $x \mapsto a \cdot x$. Let $E$ be a real Banach space and let $w_1, w_2 : X \to [0,\infty)$ be measurable finite-valued densities with $\int_A w_i(a \cdot x)\, d\tau(a) = 1$ for every $x$. If $f : X \to E$ is $A$-invariant and integrable for $w_1\,\mu$, then $f$ is integrable for $w_2\,\mu$ and

$$\int_X f\, d(w_2\,\mu) = \int_X f\, d(w_1\,\mu).$$
