# Unfolding through an intermediate closed subgroup

Let $G$ be a second-countable locally compact topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and a Haar measure $\mu$, and let $S \le T$ be closed subgroups carrying inversion-invariant Haar measures $\tau_S$ and $\tau$. Let $F : G \to \mathbb{C}$ be measurable, bounded and left $S$-invariant ($F(sx) = F(x)$). Let $w_S \ge 0$ be measurable with compact support and $\int_S w_S(s x)\, d\tau_S(s) = 1$ whenever $F(x) \neq 0$; let $\beta : T \to [0,\infty)$ be measurable with $\int_S \beta(s t)\, d\tau_S(s) = 1$ for all $t \in T$; and let $W \ge 0$ be measurable with $\int_T W(t x)\, d\tau(t) = 1$ whenever $F(x) \neq 0$. Then

$$\int_G F\, w_S\, d\mu = \int_G \Bigl( \int_T F(t x)\, \beta(t)\, d\tau(t) \Bigr) W(x)\, d\mu(x).$$

$F$ need not be $T$-invariant.
