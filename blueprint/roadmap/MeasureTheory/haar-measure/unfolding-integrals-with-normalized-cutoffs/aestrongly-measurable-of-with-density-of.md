# Measurability of invariant functions transfers from a normalized weighted measure

Let $A$ act on $X$ with measurable action map, let $\tau$ and $\mu$ be s-finite measures on $A$ and $X$ with $\mu$ preserved by every $x \mapsto a \cdot x$, and let $E$ be a real Banach space. Let $w : X \to [0,\infty)$ be a measurable finite-valued density with $\int_A w(a \cdot x)\, d\tau(a) = 1$ for every $x$. If $f : X \to E$ is $A$-invariant and a.e. strongly measurable for the measure $w\,\mu$, then $f$ is a.e. strongly measurable for $\mu$. The density may vanish, so $\mu \ll w\,\mu$ is not assumed.
