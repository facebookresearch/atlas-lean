# Measurable fundamental domains for discrete subgroups

Let $G$ be a second-countable Hausdorff topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and
$\Gamma \le G$ a discrete subgroup. Then there is a measurable set $D \subseteq G$ such that for every
$x \in G$ there is exactly one $\gamma \in \Gamma$ with $x\gamma \in D$; in particular $D$ is a
fundamental domain for the right action of $\Gamma$ on $G$ with respect to every measure on $G$.
