# Measurability of integrals over a closed subgroup on the quotient

Let $G$ be a second-countable locally compact topological group with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $H$ a closed subgroup with a Haar measure $\mu_H$ on $H$ (left-invariant) that is also right-invariant, and $f : G \to [0,\infty]$ measurable. For each coset $q \in H \backslash G$ choose a representative $\bar q$. Then $q \mapsto \int_H f(h \bar q)\, d\mu_H(h)$, a nonnegative (lower) integral with values in $[0,\infty]$, is a measurable function on $H \backslash G$.
