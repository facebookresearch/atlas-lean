# Haar measure

Let $G$ be a group with a topology and a σ-algebra. A measure $\mu$ on $G$ is a (left) Haar measure if it is
finite on compact sets, invariant under left translations, that is $\mu(gA) = \mu(A)$ for every $g \in G$ and every
measurable set $A$, and positive on nonempty open sets. No regularity is required.
