# Haar measure

Let $G$ be a group with a topology and a σ-algebra. A measure $\mu$ on $G$ is a (left) Haar measure if it is
finite on compact sets, invariant under left translations and positive on nonempty open sets. Invariance means
that for every $g \in G$ the pushforward of $\mu$ along the left translation $x \mapsto gx$ is $\mu$ itself. When left
translations are measurable, as for a topological group with its Borel σ-algebra, it says that $\mu(gA) = \mu(A)$ for
every $g \in G$ and every measurable set $A$. No regularity is required.
