# Absolutely continuous function on an interval

Let $X$ be a pseudometric space, let $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to X$, and let $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$,
with $I$ the closed interval with endpoints $a$ and $b$. Consider finite families of pairs $(a_i, b_i)_{i < n}$ of
points of $I$ for which the intervals $(\min(a_i, b_i), \max(a_i, b_i)]$, $i < n$, are pairwise disjoint. The
function $f$ is absolutely continuous on $I$ if $\sum_{i < n} \operatorname{dist}(f(a_i), f(b_i))$ tends to $0$ as
the total length $\sum_{i < n} |a_i - b_i|$ tends to $0$, over such families.
