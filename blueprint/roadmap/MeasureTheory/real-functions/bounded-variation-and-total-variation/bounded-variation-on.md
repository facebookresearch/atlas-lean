# Function of bounded variation on a set

Let $\alpha$ be a linearly ordered set, let $E$ be a space with an extended pseudodistance, such
as $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$ or a metric space, let $f \colon \alpha \to E$, and let $s \subseteq \alpha$. The
variation of $f$ on $s$ is the supremum, over $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and nondecreasing sequences
$u_0 \le u_1 \le \cdots \le u_n$ of points of $s$, of $\sum_{i < n} \operatorname{edist}(f(u_{i+1}), f(u_i))$, a
value in $[0, \infty]$. The function $f$ has bounded variation on $s$ if its variation on $s$ is finite.
