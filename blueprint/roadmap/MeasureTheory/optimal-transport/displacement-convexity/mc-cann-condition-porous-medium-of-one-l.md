# Porous-medium energies with $m > 1$ satisfy McCann's condition

For every dimension $d$ and every $m > 1$, $A(s) = s^m/(m-1)$ satisfies
[McCann's condition (A1)](mc-cann-condition.md): $A(0) = 0$ and the
[dilation profile](dilation-profile-porous-medium.md) $\lambda^{-d(m-1)}/(m-1)$ is a positive
multiple of a [negative power](convex-on-rpow-neg.md), hence convex, and it is nonincreasing.
McCann uses $A(s) = s^q$, $q > 1$, a positive multiple, in the proof of his Theorem 2.3. The
energy $\int \rho^m/(m-1)$ is the one whose Wasserstein gradient flow is the porous medium
equation $\partial_t \rho = \Delta \rho^m$.

## Sources

- R. J. McCann, *A convexity principle for interacting gases*, Adv. Math. 128 (1997), 153–179, condition (A1) and the proof of Theorem 2.3.
