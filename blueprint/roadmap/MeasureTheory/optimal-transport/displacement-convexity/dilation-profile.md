# Dilation profile of an internal energy

For $A : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a dimension $d$, the dilation profile is

$$\lambda \mapsto \lambda^d A(\lambda^{-d}).$$

If a unit mass is spread uniformly over a region of unit volume, dilating it by the factor
$\lambda > 0$ gives density $\lambda^{-d}$ on volume $\lambda^d$, so the internal energy
$\int A(\rho) \, dx$ becomes $\lambda^d A(\lambda^{-d})$.
