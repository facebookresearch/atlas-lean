# Admissible densities of the JKO scheme

A probability measure $\rho$ on a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ ($\mathbb{R}^n$ in
the paper) is *admissible* if it is absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure and has
finite second moment

$$M(\rho) = \int |x|^2 \, d\rho(x) < \infty.$$

This is the set $K$ over which the JKO scheme minimizes. Densities are represented by the measures
they define; two densities equal almost everywhere give the same measure.

## Background

- [The Wasserstein distance is finite on measures with finite $p$-th moments](../../wasserstein-distances/wasserstein-distance/wasserstein-lt-top.md):
  $W_2$ is finite on $K$.
