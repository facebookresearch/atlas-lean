# Potential energy

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. For a
potential $\Psi : E \to \mathbb{R}$ and a measure $\rho$ on $E$,

$$E(\rho) = \int \max(\Psi, 0) \, d\rho \in [0, \infty],$$

which is $\int \Psi \, d\rho$ under the standing assumption $\Psi \ge 0$ of Jordan, Kinderlehrer and
Otto.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, the functional $E$ of Section 2.
