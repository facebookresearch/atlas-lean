# One-step comparison inequality of the JKO scheme

If $\rho_0$ is [admissible](is-admissible.md) and $\rho$ is a [JKO step](is-jkostep.md) from
$\rho_0$, then

$$\tfrac12 W_2(\rho_0, \rho)^2 + h F(\rho) \le h F(\rho_0),$$

by comparing with the admissible competitor $\rho_0$, whose objective is
[$h F(\rho_0)$](jko-objective-self.md). It is the Wasserstein instance of the
[one-step energy inequality](../minimizing-movements/energy-le.md) of the abstract scheme, with energies valued in
$[-\infty, +\infty]$.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, proof of Theorem 5.1, the inequality preceding (46).
