# JKO sequence

A sequence $(\rho_k)$ of probability densities solves the JKO scheme with time step $h$ if each
$\rho_{k+1}$ is a [JKO step](is-jkostep.md) from $\rho_k$. By
[existence and uniqueness of the JKO step](exists-unique-is-jkostep.md) the sequence is determined
by $\rho_0$ under the assumptions of the paper.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, scheme (13).
