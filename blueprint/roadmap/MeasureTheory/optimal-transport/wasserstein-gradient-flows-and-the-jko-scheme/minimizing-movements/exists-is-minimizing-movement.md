# Existence of a JKO sequence on a proper metric space

Under the hypotheses of [existence of a JKO step](exists-is-minimizing-movement-step.md) (proper
space, lower semicontinuous $F$ bounded below, $h > 0$), every starting point $x_0$ admits a
[minimizing-movement sequence](is-minimizing-movement.md) with $\rho_0 = x_0$, obtained by
iterating a choice of step.
