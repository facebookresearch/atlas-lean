# The JKO step decreases the functional

For a time step $h > 0$, a [minimizing-movement step](is-minimizing-movement-step.md) $y$ from $x$
satisfies $F(y) \le F(x)$: by the [one-step energy inequality](energy-le.md),
$h F(y) \le h F(x) - \tfrac12 d(x, y)^2 \le h F(x)$.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, proof of Theorem 5.1.
