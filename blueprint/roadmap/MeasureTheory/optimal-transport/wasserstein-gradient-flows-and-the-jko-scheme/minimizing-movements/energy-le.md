# One-step energy inequality of the JKO scheme

If $y$ is a [minimizing-movement step](is-minimizing-movement-step.md) from $x$, then

$$\tfrac12 d(x, y)^2 + h F(y) \le h F(x).$$

Proof: compare the minimizer with the competitor $z = x$, for which $d(x, x) = 0$. No hypothesis on
$h$ or $F$ is needed.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, proof of Theorem 5.1, the inequality preceding (46).
