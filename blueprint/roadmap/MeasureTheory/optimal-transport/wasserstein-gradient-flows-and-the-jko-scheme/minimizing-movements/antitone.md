# The functional decreases along the JKO scheme

For a time step $h > 0$, $k \mapsto F(\rho_k)$ is nonincreasing along a
[minimizing-movement sequence](is-minimizing-movement.md): each step
[decreases the functional](le.md). For the Fokker–Planck free energy this is a discrete H-theorem,
the counterpart of the [free-energy decay](../../../../Probability/stochastic-calculus/fokker-planck-equation/antitone-on-free-energy.md)
of the continuous equation.

## Sources

- R. Jordan, D. Kinderlehrer, F. Otto, *The variational formulation of the Fokker–Planck equation*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29 (1998), 1–17, proof of Theorem 5.1.
