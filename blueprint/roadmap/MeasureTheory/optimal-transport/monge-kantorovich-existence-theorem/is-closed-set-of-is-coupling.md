# The set of couplings is closed

Let $X$ and $Y$ be Polish spaces with their Borel σ-algebras and $\mu, \nu$ probability measures
on $X$ and $Y$. The set $\Pi(\mu, \nu)$ of couplings of $\mu$ and $\nu$ is closed in the space of
probability measures on $X \times Y$ with the topology of weak convergence: the marginal maps
$\pi \mapsto \pi \circ \mathrm{pr}_1^{-1}$ and $\pi \mapsto \pi \circ \mathrm{pr}_2^{-1}$ are continuous and
the space of probability measures is Hausdorff.
