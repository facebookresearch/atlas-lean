# The product coupling

For probability measures $\mu$ on $Y$ and $\nu$ on $Z$, the product measure $\mu \otimes \nu$ is a
coupling of $\mu$ and $\nu$. In particular the set of couplings is never empty, so the infimum
defining $W_p$ runs over a nonempty set.

## Sources

- C. Villani, *Optimal Transport: Old and New*, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften 338, Springer 2009, Chapter 1, the trivial coupling (independent variables, joint law $\mu \otimes \nu$).
