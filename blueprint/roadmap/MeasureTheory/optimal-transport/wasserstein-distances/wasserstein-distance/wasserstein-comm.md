# Symmetry of the Wasserstein distance

On a second-countable pseudo-extended metric space whose σ-algebra contains the open sets,
$W_p(\mu, \nu) = W_p(\nu, \mu)$ for every real $p$ and all probability measures $\mu, \nu$: swapping
the coordinates of a coupling preserves its cost.

## Sources

- C. Villani, *Optimal Transport: Old and New*, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften 338, Springer 2009, Chapter 6, proof that $W_p$ satisfies the axioms of a distance, after Definition 6.1.
