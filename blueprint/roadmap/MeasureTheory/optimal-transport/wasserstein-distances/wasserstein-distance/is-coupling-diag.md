# Diagonal coupling

For a probability measure $\mu$, the image of $\mu$ under the diagonal map $x \mapsto (x, x)$ is a
coupling of $\mu$ with itself: the deterministic coupling given by the identity map.

## Sources

- C. Villani, *Optimal Transport: Old and New*, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften 338, Springer 2009, Definition 1.2 (deterministic coupling), with the identity map.
