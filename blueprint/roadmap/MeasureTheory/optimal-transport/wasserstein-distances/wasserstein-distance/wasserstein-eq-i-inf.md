# Wasserstein distance as an infimum over couplings

For $p > 0$,

$$W_p(\mu, \nu) = \inf_{\pi} \Bigl(\int d(x, y)^p \, d\pi(x, y)\Bigr)^{1/p},$$

the infimum over couplings $\pi$ of $\mu$ and $\nu$: the $1/p$-th power, an order isomorphism of
$[0, \infty]$, commutes with the infimum.

## Sources

- C. Villani, *Optimal Transport: Old and New*, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften 338, Springer 2009, Equation (6.2), second form: $W_p(\mu, \nu) = \inf \{ (\mathbb{E}\, d(X, Y)^p)^{1/p} : \operatorname{law}(X) = \mu, \operatorname{law}(Y) = \nu \}$.
