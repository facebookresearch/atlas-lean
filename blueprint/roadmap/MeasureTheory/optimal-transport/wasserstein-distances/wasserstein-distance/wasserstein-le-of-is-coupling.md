# Every coupling bounds the Wasserstein distance

For $p \ge 0$ and every coupling $\pi$ of $\mu$ and $\nu$,
$W_p(\mu, \nu) \le \bigl(\int d(x, y)^p \, d\pi(x, y)\bigr)^{1/p}$.

## Sources
