# Monotonicity of the Wasserstein distances in the exponent

On a second-countable pseudo-extended metric space whose σ-algebra contains the open sets, for
$0 < p \le q$ and all probability measures $\mu, \nu$, $W_p(\mu, \nu) \le W_q(\mu, \nu)$. For each
coupling, the $L^p$ norm of $d$ is at most its $L^q$ norm (Hölder's inequality on a probability
space). In particular $W_1$ is the weakest of the distances $W_p$, $p \ge 1$.
