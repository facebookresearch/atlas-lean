# Every coupling bounds the transport cost

For every coupling $\pi$ of $\mu$ and $\nu$, the optimal transport cost satisfies
$C_p(\mu, \nu) \le \int d(x, y)^p \, d\pi(x, y)$. This is the defining property of the infimum.
