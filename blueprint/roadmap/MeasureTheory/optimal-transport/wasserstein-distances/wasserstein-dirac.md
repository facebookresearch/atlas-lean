# Wasserstein distance between Dirac masses

Let $X$ be an extended metric space with its Borel σ-algebra. For $p > 0$ and $x, y \in X$,
$W_p(\delta_x, \delta_y) = d(x, y)$. The only coupling of two Dirac masses is $\delta_{(x, y)}$; here it
is enough that every coupling is concentrated on $\{(x, y)\}$.
