# The Wasserstein distance metrizes weak convergence in $P_p$

Let $X$ be a Polish metric space, $p \in [1, \infty)$ and $x_0 \in X$, and let $\mu_k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$)
and $\mu$ be Borel probability measures on $X$ with finite $p$-th moments
$\int d(x_0, x)^p \, d\mu_k(x)$ and $\int d(x_0, x)^p \, d\mu(x)$. Then $W_p(\mu_k, \mu) \to 0$ if and only
if $\mu_k \to \mu$ weakly (against bounded continuous test functions) and
$\int d(x_0, x)^p \, d\mu_k(x) \to \int d(x_0, x)^p \, d\mu(x)$.

The right-hand side is characterisation (i) of weak convergence in $P_p(X)$ (Definition 6.8).
