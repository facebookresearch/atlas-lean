# Lebesgue decomposition theorem

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be measures on a measurable space $\Omega$, with $\mu$ s-finite (a countable sum of finite
measures) and $\nu$ σ-finite. Then $\mu$ has a Lebesgue decomposition with respect to $\nu$: there are a measure $\xi$
on $\Omega$ and a measurable function $f \colon \Omega \to [0, \infty]$ such that $\xi$ and $\nu$ are mutually singular,
that is, $\xi(S) = 0$ and $\nu(\Omega \setminus S) = 0$ for some measurable set $S$, and

$$
\mu(A) = \xi(A) + \int_A f \, d\nu \qquad \text{for every measurable } A \subseteq \Omega.
$$
