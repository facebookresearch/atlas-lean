# Radon–Nikodym derivative as a ratio of densities

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be σ-finite measures on a measurable space $\Omega$. Then, for $\nu$-almost every
$x \in \Omega$,

$$
\frac{d\mu}{d\nu}(x) = \frac{\frac{d\mu}{d(\mu + \nu)}(x)}{\frac{d\nu}{d(\mu + \nu)}(x)},
$$

where the Radon–Nikodym derivatives take values in $[0, \infty]$ and the quotient is that of $[0, \infty]$:
$a / b = a \cdot b^{-1}$ with $0^{-1} = \infty$, $\infty^{-1} = 0$ and $0 \cdot \infty = 0$. Here $\frac{d\mu}{d\nu}$ is
the density of a chosen Lebesgue decomposition $\mu = \xi + f \cdot \nu$ with $\xi$ singular with respect to $\nu$,
and the zero function when there is none, and likewise for the other two derivatives.
