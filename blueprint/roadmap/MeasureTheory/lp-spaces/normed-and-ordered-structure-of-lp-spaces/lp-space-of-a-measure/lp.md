# The space $L^p$ of a measure

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $E$ be a normed additive
commutative group, and let $p \in [0, \infty]$. The space $L^p(\alpha, E; \mu)$ is the additive subgroup of the
group of $\mu$-almost everywhere classes of almost everywhere strongly measurable functions $\alpha \to E$ consisting
of the classes $f$ with $\|f\|_p < \infty$, where $\|f\|_p$ is $0$ if $p = 0$,
$\big(\int \|f\|^p \, d\mu\big)^{1/p}$ if $0 < p < \infty$, and the essential supremum of $\|f\|$ if $p = \infty$.
