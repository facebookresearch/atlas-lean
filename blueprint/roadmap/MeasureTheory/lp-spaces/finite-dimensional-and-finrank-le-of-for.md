# Finite dimension of L2 subspaces with a uniform pointwise bound

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $X$, let $\mathbb{k}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let $V$ be a subspace of $L^2(\mu; \mathbb{k})$. Suppose there is $C \in \mathbb{R}$ such that every $\varphi \in V$ satisfies $|\varphi(x)| \le C \|\varphi\|_{L^2}$ for $\mu$-almost every $x$. Then $V$ is finite-dimensional and

$$\dim V \le C^2 \, \mu(X).$$
