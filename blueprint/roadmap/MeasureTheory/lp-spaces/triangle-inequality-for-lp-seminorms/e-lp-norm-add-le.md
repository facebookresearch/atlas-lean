# Triangle inequality for the $L^p$ seminorm

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $\varepsilon$ be a topological
additive monoid with an extended seminorm $\|\cdot\|_e \colon \varepsilon \to [0, \infty]$ (such as $\mathbb{R}$,
$\mathbb{C}$, a normed space or $[0, \infty]$) in which addition is continuous, and let $p \in [1, \infty]$. For all
functions $f, g \colon \alpha \to \varepsilon$,

$$\|f + g\|_p \le \|f\|_p + \|g\|_p$$

in $[0, \infty]$. Here, for $h$ almost everywhere strongly measurable, $\|h\|_p = \big(\int \|h\|_e^p \, d\mu\big)^{1/p}$
if $p < \infty$ and $\|h\|_\infty$ is the essential supremum of $\|h\|_e$; for any other $h$, $\|h\|_p = \infty$.
