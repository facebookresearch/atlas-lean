# Smooth compactly supported functions are dense in $L^p$

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra, let $\mu$ be a
measure on $E$ with $\mu(K) < \infty$ for every compact $K \subseteq E$, let $F$ be a real normed space, and let
$1 \le p < \infty$. Then the classes $f \in L^p(E, F; \mu)$ that are equal $\mu$-almost everywhere to an infinitely
differentiable function $g \colon E \to F$ with compact support form a dense subset of $L^p(E, F; \mu)$.
