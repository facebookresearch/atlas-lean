# Comparison of $L^p$ seminorms for $p \le q$

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $\varepsilon$ be a topological space with a continuous
extended norm $\|\cdot\|_e \colon \varepsilon \to [0, \infty]$, such as $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$, a normed group or
$[0, \infty]$, and let $p \le q$ in $[0, \infty]$. Let $f \colon \alpha \to \varepsilon$ be almost everywhere strongly
measurable. If $p > 0$, then

$$\|f\|_p \le \|f\|_q \, \mu(\alpha)^{1/p - 1/q}$$

in $[0, \infty]$, where $\|\cdot\|_p$ is the $L^p$ seminorm, computed from $\|f\|_e$, $1/q$ is read as $0$ when
$q = \infty$, and the power is computed in $[0, \infty]$. If $p = 0$, the left side $\|f\|_0$ is $0$, so the same
inequality, with the exponent read as $-1/q$, and as $0$ when $q$ is $0$ or $\infty$, holds trivially.
