# $L^q \subseteq L^p$ for a finite measure and $p \le q$

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $\varepsilon$ be a topological space with a continuous extended norm
$\|\cdot\|_e \colon \varepsilon \to [0, \infty]$, such as $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$, a normed group or $[0, \infty]$, and let
$p \le q$ in $[0, \infty]$. If $f \colon \alpha \to \varepsilon$ is in $L^q(\mu)$, that is, almost everywhere strongly
measurable with $\|f\|_q < \infty$, the $L^q$ seminorm being computed from $\|f\|_e$, then $f$ is in $L^p(\mu)$.
