# Hölder's inequality with an essentially bounded factor

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, let $E$, $F$ and $G$ be normed
additive commutative groups, and let $b \colon E \times F \to G$ be continuous. Let $f \colon \alpha \to E$ be any
function, let $g \colon \alpha \to F$ be almost everywhere strongly measurable, and let $c \ge 0$ be a real number
with $\|b(f(x), g(x))\| \le c \, \|f(x)\| \, \|g(x)\|$ for almost every $x$. Then for every $p \in [0, \infty]$,

$$\|x \mapsto b(f(x), g(x))\|_p \le c \, \|f\|_p \, \|g\|_\infty$$

in $[0, \infty]$, where $\|h\|_p$ is the $L^p$ seminorm of $h$ (taken to be $\infty$ when $h$ is not almost
everywhere strongly measurable) and $\|g\|_\infty$ is the essential supremum of $\|g\|$.
