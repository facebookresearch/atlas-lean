# Hölder's inequality for functions with values in $[0, \infty]$

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a set $\alpha$ with a σ-algebra, and let $p$ and $q$ be positive real
numbers with $1/p + 1/q = 1$. For all almost everywhere measurable functions $f, g \colon \alpha \to [0, \infty]$,

$$\int f g \, d\mu \le \Big( \int f^p \, d\mu \Big)^{1/p} \Big( \int g^q \, d\mu \Big)^{1/q},$$

where the integrals are integrals of functions with values in $[0, \infty]$ and the arithmetic is that of
$[0, \infty]$, with $0 \cdot \infty = 0$.
