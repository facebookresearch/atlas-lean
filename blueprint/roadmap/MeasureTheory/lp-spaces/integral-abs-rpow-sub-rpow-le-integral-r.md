# An integral inequality for $p$-th powers with $0 < p < 1$

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a
measurable space and let $0 < p < 1$. Then for all real-valued $f, g \in L^p(\mu)$,

$$\int \bigl||f|^p - |g|^p\bigr|\,d\mu \le \int |f - g|^p\,d\mu.$$
