# $L^p$ is complete for $1 \le p \le \infty$

Let $\alpha$ be a set with a σ-algebra and a measure $\mu$, let $E$ be a complete normed
additive commutative group, and let $p \in [1, \infty]$. Then $L^p(\alpha, E; \mu)$, the space of $\mu$-almost
everywhere classes of almost everywhere strongly measurable functions $\alpha \to E$ with finite $L^p$ seminorm, is
complete for the distance $(f, g) \mapsto \|f - g\|_p$.
