# Automorphic forms and representations

Automorphic forms and automorphic representations of reductive groups over number fields and their adele rings: smooth adelic functions of moderate growth, constant terms and cusp forms, Whittaker models, the decomposition of automorphic representations into local components, multiplicity one, Eisenstein series, the spectral decomposition and trace formulas, Rankin–Selberg and doubling integrals and the L-functions they define, base change, the cohomology of arithmetic locally symmetric spaces with its Hecke action, and the Weil representation and the theta correspondence, local and global (Howe duality, the Siegel–Weil and Rallis inner product formulas). Classical modular forms, theta functions and q-series are under modular forms, q-series and partitions, adelic points of algebraic groups with their measures and class numbers under algebraic number theory, and the representations of the local groups have their own field.
