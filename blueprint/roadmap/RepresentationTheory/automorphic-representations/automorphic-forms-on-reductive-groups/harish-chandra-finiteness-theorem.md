# Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem for automorphic forms

Let $k$, $G$, $K = K_\infty K_f$, $\mathcal{Z}$ and $\mathcal{H}$ be as for
[automorphic forms](automorphic-forms-and-automorphic-representations.md). Fix a finite-dimensional
representation $\delta$ of $K_\infty$, an open compact subgroup $L$ of $G(\mathbb{A}_f)$ and an ideal $J$ of
finite codimension in $\mathcal{Z}$, and let $\mathcal{A}(\delta, L, J)$ be the space of automorphic forms
$\varphi$ on $G(\mathbb{A})$ that are right invariant under $L$, annihilated by $J$, and whose right
$K_\infty$-translates span a representation of $K_\infty$ whose irreducible constituents occur in $\delta$.

1. **Finiteness** (Harish-Chandra). $\mathcal{A}(\delta, L, J)$ is finite-dimensional. For
   $G = \mathrm{GL}_n$ this holds in particular for the subspace $\mathcal{A}(\delta, L, J, \omega)$ of forms
   with central character $\omega$.
2. **Admissibility.** For every automorphic form $\varphi$, the $\mathcal{H}$-module
   $\varphi * \mathcal{H}$ generated by $\varphi$ is admissible. Hence every automorphic representation is
   an admissible $\mathcal{H}$-module.

## Depends on

- [Automorphic forms, cusp forms and automorphic representations](automorphic-forms-and-automorphic-representations.md), for automorphic forms, the Hecke algebra $\mathcal{H}$ and admissibility

## Proof depends on

- [Siegel sets and reduction theory for adelic groups](../../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/reduction-theory-for-adelic-groups.md), for the finiteness of class numbers, that is of $G(k) \backslash G(\mathbb{A}_f) / L$, which reduces item 1 to finitely many arithmetic quotients $\Gamma_i \backslash G_\infty$ with $\Gamma_i$ arithmetic (finiteness of $G(k)$-orbits alone would not give this), for the finitely many $\Gamma_i$-conjugacy classes of maximal parabolic subgroups, and for the finite volume of the norm-one quotient, used in the induction on the rank
- [Constant terms and Harish-Chandra's convolution identity for automorphic forms](constant-terms-and-convolution-identity.md), for the reduction along the split centre $A_G(\mathbb{R})^0$ (Borel, 6.6) and the automorphy of the constant terms along the maximal parabolic subgroups on their Levi factors (Borel, 7.4 (c)), the two inputs of the induction on the rank (Borel, 8.4)
- [Rapid decay of cusp forms](rapid-decay-of-cusp-forms.md), for the boundedness of cusp forms, so that the cusp forms of a given type form a closed subspace of $L^2$ consisting of bounded functions, which is finite-dimensional by Godement's lemma
