# Constant terms and Harish-Chandra's convolution identity for automorphic forms

This node is still to be broken down. It covers basic properties of
[automorphic forms](automorphic-forms-and-automorphic-representations.md) used by the pages on
[rapid decay](rapid-decay-of-cusp-forms.md), [Harish-Chandra's finiteness theorem](harish-chandra-finiteness-theorem.md)
and [constituents of induced representations](automorphic-representations-as-constituents-of-induced-representations.md).
Let $k$, $G$, $K = K_\infty K_f$, $G_\infty$ and the centre $\mathcal{Z}$ of the enveloping algebra be as for automorphic forms.

- **Convolution identity** (Harish-Chandra). Every automorphic form $\varphi$ satisfies $\varphi = \varphi \star \alpha$ for some
  $\alpha \in C^\infty_c(U)$, $U$ any neighbourhood of $1$ in $G_\infty$, and is of uniform moderate growth: the case of automorphic
  forms of [Harish-Chandra's convolution identity](harish-chandra-convolution-identity.md), which is stated there for all smooth
  $K$-finite and $\mathcal{Z}$-finite functions on $G(k) \backslash G(\mathbb{A})$, so that the definition of automorphic forms can use it.
- **The split centre.** An automorphic form is finite under translation by the centre $Z_G(\mathbb{A})$ (Langlands's
  Lemma 3). Along the split component $A_G(\mathbb{R})^0$ of the centre, a smooth $\mathcal{Z}$-finite function satisfies
  $\varphi(xa) = \sum_i Q_i(a) (P_i \varphi)(x)$ for $a \in A_G(\mathbb{R})^0$ and $x \in G(\mathbb{A})^1$, with exponential polynomials $Q_i$ and elements $P_i$ of
  $\mathcal{Z}$ (Borel, 6.6), so that statements on $G(\mathbb{A})$ reduce to statements on $G(\mathbb{A})^1$.
- **Constant terms are automorphic forms.** For a parabolic subgroup $P = MN$ over $k$ and $g \in G(\mathbb{A})$, the
  function $m \mapsto \varphi_P(mg)$ on $M(\mathbb{A})$ obtained from the constant term of an automorphic form $\varphi$ is an automorphic
  form on $M(\mathbb{A})$: finite under a maximal compact subgroup of $M(\mathbb{A})$ containing the projection of $gKg^{-1} \cap P(\mathbb{A})$,
  with types determined by those of $\varphi$, of moderate growth, and finite under the centre of the enveloping
  algebra of $\mathfrak{m}$, through Harish-Chandra's homomorphism $\mathcal{Z}(\mathfrak{g}) \to \mathcal{Z}(\mathfrak{m})$ (Langlands's Lemma 4; Borel, 7.4 (a)–(c)).
- **Central characters and cuspidal components on the Levi**, in two separate steps. (a) Let $\mathcal{A}^\circ_P$ be the
  space of smooth $K$-finite functions $\psi$ on $N(\mathbb{A}) P(k) \backslash G(\mathbb{A})$ such that $m \mapsto \psi(mg)$ is a cuspidal automorphic
  form on $M(\mathbb{A})$ for every $g$. An irreducible subquotient $V/U$ of $\mathcal{A}^\circ_P$ can be realized with $V$ generated by a
  function $\varphi$ satisfying $\varphi(ag) = \chi(a) \varphi(g)$ for a character $\chi$ of $Z_M(\mathbb{A})$, $Z_M$ the centre of $M$ (Langlands's Lemma 6; $\chi$ is then trivial on $Z_M(k)$).
  This is a statement about a given irreducible subquotient: $\mathcal{A}^\circ_P$ itself need not be a direct sum of irreducible
  subspaces, nor of eigenspaces of the centre (for $P = M = G = \mathrm{GL}_1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, the functions $1$ and $\log \lvert x \rvert_{\mathbb{A}}$
  are $K$-finite, $\mathcal{Z}$-finite cusp forms of moderate growth, and translation by $a$ sends $\log \lvert x \rvert$ to $\log \lvert x \rvert + \log \lvert a \rvert$,
  a non-split extension that no twist by a character splits). (b) With $\chi$ fixed, the decomposition of the
  cuspidal Hilbert space with central character $\chi$ on the Levi factor, weighted by $\nu^{-2}$ for the positive
  character $\nu$ of $M(\mathbb{A})$ trivial on $M(\mathbb{A})^1$ with $\nu = \lvert \chi \rvert$ on $Z_M(\mathbb{A})$, into which the functions
  $m \mapsto \psi(mg)$, $\psi \in V$, fall, is the page on the
  [weighted cuspidal spectrum with a fixed central character](weighted-cuspidal-spectrum-with-a-fixed-central-character.md),
  which comes after the discreteness of the cuspidal spectrum; it is not a statement of this node.

## Expansion

A good expansion has pages for Harish-Chandra's convolution identity and uniform moderate growth, for the
behaviour along the split centre, for the automorphy of constant terms on the Levi factors with
Harish-Chandra's homomorphism, and for the choice of a central character for an irreducible subquotient, each
with a precise statement and the locators below.

A good expansion also writes out these steps of its own proof:

- the expansion $\varphi(xa) = \sum_i Q_i(a) (P_i \varphi)(x)$ of a smooth $\mathcal{Z}$-finite function along $A_G(\mathbb{R})^0$, with exponential polynomials $Q_i$ (Borel, 6.6), for the split centre.
