# Definite-side hypotheses for families of characters

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the existence of a system of data and conditions,
the definite-side hypotheses, that the
[window bounds for weighted Chebyshev sums of cyclic families of characters](../../NumberTheory/zeta-l-functions/dirichlet-l-functions/chebyshev-window-bounds-for-cyclic-character-families.md)
use together with Green's description of the cuspidal characters of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_p)$. The
system consists of a class of families of auxiliary number fields attached to scales (the families
of the window bounds), a selection of data for the members of each family (Hermitian lattices over
CM fields, theta realisations on indefinite and definite unitary groups with their rows, twists and
levels), constants, and conditions on them. Among the conditions are bounds for the rows of the
indefinite realisations, recursions for twisted rows, divisibility and primitivity of rows, class
number formulas and genus–mass volumes of lattices, counting estimates, separation of types, and the
definite-side condition: identities for the rows of the definite side under compatible division
data.

Its proof derives the existence of this system from the
[theta integral identities and local matching for unitary dual pairs](theta-integral-identities-for-unitary-dual-pairs.md),
the [covolume bound for the families of characters](../../AlgebraicGeometry/arakelov-geometry/covolume-bound-for-character-families.md),
the [partial local Langlands correspondence for GLₙ](../reductive-groups-over-local-fields/partial-local-langlands-correspondence-for-gl-n.md),
the [Galois representations attached to polarized cuspidal representations](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/galois-representations/galois-representations-attached-to-polarized-cuspidal-representations.md)
and published results in representation theory, automorphic forms and number theory, listed below.
The data and the conditions are specific to this proof and are defined through many auxiliary
notions; they are not yet stated as pages because each needs those notions stated and checked first.

A good expansion has definition pages for the families, the selections and the rows; a page for
each condition, or for each group of conditions that one argument establishes; pages for the steps
that establish them from the inputs below; and pages for the inputs not yet in the vault, among them
the $\ell$-adic Galois characters attached to algebraic Hecke characters, the non-vanishing of the
Dedekind zeta function $\zeta_K$ of a number field on the line $\operatorname{Re} s = 1$ away from its
simple pole at $s = 1$ (with which $(s - 1)\zeta_K^T(s)$ has no zero on $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$; the
page on Hecke $L$-functions excludes the trivial character, and the case $K = \mathbb{Q}$ does not
suffice), the theorems of Siegel–Klingen and Deligne–Ribet, the functional equations of partial zeta functions of totally real
fields (which the page on Hecke $L$-functions does not state), Weber's ray class fields, and the good
reduction of CM elliptic curves outside a prime.

- [Global Artin reciprocity](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/global-artin-reciprocity.md)

- [Chebotarev density theorem](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/chebotarev-density-theorem.md), for the existence of primes of degree one, outside a given finite set, with a given Frobenius element
- [Main theorem of complex multiplication for elliptic curves](../../NumberTheory/algebraic-number-theory/adeles-class-field-theory/hilbert-class-field/main-theorem-of-complex-multiplication.md)

- [Meromorphic continuation of Hecke L-functions](../../NumberTheory/zeta-l-functions/dirichlet-l-functions/hecke-l-function-meromorphic-continuation.md), for the entire continuation of the partial $L$-functions $L^T(s, \chi)$, with the Euler factors at a finite set $T$ of finite places deleted, of the unitary Hecke characters $\chi$ that are not of the form $|\cdot|^{it}$, and the continuation of $(s - 1)\zeta_K^T(s)$ for the partial Dedekind zeta functions
- [Non-vanishing of Hecke L-functions on the line Re s = 1](../../NumberTheory/zeta-l-functions/dirichlet-l-functions/hecke-l-function-nonvanishing-on-re-one.md), for the non-vanishing on $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$ of the continued partial $L$-functions of the unitary Hecke characters that are not norm powers, since the deleted Euler factors do not vanish there; the clause for the partial Dedekind zeta functions needs the separate input named above
- [Strong approximation for special unitary groups](../../NumberTheory/quadratic-forms-sums-of-squares/strong-approximation-special-unitary.md)
