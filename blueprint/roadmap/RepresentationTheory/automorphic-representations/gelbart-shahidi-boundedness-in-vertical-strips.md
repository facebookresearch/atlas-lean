# Boundedness in vertical strips of Langlands–Shahidi L-functions

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the theorem of Gelbart and Shahidi that the automorphic $L$-functions of
the Langlands–Shahidi method are bounded in vertical strips of finite width, which the Rankin–Selberg $L$-functions of
$\mathrm{GL}_n \times \mathrm{GL}_m$ use when $m < n - 1$. Let $F$ be a number field, $\mathbf{G}$ a quasi-split reductive group over $F$,
$\mathbf{P} = \mathbf{M}\mathbf{N}$ a maximal parabolic subgroup, $\pi$ a generic cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathbf{M}(\mathbb{A}_F)$, and
$L(s, \pi, r_i)$ the $L$-functions that the Langlands–Shahidi method attaches to the irreducible constituents $r_i$ of the
adjoint action of the $L$-group of $\mathbf{M}$ on the Lie algebra of the $L$-group of $\mathbf{N}$, through the constant terms and
Fourier coefficients of the [Eisenstein series](automorphic-forms-on-reductive-groups/eisenstein-series.md) induced from $\pi$.

- **Theorem** (Gelbart–Shahidi). For a wide class of such data, each $L(s, \pi, r_i)$, multiplied by a polynomial removing
  its finitely many poles, is bounded in every vertical strip of finite width. The class includes the Rankin–Selberg
  $L$-functions $L(s, \pi \times \pi')$ of cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb{A}_F)$, through the
  Levi subgroup $\mathrm{GL}_n \times \mathrm{GL}_m$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{n+m}$.
