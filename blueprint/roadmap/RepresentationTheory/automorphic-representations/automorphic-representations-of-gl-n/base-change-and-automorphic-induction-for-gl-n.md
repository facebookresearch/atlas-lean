# Base change and automorphic induction for GLₙ

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the base change and automorphic induction of Arthur and
Clozel for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ along cyclic, and then solvable, extensions of number fields. Let $E/F$ be a
cyclic extension of prime degree $\ell$ with Galois group generated by $\sigma$, and $\eta$ a character of
$\mathbb{A}_F^\times / F^\times$ attached to $E/F$ by class field theory.

- **Cyclic base change** (Arthur–Clozel). There is a map $\pi' \mapsto \pi$ from induced cuspidal
  automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ to those of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$,
  characterized by the Satake parameters at the unramified places ($c_w(\pi) = c_v(\pi')$ if $v$ splits in $E$,
  $c_v(\pi')^\ell$ otherwise), whose image is the set of $\sigma$-invariant $\pi$, whose fibre through a
  cuspidal $\pi'$ is $\{\pi' \otimes \eta^i\}$, and which sends a cuspidal $\pi'$ to a cuspidal $\pi$ exactly
  when $\pi' \not\simeq \pi' \otimes \eta$.
- **Automorphic induction.** A cuspidal $\pi'$ with $\pi' \simeq \pi' \otimes \eta$ is induced from a cuspidal
  representation $\pi_1$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{n/\ell}(\mathbb{A}_E)$ with $\pi_1^\sigma \not\simeq \pi_1$, and its base change
  is $\pi_1 \boxplus \pi_1^\sigma \boxplus \dots \boxplus \pi_1^{\sigma^{\ell - 1}}$.
- **Solvable extensions.** The iteration of these maps along a tower of cyclic extensions of prime
  degree, giving base change and automorphic induction along solvable extensions.
- **Method.** The comparison of the invariant trace formula of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over $F$ with the twisted
  trace formula of $\mathrm{R}_{E/F}\mathrm{GL}_n$ for the automorphism $\sigma$, and the classification of the
  residual spectrum of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ by Mœglin and Waldspurger.

A good expansion has pages for the statements of cyclic base change and automorphic induction, for the
solvable case, and for the trace formula comparison with its inputs (the twisted trace formula, the
fundamental lemma for base change, the local base change of representations), citing J. Arthur and
L. Clozel, *Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace Formula*, Annals of
Mathematics Studies 120, Princeton University Press, 1989, Chapter 3 "Base Change", with locators once it
has been consulted.
