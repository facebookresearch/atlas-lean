# Induction and Mackey theory for Heisenberg groups

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the induction arguments behind the
[Stone–von Neumann theorem](stone-von-neumann-theorem.md) for the Heisenberg group $H(W)$ of a symplectic
space $W$ over a local field $F$ of characteristic different from $2$, with a polarization
$W = X \oplus Y$ and a non-trivial character $\psi$ of $F$.

- **Smooth induction.** For a closed subgroup of an $\ell$-group, smooth induction and compact induction
  of smooth representations, Frobenius reciprocity, and the smooth representation of $H(W)$ induced from
  the character of the abelian subgroup $H(Y) = Y \oplus F$ extending $\psi$; its realization on the
  Schwartz–Bruhat functions on $X$, its irreducibility and admissibility, and the uniqueness of a smooth
  irreducible representation with central character $\psi$.
- **Unitary form.** The Stone–von Neumann–Mackey theorem for Heisenberg groups of locally compact abelian
  groups: the representation of the Heisenberg group on $L^2(L)$ is irreducible, and every unitary
  representation on which the centre acts by the given character is a multiple of it; the identification of
  $Y$ with the Pontryagin dual of $X$ through $\psi$ and the pairing.

A good expansion has pages for smooth and compact induction of representations of $\ell$-groups with
Frobenius reciprocity, for the smooth classification of representations of $H(W)$ with central character
$\psi$, and for the unitary Stone–von Neumann–Mackey theorem, each with a precise statement and the sources
below.

  admissibility, and its Schrödinger form), after C. Mœglin, M.-F. Vignéras and J.-L. Waldspurger,
  *Correspondances de Howe sur un corps p-adique*, Lecture Notes in Math. 1291, Springer, 1987, Chapter 2,

  theorem for Heisenberg groups of locally compact abelian groups), §4.2 (groups with compact open
  subgroups) and §4.3 ($\mathbb{R}^n$).
