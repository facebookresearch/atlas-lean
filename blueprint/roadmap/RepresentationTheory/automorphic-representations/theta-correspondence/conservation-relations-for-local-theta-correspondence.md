# Conservation relations for the local theta correspondence

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the conservation relations of Sun and Zhu for the first occurrence of
local theta lifts in two Witt towers, which the doubling method uses. Let $U$ be an $\epsilon$-Hermitian space over a
division algebra $D$ over a local field of characteristic zero, $\bar G(U)$ the relevant group (the metaplectic cover
when $U$ is symplectic), $\mathcal{W}_U$ the group of Witt towers of the dual spaces, and for an irreducible genuine
representation $\pi$ of $\bar G(U)$ and a Witt tower $t$, $n_t(\pi)$ the first occurrence index: the least dimension of a
space $\sigma$ in $t$ to which $\pi$ has a nonzero [theta lift](metaplectic-group-and-local-weil-representation.md).

- **Non-archimedean case** (Sun–Zhu, Theorem 1.10). If $t_1, t_2 \in \mathcal{W}_U$ differ by the anti-split Witt tower
  $t^\circ_U$, then $n_{t_1}(\pi) + n_{t_2}(\pi) = 2 \dim U + d_{D,\epsilon}$ for every irreducible $\pi$ genuine with
  respect to $t_1$.
- **Archimedean case** (Sun–Zhu, Theorem 7.6). For $U$ a real symplectic space, a complex Hermitian or skew-Hermitian
  space or a real quaternionic skew-Hermitian space, and a coset $T$ of the relevant subgroup $K_U$, there are
  $t_1 \ne t_2$ in $T$ with $n_{t_1}(\pi) + n_{t_2}(\pi) = 2 \dim U + d_{D,\epsilon}$, and
  $n_{t_3}(\pi) + n_{t_4}(\pi) \ge 2 \dim U + d_{D,\epsilon} \lvert t_3 - t_4 \rvert$ for all $t_3 \ne t_4$ in $T$.

In both cases the two first occurrences in different towers of the same type have dimensions summing to at least
$2 \dim U + d_{D,\epsilon}$, the inequality due to Rallis.
