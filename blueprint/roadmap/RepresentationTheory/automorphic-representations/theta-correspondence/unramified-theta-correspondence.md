# Unramified theta correspondence

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the local theta correspondence for unramified unitary
dual pairs over a non-archimedean local field $F$ of characteristic $0$ with residue field of odd order
$q$: $E/F$ is the unramified quadratic extension, the additive character $\psi$ is trivial on
$\mathcal{O}_F$ and not on $\mathfrak{p}^{-1}$, the splitting characters are unramified, and the Hermitian
and skew-Hermitian spaces contain self-dual lattices, so that both groups of the pair have hyperspecial
maximal compact subgroups $K$ and $K'$ fixing the vacuum vector $\varphi_0$ of the lattice model of the
[Weil representation](weil-representation-of-unitary-dual-pairs.md) $\omega$.

- **One-sided generation.** For even hyperbolic spaces, the space of vectors of $\omega$ fixed by the
  hyperspecial subgroup of one group of the pair is generated, under the other group, by the vacuum vector
  (Liu's Assumption 3.3, proved for odd $q$ in his Proposition 3.4). In the form used in Alpöge's library:
  for the unramified unitary pair in odd residue characteristic, the vectors fixed by the integral points of
  one unitary group lie in the span of the translates of $\varphi_0$ by the other, and symmetrically.
- **Unramified representations correspond, for corresponding pairs** (Howe). If $\pi$ and $\pi'$ are
  irreducible representations of the two groups with $\mathrm{Hom}(\omega, \pi \otimes \pi') \ne 0$, then $\pi$ has a
  non-zero $K$-fixed vector if and only if $\pi'$ has a non-zero $K'$-fixed vector. This is a statement about
  pairs that occur: a representation with $K$-fixed vectors need not have a non-zero theta lift to a
  prescribed smaller space; in the range of the next item, by Liu's Proposition A.5, a spherical
  representation of the larger group lifts only if its Satake parameter lies in the image of the map described
  there. The converse does not follow from Proposition A.5 alone; in the odd-$q$ range of the next item it is
  supplied at equal ranks by Liu's Corollary A.6 (1) (every spherical representation has a non-zero lift, equal
  to itself) and, for the padded parameters, by his Theorem 6.2 (2): a spherical representation of the larger
  group whose Satake parameter is padded (lies in the image of the map of the next item) has a non-zero lift to
  the smaller group, the spherical constituent with the parameter obtained by removing the padding (for
  $\mathrm{U}(4) \to \mathrm{U}(2)$ at $q = 3$, the input with parameter $(z, 3)$ goes to the representation with parameter $z$; in the
  upward direction the coordinate is appended). Here the "small" theta lift $\theta$ is the irreducible quotient of the
  big lift $\Theta$, not a lift to the smaller group.
- **Satake parameters** (Rallis; Liu for unitary pairs). For hyperbolic Hermitian and skew-Hermitian
  spaces of dimensions $2r$ and $2s$ over the unramified quadratic field extension, odd $q$, an additive
  character of conductor $\mathcal{O}_F$ and trivial splitting characters, the annihilator of the Weil
  representation in the tensor product of the two spherical Hecke algebras is generated by the elements
  $\Phi(f) - f$ for a surjective homomorphism $\Phi$ from the spherical Hecke algebra of the larger group to that of
  the smaller one. In terms of the Satake isomorphism, for $s \le r$ the Satake parameter on the larger group
  of a corresponding pair is obtained from the one on the smaller group by appending
  $q, q^3, \dots, q^{2(r-s)-1}$, up to the action of the Weyl group (with $\lvert \varpi \rvert_E = q^{-2}$), and $\Phi$ is the identity
  when $r = s$. Other ranks and parities are not covered by this statement.
- **Split places.** For $E = F \times F$ the pair is a type II pair $(\mathrm{GL}_n(F), \mathrm{GL}_m(F))$. For the untwisted
  model of the [split case](weil-representation-of-unitary-dual-pairs.md) with Mínguez's normalization, the
  unramified correspondence is the case of unramified representations of Mínguez's explicit correspondence
  (contragredient, together with the parameter of the trivial representation of $\mathrm{GL}_{m-n}(F)$ when $n \le m$). If
  the model is twisted by characters $\alpha \circ \det$ of the group of $\pi$ and $\alpha' \circ \det$ of the other group (as the
  splitting characters may require), the lift of $\pi$ becomes $\alpha' \otimes \theta(\pi \otimes \alpha^{-1})$, where $\theta$ is the lift for the
  untwisted model; at equal ranks with a twist $\chi(\det g')$ on one side this is Liu's $\pi^\vee \otimes \chi$ (Corollary
  A.6 (2)).

## Expansion

A good expansion has pages for the lattice model and the vacuum vector, for the one-sided generation
theorem in the stated range (and separately, with sources, for other parities and the dyadic case), for
the correspondence of unramified representations among corresponding pairs, for the explicit matching of
Satake parameters with its normalization, and for the split case, each with a precise statement and the
sources below, with the Satake isomorphism for hyperspecial subgroups as a prerequisite.
