# Functional equation and first-term identities for Siegel Eisenstein series

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the inputs of the proof of the
[Siegel–Weil formula](siegel-weil-formula-convergent-range.md) for anisotropic Hermitian spaces of
dimension $m$ with $0 < m \le n$, where the Siegel–Weil point $s_0 = \tfrac{m-n}{2}$ is $\le 0$ and the pole
theorem for $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 0$ does not apply directly. Let $G_n = \mathrm{U}(W_n)$ with $W_n$ split of
dimension $2n$, $I_n(s, \chi_V)$ the degenerate principal series and $E(s, \Phi)$ the
[Siegel Eisenstein series](siegel-eisenstein-series-on-unitary-groups.md).

- **Functional equation.** The global intertwining operator $M(s) \colon I_n(s, \chi_V) \to I_n(-s, \chi_V)$, its
  normalization by Hecke $L$-functions, the functional equation $E(s, \Phi) = E(-s, M(s)\Phi)$, and the action
  of the normalized operator on the submodule generated by the Siegel–Weil sections: its normalizing
  scalar has a zero at $s_0$ while the series has at most a simple pole at $-s_0 \ge 0$.
- **First-term identity.** For $m < n$, the residue at $-s_0$ of the Siegel Eisenstein series of a section
  coming from the complementary space $V'$ of dimension $2n - m$ (in the same Witt tower class) is
  proportional to the regularized theta integral of $V'$, which relates the value at $s_0$ to an ordinary
  theta integral of $V$ (Kudla–Rallis; Ichino; Yamana's Theorem 5.2 and Corollary 5.4).
- **Constants.** The comparison of Fourier coefficients of rank $m$ that fixes the constant $2$ (Yamana
  §6), the local multiplicity-one statements for the quotients of $I_n(s_0, \chi_{V,v})$ used there, and the
  case $m = n$, where $s_0 = 0$ and the identity is due to Ichino.

A good expansion has pages for the global intertwining operator and the functional equation, for the
first-term identity relating residues at $-s_0$ to the complementary space, for the local quotient
structure used, and for the rank-$m$ Fourier coefficient comparison, each with a precise statement and
the sources below.
