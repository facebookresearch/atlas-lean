# Vanishing of `H²` for `Γ₀(M)` when `6` is a unit

For every level `M`, including
`M = 0` (where `Γ₀(0)` is the subgroup of upper triangular matrices of `SL(2, ℤ)`), and every
representation `A` of `Γ₀(M)` over a commutative ring `k` in which `6` is a unit, the second
cohomology group `H²(Γ₀(M), A)` is zero.

## Sources

- I. N. Sanov, *A property of a representation of a free group*, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR (N.S.) 57 (1947), 657–659 (the matrices `!![1, 2; 0, 1]` and `!![1, 0; 2, 1]` generate a free subgroup of `SL(2, ℤ)`); K. S. Brown, *Cohomology of Groups*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 87, Springer, 1982, Section III.9 (restriction followed by corestriction is multiplication by the index).
