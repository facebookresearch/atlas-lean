# Herbrand quotients of lattices in a real representation of a cyclic group

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group and $V$ a finite-dimensional real vector space on which $G$ acts
$\mathbb{R}$-linearly. Let $M$ and $N$ be $G$-stable full lattices in $V$, that is, $G$-stable subgroups
each generated by an $\mathbb{R}$-basis of $V$. If one of the Herbrand quotients
$h(M) = |\hat H^0(G, M)| / |\hat H^{-1}(G, M)|$ and $h(N)$ is defined, then so is the other, and
$h(M) = h(N)$.

Milne's proof has three steps. The maps $M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{R} \to V$ and
$N \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{R} \to V$ are isomorphisms of $\mathbb{R}[G]$-modules, and two finite-dimensional
$\mathbb{Q}[G]$-modules that become isomorphic over $\mathbb{R}$ are already isomorphic, because the
determinant of a generic $\mathbb{Q}$-linear combination of a basis of the $\mathbb{Q}[G]$-homomorphisms
is a nonzero polynomial with rational coefficients (Lemma VII.3.2); so
$M \otimes \mathbb{Q} \cong N \otimes \mathbb{Q}$. Such an isomorphism, multiplied by a common
denominator, embeds $M$ into $N$ with finite cokernel, and
[Herbrand quotients agree across a map with finite kernel and cokernel](herbrand-quotient-finite-kernel-cokernel.md)
(Lemmas VII.3.4 and VII.3.5).
