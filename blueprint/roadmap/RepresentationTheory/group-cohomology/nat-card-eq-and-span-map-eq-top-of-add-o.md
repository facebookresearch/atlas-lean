# A Tate-style cardinality and generation criterion in degree two

Let `G` be a finite group, `X` a `ℤ`-linear representation of `G`, and `u ∈
H²(G, X)` an element of additive order `|G|`. Suppose that for every
subgroup `S`, `H²(S, X)` is finite with `|H²(S, X)| ≤ |S|`, and that there
are `ℤ`-linear maps `cor_S : H²(S, X) → H²(G, X)` with `cor_S (res_S x) = [G
: S] • x` for all `x ∈ H²(G, X)`, where `res_S` is restriction. Then `|H²(S,
X)| = |S|` for every subgroup `S`, and the restriction of `u` to `S`
generates `H²(S, X)` as an abelian group.
