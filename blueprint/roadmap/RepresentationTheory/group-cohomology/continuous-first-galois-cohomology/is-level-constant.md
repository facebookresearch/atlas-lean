# Level-constant cochain

Let `G` be a group with a homomorphism `r : G → Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)`. A function `f :
G → X` is level constant when there is a finite-dimensional intermediate
field `ℚ ⊆ F ⊆ ℚ̄` such that `f (g * s) = f g` for all `g, s ∈ G` with `r s`
in the fixing subgroup of `F`; that is, `f` factors through a finite level.
