# A finite generation criterion for ℓ-adic cohomology

Let $G$ be a profinite group, $\ell$ a prime number and $T$ a topological $G$-module which is a finitely generated
$\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-module with its natural topology, on which $G$ acts $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-linearly. Then for every
$n \ge 0$ the $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-module $H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G, T)$ of [continuous cohomology](continuous-cohomology.md)
is finitely generated if and only if

$$
H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G, T) / \ell\, H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G, T)
$$

is finite.

## Depends on

- [Continuous cohomology](continuous-cohomology.md), for $H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G, T)$
- [The ring of p-adic integers](../../../../NumberTheory/p-adic-numbers/p-adic-integers.md), for $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$

## Proof depends on

- [No divisible subgroups modulo finitely generated submodules](no-divisible-subgroups-modulo-finitely-generated-submodules.md),
  applied to the submodule $Y$ generated by lifts of generators of the quotient by $\ell$, modulo which the
  cohomology is $\ell$-divisible
