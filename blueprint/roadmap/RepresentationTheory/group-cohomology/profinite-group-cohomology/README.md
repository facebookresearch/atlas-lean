# Cohomology of profinite groups

This folder covers the cohomology of profinite groups that the pages on Galois cohomology use: cohomology with
coefficients in discrete modules and continuous cochain cohomology with coefficients in topological modules, its
functoriality, cup products and spectral sequences, cohomological dimension, and the abstract duality theorems
from which local and global Tate duality are derived. It follows Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, Chapters I–III and
VI, and Milne's *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, Chapter I, §§0–1.

Throughout, $G$ is a profinite group. A discrete $G$-module is an abelian group $A$ with the discrete topology on
which $G$ acts continuously; its cohomology $H^n(G, A)$ is defined by continuous cochains and is the continuous
cochain cohomology of $A$. The first folder treats discrete coefficients, the second topological coefficients
such as finitely generated $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-modules and $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-vector spaces; the pages below them
treat cohomological dimension and duality.
