# Tate cohomology of finite modules over groups of order divisible by ℓ^∞

Let $G$ be a profinite group and $A$ a finite discrete $G$-module. Assume that $\ell^\infty \mid \#G$ for every prime
number $\ell$ dividing the order of $A$, that is, for every $k$ some open subgroup of $G$ has index divisible by
$\ell^k$. Then, for the [Tate cohomology](tate-cohomology-of-profinite-groups.md) of $G$,

$$
\hat H^0(G, A) = A^G \qquad \text{and} \qquad \hat H^i(G, A) = 0 \quad \text{for all } i \le -1.
$$

## Depends on

- [Tate cohomology of profinite groups](tate-cohomology-of-profinite-groups.md), for $\hat H^i(G, A)$

## Proof depends on

- [The long exact Tate cohomology sequence of level-compact modules](long-exact-tate-cohomology-sequence-of-level-compact-modules.md),
  applied to $0 \to 0 \to 0 \to A$ once all universal norm groups $N_U A$ vanish, the norm $N_{U/V}$ being
  multiplication by $(U : V)$ on $A = A^V = A^U$ for small $U$
- [Level-compact modules](level-compact-modules.md), for $A$, finite and discrete, as a level-compact module
- [Tate cohomology of profinite groups](tate-cohomology-of-profinite-groups.md), for Lemma (1.9.8) or (1.9.11),
  which give $\hat H^0(G, A) = A^G$
