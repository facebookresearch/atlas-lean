# Simple modules over pro-p-groups

Let $p$ be a prime number and $G$ a pro-$p$-group, a profinite group whose finite quotients by open normal subgroups
are $p$-groups. Every discrete simple $p$-primary $G$-module $A$ (a nonzero discrete $G$-module whose elements have
$p$-power order and whose only submodules are $0$ and $A$) is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ with the trivial
action of $G$. In particular, a $p$-primary discrete $G$-module $A$ is zero if and only if $A^G = 0$.

Consequently, every finite $p$-primary $G$-module, for instance a finite module killed by $p$ over a finite
$p$-group, has a composition series whose factors are all isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ with the trivial
action, since the factors of a composition series are simple.
