# Cohomology with discrete coefficients

The cohomology $H^n(G, A)$ of a profinite group $G$ with coefficients in a discrete $G$-module $A$, following
Neukirch–Schmidt–Wingberg, Chapter I, §§2–7 and §9, Chapter II, §4, and Chapter VI, §2: its description by the
finite quotients of $G$, the long exact sequence, induced modules and Shapiro's lemma, the change of group, cup
products and the Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence, the cohomology of procyclic groups, pro-$p$-groups, and Tate
cohomology of profinite groups.
