# The action of the quotient on the cohomology of a normal subgroup

Let $G$ be a profinite group, $H$ a closed normal subgroup and $A$ a discrete $G$-module. For $\sigma \in G$ the
[conjugation](inflation-restriction-and-conjugation.md) $\sigma_* \colon H^n(H, A) \to H^n(H, A)$ (here
$\sigma H \sigma^{-1} = H$ and $\sigma A = A$) makes the [cohomology](../cohomology-with-discrete-coefficients.md)
group $H^n(H, A)$, $n \ge 0$, a discrete $G$-module. Then $H$ acts trivially on $H^n(H, A)$, so that $H^n(H, A)$ is a
discrete $G/H$-module; in particular the conjugation $\sigma_* \colon H^n(G, A) \to H^n(G, A)$ is the identity for
every $\sigma \in G$.

## Depends on

- [Inflation, restriction and conjugation](inflation-restriction-and-conjugation.md), for $\sigma_*$

## Proof depends on

- [Functoriality and the connecting homomorphisms](functoriality-and-connecting-homomorphisms.md), for
  $\delta \circ \sigma_* = \sigma_* \circ \delta$, along which the statement shifts from $H^0(H, A) = A^H$, where it
  is clear, to all $n$
- [The long exact cohomology sequence](../long-exact-cohomology-sequence.md) and
  [Induced modules are acyclic](../induced-modules-are-acyclic.md), for the dimension shifting
