# Restriction after corestriction

Let $G$ be a profinite group, $U$ an open normal subgroup and $A$ a discrete $G$-module. Then for every $n \ge 0$, on
[cohomology](../cohomology-with-discrete-coefficients.md),

$$
\operatorname{res}^G_U \circ \operatorname{cor}^U_G = N_{G/U} = \sum_{\sigma \in G/U} \sigma_* \colon
H^n(U, A) \longrightarrow H^n(U, A),
$$

the sum of the conjugations $\sigma_*$ over a system of representatives of $G/U$ (a conjugation by an element of $U$
being the identity on $H^n(U, A)$).

## Depends on

- [Inflation, restriction and conjugation](inflation-restriction-and-conjugation.md), for $\operatorname{res}$ and
  $\sigma_*$
- [Corestriction for open subgroups](corestriction.md), for $\operatorname{cor}$
- [The action of the quotient on the cohomology of a normal subgroup](action-of-the-quotient-on-the-cohomology-of-a-normal-subgroup.md),
  for the independence of the sum from the representatives

## Proof depends on

- [The double coset formula](double-coset-formula.md), for the case $V = U$, where the double cosets are the cosets
  of $U$

- J. Neukirch, A. Schmidt, K. Wingberg, *Cohomology of Number Fields*, 2nd ed., electronic edition, version 2.3
  (May 2020), <https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~schmidt/NSW2e/>: Chapter I, §5, Corollary (1.5.7), second formula,
  and the remark after it, p. 51.
