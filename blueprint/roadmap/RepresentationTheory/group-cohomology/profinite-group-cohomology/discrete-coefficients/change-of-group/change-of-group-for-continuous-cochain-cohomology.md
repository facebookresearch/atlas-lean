# Change of the group for continuous cochain cohomology

This node is still to be broken down. It covers inflation, restriction and corestriction for the
[continuous cohomology](../../continuous-cochain-cohomology/continuous-cohomology.md) $H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G, A)$ of a
profinite group $G$ with coefficients in a topological $G$-module $A$, defined on continuous cochains by the formulas
used for discrete modules, and their properties, which are the same as for discrete coefficients.

## Expansion

This node is still to be broken down. A good expansion states and links:

- the maps induced by compatible pairs on continuous cochains, and inflation and restriction as their cases, as in
  [Inflation, restriction and conjugation](inflation-restriction-and-conjugation.md);
- corestriction for an open subgroup by the cochain formula of
  [The cochain formula for corestriction](cochain-formula-for-corestriction.md), with
  $\operatorname{cor} \circ \operatorname{res} = (G : U)$;
- their compatibility with the connecting homomorphisms of the
  [long exact sequence for continuous cohomology](../../continuous-cochain-cohomology/long-exact-sequence-for-continuous-cohomology.md),
  and their agreement with the maps for discrete coefficients when $A$ is discrete.
