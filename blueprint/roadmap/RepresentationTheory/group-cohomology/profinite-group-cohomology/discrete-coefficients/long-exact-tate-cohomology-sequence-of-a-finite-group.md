# The long exact Tate cohomology sequence of a finite group

Let $G$ be a finite group and $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ an exact sequence of $G$-modules. There are connecting
homomorphisms $\delta \colon \hat H^n(G, C) \to \hat H^{n+1}(G, A)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ between the
[Tate cohomology groups](../../tate-cohomology-and-the-herbrand-quotient/tate-cohomology.md), and the sequence,
unbounded in both directions,

$$
\cdots \to \hat H^n(G, A) \to \hat H^n(G, B) \to \hat H^n(G, C) \xrightarrow{\delta} \hat H^{n+1}(G, A) \to \cdots
\qquad (n \in \mathbb{Z})
$$

is exact. It is obtained as the [long exact cohomology sequence](long-exact-cohomology-sequence.md) is, from the
complete (unrestricted) standard complex in all degrees $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
