# The inhomogeneous-to-periodic cochain comparison has the cyclic degree-three sum

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. Assume g generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The full
cochain map C^*(G,A)→C^*_{per}(A,g) is induced by the explicit periodic-to-bar resolution map.
Each periodic term is A, with alternating differentials D_g=ρ(g)-id in even source degrees and
N_G in odd source degrees. Its degree-three component sends z to Σ_hz(g,h,g).
