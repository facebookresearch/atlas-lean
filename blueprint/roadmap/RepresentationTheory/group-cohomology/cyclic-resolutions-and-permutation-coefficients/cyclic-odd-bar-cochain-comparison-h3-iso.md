# The explicit odd cochain map induces the degree-three periodic cohomology isomorphism

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. Assume g generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The
cochain homotopy equivalence induces H³(G,A)≅H³(C^*_{per}(A,g)). Its forward morphism is the
degree-three cohomology map of the explicit comparison z↦Σ_hz(g,h,g), with periodic
differentials D_g and N_G.
