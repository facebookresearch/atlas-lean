# Cyclic-even quotient classes agree exactly modulo the representation norm

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in the
given universe u. For any g∈G, let x,y∈ker(ρ(g)-id) and π be projection to ker(ρ(g)-id)/im(N_G),
where N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h). Then π(x)=π(y) if and only if x-y∈im(N_G), with the difference taken in
the coefficient module A.
