# The full cyclic isomorphism sends a right-associated triple cup to its invariant class

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group generated by g, and A,B,C k-linear
representations in universe u. For one-cocycles x,y,z, apply the explicit full-resolution
isomorphism in degree three for D=A⊗(B⊗C), followed by its quotient identification. The image of
[x∪(y∪z)] is the class of Σ_hx(g)⊗ρ_{B⊗C}(g)(y(h)⊗ρ_C(h)z(g)) in ker(N_D)/im(ρ_D(g)-id).
