# The explicit odd comparison is a homotopy equivalence of full cochain complexes

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. Assume g generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The
inhomogeneous cochain complex C^*(G,A) is homotopy equivalent to the periodic complex with terms
A and differentials ρ(g)-id in even source degrees and N_G in odd source degrees. Its forward
map is the explicit cochain comparison whose degree-three component is z↦Σ_hz(g,h,g).
