# The degree-three cyclic seed defines an augmented periodic-to-bar chain map

Let k be a commutative ring and G a finite abelian group generated by g, in universe u. The
chain map from the periodic resolution of the trivial k-representation to its bar resolution
sends the degree-zero basis to [], degree one to [g], degree two to Σ_h[h|g], and degree three
to Σ_h[g|h|g]. The recursively extended components commute with every differential and the
augmentations.
