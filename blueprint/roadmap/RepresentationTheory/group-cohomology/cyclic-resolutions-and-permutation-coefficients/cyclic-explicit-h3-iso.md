# The explicit cyclic sum yields the degree-three odd homology isomorphism

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). Assume g
generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The full seeded comparison
gives an isomorphism E_g:H³(G,A)≅H(A --D_g→ A --N_G→ A). Composing with the concrete homology
quotient identification sends a class [z] to [Σ_hz(g,h,g)] in Q_g(A).
