# The quadratic cyclic cup invariant is the generator tensor summand

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group generated by g with order(g)=2, and A,B
k-linear representations in universe u. For one-cocycles x∈Z¹(G,A), y∈Z¹(G,B), the invariant of
their cup cocycle is Σ_{h∈G}x(h)⊗h•y(g)=x(g)⊗g•y(g), as an element of A⊗_k B.
