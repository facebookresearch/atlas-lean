# The periodic and bar resolutions are homotopy equivalent through the explicit odd comparison

Let k be a commutative ring and G a finite abelian group generated by g, in universe u. The
complexes of the periodic resolution P and bar resolution B form a homotopy equivalence P≃B
whose forward map is the explicit seeded chain comparison. The reverse resolution comparison
composes with it to maps homotopic to the respective identities.
