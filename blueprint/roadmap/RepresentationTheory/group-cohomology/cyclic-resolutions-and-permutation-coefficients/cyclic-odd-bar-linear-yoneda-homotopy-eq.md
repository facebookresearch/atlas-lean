# Contravariant coefficient Hom reverses the periodic-to-bar homotopy equivalence

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. Assume g generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. For the
periodic resolution P and bar resolution B, the coefficient Hom cochain complexes Hom_G(B,A) and
Hom_G(P,A) are homotopy equivalent. The forward map Hom_G(B,A)→Hom_G(P,A) is precomposition by
the explicit chain comparison P→B, reversing its direction.
