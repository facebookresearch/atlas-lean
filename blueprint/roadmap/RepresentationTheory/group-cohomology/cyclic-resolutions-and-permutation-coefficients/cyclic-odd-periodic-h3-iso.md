# Periodic degree-three cohomology is the homology of generator difference followed by norm

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). For every
g∈G, the alternating periodic complex has degree-three homology isomorphic to H(A --D_g→ A
--N_G→ A). Its concrete quotient is Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g); this identification uses the odd
index three.
