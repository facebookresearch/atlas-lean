# The odd short-complex comparison maps third cohomology to periodic homology

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). For any g∈G,
the map H³(G,A)→H(A --D_g→ A --N_G→ A) is induced by the cyclic-sum morphism C²→C³→C⁴. Its three
component maps sum t(h,g), z(g,h,g), and w(a,g,h,g) over the indicated group variables.
