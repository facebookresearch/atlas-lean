# Invariant descent equals the full-resolution isomorphism followed by the quotient identification

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). Assume g
generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. Let E_g:H³(G,A)≅H(A --D_g→ A
--N_G→ A) be the explicit full-resolution isomorphism and q:H(A --D_g→ A --N_G→ A)≅Q_g(A) the
concrete homology identification. Then F_g=q.hom∘E_g.hom as k-linear maps H³(G,A)→Q_g(A), where
F_g([z])=[Σ_hz(g,h,g)].
