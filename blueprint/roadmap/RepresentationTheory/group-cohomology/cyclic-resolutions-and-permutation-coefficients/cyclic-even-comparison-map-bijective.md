# The explicit cyclic degree-two comparison is bijective

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in the
given universe u. If g generates G, meaning every h∈G belongs to the subgroup of powers of g,
the comparison H²(G,A)→H(N_G:A→A,ρ(g)-id:A→A) is bijective. Through the canonical quotient
identification, it sends [z] to [Σ_{h∈G}z(h,g)] in ker(ρ(g)-id)/im(N_G).
