# The explicit odd degree-three comparison is bijective for a cyclic generator

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). Assume g
generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The short-complex comparison
H³(G,A)→H(A --D_g→ A --N_G→ A) is bijective. It agrees with the isomorphism induced by the
seeded full periodic-to-bar resolution comparison.
