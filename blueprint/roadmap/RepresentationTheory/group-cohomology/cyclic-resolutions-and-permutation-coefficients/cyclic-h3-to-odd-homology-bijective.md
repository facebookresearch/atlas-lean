# The quotient-valued cyclic third-cohomology invariant is bijective

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). Assume g
generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. The k-linear map
F_g:H³(G,A)→Q_g(A), [z]↦[Σ_hz(g,h,g)], is bijective. Its codomain is the norm kernel modulo
generator differences, and the full-resolution comparison supplies its inverse.
