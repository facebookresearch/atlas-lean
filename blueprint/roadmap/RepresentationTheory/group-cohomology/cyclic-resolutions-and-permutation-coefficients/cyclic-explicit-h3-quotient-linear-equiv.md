# The explicit cyclic third-cohomology invariant is a linear equivalence onto the odd quotient

Let k be a commutative ring, G a finite abelian group, and A a k-linear G-representation, in
universe u. For g∈G put D_g=ρ(g)-id, N_G=Σ_{h∈G}ρ(h), and Q_g(A)=ker(N_G)/im(D_g). Assume g
generates G, so every element belongs to its subgroup of powers. There is a k-linear equivalence
H³(G,A)≃Q_g(A) whose forward map takes [z] to [Σ_hz(g,h,g)]. It is the explicit full-resolution
homology isomorphism followed by the concrete quotient identification.
