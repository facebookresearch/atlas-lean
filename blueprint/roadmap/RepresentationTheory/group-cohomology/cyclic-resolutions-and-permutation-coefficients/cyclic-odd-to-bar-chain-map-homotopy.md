# The explicit periodic-to-bar map is homotopic to the identity resolution lift

Let k be a commutative ring and G a finite abelian group generated by g, in universe u. The
explicit chain map P→B from the periodic to the bar resolution is homotopic to the
projective-resolution lift of id_k:P→B. Both maps lift the identity of the trivial
k-representation at the augmentations.
