# Consecutive periodic-to-bar components extend the degree-three seed

Let k be a commutative ring and G a finite abelian group generated by g, in universe u. Let P be
its periodic projective resolution and B its bar resolution. For each n≥0, the construction
gives F_{n+2}:P_{n+2}→B_{n+2} and F_{n+3}:P_{n+3}→B_{n+3} satisfying F_{n+3}≫d_B=d_P≫F_{n+2}. At
n=0 their basis images are Σ_h[h|g] and Σ_h[g|h|g]; projectivity extends the pair at each
successor.
