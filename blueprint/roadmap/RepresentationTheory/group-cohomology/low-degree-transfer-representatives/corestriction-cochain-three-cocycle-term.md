# The three-cocycle identity on a Schreier scan

The three-cocycle identity on a Schreier scan.

For successive factors a₀,a₁,a₂,a₃, the H-three-cocycle identity rearranges as f(a₀a₁,a₂,a₃) +
f(a₀,a₁,a₂a₃) = ρ(a₀)f(a₁,a₂,a₃) + f(a₀,a₁a₂,a₃) + f(a₀,a₁,a₂). Weighting by ρ(t⁻¹) gives the
stated scan formula.
