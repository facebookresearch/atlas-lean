# Kummer theory: size of the first cohomology

Let `L/K` be a finite Galois extension and `p` a natural number. Let `Δ ⊆
Kˣ` be the subgroup of elements that are `p`-th powers in `L`. Then
`H¹(Gal(L/K), μ_p(L))` has the same cardinality as the quotient of `Δ` by
its subgroup `Δ ∩ (Kˣ) ^ p` of `p`-th powers in `K`. Cardinalities are
natural-number cardinalities, which are `0` for infinite sets.
