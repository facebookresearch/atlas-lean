# Roots-of-unity representation of the Galois group

Let `K ⊆ L` be a field extension and `p` a natural number. `kummerRep K L p`
is the `ℤ`-linear representation of the group `L ≃ₐ[K] L` of `K`-algebra
automorphisms of `L` on the group `μ_p(L)` of `p`-th roots of unity of `L`
(written additively), with the natural action.
