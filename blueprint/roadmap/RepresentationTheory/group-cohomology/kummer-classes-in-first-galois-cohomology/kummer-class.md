# Kummer class

Let `K ⊆ L` be a field extension, `a ∈ Kˣ`, and `α ∈ Lˣ` with `α ^ p = a` in
`L`. The Kummer class of `a` is the class in `H¹(L ≃ₐ[K] L, μ_p(L))` of the
1-cocycle `σ ↦ σ(α) / α`, which takes values in the `p`-th roots of unity of
`L`.

Here `K ⊆ L` is a field extension, and the Galois group `L ≃ₐ[K] L` acts on
the group of `p`-th roots of unity of `L`.
