# Kummer theory: size of the Galois group

Kummer theory: for a finite Galois extension `L/K` with abelian Galois group
of exponent dividing `n`, with `K` containing a primitive `n`-th root of
unity, the cardinal of the Galois group equals the cardinal of the Kummer
quotient `Δ / Kˣⁿ`, where `Δ` is the subgroup of `Kˣ` of elements that
become `n`-th powers in `L`.
