# Kummer homomorphism

Let `K ⊆ L` be a field extension and `p` a natural number. The Kummer
homomorphism sends an element `a` of `powerSubgroup K L p` (the elements of
`Kˣ` that are `p`-th powers in `L`) to its Kummer class, the class of `σ ↦
σ(α) / α` in `H¹(L ≃ₐ[K] L, μ_p(L))` for any `α ∈ Lˣ` with `α ^ p = a`. It
is a group homomorphism from `powerSubgroup K L p` to the additive group
`H¹`, written multiplicatively.
