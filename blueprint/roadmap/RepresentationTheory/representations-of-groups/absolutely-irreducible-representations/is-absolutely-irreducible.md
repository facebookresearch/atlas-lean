# Absolutely irreducible representation

Let `k` be a field, `G` a group and `ρ` a representation of `G` on a
`k`-vector space `W`. Then `ρ` is absolutely irreducible if for every field
extension `k'` of `k` (every field `k'` with a `k`-algebra structure), the
base change of `ρ` to `k'`, the representation of `G` on `k' ⊗[k] W`, is
irreducible.
