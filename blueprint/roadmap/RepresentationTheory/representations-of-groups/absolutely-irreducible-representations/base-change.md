# Base change of a representation

Let `k` be a field, `G` a group, `ρ` a representation of `G` on a `k`-vector
space `V`, and `k'` a commutative ring with a `k`-algebra structure. The
base change of `ρ` to `k'` is the representation of `G` on the `k'`-module
`k' ⊗[k] V` in which `g` acts by the `k'`-linear extension `id ⊗ ρ g` of `ρ
g`.
