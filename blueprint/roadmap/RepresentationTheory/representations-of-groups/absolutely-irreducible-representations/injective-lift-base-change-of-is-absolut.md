# Injectivity of the linear extension of an intertwining embedding

Let `F ⊆ k₂` be a field extension, `G` a group, `ρ` an absolutely
irreducible representation of `G` on an `F`-vector space `V`, and `τ` a
representation of `G` on a `k₂`-vector space `W` (viewed also as an
`F`-vector space compatibly). If `ι : V → W` is an injective `F`-linear map
intertwining `ρ` and `τ` (`ι (ρ g v) = τ g (ι v)` for all `g` and `v`), then
its `k₂`-linear extension `k₂ ⊗[F] V → W`, `a ⊗ v ↦ a • ι v`, is injective.
