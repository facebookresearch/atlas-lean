# Proposition 7.1

Let $V, W$ be two unitary representations in $\operatorname{Rep} G$. If $V^{K\text{-fin}} \cong W^{K\text{-fin}}$ as Harish-Chandra modules, then $V \cong W$ as unitary representations. In other words, infinitesimally equivalent unitary representations in $\operatorname{Rep} G$ are isomorphic.
