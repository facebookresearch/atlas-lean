# Lemma 7.2 (Dixmier)

Let $A$ be a countable-dimensional $\mathbb{C}$-algebra and $M$ a simple $A$-module. Then $\operatorname{End}_{A}(M)=\mathbb{C}$. In particular, the center $Z$ of $A$ acts on $M$ by a character $\chi: Z \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$.
