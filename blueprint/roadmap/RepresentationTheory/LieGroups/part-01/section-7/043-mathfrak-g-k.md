# Corollary 7.3 (Schur's lemma for $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-modules)

Any endomorphism of an irreducible $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-module $M$ is a scalar. Thus the center $Z(\mathfrak{g})$ of $U(\mathfrak{g})$ acts on $M$ by an infinitesimal character $\chi: Z(\mathfrak{g}) \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$.
