# Proposition 16.2

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a Noetherian abelian category with enough projectives and finite-dimensional Hom spaces over an algebraically closed field $k$. Then (i) Let $I$ be the set labeling the isomorphism classes of indecomposable projectives $P_{i}$ of $\mathcal{C}$. Then the isomorphism classes of simple objects $L_{i}$ of $\mathcal{C}$ are labeled by the same set $I$, and $\dim \operatorname{Hom}(P_{i}, L_{j})=\delta_{ij}$, $i, j \in I$. (ii) For $M \in \mathcal{C}$ of finite length, the multiplicities $[M: L_{i}]$ equal $\dim \operatorname{Hom}(P_{i}, M)$.
