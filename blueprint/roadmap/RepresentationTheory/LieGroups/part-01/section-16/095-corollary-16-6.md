# Corollary 16.6

(i) For every $\mu \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$, there exists dominant $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ and a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$ such that $\operatorname{Hom}(V \otimes M_{\lambda-\rho}, L_{\mu}) \neq 0$. Thus $\mathcal{O}$ has enough projectives. (ii) Every projective object $P$ of $\mathcal{O}$ is a free $U(\mathfrak{n}_{-})$-module.
