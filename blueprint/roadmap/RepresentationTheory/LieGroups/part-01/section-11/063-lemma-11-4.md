# Lemma 11.4

Let $G$ be a complex reflection group, $f_1, \ldots, f_r \in \mathbb{C}[V]^G$ homogeneous of positive degree forming a minimal set of homogeneous generators of the ideal $I$. Then $f_1, \ldots, f_r$ are algebraically independent.
