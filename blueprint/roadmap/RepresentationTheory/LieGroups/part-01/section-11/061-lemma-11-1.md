# Lemma 11.1

Let $G \subset GL(V)$ be a finite subgroup, and $I \subset \mathbb{C}[V]$ be the ideal generated by positive degree elements of $\mathbb{C}[V]^G$. Let $f_1, \ldots, f_r \in \mathbb{C}[V]^G$ be homogeneous generators of $I$. Then the algebra $\mathbb{C}[V]^G$ is generated by $f_1, \ldots, f_r$; in particular, it is finitely generated.
