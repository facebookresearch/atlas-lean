# Lemma 11.3

Assume that $G$ is a complex reflection group. Let $I$ be the ideal in $\mathbb{C}[V]$ generated by positive degree elements of $\mathbb{C}[V]^G$, $F_1, \ldots, F_m \in \mathbb{C}[V]^G$ be homogeneous, and suppose that $F_1$ does not belong to the ideal in $\mathbb{C}[V]^G$ generated by $F_2, \ldots, F_m$. Suppose $g_i \in \mathbb{C}[V]$ for $1 \leq i \leq m$ are homogeneous and $\sum_{i=1}^{m} g_i F_i = 0$. Then $g_1 \in I$.
