# Corollary 14.3

For any finite-dimensional irreducible $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$ we have $\dim \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}}(V, U_{\chi})=\dim V[0]$, where $\mathfrak{g}$ acts on $U_{\chi}$ by the adjoint action. Thus $U_{\chi}$ is a Harish-Chandra $\mathfrak{g}$-bimodule.
