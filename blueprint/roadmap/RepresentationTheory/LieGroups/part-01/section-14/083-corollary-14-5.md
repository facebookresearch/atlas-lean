# Corollary 14.5

(i) Every irreducible $\mathfrak{g}$-bimodule $M$ locally finite under the adjoint $\mathfrak{g}$-action is a quotient of $V \otimes U_{\chi}$ for some finite-dimensional irreducible $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$ with trivial right action of $\mathfrak{g}$, where $\chi$ is the right infinitesimal character of $M$. (ii) Every irreducible $\mathfrak{g}$-bimodule locally finite under the adjoint $\mathfrak{g}$-action is a Harish-Chandra bimodule.
