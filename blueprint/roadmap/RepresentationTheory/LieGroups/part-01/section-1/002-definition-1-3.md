# Definition 1.3

A continuous representation is called unitary if $V$ is a Hilbert space and for all $g \in G$, the operator $\pi(g): V \rightarrow V$ is unitary; in other words, $\pi$ lands in the unitary group $U(V) \subset \operatorname{Aut}(V)$.
