# Lemma 17.1

The restriction of the adjoint representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ to its principal $\mathfrak{sl}_{2}$-subalgebra is isomorphic to $L_{2m_{1}} \oplus \ldots \oplus L_{2m_{r}}$ for appropriate $m_{i} \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.
