# Proposition 19.5

We have an isomorphism $\xi: \mathbf{M}(\lambda, \mu) \rightarrow C_{\lambda-\rho, \mu-\rho}^{\infty}(G / B)^{\text{fin}}$ as Harish-Chandra bimodules. Namely, $\xi(\Phi_{v, \ell})$ is the matrix coefficient $\psi_{v, \ell}(g):=(v, g \ell), g \in G_{c}$.
