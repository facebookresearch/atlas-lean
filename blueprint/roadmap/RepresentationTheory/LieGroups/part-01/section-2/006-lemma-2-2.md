# Lemma 2.2

The natural $K$-invariant linear map $\xi: \oplus_{\rho \in \operatorname{Irr} K} \operatorname{Hom}(\rho, V) \otimes \rho \rightarrow V^{\text{fin}}$ defined by $\xi(h \otimes u) = h(u)$ is an isomorphism.
