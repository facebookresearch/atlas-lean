# Lemma 4.5

There exists a sequence $\phi_{n} \in C_{c}(G)$ such that $\phi_{n} \rightarrow \delta_{1}$ in the weak topology as $n \rightarrow \infty$. Moreover, if $G$ is a Lie group, we can choose $\phi_{n} \in C_{c}^{\infty}(G)$.
