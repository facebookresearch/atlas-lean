# Corollary 4.6

$C_{c}(G)$ is sequentially dense in $\operatorname{Meas}_{c}(G)$. For Lie groups, $C_{c}^{\infty}(G)$ is sequentially dense in $\operatorname{Meas}_{c}(G)$.
