# Proposition 4.1 (Plancherel's theorem for compact groups)

Let $K$ be a compact group and $f_{1}, f_{2} \in L^{2}(K)$. Then $\left(f_{1}, f_{2}\right)=\sum_{\rho \in \operatorname{Irr} K} \operatorname{dim} \rho \cdot \operatorname{Tr}\left(\pi_{\rho}\left(f_{1}\right) \pi_{\rho}\left(f_{2}\right)^{\dagger}\right)$ and this series is absolutely convergent.
