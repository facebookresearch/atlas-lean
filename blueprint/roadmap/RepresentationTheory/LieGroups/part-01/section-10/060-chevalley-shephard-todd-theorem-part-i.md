# Theorem 10.6 (Chevalley-Shephard-Todd theorem, part I)

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex vector space and $G \subset GL(V)$ be a finite subgroup. Then $\mathbb{C}[V]^G$ is a polynomial algebra if and only if $G$ is a complex reflection group.
