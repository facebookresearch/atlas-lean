# Proposition 15.12

$W_{x}$ is generated by the reflections $s_{\alpha} \in W_{x}$. Moreover, the roots $\alpha$ such that $s_{\alpha} \in W_{x}$ form a root system $R_{x} \subset R$, and $W_{x}$ is the Weyl group of $R_{x}$. The corresponding dual root system $R_{x}^{\vee}$ is a root subsystem of $R^{\vee}$, i.e., $R_{x}^{\vee}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{Z}}(R_{x}^{\vee}) \cap R^{\vee}$.
