# Corollary 15.7

(i) Any $M \in \mathcal{O}$ has a canonical decomposition $M=\oplus_{\chi \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}/W} M(\chi)$ where $M(\chi)$ is the generalized eigenspace of $Z(\mathfrak{g})$ in $M$ with eigenvalue $\chi$, and this direct sum is finite. In other words, $\mathcal{O}=\oplus_{\chi \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}/W} \mathcal{O}_{\chi}$ where $\mathcal{O}_{\chi}$ is the subcategory of $\mathcal{O}$ of modules where every $z \in Z(\mathfrak{g})$ acts with generalized eigenvalue $\chi(z)$. (ii) Each $M \in \mathcal{O}_{\chi}$ has a finite filtration with successive quotients having infinitesimal character $\chi$.
