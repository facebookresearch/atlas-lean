# Definition 15.1

The category $\mathcal{O}=\mathcal{O}_{\mathfrak{g}}$ is the full subcategory of $\mathfrak{g}$-mod, which consists of finitely generated $\mathfrak{g}$-modules $M$ with weight decomposition and $P(M) \subset \cup_{i=1}^{m}(\lambda_{i}-Q_{+})$, where $\lambda_{1}, \ldots, \lambda_{m} \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$.
