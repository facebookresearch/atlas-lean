# Corollary 3.8

If $(V, \pi)$ is a continuous representation of $G$ then the action $G \times V \rightarrow V$ uniquely extends to a continuous bilinear map $\operatorname{Meas}_{c}(G) \times V \rightarrow V$, which gives rise to a continuous unital algebra homomorphism $\pi: \operatorname{Meas}_{c}(G) \rightarrow \operatorname{End}(V)$.
