# Lemma 3.4

$\operatorname{Meas}_{c}^{0}(X)$ is a sequentially dense (in particular, dense) subspace in $\operatorname{Meas}_{c}(X)$, i.e., every element $\mu \in \operatorname{Meas}_{c}(X)$ is the limit of a sequence $\mu_n \in \operatorname{Meas}_{c}^{0}(X)$ in the weak topology.
