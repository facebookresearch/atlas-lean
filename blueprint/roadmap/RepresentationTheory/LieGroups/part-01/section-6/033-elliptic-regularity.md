# Theorem 6.6 (Elliptic regularity)

Suppose $D$ is an elliptic operator with real analytic coefficients on an open set $U \subset \mathbb{R}^{N}$, and $f(x)$ is a smooth solution of the PDE $Df=0$ on $U$. Then $f$ is real analytic on $U$.
