# Definition 6.4

We say that $D$ is elliptic at $x$ if $\sigma(D)_{x}(p) \neq 0$ for nonzero $p \in T_{x}^{*} X$. We say that $D$ is elliptic (on $X$) if it is elliptic at all points $x \in X$.
