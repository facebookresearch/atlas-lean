# Proposition 12.9

Suppose $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n} \in R:=k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ are homogeneous polynomials of positive degree such that the zero set $Z(f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n})$ consists of the origin. Then $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n}$ is a regular sequence.
