# Lemma 12.3

(i) Any homogeneous lift $\{v_{i}^{*}\}$ of a homogeneous basis $\{v_{i}\}$ of $M_{0}$ to $M$ is a system of generators for $M$; in particular, if $\operatorname{dim} M_{0}\lt \infty$ then $M$ is finitely generated. (ii) If in addition $M$ is projective, then $\{v_{i}^{*}\}$ is actually a basis of $M$ (in particular, $M$ is free). Thus if $\operatorname{dim} M_{0}[i]\lt \infty$ for all $i$ then $H(M, q)=H(M_{0}, q) H(S, q)$. In particular, if $S=k[f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n}]$ then $H(M, q)=\frac{H(M_{0}, q)}{\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1-q^{d_{i}})}$.
