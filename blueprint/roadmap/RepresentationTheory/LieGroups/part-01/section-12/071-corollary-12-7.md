# Corollary 12.7

Let $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m} \in k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ be homogeneous polynomials of positive degrees. Let $Z$ be an irreducible component of the zero set $Z(f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m}) \subset k^{n}$. Then $\operatorname{dim}_{\mathfrak{m}_{0}} k[Z] \geq n-m$.
