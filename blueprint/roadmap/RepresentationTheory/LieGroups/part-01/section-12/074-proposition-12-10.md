# Proposition 12.10

Suppose $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n} \in R:=k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ are homogeneous polynomials of degrees $d_{1}, \ldots, d_{n}>0$ such that $R$ is a finitely generated module over $S:=k[f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n}]$. Then this module is free of rank $\prod_{i=1}^{n} d_{i}$. Moreover, the Hilbert polynomial of $R_{0}:=k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]/(f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m})$ (or, equivalently, of a space of free homogeneous generators of this module) is $H(R_{0}, q)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}[d_{i}]_{q}$.
