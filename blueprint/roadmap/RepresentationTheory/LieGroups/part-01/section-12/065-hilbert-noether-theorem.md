# Proposition 12.1 (Hilbert-Noether theorem)

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Let $A$ be a commutative $R$-algebra with an $R$-linear action of a finite group $G$. (i) $A$ is integral over $A^{G}$. In particular, if $A$ is finitely generated then it is module-finite over $A^{G}$. (ii) If $R$ is Noetherian and $A$ is finitely generated then so is $A^{G}$.
