# Theorem 12.2 (Chevalley-Shephard-Todd theorem, part II)

If $G$ is a complex reflection group then for any irreducible representation $\rho$ of $G$, the $\mathbb{C}[V]^{G}$-module $\operatorname{Hom}_{G}(\rho, \mathbb{C}[V])$ is free of rank $\operatorname{dim} \rho$. Thus the $G$-module $R_{0}=\mathbb{C}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]/(f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n})$ is the regular representation and $\prod_{i=1}^{n} d_{i}=|G|$. Moreover, the Hilbert polynomial $H(R_{0}, q):=\sum_{N \geq 0} \operatorname{dim} R_{0}[N] q^{N}$ is $H(R_{0}, q)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}[d_{i}]_{q}$ where $[d]_{q}:=\frac{1-q^{d}}{1-q}=1+q+\ldots+q^{d-1}$.
