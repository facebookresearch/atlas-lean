# Proposition 8.9

For every $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^*$, the Verma module $M_{\lambda}$ has a unique irreducible quotient $L_{\lambda}$. Moreover, $L_{\lambda}$ is a quotient of every highest weight $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$ with highest weight $\lambda$.
