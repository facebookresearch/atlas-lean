# Definition 8.4

Let $I_{\lambda} \in U(\mathfrak{g})$ be the left ideal generated by the elements $h-\lambda(h), h \in \mathfrak{h}$ and $e_{i}, i=1, \ldots, r$. Then the Verma module $M_{\lambda}$ is the quotient $U(\mathfrak{g})/I_{\lambda}$.
