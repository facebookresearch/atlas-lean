# Proposition 8.8

(i) If $V$ is a representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ and $v \in V$ is a vector such that $hv = \lambda(h)v$ for $h \in \mathfrak{h}$ and $e_i v = 0$ then there is a unique homomorphism $\eta: M_{\lambda} \rightarrow V$ such that $\eta(v_{\lambda}) = v$. In particular, if $V$ is generated by such $v \neq 0$ (i.e., $V$ is a highest weight representation with highest weight vector $v$) then $V$ is a quotient of $M_{\lambda}$. (ii) Every highest weight representation has a weight decomposition into finite-dimensional weight subspaces. (iii) Every highest weight representation $V$ has a unique highest weight generator, up to scaling.
