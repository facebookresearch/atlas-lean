# Proposition 18.3

For $M, N \in \mathcal{O}$ and a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$ we have $\operatorname{Hom}_{\text{fin}}(M, V \otimes N)=V \otimes \operatorname{Hom}_{\text{fin}}(M, N)$.
