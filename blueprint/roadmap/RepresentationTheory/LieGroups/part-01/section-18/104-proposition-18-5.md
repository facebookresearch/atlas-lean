# Proposition 18.5

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module. Then for any $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$, we have $\dim \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}}(M_{\lambda}, V \otimes M_{\lambda})=\dim V[0]$. Thus the multiplicity of $V$ in $\operatorname{Hom}_{\text{fin}}(M_{\lambda}, M_{\lambda})$ equals $\dim V[0]$.
