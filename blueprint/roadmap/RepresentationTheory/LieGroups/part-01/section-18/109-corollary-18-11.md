# Corollary 18.11

The category of Harish-Chandra $\mathfrak{g}$-bimodules $HC(\mathfrak{g})$ has a decomposition according to generalized infinitesimal characters: $HC(\mathfrak{g})=\oplus_{\gamma, \lambda} HC_{\chi_{\lambda+\gamma}, \chi_{\lambda}}(\mathfrak{g})$ where $\gamma \in P_{+}$ and $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}/\operatorname{Stab}(\gamma)$ (here $\operatorname{Stab}(\gamma)$ is the stabilizer of $\gamma$ in $W$). In particular, if $(\theta, \chi)$ cannot be written as $(\chi_{\lambda+\gamma}, \chi_{\lambda})$, $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$, $\gamma \in P_{+}$, then $HC_{\theta, \chi}(\mathfrak{g})=0$.
