# Proposition 20.9

(i) $X^{\vee} \in \mathcal{O}$ and has the same character and composition series as $X$. (ii) $(M_{\lambda})^{\vee}=M_{\lambda}^{\vee}, L_{\lambda}^{\vee}=L_{\lambda}$. (iii) The assignment $X \mapsto X^{\vee}$ is an involutive equivalence of categories $\mathcal{O} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{op}}$ which preserves the decomposition into $\mathcal{O}_{\chi}(S)$.
