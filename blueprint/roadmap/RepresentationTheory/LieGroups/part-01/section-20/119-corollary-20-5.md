# Corollary 20.5

(i) Every $X \in \mathcal{O}$ which is a free $U(\mathfrak{n}_{-})$-module is standardly filtered. In particular, for any $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ and finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module $V$, the module $V \otimes M_{\lambda}$ is standardly filtered. (ii) Any projective object $P \in \mathcal{O}$ is standardly filtered.
