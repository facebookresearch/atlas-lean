# Lemma 20.1

Let $X \in \mathcal{O}$ be a free $U(\mathfrak{n}_{-})$-module. Then for any $\mu \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ we have $\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal{O}}^{i}(X, M_{\mu}^{\vee})=0$ for $i>0$.
