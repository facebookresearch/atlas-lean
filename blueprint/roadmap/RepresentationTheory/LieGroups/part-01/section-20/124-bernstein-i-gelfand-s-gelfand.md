# Theorem 20.13 (Bernstein - I. Gelfand - S. Gelfand)

If $L_{\mu-\rho}$ occurs in the composition series of $M_{\lambda-\rho}$ (i.e., $d_{\lambda-\rho, \mu-\rho} \neq 0$) then $\mu \preceq \lambda$.
