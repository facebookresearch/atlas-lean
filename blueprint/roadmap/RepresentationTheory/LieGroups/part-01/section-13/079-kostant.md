# Theorem 13.5 (Kostant)

(i) The center $Z(\mathfrak{g})=U(\mathfrak{g})^{\mathfrak{g}}$ of $U(\mathfrak{g})$ is a polynomial algebra in $r$ generators $C_{i}$ of Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt filtration degrees $d_{i}$. (ii) $U(\mathfrak{g})$ is a free module over $Z(\mathfrak{g})$, and for every irreducible finite-dimensional representation $V$ of $\mathfrak{g}$, the space $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}}(V, U(\mathfrak{g}))$ is a free $Z(\mathfrak{g})$-module of rank $\operatorname{dim} V[0]$.
