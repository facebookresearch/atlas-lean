# Theorem 13.1 (Kostant)

$S\mathfrak{g}$ is a free $(S\mathfrak{g})^{\mathfrak{g}}$-module. Moreover, for every finite-dimensional irreducible representation $V$ of $\mathfrak{g}$, the space $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}}(V, S\mathfrak{g})$ is a free $(S\mathfrak{g})^{\mathfrak{g}}$ module of rank $\operatorname{dim} V[0]$, the dimension of the zero weight space of $V$.
