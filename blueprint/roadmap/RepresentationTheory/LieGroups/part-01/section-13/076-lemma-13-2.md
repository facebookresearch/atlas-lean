# Lemma 13.2

As $q \rightarrow 1$ in $(0,1)$, the function $F_{q}(x):=\prod_{\alpha \in R_{+}} \frac{1-e^{i\alpha(x)}}{1-qe^{i\alpha(x)}}$ goes to 1 in $L^{2}(\mathfrak{h}/Q^{\vee})$.
