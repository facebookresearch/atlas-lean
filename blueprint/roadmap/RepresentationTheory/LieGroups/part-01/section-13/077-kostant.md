# Theorem 13.3 (Kostant)

For $\lambda \in P_{+}$ we have $H\left(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak{g}}\left(L_{\lambda}^{*},(S\mathfrak{g})_{0}\right), q\right)=\frac{\prod_{i=1}^{r}[d_{i}]_{q}}{|W|} \mathrm{CT}\left(\prod_{\alpha \in R} \frac{1-e^{\alpha}}{1-qe^{\alpha}} \chi_{L_{\lambda}}\right)= \prod_{i=1}^{r}[d_{i}]_{q} \cdot \mathrm{CT}\left(\frac{e^{\lambda} \prod_{\alpha \in R_{+}}(1-e^{\alpha})}{\prod_{\alpha \in R}(1-qe^{\alpha})}\right)$.
