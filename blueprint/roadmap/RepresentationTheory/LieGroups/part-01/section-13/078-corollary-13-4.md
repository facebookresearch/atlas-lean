# Corollary 13.4

$\frac{1}{|W|} \mathrm{CT}\left(\prod_{\alpha \in R} \frac{1-e^{\alpha}}{1-qe^{\alpha}}\right)=\mathrm{CT}\left(\frac{\prod_{\alpha \in R_{+}}(1-e^{\alpha})}{\prod_{\alpha \in R}(1-qe^{\alpha})}\right)=\frac{1}{\prod_{i=1}^{r}[d_{i}]_{q}}$.
