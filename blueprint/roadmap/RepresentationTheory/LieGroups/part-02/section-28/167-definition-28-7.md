# Definition 28.7

A connection on an $\mathcal{O}_{X}$-module $M$ is a $\mathbf{k}$-linear morphism of sheaves $\nabla: M \rightarrow M \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}} \Omega_{X}^{1}$ such that $\nabla(f m)=f \nabla(m)+m \otimes d f$ for local sections $f$ of $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ and $m$ of $M$.
