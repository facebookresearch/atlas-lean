# Definition 28.6

A left (respectively, right) $D$-module on $X$ is a quasicoherent sheaf of left (respectively, right) $D_{X}$-modules. The categories of left (respectively, right) $D$-modules on $X$ (with obviously defined morphisms) are denoted by $\mathcal{M}_{l}(X)$ and $\mathcal{M}_{r}(X)$.
