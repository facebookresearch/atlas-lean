# Theorem 29.6 (Beilinson-Bernstein)

(i) The map $a: U(\mathfrak{g}) \rightarrow D_{\lambda}(\mathcal{F})$ factors through a map $a_{\lambda}: U_{\lambda} \rightarrow D_{\lambda}(\mathcal{F})$. (ii) One has $\operatorname{gr}(a_{\lambda})=p^{*}$ where $p$ is the Springer map $T^{*} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{N}$. (iii) $\operatorname{gr} D_{\lambda}(\mathcal{F})=\mathcal{O}(T^{*} \mathcal{F})$ and $a_{\lambda}$ is an isomorphism.
