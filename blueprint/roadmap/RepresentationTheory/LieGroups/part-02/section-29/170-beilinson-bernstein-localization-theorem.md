# Theorem 29.2 (Beilinson-Bernstein localization theorem)

The functors $\Gamma$ and $\operatorname{Loc}$ are mutually inverse equivalences. Thus the category $U_{0}-\bmod$ is canonically equivalent to the category of $D$-modules on the flag variety $\mathcal{F}$.
