# Theorem 29.1 (Beilinson-Bernstein)

(i) The homomorphism $a: U(\mathfrak{g}) \rightarrow D(\mathcal{F})$ factors through a homomorphism $a_{0}: U_{0} \rightarrow D(\mathcal{F})$. (ii) One has $\operatorname{gr}(a_{0})=p^{*}$ where $p$ is the Springer map $T^{*} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{N}$. (iii) $\operatorname{gr} D(\mathcal{F})=\mathcal{O}(T^{*} \mathcal{F})$ and $a_{0}$ is an isomorphism.
