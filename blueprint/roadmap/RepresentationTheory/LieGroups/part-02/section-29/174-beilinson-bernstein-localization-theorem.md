# Theorem 29.7 (Beilinson-Bernstein localization theorem)

If $\lambda$ is antidominant then the functors $\Gamma$ and $\operatorname{Loc}$ are mutually inverse equivalences. Thus the category $U_{\lambda}-\bmod$ is canonically equivalent to the category of $D_{\lambda}$-modules on the flag variety $\mathcal{F}$.
