# Theorem 23.6

For any $\xi \in \Xi$ there exists an indecomposable projective functor $F_{\xi}$ such that $F_{\xi}(M_{\nu - \rho}) = 0$ if $\chi_{\nu} \neq \chi_{\lambda}$ and $F_{\xi}(M_{\lambda - \rho}) = P_{\mu - \rho}$ for any proper representation $(\mu, \lambda)$ of $\xi$. The assignment $\xi \mapsto F_{\xi}$ is a bijection between $\Xi$ and the set of isomorphism classes of indecomposable projective functors.
