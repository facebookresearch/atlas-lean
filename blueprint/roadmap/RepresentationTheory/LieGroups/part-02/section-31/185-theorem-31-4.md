# Theorem 31.4

Irreducible $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-modules with (pure) infinitesimal character $\chi$ are $\pi(O, V)$ where $O$ is a $K$-orbit on $\mathcal{F}$ and $V$ an irreducible representation of $K_{x}$, $x \in O$ such that $\operatorname{Lie}(K_{x})$ acts via the character $\lambda_{x}$. Namely, $\pi(O, V)$ corresponds to $M(O, V)$ under the Beilinson-Bernstein equivalence.
