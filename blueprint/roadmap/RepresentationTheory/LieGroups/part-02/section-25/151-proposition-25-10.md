# Proposition 25.10

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories such that $\mathcal{A}$ has enough projectives and $T \colon \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor which maps projectives to projectives. Suppose that $T$ is fully faithful on projectives, i.e., for any projectives $P_0, P_1 \in \mathcal{A}$, the natural map $\operatorname{Hom}(P_1, P_0) \rightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(T(P_1), T(P_0))$ is an isomorphism. Then $T$ is fully faithful, and defines an equivalence of $\mathcal{A}$ onto the subcategory of objects $Y \in \mathcal{B}$ which admit a presentation $T(P_1) \rightarrow T(P_0) \rightarrow Y \rightarrow 0$ for some projectives $P_0, P_1 \in \mathcal{A}$.
