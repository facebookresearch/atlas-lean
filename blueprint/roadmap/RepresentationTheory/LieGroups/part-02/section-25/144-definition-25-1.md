# Definition 25.1

A proper 2-sided ideal $I$ in a (possibly non-commutative) ring $R$ is prime if whenever the product $XY$ of two 2-sided ideals $X, Y \subset R$ is contained in $I$, either $X$ or $Y$ must be contained in $I$.
