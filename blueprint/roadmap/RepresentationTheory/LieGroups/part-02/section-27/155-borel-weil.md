# Theorem 27.3 (Borel-Weil)

Let $\lambda \in P$. If $\lambda \in P_{+}$ then we have an isomorphism of $G$-modules $\Gamma\left(G / B, \mathcal{L}_{-\lambda}\right) \cong L_{\lambda}^{*}$. If $\lambda \notin P_{+}$ then $\Gamma\left(G / B, \mathcal{L}_{-\lambda}\right)=0$.
