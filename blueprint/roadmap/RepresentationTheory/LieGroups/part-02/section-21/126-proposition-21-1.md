# Proposition 21.1

$T_{w}, w \in W$ are linearly independent, so they form a basis of $H_{q}(W)$. Thus $H_{q}(W)$ is a free $\mathbb{Z}[q^{\frac{1}{2}}, q^{-\frac{1}{2}}]$-module of rank $|W|$.
