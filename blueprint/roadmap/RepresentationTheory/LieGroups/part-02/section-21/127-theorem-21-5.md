# Theorem 21.5

There exist unique polynomials $P_{y, w} \in \mathbb{Z}[q]$ such that (a) $P_{y, w}=0$ unless $y \leq w$, and $P_{w, w}=1$; (b) If $y\lt w$ then $P_{y, w}$ has degree at most $\frac{\ell(w)-\ell(y)-1}{2}$; (c) The elements $C_{w}:=q^{-\frac{\ell(w)}{2}} \sum_{y} P_{y, w}(q) T_{y} \in H_{q}(W)$ satisfy $D(C_{w})=C_{w}$.
