# Corollary 30.20

If $\lambda \in \mathfrak{h}^{*}$ is antidominant then the functors $\Gamma$, Loc restrict to mutually inverse equivalences between the category of $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-modules with infinitesimal character $\chi_{\lambda-\rho}$ and the category of $K$-equivariant $D_{\lambda}$-modules on $\mathcal{F}$.
