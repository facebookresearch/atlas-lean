# Definition 30.7

A $G$-equivariant quasicoherent sheaf on $X$ is a quasicoherent sheaf $\mathcal{E}$ on $X$ equipped with an isomorphism $\phi: \rho^{*} \mathcal{E} \cong \mathcal{O}_{G} \boxtimes \mathcal{E}$ making the appropriate diagram commutative. Thus $\phi$ comprises all the isomorphisms $\phi_{g}$, which therefore satisfy the equality $\phi_{gh}=\phi_{g} \circ g(\phi_{h})$ and depend on $g$ algebraically.
