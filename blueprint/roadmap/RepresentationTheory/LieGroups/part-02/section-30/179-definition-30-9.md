# Definition 30.9

A (strongly) $G$-equivariant $D_{X}$-module is a weakly $G$-equivariant $D_{X}$-module where the two $\mathfrak{g}$-actions agree: $b_{\phi}=b_{0}$ (or, equivalently, where $\phi$ is $D_{G \times X}$-linear).
