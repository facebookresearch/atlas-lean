# Proposition 22.7

(i) Each projective functor $F$ is a direct sum of indecomposable projective functors. Moreover, for $F \circ \Pi_{\theta}$ this sum is finite. (ii) If $F = F \circ \Pi_{\chi_{\lambda}}$ for dominant $\lambda$ is an indecomposable projective functor then $F(M_{\lambda - \rho}) = P_{\mu - \rho}$ for some $\mu \in \mathfrak{h}^*$.
