# Theorem 26.3 (Gelfand-Naimark)

The irreducible unitary representations of $\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{C})$ are Hilbert space completions of the following unitary Harish-Chandra modules: (1) Unitary principal series $\mathbf{M}\left(-\frac{m}{2}+s, \frac{m}{2}+s\right), m \in \mathbb{Z}, s \in i\mathbb{R}$; (2) Complementary series $\mathbf{M}(s, s), -1\lt s<1$; (3) The trivial representation $\mathbb{C}$. Here $\mathbf{M}\left(-\frac{m}{2}+s, \frac{m}{2}+s\right) \cong \mathbf{M}\left(\frac{m}{2}-s,-\frac{m}{2}-s\right)$, $\mathbf{M}(s, s)=\mathbf{M}(-s,-s)$ and there are no other isomorphisms.
