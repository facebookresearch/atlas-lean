# Local classification and the local intertwining relation for inner forms of unitary groups

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the two local statements under which Kaletha, Mínguez, Shin and White
prove the endoscopic classification of the discrete spectrum of inner forms of unitary groups. Let $F$ be a local field
of characteristic zero, $E/F$ a quadratic algebra, $G^* = U_{E/F}(N)$ the quasi-split unitary group, $(G, \xi)$ an inner twist
of $G^*$ with a choice $\Xi$ of extended pure inner twist, and $\psi$ a local Arthur parameter of $G^*$, with component group
$S^\natural_\psi$; these are [algebraic groups over a local field](linear-algebraic-groups-over-local-fields.md).

- **Local classification** (KMSW, Theorem* 1.6.1). There is a finite set $\Pi_\psi(G, \Xi)$ of unitary representations of
  $G(F)$, empty when $\psi$ is not relevant for $(G, \xi)$, with a map $\pi \mapsto \langle \pi, - \rangle_\Xi$ to characters of
  $S^\natural_\psi$ of a prescribed central character, satisfying the endoscopic character identities.
- **Local intertwining relation** (KMSW, Theorem* 2.6.2). For $\psi$ coming from a Levi subgroup $M^*$ and $u$ in the
  normalizer group $N_\psi(M^*, G^*)$, the normalized self-intertwining operators of the representations induced from the
  packets of $M$ define a linear form $f \mapsto f_{G, \Xi}(\psi, u^\natural)$; it depends only on the image of $u$ in $S^\natural_\psi$, and it
  equals, up to the sign $e(G)$, the endoscopic linear form attached to $s_\psi u^{-1}$.

Kaletha, Mínguez, Shin and White prove both when $\psi$ is generic and $(G, \xi)$ is realized as a pure inner twist, and
treat the remaining cases in sequels; their global classification for inner forms assumes both at every place of a
number field (their Hypothesis 3.6.3 and Theorem 5.0.5).
