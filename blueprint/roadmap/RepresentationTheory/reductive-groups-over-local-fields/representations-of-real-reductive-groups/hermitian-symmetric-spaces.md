# Hermitian symmetric spaces and their compact duals

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the structure of Hermitian symmetric spaces $G/K$ that the construction
of the holomorphic discrete series uses. Let $G$ be a connected reductive linear Lie group with compact centre,
$K$ a maximal compact subgroup, $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{k} \oplus \mathfrak{p}$ the Cartan decomposition, and $G^{\mathbb{C}}$, $K^{\mathbb{C}}$
complexifications. Suppose $G/K$ carries a $G$-invariant Hermitian symmetric structure; then $K$ contains a compact
Cartan subgroup $H$, and the roots of $(\mathfrak{g}^{\mathbb{C}}, \mathfrak{h}^{\mathbb{C}})$ are compact or noncompact as their root spaces lie
in $\mathfrak{k}^{\mathbb{C}}$ or $\mathfrak{p}^{\mathbb{C}}$.

- **Splitting of $\mathfrak{p}$.** There is an ordering of the roots in which the sum of two positive noncompact roots is
  never a root. For it, $\mathfrak{p}^{\mathbb{C}} = \mathfrak{p}_+ \oplus \mathfrak{p}_-$, with $\mathfrak{p}_\pm$ the sums of the positive and of
  the negative noncompact root spaces; $\mathfrak{p}_\pm$ are $\mathrm{Ad}\, K$-invariant abelian subalgebras, complex conjugate to
  each other, exponentiating to abelian unipotent subgroups $P_\pm$ of $G^{\mathbb{C}}$.
- **Compact dual.** $K^{\mathbb{C}} P_+$ is a parabolic subgroup of $G^{\mathbb{C}}$ with $G \cap K^{\mathbb{C}} P_+ = K$; the orbit of the base
  point is open, and $G/K \hookrightarrow G^{\mathbb{C}}/K^{\mathbb{C}} P_+$ embeds $G/K$ in its compact dual and induces the invariant complex
  structure on $G/K$.
- **Homogeneous holomorphic bundles.** A finite-dimensional holomorphic representation $V$ of $K^{\mathbb{C}}$, extended to
  $K^{\mathbb{C}} P_+$ trivially on $P_+$, defines a $G^{\mathbb{C}}$-homogeneous holomorphic vector bundle on the compact dual, whose
  restriction to $G/K$ is the $G$-homogeneous holomorphic vector bundle $G \times_K V$.
