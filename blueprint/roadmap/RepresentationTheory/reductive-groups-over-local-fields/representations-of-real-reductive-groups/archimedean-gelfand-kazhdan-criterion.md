# Archimedean Gelfand–Kazhdan criterion

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the criterion of Gelfand and Kazhdan type, in the general form of
Sun and Zhu, that the page on archimedean Whittaker models uses. Let $G$ be the group of real points of a reductive
algebraic group, $S_1, S_2$ closed subgroups with characters $\chi_1, \chi_2$, and $\sigma$ a continuous
anti-automorphism of $G$.

- **Theorem** (Sun–Zhu, Theorem 2.3 (a)). Suppose that every tempered generalized function $f$ on $G$ that is an
  eigendistribution of the bi-invariant differential operators and satisfies
  $f(s_1 g s_2) = \chi_1(s_1) f(g) \chi_2(s_2)$ for $s_i \in S_i$ is fixed by $\sigma$. Then for every pair of
  contragredient irreducible Casselman–Wallach representations $U$ and $V$ of $G$,
  $\dim \mathrm{Hom}_{S_1}(U^\infty, \mathbb{C}_{\chi_1}) \cdot \dim \mathrm{Hom}_{S_2}(V^\infty, \mathbb{C}_{\chi_2}) \le 1$.
