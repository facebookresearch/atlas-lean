# L- and ε-factors of pairs for GLₙ and of Weil–Deligne representations

This node is still to be broken down. It covers the local factors on both sides of the
[local Langlands correspondence for GLₙ](local-langlands-correspondence-for-gl-n.md). Let $F$ be a
non-archimedean local field with residue field of order $q$, $W_F$ its Weil group and $\psi$ a
nontrivial additive character of $F$.

- **The $\mathrm{GL}_n$ side** (Jacquet, Piatetski-Shapiro and Shalika). For
  [generic](whittaker-models-for-gl-n.md) irreducible representations $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$
  and $\pi'$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{n'}(F)$, the local Rankin–Selberg integrals of Whittaker functions
  $W \in \mathcal{W}(\pi, \psi)$, $W' \in \mathcal{W}(\pi', \bar\psi)$ (with a Schwartz function on
  $F^n$ when $n = n'$) converge for $\operatorname{Re} s \gg 0$, are rational functions of $q^{-s}$,
  and span a fractional ideal of $\mathbb{C}[q^s, q^{-s}]$ generated by
  $L(\pi \times \pi', s) = P(q^{-s})^{-1}$ with $P(0) = 1$; the local functional equation defines
  $\varepsilon(\pi \times \pi', s, \psi)$, of the form $c q^{-fs}$, and
  $\gamma(\pi \times \pi', s, \psi) = \varepsilon(\pi \times \pi', s, \psi)
  L(\tilde\pi \times \tilde\pi', 1-s)/L(\pi \times \pi', s)$. The factors of arbitrary irreducible
  pairs are defined from those of generic pairs through the
  [Langlands classification](langlands-classification-for-gl-n.md); $L(\pi, s) = L(\pi \times 1, s)$,
  $\varepsilon(\pi, s, \psi)$ and the conductor of $\pi$ follow.
- **The Galois side** (Tate, Langlands and Deligne). Tate's $L$- and $\varepsilon$-factors of
  characters of $F^\times \cong W_F^{\mathrm{ab}}$; the theorem of Langlands and Deligne that there
  is a unique $\varepsilon(r, \psi, dx)$ for representations $r$ of $W_F$ extending Tate's factors,
  multiplicative in exact sequences and inductive in degree zero; the factors
  $L(\rho, s) = \det(1 - q^{-s}\Phi \mid V_N^{I_F})^{-1}$ and
  $\varepsilon(\rho, s, \psi) = \varepsilon(\lvert\cdot\rvert^s r, \psi, dx)
  \det(-\Phi \mid V^{I_F}/V_N^{I_F})$ of a Frobenius-semisimple Weil–Deligne representation
  $\rho = (r, N)$ on $V$, with $\Phi$ a geometric Frobenius and $dx$ self-dual for $\psi$, applied to
  tensor products $\rho \otimes \rho'$.

A good expansion has definition pages for the local Rankin–Selberg integrals and their
convergence and rationality, for the factors of generic pairs and their functional equation, for
the extension to all irreducible pairs, for the conductor, for Tate's local factors, for the
Langlands–Deligne local constants and for the factors of Weil–Deligne representations, each
citing the sources below with locators. The pages of the Galois side belong beside the Weil–Deligne
representations of
[Galois cohomology and p-adic Hodge theory](../../../NumberTheory/galois-representations/galois-cohomology-and-p-adic-hodge-theory.md).
The recursion in Wedhorn's (2.5.2) (3) should be checked against Kudla's survey, which Wedhorn
follows there, since its printed factors do not depend on the product index.
