# Sum of squares of the Wedderburn block sizes

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and $G$ a finite group whose order $|G|$ is nonzero in
$k$. Then there are positive integers $d_1, \dots, d_n$ with $d_1^2 + \dots + d_n^2 = |G|$ such that
the group algebra $k[G]$ is isomorphic, as a $k$-algebra, to the product
$M_{d_1}(k) \times \dots \times M_{d_n}(k)$ of matrix algebras.
