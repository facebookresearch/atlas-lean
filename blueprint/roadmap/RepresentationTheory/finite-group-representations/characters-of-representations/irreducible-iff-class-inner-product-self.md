# Character criterion for irreducibility

Let $G$ be a finite group, $k$ an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, and $\sigma$ a
representation of $G$ on a finite-dimensional $k$-vector space, with character $\chi_\sigma$. Then
$\sigma$ is irreducible if and only if

$$\frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_\sigma(g)\, \chi_\sigma(g^{-1}) = 1.$$
