# Multiplicity of one irreducible representation in another

Schur's lemma as a count: let `G` be a finite group and let `σ` and `τ` be
irreducible representations of `G` on finite-dimensional complex vector
spaces. The multiplicity of `σ` in `τ` (the dimension of the space of
`G`-equivariant linear maps from `σ` to `τ`) is `1` if `σ` and `τ` are
isomorphic and `0` otherwise.
