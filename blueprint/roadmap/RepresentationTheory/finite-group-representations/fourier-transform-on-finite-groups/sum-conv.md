# Total mass of a convolution

Let $G$ be a finite group, $R$ a commutative semiring and $P, Q : G \to R$. Then the total mass of
the [convolution](conv.md) is the product of the total masses:
$\sum_{s} (P * Q)(s) = \bigl(\sum_s P(s)\bigr) \bigl(\sum_s Q(s)\bigr)$. In particular the
convolution of two probability distributions is a probability distribution (Diaconis, Saint-Flour
notes 1988, Section 4: convolution models the composition of two independent random steps).
