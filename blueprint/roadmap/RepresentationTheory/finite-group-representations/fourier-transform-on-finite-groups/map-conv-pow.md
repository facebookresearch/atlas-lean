# Convolution commutes with ring homomorphisms

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\varphi : R \to S$ a homomorphism of semirings. For all
$P, Q : G \to R$, $\varphi \circ (P * Q) = (\varphi \circ P) * (\varphi \circ Q)$, and likewise
$\varphi \circ Q^{*k} = (\varphi \circ Q)^{*k}$ for the [convolution powers](conv-pow.md). This
moves a real probability into $\mathbb{C}$ before taking Fourier transforms.
