# Fourier transform of a convolution power

Let $G$ be a finite group, $\rho$ a representation of $G$ on a complex vector space, and
$Q : G \to \mathbb{C}$. Then for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the [Fourier transform](fourier.md) of
the [convolution power](conv-pow.md) $Q^{*k}$ is $\widehat{Q^{*k}}(\rho) = \hat Q(\rho)^k$: the
transform reduces the $k$-step law of a random walk to the $k$-th power of one operator (Diaconis
and Shahshahani 1981, Lemma 1 and the remark after Corollary 1).
