# Fourier transform at a representation

For a function $P : G \to \mathbb{C}$ on a finite group and a representation $\rho$ of $G$ on a
complex vector space $V$, the Fourier transform of $P$ at $\rho$ is the endomorphism

$$\hat P(\rho) = \sum_{s \in G} P(s)\, \rho(s)$$

of $V$. Diaconis and Shahshahani write it $\rho(P)$. At the trivial one-dimensional representation it
is the total mass of $P$; for an abelian group and a character $\psi$ it is the discrete Fourier
transform $\sum_s \psi(s) P(s)$.
