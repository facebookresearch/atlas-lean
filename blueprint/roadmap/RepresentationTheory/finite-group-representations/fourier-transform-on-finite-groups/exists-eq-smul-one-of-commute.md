# Schur's lemma for an operator commuting with an irreducible representation

If $\rho$ is an irreducible representation of a finite group $G$ on a finite-dimensional complex vector space $V$ and an endomorphism $A$ of $V$ commutes with every $\rho(g)$, then $A$ is a scalar multiple of the identity (Diaconis and Shahshahani 1981,
Lemma 4, citing Serre, *Linear representations of finite groups*, Section 2.2).
