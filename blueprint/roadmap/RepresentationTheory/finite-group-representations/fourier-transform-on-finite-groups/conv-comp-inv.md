# Convolution and inversion

Let $G$ be a finite group, $R$ a commutative semiring and $P, Q : G \to R$, and write
$\check P(s) = P(s^{-1})$. Then the [convolution](conv.md) satisfies
$(P * Q)\check{} = \check Q * \check P$: reversing a two-step walk reverses the order of the steps
(a direct computation from the definition of convolution in Diaconis and Shahshahani 1981,
Section 2).
