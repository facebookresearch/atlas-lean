# The Fourier transform turns convolution into composition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\rho$ a representation of $G$ on a complex vector space. For all
$P, Q : G \to \mathbb{C}$, the [Fourier transform](fourier.md) of the [convolution](conv.md) is
the composition of the transforms: $\widehat{P * Q}(\rho) = \hat P(\rho)\, \hat Q(\rho)$
(Diaconis and Shahshahani 1981, Lemma 1).
