# Universal irreducible tensor squares for symmetric groups

For each integer $n>0$ other than $2,4,9$, there is a diagram $\lambda$ of $n$ boxes whose complex Specht representation is irreducible. Its diagonal tensor square has positive intertwiner dimension from $S^\nu$ for every diagram $\nu$ of $n$ boxes, and admits an injective intertwiner from every finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation of $S_n$. The same $\lambda$ works for both quantifiers. For a Young diagram and a canonical tableau on its boxes, take the complex permutation space on words recording the row assignment. In that space take the alternating sum of the column-stabilizer translates of the tableau’s row-word basis vector and span its orbit under the whole symmetric group. This is the Specht representation $S^\lambda$ used here. Multiplicities are complex dimensions of equivariant linear maps into tensor products with the diagonal action.

## Depends on

- Complex Specht models, irreducibility and exhaustion of irreducible representations, and intertwiner dimension.

## Proof depends on

- Construction of a universal diagram and its positivity for the eligible sizes; passage to arbitrary finite-dimensional irreducibles.

## Expansion

Separate explicit small eligible sizes from the general construction, relate the computed multiplicities to the orbit-span model, and construct the injective intertwiners for that same diagram.
