# Average dimension of fixed point spaces of a non-trivial irreducible module

For every finite group $X$, every field $F$ and every finite-dimensional representation $\rho$ of $X$ on an $F$-vector space $M$ that is irreducible and non-trivial,

$$\frac{1}{|X|}\sum_{x \in X} \dim_F \ker\bigl(\rho(x) - 1\bigr) \;\le\; \frac{\dim_F M}{p},$$

where $p$ is the least prime divisor of $|X|$. Since $p \ge 2$ this gives Vaughan-Lee's bound $\tfrac12 \dim M$.
