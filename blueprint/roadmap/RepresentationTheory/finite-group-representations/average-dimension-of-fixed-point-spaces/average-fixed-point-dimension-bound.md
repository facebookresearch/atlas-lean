# Kourovka Problem 8.5 (average dimension of fixed point spaces)

This is the proposition that, for every finite group $X$, every field $F$ and every finite-dimensional representation $\rho$ of $X$ on an $F$-vector space $M$ that is irreducible ($M \ne 0$ with no invariant subspaces other than $0$ and $M$) and non-trivial (some $\rho(x) \ne 1$),

$$\frac{1}{|X|}\sum_{x \in X} \dim_F \ker\bigl(\rho(x) - 1\bigr) \;\le\; \frac{\dim_F M}{p},$$

where $p$ is the least prime divisor of $|X|$. Since $p \ge 2$ this gives Vaughan-Lee's bound $\tfrac12 \dim M$.

Source: E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), Unsolved Problems in Group Theory: The Kourovka Notebook, 21st issue, arXiv:1401.0300v49 (2026), Archive of Solved Problems, Problem 8.5 (M. R. Vaughan-Lee).
