# Annihilators of powers of the augmentation ideal on a permutation module

For a group $G$ acting on a set $\Omega$ and a field $F$, the sequence of subspaces $\operatorname{soc}^k(F\Omega)$ of $F^\Omega$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$) defined by $\operatorname{soc}^0 = 0$ and $\operatorname{soc}^{k+1} = \{\, m \in F^\Omega : g \cdot m - m \in \operatorname{soc}^k \text{ for every } g \in G \,\}$, where $(g \cdot m)(\omega) = m(g^{-1}\omega)$: the elements annihilated by the $k$-th power of the augmentation ideal.

When $G$ is a finite $p$-group and $F$ has characteristic $p$, this is the socle series of the permutation module. In general it is not: for the group of order two swapping two points over $\mathbb{Q}$, the socle is the whole module, while $\operatorname{soc}^1$ here is the line of constant functions.
