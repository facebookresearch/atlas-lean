# Alperin's theorem on Loewy series of transitive permutation modules of p-groups

For every prime $p$, every finite $p$-group $G$ acting transitively on a finite set $\Omega$ and every field $F$ of characteristic $p$, the radical (descending Loewy) series and the socle (ascending Loewy) series of the permutation module $F\Omega$ coincide: there is $L \in \mathbb{N}$ with $J^L F\Omega = 0$ and $J^k F\Omega = \operatorname{soc}^{L-k}(F\Omega)$ for every $k \le L$. Here $F\Omega$ is the space of functions $\Omega \to F$ with $(g \cdot m)(\omega) = m(g^{-1}\omega)$, $J^0 F\Omega = F\Omega$ and $J^{k+1}F\Omega$ is spanned by the vectors $g \cdot m - m$ with $m \in J^k F\Omega$, and $\operatorname{soc}^0 = 0$, $\operatorname{soc}^{k+1} = \{m : g \cdot m - m \in \operatorname{soc}^k \text{ for all } g \in G\}$. Kourovka Problem 8.49 has a positive answer.
