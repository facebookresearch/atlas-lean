# Powers of the augmentation ideal on a permutation module

For a group $G$ acting on a set $\Omega$ and a field $F$, the sequence of subspaces $J^k F\Omega$ of $F^\Omega$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$) defined by $J^0 F\Omega = F^\Omega$ and $J^{k+1} F\Omega = \operatorname{span}_F\{\, g \cdot m - m : g \in G,\ m \in J^k F\Omega \,\}$, where $(g \cdot m)(\omega) = m(g^{-1}\omega)$. Here $J$ stands for the augmentation ideal of $FG$, spanned by the elements $g - 1$, so $J^k F\Omega$ is the image of its $k$-th power.

When $G$ is a finite $p$-group and $F$ has characteristic $p$, $J$ is the radical of $FG$ and this is the radical (Loewy) series of the permutation module. In general it is not: for the group of order two swapping two points over $\mathbb{Q}$, the module is the direct sum of the trivial and the sign line, so its radical is $0$, while $J F\Omega$ is the sign line.
