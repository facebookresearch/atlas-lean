# Radical series of a permutation module

For a group $G$ acting on a set $\Omega$ and a field $F$, the sequence of subspaces $J^k F\Omega$ of $F^\Omega$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$) defined by $J^0 F\Omega = F^\Omega$ and $J^{k+1} F\Omega = \operatorname{span}_F\{\, g \cdot m - m : g \in G,\ m \in J^k F\Omega \,\}$, where $(g \cdot m)(\omega) = m(g^{-1}\omega)$. Here $J$ stands for the augmentation ideal of $FG$, spanned by the elements $g - 1$.
