# Permutation representation on the projective line

For a prime `q` and a field `k`, `ind q k` is the permutation representation
of `GL₂(𝔽_q)` on the `k`-vector space of finitely supported functions
`ℙ¹(𝔽_q) → k`: a matrix `g` sends the indicator of a point `x` to the
indicator of `g • x`, and acts on general functions by linearity.
