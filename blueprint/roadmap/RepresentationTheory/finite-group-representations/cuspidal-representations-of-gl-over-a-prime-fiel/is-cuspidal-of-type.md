# Cuspidal representation of a given type

Let `q` be a prime, `K` a field, `V` a finite-dimensional `K`-vector space,
`θ : 𝔽_{q²}ˣ → Kˣ` a character, and `ρ` a representation of `GL₂(𝔽_q)` on
`V`. Then `ρ` is of cuspidal type `θ` when all of the following hold:

- `dim V = q - 1` (as a natural number);
- the only vector fixed by every unipotent element `[[1, t], [0, 1]]` is `0`;
- every scalar matrix `c • I`, for `c ∈ 𝔽_qˣ`, acts as the identity;
- for every `α ∈ 𝔽_{q²}ˣ`, the characteristic polynomial of `ρ` at the nonsplit-torus element of `α`, multiplied by `(X - θ α)(X - θ α⁻¹)`, equals the characteristic polynomial of the same element in the permutation representation of `GL₂(𝔽_q)` on `K`-valued functions on the projective line `ℙ¹(𝔽_q)`.
