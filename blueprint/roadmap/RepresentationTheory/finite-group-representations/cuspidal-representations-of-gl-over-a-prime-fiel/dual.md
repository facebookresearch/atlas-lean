# Duals of cuspidal representations

Let `q` be a prime, `K` a field, `V` a finite-dimensional `K`-vector space
and `θ : 𝔽_{q²}ˣ → Kˣ` a character. If a representation `ρ` of `GL₂(𝔽_q)` on
`V` is of cuspidal type `θ`, then its dual representation on the dual space
of `V` is also of cuspidal type `θ`.
