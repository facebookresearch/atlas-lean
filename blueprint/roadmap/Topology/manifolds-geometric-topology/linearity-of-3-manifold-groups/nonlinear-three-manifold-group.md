# A closed 3-manifold group with no faithful representation in $GL_4(\mathbb{R})$

There
is a closed connected topological 3-manifold $M$ with a point $x$ such that no group
homomorphism from the fundamental group $\pi_1(M, x)$ to $GL_4(\mathbb{R})$ is injective.

## Sources

- J. O. Button, *Aspherical 3-manifold groups not in GL(4, k) for any field k*, arXiv:1404.4639 (2014). See the discussion of [Kir97, Problem 3.33] in the K3 problem list. Via leanprover/lean-eval@38361be, problem `nonlinear_three_manifold_group`.
