# A closed 3-manifold group with no faithful representation in $GL_4(\mathbb{R})$

There
is a closed connected topological 3-manifold $M$ with a point $x$ such that no group
homomorphism from the fundamental group $\pi_1(M, x)$ to $GL_4(\mathbb{R})$ is injective.

## Sources

- J. O. Button, *A 3-manifold group which is not four dimensional linear*, arXiv:1210.3068 (2012). See Problem 3.33 of R. Kirby's problem list, *Problems in low-dimensional topology* (1997). Problem `nonlinear_three_manifold_group` of the lean-eval benchmark.
