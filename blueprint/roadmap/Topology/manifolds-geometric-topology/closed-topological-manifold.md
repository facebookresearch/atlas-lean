# Closed manifold, not required to be connected

A *closed $n$-manifold* is a topological $n$-manifold (without boundary) that is compact as a topological space. Here a topological $n$-manifold is a second countable Hausdorff space in which every point has an open neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$. A closed manifold is not required to be connected or nonempty: a finite disjoint union of closed $n$-manifolds is a closed $n$-manifold, and so is the empty space. This is the notion of closed manifold for which fundamental classes and Poincaré duality are stated. It differs from the closed topological manifold of the triangulation conjecture page in this field, which is in addition required to be connected and nonempty: for $n \ge 1$, the disjoint union of two $n$-spheres is a closed $n$-manifold in the present sense but not in that one.

## Expansion

A good expansion of this node links each notion it is built from: compact spaces; topological manifolds.
