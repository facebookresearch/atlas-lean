# Isomorphism problems for simply connected Liouville domains and contact manifolds are algorithmically unsolvable

There is no algorithm deciding Liouville isomorphism of simply connected Liouville domains, none computing the rank of degree-zero symplectic cohomology for such domains with vanishing first Chern class, and none deciding isomorphism of simply connected closed contact manifolds.

A *Liouville domain* is a compact manifold $M$ with boundary carrying an exact symplectic form $\omega = d\theta$ whose Liouville vector field, defined by $\iota_Z \omega = \theta$, points outward along $\partial M$; its boundary inherits the contact form $\theta|_{\partial M}$. Its *completion* $\hat M$ attaches the positive half $[0, \infty) \times \partial M$ of the symplectization of $(\partial M, \theta|_{\partial M})$, with the one-form $\hat\theta = e^r \theta|_{\partial M}$ there. A *Liouville isomorphism* between Liouville domains $M_0$ and $M_1$ is a diffeomorphism $\varphi : \hat M_0 \to \hat M_1$ with $\varphi^* \hat\theta_1 = \hat\theta_0 + dh$ for some compactly supported function $h$. A closed contact manifold is a closed manifold $N$ of odd dimension with a contact one-form $\alpha$, considered up to multiplication by positive functions, and a *contact isomorphism* $\psi : (N_0, \alpha_0) \to (N_1, \alpha_1)$ is a diffeomorphism with $\psi^* \alpha_1 = e^f \alpha_0$ for some function $f$. $SH^*(M)$ denotes symplectic cohomology.

**Theorem (Seidel).** The following problems are algorithmically unsolvable:

1. the Liouville isomorphism problem for simply connected Liouville domains;
2. the computation of the rank of $SH^0(M)$ for a given simply connected Liouville domain $M$ with $c_1(M) = 0$;
3. the isomorphism problem for simply connected closed contact manifolds.

Conventions: the source does not fix how a Liouville domain or a contact manifold is given as finite input; its proof produces the domains from finite group presentations by explicit Weinstein handle attachments (Section 6), so the statement is about any encoding in which these attachments can be carried out effectively. The manifolds involved are simply connected, so the obstruction does not come from the fundamental group of the manifolds themselves, in contrast with smooth topology, where by a theorem of Nabutovsky and Weinberger recognition is algorithmically solvable among simply connected smooth manifolds of dimension at least $5$. Seidel notes that Liouville isomorphism in clause 1 can be replaced by symplectic isomorphism of the completions.

## Expansion

Start from a finite presentation $P$ of a group $\Gamma_P$ with $H_1(\Gamma_P) = H_2(\Gamma_P) = 0$; triviality is undecidable already in this class. Novikov's construction gives a homology $n$-sphere $S_P$ with $\pi_1(S_P) \cong \Gamma_P$ and loops representing the generators. In the cotangent disc bundle $D^* S_P$ these loops give isotropic loops in the boundary, and attaching Weinstein two-handles along them produces a simply connected Liouville domain $M_P$ with $c_1(M_P) = 0$. By Viterbo's and Cieliebak's theorems, $SH^0(M_P) \cong H_0(\mathcal{L} S_P)$ is generated by the conjugacy classes of $\Gamma_P$, so its rank is $1$ exactly when $\Gamma_P$ is trivial. Comparing with the same construction for a presentation of the trivial group with the same number of generators shows that $M_P$, and also $\partial M_P$, are isomorphic to the corresponding model exactly when $\Gamma_P$ is trivial (Lemmas 6.6 and 6.7 of the source). A good expansion links the undecidability of triviality for such presentations, Novikov's homology-sphere construction, Viterbo's and Cieliebak's theorems and these two lemmas.
