# Open unit disc of the plane

The open unit disc $\{x \in \mathbb{R}^2 : |x| < 1\}$ of the Euclidean plane, an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$ and hence a smooth manifold modelled on $\mathbb{R}^2$. The [elementary diffeomorphisms](is-elementary.md) of the torus are those supported in an open set diffeomorphic to it.
