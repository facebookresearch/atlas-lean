# Burago–Ivanov–Polterovich question on the torus

The statement that there is a constant $C$ such that every diffeomorphism of the two-torus isotopic to the identity is a product of at most $C$ elementary diffeomorphisms, that is, the fragmentation norm is bounded on $\operatorname{Diff}_0(T^2)$. Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the two-torus with its standard smooth structure. A diffeomorphism $h$ of $T^2$ (smooth, with smooth inverse) is elementary when its support, the closure of $\{x : h(x) \neq x\}$, lies in an open set $U \subseteq T^2$ diffeomorphic to the open unit disc of $\mathbb{R}^2$. A diffeomorphism $f$ of $T^2$ is isotopic to the identity when there is a smooth map $H : \mathbb{R} \times T^2 \to T^2$ with $H(0, \cdot) = \mathrm{id}$, $H(1, \cdot) = f$ and every $H(t, \cdot)$ a diffeomorphism.
