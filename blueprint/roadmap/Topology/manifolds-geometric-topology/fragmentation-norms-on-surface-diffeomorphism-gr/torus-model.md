# Model space of the two-torus

The two-torus $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ is modelled on $\mathbb{R}^1 \times \mathbb{R}^1$: its smooth structure is the product of two copies of the smooth structure of the circle $S^1$, which is modelled on the Euclidean line $\mathbb{R}^1$ without boundary or corners. Smoothness of maps of $T^2$, as for [elementary diffeomorphisms](is-elementary.md), refers to this structure.
