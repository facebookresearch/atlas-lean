# Elementary diffeomorphism of the torus

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the two-torus with its standard smooth structure. A diffeomorphism $h$ of $T^2$ (smooth, with smooth inverse) is elementary when its support, the closure of $\{x : h(x) \neq x\}$, lies in an open set $U \subseteq T^2$ diffeomorphic to the open unit disc of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
