# The fragmentation norm on Diff₀ of the torus is unbounded

No constant $C$ makes every diffeomorphism of the two-torus isotopic to the identity a product of at most $C$ elementary diffeomorphisms; the fragmentation norm on $\operatorname{Diff}_0(T^2)$ is unbounded. Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the two-torus with its standard smooth structure. A diffeomorphism $h$ of $T^2$ (smooth, with smooth inverse) is elementary when its support, the closure of $\{x : h(x) \neq x\}$, lies in an open set $U \subseteq T^2$ diffeomorphic to the open unit disc of $\mathbb{R}^2$. A diffeomorphism $f$ of $T^2$ is isotopic to the identity when there is a smooth map $H : \mathbb{R} \times T^2 \to T^2$ with $H(0, \cdot) = \mathrm{id}$, $H(1, \cdot) = f$ and every $H(t, \cdot)$ a diffeomorphism.

Bowden, Hensel and Webb prove that for every genus $g \ge 1$ the group $\operatorname{Diff}_0(S_g)$ of the closed oriented surface $S_g$ is not uniformly perfect and has unbounded fragmentation norm; the torus is the case $g = 1$.
