# Torus diffeomorphism smoothly isotopic to the identity

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the two-torus with its standard smooth structure. A diffeomorphism $f$ of $T^2$ is isotopic to the identity when there is a smooth map $H : \mathbb{R} \times T^2 \to T^2$ with $H(0, \cdot) = \mathrm{id}$, $H(1, \cdot) = f$ and every $H(t, \cdot)$ a diffeomorphism.
