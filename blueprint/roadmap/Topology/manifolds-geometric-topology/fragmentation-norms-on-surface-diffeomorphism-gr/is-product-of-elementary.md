# Product of at most C elementary diffeomorphisms

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the two-torus with its standard smooth structure. A diffeomorphism $h$ of $T^2$ (smooth, with smooth inverse) is elementary when its support, the closure of $\{x : h(x) \neq x\}$, lies in an open set $U \subseteq T^2$ diffeomorphic to the open unit disc of $\mathbb{R}^2$. For $C \in \mathbb{N}$, a diffeomorphism $f$ of $T^2$ is a product of at most $C$ elementary diffeomorphisms when $f = h_1 \circ \dots \circ h_k$ for some $k \le C$ and elementary $h_1, \dots, h_k$ (the empty product being the identity).
