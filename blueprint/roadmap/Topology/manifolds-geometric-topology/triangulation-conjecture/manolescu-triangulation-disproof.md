# Manolescu's disproof of the triangulation conjecture

For every natural number $n \ge 5$
there is a closed connected topological $n$-manifold that is not triangulable.

## Sources

- C. Manolescu, *Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg–Witten Floer homology and the triangulation conjecture*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 29 (2016), via the Galewski–Stern and Matumoto reduction. Earlier, the triangulation conjecture was known false in dimension 4 (Casson). Via leanprover/lean-eval@38361be, problem `manolescu_triangulation_disproof`.
