# Sticky Cantor sets exist in every dimension at least four

For every $d \ge 4$ there is a Cantor set in $\mathbb{R}^d$ (a subset homeomorphic to $\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}}$) that is sticky (Krushkal). A subset $C \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ is sticky when there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every ambient isotopy $(H_t)_{t \in [0,1]}$ of $\mathbb{R}^d$ (homeomorphisms $H_t$ of $\mathbb{R}^d$ depending jointly continuously on $(x,t)$, with $H_0 = \mathrm{id}$) that moves every point by less than $\varepsilon$ at every time, $H_1(C) \cap C \neq \emptyset$.
