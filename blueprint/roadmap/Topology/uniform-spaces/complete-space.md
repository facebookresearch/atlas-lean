# Complete uniform space

A uniform space $X$ is complete if every Cauchy filter on $X$ converges: for every Cauchy filter $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$
there is a point $x \in X$ such that $\mathcal{F}$ is finer than the neighbourhood filter of $x$. For a metric space
this is the usual completeness, the convergence of every Cauchy sequence.
