# Non Orientable Rel

The representative non-orientable surface homeomorphic to a direct sum of `p` projective
planes with `n` discs removed, obtained by identifying the boundary of a disc in the pattern
`a₁a₁⋯aₚaₚc₁h₁c₁⁻¹⋯cₙhₙcₙ⁻¹`.
