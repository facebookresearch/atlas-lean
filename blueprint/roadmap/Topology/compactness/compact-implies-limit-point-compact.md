# Every infinite subset of a compact space has a limit point

Let $X$ be a compact topological space and let $A \subseteq X$ be an infinite subset. Then $A$ has a limit point in $X$: there is a point $x \in X$ such that every open set of $X$ containing $x$ contains a point of $A$ different from $x$.
