# Generalized tube lemma for a product of two compact sets

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces, give $X \times Y$ the product topology, and let $A \subseteq X$ and $B \subseteq Y$ be compact subsets. If $N$ is an open subset of $X \times Y$ with $A \times B \subseteq N$, then there are open sets $U \subseteq X$ and $V \subseteq Y$ with $A \subseteq U$, $B \subseteq V$ and $U \times V \subseteq N$.
