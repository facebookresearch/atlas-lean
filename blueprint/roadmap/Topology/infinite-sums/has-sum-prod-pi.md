# Sum of a finite product over a function space

Let $\iota$ be a finite type and $\kappa$ any type. For each $i \in \iota$ let $f_i : \kappa \to \mathbb{R}$ be a nonnegative family ($f_i(k) \ge 0$ for all $k$) with unconditional sum $\sum_{k \in \kappa} f_i(k) = a_i$. Then the family $x \mapsto \prod_{i \in \iota} f_i(x(i))$ indexed by the functions $x : \iota \to \kappa$ has unconditional sum $\prod_{i \in \iota} a_i$:

$$\sum_{x : \iota \to \kappa} \prod_{i \in \iota} f_i(x(i)) = \prod_{i \in \iota} \sum_{k \in \kappa} f_i(k).$$

This is the infinite-sum version of expanding a finite product of finite sums.
