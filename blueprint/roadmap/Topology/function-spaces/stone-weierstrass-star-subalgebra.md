# Stone–Weierstrass theorem for star subalgebras

Let $X$ be a compact topological space and $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Let $A$ be a subalgebra of
the algebra $C(X, \mathbb{K})$ of continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{K}$ that contains the constants, is closed
under complex conjugation $f \mapsto \bar f$, and separates points: for $x \ne y$ in $X$ there is $f \in A$ with
$f(x) \ne f(y)$. Then $A$ is dense in $C(X, \mathbb{K})$ for the topology of uniform convergence.
