# Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for simplices with any finite vertex set

Every continuous self-map of the simplex $\Delta(X)$ of probability vectors on a finite nonempty set $X$ has a fixed
point. It follows from the standard simplex case through an enumeration of $X$ by $\{0, \dots, n\}$.

## Informally uses

- [Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for the standard simplex](exists-fixed-point.md): applied to the conjugate of the map by an enumeration of the vertices.
