# Weights of a pushforward distribution

For a map $g \colon X \to Y$ from a finite set $X$, the pushforward along $g$ of a distribution $p$ on $X$ gives each
$y \in Y$ the total weight $\sum_{x : g(x) = y} p(x)$ of its preimage. In particular the $i$-th marginal of a
distribution on $\prod_i S_i$ is its pushforward along the projection $s \mapsto s_i$.
