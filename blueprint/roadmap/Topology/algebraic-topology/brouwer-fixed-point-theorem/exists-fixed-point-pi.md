# Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for products of simplices

Every continuous self-map of a finite product $\prod_i \Delta(S_i)$ of simplices with nonempty finite vertex sets
$S_i$ has a fixed point. The product is a retract of the simplex $\Delta(\prod_i S_i)$ (product distribution, then
marginals), so the statement follows from the case of a simplex with a finite vertex set. This is the form of
Brouwer's theorem used in J. Nash, "Non-cooperative games", Ann. of Math. 54 (1951), 286–295, where the product of
the players' mixed-strategy simplices is the domain of the fixed-point map.
