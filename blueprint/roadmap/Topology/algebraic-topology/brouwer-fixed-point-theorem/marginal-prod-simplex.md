# The marginals of a product distribution are its factors

For finitely many finite sets $S_j$ and distributions $\sigma_j \in \Delta(S_j)$, the $i$-th marginal of the product
distribution of the $\sigma_j$ is $\sigma_i$: summing $\prod_j \sigma_j(s_j)$ over the profiles with $s_i = t$ leaves
$\sigma_i(t)$, since every other factor sums to $1$.
