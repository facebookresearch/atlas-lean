# The product distribution depends continuously on its factors

For finitely many finite sets $S_i$, the map sending a family $(\sigma_i)_i \in \prod_i \Delta(S_i)$ to its product
distribution in $\Delta(\prod_i S_i)$ is continuous: each weight $\prod_i \sigma_i(s_i)$ is a product of coordinate
functions, continuous on the product of simplices.
