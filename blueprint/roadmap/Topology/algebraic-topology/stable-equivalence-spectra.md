# Stable equivalence: a map of sequential spectra inducing isomorphisms on all homotopy groups π_k, k ∈ Z

A map of sequential spectra is a sequence of based maps $f_n\colon E_n \to F_n$ satisfying $f_{n+1} \circ \sigma^E_n = \sigma^F_n \circ \Sigma f_n$ for all $n$. Such a map $f\colon E \to F$ induces homomorphisms $f_*\colon \pi_k E \to \pi_k F$ on the homotopy groups of the spectra, as the colimit of the maps $(f_n)_*$.

The map $f$ is a *stable equivalence* if $f_*\colon \pi_k E \to \pi_k F$ is an isomorphism for every $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.

## Expansion

A good expansion of this node links each notion it is built from: maps of directed systems and the induced maps of direct limits; maps of sequential spectra; Homotopy groups of a sequential spectrum.
