# Bottleneck cost of a matching

For a partial matching of graded barcodes $B$ and $C$, the bottleneck cost is the maximum of the distances between matched pairs and the distances from unmatched bars on either side to the diagonal. The maximum of an empty collection is zero. For a matched pair the distance is the largest endpoint discrepancy ($+\infty$ if only one death is infinite, with two infinite deaths contributing no discrepancy). An unmatched finite bar contributes half its length; an unmatched infinite bar contributes $+\infty$.
