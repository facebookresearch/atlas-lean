# Nerve of a family of sets

For a family $(U_i)_{i \in \iota}$ of subsets of a set $X$ (for instance a covering of $X$), the nerve is the
abstract simplicial complex on the index set $\iota$ whose faces are the nonempty finite sets of indices $s$ with
$\bigcap_{i \in s} U_i \ne \emptyset$ (Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, Def. 2.2).
