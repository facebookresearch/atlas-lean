# Clusters of a subset are disjoint or equal

Two single-linkage clusters of the same subset $A$ (for the same dissimilarity and scale) that share a point are
equal, since clusters are equivalence classes; so the clusters of $A$ partition $A$ (Carlsson, *Topology and data*,
Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2: single linkage produces a partition of the point cloud).
