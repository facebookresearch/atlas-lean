# $\delta$-interleaving

Let $\delta \ge 0$ and let $U, V$ be functors from $(\mathbb{R}, \le)$ to a category $C$
(persistence modules when $C$ is a category of vector spaces). A $\delta$-interleaving consists
of maps $\varphi_t \colon U_t \to V_{t+\delta}$ and $\psi_t \colon V_t \to U_{t+\delta}$, natural in
$t$, whose composites $U_t \to V_{t+\delta} \to U_{t+2\delta}$ and
$V_t \to U_{t+\delta} \to V_{t+2\delta}$ are the structure maps (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot,
*The structure and stability of persistence modules*, arXiv:1207.3674, §3.2, diagrams (3.1);
stated there for $C$ the category of vector spaces). The interleaving distance is the infimum of
the $\delta$ for which one exists.
