# Triangle inequality for interleavings

A $\delta_1$-interleaving between $U$ and $V$ and a $\delta_2$-interleaving between $V$ and $W$
compose to a $(\delta_1 + \delta_2)$-interleaving between $U$ and $W$, so the interleaving distance
satisfies the triangle inequality (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot, arXiv:1207.3674,
Prop. 4.3).
