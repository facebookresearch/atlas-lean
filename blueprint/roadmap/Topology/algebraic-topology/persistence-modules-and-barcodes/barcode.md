# Persistence barcode

A persistence barcode is a finite multiset of bars. A bar $(k,b,d)$ represents the half-open interval $[b,d)$ in degree $k\in\mathbb N$, with birth $b\in\mathbb R$, death $d\in\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$, and $b<d$. Repeated bars count separately.
