# Bottleneck distance between barcodes

For two finite multisets of graded bars $B,C$, the bottleneck distance is the infimum, over all degree-preserving partial matchings of their occurrences, of the bottleneck cost. For each matching take the maximum of the endpoint distances of matched pairs and the half-lengths of unmatched bars on both sides (zero for an empty maximum). A finite and an infinite death have infinite distance, two infinite deaths contribute zero death discrepancy, and an unpaired infinite bar has infinite half-length.
