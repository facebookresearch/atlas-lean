# Erosion distance between barcodes

Write $r_B(p,s,t)$ for the number of degree-$p$ bars of $B$ born by $s$ and dying strictly after $t$, counted with multiplicity. The erosion distance between $B$ and $C$ is the infimum of all real $\varepsilon\ge0$ such that, for every $p\in\mathbb N$ and all real $s\le t$,

$$r_B(p,s-\varepsilon,t+\varepsilon)\le r_C(p,s,t),\qquad r_C(p,s-\varepsilon,t+\varepsilon)\le r_B(p,s,t).$$

The infimum lies in $[0,+\infty]$ and is $+\infty$ if no such $\varepsilon$ exists.
