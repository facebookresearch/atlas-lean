# Algebraic stability theorem

Let $U$ and $V$ be pointwise finite-dimensional persistence modules over $\mathbb{R}$, with barcodes
$(I_i)$ and $(J_j)$, that is, interval decompositions $U \cong \bigoplus_i k_{I_i}$ and
$V \cong \bigoplus_j k_{J_j}$ into interval modules of nonempty order-connected sets. If $U$ and $V$
are $\delta$-interleaved, then for every $\delta' > \delta$ there is a $\delta'$-matching between
their barcodes; that is, the bottleneck distance of the persistence diagrams is at most the
interleaving distance (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot, arXiv:1207.3674, Thm. 4.11, the
stability inequality $d_i \ge d_b$, stated there for q-tame modules, which include the pointwise
finite-dimensional ones; the source attributes the stability theorem to Cohen-Steiner,
Edelsbrunner and Harer, *Stability of persistence diagrams*, DCG 37 (2007), and to Chazal,
Cohen-Steiner, Glisse, Guibas and Oudot, *Proximity of persistence modules and their diagrams*,
SoCG 2009).
