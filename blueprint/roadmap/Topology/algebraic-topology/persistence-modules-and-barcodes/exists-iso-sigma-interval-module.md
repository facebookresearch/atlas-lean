# Crawley-Boevey's decomposition theorem

Every pointwise finite-dimensional persistence module $V$ over $\mathbb{R}$ with values in vector
spaces over a field $k$ (each $V_t$ finite-dimensional) is isomorphic to a direct sum of interval
modules $k_I$, with $I$ ranging over a family of nonempty order-connected subsets of $\mathbb{R}$
(W. Crawley-Boevey, *Decomposition of pointwise finite-dimensional persistence modules*,
J. Algebra Appl. 14 (2015), arXiv:1210.0819, Thm. 1.1, for any totally ordered index set with a
countable dense subset; here the index set is $\mathbb{R}$). The family of intervals is the barcode.
