# Distance between two persistence bars

Two bars of different degrees have distance $+\infty$. For bars of the same degree with births $b_1,b_2\in\mathbb R$ and deaths $d_1,d_2\in\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$, the distance is the larger of $|b_1-b_2|$ and the distance between the deaths. The latter is $|d_1-d_2|$ for finite deaths, zero for two infinite deaths, and infinite when just one death is infinite.
