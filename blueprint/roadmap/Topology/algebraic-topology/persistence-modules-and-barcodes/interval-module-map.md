# Structure maps of an interval module

For a subset $I$ of a preordered set $T$ and points $s, t \in T$, the map from the fibre at $s$ to
the fibre at $t$ is the identity of $k$ when $s, t \in I$, and zero otherwise (Crawley-Boevey,
J. Algebra Appl. 14 (2015), §1, $\rho_{ts} = 1$ for $s, t \in I$). Here the fibre at $t$ is $k$
when $t \in I$ and the zero space otherwise.
