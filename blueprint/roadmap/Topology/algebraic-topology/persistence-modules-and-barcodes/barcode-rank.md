# Rank function of a barcode

For a finite multiset $D$ of graded half-open bars, its rank at degree $p\in\mathbb N$ and thresholds $s,t\in\mathbb R$ is the number, with multiplicity, of bars $(p,b,d)$ in $D$ such that $b\le s$ and $t<d$. An infinite death satisfies the second inequality for every real $t$. The count is defined even when $s>t$.
