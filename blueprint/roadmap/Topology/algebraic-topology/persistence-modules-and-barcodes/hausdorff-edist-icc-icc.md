# Hausdorff distance between closed intervals

For $a \le b$ and $c \le d$, the Hausdorff distance between $[a, b]$ and $[c, d]$ is
$\max(|a - c|, |b - d|)$, the $\ell^\infty$ distance between the points $(a, b)$ and $(c, d)$ of
the persistence plane used for bottleneck matchings (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot,
arXiv:1207.3674, §4.2, $d_\infty$). This is why a [$\delta$-matching](is-matching.md) can measure
matched bars by their Hausdorff distance.
