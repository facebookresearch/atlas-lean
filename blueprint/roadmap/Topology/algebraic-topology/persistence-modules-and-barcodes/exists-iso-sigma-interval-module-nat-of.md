# Barcodes of tame $\mathbb{N}$-persistence modules (Zomorodian–Carlsson)

Let $V$ be a persistence module over $\mathbb{N}$ with values in vector spaces over a field $k$. If
every $V_n$ is finite-dimensional and the structure maps $V_n \to V_{n+1}$ are isomorphisms for
all large $n$, then $V$ is isomorphic to a finite direct sum of interval modules $U(m, n)$,
$m \le n \le \infty$, that is, of interval modules of nonempty order-connected subsets of
$\mathbb{N}$ (G. Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 46 (2009), Prop. 2.12, from Prop. 2.11 and
the structure theorem for finitely generated graded $k[t]$-modules, Thm. 2.10; A. Zomorodian and
G. Carlsson, *Computing persistent homology*, Discrete Comput. Geom. 33 (2005)).
