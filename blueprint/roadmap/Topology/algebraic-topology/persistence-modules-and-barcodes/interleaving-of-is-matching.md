# Converse stability for decomposable modules

Let $U \cong \bigoplus_i k_{I_i}$ and $V \cong \bigoplus_j k_{J_j}$ be direct sums of interval
modules over $\mathbb{R}$, with nonempty order-connected bars $I_i$ and $J_j$. If there is a
$\delta$-matching between the barcodes $(I_i)$ and $(J_j)$, then $U$ and $V$ are
$\delta'$-interleaved for every $\delta' > \delta$; that is, the interleaving distance is at most
the bottleneck distance (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot, arXiv:1207.3674, Thm. 4.9).
