# The barcode is unique (Krull–Remak–Schmidt–Azumaya)

If a persistence module over $\mathbb{R}$ is a direct sum of interval modules of nonempty
order-connected sets in two ways, $\bigoplus_{i \in \iota} k_{I_i} \cong \bigoplus_{j \in \kappa} k_{J_j}$,
then there is a bijection $\sigma \colon \iota \to \kappa$ with $J_{\sigma(i)} = I_i$ for all $i$:
the multiset of intervals is an isomorphism invariant (Chazal, de Silva, Glisse and Oudot, *The
structure and stability of persistence modules*, arXiv:1207.3674, Thm. 1.3, from Azumaya's
theorem).
