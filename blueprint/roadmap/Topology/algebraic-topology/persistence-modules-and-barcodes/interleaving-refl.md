# Every functor is $\delta$-interleaved with itself for every $\delta \ge 0$

Every functor $U$ from $(\mathbb{R}, \le)$ to a category $C$ is $\delta$-interleaved with itself for
every $\delta \ge 0$, using its own structure maps $U_t \to U_{t+\delta}$ in both directions
(Chazal–de Silva–Glisse–Oudot, §3.2: the shift map $1_V^\delta$).
