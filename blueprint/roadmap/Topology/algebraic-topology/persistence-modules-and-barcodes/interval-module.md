# Interval module

For an order-connected subset $I$ of a preordered set $T$ (an interval when nonempty) and a field
$k$, the interval module $k_I$ is the functor from $T$ to $k$-vector spaces with fibre $k$ at every
$t \in I$ and $0$ elsewhere, with identity structure maps between points of $I$ and zero maps
otherwise (Crawley-Boevey, J. Algebra Appl. 14 (2015), §1; Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull.
AMS 2009, the modules $U(m, n)$ before Prop. 2.12). Order-connectedness is what makes the
structure maps compose.
