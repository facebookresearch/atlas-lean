# Contractible space

A topological space $X$ is *contractible* if it is homotopy equivalent to a one-point space $\{p\}$. That is, there are continuous maps $f\colon X \to \{p\}$ and $g\colon \{p\} \to X$ such that $g \circ f$ is homotopic to the identity map of $X$; the composite $f \circ g$ is automatically the identity map of $\{p\}$.
