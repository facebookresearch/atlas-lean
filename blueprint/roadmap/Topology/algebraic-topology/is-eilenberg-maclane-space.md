# Eilenberg–MacLane space

Let $X$ be a topological space, $G$ a group and $n \ge 1$ an integer. Then $X$ is an Eilenberg–MacLane space $K(G, n)$ when $X$ is nonempty and, for every base point $x \in X$, the homotopy group $\pi_n(X, x)$ is isomorphic to $G$ as a group and $\pi_i(X, x)$ has at most one element for every $i \ge 0$ with $i \neq n$. Taking $i = 0$, such a space is path-connected. No CW structure on $X$ is required.
