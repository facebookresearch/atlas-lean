# Path in a topological space

Let $X$ be a topological space and let $x, y \in X$. Give the unit interval $I = [0, 1]$ the subspace topology from the standard topology of $\mathbb{R}$. A *path* in $X$ from $x$ to $y$ is a continuous map $f\colon I \to X$ with $f(0) = x$ and $f(1) = y$. The points $x$ and $y$ are the initial point and the final point of $f$.
