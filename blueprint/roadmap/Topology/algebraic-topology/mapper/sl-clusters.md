# Single-linkage clusters of a subset

The clusters at scale $\varepsilon$ of a subset $A \subseteq X$ are the classes of $\varepsilon$-equivalence for the
dissimilarity $d$ restricted to $A$, viewed as subsets of $X$: chains must stay inside $A$ (Carlsson, *Topology and
data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2, step (4)).
