# Dimension of the Mapper complex

If the covering $(U_i)_{i \in \iota}$ has dimension at most $n$, that is, any finite set of indices whose sets $U_i$
have a common point has at most $n + 1$ elements, then every simplex of the Mapper complex has at most $n + 1$
vertices (Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2, the remark after step (5)). Clusters of the same
preimage that share a point coincide, so the vertices of a simplex lie over distinct cover elements.
