# Mapper complex

For a dissimilarity $d$ on $X$, a scale $\varepsilon$, a filter $f \colon X \to Z$ and a family
$(U_\alpha)_{\alpha \in \iota}$ of subsets of $Z$ (in applications, a covering of the reference space), the Mapper complex is the
nerve of the family of single-linkage clusters $c$ of the preimages $f^{-1}(U_\alpha)$, indexed by the pairs
$(\alpha, c)$: a finite set of such pairs spans a simplex when the clusters have a point in common (Carlsson,
*Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §3.2, steps (1)–(5), with single-linkage clustering in step (4); G. Singh,
F. Mémoli and G. Carlsson, *Topological methods for the analysis of high dimensional data sets and 3D object
recognition*, SPBG 2007).
