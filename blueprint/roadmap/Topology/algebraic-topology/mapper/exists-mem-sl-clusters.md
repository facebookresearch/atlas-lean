# The clusters of a subset cover it

Every point of a subset $A$ lies in a single-linkage cluster of $A$, so the clusters of the preimages
$f^{-1}(U_\alpha)$ cover $X$ when the $U_\alpha$ cover the image of $f$ (Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS
2009, §3.2, step (4): the clusters form a covering of $X$).
