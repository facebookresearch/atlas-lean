# Suspension of a topological space

Let $X$ be a topological space. On the cylinder $X \times [0,1]$ let $(x,t) \sim (y,s)$ if and only if $(x,t) = (y,s)$, or $t = s = 0$, or $t = s = 1$. The suspension $S(X)$ is the quotient $(X \times [0,1])/{\sim}$. When $X$ is nonempty, this collapses $X \times \{0\}$ and $X \times \{1\}$ to one point each; when $X$ is empty, $S(X)$ is empty.
