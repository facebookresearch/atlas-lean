# Cone on a topological space

Let $X$ be a topological space and give $[0,1] \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ the subspace topology. On the cylinder $X \times [0,1]$ with the product topology, let $(x,t) \sim (y,s)$ if and only if $(x,t) = (y,s)$ or $t = s = 0$. The cone $C(X)$ is the quotient $(X \times [0,1])/{\sim}$ with the quotient topology, the finest topology for which the quotient map $X \times [0,1] \to C(X)$ is continuous. When $X$ is nonempty, it collapses $X \times \{0\}$ to a single point; when $X$ is empty, the cylinder and hence $C(X)$ are empty.
