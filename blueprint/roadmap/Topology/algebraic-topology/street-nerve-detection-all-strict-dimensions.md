# Street nerve detection of Thomason weak equivalences in all supplied dimensions

In strict globular dimension $\omega$ and for each natural-number dimension $n$ (the detection comparator does not assume $n\ge1$), a functor is a weak equivalence in the inverse-image class defined by $\mathrm{Ex}^2N_{\rm St}$ if and only if its unextended Street nerve is a weak equivalence of simplicial sets. A simplicial map here is tested after realization by bijectivity on components and all positive based homotopy groups. The Street nerve uses maps from the strict orientals, with the finite-dimensional version into the given strict $n$-category.

## Depends on

- The Street nerve with finite-dimensional restrictions, its twice-extended functor, and geometric-realization weak equivalences.

## Proof depends on

- The natural comparison to the double extension preserves and reflects the simplicial weak-equivalence class, with compatible finite-dimensional nerve formulas.

## Expansion

Isolate the simplicial comparison lemma for the canonical extension maps, then apply it to each nerve naturality square. Do not infer a dimension-zero model structure from the detection equation.
