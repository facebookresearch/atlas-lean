# Fixed points and antipodal maps

Fixed-point and antipodal-map theorems of algebraic topology: Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for simplices, closed finite-dimensional balls and products of simplices, the Borsuk–Ulam theorem, and consequences such as the ham sandwich theorem; fixed points of nonexpansive and affine maps are in Analysis's fixed-point theory.
