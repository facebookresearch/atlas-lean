# Relative Hurewicz map

Let $n \ge 1$, let $(X, A)$ be a pair of spaces, and let $x_0 \in A$. Relative singular homology has integer coefficients. Fix a generator $\beta$ of the infinite cyclic group $H_n(D^n, S^{n-1})$ and a basepoint $s_0 \in S^{n-1}$. Represent elements of the relative homotopy group (for $n = 1$, the pointed set) $\pi_n(X, A, x_0)$ as homotopy classes of maps of triples $f\colon (D^n, S^{n-1}, s_0) \to (X, A, x_0)$. The *relative Hurewicz map* $h\colon \pi_n(X, A, x_0) \to H_n(X, A)$ is $h([f]) = f_*(\beta)$. Here $f_*\colon H_n(D^n, S^{n-1}) \to H_n(X, A)$ is the induced map on relative singular homology, and its value depends only on the homotopy class of $f$.

## Expansion

A good expansion of this node links each notion it is built from: the homology of a disc relative to its boundary sphere; the homotopy invariance of relative singular homology; the homomorphisms induced on relative homology by maps of pairs.
