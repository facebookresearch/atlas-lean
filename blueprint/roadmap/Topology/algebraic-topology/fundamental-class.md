# Fundamental class of a closed R-oriented manifold

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity, and let $M$ be a closed topological $n$-manifold (compact, without boundary, and not necessarily connected) with an $R$-orientation $x \mapsto \mu_x$. A *fundamental class* of $M$ for this $R$-orientation is a class $[M] \in H_n(M; R)$ whose image under the map $H_n(M; R) \to H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R)$ induced by the inclusion of pairs $(M, \emptyset) \hookrightarrow (M, M \setminus \{x\})$ equals $\mu_x$ for every $x \in M$.

## Expansion

A good expansion of this node links each notion it is built from: singular homology with coefficients; the homomorphisms induced on relative homology by maps of pairs.
