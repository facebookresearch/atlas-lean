# Čech inside Rips at twice the scale

For every $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, $\check{C}(M, \varepsilon) \subseteq VR(M, 2\varepsilon)$: points whose closed
$\varepsilon$-balls share a point are pairwise at distance at most $2\varepsilon$ (Carlsson, *Topology and data*,
Bull. AMS 2009, Prop. 2.6, first inclusion).
