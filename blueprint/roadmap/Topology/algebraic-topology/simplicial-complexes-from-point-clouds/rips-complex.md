# Vietoris–Rips complex

For a pseudometric space $M$ and a scale $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, the Vietoris–Rips complex
$VR(M, \varepsilon)$ has as faces the nonempty finite sets of points whose pairwise distances are all at most
$\varepsilon$ (Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, Def. 2.5).
