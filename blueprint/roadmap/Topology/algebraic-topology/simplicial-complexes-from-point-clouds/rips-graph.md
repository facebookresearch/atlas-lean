# The $\varepsilon$-neighbourhood graph

For a pseudometric space $M$ and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, two distinct points are adjacent when their distance
is at most $\varepsilon$. For $\varepsilon \ge 0$ this is the one-skeleton of the Vietoris–Rips complex $VR(M, \varepsilon)$ (Carlsson,
*Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, Def. 2.5 and §3.2).
