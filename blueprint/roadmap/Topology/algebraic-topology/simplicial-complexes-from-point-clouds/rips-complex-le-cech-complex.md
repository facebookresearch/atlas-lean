# Rips inside Čech at the same scale

For every $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, $VR(M, \varepsilon) \subseteq \check{C}(M, \varepsilon)$: if the pairwise
distances of a nonempty finite set are at most $\varepsilon$, any of its points lies in all the closed
$\varepsilon$-balls. With $2\varepsilon$ in place of $\varepsilon$ this is the second inclusion
$VR(M, 2\varepsilon) \subseteq \check{C}(M, 2\varepsilon)$ of Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009,
Prop. 2.6.
