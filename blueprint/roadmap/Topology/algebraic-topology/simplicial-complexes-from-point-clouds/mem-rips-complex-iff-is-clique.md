# The Vietoris–Rips complex is the clique complex of its one-skeleton

For $\varepsilon \ge 0$, a nonempty finite set of points is a face of $VR(M, \varepsilon)$ exactly when it is a
clique of the $\varepsilon$-neighbourhood graph: the Vietoris–Rips complex is determined by its edges, which is the
reason Carlsson gives for preferring it to the Čech complex (Carlsson, *Topology and data*, Bull. AMS 2009, §2.2,
the paragraph before Def. 2.5).
