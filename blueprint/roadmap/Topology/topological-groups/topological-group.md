# Topological group

A *topological group* is a group $G$ together with a topology on the set $G$ such that the multiplication map $G \times G \to G$, $(g, h) \mapsto gh$, is continuous, where $G \times G$ carries the product topology, and the inversion map $G \to G$, $g \mapsto g^{-1}$, is continuous. No separation axiom is imposed; some authors also require the topology to be $T_1$ or Hausdorff.

## Sources

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
- nLab, *topological group*, revision 42, https://ncatlab.org/nlab/revision/topological+group/42, section "Definition": a group with a topology for which the multiplication, with the product topology on its domain, and the inversion are continuous; no separation axiom is imposed.
