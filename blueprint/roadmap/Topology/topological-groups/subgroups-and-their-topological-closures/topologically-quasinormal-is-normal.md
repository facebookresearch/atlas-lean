# Kourovka Problem 9.33 (the Kümmich–Scheerer conjecture)

The statement that every topologically quasinormal subgroup of a connected Hausdorff locally compact group is normal. A closed subgroup $Q$ of a topological group $G$ is topologically quasinormal when $\overline{AQ} = \overline{QA}$ for every closed subgroup $A$ of $G$.
