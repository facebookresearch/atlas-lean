# Topologically quasinormal subgroups of connected locally compact groups are normal

Every topologically quasinormal subgroup of a connected Hausdorff locally compact group is normal (Scheiderer), the answer the Kourovka Archive gives to Problem 9.33. A closed subgroup $Q$ of a topological group $G$ is topologically quasinormal when $\overline{AQ} = \overline{QA}$ for every closed subgroup $A$ of $G$.
