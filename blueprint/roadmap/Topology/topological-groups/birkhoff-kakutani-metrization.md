# Birkhoff–Kakutani metrization theorem

Every Hausdorff first-countable topological group is
metrizable.

## Sources

- G. Birkhoff, Compositio Math. 3 (1936), 427-430; S. Kakutani, Proc. Imp. Acad. Tokyo 12 (1936), 4-7, DOI 10.3792/PIA/1195580206. Proves `Wanted` entry `birkhoff_kakutani_metrization`.
