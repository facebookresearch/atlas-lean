# Kourovka Problem 16.42 (Zelenyuk's question)

The statement that a Hausdorff abelian topological group $(G, \tau)$ is compact whenever $G$ is complete, for the uniformity of the group, in every Hausdorff group topology $\tau' \subseteq \tau$. For an abelian group the left and right uniformities coincide.
