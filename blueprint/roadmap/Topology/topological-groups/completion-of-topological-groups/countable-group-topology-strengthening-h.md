# Complete strengthenings of first-countable group topologies on countable groups

In every countable group $G$, every non-discrete Hausdorff first-countable group topology $\tau$ can be refined to a non-discrete group topology $\sigma \supseteq \tau$ in which $G$ is Raikov complete; Kourovka Problem 10.7 has a positive answer. A group $G$ with a topology is Raikov complete when every proper filter $\mathcal{F}$ on $G$ that is Cauchy for the two-sided uniformity (for every neighbourhood $U$ of $1$ some $A \in \mathcal{F}$ has $x^{-1}y \in U$ and $yx^{-1} \in U$ for all $x, y \in A$) converges to some point of $G$.
