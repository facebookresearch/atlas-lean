# Raikov completeness of a group topology

A group $G$ with a topology is Raikov complete when every proper filter $\mathcal{F}$ on $G$ that is Cauchy for the two-sided uniformity (for every neighbourhood $U$ of $1$ some $A \in \mathcal{F}$ has $x^{-1}y \in U$ and $yx^{-1} \in U$ for all $x, y \in A$) converges to some point of $G$. This is completeness for the two-sided uniformity, the weakest of the standard notions, implied by completeness for the left or the right uniformity.
