# Action continuous for the discrete topology

Let $G$ be a topological space acting on a set $M$ by a map $(g,x) \mapsto g \cdot x$. The action is continuous for the discrete topology on $M$ when, for all $x, y \in M$, the set $\{ g \in G : g \cdot x = y \}$ is open in $G$. No topology on $M$ is assumed; when $M$ carries the discrete topology the condition is equivalent to joint continuity of the action $G \times M \to M$.
