# Continuity for the discrete topology is joint continuity

Let $G$ be a topological space acting on a set $M$ by a map $(g,x) \mapsto g \cdot x$. Suppose $M$ carries the discrete topology. Then every set $\{ g \in G : g \cdot x = y \}$ ($x, y \in M$) is open in $G$ if and only if the action map $G \times M \to M$ is continuous.
