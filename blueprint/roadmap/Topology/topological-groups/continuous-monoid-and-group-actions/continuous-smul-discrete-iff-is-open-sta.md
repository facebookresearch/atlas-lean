# Continuity for the discrete topology via open stabilizers

Let $G$ be a group with a topology in which multiplication $G \times G \to G$ is continuous, acting on a set $M$. Then the sets $\{ g \in G : g \cdot x = y \}$ are open in $G$ for all $x, y \in M$ if and only if the stabilizer $\{ g \in G : g \cdot x = x \}$ of every $x \in M$ is open in $G$. No topology on $M$ is assumed.
