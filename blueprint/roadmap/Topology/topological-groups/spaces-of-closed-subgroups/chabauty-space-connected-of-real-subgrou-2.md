# A locally compact group containing ℝ with disconnected Chabauty space

There is a Hausdorff locally compact group $G$ whose space of closed subgroups is not connected in the Chabauty topology, although $G$ contains a subgroup topologically isomorphic to the additive group $\mathbb{R}$, that is, the image of a group homomorphism $\mathbb{R} \to G$ that is a topological embedding; Kourovka Problem 8.61 has a negative answer. The Chabauty topology on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \cap K = \emptyset\}$ for compact $K \subseteq G$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$.

The Kourovka Archive answers "No, not always", citing Tsybenko: the group of matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & z_1 & r \\ 0 & 1 & z_2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ with $z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $r \in \mathbb{R}$ is such a group. For locally compact abelian groups the Chabauty space is connected exactly when there is such a subgroup (I. V. Protasov, Selective survey on spaces of closed subgroups of topological groups, arXiv:1809.00139 (2018), Theorem 1.8).
