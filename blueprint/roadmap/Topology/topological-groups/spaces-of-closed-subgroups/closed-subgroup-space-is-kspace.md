# Kourovka Problem 9.46 (closed-subgroup spaces as k-spaces)

The statement that for every Hausdorff locally compact group $G$ of countable weight (second countable), the space of closed subgroups of $G$ with the $E$-topology is a $k$-space. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$. A space is a $k$-space (compactly generated) when a set is open exactly if its preimage under every continuous map from a compact Hausdorff space is open.
