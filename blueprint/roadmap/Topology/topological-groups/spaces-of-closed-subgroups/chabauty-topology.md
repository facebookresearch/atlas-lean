# Chabauty topology on closed subgroups

The Chabauty topology on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \cap K = \emptyset\}$ for compact $K \subseteq G$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$.
