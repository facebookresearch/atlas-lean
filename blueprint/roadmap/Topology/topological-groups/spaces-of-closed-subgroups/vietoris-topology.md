# Vietoris topology on closed subgroups

The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$.
