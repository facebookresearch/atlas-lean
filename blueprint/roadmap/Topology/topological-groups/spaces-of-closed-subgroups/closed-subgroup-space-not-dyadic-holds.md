# Closed-subgroup spaces of large profinite or abelian compact groups are not dyadic

If $G$ is a Hausdorff compact group that is profinite or abelian, of weight greater than $\aleph_2$ (every base of its topology has more than $\aleph_2$ members), then the space of closed subgroups of $G$ with the $E$-topology is not dyadic. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$. A topological space is dyadic when it is a continuous image of a Cantor cube $\{0,1\}^\kappa$ (product of discrete two-point spaces) for some index set $\kappa$. For compact abelian $G$ this space is dyadic exactly when the weight of $G$ is less than $\aleph_2$; the Kourovka Archive answers Problem 9.49 "Yes", citing Tsybenko.
