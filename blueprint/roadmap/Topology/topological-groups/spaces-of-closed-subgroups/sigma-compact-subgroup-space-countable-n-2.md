# Locally compact groups with σ-compact closed-subgroup space have countably many closed non-compact subgroups

If $G$ is a Hausdorff locally compact group whose space of closed subgroups is $\sigma$-compact in the $E$-topology, then $G$ has at most countably many closed non-compact subgroups; Kourovka Problem 8.63 has a positive answer. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$. Piskunov's Theorem 4 states more: this space is $\sigma$-compact exactly when $G$ is $\sigma$-compact and has countably many closed non-compact subgroups.
