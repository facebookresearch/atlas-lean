# Kourovka Problem 8.63 (σ-compact spaces of closed subgroups)

The statement that if $G$ is a Hausdorff locally compact group whose space of closed subgroups is $\sigma$-compact in the $E$-topology, then $G$ has at most countably many closed non-compact subgroups. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$.
