# Kourovka Problem 9.49 for profinite and abelian compact groups

The statement that if $G$ is a Hausdorff compact group that is totally disconnected (profinite) or abelian, of weight greater than $\aleph_2$ (every base of its topology has more than $\aleph_2$ members), then the space of closed subgroups of $G$ with the $E$-topology is not dyadic. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$. A topological space is dyadic when it is a continuous image of a Cantor cube $\{0,1\}^\kappa$ (product of discrete two-point spaces) for some index set $\kappa$. Kourovka Problem 9.49 asks this for every compact group of weight greater than $\aleph_2$; this statement covers the profinite and the abelian ones.
