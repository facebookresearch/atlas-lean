# The space of closed subgroups of a second-countable locally compact group is a k-space

For every second-countable Hausdorff locally compact group $G$, the space of closed subgroups of $G$ with the $E$-topology is a $k$-space; Kourovka Problem 9.46 has a positive answer. The $E$-topology (Vietoris topology) on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \subseteq U\}$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$. A space is a $k$-space (compactly generated) when a set is open exactly if its preimage under every continuous map from a compact Hausdorff space is open.
