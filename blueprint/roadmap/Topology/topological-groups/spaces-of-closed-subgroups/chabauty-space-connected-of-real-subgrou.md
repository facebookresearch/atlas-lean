# Kourovka Problem 8.61 (Protasov's question on Chabauty spaces)

The statement that for every Hausdorff locally compact group $G$ containing a subgroup topologically isomorphic to the additive group $\mathbb{R}$, that is, a group homomorphism $\mathbb{R} \to G$ that is a topological embedding, the space of closed subgroups of $G$ with the Chabauty topology is connected. The Chabauty topology on the set of closed subgroups of a topological group $G$ is the topology generated by the sets $\{H : H \cap K = \emptyset\}$ for compact $K \subseteq G$ and $\{H : H \cap U \neq \emptyset\}$ for open $U \subseteq G$.
