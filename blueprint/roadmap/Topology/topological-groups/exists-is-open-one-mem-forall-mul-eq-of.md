# Locally constant compactly supported functions have uniform stabilizers

Let $G$ be a topological group and $M$ a set with a distinguished element $0$. Every locally constant function
$\Phi \colon G \to M$ with compact support is invariant under left and right multiplication by the elements of some
open neighbourhood $W$ of $1$: $\Phi(gw) = \Phi(wg) = \Phi(g)$ for all $g \in G$ and $w \in W$.
