# A set closed in a closed subspace is closed in the whole space

Let $X$ be a topological space and let $A \subseteq X$ be a closed subset, with the subspace topology. If $C \subseteq A$ is closed in $A$, then $C$ is closed in $X$.

## Sources

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
