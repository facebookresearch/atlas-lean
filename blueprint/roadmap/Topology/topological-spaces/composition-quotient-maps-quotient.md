# The composite of two quotient maps is a quotient map

Let $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ be topological spaces, and let $p\colon X \to Y$ and $q\colon Y \to Z$ be quotient maps, that is, surjective maps such that a subset of the codomain is open if and only if its preimage is open. Then $q \circ p\colon X \to Z$ is a quotient map.
