# A point lies in the closure of a subset of a topological space iff every open set containing the point meets the subset

Let $X$ be a topological space, let $A \subseteq X$, and let $\overline{A}$ be the closure of $A$, the intersection of all closed sets of $X$ that contain $A$. A point $x \in X$ lies in $\overline{A}$ if and only if $U \cap A \neq \varnothing$ for every open set $U$ of $X$ with $x \in U$.
