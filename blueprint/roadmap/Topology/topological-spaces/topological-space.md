# Topological space

A *topology* on a set $X$ is a collection of subsets of $X$, called *open sets*, such that $X$ is open, the intersection of any two open sets is open, and the union of any family of open sets is open; in particular the empty set, the union of the empty family, is open. A *topological space* is a set together with a topology on it.
