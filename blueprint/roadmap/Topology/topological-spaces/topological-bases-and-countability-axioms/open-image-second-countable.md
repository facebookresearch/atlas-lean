# A continuous surjective open image of a second-countable space is second countable

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $f\colon X \to Y$ be a continuous surjective open map. If $X$ is second countable, that is, its topology has a countable basis, then $Y$ is second countable.

## Sources

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
