# Sequences in a compact set have convergent subsequences

Let $X$ be a first-countable topological space, for instance a metric space such as $\mathbb R^n$, let $s \subseteq X$
be compact, and let $(x_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a sequence of points of $s$. Then there are a point $a \in s$ and a
strictly increasing $\varphi \colon \mathbb N \to \mathbb N$ such that $x_{\varphi(n)} \to a$ as $n \to \infty$.
