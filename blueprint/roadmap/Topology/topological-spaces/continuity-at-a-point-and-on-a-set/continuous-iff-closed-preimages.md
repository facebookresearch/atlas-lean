# A map between topological spaces is continuous if and only if preimages of closed sets are closed

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $f\colon X \to Y$ be a function. Then $f$ is continuous if and only if, for every closed subset $C \subseteq Y$, the preimage $f^{-1}(C)$ is closed in $X$.
