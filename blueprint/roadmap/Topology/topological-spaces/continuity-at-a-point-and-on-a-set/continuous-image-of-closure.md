# A continuous map sends the closure of a set into the closure of its image

Let $f\colon X \to Y$ be a continuous map between topological spaces and let $A \subseteq X$. Then $f(\overline{A}) \subseteq \overline{f(A)}$, where $\overline{A}$ is the closure of $A$ in $X$ and $\overline{f(A)}$ is the closure of $f(A)$ in $Y$.
