# Closure of a subset

Let $X$ be a topological space and let $A \subseteq X$. The *closure* $\overline{A}$ of $A$ is the intersection of all closed subsets of $X$ that contain $A$. This family is not empty, since $X$ itself is closed and contains $A$.
