# A finite union of closed sets is closed

Let $X$ be a topological space and let $(C_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of closed subsets of $X$ indexed by a finite set $I$. Then the union $\bigcup_{i \in I} C_i$ is closed in $X$. In particular, for closed sets $C_1, \dots, C_n$ the set $C_1 \cup \dots \cup C_n$ is closed, and the empty union, which is $\varnothing$, is closed.
