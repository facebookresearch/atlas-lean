# Boundary of a subset

Let $X$ be a topological space and let $A \subseteq X$. The *boundary*, or *frontier*, of $A$ is the set $\partial A = \overline{A} \setminus \operatorname{Int} A$ of the points of the closure $\overline{A}$ of $A$ that do not lie in the interior $\operatorname{Int} A$ of $A$.

## Sources

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
- nLab, *boundary*, revision 16, https://ncatlab.org/nlab/revision/boundary/16, section "Of a subset of a topological space": the boundary, or frontier, of a subset is its closure minus its interior.
