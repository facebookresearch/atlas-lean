# A map into a product space is continuous iff each coordinate function is continuous

Let $(X_\alpha)_{\alpha \in J}$ be a family of topological spaces indexed by an arbitrary set $J$, and give $\prod_{\alpha \in J} X_\alpha$ the product topology. For $\beta \in J$ let $\pi_\beta$ be the projection onto the factor $X_\beta$. Let $A$ be a topological space and $f\colon A \to \prod_{\alpha \in J} X_\alpha$ a function with coordinate functions $f_\alpha = \pi_\alpha \circ f$. Then $f$ is continuous if and only if every $f_\alpha\colon A \to X_\alpha$ is continuous.

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
