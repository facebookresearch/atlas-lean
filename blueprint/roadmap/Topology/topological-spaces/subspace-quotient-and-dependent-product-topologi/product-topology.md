# Product topology

For a family $(X_\alpha)_{\alpha \in J}$ of topological spaces, the product topology on $\prod_{\alpha} X_\alpha$ is the topology generated by the subbasis of sets $\pi_\beta^{-1}(U_\beta)$ with $\beta \in J$ and $U_\beta$ open in $X_\beta$, so that its open sets are the unions of finite intersections of such sets. Mathlib defines the topology on the product of an arbitrary family of topological spaces $X_i$, the type of functions $f$ with $f(i) \in X_i$ for every index $i$, as the coarsest topology for which every projection $f \mapsto f(i)$ is continuous: in Mathlib’s order on topologies, in which finer topologies are smaller, it is the infimum over $i$ of the topologies induced by the projections. Mathlib proves that this topology is generated by the boxes made of the $f$ with $f(i) \in U_i$ for all $i \in I$, for a finite set $I$ of indices and open sets $U_i \subseteq X_i$, which are exactly the finite intersections of the subbasic sets, the whole product being the box with $I$ empty; it also proves that these boxes form a basis, so that the open sets are exactly the unions of finite intersections of subbasic sets.
