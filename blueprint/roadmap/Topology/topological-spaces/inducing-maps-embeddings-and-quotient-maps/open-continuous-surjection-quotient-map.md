# A continuous surjective open map is a quotient map

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $p\colon X \to Y$ be a surjective continuous map that is an open map, so that $p$ sends open subsets of $X$ to open subsets of $Y$. Then $p$ is a quotient map: a subset $U \subseteq Y$ is open in $Y$ if and only if $p^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$.
