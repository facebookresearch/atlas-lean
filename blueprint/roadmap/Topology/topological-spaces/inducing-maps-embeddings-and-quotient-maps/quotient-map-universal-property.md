# A function on the target of a quotient map is continuous iff its composite with the quotient map is continuous

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $q\colon X \to Y$ be a quotient map: $q$ is surjective, and a subset $U \subseteq Y$ is open in $Y$ if and only if $q^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$. Let $Z$ be a topological space and let $g\colon Y \to Z$ be a function. Then $g$ is continuous if and only if $g \circ q\colon X \to Z$ is continuous.
