# Finite-dimensional subspaces are complete

Let $\mathbb K$ be a nontrivially normed field that is complete, for instance $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, and let $E$ be a
vector space over $\mathbb K$ with a Hausdorff uniform structure for which subtraction is uniformly continuous and scalar
multiplication is continuous, for instance a normed space over $\mathbb K$. Then every finite-dimensional subspace of $E$
is complete.
