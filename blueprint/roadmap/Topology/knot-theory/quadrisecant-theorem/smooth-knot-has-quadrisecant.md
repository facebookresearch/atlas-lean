# Quadrisecant theorem (Pannwitz–Kuperberg)

Every smooth knot $K$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ that is
not unknotted has a quadrisecant.

## Sources

- E. Pannwitz (1933); G. Kuperberg, *Quadrisecants of knots and links*, J. Knot Theory Ramif. 3 (1994). Knill, §161. Via leanprover/lean-eval@38361be, problem `smooth_knot_has_quadrisecant`.
