# Deletion face of a tuple

For a natural number $p$, a tuple $x = (x_1, \dots, x_{m+1})$ of elements of $\mathbb{Z}/p$ and a position $i \in \{1, \dots, m+1\}$, the deletion face $d_i^0 x$ is the $m$-tuple obtained by omitting the $i$-th entry:

$$d_i^0 x = (x_1, \dots, x_{i-1}, x_{i+1}, \dots, x_{m+1}).$$

It is the first term of each summand of the [rack boundary](rack-boundary.md).

## Sources

- M. Niebrzydowski and J. H. Przytycki, Homology of dihedral quandles, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 213 (2009), 742-755, arXiv:math/0611803, Definition 1.1(i).
