# Rack chain groups of a dihedral quandle

For a natural number $p$, the dihedral quandle $R_p$ is $\mathbb{Z}/p$ with the operation $a * b = 2b - a$. The rack chain group $C_m(R_p)$ is the free abelian group on the $m$-tuples $(x_1, \dots, x_m)$ of elements of $\mathbb{Z}/p$.

## Sources

- M. Niebrzydowski and J. H. Przytycki, Homology of dihedral quandles, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 213 (2009), 742-755, arXiv:math/0611803, Definition 1.1.
