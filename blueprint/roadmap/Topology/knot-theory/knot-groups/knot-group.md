# Knot group

A knot here is a smooth $2\pi$-periodic map $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^3$ with nowhere-vanishing derivative that is injective on $[0, 2\pi)$; its knot group is the fundamental group $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus K, x_0)$ of the complement of its image $K$, which is isomorphic to $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K)$, at any base point $x_0$.
