# Complement of a knot

A knot here is a smooth $2\pi$-periodic map $\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^3$ with nowhere-vanishing derivative that is injective on $[0, 2\pi)$. Its complement is $\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus K$, where $K = \gamma(\mathbb{R})$ is the image of $\gamma$, with the subspace topology of $\mathbb{R}^3$. Its fundamental group is the [knot group](knot-group.md).
