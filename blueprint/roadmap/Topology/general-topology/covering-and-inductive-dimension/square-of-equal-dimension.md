# Erdős Problem 909

The statement that for every integer $n \ge 2$ there is a separable metric space $S$ of dimension $n$ whose square $S \times S$ also has dimension $n$. Dimension means small inductive dimension (for separable metric spaces it agrees with the large inductive and the covering dimension).
