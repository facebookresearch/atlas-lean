# A Menger set of reals that is not Hurewicz

There is a set of reals $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ with the Menger property $U_{fin}(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O})$ but without the Hurewicz property $U_{fin}(\mathcal{O},\Gamma)$; that is, Tsaban's Milestone Problem 5.1 has a positive answer in ZFC. For $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, call a family of open subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ whose union contains $X$ a cover of $X$. Then $X$ has the Menger property $U_{fin}(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O})$ when for every sequence $\mathcal{U}_0, \mathcal{U}_1, \dots$ of covers of $X$ there are finite $\mathcal{F}_n \subseteq \mathcal{U}_n$ such that $\bigcup_n \bigcup \mathcal{F}_n \supseteq X$. The Hurewicz property asks moreover that every $x \in X$ lie in $\bigcup \mathcal{F}_n$ for all but finitely many $n$.

Chaber and Pol proved this on a dichotomic basis, and Tsaban and Zdomskyy constructed a concrete example.
