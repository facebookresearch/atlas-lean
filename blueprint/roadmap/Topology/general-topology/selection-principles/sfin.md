# Selection hypothesis S_fin

Let $T$ be a type and $\mathfrak{U}$, $\mathfrak{V}$ collections of families of subsets of $T$. The selection hypothesis $S_{fin}(\mathfrak{U}, \mathfrak{V})$ holds when for every sequence $\mathcal{U}_0, \mathcal{U}_1, \dots$ of members of $\mathfrak{U}$ there are finite $\mathcal{F}_n \subseteq \mathcal{U}_n$ such that $\bigcup_n \mathcal{F}_n \in \mathfrak{V}$.

## Sources
