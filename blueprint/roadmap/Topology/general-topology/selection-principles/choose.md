# The property (U V) of contained subcovers

Let $T$ be a type and $\mathfrak{U}$, $\mathfrak{V}$ collections of families of subsets of $T$. The property $(\mathfrak{U}\ \mathfrak{V})$ holds when every member of $\mathfrak{U}$ contains a member of $\mathfrak{V}$ as a subfamily.
