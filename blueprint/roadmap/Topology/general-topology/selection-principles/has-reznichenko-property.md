# Reznichenko property

A topological space $Y$ has the Reznichenko (weak Fréchet–Urysohn) property when for every $A \subseteq Y$ and every $y \in \overline{A} \setminus A$ there are pairwise disjoint finite sets $F_0, F_1, \dots \subseteq A$ such that every neighbourhood of $y$ meets $F_n$ for all but finitely many $n$. The family is indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, so finitely many of the $F_n$ may be empty.
