# Open ω-covers

Let $T$ be a topological space. An open cover of $T$ here is a countable family $\mathcal{U}$ of open subsets of $T$ with $\bigcup \mathcal{U} = T$ and $T \notin \mathcal{U}$ (the source's nontrivial covers). An $\omega$-cover is such a cover in which every finite subset of $T$ lies in some member; $\Omega$ denotes the collection of open $\omega$-covers of $T$.
