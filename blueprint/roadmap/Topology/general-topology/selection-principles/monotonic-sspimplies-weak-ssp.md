# Tsaban's Milestone Problem 15.4 (Scheepers)

The statement that for every set of reals $X$, if $C_p(X)$ has the monotonic sequence selection property then it has the weak sequence selection property. For a topological space $X$, $C_p(X)$ is the space of continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{R}$ with the topology of pointwise convergence (the subspace topology of the product $\mathbb{R}^X$), and $\mathbf{0}$ is the constant zero function. $C_p(X)$ has the monotonic sequence selection property when for every family $(f^n_k)_{n,k \in \mathbb{N}}$ in $C_p(X)$ such that each sequence $(f^n_k)_k$ converges to $\mathbf{0}$ and is non-increasing ($f^n_{k+1}(x) \le f^n_k(x)$ for all $k$ and $x$) there are indices $k_n$ with $f^n_{k_n} \to \mathbf{0}$. $C_p(X)$ has the weak sequence selection property when for every family $(f^n_k)_{n,k \in \mathbb{N}}$ in $C_p(X)$ such that each sequence $(f^n_k)_{k}$ converges to $\mathbf{0}$ there are indices $k_n$ with $\mathbf{0}$ in the closure of $\{f^n_{k_n} : n \in \mathbb{N}\}$.
