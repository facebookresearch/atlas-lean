# Tsaban's Milestone Problem 10.6

The statement that for every set of reals $X$, $X$ satisfies $S_{fin}(\Omega,\Omega^{gp})$ if and only if it satisfies $(\Omega\ \Omega^{gp})$. For a set of reals $X$ (a subset of $\mathbb{R}$ with the subspace topology), covers of $X$ are countable families of open subsets of $X$ with union $X$ not containing $X$; $\Omega$ is the collection of open $\omega$-covers of $X$ (every finite subset of $X$ lies in a member) and $\Omega^{gp}$ that of its $\Omega$-groupable members (unions of pairwise disjoint nonempty finite pieces $\mathcal{F}_n$ such that $\{\bigcup \mathcal{F}_n : n \in A\} \in \Omega$ for every infinite $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$). $S_{fin}(\Omega,\Omega^{gp})$ asks that every sequence of members of $\Omega$ admit finite subfamilies $\mathcal{F}'_n$ with $\bigcup_n \mathcal{F}'_n \in \Omega^{gp}$, and $(\Omega\ \Omega^{gp})$ that every member of $\Omega$ contain a member of $\Omega^{gp}$.
