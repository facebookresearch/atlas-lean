# Menger property of a set of reals

For $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, call a family of open subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ whose union contains $X$ a cover of $X$. Then $X$ has the Menger property $U_{fin}(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O})$ when for every sequence $\mathcal{U}_0, \mathcal{U}_1, \dots$ of covers of $X$ there are finite $\mathcal{F}_n \subseteq \mathcal{U}_n$ such that $\bigcup_n \bigcup \mathcal{F}_n \supseteq X$.
