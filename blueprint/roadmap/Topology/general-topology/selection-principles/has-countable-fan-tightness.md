# Countable fan tightness

A topological space $Y$ has countable fan tightness when for every $y \in Y$ and all sets $A_0, A_1, \dots \subseteq Y$ with $y \in \overline{A_n}$ for every $n$ there are finite $F_n \subseteq A_n$ with $y \in \overline{\bigcup_n F_n}$.
