# Tsaban's Milestone Problem 16.2 (Sakai)

The statement that $C_p(\mathbb{N}^\mathbb{N})$ has the Reznichenko property. The Baire space $\mathbb{N}^\mathbb{N}$ is the set of functions $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with the product of the discrete topologies. For a topological space $X$, $C_p(X)$ is the space of continuous functions $X \to \mathbb{R}$ with the topology of pointwise convergence (the subspace topology of the product $\mathbb{R}^X$), and $\mathbf{0}$ is the constant zero function. A topological space $Y$ has the Reznichenko (weak Fréchet–Urysohn) property when for every $A \subseteq Y$ and every $y \in \overline{A} \setminus A$ there are pairwise disjoint finite sets $F_0, F_1, \dots \subseteq A$ such that every neighbourhood of $y$ meets $F_n$ for all but finitely many $n$.
