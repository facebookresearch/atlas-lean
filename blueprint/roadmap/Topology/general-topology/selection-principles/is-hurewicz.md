# Hurewicz property of a set of reals

For $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, call a family of open subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ whose union contains $X$ a cover of $X$. Then $X$ has the Hurewicz property $U_{fin}(\mathcal{O},\Gamma)$ when for every sequence $\mathcal{U}_0, \mathcal{U}_1, \dots$ of covers of $X$ there are finite $\mathcal{F}_n \subseteq \mathcal{U}_n$ such that every $x \in X$ lies in $\bigcup \mathcal{F}_n$ for all but finitely many $n$.
