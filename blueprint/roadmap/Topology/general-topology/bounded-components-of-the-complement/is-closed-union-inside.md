# A closed set together with its inside is closed

Let $X$ be a locally connected topological space with a bornology, and let $K \subseteq X$ be closed. Write $\operatorname{ins} K$ for the inside of $K$, the set of points $x \notin K$ whose connected component in $X \setminus K$ is bounded. Then $K \cup \operatorname{ins} K$ is closed in $X$.
