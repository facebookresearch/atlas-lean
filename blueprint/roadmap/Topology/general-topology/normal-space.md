# Normal space: points are closed and disjoint closed sets have disjoint open neighbourhoods

A topological space $X$ is *normal*, or a $T_4$ space, if every one-point subset $\{x\}$ of $X$ is closed and any two disjoint closed subsets $A$ and $B$ of $X$ have disjoint open neighbourhoods: there are open sets $U \supseteq A$ and $V \supseteq B$ with $U \cap V = \varnothing$.
