# Krull dimension drops on proper closed subsets of irreducible sets

Let $X$ be a topological space, let $Z \subseteq X$ be irreducible, and let $Y \subseteq Z$ be a
closed subset of $X$ with $Y \ne Z$. Then $\dim Y + 1 \le \dim Z$, where $\dim$ is the
topological Krull dimension, the supremum of the lengths of chains of irreducible closed subsets,
with values in $\{-\infty\} \cup \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$ and $\dim \emptyset = -\infty$.
