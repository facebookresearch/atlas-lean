# Measurable maps into a restricted product

Let $\iota$ be a countable index set, and for each $i \in \iota$ let $R_i$ be a second-countable topological space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $A_i \subseteq R_i$ a measurable subset. Give the restricted product $\prod'_i (R_i, A_i)$ (families $(x_i)$ with $x_i \in A_i$ for all but finitely many $i$) its restricted-product topology and the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of that topology. Then a map $f$ from a measurable space $X$ to $\prod'_i (R_i, A_i)$ is measurable if and only if every coordinate map $x \mapsto f(x)_i$ is measurable.
