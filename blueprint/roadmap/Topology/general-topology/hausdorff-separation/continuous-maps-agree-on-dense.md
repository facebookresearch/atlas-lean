# Continuous maps into a Hausdorff space that agree on a dense set are equal

Let $X$ be a topological space, let $Y$ be a Hausdorff space, and let $f, g\colon X \to Y$ be continuous maps. Let $A \subseteq X$ be dense in $X$, meaning that the closure of $A$ is $X$. If $f(a) = g(a)$ for every $a \in A$, then $f(x) = g(x)$ for every $x \in X$.
