# In a Hausdorff space a compact set and a point outside it have disjoint open neighbourhoods

Let $X$ be a Hausdorff topological space, let $K \subseteq X$ be a compact subset and let $x \in X$ be a point with $x \notin K$. Then there are disjoint open subsets $U$ and $V$ of $X$ with $K \subseteq U$ and $x \in V$.

## Sources

- James R. Munkres, Topology, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2000.
