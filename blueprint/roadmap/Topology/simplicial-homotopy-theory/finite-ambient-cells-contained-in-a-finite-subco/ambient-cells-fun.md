# Ambient cell as a nondegenerate cell of its subcomplex

Let `A` be a subcomplex of `X`. The map `ambientCellsFun A : AmbientCells A → A.N` retains the
cell's dimension and representative simplex, now equipped with membership in `A`.
Nondegeneracy is reflected by the subcomplex inclusion, so this is a nondegenerate simplex of
`A`.
