# Ambient nondegenerate cells contained in a subcomplex

For `X : SSet.{u}` and `A : X.Subcomplex`, `AmbientCells A` is the subtype of ambient cells
`s∈X.N` with `s.subcomplex ≤ A`. Here `X.N` consists of nondegenerate simplices together with
their dimensions, and `s.subcomplex` is the subcomplex generated by the representative
simplex.
