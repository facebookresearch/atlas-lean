# Semisimplicially homotopic maps induce equal homology maps

Let f, g : X ⟶ Y be semisimplicial maps in a preadditive category C and let H be a
semisimplicial homotopy between them. For n : ℕ, assume that the alternating-face complexes of
X and Y each have homology in degree n. Then their induced maps H_n(map f) and H_n(map g),
from the homology of X to that of Y, are equal.
