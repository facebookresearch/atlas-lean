# Face injections and identities generate semisimplex morphisms

Let P be a property of morphisms in SemiSimplexCategory. Suppose P holds for every identity
and, for each f : x ⟶ y and i : Fin (y.len + 2), P f implies P (f ≫ δ i). Then P holds for
every order-preserving injection f : x ⟶ y in the semisimplex category.
