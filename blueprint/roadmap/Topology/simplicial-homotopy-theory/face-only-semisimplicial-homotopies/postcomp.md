# Postcomposition sends a semisimplicial homotopy to a homotopy

Let H be a semisimplicial homotopy from f to g, for f, g : X ⟶ Y in a category C, and let p :
Y ⟶ Y' be semisimplicial. The maps H.h i ≫ p_(n+1) define a homotopy from f ≫ p to g ≫ p.
Their endpoint and face equations follow by composing those of H with the components of p.
