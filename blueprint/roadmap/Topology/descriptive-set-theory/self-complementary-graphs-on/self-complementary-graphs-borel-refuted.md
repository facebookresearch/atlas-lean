# The self-complementary graphs on ω do not form a Borel set

The set of graphs on $\omega$ isomorphic to their complement is not a Borel subset of the Cantor space $2^{\omega\times\omega}$: by the theorem of Feng Li, Ruiwen Li and Ming Xiao, and of Riley Thornton, it is $\Sigma^1_1$-complete, by a continuous reduction from isomorphism of countable graphs.
