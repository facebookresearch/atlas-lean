# Graphs on ω isomorphic to their complement

View subsets of $\omega \times \omega$ as points of the Cantor space $2^{\omega\times\omega}$. A point $G$ is a graph on $\omega$ isomorphic to its complement when $G$ is symmetric and irreflexive and some bijection $f$ of $\omega$ satisfies $(m, n) \in G \iff (f(m), f(n)) \in G^c$ for all $m, n$, where $G^c = \{(m, n) : m \ne n,\ (m, n) \notin G\}$ is the complement graph.
