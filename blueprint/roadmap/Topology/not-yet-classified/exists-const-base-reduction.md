# SSet Square Transport

Coherent reduction of a boundary lifting square to constant base.

For `y` the last vertex of `b`, there is a new top `t₁` landing strictly over
`SSet.const y` whose lifting problem is equivalent to the original one. The
proof contracts the simplex onto its last vertex, lifts the induced
boundary-cylinder homotopy along `f`, and applies deformation invariance of
`HasLift`.
