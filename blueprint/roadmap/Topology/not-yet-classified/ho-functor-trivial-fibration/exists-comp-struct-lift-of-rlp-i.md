# Exists comp Struct lift of rlp I

`2`-simplices lift along a trivial Kan fibration with prescribed
boundary: given three edges of `X` whose images are the faces of a
`2`-simplex `σ` of `Y`, there is a `2`-simplex of `X` over `σ` with
those faces, i.e. a `CompStruct` over the one determined by `σ`. This is
lifting against `∂Δ[2] ⟶ Δ[2]`.
