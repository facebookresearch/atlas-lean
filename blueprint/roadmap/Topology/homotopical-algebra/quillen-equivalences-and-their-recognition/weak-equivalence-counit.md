# The counit of a Quillen equivalence at a fibrant object

Let $L \dashv R$ be a [Quillen equivalence](is-quillen-equivalence.md) between model categories $C$ and $D$, with $L : C \to D$ and counit $\varepsilon$. If $Y$ is a fibrant object of $D$ and $R(Y)$ is cofibrant in $C$, then $\varepsilon_Y : L(R(Y)) \to Y$ is a weak equivalence.
