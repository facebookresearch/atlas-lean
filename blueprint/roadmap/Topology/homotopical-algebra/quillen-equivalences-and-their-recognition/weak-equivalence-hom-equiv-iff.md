# Weak equivalences correspond under a Quillen equivalence

Let $L \dashv R$ be a [Quillen equivalence](is-quillen-equivalence.md) between model categories $C$ and $D$, with $L : C \to D$. For every cofibrant object $X$ of $C$, every fibrant object $Y$ of $D$ and every morphism $f : L(X) \to Y$, $f$ is a weak equivalence if and only if its adjunct $\tilde f : X \to R(Y)$ is a weak equivalence.
