# Left cancellation of Quillen equivalences

Let $C$, $D$ and $E$ be model categories, let $L : C \to D$ be left adjoint to $R : D \to C$ and $L' : D \to E$ left adjoint to $R' : E \to D$, and consider the composite adjunction $L' \circ L \dashv R \circ R'$. If $L \dashv R$ is a [Quillen equivalence](is-quillen-equivalence.md), $L' \dashv R'$ is a Quillen adjunction and the composite adjunction is a Quillen equivalence, then $L' \dashv R'$ is a Quillen equivalence.
