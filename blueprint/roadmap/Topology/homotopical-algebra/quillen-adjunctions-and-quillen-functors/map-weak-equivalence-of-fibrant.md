# Right Quillen functors preserve weak equivalences between fibrant objects

Let $L \dashv R$ be a [Quillen adjunction](is-quillen-adjunction.md) between model categories $C$ and $D$, with $R : D \to C$, and let $f : X \to Y$ be a weak equivalence of $D$ between fibrant objects $X$ and $Y$. Then $R(f)$ is a weak equivalence of $C$.
