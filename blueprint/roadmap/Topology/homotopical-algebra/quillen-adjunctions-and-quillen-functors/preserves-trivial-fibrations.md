# Functors preserving trivial fibrations

Let $C$ and $D$ be categories, each with a class of fibrations and a class of weak equivalences; a trivial fibration is a morphism that is both a fibration and a weak equivalence. A functor $F : C \to D$ preserves trivial fibrations when $F(f)$ is a trivial fibration of $D$ for every trivial fibration $f$ of $C$.
