# Left Quillen functors preserve weak equivalences between cofibrant objects

Let $L \dashv R$ be a [Quillen adjunction](is-quillen-adjunction.md) between model categories $C$ and $D$, with $L : C \to D$, and let $f : X \to Y$ be a weak equivalence of $C$ between cofibrant objects $X$ and $Y$. Then $L(f)$ is a weak equivalence of $D$.
