# Quillen adjunctions

Let $C$ and $D$ be model categories and let $L : C \to D$ be left adjoint to $R : D \to C$, with a given adjunction $L \dashv R$. The adjunction is a Quillen adjunction when $L$ [preserves cofibrations](preserves-cofibrations.md) and [preserves trivial cofibrations](preserves-trivial-cofibrations.md). Equivalently, $R$ preserves fibrations and trivial fibrations: see [Quillen adjunctions through the right adjoint](is-quillen-adjunction-iff.md).
