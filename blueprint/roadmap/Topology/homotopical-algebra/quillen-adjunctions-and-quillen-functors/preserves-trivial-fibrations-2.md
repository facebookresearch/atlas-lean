# The right adjoint of a Quillen adjunction preserves trivial fibrations

Let $L \dashv R$ be a [Quillen adjunction](is-quillen-adjunction.md) between model categories $C$ and $D$, with $L : C \to D$. Then $R : D \to C$ sends every trivial fibration of $D$, a fibration that is a weak equivalence, to a trivial fibration of $C$.
