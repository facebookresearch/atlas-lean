# Functors preserving trivial cofibrations

Let $C$ and $D$ be categories, each with a class of cofibrations and a class of weak equivalences; a trivial cofibration is a morphism that is both a cofibration and a weak equivalence. A functor $F : C \to D$ preserves trivial cofibrations when $F(f)$ is a trivial cofibration of $D$ for every trivial cofibration $f$ of $C$.
