# From the diagonal at a point to an interval starting there

For a natural number $n$ and $j \le k$ in $[n] = \{0, \dots, n\}$, this is the morphism $(j, j) \to (j, k)$ of the [category of intervals](../../not-yet-classified/stable-infinity-sconstruction/interval-category.md) $\mathrm{Ar}[n]$ that moves the right endpoint from $j$ to $k$, starting at a [diagonal interval](diag.md).
