# From an interval to the diagonal at its right endpoint

For a natural number $n$ and $i \le j$ in $[n] = \{0, \dots, n\}$, this is the morphism $(i, j) \to (j, j)$ of the [category of intervals](../../not-yet-classified/stable-infinity-sconstruction/interval-category.md) $\mathrm{Ar}[n]$ that moves the left endpoint from $i$ to $j$, ending at a [diagonal interval](diag.md).
