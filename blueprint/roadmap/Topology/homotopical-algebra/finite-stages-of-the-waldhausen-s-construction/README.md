# Finite stages of the Waldhausen S-construction

The diagrams indexed by intervals $(i, j)$, $0 \le i \le j \le n$, that form the degree-$n$ part of Waldhausen's S-construction on a category with cofibrations and weak equivalences.

The finite stages are diagrams on the [interval category](../../not-yet-classified/stable-infinity-sconstruction/interval-category.md) $\mathrm{Ar}[n]$, the arrow category of $[n] = \{0 < 1 < \dots < n\}$.
