# Moving the right endpoint of an interval

For a natural number $n$ and $i \le j \le k$ in $[n] = \{0, \dots, n\}$, this is the morphism $(i, j) \to (i, k)$ of the [category of intervals](../../not-yet-classified/stable-infinity-sconstruction/interval-category.md) $\mathrm{Ar}[n]$ that keeps the left endpoint $i$ and moves the right endpoint from $j$ to $k$.
