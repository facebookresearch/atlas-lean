# The composite of a cofibration sequence is zero

Let $C$ be a category with a zero object, zero morphisms and a [Waldhausen structure without gluing](waldhausen-category-without-gluing.md). If $f : X \to Y$ and $g : Y \to Z$ form a [cofibration sequence](is-cofibration-sequence.md), then $g \circ f = 0$. With a zero object, the zero morphisms are exactly the morphisms that factor through it.
