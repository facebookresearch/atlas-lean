# Cofibration sequences

Let $C$ be a category with a zero object $0$ and a [Waldhausen structure without gluing](waldhausen-category-without-gluing.md). Morphisms $f : X \to Y$ and $g : Y \to Z$ form a cofibration sequence, or cofiber sequence, when $f$ is a cofibration and, for some morphisms $X \to 0$ and $0 \to Z$, the object $Z$ with the morphisms $g$ and $0 \to Z$ is a pushout of the span $0 \leftarrow X \xrightarrow{f} Y$. So $Z$ is the cofiber $Y / X$ of $f$ and $g$ is the quotient map. The morphisms through $0$ are the unique ones, since $0$ is both initial and terminal.
