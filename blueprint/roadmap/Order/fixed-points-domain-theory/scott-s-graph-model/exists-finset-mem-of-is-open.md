# Scott-open sets of `P(ω)` have finite character

If $V$ is a Scott-open set of subsets of $\mathbb{N}$ and $y \in V$, then some finite subset of $y$ lies in $V$. Since Scott-open sets are upper sets, $V$ is therefore the union of the basic open sets $\{z \mid e \subseteq z\}$ over the finite $e \in V$: the open sets are the sets "of finite character" (D. Scott, *Data types as lattices*, SIAM J. Comput. 5(3) (1976), 522–587, §1).
