# Continuity into `P(ω)` is tested on the subbasic open sets

A map $g : Z \to P(\omega)$ from a topological space to $P(\omega)$ with its Scott topology is continuous as soon as $\{z \mid m \in g(z)\}$ is open for every $m \in \mathbb{N}$. The reason is that the Scott-open sets of $P(\omega)$ are unions of finite intersections of the sets $\{y \mid m \in y\}$ (D. Scott, *Data types as lattices*, SIAM J. Comput. 5(3) (1976), 522–587, Appendix A, proof of Theorem 1.5).
