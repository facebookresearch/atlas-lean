# The graph of a pointwise union

For every family $F$ of functions $P(\omega) \to P(\omega)$, the graph of the pointwise union is the union of the graphs:

$$\operatorname{graph}\Big(\bigsqcup F\Big) = \bigcup_{f \in F} \operatorname{graph}(f).$$

So $\operatorname{graph}$ is Scott-continuous, indeed completely join-preserving, for the pointwise order on functions.
