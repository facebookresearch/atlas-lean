# The sets of naturals containing `n` are Scott-open

For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the set $\{y \subseteq \mathbb{N} \mid n \in y\}$ is open in the Scott topology of $P(\omega)$. Finite intersections of these sets, $\{y \mid e \subseteq y\}$ for finite $e$, form a basis of the Scott topology (D. Scott, *Data types as lattices*, SIAM J. Comput. 5(3) (1976), 522–587, §1 and Appendix A, proof of Theorem 1.5); $P(\omega)$ is the countable power of the Sierpiński space.
