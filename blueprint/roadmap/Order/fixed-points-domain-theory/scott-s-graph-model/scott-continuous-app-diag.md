# Self-application is Scott-continuous

For every $u \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, the map $x \mapsto \operatorname{app}(u, \operatorname{app}(x, x))$ is Scott-continuous. This is the function denoted by the term $\lambda x.\, u(x\,x)$, the half of the paradoxical combinator that is applied to itself.
