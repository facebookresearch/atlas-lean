# Embedding–projection pairs

For pointed $\omega$-complete partial orders $D$ and $E$, an *embedding–projection pair* is a pair of continuous maps $e : D \to E$ and $p : E \to D$ with $p \circ e = \mathrm{id}_D$ and $e \circ p \sqsubseteq \mathrm{id}_E$. Embedding–projection pairs compose, and an embedding–projection pair $(e, p)$ from $D$ to $E$ lifts to one from $[D \to D]$ to $[E \to E]$, given by $f \mapsto e \circ f \circ p$ and $g \mapsto p \circ g \circ e$.
