# Fixed points and domain theory

Least and greatest fixed points of monotone and Scott-continuous maps, Kleene iteration, and the complete partial orders that underlie denotational semantics. Scott's domain theory adds function spaces of continuous maps and concrete domains in which self-application makes sense: the [graph model](scott-s-graph-model/README.md) $P(\omega)$, of which the continuous self-maps form a retract, and the inverse limit $D_\infty \cong [D_\infty \to D_\infty]$ ([Scott's inverse-limit theorem](scott-s-inverse-limit-theorem.md)). Both are models of the untyped λ-calculus.
