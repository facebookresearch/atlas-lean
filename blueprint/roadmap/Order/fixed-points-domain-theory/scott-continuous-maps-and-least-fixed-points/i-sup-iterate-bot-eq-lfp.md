# Kleene's supremum for dcpos is the least fixed point of the ω-chain construction

For a Scott-continuous self-map $f$ of a pointed dcpo $D$, the supremum $\bigsqcup_n f^n(\bot)$ is, by
definition, the least fixed point that Kleene's construction gives for $f$ viewed as a map preserving
suprema of ω-chains. This ties the directed-set version of Kleene's theorem to the least-fixed-point
operator of the ω-chain setting used for the semantics of PCF; the two Kleene theorems for dcpos
follow from it.
