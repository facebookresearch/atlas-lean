# Directed suprema of Scott-continuous maps are computed pointwise

Let $\beta$ be a pointed dcpo and $F$ a directed set of maps $\alpha \to \beta$. The pointwise supremum $x \mapsto \bigsqcup_{f \in F} f(x)$ is the least upper bound of $F$ for the pointwise order. When the members of $F$ are Scott-continuous, so is this supremum. So the Scott-continuous maps $[\alpha \to \beta]$ form a dcpo whose directed suprema are computed pointwise (S. Abramsky and A. Jung, *Domain Theory*, Handbook of Logic in Computer Science vol. 3 (1994), Proposition 2.1.18).
