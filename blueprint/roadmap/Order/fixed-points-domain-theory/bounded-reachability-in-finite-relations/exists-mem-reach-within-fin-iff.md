# Exists mem reach Within Fin iff

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $r$ a decidable binary relation on $\alpha$, $a \in \alpha$, and let $N_k(a)$ be the set of elements reachable from $a$ in at most $k$ steps of $r$. For every $t \in \alpha$, there is $k \in \mathbb{N}$ with $t \in N_k(a)$ if and only if $t$ is reachable from $a$ by zero or more $r$-steps, that is, $(a, t)$ lies in the reflexive transitive closure of $r$.
