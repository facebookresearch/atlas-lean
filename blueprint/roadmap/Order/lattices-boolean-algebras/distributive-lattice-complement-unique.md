# Complements in a bounded distributive lattice are unique

Let $L$ be a bounded distributive lattice with least element $0$ and greatest element $1$, and let $a \in L$. Suppose that $b, c \in L$ are both complements of $a$: $a \wedge b = 0$, $a \vee b = 1$, $a \wedge c = 0$ and $a \vee c = 1$. Then $b = c$.

## Sources

- B. A. Davey and H. A. Priestley, Introduction to Lattices and Order, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2002.
