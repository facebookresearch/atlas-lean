# Equivalence relation

Let $A$ be a set. An *equivalence relation* on $A$ is a binary relation $R$ on $A$ that is reflexive, symmetric and transitive: $x \mathrel{R} x$ for every $x \in A$; $x \mathrel{R} y$ implies $y \mathrel{R} x$; and $x \mathrel{R} y$ and $y \mathrel{R} z$ together imply $x \mathrel{R} z$, for all $x, y, z \in A$.
