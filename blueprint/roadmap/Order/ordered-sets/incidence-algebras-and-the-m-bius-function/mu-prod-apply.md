# Product theorem for the Möbius function

For locally finite posets $P$ and $Q$, the Möbius function of the direct product $P \times Q$ is
the product of the Möbius functions:

$$\mu_{P \times Q}\big((s, t), (s', t')\big) = \mu_P(s, s')\, \mu_Q(t, t').$$

Stanley states it for $(s, t) \le (s', t')$; both sides vanish otherwise, so it holds for all
pairs. This is the pointwise form of the product theorem in incidence algebras, which says that
$\mu_{P \times Q}$ is the tensor product $\mu_P \otimes \mu_Q$ in the incidence algebra.

## Sources

- R. P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Proposition 3.8.2.
