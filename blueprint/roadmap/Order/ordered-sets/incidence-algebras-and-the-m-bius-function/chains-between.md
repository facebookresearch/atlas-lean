# Chains of length $i$ between two elements

For elements $a, b$ of a locally finite poset and $i \in \mathbb{N}$, a chain of length $i$ from $a$
to $b$ is a sequence $a = t_0 < t_1 < \cdots < t_i = b$. Equivalently, it is a strictly increasing map
$\{0, \dots, i\} \to P$ with value $a$ at $0$ and $b$ at $i$. All such chains lie in the finite
interval $[a, b]$, so they form a finite set.

## Sources

- R. P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., author's draft of 15 July 2011, https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf, Section 3.8, before Proposition 3.8.5.
