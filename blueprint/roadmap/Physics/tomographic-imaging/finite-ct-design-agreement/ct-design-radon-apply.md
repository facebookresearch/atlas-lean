# Finite design application law

Let `s` be a finite set of indices, `phi : ι → ℝ² → ℝ` basis functions on
the Euclidean plane, `x : ι → ℝ` coefficients, `xi ∈ ℝ²` and `p : ℝ`. If
each `phi i` with `i ∈ s` is integrable on the line `{y | ⟪y, xi⟫ = p}` with
respect to one-dimensional Hausdorff measure, then the Euclidean Radon
transform of `∑_{i ∈ s} x i * phi i` at `(xi, p)` equals `∑_{i ∈ s} x i`
times the Radon transform of `phi i` at `(xi, p)`.
