# Photon law as a product of Poisson laws

For finite index types `ι` (rays) and `κ` (cells), source intensities `I0 :
ι → ℝ≥0`, a real design matrix `A : Matrix ι κ ℝ` and attenuation
coefficients `x : κ → ℝ`, the photon law equals the product measure over `j
: ι` of the Poisson distributions with rates given by the transmitted rates
`I0 j * exp(-(A *ᵥ x) j)` (as nonnegative reals).
