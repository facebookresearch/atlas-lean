# Equal measurements give equal transmitted rates

For a finite cell index type `κ`, source intensities `I0 : ι → ℝ≥0`, a real
matrix `A : Matrix ι κ ℝ` and coefficient vectors `x, y : κ → ℝ` with `A *ᵥ
x = A *ᵥ y`, the transmitted rates `I0 j * exp(-(A *ᵥ x) j)` and `I0 j *
exp(-(A *ᵥ y) j)` agree for every ray `j`.
