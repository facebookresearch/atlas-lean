# Monochromatic attenuation inversion

In the monochromatic model, take a positive incident intensity $I_0 > 0$ and a real line integral $L$ of the attenuation coefficient, and let the transmitted intensity be $I = I_0 e^{-L}$. Then

$$-\log(I / I_0) = L.$$

This is the same inversion as [attenuation as a log ratio](attenuation-log-ratio.md), written with the ratio $I / I_0$ instead of its reciprocal: since $\log(I_0 / I) = -\log(I / I_0)$, the two statements are equivalent forms of each other under the same hypotheses.
