# Born probability

For a vector $\psi$ in $\mathbb{C}^n$ and an orthonormal basis $b$, the Born probability of outcome $i$ is the squared modulus $\lvert\langle b_i, \psi\rangle\rvert^2$ of the amplitude. It is defined for every $\psi$; when $\psi$ is normalized, $\lVert\psi\rVert = 1$, these numbers form a probability distribution on the outcomes. This is the Born rule, a physical postulate taken as a definition.
