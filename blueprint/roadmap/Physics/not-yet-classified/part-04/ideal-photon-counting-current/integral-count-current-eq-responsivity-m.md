# Mean current of an ideal photon-counting detector

Let $0 \le \eta \le 1$ be the quantum efficiency, $P \ge 0$ the optical power, $h > 0$, $\nu > 0$ (photon energy $h\nu$), $q \ne 0$ the electron charge and $T > 0$ the window length. Let $\lambda = \eta P/(h\nu)$ be the photon arrival rate and $\mathcal{R} = \eta |q|/(h\nu)$ the responsivity. If $N$ is a random count on a measure space $(\Omega, \mu)$ whose law is Poisson with mean $\lambda T$, then the window-averaged current $|q| N / T$ has mean

$$\int_\Omega \frac{|q|\, N(\omega)}{T} \, d\mu(\omega) = \mathcal{R}\, P.$$
