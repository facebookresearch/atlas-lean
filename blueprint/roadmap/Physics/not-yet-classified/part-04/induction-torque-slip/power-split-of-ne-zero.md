# Air-gap power split at nonzero slip

For any rotor current $I_2 \in \mathbb{C}$, rotor resistance $R_2$, slip $s$ and synchronous speed $\omega_s$, let $P_{ag} = 3\|I_2\|^2 R_2/s$ be the air-gap power, $\tau = P_{ag}/\omega_s$ the developed torque, $P_{cu} = 3\|I_2\|^2 R_2$ the rotor copper loss and $P_{conv} = P_{ag} - P_{cu}$ the converted power. If $s \neq 0$ and $\omega_s \neq 0$, then

$$P_{conv} = \tau \cdot (1 - s)\,\omega_s, \qquad P_{conv} = (1 - s)\, P_{ag}, \qquad P_{cu} = s\, P_{ag}.$$
