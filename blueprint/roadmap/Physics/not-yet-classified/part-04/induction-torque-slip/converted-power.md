# Converted mechanical power

For a rotor current $I_2 \in \mathbb{C}$, rotor resistance $R_2$ and slip $s$, the converted mechanical power is the air-gap power minus the rotor copper loss:

$$P_{conv} = 3\|I_2\|^2\,\frac{R_2}{s} - 3\|I_2\|^2 R_2.$$

For $s = 0$ the first term takes the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $P_{conv} = -3\|I_2\|^2 R_2$.
