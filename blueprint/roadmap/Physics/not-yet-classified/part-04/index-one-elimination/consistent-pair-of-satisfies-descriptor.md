# Descriptor trajectories pass through consistent pairs

Let $n$ and $m$ be finite index sets, and let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times m}$, $C \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$. Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^n$, $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^m$, $x : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^n$ and $y : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^m$. Suppose $x$ is differentiable and, for every $t$,

$$x'(t) = A x(t) + B y(t) + f(t), \qquad C x(t) + D y(t) + g(t) = 0.$$

Then for every $t_0 \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$C x(t_0) + D y(t_0) + g(t_0) = 0.$$

No invertibility of $D$ is assumed.
