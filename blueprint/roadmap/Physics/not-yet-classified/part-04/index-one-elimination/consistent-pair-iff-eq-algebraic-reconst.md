# Consistent initial pairs of an index-one descriptor system

Let $n$ and $m$ be finite index sets, and let $C \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$. Assume $D$ is invertible. For $g_0 \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $y_0 \in \mathbb{R}^m$,

$$C x_0 + D y_0 + g_0 = 0 \iff y_0 = -D^{-1}(C x_0 + g_0).$$
