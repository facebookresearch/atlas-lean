# Algebraic values off the reconstruction are inconsistent

Let $n$ and $m$ be finite index sets, and let $C \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$. Assume $D$ is invertible. If $g_0 \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $y_0 \in \mathbb{R}^m$ satisfy $y_0 \ne -D^{-1}(C x_0 + g_0)$, then

$$C x_0 + D y_0 + g_0 \ne 0.$$
