# Squared speed on the cylinder surface

Let $a > 0$ and $W(\zeta) = U\left(1 - a^2/\zeta^2\right) + \Gamma/(2\pi i\zeta)$ be the complex velocity of flow past the circle at zero angle of attack. On the cylinder $\zeta = a e^{i\theta}$, $|W|^2 = \left(2U\sin\theta - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi a}\right)^2$.
