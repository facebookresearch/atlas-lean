# Kutta-Joukowski lift and thin-airfoil lift coefficient

Consider the flat-plate airfoil obtained from a circle of radius $a > 0$ by the Joukowski map, with chord $c = 4a$, in a stream of density $\rho > 0$ and speed $U > 0$ at angle of attack $\alpha$, with the Kutta circulation $\Gamma = -4 \pi a U \sin\alpha$. For every contour radius $R > 2a$, the Blasius force on the contour of radius $R$ gives signed lift $-\rho U \Gamma$, the lift coefficient $L / (\rho U^2 c / 2)$ equals $2 \pi \sin\alpha$, and

$$\left| C_L - 2 \pi \alpha \right| \le \frac{\pi |\alpha|^3}{3}.$$
