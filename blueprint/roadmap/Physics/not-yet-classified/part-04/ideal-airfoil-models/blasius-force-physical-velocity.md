# Blasius force of the Joukowski airfoil flow

Let $a > 0$, $\rho > 0$, $U > 0$, $\Gamma$ and $\alpha$ real, and $R > 2a$. Let $\zeta(z) = \frac{z}{2}\big(1 + \sqrt{1 - 4a^2/z^2}\big)$ (principal root), $w(\zeta) = U\big(e^{-i\alpha} - a^2 e^{i\alpha}/\zeta^2\big) + \Gamma/(2\pi i \zeta)$, and $W(z) = w(\zeta(z)) / (1 - a^2/\zeta(z)^2)$. Then the Blasius force

$$\frac{i\rho}{2} \oint_{|z| = R} W(z)^2 \, dz = i \rho U \Gamma e^{-i\alpha}.$$
