# Physical complex velocity on the far-field Joukowski branch

For real $a, U, \Gamma, \alpha$, let $\zeta(z) = \tfrac{z}{2}\big(1 + \sqrt{1 - 4a^2/z^2}\big)$ with the principal square root, and let the circle-plane velocity be $w(\zeta) = U\big(e^{-i\alpha} - a^2 e^{i\alpha}/\zeta^2\big) + \Gamma/(2\pi i\zeta)$. The physical complex velocity is defined as
$$W(z) = \frac{w(\zeta(z))}{1 - a^2/\zeta(z)^2},$$
with division by zero read as zero; the denominator vanishes only at $\zeta = \pm a$.
