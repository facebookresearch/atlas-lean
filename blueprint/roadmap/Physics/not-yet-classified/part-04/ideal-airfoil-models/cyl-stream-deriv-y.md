# Stream function gives the horizontal velocity

For $a > 0$ and $(x, y) \ne (0, 0)$, the stream function $\psi(x, y) = U\left(y - \frac{a^2 y}{x^2 + y^2}\right) - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\log\frac{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}{a}$ of flow past a circular cylinder satisfies

$$\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial y}(x, y) = U\left(1 - \frac{a^2 (x^2 - y^2)}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}\right) - \frac{\Gamma y}{2\pi (x^2 + y^2)}.$$
