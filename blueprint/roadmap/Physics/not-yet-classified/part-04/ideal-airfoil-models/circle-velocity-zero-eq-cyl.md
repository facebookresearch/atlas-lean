# Circle-plane velocity at zero incidence is the cylinder field

For $z \ne 0$ written $z = x + iy$, the circle-plane complex velocity at zero angle of attack,

$$U\Big(1 - \frac{a^2}{z^2}\Big) + \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi i z},$$

equals $u - i v$, where

$$u = U\Big(1 - \frac{a^2(x^2 - y^2)}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}\Big) - \frac{\Gamma y}{2\pi(x^2 + y^2)}, \qquad v = -\frac{2Ua^2xy}{(x^2 + y^2)^2} + \frac{\Gamma x}{2\pi(x^2 + y^2)}$$

are the velocity components of flow past a circular cylinder of radius $a$ with circulation $\Gamma$.
