# Cylinder flow is curl-free

For potential flow past a circular cylinder of radius $a$ in a uniform stream $U$ with circulation $\Gamma$, with velocity $u = U\big(1 - a^2 (x^2 - y^2)/r^4\big) - \Gamma y / (2\pi r^2)$ and $v = -2 U a^2 x y / r^4 + \Gamma x / (2 \pi r^2)$ where $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$, the vorticity vanishes away from the origin: $\partial_x v - \partial_y u = 0$ whenever $x^2 + y^2 \ne 0$.
