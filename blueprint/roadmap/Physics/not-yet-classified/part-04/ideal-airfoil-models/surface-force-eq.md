# Surface pressure force on the cylinder

Let $a > 0$ and let the pressure satisfy Bernoulli's law $p(z) = p_\infty + \rho\,(U^2 - |W(z)|^2)/2$ for all $|z| \ge a$, where $W(z) = U(1 - a^2/z^2) + \Gamma/(2\pi i z)$. Then the pressure force on the circle $|z| = a$,

$$F_x = -a\int_0^{2\pi} p(a e^{i\theta})\cos\theta\,d\theta, \qquad F_y = -a\int_0^{2\pi} p(a e^{i\theta})\sin\theta\,d\theta,$$

is $F_x = 0$ and $F_y = -\rho U \Gamma$.
