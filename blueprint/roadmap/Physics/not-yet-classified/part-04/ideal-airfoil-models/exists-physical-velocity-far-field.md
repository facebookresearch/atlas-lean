# Far-field expansion of the Joukowski airfoil velocity

Let $a > 0$ and $U, \Gamma, \alpha$ real. Let $\zeta(z) = \tfrac{z}{2}\big(1 + \sqrt{1 - 4a^2/z^2}\big)$ (principal root) and

$$W(z) = \frac{U\big(e^{-i\alpha} - a^2 e^{i\alpha}/\zeta^2\big) + \Gamma/(2\pi i \zeta)}{1 - a^2/\zeta^2}, \qquad \zeta = \zeta(z).$$

Then $W$ is holomorphic on $\{|z| > 2a\}$, and there are $R_1 > 2a$ and $K \ge 0$ such that for all $|z| \ge R_1$,

$$\Big|W(z) - U e^{-i\alpha} - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi i z}\Big| \le \frac{K}{|z|^2}.$$
