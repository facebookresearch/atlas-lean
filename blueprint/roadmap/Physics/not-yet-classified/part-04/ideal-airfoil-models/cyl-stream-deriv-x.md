# Stream function of the lifting cylinder gives the vertical velocity

Let $a > 0$ and $x^2 + y^2 \ne 0$, and let $\psi(x,y) = U\left(y - \frac{a^2 y}{x^2+y^2}\right) - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\log\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}{a}$ and $v(x,y) = -\frac{2Ua^2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2} + \frac{\Gamma x}{2\pi(x^2+y^2)}$. Then $\partial_x \psi(x,y) = -v(x,y)$.
