# Blasius force for a far-field expansion

Let $\rho > 0$, $U > 0$ and $0 < R_0 < R_1$, and let $W$ be holomorphic on $\{R_0 < \lvert z\rvert\}$ with $W(z) = Ue^{-i\alpha} + \dfrac{\Gamma}{2\pi i z} + e(z)$ for $\lvert z\rvert \ge R_1$, where $e$ is differentiable there and $\lvert e(z)\rvert \le K/\lvert z\rvert^2$. Then for every radius $R > R_0$, the Blasius force of $W$ on the circle of radius $R$ is $i\rho U\Gamma e^{-i\alpha}$ (the Kutta–Joukowski force).
