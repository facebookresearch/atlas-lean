# Flow past a cylinder is divergence-free

For the potential flow past a circular cylinder of radius $a$ with free-stream speed $U$ and circulation $\Gamma$, with velocity $u = U\big(1 - a^2 (x^2 - y^2)/r^4\big) - \Gamma y/(2\pi r^2)$ and $v = -2 U a^2 x y / r^4 + \Gamma x/(2\pi r^2)$, where $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$, one has $u_x + v_y = 0$ at every point with $x^2 + y^2 \neq 0$.
