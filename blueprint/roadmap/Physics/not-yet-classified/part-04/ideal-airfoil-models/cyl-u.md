# Horizontal velocity of cylinder flow with circulation

For freestream speed $U$, radius $a$ and circulation $\Gamma$, the horizontal velocity at $(x, y)$ with $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$ is

$$u = U\left(1 - \frac{a^2(x^2 - y^2)}{r^4}\right) - \frac{\Gamma y}{2\pi r^2},$$

the real part of the complex velocity $W(z) = U(1 - a^2/z^2) + \Gamma/(2\pi i z)$ written as $u - iv$. At the origin, where $r = 0$, both quotients are $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $u = U$ there.
