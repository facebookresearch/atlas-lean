# Far-field bound for the physical velocity

For $a > 0$ and real $U$, $\Gamma$, $\alpha$, let $w(\zeta) = U\left(e^{-i\alpha} - a^2 e^{i\alpha}/\zeta^2\right) + \Gamma/(2\pi i \zeta)$, let $\zeta(z) = \tfrac{z}{2}\left(1 + \sqrt{1 - 4a^2/z^2}\right)$ (principal square root), and let $W(z) = w(\zeta(z))/(1 - a^2/\zeta(z)^2)$. Every $z$ with $|z| \ge 4a$ satisfies

$$\left|W(z) - U e^{-i\alpha} - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi i z}\right| \le \frac{12 |U| a^2 + a|\Gamma|}{|z|^2}.$$
