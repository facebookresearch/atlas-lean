# Integrability of the cylinder surface pressure

Let $a > 0$ and $W(\zeta) = U(1 - a^2/\zeta^2) + \Gamma/(2\pi i\zeta)$. If the pressure satisfies Bernoulli's law $p(z) = p_\infty + \rho\left(U^2 - |W(z)|^2\right)/2$ for all $|z| \ge a$, then $\theta \mapsto p(a e^{i\theta})\cos\theta$ and $\theta \mapsto p(a e^{i\theta})\sin\theta$ are integrable on $[0, 2\pi]$.
