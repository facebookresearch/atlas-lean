# Blasius force on a circle at zero incidence

Let $\rho > 0$, $a > 0$, $U > 0$ and $R \ge a$. For the circle-plane complex velocity at zero incidence, $W(\zeta) = U(1 - a^2/\zeta^2) + \Gamma/(2\pi i \zeta)$, the Blasius force $F_x - i F_y = \frac{i\rho}{2}\oint_{|z| = R} W(z)^2\,dz$ equals $i \rho U \Gamma$, that is $F_x = 0$ and $F_y = -\rho U \Gamma$.
