# Pressure on the surface of a cylinder with circulation

Let $a > 0$, and suppose that for all $|z| \ge a$ the pressure satisfies $p(z) = p_\infty + \rho\big(U^2 - |w(z)|^2\big)/2$, where $w(z) = U(1 - a^2/z^2) + \Gamma/(2\pi i z)$. Then for every $\theta$,

$$p(a e^{i\theta}) = p_\infty + \frac{\rho}{2}\Big(U^2 - \big(2U \sin\theta - \tfrac{\Gamma}{2\pi a}\big)^2\Big).$$
