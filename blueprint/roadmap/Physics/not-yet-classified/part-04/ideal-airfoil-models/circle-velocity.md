# Circle-plane complex velocity

For circle radius $a$, freestream speed $U$, circulation $\Gamma$, angle of attack $\alpha$ and a point $\zeta$ of the circle plane, the circle-plane complex velocity (the Kutta numerator) is

$$U\left(e^{-i\alpha} - \frac{a^2 e^{i\alpha}}{\zeta^2}\right) + \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi i \zeta},$$

with the value $U e^{-i\alpha}$ at $\zeta = 0$, where both quotients are $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
