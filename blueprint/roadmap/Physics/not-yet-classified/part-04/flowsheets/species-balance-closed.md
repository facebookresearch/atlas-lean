# Closed species balance in a flowsheet

Consider a flowsheet with finitely many units in which each unit's holdup of each species has derivative equal to net internal transfer plus external inflow minus external outflow plus generation. If, for a species $s$ at time $t$, external inflow minus external outflow plus generation of $s$ is zero in every unit, then the total holdup of $s$ over all units has derivative $0$ at $t$.
