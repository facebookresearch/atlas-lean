# Settling of a first-order output on a holding interval

Let $\tau > 0$ and $t_k < t_{k+1}$, and let $y$ be continuous on $[t_k, t_{k+1}]$, differentiable on $(t_k, t_{k+1})$, with $\tau y'(s) = u - y(s)$ there. Then for every $t \in [t_k, t_{k+1}]$,

$$y(t) = u + (y(t_k) - u)\,e^{-(t - t_k)/\tau}, \qquad |y(t) - u| = |y(t_k) - u|\,e^{-(t - t_k)/\tau},$$

and in particular $y(t_{k+1}) = u + (y(t_k) - u)\,e^{-(t_{k+1} - t_k)/\tau}$.
