# Link exit time

Let $N_0 \ge 0$ be the number of vehicles on the link at time $0$, and $A, D : [0,\infty) \to [0,\infty)$ the cumulative arrival and departure counts from time $0$. Under first-in-first-out order, the exit time of the vehicle entering at time $t$ is the infimum of the times $s \ge t$ with $D(s) \ge N_0 + A(t)$, and $+\infty$ if there is no such time.
