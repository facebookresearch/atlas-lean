# Counterflow effectiveness lies in [0, 1]

For $\mathrm{NTU} \ge 0$ and $C_r \le 1$, the counterflow effectiveness $\varepsilon = \dfrac{1 - e^{-\mathrm{NTU}(1 - C_r)}}{1 - C_r e^{-\mathrm{NTU}(1 - C_r)}}$ for $C_r \ne 1$, and $\varepsilon = \mathrm{NTU}/(1 + \mathrm{NTU})$ for $C_r = 1$, satisfies $0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$.
