# Equal and opposite heat

Let $C_h, C_c > 0$ and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the parallel-flow exchanger equations $C_h T_h' = -K (T_h - T_c)$, $C_c T_c' = K (T_h - T_c)$ on $[0, L]$ with inlets $T_h(0) = T_{hi}$ and $T_c(0) = T_{ci}$. Then for every $x \in [0, L]$, $C_h (T_{hi} - T_h(x)) = C_c (T_c(x) - T_{ci})$.
