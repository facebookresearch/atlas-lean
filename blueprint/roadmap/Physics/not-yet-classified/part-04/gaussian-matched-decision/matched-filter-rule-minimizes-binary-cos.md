# Gaussian matched-filter test is Bayes optimal

Let the observation in $\mathbb{R}^n$ ($n$ finite) have law $N(s_0, R)$ under hypothesis $0$ and $N(s_1, R)$ under hypothesis $1$, with a common positive definite covariance $R$. Let the priors satisfy $\pi_0, \pi_1 > 0$ and $\pi_0 + \pi_1 = 1$, and let the costs satisfy $C_{00} < C_{10}$ and $C_{11} < C_{01}$. Then the matched-filter threshold rule (with either tie-breaking choice) is measurable, and its Bayes risk is at most the Bayes risk of every randomized decision rule.
