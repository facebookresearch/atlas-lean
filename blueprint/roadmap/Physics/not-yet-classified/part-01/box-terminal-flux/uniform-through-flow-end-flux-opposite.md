# Uniform through-flow gives opposite end-face fluxes

Let $D_0, \dots, D_5 \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be face domains, $\rho_0, \dots, \rho_5 : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ outward normal current densities, and $\Phi_f = \int_{D_f} \rho_f(p)\, dp$ the flux through face $f$. Suppose $D_0$ and $D_1$ are measurable with finite and equal Lebesgue measure, and that for a constant $c \in \mathbb{R}$, $\rho_0 = -c$ on $D_0$ and $\rho_1 = c$ on $D_1$. Then

$$\Phi_1 = -\Phi_0.$$
