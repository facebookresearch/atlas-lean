# Balanced currents give zero neutral current

Let $\alpha = e^{2\pi i/3}$ and, for $X \in \mathbb{C}$, let $(X, \alpha^2 X, \alpha X)$ be the balanced positive-sequence phase set, indexed by $p \in \mathbb{Z}/3$ for the phases $a, b, c$. For every $I \in \mathbb{C}$, the neutral current of the balanced set vanishes:

$$I + \alpha^2 I + \alpha I = 0.$$
