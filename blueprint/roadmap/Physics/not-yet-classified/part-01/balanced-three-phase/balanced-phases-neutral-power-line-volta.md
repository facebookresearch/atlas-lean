# Balanced star connection: neutral current, total power and line voltages

Let $\alpha = e^{2\pi i/3}$ and, for $X \in \mathbb{C}$, let $(X, \alpha^2 X, \alpha X)$ be the balanced positive-sequence phase set, indexed by $p \in \mathbb{Z}/3$ for the phases $a, b, c$. Write $V_p$ and $I_p$ for the balanced phase sets of $V, I \in \mathbb{C}$. Then:

1. the neutral current vanishes: $I_0 + I_1 + I_2 = 0$;
2. the total complex power is $\sum_{p} V_p\, \overline{I_p} = 3\, V \overline{I}$;
3. every line-to-line voltage has magnitude $|V_p - V_{p+1}| = \sqrt{3}\, |V|$ for all $p \in \mathbb{Z}/3$.
