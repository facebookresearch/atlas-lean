# Total complex power of a balanced star

Let $\alpha = e^{2\pi i/3}$ and, for $X \in \mathbb{C}$, let $(X, \alpha^2 X, \alpha X)$ be the balanced positive-sequence phase set, indexed by $p \in \mathbb{Z}/3$ for the phases $a, b, c$. For all $V, I \in \mathbb{C}$, the sum of the per-phase complex powers $V_p \overline{I_p}$ of the balanced sets $V_p$ of $V$ and $I_p$ of $I$ is

$$V \overline{I} + (\alpha^2 V)\overline{(\alpha^2 I)} + (\alpha V)\overline{(\alpha I)} = 3\, V \overline{I}.$$
