# No supersaturation in layered affine profiles

Let $p_{\text{sat}}$ be monotone on $(0, \infty)$ and let face temperatures $T_0, \dots, T_n$ and vapor pressures $P_0, \dots, P_n$ satisfy $T_i > 0$ for $i \le n$ and $\max(P_i, P_{i+1}) \le p_{\text{sat}}(\min(T_i, T_{i+1}))$ for $i < n$. Then for every layer $i < n$ and $\theta \in [0,1]$, the linear interpolants $T = (1-\theta)T_i + \theta T_{i+1}$ and $P = (1-\theta)P_i + \theta P_{i+1}$ satisfy $T > 0$ and $P \le p_{\text{sat}}(T)$.
