# Balanced three-phase fault current

Let $E$, $Z_{th}$, $Z_f$ be complex with $Z_{th} + Z_f \ne 0$, and let $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$ be voltage and current bases. Then:

1. A pair $(V, I)$ satisfies the Thevenin and fault laws $V = E - Z_{th} I$ and $V = Z_f I$ if and only if $I = E/(Z_{th} + Z_f)$ and $V = Z_f E/(Z_{th} + Z_f)$.
2. The balanced phase currents with phase-$a$ current $E/(Z_{th} + Z_f)$ have zero zero-sequence and negative-sequence components and positive-sequence component $E/(Z_{th} + Z_f)$.
3. In per unit, the fault current $E/(Z_{th} + Z_f)$ equals the per-unit source voltage divided by the sum of the per-unit impedances $Z_{th}$ and $Z_f$.
