# Diode current

The ideal Shockley diode current from anode to cathode at anode-to-cathode voltage $V$, with saturation current $I_s$, emission coefficient $n$ and thermal voltage $V_T = k_B T/|q|$, is

$$I_D(V) = I_s\left(\exp\!\left(\frac{V}{n V_T}\right) - 1\right).$$

If $n V_T = 0$ (for instance $n = 0$, $T = 0$ or $q = 0$), the convention $x/0 = 0$ makes the formula equal to $0$.
