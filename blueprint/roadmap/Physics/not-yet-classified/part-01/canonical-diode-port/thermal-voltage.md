# Thermal voltage

The thermal voltage is

$$V_T = \frac{k_B T}{|q|},$$

where $k_B$ is the Boltzmann constant in the chosen units, $T$ the temperature and $q$ the carrier charge; the denominator is the magnitude $|q|$, not the signed charge. For $q = 0$ the formula takes the convention $x/0 = 0$, giving $V_T = 0$.
