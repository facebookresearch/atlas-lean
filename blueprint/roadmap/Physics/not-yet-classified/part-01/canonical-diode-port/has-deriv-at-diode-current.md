# Derivative of the diode current

For all parameters $I_s, n, k_B, T, q$ and every voltage $V$, the diode current $I_D(x) = I_s\left(e^{x/(n V_T)} - 1\right)$, with $V_T = k_B T/|q|$, has derivative

$$I_D'(V) = \frac{I_s}{n V_T}\, \exp\!\left(\frac{V}{n V_T}\right)$$

at $V$. When $n V_T = 0$ both sides are read with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (the function is then constant $0$ and the stated derivative is $0$).
