# Gauge strain is invariant under a change of units

Let $u_1, u_2$ be two unit systems over the seven ISQ base dimensions and let $s > 0$ be the conversion factor from $u_1$ to $u_2$ for the voltage dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}\,\mathsf{I}^{-1}$. For real $g, b, V$ with $g \neq 0$,

$$\frac{sV - sb}{sg} = \frac{V - b}{g},$$

that is, converting the voltage, the offset and the gain by the voltage factor leaves the gauge strain unchanged.
