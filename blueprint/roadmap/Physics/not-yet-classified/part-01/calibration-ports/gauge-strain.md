# Gauge strain from an affine calibration

For real gain $g$, offset $b$ and gauge voltage $V$, together with a proof that $g \neq 0$, the gauge strain is

$$\varepsilon = \frac{V - b}{g},$$

the strain determined by the affine calibration $V = g\,\varepsilon + b$. The hypothesis $g \neq 0$ is an argument of the definition, so the strain is only formed for a nonzero gain.
