# Voltage dimension

The voltage dimension is the electric-potential dimension

$$\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}\,\mathsf{I}^{-1}$$

over the seven ISQ base quantities, defined as the image in the ISQ dimension group of the charge-based voltage dimension. It is the dimension of the gauge voltage, the offset and the gain (volts per unit strain).
