# Traveling wave is positive when the amplitude is below the mean

Let $c(t, x) = c_0 + a \sin \theta$ with phase $\theta = 2\pi (x - v t)/L$ be the traveling sine wave with mean $c_0$, amplitude $a$, speed $v$ and spatial period $L$ (all real). If $|a| < c_0$, then $c(t, x) > 0$ for all real $v, L, t, x$. For $L = 0$ the phase is $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $c(t, x) = c_0$.
