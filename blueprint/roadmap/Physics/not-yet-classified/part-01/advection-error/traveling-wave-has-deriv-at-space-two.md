# Second spatial derivative of the traveling wave

Let $c(t, x) = c_0 + a \sin \theta$ with phase $\theta = 2\pi (x - v t)/L$ be the traveling sine wave with mean $c_0$, amplitude $a$, speed $v$ and spatial period $L$ (all real). For all real $c_0, a, v, L, t, x$, the spatial derivative $y \mapsto \partial_y c(t, y)$ has derivative

$$-a \Bigl(\frac{2\pi}{L}\Bigr)^2 \sin\!\Bigl(\frac{2\pi (x - v t)}{L}\Bigr)$$

at $x$. For $L = 0$ the statement holds through the convention $x/0 = 0$ (both sides are then $0$).
