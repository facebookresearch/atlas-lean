# Neutral displacement at the divergence pressure

Let $K, S, e, C_{L\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}$ with $0 < K$ and $0 < S\,e\,C_{L\alpha}$, and let $q_D = K/(S\,e\,C_{L\alpha})$. The net restoring moment about the elastic axis at dynamic pressure $q$, rigid angle $\alpha_0$ and twist $\theta$ is

$$M(q, \alpha_0, \theta) = K\theta - q\,S\,e\,C_{L\alpha}\,(\alpha_0 + \theta).$$

Then $q_D > 0$, and there exists a twist $\theta \neq 0$ with $M(q_D, 0, \theta) = 0$.
