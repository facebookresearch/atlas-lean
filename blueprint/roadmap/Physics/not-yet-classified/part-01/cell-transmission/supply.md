# Receiving supply of a cell

For free-flow speed $v$, wave speed $w$, jam density $k_j$ and cell density $k$, the receiving supply of the cell under the triangular diagram is the congested-branch flow capped at capacity, $\min\{w(k_j - k),\ vwk_j/(v + w)\}$, where the capacity term is $0$ when $v + w = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
