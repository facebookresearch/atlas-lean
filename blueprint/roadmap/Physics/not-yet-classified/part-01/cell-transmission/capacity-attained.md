# Capacity is attained at the critical density

For the triangular fundamental diagram with free-flow speed $v > 0$, wave speed $w > 0$ and jam density $k_j > 0$, both the demand $\min(vk, C)$ and the supply $\min(w(k_j - k), C)$ equal the capacity $C = vwk_j/(v + w)$ at the critical density $k_c = wk_j/(v + w)$.
