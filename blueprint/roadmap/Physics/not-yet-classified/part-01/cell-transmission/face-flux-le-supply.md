# Face flux is at most the downstream supply

For all real parameters, the cell-transmission face flux $\min(\mathrm{demand}(k_{up}), \mathrm{supply}(k_{down}))$ is at most the downstream receiving supply $\mathrm{supply}(k_{down}) = \min(w (k_j - k_{down}), q_{\max})$, where $q_{\max}$ is the capacity of the triangular fundamental diagram with free-flow speed $v$, wave speed $w$ and jam density $k_j$.
