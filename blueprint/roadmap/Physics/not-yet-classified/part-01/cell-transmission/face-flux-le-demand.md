# Cell-transmission face flux is at most the upstream demand

For a triangular fundamental diagram with free-flow speed $v$, wave speed $w$ and jam density $k_j$, the flux across a face between an upstream cell of density $k_{\mathrm{up}}$ and a downstream cell of density $k_{\mathrm{down}}$, defined as the minimum of the upstream demand $\min(v k_{\mathrm{up}}, q_{\max})$ and the downstream supply $\min(w (k_j - k_{\mathrm{down}}), q_{\max})$ with $q_{\max} = v w k_j/(v + w)$, never exceeds the upstream demand. No sign conditions are needed.
