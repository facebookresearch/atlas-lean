# Nonnegative face flux in the cell transmission model

Let $v > 0$, $w > 0$ and $k_j \ge 0$, and let the upstream and downstream densities satisfy $0 \le k_{up} \le k_j$ and $0 \le k_{down} \le k_j$. Then the face flux $\min\{\mathrm{demand}(k_{up}), \mathrm{supply}(k_{down})\}$ is nonnegative, where $\mathrm{demand}(k) = \min\{vk, q_{max}\}$, $\mathrm{supply}(k) = \min\{w(k_j - k), q_{max}\}$ and $q_{max} = v w k_j/(v + w)$.
