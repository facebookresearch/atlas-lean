# Energy of a simply supported beam mode is constant

Let $EI > 0$, $\rho A > 0$, $L > 0$ and let $k$ be a positive integer. Let $\omega_k = (k\pi/L)^2\sqrt{EI/\rho A}$ and $w(x, t) = \sin(k\pi x/L)\cos(\omega_k t)$. Define the beam energy

$$E(t) = \int_0^L \frac{\rho A\, w_t(x, t)^2 + EI\, w_{xx}(x, t)^2}{2}\,dx.$$

Then for every $t$,

$$E(t) = \frac{L}{4}\, EI \left(\frac{k\pi}{L}\right)^4.$$
