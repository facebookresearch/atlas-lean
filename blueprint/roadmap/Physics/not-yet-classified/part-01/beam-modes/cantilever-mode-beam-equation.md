# Cantilever mode solves the beam equation

Let $EI > 0$ and $\rho A > 0$, and let $L, z$ be real. With $\beta = z/L$, let

$$W(x) = (\sin z + \sinh z)(\cos \beta x - \cosh \beta x) - (\cos z + \cosh z)(\sin \beta x - \sinh \beta x),$$

$\omega = (z/L)^2\sqrt{EI/\rho A}$ and $w(x, t) = W(x)\cos(\omega t)$, where for $L = 0$ the quotients take the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $\beta = 0$ and $\omega = 0$. Then for all $x, t$,

$$\rho A\, w_{tt}(x, t) + EI\, w_{xxxx}(x, t) = 0.$$
