# Characteristic equation of the clamped-free beam

Let $\beta > 0$ and $L > 0$. For $c = (c_0, c_1, c_2, c_3) \in \mathbb{R}^4$ let

$$W_c(x) = c_0 \cos \beta x + c_1 \sin \beta x + c_2 \cosh \beta x + c_3 \sinh \beta x.$$

There is a nonzero $c$ such that $W_c$ satisfies the clamped-free conditions on $[0, L]$: $L > 0$, $W(0) = 0$, $W'(0) = 0$, $W''(L) = 0$ and $W'''(L) = 0$ if and only if

$$\cos(\beta L)\cosh(\beta L) + 1 = 0.$$
