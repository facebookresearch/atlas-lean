# Average power with peak phasors

For $\omega > 0$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}$, let $T = 2\pi/\omega$. For $V, I \in \mathbb{C}$, the peak-phasor waveforms $\operatorname{Re}(V e^{i\omega t})$ and $\operatorname{Re}(I e^{i\omega t})$ satisfy

$$\frac{1}{T} \int_a^{a+T} \operatorname{Re}\!\big(V e^{i\omega t}\big)\, \operatorname{Re}\!\big(I e^{i\omega t}\big)\, dt = \tfrac{1}{2}\, \operatorname{Re}\!\big(V \overline{I}\big).$$
