# Average power with RMS phasors

For $\omega > 0$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}$, let $T = 2\pi/\omega$. For $V, I \in \mathbb{C}$ and the RMS waveforms $v(t) = \sqrt{2}\, \operatorname{Re}(V e^{i\omega t})$ and $i(t) = \sqrt{2}\, \operatorname{Re}(I e^{i\omega t})$,

$$\frac{1}{T} \int_a^{a+T} v(t)\, i(t)\, dt = \operatorname{Re}\!\big(V \overline{I}\big).$$
