# Guarded Darcy–Weisbach friction loss

Let a friction correlation be recorded with its friction-factor convention, the duct shape, flow development and relative roughness it was calibrated for, and a formula defined on its validity domain of Reynolds numbers. Suppose it applies to a request: the correlation uses the Darcy convention, the request has the same duct shape, flow development and relative roughness, and the request's Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re}$ lies in the validity domain. Let $f > 0$ be the Darcy factor the correlation gives for the request.

Let $L, D, A, \rho, \mu > 0$, $Q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, and assume $\mathrm{Re} = \rho\, |Q/A|\, D / \mu$ and that the request's relative roughness is $\varepsilon / D$. With the loss coefficient $c = f L \rho / (2 D A^2)$ and the pressure drop $\Delta p(Q) = c\, Q |Q|$:

1. the correlation's calibrated relative roughness is $\varepsilon / D$;
2. $c > 0$;
3. $\mathrm{Re} = \rho\, |{-Q}/A|\, D / \mu$;
4. $\Delta p(Q)\, Q \ge 0$;
5. $\Delta p(-Q) = -\Delta p(Q)$.
