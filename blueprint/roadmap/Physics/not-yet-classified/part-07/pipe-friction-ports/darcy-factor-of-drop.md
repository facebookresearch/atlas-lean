# Darcy friction factor of a pressure drop

For an oriented pressure drop $\Delta p$ over a length $L$ of a duct of diameter $D$ carrying a fluid of density $\rho$ at signed mean velocity $V$, the Darcy friction factor is

$$f = \frac{2 D\, \Delta p}{\rho\, V |V|\, L},$$

defined for $L > 0$, $D > 0$, $\rho > 0$ and a drop in the direction of the flow, $\Delta p\, V > 0$ (which forces $V \ne 0$). It is the oriented Darcy–Weisbach relation $\Delta p = f\, \frac{L}{D}\, \frac{\rho V |V|}{2}$ solved for $f$.
