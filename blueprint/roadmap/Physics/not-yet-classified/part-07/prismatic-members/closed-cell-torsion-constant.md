# Torsion constant of a thin closed rectangular cell

For a thin closed rectangular cell with median side lengths $a$, $b$ and wall thickness $t$, the torsion constant is

$$J = \frac{2\,a^2 b^2\,t}{a + b},$$

which is $4A^2 t / S$ with enclosed area $A = ab$ and median perimeter $S = 2(a + b)$. For $a + b = 0$ the formula takes the convention $x/0 = 0$.
