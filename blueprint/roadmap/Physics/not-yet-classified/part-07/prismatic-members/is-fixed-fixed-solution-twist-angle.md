# Uniform-torque twist angle solves the fixed-fixed shaft problem

Let $L > 0$, $GJ > 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $\varphi(x) = \dfrac{t\,x\,(L - x)}{2\,GJ}$. Then $\varphi$ is $C^2$ on $[0, L]$, satisfies $-GJ\,\varphi''(x) = t$ for every $x \in [0, L]$ (second derivative taken within $[0, L]$), and $\varphi(0) = \varphi(L) = 0$.
