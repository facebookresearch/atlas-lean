# Uniform-load displacement solves the fixed-fixed bar problem

Let $L > 0$, $EA > 0$ and $q \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $u(x) = \dfrac{q\,x\,(L - x)}{2\,EA}$. Then $u$ is $C^2$ on $[0, L]$, satisfies $-EA\,u''(x) = q$ for every $x \in [0, L]$ (second derivative taken within $[0, L]$), and $u(0) = u(L) = 0$.
