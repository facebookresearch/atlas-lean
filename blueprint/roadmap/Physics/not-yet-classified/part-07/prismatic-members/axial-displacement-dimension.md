# Dimension of the axial displacement formula

In the ISQ dimension group, let $\mathsf{F} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ be the dimension of force and $\mathsf{L}$ that of length. Then

$$\frac{\mathsf{F}\,\mathsf{L}^{-1} \cdot \mathsf{L} \cdot \mathsf{L}}{\mathsf{F}} = \mathsf{L}:$$

a load per length times two lengths over an axial rigidity is a length.
