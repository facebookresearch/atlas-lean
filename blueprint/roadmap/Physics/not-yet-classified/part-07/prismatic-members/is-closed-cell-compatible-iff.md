# Twist rate under the closed-cell compatibility law

Let $a, b, t, G > 0$, let $q, \theta' \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $O \in \mathbb{R}^3$ be any reference point. Let $T$ be the $z$-component of the moment about $O$ of the four wall resultants $(qa, 0, 0)$, $(0, qb, 0)$, $(-qa, 0, 0)$, $(0, -qb, 0)$ acting at the wall midpoints $(0, -b/2, 0)$, $(a/2, 0, 0)$, $(0, b/2, 0)$, $(-a/2, 0, 0)$ of the median rectangle $[-a/2, a/2] \times [-b/2, b/2]$. Then the compatibility law

$$\sum_{i=1}^{4} \frac{q}{G t}\,\ell_i = 2ab\,\theta', \qquad (\ell_1, \ell_2, \ell_3, \ell_4) = (a, b, a, b)$$

holds if and only if

$$\theta' = \frac{T\,(a + b)}{2\,G\,a^2 b^2\,t}.$$
