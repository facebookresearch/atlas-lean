# Linear candidate at a fixed point lies within the rails

Let $A, \beta, v_i \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v_{\min} \le v_{\max}$. Let $v^* = \dfrac{A\,v_i}{1 + A\beta}$ be the linear candidate, and let $v_o$ be a fixed point of the rail-limited map, $v_o = \max\big(v_{\min}, \min(v_{\max}, A(v_i - \beta v_o))\big)$. If $v_o = v^*$, then $v^* \in [v_{\min}, v_{\max}]$. For $1 + A\beta = 0$ the candidate takes the convention $x/0 = 0$, so $v^* = 0$.
