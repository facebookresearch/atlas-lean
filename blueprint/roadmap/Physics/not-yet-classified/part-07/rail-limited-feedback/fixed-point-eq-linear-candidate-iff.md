# Fixed point equals the linear candidate iff the candidate is within the rails

Let $A > 0$, $\beta > 0$, $v_{\min} < v_{\max}$ and $v_i \in \mathbb{R}$. Let $v^* = \dfrac{A\,v_i}{1 + A\beta}$ be the linear candidate, and let $v_o$ be a fixed point of the rail-limited map, $v_o = \max\big(v_{\min}, \min(v_{\max}, A(v_i - \beta v_o))\big)$. Then

$$v_o = v^* \iff v^* \in [v_{\min}, v_{\max}].$$
