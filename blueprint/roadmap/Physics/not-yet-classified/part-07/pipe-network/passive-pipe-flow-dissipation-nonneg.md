# Passive pipe flow dissipates energy

On a finite directed pipe network, let $q_e$ be arc flows, $d_v$ net nodal inflows, $h_v$ nodal heads and $r_e \ge 0$ arc resistances, with flow conservation $Bq = d$ for the node-arc incidence matrix $B$ and the head drop $h(\mathrm{tail}\,e) - h(\mathrm{head}\,e) = r_e q_e |q_e|$ on every arc. Then the dissipation pairing is nonnegative: $-\sum_v h_v d_v \ge 0$.
