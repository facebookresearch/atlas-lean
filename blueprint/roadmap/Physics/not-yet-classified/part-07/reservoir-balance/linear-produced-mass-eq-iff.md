# Product identification, both directions

Let the linearized produced mass be $V_p \rho_0 c (p_0 - p)$ for pore volume $V_p$, reference density $\rho_0$, compressibility $c$ and pressure drop from $p_0$ to $p$. If $\rho_0 \ne 0$ and $p \ne p_0$, two parameter pairs give equal linearized produced mass if and only if $V_{p,1} c_1 = V_{p,2} c_2$.
