# PEC wall conditions in components

For widths $a, b$ and fields $E, B : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, the component conditions state that $a > 0$, $b > 0$, and for every $z$:
- on $x = 0$ and on $x = a$, for $0 \le y \le b$: $E_y = E_z = B_x = 0$;
- on $y = 0$ and on $y = b$, for $0 \le x \le a$: $E_x = E_z = B_y = 0$.
