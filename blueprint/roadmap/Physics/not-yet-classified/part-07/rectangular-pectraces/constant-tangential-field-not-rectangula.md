# A constant tangential field violates the PEC wall conditions

For widths $a, b$ and fields $E, B : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, the *PEC trace condition* of the rectangular guide requires $a > 0$, $b > 0$, and that the tangential components of $E$ and the normal component of $B$ vanish at every point of the four side walls $\{x = 0\}$, $\{x = a\}$ (with $0 \le y \le b$) and $\{y = 0\}$, $\{y = b\}$ (with $0 \le x \le a$), for every $z$. For all real $a$ and $b$, the constant fields $E \equiv (0, 1, 0)$ and $B \equiv 0$ do not satisfy the PEC trace condition.
