# PEC trace condition on a rectangular guide

For widths $a, b$ and fields $E, B : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, the PEC trace condition states that $a > 0$, $b > 0$, and for every $z$: on the walls $x = 0$ and $x = a$ (with $0 \le y \le b$) the tangential electric components $E_y, E_z$ and the normal magnetic component $B_x$ vanish; on the walls $y = 0$ and $y = b$ (with $0 \le x \le a$) the tangential electric components $E_x, E_z$ and the normal magnetic component $B_y$ vanish.
