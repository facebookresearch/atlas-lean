# Ebers-Moll collector current in transport form

Let $\alpha_F$ and $\alpha_R$ be nonzero. With saturation currents $I_{SF} = I_S/\alpha_F$ and $I_{SR} = I_S/\alpha_R$, the collector current $I_C = \alpha_F F - R$, where $F = I_{SF}(e^{V_{BE}/V_T} - 1)$ and $R = I_{SR}(e^{V_{BC}/V_T} - 1)$, equals

$$I_C = I_S\left(e^{V_{BE}/V_T} - e^{V_{BC}/V_T}\right) - I_S\,\frac{1 - \alpha_R}{\alpha_R}\left(e^{V_{BC}/V_T} - 1\right).$$

No condition is placed on $V_T$: for $V_T = 0$ the convention $x/0 = 0$ gives $V_{BE}/V_T = V_{BC}/V_T = 0$, and both sides are $0$.
