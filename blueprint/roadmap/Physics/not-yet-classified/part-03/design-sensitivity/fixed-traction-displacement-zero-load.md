# Fixed-traction bar displacement at zero load

For a bar fixed at $x = 0$ with axial rigidity $EA$, length $L$, end force $F$ and distributed load $f$, the constructed displacement is

$$u(x) = \int_0^x \frac{F + \int_y^L f(z)\,dz}{EA}\,dy.$$

For $f = 0$ this equals $u(x) = F x / EA$ for all real $EA$, $L$, $F$, $x$. For $EA = 0$ both sides are $0$ by the convention $a/0 = 0$.
