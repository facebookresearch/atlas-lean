# Derivative of the compliance within the design interval

Let $J(F, k) = F \cdot (F/k)$ be the compliance under end force $F$ at stiffness $k$. Let $0 < k_{\min}$ and $k \in [k_{\min}, k_{\max}]$. Then for every $F$, the map $k \mapsto J(F, k)$ has derivative $-F^2/k^2$ at $k$ within $[k_{\min}, k_{\max}]$.
