# Friis ratio form fails at zero transmit power

Let $r > 0$ and $\lambda > 0$, with unit gains and transmit power $P_t = 0$: power density $S = 0\cdot 1/(4\pi r^2)$, effective aperture $A_e = \lambda^2/(4\pi)$, and received power $P_r = S A_e$. With division by zero taken as $0$, the ratio $P_r/P_t$ is not equal to the free-space factor $(\lambda/(4\pi r))^2$.
