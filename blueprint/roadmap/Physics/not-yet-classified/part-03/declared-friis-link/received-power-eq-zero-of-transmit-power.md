# Friis link: zero transmit power gives zero received power

For $r > 0$, $\lambda > 0$, $G_t \ge 0$ and $G_r \ge 0$, suppose the power density at range $r$ is $S = 0 \cdot G_t/(4\pi r^2)$ (transmit power $P_t = 0$), the effective aperture is $A_e = G_r \lambda^2/(4\pi)$ and the received power is $P_r = S A_e$. Then $P_r = 0$.
