# Friis value iff far-field flux

Let $r > 0$, $\lambda > 0$ and $G_r > 0$, and suppose the effective aperture is $A_e = G_r \lambda^2/(4\pi)$ and the received power is $P_r = S A_e$ for a power flux density $S$. Then

$$P_r = P_t G_t G_r \left(\frac{\lambda}{4 \pi r}\right)^2 \iff S = \frac{P_t G_t}{4 \pi r^2}.$$
