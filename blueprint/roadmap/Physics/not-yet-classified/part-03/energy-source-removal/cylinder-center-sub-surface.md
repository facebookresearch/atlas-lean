# Center-to-surface temperature rise in a heated cylinder

For all real $T_s$, $R$, $k$ and $q$, the heated-cylinder profile $T(r) = T_s + q (R^2 - r^2)/(4k)$ satisfies

$$T(0) - T(R) = \frac{q R^2}{4k}.$$

No hypothesis on $k$ is needed: for $k = 0$ both sides are $0$ under the convention $x/0 = 0$.
