# Outward power of a heated cylinder

Let $T'(r) = -q r / (2k)$ be the radial gradient of the heated-cylinder profile. For radius $R > 0$, conductivity $k > 0$ and any source $q$,

$$2\pi R\,\bigl(-k\,T'(R)\bigr) = \pi R^2 q,$$

so the outward conductive power per unit length equals the heat generated in the cross-section.
