# Outlet temperature bound from a mass-flow floor

Let $0 < \dot m_{\min} \le \dot m$, $c_p > 0$, $q_0 \ge 0$ and $0 < L \le L_e$, and let $T_{\mathrm{in}}$ be any real number. Writing

$$T_{\mathrm{out}}(\dot m) = T_{\mathrm{in}} + \frac{2 q_0 L_e \sin(\pi L / (2 L_e))}{\pi \dot m c_p},$$

the outlet temperature satisfies $T_{\mathrm{out}}(\dot m) \le T_{\mathrm{out}}(\dot m_{\min})$.
