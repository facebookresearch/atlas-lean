# Heated-cylinder temperature profile

For surface temperature $T_s$, radius $R$, conductivity $k$, uniform volumetric heat source $q$ and radial coordinate $r \in \mathbb{R}$, the steady solid-cylinder temperature profile is

$$T(r) = T_s + \frac{q\,(R^2 - r^2)}{4k}.$$

For $k = 0$ the formula takes the convention $x/0 = 0$ and gives $T(r) = T_s$.
