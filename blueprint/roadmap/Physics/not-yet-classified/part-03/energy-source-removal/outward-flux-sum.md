# Outward heat removal from a heated slab

Let

$$T'(d) = \frac{T_b - T_s}{L} + \frac{H L}{2k} - \frac{H d}{k}$$

be the gradient of the slab temperature profile. For $k \ne 0$ and all real $T_s$, $T_b$, $L$ and $H$,

$$k\,T'(0) - k\,T'(L) = H L.$$

For $L = 0$ the quotient $(T_b - T_s)/L$ is $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
