# Gray parallel plates at equal temperature exchange no power

Consider two plates of common area $A$ with the view factors of infinite parallel planes, $F_{01} = F_{10} = 1$ and $F_{00} = F_{11} = 0$, emissivities $\varepsilon_0, \varepsilon_1$ with $0 < \varepsilon_i \le 1$, temperatures $T_0, T_1$ and a constant $\sigma$. Let the radiosities $J_0, J_1$ satisfy the gray radiosity equations

$$J_i = \varepsilon_i \sigma T_i^4 + (1 - \varepsilon_i) \sum_j F_{ij} J_j \qquad (i = 0, 1).$$

If $T_0 = T_1$, then the gray net power leaving plate $0$, $q_0 = \sum_j A F_{0j} (J_0 - J_j)$, is zero.
