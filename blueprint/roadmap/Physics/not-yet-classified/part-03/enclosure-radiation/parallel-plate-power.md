# Net power between two gray parallel plates (formula)

For a constant $\sigma$, plate area $A$, emissivities $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2$ and temperatures $T_1, T_2$, the parallel-plate net power is

$$q = \frac{\sigma A\, (T_1^4 - T_2^4)}{1/\varepsilon_1 + 1/\varepsilon_2 - 1}.$$

It models the net radiative power (watts) from plate 1 to plate 2 of two equal-area gray plates with the view factors of infinite parallel planes; positive values are a net outflow from plate 1, and for finite plates it is an approximation. When the denominator vanishes, or an emissivity is $0$, the formula takes the convention $x/0 = 0$.
