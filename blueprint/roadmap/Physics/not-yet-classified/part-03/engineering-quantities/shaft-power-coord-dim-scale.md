# Shaft power formula commutes with unit changes

For ISQ unit scales $u_1, u_2$ (each assigning a positive magnitude to every base unit) and a dimension $d = \prod_b b^{d_b}$, let $s(d) = \prod_b \bigl(u_1(b)/u_2(b)\bigr)^{d_b} > 0$ be the factor converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $u_1$ to $u_2$, and $s'(d)$ the factor from $u_2$ back to $u_1$.

Write $\mathsf{\Gamma} = \mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}}$ (force times length), $\mathsf{T^{-1}}$ and $\mathsf{P} = \mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-3}}$ for the torque, angular velocity and power dimensions. For all real $\tau$ and $\omega$,

$$\bigl(s(\mathsf{\Gamma})\,\tau\bigr)\bigl(s(\mathsf{T^{-1}})\,\omega\bigr) = s(\mathsf{P})\,(\tau\,\omega).$$
