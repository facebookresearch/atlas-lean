# Typed shaft power commutes with unit changes

For ISQ unit scales $u_1, u_2$ (each assigning a positive magnitude to every base unit) and a dimension $d = \prod_b b^{d_b}$, let $s(d) = \prod_b \bigl(u_1(b)/u_2(b)\bigr)^{d_b} > 0$ be the factor converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $u_1$ to $u_2$, and $s'(d)$ the factor from $u_2$ back to $u_1$.

For a torque $\tau$ (dimension $\mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}}$, coordinate $\tau_0$) and an angular velocity $\omega$ (dimension $\mathsf{T^{-1}}$, coordinate $\omega_0$), let $P(\tau, \omega)$ be the shaft power, the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-3}}$ with coordinate $\tau_0\omega_0$. Then the shaft power of the converted inputs, with coordinates $s(\mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}})\tau_0$ and $s(\mathsf{T^{-1}})\omega_0$, equals the power quantity with coordinate $s(\mathsf{M\,L^2\,T^{-3}})\,\tau_0\omega_0$.
