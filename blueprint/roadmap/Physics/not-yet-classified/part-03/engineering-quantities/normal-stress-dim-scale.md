# Typed normal stress commutes with unit changes

For ISQ unit scales $u_1, u_2$ (each assigning a positive magnitude to every base unit) and a dimension $d = \prod_b b^{d_b}$, let $s(d) = \prod_b \bigl(u_1(b)/u_2(b)\bigr)^{d_b} > 0$ be the factor converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $u_1$ to $u_2$, and $s'(d)$ the factor from $u_2$ back to $u_1$.

For a force $F$ (dimension $\mathsf{M\,L\,T^{-2}}$, coordinate $F_0$) and an area $A$ (dimension $\mathsf{L^2}$, coordinate $A_0$) with $0 < A_0$, let $\sigma(F, A)$ be the normal stress, the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M\,L^{-1}\,T^{-2}}$ with coordinate $F_0 / A_0$. Then the normal stress of the converted force and area, with coordinates $s(\mathsf{M\,L\,T^{-2}})F_0$ and $s(\mathsf{L^2})A_0$ (a positive area), equals the stress quantity with coordinate $s(\mathsf{M\,L^{-1}\,T^{-2}})\,F_0/A_0$.
