# Typed mass flow rate commutes with unit changes

For ISQ unit scales $u_1, u_2$ (each assigning a positive magnitude to every base unit) and a dimension $d = \prod_b b^{d_b}$, let $s(d) = \prod_b \bigl(u_1(b)/u_2(b)\bigr)^{d_b} > 0$ be the factor converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $u_1$ to $u_2$, and $s'(d)$ the factor from $u_2$ back to $u_1$.

For quantities $\rho$ of dimension $\mathsf{M\,L^{-3}}$, $v$ of dimension $\mathsf{L\,T^{-1}}$ and $A$ of dimension $\mathsf{L^2}$ with coordinates $\rho_0, v_0, A_0$ and $0 < A_0$, let $\dot m(\rho, v, A)$ be the mass flow rate, the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M\,T^{-1}}$ with coordinate $\rho_0 v_0 A_0$. Then the mass flow rate of the converted inputs, with coordinates $s(\mathsf{M\,L^{-3}})\rho_0$, $s(\mathsf{L\,T^{-1}})v_0$ and $s(\mathsf{L^2})A_0$ (a positive area), equals the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M\,T^{-1}}$ with coordinate $s(\mathsf{M\,T^{-1}})\,\rho_0 v_0 A_0$.
