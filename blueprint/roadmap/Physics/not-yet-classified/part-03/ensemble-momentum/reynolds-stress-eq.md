# Covariance form of the Reynolds stress

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on realizations, and let the velocity components $u_j$ and $u_i$ at a fixed time and point be square integrable. Then the Reynolds stress per unit density, the covariance $\mathbb E[u'_j u'_i]$ of the fluctuations $u' = u - \mathbb E[u]$, equals $\mathbb E[u_j u_i] - \mathbb E[u_j]\,\mathbb E[u_i]$.
