# The RMS waveform determines its phasor

For a phasor $V \in \mathbb{C}$ and angular frequency $\omega \in \mathbb{R}$, the RMS waveform is $w_{V,\omega}(t) = \sqrt{2}\, \operatorname{Re}\!\big(V e^{i\omega t}\big)$. For $\omega \ne 0$ and $V, W \in \mathbb{C}$,

$$w_{V,\omega} = w_{W,\omega} \text{ (as functions of } t) \iff V = W.$$
