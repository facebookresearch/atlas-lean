# Small-signal remainder bound along a segment

Let $\mathbb{R}^n$ carry the sup norm, let $i : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, and write the small-signal remainder at the operating point $v_0$ for the perturbation $\delta v$ as $r = i(v_0 + \delta v) - i(v_0) - Di(v_0)[\delta v]$. Assume $i$ is differentiable at $v_0$, the function $g(t) = i(v_0 + t\,\delta v)$ is $C^2$ on $\mathbb{R}$, and $|g''(t)| \le M\,\|\delta v\|^2$ for every $t \in [0, 1]$. Then

$$|r| \le \frac{M\,\|\delta v\|^2}{2}.$$
