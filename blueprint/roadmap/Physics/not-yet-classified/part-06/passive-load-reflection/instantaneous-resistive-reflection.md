# Instantaneous reflection at a resistive load

Let $Z_0 > 0$ and $R + Z_0 ≠ 0$, and let $v_+, v_-, v, i : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy, for every $t$, $v(t) = v_+(t) + v_-(t)$, $i(t) = (v_+(t) - v_-(t))/Z_0$ and $v(t) = R\,i(t)$. Then for every $t$

$$v_-(t) = \frac{R - Z_0}{R + Z_0}\,v_+(t).$$
