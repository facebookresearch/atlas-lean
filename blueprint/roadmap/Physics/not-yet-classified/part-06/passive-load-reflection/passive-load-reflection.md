# Passive load reflection criterion

Let $Z_L \in \mathbb{C}$, $Z_0 > 0$ and $Z_L + Z_0 ≠ 0$. Then

$$\Big|\frac{Z_L - Z_0}{Z_L + Z_0}\Big| \le 1 \iff \operatorname{Re} Z_L \ge 0,$$

and for all $V, I, A_+, A_- \in \mathbb{C}$ with $V = A_+ + A_-$, $I = (A_+ - A_-)/Z_0$, $V = Z_L\,I$ and $A_+ ≠ 0$,

$$|A_-| \le |A_+| \iff \operatorname{Re} Z_L \ge 0.$$
