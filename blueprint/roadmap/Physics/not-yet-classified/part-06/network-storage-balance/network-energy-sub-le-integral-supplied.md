# Finite-horizon network energy inequality

Let $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n_N \times n_B}$ be a node-branch matrix and let the branches be split by sets $\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{L}, \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{S}$ that are pairwise disjoint and cover all branches, with values $R_e \ge 0$ on $\mathcal{R}$, $L_e > 0$ on $\mathcal{L}$ and $C_e > 0$ on $\mathcal{C}$. For node potentials $\varphi(s)$, node injections $j(s)$, branch voltages $v(s)$ and branch currents $i(s)$, let

$$E(s) = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{C}} \frac{C_e v_e(s)^2}{2} + \sum_{e \in \mathcal{L}} \frac{L_e i_e(s)^2}{2}, \qquad P_S(s) = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{S}} v_e(s)\,i_e(s), \qquad P_R(s) = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{R}} R_e\,i_e(s)^2.$$ Let $a \le b$ and suppose that at every $s \in [a, b]$: $v(s) = B^\top \varphi(s)$, $B\,i(s) = j(s)$, $v_e = R_e i_e$ on $\mathcal{R}$, $i_e = C_e v_e'$ on $\mathcal{C}$, $v_e = L_e i_e'$ on $\mathcal{L}$, with $v_e$ differentiable at $s$ for $e \in \mathcal{C}$ and $i_e$ differentiable at $s$ for $e \in \mathcal{L}$. Suppose also that $\varphi \cdot j - P_S$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and $\varphi \cdot j - P_S - P_R$ is integrable on $[a, b]$. Then
$$E(b) - E(a) \le \int_a^b \big(\varphi(s) \cdot j(s) - P_S(s)\big)\,ds.$$
