# Per-unit bases

For phase voltage base $V_b > 0$ and current base $I_b > 0$, the per-unit coordinate change preserves Ohm's law ($V = Z I$ if and only if the per-unit quantities satisfy it), sends balanced three-phase power $3 V \bar I$ to the per-unit product $V_{pu} \bar I_{pu}$, and the impedance base equals the squared line-voltage base over the three-phase power base.
