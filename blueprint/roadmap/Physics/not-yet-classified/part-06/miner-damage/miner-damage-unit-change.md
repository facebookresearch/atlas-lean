# Miner damage under a change of cycle unit

For finitely many load classes $i$, cycle counts $n_i$, lives $N_i > 0$ and a factor $c > 0$, the Palmgren-Miner damage $D = \sum_i n_i/N_i$ satisfies

$$\sum_i \frac{c\,n_i}{c\,N_i} = D \qquad\text{and}\qquad \sum_i \frac{c\,n_i}{N_i} = c\,D.$$

Changing the cycle unit of both counts and lives (cycles to reversals: $c = 2$) leaves $D$ unchanged, while converting only the counts multiplies $D$ by $c$.
