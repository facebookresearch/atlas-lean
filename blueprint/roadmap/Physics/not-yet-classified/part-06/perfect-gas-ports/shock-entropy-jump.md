# Normal-shock entropy jump

For $\gamma$ and $m = M_1^2$, the dimensionless entropy jump of the normal shock is

$$\frac{\Delta s}{R} = \frac{\log P}{\gamma - 1} - \frac{\gamma \log r}{\gamma - 1},\quad P = \frac{2\gamma m - (\gamma - 1)}{\gamma + 1},\quad r = \frac{(\gamma + 1)m}{(\gamma - 1)m + 2}.$$

Each quotient is $0$ when its denominator ($\gamma - 1$, $\gamma + 1$ or $(\gamma - 1)m + 2$) vanishes, by the convention $x/0 = 0$. It has its physical meaning for $\gamma > 1$ and $m > (\gamma - 1)/(2\gamma)$, where $P, r > 0$.
