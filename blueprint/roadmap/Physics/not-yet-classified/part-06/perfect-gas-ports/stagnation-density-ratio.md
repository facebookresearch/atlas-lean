# Stagnation density

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$, $\rho > 0$, $\rho_0 > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $a^2 = \gamma R T$ and $M = |u|/a$. Suppose the stagnation energy balance $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$, the ideal-gas laws $p = \rho R T$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$, and the isentropic relation $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$ hold. Then

$$\frac{\rho_0}{\rho} = \left(\frac{T_0}{T}\right)^{1/(\gamma - 1)} = \left(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M^2\right)^{1/(\gamma - 1)}.$$
