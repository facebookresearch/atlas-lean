# Isentropic mass flux increases strictly on subsonic Mach numbers

For $p_0 > 0$, $T_0 > 0$, $R > 0$ and $\gamma > 1$, the isentropic mass flux

$$G(M) = p_0\sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R T_0}}\, M \left(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M^2\right)^{-\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}}$$

is strictly increasing on $0 < M < 1$.
