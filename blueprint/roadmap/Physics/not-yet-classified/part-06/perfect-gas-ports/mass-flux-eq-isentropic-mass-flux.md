# Mass flux of an isentropic state

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R / (\gamma - 1)$, sound speed $a > 0$ with $a^2 = \gamma R T$, and Mach number $M = |u|/a$. Suppose the stagnation energy balance $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$, densities $\rho > 0$ and $\rho_0 > 0$, the ideal-gas laws $p = \rho R T$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$, and the isentropic relation $p / \rho^\gamma = p_0 / \rho_0^\gamma$. Then

$$\rho |u| = p_0 \sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R T_0}}\, M \Big(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M^2\Big)^{-\frac{\gamma + 1}{2(\gamma - 1)}}.$$
