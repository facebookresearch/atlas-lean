# Derivative of the normal-shock entropy jump

Let $\rho_r(m) = \frac{(\gamma + 1)m}{(\gamma - 1)m + 2}$ and $p_r(m) = \frac{2\gamma m - (\gamma - 1)}{\gamma + 1}$ be the shock density and pressure ratios at squared Mach number $m$, and $\Delta s/R = \frac{\ln p_r(m) - \gamma\ln\rho_r(m)}{\gamma - 1}$. For $\gamma > 1$ and $m > (\gamma - 1)/(2\gamma)$, $\frac{d}{dm}\frac{\Delta s}{R} = \frac{2\gamma(m - 1)^2}{m((\gamma - 1)m + 2)(2\gamma m - \gamma + 1)}$.
