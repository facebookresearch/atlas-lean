# Sign of the entropy jump

For $\gamma > 1$ and $m > (\gamma - 1)/(2\gamma)$, where $m = M_1^2$, the normal-shock entropy jump $\Delta s/R = \frac{\log P - \gamma \log r}{\gamma - 1}$, with $P$ and $r$ the shock pressure and density ratios, satisfies $\Delta s/R > 0 \iff m > 1$ and $\Delta s/R < 0 \iff m < 1$.
