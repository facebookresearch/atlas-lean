# Trivial normal-shock jump

For any $\gamma$, $R$ and a state $(\rho_1, u_1, p_1, T_1)$ with $p_1 = \rho_1 R T_1$, the state satisfies the normal-shock jump conditions with itself as the downstream state. The jump conditions are the ideal-gas law at both states and conservation of $\rho u$, $p + \rho u^2$ and $c_p T + u^2/2$, with $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$.
