# No area ratio at rest

Let $G(M) = p_0 \sqrt{\gamma/(R T_0)}\, M \left(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M^2\right)^{-(\gamma+1)/(2(\gamma-1))}$ be the isentropic mass flux per unit area. For all $p_0, T_0, R, \gamma$, $G(0) = 0$, and, with division by zero read as zero, $G(1)/G(0) \ge 1$ fails. Area-ratio quotients therefore need $M > 0$.
