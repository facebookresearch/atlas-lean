# Isentropic mass flux

For stagnation pressure $p_0$, stagnation temperature $T_0$, specific gas constant $R$, heat-capacity ratio $\gamma$ and Mach number $M$, the isentropic mass flux per unit area of a perfect gas is

$$G(M) = p_0 \sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R T_0}}\, M \Bigl(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M^2\Bigr)^{-\frac{\gamma + 1}{2(\gamma - 1)}}$$

with a real power. By the convention $x/0 = 0$, $G = 0$ when $R T_0 = 0$, and the power factor is $1$ when $\gamma = 1$.
