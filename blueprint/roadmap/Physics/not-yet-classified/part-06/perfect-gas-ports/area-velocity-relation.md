# Area-velocity relation

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $\rho_0 > 0$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$. Near a station $x_0$, suppose $T > 0$, $\rho > 0$, $p = \rho R T$, $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$, $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$ and $\rho u A = \dot m$. Suppose $u(x_0) > 0$, $A(x_0) > 0$, $a^2 = \gamma R T(x_0)$, $M = |u(x_0)|/a$, and $A$, $\rho$, $u$ are differentiable at $x_0$ with derivatives $A'$, $\rho'$, $u'$. Then

$$\frac{A'}{A(x_0)} = (M^2 - 1)\,\frac{u'}{u(x_0)}.$$
