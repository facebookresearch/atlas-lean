# Existence of a radiator solution trapped in the box

Let $C > 0$, $K > 0$ and $0 \le T_{\min} \le T_{\max}$, and let $Q$ satisfy the inward sign conditions $Q - K T_{\min}^4 \ge 0$ and $Q - K T_{\max}^4 \le 0$. Let $r(T) = (Q - K T^4)/C$ and $\tilde r(T) = r\big(\min(\max(T, T_{\min}), T_{\max})\big)$ the clipped rate.

For every $T_0 \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$ there is $T : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $T(0) = T_0$ and $T'(t) = \tilde r(T(t))$ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, such that for every $t \ge 0$ both $T(t) \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$ and $T'(t) = r(T(t))$.
