# Inventory rate of a moving front

Let $I(\xi) = k_L(\xi - a) + k_R(b - \xi)$ be the inventory on $[a, b]$ with density $k_L$ left of the front $\xi$ and $k_R$ right of it. For a front $\xi(t) = st + \xi_0$ moving at constant speed $s$, $t \mapsto I(\xi(t))$ has derivative $s(k_L - k_R)$ at every $t$.
