# Shaft sizing inequality

Let $\Phi$ be a yield functional calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c > 0$: $\Phi$ of the state with axial normal stress $\sigma$ and shear $\tau$ equals $\sqrt{\sigma^2 + c\tau^2}$. For a solid circular shaft of diameter $d > 0$ under bending moment $M$ and torque $T$, with outer-fiber stresses $\sigma = 32M/(\pi d^3)$ and $\tau = 16T/(\pi d^3)$, and an allowable $A > 0$, the demand $\Phi$ at the critical point is at most $A$ if and only if

$$\left(\frac{16\sqrt{4M^2 + cT^2}}{\pi A}\right)^{1/3} \le d.$$
