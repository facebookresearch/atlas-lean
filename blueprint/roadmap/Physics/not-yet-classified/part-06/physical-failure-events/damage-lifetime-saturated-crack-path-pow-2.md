# Unloaded crack path never fails, power branch

Let $K > 0$, $r$ nonzero, and $0 < a_0 < a_c$ with $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ the critical crack length; let $a(N) = (a_0^r + rKN)^{1/r}$ and $N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(rK)$. At load frequency $0$ the event path $t \mapsto a(\min(0\cdot t, N_f))$ equals $a_0$ for every $t \ge 0$, and its damage lifetime against the threshold $a_c$ is $\infty$.
