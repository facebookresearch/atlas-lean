# Stress intensity at the critical crack length

Let $K_{IC} > 0$, $Y > 0$ and $\sigma > 0$, and let $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$. Then the mode-I stress intensity $K_I(a) = Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a}$ at the critical crack length equals the fracture toughness:

$$K_I(a_c) = K_{IC}.$$

This is the critical-crack criterion of linear-elastic fracture mechanics (Dowling, *Mechanical Behavior of Materials*, 4th ed.).
