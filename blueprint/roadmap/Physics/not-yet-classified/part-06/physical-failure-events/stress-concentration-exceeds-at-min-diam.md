# Stress concentration exceeds the allowable at the minimum diameter

Let $\Phi$ be a yield functional calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c > 0$ ($\Phi$ of the state with normal stress $\sigma$ and shear $\tau$ is $\sqrt{\sigma^2 + c\tau^2}$). Let $M$ be nonzero, $A > 0$, $K > 1$ and $T$ real, and let $d_m = (16\sqrt{4M^2 + cT^2}/(\pi A))^{1/3}$ be the minimum diameter, with bending stress $\sigma_b = M(d_m/2)/(\pi d_m^4/64)$ and shear stress $\tau = T(d_m/2)/(\pi d_m^4/32)$. Then

$$\Phi(\sigma_b, \tau) = A \quad\text{and}\quad A < \Phi(K\sigma_b, \tau).$$
