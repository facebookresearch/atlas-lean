# Mode-I stress intensity factor

The mode-I stress intensity factor in the linear-elastic fracture mechanics model is

$$K_I(a) = Y\,\sigma\,\sqrt{\pi a},$$

where $Y$ is a constant geometry factor, $\sigma$ the peak tensile stress and $a$ the crack length. For $a < 0$ the formula takes the convention $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$, so $K_I(a) = 0$.
