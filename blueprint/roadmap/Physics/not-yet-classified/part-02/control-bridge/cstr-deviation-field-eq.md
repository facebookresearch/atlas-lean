# Exact deviation form of the CSTR balance

Let $V > 0$, $Q > 0$, $k \ge 0$, and let $(c_{ss}, c_{in,ss})$ be an equilibrium: $(Q/V)(c_{in,ss} - c_{ss}) - k c_{ss} = 0$. Then for every state deviation $x$ and inlet deviation $u$,

$$\frac{Q}{V}\big((c_{in,ss} + u) - (c_{ss} + x)\big) - k(c_{ss} + x) = -\Big(\frac{Q}{V} + k\Big)x + \frac{Q}{V}u,$$

the field of the one-state linear system with state coefficient $-(Q/V + k)$ and input gain $Q/V$, with no remainder term.
