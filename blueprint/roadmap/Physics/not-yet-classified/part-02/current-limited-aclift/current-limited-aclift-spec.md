# Properties of the voltage lift for current-limited AC OPF

Fix a current-limited AC optimal power flow instance on $n$ buses and $m$ branches with incidence matrix $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$, branch admittances $y_e$, shunt admittances $y^s_k$ and bus admittance matrix $Y$. For a voltage vector $V \in \mathbb{C}^n$, the bus current is $I_k = \sum_e B_{ke}\, y_e (B^\top V)_e + y^s_k V_k$ and the complex power supplied by bus $k$ is $S_k = V_k \overline{I_k}$. $V$ is *AC feasible* if, for every bus $k$, $P^{\min}_k \le \operatorname{Re} S_k + P^d_k \le P^{\max}_k$, $Q^{\min}_k \le \operatorname{Im} S_k + Q^d_k \le Q^{\max}_k$ and $v^{\min}_k \le |V_k|^2 \le v^{\max}_k$, and $|y_e (B^\top V)_e|^2 \le i^{\max}_e$ for every branch $e$; its cost is $\sum_k c_k (\operatorname{Re} S_k + P^d_k)$.

Write $\langle M, W \rangle = \operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr}(M W)$ and let $R_k$ be the matrix holding row $k$ of $Y$ and zeros elsewhere. The coefficient matrices are $M^P_k = \tfrac12 (R_k^* + R_k)$, $M^Q_k = \tfrac{1}{2i} (R_k^* - R_k)$, $M^V_k = e_k e_k^\top$ and $M^I_e = |y_e|^2\, b_e b_e^\top$ with $b_e$ column $e$ of $B$. A matrix $W \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$ is *relaxation feasible* if it is positive semidefinite and satisfies the same constraints with $\operatorname{Re} S_k$, $\operatorname{Im} S_k$, $|V_k|^2$ and the squared branch currents replaced by $\langle M^P_k, W\rangle$, $\langle M^Q_k, W\rangle$, $\langle M^V_k, W\rangle$ and $\langle M^I_e, W\rangle$; its cost is $\sum_k c_k (\langle M^P_k, W\rangle + P^d_k)$. No rank condition is imposed. The lift of $V$ is $W = V V^*$.

Then: every lift $V V^*$ is positive semidefinite; for every matrix $M$ the map $W \mapsto \langle M, W\rangle$ is real-linear; $M^P_k$, $M^Q_k$, $M^V_k$ and $M^I_e$ are Hermitian for every bus $k$ and branch $e$; $S_k = \sum_j \overline{Y_{kj}}\, W_{kj}$ at $W = V V^*$; $V$ is AC feasible if and only if $V V^*$ is relaxation feasible; the cost of $V V^*$ equals the cost of $V$; and every lower bound on the cost of all relaxation-feasible matrices is a lower bound on the cost of all AC-feasible vectors.
