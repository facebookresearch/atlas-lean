# Closed-form plane-slider pressure solves the Reynolds problem

Let $h_0, h_L > 0$ with $h_0 \ne h_L$, let $L > 0$, and let $h(x)$ be the linear film thickness from $h_0$ at $x = 0$ to $h_L$ at $x = L$. Then

$$p(x) = \frac{6\mu U L\,(h_0 - h)(h - h_L)}{(h_0^2 - h_L^2)\,h^2}, \qquad p'(x) = \frac{6\mu U\,\bigl(h - 2h_0h_L/(h_0 + h_L)\bigr)}{h^3}$$

satisfy, on $[0, L]$: $p'$ is the derivative of $p$, $(h^3 p')' = 6\mu U (h_L - h_0)/L$, and $p(0) = p(L) = 0$.
