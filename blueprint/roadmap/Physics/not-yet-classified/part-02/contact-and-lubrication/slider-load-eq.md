# Closed-form load of a plane slider bearing

For end film thicknesses $h_0 > 0$, $h_L > 0$ with $h_0 \ne h_L$ and length $L > 0$, the load per unit width, the integral over $[0, L]$ of the slider pressure with viscosity $\mu$ and sliding speed $U$, is $W = \dfrac{6 \mu U L^2}{(h_0 - h_L)^2}\left(\log\dfrac{h_0}{h_L} - \dfrac{2 (h_0 - h_L)}{h_0 + h_L}\right)$.
