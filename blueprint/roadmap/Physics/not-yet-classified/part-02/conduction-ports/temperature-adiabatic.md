# Temperature profile of a fin with an adiabatic tip

Let $k, A, h, P, L > 0$ and let $T, q$ satisfy the steady one-dimensional fin equations on $[0, L]$: Fourier's law $T' = -q/(kA)$ and the lateral convection balance $q' = -hP(T - T_a)$, with $q$ the conducted heat rate in the $+x$ direction. Write $m = \sqrt{hP/(kA)}$ and $\theta = T - T_a$. If the tip is adiabatic, $q(L) = 0$, then $\theta(x) = \theta(0)\cosh(m(L - x))/\cosh(mL)$ for every $x \in [0, L]$.
