# Fin with an adiabatic tip and hot base conducts heat into the fin

Let $k, A, h, P, L > 0$ and let $T, q$ satisfy the steady one-dimensional fin equations on $[0, L]$: $T' = -q/(kA)$ and $q' = -hP(T - T_a)$, with $q$ the conducted heat rate in the $+x$ direction. If the tip is adiabatic, $q(L) = 0$, and the base is hotter than the fluid, $T(0) > T_a$, then $q(0) > 0$.
