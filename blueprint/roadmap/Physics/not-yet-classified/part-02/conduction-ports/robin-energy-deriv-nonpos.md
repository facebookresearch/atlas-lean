# Robin energy derivative is nonpositive

For real $D \ge 0$, $L \ge 0$, $k > 0$, $h_0 \ge 0$, $h_L \ge 0$, any $T_a$ and $t$, and any functions $u$, $u_x$,

$$-D \int_0^L u_x(t, x)^2 \, dx - \frac{D}{k}\Big(h_0 \big(u(t, 0) - T_a\big)^2 + h_L \big(u(t, L) - T_a\big)^2\Big) \le 0.$$

This is the sign of the heat-energy derivative under Robin boundary conditions.
