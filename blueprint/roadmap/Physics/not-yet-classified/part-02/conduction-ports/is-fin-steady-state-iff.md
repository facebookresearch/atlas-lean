# Steady fin equations as cable equations

For real $k$, $A$, $h$, $P$, $T_a$, $L$ and functions $T$, $q$ on $[0, L]$, the steady fin equations $T' = -q/(kA)$ and $q' = -hP(T - T_a)$ (derivatives within $[0, L]$) hold exactly when $\theta = T - T_a$ and $q$ satisfy the steady cable equations $\theta' = -r_i q$ and $q' = -\theta/r_m$ on $[0, L]$, with $r_m = 1/(hP)$ and $r_i = 1/(kA)$, where $x/0 = 0$ (so $q/(kA) = r_i = 0$ when $kA = 0$, and $r_m = \theta/r_m = 0$ when $hP = 0$).
