# Fin Parameter

For heat-transfer coefficient $h$, perimeter $P$, conductivity $k$ and cross-sectional area $A$, the fin parameter (an inverse length) is $m = \sqrt{hP/(kA)}$, with the value $0$ when $kA = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$, and also when $hP/(kA) < 0$, since the real square root is $0$ on negative numbers.
