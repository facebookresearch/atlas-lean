# Heat rate of a fin with a convective tip

Let $k, A, h, P, L > 0$ and let $T, q$ satisfy the steady one-dimensional fin equations on $[0, L]$: Fourier's law $T' = -q/(kA)$ and the lateral convection balance $q' = -hP(T - T_a)$, with $q$ the conducted heat rate in the $+x$ direction. Write $m = \sqrt{hP/(kA)}$ and $\theta = T - T_a$. If the tip loses heat by convection, $q(L) = hA\,\theta(L)$, then the heat rate at the base is $q(0) = \sqrt{hPkA}\,\theta(0)\,\dfrac{\sinh(mL) + (h/(mk))\cosh(mL)}{\cosh(mL) + (h/(mk))\sinh(mL)}$.
