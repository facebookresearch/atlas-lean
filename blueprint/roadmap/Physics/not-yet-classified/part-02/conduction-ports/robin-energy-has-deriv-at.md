# Energy identity for the heat equation with Robin boundary conditions

Let $k > 0$, $\theta = u - T_a$ and $E(\tau) = \int_0^L \theta(\tau, x)^2/2\,dx$. Suppose that at time $t$, $E'(t) = -D\int_0^L u_x(t, x)^2\,dx + D\big(\theta(t, L)u_x(t, L) - \theta(t, 0)u_x(t, 0)\big)$, and the Robin conditions $k u_x(t, 0) = h_0\theta(t, 0)$ and $-k u_x(t, L) = h_L\theta(t, L)$ hold. Then $E'(t) = -D\int_0^L u_x(t, x)^2\,dx - \frac{D}{k}\big(h_0\theta(t, 0)^2 + h_L\theta(t, L)^2\big)$.
