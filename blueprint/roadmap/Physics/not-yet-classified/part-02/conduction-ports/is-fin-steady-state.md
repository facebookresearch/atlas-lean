# Steady one-dimensional fin

For conductivity $k$, cross-sectional area $A$, convection coefficient $h$, perimeter $P$, ambient temperature $T_a$ and length $L$, a temperature $T$ and conducted heat rate $q$ (in the $+x$ direction) form a steady fin state on $[0, L]$ when, for every $x \in [0, L]$, with derivatives taken within $[0, L]$, Fourier's law $T'(x) = -q(x)/(kA)$ holds, with the value $0$ when $kA = 0$, by the convention $a/0 = 0$, and the lateral convection balance $q'(x) = -hP\,(T(x) - T_a)$ holds.
