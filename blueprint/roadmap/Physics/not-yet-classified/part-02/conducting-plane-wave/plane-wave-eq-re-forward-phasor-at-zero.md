# Lossless plane wave as the real part of the forward phasor

Let $\varepsilon > 0$, $\mu > 0$, $\omega > 0$, and at zero conductivity let $\beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon/2}\,\sqrt{2}$ be the phase constant and $\gamma = \alpha + i\beta$ the propagation constant (with attenuation constant $\alpha$ at $\sigma = 0$). Then for all real $E_0$, $x$, $t$,

$$E_0\cos(\beta x - \omega t) = \operatorname{Re}\left(e^{i\omega t}\,e^{-\gamma x}\,E_0\right).$$
