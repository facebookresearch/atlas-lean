# Skin depth

For real $\varepsilon, \mu, \omega, \sigma$ the skin depth is $\delta = 1/\alpha$, where

$$\alpha = \omega\sqrt{\tfrac{\mu\varepsilon}{2}}\sqrt{\sqrt{1 + \left(\tfrac{\sigma}{\omega\varepsilon}\right)^2} - 1}$$

is the attenuation constant, with the conventions $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$ and $x/0 = 0$ (so $\alpha = 0$ when $\mu\varepsilon \le 0$ or $\omega = 0$). When $\alpha = 0$ (for example at $\sigma = 0$) the convention $1/0 = 0$ gives $\delta = 0$.
