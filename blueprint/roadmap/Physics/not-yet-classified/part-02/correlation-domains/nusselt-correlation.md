# Nusselt correlation record

A Nusselt correlation consists of

- a citation (a string);
- the flow configuration, hydrodynamic development, thermal development, property-variation class, local or surface-averaged tag, and property-evaluation temperature it was calibrated with;
- a validity domain: a set of flow conditions (Reynolds number $Re$, Prandtl number $Pr$, length ratio $L/D$, temperature difference $T_s - T_m$, viscosity ratio $\mu_m/\mu_s$);
- a proof that every flow condition in the domain has $Re > 0$ and $Pr > 0$;
- a proof that the domain is nonempty;
- a formula assigning a real Nusselt number to each flow condition in the domain.
