# Loaded DC gain as a scaled finite-gain closed-loop gain

For all real $A_0, \beta, a, b$, the loaded DC gain

$$G_0 = \frac{a\, b\, A_0}{1 + \beta\, b\, A_0}$$

equals $a$ times the closed-loop gain $\dfrac{A}{1 + A\beta}$ of the open-loop gain $A = b A_0$ with feedback factor $\beta$:

$$G_0 = a \cdot \frac{b A_0}{1 + b A_0\, \beta}.$$

Both sides use the convention $x/0 = 0$ when $1 + \beta b A_0 = 0$.
