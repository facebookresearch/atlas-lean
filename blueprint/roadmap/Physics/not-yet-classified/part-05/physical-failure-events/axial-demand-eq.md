# Axial demand of a fixed-fixed bar under uniform load

Let $\Phi$ be a yield functional on $3\times 3$ stress matrices that is calibrated by the tensile test: $\Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma,0,0)) = |\sigma|$ for every real $\sigma$. Let $EA > 0$ be the axial rigidity and $A_{cs} > 0$ the cross-section area, and let $u(x) = q\,x\,(L-x)/(2\,EA)$ be the closed-form displacement under the uniform load $q$, with axial stress $\sigma(x) = EA\,u'(x)/A_{cs}$. Then for all real $L$, $q$, $x$,

$$\Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma(x),0,0)) = \frac{|q\,(L-2x)|}{2A_{cs}}.$$
