# Cantilever polynomial satisfies the beam equation

Let $EI \neq 0$ and $L, q, P \in \mathbb{R}$, and let

$$w(x) = \frac{q\,x^2\,(6L^2 - 4Lx + x^2)}{24\,EI} + \frac{P\,x^2\,(3L - x)}{6\,EI}$$

be the cantilever deflection under uniform load $q$ and tip force $P$. Then $EI\,w''''(x) = q$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
