# Internal axial force of a fixed-traction bar

Let $k, L, P \in \mathbb{R}$ and $f, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, and suppose $u$ solves the fixed-traction problem with rigidity $k$, distributed load $f$ and end force $P$: $u$ is $C^2$ on $[0, L]$, $-k\,u''(x) = f(x)$ for every $x \in [0, L]$, $u(0) = 0$ and $k\,u'(L) = P$, all derivatives taken within $[0, L]$. Then for every $x \in [0, L]$,

$$k\,u'(x) = P + \int_x^L f(y)\,dy.$$
