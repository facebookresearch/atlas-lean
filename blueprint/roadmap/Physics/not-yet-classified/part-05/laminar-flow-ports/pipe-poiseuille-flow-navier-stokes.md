# Cylindrical Poiseuille flow solves the Navier-Stokes equations

For $\mu \ne 0$ and any $\rho$, $\lambda$, $p_0$, $G$, $R$, cylindrical Poiseuille flow satisfies the conservative compressible Navier-Stokes equations on all of space with pressure $p_0 - Gz$, shear viscosity $\mu$, second viscosity $\lambda$ and no body force.
