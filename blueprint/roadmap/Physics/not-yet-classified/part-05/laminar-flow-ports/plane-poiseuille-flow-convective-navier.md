# Plane Poiseuille flow satisfies the convective Navier-Stokes equations

For $\mu \ne 0$ and all real $\rho, \lambda, p_0, G, H$, plane Poiseuille flow with velocity $(G y (H - y)/(2\mu), 0, 0)$, constant density $\rho$ and no body force satisfies the convective Navier-Stokes equations in three dimensions with pressure $p_0 - G x$, shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$.
