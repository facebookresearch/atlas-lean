# Plane Poiseuille flow

For density $\rho$, shear viscosity $\mu$, second viscosity $\lambda$, reference pressure $p_0$, pressure-drop rate $G$ and channel width $H$, plane Poiseuille flow is defined as the flow with constant density $\rho$, steady velocity $u = \big(G y(H - y)/(2\mu), 0, 0\big)$ where $y$ is the wall-normal coordinate, Newtonian stress built from the pressure $p = p_0 - Gx$ (with $x$ the streamwise coordinate) and viscosities $\mu$, $\lambda$, and zero body force.
