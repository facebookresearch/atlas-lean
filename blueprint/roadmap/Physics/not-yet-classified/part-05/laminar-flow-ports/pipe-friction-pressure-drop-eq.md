# Laminar Darcy-Weisbach pressure drop in a pipe

Let $u_z(x, y) = G\,(R^2 - x^2 - y^2)/(4\mu)$ be the axial velocity of cylindrical Poiseuille flow and $Q = \int_{x^2 + y^2 \le R^2} u_z\,dx\,dy$ the signed flow rate through a cross-section. Let $V = Q/(\pi R^2)$, $D = 2R$, $A = \pi R^2$, $\mathrm{Re} = \rho\,|V|\,D/\mu$, and for a Darcy factor $f$ let $c(f) = f L \rho/(2 D A^2)$. If $\rho > 0$, $\mu > 0$, $R > 0$, $L > 0$ and $G ≠ 0$, then

$$c\Big(\frac{64}{\mathrm{Re}}\Big)\,Q\,|Q| = G L, \qquad c\Big(\frac{16}{\mathrm{Re}}\Big)\,Q\,|Q| = \frac{G L}{4}.$$
