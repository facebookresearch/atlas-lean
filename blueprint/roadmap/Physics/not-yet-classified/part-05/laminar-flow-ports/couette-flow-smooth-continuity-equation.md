# Plane Couette flow satisfies the continuity equation

For all real $\rho, \mu, \lambda, p_0, U, H$, plane Couette flow with constant density $\rho$ and wall speed $U$ across gap $H$ satisfies the smooth continuity equation in three dimensions: the density is differentiable in time, the mass flux is differentiable in space, and $\partial_t \rho + \nabla\cdot(\rho u) = 0$.
