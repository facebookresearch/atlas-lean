# Couette flow stress

For plane Couette flow with constant density $\rho$, velocity $(U y / H, 0, 0)$, constant pressure $p_0$, shear viscosity $\mu$, second viscosity $\lambda$ and no body force, the Newtonian stress at every time and point is

$$\begin{pmatrix} -p_0 & \mu U/H & 0 \\ \mu U/H & -p_0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -p_0 \end{pmatrix};$$

the second viscosity drops out. When $H = 0$ the entries $\mu U/H$ (and the velocity) are $0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
