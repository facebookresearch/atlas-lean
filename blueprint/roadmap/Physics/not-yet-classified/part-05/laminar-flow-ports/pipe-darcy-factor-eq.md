# Laminar Darcy factor

For $\rho, \mu, R, L > 0$ and a nonzero pressure-drop rate $G \ne 0$ (flow in either direction), consider cylindrical Poiseuille flow in a pipe of radius $R$ with signed mean velocity $V$ (flow rate divided by $\pi R^2$). The Darcy factor $f = 2 D \Delta p / (\rho V |V| L)$ of the pressure drop $\Delta p = G L$ over the length $L$ on the diameter $D = 2R$ equals $64/\mathrm{Re}$, where $\mathrm{Re} = \rho |V| D / \mu$.
