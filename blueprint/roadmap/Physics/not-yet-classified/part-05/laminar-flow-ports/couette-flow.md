# Plane Couette flow

For constants $\rho$, $\mu$, $\lambda$, $p_0$, $U_{\text{wall}}$ and $H$, plane Couette flow is the flow with constant density $\rho$, steady velocity $(U_{\text{wall}}\,y/H, 0, 0)$ (zero when $H = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$) where $y$ is the wall-normal coordinate, the Newtonian stress for constant pressure $p_0$, shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$, and no body force.
