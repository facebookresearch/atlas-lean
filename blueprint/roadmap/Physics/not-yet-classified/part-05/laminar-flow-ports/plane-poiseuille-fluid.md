# Plane Poiseuille fluid

For constants $\rho$, $G$, $\mu$, $H$, the plane Poiseuille fluid has constant density $\rho$ and steady velocity $\big(G y (H - y)/(2\mu), 0, 0\big)$, with the value $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $a/0 = 0$, where $y$ is the wall-normal coordinate and $G = -\partial p/\partial x$ is the signed pressure-drop rate.
