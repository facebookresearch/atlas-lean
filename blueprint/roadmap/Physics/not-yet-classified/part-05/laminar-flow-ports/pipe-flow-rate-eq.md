# Hagen-Poiseuille flow rate

Let $u_z(x, y) = G\,(R^2 - x^2 - y^2)/(4\mu)$ be the axial velocity of cylindrical Poiseuille flow and $Q = \int_{x^2 + y^2 \le R^2} u_z\,dx\,dy$ the signed flow rate through a cross-section. If $\mu > 0$ and $R > 0$, then for every $G$ and every cross-section

$$Q = \frac{\pi G R^4}{8\mu}.$$
