# Plane Poiseuille flow satisfies the continuity equation

For all constants $\rho$, $\mu$, $\lambda$, $p_0$, $G$, $H$, plane Poiseuille flow with constant density $\rho$ and velocity $(G y(H - y)/(2\mu), 0, 0)$ satisfies the smooth continuity equation: the density is differentiable in time, the mass flux is differentiable in space, and $\partial_t\rho + \nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf u) = 0$.
