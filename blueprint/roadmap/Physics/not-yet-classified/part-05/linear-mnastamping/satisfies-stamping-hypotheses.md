# Stamping hypotheses

For branch sets $\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{L}, \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{S} \subseteq \{1, \dots, n_B\}$ and values $R, L, C : \{1, \dots, n_B\} \to \mathbb{R}$, the stamping hypotheses hold when the four sets are pairwise disjoint with union all branches, $R_e > 0$ for every $e \in \mathcal{R}$, $C_e > 0$ for every $e \in \mathcal{C}$, and $L_e > 0$ for every $e \in \mathcal{L}$.
