# Turning fractions

On a finite set of links, let $q_c(e, e') \ge 0$ be the turning flows of commodity $c$ from link $e$ to each $e'$, let $Q_c(e) = \sum_{e'} q_c(e, e')$ be the outflow of $c$ from $e$, and let the turning fraction be $q_c(e, e')/Q_c(e)$ (taken as $0$ when $Q_c(e) = 0$). Then each turning flow equals its turning fraction times $Q_c(e)$.
