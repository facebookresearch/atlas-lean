# Dissipation is invariant under relabeling of nodes

Let $m$, $p$ and $\alpha$ be finite index types and $e : \alpha \to m \sqcup p$ a bijection. Let $G$ be a real matrix indexed by $m \sqcup p$ and $\varphi, j \in \mathbb{R}^{m \sqcup p}$ with $G\varphi = j$. Define the relabeled potential $\varphi'(a) = \varphi(e(a))$ and matrix $G'_{ab} = G_{e(a)\,e(b)}$ for $a, b \in \alpha$. Then

$$\varphi' \cdot (G'\varphi') = \varphi \cdot j.$$
