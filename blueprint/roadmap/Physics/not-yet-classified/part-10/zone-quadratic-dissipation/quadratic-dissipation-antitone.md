# Quadratic dissipation is nonincreasing

Over a finite set of zones, let $C_i$ be real, $G_{ij} = G_{ji} \ge 0$ and $g_i \ge 0$, and let differentiable temperatures satisfy $C_i T_i' = \sum_j G_{ij}(T_j - T_i) + g_i (T_{out} - T_i)$. Then $s \mapsto \frac12 \sum_i C_i (T_i(s) - T_{out})^2$ is nonincreasing.
