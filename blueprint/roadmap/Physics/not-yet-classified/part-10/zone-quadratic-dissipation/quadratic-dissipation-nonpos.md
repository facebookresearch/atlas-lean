# Quadratic dissipation is nonpositive

On a finite set of zones with capacities $C_i$, symmetric conductances $G_{ij} = G_{ji} \ge 0$, outdoor conductances $g_i \ge 0$ and outdoor temperature $T_{\text{out}}$, let the temperatures satisfy $C_i T_i' = \sum_j G_{ij}(T_j - T_i) + g_i(T_{\text{out}} - T_i)$. Then $\frac12 \sum_i C_i (T_i - T_{\text{out}})^2$ has derivative $-\left(\frac12 \sum_{i,j} G_{ij}(T_j - T_i)^2 + \sum_i g_i (T_i - T_{\text{out}})^2\right) \le 0$.
