# Thevenin form of a one-port law

Let $K > 0$ and $j_{\mathrm{eff}} \in \mathbb{R}$, and set $V_{th} = j_{\mathrm{eff}}/K$ and $R_{th} = 1/K$. Then:

1. $R_{th} > 0$;
2. for all $V, I$: $K V = j_{\mathrm{eff}} - I \iff V = V_{th} - R_{th} I$;
3. for all $V$: if $K V = j_{\mathrm{eff}} - 0$ then $V = V_{th}$ (open circuit);
4. for all $R_L \ge 0$ and all $V, I$: if $K V = j_{\mathrm{eff}} - I$ and $V = R_L I$, then

    $$V = \frac{V_{th}\, R_L}{R_{th} + R_L}.$$
