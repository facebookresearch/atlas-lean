# Hover power

Consider an ideal actuator disk with density $\rho > 0$, area $A > 0$, axial speeds $U_\infty$ far upstream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake, satisfying the port laws (Bernoulli upstream and downstream of the disk, the disk momentum balance and the streamtube momentum balance $F = \rho A U_d (U_\infty - U_w)$, where $F$ is the axial force of the fluid on the disk). Suppose the disk hovers in still air, $U_\infty = 0$, with thrust $T = -F > 0$ and $T U_d > 0$. Then the induced power $P = \rho A U_d (U_w^2 - U_\infty^2)/2$ satisfies

$$P = T U_d = -F U_d = T \sqrt{T/(2\rho A)}.$$
