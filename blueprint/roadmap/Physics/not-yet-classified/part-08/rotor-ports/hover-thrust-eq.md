# Hover thrust

For an actuator disk of area $A > 0$ in fluid of density $\rho > 0$ obeying the momentum-theory port laws (Bernoulli upstream and downstream of the disk, force equal to area times the pressure jump, and force equal to mass flow $\rho A u_d$ times the loss of axial speed), in still air (far-upstream speed $0$), if the thrust $T = -F$ is positive, where $F$ is the force of the fluid on the disk, then $T = 2 \rho A u_d^2$, with $u_d$ the axial speed at the disk.
