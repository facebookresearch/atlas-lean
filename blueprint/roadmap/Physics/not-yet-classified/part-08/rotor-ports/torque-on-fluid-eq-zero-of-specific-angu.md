# Equal specific angular momentum gives zero rotor torque

A rotor turns at signed angular speed $\omega$ about the shaft axis $e_3$ through the point $o$, and forces $F_k$ ($k$ in a finite index set) act on the fluid at points $x_k$. Their axial torque and power are

$$\tau = e_3 \cdot \sum_k (x_k - o) \times F_k, \qquad P = \sum_k F_k \cdot \bigl(\omega\, e_3 \times (x_k - o)\bigr).$$

An inlet port and an outlet port each have density $\rho \ge 0$, area $A \ge 0$, pressure $p$, a representative point $x$ at radius $r \ge 0$, inertial velocity $c$ with swirl component $c_\theta$, and outward unit normal $n$ in the meridional plane; the port's lumped mass flow is $\rho A\,(c \cdot n)$ and its axial angular-momentum flux is $e_3 \cdot \bigl((x - o) \times (\rho A (c \cdot n)\, c + pA\, n)\bigr)$.

Assume the inlet and outlet mass flows are $-\dot m$ and $\dot m$, the axial torque $\tau$ equals the sum of the two ports' axial angular-momentum fluxes, $r_1 c_{\theta 1} = r_2 c_{\theta 2}$, and the shaft torque $\tau_{\mathrm{shaft}}$ and reaction torque $\tau_{\mathrm{reaction}}$ satisfy $\tau_{\mathrm{shaft}} + \tau_{\mathrm{reaction}} = 0$ and $\tau_{\mathrm{reaction}} = -\tau$. Then

$$\tau = 0, \qquad P = 0, \qquad -\omega\, \tau_{\mathrm{shaft}} = 0,$$

the last quantity being the shaft power out.
