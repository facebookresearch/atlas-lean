# Shaft power out is the negative of the power on the fluid

Let forces $F_k$ act on the fluid at points $x_k$ of a rotor turning at angular speed $\omega$ about the shaft axis through $o$, with axial torque $\tau$. Assume the inlet and outlet lumped mass flows are $-\dot m$ and $\dot m$, the axial torque equals the sum of the inlet and outlet axial moment fluxes, $\tau_{shaft} + \tau_{reaction} = 0$ and $\tau_{reaction} = -\tau$. Then $\tau_{shaft} = \tau$, the shaft work out $-\omega\tau_{shaft}$ equals minus the power of the forces on the fluid, and

$$-\omega \tau_{shaft} = \dot m\,(\omega r_1 c_{\theta,1} - \omega r_2 c_{\theta,2}),$$

with $r$ and $c_\theta$ the port radii and swirl velocities.
