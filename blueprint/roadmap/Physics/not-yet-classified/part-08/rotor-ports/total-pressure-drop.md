# Total pressure drops across an extracting actuator disk

Let an actuator disk state $(\rho, A, U_\infty, U_d, U_w, p_\infty, p^+, p^-, T)$ satisfy the port laws (Bernoulli upstream and downstream of the disk, and the disk and streamtube momentum balances), with $\rho > 0$ and $0 \le U_w < U_\infty$. Then

$$\Bigl(p^+ + \frac{\rho U_d^2}{2}\Bigr) - \Bigl(p^- + \frac{\rho U_d^2}{2}\Bigr) = \frac{\rho\,(U_\infty^2 - U_w^2)}{2}$$

and

$$p^- + \frac{\rho U_d^2}{2} < p^+ + \frac{\rho U_d^2}{2}.$$
