# Power-angle formulas of a synchronous generator

Let $E > 0$, $V > 0$, $X_s > 0$ and $\delta \in \mathbb{R}$. Let the generator current be

$$I = \frac{E\, e^{i\delta} - V}{i\, X_s},$$

and let $S = 3\, V\, \overline{I}$ be the three-phase complex power at the terminals (RMS phasors, terminal voltage $V$ real). Then

$$\operatorname{Re} S = \frac{3\, V E \sin\delta}{X_s}, \qquad \operatorname{Im} S = \frac{3\, V\,(E\cos\delta - V)}{X_s}.$$
