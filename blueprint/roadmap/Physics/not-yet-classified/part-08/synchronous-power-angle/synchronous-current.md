# Synchronous generator current

For real $E$ (internal voltage magnitude), $V$ (terminal voltage), $X_s$ (synchronous reactance) and $\delta$ (load angle), the generator current, directed out toward the bus, is the complex phasor

$$I = \frac{E\, e^{i\delta} - V}{i\, X_s}.$$

For $X_s = 0$ the formula takes the convention $z/0 = 0$, giving $I = 0$.
