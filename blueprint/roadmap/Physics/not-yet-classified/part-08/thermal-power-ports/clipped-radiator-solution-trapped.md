# Solutions of the clipped radiator field stay in the box

Let $C > 0$, $K, Q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $T_{\min} \le T_{\max}$, with the inward sign conditions

$$Q - K T_{\min}^4 \ge 0, \qquad Q - K T_{\max}^4 \le 0.$$

Let $r(T) = (Q - K T^4)/C$ and let the clipped rate be $\tilde r(T) = r\big(\min(\max(T, T_{\min}), T_{\max})\big)$.

If $T : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ has, at every $s \ge 0$, derivative $\tilde r(T(s))$ within $[0, \infty)$, and $T(0) \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$, then $T(t) \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$ for every $t \ge 0$.
