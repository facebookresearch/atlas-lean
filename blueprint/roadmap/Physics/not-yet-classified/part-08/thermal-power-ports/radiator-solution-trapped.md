# Forward radiator solutions stay in the box

Let $C > 0$, $K > 0$ and $0 \le T_{\min} \le T_{\max}$, and let $Q$ satisfy the inward sign conditions $Q - K T_{\min}^4 \ge 0$ and $Q - K T_{\max}^4 \le 0$. Let $r(T) = (Q - K T^4)/C$. If $T : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ has, at every $s \ge 0$, derivative $r(T(s))$ within $[0, \infty)$, and $T(0) \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$, then $T(t) \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$ for every $t \ge 0$.
