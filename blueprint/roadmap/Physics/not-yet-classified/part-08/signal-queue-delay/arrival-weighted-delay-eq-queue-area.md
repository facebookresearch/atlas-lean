# Occupancy identity for the signal cycle

Let $0 < \lambda < \mu$, $R \ge 0$, $G \ge 0$ and $\lambda(R + G) < \mu G$, and let $Q(t)$ be the deterministic queue length ($\lambda t$ for $t \le R$, then $\max\{0, \lambda R - (\mu - \lambda)(t - R)\}$). Integrating the first-in-first-out delay of the vehicle arriving at time $s$ over $s \in [0, R + G)$ against the arrival rate $\lambda\,ds$ gives the queue area $\int_0^{R+G} Q(t)\,dt$.
