# Single-mode shear flow solves the Navier-Stokes equations

For $\rho > 0$, $\nu = \mu/\rho$ and any amplitude $A$, length $L$, second viscosity $\lambda$ and spatially uniform pressure $p(t)$, the parallel shear flow with constant density $\rho$, velocity $(U(t, y), 0, 0)$ where $U(t, y) = A y/L - (A/\pi) \sin(\pi y/L)\, e^{-\nu (\pi/L)^2 t}$ (with $y/L$ and $\pi/L$ read as $0$ when $L = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$), Newtonian stress and no body force satisfies the Navier-Stokes equations in three dimensions.
