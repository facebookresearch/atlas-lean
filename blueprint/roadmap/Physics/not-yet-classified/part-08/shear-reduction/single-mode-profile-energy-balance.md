# Energy balance of the single-mode shear flow

Let $\rho > 0$, $L > 0$, $A, \mu, t \in \mathbb{R}$, $\nu = \mu/\rho$, $s = e^{-\nu(\pi/L)^2 t}$ and $U(t,y) = Ay/L - (A/\pi)\sin(\pi y/L)\,e^{-\nu(\pi/L)^2 t}$. Then the dissipation $\mu\int_0^L (\partial_y U)^2\,dy$ equals $\mu A^2(1 + s^2/2)/L$, the wall work $\mu\,\partial_y U(t,L)\,A$ equals $\mu A^2(1 + s)/L$, and the kinetic energy $\int_0^L \rho U^2/2\,dy$ has time derivative $\mu A^2 s(2 - s)/(2L)$.
