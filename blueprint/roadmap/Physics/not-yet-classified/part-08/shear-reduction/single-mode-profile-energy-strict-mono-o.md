# Kinetic energy of the single-mode shear flow increases

For $\rho, \mu, L > 0$ and $A \ne 0$, let $\nu = \mu/\rho$ and $U(t, y) = A y/L - (A/\pi)\sin(\pi y/L)\,e^{-\nu(\pi/L)^2 t}$. Then the kinetic energy per unit wall area $K(t) = \int_0^L \rho\,U(t, y)^2/2\,dy$ is strictly increasing on $t \ge 0$.
