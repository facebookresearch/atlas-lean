# Stress of a parallel shear flow

Let $U(t, y)$ be jointly $C^2$, and consider the parallel shear flow with velocity $(U(t, y), 0, 0)$, uniform pressure $p(t)$ and Newtonian stress with shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$. Then at every time $t$ and point $x$ the stress is

$$\begin{pmatrix} -p(t) & \mu U_y & 0 \\ \mu U_y & -p(t) & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -p(t) \end{pmatrix},$$

with $U_y = \partial_y U(t, y)$; the second viscosity does not appear.
