# Shear flow reduces Navier-Stokes to the heat equation

Let $\rho > 0$ and let $U(t, y)$ be jointly $C^2$. The parallel shear flow with constant density $\rho$, velocity $(U(t, y), 0, 0)$, uniform pressure $p(t)$, shear viscosity $\mu$, second viscosity $\lambda$ and no body force satisfies the Navier-Stokes equations if and only if $U_t = (\mu/\rho)\,U_{yy}$ at every time and height.
