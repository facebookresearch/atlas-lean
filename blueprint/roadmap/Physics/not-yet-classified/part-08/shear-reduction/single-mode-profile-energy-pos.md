# Single-mode shear flow has positive kinetic energy

Let $\rho > 0$, $\mu > 0$, $L > 0$, $A \ne 0$ and $t \ge 0$, and let $\nu = \mu/\rho$. For the single-mode profile

$$U(t, y) = \frac{A y}{L} - \frac{A}{\pi}\sin\frac{\pi y}{L}\,e^{-\nu (\pi/L)^2 t},$$

the kinetic energy per unit wall area $\int_0^L \rho\,U(t, y)^2/2\,dy$ is positive.
