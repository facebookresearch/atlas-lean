# Signal queue clears by the end of the cycle

Let the arrival rate be $\lambda > 0$, the discharge rate $\mu > \lambda$, the red time $R \ge 0$ and the green time $G \ge 0$, and let the queue length be $Q(t) = \lambda t$ for $t \le R$ and $Q(t) = \max(0, \lambda R - (\mu - \lambda)(t - R))$ for $t > R$. If $\lambda (R + G) \le \mu G$, then $Q(R + G) = 0$.
