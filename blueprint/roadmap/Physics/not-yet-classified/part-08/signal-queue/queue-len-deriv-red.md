# Queue growth rate during red

For a signalized queue with arrival rate $\lambda$, discharge rate $\mu$ and red phase of length $R$, whose length is $\lambda t$ for $t \le R$ and $\max\big(0, \lambda R - (\mu - \lambda)(t - R)\big)$ afterwards, the derivative of the queue length at every $t$ with $0 < t < R$ equals $\lambda$.
