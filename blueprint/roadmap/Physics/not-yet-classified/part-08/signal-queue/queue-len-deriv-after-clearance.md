# Signal queue stays empty after clearance

Let $0 < \lambda < \mu$ and $R \ge 0$, and let the queue length be $Q(t) = \lambda t$ for $t \le R$ and $Q(t) = \max\big(0, \lambda R - (\mu - \lambda)(t - R)\big)$ for $t > R$. For every $t$ with $R + \lambda R/(\mu - \lambda) < t \le R + G$, $Q'(t) = 0$.
