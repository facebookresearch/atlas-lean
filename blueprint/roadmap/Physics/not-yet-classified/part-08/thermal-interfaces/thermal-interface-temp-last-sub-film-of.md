# Last interface temperature of a layered wall matches the outside air

Let $A > 0$, inside and outside film coefficients $h_{in}, h_{out} > 0$, and $n$ layers with thicknesses $L_i \ge 0$ and conductivities $k_i > 0$. Let

$$R_{tot} = \frac{1}{h_{in}A} + \sum_{i<n}\frac{L_i}{k_i A} + \frac{1}{h_{out}A}, \qquad Q = \frac{T_{in} - T_{out}}{R_{tot}}.$$

Then the temperature after the inside film and all $n$ layers, $T_{in} - Q\big(\frac{1}{h_{in}A} + \sum_{i<n}\frac{L_i}{k_i A}\big)$, minus the outside film drop $Q/(h_{out}A)$, equals $T_{out}$.
