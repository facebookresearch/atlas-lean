# Interface temperature in a layered wall

For area $A$, inside film coefficient $h_{in}$, inside air temperature $T_{in}$, steady heat rate $Q$, and layer thicknesses $L_i$ and conductivities $k_i$, the temperature after the inside film and the first $m$ layers is $T_m = T_{in} - Q\Big(\frac{1}{h_{in} A} + \sum_{i < m} \frac{L_i}{k_i A}\Big)$, where each quotient is $0$ when its denominator is $0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
