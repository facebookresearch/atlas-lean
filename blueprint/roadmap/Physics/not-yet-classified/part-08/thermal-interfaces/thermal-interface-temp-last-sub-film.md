# Thermal interfaces: end matching at the outside film

For $n$ planar layers of thicknesses $L_i$ and conductivities $k_i$, inside and outside film coefficients $h_{\mathrm{in}}$, $h_{\mathrm{out}}$ and common area $A$, let the total resistance $R = 1/(h_{\mathrm{in}} A) + \sum_{i<n} L_i/(k_i A) + 1/(h_{\mathrm{out}} A)$ be nonzero and set $Q = (T_{\mathrm{in}} - T_{\mathrm{out}})/R$. Then the temperature after the inside film and all $n$ layers, $T_n = T_{\mathrm{in}} - Q\big(1/(h_{\mathrm{in}} A) + \sum_{i<n} L_i/(k_i A)\big)$, satisfies $T_n - Q/(h_{\mathrm{out}} A) = T_{\mathrm{out}}$.
