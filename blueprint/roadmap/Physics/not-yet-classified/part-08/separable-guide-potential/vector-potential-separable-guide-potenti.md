# Vector potential of the separable guide potential

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $a : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $A$ be the separable guide potential, whose spacetime components are $A^y(x^0, x, y, z) = a(x)\, f(x^0/c, z)$ and $0$ otherwise. At every physical time $t$ and position $(x, y, z)$ its vector potential is

$$\mathbf{A}(t, x, y, z) = \bigl(0,\ a(x)\, f(t, z),\ 0\bigr).$$
