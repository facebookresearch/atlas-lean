# Electric field of a guide potential without time conversion

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $a : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, and $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ smooth ($C^\infty$). Let $A$ be the four-potential whose components at a spacetime point $(x^0, x^1, x^2, x^3)$ are $A^y = a(x^1)\, f(x^0, x^3)$ and $0$ otherwise, with the spacetime time coordinate $x^0$ fed directly into $f$. With spacetime read as $x^0 = c\,t$, its electric field at time $t$ and position $(x, y, z)$ is

$$\mathbf{E}(t, x, y, z) = \bigl(0,\ -c\, a(x)\, \partial_1 f(c\,t, z),\ 0\bigr),$$

where $\partial_1 f$ is the derivative of $f$ in its first argument.
