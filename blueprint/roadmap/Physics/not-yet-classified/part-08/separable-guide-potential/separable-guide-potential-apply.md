# Components of the separable guide potential

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $a : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $A$ be the separable guide potential with scalar potential $0$ and vector potential $(0, a(x) f(t, z), 0)$. At a spacetime point with coordinates $(x^0, x^1, x^2, x^3)$, its component $A^\mu$ is

$$A^\mu = \begin{cases} a(x^1)\, f(x^0 / c,\ x^3) & \text{if } \mu = 2 \ (\text{the } y\text{-component}), \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
