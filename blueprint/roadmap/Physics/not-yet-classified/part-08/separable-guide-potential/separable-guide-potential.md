# Separable guide potential

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $a : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ a longitudinal profile and $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ a time/transverse mode. The separable guide potential is the electromagnetic four-potential built from the scalar potential $\phi = 0$ and the vector potential

$$\mathbf{A}(t, x, y, z) = \bigl(0,\ a(x)\, f(t, z),\ 0\bigr),$$

where $t$ is physical time; on spacetime, whose time coordinate is $x^0 = c\,t$, its components are $A^y(x^0, x, y, z) = a(x)\, f(x^0 / c, z)$ and all other components zero.
