# Attenuation ratio at unit conduction ratio

Let $\varepsilon > 0$, $\mu > 0$, $\omega > 0$ and take $\sigma = \omega\varepsilon$, so that the conduction ratio $\rho = \omega\varepsilon/\sigma$ equals $1$. With the exact attenuation constant

$$\alpha = \omega \sqrt{\frac{\mu\varepsilon}{2}}\,\sqrt{\sqrt{1 + \Bigl(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\varepsilon}\Bigr)^2} - 1}$$

and the good-conductor attenuation $\alpha_0 = \sqrt{\omega\mu\sigma/2}$,

$$\frac{\alpha}{\alpha_0} = \sqrt{\sqrt{2} - 1}.$$
