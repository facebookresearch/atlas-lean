# Relative error of the good-conductor skin-depth approximation

Let $\varepsilon, \mu, \omega, \sigma > 0$. Let the exact attenuation constant be

$$\alpha = \omega \sqrt{\frac{\mu\varepsilon}{2}}\,\sqrt{\sqrt{1 + \Bigl(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\varepsilon}\Bigr)^2} - 1}$$

with skin depth $\delta = 1/\alpha$, and let $\alpha_0 = \sqrt{\omega\mu\sigma/2}$, $\delta_0 = 1/\alpha_0$ and $\rho = \omega\varepsilon/\sigma$. Then

$$0 \le 1 - \frac{\alpha}{\alpha_0} \le \rho;$$

if $0 \le \rho < 1$ then $\delta_0 \le \delta \le \delta_0/(1 - \rho)$; and $\delta_0 = \sqrt{2/(\omega\mu\sigma)}$.
