# Skin-depth bounds for any forward propagation root

Let $\varepsilon, \mu, \omega, \sigma > 0$ and let $\gamma \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfy $\gamma^2 = -\omega^2\mu\varepsilon + i\,\omega\mu\sigma$ with $\operatorname{Im}\gamma > 0$. Let $\alpha_0 = \sqrt{\omega\mu\sigma/2}$, $\delta_0 = 1/\alpha_0$ and $\rho = \omega\varepsilon/\sigma$. Then

$$0 \le 1 - \frac{\operatorname{Re}\gamma}{\alpha_0} \le \rho,$$

and if $0 \le \rho < 1$ then

$$\delta_0 \le \frac{1}{\operatorname{Re}\gamma} \le \frac{\delta_0}{1 - \rho}.$$
