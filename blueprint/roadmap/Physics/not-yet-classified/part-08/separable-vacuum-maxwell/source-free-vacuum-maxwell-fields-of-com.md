# Component form of the source-free Maxwell equations transfers to fields

Let $c$ be a speed of light and let $\mathbf E, \mathbf B$ be vector fields of time and space with values in $\mathbb{R}^3$ that are differentiable as functions of $(t, \mathbf x)$. Let $F_E, F_B : \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be their coordinate expressions, $\mathbf E(t, \mathbf x)_i = F_E(t, x_0, x_1, x_2)_i$ and $\mathbf B(t, \mathbf x)_i = F_B(t, x_0, x_1, x_2)_i$. Suppose that $F_E, F_B$ satisfy at every $(t, x, y, z)$ the source-free vacuum Maxwell equations at speed $c$, with divergence, curl and time derivative formed from one-variable derivatives in each coordinate. Then $\mathbf E, \mathbf B$ satisfy at every time and point

$$\operatorname{div} \mathbf E = 0,\quad \operatorname{div} \mathbf B = 0,\quad \operatorname{curl} \mathbf E = -\partial_t \mathbf B,\quad \operatorname{curl} \mathbf B = \frac{1}{c^2}\, \partial_t \mathbf E.$$
