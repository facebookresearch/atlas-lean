# Wave equation for the discharge in water hammer

Let $a, g, A > 0$, and let head $h(t, x)$ and discharge $q(t, x)$ be differentiable in each variable with $h_t + \frac{a^2}{g A} q_x = 0$ and $q_t + g A\, h_x = 0$ everywhere. Suppose $q_x$ is differentiable in $x$, $h_x$ in $t$, and $h_t$ in $x$. Then at a point $(t, x)$, $q_{tt} = a^2 q_{xx}$ holds (as the $t$-derivative of $q_t$) if and only if $\partial_t h_x = \partial_x h_t$ there.
