# Water-hammer discharge wave equation

Let $a, g, A > 0$ and let $h(t, x)$, $q(t, x)$ satisfy $h_t + \frac{a^2}{g A} q_x = 0$ and $q_t + g A\, h_x = 0$, where all first partial derivatives, $q_{xx}$, $\partial_t h_x$ and $\partial_x h_t$ exist everywhere and $\partial_t h_x = \partial_x h_t$. Then $q_{tt} = a^2 q_{xx}$ at every $(t, x)$.
