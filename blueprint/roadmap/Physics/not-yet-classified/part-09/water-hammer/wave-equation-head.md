# Water-hammer head satisfies the wave equation

Let $a, g, A > 0$. Suppose the head $h(t, x)$ and discharge $q(t, x)$ have partial derivatives $h_t$, $h_x$, $q_t$, $q_x$ everywhere and satisfy

$$h_t + \frac{a^2}{gA}\,q_x = 0, \qquad q_t + gA\,h_x = 0,$$

and that $h_x$ has an $x$-derivative $h_{xx}$, $q_x$ has a $t$-derivative $q_{xt}$, $q_t$ has an $x$-derivative $q_{tx}$, with $q_{xt} = q_{tx}$. Then the head, as a scalar field on the line, satisfies the one-dimensional wave equation $h_{tt} = a^2 h_{xx}$ at every time and point.
