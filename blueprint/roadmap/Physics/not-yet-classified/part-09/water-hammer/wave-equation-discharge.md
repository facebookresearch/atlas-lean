# Water-hammer discharge satisfies the wave equation

Let $a, g, A > 0$ and let head $h(t, x)$ and discharge $q(t, x)$ be differentiable in $t$ and in $x$, with $q_x$ differentiable in $x$, $h_x$ differentiable in $t$, $h_t$ differentiable in $x$, and $h_{xt} = h_{tx}$. If $h_t + (a^2/(gA))\,q_x = 0$ and $q_t + gA\,h_x = 0$ everywhere, then $q$ satisfies the one-dimensional wave equation $q_{tt} = a^2 q_{xx}$ with speed $a$ at every time and point.
