# Head wave equation

Let $a > 0$, $g > 0$ and $A > 0$, and let $h(t, x)$ and $q(t, x)$ have partial derivatives $h_t, h_x, q_t, q_x$ everywhere satisfying the water-hammer pair

$$h_t + \frac{a^2}{g A}\,q_x = 0, \qquad q_t + g A\,h_x = 0.$$

Suppose $h_x$ has $x$-derivative $h_{xx}$, $q_x$ has $t$-derivative $q_{xt}$, $q_t$ has $x$-derivative $q_{tx}$, and $q_{xt} = q_{tx}$ everywhere. Then $h_t$ has $t$-derivative $a^2 h_{xx}$, that is $h_{tt} = a^2 h_{xx}$.
