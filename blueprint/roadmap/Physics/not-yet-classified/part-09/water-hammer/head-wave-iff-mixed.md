# Mixed-partial criterion for the water-hammer head

Let $a, g, A > 0$ and let $h$, $q$ have partial derivatives $h_t, h_x, q_t, q_x$ everywhere satisfying $h_t + \frac{a^2}{gA} q_x = 0$ and $q_t + gA\,h_x = 0$, with $h_{xx} = \partial_x h_x$, $q_{xt} = \partial_t q_x$ and $q_{tx} = \partial_x q_t$ existing everywhere. Then at any $(t,x)$, $\partial_t h_t = a^2 h_{xx}$ if and only if $q_{xt}(t,x) = q_{tx}(t,x)$.
