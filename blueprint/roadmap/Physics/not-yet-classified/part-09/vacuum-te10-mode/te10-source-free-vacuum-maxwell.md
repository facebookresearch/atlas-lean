# TE10 fields solve the vacuum Maxwell equations

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $k_c = \pi/a$, and $f(t, z) = -(E_0/\omega) \sin(\omega t - \beta z)$. The TE10 fields are

$$E = \bigl(0,\; -\sin(k_c x)\, \partial_t f,\; 0\bigr), \qquad B = \bigl(-\sin(k_c x)\, \partial_z f,\; 0,\; k_c \cos(k_c x)\, f\bigr);$$

for $\omega \ne 0$, $E = (0, E_0 \sin(k_c x) \cos(\omega t - \beta z), 0)$.
Fields $E, B$ of $(t, x, y, z)$ satisfy the *source-free vacuum Maxwell equations* at speed $c > 0$ if, at every time and point, $\nabla \cdot E = 0$, $\nabla \cdot B = 0$, $\nabla \times E = -\partial_t B$ and $\nabla \times B = c^{-2} \partial_t E$.

For all real $a, E_0, \omega, \beta$ satisfying the dispersion relation $\omega^2 = c^2 (k_c^2 + \beta^2)$, the TE10 fields satisfy the source-free vacuum Maxwell equations at every time and point. For $\omega = 0$ or $a = 0$ the definitions take the convention $x/0 = 0$.
