# TE10 mode of a rectangular vacuum guide

Let $k_c = \pi/a$. The TE10 fields with amplitude $E_0$, angular frequency $\omega$ and propagation constant $\beta$ are

$$E = \bigl(0,\; E_0 \sin(k_c x) \cos(\omega t - \beta z),\; 0\bigr),$$

$$B = \Bigl(-\tfrac{\beta E_0}{\omega} \sin(k_c x) \cos(\omega t - \beta z),\; 0,\; -\tfrac{k_c E_0}{\omega} \cos(k_c x) \sin(\omega t - \beta z)\Bigr).$$

Fields $E, B$ of $(t, x, y, z)$ satisfy the *source-free vacuum Maxwell equations* at speed $c > 0$ if, at every time and point, $\nabla \cdot E = 0$, $\nabla \cdot B = 0$, $\nabla \times E = -\partial_t B$ and $\nabla \times B = c^{-2} \partial_t E$.

Let $c > 0$ be the speed of light, $a > 0$, $b > 0$, $E_0 \ne 0$, $\omega > 0$, $\beta \ge 0$, and assume the dispersion relation $\omega^2 = c^2 (k_c^2 + \beta^2)$. Then: the TE10 fields satisfy the source-free vacuum Maxwell equations; at every time $t$ they satisfy the PEC conditions on the four side walls of the guide of widths $a, b$ (tangential $E$ and normal $B$ vanish on $x = 0, a$ for $0 \le y \le b$ and on $y = 0, b$ for $0 \le x \le a$); $E$ is not identically zero; $c\, k_c \le \omega$; $\beta = 0$ if and only if $\omega = c\, k_c$; and $\beta = \sqrt{\omega^2/c^2 - k_c^2}$.
