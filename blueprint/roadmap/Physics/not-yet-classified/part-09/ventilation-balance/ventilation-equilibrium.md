# Ventilation equilibrium concentration

For volumetric flow $Q$, outdoor concentration $c_{out}$ and constant indoor source strength $G$ (mass per time), the well-mixed ventilation equilibrium concentration is $c_{out} + G/Q$, with the value $c_{out}$ when $Q = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$. Concentrations are in one consistent unit (mass per volume).
