# Ventilation concentration is nonnegative

For room volume $V > 0$, flow $Q \ge 0$, outdoor concentration $c_{out} \ge 0$, source $G \ge 0$, initial concentration $c_0 \ge 0$ and time $t \ge 0$, the well-mixed concentration $c(t)$, which solves $c' = (Q c_{out} + G)/V - (Q/V) c$ with $c(0) = c_0$, satisfies $c(t) \ge 0$.
