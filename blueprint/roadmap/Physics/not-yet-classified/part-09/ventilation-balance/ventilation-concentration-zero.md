# Initial ventilation concentration

For room volume $V$, ventilation rate $Q$, outdoor concentration $c_{\mathrm{out}}$, generation rate $G$ and initial concentration $c_0$, the well-mixed ventilation concentration, the one-compartment solution with exchange rate $Q/V$ and input rate $(Q c_{\mathrm{out}} + G)/V$, equals $c_0$ at $t = 0$.
