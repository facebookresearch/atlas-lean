# Well-mixed ventilation concentration

For room volume $V$, ventilation rate $Q$, outdoor concentration $c_{out}$, generation rate $G$ and initial concentration $c_0$, the ventilation concentration $c(t)$ is defined as the solution of

$$c' = \frac{Q c_{out} + G}{V} - \frac{Q}{V}\,c, \qquad c(0) = c_0,$$

the one-compartment relaxation with exchange rate $Q/V$ and input rate $(Q c_{out} + G)/V$, both taken as $0$ when $V = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
