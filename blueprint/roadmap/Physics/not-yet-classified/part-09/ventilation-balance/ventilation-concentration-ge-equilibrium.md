# Ventilation concentration stays above equilibrium

Let $V > 0$ and $Q > 0$. For the well-mixed room with outdoor concentration $c_{\mathrm{out}}$, indoor source $G$ and initial concentration $c_0$, the equilibrium is $c_{\mathrm{eq}} = c_{\mathrm{out}} + G/Q$ and the concentration is $c(t) = c_{\mathrm{eq}} + (c_0 - c_{\mathrm{eq}})\,e^{-Q t/V}$. If $c_0 \ge c_{\mathrm{eq}}$, then $c(t) \ge c_{\mathrm{eq}}$ for every $t$.
