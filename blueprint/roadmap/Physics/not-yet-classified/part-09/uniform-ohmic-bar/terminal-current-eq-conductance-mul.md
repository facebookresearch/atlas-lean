# Terminal current of a uniform ohmic bar

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ be a measurable cross-section of area $A > 0$, let $\ell > 0$, $\sigma \ge 0$ and $V \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $E, J : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $E(p) = V/\ell$ and $J(p) = \sigma E(p)$ for every $p \in S$. Then the terminal current is

$$I = \int_S J\,dA = \frac{\sigma A}{\ell}\,V.$$
