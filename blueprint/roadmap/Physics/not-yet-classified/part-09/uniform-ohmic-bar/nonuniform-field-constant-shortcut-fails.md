# Constant-field shortcut fails for a nonuniform field

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ be a measurable cross-section of area $A > 0$ and let $\sigma > 0$. On a unit-length bar with axial field $E(x) = 2x$,

$$\int_0^1 2x\,dx = 1,$$

and the flux $\int_S \sigma E(0)\,dA$ of the current density at $x = 0$ through $S$ is not equal to the constant-field value $\dfrac{\sigma A}{1} \int_0^1 2x\,dx$.
