# Zone heat inventory balance

Let finitely many zones have capacities $C_i$, symmetric conductances $G_{ij} = G_{ji}$, temperatures $T_i$ differentiable at $t$, and suppose the zone equations $C_i T_i'(t) = \sum_j G_{ij}(T_j(t) - T_i(t)) + b_i(t) + P_i(t)$ hold at $t$. Then the total heat inventory $\sum_i C_i T_i$ has derivative $\sum_i (b_i(t) + P_i(t))$ at $t$.
