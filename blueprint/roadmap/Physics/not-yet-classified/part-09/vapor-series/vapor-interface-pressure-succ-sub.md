# Pressure drop across one layer of a vapor resistance series

For $n$ layers with resistances $R_0, \dots, R_{n-1}$ and boundary pressures $p_{\text{in}}$, $p_{\text{out}}$, let the series flux be $J = (p_{\text{in}} - p_{\text{out}})/\sum_{i<n} R_i$ and the interface pressures $p_m = p_{\text{in}} - J\sum_{i<m} R_i$. For every $m < n$,

$$p_m - p_{m+1} = J\,R_m.$$
