# Steady vapor flux through layers in series

For boundary partial pressures $p_{in}$, $p_{out}$ and vapor resistances $R_0, \dots, R_{n-1}$ of $n$ layers in series, the steady vapor mass flux is defined as

$$g = \frac{p_{in} - p_{out}}{\sum_{i<n} R_i},$$

with the value $0$ when the total resistance is zero. It is the physical steady flux when the total resistance is nonzero, for instance when all resistances are positive.
