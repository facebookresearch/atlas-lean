# Interface vapor pressure in a layered wall

For $n$ layers with resistances $R_0, \dots, R_{n-1}$ and boundary pressures $p_{in}, p_{out}$, the series flux is $J = (p_{in} - p_{out})/\sum_{i<n} R_i$, and the interface pressure after the first $m$ layers is $p_m = p_{in} - J\sum_{i<m} R_i$. Physical interfaces are $m \le n$.
