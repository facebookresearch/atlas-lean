# Right-traveling discharge is proportional to depth

For real $g$, $A$, $a$ and a profile $F$, the right-traveling water-hammer state with depth $h(t,x) = F(x - at)$ and discharge $q(t,x) = (gA/a)\,F(x - at)$ satisfies $q(t,x) = (gA/a)\,h(t,x)$ for all $t$, $x$, where $gA/a = 0$ when $a = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
