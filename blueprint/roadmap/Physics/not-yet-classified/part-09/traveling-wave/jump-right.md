# Joukowsky relation for a right-traveling wave

For $a \ne 0$, $A \ne 0$ and a profile $F$, let $h(t, x) = F(x - a t)$ and $q(t, x) = (g A / a) F(x - a t)$. Then for all $t$, $x_1$, $x_2$,

$$\rho g \big(h(t, x_2) - h(t, x_1)\big) = \rho a \, \frac{q(t, x_2) - q(t, x_1)}{A},$$

that is, $\Delta p = \rho a \Delta v$ with $p = \rho g h$ and $v = q/A$.
