# Joukowsky relation for a left-traveling wave

Let $a \ne 0$ and $A \ne 0$, and consider the left-traveling water-hammer wave with head $h(t, x) = F(x + at)$ and discharge $q(t, x) = -(gA/a)\,F(x + at)$. For any time $t$ and positions $x_1$, $x_2$, the pressure jump $\Delta p = \rho g\,(h(t, x_2) - h(t, x_1))$ and velocity jump $\Delta v = (q(t, x_2) - q(t, x_1))/A$ satisfy

$$\Delta p = -\rho a\,\Delta v.$$
