# Weak solution of a variable-rigidity bar exists and is unique

Let $L > 0$. Let $H^1_0(0, L)$ consist of functions $u$ with $u(x) = \int_0^x u'$ on $[0, L]$ for some $u' \in L^2(0, L)$ with $\int_0^L u' = 0$. A weak solution of $-(EA\, u')' = q$ with fixed ends is $u \in H^1_0(0, L)$ with $\int_0^L EA\, u' v' = \int_0^L q\, v$ for every $v \in H^1_0(0, L)$. Suppose $EA$ is a.e. strongly measurable on $[0, L]$, $0 < EA_{\min}$, $EA_{\min} \le EA \le EA_{\max}$ a.e. on $[0, L]$, and $q \in L^2(0, L)$. Then a weak solution $u$ exists, and every weak solution agrees with $u$ on $[0, L]$.
