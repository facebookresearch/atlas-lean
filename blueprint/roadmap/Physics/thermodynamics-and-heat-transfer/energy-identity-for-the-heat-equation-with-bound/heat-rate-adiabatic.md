# Base heat rate of a fin with an adiabatic tip

Let `k`, `A`, `h`, `P` and `L` be positive, and let `(T, q)` be a
[steady fin](is-fin-steady-state.md) on `[0, L]` with `q(L) = 0`. Then, with `θ(x) = T(x) - Ta` and
the [fin parameter](fin-parameter.md) `m = √(hP/(kA))`,

`q(0) = √(hPkA) θ(0) tanh(mL)`.
