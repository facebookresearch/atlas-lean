# Efficiency of an adiabatic-tip fin with nonzero base excess

Let `k`, `A`, `h`, `P` and `L` be positive, and let `(T, q)` be a
[steady fin](is-fin-steady-state.md) on `[0, L]` with `q(L) = 0` and `T(0) ≠ Ta`. Then, with
`θ(x) = T(x) - Ta` and the [fin parameter](fin-parameter.md) `m = √(hP/(kA))`, the heat rate over
the rate `h (PL) θ(0)` of a fin held entirely at the base temperature is

`q(0) / (h (PL) θ(0)) = tanh(mL) / (mL)`.
