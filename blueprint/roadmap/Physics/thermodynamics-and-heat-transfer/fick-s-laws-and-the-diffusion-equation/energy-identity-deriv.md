# With zero boundary values the energy has derivative −2D ∫ wx²

Let `a < b`, `T` and `D` be real. Let `w`, `wx`, `wxx` and `wt` be continuous on `[a, b] × (0, T)`,
closed in `x` and open in `t`. At every point of `(a, b) × (0, T)` let `wt`, `wx` and `wxx` be the
derivatives `∂w/∂t`, `∂w/∂x` and `∂wx/∂x`, and let `wt = D wxx` there. Let `w(a, t) = w(b, t) = 0`
for `0 < t < T`. Then at every `t₀` with `0 < t₀ < T`, the energy `E(t) = ∫_a^b w(x, t)² dx` has the
derivative

`E′(t₀) = -2D ∫_a^b wx(x, t₀)² dx`.
