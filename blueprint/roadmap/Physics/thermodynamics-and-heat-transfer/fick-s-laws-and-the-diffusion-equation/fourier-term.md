# Terms of the slab sine series

For real `c₀`, `L`, `D`, `x` and `t` and a natural number `n`, the `n`-th term of the sine series
for diffusion in the slab `[0, L]` with zero boundary values and uniform initial concentration `c₀`
is

`bₙ sin(nπx/L) exp(-D (nπ/L)² t)`,

with the [sine coefficient](fourier-coeff.md) `bₙ`. It is meant for `L > 0`; for `L = 0` the
divisions by `L` give `0` and the term is `0`.
