# For t ≥ δ > 0 the series terms are bounded by a geometric sequence

Let `c₀ ≥ 0`, `L > 0`, `D > 0` and `δ > 0`. For every natural number `n`, every real `x` and every
`t ≥ δ`, the [series term](fourier-term.md) `bₙ sin(nπx/L) exp(-D (nπ/L)² t)` satisfies

`|bₙ sin(nπx/L) exp(-D (nπ/L)² t)| ≤ (4c₀/π + 1) ρⁿ` with `ρ = exp(-D (π/L)² δ)`.
