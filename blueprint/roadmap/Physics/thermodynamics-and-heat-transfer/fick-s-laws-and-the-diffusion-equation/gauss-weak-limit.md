# For M > 0 the Gaussian tends to the point mass M δ₀ as t → 0⁺

Let `M > 0` and `D > 0`, and let `φ : ℝ → ℝ` be continuous and bounded. Then, for the
[Gaussian](gauss.md) `G(x, t) = M/√(4πDt) · exp(-x²/(4Dt))`,

`∫_ℝ G(x, t) φ(x) dx → M φ(0)` as `t → 0⁺`:

the Gaussian tends to the point mass `M δ₀` against bounded continuous test functions. The statement
is for positive `M`.
