# Linear steady profile between two boundary concentrations

For real `c₁`, `c₂`, `L` and `x`, the steady profile is

`S(x) = c₁ + ((c₂ - c₁)/L) x`,

the linear profile on `[0, L]` from `c₁` at `x = 0` to `c₂` at `x = L`. It is meant for `L > 0`; for
`L = 0` the slope `(c₂ - c₁)/0` is `0` and the profile is the constant `c₁`.
