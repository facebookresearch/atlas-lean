# Thermal resistance of layers and films in series

For a common area $A$, film coefficients $h_{in}$ and $h_{out}$, and $n$ planar layers with thicknesses $L_i$ and conductivities $k_i$, the total thermal resistance in series is $R = \frac{1}{h_{in} A} + \sum_{i < n} \frac{L_i}{k_i A} + \frac{1}{h_{out} A}$, with the convention $x/0 = 0$; for instance it is $0$ when $A = 0$, which is not a physical wall of zero resistance.
