# Heat rate through layers and films in series

Let $A > 0$, $h_{in} > 0$, $h_{out} > 0$, and $L_i > 0$, $k_i > 0$ for every layer $i < n$. Let $T_0$ be the inside air temperature, $T_1, \dots, T_{n+1}$ the successive interface temperatures and $T_{n+2}$ the outside air temperature, and suppose a common heat rate $Q$ produces the drops $T_0 - T_1 = Q/(h_{in} A)$, $T_{i+1} - T_{i+2} = Q L_i/(k_i A)$ for $i < n$, and $T_{n+1} - T_{n+2} = Q/(h_{out} A)$. Then $Q = (T_0 - T_{n+2})/R$, with $R$ the total series resistance.
