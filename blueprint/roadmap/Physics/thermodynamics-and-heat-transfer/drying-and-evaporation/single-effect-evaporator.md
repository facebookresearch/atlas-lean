# Single-effect evaporator

For a single-effect evaporator with feed $F > 0$ and solids fractions $0 < x_F < x_P \le 1$ of feed and product, the solids balance
gives the product $P = F x_F/x_P$ and the vapour $V = F(1 - x_F/x_P) > 0$. With latent heats $\lambda_s, \lambda_v > 0$ of the
condensing steam and the vapour, feed heat capacity $c_p \ge 0$, boiling temperature $T_b \ge T_F$ and no heat loss or
boiling-point elevation, the steam use satisfies $S\lambda_s = V\lambda_v + F c_p (T_b - T_F)$, so $S > 0$ and the economy satisfies
$V/S \le \lambda_s/\lambda_v$. If $x_F = x_P$ there is no vapour, and no economy is formed when $S = 0$. Multiple-effect
operation and models of boiling-point elevation are outside this result.
