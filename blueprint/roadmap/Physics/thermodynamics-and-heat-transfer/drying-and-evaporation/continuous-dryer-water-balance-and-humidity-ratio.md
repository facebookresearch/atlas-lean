# Continuous dryer water balance and humidity ratio

For a steady continuous dryer with dry-solid mass flow $m_s > 0$ and dry-air mass flow $G > 0$, moisture contents
$X_{\mathrm{in}} \ge X_{\mathrm{out}} \ge 0$ of the solid (water per dry solid) and humidity ratios $Y_{\mathrm{in}}, Y_{\mathrm{out}}$
of the air (water per dry air), the water balance is

$$m_s (X_{\mathrm{in}} - X_{\mathrm{out}}) = G (Y_{\mathrm{out}} - Y_{\mathrm{in}}).$$

For moist air at total pressure $P > 0$ treated as an ideal-gas mixture with water partial pressure $0 \le p_v < P$, the humidity
ratio is $Y = \varepsilon\,p_v/(P - p_v)$ with $\varepsilon = M_{\mathrm{water}}/M_{\mathrm{air}}$; the value $0.622$ is a rounded
model constant. The latent and sensible duties of the dryer enter the open energy balance on the same time basis. Drying kinetics
are outside this result.
