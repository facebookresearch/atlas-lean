# For k ≠ 0 the outward fluxes of the slab faces sum to HL

Let `k ≠ 0`. For all real `Ts`, `Tb`, `L` and `H`, the [slab gradient](slab-gradient.md)
`T′(d) = (Tb - Ts)/L + HL/(2k) - H d/k` satisfies

`k T′(0) - k T′(L) = HL`.

The conductive flux along `d` is `-k T′`, so `k T′(0)` and `-k T′(L)` are the outward fluxes per
unit area at the faces `d = 0` and `d = L`; their sum balances the source over the thickness.
