# For k ≥ 0, the conductive dissipation is nonnegative

Let `k ≥ 0`, let `T : ℝ → ℝ` be a temperature profile and `a ≤ b`, `z` real numbers, and suppose
`z ↦ k T'(z)²` is integrable on `[a, b]`. Then the dissipation density is nonnegative,
`k T'(z)² ≥ 0`, and so is its integral, `∫ₐᵇ k T'(z)² dz ≥ 0`. Here `T'` is the derivative of `T`,
taken to be `0` where `T` is not differentiable; no differential equation or boundary condition is
assumed. The pointwise inequality uses neither `a ≤ b` nor integrability; for `a > b` the oriented
integral would be `≤ 0`.
