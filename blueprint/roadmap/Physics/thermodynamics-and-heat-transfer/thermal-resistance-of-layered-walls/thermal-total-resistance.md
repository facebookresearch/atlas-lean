# Total thermal resistance of a layered wall

For area $A$, inside and outside film coefficients $h_{in}$, $h_{out}$, and $n$ layers of thicknesses $L_i$ and conductivities $k_i$, the total series resistance is

$$R = \frac{1}{h_{in} A} + \sum_{i < n} \frac{L_i}{k_i A} + \frac{1}{h_{out} A},$$

where each term with zero denominator is $0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
