# Interface temperature after the inside film and m layers

For reals `A`, `h_in`, `T_in`, `Q`, sequences `L`, `k` and a natural number `m`, the interface
temperature after the inside film and the first `m` layers is

`T_m = T_in - Q (1/(h_in A) + ∑_{i < m} L i/(k i A))`,

the inside air temperature `T_in` minus the heat rate `Q` times the cumulative resistance. The
number of layers of the wall does not enter; `m = n` gives the last interface. With `x/0 = 0`, a
term is `0` when its denominator is `0`.
