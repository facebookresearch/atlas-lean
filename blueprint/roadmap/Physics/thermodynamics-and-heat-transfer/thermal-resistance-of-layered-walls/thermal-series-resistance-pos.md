# For positive area, films, thicknesses and conductivities R > 0

Let `A`, `h_in` and `h_out` be positive, and `L i > 0` and `k i > 0` for every `i < n`. Then the
[series resistance](thermal-series-resistance.md)
`R = 1/(h_in A) + ∑_{i < n} L i/(k i A) + 1/(h_out A)` is positive. It is a special case of the
[positivity of the total resistance](thermal-total-resistance-pos.md), which allows `L i ≥ 0`.
