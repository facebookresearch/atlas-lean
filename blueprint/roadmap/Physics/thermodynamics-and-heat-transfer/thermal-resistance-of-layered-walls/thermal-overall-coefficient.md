# Overall heat-transfer coefficient of a layered wall

For a natural number `n`, reals `A`, `h_in`, `h_out` and sequences `L`, `k`, the overall coefficient
is `U = 1/(A R)`, with the [series resistance](thermal-series-resistance.md)
`R = 1/(h_in A) + ∑_{i < n} L i/(k i A) + 1/(h_out A)`. With `x/0 = 0` it is `0` when `A R = 0`.
