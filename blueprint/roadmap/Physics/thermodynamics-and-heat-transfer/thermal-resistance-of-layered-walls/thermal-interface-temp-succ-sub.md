# Adjacent interface temperatures differ by the layer drop

For all reals `A`, `h_in`, `T_in`, `Q`, sequences `L`, `k` and every natural number `m`, the
[interface temperature](thermal-interface-temp.md)
`T_m = T_in - Q (1/(h_in A) + ∑_{i < m} L i/(k i A))` satisfies

`T_m - T_{m+1} = Q L m/(k m A)`.
