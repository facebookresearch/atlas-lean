# For emissivities 0 < ε ≤ 1, parallel-plate radiosities exist uniquely

Let the emissivities of two plates satisfy `0 < ε_i ≤ 1`. Then for every real `σ` and all
temperatures `T`, the [radiosity equations](is-radiosity.md) with the
[parallel-plate view factors](parallel-plate-view-factor.md) have exactly one solution.
