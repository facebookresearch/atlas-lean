# By the radiosity equations, equal-temperature plates exchange nothing

Let the emissivities of two plates satisfy `0 < ε_i ≤ 1`, let `A` and `σ` be real numbers, and let
`J` solve the [radiosity equations](is-radiosity.md) with the
[parallel-plate view factors](parallel-plate-view-factor.md) for temperatures with `T₀ = T₁`. Then
the [gray net power](gray-net-power.md) leaving plate `0` is `0`.
