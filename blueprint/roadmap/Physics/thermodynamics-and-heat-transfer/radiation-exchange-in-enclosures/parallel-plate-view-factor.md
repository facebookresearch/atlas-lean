# View factors of two plates in the infinite-plane idealization

The parallel-plate view factors on two plates `0` and `1` are `F_{01} = F_{10} = 1` and
`F_{00} = F_{11} = 0`: all radiation leaving one plate reaches the other, and a flat plate does not
see itself. They model infinite parallel planes, with no loss through the edges; for finite plates
at a positive gap they are an approximation.
