# The net powers of a black enclosure sum to zero

Let `ι` be a finite set of surfaces with areas `A` and view factors `F` forming a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md). For every real `σ` and all temperatures `T`,
`∑_i P_i = 0`, where `P_i` is the [net power](net-power.md) leaving surface `i`. Only reciprocity is
used; no sign condition on `σ` or `T` is needed.
