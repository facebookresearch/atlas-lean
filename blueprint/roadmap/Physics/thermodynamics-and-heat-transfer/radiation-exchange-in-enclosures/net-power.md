# Net radiative power leaving a black surface

For a constant `σ`, areas `A`, view factors `F`, temperatures `T` on a finite set `ι` of surfaces
and a surface `i`, the net radiative power leaving `i` is

`P_i = ∑_j A_i F_{ij} σ (T_i⁴ - T_j⁴)`,

positive for a net outflow. In the physical reading `σ > 0` is the Stefan–Boltzmann constant, the
temperatures are absolute and the data form a [black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md); the formula
is defined for all real inputs.
