# A hottest surface has nonnegative net outflow

Let `ι` be a finite set of surfaces with areas `A` and view factors `F` forming a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md), let `σ > 0`, let the temperatures `T_i ≥ 0` be absolute,
and let `k` be a hottest surface: `T_j ≤ T_k` for every `j`. Then the [net power](net-power.md)
leaving `k` is `≥ 0`.
