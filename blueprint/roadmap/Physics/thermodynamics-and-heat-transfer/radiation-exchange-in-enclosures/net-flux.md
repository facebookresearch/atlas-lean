# Net radiative flux leaving a black surface

For `σ`, areas `A`, view factors `F`, temperatures `T` and a surface `i`, the net radiative flux
leaving `i` is `P_i / A_i`, the [net power](net-power.md) per unit area. In the physical reading
`A_i > 0`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `A_i = 0`.
