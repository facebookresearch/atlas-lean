# Power-law Nusselt correlation

For real $c$, $a$, $b$, a citation, a flow configuration, hydrodynamic and thermal development, a property-variation class, an averaging tag, a property-evaluation temperature and a nonempty validity domain of flow conditions on which $Re > 0$ and $Pr > 0$, the power-law correlation has these data and the formula $Nu = c\,Re^{a}\,Pr^{b}$ with real exponents.
