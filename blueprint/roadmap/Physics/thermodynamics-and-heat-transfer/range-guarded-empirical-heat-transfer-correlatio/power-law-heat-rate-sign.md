# Sign of the heat rate from a power-law Nusselt number

Let $c > 0$, $Re > 0$ and $Pr > 0$, and let $k > 0$, $L > 0$ and $A > 0$. Then the heat transfer coefficient $h = c\,Re^{a}\,Pr^{b}\,k/L$ is positive for all real $a$, $b$, and for every temperature difference $\Delta T$ the heat rate $Q = h\,A\,\Delta T$ has the sign of $\Delta T$.
