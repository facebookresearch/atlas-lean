# Flow conditions of a correlation request

Flow conditions consist of a Reynolds number $Re$, a Prandtl number $Pr$, the ratio $L/D$ of the heated length to the characteristic length (for a duct, its diameter), the surface-minus-fluid temperature difference $\Delta T = T_s - T_m$ (a temperature), and the ratio $\mu_m/\mu_s$ of the dynamic viscosity at the mean fluid temperature to that at the surface temperature.
