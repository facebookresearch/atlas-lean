# Sign of the heat rate where a power-law correlation applies

Let a power-law correlation with coefficient $c > 0$ apply to a request $r$, and let $k > 0$, $L > 0$ and $A > 0$. Then its Nusselt number gives a positive heat transfer coefficient $h = Nu\,k/L$, and the heat rate $Q = h\,A\,\Delta T$ at the temperature difference $\Delta T$ of $r$ has the sign of $\Delta T$.
