# The clipped radiator rate is 4 K_rad T_max³ / C-Lipschitz

Let `C > 0`, `K_rad > 0` and `0 ≤ T_min ≤ T_max`, and let `Q` be real. Then the
[clipped radiator rate](clipped-radiator-rate.md) is a Lipschitz function of the temperature with
the nonnegative constant `4 K_rad T_max³ / C`
([pointwise form](clipped-radiator-rate-lipschitz.md)).
