# Adding the battery loss heat keeps the lower inward sign

Let `P_c ≥ 0`, `P_d ≥ 0`, `0 < η_c ≤ 1`, `0 < η_d ≤ 1`, and let `Q_env`, `K_rad`, `T_min` be real
with `Q_env - K_rad T_min⁴ ≥ 0`. Then `Q_env + q - K_rad T_min⁴ ≥ 0`, where `q` is the
[battery loss heat](battery-loss-heat.md). The upper inward sign must still be checked on the total
heat input.
