# If P_c P_d = 0, storage power and heat depend only on P_c − P_d

Let `P_c ≥ 0` and `P_d ≥ 0` with `P_c P_d = 0`, and let `η_c`, `η_d` be real. Then with
`P = P_c - P_d`, `P⁺ = max(P, 0)` and `P⁻ = max(-P, 0)`:

`η_c P_c - P_d / η_d = η_c P⁺ - P⁻ / η_d` and `q = (1 - η_c) P⁺ + (1/η_d - 1) P⁻`,

where `q` is the [battery loss heat](battery-loss-heat.md): the storage power and the heat depend on
the terminal power only.
