# Radiator solutions starting in an inward box stay in it

Let `C > 0`, `K_rad > 0`, `0 ≤ T_min ≤ T_max` and `Q` satisfy the inward end conditions
`Q - K_rad T_min⁴ ≥ 0` and `Q - K_rad T_max⁴ ≤ 0`. Let `T : ℝ → ℝ` have, at every `s ≥ 0`, the
derivative `(Q - K_rad T(s)⁴) / C` within `[0, ∞)` (one-sided at `0`), with `T(0) ∈ [T_min, T_max]`.
Then `T(t) ∈ [T_min, T_max]` for every `t ≥ 0`.
