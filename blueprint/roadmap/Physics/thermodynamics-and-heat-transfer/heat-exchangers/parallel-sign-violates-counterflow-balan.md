# The parallel-flow sign breaks the counterflow heat balance

Let $C_h > 0$ and $L \ge 0$, and suppose that on $[0, L]$ the enthalpy flows $C_h T_h$ and $C_c T_c$ have derivatives within $[0, L]$ equal to $-K(T_h - T_c)$ and $K(T_h - T_c)$ (the parallel-flow sign). If $T_h(L) \ne T_h(0)$, then $C_h(T_h(0) - T_h(L)) \ne C_c(T_c(0) - T_c(L))$.
