# Constant temperature difference for equal capacity rates

Let $C > 0$, $UA \ge 0$ and $L > 0$, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the counterflow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with equal capacity rates $C_h = C_c = C$, conductance per unit length $UA/L$, hot inlet $T_{hi}$ at $x = 0$ and cold inlet $T_{ci}$ at $x = L$. Then $T_h(x) - T_c(x) = (T_{hi} - T_{ci})/(1 + UA/C)$ for every $x \in [0, L]$.
