# Cold-stream temperature in counterflow

The cold-stream temperature of the counterflow solution is $T_c(x) = T_{hi} - D_0 - \frac{K}{C_c} D_0 \int_0^x e^{-s y}\,dy$, with $D_0$ the hot-inlet temperature difference and $s = K(1/C_h - 1/C_c)$; its value $T_c(0)$ is the cold outlet. It is intended for $C_h, C_c > 0$.
