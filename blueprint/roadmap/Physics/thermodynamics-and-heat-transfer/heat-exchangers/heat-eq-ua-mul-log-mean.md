# Heat duty and the logarithmic mean temperature difference in parallel flow

Let $C_h > 0$, $C_c > 0$ and $L > 0$, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the parallel-flow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with conductance per unit length $UA/L$ and inlet temperatures $T_{hi}$, $T_{ci}$ at $x = 0$. Then the heat lost by the hot stream is $C_h(T_{hi} - T_h(L)) = UA \cdot \Delta_{lm}$, where $\Delta_{lm}$ is the logarithmic mean of the end differences $T_{hi} - T_{ci}$ and $T_h(L) - T_c(L)$.
