# Outlet temperatures in parallel flow

Let $C_h > 0$, $C_c > 0$ and $L > 0$, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the parallel-flow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with conductance per unit length $UA/L$ and inlet temperatures $T_{hi}$, $T_{ci}$ at $x = 0$. Then $T_h(L) = T_{hi} - \frac{C_c}{C_h + C_c}(T_{hi} - T_{ci})\big(1 - e^{-UA(1/C_h + 1/C_c)}\big)$ and $T_c(L) = T_{ci} + \frac{C_h}{C_h + C_c}(T_{hi} - T_{ci})\big(1 - e^{-UA(1/C_h + 1/C_c)}\big)$.
