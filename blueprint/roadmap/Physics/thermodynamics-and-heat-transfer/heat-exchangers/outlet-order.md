# Order of the outlet temperatures in parallel flow

Let $C_h > 0$, $C_c > 0$ and $L > 0$, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the parallel-flow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with conductance per unit length $UA/L$ and inlet temperatures $T_{hi}$, $T_{ci}$ at $x = 0$. If $UA > 0$ and $T_{hi} > T_{ci}$, then $T_{ci} < T_c(L) < T_h(L) < T_{hi}$.
