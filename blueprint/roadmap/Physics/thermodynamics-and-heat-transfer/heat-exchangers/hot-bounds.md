# Hot-stream bounds in counterflow

Let $C_h > 0$ and $C_c > 0$ be capacity rates and $K \ge 0$, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the counterflow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$: $T_h(0) = T_{hi}$, $T_c(L) = T_{ci}$, and $C_h T_h' = -K(T_h - T_c)$, $C_c T_c' = -K(T_h - T_c)$ on $[0, L]$ (derivatives of the enthalpy flows within $[0, L]$, one-sided at the endpoints when $L > 0$) (the cold stream flows against $x$). If $L \ge 0$ and $T_{hi} > T_{ci}$, then the hot outlet satisfies $T_{ci} < T_h(L)$, and $T_h(L) \le T_h(x) \le T_{hi}$ for every $x \in [0, L]$.
