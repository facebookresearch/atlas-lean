# Logarithmic mean temperature difference

The logarithmic mean of two end temperature differences $\Delta_1$, $\Delta_2$ is $(\Delta_1 - \Delta_2)/\log(\Delta_1/\Delta_2)$ when $\Delta_1 \ne \Delta_2$, and $\Delta_1$ when $\Delta_1 = \Delta_2$. It is intended for $\Delta_1$ and $\Delta_2$ of one strict sign; for other inputs it uses the total real logarithm ($\log 0 = 0$, $\log x = \log|x|$) and the convention $x/0 = 0$.
