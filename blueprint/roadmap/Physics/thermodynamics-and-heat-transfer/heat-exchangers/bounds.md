# Temperature bounds in parallel flow

Let $C_h > 0$ and $C_c > 0$ be capacity rates, and let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the parallel-flow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with conductance per unit length $K$ and inlet temperatures $T_{hi}$, $T_{ci}$ at $x = 0$: $T_h(0) = T_{hi}$, $T_c(0) = T_{ci}$, $C_h T_h' = -K(T_h - T_c)$ and $C_c T_c' = K(T_h - T_c)$ on $[0, L]$ (derivatives of the enthalpy flows within $[0, L]$, one-sided at the endpoints when $L > 0$). If $K \ge 0$ and $T_{ci} \le T_{hi}$, then $T_{ci} \le T_c(x)$ and $T_h(x) \le T_{hi}$ for every $x \in [0, L]$.
