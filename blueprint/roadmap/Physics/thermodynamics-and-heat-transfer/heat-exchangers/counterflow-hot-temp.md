# Hot-stream temperature in counterflow

With the hot inlet $T_{hi}$ at $x = 0$ and the cold inlet $T_{ci}$ at $x = L$, the hot-stream temperature of the counterflow solution is $T_h(x) = T_{hi} - \frac{K}{C_h} D_0 \int_0^x e^{-s y}\,dy$, with $D_0$ the hot-inlet temperature difference and $s = K(1/C_h - 1/C_c)$. The formula is defined for all real parameters, with the convention $x/0 = 0$; it solves the counterflow equations with both inlet conditions when $C_h, C_c > 0$ and $K, L \ge 0$.
