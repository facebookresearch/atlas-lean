# Decay rate of the temperature difference in counterflow

For a conductance per unit length $K$ and capacity rates $C_h$, $C_c$, the decay rate of the stream temperature difference in counterflow is $s = K\,(1/C_h - 1/C_c)$. It vanishes for equal capacity rates. With the convention $x/0 = 0$ it is defined for all real inputs.
