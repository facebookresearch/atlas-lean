# Effectiveness of a counterflow exchanger

The counterflow effectiveness is $\varepsilon_c(NTU, C_r) = \frac{1 - e^{-NTU(1 - C_r)}}{1 - C_r e^{-NTU(1 - C_r)}}$ for $C_r \ne 1$ and $NTU/(1 + NTU)$ for $C_r = 1$, intended for $NTU \ge 0$ and $0 < C_r \le 1$; with the convention $x/0 = 0$ it is defined for all real inputs.
