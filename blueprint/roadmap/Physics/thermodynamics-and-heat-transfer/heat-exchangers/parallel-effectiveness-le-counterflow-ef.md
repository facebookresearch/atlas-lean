# Counterflow is at least as effective as parallel flow

For real $\mathrm{NTU} \ge 0$ and $0 \le C_r \le 1$,

$$\varepsilon_p(\mathrm{NTU}, C_r) \le \varepsilon_c(\mathrm{NTU}, C_r),$$

where $\varepsilon_p(\mathrm{NTU}, C_r) = \dfrac{1 - e^{-\mathrm{NTU}(1 + C_r)}}{1 + C_r}$ is the parallel-flow effectiveness and the counterflow effectiveness is

$$\varepsilon_c(\mathrm{NTU}, C_r) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\mathrm{NTU}}{1 + \mathrm{NTU}}, & C_r = 1,\\[2ex] \dfrac{1 - e^{-\mathrm{NTU}(1 - C_r)}}{1 - C_r\,e^{-\mathrm{NTU}(1 - C_r)}}, & C_r \ne 1. \end{cases}$$
