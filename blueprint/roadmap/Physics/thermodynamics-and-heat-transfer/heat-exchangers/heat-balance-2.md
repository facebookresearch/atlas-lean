# Heat balance of a counterflow exchanger

Let $(T_h, T_c)$ solve the counterflow exchanger equations on $[0, L]$ with capacity rates $C_h$, $C_c$ and inlets $T_{hi}$ at $x = 0$, $T_{ci}$ at $x = L$. Then $C_h(T_{hi} - T_h(x)) = C_c(T_c(0) - T_c(x))$ for every $x \in [0, L]$. When $L \ge 0$, taking $x = L$ gives the port balance.
