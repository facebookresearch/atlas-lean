# Temperature difference at the hot inlet of a counterflow exchanger

For capacity rates $C_h$, $C_c$, conductance per unit length $K$, length $L$ and inlets $T_{hi}$, $T_{ci}$, the temperature difference at the hot inlet of the counterflow solution is $D_0 = (T_{hi} - T_{ci})/\big(1 + \frac{K}{C_c}\int_0^L e^{-s y}\,dy\big)$ with $s = K(1/C_h - 1/C_c)$, fixed by the cold inlet condition $T_c(L) = T_{ci}$.
