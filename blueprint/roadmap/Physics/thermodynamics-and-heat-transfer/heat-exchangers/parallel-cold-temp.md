# Cold-stream temperature in parallel flow

With inlet temperatures $T_{hi}$ and $T_{ci}$ at $x = 0$ and decay rate $r = K(1/C_h + 1/C_c)$, the cold-stream temperature of the parallel-flow solution is $T_c(x) = T_{ci} + \frac{C_h}{C_h + C_c}(T_{hi} - T_{ci})(1 - e^{-r x})$. It is intended for $C_h, C_c > 0$; with the convention $x/0 = 0$ it is defined for all real parameters.
