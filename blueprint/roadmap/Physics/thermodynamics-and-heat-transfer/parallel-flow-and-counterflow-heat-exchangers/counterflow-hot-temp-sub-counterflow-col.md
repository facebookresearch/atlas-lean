# The counterflow temperature difference is D₀ exp(−s x)

For all real `C_h`, `C_c`, `K`, `L`, `T_hi`, `T_ci` and `x`, the [hot](counterflow-hot-temp.md) and
[cold](counterflow-cold-temp.md) closed-form temperatures satisfy

`T_h(x) - T_c(x) = D₀ exp(-s x)`,

with `D₀` the [boundary constant](counterflow-inlet-diff.md) and `s` the
[counterflow rate](counterflow-rate.md). No sign condition is needed.
