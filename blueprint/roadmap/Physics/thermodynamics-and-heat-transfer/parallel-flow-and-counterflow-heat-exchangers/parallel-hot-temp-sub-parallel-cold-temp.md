# The parallel-flow temperature difference decays exponentially

Let `C_h > 0` and `C_c > 0`, and let `K`, `T_hi`, `T_ci` and `x` be real numbers. Then the
[hot](parallel-hot-temp.md) and [cold](parallel-cold-temp.md) closed-form temperatures satisfy

`T_h(x) - T_c(x) = (T_hi - T_ci) exp(-r x)`,

with `r` the [parallel-flow decay rate](parallel-decay-rate.md).
