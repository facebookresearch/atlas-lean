# For NTU ≥ 0 and 0 ≤ C_r ≤ 1, counterflow is at least as effective

Let `NTU ≥ 0` and `0 ≤ C_r ≤ 1`. Then the [parallel-flow effectiveness](parallel-effectiveness.md)
is at most the [counterflow effectiveness](counterflow-effectiveness.md) at the same `NTU` and
`C_r`: `ε_p(NTU, C_r) ≤ ε_c(NTU, C_r)`.
