# Every parallel-flow solution is the closed form on the exchanger

Let `C_h > 0` and `C_c > 0`, and let `K`, `L`, `T_hi`, `T_ci` be real. If `(T_h, T_c)` is a
[parallel-flow solution](is-parallel-flow-solution.md) on `[0, L]`, then for every `x ∈ [0, L]`,
`T_h(x)` and `T_c(x)` are the [hot](parallel-hot-temp.md) and [cold](parallel-cold-temp.md)
closed-form temperatures at `x`. No sign condition on `K` is needed.
