# In parallel flow, the hot stream loses what the cold stream gains

Let `C_h > 0` and `C_c > 0`, and let `(T_h, T_c)` be a
[parallel-flow solution](is-parallel-flow-solution.md) on `[0, L]` for real `K`, `L`, `T_hi`,
`T_ci`. Then for every `x ∈ [0, L]`,

`C_h (T_hi - T_h(x)) = C_c (T_c(x) - T_ci)`.
