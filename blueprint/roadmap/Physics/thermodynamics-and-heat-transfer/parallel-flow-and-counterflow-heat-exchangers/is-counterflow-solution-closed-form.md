# For K, L ≥ 0, the counterflow closed form is a solution

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `K ≥ 0` and `L ≥ 0`, and let `T_hi`, `T_ci` be real. Then the
[hot](counterflow-hot-temp.md) and [cold](counterflow-cold-temp.md) closed-form temperatures form a
[counterflow solution](is-counterflow-solution.md) on `[0, L]`, with the hot inlet at `x = 0` and
the cold inlet at `x = L`.
