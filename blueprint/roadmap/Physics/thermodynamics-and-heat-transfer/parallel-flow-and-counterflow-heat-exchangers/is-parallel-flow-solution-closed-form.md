# The parallel-flow closed form is a solution

Let `C_h > 0` and `C_c > 0`. For all real `K`, `L`, `T_hi` and `T_ci`, the
[hot](parallel-hot-temp.md) and [cold](parallel-cold-temp.md) closed-form temperatures form a
[parallel-flow solution](is-parallel-flow-solution.md) on `[0, L]` with both inlets at `x = 0`. No
sign condition on `K` or `L` is needed.
