# Counterflow cold-stream bounds for K, L ≥ 0 and T_hi > T_ci

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `K ≥ 0`, `L ≥ 0` and `T_ci < T_hi`, and let `(T_h, T_c)` be a
[counterflow solution](is-counterflow-solution.md) on `[0, L]`. Then the cold outlet `T_c(0)` is
below the hot inlet, `T_c(0) < T_hi`, and `T_ci ≤ T_c(x) ≤ T_c(0)` for every `x ∈ [0, L]`.
