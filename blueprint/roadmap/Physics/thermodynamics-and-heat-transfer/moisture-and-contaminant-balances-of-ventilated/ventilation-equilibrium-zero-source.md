# With Q > 0 and no indoor source the equilibrium is the outdoor value

Let `Q > 0`. For every real `c_out`, the [ventilation equilibrium](ventilation-equilibrium.md) with
source `G = 0` is `c_out + 0/Q = c_out`. At zero flow every constant concentration is an equilibrium
of the balance, so the reading as the equilibrium needs positive flow.
