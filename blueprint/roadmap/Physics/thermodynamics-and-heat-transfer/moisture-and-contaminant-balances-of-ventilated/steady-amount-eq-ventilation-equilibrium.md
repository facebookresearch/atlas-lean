# For V, Q > 0 the steady amount is the ventilation equilibrium

Let `V > 0` and `Q > 0`. For all real `c_out` and `G`, the steady amount `R/k` of the compartment
equation at rate `k = Q/V` and input `R = (Q c_out + G)/V` is

`((Q c_out + G)/V) / (Q/V) = c_out + G/Q`,

the [ventilation equilibrium](ventilation-equilibrium.md).
