# For V, Q > 0 a room started at equilibrium stays there

Let `V > 0` and `Q > 0`. For all real `c_out`, `G` and `t`, the
[ventilation concentration](ventilation-concentration.md) `c(t)` started at `c₀ = c_eq` satisfies
`c(t) = c_eq`, with the [equilibrium](ventilation-equilibrium.md) `c_eq = c_out + G/Q`.
