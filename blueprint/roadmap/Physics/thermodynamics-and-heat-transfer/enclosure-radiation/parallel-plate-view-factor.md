# View factors of infinite parallel plates

The view factors of two equal-area parallel plates in the infinite-plane idealization are $F_{01} = F_{10} = 1$ and $F_{00} = F_{11} = 0$: all radiation leaving one plate reaches the other, and a flat plate does not see itself. Finite plates at a positive gap have $F_{01} < 1$, so these view factors approximate them.
