# Unique radiosities of parallel gray plates

Let $F$ be the view factors of two equal-area parallel plates in the infinite-plane idealization, $F_{01} = F_{10} = 1$ and $F_{00} = F_{11} = 0$. If $0 < \varepsilon_i \le 1$ for both plates, then for every $\sigma$ and every pair of temperatures the radiosity equations have exactly one solution.
