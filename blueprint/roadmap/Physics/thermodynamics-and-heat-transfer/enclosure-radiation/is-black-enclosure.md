# Diffuse enclosures

Areas $A_i$ and view factors $F_{ij}$ on a finite set of surfaces form an enclosure when every area is positive, every view factor is nonnegative, every row of view factors sums to one, $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$ (an opening must be an explicit surface), and the reciprocity relation $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$ holds for all $i$, $j$.
