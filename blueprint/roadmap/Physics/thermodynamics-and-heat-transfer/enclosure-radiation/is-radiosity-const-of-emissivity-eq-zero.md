# Constant radiosities of a reflector-only enclosure

Let $(A, F)$ be enclosure data on a finite set of surfaces: areas $A_i > 0$, view factors $F_{ij} \ge 0$ with $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$. If every emissivity is zero, then every constant radiosity field solves the radiosity equations, for every $\sigma$ and every temperature field.
