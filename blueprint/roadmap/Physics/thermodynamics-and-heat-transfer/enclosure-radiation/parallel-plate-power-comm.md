# Parallel-plate exchange read from the other plate

For all real $\sigma$, $A$, $\varepsilon_1$, $\varepsilon_2$ (the power formula uses the convention $x/0 = 0$), $T_1$, $T_2$, the parallel-plate net power with the plates exchanged (emissivities $\varepsilon_2$, $\varepsilon_1$ and temperatures $T_2$, $T_1$) is the negative of the net power from plate 1.
