# Net powers of a black enclosure sum to zero

A *black enclosure* on a finite set of surfaces $i$ is a pair of areas $A_i$ and view factors $F_{ij}$ with $A_i > 0$, $F_{ij} \ge 0$, $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$ for every $i$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$ for all $i, j$. For every real $\sigma$ and all temperatures $T_i$, let $P_i = \sum_j A_i F_{ij} \sigma (T_i^4 - T_j^4)$ be the net power leaving surface $i$. Then

$$\sum_i P_i = 0.$$
