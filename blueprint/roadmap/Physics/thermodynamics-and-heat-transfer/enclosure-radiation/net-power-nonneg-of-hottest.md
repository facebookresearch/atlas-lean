# A hottest surface has nonnegative net outflow

Let $(A, F)$ be enclosure data on a finite set of surfaces: areas $A_i > 0$, view factors $F_{ij} \ge 0$ with $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$. If $\sigma > 0$, all temperatures are nonnegative and $T_j \le T_k$ for every surface $j$, then the net radiative power of surface $k$ is nonnegative.
