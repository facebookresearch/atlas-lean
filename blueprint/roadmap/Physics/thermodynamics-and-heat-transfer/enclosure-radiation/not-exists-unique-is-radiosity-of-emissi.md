# Radiosities of a reflector-only enclosure are not unique

Let $(A, F)$ be enclosure data on a finite set of surfaces: areas $A_i > 0$, view factors $F_{ij} \ge 0$ with $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$. If there is at least one surface and every emissivity is zero, then the radiosity equations do not have a unique solution, for any $\sigma$ and temperature field.
