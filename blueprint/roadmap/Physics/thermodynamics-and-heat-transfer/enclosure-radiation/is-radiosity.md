# Radiosity equations of a gray enclosure

Radiosities $J$ on a finite set of surfaces solve the radiosity equations of a diffuse gray enclosure with emissivities $\varepsilon$, view factors $F$, temperatures $T$ and constant $\sigma$ when $J_i = \varepsilon_i\,\sigma T_i^4 + (1 - \varepsilon_i)\sum_j F_{ij} J_j$ for every surface $i$: emitted power per unit area plus the reflected part of the irradiation, both in watts per unit area.
