# Gray net powers of an enclosure sum to zero

A *black enclosure* on a finite set of surfaces $i$ is a pair of areas $A_i$ and view factors $F_{ij}$ with $A_i > 0$, $F_{ij} \ge 0$, $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$ for every $i$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$ for all $i, j$. For any radiosities $J_i$, let $q_i = \sum_j A_i F_{ij} (J_i - J_j)$ be the gray net power leaving surface $i$. Then

$$\sum_i q_i = 0.$$
