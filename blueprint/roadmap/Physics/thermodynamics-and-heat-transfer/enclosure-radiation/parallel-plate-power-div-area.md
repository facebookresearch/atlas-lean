# Net radiative flux between parallel gray plates

If $A > 0$, then the parallel-plate net power divided by the plate area is $\sigma\,(T_1^4 - T_2^4)/(1/\varepsilon_1 + 1/\varepsilon_2 - 1)$, an identity between total quotients with the convention $x/0 = 0$, valid for all emissivities. It is the physical net flux between the plates only for $0 < \varepsilon_i \le 1$: for $\varepsilon_1 = 0$ and $\varepsilon_2 = 1/2$ the formal denominator is $1$, so the expression can be nonzero although a perfectly reflecting first plate exchanges no net power.
