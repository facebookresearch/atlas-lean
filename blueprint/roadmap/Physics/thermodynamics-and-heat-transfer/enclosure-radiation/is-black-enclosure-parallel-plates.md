# Equal-area parallel plates form an enclosure

Let $A > 0$. Two plates of area $A$ with the view factors of infinite parallel planes, $F_{01} = F_{10} = 1$ and $F_{00} = F_{11} = 0$, form a black enclosure: the areas are positive, the view factors are nonnegative with unit row sums $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$ holds (here because the two areas are equal).
