# Swapping plate temperatures reverses the net power

Let $q(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, T_1, T_2) = \dfrac{\sigma A (T_1^4 - T_2^4)}{1/\varepsilon_1 + 1/\varepsilon_2 - 1}$ be the parallel-plate net power. For all real $\sigma, A, \varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, T_1, T_2$,

$$q(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, T_2, T_1) = -\,q(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, T_1, T_2).$$

Both levels of division follow the convention $x/0 = 0$: an emissivity $\varepsilon_i = 0$ gives $1/\varepsilon_i = 0$, and a vanishing denominator $1/\varepsilon_1 + 1/\varepsilon_2 - 1 = 0$ gives $q = 0$.
