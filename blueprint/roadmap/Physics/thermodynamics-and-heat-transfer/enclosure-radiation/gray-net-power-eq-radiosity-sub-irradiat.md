# Net power of a gray surface as area times radiosity minus irradiation

Let $(A, F)$ be enclosure data on a finite set of surfaces: areas $A_i > 0$, view factors $F_{ij} \ge 0$ with $\sum_j F_{ij} = 1$, and reciprocity $A_i F_{ij} = A_j F_{ji}$. Then for all radiosities $J$, the net power of gray surface $i$ is $q_i = A_i\,(J_i - \sum_j F_{ij} J_j)$.
