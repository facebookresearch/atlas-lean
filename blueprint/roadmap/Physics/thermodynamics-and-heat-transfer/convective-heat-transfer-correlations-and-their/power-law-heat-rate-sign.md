# Power-law chain: h > 0 and the heat rate has the sign of ΔT

Let `c > 0`, `Re > 0`, `Pr > 0`, real exponents `a` and `b`, `k > 0`, `L > 0`, `A > 0` and a
temperature difference `ΔT`. Then for `Nu = c Re^a Pr^b`, the
[heat transfer coefficient](heat-transfer-coefficient.md) `h = Nu k / L` is positive, and the
[convective heat rate](convective-heat-rate.md) `Q = h A ΔT` has the sign of `ΔT`.
