# Where the power law applies, its Nusselt number is c Re^a Pr^b

For a [power-law correlation](power-law-correlation.md) with coefficient `c` and exponents `a`, `b`,
and a request `r` to which it [applies](applies.md), its [Nusselt number](nusselt.md) for `r` is
`c Re^a Pr^b`, with `Re` and `Pr` the Reynolds and Prandtl numbers of `r`.
