# Convective heat rate Q = h A ΔT

For a heat transfer coefficient `h`, an area `A` and the surface-minus-fluid temperature difference
`ΔT = T_s - T_m` (surface temperature `T_s`, fluid temperature `T_m`), the convective heat rate from
the surface into the fluid is `Q = h A ΔT`, with the dimension of a power. This relation is the
defining relation of `h`, a model input, not a derived heat-rate law.
