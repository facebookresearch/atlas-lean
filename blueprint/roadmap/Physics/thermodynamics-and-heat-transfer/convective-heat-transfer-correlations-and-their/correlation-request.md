# Correlation request: flow conditions with their application context

A correlation request consists of [flow conditions](flow-conditions.md) together with a
[flow configuration](flow-configuration.md), the hydrodynamic and the thermal
[development](flow-development.md) at the evaluated section, a
[property-variation class](property-variation.md), a requested
[local or averaged value](averaging-tag.md), and the [temperature](property-temperature.md) at which
the request's fluid properties were evaluated.
