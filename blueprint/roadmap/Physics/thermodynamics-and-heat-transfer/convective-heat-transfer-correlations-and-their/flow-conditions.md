# Flow conditions of a correlation request

The flow conditions of a correlation request consist of five real quantities: the Reynolds number
`Re`, the Prandtl number `Pr`, the ratio `L / D` of the heated length to the characteristic length
(for a duct, its diameter), the surface-minus-fluid temperature difference `ΔT = T_s - T_m`, a
temperature difference that also drives the heat rate `Q = h A ΔT`, and the bulk-to-surface
viscosity ratio `μ_m / μ_s`. No sign condition is imposed.
