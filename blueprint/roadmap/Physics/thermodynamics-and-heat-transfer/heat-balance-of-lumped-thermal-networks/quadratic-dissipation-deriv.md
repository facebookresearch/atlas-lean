# Dissipation identity of the quadratic functional at every time

Let `ι` be a finite index set of zones, with capacities `C i` of any sign, symmetric conductances
`G i j = G j i`, boundary conductances `g0 i` and a constant outside temperature `Tout`. Let the
zone temperatures `T i` have derivatives `Tv i(t)` and satisfy the zone equations
`C i Tv i(t) = ∑_j G i j (T j(t) - T i(t)) + g0 i (Tout - T i(t)) + P i(t)` at every time `t`. Then,
at every `t`, `V(s) = (1/2) ∑_i C i (T i(s) - Tout)²` has the derivative

`-((1/2) ∑_i ∑_j G i j (T j(t) - T i(t))² + ∑_i g0 i (T i(t) - Tout)²)`
`+ ∑_i (T i(t) - Tout) P i(t)`.
