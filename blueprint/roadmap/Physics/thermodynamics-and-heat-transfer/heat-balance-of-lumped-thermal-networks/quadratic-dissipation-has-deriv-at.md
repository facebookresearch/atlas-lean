# Dissipation identity of the quadratic functional at one time

Let `ι` be a finite index set of zones, with capacities `C i` of any sign, symmetric conductances
`G i j = G j i`, boundary conductances `g0 i` and a constant outside temperature `Tout`. Let the
zone temperatures `T i` have derivatives `Tv i(t)` at a time `t`, and let the zone equations hold at
`t`: `C i Tv i(t) = ∑_j G i j (T j(t) - T i(t)) + g0 i (Tout - T i(t)) + P i(t)` for every `i`. Then
`V(s) = (1/2) ∑_i C i (T i(s) - Tout)²` has at `t` the derivative

`-((1/2) ∑_i ∑_j G i j (T j(t) - T i(t))² + ∑_i g0 i (T i(t) - Tout)²)`
`+ ∑_i (T i(t) - Tout) P i(t)`:

edge dissipation, boundary squares and the input power pairing. No positivity of the capacities is
assumed, so `V` need not be a Lyapunov function.
