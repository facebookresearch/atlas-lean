# Affine profile across a layer

For real numbers `a`, `b` and `θ`, the affine profile between the face values `a` and `b` at the
normalised layer coordinate `θ` is `(1 - θ) a + θ b`. On `θ ∈ [0, 1]` it interpolates linearly
between `a` (at `θ = 0`) and `b` (at `θ = 1`); it is the model profile of one homogeneous layer,
not a solution of a field equation.
