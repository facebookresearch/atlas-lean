# Adjacent interface pressures differ by the flux times the resistance

Let `m < n` be natural numbers, `R : ℕ → ℝ` layer resistances and `p_in`, `p_out` real numbers. Then
the [interface pressures](vapor-interface-pressure.md) satisfy `p_m - p_{m+1} = g R_m`, with `g` the
[steady series flux](vapor-series-flux.md). The identity holds also for `m ≥ n`, where it compares
formal continuations.
