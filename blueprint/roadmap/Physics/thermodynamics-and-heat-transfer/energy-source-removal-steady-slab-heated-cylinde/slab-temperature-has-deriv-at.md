# Derivative of the slab temperature

For surface temperatures $T_s$, $T_b$, thickness $L$, conductivity $k$ and uniform volumetric source $H$, let $T(d) = T_s + \left(\frac{T_b - T_s}{L} + \frac{H L}{2k}\right) d - \frac{H d^2}{2k}$ be the steady slab temperature profile. Then at every point $d$, $T$ is differentiable with derivative the slab gradient $T'(d)$.
