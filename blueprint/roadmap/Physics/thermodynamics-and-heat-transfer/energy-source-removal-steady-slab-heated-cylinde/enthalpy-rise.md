# Coolant enthalpy rise equals the integrated linear power

Let $\dot m > 0$, $c_p > 0$ and $0 < L \le L_e$, let $q'(z) = q_0 \cos(\pi z / L_e)$, and let $T$ be a steady coolant profile on $[-L/2, L/2]$ with inlet temperature $T_{\mathrm{in}}$: $T(-L/2) = T_{\mathrm{in}}$ and, at every $z \in [-L/2, L/2]$, $T$ is differentiable with $\dot m\, c_p\, T'(z) = q'(z)$. Then

$$\dot m\, c_p\,\bigl(T(L/2) - T_{\mathrm{in}}\bigr) = \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} q'(z)\,dz \quad\text{and}\quad \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} q'(z)\,dz = \frac{2 q_0 L_e \sin(\pi L / (2 L_e))}{\pi}.$$
