# Center temperature of a heated cylinder

For a surface temperature $T_s$, radius $R$, conductivity $k$ and uniform volumetric source $q$, let $T(r) = T_s + q\,(R^2 - r^2)/(4k)$ be the steady cylinder temperature profile and $T'(r) = -q\,r/(2k)$ its radial gradient, with the convention $x/0 = 0$; the identity holds for all real parameters. Then $T(0) = T_s + q R^2/(4k)$.
