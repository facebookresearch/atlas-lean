# The slab profile satisfies the steady heat equation

For surface temperatures $T_s$, $T_b$, thickness $L$, conductivity $k$ and uniform volumetric source $H$, let $T(d) = T_s + \left(\frac{T_b - T_s}{L} + \frac{H L}{2k}\right) d - \frac{H d^2}{2k}$ be the steady slab temperature profile. If $k > 0$, then the slab gradient has derivative $-H/k$ at every point, and the steady balance $k\,T'' + H = 0$ holds.
