# Coolant outlet temperature

For mass flow $\dot m$, specific heat $c_p$, peak linear power $q_0$, extrapolated length $L_e$, heated length $L$ and inlet temperature $T_{\mathrm{in}}$, the coolant outlet temperature is

$$T_{\mathrm{out}} = T_{\mathrm{in}} + \frac{2 q_0 L_e \sin(\pi L / (2 L_e))}{\pi \dot m c_p}.$$

For $\dot m c_p = 0$ the formula takes the convention $x/0 = 0$ and gives $T_{\mathrm{out}} = T_{\mathrm{in}}$. For $L_e = 0$ the inner quotient $\pi L/(2 L_e)$ is $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$, so again $T_{\mathrm{out}} = T_{\mathrm{in}}$.
