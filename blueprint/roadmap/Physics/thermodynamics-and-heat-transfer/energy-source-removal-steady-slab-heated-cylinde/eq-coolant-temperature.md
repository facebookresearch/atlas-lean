# Uniqueness of the steady coolant temperature

Let $T$ be a steady coolant temperature on the heated length $[-L/2, L/2]$ with mass flow rate $\dot m > 0$, specific heat capacity $c_p > 0$ and inlet value $T_{in}$: $T(-L/2) = T_{in}$ and $\dot m\,c_p\,T'(z) = q_0 \cos(\pi z/L_e)$ at every point of the heated length. If $L_e > 0$, then on the heated length $T$ agrees with the integrated coolant temperature.
