# Heated-slab temperature profile

For surface temperatures $T_s$ and $T_b$, thickness $L$, conductivity $k$, uniform volumetric heat source $H$ and position $d$, the steady one-dimensional slab temperature profile is

$$T(d) = T_s + \left(\frac{T_b - T_s}{L} + \frac{H L}{2k}\right) d - \frac{H d^2}{2k}.$$

For $L = 0$ or $k = 0$ the corresponding quotients take the convention $x/0 = 0$.
