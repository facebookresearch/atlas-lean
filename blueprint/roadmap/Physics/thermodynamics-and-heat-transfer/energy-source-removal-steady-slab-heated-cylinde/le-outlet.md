# The coolant stays at or below its outlet temperature

Let $T$ be a steady coolant temperature on the heated length $[-L/2, L/2]$ with mass flow rate $\dot m > 0$, specific heat capacity $c_p > 0$ and inlet value $T_{in}$: $T(-L/2) = T_{in}$ and $\dot m\,c_p\,T'(z) = q_0 \cos(\pi z/L_e)$ at every point of the heated length. If $q_0 \ge 0$ and $0 < L \le L_e$, then $T(z) \le T_{out}$ for every $z$ in the heated length, where $T_{out}$ is the coolant outlet temperature.
