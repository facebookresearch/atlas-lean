# Integrated coolant temperature

The coolant temperature obtained by integrating $\dot m\,c_p\,T' = q_0\cos(\pi z/L_e)$ from the inlet $z = -L/2$ is $T(z) = T_{in} + \frac{q_0 L_e\,\big(\sin(\pi z/L_e) + \sin(\pi L/(2L_e))\big)}{\pi\,\dot m\,c_p}$. It is intended for $\dot m > 0$, $c_p > 0$ and $L_e > 0$; with the convention $x/0 = 0$ it is defined for all real parameters.
