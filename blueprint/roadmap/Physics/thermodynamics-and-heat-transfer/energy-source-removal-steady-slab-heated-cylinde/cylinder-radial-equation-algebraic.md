# Cylindrical Laplacian of the cylinder profile

For a surface temperature $T_s$, radius $R$, conductivity $k$ and uniform volumetric source $q$, let $T(r) = T_s + q\,(R^2 - r^2)/(4k)$ be the steady cylinder temperature profile and $T'(r) = -q\,r/(2k)$ its radial gradient. If $k \ne 0$ and $r > 0$, then $\frac{1}{r}\frac{d}{dr}\big(r\,T'(r)\big) = -q/k$.
