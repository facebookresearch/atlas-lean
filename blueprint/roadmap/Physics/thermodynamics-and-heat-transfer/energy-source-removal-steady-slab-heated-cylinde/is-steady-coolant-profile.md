# Steady coolant temperature along a heated channel

A function $T$ is a steady coolant temperature on the heated length $[-L/2, L/2]$ with mass flow rate $\dot m$, specific heat capacity $c_p$, linear power $q'(z) = q_0\cos(\pi z/L_e)$ and inlet value $T_{in}$ when $T(-L/2) = T_{in}$ and, at every point $z$ of the heated length, $T$ is differentiable with $\dot m\,c_p\,T'(z) = q'(z)$ (the linear power uses the convention $x/0 = 0$, so it is the constant $q_0$ when $L_e = 0$).
