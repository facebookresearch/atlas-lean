# With Tb = Ts, k > 0, H ≥ 0 the slab never exceeds its midplane value

Let `k > 0` and `H ≥ 0`, and let `0 ≤ d ≤ L`. With equal surface temperatures `Tb = Ts`, the
[slab temperature](slab-temperature.md) satisfies

`T(d) ≤ Ts + HL²/(8k)`,

the [midplane value](slab-temperature-midplane.md) `T(L/2)`: the midplane is a maximum of the
profile on the slab.
