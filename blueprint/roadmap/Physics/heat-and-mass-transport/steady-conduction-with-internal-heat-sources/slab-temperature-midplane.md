# With Tb = Ts and k ≠ 0 the midplane temperature is Ts + HL²/8k

Let `k ≠ 0`. For all real `Ts`, `L` and `H`, the [slab temperature](slab-temperature.md) with equal
surface temperatures `Tb = Ts`, `T(d) = Ts + (HL/(2k)) d - H d²/(2k)` (the term `(Tb - Ts)/L` is
then `0`), takes at `d = L/2` the value

`T(L/2) = Ts + HL²/(8k)`.
