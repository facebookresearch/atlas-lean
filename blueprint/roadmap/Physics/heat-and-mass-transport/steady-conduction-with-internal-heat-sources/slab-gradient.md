# Temperature gradient of the heated slab

For real `Ts`, `Tb`, `L`, `k`, `H` and `d`, the slab gradient is

`T′(d) = (Tb - Ts)/L + HL/(2k) - H d/k`,

the [derivative](slab-temperature-has-deriv-at.md) of the [slab temperature](slab-temperature.md).
With `x/0 = 0`, the term `(Tb - Ts)/L` is `0` when `L = 0` and the terms divided by `k` are `0` when
`k = 0`.
