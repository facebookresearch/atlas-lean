# The slab gradient is the derivative of the slab temperature

For all real `Ts`, `Tb`, `L`, `k`, `H` and `d`, the [slab temperature](slab-temperature.md)
`T(d) = Ts + ((Tb - Ts)/L + HL/(2k)) d - H d²/(2k)` has the derivative `T′(d)` given by the
[slab gradient](slab-gradient.md) `T′(d) = (Tb - Ts)/L + HL/(2k) - H d/k`, with the conventions of
those definitions when `L = 0` or `k = 0`.
