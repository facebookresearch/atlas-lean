# For k ≠ 0 the cylinder gradient is the derivative of the temperature

Let `k ≠ 0`. For all real `Ts`, `R`, `qvol` and `r`, the
[cylinder temperature](cylinder-temperature.md) `T(r) = Ts + qvol (R² - r²)/(4k)` has the derivative
`-qvol r/(2k)`, the [cylinder gradient](cylinder-gradient.md), at `r`.
