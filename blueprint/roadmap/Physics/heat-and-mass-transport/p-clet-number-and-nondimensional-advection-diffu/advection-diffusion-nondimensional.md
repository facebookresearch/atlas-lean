# For positive scales the scaled advection–diffusion equation has 1/Pe

Let the length, velocity, diffusivity and concentration scales `L`, `U`, `D` and `C₀` be positive,
and let `ct`, `cx`, `cxx`, `ĉt`, `ĉx` and `ĉxx` be real numbers related as the derivatives of `c`
and of the scaled `ĉ` at corresponding points under `x = L x̂`, `t = (L/U) t̂` and `c = C₀ ĉ`:

`ct = C₀ (U/L) ĉt`, `cx = (C₀/L) ĉx` and `cxx = (C₀/L²) ĉxx`.

If `ct + U cx = D cxx`, then

`ĉt + ĉx = (1/Pe) ĉxx` with the Péclet number `Pe = UL/D`.

The chain-rule relations are hypotheses here, not derived; the statement is the scaling algebra.
