# Temperature excess of a fin with an adiabatic tip

Let `k`, `A`, `h`, `P` and `L` be positive, and let `(T, q)` be a
[steady fin](is-fin-steady-state.md) on `[0, L]` with an adiabatic tip, `q(L) = 0`. Then, with
`θ(x) = T(x) - Ta` and the [fin parameter](fin-parameter.md) `m = √(hP/(kA))`, for every `x` with
`0 ≤ x ≤ L`,

`θ(x) = θ(0) cosh(m(L - x)) / cosh(mL)`.
