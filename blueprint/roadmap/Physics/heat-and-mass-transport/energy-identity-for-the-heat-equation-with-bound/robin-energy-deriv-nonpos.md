# D, L ≥ 0, k > 0 and h ≥ 0 at both ends: the Robin rate is nonpositive

Let `D ≥ 0`, `L ≥ 0`, `k > 0`, `h₀ ≥ 0` and `h_L ≥ 0`. For all functions `u` and `ux` of time and
position and all real `Ta` and `t`, the [Robin energy rate](robin-energy-has-deriv-at.md) satisfies

`-D ∫_0^L ux(t, x)² dx - (D/k) (h₀ (u(t, 0) - Ta)² + h_L (u(t, L) - Ta)²) ≤ 0`.

No integrability is needed: the integral of a nonnegative function over `[0, L]` is nonnegative, and
it is `0` for a non-integrable function.
