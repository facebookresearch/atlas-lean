# The linear slab profile meets both boundary temperatures

Let `L > 0` and let `T₀`, `T_L` be real numbers. Then the linear profile
`T(z) = T₀ + (T_L - T₀) z / L` satisfies `T(0) = T₀` and `T(L) = T_L`. It is the case of zero heat
production of the
[conductive geotherm boundary values](../../../EarthScience/solid-earth-geophysics/conductive-geotherms/geotherm-boundary.md).
