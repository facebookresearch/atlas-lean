# The linear slab profile has zero curvature and flux −k(T_L − T₀)/L

Let `L > 0` and let `T₀`, `T_L`, `k` and `z` be real numbers. Then the linear profile
`T(z) = T₀ + (T_L - T₀) z / L` has second derivative `0` at `z`, so it solves steady one-dimensional
conduction without heat production, and its Fourier heat flux is `-k T'(z) = -k (T_L - T₀) / L`. In
the physical reading `k > 0` is the thermal conductivity and `z` runs across the slab `[0, L]`; the
statement holds for all real `k` and `z`.
