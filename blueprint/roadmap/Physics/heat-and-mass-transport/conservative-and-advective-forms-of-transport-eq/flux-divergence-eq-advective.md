# Where ∂U/∂x = 0, the flux divergence equals the advective operator

Let `U, c : ℝ → ℝ` and `D`, `x` be real. Suppose `U` has derivative `0` at `x` (the one-dimensional
continuity equation for the carrier), `c` is differentiable at `x`, and `c′` is differentiable at
`x`. Then the flux `J = U c - D c′` satisfies

`J′(x) = U(x) c′(x) - D c″(x)`.

Without `U′(x) = 0` the product rule leaves the extra term `c(x) U′(x)`.
