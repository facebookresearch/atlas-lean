# Stored-energy flow of a battery on one schedule interval

For efficiencies `η_c`, `η_d`, interval durations `dt`, charge powers `P_c` and discharge powers
`P_d`, all indexed by the schedule interval `k`, the battery flow of interval `k` is
`dt_k (η_c P_{c,k} - P_{d,k} / η_d)`, the change of stored energy over the interval. With the
convention `u / 0 = 0`, `P_{d,k} / η_d = 0` when `η_d = 0`.
