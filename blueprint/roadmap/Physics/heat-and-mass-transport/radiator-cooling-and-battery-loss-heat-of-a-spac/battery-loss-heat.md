# Heat dissipated by a battery

For efficiencies `η_c`, `η_d` and terminal charge and discharge powers `P_c`, `P_d`, the battery
loss heat is `q = (1 - η_c) P_c + (1/η_d - 1) P_d`. In the physical reading `P_c, P_d ≥ 0` and
`0 < η_c, η_d ≤ 1`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `1/η_d = 0` when `η_d = 0`.
