# Battery port balance: terminal power is storage power plus loss heat

For all real `η_c`, `η_d`, `P_c` and `P_d`, the terminal power `P_c - P_d` absorbed by the battery
is the storage power `η_c P_c - P_d / η_d` plus the [battery loss heat](battery-loss-heat.md):

`P_c - P_d = (η_c P_c - P_d / η_d) + q`.
