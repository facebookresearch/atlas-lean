# Temperature rate of a lumped radiator

For a heat capacity `C`, a radiation coefficient `K_rad`, a constant heat input `Q` and a
temperature `T`, the radiator rate is `(Q - K_rad T⁴) / C`: the lumped radiator model
`C T' = Q - K_rad T⁴` reads `T' = (Q - K_rad T⁴) / C` for `C ≠ 0`. In the physical reading `C > 0`,
`K_rad > 0` and `T ≥ 0` is absolute; with the convention `u / 0 = 0` the rate is `0` when `C = 0`.
