# Temperature rate of a lumped radiator

For a heat capacity `C`, a radiation coefficient `K_rad`, a constant heat input `Q` and a
temperature `T`, the radiator rate is `(Q - K_rad T⁴) / C`, the right side of `C T' = Q - K_rad T⁴`.
In the physical reading `C > 0`, `K_rad > 0` and `T ≥ 0` is absolute; with the convention
`u / 0 = 0` the rate is `0` when `C = 0`.
