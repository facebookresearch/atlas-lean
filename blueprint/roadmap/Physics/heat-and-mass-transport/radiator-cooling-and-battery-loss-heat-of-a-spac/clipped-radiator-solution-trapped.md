# Clipped-field radiator solutions starting in an inward box stay in it

Let `C > 0`, `T_min ≤ T_max`, and let `K_rad`, `Q` satisfy the inward end conditions
`Q - K_rad T_min⁴ ≥ 0` and `Q - K_rad T_max⁴ ≤ 0`. Let `T : ℝ → ℝ` have, at every `s ≥ 0`, the
derivative within `[0, ∞)` given by the [clipped radiator rate](clipped-radiator-rate.md) at `T(s)`,
with `T(0) ∈ [T_min, T_max]`. Then `T(t) ∈ [T_min, T_max]` for every `t ≥ 0`. No sign condition on
`K_rad` or `T_min` is needed.
