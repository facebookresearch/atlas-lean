# The clipped radiator rate is globally Lipschitz

Let `C > 0`, `K_rad > 0` and `0 ≤ T_min ≤ T_max`, and let `Q` be real. Then for all real `a` and
`b`, the [clipped radiator rate](clipped-radiator-rate.md) `f` satisfies

`|f(a) - f(b)| ≤ (4 K_rad T_max³ / C) |a - b|`.

The unclipped quartic rate is not globally Lipschitz.
