# On the box, a clipped-field solution solves the radiator equation

Let `C`, `K_rad`, `Q`, `T_min`, `T_max` and `s` be real and `T : ℝ → ℝ` have at `s` the derivative
within `[0, ∞)` given by the [clipped radiator rate](clipped-radiator-rate.md) at `T(s)`. If
`T(s) ∈ [T_min, T_max]`, then `T` has at `s` the derivative within `[0, ∞)` given by the
[radiator rate](radiator-rate.md) at `T(s)`.
