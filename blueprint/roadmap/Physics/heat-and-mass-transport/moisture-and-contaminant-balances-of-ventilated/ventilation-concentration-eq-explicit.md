# For V, Q > 0 the concentration is equilibrium plus a decaying excess

Let `V > 0` and `Q > 0`. For all real `c_out`, `G`, `c₀` and `t`, the
[ventilation concentration](ventilation-concentration.md) `c(t)` is

`c(t) = c_eq + (c₀ - c_eq) e^{-(Q/V)t}`,

with the [equilibrium](ventilation-equilibrium.md) `c_eq = c_out + G/Q`.
