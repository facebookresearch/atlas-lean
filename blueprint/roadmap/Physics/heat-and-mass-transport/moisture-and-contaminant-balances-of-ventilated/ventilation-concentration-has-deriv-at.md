# For V ≠ 0 the concentration solves the well-mixed balance

Let `V ≠ 0`. For all real `Q`, `c_out`, `G`, `c₀` and `t`, the
[ventilation concentration](ventilation-concentration.md) `c(t)` has at `t` the derivative

`c′(t) = (Q/V)(c_out - c(t)) + G/V`:

the flow term plus the indoor source term, the balance of a continuously stirred tank without
reaction.
