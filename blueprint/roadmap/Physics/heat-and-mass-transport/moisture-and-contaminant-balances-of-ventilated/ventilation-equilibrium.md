# Equilibrium concentration of a well-mixed ventilated room

For a volumetric flow `Q`, an outdoor concentration `c_out` and a constant indoor source `G` (mass
per time), the ventilation equilibrium is

`c_eq = c_out + G/Q`.

It is meant for `Q > 0`; with `x/0 = 0` it is `c_out` when `Q = 0`, where it is not the equilibrium
of the balance. Concentrations are mass per volume in one consistent unit; a ppm figure must be
converted first.
