# Sine coefficients of a uniform initial concentration

For real `c₀` and `L` and a natural number `n`, the sine coefficient is `b₀ = 0` and, for `n ≥ 1`,

`bₙ = 2c₀ (1 - (-1)ⁿ)/(nπ)`:

`4c₀/(nπ)` for odd `n` and `0` for even `n`. It does not depend on `L`. For `L > 0` and `n ≥ 1` it
equals the [sine-coefficient integral](fourier-coeff-formula.md) `(2/L) ∫_0^L c₀ sin(nπx/L) dx`.
