# Spatial derivative of the error-function profile for D, t > 0

Let `D > 0` and `t > 0`. For all real `cs` and `x`, the [error-function profile](erfc-profile.md)
`C(x, t) = cs · erfc(x/√(4Dt))` has, as a function of `x`, the derivative

`∂C/∂x(x, t) = (cs/√(4Dt)) · erfc′(x/√(4Dt))`,

with [`erfc′(y) = -(2/√π) exp(-y²)`](erfc-deriv.md).
