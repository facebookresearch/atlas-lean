# Fick's first law: the flux is −D times the concentration gradient

For a real diffusivity `D` and a concentration field `c(x, t)` of position `x` and time `t`, the
Fick flux at `(x, t)` is

`J(x, t) = -D ∂c/∂x(x, t)`.

The derivative is the total one: it is `0` where `c(·, t)` is not differentiable at `x`, so results
about the flux assume that differentiability separately. In the physical reading `D > 0`.
