# Heat transfer coefficient from a Nusselt number: h = Nu k / L

For a real Nusselt number `Nu`, a fluid thermal conductivity `k` and a length `L > 0`, the heat
transfer coefficient is `h = Nu k / L`, with the dimension of a heat transfer coefficient (power per
area and temperature difference). It inverts the Nusselt group `Nu = h L / k`
([inverse](nusselt-number-heat-transfer-coefficient.md)).
