# Through the guard, a power law gives h > 0 and Q of the sign of ΔT

Let `c > 0`, let a [power-law correlation](power-law-correlation.md) with coefficient `c` apply to a
request `r`, and let `k > 0`, `L > 0` and `A > 0`. Then the
[heat transfer coefficient](heat-transfer-coefficient.md) `h = Nu k / L` built from its
[Nusselt number](nusselt.md) for `r` is positive, and the
[convective heat rate](convective-heat-rate.md) `Q = h A ΔT` at the request's own temperature
difference `ΔT` has the sign of `ΔT`. The positivity of `Re` and `Pr` comes from the validity
domain.
