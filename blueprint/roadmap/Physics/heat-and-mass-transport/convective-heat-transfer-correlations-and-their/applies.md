# When a Nusselt correlation applies to a request

A [Nusselt correlation](nusselt-correlation.md) applies to a [request](correlation-request.md) when
the request has the correlation's flow configuration, hydrodynamic development, thermal development
and property-variation class, asks for the correlation's local or averaged value, has its fluid
properties evaluated at the correlation's property temperature, and has its flow conditions in the
correlation's validity domain.
