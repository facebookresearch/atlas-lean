# Power-law Nusselt correlation Nu = c Re^a Pr^b

For real numbers `c`, `a` and `b`, a citation, a [flow configuration](flow-configuration.md),
hydrodynamic and thermal [developments](flow-development.md), a
[property-variation class](property-variation.md), a [local or averaged tag](averaging-tag.md), a
[property temperature](property-temperature.md) and a nonempty validity domain of
[flow conditions](flow-conditions.md) on which `Re > 0` and `Pr > 0`, the power-law correlation is
the [Nusselt correlation](nusselt-correlation.md) with these data and the formula
`Nu = c Re^a Pr^b`. The powers are real powers; since `Re` and `Pr` are positive on the domain, no
convention for negative bases is used.
