# Equal-area plates with the idealized view factors form an enclosure

For `A > 0`, two plates of area `A` with the
[parallel-plate view factors](parallel-plate-view-factor.md) form a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md): the areas are positive, the view factors are nonnegative
with unit row sums, and reciprocity `A F_{01} = A F_{10}` holds because the areas are equal.
