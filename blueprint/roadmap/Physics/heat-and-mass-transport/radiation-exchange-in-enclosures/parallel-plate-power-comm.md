# Exchanging both plates, or only the temperatures, negates the power

For all real `σ`, `A`, `ε₁`, `ε₂`, `T₁` and `T₂`, exchanging the roles of the plates (both
emissivities and temperatures) negates the [parallel-plate power](parallel-plate-power.md):
`P(σ, A, ε₂, ε₁, T₂, T₁) = -P(σ, A, ε₁, ε₂, T₁, T₂)`.

Equivalently, exchanging only the two temperatures, with the emissivities kept, also negates it:
`P(σ, A, ε₁, ε₂, T₂, T₁) = -P(σ, A, ε₁, ε₂, T₁, T₂)`, since the denominator `1/ε₁ + 1/ε₂ - 1` is
symmetric in the emissivities.
