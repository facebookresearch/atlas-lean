# Gray net power is radiosity minus irradiation, times the area

Let `ι` be a finite set of surfaces with areas `A` and view factors `F` forming a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md). For every radiosity field `J` and surface `i`, the
[gray net power](gray-net-power.md) is `q_i = A_i (J_i - ∑_j F_{ij} J_j)`.
