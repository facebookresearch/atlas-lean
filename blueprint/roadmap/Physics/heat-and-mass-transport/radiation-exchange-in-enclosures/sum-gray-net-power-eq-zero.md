# The gray net powers of an enclosure sum to zero

Let `ι` be a finite set of surfaces with areas `A` and view factors `F` forming a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md). For every radiosity field `J`, `∑_i q_i = 0`, where `q_i`
is the [gray net power](gray-net-power.md). Only reciprocity is used.
