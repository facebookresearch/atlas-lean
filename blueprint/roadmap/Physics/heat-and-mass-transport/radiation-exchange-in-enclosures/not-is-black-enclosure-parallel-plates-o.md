# For unequal areas, the idealized plate view factors break reciprocity

For areas `A₀ ≠ A₁`, the [parallel-plate view factors](parallel-plate-view-factor.md) do not form a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md) with these areas: reciprocity `A₀ F_{01} = A₁ F_{10}`
fails. So the parallel-plate formula is not a general two-surface enclosure formula.
