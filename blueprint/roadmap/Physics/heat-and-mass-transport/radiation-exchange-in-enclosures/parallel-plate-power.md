# Net power between two parallel gray plates

For real `σ`, `A`, `ε₁`, `ε₂`, `T₁` and `T₂`, the parallel-plate power is

`σ A (T₁⁴ - T₂⁴) / (1/ε₁ + 1/ε₂ - 1)`,

the net power from plate 1 to plate 2 of two equal-area parallel gray plates in the infinite-plane
idealization, with area `A`, emissivities `ε₁`, `ε₂` and absolute temperatures `T₁`, `T₂`; positive
values are a net outflow from plate 1. It is a power through the area `A`, not a flux. For
`0 < ε₁, ε₂ ≤ 1` the denominator is at least `1`. The formula is defined for all real inputs, with
the convention `u / 0 = 0` (so `1/ε = 0` at `ε = 0`, and the value is `0` when the denominator
vanishes).
