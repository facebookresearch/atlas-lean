# A nonempty enclosure with zero emissivities has no unique radiosity

Let `ι` be a finite set of surfaces with areas `A` and view factors `F` forming a
[black enclosure](is-black-enclosure.md), with at least one surface, and let every emissivity be
`0`. Then for every `σ` and all temperatures `T`, the [radiosity equations](is-radiosity.md) do not
have a unique solution: positive emissivity cannot be dropped from the
[uniqueness result](exists-unique-is-radiosity.md).
