# For unequal capacity rates, the counterflow constant in closed form

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `K ≥ 0`, `L ≥ 0` and `C_h ≠ C_c`, and let `T_hi`, `T_ci` be real. Then the
[boundary constant](counterflow-inlet-diff.md) is

`D₀ = (C_c - C_h)(T_hi - T_ci) / (C_c - C_h exp(-s L))`,

with `s` the [counterflow rate](counterflow-rate.md); the denominator does not vanish.
