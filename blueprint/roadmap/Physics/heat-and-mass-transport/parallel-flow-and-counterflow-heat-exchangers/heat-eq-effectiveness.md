# Parallel-flow duty in effectiveness form: Q = ε_p C_min (T_hi − T_ci)

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `L > 0`, and let `UA`, `T_hi`, `T_ci` be real. If `(T_h, T_c)` is a
[parallel-flow solution](is-parallel-flow-solution.md) on `[0, L]` with `K = UA / L`, then, with
`C_min = min(C_h, C_c)`, the [capacity-rate ratio](capacity-ratio.md) `C_r` and the
[number of transfer units](transfer-units.md) `NTU = UA / C_min`,

`C_h (T_hi - T_h(L)) = C_c (T_c(L) - T_ci) = ε_p(NTU, C_r) C_min (T_hi - T_ci)`,

with `ε_p` the [parallel-flow effectiveness](parallel-effectiveness.md). No sign condition on `UA`
is needed.
