# Equal capacity rates C: counterflow duty UA(T_hi − T_ci)/(1 + UA/C)

Let `C > 0`, `UA ≥ 0` and `L > 0`, and let `(T_h, T_c)` be a
[counterflow solution](is-counterflow-solution.md) on `[0, L]` with both capacity rates equal to `C`
and `K = UA / L`. Then the heat lost by the hot stream and the heat gained by the cold stream are
both

`C (T_hi - T_h(L)) = C (T_c(0) - T_ci) = UA (T_hi - T_ci) / (1 + UA / C)`.
