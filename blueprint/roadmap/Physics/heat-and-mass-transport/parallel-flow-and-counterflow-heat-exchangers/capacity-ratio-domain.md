# Positive capacity rates give 0 < C_r ≤ 1, and UA ≥ 0 gives NTU ≥ 0

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0` and `UA ≥ 0`. Then the [capacity-rate ratio](capacity-ratio.md) satisfies
`0 < C_r ≤ 1`, with `C_r = 1` if and only if `C_h = C_c`, and the
[number of transfer units](transfer-units.md) satisfies `NTU ≥ 0`.
