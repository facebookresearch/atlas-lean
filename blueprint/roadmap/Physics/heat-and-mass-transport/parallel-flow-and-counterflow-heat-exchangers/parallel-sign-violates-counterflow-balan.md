# With the parallel-flow sign, the counterflow port balance fails

Let `C_h > 0` and `L ≥ 0`, let `C_c` and `K` be real, and let `T_h, T_c : ℝ → ℝ` satisfy, at every
`x ∈ [0, L]` and within `[0, L]`, `(C_h T_h)' = -K (T_h - T_c)` and `(C_c T_c)' = K (T_h - T_c)`
(the cold equation with the parallel-flow sign). If `T_h(L) ≠ T_h(0)`, then

`C_h (T_h(0) - T_h(L)) ≠ C_c (T_c(0) - T_c(L))`:

the counterflow port balance fails whenever the hot temperature changes between the ends.
