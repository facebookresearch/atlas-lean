# Counterflow hot-stream bounds for K, L ≥ 0 and T_hi > T_ci

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `K ≥ 0`, `L ≥ 0` and `T_ci < T_hi`, and let `(T_h, T_c)` be a
[counterflow solution](is-counterflow-solution.md) on `[0, L]`. Then the hot outlet `T_h(L)` is
above the cold inlet, `T_ci < T_h(L)`, and `T_h(L) ≤ T_h(x) ≤ T_hi` for every `x ∈ [0, L]`. No order
between the outlets `T_h(L)` and `T_c(0)` is asserted: a temperature cross `T_c(0) > T_h(L)` is
allowed.
