# For UA > 0 and T_hi > T_ci, the parallel-flow outlets are ordered

Let `C_h > 0`, `C_c > 0`, `L > 0`, `UA > 0` and `T_ci < T_hi`. If `(T_h, T_c)` is a
[parallel-flow solution](is-parallel-flow-solution.md) on `[0, L]` with `K = UA / L`, then
`T_ci < T_c(L) < T_h(L) < T_hi`: the cold outlet stays strictly below the hot outlet.
