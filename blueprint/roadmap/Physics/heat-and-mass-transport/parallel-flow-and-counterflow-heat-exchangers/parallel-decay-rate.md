# Decay rate of the stream temperature difference in parallel flow

For real numbers `C_h`, `C_c` (capacity rates of the hot and cold streams) and `K` (conductance per
unit length), the parallel-flow decay rate is `r = K (1/C_h + 1/C_c)`. In the physical reading
`C_h, C_c > 0` and `K ≥ 0`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `1/C = 0` when a capacity rate is `0`.
