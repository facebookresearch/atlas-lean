# Mean decibel loss overestimates effective two-patch loss

Take two patches of equal
area $A>0$ at the same incident intensity $I_0>0$, with distinct positive coefficients $t_1,t_2$.
Let $T$ be total transmitted power divided by total incident power and put $L(t)=-10\log_{10}t$.
Then $L(T)<(L(t_1)+L(t_2))/2$.
