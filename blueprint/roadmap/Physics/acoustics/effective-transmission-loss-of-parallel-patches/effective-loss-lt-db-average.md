# Loss of the mean coefficient versus mean decibel loss

For distinct positive coefficients
$t_1,t_2$, put $L(t)=-10\log_{10}t$. Then $L((t_1+t_2)/2)<(L(t_1)+L(t_2))/2$.
For example, equal areas at the same incident intensity with coefficients $0.001$ and $0.1$
have an effective loss of about $12.97$ dB, not the $20$ dB average of the two patch losses.
