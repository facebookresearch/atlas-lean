# Bounds on effective loss for two parallel patches

Let both patches have the same area
$A>0$ and incident intensity $I_0>0$, with transmission coefficients $t_1,t_2>0$. Write $T$
for total transmitted power divided by total incident power and $L(t)=-10\log_{10}t$.
Then $L(\max(t_1,t_2))\le L(T)\le L(\min(t_1,t_2))$.
