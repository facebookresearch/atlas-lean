# Effective transmission loss of parallel patches

For two patches of equal positive area at the same positive incident intensity, the effective
transmission coefficient is the mean of the two coefficients. For distinct positive coefficients,
converting that mean to decibels gives a lower loss than averaging the two patch losses.
The first two results bound the loss of a mean; the last three describe the two-patch power ratio.
