# Duality of the reciprocal basis: aᵢ · bⱼ = 2π δᵢⱼ

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `b₁`, `b₂`, `b₃` be the
primitive vectors of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). Then for all indices `i` and `j`,
`aᵢ · bⱼ = 2π` if `i = j` and `aᵢ · bⱼ = 0` otherwise. Equivalently, since the dot product is
symmetric, `bᵢ · aⱼ = 2π δᵢⱼ` for all `i` and `j`.
