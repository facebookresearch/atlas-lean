# The first Brillouin zone has volume (2π)³ over the cell volume

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`). Then the
[first Brillouin zone](first-brillouin-zone.md) of `Λ` has Lebesgue volume `(2π)³ / |det B|`, the
volume of a primitive cell of the [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md).
