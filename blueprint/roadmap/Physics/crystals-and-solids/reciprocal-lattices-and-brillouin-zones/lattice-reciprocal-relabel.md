# The reciprocal lattice does not depend on the primitive vectors

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `P` be an integer
`3 × 3` matrix with `det P = ±1`. Then the [relabelled lattice](../bravais-lattices/relabel.md),
with basis matrix `B P` (the same lattice with other primitive vectors), has the same reciprocal
[lattice](../bravais-lattices/lattice.md) as `Λ`: the set of vectors of the
[reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md) does not change when the primitive vectors are changed by a
unimodular integer matrix.
