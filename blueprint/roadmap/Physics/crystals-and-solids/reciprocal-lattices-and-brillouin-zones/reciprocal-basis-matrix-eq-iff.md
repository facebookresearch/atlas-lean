# The reciprocal basis is the only basis 2π-dual to the lattice basis

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `B*` be the basis
matrix of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). For every real `3 × 3` matrix `M`,

`B* = M` if and only if `Bᵀ M = 2π · 1`.
