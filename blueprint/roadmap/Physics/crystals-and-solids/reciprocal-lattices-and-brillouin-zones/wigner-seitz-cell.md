# Wigner–Seitz cell of a Bravais lattice

For a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) `Λ`, the Wigner–Seitz cell is the
set of points `x ∈ ℝ³` with `‖x‖ ≤ ‖x - R‖` for every vector `R` of the
[lattice](../bravais-lattices/lattice.md): the points at least as close to the origin as to any
lattice point, for the Euclidean norm. Points on the boundary, equidistant from the origin and some
other lattice point, are included.
