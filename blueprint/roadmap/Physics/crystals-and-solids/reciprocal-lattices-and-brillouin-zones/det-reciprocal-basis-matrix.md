# Oriented volume of the reciprocal cell: det B* = (2π)³ / det B

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `B*` be the basis
matrix of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). Then `det B* = (2π)³ / det B`.
