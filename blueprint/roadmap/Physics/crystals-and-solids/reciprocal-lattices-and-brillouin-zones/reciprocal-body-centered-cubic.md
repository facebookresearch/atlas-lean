# The reciprocal of bcc of side a is fcc of side 4π/a

For `a > 0`, the [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md) of the
[body-centred cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic.md) with cubic cell of side `a` is the
[face-centred cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic.md) with cubic cell of side `4π / a`, as Bravais
lattices (same basis matrix).
