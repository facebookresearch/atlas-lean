# Basis matrix of the reciprocal lattice

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`). The basis matrix of the
[reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md) of `Λ` is `(2π / det B) C`, with `C` the
[cross-column matrix](cross-columns.md) of `B`.
