# Triple products of the columns give det B times the identity

For every real `3 × 3` matrix `B` with columns `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃`, and `C` its
[cross-column matrix](cross-columns.md),

`Bᵀ C = det(B) · 1`,

that is, `aᵢ · (a_{j+1} × a_{j+2})` is `det B` when `i = j` and `0` otherwise (indices modulo `3`).
