# The Wigner–Seitz cell is a fundamental domain for lattice translations

For every [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md), the
[Wigner–Seitz cell](wigner-seitz-cell.md) is a fundamental domain for the translations by vectors of
the [lattice](../bravais-lattices/lattice.md), for Lebesgue measure on `ℝ³`: it is measurable up to
a null set, almost every point of `ℝ³` has a lattice translate in the cell, and the translates of
the cell by distinct lattice vectors overlap only in sets of measure zero.
