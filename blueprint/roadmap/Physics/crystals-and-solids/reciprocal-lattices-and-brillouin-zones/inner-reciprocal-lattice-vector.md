# Reciprocal and direct lattice vectors pair to a multiple of 2π

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `B*` be the basis
matrix of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). For all integer vectors `m, n ∈ ℤ³`, the
[lattice vectors](../bravais-lattices/lattice-vector.md) `B* m` and `B n` satisfy

`(B* m) · (B n) = 2π (m₁ n₁ + m₂ n₂ + m₃ n₃)`.
