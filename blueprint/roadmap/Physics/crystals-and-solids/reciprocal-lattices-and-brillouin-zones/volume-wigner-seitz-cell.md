# The Wigner–Seitz cell has volume equal to the absolute determinant

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`). Then the
[Wigner–Seitz cell](wigner-seitz-cell.md) of `Λ` has Lebesgue volume `|det B|`, the volume of a
primitive cell.
