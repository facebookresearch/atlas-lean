# The oriented cell volume is the triple product a₁ · (a₂ × a₃)

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`). Then `det B = a₁ · (a₂ × a₃)`:
the oriented volume of the primitive cell is the scalar triple product of the primitive vectors.
