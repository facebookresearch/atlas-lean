# Lebesgue volume of the reciprocal primitive cell

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `b₁`, `b₂`, `b₃` be the
primitive vectors of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). Then the parallelepiped spanned by
`b₁`, `b₂`, `b₃` has Lebesgue volume `(2π)³ / |det B|`.
