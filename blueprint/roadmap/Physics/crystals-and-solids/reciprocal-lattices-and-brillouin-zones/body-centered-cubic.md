# Body-centred cubic lattice with cubic cell of side a

For `a > 0`, the body-centred cubic lattice whose conventional cubic cell has side `a` is the
[Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with primitive vectors
`(a / 2) (-1, 1, 1)`, `(a / 2) (1, -1, 1)` and `(a / 2) (1, 1, -1)`.
