# A reciprocal lattice vector pairs with aⱼ to 2π mⱼ

Let `Λ` be a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, whose
columns are the primitive vectors `a₁`, `a₂`, `a₃` (so `det B ≠ 0`), and let `B*` be the basis
matrix of its [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal.md). For every integer vector `m ∈ ℤ³` and every index
`j`, the reciprocal [lattice vector](../bravais-lattices/lattice-vector.md) `B* m` satisfies

`(B* m) · aⱼ = 2π mⱼ`.
