# Density of a synthetic four-atom cubic cell

For `N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³` (the exact SI value of the Avogadro constant), the
[density expression](theoretical-density-raw.md) `n A / (V_c N_A)` with `n = 4`, `A = 64` and
`V_c = 64 × 10⁻²⁴` satisfies `|n A / (V_c N_A) − 6.64216| < 0.001`: four atoms of molar mass
`64 g/mol` in a cubic cell of edge `4 Å` give a density within `0.001` of `6.64216 g/cm³`. The cell
is a synthetic example, not a real metal.
