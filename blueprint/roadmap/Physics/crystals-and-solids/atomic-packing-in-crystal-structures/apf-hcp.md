# Hexagonal close-packed packing factor π / (3√2)

For `R > 0`, `a = 2R` (spheres touching in the basal plane), the hexagonal-prism cell volume
`V_c = (3√3 / 2) a² c` and the ideal ratio `c = a √(8/3)`, the
[packing-factor expression](atomic-packing-factor-raw.md) `n (4/3) π R³ / V_c` with `n = 6` equals
`π / (3√2)`, the face-centred cubic value.
