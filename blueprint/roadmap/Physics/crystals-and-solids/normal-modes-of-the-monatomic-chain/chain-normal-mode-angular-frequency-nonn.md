# The normal-mode angular frequencies of the chain are nonnegative

Let $m > 0$ and $K \ge 0$ be real, the mass and the spring constant, and let $N \ge 1$ be a natural
number, the number of masses. For every natural number $j$, the angular frequency
$\omega_j = 2 \sqrt{K/m}\, |\sin(\pi j / N)|$ of
[normal mode](chain-normal-mode-angular-frequency.md) $j$ of the periodic monatomic chain is
nonnegative: $\omega_j \ge 0$.
