# For j ≤ N, chain modes N − j and j have equal angular frequencies

Let $m > 0$ and $K \ge 0$ be real, the mass and the spring constant, and let $N \ge 1$ be a natural
number, the number of masses. For every natural number $j \le N$, the
[normal modes](chain-normal-mode-angular-frequency.md) $N - j$ and $j$ of the periodic monatomic
chain have the same angular frequency, $\omega_{N - j} = \omega_j$, since $\sin(\pi - x) = \sin x$.

The condition $j \le N$ is needed: $N - j$ is a difference of natural numbers, truncated to $0$ when
$j > N$, and $\omega_0 = 0$ differs from $\omega_j$ whenever $K > 0$ and $\sin(\pi j / N) \ne 0$.
