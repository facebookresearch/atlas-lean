# Normal modes of the monatomic chain

The normal-mode angular frequencies $\omega_j = 2 \sqrt{K/m}\, |\sin(\pi j / N)|$ of a periodic
chain of $N \ge 1$ masses $m > 0$ joined by springs of constant $K \ge 0$, taken as the textbook
dispersion relation, and their basic properties.
