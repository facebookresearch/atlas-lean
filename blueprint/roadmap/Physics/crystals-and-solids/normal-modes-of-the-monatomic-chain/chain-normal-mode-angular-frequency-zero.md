# The mode j = 0 of the monatomic chain has angular frequency zero

Let $m > 0$ and $K \ge 0$ be real, the mass and the spring constant, and let $N \ge 1$ be a natural
number, the number of masses. The [normal mode](chain-normal-mode-angular-frequency.md) $j = 0$ of
the periodic monatomic chain has angular frequency $\omega_0 = 2 \sqrt{K/m}\, |\sin 0| = 0$.
