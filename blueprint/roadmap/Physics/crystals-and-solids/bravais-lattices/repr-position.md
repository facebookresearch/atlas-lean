# Primitive coordinates of an atomic position

Let a [crystal](bravais-crystal.md) with finite nonempty motif `F` have basis matrix `B`
(`det B ≠ 0`), lattice `L` and offsets `u_f` (`f ∈ F`). For all `n ∈ ℤ³` and `f ∈ F`, the
coordinates of the position `B n + u_f` in the [primitive basis](basis.md) are `n` plus the
coordinates of `u_f`.
