# Primitive coordinates of an atomic position

Let a [crystal with motif](bravais-crystal.md) `F` have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`), lattice `L`
and offsets `u_f` (`f ∈ F`). For all `n ∈ ℤ³` and `f ∈ F`, the coordinates of the position
`B n + u_f` in the [primitive basis](basis.md) are `n` plus the coordinates of `u_f`.
