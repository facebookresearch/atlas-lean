# Lattice of a Bravais lattice

Let a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`) and primitive
vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`. The lattice `L` is the set of integer combinations `n₀ a₀ + n₁ a₁ + n₂ a₂`
(`nᵢ ∈ ℤ`) of the primitive vectors, the subgroup of three-dimensional Euclidean space they
generate. It is a discrete subgroup of full rank.
