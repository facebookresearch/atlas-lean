# Change of basis transforms coordinates by the inverse matrix

Let `e` and `e'` be bases of three-dimensional Euclidean space and let `P` be the change-of-basis
matrix whose `j`-th column holds the coordinates of `e'_j` in the basis `e`. For every vector `x`,
the coordinates of `x` in `e'` are `P⁻¹` times its coordinates in `e`. The matrix `P` is invertible,
so `P⁻¹` is its inverse.
