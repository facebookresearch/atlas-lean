# With offsets distinct modulo the lattice, sites have distinct places

Let a [crystal](bravais-crystal.md) with finite nonempty motif `F` have basis matrix `B`
(`det B ≠ 0`), lattice `L` and offsets `u_f` (`f ∈ F`). Suppose that the offsets are pairwise
distinct modulo the lattice: `u_f - u_g ∈ L` only for `f = g`. Then distinct sites of the
[abstract crystal](physlib-crystal.md) have distinct [positions](site-position.md).
