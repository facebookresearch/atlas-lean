# Primitive basis of a Bravais lattice

For a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md), the primitive basis is the basis `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂` of
three-dimensional Euclidean space formed by the [primitive vectors](primitive-vector.md), which are
[linearly independent](linear-independent-primitive-vector.md) and three in number.
