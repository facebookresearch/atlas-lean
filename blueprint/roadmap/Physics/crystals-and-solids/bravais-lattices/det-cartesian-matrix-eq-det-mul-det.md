# Change of basis multiplies the determinant by det P

Let `e` and `e'` be bases of three-dimensional Euclidean space, with basis matrices `B` and `B'`
(see [basis matrix](cartesian-matrix.md)), and let `P` be the change-of-basis matrix whose `j`-th
column holds the coordinates of `e'_j` in `e`. Then `det B' = det P · det B`: the oriented volume
changes by the factor `det P`.
