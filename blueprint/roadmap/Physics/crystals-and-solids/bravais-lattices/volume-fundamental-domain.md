# The half-open fundamental cell has volume the absolute determinant

Let a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`) and primitive
vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`. The half-open fundamental cell `{t₀ a₀ + t₁ a₁ + t₂ a₂ : 0 ≤ tᵢ < 1}`, the
points whose primitive coordinates lie in `[0, 1)`, has Lebesgue volume `|det B|`.
