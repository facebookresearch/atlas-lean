# A unimodular relabeling preserves the cell volume

For every [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) and an integer `3 × 3` matrix `P` with `det P = ±1`
(so that `P` has an integer inverse), the parallelepipeds spanned by the primitive vectors of the
[relabeled lattice](relabel.md) and of the original lattice have the same volume.
