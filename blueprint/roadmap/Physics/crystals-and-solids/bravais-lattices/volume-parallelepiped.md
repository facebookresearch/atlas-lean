# Volume of the parallelepiped spanned by three vectors

For all vectors `v₀`, `v₁`, `v₂` of three-dimensional Euclidean space, the parallelepiped they span,
`{t₀ v₀ + t₁ v₁ + t₂ v₂ : 0 ≤ tᵢ ≤ 1}`, has Lebesgue volume `|det B|`, where `B` is their
[basis matrix](cartesian-matrix.md). For linearly dependent vectors both sides are `0`.
