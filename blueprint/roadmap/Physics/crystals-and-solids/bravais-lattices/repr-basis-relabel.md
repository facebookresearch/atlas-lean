# Relabeling transforms primitive coordinates by P inverse

For every [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md), an integer `3 × 3` matrix `P` with `det P = ±1` (so
that `P` has an integer inverse) and every `x ∈ ℝ³`, the coordinates of `x` in the primitive basis
of the [relabeled lattice](relabel.md) are `P⁻¹` times its coordinates in the old
[primitive basis](basis.md). Here `P⁻¹` is the inverse of `P` as a real matrix.
