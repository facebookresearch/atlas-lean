# The change of basis to the relabeled primitive basis is P

For every [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) and an integer `3 × 3` matrix `P` with `det P = ±1`
(so that `P` has an integer inverse), the change-of-basis matrix from the
[primitive basis](basis.md) to the primitive basis of the [relabeled lattice](relabel.md), whose
`j`-th column holds the old coordinates of the `j`-th new primitive vector, is `P`.
