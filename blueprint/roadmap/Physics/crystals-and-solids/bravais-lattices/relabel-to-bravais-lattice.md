# The lattice of a relabeled crystal is the relabeled lattice

For every [crystal](bravais-crystal.md) with finite nonempty motif `F` and an integer `3 × 3` matrix
`P` with `det P = ±1` (so that `P` has an integer inverse), the Bravais lattice of the
[relabeled crystal](relabel-2.md) is the [relabeled](relabel.md) Bravais lattice of the crystal,
with basis matrix `B P`.
