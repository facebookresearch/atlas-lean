# The site correspondence sends a site to its position

Let a [crystal with motif](bravais-crystal.md) `F` have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`), lattice `L`
and offsets `u_f` (`f ∈ F`). Suppose that the offsets are pairwise distinct modulo the lattice:
`u_f - u_g ∈ L` only for `f = g`. For every site `s` of the [abstract crystal](physlib-crystal.md),
the [site correspondence](site-equiv-positions.md) sends `s` to its [position](site-position.md).
