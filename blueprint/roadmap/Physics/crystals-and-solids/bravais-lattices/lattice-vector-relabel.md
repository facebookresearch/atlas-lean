# Relabeled lattice vectors are old lattice vectors of P n

For every [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B`, an integer `3 × 3` matrix `P`
with `det P = ±1` (so that `P` has an integer inverse) and every `n ∈ ℤ³`, the lattice vector of `n`
in the [relabeled lattice](relabel.md) is the old lattice vector of `P n`: `(B P) n = B (P n)`.
