# Basis matrix of three vectors

For three vectors `v₀`, `v₁`, `v₂` of three-dimensional Euclidean space, the basis matrix is the
real `3 × 3` matrix whose `j`-th column holds the Cartesian coordinates of `v_j`. Its determinant is
the oriented volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the vectors: its absolute value is the
[volume](volume-parallelepiped.md), and it is nonzero exactly when the vectors are
[linearly independent](det-cartesian-matrix-ne-zero-iff.md).
