# Translating a site translates its position by a lattice vector

For every [crystal with motif](bravais-crystal.md) `F` with basis matrix `B`, every `m ∈ ℤ³` and
every site `s` of the [abstract crystal](physlib-crystal.md), the [position](site-position.md) of
the translated site `m + s` is `B m` plus the position of `s`.
