# Translating a site translates its position by a lattice vector

For every [crystal](bravais-crystal.md) with finite nonempty motif `F` and basis matrix `B`, every
`m ∈ ℤ³` and every site `s` of the [abstract crystal](physlib-crystal.md), the
[position](site-position.md) of the translated site `m + s` is `B m` plus the position of `s`.
