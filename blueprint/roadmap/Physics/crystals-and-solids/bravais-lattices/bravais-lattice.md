# Bravais lattice given by a nonsingular basis matrix

A three-dimensional Bravais lattice is given by a real `3 × 3` basis matrix `B` with `det B ≠ 0`.
The `j`-th column of `B` holds the Cartesian coordinates of the `j`-th
[primitive vector](primitive-vector.md) `a_j`; the condition `det B ≠ 0` says that the three
primitive vectors are [linearly independent](det-cartesian-matrix-ne-zero-iff.md). The
[lattice](lattice.md) is the set of integer combinations of the primitive vectors.
