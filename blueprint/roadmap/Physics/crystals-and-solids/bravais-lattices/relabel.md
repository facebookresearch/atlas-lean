# Relabeling a Bravais lattice by a unimodular integer matrix

For a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B` and an integer `3 × 3` matrix `P`
with `det P = ±1` (so that `P` has an integer inverse), the relabeled Bravais lattice has basis
matrix `B P` (`P` read as a real matrix): its primitive vectors are the integer combinations of the
old ones given by the columns of `P`. Its determinant `det B · det P` is nonzero.
