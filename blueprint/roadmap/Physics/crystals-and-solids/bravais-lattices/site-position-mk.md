# A site sits at the position of the atom with the same index and label

For every [crystal with motif](bravais-crystal.md) `F`, `n ∈ ℤ³` and `f ∈ F`, the
[position](site-position.md) of the site `(n, f)` of the [abstract crystal](physlib-crystal.md) is
the [position](position.md) `B n + u_f` of the atom `(n, f)`.
