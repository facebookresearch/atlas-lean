# Lattice vectors and directions in terms of the primitive vectors

Let a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`) and primitive
vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`. For every `n ∈ ℤ³`, the
[lattice vector](lattice-vector.md) `B n` equals `n₀ a₀ + n₁ a₁ + n₂ a₂`. The
[lattice direction](../miller-indices-and-the-metric-tensor/lattice-direction.md) `[u]` of an index
triple `u ∈ ℤ³` is the lattice vector `B u`, so in the same way `[u] = u₀ a₀ + u₁ a₁ + u₂ a₂`.
