# Invariance under the 45° rotation about z forces c₁₁ − c₁₂ = 2 c₄₄

For real numbers `c₁₁`, `c₁₂` and `c₄₄`, if the [cubic Voigt matrix](cubic-voigt.md) of
`(c₁₁, c₁₂, c₄₄)` is invariant under the [action](voigt-rotate.md) of the
[rotation by 45° about the z-axis](rz45.md), then `c₁₁ - c₁₂ = 2 c₄₄`, the isotropy relation.
