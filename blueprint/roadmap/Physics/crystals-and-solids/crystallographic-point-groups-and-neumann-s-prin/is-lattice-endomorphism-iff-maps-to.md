# Lattice endomorphisms are the maps of a full-rank lattice into itself

For every natural number `n` and every real `n × n` matrix `R`, `R` is a
[lattice endomorphism](is-lattice-endomorphism.md) if and only if there is a real `n × n` matrix `P`
with `det P ≠ 0` such that `v ↦ R v` maps the [lattice](basis-lattice.md) `P ℤⁿ` into itself.
