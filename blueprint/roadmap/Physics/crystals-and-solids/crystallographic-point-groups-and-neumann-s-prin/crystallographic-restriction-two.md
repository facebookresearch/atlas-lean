# Planar crystallographic restriction: orders 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6

Let `R` be a real `2 × 2` matrix with `Rᵀ R = 1` and `det R = 1` that is a
[lattice endomorphism](is-lattice-endomorphism.md), and let `n ≥ 1` be its order: `Rⁿ = 1` and
`R^m ≠ 1` for `0 < m < n`. Then `n ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 6}`. For a matrix of finite order, being a lattice
endomorphism is equivalent to being a [lattice symmetry](is-lattice-symmetry.md)
([equivalence](is-lattice-symmetry-of-pow-eq-one.md)), so this bounds the orders of the rotations in
the point group of a planar lattice.
