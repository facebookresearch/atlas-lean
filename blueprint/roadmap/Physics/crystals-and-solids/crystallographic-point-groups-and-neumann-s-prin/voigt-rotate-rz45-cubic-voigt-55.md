# Rotated 45° about z, a cubic matrix has xy shear entry (c₁₁ − c₁₂)/2

For all real `c₁₁`, `c₁₂` and `c₄₄`, the [action](voigt-rotate.md) of the
[rotation by 45° about the z-axis](rz45.md) on the [cubic Voigt matrix](cubic-voigt.md) of
`(c₁₁, c₁₂, c₄₄)` has entry `(c₁₁ - c₁₂) / 2` at the Voigt position `(6, 6)`, the `xy` shear. Voigt
indices `1, …, 6` label the axis pairs `xx`, `yy`, `zz`, `yz`, `xz`, `xy`, in this order.
