# Six-fold rotation in a hexagonal lattice basis

The matrix `C₆ = [[1, -1], [1, 0]]` is the six-fold rotation written in a hexagonal lattice basis:
for basis vectors `a₁`, `a₂` of equal length at `120°`, the rotation by `60°` maps `a₁` to `a₁ + a₂`
and `a₂` to `-a₁`. It has integer entries and is not an orthogonal matrix; its determinant, order
and trace are those of a rotation by `60°`.
