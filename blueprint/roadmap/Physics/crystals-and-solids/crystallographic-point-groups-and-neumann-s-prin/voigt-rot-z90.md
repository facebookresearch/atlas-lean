# Voigt index permutation of the quarter-turn about z

Voigt indices `1, …, 6` label the axis pairs `xx`, `yy`, `zz`, `yz`, `xz`, `xy`, in this order. The
Voigt index permutation `ρ` of the quarter-turn `R_zᵀ = R_z⁻¹`, the rotation by `-90°` about the
`z`-axis, exchanges `1 ↔ 2` and `4 ↔ 5` and fixes `3` and `6`; with the
[signs](voigt-rot-z90-sign.md) `s`, the [action](voigt-rotate.md) of `R_zᵀ` on a Voigt matrix is
`C_{IJ} ↦ s_I s_J C_{ρ(I) ρ(J)}` ([identity](voigt-rotate-rz90-transpose-apply.md)).
