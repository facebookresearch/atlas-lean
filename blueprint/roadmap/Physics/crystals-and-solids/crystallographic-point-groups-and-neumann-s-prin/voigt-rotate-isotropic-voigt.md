# The isotropic Voigt matrix is invariant under every orthogonal matrix

Let `R` be a real `3 × 3` matrix with `Rᵀ R = 1`, and let `λ` and `μ` be real numbers. Then the
[action](voigt-rotate.md) of `R` on the [isotropic Voigt matrix](isotropic-voigt.md) of `(λ, μ)`
leaves it unchanged. Improper orthogonal matrices (`det R = -1`) are included.
