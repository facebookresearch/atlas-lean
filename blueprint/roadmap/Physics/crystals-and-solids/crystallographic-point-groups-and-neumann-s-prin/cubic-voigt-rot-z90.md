# The cubic Voigt matrix is invariant under the quarter-turn about z

For all real `c₁₁, c₁₂, c₄₄` and all Voigt indices `I` and `J`, the
[cubic Voigt matrix](cubic-voigt.md) `C` satisfies `C_{IJ} = s_I s_J C_{ρ(I) ρ(J)}`, the entry form
of invariance under the quarter-turn about `z`, with the [permutation](voigt-rot-z90.md) `ρ` and the
[signs](voigt-rot-z90-sign.md) `s`.
