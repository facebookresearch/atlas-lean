# A lattice endomorphism of finite order is a lattice symmetry

Let `n` be a natural number and `R` a real `n × n` matrix that is a
[lattice endomorphism](is-lattice-endomorphism.md), with `R^k = 1` for some integer `k > 0`. Then
`R` is a [lattice symmetry](is-lattice-symmetry.md): `R` is bijective on the lattice, with inverse
`R^(k-1)`. With the converse
([every lattice symmetry is an endomorphism](is-lattice-endomorphism-2.md)), a matrix of finite
order is a lattice endomorphism exactly when it is a lattice symmetry.
