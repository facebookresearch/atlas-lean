# Cubic invariance makes a symmetric rank-two tensor scalar

Let `S` be a real `3 × 3` matrix with `Sᵀ = S` that is invariant under conjugation by the three
quarter-turns about the coordinate axes: `R_xᵀ S R_x = S`, `R_yᵀ S R_y = S` and
`R_zᵀ S R_z = S`, with [`R_x`](rx90.md), [`R_y`](ry90.md) and [`R_z`](rz90.md). Then
`S = S₁₁ · 1`, where `S₁₁` is the first diagonal entry. Read with Neumann's principle: a symmetric
second-rank property of a cubic crystal, such as its thermal conductivity, is isotropic.
