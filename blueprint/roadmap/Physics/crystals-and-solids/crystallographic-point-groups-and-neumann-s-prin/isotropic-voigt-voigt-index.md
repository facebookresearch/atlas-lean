# Isotropic stiffness tensor in Kronecker-delta form

For all real `λ` and `μ` and all axes `a, b, c, d ∈ {x, y, z}`, the entry of the
[isotropic Voigt matrix](isotropic-voigt.md) at the [Voigt indices](voigt-index.md) of the pairs
`(a, b)` and `(c, d)` is

`λ δ_{ab} δ_{cd} + μ (δ_{ac} δ_{bd} + δ_{ad} δ_{bc})`,

the isotropic rank-four elasticity tensor.
