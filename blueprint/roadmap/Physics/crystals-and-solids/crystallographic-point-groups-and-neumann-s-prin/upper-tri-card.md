# The upper triangle of 6 × 6 indices has 21 positions

The [upper triangle](upper-tri.md) of the `6 × 6` Voigt indices has `21` elements. With the
[uniqueness](eq-of-agree-on-upper-tri.md) and [existence](exists-symm-of-upper-tri.md) of a
symmetric matrix with given upper-triangle entries, a symmetric `6 × 6` stiffness matrix with no
symmetry constraint (triclinic) has `21` independent constants.
