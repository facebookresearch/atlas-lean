# Every choice of upper-triangle entries comes from a symmetric matrix

For every function `f` from the index pairs of `{1, …, 6}²` to `ℝ`, there is a real `6 × 6` matrix
`A` with `Aᵀ = A` and `A_{IJ} = f(I, J)` for every pair `(I, J)` of the
[upper triangle](upper-tri.md) (`I ≤ J`): the `21` upper-triangle entries are independent.
