# The isotropic Voigt matrix is invariant under the quarter-turn about z

For all real `λ, μ` and all Voigt indices `I` and `J`, the
[isotropic Voigt matrix](isotropic-voigt.md) `L` satisfies `L_{IJ} = s_I s_J L_{ρ(I) ρ(J)}`, the
entry form of invariance under the quarter-turn about `z`, with the [permutation](voigt-rot-z90.md)
`ρ` and the [signs](voigt-rot-z90-sign.md) `s`.
