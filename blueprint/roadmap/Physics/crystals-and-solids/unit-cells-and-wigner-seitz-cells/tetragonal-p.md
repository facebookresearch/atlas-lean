# Primitive tetragonal lattice

For `a > 0` and `c > 0`, the primitive tetragonal lattice (Pearson symbol `tP`) is the
[Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) whose primitive vectors (columns of the
basis matrix) are `(a, 0, 0)`, `(0, a, 0)` and `(0, 0, c)`: the basis matrix is `diag(a, a, c)`.
