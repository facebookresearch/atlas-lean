# Sublattice relation between two Bravais lattices

For two [Bravais lattices](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrices `B_c` and
`B_f` and lattices `L_c` and `L_f`, the first is a sublattice of the second when `L_c ⊆ L_f`; its
cell is then called the conventional cell. For such a pair, `|det B_c| = N |det B_f|`, with `N` the
number of [points per cell](points-per-cell.md)
([volume ratio](points-per-cell-eq-volume-ratio.md)), where `|det B|` is the
[volume](../bravais-lattices/volume-parallelepiped-primitive-vector.md) of the cell spanned by the
primitive vectors. For example, for `a > 0` the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a` is a
sublattice of the
[face-centred cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/face-centered-cubic.md) with
the same conventional cube ([inclusion](simple-cubic-lattice-le-fcc.md)).
