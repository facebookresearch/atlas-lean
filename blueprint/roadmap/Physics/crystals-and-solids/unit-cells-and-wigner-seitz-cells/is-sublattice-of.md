# Sublattice relation between two Bravais lattices

For two [Bravais lattices](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with lattices `L_c` and `L_f`,
the first is a sublattice of the second when `L_c ⊆ L_f`; its cell, the conventional cell, is then
at least as large: its volume is `N` times that of a primitive cell of the second, for the number
`N ≥ 1` of [points per cell](points-per-cell.md)
([volume ratio](points-per-cell-eq-volume-ratio.md)), and equal to it when the lattices coincide.
For example, for `a > 0` the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a` is a
sublattice of the
[face-centred cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/face-centered-cubic.md) with
the same conventional cube ([inclusion](simple-cubic-lattice-le-fcc.md)).
