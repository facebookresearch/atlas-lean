# Simple and face-centred cubic lattices of equal side differ

For `a > 0`, the [simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md)
of side `a` and the
[face-centred cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/face-centered-cubic.md) with
conventional cube of side `a` have different [lattices](../bravais-lattices/lattice.md).
