# Overlaps of closed Wigner–Seitz cells avoid the interior

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have lattice `L`, and let `W` be its
[Wigner–Seitz cell](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/wigner-seitz-cell.md)
`W = {x : ‖x‖ ≤ ‖x - R‖ for every R ∈ L}` (closed: ties included). For every lattice point `R ≠ 0`
and every point `x` of the translate `R + W`, `x` is not in the interior of `W`.
