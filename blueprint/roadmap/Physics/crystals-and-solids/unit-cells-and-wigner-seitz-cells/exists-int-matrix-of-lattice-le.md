# A sublattice inclusion gives an integer change-of-basis matrix

Let two [Bravais lattices](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrices `B_c` and
`B_f` and lattices `L_c` and `L_f`. If `L_c ⊆ L_f`, then `B_c = B_f M` for some integer `3 × 3`
matrix `M`: each primitive vector of the coarse lattice is an integer combination of the fine
primitive vectors, and these combinations are the columns of `M`.
