# Distinct translates of the Wigner–Seitz cell have disjoint interiors

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have lattice `L`, and let `W` be its
[Wigner–Seitz cell](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/wigner-seitz-cell.md)
`W = {x : ‖x‖ ≤ ‖x - R‖ for every R ∈ L}` (closed: ties included). For all lattice points `R₁ ≠ R₂`,
the interiors of the translates `R₁ + W` and `R₂ + W` are disjoint. The closed translates themselves
can overlap on their boundaries ([overlap](wigner-seitz-cell-exists-overlap.md)).
