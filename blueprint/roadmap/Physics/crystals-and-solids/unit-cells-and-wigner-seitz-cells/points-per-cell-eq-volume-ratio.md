# For a sublattice, points per cell equal the ratio of cell volumes

Let two [Bravais lattices](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrices `B_c` and
`B_f` and lattices `L_c` and `L_f`, with `L_c ⊆ L_f`, and let `N` be the
[number of lattice points per cell](points-per-cell.md) of `L_f` per cell of `L_c`. Then
`|det B_c| = N |det B_f|` and `|det B_c| / |det B_f| = N`: the volume of the conventional cell over
that of the primitive cell is the natural number `N`.
