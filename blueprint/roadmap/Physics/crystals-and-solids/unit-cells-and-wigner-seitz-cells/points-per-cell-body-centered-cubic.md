# The body-centred cubic lattice has 2 points per cubic cell

For every `a > 0`, with the cube of the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a`
(basis matrix `a · 1`) as conventional cell and the
[body-centred cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/body-centered-cubic.md) of
the same cube (primitive vectors `(a/2)(-1, 1, 1)`, `(a/2)(1, -1, 1)`, `(a/2)(1, 1, -1)`):

- the [number of lattice points per cell](points-per-cell.md) is `2`;
- the absolute determinants satisfy `|det B_sc| = 2 |det B_bcc|`;
- their ratio is `|det B_sc| / |det B_bcc| = 2`.

The count `2` is the index of the cubic lattice in the body-centred cubic lattice, not the number of
its points in the closed cube, whose eight corners alone are lattice points.
