# Body-centred orthorhombic lattice

For `a, b, c > 0`, the body-centred orthorhombic lattice (Pearson symbol `oI`) is the
[Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) whose primitive vectors (columns of the
basis matrix) are `(-a/2, b/2, c/2)`, `(a/2, -b/2, c/2)` and `(a/2, b/2, -c/2)`.
