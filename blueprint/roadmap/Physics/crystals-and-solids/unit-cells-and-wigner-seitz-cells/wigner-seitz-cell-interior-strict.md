# Interior Wigner–Seitz points are strictly nearest to the origin

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have lattice `L`, and let `W` be its
[Wigner–Seitz cell](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/wigner-seitz-cell.md)
`W = {x : ‖x‖ ≤ ‖x - R‖ for every R ∈ L}` (closed: ties included). For every point `x` in the
interior of `W` and every lattice point `R ≠ 0`, `‖x‖ < ‖x - R‖`.
