# Closed conventional cell of a Bravais lattice

For a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`,
`a₂`, the conventional cell is the closed parallelepiped `{t₀ a₀ + t₁ a₁ + t₂ a₂ : 0 ≤ tᵢ ≤ 1}`.
Being closed, it contains the lattice points on its faces, edges and corners, which neighbouring
cells share; the [number of lattice points per cell](points-per-cell.md) is a quotient count
instead. For the cube of side `a > 0` and the face-centred cubic lattice, the count per cell is `4`
([count](points-per-cell-face-centered-cubic.md)), while the eight corners of the closed cube,
points of the simple cubic lattice, are already points of the face-centred cubic lattice
([inclusion](simple-cubic-lattice-le-fcc.md)).
