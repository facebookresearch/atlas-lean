# Two closed translates of the Wigner–Seitz cell meet

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have lattice `L`, and let `W` be its
[Wigner–Seitz cell](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/wigner-seitz-cell.md)
`W = {x : ‖x‖ ≤ ‖x - R‖ for every R ∈ L}` (closed: ties included). There are a point `x` and lattice
points `R₁ ≠ R₂` with `x ∈ R₁ + W` and `x ∈ R₂ + W`: closed cells overlap on their boundaries. The
proof uses the midpoint of a shortest nonzero lattice vector.
