# For admissible angles, the rhombohedral lattice

For `a > 0` and an angle `α` with `sin α ≠ 0` and `Ω = 1 - 3 cos² α + 2 cos³ α > 0`, the
rhombohedral lattice (Pearson symbol `hR`: trigonal system, rhombohedral centring) with parameter
`a` and rhombohedral angle `α` is the [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md)
whose primitive vectors (columns of the basis matrix) are `(a, 0, 0)`, `(a cos α, a sin α, 0)` and
`(a cos α, a (cos α - cos² α) / sin α, a √Ω / sin α)`. The hypotheses make the divisions and the
square root meaningful and the determinant nonzero.
