# Crystals and solids

Interatomic pair potentials of crystalline solids: the Lennard-Jones potential at its minimum, the bond modulus estimate and the Born–Landé lattice energy. Bravais lattices, unit cells, Miller indices, metric tensors and reciprocal lattices are under Discrete geometry in Geometry, crystallographic point groups under Euclidean geometry, crystal packing under Packings and coverings, structure factors under Harmonic analysis in Analysis, and lattice vibrations under Elasticity and structural mechanics. Lattice spin models are under Statistical Mechanics.
