# Conversion of rational directions to Miller–Bravais indices

For a rational direction `(u, v, w) ∈ ℚ³`, the Miller–Bravais direction is
`(U, V, T, W) = ((2u - v)/3, (2v - u)/3, -(u + v)/3, w)`. The
[three-index conversion](of-miller-bravais-dir.md) undoes it
([left inverse](of-miller-bravais-dir-to-miller-bravais.md)), and on four-index directions with
`U + V + T = 0` it undoes the three-index conversion
([right inverse](to-miller-bravais-dir-q-of-miller-bravai.md)).
