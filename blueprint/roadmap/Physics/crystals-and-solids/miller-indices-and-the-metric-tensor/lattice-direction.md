# Lattice direction of an integer index triple

For a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) with basis matrix `B` and primitive
vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, and indices `u ∈ ℤ³`, the lattice direction `[u]` is the lattice vector
`B u = u₀ a₀ + u₁ a₁ + u₂ a₂`. For `u = 0` it is the zero vector, which is not a direction.
