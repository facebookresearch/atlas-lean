# The reciprocal basis has the inverse metric tensor as Gram matrix

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`)
and primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`, with
[crystallographic reciprocal basis](cryst-reciprocal.md) `b₀`, `b₁`, `b₂` (`a_i · b_j = δ_ij`, no
factor `2π`). For all `j`, `k`, `b_j · b_k = (G⁻¹)_jk`, where `G⁻¹` is the inverse of the
[metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) `G`.
