# In the simple cubic lattice, a direction is normal to its own planes

Let `a > 0` and consider the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a`, with
basis matrix `a · 1`. For all `h ∈ ℤ³`, the [lattice direction](lattice-direction.md) `[h]` equals
`a² g_h`, where `g_h` is the [reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md): for `h ≠ 0` the
direction `[h k l]` is normal to the planes `(h k l)`.
