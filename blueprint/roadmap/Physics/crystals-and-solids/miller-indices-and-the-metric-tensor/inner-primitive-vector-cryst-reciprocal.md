# Duality of the primitive and reciprocal bases

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`)
and primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`, and let `b₀`, `b₁`, `b₂` be its
[crystallographic reciprocal basis](cryst-reciprocal.md). For all `i`, `j`, `a_i · b_j = δ_ij`: `1`
if `i = j` and `0` otherwise.
