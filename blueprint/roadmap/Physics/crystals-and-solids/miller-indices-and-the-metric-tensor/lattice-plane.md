# Planes of a family of lattice planes

For a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md), Miller indices `h ∈ ℤ³` and an
integer `n`, the `n`-th plane of the family `h` is `{x : g_h · x = n}`, where `g_h` is the
[reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) of `h`. It is a plane only for `h ≠ 0`; for
`h = 0` the set is all of space when `n = 0` and empty otherwise.
