# Cubic interplanar spacing for nonzero Miller indices

Let `a > 0` and consider the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a`, with
basis matrix `a · 1`. For all Miller indices `h = (h₀, h₁, h₂) ≠ 0`, the
[interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md) `d_h = 1 / ‖g_h‖` is `d_h = a / √(h₀² + h₁² + h₂²)`.
