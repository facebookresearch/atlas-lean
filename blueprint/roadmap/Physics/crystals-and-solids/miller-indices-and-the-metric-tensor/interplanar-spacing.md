# Interplanar spacing of a family of lattice planes

For a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and Miller indices `h ∈ ℤ³`, the
interplanar spacing is `d_h = 1 / ‖g_h‖`, where `g_h` is the
[reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) of `h`. For `h ≠ 0` it is the distance between
adjacent planes of the family `h` ([shift](plane-shift-norm.md),
[distance bound](plane-dist-ge.md)). For `h = 0` there is no family of planes, and the convention
`1 / 0 = 0` gives `d_0 = 0`.
