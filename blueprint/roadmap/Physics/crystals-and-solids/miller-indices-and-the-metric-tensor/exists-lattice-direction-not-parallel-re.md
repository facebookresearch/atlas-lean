# Outside the cubic case, a direction need not be normal to its planes

There are a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and indices `h ∈ ℤ³` such that
the [lattice direction](lattice-direction.md) `[h]` is not a real multiple of the
[reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) `g_h`: the direction `[h k l]` need not be normal
to the planes `(h k l)`. The proof uses the lattice with basis matrix `diag(1, 1, 2)` and
`h = (1, 0, 1)`.
