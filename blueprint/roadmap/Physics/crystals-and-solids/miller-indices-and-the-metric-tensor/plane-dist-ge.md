# For nonzero indices, the planes n = 0 and n = 1 are at least d apart

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and Miller indices `h` be given,
with the [reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) `g_h`. Suppose that `g_h ≠ 0`, which
holds exactly when `h ≠ 0`. For every point `x` of the plane `n = 0` and every point `y` of the
plane `n = 1` of the family `h` ([lattice planes](lattice-plane.md)), `‖x - y‖ ≥ d_h`, the
[interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md). With the [shift of length](plane-shift-norm.md)
`d_h`, this distance is attained.
