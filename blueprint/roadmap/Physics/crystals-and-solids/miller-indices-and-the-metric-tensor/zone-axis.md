# Cross product of two Miller index triples

For index triples `h`, `h' ∈ ℤ³`, the index cross product is `h × h' ∈ ℤ³`. When it is nonzero, that
is, when the plane families `h` and `h'` are not parallel, it gives their zone axis
([zone axis](zone-axis-unique.md)); for parallel triples, such as `h = h'`, it is `0`, which is not
a direction.
