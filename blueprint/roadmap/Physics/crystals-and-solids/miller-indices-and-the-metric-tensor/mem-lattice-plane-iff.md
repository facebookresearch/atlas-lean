# Membership in a lattice plane in primitive coordinates

For every [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md), all Miller indices `h ∈ ℤ³`,
every integer `n` and every vector `x`, `x` lies in the `n`-th [lattice plane](lattice-plane.md) of
the family `h` if and only if `Σ_j h_j c_j = n`, where `c₀`, `c₁`, `c₂` are the coordinates of `x`
in the primitive basis.
