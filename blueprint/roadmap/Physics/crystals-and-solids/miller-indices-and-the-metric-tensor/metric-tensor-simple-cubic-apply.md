# Entries of the metric tensor of the simple cubic lattice

Let `a > 0` and consider the
[simple cubic lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/simple-cubic.md) of side `a`, with
basis matrix `a · 1`. For all `i`, `j`, the entry `G_ij` of its [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is
`a²` if `i = j` and `0` otherwise.
