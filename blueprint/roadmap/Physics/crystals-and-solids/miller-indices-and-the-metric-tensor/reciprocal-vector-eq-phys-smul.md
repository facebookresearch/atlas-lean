# The physics reciprocal vector is 2π times the crystallographic one

For every [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and all Miller indices `h ∈ ℤ³`,
`2π g_h` is the lattice vector of `h` in the
[reciprocal lattice](../reciprocal-lattices-and-brillouin-zones/reciprocal.md) of the physics
convention (primitive vectors `b*_j` with `a_i · b*_j = 2π δ_ij`), where `g_h` is the
crystallographic [reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md).
