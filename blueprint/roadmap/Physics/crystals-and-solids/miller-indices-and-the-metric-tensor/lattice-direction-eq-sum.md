# A lattice direction is an integer combination of primitive vectors

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`)
and primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`. For all `u ∈ ℤ³`, the
[lattice direction](lattice-direction.md) `[u]` equals `u₀ a₀ + u₁ a₁ + u₂ a₂`.
