# Pairing a reciprocal vector with a primitive vector gives an index

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`)
and primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`. For all Miller indices `h ∈ ℤ³` and
every `i`, the [reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) `g_h = h₀ b₀ + h₁ b₁ + h₂ b₂` of
the Miller indices `h` satisfies `g_h · a_i = h_i`.
