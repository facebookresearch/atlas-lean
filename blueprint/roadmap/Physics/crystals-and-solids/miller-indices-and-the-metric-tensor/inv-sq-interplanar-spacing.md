# For nonzero indices, interplanar spacing from the inverse metric

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) have basis matrix `B` (`det B ≠ 0`)
and primitive vectors `a₀`, `a₁`, `a₂`, the columns of `B`. For all Miller indices `h ≠ 0`, the
[interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md) `d_h = 1 / ‖g_h‖` satisfies
`1 / d_h² = Σ_ij h_i (G⁻¹)_ij h_j`, with the inverse `G⁻¹` of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)
`G`, `G_ij = a_i · a_j`.
