# For non-parallel families, the index cross product is the zone axis

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and `h`, `h' ∈ ℤ³` be given with
`h × h' ≠ 0`, that is, `h` and `h'` linearly independent (the plane families are not parallel).
Then, with `[·]` the [lattice direction](lattice-direction.md) and `g` the
[reciprocal-lattice vectors](reciprocal-vector.md):

- `[h × h'] ≠ 0`;
- `g_h · [h × h'] = 0` and `g_h' · [h × h'] = 0`;
- every `u ∈ ℤ³` with `g_h · [u] = 0` and `g_h' · [u] = 0` has `[u] = c [h × h']` for some real `c`.
