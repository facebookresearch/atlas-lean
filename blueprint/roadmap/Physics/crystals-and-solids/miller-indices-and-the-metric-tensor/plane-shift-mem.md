# For nonzero indices, a fixed shift moves each plane to the next

Let a [Bravais lattice](../bravais-lattices/bravais-lattice.md) and Miller indices `h` be given,
with the [reciprocal-lattice vector](reciprocal-vector.md) `g_h`. Suppose that `g_h ≠ 0`, which
holds exactly when `h ≠ 0`. For every integer `n` and every point `x` of the `n`-th
[lattice plane](lattice-plane.md) of the family `h`, the point `x + g_h / ‖g_h‖²` lies in the
`(n + 1)`-st plane.
