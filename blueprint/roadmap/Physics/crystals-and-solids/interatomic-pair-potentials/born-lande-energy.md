# Born–Landé lattice energy

For real numbers `N_A` (Avogadro number), `M` (Madelung constant), `z₊` and `z₋` (cation and anion
charge numbers), `e` (elementary charge), `ε₀` (vacuum permittivity), `r₀` (equilibrium spacing) and
`n` (the empirical Born exponent), the Born–Landé lattice energy is
`-(N_A M z₊ z₋ e² / (4π ε₀ r₀)) (1 - 1/n)`. With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, it is `0` when
`ε₀ r₀ = 0`, and `1 - 1/n` is `1` when `n = 0`; the intended domain has positive constants and
`n > 1`, where the energy is [negative](born-lande-energy-neg-of-pos.md).
