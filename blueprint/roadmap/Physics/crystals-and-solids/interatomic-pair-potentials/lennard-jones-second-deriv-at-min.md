# Bond stiffness of the Lennard-Jones potential at equilibrium

For real numbers `ε > 0`, `σ > 0` and `r₀ = 2^{1/6} σ`, the derivative `φ'` of the Lennard-Jones
potential `φ(r) = 4 ε ((σ / r)¹² - (σ / r)⁶)` has derivative `72 ε / (2^{1/3} σ²)` at `r₀`: the bond
stiffness is `S₀ = φ''(r₀) = 72 ε / (2^{1/3} σ²)`.
