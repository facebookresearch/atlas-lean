# Virtual work of the boundary tractions of a box

For a box `[a, b] ⊂ ℝ³`, a stress field `σ` and a virtual displacement `v`, the boundary work is the
sum over `i` of the [upper-face work](upper-face-work.md) and the
[lower-face work](lower-face-work.md): the virtual work of the tractions on the six faces.
