# A rigid virtual displacement does no external virtual work

Under the hypotheses of the [virtual work identity](virtual-work-box.md) on the stress and body
force (`a_i < b_i`, `U` open containing the box, `σ` with continuously differentiable components on
`U` and symmetric on the box, `div σ + body = 0` on the box), for every `3 × 3` matrix `W` with
`Wᵀ = -W`, `∫_{[a,b]} body(x) · (W x) dx + W_b = 0`, where `W_b` is the
[boundary work](box-traction-work.md) for the virtual displacement `x ↦ W x`.
