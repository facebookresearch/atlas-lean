# The energy integral of the bar equals that of its unit pull-back

For a real `L > 0`, every function `EA` and all elements `u`, `v` of [`H¹₀(0, L)`](weak-h10-on.md),
`∫_0^1 κ(ξ) û'(ξ) v̂'(ξ) dξ = ∫_0^L EA(x) u'(x) v'(x) dx`, where `κ` is the
[rescaled rigidity](scaled-rigidity.md) and `û`, `v̂` are the [pull-backs](to-unit.md) of `u`, `v`.
