# Stresses in an elastic half-space

This node records an open direction: the stresses in a homogeneous isotropic elastic half-space under surface loads, from the vector
boundary-value problem with prescribed traction on the loaded region (or a concentrated boundary load) and a traction-free surface
elsewhere, and the stress distributions under footings obtained from it. The resultant of
Boussinesq's radial stress alone does not solve this problem.
