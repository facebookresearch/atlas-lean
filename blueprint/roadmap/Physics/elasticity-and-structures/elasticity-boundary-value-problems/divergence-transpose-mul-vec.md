# Product rule for the divergence of σᵀ v

For an open set `U ⊆ ℝ³`, a stress field `σ` whose components are continuously differentiable on
`U`, a continuously differentiable virtual displacement `v` on `U`, and `x ∈ U`,
`div(σᵀ v)(x) = (div σ)(x) · v(x) + ∑_{i,j} σ_ij(x) H_ij`, where `div σ` is the
[stress divergence](stress-divergence.md) and `H` the
[displacement gradient](../cauchy-stress-small-strain-and-mohr-s-circle/displacement-gradient.md) of
`v` at `x`.
