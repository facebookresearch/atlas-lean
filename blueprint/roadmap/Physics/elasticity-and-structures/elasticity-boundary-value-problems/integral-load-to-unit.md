# The load integral of the bar equals that of its unit pull-back

For a real `L > 0`, every function `q` and every element `v` of [`H¹₀(0, L)`](weak-h10-on.md),
`∫_0^1 f̂(ξ) v̂(ξ) dξ = ∫_0^L q(x) v(x) dx`, where `f̂` is the [rescaled load](scaled-load.md) and `v̂` the
[pull-back](to-unit.md) of `v`.
