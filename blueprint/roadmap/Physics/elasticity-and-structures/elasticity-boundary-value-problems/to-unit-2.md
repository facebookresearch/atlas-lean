# A weak bar solution pulled back solves the unit problem

For a real `L > 0`, functions `EA` and `q`, and a [weak bar solution](is-weak-bar-solution.md) `u`, its
[pull-back](to-unit.md) `û` satisfies `∫_0^1 κ û' v' = ∫_0^1 f̂ v` for every element `v` of the
[unit space `H¹₀(0, 1)`](../../../Analysis/partial-differential-equations/weak-solutions-of-elliptic-equations/weak-h10.md), with `κ` the [rescaled rigidity](scaled-rigidity.md) and `f̂`
the [rescaled load](scaled-load.md).
