# Axial stress of the uniform-load displacement of a bar

For real numbers $EA$ (axial rigidity), $L$ (length), $q$ (uniform axial load), $A_{cs}$
(cross-section area) and a section $x$, let $u(y) = q\, y (L - y) / (2 EA)$ be the closed-form
uniform-load displacement, which for $L > 0$ and $EA > 0$ solves the fixed-fixed bar problem with
load $q$. The axial normal stress at $x$ is

$$\sigma(x) = \frac{EA\, u'(x)}{A_{cs}}.$$

For $EA \ne 0$ this is $q (L - 2x) / (2 A_{cs})$ (equal to $0$ when $A_{cs} = 0$, by
$x / 0 = 0$); for $EA = 0$ the displacement is identically $0$ by the same convention, and so is
the stress. In the physical reading $L$, $EA$ and $A_{cs}$ are positive.
