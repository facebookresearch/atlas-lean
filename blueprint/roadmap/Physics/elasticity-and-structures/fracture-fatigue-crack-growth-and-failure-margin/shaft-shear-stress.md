# Surface torsional shear stress of a solid circular shaft

For real numbers $T$ (torque) and $d$ (diameter), the shear stress at the surface $r = d/2$ of a
solid circular shaft is, by the torsion formula,

$$\tau = \frac{T\, (d/2)}{J} , \qquad J = \frac{\pi d^4}{32} ,$$

where $J$ is the [polar second moment of area](circle-polar-moment.md) of the section. For $d \ne 0$
it equals $16 T / (\pi d^3)$ ([closed form](shaft-stresses-eq.md)). With the convention $x / 0 = 0$,
at $d = 0$ the value is $0$ for every $T$. In the model $d > 0$.
