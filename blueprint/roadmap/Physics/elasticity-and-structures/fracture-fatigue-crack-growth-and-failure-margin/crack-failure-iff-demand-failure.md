# Crack failure as a demand event ($K_I$ against $K_{IC}$)

For all real $K_{IC}$, $Y$, $\sigma$ and $a$, the [crack failure event](crack-failure.md)
$K_{IC} \le K_I(a)$ is the [failure event](demand-failure.md) with the allowable $K_{IC}$ (the
fracture toughness) and the demand $K_I(a) = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$, the
[mode-I stress intensity](mode-istress-intensity.md) (with $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$). The two
events are equal by definition.
