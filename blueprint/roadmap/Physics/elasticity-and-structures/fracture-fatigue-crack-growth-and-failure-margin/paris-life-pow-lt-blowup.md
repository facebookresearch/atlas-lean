# For $m > 2$, the power-branch Paris life precedes the blow-up count

Let $K > 0$, $0 < a_0 < a_c$ and $r < 0$, that is, growth exponent $m = 2(1 - r) > 2$. Then the
[Paris life](paris-life-pow.md) is strictly smaller than the blow-up cycle count:

$$N_f = \frac{a_c^r - a_0^r}{r K} < N^* = \frac{a_0^r}{-rK},$$

where $N^*$ is the cycle count at which the base $a_0^r + r K N$ of the
[power-branch crack](paris-crack-pow.md) vanishes. So the crack reaches $a_c$ strictly before the
blow-up.
