# For $K, a_0, a_c > 0$: the logarithmic crack reaches $a_c$ at its life

Let $K > 0$, $a_0 > 0$ and $a_c > 0$. At the [Paris life](paris-life-exp.md)
$N_f = \log(a_c/a_0)/K$, the [logarithmic-branch crack](paris-crack-exp.md) reaches the target
length:

$$a(N_f) = a_0\, e^{\log(a_c / a_0)} = a_c.$$

The target need not exceed $a_0$: for $a_c < a_0$ the life $N_f$ is negative.
