# Critical crack length $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$

For real numbers $K_{IC}$, $Y$ and $\sigma$, the critical crack length of the LEFM model is

$$a_c = \frac{1}{\pi} \Bigl(\frac{K_{IC}}{Y \sigma}\Bigr)^2.$$

With the convention $x / 0 = 0$ it is $0$ when $Y \sigma = 0$. For a positive fracture toughness
$K_{IC}$, a positive geometry factor $Y$ and a positive peak tensile stress $\sigma$, it is the
crack length at which the [mode-I stress intensity](mode-istress-intensity.md)
$Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$ [equals $K_{IC}$](mode-istress-intensity-critical-crack-le.md). For other
signs it need not be: with $K_{IC} = -1$ and $Y = \sigma = 1$ the stress intensity at $a_c$ is $1$.
