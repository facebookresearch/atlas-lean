# Positive toughness, geometry factor and stress give $a_c > 0$

If the fracture toughness $K_{IC}$, the geometry factor $Y$ and the peak tensile stress $\sigma$ are
positive, the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md) $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ is
positive. (When $Y \sigma = 0$, the convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives $a_c = 0$.)
