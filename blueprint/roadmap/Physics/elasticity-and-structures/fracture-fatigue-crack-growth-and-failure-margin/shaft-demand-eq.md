# Demand at the critical point of a solid circular shaft

Let $\Phi$ be [calibrated on the bending–torsion states](bending-torsion-calibrated.md) with shear
factor $c$ (so $c > 0$) and $\Phi(S(\sigma, \tau)) = \sqrt{\sigma^2 + c \tau^2}$ on every
[bending–torsion state](bending-torsion-state.md), let $d > 0$ and let $M$ and $T$ be real numbers.
At the critical point of the solid circular shaft of diameter $d$ under the bending moment $M$ and
the torque $T$, with the [bending stress](shaft-bending-stress.md) $\sigma$ and the [shear
stress](shaft-shear-stress.md) $\tau$, the demand is

$$\Phi(S(\sigma, \tau)) = \frac{16 \sqrt{4 M^2 + c T^2}}{\pi d^3} .$$

It depends on the load only through $M^2$ and $T^2$.
