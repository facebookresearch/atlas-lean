# Paris-law crack growth, logarithmic branch: $a_0 e^{KN}$

For real numbers $K$, $a_0$ and $N$, the logarithmic-branch Paris crack length after $N$ cycles is

$$a(N) = a_0\, e^{K N}.$$

It is the case $m = 2$ of the reduced Paris law $da/dN = K a^{m/2} = K a$, where $K$ is the
aggregate growth coefficient (not a stress intensity): for all real $K$ and $a_0$ it
[solves $da/dN = K a$](has-deriv-at-paris-crack-exp.md) and
[starts at $a_0$](paris-crack-exp-zero.md). In the physical reading $K > 0$ and $a_0 > 0$.
