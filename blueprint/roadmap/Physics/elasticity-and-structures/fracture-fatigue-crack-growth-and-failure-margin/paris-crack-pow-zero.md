# For $r \ne 0$ and $a_0 > 0$, the power-branch crack starts at $a_0$

Let $r \ne 0$ and $a_0 > 0$. For every real $K$, the [power-branch crack](paris-crack-pow.md)
$a(N) = \bigl(a_0^r + r K N\bigr)^{1/r}$ takes the value $a_0$ at zero cycles:

$$a(0) = \bigl(a_0^r\bigr)^{1/r} = a_0.$$

The condition $r \ne 0$ is used: for $r = 0$ the formula gives $a(0) = 1$, by the conventions
$0^{-1} = 0$ and $s^0 = 1$.
