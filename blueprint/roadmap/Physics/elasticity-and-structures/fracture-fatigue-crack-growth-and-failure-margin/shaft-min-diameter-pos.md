# For a nonzero load the minimum shaft diameter is positive

Let $c > 0$ and $A > 0$, and let $M$ and $T$ be real numbers, not both zero. Then the [minimum
diameter](shaft-min-diameter.md) $d_{\min} = (16 \sqrt{4 M^2 + c T^2}/(\pi A))^{1/3}$ is positive,
so $d = 0$ does not satisfy the sizing inequality $d_{\min} \le d$. At zero load $d_{\min} = 0$.
