# The logarithmic-branch crack solves $da/dN = K a$

For all real $K$ and $a_0$ and every cycle count $N$, the
[logarithmic-branch crack](paris-crack-exp.md) $a(N) = a_0 e^{KN}$ is differentiable at $N$ with

$$a'(N) = K\, a(N),$$

the reduced Paris law $da/dN = K a^{m/2}$ for the growth exponent $m = 2$. Here $K$ is the aggregate
growth coefficient of the reduced Paris law, not a stress intensity.
