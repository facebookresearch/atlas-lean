# Free-free bar problem

Free-free problem on `[0, L]` for rigidity `k`, distributed load `f` and end forces `P₀`, `P₁`:
`u` is `C²` on `[0, L]`, satisfies `-(k * u'') = f` on `[0, L]` with derivatives taken within
`[0, L]`, has end force `-(k * u' 0) = P₀` at `x = 0`, where the outward normal is `-x`, and has
end force `k * u' L = P₁` at `x = L`.
