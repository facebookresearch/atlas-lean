# Twist of a shaft fixed at one end

Let `GJ > 0`. For a circular shaft fixed at `x = 0`, with distributed torque
`t` and end torque `T` at `x = L`, every twist solution `φ` equals
`fixedTractionDisplacement GJ L T t` on `[0, L]`, and its internal torque
satisfies `GJ * φ' x = T + ∫ y in x..L, t y` there. It is the fixed-traction
bar problem with stiffness `GJ`.
