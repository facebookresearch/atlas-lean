# The Frobenius inner product is the full double contraction

On real $3 \times 3$ matrices with the Frobenius inner product
$\langle A, B \rangle = \operatorname{tr}(B A^{\mathsf T})$, for all $A$ and $B$,

$$\langle A, B \rangle = \sum_{i, j} A_{ij} B_{ij} = A : B,$$

the full double contraction. For symmetric matrices each off-diagonal shear pair $(i, j)$, $(j, i)$
contributes twice, as in the stress power $\sigma : \varepsilon$. The norm
$\|A\| = \sqrt{\langle A, A \rangle} = (\sum_{i,j} A_{ij}^2)^{1/2}$ is the Frobenius norm; the
distances, projections and completeness of the [return map](deviatoric-return.md) and of the related
pages of this topic are taken for this inner product.
