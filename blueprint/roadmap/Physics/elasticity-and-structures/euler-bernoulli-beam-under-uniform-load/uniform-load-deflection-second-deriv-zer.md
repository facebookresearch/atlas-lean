# The second derivative of the deflection vanishes at the left support

For all real `w`, `EI` and `L`, the [uniform-load deflection](uniform-load-deflection.md)
`y(x) = w * (L ^ 3 * x - 2 * L * x ^ 3 + x ^ 4) / (24 * EI)` has second derivative `y''(0) = 0`: the
bending moment, proportional to `y''`, vanishes at the left simple support.
