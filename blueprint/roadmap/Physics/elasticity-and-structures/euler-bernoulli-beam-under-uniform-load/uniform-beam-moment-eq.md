# Explicit bending-moment diagram under uniform load

For a bending stiffness `EI ≠ 0` and all real `w`, `L` and `x`, the
[bending moment](uniform-beam-moment.md) `M(x) = -EI * y''(x)` of the uniformly loaded simply
supported beam is `M(x) = (w / 2) * (L * x - x ^ 2)`. At `EI = 0` the moment is `0` instead.
