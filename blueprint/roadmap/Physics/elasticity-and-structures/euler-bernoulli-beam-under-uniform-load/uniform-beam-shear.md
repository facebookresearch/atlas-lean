# Shear force of the uniformly loaded beam

For real `w`, `EI`, `L` and `x`, the shear force of the simply supported beam under uniform load is
the derivative of the [bending moment](uniform-beam-moment.md): `V(x) = M'(x)`. It is defined for
all real inputs; with the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is identically `0` when `EI = 0`.
