# End moments and end shears under uniform load

For a bending stiffness `EI ≠ 0` and all real `w` and `L`, the
[bending moment](uniform-beam-moment.md) `M(x) = -EI * y''(x)` and the
[shear force](uniform-beam-shear.md) `V(x) = M'(x)` of the uniformly loaded simply supported beam
satisfy `M(0) = 0`, `M(L) = 0`, `V(0) = w * L / 2` and `V(L) = -(w * L / 2)`, and the shear has
derivative `V'(x) = -w` at every `x`.
