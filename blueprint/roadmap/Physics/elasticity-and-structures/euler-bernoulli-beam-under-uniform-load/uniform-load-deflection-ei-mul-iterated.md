# The uniform-load deflection solves the beam equation

For a bending stiffness `EI > 0` and all real `w` and `L`, the
[uniform-load deflection](uniform-load-deflection.md)
`y(x) = w * (L ^ 3 * x - 2 * L * x ^ 3 + x ^ 4) / (24 * EI)` satisfies the Euler–Bernoulli equation
`EI * y''''(x) = w` at every real `x`.
