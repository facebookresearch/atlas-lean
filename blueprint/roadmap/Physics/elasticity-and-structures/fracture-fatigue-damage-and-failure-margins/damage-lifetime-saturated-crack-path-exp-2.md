# At $f = 0$ a crack below $a_c$ never fails, logarithmic branch

Let $K > 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, with $a_c$ the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md),
and let $a(N) = a_0 e^{K N}$ be the [logarithmic-branch solution](paris-crack-exp.md) and
$N_f = \log(a_c / a_0)/K$ its [Paris life](paris-life-exp.md). At load frequency $f = 0$ the [event
path](saturated-crack-path.md) $a(\min(0, N_f))$ is the constant $a_0$ at every time $t \ge 0$, and,
as the damage path of every outcome $\omega$ of any set $\Omega$ against the constant threshold
$a_c$, its [damage lifetime](damage-lifetime.md) is $\infty$: the threshold is never reached.
