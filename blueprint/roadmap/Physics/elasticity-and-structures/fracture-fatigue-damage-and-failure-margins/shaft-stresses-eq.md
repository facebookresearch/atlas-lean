# For $d \ne 0$, $\sigma = 32M/(\pi d^3)$ and $\tau = 16T/(\pi d^3)$

For all real $M$ and $T$ and every real $d \ne 0$, the [outer-fiber bending
stress](shaft-bending-stress.md) $\sigma = M (d/2)/I$, $I = \pi d^4/64$, and the [surface shear
stress](shaft-shear-stress.md) $\tau = T (d/2)/J$, $J = \pi d^4/32$, of a solid circular shaft of
diameter $d$ under the bending moment $M$ and the torque $T$ are

$$\sigma = \frac{32 M}{\pi d^3} , \qquad \tau = \frac{16 T}{\pi d^3} .$$

In the model $d > 0$.
