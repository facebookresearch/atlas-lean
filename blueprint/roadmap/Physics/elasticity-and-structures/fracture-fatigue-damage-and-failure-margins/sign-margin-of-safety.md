# For a positive demand the margin of safety has the sign of the margin

For a real allowable $A$ and a demand $s > 0$, the [margin of safety](margin-of-safety.md) and the
[dimensional margin](dimensional-margin.md) have the same sign (each of them is negative, zero or
positive exactly when the other is):

$$\operatorname{sign}\left(\frac{A}{s} - 1\right) = \operatorname{sign}(A - s).$$
