# Changing the stress unit by a positive factor

Let $c > 0$ (the factor of a change of stress unit), $A$ real and $s > 0$. Expressing the
allowable and the demand as $c A$ and $c s$ multiplies the
[dimensional margin](dimensional-margin.md) by $c$ and changes neither the
[failure event](demand-failure.md) nor the [margin of safety](margin-of-safety.md):

$$
c A - c s = c (A - s), \qquad c A \le c s \iff A \le s, \qquad
\frac{c A}{c s} - 1 = \frac{A}{s} - 1.
$$
