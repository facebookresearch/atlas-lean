# The bar fails at some section exactly when the end demand reaches $A$

Let $\Phi$ be a [yield functional calibrated by the tensile test](uniaxial-calibrated.md), and let
$EA > 0$, $A_{cs} > 0$ and $L > 0$ (the fixed-fixed bar), with any real load $q$ and allowable $A$.
The [failure event](demand-failure.md) $A \le \Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma(x), 0, 0))$ holds at
some section $x \in [0, L]$, with $\sigma$ the [axial stress](axial-stress.md), exactly when

$$2 A_{cs} A \le |q| L,$$

that is, when the demand $|q| L / (2 A_{cs})$ at the end sections $x = 0$ and $x = L$ reaches the
allowable.
