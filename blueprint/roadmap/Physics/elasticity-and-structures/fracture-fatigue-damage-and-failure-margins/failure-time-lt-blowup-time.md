# For $r < 0$, the failure time comes strictly before the blow-up time

Let $K > 0$, $r < 0$ (Paris exponent $m > 2$, with $r = 1 - m/2$), $f > 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, with
$a_c$ the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md), and let $N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(r K)$
be the [Paris life](paris-life-pow.md) in the power branch. Then the failure time $N_f / f$ is
strictly smaller than the time at which the base $a_0^r + r K f t$ of the [power-branch
solution](paris-crack-pow.md) in time would reach zero:

$$\frac{N_f}{f} < \frac{a_0^r / (-r K)}{f} .$$
