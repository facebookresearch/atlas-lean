# Yield functional calibrated by the tensile test

A function $\Phi$ on real $3 \times 3$ stress matrices (tension positive), a supplied yield
functional, is calibrated by the tensile test when it returns the magnitude of the stress on every
uniaxial state:

$$\Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma, 0, 0)) = |\sigma| \qquad \text{for every real } \sigma.$$

Nothing is assumed about $\Phi$ on other stress states.
