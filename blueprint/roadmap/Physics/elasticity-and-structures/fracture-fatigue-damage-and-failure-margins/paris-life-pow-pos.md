# For $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$, $0 < a_0 < a_c$: positive power-branch life

Let $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$. Then the [power-branch Paris life](paris-life-pow.md) is
positive,

$$N_f = \frac{a_c^r - a_0^r}{r K} > 0,$$

for either sign of $r$: for $r > 0$ the numerator and the denominator are positive, and for $r < 0$
both are negative.
