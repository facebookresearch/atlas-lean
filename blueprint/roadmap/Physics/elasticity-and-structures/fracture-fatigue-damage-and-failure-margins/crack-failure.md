# Crack failure: the mode-I stress intensity reaches the toughness

For real numbers $K_{IC}$, $Y$, $\sigma$ and $a$, crack failure is the equality-inclusive event

$$K_{IC} \le K_I(a) = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}:$$

the [mode-I stress intensity](mode-istress-intensity.md) demand reaches the fracture toughness
$K_{IC}$. Since $K_I(a) = 0$ for $a < 0$ (convention $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$), at a negative
crack length the event holds exactly when $K_{IC} \le 0$. For positive $K_{IC}$, $Y$ and $\sigma$
and a crack length $a \ge 0$, it holds
[exactly from the critical crack length on](crack-failure-iff-critical-crack-length.md).
