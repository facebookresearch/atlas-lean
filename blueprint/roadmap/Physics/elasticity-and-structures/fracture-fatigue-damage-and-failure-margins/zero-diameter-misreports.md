# At zero diameter the stress formulas give zero demand and no failure

Let $\Phi$ be [calibrated on the bending–torsion states](bending-torsion-calibrated.md) with shear
factor $c$, let $A > 0$, and let $M$ and $T$ be any real numbers. At diameter $d = 0$ the convention
$x / 0 = 0$ makes the [bending stress](shaft-bending-stress.md) and the [shear
stress](shaft-shear-stress.md) both $0$, so the demand is $\Phi(S(0, 0)) = 0$ and the [failure
event](demand-failure.md) $A \le 0$ does not hold, whatever the load. The zero diameter is therefore
excluded, not covered: the sizing results take $d > 0$, and with a nonzero load [the minimum
diameter is positive](shaft-min-diameter-pos.md), so at $d = 0$ the demand would be within $A$
although $d < d_{\min}$.
