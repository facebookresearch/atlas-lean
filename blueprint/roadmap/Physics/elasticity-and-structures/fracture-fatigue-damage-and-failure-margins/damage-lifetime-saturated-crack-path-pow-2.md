# At $f = 0$ a crack below $a_c$ never fails, power branch

Let $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, with $a_c$ the [critical crack
length](critical-crack-length.md), and let $a(N) = (a_0^r + r K N)^{1/r}$ be the [power-branch
solution](paris-crack-pow.md) and $N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(r K)$ its [Paris life](paris-life-pow.md).
At load frequency $f = 0$ the [event path](saturated-crack-path.md) $a(\min(0, N_f))$ is the
constant $a_0$ at every time $t \ge 0$, and, as the damage path of every outcome $\omega$ of any set
$\Omega$ against the constant threshold $a_c$, its [damage lifetime](damage-lifetime.md) is
$\infty$: the threshold is never reached.
