# Mode-I stress intensity factor $K_I(a) = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$

For real numbers $Y$, $\sigma$ and $a$, the mode-I stress intensity factor of the adopted
linear-elastic fracture mechanics (LEFM) model is

$$K_I(a) = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a},$$

where $Y$ is a constant geometry factor, $\sigma$ the peak tensile stress (not a fatigue stress
range) and $a$ the crack length. The formula is intended for $a \ge 0$; with the convention
$\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$, it gives $K_I(a) = 0$ for every $a < 0$. In the physical reading $Y > 0$
and $\sigma > 0$. The formula is an adopted model input with a small-scale-yielding validity regime;
the results about it are its algebraic consequences, not an experimental validation of the model.
