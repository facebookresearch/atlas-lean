# For $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma > 0$, $a \ge 0$: crack failure iff $a_c \le a$

Let the fracture toughness $K_{IC}$, the geometry factor $Y$ and the peak tensile stress $\sigma$ be
positive, and let $a \ge 0$ be a crack length. The equality-inclusive
[crack failure event](crack-failure.md) $K_{IC} \le Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$ holds if and only if

$$a_c \le a,$$

where $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ is the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md): the
crack fails from the critical crack length on, the critical length included. This is the negation of
[subcriticality below $a_c$](mode-istress-intensity-lt-iff-lt-critica.md).
