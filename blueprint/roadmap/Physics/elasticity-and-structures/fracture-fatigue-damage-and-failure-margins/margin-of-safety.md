# Margin of safety for a positive demand

For a real allowable $A$ and a demand $s > 0$, the margin of safety is

$$\frac{A}{s} - 1.$$

It is defined only for a positive demand, which is a hypothesis of the definition. At $s = 0$ the
convention $x / 0 = 0$ would give $A / 0 - 1 = -1$, a
[false failure signal](ratio-at-zero-demand-misreports.md) for a positive allowable.
