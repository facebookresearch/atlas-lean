# For a positive allowable, the ratio at zero demand misreports failure

For an allowable $A > 0$, the ratio formula of the [margin of safety](margin-of-safety.md)
evaluated at zero demand is negative under the convention $x / 0 = 0$, namely
$A / 0 - 1 = -1 < 0$, which would signal failure; yet the demand $0$ does not fail, since
$A \le 0$ is false. This is why the margin of safety requires a positive demand.
