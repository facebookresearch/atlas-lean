# For $f > 0$, the logarithmic-branch lifetime is $N_f / f$

Let $K > 0$, $f > 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, with $a_c$ the [critical crack
length](critical-crack-length.md), and let $a(N) = a_0 e^{K N}$ be the [logarithmic-branch
solution](paris-crack-exp.md) and $N_f = \log(a_c / a_0)/K$ its [Paris life](paris-life-exp.md).
Take the [event path](saturated-crack-path.md) $d(t) = a(\min(f t, N_f))$ as the damage path of
every outcome $\omega$ of any set $\Omega$, with the constant threshold $a_c$. Then for every
$\omega$ its [damage lifetime](damage-lifetime.md), the infimum of the times $t \ge 0$ with
$d(t) \ge a_c$ ($\infty$ if there is none), is finite and equals $N_f / f$, the Paris life in cycles
divided by the cycles per unit time.
