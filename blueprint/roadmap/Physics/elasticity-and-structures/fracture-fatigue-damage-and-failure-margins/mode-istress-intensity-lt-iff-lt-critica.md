# For $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma > 0$, $a \ge 0$: $K_I < K_{IC}$ iff $a < a_c$

Let the fracture toughness $K_{IC}$, the geometry factor $Y$ and the peak tensile stress $\sigma$ be
positive, and let $a \ge 0$ be a crack length. Then the
[mode-I stress intensity](mode-istress-intensity.md) is subcritical exactly below the
[critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md) $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$:

$$Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a} < K_{IC} \iff a < a_c,$$

both inequalities strict. The positivity is used: for $Y \sigma = 0$ the left side holds for every
$a$ while $a_c = 0$.
