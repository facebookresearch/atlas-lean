# For $K, a_0, a_c > 0$, $r \ne 0$: power crack reaches $a_c$ at $N_f$

Let $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$, $a_0 > 0$ and $a_c > 0$. At the [Paris life](paris-life-pow.md)
$N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(rK)$, the base is $a_0^r + r K N_f = a_c^r > 0$, and the
[power-branch crack](paris-crack-pow.md) $a(N) = \bigl(a_0^r + r K N\bigr)^{1/r}$ reaches the target
length:

$$a(N_f) = a_c.$$

The target need not exceed $a_0$: for $a_c < a_0$ the life $N_f$ is negative.
