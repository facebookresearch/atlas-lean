# Outer-fiber bending stress of a solid circular shaft

For real numbers $M$ (bending moment) and $d$ (diameter), the bending stress at the outer fiber
$y = d/2$ of a solid circular shaft is, by the flexure formula,

$$\sigma = \frac{M\, (d/2)}{I} , \qquad I = \frac{\pi d^4}{64} ,$$

where $I$ is the [second moment of area](circle-second-moment.md) of the section, tension positive
on the fiber that $M$ stretches. For $d \ne 0$ it equals $32 M / (\pi d^3)$ ([closed
form](shaft-stresses-eq.md)). With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, at $d = 0$ the value is $0$ for every
$M$. In the model $d > 0$.
