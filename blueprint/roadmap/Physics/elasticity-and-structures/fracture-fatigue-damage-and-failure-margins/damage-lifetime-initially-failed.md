# A crack starting at or above the critical length has lifetime zero

Let $a_0$, $K_{IC}$, $Y$ and $\sigma$ be real numbers with $a_c \le a_0$, where
$a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ is the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md). Take the
constant path $t \mapsto a_0$ as the damage path of every outcome $\omega$ of any set $\Omega$, with
the constant threshold $a_c$. Then for every $\omega$ its [damage lifetime](damage-lifetime.md) is
$0$: the component is failed at time $0$. No crack-growth law is used; this is the [lifetime of a
component failed at time zero](damage-lifetime-eq-zero.md) for a constant path.
