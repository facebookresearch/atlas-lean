# Stress state at the critical point of a shaft in bending and torsion

For real numbers $\sigma$ and $\tau$, the bending–torsion stress state is the $3 \times 3$ stress
matrix, with index $0$ along the shaft axis and index $1$ circumferential,

$$S(\sigma, \tau) = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma & \tau & 0 \\ \tau & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} :$$

an axial normal stress $\sigma$, the axial–circumferential shear $\tau$ and no other stress. It is
the state at the critical surface point of a solid circular shaft under bending and torsion, with
$\sigma$ the [outer-fiber bending stress](shaft-bending-stress.md) and $\tau$ the [surface shear
stress](shaft-shear-stress.md).
