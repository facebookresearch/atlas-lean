# A bending–torsion calibration is a tensile-test calibration

Let $\Phi$ be a real function on $3 \times 3$ real stress matrices and $c$ a real number. If $\Phi$
is [calibrated on the bending–torsion states](bending-torsion-calibrated.md) with shear factor $c$,
then it is [calibrated by the tensile test](uniaxial-calibrated.md): for every real $\sigma$,
$\Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma, 0, 0)) = |\sigma|$. Indeed the uniaxial state is the
bending–torsion state with $\tau = 0$, where $\sqrt{\sigma^2} = |\sigma|$.
