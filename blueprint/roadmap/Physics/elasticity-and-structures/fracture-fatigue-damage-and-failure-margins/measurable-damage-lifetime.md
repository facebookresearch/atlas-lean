# Lifetime of measurable continuous monotone damage is measurable

Let $\Omega$ carry a σ-algebra. Suppose that for every time $t \ge 0$ the damage
$\omega \mapsto d(\omega, t)$ is measurable, the threshold $\omega \mapsto D(\omega)$ is
measurable, and every path $t \mapsto d(\omega, t)$ is continuous and nondecreasing. Then the
[lifetime](damage-lifetime.md) $\omega \mapsto \tau(\omega) \in [0, \infty]$ is measurable (for the
Borel σ-algebra of $[0, \infty]$): for every $t \ge 0$,
[its level set](lt-damage-lifetime-iff.md) $\{\omega : t < \tau(\omega)\}$ is
$\{\omega : d(\omega, t) < D(\omega)\}$.
