# For a nonnegative base, $a(N)^r = a_0^r + rKN$ in the power branch

Let $r \ne 0$ and let $K$, $a_0$ and $N$ be real with a nonnegative base
$B(N) = a_0^r + r K N \ge 0$. Raising the [power-branch crack](paris-crack-pow.md)
$a(N) = B(N)^{1/r}$ to the power $r$ recovers the affine base:

$$a(N)^r = a_0^r + r K N.$$

At $B(N) = 0$ both sides are $0$, by the convention $0^t = 0$ for $t \ne 0$.
