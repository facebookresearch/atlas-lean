# Demand of the axial stress through a calibrated yield functional

Let $\Phi$ be a [yield functional calibrated by the tensile test](uniaxial-calibrated.md),
$EA > 0$, $A_{cs} > 0$, and $L$, $q$, $x$ real. At the section $x$, the demand of the uniaxial
state carrying the [axial stress](axial-stress.md) $\sigma(x)$ is

$$\Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma(x), 0, 0)) = \frac{|q (L - 2x)|}{2 A_{cs}}.$$
