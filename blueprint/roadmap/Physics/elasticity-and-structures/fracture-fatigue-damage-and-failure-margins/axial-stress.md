# Axial stress of the uniform-load displacement of a bar

For real numbers $EA$ (axial rigidity), $L$ (length), $q$ (uniform axial load), $A_{cs}$
(cross-section area) and a section $x$, let $u(y) = q\, y (L - y) / (2 EA)$ be the closed-form
uniform-load displacement. The axial normal stress at $x$ is

$$\sigma(x) = \frac{EA\, u'(x)}{A_{cs}}.$$

For $EA > 0$ and $A_{cs} > 0$ it is [$q (L - 2x) / (2 A_{cs})$](axial-stress-eq.md); for $EA = 0$
the displacement is identically $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$, and so is the stress. In the physical reading $L$, $EA$ and $A_{cs}$ are positive.
