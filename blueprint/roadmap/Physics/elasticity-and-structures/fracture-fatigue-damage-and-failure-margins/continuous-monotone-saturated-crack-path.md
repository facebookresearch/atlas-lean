# The power-branch event path is continuous and nondecreasing

Let $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$, $f \ge 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, with $a_c$ the [critical crack
length](critical-crack-length.md), and let $a(N) = (a_0^r + r K N)^{1/r}$ be the [power-branch
solution](paris-crack-pow.md) and $N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(r K)$ its [Paris life](paris-life-pow.md).
Then the [event path](saturated-crack-path.md) $t \mapsto a(\min(f t, N_f))$, for times $t \ge 0$,
is continuous and nondecreasing.
