# The hit condition is the demand event at the threshold

For a damage path $d$, a threshold $D$, an outcome $\omega$ and a time $t \ge 0$, the hit
condition $d(\omega, t) - D(\omega) \in [0, \infty)$ holds exactly when the
[failure event](demand-failure.md) holds with allowable $D(\omega)$ and demand $d(\omega, t)$:
$D(\omega) \le d(\omega, t)$, equality included.
