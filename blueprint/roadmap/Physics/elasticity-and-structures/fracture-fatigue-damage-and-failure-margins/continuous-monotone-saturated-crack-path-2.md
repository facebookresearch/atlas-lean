# The logarithmic-branch event path is continuous and nondecreasing

Let $K > 0$, $a_0 > 0$ and $f \ge 0$, let $a(N) = a_0 e^{K N}$ be the [logarithmic-branch
solution](paris-crack-exp.md) and $N_f = \log(a_c / a_0)/K$ its [Paris life](paris-life-exp.md),
where $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ is the [critical crack length](critical-crack-length.md) for
any real $K_{IC}$, $Y$ and $\sigma$. Then the [event path](saturated-crack-path.md)
$t \mapsto a(\min(f t, N_f))$, for times $t \ge 0$, is continuous and nondecreasing. No relation
between $a_0$ and $a_c$ is needed: when $N_f \le 0$ the path is the constant $a(N_f)$.
