# A contact solution satisfies complementarity

For real numbers `k`, `g`, `f` and `u` with `u ≤ g` and `0 ≤ (k u - f)(v - u)` for every `v ≤ g`,
the [contact force](contact-force.md) `λ = f - k u` and the [gap](contact-gap.md) `g - u` satisfy
`λ ≥ 0`, `g - u ≥ 0` and `λ (g - u) = 0`: the force vanishes off contact and the gap vanishes under
load.
