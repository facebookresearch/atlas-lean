# Coulomb tractions are admissible closed contact states

For a real inner product space `E`, real numbers `μ ≥ 0` and `N ≥ 0`, and `v, t ∈ E` satisfying the
[vector Coulomb law](is-coulomb-traction.md), the closed contact state `(N, g = 0, t)` is
[admissible](is-admissible-contact.md): a sliding traction has norm `μ N` and a sticking one satisfies
the Coulomb bound.
