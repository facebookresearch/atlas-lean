# Film thickness of a plane slider

For real numbers `h₀` (inlet thickness), `h_L` (outlet thickness), `L` (slider length) and `x`, the
film thickness is `h(x) = h₀ + (h_L - h₀) x / L`: linear from `h₀` at `x = 0` to `h_L` at `x = L`.
With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, it is the constant `h₀` when `L = 0`; the intended domain is
`L > 0`.
