# Coulomb friction at nonzero velocity

The Coulomb friction law relating coefficient $\mu$, normal force $N$, velocity $v$ and friction force $F$ requires $F = -\mu N\,\operatorname{sign} v$ when $v \ne 0$ and $-\mu N \le F \le \mu N$ when $v = 0$. For $v \ne 0$, the law holds if and only if $F = -\mu N\,\operatorname{sign} v$.
