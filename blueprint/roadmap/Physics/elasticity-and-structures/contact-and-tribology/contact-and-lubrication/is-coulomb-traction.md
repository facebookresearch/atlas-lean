# Vector Coulomb friction law

For friction coefficient $\mu$ and compression-positive normal force $N$, a tangential traction $t$ and slip velocity $v$ in a real inner product space satisfy the vector Coulomb law when:
- if $v \ne 0$, then $t = -\mu N\, v/\|v\|$ (slip);
- if $v = 0$, then $\|t\| \le \mu N$ (stick).

The law is physically meaningful for $\mu \ge 0$ and $N \ge 0$.
