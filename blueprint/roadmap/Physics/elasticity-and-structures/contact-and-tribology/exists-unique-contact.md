# Scalar unilateral contact has exactly one solution

For real numbers `k > 0`, `g` and `f`, there is exactly one `u ≤ g` with `0 ≤ (k u - f)(v - u)` for
every `v ≤ g`: the variational inequality of
[Stampacchia's theorem](../../../Analysis/convex-analysis-and-variations/stampacchia-s-theorem/exists-unique-stampacchia.md)
on the [admissible set](admissible-set.md) with the [stiffness map](stiffness-map.md) and the load
`f`.
