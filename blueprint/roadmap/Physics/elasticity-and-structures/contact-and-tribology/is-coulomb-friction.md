# Scalar Coulomb friction law

For real numbers `μ` (friction coefficient), `N` (normal force), `v` (sliding velocity) and `F`
(friction force), the Coulomb law holds when `F = -μ N sign(v)` if `v ≠ 0` (slip) and
`F ∈ [-μ N, μ N]` if `v = 0` (stick). The quantities are measured along one tangent direction; the
law is physically meaningful for `μ ≥ 0` and `N ≥ 0`.
