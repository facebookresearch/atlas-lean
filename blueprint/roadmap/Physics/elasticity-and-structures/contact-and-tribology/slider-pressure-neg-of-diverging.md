# For a diverging film the slider pressure is negative inside

For real numbers `μ > 0`, `U > 0`, `L > 0` and a diverging film `0 < h₀ < h_L`, the
[closed-form pressure](slider-pressure.md) is negative at every `x` with `0 < x < L`: the gauge pressure
falls below ambient, where a real film cavitates, so the no-cavitation model does not apply.
