# Coulomb friction cone

For a real inner product space `E` and a real number `μ` (friction coefficient), the friction cone
is the set of pairs `(N, t) ∈ ℝ × E` with `‖t‖ ≤ μ N` and `N ≥ 0`, for the compression-positive
normal force `N` and the tangent traction `t`. The norm is the inner-product norm, and membership
depends on `t` only through `‖t‖`; the slip threshold `‖t‖ = μ N` is included, and for `μ = 0` the
cone is the ray `t = 0`, `N ≥ 0`.
