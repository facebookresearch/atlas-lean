# Slider pressure gradient

The pressure gradient of the plane slider bearing with viscosity $\mu$, sliding speed $U$, inlet film $h_0$, outlet film $h_L$ and length $L$, at position $x$: $6 \mu U (h - 2 h_0 h_L/(h_0 + h_L))/h^3$ with linear film thickness $h = h_0 + (h_L - h_0)x/L$. Each quotient is $0$ when its denominator ($L$, $h_0 + h_L$ or $h^3$) is $0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
