# Scalar unilateral contact as a Stampacchia variational inequality

For stiffness $k > 0$, obstacle $g \in \mathbb{R}$ and load $f \in \mathbb{R}$, there is exactly one $u \le g$ such that $(k u - f)(v - u) \ge 0$ for every $v \le g$. This is Stampacchia's theorem for the convex set $\{u \le g\}$ and the linear spring $u \mapsto k u$.
