# Plane-slider pressure

For viscosity $\mu$, sliding speed $U$, end film thicknesses $h_0$ and $h_L$, and length $L$, let $h(x) = h_0 + (h_L - h_0)x/L$ be the linear film thickness (equal to $h_0$ when $L = 0$). The plane-slider pressure at $x$ is

$$p(x) = \frac{6 \mu U L\,(h_0 - h(x))(h(x) - h_L)}{(h_0^2 - h_L^2)\, h(x)^2},$$

with the value $0$ when $(h_0^2 - h_L^2)\,h(x)^2 = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
