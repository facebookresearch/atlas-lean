# Admissible deviatoric stresses

The set of real $3 \times 3$ matrices $\sigma$ that are
[symmetric](../cauchy-stress-small-strain-and-mohr-s-circle/is-symmetric-stress.md),
$\sigma^{\mathsf T} = \sigma$, and trace-free, $\operatorname{tr} \sigma = 0$. In a
pressure-insensitive model of perfect plasticity the yield section of the
[return map](deviatoric-return.md) is taken to be a subset of this set.
