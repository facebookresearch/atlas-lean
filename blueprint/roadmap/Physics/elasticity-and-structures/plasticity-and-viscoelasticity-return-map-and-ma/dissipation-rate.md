# Power dissipated in the dashpot of a Maxwell branch

For a [Maxwell branch](maxwell-branch.md) with viscosity $\eta > 0$ and a real stress $s$, the power
per unit volume dissipated in the dashpot is $s^2 / \eta = s \cdot (s / \eta)$, the stress times the
strain rate $s / \eta$ of the dashpot. It is [positive exactly when](dissipation-rate-pos-iff.md)
$s \ne 0$.
