# Constitutive relation of a Maxwell branch

For a Maxwell branch with modulus `E` and viscosity `η`, a history on a time
set `I`: at every `t ∈ I` the stress `σ` has rate `σ' t`, the strain `ε` has
rate `ε' t`, and the strain rate is the spring strain rate `σ' t / E` plus the
dashpot strain rate `σ t / η`.
