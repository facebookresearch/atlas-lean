# Stress relaxation of a Maxwell branch

Let a Maxwell branch have modulus `E > 0` and viscosity `η > 0`, and let a
stress–strain history obey `ε' = σ' / E + σ / η` for `t ≥ 0`. If the strain is
held at `ε 0` for `t ≥ 0`, then `σ t = σ 0 exp(-(E / η) t)` for every `t ≥ 0`,
an exponential decay to zero with relaxation time `τ = η / E` (Roylance,
Example 2).
