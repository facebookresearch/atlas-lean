# Coupling stiffness of a laminate

For `n ∈ ℕ`, interfaces `z_0, …, z_n` (heights from the reference plane) and ply stiffnesses
`Q̄_0, …, Q̄_{n−1}` (real `3 × 3` matrices in global axes, ply `i` occupying `[z_i, z_{i+1}]`),
`B = ∑_i ((z_{i+1}² − z_i²) / 2) Q̄_i`. In the physical reading the interfaces increase from bottom
to top and the ply stiffnesses are symmetric positive definite; the formula is defined for all real
inputs.
