# Axial force of a stress distribution over a rectangular section

For a normal stress distribution `σ` (tension positive) over a rectangular section of width `b` and
height `h` (real numbers), the axial force is `N = ∫_{-h/2}^{h/2} b * σ(y) dy`, an oriented integral
(the interval is reversed when `h < 0`). It is `0` when `b * σ` is not integrable on the interval,
by the convention for integrals. The physical reading is `b > 0` and `h > 0`.
