# The static and kinematic certificates are complementary

For all real `L` and `Mp` and each critical section `e`, the moment `σ_e` of `σ = (-Mp, Mp, -Mp)`
has the sign of the hinge rotation `θ_e` of the normalized mechanism `θ = B (2 / L)`, with the
[hinge-rotation matrix](hinge-rotation.md) `B = (-2 / L, 4 / L, -2 / L)ᵀ` (a 3 × 1 matrix: rows the
critical sections at the left end, mid-span and right end; column the central deflection), so that
`Mp * |θ_e| = σ_e * θ_e`: the plastic dissipation leaves no slack. At `L = 0` the rotations are `0`
by the convention `x / 0 = 0` and both sides vanish.
