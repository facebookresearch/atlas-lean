# Work balance of the unit mechanism

For a span `L > 0` and all real `Mp` and `w`, at unit central deflection the hinge rotations
`(-2 / L, 4 / L, -2 / L)` of the [hinge-rotation matrix](hinge-rotation.md)
`B = (-2 / L, 4 / L, -2 / L)ᵀ` (a 3 × 1 matrix: rows the critical sections at the left end, mid-span
and right end; column the central deflection) absorb the plastic work
`∑_e Mp * |(B 1)_e| = 8 * Mp / L`, the uniform load of intensity `w` does the work
`(w f) · 1 = w * L / 2`, with the [work vector](uniform-load-work.md) `f = (L / 2)`, and the two are
equal if and only if `w = 16 * Mp / L ^ 2` (Neal, p. 27). The identities hold for all real `Mp` and
`w`; the physical reading of the plastic work is `Mp > 0`.
