# Bending moment of a stress distribution over a rectangular section

For a normal stress distribution `σ` (tension positive) over a rectangular section of width `b` and
height `h` (real numbers), the bending moment about the neutral axis is
`M = ∫_{-h/2}^{h/2} y * (b * σ(y)) dy`, an oriented integral (the interval is reversed when
`h < 0`). It is `0` when the integrand is not integrable on the interval, by the convention for
integrals. The physical reading is `b > 0` and `h > 0`.
