# The fully plastic stress block carries no axial force

For all real `b`, `h` and `σy`, the [axial force](section-force.md) of the
[fully plastic stress block](fully-plastic-stress.md) over the section is `0`: equal halves of the
symmetric section carry `σy` and `-σy`, consistent with a plastic neutral axis at mid-depth. The
integral is oriented; the physical reading is `b > 0`, `h > 0` and `σy > 0`.
