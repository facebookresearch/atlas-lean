# Integration by parts for a deflection vanishing at both ends

Let `L > 0` and functions `w, w', w'' : ℝ → ℝ` such that, at every `x ∈ [0, L]`, `w` has derivative
`w'(x)` and `w'` has derivative `w''(x)`, with `w''` continuous on `[0, L]`, with `w(0) = w(L) = 0`.
Then `∫₀ᴸ w'(x)² dx = −∫₀ᴸ w(x) w''(x) dx`.
