# Lower bound for the pinned-column energy

Let `EI > 0`. Let `L > 0`, and let `w` have derivative `w'` and `w'` have
derivative `w''` at every point of `[0, L]`, endpoints included, in the ordinary two-sided sense, with `w''` continuous on `[0,
L]`, `w 0 = 0` and `w L = 0`. Then for every load `P`, the quadratic energy
`EI ∫₀ᴸ (w'')² - P ∫₀ᴸ (w')²` is at least `(π² EI / L² - P) ∫₀ᴸ (w')²`.
