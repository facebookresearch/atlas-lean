# Wirtinger-type inequality for a pinned column

Let `L > 0`, and let `w` have derivative `w'` and `w'` have derivative `w''`
at every point of `[0, L]`, endpoints included, in the ordinary two-sided sense, with `w''` continuous on `[0, L]`, `w 0 = 0` and
`w L = 0`. Then `∫₀ᴸ (w')² ≤ (L / π)² ∫₀ᴸ (w'')²`.
