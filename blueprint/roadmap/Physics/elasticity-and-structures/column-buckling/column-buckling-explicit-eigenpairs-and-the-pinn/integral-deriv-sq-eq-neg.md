# Integration by parts for a deflection vanishing at both ends

Let `L > 0`, and let `w` have derivative `w'` and `w'` have derivative `w''`
at every point of `[0, L]`, endpoints included, in the ordinary two-sided sense, with `w''` continuous on `[0, L]`. If `w 0 = 0`
and `w L = 0`, then `∫₀ᴸ (w')² = -∫₀ᴸ w * w''`.
