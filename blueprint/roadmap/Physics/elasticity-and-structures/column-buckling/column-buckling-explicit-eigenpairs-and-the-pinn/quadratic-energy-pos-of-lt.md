# Pinned-column energy is positive below the critical load

Let `EI > 0` and `P < π² EI / L²`. Let `L > 0`, and let `w` have derivative
`w'` and `w'` have derivative `w''` at every point of `[0, L]`, endpoints included, in the ordinary two-sided sense, with `w''`
continuous on `[0, L]`, `w 0 = 0` and `w L = 0`. If `w x ≠ 0` for some `x ∈
[0, L]`, then `EI ∫₀ᴸ (w'')² - P ∫₀ᴸ (w')² > 0`.
