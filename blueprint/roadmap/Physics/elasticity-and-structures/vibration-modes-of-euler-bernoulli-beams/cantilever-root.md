# Least positive root of cos z cosh z + 1 = 0

The real `z₁ = inf {z > 0 : cos z cosh z + 1 = 0}`, the infimum of the positive roots of the
[characteristic function](cantilever-char.md) `f(z) = cos z cosh z + 1`. It is attained: `z₁` is the
[least positive root](is-least-cantilever-root.md), and it lies in
[`(π / 2, π)`](cantilever-root-mem-ioo.md).
