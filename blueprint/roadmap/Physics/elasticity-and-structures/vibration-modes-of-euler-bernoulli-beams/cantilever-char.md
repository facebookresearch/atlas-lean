# Characteristic function of the clamped–free beam

For a real `z`, `f(z) = cos z cosh z + 1`. For `β > 0` and `L > 0`, a
[family member](mode-family.md) with nonzero coefficients satisfies the
[clamped–free conditions](is-clamped-free.md) exactly when `f(βL) = 0`
([characteristic equation](exists-is-clamped-free-iff.md)).
