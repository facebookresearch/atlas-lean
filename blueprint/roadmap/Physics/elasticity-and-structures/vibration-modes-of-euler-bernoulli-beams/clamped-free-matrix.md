# Boundary matrix of the clamped–free beam

For a real `z`, the `4 × 4` matrix `M(z)` with rows `(1, 0, 1, 0)`, `(0, 1, 0, 1)`,
`(−cos z, −sin z, cosh z, sinh z)` and `(sin z, −cos z, sinh z, cosh z)`. At `z = βL`, applied to
the coefficients of a [family member](mode-family.md), its rows give `W(0)`, `W'(0) / β`,
`W''(L) / β²` and `W'''(L) / β³` for `β ≠ 0` ([boundary traces](mode-family-traces.md)).
