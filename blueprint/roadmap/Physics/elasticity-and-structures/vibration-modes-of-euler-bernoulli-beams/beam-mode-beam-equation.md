# At its modal frequency the supported mode solves the beam equation

For `EI > 0`, `ρA > 0`, a real `L ≠ 0` and a positive integer `k`, the
[simply supported mode](beam-mode.md) `w(x, t) = sin(kπx / L) cos(ω t)` with `ω` the
[modal frequency](beam-freq.md) `ω_k = (kπ / L)² √(EI / ρA)` satisfies
`ρA ∂²w / ∂t² + EI ∂⁴w / ∂x⁴ = 0` at every `x` and `t`.
