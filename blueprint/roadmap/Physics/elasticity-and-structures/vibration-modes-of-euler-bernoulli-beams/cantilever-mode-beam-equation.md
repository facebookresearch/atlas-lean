# The clamped–free mode solves the beam equation at its frequency

For `EI > 0`, `ρA > 0` and all reals `L`, `z`, `x` and `t`, the function `w(x, t) = W(x) cos(ω t)`,
with `W` the [clamped–free mode](cantilever-mode.md) and `ω` the
[natural frequency](cantilever-freq.md) `ω = (z / L)² √(EI / ρA)`, satisfies
`ρA ∂²w / ∂t² + EI ∂⁴w / ∂x⁴ = 0`. No root condition on `z` is needed.
