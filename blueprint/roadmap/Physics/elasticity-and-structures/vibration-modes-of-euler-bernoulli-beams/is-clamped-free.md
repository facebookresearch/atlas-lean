# Clamped–free boundary conditions

A function `W : ℝ → ℝ` satisfies the clamped–free conditions for the length `L` if `L > 0`,
`W(0) = 0`, `W'(0) = 0`, `W''(L) = 0` and `W'''(L) = 0`, derivatives being `0` where they do not
exist: zero deflection and slope at the clamp `x = 0`, and zero curvature and third derivative at
the free end `x = L` (zero bending moment and shear force when `EI > 0`).
