# Boundary traces of a family member through the boundary matrix

For all reals `β` and `L` and coefficients `c ∈ ℝ⁴`, the [family member](mode-family.md)
`W(x) = c₀ cos βx + c₁ sin βx + c₂ cosh βx + c₃ sinh βx` and the
[boundary matrix](clamped-free-matrix.md) `M = M(βL)` satisfy `W(0) = (M c)₀`, `W'(0) = β (M c)₁`,
`W''(L) = β² (M c)₂` and `W'''(L) = β³ (M c)₃`.
