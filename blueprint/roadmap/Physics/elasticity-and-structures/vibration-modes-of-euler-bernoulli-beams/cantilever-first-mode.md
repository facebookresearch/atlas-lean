# The first clamped–free mode

For `EI > 0`, `ρA > 0` and `L > 0`, let `z₁` be the [least positive root](cantilever-root.md) of
`cos z cosh z + 1 = 0`. The [clamped–free mode](cantilever-mode.md) `W` for `z₁` satisfies the
[clamped–free conditions](is-clamped-free.md) for the length `L`, has `W(L) ≠ 0`, and
`w(x, t) = W(x) cos(ω₁ t)` with `ω₁ = (z₁ / L)² √(EI / ρA)` satisfies
`ρA ∂²w / ∂t² + EI ∂⁴w / ∂x⁴ = 0` at every `x` and `t`.
