# Reflected laminate stack

For interface heights $z_0, \dots, z_n$ and ply stiffness matrices $\bar Q_0, \dots, \bar Q_{n-1}$ (real $3 \times 3$), the stack is reflected when the interfaces are symmetric about the reference plane $z = 0$ and each ply has the stiffness of its mirror ply:

$$z_{n-k} = -z_k \quad (0 \le k \le n), \qquad \bar Q_{n-1-i} = \bar Q_i \quad (0 \le i \le n-1).$$
