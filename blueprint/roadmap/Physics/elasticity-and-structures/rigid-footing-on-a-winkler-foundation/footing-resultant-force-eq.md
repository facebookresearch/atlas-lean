# Closed form of the Winkler resultant force

For all real `k_s`, `a`, `b` and `B`, the [resultant force](footing-resultant-force.md) of the
Winkler contact pressure is `∫_{-B/2}^{B/2} k_s (a + b x) dx = k_s B a`, the integral being oriented
when `B < 0`: the antisymmetric part `k_s b x` of the pressure integrates to zero.
