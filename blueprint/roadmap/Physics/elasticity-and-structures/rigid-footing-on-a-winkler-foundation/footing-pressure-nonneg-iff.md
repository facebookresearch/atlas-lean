# The full-contact criterion for a rigid footing on a Winkler foundation

For a subgrade modulus `k_s > 0`, a footing width `B > 0` and real `a` and `b`, the
[Winkler contact pressure](footing-pressure.md) `p(x) = k_s (a + b x)` is nonnegative at every
point `x ∈ [-B/2, B/2]` of the footing if and only if `a ≥ |b| B / 2`: full contact, with no
tension cutoff, is admissible exactly under this condition. Equality allows zero pressure at one
edge.
