# Moment of the Winkler contact pressure about the footing centre

For a subgrade modulus `k_s`, a uniform settlement `a`, a small rotation `b` and a footing width
`B`, all real, the moment about the footing centre, per unit out-of-plane length, of the affine
contact pressure `p(x) = k_s (a + b x)`, with `x` the lateral coordinate from the centre, is the
oriented integral `M = ∫_{-B/2}^{B/2} x k_s (a + b x) dx`, which has the units of a force (a
force times a length, per unit length). For `B > 0` it is positive when the pressure rises toward
`+x`. The physical regime is `B > 0` and `k_s > 0`, a rigid footing in full Winkler contact,
integrated without a no-tension cutoff. For `B < 0` the lower bound `-B/2` exceeds the upper bound
`B/2`, and the oriented integral is minus the integral over `[B/2, -B/2]`.
