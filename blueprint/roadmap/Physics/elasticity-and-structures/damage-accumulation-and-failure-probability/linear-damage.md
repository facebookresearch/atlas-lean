# Linear damage path in each scenario

Let $\Omega$ be a type of scenarios, and for each scenario $\omega$ let $d_0(\omega)$ be an initial
damage and $r(\omega)$ a rate, real numbers of any sign. The linear damage path in scenario $\omega$
at time $t \in [0, \infty)$ is

$$d(\omega, t) = d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t.$$

Time runs over the nonnegative reals, and the definition puts no condition on $d_0$ or $r$. In the
physical reading the rate is nonnegative, so that damage accumulates.
