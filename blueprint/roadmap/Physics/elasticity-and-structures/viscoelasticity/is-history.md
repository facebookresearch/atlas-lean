# Stress and strain histories of a Maxwell branch on a set of times

For a [Maxwell branch](maxwell-branch.md) with modulus $E$ and viscosity $\eta$, a set $I$ of real
times and real functions $\sigma$, $\sigma'$, $\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon'$ of time, the stress
history $\sigma$ and the strain history $\varepsilon$, with rates $\sigma'$ and $\varepsilon'$, obey
the constitutive relation of the branch on $I$ if at every $t \in I$, $\sigma$ has the two-sided
derivative $\sigma'(t)$ at $t$, $\varepsilon$ has the two-sided derivative $\varepsilon'(t)$ at $t$,
and

$$\varepsilon'(t) = \frac{\sigma'(t)}{E} + \frac{\sigma(t)}{\eta},$$

the strain rate of the spring plus that of the dashpot. The stress is tension positive and the
strain extension positive. The derivatives are two-sided also at an endpoint of $I$, so a step of
strain or stress at the start of a test is represented by starting $I$ after the step.
