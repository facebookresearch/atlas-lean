# Energy balance of a Maxwell branch

Let a Maxwell branch have modulus $E > 0$ and viscosity $\eta > 0$, and let stress and strain histories $\sigma$ and $\varepsilon$ obey [the constitutive relation](is-history.md) on a set $I$: both are differentiable, in the two-sided sense, at every time of $I$, and $\varepsilon' = \sigma' / E + \sigma / \eta$ there. Then at every $t \in I$ the stored energy $\sigma^2 / (2E)$ is differentiable at $t$ (two-sided), with derivative

$$\sigma(t)\, \varepsilon'(t) - \frac{\sigma(t)^2}{\eta},$$

the input power minus the dashpot dissipation.
