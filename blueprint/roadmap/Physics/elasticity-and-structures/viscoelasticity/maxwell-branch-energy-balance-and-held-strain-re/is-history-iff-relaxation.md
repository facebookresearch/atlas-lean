# Relaxation form of the Maxwell model

Let a Maxwell branch have modulus $E > 0$ and viscosity $\eta > 0$, and let $\tau = \eta / E$. For stress and strain histories $\sigma$ and $\varepsilon$ that are differentiable, in the two-sided sense, at every time of a set $I$, with derivatives $\sigma'$ and $\varepsilon'$, the series relation $\varepsilon' = \sigma' / E + \sigma / \eta$ holds at every time of $I$ if and only if the relaxation form $\sigma' + \sigma / \tau = E\, \varepsilon'$ does. The differentiability is the same as in [the constitutive relation](is-history.md). This is Maxwell's $dF/dt = E\, dS/dt - F/T$ with relaxation time $T = \tau$ and viscosity $E T = \eta$.
