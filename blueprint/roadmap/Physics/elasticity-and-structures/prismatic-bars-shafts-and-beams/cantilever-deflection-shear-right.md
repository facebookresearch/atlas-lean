# For EI ≠ 0 the cantilever polynomial has tip shear force P

Let $EI \ne 0$ and let $L$, $q$ and $P$ be real. The
[cantilever deflection](cantilever-deflection.md) satisfies

$$-EI\, w'''(L) = P:$$

the shear force $-EI\, w'''$ at the free end balances the tip force, the free-end shear condition of
the [clamped-free cantilever problem](is-cantilever-solution.md). For $EI = 0$ the left side is $0$.
