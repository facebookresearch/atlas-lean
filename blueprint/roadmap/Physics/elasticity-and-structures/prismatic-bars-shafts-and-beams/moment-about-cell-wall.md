# The wall resultants of the closed cell carry the torque 2abq

For all real $a$, $b$ and $q$ and every reference point $O \in \mathbb{R}^3$, the
[moment about O](../../classical-mechanics/rigid-bodies-and-mechanisms/statics-of-force-systems/moment-about.md)
of the [wall resultants](cell-wall-force.md) $F_i$ applied at the
[wall midpoints](cell-wall-midpoint.md) $m_i$, with no free couples, is

$$\sum_{i=1}^{4} (m_i - O) \times F_i = (0, 0, 2abq).$$

For $a, b > 0$ the cell carries the torque $T = 2abq = 2Aq$ about the member axis $+z$, with
$A = ab$ the area enclosed by the median line; it is positive for counterclockwise shear flow
($q > 0$).
