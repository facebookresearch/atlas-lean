# Wall resultants of a uniform shear flow in a closed rectangular cell

For real numbers $a$, $b$ and $q$, the wall resultants are the four vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$

$$F_1 = (q a, 0, 0), \quad F_2 = (0, q b, 0), \quad F_3 = (-q a, 0, 0), \quad F_4 = (0, -q b, 0),$$

in the order of the [wall midpoints](cell-wall-midpoint.md) (bottom, right, top, left). For
$a, b > 0$, $F_i$ is the resultant of the uniform shear flow $q$ (force per length of median line)
on wall $i$: $q$ times the wall length ($a$ for the bottom and top walls, $b$ for the right and left
walls), directed along the wall, counterclockwise about $+z$ when $q > 0$, with line of action
through the wall midpoint.
