# A free-free bar solution requires zero net load

Let $L > 0$ and let $k$, $P_0$ and $P_1$ be real numbers and $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$.
If $u$ solves the [free-free problem](is-free-free-solution.md) on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $k$, load
$f$ and end forces $P_0$ and $P_1$, then

$$P_0 + P_1 + \int_0^L f(x)\, dx = 0:$$

the end forces and the distributed load are in equilibrium. The load is continuous on $[0, L]$,
being $-k\, u''$. The condition is necessary for a solution to exist; its sufficiency is not part of
the statement.
