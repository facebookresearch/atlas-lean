# Displacement of a fixed-fixed bar under a uniform axial load

For real numbers $EA$, $L$, $q$ and $x$, the axial displacement is

$$u(x) = \frac{q\, x\, (L - x)}{2\, EA}.$$

It is the displacement of a bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, fixed at both ends, under the
uniform distributed axial load $q$: for $L > 0$ and $EA > 0$ it
[solves the fixed-fixed problem](is-fixed-fixed-solution-axial-displaceme.md) $-EA\, u'' = q$,
$u(0) = u(L) = 0$, and [every solution equals it](eq-on-axial-displacement.md) on $[0, L]$. In the
physical reading $L > 0$, $EA > 0$ and $x \in [0, L]$; the formula is defined for all real inputs,
and with the convention $a/0 = 0$ it is $0$ when $EA = 0$.
