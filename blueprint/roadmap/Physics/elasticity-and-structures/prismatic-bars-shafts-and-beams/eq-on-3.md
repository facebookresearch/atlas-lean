# Uniqueness for the fixed-traction problem

Let $k \ne 0$ and let $L$ and $P$ be real numbers and $f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. If $u$
and $v$ both solve the [fixed-traction problem](is-fixed-traction-solution.md) on $[0, L]$ with
rigidity $k$, load $f$ and end force $P$, then $u(x) = v(x)$ for every $x \in [0, L]$: both equal
the [fixed-traction displacement](fixed-traction-displacement.md) there
([every solution equals it](eq-on-fixed-traction-displacement.md)). No condition on $L$ or $f$ is
needed.
