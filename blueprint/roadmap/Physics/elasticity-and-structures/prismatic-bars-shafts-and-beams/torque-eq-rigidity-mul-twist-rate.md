# Torque equals GJ times the twist rate under the compatibility law

Let $a, b, t, G > 0$, let $q$ and $\theta'$ be real and $O \in \mathbb{R}^3$, and let $T$ be the
$z$-component of the [moment about O](moment-about-cell-wall.md) of the wall resultants of the
uniform shear flow $q$. If the [compatibility law](is-closed-cell-compatible.md) holds, then

$$T = G\, J\, \theta', \qquad J = \frac{2\, a^2 b^2\, t}{a + b},$$

with $J$ the [torsion constant](closed-cell-torsion-constant.md): $G J$ is the effective torsional
rigidity of the cell, the $GJ$ of the circular-shaft model.
