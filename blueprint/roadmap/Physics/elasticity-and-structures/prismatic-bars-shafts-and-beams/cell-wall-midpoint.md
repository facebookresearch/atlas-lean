# Wall midpoints of a thin closed rectangular cell

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the wall midpoints are the four points of $\mathbb{R}^3$

$$m_1 = (0, -b/2, 0), \quad m_2 = (a/2, 0, 0), \quad m_3 = (0, b/2, 0), \quad m_4 = (-a/2, 0, 0).$$

For $a, b > 0$ they are the midpoints of the bottom, right, top and left walls of the median
rectangle $[-a/2, a/2] \times [-b/2, b/2]$ of a thin closed rectangular cell, in the section plane
$z = 0$, listed counterclockwise about the member axis $+z$.
