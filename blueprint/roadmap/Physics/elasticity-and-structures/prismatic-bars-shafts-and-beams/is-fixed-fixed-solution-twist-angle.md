# The uniform-torque twist angle solves the fixed-fixed shaft problem

Let $L > 0$, $GJ > 0$ and $t$ real. The [twist angle](twist-angle.md)
$\varphi(x) = t\, x\, (L - x)/(2\, GJ)$ solves the [fixed-fixed problem](is-fixed-fixed-solution.md)
on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $GJ$ and constant load $t$: it is twice continuously differentiable,
$-GJ\, \varphi''(x) = t$ for every $x \in [0, L]$, and $\varphi(0) = \varphi(L) = 0$.
