# The uniform-load bar displacement solves the fixed-fixed problem

Let $L > 0$, $EA > 0$ and $q$ real. The [axial displacement](axial-displacement.md)
$u(x) = q\, x\, (L - x)/(2\, EA)$ solves the [fixed-fixed problem](is-fixed-fixed-solution.md) on
$[0, L]$ with rigidity $EA$ and constant load $q$: it is twice continuously differentiable,
$-EA\, u''(x) = q$ for every $x \in [0, L]$, and $u(0) = u(L) = 0$.
