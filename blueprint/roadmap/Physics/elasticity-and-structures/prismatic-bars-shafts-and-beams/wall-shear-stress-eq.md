# Wall shear stress of the closed cell in terms of the torque

Let $a \ne 0$, $b \ne 0$ and $t > 0$, let $q$ be real and $O \in \mathbb{R}^3$, and let $T$ be the
$z$-component of the [moment about O](moment-about-cell-wall.md) of the wall resultants of the
uniform shear flow $q$, so that $T = 2abq$. Then the [wall shear stress](wall-shear-stress.md) is

$$\tau = \frac{q}{t} = \frac{T}{2\, a b\, t}.$$
