# End slopes of the simply supported beam under a uniform load

Let $EI > 0$, and let $w$ solve the [simply supported problem](is-simply-supported-solution.md) with
bending stiffness $EI$, span $L$ and uniform load $q$. Then

$$w'(0) = \frac{q L^3}{24\, EI}, \qquad w'(L) = -\frac{q L^3}{24\, EI}.$$

The supports do not constrain the slope; these values follow from the [uniqueness](eq-on-4.md) of
the solution on $[0, L]$.
