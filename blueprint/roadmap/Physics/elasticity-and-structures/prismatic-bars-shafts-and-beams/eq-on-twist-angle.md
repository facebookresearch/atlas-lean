# Uniqueness of the uniform-torque fixed-fixed twist angle

Let $L > 0$, $GJ > 0$ and $t$ real. If $\varphi$ solves the
[fixed-fixed problem](is-fixed-fixed-solution.md) on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $GJ$ and constant load
$t$, then

$$\varphi(x) = \frac{t\, x\, (L - x)}{2\, GJ} \qquad \text{for every } x \in [0, L],$$

the [twist angle](twist-angle.md). Outside $[0, L]$, $\varphi$ is not determined.
