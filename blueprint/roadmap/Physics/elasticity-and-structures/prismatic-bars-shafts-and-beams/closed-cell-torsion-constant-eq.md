# Bredt's formula for the torsion constant of the closed cell

Let $a, b, t > 0$, and let $(\ell_1, \ell_2, \ell_3, \ell_4) = (a, b, a, b)$ be the
[wall lengths](cell-wall-length.md). The [torsion constant](closed-cell-torsion-constant.md)
$J = 2a^2b^2t/(a + b)$ satisfies

$$J = \frac{4\, (ab)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{4} \ell_i / t} = \frac{4A^2}{2(a + b)/t}, \qquad J > 0,$$

with $A = ab$ the area enclosed by the median line: Bredt's formula
$J = 4A^2 \big/ \oint \frac{ds}{t}$ for walls of constant thickness $t$.
