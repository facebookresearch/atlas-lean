# Dimension check: the bar displacement formula gives a length

In the group of physical dimensions generated by the seven ISQ base dimensions, with $\mathsf{L}$
the dimension of length and $\mathsf{F} = \mathsf{M}\, \mathsf{L}\, \mathsf{T}^{-2}$ that of force,

$$\mathsf{F}\, \mathsf{L}^{-1} \cdot \mathsf{L} \cdot \mathsf{L} \,/\, \mathsf{F} = \mathsf{L}.$$

This is the dimension of the [axial displacement](axial-displacement.md) $q\, x\, (L - x)/(2\, EA)$:
a load per length times two lengths, divided by an axial rigidity (a force), is a length. Only
dimensions are compared, not numerical values.
