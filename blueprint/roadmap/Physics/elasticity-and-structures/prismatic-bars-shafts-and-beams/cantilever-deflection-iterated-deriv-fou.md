# For EI ≠ 0 the cantilever polynomial satisfies EI w'''' = q

Let $EI \ne 0$ and let $L$, $q$, $P$ and $x$ be real. The
[cantilever deflection](cantilever-deflection.md)
$w(x) = q\, x^2 (6L^2 - 4Lx + x^2)/(24\, EI) + P\, x^2 (3L - x)/(6\, EI)$ satisfies

$$EI\, w''''(x) = q$$

at every real $x$. For $EI = 0$ the polynomial is identically $0$ and the identity holds only when
$q = 0$.
