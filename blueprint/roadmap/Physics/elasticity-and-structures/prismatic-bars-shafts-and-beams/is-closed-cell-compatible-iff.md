# Twist rate of a thin closed cell under the compatibility law

Let $a, b, t, G > 0$, let $q$ and $\theta'$ be real and $O \in \mathbb{R}^3$, and let $T$ be the
$z$-component of the [moment about O](moment-about-cell-wall.md) of the wall resultants of the
uniform shear flow $q$, so that $T = 2abq$. Then the
[compatibility law](is-closed-cell-compatible.md) $\frac{q}{Gt} \cdot 2(a + b) = 2ab\, \theta'$
holds if and only if

$$\theta' = \frac{T\, (a + b)}{2\, G\, a^2 b^2\, t}.$$
