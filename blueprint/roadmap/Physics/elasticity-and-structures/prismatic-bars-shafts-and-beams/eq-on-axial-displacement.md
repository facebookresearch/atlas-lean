# Uniqueness of the uniform-load fixed-fixed bar displacement

Let $L > 0$, $EA > 0$ and $q$ real. If $u$ solves the
[fixed-fixed problem](is-fixed-fixed-solution.md) on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $EA$ and constant load
$q$, then

$$u(x) = \frac{q\, x\, (L - x)}{2\, EA} \qquad \text{for every } x \in [0, L],$$

the [axial displacement](axial-displacement.md). Outside $[0, L]$, $u$ is not determined.
