# Cantilever deflection under a tip force alone

For all real $EI$, $L$, $P$ and $x$, the [cantilever deflection](cantilever-deflection.md) with zero
distributed load is the tip-force term:

$$w(x) = \frac{P\, x^2\, (3L - x)}{6\, EI} \qquad (q = 0).$$

With the convention $a/0 = 0$ both sides are $0$ when $EI = 0$.
