# Wall lengths of a thin closed rectangular cell

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, the wall lengths are
$(\ell_1, \ell_2, \ell_3, \ell_4) = (a, b, a, b)$, in the order of the
[wall midpoints](cell-wall-midpoint.md). For $a, b > 0$ they are the lengths along the median line
of the bottom, right, top and left walls, and the median perimeter is
$\ell_1 + \ell_2 + \ell_3 + \ell_4 = 2(a + b)$.
