# Torsion constant of a thin closed rectangular cell

For real numbers $a$, $b$ and $t$, the torsion constant of the thin closed rectangular cell is

$$J = \frac{2\, a^2 b^2\, t}{a + b}.$$

For $a, b, t > 0$ it is $J = 4A^2 t / S$ with the enclosed area $A = ab$ and the median perimeter
$S = 2(a + b)$, which is [Bredt's torsion constant](closed-cell-torsion-constant-eq.md). If moreover
$G > 0$, under the compatibility law the torque of the wall resultants is
[GJ times the twist rate](torque-eq-rigidity-mul-twist-rate.md), so $G J$ is the effective torsional
rigidity of the cell. The formula is defined for all real inputs; it is $0$ when $t = 0$, and with
the convention $x/0 = 0$ it is $0$ when $a + b = 0$.
