# The four wall resultants of the closed cell sum to zero

For all real $a$, $b$ and $q$, the [wall resultants](cell-wall-force.md) of the uniform shear flow
have zero
[resultant](../../classical-mechanics/particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/resultant-force.md):

$$F_1 + F_2 + F_3 + F_4 = 0.$$

The closed cell is self-equilibrated in force, and since the resultant vanishes the
[moment of the wall resultants](moment-about-cell-wall.md) is the same about every point: they form
a couple.
