# Bar with two free ends loaded by end forces

Let $k$, $L$, $P_0$ and $P_1$ be real numbers and $f, u \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. The
function $u$ solves the free-free problem on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $k$, distributed load $f$ and
end forces $P_0$ and $P_1$ if $u$ is twice continuously differentiable on $[0, L]$ and

$$-k\, u''(x) = f(x) \quad (x \in [0, L]), \qquad -k\, u'(0) = P_0, \qquad k\, u'(L) = P_1,$$

with the derivatives taken within $[0, L]$ (one-sided at the ends). Both end forces are measured
along $+x$: $P_0 = -k\, u'(0)$ is the force applied at $x = 0$, where the outward normal is $-x$, so
a tensile pull there gives $P_0 < 0$, and $P_1 = k\, u'(L)$ is the force applied at $x = L$, where
the outward normal is $+x$; no displacement is prescribed. The model has $L > 0$ and $k > 0$; the
definition allows any real values.
