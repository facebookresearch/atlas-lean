# Support reactions of the simply supported beam are half the total load

Let $EI > 0$, and let $w$ solve the [simply supported problem](is-simply-supported-solution.md) with
bending stiffness $EI$, span $L$ and uniform load $q$. Then

$$-EI\, w'''(0) = \frac{qL}{2}, \qquad EI\, w'''(L) = \frac{qL}{2}.$$

With the shear force $-EI\, w'''$, the reaction at each support, positive when it acts against the
load, is $qL/2$, half the total load $qL$: the shear force is $qL/2$ at the left support and $-qL/2$
at the right one.
