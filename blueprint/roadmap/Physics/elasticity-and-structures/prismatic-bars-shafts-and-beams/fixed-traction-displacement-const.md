# Fixed-traction displacement under a uniform load

For all real $k$, $L$, $P$, $q$ and $x$, the
[fixed-traction displacement](fixed-traction-displacement.md) with the constant load $f = q$ is

$$u(x) = \frac{P\, x}{k} + \frac{q\, x\, (2L - x)}{2k},$$

the end-force term $P x / k$ plus the [fixed-free displacement](fixed-free-displacement.md) with
rigidity $k$. The identity holds for every real $x$; for $k = 0$ both sides are $0$, by the
convention $a/0 = 0$.
