# Aerodynamic moment on a typical section

For real numbers `q` (dynamic pressure), `S` (reference area), `e` (offset of the aerodynamic centre
ahead of the elastic axis), `C_Lα` (lift-curve slope per radian), `α₀` (rigid angle of attack) and
`θ` (twist), the aerodynamic moment about the elastic axis is `q S e C_Lα (α₀ + θ)`. Angles, twist
and the moment are counted nose-up positive; the model is the quasi-steady linear moment law.
