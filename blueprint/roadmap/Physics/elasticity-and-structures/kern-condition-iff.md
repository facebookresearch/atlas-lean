# Kern rule for eccentrically loaded footings

Footing kern rule in load coordinates. For a rigid footing of
width `B > 0` carrying vertical load `N > 0` and moment `M` (per
unit out-of-plane length), the two edge pressures of the linear
full-contact solution are `N / B ± 6 * M / B ^ 2`. Both are
nonnegative exactly when the eccentricity ratio obeys the kern
bound `|M / N| ≤ B / 6`.
