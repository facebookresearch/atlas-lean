# Vector Coulomb friction law at a unilateral interface

For a real inner product space `E`, real numbers `μ` and `N`, a slip velocity `v ∈ E` and a tangent
traction `t ∈ E`, the Coulomb law holds when `t = -μ N v / ‖v‖` if `v ≠ 0` (slip: `-μ N` times the
unit vector `v / ‖v‖`) and `‖t‖ ≤ μ N` if `v = 0` (stick). The stick branch involves no division;
the law is physically meaningful for `μ ≥ 0` and `N ≥ 0`, where a traction obeying the law
[delivers the power](inner-eq-and-nonpos.md) `t · v = -μ N ‖v‖ ≤ 0`.
