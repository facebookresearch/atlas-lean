# The slider Reynolds equation has only the closed-form solution

For real numbers `μ` and `U`, positive end thicknesses `h₀ ≠ h_L` and `L > 0`, every
[solution](is-reynolds-solution.md) `(p, p')` of the Reynolds model satisfies, at every `x ∈ [0, L]`,
`p(x) = 6 μ U L (h₀ - h) (h - h_L) / ((h₀² - h_L²) h²)` with `h = h(x)`: the
[closed-form pressure](slider-pressure.md).
