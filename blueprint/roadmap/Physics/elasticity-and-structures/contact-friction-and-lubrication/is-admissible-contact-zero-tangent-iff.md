# Without tangent traction admissibility is unilateral complementarity

For a real inner product space `E`, a real `μ ≥ 0` and real numbers `N` and `g`, the contact state
with zero tangent traction is [admissible](is-admissible-contact.md) if and only if `N ≥ 0`, `g ≥ 0`
and `N g = 0`. In particular the frictionless scalar contact solution, with `N` the
[contact force](contact-force.md) and `g` the [gap](contact-gap.md), which satisfies
[complementarity](complementarity-of-vi.md), is admissible for every `μ ≥ 0`.
