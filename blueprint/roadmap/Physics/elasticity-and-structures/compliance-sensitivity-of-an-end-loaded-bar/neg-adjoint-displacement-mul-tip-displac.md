# Minus adjoint times tip displacement is −F²/k², for every k

For all real $F$ and $k$, with $p = F / k$ the [scalar adjoint](adjoint-displacement.md) and
$u_{\mathrm{tip}} = F / k$ the [tip displacement](tip-displacement.md),

$$-\,p\, u_{\mathrm{tip}} = -\frac{F^2}{k^2}.$$

At $k = 0$ both sides are $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.

In the physical reading $k > 0$; the identity holds for all real $F$ and $k$.
