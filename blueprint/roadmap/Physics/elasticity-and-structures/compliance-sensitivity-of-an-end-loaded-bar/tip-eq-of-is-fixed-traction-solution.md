# The tip displacement of the end-loaded bar is F / k with k = EA / L

For a length $L > 0$ and a rigidity $EA \ne 0$, every solution $u$ of the fixed-traction problem on
$[0, L]$ with zero distributed load and end force $F$ ($u$ twice continuously differentiable on
$[0, L]$, $-(EA\, u'') = 0$ on $[0, L]$, $u(0) = 0$ and $EA\, u'(L) = F$, the derivatives being
taken within $[0, L]$) satisfies

$$u(L) = \frac{F}{k}, \qquad F\, u(L) = F \cdot \frac{F}{k}, \qquad k = \frac{EA}{L}:$$

the tip displacement is that of a spring of [stiffness](axial-stiffness.md) $k$, and its load work
is the [compliance](compliance.md) $J(F, k)$. In the physical reading $EA > 0$.
