# Tip displacement and load work of the zero-load bar

Let $L > 0$, $EA \ne 0$ and $F \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $u$ solve the fixed-traction problem on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $EA$, end force $F$ and zero distributed load. With $k = EA / L$,

$$u(L) = \frac{F}{k} \qquad \text{and} \qquad F\, u(L) = F \cdot \frac{F}{k}.$$
