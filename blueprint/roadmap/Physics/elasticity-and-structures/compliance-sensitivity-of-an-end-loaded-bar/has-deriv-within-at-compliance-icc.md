# Compliance derivative −F²/k² within a positive feasible interval

For $k_{\min} > 0$, a stiffness $k \in [k_{\min}, k_{\max}]$ and every end force $F$, the
[compliance](compliance.md) $J(F, \cdot)$ has the derivative $-F^2 / k^2$ at $k$ within
$[k_{\min}, k_{\max}]$, endpoints included. It is the restriction of the two-sided derivative at
$k > 0$, so at an endpoint of a nondegenerate interval it is the one-sided derivative from inside
the interval. When $k_{\min} = k_{\max}$ the interval is the single point $k$, within which every
number is a derivative, so the statement carries no information there.
