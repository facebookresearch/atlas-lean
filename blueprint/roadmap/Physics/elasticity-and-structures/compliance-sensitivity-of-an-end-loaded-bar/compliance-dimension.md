# A force times a length has the energy dimension

Dimensions are those of the International System of Quantities: products of powers of the seven base
dimensions, such as $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ for a force and
$\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ for an energy, with $\mathsf{L}$ the length dimension. A
force times a length has the energy dimension:

$$
\dim(\text{force}) \cdot \mathsf{L} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}
= \dim(\text{energy}).
$$

Read for the [compliance](compliance.md) $J = F\, u_{\mathrm{tip}}$: the work of a force over a
length is an energy. The statement is an identity between dimensions only.
