# The compliance equals F² / k, including k = 0 where both vanish

For all real $F$ and $k$, the [compliance](compliance.md) is

$$J(F, k) = F \cdot \frac{F}{k} = \frac{F^2}{k}.$$

At $k = 0$ both sides are $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.

In the physical reading $k > 0$; the identity holds for all real $F$ and $k$.
