# For a nonzero force the compliance has no stationary point at k > 0

For an end force $F \ne 0$ and a stiffness $k > 0$, the derivative of the
[compliance](compliance.md) $J(F, \cdot)$ at $k$ is nonzero; it is the classical derivative,
[equal to](has-deriv-at-compliance.md) $-F^2 / k^2 < 0$. So no $k > 0$ is a stationary point. For
$F = 0$ the compliance is identically $0$, and every point is stationary.
