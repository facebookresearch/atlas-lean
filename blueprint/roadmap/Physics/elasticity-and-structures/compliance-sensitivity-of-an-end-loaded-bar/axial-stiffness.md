# Effective axial stiffness EA / L of a bar

For an axial rigidity $EA$, a force, and a length $L$, the effective axial stiffness of the bar is

$$k = \frac{EA}{L},$$

a force per length. In the physical reading $EA > 0$ and $L > 0$; for all real inputs the definition
is the quotient, which is $0$ at $L = 0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
