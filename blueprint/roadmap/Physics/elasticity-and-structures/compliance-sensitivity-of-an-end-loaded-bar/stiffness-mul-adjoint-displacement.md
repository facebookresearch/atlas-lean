# The scalar adjoint solves k p = F for a nonzero stiffness

For a stiffness $k \ne 0$ and every end force $F$, the [scalar adjoint](adjoint-displacement.md)
$p = F / k$ satisfies the adjoint equation $k\, p = F$. At $k = 0$ the left side is $0$, so the
equation fails for $F \ne 0$.

In the physical reading $k > 0$; the result uses only $k \ne 0$.
