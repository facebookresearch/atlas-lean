# Linear displacement solves the zero-load fixed-traction problem

Let $L > 0$ and $EA \ne 0$, and $F \in \mathbb{R}$. The function $u(x) = F x / EA$ solves the fixed-traction problem on $[0, L]$ with rigidity $EA$, end force $F$ and zero distributed load: $u$ is twice continuously differentiable on $[0, L]$, $-EA\, u''(x) = 0$ on $[0, L]$ (derivatives within $[0, L]$), $u(0) = 0$, and $EA\, u'(L) = F$.
