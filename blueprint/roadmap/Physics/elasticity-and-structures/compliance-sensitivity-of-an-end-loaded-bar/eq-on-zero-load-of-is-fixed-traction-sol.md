# Uniqueness of the zero-load fixed-traction solution

Let $EA \ne 0$ and $L, F \in \mathbb{R}$. If $u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is twice continuously differentiable on $[0, L]$ with $-EA\, u'' = 0$ on $[0, L]$ (derivatives within $[0, L]$), $u(0) = 0$ and $EA\, u'(L) = F$, then $u(x) = F x / EA$ for every $x \in [0, L]$.
