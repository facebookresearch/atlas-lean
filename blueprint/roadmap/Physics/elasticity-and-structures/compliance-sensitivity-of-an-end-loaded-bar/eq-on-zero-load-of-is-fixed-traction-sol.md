# Every solution of the end-loaded bar problem is F x / EA on the bar

For a rigidity $EA \ne 0$, every solution $u$ of the fixed-traction problem on $[0, L]$ with zero
distributed load and end force $F$ ($u$ twice continuously differentiable on $[0, L]$,
$-(EA\, u'') = 0$ on $[0, L]$, $u(0) = 0$ and $EA\, u'(L) = F$, the derivatives being taken within
$[0, L]$) satisfies $u(x) = F x / EA$ for every $x \in [0, L]$; nothing is asserted outside
$[0, L]$. No condition is put on $L$: for $L < 0$ the interval is empty and the statement is
vacuous, and for $L = 0$ it reduces to $u(0) = 0$. Together with
[existence](is-fixed-traction-solution-zero-load.md) for $L > 0$, the solution is unique on
$[0, L]$.

In the physical reading $EA > 0$ and $L > 0$; the result uses only $EA \ne 0$.
