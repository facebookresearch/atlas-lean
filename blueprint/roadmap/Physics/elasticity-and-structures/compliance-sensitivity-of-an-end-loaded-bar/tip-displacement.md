# Tip displacement F / k of a spring under an end force

For an end force $F$ and a stiffness $k$, the tip displacement is

$$u_{\mathrm{tip}} = \frac{F}{k},$$

a length when $F$ is a force and $k$ a force per length. In the physical reading $k > 0$; at $k = 0$
the quotient is $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$, not an infinite displacement.
