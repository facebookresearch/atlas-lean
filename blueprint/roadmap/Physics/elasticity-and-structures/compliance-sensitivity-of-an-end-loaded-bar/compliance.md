# Compliance: the work of the end force on the tip displacement

For an end force $F$ and a stiffness $k$, the compliance is the work of the end force on the
[tip displacement](tip-displacement.md) $u_{\mathrm{tip}} = F / k$, an energy:

$$J(F, k) = F\, u_{\mathrm{tip}} = F \cdot \frac{F}{k}.$$

It equals $F^2 / k$ for every $k$; at $k = 0$ it is $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$. In the
physical reading $k > 0$, and then $J(F, k) \ge 0$.
