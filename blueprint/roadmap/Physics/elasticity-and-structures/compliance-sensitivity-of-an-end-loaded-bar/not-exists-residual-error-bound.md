# For F ≠ 0, neither error has a residual bound uniform in k > 0

For an end force $F \ne 0$, no constant $C$ makes $|F / k - u| \le C\, |k u - F|$ hold for all
$k > 0$ and all real $u$, and no constant makes $|J(F, k) - F u| \le C\, |k u - F|$ hold for all
such $k$ and $u$, with $J(F, k) = F^2 / k$ the [compliance](compliance.md). The trial
$u = F / k + 1$ has residual $k$, state error $1$ and compliance error $|F|$. The state-error half
[holds for every force](not-exists-residual-state-error-bound.md), and the compliance-error half
[needs a nonzero force](not-exists-residual-compliance-error-bou.md).
