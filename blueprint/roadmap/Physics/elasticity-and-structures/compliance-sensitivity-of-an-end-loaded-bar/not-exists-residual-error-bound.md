# Bare residual bounds neither state nor compliance error

For every real $F \ne 0$:

- there is no $C \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|F/k - u| \le C\, |k u - F|$ for all $k > 0$ and $u \in \mathbb{R}$, and
- there is no $C \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|F^2/k - F u| \le C\, |k u - F|$ for all $k > 0$ and $u \in \mathbb{R}$.
