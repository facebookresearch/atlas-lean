# Normal-mode angular frequencies of the periodic monatomic chain

For real numbers $m > 0$ and $K \ge 0$, a natural number $N \ge 1$ and a natural number $j$, the
angular frequency of normal mode $j$ of the periodic chain of $N$ masses $m$ joined by springs of
constant $K$ is

$$\omega_j = 2 \sqrt{K / m}\, \bigl|\sin(\pi j / N)\bigr|.$$

It is the textbook dispersion relation of the chain with periodic (Born–von Kármán) boundary
conditions, taken as the definition and not derived from the equations of motion; in the notation of
Ashcroft and Mermin, $k a / 2 = \pi j / N$. The conditions $m > 0$, $K \ge 0$ and $N \ge 1$ are part
of the definition, which is made only for such data; they do not change the value. The ordinary
[frequency](../../waves-and-acoustics/frequency-wavenumber-and-wavelength/frequency.md) of mode $j$
is $\nu_j = \omega_j / (2\pi)$.
