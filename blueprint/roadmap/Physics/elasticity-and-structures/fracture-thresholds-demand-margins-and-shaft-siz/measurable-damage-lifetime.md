# Measurability of the damage lifetime

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal F)$ be a measurable space, $d : \Omega \times [0,\infty) \to \mathbb R$ a damage process and $D : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ a threshold. Suppose $\omega \mapsto d(\omega, t)$ is measurable for every $t$, $D$ is measurable, and each path $t \mapsto d(\omega,t)$ is continuous and nondecreasing. Then the damage lifetime $\omega \mapsto \inf\{t \ge 0 : d(\omega,t) \ge D(\omega)\}$ (with value $\infty$ when the set is empty) is measurable as a map to $[0,\infty]$.
