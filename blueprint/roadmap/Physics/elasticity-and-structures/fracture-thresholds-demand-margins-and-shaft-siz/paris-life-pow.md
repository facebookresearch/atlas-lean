# Paris life, power branch

For real $r, K, a_0, a_c$, the power-branch Paris life is

$$N_f = \frac{a_c^{\,r} - a_0^{\,r}}{r K},$$

with real powers and the convention $x / 0 = 0$.

For $K > 0$, $0 < a_0 < a_c$ and $r \neq 0$ it is the number of cycles for the power-branch crack to grow from $a_0$ to $a_c$. Outside that range the formula need not be a growth time: with $K = 0$, $a_0 = 1$ and $a_c = 2$ it gives $0$, but a crack with $K = 0$ never grows. Here $x^y$ is the real power: $e^{y \log x}$ for $x > 0$, $\lvert x\rvert^y \cos(\pi y)$ for $x < 0$, and for $x = 0$ it is $1$ when $y = 0$ and $0$ otherwise; in particular $x^{1/3}$ is not the usual real cube root for negative $x$.
