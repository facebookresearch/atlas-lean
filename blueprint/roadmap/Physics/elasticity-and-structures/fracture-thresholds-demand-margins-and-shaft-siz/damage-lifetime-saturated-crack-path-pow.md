# Lifetime of the saturated Paris crack path, power branch

Let $K > 0$, $r$ nonzero, $f > 0$, and $0 < a_0 < a_c$ with $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ the critical crack length. Let $a(N) = (a_0^r + rKN)^{1/r}$ be the power-branch Paris crack and $N_f = (a_c^r - a_0^r)/(rK)$ the Paris life. The damage lifetime of the event path $t \mapsto a(\min(f t, N_f))$, $t \ge 0$, against the threshold $a_c$ (the first time $t \ge 0$ at which the path is at least $a_c$, or $\infty$ if there is none) equals

$$\frac{N_f}{f}.$$
