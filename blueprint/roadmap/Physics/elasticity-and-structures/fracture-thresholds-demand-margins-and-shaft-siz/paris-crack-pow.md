# Paris-law crack length, power branch

For real $r, K, a_0, N$, the power-branch Paris crack length after $N$ cycles is

$$a(N) = \left(a_0^{\,r} + r K N\right)^{r^{-1}},$$

with real powers $x^y$ defined for all real $x, y$ (and $0^{-1} = 0$). For a growth exponent $m \ne 2$ and $r = 1 - m/2$, it is the solution with $a(0) = a_0$ of the Paris law $da/dN = K a^{m/2}$ while the base $a_0^{\,r} + r K N$ is positive; for $r < 0$ (that is, $m > 2$) this holds on a finite cycle interval.
