# Paris-law crack length, power branch

For real $r, K, a_0, N$, the power-branch Paris crack length after $N$ cycles is

$$a(N) = \left(a_0^{\,r} + r K N\right)^{r^{-1}},$$

with $0^{-1} = 0$. Here $x^y$ is the real power: $e^{y \log x}$ for $x > 0$, $\lvert x\rvert^y \cos(\pi y)$ for $x < 0$, and for $x = 0$ it is $1$ when $y = 0$ and $0$ otherwise; in particular $x^{1/3}$ is not the usual real cube root for negative $x$. For a growth exponent $m \ne 2$, $r = 1 - m/2$ and $a_0 > 0$, it is the solution with $a(0) = a_0$ of the Paris law $da/dN = K a^{m/2}$ while the base $a_0^{\,r} + r K N$ is positive. For $K > 0$ and $r < 0$ (that is, $m > 2$) the base reaches $0$ at a finite cycle count, so this holds only on a finite forward interval; for $K = 0$ the base stays $a_0^{\,r} > 0$ forever.
