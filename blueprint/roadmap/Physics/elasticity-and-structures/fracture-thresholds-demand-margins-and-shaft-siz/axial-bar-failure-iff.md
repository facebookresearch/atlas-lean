# Failure of a uniformly loaded fixed-fixed bar

Let $\Phi$ be a yield functional calibrated by the tensile test, and let $EA > 0$, $A_{cs} > 0$, $L > 0$, $q, A \in \mathbb{R}$. With $\sigma(x)$ the axial stress of the fixed-fixed bar under the uniform load $q$, there is a section $x \in [0, L]$ with $A \le \Phi(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma(x), 0, 0))$ if and only if

$$2\, A_{cs}\, A \le |q|\, L .$$
