# Zero diameter gives zero shaft demand

Let $\Phi$ be calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c$, and let $A > 0$. For all real $M$ and $T$, with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ both stress formulas vanish at $d = 0$, so the value of $\Phi$ on the bending-torsion state of the shaft at $d = 0$ is $0$ and it is not a demand failure against $A$. The hypothesis $d > 0$ of the sizing results cannot be dropped.
