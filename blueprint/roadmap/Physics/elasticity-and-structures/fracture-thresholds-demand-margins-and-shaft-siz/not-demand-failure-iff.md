# Shaft does not fail exactly above the minimum diameter

Let $\Phi$ be calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c$, and let $d > 0$ and $A > 0$. For all real $M$ and $T$, the shaft of diameter $d$ is not a demand failure (its demand is strictly below $A$) if and only if

$$\left(\frac{16 \sqrt{4 M^2 + c\, T^2}}{\pi A}\right)^{1/3} < d .$$
