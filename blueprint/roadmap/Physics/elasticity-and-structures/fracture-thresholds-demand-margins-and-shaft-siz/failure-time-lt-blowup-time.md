# Failure time precedes blow-up time, power branch

Let $K > 0$, $a_0 > 0$, $r < 0$ (growth exponent $m > 2$), $f > 0$, and let $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ be the critical crack length with $a_0 < a_c$. With $N_f = (a_c^{\,r} - a_0^{\,r})/(rK)$,

$$\frac{N_f}{f} < \frac{a_0^{\,r} / \big(-(rK)\big)}{f},$$

the right side being the time at which the power-branch base $a_0^{\,r} + rK f t$ would reach zero.
