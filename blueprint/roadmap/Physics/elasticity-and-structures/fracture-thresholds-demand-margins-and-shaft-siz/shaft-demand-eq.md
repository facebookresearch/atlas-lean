# Shaft demand under bending and torsion

Let $\Phi$ be calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c$, and let $d > 0$. For all real $M$ and $T$, applying $\Phi$ to the bending-torsion state with the outer-fiber bending stress $M(d/2)/I$ and surface shear stress $T(d/2)/J$ of a solid circular shaft gives the demand

$$\frac{16 \sqrt{4 M^2 + c\, T^2}}{\pi d^3}.$$
