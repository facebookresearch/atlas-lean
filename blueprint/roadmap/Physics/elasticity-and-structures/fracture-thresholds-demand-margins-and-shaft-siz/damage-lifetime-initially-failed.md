# Initially failed crack has zero lifetime

Let $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ be the critical crack length for real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$, with real division by zero equal to $0$. If $a_c \le a_0$, then in every scenario the damage lifetime of the constant damage path $t \mapsto a_0$ against the threshold $a_c$ is $0$. No crack-growth law is involved.

The [damage lifetime](damage-lifetime.md) of a path against a threshold is the first time $t \ge 0$ at which the damage is at least the threshold, and $\infty$ if there is none. Here the damage already reaches the threshold at time $0$, so the lifetime is $0$ even when $a_0 > a_c$ and the damage never equals $a_c$.
