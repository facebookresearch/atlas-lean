# Initially failed crack has zero lifetime

Let $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ be the critical crack length for real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$. If $a_c \le a_0$, then in every scenario the damage lifetime of the constant damage path $t \mapsto a_0$ against the threshold $a_c$ is $0$. No crack-growth law is involved.
