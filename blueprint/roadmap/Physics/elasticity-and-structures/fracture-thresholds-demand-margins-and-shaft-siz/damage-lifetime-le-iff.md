# Failure by a fixed time under a monotone damage path

If $t \mapsto d(\omega, t)$ is continuous and nondecreasing on $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ the damage lifetime satisfies

$$\tau(\omega) \le t \iff D(\omega) \le d(\omega, t),$$

so reaching the threshold with equality counts as failure.
