# Paris life precedes blow-up, power branch

If $K > 0$, $0 < a_0 < a_c$ and $r < 0$ (growth exponent $m > 2$), then the power-branch Paris life is strictly smaller than the cycle count at which the base $a_0^{\,r} + rKN$ reaches zero:

$$\frac{a_c^{\,r} - a_0^{\,r}}{r K} < \frac{a_0^{\,r}}{-(r K)} .$$
