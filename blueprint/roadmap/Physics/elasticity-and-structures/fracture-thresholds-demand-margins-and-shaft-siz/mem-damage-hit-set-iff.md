# Hit condition is the demand failure event

For $d : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \to \mathbb{R}$, $D : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $\omega \in \Omega$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$,

$$d(\omega, t) - D(\omega) \in [0, \infty) \iff D(\omega) \le d(\omega, t),$$

the demand failure event with allowable $D(\omega)$ and demand $d(\omega, t)$.
