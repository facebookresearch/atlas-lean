# Saturated logarithmic-branch path solves the Paris law before failure

Write $a(N) = a_0 e^{KN}$ for the logarithmic-branch Paris crack, $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ for the critical crack length (real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$) and $N_f = \log(a_c/a_0)/K$ for the Paris life.
For all real $K$ and $a_0$, if $f > 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ with $t \le N_f / f$, then

$$a\big(\min(f t, N_f)\big) = a(f t),$$

and $s \mapsto a(f s)$ is differentiable at $t$ with derivative $f\, K\, a(f t)$.
