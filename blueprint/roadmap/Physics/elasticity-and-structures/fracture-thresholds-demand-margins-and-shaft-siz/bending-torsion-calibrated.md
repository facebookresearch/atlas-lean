# Yield functional calibrated on bending-torsion states

A functional $\Phi$ from real $3 \times 3$ stress matrices (tension positive) to $\mathbb{R}$ is calibrated on bending-torsion states with shear factor $c$ when

- $c > 0$, and
- for all real $\sigma, \tau$, $\Phi$ applied to the bending-torsion state with axial stress $\sigma$ and shear $\tau$ equals $\sqrt{\sigma^2 + c\, \tau^2}$.

The value $c = 3$ corresponds to the von Mises (distortion-energy) criterion and $c = 4$ to the Tresca (maximum-shear) criterion. Positivity of $c$ is required because the square root of a negative number is taken to be $0$, which for $c \le 0$ would give a pure torque zero demand.
