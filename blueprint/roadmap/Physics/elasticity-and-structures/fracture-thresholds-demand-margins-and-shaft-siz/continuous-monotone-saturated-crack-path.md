# Saturated power-branch crack path is continuous and nondecreasing

Let $K > 0$, $r \ne 0$, $a_0 > 0$, and let $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ be the critical crack length for real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$, with $a_0 < a_c$. Write $a(N) = (a_0^{\,r} + rKN)^{r^{-1}}$ for the power-branch Paris crack and $N_f = (a_c^{\,r} - a_0^{\,r})/(rK)$ for its Paris life.
If $f \ge 0$, then the saturated event path $t \mapsto a(\min(f t, N_f))$ on $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ is continuous and nondecreasing.
