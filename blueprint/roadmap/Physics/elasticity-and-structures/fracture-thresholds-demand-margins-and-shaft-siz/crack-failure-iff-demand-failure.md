# Crack failure as a demand failure

For all real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma, a$, the crack failure event $K_{IC} \le Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$ is the demand failure event with allowable $K_{IC}$ and demand the mode-I stress intensity $Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a}$.

Here $\sqrt{x}$ is the real square root, taken to be $0$ for $x < 0$, so for a negative crack length the demand is $0$.
