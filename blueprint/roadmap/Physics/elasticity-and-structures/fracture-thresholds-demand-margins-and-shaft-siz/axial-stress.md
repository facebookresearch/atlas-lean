# Axial stress of a uniformly loaded fixed-fixed bar

For real $EA$ (axial rigidity), $L$ (length), $q$ (uniform load), $A_{cs}$ (cross-section area) and $x$ (section), let

$$u(y) = \frac{q\, y\, (L - y)}{2\, EA}$$

be the closed-form axial displacement of the fixed-fixed bar under the uniform load $q$ (with $x / 0 = 0$). The axial stress at $x$ is

$$\sigma(x) = \frac{EA \; u'(x)}{A_{cs}} .$$

For $L > 0$ and $EA > 0$, $u$ solves the fixed-fixed bar problem.
