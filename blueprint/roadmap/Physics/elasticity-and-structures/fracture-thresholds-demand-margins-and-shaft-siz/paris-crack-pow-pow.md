# Power-branch crack raised to r recovers the base

If $r \ne 0$ and $a_0^{\,r} + r K N \ge 0$, then the power-branch crack length $a(N) = (a_0^{\,r} + r K N)^{r^{-1}}$ satisfies

$$a(N)^{r} = a_0^{\,r} + r K N .$$

Here $x^y$ is the real power: $e^{y \log x}$ for $x > 0$, $\lvert x\rvert^y \cos(\pi y)$ for $x < 0$, and for $x = 0$ it is $1$ when $y = 0$ and $0$ otherwise. The base is assumed nonnegative, but $a_0$ itself may be any real number.
