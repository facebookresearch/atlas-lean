# Paris life, logarithmic branch

For real $K, a_0, a_c$, the logarithmic-branch Paris life is

$$N_f = \frac{\log (a_c / a_0)}{K},$$

with the conventions $x / 0 = 0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log |x|$ for $x < 0$.

For $K > 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$ it is the number of cycles for the logarithmic-branch crack $a_0 e^{KN}$ to grow from $a_0$ to $a_c$. With $K = 0$, $a_0 = 1$ and $a_c = 2$ it gives $0$, but the crack stays at $1$ forever.
