# Unloaded logarithmic-branch crack never fails

Write $a(N) = a_0 e^{KN}$ for the logarithmic-branch Paris crack, $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ for the critical crack length (real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$) and $N_f = \log(a_c/a_0)/K$ for the Paris life.
If $K > 0$ and $0 < a_0 < a_c$, then at load frequency $f = 0$ the saturated event path satisfies $a(\min(0, N_f)) = a_0$ at every time $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, and its damage lifetime against $a_c$ is $\infty$ in every scenario.
