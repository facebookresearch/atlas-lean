# Ratio margin at zero demand gives a false failure signal

Let $A > 0$. With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, the ratio formula at demand $0$ gives $A / 0 - 1 < 0$ (it equals $-1$), which reads as failure, while the demand $0$ is not a failure: $\neg (A \le 0)$. The margin of safety therefore requires a positive demand.
