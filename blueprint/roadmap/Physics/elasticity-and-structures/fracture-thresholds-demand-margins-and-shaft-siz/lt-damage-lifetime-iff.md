# Component is up exactly while damage is below threshold

If $t \mapsto d(\omega, t)$ is continuous and nondecreasing on $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ the damage lifetime $\tau(\omega) \in [0, \infty]$ satisfies

$$t < \tau(\omega) \iff d(\omega, t) < D(\omega).$$
