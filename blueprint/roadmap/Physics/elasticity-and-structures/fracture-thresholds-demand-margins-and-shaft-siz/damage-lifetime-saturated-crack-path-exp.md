# Lifetime of the saturated logarithmic-branch crack

Write $a(N) = a_0 e^{KN}$ for the logarithmic-branch Paris crack, $a_c = (K_{IC}/(Y\sigma))^2/\pi$ for the critical crack length (real $K_{IC}, Y, \sigma$) and $N_f = \log(a_c/a_0)/K$ for the Paris life.
If $K > 0$, $0 < a_0 < a_c$ and $f > 0$, then in every scenario the damage lifetime of the saturated event path $t \mapsto a(\min(f t, N_f))$ against the threshold $a_c$ is

$$\tau = \frac{N_f}{f}.$$
