# Polynomial uniqueness for a uniformly loaded simple beam

Let a slender Euler–Bernoulli beam of length $L > 0$ and constant bending stiffness $EI > 0$ carry a uniform load $w \ge 0$, with
downward-positive deflection, pin and roller supports and no shear deformation. Any polynomial $y$ with $EI\,y'''' = w$ and
$y(0) = y(L) = 0$, $y''(0) = y''(L) = 0$ equals

$$y(x) = \frac{w x}{24 EI}\bigl(L^3 - 2 L x^2 + x^3\bigr).$$

The difference of two such polynomials has zero fourth derivative, so it is a cubic, and the four support conditions force it to vanish.
The result concerns polynomial solutions only; uniqueness among all $C^4$ or weak solutions is not claimed here.
