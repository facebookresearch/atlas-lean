# Fixed-traction displacement under a uniform load

For $k, L, P, q, x \in \mathbb{R}$, let

$$u(x) = \int_0^x \frac{P + \int_y^L q\,dz}{k}\,dy$$

be the fixed-traction displacement for rigidity $k$, end force $P$ and uniform load $q$. Then

$$u(x) = \frac{P\,x}{k} + \frac{q\,x\,(2L - x)}{2k},$$

the end-force term plus the fixed-free displacement. For $k = 0$ both sides are $0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
