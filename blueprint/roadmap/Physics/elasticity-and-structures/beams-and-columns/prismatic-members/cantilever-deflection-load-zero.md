# Cantilever deflection with no distributed load

For all $EI, L, P, x \in \mathbb{R}$, the cantilever deflection

$$w(x) = \frac{q\,x^2\,(6L^2 - 4Lx + x^2)}{24\,EI} + \frac{P\,x^2\,(3L - x)}{6\,EI}$$

with $q = 0$ is the tip-force term

$$w(x) = \frac{P\,x^2\,(3L - x)}{6\,EI}.$$

For $EI = 0$ both sides take the convention $x/0 = 0$.
