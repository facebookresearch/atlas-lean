# Clamped ends change the beam solution

Let `EI > 0`, `L > 0` and `q ≠ 0`. The clamped-clamped polynomial satisfies
`EI w'''' = q`, both zero-deflection conditions and both zero-slope
conditions, but it is not a simply supported solution, and its midspan
deflection `q * L ^ 4 / (384 * EI)` differs from the simply supported one.
