# Closed-cell compatibility law

For a thin closed rectangular cell with median side lengths $a$ and $b$, wall thickness $t$, shear modulus $G$, uniform shear flow $q$ and twist rate $\theta'$, the compatibility law is the condition

$$\sum_{i=1}^{4} \frac{q}{G t}\,\ell_i = 2ab\,\theta', \qquad (\ell_1, \ell_2, \ell_3, \ell_4) = (a, b, a, b)$$

where $\ell_i$ are the lengths of the bottom, right, top and left walls along the median line. For $G t = 0$ the left side takes the convention $x/0 = 0$.
