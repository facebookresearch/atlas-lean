# Axial Displacement

Axial displacement of a fixed-fixed bar under the uniform load `q`:
`q * x * (L - x) / (2 * EA)`.

With the torsional stiffness `GJ` in place of `EA` and the uniform distributed torque `t` in place of `q`, the same formula `t * x * (L - x) / (2 * GJ)` is the twist angle of a fixed-fixed circular shaft.
