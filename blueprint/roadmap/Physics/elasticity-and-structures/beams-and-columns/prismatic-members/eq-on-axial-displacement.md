# Displacement of a uniformly loaded fixed-fixed bar

Let `L > 0` and `EA > 0`. Every solution of the fixed-fixed bar problem with
axial stiffness `EA` and uniform load `q` equals `q * x * (L - x) / (2 * EA)`
on `[0, L]`.

Read with torsional stiffness `GJ` and uniform distributed torque `t`, this says that every solution of the fixed-fixed shaft problem equals the twist angle `t * x * (L - x) / (2 * GJ)` on `[0, L]`.
