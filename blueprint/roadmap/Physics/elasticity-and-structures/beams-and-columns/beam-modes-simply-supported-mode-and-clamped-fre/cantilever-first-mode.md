# First cantilever mode

Let $EI > 0$, $\rho A > 0$ and $L > 0$. Let $z_1$ be the least positive root of $\cos z \cosh z + 1 = 0$, let $\beta = z_1/L$, and let

$$W(x) = (\sin z_1 + \sinh z_1)(\cos \beta x - \cosh \beta x) - (\cos z_1 + \cosh z_1)(\sin \beta x - \sinh \beta x),$$

$\omega_1 = (z_1/L)^2\sqrt{EI/\rho A}$ and $w(x, t) = W(x)\cos(\omega_1 t)$. Then $W$ satisfies the clamped-free conditions on $[0, L]$: $L > 0$, $W(0) = 0$, $W'(0) = 0$, $W''(L) = 0$ and $W'''(L) = 0$; its tip deflection satisfies $W(L) ≠ 0$; and for all $x, t$,

$$\rho A\, w_{tt}(x, t) + EI\, w_{xxxx}(x, t) = 0.$$
