# The global frame energy equals the local energy

For all real `EA`, `EI`, `L`, `c`, `s` and every `d ∈ ℝ⁶`, the
[global frame stiffness](frame-stiffness-global.md) `K_g = Tᵀ K_f T` satisfies
`d · K_g d = (T d) · K_f (T d)`: the quadratic form at the global displacement equals the local
quadratic form at the local displacement `T d`. The physical reading is `EA > 0`, `EI > 0`, `L > 0`
and `c² + s² = 1`, with `(c, s)` the direction cosines of the element axis.
