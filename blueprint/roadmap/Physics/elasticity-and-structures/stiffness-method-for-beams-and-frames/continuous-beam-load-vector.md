# Assembled consistent load vector of a two-span beam

For uniform loads `w1`, `w2` and lengths `L1`, `L2` (real numbers), the assembled consistent load
vector of the two-span beam is `G₁ᵀ f(w1, L1) + G₂ᵀ f(w2, L2)`, with the
[consistent load vector](uniform-load-vector.md) `f` of each span and the gathers `G₁` and `G₂` of
the [first](span-one-gather.md) and [second](span-two-gather.md) span. The physical reading is
`L1, L2 > 0`.
