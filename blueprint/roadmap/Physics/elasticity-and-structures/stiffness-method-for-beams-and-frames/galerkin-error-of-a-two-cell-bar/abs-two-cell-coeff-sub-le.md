# Residual bound on the coefficient error

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). Under a uniform load $q$, the physical stiffness is $K = 4EA/L$, the consistent load is $F = qL/2$, the physical P1 reconstruction is $u_h(x) = \alpha\,(I_h\hat u)(x/L)$ with $\alpha = qL^2/(2EA)$, $\hat u(\xi) = \xi(1 - \xi)$ and $I_h$ the nodal interpolant, and its interior coefficient is $c = u_h(L/2)$. For an approximate coefficient $\tilde c$, the residual is $r = F - K\tilde c$ and the reconstruction is $\tilde u_h(x) = \tilde c\,\varphi(x/L)$. If $EA > 0$ and $L > 0$, then $|c - \tilde c| \le L|r|/(4EA)$.
