# Recovering the unit profile

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). Under the quadratic load $Q(x/L)^2$, let $\beta = QL^2/EA$ and $u(x) = \beta\hat u_2(x/L)$ with $\hat u_2(\xi) = (\xi - \xi^4)/12$. Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, so $\beta \ne 0$ forces $EA \ne 0$. If $L \ne 0$ and $\beta \ne 0$, then $\hat u_2(\xi) = \beta^{-1}u(L\xi)$.
