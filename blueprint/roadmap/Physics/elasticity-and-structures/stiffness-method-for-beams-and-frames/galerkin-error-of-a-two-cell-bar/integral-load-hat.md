# Load is the hat integral of the load

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). Let $q$ be a uniform load. If $L > 0$, then $\int_0^Lq\,\varphi(x/L)\,dx = qL/2$.
