# Residual in the midpoint system

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). Under the quadratic load $Q(x/L)^2$, an approximate coefficient $c$ leaves the residual $F_{mid} - Kc$ in the midpoint system, with the stiffness $K = 4EA/L$ ($K = 0$ when $L = 0$, by the convention $a/0 = 0$).
