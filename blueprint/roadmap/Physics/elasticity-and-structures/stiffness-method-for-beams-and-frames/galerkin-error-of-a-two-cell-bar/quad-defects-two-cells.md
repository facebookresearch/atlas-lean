# Quadrature defects of the two-cell quadratic load

For the interior node of the partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ and the load $\xi^2$, the midpoint load exceeds the exact load $7/48$ by $1/96$, the coefficients obtained by dividing by the unit stiffness $4$ differ by $1/384$, and the exact-load coefficient $7/192$ equals $\hat u_2(\tfrac12)$.
