# Hat reconstruction of a coefficient

For the two-cell bar of length $L$, the reconstruction of an interior coefficient $c$ is $x \mapsto c\,\varphi(x/L)$, with $\varphi$ the hat function of the interior node of $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$. Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, so for $L = 0$ the argument is $0$ and the reconstruction is identically $0$.
