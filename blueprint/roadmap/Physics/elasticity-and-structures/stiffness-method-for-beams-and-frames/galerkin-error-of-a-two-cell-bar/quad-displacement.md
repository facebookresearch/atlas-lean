# Displacement under a quadratic load

The displacement of the bar under the quadratic load $Q(x/L)^2$ is $u(x) = \beta\hat u_2(x/L)$ with $\beta = QL^2/EA$ and $\hat u_2(\xi) = (\xi - \xi^4)/12$. Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, so $\beta = 0$ when $EA = 0$, and for $L = 0$ the argument $x/L$ is $0$ and $u$ is identically $0$.
