# Exact-load interior coefficient

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). Under the quadratic load $Q(x/L)^2$, the exact-load interior coefficient is $F/K$ with $F = \int_0^LQ(x/L)^2\varphi(x/L)\,dx$ and $K = 4EA/L$. Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, so the coefficient is $0$ when $K = 0$, in particular when $EA = 0$ or $L = 0$.
