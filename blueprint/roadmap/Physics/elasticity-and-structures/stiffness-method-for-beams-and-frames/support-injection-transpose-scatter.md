# Rotation components of scattered element end forces

For all `e1 = (e1₁, e1₂, e1₃, e1₄)` and `e2 = (e2₁, e2₂, e2₃, e2₄)` in `ℝ⁴`, element end forces in
the order `(v, θ, v, θ)`, the [support injection](support-injection.md) `S` and the gathers `G₁`,
`G₂` of the [first](span-one-gather.md) and [second](span-two-gather.md) span satisfy
`Sᵀ (G₁ᵀ e1 + G₂ᵀ e2) = (e1₂, e1₄ + e2₂, e2₄)`: the free-rotation components are the moment sums at
the three nodes.
