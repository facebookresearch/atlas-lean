# Axial and bending energies of a frame element add

For all real `EA`, `EI` and `L` and all `d = (u1, v1, θ1, u2, v2, θ2)`, the
[frame stiffness matrix](frame-stiffness.md) `K_f` satisfies
`d · K_f d = (u1, u2) · K_a (u1, u2) + (v1, θ1, v2, θ2) · K (v1, θ1, v2, θ2)`, where `K_a` is the
[axial stiffness](axial-stiffness.md) and `K` the [beam stiffness matrix](beam-stiffness.md). The
physical reading is `EA > 0`, `EI > 0` and `L > 0`.
