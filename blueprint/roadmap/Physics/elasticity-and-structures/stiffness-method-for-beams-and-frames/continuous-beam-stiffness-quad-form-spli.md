# The two-span beam energy splits over the spans

For all real `EI1`, `L1`, `EI2`, `L2` and every `d = (d0, …, d5)`, the
[assembled two-span stiffness](continuous-beam-stiffness.md) `K_c` satisfies
`d · K_c d = d₁ · K(EI1, L1) d₁ + d₂ · K(EI2, L2) d₂` with `d₁ = (d0, d1, d2, d3)`,
`d₂ = (d2, d3, d4, d5)` and the [beam stiffness matrix](beam-stiffness.md) `K`. The physical reading
is `EI1, L1, EI2, L2 > 0`.
