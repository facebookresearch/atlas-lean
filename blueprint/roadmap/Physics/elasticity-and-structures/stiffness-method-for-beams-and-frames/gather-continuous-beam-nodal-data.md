# Gathered nodal data are the span end data under continuity

For real `L1`, `L2` and functions `y1`, `y2 : ℝ → ℝ` with `y1(L1) = y2(0)` and `y1'(L1) = y2'(0)`,
the [nodal data](continuous-beam-nodal-data.md) `D` satisfy
`G₁ D = (y1(0), y1'(0), y1(L1), y1'(L1))` and `G₂ D = (y2(0), y2'(0), y2(L2), y2'(L2))`, the
[end data](beam-end-data.md) of the two spans, with the [first-span gather](span-one-gather.md) `G₁`
and the [second-span gather](span-two-gather.md) `G₂`.
