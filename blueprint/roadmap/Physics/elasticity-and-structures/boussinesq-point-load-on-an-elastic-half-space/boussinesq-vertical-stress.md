# Boussinesq vertical stress under a point load

For a load `P`, a horizontal radius `r` and a depth `z`, all real, the Boussinesq vertical normal
stress is `σ_z(r, z) = 3 P z³ / (2 π (r² + z²)^(5/2))`, where `(r² + z²)^(5/2) = R⁵` and
`R = √(r² + z²)` is the distance from the load point. It is the compression-positive vertical
stress at depth `z` and horizontal distance `r` from the axis of a concentrated normal load `P` on
the surface of a linear, homogeneous and isotropic elastic half-space (Love 1892, art. 162,
eq. (65)), physically meaningful for `z > 0`. At `r = z = 0` the denominator vanishes and, with
the convention `x / 0 = 0`, the value is `0`.
