# An antiderivative of the Boussinesq radial force density

For all real `P` and `z`, the function `F(r) = -P z³ (r² + z²)^(-3/2)` has derivative
`F'(r) = 2 π r σ_z(r, z)` at every real `r`, where `σ_z` is the
[Boussinesq vertical stress](boussinesq-vertical-stress.md) of the load `P`: `F` is an
antiderivative of the radial force density. At `z = 0` both `F` and the density vanish
identically; at `r = z = 0` the real power is `0^(-3/2) = 0`, by the convention `0^t = 0` for
`t ≠ 0`. The physical domain is `z > 0`.
