# The Boussinesq radial force density is integrable on the half-line

For all real `P` and `z`, the radial force density `r ↦ 2 π r σ_z(r, z)` of the
[Boussinesq vertical stress](boussinesq-vertical-stress.md) is Lebesgue integrable on `(0, ∞)`.
So the [radial force resultant](integral-boussinesq-resultant.md) is the integral of an
integrable function, not the value `0` that the convention gives to the integral of a
non-integrable one. The physical domain is `z > 0`.
