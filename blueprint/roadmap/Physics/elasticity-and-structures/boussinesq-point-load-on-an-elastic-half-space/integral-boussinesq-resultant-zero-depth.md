# The radial resultant vanishes on the loaded surface

On the loaded surface `z = 0` the [Boussinesq vertical stress](boussinesq-vertical-stress.md)
vanishes at every radius `r > 0`, so the radial resultant `∫₀^∞ 2 π r σ_z(r, 0) dr` is `0` for
every real load `P`. It therefore differs from `P` when `P ≠ 0`: the
[resultant identity](integral-boussinesq-resultant.md) needs a positive depth.
