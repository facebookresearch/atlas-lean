# The Boussinesq radial resultant equals the load at positive depth

For every real load `P` and every depth `z > 0`, `∫₀^∞ 2 π r σ_z(r, z) dr = P`, where `σ_z` is
the [Boussinesq vertical stress](boussinesq-vertical-stress.md): the radial force resultant at
depth `z` equals the applied load. The integral is the one-dimensional radial integral; its
identification with the integral of the vertical stress over the horizontal plane is not part of
the statement.
