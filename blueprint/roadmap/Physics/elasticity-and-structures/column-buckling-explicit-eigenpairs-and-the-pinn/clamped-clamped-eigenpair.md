# Clamped-clamped Euler column eigenpair

Let `EI > 0` and `L > 0`. The mode `w = 1 - cos (2πx / L)` at axial load `P =
4π²EI / L²` satisfies `EI w'''' + P w'' = 0` at every `x` and the clamped
conditions `w = w' = 0` at both ends: a buckling eigenpair of a fixed-fixed
column. The factor `4` against the pinned-pinned load `π²EI / L²` is the
standard fixed-fixed Euler result (effective length `L / 2`). This is one
explicit eigenpair.
