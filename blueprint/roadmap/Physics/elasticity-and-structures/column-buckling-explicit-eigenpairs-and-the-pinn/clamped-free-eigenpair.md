# Clamped-free Euler column eigenpair

Let $EI > 0$ and $L > 0$. With axial load $P = \pi^2 EI/(4L^2)$, the function $w(x) = 1 - \cos(\pi x/(2L))$ satisfies

$$EI\, w''''(x) + P\, w''(x) = 0 \quad\text{for every } x,$$

the clamped conditions $w(0) = 0$ and $w'(0) = 0$, and the free-end conditions $w''(L) = 0$ and $EI\, w'''(L) + P\, w'(L) = 0$.
