# Clamped-free Euler column eigenpair

Let `EI > 0` and `L > 0`. The mode `w = 1 - cos (πx / (2L))` at axial load `P
= π²EI / (4L²)` satisfies `EI w'''' + P w'' = 0` at every `x`, the clamped
conditions `w(0) = w'(0) = 0`, and the free-end conditions `w''(L) = 0` and
`EI w'''(L) + P w'(L) = 0`: a buckling eigenpair of a clamped-free column.
