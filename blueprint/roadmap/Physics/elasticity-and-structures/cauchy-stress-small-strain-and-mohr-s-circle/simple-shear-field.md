# Simple-shear displacement field

For `γ ∈ ℝ`, the simple-shear displacement field is the
[affine displacement field](affine-displacement.md) `u(x) = (γ x₁, 0, 0)`, whose matrix has `γ` at
entry `(0, 1)` and zeros elsewhere and whose offset is `0`. The deformation
`x ↦ x + u(x) = (x₀ + γ x₁, x₁, x₂)` slides each plane `x₁ = c` along the `x₀` direction by `γ c`.
