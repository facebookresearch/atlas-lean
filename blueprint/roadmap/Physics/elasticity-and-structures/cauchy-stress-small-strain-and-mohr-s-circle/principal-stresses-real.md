# Each principal stress of a symmetric stress is an eigenvalue

Let `σ` be a real [symmetric](is-symmetric-stress.md) `3 × 3` matrix, and let `λ₀, λ₁, λ₂` be the
real numbers that the spectral theorem for the [Hermitian](symmetric-is-hermitian.md) matrix `σ`
attaches to it: its eigenvalues counted with multiplicity, indexed by `0, 1, 2` in an order fixed by
a choice, not necessarily decreasing. Each `λ_i` lies in the real spectrum of `σ`, that is
`σ - λ_i 1` is not invertible. These are the principal stresses of `σ`, and they are real.
