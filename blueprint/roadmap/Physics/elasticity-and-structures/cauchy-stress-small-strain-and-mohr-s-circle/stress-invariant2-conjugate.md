# The second invariant is unchanged by an orthogonal change of axes

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `σ` and an orthogonal `3 × 3` matrix `Q`, `Qᵀ Q = 1` and `Q Qᵀ = 1`, the
[second invariant](stress-invariant2.md) `I₂ = ((tr σ)² - tr(σ²)) / 2` of `Q σ Qᵀ` equals that of
`σ`. Here `Q σ Qᵀ` holds the components of the stress in the orthonormal axes given by the rows of
`Q`; no symmetry of `σ` is needed.
