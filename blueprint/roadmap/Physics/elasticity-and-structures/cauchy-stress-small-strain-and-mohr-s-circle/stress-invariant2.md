# Second stress invariant

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `σ`, the second stress invariant is `I₂ = ((tr σ)² - tr(σ²)) / 2`, the sum
of the three principal `2 × 2` minors `σ_ii σ_jj - σ_ij σ_ji` (`i < j`). It is unchanged by an
[orthogonal change of axes](stress-invariant2-conjugate.md), for every `σ`. For a symmetric `σ` with
principal stresses `λ₀, λ₁, λ₂` it is `λ₀ λ₁ + λ₁ λ₂ + λ₂ λ₀`.
