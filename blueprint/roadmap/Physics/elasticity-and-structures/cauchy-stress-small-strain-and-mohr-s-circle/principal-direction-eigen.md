# The chosen eigenvectors are principal directions

Let `σ` be a real [symmetric](is-symmetric-stress.md) `3 × 3` matrix, with the orthonormal
eigenvectors `u₀, u₁, u₂` and eigenvalues `λ₀, λ₁, λ₂` chosen by the
[spectral theorem](spectral-decomposition.md). For each `j`, `σ u_j = λ_j u_j`: on the face with
unit normal `u_j` the traction is purely normal, with normal stress `λ_j`, so `u_j` is a principal
direction. It is one principal direction, not the only one: `-u_j` is another, and when two
principal stresses coincide every unit vector of their eigenspace is one.
