# Engineering shear of simple shear equals γ

For every `γ ∈ ℝ` and every point `x ∈ ℝ³`, the [engineering shear](engineering-shear.md) `2 ε₀₁` of
the [small strain](small-strain.md) of the [displacement gradient](displacement-gradient.md) of the
[simple-shear field](simple-shear-field.md) at `x` is exactly `γ`.
