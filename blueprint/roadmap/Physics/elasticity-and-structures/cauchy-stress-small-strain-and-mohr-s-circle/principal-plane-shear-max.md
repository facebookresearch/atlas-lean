# For s₃ ≤ s₁, the principal-plane shear bound is attained at θ = π/4

For real `s₁, s₃` with `s₃ ≤ s₁`, the [principal-plane shear](principal-plane-shear.md) at `θ = π/4`
satisfies `|τ(π/4)| = (s₁ - s₃)/2`. With the [bound](principal-plane-shear-bound.md)
`|τ(θ)| ≤ (s₁ - s₃)/2`, this makes `(s₁ - s₃)/2` the maximum of `|τ(θ)|` over `θ`. At `θ = π/4` the
shear itself is `-(s₁ - s₃)/2`; for `s₁ = s₃` it is `0` at every angle.
