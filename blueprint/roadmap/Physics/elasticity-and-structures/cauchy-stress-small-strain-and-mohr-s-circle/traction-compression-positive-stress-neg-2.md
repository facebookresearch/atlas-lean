# The converted hydrostatic stress has traction map value p v

For every `p ∈ ℝ` and `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the [compression-positive form](compression-positive-stress.md) of
the hydrostatic tension-positive stress `-p 1` is `p 1`, and the tension-positive
[traction](traction.md) map gives `p v` for it. Its physical
[compression-positive traction](compression-positive-traction.md) is `-p v`
([hydrostatic pressure](compression-positive-traction-neg-smul-o.md)).
