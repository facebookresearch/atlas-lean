# Traction flips sign on the opposite face

For every real `n × n` matrix `σ` and every `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the [traction](traction.md) on the face with
normal `-v` is the negative of the traction on the face with normal `v`: `σ (-v) = -(σ v)`. This is
the action–reaction relation between the two sides of a surface element.
