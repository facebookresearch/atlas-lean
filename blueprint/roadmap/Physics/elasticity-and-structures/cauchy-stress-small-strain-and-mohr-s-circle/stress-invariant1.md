# First stress invariant

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `σ`, the first stress invariant is its trace,
`I₁ = tr σ = σ₀₀ + σ₁₁ + σ₂₂`. It is unchanged by an
[orthogonal change of axes](stress-invariant1-conjugate.md), for every `σ`, symmetric or not. For a
symmetric `σ` it is the sum of the principal stresses.
