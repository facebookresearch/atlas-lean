# Shear stress τx'y' in axes rotated by θ

For all real `σx, σy, τ` and `θ`, the [stress in axes rotated by θ](rotated-stress2.md) of the
symmetric plane stress `[[σx, τ], [τ, σy]]` has entry `(0, 1)`
`τx'y' = -(σx - σy)/2 · sin 2θ + τ cos 2θ`. Here `θ` is the angle from the `x`-axis to the new
`x'`-axis, measured towards the `y`-axis.
