# Traction vector on a face

For `n ∈ ℕ`, a real `n × n` matrix `σ` (a Cauchy stress) and a vector `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the traction on the
face with normal `v` is `t = σ v`, with components `t_i = ∑_j σ_ij v_j`. It is defined for every
vector `v`; the physical traction on a face is the case of the unit outward normal. Stress is
tension-positive: on a face with unit normal `v`, the normal stress `v · σ v` is positive in
tension.
