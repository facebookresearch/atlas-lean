# Sign change with reversed normal keeps σ v and flips the traction

For every real `n × n` matrix `σ` and `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the
[compression-positive traction](compression-positive-traction.md) of the
[compression-positive form](compression-positive-stress.md) `-σ` on the face with normal `-v` is
`-(σ v)`: `-((-σ) (-v)) = -(σ v)`, the same [sign flip](traction-neg.md) as for the tension-positive
traction. Equivalently, the tension-positive [traction](traction.md) map applied to `-σ` on the face
with the reversed normal `-v` returns the traction of `σ` on the face with normal `v`:
`(-σ) (-v) = σ v`.
