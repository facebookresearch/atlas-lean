# Mohr's circle: the rotated stresses lie on one circle

For all real `σx, σy, τ` and every `θ`, the entries `σx'` and `τx'y'` of the
[stress in axes rotated by θ](rotated-stress2.md) of `[[σx, τ], [τ, σy]]` satisfy
`(σx' - (σx + σy)/2)² + τx'y'² = ((σx - σy)/2)² + τ²`. So the point `(σx', τx'y')` lies on the
circle with [centre](mohr-mean.md) `((σx + σy)/2, 0)` and [squared radius](mohr-radius-sq.md)
`((σx - σy)/2)² + τ²`. When `σx = σy` and `τ = 0` the circle is the single point `(σx, 0)`.
