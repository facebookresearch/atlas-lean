# Normal stress σy' in axes rotated by θ

For all real `σx, σy, τ` and `θ`, the [stress in axes rotated by θ](rotated-stress2.md) of the
symmetric plane stress `[[σx, τ], [τ, σy]]` has entry `(1, 1)`
`σy' = (σx + σy)/2 - (σx - σy)/2 · cos 2θ - τ sin 2θ`. Here `θ` is the angle from the `x`-axis to
the new `x'`-axis, measured towards the `y`-axis.
