# The mean and deviatoric parts are orthogonal

For every real `3 × 3` matrix `σ`, the part `σₘ 1` and the [deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md)
`s` are orthogonal for the Frobenius inner product `⟨A, B⟩ = tr(Aᵀ B) = ∑_ij A_ij B_ij`:
`tr((σₘ 1)ᵀ s) = σₘ tr s = 0`, where `σₘ = tr σ / 3` is the [mean stress](mean-stress.md).
