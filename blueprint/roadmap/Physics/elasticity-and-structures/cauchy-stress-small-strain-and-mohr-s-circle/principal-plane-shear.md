# Shear stress at angle θ in a principal plane

For real `s₁, s₃` and `θ`, the principal-plane shear is `τ(θ) = -(s₁ - s₃)/2 · sin 2θ`. It is the
[shear entry](rotated-stress2-01.md) of the plane stress `diag(s₁, s₃)` in axes rotated by `θ`. For
principal stresses `s₁` and `s₃` it is thus the shear stress, within the plane of their two
principal directions, on the face whose normal makes the angle `θ` with the `s₁` direction. The
definition takes any real `s₁` and `s₃`.
