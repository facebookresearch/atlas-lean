# Small-strain tensor

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `H`, the small (infinitesimal) strain is `ε = (H + Hᵀ)/2`, the symmetric
part of `H`. For `H` the [displacement gradient](displacement-gradient.md) of a displacement field
it is the linearized strain, an approximation valid for small displacement gradients; the formula is
defined for every `H`.
