# A skew linear displacement has zero small strain

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `W` with `Wᵀ = -W` and every point `x ∈ ℝ³`, the displacement
`u(x) = W x`, an infinitesimal rigid rotation, has [displacement gradient](displacement-gradient.md)
`W` at `x` and [small strain](small-strain.md) `(W + Wᵀ)/2 = 0`. The small strain is a
linearization: a finite rotation `R` gives the displacement `(R - 1) x`, whose small strain
`(R + Rᵀ)/2 - 1` is zero only for `R = 1`.
