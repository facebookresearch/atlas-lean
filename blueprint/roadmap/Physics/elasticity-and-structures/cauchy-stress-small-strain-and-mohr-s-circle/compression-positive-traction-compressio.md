# The stress-sign change negates σ v but keeps the physical traction

For every real `n × n` matrix `σ`, read as a tension-positive stress, and `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the
[compression-positive traction](compression-positive-traction.md) of the
[compression-positive form](compression-positive-stress.md) `-σ` is the tension-positive
[traction](traction.md) of `σ`: `-((-σ) v) = σ v`. The physical traction does not depend on the sign
convention. Equivalently, the tension-positive traction map applied to `-σ` gives the negative of
its value for `σ` on the same face: `(-σ) v = -(σ v)`.
