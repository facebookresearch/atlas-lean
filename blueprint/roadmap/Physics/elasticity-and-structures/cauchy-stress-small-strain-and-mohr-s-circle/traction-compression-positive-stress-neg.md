# Sign change and reversed normal leave the traction map unchanged

For every real `n × n` matrix `σ` and `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, `(-σ) (-v) = σ v`: the tension-positive
[traction](traction.md) map applied to the
[compression-positive form](compression-positive-stress.md) `-σ` on the face with the reversed
normal `-v` returns the traction of `σ` on the face with normal `v`.
