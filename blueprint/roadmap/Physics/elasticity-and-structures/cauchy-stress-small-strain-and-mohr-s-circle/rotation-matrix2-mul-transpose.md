# The axis-rotation matrix satisfies Q Qᵀ = 1

For every `θ ∈ ℝ`, the [axis-rotation matrix](rotation-matrix2.md)
`Q(θ) = [[cos θ, sin θ], [-sin θ, cos θ]]` satisfies `Q(θ) Q(θ)ᵀ = 1`, by `cos² θ + sin² θ = 1`.
With the [left-inverse identity](rotation-matrix2-transpose-mul.md) `Q(θ)ᵀ Q(θ) = 1`, `Q(θ)` is
orthogonal.
