# The tension-positive traction map changes sign with the stress sign

For every real `n × n` matrix `σ` and `v ∈ ℝⁿ`, the tension-positive [traction](traction.md) map
applied to the [compression-positive form](compression-positive-stress.md) `-σ` gives the negative
of its value for `σ` on the same face: `(-σ) v = -(σ v)`. This is a sign identity for the map
`σ ↦ σ v`; the physical traction of a compression-positive stress is its
[compression-positive traction](compression-positive-traction.md), which the change of convention
[preserves](compression-positive-traction-compressio.md).
