# Symmetric stress matrix

For `n ∈ ℕ`, a real `n × n` matrix `σ` is a symmetric stress when `σᵀ = σ`, that is `σ_ij = σ_ji`
for all indices `i, j`. For the Cauchy stress this symmetry is a model assumption: it follows from
the balance of angular momentum when there are no body couples (no couple stresses).
