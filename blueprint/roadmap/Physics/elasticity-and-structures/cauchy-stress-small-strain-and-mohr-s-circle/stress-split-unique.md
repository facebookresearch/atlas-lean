# The isotropic-traceless split of a 3 × 3 matrix is unique

Let `σ` be a real `3 × 3` matrix. If `σ = p 1 + s'` for a real number `p` and a `3 × 3` matrix `s'`
with `tr s' = 0`, then `p = tr σ / 3` is the [mean stress](mean-stress.md) of `σ` and `s'` is its
[deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md).
