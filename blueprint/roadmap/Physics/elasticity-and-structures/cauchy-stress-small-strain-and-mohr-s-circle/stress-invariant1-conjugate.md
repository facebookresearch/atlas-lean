# The first invariant is unchanged by an orthogonal change of axes

For a real `3 × 3` matrix `σ` and an orthogonal `3 × 3` matrix `Q`, `Qᵀ Q = 1` and `Q Qᵀ = 1`, the
[first invariant](stress-invariant1.md) of `Q σ Qᵀ` equals that of `σ`: `tr(Q σ Qᵀ) = tr σ`. Here
`Q σ Qᵀ` holds the components of the stress in the orthonormal axes given by the rows of `Q`; no
symmetry of `σ` is needed.
