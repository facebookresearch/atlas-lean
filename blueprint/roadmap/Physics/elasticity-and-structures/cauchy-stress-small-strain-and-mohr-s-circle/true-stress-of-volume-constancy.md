# Under volume constancy, true stress is engineering stress times 1 + e

Let `F`, `A₀`, `A` and `e` be real numbers with `e > -1`, `A₀ ≠ 0` and `A = A₀ / (1 + e)`. Then the
[true stress](true-stress.md) is the [engineering stress](eng-stress.md) times `1 + e`:
`F / A = (F / A₀) (1 + e)`. The area relation is the volume-constancy (incompressibility) model
hypothesis for uniaxial deformation: the volume `A L = A₀ L₀` is kept while the length becomes
`L = L₀ (1 + e)`. In the physical reading `A₀ > 0`; the identity holds for every nonzero `A₀`.
