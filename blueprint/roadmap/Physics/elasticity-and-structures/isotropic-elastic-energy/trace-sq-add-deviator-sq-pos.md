# The bulk-plus-deviatoric quadratic form is positive on nonzero arrays

For moduli `K > 0` and `μ > 0` and every nonzero real `3 × 3` array `ε`, symmetric or not,
`(K/2) (tr ε)² + μ ∑ᵢⱼ (dev ε)ᵢⱼ² > 0`, where `(dev ε)ᵢⱼ = εᵢⱼ - (tr ε / 3) δᵢⱼ` is the
deviator. For the small strain of a displacement gradient this is the
[positivity of the isotropic elastic energy](elastic-energy-pos.md).
