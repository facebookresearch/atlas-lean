# The isotropic elastic energy of a nonzero small strain is positive

For moduli `K > 0` and `μ > 0` and a real `3 × 3` displacement gradient `H` whose small strain
`ε = (H + Hᵀ) / 2` is nonzero, the isotropic elastic energy density
`W = (K/2) (tr ε)² + μ ∑ᵢⱼ (dev ε)ᵢⱼ²`, where `(dev ε)ᵢⱼ = εᵢⱼ - (tr ε / 3) δᵢⱼ`, is positive.
