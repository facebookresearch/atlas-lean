# Clamped–free buckling eigenpair at the load π² EI / (4L²)

For `EI > 0` (flexural rigidity) and `L > 0`, the [clamped–free mode](clamped-free-mode.md)
`w(x) = 1 − cos(πx / (2L))` and the axial load `P = π² EI / (4L²)` satisfy the Euler column equation
`EI w'''' + P w'' = 0` at every `x`, the clamped conditions `w(0) = w'(0) = 0`, and the free-end
conditions `w''(L) = 0` and `EI w'''(L) + P w'(L) = 0`. This is one explicit eigenpair: it is not
claimed to give the lowest buckling load or to belong to a complete set of modes.
