# The clamped–free mode solves the column equation for every EI

For `L > 0` and every real `EI`, the [clamped–free mode](clamped-free-mode.md)
`w(x) = 1 − cos(πx / (2L))` and the axial load `P = π² EI / (4L²)` satisfy
`EI w''''(x) + P w''(x) = 0` at every `x`, the clamped conditions `w(0) = w'(0) = 0`, and the
free-end conditions `w''(L) = 0` and `EI w'''(L) + P w'(L) = 0`. For `EI = 0` the load is `0`, and
the column equation and the last condition hold trivially. This is one explicit eigenpair: it is not
claimed to give the lowest buckling load or to belong to a complete set of modes.
