# Below the Euler load the quadratic energy is positive

Let `EI > 0`, `L > 0` and an axial load `P < π² EI / L²` (compression positive), and let functions
`w, w', w'' : ℝ → ℝ` such that, at every `x ∈ [0, L]`, `w` has derivative `w'(x)` and `w'` has
derivative `w''(x)`, with `w''` continuous on `[0, L]`, with `w(0) = w(L) = 0` and `w(x) ≠ 0` for
some `x ∈ [0, L]`. Then `EI ∫₀ᴸ w''² − P ∫₀ᴸ w'² > 0`.
