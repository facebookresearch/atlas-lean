# The stress map gives the components of the rotated stress

For every real `θ` and every symmetric real `2 × 2` matrix `σ`, the [stress map](stress-map.md)
`T_s(θ)` applied to `(σ_xx, σ_yy, τ_xy)` gives the [engineering stress components](stress-voigt.md)
of the [rotated stress](../cauchy-stress-small-strain-and-mohr-s-circle/rotated-stress2.md)
`R(θ) σ R(θ)ᵀ`, where `R(θ) = [[cos θ, sin θ], [−sin θ, cos θ]]`, the matrix that turns the axes by
`θ` (the physical rotation by `−θ`).
