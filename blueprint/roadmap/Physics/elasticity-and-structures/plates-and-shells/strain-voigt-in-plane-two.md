# The shear component of the in-plane block is the engineering shear

For every real `3 × 3` matrix `ε`, the third [engineering strain component](strain-voigt.md) of its
[in-plane block](in-plane.md) is `2 ε₀₁`, the
[engineering shear](../cauchy-stress-small-strain-and-mohr-s-circle/engineering-shear.md) `γ_xy` of
`ε`.
