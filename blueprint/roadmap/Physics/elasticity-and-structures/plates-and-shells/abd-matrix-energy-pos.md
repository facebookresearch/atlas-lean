# With positive definite plies the laminate energy is positive

For `n ≥ 1` plies, strictly increasing interfaces `z_0 < z_1 < … < z_n` and symmetric positive
definite ply stiffnesses `Q̄_i`, and `ε⁰, κ ∈ ℝ³` not both zero,
`(ε⁰, κ) · ([[A, B], [B, D]] (ε⁰, κ)) > 0`, where `[[A, B], [B, D]]` is the
[block stiffness matrix](abd-matrix.md).
