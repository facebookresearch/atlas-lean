# The strain map gives the components of the rotated strain

For every real `θ` and every symmetric real `2 × 2` matrix `ε`, the [strain map](strain-map.md)
`T_e(θ)` applied to `(ε_xx, ε_yy, γ_xy)` gives the [engineering strain components](strain-voigt.md)
of `R(θ) ε R(θ)ᵀ`, where `R(θ) = [[cos θ, sin θ], [−sin θ, cos θ]]`, the matrix that turns the axes
by `θ` (the physical rotation by `−θ`).
