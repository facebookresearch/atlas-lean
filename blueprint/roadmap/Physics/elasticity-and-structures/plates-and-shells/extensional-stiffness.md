# Extensional stiffness of a laminate

For `n ∈ ℕ`, interfaces `z_0, …, z_n` (heights from the reference plane) and ply stiffnesses
`Q̄_0, …, Q̄_{n−1}` (real `3 × 3` matrices in global axes, ply `i` occupying `[z_i, z_{i+1}]`),
`A = ∑_i (z_{i+1} − z_i) Q̄_i`. In the physical reading the interfaces increase from bottom to top
and the ply stiffnesses are symmetric positive definite; the formula is defined for all real inputs.
