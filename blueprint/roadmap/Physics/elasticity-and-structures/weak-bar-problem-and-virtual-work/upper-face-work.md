# Virtual work of the traction on an upper face of a box

For a box `[a, b] ⊂ ℝ³`, a stress field `σ`, a virtual displacement `v` and an index `i`, the
upper-face work is the integral over the face `x_i = b_i` (the other two coordinates ranging over
their intervals) of `(σ(x) e_i) · v(x)`: the
[traction](../cauchy-stress-small-strain-and-mohr-s-circle/traction.md) for the outward normal
`+e_i` against `v`.
