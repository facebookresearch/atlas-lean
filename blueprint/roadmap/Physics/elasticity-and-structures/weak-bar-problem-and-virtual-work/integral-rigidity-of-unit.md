# The energy integral of a unit function equals that of its push-forward

For a real `L > 0`, every function `EA` and all elements `w₁`, `w₂` of the
[unit space `H¹₀(0, 1)`](../../../Analysis/partial-differential-equations/weak-solutions-of-elliptic-equations/weak-h10.md), `∫_0^L EA(x) w₁'(x) w₂'(x) dx = ∫_0^1 κ(ξ) ŵ₁'(ξ) ŵ₂'(ξ) dξ`,
where `w₁`, `w₂` on the left are the [push-forwards](of-unit.md) of `ŵ₁`, `ŵ₂` and `κ` is the
[rescaled rigidity](scaled-rigidity.md).
