# The load integral of a unit function equals that of its push-forward

For a real `L > 0`, every function `q` and every element `ŵ` of the
[unit space `H¹₀(0, 1)`](../../../Analysis/partial-differential-equations/weak-solutions-of-elliptic-equations/weak-h10.md), `∫_0^L q(x) w(x) dx = ∫_0^1 f̂(ξ) ŵ(ξ) dξ`, where `w` is the
[push-forward](of-unit.md) of `ŵ` and `f̂` the [rescaled load](scaled-load.md).
