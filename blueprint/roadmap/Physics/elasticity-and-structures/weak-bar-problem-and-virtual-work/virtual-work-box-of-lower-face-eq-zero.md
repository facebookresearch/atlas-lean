# Virtual work identity with a clamped face removed

Under the hypotheses of the [virtual work identity](virtual-work-box.md) (`a_i < b_i`, `U` open
containing the box, `σ` with continuously differentiable components on `U` and symmetric on the box,
`v` continuously differentiable on `U`, `div σ + body = 0` on the box), if `v` vanishes on the lower
face `x_{i₀} = a_{i₀}`, then `∫ σ : ε(v) = ∫ body · v + ∑_i W⁺_i + ∑_{i ≠ i₀} W⁻_i`, where `W⁺_i`
and `W⁻_i` are the [upper](upper-face-work.md) and [lower](lower-face-work.md) face works: that face,
and only that face, is removed.
