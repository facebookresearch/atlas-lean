# Pairing of supported compatibility and equilibrium

Let `J` (joints), `M` (members) and `F` (free coordinates) be finite sets, with joint positions
`r_j` in the plane, member `e` joining joint `a_e` to joint `b_e`, and a matrix `S` from the free
coordinates to the joint displacement coordinates. For every `u` on the free coordinates and every
vector `N` of member coefficients, the [compatibility matrix](axial-compatibility.md) `B` satisfies
`(B S u) · N = u · ((B S)ᵀ N)`.
