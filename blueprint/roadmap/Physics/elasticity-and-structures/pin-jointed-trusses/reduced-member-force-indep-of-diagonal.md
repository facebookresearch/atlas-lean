# Determinate member forces do not depend on the axial weights

Let `n` be a finite set and `B_s` an `n × n` real matrix with `det B_s ≠ 0`. If two weight vectors
`d₁` and `d₂` and displacements `u₁` and `u₂` satisfy `(B_sᵀ diag(d₁) B_s) u₁ = f` and
`(B_sᵀ diag(d₂) B_s) u₂ = f` for the same load `f`, then `diag(d₁) (B_s u₁) = diag(d₂) (B_s u₂)`:
the member forces agree, while the displacements may differ.
