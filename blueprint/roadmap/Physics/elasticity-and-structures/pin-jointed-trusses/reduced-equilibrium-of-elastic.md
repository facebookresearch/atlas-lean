# Elastic member forces satisfy the reduced equilibrium

Let `n` be a finite set, `B_s` an `n × n` real matrix and `d` a vector of weights indexed by `n`. If
`(B_sᵀ diag(d) B_s) u = f`, then the elastic member forces `diag(d) (B_s u)` satisfy the reduced
equilibrium `B_sᵀ (diag(d) (B_s u)) = f`. No invertibility or sign condition is assumed.
