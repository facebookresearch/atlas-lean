# Elastic member forces equal the determinate statics solution

Let `n` be a finite set, `B_s` an `n × n` real matrix and `d` a vector of weights indexed by `n`. If
`det B_s ≠ 0` and `(B_sᵀ diag(d) B_s) u = f`, then the elastic member forces are the statics
solution: `diag(d) (B_s u) = (B_sᵀ)⁻¹ f`.
