# Unique displacement of a determinate system with positive weights

Let `n` be a finite set, `B_s` an `n × n` real matrix and `d` a vector of weights indexed by `n`. If
`det B_s ≠ 0` and every weight is positive, `d_e > 0`, then every load `f` has exactly one
displacement `u` with `(B_sᵀ diag(d) B_s) u = f`.
