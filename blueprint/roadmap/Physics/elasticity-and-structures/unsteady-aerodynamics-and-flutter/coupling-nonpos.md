# With a nonpositive aerodynamic factor there is no divergence

For real numbers `K > 0`, `S`, `e` and `C_Lα` with `S e C_Lα ≤ 0`, the
[divergence pressure](divergence-pressure.md) formula gives `K / (S e C_Lα) ≤ 0`, and the
[effective stiffness](effective-stiffness.md) `K - q S e C_Lα` is positive for every `q ≥ 0`.
