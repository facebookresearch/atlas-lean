# Stable, neutral and unstable circulatory witnesses

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and call $z : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^2$ a solution at load $q$ if $z''(t) + 2z'(t) + z(t) - q\,J z(t) = 0$ for every $t$. Then:

1. at $q = 0$, every $C^2$ solution satisfies $z(t) \to 0$ as $t \to \infty$;
2. at $q = 2$, $z(t) = (\cos t, \sin t)$ is a solution with Euclidean norm $\sqrt{z(t)\cdot z(t)} = 1$ for every $t$;
3. at $q = 8$, $z(t) = e^{t}(\cos 2t, \sin 2t)$ is a solution whose Euclidean norm tends to $+\infty$ as $t \to \infty$.

These are three sample loads, not a threshold classification in $q$.
