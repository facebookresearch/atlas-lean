# Positive aerodynamic factor: zero stiffness exactly at divergence

For real numbers `K`, `q`, `S`, `e` and `C_Lα` with `S e C_Lα > 0`, the
[effective stiffness](effective-stiffness.md) `K - q S e C_Lα` is `0` if and only if `q` equals the
[divergence pressure](divergence-pressure.md) `K / (S e C_Lα)`.
