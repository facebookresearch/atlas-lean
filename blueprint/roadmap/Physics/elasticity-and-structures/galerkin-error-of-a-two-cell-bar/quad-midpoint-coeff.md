# Midpoint-load interior coefficient

For the two-cell bar under $Q(x/L)^2$, the midpoint-quadrature interior coefficient is $F_{mid}/K$ with the composite midpoint load $F_{mid}$ and $K = 4EA/L$. Division is total, with $a/0 = 0$, so the coefficient is $0$ when $K = 0$, in particular when $EA = 0$ or $L = 0$.
