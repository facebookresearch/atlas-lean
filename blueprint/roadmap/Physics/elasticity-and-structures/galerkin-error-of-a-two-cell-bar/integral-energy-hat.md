# Stiffness is the energy of the hat

Consider a fixed-fixed bar of length $L$ and axial rigidity $EA$, discretized by the two-cell partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ pulled back by $x/L$, with interior hat function $\varphi$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). If $L > 0$, then $\int_0^LEA\,\bigl(\tfrac{d}{dx}\varphi(x/L)\bigr)^2\,dx = 4EA/L$.
