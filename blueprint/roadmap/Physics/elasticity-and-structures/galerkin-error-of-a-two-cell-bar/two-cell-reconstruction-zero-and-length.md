# Hat reconstructions satisfy the boundary conditions

Let $\varphi$ be the interior hat function of the partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear). For $L \ne 0$, every reconstruction $c\,\varphi(x/L)$ vanishes at $x = 0$ and $x = L$.
