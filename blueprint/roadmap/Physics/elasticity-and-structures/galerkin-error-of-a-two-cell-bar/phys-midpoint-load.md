# Midpoint interior load on the physical bar

On the two-cell mesh of $[0, L]$, the composite midpoint load of the interior node for a load $q$ is $\tfrac L2\bigl(q(\tfrac L4)\varphi(\tfrac{L/4}{L}) + q(\tfrac{3L}4)\varphi(\tfrac{3L/4}{L})\bigr)$. For $L \ne 0$ the hat arguments are $\tfrac14$ and $\tfrac34$; for $L = 0$ the load is $0$.
