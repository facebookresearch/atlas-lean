# Midpoint interior load on the physical bar

On the two-cell mesh $\{0, L/2, L\}$ of $[0, L]$, with $\varphi$ the interior hat function of the partition $\{0, \tfrac12, 1\}$ of $[0, 1]$ ($\varphi(0) = \varphi(1) = 0$, $\varphi(\tfrac12) = 1$, piecewise linear), the composite midpoint load of the interior node for a load $q$ is $\tfrac L2\bigl(q(\tfrac L4)\varphi(\tfrac{L/4}{L}) + q(\tfrac{3L}4)\varphi(\tfrac{3L/4}{L})\bigr)$. For $L \ne 0$ the hat arguments are $\tfrac14$ and $\tfrac34$; for $L = 0$ the load is $0$.
