# Molar gas constant from the Avogadro and Boltzmann constants

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For an Avogadro constant $N_A$ (dimension $\mathsf{N}^{-1}$) and a Boltzmann constant $k_B$ (dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\Theta^{-1}$), the molar gas constant $R = N_A\,k_B$ is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1}$ whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates.
