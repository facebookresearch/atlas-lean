# Molar energy of a per-particle energy

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For an Avogadro constant $N_A$ (dimension $\mathsf{N}^{-1}$) and a per-particle energy $e$ (dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$), the molar energy $E = N_A\,e$ is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$ whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates.
