# Molar energy over gas constant times temperature is dimensionless

In the ISQ, $(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}) / \big((\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1})\,\Theta\big) = 1$: the exponent $E/(R\,T)$ of a molar energy over a gas constant and a temperature is dimensionless.
