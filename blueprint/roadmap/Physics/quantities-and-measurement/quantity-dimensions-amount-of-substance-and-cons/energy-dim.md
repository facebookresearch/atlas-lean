# Dimension of energy

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of energy is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$.
