# Change-of-units factor of a base dimension

Let $B$ be a finite dimension basis and $u_1, u_2$ unit scales on $B$ (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base $b \in B$); write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_{b} (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the scaling factor of a dimension $d$, a positive real number. For a base $b \in B$ with base dimension $\mathsf{b}$, $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{b}) = u_1(b)/u_2(b)$, the ratio of the magnitudes chosen at $b$.
