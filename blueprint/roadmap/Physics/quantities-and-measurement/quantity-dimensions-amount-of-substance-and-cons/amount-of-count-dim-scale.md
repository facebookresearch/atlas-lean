# Amount of a count rescales by the amount factor

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. If $N_A > 0$, then for every particle count $N$, the amount of substance computed from the rescaled quantities with coordinates $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{N}^{-1})\,N_A$ and $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(1)\,N$ is the amount with coordinate $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{N})\,(N/N_A)$.
