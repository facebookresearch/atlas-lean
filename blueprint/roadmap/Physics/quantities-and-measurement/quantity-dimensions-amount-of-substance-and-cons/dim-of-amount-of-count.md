# Amount of a count has the amount dimension

For every Avogadro constant $N_A$ and particle count $N$, the dimension of the amount of substance $N/N_A$ is $\mathsf{N}$.

Real division by zero is $0$, so for $N_A = 0$ the coordinate of the quotient is $0$, and its tagged dimension is still $\mathsf{N}$.
