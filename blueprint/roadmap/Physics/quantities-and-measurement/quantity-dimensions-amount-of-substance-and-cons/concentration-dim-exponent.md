# Exponents of the amount concentration dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the dimension of amount concentration are: length $-3$, mass $0$, time $0$, current $0$, temperature $0$, amount $1$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{L}^{-3}\,\mathsf{N}$.
