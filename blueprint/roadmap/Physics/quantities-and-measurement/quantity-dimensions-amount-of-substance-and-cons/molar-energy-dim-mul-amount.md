# Molar energy times amount is energy

In the ISQ, $(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1})\,\mathsf{N} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$: a molar energy times an amount of substance has the energy dimension.

Equivalently, $\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}) = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$: the dimension of the Avogadro constant $N_A$ times the energy dimension is the molar energy dimension, the dimension of $E = N_A\,e$.
