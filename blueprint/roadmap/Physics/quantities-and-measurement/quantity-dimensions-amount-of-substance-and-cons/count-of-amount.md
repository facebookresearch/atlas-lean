# Particle count of an amount of substance

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For an Avogadro constant $N_A$ of dimension $\mathsf{N}^{-1}$ and an amount of substance $n$ of dimension $\mathsf{N}$, the particle count $N = n\,N_A$ is the dimensionless quantity whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates.
