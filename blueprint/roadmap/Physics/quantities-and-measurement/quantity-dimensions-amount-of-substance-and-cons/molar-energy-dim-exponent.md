# Exponents of the molar energy dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the dimension of molar energy are: length $2$, mass $1$, time $-2$, current $0$, temperature $0$, amount $-1$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$.
