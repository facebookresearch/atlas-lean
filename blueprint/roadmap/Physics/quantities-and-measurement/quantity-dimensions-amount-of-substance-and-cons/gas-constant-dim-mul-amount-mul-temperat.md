# Gas constant times amount and temperature is energy

Writing $[X]$ for the ISQ dimension of a quantity $X$: $[R]\,\mathsf{N}\,\Theta = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$: the molar gas constant times an amount of substance and a temperature has the energy dimension.

Equivalently, $\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\Theta^{-1}) = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1}$: the dimension of $N_A$ times that of $k_B$ is the dimension of $R = N_A\,k_B$.
