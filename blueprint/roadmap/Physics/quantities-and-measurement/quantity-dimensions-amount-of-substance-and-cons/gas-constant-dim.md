# Dimension of the molar gas constant

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of the molar gas constant is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2} / (\mathsf{N}\,\Theta) = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1}$: energy per amount of substance per temperature.
