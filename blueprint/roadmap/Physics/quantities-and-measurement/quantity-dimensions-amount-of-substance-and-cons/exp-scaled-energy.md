# Boltzmann factor of a molar energy

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a molar energy $E$ (dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$), a molar gas constant $R$ (dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1}$) with $R > 0$, and an absolute temperature $T$ (dimension $\Theta$) with $T > 0$, the factor is $\exp\!\big(-E/(R\,T)\big)$, where $E/(R\,T)$ is the dimensionless quotient of the quantities.
