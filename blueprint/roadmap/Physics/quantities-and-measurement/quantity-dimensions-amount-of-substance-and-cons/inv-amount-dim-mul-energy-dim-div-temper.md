# Avogadro times Boltzmann dimension is the gas constant dimension

In the ISQ, $\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\Theta^{-1}) = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}\,\Theta^{-1}$: the dimension of $N_A$ times that of $k_B$ is the dimension of $R = N_A\,k_B$.
