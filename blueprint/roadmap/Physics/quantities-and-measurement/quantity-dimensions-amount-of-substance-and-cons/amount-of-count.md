# Amount of substance of a particle count

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For an Avogadro constant $N_A$ of dimension $\mathsf{N}^{-1}$ and a dimensionless particle count $N$, the amount of substance $n = N/N_A$ is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{N}$ whose coordinate is the quotient of the coordinates (equal to $0$ when $N_A = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$).
