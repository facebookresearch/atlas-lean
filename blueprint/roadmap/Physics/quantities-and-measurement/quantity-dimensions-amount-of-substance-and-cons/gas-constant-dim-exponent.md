# Exponents of the gas constant dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the dimension of the molar gas constant are: length $2$, mass $1$, time $-2$, current $0$, temperature $-1$, amount $-1$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\Theta^{-1}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$.
