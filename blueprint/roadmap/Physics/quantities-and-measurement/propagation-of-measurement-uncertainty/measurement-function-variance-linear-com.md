# Variance of a linear combination of inputs

The variance of a linear combination of input quantities with finite second moments on a probability
space is `Var[∑ i, a i * X i] = ∑ i, ∑ j, a i * a j * cov[X i, X j]`, exactly: the variance of a sum
is the sum of all covariances, and covariance is bilinear.
