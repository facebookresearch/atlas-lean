# Power-law measurement function

The power-law measurement function `y = ∏ i, x i ^ p i` with integer exponents `p i`. It
covers products (`p i = 1`), quotients (`p i = -1` on the denominators) and powers. For `x i = 0`
and `p i < 0` the factor is `0` by the convention `0⁻¹ = 0`, so a zero denominator makes `y = 0`;
the intended domain is `x i ≠ 0` wherever `p i < 0`.
