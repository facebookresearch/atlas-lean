# Derivative of the logarithm of a power law

The gradient of the logarithm of a power law: at inputs with every `x i ≠ 0`, the function
`ξ ↦ log |y(ξ)|`, with `y(ξ) = ∏ i, ξ i ^ p i`, has derivative `∑ i, (p i / x i) • dxᵢ` at `x`:
`∂(log |y|)/∂xᵢ = p i / x i`. The logarithm is extended to negative arguments by `log y = log |y|`.
Equivalently `∂y/∂xᵢ = y * (p i / x i)`.
