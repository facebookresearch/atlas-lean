# Quadratic calibration of a voltage

For real coefficients $c_0$, $c_1$ and $c_2$, the quadratic calibration maps a voltage $v$ to

$$
f(v) = c_0 + c_1 v + c_2 v^2,
$$

for every real $v$; $c_2 = 0$ gives an affine calibration. In the physical reading $v$ is in volts,
$f(v)$ and $c_0$ are in the output unit, $c_1$ is in output units per volt and $c_2$ in output units
per volt squared, as the [dimensioned form](quad-calibration-qty.md) records.
