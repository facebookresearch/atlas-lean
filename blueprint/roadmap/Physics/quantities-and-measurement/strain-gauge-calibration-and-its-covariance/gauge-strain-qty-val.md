# The coordinate of the strain quantity is the real strain formula

For quantities $V$, $b$ and $g$ of the [voltage dimension](voltage-dim.md) such that the coordinate
of $g$ is nonzero, the coordinate of the [dimensionless strain](gauge-strain-qty.md) built from them
is the [real strain](gauge-strain.md) of their coordinates:

$$
\left\{\frac{V - b}{g}\right\} = \frac{\{V\} - \{b\}}{\{g\}},
$$

where $\{X\}$ denotes the coordinate (numerical value) of a quantity $X$.
