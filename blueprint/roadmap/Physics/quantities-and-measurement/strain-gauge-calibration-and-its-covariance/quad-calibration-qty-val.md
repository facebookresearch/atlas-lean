# Coordinate of the dimensioned quadratic calibration

Let $d$ be a dimension over the seven ISQ base quantities and $\mathcal{V}$ the
[voltage dimension](voltage-dim.md). For coefficients $c_0$, $c_1$ and $c_2$ of dimensions $d$,
$d\,\mathcal{V}^{-1}$ and $d\,\mathcal{V}^{-2}$ and a voltage $v$ of dimension $\mathcal{V}$, the
coordinate of the [dimensioned calibration](quad-calibration-qty.md) is the
[real quadratic calibration](quad-calibration.md) of the coordinates:

$$
\{f(v)\} = \{c_0\} + \{c_1\}\,\{v\} + \{c_2\}\,\{v\}^2,
$$

where $\{X\}$ denotes the coordinate (numerical value) of a quantity $X$.
