# Strain as a dimensionless quantity from voltage quantities

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be the [voltage dimension](voltage-dim.md). For quantities $V$, $b$ and $g$ of
dimension $\mathcal{V}$ such that the coordinate of $g$ is nonzero, the strain is the quotient

$$
\frac{V - b}{g},
$$

a quantity of dimension $\mathcal{V}\,\mathcal{V}^{-1}$, recast to dimension one by the identity
$\mathcal{V}\,\mathcal{V}^{-1} = 1$: the strain is dimensionless. A proof that the coordinate of $g$
is nonzero is an argument of the definition, so no division by zero occurs. The coordinate of the
strain is the [real strain](gauge-strain.md) of the coordinates of $V$, $b$ and $g$
([coordinate of the strain quantity](gauge-strain-qty-val.md)).
