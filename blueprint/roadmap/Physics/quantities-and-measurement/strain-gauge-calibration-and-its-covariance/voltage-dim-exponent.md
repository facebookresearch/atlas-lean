# Exponents of the voltage dimension at the seven ISQ base quantities

The [voltage dimension](voltage-dim.md) has exponent $2$ at length, $1$ at mass, $-3$ at time, $-1$
at electric current, and $0$ at thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance and luminous
intensity:

$$
\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}\,\mathsf{I}^{-1},
$$

the dimension of the volt, $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}$.
