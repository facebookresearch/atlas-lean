# Converting a stress value to other units and back returns it

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`, and `k'(d)` the factor back from `u₂` to `u₁`. For every real `x`,
`k'(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) (k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) x) = x`, where `M L⁻¹ T⁻²` is the
[stress](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/pressure.md) dimension.
