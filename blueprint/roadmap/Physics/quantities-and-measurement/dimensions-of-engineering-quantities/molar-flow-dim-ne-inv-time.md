# A molar flow rate is not a particle rate

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[molar-flow-rate](molar-flow-dim.md) dimension `N T⁻¹` (mol/s) differs from the inverse time `T⁻¹`
of a particle rate (1/s): an amount of substance is not a count.
