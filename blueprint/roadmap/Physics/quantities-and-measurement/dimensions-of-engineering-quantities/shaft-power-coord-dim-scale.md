# Shaft power τ ω commutes with every change of units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For every real torque `τ` and angular velocity `ω` about one axis,
the converted torque times the converted angular velocity is the converted power:
`(k(M L² T⁻²) τ) (k(T⁻¹) ω) = k(M L² T⁻³) (τ ω)`, where `M L² T⁻²` is the dimension of a torque (a
[force](force-dim.md) times a length) and `M L² T⁻³` the [power](power-dim.md) dimension.
