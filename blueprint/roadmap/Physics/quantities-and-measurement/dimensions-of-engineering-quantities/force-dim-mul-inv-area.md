# Force over area is a stress

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[force](force-dim.md) dimension times the inverse of the area dimension `L²` is the
[stress](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/stress-dim.md) dimension.
