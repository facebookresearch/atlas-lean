# Force times length and torsional stiffness have the energy dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[force](force-dim.md) dimension times the length dimension is the
[energy](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/energy-dim.md) dimension `M L² T⁻²`: a torque or a
work has the dimension of an energy.

Since the [dimension of torsional stiffness](torsional-stiffness-dim.md) per radian is defined as
force times length, it equals the energy dimension: plane angle being dimensionless, the dimension
alone does not distinguish newton metres per radian from joules.
