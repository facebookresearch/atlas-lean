# From metres to millimetres, the shaft power 12 becomes 12000000

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). The
torque `3` and the angular velocity `4` (power `12`) become `k(M L² T⁻²) · 3 = 3000000` and
`k(T⁻¹) · 4 = 4`, and their product `12000000` is the converted power `k(M L² T⁻³) · 12`.
