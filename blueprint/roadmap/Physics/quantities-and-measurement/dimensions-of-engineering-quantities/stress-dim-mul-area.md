# Stress times area is force

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[stress](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/stress-dim.md) dimension `M L⁻¹ T⁻²` times the area
dimension `L²` is the [force](force-dim.md) dimension.
