# Stress is force per area

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[stress](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/pressure.md) dimension `M L⁻¹ T⁻²` times the
area dimension `L²` is the [force](force-dim.md) dimension.

Equivalently, the [force](force-dim.md) dimension times the inverse of the area dimension `L²` is
the stress dimension.
