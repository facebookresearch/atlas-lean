# Molar-mass factor times molar-flow factor is the mass-flow factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. The
[molar-mass](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/molar-mass.md) factor times the
[molar-flow-rate](molar-flow-dim.md) factor is the [mass-flow-rate](mass-flow-dim.md) factor:
`k(M N⁻¹) k(N T⁻¹) = k(M T⁻¹)`.
