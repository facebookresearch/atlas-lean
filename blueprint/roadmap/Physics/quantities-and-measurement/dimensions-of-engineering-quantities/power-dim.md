# Dimension of power

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimension of
power is the [energy](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/energy-dim.md) dimension `M L² T⁻²`
divided by the time dimension `T`, an energy per time; its [exponents](power-dim-exponent.md) are
those of `M L² T⁻³`.
