# Stress factor times area factor is the force factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. The [stress](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/pressure.md)
factor times the area factor is the [force](force-dim.md) factor: `k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) k(L²) = k(M L T⁻²)`.
