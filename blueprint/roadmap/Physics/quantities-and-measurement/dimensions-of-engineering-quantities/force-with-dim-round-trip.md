# Converting a force quantity to other units and back returns it

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`, and `k'(d)` the factor back from `u₂` to `u₁`. For every quantity
of the [force](force-dim.md) dimension `M L T⁻²` with numerical value `x`, the quantity of the same
dimension with value `k'(M L T⁻²) (k(M L T⁻²) x)` is the original quantity.
