# From metres to millimetres, the mass flow rate 20 stays 20

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). The
density `1000`, the normal velocity `2` and the area `1 / 100` (mass flow rate `20`) become
`k(M L⁻³) · 1000 = 1 / 1000000`, `k(L T⁻¹) · 2 = 2000` and `k(L²) · (1 / 100) = 10000`, and their
product `20` is the converted mass flow rate `k(M T⁻¹) · (1000 · 2 · (1 / 100))`.
