# Molar mass times molar flow rate is a mass flow rate

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[molar-mass](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/molar-mass.md) dimension `M N⁻¹` times the
[molar-flow-rate](molar-flow-dim.md) dimension `N T⁻¹` is the [mass-flow-rate](mass-flow-dim.md)
dimension `M T⁻¹`.
