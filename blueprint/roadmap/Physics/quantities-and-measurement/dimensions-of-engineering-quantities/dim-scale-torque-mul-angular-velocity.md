# Torque factor times angular-velocity factor is the power factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. The torque factor times the angular-velocity factor is the
[power](power-dim.md) factor: `k(M L² T⁻²) k(T⁻¹) = k(M L² T⁻³)`, where a torque has dimension
`M L² T⁻²` (a [force](force-dim.md) times a length).
