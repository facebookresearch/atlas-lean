# The typed normal stress commutes with every change of units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For a normal force `F` and an area `A > 0`, the
[normal stress](normal-stress.md) of the converted force `k(M L T⁻²) F` and the converted area
`k(L²) A` is the stress `F / A` converted by `k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²)`.
