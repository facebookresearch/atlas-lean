# The Faraday constant times an amount is an electric charge

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[dimension of the Faraday constant](faraday-dim.md) times the
[amount-of-substance](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/amount-dim.md) dimension is the
electric-charge dimension `I T`.
