# Read as a force, F / A does not convert correctly

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). With
`F = A = 1`, the quotient of the converted force and area, `k(M L T⁻²) / k(L²)`, differs from
`k(M L T⁻²) · (1 / 1)`, the value converted as a [force](force-dim.md): the output of `F / A` must
be read as a stress.
