# The stress factor is the force factor over the area factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. The [force](force-dim.md) factor divided by the area factor is the
[stress](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/pressure.md) factor:
`k(M L T⁻²) / k(L²) = k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²)`. Equivalently, since the area factor is positive, the stress
factor times the area factor is the force factor: `k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) k(L²) = k(M L T⁻²)`.
