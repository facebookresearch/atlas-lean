# The typed mass flow rate commutes with every change of units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For a density `ρ`, a normal velocity `v` and an area `A > 0`, the
[mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) of the converted values `k(M L⁻³) ρ`, `k(L T⁻¹) v` and `k(L²) A`
is the mass flow rate `ρ v A` converted by `k(M T⁻¹)`.
