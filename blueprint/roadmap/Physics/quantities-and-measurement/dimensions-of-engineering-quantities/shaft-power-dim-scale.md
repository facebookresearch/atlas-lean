# The typed shaft power commutes with every change of units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For a torque `τ` and an angular velocity `ω`, the
[shaft power](shaft-power.md) of the converted torque `k(M L² T⁻²) τ` and the converted angular
velocity `k(T⁻¹) ω` is the power `τ ω` converted by `k(M L² T⁻³)`.
