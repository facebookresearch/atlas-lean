# A sum of a force and an area converts like no dimension

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). There is
no dimension `d` such that `k(M L T⁻²) F + k(L²) A = k(d) (F + A)` for all real `F` and `A`, where
`M L T⁻²` is the [force](force-dim.md) dimension: commuting with a change of units is not a property
of every function of the values.
