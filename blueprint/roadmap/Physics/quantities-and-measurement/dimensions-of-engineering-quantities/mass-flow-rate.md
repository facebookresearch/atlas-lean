# Mass flow rate ρ v A as a typed quantity

For a mass density `ρ` (dimension `M L⁻³`), a velocity `v` normal to the area (dimension `L T⁻¹`)
and an area `A` (dimension `L²`) with `A > 0`, the mass flow rate `ṁ = ρ v A` as a quantity of the
[mass-flow-rate](mass-flow-dim.md) dimension `M T⁻¹`; its value is the product of the values.
