# Shaft power τ ω as a typed quantity

For a torque `τ` (a quantity of dimension `M L² T⁻²`, a [force](force-dim.md) times a lever arm) and
an angular velocity `ω` (a quantity of dimension `T⁻¹`) about one axis, the shaft power `P = τ ω` as
a quantity of the [power](power-dim.md) dimension; its value is the product of the values.
