# Torsional stiffness and energy have the same conversion factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. The [torsional-stiffness](torsional-stiffness-dim.md) factor equals
the [energy](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/energy-dim.md) factor. Hence a conversion from
degrees to radians is not a change of units of this kind and must be applied explicitly.
