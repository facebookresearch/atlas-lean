# A mass flow rate is not a molar flow rate

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[mass-flow-rate](mass-flow-dim.md) dimension `M T⁻¹` (kg/s) differs from the
[molar-flow-rate](molar-flow-dim.md) dimension `N T⁻¹` (mol/s).
