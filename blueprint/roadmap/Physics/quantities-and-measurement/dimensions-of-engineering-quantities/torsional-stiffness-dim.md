# Dimension of torsional stiffness per radian

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimension of
torsional stiffness is the [force](force-dim.md) dimension times the length dimension `L`, a force
times a length: a torque per radian, plane angle being dimensionless; its
[exponents](torsional-stiffness-dim-exponent.md) are those of `M L² T⁻²`.
