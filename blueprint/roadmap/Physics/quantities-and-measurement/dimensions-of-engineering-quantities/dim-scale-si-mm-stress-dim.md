# From metres to millimetres, stress values are divided by 1000

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). For the
[stress](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/stress-dim.md) dimension, `k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) = 1 / 1000`:
`1 kg m⁻¹ s⁻² = 1 / 1000 kg mm⁻¹ s⁻²`.
