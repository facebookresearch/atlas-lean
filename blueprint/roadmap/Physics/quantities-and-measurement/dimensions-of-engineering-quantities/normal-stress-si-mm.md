# From metres to millimetres, the stress 5 / 2 becomes 1 / 400

Let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from the [SI base units](si-scale.md) to the [millimetre units](mm-scale.md). The
normal force `5` on the area `2` (stress `5 / 2`) becomes the force `k(M L T⁻²) · 5 = 5000` on the
area `k(L²) · 2 = 2000000`, and their quotient `1 / 400` is the converted stress
`k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) · (5 / 2)`.
