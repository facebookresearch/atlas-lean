# Torsional stiffness per radian has the energy dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[dimension of torsional stiffness](torsional-stiffness-dim.md) per radian equals the
[energy](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/energy-dim.md) dimension, because plane angle is
dimensionless: the dimension alone does not distinguish newton metres per radian from joules.
