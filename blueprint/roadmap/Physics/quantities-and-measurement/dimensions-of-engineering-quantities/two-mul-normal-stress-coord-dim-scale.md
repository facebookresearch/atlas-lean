# The wrong stress 2 F / A also commutes with changes of units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For every real normal force `F` and area `A > 0`,
`2 (k(M L T⁻²) F) / (k(L²) A) = k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) (2 F / A)`: commuting with a change of units does not
certify a formula, since the empirically wrong stress `2 F / A` commutes as well.
