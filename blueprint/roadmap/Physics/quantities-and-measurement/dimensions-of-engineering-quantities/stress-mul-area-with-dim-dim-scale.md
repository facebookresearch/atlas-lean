# Stress times area converts with the force factor

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For a quantity `σ` of the
[stress](../dimensions-of-physical-quantities/stress-dim.md) dimension and a quantity `A` of the
area dimension `L²`, the product of the converted quantities, `(k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) σ) (k(L²) A)`, read as
a force, is the force `σ A` converted by the [force](force-dim.md) factor:
`(k(M L⁻¹ T⁻²) σ) (k(L²) A) = k(M L T⁻²) (σ A)`.
