# Torque times angular velocity is power

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, a torque (the
[force](force-dim.md) dimension times length) times an angular velocity `T⁻¹` has the
[power](power-dim.md) dimension. Plane angle is dimensionless, so an angular velocity has the
dimension `T⁻¹`.
