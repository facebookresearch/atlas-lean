# Force and area have different dimensions

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[force](force-dim.md) dimension differs from the area dimension `L²`, so, as typed quantities, a
force and an area have no sum, and neither can be cast to the other.
