# Dimension of translational stiffness

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimension of
translational stiffness is the [force](force-dim.md) dimension divided by the length dimension `L`,
a force per length; its [exponents](translational-stiffness-dim-exponent.md) are those of `M T⁻²`.
