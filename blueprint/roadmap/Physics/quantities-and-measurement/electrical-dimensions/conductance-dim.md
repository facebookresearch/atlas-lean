# Dimension of electrical conductance

The dimension of electrical conductance, over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, is the inverse of the resistance dimension: $(\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}\,\mathsf{C}^{-2})^{-1} = \mathsf{M}^{-1}\,\mathsf{L}^{-2}\,\mathsf{T}\,\mathsf{C}^{2}$.
