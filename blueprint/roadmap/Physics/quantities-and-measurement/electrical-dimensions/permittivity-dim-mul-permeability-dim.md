# Permittivity times permeability is an inverse squared speed

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: $[\varepsilon]\,[\mu] = \mathsf{T}^{2}\,\mathsf{L}^{-2}$, the dimension of an inverse squared speed.
