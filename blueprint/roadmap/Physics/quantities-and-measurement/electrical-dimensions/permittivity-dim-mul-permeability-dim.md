# Permittivity times permeability is an inverse squared speed

Over the charge-based base quantities length `L`, time `T`, mass `M`, electric charge `C` and
temperature `Θ`, the [permittivity](permittivity-dim.md) dimension times the
[permeability](permeability-dim.md) dimension is `T² L⁻²`, the dimension of an inverse squared
speed.
