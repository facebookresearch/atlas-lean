# Charge is current times time in the ISQ

Let $\iota$ be the group homomorphism from dimensions over length, time, mass, charge and temperature to ISQ dimensions that sends length, time, mass and temperature to the ISQ base quantities of the same name and charge $\mathsf{C}$ to $\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$. Then $\iota(\mathsf{C}) = \mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$, the product of the ISQ base dimensions of electric current and time.
