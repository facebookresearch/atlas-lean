# The law P = V + I is not dimensionally correct

The [law](voltage-plus-current.md) `P = V + I` between a power, a voltage and a current is not
dimensionally correct: for some two choices of units and some values, it holds in one choice of
units and fails for the converted values. For example (the proof's witness), `P = 2`, `V = 1` and
`I = 1` satisfy it in SI units, while their values in the units of length, time, mass, charge and
temperature that are `2`, `3`, `5`, `7` and `11` times the SI units do not.
