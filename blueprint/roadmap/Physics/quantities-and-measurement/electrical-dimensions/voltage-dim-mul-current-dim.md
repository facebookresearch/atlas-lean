# Voltage times current has the dimension of power

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: $[V]\,[I] = [P]$, where $[V] = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{C}^{-1}$, $[I] = \mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ and $[P] = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}$.
