# Time-constant law τ = R C of a resistor–capacitor circuit

The resistor–capacitor time-constant law: a relation between a time `τ`, a resistance `R` and a
capacitance `C` (values of the time, [resistance](resistance-dim.md) and
[capacitance](capacitance-dim.md) dimensions), which holds when their numerical values satisfy
`τ = R C`.
