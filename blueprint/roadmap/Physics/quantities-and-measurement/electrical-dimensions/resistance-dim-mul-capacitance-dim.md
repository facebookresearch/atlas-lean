# Resistance times capacitance has the dimension of time

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: the product of the resistance dimension and the capacitance dimension is time, $[R]\,[C] = \mathsf{T}$.
