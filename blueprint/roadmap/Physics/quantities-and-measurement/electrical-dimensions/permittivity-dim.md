# Dimension of permittivity

The dimension of permittivity, over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, is the capacitance dimension per length: $\mathsf{C}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{2}\,\mathsf{M}^{-1}\,\mathsf{L}^{-2} / \mathsf{L} = \mathsf{C}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{2}\,\mathsf{M}^{-1}\,\mathsf{L}^{-3}$.
