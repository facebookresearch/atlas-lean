# L/R time constant

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a time $\tau$ (dimension $\mathsf{T}$), an inductance $L$ and a resistance $R$, the L/R time-constant relation holds when the coordinates satisfy $\tau = L/R$. Division by zero gives zero, so for $R = 0$ the relation reads $\tau = 0$.
