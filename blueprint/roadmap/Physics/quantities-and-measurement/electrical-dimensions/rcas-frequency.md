# The wrong law f = R C for a frequency f

A deliberately wrong law: a relation between a frequency `f` (a value of dimension `T⁻¹`), a
resistance `R` and a capacitance `C`, which holds when their numerical values satisfy `f = R C`. It
is [not dimensionally correct](rc-as-frequency-not-is-dimensionally-cor.md): the product `R C` is a
[time](resistance-dim-mul-capacitance-dim.md), not a
[frequency](resistance-dim-mul-capacitance-dim-ne-in.md).
