# Charge maps to current times time

Let $\iota$ be the group homomorphism from dimensions over length, time, mass, charge and temperature to ISQ dimensions that sends length, time, mass and temperature to the ISQ base quantities of the same name and charge $\mathsf{C}$ to $\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$. Then $\iota(\mathsf{C}) = \iota(\mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1})\cdot\iota(\mathsf{T})$: the image of charge is the image of the current dimension times the image of time.
