# Change-of-units factor of electric current

For unit choices $u_1, u_2$ of the length, time, mass, charge and temperature units, write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) > 0$ for the factor by which a coordinate of a quantity of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. Then, as real numbers, $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}) = \lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{C})/\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{T})$: a current coordinate rescales by the charge factor over the time factor.
