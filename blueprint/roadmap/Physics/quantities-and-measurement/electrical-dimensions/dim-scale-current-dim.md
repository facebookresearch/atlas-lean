# Current values convert by the charge factor over the time factor

For every two choices of units of length, time, mass, charge and temperature, the conversion factor
of the [current dimension](current-dim.md) is the conversion factor of the charge dimension divided
by that of the time dimension; the latter is positive. Here a value of dimension
`L^l T^t M^m C^c Θ^θ` is multiplied by `(ℓ₁/ℓ₂)^l (t₁/t₂)^t (m₁/m₂)^m (q₁/q₂)^c (θ₁/θ₂)^θ` when the
units change from magnitudes `ℓ₁, t₁, m₁, q₁, θ₁` to `ℓ₂, t₂, m₂, q₂, θ₂`.
