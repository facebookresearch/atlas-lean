# Charge accumulated by a constant current

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a charge $Q$ (dimension $\mathsf{C}$), a current $I$ (dimension $\mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$) and a time $t$ (dimension $\mathsf{T}$), the charge relation holds when the coordinates satisfy $Q = I\,t$.
