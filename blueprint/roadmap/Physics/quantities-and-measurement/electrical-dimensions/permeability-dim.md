# Dimension of permeability

The dimension of magnetic permeability, over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, is the inductance dimension per length: $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{C}^{-2} / \mathsf{L} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{C}^{-2}$.
