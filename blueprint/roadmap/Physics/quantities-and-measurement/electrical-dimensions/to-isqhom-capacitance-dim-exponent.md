# SI base-unit exponents of capacitance

The embedding of the charge-based dimensions into the ISQ dimensions reads a charge as electric
current times time: it keeps the length, mass and temperature exponents, adds the charge exponent to
the time exponent, takes the charge exponent as the electric-current exponent, and gives amount of
substance and luminous intensity the exponent `0`. Under it, the image of the
[dimension of capacitance](capacitance-dim.md) has the exponents `-2` at length, `-1` at mass, `4`
at time and `2` at electric current, and `0` at thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance and
luminous intensity: in SI base units, `kg⁻¹ m⁻² s⁴ A²`.
