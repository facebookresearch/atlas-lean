# The time-constant law τ = R C is dimensionally correct

The [time-constant law](rctime-constant.md) `τ = R C` between a time, a resistance and a capacitance
is dimensionally correct: for every two choices of units and all values of the quantities, it holds
for the numerical values in one choice of units exactly when it holds for the values converted to
the other. Here a choice of units assigns a positive magnitude to the unit of each of length, time,
mass, charge and temperature, and a value of dimension `L^l T^t M^m C^c Θ^θ` is multiplied by
`(ℓ₁/ℓ₂)^l (t₁/t₂)^t (m₁/m₂)^m (q₁/q₂)^c (θ₁/θ₂)^θ` when the units change from magnitudes
`ℓ₁, t₁, m₁, q₁, θ₁` to `ℓ₂, t₂, m₂, q₂, θ₂`.
