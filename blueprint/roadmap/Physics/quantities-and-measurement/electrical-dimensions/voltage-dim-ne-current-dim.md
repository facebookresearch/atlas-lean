# Voltage and current have different dimensions

Over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, the voltage dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\,\mathsf{C}^{-1}$ differs from the current dimension $\mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$; hence a sum $V + I$ of a voltage and a current has no dimension.
