# Permittivity times length is capacitance

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: $[\varepsilon]\,\mathsf{L} = [C]$, the permittivity dimension times length is the capacitance dimension.
