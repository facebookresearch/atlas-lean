# Power times time is energy in the charge-based system

Over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, the power dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}$ times $\mathsf{T}$ is the energy dimension: $[P]\,\mathsf{T} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$.
