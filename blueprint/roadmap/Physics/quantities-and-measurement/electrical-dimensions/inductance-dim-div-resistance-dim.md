# Inductance over resistance has the dimension of time

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: the inductance dimension divided by the resistance dimension is time, $[L]/[R] = \mathsf{T}$.
