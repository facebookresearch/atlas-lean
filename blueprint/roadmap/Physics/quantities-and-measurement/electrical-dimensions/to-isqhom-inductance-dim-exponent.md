# SI exponents of inductance

Let $\iota$ be the group homomorphism from dimensions over length, time, mass, charge and temperature to ISQ dimensions that sends length, time, mass and temperature to the ISQ base quantities of the same name and charge $\mathsf{C}$ to $\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$. The image under $\iota$ of the inductance dimension has the ISQ exponents: length $2$, mass $1$, time $-2$, current $-2$, temperature $0$, amount $0$, luminous intensity $0$; in SI base units, $\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-2}\,A^{-2}}$.
