# The wrong law P = V + I for power

A deliberately wrong law: a relation between a power `P`, a voltage `V` and a current `I` (values of
the [power](power-dim.md), [voltage](voltage-dim.md) and [current](current-dim.md) dimensions),
which holds when their numerical values satisfy `P = V + I`. It adds values of
[different dimensions](voltage-dim-ne-current-dim.md).
