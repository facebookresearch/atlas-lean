# Electrical dimension identities and charge as current times time

Over the charge-based base quantities length `L`, time `T`, mass `M`, electric charge `C` and
temperature `Θ`, the following hold together:

- voltage times current is power, `M L² T⁻³`;
- resistance times capacitance and inductance over resistance are both the time dimension `T`;
- conductance times resistance is the dimensionless dimension `1`;
- the permittivity dimension is `L⁻³ T² M⁻¹ C²` and the permeability dimension is `L M C⁻²`;
- under the embedding into the ISQ dimensions that reads a charge as electric current times time,
  the current dimension `C T⁻¹` is sent to the ISQ electric current, and the image of the charge
  dimension is the image of the current dimension times the image of the time dimension.

The dimensions are those of [voltage](voltage-dim.md), [current](current-dim.md),
[power](power-dim.md), [resistance](resistance-dim.md), [capacitance](capacitance-dim.md),
[inductance](inductance-dim.md), [conductance](conductance-dim.md),
[permittivity](permittivity-dim.md) and [permeability](permeability-dim.md).
