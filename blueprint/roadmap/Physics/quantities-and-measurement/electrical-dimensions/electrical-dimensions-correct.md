# Electrical dimension identities

Over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature, define current $\mathsf{C}\mathsf{T}^{-1}$, voltage $\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^2\mathsf{T}^{-2}\mathsf{C}^{-1}$, resistance $\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^2\mathsf{T}^{-1}\mathsf{C}^{-2}$, capacitance $\mathsf{C}^2\mathsf{T}^2\mathsf{M}^{-1}\mathsf{L}^{-2}$, inductance $\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^2\mathsf{C}^{-2}$, conductance as inverse resistance, permittivity as capacitance per length, permeability as inductance per length, and power $\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^2\mathsf{T}^{-3}$. Then

- $[\text{voltage}]\,[\text{current}] = [\text{power}]$;
- $[\text{resistance}]\,[\text{capacitance}] = \mathsf{T}$ and $[\text{inductance}]/[\text{resistance}] = \mathsf{T}$;
- $[\text{conductance}]\,[\text{resistance}] = 1$;
- $[\text{permittivity}] = \mathsf{L}^{-3}\mathsf{T}^{2}\mathsf{M}^{-1}\mathsf{C}^{2}$ and $[\text{permeability}] = \mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{C}^{-2}$;
- under the map to ISQ dimensions, current goes to the ISQ electric-current base dimension $\mathsf{I}$, and the image of charge equals the image of current times the image of time.
