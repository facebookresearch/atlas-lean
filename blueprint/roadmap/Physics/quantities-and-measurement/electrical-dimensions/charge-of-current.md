# Charge carried by a current over a time

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a current $I$ of dimension $\mathsf{C}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ and a time $t$ of dimension $\mathsf{T}$, the charge $Q = I\,t$ is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{C}$ whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates of $I$ and $t$.
