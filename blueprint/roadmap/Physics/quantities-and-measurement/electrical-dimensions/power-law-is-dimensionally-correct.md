# Electrical power law is dimensionally correct

A relation between quantities is dimensionally correct when, for all unit choices $u_1, u_2$ of the length, time, mass, charge and temperature units and all arguments, it holds of the coordinates exactly when it holds of the coordinates converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$, each quantity rescaled by the factor of its own dimension. Then the power law $P = V\,I$ is dimensionally correct.
