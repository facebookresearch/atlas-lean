# Time maps to the ISQ time base

Let $\iota$ be the group homomorphism from dimensions over length, time, mass, charge and temperature to ISQ dimensions that sends length, time, mass and temperature to the ISQ base quantities of the same name and charge $\mathsf{C}$ to $\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$. Then $\iota(\mathsf{T})$ is the ISQ base dimension of time.
