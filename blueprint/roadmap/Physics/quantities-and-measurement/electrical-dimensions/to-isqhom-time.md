# The ISQ image of time is the ISQ time dimension

The embedding of the charge-based dimensions into the ISQ dimensions reads a charge as electric
current times time: it keeps the length, mass and temperature exponents, adds the charge exponent to
the time exponent, takes the charge exponent as the electric-current exponent, and gives amount of
substance and luminous intensity the exponent `0`. Under it, the time dimension `T` is sent to the
ISQ time dimension.
