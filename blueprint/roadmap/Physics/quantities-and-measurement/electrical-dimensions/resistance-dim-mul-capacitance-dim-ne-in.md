# RC is not a frequency

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: $[R]\,[C] \neq \mathsf{T}^{-1}$, so a resistance times a capacitance does not have the dimension of a frequency.
