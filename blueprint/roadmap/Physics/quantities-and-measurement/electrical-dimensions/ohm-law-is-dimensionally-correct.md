# Ohm's law is dimensionally correct

[Ohm's law](ohm-law.md) `V = R I` between a voltage, a resistance and a current is dimensionally
correct: for every two choices of units and all values of the quantities, it holds for the numerical
values in one choice of units exactly when it holds for the values converted to the other. Here a
choice of units assigns a positive magnitude to the unit of each of length, time, mass, charge and
temperature, and a value of dimension `L^l T^t M^m C^c Θ^θ` is multiplied by
`(ℓ₁/ℓ₂)^l (t₁/t₂)^t (m₁/m₂)^m (q₁/q₂)^c (θ₁/θ₂)^θ` when the units change from magnitudes
`ℓ₁, t₁, m₁, q₁, θ₁` to `ℓ₂, t₂, m₂, q₂, θ₂`.
