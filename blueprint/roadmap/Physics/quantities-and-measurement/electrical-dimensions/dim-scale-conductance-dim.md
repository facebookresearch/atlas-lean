# Conductance values convert by the inverse resistance factor

For every two choices of units of length, time, mass, charge and temperature, the conversion factor
of the [conductance dimension](conductance-dim.md) is the inverse of the conversion factor of the
[resistance dimension](resistance-dim.md). Here a value of dimension `L^l T^t M^m C^c Θ^θ` is
multiplied by `(ℓ₁/ℓ₂)^l (t₁/t₂)^t (m₁/m₂)^m (q₁/q₂)^c (θ₁/θ₂)^θ` when the units change from
magnitudes `ℓ₁, t₁, m₁, q₁, θ₁` to `ℓ₂, t₂, m₂, q₂, θ₂`.
