# Conductance times resistance is dimensionless

Writing $[X]$ for the dimension of a quantity $X$ over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$: $[G]\,[R] = 1$, so the product of a conductance and a resistance is dimensionless.
