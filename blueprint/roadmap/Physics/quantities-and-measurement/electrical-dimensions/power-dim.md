# Dimension of power in the charge-based system

The dimension of power, over the base dimensions length $\mathsf{L}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, electric charge $\mathsf{C}$ and temperature $\Theta$, is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}$: the energy dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ per time.
