# Shaft power from metres to millimetres

Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the unit scale with magnitude $1$ at every ISQ base quantity and $\mathrm{mm}$ the one with magnitude $1/1000$ at length and $1$ elsewhere; $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)$ is the factor for converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $\mathrm{m}$ to $\mathrm{mm}$. Then $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([F]\,\mathsf{L})\cdot 3 = 3\,000\,000$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(\mathsf{T}^{-1})\cdot 4 = 4$, and $3\,000\,000\cdot 4 = \lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([P])\,(3\cdot 4)$.
