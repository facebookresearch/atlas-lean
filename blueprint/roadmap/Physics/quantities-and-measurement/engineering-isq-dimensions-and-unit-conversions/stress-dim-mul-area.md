# Stress times area is force

Writing $[X]$ for the ISQ dimension of a quantity $X$: $[\sigma]\,\mathsf{L}^{2} = [F]$, where $[\sigma] = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{-1}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ and $[F] = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$.

Equivalently, $[F]\,(\mathsf{L}^{2})^{-1} = [\sigma]$: force over area has the dimension of stress.
