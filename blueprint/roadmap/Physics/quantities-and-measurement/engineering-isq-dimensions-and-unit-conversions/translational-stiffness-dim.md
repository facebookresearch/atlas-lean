# Dimension of translational stiffness

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of translational stiffness is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2} / \mathsf{L} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$, a force per length.

The effective axial stiffness of a bar, a force divided by a length, has this dimension.
