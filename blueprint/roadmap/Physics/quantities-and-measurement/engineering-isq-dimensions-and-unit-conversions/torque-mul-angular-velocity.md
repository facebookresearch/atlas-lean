# Torque times angular velocity has the dimension of power

Writing $[X]$ for the ISQ dimension of a quantity $X$: $[F]\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1} = [P]$: a torque (a force times a lever arm) times an angular velocity, of dimension $\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ since plane angle is dimensionless, has the power dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}$.
