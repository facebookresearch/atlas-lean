# Power factor is the torque factor times the angular velocity factor

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. Then $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F]\,\mathsf{L})\,\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{T}^{-1}) = \lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([P])$.
