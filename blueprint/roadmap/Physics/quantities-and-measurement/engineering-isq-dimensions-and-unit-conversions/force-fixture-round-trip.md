# Force of 5 from metres to millimetres and back

Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the unit scale with magnitude $1$ at every ISQ base quantity and $\mathrm{mm}$ the one with magnitude $1/1000$ at length and $1$ elsewhere; $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)$ is the factor for converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $\mathrm{m}$ to $\mathrm{mm}$. For the force dimension, $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([F])\cdot 5 = 5000$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{mm}\to\mathrm{m}}([F])\,(\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([F])\cdot 5) = 5$.
