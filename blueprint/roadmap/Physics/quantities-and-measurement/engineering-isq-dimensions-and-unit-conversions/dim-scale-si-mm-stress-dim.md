# Stress factor from metres to millimetres

Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the unit scale with magnitude $1$ at every ISQ base quantity and $\mathrm{mm}$ the one with magnitude $1/1000$ at length and $1$ elsewhere; $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)$ is the factor for converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $\mathrm{m}$ to $\mathrm{mm}$. Then $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([\sigma]) = 1/1000$ for the stress dimension: $1\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-2}} = \tfrac{1}{1000}\ \mathrm{kg\,mm^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$.
