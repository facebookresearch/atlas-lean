# Normal stress computed in new units and converted back

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. For all real $F$ and $A$ with $A > 0$, $\lambda_{u_2\to u_1}([\sigma])\,\dfrac{\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F])\,F}{\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{L}^{2})\,A} = \dfrac{F}{A}$.
