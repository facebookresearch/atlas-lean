# Normal stress

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a force $F$ of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ and an area $A$ of dimension $\mathsf{L}^{2}$ with positive coordinate, the average normal stress $\sigma = F/A$, with $F$ the normal force component over the section, is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{-1}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ whose coordinate is the quotient of the coordinates.
