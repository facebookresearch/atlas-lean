# Mass flow rate formula commutes with unit changes

For ISQ unit scales $u_1, u_2$ (each assigning a positive magnitude to every base unit) and a dimension $d = \prod_b b^{d_b}$, let $s(d) = \prod_b \bigl(u_1(b)/u_2(b)\bigr)^{d_b} > 0$ be the factor converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $u_1$ to $u_2$, and $s'(d)$ the factor from $u_2$ back to $u_1$.

Write $\rho_{\mathrm d} = \mathsf{M\,L^{-3}}$, $\mathsf{V} = \mathsf{L\,T^{-1}}$, $\mathsf{A} = \mathsf{L^2}$ and $\dot{\mathsf M} = \mathsf{M\,T^{-1}}$ for the density, velocity, area and mass flow rate dimensions. For real $\rho$, $v$ and $A$ with $0 < A$,

$$\bigl(s(\rho_{\mathrm d})\,\rho\bigr)\bigl(s(\mathsf{V})\,v\bigr)\bigl(s(\mathsf{A})\,A\bigr) = s(\dot{\mathsf M})\,(\rho\, v\, A).$$
