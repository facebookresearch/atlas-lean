# Stress times area rescales as a force

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a stress $\sigma$ (dimension $[\sigma]$) and an area $A$ (dimension $\mathsf{L}^{2}$), let $\sigma A$ be their product, read as a force of dimension $[F] = [\sigma]\,\mathsf{L}^{2}$. Then the product of the rescaled quantities $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([\sigma])\,\sigma$ and $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{L}^{2})\,A$, read as a force, is the force with coordinate $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F])\,\sigma A$.
