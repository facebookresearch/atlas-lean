# Stress factor times area factor is the force factor

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. Then $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([\sigma])\,\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{L}^{2}) = \lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F])$ for the stress and force dimensions.

Equivalently, $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F])\,\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{L}^{2})^{-1} = \lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([\sigma])$: the stress factor is the force factor over the area factor.
