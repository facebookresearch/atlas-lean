# Dimension of molar flow rate

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of molar flow rate is $\mathsf{N}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$, an amount of substance per time.
