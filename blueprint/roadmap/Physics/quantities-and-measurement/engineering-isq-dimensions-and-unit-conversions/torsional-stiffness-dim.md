# Dimension of torsional stiffness

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of torsional stiffness is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}\cdot\mathsf{L} = \mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$, a force times a length; it is a torque per radian, plane angle being dimensionless.
