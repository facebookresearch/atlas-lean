# Exponents of the Faraday constant dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the Faraday constant dimension are: length $0$, mass $0$, time $1$, current $1$, temperature $0$, amount $-1$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{T}\,\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$.
