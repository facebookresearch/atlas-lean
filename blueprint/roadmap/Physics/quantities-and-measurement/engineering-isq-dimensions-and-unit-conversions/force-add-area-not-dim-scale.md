# Sum of a force and an area has no conversion factor

Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the unit scale with magnitude $1$ at every ISQ base quantity and $\mathrm{mm}$ the one with magnitude $1/1000$ at length and $1$ elsewhere; $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)$ is the factor for converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $\mathrm{m}$ to $\mathrm{mm}$. There is no ISQ dimension $d$ such that for all real $F$ and $A$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([F])\,F + \lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(\mathsf{L}^{2})\,A = \lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)\,(F + A)$.
