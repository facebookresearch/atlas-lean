# Twice the normal stress formula also commutes with a change of units

For unit scales $u_1, u_2$ on the seven ISQ base quantities (each assigns a positive magnitude $u_i(b)$ to every base quantity $b$), write $\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(d) = \prod_b (u_1(b)/u_2(b))^{d_b}$ for the positive factor by which a coordinate of dimension $d$ is multiplied when converted from $u_1$ to $u_2$. For all real $F$ and $A$ with $A > 0$, $\dfrac{2\,\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([F])\,F}{\lambda_{u_1\to u_2}(\mathsf{L}^{2})\,A} = \lambda_{u_1\to u_2}([\sigma])\,\dfrac{2F}{A}$; commuting with every change of units does not distinguish the stress formula $F/A$ from $2F/A$.
