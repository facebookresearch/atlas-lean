# Dimension of the Faraday constant

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the dimension of the Faraday constant is $(\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T})/\mathsf{N} = \mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}\,\mathsf{N}^{-1}$, the electric charge dimension $\mathsf{I}\,\mathsf{T}$ per amount of substance.
