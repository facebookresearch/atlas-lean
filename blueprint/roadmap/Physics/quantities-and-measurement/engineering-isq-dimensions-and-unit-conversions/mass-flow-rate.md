# Mass flow rate

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a mass density $\rho$ of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{-3}$, a normal velocity $v$ of dimension $\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ and an area $A$ of dimension $\mathsf{L}^{2}$ with positive coordinate, the mass flow rate $\dot m = \rho\,v\,A$ of a uniform (or section-averaged) normal mass flux is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates.
