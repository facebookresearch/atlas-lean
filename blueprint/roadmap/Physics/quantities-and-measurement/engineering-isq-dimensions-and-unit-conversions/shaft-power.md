# Shaft power

A quantity of dimension $d$ is a real coordinate tagged with $d$. For a torque $\tau$ of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$ (force times length) and an angular velocity $\omega$ of dimension $\mathsf{T}^{-1}$ about one axis, the shaft power $P = \tau\,\omega$ is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^{2}\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}$ whose coordinate is the product of the coordinates.
