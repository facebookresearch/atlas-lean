# Exponents of the mass flow rate dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the mass flow rate dimension are: length $0$, mass $1$, time $-1$, current $0$, temperature $0$, amount $0$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-1}$.
