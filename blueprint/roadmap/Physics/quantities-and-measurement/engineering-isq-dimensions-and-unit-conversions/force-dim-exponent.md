# Exponents of the force dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities (length $\mathsf{L}$, mass $\mathsf{M}$, time $\mathsf{T}$, electric current $\mathsf{I}$, thermodynamic temperature $\Theta$, amount of substance $\mathsf{N}$ and luminous intensity $\mathsf{J}$), the exponents of the force dimension are: length $1$, mass $1$, time $-2$, current $0$, temperature $0$, amount $0$, luminous intensity $0$. Thus the dimension is $\mathsf{L}\,\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{T}^{-2}$.
