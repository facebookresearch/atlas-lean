# Normal stress from metres to millimetres

Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the unit scale with magnitude $1$ at every ISQ base quantity and $\mathrm{mm}$ the one with magnitude $1/1000$ at length and $1$ elsewhere; $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(d)$ is the factor for converting a coordinate of dimension $d$ from $\mathrm{m}$ to $\mathrm{mm}$. Then $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([F])\cdot 5 = 5000$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}(\mathsf{L}^{2})\cdot 2 = 2\,000\,000$, and $5000/2\,000\,000 = \lambda_{\mathrm{m}\to\mathrm{mm}}([\sigma])\cdot\tfrac{5}{2}$.
