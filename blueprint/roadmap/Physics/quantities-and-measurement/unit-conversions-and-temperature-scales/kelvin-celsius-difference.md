# Temperature differences are the same in Celsius and kelvin

For all real Celsius readings `C` and differences `Δ`, the kelvin value of `C + Δ` minus the kelvin
value of `C` is `Δ` (the [Celsius-to-kelvin conversion](celsius-to-kelvin.md) adds `273.15` to
each). In the physical reading Celsius readings are at least `-273.15`; the identity holds for all
real readings.
