# Kelvin value of a Celsius reading

The Celsius-to-kelvin conversion adds the offset `273.15`: `T_K = T_C + 273.15`, for every real
reading `T_C`. In the physical reading `T_C ≥ -273.15`, so that `T_K ≥ 0`.

Read pointwise, it gives the kelvin value `t + 273.15` of a Celsius reading `t`, for every real `t`.
