# The factor of an inverse dimension is the inverse factor

Over a finite set of base quantities, let two choices of units give the unit of each base quantity
`b` the positive magnitudes `s₁(b)` and `s₂(b)`, and let `k(d) = ∏_b (s₁(b) / s₂(b))^(d_b)` be the
conversion factor of a dimension `d` from the first choice to the second, where `d_b` is the
rational exponent of `b` in `d`. For every dimension `d`, `k(d⁻¹) = k(d)⁻¹`.
