# Per-particle form of the Arrhenius factor

For an Avogadro constant `N_A > 0`, a Boltzmann constant `k_B > 0`, an energy `e` and a temperature
`T > 0`, the [Arrhenius factor](exp-scaled-energy.md) of the molar energy `N_A e`, the gas constant
`N_A k_B` and `T` is `exp (-(e / (k_B T)))`, computed from the values.
