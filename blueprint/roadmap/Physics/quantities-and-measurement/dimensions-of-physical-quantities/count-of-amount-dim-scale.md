# Converting amount and Avogadro constant together keeps the count

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For every Avogadro constant `N_A` and amount of substance `n`, the
[particle count](count-of-amount.md) of the converted values `k_A N_A` and `k_n n` is the count
`n N_A` itself, where `k_A` and `k_n` are the factors of the
[Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md) and of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md)
dimension. For example, from moles to millimoles `k_n = 1000` and `k_A = 1 / 1000`.
