# Converting the Avogadro constant and energy rescales molar energy

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For every Avogadro constant `N_A` and energy `e`, the
[molar energy](molar-energy.md) of the converted values `k(N⁻¹) N_A` and `k(M L² T⁻²) e` is
`E = N_A e` converted by the factor `k(M L² T⁻² N⁻¹)` of the [molar energy](molar-energy-dim.md)
dimension.
