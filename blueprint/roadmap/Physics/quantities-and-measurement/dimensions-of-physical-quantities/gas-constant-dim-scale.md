# Converting the Avogadro and Boltzmann constants rescales R

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For every Avogadro constant `N_A` and Boltzmann constant `k_B`, the
[molar gas constant](gas-constant.md) of the converted values `k(N⁻¹) N_A` and `k(M L² T⁻² Θ⁻¹) k_B`
is `R = N_A k_B` converted by the factor `k(M L² T⁻² N⁻¹ Θ⁻¹)` of the
[gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) dimension.
