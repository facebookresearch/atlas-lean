# Dimension of the molar gas constant

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimension of
the molar gas constant is `M L² T⁻² N⁻¹ Θ⁻¹`, an [energy](energy-dim.md) per
[amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) per [thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md).
