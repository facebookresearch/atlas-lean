# Dimension of the molar gas constant

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimension of
the molar gas constant is the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension divided by the product of the
[amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension `N` and the
[thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) dimension `Θ`, an energy per amount of substance per
temperature; its [exponents](gas-constant-dim-exponent.md) are those of `M L² T⁻² N⁻¹ Θ⁻¹`.
