# The projection to the charge-based basis forgets amount

The projection of the ISQ dimensions onto the charge-based dimensions (base quantities length, time,
mass, electric charge and temperature) keeps the length, mass and temperature exponents, takes the
electric-current exponent as the charge exponent, subtracts it from the time exponent, and drops
amount of substance and luminous intensity. It sends the
[amount-of-substance dimension](amount-dim.md) `N` to the dimensionless dimension `1`.
