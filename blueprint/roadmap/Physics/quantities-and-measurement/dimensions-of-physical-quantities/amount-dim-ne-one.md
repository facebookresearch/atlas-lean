# Amount of substance is not dimensionless

The [amount-of-substance dimension](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) `N` is
not the dimensionless dimension `1`.

In particular, for every Avogadro constant `N_A` and particle count `N`, the dimension carried by
the [amount of substance](amount-of-count.md) `N / N_A` is not `1`.
