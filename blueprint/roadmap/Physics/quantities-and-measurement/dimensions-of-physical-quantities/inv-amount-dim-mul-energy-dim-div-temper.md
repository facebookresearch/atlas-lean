# The gas-constant dimension is energy per amount per temperature

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the inverse `N⁻¹`
of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension
times the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension divided by the
[thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) dimension is the [gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md)
dimension: the dimension of `R = N_A k_B`.

Equivalently, the gas-constant dimension times the amount-of-substance dimension `N` times the
thermodynamic temperature dimension `Θ` is the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension.
