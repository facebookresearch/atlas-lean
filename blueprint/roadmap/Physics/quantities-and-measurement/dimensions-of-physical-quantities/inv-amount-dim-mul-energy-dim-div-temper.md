# The Avogadro and Boltzmann dimensions multiply to the gas-constant one

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the inverse `N⁻¹`
of the [amount of substance](amount-dim.md) dimension times the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension
divided by the [thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) dimension is the
[gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) dimension: the dimension of `R = N_A k_B`.
