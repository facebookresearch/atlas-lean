# Amount concentration times molar mass is a mass density

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[amount-concentration](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/concentration.md) dimension `N L⁻³` times the
[molar-mass](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/molar-mass.md) dimension `M N⁻¹` is the mass-density dimension `M L⁻³`.
