# Arrhenius factor of a molar energy at a positive temperature

For a molar energy `E` (a quantity of the [molar energy](molar-energy-dim.md) dimension), a molar
gas constant `R` (a quantity of the [gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) dimension) with positive
value and a [thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) `T` with positive value, the factor
`exp (-(E / (R T)))`. Its exponent is the quotient `E / (R T)` of quantities, whose dimension is the
dimensionless dimension `1`.
