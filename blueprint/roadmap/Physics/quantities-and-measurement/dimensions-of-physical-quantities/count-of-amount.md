# Particle count of an amount of substance

For an Avogadro constant `N_A` (a quantity of the [Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md),
the inverse of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md)
dimension) and an amount of substance `n` (a quantity of the
[amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension), the
particle count is `n N_A`, a quantity of the [dimensionless](dimensionless-dim.md) dimension `1`;
its value is the product of the values. In the physical reading `N_A > 0` and `n ≥ 0`.
