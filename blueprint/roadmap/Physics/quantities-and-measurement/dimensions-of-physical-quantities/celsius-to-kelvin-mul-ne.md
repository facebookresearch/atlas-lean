# Scaling a Celsius reading by c ≠ 1 does not scale its kelvin value

For every real Celsius reading `t` and every real `c ≠ 1`, the [kelvin value](../unit-conversions-and-temperature-scales/celsius-to-kelvin.md)
of `c t` differs from `c` times the kelvin value of `t`: `c t + 273.15 ≠ c (t + 273.15)`.
