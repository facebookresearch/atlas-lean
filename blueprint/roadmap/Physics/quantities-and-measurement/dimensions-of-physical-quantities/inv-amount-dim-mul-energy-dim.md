# Inverse amount times energy is molar energy

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the inverse `N⁻¹`
of the [amount of substance](amount-dim.md) dimension times the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension is
the [molar energy](molar-energy-dim.md) dimension: the dimension of `E = N_A e` for an energy `e`.
