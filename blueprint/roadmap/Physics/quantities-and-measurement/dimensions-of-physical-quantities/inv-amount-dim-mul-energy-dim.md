# Molar energy is energy per amount of substance

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the inverse `N⁻¹`
of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension
times the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension is the
[molar energy](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/chemical-potential.md) dimension: the
dimension of `E = N_A e` for an energy `e`.

Equivalently, the molar-energy dimension times the amount-of-substance dimension `N` is the
[energy](energy-dim.md) dimension.
