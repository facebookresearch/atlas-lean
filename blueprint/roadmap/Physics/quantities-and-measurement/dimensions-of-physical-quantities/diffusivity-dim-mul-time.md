# Diffusivity times time is an area

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[diffusivity](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/diffusivity.md) dimension times the time dimension is the area dimension `L²`.
