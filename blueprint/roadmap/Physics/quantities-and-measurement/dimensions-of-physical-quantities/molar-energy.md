# Molar energy of a per-particle energy

For an Avogadro constant `N_A` (a quantity of the [Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md),
the inverse of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension) and a per-particle energy `e` (a
quantity of the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension), the molar energy `E = N_A e`, a quantity of the
[molar energy](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/chemical-potential.md) dimension; its value is the product of the values. In the
physical reading `N_A > 0`.
