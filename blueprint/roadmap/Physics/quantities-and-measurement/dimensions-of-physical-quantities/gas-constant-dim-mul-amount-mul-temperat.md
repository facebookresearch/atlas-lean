# Gas constant times amount times temperature is energy

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) dimension times the [amount of substance](amount-dim.md)
dimension `N` times the [thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) dimension `Θ` is the
[energy](energy-dim.md) dimension.
