# Amount of substance of a particle count

For an Avogadro constant `N_A` (a quantity of the [Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md),
the inverse of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md)
dimension) and a particle count `N` (a quantity of the [dimensionless](dimensionless-dim.md)
dimension `1`), the amount of substance is `n = N / N_A`, a quantity of the
[amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension; its value
is the quotient of the values. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, it is `0` when `N_A = 0`. In the
physical reading `N_A > 0` and `N ≥ 0`.
