# Converting count and Avogadro constant rescales the amount

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For an Avogadro constant `N_A > 0` and a particle count `N`, the
[amount of substance](amount-of-count.md) of the converted values `k_A N_A` and `k(1) N` is the
amount `N / N_A` converted by `k_n`, where `k_A` and `k_n` are the factors of the
[Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md) and of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md)
dimension, and `k(1)` that of the dimensionless dimension.
