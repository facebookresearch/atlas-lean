# Molar energy over gas constant times temperature is dimensionless

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[molar energy](molar-energy-dim.md) dimension divided by the product of the
[gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) and [thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) dimensions
is the dimensionless dimension `1`: the exponent `E / (R T)` is dimensionless.
