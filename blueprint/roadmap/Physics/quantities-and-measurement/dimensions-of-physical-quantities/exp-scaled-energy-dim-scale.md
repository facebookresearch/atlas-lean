# The Arrhenius factor does not depend on the units

For two choices of units `u₁` and `u₂` (each assigning a positive magnitude to the unit of each of
the seven ISQ base quantities), let `k(d)` be the
[unit conversion factor](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/unit-factor.md) of a
dimension `d` from `u₁` to `u₂`. For a molar energy `E`, a gas constant `R > 0` and a temperature
`T > 0`, the [Arrhenius factor](exp-scaled-energy.md) of the converted values `k(M L² T⁻² N⁻¹) E`,
`k(M L² T⁻² N⁻¹ Θ⁻¹) R` and `k(Θ) T` equals that of `E`, `R` and `T`: the factors cancel in the
dimensionless exponent.
