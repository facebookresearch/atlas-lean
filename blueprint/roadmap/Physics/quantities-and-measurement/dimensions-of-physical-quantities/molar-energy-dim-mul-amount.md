# Molar energy times amount of substance is energy

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the
[molar energy](molar-energy-dim.md) dimension times the [amount of substance](amount-dim.md)
dimension `N` is the [energy](energy-dim.md) dimension.
