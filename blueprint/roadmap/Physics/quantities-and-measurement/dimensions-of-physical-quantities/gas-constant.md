# Molar gas constant as Avogadro times Boltzmann constant

For an Avogadro constant `N_A` (a quantity of the [Avogadro-constant dimension](avogadro-dim.md),
the inverse of the [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md) dimension) and a Boltzmann constant `k_B` (a
quantity of the [Boltzmann-constant dimension](boltzmann-dim.md) `M L² T⁻² Θ⁻¹`), the molar gas
constant `R = N_A k_B`, a quantity of the [gas-constant](gas-constant-dim.md) dimension; its value
is the product of the values. In the physical reading `N_A > 0` and `k_B > 0`.
