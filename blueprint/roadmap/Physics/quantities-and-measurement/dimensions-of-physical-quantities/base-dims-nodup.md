# Amount, length, mass, time and temperature dimensions are distinct

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimensions
`N`, `L`, `M`, `T` and `Θ` of [amount of substance](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/amount.md), length, mass, time and
[thermodynamic temperature](temperature-dim.md) are pairwise distinct.
