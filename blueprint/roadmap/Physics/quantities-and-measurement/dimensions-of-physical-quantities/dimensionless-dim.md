# The dimensionless dimension

Over the seven ISQ base quantities length `L`, mass `M`, time `T`, electric current `I`,
thermodynamic temperature `Θ`, amount of substance `N` and luminous intensity `J`, the dimensionless
dimension is `1`; its
[exponent vector](../quantity-calculus-and-dimensionless-groups/exponent-vector-one.md) is zero,
with exponent `0` at every base quantity.
