# Exponents of the pressure and stress dimension

Pressure has exponents `-1` at length, `1` at mass and `-2` at time, and `0` elsewhere.

As an exponent vector in `ℚ⁷`, in the order length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic
temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity, it is `(-1, 1, -2, 0, 0, 0, 0)`; at the seven
ISQ base quantities, the exponents are `-1` at length, `1` at mass, `-2` at time and `0` at electric
current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance and luminous intensity; this is also the
dimension of stress.
