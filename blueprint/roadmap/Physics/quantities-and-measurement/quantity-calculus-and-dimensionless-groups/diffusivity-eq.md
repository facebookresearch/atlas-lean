# Exponents of the diffusivity dimension

Diffusivity has exponents `2` at length and `-1` at time, and `0` elsewhere.

As an exponent vector in `ℚ⁷`, in the order length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic
temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity, it is `(2, 0, -1, 0, 0, 0, 0)`; at the seven
ISQ base quantities, the exponents are `2` at length, `-1` at time and `0` at mass, electric
current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance and luminous intensity.
