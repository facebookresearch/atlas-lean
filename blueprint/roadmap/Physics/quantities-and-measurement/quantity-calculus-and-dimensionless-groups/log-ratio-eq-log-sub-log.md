# The logarithm of a ratio is a difference of logarithms

For positive values `x` and `x₀` of one dimension, the [logarithm of their ratio](log-ratio.md) is
the difference `ln x - ln x₀` of the logarithms of their numerical values.

This holds for both forms of the [logarithm of the ratio](log-ratio.md): of the quotient of the
values, or of the value of the dimensionless quotient quantity `x / x₀`.
