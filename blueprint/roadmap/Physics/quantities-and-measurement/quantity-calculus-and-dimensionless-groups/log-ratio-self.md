# The log-ratio of a value to itself is zero

For a positive value `x`, the [logarithm of the ratio](log-ratio.md) of `x` to itself is `0`. Equivalently, for quantities $x, x_0$ of the same dimension with positive coordinates and coordinate ratio $x/x_0 = 1$, the logarithm of their ratio is $0$, since a ratio of positive coordinates equal to one makes the two quantities equal.
