# Exponents of the amount-concentration dimension

Concentration has exponent `-3` at length and `1` at amount of substance, and `0` elsewhere.

As an exponent vector in `ℚ⁷`, in the order length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic
temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity, it is `(-3, 0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 0)`; at the seven
ISQ base quantities, the exponents are `-3` at length, `1` at amount of substance and `0` at mass,
time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature and luminous intensity.
