# Exponents of the chemical-potential and molar-energy dimension

Chemical potential has exponents `2` at length, `1` at mass, `-2` at time and `-1` at amount of
substance, and `0` elsewhere.

As an exponent vector in `ℚ⁷`, in the order length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic
temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity, it is `(2, 1, -2, 0, 0, -1, 0)`; at the seven
ISQ base quantities, the exponents are `2` at length, `1` at mass, `-2` at time, `-1` at amount of
substance and `0` at electric current, thermodynamic temperature and luminous intensity; this is
also the dimension of molar energy.
