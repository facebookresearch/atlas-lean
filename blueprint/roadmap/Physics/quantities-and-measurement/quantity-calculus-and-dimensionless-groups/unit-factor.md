# Unit conversion factor of a dimension

The factor by which the numerical value of a quantity of dimension `d` is multiplied under a change
of unit choices from `u₁` to `u₂`: `∏_b (s₁(b) / s₂(b)) ^ (d_b)`, where `b` runs over the seven ISQ
base quantities, `s₁(b) > 0` and `s₂(b) > 0` are the magnitudes of the units chosen for `b`, and
`d_b` is the exponent of `b` in `d`.
