# Log-ratios do not depend on the units

The logarithm of the ratio of two positive values of the same dimension does not depend on the
units: a change of units multiplies both values by the same positive unit factor.

With the [unit conversion factor](unit-factor.md) `k(d)` of the dimension `d` from units `u₁` to
`u₂`: for quantities `x` and `x₀` of dimension `d` with positive values, the logarithm of the ratio
of the converted quantities, of values `k(d) x` and `k(d) x₀`, equals that of `x` and `x₀`. This
holds for both forms of the [logarithm of the ratio](log-ratio.md), and whether the converted
quantities are written as multiples of `x` and `x₀` or as quantities with the converted values.
