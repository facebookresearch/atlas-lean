# Logarithm of a ratio of like quantities

The natural logarithm `ln (x / x₀)` of the ratio of a positive value `x` to a positive reference
value `x₀` of the same dimension `d`, such as `ln (c / c°)` for a concentration `c` and a standard
concentration `c°`. It is defined only for two positive values of one dimension, so there is no
logarithm of a single dimensioned value.

Equivalently, for two quantities `x` and `x₀` of dimension `d` with positive values, it is the
natural logarithm of the value of the quotient `x / x₀` formed as a quantity, whose dimension
`d d⁻¹` is the dimensionless dimension `1`: only a dimensionless value enters the logarithm.
