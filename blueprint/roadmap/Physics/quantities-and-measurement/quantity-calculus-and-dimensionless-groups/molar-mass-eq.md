# Exponents of the molar-mass dimension

Molar mass has exponent `1` at mass and `-1` at amount of substance, and `0` elsewhere.

As an exponent vector in `ℚ⁷`, in the order length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic
temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity, it is `(0, 1, 0, 0, 0, -1, 0)`; at the seven
ISQ base quantities, the exponents are `1` at mass, `-1` at amount of substance and `0` at length,
time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature and luminous intensity.
