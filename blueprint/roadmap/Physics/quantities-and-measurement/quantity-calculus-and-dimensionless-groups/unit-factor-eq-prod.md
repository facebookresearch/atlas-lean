# The unit factor as a product over base quantities

The unit factor of a dimension `d` from unit choices `u₁` to `u₂` is the product over the seven ISQ
base quantities `b` of `(s₁(b) / s₂(b)) ^ (d_b)`, with `s₁(b)`, `s₂(b)` the magnitudes of the units
chosen for `b` and `d_b` the exponent of `b` in `d`.
