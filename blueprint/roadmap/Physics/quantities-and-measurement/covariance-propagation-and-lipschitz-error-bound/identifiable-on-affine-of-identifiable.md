# An identifiable matrix is identifiable on every affine parameter space

If $A$ is a real $m \times n$ matrix such that the calibration model $y = A\theta$ is
[identifiable](identifiable.md) ($\theta \mapsto A\theta$ injective on $\mathbb{R}^n$), then $A$
is [identifiable on](identifiable-on-affine.md) every affine subspace $S$ of $\mathbb{R}^n$:
injectivity on the whole space restricts to $S$.
