# Identifiability of $A$ on an affine parameter space $S$

For a real $m \times n$ matrix $A$ and an affine subspace $S$ of $\mathbb{R}^n$ (the declared
parameter space), $A$ is identifiable on $S$ when $\theta \mapsto A\theta$ is injective on $S$:

$$\theta_1, \theta_2 \in S \ \text{ and } \ A\theta_1 = A\theta_2 \implies \theta_1 = \theta_2 .$$

For the empty affine subspace the condition holds vacuously. On the whole space
$S = \mathbb{R}^n$ it is [identifiability](identifiable.md) of $A$
([whole-space case](identifiable-on-affine-top-iff-identifia.md)), and it holds on every $S$ when
$A$ is identifiable ([restriction](identifiable-on-affine-of-identifiable.md)).
