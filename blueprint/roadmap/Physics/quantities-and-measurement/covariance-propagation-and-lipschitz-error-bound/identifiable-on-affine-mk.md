# Identifiability on $\theta_0 + V$ via differences from $\theta_0$

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix, $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $V$ a linear subspace of
$\mathbb{R}^n$, and let $\theta_0 + V = \{\theta \in \mathbb{R}^n : \theta - \theta_0 \in V\}$ be
the affine subspace through $\theta_0$ with direction $V$. Then $A$ is
[identifiable on](identifiable-on-affine.md) $\theta_0 + V$ if and only if, for all
$\theta_1, \theta_2 \in \mathbb{R}^n$,

$$\theta_1 - \theta_0 \in V, \quad \theta_2 - \theta_0 \in V, \quad A\theta_1 = A\theta_2
\implies \theta_1 = \theta_2 .$$
