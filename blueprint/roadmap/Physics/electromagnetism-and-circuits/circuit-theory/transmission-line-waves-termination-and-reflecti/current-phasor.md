# Current phasor of forward and backward waves

For complex amplitudes `A₊` and `A₋` and real `Z₀`, `β` and `x`, the current phasor at position
`x` is

`I(x) = (A₊ e^{−iβx} − A₋ e^{iβx}) / Z₀`,

the forward wave minus the backward wave, divided by the characteristic impedance. The intended
domain is `Z₀ > 0`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, the current phasor is `0` when `Z₀ = 0`.
