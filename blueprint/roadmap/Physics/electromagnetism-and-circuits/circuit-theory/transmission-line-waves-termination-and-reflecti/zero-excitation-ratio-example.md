# Without excitation the divided reflection formula fails

In the complex numbers, `0 / 0 ≠ (3 − 1) / (3 + 1)`: with the convention `x / 0 = 0` the left side
is `0` and the right side is `1/2`. So with both amplitudes zero, a unit characteristic impedance
and the load `3`, the amplitude ratio `A₋ / A₊` is not the impedance ratio, although the
[division-free relation](load-multiplicative-relation.md) still holds; the
[divided formula](load-reflection-ratio-of-ne-zero.md) needs `A₊ ≠ 0`.
