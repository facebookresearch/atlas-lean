# Nonzero forward excitation forces a nonzero load sum

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊`, `A₋` and `Z_L` be complex numbers satisfying the
load-plane relations `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀` and the load law `V = Z_L I`, and suppose
`A₊ ≠ 0`. Then `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`: if `Z_L + Z₀ = 0`, the
[division-free relation](load-multiplicative-relation.md) gives `0 = −2 Z₀ A₊`, so `A₊ = 0`.
