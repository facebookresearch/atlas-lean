# Average load power of RMS phasors from the load law

Let `V`, `I` and `Z_L` be complex with `V = Z_L I`. Then the average over any complete period
`[a, a + 2π/ω]`, for `ω > 0` and any real `a`, of the product of the
[RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) `√2 Re(V e^{iωt})` and
`√2 Re(I e^{iωt})` is

`Re Z_L |I|²`.
