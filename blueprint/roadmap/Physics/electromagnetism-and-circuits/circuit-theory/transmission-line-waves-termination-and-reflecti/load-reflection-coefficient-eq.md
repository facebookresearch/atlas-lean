# The amplitude ratio is the reflection coefficient of the load

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊`, `A₋` and `Z_L` be complex numbers satisfying the
load-plane relations `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀` and the load law `V = Z_L I`, and suppose
`A₊ ≠ 0`. Then the amplitude ratio `A₋ / A₊` is the
[reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) `Γ = (Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)`.
