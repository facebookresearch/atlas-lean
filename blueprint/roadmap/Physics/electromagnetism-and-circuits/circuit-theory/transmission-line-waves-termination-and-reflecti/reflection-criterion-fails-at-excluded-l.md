# At Z_L = −Z₀ the passivity criterion fails

For `Z₀ > 0`, the load `Z_L = −Z₀` lies outside the domain of the reflection ratio: with the
convention `x / 0 = 0` its [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is `0`, so
`|Γ| ≤ 1`, while `Re Z_L = −Z₀ < 0`. The [passivity criterion](passive-load-reflection.md) thus
needs `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`.
