# Passivity criterion for the reflection coefficient

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and `Z_L` a complex load impedance with `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`. Then the
[reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) `Γ = (Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)` satisfies
`|Γ| ≤ 1` exactly when `Re Z_L ≥ 0`.

Written with the ratio itself, `|(Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)| ≤ 1` exactly when `Re Z_L ≥ 0`: the load
is passive exactly when its reflection ratio has modulus at most one.
