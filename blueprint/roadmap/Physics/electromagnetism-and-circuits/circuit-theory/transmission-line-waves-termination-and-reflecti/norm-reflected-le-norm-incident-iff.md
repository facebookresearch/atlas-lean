# The reflected amplitude is at most the incident one iff Re Z_L ≥ 0

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊` and `A₋` be complex numbers with `V = A₊ + A₋` and
`I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀`, the load law `V = Z_L I` for a complex `Z_L`, and a nonzero incident amplitude
`A₊ ≠ 0`. Then `|A₋| ≤ |A₊|` exactly when `Re Z_L ≥ 0`. These hypotheses already force
[`Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`](load-sum-ne-zero.md).
