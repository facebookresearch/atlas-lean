# Ratio and wave-amplitude forms of the passivity criterion

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and `Z_L` a complex load impedance with `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`. Then:

- `|(Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)| ≤ 1` exactly when `Re Z_L ≥ 0`;
- for all complex `V`, `I`, `A₊` and `A₋` with `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀`, `V = Z_L I`
  and `A₊ ≠ 0`, `|A₋| ≤ |A₊|` exactly when `Re Z_L ≥ 0`.

The guard `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0` is needed by the ratio form only: it
[fails](reflection-criterion-fails-at-excluded-l.md) at `Z_L = −Z₀`.
