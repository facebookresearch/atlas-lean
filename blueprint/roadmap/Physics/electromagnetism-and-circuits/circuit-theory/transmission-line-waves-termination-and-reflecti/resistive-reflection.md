# Reflection coefficient of a resistive load or a refractive interface

For real numbers `R` and `Z₀`, the reflection coefficient of a resistive load `R` on a line of
characteristic impedance `Z₀` is the real number

`Γ_R = (R − Z₀) / (R + Z₀)`.

The intended domain is `Z₀ > 0` and `R + Z₀ ≠ 0`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `Γ_R = 0` at
`R = −Z₀`.

Read with the refractive indices `n₁` and `n₂` of two media in place of `R` and `Z₀`, the same
number `(n₁ − n₂) / (n₁ + n₂)` is the amplitude reflection coefficient at normal incidence of the
declared interface model, the Fresnel coefficient that accompanies the
[transmission coefficient](../../fields-and-waves/electrostatics-plane-waves-and-fresnel-coefficie/fresnel-t.md)
`2 n₁ / (n₁ + n₂)`. In that reading `n₁, n₂ > 0`, and the value is `0` when `n₁ + n₂ = 0`.
