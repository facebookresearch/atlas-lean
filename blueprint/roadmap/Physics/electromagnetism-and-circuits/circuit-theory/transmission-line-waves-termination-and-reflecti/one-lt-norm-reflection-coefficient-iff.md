# The reflection coefficient exceeds one in modulus iff Re Z_L < 0

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and `Z_L` a complex load impedance with `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`. Then the
[reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) `Γ = (Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)` satisfies
`|Γ| > 1` exactly when `Re Z_L < 0`: active loads reflect more than they receive.
