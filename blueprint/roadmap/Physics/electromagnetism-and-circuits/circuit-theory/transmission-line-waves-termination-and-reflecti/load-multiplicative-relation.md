# Division-free reflection relation at the load

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊`, `A₋` and `Z_L` be complex numbers satisfying the
load-plane relations `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀` and the load law `V = Z_L I`. Then

`(Z_L + Z₀) A₋ = (Z_L − Z₀) A₊`,

with no division by a wave amplitude or by `Z_L + Z₀`.
