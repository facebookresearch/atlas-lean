# Instantaneous reflection at a resistive load

Let `Z₀ > 0` and `R` be real with `R + Z₀ ≠ 0`, and let `v₊`, `v₋`, `v` and `i` be real functions
of time such that at every time `t`

`v(t) = v₊(t) + v₋(t)`, `i(t) = (v₊(t) − v₋(t)) / Z₀` and `v(t) = R i(t)`.

Then `v₋(t) = Γ_R v₊(t)` at every time `t`, with the
[resistive reflection coefficient](resistive-reflection.md) `Γ_R = (R − Z₀) / (R + Z₀)`.
