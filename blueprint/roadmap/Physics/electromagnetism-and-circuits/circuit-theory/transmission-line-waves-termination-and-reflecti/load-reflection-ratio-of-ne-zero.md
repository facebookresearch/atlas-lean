# Reflection ratio at the load under nonzero excitation alone

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊`, `A₋` and `Z_L` be complex numbers satisfying the
load-plane relations `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀` and the load law `V = Z_L I`, and suppose
`A₊ ≠ 0`. Then

`A₋ / A₊ = (Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)`,

the [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) of the load; the denominator is
[nonzero](load-sum-ne-zero.md).
