# Load power from the load impedance and the current

For complex `V`, `I` and `Z_L` with the load law `V = Z_L I`,

`Re(V Ī) = Re Z_L |I|²`,

with `Ī` the complex conjugate of `I`. It is the time-averaged load power when `V` and `I` are RMS
phasors ([average](average-rms-waveform-load-power-eq-re-mu.md)); when they are peak phasors the
time-averaged power is half of it ([average](average-peak-waveform-load-power-eq-re-m.md)).
