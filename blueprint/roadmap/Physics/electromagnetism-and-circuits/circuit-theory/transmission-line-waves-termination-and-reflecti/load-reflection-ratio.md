# Reflection ratio at the load under a denominator guard

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊`, `A₋` and `Z_L` be complex numbers satisfying the
load-plane relations `V = A₊ + A₋`, `I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀` and the load law `V = Z_L I`, and suppose
`A₊ ≠ 0` and `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0`. Then

`A₋ / A₊ = (Z_L − Z₀) / (Z_L + Z₀)`.

The guard `Z_L + Z₀ ≠ 0` [follows](load-sum-ne-zero.md) from the other hypotheses, so the
[same ratio holds without it](load-reflection-ratio-of-ne-zero.md).
