# Phase constant of a lossless line

For real numbers `L`, `C` and `ω`, the phase constant is

`β = ω √(L C)`,

the real wavenumber of the phasors `e^{−iβx}` and `e^{iβx}`; on a lossless line the complex
propagation constant is `iβ`. The intended domain is `L, C > 0`; with the convention `√y = 0` for
`y < 0`, `β = 0` when `L C < 0`.
