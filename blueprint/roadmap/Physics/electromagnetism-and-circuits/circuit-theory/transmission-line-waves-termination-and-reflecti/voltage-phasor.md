# Voltage phasor of forward and backward waves

For complex amplitudes `A₊` and `A₋` and real `β` and `x`, the voltage phasor at position `x` is

`V(x) = A₊ e^{−iβx} + A₋ e^{iβx}`,

the superposition of a forward wave of amplitude `A₊` and a backward wave of amplitude `A₋`, with
the time convention `e^{+iωt}`.
