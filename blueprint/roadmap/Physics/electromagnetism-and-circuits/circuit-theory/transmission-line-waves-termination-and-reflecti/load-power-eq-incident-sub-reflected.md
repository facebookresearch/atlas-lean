# Load power from the incident and reflected amplitudes

Let `Z₀ > 0` be real and let `V`, `I`, `A₊` and `A₋` be complex numbers with `V = A₊ + A₋` and
`I = (A₊ − A₋) / Z₀`. Then the real part of the complex power at the load plane is

`Re(V Ī) = (|A₊|² − |A₋|²) / Z₀`,

with `Ī` the complex conjugate of `I`. It is the time-averaged load power when `V` and `I` are RMS
phasors ([average](average-rms-waveform-load-power-eq-incid.md)); when they are peak phasors the
time-averaged power is half of it ([average](average-peak-waveform-load-power-eq-inci.md)).
