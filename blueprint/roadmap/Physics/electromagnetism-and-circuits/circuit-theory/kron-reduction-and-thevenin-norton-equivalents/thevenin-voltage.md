# Thevenin voltage of a one-port

For real numbers `K` and `j_eff`, the Thevenin (open-circuit) voltage of the one-port law
`K V = j_eff − I`, with port conductance `K`, effective injected current `j_eff`, port voltage `V`
and load current `I` flowing out of the port is

`V_th = j_eff / K`.

The intended domain is `K > 0`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `V_th = 0` at `K = 0`.
