# On short circuit the load current is the Norton current

Let `K > 0` and let `j_eff`, `V` and `I` be real numbers satisfying the one-port law
`K V = j_eff − I`, with port conductance `K`, effective injected current `j_eff`, port voltage `V`
and load current `I` flowing out of the port and the short-circuit law `V = 0 · I`. Then `V = 0` and
`I = j_eff`.
