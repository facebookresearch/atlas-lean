# Worked example of a one-port with a resistive load

For the one-port law with `K = 2` and `j_eff = 4`, and the load `R_L = 3/2`: if `2 V = 4 − I` and
`V = (3/2) I`, then the [Thevenin voltage](thevenin-voltage.md) is `V_th = 2`, the
[Thevenin resistance](thevenin-resistance.md) is `R_th = 1/2`, and `V = 3/2` and `I = 1`.
