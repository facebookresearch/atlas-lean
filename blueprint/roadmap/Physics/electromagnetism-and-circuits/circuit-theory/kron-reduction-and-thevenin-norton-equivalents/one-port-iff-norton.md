# The one-port law is the Norton form

For all real `K`, `j_eff`, `V` and `I`, the one-port law `K V = j_eff − I`, with port conductance
`K`, effective injected current `j_eff`, port voltage `V` and load current `I` flowing out of the
port, holds exactly when the Norton form `I = j_eff − K V` holds: the load current is the Norton
current `j_eff` minus `K V`.
