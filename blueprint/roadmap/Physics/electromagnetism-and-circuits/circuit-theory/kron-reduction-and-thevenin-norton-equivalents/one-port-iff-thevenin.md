# For K > 0 the one-port law is the Thevenin form

Let `K > 0` and let `j_eff`, `V` and `I` be real. Then the one-port law `K V = j_eff − I`, with port
conductance `K`, effective injected current `j_eff`, port voltage `V` and load current `I` flowing
out of the port, holds exactly when the Thevenin form

`V = V_th − R_th I`

holds, with the [Thevenin voltage](thevenin-voltage.md) `V_th = j_eff / K` and the
[Thevenin resistance](thevenin-resistance.md) `R_th = 1 / K`.
