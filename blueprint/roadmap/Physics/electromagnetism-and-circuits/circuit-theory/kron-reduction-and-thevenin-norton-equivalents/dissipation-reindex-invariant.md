# Dissipation does not depend on the labelling of the nodes

Let `m`, `p` and `α` be finite index sets, `e : α → m ⊔ p` a bijection, `G` a real square matrix
indexed by `m ⊔ p`, and `φ`, `j` vectors on `m ⊔ p` with `G φ = j`. Then the relabelled matrix
`G'(a, b) = G(e(a), e(b))` and the relabelled potentials `φ' = φ ∘ e` satisfy
`φ' · (G' φ') = φ · j`. This concerns the full quadratic form; for relabelling the interior nodes of
the reduced network, see
[Kron reduction under relabelling of the interior nodes](kron-reduction-reindex-interior.md).
