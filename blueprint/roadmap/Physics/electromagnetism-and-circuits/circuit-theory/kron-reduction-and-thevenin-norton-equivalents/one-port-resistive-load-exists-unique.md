# A resistive load has exactly one operating point

Let `K > 0`, `R_L ≥ 0` and `j_eff` be real. Then there is exactly one pair `(V, I)` of real numbers
satisfying both the one-port law `K V = j_eff − I`, with port conductance `K`, effective injected
current `j_eff`, port voltage `V` and load current `I` flowing out of the port and the load law
`V = R_L I`.
