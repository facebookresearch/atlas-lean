# Nodal current balance splits over the classes of branches

For a real `n × m` matrix `B`, sets `R`, `L`, `C`, `S` forming a
[branch partition](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/is-branch-partition.md) and every vector `i` of
branch currents, `B i = B_C i_C + B_R i_R + B_L i_L + B_S i_S`, where `B_X` is the submatrix of the
columns in `X` and `i_X` the [restriction](restrict-vector.md) of `i` to `X`.
