# Hypotheses for stamping a linear network

For finitely many branches, sets `R`, `L`, `C` and `S` of branches and values `(R_e)`, `(L_e)` and
`(C_e)`, the stamping hypotheses hold when `R`, `L`, `C` and `S` form a
[branch partition](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/is-branch-partition.md) (pairwise disjoint,
covering every branch) and `R_e > 0` for `e ∈ R`, `C_e > 0` for `e ∈ C` and `L_e > 0` for `e ∈ L`.
The branches of `S` are independent voltage sources; no current-source branch occurs.
