# Branch equations give the stamped equations

Assume the [stamping hypotheses](satisfies-stamping-hypotheses.md), node potentials `v` differentiable
on `ℝ`, and branch voltages and currents `v_b`, `i_b` satisfying the
[branch equations](satisfies-branch-equations.md) whose inductor currents `t ↦ (i_b(t)_e)_{e ∈ L}` are
differentiable on `ℝ`. Then the [stamped equations](satisfies-stamped-equations.md) hold with the
inductor currents `i_L(t) = (i_b(t)_e)_{e ∈ L}` and the voltage-source currents
`i_V(t) = (i_b(t)_e)_{e ∈ S}`.
