# Ohm's law for sinusoids is the phasor equation V = R I

Let `R ≥ 0`. Let `ω > 0`, let `V` and `I` be complex phasors, and let `v` and `i` be the real
voltage and current whose values at every time `t` are the
[RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) `v(t) = √2 Re(V e^{iωt})`
and `i(t) = √2 Re(I e^{iωt})`. Then Ohm's law `v(t) = R i(t)` holds at every time `t` exactly when
the phasors satisfy `V = R I`.
