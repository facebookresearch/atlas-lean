# A capacitor's current leads its voltage by a quarter period

Let `C > 0` and `ω > 0`, and let `V` and `I` be complex phasors with `I = iωC V`. Then for every
time `t` the [RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) satisfy

`w_I(t) = ωC w_V(t + π / (2ω))`,

where `w_I(t) = √2 Re(I e^{iωt})`: the current waveform is `ωC` times the voltage waveform
advanced by a quarter period.
