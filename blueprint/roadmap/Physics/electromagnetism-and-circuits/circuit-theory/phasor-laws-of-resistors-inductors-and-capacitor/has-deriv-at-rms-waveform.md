# Differentiating an RMS waveform multiplies its phasor by iω

For a complex phasor `V` and real `ω` and `t`, the
[RMS waveform](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) `w_V(s) = √2 Re(V e^{iωs})`
has at `t` the derivative

`w_V'(t) = w_{iωV}(t) = √2 Re(iωV e^{iωt})`,

the RMS waveform of the phasor `iωV`, with the convention `e^{+iωt}`.
