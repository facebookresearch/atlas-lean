# Phasor element laws of resistors, inductors and capacitors

Let `R ≥ 0`, `L > 0` and `C > 0`. Let `ω > 0`, let `V` and `I` be complex phasors, and let `v` and
`i` be the real voltage and current whose values at every time `t` are the
[RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) `v(t) = √2 Re(V e^{iωt})`
and `i(t) = √2 Re(I e^{iωt})`. Then, with the convention `e^{+iωt}`:

- the resistor law `v = R i` at every time is equivalent to `V = R I`;
- the inductor law `v = L i'` at every time is equivalent to `V = iωL I`;
- the capacitor law `i = C v'` at every time is equivalent to `I = iωC V`.
