# At zero frequency the inductor admittance form fails

For every real `L`, the equivalence of `V = iωL I` and `I = V / (iωL)` fails at `ω = 0`: it is
not true that for all complex `V` and `I`, `V = i · 0 · L · I` exactly when
`I = V / (i · 0 · L)`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `V = 0` and `I = 1` satisfy the first
equation but not the second.
