# At zero frequency the element laws do not determine the phasors

At `ω = 0`, take the phasors `V = i` (the imaginary unit) and `I = 0`, and `R = L = 1`. The
[RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) at zero frequency are
`v = √2 Re(i) = 0` and `i = 0`, so:

- the resistor law `v(t) = 1 · i(t)` holds at every time, but `V ≠ 1 · I`;
- the inductor law `v(t) = 1 · i'(t)` holds at every time, but `V ≠ i · 0 · 1 · I`.

The phasor element laws thus need `ω ≠ 0`.
