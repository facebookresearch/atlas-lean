# The inductor law for sinusoids is the phasor equation V = iωL I

Let `L > 0`. Let `ω > 0`, let `V` and `I` be complex phasors, and let `v` and `i` be the real
voltage and current whose values at every time `t` are the
[RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) `v(t) = √2 Re(V e^{iωt})`
and `i(t) = √2 Re(I e^{iωt})`. Then the inductor law `v(t) = L i'(t)` holds at every time `t`
exactly when the phasors satisfy `V = iωL I`.
