# An inductor's voltage leads its current by a quarter period

Let `L > 0` and `ω > 0`, and let `V` and `I` be complex phasors with `V = iωL I`. Then for every
time `t` the [RMS waveforms](../average-power-of-sinusoidal-signals/rms-waveform.md) satisfy

`w_V(t) = ωL w_I(t + π / (2ω))`,

where `w_V(t) = √2 Re(V e^{iωt})`: the voltage waveform is `ωL` times the current waveform
advanced by a quarter period.
