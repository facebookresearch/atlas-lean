# A uniform shunt discharge solves the telegrapher equations

Let `C > 0` and let `R`, `L`, `G` and `V` be real. The uniform voltage `v(x, t) = V exp(−(G / C) t)`
together with zero current `i = 0` [solve the telegrapher equations](is-telegrapher-solution.md)
with constants `R`, `L`, `G`, `C` at every point `(x, t)` of the plane.
