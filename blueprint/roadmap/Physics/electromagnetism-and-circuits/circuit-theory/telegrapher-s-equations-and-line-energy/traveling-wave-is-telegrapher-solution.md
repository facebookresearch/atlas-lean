# A forward traveling wave solves the lossless telegrapher equations

Let `L > 0` and `C > 0`, and let `f` be a continuously differentiable real function. Then the
[forward wave voltage](traveling-wave-voltage.md) `v(x, t) = f(x − t / √(L C))` and the
[forward wave current](traveling-wave-current.md) `i(x, t) = f(x − t / √(L C)) / √(L / C)`
[solve the telegrapher equations](is-telegrapher-solution.md) with `R = G = 0` at every point
`(x, t)` of the plane.
