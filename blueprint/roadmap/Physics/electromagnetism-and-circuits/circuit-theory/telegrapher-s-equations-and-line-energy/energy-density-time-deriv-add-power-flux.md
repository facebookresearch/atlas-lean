# Local energy identity of the telegrapher equations

Let `R`, `L`, `G` and `C` be real per-unit-length constants, `U` an open subset of the `(x, t)`
plane, and `v`, `i` real functions of `(x, t)` that are continuously differentiable on `U` and
[solve the telegrapher equations](is-telegrapher-solution.md) on `U`. Then at every point
`(x, t) ∈ U`

`∂ₜu(x, t) + ∂ₓS(x, t) = −d(x, t)`,

with the [energy density](energy-density.md) `u = C v² / 2 + L i² / 2`, the
[power flux](power-flux.md) `S = v i` and the [dissipation density](dissipation-density.md)
`d = R i² + G v²`.
