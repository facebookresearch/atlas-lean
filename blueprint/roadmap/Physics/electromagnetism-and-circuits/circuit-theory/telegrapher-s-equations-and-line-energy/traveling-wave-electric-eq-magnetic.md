# A traveling wave has equal electric and magnetic energy densities

Let `L > 0` and `C > 0`, and let `f` be any real function. For the
[forward wave voltage](traveling-wave-voltage.md) `v(x, t) = f(x − t / √(L C))` and the
[forward wave current](traveling-wave-current.md) `i(x, t) = f(x − t / √(L C)) / √(L / C)`, at every
point `(x, t)`

`C v(x, t)² / 2 = L i(x, t)² / 2`.
