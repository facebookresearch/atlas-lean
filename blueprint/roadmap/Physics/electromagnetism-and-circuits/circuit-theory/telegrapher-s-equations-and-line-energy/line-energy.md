# Energy stored on a line segment

For real constants `L`, `C` and `ℓ`, real functions `v` and `i` of `(x, t)` and a time `t`, the
energy stored on the line segment `[0, ℓ]` is

`E(t) = ∫ from 0 to ℓ of u(x, t) dx`,

with `u = C v² / 2 + L i² / 2` the [energy density](energy-density.md). The integral is oriented,
so it changes sign for `ℓ < 0`, and it is `0` when the density is not integrable; the intended
domain is `ℓ > 0` with continuous fields.
