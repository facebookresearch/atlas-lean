# Energy per unit length of a transmission line

For real constants `L` and `C`, real functions `v` and `i` of `(x, t)` and a point `(x, t)`, the
energy per unit length stored on the line is

`u(x, t) = C v(x, t)² / 2 + L i(x, t)² / 2`,

electric plus magnetic. In the physical reading `L` and `C` are positive.
