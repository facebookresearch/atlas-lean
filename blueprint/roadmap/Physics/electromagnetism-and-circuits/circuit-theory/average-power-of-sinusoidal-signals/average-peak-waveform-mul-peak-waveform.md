# Average power of two peak waveforms carries the factor one half

For all complex `V` and `I`, a real `ω > 0` and every real `a`, the average of the product of the
[peak waveforms](peak-waveform.md) of `V` and `I` over the period `[a, a + 2π/ω]` is `(1/2) Re(V Ī)`:
with peak phasors the average power has the factor `1/2`, unlike the
[RMS formula](average-rms-waveform-mul-rms-waveform.md).
