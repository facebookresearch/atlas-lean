# Average power of two peak waveforms carries the factor one half

For all complex `V` and `I`, a real `ω > 0` and every real `a`, the average of the product of the
[peak waveforms](peak-waveform.md) of `V` and `I` over the period `[a, a + 2π/ω]` is
`(1/2) Re(V Ī)`, where `Ī` is the complex conjugate of `I`; equivalently,
`∫_a^{a + 2π/ω} Re(V e^{iωt}) Re(I e^{iωt}) dt = (2π / ω) (1/2) Re(V Ī)`. Thus with peak phasors the
average power has the factor `1/2`, unlike the
[RMS formula](average-rms-waveform-mul-rms-waveform.md).
