# Sinusoidal waveform of an RMS phasor

For a complex phasor `V` and real numbers `ω` (angular frequency) and `t` (time), the RMS waveform
is `v(t) = √2 Re(V e^{i ω t})`, with the convention `e^{+i ω t}`. For `ω > 0`, `|V|` is the
root-mean-square value of `v` over a period ([mean square](average-rms-waveform-mul-self.md)).
