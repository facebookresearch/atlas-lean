# Average power of two RMS waveforms over one period

For all complex `V` and `I`, a real `ω > 0` and every real `a`, the average of the product of the
[RMS waveforms](rms-waveform.md) `v` and `i` over the period `[a, a + 2π/ω]` is
`(ω / 2π) ∫_a^{a + 2π/ω} v(t) i(t) dt = Re(V Ī)`, where `Ī` is the complex conjugate of `I`: the
average power for RMS phasors of voltage and current sharing the frequency `ω`. Equivalently,
`∫_a^{a + 2π/ω} v(t) i(t) dt = (2π / ω) Re(V Ī)`.
