# With the wrong sign of the secondary current the power is nonzero

For real numbers `n ≠ 0`, `V₂ ≠ 0` and `I₁ ≠ 0`, with `V₁ = n V₂` and the wrong entering sign
`I₂ = n I₁`, the [net instantaneous port power](net-port-power.md) `V₁ I₁ + V₂ I₂` is nonzero: power
conservation depends on the sign convention of the entering current.
