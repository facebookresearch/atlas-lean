# Finite-horizon passivity of a network plant

A [network plant](network-plant.md) is finite-horizon passive when every
[trajectory](network-trajectory.md) and every `a ≤ b` such that the trajectory
[obeys the laws on `[a, b]`](is-network-trajectory-on.md), the [supply rate](plant-supply-rate.md) is
continuous on `[a, b]` and the supply rate minus the [loss rate](plant-loss-rate.md) is integrable on
`[a, b]` give a trajectory [finite-horizon passive on `[a, b]`](finite-horizon-passive-on.md).
