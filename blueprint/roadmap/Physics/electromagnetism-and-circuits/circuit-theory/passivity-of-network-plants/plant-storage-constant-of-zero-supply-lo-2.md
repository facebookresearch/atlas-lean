# A network trajectory with zero supply and loss keeps its storage

For a [network plant](network-plant.md) with `R_e ≥ 0` on resistor branches, `L_e > 0` on inductor
branches and `C_e > 0` on capacitor branches, a [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) that
[obeys its laws at every time](is-network-trajectory.md), and reals `a ≤ b` such that the
[supply rate](plant-supply-rate.md) and the [loss rate](plant-loss-rate.md) are `0` at every time of
`[a, b]` and their difference is integrable on `[a, b]`, the [storage](plant-storage.md) at every
`t ∈ [a, b]` equals its value at `a`.
