# Every trajectory is passive on a zero-length interval

For every [network plant](network-plant.md), every [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) and every real
`a`, `E(a) - E(a) ≤ ∫_a^a s(t) dt`, where `E` is the [storage](plant-storage.md) and `s` the
[supply rate](plant-supply-rate.md): both sides are `0`.
