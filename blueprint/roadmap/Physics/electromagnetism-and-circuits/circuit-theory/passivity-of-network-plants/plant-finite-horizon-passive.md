# Finite-horizon passivity of a network trajectory

For a [network plant](network-plant.md) with `R_e ≥ 0` on resistor branches, `L_e > 0` on inductor
branches and `C_e > 0` on capacitor branches, a [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) that
[obeys its laws at every time](is-network-trajectory.md), and reals `a ≤ b` with continuous
[supply rate](plant-supply-rate.md) on `[a, b]` and supply rate minus [loss rate](plant-loss-rate.md)
integrable on `[a, b]`, the trajectory is [finite-horizon passive on `[a, b]`](finite-horizon-passive-on.md).
