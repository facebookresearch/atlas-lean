# Trajectories obeying the network laws at every time

A [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) obeys the laws of a [network plant](network-plant.md) when, at
every time `t`: `v(t) = Bᵀ φ(t)`; `B i(t) = j(t)`; `v_e(t) = R_e i_e(t)` for `e ∈ R`; for `e ∈ C`,
`v_e` is differentiable at `t` and `i_e(t) = C_e v_e'(t)`; and for `e ∈ L`, `i_e` is differentiable
at `t` and `v_e(t) = L_e i_e'(t)`.
