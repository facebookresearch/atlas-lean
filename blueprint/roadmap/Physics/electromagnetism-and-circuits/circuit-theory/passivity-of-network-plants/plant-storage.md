# Stored energy of a network plant along a trajectory

For a [network plant](network-plant.md), a [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) and a time `t`, the
storage is the [network stored energy](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/stored-energy.md)
`∑_{e ∈ C} C_e v_e(t)² / 2 + ∑_{e ∈ L} L_e i_e(t)² / 2`.
