# A network trajectory obeys the laws on sets with unique derivatives

For a [network plant](network-plant.md) and a [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) that
[obeys its laws at every time](is-network-trajectory.md), and a set `S` of times on which derivatives
within `S` are unique (for example `[a, b]` with `a < b`), the trajectory
[obeys the laws on `S`](is-network-trajectory-on.md).
