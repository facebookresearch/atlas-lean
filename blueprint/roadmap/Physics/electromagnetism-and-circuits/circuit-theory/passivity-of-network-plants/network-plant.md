# Network plant

A network plant with `n` nodes and `m` branches consists of a real `n × m` matrix `B` (read as the
incidence matrix; no incidence structure or orientation is imposed), sets `R`, `L`, `C` and `S` of
branches (resistors, inductors, capacitors and sources) and real values `(R_e)`, `(L_e)` and
`(C_e)`, such that `R`, `L`, `C` and `S` form a
[branch partition](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/is-branch-partition.md): pairwise disjoint and
covering every branch. It carries no signals; no sign condition on the values is imposed.
