# Supply rate of a network plant along a trajectory

For a [network plant](network-plant.md), a [trajectory](network-trajectory.md) and a time `t`, the
supply rate is `φ(t) · j(t) - ∑_{e ∈ S} v_e(t) i_e(t)`: the power supplied through the node
injections minus the
[power absorbed by the source branches](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/source-absorbed-power.md).
