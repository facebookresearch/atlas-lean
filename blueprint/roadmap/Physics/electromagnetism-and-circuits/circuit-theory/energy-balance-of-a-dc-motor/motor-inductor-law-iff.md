# The inductor law of the loop is the armature equation

For all real `u`, `R`, `L`, `i`, `i'`, `ke` and `ω`, the inductance branch law `v₂ = L i'`, with
`v₂` the [branch voltage](motor-incidence-transpose-mul-vec.md) `u - R i - ke ω` of branch `2`,
holds if and only if the armature equation `u = R i + L i' + ke ω` holds.
