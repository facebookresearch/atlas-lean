# Dissipation inequality of a DC motor with equal motor constants

Let `R ≥ 0`, `b ≥ 0`, `L`, `J` and `ke = kt` be real numbers, `u, i, ω, τL : ℝ → ℝ` functions of
time, and `t` a time at which `i` and `ω` are differentiable and the armature and rotor equations
`u = R i + L i' + ke ω` and `J ω' = kt i - b ω - τL` hold. Then the derivative of the
[stored energy](motor-energy.md) at `t` is at most `u i - τL ω`: the supplied electrical power minus
the power delivered to the load.
