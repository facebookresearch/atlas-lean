# Power absorbed by the sources of the DC motor loop

For all real `u`, `R`, `i`, `ke` and `ω`, with the [branch voltages](motor-incidence-transpose-mul-vec.md)
of the motor loop and the current `i` in every branch, the
[power absorbed by the sources](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/source-absorbed-power.md) (branches `0` and `3`) is
`-(u i) + ke ω i`: the supply delivers `u i` and the back-EMF source absorbs `ke ω i`.
