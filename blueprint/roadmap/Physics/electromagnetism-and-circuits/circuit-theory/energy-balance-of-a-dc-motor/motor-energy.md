# Stored energy of a DC motor

For real numbers `L` (armature inductance), `J` (rotor inertia), `i` (armature current) and `ω`
(rotor speed), the stored energy is `E = L i² / 2 + J ω² / 2`: armature magnetic energy plus rotor
kinetic energy. In the physical reading `L` and `J` are positive; the formula is defined for all
reals.
