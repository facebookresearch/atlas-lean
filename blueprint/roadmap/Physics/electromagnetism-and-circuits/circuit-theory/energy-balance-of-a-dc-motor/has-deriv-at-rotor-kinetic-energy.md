# Rate of change of the rotor kinetic energy

Let `J`, `kt` and `b` be real numbers, `i, ω, τL : ℝ → ℝ` functions of time and `t` a time at which
`ω` is differentiable and the rotor equation `J ω'(t) = kt i(t) - b ω(t) - τL(t)` holds. Then
`s ↦ J ω(s)² / 2` has derivative `kt i ω - b ω² - τL ω` at `t`.
