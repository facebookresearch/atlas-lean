# Network stored energy of the DC motor loop

For all capacitance values, every real `L`, every vector `v` of branch voltages and every real `i`,
the [network stored energy](../energy-balance-of-rlc-networks/stored-energy.md) of the motor loop,
with no capacitor branch, the inductor set `{2}`, inductance `L` and current `i` in every branch, is
the armature energy `L i² / 2`.
