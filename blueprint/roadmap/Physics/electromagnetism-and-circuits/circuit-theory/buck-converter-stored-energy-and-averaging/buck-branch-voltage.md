# Branch voltages of the ideal buck converter

For real numbers `s` (switch state), `Vin` (input voltage) and `v` (output voltage), the branch
voltages of the four-branch network, in the order source, inductor, capacitor, resistor, are
`(s Vin, s Vin - v, v, v)`: the switched input voltage, the input-minus-output drop, and the output
voltage twice.
