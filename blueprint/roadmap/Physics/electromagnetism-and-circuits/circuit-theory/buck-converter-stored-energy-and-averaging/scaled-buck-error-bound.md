# Dimensionless error bound of buck averaging

For real numbers `L`, `C`, `R`, `Vin`, `Iref`, `Vref`, `T`, `ε`, `Ld`, `Δi₀` and `Δv₀`, the error
bound is `B = exp(κ T) (max(|Δi₀| / Iref, |Δv₀| / Vref) + |Vin| / (L Iref) · ε (1 + Ld T / 2))`,
where `κ` is the [growth-rate bound](scaled-buck-growth-rate.md), with `a / 0 = 0`.
