# The scaled buck operator norm is at most the growth-rate bound

For positive reals `L`, `C`, `R`, `Iref` and `Vref`, the operator norm of the
[scaled buck operator](scaled-buck-operator.md), for the maximum norm on `ℝ²`, is at most the
[growth-rate bound](scaled-buck-growth-rate.md)
`κ = max(Vref / (L Iref), Iref / (C Vref) + 1 / (R C))`.
