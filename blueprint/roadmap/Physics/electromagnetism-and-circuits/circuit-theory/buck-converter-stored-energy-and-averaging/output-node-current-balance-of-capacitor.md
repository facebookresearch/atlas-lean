# The capacitor law gives current balance at the output node

For real numbers `C` and `R`, functions `i, v : ℝ → ℝ` and a time `t` with
`C v'(t) = i(t) - v(t) / R`, the capacitor current plus the resistor current equals the inductor
current: `C v'(t) + v(t) / R = i(t)`. Here `v'(t)` is the derivative of `v` at `t`, taken to be `0`
where `v` is not differentiable, and `v(t) / R = 0` when `R = 0`.
