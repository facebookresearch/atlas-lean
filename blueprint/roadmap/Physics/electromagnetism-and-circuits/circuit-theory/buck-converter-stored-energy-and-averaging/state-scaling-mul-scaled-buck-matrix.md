# The scaled buck matrix is conjugate to the state matrix

For real numbers `L`, `C` and `R` and nonzero reals `Iref` and `Vref`, the
[state scaling](state-scaling.md) `S = diag(Iref, Vref)`, the [scaled state matrix](scaled-buck-matrix.md)
`Ã` and the [state matrix](buck-matrix.md) `A` satisfy `S Ã = A S`.
