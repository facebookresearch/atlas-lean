# State matrix of the ideal buck converter

For real numbers `L`, `C` and `R`, the state matrix of the ideal buck converter in the state
`(i, v)` is `A` with rows `(0, -1/L)` and `(1/C, -1/(R C))`. With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, an
entry whose denominator vanishes is `0`; the intended domain is `L, C, R > 0`.
