# The buck state equations in scaled coordinates

For real numbers `L > 0`, `C > 0`, `R`, `Vin`, `Iref > 0`, `Vref > 0`, `s`, `i` and `v`, with
`z = (i / Iref, v / Vref)` the [scaled state](scaled-state.md),
`Ã z + s b̃ = (((s Vin - v) / L) / Iref, ((i - v / R) / C) / Vref)`, where `Ã` is the
[scaled buck operator](scaled-buck-operator.md) and `b̃` the [scaled input vector](scaled-buck-input.md).
