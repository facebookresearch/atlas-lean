# Partition of the branches into R, L, C and source classes

For a number `n_B` of branches, indexed `0, …, n_B − 1`, and four sets of branches `R`, `L`, `C`
and `S` (resistor, inductor, capacitor and source branches), the four sets form a branch partition
when they are pairwise disjoint and their union is the set of all branches: every branch lies in
exactly one class.
