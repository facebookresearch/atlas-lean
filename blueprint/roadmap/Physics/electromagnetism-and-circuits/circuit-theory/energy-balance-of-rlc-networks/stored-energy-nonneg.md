# For nonnegative C and L the stored energy is nonnegative

Let `C` and `L` be sets of capacitor and inductor branches with `C_e ≥ 0` for `e ∈ C` and
`L_e ≥ 0` for `e ∈ L`. Then for all real branch voltages `v` and currents `i` the
[stored energy](stored-energy.md) `∑_{e ∈ C} C_e v_e² / 2 + ∑_{e ∈ L} L_e i_e² / 2` is
nonnegative.
