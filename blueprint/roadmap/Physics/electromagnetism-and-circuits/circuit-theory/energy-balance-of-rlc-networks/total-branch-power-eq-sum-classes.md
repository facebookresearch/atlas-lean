# Total branch power splits over the four branch classes

Let `R`, `L`, `C` and `S` be a [branch partition](is-branch-partition.md) of `n_B` branches, and
`v` and `i` real branch voltages and currents. Then

`∑ₑ v_e i_e = ∑_{e ∈ R} v_e i_e + ∑_{e ∈ L} v_e i_e + ∑_{e ∈ C} v_e i_e + ∑_{e ∈ S} v_e i_e`,

each branch being counted exactly once.
