# For physical parameters the diode current is positive iff $V > 0$

If $I_s > 0$, $n > 0$, $k_B > 0$, $T > 0$ and $q \ne 0$, then for every anode-to-cathode voltage
$V$ the [diode current](diode-current.md) satisfies

$$I_D(V) > 0 \iff V > 0:$$

the diode conducts from anode to cathode exactly in forward bias, and $I_D(V) \le 0$ for
$V \le 0$.
