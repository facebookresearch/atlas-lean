# Thermal voltage as the Einstein ratio $D / \mu$ for $\mu, q \ne 0$

For a mobility $\mu \ne 0$, a carrier charge $q \ne 0$ and all real $k_B$ and $T$, the
[Einstein diffusion coefficient](../../fields-and-waves/electrostatics-plane-waves-and-fresnel-coefficie/diffusion-coeff.md)
$D = \mu k_B T / |q|$ divided by $\mu$ is the [thermal voltage](thermal-voltage.md):

$$\frac{D}{\mu} = \frac{k_B T}{|q|} = V_T.$$
