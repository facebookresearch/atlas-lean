# For physical parameters the diode current is strictly increasing

If the saturation current $I_s$, the emission coefficient $n$, $k_B$ and $T$ are positive and the
carrier charge is nonzero, $q \ne 0$, the [diode current](diode-current.md) is strictly
increasing in the anode-to-cathode voltage: $V < W$ implies $I_D(V) < I_D(W)$.
