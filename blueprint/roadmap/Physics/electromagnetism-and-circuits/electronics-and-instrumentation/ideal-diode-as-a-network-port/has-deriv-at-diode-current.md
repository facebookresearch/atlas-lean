# Derivative of the diode current (small-signal conductance)

For all real parameters $I_s$, $n$, $k_B$, $T$, $q$ and every voltage $V$, the
[diode current](diode-current.md) $I_D$ is differentiable at $V$ with derivative, the
small-signal conductance,

$$I_D'(V) = \frac{I_s}{n V_T} \exp\frac{V}{n V_T}, \qquad V_T = \frac{k_B T}{|q|}.$$

When the scale $n V_T$ is $0$, the current is identically $0$ and, with $x / 0 = 0$, the formula
gives $0$ as well.
