# Reversing a diode branch with its voltage and current keeps the diode

Let a [diode occupy branch](diode-branch.md) $e$ with anode $a$ and cathode $c$, for any real
parameters. Reverse branch $e$ (swap its tail and head) and replace $v_e$ by $-v_e$ and $i_e$ by
$-i_e$, leaving every other branch unchanged. Then the same diode, anode $a$ and cathode $c$,
occupies branch $e$ of the reversed network with the new voltages and currents.
