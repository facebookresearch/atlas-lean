# For physical parameters the diode current stays above $-I_s$

If $I_s > 0$, $n > 0$, $k_B > 0$, $T > 0$ and $q \ne 0$, then for every anode-to-cathode voltage
$V$ the [diode current](diode-current.md) satisfies

$$I_D(V) > -I_s:$$

the reverse current never reaches the saturation current. Since
$I_D(V) + I_s = I_s \exp(V / (n V_T))$, the inequality uses only $I_s > 0$; the other conditions
make the scale $n V_T$ positive.
