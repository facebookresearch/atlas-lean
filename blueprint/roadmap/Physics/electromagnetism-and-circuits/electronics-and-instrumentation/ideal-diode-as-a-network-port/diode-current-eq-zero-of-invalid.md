# The diode current is identically zero if $n$, $T$ or $q$ is zero

For all real $I_s$ and $k_B$: if $n = 0$, $T = 0$ or $q = 0$, the scale $n V_T$ of the
[diode current](diode-current.md) is $0$ ($V_T = k_B T / |q|$ is $0$ when $q = 0$, by $x / 0 = 0$),
so $V / (n V_T) = 0$ and

$$I_D(V) = I_s (e^0 - 1) = 0 \quad \text{for every } V.$$

In particular, with such parameters the current is not strictly increasing and is not positive in
forward bias, unlike the [strictly increasing current](strict-mono-diode-current.md) of positive
parameters.
