# Event that the resolution time of one observation exceeds its budget

For resolution times $r_i(\omega)$ at the outcomes $\omega$ of a sample space and settling budgets
$b_i$, the budget-expiry event of an index $i$ is

$$\{\omega : b_i < r_i(\omega)\},$$

the outcomes at which the resolution time strictly exceeds the budget; a resolution time equal to
the budget is within budget.
