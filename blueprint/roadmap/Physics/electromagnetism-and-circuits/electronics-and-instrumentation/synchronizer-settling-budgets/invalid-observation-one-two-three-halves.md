# With thresholds $1$ and $2$, the voltage $3/2$ is invalid

For the thresholds $V_{il} = 1$ and $V_{ih} = 2$, the voltage $3/2$ is an
[invalid observation](invalid-observation.md): it lies strictly between the thresholds, so its
[observation](../logic-levels-and-noise-margins/observe.md) is `none`.
