# For positive parameters the closed-loop pole location is negative

For real numbers $\omega_p > 0$, $\beta > 0$, $b > 0$ and $A_0 > 0$, the
[closed-loop pole location](closed-loop-pole.md) is strictly negative:

$$-\omega_p\,(1 + \beta b A_0) < 0 .$$

Since for $\omega_p > 0$ it is the only zero of the loaded denominator
$1 + s/\omega_p + \beta b A_0$ ([denominator-zero characterisation](denominator-eq-zero-iff.md)),
that zero lies in the open left half-plane.
