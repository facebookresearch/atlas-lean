# For $A, \beta > 0$ the gain deficit is $1/(\beta(1 + A\beta))$

For real numbers $A > 0$ and $\beta > 0$ (the open-loop gain and the feedback factor), the
[closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md) $A / (1 + A\beta)$ falls short of the ideal gain $1/\beta$
by exactly

$$\frac{1}{\beta} - \frac{A}{1 + A\beta} = \frac{1}{\beta\,(1 + A\beta)} .$$

All denominators are positive. By this formula, for fixed $\beta > 0$ the deficit decreases to $0$
as the open-loop gain $A$ grows.
