# For positive $A_0, \beta, a, b$ the DC gain lies in $(0, a/\beta)$

For real numbers $A_0 > 0$, $\beta > 0$, $a > 0$ and $b > 0$, the
[loaded DC gain](loaded-dc-gain.md) is positive and strictly below the divider-scaled ideal gain
$a/\beta$:

$$0 < \frac{a b A_0}{1 + \beta b A_0} < \frac{a}{\beta} .$$

The denominator exceeds $1$, so the division is an ordinary one. No upper bound on the dividers $a$
and $b$ is needed.
