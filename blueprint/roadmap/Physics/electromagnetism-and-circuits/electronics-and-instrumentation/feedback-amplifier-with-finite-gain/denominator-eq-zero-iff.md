# For $\omega_p > 0$ the pole location is the only denominator zero

For real numbers $\omega_p > 0$, $\beta$, $b$, $A_0$ and every complex number $s$,

$$1 + \frac{s}{\omega_p} + \beta b A_0 = 0 \iff s = -\omega_p\,(1 + \beta b A_0),$$

where the right side is the [closed-loop pole location](closed-loop-pole.md) viewed as a complex
number. So the loaded denominator vanishes at exactly one point, which lies on the real axis. Both
directions multiply by $\omega_p \ne 0$ and rearrange.
