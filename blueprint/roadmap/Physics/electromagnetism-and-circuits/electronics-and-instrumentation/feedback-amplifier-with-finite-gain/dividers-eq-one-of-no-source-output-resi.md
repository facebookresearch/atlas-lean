# With zero source and output resistance both dividers equal 1

For real numbers $R_{in} > 0$ and $R_L > 0$, the [source divider](source-divider.md) with zero
source resistance and the [load divider](load-divider.md) with zero output resistance both equal
$1$:

$$\frac{R_{in}}{0 + R_{in}} = 1, \qquad \frac{R_L}{0 + R_L} = 1 .$$

The positivity of $R_{in}$ and $R_L$ matters: for $R_{in} = 0$ the convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives a
source divider of $0$. With both dividers equal to $1$ the loaded transfer is the
[transfer without divider losses](loaded-transfer-at-unit-dividers.md).
