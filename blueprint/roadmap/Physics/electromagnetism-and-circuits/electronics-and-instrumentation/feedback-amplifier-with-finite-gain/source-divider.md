# Source divider $R_{in}/(R_s + R_{in})$

For real numbers $R_s$ and $R_{in}$, the source divider is

$$a = \frac{R_{in}}{R_s + R_{in}} .$$

With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, it is $0$ when $R_s + R_{in} = 0$. In the amplifier model
$R_s \ge 0$ is the resistance of the signal source and $R_{in} > 0$ the input resistance of the
amplifier; then $a$ is the ratio by which the voltage divider formed by $R_s$ and $R_{in}$ scales
the source voltage at the amplifier input, and it is
[positive and at most $1$](source-divider-pos-and-le-one.md).
