# Closed-loop pole location $-\omega_p(1 + \beta b A_0)$

For real numbers $\omega_p$, $\beta$, $b$, $A_0$, the closed-loop pole location is the real number

$$p = -\omega_p\,(1 + \beta b A_0) .$$

For $\omega_p > 0$, $p$ viewed as a complex number is the only zero of the loaded denominator
$1 + s/\omega_p + \beta b A_0$ ([denominator-zero characterisation](denominator-eq-zero-iff.md)),
and for positive $\omega_p$, $\beta$, $b$, $A_0$ it is [negative](closed-loop-pole-neg.md). It is a
zero of the denominator of the [loaded closed-loop transfer](loaded-transfer.md), not necessarily a
pole of the transfer: with $a = 0$, for instance, the transfer is identically $0$.
