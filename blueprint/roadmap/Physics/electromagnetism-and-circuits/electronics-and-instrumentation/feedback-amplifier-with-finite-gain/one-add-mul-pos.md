# For $A, \beta > 0$ the loop denominator $1 + A\beta$ is positive

For real numbers $A > 0$ and $\beta > 0$, the loop denominator of the finite-gain feedback
amplifier is positive:

$$1 + A\beta > 0 .$$

Here $A$ is the open-loop gain and $\beta$ the feedback factor. The positivity makes the division in
the [closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md) $A / (1 + A\beta)$ an ordinary one.
