# Load divider is positive and at most 1 for $R_L > 0$, $R_o \ge 0$

For real numbers $R_L > 0$ and $R_o \ge 0$, the [load divider](load-divider.md) satisfies

$$0 < \frac{R_L}{R_o + R_L} \le 1 .$$

Here $R_o + R_L > 0$, so the division is an ordinary one. The value $1$ is reached at $R_o = 0$
([zero source and output resistance](dividers-eq-one-of-no-source-output-resi.md)).
