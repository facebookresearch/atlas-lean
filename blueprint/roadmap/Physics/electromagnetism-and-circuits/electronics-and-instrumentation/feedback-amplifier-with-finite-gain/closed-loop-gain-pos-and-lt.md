# For $A, \beta > 0$ the closed-loop gain lies in $(0, 1/\beta)$

For real numbers $A > 0$ and $\beta > 0$ (the open-loop gain and the feedback factor), the
[closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md) is positive and strictly below the ideal gain $1/\beta$:

$$0 < \frac{A}{1 + A\beta} < \frac{1}{\beta} .$$

Equivalently, the [gain deficit](gain-deficit.md) $1/\beta - A/(1 + A\beta)$ is positive.
