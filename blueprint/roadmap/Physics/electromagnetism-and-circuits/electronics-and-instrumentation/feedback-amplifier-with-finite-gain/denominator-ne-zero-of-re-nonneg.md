# For positive parameters no denominator zero has $\mathrm{Re}\,s \ge 0$

For real numbers $A_0 > 0$, $\omega_p > 0$, $\beta > 0$, $b > 0$ and every complex number $s$ with
$\mathrm{Re}\, s \ge 0$, the loaded denominator is nonzero:

$$1 + \frac{s}{\omega_p} + \beta b A_0 \ne 0 .$$

At a zero, $s$ would equal the real number $-\omega_p (1 + \beta b A_0)$, which is negative, so its
real part would be negative. Hence the denominator of the
[loaded closed-loop transfer](loaded-transfer.md) has no zero in the closed right half-plane.
