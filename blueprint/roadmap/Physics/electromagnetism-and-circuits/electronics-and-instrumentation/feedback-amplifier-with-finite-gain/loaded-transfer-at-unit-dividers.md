# At $a = b = 1$, $G(s) = A_0/(1 + s/\omega_p + \beta A_0)$

For all real numbers $A_0$, $\omega_p$, $\beta$ and every complex number $s$, the
[loaded closed-loop transfer](loaded-transfer.md) with both dividers equal to $1$ is

$$G(s) = \frac{A_0}{1 + s/\omega_p + \beta A_0},$$

the closed-loop transfer of the one-pole amplifier without divider losses, as with
[zero source and output resistance](dividers-eq-one-of-no-source-output-resi.md). Both sides use the
same division, so the identity also holds where the denominator vanishes (both sides are $0$ by the
convention $z / 0 = 0$) and for $\omega_p = 0$.
