# For $A, \beta > 0$ the output is the closed-loop gain times the input

Let $A > 0$ and $\beta > 0$ be real (the open-loop gain and the feedback factor), and let the real
numbers $v_i$ and $v_o$ satisfy the amplifier equation $v_o = A (v_i - \beta v_o)$ of the
finite-gain feedback amplifier. Then

$$v_o = G(A, \beta)\, v_i, \qquad G(A, \beta) = \frac{A}{1 + A\beta},$$

where $G$ is the [closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md); for $A, \beta > 0$ its denominator is
positive.
