# For $A, \beta > 0$ any amplifier-equation solution is the closed-loop gain times the input

Let $A > 0$ (open-loop gain) and $\beta > 0$ (feedback factor). If the real numbers $v_i$ (input)
and $v_o$ (output) satisfy the amplifier equation $v_o = A\,(v_i - \beta v_o)$, then

$$v_o = G(A, \beta)\, v_i,$$

where $G(A, \beta) = A/(1 + A\beta)$ is the [closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md), whose
denominator $1 + A\beta$ is [positive](one-add-mul-pos.md) here.

The other form has its own page: [For $A, \beta > 0$ any amplifier-equation solution is the closed form](eq-closed-form-of-eq.md).
