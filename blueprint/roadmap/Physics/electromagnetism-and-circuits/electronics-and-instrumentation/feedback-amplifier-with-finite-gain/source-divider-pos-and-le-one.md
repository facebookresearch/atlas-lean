# Source divider is positive and at most 1 for $R_{in} > 0$, $R_s \ge 0$

For real numbers $R_{in} > 0$ and $R_s \ge 0$, the [source divider](source-divider.md) satisfies

$$0 < \frac{R_{in}}{R_s + R_{in}} \le 1 .$$

Here $R_s + R_{in} > 0$, so the division is an ordinary one. The value $1$ is reached at $R_s = 0$
([zero source and output resistance](dividers-eq-one-of-no-source-output-resi.md)).
