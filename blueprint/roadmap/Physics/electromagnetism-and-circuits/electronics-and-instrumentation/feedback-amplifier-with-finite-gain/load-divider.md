# Load divider $R_L/(R_o + R_L)$

For real numbers $R_o$ and $R_L$, the load divider is

$$b = \frac{R_L}{R_o + R_L} .$$

With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, it is $0$ when $R_o + R_L = 0$. In the amplifier model $R_o \ge 0$
is the output resistance of the amplifier and $R_L > 0$ the load resistance; then $b$ is the ratio
by which the voltage divider formed by $R_o$ and $R_L$ scales the unloaded amplifier output at the
load, and it is [positive and at most $1$](load-divider-pos-and-le-one.md).
