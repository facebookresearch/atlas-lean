# For $\beta > 0$ and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain current is nonnegative

For real $\beta > 0$, every real $u$ and every $V_{DS} \ge 0$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) satisfies
$I(\beta, u, V_{DS}) \ge 0$. The condition $V_{DS} \ge 0$ (the forward domain) cannot be dropped:
$I(1, 1, -1) = -3/2$. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant,
$u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a
forward-biased nMOS transistor.
