# Inside the triode region the output conductance is $\beta(u - V_{DS})$

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $0 < V_{DS} < u$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) has derivative
$\beta (u - V_{DS})$ with respect to $V_{DS}$ at $V_{DS}$ ($u$ fixed): the output conductance in the
open triode region is $g_{ds} = \beta (u - V_{DS})$. Nothing is stated on the region boundaries. In
the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive
and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
