# For positive overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge u$ the saturation formula holds

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $u > 0$ and $u \le V_{DS}$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) is

$$I(\beta, u, V_{DS}) = \frac{\beta u^2}{2}.$$

At $V_{DS} = u$ the definition takes the triode branch, whose value there is also $\beta u^2 / 2$.
In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive
and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
