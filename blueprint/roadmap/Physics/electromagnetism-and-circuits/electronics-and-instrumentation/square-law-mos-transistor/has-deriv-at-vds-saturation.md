# Inside the saturation region the output conductance is zero

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $0 < u < V_{DS}$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) has derivative $0$ with
respect to $V_{DS}$ at $V_{DS}$ ($u$ fixed): in the open saturation region the output conductance
$g_{ds}$ vanishes, as the model has no channel-length modulation. Nothing is stated at $V_{DS} = u$.
In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive
and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
