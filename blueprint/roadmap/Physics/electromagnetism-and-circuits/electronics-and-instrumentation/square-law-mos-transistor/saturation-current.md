# Saturation-region drain-current formula of the square-law MOS model

For real numbers $\beta$ and $u$, the saturation formula is

$$I_{\mathrm{sat}}(\beta, u) = \frac{\beta u^2}{2}.$$

It is defined for all real inputs and does not depend on the drain–source voltage (no channel-length
modulation). In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the
gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor; the
[square-law drain current](current.md) uses it for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$.
