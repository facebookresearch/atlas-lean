# For positive overdrive the current is jointly continuous at $(u, u)$

Let $\beta > 0$ and $u > 0$. Then the [square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$
($0$ for $u \le 0$, the [triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) is continuous as a
function of the pair $(u, V_{DS})$ at the point $(u, u)$ of the boundary between the triode and
saturation regions. No differentiability at the boundary is stated. In the physical reading
$\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the
drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
