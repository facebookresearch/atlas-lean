# Triode-region drain-current formula of the square-law MOS model

For real numbers $\beta$, $u$ and $V_{DS}$, the triode (linear-region) formula is

$$I_{\mathrm{tri}}(\beta, u, V_{DS}) = \beta \left(u\, V_{DS} - \frac{V_{DS}^2}{2}\right).$$

It is defined for all real inputs. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant,
$u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a
forward-biased nMOS transistor; the [square-law drain current](current.md) uses it for $u > 0$ and
$V_{DS} \le u$.
