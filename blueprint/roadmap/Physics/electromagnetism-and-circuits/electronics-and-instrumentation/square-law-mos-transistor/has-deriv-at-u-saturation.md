# Inside the saturation region the transconductance is $\beta u$

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $0 < u < V_{DS}$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) has derivative $\beta u$
with respect to $u$ at $u$ ($V_{DS}$ fixed): the transconductance in the open saturation region is
$g_m = \beta u$. Nothing is stated on the region boundaries. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is
the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source
voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
