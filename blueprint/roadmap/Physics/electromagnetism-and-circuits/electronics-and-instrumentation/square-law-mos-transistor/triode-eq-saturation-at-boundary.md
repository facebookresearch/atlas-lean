# The triode and saturation formulas agree at $V_{DS} = u$

For all real $\beta$ and $u$, the [triode formula](triode-current.md) at $V_{DS} = u$ equals the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md):

$$\beta \left(u \cdot u - \frac{u^2}{2}\right) = \frac{\beta u^2}{2}.$$

This is an algebraic identity of the two formulas, valid also for $u \le 0$, where the device
current itself is $0$.
