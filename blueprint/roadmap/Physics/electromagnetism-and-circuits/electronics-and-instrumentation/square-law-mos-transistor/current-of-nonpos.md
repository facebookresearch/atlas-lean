# With nonpositive overdrive the drain current is zero (cutoff)

For all real $\beta$, $u$ and $V_{DS}$ with $u \le 0$, the [square-law drain current](current.md)
$I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the [triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and
$V_{DS} \le u$, the [saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) is
$0$. The cutoff branch takes priority over the other two, whatever $V_{DS}$ is. In the physical
reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and
$V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
