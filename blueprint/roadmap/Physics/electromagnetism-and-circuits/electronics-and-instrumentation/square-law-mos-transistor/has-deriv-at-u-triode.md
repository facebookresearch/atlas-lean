# Inside the triode region the transconductance is $\beta V_{DS}$

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $0 < V_{DS} < u$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) has derivative
$\beta V_{DS}$ with respect to $u$ at $u$ ($V_{DS}$ fixed): the transconductance in the open triode
region is $g_m = \beta V_{DS}$. Nothing is stated on the region boundaries. In the physical reading
$\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the
drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
