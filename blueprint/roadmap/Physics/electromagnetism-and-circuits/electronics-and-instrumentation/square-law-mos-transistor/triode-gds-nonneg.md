# For $\beta > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, $\beta(u - V_{DS})$ is nonnegative

For real $\beta > 0$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $V_{DS} \le u$,

$$0 \le \beta (u - V_{DS}),$$

the expression of the triode output conductance $g_{ds}$ (the derivative of the triode current in
$V_{DS}$ inside the triode region). The inequality is algebraic: it needs neither $u > 0$ nor
$V_{DS} \ge 0$. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$
the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
