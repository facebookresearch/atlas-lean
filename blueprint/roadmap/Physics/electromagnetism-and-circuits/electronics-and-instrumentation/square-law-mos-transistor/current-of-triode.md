# With $u > 0$ and $0 \le V_{DS} \le u$ the current is the triode value

For all real $\beta$ and real $u$, $V_{DS}$ with $u > 0$ and $0 \le V_{DS} \le u$, the
[square-law drain current](current.md) $I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the
[triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} \le u$, the
[saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) is

$$I(\beta, u, V_{DS}) = \beta \left(u\, V_{DS} - \frac{V_{DS}^2}{2}\right).$$

The statement includes the boundary $V_{DS} = u$. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device
constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a
forward-biased nMOS transistor.
