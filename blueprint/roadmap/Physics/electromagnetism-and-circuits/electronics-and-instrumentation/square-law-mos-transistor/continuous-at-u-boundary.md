# For $V_{DS} > 0$ the current is continuous in $u$ at $u = V_{DS}$

Let $\beta > 0$ and $V_{DS} > 0$. Then the [square-law drain current](current.md)
$I(\beta, u, V_{DS})$ ($0$ for $u \le 0$, the [triode formula](triode-current.md) for $u > 0$ and
$V_{DS} \le u$, the [saturation formula](saturation-current.md) for $u > 0$ and $V_{DS} > u$) is
continuous in the overdrive $u$ at $u = V_{DS}$, with $V_{DS}$ fixed: near this point $u > 0$, and
the triode and saturation formulas agree at $u = V_{DS}$. No differentiability at the boundary is
stated. In the physical reading $\beta > 0$ is the device constant, $u = V_{GS} - V_{th}$ the gate
overdrive and $V_{DS} \ge 0$ the drain–source voltage of a forward-biased nMOS transistor.
