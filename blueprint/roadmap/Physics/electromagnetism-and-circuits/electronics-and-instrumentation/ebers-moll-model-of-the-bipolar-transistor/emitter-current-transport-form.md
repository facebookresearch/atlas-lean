# Under reciprocity, the emitter current takes transport form

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_R$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers, $V_T > 0$ and
$\alpha_F > 0$, and suppose the reciprocity relation $\alpha_F I_{SF} = \alpha_R I_{SR}$. Then,
with the transport saturation current $I_S = \alpha_F I_{SF}$ and $u = e^{V_{BE}/V_T}$,
$w = e^{V_{BC}/V_T}$, the [emitter current](emitter-current.md), counted out of the emitter, is

$$I_E = I_S (u - w) + I_S \frac{1 - \alpha_F}{\alpha_F} (u - 1) .$$

No condition on $\alpha_R$ is needed.
