# Derivative of the collector current in the base-emitter voltage

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers and $V_T > 0$. As a
function of the base-emitter voltage, with $V_{BC}$ fixed, the
[collector current](collector-current.md) $I_C$ has at $V_{BE}$ the derivative

$$\frac{\partial I_C}{\partial V_{BE}} = \frac{\alpha_F I_{SF}}{V_T}\, e^{V_{BE}/V_T} ,$$

the transconductance $g_m$ of the Ebers–Moll model.
