# In forward-active bias, collector and emitter currents are positive

Let $V_T > 0$, $I_{SF} > 0$, $I_{SR} > 0$, $\alpha_F > 0$ and $\alpha_R > 0$, and let the bias be
forward active: $V_{BE} > 0$ and $V_{BC} < 0$. Then the [collector current](collector-current.md),
counted into the collector, and the [emitter current](emitter-current.md), counted out of the
emitter, are positive:

$$I_C > 0 , \qquad I_E > 0 .$$

Nothing is stated about the [base current](base-current.md), which can be negative under these
hypotheses: for $\alpha_F = 1$ it is $(1 - \alpha_R) I_R$, negative when $\alpha_R < 1$ because
the reverse branch current $I_R$ is negative.
