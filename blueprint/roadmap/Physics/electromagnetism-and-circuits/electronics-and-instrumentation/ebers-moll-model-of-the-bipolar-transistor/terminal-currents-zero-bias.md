# At zero junction voltages all three terminal currents vanish

For all real numbers $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $\alpha_R$ and $V_T$, at
$V_{BE} = V_{BC} = 0$ the [collector](collector-current.md), [base](base-current.md) and
[emitter](emitter-current.md) currents of the Ebers–Moll model are zero:

$$I_C = I_B = I_E = 0 .$$

No condition on $V_T$ is needed: the exponents $0 / V_T$ are $0$ for every $V_T$, $V_T = 0$
included.
