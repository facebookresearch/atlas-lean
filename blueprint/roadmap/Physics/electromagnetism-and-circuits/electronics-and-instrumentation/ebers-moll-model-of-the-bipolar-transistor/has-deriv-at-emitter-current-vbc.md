# Derivative of the emitter current in the base-collector voltage

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_R$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers and $V_T > 0$. As a
function of the base-collector voltage, with $V_{BE}$ fixed, the
[emitter current](emitter-current.md) $I_E$ has at $V_{BC}$ the derivative

$$\frac{\partial I_E}{\partial V_{BC}} = -\frac{\alpha_R I_{SR}}{V_T}\, e^{V_{BC}/V_T} .$$
