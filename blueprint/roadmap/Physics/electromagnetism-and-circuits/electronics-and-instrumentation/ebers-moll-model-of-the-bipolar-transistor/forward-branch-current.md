# Forward branch current of the Ebers–Moll model

For real numbers $I_{SF}$ (the forward saturation current), $V_T$ (the thermal voltage) and
$V_{BE}$ (the base-emitter voltage), the forward (base-emitter) branch current is

$$I_F = I_{SF} \left(e^{V_{BE}/V_T} - 1\right) .$$

With the convention $a / 0 = 0$, at $V_T = 0$ the exponent is $0$ and $I_F = 0$ for every
$V_{BE}$. In the device $V_T > 0$ and $I_{SF} > 0$; the definition accepts any real inputs.
