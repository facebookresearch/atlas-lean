# In forward-active bias, $I_C + I_B + I_E$ is not zero

Let $V_T > 0$, $I_{SF} > 0$, $I_{SR} > 0$ and $\alpha_R > 0$, let $\alpha_F$ be any real number,
and let the bias be forward active: $V_{BE} > 0$ and $V_{BC} < 0$. Then the
[collector](collector-current.md), [base](base-current.md) and [emitter](emitter-current.md)
currents satisfy

$$I_C + I_B + I_E \neq 0 :$$

the sum [equals twice the emitter current](collector-add-base-add-emitter-out-eq.md), and
$I_E > 0$. So reading the outward emitter current as an inflow breaks the current balance at every
such operating point.
