# Derivative of the collector current in the base-collector voltage

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers and $V_T > 0$. As a
function of the base-collector voltage, with $V_{BE}$ fixed, the
[collector current](collector-current.md) $I_C$ has at $V_{BC}$ the derivative

$$\frac{\partial I_C}{\partial V_{BC}} = -\frac{I_{SR}}{V_T}\, e^{V_{BC}/V_T} .$$
