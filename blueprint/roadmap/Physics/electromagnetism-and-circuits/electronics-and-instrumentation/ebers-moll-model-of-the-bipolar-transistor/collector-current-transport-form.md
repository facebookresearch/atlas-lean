# Under reciprocity, the collector current takes transport form

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers, $V_T > 0$ and
$\alpha_R > 0$, and suppose the reciprocity relation $\alpha_F I_{SF} = \alpha_R I_{SR}$. Then,
with the transport saturation current $I_S = \alpha_F I_{SF}$ and $u = e^{V_{BE}/V_T}$,
$w = e^{V_{BC}/V_T}$, the [collector current](collector-current.md) is

$$I_C = I_S (u - w) - I_S \frac{1 - \alpha_R}{\alpha_R} (w - 1) .$$

No condition on $\alpha_F$ is needed.
