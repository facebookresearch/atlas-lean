# Derivative of the base current in the base-collector voltage

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $\alpha_R$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers and
$V_T > 0$. As a function of the base-collector voltage, with $V_{BE}$ fixed, the
[base current](base-current.md) $I_B$ has at $V_{BC}$ the derivative

$$\frac{\partial I_B}{\partial V_{BC}} = \frac{(1 - \alpha_R) I_{SR}}{V_T}\, e^{V_{BC}/V_T} .$$
