# At $V_{BE} = 0$, $I_E = \alpha_R I_C$

For all real numbers $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $\alpha_R$, $V_T$ and $V_{BC}$, at zero
base-emitter voltage $V_{BE} = 0$ the forward branch current vanishes, and the
[emitter current](emitter-current.md) is $\alpha_R$ times the
[collector current](collector-current.md):

$$I_E = \alpha_R I_C .$$
