# In reverse-active bias, collector and emitter currents are negative

Let $V_T > 0$, $I_{SF} > 0$, $I_{SR} > 0$, $\alpha_F > 0$ and $\alpha_R > 0$, and let the bias be
reverse active: $V_{BE} < 0$ and $V_{BC} > 0$. Then the [collector current](collector-current.md),
counted into the collector, and the [emitter current](emitter-current.md), counted out of the
emitter, are negative:

$$I_C < 0 , \qquad I_E < 0 ,$$

so current leaves through the collector and enters through the emitter.
