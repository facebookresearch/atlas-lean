# At $V_{BC} = 0$, $I_C = \alpha_F I_E$ and $I_B = (1 - \alpha_F) I_E$

For all real numbers $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $\alpha_R$, $V_T$ and $V_{BE}$, at zero
base-collector voltage $V_{BC} = 0$ the reverse branch current vanishes, and the
[collector](collector-current.md), [base](base-current.md) and [emitter](emitter-current.md)
currents of the Ebers–Moll model satisfy

$$I_C = \alpha_F I_E , \qquad I_B = (1 - \alpha_F) I_E .$$
