# The collector and base currents add up to the emitter current

For all real numbers $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_F$, $\alpha_R$, $V_T$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$, the
[collector current](collector-current.md) $I_C$, the [base current](base-current.md) $I_B$ and
the [emitter current](emitter-current.md) $I_E$ of the Ebers–Moll model satisfy

$$I_C + I_B - I_E = 0 .$$

This is Kirchhoff's current law for the transistor with $I_C$ and $I_B$ counted into the device
and $I_E$ out of it: the current that enters through the collector and the base leaves through
the emitter. The identity holds for all real inputs, $V_T = 0$ included.
