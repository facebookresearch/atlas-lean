# Derivative of the emitter current in the base-emitter voltage

Let $I_{SF}$, $I_{SR}$, $\alpha_R$, $V_{BE}$ and $V_{BC}$ be real numbers and $V_T > 0$. As a
function of the base-emitter voltage, with $V_{BC}$ fixed, the
[emitter current](emitter-current.md) $I_E$ has at $V_{BE}$ the derivative

$$\frac{\partial I_E}{\partial V_{BE}} = \frac{I_{SF}}{V_T}\, e^{V_{BE}/V_T} .$$
