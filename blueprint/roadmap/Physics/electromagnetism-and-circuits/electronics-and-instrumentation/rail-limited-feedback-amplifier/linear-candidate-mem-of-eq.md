# A fixed point equal to the linear candidate lies between the rails

Let $A$, $\beta$ and $v_i$ be real numbers and $v_{\min} \le v_{\max}$. If $v_o$ is a fixed point of
the [rail-limited feedback map](rail-limited-map.md) and $v_o = c$ for the
[linear candidate](linear-candidate.md) $c = A v_i/(1 + A\beta)$, then
$v_{\min} \le c \le v_{\max}$. No condition on $A$ or $\beta$ is needed, since every fixed point
lies between ordered rails.
