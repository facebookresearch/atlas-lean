# Values at or below $v_{\min}$ clamp to $v_{\min}$

For all real numbers $v_{\min}$, $v_{\max}$ and $x$ with $x \le v_{\min}$, the
[rail clamp](rail-clamp.md) gives $\operatorname{clamp}(v_{\min}, v_{\max}, x) = v_{\min}$. No
ordering of the rails is needed; in particular $x = v_{\min}$ gives $v_{\min}$.
