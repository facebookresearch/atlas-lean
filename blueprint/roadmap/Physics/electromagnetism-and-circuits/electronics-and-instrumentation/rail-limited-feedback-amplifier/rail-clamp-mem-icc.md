# For ordered rails, the clamp lies in the rail interval

For real numbers $v_{\min} \le v_{\max}$ and every real $x$, the [rail clamp](rail-clamp.md)
satisfies $v_{\min} \le \operatorname{clamp}(v_{\min}, v_{\max}, x) \le v_{\max}$. The ordering
matters: for $v_{\min} > v_{\max}$ the interval is empty and the clamp equals $v_{\min}$.
