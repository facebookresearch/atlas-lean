# Poisson mean of the photoelectron count, clamped at zero

For real $\eta$, $P$, $h$, $\nu$ and a window length $T$, the count mean is the nonnegative real
number

$$\max(\lambda T, 0), \qquad \lambda = \frac{\eta P}{h \nu},$$

with $\lambda$ the [mean photoelectron rate](photon-arrival-rate.md). For physical inputs
($\eta \ge 0$, $P \ge 0$, $h, \nu > 0$ and $T > 0$) it is
[exactly $\lambda T$](coe-photon-count-mean.md), the mean number of photoelectrons counted in the
window; a negative product $\lambda T$ from unphysical inputs is replaced by $0$.
