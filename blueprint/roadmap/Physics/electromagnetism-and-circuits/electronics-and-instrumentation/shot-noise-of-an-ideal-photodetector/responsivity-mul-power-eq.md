# Responsivity times power equals the charge flux

For all real $\eta$, $P$, $q$, $h$ and $\nu$, the [responsivity](responsivity.md)
$R = \eta |q| / (h \nu)$ times the optical power $P$ is the charge flux $|q| \lambda$, with
$\lambda = \eta P / (h \nu)$ the [mean photoelectron rate](photon-arrival-rate.md):

$$R P = |q|\, \lambda.$$

When $h \nu = 0$ both sides are $0$ (convention $x / 0 = 0$). The identity holds for all real
inputs; it reads as the charge flux of the photoelectrons in the physical case $0 \le \eta \le 1$,
$P \ge 0$ and $h, \nu > 0$.
