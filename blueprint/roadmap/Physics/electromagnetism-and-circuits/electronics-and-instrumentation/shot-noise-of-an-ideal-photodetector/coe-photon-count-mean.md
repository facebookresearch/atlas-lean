# For physical inputs the clamped count mean equals $\lambda T$

If $\eta \ge 0$, $P \ge 0$, $h > 0$, $\nu > 0$ and $T > 0$, the
[clamped count mean](photon-count-mean.md) $\max(\lambda T, 0)$ is $\lambda T$, with
$\lambda = \eta P / (h \nu)$ the [mean photoelectron rate](photon-arrival-rate.md): under these
conditions $\lambda T \ge 0$.
