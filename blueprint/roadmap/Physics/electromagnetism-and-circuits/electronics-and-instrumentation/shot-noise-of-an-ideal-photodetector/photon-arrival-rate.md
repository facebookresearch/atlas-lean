# Mean photoelectron rate of an ideal detector

For real numbers $\eta$ (quantum efficiency), $P$ (optical power), $h$ and $\nu$ (photon energy
$h\nu$), the mean photoelectron arrival rate of an ideal detector is

$$\lambda = \frac{\eta P}{h \nu}.$$

With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, $\lambda = 0$ when $h \nu = 0$. In the physical reading
$0 \le \eta \le 1$, $P \ge 0$, $h > 0$ and $\nu > 0$; the definition takes any real inputs.
