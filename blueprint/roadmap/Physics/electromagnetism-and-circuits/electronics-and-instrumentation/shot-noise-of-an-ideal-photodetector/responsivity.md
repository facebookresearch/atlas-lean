# Ideal responsivity of a photodetector

For real numbers $\eta$ (quantum efficiency), $q$ (carrier charge), $h$ and $\nu$ (photon energy
$h\nu$), the ideal responsivity, the current per unit optical power, is

$$R = \frac{\eta\, |q|}{h \nu}.$$

With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, $R = 0$ when $h \nu = 0$. In the physical reading
$0 \le \eta \le 1$, $h > 0$ and $\nu > 0$.
