# Resistance of a uniform bar

For reals `σ`, `A` and `ℓ` and a proof that `σ > 0`, `R = ℓ / (σ A)`. With the convention
`x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `A = 0`. It is a physical resistance, [positive](resistance-pos.md) and
[reciprocal to the conductance](conductance-mul-resistance.md), on the domain `σ, A, ℓ > 0`.
