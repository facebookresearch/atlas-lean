# A nonuniform field breaks the constant-field shortcut

Let `S ⊆ ℝ²` be measurable with Lebesgue measure `A > 0`, and `σ > 0`. Then `∫₀¹ 2x dx = 1`, but the
[terminal current](terminal-current.md) of the current density `σ · (2 · 0) = 0`, the flux at
`x = 0` of the field `E(x) = 2x` on a unit-length bar, is not `(σ A / 1) · ∫₀¹ 2x dx = σ A`: a
nonuniform field needs the integral, not the constant-field value.
