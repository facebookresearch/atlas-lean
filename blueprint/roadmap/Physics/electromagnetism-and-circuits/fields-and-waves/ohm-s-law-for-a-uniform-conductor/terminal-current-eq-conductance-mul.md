# Terminal current of a uniform ohmic bar is (σ A / ℓ) V

Let `S ⊆ ℝ²` be a measurable cross-section of Lebesgue measure (area) `A > 0`, let `ℓ > 0` and `V`
be real, and let `E, J : ℝ² → ℝ` satisfy `E = V / ℓ` (the
[uniform axial field](uniform-axial-field.md)) and the local Ohm law `J = σ E` at every point of
`S`, with `σ ≥ 0`. Then the [terminal current](terminal-current.md) `I = ∫_S J` is
`I = (σ A / ℓ) V`, the [conductance](conductance.md) `σ A / ℓ` times the voltage.
