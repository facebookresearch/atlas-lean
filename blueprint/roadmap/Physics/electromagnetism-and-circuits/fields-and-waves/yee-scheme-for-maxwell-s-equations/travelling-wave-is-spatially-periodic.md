# With k = 2π m / L the travelling wave has spatial period L

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, reals `A` and `L ≠ 0`, and an
integer `m`, the [travelling wave](travelling-wave-e.md) `E_y` and its
[magnetic field](travelling-wave-h.md) `H_z` with `k = 2π m / L` are periodic in `x` with period
`L`.
