# The unit travelling wave is not periodic with period 1

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, the
[travelling wave](travelling-wave-e.md) with `A = 1` and `k = 1` and its
[magnetic field](travelling-wave-h.md) are not periodic in `x` with period `1`, so the
[error bound](periodic-yee-maxwell-error-le.md) with `L = 1` does not apply to it.
