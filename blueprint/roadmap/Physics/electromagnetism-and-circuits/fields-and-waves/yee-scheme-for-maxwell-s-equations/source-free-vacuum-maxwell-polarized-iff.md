# Polarized vacuum Maxwell equations reduce to two component equations

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0` and all `E_y, H_z : ℝ² → ℝ`, the
[polarized fields](polarized-electric-field.md) `(0, E_y, 0)` and
[`(0, 0, μ₀ H_z)`](polarized-magnetic-field.md) satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at the free-space speed of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)` if and only if, for all `t` and `x`,
`∂_t E_y = −(1 / ε₀) ∂_x H_z` and `∂_t H_z = −(1 / μ₀) ∂_x E_y` (one-variable derivatives, each `0`
where it does not exist).
