# Matching travelling magnetic field H_z

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0` and reals `A` and `k`,
`H_z(t, x) = A √(ε₀ / μ₀) cos(k (x − c t))`, with the free-space speed of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`;
the vacuum impedance is `√(μ₀ / ε₀)`. With the [travelling wave](travelling-wave-e.md)
`E_y = A cos(k (x − c t))` it forms the pair that
[satisfies the source-free vacuum Maxwell equations](travelling-wave-source-free-vacuum-maxwe.md).
