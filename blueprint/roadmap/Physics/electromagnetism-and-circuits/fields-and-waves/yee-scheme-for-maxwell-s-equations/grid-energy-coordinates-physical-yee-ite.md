# In energy coordinates the Yee iterates are leapfrog iterates

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, all reals `h` and `dt`, initial
grid fields `E0, H0 ∈ ℝᴺ` and every `n`, the [energy coordinates](grid-energy-coordinates.md) of the
`n`-th [physical Yee iterate](physical-yee-iterate.md) are the `n`-th iterate of the
[leapfrog step](grid-energy-coordinates-physical-yee-ste.md) at the free-space speed of light
`c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)` from `(√ε₀ E0, −√μ₀ H0)`.
