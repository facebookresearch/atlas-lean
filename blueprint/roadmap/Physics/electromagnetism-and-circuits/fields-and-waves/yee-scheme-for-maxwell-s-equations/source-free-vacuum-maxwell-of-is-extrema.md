# Component vacuum Maxwell equations from the extremum condition

Consider free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, with the free-space speed
of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`, and let `A` be an infinitely differentiable electromagnetic potential
that is an extremum of the free-space Lagrangian with zero current. If the electric and magnetic
fields of `A` have components `E(s, q)_i = FE(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)_i` and
`B(s, q)_i = FB(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)_i` at every time `s` and point `q`, then `FE` and `FB` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at `c`.
