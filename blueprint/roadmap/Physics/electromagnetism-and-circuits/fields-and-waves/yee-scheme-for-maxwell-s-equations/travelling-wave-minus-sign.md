# For the travelling wave v = −u, and the opposite sign fails

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0` and all reals `A` and `k`, the
energy coordinates of the [travelling wave](travelling-wave-e.md) satisfy `v = −u`, with the energy
coordinates [`u = √ε₀ E_y`](energy-u.md) and [`v = −√μ₀ H_z`](energy-v.md). If `A ≠ 0` and `k ≠ 0`,
the pair `(u, +√μ₀ H_z)` does not satisfy the wave system `u_t = c v_x`, `v_t = c u_x` at the
free-space speed of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`.
