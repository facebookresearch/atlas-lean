# The squared pair norm of the energy coordinates is twice the energy

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, every real `h` and grid fields
`E, H ∈ ℝᴺ`, `h ∑_j (√ε₀ E_j)² + h ∑_j (−√μ₀ H_j)² = 2 · h ∑_j (ε₀ E_j² + μ₀ H_j²) / 2`: the squared
pair norm of the [energy coordinates](grid-energy-coordinates.md) is twice the
[discrete energy](grid-field-energy.md).
