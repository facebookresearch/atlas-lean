# Third-derivative bounds of the polarized fields

Reals `MEt`, `MHt`, `MEx` and `MHx` bound the third derivatives of `E_y, H_z : ℝ² → ℝ` if, at every
`(t, x)`, `|∂³_t E_y| ≤ MEt`, `|∂³_t H_z| ≤ MHt`, `|∂³_x E_y| ≤ MEx` and `|∂³_x H_z| ≤ MHx`, the
third derivatives being iterated one-variable derivatives (each derivative `0` where it does not
exist).
