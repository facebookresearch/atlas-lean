# Energy coordinates of a polarized vacuum field solve the wave system

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0` and `E_y, H_z : ℝ² → ℝ` such
that the [polarized fields](polarized-electric-field.md) `(0, E_y, 0)` and
[`(0, 0, μ₀ H_z)`](polarized-magnetic-field.md) satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at the free-space speed of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`, the energy coordinates
[`u = √ε₀ E_y`](energy-u.md) and [`v = −√μ₀ H_z`](energy-v.md) satisfy `u_t = c v_x` and
`v_t = c u_x` at every `(t, x)`.
