# The travelling wave satisfies the vacuum Maxwell equations

For free space with permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0` and all reals `A` and `k`, the
polarized fields of the [travelling wave](travelling-wave-e.md) `E_y` and its
[magnetic field](travelling-wave-h.md) `H_z` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at the free-space speed of light `c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`; and if `A ≠ 0`, then `E_y(0, 0) ≠ 0`.
