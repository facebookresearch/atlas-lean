# Forward propagation constant of a plane wave in a conductor

For real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ`, the
propagation constant is the complex number `γ = α + iβ`, with the
[attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md) `α` and the [phase constant](phase-constant.md) `β`.
With the time dependence `e^{+iωt}`, a wave travelling in the positive `z` direction varies as
`e^{-γz}`. In the physical reading `ε, μ, ω > 0` and `σ ≥ 0`.
