# Squared propagation equation in a conducting medium

For real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ`, a complex
number `γ` satisfies the squared propagation equation when

`γ² = -ω²με + iωμσ`.

This is the equation of a plane wave `e^{+iωt} e^{-γz}` in a homogeneous medium with these
parameters. If `γ` satisfies it, so does `-γ`. For `ε, μ, ω > 0` and `σ ≥ 0` the
[forward propagation constant](propagation-constant.md) satisfies it with `α ≥ 0` and `β > 0`
([verification](dispersion-verification.md)), and every root with positive imaginary part has real
part `α` ([forward root](forward-root-re-eq-attenuation-constant.md)).
