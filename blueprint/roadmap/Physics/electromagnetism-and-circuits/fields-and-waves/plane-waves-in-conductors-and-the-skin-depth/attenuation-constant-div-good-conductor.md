# The normalized attenuation is √(√(1 + ρ²) − ρ)

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0`, the [attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md)
`α = ω √(μ ε / 2) √(√(1 + (σ / (ω ε))²) − 1)` and the
[good-conductor attenuation](good-conductor-attenuation.md) `α0 = √(ω μ σ / 2)` satisfy
`α / α0 = √(√(1 + ρ²) − ρ)`, where `ρ = ω ε / σ` is the [conduction ratio](conduction-ratio.md).
