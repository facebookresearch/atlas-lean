# The propagation constant is α + iβ for all real parameters

For all real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ`, the
[propagation constant](propagation-constant.md) is `γ = α + iβ`, with the
[attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md) `α` and the [phase constant](phase-constant.md) `β`.
