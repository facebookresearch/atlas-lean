# For ρ < 1 the skin depth is at most δ0 / (1 − ρ)

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0` with [conduction ratio](conduction-ratio.md) `ρ = ω ε / σ`
`< 1`, the [skin depth](skin-depth.md) `δ = 1 / α` satisfies `δ ≤ δ0 / (1 − ρ)`, where
`δ0 = 1 / √(ω μ σ / 2)` is the [good-conductor skin depth](good-conductor-skin-depth.md).
