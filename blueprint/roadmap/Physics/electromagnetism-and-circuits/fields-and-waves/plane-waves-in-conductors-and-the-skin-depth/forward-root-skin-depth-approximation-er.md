# Skin-depth error bounds for a forward root of the dispersion equation

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0`, let `γ` be a complex root of `γ² = −ω² μ ε + i ω μ σ`
with `Im γ > 0`. Then `0 ≤ 1 − Re γ / α0 ≤ ρ`, and if `0 ≤ ρ < 1`, `δ0 ≤ 1 / Re γ ≤ δ0 / (1 − ρ)`;
here `ρ = ω ε / σ`, [good-conductor attenuation](good-conductor-attenuation.md) `α0 = √(ω μ σ / 2)`
and `δ0 = 1 / α0`.
