# Error bounds of the good-conductor skin-depth approximation

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0`: the
[relative attenuation error](relative-attenuation-error.md) `1 − α / α0` lies in `[0, ρ]`, where
`ρ = ω ε / σ` is the [conduction ratio](conduction-ratio.md); if `0 ≤ ρ < 1`, the
[skin depth](skin-depth.md) `δ = 1 / α` lies in `[δ0, δ0 / (1 − ρ)]`; and the
[good-conductor skin depth](good-conductor-skin-depth.md) is `δ0 = √(2 / (ω μ σ))`.
