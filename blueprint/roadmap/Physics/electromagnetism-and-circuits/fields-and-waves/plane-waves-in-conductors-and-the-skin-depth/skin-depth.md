# Skin depth of a conducting medium

For real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ`, the skin
depth is `δ = 1/α`, with `α` the [attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md). With the
convention `1/0 = 0` it is `0` when `α = 0`, in particular for a lossless medium (`σ = 0`), whose
physical skin depth is infinite. It is meaningful when `α > 0`, which holds for `ε, μ, ω > 0` and
`σ > 0` ([positive attenuation](attenuation-constant-pos-of-sigma-pos.md)).
