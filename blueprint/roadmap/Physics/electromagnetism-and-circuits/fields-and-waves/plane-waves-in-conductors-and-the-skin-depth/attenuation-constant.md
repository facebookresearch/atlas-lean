# Attenuation constant of a plane wave in a conductor

For real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ`, the
attenuation constant is

`α = ω √(με/2) √(√(1 + (σ/(ωε))²) - 1)`.

It is defined for all real inputs, with the conventions `x/0 = 0` and `√x = 0` for `x < 0`: it is
`0` when `ωε = 0` or `με < 0`. In the physical reading `ε, μ, ω > 0` and `σ ≥ 0`.
