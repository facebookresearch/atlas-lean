# At unit conduction ratio, α / α0 = √(√2 − 1)

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ = ω ε` (so `ρ = 1`), the
[attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md) `α = ω √(μ ε / 2) √(√(1 + (σ / (ω ε))²) − 1)` and
the [good-conductor attenuation](good-conductor-attenuation.md) `α0 = √(ω μ σ / 2)` satisfy
`α / α0 = √(√2 − 1)`, about `0.644`; the relative error, about `0.356`, is well inside the bound
`ρ = 1`.
