# At σ = 0 the plane wave is the real part of the conjugate phasor

Let `ε > 0`, `μ > 0` and `ω > 0`. Let `β₀` be the [phase constant](phase-constant.md) at `σ = 0`.
For all real `E₀`, `x` and `t`, the
[plane wave](../electrostatics-plane-waves-and-fresnel-coefficie/plane-wave.md) `E₀ cos(β₀x - ωt)`
satisfies

`E₀ cos(β₀x - ωt) = Re(e^{i(β₀x - ωt)} E₀)`.

The phasor `e^{i(β₀x - ωt)} E₀` is the complex conjugate of the forward phasor `e^{i(ωt - β₀x)} E₀`
of the convention `e^{+iωt}`; both have the same real part because cosine is even, so this identity
does not test the sign convention. The forward phasor is the subject of
[the plane wave as the real part of the forward phasor](plane-wave-eq-re-forward-phasor-at-zero.md).
