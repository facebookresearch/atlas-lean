# For σ > 0 the opposite-sign propagation equation differs

Let `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0`. Then

`-ω²με - iωμσ ≠ -ω²με + iωμσ`:

the right side of the [squared propagation equation](satisfies-dispersion-equation.md) changes when
the sign of the conductive term is reversed, as happens when the time dependence `e^{-iωt}` is used
without changing the other signs.
