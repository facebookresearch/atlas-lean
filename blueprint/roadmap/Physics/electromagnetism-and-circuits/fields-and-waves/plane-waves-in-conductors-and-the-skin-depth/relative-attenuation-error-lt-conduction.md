# The relative attenuation error is below the conduction ratio

For `ε > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ω > 0` and `σ > 0`, the
[relative attenuation error](relative-attenuation-error.md) `1 − α / α0` is strictly smaller than
the [conduction ratio](conduction-ratio.md) `ρ = ω ε / σ`: the bound `ρ` is never attained.
