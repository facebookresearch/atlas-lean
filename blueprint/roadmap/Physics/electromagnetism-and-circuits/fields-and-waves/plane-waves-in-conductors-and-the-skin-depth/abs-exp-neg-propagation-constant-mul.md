# The travelling phasor has modulus e^{−αz}

For all real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ` and
every real `z`, the [propagation constant](propagation-constant.md) `γ = α + iβ` satisfies
`|e^{-γz}| = e^{-αz}`, with `α` the [attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md). No sign
condition is needed for this identity. The modulus decays in `z` when `α > 0`, which holds for
`ε, μ, ω > 0` and `σ > 0` ([positive attenuation](attenuation-constant-pos-of-sigma-pos.md)); for
`ε, μ, ω > 0` and `σ = 0` it is constant, since then `α = 0`
([zero conductivity](attenuation-constant-zero-sigma.md)).
