# The travelling phasor decays in modulus at the attenuation rate α

For all real permittivity `ε`, permeability `μ`, angular frequency `ω` and conductivity `σ` and
every real `z`, the [propagation constant](propagation-constant.md) `γ = α + iβ` satisfies
`|e^{-γz}| = e^{-αz}`: the travelling phasor decays at the rate of the
[attenuation constant](attenuation-constant.md). No sign condition is needed.
