# The exponential mode satisfies vacuum Maxwell under its dispersion

For a speed of light `c > 0` and reals `kc`, `α` and `ω` with `ω² = c² (kc² − α²)`, the separable
fields of the profile `a(x) = sin(kc x)` and the mode `f(t, z) = cos(ω t) exp(−α z)` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md) at `c`, for
every real `α`; the mode is evanescent for [`α > 0`](evanescent-mode-decay.md).
