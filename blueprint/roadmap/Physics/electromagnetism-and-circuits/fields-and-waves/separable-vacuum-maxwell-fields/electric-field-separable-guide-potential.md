# Where f is differentiable in time, E has the single component −a ∂f/∂t

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light, `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ × ℝ → ℝ`, and let `A` be the
[separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md), with scalar potential `φ = 0` and vector
potential `(0, a(x) f(t, z), 0)` at time `t` and position `(x, y, z)`. Let `t` be a time and
`(x, y, z)` a position such that `s ↦ f(s, z)` is differentiable at `t`. Then the electric field
`E = -∇φ - ∂A/∂t` there is

`E(t, x, y, z) = (0, -a(x) ∂f/∂t(t, z), 0)`.
