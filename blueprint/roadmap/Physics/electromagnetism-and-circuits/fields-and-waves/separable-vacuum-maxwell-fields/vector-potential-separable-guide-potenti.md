# Vector potential of the separable guide potential in physical time

For every speed of light `c > 0`, all functions `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ × ℝ → ℝ`, every time `t` and
every position `(x, y, z)`, the vector potential of the
[separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md) is `(0, a(x) f(t, z), 0)`.
