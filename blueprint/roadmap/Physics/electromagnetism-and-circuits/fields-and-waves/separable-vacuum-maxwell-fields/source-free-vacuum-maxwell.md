# Source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form

For a speed of light `c > 0` and fields `E` and `B` of time and three space coordinates with values
in `ℝ³`, at every point `(t, x, y, z)`: `div E = 0`, `div B = 0`, `curl E = −∂_t B` and
`curl B = c⁻² ∂_t E`, with the coordinate [divergence](space-divergence.md), [curl](space-curl.md)
and [time derivatives](time-derivative-component.md).
