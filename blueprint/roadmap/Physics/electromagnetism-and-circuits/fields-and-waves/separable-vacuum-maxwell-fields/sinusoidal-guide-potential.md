# Sinusoidal example of the separable guide potential

For a speed of light `c > 0`, the sinusoidal guide potential is the
[separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md) with `a(x) = sin x` and
`f(t, z) = cos(2t - 3z)`: vector potential `(0, sin x cos(2t - 3z), 0)` and
[scalar potential](scalar-potential-separable-guide-potenti.md) `0`.
