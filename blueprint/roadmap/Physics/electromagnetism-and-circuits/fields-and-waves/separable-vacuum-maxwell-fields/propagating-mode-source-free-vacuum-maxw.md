# The travelling cosine mode satisfies vacuum Maxwell under dispersion

For a speed of light `c > 0` and reals `kc`, `β` and `ω` with `ω² = c² (β² + kc²)`, the separable
fields of the profile `a(x) = sin(kc x)` and the mode `f(t, z) = cos(ω t − β z)` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md) at `c`.
