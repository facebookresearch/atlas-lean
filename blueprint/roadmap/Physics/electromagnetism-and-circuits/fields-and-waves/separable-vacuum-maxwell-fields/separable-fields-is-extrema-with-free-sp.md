# Profile and free-space mode equations make guide potentials extremal

Let free space have permittivity `ε₀ > 0` and permeability `μ₀ > 0`, with speed of light
`c = 1 / √(ε₀ μ₀)`. For a real `kc` and infinitely differentiable `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ² → ℝ`
(jointly in `(t, z)`) satisfying the [profile equation](profile-equation.md) `a'' = −kc² a` and the
[mode equation](mode-equation.md) `f_tt = c² (f_zz − kc² f)` at speed `c`: the electric and magnetic
fields of the [separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md) (vector potential
`A_y = a(x) f(t, z)`, the other components and the scalar potential zero) built with `c` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in field form](source-free-vacuum-maxwell-fields.md) at `c`;
the potential is an extremum of the free-space Lagrangian with zero current (its Lagrangian gradient
vanishes); and the potential and the zero current are infinitely differentiable.
