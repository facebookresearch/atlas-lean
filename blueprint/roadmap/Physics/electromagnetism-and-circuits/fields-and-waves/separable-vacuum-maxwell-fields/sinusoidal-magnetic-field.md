# Magnetic field of the sinusoidal guide potential

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light and `A` the
[sinusoidal guide potential](sinusoidal-guide-potential.md), with vector potential
`(0, sin x cos(2t - 3z), 0)` and scalar potential `0`. At every time `t` and position `(x, y, z)`
its magnetic field is

`B(t, x, y, z) = (-3 sin x sin(2t - 3z), 0, cos x cos(2t - 3z))`.
