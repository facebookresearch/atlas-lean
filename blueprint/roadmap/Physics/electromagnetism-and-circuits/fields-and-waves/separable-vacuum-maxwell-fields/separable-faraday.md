# Faraday's law for the separable fields of a smooth mode

For every `a : ℝ → ℝ` and every infinitely differentiable `f : ℝ² → ℝ`, the
[separable fields](separable-electric-field.md) `E = (0, −a(x) f_t(t, z), 0)` and
[`B = (−a(x) f_z(t, z), 0, a'(x) f(t, z))`](separable-magnetic-field.md) satisfy `curl E = −∂_t B`
at every point; the smoothness of `f` gives the symmetry of mixed partial derivatives.
