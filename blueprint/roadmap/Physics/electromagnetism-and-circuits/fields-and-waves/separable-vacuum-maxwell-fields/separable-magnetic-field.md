# Separable magnetic field

For `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ² → ℝ`, the field `B(t, x, y, z) = (−a(x) f_z(t, z), 0, a'(x) f(t, z))` of
time and three space coordinates, with `f_z` the [guide-coordinate partial derivative](partial-z.md)
and `a'` the derivative of `a` (`0` where `a` is not differentiable).
