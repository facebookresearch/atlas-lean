# Components of the electric field of the separable guide potential

For a speed of light `c > 0` and infinitely differentiable `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ² → ℝ` (jointly in
`(t, z)`), at every time `s` and point `q`, each component of the electric field of the
[separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md) (vector potential `A_y = a(x) f(t, z)`,
the other components and the scalar potential zero) equals that component of the
[separable electric field](separable-electric-field.md) at `(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)`, that is, of
`(0, −a(q₀) f_t(s, q₂), 0)`.
