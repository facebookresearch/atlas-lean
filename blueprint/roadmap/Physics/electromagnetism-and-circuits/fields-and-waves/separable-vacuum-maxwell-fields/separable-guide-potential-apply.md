# Components of the separable guide potential at a spacetime point

For a speed of light `c > 0` and functions `a : ℝ → ℝ` and `f : ℝ × ℝ → ℝ`, the
[separable guide potential](separable-guide-potential.md) at the spacetime point with coordinates
`(x⁰, x¹, x², x³)` has `y` component `a(x¹) f(x⁰/c, x³)` and every other component, the time
component included, equal to `0`: the time argument of `f` is `x⁰/c`.
