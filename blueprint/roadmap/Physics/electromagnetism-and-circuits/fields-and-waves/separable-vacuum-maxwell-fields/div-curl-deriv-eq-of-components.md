# Divergence, curl and time derivative from components

Let `G : Time → Space → ℝ³` be differentiable jointly in time and space, with components
`G(s, q)_i = F(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)_i` for a field `F` of time and three space coordinates. Then at every
time `t` and point `p`, the divergence of `G(t)` at `p`, each component of its curl and each
component of the time derivative of `s ↦ G(s, p)` equal the coordinate
[divergence](space-divergence.md), [curl](space-curl.md) and
[time derivatives](time-derivative-component.md) of `F` at `(t, p₀, p₁, p₂)`.
