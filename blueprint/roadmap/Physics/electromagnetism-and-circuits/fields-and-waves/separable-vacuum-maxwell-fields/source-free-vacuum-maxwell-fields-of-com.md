# The component form of vacuum Maxwell gives the field form

For a speed of light `c > 0` and fields `E, B : Time → Space → ℝ³` that are differentiable jointly
in time and space, with components `E(s, q)_i = FE(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)_i` and
`B(s, q)_i = FB(s, q₀, q₁, q₂)_i`: if `FE` and `FB` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in component form](source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md) at `c`, then
`E` and `B` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations in field form](source-free-vacuum-maxwell-fields.md) at `c`.
