# Mode equation of a separable guided wave

A function `f : ℝ² → ℝ` satisfies the mode equation with reals `c0` and `kc` if, for all `t` and
`z`, the derivative in `t` of the [time partial derivative](partial-time.md) `f_t` equals `c0²`
times the derivative in `z` of the [guide-coordinate partial derivative](partial-z.md) `f_z` minus
`c0² kc² f(t, z)`: `f_tt = c0² (f_zz − kc² f)`, each derivative being `0` where it does not exist.
