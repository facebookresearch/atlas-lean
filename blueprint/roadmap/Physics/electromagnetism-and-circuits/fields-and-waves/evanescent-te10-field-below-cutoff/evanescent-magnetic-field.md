# Magnetic field of the evanescent TE10 mode

For a real guide width `a`, amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and rate `κ`, the evanescent magnetic
field is the
[separable magnetic field](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/separable-magnetic-field.md) of the
[TE10 profile](te10-profile.md) `sin(k_c x)` and the [longitudinal factor](evanescent-mode.md) `f`:

`B(t, x, y, z) = (-sin(k_c x) ∂f/∂z(t, z), 0, k_c cos(k_c x) f(t, z))`,

whose [components](evanescent-magnetic-field-apply.md) are given explicitly.
