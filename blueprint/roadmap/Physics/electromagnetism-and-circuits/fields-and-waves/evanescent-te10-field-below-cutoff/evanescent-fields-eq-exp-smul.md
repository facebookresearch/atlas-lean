# The evanescent fields are e^−κz times their values at z = 0

For all real `a`, `E₀`, `ω`, `κ`, every time `t` and point `(x, y, z)`, the
[evanescent electric field](evanescent-electric-field.md) and the
[evanescent magnetic field](evanescent-magnetic-field.md) with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and
rate `κ` satisfy

`E(t, x, y, z) = e^{-κz} E(t, x, y, 0)` and `B(t, x, y, z) = e^{-κz} B(t, x, y, 0)`.
