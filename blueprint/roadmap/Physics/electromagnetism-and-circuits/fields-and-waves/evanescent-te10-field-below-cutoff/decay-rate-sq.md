# Strictly below cutoff the decay rate satisfies κ² = kc² − ω²/c²

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a` be real and `0 < ω < c k_c`, strictly below cutoff, with
the [cutoff wavenumber](cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. Then the [decay rate](decay-rate.md)
`κ = √(k_c² - ω²/c²)` satisfies `κ² = k_c² - ω²/c²`.
