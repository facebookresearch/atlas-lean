# A field frozen at a time, as a function of position

For a field `F` assigning a vector `F(t, x, y, z) ∈ ℝ³` to each time and point, and a time `t`, the
field at time `t` is the map `p ↦ F(t, p_0, p_1, p_2)` from `ℝ³` to `ℝ³`, the form on which the
[PEC trace conditions](../te10-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide/rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) are
stated.

Both the
[propagating TE10 fields](../te10-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide/te10-rectangular-guide-pectraces.md)
and the [evanescent TE10 fields](evanescent-rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) are frozen this way to
state their wall conditions.
