# Components of the evanescent electric field for ω ≠ 0

Let `a`, `E₀`, `κ` be real and `ω ≠ 0`. At every time `t` and point `(x, y, z)` the
[evanescent electric field](evanescent-electric-field.md) is

`E(t, x, y, z) = (0, E₀ sin(k_c x) e^{-κz} cos(ωt), 0)`,

with the [cutoff wavenumber](cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. For `ω = 0` the field is `0`
instead.
