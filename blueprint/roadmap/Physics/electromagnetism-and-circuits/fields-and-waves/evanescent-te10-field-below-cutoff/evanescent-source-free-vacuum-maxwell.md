# Strictly below cutoff the evanescent fields solve vacuum Maxwell

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a` be real and `0 < ω < c k_c`, strictly below cutoff, with
the [cutoff wavenumber](cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. For every real `E₀`, the
[evanescent electric field](evanescent-electric-field.md) and the
[evanescent magnetic field](evanescent-magnetic-field.md) with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and
with the [decay rate](decay-rate.md) `κ = √(k_c² - ω²/c²)` as rate satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at every time and point.
