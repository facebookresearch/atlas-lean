# For κ > 0 every evanescent field component tends to zero as z → ∞

Let `κ > 0`, and let `a`, `E₀` and `ω` be real. For every time `t`, every `x`, `y` and every
component `i`, the components of the [evanescent electric field](evanescent-electric-field.md) and
the [evanescent magnetic field](evanescent-magnetic-field.md) with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and
rate `κ` tend to `0` as `z → +∞`.
