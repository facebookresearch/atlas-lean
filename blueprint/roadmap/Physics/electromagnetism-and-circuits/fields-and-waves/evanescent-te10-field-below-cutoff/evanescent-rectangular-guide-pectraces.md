# For a, b > 0 the evanescent fields satisfy the PEC wall conditions

Let `a > 0` and `b > 0`, and let `E₀`, `ω` and `κ` be real; the rate is arbitrary. At every time
`t`, the [evanescent electric field](evanescent-electric-field.md) and the
[evanescent magnetic field](evanescent-magnetic-field.md) with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and
rate `κ`, [frozen at time `t`](field-at-time.md), satisfy the
[PEC trace conditions](../te10-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide/rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) on
the four side walls `x = 0`, `x = a`, `y = 0` and `y = b` of the guide.
