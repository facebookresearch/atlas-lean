# On the wall y = b the traces are three component equations

For fields `E, B : ℝ³ → ℝ³` (components indexed `0, 1, 2` for `x, y, z`) and all reals `b`, `x` and
`z`, the tangential electric and normal magnetic fields of a `y`-wall vanish at `(x, b, z)` if and
only if `E_x`, `E_z` and `B_y` vanish there.
