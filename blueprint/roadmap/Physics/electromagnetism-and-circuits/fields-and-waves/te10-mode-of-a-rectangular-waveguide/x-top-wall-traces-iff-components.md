# On the wall x = a the traces are three component equations

For fields `E, B : ℝ³ → ℝ³` (components indexed `0, 1, 2` for `x, y, z`) and all reals `a`, `y` and
`z`, the tangential electric and normal magnetic fields of an `x`-wall vanish at `(a, y, z)` if and
only if `E_y`, `E_z` and `B_x` vanish there.
