# A nonnegative propagation constant exists exactly at or above cutoff

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a > 0` and `ω > 0`, with the
[cutoff wavenumber](../evanescent-te10-field-below-cutoff/cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. There is `β ≥ 0` satisfying the
[dispersion relation](te10-dispersion.md) `ω² = c²(k_c² + β²)` if and only if `ω ≥ c k_c`.
