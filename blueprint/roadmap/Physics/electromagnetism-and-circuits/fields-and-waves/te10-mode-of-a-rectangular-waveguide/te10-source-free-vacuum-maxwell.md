# Under the dispersion relation the TE10 fields solve vacuum Maxwell

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. For all real `a`, `E₀`, `ω`, `β` satisfying the
[dispersion relation](te10-dispersion.md) `ω² = c²(k_c² + β²)`, with the
[cutoff wavenumber](cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`, the
[TE10 electric field](te10-electric-field.md) and the [TE10 magnetic field](te10-magnetic-field.md)
with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and propagation constant `β` satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md)
at every time and point, the inside of the guide included.
