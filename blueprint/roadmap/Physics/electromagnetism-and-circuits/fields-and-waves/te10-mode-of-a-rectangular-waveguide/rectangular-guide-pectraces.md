# PEC trace conditions on the four side walls of a rectangular guide

For reals `a`, `b` and fields `E, B : ℝ³ → ℝ³` (components indexed `0, 1, 2` for `x, y, z`), the PEC
trace conditions hold if `a > 0`, `b > 0`, and the tangential electric and normal magnetic fields
vanish on the four side walls: on the walls `x = 0` and `x = a` (points `(0, y, z)` and `(a, y, z)`
with `0 ≤ y ≤ b` and any `z`) the components `E_y`, `E_z` and `B_x` vanish, and on the walls `y = 0`
and `y = b` (points `(x, 0, z)` and `(x, b, z)` with `0 ≤ x ≤ a` and any `z`) the components `E_x`,
`E_z` and `B_y` vanish. The corner lines belong to both adjacent walls. For a nonpositive width the
conditions fail for every field.
