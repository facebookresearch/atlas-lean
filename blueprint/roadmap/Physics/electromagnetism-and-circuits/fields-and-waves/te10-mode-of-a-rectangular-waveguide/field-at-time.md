# A field frozen at a time, as a function of position

For a field `F` assigning a vector `F(t, x, y, z) ∈ ℝ³` to each time and point, and a time `t`, the
field at time `t` is the map `p ↦ F(t, p_0, p_1, p_2)` from `ℝ³` to `ℝ³`, the form on which the
[PEC trace conditions](rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) are stated.
