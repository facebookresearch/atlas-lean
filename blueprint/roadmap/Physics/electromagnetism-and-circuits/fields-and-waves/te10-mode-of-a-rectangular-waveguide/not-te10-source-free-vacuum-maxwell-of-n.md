# Off dispersion, TE10 fields with a > 0 and E₀, ω ≠ 0 violate Maxwell

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a > 0`, `E₀ ≠ 0`, `ω ≠ 0` and `β` real with
`ω² ≠ c²(k_c² + β²)`, `k_c = π/a`. Then the [TE10 electric field](te10-electric-field.md) and the
[TE10 magnetic field](te10-magnetic-field.md) with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and propagation
constant `β` do not satisfy the
[source-free vacuum Maxwell equations](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md).
