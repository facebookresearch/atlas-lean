# On the wall x = 0 the traces are three component equations

For fields `E, B : ℝ³ → ℝ³` (components indexed `0, 1, 2` for `x, y, z`) and all reals `y` and `z`,
the tangential electric and normal magnetic fields of an `x`-wall vanish at `(0, y, z)` if and only
if `E_y`, `E_z` and `B_x` vanish there.
