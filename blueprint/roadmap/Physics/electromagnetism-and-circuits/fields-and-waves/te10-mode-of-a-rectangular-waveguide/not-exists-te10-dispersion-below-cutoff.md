# Below cutoff the TE10 dispersion relation has no real solution

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a > 0` and `0 < ω < c k_c`, with the
[cutoff wavenumber](../evanescent-te10-field-below-cutoff/cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. Then no real `β`, of either sign, satisfies
the [dispersion relation](te10-dispersion.md) `ω² = c²(k_c² + β²)`.
