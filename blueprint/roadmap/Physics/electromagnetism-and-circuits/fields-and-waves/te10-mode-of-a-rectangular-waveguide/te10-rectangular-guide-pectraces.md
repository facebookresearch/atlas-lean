# For widths a, b > 0 the TE10 fields satisfy the PEC wall conditions

Let `a > 0` and `b > 0`, and let `E₀`, `ω` and `β` be real. At every time `t`, the
[TE10 electric field](te10-electric-field.md) and the [TE10 magnetic field](te10-magnetic-field.md)
with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and propagation constant `β`,
[frozen at time `t`](../evanescent-te10-field-below-cutoff/field-at-time.md), satisfy the
[PEC trace conditions](rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) on the four side walls `x = 0`, `x = a`,
`y = 0` and `y = b` of the guide; the guide has no end walls.
