# At cutoff the dispersion relation holds exactly for β = 0

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let `a > 0`, with the [cutoff wavenumber](../evanescent-te10-field-below-cutoff/cutoff-wavenumber.md)
`k_c = π/a`. At the cutoff frequency `ω = c k_c`, a real `β` satisfies the
[dispersion relation](te10-dispersion.md) if and only if `β = 0`.
