# Properties of the propagating TE10 mode of a rectangular guide

Let `c > 0` be the speed of light. Let the guide widths be `a > 0` and `b > 0`, and let `E₀ ≠ 0`,
`ω > 0` and `β ≥ 0` satisfy the [dispersion relation](te10-dispersion.md) `ω² = c²(k_c² + β²)`, with
the [cutoff wavenumber](../evanescent-te10-field-below-cutoff/cutoff-wavenumber.md) `k_c = π/a`. Then, for the
[TE10 electric field](te10-electric-field.md) and the [TE10 magnetic field](te10-magnetic-field.md)
with amplitude `E₀`, frequency `ω` and propagation constant `β`:

- they satisfy the [source-free vacuum Maxwell equations](../separable-vacuum-maxwell-fields/source-free-vacuum-maxwell.md);
- at every time they satisfy the [PEC trace conditions](rectangular-guide-pectraces.md) on the four side
  walls;
- the electric field is not identically zero;
- the frequency is at or above cutoff, `ω ≥ c k_c`, with `β = 0` if and only if `ω = c k_c`;
- `β = √(ω²/c² - k_c²)`, the [propagation constant](propagation-constant.md).
