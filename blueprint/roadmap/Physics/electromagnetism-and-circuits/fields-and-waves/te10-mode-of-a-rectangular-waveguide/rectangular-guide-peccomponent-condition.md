# PEC component conditions on the four side walls of a rectangular guide

For reals `a`, `b` and fields `E, B : ℝ³ → ℝ³` (components indexed `0, 1, 2` for `x, y, z`), the
component conditions hold if `a > 0`, `b > 0`, and on the walls `x = 0` and `x = a` (points
`(0, y, z)` and `(a, y, z)` with `0 ≤ y ≤ b` and any `z`) the components `E_y`, `E_z` and `B_x`
vanish, and on the walls `y = 0` and `y = b` (points `(x, 0, z)` and `(x, b, z)` with `0 ≤ x ≤ a`
and any `z`) the components `E_x`, `E_z` and `B_y` vanish, written as explicit component equations
face by face.
