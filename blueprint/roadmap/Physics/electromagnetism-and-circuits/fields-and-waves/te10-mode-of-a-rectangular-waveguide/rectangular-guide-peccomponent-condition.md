# PEC component conditions on the four side walls of a rectangular guide

For reals $a$, $b$ and fields $E, B : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ (components indexed $0, 1, 2$
for $x, y, z$), the PEC component conditions state that $a > 0$, $b > 0$, and for every $z$:

- at the points $(0, y, z)$ and $(a, y, z)$ with $0 \le y \le b$: $E_y = E_z = B_x = 0$;
- at the points $(x, 0, z)$ and $(x, b, z)$ with $0 \le x \le a$: $E_x = E_z = B_y = 0$.

The other form has its own page: [PEC trace conditions on the four side walls of a rectangular guide](rectangular-guide-pectraces.md).
