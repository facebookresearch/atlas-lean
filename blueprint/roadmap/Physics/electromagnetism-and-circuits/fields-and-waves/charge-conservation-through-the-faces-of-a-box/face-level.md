# Level of a box face along its normal axis

For `a, b ∈ ℝ³` the box is `[a, b] = {x ∈ ℝ³ : a_i ≤ x_i ≤ b_i for i = 0, 1, 2}`, with coordinate
axes indexed `0, 1, 2`. The level of face `f`, with [normal axis](face-axis.md) `k`, is `b_k` for an
[upper face](face-is-upper.md) and `a_k` for a lower face: the face lies in the plane `x_k` equal to
its level. The definition takes any `a` and `b`.
