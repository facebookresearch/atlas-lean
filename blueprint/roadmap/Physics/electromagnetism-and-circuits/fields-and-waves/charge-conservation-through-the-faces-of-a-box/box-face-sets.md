# Face rectangles of a box as subsets of the plane

For `a, b ∈ ℝ³` the box is `[a, b] = {x ∈ ℝ³ : a_i ≤ x_i ≤ b_i for i = 0, 1, 2}`, with coordinate
axes indexed `0, 1, 2`. The face sets of the box are its [parameter rectangles](box-face-domain.md),
seen in `ℝ × ℝ` through `y ↦ (y_0, y_1)`: the parameter domains to which the
[face flux](face-flux.md) applies.
