# The six currents −2, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0 sum to −2

The six real numbers `-2, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0` sum to `-2`. Read as the outward currents of the six faces
of a box, they satisfy the [charge-continuity condition](charge-continuity.md) with charge rate `2`
and not with rate `0`: the enclosed charge grows, and the currents do not sum to zero.
