# With zero side currents, the end currents sum to minus the charge rate

Let `I_0, …, I_5` and `q̇` be real with `I_0 + ⋯ + I_5 = -q̇`, and assume `I_f = 0` for the four
side faces `f = 2, 3, 4, 5` (insulating side faces, an assumption here). Then `I_0 + I_1 = -q̇`: the
balance involves only the two end faces `0` and `1`.
