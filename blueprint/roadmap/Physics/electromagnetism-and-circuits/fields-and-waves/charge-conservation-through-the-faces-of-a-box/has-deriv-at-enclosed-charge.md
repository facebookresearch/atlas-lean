# The enclosed charge has derivative the integral of the density rate

Let `a, b ∈ ℝ³`. Let `ρ, ρ̇ : ℝ × ℝ³ → ℝ` be jointly continuous in time and position, with
`s ↦ ρ(s, x)` differentiable at every time `s` with derivative `ρ̇(s, x)`, for every `x ∈ ℝ³`. Then
the [enclosed charge](enclosed-charge.md) `Q(s) = ∫_{[a, b]} ρ(s, x) dx` is differentiable at every
time `t`, with

`Q'(t) = ∫_{[a, b]} ρ̇(t, x) dx`.
