# Charge-continuity condition for six face fluxes

For six real face fluxes `Φ_0, …, Φ_5` and a real rate `q̇`, the charge-continuity condition is

`Φ_0 + Φ_1 + Φ_2 + Φ_3 + Φ_4 + Φ_5 = -q̇`:

the total outward flux is minus the rate of change `q̇` of the enclosed charge. It is a condition on
the numbers, not a theorem. For the outward fluxes through the faces of a box `[a, b]`, with rate
`q̇ = ∫_{[a, b]} ρ̇`, it
[follows from the local continuity law](charge-continuity-box-outward-flux.md) `ρ̇ + div J = 0` when
`a ≤ b`, `J` is continuous on the box and differentiable inside it, and `ρ̇` is integrable on the
box.
