# Charge-continuity condition for six face fluxes

For six real face fluxes `Φ_0, …, Φ_5` and a real rate `q̇`, the charge-continuity condition is

`Φ_0 + Φ_1 + Φ_2 + Φ_3 + Φ_4 + Φ_5 = -q̇`:

the total outward flux is minus the rate of change `q̇` of the enclosed charge. It is a condition on
the numbers, not a theorem; for the faces of a box it
[follows from the local continuity law](charge-continuity-box-outward-flux.md).
