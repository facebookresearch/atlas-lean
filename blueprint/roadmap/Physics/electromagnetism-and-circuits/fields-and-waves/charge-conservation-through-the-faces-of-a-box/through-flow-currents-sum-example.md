# The six currents −3, 3, 0, 0, 0, 0 sum to zero

The six real numbers `-3, 3, 0, 0, 0, 0` sum to `0`. Read as the outward currents of the six faces
of a box, they describe a through-flow: current `3` enters at face `0` and leaves at face `1`, and
the [charge-continuity condition](charge-continuity.md) holds with charge rate `0`.
