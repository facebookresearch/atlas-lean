# Equal-area end faces with densities −c and c carry opposite fluxes

For parameter domains `D_f ⊆ ℝ²` and densities `j_f : ℝ² → ℝ` indexed by the six faces
`f = 0, …, 5`, the [flux through face `f`](face-flux.md) is `Φ_f = ∫_{D_f} j_f(p) dp`. Let the end
faces `D_0` and `D_1` be measurable, of finite and equal area, and let the densities be constant,
`j_0 = -c` on `D_0` and `j_1 = c` on `D_1`, a uniform flow in the positive direction of the axis
normal to these faces. Then `Φ_1 = -Φ_0`: the outward currents of the two end faces are opposite,
both fluxes being integrals of integrable functions.
