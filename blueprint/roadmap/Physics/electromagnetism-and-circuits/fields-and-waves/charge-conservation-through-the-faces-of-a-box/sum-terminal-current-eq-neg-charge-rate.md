# Currents equal to the face fluxes inherit charge continuity

For parameter domains `D_f ⊆ ℝ²` and densities `j_f : ℝ² → ℝ` indexed by the six faces
`f = 0, …, 5`, the [flux through face `f`](face-flux.md) is `Φ_f = ∫_{D_f} j_f(p) dp`. Let
`I_0, …, I_5` be real with `I_f = Φ_f` for every face, and assume the
[charge-continuity condition](charge-continuity.md) `Φ_0 + ⋯ + Φ_5 = -q̇`. Then
`I_0 + ⋯ + I_5 = -q̇`.
