# Finite-area face: constant density k is integrable, flux area times k

For parameter domains `D_f ⊆ ℝ²` and densities `j_f : ℝ² → ℝ` indexed by the six faces
`f = 0, …, 5`, the [flux through face `f`](face-flux.md) is `Φ_f = ∫_{D_f} j_f(p) dp`. If `D_f` is
measurable with finite area `|D_f|` and `j_f = k` on `D_f`, then `j_f` is integrable on `D_f`, the
real number `|D_f|` is the area (its value in `[0, ∞]` is the same), and `Φ_f = |D_f| k`.
