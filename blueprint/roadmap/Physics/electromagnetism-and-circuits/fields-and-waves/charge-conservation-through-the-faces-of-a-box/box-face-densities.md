# Outward-normal densities of a box on the plane

For `a, b ∈ ℝ³` the box is `[a, b] = {x ∈ ℝ³ : a_i ≤ x_i ≤ b_i for i = 0, 1, 2}`, with coordinate
axes indexed `0, 1, 2`. For a current density `J : ℝ³ → ℝ³`, the face density of face `f` at
`(p_1, p_2) ∈ ℝ × ℝ` is the [outward-normal density](box-normal-density.md) of `J` on face `f` at
the parameter point `(p_1, p_2) ∈ ℝ²`.
