# At zero transmit power and unit gains the ratio form fails

Let `r > 0` and `λ > 0`, and assume the flux `S = 0 · 1 / (4π r²)` (zero transmit power, unit
gains), the aperture `Ae = 1 · λ² / (4π)` and `Pr = S Ae`. Then `Pr / 0 ≠ (λ / (4π r))²`: with the
convention `x / 0 = 0` the ratio is `0`, not the positive free-space factor.
