# For K > 0 the Hertzian-dipole directivity integrates to 4π

Let `K > 0`. The [directivity](directivity.md) `D` of the
[Hertzian-dipole intensity](short-dipole-intensity.md) `U(θ) = K sin²θ` satisfies

`∫_0^{2π} ∫_0^π D(θ) sin θ dθ dφ = 4π`.

It is a special case of [directivity integrating to 4π](radiated-power-directivity.md) for every
pattern of nonzero radiated power.
