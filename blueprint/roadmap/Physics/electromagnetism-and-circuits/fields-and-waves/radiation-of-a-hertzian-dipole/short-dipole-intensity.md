# Far-field intensity of a Hertzian dipole

For real `K` and `θ`, the far-field radiation intensity of a Hertzian (infinitesimal,
uniform-current) dipole is `U(θ) = K sin²θ`, where `θ` is the polar angle measured from the dipole
axis. In the physical reading the [intensity coefficient](short-dipole-intensity-coeff.md) `K` is
positive.
