# For K > 0 the greatest Hertzian-dipole directivity is 3/2

Let `K > 0`. On the polar range `0 ≤ θ ≤ π`, the [directivity](directivity.md) of the
[Hertzian-dipole intensity](short-dipole-intensity.md) `U(θ) = K sin²θ` has greatest value `3/2`: it
takes the value `3/2` at `θ = π/2` (broadside) and is at most `3/2` everywhere on the range.
