# Radiation resistance of a Hertzian dipole for positive η, k, I and l

Let `η, k, I, l > 0`, and let `K = η k² I² l² / (32π²)` be the
[intensity coefficient](short-dipole-intensity-coeff.md). The
[radiation resistance](radiation-resistance.md) of the [radiated power](radiated-power.md) of
`U(θ) = K sin²θ` at peak current `I` is

`R = 2P/I² = η k² l² / (6π)`.
