# For positive indices, R² + (n2 / n1) T² = 1

For `n1 > 0` and `n2 > 0`, the [reflection](fresnel-r.md) and [transmission](fresnel-t.md)
coefficients `R = (n1 − n2) / (n1 + n2)` and `T = 2 n1 / (n1 + n2)` satisfy `R² + (n2 / n1) T² = 1`:
energy conservation at a lossless interface with equal permeabilities.
