# Normal-incidence amplitude reflection coefficient

For reals `n1` and `n2` (refractive indices), `R = (n1 − n2) / (n1 + n2)`, the amplitude reflection
coefficient at normal incidence of the declared interface model. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it
is `0` when `n1 + n2 = 0`. In the physical reading `n1, n2 > 0`.
