# Einstein relation in ratio form for nonzero mobility and charge

For `μ ≠ 0`, `q ≠ 0` and all real `kB` and `T`, the [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coeff.md)
`D = μ kB T / |q|` satisfies `D / μ = kB T / |q|`.

The ratio `kB T / |q|` is the
[thermal voltage](../../electronics-and-instrumentation/ideal-diode-as-a-network-port/thermal-voltage.md)
`V_T`, so `D / μ = V_T`.
