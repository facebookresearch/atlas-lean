# Field of a uniformly charged interval

For reals `ρ`, `ε`, `L` and `x`, the field formula of a uniformly charged interval is
`E(x) = ρ / (2ε) · (2x − L)`, an explicit expression meant as the field `−φ'` of the
[potential formula](uniform-charge-potential.md) `φ(x) = ρ / (2ε) · x (L − x)`. For `ε ≠ 0` it
satisfies the one-dimensional [Gauss law](gauss-1d.md) `E(L) − E(0) = ρ L / ε`. It is `0` everywhere
when `ε = 0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
