# Field of a uniformly charged interval

For reals `ρ`, `ε`, `L` and `x`, `E(x) = ρ / (2ε) · (2x − L)`, the field `−φ'` of the
[potential](uniform-charge-potential.md) `φ(x) = ρ / (2ε) · x (L − x)` written as an explicit
formula: differentiating `φ` gives `φ'(x) = ρ / (2ε) · (L − 2x)`. It is `0` everywhere when `ε = 0`,
by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
