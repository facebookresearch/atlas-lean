# Conductivity of independent carriers

For reals `n` (carrier density), `q` (signed charge) and `μ` (mobility), `σ = n |q| μ`, the
conductivity of dilute independent carriers; the carrier sign is carried by `q` only. In the
physical reading `n ≥ 0` and `μ ≥ 0`, where `σ ≥ 0` ([nonnegativity](conductivity-nonneg.md)).
