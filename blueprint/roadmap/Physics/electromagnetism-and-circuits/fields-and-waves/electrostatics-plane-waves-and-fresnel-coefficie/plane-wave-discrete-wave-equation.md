# Discrete wave equation for the plane wave with ω = c k

For `c ≠ 0` and `ω = c k`, and all real `E0`, `x`, `t` and `h`, the [plane wave](plane-wave.md)
`u(x, t) = E0 cos(k x − ω t)` satisfies
`u(x, t + h / c) − 2 u(x, t) + u(x, t − h / c) = u(x + h, t) − 2 u(x, t) + u(x − h, t)`: the time
second difference with step `h / c` equals the space second difference with step `h`.
