# For positive bases, per unit preserves the balanced power formula

For real phase bases $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$ and all complex $V$ and $I$, the balanced three-phase
power $3 V \bar I$, divided by the [three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md)
$S_b = 3 V_b I_b$, is the product of the per-unit voltage and the conjugate per-unit current:

$$\frac{3 V \bar I}{S_b} = V_{\mathrm{pu}}\, \overline{I_{\mathrm{pu}}},$$

with $V_{\mathrm{pu}} = V / V_b$ and $I_{\mathrm{pu}} = I / I_b$. Here $V$ and $I$ are
root-mean-square phasors, so no factor $1/2$ appears.
