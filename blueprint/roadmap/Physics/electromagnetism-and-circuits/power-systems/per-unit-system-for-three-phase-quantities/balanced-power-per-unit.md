# For positive bases, per unit preserves the summed balanced phase power

For real phase bases $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$ and all complex $V$ and $I$, let $V_p$ and $I_p$, for
$p$ in the three phases, be the
[balanced phase sets](../balanced-three-phase-power/balanced-phases.md) of $V$ and $I$, that is,
$(V, \alpha^2 V, \alpha V)$ with $\alpha = e^{2\pi i/3}$, and $V_p \bar I_p$ the
[complex power](../balanced-three-phase-power/complex-power.md) of phase $p$. Then the total power
divided by the [three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md) $S_b = 3 V_b I_b$ is

$$\frac{1}{S_b} \sum_p V_p \bar I_p = V_{\mathrm{pu}}\, \overline{I_{\mathrm{pu}}},$$

with $V_{\mathrm{pu}} = V / V_b$ and $I_{\mathrm{pu}} = I / I_b$, since the
[summed phase powers](../balanced-three-phase-power/sum-complex-power-balanced-phases.md) are
$3 V \bar I$.
