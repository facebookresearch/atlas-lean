# For positive bases, V_b² over S_b is one third of the impedance base

For real phase bases $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$, the squared phase-voltage base divided by the
[three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md) is one third of the
[impedance base](impedance-base.md):

$$\frac{V_b^2}{S_b} = \frac{V_b^2}{3 V_b I_b} = \frac{Z_b}{3}.$$

So dividing the squared phase base, rather than the squared
[line-voltage base](line-voltage-base.md), by $S_b$ understates the impedance base by a factor of
three.
