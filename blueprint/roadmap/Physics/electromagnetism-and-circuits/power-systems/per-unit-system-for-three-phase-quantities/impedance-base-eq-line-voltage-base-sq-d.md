# For positive bases, Z_b is the squared line-voltage base over S_b

For real phase bases $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$, the [impedance base](impedance-base.md) is the square
of the [line-voltage base](line-voltage-base.md) divided by the
[three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md):

$$Z_b = \frac{V_b}{I_b} = \frac{V_{L,b}^2}{S_b} = \frac{(\sqrt{3}\, V_b)^2}{3 V_b I_b}.$$
