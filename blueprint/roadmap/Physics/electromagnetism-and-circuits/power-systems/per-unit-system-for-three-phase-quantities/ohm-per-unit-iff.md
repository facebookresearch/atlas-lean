# For positive bases, Ohm's law holds iff it holds per unit

For real phase bases $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$ and all complex $V$, $Z$ and $I$, Ohm's law $V = Z I$
holds if and only if it holds in per-unit coordinates:

$$V = Z I \iff V_{\mathrm{pu}} = Z_{\mathrm{pu}}\, I_{\mathrm{pu}},$$

with $V_{\mathrm{pu}} = V / V_b$, $I_{\mathrm{pu}} = I / I_b$ and $Z_{\mathrm{pu}} = Z / Z_b$,
$Z_b = V_b / I_b$
([per-unit voltage](voltage-per-unit.md), [current](current-per-unit.md) and [impedance](impedance-per-unit.md)).
