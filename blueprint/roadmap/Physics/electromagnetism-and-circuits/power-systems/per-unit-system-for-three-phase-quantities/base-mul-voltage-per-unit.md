# For a nonzero base, base times per-unit voltage is the voltage

For a real phase-voltage base $V_b \ne 0$ and every complex voltage $V$, multiplying the
[per-unit voltage](voltage-per-unit.md) by the base recovers the voltage:
$V_b \cdot V_{\mathrm{pu}} = V$. The condition $V_b \ne 0$ is needed: at $V_b = 0$ the per-unit
voltage is $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$, so the product is $0$.
