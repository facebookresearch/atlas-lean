# Complex power in per-unit coordinates

For real phase bases $V_b$ and $I_b$ and a complex power $S$, the per-unit power is
$S_{\mathrm{pu}} = S / S_b$, where $S_b = 3 V_b I_b$ is the
[three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md), read as a complex number. With the convention
$x / 0 = 0$, $S_{\mathrm{pu}} = 0$ when $S_b = 0$, that is, when $V_b = 0$ or $I_b = 0$. In the
per-unit system $V_b$ and $I_b$ are positive root-mean-square phase bases; the definition allows all
real values.
