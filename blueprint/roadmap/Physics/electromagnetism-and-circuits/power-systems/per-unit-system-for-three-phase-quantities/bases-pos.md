# For positive phase bases, the derived bases are positive

For real numbers $V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$, the [three-phase power base](three-phase-power-base.md)
$S_b = 3 V_b I_b$, the [impedance base](impedance-base.md) $Z_b = V_b / I_b$ and the
[line-voltage base](line-voltage-base.md) $V_{L,b} = \sqrt{3}\, V_b$ are all positive.
