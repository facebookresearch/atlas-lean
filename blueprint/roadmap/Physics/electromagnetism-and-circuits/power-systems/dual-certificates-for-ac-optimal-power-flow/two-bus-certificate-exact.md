# The two-bus witness passes the checks with bound 3/2

For the [two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md) and the
[two-bus witness](two-bus-certificate.md): the witness passes the [sign check](nonneg.md), its
[dual slack](dual-slack.md) equals `F Fᴴ` for its factor `F = (5/4, -2)ᵀ`, the voltage
`V = (2, 5/4)` is [AC feasible](../current-limited-ac-optimal-power-flow-and-its-vo/ac-feasible.md)
with [AC cost](../current-limited-ac-optimal-power-flow-and-its-vo/ac-cost.md) `3/2`, and the
[certified lower bound](certified-lower-bound.md) is `3/2`.
