# A relaxed point of the two-bus example

The matrix `W = [[4, 2], [2, 17/16]]`, the average of the
[lifts](../current-limited-ac-optimal-power-flow-and-its-vo/voltage-lift.md) of the voltage vectors
`(2, 5/4)` and `(2, 3/4)` of the [two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md). It is feasible for the
relaxation, is the lift of no voltage vector and has cost `2`
([checks](two-bus-relaxed-point-not-lift.md)).
