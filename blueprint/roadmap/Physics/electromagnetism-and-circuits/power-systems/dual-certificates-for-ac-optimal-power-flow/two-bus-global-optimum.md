# The certified voltage is a global minimizer of the two-bus problem

For the [two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md), every
[AC-feasible](../current-limited-ac-optimal-power-flow-and-its-vo/ac-feasible.md) voltage vector `V`
has [AC cost](../current-limited-ac-optimal-power-flow-and-its-vo/ac-cost.md) at least that of
`V* = (2, 5/4)`, which is `3/2`; `V*` is itself AC feasible
([checks](two-bus-certificate-exact.md)).
