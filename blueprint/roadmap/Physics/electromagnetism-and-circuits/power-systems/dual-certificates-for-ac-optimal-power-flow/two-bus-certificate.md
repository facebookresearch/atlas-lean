# Exact dual witness for the two-bus example

The witness for the [two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md) has multiplier `4` on the upper
active limit at bus `1`, multiplier `9/16` on the upper squared-voltage limit at bus `0`, all other
multipliers `0`, and width-one factor `F = (5/4, -2)ᵀ`. It is an
[AC dual witness](acdual-certificate.md) with rational entries.
