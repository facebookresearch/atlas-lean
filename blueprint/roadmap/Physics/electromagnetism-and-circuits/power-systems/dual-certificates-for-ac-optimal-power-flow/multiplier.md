# Multipliers of an AC dual witness by constraint

For an [AC dual witness](acdual-certificate.md), the multiplier of the constraint (group `g`, bus
`b`) is, for `g = 0, …, 5`, the multiplier of the upper active, lower active, upper reactive, lower
reactive, upper squared-voltage or lower squared-voltage limit at `b`; the multiplier of branch `e`
is its current multiplier. This is the order of the [coefficient matrices](constraint-coeff.md).
