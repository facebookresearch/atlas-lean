# A stationary voltage of the two-bus example

The voltage vector `V = (2, 3/4)` of the [two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md). It is AC
feasible, and the dual slack of the [stationarity multipliers](two-bus-stationary-multipliers.md)
annihilates it, so the Lagrangian with these multipliers is stationary there
([checks](two-bus-stationary-point-not-certificate.md)).
