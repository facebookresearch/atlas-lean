# The certified optimal voltage of the two-bus example

The voltage vector `V = (2, 5/4)` (real phasors at buses `0` and `1`) of the
[two-bus example](two-bus-cert-example.md). It is AC feasible and its cost equals the certified
bound `3/2` of the [exact witness](two-bus-certificate.md) ([checks](two-bus-certificate-exact.md)),
and its cost is at most that of every AC-feasible voltage
([global optimum](two-bus-global-optimum.md)).
