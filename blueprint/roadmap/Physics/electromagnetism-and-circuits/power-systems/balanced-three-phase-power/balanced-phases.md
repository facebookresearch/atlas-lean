# Balanced positive-sequence phase set

For a complex phasor `X`, the balanced positive-sequence (abc) phase set is the triple

`(X_a, X_b, X_c) = (X, α² X, α X) = (X, X e^{-2πi/3}, X e^{2πi/3})`,

indexed by the integers mod 3 (`0, 1, 2` for the phases `a, b, c`), where `α = e^{2πi/3}` is the
[rotation operator](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/alpha.md). The three phasors
have the common magnitude `|X|` and are `2π/3` (120°) apart: with phasors rotating as `e^{iωt}`,
phase `b` lags phase `a` by `2π/3` and phase `c` lags phase `b` by `2π/3`. `X` may be any complex
number; for `X = 0` all three phasors are `0`.
