# Balanced resistive load: total complex power is real

Let `V` be complex and `R` real with `R > 0`. Feed equal resistances `R` with the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages `(V_a, V_b, V_c) = (V, α² V, α V)`,
with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, so that phase `p` draws the current `V_p / R`. The phase
[complex powers](complex-power.md) sum to the real number

`3 |V|² / R`.

For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention, the load absorbs the real power `3 |V|² / R`
and no reactive power.
