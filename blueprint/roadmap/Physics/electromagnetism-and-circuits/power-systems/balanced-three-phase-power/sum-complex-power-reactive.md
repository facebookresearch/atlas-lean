# Balanced purely reactive load: total complex power is purely imaginary

Let `V` be complex and `X` real with `X ≠ 0`. Feed equal reactances, of impedance `i X`, with the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages `(V_a, V_b, V_c) = (V, α² V, α V)`,
with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, so that phase `p` draws the current `V_p / (i X)`. The phase
[complex powers](complex-power.md) sum to

`(3 |V|² / X) i`.

For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention, the load absorbs no real power and the reactive
power `3 |V|² / X`: positive for an inductive load (`X > 0`) and negative, that is reactive power
supplied, for a capacitive one (`X < 0`), when `V ≠ 0`.
