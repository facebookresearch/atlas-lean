# Balanced delta: apparent power is √3 line voltage times line current

For all complex `V` and `J`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, let the three branches of a delta connection
carry the [balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) currents `(J, α² J, α J)` under the
[line-to-line voltages](line-voltage.md) of the positive-sequence phase voltages
`(V, α² V, α V)`. The sum `S` of the three branch [complex powers](complex-power.md) has magnitude

`|S| = √3 V_L I_L`,

where `V_L = |V_ab|` is the line-to-line voltage magnitude, `I_L = |I_a| = |I_ab - I_ca|` the
magnitude of the [delta line current](delta-line-current.md) and `√3` the positive square root.
For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention, `|S|` is the total apparent power. When
`V = 0` or `J = 0` both sides are `0`.
