# Positive sequence: line voltages are √3 phase voltages rotated by +30°

For every complex `V`, the [line-to-line voltages](line-voltage.md) of the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages `(V, α² V, α V)`, with
`α = e^{2πi/3}`, form again a balanced positive-sequence set, built from `√3 e^{iπ/6} V`:

`(V_ab, V_bc, V_ca) = (W, α² W, α W)` with `W = √3 e^{iπ/6} V`,

where `√3` is the positive square root. So `V_ab = √3 e^{iπ/6} V_a`: each line voltage has `√3`
times the phase magnitude and, with phasors rotating as `e^{iωt}`, leads the phase voltage of its
first index (`V_ab` leads `V_a`, `V_bc` leads `V_b`, `V_ca` leads `V_c`) by `π/6` (30°).
