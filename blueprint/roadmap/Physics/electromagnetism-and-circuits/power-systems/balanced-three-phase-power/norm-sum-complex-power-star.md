# Balanced star: apparent power is √3 line voltage times line current

For all complex `V` and `I`, let a star connection carry the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages `(V, α² V, α V)` and line (equal
to phase) currents `(I, α² I, α I)`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`. The sum `S` of the three phase
[complex powers](complex-power.md) has magnitude

`|S| = √3 V_L I_L`,

where `V_L = |V_ab| = |V_a - V_b|` is the [line-to-line voltage](line-voltage.md) magnitude,
`I_L = |I_a| = |I|` the line-current magnitude and `√3` the positive square root. For RMS phasors
under the passive sign convention, `|S|` is the total apparent power. When `V = 0` or `I = 0`
both sides are `0`.
