# Complex power absorbed by one phase

For complex phasors `V` (voltage) and `I` (current), the complex power is

`S = V Ī`,

the product of `V` with the complex conjugate of `I`, defined for all complex `V` and `I`. For
RMS phasors under the passive sign convention (the current `I` enters the element at the terminal
whose voltage `V` is measured as positive), `S` is the complex power absorbed: `Re S` is the real
(average) power and `Im S` the reactive power absorbed. There is no factor `1/2`: for phasors
given by peak amplitudes, the absorbed complex power is `V Ī / 2`.
