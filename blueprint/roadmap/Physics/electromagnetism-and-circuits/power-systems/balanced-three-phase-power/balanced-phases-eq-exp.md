# Balanced positive-sequence set in exponential form

For every complex `V`, the [balanced positive-sequence set](balanced-phases.md) built from `V` is

`(V_a, V_b, V_c) = (V, V e^{-2πi/3}, V e^{2πi/3})`,

since the [rotation operator](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/alpha.md)
`α = e^{2πi/3}` satisfies `α² = e^{-2πi/3}`.
