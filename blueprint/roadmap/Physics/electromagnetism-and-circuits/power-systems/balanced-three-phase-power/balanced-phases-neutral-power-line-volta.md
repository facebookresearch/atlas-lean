# Neutral current, total power and line voltages of a balanced star

For all complex `V` and `I`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages `(V, α² V, α V)` and line (equal
to phase) currents `(I, α² I, α I)` of a star connection satisfy:

- the [neutral current](neutral-current.md) is zero, `I + α² I + α I = 0`;
- the [complex powers](complex-power.md) of the three phases sum to `3 V Ī`;
- every [line-to-line voltage](line-voltage.md) has magnitude `√3 |V|`, with `√3` the positive
  square root.

For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention, `3 V Ī` is the total complex power absorbed.
