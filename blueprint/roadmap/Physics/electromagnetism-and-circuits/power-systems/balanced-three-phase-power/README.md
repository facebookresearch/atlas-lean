# Balanced three-phase power

Three-phase quantities are complex phasors indexed by the phases `a, b, c`. With the rotation
operator `α = e^{2πi/3}`, a balanced positive-sequence (abc) set is `(X, α² X, α X)` and a
negative-sequence (acb) set is `(X, α X, α² X)`, for any complex `X`. For balanced positive-sequence
sets, the currents sum to zero, so a star connection has no neutral current; the three phase complex
powers sum to `3 V Ī`; the line-to-line voltages are `√3 e^{iπ/6}` times the phase voltages
(`√3 e^{-iπ/6}` for the negative sequence); delta line currents are `√3 e^{-iπ/6}` times the branch
currents; and in both connections the apparent power is `√3` times the line voltage times the line
current. For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention, the complex power `V Ī` of a phase is
the power it absorbs; the identities hold for all complex phasors.
