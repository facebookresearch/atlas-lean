# Balanced delta line currents have √3 times the branch magnitude

For every complex `J` and every index `p` (mod 3), the
[line current of the delta connection](delta-line-current.md) whose branch currents are the
[balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) set `(J, α² J, α J)`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, has
magnitude

`|I_p| = √3 |J|`,

with `√3` the positive square root; for `J = 0` both sides are `0`.
