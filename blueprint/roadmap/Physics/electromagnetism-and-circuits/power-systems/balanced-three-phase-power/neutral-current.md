# Neutral current of a star connection

For three complex currents `(I_a, I_b, I_c)` indexed by the integers mod 3, the neutral current is
their sum

`I_n = I_a + I_b + I_c`.

In a star (wye) connection with a neutral wire, the line currents are the phase currents; with the
line currents directed into the star point, Kirchhoff's current law at the star point makes `I_n`
the current leaving it through the neutral wire.
