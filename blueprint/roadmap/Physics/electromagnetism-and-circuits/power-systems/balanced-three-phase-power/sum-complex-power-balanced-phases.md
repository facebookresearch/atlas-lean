# Balanced three-phase complex power is three times the phase power

For all complex `V` and `I`, take the [balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase
voltages `(V_a, V_b, V_c) = (V, α² V, α V)` and phase currents `(I_a, I_b, I_c) = (I, α² I, α I)`,
with `α = e^{2πi/3}`. The [complex powers](complex-power.md) of the three phases sum to

`V_a Ī_a + V_b Ī_b + V_c Ī_c = 3 V Ī`,

since each phase contributes `V Ī` (`|α| = 1`). For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention
this is the total complex power absorbed.
