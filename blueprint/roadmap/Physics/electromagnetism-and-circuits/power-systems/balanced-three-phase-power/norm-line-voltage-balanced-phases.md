# Positive-sequence line voltages have √3 times the phase magnitude

For every complex `V` and every index `p` (mod 3), the [line-to-line voltage](line-voltage.md)
`V_p - V_{p+1}` of the [balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) phase voltages
`(V, α² V, α V)`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, has magnitude

`|V_p - V_{p+1}| = √3 |V|`,

with `√3` the positive square root; for `V = 0` both sides are `0`.
