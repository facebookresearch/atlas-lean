# Balanced delta: total complex power is three times one branch's power

For all complex `V` and `J`, with `α = e^{2πi/3}`, let the three branches of a delta connection
carry the [balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) currents
`(I_ab, I_bc, I_ca) = (J, α² J, α J)` under the [line-to-line voltages](line-voltage.md)
`(V_ab, V_bc, V_ca)` of the positive-sequence phase voltages `(V, α² V, α V)`. The branch
[complex powers](complex-power.md) sum to

`V_ab Ī_ab + V_bc Ī_bc + V_ca Ī_ca = 3 V_ab conj(J)`,

three times the power of branch `ab`. For RMS phasors under the passive sign convention this is
the total complex power absorbed by the delta load.
