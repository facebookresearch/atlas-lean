# For a nonzero phasor, the two sequences give different line voltages

For every nonzero complex `V`, the [line-to-line voltage](line-voltage.md) `V_ab = V_a - V_b` of
the [positive-sequence set](balanced-phases.md) `(V, α² V, α V)` differs from that of the
[negative-sequence set](reversed-phases.md) `(V, α V, α² V)`, where `α = e^{2πi/3}`:

`√3 e^{iπ/6} V ≠ √3 e^{-iπ/6} V`.

For `V = 0` both are `0`. So, for nonzero phase voltages, the phase sequence shows in the line
voltages through the sign of the 30° rotation.
