# Positive-sequence currents have zero neutral current

For every complex `I`, the [balanced positive-sequence](balanced-phases.md) currents
`(I, α² I, α I)` have [neutral current](neutral-current.md)

`I + α² I + α I = (1 + α + α²) I = 0`,

because the [rotation operator](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/alpha.md)
`α = e^{2πi/3}` satisfies
[`1 + α + α² = 0`](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/one-add-alpha-add-alpha-sq.md).
A star connection carrying such balanced line currents has no current in its neutral wire.
