# The dual objective of the dual vector is minus the lower bound

For all rational data and dual witnesses, `rhs · y = -L`, where `rhs` is the
[rational right-hand side](rat-lp-rhs.md), `y` the [dual vector](dual-vector.md) and
[certified lower bound](dual-lower-bound.md)
`L = d · λ + p_min · μ_lo - p_max · μ_hi + f_min · η_lo - f_max · η_hi`.
