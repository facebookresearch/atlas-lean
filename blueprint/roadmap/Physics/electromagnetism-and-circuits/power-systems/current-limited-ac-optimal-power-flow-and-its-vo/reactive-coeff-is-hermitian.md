# The reactive-power coefficient matrix is Hermitian

For every natural number $n$, every complex $n \times n$ matrix $Y$, Hermitian or not, and every
index $k$, the [reactive coefficient matrix](reactive-coeff.md)
$A^Q_k = \frac{1}{2i} (R_k^{\mathsf H} - R_k)$ is Hermitian, where $R_k$ keeps row $k$ of $Y$ and
sets the other rows to zero.
