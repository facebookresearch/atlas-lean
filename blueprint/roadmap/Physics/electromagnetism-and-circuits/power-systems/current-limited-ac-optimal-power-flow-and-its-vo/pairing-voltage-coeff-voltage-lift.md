# On a lift, the voltage pairing is the squared voltage magnitude

For every natural number $n$, every $V \in \mathbb{C}^n$ and every index $k$, the
[pairing](pairing.md) of the [voltage coefficient matrix](voltage-coeff.md) $E_k$ with the
[voltage lift](voltage-lift.md) is

$$\langle E_k, V V^{\mathsf H} \rangle = |V_k|^2.$$
