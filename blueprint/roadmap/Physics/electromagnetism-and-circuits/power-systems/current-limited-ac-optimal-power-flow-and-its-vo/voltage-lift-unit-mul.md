# The voltage lift is unchanged by a common unit-modulus factor

For every natural number $n$, every $V \in \mathbb{C}^n$ and every $u \in \mathbb{C}$ with
$|u| = 1$, the [voltage lift](voltage-lift.md) of $u V = (u V_1, \dots, u V_n)$ equals that of $V$:

$$(u V)(u V)^{\mathsf H} = |u|^2\, V V^{\mathsf H} = V V^{\mathsf H}.$$

The lift does not see a rotation of all voltage phases by the same angle.
