# The voltage lift has rank at most one

For every natural number $n$ and every $V \in \mathbb{C}^n$, the [voltage lift](voltage-lift.md)
$V V^{\mathsf H}$ has rank at most one as a complex matrix: every column is a multiple of $V$. The
rank is $0$ for [the zero vector](voltage-lift-zero.md), and $1$ when $V \ne 0$, since then some
diagonal entry $|V_k|^2$ is positive.
