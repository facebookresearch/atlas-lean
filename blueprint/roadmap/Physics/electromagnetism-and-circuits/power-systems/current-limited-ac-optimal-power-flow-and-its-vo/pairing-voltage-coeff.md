# The voltage pairing reads the real part of a diagonal entry

For every natural number $n$, every complex $n \times n$ matrix $W$ and every index $k$, the
[pairing](pairing.md) of the [voltage coefficient matrix](voltage-coeff.md) $E_k$ with $W$ is

$$\langle E_k, W \rangle = \operatorname{Re} W_{kk}.$$

No hypothesis on $W$ is needed; for Hermitian $W$ the entry $W_{kk}$ is real.
