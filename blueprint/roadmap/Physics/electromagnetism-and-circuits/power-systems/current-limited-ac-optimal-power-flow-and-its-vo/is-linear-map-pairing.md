# The pairing with a fixed matrix is real-linear

For every natural number $n$ and every complex $n \times n$ matrix $A$, the map
$W \mapsto \langle A, W \rangle = \operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr}(A W)$ from complex
$n \times n$ matrices to $\mathbb{R}$ is real-linear:
$\langle A, W + W' \rangle = \langle A, W \rangle + \langle A, W' \rangle$ and
$\langle A, c W \rangle = c\, \langle A, W \rangle$ for every real $c$. Here
$\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ is the [pairing](pairing.md).
