# The branch-current coefficient matrix is Hermitian

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$, every real matrix $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$, every
$y \in \mathbb{C}^m$ and every branch $e$, the
[branch-current coefficient matrix](branch-current-coeff.md)
$(C_e)_{ab} = |y_e|^2\, B_{ae}\, B_{be}$ is Hermitian: its entries are real and symmetric in $a$ and
$b$.
