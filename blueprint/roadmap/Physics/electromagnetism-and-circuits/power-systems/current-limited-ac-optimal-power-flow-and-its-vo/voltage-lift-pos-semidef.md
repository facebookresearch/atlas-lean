# The voltage lift is positive semidefinite

For every natural number $n$ and every $V \in \mathbb{C}^n$, the [voltage lift](voltage-lift.md)
$V V^{\mathsf H}$ is positive semidefinite: it is Hermitian, and
$x^{\mathsf H} V V^{\mathsf H} x = |V^{\mathsf H} x|^2 \ge 0$ for every $x \in \mathbb{C}^n$.
