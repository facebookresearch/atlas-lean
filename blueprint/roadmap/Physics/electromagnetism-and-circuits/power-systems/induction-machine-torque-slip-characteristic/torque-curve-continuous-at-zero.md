# The torque curve is continuous at zero slip

Let `R₂ > 0`, `ω_s > 0`, `R_th ≥ 0`, `X_eq` real and `V_th` complex. The
[torque curve](torque-curve.md) `T(s) = 3 ‖V_th‖² R₂ s / (ω_s ((R_th s + R₂)² + (X_eq s)²))` is
continuous at `s = 0`, where it takes the value `0`; its denominator there is `ω_s R₂² ≠ 0`.
