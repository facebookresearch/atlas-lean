# The air-gap power is three times a per-phase real power

For every complex `I₂` and real `R₂`, `s`, the [air-gap power](air-gap-power.md) `3 ‖I₂‖² (R₂ / s)`
equals three times the real part of the per-phase
[complex power](../balanced-three-phase-power/complex-power.md) `V conj(I₂)` with `V = (R₂ / s) I₂`,
the voltage across the slip-divided rotor resistance (RMS phasors, passive sign convention). At
`s = 0` both sides are `0` by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
