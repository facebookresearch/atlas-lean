# On the motoring range, the developed torque is the torque curve

Let `R₂ > 0`, `ω_s > 0`, `R_th ≥ 0`, `X_eq` real, `V_th` complex and `0 < s ≤ 1` (the motoring range
of the slip). The [developed torque](developed-torque.md) of the [rotor current](rotor-current.md)
`I₂ = V_th s / (R_th s + R₂ + i X_eq s)` equals
`T(s) = 3 ‖V_th‖² R₂ s / (ω_s ((R_th s + R₂)² + (X_eq s)²))`.

The right-hand side is the [torque curve](torque-curve.md) `T(s)`; read the other way, on the
motoring range the torque curve equals the developed torque of the model current.
