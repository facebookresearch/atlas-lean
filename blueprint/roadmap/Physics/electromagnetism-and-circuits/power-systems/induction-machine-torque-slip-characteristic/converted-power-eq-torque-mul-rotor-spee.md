# On the motoring range, converted power is torque times rotor speed

Let `R₂ > 0`, `ω_s > 0`, `R_th ≥ 0`, `X_eq` real, `V_th` complex and `0 < s ≤ 1` (the motoring range
of the slip), and let `I₂` be the [rotor current](rotor-current.md)
`I₂ = V_th s / (R_th s + R₂ + i X_eq s)`. Then the [converted power](converted-power.md) is the
[developed torque](developed-torque.md) times the rotor speed `(1 - s) ω_s` and `(1 - s)` times the
[air-gap power](air-gap-power.md), and the [rotor copper loss](rotor-copper-loss.md) is `s` times
the air-gap power. It is the [power split](power-split-of-ne-zero.md) for this current.
