# At zero slip the rotor current, powers and torque vanish

For every complex `V_th` and real `R_th`, `R₂`, `X_eq`, `ω_s`, at slip `s = 0` the
[rotor current](rotor-current.md) is `0`; the [air-gap power](air-gap-power.md), the
[rotor copper loss](rotor-copper-loss.md) and the [converted power](converted-power.md) of that
current are `0`; and its [developed torque](developed-torque.md) equals the value `0` of the
[torque curve](torque-curve.md) at `s = 0`.
