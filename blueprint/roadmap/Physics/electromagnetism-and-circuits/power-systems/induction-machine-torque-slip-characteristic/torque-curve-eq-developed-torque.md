# On the motoring range, the torque curve is the developed torque

Let `R₂ > 0`, `ω_s > 0`, `R_th ≥ 0`, `X_eq` real, `V_th` complex and `0 < s ≤ 1` (the motoring range
of the slip). The [torque curve](torque-curve.md)
`T(s) = 3 ‖V_th‖² R₂ s / (ω_s ((R_th s + R₂)² + (X_eq s)²))` equals the
[developed torque](developed-torque.md) of the [rotor current](rotor-current.md)
`I₂ = V_th s / (R_th s + R₂ + i X_eq s)`.
