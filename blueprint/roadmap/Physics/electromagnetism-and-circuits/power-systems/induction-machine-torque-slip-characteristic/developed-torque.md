# Developed torque of the induction machine

For a complex rotor current `I₂` and real `R₂`, `s` and `ω_s`, the developed torque is `P_ag / ω_s`,
the [air-gap power](air-gap-power.md) `3 ‖I₂‖² (R₂ / s)` over the synchronous mechanical speed
`ω_s`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when `ω_s = 0` or `s = 0`. In the model `ω_s > 0`.
