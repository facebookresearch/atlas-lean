# Converted mechanical power of the induction machine

For a complex rotor current `I₂` and real `R₂` and `s`, the converted mechanical power is the
[air-gap power](air-gap-power.md) minus the [rotor copper loss](rotor-copper-loss.md):
`3 ‖I₂‖² (R₂ / s) - 3 ‖I₂‖² R₂`. For `s ≠ 0` it is the fraction `1 - s` of the air-gap power
([power split](power-split-of-ne-zero.md)). With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `-3 ‖I₂‖² R₂` at
`s = 0`, where the model current is `0`.
