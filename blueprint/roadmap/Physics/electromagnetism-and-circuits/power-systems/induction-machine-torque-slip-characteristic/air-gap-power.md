# Air-gap power of the induction machine

For a complex rotor current `I₂` and real `R₂` and `s`, the air-gap power is
`P_ag = 3 ‖I₂‖² (R₂ / s)`, three times the power in the slip-divided rotor resistance. With the
convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` at `s = 0`; at that slip the
[model current](rotor-current-zero-slip.md) is `0` anyway, and a nonzero current at zero slip is not
a state of the model.
