# Real power of the synchronous generator

For real `E`, `V`, `X_s` and `δ`, the terminal real power is `P = Re S`, the real part of the
[three-phase complex power](total-complex-power.md) `S = 3 V conj(I)` with `I` the
[generator current](synchronous-current.md) `I = (E e^(iδ) - V) / (i X_s)`
([power–angle formula](real-power-eq.md)). In the model the internal voltage magnitude `E`, the
terminal voltage magnitude `V` and the synchronous reactance `X_s` are positive.
