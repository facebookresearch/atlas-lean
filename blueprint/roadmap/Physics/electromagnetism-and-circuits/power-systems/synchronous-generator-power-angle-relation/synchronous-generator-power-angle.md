# Power–angle relations of the synchronous generator

Let `E > 0`, `V > 0` and `X_s > 0`, and let `δ` be real. With the
[generator current](synchronous-current.md) `I = (E e^(iδ) - V) / (i X_s)` (directed out toward the
bus) and the [three-phase complex power](total-complex-power.md) `S = 3 V conj(I)` (RMS phasors),
the terminal real power is `Re S = 3 V E sin δ / X_s` and the terminal reactive power is
`Im S = 3 V (E cos δ - V) / X_s`.
