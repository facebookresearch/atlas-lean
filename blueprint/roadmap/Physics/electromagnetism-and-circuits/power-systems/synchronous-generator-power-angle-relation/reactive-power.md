# Reactive power of the synchronous generator

For real `E`, `V`, `X_s` and `δ`, the terminal reactive power is `Q = Im S`, the imaginary part of
the [three-phase complex power](total-complex-power.md) `S = 3 V conj(I)` with `I` the
[generator current](synchronous-current.md) `I = (E e^(iδ) - V) / (i X_s)`
([power–angle formula](reactive-power-eq.md)). In the model the internal voltage magnitude `E`, the
terminal voltage magnitude `V` and the synchronous reactance `X_s` are positive.
