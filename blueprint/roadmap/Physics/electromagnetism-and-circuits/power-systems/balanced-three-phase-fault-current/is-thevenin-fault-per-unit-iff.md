# For positive bases, the fault laws hold iff they hold per unit

For complex $E$, $Z_{\mathrm{th}}$, $Z_f$, $V$ and $I$ and real phase voltage and current bases
$V_b > 0$ and $I_b > 0$, the [Thévenin fault laws](is-thevenin-fault.md)
$V = E - Z_{\mathrm{th}}\, I$ and $V = Z_f\, I$ hold if and only if the per-unit quantities satisfy
$V_{\mathrm{pu}} = E_{\mathrm{pu}} - Z_{\mathrm{th,pu}}\, I_{\mathrm{pu}}$ and
$V_{\mathrm{pu}} = Z_{f,\mathrm{pu}}\, I_{\mathrm{pu}}$. Here $E_{\mathrm{pu}} = E / V_b$ and
$V_{\mathrm{pu}} = V / V_b$ are per-unit
[voltages](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/voltage-per-unit.md),
$I_{\mathrm{pu}} = I / I_b$ is the per-unit
[current](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/current-per-unit.md),
$Z_{\mathrm{pu}} = Z / Z_b$ is the per-unit
[impedance](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/impedance-per-unit.md) of each impedance
$Z$, and $Z_b = V_b / I_b$ is the
[impedance base](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/impedance-base.md).
