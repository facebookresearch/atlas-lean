# Zero total impedance, nonzero source: the fault laws have no solution

For complex $E$, $Z_{\mathrm{th}}$, $Z_f$, $V$ and $I$ with $Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f = 0$ and
$E \neq 0$, the [Thévenin fault laws](is-thevenin-fault.md) $V = E - Z_{\mathrm{th}}\, I$ and
$V = Z_f\, I$ do not both hold: together they give $E = (Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f)\, I = 0$. So zero
total impedance is excluded from the [solution of the fault laws](is-thevenin-fault-iff.md).
