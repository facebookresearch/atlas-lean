# Zero total impedance, zero source: every current solves the fault laws

For complex $Z_{\mathrm{th}}$, $Z_f$ and $I$ with $Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f = 0$, the source voltage
$E = 0$, the voltage $V = Z_f\, I$ and the current $I$ satisfy the
[Thévenin fault laws](is-thevenin-fault.md): $Z_f\, I = 0 - Z_{\mathrm{th}}\, I$ and
$Z_f\, I = Z_f\, I$. So with zero total impedance and a zero source the laws do not determine the
fault current.
