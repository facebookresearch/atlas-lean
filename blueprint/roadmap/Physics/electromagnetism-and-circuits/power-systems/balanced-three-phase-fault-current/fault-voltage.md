# Fault voltage: fault impedance times fault current

For complex $E$, $Z_{\mathrm{th}}$ and $Z_f$, the fault voltage at the faulted terminals is

$$V_f = Z_f\, I_f = \frac{Z_f\, E}{Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f},$$

where $I_f$ is the [fault current](fault-current.md). With the convention $x / 0 = 0$, $V_f = 0$
when $Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f = 0$.
