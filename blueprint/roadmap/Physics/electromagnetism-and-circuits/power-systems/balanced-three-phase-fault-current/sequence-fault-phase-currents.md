# The fault phase currents are purely positive sequence

For all complex $E$, $Z_{\mathrm{th}}$ and $Z_f$, the
[fault phase currents](fault-phase-currents.md) $(I_f, \alpha^2 I_f, \alpha I_f)$, where $I_f$ is
the [fault current](fault-current.md) and $\alpha = e^{2\pi i/3}$ is the
[rotation operator](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/alpha.md), have
[zero-sequence](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/zero-sequence.md) component $0$,
[positive-sequence](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/positive-sequence.md) component
$I_f$ and [negative-sequence](../symmetrical-components-of-three-phase-systems/negative-sequence.md)
component $0$. No condition on the total impedance is needed; when $Z_{\mathrm{th}} + Z_f = 0$ the
convention $x / 0 = 0$ gives $I_f = 0$, and all three components are $0$.
