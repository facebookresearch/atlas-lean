# 3S on the three-phase base and S on V_b I_b give equal per-unit values

For a complex power $S$ and all real $V_b$ and $I_b$, the
[per-unit value](per-unit-complex-power.md) of $3 S$ on the
[three-phase power base](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/three-phase-power-base.md)
$3 V_b I_b$ equals the per-unit value of $S$ on the per-phase base $V_b I_b$:

$$\frac{3 S}{3 V_b I_b} = \frac{S}{V_b I_b}.$$

So in balanced operation, where the three-phase power is $3 S$, a three-phase power measured on the
three-phase base and the per-phase power measured on the per-phase base have the same per-unit
value. No condition on the bases is needed; both sides are $0$
when $V_b I_b = 0$.
