# Direct-base per unit on 3 V_b I_b equals the per-unit power

For a complex power $S$ and all real $V_b$ and $I_b$, the
[per-unit power on a direct base](per-unit-complex-power.md), taken on the
[three-phase power base](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/three-phase-power-base.md)
$S_b = 3 V_b I_b$, equals the
[per-unit power](../per-unit-system-for-three-phase-quantities/power-per-unit.md) for the phase
bases $V_b$ and $I_b$: both are $S / (3 V_b I_b)$. No condition on the bases is needed; both sides
are $0$ when $V_b I_b = 0$.
