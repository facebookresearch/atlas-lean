# The phases are the DFT of the sequence components

For all complex `V_a`, `V_b`, `V_c`, the [phase array](phase-vector.md) `(V_a, V_b, V_c)` is the
discrete Fourier transform `F(k) = Σ_s e^(-2πi ks/3) g(s)` of the
[sequence array](sequence-vector.md) `g = (V₀, V₁, V₂)`: the phases are recovered from the sequence
components.
