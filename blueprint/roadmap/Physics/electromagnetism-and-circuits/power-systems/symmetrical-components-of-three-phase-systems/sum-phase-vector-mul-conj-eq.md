# Power identity as sums over the integers modulo 3

For all complex `V_a`, `V_b`, `V_c`, `I_a`, `I_b`, `I_c`, with `v`, `i` the
[phase arrays](phase-vector.md) and `v_s`, `i_s` the [sequence arrays](sequence-vector.md) of the
voltages and currents, `Σ_p v(p) conj(i(p)) = 3 Σ_s v_s(s) conj(i_s(s))`, both sums over the
integers modulo 3.
