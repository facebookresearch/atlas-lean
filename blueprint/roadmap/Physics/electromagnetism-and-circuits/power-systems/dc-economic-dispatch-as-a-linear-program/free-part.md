# Free variables recovered from an LP point

For an LP point `x = (x⁺, x⁻)` with `2 (n_G + n_N)` entries, where `x⁺` and `x⁻` are its first and
second coordinate blocks of `n_G + n_N` entries each, the free part is `x⁺ - x⁻`, the first block
minus the second, a vector of `n_G + n_N` free variables `(p, θ)`.
