# Free variables recovered from an LP point

For an LP point `x = (u, w)` with `2 (n_G + n_N)` entries, where `u` and `w` are its first and
second coordinate blocks of `n_G + n_N` entries each, the free part is `u - w`, the first block
minus the second, a vector of `n_G + n_N` free variables `(p, θ)`.
