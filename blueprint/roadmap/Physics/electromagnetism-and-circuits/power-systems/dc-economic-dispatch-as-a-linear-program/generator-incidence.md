# Generator-to-bus incidence matrix

For finitely many buses and generators, each generator `g` placed at a bus, the generator incidence
matrix `C` has entry `C(v, g) = 1` when generator `g` is at bus `v` and `0` otherwise, so `(C p)_v`
is the generation at bus `v` ([generation at a bus](generator-incidence-mul-vec-apply.md)).
