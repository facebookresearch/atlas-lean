# Lossless branch admittance

For real branch susceptances `b_e`, the lossless admittance of branch `e` is the complex number
`y_e = -i b_e`: a pure series susceptance with zero real part (no conductance) and imaginary part
`-b_e`. In the physical reading `b_e > 0`: a line of series reactance `x_e > 0` has admittance
`1/(i x_e) = -i/x_e`, so that `b_e = 1/x_e`. The definition allows all real `b_e`.
