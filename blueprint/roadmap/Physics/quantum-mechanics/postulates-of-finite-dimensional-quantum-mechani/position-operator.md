# Position operator

The position operator is $f \mapsto (x \mapsto xf(x))$ with domain all functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$; this is the operator on functions, not the self-adjoint operator on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, whose domain consists of the $f \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ with $xf \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$.
