# For a Hermitian matrix ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩ is real

For a Hermitian complex `n × n` matrix `A` (`Aᴴ = A`, with `Aᴴ` the conjugate transpose) and every
`ψ ∈ ℂⁿ`, `Im ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩ = 0`. Equivalently, `⟨ψ, A ψ⟩` equals the real number `⟨A⟩_ψ = Re ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩`,
the [expectation value](expectation-value.md), viewed as a complex number.
