# The expectation of a Hermitian observable is the inner product

Let $\mathbb{C}^n$ carry its standard inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ (conjugate-linear in the first argument); a state $\psi$ is normalized when $\lVert\psi\rVert = 1$. For an $n \times n$ complex matrix $A$, the expectation value of $A$ in $\psi$ is $\langle A\rangle_\psi = \operatorname{Re}\langle\psi, A\psi\rangle$. If $A$ is Hermitian, then $\langle\psi, A\psi\rangle = \langle A\rangle_\psi$ as complex numbers.
