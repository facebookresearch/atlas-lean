# For a Hermitian matrix ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩ is the expectation value

For a Hermitian complex `n × n` matrix `A` (`Aᴴ = A`, with `Aᴴ` the conjugate transpose) and every
`ψ ∈ ℂⁿ`, `⟨ψ, A ψ⟩` equals the real number `⟨A⟩_ψ`, the [expectation value](expectation-value.md)
`⟨A⟩_ψ = Re ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩`, viewed as a complex number.
