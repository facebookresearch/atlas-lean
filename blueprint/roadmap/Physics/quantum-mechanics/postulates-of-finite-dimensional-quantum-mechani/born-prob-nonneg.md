# Born probabilities are nonnegative

Let $\mathbb{C}^n$ carry its standard inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ (conjugate-linear in the first argument); a state $\psi$ is normalized when $\lVert\psi\rVert = 1$. For an orthonormal basis $(b_i)$, the Born probability of outcome $i$ is $p_i(\psi) = |\langle b_i, \psi\rangle|^2$. Then $p_i(\psi) \ge 0$.
