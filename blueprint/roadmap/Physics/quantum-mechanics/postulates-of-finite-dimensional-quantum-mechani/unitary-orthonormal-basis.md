# Orthonormal basis from the columns of a unitary matrix

The columns of a unitary matrix `U` as an orthonormal basis of `ℂⁿ` with the Euclidean inner
product: the `i`-th basis vector is the `i`-th column `U eᵢ` (see
[basis vectors](coe-unitary-orthonormal-basis.md)). Its coordinate map is `x ↦ Uᴴ x`, inverted by
`x ↦ U x`.
