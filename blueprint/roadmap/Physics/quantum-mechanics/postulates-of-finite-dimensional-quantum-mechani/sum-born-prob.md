# The Born probabilities of a vector sum to its squared norm

For an orthonormal basis `b = (b_0, …, b_{n−1})` of `ℂⁿ` (with the Euclidean inner product) and
every vector `ψ ∈ ℂⁿ`, `∑_i |⟨b_i, ψ⟩|² = ‖ψ‖²` (Parseval's identity): the
[Born probabilities](born-prob.md) `p_i = |⟨b_i, ψ⟩|²` sum to `‖ψ‖²`.
