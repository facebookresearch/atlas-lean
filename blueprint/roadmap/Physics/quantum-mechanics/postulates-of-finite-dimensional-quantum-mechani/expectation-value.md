# Expectation value of an observable

For a complex `n × n` matrix `A` and a vector `ψ ∈ ℂⁿ`, `⟨A⟩_ψ = Re ⟨ψ, A ψ⟩`, with the inner
product `⟨x, y⟩ = ∑_j conj(x_j) y_j` of `ℂⁿ`, conjugate-linear in `x` and linear in `y`. This is the
expectation-value postulate, taken as a definition. For a Hermitian `A` the inner product `⟨ψ, A ψ⟩`
is [real](inner-mul-vec-self-eq-expectation-value.md), so taking the real part discards nothing; the
value is an expectation in the physical sense for a Hermitian `A` and a normalized `ψ`.
