# Expectation value of an observable

For an $n \times n$ complex matrix $A$ and $\psi \in \mathbb{C}^n$, the expectation value of $A$ in $\psi$ is the real part $\operatorname{Re}\langle\psi, A\psi\rangle$ of the quadratic form of $A$ at $\psi$, defined for every matrix and every vector. Only when $A$ is Hermitian and $\psi$ is normalized is it the expectation of the observable $A$ in the state $\psi$; for Hermitian $A$ the inner product $\langle\psi, A\psi\rangle$ is already real.
