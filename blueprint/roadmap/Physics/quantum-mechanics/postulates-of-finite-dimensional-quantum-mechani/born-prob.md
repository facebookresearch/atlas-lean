# Born probability of an outcome

For an orthonormal basis `b = (b_0, …, b_{n−1})` of `ℂⁿ` (with the Euclidean inner product), a
vector `ψ ∈ ℂⁿ` and an outcome `i`, `p_i = |⟨b_i, ψ⟩|²`, with the inner product
`⟨x, y⟩ = ∑_j conj(x_j) y_j` of `ℂⁿ`, conjugate-linear in `x` and linear in `y`. This is the Born
rule, a physical postulate taken as a definition. The numbers `p_i` are
[nonnegative](born-prob-nonneg.md) for every `ψ`, and they form a probability distribution on the
outcomes when `ψ` is normalized ([they sum to one](sum-born-prob-eq-one.md)).
