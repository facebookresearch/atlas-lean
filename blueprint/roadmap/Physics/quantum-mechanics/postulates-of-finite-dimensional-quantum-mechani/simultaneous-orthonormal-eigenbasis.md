# Simultaneous diagonalization

Commuting Hermitian matrices `A` and `B` share an orthonormal eigenbasis: there are a unitary matrix
`U` and real vectors `a`, `b` with `Uᴴ * A * U = diag(a)` and `Uᴴ * B * U = diag(b)`. The joint
eigenspaces of `A` and `B` are mutually orthogonal and span the space, and the columns of `U` form
an orthonormal basis subordinate to that decomposition.
