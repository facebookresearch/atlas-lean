# No finite-dimensional canonical commutation relation

For `n ≥ 1` and real `ħ ≠ 0`, no complex `n × n` matrices `X`, `P` satisfy
`X * P - P * X = i * ħ * 1`: the trace of a commutator vanishes, while the trace of the scalar
matrix is `n * i * ħ ≠ 0`. This is why position and momentum need unbounded operators with explicit
domains; the statement concerns finite dimensions only.
