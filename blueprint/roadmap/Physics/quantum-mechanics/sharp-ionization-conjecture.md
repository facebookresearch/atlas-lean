# Sharp ionization conjecture

Can a nucleus of charge Z bind more than Z+1 electrons in the nonrelativistic Coulomb ground-state model? This is a rigorous periodic-table question about bound states, not about metastable anions or condensed-phase chemistry.

## What would settle it

For integer Z≥1 and N≥Z+2, prove that the N-electron Hamiltonian on the appropriate antisymmetric spin space has no bound ground state. Define the operator, domain, electron spin, and meaning of binding precisely; establish any equivalence to the ionization threshold used in the argument.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Nam (EMS Newsletter, December 2020) records the theorem that a minimizer exists when N < Z + 1 and presents a sharp transition at N = Z + 1 as the natural guess. His Conjecture 2 is weaker: some universal constant C, possibly C = 1, such that no minimizer exists when N > Z + C. The question on this page is the sharp form.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
