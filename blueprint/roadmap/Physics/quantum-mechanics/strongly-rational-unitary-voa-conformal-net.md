# Energy bounds and a local conformal net for strongly rational unitary VOAs

Let $V$ be a simple VOA of CFT type with a positive complex inner product and unitary structure (conjugate-linear involution fixing the normalized vacuum and conformal vector, preserving modes, with the invariant adjoint formula). Assume strong rationality: self-contragredience for the graded dual, complete reducibility of admissible modules, and finite codimension of the span of $a_{(-2)}b$.

For each $a\in V$ some $C>0$ and integers $p,k\ge0$ satisfy $\|a_{(n)}b\|\le C(1+|n|)^p\|(1+L_0)^k b\|$ for all $n\in\mathbb Z$ and $b\in V$. On the Hilbert completion let the algebra of an arc be generated by the four bounded entries of the orthogonal graph projection for each of the following operators. Choose a homogeneous state $a$ in grade $d$ and a smooth circle function $f$ with topological support contained in the arc. The candidate on the state core uses the Fourier coefficients of $f$ against physical modes $a_{(m+d-1)}$. The source uses the sum when the linear-map series is pointwise summable on the core, and otherwise uses the zero map. The source's smooth smeared operator uses this core construction when all states have polynomial energy bounds and the coefficients are rapidly decaying, and otherwise uses the zero operator. Take its specified operator closure, and include its graph-projection entries only when this closure has closed graph. This is a condition for admitting generators, not an assertion that every smearing described without those conventions is closable. The algebras for complementary arcs commute.

This same family supports a separable irreducible conformal net: it depends only on arc carriers, is isotone and local, has continuous Möbius unitaries and continuous projective smooth-diffeomorphism unitaries extending them, and is covariant under arc transport. Diffeomorphisms pointwise fixing an arc act trivially on its algebra. There is a norm-one Möbius-fixed vector spanning the fixed vectors and cyclic for the generated global algebra, and a self-adjoint operator $T$ that has nonnegative real quadratic form on its graph. At rotation parameter $t$ the Möbius action agrees with the following source-defined bounded map. At $z=\pm ir$ ($r=n+1$), choose a bounded operator $R_z$ with $(R_zy,y+zR_zy)$ in the graph of $T$ for every $y$, or zero if there is none. Put $A_r=(r^2/2)(R_{ir}+R_{-ir})$. Use the bounded map given by the pointwise norm limit on Hilbert vectors of $\exp(itA_{n+1})$ if that limit exists as a bounded operator, and the zero map otherwise. The common commutant consists of scalars. No complete rationality or braided equivalence is asserted.

## Depends on

- VOA mode and adjoint identities, graded duality and admissible modules, Fourier smearing, closable operators and graph projections, and the circle group actions.

## Proof depends on

- Energy bounds, summability and closability, strong commutation of graph projections on complementary arcs, and construction of implementers and the vacuum sector.

## Expansion

Break out the energy estimate and proof that the smearing on the energy-bounded core agrees with the Fourier series (rather than any totalized zero branch). Establish closure and graph-entry locality before extending to the net. Verify the two group actions, local triviality and the rotation generator for the same interval algebras.
