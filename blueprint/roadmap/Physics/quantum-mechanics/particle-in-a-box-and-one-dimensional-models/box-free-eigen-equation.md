# Extended box states solve the free Schrödinger equation

Free differential-expression identity: for `n ≥ 1`, `L > 0` and `m > 0`, the sine extension
`ψ_n(x) = √(2/L) * sin (n * π * x / L)` of the `n`-th box state to all of `ℝ` satisfies
`-(ħ^2/2m) ψ_n'' = E_n ψ_n` at every real `x`. Outside `[0, L]` this is a property of the extension,
not of the box Hamiltonian, whose states vanish there; the
[eigen-equation inside the box](box-eigen-equation.md) is the statement on the interior.
