# Pointwise ladder commutator for twice-differentiable functions

Pointwise ladder identity `a (a† f) (x) - a† (a f) (x) = f x` for any `f : ℝ → ℂ` that has a
derivative at `x` and whose derivative `f'` also has a derivative at `x`: a formal identity of the
differential expressions `a` and `a†`, by the product rule.
