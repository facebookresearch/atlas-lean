# Closed form of the free-electron polyene wavelength

Model estimate (free-electron + box, not a measurement or a theorem about real polyenes): closed
form of the model wavelength, `wavelength = 8 * m * c * L^2 / (h * (2k+1))`, for `k ≥ 1` double
bonds with `L = (2k+1) * a`, where `a > 0`, `m > 0`, `ħ > 0` and `c > 0`.
