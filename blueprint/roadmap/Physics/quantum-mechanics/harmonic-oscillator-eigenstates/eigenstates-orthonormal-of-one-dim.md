# Orthonormality of harmonic oscillator eigenstates

For a $d$-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator, the Cartesian eigenstates $\psi_n$, indexed by $n \in \mathbb{N}^d$ and each a product of one-dimensional oscillator eigenfunctions, satisfy $\langle\psi_n, \psi_{n'}\rangle = \delta_{nn'}$ in $L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$.
