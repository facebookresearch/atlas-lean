# Anderson localization throughout the spectrum for the two-dimensional Anderson–Bernoulli model

This node records a research direction: for the random Schrödinger operator H = −Δ + δV on ℓ²(ℤ²), with V an independent Bernoulli potential, establish localization at all energies, not only near the edge of the spectrum.

## What would settle it

Fix the Bernoulli law, the coupling δ > 0 and one localization property (spectral localization, exponential decay of eigenfunctions, or dynamical localization, which are different statements) and prove that it holds almost surely throughout the spectrum, or at all energies in a stated range of δ.

## Status

No cited source poses this question in the form above; it is a research direction motivated by the cited results. This page does not certify the status or novelty of any particular formulation. The status evidence for this direction is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Ding and Smart prove that, for energies sufficiently close to the edge of the spectrum, the resolvent on a large square is likely to decay exponentially, which implies almost sure Anderson localization near the edge of the spectrum for this model.

This page records a research direction and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
