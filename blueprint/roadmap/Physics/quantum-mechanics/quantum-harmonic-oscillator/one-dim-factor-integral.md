# One-dimensional oscillator eigenfunctions are orthonormal

For `d ∈ ℕ` and a `d`-dimensional harmonic oscillator with mass `m > 0` and frequencies
`ω_1, …, ω_d > 0`, an axis `i` and multi-indices `n, n' ∈ ℕᵈ`, let `ψ_k` be the `k`-th eigenfunction
of the one-dimensional oscillator of mass `m` and frequency `ω_i`. Then
`∫_ℝ conj(ψ_{n_i}(t)) ψ_{n'_i}(t) dt = 1` if `n_i = n'_i` and `0` otherwise.
