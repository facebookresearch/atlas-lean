# Eigenstates of the d-dimensional oscillator are orthonormal

For `d ∈ ℕ` and a `d`-dimensional harmonic oscillator with mass `m > 0` and frequencies
`ω_1, …, ω_d > 0` and multi-indices `n, n' ∈ ℕᵈ`, the eigenstates `ψ_n` and `ψ_{n'}` (products of
one-dimensional eigenfunctions, as elements of `L²(ℝᵈ)`) satisfy `⟨ψ_n, ψ_{n'}⟩ = 1` if `n = n'` and
`0` otherwise.
