# A uniform positive intensity gives the area-weighted average of τ

Let `s` be a finite set of patches with areas `A i` and transmission coefficients `τ i` (any
reals). If every patch of `s` receives the same incident intensity `I i = I₀`, with `I₀ > 0`,
then the [effective power transmission coefficient](effective-power-transmission.md) is the
area-weighted average `(∑_{i ∈ s} A i * τ i) / (∑_{i ∈ s} A i)`. Neither positive areas nor a
nonempty `s` is needed: when `∑_{i ∈ s} A i = 0` both sides are `0`, with the convention
`x / 0 = 0`. At `I₀ = 0` every weight vanishes and the effective coefficient is `0` instead.
