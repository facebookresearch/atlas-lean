# The effective coefficient is transmitted over incident power

For all real intensities `I i`, areas `A i` and coefficients `τ i` and every finite set `s` of
patches, the [effective power transmission coefficient](effective-power-transmission.md) is the
quotient of the total transmitted power by the total incident power:
`τ_eff = (∑_{i ∈ s} I i * A i * τ i) / (∑_{i ∈ s} I i * A i)`. No hypothesis is needed: when the
total incident power `∑_{i ∈ s} I i * A i` is `0`, for example when `s` is empty, both sides are
`0`, by `0⁻¹ = 0` on the left and `x / 0 = 0` on the right.
