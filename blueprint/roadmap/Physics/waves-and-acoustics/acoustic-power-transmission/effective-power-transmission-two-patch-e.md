# Two patches of equal positive area and intensity average τ₁ and τ₂

Take two patches with the same area `A > 0`, under the same incident intensity `I₀ > 0`, and
with transmission coefficients `τ₁` and `τ₂` (any reals). Their
[effective power transmission coefficient](effective-power-transmission.md) is the arithmetic
mean `(τ₁ + τ₂) / 2`.
