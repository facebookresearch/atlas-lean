# N ≥ 1 equal sources of positive intensity add 10 log₁₀ N decibels

Let `I > 0` and let `N ≥ 1` be a natural number. If the total intensity is the sum of `N` copies
of `I`, `I_tot = I + ⋯ + I = N I` (incoherent addition of `N` equal sources, taken as a
hypothesis), then the [level difference](intensity-level-difference.md) of `I_tot` over `I` is
`10 log₁₀ (I_tot / I) = 10 log₁₀ N` decibels, with `log₁₀` the base-10 logarithm.
