# At a common positive impedance, intensity level equals pressure level

For a positive rms pressure `p`, a positive reference pressure `p_ref` and a positive impedance
`Z`, the [intensity level](intensity-level.md) of `I = p² / Z` against the reference intensity
`I_ref = p_ref² / Z` is the pressure level: `10 log₁₀ (I / I_ref) = 20 log₁₀ (p / p_ref)`, with
`log₁₀` the base-10 logarithm. The same impedance `Z` enters both intensities: it cancels, and
the square gives the factor `2`.
