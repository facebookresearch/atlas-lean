# Level differences of positive intensities are reference-independent

For positive intensities `I₁`, `I₂` and a positive reference intensity `I_ref`, the difference of
their [intensity levels](intensity-level.md) is their [level difference](intensity-level-difference.md):
`10 log₁₀ (I₂ / I_ref) - 10 log₁₀ (I₁ / I_ref) = 10 log₁₀ (I₂ / I₁)`, with `log₁₀` the base-10
logarithm, so it does not depend on the reference.
