# A real function of t and x as a one-component vector field

Let `φ(t, y)` be a real function of a time `t` and a real position `y`, time first. The
associated field takes a time `t`, read as a real number, and a point `x` of one-dimensional
Euclidean space, with coordinate `x₀`, to the vector of the one-dimensional Euclidean space `ℝ¹`
whose single component is `φ(t, x₀)`. This lets the time derivative and the vector Laplacian of
fields on space, which act on vector-valued fields, be applied to `φ`
([Laplacian](laplacian-vec-scalar-wave-field.md), [time derivative](deriv-deriv-scalar-wave-field.md));
no field equation is assumed.
