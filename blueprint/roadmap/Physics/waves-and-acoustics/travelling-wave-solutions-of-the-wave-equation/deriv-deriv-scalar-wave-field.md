# The iterated time derivative of the field is the second t-derivative

Let `φ`, `φ_t` and `φ_tt` be real functions of `(t, y)`, fix a time `t` and a point `x` of
one-dimensional space with coordinate `x₀`, and let `u` be the
[one-component vector field](scalar-wave-field.md) of `φ`. Suppose that, at the point `x`, the
slice `s ↦ φ(s, x₀)` has derivative `φ_t(s, x₀)` at every real time `s`, and `s ↦ φ_t(s, x₀)` has
derivative `φ_tt(t, x₀)` at `t`. Then the time derivative of the time derivative of `s ↦ u(s, x)`,
taken at `t`, is the vector with single component `φ_tt(t, x₀)`.
