# Second space derivative of the travelling wave A cos(kx − ckt)

For all real `A`, `k`, `c`, `x` and `t`, the travelling wave `u(x, t) = A cos(k x - c k t)`
satisfies, at fixed `t`, `∂²u/∂x² (x, t) = -k² u(x, t)`: the derivative in `x` of the
[space derivative](wave-first-deriv-space.md) `-k A sin(k x - c k t)`. The wave travels towards
increasing `x` at speed `c` when `c > 0` and `k ≠ 0`. In the physical reading `c > 0` is the wave
speed; the identity holds for all real values.
