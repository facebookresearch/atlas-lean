# For V, c, A > 0 and E₀ ≠ 0, the energy ratio at T60 is 10⁻⁶

For a positive volume `V`, speed of sound `c` and absorption area `A`, and a nonzero initial
energy `E₀`, the [Sabine energy](sabine-energy.md) `E(t) = E₀ exp(-k t)`, with the
[Sabine rate](sabine-rate.md) `k`, satisfies `E(T60) / E₀ = 1 / 10⁶` at the
[reverberation time](sabine-t60.md) `T60`, whatever the value of `E₀`: a decay of `60 dB` on the
energy scale `10 log₁₀`. At `E₀ = 0` the ratio would be `0`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
