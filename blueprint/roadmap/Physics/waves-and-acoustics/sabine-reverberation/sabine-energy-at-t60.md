# For V, c, A > 0, the Sabine energy at T60 is E₀ / 10⁶

For a positive volume `V`, speed of sound `c` and absorption area `A`, and every real initial
energy `E₀`, the [Sabine energy](sabine-energy.md) `E₀ exp(-k t)` with the
[Sabine rate](sabine-rate.md) `k` equals `E₀ / 10⁶` at the [reverberation time](sabine-t60.md)
`t = T60`. For `E₀ > 0` the energy has then fallen by `60 dB` on the energy scale `10 log₁₀`.
