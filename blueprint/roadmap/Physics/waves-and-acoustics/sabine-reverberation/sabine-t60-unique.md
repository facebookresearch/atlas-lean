# For E₀, V, c, A > 0, a time t ≥ 0 with energy E₀ / 10⁶ is T60

Let the initial energy `E₀`, the volume `V`, the speed of sound `c` and the absorption area `A`
be positive. If a time `t ≥ 0` satisfies `E₀ exp(-k t) = E₀ / 10⁶`, where `k` is the
[Sabine rate](sabine-rate.md), then `t` is the [reverberation time](sabine-t60.md) `T60`. Since
`T60` is such a time ([energy at T60](sabine-energy-at-t60.md)) and is positive
([positivity](sabine-t60-pos.md)), it is the only time `t ≥ 0` at which the
[Sabine energy](sabine-energy.md) has fallen to `E₀ / 10⁶`.
