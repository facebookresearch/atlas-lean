# Exponential energy decay of the Sabine model

For real `E₀`, `k` and `t`, the Sabine energy is `E(t) = E₀ exp(-k t)`: the energy at time `t`
of a sound field with energy `E₀` at `t = 0` decaying at rate `k`, for example the
[Sabine rate](sabine-rate.md) `k = c A / (4 V)` of a room. Physically `E₀ > 0`, `k > 0` and
`t ≥ 0`; the definition takes any real values.
