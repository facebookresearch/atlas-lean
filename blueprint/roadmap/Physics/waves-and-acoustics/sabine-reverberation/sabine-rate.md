# Energy decay rate of a room in the Sabine model

For real `V`, `c` and `A`, the Sabine energy decay rate is `k = c A / (4 V)`. In the Sabine
diffuse-field model of a room, `V` is the volume, `c` the speed of sound and `A` the equivalent
absorption area, all positive; the definition takes any real values, and with the convention
`x / 0 = 0` the rate is `0` when `V = 0`.
