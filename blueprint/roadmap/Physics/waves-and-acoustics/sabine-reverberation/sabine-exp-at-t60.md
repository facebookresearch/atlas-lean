# For V, c, A > 0, the decay factor at T60 is 10⁻⁶

For a positive volume `V`, speed of sound `c` and absorption area `A`, with `k` the
[Sabine decay rate](sabine-rate.md) and `T60` the [reverberation time](sabine-t60.md), the decay
factor at `T60` is `exp(-k T60) = 1 / 10⁶`, since `k T60 = 6 ln 10`. The hypotheses cannot simply be
dropped: for zero absorption `k = 0` and, with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `T60 = 0`, so the factor
is `1`.
