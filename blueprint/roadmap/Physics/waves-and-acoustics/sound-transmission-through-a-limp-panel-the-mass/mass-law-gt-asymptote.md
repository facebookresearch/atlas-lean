# For x > 0, the limp-panel loss strictly exceeds the mass-law asymptote

For every real `x > 0`, `20 log₁₀ x < 10 log₁₀ (1 + x²)`, with `log₁₀` the base-10 logarithm. In
the normal-incidence limp-panel model ([mass law](mass-law-comparison.md)), this says that the
transmission loss `10 log₁₀ (1 + x²)` at the dimensionless ratio `x = ω m / (2 ρ c)` strictly
exceeds the mass-law asymptote `20 log₁₀ x`.
