# For x > 0, doubling x adds 20 log₁₀ 2 to the mass-law asymptote

For every real `x > 0`, `20 log₁₀ (2x) = 20 log₁₀ x + 20 log₁₀ 2`, with `log₁₀` the base-10
logarithm. In the normal-incidence limp-panel model ([mass law](mass-law-comparison.md)), doubling
the dimensionless ratio `x = ω m / (2 ρ c)`, for example by doubling the frequency or the surface
mass, raises the mass-law asymptote `20 log₁₀ x` by `20 log₁₀ 2`, about `6 dB`.
