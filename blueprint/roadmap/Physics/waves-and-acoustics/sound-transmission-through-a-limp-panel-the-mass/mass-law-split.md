# For x > 0, the loss is the mass-law asymptote plus a correction

For every real `x > 0`, `10 log₁₀ (1 + x²) = 20 log₁₀ x + 10 log₁₀ (1 + x⁻²)`, with `log₁₀` the
base-10 logarithm and `x⁻² = 1 / x²`. In the normal-incidence limp-panel model
([mass law](mass-law-comparison.md)), `10 log₁₀ (1 + x²)` is the transmission loss at the
dimensionless ratio `x = ω m / (2 ρ c)`, `20 log₁₀ x` is the mass-law asymptote, and the
correction `10 log₁₀ (1 + x⁻²)` is positive, since `1 + x⁻² > 1`.
