# Noise coefficient over damping coefficient has dimension L T^(−1/2)

The [white-noise force dimension](force-noise-dim.md) `M L T^(-3/2)` divided by the
[damping dimension](damping-dim.md) `M T⁻¹` is `L T^(-1/2)`: the noise amplitude `σ' = σ / c` of
the Langevin equation makes `σ' dW` a displacement.
