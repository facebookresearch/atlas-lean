# Dimension of the white-noise force coefficient

In the dimension system of the International System of Quantities, the coefficient `σ` of `dW` in
`c dx = -k x dt + σ dW` has the dimension `M L T^(-3/2)`: a force times the square root of a time,
since the Brownian increment `dW` has the dimension `T^(1/2)`
([exponents](force-noise-dim-exponent.md)).
