# The stationary variance σ² / (2 c k) is a squared displacement

The square of the [white-noise force dimension](force-noise-dim.md), divided by the product of the
[damping dimension](damping-dim.md) and the translational stiffness dimension `M T⁻²`, is `L²`:
the stationary variance `σ² / (2 c k)` has the dimension of a displacement squared.
