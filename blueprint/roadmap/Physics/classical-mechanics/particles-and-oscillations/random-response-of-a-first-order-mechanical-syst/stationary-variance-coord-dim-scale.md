# The stationary-variance formula commutes with a change of units

Let `u₁` and `u₂` be two choices of units for the base quantities of the International System of
Quantities, and for a dimension `D` let `s_D` be the factor by which coordinates of a quantity of
dimension `D` change from `u₁` to `u₂`. For real `σ`, `c > 0` and `k > 0`,

`(s_σ σ)² / (2 (s_c c) (s_k k)) = s_{L²} · σ² / (2 c k)`,

where `s_σ`, `s_c` and `s_k` are the factors of the [white-noise force](force-noise-dim.md),
[damping](damping-dim.md) and translational stiffness dimensions and `s_{L²}` that of a squared
length: converting the inputs and evaluating the formula gives the converted variance.
