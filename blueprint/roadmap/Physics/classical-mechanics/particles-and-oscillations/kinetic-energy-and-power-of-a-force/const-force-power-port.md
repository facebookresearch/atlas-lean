# Under a constant force, kinetic energy changes at the power ⟨F, v⟩

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m > 0`, `v₀, F ∈ E` and `t` a time. On the
[constant-force velocity](const-force-velocity.md) `v(s) = v₀ + (s / m) F`, the kinetic energy
`s ↦ m ‖v(s)‖² / 2` has derivative `⟨F, v(t)⟩` at `t`, the power delivered by the force.
