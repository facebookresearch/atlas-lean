# Velocity under a constant force

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m` a real number, `v₀, F ∈ E` and `t` a time. The velocity
at time `t` of a particle of mass `m` under the constant force `F`, started with velocity `v₀`, is

`v(t) = v₀ + (t / m) F`.

In the physical reading `m > 0`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `v(t) = v₀` when `m = 0`.
