# Work of a constant force is the kinetic-energy change and ⟨F, Δx⟩

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m > 0`, `x₀, v₀, F ∈ E` and `t₀`, `t₁` real times. On the
constant-force [position](const-force-position.md) `x` and [velocity](const-force-velocity.md) `v`,
the work of `F` over the time interval from `t₀` to `t₁` is the change of kinetic energy, and also
the inner product of `F` with the displacement:

`∫_{t₀}^{t₁} ⟨F, v(t)⟩ dt = m ‖v(t₁)‖² / 2 - m ‖v(t₀)‖² / 2 = ⟨F, x(t₁) - x(t₀)⟩`.

The integral is oriented, so both identities also hold for `t₁ < t₀`.
