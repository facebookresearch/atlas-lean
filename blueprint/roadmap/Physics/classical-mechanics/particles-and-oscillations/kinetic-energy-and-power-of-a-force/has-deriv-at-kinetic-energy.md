# Rate of change of kinetic energy

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m` a real number, `v, a : ℝ → E` functions of time and `t`
a time at which `v` has derivative `a(t)`. Then the kinetic energy `K(s) = m ‖v(s)‖² / 2` has
derivative

`K'(t) = ⟨m a(t), v(t)⟩`

at `t`: with `v` the velocity of a particle of mass `m`, this is the power of the force `m a`. In
the physical reading `m > 0`; the identity holds for every real `m`.
