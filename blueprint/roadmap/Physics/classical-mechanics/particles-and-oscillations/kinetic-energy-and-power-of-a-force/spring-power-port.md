# On an oscillator trajectory, kinetic energy changes at spring power

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0` and `ω = √(k / m)`, and let `A` and `t` be real numbers. On the oscillator
trajectory `x(s) = A cos (ω s)` with velocity `v(s) = -A ω sin (ω s)`, the kinetic energy
`s ↦ m v(s)² / 2` has derivative `(-k x(t)) v(t)` at `t`, the power of the spring force `-k x`.
