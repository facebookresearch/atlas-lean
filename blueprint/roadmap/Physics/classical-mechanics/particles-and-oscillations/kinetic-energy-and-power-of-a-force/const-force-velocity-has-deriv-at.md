# The constant-force velocity has derivative F / m

For every real inner product space `E`, real `m`, `v₀, F ∈ E` and time `t`, the
[velocity](const-force-velocity.md) `v(s) = v₀ + (s / m) F` has derivative `m⁻¹ F` at `t`. With the
convention `0⁻¹ = 0`, the derivative `m⁻¹ F` is `0` when `m = 0`, where the velocity is the constant
`v₀`.
