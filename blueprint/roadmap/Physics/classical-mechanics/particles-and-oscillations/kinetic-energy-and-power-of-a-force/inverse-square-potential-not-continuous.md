# For μ ≠ 0, the inverse-square potential is discontinuous at the centre

Let `E` be a real inner product space with a nonzero vector, and `μ ≠ 0`. Then the potential
`y ↦ -μ / ‖y‖` is not continuous at `0`, where its value is `0` by the convention `u / 0 = 0`. In
particular it has no gradient at the centre.
