# Spring work is the kinetic-energy gain and the potential-energy drop

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0` and `ω = √(k / m)`, and let `A`, `t₀`, `t₁` and `C` be real numbers. On the
oscillator trajectory `x(t) = A cos (ω t)` with velocity `v(t) = -A ω sin (ω t)`, the work of the
spring force `-k x` from `t₀` to `t₁` is

`∫_{t₀}^{t₁} (-k x(t)) v(t) dt = m v(t₁)² / 2 - m v(t₀)² / 2`,

and it is also `-((k x(t₁)² / 2 + C) - (k x(t₀)² / 2 + C))`, the decrease of the potential energy
`k x² / 2 + C` for any constant `C`. The integral is oriented.
