# Kinetic energy of a planar rigid body changes at ⟨F, V⟩ + τ θ′

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m > 0` and `J > 0`, `V, F : ℝ → E` and `θ, τ : ℝ → ℝ`, with
`V` continuously differentiable and `θ` twice continuously differentiable. Suppose the balances
`m V'(t) = F(t)` and `J θ''(t) = τ(t)` hold at every time `t`. Then at every time `t` the kinetic
energy `s ↦ m ‖V(s)‖² / 2 + J θ'(s)² / 2` has derivative

`⟨F(t), V(t)⟩ + τ(t) θ'(t)`,

the power at the force port plus the power at the torque port. Physically `V` is the velocity of
the centre of mass, `θ` the rotation angle about a fixed axis normal to the plane, and `J` the
constant moment of inertia about the parallel axis through the centre of mass; the balances are
model laws.
