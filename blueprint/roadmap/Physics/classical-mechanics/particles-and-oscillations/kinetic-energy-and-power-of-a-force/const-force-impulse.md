# Impulse of a constant force is the momentum change

Let `E` be a complete real inner product space, `m > 0`, `v₀, F ∈ E` and `t₀`, `t₁` real times. On
the [constant-force velocity](const-force-velocity.md) `v`, the impulse of `F` over the time
interval from `t₀` to `t₁` is the change of momentum:

`∫_{t₀}^{t₁} F dt = m v(t₁) - m v(t₀)`.

The integral is oriented, so the identity also holds for `t₁ < t₀`.
