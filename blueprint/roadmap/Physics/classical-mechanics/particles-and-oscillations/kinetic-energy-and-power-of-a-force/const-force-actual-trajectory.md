# For m > 0, the constant-force path solves Newton's law with its data

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m > 0` and `x₀, v₀, F ∈ E`. The
[position](const-force-position.md) `x(t) = x₀ + t v₀ + (t² / (2 m)) F` satisfies `x(0) = x₀`,
`x'(0) = v₀` and Newton's law `m x''(t) = F` at every time `t`.
