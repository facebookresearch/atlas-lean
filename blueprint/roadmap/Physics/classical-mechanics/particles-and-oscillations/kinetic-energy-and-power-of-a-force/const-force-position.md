# Position under a constant force

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m` a real number, `x₀, v₀, F ∈ E` and `t` a time. The
position at time `t` of a particle of mass `m` under the constant force `F`, started at `x₀` with
velocity `v₀`, is

`x(t) = x₀ + t v₀ + (t² / (2 m)) F`.

In the physical reading `m > 0` ([trajectory](const-force-actual-trajectory.md)); with the
convention `u / 0 = 0` the force term vanishes when `m = 0`.
