# The constant-force velocity is the derivative of the position

For every real inner product space `E`, real `m`, `x₀, v₀, F ∈ E` and time `t`, the
[position](const-force-position.md) `x(s) = x₀ + s v₀ + (s² / (2 m)) F` has derivative
`v(t) = v₀ + (t / m) F` at `t`, the [velocity](const-force-velocity.md). With the convention
`u / 0 = 0` this also holds for `m = 0`.
