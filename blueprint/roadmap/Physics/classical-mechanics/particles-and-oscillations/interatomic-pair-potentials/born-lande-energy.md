# Born–Landé lattice energy

The Born–Landé lattice energy with Avogadro number `N_A`, Madelung constant `M`, cation and anion
charge numbers `z₊`, `z₋`, elementary charge `e`, vacuum permittivity `ε₀`, equilibrium spacing
`r₀` and Born exponent `n` is

$$-\frac{N_A M z_+ z_- e^2}{4\pi \varepsilon_0 r_0}\Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{n}\Bigr),$$

for all real parameters, with division by `0` read as `0`.
