# Energy-preserving store perturbations change the mass by (ρ_L − ρ_F) s

For all real numbers `ρ_F`, `ρ_L`, `F`, `L` and `s`, perturbing the stores `(F, L)` to
`(F + ρ_L s, L - ρ_F s)` leaves the stored energy unchanged and changes the total mass by
`(ρ_L - ρ_F) s`:

`ρ_F (F + ρ_L s) + ρ_L (L - ρ_F s) = ρ_F F + ρ_L L` and
`((F + ρ_L s) + (L - ρ_F s)) - (F + L) = (ρ_L - ρ_F) s`.

So when `ρ_F ≠ ρ_L` and `s ≠ 0`, the two compositions have the same stored energy and different
total masses: the stored energy does not determine the composition. In the physical reading the
densities are positive and the stores nonnegative, which the perturbed stores satisfy when `s` is
small enough relative to `F` and `L`.
