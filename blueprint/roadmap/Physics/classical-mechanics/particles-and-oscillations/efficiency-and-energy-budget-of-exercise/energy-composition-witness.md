# Two compositions with equal energy and different masses

With energy densities `ρ_F = 2` and `ρ_L = 1`, the compositions `(F, L) = (1, 2)` and
`(3/2, 1)` both have stored energy `ρ_F F + ρ_L L = 4`
(`2 · 1 + 1 · 2 = 4` and `2 · (3/2) + 1 · 1 = 4`), but total masses `F + L` equal to `3` and
`5/2`, which differ.
