# Metabolic power is at least the mechanical power

Let `P_base ≥ 0` and `P_mech ≥ 0` be real numbers (baseline and mechanical power) and let the
efficiency `η` satisfy `0 < η ≤ 1`. Then the metabolic power of the declared power model
`P_met = P_base + P_mech / η` is at least the mechanical power:

`P_mech ≤ P_base + P_mech / η`.
