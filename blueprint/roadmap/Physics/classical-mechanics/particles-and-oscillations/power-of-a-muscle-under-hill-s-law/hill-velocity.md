# Shortening velocity under Hill's law

For real numbers `a`, `b`, `F₀` and `F`, the shortening velocity of Hill's law at force `F` is

`v(F) = b (F₀ - F) / (F + a)`,

from the shortening hyperbola `(F + a) v = b (F₀ - F)`. In the model `a`, `b` and `F₀` are
positive and `F ∈ [0, F₀]`, so `F + a > 0`. The formula is defined for all real inputs; with the
convention `u / 0 = 0` it is `0` at `F = -a`.
