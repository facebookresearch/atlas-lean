# Mechanical power under Hill's law

For real numbers `a`, `b`, `F₀` and `F`, the mechanical power at force `F` is
`P(F) = F v(F)`, where `v(F) = b (F₀ - F) / (F + a)` is the
[Hill shortening velocity](hill-velocity.md). In the model `a`, `b` and `F₀` are positive and
`F ∈ [0, F₀]`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `P(-a) = 0`.
