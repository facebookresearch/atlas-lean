# Planar scalar moment of a family of forces

For a finite index set `ρ`, positions `r_i ∈ ℝ²` and forces `F_i ∈ ℝ²` (`i ∈ ρ`), with components
indexed `0` and `1`, the planar moment about the origin is

`M(r, F) = ∑_{i ∈ ρ} (r_{i,0} F_{i,1} - r_{i,1} F_{i,0})`,

the sum of the out-of-plane components of the cross products `r_i × F_i`. Only planar scalar
moments are treated; the moment about a point `o` is `M(r - o, F)`.
