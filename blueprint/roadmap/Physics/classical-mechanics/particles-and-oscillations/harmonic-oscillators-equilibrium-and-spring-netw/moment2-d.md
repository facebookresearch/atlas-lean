# Planar scalar moment of a family of forces

For a finite index set `ρ`, positions `r_i ∈ ℝ²` and forces `F_i ∈ ℝ²` (`i ∈ ρ`), with components
indexed `0` and `1`, the planar moment about the origin is

`M(r, F) = ∑_{i ∈ ρ} (r_{i,0} F_{i,1} - r_{i,1} F_{i,0})`,

the sum of the out-of-plane components of the cross products `r_i × F_i`. The moment about a point
`o` is `M(r - o, F)`. This is the planar (scalar) moment; for the three-dimensional moment see the
[moment of a finite load system about a reference point](../../rigid-bodies-and-mechanisms/statics-of-force-systems/moment-about.md).
