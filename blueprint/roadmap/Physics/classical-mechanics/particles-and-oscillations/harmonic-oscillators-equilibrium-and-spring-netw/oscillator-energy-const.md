# For m ≠ 0, the energy is conserved along x′ = v, v′ = −(k/m) x

Let `m ≠ 0` and `k` be real numbers and `x, v : ℝ → ℝ` functions such that at every time `t`, `x`
has derivative `v(t)` and `v` has derivative `-(k / m) x(t)`. Then the
[oscillator energy](oscillator-energy.md) `(1/2) m v² + (1/2) k x²` takes the same value at any two
times: for all real `t₁` and `t₂`,

`(1/2) m v(t₁)² + (1/2) k x(t₁)² = (1/2) m v(t₂)² + (1/2) k x(t₂)²`.

No sign condition on `m` or `k` is needed. In the physical reading `m > 0` and `k > 0`.
