# For an equilibrated family, the moment does not depend on the point

For a finite index set `ρ`, positions `r_i ∈ ℝ²` and an [equilibrated](is-equilibrated.md) force
family `F` (`∑ F_i = 0`), the [planar moment](moment2-d.md) about every point `o ∈ ℝ²` equals the
moment about the origin: `M(r - o, F) = M(r, F)`.
