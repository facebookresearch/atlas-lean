# Equal moments about two unit shifts force a zero resultant

For a finite index set `ρ`, positions `r_i ∈ ℝ²` and forces `F_i ∈ ℝ²`, if the
[planar moment](moment2-d.md) about the point `(1, 0)` and the moment about `(0, 1)` both equal the
moment about the origin, then the [resultant](resultant-force.md) `∑ F_i` is `0`.
