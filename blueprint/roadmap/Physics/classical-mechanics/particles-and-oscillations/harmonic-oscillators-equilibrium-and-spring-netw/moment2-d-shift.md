# Shifting the moment point subtracts the moment of the resultant

For a finite index set `ρ`, positions `r_i ∈ ℝ²`, forces `F_i ∈ ℝ²` and a point `o ∈ ℝ²`, the
[planar moment](moment2-d.md) about `o` is the moment about the origin minus the moment of the
[resultant](resultant-force.md) `R = ∑ F_i` placed at `o`:

`M(r - o, F) = M(r, F) - (o₀ R₁ - o₁ R₀)`.
