# Internal axial-member forces have zero planar moment about every point

Let `J` be a set of joints with positions `r : J → ℝ²`, `M` a finite set of members, member `e`
joining the joints `a(e)` and `b(e)`, `c : M → ℝ` coefficients and `o ∈ ℝ²` a point. Then the
[internal endpoint forces](member-endpoint-force.md), applied at the
[endpoint positions](member-endpoint-position.md), have zero [planar moment](moment2-d.md) about
`o`: each member is a central equal and opposite pair along its separation vector. With the
[zero resultant](member-endpoint-resultant-force-eq-zero.md), the internal member forces have zero
resultant planar wrench.
