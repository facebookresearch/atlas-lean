# Normal modes with distinct eigenvalues are mass-orthogonal

Let `ι` be a finite set, `K` a symmetric real `ι × ι` matrix (`Kᵀ = K`), `m : ι → ℝ` masses, and
`v, w : ι → ℝ` with `K v = ω² (m ⊙ v)` and `K w = μ² (m ⊙ w)`, where `(m ⊙ v)_i = m_i v_i` and
`ω² ≠ μ²` are real numbers (generalized eigenvalue problems). Then `v` and `w` are orthogonal for
the [mass-weighted form](inner-product-m.md): `∑_{i ∈ ι} m_i v_i w_i = 0`. No positivity of the
masses and no nonzero condition on `v` or `w` is needed.
