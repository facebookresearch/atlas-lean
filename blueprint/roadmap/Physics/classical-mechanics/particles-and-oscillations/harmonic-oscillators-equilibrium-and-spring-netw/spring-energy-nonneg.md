# For nonnegative stiffnesses, the spring energy is nonnegative

Let `ι` be a finite set of nodes with pair weights `k_{ij} ≥ 0` and wall stiffnesses `w_i ≥ 0` for
all nodes, and displacements `u : ι → ℝ`. Then the [spring energy](spring-energy.md) satisfies
`U(k, w, u) ≥ 0`. No reciprocity of the weights is needed.
