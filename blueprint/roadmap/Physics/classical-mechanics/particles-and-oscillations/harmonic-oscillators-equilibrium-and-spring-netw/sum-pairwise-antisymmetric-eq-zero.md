# Antisymmetric pair forces sum to zero

Let `ι` be a finite set and `F : ι × ι → ℝ` a real-valued pair force with `F(j, i) = -F(i, j)`
for all `i` and `j` (action and reaction). Then `∑_{(i, j) ∈ ι × ι} F(i, j) = 0`. The hypothesis at
`i = j` gives `F(i, i) = 0`. For vector-valued pair forces the same conclusion holds component by
component.
