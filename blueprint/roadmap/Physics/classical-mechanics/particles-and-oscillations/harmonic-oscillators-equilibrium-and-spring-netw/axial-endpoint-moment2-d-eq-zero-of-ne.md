# Axial endpoint forces of a pin-jointed table have zero planar moment

Let `J` be a set of joints with positions `r : J → ℝ²`, `M` a finite set of members, member `e`
joining the joints `a(e)` and `b(e)` with `r(a(e)) ≠ r(b(e))` for every member, `N : M → ℝ` axial
forces and `o ∈ ℝ²` a point. Then the [axial endpoint forces](axial-endpoint-force.md), applied at
the [endpoint positions](member-endpoint-position.md), have zero [planar moment](moment2-d.md)
about `o`. With the [zero resultant](axial-endpoint-resultant-force-eq-zero-o.md), the internal
member forces have zero resultant planar wrench.
