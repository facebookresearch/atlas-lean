# Work of a force family under a common constant displacement

For finite sets `ρ` (forces) and `ι` (components), a force family `F : ρ → (ι → ℝ)` and a
displacement `d : ι → ℝ`, the total work is

`W(F, d) = ∑_{r ∈ ρ} ∑_{i ∈ ι} F_{r,i} d_i`,

the work of all forces when every point of application undergoes the same constant
displacement `d`. It is a finite sum, not a path integral.
