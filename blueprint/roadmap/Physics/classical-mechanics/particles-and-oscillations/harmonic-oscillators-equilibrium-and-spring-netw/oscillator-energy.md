# Energy of a one-dimensional harmonic oscillator

For real numbers `m`, `k`, `x` and `v`, the oscillator energy is

`E(m, k, x, v) = (1/2) m v² + (1/2) k x²`,

the kinetic energy of a mass `m` with velocity `v` plus the potential energy of a spring of
stiffness `k` at displacement `x`. In the physical reading `m > 0` and `k > 0`; the formula is
defined for all real inputs.
