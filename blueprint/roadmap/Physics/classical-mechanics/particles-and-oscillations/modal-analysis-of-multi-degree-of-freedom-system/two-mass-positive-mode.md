# For positive masses and spring, (m₂, −m₁) is an oscillating mode

Let `m₁ > 0`, `m₂ > 0` and `k > 0`, with `M = diag(m₁, m₂)` and `K = k [[1, -1], [-1, 1]]`. Then
the [squared frequency](two-mass-frequency-sq.md) `ω² = k (m₁ + m₂) / (m₁ m₂)` is positive, the
vector `φ = (m₂, -m₁)` satisfies `K φ = ω² M φ`, and `φ` is mass-orthogonal to the rigid
translation: `(1, 1) · (M φ) = 0`.
