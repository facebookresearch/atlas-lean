# Spectral displacement of a response on a finite horizon

For a real `T` and a function `u : ℝ → ℝ`, the spectral displacement is the supremum of `|u(t)|`
over `t ∈ [0, T]`. For `T ≥ 0` and `u` continuous on `[0, T]` it is the maximum of `|u|` there.
With the convention that the supremum of an empty or unbounded set of reals is `0`, it is `0`
when `T < 0` or when `|u|` is unbounded on `[0, T]`.
