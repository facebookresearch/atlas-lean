# Spring weights of two masses joined by one spring

For a real `k`, the pair weights of two masses joined by one spring of stiffness `k` are
`k₁₂ = k₂₁ = k` and `k₁₁ = k₂₂ = 0`: reciprocal weights without self-springs, as used by the
[spring-network energy](../harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/spring-energy.md) and [stiffness matrix](../harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/stiffness-matrix.md).
