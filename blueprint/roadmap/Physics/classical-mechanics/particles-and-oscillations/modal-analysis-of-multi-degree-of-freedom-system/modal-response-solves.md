# The modal response solves M x″ + K x = 0 with its initial data

Let `n` be a finite set, `M`, `K` and `C` real `n × n` matrices and `μ : n → ℝ` with `Cᵀ M C = 1`,
`Cᵀ K C = diag(μ)` and `μ_i ≥ 0` for every `i`, and let `x₀, v₀ ∈ ℝⁿ`. Then the
[modal response](modal-response.md) `x` is twice continuously differentiable, satisfies
`M x''(t) + K x(t) = 0` at every time `t`, and has `x(0) = x₀` and `x'(0) = v₀`.
