# Rayleigh proportional damping matrix

For real numbers `α` and `β` and real `n × n` matrices `M` and `K` (`n` a finite set), the
Rayleigh damping matrix is

`C_d = α M + β K`,

a combination of the mass matrix `M` and the stiffness matrix `K`, for the damped equation
`M x'' + C_d x' + K x = f(t)`. In a coherent unit system `α` has units of inverse time and `β` of
time.
