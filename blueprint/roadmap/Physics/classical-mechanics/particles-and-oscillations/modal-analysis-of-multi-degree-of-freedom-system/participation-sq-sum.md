# The effective modal masses sum to the mass seen by the base motion

Let `n` be a finite set and `M`, `Φ` real `n × n` matrices with `Φᵀ M Φ = 1`, and let `r ∈ ℝⁿ`.
Then the [participation factors](participation.md) `Γ = Φᵀ M r` satisfy

`∑_j Γ_j² = rᵀ M r`:

the effective modal masses `Γ_j²` sum to the total mass seen by the base motion. Any such `Φ`
works, including any mass-orthonormal choice inside a repeated eigenvalue.
