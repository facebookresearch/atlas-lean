# Spring energy of two masses and one spring

For every real `k` and displacements `u = (u₁, u₂)`, the
[spring energy](../harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/spring-energy.md) of the [two-mass weights](two-mass-weights.md) without wall
springs is `k (u₂ - u₁)² / 2`, and it equals `(1/2) uᵀ K u` with `K = k [[1, -1], [-1, 1]]` the
[two-mass stiffness matrix](stiffness-matrix-two-mass-weights.md).
