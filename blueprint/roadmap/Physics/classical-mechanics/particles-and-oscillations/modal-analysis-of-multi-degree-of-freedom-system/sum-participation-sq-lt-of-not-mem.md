# Dropping a participating mode loses effective mass

Let `n` be a finite set, `M`, `Φ` real `n × n` matrices with `Φᵀ M Φ = 1`, `r ∈ ℝⁿ`, `s` a set of
modes and `j ∉ s` a mode with nonzero [participation factor](participation.md) `Γ_j ≠ 0`. Then the
effective mass kept by the modes of `s` is strictly smaller than the total:

`∑_{i ∈ s} Γ_i² < rᵀ M r`.
