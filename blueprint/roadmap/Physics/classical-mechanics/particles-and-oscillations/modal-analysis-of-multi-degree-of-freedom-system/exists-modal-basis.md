# A mass-orthonormal modal basis exists

Let `n` be a finite set, `M` a positive-definite and `K` a positive-semidefinite real `n × n`
matrix (positive definiteness and semidefiniteness include symmetry). Then there is an invertible
real `n × n` matrix `C` with `Cᵀ M C = 1`, and a vector `μ : n → ℝ` with `Cᵀ K C = diag(μ)` and
`μ_i ≥ 0` for every `i`. In the physical reading `M` is the mass matrix, `K` the stiffness matrix,
the columns of `C` are mass-orthonormal mode shapes and `μ_i = ω_i²` the squared modal angular
frequencies.
