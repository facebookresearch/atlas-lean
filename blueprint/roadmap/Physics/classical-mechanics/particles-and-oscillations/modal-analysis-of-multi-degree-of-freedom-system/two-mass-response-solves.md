# For k ≥ 0, the two-mass response solves M x″ + K x = 0 with its data

Let `m₁ > 0`, `m₂ > 0` and `k ≥ 0`, with `M = diag(m₁, m₂)` and `K = k [[1, -1], [-1, 1]]`, and
let `x₀, v₀ ∈ ℝ²`. Then the [two-mass response](two-mass-response.md) `x` is twice continuously
differentiable, satisfies `M x''(t) + K x(t) = 0` at every time `t`, and has `x(0) = x₀` and
`x'(0) = v₀`.
