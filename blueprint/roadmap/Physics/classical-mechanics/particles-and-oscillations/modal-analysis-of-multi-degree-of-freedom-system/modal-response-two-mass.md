# The modal response of two masses is the two-mass free response

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`, let `k` be a real number and `x₀, v₀ ∈ ℝ²`. The
[modal response](modal-response.md) of the [two-mass modal matrix](two-mass-modal-matrix.md) with
entries `(0, ω²)`, `ω² = k (m₁ + m₂) / (m₁ m₂)`, initial position `x₀` and velocity `v₀` is the
[two-mass response](two-mass-response.md): the centre-of-mass drift plus the modal response of the
spring extension, an oscillation at the angular frequency `√(ω²)` when `k > 0` and the drift
`(x₀₂ - x₀₁) + (v₀₂ - v₀₁) t` when `k ≤ 0`.
