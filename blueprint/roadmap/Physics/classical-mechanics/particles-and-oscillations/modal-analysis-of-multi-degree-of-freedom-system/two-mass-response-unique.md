# For k ≥ 0, every C² two-mass solution is the two-mass response

Let `m₁ > 0`, `m₂ > 0` and `k ≥ 0`, with `M = diag(m₁, m₂)` and `K = k [[1, -1], [-1, 1]]`. If
`x : ℝ → ℝ²` is twice continuously differentiable with `M x''(t) + K x(t) = 0` at every time `t`,
then `x` is the [two-mass response](two-mass-response.md) with its own initial data `x(0)` and
`x'(0)`.
