# Squared frequency of the two-mass oscillating mode

For real numbers `m₁`, `m₂` and `k`, the squared angular frequency of the oscillating mode of two
masses joined by a spring is

`ω² = k (m₁ + m₂) / (m₁ m₂)`.

In the physical reading `m₁, m₂ > 0` and `k > 0`, where `ω² > 0`
([positive mode](two-mass-positive-mode.md)); with the convention `u / 0 = 0` it is `0` when
`m₁ m₂ = 0`.
