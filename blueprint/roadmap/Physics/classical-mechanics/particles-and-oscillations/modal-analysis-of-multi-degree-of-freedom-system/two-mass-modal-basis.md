# For positive masses, the two-mass modal matrix is mass-orthonormal

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`, let `k` be a real number, `M = diag(m₁, m₂)`, `K = k [[1, -1], [-1, 1]]`
and `C` the [two-mass modal matrix](two-mass-modal-matrix.md). Then `Cᵀ M C = 1` and
`Cᵀ K C = diag(0, ω²)`, with `ω² = k (m₁ + m₂) / (m₁ m₂)`.
