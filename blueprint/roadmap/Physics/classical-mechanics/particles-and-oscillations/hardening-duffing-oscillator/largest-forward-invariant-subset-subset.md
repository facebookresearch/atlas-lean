# For m, k, β, c > 0, only the origin is invariant at zero energy rate

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0`, `β > 0` and `c > 0`, and let `K ⊆ ℝ²` be any set. Let `Z` be the set of
states `p ∈ K` at which the energy rate `DV(p) f(p)` vanishes, with `V` the
[Duffing energy](duffing-energy.md) and `f` the [Duffing field](duffing-field.md) (that is, `v = 0`).
Then the largest forward-invariant subset of `Z`, the union of all subsets `A ⊆ Z` through every
point of which there is a forward solution on `[0, ∞)` staying in `A`, is contained in `{(0, 0)}`.
