# For m, k, β, c > 0, every forward Duffing solution tends to the origin

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0`, `β > 0` and `c > 0`, and let `γ` be
a forward solution on `[0, ∞)`: a curve `γ : ℝ → ℝ²` that at every `t ≥ 0` has derivative `f(γ(t))`
within `[0, ∞)` (one-sided at `t = 0`) of the [Duffing field](duffing-field.md) `f`. Then `γ(t) → (0, 0)` as `t → ∞`.
With `c = 0` the energy is conserved and a displaced oscillator does not converge.
