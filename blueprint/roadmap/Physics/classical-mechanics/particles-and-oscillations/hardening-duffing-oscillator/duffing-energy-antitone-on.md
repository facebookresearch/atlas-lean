# For c ≥ 0, the Duffing energy is nonincreasing along forward solutions

Let `m > 0` and `c ≥ 0`, let `k` and `β` be real numbers, and let `γ` be
a forward solution on `[0, ∞)`: a curve `γ : ℝ → ℝ²` that at every `t ≥ 0` has derivative `f(γ(t))`
within `[0, ∞)` (one-sided at `t = 0`) of the [Duffing field](duffing-field.md) `f`. Then `t ↦ V(γ(t))`, with `V` the
[Duffing energy](duffing-energy.md), is nonincreasing on `[0, ∞)`.
