# The Duffing energy rate gives no exponential decay bound

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0` and `β > 0`, and let `c` be a real number. Then there is no `α > 0` such that
`DV(p) f(p) ≤ -α V(p)` for every state `p`, where `V` is the [Duffing energy](duffing-energy.md)
and `f` the [Duffing field](duffing-field.md): at a state with `v = 0` and `x ≠ 0` the left side is
`0` and the right side is negative.
