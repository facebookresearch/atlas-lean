# For m, k, β > 0, the Duffing energy sublevel sets are compact

Let `m > 0`, `k > 0` and `β > 0`, and let `r` be a real number. Then the sublevel set
`{(x, v) ∈ ℝ² | V(x, v) ≤ r}` of the [Duffing energy](duffing-energy.md) is compact. For `β < 0`
the sublevel sets are unbounded, so `β > 0` matters.
