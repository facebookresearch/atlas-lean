# Parameters of the mass–spring–damper as a damped oscillator

Let `m > 0`, `c ≥ 0` and `k > 0`. The [damped oscillator](mass-spring-damper.md) of the
mass–spring–damper has mass `m`, stiffness `k` and damping coefficient `c`; its decay rate
`c / (2 m)` is the [damping rate](atlas-damping.md) of the forced-oscillator convention; its
angular frequency is the [natural angular frequency](atlas-natural-frequency.md) `√(k / m)`; and
its damping coefficient is `2 m` times the damping rate: `c = 2 m · (c / (2 m))`.
