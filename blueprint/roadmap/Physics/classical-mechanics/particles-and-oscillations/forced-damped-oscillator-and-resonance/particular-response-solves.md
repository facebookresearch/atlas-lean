# For D ≠ 0, the particular response solves the forced equation

Let `m`, `c`, `k`, `F` and `Ω` be real numbers with nonzero
[steady-state denominator](steady-state-denominator.md) `D = (k - m Ω²)² + (c Ω)²`. Then at every
time `t` the [particular response](particular-response.md) `x_p` satisfies

`m x_p''(t) + c x_p'(t) + k x_p(t) = F cos (Ω t)`.

No sign condition on `m`, `c` or `k` is needed.
