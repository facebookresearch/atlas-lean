# The resonant response meets its envelope at the envelope-touch times

Let `m > 0` and `ω₀ > 0`, let `F` be a real number and `n` a natural number. At the
[envelope-touch time](resonance-envelope-time.md) `t_n`, the absolute value of the
[resonant response](resonant-response.md) equals its [envelope](abs-resonant-response-le.md):

`|x_r(t_n)| = |F| |t_n| / (2 m ω₀)`.
