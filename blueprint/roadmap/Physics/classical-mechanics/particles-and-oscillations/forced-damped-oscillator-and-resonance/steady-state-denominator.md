# Steady-state denominator of the forced mass–spring–damper

For real numbers `m`, `c`, `k` and `Ω`, the steady-state denominator of the harmonic response of
`m x'' + c x' + k x = F cos (Ω t)` is

`D = (k - m Ω²)² + (c Ω)²`.

It is always `≥ 0`; for `k > 0` it vanishes exactly at the undamped exact resonance `c = 0`,
`k = m Ω²` ([criterion](steady-state-denominator-ne-zero-iff.md)).
