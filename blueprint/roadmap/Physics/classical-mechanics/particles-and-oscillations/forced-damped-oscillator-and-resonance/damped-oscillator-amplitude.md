# Amplitude of the forced damped oscillator's steady state

Let `F`, `γ`, `ω₀` and `ω` be real numbers with `D = (ω₀² - ω²)² + (2 γ ω)² > 0`, and let
`A = F (ω₀² - ω²) / D` and `B = F (2 γ ω) / D` be the
[steady-state coefficients](damped-oscillator-coefficients.md). Then the amplitude of
`A cos (ω t) + B sin (ω t)` is

`√(A² + B²) = |F| / √D`.
