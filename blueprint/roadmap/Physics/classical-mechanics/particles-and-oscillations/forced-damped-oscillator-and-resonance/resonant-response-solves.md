# For k = m ω₀², the secular response solves m x″ + k x = F cos(ω₀ t)

Let `m > 0`, `ω₀ > 0` and `k = m ω₀²`, and let `F` and `t` be real numbers. Then the
[resonant response](resonant-response.md) `x_r(t) = F t sin (ω₀ t) / (2 m ω₀)` satisfies

`m x_r''(t) + k x_r(t) = F cos (ω₀ t)`

at the time `t`; since `t` is arbitrary, it solves the undamped equation forced at its natural
frequency at every time.
