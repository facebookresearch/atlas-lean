# Mass–spring–damper as a damped harmonic oscillator

For a mass `m > 0`, a viscous damping coefficient `c ≥ 0` (force per velocity) and a stiffness
`k > 0` (force per length), the mass–spring–damper is the damped harmonic oscillator with mass `m`,
stiffness `k` and damping coefficient `c`, whose free equation of motion is
`m x'' + c x' + k x = 0` for a displacement `x` on the one-dimensional line
([equation of motion](equation-of-motion-iff.md)). The damping coefficient is `c` itself, not the
rate `c / (2 m)` of the forced-oscillator convention ([damping rate](atlas-damping.md)).
