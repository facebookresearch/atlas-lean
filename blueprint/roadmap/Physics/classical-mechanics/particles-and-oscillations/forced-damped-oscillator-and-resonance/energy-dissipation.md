# The energy of a smooth free solution decreases at the rate c x′²

Let `m > 0`, `c ≥ 0` and `k > 0`, and let `x`, a map from time to the one-dimensional Euclidean
space, be infinitely differentiable and satisfy the equation of motion `m x'' + c x' + k x = 0` of
the [damped oscillator](mass-spring-damper.md). Then at every time `t` the mechanical energy
`E = (1/2) m ‖x'‖² + (1/2) k ‖x‖²` has derivative

`E'(t) = -c ‖x'(t)‖²`,

which is `≤ 0`: the energy does not increase, and it is constant when `c = 0`.
