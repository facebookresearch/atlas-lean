# Velocity of the secular resonant response

For real numbers `m`, `ω₀`, `F` and a time `t`, the velocity of the
[resonant response](resonant-response.md) is

`v_r(t) = F (sin (ω₀ t) + ω₀ t cos (ω₀ t)) / (2 m ω₀)`,

the derivative of `x_r` ([derivative](has-deriv-at-resonant-response.md)). With the convention
`u / 0 = 0` it is identically `0` when `m ω₀ = 0`.
