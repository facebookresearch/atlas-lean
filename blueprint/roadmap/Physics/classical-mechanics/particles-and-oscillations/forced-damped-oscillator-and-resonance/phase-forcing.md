# Phase-space forcing of the mass–spring–damper

For real numbers `m`, `F`, `Ω` and a time `t`, the phase-space forcing is

`b(t) = (0, F cos (Ω t) / m)`,

the force `F cos (Ω t)` divided by the mass, entering the velocity component. In the physical
reading `m > 0`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `b(t) = (0, 0)` when `m = 0`.
