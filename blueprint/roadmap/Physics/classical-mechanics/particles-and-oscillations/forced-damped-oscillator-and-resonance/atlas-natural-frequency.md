# Natural angular frequency of the forced-oscillator convention

For real numbers `m` and `k`, the natural angular frequency is `ω₀ = √(k / m)`, the frequency in
the forced-oscillator convention `x'' + 2 γ x' + ω₀² x = f cos (Ω t)`. In the physical reading
`m > 0` and `k > 0`. The formula is defined for all real inputs: with the conventions `u / 0 = 0`
and `√u = 0` for `u < 0`, it is `0` when `m = 0` or when `k / m < 0`.
