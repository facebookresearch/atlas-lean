# Smooth solutions with given initial data equal the free trajectory

Let `m > 0`, `c ≥ 0` and `k > 0`, and let `x₀` and `v₀` be real numbers. The selected free
trajectory of the [damped oscillator](mass-spring-damper.md) of the mass–spring–damper for the
[initial conditions](initial-conditions.md) `(x₀, v₀)`:

- satisfies the equation of motion `m x'' + c x' + k x = 0` at every time;
- has position `x₀ e` and velocity `v₀ e` at time `0`, where `e` is the unit vector of the line;
- equals every infinitely differentiable solution of the equation of motion with this position
  and velocity at time `0`.
