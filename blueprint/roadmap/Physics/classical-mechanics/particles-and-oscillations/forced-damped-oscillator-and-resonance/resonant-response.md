# Secular response at undamped exact resonance

For real numbers `m`, `ω₀`, `F` and a time `t`, the resonant (secular) response is

`x_r(t) = F t sin (ω₀ t) / (2 m ω₀)`,

the response of an undamped mass `m` to the force `F cos (ω₀ t)` at the natural angular frequency
`ω₀`, starting from rest at the origin ([equation](resonant-response-solves.md)). In the physical
reading `m > 0` and `ω₀ > 0`; with the convention `u / 0 = 0` it is identically `0` when
`m ω₀ = 0`.
