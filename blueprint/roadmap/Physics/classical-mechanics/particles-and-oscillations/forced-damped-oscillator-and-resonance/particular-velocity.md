# Velocity of the particular steady-state response

For real numbers `m`, `c`, `k`, `F`, `Ω` and a time `t`, the velocity of the
[particular response](particular-response.md) is

`v_p(t) = Ω (B cos (Ω t) - A sin (Ω t))`,

with the [in-phase coefficient](steady-state-cos.md) `A` and the
[quadrature coefficient](steady-state-sin.md) `B`; it is the derivative of `x_p`
([derivative](has-deriv-at-particular-response.md)).
