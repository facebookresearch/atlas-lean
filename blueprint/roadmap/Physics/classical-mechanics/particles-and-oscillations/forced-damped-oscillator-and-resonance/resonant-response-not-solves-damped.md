# With damping c ≠ 0 and F ≠ 0, the secular response is not a solution

Let `m > 0`, `ω₀ > 0` and `k = m ω₀²`, and let `c ≠ 0` and `F ≠ 0` be real numbers. Then the
[resonant response](resonant-response.md) `x_r` does not satisfy
`m x_r''(t) + c x_r'(t) + k x_r(t) = F cos (ω₀ t)` at every time `t`.
