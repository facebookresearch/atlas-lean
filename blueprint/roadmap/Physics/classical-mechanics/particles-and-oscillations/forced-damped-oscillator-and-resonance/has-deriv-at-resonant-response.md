# The resonant response has the resonant velocity as derivative

For all real `m`, `ω₀`, `F` and `t`, the [resonant response](resonant-response.md)
`x_r(t) = F t sin (ω₀ t) / (2 m ω₀)` has derivative `v_r(t)` at `t`, where
`v_r(t) = F (sin (ω₀ t) + ω₀ t cos (ω₀ t)) / (2 m ω₀)` is the
[resonant velocity](resonant-velocity.md).
