# Free equation of motion of the mass–spring–damper

Let `m > 0`, `c ≥ 0` and `k > 0`, and let `x` map time to the one-dimensional Euclidean space. Then
`x` satisfies the equation of motion of the [damped oscillator](mass-spring-damper.md) of the
mass–spring–damper if and only if

`m x''(t) + c x'(t) + k x(t) = 0` at every time `t`.

Here `x'` and `x''` are the first and second derivatives, with the convention that a derivative is
`0` where the function is not differentiable.
