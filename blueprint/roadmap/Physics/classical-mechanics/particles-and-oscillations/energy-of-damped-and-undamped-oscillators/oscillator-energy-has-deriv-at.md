# At a time where x'' = −ω₀² x, the oscillator energy is stationary

Let `ω₀` and `t` be real numbers and `x : ℝ → ℝ` a function that is differentiable at `t` and
whose derivative `x'` has derivative `-ω₀² x(t)` at `t`. Then the energy

`E(τ) = x'(τ)² / 2 + ω₀² x(τ)² / 2`

has derivative `0` at `t`. Here `x'(τ)` is the derivative of `x` at `τ`, taken to be `0` where
`x` is not differentiable; the hypotheses are needed only at the time `t`, so this alone does not
make `E` constant ([conservation](oscillator-energy-const.md)).
