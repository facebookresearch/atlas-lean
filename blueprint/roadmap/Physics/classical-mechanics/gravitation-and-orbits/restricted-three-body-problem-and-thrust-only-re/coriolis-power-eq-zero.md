# The Coriolis acceleration of an in-plane velocity has zero power

For reals `vx` and `vy` and the velocity `v = (vx, vy, 0)` in the orbital plane, the Coriolis
acceleration `-2 e₃ × v` of the unit-rate rotation with angular velocity `e₃` (that of the
[rotating frame](rotating-frame.md)) is `(2 vy, -2 vx, 0)`, and its power vanishes:
`v · (-2 e₃ × v) = 0`. Here `×` and `·` are the cross and dot products of real triples. These are
the velocity terms of the [rotating equations](rotating-equations.md), written as
`x'' = Ω_x + 2 y'` and `y'' = Ω_y - 2 x'`.
