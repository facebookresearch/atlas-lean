# For t < 2 the burn velocity has derivative 1 / (2 − t)

For every time `t < 2`, before the depletion time of the [burn mass](burn-mass.md) `2 - t`, the
[burn velocity](burn-velocity.md) `v(t) = log (2 / (2 - t))` has derivative `v'(t) = 1 / (2 - t)`,
which is positive. The burn itself lasts for `0 ≤ t ≤ 1`.
