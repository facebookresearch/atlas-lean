# Unforced motion off the primaries stays in the region 2 Ω ≥ C

Let `μ`, `t₀` and `t₁` be real and let `x, y : ℝ → ℝ` be twice continuously differentiable on
`ℝ`. Suppose that at every time `t` of the interval `[t₀, t₁]` the point `(x(t), y(t))` is off
both primaries, `r₁ > 0` and `r₂ > 0`, and the unforced rotating equations `x'' - 2 y' = Ω_x` and
`y'' + 2 x' = Ω_y` hold, with `Ω_x`, `Ω_y` the partial derivatives of the
[effective potential](effective-potential.md) `Ω` at `(x(t), y(t))`. Let `C` be the initial value
`2 Ω(x(t₀), y(t₀)) - (x'(t₀)² + y'(t₀)²)` of the [Jacobi function](jacobi-constant.md).
Then `C ≤ 2 Ω(x(t), y(t))` for every `t` in `[t₀, t₁]`: the trajectory stays in the region
`2 Ω ≥ C`. This follows from the [Jacobi integral](jacobi-constant-eq-of-rotating-equations.md),
since `x'² + y'² ≥ 0`. In the physical reading `0 < μ < 1`; the result holds for every real `μ`.
