# Gravitational acceleration toward a point mass

For a real `m` and a real triple `d`, the position of the attracted point relative to the point
mass `m` (pointing from the mass to the point), the Newtonian acceleration is `-(m / |d|³) d`,
with gravitational constant one and the Euclidean length `|d| = √(d₁² + d₂² + d₃²)`. For `m > 0`
and `d ≠ 0` it points from the attracted point toward the mass, with magnitude `m / |d|²`. At
`d = 0` the value is the zero vector, a multiple of `d`, where the physical attraction is
undefined. In the physical reading `m > 0` and `d ≠ 0`; the definition takes any real `m` and
any `d`.
