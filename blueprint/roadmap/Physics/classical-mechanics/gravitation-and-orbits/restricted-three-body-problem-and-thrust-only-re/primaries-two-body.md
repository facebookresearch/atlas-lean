# The co-rotating primaries solve their two-body equations

For every real `μ` and every time `t`, the inertial positions `P₁(t) = R(t) (-μ, 0, 0)` of the
first primary, of mass `1 - μ`, and `P₂(t) = R(t) (1 - μ, 0, 0)` of the second, of mass `μ`, with
`R(t)` the [orientation of the rotating frame](rotating-frame.md), satisfy

`P₁'' = -μ (P₁ - P₂) / |P₁ - P₂|³` and `P₂'' = -(1 - μ) (P₂ - P₁) / |P₂ - P₁|³`

at `t`, with the Euclidean length `|·|`: each primary has the
[Newtonian acceleration](point-mass-pull.md) toward the other's mass, with gravitational constant
one. The primaries stay a unit distance apart, so unit angular speed is consistent with unit
separation and unit total mass. In the physical reading `0 < μ < 1`.
