# Distance to the second primary

For reals `μ`, `x` and `y`, `r₂ = √((x - (1 - μ))² + y²)` is the distance from the point
`(x, y)` of the orbital plane, in rotating-frame coordinates, to the second primary, of mass `μ`,
at `(1 - μ, 0)`. It is zero exactly at that primary. In the physical reading `0 < μ < 1`; the
definition takes any real `μ`.
