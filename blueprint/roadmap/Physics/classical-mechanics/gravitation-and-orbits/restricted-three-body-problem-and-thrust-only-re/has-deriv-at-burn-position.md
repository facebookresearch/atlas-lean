# For t < 2 the derivative of the burn position is the burn velocity

For every time `t < 2`, before the depletion time of the [burn mass](burn-mass.md) `2 - t`, the
[burn position](burn-position.md) `x(t) = t + (2 - t) log ((2 - t) / 2)` has derivative
`x'(t) = v(t) = log (2 / (2 - t))`, the [burn velocity](burn-velocity.md). The burn itself lasts
for `0 ≤ t ≤ 1`.
