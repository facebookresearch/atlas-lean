# The burn displacement is the integral of the velocity

The displacement over the burn interval `[0, 1]` is the integral of the velocity:
`x(1) - x(0) = ∫₀¹ v(t) dt`, with `x` the [burn position](burn-position.md) and `v` the
[burn velocity](burn-velocity.md), which is continuous on `[0, 1]`. Both sides equal
`1 - log 2`.
