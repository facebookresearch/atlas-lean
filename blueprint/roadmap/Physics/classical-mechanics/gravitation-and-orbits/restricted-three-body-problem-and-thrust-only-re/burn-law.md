# For 0 ≤ t ≤ 1 the burn obeys the variable-mass law with thrust 1

For every `t` with `0 ≤ t ≤ 1`, the [burn mass](burn-mass.md) `m(t) = 2 - t` satisfies
`m(t) · (1 / (2 - t)) = (-1) · (-1)` and `m(t) · (1 / (2 - t)) = 1`. Since `1 / (2 - t)` is the
[derivative](has-deriv-at-burn-velocity.md) `v'(t)` of the [burn velocity](burn-velocity.md) and the
mass has derivative `m' = -1` ([mass flow](has-deriv-at-burn-mass.md)), this is the reduced
variable-mass law `m v' = -ve m'` with exhaust speed `ve = 1`, and its common value, the thrust
`ve (-m')`, is `1`.
