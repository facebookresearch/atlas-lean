# Position of the target coasting at speed log 2

For a real time `t`, the position of the target, measured along the vehicle's line of motion, is
`x_T(t) = 1 - 2 log 2 + t log 2`: it coasts at the constant speed `log 2`, starts at
`x_T(0) = 1 - 2 log 2 < 0` and is at `1 - log 2` at `t = 1`. Its data are fixed explicitly,
independently of the [burn](burn-position.md).
