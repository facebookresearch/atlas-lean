# Partial derivative of the effective potential in x off the primaries

For reals `μ`, `x` and `y` such that `(x, y)` is off both primaries, `r₁ > 0` and `r₂ > 0`, the
[effective potential](effective-potential.md) `Ω`, as a function of its first coordinate with
`y` fixed, has at `x` the derivative

`Ω_x = x - (1 - μ) (x + μ) / r₁³ - μ (x - (1 - μ)) / r₂³`,

where `r₁ = √((x + μ)² + y²)` and `r₂ = √((x - (1 - μ))² + y²)` are the distances to the
[first](primary-dist-one.md) and [second](primary-dist-two.md) primaries. In the physical reading
`0 < μ < 1`; the formula holds for every real `μ`.
