# Distance to the first primary

For reals `μ`, `x` and `y`, `r₁ = √((x + μ)² + y²)` is the distance from the point
`(x, y)` of the orbital plane, in rotating-frame coordinates, to the first primary, of mass `1 - μ`,
at `(-μ, 0)`. It is zero exactly at that primary. In the physical reading `0 < μ < 1`; the
definition takes any real `μ`.
