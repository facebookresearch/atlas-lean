# At t = 1 vehicle and target have equal positions and velocities

At `t = 1` the vehicle meets the target: the [burn position](burn-position.md) equals the
[target position](target-position.md), `x(1) = x_T(1)` (both are `1 - log 2`), the
[burn velocity](burn-velocity.md) is `v(1) = log 2`, and both positions have derivative `log 2`
at `t = 1`, the target's constant speed. Moreover the [burn mass](burn-mass.md) `m(t) = 2 - t` is
positive for every `t` with `0 ≤ t ≤ 1`. The model has one line of motion and no gravity, drag or
other external force.
