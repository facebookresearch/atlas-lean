# The spatial position Q (x, y, 0) is at distance √(x² + y²)

For every rotation matrix `Q ∈ SO(3)`, real functions `x, y` of time and time `t`, the
[spatial position](spatial-state.md) `X = Q (x(t), y(t), 0)` of the [planar point](planar-path.md)
`x(t) + y(t) i` is at [distance](radius.md) `√(X · X) = √(x(t)² + y(t)²)` from the center.
