# For μ > 0, a circular state has a = r and squared angular momentum μ r

For `μ > 0` and a circular state, that is a state `(x, v)` with `r = √(x · x) > 0`, circular speed
`|v|² = v · v = μ / r` and velocity transverse to the position, `x · v = 0`: the
[specific energy](specific-energy.md) is `E = −μ / (2 r)`, the
[energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) is `a = r`, and the specific angular momentum
`h = x × v` (cross product) satisfies `h · h = μ r`.
