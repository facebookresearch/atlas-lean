# Equal planar angular momenta at two times give equal X × V

For every rotation matrix `Q ∈ SO(3)`, real functions `x, y, vx, vy` of time and times `t₀`, `t₁`:
if the planar signed angular momentum `x vy − y vx` takes the same value at `t₀` and at `t₁`, then
the specific angular momenta `X × V` of the [spatial states](spatial-state.md) `X = Q (x, y, 0)` and
`V = Q (vx, vy, 0)` at `t₀` and at `t₁` are equal vectors. No equation of motion is assumed.
