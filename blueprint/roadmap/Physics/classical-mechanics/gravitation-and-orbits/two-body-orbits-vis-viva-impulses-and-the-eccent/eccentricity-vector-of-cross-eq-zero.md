# A radial state off the center has e = −x / r and eccentricity 1

For every real `μ` and every state `(x, v)` with `r = √(x · x) > 0` and zero specific angular
momentum `h = x × v = 0` (a radial state), the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) is
`e = −x / r` and the [eccentricity](eccentricity.md) is `|e| = 1`, whatever the energy. So the
hypothesis `h ≠ 0` cannot be dropped from the [bound](eccentricity-lt-one-and-semi-major-axis.md)
`|e| < 1` at negative energy. In the physical reading `μ > 0`; the result holds for every real `μ`.
