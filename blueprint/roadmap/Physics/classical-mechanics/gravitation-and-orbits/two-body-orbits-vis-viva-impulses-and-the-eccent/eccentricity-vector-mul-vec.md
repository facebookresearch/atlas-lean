# A rotation of the state rotates the eccentricity vector

For a rotation matrix `Q ∈ SO(3)` (`Qᵀ Q = I` and `det Q = 1`), every real `μ` and every state
`(x, v)` with `x, v ∈ ℝ³`, the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) of the rotated state is
the rotated eccentricity vector, `e(Q x, Q v) = Q e(x, v)`, and the [eccentricity](eccentricity.md)
`|e|` of the rotated state equals that of `(x, v)`. No angle chart is involved. In the physical
reading `μ > 0`; the result holds for every real `μ`, including the conventional values at `μ = 0`
and `x = 0`.
