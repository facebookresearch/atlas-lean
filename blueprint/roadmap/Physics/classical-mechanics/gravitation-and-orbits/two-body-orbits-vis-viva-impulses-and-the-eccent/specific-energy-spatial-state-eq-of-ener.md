# Equal planar energies at two times give equal specific energies

For every rotation matrix `Q ∈ SO(3)`, every real `μ`, real functions `x, y, vx, vy` of time and
times `t₀`, `t₁`: if the planar energy `vx² + vy² − 2 μ / √(x² + y²)` takes the same value at `t₀`
and at `t₁`, then the [specific energies](specific-energy.md) of the spatial states
`(Q (x, y, 0), Q (vx, vy, 0))` at `t₀` and at `t₁` are equal. No equation of motion is assumed. At a
time with `x = y = 0` both energies use the convention `c / 0 = 0` (the planar energy is then
`vx² + vy²`). In the physical reading `μ > 0`; the result holds for every real `μ`.
