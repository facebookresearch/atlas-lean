# For nonzero v, the squared speed after the impulse is (speed + Δv)²

For a nonzero velocity `v ∈ ℝ³` and every real `Δv`, the velocity `v' = v + (Δv / |v|) v` after the
[tangential impulse](tangential-impulse.md) has squared speed `|v'|² = (|v| + Δv)²`, where
`|v| = √(v · v)` is the speed; so the new speed is `|v| + Δv` when `Δv ≥ −|v|`.
