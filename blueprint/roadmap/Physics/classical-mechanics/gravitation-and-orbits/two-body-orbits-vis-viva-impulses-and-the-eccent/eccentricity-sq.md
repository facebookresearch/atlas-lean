# For μ > 0 and r > 0, the eccentricity satisfies e² = 1 + 2 E h² / μ²

For `μ > 0` and a state `(x, v)` with `r = √(x · x) > 0`, the [eccentricity](eccentricity.md) `|e|`
satisfies `|e|² = 1 + 2 E (h · h) / μ²`, where `E` is the [specific energy](specific-energy.md) and
`h = x × v` is the specific angular momentum. This is an identity at one state, not a conservation
law.
