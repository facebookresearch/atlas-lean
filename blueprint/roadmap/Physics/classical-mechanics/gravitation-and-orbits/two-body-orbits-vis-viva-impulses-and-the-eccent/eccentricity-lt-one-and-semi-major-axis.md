# For μ > 0, E < 0 and h ≠ 0, the eccentricity is below 1 and a > 0

For `μ > 0` and a state `(x, v)` with negative [specific energy](specific-energy.md) `E` and nonzero
specific angular momentum `h = x × v` (which forces `x ≠ 0`), the [eccentricity](eccentricity.md)
satisfies `0 ≤ |e| < 1` and the [energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md)
`a = −μ / (2 E)` is positive. These are inequalities at one state; no ellipse or trajectory is
constructed. A [radial state](eccentricity-vector-of-cross-eq-zero.md) off the center, with `h = 0`,
has `|e| = 1`.
