# Tangential impulse at circular speed: a = r iff v is kept or reversed

For `μ > 0`, a state `(x, v)` with `r = √(x · x) > 0` at circular speed `|v|² = v · v = μ / r`, and
a real `Δv` such that the state `(x, v')` after the [tangential impulse](tangential-impulse.md)
`v' = v + (Δv / |v|) v` has negative [specific energy](specific-energy.md), the new
[energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) equals `r` if and only if `Δv = 0` (the
velocity is kept) or `Δv = −2 |v|` (the velocity is reversed, `v' = −v`). Only the speed enters: the
statement holds for a [circular state](semi-major-axis-of-circular.md) and for a radial state at
circular speed alike.
