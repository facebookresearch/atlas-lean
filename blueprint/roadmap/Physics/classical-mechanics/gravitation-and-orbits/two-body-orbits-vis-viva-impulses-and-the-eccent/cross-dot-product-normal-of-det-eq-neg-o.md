# An orthogonal map of determinant −1 reverses the normal component

For an orthogonal matrix `P` (`Pᵀ P = I`) with `det P = −1`, such as a reflection, and all reals
`x, y, vx, vy`, the component of `P (x, y, 0) × P (vx, vy, 0)` along the mapped normal `P e₃`,
`e₃ = (0, 0, 1)`, is `−(x vy − y vx)`, the opposite of the planar signed angular momentum. A
rotation keeps it instead ([rotation case](cross-spatial-state-dot-product-normal.md)).
