# Planar point of two real coordinate functions

For real functions `x, y : ℝ → ℝ` of time and a time `t`, the planar point is the complex number
`x(t) + y(t) i`: its real part is the coordinate along the first in-plane axis and its imaginary
part the coordinate along the second. It is the planar input of the
[spatial state](spatial-state.md).
