# Equal-speed chord: the squared velocity change is 2 s² (1 − cos i)

For reals `s` and `i` and vectors `u, w ∈ ℝ³` with `u · u = w · w = s²` and `u · w = s² cos i`, the
squared length of the difference is `(w − u) · (w − u) = 2 s² (1 − cos i)`. In the physical reading
`s ≥ 0` is the common speed and `i` is the angle between `u` and `w`; the identity holds for all
real `s` and `i`.
