# For positive unit factors, circular speed scales by l / s, period by s

For `μ > 0`, `R > 0`, `l > 0` and `s > 0`, with the gravitational parameter `μ' = (l³ / s²) μ` and
the radius `R' = l R` in the new units of the [change of units](unit-change.md), the circular speed
scales by `l / s` and the period by `s`: `√(μ' / R') = (l / s) √(μ / R)` and
`2 π R' / √(μ' / R') = s · 2 π R / √(μ / R)`.
