# For a rotation, X × V is x vy − y vx times the normal Q e₃

For every rotation matrix `Q ∈ SO(3)`, real functions `x, y, vx, vy` of time and time `t`, the
specific angular momentum `X × V` of the spatial position `X = Q (x(t), y(t), 0)` and velocity
`V = Q (vx(t), vy(t), 0)` ([spatial states](spatial-state.md)) is the planar signed angular momentum
times the oriented normal of the orbit plane: `X × V = (x(t) vy(t) − y(t) vx(t)) Q e₃`, with
`e₃ = (0, 0, 1)`. The planar signed angular momentum is positive when the planar motion turns from
the first in-plane axis toward the second.
