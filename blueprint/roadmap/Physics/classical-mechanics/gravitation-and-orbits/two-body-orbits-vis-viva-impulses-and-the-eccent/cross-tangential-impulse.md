# A tangential impulse multiplies x × v by one plus Δv over the speed

For all `x, v ∈ ℝ³` and every real `Δv`, the specific angular momentum at the position `x` after the
[tangential impulse](tangential-impulse.md) `v' = v + (Δv / |v|) v`, with `|v| = √(v · v)`, is
`x × v' = (1 + Δv / |v|) (x × v)` (cross product). At `v = 0` the convention `c / 0 = 0` makes the
factor `1`, and both sides are `0`. The identity alone gives no orbital plane when `x × v = 0` (a
radial state, or `v = 0`) or when the factor vanishes (`Δv = −|v|` brings the body to rest); when
both are nonzero, the new specific angular momentum is a nonzero multiple of the old one.
