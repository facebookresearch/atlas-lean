# Velocity after a tangential impulse

For a velocity `v ∈ ℝ³` and a real `Δv`, the velocity after a tangential impulse of signed magnitude
`Δv` along the flight direction is `v' = v + (Δv / |v|) v = (1 + Δv / |v|) v`, with the speed
`|v| = √(v · v)`; the position is unchanged and is not an argument. For `v ≠ 0` and `Δv < −|v|` the
factor `1 + Δv / |v|` is negative and the velocity is reversed. The intended domain is `v ≠ 0`; at
`v = 0`, which has no flight direction, the convention `c / 0 = 0` gives `v' = v = 0`.
