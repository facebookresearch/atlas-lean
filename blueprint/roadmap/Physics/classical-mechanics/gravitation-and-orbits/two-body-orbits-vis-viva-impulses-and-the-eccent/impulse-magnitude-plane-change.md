# Equal-speed plane change of transverse velocities costs 2 v sin(i / 2)

Let `x ∈ ℝ³` be a node off the attracting center, `√(x · x) > 0`, and let `v₁, v₂ ∈ ℝ³` be two
velocities transverse to it, `x · v₁ = x · v₂ = 0`, with the same speed `v > 0`,
`v₁ · v₁ = v₂ · v₂ = v²`, whose specific angular momenta `h₁ = x × v₁` and `h₂ = x × v₂` make the
angle `i` with `0 ≤ i ≤ π`: `h₁ · h₂ = cos i · √(h₁ · h₁) √(h₂ · h₂)`. Then the
[impulse magnitude](impulse-magnitude.md) of the single impulse from `v₁` to `v₂` is
`|v₂ − v₁| = 2 v sin(i / 2)`. This is the magnitude of this one specified impulse, not the cost of
an optimized transfer.
