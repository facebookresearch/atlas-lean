# For μ > 0, circular speed gives E = −μ / (2 r) and a = r

For `μ > 0` and a state `(x, v)` with `r = √(x · x) > 0` whose squared speed is the circular value
`|v|² = v · v = μ / r`, the [specific energy](specific-energy.md) is `E = −μ / (2 r)` and the
[energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) is `a = r`. Only the speed enters, not the
direction of `v`: a radial velocity at this speed gives the same `E` and `a`. A
[circular state](semi-major-axis-of-circular.md) has, in addition, its velocity transverse to `x`.
