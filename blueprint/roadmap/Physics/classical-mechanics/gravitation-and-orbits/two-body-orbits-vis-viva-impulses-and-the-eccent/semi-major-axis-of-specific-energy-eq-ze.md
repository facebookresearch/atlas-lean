# At zero energy the semi-major axis formula gives 0, not a length

For every real `μ` and every state `(x, v)` with [specific energy](specific-energy.md) `E = 0` (for
example a parabolic state, with `μ > 0` and `x ≠ 0`), the
[energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) `a = −μ / (2 E)` is `0` by the convention
`c / 0 = 0`, so `a > 0` fails. Hence the hypothesis `E < 0` of the
[positivity result](semi-major-axis-pos.md) cannot be weakened to `E ≤ 0`.
