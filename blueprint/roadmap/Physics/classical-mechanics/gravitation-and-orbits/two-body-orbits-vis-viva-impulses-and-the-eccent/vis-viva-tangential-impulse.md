# Vis-viva after a tangential impulse, for v ≠ 0 and negative new energy

For `μ > 0`, a position `x` with `r = √(x · x) > 0`, a nonzero velocity `v` and a real `Δv`, let
`v' = v + (Δv / |v|) v` be the velocity after the [tangential impulse](tangential-impulse.md), with
`|v| = √(v · v)`. If the [specific energy](specific-energy.md) `E'` of the new state `(x, v')` is
negative, then its [energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) `a' = −μ / (2 E')` satisfies
`(|v| + Δv)² = μ (2 / r − 1 / a')`, that is, `1 / a' = 2 / r − (|v| + Δv)² / μ`.
