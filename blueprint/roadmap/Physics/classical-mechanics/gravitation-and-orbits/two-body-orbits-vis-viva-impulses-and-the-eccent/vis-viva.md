# Vis-viva equation for a state of negative energy

For `μ > 0` and a state `(x, v)` off the attracting center, `r = √(x · x) > 0`, with negative
[specific energy](specific-energy.md) `E = |v|² / 2 − μ / r`, the squared speed `|v|² = v · v`
satisfies the vis-viva equation `|v|² = μ (2 / r − 1 / a)`, where `a = −μ / (2 E) > 0` is the
[energy-defined semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md).
