# Eccentricity, the length of the eccentricity vector

For a real `μ` and a state `(x, v)`, the eccentricity is the Euclidean length `|e| = √(e · e)` of
the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) `e = (v × h) / μ − x / r`, with `h = x × v` and
`r = √(x · x)`. Its intended domain is `μ > 0` and `r > 0`; by the conventions of the eccentricity
vector it is `1` when `μ = 0` and `r > 0`, and `0` at the collision point `x = 0`.
