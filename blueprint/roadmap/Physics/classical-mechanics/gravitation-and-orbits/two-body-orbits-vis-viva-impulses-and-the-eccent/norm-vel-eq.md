# On a circular orbit the speed is √(μ / R) at every time

For planar position and velocity functions `r, v : ℝ → ℂ` satisfying the
[circular-orbit premises](is-circular-orbit.md) with gravitational parameter `μ` and radius `R`
(`μ > 0`, `R > 0`, and for every real time `t`: `|r(t)| = R`, `r'(t) = v(t)` and
`v'(t) = −(μ / |r(t)|³) r(t)`), the speed at every real time `t` is `|v(t)| = √(μ / R)`, the
[circular speed](../kepler-s-third-law-for-circular-orbits/kepler-circular-speed.md) `√(G M / r)`
with `G M = μ` and `r = R`.
