# Bound on the J2 acceleration for ‖r‖ ≥ r_min, for μ > 0

Let $\mu > 0$ and $r_{min} > 0$, and let $R$ and $J_2$ be real. For every $r$ in three-dimensional
Euclidean space with $\|r\| \ge r_{min}$, the [J2 perturbing acceleration](j2-accel.md) satisfies

$$\|a(r)\| \le \frac{3\,\lvert J_2\rvert\,\mu R^2}{r_{min}^4}.$$

It follows from the [squared magnitude](j2-accel-norm-sq.md) and the
[bound on its angular factor](j2-poly-le.md).
