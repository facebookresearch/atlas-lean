# For μ ≥ 0, Lipschitz bound 4μ/r_min³ outside a ball

Let $E$ be a real inner product space, $\mu \ge 0$ and $r_{min} > 0$. For all $r, s \in E$ with
$\|r\| \ge r_{min}$ and $\|s\| \ge r_{min}$, the [inverse-square field](inverse-square-field.md)
$F(r) = (\mu/\|r\|^3)\,r$ satisfies

$$\|F(r) - F(s)\| \le \frac{4\mu}{r_{min}^3}\,\|r - s\|.$$

The constant is not claimed to be sharp. No convexity of the set $\|r\| \ge r_{min}$ is used: the
bound comes from the triangle and reverse triangle inequalities.

Equivalently, $F$ is Lipschitz on the set $\{r \in E : \|r\| \ge r_{min}\}$ with constant
$4\mu/r_{min}^3$.
