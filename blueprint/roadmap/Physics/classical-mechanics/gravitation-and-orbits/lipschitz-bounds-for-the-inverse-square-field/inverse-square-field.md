# Inverse-square field (μ/‖r‖³) r, outward for μ > 0

For a real inner product space $E$, a real number $\mu$ and $r \in E$, the inverse-square field is

$$F(r) = \frac{\mu}{\|r\|^3}\,r.$$

It is meant for $r \ne 0$; at $r = 0$ its value is $0$ (with the convention $x/0 = 0$ the scalar
factor is $0$). For $\mu > 0$ it points along $r$, away from the origin; the gravitational
acceleration toward an attracting body at the origin, with gravitational parameter $\mu$, is its
negative $-(\mu/\|r\|^3)\,r$.
