# The angular factor 5u² − 2u + 1 is at most 4 for 0 ≤ u ≤ 1

For every real $u$ with $0 \le u \le 1$, $5u^2 - 2u + 1 \le 4$. The value is $4$ at $u = 1$ (on the
axis) and $1$ at $u = 0$ (in the equatorial plane). With $u = z^2/\|r\|^2$ this is the angular
factor in the [squared magnitude of the J2 acceleration](j2-accel-norm-sq.md).
