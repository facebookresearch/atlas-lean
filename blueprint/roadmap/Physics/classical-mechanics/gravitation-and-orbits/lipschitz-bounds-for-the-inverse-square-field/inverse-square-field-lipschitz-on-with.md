# For μ ≥ 0 the field is Lipschitz on the set ‖r‖ ≥ r_min

Let $E$ be a real inner product space, $\mu \ge 0$ and $r_{min} > 0$. The
[inverse-square field](inverse-square-field.md) $F(r) = (\mu/\|r\|^3)\,r$ is Lipschitz on the set
$\{r \in E : \|r\| \ge r_{min}\}$ with constant $4\mu/r_{min}^3$:
$\|F(r) - F(s)\| \le (4\mu/r_{min}^3)\,\|r - s\|$ for all $r$ and $s$ in that set.
