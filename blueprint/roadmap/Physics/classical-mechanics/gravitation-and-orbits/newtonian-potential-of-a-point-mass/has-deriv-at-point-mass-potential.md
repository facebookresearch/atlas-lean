# Radial derivative GM/r² of the point-mass potential −GM/r

Let $G$ and $M$ be real numbers and $r > 0$. The function $\Phi(s) = -GM/s$ is differentiable at
$r$, with derivative $\Phi'(r) = GM/r^2$. The identity holds for all real $G$ and $M$; in the
physical reading $G > 0$ is the gravitational constant, $M > 0$ the mass at the origin, $r$ the
distance from it and $\Phi$ the gravitational potential per unit mass.
