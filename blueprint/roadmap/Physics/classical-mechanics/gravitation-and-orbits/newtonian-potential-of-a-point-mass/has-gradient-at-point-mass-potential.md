# Gradient (GM/‖x‖³) x of the point-mass potential off the origin

Let $E$ be a complete real inner product space, $G$ and $M$ real numbers and $x \in E$ with
$x \ne 0$. The function $\Phi(y) = -GM/\|y\|$ has gradient

$$\nabla\Phi(x) = \frac{GM}{\|x\|^3}\,x$$

at $x$: its derivative at $x$ is $v \mapsto \langle \nabla\Phi(x), v\rangle$. The formula holds for
all real $G$ and $M$, including zero and negative values; in the physical reading $G > 0$ and
$M > 0$, and the acceleration $-\nabla\Phi$ [points inward](point-mass-gradient-inward.md).
