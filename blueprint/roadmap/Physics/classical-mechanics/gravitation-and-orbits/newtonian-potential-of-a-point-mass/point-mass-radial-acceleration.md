# Radial acceleration −GM/r² of a point mass, inward for G, M > 0

Let $G > 0$, $M > 0$ and $r > 0$, and let $\Phi(s) = -GM/s$. Then $\Phi$ is differentiable at $r$
with $\Phi'(r) = GM/r^2$, the radial acceleration $-\Phi'(r)$ equals $-GM/r^2$, and $-GM/r^2 < 0$:
the acceleration points toward the origin.
