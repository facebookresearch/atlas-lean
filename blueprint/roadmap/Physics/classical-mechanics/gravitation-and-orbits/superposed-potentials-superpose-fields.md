# The gradient of a sum of two potentials is the sum of the gradients

Let $E$ be a complete real inner product space, $x \in E$, and let
$\Phi_1, \Phi_2 \colon E \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable at $x$ with gradients $g_1$ and $g_2$
(their derivatives at $x$ are $v \mapsto \langle g_1, v\rangle$ and
$v \mapsto \langle g_2, v\rangle$). Then $\Phi_1 + \Phi_2$ is differentiable at $x$ with gradient
$g_1 + g_2$, and the field of the sum, the negative gradient, is the sum of the fields:
$-(g_1 + g_2) = (-g_1) + (-g_2)$.
