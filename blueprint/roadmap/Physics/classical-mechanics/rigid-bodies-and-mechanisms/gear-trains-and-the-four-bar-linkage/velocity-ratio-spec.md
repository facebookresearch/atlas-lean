# At regular configurations the velocity ratios solve the rate equations

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with crank `b`, coupler `c` and rocker `d`, and angles
`θ₂, θ₃, θ₄` at which the [dependent-angle Jacobian](dependent-jacobian.md) has nonzero
determinant, the [velocity ratios](velocity-ratio.md) `(r₃, r₄)` satisfy
`-b sin θ₂ - c sin θ₃ r₃ + d sin θ₄ r₄ = 0` and `b cos θ₂ + c cos θ₃ r₃ - d cos θ₄ r₄ = 0`: the
loop-closure equations differentiated along a motion with `ω₂ = 1`.
