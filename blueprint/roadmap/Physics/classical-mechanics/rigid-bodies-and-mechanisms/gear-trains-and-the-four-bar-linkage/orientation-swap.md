# Reversing the orientation of the last shaft negates the train ratio

For a gear train of `n` [meshes](mesh.md), `n ≥ 0`, with [train ratio](train-ratio.md) `R`, and
angular speeds `ω₀, …, ω_n` satisfying [no-slip kinematics](is-no-slip.md) with `ω₀ ≠ 0`, the speed
`-ω_n` of the last shaft measured about the reversed axis direction satisfies `-ω_n = (-R) ω₀` and
`-ω_n ≠ R ω₀`.
