# No-slip kinematics of a gear train

Consider a gear train of `n` [meshes](mesh.md), `n ≥ 0`, on shafts `0, 1, …, n` with a common axis
direction, mesh `k` joining the upstream shaft `k` to the downstream shaft `k + 1`. Angular speeds
`ω₀, …, ω_n`, signed about the common axis direction, are no-slip when `ω_{k+1} = r_k ω_k` for
every mesh `k`, where `r_k` is the [signed ratio](ratio.md) of mesh `k`.
