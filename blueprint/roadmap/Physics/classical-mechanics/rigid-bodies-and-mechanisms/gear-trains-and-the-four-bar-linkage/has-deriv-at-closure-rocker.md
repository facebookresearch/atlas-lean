# Derivative of the loop-closure residuals in the rocker angle

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with rocker `d` and all angles `θ₂, θ₃, θ₄`, the map
`θ ↦` [residual](closure.md) at `(θ₂, θ₃, θ)` has derivative `(d sin θ₄, -d cos θ₄)` at `θ₄`, the
second column of the [dependent-angle Jacobian](dependent-jacobian.md).
