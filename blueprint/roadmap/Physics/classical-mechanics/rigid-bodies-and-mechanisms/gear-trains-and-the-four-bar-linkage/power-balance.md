# Power balance of an ideal gear train

For a gear train of `n` [meshes](mesh.md), `n ≥ 0`, with [lossless meshes](is-lossless.md) for the
port torques `a_k` and `b_k`, and angular speeds `ω₀, …, ω_n` satisfying
[no-slip kinematics](is-no-slip.md), the total power into the train through its shafts vanishes:
`T₀ ω₀ + T₁ ω₁ + ⋯ + T_n ω_n = 0`, where `T_j` is the [shaft load](shaft-load.md) of shaft `j`.
