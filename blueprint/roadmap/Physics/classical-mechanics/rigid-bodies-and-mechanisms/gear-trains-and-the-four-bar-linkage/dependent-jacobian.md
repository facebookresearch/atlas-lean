# Jacobian of the loop closure in the dependent angles

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with coupler `c` and rocker `d`, and angles `θ₃` and `θ₄`,
the Jacobian of the [loop-closure residuals](closure.md) in the dependent angles `(θ₃, θ₄)` is the
2 × 2 matrix with rows `(-c sin θ₃, d sin θ₄)` and `(c cos θ₃, -d cos θ₄)`.
