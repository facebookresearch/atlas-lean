# Derivative of the loop-closure residuals in the coupler angle

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with coupler `c` and all angles `θ₂, θ₃, θ₄`, the map
`θ ↦` [residual](closure.md) at `(θ₂, θ, θ₄)` has derivative `(-c sin θ₃, c cos θ₃)` at `θ₃`, the
first column of the [dependent-angle Jacobian](dependent-jacobian.md).
