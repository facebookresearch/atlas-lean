# Velocity ratios of a four-bar linkage

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with crank `b`, coupler `c` and rocker `d`, and angles
`θ₂, θ₃, θ₄`, the velocity ratios are the pair
`(b sin(θ₄ - θ₂) / (c sin(θ₃ - θ₄)), b sin(θ₃ - θ₂) / (d sin(θ₃ - θ₄)))`, the intended values of
`(ω₃ / ω₂, ω₄ / ω₂)`. With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, both are `0` when `sin(θ₃ - θ₄) = 0` (a dead
centre); the intended domain is the regular configurations, `sin(θ₃ - θ₄) ≠ 0`.
