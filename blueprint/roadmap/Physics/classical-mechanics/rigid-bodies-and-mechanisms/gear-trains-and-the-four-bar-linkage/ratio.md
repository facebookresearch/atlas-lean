# Signed speed ratio of a gear mesh

For a [mesh](mesh.md) with `z` teeth upstream, `z'` teeth downstream and
[sense factor](sense.md) `s` (`-1` external, `1` internal), the signed ratio is `s z / z'`: in the
no-slip model, the downstream angular speed over the upstream one. Since `z, z' ≥ 1` and `s = ±1`,
it is nonzero.
