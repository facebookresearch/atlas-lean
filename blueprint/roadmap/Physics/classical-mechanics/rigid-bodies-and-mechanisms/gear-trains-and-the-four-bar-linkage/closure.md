# Loop-closure residuals of a four-bar linkage

For a [four-bar linkage](four-bar.md) with ground `a`, crank `b`, coupler `c` and rocker `d`, and
angles `θ₂` (crank), `θ₃` (coupler) and `θ₄` (rocker), measured counterclockwise from the ground
direction `O₂ → O₄`, the loop-closure residual is the pair
`(b cos θ₂ + c cos θ₃ - d cos θ₄ - a, b sin θ₂ + c sin θ₃ - d sin θ₄)`, the two components of
`b u(θ₂) + c u(θ₃) - d u(θ₄) - a e₁` with `u(θ) = (cos θ, sin θ)`.
