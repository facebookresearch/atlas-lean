# Derivative of the cross product with a constant vector

Let `Ω` and `r'` be vectors of `ℝ³`, `r : ℝ → ℝ³` and `t` real, and suppose that each component
`s ↦ rᵢ(s)` has derivative `r'ᵢ` at `t`. Then `s ↦ Ω × r(s)` has derivative `Ω × r'` at `t`, where
`×` is the cross product of `ℝ³`, `x × y = (x₁ y₂ - x₂ y₁, x₂ y₀ - x₀ y₂, x₀ y₁ - x₁ y₀)` for
components indexed `0, 1, 2`. For a time-dependent `Ω` a further term `Ω'(t) × r(t)` would appear;
it is not part of this statement.
