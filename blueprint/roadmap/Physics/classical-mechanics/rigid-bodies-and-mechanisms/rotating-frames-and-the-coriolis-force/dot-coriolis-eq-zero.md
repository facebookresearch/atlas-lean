# The Coriolis term 2 Ω × v is perpendicular to the velocity

For all vectors `Ω` and `v` of `ℝ³`, the dot product `v · (2 (Ω × v))` is `0`, where `×` is the
cross product of `ℝ³`, `x × y = (x₁ y₂ - x₂ y₁, x₂ y₀ - x₀ y₂, x₀ y₁ - x₁ y₀)` for components
indexed `0, 1, 2`. With `Ω` the angular velocity of a rotating frame and `v` the velocity in that
frame, the Coriolis term is perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work.
