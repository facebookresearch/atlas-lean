# For e ≥ 0, the wall impulse on an incoming body points outward

Let `m > 0`, `e ≥ 0` and `v⁻ < 0` (an incoming body). The [wall impulse](wall-impulse.md)
`J = -(1 + e) m v⁻` is positive: it points out of the wall. At the wall the normal axis points from
the wall into the body's side, so an incoming body has normal velocity `v⁻ < 0`.
