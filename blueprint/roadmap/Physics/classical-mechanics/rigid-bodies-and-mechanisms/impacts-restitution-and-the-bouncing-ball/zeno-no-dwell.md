# For g, u₀ > 0 and 0 < e < 1, the flights have no dwell-time bound

Let `g > 0`, `u₀ > 0` and `0 < e < 1`. There is no `δ > 0` with `δ ≤ T_k` for every `k`, where
`T_k = (2 u₀ / g) eᵏ` is the [flight duration](flight-duration.md) of arc `k`. A positive lower
bound on the durations would allow only finitely many events before any finite time, so the
accumulating bounce sequence cannot have one.
