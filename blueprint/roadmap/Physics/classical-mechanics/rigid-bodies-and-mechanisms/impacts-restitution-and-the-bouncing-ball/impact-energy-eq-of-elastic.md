# An elastic two-body impact conserves the kinetic energy

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`. For all real `v₁` and `v₂`, the impact with coefficient of restitution
`e = 1` keeps the [kinetic energy](pair-kinetic-energy.md) `K(x₁, x₂) = m₁ x₁² / 2 + m₂ x₂² / 2` of
the pair: `K(u₁, u₂) = K(v₁, v₂)`, where `u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)`.
