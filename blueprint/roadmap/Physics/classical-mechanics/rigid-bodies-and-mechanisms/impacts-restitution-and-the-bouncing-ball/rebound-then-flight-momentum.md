# Momentum after a wall impact followed by a constant force

Let `m > 0`. For all real `e`, `v⁻`, `F` and `t`, let the body follow the
[constant-force trajectory](../../particles-and-oscillations/kinetic-energy-and-power-of-a-force/const-force-velocity.md)
started at the [rebound velocity](wall-rebound-velocity.md) `v⁺ = -e v⁻` under the force `F`, with
velocity `v⁺ + (t / m) F` at time `t` after the event. Then `m (v⁺ + (t / m) F) = m v⁻ + J + t F`:
the incoming momentum plus the [wall impulse](wall-impulse.md) `J = -(1 + e) m v⁻` plus the impulse
`t F` of the force. The identity holds for every real `t`; the physical reading is `t ≥ 0`.
