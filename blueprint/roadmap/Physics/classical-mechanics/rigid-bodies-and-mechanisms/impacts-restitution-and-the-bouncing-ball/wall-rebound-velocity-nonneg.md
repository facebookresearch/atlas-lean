# For e ≥ 0, an incoming body leaves the wall or rests on it

Let `e ≥ 0` and `v⁻ < 0` (an incoming body). The [rebound velocity](wall-rebound-velocity.md)
`v⁺ = -e v⁻` satisfies `v⁺ ≥ 0`, and `v⁺ > 0` if and only if `e > 0`. At the wall the normal axis
points from the wall into the body's side, so an incoming body has normal velocity `v⁻ < 0`.
