# Flight durations of the bouncing ball

For real `g`, `e`, `u₀` and every arc `n`, the flight duration is `T_n = 2 u_n / g`, where `u_n` is
the [take-off speed](bounce-speed.md) of arc `n`. The ball leaves the floor with upward take-off
speed `u₀` and flies under gravity `g`; each landing is a fixed-wall event with coefficient of
restitution `e`. In the model `g > 0`, `u₀ > 0` and `e ≥ 0`; with the convention `x / 0 = 0` every
duration is `0` when `g = 0`.
