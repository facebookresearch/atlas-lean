# Momentum after an impact followed by constant forces

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`. For all real `e`, `v₁`, `v₂`, `F₁`, `F₂` and `t`, with the post-impact
velocities `u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` ([first](impact-velocity.md) and
[second](impact-velocity-2.md) body), let each body follow the
[constant-force trajectory](../../particles-and-oscillations/kinetic-energy-and-power-of-a-force/const-force-velocity.md)
started at its post-impact velocity under the force `Fᵢ`, with velocity `uᵢ + (t / mᵢ) Fᵢ` at time
`t` after the event. Then the total momentum at time `t` is

`m₁ (u₁ + (t / m₁) F₁) + m₂ (u₂ + (t / m₂) F₂) = m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + t (F₁ + F₂)`,

the pre-impact total plus the external impulse `t (F₁ + F₂)`. The identity holds for every real `t`;
the physical reading is `t ≥ 0`.
