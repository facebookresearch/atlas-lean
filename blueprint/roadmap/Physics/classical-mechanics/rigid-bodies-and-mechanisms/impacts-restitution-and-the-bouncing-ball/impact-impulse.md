# Equal and opposite impulses in a two-body impact

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`. For all real `e`, `v₁` and `v₂`, with the post-impact velocities
`u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` ([first](impact-velocity.md) and
[second](impact-velocity-2.md) body), the momentum jumps are

`m₁ u₁ - m₁ v₁ = -(1 + e) μ (v₁ - v₂)` and `m₂ u₂ - m₂ v₂ = (1 + e) μ (v₁ - v₂)`,

where `μ = m₁ m₂ / (m₁ + m₂)` is the reduced mass: body 1 receives the impulse
`J = -(1 + e) μ (v₁ - v₂)` and body 2 receives `-J`.
