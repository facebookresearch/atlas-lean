# Take-off speeds of the bouncing ball

For real `e` and `u₀`, the take-off speed of arc `n` is defined by `u_0 = u₀` and
`u_(n+1) = -e (-u_n) = e u_n`: the [rebound velocity](wall-rebound-velocity.md) of the fixed-wall
event applied to the landing velocity `-u_n` of the previous arc
([closed form](bounce-speed-eq.md)). The ball leaves the floor with upward take-off speed `u₀` and
flies under gravity `g`; each landing is a fixed-wall event with coefficient of restitution `e`. In
the model `u₀ > 0` and `e ≥ 0`.
