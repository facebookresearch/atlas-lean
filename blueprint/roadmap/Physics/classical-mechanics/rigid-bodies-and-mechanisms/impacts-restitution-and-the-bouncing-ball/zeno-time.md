# Accumulation time of the bouncing ball

For real `g`, `e` and `u₀`, the accumulation time is `t_∞ = 2 u₀ / (g (1 - e))`. For `g > 0`,
`u₀ > 0` and `0 < e < 1` it is the [sum](has-sum-flight-duration.md) of the
[flight durations](flight-duration.md) and the limit of the [contact times](zeno-accumulation.md). With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when
`g = 0` or `e = 1`.
