# For positive masses, the impact laws determine the outgoing velocities

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`. For all real `e`, `v₁`, `v₂`, `u₁` and `u₂`, momentum conservation
`m₁ u₁ + m₂ u₂ = m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂` and Newton's restitution law `u₂ - u₁ = -e (v₂ - v₁)` hold if and
only if `u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)`. Velocities are components along the line of the
impact; `v₁`, `v₂` are the velocities just before the event and `u₁`, `u₂` those just after it.
