# Impulse of a fixed wall on a body

For real `m`, `e` and `v⁻`, the impulse of the wall on a body of mass `m` is `J = m (v⁺ - v⁻)`,
where [rebound velocity](wall-rebound-velocity.md) `v⁺ = -e v⁻` is the normal velocity just after
the impact ([formula](wall-impulse-eq.md)). At the wall the normal axis points from the wall into
the body's side, so an incoming body has normal velocity `v⁻ < 0`. In the model `m > 0` and `e ≥ 0`;
the definition takes any real values.
