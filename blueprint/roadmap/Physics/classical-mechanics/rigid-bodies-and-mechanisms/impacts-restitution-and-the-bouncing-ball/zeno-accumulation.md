# For g, u₀ > 0 and 0 < e < 1, the contact times accumulate

Let `g > 0`, `u₀ > 0` and `0 < e < 1`, and let `S_N` be the elapsed time `S_N = T₀ + ⋯ + T_(N-1)`
after `N` arcs, the time of the `N`-th contact with the floor, where `T_k = 2 u_k / g` is the
[flight duration](flight-duration.md) of arc `k`. Then

- `S_N` is strictly increasing in `N`;
- `S_N < t_∞` for every `N`, where `t_∞ = 2 u₀ / (g (1 - e))`;
- `S_N → t_∞` as `N → ∞`;
- the take-off speeds `u_n = eⁿ u₀` tend to `0`.

Infinitely many impacts occur before the finite time `t_∞` (Zeno accumulation).
