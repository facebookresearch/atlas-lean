# With e > 1 and unequal velocities, an impact gains kinetic energy

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`, `e > 1` and `v₁ ≠ v₂`. With the post-impact velocities
`u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` ([first](impact-velocity.md) and
[second](impact-velocity-2.md) body), `K(v₁, v₂) < K(u₁, u₂)`, where `K` is the
[kinetic energy](pair-kinetic-energy.md) `K(x₁, x₂) = m₁ x₁² / 2 + m₂ x₂² / 2` of the pair. A
coefficient of restitution above `1` would inject energy, which puts it outside the model.
