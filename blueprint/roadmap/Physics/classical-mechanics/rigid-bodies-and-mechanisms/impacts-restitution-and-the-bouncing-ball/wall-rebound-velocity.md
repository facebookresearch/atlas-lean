# Rebound velocity at a fixed wall

For real `e` and `v⁻`, the normal velocity just after the impact on a stationary wall is
`v⁺ = -e v⁻`, where `v⁻` is the normal velocity just before it and `e` the coefficient of
restitution. At the wall the normal axis points from the wall into the body's side, so an incoming
body has normal velocity `v⁻ < 0`. In the model `e ≥ 0`; the definition takes any real values.
