# For 0 ≤ e ≤ 1, a two-body impact does not increase the kinetic energy

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0` and `0 ≤ e ≤ 1`. For all real `v₁` and `v₂`, with the post-impact
velocities `u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` ([first](impact-velocity.md) and
[second](impact-velocity-2.md) body), `K(u₁, u₂) ≤ K(v₁, v₂)`, where `K` is the
[kinetic energy](pair-kinetic-energy.md) `K(x₁, x₂) = m₁ x₁² / 2 + m₂ x₂² / 2` of the pair.
