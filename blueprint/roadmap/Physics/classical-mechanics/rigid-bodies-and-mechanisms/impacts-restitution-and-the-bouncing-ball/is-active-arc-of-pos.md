# For e > 0 and u₀ > 0, every arc is active

Let `e > 0` and `u₀ > 0`. For every `n` the [take-off speed](bounce-speed.md) `u_n = eⁿ u₀` is
positive, so every arc is [active](is-active-arc.md): no arc is a rest, and the sequence of bounces
does not terminate. For `g > 0` and `e < 1` these infinitely many bounces all occur before the
finite [accumulation time](zeno-time.md).
