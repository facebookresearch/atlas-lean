# Kinetic energy after an impact followed by free flight

Let `m₁ > 0` and `m₂ > 0`. For all real `e`, `v₁`, `v₂` and `t`, with the post-impact velocities
`u₁ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ - m₂ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` and
`u₂ = (m₁ v₁ + m₂ v₂ + m₁ e (v₁ - v₂)) / (m₁ + m₂)` ([first](impact-velocity.md) and
[second](impact-velocity-2.md) body) and no external force, each body keeps its post-impact velocity
on the
[constant-force trajectory](../../particles-and-oscillations/kinetic-energy-and-power-of-a-force/const-force-velocity.md)
with force `0`, and the [kinetic energy](pair-kinetic-energy.md)
`K(x₁, x₂) = m₁ x₁² / 2 + m₂ x₂² / 2` of the pair at time `t` after the event is `K(v₁, v₂)` minus
the event loss `(1 - e²) m₁ m₂ / (2 (m₁ + m₂)) (v₁ - v₂)²`.
