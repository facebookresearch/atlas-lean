# For e = 1, finitely many impacts occur before any time

Let `g > 0` and `u₀ > 0`, and take `e = 1`. Every [flight duration](flight-duration.md) is
`2 u₀ / g`, the series of the durations diverges, and for every real `H` only finitely many `N` have
`S_N ≤ H`, where `S_N` is the elapsed time `S_N = T₀ + ⋯ + T_(N-1)` after `N` arcs, the time of the
`N`-th contact with the floor.
