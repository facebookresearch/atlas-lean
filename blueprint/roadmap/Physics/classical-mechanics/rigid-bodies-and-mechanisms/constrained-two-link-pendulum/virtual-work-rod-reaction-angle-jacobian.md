# Rod reactions do no work along velocities of the chain

For all real `c₁`, `c₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, angles `q` and angular velocities `w`, the
[virtual work](virtual-work.md) of the [rod reaction](rod-reaction.md) with multipliers `c₁`, `c₂`
at the configuration given by the [angle-to-position map](pos-map.md) at `q`, along the velocity
that the [angle derivative](angle-jacobian.md) at `q` gives to `w`, is `0`.
