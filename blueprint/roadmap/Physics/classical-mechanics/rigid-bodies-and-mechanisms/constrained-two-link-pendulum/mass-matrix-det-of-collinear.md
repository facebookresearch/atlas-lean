# With collinear links, the determinant is m₁ m₂ l₁² l₂²

For real `q₁`, `q₂` with `sin (q₁ - q₂) = 0` (links stretched or folded) and all real `m₁`, `m₂`,
`l₁`, `l₂`, the determinant of the [reduced mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) is `m₁ m₂ l₁² l₂²`. For
nonzero masses and lengths it is nonzero, so the reduced mass matrix of this open chain stays
invertible at collinear configurations.
