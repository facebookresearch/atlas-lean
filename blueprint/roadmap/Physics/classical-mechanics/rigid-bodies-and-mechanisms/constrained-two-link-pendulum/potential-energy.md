# Gravitational potential energy of the two-link pendulum

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g`, `q₁` and `q₂`, the potential energy is
`V = g (m₁ y₁ + m₂ y₂)`, where `y₁ = -l₁ cos q₁` and `y₂ = -l₁ cos q₁ - l₂ cos q₂` are the second
coordinates (heights, axis pointing up) of the [positions](pos.md) of the [two](pos-2.md) masses,
and gravity of magnitude `g` points along the downward vertical. In the model the masses and rod
lengths are positive and `g` is the gravitational acceleration, positive; the definition takes any
real values.
