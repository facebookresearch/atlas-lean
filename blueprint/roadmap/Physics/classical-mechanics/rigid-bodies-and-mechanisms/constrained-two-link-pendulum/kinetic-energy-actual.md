# Kinetic energy of the actual velocities along an angle trajectory

Let `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂` and `t` be real and `q₁, q₂ : ℝ → ℝ` have derivatives `w₁` and `w₂` at
`t`. Let `p₁(s)` and `p₂(s)` be the [positions](pos.md) of the [two](pos-2.md) masses at the angles
`q₁(s)`, `q₂(s)`, and `p₁'(t)`, `p₂'(t)` their derivatives at `t`. Then

`m₁ ‖p₁'(t)‖² / 2 + m₂ ‖p₂'(t)‖² / 2 = wᵀ M(q(t)) w / 2`,

where `w = (w₁, w₂)` and `M(q(t))` is the [reduced mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) at the angles
`q₁(t)`, `q₂(t)`.
