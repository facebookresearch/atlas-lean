# The angle derivative is the derivative of the position map

For all real `l₁`, `l₂` and angles `q`, the [angle-to-position map](pos-map.md) `q ↦ (p₁, p₂)` has
derivative at `q` the [angle derivative](angle-jacobian.md)
`w ↦ (l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁), l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁) + l₂ w₂ (cos q₂, sin q₂))`.
