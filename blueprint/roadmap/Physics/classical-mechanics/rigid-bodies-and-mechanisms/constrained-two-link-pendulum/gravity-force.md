# Gravity forces on the two masses

For real `m₁`, `m₂` and `g`, the gravity forces on the two masses are `(m₁ (0, -g), m₂ (0, -g))`,
pointing along the downward vertical (the second axis points up). They are minus the Cartesian
gradient of the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) `g (m₁ y₁ + m₂ y₂)`. In the model the masses
and rod lengths are positive and `g` is the gravitational acceleration, positive; the definition
takes any real values.
