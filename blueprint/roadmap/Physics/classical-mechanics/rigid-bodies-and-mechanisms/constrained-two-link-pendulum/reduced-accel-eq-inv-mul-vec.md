# For positive masses and lengths, the reduced acceleration is M⁻¹ f

Let `m₁, m₂, l₁, l₂ > 0`, `g` real and `q`, `w` vectors of the Euclidean plane. The
[reduced acceleration](reduced-accel.md) at `(q, w)` equals `M(q)⁻¹ f(q, w)`, where
`M(q) = [[(m₁ + m₂) l₁², m₂ l₁ l₂ cos (q₁ - q₂)], [m₂ l₁ l₂ cos (q₁ - q₂), m₂ l₂²]]` is the
[reduced mass matrix](mass-matrix.md), invertible here, and `f` the
[generalized force](generalized-force.md).
