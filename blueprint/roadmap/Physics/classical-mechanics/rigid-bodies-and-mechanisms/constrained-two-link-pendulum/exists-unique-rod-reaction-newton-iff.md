# For m, l > 0, Newton admits a unique rod reaction iff the reduced ODE

Let `m₁, m₂, l₁, l₂ > 0`, `g` real, and `q` a trajectory of angle vectors that is twice continuously
differentiable at `t`. Let `p₁`, `p₂` be the [Cartesian motion](cartesian-motion.md) of the two
masses. Newton's equations `m₁ p₁''(t) = m₁ (0, -g) + R₁` and `m₂ p₂''(t) = m₂ (0, -g) + R₂` hold
for exactly one pair of multipliers of the [rod reaction](rod-reaction.md) `(R₁, R₂)` at
`(p₁(t), p₂(t))` if and only if `q''(t)` is the [reduced acceleration](reduced-accel.md) at
`(q(t), q'(t))`.
