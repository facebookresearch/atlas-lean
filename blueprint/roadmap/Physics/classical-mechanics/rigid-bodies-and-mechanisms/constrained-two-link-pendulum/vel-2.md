# Velocity of the second mass at given angular velocities

For real `l₁`, `l₂`, `q₁`, `q₂`, `w₁` and `w₂`, the velocity of the second mass is
`l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁) + l₂ w₂ (cos q₂, sin q₂)`, the [velocity of the first mass](vel.md) plus
`l₂ w₂ (cos q₂, sin q₂)`. Along a trajectory with `q₁' = w₁` and `q₂' = w₂` it is the time
derivative of the [position](pos-2.md) of the second mass
([velocities along a trajectory](has-deriv-at-pos.md)).
