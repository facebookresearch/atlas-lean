# Generalized force of the two-link pendulum

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g` and vectors `q = (q₁, q₂)`, `w = (w₁, w₂)` of the Euclidean
plane, the generalized force is `f(q, w) = (f₁, f₂)` with
`f₁ = -m₂ l₁ l₂ sin (q₁ - q₂) w₂² - (m₁ + m₂) g l₁ sin q₁` and
`f₂ = m₂ l₁ l₂ sin (q₁ - q₂) w₁² - m₂ g l₂ sin q₂`: the gravity torques and the terms quadratic in
the angular velocities of the reduced equation `M(q) q̈ = f(q, q̇)`. In the model the masses and rod
lengths are positive and `g` is the gravitational acceleration, positive; the definition takes any
real values.
