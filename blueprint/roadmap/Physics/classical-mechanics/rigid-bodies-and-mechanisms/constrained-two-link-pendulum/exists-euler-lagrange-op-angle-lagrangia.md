# Positive masses and lengths give local Euler–Lagrange solutions

Let `m₁, m₂, l₁, l₂ > 0` and `g` real. For every time `t₀` and vectors `q₀`, `w₀` of the Euclidean
plane, there are `ε > 0` and a trajectory `q` of angle vectors with `q(t₀) = q₀` and `q'(t₀) = w₀`
such that, at every time `t` with `t₀ - ε < t < t₀ + ε`, `q` is twice continuously differentiable at
`t` and the Euler–Lagrange operator `∂L/∂q - d/dt (∂L/∂v)` of the
[two-link Lagrangian](angle-lagrangian.md) along `q` vanishes at `t`. Uniqueness and global
existence are not part of the statement.
