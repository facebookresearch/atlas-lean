# First-order field of the reduced two-link equations

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g` and a state `(q, w)` of angle and angular-velocity vectors of
the Euclidean plane, the reduced field is `(q, w) ↦ (w, a(q, w))`, where `a` is the
[reduced acceleration](reduced-accel.md) (equal to `M(q)⁻¹ f(q, w)` for positive masses and
lengths). In the model the masses and rod lengths are positive and `g` is the gravitational
acceleration, positive; the definition takes any real values.
