# Position of the second mass of the two-link pendulum

For real `l₁`, `l₂`, `q₁` and `q₂`, the position of the second mass is
`p₂ = p₁ + l₂ (sin q₂, -cos q₂)`, where [p₁](pos.md) `= l₁ (sin q₁, -cos q₁)` is the position of the
first mass: the tip of the second rod, of length `l₂` and pivoted at the first mass, at the absolute
angle `q₂` from the downward vertical (not measured from the first rod). In the model the rod
lengths are positive; the definition takes any real values.
