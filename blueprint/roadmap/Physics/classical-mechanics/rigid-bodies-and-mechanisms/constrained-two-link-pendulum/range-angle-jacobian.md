# For positive lengths, the angle derivative maps onto the rod kernel

Let `l₁ > 0` and `l₂ > 0`. For all angles `q`, the range of the
[angle derivative](angle-jacobian.md) at `q` equals the kernel of the
[rod constraint Jacobian](rod-jacobian.md) at the configuration `p` given by the
[angle-to-position map](pos-map.md). With the
[injectivity of the angle derivative](ker-angle-jacobian.md), the angle derivative is a linear
isomorphism from the angular velocities onto that kernel.
