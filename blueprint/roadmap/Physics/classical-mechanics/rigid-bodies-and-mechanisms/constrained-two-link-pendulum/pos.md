# Position of the first mass of the two-link pendulum

For real `l₁` and `q₁`, the position of the first mass in the Euclidean plane is
`p₁ = l₁ (sin q₁, -cos q₁)`: the tip of the first rod, of length `l₁` and pivoted at the origin, at
the absolute angle `q₁` measured counterclockwise from the downward vertical, with the second
coordinate axis pointing up. In the model the rod lengths are positive; the definition takes any
real values.
