# Euler–Lagrange operator of the two-link pendulum

Let `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂` and `g` be real, `q` a trajectory of angle vectors (a function of time
with values in the Euclidean plane) and `t` a time at which `q` is twice continuously
differentiable. Then the Euler–Lagrange operator `∂L/∂q - d/dt (∂L/∂v)` of the
[two-link Lagrangian](angle-lagrangian.md) along `q`, evaluated at `t`, is
`f(q(t), q'(t)) - M(q(t)) q''(t)`, where `f` is the [generalized force](generalized-force.md) and
`M` the [reduced mass matrix](mass-matrix.md). This holds for all real parameters.
