# Velocities of constrained curves lie in the rod kernel

Let `l₁`, `l₂` and `t` be real and `c : ℝ → ℝ² × ℝ²` a curve of configurations with
`g(c(s)) = (0, 0)` for every `s` near `t`, where `g` is the [rod constraint map](rod-constraint.md),
and with derivative `v` at `t`. Then `Dg(c(t)) v = (0, 0)`, where `Dg` is the
[rod constraint Jacobian](rod-jacobian.md). No sign condition on the lengths is needed.
