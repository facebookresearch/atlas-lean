# Map from link angles to the positions of the two masses

For real `l₁`, `l₂` and angles `q = (q₁, q₂)`, the map gives the Cartesian configuration `(p₁, p₂)`
of the two masses: `p₁ = l₁ (sin q₁, -cos q₁)` and `p₂ = p₁ + l₂ (sin q₂, -cos q₂)` (the
[positions](pos.md) of the [two](pos-2.md) masses), a pair of points of the Euclidean plane. In the
model the rod lengths are positive; the definition takes any real values.
