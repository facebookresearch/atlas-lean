# Explicit Lagrangian of the coplanar double pendulum

For all real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g`, `q₁`, `q₂`, `w₁` and `w₂`, the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md)
in the absolute angles is

`L = (m₁ + m₂) l₁² w₁² / 2 + m₂ l₁ l₂ cos (q₁ - q₂) w₁ w₂ + m₂ l₂² w₂² / 2`
`+ (m₁ + m₂) g l₁ cos q₁ + m₂ g l₂ cos q₂`.
