# For nonnegative lengths, the positions satisfy the rod constraints

Let `l₁ ≥ 0` and `l₂ ≥ 0`. For all angles `q₁`, `q₂`, the positions `p₁ = l₁ (sin q₁, -cos q₁)` and
`p₂ = p₁ + l₂ (sin q₂, -cos q₂)` satisfy `‖p₁‖ = l₁` and `‖p₂ - p₁‖ = l₂`. For negative lengths the
two distances are `|l₁|` and `|l₂|`.
