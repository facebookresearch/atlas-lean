# Rod-normal reactions do no virtual work along the rod kernel

For all real `c₁`, `c₂`, every configuration `p` and every pair of plane vectors `v` with
`Dg(p) v = (0, 0)`, where `Dg` is the [rod constraint Jacobian](rod-jacobian.md), the
[virtual work](virtual-work.md) of the [rod reaction](rod-reaction.md)
`(c₁ p₁ - c₂ (p₂ - p₁), c₂ (p₂ - p₁))` along `v` is `0`.
