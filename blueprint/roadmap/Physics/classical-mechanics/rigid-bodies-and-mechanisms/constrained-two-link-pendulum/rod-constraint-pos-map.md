# The angle-to-position map satisfies the rod constraints

For all real `l₁`, `l₂` and angles `q = (q₁, q₂)`, the [rod constraint map](rod-constraint.md)
vanishes at the configuration given by the [angle-to-position map](pos-map.md): `‖p₁‖² = l₁²` and
`‖p₂ - p₁‖² = l₂²`. No sign condition on the lengths is needed, since only their squares enter.
