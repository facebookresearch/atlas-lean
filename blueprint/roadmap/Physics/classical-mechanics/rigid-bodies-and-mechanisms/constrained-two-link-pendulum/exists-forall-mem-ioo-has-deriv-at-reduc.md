# For positive masses and lengths, the reduced field has local solutions

Let `m₁, m₂, l₁, l₂ > 0` and `g` real. For every time `t₀` and state `x₀`, there are `ε > 0` and a
curve `α` of states with `α(t₀) = x₀` and `α'(t) = F(α(t))` for every `t` with
`t₀ - ε < t < t₀ + ε`, where `F` is the [reduced field](reduced-field.md). Uniqueness and global
existence are not part of the statement.
