# Lagrangian of the two-link pendulum in the link angles

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g`, `q₁`, `q₂`, `w₁` and `w₂`, the Lagrangian is `L = T - V`, the
[kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) `T` at angles `q₁`, `q₂` and angular velocities `w₁`, `w₂` minus
the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) `V` at angles `q₁`, `q₂`
([explicit formula](lagrangian-eq.md)). In the model the masses and rod lengths are positive and `g`
is the gravitational acceleration, positive; the definition takes any real values.
