# Two-link Lagrangian on angle and angular-velocity vectors

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `g`, a time `t` and vectors `q = (q₁, q₂)` and `v = (v₁, v₂)` of
the Euclidean plane, the value is the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) `L` of the two-link pendulum at
angles `q₁`, `q₂` and angular velocities `v₁`, `v₂`; it does not depend on `t`. Angles and angular
velocities are taken as vectors of the Euclidean plane so that the Euler–Lagrange operator, built
from gradients, applies. In the model the masses and rod lengths are positive and `g` is the
gravitational acceleration, positive; the definition takes any real values.
