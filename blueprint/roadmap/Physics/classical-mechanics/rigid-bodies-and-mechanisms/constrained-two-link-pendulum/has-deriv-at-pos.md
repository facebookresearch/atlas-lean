# Velocities of the masses along a differentiable angle trajectory

Let `l₁`, `l₂` and `t` be real and `q₁, q₂ : ℝ → ℝ` have derivatives `w₁` and `w₂` at `t`. Then
`s ↦ p₁(s) = l₁ (sin q₁(s), -cos q₁(s))` has derivative `l₁ w₁ (cos q₁(t), sin q₁(t))` at `t`, and
`s ↦ p₂(s) = p₁(s) + l₂ (sin q₂(s), -cos q₂(s))` has derivative
`l₁ w₁ (cos q₁(t), sin q₁(t)) + l₂ w₂ (cos q₂(t), sin q₂(t))` at `t`: the [velocities](vel.md) of
the [two](vel-2.md) masses at the angles `q₁(t)`, `q₂(t)`.
