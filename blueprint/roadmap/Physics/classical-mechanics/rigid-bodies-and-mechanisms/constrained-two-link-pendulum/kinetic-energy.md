# Kinetic energy of the two-link pendulum

For real `m₁`, `m₂`, `l₁`, `l₂`, `q₁`, `q₂`, `w₁` and `w₂`, the kinetic energy is

`T = m₁ ‖v₁‖² / 2 + m₂ ‖v₂‖² / 2`,

where `v₁ = l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁)` and `v₂ = v₁ + l₂ w₂ (cos q₂, sin q₂)` are the
[velocities](vel.md) of the [two](vel-2.md) masses at angles `q₁`, `q₂` and angular velocities `w₁`,
`w₂`. In the model the masses and lengths are positive; the definition takes any real values.
