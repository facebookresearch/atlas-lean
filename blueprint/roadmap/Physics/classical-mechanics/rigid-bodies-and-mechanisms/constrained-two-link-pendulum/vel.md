# Velocity of the first mass at a given angular velocity

For real `l₁`, `q₁` and `w₁`, the velocity of the first mass at angle `q₁` and angular velocity `w₁`
is `l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁)`. Along a trajectory with `q₁' = w₁` it is the time derivative of the
[position](pos.md) `l₁ (sin q₁, -cos q₁)` ([velocities along a trajectory](has-deriv-at-pos.md)).
