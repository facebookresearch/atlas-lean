# For positive lengths, the rod Jacobian has rank two

Let `l₁ > 0` and `l₂ > 0`. For all angles `q`, the [rod constraint Jacobian](rod-jacobian.md)
`Dg(p)(v₁, v₂) = (2 ⟪p₁, v₁⟫, 2 ⟪p₂ - p₁, v₂ - v₁⟫)` at the configuration `p` given by the
[angle-to-position map](pos-map.md) has a two-dimensional range: it maps onto `ℝ²`. This holds at
every configuration of the open chain, collinear links included.
