# Rod constraint map on Cartesian configurations

For real `l₁`, `l₂` and a configuration `p = (p₁, p₂)` of two points of the Euclidean plane, the rod
constraint map is `g(p) = (‖p₁‖² - l₁², ‖p₂ - p₁‖² - l₂²)`. The rods hold, the first joining the
origin to `p₁` with length `|l₁|` and the second joining `p₁` to `p₂` with length `|l₂|`, exactly
where `g(p) = (0, 0)`. In the model the rod lengths are positive; the definition takes any real
values.
