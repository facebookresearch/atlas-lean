# The angle derivative gives the velocities of the masses

For all real `l₁`, `l₂`, angles `q = (q₁, q₂)` and angular velocities `w = (w₁, w₂)`, the
[angle derivative](angle-jacobian.md) at `q` sends `w` to `(v₁, v₂)`, where
`v₁ = l₁ w₁ (cos q₁, sin q₁)` and `v₂ = v₁ + l₂ w₂ (cos q₂, sin q₂)` are the [velocities](vel.md) of
the [two](vel-2.md) masses.
