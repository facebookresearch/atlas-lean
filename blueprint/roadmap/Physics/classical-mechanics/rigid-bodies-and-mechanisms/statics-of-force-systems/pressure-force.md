# Normal force of a uniform pressure on a positive area

For a pressure `p` (a quantity of dimension `M L⁻¹ T⁻²`) and an area `A` (dimension `L²`) with
positive value, the pressure force is the force, of dimension `M L T⁻²`, with value `-p A`: the
normal force of the uniform pressure `p` on a plane face of area `A`. The sign follows the
tension-positive convention: the force is the component along the outward unit normal of the face,
and pressure is the negative of the tension-positive normal stress, so a positive pressure pushes
against the outward normal. The positivity of the area is part of the definition.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
