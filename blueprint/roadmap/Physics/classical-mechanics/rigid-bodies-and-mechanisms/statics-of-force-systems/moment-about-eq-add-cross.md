# Changing the reference point of a moment

For a finite index set, forces `F_i` applied at points `r_i`, free couples `C_i` in `ℝ³` and
reference points `O, O' ∈ ℝ³`, the [moments](moment-about.md) satisfy `M_{O'} = M_O + (O - O') × R`,
where `R = ∑_i F_i` is the resultant. Equivalently, the [wrench about `O'`](wrench-about.md) equals the
wrench about `O` [transported](transport.md) from `O` to `O'`: the resultant is kept and the moment
gains `(O - O') × R`.
