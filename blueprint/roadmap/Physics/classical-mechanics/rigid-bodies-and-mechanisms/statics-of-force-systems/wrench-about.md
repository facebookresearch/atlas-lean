# Wrench of a finite load system about a reference point

For a finite index set, forces `F_i ∈ ℝ³` applied at points `r_i ∈ ℝ³`, free couples `C_i ∈ ℝ³` and
a reference point `O ∈ ℝ³`, the [wrench](wrench.md) about `O` is the pair formed by the
[resultant](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/resultant-force.md)
`R = ∑_i F_i` and the [moment about `O`](moment-about.md) `M_O = ∑_i ((r_i - O) × F_i + C_i)`.
