# Force, stress, moment and line load have four distinct dimensions

The dimensions of force `M L T⁻²`, stress `M L⁻¹ T⁻²`, moment (force times length) `M L² T⁻²` and
line load (force per length) `M T⁻²` are pairwise distinct. Equal dimensions with different physical
roles are not separated: a moment has the dimension of energy, and a line load that of a
translational stiffness.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
