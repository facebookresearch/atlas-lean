# Dimensioned forces balance iff their coordinates do

Let `F_r` (`r` in a finite index set) be forces, quantities of dimension `M L T⁻²`, with components
indexed by a set `ι`. The sum `Σ_r F_r` is the zero force if and only if the family of coordinates
is
[equilibrated](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/is-equilibrated.md):
its resultant is the zero vector. A quantity of dimension `d` is a coordinate value (a real number
or a vector of components in one frame) tagged with `d`; sums of quantities keep the dimension and
products multiply dimensions.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
