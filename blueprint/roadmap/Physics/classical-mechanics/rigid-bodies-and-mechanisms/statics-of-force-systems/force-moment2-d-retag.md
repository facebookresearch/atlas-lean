# Planar moment of retagged lever arms and forces

Let `r_i` and `f_i` (`i` in a finite index set) be plane coordinate vectors. The
[planar moment](force-moment2-d.md) of the lever arms `r_i` tagged with the length dimension `L` and
the forces `f_i` tagged with the force dimension `M L T⁻²` is the
[planar moment about the origin](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/moment2-d.md)
`Σ_i (r_i,0 f_i,1 - r_i,1 f_i,0)` tagged with the moment dimension `M L² T⁻²`.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
