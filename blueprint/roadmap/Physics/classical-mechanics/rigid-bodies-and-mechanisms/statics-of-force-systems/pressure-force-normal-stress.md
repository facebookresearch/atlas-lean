# Feeding a normal stress into the pressure force negates the force

For a force `F` (dimension `M L T⁻²`) and an area `A` with positive value, the
[pressure force](pressure-force.md) of the normal stress `σ = F / A` on the area `A` is `-F`. Since
the conversion records pressure as the negative of the normal stress, the pressure force is not an
inverse of the normal stress unless `F = 0`.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
