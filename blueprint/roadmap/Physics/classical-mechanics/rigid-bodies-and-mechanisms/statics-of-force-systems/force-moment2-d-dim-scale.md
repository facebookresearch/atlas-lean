# Unit conversion multiplies the planar moment by the moment factor

For unit choices `u₁`, `u₂` and a dimension `d`, let `s_d(u₁, u₂) > 0` be the factor that converts a
coordinate of dimension `d` from units `u₁` to units `u₂`. Let `x_i` (`i` in a finite index set) be
lever arms, plane vectors of dimension `L` (positions relative to the moment point, in one frame),
and `F_i` forces, plane vectors of dimension `M L T⁻²` in the same frame. Let `a`, `b` and `c` be
the factors `s_d(u₁, u₂)` for the length dimension `L`, the force dimension `M L T⁻²` and the moment
dimension `M L² T⁻²`. Converting every lever arm by `a` and every force by `b` multiplies their
[planar moment](force-moment2-d.md) by `c`.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
