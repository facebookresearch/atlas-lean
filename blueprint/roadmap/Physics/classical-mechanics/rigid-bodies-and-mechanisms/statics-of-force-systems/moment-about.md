# Moment of a finite load system about a reference point

For a finite index set, forces `F_i ∈ ℝ³` applied at points `r_i ∈ ℝ³`, free couples `C_i ∈ ℝ³` and
a reference point `O ∈ ℝ³`, the moment about `O` is `M_O = ∑_i ((r_i - O) × F_i + C_i)`: the lever
arm runs from `O` to the point of application, and each free couple contributes its moment about
every point. All vectors are components in one inertial frame.
