# The wrench about a new point is the transported wrench

For a finite index set, forces `F_i` applied at points `r_i`, free couples `C_i` in `ℝ³` and
reference points `O, O' ∈ ℝ³`, the [wrench about `O'`](wrench-about.md) equals the wrench about `O`
[transported](transport.md) from `O` to `O'`: the resultant is kept and the moment gains
`(O - O') × R`.
