# Converting a moment coordinate there and back returns it

For unit choices `u₁`, `u₂` and a dimension `d`, let `s_d(u₁, u₂) > 0` be the factor that converts a
coordinate of dimension `d` from units `u₁` to units `u₂`. For all unit choices `u₁`, `u₂` and every
real `m`, `s_d(u₂, u₁) (s_d(u₁, u₂) m) = m` for the moment dimension `d = M L² T⁻²` (force times
length).

Equivalently, for every moment `τ`, a quantity of dimension `M L² T⁻²`, multiplying its value by
`s_d(u₁, u₂)` and then by `s_d(u₂, u₁)` and tagging the result with that dimension gives `τ` back.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
