# Planar moment of dimensioned forces about a point

Let `x_i` (`i` in a finite index set) be lever arms, plane vectors of dimension `L` (positions
relative to the moment point, in one frame), and `F_i` forces, plane vectors of dimension `M L T⁻²`
in the same frame. Their planar moment about the moment point is
`τ = Σ_i (x_i,0 F_i,1 - x_i,1 F_i,0)`, formed from products of force and length components, so it is
a quantity of dimension `M L² T⁻²` (force times length). This dimension is also that of energy: the
dimension does not distinguish a moment from work. A quantity of dimension `d` is a coordinate value
(a real number or a vector of components in one frame) tagged with `d`; sums of quantities keep the
dimension and products multiply dimensions.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
