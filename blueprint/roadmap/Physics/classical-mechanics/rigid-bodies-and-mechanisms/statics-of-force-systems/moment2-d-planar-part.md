# The planar moment of projected forces is the third moment component

For a finite index set and forces `F_i` applied at points `r_i` in `ℝ³`, the
[planar moment](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/moment2-d.md)
about the origin of the [planar parts](planar-part.md) of the forces at the planar parts of the
points equals the third component of the [moment about the origin](moment-about.md) of the forces
with no free couples: `∑_i (r_{i,0} F_{i,1} - r_{i,1} F_{i,0}) = (∑_i r_i × F_i)₂`. Free couples
are discarded, since the planar moment carries none.
