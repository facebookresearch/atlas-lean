# Value of the dimensioned planar moment

Let `x_i` (`i` in a finite index set) be lever arms, plane vectors of dimension `L` (positions
relative to the moment point, in one frame), and `F_i` forces, plane vectors of dimension `M L T⁻²`
in the same frame. The value of their [planar moment](force-moment2-d.md) is the
[planar moment about the origin](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/moment2-d.md)
of the coordinates, `Σ_i (x_i,0 F_i,1 - x_i,1 F_i,0)`.

Equivalently, for plane coordinate vectors `r_i` and `f_i`, the planar moment of the lever arms
`r_i` tagged with the length dimension `L` and the forces `f_i` tagged with the force dimension is
`Σ_i (r_i,0 f_i,1 - r_i,1 f_i,0)` tagged with the moment dimension `M L² T⁻²`.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
