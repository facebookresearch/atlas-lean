# Dimensioned planar balance iff the coordinates balance

Let `x_i` (`i` in a finite index set) be lever arms, plane vectors of dimension `L` (positions
relative to the moment point, in one frame), and `F_i` forces, plane vectors of dimension `M L T⁻²`
in the same frame. The force balance `Σ_i F_i = 0` and the moment balance `τ = 0`, where
`τ = Σ_i (x_i,0 F_i,1 - x_i,1 F_i,0)` is the [planar moment](force-moment2-d.md), hold together if
and only if the force coordinates are
[equilibrated](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/is-equilibrated.md)
(zero resultant) and the
[planar moment about the origin](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/moment2-d.md)
of the coordinates is `0`.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
