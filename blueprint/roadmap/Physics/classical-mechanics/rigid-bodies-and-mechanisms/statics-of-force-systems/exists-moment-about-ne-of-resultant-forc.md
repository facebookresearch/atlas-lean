# With nonzero resultant some reference point changes the moment

For a finite index set, forces `F_i` applied at points `r_i` and free couples `C_i` in `ℝ³` with
resultant `∑_i F_i ≠ 0`, and every reference point `O ∈ ℝ³`, there is a reference point `O'` with
`M_{O'} ≠ M_O`, where `M` is the [moment](moment-about.md). With the
[zero-resultant case](moment-about-eq-of-resultant-force-eq-ze.md), the moment does not depend on
the reference point exactly when the resultant vanishes.
