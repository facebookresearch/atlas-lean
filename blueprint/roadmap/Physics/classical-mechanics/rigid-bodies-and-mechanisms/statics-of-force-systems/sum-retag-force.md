# The resultant of retagged force coordinates

Let `f_r` (`r` in a finite index set) be coordinate vectors with components indexed by a set `ι`.
The sum of the forces obtained by tagging each `f_r` with the force dimension `M L T⁻²` is the
[resultant](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/resultant-force.md)
`Σ_r f_r` tagged with the force dimension. A quantity of dimension `d` is a coordinate value (a real
number or a vector of components in one frame) tagged with `d`; sums of quantities keep the
dimension and products multiply dimensions.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
