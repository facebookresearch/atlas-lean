# Coordinates of a resultant of dimensioned forces

Let `F_r` (`r` in a finite index set) be forces, quantities of dimension `M L T⁻²`, with components
indexed by a set `ι`. The coordinates of the sum `Σ_r F_r` are the
[resultant](../../particles-and-oscillations/harmonic-oscillators-equilibrium-and-spring-netw/resultant-force.md)
`Σ_r F_r,val` of the coordinates of the forces. A quantity of dimension `d` is a coordinate value (a
real number or a vector of components in one frame) tagged with `d`; sums of quantities keep the
dimension and products multiply dimensions.

Equivalently, for coordinate vectors `f_r`, the sum of the forces obtained by tagging each `f_r`
with the force dimension is `Σ_r f_r` tagged with the force dimension.

Here `M`, `L` and `T` denote the mass, length and time dimensions.
