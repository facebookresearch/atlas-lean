# The normal stress of the pressure force is −p

For a pressure `p` and an area `A` with positive value, the normal stress `σ = F / A` of the
[pressure force](pressure-force.md) `-p A` on the same area is `-p`. The sign follows the
tension-positive convention: the force is the component along the outward unit normal of the face,
and pressure is the negative of the tension-positive normal stress, so a positive pressure pushes
against the outward normal.
