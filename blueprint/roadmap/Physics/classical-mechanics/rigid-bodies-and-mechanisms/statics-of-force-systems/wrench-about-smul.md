# Scaling lengths and forces scales the wrench

For a finite index set, real numbers `ℓ` and `f`, forces `F_i` applied at points `r_i`, free couples
`C_i` in `ℝ³` and a reference point `O ∈ ℝ³`, the [wrench](wrench-about.md) of the forces `f F_i`
applied at the points `ℓ r_i`, with couples `ℓ f C_i`, about the point `ℓ O` has resultant
`f ∑_i F_i` and moment `ℓ f M_O`. In the physical reading `ℓ > 0` and `f > 0` are the factors of a
change of length and force units, and couples and moments carry their product; the identity holds
for all real `ℓ` and `f`.
