# The displacement between two points is a free vector

For every origin `o ∈ ℝ³`, orientation `R ∈ SO(3)` and body coordinates `x, y ∈ ℝ³`,
`(o + R x) - (o + R y) = R (x - y)`: the difference of the
[inertial coordinates](body-to-inertial.md) of two points is the
[inertial component vector](body-to-inertial-vec.md) of the difference of their body coordinates,
and the frame origin cancels.
