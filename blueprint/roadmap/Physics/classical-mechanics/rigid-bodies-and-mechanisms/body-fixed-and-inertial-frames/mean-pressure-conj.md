# The mean pressure is the same in body and inertial components

For every orientation `R ∈ SO(3)` and every real 3 × 3 matrix `σ`, the
[mean pressure](mean-pressure.md) satisfies `p(R σ Rᵀ) = p(σ)`. Stress components transform as
`σ_I = R σ_B Rᵀ` between body and inertial components, so the mean pressure does not depend on the
frame.
