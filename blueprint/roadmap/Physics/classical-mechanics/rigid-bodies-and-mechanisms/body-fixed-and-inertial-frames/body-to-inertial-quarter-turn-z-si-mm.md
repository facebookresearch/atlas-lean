# Quarter-turn example in metres and in millimetres

For the [quarter turn](quarter-turn-z.md) `R` about the third axis:

- the body point `(1, 0, 0)` of the frame with origin `(2, 0, 0)` has
  [inertial coordinates](body-to-inertial.md) `(2, 1, 0)`;
- the factor converting a length coordinate from base units (metres) to the unit choice with the
  millimetre as length unit is `1000`;
- with the origin `(2000, 0, 0)` and the body point `(1000, 0, 0)`, the inertial coordinates are
  `(2000, 1000, 0)`: the same point, in millimetres.
