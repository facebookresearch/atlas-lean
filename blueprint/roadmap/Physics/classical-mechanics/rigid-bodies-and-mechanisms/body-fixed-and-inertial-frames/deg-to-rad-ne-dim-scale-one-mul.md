# Converting degrees to radians is not a change of unit scale

Let `u₁` and `u₂` be unit choices on the seven ISQ base dimensions. A dimensionless coordinate is
multiplied by the factor `1` under the change from `u₁` to `u₂`, so it does not rescale. For every
real `θ ≠ 0`, the [radian measure](deg-to-rad.md) `θ π / 180` differs from `1 · θ`: the conversion
from degrees to radians is an explicit factor, not a change of unit scale.
