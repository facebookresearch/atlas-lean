# At ninety degrees the rotation about the third axis is a quarter turn

The [rotation about the third axis](rot-z.md) by the angle `π / 2`, the
[radian measure](deg-to-rad.md) of `90` degrees, is the matrix with rows `(0, -1, 0)`, `(1, 0, 0)`
and `(0, 0, 1)`: it maps `e₁ ↦ e₂`, `e₂ ↦ -e₁` and `e₃ ↦ e₃`.
