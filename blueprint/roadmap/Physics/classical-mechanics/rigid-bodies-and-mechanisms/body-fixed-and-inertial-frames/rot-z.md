# Rotation matrix about the third axis

For a real number `θ`, an angle in radians, the rotation about the third axis is the matrix with
rows `(cos θ, -sin θ, 0)`, `(sin θ, cos θ, 0)` and `(0, 0, 1)`. It turns `e₁` towards `e₂` for
`0 < θ < π`, counterclockwise when seen from the positive third axis, and fixes `e₃`.
