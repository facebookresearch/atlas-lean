# Under the quarter turn the components of a moment change

For the [quarter turn](quarter-turn-z.md) `R` about the third axis, `R (e₂ × e₃) ≠ e₂ × e₃`: the
moment `e₂ × e₃ = e₁` has [inertial components](body-to-inertial-vec.md) `R e₁ = e₂`. So the
components of a torque change with the frame, unlike the frame-independent
[dot products](body-to-inertial-vec-dot-product.md), although torque and energy share the dimension
`M L² T⁻²`.
