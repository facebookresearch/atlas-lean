# The mean pressure rescales with the stress unit

Let `u₁` and `u₂` be unit choices on the seven ISQ base dimensions (each base unit of positive
magnitude), and `λ > 0` the factor by which a coordinate of the stress dimension `M L⁻¹ T⁻²` in
`u₁` is multiplied to give the coordinate in `u₂`. For every real 3 × 3 matrix `σ`, the
[mean pressure](mean-pressure.md) satisfies `p(λ σ) = λ p(σ)`: the mean pressure carries the stress
unit.
