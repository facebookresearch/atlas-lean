# The passive map to body components is the active rotation by R⁻¹

For every orientation `R ∈ SO(3)` and every `v ∈ ℝ³`, the [body components](inertial-to-body-vec.md)
`Rᵀ v` of the free vector with inertial components `v` equal the
[active rotation](rotate-active.md) of `v` by the inverse `R⁻¹ = Rᵀ` in `SO(3)`: turning the frame
by `R` turns components by the inverse.
