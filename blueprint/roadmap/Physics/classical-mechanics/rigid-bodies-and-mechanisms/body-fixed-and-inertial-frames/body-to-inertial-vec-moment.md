# Inertial components of a moment about the body origin

For every origin `o ∈ ℝ³`, orientation `R ∈ SO(3)`, body point `x_B ∈ ℝ³` and force with body
components `F_B ∈ ℝ³`, `R (x_B × F_B) = (x_I - o) × (R F_B)`, where `x_I = o + R x_B` are the
[inertial coordinates](body-to-inertial.md) of the point: the moment about the body origin has
inertial components given by the lever arm from the origin `o` to `x_I`.
