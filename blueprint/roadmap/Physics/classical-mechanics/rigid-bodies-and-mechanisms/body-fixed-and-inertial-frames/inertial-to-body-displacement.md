# Displaced points have body coordinates relative to the centre of mass

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions, with centre of mass `c`, centre-of-mass
trajectory `t ↦ o(t)` and orientation `t ↦ R(t) ∈ SO(3)`. At every time `t` and for every body
point `y`, the [body coordinates](inertial-to-body.md) `R(t)ᵀ (x_I - o(t))` of its displaced
position `x_I` are `y - c`.
