# The reflection of the first axis reverses the cross product

For all `a, b ∈ ℝ³`, the [reflection](reflect-x.md) `P = diag(-1, 1, 1)` satisfies
`(P a) × (P b) = -(P (a × b))`: this reflection, of determinant `-1`, reverses the cross-product
orientation, unlike a [proper rotation](body-to-inertial-vec-cross.md).
