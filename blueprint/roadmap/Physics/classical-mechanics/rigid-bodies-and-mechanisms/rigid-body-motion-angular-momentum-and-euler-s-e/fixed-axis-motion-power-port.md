# The axial torque delivers the rate of rotational kinetic energy

For a rigid body in three dimensions with centre of mass `c = 0` and inertia tensor
`I = diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)` about it, all real numbers `ω₀` and `α` and every time `t`, the
rotational kinetic energy `T(s) = ω_B(s) · (I ω_B(s)) / 2` along the
[fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) is differentiable at `t` with derivative
`τ_B · ω_B(t)`, where `τ_B = (0, 0, I_zz α)` is the [axial torque](fixed-axis-torque.md); this equals
`I_zz α θ'(t)`, the shaft power `P = τ ω` of the torque `I_zz α` at the angular velocity
`θ'(t) = ω₀ + α t`, signed about the third axis.
