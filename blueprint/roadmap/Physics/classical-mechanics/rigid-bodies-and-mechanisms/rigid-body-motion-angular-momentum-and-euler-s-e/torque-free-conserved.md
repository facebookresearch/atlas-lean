# Torque-free motion conserves rotational energy and ‖L‖²

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions whose body frame is centred at the centre of mass
(`c = 0`), with orientation `R`, body angular velocity `ω_B` and inertia tensor `I_B` about the
centre of mass, and let `U` be an open preconnected set of times on which `R` is `C²` entry by entry
and `dL_COM/dt = 0`, where `L_COM` is the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md). Then for all `s, t ∈ U` the
rotational kinetic energy `ω_B · (I_B ω_B) / 2` and the squared norm `(I_B ω_B) · (I_B ω_B)` take
the same values at `s` and at `t`; the latter is
[`L_COM · L_COM`](com-angular-momentum-dot-product-self.md).
