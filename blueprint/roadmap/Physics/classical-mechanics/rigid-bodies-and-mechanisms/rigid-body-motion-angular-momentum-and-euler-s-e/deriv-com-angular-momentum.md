# Rate of change of the angular momentum about the centre of mass

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions whose body frame is centred at the centre of
mass (`c = 0`), with orientation `R`, body angular velocity `ω_B` and inertia tensor `I_B` about the
centre of mass, and let `U` be an open set of times on which `R` is `C²` entry by entry. At every
`t ∈ U`, the [angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md) satisfies
`dL_COM/dt = R (I_B ω_B' + ω_B × (I_B ω_B))`; the body inertia `I_B` does not depend on time.
