# About a principal axis, Euler's equations hold with the axial torque

For a rigid body in three dimensions whose inertia tensor about the centre of mass is
`I_B = diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)`, all real numbers `ω₀` and `α` and every time `t`, the
[fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) satisfies
`I_B ω_B'(t) + ω_B(t) × (I_B ω_B(t)) = (0, 0, I_zz α)`, the [axial torque](fixed-axis-torque.md):
the gyroscopic term vanishes on the axis. In the physical reading the principal inertias are
nonnegative; the identity holds for all real values.
