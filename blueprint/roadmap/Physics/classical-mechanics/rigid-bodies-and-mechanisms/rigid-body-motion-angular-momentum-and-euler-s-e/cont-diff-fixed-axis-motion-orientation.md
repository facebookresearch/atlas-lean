# The fixed-axis orientation is smooth

For every rigid body in three dimensions, all real numbers `ω₀` and `α` and all indices `i, j`, the
entry `(i, j)` of the orientation `R(t) = Rz(ω₀ t + α t² / 2)` of the
[fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) is an infinitely differentiable function of `t`.
