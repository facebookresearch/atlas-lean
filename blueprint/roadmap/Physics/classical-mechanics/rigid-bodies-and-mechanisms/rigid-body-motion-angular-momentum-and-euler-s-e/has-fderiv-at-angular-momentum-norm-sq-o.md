# Without torque the squared body angular momentum is stationary

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions whose body frame is centred at the centre of
mass (`c = 0`), with orientation `R`, body angular velocity `ω_B` and inertia tensor `I_B` about the
centre of mass, and let `U` be an open set of times on which `R` is `C²` entry by entry. At a time
`t ∈ U` with `dL_COM/dt (t) = 0`, where `L_COM` is the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md), the squared Euclidean norm
`s ↦ (I_B ω_B(s)) · (I_B ω_B(s))` has derivative `0` at `t`.
