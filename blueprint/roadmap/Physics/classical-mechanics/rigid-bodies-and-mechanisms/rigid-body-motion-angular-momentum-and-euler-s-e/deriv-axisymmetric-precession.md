# Derivative of the precessing body angular velocity

For all real numbers `I_⊥`, `I₃`, `A` and `ω₃` and every time `t`, the
[precessing angular velocity](axisymmetric-precession.md)
`ω_B(t) = (A cos(ν t), A sin(ν t), ω₃)` is differentiable at `t`, with
`ω_B'(t) = (-A ν sin(ν t), A ν cos(ν t), 0)`, where `ν` is the
[precession rate](axisymmetric-precession-rate.md).
