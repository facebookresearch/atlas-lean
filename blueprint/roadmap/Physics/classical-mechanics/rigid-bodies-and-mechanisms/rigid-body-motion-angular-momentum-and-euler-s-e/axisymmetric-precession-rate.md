# Precession rate of a torque-free axisymmetric body

For real numbers `I_⊥` (transverse principal inertia), `I₃` (axial principal inertia) and `ω₃`
(axial spin), the precession rate is `ν = (I₃ - I_⊥) ω₃ / I_⊥`. With the convention `a / 0 = 0` it
is `0` when `I_⊥ = 0`; the intended domain is `I_⊥ > 0`.
