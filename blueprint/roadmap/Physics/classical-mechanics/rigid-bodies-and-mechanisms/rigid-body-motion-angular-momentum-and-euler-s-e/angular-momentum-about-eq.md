# Angular momentum about a fixed point adds the orbital term

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions with nonzero total mass `m = ∫ 1 dm`,
centre-of-mass trajectory `X` and centre-of-mass velocity `V = Ẋ`. For every fixed point `O ∈ ℝ³`
and every time `t`, the [angular momentum about `O`](angular-momentum-about.md) is
`L_O(t) = L_COM(t) + m (X(t) - O) × V(t)`, with `L_COM` the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md).
