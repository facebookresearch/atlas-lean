# For α ≠ 0 the sign-flipped torque does not drive the fixed-axis motion

For a rigid body in three dimensions with centre of mass `c = 0` and inertia tensor
`I_B = diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)` about it with `I_zz > 0`, real numbers `ω₀` and `α ≠ 0`, and every
time `t`, the [fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) satisfies neither
`I_B ω_B'(t) + ω_B(t) × (I_B ω_B(t)) = -τ_B` nor `dL_COM/dt (t) = -τ_B`, where
`τ_B = (0, 0, I_zz α)` is the [axial torque](fixed-axis-torque.md) and `L_COM` the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md).
