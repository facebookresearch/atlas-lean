# With positive axial inertia, nonzero angular acceleration needs torque

For a rigid body in three dimensions with centre of mass `c = 0` and inertia tensor
`diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)` about it with `I_zz > 0`, real numbers `ω₀` and `α ≠ 0`, and every time
`t`: the [axial torque](fixed-axis-torque.md) `(0, 0, I_zz α)`, the body angular acceleration
`ω_B'(t)` of the [fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) and the rate `dL_COM/dt (t)` of its
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md) are all nonzero.
