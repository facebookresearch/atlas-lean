# About a principal axis the balance law holds with the axial torque

For a rigid body in three dimensions with centre of mass `c = 0` and inertia tensor
`diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)` about it, all real numbers `ω₀` and `α` and every time `t`, the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md) of the
[fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md) satisfies `dL_COM/dt (t) = R(t) τ_B`, and
`R(t) τ_B = τ_B`, where `τ_B = (0, 0, I_zz α)` is the [axial torque](fixed-axis-torque.md): the
rotation about the third axis leaves it unchanged.
