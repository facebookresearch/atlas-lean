# The precessing angular velocity solves torque-free Euler's equations

For real numbers `I_⊥ > 0`, `I₃`, `A` and `ω₃`, and the axisymmetric inertia
`I = diag(I_⊥, I_⊥, I₃)`, the [precessing angular velocity](axisymmetric-precession.md) `ω_B`
satisfies `I ω_B'(t) + ω_B(t) × (I ω_B(t)) = 0` at every time `t`.
