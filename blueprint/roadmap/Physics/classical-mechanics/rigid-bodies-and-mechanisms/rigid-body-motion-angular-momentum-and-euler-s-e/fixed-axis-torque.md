# Body torque driving the fixed-axis rotation

For real numbers `I_zz` and `α`, the torque of the fixed-axis motion is the body vector
`τ_B = (0, 0, I_zz α)`. For a body with inertia `diag(I_xx, I_yy, I_zz)` about its centre of mass,
it is the torque about the centre of mass that drives the [fixed-axis motion](fixed-axis-motion.md)
with angular acceleration `α` (see [Euler's equations](fixed-axis-motion-euler.md)).
