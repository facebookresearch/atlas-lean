# Body and inertial angular momenta have the same squared norm

Let `M` be a rigid-body motion in three dimensions whose body frame is centred at the centre of
mass (`c = 0`), with orientation `R`, body angular velocity `ω_B` and inertia tensor `I_B` about the
centre of mass. At every time `t` at which `R` is differentiable,
`L_COM(t) · L_COM(t) = (I_B ω_B(t)) · (I_B ω_B(t))`, with `L_COM` the
[angular momentum about the centre of mass](com-angular-momentum.md): the squared Euclidean norms
agree.
