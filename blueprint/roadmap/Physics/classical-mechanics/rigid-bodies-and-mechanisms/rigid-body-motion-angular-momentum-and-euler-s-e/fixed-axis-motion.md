# Rotation of a rigid body about a fixed axis through its centre of mass

For a rigid body in three dimensions, given by a mass distribution with centre of mass `c`, and real
numbers `ω₀` and `α`, the fixed-axis motion keeps the centre of mass at the inertial origin,
`X(t) = 0`, and has the orientation `R(t) = Rz(θ(t)) ∈ SO(3)`, where `Rz` is the
[rotation about the third axis](../body-fixed-and-inertial-frames/rot-z.md) and `θ(t) = ω₀ t + α t² / 2` the
[rotation angle](fixed-axis-angle.md). The body point `y` is then at `R(t) (y - c)`.
