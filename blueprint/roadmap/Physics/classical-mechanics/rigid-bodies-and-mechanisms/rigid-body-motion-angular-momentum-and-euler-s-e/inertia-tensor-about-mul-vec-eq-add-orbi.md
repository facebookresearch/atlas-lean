# Parallel-axis theorem for angular momentum

Let a rigid body be given by a mass distribution written `∫ f dm`, with nonzero total mass
`m = ∫ 1 dm` and centre of mass `c`, and for a point `p` let
`I_p = ∫ (|y - p|² 1 - (y - p)(y - p)ᵀ) dm` be its inertia tensor about `p`. For every point `p` and
every `ω ∈ ℝ³`, `I_p ω = I_c ω + m (c - p) × (ω × (c - p))`: the angular momentum about `p` of the
body turning with angular velocity `ω` about `p` is the spin term about the centre of mass plus the
orbital term of the total mass at `c`, which moves with velocity `ω × (c - p)`.
