# Without external torque, wheel and body exchange angular momentum

Let `J > 0` and `T` be real numbers and `ω`, `ω'`, `h`, `u` and `τ_ext` real functions of time
such that, at every `t ∈ [0, T]`, `ω` has the derivative `ω'(t)`, `h` has the derivative `u(t)`,
`J ω'(t) = τ_ext(t) − u(t)` and `τ_ext(t) = 0`. Then for every `t ∈ [0, T]`

`J ω(t) + h(t) = J ω(0) + h(0)` and `ω(t) − ω(0) = −(h(t) − h(0)) / J`.

So the total momentum `J ω + h` is conserved on `[0, T]` whatever the command `u`: the momentum
the wheel gains relative to the body, the locked vehicle `J ω` loses. For `T < 0` the interval is
empty and nothing is claimed.
