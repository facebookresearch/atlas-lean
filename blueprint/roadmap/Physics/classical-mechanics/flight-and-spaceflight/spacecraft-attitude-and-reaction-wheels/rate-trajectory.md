# Exponentially decaying body-rate trajectory

For real numbers `I`, `k`, `ω₀` and `t`, the rate trajectory is

`ω(t) = ω₀ exp(−k t / I)`.

In the physical reading `I > 0` is the axial inertia with the wheel locked and `k > 0` the
feedback gain, so the rate decays from `ω₀` for `t ≥ 0`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, at
`I = 0` the trajectory is the constant `ω₀`.
