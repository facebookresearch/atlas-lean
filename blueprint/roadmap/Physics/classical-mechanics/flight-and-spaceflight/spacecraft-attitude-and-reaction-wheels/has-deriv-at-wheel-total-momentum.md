# Rate of the total angular momentum of a body with a reaction wheel

Let `J` be a real number, `ω`, `ω'`, `h`, `u` and `τ_ext` real functions of time, and `t` a time
at which `ω` has the derivative `ω'(t)`, `h` has the derivative `u(t)`, and the body equation
`J ω'(t) = τ_ext(t) − u(t)` holds. Then `s ↦ J ω(s) + h(s)` has the derivative `τ_ext(t)` at `t`.

In the reduced fixed-axis model, `J` is the axial inertia of the body with the wheel locked, `ω`
the body rate, `h` the wheel's angular momentum relative to the body and `u = h'` its commanded
rate; then `J ω + h` is the total angular momentum about the axis and the body equation is exact.
The identity holds for every real `J`; in the physical reading `J > 0`.
