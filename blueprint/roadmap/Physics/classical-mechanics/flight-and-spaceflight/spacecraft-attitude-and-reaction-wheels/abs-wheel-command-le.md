# The wheel command along the trajectory stays within its limit

Let `I > 0`, `k > 0` and `U_max` be real numbers and `|ω₀| ≤ U_max / k`. Then for every `t ≥ 0`
the [wheel command](wheel-command.md) along the [rate trajectory](rate-trajectory.md)
`ω(t) = ω₀ exp(−k t / I)` obeys `|u_w(ω(t))| ≤ U_max`.
