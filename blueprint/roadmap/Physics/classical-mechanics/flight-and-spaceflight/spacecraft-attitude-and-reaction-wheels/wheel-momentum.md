# Wheel momentum along the decaying rate trajectory

For real numbers `I`, `k`, `ω₀`, `h₀` and `t`, the wheel momentum trajectory is

`h_w(t) = h₀ + I ω₀ (1 − exp(−k t / I))`,

so that `I ω(t) + h_w(t) = I ω₀ + h₀` for the [rate trajectory](rate-trajectory.md)
`ω(t) = ω₀ exp(−k t / I)`. In the physical reading `I > 0` and `k > 0`. With the convention
`x / 0 = 0`, at `I = 0` the trajectory is the constant `h₀`.
