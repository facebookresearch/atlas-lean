# The density bounds of a band fail outside the band

For `ρ0 > 0`, `g > 0`, `Rs > 0` and `T > 0`, the [density](isothermal-density.md) `ρ` of the
isothermal layer satisfies `ρ(z) > ρ0 exp(−z₁ / H)` for every `z < z₁` and `ρ(z) < ρ0 exp(−z₂ / H)`
for every `z > z₂`, where `H = Rs T / |g|`: the [band bounds](isothermal-density-band-bounds.md)
need `z ∈ [z₁, z₂]`.
