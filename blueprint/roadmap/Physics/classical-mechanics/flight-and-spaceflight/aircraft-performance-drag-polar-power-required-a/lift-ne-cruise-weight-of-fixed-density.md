# At fixed density the lift cannot follow the falling weight

For `Wi > 0`, `cW > 0`, `Λ > 0` and `t > 0`, if the lift `ρ V² / 2 · S · CL` at fixed density, speed
and lift coefficient equals the initial weight `Wi`, then it differs from the
[cruise weight](cruise-weight.md) `W(t) = Wi exp(−(cW / Λ) t)`.
