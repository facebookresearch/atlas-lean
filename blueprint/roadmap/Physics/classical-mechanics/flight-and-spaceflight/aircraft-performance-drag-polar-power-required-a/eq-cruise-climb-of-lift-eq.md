# The cruise-climb is the only height schedule that balances the weight

Let `ρ0 > 0`, `g > 0`, `Rs > 0`, `T > 0` and `V, S, CL ≠ 0`. If the lift `ρ(z₀) V² / 2 · S · CL` at
the height `z₀` equals `Wi` and the lift at the height `z` equals the
[cruise weight](cruise-weight.md) `W(t) = Wi exp(−(cW / Λ) t)`, where `ρ` is the
[density](isothermal-density.md) of the isothermal layer, then `z = z₀ + H (cW / Λ) t`, with
`H = Rs T / |g|` the [scale height](scale-height.md). Since the lift at that height does equal
`W(t)` ([cruise-climb](lift-cruise-climb.md)), it is the only height at which the lift balances
the cruise weight at time `t`.
