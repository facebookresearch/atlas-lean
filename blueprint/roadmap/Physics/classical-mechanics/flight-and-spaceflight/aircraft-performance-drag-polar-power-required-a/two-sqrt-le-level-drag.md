# Level-flight drag is at least 2 √(a b)

For `ρ > 0`, `S > 0`, `CD0 ≥ 0`, `k ≥ 0`, every real `W` and every speed `V ≠ 0`, the
[level-flight drag](level-drag.md) `D(V) = a V² + b / V²` satisfies `2 √(a b) ≤ D(V)`, with
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors) (the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality).
