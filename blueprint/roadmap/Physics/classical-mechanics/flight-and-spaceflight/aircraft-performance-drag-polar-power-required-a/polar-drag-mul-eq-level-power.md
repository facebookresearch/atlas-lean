# In level flight the polar drag times speed is the power required

For `ρ ≠ 0`, `S ≠ 0` and `V ≠ 0`, if the lift `ρ V² / 2 · S · CL` equals the weight `W`, then the
[polar drag](polar-drag.md) times the speed, `ρ V² / 2 · S · (CD0 + k CL²) · V`, equals the
[power required](level-power.md) `P(V) = D(V) V`.
