# For positive weight, the least level-flight drag is 2 √(a b)

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, `2 √(a b)` is the least value of the
[level-flight drag](level-drag.md) `D(V) = a V² + b / V²` over positive speeds `V > 0`: it is
attained at some positive speed and bounds `D(V)` from below for every `V > 0`; here
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors).
