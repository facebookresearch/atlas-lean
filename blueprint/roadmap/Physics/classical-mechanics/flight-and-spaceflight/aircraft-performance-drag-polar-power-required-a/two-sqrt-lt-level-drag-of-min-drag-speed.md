# If V_md < V_s, every speed above stall has drag above 2 √(a b)

Let `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k, CLmax > 0`, and suppose the [minimum-drag speed](min-drag-speed.md)
`V_md` is below the [stall speed](stall-speed.md) `V_s = √(2 W / (ρ S CLmax))`. Then every speed
`V > 0` with lift coefficient `CL(V) = 2 W / (ρ V² S) ≤ CLmax` has level-flight drag
`D(V) > 2 √(a b)`, with `a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the
[parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and [induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors): the
unconstrained minimizer is not an operating point.
