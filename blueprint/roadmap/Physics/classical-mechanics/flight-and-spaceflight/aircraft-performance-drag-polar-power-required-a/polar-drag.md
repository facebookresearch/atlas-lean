# Drag of the parabolic drag polar

For reals `ρ` (air density), `S` (wing area), `CD0`, `k`, `CL` (lift coefficient) and `V` (speed),
the drag `D = ρ V² / 2 · S · (CD0 + k CL²)` of the declared parabolic polar `CD = CD0 + k CL²`. In
the physical reading the density, wing area and speed are positive and `CD0, k ≥ 0`.
