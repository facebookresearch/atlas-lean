# At a fixed lift coefficient the polar drag is W / Λ

For `CL ≠ 0`, if the lift `ρ V² / 2 · S · CL` equals the weight `W`, then the
[polar drag](polar-drag.md) is `W / Λ`, with `Λ = CL / (CD0 + k CL²)` the
[lift-to-drag ratio](polar-lift-to-drag.md) (both sides are `0` when `CD0 + k CL² = 0`, by the
convention `x / 0 = 0`). With thrust along the velocity balancing this drag, the weight-based fuel
law `W' = −cW T` becomes `W' = −cW W / Λ`.
