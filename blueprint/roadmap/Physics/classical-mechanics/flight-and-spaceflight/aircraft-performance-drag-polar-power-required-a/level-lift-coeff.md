# Lift coefficient for level flight

For reals `W` (weight), `ρ`, `S` and `V`, the lift coefficient `CL = 2 W / (ρ V² S)` at which the
lift `ρ V² / 2 · S · CL` equals the weight. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when
`ρ V² S = 0`, for example at `V = 0`. In the physical reading the weight, density, wing area and
speed are positive.
