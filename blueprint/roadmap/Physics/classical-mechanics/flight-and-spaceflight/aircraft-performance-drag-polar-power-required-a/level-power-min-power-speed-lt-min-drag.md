# For positive weight, flying at minimum drag needs more power

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, the [power required](level-power.md) `P(V) = D(V) V` at the
[minimum-drag speed](min-drag-speed.md) `V_md` strictly exceeds the least power `P(V_mp)` at the
[minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md).
