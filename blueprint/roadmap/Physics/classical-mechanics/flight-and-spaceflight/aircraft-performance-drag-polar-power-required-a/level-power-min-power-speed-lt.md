# For W > 0, the minimum-power speed is the unique minimizer among V > 0

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, every speed `V > 0` other than the
[minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md) `V_mp` needs strictly more power: `P(V_mp) < P(V)` for the
[power required](level-power.md) `P(V) = D(V) V`. Since `V_mp > 0`
([positivity](min-power-speed-pos.md)), it is the unique minimizer of `P` among positive speeds.
