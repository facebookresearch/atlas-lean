# Range of a cruise at constant air speed

Let `cW > 0`, `Λ > 0` and `t₁ ≥ 0`, let `W` satisfy the fuel law `W' = −cW W / Λ` at every
`t ∈ [0, t₁]`, with `W(0) > 0`, and let the air-path distance `s` satisfy `s' = V` on `[0, t₁]` for
a constant real `V`. Then `s(t₁) − s(0) = V · Λ / cW · log(W(0) / W(t₁))`: the speed times the
[Breguet endurance](breguet-duration.md).
