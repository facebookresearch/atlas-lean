# The isothermal cruise-climb keeps the lift equal to the weight

Let `g > 0`, `Rs > 0` and `T > 0`, and suppose the lift `ρ(z₀) V² / 2 · S · CL` at the height `z₀`
equals `Wi`, where `ρ` is the [density](isothermal-density.md) of the isothermal layer. Then for
every time `t`, at the same speed and lift coefficient, the lift at the height `z₀ + H (cW / Λ) t`
equals the [cruise weight](cruise-weight.md) `W(t) = Wi exp(−(cW / Λ) t)`, where `H = Rs T / |g|` is
the [scale height](scale-height.md).
