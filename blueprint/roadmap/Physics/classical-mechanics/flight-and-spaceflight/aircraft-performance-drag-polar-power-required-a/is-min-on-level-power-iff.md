# A positive speed minimizes power exactly when V⁴ = b / (3a)

For `ρ > 0`, `S > 0`, `CD0 > 0`, all real `W` and `k`, and a speed `V > 0`, `V` minimizes the
[power required](level-power.md) `P` over all positive speeds if and only if `V⁴ = b / (3a)`, with
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors). No sign of `k` or `W` is needed: for `b ≤ 0` both
sides are false.
