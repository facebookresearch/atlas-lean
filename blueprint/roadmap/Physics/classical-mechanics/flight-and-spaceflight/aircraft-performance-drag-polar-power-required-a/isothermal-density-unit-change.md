# The density formula commutes with a change of units

Let `l, s, k > 0` count the new length, time and temperature units in one old unit, and let `m` be
real (the mass factor of a change of units when `m > 0`). For `Rs > 0`, `T > 0` and all real `ρ0`,
`g` and `z`, the [density](isothermal-density.md) of the isothermal layer satisfies
`ρ(m ρ0 / l³, l g / s², l² Rs / (s² k), k T; l z) = (m / l³) ρ(ρ0, g, Rs, T; z)`: rescaled inputs
give the rescaled density.
