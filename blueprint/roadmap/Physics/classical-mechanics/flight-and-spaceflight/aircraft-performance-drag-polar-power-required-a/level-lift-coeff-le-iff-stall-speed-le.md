# At positive speeds, CL ≤ CLmax exactly when the speed is at least V_s

For `W > 0`, `ρ > 0`, `S > 0`, `CLmax > 0` and a speed `V > 0`, the
[level-flight lift coefficient](level-lift-coeff.md) `CL(V) = 2 W / (ρ V² S)` satisfies
`CL(V) ≤ CLmax` if and only if `V_s ≤ V`, where `V_s = √(2 W / (ρ S CLmax))` is the
[stall speed](stall-speed.md).
