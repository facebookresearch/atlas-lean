# Power required in level flight

For reals `W`, `ρ`, `S`, `CD0`, `k` and `V`, `P(V) = D(V) V`, the [level-flight drag](level-drag.md)
`D` times the speed: the rate at which the thrust `T = D` works along the flight path. It is the
power delivered by the thrust, not the [shaft power](shaft-power-required.md) of the engine. In the
physical reading the weight, density, wing area and speed are positive and `CD0, k ≥ 0`.
