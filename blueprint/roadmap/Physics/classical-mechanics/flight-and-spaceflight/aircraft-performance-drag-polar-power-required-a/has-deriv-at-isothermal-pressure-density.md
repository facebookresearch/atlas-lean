# Hydrostatic balance in density form

For all reals `ρ0`, `g`, `Rs`, `T` and `z`, the
[isothermal pressure profile](../../../../EarthScience/atmosphere-and-climate/isothermal-atmosphere-and-scale-height/isothermal-pressure.md)
with reference pressure `ρ0 Rs T` has derivative `p'(z) = −ρ(z) g`, where `ρ` is the
[density](isothermal-density.md) of the isothermal layer. When `Rs T = 0` both sides are `0`, by the
convention `x / 0 = 0`. In the physical reading `ρ0, g, Rs, T > 0`.
