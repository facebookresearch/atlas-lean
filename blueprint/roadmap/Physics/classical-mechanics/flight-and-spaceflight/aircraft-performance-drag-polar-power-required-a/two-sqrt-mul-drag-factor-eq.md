# For nonzero ρ and S, 2 √(a b) is 2 √(CD0 k) times the absolute weight

For `ρ ≠ 0`, `S ≠ 0` and all real `W`, `CD0` and `k`, `2 √(a b) = 2 |W| √(CD0 k)`, with
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors). For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0` it is the
[least level-flight drag](is-least-level-drag.md); since the lift equals the weight in level flight,
the greatest lift-to-drag ratio is then `W / (2 W √(CD0 k)) = 1 / (2 √(CD0 k))`. That reading needs
`W > 0`: for `W < 0` the lift is negative and the ratio at minimum drag is `−1 / (2 √(CD0 k))`.
