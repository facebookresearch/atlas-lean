# Using cm in place of g cm multiplies the endurance by g

For `g ≠ 0` and all real `Λ`, `cm`, `Wi` and `Wf`,
`Λ / cm · log(Wi / Wf) = g · Λ / (g cm) · log(Wi / Wf)`: using the mass-based coefficient `cm` where
the weight-based `g cm` belongs multiplies the [Breguet endurance](breguet-duration.md), and so the
range, by `g`.
