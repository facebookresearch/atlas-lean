# Duration of a cruise from its initial and final weights

Let `cW > 0`, `Λ > 0` and `t₁ ≥ 0`, and let `W` satisfy the fuel law `W' = −cW W / Λ` at every
`t ∈ [0, t₁]`, with `W(0) > 0`. Then `t₁ = Λ / cW · log(W(0) / W(t₁))`, the
[Breguet endurance](breguet-duration.md) from `Wi = W(0)` down to `Wf = W(t₁)`.
