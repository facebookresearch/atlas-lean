# Least drag is attainable above stall iff V_s ≤ V_md

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k, CLmax > 0`, some speed `V > 0` with lift coefficient
`CL(V) = 2 W / (ρ V² S) ≤ CLmax` has level-flight drag `D(V) = 2 √(a b)` if and only if the
[stall speed](stall-speed.md) `V_s = √(2 W / (ρ S CLmax))` is at most the
[minimum-drag speed](min-drag-speed.md) `V_md`; here `a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the
[parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and [induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors).
