# Shaft power required at efficiency η

For reals `η`, `W`, `ρ`, `S`, `CD0`, `k` and `V`, the shaft power `P(V) / η` that a propulsor of
efficiency `η` must absorb to deliver the [power required](level-power.md) `P(V) = D(V) V`. With the
convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` at `η = 0`. In the physical reading `0 < η ≤ 1` is a constant.
