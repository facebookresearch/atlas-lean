# Derivative of the power required at a nonzero speed

For all reals `W`, `ρ`, `S`, `CD0`, `k` and every speed `V ≠ 0`, the
[power required](level-power.md) `P` has derivative `P'(V) = 3 a V² − b / V²`, with
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors).
