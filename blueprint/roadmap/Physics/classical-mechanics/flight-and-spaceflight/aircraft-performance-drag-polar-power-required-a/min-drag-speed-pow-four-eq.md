# For nonzero weight, V_md⁴ = 3 V_mp⁴

For `W ≠ 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, the [minimum-drag speed](min-drag-speed.md) `V_md` and the
[minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md) `V_mp` satisfy `V_md⁴ = 3 V_mp⁴`. Since both are positive
([minimum drag](min-drag-speed-pos.md), [minimum power](min-power-speed-pos.md)),
`V_mp = V_md / 3^(1/4)`, about `0.76 V_md`.
