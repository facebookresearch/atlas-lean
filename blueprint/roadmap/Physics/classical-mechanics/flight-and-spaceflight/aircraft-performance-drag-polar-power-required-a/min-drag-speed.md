# Minimum-drag speed

For reals `W`, `ρ`, `S`, `CD0` and `k`, `V_md = √(√(b / a))` with `a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and
`b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors). For `W ≠ 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0` it is the
[positive](min-drag-speed-pos.md) root of [`V⁴ = b / a`](min-drag-speed-pow-four.md), and for
`W > 0` it is the unique positive speed of
[least level-flight drag](level-drag-eq-two-sqrt-iff-eq-min-drag-s.md). With the conventions
`x / 0 = 0` and `√x = 0` for `x ≤ 0`, `V_md = 0` when `a = 0` or `b = 0`, that is when `ρ`, `S`,
`CD0`, `k` or `W` is `0`, and when `b / a < 0`; `0` is not a positive speed. With `ρ, S > 0` and
`CD0, k ≥ 0`, the drag then has no minimum over positive speeds when exactly one of its two terms
vanishes, and every positive speed minimizes it when both vanish.
