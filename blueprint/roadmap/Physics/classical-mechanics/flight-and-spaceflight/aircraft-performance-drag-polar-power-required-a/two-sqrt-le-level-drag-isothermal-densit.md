# The drag bound 2 √(a b) at the isothermal-layer density

For `ρ0 > 0`, `Rs > 0`, `T > 0`, `S > 0`, `CD0 ≥ 0`, `k ≥ 0`, every speed `V ≠ 0` and all real `W`,
`g` and `z`, the [lower bound](two-sqrt-le-level-drag.md) `2 √(a b) ≤ D(V)` holds with the
[density](isothermal-density.md) `ρ = ρ(z)` of the isothermal layer as the air density; here
`a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the [parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and
[induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors).
