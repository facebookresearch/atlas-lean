# For positive weight, CL at the minimum-power speed is √(3 CD0 / k)

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, the [level-flight lift coefficient](level-lift-coeff.md)
`CL(V) = 2 W / (ρ V² S)` at the [minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md) `V_mp` is `√(3 CD0 / k)`.
This is `√3` times the lift coefficient `√(CD0 / k)` at minimum drag, and there the induced-drag
coefficient `k CL²` is three times the zero-lift drag coefficient `CD0`.
