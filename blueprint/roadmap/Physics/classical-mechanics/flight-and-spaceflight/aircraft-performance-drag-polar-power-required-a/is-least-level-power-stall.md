# The least power above stall is at max(V_s, V_mp)

For `W > 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k, CLmax > 0`, the least value of the [power required](level-power.md)
`P(V) = D(V) V` over the speeds `V > 0` with lift coefficient `CL(V) = 2 W / (ρ V² S) ≤ CLmax` is
`P(max(V_s, V_mp))`, where `V_s = √(2 W / (ρ S CLmax))` is the [stall speed](stall-speed.md) and
`V_mp` is the [minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md): the minimum-power speed when it is at least `V_s`,
and the stall speed otherwise.
