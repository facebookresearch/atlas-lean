# For nonzero weight the minimum-power speed solves V⁴ = b / (3a)

For `W ≠ 0` and `ρ, S, CD0, k > 0`, the [minimum-power speed](min-power-speed.md) `V_mp` satisfies
`V_mp⁴ = b / (3a)`, with `a = ρ S CD0 / 2` and `b = 2 k W² / (ρ S)` (the
[parasite-drag](parasite-drag-factor.md) and [induced-drag](induced-drag-factor.md) factors).
