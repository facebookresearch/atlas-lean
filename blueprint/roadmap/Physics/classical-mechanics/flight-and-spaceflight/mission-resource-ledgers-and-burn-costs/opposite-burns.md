# Two opposite equal burns cost twice the magnitude with zero net change

For a normed additive commutative group `E` and `b ∈ E`, the two burns `b` and `−b` have
[delta-v cost](burn-cost.md) `2 ‖b‖` and [net velocity change](net-velocity-change.md) `0`; for
`b ≠ 0` the norm of the net change is strictly below the cost.
