# Ion thrust efficiency is (P_beam / P_in) γ² and at most one

For `m_i, q_i, V_b, I_b, P_in > 0`, `0 ≤ γ ≤ 1` and `P_beam = I_b V_b ≤ P_in`, the
[thrust efficiency](thrust-efficiency.md) of the beam, `T² / (2 ṁ P_in)` with the
[ion thrust](ion-thrust.md) `T = ṁ v_e γ` and the [beam mass flow](beam-mass-flow.md)
`ṁ = I_b m_i / q_i`, equals `(P_beam / P_in) γ²`, and this is at most `1`.
