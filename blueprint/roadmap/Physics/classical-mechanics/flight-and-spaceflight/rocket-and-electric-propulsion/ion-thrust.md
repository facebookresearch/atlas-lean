# Thrust of an ion beam with divergence factor γ

For reals `m_i`, `q_i`, `V_b`, `I_b` and `γ`, `T = ṁ v_e γ`, the product of the
[beam mass flow](beam-mass-flow.md) `ṁ = I_b m_i / q_i`, the
[ion exhaust speed](ion-exhaust-speed.md) `v_e = √(2 q_i V_b / m_i)` and the divergence factor `γ`
(the mean axial cosine of the ion velocities, in `[0, 1]` in the physical reading).
