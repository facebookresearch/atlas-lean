# For an air-breather the total-flow specific impulse is smaller

For `ṁ_in > 0`, `ṁ_f > 0`, the steady mass balance `ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, a thrust `T > 0` and
`g0 > 0`, the total-flow [specific impulse](specific-impulse.md) `T / (ṁ_out g0)` is strictly
smaller than the fuel-flow one `T / (ṁ_f g0)`.
