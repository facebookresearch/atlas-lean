# Separate-flow thrust of an engine at rest

For reals `ṁ_in`, `v_in`, `p_in`, `A_in` (inlet mass flow, axial velocity, pressure and area),
`ṁ_out`, `v_e`, `p_e`, `A_e` (the same at the exhaust) and `p_a` (ambient pressure),
`T = ṁ_out v_e − ṁ_in v_in + (p_e − p_a) A_e − (p_in − p_a) A_in`. It is positive forward; the axis
points aft, along the exhaust, and the velocities are relative to the engine, positive aft.
