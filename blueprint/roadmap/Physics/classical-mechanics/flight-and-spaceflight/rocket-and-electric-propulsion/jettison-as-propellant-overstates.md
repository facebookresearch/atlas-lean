# Counting jettisoned dry mass as propellant overstates the gain

For `ve > 0`, `m₀ > 0` and `0 < m₂ < m₁`, `ve log(m₀ / m₂) = ve log(m₀ / m₁) + ve log(m₁ / m₂)` and
`ve log(m₀ / m₁) < ve log(m₀ / m₂)`: a burn logarithm that also covers a dry-mass drop from `m₁` to
`m₂` exceeds the true burn increment by `ve log(m₁ / m₂) > 0`.
