# Steady with no feed momentum, the stand force is the thrust

Under the ambient condition `F_lat + p_a A_in − p_a A_e = 0`, with negligible fuel feed momentum
(`v_f = 0`) and the steady axial momentum balance
`0 = ṁ_in v_in + ṁ_f v_f − ṁ_out v_e − (−p_in A_in + p_e A_e) + F_lat + F`, the axial force `F` of
the stand on the contents equals the [separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md)
`T = ṁ_out v_e − ṁ_in v_in + (p_e − p_a) A_e − (p_in − p_a) A_in`; the engine exerts this force
forward on the stand.
