# With fuel, the exhaust flow of an air-breather exceeds the inlet flow

Under the steady mass balance `ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, for all real `v_in`, `p_in`, `A_in`, `v_e`,
`p_e`, `A_e` and `p_a`: `ṁ_f > 0` implies `ṁ_in < ṁ_out`; `ṁ_out = ṁ_in` if and only if `ṁ_f = 0`;
and the [separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) with exhaust flow `ṁ_out` minus the one with
the exhaust flow replaced by `ṁ_in` is `ṁ_f v_e`.
