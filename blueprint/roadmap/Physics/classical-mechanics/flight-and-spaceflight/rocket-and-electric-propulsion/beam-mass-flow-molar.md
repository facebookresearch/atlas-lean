# The molar form of the beam mass flow needs the Avogadro constant

For `e > 0` (elementary charge), `N_A > 0` (Avogadro constant), a charge number `z > 0` and real `M`
(molar mass) and `I_b`, take the ion mass `M / N_A` and the charge `z e`. Then the
[beam mass flow](beam-mass-flow.md) is `I_b / (z F) · M` with `F = e N_A`, the molar flow times `M`;
and putting the molar mass `M` in place of the ion mass multiplies the mass flow by `N_A`.
