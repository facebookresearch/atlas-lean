# A rocket with ṁ_f, v_e > 0 and p_e ≥ p_a has positive thrust

With no inlet stream and no capture area (`ṁ_in = 0`, `A_in = 0`), the steady mass balance
`ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, `ṁ_f > 0`, `v_e > 0`, `p_e ≥ p_a` and `A_e ≥ 0`, the
[separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) is positive, whatever `v_in` and `p_in`.
