# At a burning instant the specific impulse is ve / g0

For `g0 > 0`, a real `ve` and a time `t` with `m'(t) < 0` and `m(t) v'(t) = −ve m'(t)`, the
[specific impulse](specific-impulse.md) of the thrust `m(t) v'(t)` at the mass flow `−m'(t)` is
`ve / g0`. In this pressure-matched model the thrust is the momentum rate `(−m') ve`.
