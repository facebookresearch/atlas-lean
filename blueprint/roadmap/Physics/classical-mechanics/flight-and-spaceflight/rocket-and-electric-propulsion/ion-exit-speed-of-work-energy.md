# Exit kinetic energy and speed of an ion from work and energy

Let `E` be a real inner product space, `m_i > 0`, `q_i` a real charge and `t₁ ≥ 0`. Let `φ : E → ℝ`
be differentiable at every point with gradient `∇φ`, and `∇φ` continuous. Let `γ`, `v`, `a : ℝ → E`
satisfy `γ' = v` and `v' = a` at every `t ∈ [0, t₁]`, Newton's law `m_i a(t) = −q_i ∇φ(γ(t))` on
`[0, t₁]`, the start at rest `v(0) = 0`, and the potential drop `φ(γ(0)) − φ(γ(t₁)) = V_b`. Then
`m_i ‖v(t₁)‖² / 2 = q_i V_b` and `‖v(t₁)‖ = √(2 q_i V_b / m_i)`, the
[ion exhaust speed](ion-exhaust-speed.md). For a positive ion falling through the beam voltage
`q_i > 0` and `V_b > 0`; the identity holds for either sign of the charge.
