# Exhaust speed of an ion falling through the beam voltage

For reals `m_i` (ion mass), `q_i` (ion charge) and `V_b` (beam voltage), `v_e = √(2 q_i V_b / m_i)`:
the speed of an ion that starts at rest and falls through the voltage `V_b`
([work and energy](ion-exit-speed-of-work-energy.md)). With the conventions `x / 0 = 0` and `√x = 0`
for `x < 0`, `v_e = 0` when `m_i = 0` or `q_i V_b / m_i < 0`. In the physical reading
`m_i, q_i, V_b > 0`.
