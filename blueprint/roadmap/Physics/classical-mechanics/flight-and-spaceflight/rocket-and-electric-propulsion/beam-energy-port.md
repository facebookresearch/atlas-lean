# Kinetic energy of each ion and kinetic power of the beam

For `m_i > 0`, `q_i > 0`, `V_b > 0` and every real `I_b`, the
[ion exhaust speed](ion-exhaust-speed.md) `v_e = √(2 q_i V_b / m_i)` satisfies
`m_i v_e² / 2 = q_i V_b`, and the [beam mass flow](beam-mass-flow.md) `ṁ = I_b m_i / q_i` satisfies
`ṁ v_e² / 2 = I_b V_b`, the [beam power](beam-power.md).
