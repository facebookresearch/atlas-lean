# Thrust of an air-breather

With the inlet pressure equal to the ambient pressure at the capture plane (`p_in = p_a`) and the
steady mass balance `ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, for all real `v_in`, `A_in`, `v_e`, `p_e` and `A_e`, the
[separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) is
`T = (ṁ_in + ṁ_f) v_e − ṁ_in v_in + (p_e − p_a) A_e`.
