# Ignoring the expelled momentum gives the wrong acceleration

For `m > 0`, `q ≠ 0`, `v ≠ ve` and every real `F`, `(q v + F) / m ≠ (ve q + F) / m`: the
[limit that ignores the expelled momentum](naive-slope-tendsto.md) differs from the
[parcel limit](parcel-slope-tendsto.md) whenever `q (v − ve) ≠ 0`.
