# Ideal rocket equation from the variable-mass law

Let `tb ≥ 0`, functions `m` and `v` with derivatives `m'` and `v'` at every `t ∈ [0, tb]`, `m > 0`
on `[0, tb]`, and the reduced variable-mass law `m(t) v'(t) = −ve m'(t)` on `[0, tb]`, for a real
`ve`. Then `v(tb) − v(0) = ve log(m(0) / m(tb))`. Neither the sign of `ve` nor continuity of the
derivatives is needed. In the model, `ve > 0` is the exhaust speed relative to the vehicle, and no
external force acts.
