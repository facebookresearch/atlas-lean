# With no expelled mass the parcel step is the fixed-mass velocity

For `m > 0` and all real `v`, `ve`, `F` and `h`, the [parcel velocity](parcel-velocity.md) with no
expelled mass (`q = 0`) is `v + (h / m) F`, the velocity at time `h` of a body of fixed mass `m`
under the constant force `F` starting at velocity `v`.
