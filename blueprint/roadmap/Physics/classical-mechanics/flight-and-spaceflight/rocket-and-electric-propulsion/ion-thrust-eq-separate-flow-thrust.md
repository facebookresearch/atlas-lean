# Ion thrust as the thrust of a pressure-matched rocket

For `m_i > 0`, `q_i > 0`, `I_b > 0` and all real `V_b`, `γ`, `v_in`, `p_in`, `p`, `A_e` and `g0`,
the [ion thrust](ion-thrust.md) `T = ṁ v_e γ` (with the [beam mass flow](beam-mass-flow.md)
`ṁ = I_b m_i / q_i` and the [ion exhaust speed](ion-exhaust-speed.md) `v_e = √(2 q_i V_b / m_i)`)
equals the [separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) of an engine with no inlet stream and no
capture area (`ṁ_in = 0`, `A_in = 0`, whatever `v_in` and `p_in`), exhaust flow `ṁ` at the axial
velocity `v_e γ`, exhaust pressure equal to the ambient pressure `p`, and any exhaust area `A_e`.
Its [effective exhaust velocity](effective-exhaust-velocity.md) `T / ṁ` is `v_e γ` and its
[specific impulse](specific-impulse.md) `T / (ṁ g0)` is `v_e γ / g0` (`0` at `g0 = 0`, by the
convention `x / 0 = 0`).
