# For nonzero remaining mass the parcel step solves the momentum balance

For reals `m`, `q` and `h` with `m − q h ≠ 0` and all real `v`, `ve`, `F` and `w`,
`(m − q h) w + q h (v − ve) = m v + F h` if and only if `w` is the
[parcel velocity](parcel-velocity.md) `v_h = (m v + F h − q h (v − ve)) / (m − q h)`.
