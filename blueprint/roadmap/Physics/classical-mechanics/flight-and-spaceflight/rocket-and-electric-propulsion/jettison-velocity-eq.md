# A zero-relative-velocity jettison leaves the velocity unchanged

For reals `m₁`, `m₂`, `v` and `w` with `m₂ ≠ 0`: a vehicle of mass `m₁` at velocity `v` drops the
mass `m₁ − m₂`, which keeps the velocity `v`, and the remaining mass `m₂` moves at `w`. Then
momentum is conserved, `m₂ w + (m₁ − m₂) v = m₁ v`, if and only if `w = v`. A jettison in the model
has `0 < m₂ ≤ m₁`.
