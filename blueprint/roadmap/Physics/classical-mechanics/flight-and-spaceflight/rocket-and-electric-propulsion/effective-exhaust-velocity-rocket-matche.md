# A pressure-matched rocket has effective exhaust velocity v_e

With no inlet stream and no capture area (`ṁ_in = 0`, `A_in = 0`), the steady mass balance
`ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, `ṁ_f > 0` and `p_e = p_a`, for all real `v_in`, `p_in`, `v_e`, `A_e` and `g0`,
the [effective exhaust velocity](effective-exhaust-velocity.md) of the
[separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) is `v_e`, and its
[specific impulse](specific-impulse.md) is `v_e / g0` (`0` at `g0 = 0`, by the convention
`x / 0 = 0`), as for the [variable-mass law](specific-impulse-eq-of-law.md).
