# Thrust of a rocket

With no inlet stream and no capture area (`ṁ_in = 0`, `A_in = 0`) and the steady mass balance
`ṁ_out = ṁ_in + ṁ_f`, for all real `v_in`, `p_in`, `v_e`, `p_e`, `A_e` and `p_a`, the
[separate-flow thrust](separate-flow-thrust.md) is `T = ṁ_f v_e + (p_e − p_a) A_e`: the exhaust
carries the propellant flow.
