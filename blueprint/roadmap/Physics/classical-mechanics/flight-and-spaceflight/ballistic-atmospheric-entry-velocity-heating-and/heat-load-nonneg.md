# The heat load of an integrable history is nonnegative

Let `C` be a [stagnation-point heating correlation](stagnation-heating-correlation.md), `t_f ≥ 0`,
and `ρ`, `r_n`, `v` real functions of time whose values lie in the validity box of `C` at every
`t ∈ [0, t_f]`, with the flux `t ↦ k_SG √(ρ(t) / r_n(t)) v(t)³` integrable on `[0, t_f]`. Then
the [heat load](heat-load.md) of this history over `[0, t_f]` is nonnegative, since the coefficient
of a correlation satisfies `k_SG ≥ 0`.
