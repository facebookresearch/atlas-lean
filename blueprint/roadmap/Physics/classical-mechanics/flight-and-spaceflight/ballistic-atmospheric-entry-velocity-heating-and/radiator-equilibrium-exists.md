# A gray radiator has a nonnegative equilibrium temperature

A gray radiator of emissivity `ε` that absorbs the flux `q_abs` per unit area and exchanges
thermal radiation with large surroundings at absolute temperature `T_env` emits `ε σ T⁴` and
absorbs `ε σ T_env⁴`, so it is in equilibrium at the temperature `T` when

`ε σ T⁴ = q_abs + ε σ T_env⁴`.

If `0 < ε ≤ 1`, `σ > 0`, `q_abs ≥ 0` and `T_env ≥ 0`, such a `T ≥ 0` exists. This is the
[existence of a nonnegative fourth-power equilibrium](../../../../EarthScience/atmosphere-and-climate/planetary-energy-balance/planetary-energy-balance-equilibrium-exi.md)
with coefficient `ε σ`, albedo `0` and flux `4 (q_abs + ε σ T_env⁴)`. The bounds `ε ≤ 1` and
`T_env ≥ 0` restrict the statement to a gray surface and an absolute temperature.
