# Altitude derivative of the ballistic deceleration

Let `H ≠ 0`, `β > 0` and `sin γ > 0`, a downward path, and let `ρ₀`, `h_e`, `v_e` and `h` be
real. Then the [drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) `a` has at `h` the derivative

`a'(h) = a(h) (2 K exp(−h / H) − 1) / H`,

with `K = ρ₀ H / (2 β sin γ)` the [ballistic entry constant](ballistic-k.md).
