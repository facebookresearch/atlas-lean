# Ballistic entry constant

For real numbers `ρ₀`, `H`, `β` and `γ`, the ballistic entry constant is

`K = ρ₀ H / (2 β sin γ)`,

for the reference density `ρ₀` and scale height `H` of an
[exponential atmosphere](exponential-density.md), the ballistic coefficient `β` and the downward
flight-path angle `γ`. It is dimensionless. In the physical reading `ρ₀, H, β > 0` and
`sin γ > 0`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `K = 0` when `β sin γ = 0`.
