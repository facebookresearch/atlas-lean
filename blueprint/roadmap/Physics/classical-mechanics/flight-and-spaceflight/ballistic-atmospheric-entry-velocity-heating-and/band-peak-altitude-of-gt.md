# Above a nonempty band the selected peak is the entry altitude

For all real `ρ₀`, `H`, `β` and `γ`, and `h_min < h_e`: if the
[candidate peak altitude](peak-deceleration-altitude.md) satisfies `h* > h_e`, the
[peak altitude selected on the band](band-peak-altitude.md) `[h_min, h_e]` is the entry altitude
`h_e`.
