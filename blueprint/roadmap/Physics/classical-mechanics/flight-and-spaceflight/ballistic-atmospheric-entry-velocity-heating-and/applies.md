# Validity box of a stagnation-point heating correlation

A [stagnation-point heating correlation](stagnation-heating-correlation.md) with box limits
`ρ_max`, `r_min` and `v_max` applies at the real free-stream density `ρ`, nose radius `r_n` and
speed `v` when the point lies in its validity box:

`0 ≤ ρ ≤ ρ_max`, `r_min ≤ r_n` and `0 ≤ v ≤ v_max`.
