# The selected altitude maximizes the deceleration on the band

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_min < h_e`.
Then the [peak altitude selected on the band](band-peak-altitude.md) `[h_min, h_e]` lies in the
band, and at every altitude `h` of the band the [drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) is at
most its value there.
