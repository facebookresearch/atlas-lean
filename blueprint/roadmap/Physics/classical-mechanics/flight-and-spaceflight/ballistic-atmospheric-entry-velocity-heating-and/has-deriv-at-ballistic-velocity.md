# The ballistic profile solves the altitude form of the drag equation

Let `H ≠ 0`, `β > 0` and `sin γ > 0`, a downward path, and let `ρ₀`, `h_e`, `v_e` and `h` be
real. Then the [finite-entry ballistic velocity profile](ballistic-velocity.md) `v` has at `h`
the derivative

`v'(h) = ρ(h) v(h) / (2 β sin γ)`,

with `ρ(h) = ρ₀ exp(−h / H)` the [exponential density](exponential-density.md): the altitude form
`dv/dh = ρ(h) v / (2 β sin γ)` of the equations of motion.
