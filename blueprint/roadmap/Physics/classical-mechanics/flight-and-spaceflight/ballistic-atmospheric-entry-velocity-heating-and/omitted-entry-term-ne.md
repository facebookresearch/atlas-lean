# The infinite-altitude normalization misses the finite entry speed

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e ≠ 0`, and let `h_e` be
real. Dropping the term `exp(−h_e / H)` from the exponent of the
[finite-entry ballistic velocity profile](ballistic-velocity.md), as in the normalization at
infinite altitude, gives at the entry altitude

`v_e exp(−K exp(−h_e / H)) ≠ v_e`,

with `K = ρ₀ H / (2 β sin γ)` the [ballistic entry constant](ballistic-k.md): the truncated
profile does not meet the entry condition `v(h_e) = v_e`.
