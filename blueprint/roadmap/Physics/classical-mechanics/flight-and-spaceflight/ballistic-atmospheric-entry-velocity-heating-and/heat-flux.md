# Stagnation-point heat flux inside the validity box

For a [stagnation-point heating correlation](stagnation-heating-correlation.md) with coefficient
`k_SG` and a point `(ρ, r_n, v)` where it [applies](applies.md), the stagnation-point heat flux
per unit area is

`q = k_SG √(ρ / r_n) v³`.

It is defined only at points of the validity box, where `ρ ≥ 0` and `r_n ≥ r_min > 0`, so the
quotient and the square root are taken in their usual domain.
