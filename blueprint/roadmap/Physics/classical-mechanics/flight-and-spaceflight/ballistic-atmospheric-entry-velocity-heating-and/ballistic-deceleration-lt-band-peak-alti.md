# The selected altitude is the only maximizer on the band

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_min < h_e`.
Then every altitude `h` of the band `[h_min, h_e]` other than the
[peak altitude selected on the band](band-peak-altitude.md) has a strictly smaller
[drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) than that altitude.
