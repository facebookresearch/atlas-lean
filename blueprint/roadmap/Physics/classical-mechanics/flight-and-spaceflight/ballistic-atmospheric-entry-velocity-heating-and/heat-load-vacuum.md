# In vacuum the heat load is zero

Let `C` be a [stagnation-point heating correlation](stagnation-heating-correlation.md), `t_f ≥ 0`,
and `ρ`, `r_n`, `v` real functions of time whose values lie in the validity box of `C` at every
`t ∈ [0, t_f]`. If the free-stream density vanishes on `[0, t_f]`, `ρ(t) = 0`, then the
[heat load](heat-load.md) over `[0, t_f]` is `0`. No integrability hypothesis is needed.
