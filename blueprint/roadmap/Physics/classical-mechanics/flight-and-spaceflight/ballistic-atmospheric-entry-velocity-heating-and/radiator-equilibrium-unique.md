# The nonnegative radiator equilibrium temperature is unique

Let `0 < ε ≤ 1`, `σ > 0` and `T_env ≥ 0`, and let `q_abs` be real. If two temperatures
`T₁ ≥ 0` and `T₂ ≥ 0` both satisfy the gray-radiator balance

`ε σ T⁴ = q_abs + ε σ T_env⁴`,

then `T₁ = T₂`. This is the
[uniqueness of a nonnegative fourth-power equilibrium](../../../../EarthScience/atmosphere-and-climate/planetary-energy-balance/planetary-energy-balance-equilibrium-uni.md)
with coefficient `ε σ`. The bounds `ε ≤ 1` and `T_env ≥ 0` restrict the statement to a gray
surface and an absolute temperature.
