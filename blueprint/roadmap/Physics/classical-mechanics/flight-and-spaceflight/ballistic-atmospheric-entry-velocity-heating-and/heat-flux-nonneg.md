# The stagnation-point heat flux is nonnegative

For a [stagnation-point heating correlation](stagnation-heating-correlation.md) and a point
`(ρ, r_n, v)` where it [applies](applies.md), the [heat flux](heat-flux.md)
`q = k_SG √(ρ / r_n) v³` is nonnegative: the coefficient of a correlation satisfies `k_SG ≥ 0`, and
`v ≥ 0` in the box.
