# Candidate peak-deceleration altitude

For real numbers `ρ₀`, `H`, `β` and `γ`, the candidate peak-deceleration altitude is

`h* = H log(2 K)`,

with `K = ρ₀ H / (2 β sin γ)` the [ballistic entry constant](ballistic-k.md); it does not depend
on the entry state. For `ρ₀, H, β, v_e > 0` and `sin γ > 0`, which give `K > 0`, it is the only
[stationary point](deriv-ballistic-deceleration-eq-zero-iff.md) and the
[strict global maximum](ballistic-deceleration-lt-peak.md) of the
[drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md). For `H > 0` and `0 < 2 K ≤ 1` it lies at or below
`h = 0`. With the conventions `log 0 = 0` and `log x = log |x|`, `h* = 0` when `K = 0` and
`h* = H log |2 K|` when `K < 0`.
