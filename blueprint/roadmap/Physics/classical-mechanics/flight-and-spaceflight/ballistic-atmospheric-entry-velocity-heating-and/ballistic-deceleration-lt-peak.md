# The candidate peak altitude is the strict global maximum

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_e` be real.
Then for every altitude `h ≠ h*` the [drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) satisfies
`a(h) < a(h*)`, where `h* = H log(2 K)` is the
[candidate peak altitude](peak-deceleration-altitude.md): `h*` is the strict global maximum of the
deceleration over all real altitudes.
