# Stagnation-point heating correlation with a validity box

A stagnation-point heating correlation of Sutton–Graves form consists of a citation, which records
provenance and is not an applicability condition, a coefficient `k_SG ≥ 0`, and a validity box
given by a largest free-stream density `ρ_max ≥ 0`, a smallest nose radius `r_min > 0` and a
largest speed `v_max ≥ 0`: the box is `0 ≤ ρ ≤ ρ_max`, `r_min ≤ r_n`, `0 ≤ v ≤ v_max`, and it is
nonempty. Its formula

`q = k_SG √(ρ / r_n) v³`

is the convective heat flux per unit area at the stagnation point of a blunt nose of radius
`r_n` moving at speed `v` through free-stream density `ρ`. In SI units `k_SG` is in
`kg^(1/2) / m` for fluxes in `W / m²`. The coefficient and the box are calibration evidence
supplied with the correlation; nothing asserts that the formula is physically adequate inside or
outside the box.
