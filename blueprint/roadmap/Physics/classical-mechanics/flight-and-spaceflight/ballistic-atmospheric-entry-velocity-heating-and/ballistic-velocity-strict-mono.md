# The ballistic speed increases strictly with altitude

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_e` be
real. Then the [finite-entry ballistic velocity profile](ballistic-velocity.md)
`h ↦ v(h) = v_e exp(K (exp(−h_e / H) − exp(−h / H)))` is strictly increasing on all of `ℝ`, so
the speed falls as the vehicle descends.
