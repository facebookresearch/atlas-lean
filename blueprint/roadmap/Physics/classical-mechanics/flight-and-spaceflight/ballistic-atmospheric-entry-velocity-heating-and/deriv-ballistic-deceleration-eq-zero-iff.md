# The deceleration is stationary exactly at the candidate peak

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_e` and `h`
be real. Then the derivative of the [drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) vanishes at `h`
exactly when

`h = h* = H log(2 K)`,

the [candidate peak altitude](peak-deceleration-altitude.md), with `K = ρ₀ H / (2 β sin γ) > 0`.
