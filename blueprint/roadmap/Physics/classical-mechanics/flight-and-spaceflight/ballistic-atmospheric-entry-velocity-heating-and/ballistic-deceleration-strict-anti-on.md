# Above the peak altitude the deceleration decreases with altitude

Let `ρ₀ > 0`, `H > 0`, `β > 0`, `sin γ > 0` (a downward path) and `v_e > 0`, and let `h_e` be real.
Then the [drag deceleration](ballistic-deceleration.md) is strictly decreasing in altitude on the
half-line `h ≥ h*` above the [candidate peak altitude](peak-deceleration-altitude.md)
`h* = H log(2 K)`: as the vehicle descends toward `h*`, the deceleration grows.
