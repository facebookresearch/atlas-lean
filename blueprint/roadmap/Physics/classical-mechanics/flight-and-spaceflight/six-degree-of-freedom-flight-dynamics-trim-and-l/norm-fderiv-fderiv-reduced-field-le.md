# Bounds on the second derivatives of the reduced field on a box

Let `r` be real and let `y ∈ ℝ⁴` have every coordinate at most `r` in absolute value (so
`r ≥ 0`). Then the [second derivatives](fderiv-fderiv-reduced-field-apply.md) of the four
components of the [reduced field](reduced-field.md) at `y` have operator norms, for the sup norm
on `ℝ⁴`, at most

`6 r`, `0`, `0` and `r`

respectively.
