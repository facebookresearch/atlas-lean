# The Lyapunov matrix is block diagonal

The [Lyapunov matrix](longitudinal-lyapunov-matrix.md) `P` is the direct sum of the translational
block `diag(1/2, 1/2)` on the coordinates `(x, z)` and the pitch block `[[3/2, 1/2], [1/2, 1/2]]`
on `(θ, q)`, with the same [block split](longitudinal-jacobian-eq-from-blocks.md) as the
[longitudinal Jacobian](longitudinal-jacobian.md).
