# Lift force of the longitudinal fixture

For real `x` and `z`, the lift at the velocity `(1 + x, z, 0)`, with unit air density and
reference area, is

`L = (V C_L / 2) (−z, 1 + x, 0)`,

with `V = √((1 + x)² + z²)` the [flight speed](flight-speed.md) and `C_L = 2` the
[lift coefficient](lift-coeff.md): it has magnitude `½ V² C_L` and points along the velocity
turned a quarter turn from forward toward up. Written without division, it is defined, and `0`,
at `V = 0`. It is given in inertial components and does not depend on the attitude.
