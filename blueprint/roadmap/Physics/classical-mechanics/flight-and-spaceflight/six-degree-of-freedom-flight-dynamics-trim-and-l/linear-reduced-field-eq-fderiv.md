# The reduced linear plant is the derivative of the field at the trim

For every state `e ∈ ℝ⁴`, the coordinates of the [reduced linear plant](linear-reduced-field.md)
at `e` are `Df(0) e`, the derivative at the trim of the [reduced field](reduced-field.md) `f`
applied to the coordinates of `e`: `A e = Df(0) e`, with `A` the
[longitudinal Jacobian](longitudinal-jacobian.md).
