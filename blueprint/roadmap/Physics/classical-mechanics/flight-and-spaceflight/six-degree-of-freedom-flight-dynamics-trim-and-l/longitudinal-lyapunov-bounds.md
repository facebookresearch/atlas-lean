# Quadratic comparison bounds for the Lyapunov function

For every state `e ∈ ℝ⁴`, with `‖e‖` the Euclidean norm, the
[quadratic Lyapunov function](longitudinal-lyapunov.md) satisfies

`(1/4) ‖e‖² ≤ V(e) ≤ 2 ‖e‖²`.

The eigenvalues of the [Lyapunov matrix](longitudinal-lyapunov-matrix.md) are `1/2` (twice) and
`1 ± 1/√2`, all in `[1/4, 2]`.
