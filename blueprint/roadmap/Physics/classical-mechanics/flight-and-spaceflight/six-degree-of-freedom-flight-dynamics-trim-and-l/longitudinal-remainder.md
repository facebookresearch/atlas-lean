# Nonlinear remainder of the reduced field about the trim

In the state order `(x, z, θ, q)`, the nonlinear remainder of the reduced longitudinal field about
the trim is

`N(x, z, θ, q) = (−x³, 0, 0, θ − sin θ)`,

so that the [reduced field](reduced-field.md) is its linear part plus `N`
([decomposition](reduced-field-eq-mul-vec-add-remainder.md)).
