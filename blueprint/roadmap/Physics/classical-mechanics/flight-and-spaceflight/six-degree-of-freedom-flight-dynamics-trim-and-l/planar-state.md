# Longitudinal state of a planar motion

For real functions `X₁`, `X₂` and `θ` of time, the longitudinal state at time `t` of the
[planar motion](planar-motion.md) is

`(x, z, θ, q) = (X₁'(t) − 1, X₂'(t), θ(t), θ'(t))`:

the deviation of the forward velocity from the trim speed `1`, the upward velocity, the pitch
angle and the pitch rate. Time derivatives are total, taken as `0` at a time where the function is
not differentiable.
