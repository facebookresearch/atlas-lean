# With C² attitude and trajectory the body velocity is differentiable

Consider a rigid motion in three dimensions with centre-of-mass coordinates `X(t) ∈ ℝ³` and
orientation `R(t) ∈ SO(3)`. Let `U` be an open set of times on which every entry of `R` and every
component of `X` is twice continuously differentiable. Then at every `t ∈ U` the
[body velocity](body-velocity.md) `v_B = Rᵀ X'` is differentiable, so its time derivative at `t`
is a genuine derivative.
