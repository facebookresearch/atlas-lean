# In the ball the orbital derivative is negative exactly off the trim

Let `e ∈ ℝ⁴` have Euclidean norm `‖e‖ ≤ 1/100`. Then the
[orbital derivative](fderiv-longitudinal-lyapunov-nonlinear-r.md) `DV(e) f(e)` of the
[Lyapunov function](longitudinal-lyapunov.md) along the
[nonlinear reduced field](nonlinear-reduced-field.md) is negative exactly when `e ≠ 0`; at the
trim it is `0`.
