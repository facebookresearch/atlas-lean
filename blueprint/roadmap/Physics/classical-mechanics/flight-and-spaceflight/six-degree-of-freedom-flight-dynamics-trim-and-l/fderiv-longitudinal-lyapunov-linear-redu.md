# Orbital derivative of the Lyapunov function along the linear plant

For every state `e ∈ ℝ⁴`, with `‖e‖` the Euclidean norm, the derivative of the
[quadratic Lyapunov function](longitudinal-lyapunov.md) along the
[reduced linear plant](linear-reduced-field.md) is

`DV(e) (A e) = eᵀ (Aᵀ P + P A) e = −‖e‖²`:

zero at the trim `e = 0` and negative at every other state.
