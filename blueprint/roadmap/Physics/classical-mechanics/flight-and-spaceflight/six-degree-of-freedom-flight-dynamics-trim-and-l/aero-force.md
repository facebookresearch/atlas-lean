# Aerodynamic force of the longitudinal fixture

For real `x` and `z`, the aerodynamic force of the fixture is the sum, in inertial components, of
the [lift](lift-force.md) and the [drag](drag-force.md):

`F_aero = (V C_L / 2) (−z, 1 + x, 0) − (V C_D / 2) (1 + x, z, 0)`,

with `V = √((1 + x)² + z²)`, `C_L = 2` and `C_D = 3/10`.
