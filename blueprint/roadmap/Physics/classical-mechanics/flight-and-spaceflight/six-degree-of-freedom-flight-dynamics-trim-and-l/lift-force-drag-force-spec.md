# Lift is perpendicular to the velocity and drag opposes it

For real `x` and `z`, write `v = (1 + x, z, 0)` and `V = √((1 + x)² + z²)` for the
[flight speed](flight-speed.md). Then the [lift](lift-force.md) `L` and the
[drag](drag-force.md) `D` satisfy

- `L · v = 0`: the lift is perpendicular to `v`;
- `L · L = (V²)²`: the lift has magnitude `V²`;
- `L · (−z, 1 + x, 0) ≥ 0`: the lift points to the up side of `v`;
- `D = −(3 V / 20) v`: the drag points against `v`;
- `D · D = (3 V² / 20)²`: the drag has magnitude `3 V² / 20`.
