# Second derivatives of the reduced field

At every state `y = (y₀, y₁, y₂, y₃) ∈ ℝ⁴` and for all directions `h` and `k` of `ℝ⁴`, indexed
from `0`, the second derivatives of the components of the [reduced field](reduced-field.md)
`f(x, z, θ, q) = (−x − x³, −z, q, −2 q − sin θ)`, as bilinear forms, are

- `−6 y₀ h₀ k₀` for the component `−x − x³`;
- `0` for the components `−z` and `q`;
- `sin(y₂) h₂ k₂` for the component `−2 q − sin θ`.
