# Drag polar of the longitudinal fixture

In the synthetic longitudinal fixture, in nondimensional units with mass, pitch inertia, gravity,
trim speed, air density and reference area `1`, the drag coefficient is given by the parabolic polar

`C_D = 1/10 + C_L² / 20`,

with the [lift coefficient](lift-coeff.md) `C_L = 2`, so [`C_D = 3/10`](drag-coeff-eq.md).
