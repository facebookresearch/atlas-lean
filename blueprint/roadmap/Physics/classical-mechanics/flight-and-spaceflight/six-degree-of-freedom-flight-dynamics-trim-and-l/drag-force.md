# Drag force of the longitudinal fixture

For real `x` and `z`, the drag at the velocity `(1 + x, z, 0)`, with unit air density and
reference area, is

`D = −(V C_D / 2) (1 + x, z, 0)`,

with `V = √((1 + x)² + z²)` the [flight speed](flight-speed.md) and `C_D` the
[drag polar](drag-coeff.md): it has magnitude `½ V² C_D` and points against the velocity. Written
without division, it is defined, and `0`, at `V = 0`. It is given in inertial components and does
not depend on the attitude.
