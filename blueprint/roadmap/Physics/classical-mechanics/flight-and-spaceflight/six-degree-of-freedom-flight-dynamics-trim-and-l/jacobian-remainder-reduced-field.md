# The first-order remainder of the reduced field at the trim

For every deviation `δ = (x, z, θ, q) ∈ ℝ⁴`, the first-order remainder of the
[reduced field](reduced-field.md) `f` at the trim is

`f(0 + δ) − f(0) − Df(0) δ = (−x³, 0, 0, θ − sin θ)`,

the [nonlinear remainder](longitudinal-remainder.md) `N(δ)`; here `f(0) = 0` and `Df(0) δ = A δ`
with `A` the [longitudinal Jacobian](longitudinal-jacobian.md).
