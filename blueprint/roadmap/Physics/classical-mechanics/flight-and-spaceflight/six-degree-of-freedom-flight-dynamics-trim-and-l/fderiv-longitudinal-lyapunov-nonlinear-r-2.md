# On the ball of radius 1/100 the orbital derivative is at most −V/4

Let `e = (x, z, θ, q) ∈ ℝ⁴` have Euclidean norm `‖e‖ ≤ r = 1/100`. Then the
[orbital derivative](fderiv-longitudinal-lyapunov-nonlinear-r.md) of the
[Lyapunov function](longitudinal-lyapunov.md) along the
[nonlinear reduced field](nonlinear-reduced-field.md) satisfies

`DV(e) f(e) ≤ −(1/2) ‖e‖² ≤ −V(e) / 4`.

Indeed the cubic sine bound `|θ − sin θ| ≤ (r / 6) ‖e‖²` and `|θ + q| ≤ 2 r` make the term
`(θ + q) (θ − sin θ)` at most `‖e‖² / 30000`.
