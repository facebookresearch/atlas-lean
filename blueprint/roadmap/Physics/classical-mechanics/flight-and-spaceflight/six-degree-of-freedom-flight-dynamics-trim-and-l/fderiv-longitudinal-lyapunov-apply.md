# Derivative of the quadratic Lyapunov function

For all states `e` and `h` of `ℝ⁴`, the derivative at `e` of the
[quadratic Lyapunov function](longitudinal-lyapunov.md) `V(e) = eᵀ P e`, applied to `h`, is

`DV(e) h = 2 hᵀ P e`,

the [Lyapunov matrix](longitudinal-lyapunov-matrix.md) `P` being symmetric.
