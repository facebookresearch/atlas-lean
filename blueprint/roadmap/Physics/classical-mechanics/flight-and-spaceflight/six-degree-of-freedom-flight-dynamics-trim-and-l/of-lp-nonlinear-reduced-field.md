# The nonlinear field is the linear plant plus the remainder

For every state `e = (x, z, θ, q) ∈ ℝ⁴`, the coordinates of the
[nonlinear reduced field](nonlinear-reduced-field.md) are those of the
[reduced linear plant](linear-reduced-field.md) plus the
[nonlinear remainder](longitudinal-remainder.md):

`f(e) = A e + (−x³, 0, 0, θ − sin θ)`.
