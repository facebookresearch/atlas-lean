# Flight speed of the longitudinal fixture

For real `x` and `z`, the flight speed at the velocity `(1 + x, z)` of the forward/up plane is

`V = √((1 + x)² + z²)`,

the Euclidean length of that velocity. The state coordinates `x` and `z` are the deviations of the
forward and upward velocity from the trim velocity `(1, 0)`.
