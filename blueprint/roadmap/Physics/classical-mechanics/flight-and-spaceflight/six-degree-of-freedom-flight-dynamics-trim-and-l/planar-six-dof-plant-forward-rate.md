# A planar plant solution moves forward at rate at least 99/100

Let `B` be any mass distribution, `g` any gravity vector and `X₁`, `X₂`, `θ` real functions of
time. If the [six-degree-of-freedom plant](six-dof-plant.md) of the
[planar motion](planar-motion.md) of `B`, with the [longitudinal maps](longitudinal-maps.md) and
the [longitudinal state](planar-state.md), holds at a time `t`, then the state at `t` lies in the
[actuator box](actuator-box.md), so the forward position rate is

`X₁'(t) = 1 + x ≥ 99/100`.

The world position is not part of the reduced state, and the
[longitudinal Jacobian](longitudinal-jacobian.md) linearizes the reduced field only.
