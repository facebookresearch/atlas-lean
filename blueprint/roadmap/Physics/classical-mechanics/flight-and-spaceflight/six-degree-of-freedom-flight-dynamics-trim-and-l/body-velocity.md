# Body components of the centre-of-mass velocity

Consider a rigid motion in three dimensions: a mass distribution with mass `m`, the inertial
coordinates `X(t) ∈ ℝ³` of its centre-of-mass trajectory and its orientation `R(t) ∈ SO(3)`,
with the body-to-inertial convention `v_I = R v_B`. Its body velocity at time `t` is

`v_B(t) = R(t)ᵀ X'(t)`,

the [body components](../../rigid-bodies-and-mechanisms/body-fixed-and-inertial-frames/inertial-to-body-vec.md)
of the velocity `X'(t)` of the centre of mass. Time derivatives are total: at a time where `X` is
not differentiable, `X'(t)` is taken to be `0`.
