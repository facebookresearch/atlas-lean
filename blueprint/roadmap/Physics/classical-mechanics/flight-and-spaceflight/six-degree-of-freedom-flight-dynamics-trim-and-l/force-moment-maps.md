# Force and moment maps on an admitted state domain

For a set `S` of states, a pair of force and moment maps consists of an admitted domain
`D ⊆ S` and two maps from `D` to `ℝ³`: the body force and the body moment about the centre of
mass at an admitted state. They are explicit model inputs, aerodynamic and propulsive
coefficient maps for example, and are defined only on `D` (a polar validity region, say), so
they are never evaluated outside it.
