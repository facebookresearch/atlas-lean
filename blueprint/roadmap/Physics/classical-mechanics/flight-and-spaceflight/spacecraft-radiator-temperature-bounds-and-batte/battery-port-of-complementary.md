# Battery port with complementary charge and discharge

Let $P_c \ge 0$ and $P_d \ge 0$ with $P_c P_d = 0$, and real $\eta_c$, $\eta_d$ (real division, with $x/0 = 0$, so the identities also hold for $\eta_d = 0$). Writing $P = P_c - P_d$ for the terminal power,

$$\eta_c P_c - \frac{P_d}{\eta_d} = \eta_c \max(P, 0) - \frac{\max(-P, 0)}{\eta_d}$$

and

$$(1 - \eta_c)\, P_c + \left(\frac{1}{\eta_d} - 1\right) P_d = (1 - \eta_c) \max(P, 0) + \left(\frac{1}{\eta_d} - 1\right) \max(-P, 0).$$
