# Battery heat keeps the lower inward sign of the radiator box

If $P_c \ge 0$, $P_d \ge 0$, $\eta_c, \eta_d \in (0, 1]$ and $0 \le Q_{\mathrm{env}} - K_{\mathrm{rad}} T_{\min}^4$, then

$$0 \le Q_{\mathrm{env}} + H - K_{\mathrm{rad}} T_{\min}^4, \qquad H = (1 - \eta_c)\, P_c + \left(\frac{1}{\eta_d} - 1\right) P_d.$$
