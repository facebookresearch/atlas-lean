# Clipped radiator rate agrees with the radiator rate on the box

For real $C$, $K_{\mathrm{rad}}$, $Q$, $T_{\min}$, $T_{\max}$ and $T \in [T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$,

$$r(\min(\max(T, T_{\min}), T_{\max})) = r(T), \qquad r(T) = \frac{Q - K_{\mathrm{rad}} T^4}{C}.$$

Real division by zero is $0$, so at zero heat capacity $C = 0$ every radiator rate is $0$ by convention.
