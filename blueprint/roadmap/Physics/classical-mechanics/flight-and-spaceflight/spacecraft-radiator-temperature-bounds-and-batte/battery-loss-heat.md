# Battery loss heat

For charge and discharge efficiencies $\eta_c, \eta_d$ and terminal charge and discharge powers $P_c, P_d$, the heat dissipated in the battery is

$$q(\eta_c, \eta_d, P_c, P_d) = (1 - \eta_c)\,P_c + \left(\frac{1}{\eta_d} - 1\right) P_d.$$

For $\eta_d = 0$ the formula takes the convention $1/0 = 0$, giving $(1 - \eta_c) P_c - P_d$.
