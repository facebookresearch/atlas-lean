# Clipped radiator rate is Lipschitz

Let $C > 0$, $K_{\mathrm{rad}} > 0$, $Q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $0 \le T_{\min} \le T_{\max}$. The clipped radiator rate $r_{\mathrm{clip}}(T) = (Q - K_{\mathrm{rad}}\operatorname{clip}(T)^4)/C$, with $\operatorname{clip}(T) = \min(\max(T, T_{\min}), T_{\max})$, is Lipschitz on $\mathbb{R}$ with the nonnegative constant

$$\frac{K_{\mathrm{rad}} \cdot 4 T_{\max}^3}{C}.$$
