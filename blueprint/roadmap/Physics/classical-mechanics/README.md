# Classical mechanics

Newtonian mechanics of particles, rigid bodies and simple systems, and its application to flight and
spaceflight: work, energy and potentials, harmonic, damped, forced and nonlinear oscillators, modal
analysis and random response, frames and rigid-body dynamics, statics, linkages and impacts,
Newtonian gravitation and orbits, aircraft and spacecraft motion, robot kinematics, dynamics and
contact, and elementary models of muscle power and the energy budget of exercise. Elastic
structures are under Elasticity and structural mechanics.
