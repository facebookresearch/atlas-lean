# Passive pipe flow

For a network with finitely many nodes and arcs, arc tails and heads, resistances $r_e$, arc flows $q_e$, nodal demands $d_v$ and nodal heads $h_v$, the flow is passive when it satisfies the pipe-flow equations, namely node balance $Bq = d$ for the node-arc incidence matrix $B$ and the head-loss law $-(B^{\mathsf T} h)_e = r_e q_e \lvert q_e\rvert$ on every arc, and every resistance is nonnegative, $r_e \ge 0$.
