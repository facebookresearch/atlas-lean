# Uniqueness of passive pipe-network flow

On a finite network with positive resistance coefficients $r_e > 0$, let $q_1, h_1$ and $q_2, h_2$ both solve the pipe-flow equations for the same nodal demands $d$: flow conservation $B q = d$ and the head-loss law $h(\mathrm{tail}\, e) - h(\mathrm{head}\, e) = r_e q_e |q_e|$ on every arc. Then $q_1 = q_2$.
