# Darcy and Fanning friction-factor conventions

A friction-factor convention is one of two cases: the Darcy (Moody) factor `f_D`, defined by the
pressure drop `Δp = f_D (L / D) ρ V² / 2` over a length `L` of a duct of hydraulic diameter `D`, or
the Fanning factor `f_F = f_D / 4`, defined by the wall shear stress `τ_w = f_F ρ V² / 2`.
