# For c > 0 a power-law correlation gives a positive Darcy factor

For `c > 0`, every real exponent `a`, and a [request](friction-request.md) to which the
[power-law correlation](power-law-friction.md) [applies as a Darcy factor](darcy-applies.md), the
[Darcy factor](darcy-factor.md) `c Re^a` is positive: the validity domain makes `Re` positive.
