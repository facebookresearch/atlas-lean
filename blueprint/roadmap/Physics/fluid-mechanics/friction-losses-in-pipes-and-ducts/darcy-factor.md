# Darcy factor of a correlation where it applies

For a [friction correlation](friction-correlation.md) and a [request](friction-request.md) to which
it [applies as a Darcy factor](darcy-applies.md), the Darcy factor is the correlation's formula
evaluated at the request's Reynolds number. It is defined only together with a proof that the
correlation applies, so no value is formed outside the validity domain, for another roughness or
from a Fanning record.
