# Power-law friction correlation f = c Re^a

For real `c` and `a`, a citation, a [convention](friction-convention.md), a
[duct shape](duct-shape.md), a
[hydrodynamic development](../../heat-and-mass-transport/convective-heat-transfer-correlations-and-their/flow-development.md),
a relative roughness `ε / D ≥ 0` and a nonempty validity domain of positive Reynolds numbers, the
power-law friction correlation is the [friction correlation](friction-correlation.md) with these
data and the formula `f = c Re^a` (a real power, taken at the positive Reynolds numbers of the
validity domain).
