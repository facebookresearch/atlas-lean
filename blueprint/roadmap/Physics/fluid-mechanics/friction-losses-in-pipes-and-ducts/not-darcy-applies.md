# No Darcy factor: Fanning record, other roughness or Re out of range

Let `C` be a [friction correlation](friction-correlation.md) and `r` a
[request](friction-request.md). If `C` is in the Fanning convention, or `r` has a relative roughness
different from that of `C`, or the Reynolds number of `r` is outside the validity domain of `C`,
then `C` does not [apply as a Darcy factor](darcy-applies.md) to `r`.
