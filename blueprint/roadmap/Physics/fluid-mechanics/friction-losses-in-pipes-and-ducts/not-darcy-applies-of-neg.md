# No Darcy factor for a negative relative roughness

A [request](friction-request.md) with a negative relative roughness gets no Darcy factor: no
[friction correlation](friction-correlation.md) [applies](darcy-applies.md) to it, since every
request that a correlation applies to has
[nonnegative roughness](relative-roughness-nonneg-of-darcy-appli.md).
