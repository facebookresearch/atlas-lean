# Darcy factor of a pressure drop along the flow

For an oriented pressure drop `Δp`, a duct length `L > 0`, a hydraulic diameter `D > 0`, a density
`ρ > 0` and a signed mean velocity `V` with the drop along the flow, `Δp V > 0`, the Darcy factor of
the drop is the formula `f = 2 D Δp / (ρ V |V| L)`, modelled on the oriented Darcy–Weisbach relation
`Δp = f (L / D) ρ V |V| / 2`. The four conditions are part of the definition, so no factor is
defined at zero flow, for a degenerate duct or fluid, or for a drop against the flow, which would
describe energy generation rather than friction. Under these four conditions the factor is
[positive](darcy-factor-of-drop-pos.md). For `f, L, D, A, ρ > 0` and `V ≠ 0`, the factor of the drop
`c Q |Q|` at `Q = A V` with the [loss coefficient](darcy-coefficient.md) `c = f L ρ / (2 D A²)`
[gives back `f`](darcy-factor-of-drop-friction-pressure-d.md).
