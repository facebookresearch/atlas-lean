# Guarded empirical friction-factor correlation

A friction-factor correlation consists of: a citation (provenance only); a
[convention](friction-convention.md), Darcy or Fanning; the [duct shape](duct-shape.md) and
[hydrodynamic development](../../heat-and-mass-transport/convective-heat-transfer-correlations-and-their/flow-development.md)
it was calibrated for; the relative roughness `ε / D` it was calibrated for, which must be
nonnegative; a validity domain of Reynolds numbers, a set that must be nonempty and contain only
positive numbers; and a formula that assigns a real factor to each Reynolds number of the validity
domain and is not defined outside it. A correlation that depends on the roughness, such as a
Colebrook-type formula, is recorded once per relative roughness, with that roughness in its formula.
The data are supplied, not validated.
