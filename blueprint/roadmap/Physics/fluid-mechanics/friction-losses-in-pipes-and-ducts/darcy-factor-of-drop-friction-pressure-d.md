# Inversion: the Darcy drop at Q = A V gives back the factor f

Let `f`, `L`, `D`, `A` and `ρ` be positive and `V ≠ 0`. The [Darcy factor](darcy-factor-of-drop.md)
of the [pressure drop](friction-pressure-drop.md) `c Q |Q|` at `Q = A V`, with the
[loss coefficient](darcy-coefficient.md) `c = f L ρ / (2 D A²)`, is `f`, in both flow directions.
The drop points along `V` ([direction](friction-pressure-drop-mul-velocity-pos.md)), so the factor
is defined.
