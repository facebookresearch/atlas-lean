# Oriented friction pressure drop, quadratic in the flow

For a loss coefficient `c` and a volumetric flow `Q` (real numbers), the friction pressure drop is
`Δp = c Q |Q|`, from the inlet face to the outlet face, with `Q` positive from the inlet to the
outlet. For the Darcy–Weisbach model `c` is the [loss coefficient](darcy-coefficient.md)
`f L ρ / (2 D A²)`.
