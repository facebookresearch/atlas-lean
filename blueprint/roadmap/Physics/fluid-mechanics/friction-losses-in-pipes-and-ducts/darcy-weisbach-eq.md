# Darcy–Weisbach pressure drop in terms of the volumetric flow

Let `f`, `L`, `D`, `A`, `ρ` and `Q` be real with `D ≠ 0` and `A > 0`. With the mean velocity
`V = Q / A`, the Darcy–Weisbach pressure drop `f (L / D) ρ V |V| / 2` equals the
[pressure drop](friction-pressure-drop.md) `c Q |Q|` with the
[loss coefficient](darcy-coefficient.md) `c = f L ρ / (2 D A²)`.
