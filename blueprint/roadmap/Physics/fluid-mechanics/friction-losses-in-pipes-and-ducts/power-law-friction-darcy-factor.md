# Where a power-law correlation applies, its Darcy factor is c Re^a

For a [power-law correlation](power-law-friction.md) with coefficient `c` and exponent `a`, and a
[request](friction-request.md) to which it [applies as a Darcy factor](darcy-applies.md), the
[Darcy factor](darcy-factor.md) is `c Re^a`, with `Re` the request's Reynolds number.
