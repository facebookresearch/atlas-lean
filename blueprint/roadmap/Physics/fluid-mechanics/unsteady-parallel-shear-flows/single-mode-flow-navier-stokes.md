# The single-mode shear flow solves the Navier–Stokes equations

For `ρ > 0` and all real `A`, `L`, `μ`, `λ` and every pressure `p(t)`, the parallel shear flow of
density `ρ` with velocity `(U(t, y), 0, 0)`, where `U` is the
[single-mode profile](single-mode-profile.md) `U` with `ν = μ / ρ`, satisfies the Navier–Stokes
equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance, and the Newtonian stress with
the uniform pressure `p(t)`, the shear viscosity `μ` and the second viscosity `λ`).
