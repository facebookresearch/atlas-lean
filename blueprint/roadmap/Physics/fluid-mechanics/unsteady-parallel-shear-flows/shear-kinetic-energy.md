# Kinetic energy per unit wall area of a shear layer

For a density `ρ`, a layer thickness `L` and a real function `U(t, y)`, the kinetic energy per unit
wall area at time `t` is `K(t) = ∫_0^L ρ U(t, y)² / 2 dy`. For `L < 0` the oriented integral is
signed and is not the energy of a layer, and when `U(t, ·)²` is not integrable on the interval the
integral is `0` by convention. The physical reading is `ρ > 0` and `L ≥ 0`.
