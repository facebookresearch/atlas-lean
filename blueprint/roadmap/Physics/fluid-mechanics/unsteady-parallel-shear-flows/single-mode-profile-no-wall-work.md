# Without the wall work the energy balance of the single mode fails

For `ρ > 0`, `μ > 0`, `L > 0`, `A ≠ 0` and `t ≥ 0`, the [kinetic energy](shear-kinetic-energy.md)
`K` of the [single-mode profile](single-mode-profile.md) `U` with `ν = μ / ρ` does not have the
derivative `-μ ∫_0^L U_y(t, y)² dy` at `t`: dropping the moving-wall term would predict a decrease,
which is false for this flow.
