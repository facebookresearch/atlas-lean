# Stokes force balance gives the terminal settling velocity

For a sphere of radius `a > 0` in a fluid of viscosity `η > 0` under a gravity magnitude `g > 0`,
with real particle and fluid densities `ρ_s` and `ρ_f`, if a velocity `v`, measured downward along
gravity, balances the Stokes drag against the buoyant weight of the sphere,
`6 π η a v = (4/3) π a³ (ρ_s - ρ_f) g`, then `v = 2 (ρ_s - ρ_f) g a² / (9 η)`.
