# The Stokes settling velocity satisfies the force balance

For a sphere radius `a > 0`, a viscosity `η > 0`, a gravity magnitude `g > 0` and real particle and
fluid densities `ρ_s` and `ρ_f`, the velocity `v = 2 (ρ_s - ρ_f) g a² / (9 η)` satisfies the
Stokes force balance `6 π η a v = (4/3) π a³ (ρ_s - ρ_f) g`.
