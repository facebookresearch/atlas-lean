# A particle lighter than the fluid rises

For a sphere radius `a > 0`, a viscosity `η > 0` and a gravity magnitude `g > 0`, a particle
lighter than the fluid, `ρ_s < ρ_f`, has negative Stokes settling velocity
`2 (ρ_s - ρ_f) g a² / (9 η) < 0`: with the velocity measured downward, along gravity, it rises.
