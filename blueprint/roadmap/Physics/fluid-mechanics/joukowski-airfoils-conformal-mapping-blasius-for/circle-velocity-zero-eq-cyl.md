# At α = 0, the circle velocity is u − iv of the cylinder flow

Let `a`, `U`, `Γ` be real and `z ≠ 0` complex, with `x = Re z` and `y = Im z`. At angle of attack
`α = 0` the [circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) is
`w(z) = U(1 − a²/z²) + Γ/(2πi z) = u(x, y) − i v(x, y)`, where `u` and `v` are the
[horizontal](cyl-u.md) and [vertical](cyl-v.md) velocities of the cylinder flow. So `w` is the
complex velocity `u − iv` of that flow, with free stream along the positive real axis and `Γ > 0`
counterclockwise. Physically `a > 0` and `‖z‖ ≥ a`; the identity holds for all real `a`, `U`, `Γ`
and every `z ≠ 0`.
