# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ > 2a, the far-field branch has modulus above ‖z‖/2

Let `a > 0` and let `z` be a complex number with `‖z‖ > 2a`. Then the
[far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md) satisfies `‖ζ(z)‖ > ‖z‖/2`. Indeed
`‖ζ(z)‖ = (‖z‖/2)‖1 + √(1 − 4a²/z²)‖`; the radicand lies in the slit plane
([slit plane](radicand-mem-slit-plane.md)), so its principal square root has positive real part and
`‖1 + √(1 − 4a²/z²)‖ > 1`.
