# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ > 2a, 1 − 4a²/z² lies in the slit plane

Let `a > 0` and let `z` be a complex number with `‖z‖ > 2a`. Then `1 − 4a²/z²` lies in the slit
plane: the complex plane with the half-line of real numbers `≤ 0` removed, where the principal
square root and logarithm are holomorphic. Indeed `‖4a²/z²‖ = 4a²/‖z‖² < 1`, so `1 − 4a²/z²` has
positive real part. It is the radicand of the [far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md).
