# 2π sin α and 2πα differ by at most π/3 times the absolute value of α³

For every real `α`, `|2π sin α − 2πα| ≤ π|α|³/3`. It follows from `|α − sin α| ≤ |α|³/6`. So the
lift coefficient `2π sin α` of the [Kutta lift](lift-coefficient-kutta.md) differs from the linear
small-angle value `2πα` by at most `π|α|³/3`.
