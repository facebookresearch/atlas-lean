# Away from the origin, the cylinder velocity field is divergence-free

For all real `U`, `a`, `Γ` and every point `(x, y)` with `x² + y² ≠ 0`, the [horizontal](cyl-u.md)
and [vertical](cyl-v.md) velocities satisfy `∂u/∂x(x, y) + ∂v/∂y(x, y) = 0`. Both partial
derivatives exist there, since `u` and `v` are rational in `x` and `y` with denominators that do not
vanish near `(x, y)`. In the physical reading `a > 0` and the flow region is `x² + y² ≥ a²`; the
identity holds at every point other than the origin, for all real `U`, `a` and `Γ`.
