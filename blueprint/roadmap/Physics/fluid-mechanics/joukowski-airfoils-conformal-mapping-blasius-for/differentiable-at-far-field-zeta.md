# For a > 0, the far-field branch is holomorphic on ‖z‖ > 2a

Let `a > 0`. At every complex `z` with `‖z‖ > 2a`, the [far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md)
`ζ(z) = (z/2)(1 + √(1 − 4a²/z²))` is complex differentiable; since this region is open, `ζ` is
holomorphic on it. There `z ≠ 0` and the radicand lies in the slit plane
([slit plane](radicand-mem-slit-plane.md)), where the principal square root is holomorphic.
