# Signed lift: the force component normal to the free stream

For a complex number `C` and a real angle `α`, the signed lift is `L = −Im(e^{iα} C)`. For a force
per unit span written as `C = F_x − i F_y`, the form of the [Blasius force](blasius-force.md), it is
`L = −F_x sin α + F_y cos α = −(sin α · Re C + cos α · Im C)`: the component of `(F_x, F_y)` along
the unit normal `(−sin α, cos α)`, which is the free-stream direction `(cos α, sin α)` turned a
quarter turn counterclockwise. At `α = 0`, `L = F_y`.
