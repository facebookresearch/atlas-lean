# The Joukowski map sends a e^(iθ) to the real point 2a cos θ

For all real `a` and `θ`, the [Joukowski map](joukowski.md) with parameter `a` sends the point
`a e^{iθ}` of the circle `‖ζ‖ = |a|` to the real number `2a cos θ`:
`J(a e^{iθ}) = a e^{iθ} + a e^{−iθ} = 2a cos θ`. As `θ` varies, the image of the circle is the
segment of the real axis from `−2|a|` to `2|a|`. For `a = 0` both sides are `0`, the left one by the
convention `x/0 = 0`, which gives `J(0) = 0`. In the airfoil model `a > 0` is the radius of the
circle and the segment from `−2a` to `2a` is a flat plate of chord `4a`; the identity holds for
every real `a`.
