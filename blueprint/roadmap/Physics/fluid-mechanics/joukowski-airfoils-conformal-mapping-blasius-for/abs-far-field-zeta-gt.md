# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ > 2a, ζ(z) lies outside the circle of radius a

Let `a > 0` and let `z` be a complex number with `‖z‖ > 2a`. Then the
[far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md) lands outside the circle of radius `a`: `‖ζ(z)‖ > a`. It
follows from `‖ζ(z)‖ > ‖z‖/2 > a` ([modulus above ‖z‖/2](norm-far-field-zeta-gt-half.md)).
