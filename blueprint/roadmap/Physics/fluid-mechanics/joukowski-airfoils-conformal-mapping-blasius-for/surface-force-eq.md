# For a > 0, the Bernoulli pressure force on the cylinder is (0, −ρUΓ)

Let `a > 0`, let `p∞`, `ρ`, `U`, `Γ` be real, and let `p : ℂ → ℝ` satisfy Bernoulli's law in the
flow region: `p(z) = p∞ + ρ(U² − ‖w(z)‖²)/2` for every `z` with `‖z‖ ≥ a`, where `w` is the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) at angle of attack `0`. Then the
[horizontal](surface-force-x.md) and [vertical](surface-force-y.md) pressure forces on the cylinder
`r = a` are `F_x = 0` and `F_y = −ρUΓ`. There is no drag; with `ρ > 0` and `U > 0`, as in the
physical reading, the force points upward exactly when `Γ < 0`, a clockwise circulation. The
identity holds for all real `p∞`, `ρ`, `U` and `Γ`.
