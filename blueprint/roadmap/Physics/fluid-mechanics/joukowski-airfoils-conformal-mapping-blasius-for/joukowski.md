# Joukowski map J(ζ) = ζ + a²/ζ

For complex numbers `a` and `ζ`, the Joukowski map with parameter `a` is `J(ζ) = ζ + a²/ζ`. It is
meant for `ζ ≠ 0`; with the convention `x/0 = 0`, its value at `ζ = 0` is `J(0) = 0`. In the airfoil
model `a` is a positive real number, the radius of the circle `‖ζ‖ = a`, which the map flattens onto
the segment of the real axis from `−2a` to `2a` ([image of the circle](joukowski-circle.md)); its
termwise derivative formula `1 − a²/ζ²` vanishes at `ζ = ±a`.
