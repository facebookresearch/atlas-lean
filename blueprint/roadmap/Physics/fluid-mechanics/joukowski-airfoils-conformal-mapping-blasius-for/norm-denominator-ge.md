# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ ≥ 4a, ‖1 − a²/ζ(z)²‖ is at least 3/4

Let `a > 0` and let `z` be a complex number with `‖z‖ ≥ 4a`, and let `ζ(z)` be the
[far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md). Then the derivative of the Joukowski map at `ζ(z)` is bounded
away from zero: `‖1 − a²/ζ(z)²‖ ≥ 3/4`. Indeed `‖ζ(z)‖ > ‖z‖/2 ≥ 2a`
([modulus above ‖z‖/2](norm-far-field-zeta-gt-half.md)), so `‖a²/ζ(z)²‖ < 1/4`. This is the
denominator of the [physical velocity](physical-velocity.md).
