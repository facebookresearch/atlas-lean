# No penetration: on x² + y² = a², the cylinder velocity is tangential

Let `U`, `a`, `Γ`, `x`, `y` be real with `a ≠ 0` and `x² + y² = a²`. Then the [horizontal](cyl-u.md)
and [vertical](cyl-v.md) velocities satisfy `x·u(x, y) + y·v(x, y) = 0`: the velocity has no radial
component, so there is no flow through the circle `r = |a|`. Indeed the circulation terms cancel and
`x u + y v = Ux(1 − a²/(x² + y²))`, which is `0` on the circle. Physically `a > 0` is the radius of
the cylinder; the identity holds for every nonzero real `a` and all real `U` and `Γ`.
