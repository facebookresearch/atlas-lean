# For a > 0, Γ = −4πaU sin α makes the circle velocity vanish at ζ = a

Let `a > 0` and let `U`, `α` be real. With the circulation `Γ = −4πaU sin α`, the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) vanishes at the trailing-edge point `ζ = a`:
`U(e^{−iα} − e^{iα}) + Γ/(2πi a) = −2iU sin α + 2iU sin α = 0`. This is the converse of the
[Kutta cancellation](kutta-cancellation.md), which derives this circulation from the vanishing.
