# Hydrostatic pressure grows with depth from its positive surface value

For a surface pressure `p₀ > 0`, a density `ρ > 0` and a gravity magnitude `g > 0`, the depth
profile `p(d) = p₀ + ρ g d` is strictly increasing in `d`, and at every depth `d ≥ 0` below the
free surface the pressure satisfies `p(d) ≥ p₀`, hence `p(d) > 0`.
