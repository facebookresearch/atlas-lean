# Hydrostatic pressure profile in depth coordinates

For all real `p₀`, `ρ`, `g` and `d`, the depth profile `p(d) = p₀ + ρ g d` has derivative
`dp/dd = ρ g` at `d`, and `p₀ + ρ g d = p₀ - ρ g (-d)`: in the depth coordinate `d = -z` it agrees
with the [height profile](hydrostatic-pressure.md) `p₀ - ρ g z` of the hydrostatic column. As a
signed identity it holds for all real inputs.
