# Hydrostatic pressure profile in height coordinates

For all real `p₀`, `ρ`, `g` and `z`, the profile `p(z) = p₀ - ρ g z` has derivative
`dp/dz = -ρ g` at `z`, and `p(0) = p₀`: it is the pressure of a hydrostatic column of constant
density `ρ` under constant gravity `g`, with the height `z` measured upward from the free surface,
where the pressure is `p₀`. As a signed identity it also accepts negative density or gravity and
points above the free surface; the [physical regime](hydrostatic-pressure-physical.md) is
`p₀, ρ, g > 0` below the surface.
