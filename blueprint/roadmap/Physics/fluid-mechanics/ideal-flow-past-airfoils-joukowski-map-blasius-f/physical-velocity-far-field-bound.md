# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ ≥ 4a, W(z) is U e^(−iα) + Γ/(2πiz) up to K/‖z‖²

Let `a > 0`, let `U`, `Γ`, `α` be real and let `W` be the
[physical complex velocity](physical-velocity.md). For every complex `z` with `‖z‖ ≥ 4a`,
`‖W(z) − U e^{−iα} − Γ/(2πi z)‖ ≤ K/‖z‖²` with `K = 12|U|a² + a|Γ|`. So the velocity is the free
stream `U e^{−iα}` plus the vortex term `Γ/(2πi z)`, up to a remainder of order `1/‖z‖²` with
explicit constants; the [existence form](exists-physical-velocity-far-field.md) packages it with
holomorphy. Physically `U > 0`; the bound holds for all real `U`, `Γ` and `α`.
