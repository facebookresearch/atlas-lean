# For a > 0, ∂ψ/∂y is the horizontal velocity u away from the origin

Let `a > 0`, let `U` and `Γ` be real, and let `(x, y)` be a point with `x² + y² ≠ 0`. Then the
[stream function](cyl-stream.md) `ψ` satisfies `∂ψ/∂y(x, y) = u(x, y)`, where `u` is the
[horizontal velocity](cyl-u.md). With the [derivative in x](cyl-stream-deriv-x.md), `ψ` generates
the velocity field: `(u, v) = (∂ψ/∂y, −∂ψ/∂x)`.
