# For nonzero a, ρ and U, the Kutta lift has coefficient 2π sin α

Let `a`, `ρ`, `U` be nonzero reals and `α` real. With the Kutta circulation `Γ = −4πaU sin α`, the
lift per unit span `−ρUΓ = 4πρaU² sin α` and the chord `c = 4a`, the
[lift coefficient](lift-coefficient.md) is `(−ρUΓ)/(ρU²(4a)/2) = 2π sin α`. The hypotheses are the
factors cancelled in this quotient; if one of `a`, `ρ`, `U` is `0`, the convention that division by
zero gives `0` makes the coefficient `0`. Physically `a`, `ρ` and `U` are positive (radius, density,
speed), and `4a` is the chord of the plate.
