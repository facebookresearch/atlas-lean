# For a > 0, the squared speed on the cylinder is (2U sin θ − Γ/(2πa))²

Let `a > 0` and let `U`, `Γ`, `θ` be real. At the point `a e^{iθ}` of the cylinder, the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) `w` at angle of attack `0` satisfies
`‖w(a e^{iθ})‖² = (2U sin θ − Γ/(2πa))²`. The velocity there is tangential
([no penetration](cyl-normal-zero.md)), of magnitude `|2U sin θ − Γ/(2πa)|`.
