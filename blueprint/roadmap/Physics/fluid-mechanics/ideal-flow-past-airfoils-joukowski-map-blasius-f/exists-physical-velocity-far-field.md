# For a > 0, W is holomorphic on ‖z‖ > 2a with an O(1/‖z‖²) remainder

Let `a > 0` and let `U`, `Γ`, `α` be real. The [physical complex velocity](physical-velocity.md) `W`
is holomorphic on `‖z‖ > 2a`, and there are real numbers `R₁ > 2a` and `K ≥ 0` such that
`‖W(z) − U e^{−iα} − Γ/(2πi z)‖ ≤ K/‖z‖²` for every `z` with `‖z‖ ≥ R₁`. One can take `R₁ = 4a` and
`K = 12|U|a² + a|Γ|` ([explicit bound](physical-velocity-far-field-bound.md)). This is the far-field
input of the [Blasius force of W](blasius-force-physical-velocity.md).
