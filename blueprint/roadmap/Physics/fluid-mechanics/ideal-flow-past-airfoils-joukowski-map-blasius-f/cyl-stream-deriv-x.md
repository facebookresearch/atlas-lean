# For a > 0, ∂ψ/∂x is minus the vertical velocity v away from the origin

Let `a > 0`, let `U` and `Γ` be real, and let `(x, y)` be a point with `x² + y² ≠ 0`. Then the
[stream function](cyl-stream.md) `ψ` satisfies `∂ψ/∂x(x, y) = −v(x, y)`, where `v` is the
[vertical velocity](cyl-v.md). With the [derivative in y](cyl-stream-deriv-y.md), `ψ` generates the
velocity field: `(u, v) = (∂ψ/∂y, −∂ψ/∂x)`.
