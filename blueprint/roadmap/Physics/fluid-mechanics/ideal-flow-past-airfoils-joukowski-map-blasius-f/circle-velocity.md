# Complex velocity of a stream past a cylinder with circulation

For real `a`, `U`, `Γ`, `α` and complex `ζ`, the circle-plane complex velocity is
`w(ζ) = U(e^{−iα} − a² e^{iα}/ζ²) + Γ/(2πi ζ)`. It is meant for `ζ ≠ 0`; with the convention
`x/0 = 0` its value at `ζ = 0` is `U e^{−iα}`.

In the model, `a > 0` is the radius of a cylinder centred at `0` and the flow region is `‖ζ‖ ≥ a`.
Read as `w = u − iv`, `w` is the velocity of a uniform stream of speed `U` past the cylinder, with
velocity `U(cos α, sin α)` at infinity (angle of attack `α` to the real axis), and with circulation
`Γ` about the cylinder (`Γ > 0` counterclockwise). At `α = 0` and `ζ ≠ 0` its real and imaginary
parts are the cylinder velocity components `u` and `−v`
([cylinder form](circle-velocity-zero-eq-cyl.md)). Under the Joukowski map it is the numerator of
the [physical velocity](physical-velocity.md), and its vanishing at `ζ = a` is the
[Kutta condition](kutta-cancellation.md) of the model.
