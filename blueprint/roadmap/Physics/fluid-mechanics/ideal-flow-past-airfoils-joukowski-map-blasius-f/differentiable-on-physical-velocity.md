# For a > 0, the physical complex velocity is holomorphic on ‖z‖ > 2a

Let `a > 0` and let `U`, `Γ`, `α` be real. The [physical complex velocity](physical-velocity.md) `W`
is complex differentiable at every point of the open region `‖z‖ > 2a`, so it is holomorphic there.
On that region the [far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md) is holomorphic and satisfies `‖ζ(z)‖ > a`,
so `ζ(z) ≠ 0` and the denominator `1 − a²/ζ(z)²` does not vanish: no division-by-zero convention is
involved.
