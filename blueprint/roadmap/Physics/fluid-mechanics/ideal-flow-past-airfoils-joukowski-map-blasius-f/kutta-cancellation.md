# For a > 0, a zero circle velocity at ζ = a forces Γ = −4πaU sin α

Let `a > 0` and let `U`, `Γ`, `α` be real. If the [circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) vanishes at
the point `ζ = a`, that is, `U(e^{−iα} − a² e^{iα}/a²) + Γ/(2πi a) = 0`, then the circulation is
`Γ = −4πaU sin α`. Since `a ≠ 0`, the hypothesis reads `−2iU sin α + Γ/(2πi a) = 0`. The converse
holds as well ([Kutta circulation](circle-velocity-kutta-trailing-edge.md)).

In the Joukowski model `ζ = a` is the preimage of the trailing edge `z = 2a` of the plate, where the
map derivative `1 − a²/ζ²` vanishes; the Kutta condition is imposed in this form, a vanishing circle
velocity at `ζ = a`. With the sign conventions of the circle velocity (`Γ > 0` counterclockwise), a
free stream with `U > 0` and `0 < α < π` gives `Γ < 0`, a clockwise circulation.
