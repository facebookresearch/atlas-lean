# For a > 0, Bernoulli's law gives the surface pressure on the cylinder

Let `a > 0`, let `p∞`, `ρ`, `U`, `Γ` be real, and let `p : ℂ → ℝ` satisfy Bernoulli's law in the
flow region: `p(z) = p∞ + ρ(U² − ‖w(z)‖²)/2` for every `z` with `‖z‖ ≥ a`, where `w` is the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) at angle of attack `0`. Then, for every real `θ`,
`p(a e^{iθ}) = p∞ + ρ(U² − (2U sin θ − Γ/(2πa))²)/2`, by the
[squared speed on the cylinder](norm-sq-circle-velocity-zero-circle-map.md). Physically `ρ > 0` is
the density and `p∞` the pressure far away; the identity holds for all real `p∞`, `ρ`, `U` and `Γ`.
