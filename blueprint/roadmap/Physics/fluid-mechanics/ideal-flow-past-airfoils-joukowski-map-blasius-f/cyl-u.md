# Horizontal velocity u of the flow past a cylinder with circulation

For real `U`, `a`, `Γ`, `x`, `y`, with `r² = x² + y²`, the horizontal velocity is
`u(x, y) = U(1 − a²(x² − y²)/r⁴) − Γy/(2πr²)`. It is meant for `r ≠ 0`; with the convention that
division by zero gives `0`, its value at the origin is `u(0, 0) = U`.

At `z = x + iy ≠ 0`, `u` is the real part of the complex velocity `U(1 − a²/z²) + Γ/(2πi z)`, read
as `u − iv` ([complex form](circle-velocity-zero-eq-cyl.md)); with the [vertical velocity](cyl-v.md)
`v` it gives the velocity field `(u, v)`. In the model, `a > 0` is the radius of a cylinder centred
at the origin, the flow region is `r ≥ a`, the free stream has speed `U` along the positive `x`-axis
and `Γ` is the circulation about the cylinder (`Γ > 0` counterclockwise).
