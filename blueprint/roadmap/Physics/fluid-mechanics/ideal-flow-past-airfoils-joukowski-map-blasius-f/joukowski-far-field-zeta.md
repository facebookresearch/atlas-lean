# For a > 0 and ‖z‖ > 2a, the Joukowski map sends ζ(z) back to z

Let `a > 0` and let `z` be a complex number with `‖z‖ > 2a`. Then the [Joukowski map](joukowski.md)
`J(ζ) = ζ + a²/ζ` sends the [far-field branch](far-field-zeta.md) `ζ(z)` back to `z`: `J(ζ(z)) = z`.
So `ζ` is a right inverse of `J` on the exterior region `‖z‖ > 2a`. There `ζ(z) ≠ 0`, so no
division-by-zero convention is involved.
