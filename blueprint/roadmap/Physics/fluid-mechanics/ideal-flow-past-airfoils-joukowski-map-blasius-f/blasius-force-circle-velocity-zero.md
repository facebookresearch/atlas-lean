# For ρ, a, U > 0 and R ≥ a, the cylinder flow has Blasius force iρUΓ

Let `ρ > 0`, `a > 0`, `U > 0` and `R ≥ a`, and let `Γ` be real. The
[Blasius force](blasius-force.md), with density `ρ` and at radius `R`, of the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) `w` at angle of attack `0` is `(iρ/2) ∮ w(z)² dz = iρUΓ`, the
integral taken counterclockwise around the circle `‖z‖ = R`; read as `F_x − i F_y`, `F_x = 0` and
`F_y = −ρUΓ`. It is the [Blasius force formula](blasius-force-eq.md) applied with `R₀ = a/2`,
`R₁ = a` and the remainder `e(z) = −Ua²/z²`. The circle `R = a` is the cylinder itself.
