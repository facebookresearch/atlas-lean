# For a > 0, the Bernoulli surface-pressure integrands are integrable

Let `a > 0`, let `p∞`, `ρ`, `U`, `Γ` be real, and let `p : ℂ → ℝ` satisfy Bernoulli's law in the
flow region: `p(z) = p∞ + ρ(U² − ‖w(z)‖²)/2` for every `z` with `‖z‖ ≥ a`, where `w` is the
[circle velocity](circle-velocity.md) at angle of attack `0`. Then `θ ↦ p(a e^{iθ}) cos θ` and
`θ ↦ p(a e^{iθ}) sin θ` are integrable on `0 ≤ θ ≤ 2π`; they are continuous, by the
[surface-pressure formula](surface-pressure-circle-map.md). So the integrals defining the
[horizontal](surface-force-x.md) and [vertical](surface-force-y.md) pressure forces are not the
value `0` that the convention assigns to a non-integrable integrand.
