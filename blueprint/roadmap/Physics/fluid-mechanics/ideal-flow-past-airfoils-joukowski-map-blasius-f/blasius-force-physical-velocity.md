# For a, ρ, U > 0, the physical velocity has Blasius force iρUΓ e^(−iα)

Let `a > 0`, `ρ > 0`, `U > 0` and `R > 2a`, and let `Γ` and `α` be real. The
[Blasius force](blasius-force.md), with density `ρ` and at radius `R`, of the
[physical complex velocity](physical-velocity.md) `W` with circulation `Γ` and angle of attack `α`
is `(iρ/2) ∮ W(z)² dz = iρUΓ e^{−iα}`, the integral taken counterclockwise around the circle
`‖z‖ = R`.

It follows from the [Blasius force formula](blasius-force-eq.md) with `R₀ = 2a`, applied with the
[far-field expansion](exists-physical-velocity-far-field.md) of `W` and the remainder
`e(z) = W(z) − U e^{−iα} − Γ/(2πi z)`. The circulation `Γ` is arbitrary here; the
[Kutta circulation](signed-lift-lift-coefficient-kutta.md) is one choice.
