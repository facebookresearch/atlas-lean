# Before clearance the queue drains at the rate mu minus lambda

For `0 < λ < μ` and a time `t` with `R < t`, `t < R + λ R / (μ - λ)` and `t < R + G`, the
[signal queue](queue-len.md) `Q(t)` (`λ t` for `t ≤ R`, `max(0, λ R - (μ - λ)(t - R))` for `t > R`)
has the derivative `Q'(t) = -(μ - λ)` at `t`.
