# A clearing cycle has a positive arrival volume

Let `0 < λ < μ` (arrival and service rates), `R ≥ 0` and `G ≥ 0` (red and green durations), with the
strict capacity condition `λ (R + G) < μ G`. Then the number of vehicles arriving in the cycle,
`λ (R + G)`, is positive: the strict capacity condition forces `G > 0`.
