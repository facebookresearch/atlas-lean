# Receiving supply min(w (kj − k), capacity) of a cell

For a free-flow speed `v`, a wave speed `w`, a jam density `kj` and a cell density `k` (real
numbers), the receiving supply of the cell is `min(w (kj - k), v w kj / (v + w))`: the
congested-branch flow `w (kj - k)` capped at the [capacity](capacity.md) of the triangular
fundamental diagram. In the physical reading `v`, `w`, `kj` are positive and `0 ≤ k ≤ kj`; the
definition takes any real values.
