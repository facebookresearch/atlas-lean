# Demand and supply both equal the capacity at the critical density

Let `v`, `w` and `kj` be positive, and let `kc = w kj / (v + w)` be the critical density. Then both
the [demand](demand.md) and the [supply](supply.md) at `kc` equal the [capacity](capacity.md):
`min(v kc, v w kj / (v + w)) = v w kj / (v + w)` and
`min(w (kj - kc), v w kj / (v + w)) = v w kj / (v + w)`, since
`v kc = w (kj - kc) = v w kj / (v + w)`.
