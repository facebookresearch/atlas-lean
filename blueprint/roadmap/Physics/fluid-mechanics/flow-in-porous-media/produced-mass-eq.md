# Produced mass identity $\Delta M = V_p \rho_0 (1 - e^{c_f (p - p_0)})$

For all real $V_p$, $\rho_0$, $c_f$, $p_0$ and $p$, the [produced mass](produced-mass.md)
$\Delta M = V_p (\rho(p_0) - \rho(p))$ for the [exponential density law](density.md)
$\rho(p) = \rho_0 e^{c_f (p - p_0)}$ is

$$\Delta M = V_p\, \rho_0 \bigl(1 - e^{c_f (p - p_0)}\bigr).$$

For $V_p, \rho_0, c_f > 0$ it is positive on depletion and negative on injection. In the physical
reading the pore volume $V_p$, the reference density $\rho_0$ and the fluid compressibility $c_f$
are positive.
