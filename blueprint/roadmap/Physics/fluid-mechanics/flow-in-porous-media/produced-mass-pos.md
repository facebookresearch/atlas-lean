# With positive parameters, depletion gives positive produced mass

If $V_p > 0$, $\rho_0 > 0$, $c_f > 0$ and $p < p_0$, then the [produced mass](produced-mass.md)
$\Delta M = V_p (\rho(p_0) - \rho(p))$ for the [exponential density law](density.md)
$\rho(p) = \rho_0 e^{c_f (p - p_0)}$ is positive:
$\Delta M = V_p \rho_0 (1 - e^{c_f (p - p_0)}) > 0$.
