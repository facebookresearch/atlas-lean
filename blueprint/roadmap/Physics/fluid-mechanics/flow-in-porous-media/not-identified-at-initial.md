# At zero pressure excursion two pore volumes give equal produced mass

For all real $\rho_0$, $c_f$ and $p_0$, the [produced mass](produced-mass.md) at $p = p_0$ is the
same for the pore volumes $V_p = 1$ and $V_p = 2$ (both are $0$), and $1 \ne 2$. So without a
pressure excursion the produced mass does not determine the pore volume; the statement exhibits this
one pair.
