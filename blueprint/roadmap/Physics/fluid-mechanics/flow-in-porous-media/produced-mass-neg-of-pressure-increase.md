# With positive parameters, injection gives negative produced mass

If $V_p > 0$, $\rho_0 > 0$, $c_f > 0$ and $p > p_0$ (a pressure increase, as on injection), then the
[produced mass](produced-mass.md) $\Delta M = V_p (\rho(p_0) - \rho(p))$ for the
[exponential density law](density.md) $\rho(p) = \rho_0 e^{c_f (p - p_0)}$ is negative:
$\Delta M = V_p \rho_0 (1 - e^{c_f (p - p_0)}) < 0$. With this sign convention production is
positive and injection negative.
