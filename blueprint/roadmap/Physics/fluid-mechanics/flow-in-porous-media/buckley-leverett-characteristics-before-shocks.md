# Buckley–Leverett characteristics before shocks

In the immiscible displacement model, let $S$ be a $C^1$ solution and $f$ a $C^1$ fractional flow on a domain without shocks, and let
a $C^1$ curve $x(t)$ stay in that domain with $x'(t) = q f'(S(x(t), t))/\phi$. Then $S(x(t), t)$ is constant, since its derivative is
$S_t + x' S_x = \frac{1}{\phi}\bigl(\phi S_t + q f'(S) S_x\bigr) = 0$; hence the curve is a straight line with speed $q f'(S)/\phi$.
This is the frontal advance equation. Shocks, entropy solutions and the Welge construction are outside this result.
