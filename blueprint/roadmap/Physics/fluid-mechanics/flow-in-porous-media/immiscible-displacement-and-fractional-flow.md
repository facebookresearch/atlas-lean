# Immiscible displacement and fractional flow

Consider incompressible one-dimensional displacement of oil by water on an interval, with water saturation $S$ between the residual
values $S_{wc} \le S \le 1 - S_{or}$, constant porosity $\phi > 0$ and constant total flux $q$, governed by
$\phi\,S_t + (q f(S))_x = 0$. With water and oil mobilities $\lambda_w(S), \lambda_o(S) \ge 0$ (relative permeability over viscosity)
and positive total mobility, the fractional flow $f = \lambda_w/(\lambda_w + \lambda_o)$ lies in $[0, 1]$; nonnegativity of the
mobilities is needed, positive total mobility alone does not suffice. Capillary pressure, gravity and thermal effects are excluded from
this model.
