# Linearized produced mass $V_p \rho_0 c_f (p_0 - p)$

For real $V_p$, $\rho_0$, $c_f$, $p_0$ and $p$, the linearized produced mass is

$$\Delta M_{\mathrm{lin}} = V_p\, \rho_0\, c_f\, (p_0 - p),$$

the [produced mass](produced-mass.md) with the density replaced by its first-order approximation
$\rho_0 (1 + c_f (p - p_0))$ about $p_0$. It
[depends on $V_p$ and $c_f$ only through the product $V_p c_f$](linear-identifies-only-product.md).
In the physical reading the pore volume $V_p$, the reference density $\rho_0$ and the fluid
compressibility $c_f$ are positive.
