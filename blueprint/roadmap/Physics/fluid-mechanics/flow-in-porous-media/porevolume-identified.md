# For positive $\rho_0$, $c_f$ and $p \ne p_0$ produced mass fixes $V_p$

Let $\rho_0 > 0$, $c_f > 0$ and $p \ne p_0$, and let $V_p$ and $\Delta M$ be real numbers such that
$\Delta M$ is [the produced mass](produced-mass.md) $\Delta M = V_p (\rho(p_0) - \rho(p))$ for the
[exponential density law](density.md) $\rho(p) = \rho_0 e^{c_f (p - p_0)}$. Then

$$V_p = \frac{\Delta M}{\rho_0 \bigl(1 - e^{c_f (p - p_0)}\bigr)},$$

whose denominator is nonzero under these hypotheses: known $\rho_0$ and $c_f$ and a nonzero pressure
excursion determine the pore volume from the produced mass. No sign of $V_p$ is assumed; in the
physical reading $V_p > 0$.
