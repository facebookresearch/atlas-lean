# If $\rho_0 (p - p_0) \ne 0$, linearized masses agree iff $V_p c_f$ do

For real $V_{p,1}$, $V_{p,2}$, $c_1$, $c_2$, $\rho_0$, $p_0$, $p$ with $\rho_0 \ne 0$ and
$p \ne p_0$, the [linearized produced masses](linear-produced-mass.md) agree,
$V_{p,1} \rho_0 c_1 (p_0 - p) = V_{p,2} \rho_0 c_2 (p_0 - p)$, if and only if
$V_{p,1} c_1 = V_{p,2} c_2$: one nondegenerate linearized observation determines exactly the product
$V_p c_f$. Without $\rho_0 \ne 0$ and $p \ne p_0$ both sides are $0$ and only the implication from
equal products remains.
