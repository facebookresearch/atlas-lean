# Left-travelling head h(t, x) = F(x + a t)

For a real `a` and a profile `F : ℝ → ℝ`, the left-travelling head is `h_L(t, x) = F(x + a t)` for
all real `t` and `x`: the profile `F` translated with speed `a`, towards decreasing `x` when
`a > 0`. Its discharge is the [left-travelling discharge](q-l.md) `-(g A / a) F(x + a t)`.
