# Right-travelling discharge q(t, x) = (g A / a) F(x − a t)

For reals `g`, `A`, `a` and a profile `F : ℝ → ℝ`, the right-travelling discharge is
`q_R(t, x) = (g A / a) F(x - a t)` for all real `t` and `x`, the discharge that accompanies the
[right-travelling head](h-r.md) `F(x - a t)`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when
`a = 0`. In the physical reading `g` is the gravitational acceleration, `A` the pipe area and `a`
the wave speed, all positive.
