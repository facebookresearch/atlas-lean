# Joukowsky jump Δp = −ρ a Δv across a left-travelling wave

Let `ρ`, `g`, `A` and `a` be real with `a ≠ 0` and `A ≠ 0`, and let `F : ℝ → ℝ` be a profile. For
the [left-travelling head](h-l.md) `h_L` and [discharge](q-l.md) `q_L` of `F`, any time `t` and any
two points `x₁` and `x₂`,

`ρ g (h_L(t, x₂) - h_L(t, x₁)) = -ρ a (q_L(t, x₂) - q_L(t, x₁)) / A`.

With the pressure `p = ρ g h` and the velocity `v = q / A` this is the Joukowsky relation
`Δp = -ρ a Δv` between two states at the same time. In the physical reading `ρ` (density), `g`, `A`
and `a` are positive.
