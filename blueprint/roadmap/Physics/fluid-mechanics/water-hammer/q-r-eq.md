# A right-travelling discharge is (g A / a) times its head

For all real `g`, `A`, `a`, `t`, `x` and every profile `F : ℝ → ℝ`, the
[right-travelling discharge](q-r.md) is `(g A / a)` times the [right-travelling head](h-r.md):
`q_R(t, x) = (g A / a) h_R(t, x)`. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` both sides are `0` when `a = 0`.
