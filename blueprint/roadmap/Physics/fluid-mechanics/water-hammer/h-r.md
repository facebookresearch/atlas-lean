# Right-travelling head h(t, x) = F(x − a t)

For a real `a` and a profile `F : ℝ → ℝ`, the right-travelling head is `h_R(t, x) = F(x - a t)` for
all real `t` and `x`: the profile `F` translated with speed `a`, towards increasing `x` when
`a > 0`. Its discharge is the [right-travelling discharge](q-r.md) `(g A / a) F(x - a t)`.
