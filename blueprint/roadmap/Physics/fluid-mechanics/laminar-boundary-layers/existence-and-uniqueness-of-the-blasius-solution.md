# Existence and uniqueness of the Blasius solution

This node covers the following result for the similarity reduction of the steady laminar boundary layer on a flat plate. The
boundary-value problem

$$f''' + \tfrac12 f f'' = 0 \ \text{on } [0, \infty), \qquad f(0) = f'(0) = 0, \qquad f'(\eta) \to 1 \ \text{as } \eta \to \infty$$

has exactly one solution. For it, $f''(\eta) > 0$ for all $\eta \ge 0$, $f''$ is decreasing, $0 \le f' < 1$ on $[0, \infty)$, and
$f''(\eta) \to 0$ as $\eta \to \infty$.

## Expansion

A good expansion states the shooting formulation through the scaling symmetry of the equation, proves existence and uniqueness
of the solution with the displayed boundary conditions, and records the monotonicity properties. Reconstructing the boundary-layer
velocity field from $f$, and any comparison with the Navier-Stokes solution, are separate nodes.

## Sources
