# Directional derivative of the kinetic energy along the velocity

Let `u` be a vector field on three-dimensional Euclidean space, differentiable at a point `x`, with
derivative `Du(x)`. The directional derivative at `x` of the kinetic energy per unit mass
`‖u‖² / 2` along `u(x)` is `⟪u(x), Du(x) u(x)⟫`, the inner product of `u(x)` with the convective
derivative `Du(x) u(x)`.
