# The Bernoulli head is constant along a streamline

Let `u` be a steady velocity field, `p` a pressure and `Φ` a potential on three-dimensional
Euclidean space, and `ρ > 0` a constant density. Let `γ` be a streamline, that is, an integral
curve of `u` on an open preconnected set `s` of parameters (for example an open interval). If
`u`, `p` and `Φ` are differentiable at every point `γ(t)`, `t ∈ s`, and the Euler balance dotted
with `u` holds there, `⟪u, Du u⟫ + (1/ρ) Dp u + DΦ u = 0`, then the Bernoulli head
`p / ρ + ‖u‖² / 2 + Φ` takes the same value at any two points `γ(a)` and `γ(b)` with `a, b ∈ s`.
