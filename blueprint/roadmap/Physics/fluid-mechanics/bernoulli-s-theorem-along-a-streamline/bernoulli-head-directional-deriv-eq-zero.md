# The Bernoulli head has zero derivative along the velocity

Let `u` be a velocity field, `p` a pressure and `Φ` a potential on three-dimensional Euclidean
space, all differentiable at a point `x`, and let `ρ > 0` be a constant density. If the Euler
balance dotted with `u(x)` holds at `x`,
`⟪u(x), Du(x) u(x)⟫ + (1/ρ) Dp(x) u(x) + DΦ(x) u(x) = 0`, then the directional derivative at `x`
of the Bernoulli head `p / ρ + ‖u‖² / 2 + Φ` along `u(x)` is zero. This local condition makes the
head [constant along a streamline](bernoulli-along-streamline.md); by itself it says nothing about
the values of the head at other points.
