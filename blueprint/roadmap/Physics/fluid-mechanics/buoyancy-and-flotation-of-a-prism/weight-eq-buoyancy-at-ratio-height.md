# Prism weight equals the buoyancy of the density-ratio height

For a fluid density `ρ > 0` and all real `ρ_b`, `g`, `A` and `H`, `ρ_b (A H) g = ρ g A h` at
`h = (ρ_b / ρ) H`: the weight of a prism of height `H`, horizontal area `A` and density `ρ_b`
equals the buoyancy `ρ g A h` of a submerged height `h`. This is a division identity, not a
flotation theorem: without `0 ≤ ρ_b ≤ ρ`, `h` need not lie in `[0, H]`.
