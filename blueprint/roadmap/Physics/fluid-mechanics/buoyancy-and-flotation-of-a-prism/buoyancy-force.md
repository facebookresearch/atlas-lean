# The net pressure force on a vertical prism is ρ g A h

For all real `P₀`, `ρ`, `g`, `A` and `h`, `(P₀ + ρ g h) A - P₀ A = ρ g A h`: for a vertical
rectangular prism of horizontal area `A` and submerged height `h` in a fluid of density `ρ` under
gravity `g`, with top pressure `P₀` and bottom pressure `P₀ + ρ g h`, each acting on the area `A`,
the bottom-minus-top force is `ρ g A h`.
