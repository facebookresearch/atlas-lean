# The submerged fraction of a floating prism is the density ratio

For densities `0 < ρ_i < ρ_w` (ice and water) and a prism thickness `H > 0`, if the submerged
depth `d` satisfies the weight balance `ρ_w d = ρ_i H`, then `d = (ρ_i / ρ_w) H`, `0 < d < H`,
and the submerged fraction is `d / H = ρ_i / ρ_w`.
