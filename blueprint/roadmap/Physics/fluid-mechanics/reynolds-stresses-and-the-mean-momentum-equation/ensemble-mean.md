# Ensemble mean of a vector field over the realizations

For a measure `μ` on a space `Ω` of realizations and a vector field `v(ω, τ, y)` with values in
`ℝⁿ⁺¹`, the ensemble mean at time `τ` and point `y` has the components
`𝔼[vᵢ](τ, y) = ∫ vᵢ(ω, τ, y) dμ(ω)`. It is the expectation of `vᵢ(τ, y)` when `μ` is a probability
measure and the component is integrable; the integral of a non-integrable function is `0` by
convention, and for a general measure it need not be an average.
