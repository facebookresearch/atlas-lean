# Without fluctuations the Reynolds stress and its divergence vanish

Let `(Ω, μ)` be a measure space of flow realizations and `u(ω, τ, y)` a velocity field with values
in `ℝⁿ⁺¹`, for a realization `ω`, a time `τ` and a point `y ∈ ℝⁿ⁺¹`. Let `μ` be a probability
measure and `U(τ, y)` a field such that, at every time `τ` and point `y`, almost every realization
has `u(ω, τ, y) = U(τ, y)`. Then at every `τ`, `y` and `x` and for every component `j`: the
[ensemble mean](ensemble-mean.md) is `U(τ, y)`, the [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) at
`(τ, y)` is the zero matrix, and the divergence at `x` of the row `y′ ↦ Rⱼ·(τ, y′)` is `0`. So the
Reynolds-stress term of the [mean momentum equation](mean-momentum.md) vanishes.
