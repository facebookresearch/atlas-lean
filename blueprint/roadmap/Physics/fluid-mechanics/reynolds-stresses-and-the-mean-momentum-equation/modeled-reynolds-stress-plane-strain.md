# Plane strain diag(1, −1, 0): Rm = diag(−4/3, 8/3, 2/3) and P = 4

For `k = 1`, `νt = 1` and the plane strain `S = diag(1, -1, 0)`, the rate of strain of the mean flow
`U = (x, -y, 0)`, the [closure](modeled-reynolds-stress.md) is `Rm = diag(-4/3, 8/3, 2/3)` and its
[extraction](mean-flow-extraction.md) is `P = 4`. Its entry `Rm₀₀ = -4/3` is negative.
