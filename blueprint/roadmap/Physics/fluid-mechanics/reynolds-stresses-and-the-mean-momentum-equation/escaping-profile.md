# Escaping profile s³/ω² exp(−s²/ω) of a counterexample

For real `s` and `ω`, the escaping profile is `φ(s, ω) = (s³ / ω²) exp(-s² / ω)`. With the
convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` at `ω = 0`. In the
[counterexample](escaping-profile-interchange-fails.md) `ω` ranges over `(0, 1]`, where the profile
is odd in `s` and its peaks become taller and narrower as `ω` decreases.
