# Production −∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ Rᵢⱼ Sᵢⱼ of turbulent kinetic energy

For real `3 × 3` matrices `R` (a Reynolds stress per unit density) and `S` (the mean rate of
strain), the mean-flow extraction is `P = -∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ Rᵢⱼ Sᵢⱼ`. It models the production of turbulent
kinetic energy per unit mass; a positive value is read as kinetic energy passing from the mean flow
to the turbulence.
