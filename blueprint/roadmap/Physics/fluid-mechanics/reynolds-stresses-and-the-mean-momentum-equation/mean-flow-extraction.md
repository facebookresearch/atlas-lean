# Production −∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ Rᵢⱼ Sᵢⱼ of turbulent kinetic energy

For real `3 × 3` matrices `R` (a Reynolds stress per unit density) and `S` (the mean rate of
strain), the mean-flow extraction is `P = -∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ Rᵢⱼ Sᵢⱼ`: the production of turbulent kinetic
energy per unit mass, the rate at which the Reynolds stress takes kinetic energy from the mean flow.
Energy leaves the mean flow when `P > 0`.
