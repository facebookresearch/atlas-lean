# The Reynolds stress of square-integrable velocities is semidefinite

Let `(Ω, μ)` be a measure space of flow realizations and `u(ω, τ, y)` a velocity field with values
in `ℝⁿ⁺¹`, for a realization `ω`, a time `τ` and a point `y ∈ ℝⁿ⁺¹`. Let `μ` be a probability
measure and let every velocity component at `(τ, y)` be square integrable. Then for every vector
`a ∈ ℝⁿ⁺¹` the [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) satisfies `∑ⱼ ∑ᵢ aⱼ aᵢ Rⱼᵢ(τ, y) ≥ 0`: its
quadratic form is the variance of `a · u(τ, y)`, so the matrix is positive semidefinite.
