# Ensemble mean of a stress field over the realizations

For a measure `μ` on a space `Ω` of realizations and a stress field `σ(ω, τ, y)` with components
`σⱼᵢ`, the ensemble mean stress at time `τ` and point `y` has the components
`𝔼[σⱼᵢ](τ, y) = ∫ σⱼᵢ(ω, τ, y) dμ(ω)`. It is the expected stress when `μ` is a probability measure
and the components are integrable; the integral of a non-integrable function is `0` by convention.
