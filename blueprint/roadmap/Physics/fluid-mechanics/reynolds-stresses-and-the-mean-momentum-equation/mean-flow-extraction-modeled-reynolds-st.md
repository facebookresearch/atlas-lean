# For a trace-free strain the closure extracts P = 2 νt ‖S‖²

For all real `k` and `νt` and every real `3 × 3` matrix `S` with trace `0`, the
[closure](modeled-reynolds-stress.md) `Rm = (2 k / 3) I - 2 νt S` has the
[extraction](mean-flow-extraction.md) `P(Rm, S) = 2 νt ‖S‖²`, where `‖S‖² = ∑ᵢ ∑ⱼ Sᵢⱼ²` is the
squared Frobenius norm: the isotropic part does no work on a trace-free strain. No symmetry of `S`
is needed.
