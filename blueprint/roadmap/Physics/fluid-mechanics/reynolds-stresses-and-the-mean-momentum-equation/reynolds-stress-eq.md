# Covariance identity for the Reynolds stress

Let `(Ω, μ)` be a measure space of flow realizations and `u(ω, τ, y)` a velocity field with values
in `ℝⁿ⁺¹`, for a realization `ω`, a time `τ` and a point `y ∈ ℝⁿ⁺¹`. Let `μ` be a probability
measure and let the components `uⱼ(·, τ, y)` and `uᵢ(·, τ, y)` be square integrable. Then the
[Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) satisfies `Rⱼᵢ(τ, y) = 𝔼[uⱼ uᵢ] - 𝔼[uⱼ] 𝔼[uᵢ]`, with the
[ensemble means](ensemble-mean.md) at `(τ, y)`.
