# Boussinesq closure Rm = (2 k / 3) I − 2 νt S of the Reynolds stress

For real `k` (turbulent kinetic energy) and `νt` (eddy viscosity) and a real `3 × 3` matrix `S`
(mean rate of strain), the Boussinesq closure of the [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) per unit
density is `Rm = (2 k / 3) I - 2 νt S`. The stress it exerts on the mean flow is
`-ρ Rm = 2 ρ νt S - (2 / 3) ρ k I`, tension positive. The definition takes any real values; the
model domain is `k ≥ 0` and a symmetric trace-free `S`, and it is a chosen closure, not a property
of turbulence.
