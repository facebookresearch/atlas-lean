# Entropy selects the compression branch

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, and let upstream and downstream states with $\rho_1, T_1, \rho_2, T_2 > 0$ satisfy the normal-shock jump conditions (perfect-gas law on both sides and conservation of mass, momentum and energy) and differ from each other. Let $a_1^2 = \gamma R T_1$ and $M_1 = |u_1|/a_1 > 0$. With $c_v = R/(\gamma - 1)$ and the specific entropy change $\Delta s = c_v\ln(T_2/T_1) + R\ln(\rho_1/\rho_2)$, we have $\Delta s > 0$ if and only if $M_1 > 1$, and $\Delta s < 0$ if and only if $M_1 < 1$.
