# Stagnation state at zero Mach number

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$ and $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, let the sound speed $a$ satisfy $a^2 = \gamma R T$ and the Mach number be $M = |u|/a$, and let the stagnation temperature $T_0$ satisfy the energy balance $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$. Let moreover $\rho > 0$, $\rho_0 > 0$, $p = \rho R T$, $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$ and $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$. If $M = 0$, then $T_0 = T$, $p_0 = p$ and $\rho_0 = \rho$.
