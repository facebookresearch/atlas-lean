# Critical-area ratio of an isentropic nozzle

Let $p_0 > 0$, $T_0 > 0$, $R > 0$, $\gamma > 1$, $M > 0$ and $A^* > 0$, and let $G(M)$ denote the isentropic mass flux per unit area at Mach number $M$ for the stagnation state $(p_0, T_0)$. If $G(M)\,A = G(1)\,A^*$, then $A/A^* = G(1)/G(M)$ and $A/A^* \ge 1$.
