# Stagnation pressure

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $a^2 = \gamma R T$, $M = |u|/a$, and $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$. With densities $\rho, \rho_0 > 0$, the ideal-gas laws $p = \rho R T$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$, and the isentropic relation $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$, we have $p_0/p = (T_0/T)^{\gamma/(\gamma - 1)} = (1 + (\gamma - 1)M^2/2)^{\gamma/(\gamma - 1)}$.
