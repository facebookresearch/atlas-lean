# Specific entropy change in terms of temperature and pressure

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, and let two states have $\rho_1, T_1, \rho_2, T_2 > 0$ with $p_1 = \rho_1 R T_1$ and $p_2 = \rho_2 R T_2$. Then the specific entropy change with $c_v = R/(\gamma - 1)$ between the specific volumes $1/\rho_1$ and $1/\rho_2$ equals $c_p \log(T_2/T_1) - R \log(p_2/p_1)$ with $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$.
