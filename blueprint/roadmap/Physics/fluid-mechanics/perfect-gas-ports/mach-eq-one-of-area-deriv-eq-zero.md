# A station with stationary area and accelerating flow is sonic

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $\rho_0 > 0$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$. Near a station $x_0$ let $T > 0$, $\rho > 0$, $p = \rho R T$, the isentropic relation $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$, the energy balance $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$ and mass conservation $\rho u A = \dot m$ hold, and at the station let $u > 0$, $A > 0$, $a^2 = \gamma R T$ and $M = |u|/a$, with $A$, $\rho$, $u$ differentiable there. If $a > 0$, $A'(x_0) = 0$ and $u'(x_0) \ne 0$, then $M = 1$.
