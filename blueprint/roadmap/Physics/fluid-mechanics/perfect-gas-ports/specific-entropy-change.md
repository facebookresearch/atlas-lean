# Specific entropy change of a calorically perfect gas

For a specific heat at constant volume $c_v$ and a specific gas constant $R$, the entropy change per unit mass from the state $(T_1, v_1)$ to the state $(T_2, v_2)$ is $c_v \log(T_2/T_1) + R \log(v_2/v_1)$. It is intended for positive temperatures and specific volumes.
