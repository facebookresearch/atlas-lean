# Density change along an isentropic duct flow

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $\rho_0 > 0$ and $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$. Near a station $x_0$ let $T > 0$, $\rho > 0$, $p = \rho R T$, $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$ and $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$ hold, and at the station let $u > 0$, $a^2 = \gamma R T$ and $M = |u|/a$. If $\rho$ and $u$ have derivatives $\rho'$ and $u'$ at $x_0$, then $\rho'/\rho = -M^2\,u'/u$.
