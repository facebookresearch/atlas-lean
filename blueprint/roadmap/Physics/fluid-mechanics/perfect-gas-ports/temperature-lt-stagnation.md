# A moving gas stagnates hotter

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$ and $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, let the sound speed $a$ satisfy $a^2 = \gamma R T$ and the Mach number be $M = |u|/a$, and let the stagnation temperature $T_0$ satisfy the energy balance $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$. If $M > 0$, then $T < T_0$.
