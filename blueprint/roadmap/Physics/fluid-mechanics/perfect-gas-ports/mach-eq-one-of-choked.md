# Choked mass flow makes the station sonic

Let $\gamma > 1$, $R > 0$, $T > 0$, $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, $a > 0$ with $a^2 = \gamma R T$, $M = |u|/a$, $c_p T + u^2/2 = c_p T_0$, $\rho, \rho_0 > 0$, $p = \rho R T$, $p_0 = \rho_0 R T_0$ and $p/\rho^\gamma = p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$. If a station of area $A > 0$ has mass flow $\rho u A = \dot m$ equal to the choked value $\dot m = G(1)A$, where $G(M)$ is the isentropic mass flux per unit area at Mach number $M$ for stagnation state $(p_0, T_0)$, then $M = 1$.
