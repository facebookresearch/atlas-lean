# Normal-shock jump conditions

For heat-capacity ratio $\gamma$ and gas constant $R$, an upstream state $(\rho_1, u_1, p_1, T_1)$ and a downstream state $(\rho_2, u_2, p_2, T_2)$ satisfy the steady normal-shock jump conditions of a calorically perfect gas when

$$p_1 = \rho_1 R T_1,\quad p_2 = \rho_2 R T_2,\quad \rho_1 u_1 = \rho_2 u_2,\quad p_1 + \rho_1 u_1^2 = p_2 + \rho_2 u_2^2,\quad c_p T_1 + \tfrac{u_1^2}{2} = c_p T_2 + \tfrac{u_2^2}{2},$$

with $c_p = \gamma R/(\gamma - 1)$, taken as $0$ when $\gamma = 1$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
