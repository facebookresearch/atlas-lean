# Darcy–Weisbach loss coefficient

For a Darcy factor $f$, a duct of length $L$, hydraulic diameter $D$ and flow area $A$, and a fluid of density $\rho$, the Darcy–Weisbach loss coefficient is $c = f L \rho/(2 D A^2)$, defined for all real inputs with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (so $c = 0$ when $D = 0$ or $A = 0$).
