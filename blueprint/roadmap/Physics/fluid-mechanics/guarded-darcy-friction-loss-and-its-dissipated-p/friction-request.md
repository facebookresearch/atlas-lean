# Requests for a friction factor

A friction-factor request consists of two real numbers, a Reynolds number and a relative roughness, together with a duct shape and the hydrodynamic development at the evaluated section. The numbers are unrestricted data; they are read physically as $Re = \rho\,|V|\,D/\mu$ on the hydraulic diameter $D$ and as $\varepsilon/D$ for wall roughness height $\varepsilon$ ($0$ for a smooth wall), but these relations are separate hypotheses wherever they are used.
