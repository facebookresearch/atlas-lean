# A front at constant speed s changes the count at rate s (kL − kR)

For all real `kL`, `kR`, `a`, `b`, `s`, `ξ₀` and `t`, with the front at `ξ(t) = s t + ξ₀`, the
[vehicle count](inventory.md) `N(t) = kL (ξ(t) - a) + kR (b - ξ(t))` has the derivative
`s (kL - kR)` at `t`. No condition places the front inside the segment.
