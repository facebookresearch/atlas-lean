# First moment of a linear pressure distribution on a panel

For a pressure gradient `c`, a panel width `w` and depths `a` and `b` (real numbers), the first
moment about depth zero of the linear pressure profile `p(z) = c * z` over the depth interval from
`a` to `b` is `M = ∫_a^b z * (w * (c * z)) dz`, an oriented integral. In the physical reading, as
for the [resultant force](linear-pressure-force.md), `w > 0` and `0 ≤ a < b`, and `M` is a force
times a length about the datum `z = 0`.
