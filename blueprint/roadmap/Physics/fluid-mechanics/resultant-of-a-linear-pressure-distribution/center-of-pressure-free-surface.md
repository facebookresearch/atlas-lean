# Center of pressure of a panel from the free surface

For a pressure gradient `c > 0`, a width `w > 0` and a depth `H > 0`, the center of pressure of a
panel from the free surface to depth `H`, the ratio of the [first moment](linear-pressure-moment.md)
`M = ∫_a^b z * (w * (c * z)) dz` to the [resultant force](linear-pressure-force.md)
`F = ∫_a^b w * (c * z) dz` with `a = 0` and `b = H`, is at depth `2 * H / 3`.
