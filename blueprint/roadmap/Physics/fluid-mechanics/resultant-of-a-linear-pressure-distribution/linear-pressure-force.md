# Resultant force of a linear pressure distribution on a panel

For a pressure gradient `c`, a panel width `w` and depths `a` and `b` (real numbers), the resultant
force of the linear pressure profile `p(z) = c * z` over the depth interval from `a` to `b` is
`F = ∫_a^b w * (c * z) dz`, an oriented integral (`∫_a^b = -∫_b^a` for `b < a`). In the physical
reading `w > 0`, `0 ≤ a < b`, `z` is the depth below the zero-pressure datum and `c` the pressure
gradient; with `c` in force per length³ and `w`, `z` in length, `F` is a force.
