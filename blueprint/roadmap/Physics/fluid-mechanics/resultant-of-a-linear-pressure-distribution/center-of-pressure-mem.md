# The center of pressure lies within the panel

For a pressure gradient `c > 0`, a width `w > 0` and depths `0 ≤ a < b`, the center of pressure, the
ratio `M / F` of the [first moment](linear-pressure-moment.md) `M = ∫_a^b z * (w * (c * z)) dz` to
the [resultant force](linear-pressure-force.md) `F = ∫_a^b w * (c * z) dz`, lies in `[a, b]`. Under
these hypotheses the force is positive, and `M / F = 2 * (b ^ 3 - a ^ 3) / (3 * (b ^ 2 - a ^ 2))`.
