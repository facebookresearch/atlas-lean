# Passive pipe flows with positive resistances have unique arc flows

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets of nodes and arcs, each arc $e$ running from $t(e)$ to $h(e)$, and
let $r_e > 0$ for every arc. If $(q_1, H_1)$ and $(q_2, H_2)$ are both
[passive pipe flows](is-passive-pipe-flow.md) with the resistances $r$ and the same nodal net
inflows $d$, then $q_1 = q_2$; the heads need not agree.
