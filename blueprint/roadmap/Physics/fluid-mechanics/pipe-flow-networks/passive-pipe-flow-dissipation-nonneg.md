# The dissipation pairing of a passive pipe flow is nonnegative

Let $V$ and $E$ be finite sets of nodes and arcs, each arc $e$ running from its tail $t(e)$ to its
head $h(e)$, and let $r$ (resistances) and $q$ (arc flows) be real functions on $E$ and $d$ (nodal
net inflows) and $H$ (heads) real functions on $V$. For a
[passive pipe flow](is-passive-pipe-flow.md), $-\sum_{i \in V} H_i d_i \geq 0$: the resistances are
nonnegative, so the [dissipation](passive-pipe-flow-dissipation.md) is.
