# Weak residual of a scalar conservation law

For a flux $f$, a field $u(t, x)$ and the time and space partial derivatives $\varphi_t$, $\varphi_x$ of a test function, the weak residual is

$$\iint_{\mathbb R^2} \bigl(u\,\varphi_t + f(u)\,\varphi_x\bigr)\,dt\,dx$$

with respect to Lebesgue measure on the $(t, x)$ plane.
