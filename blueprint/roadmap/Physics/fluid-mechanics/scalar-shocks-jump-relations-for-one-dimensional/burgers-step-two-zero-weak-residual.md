# Weak residual of a Burgers step with jump from 2 to 0

Let $\varphi$ be a smooth function on $\mathbb R^2$ with support in a bounded rectangle, with partial derivatives $\varphi_t, \varphi_x$. For Burgers' flux $f(u) = u^2/2$ and the step $u = 2$ for $x < ct$, $u = 0$ otherwise, write the weak residual as $\iint (u\varphi_t + f(u)\varphi_x)\,dt\,dx$. At the Rankine-Hugoniot speed $c = 1$ the residual is $0$; at $c = 0$ it equals $2\int \varphi(t, 0)\,dt$.
