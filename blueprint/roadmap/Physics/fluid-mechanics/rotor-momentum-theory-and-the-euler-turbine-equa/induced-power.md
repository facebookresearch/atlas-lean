# Induced power ṁ (U_w² − U∞²) / 2 of an actuator disk

For an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md), the induced power is
`ṁ (U_w² - U∞²) / 2 = ρ A U_d (U_w² - U∞²) / 2` with the mass flow `ṁ = ρ A U_d`: the kinetic power
the disk adds to the stream through it, negative when the stream loses kinetic power. In the
physical reading `ρ > 0` and `A > 0`.
