# Extracted power T U_d = ṁ (U∞² − U_w²) / 2 under the port laws

Let an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) satisfy the [port laws](port-laws.md): Bernoulli
`p∞ + ρ U∞² / 2 = p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2` from far upstream to the upstream face and
`p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2 = p∞ + ρ U_w² / 2` from the downstream face to the far wake, and the momentum
balances `T = A (p⁺ - p⁻)` of the thin disk and `T = ρ A U_d (U∞ - U_w)` of the streamtube, where
`T` is the axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow. Then the
[extracted power](extracted-power.md) is the loss of kinetic power of the stream through the disk:
`T U_d = ρ A U_d (U∞² - U_w²) / 2 = ṁ (U∞² - U_w²) / 2`, with the mass flow `ṁ = ρ A U_d`. In the
physical reading `ρ > 0` and `A > 0`; the identities need no sign condition.
