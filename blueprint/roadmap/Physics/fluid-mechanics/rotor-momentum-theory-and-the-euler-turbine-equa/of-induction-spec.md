# Every induction factor is realized by a state satisfying the port laws

For all real `ρ`, `A`, `U∞`, `p∞` and `a`, the [state with induction factor](of-induction.md) `a`
satisfies the [port laws](port-laws.md); for `U∞ ≠ 0` its
[axial induction factor](induction-factor.md) is `a`; and for `ρ > 0`, `A > 0` and `U∞ > 0` its
[power coefficient](power-coefficient.md) is `4 a (1 - a)²`. The family includes both limits of the
wind-extraction branch: the zero-extraction state `a = 0` (`U_d = U_w = U∞`, no force) and the
wake-degenerate state `a = 1/2` (`U_w = 0`). At `U∞ = 0` the induction factor of the state is `1`,
whatever `a`, by the convention `x / 0 = 0`. In the physical reading `ρ`, `A` and `U∞` are positive.
