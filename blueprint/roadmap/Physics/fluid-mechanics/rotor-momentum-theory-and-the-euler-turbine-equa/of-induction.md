# Actuator-disk state with induction factor a

For real `ρ`, `A`, `U∞`, `p∞` and `a`, the ideal disk state with axial induction factor `a` is the
[actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) with density `ρ`, disk area `A`, freestream speed `U∞`,
ambient pressure `p∞`, disk speed `U_d = (1 - a) U∞`, wake speed `U_w = (1 - 2 a) U∞`, the face
pressures `p⁺ = p∞ + ρ (U∞² - U_d²) / 2` and `p⁻ = p∞ + ρ (U_w² - U_d²) / 2` from Bernoulli on each
side, and the force `T = 2 ρ A U∞² a (1 - a)`. It satisfies the [port laws](port-laws.md) for every
`a` ([realization](of-induction-spec.md)). In the physical reading `ρ`, `A` and `U∞` are positive,
and the wind-extraction branch is `0 < a < 1/2`.
