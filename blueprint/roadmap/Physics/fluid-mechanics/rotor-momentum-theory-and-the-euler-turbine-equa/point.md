# Representative point o + r e_r + z e₃ of a port

For a point `o = (o₁, o₂, o₃)` of the shaft axis, the line through `o` along `e₃`, and a
[port](rotor-port.md) of azimuth `φ`, radius `r` and axial station `z`, the representative point of
the port has the inertial position `o + r e_r + z e₃ = (o₁ + r cos φ, o₂ + r sin φ, o₃ + z)`, with
`e_r = (cos φ, sin φ, 0)` the radial vector of the [local cylindrical frame](cyl-frame.md): the
[body-to-inertial position](../../classical-mechanics/rigid-bodies-and-mechanisms/body-fixed-and-inertial-frames/body-to-inertial.md)
of the cylindrical coordinates `(r, 0, z)` about the origin `o`.
