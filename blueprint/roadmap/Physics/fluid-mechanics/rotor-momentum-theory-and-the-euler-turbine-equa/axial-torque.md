# Axial torque e₃ · Σ (x_k − o) × F_k of finitely many forces

For finitely many forces `F_k` (indexed by a finite set) exerted on the fluid at the points `x_k`,
vectors of `ℝ³` in inertial components, the axial torque about the shaft axis through `o` is
`τ = e₃ · Σ_k (x_k - o) × F_k`, the axial component of their total moment about `o`.
