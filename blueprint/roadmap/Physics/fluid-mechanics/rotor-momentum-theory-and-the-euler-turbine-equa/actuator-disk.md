# State of an ideal actuator disk in a steady stream

A state of an ideal actuator disk in a steady stream consists of nine real numbers, with no sign
constraints:

- the density `ρ` and the disk area `A`;
- the axial speeds `U∞` far upstream, `U_d` at the disk and `U_w` in the far wake, positive along
  the flow;
- the ambient pressure `p∞`, far upstream and in the far wake, and the static pressures `p⁺` on the
  upstream face and `p⁻` on the downstream face of the disk;
- the axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow: `T` for the wind-extraction
  results and `F` for the hovering rotor, whose thrust is `T = -F`.

The [port laws](port-laws.md) relate them. In the physical reading `ρ > 0` and `A > 0`.
