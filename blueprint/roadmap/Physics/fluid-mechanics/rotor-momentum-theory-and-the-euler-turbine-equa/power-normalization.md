# Freestream kinetic power ρ A U³ / 2 through the area A

For real `ρ`, `A` and `U`, the power normalization is `ρ A U³ / 2`, the freestream kinetic power
through the area `A` of a stream of density `ρ` and speed `U`: the denominator of the
[power coefficient](power-coefficient.md). It is `0` when `ρ = 0`, `A = 0` or `U = 0`. In the
physical reading `ρ`, `A` and `U` are positive.
