# Powered hover: induced power P = T v_i = T √(T / (2 ρ A))

Let an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) in still air, `U∞ = 0`, with positive density `ρ` and
area `A`, satisfy the [port laws](port-laws.md): Bernoulli `p∞ + ρ U∞² / 2 = p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2`
upstream and `p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2 = p∞ + ρ U_w² / 2` downstream of the disk, and the momentum balances
`F = A (p⁺ - p⁻)` of the thin disk and `F = ρ A U_d (U∞ - U_w)` of the streamtube, where `F` is the
axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow. The induced velocity `v_i = U_d` is
the axial speed at the disk, positive along the flow. Let the rotor thrust `T = -F` be positive and
the disk be powered, `T v_i > 0`. Then the [induced power](induced-power.md) is `P = T v_i`, it
equals the power `-F v_i` of the disk on the stream, and `P = T √(T / (2 ρ A))`. The first two
identities hold under the port laws alone, by the [energy balance of the disk](induced-power-eq.md).
