# For 0 ≤ U_w < U∞ and ρ > 0, the total pressure drops across the disk

Let an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) satisfy the [port laws](port-laws.md): Bernoulli
`p∞ + ρ U∞² / 2 = p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2` from far upstream to the upstream face and
`p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2 = p∞ + ρ U_w² / 2` from the downstream face to the far wake, and the momentum
balances `T = A (p⁺ - p⁻)` of the thin disk and `T = ρ A U_d (U∞ - U_w)` of the streamtube, where
`T` is the axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow. Let `ρ > 0` and
`0 ≤ U_w < U∞` (the extraction side: the wind-extraction branch `0 < U_w < U∞` together with its
wake-degenerate endpoint `U_w = 0`). The area `A` is not restricted. Then the total pressure drops
across the disk, from `p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2` on the upstream face to `p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2` on the downstream
face, by `ρ (U∞² - U_w²) / 2 > 0`. So Bernoulli applied across the pressure jump, which would equate
the two total pressures, contradicts extraction.
