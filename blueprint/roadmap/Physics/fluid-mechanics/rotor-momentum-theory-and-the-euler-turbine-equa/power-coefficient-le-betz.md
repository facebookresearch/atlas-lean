# Betz limit C_P ≤ 16/27 for 0 < U_w < U∞ and ρ, A > 0

Let an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) satisfy the [port laws](port-laws.md): Bernoulli
`p∞ + ρ U∞² / 2 = p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2` from far upstream to the upstream face and
`p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2 = p∞ + ρ U_w² / 2` from the downstream face to the far wake, and the momentum
balances `T = A (p⁺ - p⁻)` of the thin disk and `T = ρ A U_d (U∞ - U_w)` of the streamtube, where
`T` is the axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow. If the density `ρ` and the
area `A` are positive, then on the wind-extraction branch `0 < U_w < U∞` the
[axial induction factor](induction-factor.md) lies in `0 < a < 1/2`, the
[power coefficient](power-coefficient.md) is at most `16/27`, and it equals `16/27` exactly when
`a = 1/3`. The limit is reached: for `ρ, A, U∞ > 0` the
[state with induction factor](of-induction.md) `1/3` has `U_w = U∞ / 3` and `C_P = 16/27`
([realization](of-induction-spec.md)).
