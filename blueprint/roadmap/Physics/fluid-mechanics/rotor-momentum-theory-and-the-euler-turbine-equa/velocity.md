# Inertial velocity c_r e_r + c_θ e_θ + c_z e₃ at a port

For a [port](rotor-port.md) of azimuth `φ` with the cylindrical velocity components
`(c_r, c_θ, c_z)`, the inertial fluid velocity at its representative point is
`c = c_r e_r + c_θ e_θ + c_z e₃ = (c_r cos φ - c_θ sin φ, c_r sin φ + c_θ cos φ, c_z)`, with `e_r`
and `e_θ` the radial and tangential vectors of the [local cylindrical frame](cyl-frame.md) at
azimuth `φ`.
