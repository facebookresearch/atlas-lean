# The lumped axial angular-momentum flux of a port is ṁ r c_θ

For every point `o` and every [port](rotor-port.md) of radius `r` and swirl `c_θ`, the
[lumped axial angular-momentum flux](axial-moment-flux.md) about `o` is
`e₃ · ((x - o) × (ṁ c + p A n)) = ṁ r c_θ`, with `x = o + r e_r + z e₃` the
[representative point](point.md) and `ṁ` the [lumped mass flow](lumped-mass-flow.md). The pressure
term `p A n` has no axial moment, since the outward normal lies in the meridional plane, and neither
the azimuth `φ` nor the axial station `z` enters.
