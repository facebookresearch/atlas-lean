# The swirl relative to a rotor turning at ω is c_θ − ω r

For every real `ω`, every point `o` and every [port](rotor-port.md) with
[representative point](point.md) `x = o + r e_r + z e₃` and [inertial velocity](velocity.md) `c`,
the fluid velocity relative to a rotor turning at `ω` about the shaft axis through `o` is
`w = c - ω e₃ × (x - o)`, and its tangential component is `w · e_θ = c_θ - ω r`, with `e_θ` the
tangential vector of the [local cylindrical frame](cyl-frame.md). The swirl `c_θ` of the Euler
torque and power formulas is the inertial one, not this rotor-relative swirl.
