# The power of forces at points of a rotor is ω times their axial torque

For every real `ω`, every point `o` and finitely many forces `F_k` at points `x_k`, the
[power](rotor-power.md) of the forces at points of a rotor turning at `ω` about the shaft axis
through `o` is `ω` times their [axial torque](axial-torque.md):
`Σ_k F_k · (ω e₃ × (x_k - o)) = ω e₃ · Σ_k (x_k - o) × F_k`.
