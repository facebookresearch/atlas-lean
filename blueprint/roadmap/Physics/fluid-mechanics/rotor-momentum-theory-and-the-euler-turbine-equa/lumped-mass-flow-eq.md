# Lumped mass flow ρ A (c_r n_r + c_z n_z) in cylindrical components

For every [port](rotor-port.md), the [lumped mass flow](lumped-mass-flow.md) is
`ṁ = ρ A (c_r n_r + c_z n_z)`, from the cylindrical components `(c_r, c_θ, c_z)` of the velocity and
`(n_r, 0, n_z)` of the normal: the dot product `c · n` is the same in the
[local cylindrical frame](cyl-frame.md), a rotation, as in inertial components, and the swirl `c_θ`
does not enter.
