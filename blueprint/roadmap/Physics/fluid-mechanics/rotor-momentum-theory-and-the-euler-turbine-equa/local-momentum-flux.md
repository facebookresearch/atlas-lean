# Area-weighted local momentum flux ṁ c + p A n of a port

For a [port](rotor-port.md) with pressure `p`, area `A`, [inertial velocity](velocity.md) `c`,
[outward unit normal](normal.md) `n` and [lumped mass flow](lumped-mass-flow.md) `ṁ = ρ A (c · n)`,
the area-weighted local momentum flux is the vector `ṁ c + p A n` of `ℝ³`, in inertial components:
the port area times the convective-plus-pressure momentum flux `ρ (c · n) c + p n` through the
outward normal at the representative point. It is a local value, not the vector momentum outflow of
a port surface.
