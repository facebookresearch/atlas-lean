# For ρ, A, U∞ > 0 and U_w ≠ U∞, C_P = 4 a (1 − a)²

Let an [actuator-disk state](actuator-disk.md) satisfy the [port laws](port-laws.md): Bernoulli
`p∞ + ρ U∞² / 2 = p⁺ + ρ U_d² / 2` from far upstream to the upstream face and
`p⁻ + ρ U_d² / 2 = p∞ + ρ U_w² / 2` from the downstream face to the far wake, and the momentum
balances `T = A (p⁺ - p⁻)` of the thin disk and `T = ρ A U_d (U∞ - U_w)` of the streamtube, where
`T` is the axial force of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow. If `ρ > 0`, `A > 0`,
`U∞ > 0` and `U_w ≠ U∞`, then the [power coefficient](power-coefficient.md) is `C_P = 4 a (1 - a)²`
for the [axial induction factor](induction-factor.md) `a = 1 - U_d / U∞`. The wake speed may be
above or below `U∞`.
