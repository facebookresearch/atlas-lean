# The Greenshields capacity is attained at half the jam density

For `vf > 0` and `kj > 0`, the [Greenshields flux](greenshields-flux.md) `q(k) = vf k (1 - k / kj)`
satisfies `q(kj / 2) = vf kj / 4` and `q(k) ≤ vf kj / 4` for every density `0 ≤ k ≤ kj`: the
capacity `vf kj / 4` is the maximum of the flux on `[0, kj]`, attained at half the jam density.
