# Actuator disk state

The state of an ideal actuator disk in a steady stream consists of nine real numbers: the density $\rho$, the disk area $A$, the axial speeds $U_\infty$ far upstream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake (positive along the flow), the ambient pressure $p_\infty$, the static pressures $p^+$ and $p^-$ on the upstream and downstream faces of the disk, and the axial force $T$ of the fluid on the disk, positive along the flow.
