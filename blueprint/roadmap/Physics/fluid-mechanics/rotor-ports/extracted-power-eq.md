# Extracted power as loss of kinetic power

Let an actuator disk state with density $\rho$, disk area $A$, axial speeds $U_\infty$ in the oncoming freestream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake, ambient pressure $p_\infty$, face pressures $p^+$, $p^-$ and axial force $F$ of the fluid on the disk satisfy the port laws. Then the extracted power is $F\,U_d = \rho A U_d\,(U_\infty^2 - U_w^2)/2$.
