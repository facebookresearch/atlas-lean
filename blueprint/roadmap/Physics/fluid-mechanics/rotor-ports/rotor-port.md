# Lumped ports of a rotor control volume

A rotor port consists of a density $\rho \ge 0$, a port area $A \ge 0$, a static pressure $p$, a representative point with azimuth $\varphi$, radius $r \ge 0$ and axial station $z$, the components $(c_r, c_\theta, c_z)$ of the inertial fluid velocity in the cylindrical frame at that point, and the meridional components $(n_r, n_z)$ of the outward unit normal, with $n_r^2 + n_z^2 = 1$.
