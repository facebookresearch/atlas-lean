# Pressure jump across an actuator disk

Let an actuator disk state with density $\rho$, disk area $A$, axial speeds $U_\infty$ in the oncoming freestream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake, ambient pressure $p_\infty$, face pressures $p^+$, $p^-$ and axial force $F$ of the fluid on the disk satisfy the port laws. Then $p^+ - p^- = \rho(U_\infty^2 - U_w^2)/2$ and $F = \rho A(U_\infty^2 - U_w^2)/2$.
