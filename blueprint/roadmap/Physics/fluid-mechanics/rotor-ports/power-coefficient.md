# Power coefficient of an actuator disk

The power coefficient of an actuator disk is $C_P = F\,U_d/(\rho A U_\infty^3/2)$, with the convention $x/0 = 0$. It is the physical power coefficient under the normalization $\rho, A, U_\infty > 0$; $U_\infty > 0$ alone does not make the denominator nonzero when $\rho$ or $A$ vanishes.
