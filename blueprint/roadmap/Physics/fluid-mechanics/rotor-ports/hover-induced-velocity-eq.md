# Induced velocity of a hovering rotor

Consider an actuator disk with density $\rho > 0$ and area $A > 0$ obeying Bernoulli's equation upstream and downstream of the disk and the momentum balances of the disk and of the streamtube. Suppose it hovers (zero far-upstream speed), exerts force $-T$ on the stream with thrust $T > 0$, and the thrust does positive work, $T v_i > 0$, where $v_i$ is the axial speed at the disk. Then

$$v_i = \sqrt{\frac{T}{2\rho A}}.$$
