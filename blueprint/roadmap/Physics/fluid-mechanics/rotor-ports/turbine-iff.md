# Euler turbomachine equation, turbine sign

Let inlet and outlet rotor ports carry the lumped mass flows $-\dot m$ and $\dot m$, let a finite family of forces $F_k$ act on the fluid at points $x_k$ of a rotor turning at angular speed $\omega$ about the shaft through $o$, let their axial torque equal the sum of the axial angular-momentum fluxes of the two ports (the steady lumped balance), and let the shaft torque $\tau_s$ and the reaction torque $\tau_r$ on the rotor satisfy $\tau_s + \tau_r = 0$ with $\tau_r$ the negative of the axial torque on the fluid. Write $r_1, c_{\theta 1}$ and $r_2, c_{\theta 2}$ for the radius and swirl velocity of the inlet and outlet, and $-\omega\tau_s$ for the shaft power out. If $\dot m > 0$, then the shaft power out is positive, and the rotor power is negative, exactly when $\omega r_2 c_{\theta 2} < \omega r_1 c_{\theta 1}$.
