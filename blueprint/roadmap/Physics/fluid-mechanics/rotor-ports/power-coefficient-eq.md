# Power coefficient in terms of the induction factor

Let an actuator disk state with density $\rho$, disk area $A$, axial speeds $U_\infty$ in the oncoming freestream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake, ambient pressure $p_\infty$, face pressures $p^+$, $p^-$ and axial force $F$ of the fluid on the disk satisfy the port laws. If $\rho > 0$, $A > 0$, $U_\infty > 0$ and $U_w \ne U_\infty$, then $C_P = 4a(1 - a)^2$ for the axial induction factor $a = 1 - U_d/U_\infty$.
