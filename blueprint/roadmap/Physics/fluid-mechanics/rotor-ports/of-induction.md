# Actuator disk with a given induction factor

For a density $\rho$, a disk area $A$, a freestream speed $U_\infty$, an ambient pressure $p_\infty$ and an induction factor $a$, the disk state has $U_d = (1 - a)U_\infty$, $U_w = (1 - 2a)U_\infty$, face pressures $p^+ = p_\infty + \rho(U_\infty^2 - U_d^2)/2$ and $p^- = p_\infty + \rho(U_w^2 - U_d^2)/2$, and force $F = 2\rho A U_\infty^2 a(1 - a)$.
