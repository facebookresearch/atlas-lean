# Actuator disk built from a construction parameter

For a density $\rho$, a disk area $A$, a freestream speed $U_\infty$, an ambient pressure $p_\infty$ and a real construction parameter $a$, the disk state has $U_d = (1 - a)U_\infty$, $U_w = (1 - 2a)U_\infty$, face pressures $p^+ = p_\infty + \rho(U_\infty^2 - U_d^2)/2$ and $p^- = p_\infty + \rho(U_w^2 - U_d^2)/2$, and force $F = 2\rho A U_\infty^2 a(1 - a)$. Its computed induction factor recovers $a$ when $U_\infty \ne 0$; for $U_\infty = 0$ it is $1$ whatever $a$ is.
