# Betz limit of the ideal wind turbine

For an ideal actuator disk obeying Bernoulli upstream and downstream of the disk, the thin-disk momentum balance $T = A(p^+ - p^-)$ and the streamtube momentum balance $T = \rho A U_d (U_\infty - U_w)$, with $\rho > 0$, $A > 0$ and $0 < U_w < U_\infty$, the induction factor $a = 1 - U_d/U_\infty$ lies in $(0, 1/2)$, the power coefficient satisfies $C_P \le 16/27$, and $C_P = 16/27$ exactly when $a = 1/3$.
