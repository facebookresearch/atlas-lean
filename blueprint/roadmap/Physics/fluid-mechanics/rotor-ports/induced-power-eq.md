# Energy balance of an actuator disk

Let an actuator disk state with density $\rho$, disk area $A$, axial speeds $U_\infty$ in the oncoming freestream, $U_d$ at the disk and $U_w$ in the far wake, ambient pressure $p_\infty$, face pressures $p^+$, $p^-$ and axial force $F$ of the fluid on the disk satisfy the port laws. If $F = -T$, then the induced power equals $-F\,U_d$, the negative of the extracted power, and equals $T\,U_d$.
