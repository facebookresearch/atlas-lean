# Disk states built from a construction parameter satisfy the port laws

For all $\rho$, $A$, $U_\infty$, $p_\infty$ and every construction parameter $a$, the disk state built from $a$ satisfies the port laws; if $U_\infty \ne 0$, its computed induction factor is $a$ (for $U_\infty = 0$ it is $1$); and under the physical normalization $\rho > 0$, $A > 0$ and $U_\infty > 0$, its power coefficient is $4a(1 - a)^2$.
