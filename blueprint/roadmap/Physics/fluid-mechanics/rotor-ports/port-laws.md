# Actuator disk port laws

An actuator disk state $(\rho, A, U_\infty, U_d, U_w, p_\infty, p^+, p^-, T)$ satisfies the port laws when

- Bernoulli holds from far upstream to the upstream face: $p_\infty + \rho U_\infty^2/2 = p^+ + \rho U_d^2/2$;
- Bernoulli holds from the downstream face to the far wake: $p^- + \rho U_d^2/2 = p_\infty + \rho U_w^2/2$;
- the momentum balance of the thin disk holds: $T = A\,(p^+ - p^-)$;
- the momentum balance of the streamtube holds: $T = \rho A U_d\,(U_\infty - U_w)$.
