# Capture in an ideal settler

In an ideal horizontal settler of length $L$, width $W$ and depth $H$ ($L, W, H > 0$) carrying a flow $Q > 0$ in plug flow with
uniform horizontal speed $u = Q/(WH)$ and no vertical mixing, a particle entering at height $z_0 \in [0, H]$ above the floor with
signed downward terminal speed $v_t$ is counted as captured upon first contact with the floor, including initial contact at $t=0$.
For $z_0>0$ it reaches the floor by the time it reaches the outlet if and only if $v_t L/u \ge z_0$; an entry at $z_0=0$ is already
captured. Equality in the trajectory condition means contact exactly at the outlet. Every entry height is
captured if and only if $v_t \ge Q/(WL)$, the overflow rate. A rising particle ($v_t < 0$) is not captured from $z_0 > 0$. For a
sphere of radius $a > 0$ in a fluid of density $\rho_f > 0$ and viscosity $\mu > 0$, the Reynolds number $2 a \rho_f \lvert v_t\rvert/\mu$
serves only as a stated diagnostic of creeping flow, not as an error bound. Hindered and turbulent settling are outside this
result.
