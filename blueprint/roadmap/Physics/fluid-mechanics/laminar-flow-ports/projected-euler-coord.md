# Euler equations projected on the velocity

Let a steady flow in three-dimensional space have constant density $\rho > 0$, a velocity field $u$ differentiable at every time, a pressure $p$, and a body force with potential $\Phi$, and let it satisfy the Euler equations. Then at every time $t_0$ and coordinate point $v$ about any origin, the coordinate fields satisfy $\langle u, (Du)\,u\rangle + \frac{1}{\rho}(Dp)\,u + (D\Phi)\,u = 0$.
