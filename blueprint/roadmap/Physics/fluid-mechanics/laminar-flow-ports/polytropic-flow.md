# Rotating gas with a polytropic enthalpy

The polytropic rotating flow is the rotating fluid under gravity with zero entropy and specific enthalpy $h = \frac{7}{2}\,p/\rho$, the enthalpy $\frac{\gamma}{\gamma - 1}\,p/\rho$ with $\gamma = 7/5$; with the convention $x/0 = 0$ the enthalpy is $0$ when $\rho = 0$.
