# Plane Couette flow is differentiable

For all parameters $\rho, \mu, \lambda, p_0, U_{\mathrm{wall}}, H$, plane Couette flow with constant density $\rho$ and velocity $(U_{\mathrm{wall}}\, y / H, 0, 0)$ has density and velocity differentiable in time at every point, and momentum density and velocity differentiable in space at every time. These are the differentiability conditions under which the conservative and convective forms of the Navier-Stokes equations are equivalent.
