# Pipe Poiseuille velocity

The cylindrical Poiseuille velocity field is $u(x, y, z) = \big(0, 0, G(R^2 - x^2 - y^2)/(4\mu)\big)$, with axial component $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$, where $x, y$ are the transverse coordinates and $G = -\partial p/\partial z$ is the signed pressure-drop rate.
