# Bernoulli function along a streamline

Let a flow in three-dimensional space have constant density $\rho > 0$, a pressure $p$ and a body force with potential $\Phi$, and let it satisfy the Euler equations. Assume that its velocity field $u$ does not depend on time and that $u(t, \cdot)$ is differentiable in space for every $t$; the pressure and enthalpy may depend on time. Suppose that at time $t_0$ the specific enthalpy is $p/\rho + e_0$ for a constant $e_0$. Let $\gamma$ be an integral curve of $u(t_0, \cdot)$ on an open preconnected set of times $s$, along which $p(t_0, \cdot)$ and $\Phi$ are differentiable. Then the Bernoulli function $h + |u|^2/2 + \Phi$ takes the same value at $\gamma(a)$ and $\gamma(b)$ for all $a, b \in s$.
