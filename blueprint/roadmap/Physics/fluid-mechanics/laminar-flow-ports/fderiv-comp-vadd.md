# Fréchet derivatives in affine coordinates

For a map $f$ from three-dimensional Euclidean space to a normed space, an origin $o$ and a point $p$, the Fréchet derivative of the coordinate expression $v \mapsto f(v + o)$ at $p - o$, applied to a coordinate vector $w$, equals the Fréchet derivative of $f$ at $p$ applied to the vector with coordinates $w$. A Fréchet derivative is taken to be the zero map where the function is not differentiable.
