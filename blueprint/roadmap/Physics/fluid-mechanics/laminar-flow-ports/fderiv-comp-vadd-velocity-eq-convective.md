# The convective term in coordinates

For a fluid flow in three-dimensional space, a time $t$, an origin $o$ and a point $p$, the Fréchet derivative of the coordinate velocity $v \mapsto u(t, v + o)$ at $p - o$ applied to $u(t, p)$ (the zero map where the velocity is not differentiable) equals the convective term $(u \cdot \nabla) u$ at $(t, p)$.
