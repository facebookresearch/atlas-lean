# Plane Poiseuille flow rate

For the plane Poiseuille velocity $u(y) = G y (H - y)/(2\mu)$, with $G = -\partial p/\partial x$ and walls at $y = 0$ and $y = H$, the flow rate per unit width through any cross-section is $\int_0^H u\,dy = G H^3/(12\mu)$, where both $u$ and the right side are $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$.
