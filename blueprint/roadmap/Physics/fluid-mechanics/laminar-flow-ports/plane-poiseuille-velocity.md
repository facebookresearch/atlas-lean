# Plane Poiseuille Velocity

For real $G$, $\mu$ and $H$, the plane Poiseuille velocity at a point with wall-normal coordinate $y$ is $\big(G y (H - y)/(2\mu), 0, 0\big)$, with first component $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$. Here $G = -\partial p/\partial x$ is the signed pressure-drop rate.
