# Poiseuille flow without a pressure gradient vs Couette flow

Let $U \ne 0$ and $H > 0$. With pressure-drop rate $G = 0$ the plane Poiseuille velocity $G y (H - y)/(2\mu)$ (with the value $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$) vanishes everywhere, for every $\mu$. Plane Couette flow with velocity $(U y/H, 0, 0)$, also without a pressure gradient, has flow rate $\int_0^H U y/H\,dy = U H/2 \ne 0$.
