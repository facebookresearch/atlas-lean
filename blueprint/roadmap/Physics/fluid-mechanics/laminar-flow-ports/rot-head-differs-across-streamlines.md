# The Bernoulli head differs between streamlines

Let $u(x) = (-\Omega x_2, \Omega x_1, 0)$ be solid-body rotation about the third axis, $\Phi(x) = g x_3$ the gravitational potential and $p(x) = \rho\Omega^2(x_1^2 + x_2^2)/2 - \rho g x_3$ the balancing pressure. If $\rho > 0$ and $\Omega \ne 0$, then the head $p/\rho + |u|^2/2 + \Phi$ takes different values at $(1, 0, 0)$ and $(2, 0, 0)$, which lie on different circular streamlines.
