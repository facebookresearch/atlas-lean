# Fanning factor of laminar pipe flow

For Hagen-Poiseuille flow with density $\rho > 0$, viscosity $\mu > 0$ and pressure gradient $G \ne 0$ in a pipe of radius $R > 0$, take the pressure drop $G L$ over a length $L > 0$, diameter $D = 2R$, mean velocity $V$ (flow rate over $\pi R^2$), Darcy friction factor $f = 2 D \Delta p / (\rho V |V| L)$ and Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re} = \rho |V| D / \mu$. Then the Fanning factor is $f/4 = 16/\mathrm{Re}$.
