# The mean pipe velocity is nonzero

Let $u_z(x) = G\,(R^2 - x_1^2 - x_2^2)/(4\mu)$ be the axial velocity of cylindrical Poiseuille flow driven by the pressure-drop rate $G = -\mathrm{d}p/\mathrm{d}z$ (the pressure is $p(z) = p_0 - Gz$, so $G > 0$ when pressure falls downstream) in a pipe of radius $R$ with viscosity $\mu$. If $G \ne 0$, $\mu > 0$ and $R > 0$, then the mean velocity is nonzero.
