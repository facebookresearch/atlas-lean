# Axial velocity on a pipe cross-section

On the cross-section at axial position $z$, the axial flux density is the Poiseuille axial velocity $G\,(R^2 - q_1^2 - q_2^2)/(4\mu)$ on the pipe disk of radius $|R|$ and zero outside it, with the convention $x/0 = 0$, so it is identically zero when $\mu = 0$.
