# Pipe Poiseuille flow

Cylindrical Poiseuille flow in all of space with constant density $\rho$, axial velocity $w = G(R^2 - x^2 - y^2)/(4\mu)$ (the value $0$ when $\mu = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$), the Newtonian stress for pressure $p_0 - Gz$ with shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$, and no body force.
