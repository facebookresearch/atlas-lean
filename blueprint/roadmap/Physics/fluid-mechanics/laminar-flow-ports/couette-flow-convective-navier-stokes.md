# Plane Couette flow solves the Navier-Stokes equations

For real constants $\rho$, $\mu$, $\lambda$, $p_0$, $U$, $H$, plane Couette flow (constant density $\rho$, velocity $(U y/H, 0, 0)$ with $y$ the wall-normal coordinate, Newtonian stress with constant pressure $p_0$, shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$, and no body force) satisfies the three-dimensional convective Navier-Stokes equations.
