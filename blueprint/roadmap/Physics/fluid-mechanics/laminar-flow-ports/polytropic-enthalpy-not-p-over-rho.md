# A polytropic enthalpy is not of the form p/ρ plus a constant

Let $u(x) = (-\Omega x_2, \Omega x_1, 0)$ be solid-body rotation about the third axis, $\Phi(x) = g x_3$ the gravitational potential and $p(x) = \rho\Omega^2(x_1^2 + x_2^2)/2 - \rho g x_3$ the balancing pressure. If $\rho > 0$ and $g \ne 0$, then there is no constant $e_0$ with $\frac{7}{2}\,p/\rho = p/\rho + e_0$ at every point.
