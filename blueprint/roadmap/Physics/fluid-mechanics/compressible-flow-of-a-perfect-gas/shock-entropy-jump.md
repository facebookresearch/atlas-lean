# Entropy jump across the distinct normal-shock branch

For real `γ` and `m`, the dimensionless entropy jump of the distinct branch is
`Δs / R = log P / (γ - 1) - γ log r / (γ - 1)`, with its pressure ratio
`P = (2 γ m - (γ - 1)) / (γ + 1)` and density ratio `r = (γ + 1) m / ((γ - 1) m + 2)`. The domain is
`γ > 1` and `m > (γ - 1) / (2 γ)`, where `P > 0` and `r > 0`. Below the threshold `P ≤ 0`, and the
value is given by the conventions `log 0 = 0` and `log x = log |x|`; no result reads it there.
