# Specific enthalpy of a calorically perfect gas

Let `γ > 1`, `ρ ≠ 0`, `cv = R / (γ - 1)`, `cp = γ R / (γ - 1)`, the internal energy `e = cv T` and
the ideal-gas law `p = ρ R T`. Then the specific enthalpy `h = e + p / ρ` is `cp T`. Internal energy
and enthalpy are both measured from zero at `T = 0`.
