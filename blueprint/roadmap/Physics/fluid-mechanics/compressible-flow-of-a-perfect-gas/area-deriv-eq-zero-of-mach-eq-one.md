# A sonic station has stationary area

Let `γ > 1`, `R > 0`, `cp = γ R / (γ - 1)`, `ρ0 > 0` and `p0 = ρ0 R T0`. On a neighbourhood of the
station `x₀` let the temperature `T(x)` and the density `ρ(x)` be positive, and let the ideal-gas
law `p = ρ R T`, the isentropic connection `p / ρ^γ = p0 / ρ0^γ` and the stagnation energy balance
`cp T + u² / 2 = cp T0` hold. At the station let `u(x₀) > 0`, `a² = γ R T(x₀)` and
`M = |u(x₀)| / a`. Let also `ρ u A = ṁ` on a neighbourhood of `x₀`, `A(x₀) > 0`, and let `A`, `ρ`,
`u` have the derivatives `A'`, `ρ'`, `u'` at `x₀`. If `M = 1`, then `A' = 0`: a necessary condition
for a throat, not an area minimum.
