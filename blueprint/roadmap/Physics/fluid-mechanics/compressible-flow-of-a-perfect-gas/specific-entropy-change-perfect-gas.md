# Entropy change of a perfect gas in temperature and pressure

Let `γ > 1`, `R > 0`, and let two states have positive densities `ρ1`, `ρ2` and temperatures `T1`,
`T2`, with `p_i = ρ_i R T_i`. Then the [specific entropy change](specific-entropy-change.md)
`Δs = cv log (T2 / T1) + R log (v2 / v1)` with `cv = R / (γ - 1)` and the specific volumes
`v_i = 1 / ρ_i` equals `cp log (T2 / T1) - R log (p2 / p1)` with `cp = γ R / (γ - 1)`.
