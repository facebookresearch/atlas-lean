# Critical-area ratio of an isentropic flow

Let `p0 > 0`, `T0 > 0`, `R > 0` and `γ > 1`. Let `M > 0` and `A* > 0`, and let a section of area `A`
at the Mach number `M` and a sonic section of area `A*` carry the same mass flow at the same
stagnation state, `G(M) A = G(1) A*`, with the [isentropic mass flux](isentropic-mass-flux.md)
`G(M) = p0 √(γ / (R T0)) M (1 + (γ - 1) M² / 2)^(-(γ + 1) / (2 (γ - 1)))`. Then
`A / A* = G(1) / G(M)` and `A / A* ≥ 1`.
