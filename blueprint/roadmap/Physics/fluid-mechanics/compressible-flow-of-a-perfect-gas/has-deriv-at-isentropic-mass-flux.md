# Derivative of the isentropic mass flux

For `γ > 1` and all real `p0`, `T0`, `R` and `M`, the
[isentropic mass flux](isentropic-mass-flux.md)
`G(M) = p0 √(γ / (R T0)) M (1 + (γ - 1) M² / 2)^(-(γ + 1) / (2 (γ - 1)))` has the derivative
`G'(M) = p0 √(γ / (R T0)) (1 - M²) (1 + (γ - 1) M² / 2)^(-(γ + 1) / (2 (γ - 1)) - 1)`. The base
`1 + (γ - 1) M² / 2` is at least `1`. When `R T0 ≤ 0` the square-root factor is `0` (by `√x = 0` for
`x < 0` and `x / 0 = 0`), and both `G` and the formula vanish.
