# Jump conditions of a steady normal shock

For a heat-capacity ratio `γ`, a specific gas constant `R`, an upstream state `(ρ1, u1, p1, T1)` and
a downstream state `(ρ2, u2, p2, T2)` (real numbers), the steady normal-shock jump conditions are
the ideal-gas law `p = ρ R T` at both states and the conservation of the mass flux `ρ u`, the
momentum flux `p + ρ u²` and the total enthalpy `cp T + u² / 2`, with `cp = γ R / (γ - 1)`. With the
convention `x / 0 = 0`, `cp = 0` when `γ = 1`. These balance laws are model inputs, not derived.
