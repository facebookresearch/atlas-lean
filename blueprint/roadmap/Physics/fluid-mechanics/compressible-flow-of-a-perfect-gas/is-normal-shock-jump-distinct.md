# The distinct branch solves the normal-shock jump conditions

Let `γ > 1`, `R > 0`, `T1 > 0`, `u1 > 0` and `p1 = ρ1 R T1`, let `a1² = γ R T1`, `M1 = |u1| / a1`
and `m = M1²`, and let `r = (γ + 1) m / ((γ - 1) m + 2)` and `P = (2 γ m - (γ - 1)) / (γ + 1)` be
the [density](shock-density-ratio.md) and [pressure](shock-pressure-ratio.md) ratios of the distinct
branch. Then the downstream state `ρ2 = ρ1 r`, `u2 = u1 / r`, `p2 = p1 P` and `T2 = T1 P / r`
satisfies the [normal-shock jump conditions](is-normal-shock-jump.md). This holds whatever the sign
of `P`: it is an algebraic statement, and the root is a positive state only above the threshold of
the [positivity criterion](is-normal-shock-jump-distinct-pos-iff.md).
