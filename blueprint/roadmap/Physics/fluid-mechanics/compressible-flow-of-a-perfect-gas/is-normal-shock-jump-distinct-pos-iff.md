# Positivity of the distinct shock branch above the pressure threshold

Let `γ > 1`, `R > 0`, `ρ1 > 0`, `T1 > 0`, `u1 > 0` and `p1 = ρ1 R T1`, let `a1² = γ R T1`,
`M1 = |u1| / a1` and `m = M1²`, and let `r` and `P` be the density and pressure ratios of the
[distinct branch](is-normal-shock-jump-distinct.md). Then its density `ρ1 r` is positive, and its
pressure `p1 P` and temperature `T1 P / r` are both positive if and only if `m > (γ - 1) / (2 γ)`.
