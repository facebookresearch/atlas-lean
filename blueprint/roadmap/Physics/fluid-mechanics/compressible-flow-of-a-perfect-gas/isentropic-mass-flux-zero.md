# At zero Mach number the flux vanishes and gives no area ratio

For all real `p0`, `T0`, `R` and `γ`, the [isentropic mass flux](isentropic-mass-flux.md)
`G(M) = p0 √(γ / (R T0)) M (1 + (γ - 1) M² / 2)^(-(γ + 1) / (2 (γ - 1)))` satisfies `G(0) = 0`, and
`G(1) / G(0) ≥ 1` fails: with the convention `x / 0 = 0` the quotient is `0`. The critical-area
ratio therefore needs `M > 0`.
