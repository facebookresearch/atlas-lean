# The laminar Fanning friction factor is 16 over the Reynolds number

Let `ρ > 0`, `μ > 0`, `R > 0`, `L > 0` and `G ≠ 0`, and let `z` be real. Then a quarter of the Darcy
friction factor `f = 2 D Δp / (ρ V |V| L)` of the oriented pressure drop `Δp = G L` over the length
`L`, on the diameter `D = 2 R`, at the mean velocity `V` (defined for `L, D, ρ > 0` and a drop along
the flow, `Δp V > 0`, which [holds here](pipe-drop-mul-mean-velocity-pos.md)), the Fanning factor
`f / 4`, equals `16 / Re`, with the Reynolds number `Re = ρ |V| D / μ` on the diameter `D = 2 R`.
