# Solid-body rotation about the vertical axis

For an angular speed `Ω`, the solid-body rotation about the third coordinate axis is the velocity
field `u(x) = (-Ω x₁, Ω x₀, 0)`, counterclockwise seen from positive `x₂` when `Ω > 0`. Points
`x = (x₀, x₁, x₂)` of three-dimensional space are written in Cartesian coordinates.
