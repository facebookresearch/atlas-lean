# Plane Poiseuille flow satisfies the smooth continuity equation

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `H`, [plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md)
satisfies the smooth continuity equation: the density is differentiable in time, the mass flux is
differentiable in space, and the continuity residual vanishes.
