# Zero pressure gradient: no Poiseuille flow, but Couette flow moves

For `U_w ≠ 0`, `H > 0` and all real `μ`, `s` and `z`, the plane Poiseuille velocity with `G = 0`
vanishes everywhere, whereas the [Couette velocity](couette-velocity.md) `(U_w x₁ / H, 0, 0)`, also
without a pressure gradient, has the flow rate per unit width `∫_0^H u₀(s, y, z) dy = U_w H / 2`,
which is nonzero.
