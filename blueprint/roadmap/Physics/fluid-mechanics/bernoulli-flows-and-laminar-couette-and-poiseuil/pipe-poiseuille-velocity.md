# Velocity of cylindrical Poiseuille flow

For a pressure-drop rate `G` (`G = -∂p/∂x₂`, signed), a viscosity `μ` and a pipe radius `R`, the
cylindrical Poiseuille velocity is `u(x) = (0, 0, G (R² - x₀² - x₁²) / (4 μ))`, with `x₀`, `x₁` the
transverse and `x₂` the axial coordinate; it is defined on all of space, and only its restriction to
the pipe `x₀² + x₁² ≤ R²` is the physical flow. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` it is `0` when
`μ = 0`.
