# Pipe flow with the pressure gradient reversed

For real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `R`, the reversed pipe flow has the density `ρ` and the
[cylindrical Poiseuille velocity](pipe-poiseuille-velocity.md) driven by `G`, but its Newtonian
stress uses the pressure `p₀ + G x₂` instead of `p₀ - G x₂`; there is no body force.
