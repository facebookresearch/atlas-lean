# A polytropic enthalpy is not pressure over density plus a constant

For `ρ > 0` and `g ≠ 0`, there is no constant `e₀` with `h(t, x) = p(x) / ρ + e₀` for all `t` and
`x`, where `h = (7 / 2) p / ρ` is the enthalpy of the [polytropic fixture](polytropic-flow.md) and
`p` its pressure. So the [Bernoulli function theorem](bernoulli-function-along-streamline.md) cannot
be applied to this fixture without checking its enthalpy hypothesis. (Along each circular streamline
of the fixture the Bernoulli function is nevertheless constant, since pressure, enthalpy, kinetic
energy and potential are each constant there; the example shows that the hypothesis must be checked,
not that constancy fails without it.)
