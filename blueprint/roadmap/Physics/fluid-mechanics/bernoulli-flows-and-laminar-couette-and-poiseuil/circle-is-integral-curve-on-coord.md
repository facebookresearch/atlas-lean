# Circles are streamlines of the rotation in any affine coordinates

For all real `R`, `Ω`, `z` and every origin `o`, the coordinate curve `t ↦ c(t) - o` of the
[circular path](circle.md) `c(t) = (R cos(Ω t), R sin(Ω t), z)` is an integral curve, on all of `ℝ`,
of the coordinate expression `v ↦ u(o + v)` of the [solid-body rotation](rot-velocity.md) `u`.
