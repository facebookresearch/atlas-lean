# Plane Poiseuille flow with physical parameters

For `H > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ρ > 0`, `G ≠ 0` and all real `λ` and `p₀`,
[plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md) has positive density, is smoothly incompressible,
satisfies the Navier–Stokes equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance and
Newtonian stress) with the pressure `p₀ - G x₀`, has both walls `x₁ = 0` and `x₁ = H` at rest, and
has through every cross-section `x₀ = s`, `x₂ = z` the flow rate per unit width `G H³ / (12 μ)`,
which is nonzero.
