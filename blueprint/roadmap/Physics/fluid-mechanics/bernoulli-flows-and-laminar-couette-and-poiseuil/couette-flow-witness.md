# Plane Couette flow with physical parameters

For `H > 0`, `μ > 0`, `ρ > 0`, `U_w ≠ 0` and all real `λ` and `p₀`,
[plane Couette flow](couette-flow.md) has positive density, is smoothly incompressible, satisfies
the Navier–Stokes equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance and Newtonian
stress) with the constant pressure `p₀`, has its lower wall `x₁ = 0` at rest and its upper wall
`x₁ = H` moving with the velocity `(U_w, 0, 0)`, and has the nonzero shear stress `μ U_w / H`.
