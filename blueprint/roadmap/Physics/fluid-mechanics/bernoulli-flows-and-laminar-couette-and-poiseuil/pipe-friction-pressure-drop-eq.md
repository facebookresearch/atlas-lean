# The Darcy–Weisbach loss with the laminar factor reproduces the drop

Let `ρ > 0`, `μ > 0`, `R > 0`, `L > 0` and `G ≠ 0`, and let `z` be real. Let
`c(f) = f L ρ / (2 D A²)` be the Darcy–Weisbach loss coefficient of the pipe, with `D = 2 R` and
`A = π R²`, and let the Reynolds number `Re = ρ |V| D / μ` on the diameter `D = 2 R`. Then, at the
[flow rate](pipe-flow-rate.md) `Q` through the cross-section `x₂ = z` (the integral of the axial
velocity over the disk `x₀² + x₁² ≤ R²`), the oriented pressure drop `c(f) Q |Q|` equals `G L` for
`f = 64 / Re` and `G L / 4` for `f = 16 / Re` (the Fanning value read as a Darcy factor), in both
flow directions. At `G = 0` there is no flow and `Re = 0`, and no friction factor is assigned.
