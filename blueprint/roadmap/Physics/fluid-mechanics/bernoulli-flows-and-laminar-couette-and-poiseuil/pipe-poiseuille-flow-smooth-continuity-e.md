# Cylindrical Poiseuille flow satisfies the smooth continuity equation

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `R`,
[cylindrical Poiseuille flow](pipe-poiseuille-flow.md) satisfies, on all of space, the smooth
continuity equation: the density is differentiable in time, the mass flux is differentiable in
space, and the continuity residual vanishes.
