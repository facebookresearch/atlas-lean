# Differentiability conditions for cylindrical Poiseuille flow

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `R`,
[cylindrical Poiseuille flow](pipe-poiseuille-flow.md) satisfies the four differentiability
conditions under which the conservative and the convective Navier–Stokes equations are equivalent:
the density and the velocity are differentiable in time at every point, and the momentum density and
the velocity are differentiable in space at every time.
