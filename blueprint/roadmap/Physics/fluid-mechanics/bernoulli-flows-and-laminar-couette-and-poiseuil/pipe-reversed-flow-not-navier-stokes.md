# Reversing only the pressure gradient breaks Navier–Stokes

For `μ ≠ 0`, `G ≠ 0` and all real `ρ`, `λ`, `p₀` and `R`, the
[reversed pipe flow](pipe-reversed-flow.md), the Poiseuille velocity driven by `G` with the pressure
`p₀ + G x₂`, does not satisfy the Navier–Stokes equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy
momentum balance and Newtonian stress): its axial momentum residual is `-2 G`.
