# Axial velocity on a pipe cross-section

For real `G`, `μ`, `R` and `z`, the axial flux density on the cross-section `x₂ = z` is the function
of `q = (q₁, q₂)` equal to the axial component `G (R² - q₁² - q₂²) / (4 μ)` of the
[pipe Poiseuille velocity](pipe-poiseuille-velocity.md) at `(q₁, q₂, z)` on the
[pipe disk](pipe-disk.md) `q₁² + q₂² ≤ R²`, and `0` outside it: the signed integrand of the flow
rate.
