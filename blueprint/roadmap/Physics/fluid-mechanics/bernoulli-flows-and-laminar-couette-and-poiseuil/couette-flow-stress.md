# Newtonian stress of plane Couette flow

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `U_w`, `H` and every time and point, the stress of
[plane Couette flow](couette-flow.md) is the constant matrix with rows `(-p₀, μ U_w / H, 0)`,
`(μ U_w / H, -p₀, 0)` and `(0, 0, -p₀)`: normal entries `-p₀`, shear entries `μ U_w / H`, and no
contribution of the second viscosity. With the convention `x / 0 = 0` the shear entries are `0` when
`H = 0`.
