# Newtonian stress of cylindrical Poiseuille flow

For `μ ≠ 0`, all real `ρ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G`, `R` and every time and point `x`, the stress of
[cylindrical Poiseuille flow](pipe-poiseuille-flow.md) is the matrix with rows
`(-(p₀ - G x₂), 0, -G x₀ / 2)`, `(0, -(p₀ - G x₂), -G x₁ / 2)` and
`(-G x₀ / 2, -G x₁ / 2, -(p₀ - G x₂))`: normal entries `-p`, axial shear entries `-G x₀ / 2` and
`-G x₁ / 2`, and no contribution of the second viscosity.
