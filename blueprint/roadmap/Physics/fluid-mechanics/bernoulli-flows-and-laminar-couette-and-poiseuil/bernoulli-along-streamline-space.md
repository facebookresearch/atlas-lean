# Bernoulli's theorem along a streamline of a steady Euler flow

Let `ρ > 0`, and let a flow of three-dimensional space have the constant density `ρ`, a steady
velocity `u` (`u(t, x)` independent of `t`) that is differentiable in `x` at every time, satisfy the
Euler equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance and inviscid stress) with
a pressure `p`, and have a body force with the potential `Φ` (the specific body force is `-∇Φ`). Let
`γ` be an integral curve of `u(t₀, ·)` on an open preconnected set `s ⊆ ℝ` such that `p(t₀, ·)` and
`Φ` are differentiable at every point `γ(t)`, `t ∈ s`. Then for all `a, b ∈ s` the head
`p / ρ + |u|² / 2 + Φ` (at the time `t₀`) takes the same value at `γ(a)` and at `γ(b)`.
