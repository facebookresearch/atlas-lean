# Bernoulli function along a streamline for enthalpy p/ρ plus a constant

Let `ρ > 0`, and let a flow of three-dimensional space with an enthalpy field `h` have the constant
density `ρ`, a steady velocity `u` (`u(t, x)` independent of `t`) that is differentiable in `x` at
every time, satisfy the Euler equations in conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance
and inviscid stress) with a pressure `p`, and have a body force with the potential `Φ` (the specific
body force is `-∇Φ`). Let `e₀` be a constant with `h(t₀, x) = p(t₀, x) / ρ + e₀` for every `x`. Let
`γ` be an integral curve of `u(t₀, ·)` on an open preconnected set `s ⊆ ℝ` such that `p(t₀, ·)` and
`Φ` are differentiable at every point `γ(t)`, `t ∈ s`. Then for all `a, b ∈ s` the Bernoulli
function `|u|² / 2 + h + Φ` (at the time `t₀`) takes the same value at `γ(a)` and at `γ(b)`. Without
the enthalpy hypothesis the enthalpy field is independent data; see the
[polytropic example](polytropic-enthalpy-not-p-over-rho.md).
