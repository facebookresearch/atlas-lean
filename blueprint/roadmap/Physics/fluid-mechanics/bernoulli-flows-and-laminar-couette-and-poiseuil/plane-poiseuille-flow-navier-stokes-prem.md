# Differentiability conditions for plane Poiseuille flow

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `H`, [plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md)
satisfies the four differentiability conditions under which the conservative and the convective
Navier–Stokes equations are equivalent: the density and the velocity are differentiable in time at
every point, and the momentum density and the velocity are differentiable in space at every time.
