# Hagen–Poiseuille flow rate

For `μ > 0`, `R > 0` and all real `G` and `z`, the [flow rate](pipe-flow-rate.md) `Q` through the
cross-section `x₂ = z` (the integral of the axial velocity over the disk `x₀² + x₁² ≤ R²`) of
cylindrical Poiseuille flow is `Q = π G R⁴ / (8 μ)`; the polar change of variables turns the disk
integral into `2 π ∫_0^R r G (R² - r²) / (4 μ) dr`.
