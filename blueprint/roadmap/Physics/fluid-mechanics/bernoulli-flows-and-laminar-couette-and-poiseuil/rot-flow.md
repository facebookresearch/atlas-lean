# Rotating fluid under gravity with inviscid stress

For real `ρ`, `Ω` and `g`, the rotating fluid has the constant density `ρ`, the steady
[solid-body rotation](rot-velocity.md) `(-Ω x₁, Ω x₀, 0)`, the inviscid stress `-p I` with the
[pressure](rot-pressure.md) `p(x) = ρ Ω² (x₀² + x₁²) / 2 - ρ g x₂`, and the specific body force
`(0, 0, -g)`, gravity along `-x₂`. It [solves the Euler equations](rot-flow-euler.md).
