# Cylindrical Poiseuille flow with physical parameters

For `ρ > 0`, `μ > 0`, `G > 0`, `R > 0` and all real `λ` and `p₀`,
[cylindrical Poiseuille flow](pipe-poiseuille-flow.md) has positive density, satisfies the smooth
continuity equation, is smoothly incompressible and satisfies the Navier–Stokes equations in
conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance and Newtonian stress) with the pressure
`p₀ - G x₂`, on all of space; its velocity vanishes on the lateral wall `x₀² + x₁² = R²`, and on the
axis it is `(0, 0, G R² / (4 μ))`, with a positive axial component.
