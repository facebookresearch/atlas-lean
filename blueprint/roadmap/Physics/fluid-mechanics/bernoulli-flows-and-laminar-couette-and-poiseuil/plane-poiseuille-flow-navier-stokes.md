# Plane Poiseuille flow solves the Navier–Stokes equations

For `μ ≠ 0` and all real `ρ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G` and `H`,
[plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md) satisfies the Navier–Stokes equations in
conservative form (continuity, Cauchy momentum balance and Newtonian stress) with the pressure
`p₀ - G x₀`, the shear viscosity `μ` and the second viscosity `λ`.
