# Differentiability conditions for plane Couette flow

For all real `ρ`, `μ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `U_w` and `H`, [plane Couette flow](couette-flow.md) satisfies the
four differentiability conditions under which the conservative and the convective Navier–Stokes
equations are equivalent: the density and the velocity are differentiable in time at every point,
and the momentum density and the velocity are differentiable in space at every time.
