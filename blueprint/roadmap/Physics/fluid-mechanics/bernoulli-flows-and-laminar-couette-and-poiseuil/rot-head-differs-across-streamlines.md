# The Bernoulli head differs between streamlines of the rotating fluid

For `ρ > 0` and `Ω ≠ 0`, the head `p / ρ + |u|² / 2 + Φ` of the [rotating fluid](rot-flow.md), with
`Φ(x) = g x₂`, takes different values at the points `(1, 0, 0)` and `(2, 0, 0)`, on the circular
streamlines of radii `1` and `2`: the head is constant along each streamline, not across them.
