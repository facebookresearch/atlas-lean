# Derivatives in affine coordinates about an origin

Let `F` be a real normed space, `f` a map from three-dimensional space to `F`, and `o`, `p` points.
In the affine coordinates about the origin `o` (the point `o + v` has coordinates `v`), the
derivative of the coordinate expression `v ↦ f(o + v)` at the coordinates `p - o` of `p`, applied to
a coordinate vector `w`, is the derivative of `f` at `p` applied to the displacement with components
`w` in the standard basis.
