# Newtonian stress of plane Poiseuille flow

For `μ ≠ 0`, all real `ρ`, `λ`, `p₀`, `G`, `H` and every time and point `x`, the stress of
[plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md) is the matrix with rows
`(-(p₀ - G x₀), G (H - 2 x₁) / 2, 0)`, `(G (H - 2 x₁) / 2, -(p₀ - G x₀), 0)` and
`(0, 0, -(p₀ - G x₀))`: normal entries `-p`, shear entries `G (H - 2 x₁) / 2`, and no contribution
of the second viscosity.
