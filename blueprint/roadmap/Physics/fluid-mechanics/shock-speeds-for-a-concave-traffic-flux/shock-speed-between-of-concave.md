# Shock speed between the characteristic speeds for a concave flux

Let `kL < kR`, and let the flux `q` be concave on `[kL, kR]` and differentiable at `kL` and at `kR`.
Then the [shock speed](shock-speed.md) `s = (q(kR) - q(kL)) / (kR - kL)` lies between the
characteristic speeds: `q'(kR) ≤ s ≤ q'(kL)`.
