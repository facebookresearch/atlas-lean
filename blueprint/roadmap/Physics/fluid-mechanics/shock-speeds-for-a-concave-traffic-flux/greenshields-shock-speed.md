# Greenshields shock speed vf (1 − (kL + kR) / kj) for kL ≠ kR

Let `vf`, `kj`, `kL` and `kR` be real with `kj ≠ 0` and `kL ≠ kR`. For the
[Greenshields flux](../greenshields-fundamental-diagram/greenshields-flux.md)
`q(k) = vf k (1 - k / kj)`, the [shock speed](shock-speed.md) between the densities `kL` and `kR` is

`(q(kR) - q(kL)) / (kR - kL) = vf (1 - (kL + kR) / kj)`.

In particular the front is stationary when `kL + kR = kj`. In the physical reading `vf` and `kj` are
positive and the densities lie between `0` and `kj`; the identity holds for all real values with
`kj ≠ 0` and `kL ≠ kR`.
