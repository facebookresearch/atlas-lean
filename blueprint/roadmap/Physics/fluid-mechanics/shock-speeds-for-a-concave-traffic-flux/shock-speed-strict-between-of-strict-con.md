# Strict shock-speed bounds for a strictly concave flux

Let `kL < kR`, and let the flux `q` be strictly concave on `[kL, kR]` and differentiable at `kL` and
at `kR`. Then the [shock speed](shock-speed.md) `s = (q(kR) - q(kL)) / (kR - kL)` lies strictly
between the characteristic speeds, `q'(kR) < s < q'(kL)`: the Lax entropy condition.
