# Reynolds stress

For a measure $\mu$ on a space of realizations and a random velocity field $u$, the Reynolds stress per unit density at $(\tau, y)$ is the covariance $\mathbb E[u'_j u'_i]$ of the velocity components, with fluctuation $u' = u - \mathbb E[u]$. As a stress on the mean flow it enters as $-\rho\,\mathbb E[u'_j u'_i]$.
