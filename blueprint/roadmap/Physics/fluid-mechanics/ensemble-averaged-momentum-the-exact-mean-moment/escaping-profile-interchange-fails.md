# Mean and derivative do not commute without domination

On the probability space $(0, 1]$ with Lebesgue measure, every realization $s \mapsto f(s, \omega) = \frac{s^3}{\omega^2}e^{-s^2/\omega}$ is differentiable with derivative $\big(\frac{3s^2}{\omega^2} - \frac{2s^4}{\omega^3}\big)e^{-s^2/\omega}$, which vanishes at $s = 0$; the ensemble mean is $s\,e^{-s^2}$, whose derivative at $0$ is $1$; and no integrable function bounds the derivatives almost everywhere on any neighbourhood of $s = 0$.
