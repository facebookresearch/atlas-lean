# Sign of energy extraction by an eddy-viscosity closure

For a $3\times 3$ real matrix $R$ and rate of strain $S$, let the mean-flow extraction be $P = -\sum_{i,j} R_{ij}S_{ij}$, and let the modeled Reynolds stress be $R = \tfrac{2k}{3}I - 2\nu_t S$. If $S$ is trace-free and $S \ne 0$, then for every real $k$, $P \ge 0$ if and only if $\nu_t \ge 0$.
