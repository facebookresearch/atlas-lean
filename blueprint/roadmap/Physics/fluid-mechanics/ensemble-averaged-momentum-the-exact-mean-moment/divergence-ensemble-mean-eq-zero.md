# The ensemble mean of an incompressible flow is incompressible

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $\varepsilon > 0$. Suppose the velocity components at time $t$ are integrable at every point, almost every realization is differentiable at every point of the ball of radius $\varepsilon$ about $x$, the derivatives at $x$ are almost everywhere strongly measurable, and an integrable function $B$ bounds the norms of the derivatives on that ball almost everywhere. If almost every realization has $\operatorname{div} u = 0$ at $(t, x)$, then $\operatorname{div}\mathbb E[u] = 0$ at $(t, x)$.
