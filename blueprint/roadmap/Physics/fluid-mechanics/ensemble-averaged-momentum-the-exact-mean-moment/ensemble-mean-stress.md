# Ensemble mean of a stress field

For a measure $\mu$ on a space of realizations and a random stress field $\sigma(\omega, \tau, y)$ on $\mathbb R^{n+1}$, the ensemble mean stress has components $\int \sigma_{ji}(\omega, \tau, y)\,d\mu(\omega)$ (a Bochner integral, $0$ when the component is not integrable). It is the expectation $\mathbb E[\sigma_{ji}(\tau, y)]$ when $\mu$ is a probability measure and the component is integrable.
