# Boussinesq closure of the Reynolds stress

For a turbulent kinetic energy $k$, an eddy viscosity $\nu_t$ and a mean rate of strain $S$ (a $3 \times 3$ matrix), the Boussinesq closure of the Reynolds stress per unit density is $R_m = \frac{2k}{3} I - 2\nu_t S$. It is intended for $k \ge 0$ and symmetric trace-free $S$.
