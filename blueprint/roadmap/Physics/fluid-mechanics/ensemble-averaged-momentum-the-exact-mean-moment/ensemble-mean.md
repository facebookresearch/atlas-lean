# Ensemble mean of a velocity field

For a measure $\mu$ on a space of realizations and a random vector field $v(\omega, \tau, y)$ on $\mathbb R^{n+1}$, the ensemble mean is the field with components $\int v_i(\omega, \tau, y)\,d\mu(\omega)$ (a Bochner integral, $0$ when the component is not integrable). It is the expectation $\mathbb E[v_i(\tau, y)]$ when $\mu$ is a probability measure and the component is integrable; for a general measure it is only the integral (for the constant velocity $1$ on a one-point space of mass $2$ it is $2$).
