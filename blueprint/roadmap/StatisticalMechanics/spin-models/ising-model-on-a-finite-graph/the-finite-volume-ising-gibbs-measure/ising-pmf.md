# Finite-volume Ising Gibbs probability mass function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The finite-volume Ising Gibbs distribution is the
probability mass function on spin configurations that gives each configuration $s$ its
[Gibbs probability](ising-prob.md) $e^{-\beta H(s)}/Z$.
