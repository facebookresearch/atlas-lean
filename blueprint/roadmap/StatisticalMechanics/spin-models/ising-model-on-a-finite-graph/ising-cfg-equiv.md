# Configuration equivalence induced by a vertex relabeling

Let $V$ and $W$ be finite sets and $\varphi \colon V \to W$ a bijection. Composition with $\varphi$,
$\omega \mapsto \omega \circ \varphi$, is a bijection from the spin configurations on $W$ to the spin
configurations on $V$, with inverse $\omega \mapsto \omega \circ \varphi^{-1}$.
