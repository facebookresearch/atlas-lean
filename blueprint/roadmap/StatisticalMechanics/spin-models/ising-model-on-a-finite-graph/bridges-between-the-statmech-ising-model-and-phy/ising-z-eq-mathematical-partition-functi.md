# Ising partition function as the canonical partition function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and a temperature $T \ge 0$, with inverse temperature $\beta(T) = 1/(k_B T)$, $k_B > 0$ the Boltzmann constant, and $\beta(0) = 0$ (so $T = 0$ gives the uniform distribution on configurations, not the zero-temperature limit). The
[Ising partition function](../the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-z.md) at inverse temperature
$\beta(T)$ equals the partition function $\sum_s e^{-\beta(T) H(s)}$ (the integral of
$e^{-\beta(T) H}$ against the counting measure) of the [Ising canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md)
at temperature $T$.
