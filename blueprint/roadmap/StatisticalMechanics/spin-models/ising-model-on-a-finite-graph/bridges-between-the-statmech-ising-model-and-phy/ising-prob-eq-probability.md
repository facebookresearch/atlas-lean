# Ising Gibbs probability as the canonical probability

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $h \in \mathbb{R}$, $k_B > 0$ any value of the Boltzmann constant (that is, in any system of units) and $T \ge 0$ a temperature, with inverse temperature $\beta(T) = 1/(k_B T)$ and $\beta(0) = 0$ (so $T = 0$ gives the uniform distribution on configurations, not the zero-temperature limit). For every configuration $s$, the
[Gibbs probability](../the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-prob.md) of $s$ at inverse temperature
$\beta(T)$ equals the canonical probability $e^{-\beta(T) H(s)}/Z$ of $s$ in the
[Ising canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) at temperature $T$ and Boltzmann constant $k_B$, with $Z$ its
partition function.
