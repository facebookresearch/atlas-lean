# Pressure from independent copies

For a finite nonempty set $\Omega$, an energy $E \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$,
the pressure is

$$p = \inf_{n \ge 1} \frac{\log Z_n}{n},$$

with $Z_n$ the [partition function of $n$ independent copies](zpow.md); since $n \mapsto \log Z_n$ is
subadditive, this is also the limit of $\log Z_n / n$ as $n \to \infty$ (Fekete's lemma).
