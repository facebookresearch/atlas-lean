# Log-partition sequence of independent copies

For a finite set $\Omega$, an energy $E \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the
log-partition sequence is $n \mapsto \log Z_n$, where $Z_n$ is the
[partition function of $n$ independent copies](zpow.md) of the system, with $\log 0 = 0$. For nonempty
$\Omega$, $\log Z_n = n \log Z_1$. For empty $\Omega$, $Z_0 = 1$ and $Z_n = 0$ for $n \ge 1$, so the sequence
is identically $0$.
