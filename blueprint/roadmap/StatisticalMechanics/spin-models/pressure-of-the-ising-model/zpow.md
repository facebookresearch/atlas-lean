# Partition function of independent copies

For a finite set $\Omega$, an energy $E \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, the partition function of $n$ independent copies of the system is the
[partition function](z.md) on $\Omega^n$ of the energy $(s_1, \dots, s_n) \mapsto \sum_{i=1}^n E(s_i)$.
