# The finite planar classical O(n) Gibbs law

On a finite vertex set of $\mathbb Z^2$ count each chosen nearest-neighbor edge by its positive direction. At every vertex take a unit vector in real dimension $n$, with normalized sphere probability, independently before tilting. Given couplings $b_e$, the interaction is $\sum_e b_e\langle\sigma_{e_-},\sigma_{e_+}\rangle$. The finite Gibbs law reweights the product sphere reference by the exponential of this interaction divided by its integral. The correlation of a vertex pair is the expectation of the inner product under this law; planar distances in the decay estimate are ambient Euclidean distances.
