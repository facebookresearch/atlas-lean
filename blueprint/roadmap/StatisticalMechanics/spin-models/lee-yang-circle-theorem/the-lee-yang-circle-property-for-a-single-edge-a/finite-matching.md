# Perfect matching with n edges

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the perfect matching with $n$ edges is the graph on the
[vertex set](matching-vertex.md) of $n$ disjoint copies of $\{0, 1\}$ formed by $n$ disjoint
[single edges](two-vertex-edge.md): the graph with no vertices for $n = 0$, and the disjoint union of a
single edge with the matching with $n - 1$ edges for $n \ge 1$.
