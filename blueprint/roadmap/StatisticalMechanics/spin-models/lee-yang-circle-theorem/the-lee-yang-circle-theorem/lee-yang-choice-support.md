# Set of variables selected by a choice

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For a choice $x = (a, \tau)$ of a Boolean $a_v$ for every vertex $v$ and a pair of Booleans $\tau_e = (\tau_e^0, \tau_e^1)$ for every edge $e$, the support of $x$ is the set of variables it
[selects](lee-yang-choice-selected.md): the variables appearing in the monomial of the
[term](lee-yang-choice-term.md) selected by $x$.
