# Half-edge variable of an incidence

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. Among the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$), the half-edge variable of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$ is
$w_{v, e}$.
