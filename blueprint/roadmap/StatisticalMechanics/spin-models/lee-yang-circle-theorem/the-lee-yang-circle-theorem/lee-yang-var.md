# Variables of the half-edge polynomial

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph. The variables of the half-edge polynomial are indexed by the
disjoint union $V \sqcup (V \times E)$: one anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$, and one half-edge
variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$; only the variables with $v$ an endpoint
of $e$ occur in the polynomial.
