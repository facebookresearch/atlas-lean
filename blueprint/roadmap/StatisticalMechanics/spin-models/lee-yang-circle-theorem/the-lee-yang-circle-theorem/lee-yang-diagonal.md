# Diagonal point with anchors at q and half-edge variables at zero

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $q \in \mathbb{C}$. The diagonal point at $q$ is the point of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$)
with every anchor variable $z_v$ equal to $q$ and every half-edge variable $w_{v, e}$ equal to $0$.
