# Successive Asano contraction along a list of incidences

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, let $L = ((v_1, e_1), \dots, (v_k, e_k))$ be a list of pairs of a vertex and an edge,
and let $p$ be a function of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$). The successive contraction of $p$ along $L$ applies, in
the order of $L$, the [Asano contraction](asano-contract.md) of the anchor variable $z_{v_l}$ and the
half-edge variable $w_{v_l, e_l}$, for $l = 1, \dots, k$.
