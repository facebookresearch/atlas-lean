# Vertex part of the half-edge polynomial

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. At a point $z$ of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$), the vertex part of the
[half-edge polynomial](lee-yang-half-edge-product.md) is $\prod_{v \in V} (1 + z_v)$.
