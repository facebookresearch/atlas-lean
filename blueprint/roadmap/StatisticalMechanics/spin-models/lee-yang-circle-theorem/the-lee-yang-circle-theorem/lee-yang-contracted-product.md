# Half-edge polynomial with every incidence contracted

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. The contracted half-edge polynomial is obtained from the
[half-edge polynomial](lee-yang-half-edge-product.md) by
[successive Asano contraction](lee-yang-contract-list.md) along a fixed enumeration of all the
[incidences](lee-yang-incidence-finset.md) $(v, e)$ of $G$, each half-edge variable $w_{v, e}$ being
contracted into the anchor variable $z_v$.
