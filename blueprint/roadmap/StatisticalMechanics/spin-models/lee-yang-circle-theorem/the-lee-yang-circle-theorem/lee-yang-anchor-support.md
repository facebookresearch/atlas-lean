# Anchor variables of the vertices with plus spin

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For a spin configuration $\sigma \colon V \to \{\text{true}, \text{false}\}$, the anchor
support of $\sigma$ is the set of anchor variables $z_v$ of the vertices $v$ with $\sigma_v$ true; it
contains no half-edge variable.
