# Term of a vertex factor selected by a Boolean choice

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $z$ be a point of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$). For a vertex $v$ and a Boolean $b$, the term
of the vertex factor $1 + z_v$ selected by $b$ is $z_v$ if $b$ is true and $1$ if $b$ is false.
