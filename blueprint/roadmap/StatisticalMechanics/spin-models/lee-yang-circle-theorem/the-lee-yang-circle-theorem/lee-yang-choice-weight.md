# Coefficient of a term of the expanded half-edge polynomial

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For a choice $x = (a, \tau)$ of a Boolean $a_v$ for every vertex $v$ and a pair of Booleans $\tau_e = (\tau_e^0, \tau_e^1)$ for every edge $e$, the coefficient of the [term](lee-yang-choice-term.md) selected by
$x$ is $\prod_{e \in E} c_e$ with $c_e = 1$ if $\tau_e^0 = \tau_e^1$ and $c_e = e^{-2\beta}$ otherwise: a
factor $e^{-2\beta}$ for every edge whose two choices differ.
