# Multi-affine function of several complex variables

Let $I$ be a set of indices. A function $p \colon \mathbb{C}^I \to \mathbb{C}$ is multi-affine when it is
affine in each variable separately: for every $i \in I$, $z \in \mathbb{C}^I$ and $t \in \mathbb{C}$,
$p(z_{i \to t}) = p(z_{i \to 0}) + t \bigl(p(z_{i \to 1}) - p(z_{i \to 0})\bigr)$, where $z_{i \to t}$ is
$z$ with its $i$-th coordinate replaced by $t$.
