# Ferromagnetic Lee–Yang polynomial has no zero in the open unit disk

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \ge 0$. For every $q \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|q| < 1$, the
[Lee–Yang polynomial](../the-lee-yang-polynomial-of-a-finite-ising-model/lee-yang-complex-polynomial.md)
of $G$ at $\beta$ does not vanish at $q$.
