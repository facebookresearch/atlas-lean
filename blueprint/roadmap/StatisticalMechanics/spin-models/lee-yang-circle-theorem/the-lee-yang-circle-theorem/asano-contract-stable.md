# Asano's contraction lemma

Let $I$ be a set of indices and $p \colon \mathbb{C}^I \to \mathbb{C}$ a [multi-affine](is-multi-affine.md),
[polydisc-stable](polydisc-stable.md) function, and let $i \ne j$ be indices. Then the
[Asano contraction](asano-contract.md) of $p$ in the variables $i$ and $j$ is polydisc stable (Asano,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 24 (1970)).
