# Lee–Yang circle theorem

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \ge 0$. Then $G$ has the
[Lee–Yang circle property](../the-lee-yang-circle-property-for-a-single-edge-a/has-lee-yang-circle.md)
at $\beta$: for the Ising model on $G$ with uniform ferromagnetic coupling, every complex zero of the
partition function as a polynomial in the fugacity $z = e^{-2\beta h}$ (the
[Lee–Yang polynomial](../the-lee-yang-polynomial-of-a-finite-ising-model/lee-yang-complex-polynomial.md))
lies on the unit circle (Lee and Yang, *Statistical theory of equations of state and phase transitions.
II*, Phys. Rev. 87 (1952)).
