# Real disagreement weight of a spin configuration

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $u_e, v_e$ the [chosen endpoints](lee-yang-endpoint0.md) of $e$. For a spin configuration $\sigma$ on $V$, the disagreement
weight is $\prod_{e \in E} c_e$, with $c_e = 1$ if $\sigma_{u_e} = \sigma_{v_e}$ and $c_e = e^{-2\beta}$
otherwise.
