# Asano contraction of two variables

Let $I$ be a set of indices, $p \colon \mathbb{C}^I \to \mathbb{C}$ and $i, j \in I$. The Asano
contraction of $p$ in the variables $i$ and $j$ is the function

$$z \mapsto p(z^{00}) + z_i \bigl(p(z^{11}) - p(z^{10}) - p(z^{01}) + p(z^{00})\bigr),$$

where $z^{ab}$ is $z$ with $z_i$ replaced by $a$ and then $z_j$ replaced by $b$. For
$p = A + B z_i + C z_j + D z_i z_j$, with $A, B, C, D$ not depending on $z_i$ and $z_j$, and $i \ne j$,
it is $A + D z_i$.
