# Edge choices induced by a spin configuration

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $u_e, v_e$ the [chosen endpoints](lee-yang-endpoint0.md) of $e$. A spin configuration $\sigma$ on $V$
induces the edge choices $\tau_e = (\sigma_{u_e}, \sigma_{v_e})$: each edge copies the spins of its two
endpoints.
