# Edge part of the half-edge polynomial

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. At a point $z$ of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$), the edge part of the
[half-edge polynomial](lee-yang-half-edge-product.md) is the product over the edges $e \in E$ of the
[edge factors](lee-yang-normalized-edge-factor.md) $1 + w_{u,e} w_{v,e} + e^{-2\beta}(w_{u,e} + w_{v,e})$,
$e = \{u, v\}$.
