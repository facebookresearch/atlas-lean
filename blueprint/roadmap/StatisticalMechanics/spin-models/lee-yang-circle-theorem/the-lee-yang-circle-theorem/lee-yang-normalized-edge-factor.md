# Edge factor in half-edge variables

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For an edge $e = \{u, v\} \in E$ and a point of the [variables](lee-yang-var.md) of the half-edge polynomial (an anchor variable $z_v$ for each vertex $v$ and a variable $w_{v,e}$ for each pair of a vertex $v$ and an edge $e$), the edge factor of
$e$ is $1 + w_{u,e} w_{v,e} + e^{-2\beta}(w_{u,e} + w_{v,e})$; it is symmetric in $u$ and $v$.
