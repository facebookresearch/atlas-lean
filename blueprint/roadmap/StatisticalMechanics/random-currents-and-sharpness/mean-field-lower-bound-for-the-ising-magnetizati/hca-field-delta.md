# Field term $\Delta_z$ of the magnetization derivative

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $o, z \in V$, and let $G^+$ be the [graph with a ghost vertex](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/with-ghost.md) $\mathfrak g$ joined to every vertex, with coupling $1$ on the edges of $G$ and $h$ on the ghost edges; currents, sources and weights are taken on
$G^+$ at inverse temperature $\beta$. The field term is

$$\Delta_z = \sum_{\partial n_1 = \{o\} \triangle \{z\},\ \partial n_2 = \emptyset} w(n_1)\, w(n_2)\, \mathbf 1_{o \not\leftrightarrow \mathfrak g \text{ in } n_1 + n_2},$$

with $\{o\} \,\triangle\, \{z\}$ the [field source set](hca-field-source.md).
