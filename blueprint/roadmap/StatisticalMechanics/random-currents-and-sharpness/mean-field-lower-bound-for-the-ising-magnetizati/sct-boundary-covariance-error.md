# Boundary covariance error

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, with the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, the boundary covariance error is

$$\sum_{x \in \Lambda_n} \#\{y \notin \Lambda_n : y \sim x\} \cdot \bigl(\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_0 \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle\bigr),$$

the expectations taken in the free-boundary Ising model on the [nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$](sct-box-graph.md) at $(\beta, h)$.
