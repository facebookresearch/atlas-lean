# Boundary correlation of an edge crossing a cut

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, a set $T$ of sites of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and an unordered pair $\{x, y\}$ of sites of
$\Lambda_n$, this is $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T$ if $x \in T$ and $y \notin T$, $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_y \rangle_T$ if $y \in T$
and $x \notin T$, and $0$ otherwise, with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T$ the [zero-field two-point function in $T$](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/corr-origin-inner.md) (the free-boundary correlation at inverse temperature $\beta$ on the subgraph induced by $T$, extended by $0$ when $x \notin T$ or $0 \notin T$).
