# Origin magnetization with the outer-layer field switched off

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, this is the expectation of $\sigma_0$ in the Ising model on the
nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$ with coupling $\beta$ on every edge and the [inner field](sct-inner-field.md): $\beta h$
at the sites of $\Lambda_{n \dot- 1}$ (with $n \dot- 1 = \max(n - 1, 0)$) and $0$ on the outer layer.
