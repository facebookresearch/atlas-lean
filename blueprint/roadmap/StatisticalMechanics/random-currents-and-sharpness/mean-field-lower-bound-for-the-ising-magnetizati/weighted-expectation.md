# Gibbs expectation with general couplings and a uniform field

For a finite simple graph $G$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, couplings $J$, $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and a function $f$ of the
configurations, this is $\sum_\sigma f(\sigma)\, w(\sigma) / Z$, with $w$ the [Boltzmann weight](weighted-weight.md) and $Z$
the [partition function](weighted-z.md).
