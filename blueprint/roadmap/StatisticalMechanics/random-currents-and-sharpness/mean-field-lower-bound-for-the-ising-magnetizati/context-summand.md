# Outside summand of a gated double-current sum

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $S \subseteq V$ and $P$ a property of currents; a current $n$ assigns a multiplicity $n_e \in \mathbb{N}$ to every edge $e \in E$, its [sources](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/sources.md) $\partial n$ are the vertices of odd total incident multiplicity, and its [weight](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/weight.md) is $w(n) = \prod_{e \in E} (\beta J_e)^{n_e}/n_e!$. For a
current $b$ on the edges of $G$ not having both endpoints in $S$, with [extension by zero](b-full.md) $\bar b$, and a current $n$ on all edges,
this is $\mathbf 1_{\partial \bar b = \emptyset}\, w(\bar b) \cdot \mathbf 1_{\partial n = \emptyset}\, w(n) \cdot \mathbf 1_{P(\bar b + n)}$.
