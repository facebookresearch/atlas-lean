# Regrouping a split pair of currents

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $S \subseteq V$. A pair of currents, each split into its part $a_i$ on the edges of $G$ with both endpoints in $S$ and its part $b_i$ on
the other edges, is regrouped as the pair of inside parts and the pair of outside parts:
$((a_1, b_1), (a_2, b_2)) \mapsto ((a_1, a_2), (b_1, b_2))$. This is a bijection.
