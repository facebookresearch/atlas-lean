# Bond term $\Delta_e$ of the magnetization derivative

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $o \in V$ and $e$ a pair of vertices, and let $G^+$ be the [graph with a ghost vertex](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/with-ghost.md) $\mathfrak g$ joined to every vertex, with coupling $1$ on the edges of $G$ and $h$ on the ghost edges; currents, sources and
weights are taken on $G^+$ at inverse temperature $\beta$. The bond term is

$$\Delta_e = \sum_{\partial n_1 = S_e,\ \partial n_2 = \emptyset} w(n_1)\, w(n_2)\, \mathbf 1_{o \not\leftrightarrow \mathfrak g \text{ in } n_1 + n_2},$$

with $S_e$ the [ghost source set](hca-bond-source.md) of $e$.
