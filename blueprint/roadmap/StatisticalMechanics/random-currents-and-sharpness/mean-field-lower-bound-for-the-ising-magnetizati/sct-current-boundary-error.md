# Boundary error of the finite-volume mean-field inequality

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, the boundary error is

$$\varepsilon_n = \sum_{T \subseteq \Lambda_n,\ 0 \in T} \mathrm{ext}(T)\, \mathrm{cut}(T),$$

with $\mathrm{ext}(T)$ the [external boundary correlation](sct-external-boundary-corr.md) of $T$ and $\mathrm{cut}(T)$ the [cut mass](sct-current-cut-mass.md) of $T$.
