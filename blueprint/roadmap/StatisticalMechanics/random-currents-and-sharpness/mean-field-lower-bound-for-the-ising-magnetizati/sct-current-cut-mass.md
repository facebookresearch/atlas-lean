# Cut mass of a box set rooted at the field ghost

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and a set $T$ of sites of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, this is the
[random-current cut mass](ising-cut-mass.md) of $T$ on the nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$ with a ghost vertex
$\mathfrak g$ joined to every site, coupling $1$ on the box edges and $h$ on the ghost edges, rooted at the ghost: with $Z$ the
sourceless current sum,

$$\frac{1}{Z^2} \sum_{\partial n_1 = \partial n_2 = \emptyset} w(n_1)\, w(n_2)\, \mathbf 1_{\{v : v \not\leftrightarrow \mathfrak g \text{ in } n_1 + n_2\} = T}.$$
