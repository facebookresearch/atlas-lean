# Reassembly of a current from its inside and outside parts

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $S \subseteq V$. For a current $a$ on the edges of $G$ with both endpoints in $S$ and a current $b$ on the other edges, this is the
current on all edges of $G$ equal to $a$ on the edges inside $S$ and to $b$ on the others.
