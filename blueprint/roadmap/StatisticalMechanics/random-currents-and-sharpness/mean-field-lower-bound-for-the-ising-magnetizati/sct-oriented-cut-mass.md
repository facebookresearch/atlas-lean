# Oriented cut mass over cuts $T \ni 0, x$ with $y \notin T$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and sites $x, y$ of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, this is

$$O(x, y) = \sum_{T \subseteq \Lambda_n,\ 0, x \in T,\ y \notin T} \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T\, \mathrm{cut}(T),$$

with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T$ the [zero-field two-point function in $T$](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/corr-origin-inner.md) (the free-boundary correlation at inverse temperature $\beta$ on the subgraph induced by $T$, extended by $0$ when $x \notin T$ or $0 \notin T$) and $\mathrm{cut}(T)$ the [cut mass](sct-current-cut-mass.md) of $T$.
