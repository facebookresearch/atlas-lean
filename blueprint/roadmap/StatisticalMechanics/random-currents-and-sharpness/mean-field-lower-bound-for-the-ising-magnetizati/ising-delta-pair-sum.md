# Four-source double-current sum with a connection pattern

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $o, x, y, g \in V$, with $\Sigma_P(A, B) = \sum_{\partial n_1 = A,\ \partial n_2 = B} w(n_1)\, w(n_2)\, \mathbf 1_{P(n_1 + n_2)}$ the [gated double-current sum](gated-source-pair-sum.md) and $u \leftrightarrow v$ in $n$ means that $u$ and $v$ are joined by a path of edges carrying positive current. The
pattern sum is

$$\Delta(o, x, y, g) = \Sigma_P\bigl(\{o, g\} \,\triangle\, \{x, y\},\ \emptyset\bigr), \qquad P(n) = \bigl(o \leftrightarrow x,\ y \leftrightarrow g \text{ and } o \not\leftrightarrow g \text{ in } n\bigr).$$
