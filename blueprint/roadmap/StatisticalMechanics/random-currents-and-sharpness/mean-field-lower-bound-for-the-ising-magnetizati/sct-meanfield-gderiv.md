# $\beta$-derivative of the box mean-field function

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this is

$$2\beta \bigl(1 - m_n^2\bigr) - \beta^2\, (m_n^2)',$$

the derivative of $g_n(\beta) = \beta^2(1 - m_n(\beta, h)^2)$, where $m_n = m_n(\beta, h)$ is the
[origin magnetization](sct-origin-mag.md) and $(m_n^2)'$ the [$\beta$-derivative of $m_n^2$](sct-origin-mag-sq-beta-deriv.md).
