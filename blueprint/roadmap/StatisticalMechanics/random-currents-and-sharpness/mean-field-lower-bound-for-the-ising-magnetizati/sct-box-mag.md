# Free-boundary site magnetization in a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and a site $x$ of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, this is the magnetization $\langle \sigma_x \rangle$ in
the free-boundary Ising model on the [nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$](sct-box-graph.md) at $(\beta, h)$.
