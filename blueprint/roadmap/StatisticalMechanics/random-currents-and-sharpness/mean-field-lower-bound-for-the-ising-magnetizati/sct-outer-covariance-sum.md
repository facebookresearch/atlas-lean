# Covariance sum over the outer layer

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, this is the sum of the [covariances](sct-box-covariance.md)
$\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_0 \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle$ in the free-boundary Ising model on the [nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$](sct-box-graph.md) at $(\beta, h)$, over the sites $x$ of the outer
layer $\Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n \dot- 1}$ (empty for $n = 0$).
