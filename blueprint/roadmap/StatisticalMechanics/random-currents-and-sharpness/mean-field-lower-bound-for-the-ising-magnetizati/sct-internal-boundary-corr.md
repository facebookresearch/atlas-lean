# Internal boundary correlation of a cut in the box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and a set $T$ of sites of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, this is the sum, over the edges of the
nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$, of their [boundary correlations](sct-edge-boundary-corr.md) for the cut $T$.
