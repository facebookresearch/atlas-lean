# $\beta$-derivative of the origin magnetization in a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this is the covariance
$\langle \sigma_0 (-H) \rangle - \langle \sigma_0 \rangle \langle -H \rangle$ in the free-boundary Ising model on the [nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$](sct-box-graph.md) at $(\beta, h)$, where
$-H(\sigma) = \sum_{\{x, y\}} \sigma_x \sigma_y + h \sum_x \sigma_x$ (sum over the edges of the box graph); it is the
$\beta$-derivative of the origin magnetization.
