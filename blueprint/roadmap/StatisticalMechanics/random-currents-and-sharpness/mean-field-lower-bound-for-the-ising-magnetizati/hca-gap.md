# Switching gap of a current

Let $H$ be a finite simple graph, $m$ a current on its edges, viewed as the multigraph with $m_e$ parallel copies of
each edge $e$, $A$ a set of vertices and $u, v$ vertices. If the sources of $m$ (the vertices of odd degree in this
multigraph) form $A$, the switching gap is the number of sets $K$ of edge copies whose odd-degree vertices form
$A$, minus the number of those whose odd-degree vertices form $A \,\triangle\, \{u, v\}$; otherwise it is $0$.
