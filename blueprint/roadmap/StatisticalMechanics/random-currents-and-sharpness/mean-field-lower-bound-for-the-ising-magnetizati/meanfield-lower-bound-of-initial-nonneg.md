# Integrated mean-field lower bound without an initial condition

Let $\beta_0 > 0$ and $m \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be such that $m^2$ is differentiable at every $\beta \ge \beta_0$,

$$(m^2)'(\beta) \ge \frac{2}{\beta} \bigl(1 - m(\beta)^2\bigr) \quad \text{for } \beta > \beta_0,$$

and $m(\beta) \ge 0$ for $\beta \ge \beta_0$. Then $m(\beta) \ge \sqrt{1 - (\beta_0/\beta)^2}$ for every $\beta \ge \beta_0$. No value of
$m(\beta_0)$ is required, unlike in the [version with $m(\beta_0) = 0$](meanfield-lower-bound.md).
