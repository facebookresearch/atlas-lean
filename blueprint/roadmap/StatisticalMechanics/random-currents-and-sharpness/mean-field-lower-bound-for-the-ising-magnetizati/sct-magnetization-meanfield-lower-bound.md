# Mean-field lower bound for the spontaneous magnetization

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $B = \{\beta \ge 0 : \varphi_\beta(S) < 1 \text{ for some finite } S \ni 0\}$, with $\varphi_\beta$ the
[Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/phi-ising.md). If $B$ is bounded above and $\tilde\beta_c = \sup B > 0$, then for
every $\beta \ge \tilde\beta_c$ the [spontaneous magnetization](../../gibbs-states-and-phase-transitions/phase-transition-of-the-ising-model/magnetization.md)
satisfies $m^*(\beta) \ge \sqrt{1 - (\tilde\beta_c/\beta)^2}$ (H. Duminil-Copin and V. Tassion, *A new proof of the sharpness of the phase transition for Bernoulli percolation and the Ising model*, Comm. Math. Phys. 343 (2016) 725–745).
