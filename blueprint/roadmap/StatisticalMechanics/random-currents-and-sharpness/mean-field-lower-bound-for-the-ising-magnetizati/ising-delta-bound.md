# Four-source lower bound for the pattern sum

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \ge 0$ and $J_e \ge 0$ for every pair $e$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle_{\beta, J}$ the zero-field [Ising expectation with couplings](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/expectation-j.md) $J$. For pairwise distinct
$o, x, y, g \in V$ with $\langle \sigma_y \sigma_g \rangle_{\beta, J} > 0$, the [pattern sum](ising-delta-pair-sum.md) satisfies

$$\Delta(o, x, y, g) \ge \langle \sigma_y \sigma_g \rangle_{\beta, J}^{-1}\ L(o, x, y, g),$$

with $L$ the [lower mass](ising-delta-lower-mass.md) (M. Aizenman, D. J. Barsky and R. Fernández, *The phase transition in a general class of Ising-type models is sharp*, J. Stat. Phys. 47 (1987) 343–374).
