# Boltzmann weight with general couplings and a uniform field

For a finite simple graph $G$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, couplings $J$ and $h \in \mathbb{R}$, the Boltzmann weight of a spin
configuration $\sigma$ is $\exp(\beta E(\sigma))$, with $E$ the [energy](weighted-energy.md)
$\sum_{e \in E} J_e \sigma_e + h \sum_x \sigma_x$.
