# Gap-weighted current sum restricted to a predicate

Let $H$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $A$ a set of vertices, $u, v$ vertices and $P$ a
property of currents. This is $\sum_m \mathbf 1_{P(m)}\, \mathrm{gap}(m)\, w(m)$, the sum over all currents $m$ on the edges
of $H$ of the [switching gap](hca-gap.md) of $m$ (for $A, u, v$) times its weight
$w(m) = \prod_e (\beta J_e)^{m_e}/m_e!$, restricted to the currents satisfying $P$.
