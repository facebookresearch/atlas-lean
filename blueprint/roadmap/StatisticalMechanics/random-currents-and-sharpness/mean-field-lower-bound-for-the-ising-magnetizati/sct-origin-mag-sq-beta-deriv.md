# $\beta$-derivative of the squared origin magnetization in a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this is $2 m_n(\beta, h)\, m_n'$, with $m_n(\beta, h) = \langle \sigma_0 \rangle_{\Lambda_n, \beta, h}$ the [origin magnetization](sct-origin-mag.md) in the free-boundary box and $m_n'$ its
[$\beta$-derivative](sct-origin-mag-beta-deriv.md).
