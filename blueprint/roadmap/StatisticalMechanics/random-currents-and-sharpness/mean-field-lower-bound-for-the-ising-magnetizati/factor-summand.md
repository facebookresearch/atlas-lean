# Inside weight with prescribed sources

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $S, A \subseteq V$; a current $n$ assigns a multiplicity $n_e \in \mathbb{N}$ to every edge $e \in E$, its [sources](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/sources.md) $\partial n$ are the vertices of odd total incident multiplicity, and its [weight](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/weight.md) is $w(n) = \prod_{e \in E} (\beta J_e)^{n_e}/n_e!$. For a current $a$ on
the edges of $G$ with both endpoints in $S$, with [extension by zero](a-full.md) $\bar a$, this is $w(\bar a)$ if $\partial \bar a = A$ and $0$ otherwise.
