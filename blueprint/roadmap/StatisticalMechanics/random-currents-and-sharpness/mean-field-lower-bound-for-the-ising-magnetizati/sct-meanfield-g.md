# Box mean-field function $\beta^2(1 - \langle\sigma_0\rangle^2)$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this is $g_n(\beta) = \beta^2 (1 - m_n(\beta, h)^2)$, with $m_n(\beta, h) = \langle \sigma_0 \rangle_{\Lambda_n, \beta, h}$ the [origin magnetization](sct-origin-mag.md) in the free-boundary box.
