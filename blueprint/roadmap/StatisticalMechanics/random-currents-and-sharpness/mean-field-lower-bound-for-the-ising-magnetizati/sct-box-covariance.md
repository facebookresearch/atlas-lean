# Origin–site covariance in a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and a site $x$ of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, this is the covariance
$\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_0 \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle$ in the free-boundary Ising model on the [nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$](sct-box-graph.md) at $(\beta, h)$.
