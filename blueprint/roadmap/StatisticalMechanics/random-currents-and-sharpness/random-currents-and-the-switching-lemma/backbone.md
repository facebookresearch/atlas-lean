# Canonical backbone path of a current

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $n$ a current and $x, y \in V$. Given a linear order on the [backbone paths](is-backbone-path.md) from $x$ to
$y$ (the paths in the subgraph of edges with odd current) and assuming one exists, the canonical backbone path
is the least of them for that order.
