# Triple-current sum with prescribed sources

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $A, B, C \subseteq V$; a current $n$ assigns a multiplicity $n_e \in \mathbb{N}$ to every edge, its [sources](sources.md) $\partial n$ are the vertices of odd total incident multiplicity, and its [weight](weight.md) is $w(n) = \prod_{e \in E} (\beta J_e)^{n_e}/n_e!$. This is
$\sum_{\partial n_1 = A,\ \partial n_2 = B,\ \partial n_3 = C} w(n_1)\, w(n_2)\, w(n_3)$, over triples of currents on the edges of
$G$.
