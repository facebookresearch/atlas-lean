# Backbone weight $\rho(\omega)$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $x, y \in V$ and $\omega$ a path from $x$ to $y$. The backbone weight is
$\rho(\omega) = Z_\omega / Z(\partial = \emptyset)$, with $Z_\omega$ the [weight of the currents with selected backbone
$\omega$](shb-backbone-num.md) and $Z(\partial = \emptyset)$ the sourceless [current sum](current-sum.md).
