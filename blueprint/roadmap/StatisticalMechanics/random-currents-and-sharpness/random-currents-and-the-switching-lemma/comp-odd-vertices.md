# Odd-degree vertices in a cluster

Let $V$ be a finite set and $\iota$ a finite index set with an endpoint map $i \mapsto \mathrm{ends}(i)$ to unordered pairs of $V$ (a multigraph). For $m \subseteq \iota$ and $u \in V$, these are the vertices of the [cluster](comp-of.md) of $u$ in $m$ that
have odd [degree](deg-k.md) in $m$.
