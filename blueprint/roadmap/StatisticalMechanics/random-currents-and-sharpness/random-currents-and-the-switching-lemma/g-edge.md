# Term of the exponential expansion of an edge weight

For a finite set $V$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, couplings $J$, a spin configuration $\sigma$, a pair $e = \{x, y\}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, this is
$(\beta J_e)^k (\sigma_x \sigma_y)^k / k!$, the $k$-th term of the expansion of $\exp(\beta J_e \sigma_x \sigma_y)$.
