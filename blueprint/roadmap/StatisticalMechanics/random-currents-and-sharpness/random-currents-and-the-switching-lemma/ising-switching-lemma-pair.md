# Switching lemma

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs, $n_1, n_2$ currents and $u \ne v$ vertices connected through
the pairs $e \in E$ with $n_1(e) + n_2(e)$ odd. Then for every $A \subseteq V$, the currents $n \le n_1 + n_2$ (pointwise) with
sources $\partial n = A \,\triangle\, \{u, v\}$ are exactly as many as those with sources $\partial n = A$, the sources being computed
with the incident flux over $E$ (R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970); M. Aizenman, *Geometric analysis of $\varphi^4$ fields and Ising models*, Comm. Math. Phys. 86 (1982) 1–48).
