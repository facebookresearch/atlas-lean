# Connectivity in a multigraph edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set and $\iota$ a finite index set with an endpoint map $i \mapsto \mathrm{ends}(i)$ to unordered pairs of $V$ (a multigraph). For $K \subseteq \iota$, connectivity through $K$ is the reflexive and transitive closure of
[adjacency through $K$](adj-step.md): $a$ and $b$ are connected when $a = b$ or they are joined by a chain of
adjacencies.
