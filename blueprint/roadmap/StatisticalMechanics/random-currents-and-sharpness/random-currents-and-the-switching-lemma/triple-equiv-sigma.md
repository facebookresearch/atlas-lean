# Reparametrizing triples of functions by their sum and two summands

For a set $E$, triples $(k, l, n)$ of functions $E \to \mathbb{N}$ correspond bijectively to the data of $m = k + l + n$ and
of the pair $(k, l)$ with $k + l \le m$ pointwise, the inverse sending $(m, (k, l))$ to $(k, l, m - k - l)$.
