# Weighted sum over splittings with prescribed sources

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $m$ a current, $F \in \mathbb{R}$ and $A \subseteq V$. With the
[weight](weight.md) $w$, this is

$$\sum_{K \le m,\ \partial K = A} F\, w(K)\, w(m - K),$$

the sum over the currents $K \le m$ (pointwise) with [sources](sources.md) $A$.
