# Degree of a vertex in a multigraph edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set and $\iota$ a finite index set with an endpoint map $i \mapsto \mathrm{ends}(i)$ to unordered pairs of $V$ (a multigraph). For $T \subseteq \iota$ and $x \in V$, the degree of $x$ in $T$ is the number of $i \in T$ with
$x \in \mathrm{ends}(i)$ (a loop at $x$ counts once).
