# Correlation function with couplings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $A \subseteq V$. The correlation is

$$\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, J} = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_\sigma \prod_{x \in A} \sigma_x\ \exp\Bigl(\beta \sum_{\{x, y\} \in E} J_{xy}\, \sigma_x \sigma_y\Bigr),$$

with $Z$ the [partition function](partition-j.md).
