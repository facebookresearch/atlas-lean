# Sourced current sum as correlation times sourceless sum

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $A \subseteq V$, with $\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, J}$ the zero-field [correlation with couplings](expectation-j.md) $J$, $\sigma_A = \prod_{x \in A} \sigma_x$ and $Z(\partial = A) = \sum_{\partial n = A} w(n)$ the [current sum](current-sum.md). If
$Z(\partial = \emptyset) \ne 0$, then $Z(\partial = A) = \langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, J}\, Z(\partial = \emptyset)$.
