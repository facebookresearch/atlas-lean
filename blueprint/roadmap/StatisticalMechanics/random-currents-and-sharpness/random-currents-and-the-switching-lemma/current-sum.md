# Current sum with prescribed sources

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $A \subseteq V$; a current $n$ assigns a multiplicity $n_e \in \mathbb{N}$ to every edge, its [sources](sources.md) $\partial n$ are the vertices of odd total incident multiplicity, and its [weight](weight.md) is $w(n) = \prod_{e \in E} (\beta J_e)^{n_e}/n_e!$. The current sum is

$$Z(\partial = A) = \sum_{\substack{n \colon E \to \mathbb{N} \\ \partial n = A}} w(n).$$
