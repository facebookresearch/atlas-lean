# Random-current representation with a field for even source sets

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $A \subseteq V$ with $|A|$ even, and let $\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, h}$ be the finite-volume
Ising correlation with coupling $1$ and field $h$. Then

$$\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, h} = \frac{Z^+(\partial = A)}{Z^+(\partial = \emptyset)},$$

where $Z^+$ is the [current sum](current-sum.md) on the [graph with a ghost vertex](with-ghost.md) joined to every
vertex, with coupling $1$ on the edges of $G$ and $h$ on the ghost edges (M. Aizenman, D. J. Barsky and R. Fernández, *The phase transition in a general class of Ising-type models is sharp*, J. Stat. Phys. 47 (1987) 343–374; H. Duminil-Copin, *Random currents expansion of the Ising model*, arXiv:1607.06933 (2016)).
