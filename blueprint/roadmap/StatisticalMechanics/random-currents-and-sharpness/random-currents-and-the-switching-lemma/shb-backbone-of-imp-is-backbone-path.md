# Odd-current path as a backbone path

For a finite simple graph $G$, a current $n$ and a path $\omega$ from $x$ to $y$ in $G$ carrying odd current on every edge,
$\omega$ is also a path in the [subgraph of edges with odd current](odd-subgraph.md), that is, a
[backbone path](is-backbone-path.md) from $x$ to $y$.
