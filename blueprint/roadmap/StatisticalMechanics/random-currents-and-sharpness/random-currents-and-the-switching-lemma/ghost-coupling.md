# Coupling on the ghost graph with uniform field

For a finite set $V$ with an added ghost vertex $\mathfrak g$, couplings $J$ on the pairs of $V$ and $h \in \mathbb{R}$, the ghost
coupling is $J_{xy}$ on a pair $\{x, y\}$ of $V$, $h$ on a pair $\{x, \mathfrak g\}$, and $0$ on $\{\mathfrak g, \mathfrak g\}$.
