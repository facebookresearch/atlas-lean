# Two-point function with couplings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $a, b \in V$. The two-point function is the zero-field
[correlation](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/expectation-j.md) $\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, J}$ of the
[source pair](source-pair.md) $A = \{a\} \,\triangle\, \{b\}$: it is $\langle \sigma_a \sigma_b \rangle_{\beta, J}$ for $a \ne b$ and $1$ for $a = b$.
