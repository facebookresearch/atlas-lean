# Output tuple of the high-temperature switching

For a set $V$, a switching output is a tuple $(x, y, A, B)$ of two vertices (the exit edge $\{x, y\}$) and two finite sets of
unordered pairs; in a [valid output](valid-2.md), $A$ lies inside $S$ with odd-degree vertices $\{o\} \,\triangle\, \{x\}$ and $B$
has odd-degree vertices $\{y\} \,\triangle\, \{z\}$.
