# Simon–Lieb boundary sum against a measure

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, sites $o, z$ and a measure $\mu$ on the configurations of
$\mathbb{Z}^d$, this is

$$\sum_{(x, y) \in \partial S} \tanh \beta\ \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S \int \sigma_y \sigma_z\, d\mu,$$

the sum over the oriented boundary edges of $S$, with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S$ the [free origin correlation in $S$](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/corr-origin-inner.md); it does not depend on $o$.
