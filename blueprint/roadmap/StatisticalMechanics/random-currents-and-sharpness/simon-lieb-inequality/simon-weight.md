# Simon weight of a boundary pair

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $S \subseteq V$ and $o \in V$. The Simon weight is
$w(x, y) = \tanh(\beta J_{xy})\, \langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, J|_S}$ with $A = \{o, x\}$, where $J|_S$ is the
[coupling restricted to $S$](../mean-field-lower-bound-for-the-ising-magnetizati/coupling-in.md) and $\langle \sigma_A \rangle$ the zero-field
[correlation](../random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/expectation-j.md) (when $x = o$, $A = \{o\}$ and $\langle \sigma_o \rangle = 0$).
