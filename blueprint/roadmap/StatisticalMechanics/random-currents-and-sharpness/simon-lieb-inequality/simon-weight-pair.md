# Simon–Lieb weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $S \subseteq V$ and $o, x, y \in V$. The Simon–Lieb weight is
$\tanh(\beta J_{xy})\, \langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle_{\beta, J|_S}$, with $J|_S$ the [coupling restricted to $S$](../mean-field-lower-bound-for-the-ising-magnetizati/coupling-in.md)
and $\langle \sigma_a \sigma_b \rangle_{\beta, J}$ the [two-point function](two-point-j.md) with couplings $J$ (equal to $1$ when $a = b$).
