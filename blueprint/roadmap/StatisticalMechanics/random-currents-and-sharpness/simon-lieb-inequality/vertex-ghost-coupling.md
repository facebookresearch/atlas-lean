# Ghost-graph coupling with a site field

For a finite set $V$ with an added ghost vertex $\mathfrak g$, couplings $J$ on the pairs of $V$ and a site field
$H \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$, this is the coupling on unordered pairs of $V \sqcup \{\mathfrak g\}$ equal to $J_{xy}$ on a pair $\{x, y\}$ of $V$,
to $H_x$ on $\{x, \mathfrak g\}$, and to $0$ on $\{\mathfrak g, \mathfrak g\}$.
