# Switching map between valid inputs and outputs

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $S \subseteq V$ and $o, z \in V$ with $o \in S$ and $z \notin S$. The [switching](high-temp-switch.md) sends every
[valid input](high-temp-switch-inputs.md) to a [valid output](high-temp-switch-outputs.md); this is the resulting map
between the two finite sets.
