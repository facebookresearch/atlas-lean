# Identification of volume sites with lattice sites

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $\Lambda \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ and a set $T$ of sites of $\Lambda$, the elements of $T$ correspond
bijectively to the [underlying sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$](plus-cut-sites.md), each element being sent to itself.
