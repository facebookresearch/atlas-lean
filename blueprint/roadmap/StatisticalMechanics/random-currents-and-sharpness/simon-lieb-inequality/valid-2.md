# Valid switching outputs

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $S \subseteq V$ and $o, z \in V$. A [switching output](high-temp-switch-output.md) $(x, y, A, B)$ is valid when
$x \in S$, $y \notin S$, every pair of $A$ is an edge of $G$ inside $S$, $B \subseteq E$, the [odd-degree vertices](has-odd-boundary.md) of $A$ form
$\{o\} \,\triangle\, \{x\}$ and those of $B$ form $\{y\} \,\triangle\, \{z\}$.
