# Plus-state two-point function at zero field

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is $\langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle^+_\beta = \int \sigma_x \sigma_y\, d\mu^+_\beta$,
with $\mu^+_\beta$ the zero-field [plus state](../../gibbs-states-and-phase-transitions/dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-state.md).
