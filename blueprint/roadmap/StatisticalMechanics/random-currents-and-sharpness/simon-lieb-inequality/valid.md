# Valid switching inputs

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $S \subseteq V$ and $o, z \in V$. A [switching input](high-temp-switch-input.md) $(F, H)$ is valid when $F \subseteq E$,
every pair of $H$ is an edge of $G$ inside $S$, the [odd-degree vertices](has-odd-boundary.md) of $F$ form $\{o\} \,\triangle\, \{z\}$ and $H$ has no odd-degree
vertex.
