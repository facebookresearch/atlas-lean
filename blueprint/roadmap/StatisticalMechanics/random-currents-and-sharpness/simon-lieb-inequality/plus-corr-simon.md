# Simon–Lieb inequality in the infinite-volume plus state

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \ge 0$, $T \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ finite with $0 \in T$, and $z \notin T$. Then

$$\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta \le \sum_{(x, y) \in \partial T} \tanh \beta\ \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T\, \langle \sigma_y \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta,$$

the sum over the oriented boundary edges of $T$ ($x \in T$, $y \notin T$ nearest neighbours), with $\langle \cdot \rangle^+_\beta$ the
zero-field [plus-state two-point function](plus-corr.md) and $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_T$ the
[free origin correlation in $T$](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/corr-origin-inner.md)
(B. Simon, *Correlation inequalities and the decay of correlations in ferromagnets*, Comm. Math. Phys. 77 (1980) 111–126; E. H. Lieb, *A refinement of Simon's correlation inequality*, Comm. Math. Phys. 77 (1980) 127–135).
