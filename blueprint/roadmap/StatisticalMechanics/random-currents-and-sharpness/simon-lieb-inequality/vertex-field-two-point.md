# Two-point function with a site field

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings, $H \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ a site field and $a, b \in V$. This is the
[expectation with site field](vertex-field-expectation.md) of $\prod_{x \in A} \sigma_x$, $A = \{a\} \,\triangle\, \{b\}$: it is
$\langle \sigma_a \sigma_b \rangle$ for $a \ne b$ and $1$ for $a = b$.
