# Boltzmann weight with a site field

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ real couplings and $H \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ a site field. The Boltzmann weight of a spin
configuration $\sigma$ is $w(\sigma) = \exp\bigl(\beta (\sum_{\{x, y\} \in E} J_{xy} \sigma_x \sigma_y + \sum_{x \in V} H_x \sigma_x)\bigr)$.
