# Simon–Lieb boundary sum in the plus state

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ and sites $o, z$, this is

$$\sum_{(x, y) \in \partial S} \tanh \beta\ \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S\, \langle \sigma_y \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta,$$

the sum over the oriented boundary edges of $S$, with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S$ the [free origin correlation in $S$](../sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-the-ising/the-duminil-copin-tassion-point-for-the-ising-mo/corr-origin-inner.md) and $\langle \cdot \rangle^+_\beta$ the
[plus-state two-point function](plus-corr.md); it does not depend on $o$.
