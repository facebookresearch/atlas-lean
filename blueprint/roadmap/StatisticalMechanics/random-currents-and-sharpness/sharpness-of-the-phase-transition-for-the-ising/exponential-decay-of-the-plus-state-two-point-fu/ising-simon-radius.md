# Simon radius of a finite set

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite set $T \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, the Simon radius is

$$\max\Bigl(\max_{x \in T} \|x\|_1,\ \max_{(x, y) \in \partial T} \|y\|_1\Bigr) + 1,$$

with $\|z\|_1 = \sum_i |z_i|$, $\partial T$ the oriented edge boundary of $T$ ($x \in T$, $y \notin T$ nearest neighbours) and an empty
maximum equal to $0$.
