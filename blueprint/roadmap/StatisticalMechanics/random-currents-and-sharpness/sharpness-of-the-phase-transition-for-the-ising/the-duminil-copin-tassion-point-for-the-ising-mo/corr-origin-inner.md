# Free origin correlation in a set, extended by zero

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is the
[free-boundary two-point function](free-corr.md) $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S$ if both $0$ and $x$ lie in $S$, and $0$ otherwise.
