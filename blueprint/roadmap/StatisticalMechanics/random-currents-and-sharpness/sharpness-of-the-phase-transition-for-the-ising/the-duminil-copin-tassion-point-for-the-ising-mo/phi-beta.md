# Percolation quantity $\varphi_p(S)$ at $p = 1 - e^{-\beta}$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is the
[Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity](../../../../Probability/percolation/sharpness/the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/phi.md)
$\varphi_p(S)$ of Bernoulli bond percolation at the [bond parameter](bond-param.md) $p = \max(1 - e^{-\beta}, 0)$, which is
$1 - e^{-\beta}$ for $\beta \ge 0$ and $0$ for $\beta < 0$.
