# Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity $\varphi_\beta(S)$ for the Ising model

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, the Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity is

$$\varphi_\beta(S) = \tanh \beta \sum_{(x, y) \in \partial S} \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S,$$

the sum over the oriented boundary edges ($x \in S$, $y \notin S$ nearest neighbours), with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_x \rangle_S$ the
[free origin correlation in $S$](corr-origin-inner.md) ($0$ when $0 \notin S$).
