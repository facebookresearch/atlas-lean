# Window sum of plus-state correlations

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $L \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, the window sum is
$\sum_{z \in L} \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta$, with $\langle \sigma_0 \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta$ the zero-field plus-state [two-point function](../../simon-lieb-inequality/plus-corr.md).
