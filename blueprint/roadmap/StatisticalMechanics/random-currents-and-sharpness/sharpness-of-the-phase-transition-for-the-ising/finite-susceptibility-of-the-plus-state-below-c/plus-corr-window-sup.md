# Supremum of plus-state window sums over windows of at most $n$ sites

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this is the supremum of the [window sums](plus-corr-window-sum.md)
$\sum_{z \in L} \langle \sigma_0 \sigma_z \rangle^+_\beta$ over the finite sets $L \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ with at most $n$ elements.
