# GHS three-point inequality at a site for every edge

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $o \in V$. This condition holds when, for every edge $e = \{x, y\}$ of $G$, the
[covariance of $\sigma_o$ with $\sigma_x \sigma_y$](cov3sym.md) is at most the [GHS bound](ghs-bound-sym.md) of $e$; equivalently, the
third Ursell function $u_3(o, x, y)$ is nonpositive for every edge $\{x, y\}$.
