# Bond-energy susceptibility

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $o \in V$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle$ the finite-volume Ising expectation on $G$ at $(\beta, h)$ (coupling $1$, field $h$). The bond-energy susceptibility is
$\sum_{\{x, y\} \in E} \bigl(\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle\bigr)$, the sum over the edges of the
[covariance of $\sigma_o$ with the bond variable](cov3sym.md).
