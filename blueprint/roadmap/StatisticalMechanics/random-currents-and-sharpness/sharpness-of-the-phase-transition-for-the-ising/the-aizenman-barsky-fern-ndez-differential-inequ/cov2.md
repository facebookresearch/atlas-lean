# Two-point spin covariance

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x, y \in V$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle$ the finite-volume Ising expectation on $G$ at $(\beta, h)$ (coupling $1$, field $h$). The spin covariance is
$\langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle$.
