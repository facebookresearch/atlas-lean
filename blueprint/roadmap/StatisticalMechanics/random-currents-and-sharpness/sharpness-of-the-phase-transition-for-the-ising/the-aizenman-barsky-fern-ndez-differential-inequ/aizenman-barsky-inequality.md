# Conditional finite-volume Aizenman–Barsky–Fernández inequality

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h, J \in \mathbb{R}$ and $o \in V$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle$ the finite-volume Ising expectation on $G$ at $(\beta, h)$ (coupling $1$, field $h$). Suppose the
[GHS three-point inequality](ghsthree-point-sym.md) holds at $o$ for every edge, and that the
[GHS bounds](ghs-bound-sym.md) sum to $J \langle \sigma_o \rangle \chi(o)$, with $\chi(o)$ the [susceptibility](susceptibility.md). Then
the [bond-energy susceptibility](bond-energy-susceptibility.md) satisfies

$$\sum_{\{x, y\} \in E} \bigl(\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle\bigr) \le J\, \langle \sigma_o \rangle\, \chi(o).$$
