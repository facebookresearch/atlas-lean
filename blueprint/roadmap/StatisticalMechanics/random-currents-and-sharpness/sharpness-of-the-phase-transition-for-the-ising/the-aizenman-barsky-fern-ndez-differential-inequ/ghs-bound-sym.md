# GHS bound for a bond

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $o \in V$ and $e = \{x, y\}$ a pair of vertices, with $\langle \cdot \rangle$ the finite-volume Ising expectation on $G$ at $(\beta, h)$ (coupling $1$, field $h$). The GHS bound for
$e$ is $\mathrm{Cov}(\sigma_o, \sigma_x) \langle \sigma_y \rangle + \mathrm{Cov}(\sigma_o, \sigma_y) \langle \sigma_x \rangle$, with $\mathrm{Cov}$ the
[spin covariance](cov2.md).
