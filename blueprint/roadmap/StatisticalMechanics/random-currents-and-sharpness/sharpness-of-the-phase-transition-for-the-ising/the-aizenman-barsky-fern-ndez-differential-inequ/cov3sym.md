# Covariance of a spin with a bond variable

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $o \in V$ and $e = \{x, y\}$ a pair of vertices, with $\langle \cdot \rangle$ the finite-volume Ising expectation on $G$ at $(\beta, h)$ (coupling $1$, field $h$). This is
$\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle$, the covariance of $\sigma_o$ with $\sigma_x \sigma_y$.
