# Beta Form Perco

Let `J > 0`, `d > 0` and `p̃_c < 1`. If `f` is differentiable on `[p̃_c, 1)` (including at
`p̃_c`), satisfies `(1/(q(1-q))) (1 - f(q)) ≤ f'(q)` on `(p̃_c, 1)` and has `f(p̃_c) ≥ 0`, then
`f(p(β)) ≥ (β - β̃_c)/β` for `β > β̃_c`, where `p(β) = 1 - e^{-βJ}` and `β̃_c = -log(1 - p̃_c)/J`.
