# Mean-field spin glasses

Disordered mean-field spin systems with Gaussian couplings: the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model and its free energy, the Parisi functional, Guerra's replica-symmetry-breaking bound and the Parisi formula.

Every pair of spins interacts, with an independent random sign and strength, so the model has
no geometry and no symmetry between configurations beyond the global flip. Its free energy
nevertheless converges, and Parisi's prediction for the limit, a variational formula over
"replica-symmetry-breaking" parameters, was proved by Talagrand in 2006 after Guerra had
established the upper bound by Gaussian interpolation.
