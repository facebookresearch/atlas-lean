# Rotated Ising pressure and spectral data

For a real diagonal eigenvalue array $\lambda$ and orthogonal matrix $U$, the energy of a spin vector is $\tfrac12\sum_i\lambda_i(Us)_i^2+\sum_i c_i s_i$ (the experiment may pass the inverse rotation as $U$). Take $N^{-1}$ times the real log of the average of its exponential over all $2^N$ spin vectors. The empirical spectral law and empirical field law put equal mass on each of the $N$ coordinates for $N>0$. At each size Haar rotation is the right-invariant orthogonal probability law. For random spectral data the relevant conditional Haar hypothesis is the joint-law factorization of the data and rotation, not independence between the fields and the eigenvalues or across different sizes. The field metric is the infimum of the integral of the absolute coordinate difference over probability couplings, written with the convention from the field statement.
