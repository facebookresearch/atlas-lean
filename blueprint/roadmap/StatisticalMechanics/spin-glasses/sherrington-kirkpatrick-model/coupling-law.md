# Law of the couplings

For $N \in \mathbb{N}$, the law of the couplings is the product measure on $\mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$ under
which the couplings $(g_{ij})_{i, j \le N}$ are independent standard Gaussian variables; only those
with $i < j$ enter the Hamiltonian (Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*, Ann. of Math. 163 (2006), after (1.1)).
