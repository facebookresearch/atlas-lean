# Replica-symmetric parameters are admissible

For every $q_1 \in [0, 1]$, the parameters $k = 1$, $m = (0, 1, 1, \dots)$ (that is, $m_0 = 0$ and
$m_\ell = 1$ for $\ell \ge 1$) and $q = (0, q_1, 1, 1, \dots)$ are
[admissible Parisi parameters](is-parisi-parameter.md) (Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*, Ann. of Math. 163 (2006), (1.6)–(1.7)). Evaluating the
functional at these parameters gives the replica-symmetric expression; its minimum over $q_1$ is the
replica-symmetric solution, the right one at high temperature (the case $k = 1$ discussed after
Theorem 1.1). In particular, the set over which the Parisi infimum
runs is nonempty.
