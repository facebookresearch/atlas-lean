# Guerra's replica-symmetry-breaking bound for the SK model

Let $\beta > 0$, $h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $(k, m, q)$ be [admissible Parisi parameters](is-parisi-parameter.md).
For every $\varepsilon > 0$, the [free energy](free-energy.md) satisfies
$F_N \le \mathcal{P}_k(m, q) + \varepsilon$ for all large enough $N$, where $\mathcal{P}_k$ is the
[Parisi functional](parisi-functional.md) for $\xi(x) = \beta^2 x^2/2$; that is,
$\limsup_{N \to \infty} F_N \le \mathcal{P}_k(m, q)$. Talagrand, *The Parisi formula*, Ann. of Math. 163 (2006) attributes the bound "left-hand
side of (1.14) $\le$ right-hand side" to F. Guerra, *Broken replica symmetry bounds in the mean field
spin glass model*, Comm. Math. Phys. 233 (2003), 1–12, who proved it with an interpolation scheme.
