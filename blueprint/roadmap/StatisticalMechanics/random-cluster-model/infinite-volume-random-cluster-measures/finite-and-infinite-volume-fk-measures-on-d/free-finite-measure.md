# Free random-cluster measure on a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the free box measure $\phi^0_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the probability measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) obtained by pushing the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-pmf.md) of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ forward under the [extension](extend-edge.md) by closed pairs. Under it, the edges of $\Lambda_n$ follow the free random-cluster law, the other pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$ (loops and non-adjacent pairs) are independent fair bits, and all remaining pairs are closed.
