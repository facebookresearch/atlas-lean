# Extension of a box configuration by closed edges

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a configuration $\omega$ of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ (an assignment of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$), the extension $\bar\omega$ is the configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that gives every pair of vertices of $\Lambda_n$ its state in $\omega$ and declares every other unordered pair of sites closed.
