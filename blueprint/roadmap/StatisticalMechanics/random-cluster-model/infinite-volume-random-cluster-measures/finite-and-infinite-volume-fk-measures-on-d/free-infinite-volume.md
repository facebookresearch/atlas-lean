# Free infinite-volume random-cluster measure

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure $\phi^0_{p,q}$ is a probability measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) chosen as the weak limit of some subsequence of the [free box measures](free-finite-measure.md) $(\phi^0_{\Lambda_n,p,q})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$; such a subsequence exists because the configuration space is compact.
