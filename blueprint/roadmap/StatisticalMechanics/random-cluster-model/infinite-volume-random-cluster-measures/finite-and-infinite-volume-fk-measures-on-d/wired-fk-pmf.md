# Wired random-cluster measure as a probability mass function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. The wired random-cluster measure is the probability distribution on the assignments of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices that gives each $\omega$ the mass $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega)$, the [wired random-cluster probability](wired-fk-prob.md).
