# Wired random-cluster partition function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\partial \subseteq V$ and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The wired partition function is $Z^w = \sum_\omega w^w(\omega)$, the sum of the [wired weights](wired-fk-weight.md) over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included).
