# Free infinite-volume measure is a subsequential weak limit

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, there is a strictly increasing $\varphi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ such that the [free box measures](free-finite-measure.md) $\phi^0_{\Lambda_{\varphi(n)},p,q}$ converge weakly to the [free infinite-volume measure](free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,q}$.
