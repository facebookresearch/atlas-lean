# Wired random-cluster weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\partial \subseteq V$ and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The wired weight of an assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices is

$$w^w(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k^w(\omega)},$$

where $o(\omega)$ and $c(\omega)$ are the numbers of open and closed edges of $G$ and $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph on $V$ formed by the open edges of $G$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
