# Wired random-cluster measure on a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the wired box measure $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the probability measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) obtained by pushing the [wired random-cluster probability](wired-fk-pmf.md) of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ forward under the [extension](extend-edge.md) by closed pairs.
