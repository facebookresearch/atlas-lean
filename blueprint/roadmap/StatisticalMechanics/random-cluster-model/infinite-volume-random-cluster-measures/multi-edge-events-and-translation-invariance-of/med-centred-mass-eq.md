# Wired box measure of an all-open event equals its box mass

Let $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of the box $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$. Then the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) $\phi^1_m$ at $(p, 2)$ of the event that all pairs of $t$ (as pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$) are open equals $\phi^w_{\Lambda_m,\partial\Lambda_m,p,2}[t]$, where $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,2}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) at $q = 2$ and $\mu[t]$ denotes the [mass of the all-open event](med-multi-mass-prob.md) $\sum_\omega \mathbf{1}[\text{all pairs of } t \text{ open in } \omega]\, \mu(\omega)$.
