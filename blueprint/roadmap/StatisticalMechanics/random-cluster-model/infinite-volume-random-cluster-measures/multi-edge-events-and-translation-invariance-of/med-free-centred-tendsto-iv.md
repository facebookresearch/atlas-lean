# Free all-open probabilities converge to the infinite-volume value

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$. For a finite set $t$ of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$, $O_t$ denotes the event that all pairs of $t$ (viewed as pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$) are open. Then $\phi^0_m(O_t) \to \phi^0_{p,2}(O_t)$ as $m \to \infty$, where $\phi^0_{m}$ is the [free box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$ and $\phi^0_{p,2}$ is the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures).
