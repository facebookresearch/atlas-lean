# Equal wired probabilities from eventually equal translated masses

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and let $t, t'$ be finite sets of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$. Suppose that for all sufficiently large $m \ge N$,

$$\phi^w_{\Lambda_m + v,\partial\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v] = \phi^w_{\Lambda_m,\partial\Lambda_m,p,2}[t'^{(m)}],$$

where $t^{(m)}$ is the image of $t$ in $\Lambda_m$ under the inclusion $\Lambda_N \subseteq \Lambda_m$, $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,2}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) at $q = 2$, $\mu[t]$ denotes the [mass of the all-open event](med-multi-mass-prob.md) $\sum_\omega \mathbf{1}[\text{all pairs of } t \text{ open in } \omega]\, \mu(\omega)$, $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$, and $\Lambda_m + v$ is the translated box with its nearest-neighbour graph (and, for the wired measure, boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$), and $t + v$ is the image of a set $t$ of pairs of $\Lambda_m$ under $x \mapsto x + v$. Then $\phi^1_{p,2}(O_t) = \phi^1_{p,2}(O_{t'})$, where $O_t$ is the event that all pairs of $t$ are open and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
