# Translation covariance of free box masses

Let $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of the box $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$\phi_{\Lambda_m,p,2}[t] = \phi_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t + v],$$

where $\phi_{G,p,2}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$ at $q = 2$, $\mu[t]$ denotes the [mass of the all-open event](med-multi-mass-prob.md) $\sum_\omega \mathbf{1}[\text{all pairs of } t \text{ open in } \omega]\, \mu(\omega)$, and $\Lambda_m + v$ is the translated box with its nearest-neighbour graph (and, for the wired measure, boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$), and $t + v$ is the image of a set $t$ of pairs of $\Lambda_m$ under $x \mapsto x + v$.
