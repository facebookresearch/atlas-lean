# Open lattice edges between box vertices are open box edges

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $x, y \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$. If $x$ and $y$ are adjacent in the open subgraph of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (the nearest-neighbour edges open in $\omega$), then $\{x, y\}$ is an edge of $\Lambda_n$ open in the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n$.
