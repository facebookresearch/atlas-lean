# Open edges of a box are open edges of $\mathbb{Z}^d$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration $\omega$ of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $x, y \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$: if $\{x, y\}$ is an edge of $\Lambda_n$ open in the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n$, then $x$ and $y$ are adjacent in the open subgraph of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (the nearest-neighbour edges open in $\omega$).
