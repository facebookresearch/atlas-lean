# Paths to $\partial\Lambda_{n+1}$ pass through $\partial\Lambda_n$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \ge 1$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and $v \in \partial\Lambda_{n+1}$, where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$. If the origin and $v$ are joined by a path of edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in $\omega$, then the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n$ joins the origin to some vertex of $\partial\Lambda_n$ by open edges of $\Lambda_n$.
