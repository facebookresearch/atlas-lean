# Open connections in a box are open connections in $\mathbb{Z}^d$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $x, y \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$. If $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, then they are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges of $\mathbb{Z}^d$.
