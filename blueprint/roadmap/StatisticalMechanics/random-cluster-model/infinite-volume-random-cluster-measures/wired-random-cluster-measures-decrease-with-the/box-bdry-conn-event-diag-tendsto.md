# Wired $q = 2$ percolation probability as the limit of $0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n$ in $\Lambda_n$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$, $\phi^1_n(0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n) \to \theta^1(p, 2)$ as $n \to \infty$, where $\phi^1_{m}$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$, $\{0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n\}$ is the [box-boundary connection event](../box-boundary-connection-probabilities-for-the-wi/box-bdry-conn-event.md) (the origin joined to $\partial\Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ by open edges of $\Lambda_n$), and $\theta^1$ is the [wired percolation probability](../../critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md).
