# Restriction to $\Lambda_n$ factors through $\Lambda_m$

For $d, n, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \le m$ and every configuration $\omega$ of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, restricting $\omega$ to $\Lambda_m$ and then to $\Lambda_n$ gives the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n$ (where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$).
