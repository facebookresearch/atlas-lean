# Edge inclusion into $\mathbb{Z}^d$ factors through $\Lambda_m$

For $d, n, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \le m$ and every unordered pair $e$ of vertices of $\Lambda_n$, mapping $e$ into $\Lambda_m$ and then into $\mathbb{Z}^d$ gives the same pair of sites as mapping $e$ directly into $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$).
