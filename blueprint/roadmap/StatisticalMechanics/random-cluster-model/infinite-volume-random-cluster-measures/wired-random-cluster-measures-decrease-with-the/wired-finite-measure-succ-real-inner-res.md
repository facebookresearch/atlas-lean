# Wired $\Lambda_{n+1}$ measure of a $\Lambda_n$ cylinder event as a sum

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, and $S$ a set of configurations of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ whose cylinder event $C_S$ is measurable. Then

$$\phi^1_{n+1}(C_S) = \sum_{\rho :\ \rho|_n \in S} \phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,2}(\rho),$$

where $\phi^1_{m}$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$, the sum runs over the configurations $\rho$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$, $\rho|_n$ is the [restriction](../the-wired-box-measure-as-a-conditional-measure-o/inner-restrict.md) of $\rho$ to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$, $K$ is the clique on $\partial\Lambda_{n+1} = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$, and $\phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,2}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with boundary wiring $K$ (the wired measure).
