# Wired infinite-volume boundary connection tends to $\theta^1(p, q)$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, $\phi^1_{p,q}(0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n) \to \theta^1(p, q)$ as $n \to \infty$, where $\phi^1_{p,q}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, $\{0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n\}$ is the [box-boundary connection event](../box-boundary-connection-probabilities-for-the-wi/box-bdry-conn-event.md) (the origin joined to $\partial\Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ by open edges of $\Lambda_n$), and $\theta^1$ is the [wired percolation probability](../../critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md).
