# Wired box measures decrease on inner increasing events for $q \ge 1$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing set $A$ of configurations of $\Lambda_n$,

$$\sum_{\rho :\ \rho|_n \in A} \phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\rho) \le \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^w_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $K$ is the clique on $\partial\Lambda_{n+1} = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$, $\phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with boundary wiring $K$ (the wired measure), $\rho|_n$ is the [restriction](../the-wired-box-measure-as-a-conditional-measure-o/inner-restrict.md) of $\rho$ to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$, and $\phi^w_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) of $\Lambda_n$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$.
