# Masses of the conditioning fibres sum to one

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Let $F$ be the set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with both elements in $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$\sum_{\psi} \sum_{\sigma \in \mathcal{F}_\psi} \phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\sigma) = 1,$$

where the outer sum runs over the configurations $\psi$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ closed on every pair of $F$, $\mathcal{F}_\psi$ is the set of configurations agreeing with $\psi$ off $F$, $K$ is the clique on $\partial\Lambda_{n+1} = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$, and $\phi^K_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with boundary wiring $K$ (the wired measure).
