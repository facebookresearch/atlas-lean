# Open path from the origin out of $\Lambda_n$ gives $0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n$ inside $\Lambda_n$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \ge 1$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_n$. If the origin and $v$ are joined by a path in the open subgraph of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (the nearest-neighbour edges open in $\omega$), then the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ joins the origin to some vertex of $\partial\Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ by a path of open edges of $\Lambda_n$.
