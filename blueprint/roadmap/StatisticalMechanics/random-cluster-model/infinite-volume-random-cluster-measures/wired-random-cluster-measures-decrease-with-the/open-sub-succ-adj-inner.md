# Open edges of $\Lambda_n$ are open edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration $\omega$ of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $x, y \in \Lambda_n$: $\{x, y\}$ is an edge of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_{n+1}$ if and only if it is an edge of $\Lambda_n$ open in the restriction of $\omega$ to $\Lambda_n$ (where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$).
