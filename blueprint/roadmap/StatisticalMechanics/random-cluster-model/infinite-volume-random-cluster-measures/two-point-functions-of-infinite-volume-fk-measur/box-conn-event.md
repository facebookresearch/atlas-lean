# Event that two sites are joined by open edges inside a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and vertices $x, y$ of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, the box connection event is the set of configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) in which $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges of $\Lambda_n$.
