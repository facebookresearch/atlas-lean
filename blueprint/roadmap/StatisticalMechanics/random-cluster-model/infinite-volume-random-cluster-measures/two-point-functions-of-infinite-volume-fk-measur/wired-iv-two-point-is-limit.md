# Convergence to the wired two-point function under stochastic decrease

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, and suppose $\phi^1_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q} \preceq \phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ for every $n$, where $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) and $\mu \preceq \nu$ denotes stochastic domination ($\mu(A) \le \nu(A)$ for every measurable increasing event $A$). Then for all vertices $x, y$ of $\Lambda_N$, $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(B_{x,y}) \to \inf_k \phi^1_{\Lambda_k,p,q}(B_{x,y})$ as $n \to \infty$, where $B_{x,y}$ is the [box connection event](box-conn-event.md) of $x, y$ (the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ in which $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open edges of $\Lambda_N$) and the limit is the [infimum value](wired-iv-two-point.md).
