# Masses of clopen increasing events converge along decreasing measures

Let $E$ be a countable set and $(\mu_n)$ probability measures on $\{0, 1\}^E$ (product topology) with $\mu_{n+1} \preceq \mu_n$ for every $n$, where $\mu \preceq \nu$ denotes stochastic domination ($\mu(A) \le \nu(A)$ for every measurable increasing event $A$). For every increasing event $A$ that is both open and closed, $\mu_n(A) \to \inf_k \mu_k(A)$ as $n \to \infty$.
