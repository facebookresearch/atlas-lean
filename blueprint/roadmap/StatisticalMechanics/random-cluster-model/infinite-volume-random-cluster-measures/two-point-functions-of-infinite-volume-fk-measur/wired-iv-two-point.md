# Infimum of wired box probabilities of a box connection event

For $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$ and vertices $x, y$ of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, the value is

$$\inf_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(B_{x,y}) \in [0, 1],$$

where $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) and $B_{x,y}$ is the [box connection event](box-conn-event.md) of $x, y$ (the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ in which $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open edges of $\Lambda_N$).
