# Wired random-cluster probabilities are positive

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, $\partial$ a set of its vertices, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Then the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega)$ is positive for every assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices.
