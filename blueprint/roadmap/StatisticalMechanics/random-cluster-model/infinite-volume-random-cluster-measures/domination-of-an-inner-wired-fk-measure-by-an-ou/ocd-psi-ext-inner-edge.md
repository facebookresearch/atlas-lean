# Glued configuration takes the inner value on inner edges

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and every unordered pair $e$ of elements of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, $(\psi \oplus \omega)(\iota(e)) = \omega(e)$, where $\oplus$ is the [gluing](ocd-psi-ext.md) and $\iota(\{x, y\}) = \{\iota x, \iota y\}$.
