# Number of outside-only clusters

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. The outside count $N_{\mathrm{out}}(\psi)$ is the number of [outside-only components](ocd-outside-only-comp.md) (components of $O_\psi$ containing no vertex of $\iota(V_{\mathrm{in}})$).
