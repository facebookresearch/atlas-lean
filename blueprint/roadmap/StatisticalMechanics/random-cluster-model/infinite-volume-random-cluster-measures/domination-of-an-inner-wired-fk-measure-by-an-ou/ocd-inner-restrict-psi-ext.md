# Restricting a glued configuration recovers the inner one

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, the [restriction](ocd-inner-restrict.md) of the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) $\psi \oplus \omega$ (inner pairs take their state from $\omega$, other pairs from $\psi$) is $\omega$.
