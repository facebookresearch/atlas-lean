# Full-graph reachability from an outside-only vertex stays outside

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). Let $\omega$ and $\psi$ be configurations on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and let $u, w \in V_{\mathrm{out}}$ with $u$ [outside-only](ocd-outside-only.md). If $u$ and $w$ are joined by a path in the [full cluster graph](ocd-full-graph.md) $F_{\omega,\psi}$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi \oplus \omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$), then they are joined by a path in the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not inner pairs, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$).
