# Image of an inner-graph edge in the outer graph

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. The image of an unordered pair $e = \{x, y\}$ of elements of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ is the unordered pair $\iota(e) = \{\iota x, \iota y\}$ of elements of $V_{\mathrm{out}}$.
