# Glued configuration agrees with the outer one off the inner edge set

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For every configuration $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) $\psi \oplus \omega$ (inner pairs take their state from $\omega$, other pairs from $\psi$) agrees with $\psi$ on every pair that is not an inner pair.
