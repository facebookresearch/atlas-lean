# Injectivity of the edge map

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. Then the induced map $\{x, y\} \mapsto \{\iota x, \iota y\}$ on unordered pairs is injective.
