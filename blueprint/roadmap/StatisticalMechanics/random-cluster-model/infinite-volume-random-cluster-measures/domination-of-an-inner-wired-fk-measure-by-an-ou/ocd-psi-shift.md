# Outer factor of the FK weight of a glued configuration

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The outer factor of a configuration $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ is $s(\psi) = \pi^{\mathrm{out}}(\psi)\, q^{N_{\mathrm{out}}(\psi)}$, the [outer edge factor](ocd-psi-edge-factor.md) times $q$ raised to the [number of outside-only components](ocd-outside-shift.md).
