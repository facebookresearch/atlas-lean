# Lattice inclusion matches induced adjacency

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $S_{\mathrm{out}}$ be finite sets of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, each carrying the nearest-neighbour graph induced from $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and let $\iota : S_{\mathrm{in}} \to S_{\mathrm{out}}$ satisfy $\iota(x) = x$ as sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then $\iota$ [matches adjacency](ocd-adj-match.md): $x, y \in S_{\mathrm{in}}$ are nearest neighbours if and only if $\iota x$ and $\iota y$ are.
