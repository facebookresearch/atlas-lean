# Positivity of the outer edge weight

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $0 < p < 1$. For every configuration $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, the [outer edge factor](ocd-psi-edge-factor.md) $\pi^{\mathrm{out}}(\psi)$ is positive.
