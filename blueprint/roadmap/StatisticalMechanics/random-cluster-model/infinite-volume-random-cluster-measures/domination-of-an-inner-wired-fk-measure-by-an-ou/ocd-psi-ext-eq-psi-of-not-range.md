# Glued configuration equals the outer one at each non-inner edge

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and every unordered pair $e$ of $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ that is not an [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md), $(\psi \oplus \omega)(e) = \psi(e)$, where $\oplus$ is the [gluing](ocd-psi-ext.md).
