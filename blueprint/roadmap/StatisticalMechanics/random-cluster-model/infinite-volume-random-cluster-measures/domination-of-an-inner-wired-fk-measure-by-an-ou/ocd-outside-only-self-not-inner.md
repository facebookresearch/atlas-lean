# Outside-only vertices are not inner

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. An [outside-only](ocd-outside-only.md) vertex $u$ is not in $\iota(V_{\mathrm{in}})$.
