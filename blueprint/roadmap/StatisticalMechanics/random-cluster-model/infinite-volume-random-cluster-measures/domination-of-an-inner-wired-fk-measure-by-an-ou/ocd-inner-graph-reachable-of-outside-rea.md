# Outside reachability gives inner cluster reachability

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). Let $\omega$ and $\psi$ be configurations on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$. If $\iota x$ and $\iota y$ are joined by a path in the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not inner pairs, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$), then $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path in the [inner cluster graph](ocd-inner-graph.md) $I_{\omega,\psi}$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ open in $\omega$ together with the edges of $W_\psi$).
