# Restriction pulls back all-open events to their images

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For every finite set $t$ of unordered pairs of elements of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, a configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ has all pairs of $t$ open in its [restriction](ocd-inner-restrict.md) $\rho|_{\mathrm{in}}$ if and only if all pairs of $\iota(t) = \{\iota(e) : e \in t\}$ are open in $\rho$.
