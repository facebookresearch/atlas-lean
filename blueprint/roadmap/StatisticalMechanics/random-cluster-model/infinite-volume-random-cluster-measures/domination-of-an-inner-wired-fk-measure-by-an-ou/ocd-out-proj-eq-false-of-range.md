# Projected configuration is closed on inner edges

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For every configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and every [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md) $e$, the [projection](ocd-out-proj.md) $\Pi(\rho)$ is closed at $e$.
