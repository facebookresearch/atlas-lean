# Cluster graph of a glued configuration

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For configurations $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, the full cluster graph $F_{\omega,\psi}$ is the graph on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ whose edges are the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) $\psi \oplus \omega$ (inner pairs take their state from $\omega$, other pairs from $\psi$) together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$.
