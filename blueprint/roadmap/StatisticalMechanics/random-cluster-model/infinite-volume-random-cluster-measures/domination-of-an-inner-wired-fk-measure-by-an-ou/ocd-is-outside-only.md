# Outside-only property of an outside cluster

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. A connected component of the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not inner pairs, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$) is outside-only when it contains no vertex of $\iota(V_{\mathrm{in}})$ (equivalently, one, hence every, of its vertices is [outside-only](ocd-outside-only.md)).
