# Glued value on $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$ is the inner value on $\{x, y\}$

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and all $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$, $(\psi \oplus \omega)(\{\iota x, \iota y\}) = \omega(\{x, y\})$, where $\oplus$ is the [gluing](ocd-psi-ext.md).
