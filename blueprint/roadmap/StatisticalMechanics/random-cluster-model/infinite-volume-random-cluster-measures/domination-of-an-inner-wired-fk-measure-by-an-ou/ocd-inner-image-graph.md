# Image of the inner open subgraph in the outer graph

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For a configuration $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, the inner image graph is the graph on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ whose edges are the edges $e$ of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ that are inner pairs and are open in the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) of the all-closed configuration with $\omega$ (so $e = \iota(e')$ with $e'$ open in $\omega$ for the chosen preimage $e'$).
