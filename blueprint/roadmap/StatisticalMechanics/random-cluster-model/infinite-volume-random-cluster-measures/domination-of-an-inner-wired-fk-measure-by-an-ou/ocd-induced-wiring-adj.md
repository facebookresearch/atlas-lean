# Induced-wiring adjacency: distinct and joined in the outside graph

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. Two vertices $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are adjacent in the [induced wiring](ocd-induced-wiring.md) $W_\psi$ if and only if $x \ne y$ and $\iota x$, $\iota y$ are joined by a path in the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not inner pairs, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$).
