# Free inner measure is dominated by the conditional outer measure

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map that [matches adjacency](ocd-adj-match.md) ($x \sim y$ in $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ if and only if $\iota x \sim \iota y$ in $G_{\mathrm{out}}$), and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, let $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For every increasing set $A$ of configurations on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$,

$$\phi_{G_{\mathrm{in}}}(A) \le \sum_{\rho :\ \rho|_{\mathrm{in}} \in A} \phi^K(\rho \mid \psi),$$

where $\phi_{G_{\mathrm{in}}}$ is the (free) [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G_{\mathrm{in}}$, $\rho|_{\mathrm{in}}$ is the [restriction](ocd-inner-restrict.md) $e \mapsto \rho(\iota e)$, $\phi^K(\cdot \mid \psi)$ is the [conditional measure](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/cond-bc-prob.md) of $\phi^K_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ given agreement with $\psi$ off the inner pairs, and $\phi^{K}_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ is the random-cluster probability on $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ with boundary wiring the clique $K$ on $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the wired measure; see [random-cluster probability with a boundary wiring](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md)). For a function $\mu$ on configurations and a set $A$, $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$.
