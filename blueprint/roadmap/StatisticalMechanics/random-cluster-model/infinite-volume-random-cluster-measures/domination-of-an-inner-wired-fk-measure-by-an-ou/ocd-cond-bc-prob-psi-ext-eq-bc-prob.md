# Conditional outer measure is the induced-wiring FK measure

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map that [matches adjacency](ocd-adj-match.md) ($x \sim y$ in $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ if and only if $\iota x \sim \iota y$ in $G_{\mathrm{out}}$), and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$,

$$\phi^K(\psi \oplus \omega \mid \psi) = \phi^{W_\psi}_{G_{\mathrm{in}}}(\omega),$$

where $\psi \oplus \omega$ is the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) (inner pairs take their state from $\omega$, other pairs from $\psi$), $\phi^K(\cdot \mid \psi)$ is the [conditional measure](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/cond-bc-prob.md) of $\phi^K_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ given agreement with $\psi$ off the inner pairs, $\phi^{K}_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ is the random-cluster probability on $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ with boundary wiring the clique $K$ on $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the wired measure; see [random-cluster probability with a boundary wiring](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md)), $W_\psi$ is the [induced wiring](ocd-induced-wiring.md) on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ (distinct $x, y$ with $\iota x$ and $\iota y$ joined by a path in $O_\psi$), and $\phi^{W_\psi}_{G_{\mathrm{in}}}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md) on $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ with boundary wiring $W_\psi$.
