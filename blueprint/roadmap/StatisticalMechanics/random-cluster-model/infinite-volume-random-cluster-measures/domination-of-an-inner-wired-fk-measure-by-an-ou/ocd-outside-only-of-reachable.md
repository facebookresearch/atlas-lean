# Outside-only property is constant on outside clusters

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. If $u \in V_{\mathrm{out}}$ is [outside-only](ocd-outside-only.md) and $v$ is joined to $u$ by a path in the outside graph $O_\psi$, then $v$ is outside-only.
