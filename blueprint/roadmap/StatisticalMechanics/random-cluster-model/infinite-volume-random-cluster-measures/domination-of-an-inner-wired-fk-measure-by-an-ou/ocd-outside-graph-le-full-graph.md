# Outside graph is a subgraph of the full cluster graph

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices. For all configurations $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not inner pairs, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$) is a subgraph of the [full cluster graph](ocd-full-graph.md) $F_{\omega,\psi}$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi \oplus \omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$).
