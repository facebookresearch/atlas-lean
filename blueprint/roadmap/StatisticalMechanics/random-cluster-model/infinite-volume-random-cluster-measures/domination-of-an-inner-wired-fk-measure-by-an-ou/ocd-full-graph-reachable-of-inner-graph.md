# Inner cluster reachability gives full-graph reachability of images

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map that [matches adjacency](ocd-adj-match.md) ($x \sim y$ in $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ if and only if $\iota x \sim \iota y$ in $G_{\mathrm{out}}$), and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). Let $\omega$ and $\psi$ be configurations on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$. If $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path in the [inner cluster graph](ocd-inner-graph.md) $I_{\omega,\psi}$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ open in $\omega$ together with the edges of $W_\psi$), then $\iota x$ and $\iota y$ are joined by a path in the [full cluster graph](ocd-full-graph.md) $F_{\omega,\psi}$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi \oplus \omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$).
