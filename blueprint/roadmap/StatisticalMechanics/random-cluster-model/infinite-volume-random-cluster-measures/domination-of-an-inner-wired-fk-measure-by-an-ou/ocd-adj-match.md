# Adjacency matching of the inner embedding

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. The map $\iota$ matches adjacency when, for all $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$, $x$ and $y$ are adjacent in $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ if and only if $\iota x$ and $\iota y$ are adjacent in $G_{\mathrm{out}}$.
