# Outer Bernoulli edge weight of a configuration

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $p \in \mathbb{R}$. The outer edge factor of a configuration $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ is

$$\pi^{\mathrm{out}}(\psi) = \prod_{e} \begin{cases} p & \text{if } e \text{ is open in } \psi, \\ 1 - p & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}$$

the product over the edges $e$ of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ that are not [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md).
