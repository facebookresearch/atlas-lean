# Glued configuration on inner edges ignores the outer configuration

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. For all configurations $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\omega$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and every [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md) $e$, $(\psi \oplus \omega)(e) = (\mathbf{0} \oplus \omega)(e)$, where $\oplus$ is the [gluing](ocd-psi-ext.md) and $\mathbf{0}$ the all-closed configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$.
