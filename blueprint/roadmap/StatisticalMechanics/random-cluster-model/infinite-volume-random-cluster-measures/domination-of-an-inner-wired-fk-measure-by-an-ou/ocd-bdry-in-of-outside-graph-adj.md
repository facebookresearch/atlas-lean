# Inner vertex with an outside-graph edge lies on the inner boundary

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $S_{\mathrm{out}}$ be finite sets of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, each carrying the nearest-neighbour graph induced from $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and let $\iota : S_{\mathrm{in}} \to S_{\mathrm{out}}$ satisfy $\iota(x) = x$ as sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq S_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\partial_{\mathrm{in}} \subseteq S_{\mathrm{in}}$ be boundary sets such that no $\iota(x)$ lies in $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$, and every $x \in S_{\mathrm{in}}$ having a nearest neighbour $z \in \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus S_{\mathrm{in}}$ lies in $\partial_{\mathrm{in}}$. For every configuration $\psi$ on $S_{\mathrm{out}}$, every $x \in S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $z \in S_{\mathrm{out}}$: if $\iota(x)$ and $z$ are adjacent in the [outside graph](ocd-outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$, then $x \in \partial_{\mathrm{in}}$.
