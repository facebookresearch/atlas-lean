# Restriction of an outer configuration to inner edges

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. The restriction of a configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ is the configuration $\rho|_{\mathrm{in}}$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ given by $\rho|_{\mathrm{in}}(e) = \rho(\iota(e))$ for every unordered pair $e$ of elements of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, where $\iota(\{x, y\}) = \{\iota x, \iota y\}$.
