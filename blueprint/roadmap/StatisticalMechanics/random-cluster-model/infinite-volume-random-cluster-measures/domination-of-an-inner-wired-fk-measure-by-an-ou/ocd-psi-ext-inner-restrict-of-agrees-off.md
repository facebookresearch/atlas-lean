# Agreeing configuration is the gluing of its own restriction

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. If configurations $\psi$ and $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ agree on every pair that is not an [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md), then $\psi \oplus \rho|_{\mathrm{in}} = \rho$, where $\oplus$ is the [gluing](ocd-psi-ext.md) and $\rho|_{\mathrm{in}}$ the [restriction](ocd-inner-restrict.md).
