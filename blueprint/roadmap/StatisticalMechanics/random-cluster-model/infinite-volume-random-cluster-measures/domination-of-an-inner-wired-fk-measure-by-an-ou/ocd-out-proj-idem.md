# Idempotence of the inner-edge projection

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. The [projection](ocd-out-proj.md) $\Pi$ that closes all inner pairs is idempotent: $\Pi(\Pi(\rho)) = \Pi(\rho)$ for every configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$.
