# Law of total probability over outside configurations

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). For every set $B$ of configurations on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$,

$$\phi^K_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}(B) = \sum_{\psi} \phi^K_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}(\mathcal{F}_\psi)\, \phi^K(B \mid \psi),$$

where the sum runs over the configurations $\psi$ that are closed on every inner pair (the fixed points of the [projection](ocd-out-proj.md)), $\mathcal{F}_\psi$ is the set of configurations agreeing with $\psi$ off the inner pairs, $\phi^{K}_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ is the random-cluster probability on $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ with boundary wiring the clique $K$ on $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$ (the wired measure; see [random-cluster probability with a boundary wiring](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md)), and $\phi^K(\cdot \mid \psi)$ is the [conditional measure](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/cond-bc-prob.md) of $\phi^K_{G_{\mathrm{out}}}$ given agreement with $\psi$ off the inner pairs. For a function $\mu$ on configurations and a set $A$, $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$.
