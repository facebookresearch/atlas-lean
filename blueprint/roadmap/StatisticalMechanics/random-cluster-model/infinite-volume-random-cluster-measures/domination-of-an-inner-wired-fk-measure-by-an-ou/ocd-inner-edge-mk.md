# Edge map sends $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. For all $x, y \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$, the [image](ocd-inner-edge.md) of the pair $\{x, y\}$ is $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$.
