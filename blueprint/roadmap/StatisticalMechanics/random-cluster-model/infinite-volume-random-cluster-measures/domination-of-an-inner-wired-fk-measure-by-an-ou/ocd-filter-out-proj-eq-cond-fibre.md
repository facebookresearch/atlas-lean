# Projection fibres are conditioning fibres

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. Configurations on $V$ are assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops included); $\iota$ maps a pair $\{x, y\}$ to $\{\iota x, \iota y\}$, and the images of pairs of $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ are the [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md). Let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ that is closed on every inner pair (equivalently, fixed by the [projection](ocd-out-proj.md) $\Pi$ that closes all inner pairs). Then $\{\rho : \Pi(\rho) = \psi\}$ is the set of configurations that agree with $\psi$ on every non-inner pair.
