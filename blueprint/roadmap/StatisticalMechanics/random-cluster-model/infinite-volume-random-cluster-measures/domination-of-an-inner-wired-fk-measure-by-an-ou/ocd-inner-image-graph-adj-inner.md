# Endpoints of inner image edges are inner vertices

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ an injective map. Let $\omega$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$. If $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in the [inner image graph](ocd-inner-image-graph.md) of $\omega$, then both lie in $\iota(V_{\mathrm{in}})$.
