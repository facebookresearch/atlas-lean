# Positivity of the outer factor

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. For every configuration $\psi$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, the [outer factor](ocd-psi-shift.md) $s(\psi)$ is positive.
