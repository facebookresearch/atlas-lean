# Graph of open non-inner edges

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. The outside open graph is the graph on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ whose edges are the edges $\{x, y\}$ of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ that are open in $\psi$ and are not of the form $\{\iota a, \iota b\}$ with $a, b \in V_{\mathrm{in}}$.
