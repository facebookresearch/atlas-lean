# Projection keeps non-inner edges

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. For every configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ and every unordered pair $e$ that is not an [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md), $\Pi(\rho)(e) = \rho(e)$, where $\Pi$ is the [projection](ocd-out-proj.md).
