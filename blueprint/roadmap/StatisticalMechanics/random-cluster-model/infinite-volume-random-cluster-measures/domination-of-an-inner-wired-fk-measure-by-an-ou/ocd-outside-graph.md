# Outside graph with the wired boundary clique

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map, and let $\partial_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq V_{\mathrm{out}}$ be a set of boundary vertices, and let $\psi$ be a configuration on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$. The outside graph $O_\psi$ is the graph on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ whose edges are the [outside open edges](ocd-outside-open-graph.md) (edges of $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ open in $\psi$ that are not [inner pairs](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md)) together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial_{\mathrm{out}}$.
