# Projection closing every inner edge

Let $G_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $G_{\mathrm{out}}$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $V_{\mathrm{out}}$, and $\iota : V_{\mathrm{in}} \to V_{\mathrm{out}}$ a map. The projection of a configuration $\rho$ on $V_{\mathrm{out}}$ is the [glued configuration](ocd-psi-ext.md) $\Pi(\rho) = \rho \oplus \mathbf{0}$ of $\rho$ with the all-closed configuration $\mathbf{0}$ on $V_{\mathrm{in}}$: it closes every [inner pair](ocd-inner-edge-finset.md) and keeps the state of every other pair.
