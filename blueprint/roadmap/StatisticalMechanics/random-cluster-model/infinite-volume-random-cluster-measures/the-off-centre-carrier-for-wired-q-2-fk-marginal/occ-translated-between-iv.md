# Free density is at most each translated wired marginal

Let $d, N, k \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and let $e$ be an unordered pair $e$ of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ (viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$). Then

$$\theta^0_e(p) \le \phi^{w,v}_{N+k}(e^{(N+k)} + v \text{ open}),$$

where $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](../../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume measure $\phi^0_{p,2}$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), and $\phi^{w,v}_{m}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) at $q = 2$ of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$, $\phi^w_m$ the one of $\Lambda_m$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$, $e^{(m)}$ is the pair $e$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$, and $\mu(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md).
