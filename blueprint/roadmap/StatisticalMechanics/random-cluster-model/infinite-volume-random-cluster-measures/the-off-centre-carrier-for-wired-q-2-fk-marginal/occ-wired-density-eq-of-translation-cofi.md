# Equal wired densities from equal translated marginals

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and let $e, e'$ be unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$. If for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$

$$\phi^{w,v}_{N+k}(e'^{(N+k)} + v \text{ open}) = \phi^w_{N+k}(e^{(N+k)} \text{ open}),$$

then $\theta^1_e(p) = \theta^1_{e'}(p)$. Here $\phi^{w,v}_{m}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) at $q = 2$ of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$, $\phi^w_m$ the one of $\Lambda_m$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$, $e^{(m)}$ is the pair $e$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$, and $\mu(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md), and $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
