# Off-centre carrier vanishes by translation covariance

Let $d, N, k \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $e$ an unordered pair of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$\phi^w_{m}(e^{(m)} \text{ open}) - \phi^{w,v}_{m}(e^{(m)} + v \text{ open}) = 0, \qquad m = N + k,$$

where, at parameters $(p, q)$, $\phi^w_m$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) of $\Lambda_m$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$, $\phi^{w,v}_m$ the one of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$, $e^{(m)}$ is $e$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$, and $\mu(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md). The left side is the off-centre carrier.
