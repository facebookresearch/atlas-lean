# Off-centre carrier tends to zero

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $e$ an unordered pair of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. Then, at $q = 2$,

$$\phi^w_{N+k}(e^{(N+k)} \text{ open}) - \phi^{w,v}_{N+k}(e^{(N+k)} + v \text{ open}) \to 0 \qquad (k \to \infty),$$

where $\phi^{w,v}_{m}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) at $q = 2$ of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$, $\phi^w_m$ the one of $\Lambda_m$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$, $e^{(m)}$ is the pair $e$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$, and $\mu(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md).
