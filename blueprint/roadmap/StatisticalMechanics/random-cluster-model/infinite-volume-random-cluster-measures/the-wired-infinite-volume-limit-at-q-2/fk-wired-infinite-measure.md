# Wired infinite-volume measure as limit on increasing cylinder events

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, and let $S$ be an increasing set of configurations of $\Lambda_N$. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$. Then $\phi^1_m(C_S) \to \phi^1_{p,2}(C_S)$ as $m \to \infty$ along the full sequence, where $\phi^1_{m}$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$ and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).

The general statement (all $q \ge 1$, weak convergence) is in G. Grimmett, *The Random-Cluster Model* (2006), Chapter 4.
