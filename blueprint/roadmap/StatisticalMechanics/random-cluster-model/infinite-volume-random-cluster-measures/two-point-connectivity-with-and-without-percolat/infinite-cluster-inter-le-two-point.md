# Joint percolation at most connectivity given no two infinite clusters

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mu$ be a finite measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) with $\mu(\mathcal{T}) = 0$, where $\mathcal{T}$ is the event that there are at least two infinite open clusters. Then for all $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$

$$\mu(|C(x)| = \infty,\ |C(y)| = \infty) \le \mu(x \leftrightarrow y),$$

where $\{|C(x)| = \infty\}$ is the [event that the open cluster of $x$ is infinite](cluster-infinite-event.md) and $x \leftrightarrow y$ means that $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges.
