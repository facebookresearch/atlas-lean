# Uniform decay of two-point connectivity without percolation

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mu$ be a finite measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) with $\mu(|C(0)| = \infty) = 0$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $n \ge 1$ such that $\mu(0 \leftrightarrow x) < \varepsilon$ for every $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_n$, where $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $x \leftrightarrow y$ means that $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges.
