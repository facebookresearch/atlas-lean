# Two-point lower bound from FKG, invariance, uniqueness and percolation

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mu$ be a finite measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) such that, for every $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$,

- $\mu(|C(0)| = \infty)\, \mu(|C(x)| = \infty) \le \mu(|C(0)| = \infty,\ |C(x)| = \infty)$, and
- $\mu(|C(x)| = \infty) = \mu(|C(0)| = \infty)$;

and moreover $\mu(\mathcal{T}) = 0$ and $\mu(|C(0)| = \infty) > 0$. Then there is $c > 0$ with $c \le \mu(0 \leftrightarrow x)$ for every $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$. Here $\{|C(x)| = \infty\}$ is the [event that the open cluster of $x$ is infinite](cluster-infinite-event.md), $\mathcal{T}$ is the event that there are at least two infinite open clusters, and $x \leftrightarrow y$ means that $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges.
