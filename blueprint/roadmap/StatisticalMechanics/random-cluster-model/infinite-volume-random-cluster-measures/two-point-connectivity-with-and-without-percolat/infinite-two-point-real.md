# Two-point connectivity of a bond measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a measure $\mu$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, the two-point connectivity is $\mu(x \leftrightarrow y)$, the real value (an infinite value read as $0$) of the $\mu$-measure of the event that $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges.
