# Disconnected sites in infinite clusters give two infinite clusters

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, every configuration in which the open clusters of $x$ and of $y$ are both infinite either has $x \leftrightarrow y$ or has at least two infinite open clusters; here $x \leftrightarrow y$ means that $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges.
