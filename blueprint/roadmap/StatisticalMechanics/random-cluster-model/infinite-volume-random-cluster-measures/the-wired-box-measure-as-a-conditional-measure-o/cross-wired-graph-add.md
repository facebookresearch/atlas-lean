# Extended wired graph with an extra wiring added

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration $\omega$ of $\Lambda_n$ and a graph $D$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$, where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, the extra-wired graph is the graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ whose edges are those of the wired graph $\tilde H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md) $\tilde\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ lying in $\partial\Lambda_n$) together with the edges of $D$.
