# Extended-graph components as inner components plus annulus vertices

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, and $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$. The [component map](cross-component-equiv-fun.md) is a bijection from the disjoint union of the connected components of the wired graph $H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$) and the vertices of the annulus $A_n = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$ onto the connected components of the wired graph $\tilde H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ for $\tilde\omega$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md) $\tilde\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$).
