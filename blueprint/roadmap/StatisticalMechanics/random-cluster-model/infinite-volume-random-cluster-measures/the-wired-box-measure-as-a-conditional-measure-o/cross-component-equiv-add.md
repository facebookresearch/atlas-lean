# Extra-wired components as inner plus annulus-wiring components

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$, and a graph $D$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ no edge of which has an endpoint in $\Lambda_n$. The [component map](cross-component-equiv-add-fun.md) is a bijection from the disjoint union of the connected components of the wired graph $H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$) and of the restriction of $D$ to the annulus $A_n = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$ onto the components of the graph whose edges are those of the wired graph $\tilde H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ for $\tilde\omega$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md) $\tilde\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$) together with those of $D$.
