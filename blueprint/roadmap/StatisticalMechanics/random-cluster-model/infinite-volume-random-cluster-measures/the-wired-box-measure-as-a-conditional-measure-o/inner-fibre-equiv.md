# Configurations closed off the inner edges as inner-box configurations

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$ is a bijection from the configurations of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that are closed on every pair not having both elements in $\Lambda_n$ onto the configurations of $\Lambda_n$; its inverse is the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md).
