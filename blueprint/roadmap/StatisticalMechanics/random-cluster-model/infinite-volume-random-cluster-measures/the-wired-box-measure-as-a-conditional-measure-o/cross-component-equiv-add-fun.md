# Map from inner and annulus-wiring components to extra-wired ones

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$, and a graph $D$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ no edge of which has an endpoint in $\Lambda_n$. The map goes from the disjoint union of the connected components of the wired graph $H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$) and of the [restriction of $D$ to the annulus](annulus-subgraph.md) $A_n = \Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$ to the components of the graph whose edges are those of the wired graph $\tilde H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ for $\tilde\omega$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md) $\tilde\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_n$) together with those of $D$; it sends each component to the component containing its image under the inclusions.
