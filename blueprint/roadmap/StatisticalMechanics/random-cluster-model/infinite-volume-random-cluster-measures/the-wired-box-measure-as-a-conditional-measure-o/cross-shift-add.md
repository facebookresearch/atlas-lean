# Annulus constant counting the components of an extra wiring

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ and a graph $D$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$, where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, the constant is

$$(1 - p)^{E^{\mathrm{out}}_n}\, q^{k(D_A)},$$

where $E^{\mathrm{out}}_n$ is the [number of edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$](non-inner-edge-card.md) and $k(D_A)$ is the number of connected components of the [graph induced by $D$ on the annulus](annulus-subgraph.md) $\Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n$.
