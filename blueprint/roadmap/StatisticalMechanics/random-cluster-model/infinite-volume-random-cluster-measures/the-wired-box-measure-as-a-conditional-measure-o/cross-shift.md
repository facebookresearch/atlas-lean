# Annulus constant linking the extended and wired inner weights

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the constant is $(1 - p)^{E^{\mathrm{out}}_n}\, q^{|\Lambda_{n+1} \setminus \Lambda_n|}$, where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ and $E^{\mathrm{out}}_n$ is the [number of edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$](non-inner-edge-card.md).
