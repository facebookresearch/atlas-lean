# Extended open graph with the inner box boundary wired

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a configuration $\omega$ of $\Lambda_n$, where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, the extended wired graph is the wired graph $\tilde H_\omega$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [closed-annulus extension](cross-ext.md) $\tilde\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ lying in $\partial\Lambda_n$).
