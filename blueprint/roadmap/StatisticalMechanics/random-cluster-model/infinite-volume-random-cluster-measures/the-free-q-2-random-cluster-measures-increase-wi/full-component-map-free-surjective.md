# Surjectivity of the free cluster map

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$) and $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$. The [cluster map](full-component-map-free.md) from the components of the inner graph $I^0_{\omega,\psi}$ and the avoiding components of the outer open graph $O^0_\psi$ to the components of the glued open graph $F^0_{\omega,\psi}$ is surjective.
