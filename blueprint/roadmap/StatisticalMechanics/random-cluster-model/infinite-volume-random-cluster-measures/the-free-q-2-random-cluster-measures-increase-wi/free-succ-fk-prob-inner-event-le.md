# One-step box monotonicity of free measures for $q \ge 1$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q \ge 1$, and $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$. For every increasing set $A$ of configurations of $\Lambda_n$,

$$\sum_{\omega \in A} \phi_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega) \le \sum_{\rho :\ \rho|_n \in A} \phi_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\rho),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$ and $\rho|_n$ is the restriction of $\rho$ to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$.
