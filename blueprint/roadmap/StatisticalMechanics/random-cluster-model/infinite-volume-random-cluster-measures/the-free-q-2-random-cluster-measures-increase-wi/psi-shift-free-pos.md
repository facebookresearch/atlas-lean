# Positivity of the outer weight factor

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Then the [outer weight factor](psi-shift-free.md) $s^0(\psi)$ is positive.
