# Free $q = 2$ box measures of increasing cylinders converge

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, and let $S$ be an increasing set of configurations of $\Lambda_N$. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ (assignments of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$), $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$. Then $\phi^0_m(C_S) \to L(S)$ as $m \to \infty$, where $\phi^0_{m}$ is the [free box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$ and $L(S) = \sup_{k \in \mathbb{N}} \phi^0_{N+k}(C_S)$ is the [limit value](fk-free-limit-free-infinite-volume-eq.md).
