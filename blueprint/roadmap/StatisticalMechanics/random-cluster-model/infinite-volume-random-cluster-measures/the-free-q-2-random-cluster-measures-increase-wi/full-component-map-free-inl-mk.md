# Cluster map on inner clusters

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$) and $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$. For every $x \in \Lambda_n$, the [cluster map](full-component-map-free.md) sends the component of $x$ in the inner graph $I^0_{\omega,\psi}$ to the component of $x$ in the glued open graph $F^0_{\omega,\psi}$.
