# Free box measure of a cylinder event as a finite sum

Let $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$, and let $S$ be a set of configurations of $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ whose cylinder event $C_S$ (configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_m$ in $S$) is measurable. Then

$$\phi^0_{\Lambda_m,p,q}(C_S) = \sum_{\omega \in S} \phi_{\Lambda_m,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $\phi^0_{\Lambda_m,p,q}$ is the [free box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md) and $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$.
