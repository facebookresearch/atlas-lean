# Glued open graph is the union of inner and outer open edges

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$) and $\omega$ a configuration of $\Lambda_n$. The graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ formed by the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that are open in the [glued configuration](../boundary-conditions-induced-on-a-box-by-the-oute/psi-ext.md) $\psi \oplus \omega$ (pairs with both elements in $\Lambda_n$ take their state from $\omega$, the others from $\psi$) equals the [glued open graph](full-graph-free.md) $F^0_{\omega,\psi}$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, viewed in $\Lambda_{n+1}$, together with the edges of $O^0_\psi$).
