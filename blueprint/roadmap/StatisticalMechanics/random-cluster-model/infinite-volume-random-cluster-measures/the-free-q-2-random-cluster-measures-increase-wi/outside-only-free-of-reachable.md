# Avoiding the inner box is constant on outer open clusters

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$). If $u \in \Lambda_{n+1}$ is [avoiding](outside-only-free.md) and $v$ is joined to $u$ by a path in the outer open graph $O^0_\psi$, then $v$ is avoiding.
