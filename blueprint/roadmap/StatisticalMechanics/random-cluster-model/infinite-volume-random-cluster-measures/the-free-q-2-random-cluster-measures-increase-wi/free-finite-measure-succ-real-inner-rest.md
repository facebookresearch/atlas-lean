# Next-box free measure of a cylinder event as a finite sum

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$, and $S$ a set of configurations of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ whose cylinder event $C_S$ is measurable. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_n$, $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$ lies in $S$. Then

$$\phi^0_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(C_S) = \sum_{\rho :\ \rho|_n \in S} \phi_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\rho),$$

where the sum runs over the configurations $\rho$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$, $\rho|_n$ is the restriction of $\rho$ to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$, $\phi^0_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}$ is the [free box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md), and $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$.
