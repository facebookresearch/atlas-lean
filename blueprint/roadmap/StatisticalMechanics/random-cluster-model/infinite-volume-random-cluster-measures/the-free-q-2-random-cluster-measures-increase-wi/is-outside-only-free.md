# Outer open cluster avoiding the inner box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$). A connected component of the [outer open graph](../boundary-conditions-induced-on-a-box-by-the-oute/outside-open-graph.md) $O^0_\psi$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in $\psi$ that do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$) avoids the inner box when it contains no vertex of $\Lambda_n$ (equivalently, one, hence every, of its vertices is [avoiding](outside-only-free.md)).
