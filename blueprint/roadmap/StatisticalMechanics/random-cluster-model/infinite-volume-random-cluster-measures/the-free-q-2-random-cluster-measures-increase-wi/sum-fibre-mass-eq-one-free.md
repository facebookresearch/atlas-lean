# Fibre masses of the free measure sum to one

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$ and $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$. Let $F$ be the set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with both elements in $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$\sum_{\psi} \sum_{\sigma \in \mathcal{F}_\psi} \phi_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\sigma) = 1,$$

where the outer sum runs over the configurations $\psi$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ closed on every pair of $F$, $\mathcal{F}_\psi$ is the set of configurations of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ agreeing with $\psi$ off $F$, and $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$.
