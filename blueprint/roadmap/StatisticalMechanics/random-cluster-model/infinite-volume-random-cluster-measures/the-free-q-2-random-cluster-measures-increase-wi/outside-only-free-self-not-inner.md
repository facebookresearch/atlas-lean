# A vertex whose outer cluster avoids the inner box lies outside it

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$). An [avoiding](outside-only-free.md) vertex $u$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ does not lie in $\Lambda_n$.
