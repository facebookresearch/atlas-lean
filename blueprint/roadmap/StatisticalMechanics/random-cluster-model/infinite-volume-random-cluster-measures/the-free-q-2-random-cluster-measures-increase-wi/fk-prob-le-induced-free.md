# Free measure is dominated by induced-wiring measures for $q \ge 1$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be a configuration of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing set $A$ of configurations of $\Lambda_n$,

$$\sum_{\omega \in A} \phi_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega) \le \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^{W^0_\psi}_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$, $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md), and $W^0_\psi$ is the [free induced wiring](induced-wiring-free.md) on $\Lambda_n$ (distinct $x, y \in \Lambda_n$ joined by a path in the outer open graph $O^0_\psi$).
