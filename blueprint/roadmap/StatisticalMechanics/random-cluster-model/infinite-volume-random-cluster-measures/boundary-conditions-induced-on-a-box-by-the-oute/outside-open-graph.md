# Open edges of an outer configuration outside the inner box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration). The outer open graph is the graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ whose edges are the nearest-neighbour pairs $\{x, y\}$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that are open in $\psi$ and do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$.
