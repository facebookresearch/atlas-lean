# Domain Markov property for the wired measure on nested boxes

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and $\omega$ one for the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. Let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, and let $F$ be the set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with both elements in $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$\phi^{K}_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,q}(\psi \oplus \omega \mid F, \psi) = \phi^{W_\psi}_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $K$ is the clique on $\partial\Lambda_{n+1}$, the left side is the [conditional measure](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/cond-bc-prob.md) of the random-cluster measure on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with boundary wiring $K$ (the wired measure) given that the configuration agrees with $\psi$ off $F$, $\psi \oplus \omega$ is the [glued configuration](psi-ext.md) (the pairs with both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$ take their state from $\omega$, all other pairs from $\psi$), $W_\psi$ is the [induced wiring](induced-wiring.md) on $\Lambda_n$ (distinct vertices of $\Lambda_n$ joined by a path in the outer graph $O_\psi$), $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/bc-prob.md), with weight $p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k_C(\omega)}$ ($k_C$ counting the components of the graph of open edges together with the edges of $C$), and $\partial\Lambda_m$ is the vertex boundary of $\Lambda_m$ (the sites of $\Lambda_m$ not in $\Lambda_{m-1}$, with $m - 1$ truncated at $0$).
