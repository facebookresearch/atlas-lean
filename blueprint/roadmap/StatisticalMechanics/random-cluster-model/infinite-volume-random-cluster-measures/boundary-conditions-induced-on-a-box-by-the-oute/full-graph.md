# Wired open graph of a glued configuration

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and $\omega$ one for the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. The glued graph is the graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ whose edges are the [inner open edges](inner-image-graph.md) (edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, viewed in $\Lambda_{n+1}$) together with the edges of the [outer graph](outside-graph.md) $O_\psi$ on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that are open in $\psi$ and do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$, together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_{n+1}$).
