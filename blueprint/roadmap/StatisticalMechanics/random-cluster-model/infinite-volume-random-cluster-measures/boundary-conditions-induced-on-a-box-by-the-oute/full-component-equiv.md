# Component decomposition of the wired glued graph

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and $\omega$ one for the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. The [component map](full-component-map.md) is a bijection from the disjoint union of the connected components of the [inner graph](inner-graph.md) on $\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$ together with the edges of the induced wiring $W_\psi$) and the connected components of the outer graph $O_\psi$ that contain no vertex of $\Lambda_n$ onto the connected components of the [glued graph](full-graph.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, viewed in $\Lambda_{n+1}$, together with the edges of the outer graph $O_\psi$).
