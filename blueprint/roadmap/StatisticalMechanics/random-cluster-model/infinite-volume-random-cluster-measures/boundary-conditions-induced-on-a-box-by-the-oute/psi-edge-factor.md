# Outer Bernoulli edge factor of an outer configuration

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and let $p \in \mathbb{R}$. The outer edge factor is

$$\pi^{\mathrm{out}}(\psi) = \prod_{e} \begin{cases} p & \text{if } e \text{ is open in } \psi, \\ 1 - p & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}$$

the product over the edges $e$ of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ that do not have both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$.
