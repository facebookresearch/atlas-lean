# Wired glued open graph is the union of inner and outer graphs

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and $\omega$ one for the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. The graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ formed by the edges of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ open in the [glued configuration](psi-ext.md) $\psi \oplus \omega$ (the pairs with both endpoints in $\Lambda_n$ take their state from $\omega$, all other pairs from $\psi$) together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial\Lambda_{n+1}$ equals the [glued graph](full-graph.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, viewed in $\Lambda_{n+1}$, together with the edges of the outer graph $O_\psi$), where $\partial\Lambda_m$ is the vertex boundary of $\Lambda_m$ (the sites of $\Lambda_m$ not in $\Lambda_{m-1}$, with $m - 1$ truncated at $0$).
