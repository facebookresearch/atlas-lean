# Glued and inner graphs have the same connections on the box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ carry its nearest-neighbour edges (so $\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_{n+1}$), and let $\psi$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the outer configuration), and $\omega$ one for the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. For all $x, y \in \Lambda_n$, $x$ and $y$ are joined by a path in the [glued graph](full-graph.md) on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$, viewed in $\Lambda_{n+1}$, together with the edges of the outer graph $O_\psi$) if and only if they are joined by a path in the [inner graph](inner-graph.md) on $\Lambda_n$ (the edges of $\Lambda_n$ open in $\omega$ together with the edges of the induced wiring $W_\psi$).
