# Inner open edges viewed in the larger box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, and let $\omega$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$. The inner image graph is the graph on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ whose edges are the nearest-neighbour pairs $\{x, y\}$ with $x, y \in \Lambda_n$ that are open in $\omega$.
