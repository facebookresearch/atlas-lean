# Wired probability that the origin connects to the outside of a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the crossing probability is $\phi^1_{p,q}(\mathrm{Cross}_n)$, where $\phi^1_{p,q}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\mathrm{Cross}_n$ is the event that the origin is joined by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges to some site outside $\Lambda_{n-1} = \{-(n-1), \dots, n-1\}^d$ ($n - 1$ truncated at $0$).
