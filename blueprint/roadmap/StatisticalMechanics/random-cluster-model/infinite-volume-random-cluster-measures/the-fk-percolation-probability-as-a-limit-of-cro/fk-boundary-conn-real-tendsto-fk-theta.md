# Wired crossing probabilities converge to the percolation probability

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the [crossing probabilities](fk-boundary-conn-real.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}(\mathrm{Cross}_n)$ converge to the [wired percolation probability](../../critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md) $\theta^1(p, q)$ as $n \to \infty$.
