# FKG lattice condition for the wired measure when $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For all assignments $a, b$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices,

$$\phi^w(a)\, \phi^w(b) \le \phi^w(a \vee b)\, \phi^w(a \wedge b),$$

where $\phi^w = \phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the wired random-cluster probability $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k^w(\omega)} / Z^w$ (with $o, c$ the numbers of open and closed edges of $G$, $k^w(\omega)$ the number of components of the graph formed by the open edges of $G$ and all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$, and $Z^w$ the sum of the numerators over all configurations), and $a \vee b$, $a \wedge b$ are the coordinatewise maximum and minimum (closed $<$ open).
