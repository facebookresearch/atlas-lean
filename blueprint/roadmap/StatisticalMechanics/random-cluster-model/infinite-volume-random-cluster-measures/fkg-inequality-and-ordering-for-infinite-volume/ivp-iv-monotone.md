# Wired $q = 2$ measure dominates the free one on increasing cylinders

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ (assignments of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$), $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$. For every increasing set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N$, $\phi^0_{p,2}(C_S) \le \phi^1_{p,2}(C_S)$, where $\phi^0_{p,2}$ is the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures) and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
