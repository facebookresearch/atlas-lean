# FKG inequality for the wired $q = 2$ infinite-volume measure

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ (assignments of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$), $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$. For all increasing sets $S_1, S_2$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N$,

$$\phi^1_{p,2}(C_{S_1})\, \phi^1_{p,2}(C_{S_2}) \le \phi^1_{p,2}(C_{S_1} \cap C_{S_2}),$$

where $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
