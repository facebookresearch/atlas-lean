# FKG inequality for free $q = 2$ box measures on smaller-box events

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le m$, and $0 < p < 1$. For a set $S$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ (assignments of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$), $C_S$ denotes the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$. For all increasing sets $S_1, S_2$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N$,

$$\phi^0_m(C_{S_1})\, \phi^0_m(C_{S_2}) \le \phi^0_m(C_{S_1} \cap C_{S_2}),$$

where $\phi^0_{m}$ is the [free box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at parameters $(p, 2)$.
