# Closed-annulus wired bound on origin-to-$\partial\Lambda_n$, $q = 2$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, and let $F$ be the set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{n+1}$ with both elements in $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$\sum_{\rho \in A_n} \phi^{K}_{\Lambda_{n+1},p,2}(\rho \mid F, \mathbf{0}) \le \phi^1_n(0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n),$$

where $K$ is the clique on $\partial\Lambda_{n+1}$ (so the measure on $\Lambda_{n+1}$ is the wired one), $\phi^K(\cdot \mid F, \mathbf{0})$ is its [conditional measure](../../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/cond-bc-prob.md) given agreement with the all-closed configuration off $F$, $A_n$ is the [event](inner-bdry-fin-event.md) that the restriction to $\Lambda_n$ joins the origin to $\partial\Lambda_n$ by open edges of $\Lambda_n$, and $\phi^1_n$ is the [wired box measure](../finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_n$ at $(p, 2)$ of the [box-boundary connection event](../box-boundary-connection-probabilities-for-the-wi/box-bdry-conn-event.md).
