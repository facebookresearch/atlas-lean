# Vanishing shell edge fraction when $(n - m(n))/n \to 0$

Let $d \ge 1$ and $m : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $m(n) \le n$ for every $n$ and $(n - m(n))/n \to 0$ (with $x/0 = 0$). Then $(E(n) - E(m(n)))/E(n) \to 0$, where $E(k)$ is the number of edges of $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ and the quotient is read as $0$ when $E(n) = 0$.
