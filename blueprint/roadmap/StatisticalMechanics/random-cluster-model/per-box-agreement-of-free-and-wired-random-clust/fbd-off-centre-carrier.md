# Wired-marginal difference between a box and its translate

For $d, N, k \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, an unordered pair $e$ of vertices of $\Lambda_N$, and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, with $m = N + k$, the off-centre carrier is

$$\phi^w_{m}(e^{(m)} \text{ open}) - \phi^{w,v}_{m}(e^{(m)} + v \text{ open}),$$

where $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, $e^{(m)}$ is $e$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$, $\phi^w_m$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) of $\Lambda_m$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$ at $(p, q)$, $\phi^{w,v}_m$ is the one of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$, and the probabilities are [edge marginals](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md).
