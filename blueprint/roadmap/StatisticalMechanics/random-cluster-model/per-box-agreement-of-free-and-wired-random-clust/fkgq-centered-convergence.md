# Convex limit of centered tilt free energies of boxes for $d, q \ge 1$

Let $d \ge 1$ and $q \ge 1$. There is a convex function $G : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $G(0) = 0$ such that, for every $s \in \mathbb{R}$, the [centered free energy](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/cfe-centered.md) of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ at cluster weight $q$ and tilt $s$ converges to $G(s)$ as $n \to \infty$.
