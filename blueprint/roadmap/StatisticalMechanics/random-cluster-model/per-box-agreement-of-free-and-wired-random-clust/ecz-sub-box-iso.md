# Sub-box as an induced subgraph of a larger box

For $d, a, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $a \le n$, the identity on sites is a graph isomorphism from the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_a$ onto the graph induced by the nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n$ on the vertices lying in $\Lambda_a$, where $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$.
