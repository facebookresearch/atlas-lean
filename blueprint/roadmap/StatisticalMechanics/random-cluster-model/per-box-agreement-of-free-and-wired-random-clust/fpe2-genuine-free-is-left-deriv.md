# Free densities of genuine box edges as left derivatives

For $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $G : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the condition holds when $\theta^0_{e'}(\sigma(t)) = G'_-(t)$ for every edge $e'$ of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, where $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), $G'_-$ is the [left derivative](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/pressure-left-deriv.md) (read as $0$ where it does not exist), and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
