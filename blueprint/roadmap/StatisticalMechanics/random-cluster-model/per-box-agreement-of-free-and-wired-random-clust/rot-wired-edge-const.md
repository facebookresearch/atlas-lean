# Every lattice edge has the wired $q = 2$ density of $\{0, e_1\}$

Let $d \ge 1$, $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ nearest neighbours, and $0 < p < 1$. Then $\theta^1_{\{x, y\}}(p) = \theta^1_{\{0, e_1\}}(p)$, where $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$) and $e_1$ is the first coordinate vector.
