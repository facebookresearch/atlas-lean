# Wired off-centre upper bound by the recentred small-box marginal

Let $d, R, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $R \ge 1$ and $R + 1 \le n$, $0 < p < 1$, and $u, v \in \Lambda_{n-R-1}$ with $v - u \in \Lambda_R$, where $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$. Then

$$\phi^w_{\Lambda_n,p,2}(\{u, v\} \text{ open}) \le \phi^w_{\Lambda_R,p,2}(\{0, v - u\} \text{ open}),$$

where $\phi^w_{\Lambda_k,p,2}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) of $\Lambda_k$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_k = \Lambda_k \setminus \Lambda_{k-1}$ (the pairs need not be edges).
