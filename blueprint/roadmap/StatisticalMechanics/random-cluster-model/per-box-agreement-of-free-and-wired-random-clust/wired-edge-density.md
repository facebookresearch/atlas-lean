# Wired infinite-volume edge density

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $q, p \in \mathbb{R}$ and an unordered pair $e$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the wired edge density $\theta^1_{e,q}(p)$ is, when $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the probability that $e$ is open under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures); otherwise it is $0$.
