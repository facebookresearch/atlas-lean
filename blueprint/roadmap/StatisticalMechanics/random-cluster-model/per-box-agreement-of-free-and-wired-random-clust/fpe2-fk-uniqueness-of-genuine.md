# Countable-exception $q = 2$ uniqueness from one-sided derivative data

Let $d \ge 1$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, suppose $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ has at least one edge, and let $G : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex. Suppose that for every edge $e'$ of $\Lambda_N$ and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, $\theta^0_{e'}(\sigma(t)) = G'_-(t)$ and $\theta^1_{e'}(\sigma(t)) = G'_+(t)$. Then the set of $t$ for which some increasing set $A$ of configurations of $\Lambda_N$ has $\mu^0_t(A) \ne \mu^1_t(A)$ is countable. Here $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$), $g'_-$ and $g'_+$ are the [left](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/pressure-left-deriv.md) and [right derivatives](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/pressure-right-deriv.md) (each read as $0$ where the one-sided derivative does not exist), $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md), and $\mu^0_t$ and $\mu^1_t$ are the [free](fuc-free-mass.md) and [wired mass functions](fuc-wired-mass.md) on configurations of $\Lambda_N$ (the probability, under the free, resp. wired, infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ at $p = \sigma(t)$ and $q = 2$, that the restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ equals the given configuration), and $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$.
