# Restriction of a box to all tiling tags

For $d, m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the graph induced by the nearest-neighbour graph of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ on the vertices whose [cell tag](ecz-van-hove-tag.md) lies in the set of all tags (including the absence of a tag) is isomorphic, through the identity on vertices, to the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n$.
