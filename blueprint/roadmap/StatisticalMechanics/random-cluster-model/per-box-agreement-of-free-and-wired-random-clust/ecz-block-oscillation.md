# Vanishing block oscillation of the edge free energy

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s \in \mathbb{R}$, the block-oscillation condition holds when there are $b : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $b(n) \to \infty$ and $\mathrm{osc} : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\mathrm{osc}(j) \to 0$ such that $|F(n) - F(K_{b(n)})| \le \mathrm{osc}(b(n))$ for every $n$, where $F$ is the [per-edge free energy](ecz-edge-free-energy.md) of boxes at $s$ and $K_j = 2^{j+1} - 1$ are the [dyadic scales](../free-energy-of-the-random-cluster-model/agl-k.md).
