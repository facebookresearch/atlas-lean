# Free off-centre lower bound by the recentred small-box marginal

Let $d, R, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $R \le n$, $0 < p < 1$, and $u, v \in \Lambda_{n-R}$ with $v - u \in \Lambda_R$, where $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$. Then

$$\phi_{\Lambda_R,p,2}(\{0, v - u\} \text{ open}) \le \phi_{\Lambda_n,p,2}(\{u, v\} \text{ open}),$$

where $\phi_{\Lambda_k,p,2}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_k$ (the pairs need not be edges).
