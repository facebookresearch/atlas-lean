# Countably many parameters admit a separating increasing event

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\Omega = \{0, 1\}^E$, $e \in E$, and for each $p \in \mathbb{R}$ let $\mu_p, \nu_p : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and let $P_p$ be a [monotone coupling](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/is-monotone-coupling-fun.md) of $\mu_p$ and $\nu_p$. Suppose $p \mapsto \nu_p(e \text{ open})$ is nondecreasing and that at each point $p$ where it is continuous, $\mu_p(e' \text{ open}) = \nu_p(e' \text{ open})$ for every $e' \in E$. Then the set of $p$ for which some increasing $A \subseteq \Omega$ has $\mu_p(A) \ne \nu_p(A)$ is countable. Here $\mu(e \text{ open}) = \sum_{\omega :\ \omega(e) \text{ open}} \mu(\omega)$ is the [edge marginal](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md) and $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$ is the [mass](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/event-mass-prob.md) of $A$.
