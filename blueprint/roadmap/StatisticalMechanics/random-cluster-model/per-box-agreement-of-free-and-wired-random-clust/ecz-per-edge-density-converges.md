# Convergence of the per-edge free energy of boxes

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s \in \mathbb{R}$, the convergence condition holds when there is $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ with $u(n)/E(n) \to \rho$ as $n \to \infty$, where $u(n) = -\log Z^{e}_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(s), 2)$ is the [negative log edge partition function](ecz-u.md) of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with its nearest-neighbour graph, $E(n)$ is the number of edges of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, and the quotient is read as $0$ when $E(n) = 0$.
