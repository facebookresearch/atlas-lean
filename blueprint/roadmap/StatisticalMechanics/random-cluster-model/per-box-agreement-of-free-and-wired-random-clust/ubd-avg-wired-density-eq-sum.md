# Average wired $q = 2$ edge density of a box as a mean of marginals

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the [average wired edge density](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/wpd-avg-wired-density.md) of $\Lambda_n$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$ at $q = 2$ equals

$$\frac{1}{E(n)} \sum_{e} \phi^w_n(e \text{ open}),$$

the sum running over the edges $e$ of $\Lambda_n$, with $1/E(n)$ read as $0$ when $E(n) = 0$; here $E(n)$ is the number of edges of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $\phi^w_n$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ at $(\sigma(t), 2)$.
