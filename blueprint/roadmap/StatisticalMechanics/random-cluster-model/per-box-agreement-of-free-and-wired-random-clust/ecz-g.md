# Upper envelope for the per-edge free energy

For $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s \in \mathbb{R}$, the value is

$$g(m) = \frac{2m}{2m+1}\, f(m) - \Big(1 - \frac{2m}{2m+1}\Big) \log(1 - \sigma(s)),$$

where $f(m)$ is the [per-edge negative log edge partition function](ecz-f.md) of $\Lambda_m$ at $s$ and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
