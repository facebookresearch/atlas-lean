# Wired unit-edge marginal at $0$ on $\Lambda_{\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor}$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $q, t \in \mathbb{R}$ and a direction $\pm e_j$, let $R = \lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. If $R \ge 1$, the value is the probability that the [unit edge](ocs-unit-edge.md) $\{0, \pm e_j\}$ is open under the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) on $\Lambda_R = \{-R, \dots, R\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_R$ at $(\sigma(t), q)$; if $R = 0$ it is $0$. Here $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
