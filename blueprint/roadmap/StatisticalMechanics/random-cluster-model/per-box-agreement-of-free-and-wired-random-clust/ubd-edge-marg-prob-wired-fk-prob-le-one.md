# Wired FK edge marginals are at most one

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $\partial \subseteq V$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$, and $e$ an unordered pair of elements of $V$. Then $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(e \text{ open}) \le 1$, where $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md).
