# Total deviation of free unit-edge marginals from the density

For $d, N, n \in \mathbb{N}$, an unordered pair $e'$ of vertices of $\Lambda_N$, and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the free deviation is $\Delta^0_n = \sum_{j=1}^{d} \sum_{\pm} |\theta^0_{e'}(\sigma(t)) - m_n(j, \pm)|$, where $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), $m_n(j, \pm)$ is the [free unit-edge marginal](ocs-unit-marg.md) (for $R = \lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor \ge 1$, the probability that the unit edge $\{0, \pm e_j\}$ is open under the random-cluster measure on $\Lambda_R$ at $(\sigma(t), 2)$; $0$ when $R = 0$), and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
