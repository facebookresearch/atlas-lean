# Average edge density of a random-cluster measure

For a simple graph $G = (V, E)$ on a finite set and $q, s \in \mathbb{R}$, the average edge density is

$$\frac{1}{|E|} \sum_\omega \phi_{G,\sigma(s),q}(\omega)\, o(\omega),$$

the [expected number of open edges](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-expect.md) under the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) at $p = \sigma(s)$ divided by $|E|$ ($1/|E|$ read as $0$ when $G$ has no edges), where $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
