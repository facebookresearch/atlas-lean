# All box edges have the same free $q = 2$ density

Let $d \ge 1$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $e'$ an edge of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [free rotation residue](ocs-free-rotation-residue.md) holds: every edge $e$ of every box $\Lambda_m$ satisfies $\theta^0_e(\sigma(t)) = \theta^0_{e'}(\sigma(t))$, where $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures) and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
