# Interface subadditivity of $-\log$ of the edge partition function

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V$ and $W$, and let $K$ be [glued from $G$ and $H$ along an interface](../free-energy-of-the-random-cluster-model/fis-cross-interface.md), with [interface edge set](../free-energy-of-the-random-cluster-model/fis-interface.md) $I$. For $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$,

$$-\log Z^{e}_K(p, q) \le -\log Z^{e}_G(p, q) - \log Z^{e}_H(p, q) - |I| \log(1 - p),$$

where $Z^{e}_G(p, q)$ is the [edge partition function](ecz-fk-zedge.md) (the sum of the random-cluster weights over the configurations closed on every pair that is not an edge of $G$).
