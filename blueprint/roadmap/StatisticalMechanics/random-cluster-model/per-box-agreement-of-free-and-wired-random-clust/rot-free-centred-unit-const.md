# Unit edges at the origin have the free density of $\{0, e_1\}$

Let $d \ge 1$, $j$ a coordinate, a sign $\pm$, and $0 < p < 1$. Then $\theta^0_{\{0, \pm e_j\}}(p) = \theta^0_{\{0, e_1\}}(p)$, where $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures) and $e_1$ is the first coordinate vector.
