# Wired $q = 2$ edge density lies in $[0, 1]$

For $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, an unordered pair $e'$ of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, $0 \le \theta^1_{e'}(\sigma(t)) \le 1$, where $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$) and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
