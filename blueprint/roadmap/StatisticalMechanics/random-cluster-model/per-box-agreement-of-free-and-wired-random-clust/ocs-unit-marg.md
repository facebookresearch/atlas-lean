# Free unit-edge marginal in the square-root box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a signed coordinate vector $\pm e_j$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, let $R = \lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. If $R \ge 1$ the value is the probability that the [unit edge](ocs-unit-edge.md) $\{0, \pm e_j\}$ is open under the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $\Lambda_R$ at $(\sigma(t), 2)$; if $R = 0$ it is $0$. Here $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
