# Coordinate permutation as a box symmetry

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a permutation $\pi$ of the coordinates, the [coordinate permutation](rot-perm-equiv.md) $x \mapsto (x_{\pi^{-1}(i)})_i$ is a [box symmetry](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/box-sym.md) of $\mathbb{Z}^d$: it preserves nearest-neighbour adjacency and maps each box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ onto itself.
