# Free uniform bulk deviation at $q = 2$

For $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, the free uniform bulk deviation holds when for every edge $e'$ of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, there are sets $I_n$ of edges of $\Lambda_n$ and numbers $\delta_n \ge 0$ with $\delta_n \to 0$ such that $|\phi_n(e \text{ open}) - \theta^0_{e'}(\sigma(t))| \le \delta_n$ for every $e \in I_n$, and $(E(n) - |I_n|)/E(n) \to 0$ (read as $0$ when $E(n) = 0$). Here $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$ (the probability that $e$ is open under the free infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), $\phi_n = \phi_{\Lambda_n,\sigma(t),2}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, $E(n)$ is the number of edges of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, and $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ is the [logistic function](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/fsc-logistic.md).
