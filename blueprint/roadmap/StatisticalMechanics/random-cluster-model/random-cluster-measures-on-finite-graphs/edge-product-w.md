# Inhomogeneous Bernoulli edge product

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and let $p_e \in \mathbb{R}$ be indexed by the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. For a configuration $\omega$, the product is $\prod_{e \in E} \big(p_e$ if $e$ is open, $1 - p_e$ if $e$ is closed$\big)$, over the edges of $G$ only.
