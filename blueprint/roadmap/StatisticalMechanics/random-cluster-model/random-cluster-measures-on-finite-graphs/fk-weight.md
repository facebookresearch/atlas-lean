# Unnormalized random-cluster weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The random-cluster weight of a configuration $\omega$ is $w_{G,p,q}(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$, where $o$ and $c$ are the numbers of open and closed edges of $G$ and $k(\omega)$ is the [number of open clusters](num-clusters.md) (connected components of the open subgraph, isolated vertices included). States of non-edge pairs do not affect the weight.
