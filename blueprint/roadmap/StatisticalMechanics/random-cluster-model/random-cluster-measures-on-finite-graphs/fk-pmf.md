# Ambient random-cluster law as a probability mass function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. The random-cluster law is the probability distribution on configurations (assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of elements of $V$, loops and non-edges included) giving each $\omega$ the mass $\phi_{G,p,q}(\omega)$, where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](fk-prob.md). Under it, the states of the non-edge pairs are independent fair bits.
