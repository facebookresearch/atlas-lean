# FKG inequality for connection events for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $0 < p < 1$, $q \ge 1$, and $x, y, u, v \in V$. Then

$$\phi_{G,p,q}(x \leftrightarrow y)\, \phi_{G,p,q}(u \leftrightarrow v) \le \phi_{G,p,q}(x \leftrightarrow y,\ u \leftrightarrow v),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](fk-prob.md) and $\{x \leftrightarrow y\}$ is the [connection event](conn-event.md).
