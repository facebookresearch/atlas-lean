# Inhomogeneous Bernoulli product over all edges but one

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, let $p_e \in \mathbb{R}$ be indexed by the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $e_0$ an unordered pair. For a configuration $\omega$, the product is taken over the edges $e \in E \setminus \{e_0\}$ of the factor $p_e$ if $e$ is open in $\omega$ and $1 - p_e$ otherwise.
