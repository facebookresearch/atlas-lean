# Probability of an event under the inhomogeneous random-cluster measure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, let $(p_e)$, $e$ ranging over the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, be edge parameters, $q \in \mathbb{R}$, and $A$ a set of configurations. Its probability is $\langle \mathbf{1}_A \rangle_{\mathbf p} = \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}(\omega)$, where $\phi^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}$ is the [inhomogeneous random-cluster probability](fk-prob-w.md); it is the probability of $A$ when $0 \le p_e \le 1$ on the edges of $G$ and $q > 0$.
