# Boundary-preserving automorphisms preserve wired FK edge marginals

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $\partial \subseteq V$, and let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $V$ with $\sigma(x) \sim \sigma(y) \iff x \sim y$ for all $x, y$ and $\sigma(x) \in \partial \iff x \in \partial$. For all $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ and every unordered pair $e$, $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\sigma(e) \text{ open}) = \phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(e \text{ open})$, where $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) and $\mu(e \text{ open}) = \sum_{\omega :\ \omega(e) \text{ open}} \mu(\omega)$ is the [edge marginal](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md).
