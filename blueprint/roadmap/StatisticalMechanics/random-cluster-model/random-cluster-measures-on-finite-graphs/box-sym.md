# Box symmetry of $\mathbb{Z}^d$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a box symmetry is a bijection $\tau : \mathbb{Z}^d \to \mathbb{Z}^d$ such that $\tau(x)$ and $\tau(y)$ are nearest neighbours exactly when $x$ and $y$ are, and $\tau(x) \in \Lambda_n \iff x \in \Lambda_n$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, where $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$.
