# Unnormalized inhomogeneous random-cluster expectation

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, let $(p_e)$, $e$ ranging over the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, be edge parameters, $q \in \mathbb{R}$, and $g$ a real function of configurations. The unnormalized expectation is $\sum_\omega g(\omega)\, w^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}(\omega)$, where $w^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}$ is the [inhomogeneous random-cluster weight](fk-weight-w.md).
