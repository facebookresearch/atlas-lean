# Expectation under the inhomogeneous random-cluster measure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, let $(p_e)$, $e$ ranging over the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, be edge parameters, $q \in \mathbb{R}$, and $g$ a real function of configurations. The expectation is $\langle g \rangle_{\mathbf p} = \sum_\omega g(\omega)\, \phi^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}(\omega)$, where $\phi^{\mathbf p}_{G,q}$ is the [inhomogeneous random-cluster probability](fk-prob-w.md); it is the expectation of $g$ when $0 \le p_e \le 1$ on the edges of $G$ and $q > 0$.
