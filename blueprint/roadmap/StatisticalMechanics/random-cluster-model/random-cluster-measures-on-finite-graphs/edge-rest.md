# Bernoulli product over the edges other than one edge

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, and $e$ an unordered pair of elements of $V$. For a configuration $\omega$, the product is taken over the edges $e' \in E \setminus \{e\}$ of the factor $p$ if $e'$ is open in $\omega$ and $1 - p$ otherwise.
