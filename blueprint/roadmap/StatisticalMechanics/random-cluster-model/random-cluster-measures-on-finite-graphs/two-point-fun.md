# Two-point function of the free random-cluster measure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x, y \in V$. The two-point function is $\phi_{G,p,q}(x \leftrightarrow y) = \sum_{\omega \in \{x \leftrightarrow y\}} \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega)$, where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](fk-prob.md) and $\{x \leftrightarrow y\}$ is the [connection event](conn-event.md); it is the probability that $x$ and $y$ are joined by an open path when $0 \le p \le 1$ and $q > 0$.
