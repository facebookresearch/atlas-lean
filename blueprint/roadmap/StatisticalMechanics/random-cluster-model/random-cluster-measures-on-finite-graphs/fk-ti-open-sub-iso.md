# Automorphism as an isomorphism of open subgraphs

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, and let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $V$ with $\sigma(x) \sim \sigma(y) \iff x \sim y$ for all $x, y$. For every configuration $\omega$, $\sigma$ is a graph isomorphism from the [open subgraph](open-sub.md) of $\omega^\sigma$ onto the open subgraph of $\omega$, where $\omega^\sigma$ is the [relabelled configuration](re-cfg.md) $\omega^\sigma(e) = \omega(\sigma(e))$.
