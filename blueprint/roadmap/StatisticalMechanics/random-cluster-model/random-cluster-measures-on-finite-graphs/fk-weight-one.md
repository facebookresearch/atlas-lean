# At $q = 1$ the random-cluster weight is the Bernoulli product

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. For every configuration $\omega$, the [random-cluster weight](fk-weight.md) at $q = 1$ equals the [Bernoulli product](edge-product.md) $p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)}$.
