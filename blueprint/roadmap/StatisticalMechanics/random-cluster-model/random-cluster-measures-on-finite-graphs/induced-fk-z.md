# Total FK weight of a fibre

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, $F$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $\psi$ a configuration. The fibre weight is $Z_F(\psi) = \sum_{\rho \in \mathcal{F}_\psi} w_{G,p,q}(\rho)$, where $w_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster weight](fk-weight.md) and $\mathcal{F}_\psi$ is the [fibre](cond-fibre.md) of configurations agreeing with $\psi$ off $F$.
