# Graph automorphisms preserve the free FK probability

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, and let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $V$ with $\sigma(x) \sim \sigma(y) \iff x \sim y$ for all $x, y$. For all $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ and every configuration $\omega$, $\phi_{G,p,q}(\omega^\sigma) = \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega)$, where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](fk-prob.md) and $\omega^\sigma$ is the [relabelled configuration](re-cfg.md) $\omega^\sigma(e) = \omega(\sigma(e))$.
