# Open-subgraph isomorphism for a pulled-back configuration

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V$ and $W$, and let $\sigma : V \to W$ be a bijection with $x \sim_G y \iff \sigma(x) \sim_H \sigma(y)$ for all $x, y$. For every configuration $\omega$ on $W$, $\sigma$ is a graph isomorphism from the [open subgraph](open-sub.md) of $G$ for $\omega^\sigma$ onto the open subgraph of $H$ for $\omega$, where $\omega^\sigma$ is the [pulled-back configuration](re-cfg-iso.md) on $V$, $\omega^\sigma(e) = \omega(\sigma(e))$.
