# Random-cluster expectation of an observable

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, and $X$ a real function of configurations. The expectation is $\phi_{G,p,q}[X] = \sum_\omega \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega) X(\omega)$, the sum running over all configurations, where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](fk-prob.md); it is the expectation of $X$ when $0 \le p \le 1$ and $q > 0$.
