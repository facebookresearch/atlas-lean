# Vertex boundary of a translated box

For $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, a vertex $y$ of the [translated box](fvs-trans-box.md) $\Lambda_m + v$ is a boundary vertex when $y - v \in \partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$ ($m - 1$ truncated at $0$), where $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$.
