# Logarithmic $\beta$-derivative of the random-cluster weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$, let $J_e$ be couplings indexed by the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For a configuration $\omega$, the score is

$$\sum_{e \in E} \frac{J_e}{p_e(\beta)} \big(\mathbf{1}_e(\omega) - p_e(\beta)\big),$$

where $p_e(\beta) = 1 - e^{-\beta J_e}$ are the [edge parameters](beta-params.md), $\mathbf{1}_e(\omega)$ is $1$ if $e$ is open in $\omega$ and $0$ otherwise, and $x/0 = 0$.
