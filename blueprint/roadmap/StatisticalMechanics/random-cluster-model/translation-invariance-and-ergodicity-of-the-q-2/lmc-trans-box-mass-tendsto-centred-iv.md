# Translated wired box masses converge to the infinite-volume mass

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, viewed also as pairs of sites. Then $\phi^w_{\Lambda_{N+m} + v,p,2}[t^{(N+m)} + v] \to \phi^1_{p,2}(O_t)$ as $m \to \infty$, where $\phi^w_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v]$ is the [translated wired box mass](lmc-trans-box-mass.md) (the probability, under the wired random-cluster measure on the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$ at $(p, 2)$, that every pair of $t^{(m)} + v$ is open, $t^{(m)}$ being $t$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$), $O_t$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $t$ is open, and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
