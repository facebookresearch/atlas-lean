# Translate of an edge of $\Lambda_N$ as an edge of $\Lambda_{N+r}$

For $d, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ and $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ with $\max_i |v_i| \le r$, the translate sends an unordered pair $\{a, b\}$ of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ to the pair $\{v + a, v + b\}$ of vertices of $\Lambda_{N+r}$, where $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$.
