# Density of cylinder functions among bounded continuous functions

For every $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the [cylinder functions](idc-cylinder-bcf-set.md) (the algebra generated by the edge indicators $\omega \mapsto \mathbf{1}[e \text{ open}]$, viewed as bounded continuous functions) are dense in the bounded continuous real functions on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) for the supremum norm.
