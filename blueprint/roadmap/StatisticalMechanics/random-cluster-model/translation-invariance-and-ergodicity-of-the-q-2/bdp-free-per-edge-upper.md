# Free $q = 2$ box edge marginals are at most the free density

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $e$ an unordered pair of vertices of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, and $0 < p < 1$. Then $\phi_n(e \text{ open}) \le \theta^0_e(p)$, where $\phi_n(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md) of the random-cluster probability on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$ and $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
