# Translated wired box measure

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$ and $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, the translated wired box measure is the image of the wired box measure $\phi^1_n$ under the shift $\tau_g$, where $\phi^1_n$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ at $(p, q)$ and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
