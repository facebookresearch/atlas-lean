# Continuous monomial indicator that every edge of a set is open

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite set $s$ of unordered pairs of sites, the monomial indicator is the continuous function $\omega \mapsto \prod_{e \in s} \mathbf{1}[e \text{ open in } \omega]$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites); it is the indicator of the event that every pair of $s$ is open (the constant $1$ when $s$ is empty).
