# Wired shift invariance of an all-open event from decaying differences

Let $d, N, N' \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, $t$ a finite set of pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ and $t'$ a finite set of pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_{N'}$ with $t + v = t'$ as sets of pairs of sites. If

$$\phi^w_{\Lambda_{N+m} + v,p,2}[t^{(N+m)} + v] - \phi^1_{N'+m}(O_{t'}) \to 0 \qquad (m \to \infty),$$

then $\phi^1_{p,2}(O_t) = \phi^1_{p,2}(O_{t + v})$. Here $\phi^w_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v]$ is the [translated wired box mass](lmc-trans-box-mass.md) (the probability, under the wired random-cluster measure on the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$ at $(p, 2)$, that every pair of $t^{(m)} + v$ is open, $t^{(m)}$ being $t$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$), $O_t$ is the event that every pair of $t$ is open, $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
