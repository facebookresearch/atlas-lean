# Free placement hypothesis for a shift via box-mass limits

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, the free placement hypothesis holds when for every finite set $s$ of unordered pairs of sites there are $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and a set $t$ of pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ with $s = t$ (as pairs of sites) such that $\phi_{\Lambda_{N+m} - g,p,2}[t^{(N+m)} - g] \to \phi^0_{p,2}(O_{s - g})$ as $m \to \infty$, where $\phi_{\Lambda_{N+m} - g,p,2}[t^{(N+m)} - g]$ is the [translated free box mass](lmc-trans-box-mass-free.md) (the probability, under the random-cluster measure on the translated box $\Lambda_{N+m} - g$ at $(p, 2)$, that every pair of $t^{(N+m)} - g$ is open, $t^{(N+m)}$ being $t$ viewed in $\Lambda_{N+m}$), $O_s$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $s$ is open, and $\phi^0_{p,2}$ is the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures).
