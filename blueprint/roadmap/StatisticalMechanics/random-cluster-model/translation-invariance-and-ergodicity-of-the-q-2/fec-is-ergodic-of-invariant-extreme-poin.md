# Extreme invariant measures are ergodic

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$, and let $G$ act on configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$ by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(g^{-1}e)$. If $\mu$ is an [extreme point](is-invariant-extreme-point.md) of the translation-invariant probability measures on $\{0, 1\}^E$, then $\mu$ is ergodic: it is translation invariant and every measurable $G$-invariant event has measure $0$ or $\mu(\{0, 1\}^E)$.
