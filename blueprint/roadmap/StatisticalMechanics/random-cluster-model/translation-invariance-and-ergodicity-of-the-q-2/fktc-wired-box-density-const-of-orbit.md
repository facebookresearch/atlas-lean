# Wired edge density is constant on orbits of a box symmetry

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $e_0$ a pair of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and $B$ a finite set of such pairs, where $\tau$ is a [box symmetry](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/box-sym.md) of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and every pair of $B$ has the form $\tau^k(e_0)$ for some $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $\theta^1_e(p) = \theta^1_{e_0}(p)$ for every $e \in B$, where $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
