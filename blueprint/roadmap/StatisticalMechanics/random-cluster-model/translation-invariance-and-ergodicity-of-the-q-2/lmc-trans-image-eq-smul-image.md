# Images of translated edge sets are shifted images

For $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and a finite set $t$ of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, the set $t + v$ of pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_m + v$, viewed as pairs of sites, equals the translate by $v$ of $t$ viewed as pairs of sites.
