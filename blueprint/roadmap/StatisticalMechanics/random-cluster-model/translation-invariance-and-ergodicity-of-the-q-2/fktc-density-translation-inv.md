# Translation invariance of free and wired densities if marginals merge

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $v, x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $0 < p < 1$. Suppose there is $N$ with $x, y, x + v, y + v \in \Lambda_N$ such that

$$\phi^w_{N+k}(\{x, y\} \text{ open}) - \phi^w_{N+k}(\{x + v, y + v\} \text{ open}) \to 0 \qquad (k \to \infty),$$

and there is (possibly another) $N$ with these four sites in $\Lambda_N$ such that the same holds for the free marginals $\phi_{N+k}$. Then $\theta^1_{\{x,y\}}(p) = \theta^1_{\{x+v,y+v\}}(p)$ and $\theta^0_{\{x,y\}}(p) = \theta^0_{\{x+v,y+v\}}(p)$. Here $\phi^w_k(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md) of the wired random-cluster probability on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_k = \Lambda_k \setminus \Lambda_{k-1}$ at $(p, 2)$, $\phi_k(e \text{ open})$ is the corresponding free marginal, $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$, and $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
