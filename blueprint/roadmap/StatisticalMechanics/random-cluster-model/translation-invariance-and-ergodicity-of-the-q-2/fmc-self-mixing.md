# Self-mixing on cylinder events

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$, and let $G$ act on configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$ by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(g^{-1}e)$, with $E$ countable. A measure $\mu$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ is self-mixing when for every measurable cylinder event $A$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $g \in G$ with $|\mu(A \cap \tau_g^{-1}(A)) - \mu(A)^2| < \varepsilon$ (real values of $\mu$).
