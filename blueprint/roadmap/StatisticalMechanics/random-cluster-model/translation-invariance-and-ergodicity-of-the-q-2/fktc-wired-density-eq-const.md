# Constant wired density gives a lower bound on expected open edges

Let $d, N, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le n$, $0 < p < 1$, $e_0$ a pair of vertices of $\Lambda_N$, and $B$ a finite set of such pairs with $\theta^1_e(p) = \theta^1_{e_0}(p)$ for every $e \in B$. Then

$$|B|\, \theta^1_{e_0}(p) \le \sum_\omega o_B(\omega)\, \phi^w_{\Lambda_n,p,2}(\omega),$$

where the sum runs over configurations of $\Lambda_n$, $o_B(\omega)$ is the [number of open pairs](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/dfi-open-edge-count.md) of $\omega$ among the pairs of $B$ (viewed in $\Lambda_n$), $\phi^w_{\Lambda_n,p,2}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_n$, and $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
