# Shift-decay functional of a sequence of measures

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, probability measures $(\mu_n)$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites), $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and a bounded continuous function $f$, the shift-decay functional is $D_n(f) = \int f\, d\mu_n - \int f \circ \tau_g\, d\mu_n$, where $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
