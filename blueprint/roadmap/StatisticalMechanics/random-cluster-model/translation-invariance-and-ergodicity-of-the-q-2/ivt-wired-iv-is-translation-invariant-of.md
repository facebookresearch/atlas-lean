# Wired translation invariance from integral shift decay

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Suppose that for every $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and for every strictly increasing $\varphi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ along which the wired box measures $\phi^1_{\varphi(n)}$ converge weakly to $\phi^1_{p,q}$, [wired integral shift decay](ivt-wired-integral-shift-decay.md) holds for $(g, \varphi)$. Then $\phi^1_{p,q}$ is translation invariant: every shift $\tau_g$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, preserves it, where $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$, $\phi^1_{p,q}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), and $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, q)$.
