# Equal free probabilities for $t$ and $v + t$ from matching box limits

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, viewed also as pairs of sites. If $\phi_{\Lambda_{N+m} + v,p,2}[t^{(N+m)} + v] \to \phi^0_{p,2}(O_{t + v})$ as $m \to \infty$, then $\phi^0_{p,2}(O_t) = \phi^0_{p,2}(O_{t + v})$. Here $\phi_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v]$ is the [translated free box mass](lmc-trans-box-mass-free.md) (the probability, under the random-cluster measure on the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ at $(p, 2)$, that every pair of $t^{(m)} + v$ is open, $t^{(m)}$ being $t$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$), $O_s$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $t$ is open (similarly $O_{t+v}$ for the translate), and $\phi^0_{p,2}$ is the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures).
