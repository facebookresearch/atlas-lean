# Every finite family of edge sets is realized in some box

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite family $\mathcal{P}$ of finite sets of unordered pairs of sites, there is $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that every $T \in \mathcal{P}$ is realised in $\Lambda_N$, where a set of pairs of sites is realised in $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ when each of its pairs has both elements in $\Lambda_N$.
