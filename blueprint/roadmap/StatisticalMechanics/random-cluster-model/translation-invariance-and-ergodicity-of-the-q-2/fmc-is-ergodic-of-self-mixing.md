# Translation-invariant self-mixing measures are ergodic

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$, and let $G$ act on configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$ by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(g^{-1}e)$, with $E$ countable. If $\mu$ is a translation-invariant probability measure on $\{0, 1\}^E$ that is [self-mixing](fmc-self-mixing.md), then $\mu$ is ergodic.
