# Inclusion–exclusion coefficient of a multi-open event

For a set $E$, finite sets $F, T \subseteq E$ and a pattern $\tau : E \to \{0, 1\}$, the coefficient is

$$\prod_{e \in T} (2\tau_e - 1) \cdot \prod_{e \in F \setminus T} (1 - \tau_e),$$

that is, the product of $\pm 1$ over $T$ ($+1$ where $\tau_e$ is open) times the product over $F \setminus T$ of $1$ if $\tau_e$ is closed and $0$ if it is open.
