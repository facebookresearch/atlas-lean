# Decay of the edge-indicator functional from equal wired densities

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, $e, e'$ unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ (viewed as pairs of sites), $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ with $e - g = e'$, and $0 < p < 1$. If $\theta^1_e(p) = \theta^1_{e'}(p)$, then

$$\int \mathbf{1}_e\, d\phi^1_n - \int \mathbf{1}_e \circ \tau_g\, d\phi^1_n \to 0 \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\mathbf{1}_e(\omega) = \mathbf{1}[e \text{ open}]$, $\phi^1_n$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$, and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
