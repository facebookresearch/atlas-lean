# Disjoint-box factorization bound for box measures

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and measures $(\mu_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites), the factorization bound holds when for every finite family $\mathcal{P}$ of finite sets of pairs of sites and every $N$ such that every $T \in \mathcal{P}$ is realised in $\Lambda_N$, there are $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $r \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$, $T \cup (T' - g)$ is realised in $\Lambda_{N+r}$ and

$$\mu_{N+r}\big(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})\big) \le \mu_N(O_T)\, \mu_N(O_{T'}),$$

where a set of pairs of sites is realised in $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ when each of its pairs has both elements in $\Lambda_N$, $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$, and measures are taken as real values.
