# Submodule of functions with vanishing wired shift decay

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\varphi : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, the wired decay set is the linear subspace of continuous real functions $h$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites) such that $D_n(h) \to 0$, where $D_n(h) = \int h\, d\phi^1_{\varphi(n)} - \int h \circ \tau_g\, d\phi^1_{\varphi(n)}$, $\phi^1_m$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
