# Finite-box product domination from the factorization bound

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$, suppose the [disjoint-box factorization bound](fwm-wired-disjoint-box-factorise.md) holds for the wired box measures $(\phi^1_m)_m$ at $(p, 2)$, and let $\mathcal{P}$ be a finite family of finite sets of unordered pairs of sites each realised in $\Lambda_N$. Then there is $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ such that for all sufficiently large $m$ and all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$,

$$\phi^1_m\big(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})\big) \le \phi^1_N(O_T)\, \phi^1_N(O_{T'}),$$

where a set of pairs of sites is realised in $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ when each of its pairs has both elements in $\Lambda_N$, $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
