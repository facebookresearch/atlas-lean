# Domain monotonicity of wired $q = 2$ box measures

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le m$, $0 < p < 1$, and $S$ an increasing set of configurations of $\Lambda_N$. Then $\phi^1_m(C_S) \le \phi^1_N(C_S)$, where $C_S$ is the cylinder event of the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose restriction to the pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$ lies in $S$, and $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$.
