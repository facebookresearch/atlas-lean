# Translated wired box mass equals the centered box mass

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le m$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, viewed also as pairs of sites. Then $\phi^w_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v] = \phi^1_m(O_t)$, where $\phi^w_{\Lambda_m + v,p,2}[t^{(m)} + v]$ is the [translated wired box mass](lmc-trans-box-mass.md) (the probability, under the wired random-cluster measure on the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m + v$ at $(p, 2)$, that every pair of $t^{(m)} + v$ is open, $t^{(m)}$ being $t$ viewed in $\Lambda_m$), $O_t$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $t$ is open, and $\phi^1_m$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$.
