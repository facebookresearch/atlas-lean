# Translation invariance of the wired density given merging marginals

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $v, x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $0 < p < 1$. Suppose there is $N$ with $x, y, x + v, y + v \in \Lambda_N$ such that $\phi^w_{N+k}(\{x, y\} \text{ open}) - \phi^w_{N+k}(\{x + v, y + v\} \text{ open}) \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$. Then $\theta^1_{\{x,y\}}(p) = \theta^1_{\{x+v,y+v\}}(p)$, where $\phi^w_m(e \text{ open})$ is the [edge marginal](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/edge-marg-prob.md) of the wired random-cluster probability on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ with boundary $\partial\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m \setminus \Lambda_{m-1}$ at $(p, 2)$ and $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
