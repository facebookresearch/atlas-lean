# Upper decorrelation of the wired measure from factorization

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$, and suppose the [disjoint-box factorization bound](fwm-wired-disjoint-box-factorise.md) holds for the wired box measures $(\phi^1_m)_m$ at $(p, 2)$. Then $\phi^1_{p,2}$ has [upper decay](fmu-upper-decay.md): for every finite family $\mathcal{P}$ of finite sets of pairs of sites and every $\delta > 0$ there is $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ with $\phi^1_{p,2}(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})) \le \phi^1_{p,2}(O_T)\, \phi^1_{p,2}(O_{T'}) + \delta$ for all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$. Here $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
