# Domain monotonicity for translated pairs of multi-open events

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le m$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $0 < p < 1$, and $T, T'$ finite sets of unordered pairs of sites such that $T \cup (T' - g)$ is realised in $\Lambda_N$. Then

$$\phi^1_m\big(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})\big) \le \phi^1_N\big(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})\big),$$

where a set of pairs of sites is realised in $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$ when each of its pairs has both elements in $\Lambda_N$, $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, and $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$.
