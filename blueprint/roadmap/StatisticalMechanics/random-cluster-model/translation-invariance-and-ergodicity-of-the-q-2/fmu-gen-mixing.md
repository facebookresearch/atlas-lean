# Mixing for finite families of cylinder events

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$, and let $G$ act on configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$ by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(g^{-1}e)$. A measure $\mu$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ is mixing for finite families when for every finite family $\mathcal{A}$ of measurable cylinder events (events determined by the states of finitely many coordinates) and every $\delta > 0$ there is $g \in G$ with $|\mu(A \cap \tau_g^{-1}(B)) - \mu(A)\mu(B)| < \delta$ for all $A, B \in \mathcal{A}$ (real values of $\mu$).
