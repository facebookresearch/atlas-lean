# Domain monotonicity of wired multi-open probabilities

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $N \le m$, $0 < p < 1$, and $t$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_N$. Then $\phi^1_m(O_t) \le \phi^1_N(O_t)$, where $O_t$ is the event that every pair of $t$ (as pairs of sites) is open and $\phi^1_k$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_k = \{-k, \dots, k\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$.
