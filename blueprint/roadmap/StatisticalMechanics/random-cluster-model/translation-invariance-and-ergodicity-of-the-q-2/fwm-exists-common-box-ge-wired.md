# Wired box products approximate infinite-volume products

Let $d, N_0 \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $\delta > 0$, and $\mathcal{P}$ a finite family of finite sets of unordered pairs of sites. There is $m \ge N_0$ such that for all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$,

$$\big|\phi^1_m(O_T)\, \phi^1_m(O_{T'}) - \phi^1_{p,2}(O_T)\, \phi^1_{p,2}(O_{T'})\big| < \delta,$$

where $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\phi^1_m$ is the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) on $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$ at $(p, 2)$, and $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures).
