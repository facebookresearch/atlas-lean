# Finite-box disjoint product domination of multi-open events

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and measures $(\mu_m)_{m \in \mathbb{N}}$ on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites), finite-box disjoint product domination holds when for every finite family $\mathcal{P}$ of finite sets of pairs of sites and every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ there is $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ such that for all sufficiently large $m$ and all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$, $\mu_m(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})) \le \mu_N(O_T)\,\mu_N(O_{T'})$; here $O_T$ is the event that every pair of the finite set $T$ is open, $\mathbb{Z}^d$ acts on configurations by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(e - g)$, where $e - g$ is the pair $e$ translated by $-g$, and measures are taken as real values.
