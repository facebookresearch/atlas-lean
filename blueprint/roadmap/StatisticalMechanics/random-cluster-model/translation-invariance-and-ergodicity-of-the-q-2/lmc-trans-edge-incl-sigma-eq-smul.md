# Translated box edges are shifted centered box edges

For $d, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and an unordered pair $e$ of vertices of $\Lambda_m = \{-m, \dots, m\}^d$, the pair $e + v$ of vertices of the translated box $\Lambda_m + v$, viewed as a pair of sites, is the translate by $v$ of $e$ viewed as a pair of sites.
