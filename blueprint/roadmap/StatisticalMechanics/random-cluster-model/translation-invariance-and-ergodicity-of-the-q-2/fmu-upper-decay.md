# Upper decay of correlations of multi-open events

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$, and let $G$ act on configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$ by $(\tau_g\omega)(e) = \omega(g^{-1}e)$. A measure $\mu$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ has upper decay when for every finite family $\mathcal{P}$ of finite subsets of $E$ and every $\delta > 0$ there is $g \in G$ with $\mu(O_T \cap \tau_g^{-1}(O_{T'})) \le \mu(O_T)\mu(O_{T'}) + \delta$ for all $T, T' \in \mathcal{P}$, where $O_T$ is the event that every coordinate of $T$ is open (real values of $\mu$).
