# $2n(2n+1)^{d-1}$ sites of $\Lambda_n$ stay inside after a unit shift

Let $d \ge 1$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $i$ a coordinate direction and $b \in \{1, -1\}$. The number of sites $x \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with $x_i + b \in \{-n, \dots, n\}$ (that is, $x + b e_i \in \Lambda_n$) is $2n\,(2n + 1)^{d-1}$.
