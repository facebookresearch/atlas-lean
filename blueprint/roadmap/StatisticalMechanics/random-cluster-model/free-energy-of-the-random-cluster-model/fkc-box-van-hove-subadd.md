# Van Hove subadditivity of the box free energy for $q \ge 1$

Let $d, m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and $q \ge 1$, and put $T = \lfloor (2n + 1)/(2m + 1) \rfloor$, the number of translates of $\Lambda_m$ that fit along a side of $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$u(n) \le T^d\, u(m) + \big(|E_n| - T^d\, |E_m|\big)\big(-\log(1 - \sigma(s))\big),$$

where $u(n) = -\log Z^E_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(s), q)$ is the [box free energy](fkc-u.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, $|E_n|$ is the number of edges of $\Lambda_n$, and $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$.
