# Isomorphism of translated induced lattice graphs

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, let $V, W$ be finite sets with maps $f : V \to \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $g : W \to \mathbb{Z}^d$, and let $\varphi : V \to W$ be a bijection with $g(\varphi(x)) = f(x) + v$ for every $x \in V$. Equip $V$ with the graph in which $x, y$ are adjacent when $f(x), f(y)$ are nearest neighbours in $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and $W$ likewise with $g$. Then $\varphi$ is a graph isomorphism between these two graphs.
