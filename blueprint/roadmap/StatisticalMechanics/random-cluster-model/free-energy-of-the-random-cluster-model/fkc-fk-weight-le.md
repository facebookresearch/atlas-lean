# Random-cluster weight is at most $q^{|V|}$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, the [random-cluster weight](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-weight.md) satisfies $p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)} \le q^{|V|}$, where $o(\omega)$ and $c(\omega)$ count the open and closed edges of $G$ and $k(\omega)$ is the number of open clusters.
