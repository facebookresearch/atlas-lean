# Doubling bound for the box free energy when $q \ge 1$

Let $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and $q \ge 1$. Then

$$u(2K + 1) \le 2^d\, u(K) + \big(2d\,|J| + (2^d + 1)\cdot 2d\,|J|\big)\big(-\log(1 - \sigma(s))\big),$$

where $u(n) = -\log Z^E_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(s), q)$ is the [box free energy](fkc-u.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$, and $J$ is the [set of sites of $\Lambda_{2K+1}$ with some coordinate equal to $0$](qp-jset.md).
