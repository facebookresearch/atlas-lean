# The all-tag restriction has the partition function of $\Lambda_{2K+1}$

For $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the graph induced by the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ on the sites whose [orthant tag](qp-box-tag.md) (or absence of tag) lies in the set of all possible tag values, that is on all sites, has the same ambient normalizer $Z(p, q)$ as the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_{2K+1}$, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
