# Doubling inequality for $-\log Z$ of the free $q = 2$ FK model

For $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$u_t(2K + 1) \le 2^d\, u_t(K) + \big(2d\,|J| + (2^d + 1)\cdot 2d\,|J|\big)\big(-\log(1 - \sigma(t))\big),$$

where $u_t(n) = -\log Z_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(t), 2)$ is the [negative log ambient normalizer](agl-ambient-neg-log-z.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$, $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$, and $J$ is the [set of sites of $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ with some coordinate equal to $0$](qp-jset.md). The coefficient equals $(2^d + 2)\cdot 2d\,|J|$.
