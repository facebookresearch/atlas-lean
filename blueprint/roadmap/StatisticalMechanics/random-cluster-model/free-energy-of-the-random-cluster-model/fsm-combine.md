# Gluing edge configurations on a disjoint union

Let $V$ and $W$ be sets, $\sigma$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $\tau$ one for $W$. The glued configuration on the unordered pairs of $V \sqcup W$ gives a pair inside $V$ its $\sigma$-state, a pair inside $W$ its $\tau$-state, and declares every pair with one element in $V$ and one in $W$ closed.
