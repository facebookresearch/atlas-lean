# Connected components of a disjoint union of graphs

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on sets $V$ and $W$. The map sending a connected component of the disjoint union $G \sqcup H$ to the corresponding component of $G$ or of $H$ is a bijection from the components of $G \sqcup H$ onto the disjoint union of the components of $G$ and the components of $H$.
