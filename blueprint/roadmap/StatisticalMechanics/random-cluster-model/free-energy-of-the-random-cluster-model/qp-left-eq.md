# Left part of a tag split has the block partition function

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$ and let $\tau : U \to T$ assign a tag to each vertex, let $S$ be a finite set of tags, $\theta$ a tag not in $S$, and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. Let $R$ be the [graph induced on the vertices with tags in $S \cup \{\theta\}$](qp-rest-g.md). Then the graph induced by $R$ on its vertices of tag $\theta$ has the same ambient normalizer as the [block](qp-blk-g.md) of $\theta$: both equal $Z(p, q)$ for the block, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
