# FK partition function at least the all-closed weight for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then $(1 - p)^{|E|} \le Z_G(p, q)$, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
