# Box free energy $-\log Z^E$ of the random-cluster model

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the box free energy is

$$u(n) = -\log Z^E_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(s), q),$$

where $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ carries its nearest-neighbour edges, $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$, $Z^E_G(p, q)$ is the [edge partition function](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/ecz-fk-zedge.md) $Z^E_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$, the sum over the configurations $\omega$ that are closed on every unordered pair of vertices that is not an edge of $G$ ($o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters), and $\log x$ means $\log |x|$ for $x \ne 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$.
