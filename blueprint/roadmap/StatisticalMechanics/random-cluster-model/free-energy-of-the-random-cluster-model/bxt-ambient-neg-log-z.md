# Negative log ambient normalizer of the box $\Lambda_n$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the negative log ambient normalizer of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ with its nearest-neighbour edges is

$$u_t(n) = -\log Z_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(t), 2),$$

where $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ and $Z_G(p, q)$ is the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
