# Per-edge free energy converges along $K_j$ for $d, q \ge 1$

For $d \ge 1$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and $q \ge 1$, there is $L \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(K_j) \to L$ as $j \to \infty$, where $f(n) = u(n)/|E_n|$ is the [per-edge box free energy](fkc-f.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $K_j = 2^{j+1} - 1$ are the [scales](agl-k.md).
