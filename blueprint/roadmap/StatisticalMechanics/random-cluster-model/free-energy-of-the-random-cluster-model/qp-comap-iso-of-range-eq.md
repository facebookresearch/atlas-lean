# Isomorphism of induced graphs from injections with equal range

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$ and $f : V \to U$, $g : W \to U$ injective maps with the same range. Equip $V$ (resp. $W$) with the graph in which two elements are adjacent when their images under $f$ (resp. $g$) are adjacent in $K_0$. Then $g^{-1} \circ f : V \to W$ is a graph isomorphism between these graphs.
