# Unit shifts of sites staying in the box are pairs in direction $i$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $i$ a coordinate direction and $b \in \{1, -1\}$. If $x \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $x + b e_i \in \Lambda_n$, then $(x, x + b e_i)$ is an [ordered nearest-neighbour pair of $\Lambda_n$ in direction $i$](edge-axis-i.md).
