# Iterated and single induced graphs share the FK partition function

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$, let $V, X$ be finite sets, and let $g : W \to U$, $f : V \to W$ and $h : X \to U$ be maps such that $g \circ f$ and $h$ are injective with the same range. Then for all $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the graph on $V$ obtained by pulling back $K_0$ along $g$ and then along $f$ and the graph on $X$ obtained by pulling back $K_0$ along $h$ have the same ambient normalizer $Z(p, q)$, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
