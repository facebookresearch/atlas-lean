# Inclusion of summand components into a disjoint union

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on sets $V$ and $W$. The map from the disjoint union of the connected components of $G$ and of $H$ to the components of $G \sqcup H$ sends the component of $a$ in $G$ to the component of $a$ in $G \sqcup H$, and likewise for $H$.
