# Convergence of the per-edge box free energy

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the per-edge box free energy converges when there is $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ with $f(n) \to \rho$ as $n \to \infty$, where $f(n)$ is the [per-edge box free energy](fkc-f.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$.
