# Summability of doubling corrections relative to edge counts

Let $d \ge 1$ and let $(I_j)$ be nonnegative reals with

$$I_j \le \big((2^d + 2)(2d \cdot d) + d(d + 2)\big)\,(4K_j + 3)^{d-1}$$

for every $j$, where $K_j = 2^{j+1} - 1$. Then $\sum_j I_j / E(K_{j+1})$ converges, where $E(n)$ is the [number of edges](agl-e.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$.
