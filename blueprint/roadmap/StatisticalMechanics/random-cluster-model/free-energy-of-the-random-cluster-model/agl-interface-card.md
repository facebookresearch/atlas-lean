# Number of edges between a vertex subset and its complement

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$ and $P \subseteq U$. The interface count is the number of edges of the [transported graph](agl-glue-graph.md) on $P \sqcup (U \setminus P)$ that are not edges of the disjoint union of the graphs induced by $K_0$ on $P$ and on $U \setminus P$; equivalently, the number of edges of $K_0$ with one endpoint in $P$ and the other in $U \setminus P$.
