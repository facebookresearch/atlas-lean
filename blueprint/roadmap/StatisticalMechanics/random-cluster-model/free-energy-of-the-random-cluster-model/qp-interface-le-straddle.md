# Interface edges of a split are at most the straddling edges

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$ and $P \subseteq U$. The [interface count](agl-interface-card.md) of the split of $K_0$ into $P$ and $U \setminus P$ is at most the number of edges of $K_0$ straddling $P$, where an edge straddles a set $A$ of vertices when exactly one of its endpoints lies in $A$.
