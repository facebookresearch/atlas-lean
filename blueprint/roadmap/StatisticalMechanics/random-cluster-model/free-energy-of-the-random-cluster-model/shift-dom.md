# Sites of $\Lambda_n$ whose shift in coordinate $i$ stays in the box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a coordinate direction $i$ and $b \in \mathbb{Z}$, the shift domain is the set of sites $x \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with $x_i + b \in \{-n, \dots, n\}$, that is, with $x + b e_i \in \Lambda_n$.
