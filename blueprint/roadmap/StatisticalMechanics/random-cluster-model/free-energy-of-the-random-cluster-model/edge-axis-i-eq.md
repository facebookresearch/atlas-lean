# Pairs in direction $i$ are the forward and backward shifts

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a coordinate direction $i$, the set of [ordered nearest-neighbour pairs of $\Lambda_n$ in direction $i$](edge-axis-i.md) is

$$\{(x, x + e_i) : x \in \Lambda_n,\ x + e_i \in \Lambda_n\} \cup \{(x, x - e_i) : x \in \Lambda_n,\ x - e_i \in \Lambda_n\},$$

where $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $e_i$ is the $i$-th unit vector.
