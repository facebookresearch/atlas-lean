# Box Fekete data from a uniform bound and an oscillation bound

Let $d \ge 1$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Suppose that there is $M$ with $|u_t(K_j)/E(K_j)| \le M$ for all $j$, and that there are $b : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ with $b(n) \to \infty$ and $(\mathrm{osc}_j)$ with $\mathrm{osc}_j \to 0$ such that $|F_{\Lambda_n}(t) - F_{\Lambda_{K_{b(n)}}}(t)| \le \mathrm{osc}_{b(n)}$ for every $n$. Then the [box data at $t$](agl-additive-box-fekete-data.md) hold. Here $K_j = 2^{j+1} - 1$, $u_t(n)$ is the [negative log ambient normalizer](agl-ambient-neg-log-z.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ at $q = 2$, $E(n)$ its [number of edges](agl-e.md), and $F_{\Lambda_n}(t)$ the [ambient tilted log-normalizer](../convexity-of-the-free-energy-and-edge-densities/ivp2-ambient-tilt-log-z.md) of $\Lambda_n$ at $q = 2$.
