# Components of a disjoint union sent to components of the summands

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on sets $V$ and $W$. The map from the connected components of the disjoint union $G \sqcup H$ to the disjoint union of the components of $G$ and of $H$ sends the component of a vertex $a \in V$ to the component of $a$ in $G$, and the component of $b \in W$ to the component of $b$ in $H$.
