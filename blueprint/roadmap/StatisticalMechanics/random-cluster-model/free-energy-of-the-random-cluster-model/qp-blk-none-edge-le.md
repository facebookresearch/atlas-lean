# The hyperplane block has at most $2d|J|$ edges

For $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$, the graph induced by the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ on $J$ has at most $2d\,|J|$ edges, where $J$ is the [set of sites of $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ with some coordinate equal to $0$](qp-jset.md).
