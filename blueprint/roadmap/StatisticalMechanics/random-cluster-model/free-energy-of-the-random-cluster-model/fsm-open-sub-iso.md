# Graph isomorphism restricted to open subgraphs

Let $\varphi : G \to H$ be an isomorphism of simple graphs on finite sets $V$ and $W$, and let $\omega$ be an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of elements of $W$. Let $\omega^\varphi$ be the configuration on $V$ given by $\omega^\varphi(\{a, b\}) = \omega(\{\varphi(a), \varphi(b)\})$. Then $\varphi$ is an isomorphism from the open subgraph of $G$ under $\omega^\varphi$ onto the open subgraph of $H$ under $\omega$ (the open subgraph keeps the edges that are open).
