# Ordered neighbour pairs of $\Lambda_n$ as a union over directions

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the set of ordered pairs $(x, y)$ of nearest-neighbour sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with both $x, y \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ is the union over the coordinate directions $i \in \{1, \dots, d\}$ of the [ordered nearest-neighbour pairs of $\Lambda_n$ in direction $i$](edge-axis-i.md).
