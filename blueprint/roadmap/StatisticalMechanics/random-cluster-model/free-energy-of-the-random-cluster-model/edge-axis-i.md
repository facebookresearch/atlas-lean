# Ordered nearest-neighbour pairs of $\Lambda_n$ in direction $i$

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a coordinate direction $i$, the pairs in direction $i$ are the elements $(x, y)$ of the set of ordered pairs $(x, y)$ of nearest-neighbour sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with both $x, y \in \Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ with $x_i \ne y_i$.
