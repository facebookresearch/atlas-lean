# Per-edge box free energy

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $s, q \in \mathbb{R}$, the per-edge box free energy is $f(n) = u(n)/|E_n|$, where $u(n) = -\log Z^E_{\Lambda_n}(\sigma(s), q)$ is the [box free energy](fkc-u.md) of $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$, $|E_n|$ is the number of edges of $\Lambda_n$, and the quotient is read as $0$ when $|E_n| = 0$.
