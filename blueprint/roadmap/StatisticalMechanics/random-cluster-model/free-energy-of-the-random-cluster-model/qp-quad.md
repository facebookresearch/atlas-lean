# Translation of $\Lambda_K$ onto an orthant block of $\Lambda_{2K+1}$

For $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$ and a sign pattern $s \in \{+, -\}^d$, the map $y \mapsto y + v_s$, with $v_s$ the [translation vector](qp-vshift.md) $(\pm(K + 1))_i$, is a bijection from $\Lambda_K = \{-K, \dots, K\}^d$ onto the set of sites of $\Lambda_{2K+1}$ with [orthant tag](qp-box-tag.md) $s$.
