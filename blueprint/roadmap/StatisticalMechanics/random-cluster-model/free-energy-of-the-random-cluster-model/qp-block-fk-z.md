# Orthant blocks have the FK partition function of $\Lambda_K$

Let $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$, $s \in \{+, -\}^d$ a sign pattern, and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The graph induced by the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ on the sites with [orthant tag](qp-box-tag.md) $s$ has the same ambient normalizer as the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_K = \{-K, \dots, K\}^d$: both equal $Z_{\Lambda_K}(p, q)$, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
