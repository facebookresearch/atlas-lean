# Isomorphism invariance of the ambient FK normalizer

Let $G$ and $H$ be isomorphic finite simple graphs and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $Z_G(p, q) = Z_H(p, q)$, where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
