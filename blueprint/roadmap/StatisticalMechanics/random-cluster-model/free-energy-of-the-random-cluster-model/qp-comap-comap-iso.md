# Isomorphism of an iterated induced graph with a single one

Let $K_0$ be a simple graph on a finite set $U$, and let $g : W \to U$, $f : V \to W$ and $h : X \to U$ be maps such that $g \circ f$ and $h$ are injective with the same range. Pull back $K_0$ along $g$ (vertices of $W$ adjacent when their images are) and then along $f$, and pull back $K_0$ along $h$. The map $h^{-1} \circ g \circ f : V \to X$ is a graph isomorphism between the two pulled-back graphs.
