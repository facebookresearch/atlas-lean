# Ambient FK normalizer is supermultiplicative under disjoint union

Let $G$ and $H$ be finite simple graphs, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. Then

$$Z_G(p, q)\, Z_H(p, q) \le Z_{G \sqcup H}(p, q),$$

where $G \sqcup H$ is the disjoint union and $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
