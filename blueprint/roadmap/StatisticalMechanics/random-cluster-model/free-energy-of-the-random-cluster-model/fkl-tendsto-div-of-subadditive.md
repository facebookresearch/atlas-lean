# Fekete's lemma with a general normalization

Let $(u_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a subadditive ($u_{m+n} \le u_m + u_n$ for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$) sequence of reals with $\{u_n/n : n \in \mathbb{N}\}$ bounded below (with $u_0/0$ read as $0$), and let $(e_n)$ be a real sequence with $e_n/n \to c$ for some $c \ne 0$. Then $u_n/e_n \to L/c$, where $L = \inf_{n \ge 1} u_n/n$ (and $u_n/e_n$ is read as $0$ when $e_n = 0$).
