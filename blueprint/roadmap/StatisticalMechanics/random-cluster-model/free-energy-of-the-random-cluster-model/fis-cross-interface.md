# Graph glued from two graphs along an interface

Let $G$ and $H$ be simple graphs on finite sets $V$ and $W$, and $K$ a simple graph on the disjoint union $V \sqcup W$. $K$ is glued from $G$ and $H$ along an interface when:

- every edge of $G$ and every edge of $H$ is an edge of $K$ (the disjoint union $G \sqcup H$ is a subgraph of $K$);
- every edge of $K$ joining two vertices of $V$ is an edge of $G$;
- every edge of $K$ joining two vertices of $W$ is an edge of $H$.

So the remaining edges of $K$ join $V$ to $W$.
