# An edge leaving an orthant ends on a coordinate hyperplane

Let $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$, $s \in \{+, -\}^d$, and let $x, y$ be adjacent sites of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$. If $x$ has [orthant tag](qp-box-tag.md) $s$ and $y$ does not, then $y$ has some coordinate equal to $0$.
