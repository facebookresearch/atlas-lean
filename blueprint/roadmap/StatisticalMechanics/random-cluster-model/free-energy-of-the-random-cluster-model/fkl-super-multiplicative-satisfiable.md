# Hypotheses of the normalized Fekete lemma are satisfiable

There are a subadditive ($u_{m+n} \le u_m + u_n$ for all $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$) sequence $(u_n)$ of reals, a real sequence $(e_n)$ and $c \ne 0$ such that $\{u_n/n : n \in \mathbb{N}\}$ is bounded below (with $u_0/0$ read as $0$), $e_n/n \to c$, and $u_n/e_n \to L/c$, where $L = \inf_{n \ge 1} u_n/n$. (For instance $u = 0$ and $e_n = n$.)
