# Isomorphism of a box with its translated copy in the complement part

Let $d, N, m \in \mathbb{N}$, let $P$ be a set of sites of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, let $v \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, and let $\varphi : \Lambda_m \to \Lambda_N \setminus P$ be a bijection with $\varphi(y) = y + v$ for every $y \in \Lambda_m$. Then $\varphi$ is a graph isomorphism from the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_m$ onto the graph induced on $\Lambda_N \setminus P$ by the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_N$.
