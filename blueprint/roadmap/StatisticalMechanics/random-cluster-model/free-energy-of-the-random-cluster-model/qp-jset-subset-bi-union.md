# Hyperplane set lies in the union of the coordinate slabs

For $d, K \in \mathbb{N}$, the set $J$ of sites of $\Lambda_{2K+1} = \{-(2K+1), \dots, 2K+1\}^d$ with some coordinate equal to $0$ is contained in the union over the coordinate directions $i$ of the [slabs](qp-jslab.md) $\{x \in \Lambda_{2K+1} : x_i = 0\}$.
