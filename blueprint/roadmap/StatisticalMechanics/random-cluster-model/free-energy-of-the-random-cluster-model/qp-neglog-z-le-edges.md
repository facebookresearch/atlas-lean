# Bound $-\log Z \le |E|\,(-\log(1-p))$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then

$$-\log Z_G(p, q) \le |E|\,\big(-\log(1 - p)\big),$$

where $Z_G(p, q)$ denotes the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) $Z_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ (sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, loops and non-edges included; $o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
