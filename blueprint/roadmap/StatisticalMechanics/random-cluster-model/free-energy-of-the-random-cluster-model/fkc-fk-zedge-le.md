# Edge partition function is at most $2^{|E|} q^{|V|}$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then $Z^E_G(p, q) \le 2^{|E|} q^{|V|}$, where $Z^E_G(p, q)$ is the [edge partition function](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/ecz-fk-zedge.md) $Z^E_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$, the sum over the configurations $\omega$ that are closed on every unordered pair of vertices that is not an edge of $G$ ($o, c$ count open and closed edges, $k$ the open clusters).
