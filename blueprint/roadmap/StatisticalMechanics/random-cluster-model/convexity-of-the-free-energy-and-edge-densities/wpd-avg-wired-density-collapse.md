# Average wired density collapse at $q = 2$

For $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, the collapse condition holds when, for every edge $e$ of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (a pair of nearest neighbours in $\Lambda_N$), viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\rho^w_{\Lambda_n}(t) \to \theta^1_e(\sigma(t)) \quad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\rho^w_{\Lambda_n}$ is the [average wired edge density](wpd-avg-wired-density.md) at $q = 2$ of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n$ with boundary set its [vertex boundary](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/box-boundary.md) (the sites of $\Lambda_n$ not in $\Lambda_{n-1}$), $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$ is the [logistic function](fsc-logistic.md), and $\theta^1_e$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$: for $p \in (0, 1)$, $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the probability that $e$ is open under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), and $\theta^1_e(p) = 0$ for other $p$.
