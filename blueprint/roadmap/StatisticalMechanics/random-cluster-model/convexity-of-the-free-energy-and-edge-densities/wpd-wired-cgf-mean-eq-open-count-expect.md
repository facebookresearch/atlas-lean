# Tilted mean of open edges equals the wired FK expectation

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, let $q > 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$\sum_\omega \phi^w_{G,\partial,\sigma(t),q}(\omega)\, o(\omega) = \frac{\sum_\omega q^{k^w(\omega)}\, o(\omega)\, e^{t\, o(\omega)}}{\sum_\omega q^{k^w(\omega)}\, e^{t\, o(\omega)}},$$

where the sums run over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, $o(\omega)$ is the number of open edges of $G$, $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$ is the [logistic function](fsc-logistic.md), and $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k^w(\omega)} / Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q)$ on assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of vertices; here $o(\omega)$ and $c(\omega)$ are the numbers of open and closed edges of $G$, $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph whose edges are the open edges of $G$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$, and $Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q)$ is the [wired partition function](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-z.md), the sum of the numerators over all $\omega$ (the quotient is read as $0$ if this sum is $0$). The right-hand side is the quotient of the [exponential sums](ivp2-ssum.md) $S_1(t)/S_0(t)$ with weights $q^{k^w(\omega)}$ and exponents $o(\omega)$.
