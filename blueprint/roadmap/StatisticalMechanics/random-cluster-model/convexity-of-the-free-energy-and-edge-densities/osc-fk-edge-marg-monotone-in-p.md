# Monotonicity in $p$ of free $q = 2$ edge marginals on a finite graph

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, $e$ an unordered pair of vertices of $G$, and $0 < p_1 \le p_2 < 1$. Then

$$\phi_{G,p_1,2}(e \text{ open}) \le \phi_{G,p_2,2}(e \text{ open}),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,2}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) on $G$ at $q = 2$ (the normalized weight $p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} 2^{k(\omega)}$ on assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of vertices, with $o, c$ counting open and closed edges of $G$ and $k$ the open clusters).
