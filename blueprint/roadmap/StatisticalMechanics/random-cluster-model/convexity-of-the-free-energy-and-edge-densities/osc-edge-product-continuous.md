# Continuity in $p$ of the Bernoulli edge weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. Then the [Bernoulli edge product](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/edge-product.md)

$$p \mapsto \prod_{e \in E} \begin{cases} p & \text{if } e \text{ is open in } \omega, \\ 1 - p & \text{if } e \text{ is closed in } \omega \end{cases}$$

is a continuous function of $p \in \mathbb{R}$.
