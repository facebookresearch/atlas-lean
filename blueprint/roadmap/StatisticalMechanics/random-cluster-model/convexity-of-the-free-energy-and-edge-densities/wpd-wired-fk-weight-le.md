# Wired and free FK weights within a factor $q^{|\partial|}$, $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, let $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices,

$$w^w(\omega) \le w(\omega) \le q^{|\partial|}\, w^w(\omega),$$

where $w(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k(\omega)}$ is the [random-cluster weight](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-weight.md) and $w^w(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k^w(\omega)}$ the [wired weight](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-weight.md); $o(\omega)$ and $c(\omega)$ are the numbers of open and closed edges of $G$, $k(\omega)$ is the number of open clusters of $G$ (isolated vertices included), and $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph whose edges are the open edges of $G$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
