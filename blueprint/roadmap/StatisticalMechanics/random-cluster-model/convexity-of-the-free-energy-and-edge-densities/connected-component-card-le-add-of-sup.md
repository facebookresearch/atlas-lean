# Adding edges within a vertex set drops components by at most its size

Let $V$ be a finite set, $G$ and $K$ simple graphs on $V$, and $P \subseteq V$ such that both endpoints of every edge of $K$ lie in $P$. Then

$$k(G) \le k(G \cup K) + |P|,$$

where $k(H)$ is the number of connected components of a graph $H$ and $G \cup K$ is the graph on $V$ whose edges are those of $G$ and those of $K$.
