# Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for weighted exponential sums

Let $I$ be a finite set, $w, a : I \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_i \ge 0$ for all $i$, and $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$S_1(t)^2 \le S_2(t)\, S_0(t),$$

where $S_0(t) = \sum_i w_i e^{a_i t}$, $S_1(t) = \sum_i w_i a_i e^{a_i t}$ and $S_2(t) = \sum_i w_i a_i^2 e^{a_i t}$ are the [weighted exponential sums](ivp2-ssum.md).
