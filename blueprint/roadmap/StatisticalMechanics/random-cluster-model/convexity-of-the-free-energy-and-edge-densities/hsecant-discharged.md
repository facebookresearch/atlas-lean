# Tilt data give wired density at $p'$ at most free density at $p > p'$

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $e$ be an unordered pair of vertices of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (not necessarily an edge), viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and assume the [finite-volume tilt data](fksecant-data.md) for $e$ (the tilted bound holding, for each $p' < p$ in $(0, 1)$ and each $\varepsilon > 0$, for all sufficiently large $n$). Then for all $p, p' \in (0, 1)$ with $p' < p$,

$$\theta^1_e(p') \le \theta^0_e(p),$$

where $\theta^1_e$ and $\theta^0_e$ are the [wired](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) and [free edge densities](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$.
