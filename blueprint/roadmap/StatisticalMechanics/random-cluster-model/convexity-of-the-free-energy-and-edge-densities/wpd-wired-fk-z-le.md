# Sandwich $Z^w \le Z \le q^{|\partial|} Z^w$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, let $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then

$$Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q) \le Z_G(p, q) \le q^{|\partial|}\, Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q),$$

where $Z_G$ is the [ambient random-cluster normalizer](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-z.md) and $Z^w_{G,\partial}$ the [wired partition function](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-z.md), both sums over all assignments of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices.
