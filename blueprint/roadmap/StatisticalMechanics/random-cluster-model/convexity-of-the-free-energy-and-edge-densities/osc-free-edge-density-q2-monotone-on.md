# Free $q = 2$ edge density is monotone in $p$ on $(0, 1)$

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $e$ be an unordered pair of vertices of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (not necessarily an edge), viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then $p \mapsto \theta^0_e(p)$ is nondecreasing on $(0, 1)$, where $\theta^0_e$ is the [free edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$: for $p \in (0, 1)$, $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the probability that $e$ is open under the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), and $\theta^0_e(p) = 0$ for other $p$.
