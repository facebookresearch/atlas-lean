# Tilted finite-volume bounds for all $\varepsilon > 0$ force $b \le a$

Let $q \ge 1$, $0 < \lambda < 1$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $(S_n)$, $(V_n)$ be real sequences with $V_n > 0$ for all $n$, $S_n/V_n \to 0$ and $V_n \to \infty$. If for every $\varepsilon > 0$ and every $n$

$$\varepsilon \le \frac{q^{S_n}\, \lambda^{(b - a - 2\varepsilon) V_n}}{\varepsilon},$$

then $b \le a$.
