# Limits of ambient tilted log normalizers are convex

Let $W$ be a finite set, $(G_i)_{i \in I}$ simple graphs on $W$ each having at least one edge, $\mathcal{F}$ a proper filter on $I$, $q > 0$, and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $F_{G_i,q}(t) \to g(t)$ along $\mathcal{F}$ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, where $F_{G,q}$ is the [ambient tilted log-normalizer](ivp2-ambient-tilt-log-z.md). Then $g$ is convex on $\mathbb{R}$.
