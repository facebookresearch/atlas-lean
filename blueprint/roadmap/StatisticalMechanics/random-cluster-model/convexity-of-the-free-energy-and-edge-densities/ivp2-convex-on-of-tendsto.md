# Pointwise limits of convex functions are convex

Let $(f_i)_{i \in I}$ be convex functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, let $\mathcal{F}$ be a proper filter on $I$ (for instance $i \to \infty$ along $\mathbb{N}$), and let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $f_i(x) \to g(x)$ along $\mathcal{F}$ for every $x$. Then $g$ is convex on $\mathbb{R}$.
