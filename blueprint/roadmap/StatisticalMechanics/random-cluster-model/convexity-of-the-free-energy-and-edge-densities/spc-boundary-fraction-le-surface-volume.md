# Non-bulk edge fraction bound from a boundary bound on their count

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $E_n$ be the edge set of the nearest-neighbour graph on the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $\partial\Lambda_n$ its [vertex boundary](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/box-boundary.md) (the sites of $\Lambda_n$ not in $\Lambda_{n-1}$, with $n - 1$ truncated at $0$). Let $C \ge 0$ and let $I \subseteq E_n$ satisfy $|E_n| - |I| \le C\, |\partial\Lambda_n|$. If $|E_n| > 0$, then

$$\frac{|E_n| - |I|}{|E_n|} \le C\, \frac{|\partial\Lambda_n|}{|E_n|}.$$
