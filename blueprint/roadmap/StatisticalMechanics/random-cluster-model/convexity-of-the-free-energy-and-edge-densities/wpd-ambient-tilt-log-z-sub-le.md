# Free-wired log-normalizer gap $\le |\partial|\log q/|E|$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices. If $q \ge 1$ and $|E| > 0$, then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$

$$\big|F_{G,q}(t) - F^w_{G,\partial,q}(t)\big| \le \frac{|\partial|}{|E|} \log q,$$

where $F_{G,q}$ is the [ambient tilted log-normalizer](ivp2-ambient-tilt-log-z.md) and $F^w_{G,\partial,q}$ the [wired one](wpd-wired-ambient-tilt-log-z.md).
