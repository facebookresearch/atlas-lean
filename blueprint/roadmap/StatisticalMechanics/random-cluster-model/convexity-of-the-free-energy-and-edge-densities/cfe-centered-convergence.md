# Pointwise convergence of box centered free energies

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the centered convergence condition is the following property of the pair $(d, g)$, at cluster weight $q = 2$:

- for every real $t$, the [centered free energy](cfe-centered.md) at $q = 2$ and parameter $t$ of the nearest-neighbour graph on the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ converges to $g(t)$ as $n \to \infty$; and
- $g(0) = 0$.

Since limits are unique, for a given $d$ at most one function $g$ has this property; nothing is asserted about any other $g$.
