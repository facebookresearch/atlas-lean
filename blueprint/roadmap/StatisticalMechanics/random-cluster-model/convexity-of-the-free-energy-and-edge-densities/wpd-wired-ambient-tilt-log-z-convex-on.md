# Convexity of the wired tilted log normalizer

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices. If $q > 0$ and $|E| > 0$, then the [wired per-edge ambient tilted log-normalizer](wpd-wired-ambient-tilt-log-z.md) $t \mapsto F^w_{G,\partial,q}(t)$ is convex on $\mathbb{R}$.
