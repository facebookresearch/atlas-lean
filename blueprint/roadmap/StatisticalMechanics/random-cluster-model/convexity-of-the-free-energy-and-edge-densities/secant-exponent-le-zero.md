# Volume-growth lemma for an eventual tilt bound

Let $q \ge 1$, $0 < \lambda < 1$, $\varepsilon > 0$ and $m \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $(S_n)$, $(V_n)$ be real sequences with $V_n > 0$ for all $n$, $S_n/V_n \to 0$ and $V_n \to \infty$. If

$$\varepsilon \le \frac{q^{S_n}\, \lambda^{m V_n}}{\varepsilon}$$

for all sufficiently large $n$, then $m \le 0$.
