# Wired $q = 2$ edge density is monotone in $t$

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $e$ be an unordered pair of vertices of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (not necessarily an edge), viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then $s \mapsto \theta^1_e(\sigma(s))$ is nondecreasing on $\mathbb{R}$, where $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$ is the [logistic function](fsc-logistic.md) and $\theta^1_e$ is the [wired edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/wired-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$: for $p \in (0, 1)$, $\theta^1_e(p)$ is the probability that $e$ is open under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), and $\theta^1_e(p) = 0$ for other $p$.
