# Limits of tilted log normalizers on a fixed box are attained

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, let $(G_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be simple graphs on the vertex set of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $F_{G_n,2}(t) \to g(t)$ as $n \to \infty$ for every $t$, where $F_{G,2}$ is the [ambient tilted log-normalizer](ivp2-ambient-tilt-log-z.md) at $q = 2$. Then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$ there is an $n$ with $g(t) = F_{G_n,2}(t)$.
