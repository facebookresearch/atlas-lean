# Ambient tilted log normalizer is determined by the graph

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, let $H$ be a simple graph on the vertex set of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d$, and let $t \in \mathbb{R}$. The value $F_{H,2}(t)$ of the [ambient tilted log-normalizer](ivp2-ambient-tilt-log-z.md) of $H$ at $q = 2$ depends only on $H$ and $t$: any two ways of deciding adjacency in $H$ give the same value.
