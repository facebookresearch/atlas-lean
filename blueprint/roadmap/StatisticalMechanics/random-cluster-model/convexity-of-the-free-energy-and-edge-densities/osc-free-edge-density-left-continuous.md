# Left-continuity of the free $q = 2$ edge density in $t$

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$, let $e$ be an unordered pair of vertices of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (not necessarily an edge), viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, and let $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $s \mapsto \theta^0_e(\sigma(s))$ is left-continuous at $t$ (continuous at $t$ within $(-\infty, t)$), where $\sigma(s) = e^s/(1 + e^s)$ is the [logistic function](fsc-logistic.md) and $\theta^0_e$ is the [free edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) of $e$ at $q = 2$: for $p \in (0, 1)$, $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the probability that $e$ is open under the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), and $\theta^0_e(p) = 0$ for other $p$.
