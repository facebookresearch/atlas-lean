# Collapse of average edge densities to the free density

Let $d, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $(G_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be simple graphs on the vertex set of the box $\Lambda_N = \{-N, \dots, N\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$. The collapse condition holds when, for every edge $e$ of the box $\Lambda_N$ (a pair of nearest neighbours in $\Lambda_N$) and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the [average edge density](fpd-avg-density.md) of $G_n$ at $t$ converges as $n \to \infty$ to $\theta^0_e(\sigma(t))$, where $\sigma(t) = e^t/(1 + e^t)$ and $\theta^0_e(p)$ is the [free edge density](../per-box-agreement-of-free-and-wired-random-clust/free-edge-density.md) at $q = 2$, that is, the probability that $e$ is open under the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures).
