# Set where coupled measures differ is countable under a convex pressure

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\Omega = \{0, 1\}^E$ with the coordinatewise order, and $e \in E$. Let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex and, for each $p \in \mathbb{R}$, let $\mu_p, \nu_p : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $P_p : \Omega \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be such that $P_p$ is a [monotone coupling](../fkg-inequality-and-monotonicity-of-random-cluste/is-monotone-coupling-fun.md) of $\mu_p$ and $\nu_p$: $P_p \ge 0$, $P_p$ has marginals $\mu_p$ and $\nu_p$, and $P_p(\omega, \omega') = 0$ unless $\omega \le \omega'$. For a function $\mu$ on $\Omega$ write $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$ and $\mu(\omega_f = 1)$ for the mass of $\{\omega : \omega_f = 1\}$. Assume that $\nu_p(\omega_e = 1) = g'_+(p)$ for every $p$, the [right derivative](pressure-right-deriv.md) of $g$ at $p$, and that at every $p$ at which $p \mapsto \nu_p(\omega_e = 1)$ is continuous, $\mu_p(\omega_f = 1) = \nu_p(\omega_f = 1)$ for every $f \in E$. Then the set of $p \in \mathbb{R}$ for which some increasing event $A \subseteq \Omega$ has $\mu_p(A) \ne \nu_p(A)$ is countable.
