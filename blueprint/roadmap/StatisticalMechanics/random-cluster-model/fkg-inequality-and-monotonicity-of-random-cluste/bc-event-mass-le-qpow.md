# Boundary-graph comparison $\varphi^{C'}(A) \le q^N \varphi^C(A)$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, let $C \le C'$ be simple graphs on $V$ ($C$ a subgraph of $C'$), $0 < p < 1$, $q \ge 1$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$. Suppose that $k_C(\omega) \le k_{C'}(\omega) + N$ for every configuration $\omega$, where $k_C(\omega)$ is the [number of clusters under the wiring $C$](num-clusters-bc.md). Then for every set $A$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi^{C'}_{G,p,q}(A) \le q^N\, \phi^C_{G,p,q}(A),$$

where $\phi^C_{G,p,q}(A)$ is the [mass of $A$](bc-event-mass.md) under the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](bc-prob.md).
