# Boundary-graph FK measure increases in $p$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, $0 < p_1 \le p_2 < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing (if $\omega \in A$ and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, then $\omega' \in A$) set $A$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi^C_{G,p_1,q}(A) \le \phi^C_{G,p_2,q}(A),$$

where $\phi^C_{G,p,q}(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^C_{G,p,q}(\omega)$ and $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](bc-prob.md).
