# Sandwich between the free and all-wired boundary conditions

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing (if $\omega \in A$ and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, then $\omega' \in A$) set $A$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi^{\emptyset}_{G,p,q}(A) \le \phi^C_{G,p,q}(A) \le \phi^{K_V}_{G,p,q}(A),$$

where $\phi^C_{G,p,q}(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^C_{G,p,q}(\omega)$, $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](bc-prob.md), $\emptyset$ denotes the edgeless graph and $K_V$ the complete graph on $V$ (all vertices wired together).
