# Mixed supermodularity of free and wired cluster counts

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices. For all assignments $a, b$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$k(a) + k^w(b) \le k(a \wedge b) + k^w(a \vee b),$$

where $k(\omega)$ is the number of open clusters (connected components of the graph formed by the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$, isolated vertices included) and $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph whose edges are the open edges of $G$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$. Configurations are ordered coordinatewise (closed $<$ open), and $a \wedge b$, $a \vee b$ denote the coordinatewise minimum and maximum.
