# Cluster count plus open-edge count is monotone

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph. For all assignments $b, c$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included) with $b \le c$ coordinatewise,

$$k(b) + o(b) \le k(c) + o(c),$$

where $k(\omega)$ is the number of open clusters (isolated vertices included) and $o(\omega)$ the [number of open edges](ch-open-count.md) of $G$.
