# FKG lattice condition for the random-cluster measure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For all assignments $a, b$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi_{G,p,q}(a)\, \phi_{G,p,q}(b) \le \phi_{G,p,q}(a \vee b)\, \phi_{G,p,q}(a \wedge b),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md). Configurations are ordered coordinatewise (closed $<$ open), and $a \wedge b$, $a \vee b$ denote the coordinatewise minimum and maximum.
