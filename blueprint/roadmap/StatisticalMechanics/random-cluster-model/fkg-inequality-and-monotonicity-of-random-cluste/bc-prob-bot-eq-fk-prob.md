# Empty wiring gives the free random-cluster measure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. For every assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring](bc-prob.md) given by the edgeless graph equals the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md): $\phi^{\emptyset}_{G,p,q}(\omega) = \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega)$.
