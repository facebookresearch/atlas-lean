# FKG inequality for increasing random-cluster events

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For all increasing sets $A, B$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi_{G,p,q}(A)\, \phi_{G,p,q}(B) \le \phi_{G,p,q}(A \cap B),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega)$ and $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md).
