# Monotone coupling of two mass functions

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\Omega = \{0, 1\}^E$ with the coordinatewise order, and $\mu, \nu : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. A function $P : \Omega \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ is a monotone coupling of $\mu$ and $\nu$ when:

- $P(\omega, \omega') \ge 0$ for all $\omega, \omega'$;
- $P(\omega, \omega') = 0$ unless $\omega \le \omega'$;
- $\sum_{\omega'} P(\omega, \omega') = \mu(\omega)$ for every $\omega$;
- $\sum_{\omega} P(\omega, \omega') = \nu(\omega')$ for every $\omega'$.
