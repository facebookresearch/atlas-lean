# Number of clusters under a boundary wiring

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The number of clusters under the wiring $C$ is the number $k_C(\omega)$ of connected components of the graph on $V$ whose edges are the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ together with the edges of $C$.
