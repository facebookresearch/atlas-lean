# Free box measure dominated by the wired one for $q \ge 1$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then the [free box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-finite-measure.md) $\phi^0_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is stochastically dominated by the [wired box measure](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$: $\phi^0_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(A) \le \phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(A)$ for every measurable increasing set $A$ of configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Both are the free and wired random-cluster measures on the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ (the wired one with its vertex boundary wired), extended to all unordered pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ by declaring every pair outside the box closed.
