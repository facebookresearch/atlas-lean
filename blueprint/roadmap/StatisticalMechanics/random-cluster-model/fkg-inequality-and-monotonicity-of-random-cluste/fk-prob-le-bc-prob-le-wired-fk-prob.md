# Boundary wirings lie between free and wired when $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, let $C$ be a simple graph on $V$ every edge of which has both endpoints in $\partial$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing (if $\omega \in A$ and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, then $\omega' \in A$) set $A$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\phi_{G,p,q}(A) \le \phi^C_{G,p,q}(A) \le \phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(A),$$

where $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$, $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md), $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](bc-prob.md), and $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md).
