# Supermodularity of the random-cluster cluster count

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph. For all assignments $a, b$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$k(a) + k(b) \le k(a \vee b) + k(a \wedge b),$$

where $k(\omega)$ is the number of open clusters (connected components of the graph formed by the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$, isolated vertices included). Configurations are ordered coordinatewise (closed $<$ open), and $a \wedge b$, $a \vee b$ denote the coordinatewise minimum and maximum.
