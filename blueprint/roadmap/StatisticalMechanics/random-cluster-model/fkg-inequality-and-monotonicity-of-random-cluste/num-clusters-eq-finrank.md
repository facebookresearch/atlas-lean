# Number of open clusters equals the dimension of the Laplacian kernel

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The number $k(\omega)$ of open clusters (connected components of the graph formed by the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$, isolated vertices included) equals the dimension over $\mathbb{R}$ of the [kernel of the Laplacian](ker-lap.md) of that graph.
