# Supermodularity of the number of connected components

Let $V$ be a finite set and $G_1, G_2$ simple graphs on $V$. Then

$$k(G_1) + k(G_2) \le k(G_1 \cup G_2) + k(G_1 \cap G_2),$$

where $k(H)$ is the number of connected components of $H$, and $G_1 \cup G_2$, $G_1 \cap G_2$ are the graphs on $V$ whose edge sets are the union and the intersection of those of $G_1$ and $G_2$.
