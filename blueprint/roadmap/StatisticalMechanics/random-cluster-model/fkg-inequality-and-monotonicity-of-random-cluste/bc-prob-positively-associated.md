# FKG inequality for the random-cluster measure, $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For all nondecreasing functions $f, g$ from configurations (assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included), ordered coordinatewise) to $\mathbb{R}$,

$$\Big(\sum_\omega \phi^C_{G,p,q}(\omega) f(\omega)\Big) \Big(\sum_\omega \phi^C_{G,p,q}(\omega) g(\omega)\Big) \le \sum_\omega \phi^C_{G,p,q}(\omega) f(\omega) g(\omega),$$

where $\phi^C_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C$](bc-prob.md) (Fortuin, Kasteleyn and Ginibre, 1971; see Grimmett, *The Random-Cluster Model* (2006), Chapter 3).
