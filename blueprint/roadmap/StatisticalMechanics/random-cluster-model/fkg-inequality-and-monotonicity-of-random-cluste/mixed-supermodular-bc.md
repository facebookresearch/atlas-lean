# Mixed supermodularity of cluster counts for nested wirings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $C \le C'$ simple graphs on $V$ ($C$ a subgraph of $C'$). For all assignments $a, b$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$k_C(a) + k_{C'}(b) \le k_C(a \wedge b) + k_{C'}(a \vee b),$$

where $k_C(\omega)$ is the [number of clusters under the wiring $C$](num-clusters-bc.md). Configurations are ordered coordinatewise (closed $<$ open), and $a \wedge b$, $a \vee b$ denote the coordinatewise minimum and maximum.
