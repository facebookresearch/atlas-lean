# Monotone couplings with equal edge marginals agree on up-sets

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\mu, \nu : \{0, 1\}^E \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $P$ be a [monotone coupling](is-monotone-coupling-fun.md) of $\mu$ and $\nu$. If $\mu(\omega_e = 1) = \nu(\omega_e = 1)$ for every $e \in E$ (the [edge marginals](edge-marg-prob.md)), then $\mu(A) = \nu(A)$ for every increasing set $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, where $\mu(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \mu(\omega)$ is the [mass of $A$](event-mass-prob.md).
