# Edge density lies between $p/(p+q(1-p))$ and $p$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$, $q \ge 1$, and $e \in E$. Then

$$\frac{p}{p + q(1 - p)} \le \phi_{G,p,q}(e \text{ open}) \le p,$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}(e \text{ open})$ is the [probability that $e$ is open](edge-marginal-open.md) under the random-cluster probability $\phi_{G,p,q}$.
