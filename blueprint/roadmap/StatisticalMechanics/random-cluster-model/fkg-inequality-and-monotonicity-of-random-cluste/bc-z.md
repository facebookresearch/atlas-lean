# Partition function with a boundary wiring

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, and $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$. The partition function with boundary wiring $C$ is $Z^C_G(p, q) = \sum_\omega w^C(\omega)$, the sum over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included) of the [weight with boundary wiring $C$](bc-weight.md).
