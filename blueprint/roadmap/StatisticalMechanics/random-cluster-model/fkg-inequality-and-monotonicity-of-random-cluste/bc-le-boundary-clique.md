# Boundary-supported wirings lie below the boundary clique

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\partial \subseteq V$, and $C$ a simple graph on $V$ every edge of which has both endpoints in $\partial$. Then $C$ is a subgraph of the boundary clique $K_\partial$ (the graph on $V$ whose edges are all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$).
