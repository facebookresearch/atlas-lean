# Kernel of the Laplacian of the open subgraph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The Laplacian kernel of $\omega$ is the kernel of the Laplacian matrix $L = D - A$ of the open subgraph (the graph on $V$ formed by the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$, with degree matrix $D$ and adjacency matrix $A$), acting on $\mathbb{R}^V$; it is a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^V$.
