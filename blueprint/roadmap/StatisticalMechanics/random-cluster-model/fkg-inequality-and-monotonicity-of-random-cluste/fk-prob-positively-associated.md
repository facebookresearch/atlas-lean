# FKG inequality for the random-cluster model

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For all nondecreasing functions $f, g$ from configurations (assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included), ordered coordinatewise) to $\mathbb{R}$,

$$\Big(\sum_\omega \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega) f(\omega)\Big) \Big(\sum_\omega \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega) g(\omega)\Big) \le \sum_\omega \phi_{G,p,q}(\omega) f(\omega) g(\omega),$$

where $\phi_{G,p,q}$ is the [random-cluster probability](../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) (Fortuin–Kasteleyn–Ginibre, Comm. Math. Phys. 22, 1971; Grimmett, *The Random-Cluster Model*, 2006, Theorem 3.8).
