# Wirings with the boundary clique dominate the wired measure, $q \ge 1$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, let $C'$ be a simple graph on $V$ containing the boundary clique $K_\partial$ (the graph on $V$ whose edges are all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$), $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. For every increasing (if $\omega \in A$ and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, then $\omega' \in A$) set $A$ of assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included),

$$\sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega) \le \sum_{\omega \in A} \phi^{C'}_{G,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) and $\phi^{C'}_{G,p,q}$ the [random-cluster probability with boundary wiring $C'$](bc-prob.md).
