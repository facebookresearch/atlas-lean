# Partition function of a conditioning fibre

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $C$ a simple graph on $V$, $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$, $F$ a finite set of unordered pairs of vertices and $\psi$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The fibre partition function is

$$Z^C_{F,\psi} = \sum_{\rho} w^C(\rho),$$

the sum over the configurations $\rho$ that agree with $\psi$ on every pair outside $F$, of the [weight with boundary wiring $C$](bc-weight.md) $w^C(\rho) = p^{o(\rho)}(1 - p)^{c(\rho)} q^{k_C(\rho)}$.
