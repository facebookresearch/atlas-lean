# Edwards–Sokal joint probability

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. The Edwards–Sokal joint probability of a coloring $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ and an assignment $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices is $W_p(\sigma, \omega) / Z^{ES}_q(p)$, a quotient with zero denominator being read as $0$. Here $W_p$ is the [Edwards–Sokal weight](../the-edwards-sokal-coupling/es-weight.md) $W_p(\sigma, \omega) = \prod_{e \in E} f_e$, where for $e = \{x, y\}$ the factor $f_e$ is $p\,\mathbf{1}[\sigma_x = \sigma_y]$ if $e$ is open in $\omega$ and $1 - p$ if $e$ is closed, and $Z^{ES}_q(p) = \sum_\omega \sum_\sigma W_p(\sigma, \omega)$ is the [Edwards–Sokal partition function](../the-edwards-sokal-coupling/es-z.md), the sums running over all assignments $\omega$ (loops and non-edges included) and all colorings $\sigma$. For $0 \le p \le 1$ and $q \ge 1$ the weights are nonnegative with positive total, and the quotient is a probability distribution on pairs $(\sigma, \omega)$. For $q = 0$ (and $V$ nonempty) there are no colorings, so $Z^{ES}_0(p) = 0$ and the value is $0$.
