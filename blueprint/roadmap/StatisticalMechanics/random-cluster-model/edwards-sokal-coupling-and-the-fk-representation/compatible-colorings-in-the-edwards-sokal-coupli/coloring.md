# Vertex coloring induced by a coloring of open clusters

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\omega$ one of the assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included). For a coloring $\tau$ of the open clusters of $\omega$ (the connected components of the graph on $V$ formed by the edges of $G$ that are open in $\omega$, isolated vertices included) with colors in $\{0, \dots, q - 1\}$, the induced vertex coloring gives each vertex $v$ the color $\tau(C_v)$ of its open cluster $C_v$.
