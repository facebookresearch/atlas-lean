# Finite-volume plus-boundary magnetization at the origin

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the finite-volume magnetization is the expectation $\langle \sigma_0 \rangle^+_{n,\beta}$ of the spin at the origin under the [Ising measure on $\Lambda_n$ with plus boundary condition](../../gibbs-states-and-phase-transitions/dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-measure.md) at inverse temperature $\beta$ and zero external field, where the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ and the spin of a site is $+1$ or $-1$.
