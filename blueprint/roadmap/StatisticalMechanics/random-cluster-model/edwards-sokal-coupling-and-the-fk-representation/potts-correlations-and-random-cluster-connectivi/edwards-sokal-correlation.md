# Edwards–Sokal correlation identity

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \ge 1$ an integer, $\beta, J \in \mathbb{R}$, $x, y \in V$, and $p = 1 - e^{-\beta J}$. If $0 < p < 1$ (equivalently $\beta J > 0$, the ferromagnetic case), then

$$\mu_{G,q,\beta,J}(\sigma_x = \sigma_y) - \frac{1}{q} = \Big(1 - \frac{1}{q}\Big)\, \phi_{G,p,q}(x \leftrightarrow y),$$

where $\mu_{G,q,\beta,J}(\sigma_x = \sigma_y)$ is the [Potts two-point agreement probability](potts-agree-prob.md) and $\phi_{G,p,q}(x \leftrightarrow y)$ the [random-cluster connection probability](../ising-two-point-function-via-the-edwards-sokal-c/conn-prob.md) (Edwards–Sokal, Phys. Rev. D 38, 1988; Grimmett, *The Random-Cluster Model*, 2006, Theorem 1.16).
