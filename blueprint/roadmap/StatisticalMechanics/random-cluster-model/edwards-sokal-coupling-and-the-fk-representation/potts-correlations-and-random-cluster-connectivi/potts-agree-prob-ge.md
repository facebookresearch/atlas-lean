# Potts two-point agreement at least $1/q$ for $q \ge 2$, $\beta J > 0$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \ge 2$ an integer, $\beta, J \in \mathbb{R}$ with $0 < 1 - e^{-\beta J} < 1$ (that is, $\beta J > 0$), and $x, y \in V$. Then

$$\frac{1}{q} \le \mu_{G,q,\beta,J}(\sigma_x = \sigma_y),$$

the [Potts two-point agreement probability](potts-agree-prob.md).
