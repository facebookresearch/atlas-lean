# Ising bond variable of a two-state coloring

Let $V$ be a finite set and $\sigma : V \to \{0, 1\}$. The Ising bond of an unordered pair $\{x, y\}$ is $s(\sigma_x)\, s(\sigma_y)$, where $s(0) = 1$ and $s(1) = -1$ is the [Ising spin](../ising-two-point-function-via-the-edwards-sokal-c/ising-spin.md) of a color (so color $0$ is spin $+1$).
