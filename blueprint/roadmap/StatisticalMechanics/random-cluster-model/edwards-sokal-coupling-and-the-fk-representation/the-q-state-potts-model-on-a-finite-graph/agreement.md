# Agreement count of a spin configuration

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $q \in \mathbb{N}$. The agreement count of a coloring $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ is the number (as a real number) of edges $\{x, y\} \in E$ with $\sigma_x = \sigma_y$:

$$a(\sigma) = \sum_{\{x, y\} \in E} \mathbf{1}[\sigma_x = \sigma_y].$$
