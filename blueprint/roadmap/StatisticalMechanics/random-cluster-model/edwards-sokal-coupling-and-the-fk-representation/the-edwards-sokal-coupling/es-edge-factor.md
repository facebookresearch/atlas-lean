# Edwards–Sokal edge factor

Let $V$ be a finite set, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. The Edwards–Sokal factor of a pair $e = \{x, y\}$ is $p\,\mathbf{1}[\sigma_x = \sigma_y]$ if $e$ is open in $\omega$, and $1 - p$ if $e$ is closed.
