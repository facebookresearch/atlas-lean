# Spin configuration constant on open clusters

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. A coloring $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ is constant on open clusters when $\sigma_x = \sigma_y$ for every edge $\{x, y\}$ of $G$ that is open in $\omega$ (equivalently, $\sigma$ is constant on each open cluster).
