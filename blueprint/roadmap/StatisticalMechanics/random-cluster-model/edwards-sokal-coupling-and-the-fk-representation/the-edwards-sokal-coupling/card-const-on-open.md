# There are $q^{k(\omega)}$ colorings constant on the open clusters

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The number of colorings $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ that are [constant on open clusters](const-on-open.md) equals $q^{k(\omega)}$, where $k(\omega)$ is the [number of open clusters](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/num-clusters.md) (connected components of the graph formed by the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$, isolated vertices included).
