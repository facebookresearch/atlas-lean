# Edwards–Sokal partition function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $q \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. The Edwards–Sokal partition function is

$$Z^{ES}_q(p) = \sum_\omega \sum_\sigma W_p(\sigma, \omega),$$

the sums running over all assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (loops and non-edges included) and all colorings $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$, where $W_p$ is the [Edwards–Sokal weight](es-weight.md).
