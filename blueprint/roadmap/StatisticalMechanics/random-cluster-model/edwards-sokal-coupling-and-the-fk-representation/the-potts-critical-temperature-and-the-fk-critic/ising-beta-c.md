# Critical inverse temperature of the $d$-dimensional Ising model

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the critical inverse temperature of the Ising model on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ is

$$\beta_c(d) = \sup\{\beta > 0 : m^*_d(\beta) = 0\},$$

the supremum in $\mathbb{R}$, where $m^*_d(\beta)$ is the [spontaneous magnetization](../../../gibbs-states-and-phase-transitions/phase-transition-of-the-ising-model/magnetization.md), the expected spin at the origin under the zero-field plus state at inverse temperature $\beta$. The supremum of a set that is empty or not bounded above is taken to be $0$; in particular, if $m^*_d(\beta) = 0$ for every $\beta > 0$, then $\beta_c(d) = 0$.
