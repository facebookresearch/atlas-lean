# Potts inverse temperature of an FK edge parameter

For $q, p \in \mathbb{R}$, the Potts inverse temperature corresponding to the edge parameter $p$ is

$$\beta_q(p) = -\frac{q - 1}{q} \log(1 - p),$$

where $(q - 1)/q$ is read as $0$ when $q = 0$ and $\log x$ means $\log |x|$ for $x \ne 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$.
