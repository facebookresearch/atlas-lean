# Inverse-temperature map of the Potts model at $q = 2$ is the Ising map

For every $p \in \mathbb{R}$, the [Potts inverse-temperature map](p-to-beta.md) at $q = 2$ equals the [Ising one](../the-ising-model-as-the-two-state-potts-model/beta-of-p.md):

$$-\frac{2 - 1}{2} \log(1 - p) = -\frac{1}{2} \log(1 - p),$$

where $\log x$ means $\log |x|$ for $x \ne 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$.
