# FK edge-parameter map of the Potts model at $q = 2$ is the Ising map

For every $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the [Potts edge-parameter map](beta-to-p.md) at $q = 2$ equals the [Ising one](../the-ising-model-as-the-two-state-potts-model/p-of-beta.md):

$$1 - e^{-\frac{2}{2 - 1}\beta} = 1 - e^{-2\beta}.$$
