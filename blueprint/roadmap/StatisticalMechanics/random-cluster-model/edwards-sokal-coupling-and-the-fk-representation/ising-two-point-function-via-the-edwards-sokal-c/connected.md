# Open connection between two vertices

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. Two vertices $x, y \in V$ are connected, $x \leftrightarrow y$, when there is a path from $x$ to $y$ in $G$ all of whose edges are open in $\omega$ (in particular $x \leftrightarrow x$).
