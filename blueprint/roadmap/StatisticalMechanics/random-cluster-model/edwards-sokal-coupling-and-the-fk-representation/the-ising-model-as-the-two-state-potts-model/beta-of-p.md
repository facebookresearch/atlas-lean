# Ising inverse temperature of an FK edge parameter

For $p \in \mathbb{R}$, the Ising inverse temperature corresponding to the edge parameter $p$ is

$$\beta(p) = -\tfrac{1}{2} \log(1 - p),$$

where $\log x$ means $\log |x|$ for $x \ne 0$ and $\log 0 = 0$. On the physical domain $0 \le p < 1$ this is the inverse of $\beta \mapsto 1 - e^{-2\beta}$ on $[0, \infty)$. At $p = 1$ the value is $\beta(1) = 0$, and for $p > 1$ it equals $-\tfrac{1}{2}\log(p - 1)$.
