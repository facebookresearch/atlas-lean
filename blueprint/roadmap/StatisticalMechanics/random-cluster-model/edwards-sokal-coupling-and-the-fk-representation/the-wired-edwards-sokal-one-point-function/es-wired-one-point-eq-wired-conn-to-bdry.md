# Edwards–Sokal one-point identity

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices that is nonempty, and let $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x \in V$. Then the [wired Edwards–Sokal one-point function](es-wired-one-point.md) with boundary color $0$ (spin $+1$) equals the [wired probability that $x$ is connected to the boundary](wired-conn-to-bdry-prob.md) at $q = 2$:

$$\langle s(\sigma_x) \rangle^{ES,0}_p = \phi^w_{G,\partial,p}(x \leftrightarrow \partial)$$

(Edwards and Sokal, Phys. Rev. D 38 (1988); Grimmett, *The Random-Cluster Model*, Theorem 1.16).
