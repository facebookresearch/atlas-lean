# Wired colorings as cluster colorings with a pinned boundary cluster

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, $b \in \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$, $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices, and $v_0 \in \partial$. Consider the [wired graph](../the-edwards-sokal-coupling-with-wired-boundary-c/wired-sub.md) $H_\omega$ on $V$, whose edges are the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$. The map $\tau \mapsto (v \mapsto \tau(C_v))$, with $C_v$ the connected component of $H_\omega$ containing $v$, is a bijection from the colorings $\tau$ of the components of $H_\omega$ with $\tau(C_{v_0}) = b$ onto the colorings $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ that are [constant along the edges of $H_\omega$](../the-edwards-sokal-coupling-with-wired-boundary-c/const-on-wired.md) and equal $b$ on $\partial$.
