# Open connection of a vertex to the boundary

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. A vertex $x$ is connected to the boundary, $x \leftrightarrow \partial$, when some $y \in \partial$ is joined to $x$ by a path of edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ (if $x \in \partial$, it is connected to the boundary).
