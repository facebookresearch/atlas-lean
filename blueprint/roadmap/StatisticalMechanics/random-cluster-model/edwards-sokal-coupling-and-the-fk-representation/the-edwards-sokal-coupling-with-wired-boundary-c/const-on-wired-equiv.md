# Colorings constant on wired clusters as cluster colorings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. Consider the [wired graph](wired-sub.md) $H_\omega$ on $V$, whose edges are the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$. The map $\tau \mapsto (v \mapsto \tau(C_v))$, with $C_v$ the connected component of $H_\omega$ containing $v$, is a bijection from the colorings of the connected components of $H_\omega$ with colors in $\{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ onto the colorings $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ that are [constant along the edges of $H_\omega$](const-on-wired.md).
