# Functions pinned at a point as functions on its complement

Let $A$ be a set, $c_0 \in A$, $q \in \mathbb{N}$ and $b \in \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$. Restriction to $A \setminus \{c_0\}$ is a bijection from the functions $\tau : A \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ with $\tau(c_0) = b$ onto all functions $A \setminus \{c_0\} \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$; its inverse extends a function by the value $b$ at $c_0$.
