# Coloring constant on the wired graph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices, $q \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. A coloring $\sigma : V \to \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$ is constant on the wired graph when $\sigma_x = \sigma_y$ for every edge $\{x, y\}$ of the [wired graph](wired-sub.md) $H_\omega$ on $V$, whose edges are the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
