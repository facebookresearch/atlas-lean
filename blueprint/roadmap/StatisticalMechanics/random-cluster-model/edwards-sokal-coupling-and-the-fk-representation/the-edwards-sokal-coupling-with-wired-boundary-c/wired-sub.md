# Open-edge graph with a boundary clique

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices and $\omega$ an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices. The wired graph $H_\omega$ is the simple graph on $V$ whose edges are the edges of $G$ that are open in $\omega$ together with all pairs $\{x, y\}$ of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
