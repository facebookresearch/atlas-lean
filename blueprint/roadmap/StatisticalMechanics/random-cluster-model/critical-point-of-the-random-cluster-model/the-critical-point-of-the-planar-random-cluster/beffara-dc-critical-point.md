# Beffara–Duminil-Copin theorem, conditional form

Let $q \ge 1$, and suppose that the wired random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ does not percolate below $\sqrt{q}/(1 + \sqrt{q})$ and percolates above it: $\theta^1(r, q) = 0$ for every $r \in (0, 1)$ with $r < \sqrt{q}/(1 + \sqrt{q})$, and $\theta^1(r, q) > 0$ for every $r \in (0, 1)$ with $r > \sqrt{q}/(1 + \sqrt{q})$. Then

$$p_c(q) = \frac{\sqrt{q}}{1 + \sqrt{q}},$$

where $p_c(q)$ is the [critical point](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-pc.md) $\sup\{p \in (0, 1) : \theta^1(p, q) = 0\}$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $\theta^1(p, q)$ is the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^2$. The two hypotheses hold for every $q \ge 1$ by the theorem of V. Beffara and H. Duminil-Copin (*The self-dual point of the two-dimensional random-cluster model is critical for $q \ge 1$*, Probab. Theory Related Fields 153, 2012).
