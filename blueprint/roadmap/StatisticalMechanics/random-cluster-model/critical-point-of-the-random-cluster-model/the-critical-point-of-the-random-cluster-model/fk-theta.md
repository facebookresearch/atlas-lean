# Wired random-cluster percolation probability $\theta(p, q)$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the wired percolation probability $\theta^1(p, q)$ is the probability, under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), of the event that the open cluster of the origin (the set of sites joined to the origin by paths of open nearest-neighbour edges) is infinite.
