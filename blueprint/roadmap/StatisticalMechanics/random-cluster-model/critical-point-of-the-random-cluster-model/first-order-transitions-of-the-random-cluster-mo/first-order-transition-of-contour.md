# First-order transition from $\theta^0 = 0$ and $\theta^1 > 0$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. If $\theta^0(p, q) = 0$ and $\theta^1(p, q) > 0$, then $(p, q)$ is a [first-order transition](is-first-order-transition.md) of the random-cluster model on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Here $\theta^0$ is the [free percolation probability](fk-theta-free.md) $\theta^0(p, q)$: the probability, under the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), that the open cluster of the origin is infinite, and $\theta^1(p, q)$ is the probability, under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), that the open cluster of the origin is infinite (the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md)).
