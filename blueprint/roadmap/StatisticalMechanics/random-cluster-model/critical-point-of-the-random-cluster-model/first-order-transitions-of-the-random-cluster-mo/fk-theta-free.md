# Free percolation probability

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$, the free percolation probability $\theta^0(p, q)$ is the probability, under the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^0_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the free box measures), of the event that the open cluster of the origin (the set of sites joined to the origin by paths of open nearest-neighbour edges) is infinite.
