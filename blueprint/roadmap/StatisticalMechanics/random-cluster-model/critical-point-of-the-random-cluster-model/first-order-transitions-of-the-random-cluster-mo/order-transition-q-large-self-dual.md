# Free and wired measures differ at $\sqrt{q}/(1+\sqrt{q})$ for $q > 4$ if first order

Let $q > 4$ and let $p_{sd} = \sqrt{q}/(1 + \sqrt{q})$ be the [self-dual point](../the-self-dual-point-of-the-planar-random-cluster/self-dual-point.md), which lies in $(0, 1)$. If, on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $\theta^0(p_{sd}, q) = 0$ and $\theta^1(p_{sd}, q) > 0$, then $(p_{sd}, q)$ is a [first-order transition](is-first-order-transition.md) and the free and wired infinite-volume random-cluster measures $\phi^0_{p_{sd},q}$ and $\phi^1_{p_{sd},q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are different. Here $\theta^0$ and $\theta^1$ are the [free](fk-theta-free.md) and [wired percolation probabilities](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md).
