# Free percolation from domination and free–wired agreement at $p'$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $q > 0$ and $p, p' \in (0, 1)$ with $p_c(q) < p'$. If the free infinite-volume measure $\phi^0_{p',q}$ is stochastically dominated by $\phi^0_{p,q}$ (that is, $\phi^0_{p',q}(A) \le \phi^0_{p,q}(A)$ for every measurable increasing event $A$) and $\theta^0(p', q) = \theta^1(p', q)$, then $\theta^0(p, q) > 0$. Here $\phi^0_{p,q}$ is the [free infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/free-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, $\theta^0$ is the [free percolation probability](../first-order-transitions-of-the-random-cluster-mo/fk-theta-free.md), and $p_c(q)$ is the [critical point](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-pc.md) $p_c(q) = \sup\{p \in (0, 1) : \theta^1(p, q) = 0\}$ of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (with $\sup \emptyset = 0$), $\theta^1(p, q)$ being the probability, under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), that the open cluster of the origin is infinite (the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md)).
