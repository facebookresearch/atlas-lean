# No percolation for $p \le p_c$ at $q = 2$, given $\theta^1(p_c, 2) = 0$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and assume that $\theta^1(p_c(2), 2) = 0$ whenever $0 < p_c(2) < 1$. Then $\theta^1(p, 2) = 0$ for every $p \in (0, 1)$ with $p \le p_c(2)$. Here $p_c(2)$ is the [critical point](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-pc.md) $\sup\{p \in (0, 1) : \theta^1(p, 2) = 0\}$ of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (with $\sup \emptyset = 0$), and $\theta^1(p, q)$ is the probability, under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), that the open cluster of the origin is infinite (the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md)).
