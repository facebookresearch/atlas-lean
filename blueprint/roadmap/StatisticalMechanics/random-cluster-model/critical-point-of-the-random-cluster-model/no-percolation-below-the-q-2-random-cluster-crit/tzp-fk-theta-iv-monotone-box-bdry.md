# Wired box-boundary connection probability is monotone in $p$ at $q = 2$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p_1 \le p_2 < 1$. Then

$$\phi^1_{p_1,2}(0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n) \le \phi^1_{p_2,2}(0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n),$$

where $\phi^1_{p,2}$ is the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ at $q = 2$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures) and $\{0 \leftrightarrow \partial\Lambda_n\}$ is the event that the origin is joined to some site of the [vertex boundary](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/box-boundary.md) $\partial\Lambda_n$ of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (the sites of $\Lambda_n$ not in $\Lambda_{n-1}$, with $n - 1$ truncated at $0$) by a path of open nearest-neighbour edges inside $\Lambda_n$.
