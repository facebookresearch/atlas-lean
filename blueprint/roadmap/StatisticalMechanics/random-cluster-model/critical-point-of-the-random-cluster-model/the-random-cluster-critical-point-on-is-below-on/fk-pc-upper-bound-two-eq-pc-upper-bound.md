# Peierls series for $d = 2$ equals the Bernoulli Peierls series

For every $p \in \mathbb{R}$, the [Peierls series](fk-pc-upper-bound.md) for $d = 2$ equals the Peierls series of Bernoulli percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^2$:

$$\sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, (2 \cdot 2)^\ell (1 - p)^\ell = \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, 4^\ell (1 - p)^\ell,$$

each series being read as $0$ when it diverges.
