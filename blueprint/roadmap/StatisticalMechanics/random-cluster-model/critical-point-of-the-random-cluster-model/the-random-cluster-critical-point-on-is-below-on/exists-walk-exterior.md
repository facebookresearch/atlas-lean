# Finite cluster in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ has a reachable point outside it

Let $\omega$ be a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $o \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ a site whose open cluster is finite. Then there is a site $z$ outside the open cluster of $o$ that is joined to $o$ by a walk in the nearest-neighbour graph of $\mathbb{Z}^2$.
