# Finite cluster in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ gives a closed lattice circuit

Let $\omega$ be a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $o \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ a site whose open cluster is finite. Then there is a cycle $c$ in the nearest-neighbour graph of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ such that $\omega$ lies in the [closed-circuit event](closed-circuit-event.md) of $c$: every primal edge crossed by $c$, read as a cycle of faces, is closed in $\omega$.
