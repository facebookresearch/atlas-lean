# Percolation from a Peierls bound with series below 1

Let $0 < p < 1$ and $q > 0$. If the [Peierls bound](fk-non-percolation-bound.md) $1 - \theta^1(p, q) \le \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, 4^\ell (1 - p)^\ell$ holds and the [Peierls series](fk-pc-upper-bound.md) for $d = 2$ (read as $0$ when it diverges) is less than $1$, then $\theta^1(p, q) > 0$, where $\theta^1$ is the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md) on $\mathbb{Z}^2$.
