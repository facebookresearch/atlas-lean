# Extended box configuration keeps box edge states

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\omega$ be a configuration of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ (an assignment of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$), and let $b$ be such a pair. Then the [extension](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/extend-edge.md) $\bar\omega$ of $\omega$ to $\mathbb{Z}^d$ gives $b$, viewed as a pair of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the state $\omega(b)$.
