# Single-edge open event is clopen

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and every unordered pair $e$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the event $\{\omega : e \text{ is open in } \omega\}$ is both open and closed in the product topology on the space of configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites).
