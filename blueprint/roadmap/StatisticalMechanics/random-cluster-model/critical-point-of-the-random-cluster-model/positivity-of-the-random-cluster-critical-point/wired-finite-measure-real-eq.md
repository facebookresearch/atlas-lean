# Wired box measure as a finite sum over box configurations

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $q > 0$, and let $A$ be a measurable set of configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then

$$\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}(A) = \sum_{\omega :\ \bar\omega \in A} \phi^w_{\Lambda_n,\partial\Lambda_n,p,q}(\omega),$$

where $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ is the [wired box measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md), the sum runs over the configurations $\omega$ of the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ whose [extension](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/extend-edge.md) $\bar\omega$ (equal to $\omega$ on pairs of box vertices and closed elsewhere) lies in $A$, and $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}$ is the [wired random-cluster probability](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-fk-prob.md) $\phi^w_{G,\partial,p,q}(\omega) = p^{o(\omega)}(1 - p)^{c(\omega)} q^{k^w(\omega)} / Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q)$ on assignments $\omega$ of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of vertices; $o(\omega)$ and $c(\omega)$ count the open and closed edges of $G$, $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph whose edges are the open edges of $G$ and all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$, and $Z^w_{G,\partial}(p, q)$ is the sum of the numerators over all $\omega$, here for the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n$ with $\partial\Lambda_n$ its [vertex boundary](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/box-boundary.md).
