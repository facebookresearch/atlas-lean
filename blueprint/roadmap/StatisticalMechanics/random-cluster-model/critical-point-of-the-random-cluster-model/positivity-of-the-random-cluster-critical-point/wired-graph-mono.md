# Monotonicity of the wired graph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$. If $a \le b$ are assignments of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (coordinatewise, closed $<$ open), then the wired graph of $a$ is a subgraph of the wired graph of $b$, where the wired graph of $\omega$ has as edges the edges of $G$ open in $\omega$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
