# Event of some open self-avoiding walk is clopen

For $d \ge 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the [open-walk event](fk-exists-open-walk.md) (some self-avoiding walk of $m$ steps from the origin has all its edges open) is both open and closed in the product topology on the space of configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (assignments of open/closed states to all unordered pairs of sites).
