# Percolation gives open self-avoiding walks of every length

Let $d \ge 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Every configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ in which the open cluster of the origin is infinite lies in the [open-walk event](fk-exists-open-walk.md): some self-avoiding walk of $m$ steps from the origin has all its edges open.
