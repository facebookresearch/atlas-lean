# Event of some open self-avoiding walk is increasing

For $d \ge 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the [open-walk event](fk-exists-open-walk.md) (some self-avoiding walk of $m$ steps from the origin has all its edges open) is increasing: if $\omega$ lies in it and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, then $\omega'$ lies in it.
