# Bernoulli bound $p^m$ for a fixed self-avoiding walk in the box

Let $d, n, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$. A word $g = (g_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of steps $g_k = (i_k, s_k)$, with $i_k \in \{1, \dots, d\}$ a coordinate direction and $s_k \in \{+1, -1\}$ a sign, encodes the lattice path $x_0 = 0$, $x_{k+1} = x_k + s_k e_{i_k}$ in $\mathbb{Z}^d$; its $k$-th edge is $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$. If the path of $g$ is self-avoiding up to step $m$ (the first $m + 1$ vertices $x_0, \dots, x_m$ of its path are distinct), then the total mass, under the [random-cluster probability](../../random-cluster-measures-on-finite-graphs/fk-prob.md) $\phi_{\Lambda_n,p,1}$ at $q = 1$ of the nearest-neighbour graph on the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, of the box configurations $\omega$ whose extension $\bar\omega$ lies in the event that the first $m$ edges $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$, $0 \le k < m$, of its path are all open is at most $p^m$. Here the [extension](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/extend-edge.md) $\bar\omega$ of a configuration $\omega$ of the box (an assignment of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$) is the configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that agrees with $\omega$ on pairs of box vertices and declares every other pair closed, and $\phi_{\Lambda_n,p,1}$ makes box edges independent Bernoulli($p$) and the other pairs of box vertices independent fair bits.
