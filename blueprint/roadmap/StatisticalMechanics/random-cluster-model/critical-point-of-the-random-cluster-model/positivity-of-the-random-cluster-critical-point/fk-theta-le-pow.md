# Wired percolation bound $\theta^1(p, q) \le (2dp)^m$ for $q \ge 1$

Let $d \ge 1$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then for every $m \in \mathbb{N}$

$$\theta^1(p, q) \le (2dp)^m,$$

where $\theta^1(p, q)$ is the probability, under the [wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-infinite-volume.md) $\phi^1_{p,q}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ (a chosen subsequential weak limit of the wired box measures), that the open cluster of the origin is infinite (the [wired percolation probability](../the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-theta.md)).
