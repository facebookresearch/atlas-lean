# Open self-avoiding walk in a box gives $m$ distinct open box edges

Let $d, n, m \in \mathbb{N}$. A word $g = (g_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of steps $g_k = (i_k, s_k)$, with $i_k \in \{1, \dots, d\}$ a coordinate direction and $s_k \in \{+1, -1\}$ a sign, encodes the lattice path $x_0 = 0$, $x_{k+1} = x_k + s_k e_{i_k}$ in $\mathbb{Z}^d$; its $k$-th edge is $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$. Suppose the path of $g$ is self-avoiding up to step $m$ (the first $m + 1$ vertices $x_0, \dots, x_m$ of its path are distinct) and that each of its first $m$ edges has both endpoints in the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$. Then there is a set $F$ of exactly $m$ edges of the nearest-neighbour graph on $\Lambda_n$ such that every configuration $\omega$ of the box whose extension $\bar\omega$ lies in the event that the first $m$ edges $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$, $0 \le k < m$, of its path are all open has all edges of $F$ open. Here the [extension](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/extend-edge.md) $\bar\omega$ of a configuration $\omega$ of the box (an assignment of states to the unordered pairs of vertices of $\Lambda_n$) is the configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that agrees with $\omega$ on pairs of box vertices and declares every other pair closed.
