# Event of an open self-avoiding walk of $m$ steps

Let $d \ge 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$. A word $g = (g_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of steps $g_k = (i_k, s_k)$, with $i_k \in \{1, \dots, d\}$ a coordinate direction and $s_k \in \{+1, -1\}$ a sign, encodes the lattice path $x_0 = 0$, $x_{k+1} = x_k + s_k e_{i_k}$ in $\mathbb{Z}^d$; its $k$-th edge is $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$. The open-walk event is the union, over all words $w = (w_0, \dots, w_{m-1})$ of $m$ steps whose [padded word](fk-ext-word.md) is self-avoiding up to step $m$ (the first $m + 1$ vertices $x_0, \dots, x_m$ of its path are distinct), of the event that the first $m$ edges $\{x_k, x_{k+1}\}$, $0 \le k < m$, of its path are all open. Equivalently, it is the event that some self-avoiding walk of $m$ steps from the origin of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ has all its edges open.
