# Wired box bound $(2dp)^m$ on the open-walk event for $q \ge 1$

Let $d \ge 1$, $n, m \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$ and $q \ge 1$. Then the [wired box measure](../../infinite-volume-random-cluster-measures/finite-and-infinite-volume-fk-measures-on-d/wired-finite-measure.md) $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n,p,q}$ (the wired random-cluster measure on the box $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, with its vertex boundary wired, extended to $\mathbb{Z}^d$ by declaring every pair outside the box closed) gives the [open-walk event](fk-exists-open-walk.md) probability at most $(2dp)^m$.
