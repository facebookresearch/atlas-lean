# Padding a finite lattice word to an infinite word

Let $d \ge 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $w = (w_0, \dots, w_{m-1})$ be a word of $m$ lattice steps, each step a pair (coordinate direction in $\{1, \dots, d\}$, sign in $\{+1, -1\}$). Its padding is the infinite word $(g_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ with $g_k = w_k$ for $k < m$ and $g_k = (1, -1)$, the step $-e_1$, for $k \ge m$.
