# Wired cluster count is antitone

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\partial \subseteq V$. If $a \le b$ are assignments of open/closed states to the unordered pairs of vertices (coordinatewise, closed $<$ open), then $k^w(b) \le k^w(a)$, where $k^w(\omega)$ is the number of connected components of the graph whose edges are the open edges of $G$ together with all pairs of distinct vertices of $\partial$.
