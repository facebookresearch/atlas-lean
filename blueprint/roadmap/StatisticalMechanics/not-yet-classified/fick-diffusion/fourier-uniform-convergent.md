# Uniform convergence of the slab series

For $c_0 \ge 0$, $L > 0$, $D > 0$ and $\delta > 0$, the partial sums of the sine series $\sum_n b_n \sin(n\pi x/L)\, e^{-D (n\pi/L)^2 t}$ for diffusion in $[0, L]$ with zero boundary values and uniform initial concentration $c_0$ converge uniformly on $[0, L] \times [\delta, \infty)$ to the sum of the series.
