# Pressure decomposition on the $L \times L$ torus for $\beta \ge 0$

For $L > 2$ and $\beta \ge 0$,

$$\frac{\log Z_{T_L}(\beta)}{L^2} = \log 2 + 2\log\cosh\beta + \frac{\log X_{T_L}(\tanh\beta)}{L^2},$$

where $T_L$ is the [$L \times L$ torus graph](../the-square-lattice-torus/ons-torus-graph.md) on $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$, $Z_G(\beta) = \sum_{s} \exp\big(\beta \sum_{\{x,y\} \in E} s_x s_y\big)$ is the [zero-field Ising partition function](../high-temperature-expansion-of-the-ising-partitio/ons-z.md) with unit couplings, the sum over all spin configurations $s : V \to \{+1, -1\}$, and $X_G(x) = \sum_{F} x^{|F|}$ is the [even-subgraph generating function](../high-temperature-expansion-of-the-ising-partitio/ons-x.md), the sum over the subsets $F \subseteq E$ in which every vertex has even degree.
