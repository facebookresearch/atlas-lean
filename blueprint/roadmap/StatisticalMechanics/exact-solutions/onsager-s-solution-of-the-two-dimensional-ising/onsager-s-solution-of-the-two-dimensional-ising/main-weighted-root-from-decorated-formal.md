# Edge-weighted root identity from the decorated-torus formal identity

For $L > 2$, if the [decorated formal Kac–Ward identity](../../spin-kac-ward-identities/ons-decorated-formal-kac-ward-identity.md) holds on the $L \times L$ torus, then the [edge-weighted root identity](../../spin-kac-ward-identities/ons-weighted-root-identity.md) holds on it.
