# Onsager's formula from the spin Kac-Ward identities

Suppose the [spin Kac–Ward identities](../from-the-spin-kac-ward-identities-to-onsager-s-f/ons-spin-kac-ward-identities.md) hold at every $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. Then for every $\beta \ge 0$, $p_L(\beta) \to \log 2 + I(\beta)$ as $L \to \infty$, where $p_L(\beta)$ is the [torus pressure sequence](../ons-torus-pressure-seq.md): $\log Z_{T_L}(\beta)/L^2$ for $L > 2$ and $0$ for $L \le 2$, and $I(\beta)$ is [Onsager's free-energy integral](../onsager-s-free-energy-and-the-torus-pressure/ons-free-energy-integral.md).
