# Kramers-Wannier critical point

The [critical inverse temperature](../the-critical-point-of-the-square-lattice-ising-m/ons-beta-c.md) $\beta_c = \tfrac{1}{2}\log(1 + \sqrt 2)$ satisfies $\sinh(2\beta_c) = 1$ (Kramers and Wannier, *Statistics of the two-dimensional ferromagnet. Part I*, Phys. Rev. 60 (1941)).
