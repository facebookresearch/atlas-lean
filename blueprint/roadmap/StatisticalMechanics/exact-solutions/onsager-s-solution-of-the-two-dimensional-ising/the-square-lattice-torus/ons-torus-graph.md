# The $L \times L$ square-lattice torus graph

For $L > 2$, the torus graph $T_L$ is the simple graph on $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ whose adjacency is the [nearest-neighbour relation](ons-torus-adj.md): $u \sim v$ when they agree in one coordinate and differ by $\pm 1$ in the other. It is symmetric and loopless because $L > 2$.
