# The torus graph is a circulant graph with jumps $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$

For $L > 2$, the [$L \times L$ torus graph](ons-torus-graph.md) equals the circulant graph on the group $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ with jump set $\{(1, 0), (0, 1)\}$: $u$ and $v$ are adjacent exactly when $u \ne v$ and $u - v$ or $v - u$ lies in $\{(1, 0), (0, 1)\}$.
