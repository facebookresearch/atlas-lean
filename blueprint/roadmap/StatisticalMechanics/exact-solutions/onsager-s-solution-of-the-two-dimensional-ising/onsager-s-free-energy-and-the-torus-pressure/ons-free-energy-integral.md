# Onsager's free-energy double integral

For $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the free-energy integral is

$$\frac{1}{8\pi^2} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \log g_\beta(k_1, k_2)\, dk_2\, dk_1,$$

where $g_\beta(k_1, k_2) = \cosh^2(2\beta) - \sinh(2\beta)(\cos k_1 + \cos k_2)$ is the [Onsager integrand](../the-critical-point-of-the-square-lattice-ising-m/ons-g-int.md), $\log$ is the real logarithm ($\log x = \log|x|$, $\log 0 = 0$), and a non-integrable integrand gives $0$.
