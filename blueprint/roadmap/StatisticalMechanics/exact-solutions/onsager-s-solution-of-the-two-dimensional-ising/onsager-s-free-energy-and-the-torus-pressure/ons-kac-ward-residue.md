# Limit statement for $\log X_L(\tanh\beta)/L^2$ on the torus

For $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the residue statement holds when

$$\frac{\log X_{T_L}(\tanh\beta)}{L^2} \to I(\beta) - 2\log\cosh\beta \qquad (L \to \infty),$$

where the sequence is taken to be $0$ for $L \le 2$, $X_G(x) = \sum_{F} x^{|F|}$ is the [even-subgraph generating function](../high-temperature-expansion-of-the-ising-partitio/ons-x.md), the sum over the subsets $F \subseteq E$ in which every vertex has even degree, $T_L$ is the [$L \times L$ torus graph](../the-square-lattice-torus/ons-torus-graph.md) on $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$, and $I(\beta)$ is [Onsager's free-energy integral](ons-free-energy-integral.md).
