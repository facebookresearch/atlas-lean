# Even-subgraph generating function of a graph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The even-subgraph generating function is $X_G(x) = \sum_F x^{|F|}$, the sum over the subsets $F \subseteq E$ in which every vertex has even degree (the empty set contributes $1$).
