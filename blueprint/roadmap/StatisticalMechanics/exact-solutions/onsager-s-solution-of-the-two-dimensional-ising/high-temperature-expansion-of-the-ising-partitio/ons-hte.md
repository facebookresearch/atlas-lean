# High-temperature expansion

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $Z_G(\beta) = 2^{|V|} (\cosh\beta)^{|E|}\, X_G(\tanh\beta)$, where $Z_G(\beta) = \sum_{s} \exp\big(\beta \sum_{\{x,y\} \in E} s_x s_y\big)$ is the [zero-field Ising partition function](ons-z.md) with unit couplings, the sum over all spin configurations $s : V \to \{+1, -1\}$, and $X_G(x) = \sum_{F} x^{|F|}$ is the [even-subgraph generating function](ons-x.md), the sum over the subsets $F \subseteq E$ in which every vertex has even degree (van der Waerden, *Die lange Reichweite der regelmässigen Atomanordnung in Mischkristallen*, 1941).
