# Zero-field Ising partition function with unit couplings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. The partition function is $Z_G(\beta) = \sum_s \exp\big(\beta \sum_{\{x,y\} \in E} s_x s_y\big)$, the sum over all spin configurations $s : V \to \{+1, -1\}$ (zero field, unit couplings).
