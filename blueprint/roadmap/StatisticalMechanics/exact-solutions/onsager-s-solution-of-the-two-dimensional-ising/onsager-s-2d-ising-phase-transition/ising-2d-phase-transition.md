# Onsager's phase transition for the two-dimensional Ising model

There exist a function $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a real number $\beta_c > 0$ such that, for every real $\beta$, the [free energy densities](finite-ising-free-energy-seq.md) $\log Z_{n+1}(\beta)/(n + 1)^2$ of the periodic $(n + 1) \times (n + 1)$ square lattice converge to $F(\beta)$ as $n \to \infty$, and $F$ is not real analytic at $\beta_c$. Here $Z_L(\beta) = \sum_\sigma e^{-\beta H_L(\sigma)}$ is the [partition function](ising-partition-function.md), with $H_L$ the zero-field nearest-neighbour [energy](ising-energy.md) with unit couplings (Onsager, *Crystal statistics. I. A two-dimensional model with an order-disorder transition*, Phys. Rev. 65 (1944)).
