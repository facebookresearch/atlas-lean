# Torus pressure sequence of the zero-field Ising model

For $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $L \in \mathbb{N}$, the torus pressure is $p_L(\beta) = \log Z_{T_L}(\beta)/L^2$, the [finite-volume pressure](ons-pressure-finite.md) of the [$L \times L$ torus graph](the-square-lattice-torus/ons-torus-graph.md) $T_L$, when $L > 2$, and $p_L(\beta) = 0$ when $L \le 2$.
