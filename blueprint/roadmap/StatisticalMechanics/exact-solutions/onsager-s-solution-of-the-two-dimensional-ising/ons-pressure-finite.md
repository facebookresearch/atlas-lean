# Finite-volume pressure

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a simple graph on a finite set $V$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. The finite-volume pressure is $\log Z_G(\beta) / |V|$ (read as $0$ when $V$ is empty), where $Z_G(\beta) = \sum_{s} \exp\big(\beta \sum_{\{x,y\} \in E} s_x s_y\big)$ is the [zero-field Ising partition function](high-temperature-expansion-of-the-ising-partitio/ons-z.md) with unit couplings, the sum over all spin configurations $s : V \to \{+1, -1\}$.
