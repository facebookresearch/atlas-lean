# Spin Kac-Ward identities at inverse temperature $\beta$

For $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the spin Kac–Ward identities hold when for every $L > 2$, with $x = \tanh\beta$,

$$S^{11}_L(x)^2 = D_L(\beta; 0, 0), \quad S^{01}_L(x)^2 = D_L(\beta; 1, 0), \quad S^{10}_L(x)^2 = D_L(\beta; 0, 1), \quad S^{00}_L(x)^2 = D_L(\beta; 1, 1),$$

where $S^{ab}_L(x)$ are the signed sector sums $S^{00} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} + Z_{01} - Z_{11}$, $S^{01} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} - Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{10} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} + Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{11} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} - Z_{01} - Z_{11}$ of the [sector weights](../../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-sector-weight.md) $Z_h(x) = \sum_{F \text{ even},\ [F] = h} x^{|F|}$ of the torus $T_L$, and $D_L(\beta; a, b)$ is the [sector determinant](../../kac-ward-matrices/ons-spin-det.md) $\prod_{i,j=0}^{L-1} \Delta_\beta\big(2\pi(i + a/2)/L,\ 2\pi(j + b/2)/L\big)$.
