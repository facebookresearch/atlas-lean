# Spin Kac-Ward identities via twisted Kac-Ward determinants

For $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the matrix identities hold when for every $L > 2$, with $x = \tanh\beta$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$ and $\zeta_L(a) = e^{i\pi a/L}$,

$$S^{11}_L(x)^2 = \det(1 - K_{0,0}), \quad S^{01}_L(x)^2 = \det(1 - K_{1,0}), \quad S^{10}_L(x)^2 = \det(1 - K_{0,1}), \quad S^{00}_L(x)^2 = \det(1 - K_{1,1}),$$

where $K_{a,b}$ is the [twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../../kac-ward-matrices/ons-kwmat-phase.md) of the torus with parameters $x$, $\omega$, $u = \zeta_L(a)$, $v = \zeta_L(b)$, and $S^{ab}_L(x)$ are the signed sector sums $S^{00} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} + Z_{01} - Z_{11}$, $S^{01} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} - Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{10} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} + Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{11} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} - Z_{01} - Z_{11}$ of the [sector weights](../../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-sector-weight.md) $Z_h(x) = \sum_{F \text{ even},\ [F] = h} x^{|F|}$ of the torus $T_L$.
