# Rooted closed walks as lists of first-return segments

For a set $E$ and $e, r \in E$ with $e \ne r$, taking [first-return factors](ons-first-return-factors.md) is a bijection from the [rooted lists](ons-valid-rooted-list.md) (lists starting and ending at $e$ with no entry equal to $r$) onto the [valid factor lists](ons-valid-factor-list.md) (lists of first-return segments at $e$ avoiding $r$); its inverse is [gluing](ons-glue.md) at $e$.
