# Matrix bucket: walks avoiding two given states

For a finite set $E$, $M \in \mathbb{C}^{E \times E}$, $e, r \in E$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the bucket is the sum of the loop weights $\prod_{k=0}^{n} M(v_k, v_{k+1})$ (indices mod $n + 1$) over all sequences $(v_0, \dots, v_n) \in E^{n+1}$ that contain neither $e$ nor $r$.
