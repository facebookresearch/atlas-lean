# Masked darts after pairwise elimination along a list

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $\mathcal{F}$ of darts of the torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ and a list $(e_1, \dots, e_k)$ of darts, the mask after elimination is $\mathcal{F} \cup \{e_1, \bar e_1, \dots, e_k, \bar e_k\}$, obtained by [adding each dart and its reverse](ons-add-dart-pair.md) in turn ($\mathcal{F}$ itself for the empty list).
