# Loop sum vanishes under a fixed-point-free sign-reversing involution

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\Lambda \in \mathbb{C}^{E \times E}$, $n \ge 1$, $S$ a finite set of sequences in $E^n$, and $g : E^n \to E^n$ such that, for every $v \in S$: $g(v) \in S$, $W_\Lambda(g(v)) = -W_\Lambda(v)$, $g(g(v)) = v$ and $g(v) \ne v$. Then $\sum_{v \in S} W_\Lambda(v) = 0$, where $W_\Lambda(v) = \prod_k \Lambda(v_k, v_{k+1})$ (indices mod $n$) is the loop weight.
