# State-weighted reversible turn matrix

For a set $E$, a direction map $\delta : E \to \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, $\omega \in \mathbb{C}$, weights $\alpha, \gamma : E \to \mathbb{C}$ and $q : E \times E \to \mathbb{C}$, the matrix has entry $\alpha(x)\, \gamma(y)\, q(x, y)\, w_\omega(\delta(y), \delta(x))$ at $(x, y)$, where $w_\omega$ is the [turn weight](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-turn-w.md).
