# Exponential of minus half the series avoiding a dart and its reverse

For $L \ge 1$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ and a dart $e$ of the torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$, the value is

$$\exp\Big(-\frac{1}{2} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{N_n}{n + 1}\Big),$$

where $N_n$ is the [twisted bucket](ons-phase-bucket-n.md) of walks of $n + 1$ darts avoiding $e$ and its reverse at parameters $x$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$, $a$, $b$, and a non-summable series counts as $0$.
