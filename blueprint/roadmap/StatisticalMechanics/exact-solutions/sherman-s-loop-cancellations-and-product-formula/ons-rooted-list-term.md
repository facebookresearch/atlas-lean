# Rooted term: walk weight over its number of first-return factors

For a set $E$, $\Lambda \in \mathbb{C}^{E \times E}$, $e \in E$ and a list $w$, the term is $w_\Lambda(w)/k$, where $k$ is the number of [first-return factors](ons-first-return-factors.md) of $w$ at $e$ (division by $0$ giving $0$) and $w_\Lambda(s) = \prod_{i} \Lambda(s_i, s_{i+1})$ is the [edge weight](ons-edge-weight.md) of a list $s$ (the product over consecutive pairs, $1$ for lists of length at most $1$).
