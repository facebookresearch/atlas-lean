# Walk-series square root of the twisted Kac-Ward determinant

For $L \ge 1$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the value is the [walk-series root](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-det-walk-root.md) $\exp\big(-\tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{n \ge 0} \operatorname{tr}(K_{a,b}^{n+1})/(n + 1)\big)$ of the twisted Kac–Ward matrix, where $K_{a,b}$ is the [twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-kwmat-phase.md) of the torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ with parameters $x$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$, $u = e^{i\pi a/L}$, $v = e^{i\pi b/L}$ (a non-summable series counting as $0$).
