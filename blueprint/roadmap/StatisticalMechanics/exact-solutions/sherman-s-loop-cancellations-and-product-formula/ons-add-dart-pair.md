# Adding a dart and its reverse to the masked set

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $\mathcal{F}$ of darts of the torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ and a dart $e$, the enlarged set is $\{e, \bar e\} \cup \mathcal{F}$, where $\bar e$ is the reverse of $e$ and the reverse of a dart $(p, \mu)$ is $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$, with $s(\mu)$ the unit step of direction $\mu$.
