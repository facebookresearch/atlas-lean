# Internal part of a loop exponent

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $(d_k = (p_k, \mu_k))_{k < n}$ of darts, the internal exponent is $\sum_k \sum_{i \in I(\mu_{k+1} + 2,\ \mu_k)} [c_{p_k, i}]$ (indices mod $n$), where $I(\alpha, \beta) = \{i \in \{0, 1, 2\} : (i < \alpha) \ne (i < \beta)\}$ is the [chain path](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md) and $c_{p,i}$ the [chain edge](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-edge.md) $\{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$.
