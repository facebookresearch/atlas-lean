# Scalar of a Kac-Ward transition

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$ and darts $d_2 = (p_2, \mu_2)$, $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, the transition scalar is $\pi_{u,v}(\mu_1)\, w_\omega(\mu_1, \mu_2)$ if $p_2 = p_1 + s(\mu_1)$ and $0$ otherwise, where $\pi_{u,v}$ is the [direction phase](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-dir-phase.md) and $w_\omega$ the [turn weight](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-turn-w.md).
