# External part of a loop exponent

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $(d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ of darts, the external exponent is $\sum_{k} [\{d_k, \bar d_k\}]$, counting each external edge with the number of indices $k$ that produce it, where $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
