# Edge-weighted spin-character sum over even subgraphs

For $L > 2$, a weight $w$ on unordered pairs of sites and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the sum is $\sum_F \chi_{a,b}([F]) \prod_{f \in F} w(f)$ over the even subgraphs $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$ (sets of edges in which every site has even degree), where $[F]$ is the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md) of a torus edge set $F$ and $\chi_{a,b}(h) = (-1)^{(1+a)h_1 + (1+b)h_2 + h_1 h_2}$ is the [spin character](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-spin-character.md).
