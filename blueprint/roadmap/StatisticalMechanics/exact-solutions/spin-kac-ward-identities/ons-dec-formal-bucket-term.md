# Formal-logarithm term from closed walks of given length and exponent

For $L \ge 1$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$, a weight $\eta$ on decorated edges, $r \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finitely supported exponent $m$, the term is

$$\frac{1}{r + 1} \Big(\sum_{d :\ \varepsilon(d) = m} \kappa_{\omega,u,v}(d)\Big)\, \eta^m,$$

the sum over sequences $d$ of $r + 1$ darts, where $\varepsilon(d) = \sum_{k} \varepsilon(d_k, d_{k+1})$ is the [loop exponent](ons-dec-loop-exponent.md) and $\kappa(d) = \prod_k \kappa(d_k, d_{k+1})$ the [loop scalar](ons-dec-loop-scalar.md) of a cyclic dart sequence $d$ (indices mod its length) and $\eta^m = \prod_e \eta(e)^{m(e)}$.
