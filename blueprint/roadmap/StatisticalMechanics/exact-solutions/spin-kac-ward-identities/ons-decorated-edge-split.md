# Split of a decorated even subgraph into a rooted cycle and remainder

For $L > 2$, a dart $d$ and an even subgraph $D$ of the [decorated torus graph](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ (vertices the darts of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$; external edges $\{d, \bar d\}$ joining each dart to its reverse, and chain edges $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, joining the four darts at each site $p$) containing $\{d, \bar d\}$, a split of $D$ at $d$ consists of a closed walk $\gamma$ from $d$ to $d$ in $\mathcal{D}_L$ and an even subgraph $R$ of $\mathcal{D}_L$ such that $\gamma$ is a cycle, the second vertex of $\gamma$ is $\bar d$, $D = E(\gamma) \cup R$ with $E(\gamma) \cap R = \emptyset$ ($E(\gamma)$ the set of edges of $\gamma$), and no edge of $R$ is incident to a vertex of $\gamma$ (in particular to $d$ or $\bar d$); here $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
