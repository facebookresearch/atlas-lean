# Degree-$n$ part of a power series evaluated at a point

For a set $E$, a power series $\Phi = \sum_m \phi_m X^m$ in variables indexed by $E$, weights $\eta : E \to \mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the degree-$n$ value is $\sum_{|m| = n} \phi_m\, \eta^m$, the sum over finitely supported exponents of total degree $n$ (a non-summable family counting as $0$), where $\eta^m = \prod_e \eta(e)^{m(e)}$.
