# Edge-weighted root identity on the torus

For $L > 2$, the edge-weighted root identity holds when for all $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, every weight $w$ on unordered pairs of sites and every $q$ with $0 \le q < 1/(2N^2)$ with $N = 4L^2$ the number of darts, and $|w(f)| \le q$ for every pair $f$, $R(K) = \sum_F \chi_{a,b}([F]) \prod_{f \in F} w(f)$, the sum over the even subgraphs $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$ (sets of edges in which every site has even degree), where $K = K^w_{a,b}$ is the [edge-weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-kwmat-weighted-phase.md) with weights $w$, turning phase $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$ and boundary phases $e^{i\pi a/L}, e^{i\pi b/L}$, $R(\Lambda) = \exp\big(-\tfrac12 \sum_{n \ge 0} \operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{n+1})/(n+1)\big)$ is the [walk-series root](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-det-walk-root.md) (a non-summable series counting as $0$), $[F]$ is the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md) of a torus edge set $F$, and $\chi_{a,b}(h) = (-1)^{(1+a)h_1 + (1+b)h_2 + h_1 h_2}$ is the [spin character](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-spin-character.md).
