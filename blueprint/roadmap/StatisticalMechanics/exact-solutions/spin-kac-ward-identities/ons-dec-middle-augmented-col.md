# Column-scaled augmented Kac-Ward matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, $\omega, t \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $p$, the augmented matrix is indexed by the darts together with two extra indices and has the block form

$$\begin{pmatrix} K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta'}(\omega; 1, 1) & t\, U \\ V & 0 \end{pmatrix},$$

where $\eta'$ is $\eta$ with the weight of the middle chain edge $c_{p,1}$ multiplied by $0$, $K^{\mathrm{dec}}$ is the [decorated weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-kwmat-decoration-weighted-phase.md) with boundary phases $u = v = 1$, and $U$, $V$ are the [middle coupling blocks](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-middle-u.md) at $p$ (with $V$ taken at [phases $1, 1$](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-middle-v.md)).
