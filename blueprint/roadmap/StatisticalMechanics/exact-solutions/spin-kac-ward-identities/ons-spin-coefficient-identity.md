# Spin-character sums squared as twisted determinants at activity $x$

For $L > 2$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, the identity holds when for all $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$

$$\Big(\sum_{F} \chi_{a,b}([F])\, x^{|F|}\Big)^2 = \det\big(1 - K_{a,b}(x)\big),$$

the sum over the even subgraphs $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$ (sets of edges in which every site has even degree), where $[F]$ is the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md) of a torus edge set $F$, $\chi_{a,b}(h) = (-1)^{(1+a)h_1 + (1+b)h_2 + h_1 h_2}$ is the [spin character](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-spin-character.md), and $K_{a,b}(x)$ is the [twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-kwmat-phase.md) with parameters $x$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$, $e^{i\pi a/L}$, $e^{i\pi b/L}$.
