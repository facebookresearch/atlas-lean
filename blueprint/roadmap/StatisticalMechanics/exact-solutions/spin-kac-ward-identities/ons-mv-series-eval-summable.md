# Absolute summability of a power series evaluated at weights

For a set $E$, a power series $\Phi = \sum_m \phi_m X^m$ and weights $\eta : E \to \mathbb{C}$, the evaluation is absolutely summable when the family $|\phi_m\, \eta^m|$, over all finitely supported exponents $m$, is summable; here $\eta^m = \prod_e \eta(e)^{m(e)}$.
