# Spin weight of a decorated even subgraph

For $L > 2$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ and a decorated even subgraph $D$, the spin weight is $\chi_{a,b}([F])\, x^{|F|}$ with $F = \pi(D)$, where $\pi(D)$ is the [torus even subgraph corresponding to $D$](ons-original-edges-of-decorated.md), $[F]$ is the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md) of a torus edge set $F$, and $\chi_{a,b}(h) = (-1)^{(1+a)h_1 + (1+b)h_2 + h_1 h_2}$ is the [spin character](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-spin-character.md).
