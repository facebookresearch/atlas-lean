# Monomial of a Kac-Ward transition

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$ and darts $d_2, d_1$ of $T_L$, the transition monomial is $\kappa_{\omega,u,v}(d_2, d_1)\, X^{\varepsilon(d_2, d_1)}$, where $\varepsilon(d_2, d_1) = [\{d_1, \bar d_1\}] + \sum_{i} [c_{p(d_2), i}]$ is the [transition exponent](ons-dec-transition-exponent.md) over $i \in$ [the chain path](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md) between positions $\mu_1 + 2$ and $\mu_2$, and $\kappa(d_2, d_1)$ is the [transition scalar](ons-dec-transition-scalar.md).
