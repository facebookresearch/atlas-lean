# Exponent of a Kac-Ward transition

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and darts $d_2 = (p_2, \mu_2)$, $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, the transition exponent is

$$\varepsilon(d_2, d_1) = [\{d_1, \bar d_1\}] + \sum_{i \in I(\mu_1 + 2,\ \mu_2)} [c_{p_2, i}],$$

where $[e]$ is the unit exponent at $e$, $I(\alpha, \beta) = \{i \in \{0, 1, 2\} : (i < \alpha) \ne (i < \beta)\}$ is the [chain path](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md), $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$ is the [chain edge](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-edge.md), and $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
