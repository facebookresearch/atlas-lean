# Sector weight of a homology class as a polynomial

For $L > 2$ and $h \in (\mathbb{Z}/2)^2$, the sector polynomial is $Z_h(X) = \sum_{F} X^{|F|} \in \mathbb{R}[X]$, the sum over the even subgraphs $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$ (sets of edges in which every site has even degree) with $[F] = h$, where $[F]$ is the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md) of a torus edge set $F$.
