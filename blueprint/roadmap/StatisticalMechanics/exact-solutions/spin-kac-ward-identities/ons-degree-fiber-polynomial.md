# Homogeneous degree-$n$ part of a power series as a polynomial

For a finite set $E$, a power series $\Phi = \sum_m \phi_m X^m$ in variables indexed by $E$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the degree-$n$ part is the polynomial $\sum_{|m| = n} \phi_m X^m$ (finitely many exponents $m$ have total degree $n$).
