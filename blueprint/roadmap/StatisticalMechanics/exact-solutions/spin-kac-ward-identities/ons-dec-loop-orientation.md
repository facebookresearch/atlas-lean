# Rotations of a loop and of its reversal

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a cyclic sequence $d$ of $n$ darts, the orientation map sends $(+, k)$ to the [rotation](../sherman-s-loop-cancellations-and-product-formula/ons-rotate.md) of $d$ by $k$ and $(-, k)$ to the rotation by $k$ of the [reversed loop](../sherman-s-loop-cancellations-and-product-formula/ons-loop-rev.md) $j \mapsto \bar d_{-j}$, for $k \in \mathbb{Z}/n$.
