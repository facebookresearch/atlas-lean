# One-edge decorated walk along an external edge

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a dart $d$ of $T_L$, the step walk is the walk of length $1$ in the [decorated torus graph](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ (vertices the darts of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$; external edges $\{d, \bar d\}$ joining each dart to its reverse, and chain edges $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, joining the four darts at each site $p$) from $d$ to $\bar d$ along the external edge $\{d, \bar d\}$, where $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
