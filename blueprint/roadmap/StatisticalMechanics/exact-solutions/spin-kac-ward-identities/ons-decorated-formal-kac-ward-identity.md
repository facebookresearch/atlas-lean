# Formal Kac-Ward identity on the torus

For $L > 2$, the formal Kac–Ward identity holds when for all $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ the [formal Kac–Ward root](ons-dec-formal-root.md) with $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$ and $\zeta_L(a) = e^{i\pi a/L}$ (boundary phases $\zeta_L(a), \zeta_L(b)$) equals the [decorated spin polynomial](ons-decorated-spin-polynomial.md) $\sum_D \chi_{a,b}([\pi(D)])\, X^D$, viewed as a power series.
