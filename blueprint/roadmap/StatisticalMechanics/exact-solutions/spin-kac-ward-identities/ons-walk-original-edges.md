# Torus edges underlying a decorated walk

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a walk $\gamma$ of the decorated torus graph, the set consists of the
[projections](ons-dec-edge-projection.md) to pairs of sites of the [external edges](ons-walk-external-edges.md)
traversed by $\gamma$: the external edge joining a dart $d = (p, \mu)$ to its reverse $\bar d = (p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$
projects to the torus edge $\{p, p + s(\mu)\}$.
