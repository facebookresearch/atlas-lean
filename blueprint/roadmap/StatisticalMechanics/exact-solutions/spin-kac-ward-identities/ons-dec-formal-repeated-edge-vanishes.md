# Vanishing of repeated-edge coefficients of the formal Kac-Ward root

The repeated-edge vanishing statement holds when for every $L > 2$, all $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, every exponent $m$ and every decorated edge $e$ with $m(e) \ge 2$, the coefficient at $X^m$ of the [formal Kac–Ward root](ons-dec-formal-root.md) with $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$, $u = e^{i\pi a/L}$, $v = e^{i\pi b/L}$ is $0$.
