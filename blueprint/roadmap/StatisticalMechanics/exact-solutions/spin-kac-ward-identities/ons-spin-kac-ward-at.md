# Four spin Kac-Ward identities on the torus at activity $x$

For $L > 2$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, the identities hold when

$$S^{11}_L(x)^2 = D_L(x; 0, 0), \quad S^{01}_L(x)^2 = D_L(x; 1, 0), \quad S^{10}_L(x)^2 = D_L(x; 0, 1), \quad S^{00}_L(x)^2 = D_L(x; 1, 1),$$

where $S^{ab}_L(x)$ are the signed sector sums $S^{00} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} + Z_{01} - Z_{11}$, $S^{01} = Z_{00} + Z_{10} - Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{10} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} + Z_{01} + Z_{11}$, $S^{11} = Z_{00} - Z_{10} - Z_{01} - Z_{11}$ of the [sector weights](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-sector-weight.md) $Z_h(x)$ of $T_L$ and $D_L(x; a, b) = \prod_{i,j=0}^{L-1}\big((1 + x^2)^2 - 2x(1 - x^2)(\cos\theta_i^a + \cos\theta_j^b)\big)$, $\theta_i^a = 2\pi(i + a/2)/L$, is the [sector determinant in $x$](ons-spin-det-x.md).
