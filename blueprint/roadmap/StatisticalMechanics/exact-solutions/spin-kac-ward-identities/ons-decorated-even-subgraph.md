# Even subgraphs of the decorated torus graph

For $L > 2$, the decorated even subgraphs are the even subgraphs of the [decorated torus graph](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ (vertices the darts of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$; external edges $\{d, \bar d\}$ joining each dart to its reverse, and chain edges $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, joining the four darts at each site $p$): sets of edges in which every dart has even degree.
