# Chosen split of a decorated even subgraph at an external edge

For $L > 2$, a dart $d$ and an even subgraph $D$ of the decorated torus graph containing $\{d, \bar d\}$, the chosen split is a [split](ons-decorated-edge-split.md) of $D$ at $d$: a cycle of the decorated graph from $d$ whose second vertex is $\bar d$, and a decorated even subgraph $R$, with the properties listed there (one such split exists, and a fixed one is chosen).
