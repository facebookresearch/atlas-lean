# Column weights of the column-scaled augmented matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and $t \in \mathbb{C}$, the column weight is $\eta(\{d, \bar d\})$ at a dart $d$ and $t$ at each of the two extra indices, where $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
