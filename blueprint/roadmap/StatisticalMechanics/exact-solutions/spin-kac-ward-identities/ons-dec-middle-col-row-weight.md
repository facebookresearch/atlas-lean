# Row weights of the column-scaled augmented matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and a site $p$, the row weight is $1$ at every dart and $\eta(c_{p,1})$ at each of the two extra indices, where $c_{p,1}$ is the middle [chain edge](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-chain-edge.md) $\{(p, 1), (p, 2)\}$.
