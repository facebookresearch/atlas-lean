# Masked closed-walk weight through a dart but not its reverse

Let $L \ge 1$, $\eta$ a complex weight on the decorated edges, $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, a finite set $\mathcal{F}$ of darts, a dart $e$ of $T_L$, and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The value is the sum of the loop weights under $K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta}$ with every row and column indexed by a dart of $\mathcal{F}$ set to $0$ over all sequences $(v_0, \dots, v_n)$ of $n + 1$ darts that contain $e$ but not $\bar e$. Here $K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta}$ is the [decorated edge-weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-kwmat-decoration-weighted-phase.md) with decorated weights $\eta$, turning phase $\omega$ and boundary phases $\zeta_L(a), \zeta_L(b)$, $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$ and $\zeta_L(a) = e^{i\pi a/L}$, the loop weight of a cyclic sequence $(v_0, \dots, v_n)$ under a matrix $\Lambda$ is $\prod_{k=0}^{n} \Lambda(v_k, v_{k+1})$, indices mod $n + 1$, and $\bar d$ is the reverse of a dart $d = (p, \mu)$, namely $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
