# Cycle removal identity for decorated even subgraphs

For $L > 2$, the cycle removal identity holds when for all $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, every weight $w$ on the unordered pairs of sites of $T_L$ and every dart $d$,

$$R\big(K^{\{d, \bar d\}}\big) \cdot \sum_s f(K; d, \bar d; s) = \sum_{D} w_K\big(\gamma(D)\big)\, W_w\big(\rho(D)\big),$$

the right sum over the [decorated even subgraphs containing $\{d, \bar d\}$](ons-decorated-containing-edge.md), where $K = K^w_{a,b}$ is the [edge-weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-kwmat-weighted-phase.md) with weights $w$, turning phase $\omega = e^{i\pi/4}$ and boundary phases $e^{i\pi a/L}, e^{i\pi b/L}$, $K^{\{d, \bar d\}}$ is $K$ with the rows and columns of $d$ and $\bar d$ set to $0$, $R(\Lambda) = \exp\big(-\tfrac12 \sum_{n \ge 0} \operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{n+1})/(n+1)\big)$ is the [walk-series root](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-det-walk-root.md) (a non-summable series counting as $0$), $f(\Lambda; e, \bar e; s)$ is the [first-return weight](../sherman-s-loop-cancellations-and-product-formula/ons-first-return-weight.md) of a list $s$ of darts and the series over $s$ runs over all lists (a non-summable series counting as $0$), $(\gamma(D), \rho(D))$ is the [chosen split](ons-decorated-edge-split-2.md) of $D$ into a cycle through $\{d, \bar d\}$ (read as its [first-return list](../trivalent-decoration-of-the-torus/ons-dec-cycle-first-return.md)) and a remainder, $w_K$ is the [edge weight](../sherman-s-loop-cancellations-and-product-formula/ons-edge-weight.md) of a list, and $W_w$ the [weighted spin weight](ons-decorated-weighted-spin-weight.md).
