# Onsager's formula

For every $\beta \ge 0$, the torus pressures $p_L(\beta)$ converge to $\log 2 + I(\beta)$ as $L \to \infty$, where $p_L(\beta) = \log Z_{T_L}(\beta)/L^2$ for $L > 2$ (and $0$ for $L \le 2$) is the [torus pressure sequence](../onsager-s-solution-of-the-two-dimensional-ising/ons-torus-pressure-seq.md) of the zero-field Ising model with unit couplings, and $I(\beta)$ is [Onsager's free-energy integral](../onsager-s-solution-of-the-two-dimensional-ising/onsager-s-free-energy-and-the-torus-pressure/ons-free-energy-integral.md) (Onsager, *Crystal statistics. I*, 1944).
