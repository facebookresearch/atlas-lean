# Second difference of a formal series in one variable

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a power series $\Phi = \sum_m \phi_m X^m$ in the decorated-edge variables and a decorated edge $e$, the second difference is $\sum_m \big(2^{m(e)} - 2 + 0^{m(e)}\big) \phi_m X^m$ (with $0^0 = 1$, so the factor vanishes for $m(e) \in \{0, 1\}$).
