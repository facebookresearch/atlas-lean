# Formal logarithm of the Kac-Ward root

For $L \ge 1$ and $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$, the formal logarithm is the power series whose coefficient at each exponent $m$ is the [log coefficient](ons-dec-formal-log-coeff.md) at $m$; the series are formal power series in commuting variables $X_e$ indexed by the [decorated edges](ons-dec-edge.md) $e$ (unordered pairs of darts of $T_L$), with $X^m = \prod_e X_e^{m(e)}$ for a finitely supported exponent $m$.
