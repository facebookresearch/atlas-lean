# Sector determinant as a real polynomial

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the polynomial is

$$\prod_{i=0}^{L-1} \prod_{j=0}^{L-1} \Big((1 + X^2)^2 - 2\big(\cos\theta_i^a + \cos\theta_j^b\big)\, X (1 - X^2)\Big) \in \mathbb{R}[X], \qquad \theta_i^a = \frac{2\pi(i + a/2)}{L}$$

(the empty product $1$ when $L = 0$).
