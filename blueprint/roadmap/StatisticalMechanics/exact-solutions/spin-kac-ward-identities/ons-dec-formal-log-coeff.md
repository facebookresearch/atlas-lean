# Coefficient of the formal Kac-Ward logarithm

For $L \ge 1$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$ and a finitely supported exponent $m$ on the decorated edges, the coefficient is

$$-\frac{1}{2} \sum_{r=0}^{|m| - 1} \frac{1}{r + 1} \sum_{d :\ \varepsilon(d) = m} \kappa_{\omega,u,v}(d),$$

the inner sum over sequences $d$ of $r + 1$ darts, where $|m| = \sum_e m(e)$ is the total degree (the coefficient is $0$ for $m = 0$) and $\varepsilon(d) = \sum_{k} \varepsilon(d_k, d_{k+1})$ is the [loop exponent](ons-dec-loop-exponent.md) and $\kappa(d) = \prod_k \kappa(d_k, d_{k+1})$ the [loop scalar](ons-dec-loop-scalar.md) of a cyclic dart sequence $d$ (indices mod its length).
