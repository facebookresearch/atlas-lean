# Scalar of a closed Kac-Ward walk

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $(d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ of darts, the loop scalar is $\prod_{k} \kappa_{\omega,u,v}(d_k, d_{k+1})$ (indices mod $n$), where $\kappa(d_2, d_1)$ is the [transition scalar](ons-dec-transition-scalar.md).
