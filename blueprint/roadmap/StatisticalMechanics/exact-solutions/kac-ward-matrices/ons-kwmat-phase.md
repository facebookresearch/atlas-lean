# Twisted Kac-Ward matrix with direction phases

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x, \omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$, the twisted Kac–Ward matrix is indexed by darts $(p, \mu)$ of $T_L$ (a site $p$ and a direction $\mu \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) and has entry

$$K_{\mathrm{ph}}(d_2, d_1) = \pi_{u,v}(\mu_1)\, K(d_2, d_1)$$

for darts $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, $d_2$, where $K$ is the [Kac–Ward matrix](ons-kwmat.md) with parameters $x, \omega$ and $\pi_{u,v}(\mu)$ is the [direction phase](ons-dir-phase.md) ($u, v, u^{-1}, v^{-1}$ for $\mu = 0, 1, 2, 3$, with $0^{-1} = 0$).
