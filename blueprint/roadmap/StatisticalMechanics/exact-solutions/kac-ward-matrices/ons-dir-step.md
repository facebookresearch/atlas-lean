# Neighbouring site in a given direction on the torus

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a direction $\mu$ and a site $p = (p_1, p_2)$ of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the neighbour is $(p_1 + 1, p_2)$, $(p_1, p_2 + 1)$, $(p_1 - 1, p_2)$, $(p_1, p_2 - 1)$ for $\mu = 0, 1, 2, 3$ respectively.
