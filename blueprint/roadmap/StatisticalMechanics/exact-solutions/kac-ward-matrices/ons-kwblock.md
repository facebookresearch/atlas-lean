# $4 \times 4$ block of the Kac–Ward matrix between a site and the origin

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $x, \omega \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $g$ of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the block is the $4 \times 4$ matrix with entries $K((g, \alpha), (0, \beta))$ for directions $\alpha, \beta$, where $K$ is the [Kac–Ward matrix](ons-kwmat.md) with parameters $x, \omega$.
