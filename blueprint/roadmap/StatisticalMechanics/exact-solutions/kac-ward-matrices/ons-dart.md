# Darts of the square-lattice torus

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a dart of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$) is a pair $(p, \mu)$ of a site $p \in T_L$ and a direction $\mu \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$; there are $4L^2$ darts when $L \ge 1$.
