# Translation of a dart by a lattice vector

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a vector $t = (t_1, t_2)$ and a dart $((p_1, p_2), \mu)$ of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the translated dart is $((p_1 + t_1, p_2 + t_2), \mu)$.
