# Turn weight between two lattice directions

For $\omega \in \mathbb{C}$ and directions $\mu, \nu \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, the turn weight $w_\omega(\mu, \nu)$ is $1$ if $\nu = \mu$, $\omega$ if $\nu = \mu + 1$, $0$ if $\nu = \mu + 2$, and $\omega^{-1}$ if $\nu = \mu + 3$, indices mod $4$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
