# Masked edge-weighted bucket: walks avoiding a dart and its reverse

Let $L \ge 1$, let $w$ assign a complex weight to each unordered pair of sites of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$, and let $\omega \in \mathbb{C}$, $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, a finite set $\mathcal{F}$ of forbidden darts, a dart $e$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The bucket is the sum, over all sequences $(d_0, \dots, d_n)$ of $n + 1$ darts that contain neither $e$ nor its reverse, of their loop weights under the matrix obtained from $K$ by setting to $0$ every row and column indexed by a dart of $\mathcal{F}$. Here $K = K^{w}_{\omega}(\zeta_L(a), \zeta_L(b))$ is the [edge-weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](ons-kwmat-weighted-phase.md), $\zeta_L(a) = e^{i\pi a/L}$ is the [boundary phase](ons-spin-phase.md), the loop weight of a cyclic dart sequence $(d_0, \dots, d_n)$ under a matrix $\Lambda$ is $\prod_{k=0}^{n} \Lambda(d_k, d_{k+1})$, indices mod $n + 1$, and the reverse of a dart $(p, \mu)$ is $(p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$.
