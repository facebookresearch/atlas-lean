# Edge weight function with one weight scaled by $t$

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a weight $w$ on unordered pairs of sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), a pair $e$ and $t \in \mathbb{C}$, the scaled weight is $f \mapsto t\, w(f)$ if $f = e$ and $f \mapsto w(f)$ otherwise.
