# Sector determinant with boundary phases

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the sector determinant is

$$\prod_{i=0}^{L-1} \prod_{j=0}^{L-1} \Delta_\beta\Big(\frac{2\pi(i + a/2)}{L},\ \frac{2\pi(j + b/2)}{L}\Big),$$

where $\Delta_\beta(k_1, k_2) = (1 + \tanh^2\beta)^2 - 2\tanh\beta\,(1 - \tanh^2\beta)(\cos k_1 + \cos k_2)$ is the [symbol dispersion](../onsager-s-solution-of-the-two-dimensional-ising/ons-symbol-dispersion.md) (the empty product is $1$ when $L = 0$).
