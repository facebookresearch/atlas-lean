# Side-colouring property of closed dual walks

Let $V$ be a finite set and let $G_p$ (primal) and $G_d$ (dual) be simple graphs on $V$ and $\psi$ a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. Closed dual walks have side colourings when for every closed walk $W$ of $G_d$ there is $c : V \to \{0, 1\}$ such that for all adjacent $u, v$ in $G_p$, $\{u, v\} \in \psi^{-1}(E(W))$ if and only if $c(u) \ne c(v)$, where $E(W)$ is the set of edges traversed by $W$.
