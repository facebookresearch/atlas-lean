# Cycle-parity compatibility of a dual edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set, $G_p$ a simple graph on $V$, $\psi$ a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $F$ a finite set of pairs. $F$ is cycle-parity compatible when $\psi^{-1}(F)$ is even on cycles of $G_p$, where a set $\delta$ of pairs is even on cycles of $G_p$ when every closed walk of $G_p$ traverses edges of $\delta$ an even number of times (counted with multiplicity).
