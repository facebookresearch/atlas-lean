# Even subgraphs of a finite graph decompose into closed walks

For every simple graph $G_d$ on a finite set $V$, every [even subgraph](even-subgraphs.md) $F$ of $G_d$ is the union of the edge sets of finitely many closed walks of $G_d$ whose edge sets are pairwise disjoint (the property of [walk decomposition](even-subgraph-walk-decomp.md)).
