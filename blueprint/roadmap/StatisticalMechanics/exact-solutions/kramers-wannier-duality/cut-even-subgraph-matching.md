# Proportionality of the cut sum and the dual even-subgraph sum

Let $V$ be a finite set and let $G_p$ (primal) and $G_d$ (dual) be simple graphs on $V$, and let $t, c \in \mathbb{R}$. The cut matching with multiplier $c$ holds when

$$\sum_{s} t^{|C_p(s)|} = c \sum_{F} t^{|F|},$$

where the left sum runs over all spin configurations $s : V \to \{+1, -1\}$, $C_p(s)$ is the [cut set](cut-edges.md) of $s$ in $G_p$, and the right sum runs over the [even subgraphs](even-subgraphs.md) $F$ of $G_d$.
