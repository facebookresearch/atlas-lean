# Edge crossing from cycle-parity compatibility of even subgraphs

Let $V$ be a finite set and let $G_p$ (primal) and $G_d$ (dual) be simple graphs on $V$. Suppose $V$ is nonempty and $G_p$ is connected, and let $\psi$ be a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$ such that $\psi$ maps edges of $G_p$ to edges of $G_d$, $\psi^{-1}$ maps edges of $G_d$ to edges of $G_p$, and $\psi(C)$ is an even subgraph of $G_d$ for every cut set $C$ of $G_p$, and suppose that for every even subgraph $F$ of $G_d$ the set $\psi^{-1}(F)$ is even on cycles of $G_p$ (traversed an even number of times by every closed walk of $G_p$). Then $\psi$, with these properties, is an [edge crossing](edge-crossing.md) from $G_p$ to $G_d$: in addition, $\psi^{-1}(F)$ is a cut set of $G_p$ for every even subgraph $F$ of $G_d$.
