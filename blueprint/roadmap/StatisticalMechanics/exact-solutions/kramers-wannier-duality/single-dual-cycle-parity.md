# Cycle-parity compatibility of a family of dual cycle sets

Let $V$ be a finite set, $G_p$ a simple graph on $V$, $\psi$ a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $\mathcal{C}$ a property of finite sets of pairs. The condition holds when $\psi^{-1}(C)$ is even on cycles of $G_p$ for every set $C$ with property $\mathcal{C}$, where a set $\delta$ of pairs is even on cycles of $G_p$ when every closed walk of $G_p$ traverses edges of $\delta$ an even number of times (counted with multiplicity).
