# Edge crossing from side colourings of dual walks

Let $V$ be a finite set and let $G_p$ (primal) and $G_d$ (dual) be simple graphs on $V$. Suppose $V$ is nonempty and $G_p$ is connected, and let $\psi$ be a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$ such that $\psi$ maps edges of $G_p$ to edges of $G_d$, $\psi^{-1}$ maps edges of $G_d$ to edges of $G_p$, and $\psi(C)$ is an even subgraph of $G_d$ for every cut set $C$ of $G_p$, suppose every even subgraph of $G_d$ [decomposes into edge-disjoint closed walks](even-subgraph-walk-decomp.md), and suppose that closed dual walks have [side colourings](dual-walk-has-side-colouring.md): for every closed walk $W$ of $G_d$ there is $c : V \to \{0, 1\}$ such that for all adjacent $u, v$ in $G_p$, $\{u, v\} \in \psi^{-1}(E(W))$ if and only if $c(u) \ne c(v)$, where $E(W)$ is the set of edges traversed by $W$. Then $\psi$, with these properties, is an [edge crossing](edge-crossing.md) from $G_p$ to $G_d$: in addition, $\psi^{-1}(F)$ is a cut set of $G_p$ for every even subgraph $F$ of $G_d$.
