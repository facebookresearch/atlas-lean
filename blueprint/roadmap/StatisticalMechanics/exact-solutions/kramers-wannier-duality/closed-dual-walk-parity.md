# Cycle-parity compatibility of closed dual walks

Let $V$ be a finite set and let $G_p$ (primal) and $G_d$ (dual) be simple graphs on $V$ and $\psi$ a bijection of the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. The closed-walk parity condition holds when, for every closed walk $W$ of $G_d$, the set $\psi^{-1}(E(W))$ is even on cycles of $G_p$, where $E(W)$ is the set of edges traversed by $W$ and a set $\delta$ of pairs is even on cycles of $G_p$ when every closed walk of $G_p$ traverses edges of $\delta$ an even number of times (counted with multiplicity).
