# Component of a vertex in the graph spanned by an edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set, $F$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $f \in V$. The component graph of $f$ is the graph on $V$ whose edges are the edges of the [graph spanned by $F$](subgraph-f.md) both of whose endpoints are joined to $f$ by a path in that graph.
