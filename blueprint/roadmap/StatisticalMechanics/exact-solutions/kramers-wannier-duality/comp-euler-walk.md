# Closed Eulerian walk of a component of an even edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set, $F$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ containing no loop and even (every vertex lies in an even number of pairs of $F$), and $f \in V$. The walk is a chosen closed walk from $f$ to $f$ in the [component graph](comp-f.md) of $f$ that traverses every edge of that component exactly once (an Eulerian circuit).
