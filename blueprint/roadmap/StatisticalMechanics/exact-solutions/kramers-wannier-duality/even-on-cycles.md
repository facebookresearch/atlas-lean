# Evenness of an edge set on every closed walk

Let $G_p$ be a simple graph on a set $V$ and $\delta$ a finite set of unordered pairs. $\delta$ is even on cycles when every closed walk of $G_p$ has even [crossing parity](walk-parity.md) with respect to $\delta$, that is, traverses edges of $\delta$ an even number of times.
