# Self-dual edge crossing of $K_4$ by complementary edges

The [complementary-edge bijection](k4-comp-equiv.md) of $K_4$, the complete graph on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, is an [edge crossing](edge-crossing.md) from $K_4$ to itself: it maps edges to edges, cut sets to even subgraphs, and even subgraphs back to cut sets.
