# Image of a decorated walk in the finer torus

For $L > 2$ and a walk $(d_0, d_1, \dots, d_m)$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, the embedded walk in the finer torus graph $T_{8L}$ is the concatenation of the [four-step routes](ons-dec-edge-route.md) of its steps $(d_{k}, d_{k+1})$ (the trivial walk at $\iota(d_0)$ when $m = 0$).
