# Directions of the expanded dart list of a decorated walk

For $L > 2$ and a walk of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, the list consists of the directions of the darts of its [expanded dart list](ons-dec-expanded-dart-list.md) in the finer torus $T_{8L} = (\mathbb{Z}/8L\mathbb{Z})^2$, in order.
