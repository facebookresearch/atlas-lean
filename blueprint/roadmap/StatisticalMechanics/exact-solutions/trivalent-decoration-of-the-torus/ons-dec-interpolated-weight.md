# Decorated weights with internal edges weighted by t

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a weight $w$ on unordered pairs of sites of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ and $t \in \mathbb{C}$, the interpolated weight of a decorated edge is $w(\{p, p'\})$ for an external edge $\{(p, \mu), (p', \nu)\}$ and $t$ for every other decorated edge; here a decorated edge is external when it has the form $\{d, \bar d\}$.
