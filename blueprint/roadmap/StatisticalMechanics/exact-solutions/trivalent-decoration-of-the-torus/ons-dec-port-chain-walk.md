# Internal walk between two ports at a site

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a site $p$ and directions $\alpha, \beta$, the chain walk is a chosen walk of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ from $(p, \alpha)$ to $(p, \beta)$ whose set of edges is exactly the set $\{c_{p,i} : i \in I(\alpha, \beta)\}$ of [chain edges between the two ports](ons-dec-chain-path-edges.md).
