# Decorated walk of a chain of darts

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a dart $d_0$ and a list $(d_1, \dots, d_m)$ of darts in which each $d_{k+1}$ starts where $d_k$ ends, the chain walk is the walk of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ from $d_0$ to the [last dart](ons-dec-kwchain-last.md) obtained by concatenating the [transition walks](ons-dec-transition-walk.md) from $d_k$ to $d_{k+1}$ (the trivial walk when $m = 0$).
