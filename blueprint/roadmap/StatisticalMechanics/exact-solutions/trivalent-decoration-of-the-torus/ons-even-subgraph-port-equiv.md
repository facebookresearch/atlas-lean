# Equivalence between even torus subgraphs and port patterns

For $L > 2$, the map sending an even subgraph $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$ to its [port pattern](ons-even-subgraph-port-pattern.md) is a bijection onto the port patterns, with inverse $P \mapsto$ the [torus edge set](ons-port-pattern-edges.md) of $P$.
