# Torus walk of four lattice steps

For $N > 2$, directions $r_0, \dots, r_3$ and a site $z$ of $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^2$, the walk is the walk of four steps in the $N \times N$ torus graph from $z$ through $z + s(r_0)$, $z + s(r_0) + s(r_1)$, $z + s(r_0) + s(r_1) + s(r_2)$ to the [endpoint](ons-four-dir-steps.md).
