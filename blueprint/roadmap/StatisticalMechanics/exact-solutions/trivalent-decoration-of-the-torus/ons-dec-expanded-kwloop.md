# Expanded loop of a decorated cycle in Kac-Ward order

For $L > 2$ and a closed walk $q$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ that is a cycle (so its expanded list has length $\ell \ge 1$), the Kac–Ward loop is $k \mapsto g_{-k}$, indices mod $\ell$, where $g$ is the [expanded geometric loop](ons-dec-expanded-geometric-loop.md) of $q$.
