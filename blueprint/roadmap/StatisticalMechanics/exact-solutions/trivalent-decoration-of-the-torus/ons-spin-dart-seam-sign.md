# Seam sign of a dart

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ and a dart $d$, the seam sign is $(-1)^{a\, \epsilon_x(d) + b\, \epsilon_y(d)}$, where $\epsilon_x$ and $\epsilon_y$ are the [vertical-seam](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-x-wrap-sign.md) and [horizontal-seam crossing signs](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-y-wrap-sign.md) of $d$.
