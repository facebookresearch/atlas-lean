# Decorated character sum with weights on all edges

For $L > 2$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the sum is

$$\sum_{D} \chi_{a,b}\big([\pi(D)]\big) \prod_{e \in D} \eta(e),$$

over the even subgraphs $D$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, where $\pi(D)$ is the [corresponding torus even subgraph](../spin-kac-ward-identities/ons-original-edges-of-decorated.md) and $\chi_{a,b}$ is the [spin character](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-spin-character.md) and $[F]$ the [mod 2 homology class](../homology-and-turning-of-loops-on-the-square-latt/ons-even-homology.md).
