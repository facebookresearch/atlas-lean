# Augmented Kac-Ward matrix with two side states

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, $\omega, u, v, t \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $p$, the augmented matrix is indexed by the darts and two side states and has the block form

$$\begin{pmatrix} K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta'}(\omega; u, v) & U \\ t\, V & 0 \end{pmatrix},$$

where $\eta'$ is $\eta$ with the weight of the middle chain edge $c_{p,1}$ multiplied by $0$, $K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta}(\omega; u, v)$ is the [decorated weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](ons-kwmat-decoration-weighted-phase.md), and $U$, $V$ are the [column](ons-dec-middle-u.md) and [row factors](ons-dec-middle-v.md) of the middle-edge term at $p$ (with $V$ at phases $u, v$).
