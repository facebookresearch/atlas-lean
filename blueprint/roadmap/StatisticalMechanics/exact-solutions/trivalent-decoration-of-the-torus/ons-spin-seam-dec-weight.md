# Decorated weights twisted by seam signs

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the twisted weight of an external edge $e = \{d, \bar d\}$ is $\varsigma_{a,b}(d)\, \eta(e)$, where $\varsigma_{a,b}$ is the [seam sign](ons-spin-dart-seam-sign.md) and $d$ is a chosen dart with $e = \{d, \bar d\}$; every other decorated edge keeps its weight $\eta(e)$.
