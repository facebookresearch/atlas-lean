# Destination-side internal weight to a port

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, a site $p$ and a direction $\mu$, the factor is $\eta(c_{p,0})$ for $\mu = 0$, $1$ for $\mu = 1, 2$, and $\eta(c_{p,2})$ for $\mu = 3$, where $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$.
