# Balanced augmented Kac-Ward matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, $\omega, t, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $p$, the balanced matrix is

$$\begin{pmatrix} K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta'}(\omega; 1, 1) & t\lambda\, U \\ \lambda^{-1} V & 0 \end{pmatrix},$$

indexed by the darts and two side states, where $\eta'$ is $\eta$ with the weight of the middle chain edge $c_{p,1}$ multiplied by $0$, $K^{\mathrm{dec}}_{\eta}(\omega; u, v)$ is the [decorated weighted twisted Kac–Ward matrix](ons-kwmat-decoration-weighted-phase.md), $U$ and $V$ are the [column](ons-dec-middle-u.md) and [row factors](ons-dec-middle-v.md) at $p$ (with $V$ at phases $1, 1$), and $0^{-1} = 0$.
