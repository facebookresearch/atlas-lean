# Internal edges between two ports at a site

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a site $p$ and directions $\alpha, \beta \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, the set is $\{c_{p,i} : i \in I(\alpha, \beta)\}$, the chain edges on the path between the darts $(p, \alpha)$ and $(p, \beta)$, where $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$ and $I(\alpha, \beta) = \{i \in \{0, 1, 2\} : (i < \alpha) \ne (i < \beta)\}$ is the set of [chain indices between ports](ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md) $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
