# Port pattern of an even subgraph of the torus

For $L > 2$ and an even subgraph $F$ of the torus graph $T_L$, the port pattern assigns to each dart $d = (p, \mu)$ the bit $1$ if the edge $\{p, p + s(\mu)\}$ belongs to $F$ and $0$ otherwise; this assignment is invariant under reversal of darts and sums to $0$ at each site because $F$ is even.
