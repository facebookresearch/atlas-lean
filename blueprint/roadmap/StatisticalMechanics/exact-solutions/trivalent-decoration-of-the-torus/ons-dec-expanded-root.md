# First dart of the expanded dart list of a decorated cycle

For $L > 2$ and a closed walk $q$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ at $d$, the root is the first entry of the [expanded dart list](ons-dec-expanded-dart-list.md) of $q$, or $(\iota(d), 0)$ if that list is empty; here $\iota(p, \mu) = (8\bar p_1 + o_1(\mu),\ 8\bar p_2 + o_2(\mu)) \in T_{8L}$ is the [embedded position](ons-dec-embed-vertex.md) of a dart, with $\bar p_i \in \{0, \dots, L - 1\}$ the standard representatives and $o(\mu)$ the [offset](ons-dec-embed-offset.md).
