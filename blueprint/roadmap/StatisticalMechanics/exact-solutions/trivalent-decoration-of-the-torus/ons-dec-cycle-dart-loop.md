# Closed dart walk of a rooted decorated cycle

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a closed walk $q$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ at a dart $d$, the dart loop is the [dart list](ons-dec-cycle-dart-list.md) of $q$ viewed as a sequence indexed by $\{0, \dots, \ell - 1\}$, $\ell$ its length.
