# First-return loop of a rooted decorated cycle

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a closed walk $q$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ at a dart $d$, the first-return list is $(d, x_m, x_{m-1}, \dots, x_2, d)$, where $(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_m)$ are the starting darts of the external steps of $q$ in order (the [external darts](ons-dec-walk-external-darts.md)), with the first one dropped and the rest reversed.
