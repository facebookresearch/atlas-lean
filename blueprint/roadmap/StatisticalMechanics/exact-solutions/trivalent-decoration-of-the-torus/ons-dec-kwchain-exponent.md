# Transition exponent sum along a dart chain

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a starting dart $d_0$ and a list $(d_1, \dots, d_m)$ of darts, the chain exponent is $\sum_{k=1}^{m} \varepsilon(d_k, d_{k-1})$ ($0$ for the empty list), where $\varepsilon$ is the [transition exponent](../spin-kac-ward-identities/ons-dec-transition-exponent.md).
