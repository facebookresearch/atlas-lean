# Port-pattern bit of the decorated edge between two darts

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a port pattern $P$ and darts $d = (p, \mu)$, $e = (p', \nu)$, the bit is $P(d)$ if $e = \bar d$; otherwise, if $p = p'$, it is the $i$-th [chain bit](ons-port-chain-bit.md) of $P$ at $p$ for $\{\mu, \nu\} = \{i, i + 1\}$, and $0$ for other pairs of directions; and it is $0$ if $p \ne p'$. Here a [port pattern](ons-port-pattern.md) $P$ assigns a bit $P(d) \in \mathbb{Z}/2$ to each dart with $P(\bar d) = P(d)$ and $\sum_{\mu} P(p, \mu) = 0$ at every site $p$.
