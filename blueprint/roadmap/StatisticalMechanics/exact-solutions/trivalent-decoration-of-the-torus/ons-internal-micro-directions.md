# Micro directions along internal decorated edges at a site

For a list $(a_0, a_1, \dots, a_k)$ of directions, the micro direction list is the concatenation over consecutive pairs $(a_j, a_{j+1})$ of $(a_{j+1}, a_{j+1}, a_j + 2, a_j + 2)$ (directions mod $4$); it is empty for lists of length at most $1$.
