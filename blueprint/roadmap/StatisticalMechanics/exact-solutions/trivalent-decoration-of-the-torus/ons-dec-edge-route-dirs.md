# Step directions of the route of a decorated edge

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and darts $d = (p, \mu)$, $e = (p', \nu)$, the route directions $(r_0, r_1, r_2, r_3)$ are $(\mu, \mu, \mu, \mu)$ if $e = \bar d$; otherwise they are $(1, 1, 2, 2)$, $(0, 0, 3, 3)$, $(2, 2, 3, 3)$, $(1, 1, 0, 0)$, $(3, 3, 0, 0)$, $(2, 2, 1, 1)$ for $(\mu, \nu) = (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 2), (2, 1), (2, 3), (3, 2)$ respectively, and $(0, 0, 0, 0)$ in all other cases.
