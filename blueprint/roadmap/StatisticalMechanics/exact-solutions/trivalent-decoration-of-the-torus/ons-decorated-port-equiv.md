# Equivalence between even decorated subgraphs and port patterns

For $L > 2$, the map sending an even subgraph $D$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ to its [port pattern](ons-port-pattern-of-decorated.md) is a bijection onto the port patterns, with inverse $P \mapsto$ the [decorated edge set](ons-decorated-edges.md) of $P$.
