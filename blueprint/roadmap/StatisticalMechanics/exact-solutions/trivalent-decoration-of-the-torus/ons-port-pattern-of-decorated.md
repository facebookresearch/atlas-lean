# Port pattern of an even decorated subgraph

For $L > 2$ and an even subgraph $D$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, the port pattern assigns to each dart $d$ the [port bit](ons-decorated-port-bit.md) $1$ if $\{d, \bar d\} \in D$ and $0$ otherwise; it is reversal invariant, and its four bits sum to $0$ at each site because $D$ is even.
