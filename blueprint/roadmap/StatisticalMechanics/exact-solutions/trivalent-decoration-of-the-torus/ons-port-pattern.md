# Port pattern on the darts of the torus

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a port pattern is a function $P$ from the darts of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ to $\mathbb{Z}/2$ such that $P(\bar d) = P(d)$ for every dart $d$ and $P(p, 0) + P(p, 1) + P(p, 2) + P(p, 3) = 0$ at every site $p$; here $\bar d = (p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$ is the reverse of the dart $d = (p, \mu)$.
