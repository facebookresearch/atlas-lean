# Column weights of the balanced augmented matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and $t, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$, the column weight is $\eta(\{d, \bar d\})$ at a dart $d$ and $t\lambda$ at each side state, where $\bar d = (p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$ is the reverse of the dart $d = (p, \mu)$.
