# Parity condition of the decoration at one site

For port bits $b_0, b_1, b_2, b_3 \in \mathbb{Z}/2$ and chain bits $y_0, y_1, y_2 \in \mathbb{Z}/2$, the parity condition is

$$b_0 + y_0 = 0, \quad b_1 + y_0 + y_1 = 0, \quad b_2 + y_1 + y_2 = 0, \quad b_3 + y_2 = 0,$$

the evenness of the four darts of a site on the path $0 - 1 - 2 - 3$.
