# Decorated walk of a Kac-Ward transition

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and darts $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, $d_2 = (p_2, \mu_2)$ with $p_2 = p_1 + s(\mu_1)$, the transition walk in the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ from $d_1$ to $d_2$ is the external step from $d_1$ to $\bar d_1 = (p_2, \mu_1 + 2)$ followed by the [chain path](ons-dec-port-chain-path.md) at $p_2$ from direction $\mu_1 + 2$ to direction $\mu_2$.
