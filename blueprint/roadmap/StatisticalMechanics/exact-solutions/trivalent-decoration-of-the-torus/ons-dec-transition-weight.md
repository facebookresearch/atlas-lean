# Decorated weight of a Kac-Ward transition

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and darts $d_2 = (p_2, \mu_2)$, $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, the transition weight is

$$\eta(\{d_1, \bar d_1\}) \prod_{i \in I(\mu_1 + 2,\ \mu_2)} \eta(c_{p_2, i}),$$

where $\bar d = (p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$ is the reverse of the dart $d = (p, \mu)$, $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$, and $I(\alpha, \beta) = \{i \in \{0, 1, 2\} : (i < \alpha) \ne (i < \beta)\}$ is the set of [chain indices between ports](ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md) $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
