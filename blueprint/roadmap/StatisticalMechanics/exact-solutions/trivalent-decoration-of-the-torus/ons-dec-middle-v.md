# Row factor of the rank-two middle-edge term

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, $\omega, u, v \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $p$, the row factor $V$ is the matrix indexed by sides and darts with entry at $(\sigma, d)$, $d = (p', \mu)$, equal to

$$\eta(c_{p,1})\, \pi_{u,v}(\mu)\, \eta(\{d, \bar d\})\, f^{\mathrm{src}}(\mu)\, w_\omega(\mu, \delta(\sigma))$$

if $p = p' + s(\mu)$ and $\sigma(\mu) = \sigma$, and $0$ otherwise; here $f^{\mathrm{src}}$ is the [source factor](ons-dec-middle-source-factor.md) at $p$, $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$, $\sigma(\mu)$ is the [side](ons-dec-middle-side.md) of a direction ($0$ for $\mu \in \{0, 1\}$, $1$ for $\mu \in \{2, 3\}$), $\delta(\sigma) \in \{0, 2\}$ is the [representative direction](ons-dec-middle-dir.md) of a side, $\pi_{u,v}$ is the [direction phase](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-dir-phase.md), and $w_\omega$ is the [turn weight](../kac-ward-matrices/ons-turn-w.md).
