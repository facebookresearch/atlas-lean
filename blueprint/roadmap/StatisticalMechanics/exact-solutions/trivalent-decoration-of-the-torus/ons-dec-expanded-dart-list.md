# Expanded dart list of a decorated walk in the finer torus

For $L > 2$ and a walk of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, the expanded list is the concatenation, over its steps $(d, e)$ in order, of the four [micro darts](ons-dec-edge-micro-darts.md) of $(d, e)$ in the finer torus $T_{8L} = (\mathbb{Z}/8L\mathbb{Z})^2$.
