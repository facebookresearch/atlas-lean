# Equivalence between even subgraphs of the torus and its decoration

For $L > 2$, the decoration bijection between the even subgraphs of the torus graph $T_L$ and the even subgraphs of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ is the composite of the [port-pattern bijection for the torus](ons-even-subgraph-port-equiv.md) with the inverse of the [port-pattern bijection for the decoration](ons-decorated-port-equiv.md).
