# Adjacency of the decorated torus graph

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, two darts $(p, \mu)$ of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ (a site $p$ and a direction $\mu \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ with unit step $s(\mu)$: $s(0) = (1, 0)$, $s(1) = (0, 1)$, $s(2) = (-1, 0)$, $s(3) = (0, -1)$) $d, e$ are adjacent in the decorated graph when $e = \bar d$ (an external edge, where $\bar d = (p + s(\mu), \mu + 2)$ is the reverse of the dart $d = (p, \mu)$) or when they are [internally adjacent](ons-dec-internal-adj.md): they share their site and their directions differ by $1$ as integers in $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$.
