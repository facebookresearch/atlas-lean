# Reversal-invariant internal factor of the augmented matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$ and a site $p$, the internal factor on augmented states is: for darts $d_2 = (p_2, \mu_2)$, $d_1 = (p_1, \mu_1)$, $\prod_{i \in I(\mu_1 + 2, \mu_2)} \eta'(c_{p_2, i})$ if $p_2 = p_1 + s(\mu_1)$ and $0$ otherwise; for a dart $d_2$ and a side $\sigma$, the [destination factor](ons-dec-middle-dest-factor.md) of $\mu_2$ if $p_2 = p$ and $\sigma(\mu_2) = \sigma$, else $0$; for a side $\sigma$ and a dart $d_1$, the [source factor](ons-dec-middle-source-factor.md) of $\mu_1$ if $p = p_1 + s(\mu_1)$ and $\sigma(\mu_1) = \sigma$, else $0$; and $0$ between side states. Here $\eta'$ is $\eta$ with the weight of the middle chain edge $c_{p,1}$ multiplied by $0$, $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$, $I(\alpha, \beta) = \{i \in \{0, 1, 2\} : (i < \alpha) \ne (i < \beta)\}$ is the set of [chain indices between ports](ons-dec-chain-path-indices.md) $\alpha$ and $\beta$, and $\sigma(\mu)$ is the [side](ons-dec-middle-side.md) of a direction ($0$ for $\mu \in \{0, 1\}$, $1$ for $\mu \in \{2, 3\}$).
