# Closed decorated walk of a valid Kac-Ward loop

Let $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$, and $(d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ darts with $d_k$ starting where $d_{k+1}$ ends for every $k$ (indices mod $n$). The loop walk is the closed walk of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$ at $d_0$ obtained by traversing the darts in walking order $d_0, d_{n-1}, \dots, d_1, d_0$ and joining each consecutive pair by its [transition walk](ons-dec-transition-walk.md) (the [chain walk](ons-dec-walk-of-kwchain.md) of this list).
