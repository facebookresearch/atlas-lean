# Row weights of the augmented Kac-Ward matrix

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a decorated weight $\eta$, $t \in \mathbb{C}$ and a site $p$, the row weight is $1$ at every dart and $t\,\eta(c_{p,1})$ at each side state, where $c_{p,i} = \{(p, i), (p, i + 1)\}$, $i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, is the [chain edge](ons-dec-chain-edge.md) at $p$.
