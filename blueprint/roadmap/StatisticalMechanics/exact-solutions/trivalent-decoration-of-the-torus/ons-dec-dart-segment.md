# Route points of a decorated edge after its start

For $L > 2$ and an oriented edge $(d, e)$ of the [decorated graph](ons-dec-graph.md) $\mathcal{D}_L$, the segment is the list of points of the finer torus $T_{8L} = (\mathbb{Z}/8L\mathbb{Z})^2$ visited by the [four-step route](ons-dec-edge-route.md) from $\iota(d)$ to $\iota(e)$, with the starting point omitted.
