# Position of a dart in the $8L \times 8L$ torus

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a dart $(p, \mu)$ of $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$, the position is $\iota(p, \mu) = (8\bar p_1 + o_1(\mu),\ 8\bar p_2 + o_2(\mu))$ reduced in $(\mathbb{Z}/8L\mathbb{Z})^2$, where $\bar p_i \in \{0, \dots, L - 1\}$ is the standard representative of $p_i$ ($|p_i|$ when $L = 0$) and $o(\mu)$ is the [offset](ons-dec-embed-offset.md).
