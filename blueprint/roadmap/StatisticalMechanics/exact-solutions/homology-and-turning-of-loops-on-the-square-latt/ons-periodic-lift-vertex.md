# Points of the periodic lift of a torus loop

Let $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$, $v$ a sequence of $n$ darts of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), $m_x, m_y, r \in \mathbb{Z}$, and $i \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$. The lift point is $\mathrm{pos}_d(i) + (L r m_x, L r m_y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, where $d$ is the [lift direction sequence](ons-lift-dir.md) of $v$ and $\mathrm{pos}_d(i) = \sum_{j < i} s(d_j)$ is the position after the first $i$ steps of the direction sequence $d$.
