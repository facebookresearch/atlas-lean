# Mod 2 intersection number of two torus edge sets

Let $L \ge 1$ and let $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ be the discrete torus. For finite sets $F, G$ of unordered pairs of sites, the intersection number is

$$F \cdot G = \sum_{p \in T_L} \big(h_F(p)\, v_G(p - (0, 1)) + v_F(p)\, h_G(p - (1, 0))\big) \in \mathbb{Z}/2,$$

where $h_F(p)$ and $v_F(p)$ are the bits in $\mathbb{Z}/2$ indicating whether the horizontal edge $\{p, p + (1, 0)\}$, resp. the vertical edge $\{p, p + (0, 1)\}$, belongs to $F$.
