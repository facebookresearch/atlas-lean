# Sector weight of the trivial homology class

For $L > 2$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $Z_{00}(x)$ is the [sector weight](ons-sector-weight.md) of the class $(0, 0)$: the sum of $x^{|F|}$ over the even subgraphs of the nearest-neighbour graph of $T_L$ (sets $F$ of edges in which every site has even degree) of homology class $(0, 0)$.
