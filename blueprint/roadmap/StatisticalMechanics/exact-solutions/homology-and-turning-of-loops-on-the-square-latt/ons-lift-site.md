# Sites of a closed torus walk in lift order

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $v$ of $n$ darts of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the lift sites are $p_{-k}$ for $k = 0, \dots, n - 1$, where $v_j = (p_j, \mu_j)$ and indices are taken mod $n$ (so the order is $p_0, p_{n-1}, p_{n-2}, \dots, p_1$).
