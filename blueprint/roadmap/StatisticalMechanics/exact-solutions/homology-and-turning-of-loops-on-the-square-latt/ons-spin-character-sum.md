# Spin-character sum over even subgraphs of the torus

For $L > 2$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$, the spin-character sum is

$$\sum_{F \text{ even}} \chi_{a,b}([F])\, x^{|F|},$$

the sum over the even subgraphs of the nearest-neighbour graph of $T_L$ (sets $F$ of edges in which every site has even degree), where $[F] \in (\mathbb{Z}/2)^2$ is the [mod 2 homology class](ons-even-homology.md) and $\chi_{a,b}$ is the [spin-structure character](ons-spin-character.md).
