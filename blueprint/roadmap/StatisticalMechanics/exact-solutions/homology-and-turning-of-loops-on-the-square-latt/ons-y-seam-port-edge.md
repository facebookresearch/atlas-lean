# Horizontal-seam edge in a given column

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in \mathbb{Z}/L$, the seam edge of column $x$ is the edge of the dart $((x, -1), 1)$, namely $\{(x, -1), (x, 0)\}$, in the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$).
