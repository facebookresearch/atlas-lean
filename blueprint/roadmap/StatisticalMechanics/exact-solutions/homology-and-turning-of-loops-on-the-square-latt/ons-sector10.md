# Sector weight of the homology class $(1, 0)$

For $L > 2$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $Z_{10}(x)$ is the [sector weight](ons-sector-weight.md) of the class $(1, 0)$: the sum of $x^{|F|}$ over the even subgraphs of the nearest-neighbour graph of $T_L$ (sets $F$ of edges in which every site has even degree) of homology class $(1, 0)$.
