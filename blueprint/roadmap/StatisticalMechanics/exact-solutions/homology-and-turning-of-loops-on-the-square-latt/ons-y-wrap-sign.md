# Signed crossing of the horizontal seam by a dart

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a dart $((p_1, p_2), \mu)$ of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the sign is $+1$ if $\mu = 1$ and $p_2 = -1$, $-1$ if $\mu = 3$ and $p_2 = 0$ (and the first case does not apply), and $0$ otherwise.
