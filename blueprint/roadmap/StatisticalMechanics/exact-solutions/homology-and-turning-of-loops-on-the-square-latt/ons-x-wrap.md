# Crossing of the vertical seam by a dart

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a dart $((p_1, p_2), \mu)$ of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$) crosses the vertical seam when $\mu = 0$ and $p_1 = -1$, or $\mu = 2$ and $p_1 = 0$.
