# Prefix sum of a function on $\mathbb{Z}/L$

For $L \ge 1$, a commutative monoid $A$, a function $f : \mathbb{Z}/L \to A$ and $z \in \mathbb{Z}/L$, the prefix sum is $\sum_{j=1}^{\bar z} f(j)$, where $\bar z \in \{0, \dots, L - 1\}$ is the standard representative of $z$ (the empty sum, $0$, when $z = 0$).
