# Lattice point of a period state

For $n \ge 1$, a direction sequence $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ and a state $(r, i)$ with $r \in \mathbb{N}$ and $i \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the lattice point is $\mathrm{pos}_d(i) + r\, D$, where $\mathrm{pos}_d(i) = \sum_{j < i} s(d_j)$ is the position after the first $i$ steps of the direction sequence $d$ and $D = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} s(d_j)$ is the [displacement](ons-path-displacement.md) of one period.
