# Vertical-seam edge in a given row

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and $y \in \mathbb{Z}/L$, the seam edge of row $y$ is the edge of the dart $((-1, y), 0)$, namely $\{(-1, y), (0, y)\}$, in the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$).
