# Mod 2 homology class of an edge set

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the class is $[F] = (a, b) \in (\mathbb{Z}/2)^2$, where $a$ is the parity of the number of edges of $F$ of the form $\{(0, y), (-1, y)\}$ (crossing the vertical seam) and $b$ is the parity of the number of edges of the form $\{(x, 0), (x, -1)\}$ (crossing the horizontal seam).
