# Sector weight of a homology class

For $L > 2$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $h \in (\mathbb{Z}/2)^2$, the sector weight is

$$Z_h(x) = \sum_{F \text{ even},\ [F] = h} x^{|F|},$$

the sum over the even subgraphs of the nearest-neighbour graph of $T_L$ (sets $F$ of edges in which every site has even degree) whose [mod 2 homology class](ons-even-homology.md) is $h$.
