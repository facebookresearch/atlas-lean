# Directions along a periodic arc

For $n, N \in \mathbb{N}$, a direction sequence $d = (d_0, \dots, d_{n-1})$ and indices $i_{\min}, i_{\max} \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the arc directions are the list of $d_i$ over the states $(r, i)$ of the [periodic arc](ons-periodic-arc-states.md), in order, with the last state omitted.
