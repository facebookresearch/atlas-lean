# Vertical edge bit as an element of $\mathbb{Z}/2$

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), and a site $p$, the value is the [vertical bit](ons-vertical-bit.md) $v_F(p)$ viewed as an element of the field $\mathbb{Z}/2$.
