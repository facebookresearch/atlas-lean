# Signed boundary coefficient along a torus loop

Let $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$, $P : T_L \to \mathbb{Z}/2$ a function on the sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), and $v$ a sequence of $n$ darts. For $k \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the coefficient is the [outgoing boundary coefficient](ons-outgoing-boundary-coeff.md) of $P$ at the $k$-th [lift site](ons-lift-site.md) in the $k$-th [lift direction](ons-lift-dir.md).
