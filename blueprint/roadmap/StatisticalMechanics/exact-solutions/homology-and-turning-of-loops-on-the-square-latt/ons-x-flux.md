# Horizontal flux of an edge set through a column

Let $L \ge 1$ and let $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ be the discrete torus. For a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites and $x \in \mathbb{Z}/L$, the flux is $\sum_{y \in \mathbb{Z}/L} h_F(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}/2$, the parity of the number of horizontal edges $\{(x, y), (x + 1, y)\}$ in $F$; here $h_F(p)$ and $v_F(p)$ are the bits in $\mathbb{Z}/2$ indicating whether the horizontal edge $\{p, p + (1, 0)\}$, resp. the vertical edge $\{p, p + (0, 1)\}$, belongs to $F$.
