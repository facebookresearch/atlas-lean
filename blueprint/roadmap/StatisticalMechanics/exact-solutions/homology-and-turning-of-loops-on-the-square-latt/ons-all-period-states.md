# States of the first $N + 1$ periods of a periodic walk

For $n, N \in \mathbb{N}$, the list of period states is

$$(0, 0), (0, 1), \dots, (0, n - 1),\ (1, 0), \dots, (1, n - 1),\ \dots,\ (N, 0), \dots, (N, n - 1),$$

the concatenation of the [one-period lists](ons-period-state-list.md) for $r = 0, 1, \dots, N$. It consists of $N + 1$ complete periods and has length $(N + 1)n$; for $n = 0$ it is the empty list.
