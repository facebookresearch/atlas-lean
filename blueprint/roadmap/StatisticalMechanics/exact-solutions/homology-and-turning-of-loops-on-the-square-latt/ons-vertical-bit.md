# Vertical edge bit of an edge set at a site

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), and a site $p$, the vertical bit $v_F(p) \in \mathbb{Z}/2$ is $1$ if the edge $\{p, p + (0, 1)\}$ belongs to $F$ and $0$ otherwise.
