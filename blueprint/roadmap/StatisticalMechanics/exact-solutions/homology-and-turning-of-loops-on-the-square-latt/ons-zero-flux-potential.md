# Zero-flux face potential of a torus edge set

Let $L \ge 1$ and let $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ be the discrete torus. For a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites and a site $p = (p_1, p_2)$, the potential is

$$\sum_{j=1}^{\bar p_2} A(0, j) + \sum_{i=1}^{\bar p_1} B(i, p_2) \in \mathbb{Z}/2,$$

where $A$ and $B$ are the [horizontal](ons-zero-flux-horizontal.md) and [vertical parts](ons-zero-flux-vertical.md) of the construction and $\bar a \in \{0, \dots, L - 1\}$ denotes the standard representative of $a \in \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$.
