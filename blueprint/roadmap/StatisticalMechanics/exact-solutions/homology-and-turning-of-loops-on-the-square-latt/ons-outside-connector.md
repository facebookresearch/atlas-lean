# Outside connector around the bounding box of a periodic arc

For $d_x, d_y, t \in \mathbb{Z}$, write $a^+ = \max(a, 0)$. The outside connector is the direction list consisting of one step $0$, then $(t - d_y)^+$ steps $1$, then $(d_x + 2)^+$ steps $2$, then $t^+$ steps $3$, and finally one step $0$, where directions are labelled $0, 1, 2, 3$ with unit steps $s(0) = (1, 0)$, $s(1) = (0, 1)$, $s(2) = (-1, 0)$, $s(3) = (0, -1)$.
