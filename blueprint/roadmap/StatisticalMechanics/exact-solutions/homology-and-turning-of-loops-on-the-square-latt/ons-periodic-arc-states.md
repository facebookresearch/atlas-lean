# States of a periodic arc across $N$ periods

For $n, N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $i_{\min}, i_{\max} \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the arc states are obtained from the [list of the first $N + 1$ periods](ons-all-period-states.md) by deleting its first $i_{\min}$ entries and keeping the next $Nn + i_{\max} + 1 - i_{\min}$ entries (natural subtraction). When $Nn + i_{\max} \ge i_{\min}$ this is the run of states from $(0, i_{\min})$ to $(N, i_{\max})$.
