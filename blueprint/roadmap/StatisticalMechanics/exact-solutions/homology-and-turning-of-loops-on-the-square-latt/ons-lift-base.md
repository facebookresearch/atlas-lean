# Base point in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ over the start of a torus loop

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $v$ of $n$ darts of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the base point is $(\bar a, \bar b) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, where $(a, b)$ is the site of the dart $v_0$ (the first dart in [lift order](ons-lift-site.md)) and $\bar a \in \{0, \dots, L - 1\}$ denotes the standard representative of $a \in \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$ (the absolute value $|a|$ when $L = 0$).
