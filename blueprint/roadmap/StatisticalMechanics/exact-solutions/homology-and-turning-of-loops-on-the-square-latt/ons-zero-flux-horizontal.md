# Horizontal part of the zero-flux potential construction

Let $L \ge 1$ and let $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ be the discrete torus. For a finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites and a site $p = (p_1, p_2)$, the value is $h_F(p) + \mathbf{1}[p_2 = 0]\, \Phi_x(-1) \in \mathbb{Z}/2$, where $\Phi_x(-1)$ is the [horizontal flux](ons-x-flux.md) of $F$ through column $-1$ and $h_F(p)$ and $v_F(p)$ are the bits in $\mathbb{Z}/2$ indicating whether the horizontal edge $\{p, p + (1, 0)\}$, resp. the vertical edge $\{p, p + (0, 1)\}$, belongs to $F$.
