# Direction sequence of the lift of a closed torus walk

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$ and a sequence $v$ of $n$ darts of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), the lift direction sequence is $d_k = \mu_{-k}$ for $k = 0, \dots, n - 1$, where $v_j = (p_j, \mu_j)$ and indices are taken mod $n$ (so $d_0 = \mu_0$, $d_1 = \mu_{n-1}$, and so on).
