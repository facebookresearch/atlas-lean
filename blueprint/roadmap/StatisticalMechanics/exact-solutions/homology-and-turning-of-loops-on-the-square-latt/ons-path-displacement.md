# Total displacement of a list of lattice directions

For a list $(d_0, \dots, d_{m-1})$ of directions, the displacement is $\sum_{i} s(d_i) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ ($0$ for the empty list), where directions are labelled $0, 1, 2, 3$ with unit steps $s(0) = (1, 0)$, $s(1) = (0, 1)$, $s(2) = (-1, 0)$, $s(3) = (0, -1)$.
