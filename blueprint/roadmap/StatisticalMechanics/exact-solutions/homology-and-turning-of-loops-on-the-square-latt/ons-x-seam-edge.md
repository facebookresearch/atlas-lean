# Edges crossing the vertical seam of the torus

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, an unordered pair $e$ of sites of the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$) crosses the vertical seam when $e = \{(0, y), (-1, y)\}$ for some $y \in \mathbb{Z}/L$.
