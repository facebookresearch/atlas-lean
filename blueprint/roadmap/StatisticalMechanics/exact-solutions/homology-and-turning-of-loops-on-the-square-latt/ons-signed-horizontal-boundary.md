# Signed potential difference across a horizontal edge

For $L \in \mathbb{N}$, $P : T_L \to \mathbb{Z}/2$ on the discrete torus $T_L = (\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ ($L \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$), and a site $p = (p_1, p_2)$, the value is $\beta_h(p) = \overline{P(p)} - \overline{P(p_1, p_2 - 1)} \in \{-1, 0, 1\}$, where for $P : T_L \to \mathbb{Z}/2$ write $\overline{P(p)} \in \{0, 1\}$ for its value as an integer.
