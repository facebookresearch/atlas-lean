# Boltzmann weight with inhomogeneous couplings and site fields

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ allowed), $J$ real couplings on the pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. The Boltzmann weight of a spin configuration $\sigma$ is

$$w(\sigma) = \exp\Bigl(\sum_{e \in E} J_e \sigma_e + \sum_{x \in V} h_x \sigma_x\Bigr),$$

with $\sigma_e = \sigma_x \sigma_y$ for $e = \{x, y\}$.
