# Edge-removal weight with hyperbolic tangent factors

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ allowed), $J$ real couplings on the pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. For a finite set $S$ of pairs, the edge-removal weight is

$$\rho(S) = \zeta(S) \prod_{e \in S} \tanh J_e = \frac{Z_{E \setminus S}}{Z_E} \prod_{e \in S} \sinh J_e,$$

with $\zeta$ the [edge-removal ratio](ghy-zeta.md) and $Z_F$ the [partition function](zj.md) with pair set
$F$.
