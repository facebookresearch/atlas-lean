# Monotonicity of correlations

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, let $h \ge 0$ and $A \subseteq V$. Then $\beta \mapsto \langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, h}$ is
nondecreasing on $[0, \infty)$, where $\langle \cdot \rangle_{\beta, h}$ is the
[finite-volume Ising expectation](ising-expectation.md) and $\sigma_A = \prod_{x \in A} \sigma_x$ is the [spin product](spin-prod.md): this is a consequence of the
second Griffiths inequality (Griffiths, J. Math. Phys. 8 (1967)).
