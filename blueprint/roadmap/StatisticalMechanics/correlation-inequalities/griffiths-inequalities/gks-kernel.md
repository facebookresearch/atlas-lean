# Nonnegativity of the GKS positivity kernel

Let $V$ be a finite set, $I$ a finite index set, $c_i \ge 0$ and $d_i \ge 0$ for $i \in I$, and let
$f_i$ ($i \in I$) and $g$ be [spin monomials](is-spin-monomial.md). Then

$$\sum_{s} g(s) \prod_{i \in I} \bigl(c_i + d_i\, f_i(s)\bigr) \ge 0,$$

the sum over all spin configurations $s$ on $V$.
