# Edge-removal ratio of partition functions

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ allowed), $J$ real couplings on the pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. For a finite set $S$ of pairs, the edge-removal ratio is

$$\zeta(S) = \frac{Z_{E \setminus S}}{Z_E} \prod_{e \in S} \cosh J_e,$$

with $Z_F$ the [partition function](zj.md) with pair set $F$ (the same couplings and fields).
