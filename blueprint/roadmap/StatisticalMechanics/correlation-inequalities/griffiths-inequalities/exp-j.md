# Gibbs expectation with inhomogeneous couplings and site fields

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ allowed), $J$ real couplings on the pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. The Gibbs expectation of a function $f$ of the spin configurations is

$$\langle f \rangle_{E, J, h} = \frac{\sum_\sigma f(\sigma)\, w(\sigma)}{Z},$$

with $w$ the [Boltzmann weight](w-j.md) and $Z = \sum_\sigma w(\sigma)$ the
[partition function](zj.md).
