# First Griffiths inequality (GKS I)

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, let $\beta \ge 0$, $h \ge 0$ and $A \subseteq V$. Then $\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{\beta, h} \ge 0$,
where $\sigma_A = \prod_{x \in A} \sigma_x$ is the [spin product](spin-prod.md) and $\langle \cdot \rangle_{\beta, h}$ is the
[finite-volume Ising expectation](ising-expectation.md) (R. B. Griffiths, *Correlations in Ising ferromagnets. I*, J. Math. Phys. 8 (1967); D. G. Kelly and S. Sherman, *General Griffiths' inequalities on correlations in Ising ferromagnets*, J. Math. Phys. 9 (1968)).
