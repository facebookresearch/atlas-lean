# Griffiths monotonicity of correlations in the edge set

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E_1 \subseteq E_2$ finite sets of unordered pairs of elements of $V$, $J$ real
couplings with $J_e \ge 0$ for every $e \in E_2$, and $h_x \ge 0$ for every $x \in V$, and suppose no pair
of $E_2 \setminus E_1$ is a loop $\{x, x\}$. Then for every $A \subseteq V$,
$\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{E_1, J, h} \le \langle \sigma_A \rangle_{E_2, J, h}$, with
$\langle \cdot \rangle_{E, J, h}$ the [Gibbs expectation](exp-j.md): adding pairs with nonnegative couplings
increases every correlation (R. B. Griffiths, "Correlations in Ising ferromagnets I", J. Math. Phys. 8
(1967) 478–483).
