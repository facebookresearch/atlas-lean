# Finite-volume Ising expectation

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The finite-volume Ising expectation of a function $f$ of the
spin configurations is $\langle f \rangle_{\beta, h} = \sum_s P(s) f(s)$, with $P(s) = e^{-\beta H(s)}/Z$
the [Gibbs probability](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-prob.md).
