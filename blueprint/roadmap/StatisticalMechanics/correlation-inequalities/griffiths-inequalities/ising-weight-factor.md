# Boltzmann weight as a product of cosh-plus-sinh factors

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For every spin configuration $s$, the Boltzmann weight
factorizes as

$$e^{-\beta H(s)} = \prod_{\{x, y\} \in E} \bigl(\cosh \beta + \sigma_x \sigma_y \sinh \beta\bigr) \prod_{x \in V} \bigl(\cosh(\beta h) + \sigma_x \sinh(\beta h)\bigr).$$
