# Griffiths' second inequality for inhomogeneous couplings

Let $V$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ (loops $\{x, x\}$ allowed), $J$ real couplings on the pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, with $J_e \ge 0$ for every $e \in E$ and $h_x \ge 0$ for every $x$. For all
$A, B \subseteq V$, the [Gibbs expectation](exp-j.md) $\langle \cdot \rangle_{E, J, h}$ with weights $\exp\bigl(\sum_{e \in E} J_e \sigma_e + \sum_x h_x \sigma_x\bigr)$, $\sigma_{\{x,y\}} = \sigma_x \sigma_y$ satisfies

$$\langle \sigma_A \rangle_{E,J,h} \langle \sigma_B \rangle_{E,J,h} \le \langle \sigma_{A \triangle B} \rangle_{E,J,h}$$

(R. B. Griffiths, "Correlations in Ising ferromagnets I", J. Math. Phys. 8 (1967) 478–483;
J. Ginibre, "General formulation of Griffiths' inequalities", Comm. Math. Phys. 16 (1970) 310–328).
