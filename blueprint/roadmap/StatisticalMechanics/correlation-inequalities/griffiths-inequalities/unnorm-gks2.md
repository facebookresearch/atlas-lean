# Unnormalized second Griffiths inequality

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, let $\beta \ge 0$, $h \ge 0$ and $A, B \subseteq V$, and let $w(s) = e^{-\beta H(s)}$ be the
[Boltzmann weight](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-weight.md).
Then

$$\Bigl(\sum_s \sigma_A(s)\, w(s)\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_s \sigma_B(s)\, w(s)\Bigr) \le \Bigl(\sum_s \sigma_{A \triangle B}(s)\, w(s)\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_s w(s)\Bigr),$$

that is, $Z^2 \bigl(\langle \sigma_{A \triangle B} \rangle - \langle \sigma_A \rangle \langle \sigma_B \rangle\bigr) \ge 0$.
