# FKG inequality

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, let $\beta \ge 0$ and $h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $f$ and $g$ be increasing functions of the
spin configurations (for the coordinatewise order with plus above minus). Then, under the Ising Gibbs
probability $P(s) = e^{-\beta H(s)}/Z$,

$$\Bigl(\sum_s P(s) f(s)\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_s P(s) g(s)\Bigr) \le \sum_s P(s) f(s) g(s)$$

(C. M. Fortuin, P. W. Kasteleyn and J. Ginibre, *Correlation inequalities on some partially ordered
sets*, Comm. Math. Phys. 22 (1971)).
