# Inhomogeneous ghost-vertex couplings

Let $V$ be a finite set, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs of $V$ and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site
fields, and let $g$ be an added ghost vertex. The ghost-vertex couplings on unordered pairs of
$V \sqcup \{g\}$ are $2K_{\{x, y\}}$ on a pair $\{x, y\}$ of sites, $2h_x$ on a pair $\{x, g\}$, and $0$
on $\{g, g\}$.
