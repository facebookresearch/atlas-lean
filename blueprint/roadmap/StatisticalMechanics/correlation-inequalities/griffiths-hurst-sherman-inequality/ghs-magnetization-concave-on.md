# GHS concavity of the magnetization in the field

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \ge 0$ and $o \in V$. The magnetization
$h \mapsto \langle \sigma_o \rangle_{\beta, h}$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle_{\beta, h}$ the
[finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md), is concave on
$[0, \infty)$ (R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, *Concavity of magnetization of an Ising ferromagnet in a positive external field*, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970) 790–795).
