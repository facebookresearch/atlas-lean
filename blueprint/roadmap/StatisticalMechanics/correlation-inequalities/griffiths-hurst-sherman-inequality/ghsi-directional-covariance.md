# Covariance with the directional spin

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) with pair set $E$, couplings $K$ and fields $h$. For a direction $d \in \mathbb{R}^V$ and a function $f$ of the
configurations, the covariance of $f$ with the [directional spin](ghsi-dir-spin.md)
$D = \sum_x d_x \sigma_x$ is $\langle f D \rangle - \langle f \rangle \langle D \rangle$.
