# Two-point correlation on the disagreement subgraph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $o, x \in V$ and $A \subseteq V$. If
$o \notin A$ and $x \notin A$, this is the zero-field correlation $\langle \prod_{z \in \{o, x\}} \sigma_z \rangle$
under the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on $V$ with the edges of $G$ having both
endpoints in $V \setminus A$ and couplings $2\beta$; if $o \in A$ or $x \in A$, it is $0$.
