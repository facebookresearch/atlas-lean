# Magnetization on the agreement subgraph

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $y \in V$ and $A \subseteq V$. If $y \in A$, this is the expectation of $\sigma_y$
under the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on $V$ with the edges of $G$ having
both endpoints in $A$, couplings $2\beta$ and field $2\beta h$ at every site; if $y \notin A$, it is $0$.
