# Two-replica expectation with inhomogeneous couplings

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $w(a) = \exp\bigl(\sum_{e \in E} K_e \sigma_e(a) + \sum_x h_x \sigma_x(a)\bigr)$ and $Z = \sum_a w(a)$. For a function $F$ of two configurations,
the two-replica expectation is

$$\frac{1}{Z^2} \sum_{a} \sum_{b} w(a)\, w(b)\, F(a, b),$$

the expectation of $F(a, b)$ over two independent replicas of the model.
