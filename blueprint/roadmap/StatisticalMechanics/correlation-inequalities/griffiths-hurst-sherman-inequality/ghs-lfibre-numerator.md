# Fibre weight integrated against an observable

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For a function $F$ of two configurations and a configuration $q$, the fibre numerator is
$\sum_a w(a)\, w(\bar a)\, F(a, \bar a)$, with $w(a) = e^{-\beta H(a)}$ the [Boltzmann weight](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-weight.md) and $\bar a = \mathrm{mate}_q(a)$ the [mate](ghs-lmate.md) of $a$ over $q$.
