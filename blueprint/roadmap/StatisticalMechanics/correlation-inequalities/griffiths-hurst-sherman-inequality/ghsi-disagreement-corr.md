# Inhomogeneous two-point correlation on the disagreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs, $o, x \in V$ and
$A \subseteq V$. If $o \notin A$ and $x \notin A$, this is the zero-field correlation
$\langle \prod_{z \in \{o, x\}} \sigma_z \rangle$ (which is $\langle \sigma_o \rangle$ when $o = x$) under the
[Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on $V$ with the pairs of $E$ with both endpoints in $V \setminus A$,
couplings $2K$ and no field; if $o \in A$ or $x \in A$, it is $0$.
