# Zero-field expectation on the disagreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $q$ be a configuration. For a function $F$ of the configurations,

$$\frac{\sum_a U_q(a)\, F(a)}{\sum_a U_q(a)},$$

the sums over all configurations $a$ on $V$, where $U_q$ is the
[zero-field disagreement-set weight](ghs-luweight.md) with couplings $2\beta$; under this normalized
weight the spins on the agreement set are uniform and independent.
