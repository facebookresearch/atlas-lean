# Spin covariance in the inhomogeneous model

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) with pair set $E$, couplings $K$ and fields $h$. For sites $o, x$, the spin covariance is
$\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle$.
