# Induced-subgraph partition function with doubled coupling and field

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $A \subseteq V$. This is the partition function with the edges of $G$ having both
endpoints in $A$, coupling $2\beta$, field $2\beta h$ at the sites of $A$ and $0$ elsewhere,
summed over all spin configurations of $V$.
