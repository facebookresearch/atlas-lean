# Two-replica Ising expectation

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For a function $F$ of two configurations, the two-replica expectation is
$\sum_a \sum_b P(a) P(b) F(a, b)$, with $P$ the
[Gibbs probability](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-prob.md):
the expectation of $F(a, b)$ over two independent copies $a, b$ of the Ising Gibbs distribution.
