# Inhomogeneous conditional expectation of $t_y$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. For a site $y$ and a configuration $q$, this is the
[conditional two-replica expectation](ghsi-fibre-expectation.md) given $q$ of the rotated variable
$t_y = (\sigma_y(a) + \sigma_y(b))/2$.
