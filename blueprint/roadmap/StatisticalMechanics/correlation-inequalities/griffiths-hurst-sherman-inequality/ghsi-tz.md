# Inhomogeneous induced-subgraph partition function with field

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $A \subseteq V$. This is the partition function with the pairs of $E$ with both endpoints in $A$,
couplings $2K$, field $2h_x$ at the sites $x \in A$ and $0$ elsewhere, summed over all spin configurations of $V$.
