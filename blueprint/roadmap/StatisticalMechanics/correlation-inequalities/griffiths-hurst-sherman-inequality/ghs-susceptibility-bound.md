# Susceptibility bound $\chi(h) \le m(h)/h \le 1/h$ from concavity

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $h > 0$ and $o \in V$, and write
$m(t) = \langle \sigma_o \rangle_{\beta, t}$ for the
[finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md). If $m$ is concave on
$[0, \infty)$, then $m'(h) \le m(h)/h$ and $m'(h) \le 1/h$ (R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, *Concavity of magnetization of an Ising ferromagnet in a positive external field*, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970) 790–795).
