# Concavity from a nonpositive second derivative on $(0, \infty)$

Let $\varphi \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on $[0, \infty)$ and differentiable on
$(0, \infty)$, with $\varphi'$ differentiable on $(0, \infty)$ and $\varphi''(h) \le 0$ for every $h > 0$.
Then $\varphi$ is concave on $[0, \infty)$.
