# Inhomogeneous magnetization on the agreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs, $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$
site fields, $y \in V$ and $A \subseteq V$. If $y \in A$, this is the expectation of $\sigma_y$ under the
[Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on $V$ with the edges of $G$ having both endpoints
in $A$, couplings $2K$ and site fields $2h$ (sites outside $A$ carry no edges); if $y \notin A$, it is
$0$.
