# Field derivative of the two-point covariance

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md) at $(\beta, h)$ and $M = \sum_{z \in V} \sigma_z$ the total spin. For sites $o, x$,
the field derivative of the covariance $\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle$
is the expression

$$\beta\bigl(\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x M \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle \langle M \rangle\bigr) - \beta\bigl(\langle \sigma_o M \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle M \rangle\bigr) \langle \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle\, \beta\bigl(\langle \sigma_x M \rangle - \langle \sigma_x \rangle \langle M \rangle\bigr).$$
