# Zero-field correlation in an induced subgraph

Let $W$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of $W$, $J$ real couplings and
$S, P \subseteq W$. This is the correlation $\langle \sigma_P \rangle$, $\sigma_P = \prod_{z \in P} \sigma_z$,
under the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on $W$ with the pairs of $E$ with both endpoints in $S$,
couplings $J$ and no field.
