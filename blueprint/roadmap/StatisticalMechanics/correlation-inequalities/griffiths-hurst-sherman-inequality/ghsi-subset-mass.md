# Inhomogeneous weight of an agreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $V^+ = V \sqcup \{g\}$ the vertex set with an added ghost vertex $g$, joined to every site in the [ghost graph](../../random-currents-and-sharpness/random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/with-ghost.md), and let $J$ be the [ghost-vertex couplings](ghsi-ghost-coupling.md) of couplings $K$ and
fields $h$. For $A \subseteq V$, the weight of $A$ is the product $Z_{A \cup \{g\}} \cdot Z_{V \setminus A}$,
where $Z_S$ is the zero-field partition function with couplings $J$ on the edges of the ghost graph with
both endpoints in $S$, summed over all spin configurations of $V \sqcup \{g\}$.
