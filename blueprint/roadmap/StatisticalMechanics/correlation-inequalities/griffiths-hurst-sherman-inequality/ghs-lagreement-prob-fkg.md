# FKG lattice condition for the agreement law when $\beta, h \ge 0$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\beta \ge 0$ and $h \ge 0$, and let $\pi$ be the
[law of the agreement configuration](ghs-lagreement-prob.md) of two independent replicas. Then $\pi$
satisfies the FKG lattice condition: $\pi(q)\, \pi(q') \le \pi(q \vee q')\, \pi(q \wedge q')$ for all
configurations $q, q'$, with $\vee$ and $\wedge$ the coordinatewise maximum and minimum (plus above
minus).
