# Three-site backbone factor

Let $V$ and $\iota$ be finite sets and let each index $i \in \iota$ label an edge with endpoints $\mathrm{ends}(i)$, an unordered pair of elements of $V$ (loops allowed); a current is a finite set of edge indices; a current $m$ connects $u$ and $v$ when they are joined by a chain of edges of the current, each step between two distinct endpoints of one edge. For sites $o, x, y$, the three-site backbone factor of
$m$ is

$$1 - [m \text{ connects } x, y] - [m \text{ connects } o, y] - [m \text{ connects } o, x],$$

with $[\cdot] \in \{0, 1\}$ the indicator. Since connection is transitive, it is $1$, $0$ or $-2$
according as $m$ connects none, exactly one or all three of the pairs.
