# GHS gap on a pair equals the third Ursell function

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md) at $(\beta, h)$. For all sites $o, x, y$ (not necessarily adjacent), the
three-point covariance $\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle$
minus the GHS bound
$(\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle) \langle \sigma_y \rangle + (\langle \sigma_o \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle) \langle \sigma_x \rangle$
equals the [third Ursell function](eg-ursell3.md) $u_3(o, x, y)$.
