# GHS sign condition

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $o \in V$. The GHS sign condition at $(\beta, h)$ and root $o$ holds when the
[third Ursell function](eg-ursell3.md) satisfies $u_3(o, x, y) \le 0$ for all sites $x, y$ such that $o$,
$x$ and $y$ are pairwise distinct. It [holds for $\beta, h \ge 0$](ghs-sign-dominance.md).
