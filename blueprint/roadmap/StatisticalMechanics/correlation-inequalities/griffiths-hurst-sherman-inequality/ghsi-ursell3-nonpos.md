# Third Ursell function nonpositive for $o \ne x$, $K, h \ge 0$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, with $K_e \ge 0$ for every pair $e$ and $h_x \ge 0$ for every site. For all sites
$o, x, y$ with $o \ne x$, the [third Ursell function](ghsi-ursell3.md) of the inhomogeneous model satisfies
$u_3(o, x, y) \le 0$.
