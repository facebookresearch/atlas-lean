# Fibre factorization property for $u_o u_x$ and $t_y$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $o, x, y \in V$. The fibre factorization property holds when, for every
configuration $q$, the [conditional two-replica expectations](ghs-lfibre-expectation.md) given the
agreement configuration $q$ satisfy

$$\mathbb{E}[u_o u_x t_y \mid q] = \mathbb{E}[u_o u_x \mid q]\; \mathbb{E}[t_y \mid q],$$

with the rotated variables $u_w = (\sigma_w(a) - \sigma_w(b))/2$ and $t_w = (\sigma_w(a) + \sigma_w(b))/2$:
conditioned on the agreement configuration, $u_o u_x$ and $t_y$ are uncorrelated.
