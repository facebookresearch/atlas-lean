# Conditional expectation of $u_o u_x$ given the agreement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For sites $o, x$ and a configuration $q$, this is the
[conditional two-replica expectation](ghs-lfibre-expectation.md) given $q$ of $u_o u_x$, with the rotated
variables $u_w = (\sigma_w(a) - \sigma_w(b))/2$.
