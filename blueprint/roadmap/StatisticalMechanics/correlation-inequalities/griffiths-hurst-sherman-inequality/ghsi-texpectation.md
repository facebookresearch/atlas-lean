# Inhomogeneous expectation with field on the agreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $q$ be a configuration. For a function $F$ of the configurations,

$$\frac{\sum_a T_q(a)\, F(a)}{\sum_a T_q(a)},$$

the sums over all configurations $a$ on $V$, where $T_q$ is the
[agreement-set weight](ghsi-tweight.md) with couplings $2K$ and fields $2h$; under this normalized weight
the spins off the agreement set are uniform and independent.
