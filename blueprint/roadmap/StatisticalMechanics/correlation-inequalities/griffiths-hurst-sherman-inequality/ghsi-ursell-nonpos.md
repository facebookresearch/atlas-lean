# Griffiths–Hurst–Sherman inequality for inhomogeneous ferromagnets

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, with $K_e \ge 0$ for every pair $e$ and $h_x \ge 0$ for every site, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) with pair set $E$, couplings $K$ and fields $h$.
For all sites $o, x, y$ with $o \ne x$,

$$u_3(o, x, y) = \langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \sigma_y \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle + 2 \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle \le 0$$

(R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, *Concavity of magnetization of an Ising ferromagnet in a positive external field*, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970); J. L. Lebowitz, *GHS and other inequalities*, Comm. Math. Phys. 35 (1974)).
