# Current connecting all three site pairs

Let $V$ and $\iota$ be finite sets and let each index $i \in \iota$ label an edge with endpoints $\mathrm{ends}(i)$, an unordered pair of elements of $V$ (loops allowed); a current is a finite set of edge indices; a current $m$ connects $u$ and $v$ when they are joined by a chain of edges of the current, each step between two distinct endpoints of one edge. For sites $o, x, y \in V$, the current $m$ connects all
three pairs when it connects $x$ and $y$, $o$ and $y$, and $o$ and $x$.
