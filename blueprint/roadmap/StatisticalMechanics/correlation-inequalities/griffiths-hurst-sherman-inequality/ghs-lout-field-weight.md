# Field weight of the disagreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For configurations $q$ and $a$, with $A_q = \{v : q(v) = +\}$ the [agreement set](ghs-lagree-finset.md) of $q$, the field weight of the
disagreement set is $\exp\bigl(2\beta h \sum_{v \notin A_q} \sigma_v(a)\bigr)$.
