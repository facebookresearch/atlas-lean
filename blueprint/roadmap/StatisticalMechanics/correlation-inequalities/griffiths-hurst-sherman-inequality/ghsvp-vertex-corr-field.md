# Correlation with field in an induced subgraph

Let $W$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of $W$, $J$ real couplings, $h \in \mathbb{R}^W$
site fields and $S, P \subseteq W$. This is the correlation $\langle \sigma_P \rangle$,
$\sigma_P = \prod_{z \in P} \sigma_z$, under the [Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) on
$W$ with the pairs of $E$ with both endpoints in $S$, couplings $J$ and fields $h$ (at every site of $W$).
