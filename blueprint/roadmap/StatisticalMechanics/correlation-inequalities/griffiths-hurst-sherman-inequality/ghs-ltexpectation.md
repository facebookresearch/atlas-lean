# Expectation with field on the agreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $q$ be a configuration. For a function $F$ of the configurations,

$$\frac{\sum_a T_q(a)\, F(a)}{\sum_a T_q(a)},$$

the sums over all configurations $a$ on $V$, where $T_q$ is the
[agreement-set weight](ghs-ltweight.md) with couplings $2\beta$ and field $2\beta h$ on the subgraph induced
by the agreement set; under this normalized weight the spins off the agreement set are uniform and
independent.
