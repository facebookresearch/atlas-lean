# Griffiths' second inequality for two distinct spins

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\beta \ge 0$ and $h \ge 0$, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md) at $(\beta, h)$. For distinct sites
$x \ne y$, $\langle \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle \le \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle$.
