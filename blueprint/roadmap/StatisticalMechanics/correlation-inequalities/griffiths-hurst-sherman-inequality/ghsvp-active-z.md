# Induced-subgraph partition function over the active sites only

Let $W$ be a finite set, $E$ a finite set of unordered pairs of $W$, $J$ real couplings and $S \subseteq W$.
This is the zero-field partition function with the pairs of $E$ with both endpoints in $S$ and couplings $J$, summed over the
spin configurations on $S$ only (the spins off $S$ are set to minus; they do not enter the weight).
