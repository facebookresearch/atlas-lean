# Griffiths–Hurst–Sherman inequality for distinct sites

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\beta \ge 0$ and $h \ge 0$, and let $o \in V$. For all sites $x, y$ such that $o$, $x$
and $y$ are pairwise distinct, the [third Ursell function](eg-ursell3.md) $u_3(o, x, y)$ is nonpositive (R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, *Concavity of magnetization of an Ising ferromagnet in a positive external field*, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970) 790–795); proof via J. L.
Lebowitz, "GHS and other inequalities", Comm. Math. Phys. 35 (1974) 87–92.
