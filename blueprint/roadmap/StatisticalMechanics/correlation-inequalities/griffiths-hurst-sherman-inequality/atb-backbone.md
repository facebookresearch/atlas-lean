# Backbone cluster of a site in a current

Let $V$ and $\iota$ be finite sets and let each index $i \in \iota$ label an edge with endpoints $\mathrm{ends}(i)$, an unordered pair of elements of $V$ (loops allowed); a current is a finite set of edge indices; a current $m$ connects $u$ and $v$ when they are joined by a chain of edges of the current, each step between two distinct endpoints of one edge. The cluster of a site $o$ in $m$ is the set of sites
$v \in V$ that $m$ connects to $o$ (including $o$ itself).
