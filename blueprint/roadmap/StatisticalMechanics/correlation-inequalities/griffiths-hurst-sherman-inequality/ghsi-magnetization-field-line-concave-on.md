# GHS concavity along a field line

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, with $K_e \ge 0$ for every pair $e$ and $h_x \ge 0$ for every site, let $d \in \mathbb{R}^V$
be a direction with $d_x \ge 0$ for every $x$, and let $o \in V$ with $d_o = 0$. Then the magnetization
$t \mapsto \langle \sigma_o \rangle_{K, h + t d}$, with $\langle \cdot \rangle_{K, h'}$ the
[Gibbs expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/exp-j.md) with pair set $E$, couplings $K$ and fields $h'$,
is concave on $[0, \infty)$ (R. B. Griffiths, C. A. Hurst and S. Sherman, *Concavity of magnetization of an Ising ferromagnet in a positive external field*, J. Math. Phys. 11 (1970)).
