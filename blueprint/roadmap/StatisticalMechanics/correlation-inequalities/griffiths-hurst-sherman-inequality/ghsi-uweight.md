# Inhomogeneous zero-field disagreement-set weight

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs. For configurations
$q$ and $a$, with $A_q$ the [agreement set](ghs-lagree-finset.md) of $q$, the disagreement-set weight is
$U_q(a) = \exp\bigl(\sum_{e \in E,\ e \subseteq V \setminus A_q} 2K_e\, \sigma_e(a)\bigr)$, the zero-field
Boltzmann weight with couplings $2K$ on the subgraph induced by $V \setminus A_q$.
