# Total two-replica weight of an agreement fibre

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For a configuration $q$, the total two-replica weight of the fibre over $q$ is
$\sum_a w(a)\, w(\bar a)$, the sum over all configurations $a$ of the [fibre weights](ghs-lfibre-weight.md),
with $w(a) = e^{-\beta H(a)}$ the [Boltzmann weight](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-weight.md) and $\bar a = \mathrm{mate}_q(a)$ the [mate](ghs-lmate.md) of $a$ over $q$.
