# Inhomogeneous zero-field expectation on the disagreement set

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $q$ a configuration.
For a function $F$ of the configurations,

$$\frac{\sum_a U_q(a)\, F(a)}{\sum_a U_q(a)},$$

the sums over all configurations $a$ on $V$, where $U_q$ is the
[zero-field disagreement-set weight](ghsi-uweight.md) with couplings $2K$; under this normalized weight the
spins on the agreement set are uniform and independent.
