# Weight of an agreement set in the duplicated system

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $V^+ = V \sqcup \{g\}$ the vertex set with an added ghost vertex $g$, joined to every site in the [ghost graph](../../random-currents-and-sharpness/random-currents-and-the-switching-lemma/with-ghost.md), and let $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For $A \subseteq V$, the weight of $A$ in the
duplicated system is the product $Z_{A \cup \{g\}} \cdot Z_{V \setminus A}$, where $Z_S$ is the zero-field
partition function on the edges of the ghost graph with both endpoints in $S$, with coupling $2\beta$ on
the edges of $G$ and $2\beta h$ on the ghost edges, summed over all spin configurations of $V \sqcup \{g\}$.
