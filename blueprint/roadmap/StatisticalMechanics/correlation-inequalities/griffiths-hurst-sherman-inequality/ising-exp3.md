# Three-replica Ising expectation

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For a function $F$ of three configurations, the three-replica expectation is
$\sum_a \sum_b \sum_c P(a) P(b) P(c) F(a, b, c)$, with $P$ the
[Gibbs probability](../../spin-models/ising-model-on-a-finite-graph/the-finite-volume-ising-gibbs-measure/ising-prob.md):
the expectation of $F(a, b, c)$ over three independent copies of the Ising Gibbs distribution.
