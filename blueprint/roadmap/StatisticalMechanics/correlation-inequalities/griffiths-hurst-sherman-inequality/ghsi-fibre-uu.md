# Inhomogeneous conditional expectation of $u_o u_x$

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields. For sites $o, x$ and a configuration $q$, this is the
[conditional two-replica expectation](ghsi-fibre-expectation.md) given $q$ of $u_o u_x$, with the rotated
variables $u_w = (\sigma_w(a) - \sigma_w(b))/2$.
