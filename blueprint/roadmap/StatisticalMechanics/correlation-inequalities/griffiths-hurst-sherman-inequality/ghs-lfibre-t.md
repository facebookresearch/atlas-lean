# Conditional expectation of $t_y$ given the agreement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For a site $y$ and a configuration $q$, this is the
[conditional two-replica expectation](ghs-lfibre-expectation.md) given $q$ of the rotated variable
$t_y = (\sigma_y(a) + \sigma_y(b))/2$.
