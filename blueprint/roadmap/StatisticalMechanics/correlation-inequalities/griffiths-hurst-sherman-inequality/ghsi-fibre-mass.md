# Inhomogeneous total weight of an agreement fibre

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph, $K$ real couplings on unordered pairs and $h \colon V \to \mathbb{R}$ site fields, and let $w(a) = \exp\bigl(\sum_{e \in E} K_e \sigma_e(a) + \sum_x h_x \sigma_x(a)\bigr)$. For a configuration $q$, the total two-replica weight of the fibre
over $q$ is $\sum_a w(a)\, w(\bar a)$, with $\bar a = \mathrm{mate}_q(a)$ the [mate](ghs-lmate.md) of $a$ over $q$.
