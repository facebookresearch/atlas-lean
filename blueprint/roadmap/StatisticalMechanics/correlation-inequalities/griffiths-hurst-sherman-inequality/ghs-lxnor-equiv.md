# Change of variables from replica pairs to agreement and first copy

For a finite set $V$, the map $(a, b) \mapsto (q, a)$, with $q$ the
[agreement configuration](ghs-lagreement.md) of $a$ and $b$, is a bijection of the pairs of spin
configurations on $V$; its inverse is $(q, a) \mapsto (a, \mathrm{mate}_q(a))$, with the
[mate](ghs-lmate.md) of $a$ over $q$.
