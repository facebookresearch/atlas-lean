# Lebowitz four-point combination

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite simple graph and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $\langle \cdot \rangle$ be the [finite-volume Ising expectation](../griffiths-inequalities/ising-expectation.md) at $(\beta, h)$. For sites $o, x, y$, Lebowitz's combination is

$$\langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \sigma_y \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle - \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \sigma_y \rangle,$$

which differs from the [third Ursell function](eg-ursell3.md) $u_3(o, x, y)$ by $2 \langle \sigma_o \rangle \langle \sigma_x \rangle \langle \sigma_y \rangle$.
