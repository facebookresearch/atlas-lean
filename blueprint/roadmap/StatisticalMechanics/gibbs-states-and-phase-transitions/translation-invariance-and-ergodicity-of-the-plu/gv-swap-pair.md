# Involution swapping inner-set parts of configuration pairs

For finite sets $S_{\mathrm{in}} \subseteq S_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, this map sends a pair
$(\sigma, \tau)$ of spins $\sigma$ on $S_{\mathrm{out}}$ and $\tau$ on $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ to the pair made of $\sigma$
[overwritten](gv-ovr.md) by $\tau$ on $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and of the [restriction](gv-res.md) of $\sigma$ to
$S_{\mathrm{in}}$: it exchanges the $S_{\mathrm{in}}$ parts, and is an involution.
