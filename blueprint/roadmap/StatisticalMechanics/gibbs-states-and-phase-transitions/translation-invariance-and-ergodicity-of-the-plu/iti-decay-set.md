# Continuous functions with decaying shifted integrals

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\varphi \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, this is
the linear subspace of the continuous functions $f$ on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ such that
$\int f \, d\mu^+_{\varphi(n)} - \int f \circ \theta_g \, d\mu^+_{\varphi(n)} \to 0$, with $\mu^+_n$ the
[plus-boundary box measures](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-measure.md) $\mu^+_n$ and $\theta_g$ the shift by $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$.
