# Shifted minus-boundary box measure

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is the image of the
[minus-boundary box measure](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/minus-measure.md) in $\Lambda_n$ under the shift $\theta_g$,
$(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$.
