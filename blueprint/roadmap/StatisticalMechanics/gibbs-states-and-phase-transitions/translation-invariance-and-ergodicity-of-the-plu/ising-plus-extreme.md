# Extremality of the plus state

For $d \ge 1$ and $\beta, h \ge 0$, the [plus state](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-state.md) $\mu^+$ is an extreme point of
the translation-invariant probability measures: it is a translation-invariant probability measure, and
whenever $\mu^+ = a \nu_1 + b \nu_2$ with $\nu_1, \nu_2$ translation-invariant probability measures,
$a, b > 0$ and $a + b = 1$, then $\nu_1 = \nu_2$ (H.-O. Georgii, *Gibbs Measures and Phase Transitions*,
2nd edition, de Gruyter (2011), Chapter 14).
