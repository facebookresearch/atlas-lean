# Difference between an integral and its shifted version

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a sequence $(\mu_n)$ of probability measures on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$,
$g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and a bounded continuous function $f$, this is the sequence
$n \mapsto \int f \, d\mu_n - \int f \circ \theta_g \, d\mu_n$, with $\theta_g$ the shift by $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$.
