# Gibbs probability on a set with a boundary condition

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration $\eta$, a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ and
spins $\tau$ on $S$, the Gibbs probability of $\tau$ is $w_\eta(\tau)/Z_\eta$, the
[Boltzmann weight](gv-weight.md) divided by the [partition function](gv-z.md).
