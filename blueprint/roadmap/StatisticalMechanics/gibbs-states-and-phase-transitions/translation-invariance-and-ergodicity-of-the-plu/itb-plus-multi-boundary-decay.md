# Asymptotic shift homogeneity of all-plus events in plus boxes

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\varphi \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, this
condition holds when for every finite set $T \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$,
$\mu^+_{\varphi(n)}(O_T) - \mu^+_{\varphi(n)}(O_{T - g}) \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$, with $\mu^+_n$ the
[plus-boundary box measures](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-measure.md) $\mu^+_n$, $O_T = \{\omega : \omega_x = + \text{ for all } x \in T\}$ and $T - g = \{x - g : x \in T\}$.
