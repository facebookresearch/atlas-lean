# Decay of shifted cylinder integrals for minus box measures

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\varphi \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, this
condition holds when for every [cylinder function](pstc-cylinder-bcf-set.md) $f$,
$\int f \, d\mu^-_{\varphi(n)} - \int f \circ \theta_g \, d\mu^-_{\varphi(n)} \to 0$, with $\mu^-_n$ the
minus-boundary box measures ([definition](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/minus-measure.md)) and $\theta_g$ the shift by $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$.
