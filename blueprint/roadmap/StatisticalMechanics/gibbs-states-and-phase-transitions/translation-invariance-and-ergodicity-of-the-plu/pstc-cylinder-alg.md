# Subalgebra of cylinder functions

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, this is the subalgebra of the continuous real functions on the configurations of
$\mathbb{Z}^d$ generated by the [indicators](pstc-coord-cm.md) $\omega \mapsto \mathbf 1_{\omega_x = +}$,
$x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$: the polynomials in finitely many of them (the cylinder functions).
