# Translation invariance of the plus state

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \ge 0$, the [plus state](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-state.md) of the Ising model on
$\mathbb{Z}^d$ is invariant under every lattice shift $\theta_g$, $(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$ (S. Friedli
and Y. Velenik, *Statistical Mechanics of Lattice Systems*, Cambridge University Press (2017),
Chapter 3).
