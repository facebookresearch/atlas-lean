# Ergodicity of the plus state

For $d \ge 1$ and $\beta, h \ge 0$, the [plus state](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-state.md) of the Ising model on
$\mathbb{Z}^d$ is ergodic under lattice translations: it is translation invariant, and every measurable event
$s$ with $\theta_g^{-1}(s) = s$ for every $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ has probability $0$ or $1$, where $\theta_g$ is the shift by $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$, $(\theta_g \omega)_x = \omega_{x - g}$
(H.-O. Georgii, *Gibbs Measures and Phase Transitions*, 2nd edition, de Gruyter (2011), Chapter 14).
