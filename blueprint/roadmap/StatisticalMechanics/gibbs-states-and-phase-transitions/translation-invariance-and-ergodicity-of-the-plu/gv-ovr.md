# Overwriting a configuration on an inner set

For finite sets $S_{\mathrm{in}} \subseteq S_{\mathrm{out}} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, spins $\sigma$ on $S_{\mathrm{out}}$
and spins $\tau$ on $S_{\mathrm{in}}$, this is the spin assignment on $S_{\mathrm{out}}$ equal to $\tau$ on
$S_{\mathrm{in}}$ and to $\sigma$ elsewhere.
