# Energy on a set with a boundary condition

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration $\eta$, a finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, $h \in \mathbb{R}$ and spins
$\tau$ on $S$, the energy is

$$-\sum_{\{x, y\}} \sigma_x \sigma_y - h \sum_{x \in S} \sigma_x,$$

the first sum over the [bonds touching](gv-bond-touch.md) $S$, evaluated at the [glued configuration](gv-glue.md) $\eta\tau$ (equal to $\tau$ on $S$ and to $\eta$ off $S$).
