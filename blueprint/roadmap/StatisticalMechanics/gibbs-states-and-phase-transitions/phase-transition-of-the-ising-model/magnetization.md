# Spontaneous magnetization of the Ising model

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the spontaneous magnetization is
$m^*(\beta) = \int \sigma_0 \, d\mu^+_\beta$, the expected spin at the origin under the zero-field
[plus state](../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-state.md) $\mu^+_\beta$ (a fixed subsequential weak limit of the
plus-boundary box measures).
