# Sup-based $\beta_c$ equals $-\tfrac{1}{2}\log(1 - p_c)$ for $d \ge 2$

For $d \ge 2$, with $m^*$ the [spontaneous magnetization](magnetization.md),
$\sup\{\beta > 0 : m^*(\beta) = 0\} = -\frac12 \log(1 - p_c(d, 2))$, where $p_c(d, 2)$ is
the [critical point](../../random-cluster-model/critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-pc.md) of the random-cluster model with $q = 2$ (the supremum of a set that is empty or unbounded
above being $0$).
