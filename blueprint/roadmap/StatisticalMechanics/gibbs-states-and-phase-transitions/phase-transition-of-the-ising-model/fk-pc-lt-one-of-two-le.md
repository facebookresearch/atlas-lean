# FK critical point is less than one for $d \ge 2$, $q \ge 1$

For $d \ge 2$ and $q \ge 1$, the [critical point](../../random-cluster-model/critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/the-critical-point-of-the-random-cluster-model/fk-pc.md) $p_c(d, q)$ of the random-cluster model on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ satisfies $p_c(d, q) < 1$ (G. Grimmett, *The
Random-Cluster Model*, Springer (2006), Theorem 5.5).
