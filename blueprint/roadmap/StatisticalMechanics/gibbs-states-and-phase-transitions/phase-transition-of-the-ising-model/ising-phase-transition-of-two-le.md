# Existence of the Ising phase transition

For $d \ge 2$, there is $\beta_c > 0$ such that the [spontaneous magnetization](magnetization.md) $m^*(\beta)$ vanishes for $0 < \beta < \beta_c$ and is
positive for $\beta > \beta_c$; nothing is asserted at $\beta = \beta_c$ (R. Peierls, *On Ising's model of
ferromagnetism*, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 32 (1936); R. B. Griffiths, Phys. Rev. 136 (1964);
H. Duminil-Copin, *Lectures on the Ising and Potts models on the hypercubic lattice*, 2017).
