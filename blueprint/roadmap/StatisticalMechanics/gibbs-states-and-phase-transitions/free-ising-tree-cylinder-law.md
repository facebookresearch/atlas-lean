# The free Ising cylinder law on a regular tree

The vertices are finite words over $d$ letters without consecutive repeats; join a word to each allowed one-letter extension, giving the empty-word root but using the full automorphism group. At parameter $\theta=\tanh\beta$, the cylinder probability of prescribed spins $\eta$ on a nonempty finite connected set is $\tfrac12\prod_{\{v,w\}}(1+\theta\eta_v\eta_w)/2$, the product over induced edges. For a factor of IID use the product of uniform $[0,1]$ vertex laws and a measurable label-to-spin map. Equivariance is almost everywhere for each fixed automorphism, with no shared exceptional set across the automorphism group.
