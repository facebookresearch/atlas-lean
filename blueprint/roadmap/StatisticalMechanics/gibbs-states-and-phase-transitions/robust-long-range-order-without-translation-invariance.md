# Long-range order for continuous-symmetry spin models without translation invariance

This node records a research direction: prove low-temperature long-range order for three-dimensional spin models with continuous symmetry when the couplings are not exactly translation invariant.

## What would settle it

Fix a spin model (for example a classical Heisenberg model on ℤ³), a class of small inhomogeneous ferromagnetic perturbations of the couplings, an order parameter and the thermodynamic limit, and prove long-range order at low temperature uniformly over that class.

## Status

The cited source poses a closely related question (1999); this page does not certify its present status. The status evidence for this direction is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Lieb poses this as Problem C: the known proofs of long-range order for models with continuous symmetry use infrared bounds, which require strict translation invariance, while the Peierls argument for discrete symmetry is robust and needs only lower bounds on the exchange energies; he asks for a robust proof for the continuous-symmetry cases.

This page records a research direction and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
