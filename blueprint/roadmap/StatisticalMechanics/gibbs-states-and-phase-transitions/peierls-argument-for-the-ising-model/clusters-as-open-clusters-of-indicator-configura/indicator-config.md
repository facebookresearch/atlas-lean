# Bond configuration open on the pairs inside a set

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a finite set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is the bond configuration on the
unordered pairs of sites that is open exactly on the pairs $\{x, y\}$ with $x \in K$ and $y \in K$
(whether or not they are nearest neighbours, loops included).
