# Splicing a closed walk into another at a shared vertex

For a simple graph $G$, a closed walk $p$ based at $u$ that visits a vertex $w$, and a closed walk $c$ based
at $w$, the spliced walk follows $p$ from $u$ up to its first visit to $w$, then traverses $c$, then
follows the rest of $p$ back to $u$.
