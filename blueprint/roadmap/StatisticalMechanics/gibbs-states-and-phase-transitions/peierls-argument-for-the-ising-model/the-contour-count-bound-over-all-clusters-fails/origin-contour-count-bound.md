# Peierls series bound on the origin-cluster contour sum

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this condition holds when the sum of $e^{-2\beta \ell(K)}$ over
the subsets $K$ of $\Lambda_n$ containing the origin, with $\ell(K)$ the number of bonds touching $\Lambda_n$
with exactly one endpoint in $K$, is at most the [Peierls series](../the-peierls-argument-contour-sums-and-the-long-r/peierls-bound.md) (taken to be $0$ when it diverges).
