# Frame of faces around a rectangle

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner $f \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. For $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the frame of the rectangle $[0, m] \times [0, n]$ is the set of
faces with lower-left corners $(a, -1)$ and $(a, n)$ for $-1 \le a \le m$, and $(-1, b)$ and $(m, b)$ for
$-1 \le b \le n$: the faces just outside the rectangle, which support its contour.
