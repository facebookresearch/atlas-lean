# Filled minus cluster of the origin as a finite set

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a spin assignment $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ such that the configuration $\sigma_\tau$ equal to $\tau$ in $\Lambda_n$ and plus outside is minus at
the origin, this is the [filled](spin-compatible-fill.md) [minus cluster](../contours-of-minus-clusters/minus-cluster.md)
of the origin in $\sigma_\tau$; it is finite.
