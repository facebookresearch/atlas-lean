# Filled-cluster winding hypothesis

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, this condition holds when
for every spin assignment $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n$ such that the configuration $\sigma_\tau$ equal to $\tau$ in $\Lambda_n$ and plus outside is minus at the origin, there is a
[connected cluster](../counting-connected-contours/is-connected-cluster.md) $K \subseteq \Lambda_n$ such that the [contour event](../contours-of-minus-clusters/contour-event.md) of $K$ for $B_n$ holds for $\sigma_\tau$, and there are a finite set $T$ of faces containing every face
incident to an edge of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$), on which that graph is
[connected](../euler-circuits-and-single-dual-circuits-for-plan/face-boundary-connected.md), and an integer
$j$ with $0 \le j < \ell(K)$ and $(j, 0) \in T$, where $\ell(K)$ is the contour length of $K$ for $B_n$.
