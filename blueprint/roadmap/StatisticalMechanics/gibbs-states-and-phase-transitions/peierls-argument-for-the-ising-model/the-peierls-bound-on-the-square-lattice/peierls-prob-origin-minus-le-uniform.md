# Peierls bound

There is $\beta_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for every $\beta \ge \beta_0$, twice the [Peierls series](../the-peierls-argument-contour-sums-and-the-long-r/peierls-bound.md)
on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $2 \sum_{\ell} \ell\, 4^\ell e^{-2\beta \ell}$, is less than $1/2$, and for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$
the [probability that the origin is minus](../the-peierls-argument-contour-sums-and-the-long-r/prob-origin-minus.md)
under the zero-field [finite-volume Gibbs measure](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/fv-measure.md) in $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ with plus boundary condition and the bonds touching
$\Lambda_n$ is at most that quantity, uniformly in $n$.
