# Peierls bound in every box when $4 e^{-2\beta} < 1$

Let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ with [Peierls ratio](../the-peierls-argument-contour-sums-and-the-long-r/peierls-ratio.md)
$4 e^{-2\beta} < 1$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the
[probability that the origin is minus](../the-peierls-argument-contour-sums-and-the-long-r/prob-origin-minus.md)
under the zero-field [finite-volume Gibbs measure](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/fv-measure.md) in
$\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ with plus boundary condition and the bonds touching $\Lambda_n$ is at most
[twice the Peierls series](../outer-contours-on-the-square-lattice/two-peierls-bound.md)
$2 \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, 4^\ell e^{-2\beta \ell}$, a series that converges as $4 e^{-2\beta} < 1$. The [uniform Peierls bound](peierls-prob-origin-minus-le-uniform.md)
gives the same bound for every $\beta$ beyond some $\beta_0$, together with its being less than $1/2$.
