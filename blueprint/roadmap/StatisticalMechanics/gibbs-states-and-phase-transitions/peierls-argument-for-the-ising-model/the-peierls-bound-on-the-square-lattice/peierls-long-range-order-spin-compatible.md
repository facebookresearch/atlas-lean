# Plus and minus box measures on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ differ for large $\beta$

There is $\beta_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $\beta \ge \beta_0$, the zero-field
[plus-boundary](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/plus-measure.md) and
[minus-boundary](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/minus-measure.md) Ising measures in
$\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ are different. The [uniform Peierls bound](peierls-prob-origin-minus-le-uniform.md) is the
quantitative form.
