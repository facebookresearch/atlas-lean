# Cycles of length less than $M$ based near the origin

For $M \in \mathbb{N}$, this is the finite family of the simple cycles $\gamma$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ of length $\ell < M$
based at a site of the anchor set $A_\ell = \{(j, 0), (j, -1) : 0 \le j < \ell\}$, each recorded with its
length and base point.
