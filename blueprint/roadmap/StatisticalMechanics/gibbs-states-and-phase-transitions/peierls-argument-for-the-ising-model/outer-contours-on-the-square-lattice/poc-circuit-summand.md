# Contour-event probability of the interior of a closed walk

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, an [interior assignment](interior-assignment.md) $I$ and a closed walk
$\gamma$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, this is the [probability of the contour event](../contours-of-minus-clusters/prob-contour.md)
of $I(\gamma)$ under the zero-field [finite-volume Gibbs measure](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/fv-measure.md)
in $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ with plus boundary condition and the bonds touching $\Lambda_n$: the term of
$\gamma$ in the circuit sum.
