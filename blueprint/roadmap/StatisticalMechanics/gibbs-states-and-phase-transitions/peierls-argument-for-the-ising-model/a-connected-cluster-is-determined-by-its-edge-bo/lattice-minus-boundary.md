# Hypercubic lattice with the edge boundary removed

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, this is the graph on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose edges are the
nearest-neighbour edges that do not [straddle](edge-straddles.md) $K$: the hypercubic lattice with the
[edge boundary](boundary-edge-set.md) of $K$ removed.
