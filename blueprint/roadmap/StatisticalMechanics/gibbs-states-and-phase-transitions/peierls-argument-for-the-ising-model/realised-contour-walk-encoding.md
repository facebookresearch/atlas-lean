# Closed-walk encoding of realised contours of length $\ell$

For $d, n, \ell \in \mathbb{N}$, this condition holds when there are a set of at most $\ell$ anchor sites, a map
$\mathrm{enc}$ from finite sets of bonds to closed nearest-neighbour walks of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and a map
$\mathrm{dec}$ from closed walks to finite sets of bonds such that every
[realised contour](injectivity-of-contours-and-the-reduction-to-cou/realised-contours.md) $F$ of length
$\ell$ in $\Lambda_n$ is sent by $\mathrm{enc}$ to a walk of length $\ell$ based at an anchor, with
$\mathrm{dec}(\mathrm{enc}(F)) = F$.
