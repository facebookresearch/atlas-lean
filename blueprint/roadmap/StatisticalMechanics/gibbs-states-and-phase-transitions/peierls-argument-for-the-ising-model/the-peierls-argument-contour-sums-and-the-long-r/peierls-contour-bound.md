# Peierls bound on the probability that the origin is minus

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this condition holds when the
[probability that the origin is minus](prob-origin-minus.md) under the zero-field [finite-volume Gibbs measure](../../dlr-states-of-the-ising-model/fv-measure.md) in
