# Two faces flanking a primal edge

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner $f \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. For sites $p, q \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the flanking faces are: if $p_0 = q_0$, the faces with
lower-left corners $(p_0 - 1, m)$ and $(p_0, m)$, $m = \min(p_1, q_1)$; otherwise the faces with lower-left
corners $(a, m - 1)$ and $(a, m)$, $a = \min(p_0, q_0)$, $m = \min(p_1, q_1)$. For a nearest-neighbour edge
$\{p, q\}$ these are the two faces on either side of it.
