# Two faces flanking an unordered pair of sites

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner $f \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. For an unordered pair $\{p, q\}$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, this is the unordered pair of
[faces flanking](flank-faces.md) $p$ and $q$; the definition does not depend on the order of $p$ and $q$.
