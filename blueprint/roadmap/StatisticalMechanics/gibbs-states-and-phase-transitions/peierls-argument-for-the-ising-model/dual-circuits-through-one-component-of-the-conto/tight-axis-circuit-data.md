# Tight axis circuit data: contour edges in one axis-face component

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For $n, \ell \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, this condition holds
when every [realised contour](../injectivity-of-contours-and-the-reduction-to-cou/realised-contours.md)
$F$ of length $\ell$ is the contour of some set $K \subseteq \Lambda_n$ (the bonds of $B_n$ with exactly one
endpoint in $K$) with contour length $\ell(K)$ (the number of bonds of $B_n$ with exactly one endpoint in $K$) equal to $\ell$ for which there are a finite set $T$ of faces containing every face incident to an edge of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$) and an integer $j$ with $0 \le j < \ell(K)$ and $(j, 0) \in T$ such that every edge of the face-boundary graph lies in the connected component of the face $(j, 0)$.
