# Component of a face in the face-boundary graph

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For a set $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and a face $f$, this is the graph on all faces whose
edges are the edges $\{a, b\}$ of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$) with $a$ and $b$ joined to $f$ by paths in that graph:
the connected component of $f$, as a graph on all faces.
