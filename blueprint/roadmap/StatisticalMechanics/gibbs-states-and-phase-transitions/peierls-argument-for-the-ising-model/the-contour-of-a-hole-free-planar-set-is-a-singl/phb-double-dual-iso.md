# Shift isomorphism: lattice minus barrier edges to face region graph

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, the shift $x \mapsto x - (1, 1)$ is a graph isomorphism from
the nearest-neighbour graph of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with the edges having exactly one endpoint in $K$ removed, onto
the face-region graph of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$): the graph in which two adjacent elements $f, g$ of
$\mathbb{Z}^2$ are joined unless the primal edge they share is an edge of that face-boundary graph.
