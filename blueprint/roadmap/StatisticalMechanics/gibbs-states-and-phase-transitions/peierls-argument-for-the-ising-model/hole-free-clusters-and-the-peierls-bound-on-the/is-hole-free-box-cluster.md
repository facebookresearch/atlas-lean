# Hole-free connected cluster through the origin

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, a finite set
$K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ is a hole-free cluster when it is a
[connected cluster](../counting-connected-contours/is-connected-cluster.md) through the origin and, with
$\ell(K)$ its contour length for $B_n$, there are a finite set $T$ of faces containing every face incident to an edge of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$) and an integer $j$ with $0 \le j < \ell(K)$ and $(j, 0) \in T$ such that every edge of the face-boundary graph lies in the connected component of the face $(j, 0)$.
