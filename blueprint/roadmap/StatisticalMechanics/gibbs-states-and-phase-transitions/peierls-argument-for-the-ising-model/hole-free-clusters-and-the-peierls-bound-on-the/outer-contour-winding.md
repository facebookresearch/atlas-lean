# Winding hypothesis for outer contours on $\mathbb{Z}^2$

The faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ are the unit squares, each labelled by its lower-left corner. For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, this condition holds when
for every spin assignment $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n$ such that the configuration $\sigma_\tau$ equal to $\tau$ in $\Lambda_n$ and plus outside is minus at the origin, there is a
[connected cluster](../counting-connected-contours/is-connected-cluster.md) $K \subseteq \Lambda_n$ such that the [contour event](../contours-of-minus-clusters/contour-event.md) of $K$ for $B_n$ holds for $\sigma_\tau$ and, with $\ell(K)$ the contour length of $K$ for $B_n$,
there are a finite set $T$ of faces containing every face incident to an edge of the face-boundary graph of $K$ (adjacent faces joined when the primal edge between them has exactly one endpoint in $K$) and an integer $j$ with $0 \le j < \ell(K)$ and $(j, 0) \in T$ such that every edge of the face-boundary graph lies in the connected component of the face $(j, 0)$.
