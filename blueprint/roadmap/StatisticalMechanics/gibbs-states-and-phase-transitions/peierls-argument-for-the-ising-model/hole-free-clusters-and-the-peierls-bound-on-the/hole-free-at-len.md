# Hole-free clusters with contour length $\ell$

For $n, \ell \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^2$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, this is the set of
[hole-free clusters](is-hole-free-box-cluster.md) $K \subseteq \Lambda_n$ whose contour length $\ell(K)$ (the number of bonds of $B_n$ with exactly one endpoint in $K$) equals
$\ell$.
