# Peierls series bound on the connected-cluster contour sum

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, this condition holds when the sum of $e^{-2\beta \ell(K)}$ over
the [connected clusters](conn-cluster-family.md) $K$ in $\Lambda_n$, with $\ell(K)$ the
[contour length](../contours-of-minus-clusters/contour-len.md) of $K$ for the bonds touching $\Lambda_n$,
is at most the Peierls series $\sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell (2d)^\ell e^{-2\beta \ell}$ (taken to be $0$ when it diverges).
