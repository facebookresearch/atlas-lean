# Contours of length $\ell$ realised by connected clusters in a box

For $d, n, \ell \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\Lambda_n = \{-n, \dots, n\}^d$ and $B_n$ the nearest-neighbour bonds with at
least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$, the realised contours of length $\ell$ are the sets of bonds of $B_n$
with exactly one endpoint in $K$, for the
[connected clusters](../counting-connected-contours/is-connected-cluster.md) $K \subseteq \Lambda_n$ with
exactly $\ell$ such bonds.
