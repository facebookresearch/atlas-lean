# Involution swapping inner-box parts of configuration pairs

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $n \le m$, this map sends a pair $(\sigma, \tau)$ of spins $\sigma$ on $\Lambda_m$
and $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n$ to the pair made of $\sigma$ [overwritten](ovr-box.md) by $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n$ and of
the [restriction](res-box.md) of $\sigma$ to $\Lambda_n$: it exchanges the $\Lambda_n$ parts of the two, and is an
involution.
