# Bonds touching the box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the bonds touching the box $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ are the unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$
of nearest neighbours of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with at least one endpoint in $\Lambda_n$; they define the plus and
minus boundary conditions.
