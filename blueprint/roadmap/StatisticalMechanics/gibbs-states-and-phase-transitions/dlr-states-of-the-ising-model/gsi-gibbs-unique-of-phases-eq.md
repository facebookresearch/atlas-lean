# Uniqueness of the Gibbs state

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \ge 0$, and suppose the [plus state](plus-state.md) and the
[minus state](minus-state.md) are equal. Then every probability measure $\mu$ on the configurations of
$\mathbb{Z}^d$ that is a [DLR state](is-dlrstate.md) equals the minus state (hence also the plus state) (S. Friedli and
Y. Velenik, *Statistical Mechanics of Lattice Systems*, Cambridge University Press (2017), Chapter 3).
