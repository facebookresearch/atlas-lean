# Stochastic domination implies domination on increasing clopen events

Let $E$ be a countable set and $\mu, \nu$ measures on $\{+, -\}^E$ with $\nu$ finite. If
$\mu(A) \le \nu(A)$ for every measurable increasing event $A$, then $\mu$ is
[dominated by $\nu$ on increasing clopen events](stochastically-dominated-clopen.md).
