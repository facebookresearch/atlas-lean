# Chosen subsequential weak limit of a sequence of measures

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a sequence $(\rho_n)$ of probability measures on the spin configurations $\omega \in \{+, -\}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$ (with the product topology and its Borel σ-algebra), this is one
fixed choice of an [infinite-volume state](infinite-volume-state.md) of the sequence, that is, of a weak
limit of some subsequence; such limits [exist](exists-infinite-volume-state.md).
