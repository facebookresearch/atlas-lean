# Increasing clopen events

For a set $E$, these are the events $A \subseteq \{+, -\}^E$ that are both closed and open in the product
topology and increasing (if $\omega \in A$ and $\omega \le \omega'$ coordinatewise, with plus above minus,
then $\omega' \in A$). They are closed under finite intersections, and for countable $E$ they generate
the Borel σ-algebra.
