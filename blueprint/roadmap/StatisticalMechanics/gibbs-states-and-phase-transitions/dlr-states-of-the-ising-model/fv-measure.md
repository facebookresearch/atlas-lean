# Finite-volume Gibbs measure on configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\eta$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, $B$ a finite set of unordered pairs of
sites and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The finite-volume Gibbs measure in $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ with boundary
condition $\eta$ and bond set $B$ is the measure on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$

$$\sum_{\tau} P_\eta(\tau)\, \delta_{\eta \tau},$$

the sum over all spin assignments $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n$, where $P_\eta(\tau)$ is the
[finite-volume Gibbs probability](fv-prob.md) and $\eta\tau$ the [glued configuration](glue.md).
