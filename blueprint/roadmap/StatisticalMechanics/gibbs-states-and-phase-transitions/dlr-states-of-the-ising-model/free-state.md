# Infinite-volume free state as a subsequential weak limit

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, the free state is the [chosen subsequential weak limit](limit-state.md) of the
[free-boundary box measures](free-measure.md) in the boxes $\Lambda_n$: a probability
measure on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ to which some subsequence converges weakly.
