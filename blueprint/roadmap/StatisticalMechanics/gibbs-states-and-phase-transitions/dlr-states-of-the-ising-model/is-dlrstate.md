# DLR state of the Ising model on $\mathbb{Z}^d$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. A finite measure $\mu$ on the spin configurations $\omega \in \{+, -\}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$ (with the product topology and its Borel σ-algebra) is a DLR
state when, for every $n$ and $\mu$-almost every $\eta$, the conditional distribution of $\mu$ given the
spins outside $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ (a regular version, with respect to the σ-algebra generated by the spins
outside $\Lambda_n$), evaluated at $\eta$, equals the [finite-volume Gibbs measure](fv-measure.md) in $\Lambda_n$ with boundary condition
$\eta$ and the [bonds touching](bond-finset-touch.md) $\Lambda_n$.
