# Bonds inside the box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, the internal bonds of the box $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ are the unordered pairs
$\{x, y\}$ of nearest neighbours of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with both $x$ and $y$ in $\Lambda_n$; they define the
free boundary condition.
