# Free-boundary Ising measure in a box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, the free-boundary Ising measure in $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ is
the [finite-volume Gibbs measure](fv-measure.md) with boundary condition all minus and the [internal bonds](bond-finset-internal.md) of
$\Lambda_n$ only: a probability measure on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that are minus outside
$\Lambda_n$, whose spins in $\Lambda_n$ do not interact with the outside.
