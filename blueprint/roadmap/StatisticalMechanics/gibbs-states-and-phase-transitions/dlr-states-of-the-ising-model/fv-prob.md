# Finite-volume Gibbs probability with a boundary condition

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\eta$ a configuration of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, $B$ a finite set of unordered pairs of
sites and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. For spins $\tau$ on $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$, the finite-volume Gibbs probability
of $\tau$ with boundary condition $\eta$ is $w_\eta(\tau)/Z_\eta$, the
[Boltzmann weight](fv-weight.md) divided by the [partition function](fv-z.md).
