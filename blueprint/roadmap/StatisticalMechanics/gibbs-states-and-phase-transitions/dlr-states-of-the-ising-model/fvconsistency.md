# Consistency of the plus measures under the specification

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, the consistency condition holds when for all
$n \le m$ and every bounded continuous function $f$ on the configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$,

$$\int \Bigl(\int f\, d\gamma^\eta_n\Bigr)\, d\mu^+_m(\eta) = \int f\, d\mu^+_m,$$

where $\mu^+_m$ is the [plus-boundary measure](plus-measure.md) in $\Lambda_m$ and $\gamma^\eta_n$ is
the [finite-volume Gibbs measure](fv-measure.md) in $\Lambda_n$ with boundary condition $\eta$ and the [bonds touching](bond-finset-touch.md)
$\Lambda_n$: the plus measures in $\Lambda_m$ are invariant under the specification in every
$\Lambda_n \subseteq \Lambda_m$.
