# Infinite-volume state of a sequence of measures

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a probability measure $\mu$ on the spin configurations $\omega \in \{+, -\}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$ (with the product topology and its Borel σ-algebra) is an infinite-volume state of a
sequence $(\rho_n)$ of probability measures when some subsequence $(\rho_{\varphi(k)})$, $\varphi$ strictly
increasing, converges weakly to $\mu$.
