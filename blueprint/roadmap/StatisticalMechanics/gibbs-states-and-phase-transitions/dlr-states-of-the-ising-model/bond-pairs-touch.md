# Ordered nearest-neighbour pairs touching the box

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, these are the ordered pairs $(x, y)$ of nearest neighbours of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with at
least one of $x, y$ in the box $\Lambda_n = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d : |x_i| \le n \text{ for all } i\}$ (the other then lies in $\Lambda_{n+1}$).
