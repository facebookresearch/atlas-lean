# Variance of a linear combination as a quadratic form

Let $R_i$, $i$ in a finite index set, be square-integrable real random variables on a finite measure space with covariance matrix $\Sigma$, and $w$ a real weight vector. Then

$$\operatorname{Var}\Big(\sum_i w_iR_i\Big) = w^\top\Sigma w.$$

No positivity or normalization of $w$ is assumed.
