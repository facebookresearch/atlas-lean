# Cell Mass mul cell Mean

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$, $G : \alpha \to C$, $Y : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ and $c \in C$. With the cell mass $m_p(c) = \sum_{x : G(x) = c} p(x)$ and cell mean $E[Y \mid G = c]$ (equal to $0$ when $m_p(c) = 0$),

$$m_p(c)\, E[Y \mid G = c] = \sum_{x \in \alpha,\ G(x) = c} p(x)\, Y(x).$$

On a cell of mass $0$ both sides vanish, since nonnegativity forces $p = 0$ on that cell.
