# Tower property for every version of the conditional mean

Let $\alpha$ and $C$ be finite sets, $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$, $G : \alpha \to C$ and $Y : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, and write $m_p(c) = \sum_{x : G(x) = c} p(x)$ for the cell masses and $E[Y \mid G = c]$ for the cell means. If $m : C \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $m(c) = E[Y \mid G = c]$ for every $c$ with $m_p(c) \ne 0$, and is arbitrary on cells of mass $0$, then

$$\sum_{x \in \alpha} p(x)\, m(G(x)) = \sum_{x \in \alpha} p(x)\, Y(x).$$
