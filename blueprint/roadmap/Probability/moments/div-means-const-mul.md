# Ratios of means under a common scaling

Let $X,Y$ be real-valued functions on a measure space with measure $\mu$. For any real $a\ne 0$,

$$
\frac{\int aX\,d\mu}{\int aY\,d\mu}=\frac{\int X\,d\mu}{\int Y\,d\mu}.
$$

These are Bochner integrals, taken as zero for nonintegrable functions. If $\int Y\,d\mu=0$, both ratios are zero by the convention $x/0=0$.
