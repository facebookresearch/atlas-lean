# Covariance under a finite probability mass function

For a probability mass function $q$ on a finite type with measurable singletons and real functions $X, Y$, the covariance of $X$ and $Y$ under the measure of $q$ equals $\sum_a q(a)(X(a) - \bar X)(Y(a) - \bar Y)$, where $\bar X = \sum_a q(a) X(a)$ and $\bar Y = \sum_a q(a) Y(a)$.
