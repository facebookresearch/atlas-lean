# Expectation under a finite weight

A weight on a finite type $\alpha$ is a function $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$; it is a finite
distribution when $p \ge 0$ and $\sum_x p(x) = 1$. The expectation of $X : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$
under $p$ is the finite sum $\sum_x p(x) X(x)$, the dot product of $p$ and $X$; for a finite
distribution it is the mean of $X$.
