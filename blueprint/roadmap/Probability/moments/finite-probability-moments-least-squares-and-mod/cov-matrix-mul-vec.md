# Covariance under a linear map

Let $\alpha$ and $n$ be finite sets, $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ any weight, $X : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}^n$ and $A$ a real $m \times n$ matrix. The weighted covariance matrix of the transformed vector $A X$ is $A \operatorname{Cov}_p(X) A^{\mathsf T}$. The weight need not be nonnegative or sum to one.
