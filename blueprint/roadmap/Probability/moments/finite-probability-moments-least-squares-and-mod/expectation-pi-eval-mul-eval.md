# Expectation of a product of two distinct i.i.d. coordinates

Let $\alpha$ and $\iota$ be finite sets and $p$ a weight on $\alpha$ with $\sum_a p(a) = 1$. Under the i.i.d. weight $\omega \mapsto \prod_k p(\omega_k)$ on samples $\omega \in \alpha^\iota$, for distinct coordinates $i \ne j$ and functions $f, g : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{E}[f(\omega_i)\, g(\omega_j)] = \mathbb{E}_p[f]\, \mathbb{E}_p[g]$.
