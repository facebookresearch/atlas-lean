# Interval from a pivot

For a realized statistic $t$, a scale $s$ and an acceptance region $[lo, hi]$, the pivot interval is
$[t - hi \cdot s, t - lo \cdot s]$. For $0 < s$ it is the set of parameter values $\theta$ whose
pivot $(t - \theta) / s$ lies in $[lo, hi]$. It is computed from data and carries no coverage claim;
a coverage probability follows only from a hypothesis on the law of the pivot.
