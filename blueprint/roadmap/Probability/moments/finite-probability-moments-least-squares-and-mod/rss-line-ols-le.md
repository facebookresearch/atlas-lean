# The least-squares line minimizes the residual sum of squares

For data $x, y : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite index set and every line $a + b x$, the residual sum of squares of the least-squares line is at most that of $a + b x$: $\mathrm{RSS}(\hat a, \hat b) \le \mathrm{RSS}(a, b)$. This holds for all data, including $S_{xx} = 0$.
