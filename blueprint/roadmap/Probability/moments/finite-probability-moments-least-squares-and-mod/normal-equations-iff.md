# Normal equations

For data $x, y : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite index set with $S_{xx} \ne 0$ and reals $a, b$, the residuals of the line $a + b x$ sum to zero and are orthogonal to $x$, $\sum_i (y_i - a - b x_i) = 0$ and $\sum_i x_i (y_i - a - b x_i) = 0$, exactly when $a$ and $b$ are the least-squares intercept and slope.
