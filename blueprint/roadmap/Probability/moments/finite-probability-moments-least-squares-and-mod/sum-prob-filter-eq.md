# Additivity of event weights over the fibres of a map

For a weight $p$ on a set $\alpha$, a map $f : \alpha \to \iota$ to a finite set and a finite set $s \subseteq \alpha$, $\sum_{i \in \iota} P(\{x \in s : f(x) = i\}) = P(s)$, where $P(A) = \sum_{a \in A} p(a)$ is the event weight. The identity holds for signed weights; for a probability law it is the law of total probability for the partition of $s$ into the fibres of $f$.
