# Independence implies zero covariance

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite sets and $p_X, p_Y$ weights on them summing to one. Under the product weight $p_X(x)\, p_Y(y)$ on $\alpha \times \beta$, the functions $f(x)$ and $g(y)$ have covariance $0$, for all $f : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ and $g : \beta \to \mathbb{R}$.
