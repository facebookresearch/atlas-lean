# Expectation of the sample mean of an i.i.d. sample

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $p$ a weight on $\alpha$ with $\sum_a p(a) = 1$, and $\iota$ a nonempty finite index set. Under the i.i.d. weight $\omega \mapsto \prod_i p(\omega_i)$ on samples $\omega \in \alpha^\iota$, the expectation of the sample mean of $X(\omega_i)$ is $\mathbb{E}_p[X]$, for every $X : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$.
