# Integration against a finite PMF is a finite expectation

For a probability mass function $q$ on a finite measurable space whose singletons are measurable and $X : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, the integral
of $X$ against the measure of $q$ equals $\sum_a q(a) X(a)$, the expectation of $X$ under the real
weights $q(a)$.
