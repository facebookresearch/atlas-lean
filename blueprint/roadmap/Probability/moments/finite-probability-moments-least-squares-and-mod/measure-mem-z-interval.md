# Known-variance normal location model

Let $X(\omega) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be the observations, with $0 < n$, and let $\sigma > 0$ be known.
If, under $\mu$, the pivot $(\bar x - \theta) / (\sigma / \sqrt n)$ of the sample mean has the
standard normal law, then the interval $\bar x \pm z \sigma / \sqrt n$ contains $\theta$ with
probability $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$. The normal law of the pivot is a hypothesis of the statement.
