# Event weights of product events under a product weight

For weights $p_X$ on $\alpha$ and $p_Y$ on $\beta$ and finite sets $s \subseteq \alpha$, $t \subseteq \beta$, the product weight $p_X(x)\, p_Y(y)$ gives the product event $s \times t$ the weight $P_X(s)\, P_Y(t)$. The weights may be signed; for probability laws $p_X, p_Y$ this is the probability of a product event under independent coordinates.
