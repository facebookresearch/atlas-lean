# Bessel's correction

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $p$ a weight on $\alpha$ with $\sum_a p(a) = 1$, and $\iota$ a finite index set with $n \ge 2$ elements. Under the i.i.d. weight $\omega \mapsto \prod_i p(\omega_i)$ on samples $\omega \in \alpha^\iota$, the divide-by-$(n-1)$ sample variance of the observations $X(\omega_i)$ has expectation $\operatorname{Var}_p(X)$, for every $X : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$. No separate finite-variance hypothesis is needed because $\alpha$ is finite.
