# Least-squares residuals are orthogonal to the columns of the design matrix

Let $X$ be a real matrix with finite row and column index sets such that $X^{\mathsf T} X$ is invertible, and let $\hat\beta$ be the least-squares estimator for responses $y$. Then $X^{\mathsf T}(y - X\hat\beta) = 0$: the residual is orthogonal to every column of $X$.
