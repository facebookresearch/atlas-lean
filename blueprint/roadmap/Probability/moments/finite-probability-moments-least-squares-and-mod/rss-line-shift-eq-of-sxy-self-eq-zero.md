# Least squares is not unique for a constant regressor

For data $x, y : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite index set with $S_{xx} = 0$, so that $x$ is constant, and reals $a, b, c$, adding $c$ to the slope and $-c\bar x$ to the intercept does not change the residual sum of squares: $\mathrm{RSS}(a - c\bar x, b + c) = \mathrm{RSS}(a, b)$. So the least-squares minimizer is not unique.
