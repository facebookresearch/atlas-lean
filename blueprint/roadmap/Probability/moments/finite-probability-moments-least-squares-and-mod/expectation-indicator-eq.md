# Posterior coverage of a credible interval

Let $\Theta$ be a finite parameter set, $\pi$ a posterior distribution on it (nonnegative, summing to one), $\theta : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$ and $[l, h]$ a credible interval of level $c$, that is, $\pi(\{t : \theta(t) \in [l, h]\}) = c$. Then the posterior expectation of the indicator of $\theta \in [l, h]$ is $c$. This is a statement about the posterior only and asserts no frequentist coverage.
