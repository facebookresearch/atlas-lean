# The product weight of a weight of total one sums to one

Let $\alpha$ and $\iota$ be finite sets and $p$ a weight on $\alpha$ with $\sum_a p(a) = 1$. Then the product weight $\omega \mapsto \prod_i p(\omega_i)$ on $\alpha^\iota$ also sums to $1$. The product weight $\omega \mapsto \prod_i p(\omega_i)$ is the law of an i.i.d. sample only when $p$ is a probability law ($p \ge 0$ and $\sum_a p(a) = 1$); the identity itself is algebraic and does not use nonnegativity.
