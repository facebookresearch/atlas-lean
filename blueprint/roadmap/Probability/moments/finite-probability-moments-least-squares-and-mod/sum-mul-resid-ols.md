# Least-squares residuals are orthogonal to the regressor

For data $x, y : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite index set, the residuals of the least-squares line are orthogonal to $x$: $\sum_i x_i\,(y_i - \hat a - \hat b x_i) = 0$, where $\hat a$ and $\hat b$ are the least-squares intercept and slope. When $S_{xx} = 0$ the slope is $0$ by the convention for division by zero, the intercept is $\bar y$, and the identity still holds.
