# Zero covariance does not imply independence

Zero covariance does not imply independence. Under the uniform weight on a three-point set, $X = (-1, 0, 1)$ and $Y = X^2$ have covariance $0$, but the events $\{X = 0\}$ and $\{Y = 0\}$ have joint probability $1/3$, while the product of their probabilities is $1/9$.
