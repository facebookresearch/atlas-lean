# The least-squares slope of integrable responses is integrable

For a fixed design $x : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite index set and integrable random responses $Y_i$ on a measure space, the least-squares slope $\hat b(x, Y) = S_{xY}/S_{xx}$ is integrable. When $S_{xx} = 0$ the slope is $0$ by the convention for division by zero.
