# Expectation of a product under a product weight

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite sets with weights $p_X$ and $p_Y$. Under the product weight $p_X(x)\, p_Y(y)$ on $\alpha \times \beta$, the expectation of $f(x)\, g(y)$ is $\mathbb{E}_{p_X}[f]\, \mathbb{E}_{p_Y}[g]$, for all $f : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ and $g : \beta \to \mathbb{R}$. The weights need not be nonnegative or sum to one; for probability laws the product weight is the law of independent coordinates.
