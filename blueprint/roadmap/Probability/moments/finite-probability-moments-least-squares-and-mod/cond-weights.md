# Weights conditioned on an event of positive weight

For a weight $p$ on a finite set $\Omega$ and an event $A$ of positive weight $P(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} p(\omega) > 0$, the weights conditioned on $A$ are $p(\omega)/P(A)$ for $\omega \in A$ and $0$ off $A$. Positivity of $P(A)$ is required to form them, so an event of weight $0$ has no conditioned weights. For nonnegative weights they form a probability law.
