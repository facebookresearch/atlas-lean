# Covariance under a finite weight

A weight on a finite type $\alpha$ is a function $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$; it is a finite
distribution when $p \ge 0$ and $\sum_x p(x) = 1$. For $X, Y : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, the
covariance under $p$ is $E[(X - E X)(Y - E Y)]$, where $E Z = \sum_x p(x) Z(x)$. It is a finite sum
and needs no integrability hypotheses.
