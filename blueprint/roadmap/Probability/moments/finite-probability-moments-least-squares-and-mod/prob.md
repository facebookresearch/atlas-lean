# Weight of an event under a finite weight

A weight on a finite type $\alpha$ is a function $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$; it is a probability law (a finite distribution) when $p \ge 0$ and $\sum_x p(x) = 1$. The weight of an event $s \subseteq \alpha$ under $p$ is the real finite sum $P(s) = \sum_{x \in s} p(x)$; it is the probability of $s$ when $p$ is a probability law. For signed weights, such as $(-1, 2)$ on two points, an event weight can be negative even though the total is one.
