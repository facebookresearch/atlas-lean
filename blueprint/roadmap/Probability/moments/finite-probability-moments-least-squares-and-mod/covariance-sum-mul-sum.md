# Covariance of two linear combinations

For a finite set $\alpha$, any weight $p$, finite index sets $s$ and $t$, coefficients $a_i, b_j$ and functions $X_i, Y_j : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, the weighted covariance is bilinear: $\operatorname{Cov}_p\bigl(\sum_{i \in s} a_i X_i, \sum_{j \in t} b_j Y_j\bigr) = \sum_{i \in s} \sum_{j \in t} a_i b_j \operatorname{Cov}_p(X_i, Y_j)$. The weight need not be nonnegative or sum to one.
