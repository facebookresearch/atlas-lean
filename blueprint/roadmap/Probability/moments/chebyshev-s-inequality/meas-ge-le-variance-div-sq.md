# Chebyshev's inequality

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $X$ be a real function in $L^2(\mu)$, almost
everywhere strongly measurable with $\int X^2 \, d\mu < \infty$, and let $c > 0$. With $EX = \int X \, d\mu$ and
$\operatorname{Var}(X) = \int (X - EX)^2 \, d\mu$,

$$\mu\big(\{\omega : |X(\omega) - EX| \ge c\}\big) \le \frac{\operatorname{Var}(X)}{c^2}.$$
