# Ratios of differences of means under an affine map

Let $X,Y,U,W$ be integrable real random variables on a probability space. For every real $a\ne 0$ and every real $b$,

$$
\frac{E[aX+b]-E[aY+b]}{E[aU+b]-E[aW+b]}=\frac{E[X]-E[Y]}{E[U]-E[W]}.
$$

If $E[U]=E[W]$, both ratios are zero by the convention $x/0=0$.
