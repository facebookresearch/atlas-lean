# Variance of stopped sums

Let $N$ be a measurable count bounded almost surely by some $n_{max}$, and $X_0, X_1, \ldots$ measurable, square-integrable, identically distributed and mutually independent, with $N$ independent of the $X_i$. Then

$$\mathrm{Var}\Big(\sum_{i < N} X_i\Big) = \mathbb{E}[N]\,\mathrm{Var}(X_0) + \mathrm{Var}(N)\,(\mathbb{E}X_0)^2.$$
