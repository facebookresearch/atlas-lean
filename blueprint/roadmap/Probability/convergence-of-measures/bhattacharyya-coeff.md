# The Bhattacharyya coefficient

Let $S$ be a finite set and $p, q \colon S \to \mathbb{R}$. The Bhattacharyya coefficient of $p$ and $q$ is

$$\mathrm{BC}(p, q) = \sum_{x \in S} \sqrt{p(x)\, q(x)},$$

where the real square root of a negative number is taken to be $0$. For probability vectors $p$ and $q$ it is the unsquared classical (root) fidelity.
