# The coupling inequality

Let $p$ and $q$ be probability mass functions on a finite set $\alpha$ and $r$ a coupling of $p$ and $q$, a probability mass function on $\alpha \times \alpha$ with marginals $p$ and $q$. Then the total variation distance between $p$ and $q$ is at most the $r$-probability that the two coordinates differ, $r(\{(a, b) : a \ne b\})$.
