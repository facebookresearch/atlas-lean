# Borel sigma-algebra of D equals the coordinate sigma-algebra; fdd determine laws

Let $(E,r)$ be a Polish space, $T>0$, and $T_0\subseteq[0,T]$ a dense set with $T\in T_0$. Then the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of the [Skorokhod space](skorokhod-space-d-0-t-e-for-a-metric-space-e-j1.md) $D([0,T],E)$ equals the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the evaluations $\pi_t(x)=x(t)$, $t\in T_0$. Consequently two probability measures on $D([0,T],E)$ whose finite-dimensional distributions at all finite sets of times in $T_0$ agree are equal. In particular, the laws of two càdlàg processes with equal finite-dimensional distributions at the times $(\mathbb Q\cap[0,T))\cup\{T\}$ coincide.

The terminal time must belong to $T_0$: for irrational $T$, the càdlàg path $f=\mathbf 1_{\{T\}}$ (equal to $1$ at $T$ and $0$ elsewhere) and $g=0$ agree at every rational time in $[0,T]$ but differ at $T$.

## Expansion

A full page must establish:

- measurability of each $\pi_t$ for the Borel $\sigma$-algebra (one inclusion);
- the reverse inclusion, using separability: every open ball of $D([0,T],E)$ is measurable for the evaluations at times in $T_0$, by approximating $d(x,\cdot)$ through right-continuity at times of $T_0$ and the value at $T$;
- the uniqueness of laws from the $\pi$-$\lambda$ theorem applied to finite-dimensional cylinder sets;
- the rational-times corollary and the counterexample at an irrational terminal time.
