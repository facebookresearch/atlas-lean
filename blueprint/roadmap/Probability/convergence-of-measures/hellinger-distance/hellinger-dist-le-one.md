# The Hellinger distance between probability measures is at most one

For probability measures $\mu$ and $\nu$, $d_{\mathrm{Hell}}(\mu, \nu) \le 1$. This is the purpose of the factor
$\tfrac12$ in the definition; here it follows from
$d_{\mathrm{Hell}} \le d_{\mathrm{TV}}^{1/2}$ and $d_{\mathrm{TV}} \le 1$.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), Section 7.2.4, the remark after (7.22).
