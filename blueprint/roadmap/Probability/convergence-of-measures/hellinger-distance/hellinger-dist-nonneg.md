# The Hellinger distance is nonnegative

For all finite measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ (indeed for all measures, as a property of the definition), $d_{\mathrm{Hell}}(\mu, \nu) \ge 0$, since it is a square root.

## Sources

- M. Dashti and A. M. Stuart, *The Bayesian Approach to Inverse Problems*, arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification* (Springer, 2017), Section 7.2.4, Definition 7.11 and the remark after (7.22).
