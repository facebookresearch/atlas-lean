# Delta method in one dimension

Let real random variables $X_n$, indexed along a countably generated filter such as $n \to \infty$,
satisfy $r_n (X_n - \theta) \to Z$ in distribution, where $r_n \to \infty$. If $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$
has derivative $g'(\theta)$ at $\theta$, and $X_n$ and $g(X_n)$ are almost everywhere measurable, then
$r_n \big(g(X_n) - g(\theta)\big) \to g'(\theta) Z$ in distribution.
