# Convergence in distribution to a constant implies convergence in probability

Let $P$ and $P'$ be probability measures on $\Omega$ and $\Omega'$, let $E$ be a pseudometric space
with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, let $l$ be a filter on an index set $\iota$, and let
$X_i : \Omega \to E$. If the $X_i$ converge in distribution along $l$ to the constant $c \in E$,
then they converge to $c$ in probability along $l$.
