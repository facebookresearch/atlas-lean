# Delta method

Let $E$ and $F$ be second-countable real normed spaces, and let random vectors $X_n$ in $E$ on a
probability space, indexed along a countably generated filter such as $n \to \infty$, satisfy
$r_n (X_n - \theta) \to Z$ in distribution, where $r_n \to \infty$. If $g : E \to F$ is Fréchet
differentiable at $\theta$ with derivative $L$, and $X_n$ and $g(X_n)$ are almost everywhere
measurable, then $r_n \big(g(X_n) - g(\theta)\big) \to LZ$ in distribution. This is the first-order
statement only: when $L = 0$ the limit is the constant $0$, and no second-order limit law is asserted.
