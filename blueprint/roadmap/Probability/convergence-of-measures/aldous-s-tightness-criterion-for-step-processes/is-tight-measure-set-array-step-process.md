# From tightness of the polygons in C to tightness of the step processes in D

For a triangular array of measurable random variables $(\xi_{n,i})$ on a measure space, if the laws of the polygonal processes of the rows are tight in $C([0, 1])$, then the laws of the step processes of the rows are tight in the Skorokhod space $D([0, 1])$ with the J1 topology.
