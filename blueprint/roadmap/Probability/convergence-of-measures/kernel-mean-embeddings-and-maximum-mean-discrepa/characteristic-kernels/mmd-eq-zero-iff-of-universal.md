# The MMD of a universal kernel characterizes distributions

Let $X$ be a compact metric space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $H$ a real reproducing
kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with continuous kernel $k$, such that for every continuous
$f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ some $g \in H$ satisfies $|f(x) - g(x)| \le \varepsilon$
for all $x$. Then for Borel probability measures $P$ and $Q$ on $X$, $\mathrm{MMD}(P, Q) = 0$ if
and only if $P = Q$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Theorem 5.
