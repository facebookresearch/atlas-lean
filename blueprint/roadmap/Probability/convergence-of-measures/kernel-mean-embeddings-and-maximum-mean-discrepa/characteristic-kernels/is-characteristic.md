# Characteristic kernel

Let $X$ be a measurable space and $H$ a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$
with [feature map](../reproducing-kernel/feature-map.md) $\varphi$. The kernel of $H$ is characteristic if the [mean
embedding](../kernel-mean-embedding/mean-embedding.md) is injective on the probability measures for which $\varphi$ is
Bochner integrable: for such $P$ and $Q$, $\mu_P = \mu_Q$ implies $P = Q$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Section 2.2, after Theorem 5
  (following Fukumizu et al., 2008).
