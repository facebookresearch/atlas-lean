# The unit ball attains the norm of a vector

Let $H$ be a real inner product space and $v \in H$. Then on the closed unit ball of $H$ the
function $f \mapsto \langle f, v\rangle$ attains its supremum $\|v\|$: the Cauchy–Schwarz step in
the proof of Lemma 4 of Gretton et al.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, proof of Lemma 4.
