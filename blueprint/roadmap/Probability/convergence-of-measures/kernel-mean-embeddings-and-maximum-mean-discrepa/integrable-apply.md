# Elements of an RKHS are integrable against measures with integrable feature map

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $H$ a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with
[feature map](feature-map.md) $\varphi$, and $P$ a measure on $X$ for which $\varphi$ is Bochner
integrable. Then every $f \in H$ is $P$-integrable, since $f(x) = \langle f, \varphi(x)\rangle$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, proof of Lemma 3.
