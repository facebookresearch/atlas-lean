# Reproducing property of the kernel mean embedding

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $H$ a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with
[feature map](../reproducing-kernel/feature-map.md) $\varphi$, and $P$ a measure on $X$ for which $\varphi$ is Bochner
integrable. Then the [mean embedding](mean-embedding.md) satisfies
$\langle f, \mu_P\rangle = \int f \, dP$ for every $f \in H$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Lemma 3.
