# Mean embeddings exist for continuous kernels on compact spaces

Let $X$ be a compact metric space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $H$ a real reproducing
kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ whose [feature map](../reproducing-kernel/feature-map.md) $\varphi$ is
continuous. Then $\varphi$ is Bochner integrable for every finite measure on $X$, so the mean
embedding of every Borel probability measure exists (the setting of Theorem 5 of Gretton et al.).

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Theorem 5.
