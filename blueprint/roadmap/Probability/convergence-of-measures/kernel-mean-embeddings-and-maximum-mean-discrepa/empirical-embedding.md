# Empirical mean embedding

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with [feature
map](feature-map.md) $\varphi$. For a nonempty sample $x_1, \dots, x_m \in X$ ($m \ge 1$), the empirical mean
embedding is $\mu_X = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^m \varphi(x_i) \in H$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Section 2.2, before
  equation (5).
