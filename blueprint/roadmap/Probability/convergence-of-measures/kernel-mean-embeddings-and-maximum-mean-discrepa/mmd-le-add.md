# Triangle inequality for the MMD

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $H$ a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$, and
$P$, $Q$, $R$ measures on $X$ for which the [feature map](feature-map.md) is Bochner integrable.
Then $\mathrm{MMD}(P, R) \le \mathrm{MMD}(P, Q) + \mathrm{MMD}(Q, R)$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Section 2.2 (pseudometric
  axioms as in its footnote 3).
