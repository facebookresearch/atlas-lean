# Canonical feature map of a reproducing kernel Hilbert space

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on a set $X$. The feature map sends
$x \in X$ to the representer $\varphi(x) = k(\cdot, x) \in H$ of evaluation at $x$:
$\langle f, \varphi(x)\rangle = f(x)$ for every $f \in H$.
