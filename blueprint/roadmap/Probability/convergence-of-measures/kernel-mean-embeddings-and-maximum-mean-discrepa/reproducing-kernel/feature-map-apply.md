# Kernel functions are feature maps

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with kernel $k$ and
[feature map](feature-map.md) $\varphi$. Then $\varphi(y)(x) = k(x, y)$ for all $x, y \in X$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Section 2.2.
