# Reproducing property of the feature map

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with [feature
map](feature-map.md) $\varphi$. For every $f \in H$ and $x \in X$, $\langle f, \varphi(x)\rangle = f(x)$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Section 2.2.
