# Norm of a feature map

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$ with kernel $k$ and [feature
map](feature-map.md) $\varphi$. Then $\|\varphi(x)\| = \sqrt{k(x, x)}$ for every $x \in X$.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, proof of Lemma 3.
