# Operator-valued and scalar kernels

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of real functions on $X$. Its operator-valued
kernel $K(x, y)$, a linear map $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, is multiplication by the scalar kernel
$k(x, y) = \langle \varphi(x), \varphi(y)\rangle$, where $\varphi$ is the [feature
map](feature-map.md).
