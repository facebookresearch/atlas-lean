# Symmetry of the MMD

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $H$ a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$, and
$P$, $Q$ measures on $X$ for which the [feature map](feature-map.md) is Bochner integrable. Then
$\mathrm{MMD}(P, Q) = \mathrm{MMD}(Q, P)$.

## Sources

  axioms as in its footnote 3).
