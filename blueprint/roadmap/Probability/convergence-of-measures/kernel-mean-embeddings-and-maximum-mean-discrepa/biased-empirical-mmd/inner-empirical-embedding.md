# Reproducing property of the empirical mean embedding

Let $H$ be a real reproducing kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$. For $f \in H$ and a nonempty
sample $x_1, \dots, x_m \in X$, the [empirical mean embedding](empirical-embedding.md) satisfies
$\langle f, \mu_X\rangle = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^m f(x_i)$.

## Sources

  equation (5).
