# Two coordinates of an i.i.d. sample are independent copies

Let $P$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $X$, let $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $i \ne j$ in
$\{1, \dots, m\}$. Under the product measure $P^{\otimes m}$, the pair $(x_i, x_j)$ has law
$P \otimes P$: two coordinates of an i.i.d. sample are independent copies.

## Sources

- A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch, B. Schölkopf and A. Smola, *A kernel two-sample
  test*, Journal of Machine Learning Research 13 (2012), 723–773, Lemma 6.
