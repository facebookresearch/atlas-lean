# Continuous mapping theorem on a Polish space

Let $E$ be a Polish space and $F$ a topological space, both with their Borel σ-algebras, let $\mu_n \to \mu$ be a weakly convergent sequence of probability measures on $E$, and let $g : E \to F$ be measurable with $\mu(\{x : g \text{ is not continuous at } x\}) = 0$. Then the image measures $\mu_n \circ g^{-1}$ converge weakly to $\mu \circ g^{-1}$.
