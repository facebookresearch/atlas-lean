# Continuous mapping theorem

Let $\Omega$ and $\Omega'$ be topological spaces whose open sets are measurable, with closed sets of $\Omega$ approximable from outside by continuous functions, and let $\mu_i \to \mu$ be weak convergence of probability measures on $\Omega$ along a countably generated filter. If $g : \Omega \to \Omega'$ is measurable and its discontinuity set is $\mu$-null, then the image measures $\mu_i \circ g^{-1}$ converge weakly to $\mu \circ g^{-1}$.
