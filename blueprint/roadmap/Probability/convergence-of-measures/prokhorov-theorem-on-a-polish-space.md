# Prokhorov's theorem: tight sets of probability measures are relatively compact

Let $E$ be a Hausdorff topological space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and give the space of Borel probability measures on $E$ the topology of weak convergence (convergence of $\int f\,d\mu$ for every bounded continuous $f:E\to\mathbb R$). If a set $S$ of probability measures is tight, that is, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a compact $K\subseteq E$ with $\mu(E\setminus K)\le\varepsilon$ for every $\mu\in S$, then the closure of $S$ is compact.
