# Tightness in $C([0,1])$ from a modulus-of-continuity bound

Let $W_n$ be measurable random elements of $C([0,1])$ on a probability space with $W_n(0) = 0$ for all $n$. Suppose for all $\varepsilon, b > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that eventually $P(\exists s, t : |s - t| \le \delta,\ |W_n(s) - W_n(t)| \ge \varepsilon) \le b$. Then the laws of the $W_n$ are tight.

## Sources

P. Billingsley, *Convergence of Probability Measures*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1999, Theorem 7.3.
