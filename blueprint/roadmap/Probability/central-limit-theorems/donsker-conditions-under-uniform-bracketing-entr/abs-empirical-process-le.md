# Abs empirical Process le

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $S$, let $X_0, X_1, \dots : \Omega \to S$ be arbitrary maps, and let $h : S \to \mathbb{R}$ be $\mu$-integrable with $0 \le h(x) \le w$ for all $x$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $\omega \in \Omega$, the empirical process $\mathbb{G}_n h(\omega) = n^{-1/2} \sum_{k<n} (h(X_k(\omega)) - \int h \, d\mu)$ satisfies

$$\lvert \mathbb{G}_n h(\omega) \rvert \le \sqrt{n}\, w.$$
