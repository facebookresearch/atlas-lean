# The uniform bracketing entropy is attained

Let $S$ be a measurable space, $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of real functions on $S$ and $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $H(\varepsilon) \in [0, \infty]$ be the [uniform bracketing entropy](uniform-bracketing-entropy.md), the infimum of $\sqrt{\log n}$ over the sizes $n$ of uniform bracket covers of $F$ of width $\varepsilon$, with $\log 0 = 0$. If $H(\varepsilon) < \infty$, then there is $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $F$ has a uniform bracket cover of width $\varepsilon$ with $n$ brackets and

$$\sqrt{\log n} \le H(\varepsilon),$$

so the infimum is attained. The number $n$ may be $0$ or $1$, for which $\sqrt{\log n} = 0$; for the empty family the cover by zero brackets attains $H(\varepsilon) = 0$.
