# Exists dyadic covers

Let $S$ be a measurable space and $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of real functions on $S$ whose uniform bracketing entropy integral is finite,

$$\int_{(0, 1]} H(\varepsilon) \, d\varepsilon < \infty,$$

where $H(\varepsilon) \in [0, \infty]$ is the infimum of $\sqrt{\log n}$ over the sizes $n$ of uniform bracket covers of $F$ of width $\varepsilon$. Then there are $N_0, N_1, \dots \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for every $k$, $F$ has a uniform bracket cover of width $2^{-k}$ with $N_k$ brackets, and

$$\sum_{k=0}^\infty 2^{-k} \sqrt{\log N_k} < \infty,$$

with $\log 0 = 0$.
