# Is Uniform Bracket Cover

Let $S$ be a measurable space, $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a family of real functions on $S$, $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The family admits a uniform bracket cover of width $\varepsilon$ with $n$ brackets if there are measurable functions $l_1, \dots, l_n, u_1, \dots, u_n : S \to \mathbb{R}$ such that for every $j$ and every $x \in S$,

$$l_j(x) \le u_j(x) \quad \text{and} \quad u_j(x) - l_j(x) \le \varepsilon,$$

and every $F_i$ lies in some bracket: for each $i$ there is $j$ with $l_j(x) \le F_i(x) \le u_j(x)$ for all $x \in S$.
