# The polygonal process starts at zero

For every $n$, every triangular array $(\xi_{n,i})$ and every outcome $\omega$, the polygonal process of row $n$, the linear interpolation in $C([0,1])$ of the partial sums $k \mapsto \sum_{i<k} \xi_{n,i}(\omega)$ at the times $k/n$, takes the value $0$ at time $0$.
