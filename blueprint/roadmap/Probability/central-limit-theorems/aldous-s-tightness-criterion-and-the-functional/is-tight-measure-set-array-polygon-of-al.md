# Aldous's tightness criterion

On a probability space, let $(\xi_{n,i})$ be a triangular array with each $\xi_{n,i}$ measurable for $\mathcal F_{n,i+1}$, where $(\mathcal F_{n,i})_i$ is a filtration for each row. If the largest increment of row $n$ tends to $0$ in probability, $\mathbb{P}(|\xi_{n,i}| > \varepsilon \text{ for some } i < n) \to 0$ for every $\varepsilon > 0$, and the partial sums satisfy Aldous's condition, then the laws of the polygonal processes of the rows on $C([0, 1])$ form a tight family.

## Sources

- J. Jacod and A. N. Shiryaev, *Limit Theorems for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., Springer (2003), Theorem VI.4.5 (Aldous's criterion for tightness in $D$) with Proposition VI.3.26 (tightness in $D$ plus vanishing jumps gives $C$-tightness). The paths start at $0$, so the compact-containment condition (i) of VI.4.5 is not needed.
- D. Aldous, "Stopping times and tightness", Annals of Probability 6 (1978), 335–340.
