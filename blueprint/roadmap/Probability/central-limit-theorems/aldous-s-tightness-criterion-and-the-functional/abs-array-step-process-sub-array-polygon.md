# The polygonal process stays within the largest increment of the step process

For a triangular array $(\xi_{n,i})$, an outcome $\omega$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $c \ge 0$ with $|\xi_{n,i}(\omega)| \le c$ for every $i < n$, the step process and the polygonal process of row $n$ differ by at most $c$ at every time $t \in [0, 1]$.
