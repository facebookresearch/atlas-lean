# Predictable cut bound

For real terms $c_0, c_1, \dots$, a bound $K \ge 0$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the terms $c_k$, $k < m$, whose running sum $\sum_{j \le k} c_j$ is at most $K$ add up to at most $K$: $\sum_{k<m} c_k\, \mathbf 1\{\sum_{j \le k} c_j \le K\} \le K$. No sign condition on $c$ is needed. With $c_k = \mathbb{E}[X_k^2 \mid \mathcal F_k]$ this is the pointwise core of a predictable truncation of the conditional variances.
