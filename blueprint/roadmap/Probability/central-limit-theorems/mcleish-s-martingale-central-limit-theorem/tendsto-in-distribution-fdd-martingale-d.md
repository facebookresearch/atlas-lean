# Finite-dimensional convergence of martingale step sums

Let row $n$ of $\xi$ be a square-integrable martingale difference sequence for $\mathcal F_n$
satisfying the Lindeberg condition. Assume that the realized quadratic variation
$\sum_{j < \lfloor n t \rfloor} \xi_{n,j}^2$ tends to $t$ in probability for every $t \in [0,1]$,
and that $Z$ is a measurable map from a probability space into the space $C([0,1])$ of continuous
paths whose finite-dimensional laws are the centered Gaussian laws of Brownian motion (covariance
$\min(s, u)$). Then for every finite set of times $I \subseteq [0,1]$,
$(\sum_{i < \lfloor n t \rfloor} \xi_{n,i})_{t \in I} \to (Z_t)_{t \in I}$ in distribution.
