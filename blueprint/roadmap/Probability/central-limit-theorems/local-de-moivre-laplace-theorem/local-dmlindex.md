# Lattice index in the local De Moivre–Laplace theorem

For $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$k_n = \Big\lfloor \frac{n}{2} + \frac{x \sqrt{n}}{2} \Big\rfloor ,$$

the natural number obtained by rounding down (and $0$ if the argument is negative). It is the
lattice point near $n/2 + x\sqrt{n}/2$, so that $(2 k_n - n)/\sqrt{n} \approx x$.
