# The complementary De Moivre–Laplace index tends to infinity

Let $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $k_n = \lfloor n/2 + x \sqrt{n}/2 \rfloor$ be the [lattice
index](local-dmlindex.md). Then $n - k_n \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$ (with $n - k_n$ truncated
at $0$), so both $k_n$ and $n - k_n$ are eventually arbitrarily large.
