# Binomial point mass is equivalent to its Gaussian approximation

Let $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $k_n = \lfloor n/2 + x \sqrt{n}/2 \rfloor$ be the [lattice
index](local-dmlindex.md). Then the [binomial point mass](binom-prob-half.md) and its [Gaussian
approximation](gaussian-approx-half.md) are asymptotically equivalent along $k_n$:
$b(n, k_n) \sim g(n, k_n)$ as $n \to \infty$.
