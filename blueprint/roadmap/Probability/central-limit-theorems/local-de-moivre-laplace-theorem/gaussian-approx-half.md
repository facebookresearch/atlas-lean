# Gaussian approximation to the symmetric binomial point mass

For natural numbers $n$ and $k$,

$$g(n, k) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi n}}\, \exp\Big(-\frac{(k - n/2)^2}{n/2}\Big),$$

the Gaussian (local central limit) approximation to the [binomial point mass](binom-prob-half.md)
$b(n, k)$. With division by zero read as $0$, $g(0, k) = 0$.
