# Binomial point mass is equivalent to a fixed Gaussian value

Let $m, j : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ be sequences with $m(n) \to \infty$ and
$(2 j(n) - m(n)) / \sqrt{m(n)} \to x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then

$$\binom{m(n)}{j(n)} 2^{-m(n)} \sim \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi\, m(n)}}\; e^{-x^2/2} \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

that is, the ratio of the two sides tends to $1$: the point mass of a symmetric binomial count is
equivalent to the Gaussian expression evaluated at the fixed limit $x$.
