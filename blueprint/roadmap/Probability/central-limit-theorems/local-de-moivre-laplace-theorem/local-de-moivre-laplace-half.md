# Local De Moivre–Laplace theorem for $p = 1/2$

Let $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $k_n = \lfloor n/2 + x \sqrt{n}/2 \rfloor$ be the [lattice
index](local-dmlindex.md). Then the [binomial point mass](binom-prob-half.md) divided by its
[Gaussian approximation](gaussian-approx-half.md) tends to $1$:

$$\frac{b(n, k_n)}{g(n, k_n)} \to 1 \qquad (n \to \infty).$$

The [local limit theorem for the simple random walk](local-de-moivre-laplace-half-of-tendsto.md)
treats arbitrary integer displacement sequences, with the Gaussian value at the fixed limit $x$.
