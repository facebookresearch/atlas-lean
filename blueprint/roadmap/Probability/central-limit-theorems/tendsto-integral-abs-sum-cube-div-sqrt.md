# Mean absolute sum over the unit cube

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ let

$$
I_n = \int_{[-1/2,\, 1/2]^n} \left| \sum_{i=1}^n x_i \right| dx.
$$

Then $I_n / \sqrt{n} \to 1 / \sqrt{6\pi}$ as $n \to \infty$.
