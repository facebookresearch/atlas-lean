# Normalized partial-sum step path

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and increments $y_0, \dots, y_{n-1}$, the normalized partial-sum step path is the path in the Skorokhod space $D([0, 1])$ given for $n > 0$ by $t \mapsto n^{-1/2} \sum_{i<n} y_i\, \mathbf 1\{(i+1)/n \le t\}$; for $n = 0$ it is the zero path.
