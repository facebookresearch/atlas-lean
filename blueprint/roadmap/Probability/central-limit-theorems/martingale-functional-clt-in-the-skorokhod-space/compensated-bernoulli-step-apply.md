# Formula for the compensated Bernoulli counting process

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a real $p$, real increments $\xi_i$ (Bernoulli counts in the application), an outcome $\omega$ and $t \in [0, 1]$, the compensated Bernoulli counting process at $t$ is $\bigl(N_{\lfloor nt \rfloor} - p \lfloor nt \rfloor\bigr)/\sqrt n$, with $N_k = \sum_{i<k} \xi_i(\omega)$. For $n = 0$ it is $0$.
