# Compensated Bernoulli counting process

For real increments $\xi_i$, a real $p$ and partial sums $N_k = \sum_{i<k} \xi_i$, the compensated counting process is the path in the Skorokhod space $D([0, 1])$ given for $n > 0$ by $t \mapsto (N_{\lfloor nt\rfloor} - p\lfloor nt\rfloor)/\sqrt n$, and the zero path for $n = 0$. It is the normalized partial-sum step process of the centred increments $\xi_i - p$. For counting increments $\xi_i \in \{0, 1\}$ with success probability $p$ it is the compensated Bernoulli counting process: counts minus compensator, diffusively scaled.
