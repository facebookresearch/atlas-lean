# Functional CLT for the compensated Bernoulli counting process

If the counting increments $\xi_i$ are i.i.d. and $\{0,1\}$-valued with success probability
$p \in (0,1)$, then the compensated counting process converges in distribution, in the Skorokhod
$J_1$ topology on $D([0,1])$, to $\sqrt{p(1-p)}\, W$, where $W$ is a continuous version of Brownian
motion.

## Sources

- P. Billingsley, *Convergence of Probability Measures*, 2nd ed., Wiley (1999), §14 ("Donsker's Theorem Again", p. 146), Donsker's theorem in $D[0,1]$,, specialized to Bernoulli increments; Andersen–Borgan–Gill–Keiding (1993), §II.5 for the counting-process reading.
