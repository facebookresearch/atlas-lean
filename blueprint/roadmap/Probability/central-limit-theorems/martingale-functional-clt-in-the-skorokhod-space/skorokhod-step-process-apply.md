# Formula for the normalized partial-sum step process

For every $n$, every sequence of random variables $X_0, X_1, \dots$, every outcome $\omega$ and every $t \in [0, 1]$, the normalized partial-sum step process takes the value $\sum_{i < \lfloor n t\rfloor} X_i(\omega) / \sqrt n$ at $t$. For $n = 0$ the sum is empty and the value is $0$.
