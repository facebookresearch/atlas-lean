# Conditional variances of the compensated Bernoulli increments

On a probability space, let $\xi_0, \xi_1, \dots$ be i.i.d. strongly measurable random variables with values in $\{0, 1\}$ and mean $p$. For $i < j$, the conditional expectation of $(\xi_j - p)^2$ given the natural filtration of $\xi$ at time $i$ is almost surely the constant $p(1 - p)$. Informally, the predictable quadratic variation of the compensated Bernoulli counting process at time $t$ is therefore the deterministic $p(1-p)\lfloor n t\rfloor / n$.
