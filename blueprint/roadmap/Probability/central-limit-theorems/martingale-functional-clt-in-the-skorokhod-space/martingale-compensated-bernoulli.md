# The compensated Bernoulli counting process is a martingale

On a probability space, let $\xi_0, \xi_1, \dots$ be i.i.d. strongly measurable random variables with values in $\{0, 1\}$ and mean $p$. Then the partial sums $\sum_{i \le k} (\xi_i - p)$, counts minus compensator, form a martingale for the natural filtration of $\xi$. Its diffusive rescaling is the compensated Bernoulli counting process.
