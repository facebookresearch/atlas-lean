# Normalized partial-sum step process

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, random variables $X_0, X_1, \dots$ and an outcome $\omega$, the normalized partial-sum step process is the path in the Skorokhod space $D([0, 1])$ given for $n > 0$ by $t \mapsto n^{-1/2} \sum_{i < \lfloor nt\rfloor} X_i(\omega)$; for $n = 0$ it is the zero path. Unlike Donsker's polygonal process it is a pure-jump càdlàg path, the natural path of a discrete-time martingale embedded in continuous time.
