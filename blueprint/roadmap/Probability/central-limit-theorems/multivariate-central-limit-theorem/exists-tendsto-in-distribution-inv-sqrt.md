# Multivariate central limit theorem with a constructed limit

For independent, identically distributed, square-integrable random vectors $X_0, X_1, \dots$ in a
finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$ with mean $\mu$, there is a continuous linear map
$A : E \to E$ such that, for $z$ standard Gaussian on $E$, the vector $Az$ is Gaussian, each projection
$\langle Az, t \rangle$ is a centered Gaussian with variance $\operatorname{Var}\langle X_0, t \rangle$,
and $\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \big( \sum_{k < n} X_k - n\mu \big) \to Az$ in distribution.
