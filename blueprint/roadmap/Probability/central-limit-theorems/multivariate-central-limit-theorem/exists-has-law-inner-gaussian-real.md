# Gaussian vectors with a given covariance

For every square-integrable random vector $X_0$ in a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$,
there is a continuous linear map $A : E \to E$ such that, for $z$ standard Gaussian on $E$, every
projection $\langle Az, t \rangle$ is a centered Gaussian with variance
$\operatorname{Var}\langle X_0, t \rangle$. One can take $A$ to be the positive square root of the
covariance of $X_0$, written in an orthonormal basis.
