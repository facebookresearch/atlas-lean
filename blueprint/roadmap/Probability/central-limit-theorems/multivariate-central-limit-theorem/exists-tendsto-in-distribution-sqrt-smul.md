# Multivariate central limit theorem

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space and $X_0, X_1, \dots$ mutually independent, identically distributed, square-integrable random vectors in $E$ on a probability space. Then there is a centred Gaussian random vector $Z$ in $E$ with the covariance of $X_0$ (possibly singular) such that $\sqrt B\big(\frac1B\sum_{b<B}X_b - \mathbb{E}X_0\big)$ converges in distribution to $Z$.
