# Convergence of characteristic functions to the Poisson law

For $\lambda \ge 0$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the characteristic functions of the [approximating
binomial laws](binomial-poisson-approx.md) converge to that of the Poisson law of rate
$\lambda$:

$$\varphi_{\mathrm{Bin}(n, \lambda/(n + \lambda))}(t) \to \exp\big(\lambda\,(e^{it} - 1)\big) \qquad (n \to \infty).$$
