# Success probability of the binomial approximation to Poisson

For $\lambda \ge 0$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p_n = \lambda / (n + \lambda) \in [0, 1]$ (with
$p_0 = 0$ when $\lambda = 0$), the success probability of the binomial approximation to the
Poisson law. Its expected number of successes satisfies $n p_n \to \lambda$
([limit](tendsto-mul-poisson-approx-prob.md)).
