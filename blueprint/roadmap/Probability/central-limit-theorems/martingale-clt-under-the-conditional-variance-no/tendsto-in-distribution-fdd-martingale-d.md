# Finite-dimensional convergence of martingale step sums from the predictable quadratic variation

Let row $n$ of $\xi$ be a square-integrable martingale difference sequence for $\mathcal F_n$
satisfying the Lindeberg condition, with predictable quadratic variation
$\sum_{j < \lfloor n t \rfloor} E[\xi_{n,j}^2 \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}] \to t$ in probability for every
$t \in [0,1]$. If $Z$ is a measurable map from a probability space into the space $C([0,1])$ of continuous paths,
with the centered Gaussian finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion (covariance $\min(s, u)$), then for every finite $I \subseteq [0,1]$,
$(\sum_{i < \lfloor n t \rfloor} \xi_{n,i})_{t \in I} \to (Z_t)_{t \in I}$ in distribution.
