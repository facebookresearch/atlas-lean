# Iterating a variance contraction

Let $\alpha$ be finite, $\pi : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ a weight, $T$ a map on functions $\alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, and $c \ge 0$ a constant such that $\operatorname{Var}_\pi(Tf) \le c \operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)$ for every $f$. Then $\operatorname{Var}_\pi(T^t f) \le c^t \operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)$ for every $f$ and every $t \in \mathbb{N}$. The one-step bound must hold for every function, since it is applied to the iterates; no stochasticity, stationarity or gap assumption is needed for this implication.
