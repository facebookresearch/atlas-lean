# Finite Markov chains satisfy the general Markov property

Let $X = (X_n)$ be a [finite-state Markov chain](is-finite-markov-chain.md) with transition
matrix $P$ with respect to a filtration $(\mathcal{F}_n)$ under a probability measure $\mu$, and
let $\kappa_P$ be the [transition kernel](finite-chain-kernel.md) of $P$. Then $X$ is a [Markov
chain with respect to $(\mathcal{F}_n)$](../chapman-kolmogorov-equations/is-markov-chain.md) with
transition kernel $\kappa_P$ under $\mu$.
