# Time reversal equals the chain exactly under detailed balance

Let $P$ be a stochastic matrix on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ and $\pi$ a stationary distribution of $P$ with $\pi_i > 0$ for every $i$. The time reversal $\hat P_{ij} = \pi_j P_{ji} / \pi_i$ equals $P$ if and only if $P$ satisfies detailed balance with respect to $\pi$, $\pi_i P_{ij} = \pi_j P_{ji}$ for all $i, j$.
