# A Poincaré inequality bounds the mixing time of the lazy chain

Under the hypotheses of [mixing from a Poincaré inequality](sq-two-mul-tv-dist-pow-le.md), if $(1 - 1/B)^t\,(1/\pi(x) - 1) \le 1/4$ for every state $x$, then the lazy chain $(I+K)/2$ is [$1/4$-mixed](is-mixed-at.md) at time $t$. Combined with the canonical-path bound $B = \bar\rho(\Gamma)$ this is how A. Sinclair, *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* 1 (1992), turns paths into mixing times (Proposition 1(i) with Theorem 5).
