# A Glauber proposal for proper colorings

For a simple graph $G$ on $n$ vertices, a coloring $c$ of its vertices with $q$ colors, a site $i$ and a color $\kappa$, the Glauber proposal recolors $i$ with $\kappa$ if the result is a proper coloring of $G$ (adjacent vertices get different colors), and otherwise leaves $c$ unchanged.
