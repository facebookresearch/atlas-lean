# Mixing time and hitting times of large sets

For an irreducible finite Markov chain with transition matrix $P$ and stationary distribution $\pi$, let $T(1/2) = \max\{\mathbb E_x\tau_A : \pi(A) \ge 1/2\}$, where $\tau_A$ is the hitting time of $A$, and let $t_{\mathrm{mix}}$ be the total variation mixing time at level $1/4$. The claim (Peres's question) is that there is a universal $c > 0$ with $t_{\mathrm{mix}} \le c\,T(1/2)$ for every such chain that is reversible and lazy ($P(x, x) \ge 1/2$).
