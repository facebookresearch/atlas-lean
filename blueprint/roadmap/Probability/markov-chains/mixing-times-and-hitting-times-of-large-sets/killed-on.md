# Markov chain killed on a set

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $P$ be a real $n \times n$ matrix and let $A \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$. The matrix $P$ killed on $A$ is

$$P_A(y, z) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } y \in A \text{ or } z \in A, \\ P(y, z) & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

For a transition matrix $P$ these are the transitions of the chain killed on entering $A$.
