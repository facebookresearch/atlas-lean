# Finite projections of the Markov path measure

Let $E$ be a Polish space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $(K_t)_{t \ge 0}$ Markov kernels on $E$
satisfying the Chapman–Kolmogorov equations, and $x \in E$. For every finite set
$J \subset [0, \infty)$, the image of the [path measure](path-measure.md) $P_x$ under the restriction
$\omega \mapsto (\omega_t)_{t \in J}$ is the [Markov law at the times $J$](finite-law.md).
