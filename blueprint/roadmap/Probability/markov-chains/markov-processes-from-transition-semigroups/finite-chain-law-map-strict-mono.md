# Restricting a finite Markov law along an increasing reindexing

Let $(K_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be Markov kernels on a measurable space $E$ satisfying the Chapman–Kolmogorov
equations, $x \in E$, $t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_{n-1}$ times with $s \le t_i$ for every $i$, and
$e : \{0, \dots, m-1\} \to \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ strictly increasing. Then the image of the
[finite-dimensional law](finite-chain-law.md) of $(t_0, \dots, t_{n-1})$ started from $x$ at time
$s$ under $(z_0, \dots, z_{n-1}) \mapsto (z_{e(0)}, \dots, z_{e(m-1)})$ is the finite-dimensional
law of $(t_{e(0)}, \dots, t_{e(m-1)})$ started from $x$ at time $s$.
