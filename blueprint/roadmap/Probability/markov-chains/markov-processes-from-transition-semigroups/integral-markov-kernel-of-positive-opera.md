# Kernel of a positive operator represents it on continuous functions

Let $E$ be a compact metrizable space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $T$ a positive continuous
linear operator on $C(E, \mathbb{R})$ with $T1 = 1$, and $K_T$ its [Markov
kernel](markov-kernel-of-positive-operator.md). Then for every continuous $f : E \to \mathbb{R}$ and
every $x \in E$,

$$\int_E f(y)\, K_T(x, dy) = (Tf)(x) .$$
