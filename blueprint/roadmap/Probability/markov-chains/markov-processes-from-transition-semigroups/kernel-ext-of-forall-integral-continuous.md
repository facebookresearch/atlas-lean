# Markov kernels are determined by integrals of continuous functions

Let $E$ be a compact metrizable space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let $K, L$ be Markov
kernels from $E$ to $E$. If $\int_E f(y)\, K(x, dy) = \int_E f(y)\, L(x, dy)$ for every continuous
$f : E \to \mathbb{R}$ and every $x \in E$, then $K = L$.
