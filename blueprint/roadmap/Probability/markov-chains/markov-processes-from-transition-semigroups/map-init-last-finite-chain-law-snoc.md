# Appending one time to a finite chain law

Let $K_t$ be a family of Markov kernels on $E$. For a start state $x$, times $\tau_0, \dots, \tau_n$ and an appended time $b$, the joint law of the first $n + 1$ states and the appended state under the finite chain law at times $(\tau, b)$ is the chain law at $\tau$ followed by one transition $K_{b - \tau_n}$ from the last state $\tau_n$ (truncated subtraction on nonnegative reals). The times need not be ordered.

## Sources

- S. N. Ethier and T. G. Kurtz, *Markov Processes: Characterization and Convergence*, Wiley, 1986, ch. 4, §1, (1.10).
