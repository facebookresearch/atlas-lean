# Increasing reindexing of a subset within a finite set

Let $J \subseteq I$ be finite subsets of $[0, \infty)$, with increasing enumerations
$j_0 < \dots < j_{|J|-1}$ and $i_0 < \dots < i_{|I|-1}$. The increasing reindexing of $J$ within $I$
is the strictly increasing map $e : \{0, \dots, |J| - 1\} \to \{0, \dots, |I| - 1\}$ with
$i_{e(a)} = j_a$ for every $a$.
