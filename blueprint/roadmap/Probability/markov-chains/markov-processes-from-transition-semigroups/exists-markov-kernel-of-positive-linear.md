# Representation of positive unital operators by Markov kernels

Let $E$ be a compact metrizable space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let
$T : C(E, \mathbb{R}) \to C(E, \mathbb{R})$ be a positive linear map ($f \ge 0$ implies $Tf \ge 0$)
with $T1 = 1$. Then there is a Markov kernel $K$ from $E$ to $E$ such that

$$\int_E f(y)\, K(x, dy) = (Tf)(x) \qquad \text{for every } f \in C(E, \mathbb{R}) \text{ and every } x \in E .$$

Continuity of $T$ is not assumed: it is not needed, since each $Tf$ is already continuous.
