# Finite-dimensional law of a Markov semigroup from a starting point

For kernels $(K_t)_{t \ge 0}$ on $E$, a starting point $x \in E$, a starting time $s \ge 0$ and
requested times $\mathbf t = (t_0, \dots, t_{n-1})$, the finite-dimensional law is the measure
$F_{s, \mathbf t}(x, \cdot)$ on $E^n$, where $F_{s, \mathbf t}$ is the [finite-dimensional
kernel](finite-chain-kernel.md).
