# Ordered finite projections of the Markov path measure

Let $E$ be a Polish space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, $(K_t)_{t \ge 0}$ Markov kernels on $E$
satisfying the Chapman–Kolmogorov equations, $x \in E$, and $J \subset [0, \infty)$ a finite set
with increasing enumeration $j_0 < \dots < j_{k-1}$. Then the image of the [path
measure](path-measure.md) $P_x$ under $\omega \mapsto (\omega_{j_0}, \dots, \omega_{j_{k-1}})$ is the
[finite-dimensional law](finite-chain-law.md) $F_{0, (j_0, \dots, j_{k-1})}(x, \cdot)$.
