# Positive operators on $C(E)$ are given by Markov kernels

Let $E$ be a compact metrizable space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let $T$ be a positive
continuous linear operator on $C(E, \mathbb{R})$ with $T1 = 1$. Then there is a Markov kernel $K$
from $E$ to $E$ with $\int_E f(y)\, K(x, dy) = (Tf)(x)$ for every continuous $f : E \to \mathbb{R}$
and every $x \in E$.
