# Markov kernel of a positive unital operator

Let $E$ be a compact metrizable space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra and $T$ a positive continuous
linear operator on $C(E, \mathbb{R})$ with $T1 = 1$. Its Markov kernel $K_T$ is a Markov kernel from
$E$ to $E$ with $\int_E f(y)\, K_T(x, dy) = (Tf)(x)$ for every continuous $f$ and every $x$; one
[exists](exists-markov-kernel-of-positive-operato.md), and it is
[unique](kernel-ext-of-forall-integral-continuous.md).
