# Energy comparison under a normal contraction

Let $S$ be a finite set, $m : S \to \mathbb{R}$ with $m(x) \ge 0$ for every $x$, and $Q$ a real
$S \times S$ matrix with $Q(x, y) \ge 0$ for $x \ne y$ and nonnegative [killing rate](killing-rate.md)
$k(x) = -\sum_y Q(x, y) \ge 0$. Let $u, v : S \to \mathbb{R}$, with $v$ a normal contraction of
$u$: $|v(x) - v(y)| \le |u(x) - u(y)|$ and $|v(x)| \le |u(x)|$ for all $x, y$. Then the
[form](form.md) of $(m, Q)$ satisfies

$$\mathcal E(v, v) \le \mathcal E(u, u) .$$

Every normal contraction operates on the form: this is condition (E.4)″ of Fukushima, Oshima and
Takeda for the form (1.2.27).

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, condition (E.4)″, and equation (1.2.27).
