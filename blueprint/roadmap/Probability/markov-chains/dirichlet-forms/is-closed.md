# Closed symmetric form

A [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) $\mathcal E$ on $L^2(X; m)$ is closed if every sequence
$u_n \in D[\mathcal E]$ that is Cauchy for $\mathcal E_1$ (for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is
$N$ with $\mathcal E_1(u_n - u_k, u_n - u_k) < \varepsilon$ for all $n, k \ge N$) converges for
$\mathcal E_1$ to some $w \in D[\mathcal E]$: $\mathcal E_1(u_n - w, u_n - w) \to 0$. Here
$\mathcal E_1$ is [the form $\mathcal E + (\cdot, \cdot)_{L^2(m)}$](energy-one.md). This is condition
(E.3) of Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, condition (E.3).
