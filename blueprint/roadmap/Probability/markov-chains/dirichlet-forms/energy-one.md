# The form $\mathcal E_1$

Let $\mathcal E$ be a [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$. For $u, v \in L^2(X; m)$,

$$\mathcal E_1(u, v) = \mathcal E(u, v) + (u, v)_{L^2(m)} ,$$

which is equation (1.1.1) of Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda with $\alpha = 1$.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, equation (1.1.1).
