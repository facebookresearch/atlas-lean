# Closed forms are closable

Every [closed](is-closed.md) [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$ is
[closable](is-closable.md), that is, satisfies condition (1.1.2) of Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda:
it is its own closed extension.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, condition (1.1.2).
