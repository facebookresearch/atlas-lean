# Exceptional sets of a symmetric form

Let $X$ be a topological space with a measure $m$ and $\mathcal E$ a [symmetric
form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$. A set $N \subseteq X$ is $\mathcal E$-exceptional if
$N \subseteq \bigcap_k (X \setminus F_k)$ for some [$\mathcal E$-nest](is-nest.md) $(F_k)$. A
property holds $\mathcal E$-quasi-everywhere if it holds outside an $\mathcal E$-exceptional set.

## Sources

- M. Röckner and B. Schmuland, *Quasi-regular Dirichlet forms: examples and counterexamples*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 47 (1995), 165–200, Definition 0.5(iii).
