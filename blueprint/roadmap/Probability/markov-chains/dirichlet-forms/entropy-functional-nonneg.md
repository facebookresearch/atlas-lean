# The entropy-like functional is nonnegative

Let $S$ be a finite set and $\pi$ a probability vector on $S$ ($\pi(x) \ge 0$,
$\sum_x \pi(x) = 1$). Then the [entropy-like functional](entropy-functional.md) satisfies
$\mathcal L_\pi(f) \ge 0$ for every $f : S \to \mathbb{R}$. It follows from
$t \log(t/N) \ge t - N$ for $t \ge 0$ and $N > 0$.

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and L. Saloff-Coste, *Logarithmic Sobolev inequalities for finite Markov chains*, Annals of Applied Probability 6 (1996), 695–750, Section 3.1.
