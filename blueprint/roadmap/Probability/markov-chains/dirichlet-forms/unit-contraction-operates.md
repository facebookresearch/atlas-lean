# The unit contraction operates on a form

Let $\mathcal E$ be a [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$. The unit contraction
operates on $\mathcal E$ if for every $u \in D[\mathcal E]$ the [unit contraction](unit-contraction-lp.md)
$v = (0 \vee u) \wedge 1$ lies in $D[\mathcal E]$ and $\mathcal E(v, v) \le \mathcal E(u, u)$. This is
condition (E.4)′ of Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda. By their Theorem 1.4.1 it is equivalent, for
closed forms, to their Markov condition (E.4). For closable forms that are not closed it is
strictly stronger than (E.4): they note in Section 1.1 that Markovian closable forms need not
satisfy (E.4)′.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, condition (E.4)′, and Theorem 1.4.1.
