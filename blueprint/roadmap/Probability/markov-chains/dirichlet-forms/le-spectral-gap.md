# Poincaré constants bound the spectral gap from below

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi$ a probability vector on $S$ ($\pi \ge 0$, $\sum_x \pi(x) = 1$), $P$ a real $S \times S$ matrix
and $c$ a real number. Suppose some function has nonzero [variance](weighted-variance.md)
$\operatorname{Var}_\pi$, and $c \operatorname{Var}_\pi(f) \le \mathcal E_{\pi, P - I}(f, f)$ for every
$f : S \to \mathbb{R}$, where $\mathcal E_{\pi, P - I}$ is the [form](form.md) of the generator
$P - I$. Then $c \le \lambda(P, \pi)$, the [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md).

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and L. Saloff-Coste, *Logarithmic Sobolev inequalities for finite Markov chains*, Annals of Applied Probability 6 (1996), 695–750, equation (1.4).
