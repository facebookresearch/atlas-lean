# Symmetric form on $L^2(X; m)$

Let $m$ be a measure on a measurable space $X$. A symmetric form on $L^2(X; m)$ consists of

1. a dense linear subspace $D[\mathcal E]$ of $L^2(X; m)$, the domain, and
2. a bilinear form $\mathcal E : L^2(X; m) \times L^2(X; m) \to \mathbb{R}$

such that $\mathcal E(u, v) = \mathcal E(v, u)$ and $\mathcal E(u, u) \ge 0$ for all
$u, v \in D[\mathcal E]$. These are conditions (E.1) and (E.2) of Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda. The
bilinear form is given on all of $L^2(X; m)$, but only its values on $D[\mathcal E]$ matter.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, conditions (E.1) and (E.2).
