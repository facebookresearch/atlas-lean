# Unit contraction on $L^2$

For $u \in L^2(X; m)$, the unit contraction $(0 \vee u) \wedge 1 \in L^2(X; m)$ is the class of the
function $x \mapsto \max(0, \min(u(x), 1))$, obtained by composing $u$ with the
[$1$-Lipschitz map](lipschitz-with-unit-clamp.md) $t \mapsto \max(0, \min(t, 1))$, which fixes $0$
and therefore maps $L^2(X; m)$ to itself.

## Sources
