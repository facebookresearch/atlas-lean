# Unit contraction

For a function $u : S \to \mathbb{R}$, its unit contraction is the function
$(0 \vee u) \wedge 1$, that is $x \mapsto \max(0, \min(u(x), 1))$.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1, condition (E.4)′.
