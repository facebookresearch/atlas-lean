# $\mathcal E_1$ dominates $\mathcal E$

Let $\mathcal E$ be a [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$. Then
$\mathcal E(u, u) \le \mathcal E_1(u, u)$ for every $u \in L^2(X; m)$, where
$\mathcal E_1(u, u) = \mathcal E(u, u) + \|u\|_{L^2(m)}^2$ is the [form $\mathcal E_1$](energy-one.md).
