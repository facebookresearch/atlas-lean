# Regular symmetric form

Let $X$ be a topological space with a measure $m$ and $\mathcal E$ a [symmetric
form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$. Then $\mathcal E$ is regular if $D[\mathcal E] \cap C_c(X)$
is $\mathcal E_1$-dense in $D[\mathcal E]$ and uniformly dense in $C_c(X)$, that is:

1. for every $u \in D[\mathcal E]$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $v \in D[\mathcal E]$ having a
   continuous compactly supported $m$-version with $\mathcal E_1(u - v, u - v) < \varepsilon$;
2. for every $g \in C_c(X)$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $f \in C_c(X)$, an $m$-version of an
   element of $D[\mathcal E]$, with $|f(x) - g(x)| < \varepsilon$ for every $x \in X$.
