# The log-Sobolev constant is nonnegative

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi$ a probability vector on $S$ ($\pi \ge 0$, $\sum_x \pi(x) = 1$) and
$P$ a real $S \times S$ matrix with nonnegative entries and rows summing to $1$. Then the
[log-Sobolev constant](log-sobolev-constant.md) satisfies $\alpha(P, \pi) \ge 0$.
