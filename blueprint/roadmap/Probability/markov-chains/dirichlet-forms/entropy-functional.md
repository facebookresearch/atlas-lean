# Entropy-like functional of the log-Sobolev inequality

Let $S$ be a finite set and $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$. For $f : S \to \mathbb{R}$ put
$\|f\|_2^2 = \sum_y \pi(y) f(y)^2$ and

$$\mathcal L_\pi(f) = \sum_{x \in S} f(x)^2 \log\frac{f(x)^2}{\|f\|_2^2}\, \pi(x),$$

with the conventions $\log 0 = 0$ and $t/0 = 0$: points where $f$ vanishes contribute $0$, and
$\mathcal L_\pi(f) = 0$ when $\|f\|_2^2 = 0$.

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and L. Saloff-Coste, *Logarithmic Sobolev inequalities for finite Markov chains*, Annals of Applied Probability 6 (1996), 695–750, equation (1.6).
