# Expansion of the entropy functional near the constant $1$

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi$ a probability vector on $S$ with $\pi(x) > 0$ for every $x$,
$g : S \to \mathbb{R}$, and $\varepsilon, A$ real numbers with $\varepsilon \ge 0$,
$|2 g(x) + \varepsilon g(x)^2| \le A$ for every $x$, and $\varepsilon A \le 1/2$. Then

$$\mathcal L_\pi(1 + \varepsilon g) \ge 2 \varepsilon^2 \operatorname{Var}_\pi(g) - \varepsilon^3 (2K + 8A^3), \qquad K = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{x, y} |g(x) - g(y)|\, |g(x)^2 - g(y)^2|\, \pi(x)\pi(y),$$

where $\mathcal L_\pi$ is the [entropy-like functional](entropy-functional.md) and
$\operatorname{Var}_\pi$ the [variance](weighted-variance.md).
