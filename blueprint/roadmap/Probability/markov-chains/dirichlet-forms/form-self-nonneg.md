# The form of a sub-Markovian generator is nonnegative

Let $S$ be a finite set, $m : S \to \mathbb{R}$ with $m \ge 0$, and $Q$ a real $S \times S$ matrix
with $Q(x, y) \ge 0$ for $x \ne y$ and nonnegative [killing rate](killing-rate.md)
$k(x) = -\sum_y Q(x, y) \ge 0$. Then the [form](form.md) of $(m, Q)$ satisfies
$\mathcal E(u, u) \ge 0$ for every $u : S \to \mathbb{R}$.
