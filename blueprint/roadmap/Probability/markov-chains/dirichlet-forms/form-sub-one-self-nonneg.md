# The Dirichlet form of a Markov kernel is nonnegative

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\pi \ge 0$, and $P$ a real $S \times S$
matrix with nonnegative entries and rows summing to $1$. Then the [form](form.md) of
$(\pi, P - I)$ satisfies $\mathcal E_{\pi, P - I}(f, f) \ge 0$ for every $f : S \to \mathbb{R}$.
