# Regular forms are quasi-regular

Let $X$ be a metrizable, locally compact, second-countable topological space with its Borel
$\sigma$-algebra, and $m$ a measure on $X$ that is finite on compact sets (a Radon measure). Every
[regular](is-regular.md) [Dirichlet form](is-dirichlet-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$ is
[quasi-regular](is-quasi-regular.md). Röckner and Schmuland state this (Proposition 0.9) for regular
semi-Dirichlet forms, citing Ma and Röckner, Chapter IV, Example 4a. Metrizability cannot be
dropped: Röckner and Schmuland (Remark 0.10 and Section 5(a)) give a regular Dirichlet form on a
separable compact non-metrizable Hausdorff space that is not quasi-regular.

## Sources

- M. Röckner and B. Schmuland, *Quasi-regular Dirichlet forms: examples and counterexamples*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 47 (1995), 165–200, Proposition 0.9, Remark 0.10 and Section 5(a).
- Z.-M. Ma and M. Röckner, *Introduction to the Theory of (Non-Symmetric) Dirichlet Forms*, Universitext, Springer, 1992, Chapter IV, Example 4a.
