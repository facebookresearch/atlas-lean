# Dirichlet form

A [symmetric form](symmetric-form.md) on $L^2(X; m)$ is a Dirichlet form if it is
[closed](is-closed.md) and [the unit contraction operates on it](unit-contraction-operates.md).
Fukushima, Oshima and Takeda define a Dirichlet form as a symmetric form on $L^2(X; m)$ which is
not only Markovian but also closed; for closed forms their Markov condition (E.4) and the unit
contraction condition (E.4)′ are equivalent, as they note in Section 1.1 and prove in Theorem
1.4.1.

## Sources

- M. Fukushima, Y. Oshima and M. Takeda, *Dirichlet Forms and Symmetric Markov Processes*, 2nd revised and extended ed., de Gruyter Studies in Mathematics 19, de Gruyter, 2011, Section 1.1 and Theorem 1.4.1.
