# Log-Sobolev inequality with the log-Sobolev constant

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi$ a probability vector on $S$ ($\pi \ge 0$, $\sum_x \pi(x) = 1$) and
$P$ a real $S \times S$ matrix with nonnegative entries and rows summing to $1$. Then for every
$f : S \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\alpha(P, \pi)\, \mathcal L_\pi(f) \le \mathcal E_{\pi, P - I}(f, f),$$

where $\alpha$ is the [log-Sobolev constant](log-sobolev-constant.md), $\mathcal L_\pi$ the
[entropy-like functional](entropy-functional.md) and $\mathcal E_{\pi, P - I}$ the [Dirichlet form
of $P$](form-sub-one.md).

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and L. Saloff-Coste, *Logarithmic Sobolev inequalities for finite Markov chains*, Annals of Applied Probability 6 (1996), 695–750, equations (1.6) and (1.7).
