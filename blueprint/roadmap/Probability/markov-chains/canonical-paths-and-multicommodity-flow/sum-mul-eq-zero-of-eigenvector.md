# Eigenvectors for eigenvalues other than $1$ have mean zero

If $P$ has unit row sums and satisfies detailed balance with respect to $\pi$, then $\pi$ is stationary, so $\sum_x \pi(x)(Pf)(x) = \sum_x \pi(x) f(x)$ for every $f$. Hence an eigenvector $f$ for an eigenvalue $\mu \ne 1$ has $\sum_x \pi(x) f(x) = 0$: it is orthogonal to the constants in the $\pi$-weighted inner product, as used in the proof of Theorem 5 of A. Sinclair, *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* 1 (1992).
