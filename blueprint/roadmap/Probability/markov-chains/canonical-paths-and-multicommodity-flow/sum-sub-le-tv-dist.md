# Excess mass on a set is at most the total variation distance

Let $S$ be a finite set and $\mu, \nu : S \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\sum_y \mu(y) = \sum_y \nu(y) = 1$
(the entries need not be nonnegative). Then for every $T \subseteq S$,

$$\sum_{z \in T} \bigl(\nu(z) - \mu(z)\bigr) \le d_{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{y \in S} |\mu(y) - \nu(y)| .$$
