# Telescoping along a path

Let $\psi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and consider the path that starts at $x$ and then visits
$v_1, \dots, v_k$, with [endpoint](path-end.md) $z$. Summing the increments
$\psi(v) - \psi(u)$ over its [oriented edges](path-edges.md) $(u, v)$ gives

$$\sum_{(u, v)} \bigl(\psi(v) - \psi(u)\bigr) = \psi(z) - \psi(x) .$$
