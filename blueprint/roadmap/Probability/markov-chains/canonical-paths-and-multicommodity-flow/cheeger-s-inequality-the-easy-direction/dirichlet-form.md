# Dirichlet form of a Markov kernel

For real weights $K : \alpha \times \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\pi : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ on a finite set $\alpha$ and a function $f : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$, the Dirichlet form is $\mathcal E(f) = \tfrac12 \sum_{x, y} \pi(x) K(x, y)\, (f(y) - f(x))^2$. It is the Dirichlet form of a Markov chain when $K$ is a Markov kernel and $\pi$ a stationary law; no such condition enters the definition.
