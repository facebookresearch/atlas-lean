# Detailed balance for powers

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $P$ a real $S \times S$ matrix with
$\pi(x) P(x, y) = \pi(y) P(y, x)$ for all $x, y$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and all
$x, y$,

$$\pi(x)\, P^n(x, y) = \pi(y)\, P^n(y, x) .$$

Sinclair uses this in the proof of his Lemma 7.

## Sources

- A. Sinclair, *Improved bounds for mixing rates of Markov chains and multicommodity flow*,
  Combinatorics, Probability and Computing 1 (1992), 351–370, proof of Lemma 7.
