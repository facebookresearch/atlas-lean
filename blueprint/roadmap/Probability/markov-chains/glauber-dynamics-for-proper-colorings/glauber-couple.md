# Synchronous coupling of two Glauber steps

For a simple graph $G$ on $n \ge 1$ vertices, $q \ge 1$ colors and colorings $c, d$, the synchronous coupling of two Glauber steps picks one uniformly random site $i$ and one uniformly random color $\kappa$ and applies the Glauber proposal $(i, \kappa)$ to both colorings, each with its own rejection test: the law of $\bigl(\text{move}(c, i, \kappa), \text{move}(d, i, \kappa)\bigr)$.
