# Hamming contraction of the Glauber coupling for proper colorings

Let $n, q > 0$. For Glauber dynamics on the proper $q$-colorings of a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices in
which every vertex has degree at most $\Delta$, the expected Hamming distance after one step of the synchronous
rejection coupling is at most $(1 - 1/n + 3\Delta/(n q))$ times the current Hamming distance. The
constant $3\Delta$ is specific to this coupling, and the factor is a strict contraction only when $q > 3\Delta$.
