# Expectations under the synchronous Glauber coupling

For a simple graph $G$ on $n \ge 1$ vertices, $q \ge 1$ colors, colorings $c, d$ and a real function $F$ on pairs of colorings, the expectation of $F$ under the synchronous Glauber coupling of $c$ and $d$ is $\frac{1}{nq} \sum_{i} \sum_{\kappa} F\bigl(\text{move}(c, i, \kappa), \text{move}(d, i, \kappa)\bigr)$, the uniform average over sites $i$ and proposed colors $\kappa$, where $\text{move}$ is the Glauber proposal.
