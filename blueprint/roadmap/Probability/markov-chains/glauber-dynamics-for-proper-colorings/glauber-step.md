# One Glauber step for proper colorings

For a simple graph $G$ on $n \ge 1$ vertices, $q \ge 1$ colors and a coloring $c$, one Glauber step (heat bath with rejection) picks a uniformly random site $i$ and a uniformly random color $\kappa$ and applies the Glauber proposal: recolor $i$ with $\kappa$ if the result is a proper coloring, otherwise stay at $c$. The step is the resulting probability mass function on colorings.
