# Step map stopped at a level

For a step map $f : S \times D \to S$, a level function $V : S \to [0, \infty]$ and a level $c$, the step map stopped at level $c$ leaves a state $x$ with $c \le V(x)$ unchanged and moves every other state by $f$: its value at $(x, d)$ is $x$ if $c \le V(x)$ and $f(x, d)$ otherwise.
