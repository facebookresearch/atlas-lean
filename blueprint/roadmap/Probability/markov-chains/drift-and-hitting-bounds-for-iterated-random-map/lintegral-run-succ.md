# Splitting off one shock of the input path

Let $D$ carry a probability law, let the inputs be independent with that law, and let $f : S \times D \to S$ be jointly measurable and $V : S \to [0, \infty]$ measurable. The expectation of $V$ at mark $n + 1$ of the chain started at $x_0$ equals the expectation, over the inputs up to mark $n$, of $\mathbb{E}_d[V(f(x_n, d))]$ for a fresh input $d$: integrating out one shock.
