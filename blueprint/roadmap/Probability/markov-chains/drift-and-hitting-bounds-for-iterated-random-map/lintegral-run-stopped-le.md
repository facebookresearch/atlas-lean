# Additive drift bound along the stopped chain

Let $D$ carry a probability law, let the inputs be independent with that law, and let $f : S \times D \to S$ be jointly measurable and $V : S \to [0, \infty]$ measurable, and let $c, \beta \in [0, \infty]$. If $\mathbb{E}[V(f(x, d))] \le V(x) + \beta$ at every state with $V(x) < c$, then the chain stopped at level $c$ and started at $x_0$ has $\mathbb{E}[V]$ at most $V(x_0) + n \beta$ at every mark $n$.
