# Irreducible chains have positive paths

Let $P$ be an [irreducible](is-irreducible.md) [stochastic matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) on the
states $\{0, \dots, M\}$. For any two states $i, j$ there is a path from $i$ to $j$ (a finite
list of states starting at $i$ and ending at $j$) whose [path product](list-path-product.md), the
product of the transition probabilities $P(x, y)$ over consecutive states $x, y$, is strictly
positive.
