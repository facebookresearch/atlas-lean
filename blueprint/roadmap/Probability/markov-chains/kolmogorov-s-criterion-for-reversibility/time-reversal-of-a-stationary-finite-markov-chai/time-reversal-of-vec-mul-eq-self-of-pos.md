# Time reversal of a stationary finite Markov chain, full support

Let $P$ be a row-stochastic matrix on a finite state set $\iota$ and $\pi$ a stationary probability vector for $P$, $\pi P = \pi$, with $\pi_i > 0$ for every state $i$. Then the reversed matrix $\hat P_{ij} = \pi_j P_{ji} / \pi_i$ is row-stochastic, and $\hat P = P$ if and only if $\pi$ and $P$ are in detailed balance: $\pi_i P_{ij} = \pi_j P_{ji}$ for all $i, j$. The second conclusion alone, $\hat P = P$ if and only if detailed balance holds, under the same hypotheses (including the case of the numbered states $\{0, \dots, M\}$), is also available on its own.
