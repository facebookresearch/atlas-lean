# Kolmogorov's cycle theorem, sufficiency

Let $P$ be an [irreducible](is-irreducible.md) [stochastic matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) on the
states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ satisfying [Kolmogorov's cycle condition](kolmogorov-cycle-condition.md).
Then $P$ is [reversible](is-reversible.md): there is a probability distribution $\pi$ on the
states that is stationary, $\pi P = \pi$, and satisfies the detailed balance equations
$\pi(i) P(i, j) = \pi(j) P(j, i)$ for all states $i, j$.
