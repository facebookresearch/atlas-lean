# Paths from positive matrix powers

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$. If $n > 0$
and the $(i, j)$ entry of the [matrix power](mat-pow.md) $P^n$ is positive, then there is a path
from $i$ to $j$ (a finite list of states starting at $i$ and ending at $j$) whose [path
product](list-path-product.md), the product of the transition probabilities $P(x, y)$ over
consecutive states $x, y$, is positive.
