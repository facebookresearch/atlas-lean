# Powers of a stochastic matrix are nonnegative

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$. For every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$ and all states $i, j$, the entry $P^n(i, j)$ of the [matrix power](mat-pow.md)
$P^n$ is nonnegative ($P^0$ being the identity matrix).
