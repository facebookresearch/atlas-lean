# Limit profile of the random transposition shuffle

Let $d_n(t)$ be the total variation distance from uniform after $t$ steps of a random walk on the symmetric group $S_n$ started at the identity. For the random transposition shuffle (apply the transposition of a uniform pair $(i, j)$), the claim (AimPL Problem 5.3) is that for every real $c$, $d_n(\lfloor\tfrac12n\log n + cn\rfloor) \to d_{TV}(\mathrm{Poi}(1 + e^{-2c}), \mathrm{Poi}(1))$.
