# Cutoff for the random-to-random shuffle

Let $d_n(t)$ be the total variation distance from uniform after $t$ steps of a random walk on the symmetric group $S_n$ started at the identity. For the random-to-random shuffle (a card at a uniform position is removed and reinserted at an independent uniform position), the claim (AimPL Conjecture 5.1) is that there is cutoff at $t_n = \tfrac34n\log n$: for every $\eta \in (0, 1)$, $d_n(\lceil(1 + \eta)t_n\rceil) \to 0$ and $d_n(\lfloor(1 - \eta)t_n\rfloor) \to 1$.
