# Tabloid Specht modules and all-copy subgroups on occupied boards

For a Young diagram $\lambda$ on a finite set of labels, the tabloid Hilbert space has orthonormal basis given by labellings modulo row permutations. The symmetric group acts by relabelling. Alternating the base tabloid by its column permutations gives the polytabloid; its orbit spans $V_\lambda$ with the inherited Hilbert norm. Relabel to the chosen occupied cells of a board via a bijection, and let the row and column subgroups preserve each occupied fiber separately.

For a unitary irreducible of either subgroup, an all-copy family in the restriction consists of isometric complex-linear equivariant embeddings with pairwise orthogonal image spaces such that the image of every intertwiner from that irreducible is contained in their sum. The families for the two different subgroups need not be mutually orthogonal. The overlap operators map via one embedding and then the adjoint of the other, and are measured in the induced operator norm.
