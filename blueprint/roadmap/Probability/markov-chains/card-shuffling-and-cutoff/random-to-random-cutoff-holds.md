# The random-to-random shuffle has cutoff at (3/4) n log n

Let $d_n(t)$ be the total variation distance from uniform after $t$ steps of a random walk on the symmetric group $S_n$ started at the identity. For the random-to-random shuffle there is total variation cutoff at $\tfrac34n\log n$ (Bernstein and Nestoridi, with Subag's lower bound).

## Sources

- Problem: AimPL: Markov chain mixing times, Conjecture 5.1 (E. Nestoridi), http://aimpl.org/markovmixing/5/.
- Resolution: M. Bernstein and E. Nestoridi, Cutoff for random to random card shuffle, Ann. Probab. 47 (2019), no. 5, 3303-3320, arXiv:1703.06210, https://doi.org/10.1214/19-AOP1340.
- Resolution (lower bound): E. Subag, A lower bound for the mixing time of the random-to-random insertions shuffle, Electron. J. Probab. 18 (2013), no. 20, 1-20, arXiv:1112.5847, https://doi.org/10.1214/EJP.v18-1950.
