# Transition semigroup of a continuous-time Markov chain

Let $Q$ be a real square matrix indexed by a finite set. Its transition function is the matrix
exponential

$$P(t) = e^{tQ} = \sum_{k \ge 0} \frac{t^k Q^k}{k!}, \qquad t \in \mathbb{R} .$$

When $Q$ is a rate matrix and $t \ge 0$, $P(t)$ is the transition matrix at time $t$ of the finite
continuous-time Markov chain with generator $Q$.
