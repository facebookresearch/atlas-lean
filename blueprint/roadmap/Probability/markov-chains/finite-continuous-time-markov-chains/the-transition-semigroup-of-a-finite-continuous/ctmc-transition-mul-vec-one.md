# Transition function fixes the constant vector

Let $Q$ be a real square matrix indexed by a finite set with $Q\mathbf 1 = 0$, where $\mathbf 1$ is
the constant vector with all entries $1$. Then $P(t)\, \mathbf 1 = \mathbf 1$ for every real $t$,
where $P(t) = e^{tQ}$ is the [transition function](ctmc-transition.md).
