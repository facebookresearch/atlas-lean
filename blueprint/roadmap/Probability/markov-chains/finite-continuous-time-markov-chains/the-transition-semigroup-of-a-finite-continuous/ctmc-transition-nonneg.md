# Nonnegativity of the transition function of a rate matrix

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](../rate-matrices-of-finite-continuous-time-markov-c/is-rate-matrix.md)
indexed by a finite set. For every $t \ge 0$, all entries of the [transition
function](ctmc-transition.md) $P(t) = e^{tQ}$ are nonnegative; this follows by uniformization.
