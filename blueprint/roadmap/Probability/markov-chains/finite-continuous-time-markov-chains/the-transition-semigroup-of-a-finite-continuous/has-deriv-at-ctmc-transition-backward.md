# Kolmogorov backward equation

Let $Q$ be a real square matrix indexed by a finite set, with [transition function](ctmc-transition.md)
$P(t) = e^{tQ}$. Then $P$ is differentiable at every real $t$, with

$$P'(t) = Q\, P(t) .$$

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Theorem 2.1.1.
