# Transition function of a rate matrix is stochastic

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](../rate-matrices-of-finite-continuous-time-markov-c/is-rate-matrix.md)
indexed by a finite set. For every $t \ge 0$, the [transition function](ctmc-transition.md)
$P(t) = e^{tQ}$ is a stochastic matrix: its entries are nonnegative and its rows sum to $1$.

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Theorem 2.1.2.
