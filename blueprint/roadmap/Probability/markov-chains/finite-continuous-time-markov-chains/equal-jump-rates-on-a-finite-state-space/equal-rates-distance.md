# Mean jump count recovered from the mismatch probability

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a mismatch probability $p$, write $m = n + 1$. The equal-rates distance is $-\frac{n}{m} \log\bigl(1 - \frac{m}{n} p\bigr)$, which recovers the mean number of jumps from the probability that the chain has left its start, below the saturation level $p < n/(n+1)$. For $n = 0$ the expression is $0$ by the convention for division by zero, and at or above saturation the real logarithm of a nonpositive number is read as $\log|y|$ (with $\log 0 = 0$).
