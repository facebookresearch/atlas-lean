# Equal jump rates give the sticky kernel

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and real $\mu$ and $t$, the equal-rates matrix on $n + 1$ states equals $c I + (1 - c)\, \mathbf 1 \pi^{\mathsf T}$ with $c = e^{-(n+1)\mu t/n}$ and $\pi_j = 1/(n+1)$. For $\mu, t \ge 0$, so that $c \in (0, 1]$, this is the sticky kernel: keep the current state with probability $c$, otherwise redraw a state uniformly.
