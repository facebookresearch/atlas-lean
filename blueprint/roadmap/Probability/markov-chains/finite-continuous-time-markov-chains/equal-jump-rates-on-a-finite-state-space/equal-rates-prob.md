# Transition probability under equal jump rates

On $n + 1$ states, with rate $\mu$ and time $t$, when all $n$ possible jumps are equally likely, write $m = n + 1$ and $c = e^{-m\mu t/n}$. The transition probability from $i$ to $j$ is $\frac1m + \frac nm c$ if $i = j$ and $\frac1m - \frac1m c$ if $i \ne j$. For $n = 0$ the exponent is $0$ by the convention for division by zero.
