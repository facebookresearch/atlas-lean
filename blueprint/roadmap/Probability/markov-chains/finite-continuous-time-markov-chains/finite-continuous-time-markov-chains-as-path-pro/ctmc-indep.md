# Early and late steps of the continuous-time chain are independent

For a rate matrix $Q$ of a finite-state continuous-time Markov chain and every $n$, under the law of the chain's i.i.d. input sequence of steps (a jump choice and a standard exponential clock per step), the σ-algebra generated by the first $n$ steps is independent of the σ-algebra generated by the steps from index $n$ on.
