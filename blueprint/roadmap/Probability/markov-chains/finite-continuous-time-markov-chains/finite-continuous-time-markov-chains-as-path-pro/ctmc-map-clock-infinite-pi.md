# Every clock of an i.i.d. step sequence is standard exponential

Let $\iota$ be a measurable space of states (not necessarily finite) and $\nu$ a probability step law on pairs of a jump map $\iota \to \iota$ and a real clock whose clock coordinate is standard exponential. Under the i.i.d. law $\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$ of the step sequence, every clock $E_k$ is standard exponential.
