# Jump chain of the shifted steps

For a set $S$, $i \in S$, a step sequence $\omega$ and $n, m \in \mathbb{N}$, the [jump
chain](ctmc-jump-chain.md) started at $Y_n(i, \omega)$ and driven by the [shifted
steps](ctmc-shift.md) $\theta_n \omega$ is the jump chain after time $n$:
$Y_m(Y_n(i, \omega), \theta_n \omega) = Y_{n+m}(i, \omega)$.
