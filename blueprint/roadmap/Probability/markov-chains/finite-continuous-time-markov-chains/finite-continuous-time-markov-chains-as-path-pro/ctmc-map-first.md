# First step and remaining steps of the continuous-time chain

For a rate matrix $Q$ of a finite-state continuous-time Markov chain, under the law of the chain's i.i.d. input sequence of steps, the pair formed by the first step and the sequence of remaining steps has the product law of the one-step law and the law of the whole input sequence: the first step is independent of the rest, and the rest is distributed like the whole sequence.
