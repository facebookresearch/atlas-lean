# Law of the driving step sequence of a Markov chain

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](../rate-matrices-of-finite-continuous-time-markov-c/is-rate-matrix.md) on a
finite set $S$. The law of the step sequence is the infinite product measure

$$\mathbb P = \bigotimes_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mu$$

on sequences $\omega = ((\xi_n, E_n))_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ with $\xi_n : S \to S$ and
$E_n \in \mathbb{R}$, where $\mu$ is the [law of one step](ctmc-step-measure.md): the steps are
independent and identically distributed.
