# Non-explosion

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](../rate-matrices-of-finite-continuous-time-markov-c/is-rate-matrix.md) on a
finite set $S$ and $i \in S$. Under the [law of the step sequence](ctmc-input-measure.md), almost
surely the [jump times](ctmc-jump-time.md) $T_n$ of the chain started at $i$ tend to $\infty$ as
$n \to \infty$; hence the [path](ctmc-path.md) $X_t$ is a state of the jump chain for every
$t \ge 0$.

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Section 2.7.
