# Additivity of jump times under shifting

Let $Q$ be a real square matrix indexed by a set $S$, $i \in S$, $\omega$ a step sequence and
$n, m \in \mathbb{N}$. Then, in $[0, \infty]$,

$$T_{n+m}(i, \omega) = T_n(i, \omega) + T_m\bigl(Y_n(i, \omega), \theta_n \omega\bigr),$$

where $T$ are the [jump times](ctmc-jump-time.md), $Y$ the [jump chain](ctmc-jump-chain.md) and
$\theta_n \omega$ the [shifted steps](ctmc-shift.md).
