# Early and late steps of an i.i.d. step sequence are independent

Let $\iota$ be a measurable space of states (not necessarily finite) and $\nu$ a probability step law on pairs of a jump map $\iota \to \iota$ and a real clock. Under the i.i.d. law $\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$ of the step sequence, the σ-algebra generated by the first $n$ steps is independent of the σ-algebra generated by the steps from index $n$ on, for every $n$.
