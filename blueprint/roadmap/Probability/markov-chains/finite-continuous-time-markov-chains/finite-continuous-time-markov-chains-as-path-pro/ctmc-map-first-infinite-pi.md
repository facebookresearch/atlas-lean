# First step and remaining steps of an i.i.d. step sequence

For a step law $\nu$, under the i.i.d. law $\nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$ of the step sequence, the pair formed by the first step and the shifted sequence of remaining steps has the product law $\nu \otimes \nu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$: the first step is independent of the rest, and the rest has the law of the whole sequence.
