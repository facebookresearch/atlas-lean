# Holding times of the shifted steps

Let $Q$ be a real square matrix indexed by a set $S$, $i \in S$, $\omega$ a step sequence and
$n, m \in \mathbb{N}$. The $m$-th [holding time](ctmc-holding-time.md) of the chain started at the
jump-chain state $Y_n(i, \omega)$ and driven by the [shifted steps](ctmc-shift.md) $\theta_n \omega$
is the $(n + m)$-th holding time of the original chain:
$S_m(Y_n(i, \omega), \theta_n \omega) = S_{n+m}(i, \omega)$.
