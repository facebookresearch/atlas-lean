# Invariance under the semigroup

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ a row vector and $Q$ a real $S \times S$ matrix,
with [transition function](../the-transition-semigroup-of-a-finite-continuous/ctmc-transition.md)
$P(t) = e^{tQ}$. Then $\pi Q = 0$ if and only if $\pi P(t) = \pi$ for every real $t$.

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Section 3.5.
