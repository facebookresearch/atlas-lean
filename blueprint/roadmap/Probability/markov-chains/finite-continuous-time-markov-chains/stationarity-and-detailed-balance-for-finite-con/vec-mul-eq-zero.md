# Detailed balance implies invariance for the generator

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $Q$ a real $S \times S$ matrix whose rows sum
to $0$, in [detailed balance](is-detailed-balance.md) with $\pi$. Then $\pi Q = 0$.

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Section 3.7.
