# Detailed balance implies invariance for the transition semigroup

Let $S$ be a finite set, $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $Q$ a real $S \times S$ matrix whose rows sum
to $0$ (for instance a rate matrix), in [detailed balance](is-detailed-balance.md) with $\pi$. Then
$\pi P(t) = \pi$ for every real $t$, where $P(t) = e^{tQ}$ is the [transition
function](../the-transition-semigroup-of-a-finite-continuous/ctmc-transition.md).
