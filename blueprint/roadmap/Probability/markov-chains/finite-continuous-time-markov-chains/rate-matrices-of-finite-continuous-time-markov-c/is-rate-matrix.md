# Rate matrix

A real square matrix $Q$ indexed by a finite set $S$ is a rate matrix (a $Q$-matrix in Norris's
terminology) if

1. $Q(i, j) \ge 0$ for all $i \ne j$ (the jump rates are nonnegative), and
2. $\sum_{j \in S} Q(i, j) = 0$ for every $i \in S$ (each row sums to $0$).

## Sources

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics 2, Cambridge University Press, 1997, Section 2.1.
