# Uniformization of a rate matrix

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](is-rate-matrix.md) indexed by a finite set $S$ and $c$ a real number with
$-Q(i, i) \le c$ for every $i \in S$. Then every entry of $Q + cI$ is nonnegative.

## Sources

- A. Jensen, *Markoff chains as an aid in the study of Markoff processes*, Skandinavisk Aktuarietidskrift 36 (1953), 87–91.
