# Mean hitting time from inside the target set is zero

Let $Q$ be a [rate matrix](../rate-matrices-of-finite-continuous-time-markov-c/is-rate-matrix.md) on a finite set $S$, with exit rates $q(i) = -Q(i, i)$ and [jump matrix](../finite-continuous-time-markov-chains-as-path-pro/ctmc-jump-matrix.md) $\Pi$. Write $\mathbb E_i$ for the expectation, under the [law of the step sequence](../finite-continuous-time-markov-chains-as-path-pro/ctmc-input-measure.md), of a functional of the chain started at $i$, and $H_A$ for the [hitting time](ctmc-hitting-time.md) of $A \subseteq S$, valued in $[0, \infty]$. Let $A \subseteq S$ and $i \in A$. Then $\mathbb E_i H_A = 0$.
