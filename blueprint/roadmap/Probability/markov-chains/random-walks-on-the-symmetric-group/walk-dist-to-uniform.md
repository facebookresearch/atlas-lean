# Distance to uniformity of a random walk on a finite group

Let $G$ be a finite group, $\mu : G \to \mathbb{R}$ any function, $P(x, y) = \mu(x^{-1}y)$, and $t \in \mathbb{N}$. The distance to uniformity after $t$ steps from the identity is

$$d(t) = \frac12 \sum_{y \in G} \Big| P^t(1, y) - \frac{1}{|G|} \Big|.$$

When $\mu$ is a probability distribution on $G$, this is the total variation distance between the law of the walk at time $t$ and the uniform distribution on $G$.
