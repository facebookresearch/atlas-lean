# Transition matrix of a random walk on a group

Let $G$ be a group and $\mu : G \to \mathbb{R}$. The transition matrix of the random walk on $G$ with step law $\mu$, which multiplies the current state on the right by an independent step of law $\mu$, is

$$P(x, y) = \mu(x^{-1} y), \qquad x, y \in G.$$
