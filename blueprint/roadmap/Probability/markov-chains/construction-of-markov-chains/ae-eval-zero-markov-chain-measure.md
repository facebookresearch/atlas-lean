# Canonical Markov law started from a point starts there almost surely

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, 1, \dots, M\}$, let $\kappa_P$ be its [transition
kernel](../finite-markov-chains/finite-chain-kernel.md), and let $i$ be a state. Under the
[canonical Markov chain measure](markov-chain-measure.md) $P_{\delta_i}$ on the path space
$\{0, \dots, M\}^{\mathbb{N}}$ with initial distribution the point mass $\delta_i$ and transition
kernel $\kappa_P$, almost every path $\omega$ satisfies $\omega_0 = i$.
