# A Markov kernel run on a Poisson clock is a pure-jump Markov process

Let $S$ be a Polish space, $\mu$ a probability measure on $S$, $c > 0$ and $K$ a Markov kernel on $S$. Let $Y_0, Y_1, \dots$ be a Markov chain with initial law $\mu$ and
kernel $K$, given by the [Markov chain construction theorem](markov-chain-construction.md), independent of a [Poisson process](../../point-processes/poisson-processes/is-poisson-process.md) of rate $c$ with ring times $0 < T_1 < T_2 < \dots$, and set $X_t = Y_j$ for
$T_j \le t < T_{j+1}$ ($T_0 = 0$). Then $X$ has piecewise-constant càdlàg paths with finitely many jumps on bounded intervals, and it is a time-homogeneous
Markov process for the filtration $\mathcal F_t$ generated by the clock up to time $t$ and $Y_0, \dots, Y_{N_t}$, with generator $c(K - I)$ on bounded measurable functions,
by the memorylessness of the clock and the Markov property of $Y$. For measurable $A \subseteq S \times S$ let $N_A(t) = \#\{j \ge 1 : T_j \le t,\ (Y_{j-1}, Y_j) \in A\}$, counting self-transitions. Then for
every $\mathcal F$-stopping time $\tau$ with $\mathbb E\tau < \infty$,

$$\mathbb E\, N_A(\tau) = \mathbb E \int_0^\tau c\, K\bigl(X_s, A_{X_s}\bigr)\, ds, \qquad A_x = \{y : (x, y) \in A\},$$

because $N_A(t) - \int_0^t c K(X_s, A_{X_s})\, ds$ is a martingale with jumps bounded by $1$ and compensator rate at most $c$, so optional stopping applies. Taking $A$ inside the
off-diagonal $\{x \ne y\}$, which is measurable because $S$ is Polish, counts only genuine changes of state. For example, if $S$ is a single point and
$K$ is the identity, the off-diagonal count is $0$, while $A = S \times S$ counts every ring and both sides equal $c\, \mathbb E\tau$.
