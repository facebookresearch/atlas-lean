# Interchange process: distance to uniform

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, let $S$ be the group of permutations of the vertex set $\{0, 1\}^n$ of the hypercube, and let $L$ be the generator of the unit-rate interchange process on the hypercube. The distance to uniformity at time $t$ is the total variation distance between the law $e^{tL}(\mathrm{id}, \cdot)$ of the process started at the identity and the uniform distribution on $S$:

$$d(t) = \frac12 \sum_{\tau \in S} \Big| e^{tL}(\mathrm{id}, \tau) - \frac{1}{|S|} \Big|, \qquad |S| = (2^n)!.$$
