# Mixing time of the interchange process on the hypercube

The interchange process on the hypercube $\{0, 1\}^n$ assigns distinct labels to the vertices and, at rate $1$ for each edge, exchanges the labels at its endpoints; $t_{\mathrm{mix}}(n)$ is the time at which its total variation distance from the uniform distribution on permutations first falls below $1/e$. The claim (Wilson's conjecture, AimPL Problem 4.2) is that there are constants $0 < c \le C$ with $cn \le t_{\mathrm{mix}}(n) \le Cn$ for every $n \ge 1$.
