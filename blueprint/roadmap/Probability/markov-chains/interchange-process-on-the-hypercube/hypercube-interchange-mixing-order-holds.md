# The interchange process on the hypercube mixes in linear time

The interchange process on the hypercube $\{0, 1\}^n$ assigns distinct labels to the vertices and, at rate $1$ for each edge, exchanges the labels at its endpoints; $t_{\mathrm{mix}}(n)$ is the time at which its total variation distance from the uniform distribution on permutations first falls below $1/e$. There are constants $0 < c \le C$ with $cn \le t_{\mathrm{mix}}(n) \le Cn$ for all $n \ge 1$ (Hermon and Salez, 2021).
