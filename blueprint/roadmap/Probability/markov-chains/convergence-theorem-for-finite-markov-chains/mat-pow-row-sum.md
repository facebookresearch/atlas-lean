# Powers of a stochastic matrix are stochastic

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every state $i$, the row $i$ of the
[matrix power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md) $P^n$ sums to $1$:
$\sum_j P^n(i, j) = 1$.
