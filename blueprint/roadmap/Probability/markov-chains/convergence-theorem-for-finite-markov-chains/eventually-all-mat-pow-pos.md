# Powers of an ergodic chain are eventually all positive

Let $P$ be an [ergodic](is-ergodic.md) [stochastic
matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md) on the states
$\{0, \dots, M\}$. Then there is $w > 0$ such that for every $m \ge w$ all entries of the
[matrix power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md) $P^m$ are strictly positive.
