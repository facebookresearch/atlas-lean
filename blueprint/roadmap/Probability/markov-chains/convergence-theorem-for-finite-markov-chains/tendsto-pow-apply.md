# Convergence of matrix powers to the stationary distribution

Let $S$ be a finite set and $P$ a stochastic $S \times S$ matrix (nonnegative entries, rows
summing to $1$) that is primitive: some power $P^k$ with $k > 0$ has all entries strictly
positive. Let $\pi$ be a stationary distribution: $\pi \ge 0$, $\sum_y \pi(y) = 1$ and
$\pi P = \pi$. Then for all $x, y \in S$,

$$P^n(x, y) \to \pi(y) \qquad (n \to \infty) .$$
