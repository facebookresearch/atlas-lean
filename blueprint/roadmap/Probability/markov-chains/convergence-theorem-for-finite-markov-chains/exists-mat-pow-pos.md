# Pairwise positive powers of an ergodic chain

Let $P$ be an [ergodic](is-ergodic.md) [stochastic
matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md) on the states
$\{0, \dots, M\}$. Then for every pair of states $i, j$ there is $n > 0$ with $P^n(i, j) > 0$;
that is, $P$ is [irreducible](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/is-irreducible.md).
This per-pair condition is strictly weaker than ergodicity, which asks for one power working for
all pairs at once.
