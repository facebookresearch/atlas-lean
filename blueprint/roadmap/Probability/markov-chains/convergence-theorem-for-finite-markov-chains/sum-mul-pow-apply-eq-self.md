# Sum mul pow apply eq self

Let $S$ be a finite set, $R$ a semiring, $P$ an $S \times S$ matrix over $R$ and $\pi : S \to R$ a row vector with $\pi P = \pi$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $y \in S$,

$$\sum_{x \in S} \pi(x)\, (P^n)_{x y} = \pi(y),$$

the $y$-coordinate of the identity $\pi P^n = \pi$. No stochasticity of $P$ and no nonnegativity or normalization of $\pi$ is needed.
