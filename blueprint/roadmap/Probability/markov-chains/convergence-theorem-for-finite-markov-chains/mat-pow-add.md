# Additivity of matrix powers of a stochastic matrix

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ with [matrix powers](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md)
$P^n$. For all $a, b \in \mathbb{N}$ and all states $i, j$,

$$P^{a+b}(i, j) = \sum_{k} P^a(i, k)\, P^b(k, j) .$$
