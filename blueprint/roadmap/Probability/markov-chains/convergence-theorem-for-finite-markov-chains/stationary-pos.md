# Stationary distribution of a primitive chain has full support

Let $S$ be a finite set and $P$ a real $S \times S$ matrix that is primitive: its entries are
nonnegative and some power $P^k$ with $k > 0$ has all entries strictly positive. If
$\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $\pi(y) \ge 0$ for every $y$, $\sum_y \pi(y) = 1$ and
$\pi P = \pi$, then $\pi(y) > 0$ for every $y \in S$.
