# Power of a stochastic matrix

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ and $p \in \mathbb{N}$. The $p$-th power of $P$ is the stochastic
matrix with entries the [matrix power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md)
$P^p(i, j)$; its entries are nonnegative and its rows sum to $1$ (for $p = 0$ it is the identity
matrix).
