# First power of a stochastic matrix

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$. Its first [matrix
power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md) is $P$ itself:
$P^1(i, j) = P(i, j)$ for all states $i, j$.
