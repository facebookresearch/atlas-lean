# A stationary vector is invariant under repeated transition

Let $S$ be a finite index set and $P$ an $S \times S$ matrix over a semiring. If the row vector $\pi$ satisfies $\pi P = \pi$, then $\pi P^n = \pi$ for every $n \ge 0$. No stochasticity of $P$ and no nonnegativity or normalization of $\pi$ is required. In coordinates: $\sum_{x \in S} \pi(x)\, (P^n)_{xy} = \pi(y)$ for every $n$ and every $y \in S$.
