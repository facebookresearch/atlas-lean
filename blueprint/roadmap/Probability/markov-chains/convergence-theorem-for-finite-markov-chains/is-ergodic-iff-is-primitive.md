# Ergodicity is primitivity of the transition matrix

A [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md) $P$ on
the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ is [ergodic](is-ergodic.md) if and only if its [underlying real
matrix](to-matrix.md) is primitive, that is, has nonnegative entries and some power $P^k$ with
$k > 0$ has all entries strictly positive. Nonnegativity holds for every stochastic matrix, so
the two conditions coincide.
