# Underlying matrix of a stochastic matrix power

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ and $p \in \mathbb{N}$. The [underlying real matrix](to-matrix.md) of
the [stochastic matrix $P^p$](pow.md) is the $p$-th power of the underlying real matrix of $P$.
