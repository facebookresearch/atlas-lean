# Powers of a power of a stochastic matrix

Let $P$ be a [stochastic matrix](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/stochastic-matrix.md)
on the states $\{0, \dots, M\}$ and $p, n \in \mathbb{N}$. The $n$-th [matrix
power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md) of the [stochastic matrix
$P^p$](pow.md) is $P^{pn}$: $(P^p)^n(i, j) = P^{pn}(i, j)$ for all states $i, j$.
