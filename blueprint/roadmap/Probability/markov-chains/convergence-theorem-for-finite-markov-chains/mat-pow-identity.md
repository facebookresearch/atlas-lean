# Powers of the identity chain are the identity matrix

For every $M$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-th [matrix
power](../kolmogorov-s-criterion-for-reversibility/mat-pow.md) of the [identity chain](identity.md)
on $\{0, \dots, M\}$ is the identity matrix: its $(i, j)$ entry is $1$ if $i = j$ and $0$ otherwise.
