# Conductance of a set for a Markov kernel

For a kernel $K$ and a weight $\pi$ on a finite set $\alpha$, the conductance of a set $S$ is the stationary flow out of $S$ divided by the stationary mass of $S$, $\Phi(S) = \sum_{x \in S} \sum_{y \notin S} \pi(x) K(x, y) \big/ \sum_{x \in S} \pi(x)$, which is $0$ when the mass of $S$ is $0$ by the convention for division by zero.
