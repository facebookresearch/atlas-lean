# Square integrability of ordinary time integrals

For a real process $H$ indexed by nonnegative time, let

$$D(H)_t(\omega)=\int_0^t H_s(\omega)\,ds.$$

Write $e_T(H)=\int_\Omega\int_{(0,T]}|H_s(\omega)|^2\,ds\,dP(\omega)$ for its extended nonnegative time-and-sample energy.

Let $P$ be any measure and $H$ a real progressively measurable process for a filtration on the same space. Suppose every path is continuous and $e_t(H)<\infty$. Then the actual random variable $D(H)_t$ belongs to $L^2(P)$.

Adaptedness of the primitive supplies its measurability in the ambient sigma algebra. The squared-integral estimate supplies its finite second moment. No probability normalization, finite total measure, or complete filtration is assumed.
