# Uniform bound for the ordinary primitive

For continuous $H:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ and $T\ge0$,

$$\sup_{0\le t\le T}\left|\int_0^t H_s\,ds\right|^2\le T\int_{(0,T]}|H_s|^2\,ds.$$

Each fixed-time Cauchy–Schwarz bound is enlarged to the terminal horizon by monotonicity of the nonnegative integral and $t\le T$. The supremum is taken in the extended nonnegative reals, so its definition does not require a prior boundedness proof. The theorem proves the bound rather than assuming it. A constant integrand attains equality.
