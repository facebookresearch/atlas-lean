# Adaptedness of ordinary time integrals

For a real process $H$ indexed by nonnegative time, let

$$D(H)_t(\omega)=\int_0^t H_s(\omega)\,ds.$$

Let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0}$ be a filtration on a measurable space $\Omega$. If $H$ is progressively measurable, then $D(H)_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable for every $t\ge0$.

No measure, filtration completeness, or path continuity is assumed. The integral is the actual Bochner integral, including its totalized value when a path is not integrable. The measurability proof clips time at $t$ and uses measurability of integration over the fixed finite interval. This regularity result alone makes no assertion of path integrability.
