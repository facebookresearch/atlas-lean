# Continuity of the ordinary primitive

If $H:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, then

$$t\longmapsto\int_0^t H_s\,ds$$

is continuous on $[0,\infty)$, including at zero. The proof continuously extends $H$ to real time by $H(\max(s,0))$, applies the fundamental theorem of calculus to its primitive, and restricts back to nonnegative time. The integrals on every finite interval are genuinely integrable.
