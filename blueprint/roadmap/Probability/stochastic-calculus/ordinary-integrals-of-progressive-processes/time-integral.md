# Progressiveness of ordinary time integrals

For a real process $H$ indexed by nonnegative time, let

$$D(H)_t(\omega)=\int_0^t H_s(\omega)\,ds.$$

Let $H$ be progressively measurable for a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0}$, with continuous paths for every sample. Then $D(H)$ is progressively measurable for that same filtration.

The primitive is adapted by the measurability-of-integration result and has continuous paths by the fundamental theorem of calculus. Continuous adapted real processes are progressive. No completeness or right continuity of the filtration is required, and no almost-sure continuity assumption is silently strengthened to every-path continuity.
