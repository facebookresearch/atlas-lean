# Squared ordinary-integral bound

For a continuous real function $H$ on nonnegative time and $t\ge0$,

$$\left|\int_0^t H_s\,ds\right|^2\le t\int_{(0,t]}|H_s|^2\,ds.$$

This is Cauchy–Schwarz against the constant function one on the interval. Continuity ensures square integrability there. The formal statement uses extended nonnegative real values, includes $t=0$, and keeps the exact factor $t$. Constant integrands attain equality.
