# Almost sure Hölder bound on the dyadic rationals

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space and $X = (X_t)_{t \in \mathbb{R}}$ a real process, and let
$\alpha, \beta > 0$ and $K \ge 0$ be such that for all $s, t$ the variable $|X_s - X_t|^\beta$ is
integrable and

$$\mathbb E |X_s - X_t|^\beta \le K |s - t|^{1 + \alpha} .$$

Then for every $\gamma$ with $0 < \gamma < \alpha/\beta$, almost surely there is $C > 0$ with
$|X_q - X_r| \le C |q - r|^\gamma$ for all [dyadic rationals](dyadic-rationals.md) $q, r \in [0, 1]$: a single
full-measure set carries the Hölder bound for the original process.
