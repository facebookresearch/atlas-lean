# Hölder bound on dyadics for a Kolmogorov process

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $\alpha > 0$, $\beta$ real and $M \ge 0$, and let
$X = (X_t)_{t \in \mathbb{R}}$ be a real process such that for all $s, t$ the pair $(X_s, X_t)$ is measurable
and $\mathbb E |X_s - X_t|^\beta \le M |s - t|^{1 + \alpha}$ (an expectation in $[0, \infty]$). Then for every
$\gamma$ with $0 < \gamma < \alpha/\beta$, almost surely there is $C > 0$ with

$$|X_q - X_r| \le C |q - r|^\gamma \qquad \text{for all dyadic rationals } q, r \in [0, 1] .$$

No claim is made about the paths at non-dyadic times.
