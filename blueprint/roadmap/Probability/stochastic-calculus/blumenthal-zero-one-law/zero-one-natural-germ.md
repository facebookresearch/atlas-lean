# Blumenthal zero-one law

Let $E$ be a separable pseudometric abelian group with continuous addition and subtraction and its
Borel $\sigma$-algebra, and let $B$ be a [continuous process with independent
increments](is-continuous-independent-increment-proc.md) in $E$, adapted to a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)$, with
deterministic initial value $B_0 = x$ almost surely and measurable coordinates. Then every event $A$ of the
[natural germ $\sigma$-algebra](natural-germ.md) $\mathcal F^B_{0+}$ has $\mu(A) = 0$ or $\mu(A) = 1$. This
includes the classical zero–one law for the natural filtration of Brownian motion.
