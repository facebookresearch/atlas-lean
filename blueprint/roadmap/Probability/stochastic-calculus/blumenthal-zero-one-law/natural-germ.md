# Germ sigma-algebra of the natural filtration

For a process $B$ with measurable coordinates, its natural germ $\sigma$-algebra is the [germ
$\sigma$-algebra at zero](germ-sigma-algebra-zero.md) of its [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md):
$\mathcal F^B_{0+} = \bigcap_{t > 0} \sigma(B_u : u \le t)$.
