# Natural filtration is the smallest adapted filtration

If $B$ is a [continuous process with independent increments](is-continuous-independent-increment-proc.md)
adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$, with measurable coordinates, then its [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md)
satisfies $\mathcal F^B_t \subseteq \mathcal F_t$ for every $t \ge 0$.
