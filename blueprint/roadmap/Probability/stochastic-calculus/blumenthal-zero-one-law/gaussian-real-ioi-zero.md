# Half-line mass of a centered Gaussian

For every variance $v > 0$, the centered Gaussian law $N(0, v)$ gives mass $1/2$ to $(0, \infty)$, by symmetry
and because it has no atoms. This is the value $P(B_t > 0) = 1/2$ used in the proof that [Brownian motion
oscillates immediately after time zero](ae-exists-pos-and-neg.md).
