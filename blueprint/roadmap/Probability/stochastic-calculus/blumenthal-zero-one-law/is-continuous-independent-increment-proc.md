# Continuous process with independent increments

Let $E$ be an abelian group equipped with a $\sigma$-algebra and a topology (no compatibility between
these structures and the group operations is assumed), $(\mathcal F_t)_{t \ge 0}$ a filtration on a measurable
space $\Omega$, $\mu$ a measure and $x \in E$. A process $B = (B_t)_{t \ge 0}$ with values in $E$ is a continuous
process with independent increments started at $x$ and adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$ if

1. $\mu$ is a probability measure;
2. $B_0 = x$ $\mu$-almost surely;
3. $B$ is adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$;
4. for all $s < t$, the increment $B_t - B_s$ is independent of $\mathcal F_s$;
5. $\mu$-almost every path $t \mapsto B_t$ is continuous.

No Gaussian-law or stationary-increment condition is imposed.
