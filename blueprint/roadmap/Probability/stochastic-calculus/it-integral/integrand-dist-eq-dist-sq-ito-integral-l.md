# Itô isometry for Itô-approximable integrands

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H, K$ be [Itô-approximable](is-ito-approximable.md) on $[0, t]$. Then the [squared
$L^2(P \otimes du)$ distance](integrand-dist.md) between $H$ and $K$ on $(0, t] \times \Omega$ equals
$\bigl\| \int_0^t H\, dB - \int_0^t K\, dB \bigr\|_{L^2(P)}^2$, the squared distance of their [$L^2$ Itô
integrals](ito-integral-l2.md).
