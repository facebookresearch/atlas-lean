# Integrand distance as a squared $L^2$ seminorm

Let $P$ be a finite measure and $X, Y$ real processes whose [restrictions to $(0, t]$](restrict-ioc.md) are jointly
measurable. Then the [integrand distance](integrand-dist.md) is the squared $L^2$ seminorm of $X - Y$ on
$\Omega \times (0, t]$ with the product of $P$ and Lebesgue measure:

$$d_t(X, Y) = \| X - Y \|_{L^2(P \otimes du|_{(0, t]})}^2 .$$
