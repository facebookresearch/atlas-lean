# Itô isometry for elementary integrands, distance form

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H, K$ be [elementary integrands](elementary-integrand.md) and $t \ge 0$. Then the [squared
$L^2(P \otimes du)$ distance](integrand-dist.md) between their processes on $(0, t] \times \Omega$ equals the
squared $L^2(P)$ distance between their [elementary Itô integrals](ito-integral.md) at time $t$.
