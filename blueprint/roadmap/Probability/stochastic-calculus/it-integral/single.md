# Single-step elementary integrand

For a filtration $F$, times $a \le b$, an $F_a$-measurable $\xi$ and $C$ with $|\xi| \le C$, the single-step
integrand is the [elementary integrand](elementary-integrand.md) with one interval $(a, b]$, coefficient $\xi$
and bound $C$: the process $\xi\, \mathbf 1_{(a, b]}$.
