# Convergence of truncations

Let $H$ be a jointly measurable real process with $\mathbb E \int_0^t H_u^2\, du < \infty$. Then the truncations
$\tau_M(H) = H\, \mathbf 1_{\{-M < H \le M\}}$ converge to $H$ in $L^2(P \otimes du)$ on $(0, t] \times \Omega$ as
$M \to \infty$ through the natural numbers ([integrand distance](integrand-dist.md) tending to $0$).
