# Measurability of the $L^2$ Itô integral

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H$ be [Itô-approximable](is-ito-approximable.md) on $[0, t]$. Then the [$L^2$ Itô
integral](ito-integral-l2.md) $\int_0^t H\, dB$ is almost surely equal to an $F_t$-measurable function.
