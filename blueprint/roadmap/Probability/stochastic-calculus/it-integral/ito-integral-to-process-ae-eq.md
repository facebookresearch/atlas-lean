# Itô integral agrees with the elementary integral on elementary integrands

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $K$ be an [elementary integrand](elementary-integrand.md). Then for every $t \ge 0$ the [Itô
integral](ito-integral-2.md) of the process of $K$ agrees almost surely with the [elementary Itô
integral](ito-integral.md) of $K$.
