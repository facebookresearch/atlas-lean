# Difference isometry

Let $B$ be a real Brownian motion adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H_1, H_2$ be $F$-progressive with $\mathbb E \int_0^t H_i(u)^2\, du < \infty$. Then
$\int_0^t H_1\, dB - \int_0^t H_2\, dB$ is square integrable and
$\mathbb E\bigl[ (\int_0^t H_1\, dB - \int_0^t H_2\, dB)^2 \bigr] = \mathbb E \int_0^t (H_1(u) - H_2(u))^2\, du$.
