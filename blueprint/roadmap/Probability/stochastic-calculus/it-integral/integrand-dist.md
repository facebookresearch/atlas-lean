# Squared $L^2$ distance between integrands

For a measure $P$, real processes $H, K$ and $t \ge 0$, let

$$d_t(H, K) = \int_\Omega \int_{(0, t]} |H_u(\omega) - K_u(\omega)|^2\, du\, dP(\omega) \in [0, \infty],$$

both integrals being lower Lebesgue integrals of $[0, \infty]$-valued functions (suprema of the
integrals of measurable simple minorants), so that $d_t(H, K)$ is defined for arbitrary, possibly
nonmeasurable processes. When the restrictions of $H$ and $K$ to $(0, t] \times \Omega$ are jointly
measurable and $P$ is finite (the source's case), Tonelli's theorem makes it the squared distance of
$H$ and $K$ in $L^2(P \otimes du)$ on $(0, t] \times \Omega$.
