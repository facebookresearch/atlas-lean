# Square integrability of the Itô integral

For every measure $P$, filtration $F$, real processes $B, H$ and $t \ge 0$, the [Itô
integral](ito-integral-2.md) $\int_0^t H\, dB$ is square integrable: it is a representative of an element of
$L^2(P)$, the [$L^2$ Itô integral](ito-integral-l2.md) (which is $0$ when $H$ is not Itô-approximable).
