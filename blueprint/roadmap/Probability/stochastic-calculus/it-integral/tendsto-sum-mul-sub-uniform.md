# Riemann–Stieltjes integration by parts, uniform partitions

Let $g$ be of class $C^1$, $w : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ continuous and $t \ge 0$. Then

$$\sum_{k < n} g\Bigl(\frac{tk}{n}\Bigr) \Bigl( w\Bigl(\frac{t(k+1)}{n}\Bigr) - w\Bigl(\frac{tk}{n}\Bigr) \Bigr) \to g(t) w(t) - g(0) w(0) - \int_0^t g'(u) w(u)\, du \qquad (n \to \infty) .$$
