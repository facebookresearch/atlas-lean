# Convergence of the delayed averages

Let $P$ be a finite measure, $H$ a progressive process, $M \in \mathbb{R}$ and $t \ge 0$. Then the [delayed-average
approximations](delayed-average.md) of the truncation $\tau_M(H)$ converge to $\tau_M(H)$ in $L^2(P \otimes du)$ on
$(0, t] \times \Omega$: $d_t(H^{(n)}, \tau_M(H)) \to 0$ as the mesh $t/(n + 1)$ tends to $0$, where $d_t$ is the
[integrand distance](integrand-dist.md).

## Sources

- I. Karatzas and S. E. Shreve, *Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1991, proof of Lemma 3.2.4(b).
