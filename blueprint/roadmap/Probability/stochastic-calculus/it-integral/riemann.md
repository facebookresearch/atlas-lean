# Left-point step approximation of a deterministic integrand

For a filtration $F$, $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $t \ge 0$ and $n \ge 1$, the left-point approximation of $g$ is the
[elementary integrand](elementary-integrand.md) with times $tk/n$ ($k = 0, \dots, n$) and constant coefficient
$g(tk/n)$ on $(tk/n, t(k+1)/n]$, with bound $\sum_{k < n} |g(tk/n)|$: its process is
$\sum_{k < n} g(tk/n)\, \mathbf 1_{(tk/n, t(k+1)/n]}$. For $n = 0$ it has no cells: its only time is $0$,
its process is $0$ and its bound is $0$. Deterministic coefficients are measurable for every
$\sigma$-algebra.
