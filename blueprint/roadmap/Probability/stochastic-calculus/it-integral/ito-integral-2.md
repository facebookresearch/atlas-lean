# Itô integral process

Let $P$ be a measure, $F$ a filtration, and $B, H$ real processes. The Itô integral at time $t$,
$\int_0^t H\, dB$, is a real random variable chosen as follows: an $F_t$-measurable representative of the
[$L^2$ Itô integral](ito-integral-l2.md) when the latter has one up to a null set (as it does when $B$ is a
pre-Brownian motion for $F$ with independent increments and $H$ is Itô-approximable), and otherwise any
representative of the $L^2$ Itô integral.
