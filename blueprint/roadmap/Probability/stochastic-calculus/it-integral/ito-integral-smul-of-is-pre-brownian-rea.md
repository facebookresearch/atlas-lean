# Homogeneity of the Itô integral for a pre-Brownian integrator

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H$ be $F$-progressive with $\mathbb E \int_0^t H_u^2\, du < \infty$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}$. Then almost surely
$\int_0^t a H\, dB = a \int_0^t H\, dB$; path continuity of $B$ is not needed.
