# $L^2$ convergence of left-point approximations of a continuous function

Let $P$ be a probability measure, $F$ a filtration, $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ continuous and $t \ge 0$. Then the [left-point
approximations](riemann.md) of $g$ converge to the deterministic process $(u, \omega) \mapsto g(u)$ in
$L^2(P \otimes du)$ on $(0, t] \times \Omega$ ([integrand distance](integrand-dist.md) tending to $0$).
