# Lebesgue differentiation along shrinking off-center intervals

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be locally integrable. Then for almost every $u \in \mathbb{R}$ the following holds: for all
sequences $a_n, b_n$ with $a_n < b_n$ eventually, $|u - (a_n + b_n)/2| \le 2 (b_n - a_n)$ eventually, and
$b_n - a_n \to 0$,

$$\frac{1}{b_n - a_n} \int_{a_n}^{b_n} f(x)\, dx \to f(u) .$$
