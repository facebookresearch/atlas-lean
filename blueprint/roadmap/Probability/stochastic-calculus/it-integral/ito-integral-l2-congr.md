# Locality of the Itô integral

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion (the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion) adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ independent of $F_s$ for $s \le t$, and let $t \ge 0$. Let $H, K$ be real processes with $H_u(\omega) = K_u(\omega)$ for all $u \in (0, t]$ and all $\omega$. Then
their [$L^2$ Itô integrals](ito-integral-l2.md) at time $t$ coincide.
