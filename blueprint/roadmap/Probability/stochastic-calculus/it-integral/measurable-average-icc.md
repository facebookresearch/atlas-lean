# Measurability of time averages of a progressive process

Let $H$ be progressive for a filtration $F$, $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ and $T \ge 0$ with $b \le T$. Then
$\omega \mapsto \frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b H_{\max(s, 0)}(\omega)\, ds$ (read as $0$ when $b \le a$ or the integrand is
not integrable) is $F_T$-measurable.
