# Pathwise jump robustness

Let $c_0, c_1, \dots$ be continuous increments with $|c_i| \le M$ for $i \le N$, and add the jump increments of a finite set of jumps with times $\tau_k$ and sizes $\xi_k$ on the grid of mesh $1/n$. If any two distinct jump times are at least $2/n$ apart, the realized bipower variation of the first $N + 1$ increments changes by at most $2M \sum_k |\xi_k|$.
