# A jump increment collects the jumps in its grid cell

For a finite set $s$ of jump indices with jump times $\tau_k$ and sizes $\xi_k$, the increment of the pure-jump path $t \mapsto \sum_{k \in s} \xi_k \mathbf 1\{\tau_k \le t\}$ over the $i$-th cell $(i/n, (i+1)/n]$ of the grid of mesh $1/n$ is the sum of the sizes $\xi_k$ of the jumps with $\tau_k$ in that cell. For $n = 0$ both endpoints are $0$ by the convention for division by zero, the cell is empty, and the increment is $0$.
