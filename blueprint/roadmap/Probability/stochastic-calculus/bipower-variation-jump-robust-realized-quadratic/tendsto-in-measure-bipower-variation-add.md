# Jump robustness of bipower variation

On a finite measure space, let $c_{n,i}$ be continuous increments whose largest absolute value over the $L_n + 1$ increments used tends to $0$ in probability. Add the increments over the grid cells $(i/n, (i+1)/n]$ of $K$ random jumps with measurable sizes $\xi_k$ at measurable, almost surely distinct times $\tau_k$, with $K$ measurable. Then the bipower variation over $L_n$ steps changes by an amount tending to $0$ in probability.
