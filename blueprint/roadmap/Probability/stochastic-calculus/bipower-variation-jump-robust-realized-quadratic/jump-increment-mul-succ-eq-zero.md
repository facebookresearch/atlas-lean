# Well-separated jumps never fill two adjacent grid cells

For a finite set of jumps with times $\tau_k$ and sizes $\xi_k$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, if any two distinct jump times are at least $2/n$ apart, then no two adjacent cells of the grid of mesh $1/n$ both contain jumps: $J_n(i)\, J_n(i+1) = 0$ for every $i$.
