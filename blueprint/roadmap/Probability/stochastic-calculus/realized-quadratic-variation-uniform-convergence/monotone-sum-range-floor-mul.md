# Partial sums of nonnegative terms up to the integer part of nt are monotone

For nonnegative reals $g_0, g_1, \dots$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the function $t \mapsto \sum_{j < \lfloor nt \rfloor} g_j$ is nondecreasing on $\mathbb{R}$, where $\lfloor nt \rfloor$ is the natural-number floor, $0$ for $nt < 1$.
