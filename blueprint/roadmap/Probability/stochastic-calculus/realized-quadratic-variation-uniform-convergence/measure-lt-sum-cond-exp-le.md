# One-row Lenglart-type bound

On a probability space with a filtration $(\mathcal F_i)$, let $x_0, x_1, \dots$ be square-integrable with each $x_i$ strongly $\mathcal F_{i+1}$-measurable, and write $c_j = \mathbb{E}[x_j^2 \mid \mathcal F_j]$. For $C \ge 0$, $M > 0$ and $K \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\mathbb{P}\Bigl(\sum_{j<K} c_j > M + 1\Bigr) \le \mathbb{P}\Bigl(\sum_{j<K} x_j^2 > C\Bigr) + \frac{C + 1}{M} + L,$$

where $L = \sum_{j<K} \mathbb{E}[x_j^2 \mathbf 1\{|x_j| > 1\}]$ is the Lindeberg sum at level $1$.
