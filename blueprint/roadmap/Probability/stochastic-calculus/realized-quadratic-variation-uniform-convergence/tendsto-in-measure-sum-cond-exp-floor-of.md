# Predictable quadratic variation from realized quadratic variation

Let row $n$ of a triangular array $\xi$ be square-integrable, with $\xi_{n,i}$ measurable for
$\mathcal F_{n,i+1}$, and satisfy the Lindeberg condition. If the realized variation
$\sum_{j < \lfloor n t \rfloor} \xi_{n,j}^2$ tends to $t$ in probability for every $t \in [0,1]$,
then so does the predictable variation
$\sum_{j < \lfloor n t \rfloor} E[\xi_{n,j}^2 \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}]$.
