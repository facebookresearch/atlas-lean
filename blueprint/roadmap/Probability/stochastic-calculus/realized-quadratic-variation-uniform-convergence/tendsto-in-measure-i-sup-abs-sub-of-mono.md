# Uniform convergence from pointwise convergence for monotone processes

Let $f_n(t)$ be random processes on a measure space such that, for every $n$, almost every path $t \mapsto f_n(t)$ is nondecreasing on $[0, 1]$, and $f_n(t) \to t$ in measure for every $t \in [0, 1]$. Then $\sup_{t \in [0,1]} |f_n(t) - t| \to 0$ in measure.
