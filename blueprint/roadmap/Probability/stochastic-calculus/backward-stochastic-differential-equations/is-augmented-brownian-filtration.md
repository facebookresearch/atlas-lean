# Augmented Brownian filtration

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and $B = (B_t)_{t \ge 0}$ a real process. A filtration
$(F_t)_{t \ge 0}$ is the augmented filtration of $B$ if for every $t \ge 0$

$$F_t = \sigma\bigl(B_s : s \le t\bigr) \vee \sigma\bigl(\{ N : N \text{ measurable},\ P(N) = 0 \}\bigr),$$

the $\sigma$-algebra generated by $B_s$, $s \le t$, together with the $P$-null measurable sets. This is
the filtration of the BSDE theory (Peng, Section 1.1, where the null sets are left implicit); solutions
of BSDEs are adapted to it because of the Brownian martingale representation theorem.

## Sources

- S. Peng, *Backward stochastic differential equation, nonlinear expectation and their applications*, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians 2010, Vol. I, Hindustan Book Agency, 2010, 393–432, Section 1.1.
