# Negative probability flux of the Fokker–Planck equation

For $\Psi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $D \in \mathbb{R}$ and $p : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the negative probability flux of the
gradient-drift Fokker–Planck equation at $(t, x)$ is

$$G(t, x) = p(t, x)\, \Psi'(x) + D\, \partial_x p(t, x);$$

the flux itself is $-G$. Derivatives of non-differentiable functions are read as $0$.
