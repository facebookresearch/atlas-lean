# A zero-flux density determines the drift

Let $\Psi, p : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p$ differentiable and $p > 0$ everywhere, and $D \in \mathbb{R}$. If the gradient
drift $-\nabla \Psi$ has zero [probability flux](flux.md) for $p$, then
$\partial_i \Psi(x) = -D\, \partial_i \log p(x)$ for every $i$ and every $x$.
