# Identifiability of the potential

Let $\Psi_1, \Psi_2, p : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, $p > 0$ everywhere, and $D \in \mathbb{R}$. If the
gradient drifts $-\nabla \Psi_1$ and $-\nabla \Psi_2$ both have zero [probability flux](flux.md) for $p$,
$-\nabla \Psi_j\, p - D \nabla p = 0$, then $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$ differ by a constant: there is $C$ with
$\Psi_1(x) = \Psi_2(x) + C$ for all $x$.
