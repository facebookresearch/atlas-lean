# Flux of the Gibbs density under a rotational gradient drift

Let $\Psi : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, $D \ne 0$ and $A$ any real $n \times n$ matrix. Under the drift
$-(\nabla \Psi + A \nabla \Psi)$ the [probability flux](flux.md) of the [Gibbs density](gibbs.md) is the
rotational part: for all $y$ and $i$,

$$J_i(y) = -(A \nabla \Psi(y))_i\, e^{-\Psi(y)/D} .$$
