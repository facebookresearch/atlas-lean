# Partial derivative on Euclidean coordinate space

For $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, $i \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the partial derivative is
$\partial_i f(x) = Df(x)\, e_i$, the Fréchet derivative applied to the $i$-th basis vector, read as $0$ when $f$
is not differentiable at $x$.
