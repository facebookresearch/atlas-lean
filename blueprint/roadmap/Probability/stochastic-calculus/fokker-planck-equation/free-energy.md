# Free energy of a Fokker–Planck density

For a potential $\Psi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $D, a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ and $p : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the free energy of the
density $p(t, \cdot)$ on $[a, b]$ is

$$F(t) = \int_a^b \Bigl( \Psi(x)\, p(t, x) + D\, p(t, x) \log p(t, x) \Bigr) dx,$$

with $\log 0 = 0$ (and the logarithm of the absolute value for negative arguments) and an integral of a
non-integrable function read as $0$.
