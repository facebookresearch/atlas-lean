# The Gibbs density is stationary

Let $\Psi : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, $D \ne 0$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$. Then for the gradient drift
$-\nabla \Psi$, the [Fokker–Planck operator](fokker-planck-op.md) vanishes on $c\, e^{-\Psi/D}$:
$-\operatorname{div} J(x) = 0$ for every $x$.
