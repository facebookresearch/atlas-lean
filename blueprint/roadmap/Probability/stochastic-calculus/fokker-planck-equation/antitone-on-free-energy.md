# Free-energy decay

Let $a \le b$, $D \in \mathbb{R}$, $\Psi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ of class $C^2$, $I \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ an open interval
(convex open set) of times, and $p : \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ of class $C^2$ with $p(t, x) > 0$ for all
$t \in I$ and all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, a [no-flux solution](is-no-flux-solution-on.md) of the gradient-drift
Fokker–Planck equation on $[a, b]$ for $t \in I$. Then the [free energy](free-energy.md) $t \mapsto F(t)$ is
nonincreasing on $I$.
