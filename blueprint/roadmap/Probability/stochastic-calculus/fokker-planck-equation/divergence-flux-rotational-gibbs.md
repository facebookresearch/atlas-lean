# Rotational drifts share the Gibbs stationary density

Let $\Psi : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be of class $C^2$, $D \ne 0$, and $A$ a real antisymmetric $n \times n$ matrix
($A^\top = -A$). Then for the drift $b = -(\nabla \Psi + A \nabla \Psi)$ and the [Gibbs density](gibbs.md)
$p = e^{-\Psi/D}$, the [probability flux](flux.md) $J = b p - D \nabla p$ is divergence free:
$\operatorname{div} J(x) = 0$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$. So the stationary density does not identify the
drift.
