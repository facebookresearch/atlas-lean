# Weighted quadratic-variation error of Brownian motion

For $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, a real process $B$, $t \ge 0$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the weighted
quadratic-variation error along the uniform partition of $[0, t]$ into $n$ cells is

$$E_n = \sum_{k < n} \varphi\bigl(B_{tk/n}\bigr) \Bigl( \bigl(B_{t(k+1)/n} - B_{tk/n}\bigr)^2 - \frac{t}{n} \Bigr) .$$
