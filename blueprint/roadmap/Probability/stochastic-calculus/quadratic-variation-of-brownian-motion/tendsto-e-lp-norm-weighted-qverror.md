# Weighted quadratic variation of Brownian motion

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion adapted to a filtration $F$, with increments $B_t - B_s$ ($s \le t$)
independent of $F_s$, and let $\varphi$ be measurable with $|\varphi| \le C$. Then for every $t \ge 0$, the
[weighted quadratic-variation error](weighted-qverror.md)
$\sum_{k < n} \varphi(B_{t_k}) \bigl((\Delta B_k)^2 - \Delta t\bigr)$ tends to $0$ in $L^2(P)$ as $n \to \infty$.
