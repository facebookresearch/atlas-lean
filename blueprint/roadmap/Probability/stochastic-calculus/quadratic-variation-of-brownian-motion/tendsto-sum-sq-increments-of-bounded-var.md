# Quadratic sums of a continuous function of bounded variation vanish

Let $f : [0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous, $t \ge 0$, and suppose $f$ has bounded variation on $[0, t]$.
Then along the uniform partitions of $[0, t]$,

$$\sum_{k < n} \bigl( f(t(k+1)/n) - f(tk/n) \bigr)^2 \to 0 \qquad (n \to \infty):$$

a continuous function of finite variation has zero quadratic variation.
