# Quadratic variation of Brownian motion

For every pre-Brownian motion $B$ (no filtration and no measurability of the coordinates is assumed) and
every $t \ge 0$, along the uniform partitions of $[0, t]$,

$$\sum_{k < n} \bigl( B_{t(k+1)/n} - B_{tk/n} \bigr)^2 \to t \quad \text{in } L^2(P) \qquad (n \to \infty):$$

the quadratic variation of Brownian motion is $[B]_t = t$.

## Sources

- B. Øksendal, *Stochastic Differential Equations*, 6th ed., Springer, 2003, Exercise 2.17.
- I. Karatzas and S. E. Shreve, *Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1991,
  Theorem 1.5.8 (for continuous square-integrable martingales, in probability).
