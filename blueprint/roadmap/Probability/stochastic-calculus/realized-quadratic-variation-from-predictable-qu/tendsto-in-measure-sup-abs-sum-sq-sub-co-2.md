# Realized and predictable quadratic variations agree along a row

Let row $n$ of a triangular array $\xi$ be square-integrable, with $\xi_{n,i}$ measurable for
$\mathcal F_{n,i+1}$, and satisfy the Lindeberg condition. If the predictable quadratic variation
$\sum_{j < \lfloor n t \rfloor} E[\xi_{n,j}^2 \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}]$ tends to $t$ in probability
for every $t \in [0,1]$, then
$\max_{i \le n} |\sum_{j < i} \xi_{n,j}^2 - \sum_{j < i} E[\xi_{n,j}^2 \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}]| \to 0$
in probability.
