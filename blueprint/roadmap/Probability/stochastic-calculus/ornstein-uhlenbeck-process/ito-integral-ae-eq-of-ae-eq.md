# Elementary Itô integrals agree for modifications

Let $H$ be an [elementary integrand](../it-integral/elementary-integrand.md) and $B, B'$ two real processes with
$B_t = B'_t$ almost surely for each $t$. Then for every $t \ge 0$ the elementary Itô integrals of $H$ against $B$
and against $B'$ agree almost surely.
