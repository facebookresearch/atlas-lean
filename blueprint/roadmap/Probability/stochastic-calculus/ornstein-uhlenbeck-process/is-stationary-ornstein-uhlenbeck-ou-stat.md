# Doob's construction of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion, $\theta > 0$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [stationary time
change](ou-stationary-of-brownian.md)

$$X_t = \sigma e^{-\theta t} B_{e^{2\theta t}/(2\theta)}, \qquad t \ge 0,$$

is a [stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process](is-stationary-ornstein-uhlenbeck.md) with rate $\theta$ and noise
amplitude $\sigma$.

## Sources

- J. L. Doob, *The Brownian movement and stochastic equations*, Annals of Mathematics 43 (1942), 351–369.
