# Conditional expectation of a Gaussian process given its past

Let $X = (X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be a real Gaussian process with strongly measurable coordinates and $\mathbb E X_t = 0$ for
every $t$, let $s, t \ge 0$, and let $c$ be a real number with $\operatorname{Cov}(X_u, X_t) = c \operatorname{Cov}(X_u, X_s)$
for every $u \le s$. Then $\mathbb E[X_t \mid \mathcal F_s] = c X_s$ almost surely, where $(\mathcal F_s)$ is the natural
filtration of $X$.
