# Existence of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero

For every $\theta > 0$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ there are a measure $\mu$ on the path space $\mathbb{R}^{[0, \infty)}$ and a
process $X$ on it that is an [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with
rate $\theta$ and noise amplitude $\sigma$ under $\mu$.
