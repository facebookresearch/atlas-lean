# Ornstein–Uhlenbeck covariance converges to the stationary one

For $\theta > 0$ and real $\sigma, s, t$, the [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck covariance](ou-covariance.md) satisfies
$c(s + h, t + h) \to \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta} e^{-\theta |t - s|}$ as $h \to \infty$, the [stationary
covariance](ou-stationary-covariance.md).
