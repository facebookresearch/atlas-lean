# Doob's construction of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process from zero

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion, $\theta > 0$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [time change](ou-of-brownian.md)

$$X_t = \sigma e^{-\theta t} B_{(e^{2\theta t} - 1)/(2\theta)}, \qquad t \ge 0,$$

is an [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with rate $\theta$ and noise
amplitude $\sigma$.

## Sources

- J. L. Doob, *The Brownian movement and stochastic equations*, Annals of Mathematics 43 (1942), 351–369.
