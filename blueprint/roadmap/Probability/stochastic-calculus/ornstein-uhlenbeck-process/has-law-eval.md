# One-time law of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process from zero

If $X$ is an [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with rate $\theta > 0$
and noise amplitude $\sigma$, then for every $t \ge 0$

$$X_t \sim N\Bigl(0,\ \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta}\bigl(1 - e^{-2\theta t}\bigr)\Bigr) .$$
