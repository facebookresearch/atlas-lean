# Mean reversion of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

Let $X$ be a [stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process](is-stationary-ornstein-uhlenbeck.md) with rate $\theta > 0$
and noise amplitude $\sigma$, with strongly measurable coordinates, and $s \le t$. Then, with
$(\mathcal F_s)$ the natural filtration of $X$,

$$\mathbb E[X_t \mid \mathcal F_s] = e^{-\theta(t-s)} X_s \qquad \text{almost surely.}$$
