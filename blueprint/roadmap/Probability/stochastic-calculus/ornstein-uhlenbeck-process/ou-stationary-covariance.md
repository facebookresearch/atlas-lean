# Covariance of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

For real $\theta, \sigma, s, t$, the covariance function of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process is

$$\frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta}\, e^{-\theta |t - s|},$$

with $\sigma^2/0 = 0$; it is the covariance of interest for $\theta > 0$.

## Sources

- G. E. Uhlenbeck and L. S. Ornstein, *On the theory of the Brownian motion*, Physical Review 36 (1930), 823–841.
