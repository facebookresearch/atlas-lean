# Existence of a pre-Brownian motion on path space

There is a measure $\mu$ on the path space $\mathbb{R}^{[0, \infty)}$ under which the coordinate process
$(f \mapsto f(t))_{t \ge 0}$ is a pre-Brownian motion (has the finite-dimensional laws of Brownian motion); it
is obtained by Kolmogorov extension of the Brownian finite-dimensional laws.
