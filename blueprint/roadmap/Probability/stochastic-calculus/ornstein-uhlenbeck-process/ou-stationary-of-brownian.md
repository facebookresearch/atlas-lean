# Doob's stationary time change of Brownian motion

For $\theta, \sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and a real process $B = (B_t)_{t \ge 0}$, Doob's stationary time change is the process
$t \mapsto \sigma e^{-\theta t} B_{\rho(t)}$, $t \ge 0$, where $\rho$ is the [stationary Doob clock](ou-stationary-clock.md)
($\rho(t) = e^{2\theta t}/(2\theta)$ for $\theta > 0$).
