# Mean reversion of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process from zero

Let $X$ be an [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with rate $\theta > 0$
and noise amplitude $\sigma$, with strongly measurable coordinates, and $s \le t$. Then, with
$(\mathcal F_s)$ the natural filtration of $X$, $\mathbb E[X_t \mid \mathcal F_s] = e^{-\theta(t-s)} X_s$ almost surely.
