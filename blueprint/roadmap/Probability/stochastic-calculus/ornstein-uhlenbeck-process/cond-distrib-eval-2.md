# Transition law of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

Let $X$ be a [stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process](is-stationary-ornstein-uhlenbeck.md) with rate $\theta > 0$
and noise amplitude $\sigma$, with measurable coordinates, and $s \le t$. Then for almost every $x$ (with
respect to the law of $X_s$), the conditional law of $X_t$ given $X_s = x$ is

$$N\Bigl( e^{-\theta(t-s)} x,\ \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta} \bigl(1 - e^{-2\theta(t-s)}\bigr) \Bigr) .$$
