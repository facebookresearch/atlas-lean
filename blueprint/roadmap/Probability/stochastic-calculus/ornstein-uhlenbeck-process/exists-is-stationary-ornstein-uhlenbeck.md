# Existence of the stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

For every $\theta > 0$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ there are a measure $\mu$ on the path space $\mathbb{R}^{[0, \infty)}$ and a
process $X$ on it that is a [stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process](is-stationary-ornstein-uhlenbeck.md) with rate
$\theta$ and noise amplitude $\sigma$ under $\mu$.
