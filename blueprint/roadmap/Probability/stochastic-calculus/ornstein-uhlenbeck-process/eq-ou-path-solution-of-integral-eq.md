# Uniqueness of the pathwise Langevin solution

Let $\theta, \sigma, x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, let $w, x : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous, and suppose that for all $t \ge 0$

$$x(t) = x_0 - \theta \int_0^t x(u)\, du + \sigma \bigl(w(t) - w(0)\bigr) .$$

Then $x(t)$ equals the [pathwise solution](ou-path-solution.md) at every $t \ge 0$.
