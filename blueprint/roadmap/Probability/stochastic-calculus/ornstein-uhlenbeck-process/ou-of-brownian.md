# Doob's time change of Brownian motion

For $\theta, \sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and a real process $B = (B_t)_{t \ge 0}$, Doob's time change is the process

$$t \mapsto \sigma e^{-\theta t} B_{\tau(t)}, \qquad t \ge 0,$$

where $\tau$ is [Doob's clock](ou-clock.md) $\tau(t) = (e^{2\theta t} - 1)/(2\theta)$.
