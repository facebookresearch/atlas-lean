# Relaxation to stationarity

Let $X$ be an [Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with rate $\theta > 0$
and noise amplitude $\sigma$. For all $s, t \ge 0$,

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_{s + h}, X_{t + h}) \to \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta} e^{-\theta|t - s|} \qquad (h \to \infty) .$$
