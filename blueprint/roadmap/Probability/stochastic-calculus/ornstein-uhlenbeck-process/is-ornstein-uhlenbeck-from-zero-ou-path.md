# The pathwise Langevin solution is an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

Let $B$ be a real Brownian motion, $\theta > 0$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [pathwise solution](ou-path-solution.md)
of the Langevin equation driven by the path $u \mapsto B_{\max(u, 0)}$ and started at $0$ is an
[Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process started at zero](is-ornstein-uhlenbeck-from-zero.md) with rate $\theta$ and noise
amplitude $\sigma$. No filtration and no measurability of the coordinates of $B$ is assumed.

## Sources

- I. Karatzas and S. E. Shreve, *Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1991, Section 5.6.
