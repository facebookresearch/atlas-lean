# Gaussian process laws are determined by mean and covariance

Let $X = (X_t)_{t \in T}$ be a real Gaussian process under $P$ and $Y = (Y_t)_{t \in T}$ a real Gaussian process
under $Q$ (possibly on a different space), with $\mathbb E_P X_t = \mathbb E_Q Y_t$ and
$\operatorname{Cov}_P(X_s, X_t) = \operatorname{Cov}_Q(Y_s, Y_t)$ for all $s, t$. Then for every finite set $I \subseteq T$ the
vectors $(X_t)_{t \in I}$ under $P$ and $(Y_t)_{t \in I}$ under $Q$ have the same law.
