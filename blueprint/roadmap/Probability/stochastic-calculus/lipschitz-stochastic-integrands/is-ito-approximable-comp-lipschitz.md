# Itô approximation after Lipschitz composition

Let $P$ be a finite measure, $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0}$ a filtration, and $X$ a real progressively measurable process with finite squared time-and-probability energy on $(0,t]$. If $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is globally Lipschitz, then the process $f(X)$ is Itô-approximable on $(0,t]$ with respect to the same filtration and measure.

Lipschitz continuity preserves progressiveness, and the finite-energy composition result supplies the other hypothesis of the existing approximation theorem. The conclusion provides actual simple predictable approximants in the existing integrand-distance sense. It does not assume such approximants as an additional hypothesis, and it allows $f(0)\ne0$.
