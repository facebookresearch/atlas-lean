# Finite energy under Lipschitz composition

Let $P$ be a finite measure, let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0}$ be a filtration, and let $X$ be a real progressively measurable process. Suppose $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is globally Lipschitz. If

$$\int_\Omega\int_{(0,t]}|X_s|^2\,ds\,dP<\infty,$$

then

$$\int_\Omega\int_{(0,t]}|f(X_s)|^2\,ds\,dP<\infty.$$

The coefficient need not vanish at zero. Its constant offset $f(0)$ has finite energy because the time interval and the measure are finite. The proof combines the Lipschitz difference estimate relative to zero with this constant offset, using the squared triangle bound. No completeness or continuity of the filtration is assumed.
