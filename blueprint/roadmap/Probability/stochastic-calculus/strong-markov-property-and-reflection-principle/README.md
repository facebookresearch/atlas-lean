# Strong Markov property and reflection principle

Brownian motion restarts afresh at stopping times of its natural filtration. Applied at the
hitting time $T_a$ of a level, this gives the reflection principle, the law of the running
maximum $S_t$ (that of $|B_t|$) and the law of $T_a$ (Le Gall, ENS notes *Intégration,
Probabilités et Processus Aléatoires*, Section 14.5; Le Gall, *Brownian Motion, Martingales, and
Stochastic Calculus*, Chapter 2).
