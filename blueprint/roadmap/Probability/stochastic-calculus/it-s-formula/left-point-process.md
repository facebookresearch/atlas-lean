# Left-point step process along a Brownian path

For $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, a real process $B$, $t \ge 0$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the left-point step process of
$u \mapsto g(B_u)$ on the uniform partition of $[0, t]$ into $n$ cells is

$$u \mapsto \sum_{k < n} g\bigl(B_{tk/n}\bigr)\, \mathbf 1_{(tk/n,\ t(k+1)/n]}(u), \qquad u \ge 0$$

(the zero process for $n = 0$).
