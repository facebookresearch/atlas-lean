# Second-order Taylor sums along a continuous path

Let $f$ be of class $C^2$, $w : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ continuous, $t \ge 0$, and $(n_j)$ a sequence of natural
numbers tending to $\infty$ along which the quadratic sums stay bounded:
$\sum_{k < n_j} (\Delta_k w)^2 \le M$ for every $j$, where $\Delta_k w = w(t(k+1)/n_j) - w(tk/n_j)$ and
$w_k = w(tk/n_j)$. Then

$$f(w(t)) - f(w(0)) - \sum_{k < n_j} \Bigl( f'(w_k)\, \Delta_k w + \frac{1}{2} f''(w_k)\, (\Delta_k w)^2 \Bigr) \to 0 \qquad (j \to \infty) .$$
