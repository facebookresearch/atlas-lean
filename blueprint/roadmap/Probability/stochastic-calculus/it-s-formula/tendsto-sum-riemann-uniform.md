# Left-point Riemann sums of a continuous function

Let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and $t \ge 0$. Then the left-point Riemann sums on the uniform
partition of $[0, t]$ converge to the integral:

$$\sum_{k < n} g\Bigl(\frac{tk}{n}\Bigr) \frac{t}{n} \to \int_0^t g(u)\, du \qquad (n \to \infty) .$$
