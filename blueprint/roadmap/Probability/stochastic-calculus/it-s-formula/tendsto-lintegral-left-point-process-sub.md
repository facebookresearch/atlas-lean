# Left-point approximation of a Lipschitz function of Brownian motion

Let $B$ be a pre-Brownian motion that is jointly measurable in $(u, \omega)$, and $g$ with
$|g(x) - g(y)| \le L |x - y|$. Then for every $t \ge 0$, the [left-point step processes](left-point-process.md)
$H_n$ of $u \mapsto g(B_u)$ converge to it in $L^2(P \otimes du)$ on $(0, t] \times \Omega$:

$$\mathbb E \int_0^t \bigl| H_n(u) - g(B_u) \bigr|^2\, du \to 0 \qquad (n \to \infty) .$$
