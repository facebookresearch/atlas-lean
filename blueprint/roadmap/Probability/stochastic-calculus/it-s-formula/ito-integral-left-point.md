# Itô integral of the left-point integrand is the left-point sum

Let $F$ be a filtration, $B$ an adapted real process, $g$ measurable with $|g| \le C$, $t \ge 0$ and
$n \in \mathbb{N}$. For every $\omega$, the Itô integral at time $t$ of the [left-point elementary
integrand](left-point.md) is the left-point Itô sum

$$\sum_{k < n} g\bigl(B_{tk/n}\bigr) \bigl( B_{t(k+1)/n} - B_{tk/n} \bigr) .$$
