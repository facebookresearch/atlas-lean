# Left-point elementary integrand along a Brownian path

Let $F$ be a filtration, $B$ an adapted real process, $g$ measurable with $|g| \le C$, $t \ge 0$ and
$n \in \mathbb{N}$. For $n \ge 1$ the left-point [elementary integrand](../it-integral/elementary-integrand.md) is the elementary
integrand with times $tk/n$ ($k = 0, \dots, n$), coefficient $g(B_{tk/n})$ on the cell $(tk/n, t(k+1)/n]$, and
bound $C$: its process is $\sum_{k < n} g(B_{tk/n})\, \mathbf 1_{(tk/n, t(k+1)/n]}$. For $n = 0$ it has no cells:
its only time is $0$, its process is $0$, and its bound is still $C$.
