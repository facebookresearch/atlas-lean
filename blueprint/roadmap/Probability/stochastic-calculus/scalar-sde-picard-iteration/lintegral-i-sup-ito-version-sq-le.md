# Maximal second moment of a continuous Itô version

Let $B$ have real Brownian finite-dimensional laws, be adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$, and have increments independent of the past filtration. Let $H$ be progressive and have finite integrated second moment on a fixed horizon $[0,t]$.

Let $J$ be a martingale with continuous paths for every sample, and suppose for every fixed $s\ge0$ that $J_s$ equals the actual Itô integral of $H$ at $s$ almost surely. Then

$$\mathbb E\sup_{0\le s\le t}|J_s|^2\le4\,\mathbb E\int_0^t|H_s|^2\,ds.$$

These are nonnegative extended integrals. Doob's inequality bounds the running maximum by four times the terminal second moment, and the actual isometry identifies that moment with input energy. Fixed-time square integrability of the chosen version follows from its equality to the selected Itô representative. Finite input energy is needed only at the specified horizon for the isometry. Completeness, right continuity and continuity of the driving paths are not assumed.
