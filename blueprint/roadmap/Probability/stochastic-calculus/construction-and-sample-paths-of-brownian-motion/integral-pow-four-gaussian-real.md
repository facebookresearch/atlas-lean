# Fourth moment of a centered Gaussian

For every variance $v \ge 0$, the centered Gaussian law satisfies

$$\int x^4 \, dN(0, v)(x) = 3 v^2 .$$

It is the fourth derivative at $0$ of the moment generating function $e^{v t^2/2}$, and the moment used in
Kolmogorov's criterion for Brownian motion.
