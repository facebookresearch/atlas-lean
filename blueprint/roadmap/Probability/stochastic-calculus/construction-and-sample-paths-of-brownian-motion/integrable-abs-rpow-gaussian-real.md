# Absolute moments of a centered Gaussian are integrable

For every variance $v \ge 0$ and every $\beta > 0$, the function $x \mapsto |x|^\beta$ is integrable for the
centered Gaussian law $N(0, v)$ (for $v = 0$, the point mass at $0$): all absolute moments of a Gaussian are
finite.

## Sources

- J.-F. Le Gall, *Brownian Motion, Martingales, and Stochastic Calculus*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 274, Springer, 2016, Chapter 1.
