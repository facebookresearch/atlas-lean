# Brownian motion with an independent Gaussian start

Let $v \ge 0$. There exist a measure $\mu$ on the path space of functions $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \to \mathbb{R}$, a process $B = (B_t)_{t \ge 0}$ and a random variable $X_0$ on that space such that

- $B$ is a real Brownian motion under $\mu$ (its finite-dimensional laws are those of Brownian motion and its paths are almost surely continuous);
- $X_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, v)$ under $\mu$;
- $X_0$ is independent under $\mu$ of the path process $\omega \mapsto (t \mapsto B_t(\omega))$.
