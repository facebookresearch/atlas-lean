# Brownian paths are nowhere differentiable

Let $B$ be a real Brownian motion. Almost surely, the path $s \mapsto B_s$, extended to $s < 0$ by the
constant $B_0$, is differentiable at no time $t > 0$ (theorem of Paley, Wiener and Zygmund). The
statement concerns the positive times $t > 0$.

## Background

- [The Weierstrass function is nowhere differentiable](../../../Analysis/real-functions/continuity-and-functional-equations/weierstrass-function/weierstrass-fun-nowhere-differentiable.md):
  a deterministic continuous nowhere differentiable function; Brownian paths give such functions
  almost surely.
