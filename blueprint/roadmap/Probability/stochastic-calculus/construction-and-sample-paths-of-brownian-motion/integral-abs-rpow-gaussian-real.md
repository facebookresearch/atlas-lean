# Scaling of the absolute moments of a centered Gaussian

For every variance $v \ge 0$ and every $\beta \ge 0$,

$$\int |x|^\beta \, dN(0, v)(x) = v^{\beta/2} \int |x|^\beta \, dN(0, 1)(x),$$

since $N(0, v)$ is the image of $N(0, 1)$ under $x \mapsto \sqrt v\, x$ (with $0^0 = 1$ for $v = \beta = 0$).
