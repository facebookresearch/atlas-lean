# Real mean-square stability of scalar SDEs

Let $B$ have real Brownian finite-dimensional laws, be adapted to $(\mathcal F_t)$, and have increments independent of the past filtration. Let $X,Y$ be scalar solutions driven by this same $B$ and filtration, with deterministic initial values $x,y$ and common coefficients $\sigma,b$, both globally Lipschitz with constant $K\ge0$.

For $0\le t\le T$,

$$\mathbb E|X_t-Y_t|^2\le 3|x-y|^2\exp\bigl(3K^2(1+T)t\bigr).$$

This is an actual real expectation: square integrability of both fixed-time solution values follows from their equations and the drift Lipschitz bound. The squared difference is therefore integrable before converting the nonnegative-integral estimate. Filtration completeness, right continuity, and continuity of the driving paths are not assumed. The nonoptimal constant is uniform on the chosen horizon.
