# Fixed-time square integrability of scalar SDE solutions

Let $P$ be a finite measure and let $X$ be a scalar solution with drift $b$ and diffusion coefficient $\sigma$. If $b$ is globally Lipschitz, then $X_t\in L^2(P)$ for every $t\ge0$.

No Lipschitz assumption on $\sigma$, Brownian assumption on the integrator, or completeness of the filtration is needed for this membership conclusion. The selected Itô integral always has an $L^2$ representative; this assertion alone does not grant an isometry for an inadmissible integrand. The drift term belongs to $L^2$ by continuous paths, finite integrated energy and the linear-growth bound for $b$. The actual integral equation then gives square integrability of $X_t$.
