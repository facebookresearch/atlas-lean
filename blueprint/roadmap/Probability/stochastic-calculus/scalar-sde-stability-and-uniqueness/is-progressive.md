# Progressive measurability of scalar SDE solutions

Every scalar solution of the stochastic integral equation is progressively measurable for its specified filtration. On every finite horizon $[0,T]$, the map $(t,\omega)\mapsto X_t(\omega)$ is measurable for the product of the Borel time sigma algebra and $\mathcal F_T$.

This follows solely from adaptedness and continuous sample paths. It requires neither filtration completeness nor assumptions on the driving process or coefficients.
