# Capacity-two chordal Loewner trace and Hausdorff gauge

Given an upper-half-plane trace, the surviving domain at time $t$ is the unbounded component of the upper half-plane with the past trace removed. A capacity-two conformal mapping witness is a pair of inverse holomorphic maps between this domain and the half-plane, normalized by $z(g_t(z)-z)\to2t$ at infinity, with inverse-map limit at the driver giving the tip. At each surviving point the within-nonnegative-time derivative is $2/(g_t(z)-U_t)$; the initial map is identity. Ordinary chordal SLE uses $U_t=\sqrt\kappa B_t$ for Brownian motion. For a real gauge, extend its values to finite nonnegative radii via the nonnegative-real embedding and use the Hausdorff measure formed by infima of sums of gauge values of diameters. The segment is the image of the closed time interval.
