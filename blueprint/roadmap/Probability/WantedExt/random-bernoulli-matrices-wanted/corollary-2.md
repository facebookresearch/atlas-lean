# Corollary 2

This is the result stated in the paper's abstract:

For any sequence `Mₙ` of `n × n` random matrices with mutually independent `Rademacher` entries
we have that

`P(Mₙ is singular) = (1/2 + oₙ(1))ⁿ`.
