# Ranking forgets the order

For a real sequence $p$ that is bounded above and a bijection $\sigma$ of $\mathbb{N}$, the sequence of $k$-th largest values of $p \circ \sigma$ equals the sequence of [$k$-th largest values](rank-desc.md) ($k = 0, 1, \dots$; each the supremum over $(k+1)$-element index sets of the minimum, in general not an entry of $p$).
