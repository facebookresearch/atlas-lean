# The map to the sequence of k-th largest values is measurable

The map sending a real sequence $p$ to the sequence of its [$k$-th largest values](rank-desc.md) $(\text{the } k\text{-th largest value of } p)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$, each entry the real supremum of the minima over $(k+1)$-element index sets (read as $0$ when unbounded, and in general not an entry of $p$), is measurable from $\mathbb{R}^\mathbb{N}$ to $\mathbb{R}^\mathbb{N}$.
