# The k-th largest values of a nonnegative sequence are nonnegative

For a nonnegative real sequence $p$ that is bounded above, each of its [$k$-th largest values](rank-desc.md) ($k = 0, 1, \dots$; each the supremum over $(k+1)$-element index sets of the minimum, in general not an entry of $p$) is nonnegative.
