# Normalising constant of a posterior

For a prior $\mu_0$ and a potential $\Phi$, the normalising constant is

$$
Z = \int \exp(-\Phi(u)) \, \mu_0(du).
$$

The integral is read as $0$ when $\exp(-\Phi)$ is not $\mu_0$-integrable. When $\Phi$ depends on data $y$ this is the evidence $Z(y)$.
