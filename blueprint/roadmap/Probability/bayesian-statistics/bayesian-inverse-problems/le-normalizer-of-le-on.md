# Lower bound for a normalising constant

If $\Phi \le T$ on a measurable set $K$ of finite prior mass and $\exp(-\Phi)$ is integrable, then

$$
e^{-T} \mu_0(K) \le Z = \int \exp(-\Phi) \, d\mu_0 .
$$

Dashti and Stuart use this bound "repeatedly in what follows, without comment".

## Sources

- Masoumeh Dashti and Andrew M. Stuart, *The Bayesian approach to inverse problems*, in *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification*, Springer (2017), 311–428, DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-12385-1_7; numbering of arXiv:1302.6989v4 (2015), inequality (4.2) and Remarks 4.4.
