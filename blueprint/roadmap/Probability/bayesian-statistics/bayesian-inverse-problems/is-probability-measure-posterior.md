# The posterior is a probability measure

If $\exp(-\Phi)$ is $\mu_0$-integrable and $Z = \int \exp(-\Phi) \, d\mu_0 > 0$, the posterior
$Z^{-1} \exp(-\Phi) \, \mu_0$ has total mass one.
