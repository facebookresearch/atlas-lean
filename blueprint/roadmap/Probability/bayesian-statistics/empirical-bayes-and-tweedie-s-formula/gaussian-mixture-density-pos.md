# The marginal density of a Gaussian location mixture is positive

If $g$ is a finite nonzero measure on $\mathbb R$ and $v > 0$, the [marginal density](gaussian-mixture-density.md) satisfies $f(x) > 0$ for every $x$, because the Gaussian density is positive everywhere. So the [posterior mean](gaussian-posterior-mean.md) and $\log f$ are well defined at every point.
