# Law of the pooled count

For a nonempty finite set of cells with size factors `sⱼ > 0` and `S = ∑ⱼ sⱼ`, `a > 0`, `b > 0` and
`K ∈ ℕ`, the sum of the [joint marginal likelihood](pooled-marginal-lik.md) over the count vectors
`k` with `∑ⱼ kⱼ = K` is the
[negative binomial mass](../../distributions/discrete/negative-binomial-and-beta-binomial-distribution/negative-binomial/neg-binomial-real.md)
`NB(K; a, S a / b)`: the pooled count follows the one-cell
[marginal law](marginal-lik-eq-neg-binomial-real.md) at the total exposure `S`.
