# Limit of the posterior-moment expressions, deterministic core

For arbitrary real numbers `a` and `b`, let real numbers `S` and natural numbers `K` be indexed
along a filter, with `S → ∞` and `K / S → r₀`. Then `(a + K) / (b + S) → r₀` and
`(a + K) / (b + S)² → 0`. For `a > 0`, `b > 0` and `S ≥ 0` these expressions are the
[mean](integral-id-posterior.md) and the [variance](variance-posterior.md) of the posterior
`Gamma(a + K, b + S)`, and the prior washes out.
