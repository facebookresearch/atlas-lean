# Closed form of the marginal likelihood

For `a > 0`, `b > 0`, `s ≥ 0` and `k ∈ ℕ`, the [marginal likelihood](marginal-lik.md) is
`m(k) = a⁽ᵏ⁾ s^k b^a / ((b + s)^(a + k) k!)`, where `a⁽ᵏ⁾ = a (a + 1) ⋯ (a + k − 1)` is the rising
factorial, with `a⁽⁰⁾ = 1`, and `b^a`, `(b + s)^(a + k)` are real powers.
