# Counting histories by their number of ones

For $N, j \in \mathbb{N}$, there are $\binom{N}{j}$ histories $h \in \{0, 1\}^N$ with exactly $j$ ones.

## Sources

- Thomas L. Griffiths and Zoubin Ghahramani, *The Indian buffet process: an introduction and review*, J. Mach. Learn. Res. 12 (2011), 1185–1224, Section 4.5, before Equation 16.
