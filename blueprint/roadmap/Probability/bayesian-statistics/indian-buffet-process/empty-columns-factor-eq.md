# The empty-column factor as compound interest

For all $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ and $N, K \in \mathbb{N}$, the [empty-column factor](empty-columns-factor.md) satisfies

$$\Big(\frac{N!}{\prod_{j=1}^{N} (j + \alpha/K)}\Big)^K = \Big(\prod_{j=1}^{N} \Big(1 + \frac{\alpha/j}{K}\Big)^K\Big)^{-1},$$

with $\alpha/K = 0$ when $K = 0$ and the inverse of $0$ read as $0$.
