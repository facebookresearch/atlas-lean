# The history rates sum to the harmonic number

For every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, summed over the $2^N - 1$ histories $h \in \{0, 1\}^N$ with at least one one,

$$\sum_{h \ne 0} \frac{(m_h - 1)!\, (N - m_h)!}{N!} = H_N,$$

where $m_h$ is the [number of ones](ones-count.md) of $h$.
