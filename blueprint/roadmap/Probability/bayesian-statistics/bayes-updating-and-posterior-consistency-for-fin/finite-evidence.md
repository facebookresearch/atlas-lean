# Evidence of an observation under a finite prior

Let $\Theta$ be a finite set of hypotheses with prior weights $\pi : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $L : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$ be the likelihood weights of an observation. The evidence (marginal likelihood) of the observation is

$$E(\pi, L) = \sum_{\theta \in \Theta} \pi(\theta)\, L(\theta).$$
