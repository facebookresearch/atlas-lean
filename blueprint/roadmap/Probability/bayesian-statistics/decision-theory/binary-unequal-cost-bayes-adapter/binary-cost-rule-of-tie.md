# Binary Cost Rule of tie

Identify decisions and truths with $\{0, 1\}$ (false $= 0$, true $= 1$).
Let $\pi_0, \pi_1$ and $C_{ij}$ be real, $p_0, p_1 : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$, $\tau \in \{0,1\}$ and $x \in \mathcal{X}$. If

$$\pi_1 (C_{01} - C_{11})\, p_1(x) = \pi_0 (C_{10} - C_{00})\, p_0(x),$$

then the finite-cost threshold rule with tie decision $\tau$ decides $\tau$ at $x$.
