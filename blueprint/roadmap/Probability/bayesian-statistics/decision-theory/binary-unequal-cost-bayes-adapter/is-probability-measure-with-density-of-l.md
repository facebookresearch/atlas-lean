# Is Probability Measure with Density of lintegral eq one

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\mathcal{X}$ and $p : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ with

$$\int^{-} \max(p(x), 0) \, d\mu(x) = 1.$$

Then the measure $\max(p, 0) \cdot \mu$ with density $\max(p, 0)$ with respect to $\mu$ is a probability measure.
