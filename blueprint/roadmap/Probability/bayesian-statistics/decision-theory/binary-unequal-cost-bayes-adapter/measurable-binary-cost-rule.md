# Measurable binary Cost Rule

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a measurable space and $p_0, p_1 : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ measurable. For all real $\pi_0, \pi_1$, costs $C_{00}, C_{01}, C_{10}, C_{11}$ and tie decision $\tau \in \{0, 1\}$, the finite-cost threshold rule

$$x \mapsto \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \pi_0 (C_{10} - C_{00})\, p_0(x) < \pi_1 (C_{01} - C_{11})\, p_1(x), \\ \tau & \text{if the two sides are equal}, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

is a measurable map $\mathcal{X} \to \{0, 1\}$.
