# Commit Value

Let $\Omega$ (states) and $E$ (evidence outcomes) be finite sets, $w : \Omega \times E \to \mathbb{R}$ a joint weight, and $U(a, \omega)$ the utility of action $a$ in state $\omega$. The unnormalized expected utility of committing to action $a$ is

$$V_{\mathrm{commit}}(a) = \sum_{\omega \in \Omega} \sum_{e \in E} w(\omega, e)\, U(a, \omega).$$

The weights may be signed or unnormalized.
