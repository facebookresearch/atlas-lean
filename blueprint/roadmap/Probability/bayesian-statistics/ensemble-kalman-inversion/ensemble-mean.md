# Ensemble mean

For an ensemble $v^{(1)}, \dots, v^{(J)}$ in a real vector space, $\overline v = \frac1J \sum_{j=1}^J v^{(j)}$ (for $J = 0$ the empty ensemble has mean $0$). It
is used both for the particles, $\overline u$, and for their images under the forward map,
$\overline G$.
