# The ensemble mean stays in a subspace

If every member $v^{(j)}$ lies in a linear subspace $S$, so does $\overline v = \frac1J \sum_j v^{(j)}$.

## Sources

- Marco A. Iglesias, Kody J. H. Law and Andrew M. Stuart, *Ensemble Kalman methods for inverse problems*, Inverse Problems 29 (2013), no. 4, 045001, DOI 10.1088/0266-5611/29/4/045001; arXiv:1209.2736, proof of Theorem 2.1.
