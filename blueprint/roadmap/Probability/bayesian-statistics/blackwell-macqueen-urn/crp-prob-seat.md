# Seating multiplies the closed-form law by the seating probability

For every real $\theta > 0$, every set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every seating choice $c$, the [closed-form law](crp-prob.md) of the partition obtained by [seating](seat.md) customer $n$ by $c$ is $p_\theta(\pi)$ times the [seating weight](seat-weight.md) of $c$.
