# Seating a customer by a seating choice

For a set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, a seating choice is either "new table" or a block $B$ of $\pi$; seating customer $n$ by the choice gives the partition of $\{0, \dots, n\}$ [with a new table](new-table.md) in the first case and [with $n$ added to $B$](join-table.md) in the second.
