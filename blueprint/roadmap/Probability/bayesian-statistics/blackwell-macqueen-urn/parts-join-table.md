# Blocks of the partition after joining a table

For every set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every block $B$ of $\pi$, the blocks of the partition [after seating customer $n$ at the table $B$](join-table.md) are $B \cup \{n\}$ and the blocks of $\pi$ other than $B$, viewed as subsets of $\{0, \dots, n\}$.
