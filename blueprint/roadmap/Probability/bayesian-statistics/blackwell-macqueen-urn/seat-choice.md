# Seating choice distribution of a customer

For a real number $\theta > 0$ and a set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the probability distribution on the seating choices of customer $n$ (a new table or a block of $\pi$) given by the [seating weights](seat-weight.md): $\theta/(n + \theta)$ for a new table and $|B|/(n + \theta)$ for the table $B$.
