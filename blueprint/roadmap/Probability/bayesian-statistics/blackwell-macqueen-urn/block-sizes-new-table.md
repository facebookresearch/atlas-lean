# Block Sizes new Table

Let $\pi$ be a set partition of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, and let $\pi'$ be the partition of $\{0, \dots, n\}$ obtained by seating customer $n$ at a new table, so its blocks are the blocks of $\pi$ together with the singleton $\{n\}$. Then the multiset of block sizes of $\pi'$ is the multiset of block sizes of $\pi$ with one extra part equal to $1$:

$$\{\!\{ |B| : B \in \pi' \}\!\} = \{\!\{ 1 \}\!\} + \{\!\{ |B| : B \in \pi \}\!\}.$$
