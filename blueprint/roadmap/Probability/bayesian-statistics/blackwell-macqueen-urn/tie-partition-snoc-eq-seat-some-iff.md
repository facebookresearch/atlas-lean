# Last draw joins a block iff it matches that block

Let $X$ be a set, $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1} \in X$, $x \in X$, let $B$ be a block of the [tie partition](tie-partition.md) $\pi$ of $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ and $i \in B$. The tie partition of $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}, x)$ is obtained from $\pi$ by adding $n$ to the block $B$ (joining the table $B$) if and only if $x = x_i$.
