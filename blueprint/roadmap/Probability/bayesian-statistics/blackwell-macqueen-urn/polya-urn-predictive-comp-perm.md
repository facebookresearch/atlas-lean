# Pólya urn predictive law is invariant under reordering

For a measurable space $X$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, a measure $H$, $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1} \in X$ and a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the [Pólya urn predictive law](../dirichlet-process/polya-urn-predictive.md) given $(x_{\sigma(0)}, \dots, x_{\sigma(n-1)})$ equals the predictive law given $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$.
