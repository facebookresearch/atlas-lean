# Moments of the Dirichlet process for at most two draws

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $\alpha > 0$, $H$ a probability measure on $X$, $n \le 2$ and $A_0, \dots, A_{n-1} \subseteq X$ measurable. If $G$ is the [Dirichlet process](../dirichlet-process/dirichlet-process.md) with parameter $\alpha$ and base measure $H$, then

$$\mathbb{E}\Big[\prod_{i<n} G(A_i)\Big] = P_n(X_0 \in A_0, \dots, X_{n-1} \in A_{n-1})$$

for the [Blackwell–MacQueen urn law](blackwell-mac-queen.md) $P_n$. The [same identity for every $n$](../dirichlet-process/lintegral-dirichlet-process-prod.md) is the general moment formula.
