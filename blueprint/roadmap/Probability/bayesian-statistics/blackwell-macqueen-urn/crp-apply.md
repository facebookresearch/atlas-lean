# Law of the Chinese restaurant process

For every real $\theta > 0$ and every set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the [Chinese restaurant process](crp.md) gives $\pi$ the probability

$$\mathrm{CRP}_n(\pi) = \frac{\theta^{|\pi|} \prod_{B \in \pi} (|B| - 1)!}{\theta(\theta + 1) \cdots (\theta + n - 1)}.$$

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Combinatorial Stochastic Processes*, Lecture Notes in Math. 1875, Springer (2006), §3.1.
