# Exchangeability of the Blackwell–MacQueen urn

For $\alpha > 0$, a probability measure $H$ on a measurable space $X$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n - 1\}$, the image of the [Blackwell–MacQueen urn law](blackwell-mac-queen.md) $P_n$ under $(x_i)_i \mapsto (x_{\sigma(i)})_i$ is $P_n$: relabelling the draws does not change their joint law.

## Sources

- David Blackwell and James B. MacQueen, *Ferguson distributions via Pólya urn schemes*, Ann. Statist. 1 (1973), no. 2, 353–355, DOI 10.1214/aos/1176342372.
