# Measurability of the Pólya urn predictive law

For a measurable space $X$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, a measure $H$ on $X$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the [Pólya urn predictive law](../dirichlet-process/polya-urn-predictive.md) depends measurably on the previous draws $(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}) \in X^n$ (for the Giry σ-algebra on measures).
