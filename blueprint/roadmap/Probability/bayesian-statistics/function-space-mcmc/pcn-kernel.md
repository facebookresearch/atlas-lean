# The pCN proposal kernel

For a probability measure $\mu_0$ on a separable real normed space $E$ with its Borel σ-algebra and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, the pCN proposal as a Markov kernel: $Q(u, \cdot)$ is the law of $\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u + \beta \xi$ with
$\xi \sim \mu_0$. For $\mu_0 = N(0, C)$ this is $N(\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u, \beta^2 C)$.
