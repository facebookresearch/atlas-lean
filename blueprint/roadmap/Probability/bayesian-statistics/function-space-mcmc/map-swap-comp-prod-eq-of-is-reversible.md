# Detailed balance makes the joint law symmetric

If a Markov kernel $Q$ is reversible with respect to a finite measure $\mu_0$, then the joint law
$\mu_0(du) \, Q(u, dv)$ on pairs is invariant under $(u, v) \mapsto (v, u)$. Detailed balance is this
identity on rectangles $A \times B$; rectangles form a π-system generating the product σ-algebra.
