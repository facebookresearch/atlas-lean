# Measurability of the acceptance probability

If $\Phi$ is measurable, then $(u, v) \mapsto \min\{1, \exp(\Phi(u) - \Phi(v))\}$ is measurable on the product
space, so the accepted part $a(u, v)\, Q(u, dv)$ of the [Metropolis–Hastings kernel](mh-kernel.md) is a
kernel.
