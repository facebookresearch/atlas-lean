# Measurability of the pCN proposal map

On a separable normed space with its Borel σ-algebra, $(u, \xi) \mapsto \sqrt{1 - \beta^2}\, u + \beta \xi$ is
measurable for the product σ-algebra, which is what the construction of the [pCN kernel](pcn-kernel.md)
needs.
