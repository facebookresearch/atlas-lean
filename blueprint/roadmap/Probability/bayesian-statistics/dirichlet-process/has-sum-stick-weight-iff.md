# Stick-breaking weights sum to one iff the leftover stick tends to zero

If $b_k \in [0, 1]$ for every $k$, then $\sum_{k \ge 0} \pi_k(b) = 1$ if and only if $R_k(b) \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$, where $\pi_k(b)$ are the [stick-breaking weights](stick-weight.md) and $R_k(b)$ is the [leftover stick](stick-remainder.md).
