# Dirichlet process

For $\alpha > 0$ and a probability measure $H$ on a measurable space $X$, the Dirichlet process $\mathrm{DP}(\alpha, H)$ is the law, on measures on $X$ with the Giry σ-algebra, of the stick-breaking measure $\sum_k \pi_k \delta_{\theta_k}$ of [Sethuraman's construction](sethuraman.md): i.i.d. $\mathrm{Beta}(1, \alpha)$ proportions and, independently, i.i.d. $H$ atoms. Ferguson's (1973) definition by Dirichlet finite-dimensional marginals is equivalent by Sethuraman's theorem: see [finite-dimensional marginals of the Dirichlet process](map-dirichlet-process-partition.md).
