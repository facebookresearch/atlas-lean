# Variance of the Dirichlet process

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with concentration $\alpha$ and base measure $H$ given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space, and every measurable $A$,

$$\operatorname{Var}\big(G(A)\big) = \frac{H(A)\,(1 - H(A))}{\alpha + 1}.$$

## Sources

- Thomas S. Ferguson, *A Bayesian analysis of some nonparametric problems*, Ann. Statist. 1 (1973), no. 2, 209–230, DOI 10.1214/aos/1176342360.
