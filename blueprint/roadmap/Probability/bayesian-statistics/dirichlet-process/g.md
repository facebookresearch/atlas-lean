# Random measure of a stick-breaking process

For a [stick-breaking process](stick-breaking-process.md) with proportions $\beta_k$ and atoms $\theta_k$, the random measure is $G(\omega) = \sum_{k \ge 0} \max(\pi_k(\omega), 0)\, \delta_{\theta_k(\omega)}$, the [stick-breaking measure](stick-breaking-measure.md) of $(\beta_k(\omega))_k$ and $(\theta_k(\omega))_k$; almost surely all weights are nonnegative and $G = \sum_k \pi_k \delta_{\theta_k}$.
