# The Dirichlet-process weights sum to $1$ almost surely

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$, $\mu$-almost surely $\sum_{k \ge 0} \pi_k = 1$: the leftover stick tends to $0$.

## Sources

- Jayaram Sethuraman, *A constructive definition of Dirichlet priors*, Statist. Sinica 4 (1994), no. 2, 639–650.
