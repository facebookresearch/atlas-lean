# Measurability of the stick-breaking measure in its parameters

For a measurable space $X$, the map $(b, \theta) \mapsto \sum_k \max(\pi_k(b), 0)\, \delta_{\theta_k}$ from $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \times X^{\mathbb{N}}$ to measures on $X$, sending the proportions and atoms to the [stick-breaking measure](stick-breaking-measure.md), is measurable for the product σ-algebra and the Giry σ-algebra on measures (generated by the evaluations $\nu \mapsto \nu(A)$, $A$ measurable).
