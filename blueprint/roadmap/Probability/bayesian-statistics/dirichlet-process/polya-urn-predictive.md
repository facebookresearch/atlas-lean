# Blackwell–MacQueen urn predictive law

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, $H$ a measure on $X$ and $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1} \in X$. The predictive law of the Blackwell–MacQueen urn after these observations is

$$\frac{\alpha H + \sum_{i<n} \delta_{x_i}}{\alpha + n}.$$

For $\alpha > 0$ and a probability measure $H$ it is a [probability measure](is-probability-measure-polya-urn-predict.md). For other parameters the formula is read with $\alpha$ replaced by $\max(\alpha, 0)$ in the numerator, $\alpha + n$ replaced by $\max(\alpha + n, 0)$, and $1/0 = \infty$ (for instance $\alpha = n = 0$ gives the zero measure).
