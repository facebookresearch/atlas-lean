# Sethuraman's canonical model is a Dirichlet process

For $\alpha > 0$ and a probability measure $H$, [Sethuraman's construction](sethuraman.md) is a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with concentration $\alpha$ and base measure $H$. In particular Dirichlet processes exist for every $\alpha > 0$ and every probability measure $H$.
