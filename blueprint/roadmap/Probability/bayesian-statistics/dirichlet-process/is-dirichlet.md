# Sethuraman's stick-breaking representation of the Dirichlet process

A [stick-breaking process](stick-breaking-process.md) on $(\Omega, \mu)$ with base measure $H$ is a Dirichlet process $\mathrm{DP}(\alpha, H)$ with concentration $\alpha$ if $\alpha > 0$ and every proportion $\beta_k$ has law $\mathrm{Beta}(1, \alpha)$. This is Sethuraman's construction; Sethuraman (1994, Theorem 3.4) showed that the resulting random measure has Ferguson's Dirichlet finite-dimensional marginals (see [finite-dimensional marginals of the Dirichlet process](map-dirichlet-process-partition.md)).

## Sources

- Jayaram Sethuraman, *A constructive definition of Dirichlet priors*, Statist. Sinica 4 (1994), no. 2, 639–650, Section 2 and Theorem 3.4.
