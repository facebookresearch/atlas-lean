# Beta law is concentrated on the unit interval

For all real $a, b$, let $\mathrm{Beta}(a, b)$ be the measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with density $x^{a-1}(1 - x)^{b-1}/B(a, b)$ on $(0, 1)$ and $0$ elsewhere (negative values of the density replaced by $0$). It is concentrated on $[0, 1]$: almost every $x$ lies in $[0, 1]$. For $a, b > 0$ it is the Beta probability distribution.
