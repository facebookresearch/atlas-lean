# Stick-breaking pieces and leftover make up the whole stick

For all real proportions $b = (b_k)_{k \ge 0}$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\sum_{k<n} \pi_k(b) + R_n(b) = 1,$$

where $\pi_k(b)$ are the [stick-breaking weights](stick-weight.md) and $R_n(b)$ is the [leftover stick](stick-remainder.md).
