# Mixed second moment of the Dirichlet process

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with concentration $\alpha$ and base measure $H$ given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space, and all measurable $A, B$,

$$\mathbb{E}[G(A)\, G(B)] = \frac{\alpha H(A) H(B) + H(A \cap B)}{\alpha + 1}.$$

## Sources

- Thomas S. Ferguson, *A Bayesian analysis of some nonparametric problems*, Ann. Statist. 1 (1973), no. 2, 209–230, DOI 10.1214/aos/1176342360.
