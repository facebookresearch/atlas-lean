# Mean of the Dirichlet process

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with base measure $H$ given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space, $\mathbb{E}[G(A)] = H(A)$ for every measurable $A$.

## Sources

- Thomas S. Ferguson, *A Bayesian analysis of some nonparametric problems*, Ann. Statist. 1 (1973), no. 2, 209–230, DOI 10.1214/aos/1176342360.
