# Stick-breaking measure

For proportions $b = (b_k)_{k \ge 0}$ of real numbers and atoms $\theta = (\theta_k)_{k \ge 0}$ in a measurable space $X$, the stick-breaking measure is the discrete measure

$$\sum_{k \ge 0} \max\big(\pi_k(b), 0\big)\, \delta_{\theta_k}$$

with the [stick-breaking weights](stick-weight.md) $\pi_k(b)$; negative weights, which do not occur for proportions in $[0, 1]$, are replaced by $0$.
