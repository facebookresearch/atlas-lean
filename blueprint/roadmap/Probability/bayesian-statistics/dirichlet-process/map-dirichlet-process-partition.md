# Finite-dimensional marginals of the Dirichlet process

Let $\alpha > 0$, $H$ a probability measure on a measurable space $X$, and $A_0, \dots, A_m$ a measurable partition of $X$ (pairwise disjoint, with union $X$) such that $H(A_i) > 0$ for every $i$. If $G \sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha, H)$ is the [Dirichlet process](dirichlet-process.md), then

$$\big(G(A_0), \dots, G(A_m)\big) \sim \mathrm{Dirichlet}\big(\alpha H(A_0), \dots, \alpha H(A_m)\big).$$
