# Sethuraman's canonical probability space

For $\alpha > 0$ and a probability measure $H$ on a measurable space $X$, Sethuraman's canonical space is $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \times X^{\mathbb{N}}$ with the product probability measure $\big(\bigotimes_{k} \mathrm{Beta}(1, \alpha)\big) \otimes \big(\bigotimes_k H\big)$: i.i.d. $\mathrm{Beta}(1, \alpha)$ proportions and, independently, i.i.d. $H$ atoms.
