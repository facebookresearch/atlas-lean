# Sethuraman's construction

For $\alpha > 0$ and a probability measure $H$ on $X$, Sethuraman's construction is the [stick-breaking process](stick-breaking-process.md) on [Sethuraman's canonical space](sethuraman-measure.md) whose proportions $\beta_k$ and atoms $\theta_k$ are the coordinates of the two factors, with base measure $H$.
