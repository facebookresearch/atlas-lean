# Probability that two Dirichlet process draws pick the same piece

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with concentration $\alpha$ given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space, the [expected sum of squared weights](sum-sq-weight.md) is

$$\sum_{k \ge 0} \mathbb{E}[\pi_k^2] = \frac{1}{1 + \alpha}:$$

two draws from a Dirichlet process pick the same piece with probability $1/(1 + \alpha)$, the probability that the second customer of the Chinese restaurant process with parameter $\alpha$ joins the first one's table.
