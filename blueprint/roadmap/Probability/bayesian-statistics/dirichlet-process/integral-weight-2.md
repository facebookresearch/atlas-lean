# Mean of a Dirichlet-process weight

For a [Dirichlet process](is-dirichlet.md) with concentration $\alpha$ given by a stick-breaking process on a probability space and every $k \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\mathbb{E}[\pi_k] = \frac{\alpha^k}{(1 + \alpha)^{k+1}}$$

(this is $\alpha^{k-1}/(1 + \alpha)^k$ for Sethuraman's weight $p_k$, indexed from $1$).

## Sources

- Jayaram Sethuraman, *A constructive definition of Dirichlet priors*, Statist. Sinica 4 (1994), no. 2, 639–650.
