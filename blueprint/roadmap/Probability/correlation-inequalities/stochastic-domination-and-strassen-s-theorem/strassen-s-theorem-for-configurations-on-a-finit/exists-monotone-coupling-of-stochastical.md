# Strassen's theorem for configurations on a finite set

Let $E$ be a finite set and $\mu, \nu$ probability measures on $\{0,1\}^E$ with $\mu \preceq \nu$ ([stochastic domination](../stochastic-domination-of-measures-on-configurati/stochastically-dominated.md)). Then there is a finite measure on $\{0,1\}^E \times \{0,1\}^E$ that is a [monotone coupling](../monotone-couplings-and-stochastic-domination/monotone-coupling.md) of $\mu$ and $\nu$. The converse is the [easy direction of Strassen's theorem](../monotone-couplings-and-stochastic-domination/stochastically-dominated.md).

## Sources

- V. Strassen, *The existence of probability measures with given marginals*, Ann. Math. Statist. 36 (1965), 423–439.
