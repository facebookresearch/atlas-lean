# Measure on ordered pairs from a mass vector

Let $E$ be a finite set and $c$ a real function on the ordered pairs $\{(\omega, \omega') : \omega \le \omega'\}$ of configurations in $\{0,1\}^E$. The associated measure on $\{0,1\}^E \times \{0,1\}^E$ is

$$\kappa_c = \sum_{\omega \le \omega'} \max(c(\omega, \omega'), 0)\, \delta_{(\omega, \omega')},$$

putting mass $c(q)$ on each ordered pair $q$ where $c(q) \ge 0$ (negative values are replaced by $0$).
