# Stochastic domination of measures on configurations

Let $E$ be a set and $\mu, \nu$ measures on $\{0,1\}^E$ (with its product σ-algebra). The measure $\mu$ is *stochastically dominated* by $\nu$, written $\mu \preceq \nu$, if

$$\mu(A) \le \nu(A) \quad \text{in } [0, \infty]$$

for every measurable increasing event $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$. No finiteness or normalisation is assumed.
