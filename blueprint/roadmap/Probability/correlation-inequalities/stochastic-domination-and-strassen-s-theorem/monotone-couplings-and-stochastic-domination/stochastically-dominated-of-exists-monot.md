# Existence of a monotone coupling implies stochastic domination

Let $E$ be a finite set and $\mu, \nu$ measures on $\{0,1\}^E$. If there is a finite measure $\kappa$ on $\{0,1\}^E \times \{0,1\}^E$ that is a [monotone coupling](monotone-coupling.md) of $\mu$ and $\nu$, then $\mu$ is stochastically dominated by $\nu$.
