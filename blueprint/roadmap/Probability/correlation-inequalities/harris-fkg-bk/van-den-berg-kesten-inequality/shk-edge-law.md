# Edge law of inhomogeneous bond percolation

Let $E$ be a set, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $J : E \to \mathbb{R}$. The edge law of inhomogeneous bond percolation gives the edge $e$ the weight $1 - e^{-\beta J_e}$ for the value $1$ (open) and $e^{-\beta J_e}$ for the value $0$ (closed). It is a probability law on $\{0,1\}$ when $\beta J_e \ge 0$.
