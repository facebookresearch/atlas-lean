# Open subgraph of a configuration

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a vertex set $V$ and $\omega \in \{0,1\}^{V^{(2)}}$ a configuration on the set $V^{(2)}$ of unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of vertices (pairs $\{x, x\}$ included). The open subgraph of $G$ is the graph on $V$ in which $x$ and $y$ are adjacent if they are adjacent in $G$ and $\omega(\{x, y\}) = 1$.
