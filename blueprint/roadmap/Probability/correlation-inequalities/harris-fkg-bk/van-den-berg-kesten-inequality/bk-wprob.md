# BK inequality for product weights on $n$ ordered coordinates

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\varphi : \{0,\dots,n-1\} \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\varphi_i(b) \ge 0$ and $\varphi_i(0) + \varphi_i(1) = 1$ for all $i, b$. For all increasing events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}}$,

$$P_\varphi(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B),$$

where $P_\varphi$ is the [weighted probability](wprob.md).
