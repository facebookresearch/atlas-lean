# Disjoint occurrence commutes with relabeling coordinates

Let $e : E \to F$ be a bijection and $\tau(\omega) = \omega \circ e$ the [relabelling map](cfg-equiv.md) $\{0,1\}^F \to \{0,1\}^E$. For all events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$\tau^{-1}(A \mathbin{\square} B) = \tau^{-1}(A) \mathbin{\square} \tau^{-1}(B).$$
