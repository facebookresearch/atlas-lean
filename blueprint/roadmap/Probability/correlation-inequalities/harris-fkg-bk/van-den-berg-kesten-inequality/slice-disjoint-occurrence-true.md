# True slice of a disjoint occurrence

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n\}}$, with [slices](slice.md) $A_0, A_1$ and $B_0, B_1$ at the first coordinate. Then

$$(A \mathbin{\square} B)_1 \subseteq (A_0 \mathbin{\square} B_1) \cup (A_1 \mathbin{\square} B_0):$$

the first coordinate is used by at most one of the two witnesses.
