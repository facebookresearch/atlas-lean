# Product weight of a configuration

Let $E$ be a finite set and $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$, where $\varphi_x(b)$ is the weight of the value $b$ at the coordinate $x$. The product weight of a configuration $\omega \in \{0,1\}^E$ is

$$\Pi_\varphi(\omega) = \prod_{x \in E} \varphi_x(\omega(x)).$$
