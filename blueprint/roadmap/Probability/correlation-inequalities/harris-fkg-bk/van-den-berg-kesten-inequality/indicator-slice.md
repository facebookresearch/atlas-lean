# Indicator of an event at a prepended configuration

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $S \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n\}}$, $x \in \{0,1\}$ and $\omega' \in \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}}$. Then $\mathbf{1}_S(x, \omega') = \mathbf{1}_{S_x}(\omega')$, where $(x, \omega')$ is the configuration with first coordinate $x$ followed by $\omega'$ and $S_x$ is the [slice](slice.md) of $S$ at $x$.
