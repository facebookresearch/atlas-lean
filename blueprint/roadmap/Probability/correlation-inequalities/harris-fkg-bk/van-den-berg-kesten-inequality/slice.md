# Slice of an event at the first coordinate

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $C \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0, \dots, n\}}$ an event. For $x \in \{0,1\}$ and $\omega' \in \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}}$, write $(x, \omega')$ for the configuration on $\{0,\dots,n\}$ with value $x$ at $0$ and $\omega'(j)$ at $j+1$. The slice of $C$ at $x$ is

$$C_x = \{\omega' \in \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}} : (x, \omega') \in C\}.$$
