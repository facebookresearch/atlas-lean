# Product weight is invariant under relabeling coordinates

Let $E$ and $F$ be finite sets, $e : E \to F$ a bijection, $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\omega \in \{0,1\}^F$. Then

$$\Pi_\varphi(\omega \circ e) = \Pi_{\varphi'}(\omega), \qquad \varphi'_y = \varphi_{e^{-1}(y)},$$

where $\Pi$ is the [product weight](pweight.md).
