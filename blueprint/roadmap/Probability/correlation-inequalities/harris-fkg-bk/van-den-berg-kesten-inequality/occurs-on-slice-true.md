# Occurrence with first coordinate true passes to the true slice

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $C \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n\}}$, $K \subseteq \{0,\dots,n\}$ and $\omega' \in \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}}$. If $C$ occurs on $K$ at $(1, \omega')$, then the [slice](slice.md) $C_1$ occurs on the [shifted set](slice-set.md) $K' = \{j : j+1 \in K\}$ at $\omega'$.
