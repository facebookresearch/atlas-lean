# Support of the first-exit decomposition events

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $S, \Lambda \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ finite. The first-exit support is the set of unordered pairs of sites consisting of all pairs $\{x, y\}$ with $x, y \in S$, the nearest-neighbour boundary edges $\{x, y\}$ with $x \in S$ and $y \notin S$, and all pairs $\{x, y\}$ with $x, y \in \Lambda$. It is the support of the events in the first-exit decomposition.
