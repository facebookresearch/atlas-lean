# BK inequality for three increasing events

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. Let $F \subseteq E$ be finite and $P, Q, R \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ increasing events depending only on the coordinates in $F$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p\big(P \mathbin{\square} (Q \mathbin{\square} R)\big) \le \mathbb{P}_p(P)\, \mathbb{P}_p(Q)\, \mathbb{P}_p(R).$$
