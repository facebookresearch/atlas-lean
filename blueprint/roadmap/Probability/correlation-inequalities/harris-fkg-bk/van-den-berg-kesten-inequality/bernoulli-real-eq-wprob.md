# Bernoulli product measure as a weighted probability

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$, $\beta_p$ the Bernoulli($p$) measure on $\{0,1\}$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. For every $S \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(S) = P_\varphi(S), \qquad \varphi_x(b) = \beta_p(\{b\}),$$

where $P_\varphi$ is the [weighted probability](wprob.md).
