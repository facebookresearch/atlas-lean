# Van den Berg–Kesten inequality

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$ (independent coordinates, each equal to $1$ with probability $p$). For all increasing events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A)\, \mathbb{P}_p(B),$$

where $A \mathbin{\square} B$ is the [disjoint occurrence](disjoint-occurrence.md) of $A$ and $B$.

## Sources

- J. van den Berg and H. Kesten, *Inequalities with applications to percolation and reliability*, J. Appl. Probab. 22 (1985), 556–569.
