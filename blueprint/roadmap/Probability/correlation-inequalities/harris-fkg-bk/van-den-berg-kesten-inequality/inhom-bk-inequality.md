# BK inequality for inhomogeneous Bernoulli measures on a finite set

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p : E \to [0,1]$, and $\mathbb{P}_{\mathbf{p}}$ the [inhomogeneous Bernoulli product measure](inhom-bernoulli-product-measure.md) on $\{0,1\}^E$. For all increasing events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$\mathbb{P}_{\mathbf{p}}(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_{\mathbf{p}}(A)\, \mathbb{P}_{\mathbf{p}}(B).$$
