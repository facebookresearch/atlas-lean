# Open connection between two vertices inside a set

Let $G$ be a simple graph on $V$, $\omega \in \{0,1\}^{V^{(2)}}$ a configuration on unordered pairs, $A \subseteq V$ and $x, y \in A$. Then $x$ and $y$ are connected inside $A$ if there is a path from $x$ to $y$ in the [open subgraph](open-sub.md) of $G$ all of whose vertices lie in $A$, that is, if $y$ is reachable from $x$ in the subgraph of the open subgraph induced by $A$.
