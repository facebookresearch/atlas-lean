# Configuration relabeling agrees with the general arrow equivalence

Let $e : E \to F$ be a bijection. The [relabelling map](cfg-equiv.md) $\omega \mapsto \omega \circ e$ coincides with the bijection $\{0,1\}^F \to \{0,1\}^E$ obtained by transporting functions along $e^{-1}$ on the domain and along the identity on $\{0,1\}$.
