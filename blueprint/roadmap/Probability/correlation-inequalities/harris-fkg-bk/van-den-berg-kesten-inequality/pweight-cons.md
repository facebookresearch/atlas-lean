# Product weight factors over the first coordinate

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varphi : \{0,\dots,n\} \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$, $x \in \{0,1\}$ and $\omega' \in \{0,1\}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}}$. Then

$$\Pi_\varphi(x, \omega') = \varphi_0(x)\, \Pi_\psi(\omega'), \qquad \psi_j = \varphi_{j+1},$$

where $\Pi$ denotes the [product weight](pweight.md) and $(x, \omega')$ the configuration with first coordinate $x$ followed by $\omega'$.
