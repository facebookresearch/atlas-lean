# Section of a disjoint occurrence

Let $E$ be a set, $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ and $F \subseteq E$ finite. With $X_F = \{\eta \in \{0,1\}^F : \bar\eta \in X\}$ the [section](../harris-inequality/ih-section.md) of an event $X$ on $F$ ($\bar\eta$ equals $\eta$ on $F$ and $0$ off $F$),

$$(A \mathbin{\square} B)_F \subseteq A_F \mathbin{\square} B_F.$$
