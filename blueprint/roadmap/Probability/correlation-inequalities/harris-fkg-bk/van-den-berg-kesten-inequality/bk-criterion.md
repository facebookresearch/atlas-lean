# BK criterion for Bernoulli product measures

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$, $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$ and $s$ a finite index set. Let $P_i, Q_i, R_i \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ ($i \in s$) be increasing events. If

$$C \subseteq \bigcup_{i \in s} P_i \mathbin{\square} (Q_i \mathbin{\square} R_i),$$

then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(C) \le \sum_{i \in s} \mathbb{P}_p(P_i)\, \mathbb{P}_p(Q_i)\, \mathbb{P}_p(R_i).$$

## Sources

- J. van den Berg and H. Kesten, *Inequalities with applications to percolation and reliability*, J. Appl. Probab. 22 (1985), 556–569.
