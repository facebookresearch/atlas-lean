# Occurrence of a finitely dependent event within its window

Let $E$ be a set, $F \subseteq E$ finite, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ an event depending only on the coordinates in $F$, $K \subseteq E$ and $\omega, \omega' \in \{0,1\}^E$. If $A$ occurs on $K$ at $\omega$ and $\omega'$ agrees with $\omega$ on $F$, then $A$ occurs on $K \cap F$ at $\omega'$.
