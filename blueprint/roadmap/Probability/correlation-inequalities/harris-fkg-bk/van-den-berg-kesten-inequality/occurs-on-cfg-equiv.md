# Occurrence is invariant under relabeling coordinates

Let $e : E \to F$ be a bijection, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$, $K \subseteq E$ and $\omega \in \{0,1\}^F$. Then the event $\{\eta \in \{0,1\}^F : \eta \circ e \in A\}$ occurs on $e(K)$ at $\omega$ if and only if $A$ occurs on $K$ at $\omega \circ e$.
