# BK inequality on countable configuration spaces

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. Let $F \subseteq E$ be finite and $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ increasing events depending only on the coordinates in $F$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A)\, \mathbb{P}_p(B).$$

## Sources

- J. van den Berg and H. Kesten, *Inequalities with applications to percolation and reliability*, J. Appl. Probab. 22 (1985), 556–569.
