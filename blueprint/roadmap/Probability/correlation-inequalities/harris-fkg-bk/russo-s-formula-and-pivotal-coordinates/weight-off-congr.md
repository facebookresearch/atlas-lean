# Off-coordinate weight depends only on the other coordinates

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $e \in E$, and let $\omega, \omega' \in \{0,1\}^E$ satisfy $\omega(e') = \omega'(e')$ for every $e' \ne e$. Then $W_p^{(e)}(\omega) = W_p^{(e)}(\omega')$, where $W_p^{(e)}$ is the [product of coordinate weights off $e$](weight-off.md).
