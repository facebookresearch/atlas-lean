# Contribution of one coordinate to Russo's formula

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ an increasing event and $e \in E$. Then

$$\sum_{\omega \in A} W_p^{(e)}(\omega)\, s_e(\omega) = \mathrm{Piv}_p(A, e),$$

where $s_e(\omega) = 1$ if $\omega(e) = 1$ and $-1$ otherwise, $W_p^{(e)}$ is the [weight off $e$](weight-off.md) and $\mathrm{Piv}_p(A, e)$ the [pivotal weight](pivotal-prob.md). This is the contribution of $e$ to the derivative of $P_p(A)$.
