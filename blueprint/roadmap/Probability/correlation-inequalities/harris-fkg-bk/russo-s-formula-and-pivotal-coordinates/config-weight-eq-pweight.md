# Configuration weight as a product weight

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\omega \in \{0,1\}^E$. The [Bernoulli product weight](config-weight.md) $W_p(\omega)$ equals the general product weight $\prod_{x \in E} \varphi_x(\omega(x))$ with coordinate weights $\varphi_x(1) = p$ and $\varphi_x(0) = 1 - p$ for every $x$.
