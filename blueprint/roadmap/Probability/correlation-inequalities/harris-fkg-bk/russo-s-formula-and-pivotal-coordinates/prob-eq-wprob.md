# Event weight as a product-weight probability

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$. The [Bernoulli weight](prob.md) $P_p(A)$ equals the product-weight mass $\sum_{\omega \in A} \prod_{x \in E} \varphi_x(\omega(x))$ with coordinate weights $\varphi_x(1) = p$ and $\varphi_x(0) = 1 - p$ for every $x$.
