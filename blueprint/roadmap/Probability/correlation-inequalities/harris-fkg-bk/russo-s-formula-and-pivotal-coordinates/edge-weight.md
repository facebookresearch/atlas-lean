# Bernoulli weight of a single coordinate

Let $E$ be a set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $\omega \in \{0,1\}^E$ and $e \in E$. The weight of the coordinate $e$ in $\omega$ is

$$w_p(\omega, e) = \begin{cases} p & \omega(e) = 1, \\ 1 - p & \omega(e) = 0. \end{cases}$$

No restriction is placed on $p$; for $p \in [0,1]$ it is the probability of the value $\omega(e)$ under a Bernoulli($p$) law.
