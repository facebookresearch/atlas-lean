# Pivotal coordinate for an event

Let $E$ be a set, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ an event, $e \in E$ and $\omega \in \{0,1\}^E$. The coordinate $e$ is *pivotal* for $A$ at $\omega$ if exactly one of the configurations $\omega^{e,1}$ and $\omega^{e,0}$ (that is, $\omega$ with $e$ opened, respectively closed) lies in $A$. The condition does not depend on the value $\omega(e)$.
