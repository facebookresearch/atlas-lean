# Bernoulli weight of an event

Let $E$ be a finite set and $p \in \mathbb{R}$. The weight of an event $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ is

$$P_p(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} W_p(\omega),$$

where $W_p$ is the [Bernoulli product weight](config-weight.md). For $p \in [0,1]$ it is the probability of $A$ under independent Bernoulli($p$) coordinates.
