# Weight of the event that a coordinate is pivotal

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ and $e \in E$. The pivotal weight of $e$ for $A$ is the [weight](prob.md) of the event that $e$ is [pivotal](is-pivotal.md) for $A$:

$$\mathrm{Piv}_p(A, e) = \sum_{\omega :\ e \text{ pivotal for } A \text{ at } \omega} W_p(\omega).$$
