# Section of an event on a finite coordinate set

Let $E$ be a set, $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ an event and $F \subseteq E$ finite. The section of $A$ on $F$ is the set of configurations $\eta \in \{0,1\}^F$ whose [closed filling](ih-fill.md) $\bar\eta$ (equal to $\eta$ on $F$ and to $0$ off $F$) lies in $A$:

$$A_F = \{\eta \in \{0,1\}^F : \bar\eta \in A\}.$$
