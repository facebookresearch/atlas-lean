# Filling a finite configuration with closed coordinates

Let $E$ be a set and $F \subseteq E$ a finite subset. For a configuration $\eta \in \{0,1\}^F$, its closed filling is the configuration $\bar\eta \in \{0,1\}^E$ given by

$$\bar\eta(e) = \begin{cases} \eta(e) & e \in F, \\ 0 & e \notin F. \end{cases}$$
