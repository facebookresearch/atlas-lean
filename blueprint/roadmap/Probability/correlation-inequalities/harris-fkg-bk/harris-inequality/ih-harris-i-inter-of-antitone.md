# Positive correlation passes to decreasing intersections

Let $E$ be a set and $\mu$ a probability measure on $\{0,1\}^E$ (with its product σ-algebra). Let $(A_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and $(B_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be decreasing sequences of measurable events such that

$$\mu(A_n)\,\mu(B_n) \le \mu(A_n \cap B_n) \quad \text{for every } n.$$

Then, with $A = \bigcap_n A_n$ and $B = \bigcap_n B_n$,

$$\mu(A)\,\mu(B) \le \mu(A \cap B).$$
