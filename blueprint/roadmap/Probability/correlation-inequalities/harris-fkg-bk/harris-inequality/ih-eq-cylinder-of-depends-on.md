# Finitely dependent event is a cylinder over its section

Let $E$ be a set, $F \subseteq E$ finite, and $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ an event depending only on the coordinates in $F$. Then $A$ is the cylinder over its [section](ih-section.md) $A_F$:

$$A = \{\omega \in \{0,1\}^E : \omega|_F \in A_F\}.$$
