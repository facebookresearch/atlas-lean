# Harris inequality for finitely dependent increasing events

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0,1]$, and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$ with parameter $p$. Let $F \subseteq E$ be finite and let $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ be increasing events that both depend only on the coordinates in $F$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A)\,\mathbb{P}_p(B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A \cap B).$$

## Sources

- T. E. Harris, *A lower bound for the critical probability in a certain percolation process*, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 56 (1960), 13–20.
