# Continuity from above of a finite measure, real-valued form

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and $(A_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ a decreasing sequence of measurable sets. Then

$$\mu(A_n) \to \mu\Big(\bigcap_n A_n\Big) \quad \text{as } n \to \infty,$$

as a limit of real numbers.
