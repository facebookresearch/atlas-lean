# Reimer's inequality for product weights, assuming the core case

Assume the [statement of Reimer's inequality](reimer-wprob-core.md) for product weights on $\{0,1\}^n$ for every $n$. Let $E$ be a finite set and $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ a product probability weight ($\varphi_x(b) \ge 0$, $\varphi_x(0) + \varphi_x(1) = 1$). Then for all events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$P_\varphi(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B).$$
