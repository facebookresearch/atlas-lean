# Reimer's inequality for Bernoulli measures, assuming the core case

Assume the [statement of Reimer's inequality](reimer-wprob-core.md) for product weights on $\{0,1\}^n$ for every $n$. Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. Then for all events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A)\, \mathbb{P}_p(B).$$
