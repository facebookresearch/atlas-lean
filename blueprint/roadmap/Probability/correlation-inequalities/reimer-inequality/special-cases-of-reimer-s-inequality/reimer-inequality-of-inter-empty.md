# Reimer's inequality for disjoint events

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. If $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ satisfy $A \cap B = \emptyset$, then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A)\, \mathbb{P}_p(B);$$

indeed the left-hand side is $0$ since $A \mathbin{\square} B \subseteq A \cap B$.
