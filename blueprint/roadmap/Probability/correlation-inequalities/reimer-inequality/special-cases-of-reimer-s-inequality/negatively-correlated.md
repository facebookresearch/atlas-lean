# Negatively correlated events

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ a weight and $P_\varphi(S) = \sum_{\omega \in S} \prod_x \varphi_x(\omega(x))$. Events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ are *negatively correlated* under $\varphi$ if

$$P_\varphi(A \cap B) \le P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B).$$
