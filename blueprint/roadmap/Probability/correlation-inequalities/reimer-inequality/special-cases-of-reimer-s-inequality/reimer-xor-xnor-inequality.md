# Reimer's inequality for the XOR and XNOR events

Let $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^{\{0,1\}}$. With $X = \{\omega_0 \ne \omega_1\}$ and $Y = \{\omega_0 = \omega_1\}$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(X \mathbin{\square} Y) \le \mathbb{P}_p(X)\, \mathbb{P}_p(Y).$$

The events $X$ and $Y$ are neither increasing nor decreasing, and they are disjoint.
