# Lower sets are families closed under erasing an element

Let $\alpha$ be a set and $\mathcal{A}$ a finite family of finite subsets of $\alpha$. Then $\mathcal{A}$ is a lower set (closed under taking finite subsets: $s \in \mathcal{A}$ and $t \subseteq s$ imply $t \in \mathcal{A}$) if and only if $s \setminus \{i\} \in \mathcal{A}$ for every $s \in \mathcal{A}$ and every $i \in \alpha$.
