# Doubled family of disjoint pairs

Let $\alpha$ be a set and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ finite families of finite subsets of $\alpha$. The doubled family of their disjoint pairs is the family of subsets of $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$

$$\mathcal{A} \boxtimes \mathcal{B} = \{S \oplus T : S \in \mathcal{A},\ T \in \mathcal{B},\ S \cap T = \emptyset\},$$

where $S \oplus T$ places $S$ on the left copy and $T$ on the right copy of $\alpha$ ([encoding of pairs](dbl.md)).
