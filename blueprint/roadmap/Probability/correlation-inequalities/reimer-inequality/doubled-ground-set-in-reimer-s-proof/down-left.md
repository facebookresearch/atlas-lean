# Down-compression at the left copy of a coordinate

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a finite family of finite subsets of $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$. The down-compression of $\mathcal{A}$ at the left copy $i^L$ replaces each $S \in \mathcal{A}$ with $i^L \in S$ by $S \setminus \{i^L\}$ whenever $S \setminus \{i^L\} \notin \mathcal{A}$, and keeps every other member.
