# Size of the doubled disjoint-pair family

Let $\alpha$ be a set and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ finite families of finite subsets of $\alpha$. The [doubled family](box-doubled.md) $\mathcal{A} \boxtimes \mathcal{B}$ has as many members as there are disjoint pairs in $\mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B}$:

$$|\mathcal{A} \boxtimes \mathcal{B}| = \#\{(S, T) \in \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} : S \cap T = \emptyset\}.$$
