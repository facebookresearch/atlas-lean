# Up-compression at a point of the doubled ground set

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $a \in \alpha \sqcup \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a finite family of finite subsets of $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$. The up-compression of $\mathcal{A}$ at $a$ replaces each $S \in \mathcal{A}$ with $a \notin S$ by $S \cup \{a\}$ whenever $S \cup \{a\} \notin \mathcal{A}$, and keeps every other member.
