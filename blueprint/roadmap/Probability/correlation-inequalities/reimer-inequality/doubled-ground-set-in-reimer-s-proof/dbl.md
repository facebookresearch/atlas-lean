# Encoding a pair of finsets in the doubled ground set

Let $\alpha$ be a set and $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$ the doubled ground set, whose elements are the left copies $a^L$ and the right copies $a^R$ of the elements $a \in \alpha$. A pair $(S, T)$ of finite subsets of $\alpha$ is represented by the finite subset

$$S \oplus T = \{a^L : a \in S\} \cup \{a^R : a \in T\} \subseteq \alpha \sqcup \alpha.$$
