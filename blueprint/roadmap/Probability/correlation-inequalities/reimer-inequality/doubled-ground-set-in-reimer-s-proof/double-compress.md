# Double compression at a coordinate

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a finite family of finite subsets of $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$. The double compression of $\mathcal{A}$ at $i$ is the [down-compression at the left copy](down-left.md) $i^L$ followed by the [up-compression at the right copy](up-right.md) $i^R$.
