# Weighted disjoint pairs bounded by the product of family weights

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $w_x(b) \ge 0$ for all $x, b$. For all families $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ of subsets of $\alpha$,

$$D_w(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \le M_w(\mathcal{A})\, M_w(\mathcal{B}),$$

where $D_w$ is the [weighted number of disjoint pairs](wdpairs.md) and $M_w$ the [family weight](fmarg.md): drop the disjointness constraint.
