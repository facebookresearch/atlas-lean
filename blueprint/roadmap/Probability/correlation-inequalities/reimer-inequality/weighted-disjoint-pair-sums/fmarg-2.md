# Weight of a family off one coordinate

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a family of subsets of $\alpha$. The weight of $\mathcal{A}$ off $i$ is $M_w^{(i)}(\mathcal{A}) = \sum_{S \in \mathcal{A}} w^{(i)}(S)$, with $w^{(i)}$ the [product weight off $i$](fwt-2.md).
