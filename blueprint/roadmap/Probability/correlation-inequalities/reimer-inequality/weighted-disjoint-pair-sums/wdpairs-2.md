# Weighted number of disjoint pairs off one coordinate

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ families of subsets of $\alpha$. The weighted number of disjoint pairs off $i$ is

$$D_w^{(i)}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) = \sum_{\substack{(S, T) \in \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} \\ S \cap T = \emptyset}} w^{(i)}(S)\, w^{(i)}(T),$$

with $w^{(i)}$ the [product weight off $i$](fwt-2.md).
