# Weighted number of disjoint pairs

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ families of subsets of $\alpha$. The weighted number of disjoint pairs is

$$D_w(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) = \sum_{\substack{(S, T) \in \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} \\ S \cap T = \emptyset}} w(S)\, w(T),$$

with $w(S)$ the [product weight](fwt.md) of $S$.
