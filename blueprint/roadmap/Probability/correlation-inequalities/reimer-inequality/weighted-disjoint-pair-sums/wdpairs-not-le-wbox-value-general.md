# Box-value bound fails for non-symmetric weights

There is a nonnegative weight $w$ on the one-point set $\{0\}$ such that, for $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{B} = \{\emptyset\}$,

$$D_w(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) > \Big(\prod_x w_x(1)\Big)^2 \,|\mathcal{U}|,$$

where $\mathcal{U}$ is the doubled family of the disjoint pairs of $\mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B}$ (a single set): the left side is $w_0(0)^2$ and the right side $w_0(1)^2$, so any $w$ with $w_0(0) > w_0(1) \ge 0$ works.
