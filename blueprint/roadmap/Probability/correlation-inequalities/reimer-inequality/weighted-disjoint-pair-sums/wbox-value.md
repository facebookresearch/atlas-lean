# Box value of a doubled family under a symmetric weight

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\mathcal{U}$ a family of subsets of the doubled ground set $\alpha \sqcup \alpha$. The box value of $\mathcal{U}$ is

$$\Big(\prod_{x \in \alpha} w_x(1)\Big)^2 |\mathcal{U}|.$$

For a symmetric weight ($w_x(0) = w_x(1)$ for every $x$) and $\mathcal{U}$ the doubled family of the disjoint pairs of $\mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B}$, it equals the [weighted number of disjoint pairs](wdpairs.md) of $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$.
