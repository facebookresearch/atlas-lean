# Independence of events determined by disjoint coordinate sets

Let $E$ be a finite set, $K, L \subseteq E$ disjoint, and $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\varphi_x(0) + \varphi_x(1) = 1$ for every $x$ (no sign condition is needed). If $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ is determined by $K$ and $B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ is determined by $L$, then

$$P_\varphi(A \cap B) = P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B),$$

where $P_\varphi(S) = \sum_{\omega \in S} \prod_{x} \varphi_x(\omega(x))$.
