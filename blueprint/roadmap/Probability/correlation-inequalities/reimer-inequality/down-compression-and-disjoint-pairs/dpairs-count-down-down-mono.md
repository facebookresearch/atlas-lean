# Down-compression does not decrease the disjoint-pair count

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ finite families of finite subsets of $\alpha$. With $C_i$ the down-compression at $i$ (each $S \ni i$ is replaced by $S \setminus \{i\}$ when the latter is not already in the family),

$$N(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \le N(C_i\mathcal{A}, C_i\mathcal{B}),$$

where $N$ is the [number of disjoint pairs](dpairs-count.md).
