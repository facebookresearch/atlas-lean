# Reimer's inequality on one coordinate

Let $\varphi : \{0\} \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a probability weight on one coordinate ($\varphi_0(b) \ge 0$, $\varphi_0(0) + \varphi_0(1) = 1$) and $P_\varphi(S) = \sum_{\omega \in S} \varphi_0(\omega_0)$. For all events $A, B \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\{0\}}$,

$$P_\varphi(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B).$$
