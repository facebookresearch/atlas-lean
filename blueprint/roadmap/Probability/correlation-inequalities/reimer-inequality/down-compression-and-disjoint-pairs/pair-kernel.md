# Weight kernel of disjoint pairs off a coordinate

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $i \in \alpha$. For subsets $S, T \subseteq \alpha$, the pair kernel off $i$ is $w^{(i)}(S)\, w^{(i)}(T)$ if $S \cap T = \emptyset$ and $0$ otherwise, where $w^{(i)}$ is the [product weight off $i$](../weighted-disjoint-pair-sums/fwt-2.md).
