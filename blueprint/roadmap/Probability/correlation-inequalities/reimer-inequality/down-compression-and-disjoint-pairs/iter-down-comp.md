# Iterated down-compression along a list of coordinates

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $c_1, \dots, c_k$ a finite list of elements of $\alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a finite family of finite subsets of $\alpha$. The iterated down-compression is $C_{c_1} C_{c_2} \cdots C_{c_k} \mathcal{A}$: down-compression at each coordinate of the list in turn, the last one first and the head $c_1$ last.
