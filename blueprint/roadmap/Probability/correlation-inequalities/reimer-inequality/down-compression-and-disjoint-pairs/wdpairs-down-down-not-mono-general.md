# Weighted down-compression monotonicity fails for general weights

Without the condition $w_i(1) \le w_i(0)$, [down-compression monotonicity of weighted disjoint pairs](wdpairs-down-down-mono.md) can fail: there are a nonnegative weight $w$ on the one-point set $\{0\}$, families $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ of subsets of $\{0\}$ and a coordinate $i$ such that

$$D_w(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) > D_w(C_i\mathcal{A}, C_i\mathcal{B}).$$
