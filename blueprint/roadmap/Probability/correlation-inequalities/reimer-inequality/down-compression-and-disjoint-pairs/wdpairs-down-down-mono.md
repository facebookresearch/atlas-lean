# Down-compression does not decrease weighted disjoint pairs

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $w : \alpha \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative and $i \in \alpha$ with $w_i(1) \le w_i(0)$. For all families $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ of subsets of $\alpha$, with $C_i$ the down-compression at $i$,

$$D_w(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \le D_w(C_i\mathcal{A}, C_i\mathcal{B}),$$

where $D_w$ is the [weighted number of disjoint pairs](../weighted-disjoint-pair-sums/wdpairs.md).
