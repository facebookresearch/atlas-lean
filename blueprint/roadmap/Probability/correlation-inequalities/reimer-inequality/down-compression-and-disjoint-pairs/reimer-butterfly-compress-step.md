# One compression step on the doubled disjoint-pair family

Let $\alpha$ be a set, $i \in \alpha$ and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ finite families of finite subsets of $\alpha$, with $\mathcal{A}^\pm, \mathcal{B}^\pm$ as in the [fiber recursion](dpairs-count-fiber-recursion.md). Let $\mathcal{U} = \mathcal{A} \boxtimes \mathcal{B}$ be the [doubled disjoint-pair family](../doubled-ground-set-in-reimer-s-proof/box-doubled.md) and $\mathcal{U}'$ its [double compression](../doubled-ground-set-in-reimer-s-proof/double-compress.md) at $i$. Then

- $|\mathcal{U}'| = |\mathcal{U}|$;
- $\mathcal{U}'$ is closed under erasing $i^L$ and under inserting $i^R$;
- $|\mathcal{U}| = |\mathcal{A}^- \boxtimes \mathcal{B}^-| + |\mathcal{A}^+ \boxtimes \mathcal{B}^-| + |\mathcal{A}^- \boxtimes \mathcal{B}^+|$;
- $|\mathcal{A}^+| + |\mathcal{A}^-| = |\mathcal{A}|$ and $|\mathcal{B}^+| + |\mathcal{B}^-| = |\mathcal{B}|$.
