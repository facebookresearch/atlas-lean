# Reimer's inequality for events on disjoint coordinate sets

Let $E$ be a finite set, $\varphi : E \times \{0,1\} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\varphi_x(0) + \varphi_x(1) = 1$ for every $x$ (no sign condition), and $P_\varphi(C) = \sum_{\omega \in C} \prod_x \varphi_x(\omega(x))$. If $S, T \subseteq E$ are disjoint, $A$ depends only on $S$ and $B$ depends only on $T$, then Reimer's inequality holds with equality:

$$P_\varphi(A \mathbin{\square} B) = P_\varphi(A)\, P_\varphi(B).$$
