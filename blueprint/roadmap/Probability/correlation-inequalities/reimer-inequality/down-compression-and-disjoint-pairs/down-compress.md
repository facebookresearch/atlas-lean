# Down-compression of a family of configurations

Let $E$ be a finite set, $i \in E$ and $\mathcal{A}$ a family of configurations in $\{0,1\}^E$. The down-compression of $\mathcal{A}$ at $i$ is obtained by identifying configurations with their supports and down-compressing the family of supports at $i$: each support $S \ni i$ is replaced by $S \setminus \{i\}$ when the latter is not already a support of a member of $\mathcal{A}$; the resulting sets are turned back into configurations.
