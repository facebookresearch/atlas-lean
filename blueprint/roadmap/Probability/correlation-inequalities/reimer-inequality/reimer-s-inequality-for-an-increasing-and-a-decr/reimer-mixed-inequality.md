# Reimer's inequality for mixed monotone events, Bernoulli measure

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0,1]$ and $\mathbb{P}_p$ the Bernoulli product measure on $\{0,1\}^E$. If $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ is increasing and $B \subseteq \{0,1\}^E$ is decreasing, then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A \mathbin{\square} B) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A)\, \mathbb{P}_p(B).$$
