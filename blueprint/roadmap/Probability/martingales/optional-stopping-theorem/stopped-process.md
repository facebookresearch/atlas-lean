# Stopped submartingales are submartingales

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal G_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a filtration such
that the restriction of $\mu$ to each $\mathcal G_n$ is σ-finite, let $(f_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a real-valued
submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal G_n)$ and $\mu$, and let $\tau$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal G_n)$ with
values in $\mathbb N \cup \{\infty\}$. Then the stopped process $n \mapsto f_{\tau \wedge n}$, whose value at $\omega$ is
$f_{\min(\tau(\omega), n)}(\omega)$, is a submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal G_n)$ and $\mu$.
