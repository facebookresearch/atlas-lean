# Martingales and optional stopping

Discrete-time martingales, sub- and supermartingales: convergence theorems (almost sure, L^p, backwards, Lévy's downward theorem), uniform integrability, Doob's maximal inequalities, orthogonality and conditional variance of increments, optional stopping in all its forms, and Wald's equations for stopped sums. Finite-horizon optimal stopping by the Snell envelope, and the secretary problem. Martingale measures and the fundamental theorem of asset pricing.
