# The sampled filtration at a scheduled index

Let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in I}$ be a filtration indexed by a preordered set $I$, and let $e:\{0,\ldots,n\}\to I$ be a monotone schedule. For its [sampled filtration](finite-schedule-filtration.md) $(\mathcal G_k)_{k\in\mathbb N}$, every index $j\in\{0,\ldots,n\}$ satisfies $\mathcal G_j=\mathcal F_{e(j)}$, since $\min(j,n)=j$.
