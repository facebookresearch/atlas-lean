# Doob's maximal inequality for nonnegative submartingales

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal G_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a filtration,
and let $(f_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a real submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal G_n)$ and $\mu$ with
$f_n(\omega) \ge 0$ for all $n$ and $\omega$. Let $n \in \mathbb N$, put $\bar f_n = \max_{0 \le k \le n} f_k$, and let
$\lambda \ge 0$ be real. Then

$$\lambda \, \mu(\{\bar f_n \ge \lambda\}) \le \int_{\{\bar f_n \ge \lambda\}} f_n \, d\mu.$$
