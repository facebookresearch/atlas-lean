# Wald's equation

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be identically distributed real random
variables with $X_0$ integrable, let $(\mathcal{F}_k)$ be a filtration such that each $X_k$ is
independent of $\mathcal{F}_k$, and let $T$ be an $\mathbb{N}$-valued stopping time with
$\mathbb{E}[T] < \infty$. Then, with $S_T = \sum_{i < T} X_i$,

$$\mathbb{E}[S_T] = \mathbb{E}[X_0]\; \mathbb{E}[T] .$$
