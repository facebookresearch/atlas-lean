# Diagonal identity in Wald's second equation

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be identically distributed real random
variables with $\mathbb{E}[X_0^2] < \infty$, let $(\mathcal{F}_k)$ be a filtration such that each
$X_k$ is independent of $\mathcal{F}_k$, and let $T$ be an $\mathbb{N}$-valued stopping time with
$\mathbb{E}[T] < \infty$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}\Big[\sum_{i < T} X_i^2\Big] = \mathbb{E}[X_0^2]\; \mathbb{E}[T] .$$
