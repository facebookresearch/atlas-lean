# Square-integrability of a stopped partial sum

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be identically distributed real random
variables with $\mathbb{E}[X_0] = 0$ and $\mathbb{E}[X_0^2] < \infty$, and let $(\mathcal{F}_k)$
be a filtration such that each $X_k$ is independent of $\mathcal{F}_k$ and
$\mathcal{F}_{k+1}$-measurable. Let $\tau$ be an $\mathbb{N}$-valued stopping time with
$\mathbb{E}[\tau] < \infty$. Then $\big(\sum_{i < \tau} X_i\big)^2$ is integrable.
