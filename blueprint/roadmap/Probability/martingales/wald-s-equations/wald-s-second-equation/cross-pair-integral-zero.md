# Vanishing of a single cross term in Wald's second equation

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be identically distributed real random
variables with $X_0$ integrable and $\mathbb{E}[X_0] = 0$, let $(\mathcal{F}_k)$ be a filtration
and $T$ an $\mathbb{N}$-valued stopping time. Suppose that each $X_k$ is independent of
$\mathcal{F}_k$, and that $X_j$ is $\mathcal{F}_k$-measurable whenever $j < k$. Then for $j < i$,

$$\mathbb{E}\big[X_j\, X_i\, \mathbf{1}_{\{T > i\}}\big] = 0 .$$
