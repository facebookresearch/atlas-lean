# Stopping rules sampled on a finite nonuniform schedule

Let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be a filtration, let $0 \le e_0 < e_1 < \dots < e_m = T$ be a finite strictly increasing schedule ending at the mandatory terminal date $T$ ($m \ge 0$), and extend it to $s_k = e_{\min(k, m)}$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Let $\mathcal G_k = \mathcal F_{s_k}$. Then $\tau = e_\iota$ gives a bijection between stopping times $\iota$ for $(\mathcal G_k)$ with values in $\{0, \dots, m\}$ and stopping times $\tau$ for $(\mathcal F_t)$ with values in $\{e_0, \dots, e_m\}$, with inverse $\iota$ equal to the index of $\tau$. It preserves payoffs: for any process $Z$, $Z_\tau = Z_{e_\iota}$ pointwise. No probability or integrability assumption is needed. For $m = 0$ both sides consist of the constant rule at $T$.
