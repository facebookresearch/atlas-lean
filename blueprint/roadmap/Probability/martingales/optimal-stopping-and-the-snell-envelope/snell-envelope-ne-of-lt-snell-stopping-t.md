# Before the first meeting time the envelope exceeds the reward

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ a reward sequence, with [Snell
envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$ and [first meeting time](snell-stopping-time.md)
$\tau^*$. If $k < \tau^*(\omega)$, then $S_k(\omega) \ne G_k(\omega)$.
