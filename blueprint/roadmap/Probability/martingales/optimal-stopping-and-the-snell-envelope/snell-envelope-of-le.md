# The Snell envelope equals the final reward from the horizon on

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ a reward sequence, with [Snell
envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$. If $N \le n$, then $S_n = G_N$.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in
  Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, equation (1.1.6).
