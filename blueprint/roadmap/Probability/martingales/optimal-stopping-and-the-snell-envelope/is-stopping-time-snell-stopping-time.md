# The first meeting time is a stopping time

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ an adapted reward sequence,
with [Snell envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$. Then the [first meeting
time](snell-stopping-time.md) $\tau^* = \min\{k \le N : S_k = G_k\}$ is a stopping time for
$(\mathcal{G}_k)$.
