# The Snell envelope dominates the reward up to the horizon

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ a reward sequence, with [Snell
envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$. Then $G_n(\omega) \le S_n(\omega)$ for every
$n \le N$ and every $\omega$.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in
  Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, Theorem 1.2, part (1.1.13).
