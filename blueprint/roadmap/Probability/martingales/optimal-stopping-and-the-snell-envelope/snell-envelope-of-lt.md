# Wald–Bellman recursion for the Snell envelope

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ a reward sequence, with [Snell
envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$. If $n < N$, then
$S_n = \max\big(G_n, \mathbb{E}[S_{n+1} \mid \mathcal{G}_n]\big)$ pointwise.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in
  Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, equation (1.1.7).
