# The Snell envelope is a supermartingale

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration, and $(G_k)$ an adapted reward
sequence with every $G_k$ integrable. Then the [Snell envelope](snell-envelope.md) with horizon
$N$ is a supermartingale for $(\mathcal{G}_k)$.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, Theorem 1.2 (finite horizon), part (1.1.13) and its proof (1.1.18).
