# At the first meeting time the envelope equals the reward

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration and $(G_k)$ a reward sequence, with [Snell
envelope](snell-envelope.md) $S$ of horizon $N$ and [first meeting time](snell-stopping-time.md)
$\tau^*$. Then $S_{\tau^*(\omega)}(\omega) = G_{\tau^*(\omega)}(\omega)$ for every $\omega$.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in
  Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, equation (1.1.8).
