# The Snell envelope stopped at the first meeting time is a martingale

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure, $(\mathcal{G}_k)$ a filtration, and $(G_k)$ an adapted reward
sequence with every $G_k$ integrable, let $S$ be the [Snell envelope](snell-envelope.md) with
horizon $N$ and $\tau^*$ the [first meeting time](snell-stopping-time.md). Then the stopped process
$(S_{k \wedge \tau^*})_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ is a martingale for $(\mathcal{G}_k)$.

## Sources

- G. Peskir and A. Shiryaev, *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*, Lectures in Mathematics ETH Zürich, Birkhäuser, 2006, Chapter I, §1.1, Theorem 1.2 (finite horizon), part (1.1.14) with $n = 0$ and its proof (1.1.19)–(1.1.20).
