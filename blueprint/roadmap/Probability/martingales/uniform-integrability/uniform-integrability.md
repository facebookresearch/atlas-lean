# Uniform integrability

A family $\{X_i\}_{i \in I}$ of real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mathcal F, P)$ is
*uniformly integrable* if

$$\lim_{M \to \infty} \Big( \sup_{i \in I} E\big(|X_i| ; |X_i| > M\big) \Big) = 0,$$

where $E(Y ; A) = \int_A Y \, dP$ and the supremum is taken in $[0, \infty]$.
