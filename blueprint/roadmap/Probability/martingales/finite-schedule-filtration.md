# Filtration sampled on a finite schedule

Let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in I}$ be a filtration on a measurable space, indexed by a preordered set $I$. Let $n\ge 0$ and let $e:\{0,\ldots,n\}\to I$ be monotone. The filtration sampled on this schedule is indexed by $k\in\mathbb N$ and given by $\mathcal G_k=\mathcal F_{e(\min(k,n))}$. It is constant from index $n$ onward, at the original filtration's value at the terminal date $e(n)$.
