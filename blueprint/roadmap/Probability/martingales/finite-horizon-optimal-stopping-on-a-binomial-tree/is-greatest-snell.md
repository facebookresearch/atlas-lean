# The Snell envelope is the optimal stopping value

On a binary tree with up probability $0 \le p \le 1$ and discount factor $\delta \ge 0$, for any exercise payoff $g$ on histories and any horizon $N$, the Snell envelope at the root is the largest value at the root of any nonanticipating stopping rule over horizon $N$, and it is attained. The envelope satisfies the backward recursion $\max(\text{exercise payoff}, \text{discounted continuation value})$.
