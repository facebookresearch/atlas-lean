# Harmonic tail of the secretary problem

For natural numbers $n$ and $r$, $T_r = \sum_{j=r}^{n-1} 1/j$ (with $1/0 = 0$, and $T_r = 0$ for
$r \ge n$), which equals $\sum_{k=r+1}^{n} 1/(k - 1)$ in Ferguson's notation.

## Sources

- T. S. Ferguson, *Optimal Stopping and Applications*, online text (UCLA Department of
  Mathematics), Chapter 2, §2.1, equation (4).
