# Predictable sequence

Let $(\mathcal F_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a filtration on a probability space. A sequence $(H_n)_{n \ge 1}$ of real
random variables is *predictable* with respect to $(\mathcal F_n)$ if $H_n$ is $\mathcal F_{n-1}$-measurable for every
$n \ge 1$: the value at time $n$ is determined by the information available at time $n - 1$.
