# Bounded nonnegative predictable transforms of a submartingale

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal G_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a filtration,
let $(f_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be a real submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal G_n)$ and $\mu$, and let
$\xi_n \colon \Omega \to \mathbb R$, $n \in \mathbb N$, satisfy $0 \le \xi_n(\omega) \le R$ for all $n$ and $\omega$,
for some real $R$, with $\xi_{n+1}$ measurable for $\mathcal G_n$ for every $n$. Then

$$n \mapsto \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \xi_{k+1} \, (f_{k+1} - f_k)$$

is a submartingale with respect to $(\mathcal G_n)$ and $\mu$.
