# Conditional expectations along a filtration are strongly adapted

Let $(\mathcal{F}_n)$ be a decreasing filtration of sub-$\sigma$-algebras of the ambient
$\sigma$-algebra on $\Omega$, indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ with the reversed order, let $\mu$ be a
measure and $g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. Then the process
$n \mapsto \mathbb{E}[g \mid \mathcal{F}_n]$ is adapted: each term is $\mathcal{F}_n$-measurable.
No integrability of $g$ is needed.
