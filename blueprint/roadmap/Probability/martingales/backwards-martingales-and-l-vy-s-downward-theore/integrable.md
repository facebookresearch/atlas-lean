# Terms of a backwards martingale are integrable

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $(\mathcal{F}_n)$ a sequence of $\sigma$-algebras on $\Omega$ and $(M_n)$
a [backwards martingale](is-backwards-martingale.md) for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then every $M_n$ is
integrable, since it agrees almost everywhere with the conditional expectation
$\mathbb{E}[M_0 \mid \mathcal{F}_n]$.
