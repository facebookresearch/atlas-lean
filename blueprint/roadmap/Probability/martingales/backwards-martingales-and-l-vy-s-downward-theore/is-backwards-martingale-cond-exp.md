# Conditional expectations form a backwards martingale

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, let $g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and let
$(\mathcal{F}_n)$ be a decreasing sequence of sub-$\sigma$-algebras of $\mathcal{F}$. Then
$(\mathbb{E}[g \mid \mathcal{F}_n])_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ is a [backwards
martingale](is-backwards-martingale.md) for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. No integrability of $g$ is assumed:
for a non-integrable $g$ every conditional expectation is $0$.
