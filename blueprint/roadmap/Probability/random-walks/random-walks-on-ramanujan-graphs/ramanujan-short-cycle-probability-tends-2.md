# Cycle density in infinite Ramanujan graphs

Let $G$ be an infinite connected graph in which every vertex has degree $d$, and $o$ a vertex. $G$ is Ramanujan at $o$ if $\limsup_n p_n(o, o)^{1/n} = 2\sqrt{d-1}/d$, the spectral radius of the $d$-regular tree, where $p_n(o, o)$ is the return probability of simple random walk. A vertex lies on a nontrivial cycle of length at most $L$ if some closed walk at it of length between $1$ and $L$ never immediately backtracks. If $G$ is Ramanujan at $o$, then for every $L \ge 1$ the probability that simple random walk from $o$ is at time $n$ at a vertex on a nontrivial cycle of length at most $L$ tends to $0$. Lyons and Peres (2015) proved this for infinite Ramanujan graphs.
