# Vertex on a short nontrivial cycle

Let $G$ be a simple graph, $x$ a vertex and $L \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $x$ lies on a nontrivial cycle of length at most $L$ if there is a closed walk $x = w_0, w_1, \dots, w_m = x$ in $G$ with $1 \le m \le L$ that never immediately backtracks in its interior:

$$w_{i-1} \ne w_{i+1} \quad \text{for all } 0 < i < m.$$
