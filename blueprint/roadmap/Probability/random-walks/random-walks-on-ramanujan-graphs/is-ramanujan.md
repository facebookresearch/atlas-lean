# Ramanujan graph (spectral radius of the regular tree)

Let $G$ be a simple graph, $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $o$ a vertex, and let $W_n(o, o)$ be the number of closed walks of length $n$ at $o$. Then $G$ is Ramanujan of degree $d$ (seen from $o$) if

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} \Big( \frac{W_n(o, o)}{d^n} \Big)^{1/n} = \frac{2\sqrt{d - 1}}{d},$$

the spectral radius of the $d$-regular tree. For a $d$-regular graph, $W_n(o, o)/d^n$ is the probability that simple random walk from $o$ is back at $o$ at time $n$.
