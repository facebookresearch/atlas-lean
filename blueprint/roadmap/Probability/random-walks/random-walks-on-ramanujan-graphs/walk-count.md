# Number of walks of length $n$ in a graph

Let $G$ be a simple graph, $o, y$ vertices and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $W_n(o, y)$ is the number of walks of length $n$ from $o$ to $y$ in $G$, that is, of sequences $o = w_0, w_1, \dots, w_n = y$ of vertices with $w_{i} \sim w_{i+1}$ for each $i < n$. When there are infinitely many such walks the count is read as $0$.
