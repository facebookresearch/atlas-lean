# For lattice scores the tail weight is constant on open lattice cells

Let `h > 0`, let `q` and `f` be real weights and scores on a finite alphabet with every score
`f b` in the lattice `hℤ`, and let `L` be a natural number and `k` an integer. For all `a` and `a'`
in the open interval `(0, h)`, the
[score probabilities](score-prob.md) of
`{M_L > k h + a}` and of `{M_L > k h + a'}` are equal, where `M_L` is the
[maximal segment score](max-seg-score.md) of the
scores of `L` letters: the tail is constant for `t` in each open lattice cell `(k h, (k + 1) h)`.
When `q` is a probability vector these are probabilities for `L` independent letters of law `q`.
