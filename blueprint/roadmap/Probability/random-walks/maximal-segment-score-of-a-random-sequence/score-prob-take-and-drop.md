# Events on two consecutive blocks have product weight

For real weights `q` and scores `f` on a finite alphabet, natural numbers `k ≤ n` and properties `A`
and `B` of score lists, the [score probability](score-prob.md) over words of length `n` that the
first `k` scores satisfy `A` and the remaining `n - k` satisfy `B` is the product of the score
probabilities of `A` for length `k` and of `B` for length `n - k`. For a probability vector `q`
these are independent events.
