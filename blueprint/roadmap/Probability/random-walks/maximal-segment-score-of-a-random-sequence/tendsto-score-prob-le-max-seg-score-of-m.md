# Nondegenerate scores of nonnegative mean give unbounded segment scores

Let `q` be a probability vector and `f` real scores on a finite alphabet with nonnegative mean
score, `∑_b q_b f(b) ≥ 0`, and some letter `b` of positive probability with nonzero score. Then for
every real `t`, `P(Mₙ ≥ t) → 1` as `n → ∞`, where `Mₙ` is the
[maximal segment score](max-seg-score.md) of the scores of `n` independent letters with law `q`.
