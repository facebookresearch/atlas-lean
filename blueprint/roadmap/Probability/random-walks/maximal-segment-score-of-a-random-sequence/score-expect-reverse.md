# Reversing the score sequence does not change the weighted average

For real weights `q` and scores `f` on a finite alphabet, every natural number `n` and every real
functional `F` of score lists, the [weighted average](score-expect.md) over words of length `n` of
`F` of the reversed score list equals that of `F`. For a probability vector `q`, reversing an
i.i.d. score sequence does not change its law.
