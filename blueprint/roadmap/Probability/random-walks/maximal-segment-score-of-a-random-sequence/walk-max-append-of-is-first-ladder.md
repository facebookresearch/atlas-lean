# Ladder decomposition of the walk maximum

For a list of real scores `u` that
[ends at the first strict ascending ladder epoch](is-first-ladder.md) of its walk and any list of
real scores `v`, the [walk maximum](walk-max.md) of `u` followed by `v` is the ladder height
`S_{|u|}`, the sum of `u`, plus the walk maximum of `v`. Iterating, the walk maximum is the sum of
the successive ladder heights.
