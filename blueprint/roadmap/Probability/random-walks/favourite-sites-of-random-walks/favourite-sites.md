# Favourite sites of a lattice walk

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ be the steps of a walk with positions $s_l = X_0 + \dots + X_{l-1}$ and local times $f_k(x) = \#\{0 \le l \le k : s_l = x\}$. The set of favourite sites at time $k$ is

$$F(k) = \{x \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : f_k(y) \le f_k(x) \text{ for all } y \in \mathbb{Z}^2\},$$

the sites of maximal local time.
