# Number of favourite sites of planar random walk

For simple random walk on $\mathbb Z^2$, a site is favourite at time $k$ if its local time (number of visits up to time $k$) is maximal. The claim (Erdős Problem 1166) is that there is $C$ such that almost surely, for all large $n$, at most $(\log n)^C$ sites are favourite at some time $k \le n$.
