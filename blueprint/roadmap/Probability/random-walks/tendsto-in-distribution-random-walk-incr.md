# Finite-dimensional distributions of the rescaled random walk

On a probability space, let $R_0, R_1, \dots$ be i.i.d. measurable random variables with mean $0$, second moment $1$ and $|R_i| \le 1$, let $S_k = \sum_{i<k} R_i$, and let $f_n(s) = S_{\lfloor ns\rfloor}/\sqrt n + (ns - \lfloor ns\rfloor)\, R_{\lfloor ns\rfloor}/\sqrt n$ be the rescaled linearly interpolated walk. For times $0 \le t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_m$ and independent $Z_j \sim N(0, t_{j+1} - t_j)$ on a probability space, the vector of increments $(f_n(t_{j+1}) - f_n(t_j))_{j<m}$ converges in distribution to $(Z_j)_{j<m}$.
