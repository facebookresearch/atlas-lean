# Walk increments inside an excursion are at most its height

For every path of real scores and natural numbers `k` and `b` with `T_k ≤ b ≤ T_{k+1}`, where `T_k`
is the `k`-th [ladder epoch](ladder-epoch.md), the increment `S_b - S_{T_k}` is at most the
[height](excursion-height.md) `Y_k` of the `k`-th excursion.
