# Cramér–Lundberg constant of the all-time maximum, nonlattice case

For [nonlattice ladder renewal data](ladder-renewal-data.md) with ladder-height measure `G` and
root `θ`, the Cramér–Lundberg constant is `C_∞ = (1 - G(ℝ)) / (θ ∫ y e^{θ y} dG(y))`, with the
convention `x / 0 = 0`. It is the constant of the classical Cramér–Lundberg asymptotic
`P(M' > t) ~ C_∞ e^{-θ t}` of the all-time maximum `M'`, and it enters the
[excursion-height renewal constant](tail-const.md).
