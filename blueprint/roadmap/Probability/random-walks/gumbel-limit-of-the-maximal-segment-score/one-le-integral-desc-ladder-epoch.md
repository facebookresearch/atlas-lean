# The mean descending ladder epoch on the path space is at least one

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℝ` and `s ≥ 0` a real with `e^{s y}` integrable for `ν` and
`∫ e^{s y} dν(y) < 1`. Then the [first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md) satisfies
`E τ₋ ≥ 1` under the product measure `ν^ℕ`.
