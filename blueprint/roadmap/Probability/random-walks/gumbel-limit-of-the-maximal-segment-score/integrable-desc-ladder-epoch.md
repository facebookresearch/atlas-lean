# Finite mean of the first descending ladder epoch on the path space

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℝ` and `s ≥ 0` a real with `e^{s y}` integrable for `ν` and
`ρ = ∫ e^{s y} dν(y) < 1`. Then the [first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md) `τ₋` is
integrable under the product measure `ν^ℕ`: `E τ₋ = ∑_k P(τ₋ > k) ≤ ∑_k ρ^k`, since
`{τ₋ > k} ⊆ {S_k ≥ 0}`.
