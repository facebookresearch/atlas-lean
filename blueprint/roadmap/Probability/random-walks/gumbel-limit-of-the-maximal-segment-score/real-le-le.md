# Upper bound on the distribution of the statistic from m excursions

For a probability measure `P`, a [ladder input](ladder-input.md) under `P` for statistics `M n`,
with excursion heights of distribution function `F` and completed-excursion counts `N n`, and for
natural numbers `n` and `m` and a real `t`, `P(M n ≤ t) ≤ F(t)^m + P(N n < m)`: if at least `m`
excursions are complete, the first `m` heights are all at most `M n`.
