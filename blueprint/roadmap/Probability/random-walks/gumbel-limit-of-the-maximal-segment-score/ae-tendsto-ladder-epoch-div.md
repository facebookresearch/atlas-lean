# Strong law of large numbers for the ladder epochs

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℝ` and `s ≥ 0` a real with `e^{s y}` integrable for `ν` and
`∫ e^{s y} dν(y) < 1`. Then for almost every path under the product measure `ν^ℕ`, the
[ladder epochs](ladder-epoch.md) satisfy `T_k / k → E τ₋`, the mean of the
[first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md).
