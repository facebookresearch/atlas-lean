# Excursion lengths on the canonical path space are i.i.d.

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℝ` and `s ≥ 0` a real with `e^{s y}` integrable for `ν` and
`∫ e^{s y} dν(y) < 1`. Then under the product measure `ν^ℕ` the
[excursion lengths](excursion-length.md) `τ₋⁽⁰⁾, τ₋⁽¹⁾, …` are independent, and each has the law of
the [first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md) `τ₋`.
