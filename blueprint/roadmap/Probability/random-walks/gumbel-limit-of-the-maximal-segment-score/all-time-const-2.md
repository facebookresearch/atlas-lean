# Cramér–Lundberg constant of the all-time maximum, lattice case

For [lattice ladder renewal data](lattice-ladder-renewal-data.md) with ladder-height probabilities
`g j`, root `θ`, span `h` and tilted ladder mean `μ_θ`, the lattice Cramér–Lundberg constant is
`C_∞ = (1 - ∑_j g j) / ((e^{θ h} - 1) μ_θ)`, with the conventions `x / 0 = 0` and `∑_j g j = 0`
for a non-summable sequence. It is the constant of the classical lattice asymptotic
`P(M' > k h) ~ C_∞ e^{-θ h k}` of the all-time maximum `M'`, and it enters the
[lattice excursion-height renewal constant](tail-const-2.md).
