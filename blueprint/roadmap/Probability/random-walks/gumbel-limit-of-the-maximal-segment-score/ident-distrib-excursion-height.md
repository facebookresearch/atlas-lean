# The excursion heights of Cramér scores are identically distributed

For scores `X 0, X 1, …` on `(Ω, P)` satisfying the [Cramér conditions](cramer-scores.md) and every
natural number `i`, the [height](excursion-height.md) `Y_i` of the `i`-th excursion of the score
path has the law of the [maximal height of the first excursion](excursion-max.md).
