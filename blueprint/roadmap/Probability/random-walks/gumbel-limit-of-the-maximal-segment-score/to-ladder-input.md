# The ladder input built from Cramér scores

For scores `X 0, X 1, …` on `(Ω, P)` satisfying the [Cramér conditions](cramer-scores.md), this
construction gives a [ladder input](ladder-input.md) for their
[maximal segment scores](max-segment-score-rv.md): the excursion heights are the
[heights](excursion-height.md) `Y_i` of the descending ladder excursions of the score path, `Nₙ` is
the [number of excursions completed by time `n`](ladder-count.md), `F(t)` is the probability that
the [maximal height of the first excursion](excursion-max.md) is at most `t`, and `A = E τ₋` is the
mean [first descending ladder epoch](desc-ladder-epoch.md).
