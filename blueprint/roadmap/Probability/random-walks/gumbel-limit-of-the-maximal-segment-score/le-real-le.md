# Lower bound on the distribution of the statistic from m excursions

For a probability measure `P`, a [ladder input](ladder-input.md) under `P` for statistics `M n`,
with excursion heights of distribution function `F` and completed-excursion counts `N n`, and for
natural numbers `n` and `m` and a real `t`, `F(t)^m - P(m ≤ N n) ≤ P(M n ≤ t)`: if fewer than `m`
excursions are complete, `M n` is at most the largest of the first `m` excursion heights.
