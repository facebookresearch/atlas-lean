# Lattice excursion-height tail, derived

Let `P` be a probability measure, `Y` a measurable real random variable, `θ > 0`, `h > 0`, and `R`
[lattice ladder renewal data](lattice-ladder-renewal-data.md) for `Y` with root `θ` and span `h`.
Then `P(Y > k h) e^{θ (k + 1) h} → C` as `k → ∞` over the natural numbers, where
`C = e^{θ h} C_∞ (1 - E e^{θ D})` is the
[lattice excursion-height renewal constant](tail-const-2.md). The statement has no
renewal-theorem hypothesis. This is the tail condition of a
[lattice renewal input](lattice-renewal-input.md).
