# Ladder-good score paths

A path of real scores is ladder-good when, for every time `t`, the
[path seen from time `t`](path-shift.md) [drops strictly below its start](has-desc-ladder.md):
every restart of the walk eventually goes below its starting value. On such paths every excursion
ends with a descent, so the [ladder epochs](ladder-epoch.md) are strictly increasing, and for
scores satisfying the [Cramér conditions](cramer-scores.md)
[almost every path is ladder-good](ae-is-ladder-good.md).
