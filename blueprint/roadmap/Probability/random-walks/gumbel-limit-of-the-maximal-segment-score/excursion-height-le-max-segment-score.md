# Lower ladder sandwich for the maximal segment score

For a [ladder-good](is-ladder-good.md) path of real scores and natural numbers `n` and `i` with
`i < Nₙ`, the [ladder count](ladder-count.md), the [height](excursion-height.md) `Y_i` of the
`i`-th excursion is at most the [maximal segment score](max-segment-score.md) `Mₙ` of the first `n`
scores: every excursion completed by time `n` has height at most `Mₙ`.
