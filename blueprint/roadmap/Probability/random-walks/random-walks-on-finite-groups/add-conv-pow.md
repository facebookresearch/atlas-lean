# Convolution powers on a finite abelian group

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$, with convolution
$(f * g)(x) = \sum_{y \in G} f(y)\, g(x - y)$. The convolution powers are $f^{*0} = \delta_0$, the
point mass at $0$, and $f^{*(k+1)} = f * f^{*k}$. For a probability $f$, $f^{*k}$ is the law after
$k$ steps of the random walk driven by $f$.
