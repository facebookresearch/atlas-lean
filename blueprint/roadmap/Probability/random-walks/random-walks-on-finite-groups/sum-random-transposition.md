# The random-transposition measure is a probability

For $n \ge 1$, the random-transposition measure $T$ on $S_n$ ($T(\mathrm{id}) = 1/n$,
$T(\tau) = 2/n^2$ for transpositions $\tau$, $0$ otherwise) has total mass
$\sum_\sigma T(\sigma) = 1$, since $1/n + (2/n^2)\binom{n}{2} = 1$.

- P. Diaconis and M. Shahshahani, *Generating a random permutation with random transpositions*,
