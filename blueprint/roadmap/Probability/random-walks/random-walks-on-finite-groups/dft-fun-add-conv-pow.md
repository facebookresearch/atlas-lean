# Fourier transform of a convolution power on a finite abelian group

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group, $f : G \to \mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, with Fourier
transform $\hat f(\psi) = \sum_{x \in G} \psi(x) f(x)$ at a character $\psi : G \to \mathbb{C}^\times$.
Then the [convolution power](add-conv-pow.md) satisfies $\widehat{f^{*k}}(\psi) = \hat f(\psi)^k$ for
every character $\psi$.
