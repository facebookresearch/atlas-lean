# Upper bound lemma for a random walk on a finite abelian group

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and $P : G \to \mathbb{R}$ a function of total mass $1$. If
$Q : G \to \mathbb{R}$ is the $k$-th convolution power of $P$ (that is, the complex
values of $Q$ are those of the $k$-th convolution power of $P$), then

$$4\,\|Q - U\|_{TV}^2 \le \sum_{\psi \ne 1} |\hat P(\psi)|^{2k}.$$

Since $\hat{P^{*k}} = \hat P^{\,k}$, this is the [upper bound lemma on a finite abelian group](four-mul-tv-dist-uniform-sq-le.md)
applied to $Q$. It is the form in which Diaconis uses the lemma: equation (3.3) of the
Saint-Flour notes for the cube $\mathbb{Z}_2^n$, and the first display of the proof of Theorem 2.1
there for simple random walk on $\mathbb{Z}/n$.

## Sources

- P. Diaconis, *Applications of non-commutative Fourier analysis to probability problems*,
