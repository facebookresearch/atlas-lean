# The random-transposition measure is a class function

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $T$ be the random-transposition measure on the symmetric group $S_n$:
$T(\mathrm{id}) = 1/n$, $T(\tau) = 2/n^2$ for every transposition $\tau$, and $T(\sigma) = 0$
otherwise. Then $T(h \sigma h^{-1}) = T(\sigma)$ for all $\sigma, h \in S_n$, since
conjugation fixes the identity and maps transpositions to transpositions.

- P. Diaconis and M. Shahshahani, *Generating a random permutation with random transpositions*,
