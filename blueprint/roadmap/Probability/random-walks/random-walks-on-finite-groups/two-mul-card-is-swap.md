# Number of transpositions in the symmetric group

For $n \ge 2$, the symmetric group $S_n$ has $\binom{n}{2}$ transpositions:
$2\, \#\{\tau \in S_n : \tau \text{ a transposition}\} = n(n - 1)$.

## Sources

- P. Diaconis and M. Shahshahani, *Generating a random permutation with random transpositions*,
  Z. Wahrsch. Verw. Gebiete 57 (1981), 159–179, proof of Corollary 1.
