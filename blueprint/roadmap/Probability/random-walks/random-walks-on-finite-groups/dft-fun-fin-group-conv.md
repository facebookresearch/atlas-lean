# Convolution theorem on a finite abelian group

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and $f, g : G \to \mathbb{C}$, with
$(f * g)(x) = \sum_y f(y)\, g(x - y)$ and $\hat f(\psi) = \sum_x \psi(x) f(x)$. Then
$\widehat{f * g}(\psi) = \hat f(\psi)\, \hat g(\psi)$ for every character $\psi$: transforms turn
convolution into multiplication.
