# Benjamini's cover-time conjecture

For simple random walk on a graph $G$ with $n$ vertices, consider the probability that it visits every vertex within $m$ steps. The conjecture is that for every $C > 0$ there is $c < 1$ such that for all large $n$, every simple graph on $n$ vertices and every start vertex, this probability with $m = \lfloor Cn\rfloor$ is at most $c^n$.
