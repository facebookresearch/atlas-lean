# Linear cover time is exponentially unlikely

For simple random walk on a graph $G$ with $n$ vertices, consider the probability that it visits every vertex within $m$ steps. For every $C > 0$ there is $c < 1$ such that, for all large $n$, simple random walk on any simple $n$-vertex graph from any start covers the graph within $\lfloor Cn\rfloor$ steps with probability at most $c^n$ (Dubroff and Kahn).

## Sources

- Resolution: Q. Dubroff and J. Kahn, Linear cover time is exponentially unlikely, Ann. Probab. 53 (2025), doi:10.1214/24-AOP1699, arXiv:2109.01237, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01237.
