# Tail σ-algebra of a sequence of σ-algebras

Given σ-algebras
$\mathcal{A}_0, \mathcal{A}_1, \dots$ on $\Omega$, the tail σ-algebra is

$$\mathcal{T} = \bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \sigma(\mathcal{A}_n, \mathcal{A}_{n+1}, \dots),$$

where $\sigma(\mathcal{A}_n, \mathcal{A}_{n+1}, \dots)$ is the smallest σ-algebra containing
every $\mathcal{A}_i$ with $i \ge n$. It contains the information observable by looking only at
events arbitrarily far into the future.
