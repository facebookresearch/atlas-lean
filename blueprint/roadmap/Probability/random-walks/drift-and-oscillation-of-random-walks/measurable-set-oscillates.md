# Tail measurability of the oscillation event

Let $X_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ for
$n \in \mathbb{N}$ be a sequence of functions with partial sums $S_m = \sum_{i < m} X_i$. Then the
event that $\limsup_{m \to \infty} S_m = +\infty$ and $\liminf_{m \to \infty} S_m = -\infty$,
with the limit superior and inferior taken in the extended reals, belongs to the tail
σ-algebra of $X$.
