# A stopping-time event is independent of finitely many later observations

Let $\mu$ be a
probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a
filtration on $\Omega$, and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a
measurable space $S$ that are mutually independent under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the
σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, every finite set $F \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ and all sets $A_k \subseteq S$
($k \in \mathbb{N}$) such that $A_k$ is measurable for every $k \in F$,
$\mu(\{T = n\} \cap \bigcap_{k \in F} X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A_k)) =
\mu(\{T = n\}) \prod_{k \in F} \mu(X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A_k))$.
