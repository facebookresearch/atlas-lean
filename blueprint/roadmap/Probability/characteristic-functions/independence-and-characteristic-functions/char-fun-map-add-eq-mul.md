# Characteristic function of a sum of independent random vectors

Let $P$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, and let $E$ be a real inner product space whose
topology is second countable, with its Borel σ-algebra. Let $X, Y \colon \Omega \to E$ be almost everywhere measurable
and independent with respect to $P$. Then the characteristic function of the image measure $P \circ (X + Y)^{-1}$ is
the product of the characteristic functions of $P \circ X^{-1}$ and $P \circ Y^{-1}$.
