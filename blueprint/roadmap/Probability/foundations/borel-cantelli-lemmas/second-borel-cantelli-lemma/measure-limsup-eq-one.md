# Second Borel–Cantelli lemma for independent measurable sets

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $(s_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ be measurable sets that
are mutually independent with respect to $\mu$. Independence of a family includes the product formula for its empty
subfamily, $\mu(\Omega) = 1$, so $\mu$ is a probability measure. If $\sum_n \mu(s_n) = \infty$, the sum taken in
$[0, \infty]$, then $\mu\big(\limsup_n s_n\big) = 1$, where $\limsup_n s_n = \bigcap_{m} \bigcup_{n \ge m} s_n$.
