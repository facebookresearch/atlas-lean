# Map comp eq with Density

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra and $\mu$ an additive Haar measure on $E$. Let $T : E \to E$ be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse such that $T^{-1}$ has Fréchet derivative $S'(x)$ at every $x \in E$. Let $Z : \Omega \to E$ be a random variable on $(\Omega, \mathbb{P})$ that has a density with respect to $\mu$ ($Z$ is a.e. measurable and its law is absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$), and let $p_Z = d(Z_{\#}\mathbb{P})/d\mu$ be its density. Then the law of $T \circ Z$ has density $x \mapsto p_Z(T^{-1}(x))\, \lvert \det S'(x) \rvert$:

$$(T \circ Z)_{\#} \mathbb{P} = \bigl(x \mapsto p_Z(T^{-1}(x))\, \lvert \det S'(x) \rvert\bigr) \cdot \mu.$$
