# Quasi Measure Preserving of differentiable symm

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra and $\mu$ an additive Haar measure on $E$. Let $T : E \to E$ be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse such that $T^{-1}$ is differentiable everywhere. Then $T$ is quasi-measure-preserving from $\mu$ to $\mu$: $T$ is measurable and $\mu(T^{-1}(N)) = 0$ whenever $\mu(N) = 0$. Indeed the preimage of a null set under $T$ is its image under the differentiable map $T^{-1}$, hence null.
