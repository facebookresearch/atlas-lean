# Independent π-systems generate independent σ-algebras, for any index set

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal M_0)$ and let $\iota$ be any index set. For each
$i \in \iota$ let $\pi_i$ be a π-system of subsets of $\Omega$, a family that contains the intersection of any two of
its members whenever that intersection is nonempty, and let $\mathcal M_i$ be the σ-algebra it generates, assumed
contained in $\mathcal M_0$. If the π-systems are independent with respect to $\mu$, that is,
$\mu\big(\bigcap_{j \in J} A_j\big) = \prod_{j \in J} \mu(A_j)$ for every finite set $J \subseteq \iota$ and every choice of
$A_j \in \pi_j$, then the σ-algebras $(\mathcal M_i)_{i \in \iota}$ are independent with respect to $\mu$.
