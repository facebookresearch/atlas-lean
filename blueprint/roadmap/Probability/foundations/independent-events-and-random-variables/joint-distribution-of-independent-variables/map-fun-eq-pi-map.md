# Joint law of a finite independent family of random variables

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $\iota$ be a finite index set, and for each
$i \in \iota$ let $f_i \colon \Omega \to \beta_i$ be an almost everywhere measurable map into a measurable space
$\beta_i$. If the family $(f_i)_{i \in \iota}$ is independent with respect to $\mu$, then the image of $\mu$ under
$\omega \mapsto (f_i(\omega))_{i \in \iota}$ is the product measure $\prod_{i \in \iota} \mu \circ f_i^{-1}$ on
$\prod_{i \in \iota} \beta_i$.
