# Coin fixing

For every probability mass function $p$ on $\alpha$, every family $f : \alpha \to \mathrm{PMF}(\beta)$ and every outcome $b \in \beta$, some $a$ in the support of $p$ does at least as well as the average:

$$\exists\, a \in \operatorname{supp} p,\quad \sum_{a'} p(a')\, f(a')(b) \le f(a)(b),$$

where the left side is the probability of $b$ under the bind of $p$ with $f$ (values in $[0, \infty]$).
