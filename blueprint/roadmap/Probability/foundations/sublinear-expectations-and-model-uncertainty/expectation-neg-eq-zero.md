# A G-normal random variable has zero lower mean

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{H}, \mathbb{E})$ be a [sublinear expectation
space](sublinear-expectation-space.md). If $X$ is [G-normally distributed](is-gnormal.md), then
$\mathbb{E}[-X] = 0$, so $X$ has no mean uncertainty.

## Sources

- S. Peng, *Nonlinear Expectations and Stochastic Calculus under Uncertainty*,
  arXiv:1002.4546v1, 2010, Chapter II, Remark 1.5.
