# Coherent risk measures of constants

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a linear space of real functions on a set $\Omega$ and let $\rho$ be a
[coherent risk measure](is-coherent-risk-measure.md) on $\mathcal{H}$. Then $\rho(c) = -c$ for
every constant $c$; this follows from $\rho(1) = -\rho(-1) = -1$ and positive homogeneity.
