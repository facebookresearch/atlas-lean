# Sublinear expectations give coherent risk measures

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a linear space of real functions on a set $\Omega$. If $\mathbb{E}$ is a
[sublinear expectation](../sublinear-expectations/is-sublinear-expectation.md) on $\mathcal{H}$, then
$\rho(X) = \mathbb{E}[-X]$ is a [coherent risk measure](is-coherent-risk-measure.md) on
$\mathcal{H}$.

## Sources
