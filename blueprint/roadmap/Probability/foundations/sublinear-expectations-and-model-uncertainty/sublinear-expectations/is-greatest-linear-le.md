# Representation of a sublinear functional as a maximum of linear functionals

Let $V$ be a real vector space and let $E : V \to \mathbb{R}$ be subadditive and positively
homogeneous ($E(cx) = c\,E(x)$ for $c > 0$). Then for every $x \in V$,

$$E(x) = \max\{f(x) : f \text{ linear on } V,\ f \le E\},$$

the maximum being attained.

## Sources

- S. Peng, *Nonlinear Expectations and Stochastic Calculus under Uncertainty*,
