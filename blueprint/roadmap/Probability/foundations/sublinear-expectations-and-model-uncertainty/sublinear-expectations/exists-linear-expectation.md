# Representation of a sublinear expectation, pointwise form

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a linear space of real functions on a set $\Omega$ and let $\mathbb{E}$ be a
[sublinear expectation](is-sublinear-expectation.md) on $\mathcal{H}$. Then for every
$X \in \mathcal{H}$ there is a linear functional $f$ on $\mathcal{H}$ such that $f(Y) \le \mathbb{E}[Y]$
for every $Y \in \mathcal{H}$ and $f(X) = \mathbb{E}[X]$; moreover $f$ is a linear expectation:
$f(Y) \le f(Z)$ whenever $Y \le Z$ in $\mathcal{H}$, and $f(c) = c$ for every constant $c$. The
linear expectations obtained this way are in general only finitely additive (Remark 2.2 of the
source).
