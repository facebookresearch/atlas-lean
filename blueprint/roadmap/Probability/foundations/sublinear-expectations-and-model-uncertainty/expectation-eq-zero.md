# A G-normal random variable has zero upper mean

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{H}, \mathbb{E})$ be a [sublinear expectation
space](sublinear-expectation-space.md). If $X$ is [G-normally distributed](is-gnormal.md), then
$\mathbb{E}[X] = 0$. The proof compares $\mathbb{E}[X + \bar{X}] = 2\,\mathbb{E}[X]$, by
independence and identical distribution of the copy $\bar{X}$, with
$\mathbb{E}[X + \bar{X}] = \mathbb{E}[\sqrt{2}\, X] = \sqrt{2}\, \mathbb{E}[X]$, the defining
scaling with $a = b = 1$.
