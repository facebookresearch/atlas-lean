# Peng's test-function class $C_{l.Lip}$

Let $F$ be a seminormed abelian group. A function $\varphi : F \to \mathbb{R}$ belongs to
$C_{l.Lip}(F)$ if there are $C \in \mathbb{R}$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

$$|\varphi(x) - \varphi(y)| \le C\, (1 + \|x\|^m + \|y\|^m)\, \|x - y\| \qquad \text{for all } x, y \in F .$$

The class does not depend on the choice of an equivalent norm.

## Sources

- S. Peng, *Nonlinear Expectations and Stochastic Calculus under Uncertainty*,
  arXiv:1002.4546v1, 2010, Chapter I, Section 1.
