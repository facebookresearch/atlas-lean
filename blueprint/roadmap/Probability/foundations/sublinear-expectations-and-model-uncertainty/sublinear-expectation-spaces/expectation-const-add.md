# Cash translatability

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{H}, \mathbb{E})$ be a [sublinear expectation
space](sublinear-expectation-space.md). For every $X \in \mathcal{H}$ and every $c \in \mathbb{R}$,
$\mathbb{E}[c + X] = c + \mathbb{E}[X]$.

## Sources

- S. Peng, *Nonlinear Expectations and Stochastic Calculus under Uncertainty*,
  arXiv:1002.4546v1, 2010, Chapter I, Remark 1.4 (vi).
