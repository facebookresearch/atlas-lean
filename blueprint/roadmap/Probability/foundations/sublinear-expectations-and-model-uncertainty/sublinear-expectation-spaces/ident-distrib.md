# Identically distributed random vectors

Let $(\Omega_1, \mathcal{H}_1, \mathbb{E}_1)$ and $(\Omega_2, \mathcal{H}_2, \mathbb{E}_2)$ be
[sublinear expectation spaces](sublinear-expectation-space.md) and let $F$ be a seminormed
abelian group. Maps $X_1 : \Omega_1 \to F$ and $X_2 : \Omega_2 \to F$ are identically distributed
if $\mathbb{E}_1[\varphi(X_1)] = \mathbb{E}_2[\varphi(X_2)]$ for every
$\varphi \in C_{l.Lip}(F)$ ([Peng's test functions](is-loc-lip.md)).

## Sources
