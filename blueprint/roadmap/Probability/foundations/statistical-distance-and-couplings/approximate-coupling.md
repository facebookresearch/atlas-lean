# Approximate coupling of discrete distributions

Let $\mu, \nu$ be discrete probability distributions on a set $\alpha$ (possibly infinite) and $\varepsilon \in [0, \infty]$. The pair is *coupled within* $\varepsilon$ if there is a discrete distribution $\omega$ on $\alpha \times \alpha$ with first marginal $\mu$ and second marginal $\nu$ (a coupling) whose *disagreement probability* $\omega(\{(a, b) : a \ne b\})$ is at most $\varepsilon$. Being coupled within $0$ is equivalent to $\mu = \nu$; the relation is symmetric, monotone in $\varepsilon$, and preserved by applying a common map to both coordinates.
