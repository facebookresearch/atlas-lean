# Almost-everywhere uniqueness of the MMSE predictor

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal F)$, let $\mathcal G \subseteq \mathcal F$ be a sub-σ-algebra, and let $Y, g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable with respect to $\mu$, with $g$ strongly $\mathcal G$-measurable. If

$$\int (Y - g)^2\,d\mu \le \int \big(Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal G]\big)^2\,d\mu,$$

then $g = \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal G]$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.
