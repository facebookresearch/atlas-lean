# Uniqueness of the conditional expectation

Let $\mathcal M \subseteq \mathcal M_0$ be σ-algebras on $\alpha$, let $\mu$ be a measure on $(\alpha, \mathcal M_0)$
whose restriction to $\mathcal M$ is σ-finite, let $E$ be a real Banach space, and let $f \colon \alpha \to E$ be
integrable. Let $g \colon \alpha \to E$ be almost everywhere equal to a function that is strongly measurable for
$\mathcal M$, integrable on every $s \in \mathcal M$ with $\mu(s) < \infty$, and such that
$\int_s g \, d\mu = \int_s f \, d\mu$ for every such $s$. Then $g = \mu[f \mid \mathcal M]$ $\mu$-almost everywhere,
where $\mu[f \mid \mathcal M]$ is the conditional expectation of $f$ given $\mathcal M$.
