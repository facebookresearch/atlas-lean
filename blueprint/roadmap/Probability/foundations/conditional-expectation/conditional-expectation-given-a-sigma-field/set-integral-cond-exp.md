# The conditional expectation has the integrals of the function over the conditioning σ-field

Let $\mathcal M \subseteq \mathcal M_0$ be σ-algebras on $\alpha$, let $\mu$ be a measure on $(\alpha, \mathcal M_0)$
whose restriction to $\mathcal M$ is σ-finite, let $E$ be a real Banach space, and let $f \colon \alpha \to E$ be
integrable. Then the conditional expectation $\mu[f \mid \mathcal M]$ satisfies

$$\int_s \mu[f \mid \mathcal M] \, d\mu = \int_s f \, d\mu$$

for every $s \in \mathcal M$.
