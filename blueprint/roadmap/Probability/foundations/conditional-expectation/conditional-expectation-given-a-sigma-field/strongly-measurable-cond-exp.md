# The conditional expectation is measurable for the conditioning σ-field

Let $\alpha$ carry a σ-algebra $\mathcal M_0$ and a measure $\mu$, let $\mathcal M$ be a σ-algebra on $\alpha$, let $E$
be a real normed space, not necessarily complete, and let $f \colon \alpha \to E$. The conditional expectation $\mu[f \mid \mathcal M]$ is defined
for every such $f$, and is $0$ unless $\mathcal M \subseteq \mathcal M_0$, the restriction of $\mu$ to $\mathcal M$ is
σ-finite and $f$ is integrable. In every case $\mu[f \mid \mathcal M]$ is strongly measurable for $\mathcal M$; for
$E = \mathbb R$, it is $\mathcal M$-measurable.
