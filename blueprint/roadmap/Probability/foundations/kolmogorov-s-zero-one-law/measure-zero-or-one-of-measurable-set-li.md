# Kolmogorov's zero–one law for independent σ-algebras

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal M_0)$, and let $(\mathcal M_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a
family of σ-algebras on $\Omega$, each contained in $\mathcal M_0$, indexed by a nonempty set $\iota$ ordered as a join
semilattice without a largest element (for example $\mathbb N$). Suppose the family is independent with respect to
$\mu$. Let $t \subseteq \Omega$ be measurable for the tail σ-algebra
$\limsup_i \mathcal M_i = \bigcap_{n \in \iota} \bigvee_{i \ge n} \mathcal M_i$, where $\bigvee$ is the σ-algebra generated by
a union. Then $\mu(t) = 0$ or $\mu(t) = 1$.
