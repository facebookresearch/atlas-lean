# Mass cons

Let $T$ be a finite alphabet, let $q$ assign to every finite word a probability distribution on $T$, and write $\varepsilon$ for the empty word and $q_a(u) = q(a u)$ for the kernel shifted by $a$. For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $a \in T$ and word $w$, the horizon-$(n+1)$ word law gives

$$\mathcal{L}_{n+1}(q)(a w) = q(\varepsilon)(a) \cdot \mathcal{L}_n(q_a)(w).$$
