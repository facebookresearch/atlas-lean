Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra and $\mu$ an additive Haar measure on $E$. Let $T : E \to E$ be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse such that $T^{-1}$ has Fréchet derivative $S'(x)$ at every $x \in E$. Let $Z : \Omega \to E$ be any map on a measure space $(\Omega, \mathbb{P})$ and let $p_Z = d(Z_{\#}\mathbb{P})/d\mu$ be the Radon–Nikodym derivative of its law with respect to $\mu$. Then

$$x \mapsto p_Z(T^{-1}(x))\, \lvert \det S'(x) \rvert$$

is $\mu$-almost everywhere measurable.
