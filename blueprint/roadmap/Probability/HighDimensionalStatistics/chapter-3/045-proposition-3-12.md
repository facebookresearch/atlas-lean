# Proposition 3.12

The Sobolev ellipsoids enjoy the following properties: (i) For any $Q > 0$, $0 < \beta' < \beta$ implies $\Theta(\beta, Q) \subset \Theta(\beta', Q)$. (ii) For any $Q > 0$, $\beta > 1/2$ implies $f$ is continuous.
