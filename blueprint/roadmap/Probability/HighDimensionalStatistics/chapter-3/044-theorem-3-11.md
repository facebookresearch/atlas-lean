# Theorem 3.11

Fix $\beta \geq 1$ and $L > 0$ and let $\{\varphi_j\}_{j \geq 1}$ denote the trigonometric basis of $L_2([0,1])$. Moreover, let $\{a_j\}_{j \geq 1}$ be defined as in (3.9). A function $f \in W(\beta, L)$ can be represented as $f = \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \theta_j^* \varphi_j$, where the sequence $\{\theta_j^*\}_{j \geq 1}$ belongs to the Sobolev ellipsoid of $\ell_2(\mathbb{N})$ defined by $\Theta(\beta, Q) = \{ \theta \in \ell_2(\mathbb{N}) \mid \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} a_j^2 \theta_j^2 \leq Q \}$ for $Q = L^2 / \pi^{2\beta}$.
