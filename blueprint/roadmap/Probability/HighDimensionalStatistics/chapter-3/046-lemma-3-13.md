# Lemma 3.13

Assume that $\{X_1, \ldots, X_n\}$ is the regular design, i.e., $X_i = (i-1)/n$. Then, for any $M \leq n-1$, the design matrix $\Phi = \{\varphi_j(X_i)\}_{1 \leq i \leq n,\, 1 \leq j \leq M}$ satisfies the ORT condition.
