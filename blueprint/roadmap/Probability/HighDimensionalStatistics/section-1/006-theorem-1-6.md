# Theorem 1.6

Let $X = (X_1, \ldots, X_n)$ be a vector of independent sub-Gaussian random variables that have variance proxy $\sigma^2$. Then, the random vector $X$ is sub-Gaussian with variance proxy $\sigma^2$.
