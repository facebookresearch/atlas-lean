# Lemma 1.12

Let $X \sim \mathsf{subG}(\sigma^2)$. Then the random variable $Z = X^2 - \mathbb{E}[X^2]$ is sub-exponential: $Z \sim \mathsf{subE}(16\sigma^2)$.
