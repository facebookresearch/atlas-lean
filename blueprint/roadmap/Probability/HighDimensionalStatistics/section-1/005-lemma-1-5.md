# Lemma 1.5

If (1.3) holds, i.e., $\mathbb{P}[X > t] \leq \exp(-t^2/(2\sigma^2))$ and $\mathbb{P}[X < -t] \leq \exp(-t^2/(2\sigma^2))$ for all $t > 0$, then for any $s > 0$, it holds $$\mathbb{E}[\exp(sX)] \leq e^{4\sigma^2 s^2}.$$
