# Lemma 1.15

Consider a linear form $x \mapsto c^\top x$, $x, c \in \mathbb{R}^d$. Then for any convex polytope $\mathsf{P} \subset \mathbb{R}^d$, $$\max_{x \in \mathsf{P}} c^\top x = \max_{x \in \mathcal{V}(\mathsf{P})} c^\top x$$ where $\mathcal{V}(\mathsf{P})$ denotes the set of vertices of $\mathsf{P}$.
