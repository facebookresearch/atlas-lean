# Lemma 1.3

Let $X \sim \mathsf{subG}(\sigma^2)$. Then for any $t > 0$, it holds $$\mathbb{P}[X > t] \leq \exp\left(-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}\right), \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbb{P}[X < -t] \leq \exp\left(-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}\right).$$
