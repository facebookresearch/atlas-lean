# Lemma 1.8 (Hoeffding's lemma (1963))

Let $X$ be a random variable such that $\mathbb{E}(X) = 0$ and $X \in [a, b]$ almost surely. Then, for any $s \in \mathbb{R}$, it holds $$\mathbb{E}[e^{sX}] \leq e^{s^2(b-a)^2/8}.$$ In particular, $X \sim \mathsf{subG}\!\left(\frac{(b-a)^2}{4}\right)$.
