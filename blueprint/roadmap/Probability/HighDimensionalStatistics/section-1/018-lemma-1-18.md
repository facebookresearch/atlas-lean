# Lemma 1.18

Fix $\varepsilon \in (0,1)$. Then the unit Euclidean ball $\mathcal{B}_2$ has an $\varepsilon$-net $\mathcal{N}$ with respect to the Euclidean distance of cardinality $|\mathcal{N}| \leq (3/\varepsilon)^d$.
