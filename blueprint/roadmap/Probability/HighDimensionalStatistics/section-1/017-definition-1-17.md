# Definition 1.17

Fix $K \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. A set $\mathcal{N}$ is called an $\varepsilon$-net of $K$ with respect to a distance $d(\cdot, \cdot)$ on $\mathbb{R}^d$, if $\mathcal{N} \subset K$ and for any $z \in K$, there exists $x \in \mathcal{N}$ such that $d(x, z) \leq \varepsilon$.
