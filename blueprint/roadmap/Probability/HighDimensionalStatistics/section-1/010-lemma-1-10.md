# Lemma 1.10

Let $X$ be a centered random variable such that $\mathbb{P}(|X| > t) \leq 2e^{-2t/\lambda}$ for some $\lambda > 0$. Then, for any positive integer $k \geq 1$, $$\mathbb{E}[|X|^k] \leq \lambda^k k!.$$ Moreover, $$\left(\mathbb{E}[|X|^k]\right)^{1/k} \leq 2\lambda k,$$ and the moment generating function of $X$ satisfies $$\mathbb{E}\left[e^{sX}\right] \leq e^{2s^2\lambda^2}, \qquad \forall\, |s| \leq \frac{1}{2\lambda}.$$
