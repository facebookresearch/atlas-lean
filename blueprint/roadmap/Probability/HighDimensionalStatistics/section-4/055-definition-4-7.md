# Definition 4.7

A covariance matrix $\Sigma \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is said to satisfy the spiked covariance model if it is of the form $$\Sigma = \theta v v^{\top} + I_d$$ where $\theta > 0$ and $v \in \mathcal{S}^{d-1}$. The vector $v$ is called the spike.
