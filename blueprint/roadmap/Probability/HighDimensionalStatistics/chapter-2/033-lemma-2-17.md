# Lemma 2.17

Fix a positive integer $k \leq d$ and assume that $\mathbb{X}$ satisfies assumption $\mathrm{INC}(k)$. Then, for any $S \subset \{1, \ldots, d\}$ such that $|S| \leq k$ and any $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ that satisfies the cone condition $|\theta_{S^c}|_1 \leq 3|\theta_S|_1$, it holds $|\theta_S|_2^2 \leq 2 |\mathbb{X}\theta|_2^2 / n$.
