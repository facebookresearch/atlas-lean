# Proposition 2.1

The least squares estimator $\hat{\mu}^{\mathrm{LS}} = \mathbb{X}\hat{\theta}^{\mathrm{LS}} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ satisfies $\mathbb{X}^{\top} \hat{\mu}^{\mathrm{LS}} = \mathbb{X}^{\top} Y$. Moreover, $\hat{\theta}^{\mathrm{LS}}$ can be chosen to be $\hat{\theta}^{\mathrm{LS}} = (\mathbb{X}^{\top} \mathbb{X})^{\dagger} \mathbb{X}^{\top} Y$, where $(\mathbb{X}^{\top}\mathbb{X})^{\dagger}$ denotes the Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse of $\mathbb{X}^{\top}\mathbb{X}$.
