# Corollary 2.8

Under the assumptions of Theorem 2.6, for any $\delta > 0$, with probability $1 - \delta$, it holds $$\mathsf{MSE}(\mathbb{X}\hat{\theta}_{\mathcal{B}_0(k)}^{\mathrm{LS}}) \lesssim \frac{\sigma^2 k}{n} \log\!\left(\frac{ed}{2k}\right) + \frac{\sigma^2 k}{n} \log(6) + \frac{\sigma^2}{n} \log(1/\delta).$$
