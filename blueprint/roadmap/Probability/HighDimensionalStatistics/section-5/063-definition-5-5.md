# Definition 5.5

The Kullback-Leibler divergence between probability measures $\mathbb{P}_1$ and $\mathbb{P}_0$ is given by $$\mathsf{KL}(\mathbb{P}_1, \mathbb{P}_0) = \begin{cases} \int \log \left( \frac{\mathrm{d}\mathbb{P}_1}{\mathrm{d}\mathbb{P}_0} \right) \mathrm{d}\mathbb{P}_1, & \text{if } \mathbb{P}_1 \ll \mathbb{P}_0, \\ \infty, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases}$$
