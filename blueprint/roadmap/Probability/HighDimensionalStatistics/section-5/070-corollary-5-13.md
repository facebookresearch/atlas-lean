# Corollary 5.13

The minimax rate of estimation over $\mathbb{R}^d$ in the Gaussian sequence model is $\phi(\mathbb{R}^d) = \sigma^2 d/n$. Moreover, it is attained by the least squares estimator $\hat{\theta}^{\mathrm{LS}} = \mathbf{Y}$.
