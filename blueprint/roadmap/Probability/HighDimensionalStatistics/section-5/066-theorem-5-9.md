# Theorem 5.9

Assume that $\Theta$ contains two hypotheses $\theta_0$ and $\theta_1$ such that $|\theta_0 - \theta_1|_2^2 = 8\alpha^2\sigma^2/n$ for some $\alpha \in (0, 1/2)$. Then $$\inf_{\hat{\theta}} \sup_{\theta \in \Theta} \mathbb{P}_{\theta}\!\left(|\hat{\theta} - \theta|_2^2 \geq \frac{2\alpha\sigma^2}{n}\right) \geq \frac{1}{2} - \alpha.$$
