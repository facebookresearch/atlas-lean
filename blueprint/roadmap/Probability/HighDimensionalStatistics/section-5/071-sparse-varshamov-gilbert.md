# Lemma 5.14 (Sparse Varshamov-Gilbert)

There exist positive constants $C_1$ and $C_2$ such that the following holds for any two integers $k$ and $d$ such that $1 \leq k \leq d/8$. There exist binary vectors $\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_M \in \{0, 1\}^d$ such that (i) $\rho(\omega_i, \omega_j) \geq k/2$ for all $i \neq j$, (ii) $\log(M) \geq \frac{k}{8} \log\!\left(1 + \frac{d}{2k}\right)$, and (iii) $|\omega_j|_0 = k$ for all $j$.
