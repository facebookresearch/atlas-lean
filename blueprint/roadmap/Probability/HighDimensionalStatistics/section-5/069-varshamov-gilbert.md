# Lemma 5.12 (Varshamov-Gilbert)

For any $\gamma \in (0, 1/2)$, there exist binary vectors $\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_M \in \{0, 1\}^d$ such that (i) $\rho(\omega_j, \omega_k) \geq \left(\frac{1}{2} - \gamma\right) d$ for all $j \neq k$, and (ii) $M = \lfloor e^{\gamma^2 d} \rfloor \geq e^{\gamma^2 d / 2}$.
