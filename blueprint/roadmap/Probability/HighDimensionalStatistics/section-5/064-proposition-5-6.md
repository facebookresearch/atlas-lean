# Proposition 5.6

Let $\mathbb{P}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ be two probability measures. Then 1. $\mathsf{KL}(\mathbb{P}, \mathbb{Q}) \geq 0$. 2. If $\mathbb{P}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ are product measures, i.e., $\mathbb{P} = \bigotimes_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{P}_i$ and $\mathbb{Q} = \bigotimes_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{Q}_i$, then $\mathsf{KL}(\mathbb{P}, \mathbb{Q}) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathsf{KL}(\mathbb{P}_{i}, \mathbb{Q}_{i})$.
