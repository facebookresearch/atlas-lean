# Density of the Gaussian β-ensemble

The unnormalised density

$$w_{\beta, N}(\lambda) = \prod_{1 \le i < j \le N} |\lambda_i - \lambda_j|^\beta \,
\exp\Bigl(-\frac{\beta N}{4} \sum_{i=1}^N \lambda_i^2\Bigr), \qquad \lambda \in \mathbb{R}^N,$$

of the [Gaussian β-ensemble](beta-ensemble.md), with values in $[0, \infty]$.

## Sources

- A. Guionnet, "Large deviations and stochastic calculus for large random matrices", *Probability
  Surveys* 1 (2004), 72–172, the law $Q^N_\beta$ after formula (3.1.1).
