# Empirical spectral measure

For $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^N$ with $N \ge 1$, the probability measure
$L_N = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_{\lambda_i}$ on $\mathbb{R}$, built with the
[empirical measure of a sample](../sanov-s-theorem/sample-empirical-measure.md) used in Sanov's theorem. For $N = 0$
it is $\delta_0$, a convention that plays no role in asymptotic statements.
