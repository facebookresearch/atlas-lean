# The Gaussian β-ensemble

For $\beta > 0$ and $N \ge 1$, the probability law $Q^N_\beta$ on $\mathbb{R}^N$ with density
$w_{\beta,N} / Z^N_\beta$ with respect to Lebesgue measure, where $w_{\beta,N}$ is the
[density](beta-ensemble-weight.md) and $Z^N_\beta = \int w_{\beta,N}$. For $\beta = 1, 2, 4$ it is
the joint law of the (unordered) eigenvalues of a GOE, GUE or GSE matrix whose entries have variance
of order $1/N$, normalised so that the spectrum fills $[-2, 2]$: the change of variables to
eigenvalues and eigenvectors produces the Vandermonde factor $|\Delta(\lambda)|^\beta$, and the
eigenvectors are Haar distributed and independent of the eigenvalues.

## Sources

- A. Guionnet, "Large deviations and stochastic calculus for large random matrices", *Probability
  Surveys* 1 (2004), 72–172, §3.1, formula (3.1.1) and the law $Q^N_\beta$.
