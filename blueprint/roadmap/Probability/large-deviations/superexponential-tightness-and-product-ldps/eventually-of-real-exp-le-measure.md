# Local LDP lower bound near a point

Let $X$ be a topological space whose open sets are measurable, and let measures $\mu_n$ on $X$
satisfy the [large deviation principle](../large-deviation-principle/satisfies-ldp.md) with rate
function $I$. Let $U \subseteq X$ be open, $x \in U$ with $I(x) < \infty$, and $\varepsilon > 0$.
Then for all sufficiently large $n$, $e^{n(-I(x) - \varepsilon)} \le \mu_n(U)$.
