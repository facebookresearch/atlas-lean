# Local LDP upper bound near a point

Let $X$ be a regular topological space with measurable open sets, and let probability measures
$\mu_n$ on $X$ satisfy the [large deviation principle](../large-deviation-principle/satisfies-ldp.md)
with rate function $I$. If $x \in X$ and $q \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $q < I(x)$, then some open
neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ has $\mu_n(U) \le e^{-nq}$ for all sufficiently large $n$.
