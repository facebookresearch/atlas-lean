# One step of the superexponential tightness construction

Let $X$ be a complete separable pseudometric space with measurable open sets, and let finite
measures $\mu_n$ on $X$ satisfy the [large deviation principle](../large-deviation-principle/satisfies-ldp.md)
with a good rate function $I$. Let $\ell \ge 0$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there are a finite set
$t \subseteq X$ and a compact set $C \subseteq X$ such that for every $n$,

$$\mu_n\Big(X \setminus \Big(\bigcup_{y \in t} B\big(y, \tfrac{1}{k+1}\big) \cup C\Big)\Big) \le 2^{-(k+1)}\, e^{-n\ell} .$$
