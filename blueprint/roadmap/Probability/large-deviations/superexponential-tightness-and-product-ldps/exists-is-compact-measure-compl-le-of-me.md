# Superexponential tightness, metric form

Let $X$ be a complete separable pseudometric space with measurable open sets, and let finite
measures $\mu_n$ on $X$ satisfy the [large deviation principle](../large-deviation-principle/satisfies-ldp.md)
with a good rate function. Then for every $\ell \in \mathbb{R}$ there is a compact set
$K \subseteq X$ with $\mu_n(X \setminus K) \le e^{-n\ell}$ for every $n$.
