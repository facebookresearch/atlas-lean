# Product large deviation principle

Let probability measures $(\mu_n)$ on a Polish space $X$ and $(\nu_n)$ on a Polish space $Y$ satisfy
large deviation principles with good rate functions $I$ and $J$. Then the product measures
$\mu_n \otimes \nu_n$ satisfy the large deviation principle on $X \times Y$ with the good rate function

$$K(x, y) = I(x) + J(y).$$

The lower bound is local: every neighbourhood of $(x, y)$ contains a product $U \times V$ of
neighbourhoods. The upper bound is first proved near each point, then on compact sets by a finite
subcover, and finally on closed sets by superexponential tightness of both factors.

## Informally uses

- [Superexponential tightness](exists-is-compact-measure-compl-le.md): Step 3 of Varadhan's proof
