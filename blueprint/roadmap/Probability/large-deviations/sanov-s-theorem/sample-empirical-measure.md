# Empirical measure of a sample

For points $x_0, x_1, \dots$ of a measurable space $X$ and $n \ge 1$, the empirical measure is
the probability measure $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i < n} \delta_{x_i}$; for $n = 0$ it is
$\delta_{x_0}$. This is the empirical distribution of Sanov's theorem.
