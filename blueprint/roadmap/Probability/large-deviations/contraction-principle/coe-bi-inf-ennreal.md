# Infima in $[0, \infty]$ and in $[-\infty, \infty]$ agree

For every index set $\iota$, every subset $s \subseteq \iota$ (empty or not) and every
$f : \iota \to [0, \infty]$, the infimum $\inf_{i \in s} f(i)$ computed in $[0, \infty]$ and then
viewed in $[-\infty, \infty]$ equals the infimum of the values $f(i)$, $i \in s$, computed in
$[-\infty, \infty]$; both are $\infty$ when $s$ is empty.
