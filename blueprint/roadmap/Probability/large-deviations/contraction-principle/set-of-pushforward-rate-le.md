# Sub-level sets of the push-forward rate

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces with $Y$ Hausdorff, let $F : X \to Y$ be continuous, let $I$
be a good rate function on $X$, and let $J$ be the [push-forward rate function](pushforward-rate.md).
Then for every finite level $a \in [0, \infty)$,

$$\{y \in Y : J(y) \le a\} = F(\{x \in X : I(x) \le a\}) .$$

At $a = \infty$ the left side is all of $Y$ while the right side is the range of $F$.
