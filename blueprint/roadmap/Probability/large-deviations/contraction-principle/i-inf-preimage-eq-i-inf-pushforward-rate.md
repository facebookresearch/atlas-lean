# Infimum over a preimage

Let $F : X \to Y$ be a map between sets, $I : X \to [0, \infty]$ and $C \subseteq Y$, and let
$J(y) = \inf\{I(x) : F(x) = y\}$ be the [push-forward rate function](pushforward-rate.md). Then,
in $[-\infty, \infty]$,

$$\inf_{x \in F^{-1}(C)} I(x) = \inf_{y \in C} J(y) .$$

This is the identity behind both bounds of the contraction principle.
