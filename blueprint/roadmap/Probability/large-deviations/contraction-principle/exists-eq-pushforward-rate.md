# The contraction infimum is attained

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces with $Y$ Hausdorff, let $F : X \to Y$ be continuous and let
$I$ be a good rate function on $X$. Let $J$ be the [push-forward rate function](pushforward-rate.md).
Then for every $y \in Y$ with $J(y) < \infty$ there is $x \in X$ with $F(x) = y$ and
$I(x) = J(y)$. The minimiser exists because the fibre $F^{-1}\{y\}$ is closed and meets a compact
sub-level set of the lower semicontinuous $I$.
