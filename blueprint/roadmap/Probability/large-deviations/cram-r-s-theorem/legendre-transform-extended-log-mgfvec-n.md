# Nonnegativity of the Legendre transform of the log moment

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$. Then for every
$x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ the Legendre transform of the [extended log moment generating
function](extended-log-mgfvec.md) satisfies
$\bar\Lambda^*(x) = \sup_t \big(\langle t, x\rangle - \bar\Lambda(t)\big) \ge 0$, by the
direction $t = 0$.
