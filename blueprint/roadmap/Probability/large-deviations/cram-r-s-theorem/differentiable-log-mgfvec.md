# Differentiability of the log moment generating function

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and let $X_0 : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be measurable with
$\mathbb{E}[e^{\langle t, X_0\rangle}] < \infty$ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$. Then
$\Lambda(t) = \log \mathbb{E}[e^{\langle t, X_0\rangle}]$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}^d$. The
proof differentiates under the integral, with a dominating function built from finitely many
exponential moments.
