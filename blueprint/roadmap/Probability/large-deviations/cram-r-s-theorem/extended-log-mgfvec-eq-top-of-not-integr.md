# Extended log moment is infinite at non-integrable directions

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ such
that $\omega \mapsto e^{\langle t, X(\omega)\rangle}$ is almost everywhere strongly measurable but
not integrable. Then the [extended log moment generating function](extended-log-mgfvec.md)
satisfies $\bar\Lambda(t) = \infty$.
