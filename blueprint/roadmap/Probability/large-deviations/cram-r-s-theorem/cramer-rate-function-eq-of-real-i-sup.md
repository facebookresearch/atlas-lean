# Cramér rate function as a real supremum under finite moments

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ with $e^{\langle t, X\rangle}$
integrable for every $t$, with [real log moment generating function](log-mgfvec.md) $\Lambda$.
Let $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ be such that $t \mapsto \langle t, x\rangle - \Lambda(t)$ is bounded
above. Then the [Cramér rate function](cramer-rate-function.md) is the finite value

$$I(x) = \sup_{t \in \mathbb{R}^d} \big(\langle t, x\rangle - \Lambda(t)\big) \ge 0 .$$
