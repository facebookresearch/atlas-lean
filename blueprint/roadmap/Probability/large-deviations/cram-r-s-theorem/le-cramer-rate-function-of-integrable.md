# Fenchel–Young lower bound at integrable directions

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$, and $x, t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ with
$e^{\langle t, X\rangle}$ integrable. Then
$\max\big(\langle t, x\rangle - \Lambda(t), 0\big) \le I(x)$, where $\Lambda$ is the [real log
moment generating function](log-mgfvec.md) and $I$ the [Cramér rate
function](cramer-rate-function.md).
