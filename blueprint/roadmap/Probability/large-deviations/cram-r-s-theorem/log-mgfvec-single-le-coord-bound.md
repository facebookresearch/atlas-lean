# The coordinate bound dominates the log moment generating function in the coordinate directions

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ and $i \in \{1, \dots, d\}$.
Then $\Lambda(e_i) \le C$ and $\Lambda(-e_i) \le C$, where $\Lambda$ is the [real log moment
generating function](log-mgfvec.md) of $X$ and $C$ the [coordinate bound](coord-bound.md).
