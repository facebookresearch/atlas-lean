# Cramér's theorem

For i.i.d. random vectors with a finite moment generating function, the empirical means satisfy a
large deviation principle whose rate function is the Legendre transform of the logarithmic moment
generating function. In $\mathbb{R}^d$ under a finite moment generating function, the upper
bound follows from Chernoff bounds in half-spaces and exponential tightness, and the lower bound
from exponential tilting, after a quadratic perturbation of the log-moment generating
function reduces every point to a gradient point.
