# Extended log moment agrees with the real one at integrable directions

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ with
$e^{\langle t, X\rangle}$ integrable. Then the [extended log moment generating
function](extended-log-mgfvec.md) at $t$ is the real number $\Lambda(t)$, the value of the [real
log moment generating function](log-mgfvec.md).
