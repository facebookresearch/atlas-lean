# Cramér rate function as a supremum when all moments are integrable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ with $e^{\langle t, X\rangle}$
integrable for every $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$, and let $\Lambda$ be its [real log moment generating
function](log-mgfvec.md). Then for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ the [Cramér rate
function](cramer-rate-function.md) is

$$I(x) = \sup_{t \in \mathbb{R}^d} \max\big(\langle t, x\rangle - \Lambda(t), 0\big) \in [0, \infty].$$
