# Exponential moments of the partial sums

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be measurable, independent random
vectors in $\mathbb{R}^d$, each identically distributed with $X_0$, with
$\mathbb{E}[e^{\langle t, X_0\rangle}] < \infty$ for every $t$. Let $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$, $n > 0$
and $S_n = \sum_{i < n} X_i$. Then $e^{\langle t, S_n\rangle}$ is integrable and
$\mathbb{E}[e^{\langle t, S_n\rangle}] = e^{n \Lambda(t)}$, where
$\Lambda(t) = \log \mathbb{E}[e^{\langle t, X_0\rangle}]$.
