# Cramér rate function vanishes at the mean

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and let $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be integrable with
$e^{\langle t, X\rangle}$ integrable for every $t \in \mathbb{R}^d$. Then the [Cramér rate
function](cramer-rate-function.md) vanishes at the mean: $I(\mathbb{E}[X]) = 0$.
