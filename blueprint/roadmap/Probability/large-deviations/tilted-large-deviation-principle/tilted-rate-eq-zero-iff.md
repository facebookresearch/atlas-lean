# Zeros of the tilted rate function

Let $F : X \to \mathbb{R}$, $I : X \to [0, \infty]$ and $S = \sup_{y \in X} (F(y) - I(y))$ in
$[-\infty, \infty]$. Then for every $x \in X$, the tilted rate
$J(x) = \big(S - (F(x) - I(x))\big)^+ \in [0, \infty]$ vanishes if and only if
$F(x) - I(x) = S$, that is, exactly at the maximisers of $F - I$. Here $(\cdot)^+$ sends negative
values of $[-\infty, \infty]$ to $0$.
