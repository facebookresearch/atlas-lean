# Mass of a measurable set under a tilted measure

Let $X$ be a measurable space, $\mu_n$ measures on $X$, $F : X \to \mathbb{R}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$
and $A \subseteq X$ measurable. Then the [tilted measure](tilted-measure.md) gives

$$Q_n(A) = \Big(\int e^{nF} \, d\mu_n\Big)^{-1} \int_A e^{nF} \, d\mu_n$$

in $[0, \infty]$ (with $0^{-1} = \infty$ and $\infty^{-1} = 0$).
