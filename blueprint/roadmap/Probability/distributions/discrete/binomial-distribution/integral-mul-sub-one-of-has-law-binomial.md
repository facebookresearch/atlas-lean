# Second factorial moment of a binomial random variable

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0, 1]$, and let $X$ be a real random variable on a measure space
$(\Omega, P)$ whose law under $P$ is the binomial law $\operatorname{Bin}(n, p)$ on $\mathbb{R}$.
Then

$$\int_\Omega X (X - 1) \, dP = n (n - 1)\, p^2 .$$
