# Le Cam's inequality

Let $p \in [0, 1]^m$ and $\lambda = \sum_i p_i$. Then the series
$\sum_{j \ge 0} \bigl| \pi_p(j) - e^{-\lambda} \lambda^j / j! \bigr|$ converges and

$$\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \Bigl| \pi_p(j) - e^{-\lambda} \frac{\lambda^j}{j!} \Bigr| \le 2 \sum_i p_i^2,$$

where $\pi_p$ is the [Poisson-binomial mass function](../poisson-binomial-law/poisson-binomial-pmf.md).

## Sources

- L. Le Cam, *An approximation theorem for the Poisson binomial distribution*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 10 (1960), 1181–1197.
