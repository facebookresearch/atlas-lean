# Poisson-binomial law with equal probabilities is binomial

For $m, j \in \mathbb{N}$ and every real $a$, the [Poisson-binomial mass
function](poisson-binomial-pmf.md) of the constant vector $(a, \dots, a) \in \mathbb{R}^m$ is

$$\pi_{(a, \dots, a)}(j) = \binom{m}{j} a^j (1 - a)^{m - j},$$

where $m - j$ is truncated at $0$ (for $j > m$ both sides vanish).
