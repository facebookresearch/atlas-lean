# Poisson-binomial mass at zero after adding a trial

Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$, $q \in \mathbb{R}^m$, and let $(a, q) \in \mathbb{R}^{m+1}$ be the vector with first entry
$a$ followed by $q$. Then the [Poisson-binomial mass function](poisson-binomial-pmf.md) satisfies
$\pi_{(a, q)}(0) = (1 - a)\, \pi_q(0)$: no success requires a failure of the new trial.
