# First-step recurrence for the Poisson-binomial mass function

Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$, $q \in \mathbb{R}^m$, and let $(a, q) \in \mathbb{R}^{m+1}$ be the vector with first entry
$a$ followed by $q$. For every $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the [Poisson-binomial mass
function](poisson-binomial-pmf.md) satisfies

$$\pi_{(a, q)}(j + 1) = a\, \pi_q(j) + (1 - a)\, \pi_q(j + 1) .$$
