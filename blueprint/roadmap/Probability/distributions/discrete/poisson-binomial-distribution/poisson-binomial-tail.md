# Upper tail of the Poisson-binomial law

For $p \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the upper tail of the Poisson-binomial law is

$$T_k(p) = \sum_{j=k}^{m} \pi_p(j),$$

where $\pi_p$ is the [Poisson-binomial mass function](poisson-binomial-pmf.md) (which vanishes
above $m$); for $p \in [0, 1]^m$ it is $P(S \ge k)$ for the number $S$ of successes.
