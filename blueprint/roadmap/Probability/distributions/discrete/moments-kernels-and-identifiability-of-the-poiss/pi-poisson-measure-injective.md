# A product of Poisson laws determines its means

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set. A product of Poisson laws determines its vector of means: for $r, s \in [0, \infty)^\iota$, with $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$,

$$\bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(r_i) = \bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(s_i) \implies r = s.$$
