# The product Poisson kernel

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set. The product Poisson kernel is the Markov kernel from $[0, \infty)^\iota$ to $\mathbb{N}^\iota$ that sends a vector of means $r = (r_i)_{i \in \iota}$ to the law of independent Poisson counts with those means:

$$r \longmapsto \bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(r_i), \qquad \mathrm{Po}(r_i)(\{n\}) = e^{-r_i} \frac{r_i^n}{n!}.$$
