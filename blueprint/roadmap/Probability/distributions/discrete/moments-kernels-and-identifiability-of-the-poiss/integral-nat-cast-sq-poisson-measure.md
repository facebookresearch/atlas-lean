# Second moment of the Poisson distribution

Let $r \ge 0$ and let $\mathrm{Po}(r)$ be the Poisson law on $\mathbb{N}$ with mean $r$, $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$. Then its second moment is

$$\int_{\mathbb{N}} n^2 \, d\mathrm{Po}(r)(n) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty n^2 \, e^{-r} \frac{r^n}{n!} = r + r^2.$$
