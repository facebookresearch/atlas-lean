# Self-convolution as a sum over the lattice

Let $d\ge 0$ and let $p:\mathbb Z^d\to\mathbb R$ be any function. Discrete self-convolution at $z\in\mathbb Z^d$ sums the products $p(a)p(b)$ over pairs $(a,b)$ with $a+b=z$. Each such pair is uniquely of the form $(x,z-x)$, so

$$ (p*p)(z)=\sum_{x\in\mathbb Z^d}p(x)p(z-x). $$

Both sides use the totalized sum, which is zero for a nonsummable family. The reindexing therefore requires no summability or probability assumption.
