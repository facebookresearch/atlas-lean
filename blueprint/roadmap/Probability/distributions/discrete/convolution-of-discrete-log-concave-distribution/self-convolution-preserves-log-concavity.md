# Self-convolution of discrete log-concave distributions

Call a probability mass function $p$ on $\mathbb Z^d$ log-concave if it is the restriction to $\mathbb Z^d$ of a function $f : \mathbb R^d \to [0, \infty)$ with $f((1-t)x + ty) \ge f(x)^{1-t}f(y)^t$ for all $x, y$ and $t \in [0, 1]$; its self-convolution is $(p * p)(z) = \sum_x p(x)p(z - x)$. The question (Fradelizi) asks whether, in every dimension $d$, the self-convolution of a log-concave probability mass function on $\mathbb Z^d$ is again the restriction of such a function.
