# Self-convolution on $\mathbb{Z}^d$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p : \mathbb{Z}^d \to \mathbb{R}$. The self-convolution of $p$ is the function $p * p : \mathbb{Z}^d \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$(p * p)(z) = \sum_{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d} p(x)\, p(z - x),$$

where an unconditional sum that does not converge is given the value $0$.
