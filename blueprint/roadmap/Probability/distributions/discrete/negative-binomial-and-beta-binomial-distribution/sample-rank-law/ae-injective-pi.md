# Atomless i.i.d. scores are almost surely distinct

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $\nu$ a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ without atoms
($\nu(\{x\}) = 0$ for every $x$). Under the product measure $\nu^{\otimes \iota}$ on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$,
almost every $\omega$ has pairwise distinct coordinates: for $a \ne b$ the pair
$(\omega_a, \omega_b)$ has law $\nu \otimes \nu$, which gives the diagonal measure
$\int \nu(\{x\})\, d\nu(x) = 0$. No density is assumed.
