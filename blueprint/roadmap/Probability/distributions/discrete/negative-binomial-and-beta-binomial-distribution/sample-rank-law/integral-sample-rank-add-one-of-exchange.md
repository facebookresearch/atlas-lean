# Mean of the sample rank for exchangeable scores without ties

Let $m, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$, and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on the score
space $\mathbb{R}^{\{0, \dots, m\} \sqcup \{1, \dots, N\}}$ that is invariant under every permutation
of the coordinates and under which the scores are almost surely pairwise distinct. Then the rank
$K = J + 1$, where $J$ is the [sample rank](sample-rank.md), has mean

$$\int (J + 1)\, d\mu = 1 + \frac{N r}{m + 2} .$$

No moment assumption on the scores is needed: $K$ is bounded.
