# Distribution function of the rank (i.i.d. atomless scores)

Let $m, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$, and let the scores be i.i.d. with law $\nu$, a
probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ without atoms. Then for every $t \in \mathbb{N}$, with $J$ the [sample
rank](sample-rank.md),

$$P(J + 1 \le t) = \sum_{j=0}^{t - 1} \mathrm{BB}(N; r, m + 2 - r)(j) .$$
