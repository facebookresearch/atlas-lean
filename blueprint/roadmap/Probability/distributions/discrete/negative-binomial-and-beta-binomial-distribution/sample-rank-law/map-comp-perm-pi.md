# Product measures are exchangeable

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $\nu$ a $\sigma$-finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$. For every permutation
$\sigma$ of $\iota$, the image of the product measure $\nu^{\otimes \iota}$ on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ under
$\omega \mapsto \omega \circ \sigma$ is $\nu^{\otimes \iota}$.
