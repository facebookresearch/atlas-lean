# Distribution function of the rank for exchangeable scores without ties

Let $m, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$, and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on the score
space that is invariant under every permutation of the coordinates and under which the scores are
almost surely pairwise distinct. Then for every $t \in \mathbb{N}$, with $J$ the [sample
rank](sample-rank.md),

$$F(t) = \mu(J + 1 \le t) = \sum_{j=0}^{t - 1} \mathrm{BB}(N; r, m + 2 - r)(j) .$$
