# The sample rank

For $r \in \mathbb{N}$ and a score vector $\omega$ with real scores $\omega_0, \dots, \omega_m$ and synthetic
scores $\tilde\omega_1, \dots, \tilde\omega_N$, the sample rank $J(\omega)$ is the number of synthetic
scores strictly below the [$r$-th real order statistic](../window-probabilities/real-order-statistic.md) $S_{(r)}(\omega)$; the
rank of $S_{(r)}$ among the synthetic scores is $K = J + 1$.
