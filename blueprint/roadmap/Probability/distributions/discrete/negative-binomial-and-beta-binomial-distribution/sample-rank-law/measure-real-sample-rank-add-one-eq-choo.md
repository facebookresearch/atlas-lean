# Binomial-ratio form of the sample-rank law

Let $m, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$, and let the scores be i.i.d. with law $\nu$, a
probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ without atoms. Then for $1 \le k \le N + 1$ the rank $K = J + 1$,
where $J$ is the [sample rank](sample-rank.md), satisfies

$$P(K = k) = \frac{\binom{k + r - 2}{r - 1} \binom{N + m + 2 - k - r}{m + 1 - r}}{\binom{N + m + 1}{m + 1}} .$$
