# Mean of the sample rank (i.i.d. atomless scores)

Let $m, N, r \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$, and let the $m + 1$ real and $N$ synthetic scores be
i.i.d. with law $\nu$, a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ without atoms. Then the rank $K = J + 1$,
where $J$ is the [sample rank](sample-rank.md), satisfies $\mathbb E K = 1 + N r/(m + 2)$, with no moment
assumption on $\nu$.
