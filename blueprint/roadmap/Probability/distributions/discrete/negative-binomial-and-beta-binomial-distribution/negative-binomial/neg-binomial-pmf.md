# Negative binomial mass function with real shape

For real $r$ and $p$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the negative binomial mass function is

$$\mathrm{NB}_{r, p}(k) = \frac{r^{(k)}}{k!}\, p^r (1 - p)^k,$$

with the rising factorial $r^{(k)} = r(r+1)\cdots(r+k-1)$ and the real power $p^r$ (the usual power of a positive base, $0^0 = 1$ and $0^s = 0$ for $s \ne 0$, and $x^s = |x|^s \cos(\pi s)$ for $x < 0$). It is a probability
mass function on $\mathbb{N}$ for $r > 0$ and $0 < p \le 1$.

## Sources

- N. L. Johnson, A. W. Kemp and S. Kotz, *Univariate Discrete Distributions*, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2005, Chapter 5.
