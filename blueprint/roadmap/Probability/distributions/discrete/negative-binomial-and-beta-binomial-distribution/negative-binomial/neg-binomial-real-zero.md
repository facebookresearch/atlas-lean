# Zero class of the negative binomial law

For all real $\theta, \mu$, the [negative binomial mass function](neg-binomial-real.md) satisfies
$\mathrm{NB}(0; \theta, \mu) = (\theta/(\theta + \mu))^\theta$, with $\theta/0 = 0$ and real powers: the usual power of a positive base, $0^0 = 1$ and $0^s = 0$ for $s \ne 0$, and $x^s = |x|^s \cos(\pi s)$ for $x < 0$.
