# Variance of the negative binomial law

For $\theta > 0$ and $\mu \ge 0$,

$$\sum_{k \ge 0} k^2\, \mathrm{NB}(k; \theta, \mu) - \Bigl( \sum_{k \ge 0} k\, \mathrm{NB}(k; \theta, \mu) \Bigr)^2 = \mu + \frac{\mu^2}{\theta},$$

where $\mathrm{NB}$ is the [negative binomial law](neg-binomial-real.md) with size $\theta$ and mean $\mu$.
