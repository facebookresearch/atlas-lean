# Greenwood–Yule, mean form

For $\theta, \mu > 0$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the [mixed Poisson mass](poisson-mixture-real.md) over the
gamma law $\Gamma(\theta, \theta/\mu)$ with shape $\theta$ and rate $\theta/\mu$ (mean $\mu$, variance
$\mu^2/\theta$) is the [negative binomial mass](neg-binomial-real.md) with size $\theta$ and mean $\mu$:
$p_{\Gamma(\theta, \theta/\mu)}(k) = \mathrm{NB}(k; \theta, \mu)$.

## Sources

- M. Greenwood and G. U. Yule, *An inquiry into the nature of frequency distributions representative of multiple happenings*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society 83 (1920), 255–279.
