# Greenwood–Yule

For $a, b > 0$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the [mixed Poisson mass](../mixed-poisson/poisson-mixture-real.md) over the gamma law
$\Gamma(a, b)$ with shape $a$ and rate $b$ is the [negative binomial mass](../negative-binomial/neg-binomial-real.md) with
size $a$ and mean $a/b$: $p_{\Gamma(a, b)}(k) = \mathrm{NB}(k; a, a/b)$.

## Sources

- M. Greenwood and G. U. Yule, *An inquiry into the nature of frequency distributions representative of multiple happenings*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society 83 (1920), 255–279.
