# Immigration–death stationary law

For real $\kappa$ and $\gamma \ne 0$, the Poisson law with mean $\kappa/\gamma$,
$p(m) = e^{-\kappa/\gamma} (\kappa/\gamma)^m / m!$, satisfies global balance for the immigration–death
chain: its [net probability flow](immigration-death-flow.md) vanishes at every state $n$. The identity
holds for all real $\kappa$; the chain is meaningful for $\kappa \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$.
