# Fano factor of bursty expression

For $a, b > 0$, the [negative binomial law](../negative-binomial/neg-binomial-real.md) $\mathrm{NB}(\cdot; a, ab)$ with size $a$
and mean $ab$ has Fano factor

$$\frac{\sum_k k^2\, \mathrm{NB}(k; a, ab) - \bigl(\sum_k k\, \mathrm{NB}(k; a, ab)\bigr)^2}{\sum_k k\, \mathrm{NB}(k; a, ab)} = 1 + b .$$
