# Second factorial moment of the beta-binomial law

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\alpha, \beta > 0$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} k (k - 1)\, \mathrm{BB}(n; \alpha, \beta)(k) = \frac{n (n - 1)\, \alpha (\alpha + 1)}{(\alpha + \beta)(\alpha + \beta + 1)},$$

where $\mathrm{BB}$ is the [beta-binomial mass function](beta-binomial-pmf.md).
