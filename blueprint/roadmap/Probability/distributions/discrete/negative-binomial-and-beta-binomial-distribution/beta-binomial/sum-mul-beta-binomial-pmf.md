# Mean of the beta-binomial law

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\alpha, \beta > 0$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} k\, \mathrm{BB}(n; \alpha, \beta)(k) = \frac{n \alpha}{\alpha + \beta} = n\pi, \qquad \pi = \frac{\alpha}{\alpha + \beta},$$

where $\mathrm{BB}$ is the [beta-binomial mass function](beta-binomial-pmf.md).
