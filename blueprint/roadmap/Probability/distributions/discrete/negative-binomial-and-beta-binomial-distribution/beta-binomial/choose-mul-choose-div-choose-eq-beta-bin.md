# The binomial ratio is the beta-binomial mass

Let $m, N, r, j \in \mathbb{N}$ with $1 \le r \le m + 1$ and $j \le N$, and put $p = r - 1 + j$. Then

$$\frac{\binom{p}{r - 1} \binom{m + N - p}{m + 1 - r}}{\binom{m + 1 + N}{m + 1}} = \mathrm{BB}(N; r, m + 2 - r)(j),$$

where $\mathrm{BB}$ is the [beta-binomial mass function](beta-binomial-pmf.md).
