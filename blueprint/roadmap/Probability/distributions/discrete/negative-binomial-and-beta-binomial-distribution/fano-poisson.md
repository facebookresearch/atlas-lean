# Fano factor of the Poisson law

For $m > 0$, the Poisson law with mean $m$, $p(k) = e^{-m} m^k / k!$, has Fano factor

$$\frac{\sum_k k^2 p(k) - \bigl(\sum_k k\, p(k)\bigr)^2}{\sum_k k\, p(k)} = 1 ;$$

in particular the stationary law of the [immigration–death chain](immigration-death-flow-poisson.md)
has Fano factor $1$.
