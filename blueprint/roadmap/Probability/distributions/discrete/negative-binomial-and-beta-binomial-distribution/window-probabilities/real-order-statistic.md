# The $r$-th real order statistic

Consider score vectors $\omega$ indexed by $m + 1$ real labels $i \in \{0, \dots, m\}$ and $N$ synthetic
labels $s \in \{1, \dots, N\}$. For $r \in \mathbb{N}$, the $r$-th real order statistic $S_{(r)}(\omega)$ is
the $r$-th smallest of the real scores $\omega_i$, counted with multiplicity: the least $\omega_i$
such that at least $r$ real scores are $\le \omega_i$, and $+\infty$ if there is none. It is finite for
$1 \le r \le m + 1$ ([statement](exists-real-order-statistic-eq.md)); for $r = 0$ it is the minimum of
the real scores.
