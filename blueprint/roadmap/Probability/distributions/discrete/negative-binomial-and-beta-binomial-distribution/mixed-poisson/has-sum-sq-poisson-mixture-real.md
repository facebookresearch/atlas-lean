# Second moment of a mixed Poisson law

Let $\nu$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on $[0, \infty)$ with $\int r^2\, d\nu < \infty$.
Then the series $\sum_{k \ge 0} k^2\, p_\nu(k)$ converges to $\int r^2\, d\nu + \int r\, d\nu$, where
$p_\nu$ is the [mixed Poisson mass function](poisson-mixture-real.md): $\mathbb E N^2 = \mathbb E \Lambda^2 + \mathbb E \Lambda$.
