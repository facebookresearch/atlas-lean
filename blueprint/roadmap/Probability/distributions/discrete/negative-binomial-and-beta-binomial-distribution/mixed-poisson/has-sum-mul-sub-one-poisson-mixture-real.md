# Second factorial moment of a mixed Poisson law

Let $\nu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on $[0, \infty)$ with $\int r^2\, d\nu < \infty$ (not
necessarily finite). Then the series $\sum_{k \ge 0} k (k - 1)\, p_\nu(k)$ converges to
$\int r^2\, d\nu(r)$, where $p_\nu$ is the [mixed Poisson mass function](poisson-mixture-real.md):
$\mathbb E[N(N - 1)] = \mathbb E \Lambda^2$.
