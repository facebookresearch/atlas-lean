# Mean of a mixed Poisson law

Let $\nu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on $[0, \infty)$ with $\int |r|\, d\nu < \infty$ (not
necessarily finite). Then the series $\sum_{k \ge 0} k\, p_\nu(k)$ converges to $\int r\, d\nu(r)$, where
$p_\nu$ is the [mixed Poisson mass function](poisson-mixture-real.md): $\mathbb E N = \mathbb E \Lambda$.
