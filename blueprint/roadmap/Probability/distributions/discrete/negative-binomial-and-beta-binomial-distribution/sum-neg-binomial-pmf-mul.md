# Convolution of negative binomial laws with a common $p$

For all real $r_1, r_2$, every $p > 0$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{NB}_{r_1, p}(k)\, \mathrm{NB}_{r_2, p}(n - k) = \mathrm{NB}_{r_1 + r_2, p}(n),$$

where $\mathrm{NB}_{r, p}$ is the [negative binomial mass function](neg-binomial-pmf.md).
