# Separation distance between probability mass functions

For probability mass functions $p$ and $q$ on a finite set $\alpha$, the separation distance of $p$ from $q$ is

$$s(p, q) = \max_{a \in \alpha,\ q(a) > 0} \Bigl(1 - \frac{p(a)}{q(a)}\Bigr),$$

the maximum over the support of $q$ only, with the ratio $p/q$ in that direction. Points with $q(a) = 0$ are excluded; for identical point masses the value is $0$.
