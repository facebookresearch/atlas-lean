# Point masses of the hypergeometric distribution

Let $N, K, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $K \le N$ and $n \le N$. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the
[hypergeometric distribution](hypergeometric.md) gives $k$ the mass $h(N, K, n, k)$, the value of
the [hypergeometric mass function](hypergeometric-real.md).
