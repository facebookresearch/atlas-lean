# Variance of the hypergeometric distribution

For $N > 1$, $K \le N$ and $n \le N$, the hypergeometric mass function
$h(k) = \binom{K}{k}\binom{N - K}{n - k} / \binom{N}{n}$ satisfies

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(k - \frac{nK}{N}\right)^2 h(k) = \frac{nK(N - K)(N - n)}{N^2 (N - 1)}:$$

this is the variance of the hypergeometric distribution.
