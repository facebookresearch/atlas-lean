# Normalization of the hypergeometric distribution

For $N, K, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $K \le N$ and $n \le N$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} h(N, K, n, k) = 1,$$

where $h$ is the [hypergeometric mass function](hypergeometric-real.md); this is Vandermonde's
identity $\sum_k \binom{K}{k}\binom{N - K}{n - k} = \binom{N}{n}$.
