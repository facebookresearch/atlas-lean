# Hypergeometric distribution

Let $N, K, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $K \le N$ and $n \le N$. The hypergeometric distribution is the
probability distribution on $\mathbb{N}$ giving $k$ the mass $h(N, K, n, k)$ of the [hypergeometric
mass function](hypergeometric-real.md), supported on $\{0, \dots, n\}$: the law of the number of
marked items in $n$ draws without replacement from $N$ items of which $K$ are marked.
