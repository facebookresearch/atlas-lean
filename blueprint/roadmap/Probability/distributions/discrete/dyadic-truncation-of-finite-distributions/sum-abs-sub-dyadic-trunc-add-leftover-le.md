# Sum abs sub dyadic Trunc add leftover le

Let $L$ be a finite set with $K$ elements and $\pi : L \to \mathbb{R}$ a probability vector, $\pi_l \ge 0$ for all $l$ and $\sum_{l} \pi_l = 1$. For $t \in \mathbb{N}$ let $q_l = \lfloor 2^t \pi_l \rfloor / 2^t$ be the dyadic truncation at precision $t$ and $\rho = 1 - \sum_l q_l$ the leftover mass. Then

$$\sum_{l \in L} \lvert \pi_l - q_l \rvert + \rho \le \frac{2K}{2^t}.$$
