# Dyadic Leftover nonneg

Let $L$ be a finite set and $\pi : L \to \mathbb{R}$ a probability vector, $\pi_l \ge 0$ for all $l$ and $\sum_{l} \pi_l = 1$. For $t \in \mathbb{N}$ let $q_l = \lfloor 2^t \pi_l \rfloor / 2^t$ be the dyadic truncation at precision $t$. Then the leftover mass is nonnegative:

$$1 - \sum_{l \in L} q_l \ge 0.$$
