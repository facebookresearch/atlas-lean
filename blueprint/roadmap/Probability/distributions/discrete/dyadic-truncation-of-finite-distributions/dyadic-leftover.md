# Leftover mass of a dyadic truncation

Let $L$ be a finite set, $\pi : L \to \mathbb{R}$ and $t \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $q_l = \lfloor 2^t \pi_l \rfloor_+ / 2^t$ be the dyadic truncation of $\pi$ at precision $t$ (with $\lfloor x \rfloor_+ = 0$ for $x < 0$). The leftover mass is

$$\rho = 1 - \sum_{l \in L} q_l.$$

When $\pi$ is a probability vector, $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and $\rho$ is the mass assigned to a reject outcome. For other real vectors it need not be a mass: weight $2$ on a single outcome at precision $0$ gives $\rho = -1$.
