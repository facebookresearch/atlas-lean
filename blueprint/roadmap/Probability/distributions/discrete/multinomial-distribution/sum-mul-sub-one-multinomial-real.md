# Second factorial moment of the multinomial distribution

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p$ a probability vector on $\iota$ ($p_l \ge 0$,
$\sum_l p_l = 1$), and $i \in \iota$. Then $\sum_x x_i (x_i - 1)\, M_N(p; x) = N (N - 1)\, p_i^2$, the
sum running over the count vectors $x$ with total $N$, where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass
function](multinomial-real.md): $\mathbb E[X_i (X_i - 1)] = N(N-1) p_i^2$.
