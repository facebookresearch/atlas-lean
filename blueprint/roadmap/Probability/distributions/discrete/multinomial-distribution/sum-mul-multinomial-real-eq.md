# Mean of the multinomial mass function, polynomial form

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ any real vector, and $i \in \iota$.
Then

$$\sum_{x} x_i\, M_N(p; x) = N p_i \Bigl(\sum_j p_j\Bigr)^{N - 1},$$

the sum running over the count vectors $x$ with total $N$, where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass
function](multinomial-real.md) and $N - 1$ is truncated at $0$ (for $N = 0$ both sides vanish).
