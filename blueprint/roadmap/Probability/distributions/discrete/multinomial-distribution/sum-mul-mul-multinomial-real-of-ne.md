# Mixed second moment of the multinomial distribution

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p$ a probability vector on $\iota$ ($p_l \ge 0$,
$\sum_l p_l = 1$), and $i \ne j$ in $\iota$. Then
$\sum_x x_i x_j\, M_N(p; x) = N (N - 1)\, p_i p_j$, the sum running over the count vectors $x$ with
total $N$, where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md): $\mathbb E[X_i X_j] = N(N-1) p_i p_j$.
