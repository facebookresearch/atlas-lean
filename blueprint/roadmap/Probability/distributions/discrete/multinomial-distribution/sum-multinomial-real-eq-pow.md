# Multinomial theorem

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ any real vector. Then

$$\sum_{x} M_N(p; x) = \Bigl(\sum_i p_i\Bigr)^N,$$

the sum running over the count vectors $x \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$ with total $N$, where $M_N$ is the
[multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md).
