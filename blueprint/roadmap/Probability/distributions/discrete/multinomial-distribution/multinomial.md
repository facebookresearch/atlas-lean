# Multinomial distribution

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p$ a probability vector on $\iota$ ($p_i \ge 0$,
$\sum_i p_i = 1$). The multinomial distribution of $N$ throws with cell probabilities $p$ is the
probability distribution on count vectors $x \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$ with mass $M_N(p; x)$, the
[multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md); it is supported on the count vectors with total $N$.
