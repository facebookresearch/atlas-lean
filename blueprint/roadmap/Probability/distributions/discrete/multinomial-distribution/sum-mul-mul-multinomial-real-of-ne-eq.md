# Mixed second moment, polynomial form

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ any real vector, and $i \ne j$ in
$\iota$. Then

$$\sum_{x} x_i x_j\, M_N(p; x) = N (N - 1)\, p_i p_j \Bigl(\sum_l p_l\Bigr)^{N - 2},$$

the sum running over the count vectors $x$ with total $N$, where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass
function](multinomial-real.md) and $N - 2$ is truncated at $0$ (for $N \le 1$ both sides vanish).
