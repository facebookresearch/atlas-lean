# Normalization of the multinomial distribution

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $\sum_i p_i = 1$ (no sign
condition). Then $\sum_x M_N(p; x) = 1$, the sum running over the count vectors $x$ with total $N$,
where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md).
