# Support of the multinomial mass lies in the antidiagonal

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. If $x \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$
does not belong to the finite set of count vectors with total $N$, then $M_N(p; x) = 0$, where $M_N$
is the [multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md). Equality of the support with that set needs
all $p_i > 0$: for $p = (1, 0)$ and $N = 1$, the vector $(0, 1)$ has total $1$ but mass $0$.
