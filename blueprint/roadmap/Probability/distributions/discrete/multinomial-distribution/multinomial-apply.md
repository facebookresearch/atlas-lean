# Mass of the multinomial distribution at a count vector

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p$ a probability vector on $\iota$
($p_i \ge 0$, $\sum_i p_i = 1$). For every count vector $x \in \mathbb{N}^\iota$, the [multinomial
distribution](multinomial.md) gives $x$ the mass $M_N(p; x)$ of the [multinomial mass
function](multinomial-real.md).
