# Mean of the multinomial distribution

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p$ a probability vector on $\iota$ ($p_j \ge 0$,
$\sum_j p_j = 1$), and $i \in \iota$. Then $\sum_x x_i\, M_N(p; x) = N p_i$, the sum running over the
count vectors $x$ with total $N$, where $M_N$ is the [multinomial mass function](multinomial-real.md):
$\mathbb E X_i = N p_i$. It follows from the [polynomial identity](sum-mul-multinomial-real-eq.md)
valid for every real $p$.
