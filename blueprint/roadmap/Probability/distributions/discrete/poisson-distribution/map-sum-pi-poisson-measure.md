# The total of independent Poisson counts is Poisson

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and $r = (r_i)_{i \in \iota}$ with $r_i \ge 0$. Under the product measure $\bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(r_i)$ on $\mathbb{N}^\iota$, where $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$, the total count $N \mapsto \sum_{i \in \iota} N_i$ has the Poisson law with the summed mean:

$$\Big(\bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(r_i)\Big) \circ \Big(N \mapsto \sum_{i \in \iota} N_i\Big)^{-1} = \mathrm{Po}\Big(\sum_{i \in \iota} r_i\Big).$$
