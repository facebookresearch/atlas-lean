# Sums of independent Poisson variables over a nonempty finite set

**Finite sums of independent Poisson variables are Poisson.** Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and let $(K_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be measurable $\mathbb{N}$-valued random variables that are mutually independent under $P$. Let $s$ be a nonempty finite subset of $\iota$ and $\lambda_i \ge 0$ such that $K_i \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda_i)$ under $P$ for every $i \in s$, where $\mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda)(\{n\}) = e^{-\lambda} \lambda^n / n!$. Then

$$\sum_{i \in s} K_i \sim \mathrm{Poisson}\Big(\sum_{i \in s} \lambda_i\Big).$$

This is the fact stated in Griffiths and Ghahramani 2011, Section 4.6.
