# Measurability of products of Poisson laws in their means

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set. The map sending a vector of means $r = (r_i)_{i \in \iota} \in [0, \infty)^\iota$ to the product measure

$$r \longmapsto \bigotimes_{i \in \iota} \mathrm{Po}(r_i) \quad \text{on } \mathbb{N}^\iota,$$

where $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$, is measurable for the product Borel σ-algebra on $[0, \infty)^\iota$ and the standard σ-algebra on measures on $\mathbb{N}^\iota$ (generated by the evaluations $\mu \mapsto \mu(A)$ on measurable sets $A$).
