# Poisson distribution

The Poisson law $\mathrm{Po}(\lambda)$ on the natural numbers has mass $e^{-\lambda} \lambda^k / k!$ at $k$. The sum of two independent Poisson variables is Poisson; this topic extends the closure under independent sums to finitely many summands.
