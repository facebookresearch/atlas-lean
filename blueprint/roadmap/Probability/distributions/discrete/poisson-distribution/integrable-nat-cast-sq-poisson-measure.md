# A Poisson variable has a finite second moment

Let $r \ge 0$ and let $\mathrm{Po}(r)$ be the Poisson law on $\mathbb{N}$ with mean $r$, $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$. The real function $n \mapsto n^2$ on $\mathbb{N}$ is integrable with respect to $\mathrm{Po}(r)$:

$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty n^2 \, e^{-r} \frac{r^n}{n!} < \infty.$$
