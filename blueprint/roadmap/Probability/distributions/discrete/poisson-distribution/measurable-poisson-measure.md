# Measurability of the Poisson law in its mean

The map $r \mapsto \mathrm{Po}(r)$ from $[0, \infty)$ to probability measures on $\mathbb{N}$, where $\mathrm{Po}(r)(\{n\}) = e^{-r} r^n / n!$, is measurable for the Borel σ-algebra on $[0, \infty)$ and the standard σ-algebra on measures on $\mathbb{N}$ (generated by the evaluations $\mu \mapsto \mu(A)$ on measurable sets $A$).
