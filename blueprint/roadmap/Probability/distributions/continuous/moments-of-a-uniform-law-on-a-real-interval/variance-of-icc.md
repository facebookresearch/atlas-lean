# Variance of Icc

Let $a < b$ be real numbers and let $X$ be a real random variable on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ that is uniform on $[a, b]$, meaning that its law under $P$ is Lebesgue measure on $[a, b]$ normalized by $b - a$. Then the variance of $X$ under $P$ is

$$\mathrm{Var}_P[X] = \frac{(b - a)^2}{12}.$$
