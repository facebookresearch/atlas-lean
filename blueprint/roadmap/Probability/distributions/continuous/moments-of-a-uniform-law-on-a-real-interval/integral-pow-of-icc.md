# Integral pow of Icc

Let $a < b$ be real numbers and let $X$ be a real random variable on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ that is uniform on $[a, b]$, meaning that its law under $P$ is Lebesgue measure on $[a, b]$ normalized by $b - a$. Then for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\int_\Omega X^k \, dP = \frac{b^{k+1} - a^{k+1}}{(k + 1)(b - a)}.$$
