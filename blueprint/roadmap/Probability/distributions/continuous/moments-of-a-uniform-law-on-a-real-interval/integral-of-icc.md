# Integral of Icc

Let $a < b$ be real numbers and let $X$ be a real random variable on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ that is uniform on $[a, b]$, meaning that its law under $P$ is Lebesgue measure on $[a, b]$ normalized by $b - a$. Then its mean is the midpoint of the interval:

$$\int_\Omega X \, dP = \frac{a + b}{2}.$$
