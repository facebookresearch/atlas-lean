# Density of Student's $t$ distribution

For $r, x \in \mathbb{R}$ let

$$f_r(x) = \frac{\Gamma\big(\frac{r+1}{2}\big)}{\sqrt{r \pi}\, \Gamma\big(\frac{r}{2}\big)} \Big(1 + \frac{x^2}{r}\Big)^{-(r+1)/2}.$$

For $r > 0$ this is the density of Student's $t$ distribution with $r$ degrees of freedom. The formula is used for every real $r$, with real powers, $\sqrt{\cdot}$ of a negative number read as $0$ and division by $0$ read as $0$.
