# Pinelis's constant for Student versus normal

Pinelis's sharp constant for the total variation distance between Student's $t$ distribution and the standard normal law is

$$\kappa = \frac12 \sqrt{\frac{7 + 5\sqrt 2}{\pi\, e^{1 + \sqrt 2}}} = 0.3164\ldots$$

It is twice the constant $C$ of Pinelis's Theorem 1.2, which bounds the Kolmogorov distance $d_{\mathrm{Ko}} = \frac12 d_{\mathrm{TV}}$.
