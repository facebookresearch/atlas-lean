# Asymptotic total variation distance between Student t and normal laws

Let $d_{TV}(t_r, Z) = \frac12\int|f_r - \varphi|$ be the total variation distance between Student's $t$ law with $r$ degrees of freedom (density $f_r$) and the standard normal law (density $\varphi$), and $\kappa = \frac12\sqrt{(7 + 5\sqrt2)/(\pi e^{1+\sqrt2})} \approx 0.3164$. The claim (AimPL Problem 1.25 (b), (c)) is that $r\,d_{TV}(t_r, Z) \to \kappa$ as $r \to \infty$.
