# Exponentially distributed random variables are integrable

Let $r > 0$ and let $X$ be a real random variable on a measure space $(\Omega, \mu)$ whose law
under $\mu$ is the exponential law $\operatorname{Exp}(r)$, of density $r e^{-rx}$ on $[0, \infty)$.
Then $X$ is integrable with respect to $\mu$.
