# Power times exponential density in gamma-integrand form

For all real $r, x$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$r e^{-rx} x^n = r \bigl( x^{(n + 1) - 1} e^{-rx} \bigr),$$

where $x^{(n+1) - 1}$ is the real power with exponent $(n + 1) - 1 = n$; for $x < 0$ the real power
is defined through $x^y = e^{y \log |x|} \cos(\pi y)$, which for integer $y = n$ agrees with $x^n$.
