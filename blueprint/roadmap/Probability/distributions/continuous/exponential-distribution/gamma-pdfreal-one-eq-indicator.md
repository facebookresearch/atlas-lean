# Exponential density as an indicator on the half-line

For every real $r$ and every $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the gamma density with shape $1$ and
rate $r$, $x \mapsto \frac{r^1}{\Gamma(1)} x^0 e^{-rx}$ for $x \ge 0$ and $0$ for $x < 0$, satisfies

$$\mathrm{gamma}_{1, r}(x)\, f(x) = \mathbf 1_{[0, \infty)}(x)\, r e^{-rx} f(x) \qquad \text{for all } x \in \mathbb{R} .$$
