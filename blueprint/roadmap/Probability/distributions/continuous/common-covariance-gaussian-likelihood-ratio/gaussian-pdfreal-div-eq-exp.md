For means $m_1, m_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, a variance $v > 0$ and $y \in \mathbb{R}$, the ratio of the scalar Gaussian densities $\varphi_{m, v}(y) = (2\pi v)^{-1/2} e^{-(y - m)^2 / (2v)}$ is

$$\frac{\varphi_{m_1, v}(y)}{\varphi_{m_0, v}(y)} = \exp\Bigl(\frac{(m_1 - m_0)\, y}{v} - \frac{m_1^2 - m_0^2}{2v}\Bigr).$$
