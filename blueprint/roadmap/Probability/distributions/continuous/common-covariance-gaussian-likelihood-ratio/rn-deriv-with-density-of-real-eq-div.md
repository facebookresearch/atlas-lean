# Rn Deriv with Density of Real eq div

Let $\nu$ be a σ-finite measure on $\alpha$ and let $f, g : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable with $f(x) > 0$ and $g(x) > 0$ for every $x$. Then the Radon–Nikodym derivative of $f \cdot \nu$ with respect to $g \cdot \nu$ is $f / g$ almost everywhere:

$$\frac{d(f \cdot \nu)}{d(g \cdot \nu)}(x) = \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} \quad \text{for } \nu\text{-almost every } x.$$
