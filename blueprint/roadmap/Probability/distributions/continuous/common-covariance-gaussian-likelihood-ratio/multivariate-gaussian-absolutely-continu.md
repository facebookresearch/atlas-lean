# Absolute continuity, density discharged

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and identify vectors in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with column vectors. Write $N(s, R)$ for the multivariate Gaussian law on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with mean $s$ and covariance $R$. If $R$ is a positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix, then for all means $s_1, s_0 \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$,

$$N(s_1, R) \ll N(s_0, R),$$

that is, $N(s_1, R)$ is absolutely continuous with respect to $N(s_0, R)$.
