# Rn Deriv multivariate Gaussian self eq one

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and identify vectors in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with column vectors. Write $N(s, R)$ for the multivariate Gaussian law on $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ with mean $s$ and covariance $R$. If $R$ is positive definite, then for every $s \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$,

$$\frac{dN(s, R)}{dN(s, R)}(y) = 1 \quad \text{for Lebesgue-almost every } y \in \mathbb{R}^\iota.$$
