# Not exists affine log gaussian PDFReal ratio of ne variance

Common covariance is needed for the affine log-likelihood ratio. With common mean $0$ and unequal variances $2$ and $1$, there are no $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for all $y \in \mathbb{R}$

$$\frac{\varphi_{0, 2}(y)}{\varphi_{0, 1}(y)} = e^{a y + b},$$

where $\varphi_{m, v}(y) = (2\pi v)^{-1/2} e^{-(y - m)^2 / (2v)}$ is the scalar Gaussian density with mean $m$ and variance $v$. The log-ratio is quadratic in $y$.
