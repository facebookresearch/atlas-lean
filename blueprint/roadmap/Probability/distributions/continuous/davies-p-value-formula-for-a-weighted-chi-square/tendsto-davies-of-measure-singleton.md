# Davies' formula for the upper tail

Let $k$ be a finite index set, $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^k$, and let $Q = \sum_j \lambda_j \xi_j^2$ with $\xi_j$ independent standard normal, so that $Q$ has the weighted chi-square law $\sum_j \lambda_j \chi^2_1$. Let $\varphi(t) = \prod_j (1 - 2 i \lambda_j t)^{-1/2}$ (principal branch) be its characteristic function. If $P(Q = q) = 0$, then as $T \to \infty$,

$$\frac12 + \frac{1}{\pi} \int_0^T \frac{\operatorname{Im}\bigl(e^{-i t q} \varphi(t)\bigr)}{t} \, dt \;\longrightarrow\; P(Q > q).$$
