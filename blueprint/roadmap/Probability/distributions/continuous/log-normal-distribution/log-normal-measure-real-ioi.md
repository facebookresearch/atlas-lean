# Log-normal survival function

Let $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v \ge 0$, and let $\mathrm{LN}(\mu, v)$ be the log-normal law, the image of the Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu, v)$ under $\exp$. For every $t > 0$,

$$\mathrm{LN}(\mu, v)\big((t, \infty)\big) = \mathcal{N}(\mu, v)\big((\log t, \infty)\big),$$

that is, $P(X > t) = P(Y > \log t)$ for $X = e^Y$ with $Y \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, v)$.
