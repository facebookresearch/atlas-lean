# Mixed moments of the beta law

For $\alpha, \beta > 0$, $k, m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $X \sim \operatorname{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$,

$$\mathbb E\bigl[X^k (1 - X)^m\bigr] = \frac{B(\alpha + k, \beta + m)}{B(\alpha, \beta)},$$

where $B(\alpha, \beta) = \Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)/\Gamma(\alpha + \beta)$ is the beta function.
