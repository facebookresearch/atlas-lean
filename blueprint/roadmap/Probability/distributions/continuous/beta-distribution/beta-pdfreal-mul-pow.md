# Beta-function identity for densities

Let $\alpha, \beta > 0$ and $k, m \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $f_{\alpha, \beta}$ be the
$\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$ density, $f_{\alpha, \beta}(x) = x^{\alpha - 1}(1 - x)^{\beta - 1}/B(\alpha, \beta)$
for $0 < x < 1$ and $0$ otherwise. Then for every real $x$,

$$f_{\alpha, \beta}(x)\, x^k (1 - x)^m = \frac{B(\alpha + k, \beta + m)}{B(\alpha, \beta)}\, f_{\alpha + k, \beta + m}(x) .$$
