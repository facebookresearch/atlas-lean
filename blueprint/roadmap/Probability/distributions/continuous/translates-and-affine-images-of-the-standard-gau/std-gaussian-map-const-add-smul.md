# Std Gaussian map const add smul

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, let $Z$ have the standard Gaussian law $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and let $x \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the image of the standard Gaussian under $z \mapsto x + \sigma z$ is the multivariate Gaussian with mean $x$ and covariance matrix $\sigma^2 I$:

$$x + \sigma Z \sim \mathcal{N}(x, \sigma^2 I).$$
