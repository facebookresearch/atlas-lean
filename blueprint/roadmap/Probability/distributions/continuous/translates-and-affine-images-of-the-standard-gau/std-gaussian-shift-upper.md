# Std Gaussian shift upper

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, let $\gamma$ be the standard Gaussian measure $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and write $\Phi$ for the distribution function of $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ and $\Phi^{-1}$ for its quantile function. Let $B \subseteq \mathbb{R}^\iota$ be measurable, $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, and $p \in (0, 1)$ with $\gamma(B) \le p$. Then

$$\gamma\big(\{ z : z + v \in B \}\big) \le \Phi\big(\Phi^{-1}(p) + \lVert v \rVert\big),$$

that is, $P(Z + v \in B) \le \Phi(\Phi^{-1}(p) + \lVert v \rVert)$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$.
