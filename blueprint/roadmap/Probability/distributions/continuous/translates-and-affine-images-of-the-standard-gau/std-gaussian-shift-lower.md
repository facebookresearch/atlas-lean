# Gaussian Neyman–Pearson shift bound

**Gaussian Neyman–Pearson shift bound** (Cohen, Rosenfeld and Kolter, Lemma 4). Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, let $\gamma$ be the standard Gaussian measure $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and write $\Phi$ for the distribution function of $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ and $\Phi^{-1}$ for its quantile function. Let $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^\iota$ be measurable, $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, and $p \in (0, 1)$ with $p \le \gamma(A)$. Then

$$\Phi\big(\Phi^{-1}(p) - \lVert v \rVert\big) \le \gamma\big(\{ z : z + v \in A \}\big),$$

that is, $\Phi(\Phi^{-1}(p) - \lVert v \rVert) \le P(Z + v \in A)$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$.
