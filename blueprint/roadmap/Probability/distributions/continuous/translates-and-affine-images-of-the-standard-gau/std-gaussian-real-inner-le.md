# Std Gaussian real inner le

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set and let $\gamma$ be the standard Gaussian measure $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$. Write $\Phi$ for the distribution function of $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$. For every nonzero $v \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and every $s \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\gamma\big(\{ z : \langle z, v \rangle \le s \}\big) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{s}{\lVert v \rVert}\right).$$
