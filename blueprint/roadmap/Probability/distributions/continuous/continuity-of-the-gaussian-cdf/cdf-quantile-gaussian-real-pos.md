# Cdf Quantile gaussian Real pos

Let $\Phi(x) = N(0, 1)((-\infty, x])$ be the standard normal cumulative distribution function. Let $\Phi^{-1}(u) = \inf\{x \in \mathbb{R} : u \le \Phi(x)\}$ for $u \in (0, 1)$ and $\Phi^{-1}(u) = 0$ for $u \notin (0, 1)$ be the standard normal quantile function. For every $u \in (1/2, 1)$, $\Phi^{-1}(u) > 0$.
