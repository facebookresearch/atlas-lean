# Continuous cdf gaussian Real

Write $N(\mu, v)$ for the Gaussian law on $\mathbb{R}$ with mean $\mu$ and variance $v \ge 0$, and $F_{\mu, v}(x) = N(\mu, v)((-\infty, x])$ for its cumulative distribution function. For every $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and every variance $v > 0$, the function $F_{\mu, v}$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.
