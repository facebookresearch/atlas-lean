Write $N(\mu, v)$ for the Gaussian law on $\mathbb{R}$ with mean $\mu$ and variance $v \ge 0$, and $F_{\mu, v}(x) = N(\mu, v)((-\infty, x])$ for its cumulative distribution function. Let $\Phi(x) = N(0, 1)((-\infty, x])$ be the standard normal cumulative distribution function. For all $\mu, x \in \mathbb{R}$ and every variance $v > 0$,

$$F_{\mu, v}(x) = \Phi\Bigl(\frac{x - \mu}{\sqrt{v}}\Bigr).$$
