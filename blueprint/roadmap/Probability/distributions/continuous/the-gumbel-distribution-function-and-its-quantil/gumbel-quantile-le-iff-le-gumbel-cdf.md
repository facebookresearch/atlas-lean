# Order characterization of the quantile

Let $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$, $\beta > 0$ and $0 < q < 1$, and let $F_{\mu,\beta}(x) = \exp(-\exp(-(x - \mu)/\beta))$ be the Gumbel distribution function and $Q_{\mu,\beta}(q) = \mu - \beta \log(-\log q)$ the Gumbel quantile. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$Q_{\mu,\beta}(q) \le x \iff q \le F_{\mu,\beta}(x).$$
