# Gumbel CDF gumbel Quantile

Let $\mu, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\beta \ne 0$, let $F_{\mu,\beta}(x) = \exp(-\exp(-(x - \mu)/\beta))$ be the Gumbel distribution function and $Q_{\mu,\beta}(q) = \mu - \beta \log(-\log q)$ the Gumbel quantile. For every $q$ with $0 < q < 1$,

$$F_{\mu,\beta}\big(Q_{\mu,\beta}(q)\big) = q.$$
