# Gumbel Quantile gumbel CDF

Let $\mu, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\beta \ne 0$, let $F_{\mu,\beta}(x) = \exp(-\exp(-(x - \mu)/\beta))$ be the Gumbel distribution function and $Q_{\mu,\beta}(q) = \mu - \beta \log(-\log q)$ the Gumbel quantile. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$Q_{\mu,\beta}\big(F_{\mu,\beta}(x)\big) = x.$$
