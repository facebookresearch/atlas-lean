# Real moments of the gamma density

For shape $a > 0$, rate $r > 0$ and a real $b$ with $a + b > 0$, the gamma density $f_{a,r}$ satisfies
$$\int_{\mathbb{R}} f_{a,r}(y) \, y^b \, dy = \frac{\Gamma(a + b)}{\Gamma(a) \, r^b},$$
as the Lebesgue integral of a nonnegative function; the density vanishes on the negative reals.
