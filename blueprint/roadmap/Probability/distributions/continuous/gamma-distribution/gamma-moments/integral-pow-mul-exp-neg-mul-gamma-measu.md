# Gamma integrals

Let $a, b > 0$ and $c$ real with $b + c > 0$, and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

$$\int x^k e^{-cx}\, d\Gamma(a, b)(x) = \frac{a^{(k)}\, b^a}{(b + c)^{a + k}},$$

where $\Gamma(a, b)$ is the gamma law with shape $a$ and rate $b$ and
$a^{(k)} = a(a+1)\cdots(a+k-1)$ is the rising factorial. The parameter $c$ may be negative as long
as it does not cancel the rate.
