# Real moments of the gamma law

Let $a, r > 0$ and $p > -a$. Then the gamma law $\Gamma(a, r)$ with shape $a$ and rate $r$ satisfies

$$\int x^p \, d\Gamma(a, r)(x) = \frac{\Gamma(a + p)}{\Gamma(a)\, r^p} .$$

The law is carried by $[0, \infty)$, and $\{0\}$ is a null set, so the values of the real power
$x^p$ for $x \le 0$ play no role.
