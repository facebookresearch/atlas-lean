# Moment generating function determines the gamma law

Let $a, r > 0$, let $\Gamma(a, r)$ be the gamma law with shape $a$ and rate $r$, and let $\nu$ be a
probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$. If the moment generating functions agree at every real $t$,

$$\int e^{tx}\, d\nu(x) = \int e^{tx}\, d\Gamma(a, r)(x) \qquad \text{for all } t \in \mathbb{R},$$

where an integral of a non-integrable function is read as $0$, then $\nu = \Gamma(a, r)$.
