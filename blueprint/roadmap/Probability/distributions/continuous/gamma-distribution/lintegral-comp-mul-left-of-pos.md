# Lintegral comp mul left of pos

Let $\sigma > 0$ and let $F : \mathbb{R} \to [0, \infty]$ be any function. Then the lower Lebesgue integrals with respect to Lebesgue measure satisfy the linear substitution $z = \sigma t$:

$$\int^{-} F(z) \, dz = \sigma \int^{-} F(\sigma t) \, dt.$$

No measurability of $F$ is required.
