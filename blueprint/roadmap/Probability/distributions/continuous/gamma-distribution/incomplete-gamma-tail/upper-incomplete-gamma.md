# Upper incomplete gamma function

For real $a$ and $x$, the upper incomplete gamma function is

$$\Gamma(a, x) = \int_{(x, \infty)} t^{a - 1} e^{-t}\, dt,$$

with $t^{a-1}$ the real power (the usual power of a positive base, $0^0 = 1$ and $0^s = 0$ for $s \ne 0$, and $x^s = |x|^s \cos(\pi s)$ for $x < 0$). The integral converges for $a > 0$ and $x \ge 0$ (and for every $a$
when $x > 0$); when the integrand is not integrable on $(x, \infty)$, for instance for $a \le 0$ and
$x = 0$, the integral is read as $0$.
