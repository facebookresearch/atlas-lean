# Laplace transform of the gamma density

For shape $a > 0$, rate $r > 0$ and $c \ge 0$, the gamma density $f_{a,r}$ satisfies
$$\int_{\mathbb{R}} f_{a,r}(y) \, e^{-cy} \, dy = \left(\frac{r}{r + c}\right)^a,$$
as the Lebesgue integral of a nonnegative function. This is the moment generating function of the
gamma distribution at $-c$.
