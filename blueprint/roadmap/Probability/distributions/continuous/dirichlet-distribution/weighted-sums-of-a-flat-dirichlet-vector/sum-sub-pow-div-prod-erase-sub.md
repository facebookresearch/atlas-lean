# Partial fractions of $(x - X)^n$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n + 1$ pairwise distinct real nodes $w_0, \dots, w_n$ and every real $x$,

$$\sum_{j=0}^{n} \frac{(x - w_j)^n}{\prod_{m \ne j} (w_j - w_m)} = (-1)^n .$$

This is Lagrange interpolation of the polynomial $(x - X)^n$, and it is why the [distribution
function of a weighted sum of a flat Dirichlet vector](dirichlet-measure-one-real-weighted-sum.md)
equals $1$ for $x$ above every weight.
