# Characteristic function of the exponential distribution

Write $\mathrm{Exp}(r)$ for the exponential law of rate $r$, with density $r e^{-r x}$ on $[0, \infty)$. For every rate $r > 0$ and every $u \in \mathbb{R}$, the characteristic function of $\mathrm{Exp}(r)$ is

$$\int e^{i u x} \, d\mathrm{Exp}(r)(x) = \frac{r}{r - i u}.$$
