# Chi-square moment generating functions multiply by adding degrees

Let $s$ be a nonempty finite index set, $p_i \ge 1$ natural numbers for $i \in s$, and $t < 1/2$.
Then the moment generating functions $M_p(t) = \int e^{tx}\, d\chi^2_p(x)$ of the [chi-square
laws](chi-square-measure.md) satisfy

$$\prod_{i \in s} M_{p_i}(t) = M_{\sum_{i \in s} p_i}(t) .$$
