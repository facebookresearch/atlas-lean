# One-dimensional marginal of the standard Gaussian

Let $r$ be a standard Gaussian vector in a finite-dimensional real inner product space and $u$ a
unit vector. Then $\langle u, r\rangle$ has the standard normal law $N(0, 1)$. The proof compares
characteristic functions: $\mathbb{E}\, e^{i t \langle u, r\rangle} = e^{-\|t u\|^2/2} = e^{-t^2/2}$.
