# Characteristic function of a pair of inner products

For vectors $u$, $v$ of a finite-dimensional real inner product space and a standard Gaussian
vector $r$, the characteristic function of $(\langle u, r\rangle, \langle v, r\rangle) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ at
$t = (t_0, t_1)$ is $\exp\left(-\|t_0 u + t_1 v\|^2 / 2\right)$: the pairing
$\langle t, (\langle u, r\rangle, \langle v, r\rangle)\rangle$ is $\langle r, t_0 u + t_1 v\rangle$. So the pair is a centred Gaussian
vector whose covariance matrix is the Gram matrix of $u$ and $v$.
