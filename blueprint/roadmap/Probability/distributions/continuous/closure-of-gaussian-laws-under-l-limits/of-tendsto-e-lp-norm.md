# Closure of Gaussian laws under $L^2$ limits

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and $E$ a separable real Banach space with its Borel
$\sigma$-algebra. Let $X_n : \Omega \to E$ be random vectors with Gaussian laws under $P$ (the
image of the law under every continuous linear functional is a possibly degenerate real Gaussian),
and $Y : \Omega \to E$ almost everywhere strongly measurable with $\|X_n - Y\|_{L^2(P)} \to 0$.
Then $Y$ has a Gaussian law.
