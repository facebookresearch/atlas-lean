# Closure of Gaussian processes under $L^2$ limits

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space, $T$ an index set, $X^{(n)} = (X^{(n)}_t)_{t \in T}$ for
$n \in \mathbb{N}$ real Gaussian processes under $P$ (every finite family
$(X^{(n)}_{t_1}, \dots, X^{(n)}_{t_k})$ has a Gaussian law), and $Y = (Y_t)_{t \in T}$ a family of
almost everywhere strongly measurable real random variables with $X^{(n)}_t \to Y_t$ in $L^2(P)$
for every $t \in T$. Then $Y$ is a Gaussian process.
