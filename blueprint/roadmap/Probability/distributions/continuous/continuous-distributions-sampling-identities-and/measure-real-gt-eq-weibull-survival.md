# Survival of an actual Weibull variable

Let $k > 0$, $\lambda > 0$, and let $T$ be a real random variable on $(\Omega, P)$ whose law is the Weibull distribution with shape $k$ and scale $\lambda$, which has density $\frac{k}{\lambda} (x/\lambda)^{k-1} e^{-(x/\lambda)^k}$ for $x > 0$ and $0$ for $x \le 0$. Then for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$P(T > x) = S(x),$$

where $S(x) = 1$ for $x < 0$ and $S(x) = e^{-(x/\lambda)^k}$ for $x \ge 0$ is the Weibull survival function.
