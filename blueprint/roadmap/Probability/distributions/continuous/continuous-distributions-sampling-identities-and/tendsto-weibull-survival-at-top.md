# Tendsto weibull Survival at Top

Let $k, \lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $S(x) = 1$ for $x < 0$ and $S(x) = \exp\bigl(-(x/\lambda)^k\bigr)$ for $x \ge 0$ be the Weibull survival function with shape $k$ and scale $\lambda$ (real powers, with $x / 0 = 0$). If $k > 0$ and $\lambda > 0$, then

$$\lim_{x \to \infty} S(x) = 0.$$
