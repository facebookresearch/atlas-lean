# Weibull Survival zero

Let $k, \lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $S(x) = 1$ for $x < 0$ and $S(x) = \exp\bigl(-(x/\lambda)^k\bigr)$ for $x \ge 0$ be the Weibull survival formula with shape $k$ and scale $\lambda$ (real powers, with $x / 0 = 0$ and $0^k = 0$ for every $k \ne 0$). If $k \neq 0$, then for every $\lambda$,

$$S(0) = 1.$$

For shape $k > 0$ and scale $\lambda > 0$, $S$ is the survival function of the Weibull distribution; for other parameters it need not be a survival function (shape $1$ and scale $-1$ give $S(1) = e > 1$, and scale $0$ gives $S \equiv 1$).
