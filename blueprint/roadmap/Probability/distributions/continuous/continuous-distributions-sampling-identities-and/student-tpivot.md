# Student TPivot

For real observations $X_0(\omega), \dots, X_{n-1}(\omega)$ with sample mean $\bar{X}_n(\omega)$ and sample standard deviation $s_n(\omega)$, the Student-t pivot for the mean $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ is

$$T_n(\omega) = \frac{\sqrt{n}\, \bigl( \bar{X}_n(\omega) - \mu \bigr)}{s_n(\omega)},$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$, so $T_n(\omega) = 0$ wherever $s_n(\omega) = 0$.
