# Nonnegativity of the Maxwell speed density formula

Let $a \ge 0$ and let $f_a(v) = \sqrt{2/\pi}\, v^2 a^{-3} \exp(-v^2 / (2a^2))$ be the Maxwell speed formula (with $x / 0 = 0$). Then $f_a(v) \ge 0$ for every $v \in \mathbb{R}$.

For a scale $a > 0$, $f_a$ restricted to $v \ge 0$ is the probability density of the Maxwell speed distribution. At $a = 0$ the convention makes $f_0$ the zero function, which is not a probability density.
