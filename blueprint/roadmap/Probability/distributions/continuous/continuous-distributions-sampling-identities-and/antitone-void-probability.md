# The void probability decreases with the volume

Let $\kappa \ge 0$ and let $p_\kappa(v) = e^{-\kappa v}$ be the void-probability formula. Then $p_\kappa$ is nonincreasing in the volume: $p_\kappa(w) \le p_\kappa(v)$ for all real $v \le w$.

For an intensity $\kappa \ge 0$ and a volume $v \ge 0$, $p_\kappa(v)$ is the probability that a Poisson process of intensity $\kappa$ puts no point in a region of volume $v$. When $\kappa > 0$ and $v < 0$ the formula exceeds $1$ and is not a probability.
