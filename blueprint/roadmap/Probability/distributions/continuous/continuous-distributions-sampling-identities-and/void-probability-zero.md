# Void Probability zero

For every $\kappa \in \mathbb{R}$, the void-probability formula $p_\kappa(v) = e^{-\kappa v}$ at zero volume is $1$:

$$p_\kappa(0) = 1.$$

For an intensity $\kappa \ge 0$, $p_\kappa(0)$ is the probability that a Poisson process of intensity $\kappa$ puts no point in a region of volume $0$; no Poisson process has a negative intensity.
