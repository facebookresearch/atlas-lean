# Avrami substitution

Let $\kappa, v, K, t, n \in \mathbb{R}$ with extended volume $\kappa v = K t^n$, where $t^n$ is the real power. Then the transformed fraction takes the Avrami form

$$1 - e^{-\kappa v} = 1 - e^{-K t^n}.$$

No further hypothesis on $t$, $K$ or $n$ is needed.

With a nonnegative intensity and volume, $1 - e^{-\kappa v}$ is the transformed fraction of the Johnson–Mehl–Avrami–Kolmogorov model. For a negative extended volume the expression is negative and is not a fraction. Here $t^n$ is the real power: $e^{n \log t}$ for $t > 0$, $\lvert t\rvert^n \cos(\pi n)$ for $t < 0$, and for $t = 0$ it is $1$ when $n = 0$ and $0$ otherwise.
