# Second moment of the Maxwell speed density

Let $a > 0$ and let $f_a(v) = \sqrt{2/\pi}\, \dfrac{v^2}{a^3} \exp\bigl(-\dfrac{v^2}{2a^2}\bigr)$ be the Maxwell speed density. Then its second moment is

$$\int_0^\infty v^2 f_a(v) \, dv = 3 a^2.$$

With $a^2 = k_B T / m$ this reads $\frac{1}{2} m \langle v^2 \rangle = \frac{3}{2} k_B T$.
