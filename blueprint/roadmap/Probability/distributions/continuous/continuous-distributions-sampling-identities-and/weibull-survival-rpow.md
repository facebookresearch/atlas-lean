# Weibull form under both hypotheses

Let $k > 0$, $\lambda > 0$ and $c > 0$, and let $S_{k,\lambda}$ denote the Weibull survival function ($S_{k,\lambda}(x) = 1$ for $x < 0$ and $e^{-(x/\lambda)^k}$ for $x \ge 0$). Then for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$S_{k,\lambda}(x)^c = S_{k,\, \lambda / c^{1/k}}(x).$$

So a $c$-th power of a Weibull survival function, as produced by a product of $c = n$ independent links or $c = V / V_0$ independent pieces, is again of Weibull form with the same shape.
