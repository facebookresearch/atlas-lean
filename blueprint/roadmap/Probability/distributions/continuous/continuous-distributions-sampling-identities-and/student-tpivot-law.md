# Student-t pivot prerequisites

Let $(X_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ be real-valued functions on a measurable space $\Omega$ with a measure $P$, and let $\mu, \sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The Student-t pivot prerequisites hold when

- $n \ge 2$;
- $\sigma > 0$;
- $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ are mutually independent under $P$;
- each $X_i$ with $i < n$ has law $N(\mu, \sigma^2)$ under $P$.

Only $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ are constrained; the later terms of the sequence are arbitrary functions.
