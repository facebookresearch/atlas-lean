Write $\mathrm{Exp}(r)$ for the exponential law of rate $r$, with density $r e^{-r x}$ on $[0, \infty)$. Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $a, b > 0$, and let $X, Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be independent random variables with laws $X \sim \mathrm{Exp}(a)$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Exp}(b)$ ($X$ and $Y$ a.e. measurable with these pushforward laws). Then

$$\mu(\{\omega : X(\omega) < Y(\omega)\}) = \frac{a}{a + b}.$$
