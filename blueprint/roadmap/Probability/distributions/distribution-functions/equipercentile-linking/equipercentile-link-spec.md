# Equipercentile linking transports laws

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on $\mathbb{R}$ with continuous, strictly increasing
cumulative distribution functions, and let $e(x) = Q_\nu(F_\mu(x))$ be the equipercentile link, with
$Q_\nu$ the quantile function of $\nu$. Then $e$ pushes $\mu$ forward to $\nu$, and
$Q_\mu \circ F_\nu$ is a two-sided inverse of $e$.
