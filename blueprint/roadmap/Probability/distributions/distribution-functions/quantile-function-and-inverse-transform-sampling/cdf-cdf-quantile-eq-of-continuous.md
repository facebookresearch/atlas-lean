# Quantile inversion under a continuous cdf

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ whose distribution function $F$ is continuous, and let $Q$ be its generalized inverse quantile. Then $F(Q(u)) = u$ for every $0 < u < 1$. The inequality $F(Q(u)) \ge u$ always holds; continuity rules out $>$, since just below the quantile the distribution function is below $u$.
