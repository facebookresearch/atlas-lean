# Ergodic theorem (Birkhoff's ergodic theorem)

Let $\phi$ be a measure-preserving transformation of $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$. Then for any $X \in L^1$ we have $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{m=0}^{n-1} X(\phi^m \omega) \to E(X \mid \mathcal{I})$ a.s. and in $L^1$.
