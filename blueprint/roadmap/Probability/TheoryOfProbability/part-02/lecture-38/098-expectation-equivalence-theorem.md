# Expectation equivalence theorem

If $Z$ is bounded and measurable then for all $s \geq 0$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ we have $E_x(Z \mid \mathcal{F}_s^+) = E_x(Z \mid \mathcal{F}_s^o)$.
