# Theorem (General optional stopping, alternative condition)

Let $X_n$ be a uniformly integrable submartingale. If $E|X_n| < \infty$ and $X_n \mathbf{1}_{(N > n)}$ is uniformly integrable, then $X_{N \wedge n}$ is uniformly integrable.
