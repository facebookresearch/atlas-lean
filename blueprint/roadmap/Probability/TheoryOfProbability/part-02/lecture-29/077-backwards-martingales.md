# Theorem (Backwards martingales)

Suppose $E(X_{n+1} \mid \mathcal{F}_n) = X_n$ with $n \leq 0$ (and $\mathcal{F}_n$ increasing as $n$ increases). Then $X_{-\infty} = \lim_{n \to -\infty} X_n$ exists a.s. and in $L^1$.
