# Definition (Absolutely continuous)

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be $\sigma$-finite measures on $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$. Say $\nu \ll \mu$ (or $\nu$ is absolutely continuous w.r.t. $\mu$) if $\mu(A) = 0$ implies $\nu(A) = 0$.
