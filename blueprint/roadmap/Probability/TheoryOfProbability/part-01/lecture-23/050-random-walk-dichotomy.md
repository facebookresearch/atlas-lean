# Theorem (Random walk dichotomy)

If $X_i$ are i.i.d. in $\mathbb{R}$ and $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ is a random walk on $\mathbb{R}$ then one of the following occurs with probability one: (1) $S_n = 0$ for all $n$, (2) $S_n \to \infty$, (3) $S_n \to -\infty$, (4) $-\infty = \liminf S_n < \limsup S_n = \infty$.
