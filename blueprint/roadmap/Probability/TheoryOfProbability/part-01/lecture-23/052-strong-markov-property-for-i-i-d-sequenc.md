# Theorem (Strong Markov property for i.i.d. sequences)

Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ be i.i.d. and $N$ a stopping time with $N < \infty$. Conditioned on $\{N < \infty\}$, the conditional law of $\{X_{N+n},\, n \geq 1\}$ is independent of $\mathcal{F}_N$ and has the same law as the original sequence.
