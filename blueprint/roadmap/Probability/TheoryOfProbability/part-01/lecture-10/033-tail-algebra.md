# Definition (tail σ-algebra)

Consider a sequence of random variables $X_n$ on some probability space. Write $\mathcal{F}'_n = \sigma(X_n, X_{n+1}, \ldots)$ and $\mathcal{T} = \bigcap_n \mathcal{F}'_n$. $\mathcal{T}$ is called the tail $\sigma$-algebra. It contains the information you can observe by looking only at stuff arbitrarily far into the future.
