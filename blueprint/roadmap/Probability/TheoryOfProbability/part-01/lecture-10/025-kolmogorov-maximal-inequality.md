# Theorem (Kolmogorov maximal inequality)

Suppose $X_i$ are independent with mean zero and finite variances, and $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$. Then $P\!\left(\max_{1 \leq k \leq n} |S_k| \geq x\right) \leq x^{-2} \operatorname{Var}(S_n) = x^{-2} E|S_n|^2$.
