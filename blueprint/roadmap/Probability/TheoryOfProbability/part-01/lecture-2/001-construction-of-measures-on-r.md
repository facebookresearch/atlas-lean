# Theorem (construction of measures on R)

For each right-continuous, non-decreasing function $F$, tending to $0$ at $-\infty$ and to $1$ at $\infty$, there is a unique measure defined on the Borel sets of $\mathbb{R}$ with $P((a,b]) = F(b) - F(a)$.
