# Theorem (Lecture 9)

Suppose $A_1, A_2, \ldots$ are pairwise independent and $\sum P(A_n) = \infty$, and write $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbf{1}_{A_i}$. Then the ratio $S_n / E[S_n]$ tends a.s. to $1$.
