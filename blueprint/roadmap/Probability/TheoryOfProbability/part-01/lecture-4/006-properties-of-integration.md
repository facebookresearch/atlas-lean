# Theorem (properties of integration)

If $f$ and $g$ are integrable then: (1) If $f \geq 0$ a.s. then $\int f \, d\mu \geq 0$. (2) For $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, $\int (af + bg) \, d\mu = a \int f \, d\mu + b \int g \, d\mu$. (3) If $g \leq f$ a.s. then $\int g \, d\mu \leq \int f \, d\mu$. (4) If $g = f$ a.e. then $\int g \, d\mu = \int f \, d\mu$. (5) $\left| \int f \, d\mu \right| \leq \int |f| \, d\mu$.
