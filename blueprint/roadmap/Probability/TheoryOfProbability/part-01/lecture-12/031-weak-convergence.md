# Definition (weak convergence)

Let $X$ be a random variable, $X_n$ a sequence of random variables. Say $X_n$ converge in distribution or converge in law to $X$ if $\lim_{n \to \infty} F_{X_n}(x) = F_X(x)$ at all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ at which $F_X$ is continuous. Also say that the $F_n = F_{X_n}$ converge weakly to $F = F_X$.
