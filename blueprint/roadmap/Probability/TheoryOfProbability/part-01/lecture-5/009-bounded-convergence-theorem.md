# Bounded convergence theorem

Consider probability measure $\mu$ and suppose $|f_n| \leq M$ a.s. for all $n$ and some fixed $M > 0$, and that $f_n \to f$ in probability. Then $\int f \, d\mu = \lim_{n \to \infty} \int f_n \, d\mu$.
