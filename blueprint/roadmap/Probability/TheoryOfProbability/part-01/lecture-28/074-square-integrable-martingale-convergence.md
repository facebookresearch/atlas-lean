# Theorem (Square integrable martingale convergence)

Suppose $X_n$ is a martingale with $E[X_n^2] < \infty$ for all $n$. Then $\lim_{n \to \infty} X_n$ exists and is finite a.s. on $\{A_\infty < \infty\}$.
