# Theorem (Uniform integrability equivalences)

If $X_n \to X$ in probability then the following are equivalent: (1) the $X_n$ are uniformly integrable, (2) $X_n \to X$ in $L^1$, (3) $E|X_n| \to E|X| < \infty$.
