# Weak limit of an increasing chain dominates its terms

Let $E$ be a countable set and $(\mu_n)$, $\nu$ probability measures on $\{0, 1\}^E$ with $\mu_n \preceq \mu_{n+1}$ for every $n$ and $\mu_n \to \nu$ [weakly](weak-converges-to.md). Then $\mu_n(A) \le \nu(A)$ for every clopen [increasing](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event $A$ and every $n$.
