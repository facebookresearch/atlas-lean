# Convergence of increasing-event probabilities along an increasing chain

Let $E$ be a countable set and $(\mu_n)$ probability measures on $\{0, 1\}^E$ with $\mu_n \preceq \mu_{n+1}$ for every $n$ ([stochastic domination](../../correlation-inequalities/stochastic-domination-and-strassen-s-theorem/stochastic-domination-of-measures-on-configurati/stochastically-dominated.md)). For every clopen [increasing](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event $A$, $\mu_n(A) \to \sup_k \mu_k(A)$ as $n \to \infty$.
