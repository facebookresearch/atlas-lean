# Increasing-event probabilities along a decreasing chain are antitone

Let $E$ be a set and $(\mu_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ finite measures on $\{0, 1\}^E$ with $\mu_{n+1} \preceq \mu_n$ for every $n$ ([stochastic domination](../../correlation-inequalities/stochastic-domination-and-strassen-s-theorem/stochastic-domination-of-measures-on-configurati/stochastically-dominated.md)). For every measurable [increasing](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event $A$, the sequence $n \mapsto \mu_n(A)$ is nonincreasing.
