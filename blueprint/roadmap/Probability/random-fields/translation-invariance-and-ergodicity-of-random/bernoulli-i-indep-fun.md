# Independence of coordinates under the Bernoulli product measure

For a set $E$ and $p \in [0, 1]$, the coordinates $\omega \mapsto \omega_e$, $e \in E$, are mutually independent under the [Bernoulli product measure](../bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$. This is a special case of the independence of the coordinates under any product of probability measures.
