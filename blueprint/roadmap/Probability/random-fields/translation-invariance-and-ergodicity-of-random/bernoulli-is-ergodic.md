# Ergodicity of Bernoulli measures under free infinite-group actions

Let an infinite group $G$ act on a countable set $E$ with injective orbit maps ($g \mapsto g \cdot a$ is injective for every $a \in E$), and let $p \in [0, 1]$. Then the [Bernoulli product measure](../bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ is [ergodic](is-ergodic.md) for the shift action of $G$.
