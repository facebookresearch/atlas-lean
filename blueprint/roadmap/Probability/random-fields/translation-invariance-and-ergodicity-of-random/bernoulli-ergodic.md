# Bernoulli zero-one law for infinite groups acting finite-to-one

Let an infinite group $G$ act on a countable set $E$ so that for all $a, b \in E$ only finitely many $g \in G$ satisfy $g \cdot a = b$. Let $p \in [0, 1]$ and let $s \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ be a measurable set invariant under every [shift](shift.md): $\{\omega : g \cdot \omega \in s\} = s$ for all $g \in G$. Then the [Bernoulli product measure](../bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) satisfies $\mathbb{P}_p(s) = 0$ or $\mathbb{P}_p(s) = 1$.
