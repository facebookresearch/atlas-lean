# Events depending on finitely many coordinates are cylinders

Let $E$ be a set, $F \subseteq E$ finite and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an event whose indicator depends only on the coordinates in $F$ (two configurations that agree on $F$ both lie in $A$ or both do not). Then $A$ is the cylinder over its projection: $A = \{\omega : \omega|_F \in A_F\}$ with $A_F = \{\omega|_F : \omega \in A\}$.
