# Finite projections of increasing events are increasing

Let $E$ be a set, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an [increasing](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event and $F \subseteq E$ finite. The projection $\{\omega|_F : \omega \in A\} \subseteq \{0, 1\}^F$ is increasing.
