# Log-modularity of Bernoulli product weights

Let $E$ be a finite set and $p \in [0, 1]$. For all configurations $a, b \in \{0, 1\}^E$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(\{a\})\, \mathbb{P}_p(\{b\}) = \mathbb{P}_p(\{a \vee b\})\, \mathbb{P}_p(\{a \wedge b\}),$$

where $\vee$ and $\wedge$ are the coordinatewise maximum and minimum. In particular the Bernoulli product weights satisfy the FKG lattice condition.
