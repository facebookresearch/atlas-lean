# Bernoulli product measure of an event on a finite index set

Let $E$ be a finite set, $p \in [0, 1]$ and $S \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(S) = \sum_{\omega \in S} \prod_{e \in E} p^{\omega_e} (1 - p)^{1 - \omega_e},$$

the [weight](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/prob.md) of $S$ at $p$.
