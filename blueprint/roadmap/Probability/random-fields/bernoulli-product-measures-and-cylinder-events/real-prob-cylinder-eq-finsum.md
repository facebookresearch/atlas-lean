# Probability of a finitely dependent event as a polynomial in $p$

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0, 1]$, $F \subseteq E$ finite and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an event whose indicator depends only on the coordinates in $F$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A) = \sum_{\eta \in A_F} \prod_{e \in F} p^{\eta_e} (1 - p)^{1 - \eta_e}, \qquad A_F = \{\omega|_F : \omega \in A\}.$$
