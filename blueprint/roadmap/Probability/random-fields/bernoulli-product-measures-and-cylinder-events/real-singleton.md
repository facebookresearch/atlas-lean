# Mass of a configuration under a finite Bernoulli product measure, real form

Let $E$ be a finite set and $p \in [0, 1]$. For every $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$, $\mathbb{P}_p(\{\omega\}) = \prod_{e \in E} \mu_p(\{\omega_e\})$ as real numbers, where $\mu_p$ is the [Bernoulli distribution](bernoulli-measure.md).
