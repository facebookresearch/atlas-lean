# Bernoulli product measure on bonds is the bond percolation law

Let $V$ be a set and $p \in [0, 1]$. On configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^{\mathcal{P}}$ of the unordered pairs $\mathcal{P}$ of elements of $V$, the [Bernoulli product measure](bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ is the [Bernoulli bond law on $V$](../../percolation/phase-transition/percolation-on-product-graphs/gbernoulli-law.md) with parameter $p$.
