# Bernoulli product measure of a cylinder event, real-valued form

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0, 1]$, $F \subseteq E$ finite and $S \subseteq \{0, 1\}^F$. Then $\mathbb{P}_p^E(\{\omega : \omega|_F \in S\}) = \mathbb{P}_p^F(S)$ as real numbers, where $\mathbb{P}_p^E$ and $\mathbb{P}_p^F$ are the [Bernoulli product measures](bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ and $\{0, 1\}^F$.
