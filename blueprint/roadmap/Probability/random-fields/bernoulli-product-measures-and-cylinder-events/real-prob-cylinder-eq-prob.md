# Probability of a finitely dependent event as a finite weight

Let $E$ be a countable set, $p \in [0, 1]$, $F \subseteq E$ finite and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an event whose indicator depends only on the coordinates in $F$. Then $\mathbb{P}_p(A)$ equals the [weight](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/prob.md) at $p$ of the projection $A_F = \{\omega|_F : \omega \in A\} \subseteq \{0, 1\}^F$.
