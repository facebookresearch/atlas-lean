# Differentiability in $p$ of the probability of a cylinder event

Let $E$ be a set, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an event and $F \subseteq E$ finite, with projection $A_F = \{\omega|_F : \omega \in A\}$. The [weight](../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/prob.md) $q \mapsto \sum_{\eta \in A_F} \prod_{e \in F} q^{\eta_e} (1 - q)^{1 - \eta_e}$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ (it is a polynomial in $q$).
