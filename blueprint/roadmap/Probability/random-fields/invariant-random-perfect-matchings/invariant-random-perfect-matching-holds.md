# Every infinite connected Cayley graph has an invariant random perfect matching

Every infinite connected Cayley graph of a finitely generated group $\Gamma$ admits a $\Gamma$-invariant random perfect matching (Csóka and Lippner, 2017).

## Sources

- Problem: M. Abért, Some questions (2010), Question 27, https://www.renyi.hu/~abert/questions.pdf.
- Resolution: E. Csóka and G. Lippner, Invariant random perfect matchings in Cayley graphs, Groups Geom. Dyn. 11 (2017), 211-243, arXiv:1211.2374, https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2374.
- Resolution (amenable case): C. T. Conley, A. S. Kechris and R. D. Tucker-Drob, Ultraproducts of measure preserving actions and graph combinatorics, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 33 (2013), no. 2, 334-374, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0143385711001143.
