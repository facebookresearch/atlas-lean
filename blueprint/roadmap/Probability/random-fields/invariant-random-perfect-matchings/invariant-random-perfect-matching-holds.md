# Every Cayley graph has an invariant random perfect matching

Every infinite connected Cayley graph of a finitely generated group $\Gamma$ admits a $\Gamma$-invariant random perfect matching (Csóka and Lippner, 2017).

## Sources

- M. Abért, Some questions (2010), Question 27, https://www.renyi.hu/~abert/questions.pdf; resolution: E. Csóka and G. Lippner, Invariant random perfect matchings in Cayley graphs, Groups Geom. Dyn. 11 (2017), 211-243, arXiv:1211.2374, Corollary 1.3: "Every Cayley graph admits an invariant random perfect matching" (Theorem 1.1: factor of IID perfect matchings for non-amenable Cayley graphs; Theorem 1.2 with Conley–Kechris–Tucker-Drob for the amenable case), invariant random subgraphs being Γ-invariant probability distributions on subgraphs of a locally finite Cayley graph (Introduction), https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2374.
