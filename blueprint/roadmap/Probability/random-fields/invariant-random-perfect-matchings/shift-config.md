# Left translation of an edge configuration

Let $\Gamma$ be a group and let $\omega$ assign the value open or closed to every unordered pair $\{g, h\}$ of elements of $\Gamma$. For $\gamma \in \Gamma$, the left translate $\gamma \cdot \omega$ is the configuration

$$(\gamma \cdot \omega)(\{g, h\}) = \omega(\{\gamma^{-1} g, \gamma^{-1} h\}).$$
