# Number of open clusters under a boundary condition

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$, $B \subseteq V$ a set of boundary vertices and $\omega$ a configuration on the unordered pairs of elements of $V$. The number of open clusters under a [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) is the [free count](num-clusters-free.md) for the free and the periodic boundary conditions and the [wired count](num-clusters-wired.md) for the wired boundary condition. In the periodic case the periodic identification is part of the graph $G$.
