# Averaging the outside conditional distribution recovers the measure

Let $E$ be a countable set, $\mu$ a finite measure on $\{0, 1\}^E$ and $\Lambda \subseteq E$. Averaging the [conditional distribution given the outside](gibbs-conditional.md) $\kappa_\Lambda$ against the restriction of $\mu$ to the [outside σ-algebra](outside-sigma.md) $\mathcal{F}_{\Lambda^c}$ gives back $\mu$:

$$\int \kappa_\Lambda(\omega, \cdot)\, \mu(d\omega) = \mu.$$
