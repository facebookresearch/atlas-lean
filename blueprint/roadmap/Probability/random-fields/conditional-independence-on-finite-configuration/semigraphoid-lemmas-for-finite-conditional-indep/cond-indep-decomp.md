# Decomposition

Let $V$ and $\alpha$ be finite sets, $p$ a real weight on assignments $V \to \alpha$, and $X, Y, W, S \subseteq V$ with $X$ disjoint from $Y$. If $X$ and $Y \cup W$ are conditionally independent given $S$ under $p$, in the marginal sense $p(x_{X \cup Y \cup W \cup S})\, p(x_S) = p(x_{X \cup S})\, p(x_{Y \cup W \cup S})$ for every assignment $x$, then $X$ and $W$ are conditionally independent given $S$: the part $Y$ may be dropped.
