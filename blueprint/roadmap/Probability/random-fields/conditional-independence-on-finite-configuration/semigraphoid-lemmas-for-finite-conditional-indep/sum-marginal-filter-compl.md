# Summing a marginal over a subset of its variables

Let $V$ and $\alpha$ be finite sets, $p$ a real function on assignments $V \to \alpha$, $U \subseteq V$, $T \subseteq U$ a finite set of variables and $x$ an assignment. Summing the marginal of $p$ on $U$ over all assignments $z$ that agree with $x$ outside $T$ gives the marginal of $p$ on $U \setminus T$ at $x$: summing out the variables of $T$ removes them from the marginal's variable set.
