# Exponentially weighted ladder tails at the Cramér root

Let $g$ be a nonnegative real sequence on $\mathbb{N}$ and $\theta > 0$ with $\sum_k g_k e^{\theta k} = 1$. Then $\sum_k e^{\theta k} \sum_{j > k} g_j = (1 - \sum_k g_k)/(e^\theta - 1)$. No condition on $g_0$ or on $\sum_k g_k$ is needed: for $g$ concentrated at $0$ with weight $1$ both sides are $0$. Applied to the first strict ascending ladder height of a random walk, the left side is $\sum_k e^{\theta k}\, \mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} > k)$.
