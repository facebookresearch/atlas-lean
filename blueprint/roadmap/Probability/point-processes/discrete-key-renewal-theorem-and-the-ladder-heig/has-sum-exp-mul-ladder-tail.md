# Exponentially weighted ladder tails at the Cramér root

Let $g \ge 0$ be a defective ladder-height law on $\mathbb{N}$ and $\theta > 0$ with $\sum_k g_k e^{\theta k} = 1$. Then $\sum_k e^{\theta k} \sum_{j > k} g_j = (1 - \sum_k g_k)/(e^\theta - 1)$; for the first strict ascending ladder height of a random walk the left side is $\sum_k e^{\theta k}\, \mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} > k)$.
