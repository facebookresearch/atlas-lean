# Discrete key renewal theorem

Let $f \ge 0$ be a law on $\{1, 2, \dots\}$ ($f_0 = 0$, $\sum_k f_k = 1$) with aperiodic support and finite mean $\mu = \sum_k k f_k$, and let $z$ be summable. Then every solution of the renewal equation $Z_n = z_n + \sum_{k \le n} f_k Z_{n-k}$ converges to $\sum_k z_k / \mu$.
