# Exponentially tilted key renewal theorem

Let $g \ge 0$ be a nonnegative sequence on $\mathbb{N}$ (not required to sum to $1$; only the tilted coefficients are normalised) with $g_0 = 0$ and aperiodic support ($\gcd\{k : g_k > 0\} = 1$), and let $\theta$ satisfy $\sum_k g_k e^{\theta k} = 1$ with finite tilted mean $\mu = \sum_k k\, g_k e^{\theta k}$. If $\sum_k e^{\theta k} z_k$ is summable, then every solution $Z$ of the renewal equation with interarrival weights $g$ and forcing $z$ satisfies $e^{\theta n} Z_n \to \sum_k e^{\theta k} z_k / \mu$.
