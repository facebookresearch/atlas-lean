# Discrete renewal equation

A sequence $Z$ solves the discrete renewal equation with interarrival weights $f$ and forcing $z$, all real sequences on $\mathbb{N}$, when $Z_n = z_n + \sum_{k=0}^{n} f_k\, Z_{n-k}$ for every $n$. With $f_0 = 0$ this is the recursion $Z_n = z_n + \sum_{k=1}^{n} f_k\, Z_{n-k}$.
