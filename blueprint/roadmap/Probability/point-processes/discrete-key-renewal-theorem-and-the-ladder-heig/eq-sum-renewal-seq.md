# Solutions of the renewal equation are convolutions with the renewal sequence

Let $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f_0 = 0$, and let $Z$ solve the discrete renewal equation with interarrival weights $f$ and forcing $z$. Then $Z_n = \sum_{k \le n} z_k\, u_{n-k}$ for every $n$, where $u$ is the renewal sequence of $f$: every solution is the convolution of the forcing with the renewal sequence.
