# Tail of a ladder-height law

For a real sequence $g$ on $\mathbb{N}$ (signed values allowed) and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the ladder tail is $\sum_{j > k} g_j$, an unconditional sum read as $0$ when not summable. When $g$ is the possibly defective law of the first strict ascending ladder height of a random walk, it is $\mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} > k)$.
