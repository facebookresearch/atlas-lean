# Tail of a ladder-height law

For a possibly defective ladder-height law $g$ on $\mathbb{N}$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the ladder tail is $\sum_{j > k} g_j$, an unconditional sum read as $0$ when not summable. For the first strict ascending ladder height of a random walk it is $\mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} > k)$.
