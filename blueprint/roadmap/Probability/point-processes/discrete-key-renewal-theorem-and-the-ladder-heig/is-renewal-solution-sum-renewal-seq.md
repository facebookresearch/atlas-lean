# Convolution with the renewal sequence solves the renewal equation

Let $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f_0 = 0$ and let $z$ be any forcing. Then $Z_n = \sum_{k \le n} z_k\, u_{n-k}$, the convolution of the forcing with the renewal sequence $u$ of $f$, solves the discrete renewal equation with interarrival weights $f$ and forcing $z$.
