# The renewal sequence solves the renewal equation

For a real sequence $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f_0 = 0$ (signed coefficients allowed), the renewal sequence of $f$ solves the discrete renewal equation with weights $f$ and forcing $\delta_0$ (the sequence $1, 0, 0, \dots$). For an interarrival law $f$ this is the renewal equation of the renewal process.
