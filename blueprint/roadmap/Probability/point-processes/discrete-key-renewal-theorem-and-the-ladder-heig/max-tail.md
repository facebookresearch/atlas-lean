# Tail of the maximum of the score walk

For integer scores $X$ with score walk $S$ on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the tail of the maximum $M = \sup_n S_n$ is the real value of $P(S_n > k \text{ for some } n)$, with infinite measure read as $0$. For a probability measure $P$ it is $\mathbb{P}(M > k)$.
