# Renewal sequence of an interarrival law

For an interarrival law $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$, of which only $f_1, f_2, \dots$ are used, the renewal sequence is $u_0 = 1$ and $u_{n+1} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} f_{k+1}\, u_{n-k}$. When $f$ is the law of a strictly positive integer waiting time, $u_n$ is the probability that some renewal epoch equals $n$.
