# The Cramér root forces a defective ladder law

Let $g$ be a nonnegative real sequence on $\mathbb{N}$ with $g_0 = 0$, and let $\theta > 0$ satisfy $\sum_k g_k e^{\theta k} = 1$: the tilted sequence has unit sum, while $g$ itself need not. Then $\sum_k g_k < 1$. For example, $\theta = \log 2$ and $g$ concentrated at $1$ with weight $\tfrac12$. Applied to the ladder-height law of a score walk, a positive Cramér root forces a defective ladder law, $\mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty) < 1$.
