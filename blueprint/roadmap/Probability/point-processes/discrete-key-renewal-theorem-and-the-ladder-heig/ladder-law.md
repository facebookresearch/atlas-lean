# Ladder-height law of the score walk

For integer scores $X$ with score walk $S$ on a measure space and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the ladder-height law is $g_j = \mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} = j)$, the measure of the union over $n$ of the ladder events of epoch $n$ and height $j$, as a real number. It is in general defective.
