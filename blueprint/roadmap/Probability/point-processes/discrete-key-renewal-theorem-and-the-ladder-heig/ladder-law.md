# Ladder-height law of the score walk

For integer scores $X$ with score walk $S$ on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ and $j \in \mathbb{N}$, the ladder-height law $g_j$ is the real value of the measure of the union over $n$ of the ladder events of epoch $n$ and height $j$, with infinite measure read as $0$. For a probability measure $P$ it is $g_j = \mathbb{P}(\tau_+ < \infty, S_{\tau_+} = j)$, in general a defective law.
