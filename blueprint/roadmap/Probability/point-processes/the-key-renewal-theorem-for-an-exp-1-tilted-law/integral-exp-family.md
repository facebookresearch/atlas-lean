# Integrals against the exponential family

For $\theta > -1$ and every $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the integral of $f$ against $G_\theta = (1 + \theta)^{-1} \cdot \operatorname{Exp}(1 + \theta)$ is $\int_{(0,\infty)} e^{-(1+\theta)x} f(x)\, dx$, as an equality of Bochner integrals, each read as $0$ when its integrand is not integrable (for $\theta = 0$ and $f(x) = e^x$ both are $0$, although the positive integral diverges).
