# The exponential family lives on the half-line

For every real $\theta$, the measure $G_\theta = (1 + \theta)^{-1} \cdot \operatorname{Exp}(1 + \theta)$ is concentrated on $[0, \infty)$: $G_\theta$-almost every $y$ has $y \ge 0$. Here $(1+\theta)^{-1}$ is read as a nonnegative extended real, so it is $0$ when $1 + \theta \le 0$.
