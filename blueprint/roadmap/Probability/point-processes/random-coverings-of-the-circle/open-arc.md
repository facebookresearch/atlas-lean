# Open arc on the circle $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$

Let $\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ be the circle of length $1$, $x \in \mathbb{T}$ and $\ell \in \mathbb{R}$. The open arc of length $\ell$ starting at $x$ is

$$\{x + t \bmod 1 : 0 < t < \ell\} \subseteq \mathbb{T}.$$

It is empty for $\ell \le 0$ and is the whole circle for $\ell > 1$.
