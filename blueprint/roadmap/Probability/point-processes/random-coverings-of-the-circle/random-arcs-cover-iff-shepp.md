# Dvoretzky's covering problem

Place independent uniformly random open arcs of lengths $1 > \ell_0 \ge \ell_1 \ge \dots \ge 0$, $\ell_n \to 0$, $\sum_n\ell_n = \infty$, on the circle of length $1$. The claim (Erdős Problem 526, Shepp's criterion) is that the circle is covered almost surely if and only if $\sum_n(n+1)^{-2}\exp(\ell_0 + \dots + \ell_n) = \infty$.
