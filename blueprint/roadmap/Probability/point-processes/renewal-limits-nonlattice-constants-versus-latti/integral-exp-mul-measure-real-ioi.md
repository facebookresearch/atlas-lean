# Layer-cake form of the Cramér–Lundberg numerator

Let $G$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on $[0, \infty)$, let $\theta > 0$, and suppose $y \mapsto e^{\theta y}$ is $G$-integrable. Then $\int_{(0,\infty)} e^{\theta s}\, G(s, \infty)\, ds = \bigl(\int e^{\theta y}\, dG(y) - G(\mathbb{R})\bigr)/\theta$.
