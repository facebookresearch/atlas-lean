# Lattice liminf

Let $h > 0$, $\theta \ge 0$, and let $F \ge 0$ be a lattice step function, $F(t) = F(h \lceil t/h\rceil)$ for all $t$, such that $e^{\theta k h} F(k h) \to C$ as $k \to \infty$ through the natural numbers. Then $\liminf_{t \to \infty} e^{\theta t} F(t) = e^{-\theta h} C$: just above each lattice point the scaled tail drops by the factor $e^{-\theta h}$ (for $t \in (kh, (k+1)h]$ the value of $F$ is $F((k+1)h)$).
