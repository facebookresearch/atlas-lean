# The Cramér–Lundberg pair is admissible

Let $G$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on $[0, \infty)$ with Cramér root $\theta > 0$, $\int e^{\theta y}\, dG(y) = 1$, and integrable tilted mean $\int y\, e^{\theta y}\, dG(y)$, and let $Z$ be measurable, bounded and zero on $(-\infty, 0)$. Then the tilted pair $\bigl(e^{\theta t} Z(t),\ e^{\theta t} \mathbf 1\{t \ge 0\}\, G(t, \infty)\bigr)$ is admissible for the key renewal theorem; the antitone majorant of the input is the tilted tail $\int_{(t,\infty)} e^{\theta x}\, dG(x)$.
