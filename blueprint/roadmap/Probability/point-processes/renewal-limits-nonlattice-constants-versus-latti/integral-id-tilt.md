# Mean of the tilted ladder law

Let $G$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with Cramér root $\theta$, $\int e^{\theta y}\, dG(y) = 1$. Then $\int y \, dG_\theta(y) = \int y\, e^{\theta y}\, dG(y)$ for the tilted measure $G_\theta = e^{\theta y} dG(y)$, as an equality of Bochner integrals, each read as $0$ when its integrand is not integrable. It is the mean of $G_\theta$ only when $y$ is $G_\theta$-integrable: for $\theta = 0$ and a standard Cauchy law the root condition holds but there is no first moment.
