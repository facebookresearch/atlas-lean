# Nonlattice constant identification

Let $G$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with Cramér root $\theta$, $\int e^{\theta y}\, dG(y) = 1$, and let $Z(t) = z(t) + \int Z(t - y)\, dG(y)$ for every $t$. Assume the key renewal theorem for the tilted law $G^\theta = e^{\theta y} dG(y)$: every solution $Y$ of $Y(t) = y(t) + \int Y(t - x)\, dG^\theta(x)$ with $(Y, y)$ an admissible pair tends to $\int_{(0,\infty)} y / \int x\, dG^\theta(x)$. This holds when $G^\theta$ is a nonlattice probability law on $[0, \infty)$ (Feller, Section XI.1); here it is a hypothesis. If the tilted pair $(e^{\theta t} Z(t), e^{\theta t} z(t))$ is admissible, then $e^{\theta t} Z(t) \to \int_{(0,\infty)} e^{\theta s} z(s)\, ds / \int y\, e^{\theta y}\, dG(y)$, the scaled limit $C$; it may be $0$, and when $C \ne 0$ this says $Z(t) \sim C e^{-\theta t}$.
