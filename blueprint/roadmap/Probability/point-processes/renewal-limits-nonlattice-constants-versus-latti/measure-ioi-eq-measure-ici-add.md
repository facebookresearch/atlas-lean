# Strict versus weak lattice tails

Let $h > 0$ and let $\mu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ concentrated on the lattice $h\mathbb{Z}$. Then $\mu(k h, \infty) = \mu[(k+1) h, \infty)$ for every integer $k$. Consequently, for a finite $\mu$ and real $\theta$, if $e^{\theta k h} \mu[kh, \infty) \to C$ as $k \to \infty$, then $e^{\theta k h} \mu(kh, \infty) \to e^{-\theta h} C$.
