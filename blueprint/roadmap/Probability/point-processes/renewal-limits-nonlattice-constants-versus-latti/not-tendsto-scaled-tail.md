# Lattice dichotomy

Let $h > 0$, $\theta > 0$, and let $F$ be a lattice step function, $F(t) = F(h \lceil t/h\rceil)$ for all $t$, such that $e^{\theta k h} F(k h) \to C$ with $C \ne 0$. Then for no real $L$ does $e^{\theta t} F(t)$ tend to $L$ as $t \to \infty$ through the reals, so the nonlattice conclusion $F(t) \sim L e^{-\theta t}$ fails. The obstruction is the offset $h/2$ below the lattice points, where the scaled tail tends to $e^{-\theta h/2} C \ne C$.
