# Exponential tilting of a renewal equation

Let $G$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with $\int e^{\theta y}\, dG(y) = 1$, and suppose $Z(t) = z(t) + \int Z(t - y)\, dG(y)$ for every real $t$. Then for every $t$, $e^{\theta t} Z(t) = e^{\theta t} z(t) + \int e^{\theta (t - y)} Z(t - y)\, dG^\theta(y)$, where $G^\theta$ is the tilted probability measure $e^{\theta y} dG(y)$: the scaled function solves a proper renewal equation driven by $G^\theta$.
