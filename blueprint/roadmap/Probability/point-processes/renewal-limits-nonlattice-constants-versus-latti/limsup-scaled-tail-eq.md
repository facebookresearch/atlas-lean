# Lattice limsup

Let $h > 0$, $\theta \ge 0$, and let $F \ge 0$ be a lattice step function, $F(t) = F(h \lceil t/h\rceil)$ for all $t$, such that $e^{\theta k h} F(k h) \to C$ as $k \to \infty$ through the natural numbers. Then $\limsup_{t \to \infty} e^{\theta t} F(t) = C$: the lattice constant is the upper envelope of the scaled tail.
