# Arrival counts of exponential interarrivals are Poisson

Let $r > 0$ and let $T_0, T_1, \dots$ be independent random variables, each with the exponential
law of rate $r$, with [counting process](count.md) $N$. Then for every $t \ge 0$ the number of
arrivals $N_t$ in $[0, t]$ has the Poisson law of mean $rt$.

## Sources

- S. Karlin and H. M. Taylor, *A First Course in Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., Academic Press,
  1975, Chapter 4, Section 2, Theorem 2.1.
