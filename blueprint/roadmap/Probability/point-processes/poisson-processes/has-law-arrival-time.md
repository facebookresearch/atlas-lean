# Arrival times of exponential interarrivals are Erlang

Let $r > 0$ and let $T_0, T_1, \dots$ be independent random variables, each with the exponential
law of rate $r$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the arrival time
$S_{n+1} = T_0 + \dots + T_n$ has the Gamma (Erlang) law with shape $n + 1$ and rate $r$.

## Sources

- S. Karlin and H. M. Taylor, *A First Course in Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., Academic Press,
  1975, Chapter 4, Section 2.
