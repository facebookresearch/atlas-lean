# Poisson process

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and $r \ge 0$. A process $N = (N_t)_{t \ge 0}$ of
$\mathbb{N}$-valued functions on $\Omega$ is a Poisson process of rate $r$ if: $N_0 = 0$ almost
surely; almost every path $t \mapsto N_t$ is nondecreasing; the increments over consecutive
intervals are independent; and for $0 \le s \le t$ the increment $N_t - N_s$ has the Poisson law of
mean $r(t - s)$.
