# Thinning of a Poisson process

For a counting process $N$ and marks $B_0, B_1, \dots \in \{\text{true}, \text{false}\}$, the
thinning of $N$ by $B$ at time $t$ is the number of indices $i < N_t$ with $B_i = \text{true}$:
the number of points of $N$ in $[0, t]$ that are kept when the $i$-th point (in time order,
counting from $0$) is kept if and only if $B_i$ is true.
