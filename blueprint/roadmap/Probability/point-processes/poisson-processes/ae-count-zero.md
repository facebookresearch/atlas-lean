# Counting process starts at zero almost surely

Let $r > 0$ and let $T_0, T_1, \dots$ be random variables on $(\Omega, P)$, each with the
exponential law of rate $r$. Then the [counting process](count.md) of the interarrival times
$T_i$ satisfies $N_0 = 0$ almost surely, since every interarrival time is positive almost surely.
