# Exponential interarrivals give a Poisson process

Let $r > 0$ and let $T_0, T_1, \dots$ be independent random variables on $(\Omega, P)$, each with
the exponential law of rate $r$. Then their [counting process](count.md) is a [Poisson
process](is-poisson-process.md) of rate $r$.

## Sources

- J. F. C. Kingman, *Poisson Processes*, Oxford University Press, 1993, Chapter 4.
- S. Karlin and H. M. Taylor, *A First Course in Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., Academic Press,
  1975, Chapter 4.
