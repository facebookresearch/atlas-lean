# Superposition

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space, and let $N^1$ and $N^2$ be [Poisson
processes](is-poisson-process.md) on $\Omega$ of rates $r_1, r_2 \ge 0$ that are independent as
random paths $\omega \mapsto (N^k_t(\omega))_{t \ge 0}$. Then $N^1 + N^2$ is a Poisson process of
rate $r_1 + r_2$.

## Sources

- J. F. C. Kingman, *Poisson Processes*, Oxford University Press, 1993, Section 2.2.
