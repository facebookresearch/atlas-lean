# AR(1) step with equal variances, a numerical example

On a probability space, let $Y$ and $\varepsilon$ be independent square-integrable random variables with $\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon) = 3$, and let $X = \tfrac12 Y + \varepsilon$ with $\operatorname{Var}(Y) = \operatorname{Var}(X)$ (equal variances, not equality of laws). Then $\operatorname{Var}(X) = 4$.
