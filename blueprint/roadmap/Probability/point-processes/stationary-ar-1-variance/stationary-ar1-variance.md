# Variance of an AR(1) step with equal input and output variances

On a probability space, let $Y$ and $\varepsilon$ be independent square-integrable random variables, $\rho^2 \ne 1$, and $X = \rho Y + \varepsilon$ with $\operatorname{Var}(Y) = \operatorname{Var}(X)$ (equal variances, which stationarity of the AR(1) step implies but which do not make the laws of $X$ and $Y$ equal). Then $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon)/(1 - \rho^2)$.
