# Variance of a truncated moving-average sum

Let $(\varepsilon_t)_{t \in \mathbb{Z}}$ be independent square-integrable random variables with a common variance $\sigma^2 = \operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_0)$, and let $\rho^2 \ne 1$. For every $t$ and $N$, the truncated moving-average sum $S_N(t) = \sum_{k<N} \rho^k \varepsilon_{t-k}$ has variance $\sigma^2 (1 - \rho^{2N})/(1 - \rho^2)$.
