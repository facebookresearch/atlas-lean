# Variance of the difference of two moving-average truncations

Let $(\varepsilon_t)_{t \in \mathbb{Z}}$ be independent square-integrable random variables with a common variance $\sigma^2 = \operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_0)$, let $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $S_N(t) = \sum_{k<N} \rho^k \varepsilon_{t-k}$ be the truncated moving-average sum. For $N \le M$ and every $t$, $\operatorname{Var}(S_M(t) - S_N(t)) = \sigma^2 \sum_{k=N}^{M-1} \rho^{2k}$.
