# Partial sums as renewal epochs

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be real random variables on $\Omega$. Define

$$S_n(\omega) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} X_i(\omega), \qquad n \in \mathbb{N},$$

so $S_0 = 0$. The increments are arbitrary real numbers. When they are positive, as for the recurrence times of a renewal process, the partial sums increase strictly and $S_n$ are the renewal epochs of the zero-delay renewal process with recurrence times $X_i$.
