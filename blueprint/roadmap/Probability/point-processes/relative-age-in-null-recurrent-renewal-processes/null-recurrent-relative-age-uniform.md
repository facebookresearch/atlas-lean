# Relative age in null recurrent renewal processes

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent, identically distributed, strictly positive recurrence times that are non-lattice ($P(X_0 \in d\mathbb Z) < 1$ for every $d > 0$) with $\mathbb E X_0 = \infty$, and let $S_k = X_0 + \dots + X_{k-1}$. At time $t$, with $S_k \le t < S_{k+1}$, the relative age is $U_t = (t - S_k)/X_k$. The question (Thorisson's Problem 1.1) asks whether always $P(U_t \le u) \to u$ as $t \to \infty$ for every $u \in [0, 1]$, i.e. $U_t$ converges in distribution to the uniform law on $[0, 1]$.
