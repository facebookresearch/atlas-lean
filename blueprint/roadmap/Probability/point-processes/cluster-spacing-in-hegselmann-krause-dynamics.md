# Cluster spacing in Hegselmann–Krause dynamics

This node records a research direction: explain why bounded-confidence (Hegselmann–Krause) averaging dynamics started from many agents tend to end in clusters separated by about twice the confidence radius (the "2R" phenomenon). The informal observation is not a precise statement.

## What would settle it

Fix the exact dynamics, the distribution of the initial positions, the domain and boundary convention, and the mode of convergence. Then prove a quantitative asymptotic statement about the spacing of the limiting clusters as the density of agents grows. A statement in expectation, an almost-sure statement and a statement about every gap are different targets.

## Status

No cited source poses this question in the form above; it is a research direction motivated by the cited results. This page does not certify the status or novelty of any particular formulation.

Dey, Etesami and Gopalan state that it was introduced in 2007 without a precise statement, treats the dynamics on stationary point processes, proves a result in expectation, and states a stronger result supported by simulation.

This page records a research direction and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
