# Point counts of a Poisson point process are Poisson

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $E$ and let $\eta$ be distributed as the
[Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) with intensity $\mu$. For every measurable
$B \subseteq E$, the number of points $\eta(B)$ has the Poisson law of mean $\mu(B)$.
