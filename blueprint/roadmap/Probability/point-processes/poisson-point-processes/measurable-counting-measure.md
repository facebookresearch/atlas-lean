# Measurability of the counting measure in the points

Let $E$ be a measurable space and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The map
$(x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}) \mapsto \sum_{i < n} \delta_{x_i}$ from $E^n$ to the space of measures on
$E$ (with the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the evaluations $\nu \mapsto \nu(s)$, $s$ measurable)
is measurable.
