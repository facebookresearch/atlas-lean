# Restriction theorem

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $E$, let $\eta$ be distributed as the
[Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) with intensity $\mu$, and let
$(A_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a finite family of pairwise disjoint measurable subsets of $E$. Then the
restrictions $(\eta|_{A_i})_{i \in \iota}$ are independent, and $\eta|_{A_i}$ is distributed as
the Poisson point process with intensity $\mu|_{A_i}$: the joint law of the restrictions is the
product of these laws.

## Sources

- G. Last and M. Penrose, *Lectures on the Poisson Process*, IMS Textbooks, Cambridge University
  Press, 2017, Theorem 5.2.
