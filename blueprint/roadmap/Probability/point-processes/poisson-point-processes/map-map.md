# Mapping theorem

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $E$ and let $T : E \to F$ be a measurable map.
If $\eta$ is distributed as the [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) with intensity
$\mu$, then the image measure $\eta \circ T^{-1}$ is distributed as the Poisson point process with
intensity $\mu \circ T^{-1}$.

## Sources

- G. Last and M. Penrose, *Lectures on the Poisson Process*, IMS Textbooks, Cambridge University
  Press, 2017, Theorem 5.1.
