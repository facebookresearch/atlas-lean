# Lifetime of a linear damage path

Let $d_0, r, D : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $\omega \in \Omega$ with $r(\omega) > 0$ and $d_0(\omega) < D(\omega)$. The damage lifetime $\tau(\omega) \in [0, \infty]$ of the linear damage path $t \mapsto d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t$ against the threshold $D(\omega)$ (the first time $t \ge 0$ at which the damage reaches the threshold, equality included) is

$$\tau(\omega) = \frac{D(\omega) - d_0(\omega)}{r(\omega)},$$

a finite value, viewed in the extended nonnegative reals.
