# Failure of a linear damage path by a fixed time

Let $d_0, r, D : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $\omega \in \Omega$ with $r(\omega) \ge 0$, and $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$. The damage lifetime $\tau(\omega) \in [0, \infty]$ of the linear damage path $s \mapsto d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, s$ against the threshold $D(\omega)$ satisfies

$$\tau(\omega) \le t \iff D(\omega) \le d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t .$$
