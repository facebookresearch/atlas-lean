# Linear damage path

Let $\Omega$ be a type of scenarios and let $d_0, r : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. The linear damage path is the function $\Omega \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \to \mathbb{R}$ that assigns to a scenario $\omega$ and a time $t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ the damage

$$d(\omega, t) = d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t .$$

No sign condition is imposed on $d_0$ or $r$.
