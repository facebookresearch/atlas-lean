# Hazard measure of the unit exponential law

For a measure $F$ on $\mathbb R$, its hazard measure is

$$\Lambda_F(B) = \int_B \frac{dF(s)}{F([s, \infty))},$$

the measure with density $s \mapsto F([s, \infty))^{-1} \in [0, \infty]$ with respect to $F$, where $1/0 = \infty$.

In the exponential censoring model, $\Lambda_0$ is the hazard measure of the exponential distribution $\mu_0$ with rate $1$:

$$\Lambda_0(B) = \int_B \frac{d\mu_0(s)}{\mu_0([s, \infty))} \qquad (B \subseteq \mathbb R \text{ Borel}).$$
