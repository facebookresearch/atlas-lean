# Uniform measure on the two-coin sample space

On the sample space $\Omega_0 = \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^2$ of the two-coin censoring model (censoring coin $c$, death coin $d$), the measure $\mu_0$ is one quarter of the counting measure:

$$\mu_0(A) = \frac{\# A}{4} \qquad (A \subseteq \Omega_0).$$

It gives mass $1/4$ to each of the four outcomes $(c, d)$: it is the uniform distribution on $\Omega_0$.
