# Volterra identity for the unit exponential survival function and Lebesgue measure

Let $S(s) = e^{-s}$ for $s \in \mathbb R$. For all real $a \le b$,

$$\frac{1}{S(b)} - \frac{1}{S(a)} = \int_{(a, b]} \frac{ds}{S(s)},$$

the integral being with respect to Lebesgue measure; that is, $e^b - e^a = \int_{(a, b]} e^s\, ds$.

## Sources

- R. D. Gill and S. Johansen, A survey of product-integration with a view toward application in survival analysis, Ann. Statist. 18 (1990), 1501–1555.
