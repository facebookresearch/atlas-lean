# Mean of the remainder in the exponential model

In the model with failure time $T \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, deterministic censoring at time $1$, survival function $S(t) = e^{-t}$ and its hazard measure $\Lambda$, let $R(t)$ be the vanishing-risk remainder of the one-subject Kaplan–Meier estimator. For $t \ge 1$, $\mathbb{E}[R(t)] = e^{t-1} - 1$.
