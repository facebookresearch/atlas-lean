# Survival function of the exponential censoring model

The survival function of the exponential censoring model is the function $S : \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$,

$$S(s) = e^{-s} \qquad (s \in \mathbb R).$$

The tail probability $\mu_0((s, \infty))$ of the exponential law $\mu_0$ with rate $1$ is computed on [its own page](measure-real-ioi.md).
