# The hazard measure of the unit exponential law is Lebesgue measure on the positive half-line

For a measure $F$ on $\mathbb R$, its hazard measure is

$$\Lambda_F(B) = \int_B \frac{dF(s)}{F([s, \infty))},$$

the measure with density $s \mapsto F([s, \infty))^{-1} \in [0, \infty]$ with respect to $F$, where $1/0 = \infty$.

Let $\mu_0$ be the exponential distribution with rate $1$ on $\mathbb R$. Then the restriction of its hazard measure to $(0, \infty)$ is Lebesgue measure $\lambda$ on $(0, \infty)$:

$$\Lambda_{\mu_0}|_{(0, \infty)} = \lambda|_{(0, \infty)}.$$
