# Bootstrap validity with a given Kaplan–Meier limit

Fix strictly increasing grid times $t_j$ and let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent and identically distributed observations $(T, \delta)$ on a probability space with observed times almost surely on the grid, every grid time having positive mean at-risk indicator, and let $Z$ be an almost everywhere measurable random vector on a probability space whose projections $\langle Z, s\rangle$ are centered normal with the variance of the encoded observation's projection. If $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S) \Rightarrow W$, where $\hat S_n$ is the grid Kaplan–Meier estimator of the first $n$ observations and $S$ the Kaplan–Meier map at the mean encoded observation, then for almost every data sequence the conditional law of $\sqrt n(\hat S^*_n - \hat S_n)$ under resampling with replacement converges to the law of $W$.
