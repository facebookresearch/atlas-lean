# The mean bias of the one-subject Kaplan–Meier ratio is the mean remainder

On a probability space, let $T$ and $C$ be measurable and independent with $\min(T, C) > 0$ almost surely, $\Lambda$ the hazard measure of the law of $T$, and $t \ge 0$. Let $S$ be nonincreasing with $S(0) = 1$ and $S(t) > 0$, with $1/S$ integrable on $(0, t]$ against $\Lambda$ and $1/S(b) - 1/S(a) = \int_{(a,b]}S^{-1}\,d\Lambda$ for $0 \le a \le b \le t$. Then $\mathbb{E}[\hat S(t)/S(t) - 1] = \mathbb{E}[R(t)]$, where $\hat S$ is the one-subject Kaplan–Meier estimator and $R$ its remainder.
