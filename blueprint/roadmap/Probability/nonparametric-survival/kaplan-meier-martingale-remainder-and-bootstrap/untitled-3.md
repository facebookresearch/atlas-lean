# Exponential law of the failure time

The law of the failure time in the exponential censoring model is the exponential distribution with rate $1$ on $\mathbb R$:

$$\mu_0(B) = \int_{B \cap [0, \infty)} e^{-x}\, dx \qquad (B \subseteq \mathbb R \text{ Borel}).$$

The sample space of the model is $(\mathbb R, \mu_0)$.
