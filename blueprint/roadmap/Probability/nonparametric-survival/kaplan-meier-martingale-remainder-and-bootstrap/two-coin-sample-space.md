# Sample space of the two-coin censoring model

The sample space of the two-coin censoring model is

$$\Omega_0 = \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\} \times \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\},$$

with all subsets measurable. A point is $\omega = (c, d)$: the first coordinate $c$ is the censoring coin ($c = \mathrm{true}$ means the subject is censored at time $1$), the second coordinate $d$ is the death coin ($d = \mathrm{true}$ means an uncensored subject dies at step $2$).
