# History filtration of the two-coin censoring model

On the sample space $\Omega_0 = \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^2$ of the two-coin censoring model, with points $\omega = (c, d)$ (censoring coin $c$, death coin $d$) and the power set as ambient $\sigma$-algebra, the filtration $(\mathcal F_n)_{n \in \mathbb N}$ is

$$\mathcal F_n = \begin{cases} \{\emptyset, \Omega_0\}, & n = 0, \\ \sigma(c) = \{\{\omega : c \in B\} : B \subseteq \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}\}, & n = 1, \\ 2^{\Omega_0}, & n \ge 2. \end{cases}$$

Nothing is known at time $0$, the censoring coin is revealed at time $1$, and everything is known from time $2$ on.
