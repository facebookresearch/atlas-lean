# The discrete Kaplan–Meier remainder is genuinely nonzero

In an explicit two-step model with a censoring coin revealed at time 1 and a death coin at time 2 (fair and independent), hazard increments $0$ then $1/2$, and a single subject at risk at step 1 and at step 2 only if not censored, Aalen's model and all hypotheses of Gill's Kaplan–Meier martingale hold. Then $\mathbb{E}[R_2 \mid \mathcal F_1]$ equals the censoring indicator and is not almost surely $0$.
