# The time-one σ-algebra of the two-coin model is not trivial

In the two-coin censoring model, with sample space $\Omega_0 = \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^2$ of points $\omega = (c, d)$ and the filtration with $\mathcal F_1 = \sigma(c)$ (the preimages of subsets of $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$ under $\omega \mapsto c$), the $\sigma$-algebra at time $1$ is not the trivial $\sigma$-algebra:

$$\mathcal F_1 \ne \{\emptyset, \Omega_0\}.$$

See the [filtration](filtration.md) of the model.
