# Mean bias of Kaplan–Meier in an explicit model

In an explicit two-step model with a censoring coin revealed at time 1 and a death coin at time 2 (fair and independent), hazard increments $0$ then $1/2$, and a single subject at risk at step 1 and at step 2 only if not censored, Aalen's model and all hypotheses of Gill's Kaplan–Meier martingale hold. Then $\mathbb{E}[\hat S_2/S_2] - 1 = \mathbb{E}[R_2] = 1/2$.
