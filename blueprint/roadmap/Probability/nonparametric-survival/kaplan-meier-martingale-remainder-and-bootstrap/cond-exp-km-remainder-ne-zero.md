# The continuous-time Kaplan–Meier remainder is genuinely nonzero

In the model with failure time $T \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, deterministic censoring at time $1$, survival function $S(t) = e^{-t}$ and its hazard measure $\Lambda$, let $R(t)$ be the vanishing-risk remainder of the one-subject Kaplan–Meier estimator. For $t > 1$, $\mathbb{E}[R(t) \mid \mathcal F_1] = \mathbf 1\{T > 1\}(e^t - e)$ is not almost surely $0$.
