# The continuous-time Kaplan–Meier remainder is genuinely nonzero

In the model with failure time $T \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, deterministic censoring at time $1$, survival function $S(t) = e^{-t}$ and its hazard measure $\Lambda$, let $R(t)$ be the vanishing-risk remainder of the one-subject Kaplan–Meier estimator. Let $\mathcal F_1$ be the observed history at time $1$: the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the failure and censoring counting processes $N(u)$ and $N^c(u)$ for $u \le 1$. For $t > 1$, $\mathbb{E}[R(t) \mid \mathcal F_1] = \mathbf 1\{T > 1\}(e^t - e)$ is not almost surely $0$.
