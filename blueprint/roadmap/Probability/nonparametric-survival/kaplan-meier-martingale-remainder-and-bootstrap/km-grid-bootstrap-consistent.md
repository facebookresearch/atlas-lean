# Bootstrap consistency for grid Kaplan–Meier

Fix strictly increasing grid times $t_j$ and encode an observation $(T, \delta)$ by the indicators of a death at $t_j$ and of being at risk at $t_j$; for a vector $\theta$ of such coordinates let $\mathrm{KM}(\theta)_j = \prod_{l\le j}(1 - \theta(\text{death}, l)/\theta(\text{risk}, l))$. Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent and identically distributed with observed times almost surely on the grid, $\theta$ the mean of the encoded observation with positive at-risk coordinates, and $Z$ centered Gaussian with the covariance of the encoded observation. Then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - \mathrm{KM}(\theta)) \Rightarrow D\mathrm{KM}(\theta)Z$, and for almost every data sequence the conditional law of $\sqrt n(\hat S^*_n - \hat S_n)$ converges to the same limit.
