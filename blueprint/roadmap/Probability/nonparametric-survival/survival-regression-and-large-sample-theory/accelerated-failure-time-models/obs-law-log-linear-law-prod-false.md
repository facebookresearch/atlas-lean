# Censored part of the observed law in the log-linear model

For a finite measure $\mu_0$ (the error measure), $\sigma > 0$ and an s-finite measure $\mu_C$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let $Q$ be the observed-data measure of the log-linear measure and $\mu_C$, and $S_0(z) = \mu_0(z, \infty)$. Then $Q(A \times \{0\}) = \int_AS_0((c - \eta)/\sigma)\,d\mu_C(c)$ in $[0, \infty]$ for every measurable $A$; for an error law $\mu_0$ and an independent log censoring time $\log C \sim \mu_C$, this is $P(Y \in A, \delta = 0)$.
