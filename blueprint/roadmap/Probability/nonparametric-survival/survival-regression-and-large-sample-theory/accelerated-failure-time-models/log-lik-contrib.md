# Log-likelihood contribution of a censored observation

For any real scale $\sigma$, an observation $(y, \delta)$ contributes $\log f_0(z) - \log\sigma$ to the log-likelihood if $\delta$ and $\log S_0(z)$ otherwise, with $z = (y - \eta)/\sigma$. Here $\log$ is the real logarithm extended by $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$, and division by zero gives $0$ (so $z = 0$ when $\sigma = 0$).
