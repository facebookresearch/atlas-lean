# Mass below a point under acceleration

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. Then $\mu_\eta(-\infty, t) = \mu_0(-\infty, e^{-\eta}t)$ in $[0, \infty]$ for every $t$; for $T_0 \sim \mu_0$ and $T = e^\eta T_0$ this reads $P(T < t) = P(T_0 < e^{-\eta}t)$.
