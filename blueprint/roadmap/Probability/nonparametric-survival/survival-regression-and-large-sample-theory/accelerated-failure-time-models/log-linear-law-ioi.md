# Survival of the log-linear law

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and $\sigma > 0$, the log-linear measure $\nu$, the image of $\mu_0$ under $e \mapsto \eta + \sigma e$, satisfies $\nu(y, \infty) = \mu_0((y - \eta)/\sigma, \infty)$ in $[0, \infty]$; for an error $\varepsilon \sim \mu_0$ and $\log T = \eta + \sigma\varepsilon$ this reads $P(\log T > y) = P(\varepsilon > (y - \eta)/\sigma)$.
