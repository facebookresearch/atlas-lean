# Survival of the observed time under independent censoring

For measures $\mu_T$ and $\mu_C$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let $Q$ be the observed-data measure, the image of $\mu_T \otimes \mu_C$ under $(a, c) \mapsto (\min(a, c), \mathbf 1\{a \le c\})$. If $\mu_C$ is s-finite, then $Q((y, \infty) \times \{0, 1\}) = \mu_T(y, \infty)\,\mu_C(y, \infty)$ in $[0, \infty]$, with $0 \cdot \infty = 0$. For independent $\log T \sim \mu_T$ and $\log C \sim \mu_C$ and $(Y, \delta) = (\min(\log T, \log C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$, this reads $P(Y > y) = P(\log T > y)P(\log C > y)$.
