# Distribution function of the AFT law at a rescaled point

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. For every $q$, the real numbers $\mu_\eta(-\infty, e^\eta q]$ and $\mu_0(-\infty, q]$ are equal, an infinite mass being read as $0$; for a probability law $\mu_0$ this is $F(e^\eta q \mid \eta) = F_0(q)$.
