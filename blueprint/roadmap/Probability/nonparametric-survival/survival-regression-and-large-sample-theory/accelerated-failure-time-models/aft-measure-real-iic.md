# Distribution-function transform of an accelerated failure time law

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. Then for every $t$ the real numbers $\mu_\eta(-\infty, t]$ and $\mu_0(-\infty, e^{-\eta}t]$ are equal, an infinite mass being read as $0$; for a probability law $\mu_0$ this is $F(t \mid \eta) = F_0(e^{-\eta}t)$.
