# When an accelerated failure time law has proportional hazards

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. The measure $\mu_\eta$ has proportional hazards relative to $\mu_0$ with ratio $c$ if and only if $\mu_0$ is a probability law on $[0, \infty)$, $c > 0$, and $S_0(e^{-\eta}t) = S_0(t)^c$ for every $t \ge 0$.
