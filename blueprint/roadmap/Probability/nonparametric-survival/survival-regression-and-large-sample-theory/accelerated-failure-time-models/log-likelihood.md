# Logarithm of the censored likelihood

In the censored log-linear accelerated failure time model, subject $i$ has log follow-up time $y_i$, event indicator $\delta_i$ and covariates $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^p$; with real functions $f_0, f_0', S_0 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (in the model, the error density, its derivative and the error survival function, though nothing here requires that), scale $\sigma$ and standardized residuals $z_i = (y_i - \langle\beta, x_i\rangle)/\sigma$, the log-likelihood is $\ell(\beta, \sigma) = \sum_i\delta_i(\log f_0(z_i) - \log\sigma) + (1 - \delta_i)\log S_0(z_i)$, and $r(z, \delta)$ is $-f_0'(z)/f_0(z)$ for an event and $f_0(z)/S_0(z)$ for a censored observation. If $\sigma > 0$ and no factor of the likelihood vanishes, its logarithm is $\ell(\beta, \sigma)$.
