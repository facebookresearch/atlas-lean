# Likelihood contribution of a censored observation

For error density $f_0$, error survival $S_0$, linear predictor $\eta$ and any real scale $\sigma$, an observation $(y, \delta)$ contributes $f_0(z)/\sigma$ if $\delta$ (an observed failure) and $S_0(z)$ otherwise, with $z = (y - \eta)/\sigma$. Division by zero gives $0$, so for $\sigma = 0$ one has $z = 0$ and the failure contribution is $0$.
