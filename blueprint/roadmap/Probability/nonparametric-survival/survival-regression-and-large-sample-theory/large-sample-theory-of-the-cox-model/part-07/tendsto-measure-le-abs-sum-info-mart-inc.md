# Pointwise convergence of the Cox information martingale

In the discrete-time Cox counting-process model on a probability space, for each $n$ there are $n$ subjects with observed grid times $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariates $|z_i| \le B$ (with $B \ge 0$), observed through a filtration $(\mathcal F_{n,k})_k$ that may depend on $n$, the covariates being $\mathcal F_{n,0}$-measurable; step $k$ is the grid time $k + 1$, subject $i$ is at risk there when $k + 1 \le t_i$, with indicator $Y_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k}$-measurable, and has an event there when $t_i = k + 1$ and $\delta_i$, with indicator $\Delta N_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k+1}$-measurable; the intensity is $\mathbb{E}[\Delta N_i(k) \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}] = Y_i(k)e^{\beta_0z_i}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}$ almost surely, with hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \ge 0$ that may depend on $n$; and almost surely at most one event occurs per step. Write $S^{(j)}(b, k) = \sum_lY_l(k)z_l^je^{bz_l}$ and $V(b, k) = S^{(2)}/S^{(0)} - (S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})^2$. Let $M_{n,K}(b) = \frac1n\sum_{k<K}V(b, k)\big(\Delta\bar N(k) - \Delta\alpha_{n,k}S^{(0)}(\beta_0, k)\big)$, with $\Delta\bar N(k) = \sum_i\Delta N_i(k)$ and division by $n = 0$ giving $0$, be the sum of the normalized information martingale increments before the horizon $K$: the information increments $V(b, k)\Delta\bar N(k)$ summed over $k < K$, minus the information compensator at $K$, divided by $n$. For every deterministic sequence of horizons $K_n$, every $b$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, $P(|M_{n,K_n}(b)| \ge \varepsilon) \to 0$.
