# Scalar time-dependent Cox information

For a scalar covariate process $z_l(t)$, the time-dependent Cox information is $I(b) = \sum_{i:\delta_i}\mathrm{Var}_i(b)$, where $\mathrm{Var}_i(b)$ is the variance of the covariate values $z_l(T_i)$, evaluated at the event time $T_i$, over the risk set $R(T_i) = \{l : T_l \ge T_i\}$ (subjects tied with $i$ included), weighted by $e^{bz_l(T_i)}$.
