# Deterministic core of consistency for the Cox estimator

If $\varepsilon > 0$, the Cox information exceeds $m$ on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$ and $|U(\beta_0)| < \varepsilon m$, then the log partial likelihood has a maximizer, and any $\hat b$ that maximizes it whenever a maximizer exists is a root of the score within $\varepsilon$ of $\beta_0$.
