# Existence of the Cox estimator with probability tending to one

Let $\ell_n$ be the scalar Cox log partial likelihood of the $n$ subjects in row $n$, with score $U_n$ and observed information $I_n$. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. Then the outer probability that the log partial likelihood has no maximizer tends to $0$.
