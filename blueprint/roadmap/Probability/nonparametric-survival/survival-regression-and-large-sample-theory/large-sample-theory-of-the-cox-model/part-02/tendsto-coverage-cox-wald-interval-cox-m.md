# Coverage of the Cox Wald interval under Andersen–Gill conditions

Let $\ell_n$ be the scalar Cox log partial likelihood of the $n$ subjects in row $n$, with score $U_n$ and observed information $I_n$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. If $Z \sim N(0, v)$, $\hat\beta_n$ is almost everywhere measurable, and the statistic $\sqrt{n/I_n(\hat\beta_n)}$ ($\sqrt n$ times the standard error $1/\sqrt{I_n(\hat\beta_n)}$) is almost everywhere measurable, then for every $c$, $\hat\beta_n \pm c/\sqrt{I_n(\hat\beta_n)}$ (with $\sqrt x = 0$ for $x \le 0$ and division by zero read as $0$) covers $\beta_0$ with probability tending to $N(0, 1)([-c, c])$.
