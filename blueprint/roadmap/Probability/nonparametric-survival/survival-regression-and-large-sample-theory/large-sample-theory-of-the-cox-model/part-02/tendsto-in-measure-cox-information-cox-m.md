# Consistency of the Cox information at the estimator

Let $\ell_n$ be the scalar Cox log partial likelihood of the $n$ subjects in row $n$, with score $U_n$ and observed information $I_n$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. Then $I_n(\hat\beta_n)/n \to v$ in probability.
