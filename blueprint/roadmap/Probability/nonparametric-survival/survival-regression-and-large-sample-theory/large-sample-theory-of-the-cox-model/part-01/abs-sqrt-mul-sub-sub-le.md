# Deterministic core of asymptotic normality

Let $\ell$ be a concave criterion on $\mathbb{R}$ with score $U = \ell'$ and observed information $I = -U' \ge 0$. Let $n > 0$, $v > 0$ and $\eta \le v/2$. If $U(\hat b) = 0$, $|\hat b - \beta_0| \le \varepsilon$ and $|I(b)/n - v| < \eta$ on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$, then $\big|\sqrt n(\hat b - \beta_0) - \frac{U(\beta_0)/\sqrt n}{v}\big| \le \frac{|U(\beta_0)/\sqrt n|\cdot2\eta}{v^2}$.
