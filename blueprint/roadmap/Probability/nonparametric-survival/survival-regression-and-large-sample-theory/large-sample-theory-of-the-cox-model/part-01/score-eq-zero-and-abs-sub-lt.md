# Deterministic core of consistency

Let $\ell$ be a concave criterion on $\mathbb{R}$ with score $U = \ell'$ and observed information $I = -U' \ge 0$. If $\varepsilon > 0$, $I > m$ on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$ and $|U(\beta_0)| < \varepsilon m$, then $\ell$ has a maximizer, and any $\hat b$ that maximizes $\ell$ whenever a maximizer exists satisfies $U(\hat b) = 0$ and $|\hat b - \beta_0| < \varepsilon$.
