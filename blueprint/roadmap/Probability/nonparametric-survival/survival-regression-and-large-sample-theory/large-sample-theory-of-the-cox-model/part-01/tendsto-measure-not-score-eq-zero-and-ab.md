# The outer probability that the maximizer is not a root near the truth tends to zero

For each $n$ and $\omega$, let $\ell_n$ be a concave criterion with score $U_n$ and information $I_n \ge 0$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$, the outer probability that $\hat\beta_n$ is not a root of $U_n$ with $|\hat\beta_n - \beta_0| < \varepsilon$ tends to $0$.
