# Existence of a maximizer with probability tending to one

For each $n$ and $\omega$, let $\ell_n$ be a concave criterion with score $U_n$ and information $I_n \ge 0$. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. Then the outer probability that $\ell_n$ has no maximizer tends to $0$.
