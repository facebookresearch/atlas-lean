# Signs of the score near a point

Let $\ell$ be a concave criterion on $\mathbb{R}$ with score $U = \ell'$ and observed information $I = -U' \ge 0$. If $\varepsilon > 0$, $I > m$ on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$ and $|U(\beta_0)| < \varepsilon m$, then $U(\beta_0 + \varepsilon) < 0 < U(\beta_0 - \varepsilon)$.
