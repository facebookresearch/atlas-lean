# Bootstrap validity for the grid Cox estimator with fixed data

Fix distinct grid times $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and distinct covariate levels $c_1, \dots, c_q$, and encode an observation $(T, d, z)$ by the indicators of an observed event at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$ and of being at risk at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$. For a vector $\mu$ of such coordinates let $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$, $S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ac_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$ and $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)(c_a - S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})$, a quotient with $S^{(0)} = 0$ being $0$. Let fixed data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ have every observed time on the grid and every covariate among the levels, let $\hat\beta_n$ be the Cox estimator of $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$, and let $\hat\beta^*_n$ be the same estimator recomputed on $n$ whole observation triples drawn independently and with replacement from the empirical distribution of $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$. For a direction $s$ write $a_i = \langle \text{encoding of } x_i, s\rangle$ and $\bar a_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}a_i$. Let $Z$ be an almost everywhere measurable random vector on a probability space with projections $\langle Z, s\rangle \sim N(0, v(s))$. Suppose that in every direction $s$ the empirical variance $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}(a_i - \bar a_n)^2$ converges to $v(s)$ and, separately, the centered observations satisfy the Lindeberg condition: for every $\varepsilon > 0$, $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}(a_i - \bar a_n)^2\mathbf 1\{|a_i - \bar a_n| > \varepsilon\sqrt n\} \to 0$. Suppose also that the encoded means converge to $\theta$, every grid time has positive at-risk mass under $\theta$, $\Psi(\theta, \beta_0) = 0$ and the limiting information is nonzero. Then under the resampling law given the data, $\sqrt n(\hat\beta^*_n - \hat\beta_n)$ converges in distribution to $LZ$, with $L$ the derivative of the Cox root.
