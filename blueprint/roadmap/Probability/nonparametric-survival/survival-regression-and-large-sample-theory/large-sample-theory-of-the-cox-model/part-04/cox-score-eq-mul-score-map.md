# The Cox score is n times the score map at the sample mean

Fix distinct grid times $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and distinct covariate levels $c_1, \dots, c_q$, and encode an observation $(T, d, z)$ by the indicators of an observed event at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$ and of being at risk at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$. For a vector $\mu$ of such coordinates let $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$, $S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ac_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$ and $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)(c_a - S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})$, a quotient with $S^{(0)} = 0$ being $0$. If the first $n$ observed times lie among the $t_j$ and the covariates among the $c_a$ (both injective), then $U_n(b) = n\Psi(\bar x_n, b)$ for every $b$, with $\bar x_n$ the mean of the encoded observations.
