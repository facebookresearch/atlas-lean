# Implicit-function chart of the Cox root

Fix grid times $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and covariate levels $c_1, \dots, c_q$, and encode an observation $(T, d, z)$ by the indicators of an observed event at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$ and of being at risk at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$. For a vector $\mu$ of such coordinates let $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$, $S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ac_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$ and $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)(c_a - S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})$, a quotient with $S^{(0)} = 0$ being $0$. If $\Psi(\theta, \beta_0) = 0$, every $S^{(0)}(\theta, \beta_0, j) \ne 0$ and $v = -\partial_b\Psi(\theta, \beta_0) \ne 0$, there are a measurable $g$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ with $g(\theta) = \beta_0$, $g$ strictly differentiable at $\theta$ with derivative $v^{-1}\partial_\mu\Psi(\theta, \beta_0)$, and for $\mu$ within $\varepsilon$ of $\theta$, $g(\mu)$ is the only root of $\Psi(\mu, \cdot)$ within $\varepsilon$ of $g(\mu)$.
