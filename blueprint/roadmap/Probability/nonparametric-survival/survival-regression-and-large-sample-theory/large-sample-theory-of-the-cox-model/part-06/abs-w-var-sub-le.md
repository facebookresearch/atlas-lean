# The weighted variance is Lipschitz in the coefficient

For nonnegative weights $Y_l$ and covariates $z_l$, write $S^{(j)}(b) = \sum_lY_lz_l^je^{bz_l}$, $\bar z(b) = S^{(1)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b)$ and $V(b) = S^{(2)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b) - \bar z(b)^2$. Division by zero gives $0$ ($x/0 = 0$), so $\bar z$ and $V$ are $0$ when every weight is zero. If $|z_l| \le B$ with $B \ge 0$, then $|V(b) - V(b')| \le 6B^3|b - b'|$.
