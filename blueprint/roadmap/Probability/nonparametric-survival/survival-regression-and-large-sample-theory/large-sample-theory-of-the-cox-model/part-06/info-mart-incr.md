# Increment of the Cox information martingale

The normalized information martingale increment at coefficient $b$ is $\frac{V(b, k)}n\big(\Delta\bar N(k) - \Delta\alpha_kS^{(0)}(\beta_0, k)\big)$, where $V(b, k)$ is the weighted risk-set variance (equal to $0$ when the risk set at step $k$ is empty) and division by $n = 0$ gives $0$.
