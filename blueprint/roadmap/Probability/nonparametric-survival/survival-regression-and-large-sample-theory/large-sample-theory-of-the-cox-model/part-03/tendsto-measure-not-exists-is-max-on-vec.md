# Existence of the vector Cox estimator with probability tending to one

For each $n$ let survival data with covariate vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$ be random, with Cox score vector $U_n$, observed information matrix $I_n$. Assume the vector Andersen–Gill conditions: $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$; for some $r > 0$ and every $\eta > 0$, the outer probability that $|I_n(b)_{jk}/n - \Sigma(b)_{jk}| \ge \eta$ for some $b$ with $\|b - \beta_0\|_\infty \le r$ and some entry $(j, k)$ tends to $0$; $\Sigma$ continuous at $\beta_0$; and $\Sigma(\beta_0)$ positive definite. Then the outer probability that the log partial likelihood has no maximizer tends to $0$.
