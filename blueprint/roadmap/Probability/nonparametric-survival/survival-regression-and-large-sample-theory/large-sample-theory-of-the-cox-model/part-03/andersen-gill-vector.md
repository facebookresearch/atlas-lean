# Vector Andersen–Gill conditions

For random survival data with covariate vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$, the vector Andersen–Gill conditions at $\beta_0$ with limiting information $\Sigma$ and radius $r > 0$ are: $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$; $I_n(b)/n \to \Sigma(b)$ entrywise, uniformly on the closed sup-norm ball $\|b - \beta_0\|_\infty \le r$, in probability, meaning that for every $\eta > 0$ the outer probability that $|I_n(b)_{jk}/n - \Sigma(b)_{jk}| \ge \eta$ for some $b$ in that ball and some entry $(j, k)$ tends to $0$; every entry of $\Sigma$ is continuous at $\beta_0$; and $\Sigma(\beta_0)$ is positive definite.
