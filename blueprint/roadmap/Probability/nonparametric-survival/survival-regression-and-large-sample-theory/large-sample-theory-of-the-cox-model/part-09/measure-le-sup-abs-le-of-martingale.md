# Lenglart’s inequality for a martingale and its predictable variation

On a filtered probability space indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, let $M$ be a square-integrable martingale with $M_0 = 0$ almost surely, and let $\langle M\rangle_K = \sum_{j<K}\mathbb{E}[(M_{j+1} - M_j)^2 \mid \mathcal F_j]$. Then for every $K$ and $\varepsilon, \eta > 0$, $P(\max_{k\le K}|M_k| \ge \varepsilon) \le \eta/\varepsilon^2 + P(\langle M\rangle_K \ge \eta)$.
