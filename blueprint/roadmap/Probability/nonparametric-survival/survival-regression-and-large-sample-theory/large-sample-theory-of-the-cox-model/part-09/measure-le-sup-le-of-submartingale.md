# Lenglart’s inequality for a Doob compensator

On a filtered probability space indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, let $X$ be adapted and integrable and $A$ predictable ($A_{k+1}$ is $\mathcal F_k$-measurable) and integrable, such that $A - X$ is a submartingale, and suppose that almost surely $X_k \ge 0$ for every $k$, $X_0 = 0$, $A$ is nondecreasing and $A_0 = 0$. Then for $\varepsilon, \eta > 0$, $P(\max_{k\le K}X_k \ge \varepsilon) \le \mathbb{E}[A_K \wedge \eta]/\varepsilon + P(A_K \ge \eta)$.
