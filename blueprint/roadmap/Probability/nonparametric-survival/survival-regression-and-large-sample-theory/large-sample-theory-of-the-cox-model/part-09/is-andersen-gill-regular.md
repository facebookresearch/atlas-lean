# Andersen–Gill regularity on a grid

Let $P$ be a measure and, for each $n$, let $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and $z_i \in \mathbb{R}$ be random grid times and covariates of $n$ subjects, with $S^{(j)}(b, k) = \sum_l\mathbf 1\{k + 1 \le t_l\}z_l^je^{bz_l}$. Fix horizons $K_n$, real hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,k}$, deterministic functions $s_n(j, k, b)$, constants $\kappa$, $\Lambda$ and a function $i$. Andersen–Gill regularity of these given inputs on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ means: $r \ge 0$ and $\kappa > 0$; for $j = 0, 1, 2$ and every $\eta > 0$, $P\big(\exists k < K_n, \exists b \in [\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r] : |S^{(j)}(b, k)/n - s_n(j, k, b)| \ge \eta\big) \to 0$ (uniform convergence in $P$-measure, in probability when $P$ is a probability measure); $s_n(0, k, b) \ge \kappa$ for every $n$, $k < K_n$ and $b$ in the interval; $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \le \Lambda$ for every $n$; and the deterministic information compensators $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}v(s_n(0, k, b), s_n(1, k, b), s_n(2, k, b))s_n(0, k, \beta_0)$, with $v(x_0, x_1, x_2) = x_2/x_0 - (x_1/x_0)^2$ and division by zero equal to zero, converge to $i(b)$ uniformly on the interval.
