# The limiting Cox information is continuous

In the discrete-time Cox counting-process model on a probability space, for each $n$ there are $n$ subjects with observed grid times $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariates $|z_i| \le B$ (with $B \ge 0$), observed through a filtration $(\mathcal F_{n,k})_k$ that may depend on $n$, the covariates being $\mathcal F_{n,0}$-measurable; step $k$ is the grid time $k + 1$, subject $i$ is at risk there when $k + 1 \le t_i$, with indicator $Y_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k}$-measurable, and has an event there when $t_i = k + 1$ and $\delta_i$, with indicator $\Delta N_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k+1}$-measurable; the intensity is $\mathbb{E}[\Delta N_i(k) \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}] = Y_i(k)e^{\beta_0z_i}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}$ almost surely, with hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \ge 0$ that may depend on $n$; and almost surely at most one event occurs per step. Write $S^{(j)}(b, k) = \sum_lY_l(k)z_l^je^{bz_l}$ and $V(b, k) = S^{(2)}/S^{(0)} - (S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})^2$. Assume Andersen–Gill regularity on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ for given deterministic functions $s_n(j, k, b)$, constants $\kappa$, $\Lambda$ and a function $i$: $r \ge 0$, $S^{(j)}(b, k)/n - s_n(j, k, b) \to 0$ uniformly over $k < K_n$ and the interval in probability ($j = 0, 1, 2$), $s_n(0, k, b) \ge \kappa > 0$ for $k < K_n$ and $b$ in the interval, $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \le \Lambda$ for every $n$, and the deterministic compensators $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}v(s_n(0, k, b), s_n(1, k, b), s_n(2, k, b))s_n(0, k, \beta_0)$ converge to $i(b)$ uniformly on the interval, where $v(x_0, x_1, x_2) = x_2/x_0 - (x_1/x_0)^2$ with division by zero equal to zero. If $r > 0$, then $i$ is continuous at $\beta_0$.
