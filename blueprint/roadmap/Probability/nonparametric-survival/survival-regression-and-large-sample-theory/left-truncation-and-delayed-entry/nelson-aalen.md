# Delayed-entry Nelson–Aalen estimate

Consider left-truncated survival data on increasing grid times $t_0 < \dots < t_{k-1}$: subject $i$ enters at a grid index $e_i$ (or before $t_0$), leaves at $t_{x_i}$ with $e_i < x_i$, with or without an event; $d_j$ and $c_j$ are the events and censorings at $t_j$ and $Y_j = \#\{i : e_i < j \le x_i\}$ the number at risk, with support $\mathrm{supp} = \{j : Y_j > 0\}$. The delayed-entry Nelson–Aalen estimate is $\hat A(t) = \sum_{j\in\mathrm{supp},\,t_j\le t}d_j/Y_j$. This formula is defined for all data; only when the support is [gap-free](gap-free.md) is $\hat A$ an estimate of the cumulative hazard, accumulated from the first supported time. If the support has a gap, the sum adds hazards from disconnected stretches and has no cumulative-hazard interpretation.
