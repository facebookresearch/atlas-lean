# Observed delayed-entry at-risk mass

Let $\mu$ be a measure on the grid triples $((T, C), L)$ of an event time, a censoring time and an entry time. The delayed-entry at-risk mass at a grid point $j$ is the real number $\mu(\{L < j \le \min(T, C)\})$, taken to be $0$ if this mass is infinite. When $\mu$ is the joint law of $((T, C), L)$, it is the probability $P(L < j \le \min(T, C))$.
