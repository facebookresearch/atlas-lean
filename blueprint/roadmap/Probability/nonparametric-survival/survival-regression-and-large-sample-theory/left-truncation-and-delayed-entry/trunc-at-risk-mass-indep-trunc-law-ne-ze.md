# When the delayed-entry at-risk mass is positive

Let the event time $T \sim p_T$, censoring time $C \sim p_C$ and entry time $L \sim p_L$ on a finite grid be mutually independent, and $X = \min(T, C)$. Then $P(L < j \le X) \ne 0$ if and only if $P(T \ge j)$, $P(C \ge j)$ and $P(L < j)$ are all nonzero.
