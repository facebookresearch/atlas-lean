# Predictable quadratic variation of a compensated counting process

On a finite measure space, let $N$ be adapted with unit jumps and integrable, $M$ its compensated process and $\Delta A_{k+1} = \mathbb{E}[N_{k+1} - N_k \mid \mathcal F_k]$. Then $\langle M\rangle_n = \sum_{k<n}\Delta A_{k+1}(1 - \Delta A_{k+1})$ almost surely.
