# Nelson–Aalen process

For real processes $N$ and $Y$ (in Aalen's model, a counting process and a number at risk), the Nelson–Aalen process is $\hat A_n = \sum_{k<n}(N_{k+1} - N_k)/Y_{k+1}$, a step with $Y_{k+1} = 0$ contributing $0$.

## Sources

- P. K. Andersen, Ø. Borgan, R. D. Gill and N. Keiding, "Statistical Models Based on Counting Processes", Springer (1993), Sections II.3–II.4 (stochastic integrals, predictable variation), II.6 (product integrals, Duhamel's equation), IV.1 (Nelson–Aalen), IV.3 (Kaplan–Meier).
