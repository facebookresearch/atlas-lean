# The compensator of a counting process is nondecreasing

For a discrete counting process $N$ and any measure $\mu$, each compensator increment $\mu[N_{n+1} - N_n \mid \mathcal F_n]$, with $\mu[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_n]$ Mathlib's conditional expectation (the zero function when $\mu$ is not σ-finite on $\mathcal F_n$ or the increment is not integrable), is $\mu$-almost everywhere nonnegative.
