# Discrete stochastic integrals of bounded integrands are martingales

On a filtered finite measure space indexed by $n \in \mathbb{N}$, write $(H \bullet M)_n = \sum_{k<n}H_{k+1}(M_{k+1} - M_k)$ for the discrete stochastic integral. On a finite measure space, if $M$ is a martingale and $H$ is predictable with each $H_{n+1}$ bounded, then $H \bullet M$ is a martingale.
