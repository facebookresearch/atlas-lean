# Uniqueness of marked compensators over countably many mark sets

On a finite measure space, let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ be a filtration, $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$, and $X$ a random time with $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ such that every $\mathcal F_s$-event contains $\{X > s\}$ or misses it. Suppose also $B \cap \{X \le s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ for $B \in \mathcal F_t$, $s \le t$. A càdlàg compensator of a real process $N$ here is an $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable real process $A$ with right-continuous paths, almost every path of locally finite variation and tending to $0$ at $-\infty$, such that $N - A$ is a martingale. Let $i$ range over a countable index set, and for each $i$ let $A_i$ and $A'_i$ be càdlàg compensators of the same process $N_i$, so that $N_i - A_i$ and $N_i - A'_i$ are martingales. Then almost surely $A_i(t) = A'_i(t)$ for all $i$ and all $t$.
