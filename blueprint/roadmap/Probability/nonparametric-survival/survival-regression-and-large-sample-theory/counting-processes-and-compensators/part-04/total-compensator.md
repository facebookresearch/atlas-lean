# Aggregated compensator

For a measure $\mu$ on $\Omega$ and a finite cohort of subjects with latent times $(T_i, C_i)$, the aggregated compensator is $A(t) = \sum_i\int_{(-\infty, t]}Y_i\,d\Lambda_i$, each integral computed in $[0, \infty]$ and read as a real number ($0$ if infinite), with $\Lambda_i$ the hazard measure of the image measure $\mu \circ T_i^{-1}$ (the law of $T_i$ when $\mu$ is a probability measure).
