# Uniqueness of the compensating random measure of a marked single jump

On a finite measure space, let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ be a filtration, $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$, and $X$ a random time with $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ such that every $\mathcal F_s$-event contains $\{X > s\}$ or misses it. Suppose also $B \cap \{X \le s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ for $B \in \mathcal F_t$, $s \le t$. A càdlàg compensator of a real process $N$ here is an $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable real process $A$ with right-continuous paths, almost every path of locally finite variation and tending to $0$ at $-\infty$, such that $N - A$ is a martingale. On a countably generated mark space $E$, let $A_t$ and $A'_t$ be finite measures on $E$ (for each $t$ and outcome), and for each measurable $B \subseteq E$ let $N(B)$ be a real process. Suppose that for every measurable $B$ both $t \mapsto A_t(B)$ and $t \mapsto A'_t(B)$ are càdlàg compensators of $N(B)$, so that $N(B) - A_\cdot(B)$ and $N(B) - A'_\cdot(B)$ are martingales. Then almost surely $A_t = A'_t$ for all $t$.
