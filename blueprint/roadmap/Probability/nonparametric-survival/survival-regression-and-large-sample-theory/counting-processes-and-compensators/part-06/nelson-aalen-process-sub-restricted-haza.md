# Martingale representation of the Nelson–Aalen estimator

For processes $N, Y$ and a cumulative hazard $\alpha$, pathwise $\hat A - \Lambda^* = Y^{-1} \bullet (N - Y \bullet \alpha)$, where $\hat A$ is the Nelson–Aalen process and $\Lambda^*$ the restricted hazard. Here $Y^{-1}$ is read as $0$ where $Y = 0$ (zero risk).
