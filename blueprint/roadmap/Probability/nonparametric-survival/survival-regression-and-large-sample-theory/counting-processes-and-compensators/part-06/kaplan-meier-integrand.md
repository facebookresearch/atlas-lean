# Integrand of the Kaplan–Meier martingale representation

The integrand of Gill's Kaplan–Meier representation at step $k$ is $\hat S_{k-1}/S_k \cdot Y_k^{-1}$ with $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$, where $k - 1$ is natural-number subtraction (so at $k = 0$ the factor is $\hat S_0$), $Y_k^{-1}$ is read as $0$ when $Y_k = 0$ (zero risk), and $\hat S_{k-1}/S_k = 0$ when $S_k = 0$. In the representation the increment from $k$ to $k + 1$ uses its value at $k + 1$, $\hat S_k/S_{k+1} \cdot Y_{k+1}^{-1}$.
