# Duhamel’s equation in quotient form

Let $\mathcal P_A(n) = \prod_{k<n}(1 - (A_{k+1} - A_k))$ be the discrete product integral, $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$ and $\hat S = \mathcal P_{\hat A}$ the Kaplan–Meier process. If $B_{k+1} - B_k \ne 1$ for every $k < n$, then $\mathcal P_A(n)/\mathcal P_B(n) - 1 = -\sum_{k<n}\frac{\mathcal P_A(k)}{\mathcal P_B(k+1)}(\Delta A_{k+1} - \Delta B_{k+1})$.
