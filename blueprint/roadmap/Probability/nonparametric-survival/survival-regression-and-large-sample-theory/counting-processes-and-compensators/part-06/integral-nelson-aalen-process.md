# Mean of the Nelson–Aalen estimator

In discrete time, let $N$ and $Y$ be real processes and $\alpha$ a deterministic real sequence, with $Y$ predictable and taking values in $\{0\} \cup [1, \infty)$, such that $N - Y \bullet \alpha$ is a martingale, where $(Y \bullet \alpha)_n = \sum_{k<n} Y_{k+1}(\alpha_{k+1} - \alpha_k)$. No integer jumps are required of $N$; Aalen's model, with $N$ a counting process, $Y$ a number at risk and $\alpha$ a cumulative hazard, is the main application. Let $\hat A = Y^{-1} \bullet N$ be the Nelson–Aalen process (steps with $Y = 0$ contribute $0$) and $\Lambda^* = \mathbf 1\{Y \ne 0\} \bullet \alpha$ the restricted hazard. On a probability space, $\mathbb{E}[\hat A_n] = \sum_{k<n}(\alpha_{k+1} - \alpha_k)P(Y_{k+1} \ne 0)$: $\hat A$ is unbiased for $\Lambda^*$, with bias $-\sum(\alpha_{k+1} - \alpha_k)P(Y_{k+1} = 0)$ for $\alpha_n - \alpha_0$.
