# A necessary condition for integrating jumps against the compensated process

On a finite measure space, let $N$ be integrable with unit jumps, $M$ its compensated process (the martingale part of its Doob decomposition) and $\Delta N_k = N_k - N_{k-1}$. If $\Delta N \bullet M$ is a martingale, then for every $k$, almost surely $(N_{k+1} - N_k)\big(1 - \mathbb{E}[N_{k+1} - N_k \mid \mathcal F_k]\big) = 0$: $N$ can only jump where its compensator jump equals $1$.
