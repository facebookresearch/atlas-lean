# Nothing is learned after exit

In the random-censorship model, a subject has latent failure time $T$ and censoring time $C$ on a measurable space, observed time $X = \min(T, C)$, counting process $N(t) = \mathbf 1\{T \le C, T \le t\}$, censoring process $N^c(t) = \mathbf 1\{C < T, C \le t\}$ and at-risk process $Y(t) = \mathbf 1\{t \le X\}$. Let $\mathcal F_t = \sigma(N(u), N^c(u) : u \le t)$ be the history filtration (for measurable $T$ and $C$) and $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$ the strict past. For every $s, u$ and $B \in \mathcal F_u$, $B \cap \{X \le s\} \in \mathcal F_s$.
