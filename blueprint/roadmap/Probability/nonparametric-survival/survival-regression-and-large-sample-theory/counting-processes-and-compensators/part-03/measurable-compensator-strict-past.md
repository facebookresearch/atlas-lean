# The compensator is predictable

In the random-censorship model, a subject has latent failure time $T$ and censoring time $C$ on a measurable space, observed time $X = \min(T, C)$, counting process $N(t) = \mathbf 1\{T \le C, T \le t\}$, censoring process $N^c(t) = \mathbf 1\{C < T, C \le t\}$ and at-risk process $Y(t) = \mathbf 1\{t \le X\}$. Let $\mathcal F_t = \sigma(N(u), N^c(u) : u \le t)$ be the history filtration (for measurable $T$ and $C$) and $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$ the strict past. For every measure $\Lambda$ on $\mathbb{R}$ (in the model, the hazard measure of the failure-time law; nothing here requires that), the compensator $A(t) = \int_{(-\infty, t]} Y\,d\Lambda$ is $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable.
