# Hazard measure

The hazard measure of a measure $F$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is $d\Lambda(s) = F[s, \infty)^{-1}\,dF(s)$, computed in $[0, \infty]$ with $0^{-1} = \infty$ and $\infty^{-1} = 0$, so that $\Lambda(-\infty, t] = \int_{(-\infty, t]}F[s, \infty)^{-1}\,dF(s)$ is the cumulative hazard. The inverse is infinite only on the $F$-null set where $F[s, \infty) = 0$; for a law $F$ this is the usual $d\Lambda(s) = dF(s)/F[s, \infty)$.
