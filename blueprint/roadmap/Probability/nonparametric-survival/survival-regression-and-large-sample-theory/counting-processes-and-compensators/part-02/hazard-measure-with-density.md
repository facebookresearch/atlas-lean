# Hazard rate of a measure with a density

If $F$ is the measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with measurable density $f : \mathbb{R} \to [0, \infty]$ with respect to Lebesgue measure, its hazard measure has density $\lambda(s) = f(s)\,F[s, \infty)^{-1}$ with respect to Lebesgue measure, the hazard rate, computed in $[0, \infty]$ with $0^{-1} = \infty$, $\infty^{-1} = 0$ and $0 \cdot \infty = 0$.
