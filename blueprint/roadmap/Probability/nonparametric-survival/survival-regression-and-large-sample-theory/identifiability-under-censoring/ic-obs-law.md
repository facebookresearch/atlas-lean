# Observed-data law under interval censoring

Let $\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$ be a finite ordered grid of times. Under interval censoring, the observed-data law is the law of $(S, \{c \in S : T \le c\})$ for a set $S$ of grid points with law $p_S$, independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$ on the grid.
