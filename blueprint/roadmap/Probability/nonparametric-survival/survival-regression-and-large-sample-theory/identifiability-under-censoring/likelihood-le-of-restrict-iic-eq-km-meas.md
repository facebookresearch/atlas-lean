# Any extension of the Kaplan–Meier measure is a maximum likelihood estimate

For grouped right-censored data whose observation times are all at most $s$, let $\hat\mu_\tau$ be the Kaplan–Meier measure placing the residual mass at $\tau$. If $s < \tau$ and a probability measure $\mu$ agrees with $\hat\mu_\tau$ on $(-\infty, s]$, then every probability measure has censored-data likelihood at most that of $\mu$.
