# Indistinguishable point masses under interval censoring

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. If $a \le c \iff b \le c$ for every grid point $c$ with $P(c \in S) > 0$, the point masses at $a$ and $b$ give the same observed-data law.
