# Parametric identification with an unknown censoring law

Let $F_\theta$ be probability measures and $G_{\theta,\eta}$ finite measures on $\mathbb{R}$, depending arbitrarily on a parameter $\theta$ and a nuisance $\eta$, and let $P_{\theta,\eta}$ be the observed-data measure of $(F_\theta, G_{\theta,\eta})$, the image of the product $F_\theta \otimes G_{\theta,\eta}$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$. Suppose $\theta$ is determined by the restriction of $F_\theta$ to $(-\infty, h]$ and $P_{\theta,\eta}([h, \infty) \times \{0, 1\}) \ne 0$ for every $\theta$ and $\eta$. If $P_{\theta,\eta} = P_{\theta',\eta'}$, then $\theta = \theta'$, even when $\eta \ne \eta'$. When the $G_{\theta,\eta}$ are probability laws, $P_{\theta,\eta}$ is the law of $(X, \delta) = (T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$ for independent $T \sim F_\theta$ and $C \sim G_{\theta,\eta}$, and the condition reads $P(X \ge h) \ne 0$.
