# The inspect law determines the distribution function on the inspection set

Let $\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$ be a finite ordered grid of times. If two event-time laws $p_T, p_T'$ on the grid give the same law of what is seen at a set $S$ of grid points, namely of $\{c \in S : T \le c\}$, then their distribution functions agree at every $c \in S$.
