# Agreement on the inspection set gives the same inspect law

Let $\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$ be a finite ordered grid of times. If two event-time laws $p_T, p_T'$ on the grid have the same distribution function at every point of a set $S$ of grid points, they give the same law of what is seen at $S$, namely of $\{c \in S : T \le c\}$.
