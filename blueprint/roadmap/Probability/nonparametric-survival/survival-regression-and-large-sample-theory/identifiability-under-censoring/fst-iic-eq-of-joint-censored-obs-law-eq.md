# Point identification without early censoring

If two measures $\mu, \mu'$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ have the same observed-data measure $P$ and $P((-\infty, t) \times \{0\}) = 0$ (no censoring strictly before $t$), then $\mu((-\infty, t] \times \mathbb{R}) = \mu'((-\infty, t] \times \mathbb{R})$; for joint laws of $(T, C)$ they give the same $P(T \le t)$.
