# Identification up to the censoring horizon

Let the event time $T \sim p_T$ and censoring time $C \sim p_C$ on a finite grid be independent, with observed data $(\min(T, C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $P(C = h) \ne 0$ and two event-time laws give the same observed-data law, they agree at every $t \le h$.
