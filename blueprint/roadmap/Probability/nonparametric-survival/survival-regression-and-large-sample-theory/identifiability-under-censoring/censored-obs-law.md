# Observed-data law of right-censored times

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the observed-data measure is the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$. For independent $T \sim F$ and $C \sim G$ with probability laws $F$ and $G$, it is the law of $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$.
