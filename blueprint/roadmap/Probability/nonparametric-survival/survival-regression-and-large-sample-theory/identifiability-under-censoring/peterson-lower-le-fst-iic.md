# The Peterson lower bound

Let $\mu$ be an arbitrary measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $P$ its observed-data measure, the image of $\mu$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$. Then $P((-\infty, t] \times \{1\}) \le \mu((-\infty, t] \times \mathbb{R})$ in $[0, \infty]$; for a joint law $\mu$ of $(T, C)$ this is $P(X \le t, \delta = 1) \le P(T \le t)$.
