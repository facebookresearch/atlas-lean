# At-risk mass under independent censoring

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let $P$ be the observed-data measure, the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$; when $F$ and $G$ are the probability laws of independent $T$ and $C$, $P$ is the law of $(X, \delta) = (T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $G$ is s-finite, then $P([s, \infty) \times \{0, 1\}) = F[s, \infty)\,G[s, \infty)$ in $[0, \infty]$, with $0 \cdot \infty = 0$; for probability laws this is $P(X \ge s) = F[s, \infty)G[s, \infty)$.
