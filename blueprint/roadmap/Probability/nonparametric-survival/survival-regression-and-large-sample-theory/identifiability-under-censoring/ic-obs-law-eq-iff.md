# The identified part of the event-time law under interval censoring

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. Two event-time laws give the same observed-data law if and only if their distribution functions agree at every grid point $c$ with $P(c \in S) > 0$.
