# The Kaplan–Meier estimate at or after every observation time is the residual mass

For grouped right-censored data, if every observation time is at most $t$, then the Kaplan–Meier estimate at $t$ equals the residual mass: both are the product of all Kaplan–Meier factors. For data with at least one observation this means $t$ is at or after the last observation time; with no observations the condition holds for every $t$ and both sides are the empty product $1$.
