# Observed-data law of right-censored grid times

For a measure $\mu$ on the pairs $(T, C)$ of a finite grid, the observed-data measure is the image of $\mu$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (\min(t, c), \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$. When $\mu$ is the joint law of $(T, C)$, it is the law of $(\min(T, C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$.
