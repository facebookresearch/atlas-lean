# The identifiable horizon

Let the event time $T \sim p_T$ and censoring time $C \sim p_C$ on a finite grid be independent, with observed data $(\min(T, C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $h$ is the censoring horizon of $p_C$, two event-time laws give the same observed-data law if and only if they agree at every $t \le h$.
