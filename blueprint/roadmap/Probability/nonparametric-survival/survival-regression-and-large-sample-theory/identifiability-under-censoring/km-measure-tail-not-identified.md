# The tail of the Kaplan–Meier maximum likelihood estimate is not identified

For grouped right-censored data whose observation times are all at most $s$, let $\hat\mu_\tau$ be the Kaplan–Meier measure placing the residual mass at $\tau$. If the residual mass is nonzero and $\tau \ne \tau'$ both exceed $s$, then $\hat\mu_\tau \ne \hat\mu_{\tau'}$, yet they agree on $(-\infty, s]$ and both maximize the censored-data likelihood over all probability measures on $\mathbb{R}$.
