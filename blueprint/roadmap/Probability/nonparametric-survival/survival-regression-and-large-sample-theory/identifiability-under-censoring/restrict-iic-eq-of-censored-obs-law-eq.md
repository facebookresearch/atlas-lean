# Identification up to the horizon

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the observed-data measure of $(F, G)$ is the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$, the law of $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$ for independent $T \sim F$ and $C \sim G$ when $F$ and $G$ are probability laws; two measures $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. If $G$ is finite with $G[h, \infty) \ne 0$ and the observed-data measures of $(F, G)$ and $(F', G)$ agree, then $F$ and $F'$ agree on $(-\infty, h]$.
