# The inspect law is as informative as the distribution function on the inspection set

Let $\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$ be a finite ordered grid of times. For event-time laws $p_T, p_T'$ on the grid and a set $S$ of grid points, where what is seen of an event at $T$ is $\{c \in S : T \le c\}$, the two laws give the same law of what is seen if and only if their distribution functions agree at every point of $S$.
