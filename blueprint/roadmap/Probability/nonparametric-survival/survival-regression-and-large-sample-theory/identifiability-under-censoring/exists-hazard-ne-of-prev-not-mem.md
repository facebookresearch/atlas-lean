# A previous time inspected with probability zero leaves the hazard unidentified

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. Write $h(j) = P(T = j) / P(T \ge j)$ for the [discrete hazard](../../censored-survival-data/hazard.md), with $0/0 = 0$, the value when $P(T \ge j) = 0$. If $j \ge 1$ and $P(j - 1 \in S) = 0$, there are event-time laws with the same observed-data law and different hazards at $j$: the point masses at $j - 1$ and $j$, with hazards $0$ and $1$. The hazard $0$ of the point mass at $j - 1$ comes from the convention, since it has $P(T \ge j) = 0$. If $j$ is the last grid point, every law with $P(T \ge j) > 0$ has hazard $1$ at $j$, so there the hazard is not left unidentified among laws with positive risk; the two laws differ only through $0/0 = 0$.
