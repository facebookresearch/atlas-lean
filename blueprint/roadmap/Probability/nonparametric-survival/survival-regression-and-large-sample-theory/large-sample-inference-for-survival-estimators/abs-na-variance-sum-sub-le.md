# Pathwise law-of-large-numbers bound for the variance sum

Let $n > 0$. If at every step $i < k$, $|Y_{i+1}/n - \bar y_{i+1}| \le \delta \le \varepsilon/2$ with $\bar y_{i+1} \ge \varepsilon > 0$ and $0 \le \Delta\alpha_{i+1} \le \eta$, then the Nelson–Aalen variance sum differs from $\sum_{i<k}\Delta\alpha_{i+1}/\bar y_{i+1}$ by at most $(2\delta/\varepsilon^2 + 2\eta/\varepsilon)(\alpha_k - \alpha_0)$.
