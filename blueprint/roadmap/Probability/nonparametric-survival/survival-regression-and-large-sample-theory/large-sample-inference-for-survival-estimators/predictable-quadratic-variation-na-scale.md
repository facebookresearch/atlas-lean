# Conditional square sum of the scaled Nelson–Aalen process

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $N$ and $Y$ real-valued processes on the grid and $\alpha$ a deterministic real sequence (no counting-process structure is assumed; the counting model with at-risk process $Y$ and cumulative hazard $\alpha$ is an application), $\hat A = Y^{-1}\bullet N$ the Nelson–Aalen process, $\Lambda^* = \mathbf 1\{Y \ne 0\}\bullet\alpha$ and $M = N - Y\bullet\alpha$. Here $Y^{-1}$ is read as $0$ where $Y = 0$ (zero risk). Let $Y$ be predictable with values in $\{0\} \cup [1, \infty)$ and suppose that for $j < k$, $(\Delta M_{j+1})^2$ is $\mu$-integrable and $\mu[(\Delta M_{j+1})^2 \mid \mathcal F_j] = Y_{j+1}\Delta\alpha_{j+1}(1 - \Delta\alpha_{j+1})$ $\mu$-almost everywhere, where $\mu[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_j]$ is Mathlib's conditional expectation with respect to $\mu$ (the zero function when $\mu$ is not σ-finite on $\mathcal F_j$). Then the conditional square sum of $\sqrt n(\hat A - \Lambda^*)$ up to $k$, formed with $\mu[\cdot \mid \mathcal F]$, equals $n\sum_{i<k}Y_{i+1}^{-1}\Delta\alpha_{i+1}(1 - \Delta\alpha_{i+1})$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.
