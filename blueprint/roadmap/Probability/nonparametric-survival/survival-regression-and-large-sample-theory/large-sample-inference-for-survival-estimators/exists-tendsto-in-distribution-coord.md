# Limit law of one fixed-grid survival coordinate

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ (event time, cause, censoring time on a finite grid) be independent and identically distributed with law $\mu$, with $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$, and consider the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen estimator at grid indices up to $m$; for a coordinate $a$ (a survival or cumulative-incidence value at some $j \le m$), let $v_a$ be its asymptotic variance and $\theta_a$ its plug-in limit. Then $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_{n,a} - \theta_a)$ converges in distribution to $N(0, v_a)$.
