# Simultaneous Wald band for survival estimands under independent censoring

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of the censoring time $C \sim p_C$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. For a finite family $(g_i)_{i\in\iota}$ of coordinates of the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen estimator at grid indices up to $m$ with asymptotic variances $v_{g_i} > 0$, there is a jointly Gaussian centered vector $Y$ with $Y_i \sim N(0, v_{g_i})$ such that, for every $z$ and every standard-error vector $s_n$ that is almost everywhere measurable for each $n$ and converges in probability to $(\sqrt{v_{g_i}})_i$, the simultaneous Wald band $\hat\theta_{n,g_i} \pm zs_{n,i}/\sqrt n$ covers all estimands (each the survival $P(T > j)$ or the cumulative incidence $P(T \le j, J = c)$) with probability tending to $P(|Y_i| \le z\sqrt{v_{g_i}}\ \forall i)$, which is at least $1 - |\iota|(1 - N(0,1)([-z, z]))$ and, for a nonempty family, at most $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
