# Asymptotic distribution of the Kaplan–Meier estimator with a constructed limit

Let subjects $Z_i = (T_i, C_i)$ take values in $\{0, \dots, k-1\}^2$, with observed cell $(\min(T_i, C_i), \mathbf 1\{T_i \le C_i\})$; let $p$ be the vector of cell probabilities, $\hat p_n$ the empirical cell proportions of the first $n$ subjects, $e_j = p(j, 1)$ and $Y_j = \sum_{l\ge j}(p(l, 1) + p(l, 0))$. Suppose the $Z_i$ are independent with the independent censoring law (independent $T \sim p_T$ and $C \sim p_C$), and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. The Kaplan–Meier map at $m$ is $g(v) = \prod_{j\le m}\big(1 - v(j, 1)/\sum_{l\ge j}(v(l, 1) + v(l, 0))\big)$; $L$ denotes its derivative at $p$. Then there is a linear map $A$ on the cell space such that, for $W$ standard Gaussian on the cell space, each $\langle AW, t\rangle$ is $N(0, \mathrm{Var}\langle e_{\mathrm{cell}(Z)}, t\rangle)$ under the subject law (so $AW$ is the limit of $\sqrt n(\hat p_n - p)$), and $\sqrt n(\hat S_n(m) - P(T > m))$ converges in distribution to $L(AW)$.
