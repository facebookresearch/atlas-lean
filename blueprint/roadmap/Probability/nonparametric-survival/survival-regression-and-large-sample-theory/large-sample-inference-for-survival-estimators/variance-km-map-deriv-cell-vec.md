# Variance identification for the Kaplan–Meier derivative

Let $\mu$ be a probability law of $(T, C)$ on a finite grid, with cell probability vector $p$, Kaplan–Meier map $g$ at $m$ and population Greenwood sum $\tau^2$. If $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$ and $g(p) \ne 0$, the variance under $\mu$ of the Kaplan–Meier derivative at $p$ applied to the cell indicator is $g(p)^2\tau^2$.
