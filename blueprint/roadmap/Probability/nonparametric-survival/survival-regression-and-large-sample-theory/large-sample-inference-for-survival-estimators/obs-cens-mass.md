# Observed censoring mass

Let $\mu$ be a measure on the pairs $(T, C)$ of an event time and a censoring time in a finite grid. The observed censoring mass at a grid point $j$ is the real number $\mu(\{\min(T, C) = j,\ T > C\})$, taken to be $0$ if this mass is infinite. When $\mu$ is the joint law of $(T, C)$, it is the probability that follow-up ends at $j$ with a censoring.
