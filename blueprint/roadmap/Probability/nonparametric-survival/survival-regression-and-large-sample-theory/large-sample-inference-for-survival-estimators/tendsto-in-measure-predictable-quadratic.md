# Conditional square sums of the scaled Nelson–Aalen process converge

Let $\mu$ be a measure and, for each $n$, let $Y_n$ be predictable with values in $\{0\} \cup [1, \infty)$, and let the increments of $M_n = N_n - Y_n\bullet\alpha_n$ be $\mu$-square-integrable with the binomial conditional second moment $\mu[(\Delta M_{n,j+1})^2 \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}] = Y_{n,j+1}\Delta\alpha_{n,j+1}(1 - \Delta\alpha_{n,j+1})$ $\mu$-almost everywhere at the first $k_n$ steps, with $\mu[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_{n,j}]$ Mathlib's conditional expectation. Suppose the increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,i}$ are nonnegative with $\max_{i\le k_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,i} \to 0$ and $\alpha_n(k_n) - \alpha_n(0) \le L$, the deterministic $\bar y_n \ge \varepsilon > 0$ satisfy $\max_{i<k_n}|Y_{n,i+1}/n - \bar y_{n,i+1}| \to 0$ in $\mu$-measure, and $\sum_{i<k_n}\Delta\alpha_{n,i+1}/\bar y_{n,i+1} \to \sigma^2$. Then the conditional square sums of $\sqrt n(\hat A_n - \Lambda^*_n)$ up to $k_n$ converge to $\sigma^2$ in $\mu$-measure (in probability when $\mu$ is a probability measure).
