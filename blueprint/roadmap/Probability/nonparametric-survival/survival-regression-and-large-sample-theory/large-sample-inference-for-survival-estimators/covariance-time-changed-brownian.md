# Covariance of a time-changed Brownian motion

Let $(B_s)_{s\ge0}$ be a real process with the finite-dimensional laws of standard Brownian motion (no path continuity is assumed) and $\sigma^2 : [0, \tau] \to [0, \infty)$ nondecreasing. If $Z$ is a process on $[0, \tau]$ with continuous paths such that, almost surely, $Z_t = B(\sigma^2(t))$ for all $t$ simultaneously, then $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_s, Z_t) = \sigma^2(\min(s, t))$.
