# Kaplan–Meier limit on a fixed grid

Let $\hat A_n$ and $\hat S_n$ be the Nelson–Aalen and Kaplan–Meier processes on the grid $1, \dots, K$, suppose $\sqrt n(\hat A_n - \Lambda) \Rightarrow G$ in $\mathbb{R}^K$ (sup norm), and let $S_j = \prod_{i\le j}(1 - \Delta\Lambda_i)$. Then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S)$ converges in distribution to the image of $G$ under the derivative of the product integral; coordinatewise the limit is $-\sum_{i\le j}S_{i-1}\Delta G_i\prod_{i<l\le j}(1 - \Delta\Lambda_l)$.
