# The Kaplan–Meier product-rule map vanishes at its base point

For every cell vector $p$, the [product-rule linear map](km-map-deriv.md) of the Kaplan–Meier map at $p$ vanishes in the direction $p$; no nonzero-risk condition is needed. It is the derivative of the Kaplan–Meier map at $p$ only when every at-risk mass $Y_j$, $j \le m$, is nonzero.
