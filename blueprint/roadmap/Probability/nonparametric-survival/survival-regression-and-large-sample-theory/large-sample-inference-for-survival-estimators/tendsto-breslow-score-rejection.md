# Asymptotic size of the Breslow score test

If $(O - E)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow N(0, v)$ with $v \ne 0$ under the null and $\sqrt{I/n} \to \sqrt v$ in probability, almost everywhere measurable for each $n$,, then the rejection probability of the two-sided Breslow score test at critical value $z$ tends to $N(0, 1)(\mathbb{R}\setminus[-z, z])$.
