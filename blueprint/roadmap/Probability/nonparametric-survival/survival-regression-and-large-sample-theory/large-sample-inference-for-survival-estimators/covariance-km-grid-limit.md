# Greenwood covariance of the Kaplan–Meier grid limit

On a probability space, if the Nelson–Aalen grid limit has square-integrable uncorrelated increments $W_i$ with variances $v_i$ and no hazard jump $\Delta\Lambda_i$ equals $1$, the Kaplan–Meier limit has covariance $S_aS_b\sum_{i\le\min(a,b)}v_i/(1 - \Delta\Lambda_i)^2$, where $S_j = \prod_{i\le j}(1 - \Delta\Lambda_i)$.
