# Cell probability vector

Let $\mu$ be a measure on the pairs $(T, C)$ of an event time and a censoring time in a finite grid. Its cell probability vector gives the cell $(j, 1)$ the real number $\mu(\{\min(T, C) = j,\ T \le C\})$ and the cell $(j, 0)$ the real number $\mu(\{\min(T, C) = j,\ T > C\})$, each taken to be $0$ if the mass is infinite. When $\mu$ is the joint law of $(T, C)$, these are the probabilities of the observed cells.
