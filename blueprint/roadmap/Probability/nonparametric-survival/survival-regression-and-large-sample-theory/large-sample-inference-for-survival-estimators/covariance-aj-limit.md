# Covariance of the Aalen–Johansen grid limit

On a probability space, for a square-integrable limit $G$ of the cause-specific Nelson–Aalen increments with covariance matrix $\Gamma$, the covariance of the coordinates $(j, c)$ and $(j', c')$ of the Aalen–Johansen limit is $\sum_p\sum_qa(j, c; p)a(j', c'; q)\Gamma_{pq}$, where $a(j, c; p)$ is the derivative of the Aalen–Johansen map in the direction of the $p$-th unit vector.
