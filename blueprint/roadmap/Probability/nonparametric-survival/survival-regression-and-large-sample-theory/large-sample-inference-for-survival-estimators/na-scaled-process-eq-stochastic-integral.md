# Martingale representation of the scaled Nelson–Aalen process

Let $N$ and $Y$ be real-valued processes on the grid and $\alpha$ a deterministic real sequence (no counting-process structure is assumed; the counting model with at-risk process $Y$ and cumulative hazard $\alpha$ is an application), $\hat A = Y^{-1}\bullet N$ the Nelson–Aalen process, $\Lambda^* = \mathbf 1\{Y \ne 0\}\bullet\alpha$ and $M = N - Y\bullet\alpha$. Here $Y^{-1}$ is read as $0$ where $Y = 0$ (zero risk). Then $\sqrt n(\hat A - \Lambda^*) = (\sqrt nY^{-1})\bullet M$.
