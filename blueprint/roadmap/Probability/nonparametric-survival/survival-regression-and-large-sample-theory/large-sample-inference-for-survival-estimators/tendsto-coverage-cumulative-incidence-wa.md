# Aalen–Johansen Wald interval at a grid time

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of the censoring time $C \sim p_C$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. For a cause $c$ and $j \le m$ whose cumulative-incidence coordinate has asymptotic variance $v > 0$, and standard errors $s_n$, each almost everywhere measurable, with $s_n \to \sqrt v$ in probability, the interval $\hat F_c(j) \pm zs_n/\sqrt n$ covers $P(T \le j, J = c)$ with probability tending to $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
