# Competition ranks in the reversal example

In the reversal example, the pooled sample is $x = (0, 3, 1, 1)$, with groups $A = \{0, 1\}$ and
$B = \{2, 3\}$ of indices; the order-preserving recoding fixes $0$ and $3$ and sends $1$ to $2$. The
entries at indices $0, 1, 2, 3$ (with values $0, 3, 1, 1$) have competition ranks $1, 4, 2, 2$.
