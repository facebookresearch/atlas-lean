# Cond Indep Discrete compl

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T \subseteq \Omega$ measurable, $V : \Omega \to \beta$ with $\beta$ a measurable space, and $W : \Omega \to \gamma$. Say that $T$ is conditionally independent of $V$ given $W$ if for every measurable $B \subseteq \beta$ and every $w \in \gamma$,

$$\mu(T \cap \{V \in B\} \cap \{W = w\})\, \mu(W = w) = \mu(T \cap \{W = w\})\, \mu(\{V \in B\} \cap \{W = w\}).$$

If $T$ is conditionally independent of $V$ given $W$ in this sense, then so is the complement $T^c$.
