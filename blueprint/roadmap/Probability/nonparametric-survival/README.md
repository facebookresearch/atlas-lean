# Nonparametric statistics and survival analysis

Park and Pham's theorem on when the supremum of a positive empirical process over a finite family of nonnegative functions is large compared with its expectation is under [suprema of selector and empirical processes](../high-dimensional-probability/suprema-of-selector-and-empirical-processes/README.md) in High-dimensional and geometric probability.
