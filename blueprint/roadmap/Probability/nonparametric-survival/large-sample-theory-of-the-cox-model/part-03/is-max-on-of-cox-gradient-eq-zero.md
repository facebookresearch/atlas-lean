# A root of the Cox score vector is a maximizer

For survival data with observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariate vectors $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^p$, let $U(\beta)$ be the Cox score vector and $I(\beta)$ the observed information matrix. If $U(b) = 0$, then $b$ maximizes the log partial likelihood.
