# The scalar time-dependent score with fixed covariates at the event times

For survival data with a scalar time-dependent covariate $z_i(t)$, let $\ell(b)$ be the time-dependent log partial likelihood, $U(b) = \sum_{i:\delta_i}(z_i(T_i) - \bar z_i(b))$ its score and $I(b) = \sum_{i:\delta_i}\mathrm{Var}_i(b)$ its information. If at every event time the covariates equal fixed covariates $z'$, then $U$ is the fixed-covariate Cox score with covariates $z'$.
