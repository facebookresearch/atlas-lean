# Asymptotic distribution of the Cox estimator

Let $\ell_n$ be the scalar Cox log partial likelihood of the first $n$ subjects, with score $U_n$ and observed information $I_n$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$. If $\hat\beta_n$ is measurable, then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0) \Rightarrow Z/v$.
