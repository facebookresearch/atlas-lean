# Asymptotic normality of the Cox estimator

In the discrete-time Cox counting-process model on a probability space, for each $n$ there are $n$ subjects with observed grid times $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariates $|z_i| \le B$ known at time $0$, observed through a filtration $(\mathcal F_k)$; subject $i$ is at risk at step $k$ when $k + 1 \le t_i$ ($Y_i(k)$, predictable) and has an event there when $t_i = k + 1$ and $\delta_i$ ($\Delta N_i(k)$, adapted); the intensity is $\mathbb{E}[\Delta N_i(k) \mid \mathcal F_k] = Y_i(k)e^{\beta_0z_i}\Delta\alpha_k$ with $\Delta\alpha_k \ge 0$; and almost surely at most one event occurs per step. Write $S^{(j)}(b, k) = \sum_lY_l(k)z_l^je^{bz_l}$ and $V(b, k) = S^{(2)}/S^{(0)} - (S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})^2$. Assume Andersen–Gill regularity on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$: deterministic functions $s_n(j, k, b)$ with $S^{(j)}(b, k)/n - s_n(j, k, b) \to 0$ uniformly over $k < K_n$ and the interval in probability ($j = 0, 1, 2$), $s_n(0, k, b) \ge \kappa > 0$, $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_k \le \Lambda$, and the deterministic compensators $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_kv(s_n(\cdot, k, b))s_n(0, k, \beta_0)$ converge to $i(b)$ uniformly, where $v(x_0, x_1, x_2) = x_2/x_0 - (x_1/x_0)^2$. Suppose every observed event lies on the grid steps $1, \dots, K_n$. Let $\hat\beta_n$ maximize the Cox log partial likelihood whenever a maximizer exists. If $r > 0$, $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$ and $\hat\beta_n$ is measurable, then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0)$ converges in distribution to $Z/v \sim N(0, v^{-1})$ for any $Z \sim N(0, v)$.
