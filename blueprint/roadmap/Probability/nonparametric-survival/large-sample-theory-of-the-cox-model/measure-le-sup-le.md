# Lenglart’s domination inequality

On a filtered probability space indexed by $\mathbb{N}$, let $X \ge 0$ be adapted and integrable and $A$ predictable ($A_{k+1}$ is $\mathcal F_k$-measurable), integrable and nondecreasing with $A_0 = 0$. If $\mathbb{E}X_\tau \le \mathbb{E}A_\tau$ for every stopping time $\tau \le K$, then for $\varepsilon, \eta > 0$,

$$P\Big(\max_{k\le K}X_k \ge \varepsilon\Big) \le \frac{\mathbb{E}[A_K \wedge \eta]}{\varepsilon} + P(A_K \ge \eta).$$
