# Pointwise compensator limit from a uniform one

Let $I^*_n(b) = \frac1n\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_kV(b, k)S^{(0)}(\beta_0, k)$ be the normalized information compensator. For a measure $P$: if $r \ge 0$, $v \ge 0$, $i(\beta_0) = v$ and $I^*_n \to i$ uniformly on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ in $P$-measure, then $I^*_n(\beta_0) \to v$ in $P$-measure (both in probability when $P$ is a probability measure).
