# Lipschitz dependence of the information martingale on the coefficient

In the discrete-time Cox counting-process model under a measure $P$ (not necessarily a probability measure), for each $n$ there are $n$ subjects with observed grid times $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariates $|z_i| \le B$ (with $B \ge 0$), observed through a filtration $(\mathcal F_{n,k})_k$ that may depend on $n$, the covariates being $\mathcal F_{n,0}$-measurable; step $k$ is the grid time $k + 1$, subject $i$ is at risk there when $k + 1 \le t_i$, with indicator $Y_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k}$-measurable, and has an event there when $t_i = k + 1$ and $\delta_i$, with indicator $\Delta N_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k+1}$-measurable; the intensity is $P[\Delta N_i(k) \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}] = Y_i(k)e^{\beta_0z_i}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}$ $P$-almost everywhere, with hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \ge 0$ that may depend on $n$ and $P[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}]$ Mathlib's conditional expectation under $P$ (the zero function when $P$ is not σ-finite on $\mathcal F_{n,k}$ or $\Delta N_i(k)$ is not $P$-integrable); and $P$-almost everywhere at most one event occurs per step. Write $S^{(j)}(b, k) = \sum_lY_l(k)z_l^je^{bz_l}$ and $V(b, k) = S^{(2)}/S^{(0)} - (S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})^2$. Let $M_{n,K}(b) = \frac1n\sum_{k<K}V(b, k)\big(\Delta\bar N(k) - \Delta\alpha_{n,k}S^{(0)}(\beta_0, k)\big)$, with $\Delta\bar N(k) = \sum_i\Delta N_i(k)$ and division by $n = 0$ giving $0$, be the sum of the normalized information martingale increments before the horizon $K$: the information increments $V(b, k)\Delta\bar N(k)$ summed over $k < K$, minus the information compensator at $K$, divided by $n$. Let $\bar A_n(K) = \sum_{k<K}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}S^{(0)}(\beta_0, k)$ be the compensator of the total count over the first $K$ steps. For $n > 0$, every horizon $K$ and all $b, b'$, $|M_{n,K}(b) - M_{n,K}(b')| \le 6B^3|b - b'|(1 + \bar A_n(K)/n)$ pathwise, with both parameter values compared at the same horizon $K$.
