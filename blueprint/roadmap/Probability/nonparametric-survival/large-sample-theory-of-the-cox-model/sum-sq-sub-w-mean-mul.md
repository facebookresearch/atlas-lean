# Information identity for the risk set

For nonnegative weights $Y_l$ and covariates $z_l$, write $S^{(j)}(b) = \sum_lY_lz_l^je^{bz_l}$, $\bar z(b) = S^{(1)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b)$ and $V(b) = S^{(2)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b) - \bar z(b)^2$. Then $\sum_i(z_i - \bar z(b))^2Y_ie^{bz_i}c = cV(b)S^{(0)}(b)$ for every $c$.
