# Consistency of the maximizer of a random concave criterion

For each $n$ and $\omega$, let $\ell_n$ be a concave criterion with score $U_n$ and information $I_n \ge 0$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. Then $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in probability.
