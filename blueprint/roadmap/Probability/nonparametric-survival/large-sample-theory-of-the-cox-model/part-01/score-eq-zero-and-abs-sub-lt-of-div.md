# Normalized deterministic core of consistency

Let $\ell$ be a concave criterion on $\mathbb{R}$ with score $U = \ell'$ and observed information $I = -U' \ge 0$. Let $n > 0$, $v > 0$, $\varepsilon > 0$, and suppose on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$ that $|i(b) - v| < v/4$ and $|I(b)/n - i(b)| < v/4$, and $|U(\beta_0)/n| < \varepsilon v/4$. Then any $\hat b$ that maximizes $\ell$ whenever a maximizer exists is a root of $U$ within $\varepsilon$ of $\beta_0$.
