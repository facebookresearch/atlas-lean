# Andersen–Gill regularity on a grid

Andersen–Gill regularity on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ ($r \ge 0$) asks for deterministic $s_n(j, k, b)$ with: $S^{(j)}(b, k)/n - s_n(j, k, b) \to 0$ uniformly over $k < K_n$ and the interval in probability for $j = 0, 1, 2$; $s_n(0, k, b) \ge \kappa > 0$; $\sum_{k<K_n}\Delta\alpha_k \le \Lambda$; and deterministic information compensators converging uniformly to a function $i$ on the interval.
