# Counting-process conditions for the Cox model on a grid

In the discrete-time Cox counting-process model under a measure $P$ (not necessarily a probability measure), for each $n$ there are $n$ subjects with observed grid times $t_i \in \mathbb{N}$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariates $|z_i| \le B$ (with $B \ge 0$), observed through a filtration $(\mathcal F_{n,k})_k$ that may depend on $n$, the covariates being $\mathcal F_{n,0}$-measurable; step $k$ is the grid time $k + 1$, subject $i$ is at risk there when $k + 1 \le t_i$, with indicator $Y_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k}$-measurable, and has an event there when $t_i = k + 1$ and $\delta_i$, with indicator $\Delta N_i(k)$ $\mathcal F_{n,k+1}$-measurable; the intensity is $P[\Delta N_i(k) \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}] = Y_i(k)e^{\beta_0z_i}\Delta\alpha_{n,k}$ $P$-almost everywhere, with hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_{n,k} \ge 0$ that may depend on $n$ and $P[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_{n,k}]$ Mathlib's conditional expectation under $P$ (the zero function when $P$ is not σ-finite on $\mathcal F_{n,k}$ or $\Delta N_i(k)$ is not $P$-integrable); and $P$-almost everywhere at most one event occurs per step.
