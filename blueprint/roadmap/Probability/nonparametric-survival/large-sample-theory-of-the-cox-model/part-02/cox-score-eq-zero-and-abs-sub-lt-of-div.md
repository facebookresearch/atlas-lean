# Normalized deterministic core of consistency for the Cox estimator

Let $n > 0$, $v > 0$, $\varepsilon > 0$, and suppose on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$ that $|i(b) - v| < v/4$ and $|I_n(b)/n - i(b)| < v/4$, and $|U_n(\beta_0)/n| < \varepsilon v/4$, for the Cox score $U_n$ and information $I_n$. Then any $\hat b$ that maximizes the log partial likelihood whenever a maximizer exists is a root of $U_n$ within $\varepsilon$ of $\beta_0$.
