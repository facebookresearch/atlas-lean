# Asymptotic normality of the Cox estimator under Andersen–Gill conditions

Let $\ell_n$ be the scalar Cox log partial likelihood of the $n$ subjects in row $n$, with score $U_n$ and observed information $I_n$, and $\hat\beta_n$ a maximizer whenever one exists. Suppose $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, and $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly in probability near $\beta_0$, with $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v > 0$. If $Z \sim N(0, v)$ and $\hat\beta_n$ is almost everywhere measurable, then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0) \Rightarrow Z/v \sim N(0, v^{-1})$.

## Sources

- P. K. Andersen and R. D. Gill, "Cox's regression model for counting processes: a large sample study", Ann. Statist. 10 (1982), 1100–1120, Theorems 3.2 and 3.3.
