# Weighted risk-set covariance matrix

For subject $i$ with risk set $R_i = \{l : T_l \ge T_i\}$, the $e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle}$-weighted covariance matrix of the covariate vectors over $R_i$ has entries $\sum_{l\in R_i}w_lx_{lj}x_{lk}/\sum_{l\in R_i}w_l - \bar x_{ij}\bar x_{ik}$ with $w_l = e^{\langle\beta, x_l\rangle}$; since $i \in R_i$, the denominator is positive.
