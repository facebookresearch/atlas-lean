# Weighted risk-set variance

For real weights $Y_l$ and a scalar covariate $z_l$, with $S^{(j)}(b) = \sum_lY_lz_l^je^{bz_l}$, the weighted variance is $V(b) = S^{(2)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b) - (S^{(1)}(b)/S^{(0)}(b))^2$, with quotients by $S^{(0)}(b) = 0$ read as $0$, so $V(b) = 0$ when $S^{(0)}(b) = 0$. When each $Y_l$ is the indicator that subject $l$ is at risk, this is the $e^{bz}$-weighted variance of the covariate over the risk set.
