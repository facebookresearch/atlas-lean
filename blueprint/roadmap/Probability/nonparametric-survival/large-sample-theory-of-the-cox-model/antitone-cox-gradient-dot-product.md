# The Cox score is nonincreasing along every line

For survival data with observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and covariate vectors $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^p$, let $U(\beta)$ be the Cox score vector and $I(\beta)$ the observed information matrix. For all $\beta, u$, the function $t \mapsto \langle U(\beta + tu), u\rangle$ is nonincreasing.
