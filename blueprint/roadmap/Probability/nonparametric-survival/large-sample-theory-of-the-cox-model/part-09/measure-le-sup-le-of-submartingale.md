# Lenglart’s inequality for a Doob compensator

On a filtered probability space, let $X \ge 0$ be adapted and integrable with $X_0 = 0$ and $A$ predictable, integrable and nondecreasing with $A_0 = 0$, such that $A - X$ is a submartingale. Then for $\varepsilon, \eta > 0$, $P(\max_{k\le K}X_k \ge \varepsilon) \le \mathbb{E}[A_K \wedge \eta]/\varepsilon + P(A_K \ge \eta)$.
