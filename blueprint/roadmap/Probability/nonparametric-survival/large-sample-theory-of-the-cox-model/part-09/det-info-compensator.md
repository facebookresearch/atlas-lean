# Deterministic Cox information compensator

For deterministic limits $s(j, k, b)$ of $S^{(j)}(b, k)/n$ and hazard increments $\Delta\alpha_k$, the deterministic information compensator is $\sum_{k<K}\Delta\alpha_kv(s(0, k, b), s(1, k, b), s(2, k, b))s(0, k, \beta_0)$, with $v(s_0, s_1, s_2) = s_2/s_0 - (s_1/s_0)^2$.
