# Bootstrap validity for the grid Cox estimator with fixed data

Fix distinct grid times $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and distinct covariate levels $c_1, \dots, c_q$, and encode an observation $(T, d, z)$ by the indicators of an observed event at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$ and of being at risk at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$. For a vector $\mu$ of such coordinates let $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$, $S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ac_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$ and $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)(c_a - S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})$. Let fixed data have every observed time on the grid and every covariate among the levels, and let $Z$ be an almost everywhere measurable random vector on a probability space with projections $\langle Z, s\rangle \sim N(0, v(s))$. Suppose the plug-in variances of the encoded data converge to $v(s)$ and satisfy the Lindeberg condition in every direction, the encoded means converge to $\theta$, every grid time has positive at-risk mass under $\theta$, $\Psi(\theta, \beta_0) = 0$ and the limiting information is nonzero. Then conditionally on the data, $\sqrt n(\hat\beta^*_n - \hat\beta_n)$ converges in distribution to $LZ$, with $L$ the derivative of the Cox root.
