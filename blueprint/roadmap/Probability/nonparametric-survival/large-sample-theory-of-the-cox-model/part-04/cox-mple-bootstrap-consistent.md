# Bootstrap consistency for the grid Cox estimator

Fix distinct grid times $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and distinct covariate levels $c_1, \dots, c_q$, and encode an observation $(T, d, z)$ by the indicators of an observed event at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$ and of being at risk at $t_j$ with covariate $c_a$. For a vector $\mu$ of such coordinates let $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$, $S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j) = \sum_ac_ae^{bc_a}\mu(\text{at risk}, j, a)$ and $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)(c_a - S^{(1)}/S^{(0)})$, a quotient with $S^{(0)} = 0$ being $0$. Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent and identically distributed observations with observed times almost surely on the grid and covariates among the levels, $\theta$ the mean of the encoded observation, every grid time with positive at-risk probability, $\Psi(\theta, \beta_0) = 0$ and nonzero limiting information; let $Z$ be an almost everywhere measurable random vector on a probability space, centered Gaussian with the covariance of the encoded observation and $L$ the derivative of the Cox root. Let $\hat\beta_n$ be the Cox estimator of $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ and, given the data, let $\hat\beta^*_n$ be the same estimator recomputed on $n$ whole observation triples drawn independently and with replacement from the empirical distribution of $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$. Then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0) \Rightarrow LZ$, and for almost every data sequence the conditional law of $\sqrt n(\hat\beta^*_n - \hat\beta_n)$ given the data converges to the same limit.
