# Cox score map on the indicator space

For an arbitrary coordinate vector $\mu$ (not necessarily an encoded sample mean), the Cox score map is $\Psi(\mu, b) = \sum_{j,a}\mu(\text{event}, j, a)\big(c_a - S^{(1)}(\mu, b, j)/S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j)\big)$, where a quotient with $S^{(0)}(\mu, b, j) = 0$ is $0$; at the mean of the encoded sample it is the Cox score divided by $n$.
