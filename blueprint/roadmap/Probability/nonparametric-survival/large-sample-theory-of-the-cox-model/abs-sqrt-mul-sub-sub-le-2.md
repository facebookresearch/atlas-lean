# Deterministic core of asymptotic normality for the Cox estimator

Let $n > 0$, $v > 0$ and $\eta \le v/2$. If $\hat b$ is a root of the Cox score with $|\hat b - \beta_0| \le \varepsilon$ and $|I_n(b)/n - v| < \eta$ on $[\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon]$, then $|\sqrt n(\hat b - \beta_0) - (U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n)/v| \le |U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n|\cdot2\eta/v^2$.
