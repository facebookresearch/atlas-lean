# Vector Andersen–Gill conditions

For random survival data with covariate vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$, the vector Andersen–Gill conditions at $\beta_0$ with limiting information $\Sigma$ and radius $r > 0$ are: $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$; $I_n(b)/n \to \Sigma(b)$ entrywise, uniformly on the closed ball of radius $r$ about $\beta_0$, in probability; every entry of $\Sigma$ is continuous at $\beta_0$; and $\Sigma(\beta_0)$ is positive definite.
