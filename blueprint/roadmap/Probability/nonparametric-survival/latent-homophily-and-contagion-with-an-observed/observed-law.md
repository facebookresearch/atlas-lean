# Joint law of the observed triple

For a probability mass function $\mu$ on a latent space $\Omega$, a tie indicator $A$, a treatment indicator $D$ and potential outcomes $Y_0, Y_1$, the observed law is the image of $\mu$ under the observation map $\omega \mapsto (A(\omega), D(\omega), Y_{D(\omega)}(\omega))$: the joint law of the observed triple.
