# Average effect under the contagion mechanism

In the two-mechanism example of latent homophily and contagion, with latent coordinates (ego trait $a$, peer trait $b$, shared environment $e$) uniform on $\{0,1\}^3$ and tie present exactly when $a = b$, the contagion mechanism has average effect $1/2$: the ego intervention moves the peer's outcome from $0$ to $1$ on the tie, which has probability $1/2$, and not at all off it.
