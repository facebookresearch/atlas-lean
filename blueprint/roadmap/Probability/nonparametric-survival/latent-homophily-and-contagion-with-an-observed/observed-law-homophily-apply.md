# Observed law under homophily

In the two-mechanism example with latent coordinates (ego trait $a$, peer trait $b$, shared environment $e$) in $\{0,1\}^3$ drawn uniformly, under the homophily mechanism the observed triple (tie $t$, ego behavior $d$, peer outcome $y$) has mass $1/4$ when $y = \mathbf 1\{t = d\}$ and mass $0$ otherwise.
