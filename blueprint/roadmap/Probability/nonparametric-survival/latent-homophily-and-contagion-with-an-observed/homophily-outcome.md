# Peer outcome under homophily

In the two-mechanism example of latent homophily and contagion, with latent coordinates (ego trait $a$, peer trait $b$, shared environment $e$) in $\{0,1\}^3$, the peer's outcome under the homophily mechanism is $\mathbf 1\{b \oplus e = 1\}$, from the peer trait and the shared environment only; it does not depend on ego's behavior.
