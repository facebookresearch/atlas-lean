# Peer outcome under contagion when ego does not act

In the two-mechanism example of latent homophily and contagion, with latent coordinates (ego trait $a$, peer trait $b$, shared environment $e$) in $\{0,1\}^3$ and tie present exactly when $a = b$, the peer's outcome under the contagion mechanism had ego not acted is $0$ on the tie and the homophily outcome $\mathbf 1\{b \ne e\}$ off it.
