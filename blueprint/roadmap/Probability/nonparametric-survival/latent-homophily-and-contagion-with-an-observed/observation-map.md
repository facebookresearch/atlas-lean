# Observed tie and behaviors

For a latent space $\Omega$, a tie indicator $A$, a treatment indicator $D$ and potential outcomes $Y_0, Y_1 : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, the observation map sends $\omega$ to the observed triple $(A(\omega), D(\omega), Y_{D(\omega)}(\omega))$: the tie, ego's behavior and the peer's observed outcome, which is $Y_1$ where $D$ holds and $Y_0$ elsewhere.
