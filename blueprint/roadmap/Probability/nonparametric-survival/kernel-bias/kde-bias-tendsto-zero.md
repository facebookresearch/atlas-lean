# Pointwise bias of the KDE vanishes

Let $f$ be a measurable probability density on $\mathbb{R}$ with $0 \le f \le B$, let $K$ be a measurable integrable kernel with $\int K = 1$, and let $t$ be a point where $f$ is continuous. Let $X_1, \dots, X_n$, $n \ge 1$, be random variables on a probability space, each with density $f$. Then the bias of the kernel density estimator at $t$, $\mathbb{E}[\hat f_h(t)] - f(t)$, tends to $0$ as the bandwidth $h \to 0^+$.
