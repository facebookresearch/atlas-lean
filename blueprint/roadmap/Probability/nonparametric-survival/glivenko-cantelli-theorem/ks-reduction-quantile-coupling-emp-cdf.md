# Distribution-free reduction of the Kolmogorov–Smirnov distance by quantile coupling

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with distribution function $F(x) = \mu((-\infty, x])$ and quantile function $Q(u) = \inf\{x : u \le F(x)\}$ for $u \in (0,1)$. Let $u_1, \dots, u_{n+1} \in (0,1)$, let $G$ be the empirical distribution function of $u_1, \dots, u_{n+1}$ and $H$ that of $Q(u_1), \dots, Q(u_{n+1})$. Then

$$\sup_{x \in \mathbb{R}} |H(x) - F(x)| \le \sup_{v \in [0,1]} |G(v) - v|.$$

No continuity of $F$ is assumed.
