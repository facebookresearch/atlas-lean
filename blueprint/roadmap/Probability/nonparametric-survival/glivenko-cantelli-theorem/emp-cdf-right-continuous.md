# Empirical distribution functions are right-continuous

For a sample $x_1, \dots, x_n$ of real numbers write $F_n(t) = \#\{i : x_i \le t\}/n$ for its empirical distribution function (with $F_n \equiv 0$ when $n = 0$). For every sample, $t \mapsto F_n(t)$ is right-continuous at every point $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
