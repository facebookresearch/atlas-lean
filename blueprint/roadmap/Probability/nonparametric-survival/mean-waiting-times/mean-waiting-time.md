# Mean waiting time under a scaled deterministic hazard

For a hazard $h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and a scale $c$, the mean waiting time is $\int_{(0,\infty)} \exp\bigl(-c \int_0^t h(r)\, dr\bigr)\, dt$, the integral of the survival function of the scaled deterministic hazard $c\,h$, read as $0$ when the survival function is not integrable.
