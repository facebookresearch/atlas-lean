# Aalen–Johansen limit law for grouped competing-risks data

On a probability space, for random grouped competing-risks data sets on a fixed grid, if the cause-specific Nelson–Aalen increments $d_{i,c}/Y_i$ satisfy $\sqrt n(\cdot - x) \Rightarrow G$, then the Aalen–Johansen estimates at the grid times satisfy $\sqrt n(\hat F_n - F(x)) \Rightarrow DF(x)G$.
