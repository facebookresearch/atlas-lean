# Wald coverage of survival estimands under independent censoring

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of the censoring time $C \sim p_C$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. For a nondegenerate coordinate $a$ with consistent measurable standard errors, the Wald interval covers the estimand (the survival $P(T > j)$ or the cumulative incidence $P(T \le j, J = c)$) with probability tending to $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
