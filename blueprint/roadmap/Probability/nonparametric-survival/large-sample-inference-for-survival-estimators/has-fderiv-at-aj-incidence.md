# Differentiability of the Aalen–Johansen map

For cause-specific hazard increments $x_{i,c}$ at grid times $i = 1, \dots, K$ and causes $c = 1, \dots, r$, the Aalen–Johansen map is $F_c(j) = \sum_{i\le j}S_{i-1}x_{i,c}$ with $S_{i-1} = \prod_{l<i}(1 - \sum_ex_{l,e})$. Then the map $x \mapsto F(x)$ is Fréchet differentiable everywhere, with the derivative given by the Aalen–Johansen derivative formula.
