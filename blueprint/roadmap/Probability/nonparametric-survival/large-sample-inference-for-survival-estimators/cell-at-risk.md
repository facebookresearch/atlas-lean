# At-risk functional on cell space

The at-risk functional at $j$ is the linear map $v \mapsto \sum_{l\ge j}(v(l, 1) + v(l, 0))$; at the cell probabilities of a joint law of $(T, C)$ it is $P(\min(T, C) \ge j)$ and at the empirical proportions of the first $n$ subjects it is $n_j/n$, which is $0$ for $n = 0$.
