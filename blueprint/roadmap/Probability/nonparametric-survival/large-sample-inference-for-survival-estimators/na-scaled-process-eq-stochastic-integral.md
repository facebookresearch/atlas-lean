# Martingale representation of the scaled Nelson–Aalen process

Let $N$ be a counting process, $Y$ an at-risk process, $\alpha$ a deterministic cumulative hazard, $\hat A = Y^{-1}\bullet N$ the Nelson–Aalen process, $\Lambda^* = \mathbf 1\{Y \ne 0\}\bullet\alpha$ and $M = N - Y\bullet\alpha$. Then $\sqrt n(\hat A - \Lambda^*) = (\sqrt nY^{-1})\bullet M$.
