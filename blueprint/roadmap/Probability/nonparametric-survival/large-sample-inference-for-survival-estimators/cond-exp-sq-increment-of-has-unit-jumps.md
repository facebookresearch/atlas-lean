# Binomial conditional variance for a single subject

On a finite measure space, let $N$ be adapted and integrable with unit jumps, $Y \in \{0, 1\}$ predictable and integrable, and $N - Y\bullet\alpha$ a martingale. Then $\mathbb{E}[(\Delta M_{k+1})^2 \mid \mathcal F_k] = Y_{k+1}\Delta\alpha_{k+1}(1 - \Delta\alpha_{k+1})$ almost surely, $M = N - Y\bullet\alpha$.
