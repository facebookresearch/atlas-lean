# Asymptotic variance of a fixed-grid survival coordinate

For a measure $\mu$ on the subject records $((T, J), C)$ (event time, cause and censoring time on a finite grid) and a coordinate $a$ of the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen estimator at horizon $m$, the asymptotic variance is Mathlib's variance with respect to $\mu$ of the influence function of $a$; when $\mu$ is the subject law, it is the variance of the influence function under that law.
