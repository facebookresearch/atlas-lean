# Riemann–Stieltjes convergence on a uniform grid

Let $\tau > 0$, let $\Lambda$ be a continuous cumulative hazard, $y$ continuous on $[0, \tau]$ with $y \ge \varepsilon > 0$ there, and $K_n \to \infty$ with mesh $h_n = \tau/K_n$. For $t \in [0, \tau]$, with $\lfloor\cdot\rfloor_+$ the natural-number floor,

$$\sum_{i<\lfloor t/h_n\rfloor_+}\frac{\Lambda((i+1)h_n) - \Lambda(ih_n)}{y((i+1)h_n)} \to \int_{(0,t]}\frac{d\Lambda}{y}.$$
