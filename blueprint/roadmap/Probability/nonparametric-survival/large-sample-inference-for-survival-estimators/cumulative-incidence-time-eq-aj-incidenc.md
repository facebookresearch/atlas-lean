# The Aalen–Johansen estimate as a grid map

For grouped competing-risks data with cause-specific death counts $d_{i,c}$ and numbers at risk $Y_i$, the Aalen–Johansen estimate of the cumulative incidence of cause $c$ at the $j$-th time is the Aalen–Johansen map of the increments $d_{i,c}/Y_i$, where an increment with an empty risk set ($Y_i = 0$) is $0$.
