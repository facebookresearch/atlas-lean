# Simultaneous Wald band for survival estimands under independent censoring

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of the censoring time $C \sim p_C$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. For a finite family of nondegenerate coordinates with consistent standard errors, the simultaneous Wald band covers all estimands with probability tending to $P(|Y_i| \le z\sqrt{v_{g_i}}\ \forall i)$, which lies between $1 - |\iota|(1 - N(0,1)([-z, z]))$ and $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
