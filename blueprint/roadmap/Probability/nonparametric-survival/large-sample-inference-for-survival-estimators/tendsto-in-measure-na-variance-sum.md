# Variance identification from a law of large numbers

For each $n$, let $Y_n$ be an at-risk process and $\alpha_n$ a cumulative hazard on a grid with $k_n$ steps, and $\bar y_n \ge \varepsilon > 0$ deterministic. Suppose the hazard increments are nonnegative, eventually uniformly small and of total at most $L$; $P(\exists i < k_n : |Y_n(i+1)/n - \bar y_n(i+1)| > \delta) \to 0$ for every $\delta > 0$; and $\sum_{i<k_n}\Delta\alpha_n/\bar y_n \to \sigma^2$. Then the Nelson–Aalen variance sums converge in probability to $\sigma^2$.
