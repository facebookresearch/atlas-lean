# Simultaneous fixed-grid Wald band

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ (event time, cause, censoring time on a finite grid) be independent and identically distributed with law $\mu$, with $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$, and consider the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen estimator at grid indices up to $m$; for a coordinate $a$ (a survival or cumulative-incidence value at some $j \le m$), let $v_a$ be its asymptotic variance and $\theta_a$ its plug-in limit. For a finite family $(g_i)_{i\in\iota}$ of coordinates with $v_{g_i} > 0$, there is a jointly Gaussian centered vector $Y$ with $Y_i \sim N(0, v_{g_i})$ such that, for every $z$ and every standard-error vector $s_n$ that is almost everywhere measurable for each $n$ and converges in probability to $(\sqrt{v_{g_i}})_i$, the band $\hat\theta_{n,g_i} \pm zs_{n,i}/\sqrt n$ covers every $\theta_{g_i}$ with probability tending to $P(|Y_i| \le z\sqrt{v_{g_i}}\ \forall i)$, which is at least $1 - |\iota|(1 - N(0,1)([-z, z]))$ and, for a nonempty family, at most $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
