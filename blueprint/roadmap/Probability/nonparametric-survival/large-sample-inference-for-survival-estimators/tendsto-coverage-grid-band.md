# Simultaneous asymptotic coverage of a grid band

On a finite grid, suppose $(\sqrt n(\hat e_n(j) - A_j))_j \Rightarrow Z$ where each $Z_j \sim N(0, v_j)$ with $v_j \ne 0$, and $s_n(j) \to \sqrt{v_j}$ in probability, the vector $s_n$ almost everywhere measurable for each $n$. Then the probability that the band with standard errors $s_n/\sqrt n$ contains $A$ tends to $P(|Z_j| \le z\sqrt{v_j}\text{ for all }j)$.
