# Nelson–Aalen limit with a time-changed Brownian limit

Let $\tau > 0$ and, for each $n$, let a counting process and an at-risk process be observed on the grid of $K_n$ steps of $[0, \tau]$, with deterministic cumulative hazard $\alpha_n$ and measurable processes. Suppose the probability that the risk set is empty at some grid step tends to $0$, and that the normalized Nelson–Aalen martingale paths have almost everywhere measurable continuous companions with tight laws, uniformly close to them in probability, whose finite-dimensional laws converge to those of an almost everywhere measurable continuous process $Z$. If moreover $Z_t = B(\sigma^2(t))$ for all $t$ almost surely, for a standard Brownian motion $B$ and a nondecreasing $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, then the error paths $\sqrt n(\hat A_n - (\alpha_n - \alpha_n(0)))$ converge in distribution to $Z$ in $D([0, \tau])$, $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_s, Z_t) = \sigma^2(\min(s, t))$ and $Z_t \sim N(0, \sigma^2(t))$.
