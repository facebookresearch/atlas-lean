# Nelson–Aalen limit in the Skorokhod space, given the martingale limit

For each $n$, let real-valued measurable processes $N_n$ and $Y_n$ (no counting-process structure is assumed; a counting process and its at-risk process are an application) be observed on the grid of $K_n$ steps of $[0, \tau]$, $\tau > 0$, with a deterministic real sequence $\alpha_n$ as cumulative hazard. Suppose the probability that the risk set is empty at some grid step tends to $0$, and that the normalized Nelson–Aalen martingale paths have almost everywhere measurable continuous companions with tight laws, uniformly close to them in probability, whose finite-dimensional laws converge to those of an almost everywhere measurable continuous process $Z$. Then the error paths $\sqrt n(\hat A_n - (\alpha_n - \alpha_n(0)))$ converge in distribution to $Z$ in $D([0, \tau])$ with the J1 topology.
