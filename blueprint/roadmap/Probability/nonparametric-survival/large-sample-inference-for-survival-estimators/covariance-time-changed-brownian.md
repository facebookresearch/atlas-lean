# Covariance of a time-changed Brownian motion

Let $B$ be a real Brownian motion and $\sigma^2$ nondecreasing on $[0, \tau]$. If the paths of a continuous process $Z$ are almost surely $t \mapsto B(\sigma^2(t))$, then $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_s, Z_t) = \sigma^2(\min(s, t))$.

## Sources
