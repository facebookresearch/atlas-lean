# Greenwood’s estimate as a function of cell proportions

For every $n$, $n\hat V_n(m) = g(\hat p_n)^2\tau^2(\hat p_n)$, where $\hat V_n$ is Greenwood's variance estimate for the first $n$ subjects, and $g$ and $\tau^2$ use the zero-denominator conventions of the [Kaplan–Meier map](km-map.md) and the [Greenwood sum](greenwood-sum.md).
