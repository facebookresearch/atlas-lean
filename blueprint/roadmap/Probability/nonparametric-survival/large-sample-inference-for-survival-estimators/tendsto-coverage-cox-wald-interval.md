# Asymptotic coverage of the Cox Wald interval

For a scalar Cox model with coefficient $\beta$ and estimators $\hat\beta_n$, if $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta) \Rightarrow N(0, v)$ with $v \ne 0$ and $\sqrt{n/I_n(\hat\beta_n)} \to \sqrt v$ in probability ($I_n$ the observed information), almost everywhere measurable for each $n$,, then $\hat\beta_n \pm z/\sqrt{I_n(\hat\beta_n)}$ (with $\sqrt x = 0$ for $x \le 0$ and $1/0 = 0$) covers $\beta$ with probability tending to $N(0, 1)([-z, z])$.
