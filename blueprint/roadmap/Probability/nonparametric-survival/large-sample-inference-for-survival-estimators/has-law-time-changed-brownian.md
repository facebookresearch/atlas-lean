# Marginal law of a time-changed Brownian motion

Let $(B_s)_{s\ge0}$ be a real process with the finite-dimensional laws of standard Brownian motion (no path continuity is assumed) and $\sigma^2 : [0, \tau] \to [0, \infty)$ any function. If $Z$ is a process on $[0, \tau]$ with continuous paths such that, almost surely, $Z_t = B(\sigma^2(t))$ for all $t$ simultaneously, then $Z_t \sim N(0, \sigma^2(t))$ for every $t$.
