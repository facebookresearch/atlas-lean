# Covariance of the joint survival limit equals the influence covariance

If $Y$ is the Gaussian limit of the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen central limit theorem for independent identically distributed subjects with law $\mu$, then $\mathrm{Cov}(Y_a, Y_b)$ is the covariance of the influence coordinates $a, b$ under $\mu$.
