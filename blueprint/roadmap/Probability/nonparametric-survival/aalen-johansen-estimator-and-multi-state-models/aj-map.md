# Joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen map

Up to a horizon $m$, the Aalen–Johansen map sends a cell vector $u$ to the product-limit survivals $\prod_{l\le j}(1 - \sum_cu_{l,c}/u_l)$ and the cumulative incidences $\sum_{l\le j}S(l-)u_{l,c}/u_l$, $j \le m$. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
