# Aalen–Johansen influence coefficient

For a cell vector $u$, the influence coefficient of the cause-$c'$ increment at $l$ for $F_c(j)$ is $\big(\mathbf 1\{l \le j, c' = c\}S(l-) - \sum_{l<l'\le j}\prod_{i<l',\,i\ne l}(1 - h_i)u_{l',c}/u_{l'}\big)/u_l$. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
