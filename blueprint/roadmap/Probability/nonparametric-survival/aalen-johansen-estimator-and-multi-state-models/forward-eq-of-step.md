# Forward equation for the product-integral of a step function

For strictly increasing times $t_j$ and matrices $A_j$, let $\Lambda(u) = \sum_{t_j\le u}A_j$ and $P(s, t) = \prod_{s<t_j\le t}(I + A_j)$ in time order. Every product-integral $M$ of $\Lambda$ over $[s, t]$ satisfies $M = I + \sum_{s<t_j\le t}P(s, t_j-)A_j$.
