# Kaplan–Meier limit under independent competing-risks censoring

Let competing-risks subjects on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J)$ independent of $C$ and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. Then for every $j \le m$, $\sqrt n(\hat S(j) - P(T > j))$ converges in distribution to a centered normal law with the variance of the Kaplan–Meier influence coordinate.
