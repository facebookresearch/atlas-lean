# Joint survival central limit theorem under independent censoring

Let competing-risks subjects on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of $C \sim p_C$ and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. Then $\sqrt n(\hat S(j) - P(T > j), \hat F_c(j) - P(T \le j, J = c))_{c, j\le m}$ converges in distribution to a centered Gaussian vector whose covariance is that of the influence vector of one subject.
