# Entries of any product-integral of competing-risks data

For grouped competing-risks data viewed as multi-state data with a transient state and one absorbing state per cause, let $\hat P$ be the Aalen–Johansen transition matrix and $s$ precede every observation time. For $s \le t$, any product-integral $M$ of the Nelson–Aalen intensity over $[s, t]$ has transient-to-cause-$c$ entry $\hat F_c(t)$ and transient-to-transient entry $\hat S(t)$.
