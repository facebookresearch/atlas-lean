# Covariance of increment sums

For one competing-risks subject with law $\mu$ and centered increments $N_{l,c} = \mathbf 1\{X = l, \delta = 1, J = c\} - \lambda_{l,c}\mathbf 1\{X \ge l\}$, the covariance of $\sum\gamma_{l,c}N_{l,c}$ and $\sum\gamma'_{l,c}N_{l,c}$ is the increment Gram form of $\gamma, \gamma'$ at the mean cell vector.
