# Identification of survival and cumulative incidences

If $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ is independent of $C \sim p_C$ on a finite grid and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$, then the joint Aalen–Johansen map at the mean cell vector equals the survival $P(T > j)$ and the cumulative incidences $P(T \le j, J = c)$ for $j \le m$.
