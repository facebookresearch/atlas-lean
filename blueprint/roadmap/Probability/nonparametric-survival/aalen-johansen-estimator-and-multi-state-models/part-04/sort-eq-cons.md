# The increasing enumeration of a partition starts at the left endpoint

For reals $s$ and $t$, a partition of $[s, t]$ is a finite set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ with $s \in P$, $t \in P$ and $s \le x \le t$ for every $x \in P$.

For all reals $s$ and $t$ and every partition $P$ of $[s, t]$, the list of the elements of $P$ sorted in non-decreasing order begins with $s$: it has the form

$$(s, p_1, \dots, p_q)$$

for some $q \ge 0$ and reals $p_1, \dots, p_q$.
