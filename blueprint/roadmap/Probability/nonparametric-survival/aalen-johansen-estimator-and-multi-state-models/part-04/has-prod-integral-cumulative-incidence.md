# The cumulative incidence is a product-integral entry

For grouped competing-risks data viewed as multi-state data with a transient state and one absorbing state per cause, let $\hat P$ be the Aalen–Johansen transition matrix and $s$ precede every observation time. For $s \le t$, the product-integral of the Nelson–Aalen intensity over $[s, t]$ exists and its entry from the transient state to cause $c$ is the cumulative incidence $\hat F_c(t)$.
