# Partition of an interval by its endpoints and the jump times inside it

For reals $s$ and $t$, a partition of $[s, t]$ is a finite set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ with $s \in P$, $t \in P$ and $s \le x \le t$ for every $x \in P$.

For real times $t_0, \dots, t_{k-1}$ (no ordering is assumed) and reals $s \le t$, the partition of $[s, t]$ by its endpoints and the jump times in $(s, t]$ is the set

$$\{s, t\} \cup \{t_j : 0 \le j \le k-1,\ s < t_j \le t\},$$

which is a partition of $[s, t]$.
