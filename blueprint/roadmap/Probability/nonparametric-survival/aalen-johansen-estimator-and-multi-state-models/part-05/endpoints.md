# Two-point partition of a closed interval

For reals $s$ and $t$, a partition of $[s, t]$ is a finite set $P \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ with $s \in P$, $t \in P$ and $s \le x \le t$ for every $x \in P$.

For reals $s \le t$, the two-point partition of $[s, t]$ is the set

$$\{s, t\},$$

which is a partition of $[s, t]$ ($\{s\}$ when $s = t$).
