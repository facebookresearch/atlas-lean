# Covariance of the joint survival limit

If $Y$ is the Gaussian limit of the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen central limit theorem for independent identically distributed subjects with law $\mu$ and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$, then $\mathrm{Cov}(Y_a, Y_b)$ is the increment Gram form of the influence coefficients of $a$ and $b$ at the mean cell vector.
