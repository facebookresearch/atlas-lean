# Coordinates of the joint Kaplan–Meier and Aalen–Johansen estimator up to a horizon

For natural numbers $k$ and $r$ (grid indices $\{0, \dots, k-1\}$, causes $\{0, \dots, r-1\}$) and a horizon $m \in \{0, \dots, k-1\}$, the coordinate set is

$$\mathrm{Coord}_{k,r}(m) = \big(\{\star\} \sqcup \{0, \dots, r-1\}\big) \times \{j \in \{0, \dots, k-1\} : j \le m\}.$$

The coordinate $(\star, j)$ labels the Kaplan–Meier survival at grid index $j$, and the coordinate $(c, j)$ labels the Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence of cause $c$ at grid index $j$.
