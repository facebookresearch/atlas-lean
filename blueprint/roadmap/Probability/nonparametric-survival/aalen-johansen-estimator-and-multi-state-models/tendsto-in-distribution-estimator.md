# Joint survival central limit theorem with its covariance

Let competing-risks subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed with law $\mu$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$; consider the joint vector of the Kaplan–Meier survival and the Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidences at grid indices $j \le m$. Then $\sqrt n$ times the joint estimator minus its plug-in limit converges in distribution to a Gaussian vector $Y$ such that every $\sum_as_aY_a$ is centered normal with the variance of $\sum_as_a$ (influence coordinate $a$ of one subject).
