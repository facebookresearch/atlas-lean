# Rank vector of a permutation

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, the rank vector of $\sigma$ is

$$r_\sigma = (\sigma(i) + 1)_{i=0}^{n-1} \in \mathbb{N}^n,$$

a vector whose entries are $1, \dots, n$, each exactly once.

## Sources

- J. Hájek, Z. Šidák and P. K. Sen, *Theory of Rank Tests*, 2nd ed., Academic Press (1999).
