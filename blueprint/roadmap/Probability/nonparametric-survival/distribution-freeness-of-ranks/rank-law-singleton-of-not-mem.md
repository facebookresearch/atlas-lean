# The uniform rank law charges only rank vectors

If $r \in \mathbb{N}^n$ is not the rank vector $(\sigma(i) + 1)_i$ of any permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, then the uniform rank law gives $\{r\}$ probability $0$.

## Sources

- J. Hájek, Z. Šidák and P. K. Sen, *Theory of Rank Tests*, 2nd ed., Academic Press (1999).
