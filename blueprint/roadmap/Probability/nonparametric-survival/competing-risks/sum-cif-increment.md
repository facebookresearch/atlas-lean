# The cause-specific increments add up to the Kaplan–Meier jump

In grouped competing-risks data, at each observation time $t_j$,

$$\sum_c \Delta_{j,c} = \hat S(t_j^-) \, \frac{d_j}{Y_j},$$

the jump at $t_j$ of the Kaplan–Meier estimate of the cause-blind censored data, where a ratio with
denominator $0$ is taken to be $0$.
