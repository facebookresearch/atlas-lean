# Mass conservation for the Aalen–Johansen estimates

In grouped competing-risks data,

$$\sum_c \sum_j \Delta_{j,c} + \prod_j \Bigl(1 - \frac{d_j}{Y_j}\Bigr) = 1,$$

where a ratio with denominator $0$ is taken to be $0$. That is, the estimated cumulative incidences of
all causes at the end of follow-up, together with the overall survival left after the last observation
time, add up to $1$.
