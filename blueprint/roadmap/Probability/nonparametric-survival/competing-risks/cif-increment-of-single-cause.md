# Aalen–Johansen increments with a single cause

With a single failure cause, the Aalen–Johansen increment at $t_j$ is the Kaplan–Meier jump
$\hat S(t_j^-) \, d_j / Y_j$ of the cause-blind censored data, where
$\hat S(t_j^-) = \prod_{l < j} (1 - d_l / Y_l)$ and a ratio $d_l / Y_l$ is $0$ when nobody is at risk
($Y_l = 0$, hence $d_l = 0$).
