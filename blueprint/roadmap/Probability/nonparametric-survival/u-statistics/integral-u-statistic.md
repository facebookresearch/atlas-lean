# Unbiasedness

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\alpha$, $m \le n$, and let $h : \alpha^m \to \mathbb{R}$ be integrable for $\mu^{\otimes m}$. If $X_1, \dots, X_n$ are i.i.d. with law $\mu$, the [U-statistic](u-statistic.md) $U_n = U(h; X_1, \dots, X_n)$ satisfies

$$\mathbb{E}[U_n] = \mathbb{E}\, h(X_1, \dots, X_m).$$
