# Degeneracy of a U-statistic kernel

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $m \ge 1$, and $h$ a symmetric square-integrable kernel of $m$ arguments with respect to $\mu^{\otimes m}$; let $h_1(y) = \mathbb{E}[h(y, X_2, \dots, X_m)]$ be its first Hoeffding projection (for $X_j$ i.i.d. $\mu$) and $\zeta_1 = \operatorname{Var}(h_1(X_1))$. Then $\zeta_1 = 0$ if and only if $h_1$ is almost surely constant (equal to its mean).
