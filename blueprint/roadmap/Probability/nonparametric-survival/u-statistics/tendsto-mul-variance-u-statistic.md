# Asymptotic variance of a U-statistic

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\alpha$ and $h : \alpha^m \to \mathbb{R}$ a symmetric kernel that is square integrable under $\mu^{\otimes m}$. For i.i.d. samples of size $n$ from $\mu$, the U-statistic $U_n$ with kernel $h$ satisfies $n \operatorname{Var}(U_n) \to m^2 \operatorname{Var}(h_1(X_1))$ as $n \to \infty$, where $h_1$ is the first Hoeffding projection of $h$.

## Sources

- W. Hoeffding, "A class of statistics with asymptotically normal distribution", Annals of Mathematical Statistics 19 (1948), 293–325, Section 5.
