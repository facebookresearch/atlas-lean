# Variance of a degenerate U-statistic

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $m \ge 1$, and $h$ a symmetric square-integrable kernel of $m$ arguments with respect to $\mu^{\otimes m}$; let $h_1(y) = \mathbb{E}[h(y, X_2, \dots, X_m)]$ be its first Hoeffding projection (for $X_j$ i.i.d. $\mu$) and $\zeta_1 = \operatorname{Var}(h_1(X_1))$. If $\zeta_1 = 0$, then $n\operatorname{Var}(U_n) \to 0$, where $U_n$ is the U-statistic of $h$ on an i.i.d. sample of size $n$ from $\mu$.
