# Hájek projection of a symmetric kernel

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\alpha$ and $h : \alpha^m \to \mathbb{R}$ a symmetric kernel, $h(y \circ \sigma) = h(y)$ for every permutation $\sigma$ of the $m$ arguments. Then the symmetrization in the Hájek projection is the identity, and for every sample $x \in \alpha^n$ the Hájek projection is $\theta + \frac{m}{n} \sum_{i} (h_1(x_i) - \theta)$, where $\theta = \int h \, d\mu^{\otimes m}$ and $h_1$ is the first Hoeffding projection of $h$. For $n = 0$ the factor $m/n$ is $0$ by the convention for division by zero.
