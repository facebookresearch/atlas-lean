# Hájek projection remainder has squared L² norm o(1/n)

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\alpha$ and $h : \alpha^m \to \mathbb{R}$ a symmetric kernel that is square integrable under $\mu^{\otimes m}$. For i.i.d. samples of size $n$ from $\mu$, the U-statistic $U_n$ with kernel $h$ and its Hájek projection $\hat H_n$ satisfy $n\, \mathbb{E}[(U_n - \hat H_n)^2] \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$: the remainder is $o(n^{-1/2})$ in $L^2$.
