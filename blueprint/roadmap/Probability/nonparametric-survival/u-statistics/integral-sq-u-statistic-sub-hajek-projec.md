# Hájek projection: the L² identity

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $m \le n$, and $h$ a kernel of order $m$ that is symmetric under
permutations of its arguments and square-integrable under $\mu^{\otimes m}$. Write $U$ for the
U-statistic of $h$ on $n$ i.i.d. observations with law $\mu$, $\theta = E[h]$, and $h_1$ for the
first-order Hoeffding projection of $h$. With $\hat H$ the Hájek projection of $U$,
$E[(U - \hat H)^2] = \operatorname{Var}(U) - m^2 \operatorname{Var}(h_1) / n$. For $n = 0$ (so $m = 0$) the correction term is $0$ by the convention for division by zero.
