# Exposure Potential eq of mem

Let $e : U \times \Omega \to \Delta$ be an exposure mapping and $y^r : U \times \Omega \to \mathbb R$ randomization potential outcomes, and suppose the exposure mapping is sufficient: $e_i(z) = e_i(z')$ implies $y^r_i(z) = y^r_i(z')$ for every unit $i$ and assignments $z, z'$. Let $y_i(d)$ be the exposure-specific potential outcome ($y^r_i$ at a chosen assignment giving unit $i$ exposure $d$, and $0$ if there is none). Then for every unit $i$, assignment $z$ and exposure $d$ with $e_i(z) = d$,

$$y_i(d) = y^r_i(z).$$
