# The tied term is the hypergeometric variance

For $Y \ge 2$, $Y_1 \le Y$ and $d \le Y$, the tied log-rank variance term equals

$$\sum_{k=0}^{d} \Bigl(k - \frac{d Y_1}{Y}\Bigr)^2 h(k),$$

where $h$ is the law of the number of group-1 subjects among $d$ drawn without replacement from $Y$
subjects of which $Y_1$ are in group 1, and $d Y_1 / Y$ is its mean (Mantel 1966).
