# Factored form of the tied log-rank variance term

For $Y \ge 2$ and any natural numbers $Y_1$ and $d$, the tied log-rank variance term equals
$$d \cdot \frac{Y_1 (Y - Y_1)}{Y^2} \cdot \frac{Y - d}{Y - 1},$$
with the differences taken in the real numbers. Only when $Y_1 \le Y$ and $d \le Y$ do the factors read
as the Breslow contribution of the $d$ tied events and the finite-population correction; otherwise the
factors can be negative.
