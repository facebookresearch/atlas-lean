# Tied log-rank variance term

At one event time with $Y$ subjects at risk, $Y_1$ of them in group 1, and $d$ failures, the tied
log-rank variance term is
$$\frac{d \, Y_1 (Y - Y_1)(Y - d)}{Y^2 (Y - 1)}$$
when $Y \ge 2$, and $0$ when $Y \le 1$; the formula is evaluated in the real numbers. It is the
conditional (hypergeometric) variance of the number of group-1 failures given the margins (Mantel
1966); for $Y \le 1$ that number is deterministic and the term is $0$.
