# Tied log-rank variance term

For natural numbers $Y$, $Y_1$ and $d$, the tied log-rank variance term is

$$\frac{d \, Y_1 (Y - Y_1)(Y - d)}{Y^2 (Y - 1)}$$

when $Y \ge 2$, and $0$ when $Y \le 1$; the formula is evaluated in the real numbers, so it is defined
for all $Y$, $Y_1$ and $d$. At one event time with $Y$ subjects at risk, $Y_1 \le Y$ of them in group 1,
and $d \le Y$ failures, it is the conditional (hypergeometric) variance of the number of group-1 failures
given the margins (Mantel 1966); for $Y \le 1$ that number is deterministic and the term is $0$.
