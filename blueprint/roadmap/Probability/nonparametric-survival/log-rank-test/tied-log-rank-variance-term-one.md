# A single failure gives the Breslow term

For $Y \ge 2$ and $Y_1 \le Y$, the tied log-rank variance term with a single failure, $d = 1$, is
$Y_1 (Y - Y_1) / Y^2$, the summand of the Breslow information at one risk set. The hypothesis
$Y_1 \le Y$ only restricts to valid counts; the identity follows from the factored form.
