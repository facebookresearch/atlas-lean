# Breslow's information overstates the tied variance

If $Y_1 \le Y$ and $1 \le d \le Y$, the tied log-rank variance term is at most
$d \, Y_1 (Y - Y_1) / Y^2$, the Breslow contribution of the $d$ tied events: under ties, Breslow's
information overstates the variance. The hypothesis $d \le Y$ only restricts to valid counts; the
inequality does not need it.
