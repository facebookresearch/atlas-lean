# Map obs RY const Outcome Law

Let $P_{\mathrm{const}} = \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,1)}$ and $P_{\mathrm{sm}} = \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,0)}$ on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ (points $(r, y)$, $r = 1$ for response). Let $o(r, y) = (r, y)$ for $r = 1$ and $o(r, y) = (0, 0)$ for $r = 0$ be the observed-data map. Then the two laws have the same observed-data law:

$$P_{\mathrm{const}} \circ o^{-1} = P_{\mathrm{sm}} \circ o^{-1} \ \Bigl(= \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,0)}\Bigr).$$
