# Self Masking Law

The self-masking (missing-not-at-random) full-data law is the probability measure on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ (points $(r, y)$, $r = 1$ for response) under which $r$ is a fair coin and $y = 1$ exactly on response:

$$P_{\mathrm{sm}} = \tfrac12\, \delta_{(1, 1)} + \tfrac12\, \delta_{(0, 0)},$$

so the nonrespondents are those with $y = 0$.
