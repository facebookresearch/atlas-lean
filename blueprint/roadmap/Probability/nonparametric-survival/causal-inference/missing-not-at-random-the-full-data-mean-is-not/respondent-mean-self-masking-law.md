# Mean imputation is not identification

Under the self-masking law $P = \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,0)}$ on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ (points $(r, y)$, $r = 1$ for response), the respondents' mean is $1$ while the full-data mean is $1/2$:

$$\frac{\int_{\{r = 1\}} y \, dP}{P(r = 1)} = 1, \qquad \int y \, dP = \frac12.$$
