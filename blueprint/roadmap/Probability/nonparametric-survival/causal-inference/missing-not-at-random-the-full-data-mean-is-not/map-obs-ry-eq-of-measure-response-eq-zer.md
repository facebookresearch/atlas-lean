# Map obs RY eq of measure response eq zero

Full-data laws are measures on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$, a point $(r, y)$ recording the response indicator $r$ ($r = 1$ for a respondent) and the outcome $y$. The observed data of $(r, y)$ are $o(r, y) = (r, y)$ if $r = 1$ and $o(r, y) = (0, 0)$ if $r = 0$; the observed-data law of $P$ is the pushforward $P \circ o^{-1}$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be probability measures on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ with $P(r = 1) = Q(r = 1) = 0$. Then they have the same observed-data law:

$$P \circ o^{-1} = Q \circ o^{-1}.$$
