# Shadow variable assumption

Let $\mathcal{Y}$ and $\mathcal{Z}$ be measurable spaces with measurable singletons and let $P$ be a measure on $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\} \times \mathcal{Y} \times \mathcal{Z}$, with coordinates $R$, $Y$, $Z$. $P$ satisfies the shadow variable assumption, $Z \perp R \mid Y$, when for every measurable $B \subseteq \mathcal{Z}$ and every $y \in \mathcal{Y}$

$$P(R = \mathrm{true}, Z \in B, Y = y)\; P(Y = y) = P(R = \mathrm{true}, Y = y)\; P(Z \in B, Y = y),$$

all measures being read as real numbers (an infinite value counts as $0$).
