# Integrable ipw cond Exp of pos

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal F)$, $\mathcal G \subseteq \mathcal F$ a sub-σ-algebra (the always-observed covariate information), and $R \in \mathcal F$ the response event. Write $P(A \mid \mathcal G) = \mathbb E_\mu[\mathbf 1_A \mid \mathcal G]$. Let $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ be integrable, and suppose strong positivity holds: there is $\varepsilon > 0$ with $P(R \mid \mathcal G) \ge \varepsilon$ $\mu$-a.e. Then the inverse-response-weighted outcome

$$\mathbf 1_R(\omega)\, \frac{Y(\omega)}{P(R \mid \mathcal G)(\omega)}$$

is $\mu$-integrable (with $y / 0 = 0$). No missing-at-random assumption is needed.
