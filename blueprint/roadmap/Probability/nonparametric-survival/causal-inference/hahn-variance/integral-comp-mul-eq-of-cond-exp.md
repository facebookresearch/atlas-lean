# Integral comp mul eq of cond Exp

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathcal{X}$ measurable, and write $\mathbb{E}[\,\cdot \mid X]$ for conditional expectation given $\sigma(X)$. Let $f : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable, $k : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$, and $g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ integrable with $f(X)\, g$ integrable. If $\mathbb{E}[g \mid X] = k(X)$ $\mu$-a.e., then

$$\int_\Omega f(X)\, g \, d\mu = \int_\Omega f(X)\, k(X)\, d\mu.$$
