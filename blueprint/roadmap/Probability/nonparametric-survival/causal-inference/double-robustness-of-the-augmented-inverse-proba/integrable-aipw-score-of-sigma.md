# Integrable aipw Score Of Sigma

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mu)$ be a measure space, $T \in \mathcal{F}$, and $Y, Y_0, Y_1, e, g_0, g_1 : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. Assume

- consistency: $Y = \mathbf{1}_T Y_1 + \mathbf{1}_{T^c} Y_0$ $\mu$-a.e.;
- $Y_0, Y_1, g_0, g_1$ are $\mu$-integrable;
- $e$ is almost everywhere strongly measurable and, for some $\varepsilon > 0$, $\varepsilon \le e \le 1 - \varepsilon$ $\mu$-a.e.

Then the AIPW score $\psi(e, g_0, g_1) = g_1 - g_0 + \mathbf{1}_T (Y - g_1)/e - \mathbf{1}_{T^c} (Y - g_0)/(1 - e)$ is $\mu$-integrable.
