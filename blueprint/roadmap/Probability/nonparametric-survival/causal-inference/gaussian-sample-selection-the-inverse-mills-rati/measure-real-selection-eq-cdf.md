# Measure Real selection eq cdf

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $u : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ a standard normal random variable ($u \sim N(0,1)$ under $\mu$) and $c \in \mathbb R$. Then the probit selection probability is the standard normal distribution function at $c$:

$$\mu(\{\omega : -c \le u(\omega)\}) = \Phi(c).$$
