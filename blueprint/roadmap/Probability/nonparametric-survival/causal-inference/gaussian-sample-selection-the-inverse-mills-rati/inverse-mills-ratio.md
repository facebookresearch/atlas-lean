# Inverse Mills Ratio

For $c \in \mathbb R$, the inverse Mills ratio (Heckman's $\lambda$ at $Z = -c$ with unit selection variance) is

$$\lambda(c) = \frac{\varphi(c)}{\Phi(c)},$$

with $\varphi(x) = (2\pi)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/2}$ the standard normal density and $\Phi$ the standard normal distribution function.
