# Integral cond eq integral of mcar

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $R \subseteq \Omega$ measurable, $X : \Omega \to \beta$ with $\beta$ a measurable space, and $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ measurable and integrable. Suppose the outcome is missing completely at random ($\mathbf 1_R$ independent of $(X, Y)$) and $\mu(R) > 0$. Then the mean of $Y$ under the conditional law given response equals its full-data mean:

$$\int Y \, d\mu[\,\cdot \mid R] = \int Y \, d\mu.$$
