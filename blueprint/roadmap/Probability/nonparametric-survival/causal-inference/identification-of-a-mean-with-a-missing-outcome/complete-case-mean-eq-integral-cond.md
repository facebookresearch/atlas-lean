# Complete Case Mean eq integral cond

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $R \subseteq \Omega$ measurable and $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$. Then the complete-case mean is the mean of $Y$ under the conditional law given response:

$$\frac{\int \mathbf 1_R\, Y \, d\mu}{\mu(R)} = \int Y \, d\mu[\,\cdot \mid R],$$

where $\mu[\,\cdot \mid R] = \mu(R)^{-1}\, \mu|_R$ and both sides are $0$ when $\mu(R) = 0$.
