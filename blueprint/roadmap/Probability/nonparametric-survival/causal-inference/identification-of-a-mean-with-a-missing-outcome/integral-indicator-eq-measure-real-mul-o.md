# Integral indicator eq measure Real mul of indep

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $R \subseteq \Omega$ measurable and $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ measurable. Suppose $R$ is independent of $Y$, in the form: for every measurable $B \subseteq \mathbb R$, $\mu(R \cap \{Y \in B\}) = \mu(R)\, \mu(Y \in B)$ (discrete conditional independence given a constant). Then

$$\int \mathbf 1_R\, Y \, d\mu = \mu(R) \int Y \, d\mu.$$
