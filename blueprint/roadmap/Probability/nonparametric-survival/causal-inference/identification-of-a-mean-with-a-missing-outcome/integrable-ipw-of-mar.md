# Integrable ipw of mar

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $R \subseteq \Omega$ measurable, $X : \Omega \to \mathcal X$ measurable with $\mathcal X$ finite with measurable singletons, and $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ measurable and integrable. Let $\pi(x) = \mu(R \cap \{X = x\}) / \mu(X = x)$ (with $\pi(x) = 0$ when $\mu(X = x) = 0$). Suppose the outcome is missing at random given $X$: for every measurable $B \subseteq \mathbb R$ and every $x$, $\mu(R \cap \{Y \in B\} \cap \{X = x\})\, \mu(X = x) = \mu(R \cap \{X = x\})\, \mu(\{Y \in B\} \cap \{X = x\})$; and response positivity holds: there is $\varepsilon > 0$ with $\pi(x) \ge \varepsilon$ whenever $\mu(X = x) \ne 0$. Then the inverse-response-weighted outcome $\mathbf 1_R\, Y / \pi(X)$ is $\mu$-integrable.
