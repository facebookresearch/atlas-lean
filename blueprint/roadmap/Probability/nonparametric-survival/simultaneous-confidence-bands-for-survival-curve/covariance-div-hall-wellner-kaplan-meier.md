# Hall–Wellner transformation of the Kaplan–Meier limit

Let the grid limit $Z$ of the Nelson–Aalen estimator satisfy $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_i, Z_j) = \sigma^2(\min(i, j))$ with $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, and $K = \sigma^2/(1 + \sigma^2)$. If $S_i \ne 0$, then for $i \le j$ the Kaplan–Meier limit $-SZ$ weighted by $S(1 + \sigma^2)$ has covariance $K_i(1 - K_j)$.
