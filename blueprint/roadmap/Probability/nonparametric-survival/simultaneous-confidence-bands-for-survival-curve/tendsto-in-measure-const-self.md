# Convergence in measure of a constant sequence

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space, $P$ a measure on it, $E$ a pseudometric space and $a \in E$. The sequence of constant functions $f_n(\omega) = a$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, converges in $P$-measure to the constant function $a$: for every $\varepsilon > 0$,

$$
P\{\omega : d(f_n(\omega), a) \ge \varepsilon\} \to 0 \quad (n \to \infty).
$$
