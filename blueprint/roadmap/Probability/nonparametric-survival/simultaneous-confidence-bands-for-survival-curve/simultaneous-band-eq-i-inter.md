# The band is the intersection of pointwise intervals

For an estimate $e$, weights $w$, a critical value $c$ and sample size $n$, the simultaneous band is $\{f : |e_i - f_i| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\ \forall i\}$ and the pointwise interval at $i$ is $\{x : |e_i - x| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\}$. Then the simultaneous band is the set of curves whose value at every $i$ lies in the pointwise interval at $i$.
