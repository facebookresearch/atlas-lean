# Asymptotic pointwise coverage

On a finite grid, suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta) \Rightarrow Z$, estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat c_n \to c$ in probability, and let $M = \max_i|Z_i|/w_i$. If $|Z_i|/w_i$ has no atom at $c$, the interval at $i$ covers $\theta_i$ with probability tending to $P(|Z_i|/w_i \le c)$.
