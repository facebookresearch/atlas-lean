# Hall–Wellner covariance in matrix form

Let the grid limit $Z$ of the Nelson–Aalen estimator satisfy $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_i, Z_j) = \sigma^2(\min(i, j))$ with $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, and $K = \sigma^2/(1 + \sigma^2)$. Then the covariance matrix of $(Z_i/(1 + \sigma^2_i))_i$ is the Hall–Wellner covariance matrix.
