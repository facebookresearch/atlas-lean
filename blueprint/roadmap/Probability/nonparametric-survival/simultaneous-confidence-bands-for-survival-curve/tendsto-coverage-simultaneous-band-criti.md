# Asymptotically exact simultaneous coverage

On a nonempty finite grid, suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta) \Rightarrow Z$, estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat c_n \to c$ in probability, with $\hat w_n$ and $\hat c_n$ almost everywhere measurable for every $n$, and let $M = \max_i|Z_i|/w_i$. If $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, $M$ has an atomless law, and $\hat c_n$ converges in probability to the level-$(1 - \alpha)$ critical value of $M$, the simultaneous coverage probability tends to $1 - \alpha$.
