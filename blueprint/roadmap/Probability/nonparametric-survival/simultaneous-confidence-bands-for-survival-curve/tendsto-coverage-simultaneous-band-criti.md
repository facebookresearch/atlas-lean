# Asymptotically exact simultaneous coverage

On a finite grid, suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta) \Rightarrow Z$, estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat c_n \to c$ in probability, and let $M = \max_i|Z_i|/w_i$. If $M$ has an atomless law and $\hat c_n$ converges in probability to the level-$(1 - \alpha)$ critical value of $M$, the simultaneous coverage probability tends to $1 - \alpha$.
