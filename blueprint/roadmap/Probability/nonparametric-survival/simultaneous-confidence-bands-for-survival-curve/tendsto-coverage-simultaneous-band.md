# Asymptotic coverage of a simultaneous band

On a nonempty finite grid, suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta) \Rightarrow Z$, estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat c_n \to c$ in probability, with $\hat w_n$ and $\hat c_n$ almost everywhere measurable for every $n$, and let $M = \max_i|Z_i|/w_i$. If $M$ has no atom at $c$, the probability that $\hat\theta_n \pm \hat c_n\hat w_n/\sqrt n$ covers $\theta$ at every grid point tends to $P(M \le c)$.
