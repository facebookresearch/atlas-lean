# Simultaneous Kaplan–Meier bands under independent censoring

Let subjects $W_i = ((T_i, J_i), C_i)$ on a finite grid be independent and identically distributed, with $(T, J) \sim p_{TJ}$ independent of the censoring time $C \sim p_C$, and $P(\min(T, C) \ge m) \ne 0$. There is a centered Gaussian limit $Y$ on the grid $\{j \le m\}$, with every $\sum_js_jY_j$ normal with the variance of the corresponding combination of Kaplan–Meier influence coordinates, such that for estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat c_n \to c$ in probability, almost everywhere measurable for every $n$, with $P(\max_j|Y_j|/w_j = c) = 0$, the band $\hat S(j) \pm \hat c_n\hat w_{n,j}/\sqrt n$ covers $P(T > j)$ at all $j \le m$ with probability tending to $P(\max_j|Y_j|/w_j \le c)$.
