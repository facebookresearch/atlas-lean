# Hall–Wellner transformation of the Nelson–Aalen limit

Let the grid limit $Z$ of the Nelson–Aalen estimator satisfy $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_i, Z_j) = \sigma^2(\min(i, j))$ with $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, and $K = \sigma^2/(1 + \sigma^2)$. Then for $i \le j$, $\mathrm{Cov}\big(Z_i/(1 + \sigma^2_i), Z_j/(1 + \sigma^2_j)\big) = K_i(1 - K_j)$, the Brownian-bridge covariance.
