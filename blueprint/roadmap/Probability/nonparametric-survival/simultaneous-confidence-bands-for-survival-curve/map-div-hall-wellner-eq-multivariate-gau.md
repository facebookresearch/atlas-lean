# Hall–Wellner transformation at the level of laws

Let the grid limit $Z$ of the Nelson–Aalen estimator satisfy $\mathrm{Cov}(Z_i, Z_j) = \sigma^2(\min(i, j))$ with $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, and $K = \sigma^2/(1 + \sigma^2)$. If $Z$ is a centered Gaussian vector, then $(Z_i/(1 + \sigma^2_i))_i$ is centered Gaussian with the Brownian-bridge covariance at the times $K_i$.
