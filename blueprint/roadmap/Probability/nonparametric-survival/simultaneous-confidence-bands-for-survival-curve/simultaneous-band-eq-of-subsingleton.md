# Bands on a one-point grid

For an estimate $e$, weights $w$, a critical value $c$ and sample size $n$, the simultaneous band is $\{f : |e_i - f_i| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\ \forall i\}$ and the pointwise interval at $i$ is $\{x : |e_i - x| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\}$. On a one-point grid, the simultaneous band is the pointwise interval.
