# Bonferroni bound for band non-coverage

For an estimate $e$, weights $w$, a critical value $c$ and sample size $n$, the simultaneous band is $\{f : |e_i - f_i| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\ \forall i\}$ and the pointwise interval at $i$ is $\{x : |e_i - x| \le cw_i/\sqrt n\}$. On a finite grid, the probability that the band fails to cover $\theta$ is at most the sum over $i$ of the probabilities that the interval at $i$ fails to cover $\theta_i$.
