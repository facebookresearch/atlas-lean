# The two named MAR predicates agree

Let $\Omega$ be a standard Borel space, $\mu$ a finite measure on it, $R \subseteq \Omega$ a measurable response event, $X : \Omega \to \mathcal X$ measurable with $\mathcal X$ countable with measurable singletons, and $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ a measurable real outcome. Then the two forms of missing at random agree: $\mathbf 1_R$ is conditionally independent of $Y$ given $\sigma(X)$ under $\mu$ if and only if for every measurable $B \subseteq \mathbb R$ and every $x \in \mathcal X$,

$$\mu(R \cap \{Y \in B\} \cap \{X = x\})\, \mu(X = x) = \mu(R \cap \{X = x\})\, \mu(\{Y \in B\} \cap \{X = x\}).$$
