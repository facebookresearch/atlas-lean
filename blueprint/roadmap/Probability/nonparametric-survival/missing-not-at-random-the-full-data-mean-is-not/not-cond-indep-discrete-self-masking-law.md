# Not cond Indep Discrete self Masking Law

Under the self-masking law $P = \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,0)}$ on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ (points $(r, y)$), the response event $\{r = 1\}$ is not independent of the outcome $y$, even given the trivial covariate: it is not true that $P(r = 1, y \in B) = P(r = 1)\, P(y \in B)$ for all measurable $B$. Indeed, for $B = \{1\}$,

$$P(r = 1,\ y = 1) = \frac12 \ne \frac14 = P(r = 1)\, P(y = 1).$$
