# Cond Indep Discrete const Outcome Law

Under the constant-outcome law $P = \tfrac12 \delta_{(1,1)} + \tfrac12 \delta_{(0,1)}$ on $\{0,1\} \times \mathbb R$ (points $(r, y)$), the response event $\{r = 1\}$ is independent of the outcome $y$: for every measurable $B \subseteq \mathbb R$,

$$P(r = 1,\ y \in B) = P(r = 1)\, P(y \in B),$$

that is, discrete conditional independence given the trivial covariate.
