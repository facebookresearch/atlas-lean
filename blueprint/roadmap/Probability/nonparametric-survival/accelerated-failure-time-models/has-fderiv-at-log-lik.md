# The regression score is the derivative of the log-likelihood

In the censored log-linear accelerated failure time model, subject $i$ has log follow-up time $y_i$, event indicator $\delta_i$ and covariates $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^p$; with error density $f_0$ (derivative $f_0'$), error survival $S_0$, scale $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and standardized residuals $z_i = (y_i - \langle\beta, x_i\rangle)/\sigma$, the log-likelihood is $\ell(\beta, \sigma) = \sum_i\delta_i(\log f_0(z_i) - \log\sigma) + (1 - \delta_i)\log S_0(z_i)$, and $r(z, \delta)$ is $-f_0'(z)/f_0(z)$ for an event and $f_0(z)/S_0(z)$ for a censored observation. Here $\sigma$ is any real number, with the conventions of [the log-likelihood](log-lik.md) and [the regression score](score.md): division by zero, $0^{-1}$ and $\log 0$ are $0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$; at $\sigma = 0$ every $z_i$ is $0$, $\ell(\cdot, 0)$ is constant and $U_\beta = 0$. Suppose every contribution is regular at $z_i$ ($f_0(z_i) \ne 0$ with derivative $f_0'(z_i)$ for an event, $S_0(z_i) \ne 0$ with $S_0' = -f_0$ there for a censored observation). Then $\ell(\cdot, \sigma)$ is differentiable at $\beta$ with derivative $v \mapsto \langle v, U_\beta\rangle$, $U_\beta = \sigma^{-1}\sum_ir(z_i, \delta_i)x_i$.
