# The zero predictor gives the baseline

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. With $\eta = 0$ the accelerated failure time measure is $\mu_0$.
