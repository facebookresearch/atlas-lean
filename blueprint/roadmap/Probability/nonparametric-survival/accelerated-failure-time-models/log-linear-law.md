# Log-linear law of a failure time

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$, a location $\eta$ and a scale $\sigma$, the log-linear measure is the image of $\mu_0$ under $e \mapsto \eta + \sigma e$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of an error $\varepsilon$, it is the law of $\log T = \eta + \sigma\varepsilon$.
