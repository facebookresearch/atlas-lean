# Observed-data law under independent censoring

For measures $\mu_T$ and $\mu_C$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the observed-data measure is the image of the product measure $\mu_T \otimes \mu_C$ under $(a, c) \mapsto (\min(a, c), \mathbf 1\{a \le c\})$. When $\mu_T$ and $\mu_C$ are the laws of the log failure time $\log T$ and of an independent log censoring time $\log C$, it is the law of $(\min(\log T, \log C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$.
