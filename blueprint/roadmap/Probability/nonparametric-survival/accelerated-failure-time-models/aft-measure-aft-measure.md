# Composition of accelerations

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. Accelerating by $\eta_1$ and then by $\eta_2$ gives the accelerated failure time measure with predictor $\eta_1 + \eta_2$.
