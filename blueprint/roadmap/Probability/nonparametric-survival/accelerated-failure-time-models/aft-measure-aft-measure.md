# Composition of accelerations

For a baseline law $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time law is the law of $e^\eta T_0$ with $T_0 \sim \mu_0$. Accelerating by $\eta_1$ and then by $\eta_2$ gives the accelerated failure time law with predictor $\eta_1 + \eta_2$.
