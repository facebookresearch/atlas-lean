# Identifiability of the log-linear model for one covariate value

Let the error law be finite with strictly decreasing survival function and the log censoring law nonzero and finite. If the log-linear models with $(\eta, \sigma)$ and $(\eta', \sigma')$, $\sigma, \sigma' > 0$, give the same observed-data law, then $\eta = \eta'$ and $\sigma = \sigma'$.
