# Survival transform of an accelerated failure time law

For a measure $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time measure $\mu_\eta$ is the image of $\mu_0$ under $t \mapsto e^\eta t$; when $\mu_0$ is the law of $T_0$, $\mu_\eta$ is the law of $e^\eta T_0$. Then $\mu_\eta(t, \infty) = \mu_0(e^{-\eta}t, \infty)$ in $[0, \infty]$ for every $t$; for a probability law $\mu_0$ this is the survival transform $S(t \mid \eta) = S_0(e^{-\eta}t)$.
