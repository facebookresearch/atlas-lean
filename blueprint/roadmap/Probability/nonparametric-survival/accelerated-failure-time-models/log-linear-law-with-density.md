# Density of the log-linear law

If $\mu_0$ is the measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with measurable density $g : \mathbb{R} \to [0, \infty]$ with respect to Lebesgue measure and $\sigma > 0$, then the log-linear measure, the image of $\mu_0$ under $e \mapsto \eta + \sigma e$, has density $t \mapsto \sigma^{-1}g((t - \eta)/\sigma)$; for an error law $\mu_0$ this is the density of $\log T = \eta + \sigma\varepsilon$.
