# Cumulative-hazard transform of an accelerated failure time law

For a baseline law $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time law is the law of $e^\eta T_0$ with $T_0 \sim \mu_0$. If $S_0(e^{-\eta}t) > 0$, then $S(t \mid \eta) > 0$ and $H(t \mid \eta) = H_0(e^{-\eta}t)$, where $H = -\log S$.
