# Hazard transform of an accelerated failure time law

For a baseline law $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time law is the law of $e^\eta T_0$ with $T_0 \sim \mu_0$. If $S_0(e^{-\eta}t) > 0$ and $H_0 = -\log S_0$ has derivative $h_0$ at $e^{-\eta}t$, then $S(t \mid \eta) > 0$ and the cumulative hazard of the accelerated law has derivative $e^{-\eta}h_0$ at $t$.
