# Identifiability of the log-linear accelerated failure time model

In the model $\log T_i = \langle\beta, x_i\rangle + \sigma\varepsilon_i$ with independent censoring, suppose the error law is finite with strictly decreasing survival, each subject's log censoring law is nonzero and finite and free of the parameters, and the design has full column rank. If $(\beta, \sigma)$ and $(\beta', \sigma')$ with positive scales give the same observed-data law for every subject, then $\beta = \beta'$ and $\sigma = \sigma'$.
