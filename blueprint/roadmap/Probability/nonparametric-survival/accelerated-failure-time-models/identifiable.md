# Identifiability of the log-linear accelerated failure time model

In the model $\log T_i = \langle\beta, x_i\rangle + \sigma\varepsilon_i$ for $n \ge 1$ subjects, let the error measure $\mu_0$ be finite with strictly decreasing survival function $z \mapsto \mu_0(z, \infty)$, let each subject $i$ have a nonzero finite log-censoring measure $\mu_{C,i}$ that does not depend on the parameters, and let the design have full column rank; the observed-data measure of a pair $(\nu, \mu)$ of measures on $\mathbb{R}$ is the image of $\nu \otimes \mu$ under $(a, c) \mapsto (\min(a, c), \mathbf 1\{a \le c\})$. For $\sigma, \sigma' > 0$ let $\nu_i$ and $\nu_i'$ be the images of $\mu_0$ under $e \mapsto \langle\beta, x_i\rangle + \sigma e$ and $e \mapsto \langle\beta', x_i\rangle + \sigma' e$. If the observed-data measures of $(\nu_i, \mu_{C,i})$ and $(\nu_i', \mu_{C,i})$ agree for every subject, then $\beta = \beta'$ and $\sigma = \sigma'$. When $\mu_0$ and the $\mu_{C,i}$ are probability laws, these are the laws of $(\min(\log T_i, \log C_i), \mathbf 1\{T_i \le C_i\})$ under independent censoring.
