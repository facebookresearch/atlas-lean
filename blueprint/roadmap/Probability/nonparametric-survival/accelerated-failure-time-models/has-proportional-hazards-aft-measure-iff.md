# When an accelerated failure time law has proportional hazards

For a baseline law $\mu_0$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the accelerated failure time law is the law of $e^\eta T_0$ with $T_0 \sim \mu_0$. The accelerated law has proportional hazards relative to $\mu_0$ with ratio $c$ if and only if $\mu_0$ is a probability law on $[0, \infty)$, $c > 0$, and $S_0(e^{-\eta}t) = S_0(t)^c$ for every $t \ge 0$.
