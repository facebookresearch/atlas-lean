# Lifetime (D − d0) / r of a positive-rate linear path started below D

For a scenario $\omega$ with rate $r(\omega) > 0$ and initial damage below the threshold,
$d_0(\omega) < D(\omega)$, the
[lifetime](../../../Physics/elasticity-and-structures/fracture-fatigue-damage-and-failure-margins/damage-lifetime.md) of the
[linear damage path](linear-damage.md) $d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t$ against the threshold
$D(\omega)$, the first time $t \ge 0$ at which $d_0(\omega) + r(\omega)\, t \ge D(\omega)$, is

$$\tau(\omega) = \frac{D(\omega) - d_0(\omega)}{r(\omega)},$$

a positive finite value in $[0, \infty]$. The threshold counts as reached when the damage equals it.
The rate must be positive: for $r(\omega) = 0$ and $d_0(\omega) < D(\omega)$ the threshold is never
reached and the lifetime is $\infty$, while the formula would give $0$ by the convention
$x / 0 = 0$.
