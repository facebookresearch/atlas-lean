# Integral cond eq inv mul

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $s \subseteq \Omega$ a set, and $f : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. With $\mu(\cdot \mid s) = \mu(s)^{-1}\, \mu(\cdot \cap s)$ the conditional measure,

$$\int f \, d\mu(\cdot \mid s) = \mu(s)^{-1} \int_s f \, d\mu,$$

where on the right $\mu(s)$ is read as a real number (an infinite value counts as $0$) and $0^{-1} = 0$. No measurability or integrability is assumed; integrals of non-integrable functions are $0$.
