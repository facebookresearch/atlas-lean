# Integral eq sum preimage

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $\mathcal{X}$ be a finite set with measurable singletons, and let $X : \Omega \to \mathcal{X}$ be measurable. For every $\mu$-integrable $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\int Y \, d\mu = \sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \int_{\{X = x\}} Y \, d\mu.$$
