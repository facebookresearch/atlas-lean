# Cond Exp indicator compl

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathcal{X}$ measurable, and write $\mathbb{E}[\,\cdot \mid X]$ for conditional expectation given $\sigma(X)$. Let $T \subseteq \Omega$ be measurable and $e : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{1}_T \mid X] = e(X)$ $\mu$-a.e. Then

$$\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{1}_{T^c} \mid X] = 1 - e(X) \quad \mu\text{-a.e.}$$
