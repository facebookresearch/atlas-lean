# Block randomization is a probability measure

Let $U$ be a finite population, $B_1, \dots, B_m \subseteq U$ blocks and $n_1, \dots, n_m \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n_b \le |B_b|$ for every $b$. Then block randomization, the product over $b$ of complete randomization of $n_b$ treated units within $B_b$, is a probability measure on the tuples $(S_1, \dots, S_m)$ of treated sets $S_b \subseteq B_b$.
