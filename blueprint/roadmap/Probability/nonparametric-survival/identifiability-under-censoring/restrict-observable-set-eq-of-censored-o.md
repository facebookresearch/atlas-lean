# The observed law identifies the failure law on the observable set

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the observed-data measure of $(F, G)$ is the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$, the law of $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$ for independent $T \sim F$ and $C \sim G$ when $F$ and $G$ are probability laws; two measures $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. If $G$ is finite and $F, F'$ give the same observed-data measure, they agree on the observable set of $G$.
