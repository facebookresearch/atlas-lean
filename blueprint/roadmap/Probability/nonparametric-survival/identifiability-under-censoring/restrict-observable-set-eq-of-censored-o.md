# The observed law identifies the failure law on the observable set

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent, with observed data $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$; two failure-time laws $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. If $G$ is finite and $F, F'$ give the same observed-data law, they agree on the observable set of $G$.
