# Parametric identification with an unknown censoring law

Let failure-time probability laws $F_\theta$ and finite censoring laws $G_{\theta,\eta}$ depend arbitrarily on a parameter $\theta$ and a nuisance $\eta$. If $\theta$ is determined by $F_\theta$ on $(-\infty, h]$ and every model has $P(X \ge h) \ne 0$, then the observed-data law determines $\theta$.
