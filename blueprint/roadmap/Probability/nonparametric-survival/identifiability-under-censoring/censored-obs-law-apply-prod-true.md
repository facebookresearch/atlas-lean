# Law of observed failures under censoring

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent, with observed data $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$; two failure-time laws $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. For measurable $A$, $P(T \wedge C \in A, T \le C) = \int_AG[t, \infty)\,dF(t)$.
