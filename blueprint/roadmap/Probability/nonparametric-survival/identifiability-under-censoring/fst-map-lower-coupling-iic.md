# The lower coupling attains the lower bound

For a measure $P$ on $\mathbb{R} \times \{0, 1\}$, the image $\mu$ of $P$ under the lower coupling at $t$ satisfies $\mu((-\infty, t] \times \mathbb{R}) = P((-\infty, t] \times \{1\})$, the Peterson lower bound; for an observed-data law $P$ this reads $P(T \le t) = P(X \le t, \delta = 1)$ under the coupling.
