# Redistributing mass beyond the censoring horizon on a finite grid preserves the observed law

Let the event time $T \sim p_T$ and censoring time $C \sim p_C$ on a finite grid be independent, with observed data $(\min(T, C), \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $p_C$ puts no mass after $h$ and two event-time laws agree at every $t \le h$, they give the same observed-data law.
