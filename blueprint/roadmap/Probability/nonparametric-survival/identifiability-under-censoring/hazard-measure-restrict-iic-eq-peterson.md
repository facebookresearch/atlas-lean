# Peterson representation of the hazard

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent with observed-data law $P$ of $(X, \delta) = (T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $F, G$ are finite and $P(X \ge h) \ne 0$, then on $(-\infty, h]$ the hazard of $F$ is $d\Lambda(s) = P(X \in ds, \delta = 1)/P(X \ge s)$.
