# Exact identification under independent censoring

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent, with observed data $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$; two failure-time laws $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. For probability laws $F, F'$ and finite $G$, the observed-data laws agree if and only if $F$ and $F'$ agree on $\{s : G[s, \infty) \ne 0\}$.
