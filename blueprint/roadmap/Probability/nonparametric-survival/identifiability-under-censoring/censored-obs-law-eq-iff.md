# Exact identification under independent censoring

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, the observed-data measure of $(F, G)$ is the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$, the law of $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$ for independent $T \sim F$ and $C \sim G$ when $F$ and $G$ are probability laws. For probability measures $F, F'$ and a finite measure $G$, the observed-data measures of $(F, G)$ and $(F', G)$ agree if and only if $F$ and $F'$ agree on $\{s : G[s, \infty) \ne 0\}$.
