# The lower coupling reproduces the observed law

For every measure $P$ on $\mathbb{R} \times \{0, 1\}$ and every $t$, the image of $P$ under the lower coupling at $t$ is a measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ whose observed-data measure is $P$; for an observed-data law $P$ it is a joint law of $(T, C)$ producing $P$.
