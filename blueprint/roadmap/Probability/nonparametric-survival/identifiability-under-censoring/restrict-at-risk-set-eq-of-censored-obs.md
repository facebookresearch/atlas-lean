# Identification on the whole at-risk set

For probability measures $F, F'$ and finite measures $G, G'$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let the observed-data measures of $(F, G)$ and $(F', G')$, the images of $F \otimes G$ and $F' \otimes G'$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$, both equal $P$. Then $F$ and $F'$ agree on the at-risk set $\{s : P([s, \infty) \times \{0, 1\}) \ne 0\}$; when $G$ and $G'$ are probability laws, $P$ is the law of $(X, \delta) = (T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$ and this set is $\{s : P(X \ge s) \ne 0\}$.
