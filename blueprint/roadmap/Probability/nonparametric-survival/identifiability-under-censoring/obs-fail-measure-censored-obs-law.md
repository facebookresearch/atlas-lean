# Observed failures under independent censoring

For measures $F$ and $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let $P$ be the observed-data measure, the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$; when $F$ and $G$ are the probability laws of independent $T$ and $C$, $P$ is the law of $(X, \delta) = (T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$. If $G$ is s-finite, the observed-failure measure $A \mapsto P(A \times \{1\})$ is $G[s, \infty)\,dF(s)$, the measure with density $s \mapsto G[s, \infty)$ with respect to $F$; for probability laws this is $P(X \in ds, \delta = 1) = G[s, \infty)\,dF(s)$.
