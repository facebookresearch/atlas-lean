# The cumulative hazard is identified up to the horizon

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent, with observed data $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$; two failure-time probability laws $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. If $G$ is finite with $G[h, \infty) \ne 0$ and the observed-data laws agree, then $\Lambda(-\infty, t] = \Lambda'(-\infty, t]$ for every $t \le h$.
