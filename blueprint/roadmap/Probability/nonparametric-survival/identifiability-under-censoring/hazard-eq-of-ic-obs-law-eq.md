# Identification of the hazard under interval censoring

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. If two event-time laws give the same observed-data law, $j$ is inspected or is the last grid point, and $j - 1$ is inspected or $j = 0$, then they have the same hazard at $j$.
