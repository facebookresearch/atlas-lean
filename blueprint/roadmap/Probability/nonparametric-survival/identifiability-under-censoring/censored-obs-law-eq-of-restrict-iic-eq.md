# Nothing beyond the horizon is identified

Let the failure time $T \sim F$ and censoring time $C \sim G$ be independent, with observed data $(T \wedge C, \mathbf 1\{T \le C\})$; two failure-time laws $F, F'$ are compared under the same $G$. If $G(h, \infty) = 0$ and probability laws $F, F'$ agree on $(-\infty, h]$, they give the same observed-data law, however they distribute $P(T > h)$.
