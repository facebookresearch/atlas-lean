# Characterization of hazard identifiability under interval censoring

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. The observed-data law determines the hazard at $j$ for every pair of event-time laws if and only if $j$ is inspected or is the last grid point, and $j - 1$ is inspected or $j = 0$.
