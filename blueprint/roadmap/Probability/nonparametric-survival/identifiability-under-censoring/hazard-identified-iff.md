# Characterization of hazard identifiability under interval censoring

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. Say that a grid point $c$ is inspected with positive probability when $P(c \in S) > 0$. The observed-data law determines the hazard at $j$ for every pair of event-time laws if and only if $P(j \in S) > 0$ or $j$ is the last grid point, and $P(j - 1 \in S) > 0$ or $j = 0$.
