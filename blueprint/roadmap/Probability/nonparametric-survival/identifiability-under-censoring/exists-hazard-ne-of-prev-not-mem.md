# A previous time inspected with probability zero leaves the hazard unidentified

Under interval censoring on a finite grid, an inspection set $S$ with law $p_S$ is independent of the event time $T \sim p_T$, and one observes $S$ together with the inspection times in $S$ at or after $T$. If $j \ge 1$ and $P(j - 1 \in S) = 0$, there are event-time laws with the same observed-data law and different hazards at $j$.
