# Peterson lower bound under independent censoring

For an s-finite measure $F$ and a probability measure $G$ on $\mathbb{R}$, let $P$ be the observed-data measure of $(F, G)$, the image of $F \otimes G$ under $(t, c) \mapsto (t \wedge c, \mathbf 1\{t \le c\})$. Then $P((-\infty, t] \times \{1\}) \le F(-\infty, t]$ in $[0, \infty]$; for independent $T \sim F$ and $C \sim G$ with $F$ a probability law, this is $P(X \le t, \delta = 1) \le P(T \le t)$.
