# Numbers at risk agree when everyone enters first

Consider left-truncated survival data on increasing grid times $t_0 < \dots < t_{k-1}$: subject $i$ enters at a grid index $e_i$ (or before $t_0$), leaves at $t_{x_i}$ with $e_i < x_i$, with or without an event; $d_j$ and $c_j$ are the events and censorings at $t_j$ and $Y_j = \#\{i : e_i < j \le x_i\}$ the number at risk. If every subject entered before $t_0$, then the delayed-entry numbers at risk equal those without entry times at every grid time.
