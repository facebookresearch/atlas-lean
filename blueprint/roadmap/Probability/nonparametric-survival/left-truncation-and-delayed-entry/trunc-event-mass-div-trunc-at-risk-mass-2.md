# Identification of the hazard from the sampled population

Let the event time $T \sim p_T$, censoring time $C \sim p_C$ and entry time $L \sim p_L$ on a finite grid be mutually independent, and $X = \min(T, C)$. If $P(L < j \le X) \ne 0$, then among sampled subjects (conditional on $L < X$) the ratio of the truncated event mass to the delayed-entry at-risk mass at $j$ is $P(T = j)/P(T \ge j)$.
