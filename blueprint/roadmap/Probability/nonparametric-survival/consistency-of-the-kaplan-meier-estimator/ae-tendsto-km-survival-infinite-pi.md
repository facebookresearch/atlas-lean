# Consistency of Kaplan–Meier on the canonical sequence space

Give the space of sequences $\omega = (\omega_0, \omega_1, \dots)$ of pairs in the grid
$\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$ the infinite product of the law of the independent censoring model, and let
subject $i$ be the coordinate $Z_i(\omega) = \omega_i$. If $\Pr[\min(T, C) \ge m] \ne 0$, then almost
surely the Kaplan–Meier estimate of the first $n$ subjects at the grid time $m$ converges to
$\Pr[T > m]$.
