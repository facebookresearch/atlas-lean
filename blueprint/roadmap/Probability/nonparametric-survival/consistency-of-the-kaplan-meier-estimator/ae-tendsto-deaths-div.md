# Strong law for the death proportions

Let $Z_i = (T_i, C_i)$ be independent random pairs in a finite grid with a common law $\mu$, and let
$d_j(n)$ be the number of deaths at the grid point $j$ in the empirical data of the first $n$ subjects.
Then almost surely

$$\frac{d_j(n)}{n} \to \Pr[\min(T, C) = j, \ T \le C] \quad (n \to \infty),$$

where $(T, C)$ has law $\mu$. No independence between $T$ and $C$ is assumed.
