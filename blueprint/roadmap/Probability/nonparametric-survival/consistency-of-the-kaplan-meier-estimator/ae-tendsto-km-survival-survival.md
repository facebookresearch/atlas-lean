# Consistency of the Kaplan–Meier estimator at a grid time, survival form

Let the subjects $Z_i = (T_i, C_i)$ be independent, each with the law of the independent censoring
model on the grid $\{0, 1, \dots, k - 1\}$. If $\Pr[\min(T, C) \ge m] \ne 0$, then almost surely the
Kaplan–Meier estimate $\hat S_n(m)$ of the first $n$ subjects at the grid time $m$ converges to
$\Pr[T > m]$.
