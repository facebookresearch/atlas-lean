# Strong law for the at-risk proportions

Let $Z_i = (T_i, C_i)$ be independent random pairs in a finite grid with a common law $\mu$, and let
$Y_j(n)$ be the number at risk at the grid point $j$ in the empirical data of the first $n$ subjects.
Then almost surely
$$\frac{Y_j(n)}{n} \to \Pr[\min(T, C) \ge j] \quad (n \to \infty),$$
where $(T, C)$ has law $\mu$. No independence between $T$ and $C$ is assumed.
