# Kaplan–Meier estimate of the empirical data

In the empirical data of the first $n$ subjects, the Kaplan–Meier estimate at the grid time $m$ is

$$\hat S(m) = \prod_{j \le m} \Bigl(1 - \frac{d_j}{Y_j}\Bigr),$$

the product of the Kaplan–Meier factors over the grid points $j \le m$, where a ratio with denominator
$0$ is taken to be $0$.
