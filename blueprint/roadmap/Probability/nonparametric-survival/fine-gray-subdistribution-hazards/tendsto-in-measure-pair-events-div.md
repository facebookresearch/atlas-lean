# Law of large numbers for paired event counts

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. Then the number of cause-$c$ events in the first $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ pairs divided by $n$ converges in probability to $(p_a + p_b)/2$.
