# Weighted Fine–Gray partial likelihood

The inverse-probability-of-censoring weighted Fine–Gray partial likelihood is $\prod_{i:\varepsilon_i=c}e^{\langle\beta, x_i\rangle}/\sum_lw_l(X_i)e^{\langle\beta, x_l\rangle}$ with the Fine–Gray weights $w_l$. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.

## Sources

- J. P. Fine and R. J. Gray, "A proportional hazards model for the subdistribution of a competing risk", J. Amer. Statist. Assoc. 94 (1999), 496–509.
