# Subdistribution hazard of the explicit Fine–Gray family

Consider the family in which, given covariates $x$, a latent $\omega \sim \mathrm{Exp}(e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle})$ gives failure time $\omega/(1 - \omega)$ from the cause of interest if $\omega < 1$ and time $1$ from a competing cause otherwise. At every $t \ge 0$ the subdistribution hazard of the cause of interest is $e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle}/(1 + t)^2$.
