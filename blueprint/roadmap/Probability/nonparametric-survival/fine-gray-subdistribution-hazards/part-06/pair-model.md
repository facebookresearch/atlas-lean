# Infinite product model for the pairs

Let $\mu_0$ be a measure on a measurable space $S$. The paired product model is the measure on the sequence space $S^{\mathbb{N}}$, with the product $\sigma$-algebra, defined as follows.

- If $\mu_0$ is a probability measure, it is the infinite product measure $\bigotimes_{k \in \mathbb{N}} \mu_0$: the measure that assigns to every measurable cylinder $\{\omega : \omega_k \in A_k \text{ for } k \in F\}$, with $F \subset \mathbb{N}$ finite and each $A_k \subseteq S$ measurable, the mass $\prod_{k \in F} \mu_0(A_k)$.
- Otherwise it is the zero measure.
