# Cause-of-interest event mass of a status map

Let $S$ be a measurable space, $\mu_0$ a measure on $S$, $\iota$ a set of causes with cause of interest $c \in \iota$, and $\varepsilon$ a map from $S$ to $\iota \cup \{\star\}$ ($\star$ meaning censored). The cause-of-interest event mass of $\varepsilon$ is

$$
p(\varepsilon) = \mu_0\bigl(\{x \in S : \varepsilon(x) = c\}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R},
$$

where the measure of a set that need not be measurable is its outer measure, and an infinite value is read as $0$. In the paired design the two status maps $\varepsilon_a, \varepsilon_b$ give $p_a = p(\varepsilon_a)$ and $p_b = p(\varepsilon_b)$.
