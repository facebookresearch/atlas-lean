# Information limit of the paired design

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. The information limit is $i(b) = \frac{p_a + p_b}{2}m(b)(1 - m(b))$.
