# Weighted sup of a rank-one vector with matching weights

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty index set. For weights $w$ and a vector $u$ on $\iota$, the [weighted sup statistic](../../simultaneous-confidence-bands-for-survival-curve/weighted-sup.md) is $\max_{j \in \iota} |u_j / w_j|$.

Let $g : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $g_j \ne 0$ for every $j$. For every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the weighted sup of the vector $(g_j t)_{j}$ with weights $(|g_j|)_{j}$ is

$$
\max_{j \in \iota} \left| \frac{g_j t}{|g_j|} \right| = |t|.
$$
