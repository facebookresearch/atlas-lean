# The paired Fine–Gray information

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. For every censoring weight function, $I_n(b)$ equals the number of cause-$c$ events in the complete pairs times $m(b)(1 - m(b))$.
