# Left limits at continuity points of the cumulative incidence

Let $T$ be a failure time and $J$ a failure cause on a finite measure space with measure $P$, $F_c(t) = P(T \le t, J = c)$ the cumulative incidence of cause $c$ and $F_c(t-) = P(T < t, J = c)$. If $F_c$ is continuous at $t$, then $F_c(t-) = F_c(t)$.
