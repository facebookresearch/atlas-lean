# Covariance of the Fine–Gray cumulative-incidence limit

On a finite measure space, for a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. For square-integrable $Z = (Z_\beta, Z_\Lambda)$ and $Y = DF(b, \Lambda)Z$, $\mathrm{Cov}(Y_i, Y_j) = s_is_j\big(x^2\Lambda_i\Lambda_j\mathrm{Var}Z_\beta + x\Lambda_i\mathrm{Cov}(Z_\beta, Z_{\Lambda,j}) + x\Lambda_j\mathrm{Cov}(Z_{\Lambda,i}, Z_\beta) + \mathrm{Cov}(Z_{\Lambda,i}, Z_{\Lambda,j})\big)$.
