# Closed-form cumulative incidence of the proportional subdistribution hazards family

For $\theta, t \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$
\phi(\theta, t) = 1 - \exp\left(-\theta \frac{t}{1+t}\right),
$$

with real division ($a/0 = 0$), so $\phi(\theta, -1) = 0$. With $\theta = e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle}$ it agrees with the cumulative incidence of the family for $t \ge 0$, as stated in the [closed-form identity](cif-ph-measure-of-nonneg.md).

## Sources

- J. P. Fine and R. J. Gray, "A proportional hazards model for the subdistribution of a competing risk", J. Amer. Statist. Assoc. 94 (1999), 496–509, Section 2.
