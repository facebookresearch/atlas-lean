# The cause of interest occurs in the explicit family

Consider the family in which, given covariates $x$, a latent $\omega \sim \mathrm{Exp}(e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle})$ gives failure time $\omega/(1 - \omega)$ from the cause of interest if $\omega < 1$ and time $1$ from a competing cause otherwise. For every $t > 0$, the cumulative incidence of the cause of interest is positive.
