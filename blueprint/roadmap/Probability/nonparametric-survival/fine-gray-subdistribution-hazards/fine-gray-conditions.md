# Fine–Gray regularity conditions

The Fine–Gray conditions for a scalar covariate ask for nonnegative censoring weights, $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, uniform convergence in probability of $I_n(b)/n$ to $i(b)$ on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ with $r > 0$, continuity of $i$ at $\beta_0$, and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$.
