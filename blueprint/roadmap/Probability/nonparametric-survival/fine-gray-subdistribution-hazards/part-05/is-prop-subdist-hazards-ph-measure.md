# The explicit family has proportional subdistribution hazards

Consider the family in which, given covariates $x$, a latent $\omega \sim \mathrm{Exp}(e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle})$ gives failure time $\omega/(1 - \omega)$ from the cause of interest if $\omega < 1$ and time $1$ from a competing cause otherwise. Then the family satisfies the proportional subdistribution hazards model with baseline $\lambda_0(t) = 1/(1 + t)^2$ and coefficient $\beta$.
