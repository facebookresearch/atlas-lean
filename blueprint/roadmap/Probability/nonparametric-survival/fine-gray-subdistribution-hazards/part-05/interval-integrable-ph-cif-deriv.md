# Integrability of the derivative of the closed-form cumulative incidence on a compact interval

For $\theta, t \in \mathbb{R}$ let

$$
\phi(\theta, t) = 1 - \exp\left(-\theta \frac{t}{1+t}\right), \qquad \phi'(\theta, t) = \frac{\theta}{(1+t)^2} \exp\left(-\theta \frac{t}{1+t}\right),
$$

as in the [closed-form cumulative incidence](../part-01/ph-cif.md) and [its derivative](../part-01/ph-cif-deriv.md), with real division ($a/0 = 0$).

For every $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$ and every $t \ge 0$, the function $s \mapsto \phi'(\theta, s)$ is integrable with respect to Lebesgue measure on the interval $[0, t]$.
