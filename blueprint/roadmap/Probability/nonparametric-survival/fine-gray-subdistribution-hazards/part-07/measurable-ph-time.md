# Measurability of the failure time in the proportional subdistribution hazards family

On $\mathbb{R}$ with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra, let

$$
T(\omega) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\omega}{1-\omega} & \text{if } \omega < 1,\\ 1 & \text{if } \omega \ge 1 \end{cases}
$$

be the failure time of the [proportional subdistribution hazards family](ph-time.md). Then $T : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is Borel measurable.
