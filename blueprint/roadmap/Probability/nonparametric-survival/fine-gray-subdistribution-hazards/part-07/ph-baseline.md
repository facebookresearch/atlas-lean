# Baseline subdistribution hazard of the proportional subdistribution hazards family

The baseline subdistribution hazard of the family is the function $\lambda_0 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$
\lambda_0(t) = \frac{1}{(1+t)^2},
$$

with real division, so $\lambda_0(-1) = 0$. The family has proportional subdistribution hazards with this baseline, as stated in [the proportional subdistribution hazards property of the family](../part-05/is-prop-subdist-hazards-ph-measure.md).
