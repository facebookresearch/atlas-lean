# Asymptotically exact coverage of the Fine–Gray band

For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. On probability spaces, suppose $\sqrt n((\hat\beta_n, \hat\Lambda_n) - (\beta_0, \Lambda_0)) \Rightarrow Z$ on a nonempty finite grid with $\hat\beta_n, \hat\Lambda_n$ almost everywhere measurable, and that almost everywhere measurable weights $\hat w_n$ converge in probability to positive weights $w$. Let $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, let the weighted sup $\sup_jw_j^{-1}|(DF(\beta_0, \Lambda_0)Z)_j|$ (the weighted-sup statistic of the limit) have an atomless law, and let almost everywhere measurable $\hat q_n$ converge in probability to its level-$(1 - \alpha)$ critical value. Then the probability that the simultaneous band around $F(\hat\beta_n, \hat\Lambda_n)$ with weights $\hat w_n$ and critical value $\hat q_n$ contains $F(\beta_0, \Lambda_0)$ tends to $1 - \alpha$.
