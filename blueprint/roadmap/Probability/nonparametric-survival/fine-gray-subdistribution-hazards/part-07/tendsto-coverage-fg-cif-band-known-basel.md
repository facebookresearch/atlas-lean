# Fine–Gray cumulative-incidence band with a known baseline

Assume the Fine–Gray conditions: nonnegative weights, $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ ($r > 0$) in probability, $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$; let $\hat\beta_n$ maximize the weighted Fine–Gray criterion whenever a maximizer exists. For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. On a nonempty finite grid, with $\Lambda_0$ known, $\hat\beta_n$ almost everywhere measurable, and estimated weights $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and critical values $\hat q_n \to q$ in probability, almost everywhere measurable for every $n$, the band $F(\hat\beta_n, \Lambda_0) \pm \hat q_n\hat w_n/\sqrt n$ covers $F(\beta_0, \Lambda_0)$ at every grid point with probability tending to $P(\max_j|s_jx\Lambda_{0j}Z/v|/w_j \le q)$, if that limit has no atom at $q$.
