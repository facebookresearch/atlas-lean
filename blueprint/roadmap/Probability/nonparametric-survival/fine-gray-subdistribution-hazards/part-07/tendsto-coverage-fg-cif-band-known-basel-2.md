# Known-baseline Fine–Gray band with natural weights

Assume the Fine–Gray conditions: nonnegative weights, $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ ($r > 0$) in probability, $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$; let $\hat\beta_n$ maximize the weighted Fine–Gray criterion whenever a maximizer exists. For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. On a finite nonempty grid, let $\hat w_n$ be estimated weights and $\hat q_n$ estimated critical values, and let $\hat\beta_n$, $\hat w_n$ and $\hat q_n$ be almost everywhere measurable. If $x \ne 0$, $\Lambda_{0j} \ne 0$ for every $j$, $\hat w_n \to (|s_jx\Lambda_{0j}|)_j$ in probability, $\hat q_n \to q$ in probability, and $P(|Z/v| = q) = 0$, then the probability that the band with centres $F_j(\hat\beta_n, \Lambda_0)$, weights $\hat w_n$ and critical value $\hat q_n$ covers $(F_j(\beta_0, \Lambda_0))_j$ tends to $P(|Z/v| \le q)$, the same as for a single pointwise interval.
