# Positivity of the paired score variance

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. If with positive probability the covariate-$1$ member fails from cause $c$ and the covariate-$0$ member does not, then $\sigma^2 > 0$.
