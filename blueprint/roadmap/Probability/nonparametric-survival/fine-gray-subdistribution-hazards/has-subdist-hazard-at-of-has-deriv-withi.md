# The subdistribution hazard from a right derivative

Let $T$ be a failure time and $J$ a failure cause on a probability space, $F_c(t) = P(T \le t, J = c)$ the cumulative incidence of cause $c$ and $F_c(t-) = P(T < t, J = c)$. If $s \mapsto F_c(s-)$ has right derivative $f$ at $t$, then $\lambda^*_c(t) = f/(1 - F_c(t-))$, with the conventions $P[\cdot \mid \emptyset] = 0$ and $x/0 = 0$ when $F_c(t-) = 1$.

## Sources

- J. P. Fine and R. J. Gray, "A proportional hazards model for the subdistribution of a competing risk", J. Amer. Statist. Assoc. 94 (1999), 496–509.
