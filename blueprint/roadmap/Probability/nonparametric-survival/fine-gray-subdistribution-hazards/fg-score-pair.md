# The paired Fine–Gray score is a sum over pairs

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. For every censoring weight function, the Fine–Gray score of the first $n$ subjects is the sum of the score contributions of the $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ complete pairs.
