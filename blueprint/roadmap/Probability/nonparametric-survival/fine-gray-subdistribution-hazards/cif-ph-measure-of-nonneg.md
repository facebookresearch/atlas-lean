# Cumulative incidence of the explicit Fine–Gray family

Consider the family in which, given covariates $x$, a latent $\omega \sim \mathrm{Exp}(e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle})$ gives failure time $\omega/(1 - \omega)$ from the cause of interest if $\omega < 1$ and time $1$ from a competing cause otherwise. For $t \ge 0$, the cumulative incidence of the cause of interest is $1 - \exp(-e^{\langle\beta, x\rangle}t/(1 + t))$.
