# Existence of the Fine–Gray estimator with probability tending to one

Assume the Fine–Gray conditions: nonnegative weights, $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ ($r > 0$) in probability, $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$; let $\hat\beta_n$ maximize the weighted Fine–Gray criterion whenever a maximizer exists. Then the probability that the weighted Fine–Gray criterion has no maximizer tends to $0$.
