# Asymptotic normality of the Fine–Gray estimator in the paired design

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. Let the events that each member fails from cause $c$ be measurable, let the censoring weights $G$ be nonnegative, and let $\sigma^2 = \mathbb{E}\big[(\text{pair score at }\beta_0)^2\big]/2$, $v = \frac{p_a + p_b}{2}m(\beta_0)(1 - m(\beta_0))$ and $Z \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$. If $e^{\beta_0}p_b = p_a$ with $p_b > 0$, then any maximizer $\hat\beta_n$ of the weighted Fine–Gray criterion (whenever one exists), measurable, satisfies $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0) \Rightarrow Z/v \sim N(0, \sigma^2/v^2)$.
