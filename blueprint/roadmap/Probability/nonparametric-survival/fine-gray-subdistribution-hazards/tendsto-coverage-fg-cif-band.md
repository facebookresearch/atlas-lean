# Asymptotic coverage of the Fine–Gray cumulative-incidence band

For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. Suppose the grid is finite and nonempty, $\sqrt n((\hat\beta_n, \hat\Lambda_n) - (\beta_0, \Lambda_0)) \Rightarrow Z$, $\hat w_n \to w > 0$ and $\hat q_n \to q$ in probability, and $\hat\beta_n$, $\hat\Lambda_n$, $\hat w_n$ and $\hat q_n$ are almost everywhere measurable for every $n$. If $\max_j|Y_j|/w_j$ has no atom at $q$ for $Y = DF(\beta_0, \Lambda_0)Z$, then the band $F(\hat\beta_n, \hat\Lambda_n) \pm \hat q_n\hat w_n/\sqrt n$ covers $F(\beta_0, \Lambda_0)$ at every grid point with probability tending to $P(\max_j|Y_j|/w_j \le q)$.
