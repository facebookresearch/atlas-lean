# Known-baseline Fine–Gray band with natural weights

Assume the Fine–Gray conditions: nonnegative weights, $U_n(\beta_0)/\sqrt n \Rightarrow Z$, $I_n(b)/n \to i(b)$ uniformly on $[\beta_0 - r, \beta_0 + r]$ ($r > 0$) in probability, $i$ continuous at $\beta_0$ and $i(\beta_0) = v \ne 0$; let $\hat\beta_n$ maximize the weighted Fine–Gray criterion whenever a maximizer exists. For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. If $x \ne 0$, $\Lambda_{0j} \ne 0$ and the weights converge to $|s_jx\Lambda_{0j}|$, the limiting simultaneous coverage of the known-baseline band is $P(|Z/v| \le q)$, the same as for a single pointwise interval.
