# Gaussian joint limits give Gaussian cumulative-incidence limits

For a scalar covariate value $x$, coefficient $b$ and baseline cumulative subdistribution hazards $\Lambda_j$ on a grid, let $F_j(b, \Lambda) = 1 - \exp(-e^{bx}\Lambda_j)$ and $s_j = (1 - F_j)e^{bx}$. If $Z$ is a Gaussian random element of $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}^{\text{grid}}$, then $DF(b, \Lambda)Z$ is Gaussian.
