# Limit variance of the paired Fine–Gray score

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. The limit variance of the normalized score is $\sigma^2 = \mathbb{E}\big[(\text{pair score at }\beta_0)^2\big]/2$.
