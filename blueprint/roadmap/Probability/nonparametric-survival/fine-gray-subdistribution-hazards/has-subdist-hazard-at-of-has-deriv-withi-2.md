# The subdistribution hazard of a continuous cumulative incidence

Let $T$ be a failure time and $J$ a failure cause on a probability space, $F_c(t) = P(T \le t, J = c)$ the cumulative incidence of cause $c$ and $F_c(t-) = P(T < t, J = c)$. If $F_c$ is continuous at every point of some interval $[t, t + \delta)$, including $t$, and has right derivative $f$ at $t$, then $\lambda^*_c(t) = f/(1 - F_c(t))$, with the conventions $P[\cdot \mid \emptyset] = 0$ and $x/0 = 0$ when $F_c(t) = 1$.
