# The Fine–Gray conditions hold in the paired design

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. If $e^{\beta_0}p_b = p_a$ with $p_b > 0$, then for any $r > 0$, nonnegative censoring weights, $i(b) = \frac{p_a + p_b}{2}m(b)(1 - m(b))$, $v = i(\beta_0)$ and $Z \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$, the Fine–Gray conditions hold.
