# The subdistribution-hazard quotient in terms of the cumulative incidence

Let $T$ be a failure time and $J$ a failure cause on a probability space, $F_c(t) = P(T \le t, J = c)$ the cumulative incidence of cause $c$ and $F_c(t-) = P(T < t, J = c)$. For $h \ge 0$, the difference quotient equals $\big(F_c((t + h)-) - F_c(t-)\big)/\big(h(1 - F_c(t-))\big)$.
