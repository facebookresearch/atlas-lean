# Score contribution of a pair

In the paired design, independent identically distributed pairs consist of a covariate-$1$ and a covariate-$0$ subject observed at time $1$, with $p_a$ and $p_b$ the probabilities that each fails from cause $c$; $m(b) = e^b/(e^b + 1)$. The score contribution of a pair at $b$ is $(1 - m(b))$ if its covariate-$1$ member fails from cause $c$, minus $m(b)$ if its covariate-$0$ member does.
