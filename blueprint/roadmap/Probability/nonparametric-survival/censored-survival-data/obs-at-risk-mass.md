# Observed at-risk mass

Let $\mu$ be a measure on the pairs $(T, C)$ of an event time and a censoring time in a finite grid. The
observed at-risk mass at a grid point $j$ is the real number $\mu(\{\min(T, C) \ge j\})$, taken to be $0$
if this mass is infinite. When $\mu$ is the joint law of $(T, C)$, it is the probability that a subject is
still under observation at $j$.
