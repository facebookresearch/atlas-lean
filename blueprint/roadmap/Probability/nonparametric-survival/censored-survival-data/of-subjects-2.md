# Grouping competing-risks subject records

Let $t_1 < \dots < t_k$ be observation times, and let each of $n$ subjects be recorded at a time
$t_{g(i)}$, either failing from one of $r$ causes or censored. The grouped competing-risks data count,
at each $t_j$, the subjects recorded there that fail from each cause $c$, and the subjects recorded
there that are censored.
