# Hazard identity under a positive censoring survival

Under independent censoring with $\Pr[C \ge j] \neq 0$, the ratio of the observed event mass to the
observed at-risk mass at $j$ equals the hazard of $T$ at $j$, where a ratio with denominator $0$ is taken
to be $0$. When $\Pr[T \ge j] = 0$, both sides are $0$ by this convention, so the identity identifies
nothing there.
