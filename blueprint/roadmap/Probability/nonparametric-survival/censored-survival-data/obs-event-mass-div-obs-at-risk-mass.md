# Identification of the discrete hazard under independent censoring

Let $X = \min(T, C)$ and $\delta = 1[T \le C]$. Under independent censoring, if the observed at-risk
mass $\Pr[X \ge j]$ is nonzero, then

$$\frac{\Pr[X = j, \ \delta = 1]}{\Pr[X \ge j]} = \frac{\Pr[T = j]}{\Pr[T \ge j]}:$$

the observed ratio is the hazard of $T$ at $j$, a quotient with a nonzero denominator (Kalbfleisch and
Prentice, Sections 1.2 and 3.2).
