# Observed at-risk mass with competing risks

With competing risks, let $\mu$ be a measure on the triples $((T, J), C)$ of an event time $T$ and a
censoring time $C$ in a finite grid and a cause $J$ in a finite set. The observed at-risk mass at a grid
point $j$ is the real number $\mu(\{\min(T, C) \ge j\})$, taken to be $0$ if this mass is infinite. When
$\mu$ is the joint law of $((T, J), C)$, it is the probability that a subject is still under observation
at $j$.
