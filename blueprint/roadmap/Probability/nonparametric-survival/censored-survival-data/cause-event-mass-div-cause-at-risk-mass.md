# Cause-specific hazard identity under a positive censoring survival

Under independent censoring with $\Pr[C \ge j] \neq 0$, the ratio of the observed cause-$c$ event mass to
the observed at-risk mass at $j$ equals the cause-specific hazard, where a ratio with denominator $0$ is
taken to be $0$. When $\Pr[T \ge j] = 0$, both sides are $0$ by this convention.
