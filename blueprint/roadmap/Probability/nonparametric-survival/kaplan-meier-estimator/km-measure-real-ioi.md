# Survival function of the Kaplan–Meier measure

For [grouped right-censored data](censored-data.md), $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ and every $t < \tau$, the [Kaplan–Meier measure](km-measure.md) $\hat\mu_\tau$ satisfies

$$\hat\mu_\tau\big((t, \infty)\big) = \hat S(t),$$

the [Kaplan–Meier estimate](km-survival.md): the survival function of the Kaplan–Meier measure is the Kaplan–Meier estimate on $(-\infty, \tau)$.
