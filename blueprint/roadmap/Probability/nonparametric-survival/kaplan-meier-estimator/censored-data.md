# Grouped right-censored survival data

Right-censored survival data grouped at $k$ distinct observation times consist of

- strictly increasing times $t_1 < t_2 < \dots < t_k$ in $\mathbb{R}$;
- numbers of deaths $d_j \in \mathbb{N}$ at time $t_j$;
- numbers of censored subjects $c_j \in \mathbb{N}$ at time $t_j$.
