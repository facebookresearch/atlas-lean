# Kaplan–Meier is the nonparametric MLE

For [grouped right-censored data](censored-data.md) and $\tau > t_j$ for every $j$, every probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ has [censored-data likelihood](likelihood.md) at most that of the [Kaplan–Meier measure](km-measure.md) $\hat\mu_\tau$:

$$L(\mu) \le L(\hat\mu_\tau).$$

## Sources

- Edward L. Kaplan and Paul Meier, *Nonparametric estimation from incomplete observations*, J. Amer. Statist. Assoc. 53 (1958), no. 282, 457–481, DOI 10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452.
