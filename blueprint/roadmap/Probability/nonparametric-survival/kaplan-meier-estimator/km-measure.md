# Kaplan–Meier measure

For [grouped right-censored data](censored-data.md) and a point $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ (in applications taken after the last observation time), the Kaplan–Meier measure is the discrete measure on $\mathbb{R}$ with the [jumps](km-mass.md) $\hat S(t_j^-)\, d_j/n_j$ at the times $t_j$ and the [leftover mass](km-tail.md) $\prod_j (1 - d_j/n_j)$ at $\tau$.
