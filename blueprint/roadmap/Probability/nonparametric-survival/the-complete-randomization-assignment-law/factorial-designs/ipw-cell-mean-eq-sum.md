# Inverse-probability-weighted cell mean as a sum over all units

Let $U$ be a finite population of $N$ units. For outcomes $y$, cell probabilities $\pi_{ic}$, a cell $c$ and an assignment $f$,

$$\sum_{i : f(i) = c} \frac{y_i}{N \pi_{ic}} = \sum_{i \in U} \mathbf 1[f(i) = c]\, \frac{y_i}{N \pi_{ic}}.$$
