# Mean squared error of the difference in means under complete randomization

Let $\iota$ be a finite population of $N$ units with potential outcomes $y_0, y_1 : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $1 \le n_1 \le N - 1$, with $n_0 = N - n_1$ control units. Under complete randomization, the mean squared error of the difference in means as an estimator of the average treatment effect is exactly $S_1^2/n_1 + S_0^2/n_0 - S_\tau^2/N$, where $S_1^2$, $S_0^2$ and $S_\tau^2$ are the population variances (with divisor $N - 1$) of $y_1$, $y_0$ and the unit effects $y_1 - y_0$.
