# Expectation of Neyman's variance estimator under complete randomization

Let $\iota$ be a finite population of $N$ units with potential outcomes $y_0, y_1 : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $2 \le n_1 \le N - 2$, with $n_0 = N - n_1$. Under complete randomization, Neyman's variance estimator $s_1^2/n_1 + s_0^2/n_0$, built from the sample variances (divisor one less than the arm size) of $y_1$ on the treated and $y_0$ on the controls, has expectation $S_1^2/n_1 + S_0^2/n_0$, where $S_1^2$ and $S_0^2$ are the population variances (divisor $N - 1$).

## Sources

- J. Neyman, "On the application of probability theory to agricultural experiments" (1923); translated by D. M. Dabrowska and T. P. Speed, Statistical Science 5(4) (1990), 465–472.
