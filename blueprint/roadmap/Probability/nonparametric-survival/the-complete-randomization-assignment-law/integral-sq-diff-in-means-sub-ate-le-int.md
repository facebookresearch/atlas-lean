# Neyman's variance estimator is conservative

Let $\iota$ be a finite population of $N$ units with potential outcomes $y_0, y_1 : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $2 \le n_1 \le N - 2$. Under complete randomization, the mean squared error of the difference in means for the average treatment effect is at most the expectation of Neyman's variance estimator $s_1^2/n_1 + s_0^2/n_0$, with equality if and only if the treatment effect $y_{1,i} - y_{0,i}$ is the same for every unit.

## Sources

- J. Neyman, "On the application of probability theory to agricultural experiments" (1923); translated by D. M. Dabrowska and T. P. Speed, Statistical Science 5(4) (1990), 465–472.
