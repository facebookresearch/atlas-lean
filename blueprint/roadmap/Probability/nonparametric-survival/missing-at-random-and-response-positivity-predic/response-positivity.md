# Positivity

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $R \subseteq \Omega$ the response event and $X : \Omega \to \mathcal X$ a covariate. Response positivity holds if there is $\varepsilon > 0$ such that for every $x \in \mathcal X$ with $\mu(X = x) \ne 0$,

$$\varepsilon \le \frac{\mu(R \cap \{X = x\})}{\mu(X = x)}.$$
