# Measure Real inter preimage eq propensity mul

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T \subseteq \Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathcal X$ and $x \in \mathcal X$. Then

$$\mu(T \cap \{X = x\}) = e(x)\, \mu(X = x),$$

where the propensity is $e(x) = \mu(T \cap \{X = x\}) / \mu(X = x)$, taken to be $0$ when $\mu(X = x) = 0$. The identity holds also when $\mu(X = x) = 0$.
