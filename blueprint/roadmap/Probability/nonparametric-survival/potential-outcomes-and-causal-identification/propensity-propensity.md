# Calibration of the propensity score

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T$ a measurable event and $X$ a measurable covariate with values in a finite set with measurable singletons, with [propensity score](propensity.md) $e$. For every $c \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\mu(e(X) = c) \ne 0$, the propensity of $T$ given $e(X) = c$ equals $c$: among units with score $c$, the proportion treated is $c$.
