# Sample variance over a set of units

For values $y_i \in \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $S$ of units, the sample variance of $y$ over $S$ with divisor $|S| - 1$ is

$$s_S^2 = \frac{1}{|S| - 1} \sum_{i \in S} (y_i - \bar y_S)^2,$$

with $\bar y_S$ the [sample mean](sample-mean.md); for $|S| \le 1$ its value is $0$ by convention.
