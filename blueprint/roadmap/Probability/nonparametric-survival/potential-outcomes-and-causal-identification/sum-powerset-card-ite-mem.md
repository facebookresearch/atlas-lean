# Sum powerset Card ite mem

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with $N = |\iota|$ elements, let $n_1 \in \mathbb N$ and $i \in \iota$. Then the number of $n_1$-element subsets of $\iota$ that contain $i$, multiplied by $N$, equals $\binom{N}{n_1} n_1$:

$$N \cdot \#\{S \subseteq \iota : |S| = n_1,\ i \in S\} = \binom{N}{n_1}\, n_1.$$
