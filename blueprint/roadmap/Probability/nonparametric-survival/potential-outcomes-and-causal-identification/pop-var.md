# Variance of a finite population

For a finite population of $N$ units with values $y_i \in \mathbb{R}$, the population variance with divisor $N - 1$ is

$$S^2 = \frac{1}{N - 1} \sum_{i} (y_i - \bar y)^2,$$

with $\bar y$ the [population mean](pop-mean.md); for $N \le 1$ its value is $0$ by convention.
