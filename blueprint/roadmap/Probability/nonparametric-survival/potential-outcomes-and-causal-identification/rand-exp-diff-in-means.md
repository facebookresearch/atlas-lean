# Unbiasedness of the difference in means under complete randomization

For a finite population of $N$ units with potential outcomes $y_{0i}, y_{1i}$ and $1 \le n_1 \le N - 1$ (so that there is at least one treated and one control unit), the [difference in means](diff-in-means.md) is unbiased for the [average treatment effect](ate.md) under [complete randomization](rand-exp.md):

$$\mathbb{E}_{n_1}[\hat\tau] = \tau.$$

## Sources
