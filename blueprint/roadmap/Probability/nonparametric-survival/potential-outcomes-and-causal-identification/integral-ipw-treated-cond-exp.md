# Inverse probability weighting with a general covariate

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $(\Omega, \mathcal F)$, let $\mathcal G \subseteq \mathcal F$ be a sub-σ-algebra (the covariate information, for example the σ-algebra generated by a covariate $X$), $T \in \mathcal F$ an event and $e = \mathbb{E}[\mathbf 1_T \mid \mathcal G]$ the propensity score. Let $Y_0, Y_1 : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ with $Y_1$ integrable, and let $Y$ be the [observed outcome](observed.md). Assume mean ignorability for $Y_1$,

$$\mathbb{E}[\mathbf 1_T Y_1 \mid \mathcal G] = e\, \mathbb{E}[Y_1 \mid \mathcal G] \quad \text{almost surely}$$

(implied by $Y_1 \perp T \mid \mathcal G$), and overlap in the strong form $e \ge \varepsilon$ almost surely for some $\varepsilon > 0$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}\Big[\mathbf 1_T \frac{Y}{e}\Big] = \mathbb{E}[Y_1].$$
