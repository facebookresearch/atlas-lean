# Average treatment effect on the treated in the post period

For a measure space $(\Omega, \mu)$, a treated group $G$ and post-period potential outcomes $Y_{\mathrm{post}}(0), Y_{\mathrm{post}}(1)$, the post-period average treatment effect on the treated is the average of $Y_{\mathrm{post}}(1) - Y_{\mathrm{post}}(0)$ over $G$ (taken to be $0$ if $\mu(G) \in \{0, \infty\}$ or the difference is not integrable on $G$).
