# Strong ignorability given the propensity score

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T$ a measurable event, $V$ a measurable variable in a measurable space and $X$ a measurable covariate with values in a finite set with measurable singletons. If treatment is [strongly ignorable](strongly-ignorable.md) given $X$ for $V$, then it is strongly ignorable given the [propensity score](propensity.md) $e(X)$ for $V$.

## Sources

- Paul R. Rosenbaum and Donald B. Rubin, *The central role of the propensity score in observational studies for causal effects*, Biometrika 70 (1983), no. 1, 41–55, DOI 10.1093/biomet/70.1.41, Theorem 3.
