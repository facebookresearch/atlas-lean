# Strong ignorability given a balancing score

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T$ a measurable event, $V$ a measurable variable in a measurable space, $X$ a measurable covariate with values in a finite set $\mathcal X$ with measurable singletons, and $b$ a function on $\mathcal X$ that is a balancing score ($T$ is [conditionally independent](cond-indep-discrete.md) of $X$ given $b(X)$). If treatment is [strongly ignorable](strongly-ignorable.md) given $X$ for $V$, then it is strongly ignorable given $b(X)$ for $V$.

## Sources

- Paul R. Rosenbaum and Donald B. Rubin, *The central role of the propensity score in observational studies for causal effects*, Biometrika 70 (1983), no. 1, 41–55, DOI 10.1093/biomet/70.1.41, Theorem 3.
