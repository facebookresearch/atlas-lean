# The propensity score is a balancing score

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, $T$ a measurable event and $X$ a measurable covariate with values in a finite set with measurable singletons. Then the [propensity score](propensity.md) $e(X)$ is a balancing score: $T$ is [conditionally independent](cond-indep-discrete.md) of $X$ given $e(X)$.

## Sources

- Paul R. Rosenbaum and Donald B. Rubin, *The central role of the propensity score in observational studies for causal effects*, Biometrika 70 (1983), no. 1, 41–55, DOI 10.1093/biomet/70.1.41, Theorem 1.
