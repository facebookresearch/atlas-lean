# Derivative of the log partial likelihood is the Cox score

For observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$, a scalar covariate $z_i$ and every $b \in \mathbb{R}$, the [scalar log partial likelihood](log-partial-likelihood-3.md) $\ell_1$ is differentiable at $b$ with derivative the [Cox score](cox-score.md): $\ell_1'(b) = U(b)$.
