# Log of the Cox partial likelihood is the log partial likelihood

For observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$, covariates $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^p$ and every $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p$, the logarithm of the [partial likelihood](partial-likelihood.md) is the [log partial likelihood](log-partial-likelihood.md): $\log L(\beta) = \ell(\beta)$.
