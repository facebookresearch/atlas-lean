# Hessian quadratic form of the Cox log partial likelihood

For $\beta, u \in \mathbb{R}^p$, the derivative at $t = 0$ of $t \mapsto \langle U(\beta + t u), u \rangle$
is
$$-\sum_{i : \delta_i = 1} V_i(\beta; u):$$
the Hessian quadratic form of the Cox log partial likelihood in the direction $u$ is minus the observed
information along $u$, with the weighted variances taken at $\beta$.
