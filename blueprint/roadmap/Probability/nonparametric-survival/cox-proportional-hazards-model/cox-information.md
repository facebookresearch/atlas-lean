# Cox observed information for a scalar covariate

For observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i \in \{0, 1\}$, a scalar covariate $z_i$ and $b \in \mathbb{R}$, the Cox observed information is $I(b) = \sum_{i : \delta_i = 1} V_i(b)$, with $V_i(b)$ the [weighted variance over the risk set](risk-var.md).
