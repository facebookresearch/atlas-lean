# Weighted mean vector along a coordinate axis

Along a coordinate axis, $\beta = b e_j$, component $j$ of the weighted mean covariate vector is the
scalar weighted mean of the $j$-th covariate $z_l = x_{l,j}$ at $b$:

$$\bar x_i(b e_j)_j = \frac{\sum_{l \in R(T_i)} z_l \, e^{b z_l}}{\sum_{l \in R(T_i)} e^{b z_l}}.$$
