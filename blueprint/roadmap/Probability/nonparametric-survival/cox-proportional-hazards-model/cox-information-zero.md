# The Cox information at $\beta = 0$ is the Breslow information

For observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$ and group labels $g_i$, the [Cox observed information](cox-information.md) of the [group indicator](group-indicator.md) at $b = 0$ is the [Breslow information](breslow-information.md) $\sum_{i : \delta_i = 1} n_1 n_0/n^2$ (not the tied log-rank variance).
