# Derivative of the Cox score is minus the information

For observed times $T_i$, event indicators $\delta_i$, a scalar covariate $z_i$ and every $b \in \mathbb{R}$, the [Cox score](cox-score.md) is differentiable at $b$ with derivative minus the [observed information](cox-information.md): $U'(b) = -I(b)$.
