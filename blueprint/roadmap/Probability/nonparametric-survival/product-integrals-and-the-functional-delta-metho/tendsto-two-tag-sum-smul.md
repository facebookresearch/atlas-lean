# Two-tag sums converge to the difference quotient of the product integral

For functions $A, V : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and reals $a, b$, say that $V$ is a control for $A$ on $[a, b]$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for all $a \le y \le x \le b$.

For a finite set $Q \subset \mathbb{R}$ and $x \in Q$, let $x^-$ be the largest element of $Q$ smaller than $x$, and $x^- = x$ if there is none. For a finite set $s \subset \mathbb{R}$ and reals $a, b$, let $Q_{a,b}(s) = \{a, b\} \cup \{x \in s : a < x < b\}$ be the partition of $[a, b]$ generated by $s$. The two-tag sum of $F, G, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ over $Q$ is

$$
T(F, G; h)(Q) = \sum_{x \in Q} F(x^-)\,\big(h(x) - h(x^-)\big)\,G(x).
$$

For $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and reals $u, v$, write $P(f)(u, v] = \prod_{(u, v]}(1 + df)$ for the product integral: the limit, as $s$ increases through the finite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ ordered by inclusion, of the partition products $\prod_{x \in Q_{u,v}(s)} \big(1 + f(x) - f(x^-)\big)$, an unspecified real number when this limit does not exist. For $u \le v$ these are the products $\prod_i \big(1 + f(t_i) - f(t_{i-1})\big)$ over the partitions $u = t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_m = v$ of $[u, v]$.

Let $a \le b$, let $A, V, W, k : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $t \ne 0$, and write $A + tk$ for the function $x \mapsto A(x) + t\,k(x)$. Suppose that $V$ is a control for $A$ on $[a, b]$ and $W$ is a control for $A + tk$ on $[a, b]$. Then, as $s$ increases through the finite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ ordered by inclusion,

$$
\sum_{x \in Q_{a,b}(s)} P(A)(a, x^-]\,\big(k(x) - k(x^-)\big)\,P(A + tk)(x, b] \;\longrightarrow\; t^{-1}\big(P(A + tk)(a, b] - P(A)(a, b]\big).
$$
