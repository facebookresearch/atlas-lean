# Kaplan–Meier weak limit on an explicit fixed grid

On probability spaces, let grouped censored data sets $D_n$ share a fixed grid $t$, let the true cumulative hazard on the grid be $A(t_j)$ for a monotone right-continuous $A$, and suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - A\circ t) \Rightarrow G$; let $H = -D\mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))(\Delta G)$ and $S = \mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))$. Then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S) \Rightarrow H$.
