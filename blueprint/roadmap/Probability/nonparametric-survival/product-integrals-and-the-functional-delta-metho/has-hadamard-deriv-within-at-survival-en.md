# Hadamard differentiability of the continuous-time survival map

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For $\tau \ge 0$ and $-\Lambda \in \mathcal B_M$, the map $\Lambda \mapsto \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 - d\Lambda)$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\Lambda$ along $\{x : -x \in \mathcal B_M\}$, tangentially to $\overline{BV}$, with derivative $h \mapsto \Psi_{-\Lambda}(-h)$.
