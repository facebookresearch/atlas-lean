# The Stieltjes hypotheses with continuous integrators

Let $\Psi$ be Duhamel operators on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ satisfying, on a domain $D$ of integrators of bounded variation, the bound $\|\Psi(A, B)\| \le C$ and agreement of $\Psi(A, B)h(t)$ with the limit of the two-tag sums $\sum P(A)(0, u](h(x) - h(u))P(B)(x, t]$ at $h = B - A$ and on a class $S$; let $P$ be the product integral on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$. With $S$ the continuous paths of bounded variation and $D$ closed under freezing, the product integral on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ with $\Psi$ satisfies the Stieltjes product-integral hypotheses on $D$.
