# Duhamel derivative of the grid product integral

The Duhamel derivative of the grid product integral at $x$ is $h \mapsto \big(\sum_{i\le j}\prod_{l<i}(1 + x_l)\,h_i\prod_{i<l\le j}(1 + x_l)\big)_j$.

## Sources

- R. D. Gill and S. Johansen, "A survey of product-integration with a view toward application in survival analysis", Ann. Statist. 18 (1990), 1501–1555.
