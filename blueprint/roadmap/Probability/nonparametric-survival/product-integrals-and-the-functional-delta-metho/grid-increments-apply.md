# Coordinates of the grid increment map

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and index the coordinates of $\mathbb{R}^k$ by $\{0, 1, \dots, k-1\}$. The [grid increment map](grid-increments.md) is a continuous linear map $\Delta : \mathbb{R}^k \to \mathbb{R}^k$. For every $A \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and every index $j$,

$$
(\Delta A)_j = \begin{cases} A_j - A_{j-1}, & j > 0, \\ A_0, & j = 0. \end{cases}
$$
