# Convergence of the Duhamel sums at a path

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For $\tau \ge 0$, $\theta$ of bounded variation and $h \in \overline{BV}$, the two-tag sums defining $\Psi_\theta(h)$ converge.
