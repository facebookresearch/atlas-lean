# Lipschitz dependence of the product integral on the integrator

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. For integrators $A, B$ with controls of total increase at most $M$ on $[a, b]$, $|P(B)(a, b] - P(A)(a, b]| \le 6e^{2M}\sup_{[a,b]}|B - A|$.
