# Bound on two-tag sums against a controlled integrator

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. For $a \le b$, $g$ controlled by $U$, and weights $|F| \le M_1$, $|G| \le \varepsilon$ on $[a, b]$, every two-tag sum $\sum F(u)(g(x) - g(u))G(x)$ over a partition of $[a, b]$ is at most $M_1\varepsilon(U(b) - U(a))$ in absolute value.
