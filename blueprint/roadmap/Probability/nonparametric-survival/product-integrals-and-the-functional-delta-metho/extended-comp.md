# Extended continuous mapping theorem for outer weak convergence

For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. Write $X_n \rightsquigarrow L$ for outer weak convergence: $E^*f(X_n) \to \int f\,dL$ for every bounded continuous $f \ge 0$. Let $U_n \rightsquigarrow L$ in a space in which every closed set is a decreasing limit of bounded continuous functions into $[0, 1]$ (as in a metric space), and let $g_n(x_n) \to g_0(x)$ whenever $x_n \to x \in X_0$ with $x_n \in D_n$. If $P^*(U_n \notin D_n) \to 0$, $L$ is carried by $X_0$ and $g_0$ is $L$-almost everywhere measurable, then $g_n(U_n) \rightsquigarrow g_0(L)$.
