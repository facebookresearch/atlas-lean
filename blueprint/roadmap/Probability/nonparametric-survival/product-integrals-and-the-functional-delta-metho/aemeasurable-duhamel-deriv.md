# Measurability of the Duhamel derivative

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For $\theta \in \mathcal B_M$, $\Psi_\theta$ is almost everywhere measurable for every Borel law carried by $\overline{BV}$.
