# Functional delta theorem

On probability spaces, let $r_n \to \infty$ and $r_n(X_n - \theta) \Rightarrow G$ in a normed space, with $G \in E_0$ and $X_n \in D$ almost surely. If $\varphi$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ along $D$ tangentially to $E_0$ with derivative $\varphi'$, and $r_n(\varphi(X_n) - \varphi(\theta))$ and $\varphi'(G)$ are measurable, then $r_n(\varphi(X_n) - \varphi(\theta)) \Rightarrow \varphi'(G)$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", (1998), Theorem 18.11, Theorem 20.8, §20.3.
