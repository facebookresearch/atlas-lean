# Backward factors of close integrators are close

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. For integrators $A, B$ with controls of total increase at most $M$ on $[a, b]$, the backward factors $P(B)(x, b]$ and $P(A)(x, b]$ are uniformly close when $\sup|B - A|$ is small, with constant $6e^{2M}$.
