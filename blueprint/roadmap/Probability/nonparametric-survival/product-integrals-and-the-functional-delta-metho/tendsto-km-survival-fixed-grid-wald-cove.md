# Coverage of fixed-grid Kaplan–Meier Wald bands

On probability spaces, let grouped censored data sets $D_n$ share a fixed grid $t$, let the true cumulative hazard on the grid be $A(t_j)$ for a monotone right-continuous $A$, and suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - A\circ t) \Rightarrow G$; let $H = -D\mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))(\Delta G)$ and $S = \mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))$. If standard errors $\hat s_n \to s$ in probability, with $\hat s_n$ almost everywhere measurable for every $n$, and every face of the rectangle $\{|h_j| \le zs_j\}$ is null for $H$, the probability that the band $\hat S_n \pm z\hat s_n/\sqrt n$ contains $S$ converges to $P(|H_j| \le zs_j\ \forall j)$.
