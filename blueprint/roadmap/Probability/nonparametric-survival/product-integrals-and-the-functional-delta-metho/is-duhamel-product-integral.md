# Duhamel hypotheses for a product-integral map

A map $P : E \to F$ with operators $\Psi(A, B) : E \to F$ satisfies the Duhamel hypotheses on a domain $D$ with bound $C$ and continuity class $S$ if $P(B) - P(A) = \Psi(A, B)(B - A)$, $\|\Psi(A, B)\| \le C$ for $A, B \in D$, and, for $A \in D$ and $s \in S$, $\Psi(A, B)s \to \Psi(A, A)s$ as $B \to A$ within $D$.

## Sources

- R. D. Gill and S. Johansen, "A survey of product-integration with a view toward application in survival analysis", Ann. Statist. 18 (1990), 1501–1555.
