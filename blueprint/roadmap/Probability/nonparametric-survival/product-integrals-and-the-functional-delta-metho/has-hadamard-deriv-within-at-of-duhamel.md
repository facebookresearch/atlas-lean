# Duhamel representation implies Hadamard differentiability

Let $\theta \in D$ and suppose $P(B) - P(\theta) = \Psi(B)(B - \theta)$ for $B \in D$, $\|\Psi(B)\| \le C$, and $\Psi(B)s \to \Psi(\theta)s$ as $B \to \theta$ within $D$ for every $s \in S$. If $E_0 \subseteq \overline S$, then $P$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ tangentially to $E_0$ along $D$, with derivative $\Psi(\theta)$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", (1998), Theorem 20.8, §20.3.
