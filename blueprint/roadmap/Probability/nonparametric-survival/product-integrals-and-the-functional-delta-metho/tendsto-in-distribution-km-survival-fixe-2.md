# Studentized fixed-grid Kaplan–Meier limit

On probability spaces, let grouped censored data sets $D_n$ share a fixed grid $t$, let the true cumulative hazard on the grid be $A(t_j)$ for a monotone right-continuous $A$, and suppose $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - A\circ t) \Rightarrow G$; let $H = -D\mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))(\Delta G)$ and $S = \mathcal P(-\Delta(A\circ t))$. If standard errors $\hat s_n \to s$ in probability, with $\hat s_n$ almost everywhere measurable for every $n$ and no zero coordinate of $s$, then $(\sqrt n(\hat S_n(j) - S_j)/\hat s_n(j))_j \Rightarrow (H_j/s_j)_j$.
