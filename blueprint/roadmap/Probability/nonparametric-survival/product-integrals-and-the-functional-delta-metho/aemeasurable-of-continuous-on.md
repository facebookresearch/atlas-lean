# Almost-everywhere measurability of a function continuous on a closed set of full measure

Let $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ be the space of bounded real functions on $[0, \tau]$ with the sup norm. Equip $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ with a $\sigma$-algebra equal to the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of its norm topology, and $\mathbb{R}$ with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra.

Let $f : \ell^\infty([0, \tau]) \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on a closed set $S \subseteq \ell^\infty([0, \tau])$, and let $L$ be a measure on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ such that $h \in S$ for $L$-almost every $h$. Then $f$ is $L$-almost everywhere measurable: it agrees $L$-almost everywhere with a measurable function.
