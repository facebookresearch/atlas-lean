# Gill–Johansen limit for product integrals with outer probability

Let $P : E \to F$ be a map between normed spaces with Duhamel operators $\Psi(A, B)$ satisfying, on a domain $D$, the Duhamel equation $P(B) - P(A) = \Psi(A, B)(B - A)$, the bound $\|\Psi(A, B)\| \le C$, and, for $A \in D$ and $s \in S$, $\Psi(A, B)s \to \Psi(A, A)s$ as $B \to A$ within $D$. For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. Write $X_n \rightsquigarrow L$ for outer weak convergence: $E^*f(X_n) \to \int f\,dL$ for every bounded continuous $f \ge 0$. If $r_n \to \infty$, $r_n(X_n - \theta) \rightsquigarrow L$ with $L$ carried by $\overline S$, $\theta \in D$ and $P^*(X_n \notin D) \to 0$, then $r_n(P(X_n) - P(\theta)) \rightsquigarrow$ the image of $L$ under $\Psi(\theta, \theta)$.
