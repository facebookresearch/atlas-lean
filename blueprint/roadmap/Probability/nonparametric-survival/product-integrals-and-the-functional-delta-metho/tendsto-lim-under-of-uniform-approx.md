# Convergence of a net of reals that is a uniform limit of Cauchy nets

Consider real-valued nets indexed by the finite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$, directed by inclusion. Let $u$ be such a net, and suppose that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a Cauchy net $v$ of the same kind with $|u(s) - v(s)| \le \varepsilon$ for every finite set $s \subset \mathbb{R}$. Then $u$ converges: as $s$ increases through the finite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ ordered by inclusion, $u(s)$ tends to $\lim_{s} u(s)$.
