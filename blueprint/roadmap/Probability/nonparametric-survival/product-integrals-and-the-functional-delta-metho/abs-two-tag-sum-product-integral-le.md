# Uniform bound for the Duhamel kernel

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. For integrators $A, B$ with controls of total increase at most $M$ on $[a, b]$ and $|h| \le \delta$ there, every two-tag sum $\sum P(A)(a, u](h(x) - h(u))P(B)(x, b]$ is at most $6\delta e^{2M}$ in absolute value.
