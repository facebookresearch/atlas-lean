# Functional delta theorem for the continuous-time product integral

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. Write $X_n \rightsquigarrow L$ for outer weak convergence: $E^*f(X_n) \to \int f\,dL$ for every bounded continuous $f \ge 0$. If $r_n \to \infty$, $r_n(X_n - \theta) \rightsquigarrow L$ in $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ with $L$ carried by $\overline{BV}$, $\theta \in \mathcal B_M$ and $P^*(X_n \notin \mathcal B_M) \to 0$, then $r_n(P(X_n) - P(\theta)) \rightsquigarrow \Psi_\theta(L)$.
