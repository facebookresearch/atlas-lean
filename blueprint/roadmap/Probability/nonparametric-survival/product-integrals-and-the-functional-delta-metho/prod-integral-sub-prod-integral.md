# Discrete Duhamel equation

On a grid of $k$ points, let $\mathcal P(x)_j = \prod_{i\le j}(1 + x_i)$ be the product integral of increments $x \in \mathbb{R}^k$. Then $\mathcal P(y)_j - \mathcal P(x)_j = \sum_{i\le j}\prod_{l<i}(1 + x_l)(y_i - x_i)\prod_{i<l\le j}(1 + y_l)$.

## Sources

- R. D. Gill and S. Johansen, "A survey of product-integration with a view toward application in survival analysis", Ann. Statist. 18 (1990), 1501–1555.
