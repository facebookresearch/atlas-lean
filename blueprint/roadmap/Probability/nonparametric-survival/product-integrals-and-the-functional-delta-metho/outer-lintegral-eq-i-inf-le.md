# Outer integral with everywhere majorants

For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. Then $E^*f = \inf\{\int g\,d\mu : g\text{ measurable}, f \le g\text{ everywhere}\}$.
