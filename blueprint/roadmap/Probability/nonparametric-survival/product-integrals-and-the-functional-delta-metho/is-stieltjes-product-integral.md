# Continuous-time product integral as a hypothesis

A map $P$ on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ with Duhamel operators $\Psi$ is a Stieltjes product integral on a domain $D$ if it satisfies the Duhamel hypotheses, its continuity class is uniformly dense in $C[0, \tau]$, and on pure-jump integrators with jumps $a_j$ at times $0 < s_0 < s_1 < \cdots$ it equals $P(A)(t) = \prod_{s_j\le t}(1 + a_j)$.
