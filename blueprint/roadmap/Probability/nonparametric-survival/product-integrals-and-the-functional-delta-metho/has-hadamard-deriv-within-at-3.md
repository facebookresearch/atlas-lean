# Hadamard differentiability

A map $\varphi : E \to F$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ along a domain $D$, tangentially to $E_0$, with derivative $\varphi'$ if for every $h \in E_0$, $t^{-1}(\varphi(\theta + tk) - \varphi(\theta)) \to \varphi'(h)$ as $t \downarrow 0$ and $k \to h$ with $\theta + tk \in D$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", (1998), Theorem 18.11, Theorem 20.8, §20.3.
