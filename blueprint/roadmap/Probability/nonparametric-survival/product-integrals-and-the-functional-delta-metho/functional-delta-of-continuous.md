# Functional delta theorem for continuous maps

On probability spaces, let $r_n \to \infty$ and $r_n(X_n - \theta) \Rightarrow G$ with $G \in E_0$ and $X_n \in D$ almost surely. If $\varphi$ and $\varphi'$ are continuous and $\varphi$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ along $D$ tangentially to $E_0$ with derivative $\varphi'$, then $r_n(\varphi(X_n) - \varphi(\theta)) \Rightarrow \varphi'(G)$.
