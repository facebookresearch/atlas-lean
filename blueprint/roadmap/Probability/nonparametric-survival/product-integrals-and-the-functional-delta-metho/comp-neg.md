# Hadamard differentiability under negation

If $\varphi$ is Hadamard differentiable at $-\theta$ along $D$ tangentially to $E_0$ with derivative $\varphi'$, then $x \mapsto \varphi(-x)$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ along $-D$ tangentially to $-E_0$ with derivative $h \mapsto \varphi'(-h)$.
