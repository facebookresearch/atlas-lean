# Hadamard differentiability of the continuous-time product integral

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For $\tau \ge 0$ and $\theta \in \mathcal B_M$, the map $x \mapsto P(x)$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\theta$ along $\mathcal B_M$ tangentially to $\overline{BV}$, with derivative $\Psi_\theta$.
