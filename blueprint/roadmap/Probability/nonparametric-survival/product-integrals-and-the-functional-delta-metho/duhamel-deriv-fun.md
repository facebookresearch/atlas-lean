# Duhamel integral

The Duhamel integral $\int_{(a,b]}P(A)(a, s)\,dh(s)\,P(A)(s, b]$ is the limit along refinement of the two-tag sums $\sum P(A)(a, u](h(x) - h(u))P(A)(x, b]$ over partitions of $[a, b]$. When this limit does not exist, the value is unspecified; it exists for every $h$ that is a uniform limit of functions of bounded variation.
