# Extended continuous mapping theorem

On probability spaces, let $U_n \Rightarrow V$ in a space with outer approximations of closed sets, and let $g_n, g_0$ satisfy $g_n(x_n) \to g_0(x)$ whenever $x_n \to x \in X_0$ with $x_n \in D_n$. If $U_n \in D_n$ almost surely, $V \in X_0$ almost surely, and $g_n(U_n)$, $g_0(V)$ are measurable, then $g_n(U_n) \Rightarrow g_0(V)$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", (1998), Theorem 18.11, Theorem 20.8, §20.3.
