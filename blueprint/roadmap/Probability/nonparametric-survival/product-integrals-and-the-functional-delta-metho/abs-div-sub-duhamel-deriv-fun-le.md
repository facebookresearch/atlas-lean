# Quantitative Hadamard estimate for the product integral

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. Let $a \le b$, let $A$ be controlled on $[a, b]$ by $V$ with $V(b) - V(a) \le M$, and let $\Psi_A(h)$ be the Duhamel integral $\int_{(a,b]}P(A)(a, s)\,dh(s)\,P(A)(s, b]$, assuming the two-tag sums $\sum P(A)(a, u](h(x) - h(u))P(A)(x, b]$ converge to it. Let $t > 0$, let $A + tk$ be controlled on $[a, b]$ by $W$ with $W(b) - W(a) \le M$, let $g$ be controlled on $[a, b]$ by $U$, and on $[a, b]$ let $|k - h| \le \delta_1$, $|h - g| \le \delta_2$ and $|k| \le \delta_3$. Then $\big|t^{-1}(P(A + tk)(a, b] - P(A)(a, b]) - \Psi_A(h)\big| \le \big(\delta_1 + 2\delta_2 + e^Mt\delta_3(U(b) - U(a))\big)\cdot 6e^{2M}$.
