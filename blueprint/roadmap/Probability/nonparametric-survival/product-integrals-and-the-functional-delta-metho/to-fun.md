# Path of a bounded function on an interval, extended by zero

Let $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ be the space of bounded real functions on $[0, \tau]$ with the sup norm. The path of $x \in \ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ is the function $\bar x : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ given by

$$
\bar x(t) = \begin{cases} x(t), & t \in [0, \tau], \\ 0, & t \notin [0, \tau]. \end{cases}
$$
