# Continuous-time Kaplan–Meier limit law

On probability spaces, let $P$ be a product-integral map on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$, $P(A)(t) = \prod_{(0,t]}(1 + dA)$, given as a hypothesis together with Duhamel operators $\Psi$ on a domain $D$ satisfying the Duhamel equation, a uniform bound, the limit $\Psi(A, B)s \to \Psi(A, A)s$ as $B \to A$ within $D$ for $A \in D$ and $s$ in a class $S$ whose uniform closure contains $C[0, \tau]$, and the product formula on pure-jump integrators. Let $\hat\Lambda_n$ and $\hat S_n$ be the Nelson–Aalen and Kaplan–Meier estimates of grouped censored samples with positive observation times, as elements of $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$, and $S_\Lambda = P(-\Lambda)$ the survival function. If $-\Lambda \in D$, $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - \Lambda) \Rightarrow G$ with $-G \in \overline S$ almost surely, $-\hat\Lambda_n \in D$ almost surely, and $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S_\Lambda)$ is almost everywhere measurable (for the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$), then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S_\Lambda) \Rightarrow -\Psi(-\Lambda, -\Lambda)G$.
