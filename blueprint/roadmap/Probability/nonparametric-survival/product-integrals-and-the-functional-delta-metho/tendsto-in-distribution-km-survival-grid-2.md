# Kaplan–Meier limit law from the cumulative-hazard central limit theorem

On probability spaces, for random grouped censored data sets on a common grid, if $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - \Lambda) \Rightarrow G$ at the grid times, then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S) \Rightarrow -D\mathcal P(-\Delta\Lambda)(\Delta G)$ with $S = \mathcal P(-\Delta\Lambda)$.
