# Subadditivity of the limit superior for sequences in the extended nonnegative reals

For sequences $u, v : \mathbb{N} \to [0, \infty]$,

$$
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \big(u_n + v_n\big) \le \limsup_{n \to \infty} u_n + \limsup_{n \to \infty} v_n,
$$

with addition in $[0, \infty]$ (where $a + \infty = \infty$).
