# Hadamard differentiability of a product integral

Let $P : E \to F$ be a map between normed spaces with Duhamel operators $\Psi(A, B)$ satisfying, on a domain $D$, the Duhamel equation $P(B) - P(A) = \Psi(A, B)(B - A)$, the bound $\|\Psi(A, B)\| \le C$, and, for $A \in D$ and $s \in S$, $\Psi(A, B)s \to \Psi(A, A)s$ as $B \to A$ within $D$. Then at every $\theta \in D$, $P$ is Hadamard differentiable along $D$, tangentially to any $E_0 \subseteq \overline S$, with derivative $\Psi(\theta, \theta)$.
