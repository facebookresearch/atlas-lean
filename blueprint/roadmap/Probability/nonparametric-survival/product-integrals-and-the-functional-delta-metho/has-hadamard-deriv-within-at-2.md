# Hadamard differentiability of the Stieltjes product integral

Let $P$ be a product-integral map on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$, $P(A)(t) = \prod_{(0,t]}(1 + dA)$, given as a hypothesis together with Duhamel operators $\Psi$ on a domain $D$ satisfying the Duhamel equation, a uniform bound, the limit $\Psi(A, B)s \to \Psi(A, A)s$ as $B \to A$ within $D$ for $A \in D$ and $s$ in a class $S$ whose uniform closure contains $C[0, \tau]$, and the product formula on pure-jump integrators. Then $P$ is Hadamard differentiable at every $\theta \in D$ along $D$, tangentially to $C[0, \tau]$, with derivative $\Psi(\theta, \theta)$.
