# Bound on the backward product-integral factor

A function $A$ is controlled on $[a, b]$ by a nondecreasing $V$ if $|A(x) - A(y)| \le V(x) - V(y)$ for $a \le y \le x \le b$; write $P(A)(y, x] = \prod_{(y,x]}(1 + dA)$ for the product integral. If $A$ is controlled by $V$ on $[a, b]$, then $|P(A)(x, b]| \le e^{V(b) - V(a)}$ for $x \in [a, b]$.
