# Lipschitz bound for the grid product integral

On a grid of $k$ points, let $\mathcal P(x)_j = \prod_{i\le j}(1 + x_i)$ be the product integral of increments $x \in \mathbb{R}^k$. Then $|\mathcal P(y)_j - \mathcal P(x)_j| \le e^{\sum|x_i|}e^{\sum|y_i|}\sum_{i\le j}|y_i - x_i|$, uniformly in the grid size.

## Sources

- R. D. Gill and S. Johansen, "A survey of product-integration with a view toward application in survival analysis", Ann. Statist. 18 (1990), 1501–1555.
