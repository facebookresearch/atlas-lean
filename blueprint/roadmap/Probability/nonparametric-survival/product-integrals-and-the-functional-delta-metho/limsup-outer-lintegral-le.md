# Limsup half of the converse outer portmanteau theorem

For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. On a probability space, if $\limsup P^*(X_n \in F) \le L(F)$ for every closed $F$, then $\limsup E^*f(X_n) \le \int f\,dL$ for every bounded continuous $f \ge 0$.
