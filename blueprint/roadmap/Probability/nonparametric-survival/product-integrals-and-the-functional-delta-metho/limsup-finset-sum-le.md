# Limit superior of a finite sum of sequences in the extended nonnegative reals

Let $s$ be a finite set of indices and, for each index $i$, let $u_i : \mathbb{N} \to [0, \infty]$ be a sequence. Then

$$
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i \in s} u_i(n) \le \sum_{i \in s} \limsup_{n \to \infty} u_i(n),
$$

with sums in $[0, \infty]$ (where $a + \infty = \infty$) and an empty sum equal to $0$.
