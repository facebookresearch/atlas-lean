# Functional delta theorem in the space of bounded functions

On probability spaces, in $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ with the uniform topology, if $\sqrt n(\hat\Lambda_n - \Lambda) \Rightarrow G$ with almost every path of $G$ continuous, a map $\Phi$ is Hadamard differentiable at $\Lambda$ along a set $D$ tangentially to $C[0, \tau]$ with derivative $\Phi'$, $\hat\Lambda_n \in D$ almost surely, and $\sqrt n(\Phi(\hat\Lambda_n) - \Phi(\Lambda))$ and $\Phi'(G)$ are almost everywhere measurable, then $\sqrt n(\Phi(\hat\Lambda_n) - \Phi(\Lambda)) \Rightarrow \Phi'(G)$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", (1998), Theorem 18.11, Theorem 20.8, §20.3.
