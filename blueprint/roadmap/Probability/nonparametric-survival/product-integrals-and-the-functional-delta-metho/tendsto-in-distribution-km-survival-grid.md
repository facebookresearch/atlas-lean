# Kaplan–Meier limit law on a fixed grid

On probability spaces, for random grouped censored data sets on a common grid, if the Nelson–Aalen increments satisfy $\sqrt n(\Delta\hat\Lambda_n - \lambda) \Rightarrow G$, then $\sqrt n(\hat S_n - S) \Rightarrow -D\mathcal P(-\lambda)G$ with $S = \mathcal P(-\lambda)$; coordinatewise the limit is $-\sum_{i\le j}S(t_{i-1})G_i\prod_{i<l\le j}(1 - \lambda_l)$.
