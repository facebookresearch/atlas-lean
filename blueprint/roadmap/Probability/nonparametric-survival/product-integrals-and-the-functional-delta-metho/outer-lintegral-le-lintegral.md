# The outer integral is at most any measurable majorant

For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. If $g$ is measurable with $f \le g$ almost everywhere, then $E^*f \le \int g\,d\mu$.
