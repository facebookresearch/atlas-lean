# Superadditivity bound for outer integrals

For an arbitrary map $f$ into $[0, \infty]$, $E^*f$ denotes the outer integral (the infimum of $\int g$ over measurable $g \ge f$ almost everywhere) and $P^*$ the outer probability. On a probability space, if $M \le f_1 + f_2$ pointwise, then $M \le E^*f_1 + E^*f_2$.
