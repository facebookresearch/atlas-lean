# Hypotheses on the Duhamel operator

Duhamel operators $\Psi(A, B)$ on $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ satisfy the operator hypotheses on a domain $D$ with bound $C$ and class $S$ if $\|\Psi(A, B)\| \le C$ and $\Psi(A, B)h(t)$ is the limit of the two-tag sums $\sum P(A)(0, u](h(x) - h(u))P(B)(x, t]$ for $h = B - A$ and for $h \in S$.
