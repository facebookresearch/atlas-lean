# The Duhamel derivative is Lipschitz on the tangent set

On $[0, \tau]$, let $\mathcal B_M$ be the paths of total variation at most $M$ and $\overline{BV}$ the uniform closure of the paths of bounded variation; $P(x) = \prod_{(0,\tau]}(1 + dx)$ and $\Psi_\theta(h) = \int_{(0,\tau]}P(\theta)(0, s)\,dh(s)\,P(\theta)(s, \tau]$. For $\theta \in \mathcal B_M$ and $h, h' \in \overline{BV}$, $|\Psi_\theta(h) - \Psi_\theta(h')| \le 6e^{2M}\|h - h'\|$.
