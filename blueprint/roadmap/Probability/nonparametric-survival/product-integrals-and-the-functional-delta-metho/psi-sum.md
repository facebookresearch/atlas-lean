# Two-tag Duhamel sum for paths on an interval

Let $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $\ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ be the space of bounded real functions on $[0, \tau]$ with the sup norm. For $A \in \ell^\infty([0, \tau])$ let $\bar A : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be its extension by $0$ outside $[0, \tau]$. For a finite set $Q \subset \mathbb{R}$ and $x \in Q$, let $x^-$ be the largest element of $Q$ smaller than $x$, and $x^- = x$ if there is none. For a finite set $s \subset \mathbb{R}$ and reals $a, b$, let $Q_{a,b}(s) = \{a, b\} \cup \{x \in s : a < x < b\}$ be the partition of $[a, b]$ generated by $s$. The two-tag sum of $F, G, h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ over $Q$ is

$$
T(F, G; h)(Q) = \sum_{x \in Q} F(x^-)\,\big(h(x) - h(x^-)\big)\,G(x).
$$

For $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and reals $u, v$, write $P(f)(u, v] = \prod_{(u, v]}(1 + df)$ for the product integral: the limit, as $s$ increases through the finite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ ordered by inclusion, of the partition products $\prod_{x \in Q_{u,v}(s)} \big(1 + f(x) - f(x^-)\big)$, an unspecified real number when this limit does not exist. For $u \le v$ these are the products $\prod_i \big(1 + f(t_i) - f(t_{i-1})\big)$ over the partitions $u = t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_m = v$ of $[u, v]$.

For $A, B, h \in \ell^\infty([0, \tau])$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $s \subset \mathbb{R}$, the two-tag Duhamel sum is the two-tag sum of $F(u) = P(\bar A)(0, u]$ and $G(x) = P(\bar B)(x, t]$ against $\bar h$ over the partition of $[0, t]$ generated by $s$:

$$
\Psi_{A,B,h,t}(s) = \sum_{x \in Q_{0,t}(s)} P(\bar A)(0, x^-]\,\big(\bar h(x) - \bar h(x^-)\big)\,P(\bar B)(x, t].
$$
