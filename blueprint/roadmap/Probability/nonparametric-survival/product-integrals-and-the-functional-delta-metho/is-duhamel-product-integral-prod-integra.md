# The grid product integral satisfies the Duhamel hypotheses

On a grid of $k$ points, let $\mathcal P(x)_j = \prod_{i\le j}(1 + x_i)$ be the product integral of increments $x \in \mathbb{R}^k$. On every ball $\{x : \sum_l|x_l| \le M\}$, $\mathcal P$ satisfies the Duhamel hypotheses with the two-point Duhamel operator, bound $ke^{2M}$ and continuity on all of $\mathbb{R}^k$.
