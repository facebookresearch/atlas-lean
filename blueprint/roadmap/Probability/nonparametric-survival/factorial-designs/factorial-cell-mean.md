# Observed cell mean in a factorial design

Let $U$ be a finite population of $N$ units. For outcomes $y : U \to \mathbb{R}$, a cell $c \in \{0,1,2,3\}$ and an assignment $f : U \to \{0,1,2,3\}$, the observed mean of cell $c$ is

$$\bar y_c(f) = \frac{1}{\#\{i : f(i) = c\}} \sum_{i : f(i) = c} y_i,$$

with the value $0$ for an empty cell.
