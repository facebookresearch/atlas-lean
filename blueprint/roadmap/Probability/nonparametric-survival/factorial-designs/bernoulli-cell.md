# Independent cell assignment of a unit

For a unit $i$ and cell probabilities $\pi_{i0}, \dots, \pi_{i3} \in \mathbb{R}$, the per-unit assignment law is the measure on $\{0,1,2,3\}$ giving cell $c$ the mass $\pi_{ic}$ (a negative weight counts as $0$). Independent assignment of all units is the product of these laws over the units.
