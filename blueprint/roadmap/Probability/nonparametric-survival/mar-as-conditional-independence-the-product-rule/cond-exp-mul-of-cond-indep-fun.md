# Product rule for conditionally independent real random variables

Let $\Omega$ be a standard Borel space with σ-algebra $\mathcal F$, $\mu$ a finite measure on it, and $\mathcal G \subseteq \mathcal F$ a sub-σ-algebra. Let $f, g : \Omega \to \mathbb R$ be measurable and conditionally independent given $\mathcal G$ under $\mu$, with $f$, $g$ and $fg$ integrable. Then

$$\mathbb E_\mu[fg \mid \mathcal G] = \mathbb E_\mu[f \mid \mathcal G]\, \mathbb E_\mu[g \mid \mathcal G] \quad \mu\text{-a.e.}$$
