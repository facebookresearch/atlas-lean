# A right-continuous martingale is stopped at exit

On a finite measure space with a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ and a random time $X$, suppose $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ and $B \cap \{X \le s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ for $B \in \mathcal F_t$, $s \le t$. Then a martingale with almost surely right-continuous paths satisfies $M_t = M_{\min(t, X)}$ for all $t$, almost surely.
