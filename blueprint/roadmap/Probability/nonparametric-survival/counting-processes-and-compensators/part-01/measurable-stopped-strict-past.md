# Stopping preserves predictability

For a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ with strict past $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$ and a random time $X$: if $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ for all $s$, each $A_t$ is $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable and every path of $A$ is right-continuous, then $A_{\min(t, X)}$ is $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable.
