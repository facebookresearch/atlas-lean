# Uniqueness of compensators for a single-jump history

On a finite measure space, let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ be a filtration and $X$ a random time with $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ such that every $\mathcal F_s$-event contains $\{X > s\}$ or misses it. A compensator of $N$ here is an $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable process $A$, stopped at $X$, with almost every path of locally finite variation tending to $0$ at $-\infty$, such that $N - A$ is a martingale. Then two compensators of the same process $N$ are indistinguishable.
