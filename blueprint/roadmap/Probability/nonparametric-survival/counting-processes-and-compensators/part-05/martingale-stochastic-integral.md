# Martingale property of the discrete stochastic integral

On a filtered finite measure space indexed by $n \in \mathbb{N}$, write $(H \bullet M)_n = \sum_{k<n}H_{k+1}(M_{k+1} - M_k)$ for the discrete stochastic integral. On a finite measure space, if $M$ is a martingale, $H$ is predictable and every $H_{n+1}(M_{n+1} - M_n)$ is integrable, then $H \bullet M$ is a martingale.
