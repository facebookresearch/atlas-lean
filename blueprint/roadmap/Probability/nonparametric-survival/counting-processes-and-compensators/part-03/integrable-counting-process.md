# Integrability of the counting process

In the random-censorship model, a subject has latent failure time $T$ and censoring time $C$ on a finite measure space, observed time $X = \min(T, C)$, counting process $N(t) = \mathbf 1\{T \le C, T \le t\}$, censoring process $N^c(t) = \mathbf 1\{C < T, C \le t\}$ and at-risk process $Y(t) = \mathbf 1\{t \le X\}$. On a finite measure space with $T$ and $C$ measurable, $N(t)$ is integrable.
