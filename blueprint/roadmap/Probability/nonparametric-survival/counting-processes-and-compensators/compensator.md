# Compensator of a counting process

For a latent failure time $T$, censoring time $C$ and hazard measure $\Lambda$, the compensator is the real number $A(t) = \int_{(-\infty, t]}Y(s)\,d\Lambda(s)$, with $Y$ the at-risk process, when this integral is finite; the integral is computed in $[0, \infty]$ and an infinite value is read as $A(t) = 0$.
