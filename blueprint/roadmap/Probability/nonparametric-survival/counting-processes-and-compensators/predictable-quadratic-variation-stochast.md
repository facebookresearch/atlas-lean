# Predictable quadratic variation of a discrete stochastic integral

On a filtered measurable space indexed by $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with an arbitrary measure $\mu$, write $(H \bullet M)_n = \sum_{k<n}H_{k+1}(M_{k+1} - M_k)$ for the discrete stochastic integral. Let $\langle M\rangle_n = \sum_{k<n}\mu[(M_{k+1} - M_k)^2 \mid \mathcal F_k]$, with $\mu[\cdot \mid \mathcal F_k]$ Mathlib's conditional expectation (the zero function when $\mu$ is not σ-finite on $\mathcal F_k$). If $H$ is predictable and the relevant squares are $\mu$-integrable, then $\langle H \bullet M\rangle = H^2 \bullet \langle M\rangle$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.
