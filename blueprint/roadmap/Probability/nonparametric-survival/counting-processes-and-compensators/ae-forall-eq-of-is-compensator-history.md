# Compensator uniqueness in the censoring model

In the random-censorship model, a subject has latent failure time $T$ and censoring time $C$ on a finite measure space, observed time $X = \min(T, C)$, counting process $N(t) = \mathbf 1\{T \le C, T \le t\}$, censoring process $N^c(t) = \mathbf 1\{C < T, C \le t\}$ and at-risk process $Y(t) = \mathbf 1\{t \le X\}$. Let $\mathcal F_t = \sigma(N(u), N^c(u) : u \le t)$ be the history filtration (for measurable $T$ and $C$) and $\mathcal F_{t-} = \bigvee_{s<t}\mathcal F_s$ the strict past. A compensator of $N$ here is an $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable process $A$, stopped at $X$, with almost every path of locally finite variation tending to $0$ at $-\infty$, such that $N - A$ is a martingale. Then two compensators of $N$ are indistinguishable.
