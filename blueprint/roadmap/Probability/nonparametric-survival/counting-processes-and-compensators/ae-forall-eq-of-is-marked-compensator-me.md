# Uniqueness of the compensating random measure of a marked single jump

Let $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in\mathbb{R}}$ be a filtration and $X$ a random time with $\{X > s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ such that every $\mathcal F_s$-event contains $\{X > s\}$ or misses it. Suppose also $B \cap \{X \le s\} \in \mathcal F_s$ for $B \in \mathcal F_t$, $s \le t$. A càdlàg compensator here is an $\mathcal F_{t-}$-measurable process with right-continuous paths, almost every path of locally finite variation and tending to $0$ at $-\infty$, whose difference from the compensated process is a martingale. On a countably generated mark space, let $A_t$ and $A'_t$ be finite measures such that for every measurable mark set $B$ both $t \mapsto A_t(B)$ and $t \mapsto A'_t(B)$ are càdlàg compensators of the same process $N(B)$. Then almost surely $A_t = A'_t$ for all $t$.
