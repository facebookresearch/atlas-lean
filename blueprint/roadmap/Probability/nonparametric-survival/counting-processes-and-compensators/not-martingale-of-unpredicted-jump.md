# Integrating unpredicted jumps does not give a martingale

On a finite measure space, let $N$ have unit jumps with integrable values. If the set where $N$ jumps at step $k + 1$ while $\mathbb{E}[N_{k+1} - N_k \mid \mathcal F_k] < 1$ has positive measure, then $\Delta N \bullet M$ is not a martingale, $M$ the compensated process.
