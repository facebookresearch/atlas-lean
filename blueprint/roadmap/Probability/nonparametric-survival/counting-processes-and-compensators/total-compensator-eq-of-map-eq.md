# Multiplicative intensity for a common law

If every $T_i$ has the same law $F$ with hazard measure $\Lambda$, and each $\int_{(-\infty, t]}Y_i\,d\Lambda$ is finite, then the aggregated compensator is $\int_{(-\infty, t]}Y\,d\Lambda$ with $Y$ the number at risk (Aalen's multiplicative intensity model).
