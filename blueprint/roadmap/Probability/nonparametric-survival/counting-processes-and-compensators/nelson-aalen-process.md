# Nelson–Aalen process

For a counting process $N$ and an at-risk process $Y$, the Nelson–Aalen process is $\hat A_n = \sum_{k<n}(N_{k+1} - N_k)/Y_{k+1}$, a step with nobody at risk contributing $0$.
