# Compensator identity for one subject of a cohort

Consider an indexed family of subjects, finite or infinite, with measurable latent failure and censoring times $(T_i, C_i)$ on a probability space, the pairs mutually independent and $T_i$ independent of $C_i$ for each $i$; let $\Lambda_i$ be the hazard measure of the law of $T_i$ and $(\mathcal F_t)$ the joint history filtration. Then for each $i$, $N_i(t) - \int_{(-\infty, t]}Y_i\,d\Lambda_i$ is a martingale for $(\mathcal F_t)$.
