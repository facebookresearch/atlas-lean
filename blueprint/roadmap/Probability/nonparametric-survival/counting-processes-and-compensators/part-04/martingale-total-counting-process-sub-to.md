# Compensator identity for the aggregated process

Consider finitely many subjects with measurable latent failure and censoring times $(T_i, C_i)$ on a probability space, the pairs mutually independent and $T_i$ independent of $C_i$ for each $i$; let $\Lambda_i$ be the hazard measure of the law of $T_i$ and $(\mathcal F_t)$ the joint history filtration. Then $N(t) - A(t) = \sum_i\big(N_i(t) - \int_{(-\infty, t]}Y_i\,d\Lambda_i\big)$ is a martingale for $(\mathcal F_t)$.
