# Multiplicative intensity for a common law

For a measure $\mu$ on $\Omega$ and a finite cohort of subjects with latent times $(T_i, C_i)$, if every $T_i$ has the same image measure $\mu \circ T_i^{-1} = F$ (the same law when $\mu$ is a probability measure) with hazard measure $\Lambda$, and each $\int_{(-\infty, t]}Y_i\,d\Lambda$ is finite, then the aggregated compensator is the real number $\int_{(-\infty, t]}Y\,d\Lambda$ with $Y$ the number at risk (Aalen's multiplicative intensity model).
