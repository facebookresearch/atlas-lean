# Martingale representation of the Kaplan–Meier estimator

Let $\mathcal P_A(n) = \prod_{k<n}(1 - (A_{k+1} - A_k))$ be the discrete product integral, $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$ and $\hat S = \mathcal P_{\hat A}$ the Kaplan–Meier process. If no increment of $\alpha$ equals $1$ and $M = N - Y \bullet \alpha$, then pathwise

$$\frac{\hat S}{S} - 1 = -\frac{\hat S_-}{SY}\bullet M + \frac{\hat S_-}{S}\mathbf 1\{Y = 0\}\bullet\alpha.$$
