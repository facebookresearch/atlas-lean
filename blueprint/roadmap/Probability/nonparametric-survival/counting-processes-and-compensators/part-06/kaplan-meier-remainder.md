# Vanishing-risk remainder integrand

The vanishing-risk remainder integrand of the Kaplan–Meier representation at step $k$ is $\hat S_{k-1}/S_k\cdot\mathbf 1\{Y_k = 0\}$ with $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$, where $k - 1$ is natural-number subtraction (so at $k = 0$ the factor is $\hat S_0$) and $\hat S_{k-1}/S_k = 0$ when $S_k = 0$. In the representation the increment from $k$ to $k + 1$ uses its value at $k + 1$, $\hat S_k/S_{k+1} \cdot \mathbf 1\{Y_{k+1} = 0\}$.
