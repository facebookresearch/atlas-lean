# Integrand of the Kaplan–Meier martingale representation

The integrand of Gill's Kaplan–Meier representation at step $k$ is $\hat S_{k-1}/(S_kY_k)$ with $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$, where $k - 1$ is natural-number subtraction (so at $k = 0$ the factor is $\hat S_0$) and a zero $S_k$ or $Y_k$ makes the value $0$ ($x/0 = 0$, $0^{-1} = 0$).
