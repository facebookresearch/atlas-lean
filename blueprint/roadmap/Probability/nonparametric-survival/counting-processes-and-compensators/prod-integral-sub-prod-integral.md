# Duhamel’s equation for discrete product integrals

Let $\mathcal P_A(n) = \prod_{k<n}(1 - (A_{k+1} - A_k))$ be the discrete product integral, $S = \mathcal P_\alpha$ and $\hat S = \mathcal P_{\hat A}$ the Kaplan–Meier process. Then for all sequences $A, B$,

$$\mathcal P_A(n) - \mathcal P_B(n) = -\sum_{k<n}\mathcal P_A(k)\big(\Delta A_{k+1} - \Delta B_{k+1}\big)\prod_{k<i<n}(1 - \Delta B_{i+1}).$$
