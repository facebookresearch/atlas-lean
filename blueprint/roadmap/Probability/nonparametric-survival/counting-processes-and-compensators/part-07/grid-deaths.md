# Counting process of grouped censored data

For grouped censored data with death counts $d_0, \dots, d_{k-1}$ at $k$ ordered observation times, the grid counting process is $N_n = \sum_{i<\min(n,k)}d_i$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$: past the last observation time the count no longer grows, and $N_n = 0$ for every $n$ when $k = 0$.
