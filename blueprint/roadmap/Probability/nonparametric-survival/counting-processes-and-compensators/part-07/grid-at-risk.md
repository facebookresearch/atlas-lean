# At-risk process of grouped censored data

For grouped censored data with numbers at risk $n_0, \dots, n_{k-1}$ at ordered observation times, the grid at-risk process is $Y_m = n_{m-1}$ for $1 \le m \le k$ and $Y_m = 0$ for $m > k$. At $m = 0$, where $m - 1$ is natural-number subtraction, $Y_0 = n_0$ when $k > 0$ and $Y_0 = 0$ when $k = 0$.
