# No maximum when all pairs agree

For `n ≥ 1` real pairs with
`y i 0 = y i 1` for every `i`, the log-likelihood at `α_i = y i 0` is unbounded above over `s > 0`:
for every `M` there is `s > 0` with log-likelihood greater than `M`.

## Sources

- J. Neyman and E. L. Scott, "Consistent estimates based on partially consistent observations", Econometrica 16 (1948) 1–32; derived.
