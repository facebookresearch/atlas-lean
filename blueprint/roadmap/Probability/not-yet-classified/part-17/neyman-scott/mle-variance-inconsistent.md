# Inconsistency of the maximum-likelihood variance

Let `μ` be a
probability measure and `y i t`, `i ∈ ℕ`, `t ∈ {0, 1}`, independent real random variables with
`y i t` Gaussian of mean `α_i` (arbitrary reals) and variance `σ² > 0`. Then for every `n ≥ 1`, the
estimate `σ̂²_n` from the first `n` pairs has expectation `σ² / 2`, and `σ̂²_n → σ² / 2` almost
surely as `n → ∞`.

## Sources

- R. Koenker, lecture notes "Incidental parameters and dynamic bias in panel data", for the expectation, stated there for general `T`; J. Neyman and E. L. Scott, "Consistent estimates based on partially consistent observations", Econometrica 16 (1948) 1–32; the almost-sure limit is derived.
