# Wilcoxon's normal null

Let `μ` be a probability measure, `σ² > 0`, and
`D_1, …, D_n : Ω → ℝ` independent measurable random variables, each Gaussian with mean `0` and
variance `σ²`. Then for every `t`, `P(W⁺ = t) = c_n(t) / 2ⁿ`.
