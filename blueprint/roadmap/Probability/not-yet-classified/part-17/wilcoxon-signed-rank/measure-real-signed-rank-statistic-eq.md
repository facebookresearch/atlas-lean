# Exact null law of the signed-rank statistic

Let `μ` be a probability
measure and `D_1, …, D_n : Ω → ℝ` independent measurable random variables, each with a law that
has no atoms and is symmetric about `0` (the law of `D_i` equals the law of `-D_i`). Then for
every `t`, `P(W⁺ = t) = c_n(t) / 2ⁿ`.

## Sources

- F. Wilcoxon, "Individual comparisons by ranking methods", Biometrics Bull. 1 (1945) 80–83; the continuous symmetric null generalizes its normal null.
