# Range of `H`

For an assignment `g` of `N ≥ 1`
positions to `C` samples of sizes `n_i ≥ 1` and every ranking `σ`,
`0 ≤ H ≤ (N³ - ∑ n_i³) / (N (N + 1))`, and some ranking attains the upper bound.

## Sources

- W. H. Kruskal, "A nonparametric test for the several sample problem", Ann. Math. Statist. 23 (1952) 525–540, p. 535, equations (7.1)–(7.2); the lower bound is derived.
