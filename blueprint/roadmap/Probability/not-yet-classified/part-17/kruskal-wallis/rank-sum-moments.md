# Null moments of the rank sums

For an assignment `g` of `N ≥ 2` positions to `C` samples of sizes `n_i ≥ 1`, under the uniform
law on rankings, `E R_i = n_i (N + 1)/2`, `Var R_i = n_i (N - n_i)(N + 1)/12`, and for `i ≠ j`,
`Cov(R_i, R_j) = -n_i n_j (N + 1)/12`.

## Sources

- W. H. Kruskal and W. A. Wallis, "Use of ranks in one-criterion variance analysis", J. Amer. Statist. Assoc. 47 (1952) 583–621, Section 3; W. H. Kruskal, "A nonparametric test for the several sample problem", Ann. Math. Statist. 23 (1952) 525–540, p. 527.
