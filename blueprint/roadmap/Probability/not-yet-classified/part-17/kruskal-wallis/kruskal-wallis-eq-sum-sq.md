# The two forms of `H`

For an assignment `g` of `N ≥ 1` positions to
`C` samples, each of size `n_i ≥ 1`, and every ranking `σ`,
`H = 12 / (N (N + 1)) ∑_i n_i (R_i / n_i - (N + 1)/2)²`.

## Sources

- W. H. Kruskal, "A nonparametric test for the several sample problem", Ann. Math. Statist. 23 (1952) 525–540, p. 526.
