# Two samples

For an assignment `g` of `N` positions to two samples
of sizes `n₀, n₁ ≥ 1` and every ranking `σ`,
`H = 12 (R_0 - n_0 (N + 1)/2)² / ((N + 1) n_0 (N - n_0))`.

## Sources

- W. H. Kruskal, "A nonparametric test for the several sample problem", Ann. Math. Statist. 23 (1952) 525–540, p. 526.
