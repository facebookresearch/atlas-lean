# Null mean and variance of `H`

For an assignment `g` of
`N ≥ 2` positions to `C` samples of sizes `n_i ≥ 1`, under the uniform law on rankings,
`E H = C - 1` and
`Var H = 2 (C - 1) - 2 (3C² - 6C + N (2C² - 6C + 1)) / (5 N (N + 1)) - (6/5) ∑_i 1 / n_i`.

## Sources

- W. H. Kruskal, "A nonparametric test for the several sample problem", Ann. Math. Statist. 23 (1952) 525–540, p. 526 and p. 535, equations (6.2)–(6.5).
