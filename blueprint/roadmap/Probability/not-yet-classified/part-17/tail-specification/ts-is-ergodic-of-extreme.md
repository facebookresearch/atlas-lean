# Ts is Ergodic of extreme

A translation-invariant probability measure that is a specification state and is extreme among
all specification probability measures (`hext`) is ergodic.

The literature characterizes ergodicity by extremality among *invariant* Gibbs measures
(Georgii 1988, §14); `hext` asks for extremality among all specification measures, a stronger
hypothesis, and only the implication to ergodicity is proved.
