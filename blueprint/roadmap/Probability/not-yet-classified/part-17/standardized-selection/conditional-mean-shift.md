# Conditional Mean Shift

The totalized integral shift `(∫ ω, f ω ∂μ[|s]) - ∫ ω, f ω ∂μ`, where `μ[|s]` is the
normalized conditional measure on `s` and the second term is the unnormalized Bochner integral
against `μ`. When `μ` is a probability measure, `f` is integrable, and `s` is a measurable event
of positive mass, this is the difference of population means (the selection differential of `f`
under selection on `s`).
