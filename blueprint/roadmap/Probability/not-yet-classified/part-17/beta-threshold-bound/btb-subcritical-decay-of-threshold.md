# Btb subcritical decay of threshold

Exponential decay below the threshold: for `β < β₁` and `f_n` satisfying the differential
inequality `hdiff` and the monotonicity `hfmono` on `[β - 2δ, β]`, `f_n(β - 2δ) ≤ M e^{-n δ / Q}`
for some `Q > 0`. `hdiff` is the input from the OSSS inequality and is not derived here.
