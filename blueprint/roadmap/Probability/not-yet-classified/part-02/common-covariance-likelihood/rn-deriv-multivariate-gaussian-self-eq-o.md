# Gaussian Radon–Nikodym derivative with equal means is one

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $s \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive definite. Then

$$\frac{d\,\mathcal N(s, R)}{d\,\mathcal N(s, R)}(y) = 1$$

for Lebesgue-almost every $y \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$.
