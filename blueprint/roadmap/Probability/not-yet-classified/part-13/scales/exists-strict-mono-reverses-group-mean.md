# Monotone recoding can reverse a group-mean comparison

There are a strictly increasing $f : \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$, four values $x_1, \dots, x_4$ and disjoint nonempty groups $A, B$ such that the ranks of $f(x_i)$ equal the ranks of $x_i$, the mean of $x$ over $B$ is below its mean over $A$, and the mean of $f(x)$ over $A$ is below its mean over $B$.
