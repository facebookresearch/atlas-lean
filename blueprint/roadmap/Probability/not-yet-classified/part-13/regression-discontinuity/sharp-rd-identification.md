# Identification in the sharp regression-discontinuity design

On a finite measure space, let the running variable $X$ be measurable, treatment be $\mathbf 1\{X \ge c\}$, and the potential outcomes $Y(0), Y(1)$ be integrable. Let $m_d$ be a version of $x \mapsto \mathbb E[Y(d) \mid X = x]$, continuous at $c$, for $d = 0, 1$, and suppose every one-sided neighborhood $(c, c + \varepsilon)$ and $(c - \varepsilon, c)$ has positive probability under the law of $X$. Then for any version $r$ of $x \mapsto \mathbb E[Y \mid X = x]$, with $Y$ the observed outcome, that has one-sided limits at $c$,

$$\lim_{x \downarrow c} r(x) - \lim_{x \uparrow c} r(x) = m_1(c) - m_0(c).$$
