# Graded-response category probabilities sum to one

In the graded response model with discrimination $a$ and thresholds $b_1, \dots, b_m$, let $P^\ast_0 = 1$, $P^\ast_j = \sigma(a(\theta - b_j))$ for $1 \le j \le m$ and $P^\ast_{m+1} = 0$, with $\sigma$ the logistic function, and let the category probabilities be $P_j = P^\ast_j - P^\ast_{j+1}$. Then $\sum_{j=0}^{m} P_j = 1$ for every $a$, $b$ and $\theta$.
