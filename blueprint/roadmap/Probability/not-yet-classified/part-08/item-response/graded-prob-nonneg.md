# Graded-response category probabilities are nonnegative

Let $a \ge 0$, thresholds $b_1 \le \dots \le b_m$, and ability $\theta$. With cumulative probabilities $P^*_0 = 1$, $P^*_j = \sigma\big(a(\theta - b_j)\big)$ for $1 \le j \le m$ (logistic $\sigma$) and $P^*_{m+1} = 0$, every category probability $P_j = P^*_j - P^*_{j+1}$, $0 \le j \le m$, is nonnegative.
