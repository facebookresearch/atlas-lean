# Two-parameter logistic model is invariant under positive affine rescaling

Let $a_i > 0$ be item discriminations, $b_i$ difficulties, $\theta$ an ability, and $A > 0$, $B$ real. Then $a_i/A > 0$ for every item, and the two-parameter logistic law (items independent, item $i$ correct with probability $\sigma(a_i(\theta - b_i))$, $\sigma$ the logistic function) with parameters $(a, b, \theta)$ equals the law with parameters $(a_i/A,\ A b_i + B,\ A\theta + B)$.
