# Wald consistency of the MLE with identifiability of the law

Let $\Theta$ be a pseudometric space, $\nu$ a $\sigma$-finite measure, $K \subseteq \Theta$ compact and $\theta_0 \in K$. For $\theta \in K$ let $p_\theta$ be measurable with $p_\theta > 0$ $\nu$-a.e. and $p_\theta\,\nu$ a probability measure. Let $X_0$ be a.e. measurable with law $p_{\theta_0}\nu$, let $\log p_\theta$ be integrable under $p_{\theta_0}\nu$ for every $\theta \in K$, and assume identifiability: $p_\theta \nu = p_{\theta_0}\nu$ with $\theta \in K$ implies $\theta = \theta_0$. Assume $\theta \mapsto \log p_\theta(x)$ is continuous on $K$ for every $x$, and the uniform law of large numbers: almost surely $\frac1n \sum_{i<n} \log p_\theta(X_i) \to \mathbb E \log p_\theta(X_0)$ uniformly on $K$. Then every maximum-likelihood estimator $\hat\theta_n$ on $K$ (maximizing the average log-likelihood over $K$) converges to $\theta_0$ almost surely.
