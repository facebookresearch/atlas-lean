# Monotone latent curves give nonnegative item covariance

Let the latent state space $Z$ be a finite linear order with law $\pi$, and let item curves $q_i(z) \in [0, 1]$ give conditionally independent binary responses. If the curves of two distinct items $i$ and $j$ are both monotone in the latent state, then the covariance of their response indicators under the observed mixture law is nonnegative. This is the Fortuin–Kasteleyn–Ginibre inequality on the chain $Z$, transferred to the observed law.
