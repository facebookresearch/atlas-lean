# Fisher information adds over iid observations

Let $\nu$ be a measure and $p_\theta$ a family of densities with respect to $\nu$. At a parameter $\theta$, suppose that $p_\theta(x) > 0$ for every $x$, that $p_\theta$ is measurable and differentiable in $\theta$ at every $x$, that $p_\theta\,\nu$ is a probability measure, and that the score $\partial_\theta\log p_\theta$ is integrable, square integrable and has mean zero under $p_\theta\,\nu$. Then the Fisher information at $\theta$ of $n$ independent observations, with joint density $\prod_i p_\theta(x_i)$ with respect to $\nu^{\otimes n}$, is $n$ times the information of a single observation.
