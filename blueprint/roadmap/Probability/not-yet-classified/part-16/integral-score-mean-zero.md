# Score has mean zero under regularity

Let $\nu$ be a measure and $p_\theta$, $\theta \in \mathbb R$, measurable functions such that $p_{\theta_0}(x) > 0$ for all $x$, $\theta \mapsto p_\theta(x)$ is differentiable everywhere for each $x$, every $p_\theta\,\nu$ is a probability measure, and some $\nu$-integrable $C$ satisfies $|\partial_\theta p_t(x)| \le C(x)$ for all $t$ and $x$. Then the score $\partial_\theta \log p_\theta(x)$ at $\theta_0$ has mean zero under $p_{\theta_0}\,\nu$.
