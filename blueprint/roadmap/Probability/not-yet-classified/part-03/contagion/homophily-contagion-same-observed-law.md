# Homophily and contagion give the same observed law

Let ego trait, peer trait and shared environment be independent uniform bits. A tie is present when the two traits agree, and ego behavior is ego trait XOR environment. Under homophily, peer behavior is peer trait XOR environment and does not depend on ego. Under contagion, on a tie the peer acts exactly when ego acts, and off the tie the peer behaves as under homophily. Both mechanisms induce the same joint law of (tie, ego behavior, observed peer behavior).
