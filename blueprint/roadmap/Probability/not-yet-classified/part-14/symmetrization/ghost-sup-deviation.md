# Ghost-sample sup deviation

For a finite class of functions $f_1, \dots, f_M$ on $Z$ and samples $S = (S_1, \dots, S_n)$ and $S' = (S'_1, \dots, S'_n)$ in $Z$, the ghost-sample deviation is

$$\sup_{j \le M} \Bigl|\frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n f_j(S_i) - \frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n f_j(S'_i)\Bigr|,$$

with the value $0$ when $n = 0$, by the convention $1/0 = 0$, and $0$ when $M = 0$ (empty supremum).
