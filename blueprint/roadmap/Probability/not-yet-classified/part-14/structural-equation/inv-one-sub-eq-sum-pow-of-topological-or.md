# Inverse of I - B for DAG-supported B

Let $B$ be a square matrix over a commutative ring, indexed by a finite set $V$, and suppose there is a ranking $\mathrm{ord} : V \to \{0, \dots, |V| - 1\}$ with $B_{ij} \neq 0$ only when $\mathrm{ord}(j) < \mathrm{ord}(i)$. Then $(I - B)^{-1} = \sum_{k < |V|} B^k$.
