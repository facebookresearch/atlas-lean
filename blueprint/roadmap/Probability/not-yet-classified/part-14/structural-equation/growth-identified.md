# Growth identified

In the three-time-point linear growth model with loading matrix $\Lambda$ with rows $(1, 0), (1, 1), (1, 2)$, symmetric $2 \times 2$ intercept-slope covariance $G$ and error variances $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^3$, the model covariance is $\Lambda G \Lambda^\top + \mathrm{diag}(\theta)$. If two parameter sets with symmetric $G, G'$ give equal model covariances, $\Lambda G \Lambda^\top + \mathrm{diag}(\theta) = \Lambda G' \Lambda^\top + \mathrm{diag}(\theta')$, then $G = G'$ and $\theta = \theta'$.
