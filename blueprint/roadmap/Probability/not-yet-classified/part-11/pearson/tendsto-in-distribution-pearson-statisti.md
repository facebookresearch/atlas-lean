# Pearson's chi-square theorem

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent, identically distributed observations in a finite set of $k \ge 2$ cells, with cell probabilities $P(X_0 = i) = p_i > 0$. Let $N_i$ be the number of $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ in cell $i$. Then Pearson's statistic $\sum_i (N_i - n p_i)^2 / (n p_i)$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $k - 1$ degrees of freedom.
