# Omitted-variable formula for the OLS slope

For finite samples $x, z, y$, suppose $\theta = (\theta_0, \theta_1, \theta_2)$ solves the normal equations of the long regression of $y$ on $(1, x, z)$ and the sample sum of squares of $x$ about its mean, $S_{xx}$, is nonzero. Then the short least-squares slope of $y$ on $x$ equals $\theta_1 + \theta_2 b_{zx}$, where $b_{zx}$ is the least-squares slope of $z$ on $x$.
