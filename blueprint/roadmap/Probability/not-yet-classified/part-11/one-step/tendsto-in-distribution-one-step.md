# One-step estimator asymptotic normality

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $X_0, X_1, \dots$ i.i.d. on a probability space with law $P_{\theta_0}$ (density $p(\theta_0, \cdot)$ with respect to $\nu$), and $s(\theta, x)$ the score, jointly measurable, with $\mathbb{E}\|s(\theta_0, X)\|^2 < \infty$ and $\mathbb{E}\, s(\theta_0, X) = 0$. Suppose that for some $\delta > 0$ and integrable measurable $M$, each $s(\cdot, x)$ is differentiable on the ball of radius $\delta$ about $\theta_0$ with $\|D_\theta s(\theta, x) - D_\theta s(\theta_0, x)\| \le M(x)\|\theta - \theta_0\|$ there; that $H(x) = D_\theta s(\theta_0, x)$ has $n^{-1} \sum_{i < n} H(X_i) \to \mathbb{E} H$ in probability with $\mathbb{E} H = -J$, where the Fisher information $J$ is invertible; and that $Y$ is an almost-everywhere measurable random vector on a probability space with $\langle Y, t \rangle \sim N(0, \langle J t, t \rangle)$ for every $t$. If the starters $\tilde\theta_n$ are almost-everywhere measurable and $\sqrt{n}(\tilde\theta_n - \theta_0)$ is bounded in probability, then

$$\sqrt{n}\Big(\tilde\theta_n + J^{-1} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i < n} s(\tilde\theta_n, X_i) - \theta_0\Big) \Rightarrow J^{-1} Y.$$
