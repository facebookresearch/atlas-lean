# Affine half-space probability under the standard Gaussian

Let $Z$ be standard Gaussian on $\mathbb R^n$ with the Euclidean norm and let $L$ be a nonzero linear functional on $\mathbb R^n$. For all real $a$ and $t$,

$$P(a + L Z \le t) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{t - a}{\lVert L\rVert}\right),$$

where $\Phi$ is the standard normal distribution function and $\lVert L\rVert$ is the Euclidean operator norm of $L$.
