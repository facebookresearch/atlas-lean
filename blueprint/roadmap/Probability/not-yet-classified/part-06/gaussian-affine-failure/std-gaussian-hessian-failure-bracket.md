# Gaussian failure bracket from a Hessian bound

Let $R \ge 0$ and let $g : \mathbb R^n \to \mathbb R$ be $C^2$ at every point of the closed ball $B_R$ of radius $R$ about $0$, with $\|D^2 g(y)\| \le M$ on $B_R$ and $Dg(0) \ne 0$. With $Z$ standard Gaussian, $\Phi$ the standard normal CDF and $\delta = M R^2/2$,

$$\Phi\!\left(\frac{-\delta - g(0)}{\|Dg(0)\|}\right) - P(Z \notin B_R) \le P(g(Z) \le 0) \le \Phi\!\left(\frac{\delta - g(0)}{\|Dg(0)\|}\right) + P(Z \notin B_R).$$
