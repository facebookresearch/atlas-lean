# Finite-class holdout bound

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $f_1, \dots, f_M$ measurable losses with values in $[0, 1]$ $\mu$-almost everywhere, $n > 0$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$. For an i.i.d. sample of size $n$ from $\mu$, the probability that some $j$ has $\left|\frac1n\sum_{i} f_j(Z_i) - \int f_j\,d\mu\right| \ge \varepsilon$ is at most $2Me^{-2n\varepsilon^2}$.
