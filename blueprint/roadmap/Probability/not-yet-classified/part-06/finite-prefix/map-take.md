# Prefix marginals of autoregressive word laws

Let $q$ assign to each finite word over a finite token set a probability distribution on the next token, and let $\mathrm{law}_n$ be the distribution of length-$n$ words obtained by drawing each token from $q$ applied to the prefix drawn so far. For $m \le n$, the distribution of the length-$m$ prefix of a $\mathrm{law}_n$ word is $\mathrm{law}_m$.
