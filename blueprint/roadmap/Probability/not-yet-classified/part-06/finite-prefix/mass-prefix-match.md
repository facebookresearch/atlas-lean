# Mass of a word under a next-token law

Let $q$ assign to every finite prefix a probability distribution on a finite token set, and let the horizon-$n$ law generate a word of length $n$ by drawing each token from $q$ at the current prefix. For every word $w$ of length $n$, its probability under this law is the product, in order, of the next-token probabilities $q(\text{prefix})(\text{next token})$ over its proper prefixes.
