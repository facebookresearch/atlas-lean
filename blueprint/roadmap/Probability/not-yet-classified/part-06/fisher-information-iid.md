# Fisher information adds over iid observations

The joint n-sample information is
`n` times the single-observation information. The score mean-zero interface is discharged
at integration by `integral_score_mean_zero` (L7-14).

## Sources

- Cramér (1946), ch. 32; Rao (1945); Lehmann & Casella (1998), ch. 2; see the module References. The `hS_int`/`hS2_int` hypotheses are this formalization's added regularity.
