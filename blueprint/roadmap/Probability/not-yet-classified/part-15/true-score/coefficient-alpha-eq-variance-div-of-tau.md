# Coefficient alpha under tau-equivalence

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure and $k \ge 2$ items $X_i = T + c_i + E_i$ with a square-integrable true score $T$, constants $c_i$ and square-integrable errors $E_i$, where $\operatorname{Cov}(T, E_i) = 0$ for all $i$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(E_i, E_j) = 0$ for $i \ne j$. If $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_i X_i) > 0$, then coefficient alpha

$$\frac{k}{k - 1}\left(1 - \frac{\sum_i \operatorname{Var}(X_i)}{\operatorname{Var}(\sum_i X_i)}\right) = \frac{\operatorname{Var}(k T)}{\operatorname{Var}(\sum_i X_i)}.$$
