# Portfolio variance

Let $p$ be any real weights on a finite set $\Omega$, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$ and $w \in \mathbb{R}^n$, with expectation, variance and covariance taken as weighted sums under $p$. Then $w^\top \Sigma\, w = \operatorname{Var}_p(w \cdot X)$, where $\Sigma_{ij}$ is the covariance of coordinates $i$ and $j$ of $X$.
