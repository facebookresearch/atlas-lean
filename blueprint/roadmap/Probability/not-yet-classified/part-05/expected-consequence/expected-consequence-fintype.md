# Expected consequence over finitely many damage states

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on hazard values, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel from hazards to a finite set of damage states with measurable singletons, and $L$ a loss on damage states with values in $[0, \infty]$. Then the expected consequence $\int \int L\,d\kappa(a)\,d\mu(a)$ equals

$$\int \sum_b \kappa(a, \{b\})\,L(b)\,d\mu(a).$$
