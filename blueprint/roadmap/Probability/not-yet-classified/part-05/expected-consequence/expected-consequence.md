# Expected consequence

For a Markov kernel $\kappa$ from hazard outcomes to damage states, a probability measure $\mu$ on hazard outcomes and a loss $L$ with values in $[0, \infty]$, the expected consequence is

$$\int \left( \int L(b)\, \kappa(a, db) \right) \mu(da) \in [0, \infty],$$

the loss averaged over damage states for each hazard outcome and then over the hazard law.
