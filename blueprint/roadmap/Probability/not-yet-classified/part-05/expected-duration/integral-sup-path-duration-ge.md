# Expected longest path is at least the longest expected path

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let the job durations $D_j$ be integrable, and let finitely many paths (at least one) each be a finite multiset of jobs, with path duration the sum of its job durations. Then $\mathbb{E}\big[\max_p \sum_{j \in p} D_j\big] \ge \max_p \sum_{j \in p} \mathbb{E}[D_j]$.
