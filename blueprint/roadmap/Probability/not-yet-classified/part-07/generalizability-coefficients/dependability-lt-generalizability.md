# Dependability is below generalizability

For a crossed person-by-item design with $m \ge 1$ items, person variance $\sigma_P^2 > 0$, item variance $\sigma_I^2 > 0$ and residual variance $\sigma_e^2 \ge 0$, the dependability coefficient $\sigma_P^2/(\sigma_P^2 + (\sigma_I^2 + \sigma_e^2)/m)$ is strictly below the generalizability coefficient $\sigma_P^2/(\sigma_P^2 + \sigma_e^2/m)$.
