# Normal limit of an asymptotically linear estimator (scalar)

If
`√n (θ̂ₙ - θ) = n^{-1/2} ∑ φ(Xᵢ) + oₚ(1)` with `φ` an influence function (integrable,
square integrable, mean zero), then `√n (θ̂ₙ - θ) ⇒ N(0, E φ²)`: the central limit theorem gives the limit of the linear part and Slutsky's lemma removes the remainder. The `oₚ(1)` remainder is a hypothesis of the statement.
