# Normal limit of an asymptotically linear estimator (vector)

Let $X_1, X_2, \dots$ be independent with common law $\mu_X$, and let the estimator take values in a finite-dimensional real inner product space. If
`√n (θ̂ₙ - θ) = n^{-1/2} ∑ φ(Xᵢ) + oₚ(1)` with `φ` an influence function for $\mu_X$ (integrable,
square integrable, mean zero) and each `θ̂ₙ` measurable, then `√n (θ̂ₙ - θ) ⇒ Y` with `⟪Y, t⟫ ~ N(0, E ⟪φ, t⟫²)`: the multivariate central limit theorem plus Slutsky's lemma. As in the scalar case, the `oₚ(1)` remainder is a hypothesis of the statement.
