# DeGroot opinions converge to consensus

Let $T$ be a primitive row-stochastic matrix on a finite set, and let $\pi$ be a probability vector with $\pi T = \pi$. For every initial opinion vector $x_0$, the opinions $T^t x_0$ converge as $t \to \infty$ to the constant vector whose entries all equal $\pi \cdot x_0$.
