# DeGroot opinion dynamics

For a finite set of agents, a real square matrix $T$, an initial belief vector $x_0$ and $t \in \mathbb N$, the DeGroot belief vector after $t$ rounds of updating is $T^t x_0$. No conditions on $T$ are needed for the definition (B. Golub and M. O. Jackson, Naive learning in social networks and the wisdom of crowds, American Economic Journal: Microeconomics 2 (2010), equation (1)).
