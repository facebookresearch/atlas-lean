# DeGroot opinions converge to consensus

Let a nonempty finite set of agents have a row-stochastic and primitive influence matrix $T$. Then there are influence weights $\pi$, entrywise positive, summing to $1$, with $\pi T = \pi$, such that for every initial belief vector $x_0$ the opinions $T^t x_0$ converge as $t \to \infty$ to the constant vector with every entry $\pi \cdot x_0$. This is Lemma 3 of Golub and Jackson, \"Naive learning in social networks and the wisdom of crowds\" (2010), with the weights derived.
