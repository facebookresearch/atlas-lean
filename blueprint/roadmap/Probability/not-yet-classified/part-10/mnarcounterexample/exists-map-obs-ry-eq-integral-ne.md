# Nonidentification of the mean under unmodeled MNAR

Let $P$ be a probability law of a response indicator $R$ and real outcome $Y$ with $Y$ integrable and $P(R = 0) \ne 0$, where the observed data reveal $Y$ only when $R = 1$. For every functional $F$ of the observed-data law there is a probability law $Q$ with $Y$ integrable and the same observed-data law as $P$ such that $F$ of that observed-data law differs from $E_Q[Y]$.
