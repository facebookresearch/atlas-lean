# Sequence of independent uniform random signs

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space and $\varepsilon_0, \varepsilon_1, \dots : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. The sequence is a sequence of independent uniform random signs if

- each $\varepsilon_k$ is measurable,
- the $\varepsilon_k$ are mutually independent under $\mu$,
- for each $k$, $\varepsilon_k = 1$ or $\varepsilon_k = -1$ $\mu$-almost everywhere, and
- $\mu(\varepsilon_k = 1) = 1/2$ for each $k$.
