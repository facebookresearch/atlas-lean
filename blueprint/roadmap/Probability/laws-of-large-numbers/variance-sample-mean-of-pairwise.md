# Variance of the sample mean under pairwise independence

Let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ ($n \ge 1$) be square-integrable real random variables that are pairwise independent and have common variance $\sigma^2$. Then the sample mean $\frac{1}{n}\sum_i X_i$ has variance $\sigma^2 / n$. Pairwise independence suffices; mutual independence is not needed.
