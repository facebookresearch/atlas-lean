# Truncation preserves almost-sure limits of bounded partial sums

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$, let $A \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $Y_0, Y_1, \dots$ be real
functions on $\Omega$ with $|Y_n(\omega)| \le A$ for all $n$ and $\omega$. If the partial sums
$\sum_{i < n} Y_i$ converge $\mu$-almost everywhere to some limit, then so do the partial sums
of the [truncations](truncated-rv.md) $Y_i \mathbf{1}_{\{|Y_i| \le A\}}$ (which equal the $Y_i$).
