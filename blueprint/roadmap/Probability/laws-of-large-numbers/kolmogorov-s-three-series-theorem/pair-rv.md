# Pair of coordinates from two independent copies

For real functions $Y_0, Y_1, \dots$ on $\Omega$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the pair of the $n$-th
coordinates of two copies is the map $\Omega \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^2$,
$(\omega, \omega') \mapsto (Y_n(\omega), Y_n(\omega'))$. Under the product measure
$\mu \otimes \mu$ the two coordinates are independent copies of $Y_n$.
