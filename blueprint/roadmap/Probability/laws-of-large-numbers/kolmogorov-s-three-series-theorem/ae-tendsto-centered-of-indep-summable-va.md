# Almost sure convergence of centered truncated sums

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent measurable
real random variables, let $A > 0$, and let $Y_n = X_n \mathbf{1}_{\{|X_n| \le A\}}$ be the
[truncations](truncated-rv.md). If $\sum_n \operatorname{Var}(Y_n) < \infty$, then almost surely
the partial sums $\sum_{i < n} \big(Y_i - \mathbb{E}[Y_i]\big)$ converge to a finite limit.
