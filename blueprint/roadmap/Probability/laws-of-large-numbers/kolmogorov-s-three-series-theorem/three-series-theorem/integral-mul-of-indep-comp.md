# Expectation of a product of independent functions factors

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $f, g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be independent random
variables, and let $\varphi, \psi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable with
$\varphi \circ f$ and $\psi \circ g$ almost everywhere strongly measurable. Then

$$\int \varphi(f)\, \psi(g) \, d\mu = \int \varphi(f) \, d\mu \cdot \int \psi(g) \, d\mu ,$$

where each integral is the Bochner integral (taken to be $0$ for a non-integrable integrand).
