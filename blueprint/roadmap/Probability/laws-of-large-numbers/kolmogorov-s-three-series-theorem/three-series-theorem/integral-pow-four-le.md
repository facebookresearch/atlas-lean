# Fourth-moment bound for sums of bounded independent variables

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, let $Z_0, Z_1, \dots$ be independent measurable real random
variables, and let $B \ge 0$ with $|Z_k| \le B$ everywhere and $\mathbb{E}[Z_k] = 0$ for every $k$.
For $m \le n$ put $S = \sum_{m \le k < n} Z_k$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}[S^4] \le 3\, \mathbb{E}[S^2]^2 + B^2\, \mathbb{E}[S^2], \qquad \mathbb{E}[S^2] = \sum_{m \le k < n} \mathbb{E}[Z_k^2], \qquad \mathbb{E}[S] = 0 .$$
