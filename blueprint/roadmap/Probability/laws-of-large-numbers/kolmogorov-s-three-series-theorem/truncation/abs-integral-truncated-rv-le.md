# Mean bound for a truncated random variable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be real functions on
$\Omega$ with $X_n$ measurable, and let $A \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [truncation](truncated-rv.md)
$Y_n = X_n \mathbf{1}_{\{|X_n| \le A\}}$ satisfies $\big|\int Y_n \, d\mu\big| \le |A|$.
