# Truncated random variables lie in every Lp space

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\Omega$, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be real functions on $\Omega$
with $X_n$ measurable, and let $A \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the [truncation](truncated-rv.md)
$Y_n = X_n \mathbf{1}_{\{|X_n| \le A\}}$ lies in $L^p(\mu)$ for every $p \in [0, \infty]$.
