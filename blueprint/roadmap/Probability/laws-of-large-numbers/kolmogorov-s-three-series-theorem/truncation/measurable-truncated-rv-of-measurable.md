# Measurability of a truncated random variable

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be real functions on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $A \in \mathbb{R}$ and
let $n$ be such that $X_n$ is measurable. Then the [truncation](truncated-rv.md)
$Y_n = X_n \mathbf{1}_{\{|X_n| \le A\}}$ is measurable; the other $X_i$ need not be.
