# Truncation factors through scalar truncation

For real functions $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on $\Omega$, $A \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the
[truncation](truncated-rv.md) $Y_n$ equals $\tau_A \circ X_n$, where
$\tau_A(x) = x \mathbf{1}_{\{|x| \le A\}}$ is the [scalar truncation](truncation-fun.md).
