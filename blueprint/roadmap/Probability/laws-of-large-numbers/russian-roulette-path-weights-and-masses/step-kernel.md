# Continuation kernel of a stopped Markov chain

For real functions $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $P : S \times S \to \mathbb{R}$ on a set of states, the continuation (survival) kernel is $Q(i, j) = (1 - q(i))\, P(i, j)$. When $q$ gives stop probabilities in $[0, 1]$ and $P$ is a stochastic next-state kernel, $Q$ is the sub-stochastic kernel of surviving and moving.
