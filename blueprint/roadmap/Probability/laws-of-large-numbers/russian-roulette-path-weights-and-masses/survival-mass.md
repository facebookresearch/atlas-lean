# Survival mass of paths of length n

For a finite set of states $S$, a survival kernel $Q$, a start state $i$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the survival mass at level $n$ is the total survival weight $\sum \prod_{k<n} Q(x_k, x_{k+1})$ over all paths $(x_0, \dots, x_n)$ with $x_0 = i$: the raw mass of paths that have not stopped in their first $n$ transitions.
