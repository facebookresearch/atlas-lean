# Completed-path weight

For real functions $q : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and $Q : S \times S \to \mathbb{R}$ and a path $x = (x_0, \dots, x_n)$ of states, the completed-path weight is $q(x_n) \prod_{k<n} Q(x_k, x_{k+1})$. For stop probabilities $q$ and a survival kernel $Q$ it is the survival weight of the path times the stop probability at its final state.
