# Certain extinction in the critical and subcritical cases

Let $b, d$ be real numbers with $0 < d$ and $b \le d$. Then [Kendall's extinction
probability](extinction-prob.md) satisfies $\alpha(t) \to 1$ as $t \to \infty$. For a birth rate
$b \ge 0$, so $0 \le b \le d$ (the critical and subcritical cases), this says that extinction is
certain in the limit.
