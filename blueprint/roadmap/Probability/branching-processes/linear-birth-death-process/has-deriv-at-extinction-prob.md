# Backward Kolmogorov equation for the extinction probability

Let $b, d \ge 0$ and $t \ge 0$, and let $\alpha$ be [Kendall's extinction
probability](extinction-prob.md). Then $\alpha$ is differentiable at $t$, with

$$\alpha'(t) = d - (b + d)\, \alpha(t) + b\, \alpha(t)^2 .$$

## Sources

- S. Karlin and H. M. Taylor, *A First Course in Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., Academic Press,
  1975, Chapter 4.
