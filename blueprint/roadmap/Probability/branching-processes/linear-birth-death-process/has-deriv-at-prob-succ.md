# Forward Kolmogorov equation for positive states

Let $b, d \ge 0$, $t \ge 0$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $p_k(s)$ denote [Kendall's
law](prob.md) at time $s$. Then $s \mapsto p_{n+1}(s)$ is differentiable at $t$, with

$$p_{n+1}'(t) = b\, n\, p_n(t) - (b + d)(n + 1)\, p_{n+1}(t) + d\,(n + 2)\, p_{n+2}(t) .$$
