# Borel law

Let $r \ge 0$ and $n \ge 1$, and let $p(j) = e^{-r} r^j / j!$ be the Poisson($r$) weights. Then
the [hitting weight](hitting-weight.md) for one ancestor is

$$H_p(1, n) = \frac{e^{-rn} (rn)^{n-1}}{n!} .$$

That is, for Poisson($r$) offspring and one ancestor, the total progeny $T$ satisfies
$P(T = n) = e^{-rn} (rn)^{n-1} / n!$ for $n \ge 1$.

## Sources

- É. Borel, *Sur l'emploi du théorème de Bernoulli pour faciliter le calcul d'une infinité de
  coefficients*, Comptes Rendus de l'Académie des Sciences de Paris 214 (1942), 452–456.
