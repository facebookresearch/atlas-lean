# Probability of first passage at time n

Let $\nu$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{N}$, let $k, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and put
$p(j) = \nu(\{j\})$. Under the product measure $\nu^{\otimes n}$ on $\mathbb{N}^n$, the set of
$x$ with $x_0 + \dots + x_{n-1} + k = n$ that are [first-passage sequences](is-first-passage.md)
(the walk from $k$ first hits $0$ at time $n$) has measure equal to the [hitting
weight](hitting-weight.md) $H_p(k, n)$. When $\nu$ is a probability measure this is the
probability $P(T = n)$ that the total progeny of $k$ ancestors is $n$.
