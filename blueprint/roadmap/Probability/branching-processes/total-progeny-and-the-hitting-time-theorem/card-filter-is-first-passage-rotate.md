# Cycle lemma

Let $k, n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $k \ge 1$, and let $x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}) \in \mathbb{N}^n$
satisfy $x_0 + \dots + x_{n-1} + k = n$. Then exactly $k$ of the $n$ cyclic shifts
$i \mapsto x_{(i + r) \bmod n}$, $0 \le r < n$, are [first-passage
sequences](is-first-passage.md) for $k$ and $n$.

## Sources

- A. Dvoretzky and Th. Motzkin, *A problem of arrangements*, Duke Mathematical Journal 14
  (1947), 305–313.
