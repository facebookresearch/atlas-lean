# Borel–Tanner law

Let $r \ge 0$ and $k \le n$ be natural numbers, and let $p(j) = e^{-r} r^j / j!$ be the
Poisson($r$) weights. Then the [hitting weight](hitting-weight.md) satisfies

$$n \, H_p(k, n) = k \, \frac{e^{-rn} (rn)^{n-k}}{(n-k)!} .$$

That is, for Poisson($r$) offspring and $k$ ancestors, the total progeny $T$ satisfies
$n \, P(T = n) = k \, e^{-rn} (rn)^{n-k} / (n-k)!$.

## Sources

- J. C. Tanner, *A derivation of the Borel distribution*, Biometrika 48 (1961), 222–224.
- F. A. Haight and M. A. Breuer, *The Borel–Tanner distribution*, Biometrika 47 (1960), 143–150.
