# Mean generation size of a Galton–Watson process

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) with [mean](mean.md)
$m \in [0, \infty]$, and let $Z_n$ have the [law of the $n$-th generation size](law.md). Then
$E[Z_n] = m^n$ in $[0, \infty]$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

## Sources

- K. B. Athreya and P. E. Ney, *Branching Processes*, Springer, 1972, Chapter I.
