# Generating function of the n-th generation size

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) with generating function
$f(s) = \sum_{k \ge 0} \nu(\{k\})\, s^k$, and let $Z_n$ have the [law of the $n$-th generation
size](law.md). Then $E[s^{Z_n}] = f^{\circ n}(s)$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every
$s \in [0, 1]$.

## Sources

- K. B. Athreya and P. E. Ney, *Branching Processes*, Springer, 1972, Chapter I.
