# Galton–Watson extinction criterion

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) with $\nu(\{1\}) \ne 1$,
let $q$ be the [extinction probability](extinction-prob.md) and let $m \in [0, \infty]$ be the
[mean offspring number](mean.md). Then $q = 1$ if and only if $m \le 1$: extinction is certain
exactly when the mean offspring number is at most $1$.

## Sources

- K. B. Athreya and P. E. Ney, *Branching Processes*, Springer, 1972, Chapter I.
