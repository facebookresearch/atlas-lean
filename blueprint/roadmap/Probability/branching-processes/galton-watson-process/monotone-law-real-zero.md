# Extinction by generation n is nondecreasing in n

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) and let $Z_n$ have the
[law of the $n$-th generation size](law.md). Then $n \mapsto P(Z_n = 0)$ is nondecreasing.

Equivalently, survival to generation $n$, $n \mapsto 1 - P(Z_n = 0)$, is nonincreasing.
