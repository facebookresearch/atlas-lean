# Extinction by generation n converges to the extinction probability

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) and let $Z_n$ have the
[law of the $n$-th generation size](law.md). Then $P(Z_n = 0) \to q$ as $n \to \infty$, where $q$
is the [extinction probability](extinction-prob.md).

Equivalently, survival to generation $n$ converges to the survival probability:
$1 - P(Z_n = 0) \to 1 - q$.
