# Extinction probability is the least fixed point

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ (the offspring law) with generating function
$f(s) = \sum_{k \ge 0} \nu(\{k\})\, s^k$. Then the [extinction probability](extinction-prob.md)
$q$ is the least element of $\{s \in [0, 1] : f(s) = s\}$: it is a fixed point of $f$ in
$[0, 1]$, and $q \le s$ for every fixed point $s \in [0, 1]$ of $f$.

## Sources

- K. B. Athreya and P. E. Ney, *Branching Processes*, Springer, 1972, Chapter I.
