# Limit of generating-function iterates is the least fixed point

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$ with generating function
$f(s) = \sum_{k \ge 0} \nu(\{k\})\, s^k$. Then $\sup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} f^{\circ n}(0)$ is the
least element of $\{s \in [0, 1] : f(s) = s\}$, the least fixed point of $f$ in $[0, 1]$.

- K. B. Athreya and P. E. Ney, *Branching Processes*, Springer, 1972, Chapter I.
