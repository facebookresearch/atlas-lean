## Extinction: specialisation of the least-fixed-point interface

For the binary offspring law $\nu_p$, the extinction probability is the least fixed point of its generating function $1 - p + p s^2$ in $[0, 1]$, and $P(Z_n = 0)$ is the $n$-th iterate of that generating function at $0$: these are the general results [Extinction probability is the least fixed point](../galton-watson-process/is-least-extinction-prob.md) and [Probability of extinction by generation n](../galton-watson-process/law-real-zero.md) at $\nu = \nu_p$.
