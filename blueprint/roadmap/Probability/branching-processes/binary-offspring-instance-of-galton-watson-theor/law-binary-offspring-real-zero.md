# Law binary Offspring real zero

Let $0 \le p \le 1$, and let $Z_n$ be the generation sizes of the Galton–Watson process started from one individual with offspring law $\nu_p = (1 - p)\delta_0 + p\,\delta_2$. Let $f(s) = \int s^{k}\, d\nu_p(k)$ be the probability generating function of $\nu_p$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$,

$$\mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0) = f^{\circ n}(0),$$

the $n$-fold iterate of $f$ evaluated at $0$.
