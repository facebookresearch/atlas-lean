# Is Least extinction Prob binary Offspring

Let $0 \le p \le 1$, let $\nu_p = (1 - p)\delta_0 + p\,\delta_2$, and consider the Galton–Watson process started from one individual with offspring law $\nu_p$, with generation sizes $Z_n$ and extinction probability $q = \sup_n \mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0)$. Let $f(s) = \int s^{k} \, d\nu_p(k)$ be the probability generating function of $\nu_p$. Then $q$ is the least element of

$$\{ s \in [0, 1] : f(s) = s \}.$$
