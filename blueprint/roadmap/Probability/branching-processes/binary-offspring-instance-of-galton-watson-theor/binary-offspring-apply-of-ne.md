# Binary Offspring apply of ne

For every $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \ne 0$ and $n \ne 2$, the binary offspring measure $\nu_p = (1 - p)^{+} \delta_0 + p^{+} \delta_2$ satisfies

$$\nu_p(\{n\}) = 0.$$

It is a probability measure, the binary offspring law, exactly when $0 \le p \le 1$.
