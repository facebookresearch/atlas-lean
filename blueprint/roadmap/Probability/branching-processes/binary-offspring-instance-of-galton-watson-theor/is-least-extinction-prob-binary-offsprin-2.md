# Is Least extinction Prob binary Offspring'

Let $0 \le p \le 1$ and let $q$ be the extinction probability $\sup_n \mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0)$ of the Galton–Watson process started from one individual with offspring law $\nu_p = (1 - p)\delta_0 + p\,\delta_2$. Then $q$ is the least element of

$$\{ s \in [0, 1] : s = 1 - p + p s^2 \}.$$
