# Extinction Prob binary Offspring of gt

Let $1/2 < p \le 1$ and let $q$ be the extinction probability $\sup_n \mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0)$ of the Galton–Watson process started from one individual with offspring law $\nu_p = (1 - p)\delta_0 + p\,\delta_2$. Then

$$q = \frac{1 - p}{p} \in [0, 1).$$

In particular $q = 0$ when $p = 1$.
