# Binary Offspring

For $p \in \mathbb{R}$, the binary offspring law is the measure on $\mathbb{N}$

$$\nu_p = (1 - p)^{+}\, \delta_0 + p^{+}\, \delta_2,$$

where $x^{+} = \max(x, 0)$. For $0 \le p \le 1$ it gives mass $1 - p$ to $0$ and $p$ to $2$. For $p \notin [0, 1]$ it is a finite measure that need not have total mass $1$; for example $\nu_2 = 2\delta_2$.
