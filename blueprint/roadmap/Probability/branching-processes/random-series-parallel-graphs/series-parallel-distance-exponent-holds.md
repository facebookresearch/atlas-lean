# The distance exponent vanishes at criticality

In the Hambly–Jordan random series-parallel graph, at each step every edge is replaced independently by two edges in series with probability $p$ and by two parallel edges otherwise; let $D_n(p)$ be the graph distance between the two extremal vertices after $n$ steps. The exponent $\alpha(p) = \lim_n\frac1n\log\mathbb ED_n(p)$ exists for every $p$, $\alpha(1/2) = 0$, and $\alpha(1/2 + \varepsilon) \sim \pi\sqrt{\varepsilon/6}$ as $\varepsilon \to 0^+$ (Chen, Derrida, Duquesne and Shi).
