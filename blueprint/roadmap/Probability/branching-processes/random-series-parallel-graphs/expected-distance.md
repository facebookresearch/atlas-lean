# Expected extremal distance in the random series-parallel graph

Let $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $D_n(p)$ be the distance between the two extremal vertices of the Hambly–Jordan random series-parallel graph after $n$ steps, with $p$ clamped to $[0, 1]$. The expected extremal distance is

$$\mathbb{E}[D_n(p)] = \sum_{k \in \mathbb{N}} k \, \mathbb{P}(D_n(p) = k),$$

computed in $[0, \infty]$ and read as $0$ if it is infinite. It is in fact finite, since $D_n(p) \le 2^n$.
