# Gaudio–Jaillet lower bound for the BHH constant

The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley
constant $\beta_2$ of the unit square satisfies $0.6277 \le \beta_2$.

## Sources

- J. Gaudio and P. Jaillet, *An improved lower bound for the Traveling Salesman constant*, Oper. Res. Lett. 48
  (2020), 67–70, arXiv:1907.02390, Theorem 4.
