# Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley lower bound

The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley constant $\beta_2$
for uniform points in the unit square satisfies $\beta_2 \ge 5/8 = 0.625$.

## Sources

- J. Beardwood, J. H. Halton and J. M. Hammersley, *The shortest path through many points*, Proc. Cambridge Philos.
  Soc. 55 (1959), 299–327; catalogue https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems, `constants/12a.md`.
