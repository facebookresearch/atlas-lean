# Gaudio–Jaillet corrected lower bound for the BHH constant

The Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley
constant $\beta_2$ satisfies $\frac{5}{8} + \frac{19}{10368} \le \beta_2$.

## Sources

- J. Gaudio and P. Jaillet, *An improved lower bound for the Traveling Salesman constant*, Oper. Res. Lett. 48
  (2020), 67–70, arXiv:1907.02390, Theorem 3, correcting the lower bound of S. Steinerberger, *New bounds for the traveling salesman constant*, Adv. in Appl. Probab. 47 (2015), 27–36.
