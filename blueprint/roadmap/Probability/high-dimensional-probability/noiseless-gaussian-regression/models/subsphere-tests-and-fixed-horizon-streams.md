# Affine subsphere tests and fixed-horizon Gaussian streams

An affine subsphere test is a real linear $n$-plane $V\subseteq\mathbb R^d$, a center $a\in V^\perp$, and $r>0$ with $\|a\|^2+r^2=1$. Its measure is the pushforward of normalized spherical volume of $V$ by $u\mapsto a+ru$. The radius-weighted functional takes the extended supremum over these tests of $r^{d/16}$ times the lower integral of the test function. For a fixed-horizon stream an initial PMF and a transition PMF on a finite measurable-singleton state type act on exact Gaussian observations. The suffix starts at a point mass at its specified state and uses the original transition indices at its time offset. Its output law is an ambient probability measure at the terminal state. The suffix estimate requires unit concentration of the output law at every state; the Euclidean-radius estimate does not. These fixed-horizon definitions are separate from the source's completed stopping PMFs, with joint and fixed-signal admissibility used for its stopping estimates.
