# Differential entropy of a real random variable

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ a real random variable, and let $f$ be the density of the law of $X$ with respect to Lebesgue measure (its Radon–Nikodym derivative, read as a real function). The differential entropy of $X$ is

$$H(X) = -\int_{\mathbb{R}} f(x) \log f(x)\, dx,$$

with $0 \log 0 = 0$, and $H(X) = 0$ when $f \log f$ is not Lebesgue integrable.
