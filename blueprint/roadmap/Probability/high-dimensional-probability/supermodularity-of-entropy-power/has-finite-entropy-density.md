# Real random variable with a finite-entropy density

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a measure space and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ a real random variable. Then $X$ has a density of finite entropy if

- the law of $X$ under $P$ has a density $f$ with respect to Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}$ (in particular $X$ is almost everywhere measurable with respect to $P$ and its law is absolutely continuous), and
- the function $x \mapsto f(x) \log f(x)$ is Lebesgue integrable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $0 \log 0 = 0$.
