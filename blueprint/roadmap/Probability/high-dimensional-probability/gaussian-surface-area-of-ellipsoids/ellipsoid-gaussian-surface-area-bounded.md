# Gaussian surface area of ellipsoids

The Gaussian surface area of $A \subseteq \mathbb R^n$ is $\Gamma(A) = \liminf_{\delta \to 0^+}\gamma_n(A_\delta \setminus A)/\delta \in [0, \infty]$, where $\gamma_n$ is the standard Gaussian measure and $A_\delta$ the closed $\delta$-neighbourhood of $A$. An ellipsoid is a set $\{x : \|T(x - c)\| \le 1\}$ with $T$ invertible linear and $c \in \mathbb R^n$. The question (O'Donnell) asks whether there is a constant $C$ with $\Gamma(A) \le C$ for every ellipsoid $A$ in every dimension $n$.
