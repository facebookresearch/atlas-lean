# Sup Deviation

For a measure $\mu$ on $Z$, a finite class of functions $f_1, \dots, f_M : Z \to \mathbb{R}$ and a sample $S_1, \dots, S_n$ in $Z$, the uniform deviation is

$$\sup_{j} \Bigl\lvert \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_j(S_i) - \int f_j\,d\mu \Bigr\rvert,$$

the largest absolute gap between the empirical mean on $S$ and the population mean under $\mu$. When $n = 0$ the empirical mean is $0$, by the convention $0^{-1} = 0$, and the supremum over an empty class ($M = 0$) is $0$.
