# Boundedness of Bernoulli processes

For a finite nonempty $T \subseteq \mathbb R^n$ let $b(T) = \mathbb E\max_{t \in T}\langle t, \varepsilon\rangle$ for uniform random signs $\varepsilon \in \{\pm1\}^n$ and $g(T) = \mathbb E\max_{t \in T}\langle t, G\rangle$ for a standard Gaussian vector $G$. There is a universal constant $L$ such that every finite nonempty $T \subseteq \mathbb R^n$ admits $T'$ with $g(T') \le L\,b(T)$ and every point of $T$ within $\ell^1$-distance $L\,b(T)$ of $T'$. This is the theorem of Bednorz and Latała (2014) proving Talagrand's Bernoulli conjecture.
