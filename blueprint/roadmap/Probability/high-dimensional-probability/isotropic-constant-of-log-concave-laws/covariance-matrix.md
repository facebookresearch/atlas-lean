# Covariance matrix

For a measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$, the covariance matrix of $\mu$ is
the $n \times n$ real matrix whose $(i, j)$ entry is
$\int (x_i - m_i)(x_j - m_j) \, d\mu(x)$, where $m_i = \int x_i \, d\mu(x)$ is the mean of the
$i$-th coordinate.
