# Centrally symmetric convex body

For a natural number $n$, a full-dimensional convex
body $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is centrally symmetric when $K = -K$, that is, for every
$x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $x \in K$ if and only if $-x \in K$.
