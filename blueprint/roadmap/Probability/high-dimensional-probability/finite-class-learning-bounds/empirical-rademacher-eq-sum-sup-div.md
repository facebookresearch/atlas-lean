# Empirical Rademacher complexity in unnormalized form

For a finite set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, let $\hat{\mathfrak R}(A) = 2^{-n}\sum_{\sigma \in \{\pm1\}^n}\max_{a \in A}\frac1n\sum_i\sigma_ia_i$ be its empirical Rademacher complexity (one-sided, $0$ for $A = \emptyset$). For nonempty $A$ and every $n$ (including $n = 0$), $\hat{\mathfrak R}(A) = \frac1n\cdot2^{-n}\sum_\sigma\max_{a \in A}\sum_i\sigma_ia_i$.
