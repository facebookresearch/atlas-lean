# Semicircle density

The semicircle density is the function $p : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$,
$p(x) = \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{2\pi}$, where the square root of a negative number is taken to
be $0$. So $p$ is $\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{4 - x^2}$ on $[-2, 2]$ and vanishes outside it.
