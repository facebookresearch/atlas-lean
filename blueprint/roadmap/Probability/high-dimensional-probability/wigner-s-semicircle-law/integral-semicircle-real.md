# Integrals against the semicircle distribution

For every function $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\int g \, d\sigma = \int_{-2}^{2} \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{2\pi} \, g(x) \, dx,$$

where $\sigma$ is the semicircle distribution. Both sides are Bochner integrals: the two
integrands are integrable together, and each side is $0$ when its integrand is not integrable.
