# Empirical spectral measure of a Hermitian matrix

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $W$ be a Hermitian $n \times n$
complex matrix with real eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n$, listed with multiplicity. The
empirical spectral measure of $W$ is the probability measure
$\frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \delta_{\lambda_j}$ on $\mathbb{R}$, where $\delta_\lambda$ is the
Dirac measure at $\lambda$. The size $n = 0$ is excluded: there the formula would give the zero
measure.
