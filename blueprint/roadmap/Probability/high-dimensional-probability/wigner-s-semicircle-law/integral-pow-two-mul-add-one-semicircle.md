# The odd moments of the semicircle distribution vanish

For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the semicircle distribution $\sigma$ satisfies
$\int x^{2k+1} \, d\sigma(x) = 0$; in particular $\sigma$ has mean $0$. (The density is even.)
