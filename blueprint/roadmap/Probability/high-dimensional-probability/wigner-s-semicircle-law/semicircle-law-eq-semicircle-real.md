# The two semicircle distributions agree

The [semicircle distribution](semicircle-law.md) in the statement of Wigner's semicircle law equals the
[semicircle distribution defined by its density](semicircle-real.md) $\sigma$: both are Lebesgue measure on
$[-2, 2]$ with density $\frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{2\pi}$. So it is a probability measure, and its moments are the
Catalan numbers.
