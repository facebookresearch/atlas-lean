# Unit directional variance of a restricted Gaussian

The question (O'Donnell) asks whether, if $K \subseteq \mathbb R^n$ is closed and convex with positive standard Gaussian measure, $\theta$ is a unit vector, and the standard Gaussian conditioned on $K$ has variance $1$ in direction $\theta$, then $K$ is a cylinder in direction $\theta$: $x + s\theta \in K$ for all $x \in K$ and $s \in \mathbb R$.
