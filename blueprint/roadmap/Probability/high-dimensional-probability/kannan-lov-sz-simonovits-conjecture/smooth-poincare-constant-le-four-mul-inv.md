# Cheeger's inequality

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^n$ and let
$\psi_\mu \in [0, \infty]$ be the inverse Cheeger constant of $\mu$. Then the smooth Poincaré
constant of $\mu$ is at most $4 \psi_\mu^2$.

## Sources

- Klartag, arXiv:2303.14938v1, Introduction, inequality (4), which attributes the inequality to Cheeger and states it for log-concave measures.
