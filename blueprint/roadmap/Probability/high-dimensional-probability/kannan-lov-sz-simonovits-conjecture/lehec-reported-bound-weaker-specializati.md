# Lehec's reported polylogarithmic bound on the KLS constant, weakened

There are a real
$C > 0$ and an integer $N \ge 3$ such that for every $n \ge N$ the dimensional KLS constant
$\psi_n \in [0, \infty]$ satisfies $\psi_n \le C (\log n)^{3.083}$.

The exponent $3.083$ lies just above Lehec's reported exponent $3.082\ldots$, which the source gives only to this
precision; $3.083$ is not claimed to be the exact exponent.

## Sources

- B. Klartag, *Logarithmic bounds for isoperimetry and slices of convex sets*, Ars Inveniendi Analytica (2023),
  Paper No. 4, arXiv:2303.14938v1, Introduction, reporting a personal communication from J. Lehec.
