# Lower Poincaré–Cheeger comparison with coefficient 1/π

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $\mu$ be an
isotropic log-concave probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^n$, with inverse Cheeger constant
$\psi(\mu) \in [0, \infty]$ and smooth Poincaré constant $C_P(\mu) \in [0, \infty]$. Then
$\frac{1}{\pi} \psi(\mu)^2 \le C_P(\mu)$.

## Sources

- Catalogue https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems, `constants/20c.md`, the comparison of $\psi_\mu$ and $C_P(\mu)$.
- B. Klartag, *Logarithmic bounds for isoperimetry and slices of convex sets*, Ars Inveniendi Analytica (2023),
  Paper No. 4, arXiv:2303.14938v1, which states the weaker coefficient $1/9$.
