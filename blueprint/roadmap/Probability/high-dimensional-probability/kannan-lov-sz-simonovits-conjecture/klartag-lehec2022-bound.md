# Klartag–Lehec polylogarithmic bound on the KLS constant

There are a real $C > 0$ and an
integer $N \ge 3$ such that for every $n \ge N$ the dimensional KLS constant
$\psi_n \in [0, \infty]$ satisfies $\psi_n \le C (\log n)^5$.
