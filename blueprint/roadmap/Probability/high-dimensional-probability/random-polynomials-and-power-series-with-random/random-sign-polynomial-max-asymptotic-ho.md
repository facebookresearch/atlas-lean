# Halász's asymptotic for random sign polynomials

Let $\varepsilon_0, \varepsilon_1, \dots$ be independent uniform random signs. There is $C > 0$ such that almost surely $\max_{|z| = 1}|\sum_{k=0}^n\varepsilon_kz^k|/\sqrt{n\log n} \to C$. Halász (1973) proved this with $C = 1$; the order of magnitude is due to Salem and Zygmund.

## Sources

- Problem: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problems, Problem #523, https://www.erdosproblems.com/523 (status PROVED).
- Resolution: G. Halász, On a result of Salem and Zygmund concerning random polynomials, Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. (1973), 369-377.
