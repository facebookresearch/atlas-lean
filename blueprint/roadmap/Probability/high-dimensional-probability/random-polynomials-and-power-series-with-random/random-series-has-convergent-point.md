# Convergent points of random power series

Let $\varepsilon_0, \varepsilon_1, \dots$ be independent uniform random signs. The claim (Erdős Problem 527) is that if $a_n \in \mathbb R$ with $\sum_na_n^2 = \infty$ and $a_n = o(n^{-1/2})$, then almost surely there is $z$ with $|z| = 1$ at which $\sum_n\varepsilon_na_nz^n$ converges.
