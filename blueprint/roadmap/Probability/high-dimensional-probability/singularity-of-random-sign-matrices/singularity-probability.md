# Singularity probability of a random sign matrix

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The singularity probability of an $n \times n$ random sign matrix $B_n$, whose entries are independent and uniform on $\{-1, 1\}$, is the proportion of the $2^{n^2}$ sign patterns whose matrix has determinant $0$:

$$p_n = \frac{\#\{B \in \{-1, 1\}^{n \times n} : \det B = 0\}}{2^{n^2}}.$$

The determinant is taken over $\mathbb{Z}$, which is the same as over $\mathbb{R}$.
