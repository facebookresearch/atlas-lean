# Singularity of random Bernoulli matrices

Let $B_n$ be the $n \times n$ matrix with independent entries uniform on $\{-1, 1\}$. $P(B_n\text{ singular})^{1/n} \to 1/2$ (Tikhomirov, 2020).

## Sources

- Problem: A. Litvak (with K. Tikhomirov), Singularity of sparse Bernoulli matrices, in New Perspectives and Computational Challenges in High Dimensions, Oberwolfach Reports 17 (2020), no. 6, p. 390, (1), DOI 10.4171/OWR/2020/6.
- Resolution: K. Tikhomirov, Singularity of random Bernoulli matrices, Ann. of Math. (2) 191 (2020), 593-634, arXiv:1812.09016, https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.09016.
