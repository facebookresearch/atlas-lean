# The R-transform

The R-transform of a moment sequence $m$ is the formal power series

$$R(z) = \sum_{n \ge 0} \kappa_{n+1} z^n$$

of its [free cumulants](free-cumulant.md). For an element $a$ of a noncommutative probability space
it depends only on the law of $a$, and by
[additivity of free cumulants](free-cumulant-add-of-are-free.md) it satisfies
$R_{a+b} = R_a + R_b$ when $a$ and $b$ are free: it linearises free additive convolution.
