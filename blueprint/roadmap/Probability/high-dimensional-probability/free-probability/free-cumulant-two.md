# The second free cumulant is the variance

For every moment sequence, $\kappa_2 = m_2 - m_1^2$. The noncrossing partitions of two points are
the one-block partition and the partition into two singletons, so the moment-cumulant formula reads
$m_2 = \kappa_2 + \kappa_1^2$ with $\kappa_1 = m_1$. Free and classical cumulants first differ at
order four, where the crossing partition $\{\{1,3\},\{2,4\}\}$ is excluded from the free sum.

## Informally uses

- [The first free cumulant is the mean](free-cumulant-one.md): $\kappa_1 = m_1$.
