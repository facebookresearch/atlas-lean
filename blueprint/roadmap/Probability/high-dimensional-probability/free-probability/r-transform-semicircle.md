# The R-transform of the semicircle law is $z$

The R-transform of the standard semicircle law is $R_\sigma(z) = z$, as a formal power series. It
follows at once from [its free cumulants](free-cumulant-semicircle.md): only $\kappa_2 = 1$ is
nonzero. More generally a semicircle law of variance $s^2$ has $R(z) = s^2 z$, so free sums of
semicircular variables are semicircular.
