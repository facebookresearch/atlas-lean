# Moments of the semicircle law

The sequence $n \mapsto \int x^n \, d\sigma(x)$ of moments of the semicircle law $\sigma$, viewed as
complex numbers so that it can be fed to the [free cumulants](free-cumulant.md). Its terms are
$C_{n/2}$ for even $n$ and $0$ for odd $n$.
