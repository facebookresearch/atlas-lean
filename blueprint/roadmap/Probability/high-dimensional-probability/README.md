# High-dimensional and geometric probability

Probability in high dimension and on geometric objects: log-concave and uniform convex-body laws, isotropic constants, the KLS and thin-shell conjectures and their bounds, random matrices (the semicircle law and its Catalan moments, operator norms and the Bai–Yin theorem, singularity of Bernoulli matrices, Tracy–Widom limits, singular values of Gaussian matrices), random Euclidean optimization (Beardwood–Halton–Hammersley), suprema of Gaussian processes (generic chaining, the $\gamma_2$ functional, the majorizing measure theorem and Sudakov minoration), suprema of selector and positive empirical processes (Park–Pham theorems), and uniform laws of large numbers via Rademacher complexity.
