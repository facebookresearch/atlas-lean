# Weak variance $\sigma_*$ of a Gaussian series

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $A_1, \dots, A_n$ be real $d \times d$ matrices. Define

$$\sigma_* = \sup_{v \in \mathbb{R}^d,\ \|v\|_2 = 1} \Big( \sum_{k=1}^n (v^{\mathsf T} A_k v)^2 \Big)^{1/2},$$

which is $0$ when $d = 0$. For symmetric matrices $A_k$ this is the weak variance of the Gaussian series $\sum_k g_k A_k$ (Bandeira, Definition 4.15); for nonsymmetric matrices the formula is still defined, but only the symmetric part of each $A_k$ enters it.
