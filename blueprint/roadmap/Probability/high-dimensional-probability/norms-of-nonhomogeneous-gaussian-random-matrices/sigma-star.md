# Weak variance $\sigma_*$ of a Gaussian series

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $A_1, \dots, A_n$ be real $d \times d$ matrices. The weak variance is

$$\sigma_* = \sup_{v \in \mathbb{R}^d,\ \|v\|_2 = 1} \Big( \sum_{k=1}^n (v^{\mathsf T} A_k v)^2 \Big)^{1/2}$$

(Bandeira, Definition 4.15), and is $0$ when $d = 0$.
