# The improved noncommutative Khintchine inequality fails

For real symmetric $d \times d$ matrices $A_1, \dots, A_n$ and independent standard Gaussians $g_k$, let $\sigma = \|\sum_kA_k^2\|^{1/2}$ and $\sigma_* = \max_{\|v\| = 1}(\sum_k(v^\top A_kv)^2)^{1/2}$, with $\|\cdot\|$ the spectral norm. The conjecture is false: for every $C$ there are $d$, $n$ and such matrices with $\mathbb E\|\sum_kg_kA_k\| > C(\sigma + (\log d)^{1/2}\sigma_*)$ (Bandeira, Boedihardjo and van Handel).
