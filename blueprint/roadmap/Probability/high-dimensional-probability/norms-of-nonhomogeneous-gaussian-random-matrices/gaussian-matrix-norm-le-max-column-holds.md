# The largest column controls the norm of a Gaussian matrix

Let $X$ be a $d \times d$ symmetric random matrix with independent centered Gaussian entries $X_{ij} = b_{ij}g_{ij}$ ($i \le j$) of arbitrary variances $b_{ij}^2$, and $\|X\|$ its spectral norm. There is a universal $C$ with $\mathbb E\|X\| \le C\,\mathbb E\max_k\|Xe_k\|_2$, by the theorem of Latała, van Handel and Youssef (2018).
