# Symmetric Gaussian matrix with a variance profile

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $b \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ and let $g = (g_{ij})_{i, j \le d}$ be real numbers. The associated Gaussian matrix $X \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ has entries

$$X_{ij} = b_{ij}\, g_{\min(i,j), \max(i,j)}.$$

When $b$ is symmetric and the $g_{ij}$ are independent standard Gaussians, $X$ is a symmetric random matrix whose entries on and above the diagonal are independent centered Gaussians, $X_{ij}$ of variance $b_{ij}^2$.
