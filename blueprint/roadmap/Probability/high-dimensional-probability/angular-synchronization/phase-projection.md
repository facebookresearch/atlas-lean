# Entrywise phase projection

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a \in \mathbb{C}^n$, the entrywise phase projection $P(a) \in \mathbb{C}^n$ is defined by

$$P(a)_k = \begin{cases} \dfrac{a_k}{\lvert a_k \rvert}, & a_k \neq 0, \\ 1, & a_k = 0, \end{cases}$$

for every coordinate $k$. Thus every entry of $P(a)$ has modulus $1$.
