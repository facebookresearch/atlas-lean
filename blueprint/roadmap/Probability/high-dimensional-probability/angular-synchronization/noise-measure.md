# Noise distribution for angular synchronization

In dimension $n$, the noise distribution of angular synchronization is the product probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^{\{0,\dots,n-1\}^2 \times \{0,1\}}$ under which the coordinates $\omega(k, l, i)$, for $k, l \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and $i \in \{0, 1\}$, are independent real Gaussians with mean $0$ and variance $1/2$.
