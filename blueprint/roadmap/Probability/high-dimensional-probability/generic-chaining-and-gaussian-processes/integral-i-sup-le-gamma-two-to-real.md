# Generic chaining bound, real form

Under the hypotheses of the generic chaining bound,
$\mathbb{E}\sup_{t \in T} X_t \le 10\,\gamma_2(T, d)$ as real numbers (Talagrand, *Upper and
Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., 2021, Theorem 2.7.11). This form uses that
$\gamma_2(T, d)$ is finite because $T$ is.
