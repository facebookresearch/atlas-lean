# The chaining bound for a centred process

If, in the chaining argument along a finite chain, every $X_t$ is moreover integrable and
centred, then $\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t = \mathbb{E}\sup_t (X_t - X_{t_0})$ (Talagrand's (2.5)) and

$$\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t \le 7\sup_t\sum_{n=1}^M 2^{n/2} d(\pi_n(t), \pi_{n-1}(t))$$

(Talagrand, *Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., 2021, Section 2.4). This
is the common step behind the generic chaining bound and Dudley's bound, which differ only in the
choice of the approximation maps.
