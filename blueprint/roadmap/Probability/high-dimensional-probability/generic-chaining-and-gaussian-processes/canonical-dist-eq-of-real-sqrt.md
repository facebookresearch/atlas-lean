# The canonical distance of square-integrable increments

For a real process $(X_t)_{t \in T}$ on a measure space $(\Omega, P)$ and indices $s, t$ with $X_s - X_t$ square integrable, the canonical distance of the process at $(s, t)$ is $\bigl(\int (X_s - X_t)^2 \, dP\bigr)^{1/2}$, read as an extended nonnegative real.
