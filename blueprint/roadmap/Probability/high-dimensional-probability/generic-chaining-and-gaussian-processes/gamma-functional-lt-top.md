# $\gamma_\alpha$ is finite on a finite space

Let $\alpha$ be a real number. If $T$ is finite and $d$ is a finite distance function vanishing on the diagonal,
then
$\gamma_\alpha(T, d) < \infty$: take the trivial partition while $N_n < \operatorname{card} T$
and the partition into points afterwards, so only finitely many terms of the series are nonzero
(compare Talagrand, *Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., 2021, Exercise
2.7.5, which gives the quantitative bound $\gamma_\alpha \le K(\alpha)\Delta(T)(\log
\operatorname{card} T)^{1/\alpha}$). With the diameter bound it shows that the
generic chaining bound is not vacuous.
