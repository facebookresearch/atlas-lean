# Dudley's sum is finite on a finite space

For a finite metric space, $e_n(T) = 0$ as soon as $N_n \ge \operatorname{card} T$ (take
$T_n = T$), and every $e_n(T)$ is finite, so $\sum_{n \ge 0} 2^{n/2} e_n(T) < \infty$ and Dudley's
bound is not vacuous (Talagrand, *Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed.,
2021, (2.36)).
