# The diameter for the extended distance is the usual diameter

For a set $T$ with an extended distance $\operatorname{edist} : T \times T \to [0, \infty]$ and every $A \subseteq T$,
the [diameter](diam-with.md) of $A$ for this distance,
$\Delta(A) = \sup_{s, t \in A} \operatorname{edist}(s, t)$, is the usual (extended) diameter of $A$; the two agree by
definition.
