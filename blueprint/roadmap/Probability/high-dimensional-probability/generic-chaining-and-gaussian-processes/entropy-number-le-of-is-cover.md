# Entropy numbers from finite covers

Let $T$ be an extended pseudometric space, $n \ge 0$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$. If a finite set $F \subseteq T$ with at
most $N_n$ points is an $\varepsilon$-cover of $T$ (every point of $T$ lies within distance $\varepsilon$ of a point
of $F$), then the [entropy number](entropy-number.md) satisfies $e_n(T) \le \varepsilon$ (Talagrand, *Upper and
Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes*, 2nd ed., 2021, the reformulation of (2.36) by coverings with balls). The
least cardinality of such a cover is the covering number of $T$ at scale $\varepsilon$, so this compares covering
numbers with Talagrand's entropy numbers.
