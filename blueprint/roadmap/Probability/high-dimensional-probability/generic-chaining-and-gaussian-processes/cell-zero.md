# The first partition is trivial

In an admissible sequence of partitions, $\operatorname{card} \mathcal{A}_0 \le N_0 = 1$ forces
$\mathcal{A}_0 = \{T\}$: every cell $A_0(t)$ is the whole space. In particular the term $n = 0$ of
$\sum_n 2^{n/2}\Delta(A_n(t))$ is the diameter $\Delta(T)$, which is why
$\Delta(T) \le \gamma_2(T, d)$.
