# Potter's bound

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be nonincreasing, nonnegative and bounded, with $W(x) > 0$ for
all sufficiently large $x$, and suppose that for some $\rho < 0$, $W(cx)/W(x) \to c^{\rho}$ as
$x \to \infty$ for every $c > 0$. Then for every $\delta > 0$ there are constants $C > 0$ and
$Y > 0$ such that

$$\frac{W(xu)}{W(x)} \le C\, u^{\rho - \delta} \qquad \text{for all } x \ge Y \text{ and } u \in (0, 1].$$

The proof chains the ratio limits at the dyadic scales $1/2$ and $2$.

## Sources

- N. H. Bingham, C. M. Goldie and J. L. Teugels, *Regular Variation*, Cambridge University
  Press, 1987, Theorem 1.5.6.
