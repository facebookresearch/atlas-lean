# Stable distribution of a given index

A measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is stable of index $\alpha$ if $\mu$ is a probability measure,
$0 < \alpha \le 2$, and for every $n \ge 1$ there is $b \in \mathbb{R}$ such that the $n$-fold
convolution power $\mu^{*n}$ is the image of $\mu$ under $x \mapsto n^{1/\alpha} x + b$: the sum
of $n$ i.i.d. variables with law $\mu$ has the law of $n^{1/\alpha} X + b$ with $X \sim \mu$. The
dilation is fixed to $n^{1/\alpha}$ and the centering $b$ is arbitrary. Degenerate (Dirac) laws
are stable of several indices.
