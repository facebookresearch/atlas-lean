# Nondegenerate Gaussian is not stable of index 1

For $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$, the Gaussian law $N(\mu, v)$ is not [stable of index
$1$](is-stable.md). Indeed, for $n = 4$ the convolution power has variance $4v$, while every
image of $N(\mu, v)$ under $x \mapsto 4x + b$ has variance $16v$.
