# Domain of attraction of a given index

A measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ is in the domain of attraction of index $\alpha$ if there is a
[nondegenerate](is-nondegenerate.md) measure $G$, [stable of index $\alpha$](is-stable.md), such
that $\mu$ is in the [domain of attraction](in-domain-of-attraction.md) of $G$. The domain of
attraction of a measure $G$ alone does not determine an index: degenerate limits are stable of
several indices.
