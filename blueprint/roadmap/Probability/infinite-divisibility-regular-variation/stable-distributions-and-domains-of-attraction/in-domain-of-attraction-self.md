# Stable law lies in its own domain of attraction

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$ that is [stable of index $\alpha$](is-stable.md)
for some $\alpha$. Then $\mu$ is in its own [domain of attraction](in-domain-of-attraction.md): it
is a normalized weak limit of its own convolution powers. For $n \ge 1$ the stability identity
inverts exactly; for $n = 0$ the normalizer $1$ is used.
