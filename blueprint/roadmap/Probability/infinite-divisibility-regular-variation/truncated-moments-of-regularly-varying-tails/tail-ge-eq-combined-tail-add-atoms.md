# Non-strict absolute tail as combined tail plus endpoint atoms

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$ and $s > 0$. Then

$$\mu\{y : |y| \ge s\} = \mu((s, \infty)) + \mu((-\infty, -s)) + \mu(\{s\}) + \mu(\{-s\}) :$$

the non-strict absolute tail is the combined strict tail plus both endpoint atoms.
