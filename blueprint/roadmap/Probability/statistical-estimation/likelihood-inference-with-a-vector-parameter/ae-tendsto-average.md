# Strong law of large numbers for Banach-valued transforms

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent observations with law $\mu$ on a probability space and $g$ a strongly measurable $\mu$-integrable map into a separable real Banach space. Then $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}g(X_i) \to \int g\,d\mu$ almost surely.
