# Directional Cramér–Rao inequality

Let $p_{\theta'}$ be positive probability densities with respect to $\nu$ for $\theta'$ near $\theta$ in a finite-dimensional real inner product space, differentiable in the parameter at $\theta$, with a measurable score $s_\theta$ of finite second moment under $P_\theta$, and let $T$ be an estimator with finite second moment, unbiased for $g(\theta')$ for $\theta'$ near $\theta$, with $g$ differentiable at $\theta$. If, along the line through $\theta$ in direction $u$, $\int p\,d\nu$ and $\int Tp\,d\nu$ can be differentiated under the integral sign at $\theta$, then

$$\big(Dg(\theta)u\big)^2 \le \langle I(\theta)u, u\rangle\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T).$$

## Sources

- Rao (1945), Cramér (1946), ch. 32 (several parameters); Lehmann & Casella (1998), Theorem 2.6.6.
