# Directional Cramér–Rao inequality on an inner product parameter space

Let $p_{\theta'}$, for $\theta'$ in a finite-dimensional real inner product space, be functions with $p_{\theta'}(x) > 0$ for every $\theta'$ and $x$, $\int p_{\theta'}\,d\nu = 1$ for $\theta'$ near $\theta$, $p_\theta$ measurable, and $\theta' \mapsto p_{\theta'}(x)$ differentiable at $\theta$ for every $x$, with a measurable score $s_\theta$ of finite second moment under $P_\theta$, and let $T$ be an estimator with finite second moment, unbiased for $g(\theta')$ for $\theta'$ near $\theta$, with $g$ differentiable at $\theta$. If, along the line through $\theta$ in direction $u$, $\int p\,d\nu$ and $\int Tp\,d\nu$ can be differentiated under the integral sign at $\theta$, then

$$\big(Dg(\theta)u\big)^2 \le \langle I(\theta)u, u\rangle\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T).$$

## Sources
