# Gaussian quadratic forms are chi-square

Let $S$ be a symmetric positive definite operator on a real inner product space $E$ of finite dimension $d \ge 1$, and $V$ an almost everywhere measurable random vector with $\langle V, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle St, t\rangle)$ for every $t$. Then $\langle S^{-1}V, V\rangle$ has the chi-square law with $d$ degrees of freedom.
