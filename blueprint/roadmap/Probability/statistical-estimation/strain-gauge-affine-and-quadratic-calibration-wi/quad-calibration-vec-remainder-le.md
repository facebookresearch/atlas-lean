# Pointwise remainder bound for a quadratic calibration

Let $a, b, c_0, c_1, c_2 \in \mathbb{R}$ and $x, \delta \in \mathbb{R}^1$ with $a \le x_0 \le b$ and $a \le x_0 + \delta_0 \le b$. The first-order remainder of the quadratic calibration $f : \mathbb{R}^1 \to \mathbb{R}^1$ satisfies

$$\big|\big(f(x + \delta) - f(x) - Df(x)\,\delta\big)_0\big| \le \frac{2|c_2|}{2}\, \|\delta\|^2,$$

with the sup norm. The bound is pointwise and says nothing about moments under a law.
