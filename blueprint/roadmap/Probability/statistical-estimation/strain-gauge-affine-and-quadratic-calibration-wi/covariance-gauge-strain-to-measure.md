# Measure-theoretic covariance of gauge strains

Let $\Omega$ be a finite type with a σ-algebra in which singletons are measurable, and let $q$ be a probability mass function on $\Omega$, viewed as a probability measure. For gains $g \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with every $g_i \ne 0$, offsets $b$, voltages $V : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$ and indices $i, j$, the covariance under $q$ of the calibrated strains $\varepsilon_i = (V_i - b_i)/g_i$ and $\varepsilon_j$ is

$$\operatorname{Cov}_q(\varepsilon_i, \varepsilon_j) = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}_{\tilde q}(V_i, V_j)}{g_i\, g_j},$$

where $\operatorname{Cov}_{\tilde q}$ is the finite covariance $\sum_\omega \tilde q(\omega)(X - \mathbb{E} X)(Y - \mathbb{E} Y)$ with the real weights $\tilde q(\omega) = q(\omega)$.
