# Dimensioned quadratic calibration

Let $\mathsf{d}$ be an ISQ dimension and $\mathsf{V}$ the electric-potential dimension. For quantities $c_0$ of dimension $\mathsf{d}$, $c_1$ of dimension $\mathsf{d}\,\mathsf{V}^{-1}$, $c_2$ of dimension $\mathsf{d}\,\mathsf{V}^{-2}$ and a voltage $v$ of dimension $\mathsf{V}$, the dimensioned quadratic calibration is the quantity of dimension $\mathsf{d}$

$$c_0 + c_1 v + c_2 (v v),$$

where $c_1 v$ and $c_2 (vv)$ are recast to dimension $\mathsf{d}$ by the dimension identities $\mathsf{d}\,\mathsf{V}^{-1}\mathsf{V} = \mathsf{d}$ and $\mathsf{d}\,\mathsf{V}^{-2}\mathsf{V}^2 = \mathsf{d}$.
