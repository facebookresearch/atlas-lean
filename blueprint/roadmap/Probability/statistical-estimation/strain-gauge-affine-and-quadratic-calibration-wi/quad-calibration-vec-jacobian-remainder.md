# Jacobian remainder of a quadratic calibration

For all real $c_0, c_1, c_2$ and $x, \delta \in \mathbb{R}^1$, the first-order remainder $f(x + \delta) - f(x) - Df(x)\,\delta$ of the quadratic calibration $f : \mathbb{R}^1 \to \mathbb{R}^1$ has sole coordinate

$$c_2\, \big((x_0 + \delta_0) - x_0\big)^2 = c_2\, \delta_0^2,$$

the Taylor remainder at the base $x_0$.
