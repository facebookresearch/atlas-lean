# Quadratic calibration under a change of units

Fix two systems of unit scales, an ISQ dimension $\mathsf{d}$, and let $s_{\mathsf{X}} > 0$ denote the conversion factor of a dimension $\mathsf{X}$; let $\mathsf{V}$ be the electric-potential dimension. For all real $c_0, c_1, c_2, v$, with $f(c_0, c_1, c_2; v) = c_0 + c_1 v + c_2 v^2$,

$$f\big(s_{\mathsf{d}}\, c_0,\ s_{\mathsf{d}\mathsf{V}^{-1}}\, c_1,\ s_{\mathsf{d}\mathsf{V}^{-2}}\, c_2;\ s_{\mathsf{V}}\, v\big) = s_{\mathsf{d}}\, f(c_0, c_1, c_2; v).$$
