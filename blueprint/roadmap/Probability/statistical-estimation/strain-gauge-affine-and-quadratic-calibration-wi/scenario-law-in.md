# Finite scenario law in a voltage interval

For a finite scenario type $\Omega$, reals $a, b$, weights $p : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and voltages $V : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $(p, V)$ is a scenario law in $[a, b]$ when

- $p(\omega) \ge 0$ for every $\omega$,
- $\sum_\omega p(\omega) = 1$, and
- $V(\omega) \in [a, b]$ for every $\omega$.

Scenarios of probability zero are allowed.
