# Measure-theoretic variance of a quadratic calibration

Let $\Omega$ be a finite type with a σ-algebra in which singletons are measurable, and let $q$ be a probability mass function on $\Omega$, viewed as a probability measure. For $V : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and real $c_0, c_1, c_2, v_0$, with $f(v) = c_0 + c_1 v + c_2 v^2$, $\ell(v) = f(v_0) + (c_1 + 2c_2 v_0)(v - v_0)$ and $R(v) = c_2 (v - v_0)^2$,

$$\operatorname{Var}_q(f(V)) = \operatorname{Var}_q(\ell(V)) + 2 \operatorname{Cov}_q(\ell(V), R(V)) + \operatorname{Var}_q(R(V)).$$
