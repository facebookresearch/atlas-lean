# Dimensioned strain from an affine gauge calibration

For quantities $g, b, V$ of electric-potential dimension $\mathsf{M}\,\mathsf{L}^2\,\mathsf{T}^{-3}\,\mathsf{I}^{-1}$ with $g \ne 0$, the dimensioned strain is the quotient $(V - b)/g$, a quantity of dimension $\mathsf{V}\cdot\mathsf{V}^{-1}$, recast as a dimensionless quantity through the identity $\mathsf{V}\cdot\mathsf{V}^{-1} = 1$.
