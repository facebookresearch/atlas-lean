# Collapsing does not characterize nonexistence of the MLE

For an $I \times J \times K$ contingency table $n$ of counts, the maximum likelihood estimate under the no three-way interaction model exists if some table with strictly positive real entries has the same two-way margins as $n$. A collapsing to a $2 \times 2 \times 2$ table merges the three levels of each variable into two nonempty classes and adds the corresponding cells. The conjecture is false: for some $3 \times 3 \times 3$ table, the estimate failing to exist is not equivalent to its failing to exist for some collapsing of the table. The refutation is due to Eriksson (see also Eriksson, Fienberg, Rinaldo and Sullivant).
