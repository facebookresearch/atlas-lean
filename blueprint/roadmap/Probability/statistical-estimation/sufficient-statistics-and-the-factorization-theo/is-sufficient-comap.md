# Discrete sufficiency is σ-algebra sufficiency

Let $X$ be a finite set and $\mathcal{T}$ a set, both with the discrete σ-algebra, let $(p_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be probability mass functions on $X$, and let $T : X \to \mathcal{T}$ be a statistic that is [sufficient for the discrete family](is-sufficient-pmf.md) $(p_\theta)$. Then the σ-algebra $\sigma(T)$ generated by $T$ is [sufficient](is-sufficient.md) for the probability measures $(P_\theta)$ of the mass functions $p_\theta$.
