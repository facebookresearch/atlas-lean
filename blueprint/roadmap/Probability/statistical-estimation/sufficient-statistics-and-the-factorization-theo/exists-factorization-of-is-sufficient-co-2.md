# Halmos–Savage factorization for countable families

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $\mathcal{T}$ be measurable spaces, $T : \Omega \to \mathcal{T}$ a measurable statistic, $\mu$ a σ-finite measure on $\Omega$ and $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ probability measures on $\Omega$ indexed by a countable set $\Theta$, with $P_\theta \ll \mu$ for every $\theta$. If the σ-algebra $\sigma(T)$ is [sufficient](is-sufficient.md) for $(P_\theta)$, then there are measurable $g_\theta : \mathcal{T} \to [0, \infty]$ and $h : \Omega \to [0, \infty]$, $h$ free of $\theta$, with

$$\frac{dP_\theta}{d\mu} = g_\theta(T)\, h \quad \text{for every } \theta \in \Theta,$$

that is, $P_\theta$ is the measure with density $g_\theta(T)\,h$ with respect to $\mu$.

## Sources

- P. R. Halmos and L. J. Savage, "Application of the Radon–Nikodym theorem to the theory of sufficient statistics", *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 20 (1949), 225–241.
