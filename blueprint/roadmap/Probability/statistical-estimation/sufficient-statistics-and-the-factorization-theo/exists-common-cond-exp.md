# Common conditional expectation under a sufficient σ-algebra

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space, $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ probability measures on it and $\mathcal{G}$ a σ-algebra [sufficient](is-sufficient.md) for $(P_\theta)$. Every $\mathcal{F}$-measurable real function $W$ that is integrable under every $P_\theta$ has one $\mathcal{G}$-measurable function $\eta$ with $\eta = \mathbb{E}_{P_\theta}[W \mid \mathcal{G}]$ $P_\theta$-almost everywhere for every $\theta$. (Measurability of $W$ itself, not only almost everywhere under each $P_\theta$, is what lets one approximating sequence serve every parameter.)

## Sources

- P. R. Halmos and L. J. Savage, *Application of the Radon–Nikodym theorem to the theory of sufficient statistics*, Ann. Math. Statist. 20 (1949), 225–241.
