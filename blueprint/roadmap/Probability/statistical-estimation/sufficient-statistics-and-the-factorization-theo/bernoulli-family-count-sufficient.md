# The count is sufficient for a Bernoulli sample

For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ (including $n = 0$, where the sample space is a single point), let $P_p$, $p \in [0,1]$, be the law on $\{0,1\}^n$ with counting-measure density $p^{C(\omega)} (1-p)^{n - C(\omega)}$, where $C(\omega)$ is the number of successes in $\omega$ and $0^0 = 1$. Then the σ-algebra generated by $C$ is [sufficient](is-sufficient.md) for $(P_p)_{p \in [0,1]}$: the density factors as $g_p(C(\omega)) \cdot 1$ with $g_p(k) = p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$.
