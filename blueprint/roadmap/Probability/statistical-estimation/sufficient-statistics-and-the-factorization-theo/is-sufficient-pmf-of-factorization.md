# Discrete factorization, sufficiency

Let $X$, $\Theta$ and $\mathcal{T}$ be sets, $(p_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ a family of probability mass functions on $X$ and $T : X \to \mathcal{T}$ a statistic, with $P_\theta(T = t) = \sum_{x : T(x) = t} p_\theta(x)$. If $p_\theta(x) = g_\theta(T(x))\, h(x)$ for all $\theta, x$, for functions $g_\theta : \mathcal{T} \to [0, \infty)$ and $h : X \to [0, \infty)$ with $h$ free of $\theta$, then $T$ is [sufficient](is-sufficient-pmf.md) for $(p_\theta)$.
