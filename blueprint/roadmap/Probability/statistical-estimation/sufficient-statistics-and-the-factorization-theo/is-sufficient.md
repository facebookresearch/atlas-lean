# Sufficient sub-σ-algebra

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space and $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ a family of measures on it. A σ-algebra $\mathcal{G}$ on $\Omega$ is sufficient for $(P_\theta)$ if $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$, every $P_\theta$ is finite, and for every $A \in \mathcal{F}$ there is one $\mathcal{G}$-measurable function $f$ that is a version of the conditional probability $\mathbb{E}_{P_\theta}[\mathbf{1}_A \mid \mathcal{G}]$ under every $P_\theta$.
