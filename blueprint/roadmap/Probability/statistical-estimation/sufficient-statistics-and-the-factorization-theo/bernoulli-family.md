# Bernoulli sample as a density family

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the $n$-trial Bernoulli family is the family of densities on $\{0,1\}^n$ (written as $n$-tuples of booleans), with respect to counting measure and indexed by $p \in [0,1]$,

$$f_p(\omega) = p^{\#\{i : \omega_i = 1\}} (1-p)^{\#\{i : \omega_i = 0\}},$$

with $0^0 = 1$. Each $f_p$ sums to $1$, so the endpoint laws $p = 0$ and $p = 1$ belong to the family.
