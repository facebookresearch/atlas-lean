# Factorization implies sufficiency

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space, $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ a sub-σ-algebra, $\nu$ a measure on $\mathcal{F}$ whose restriction to $\mathcal{G}$ is σ-finite, and $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ finite measures with $P_\theta = g_\theta \cdot \nu$ for $\mathcal{G}$-measurable $g_\theta : \Omega \to [0, \infty]$. Then $\mathcal{G}$ is [sufficient](is-sufficient.md) for $(P_\theta)$. This is the sufficiency half of the Halmos–Savage theorem.

## Sources

- P. R. Halmos and L. J. Savage, *Application of the Radon–Nikodym theorem to the theory of sufficient statistics*, Ann. Math. Statist. 20 (1949), 225–241.
