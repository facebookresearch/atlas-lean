# Affine recodings preserve ratios of differences

For $a \ne 0$, $b \in \mathbb{R}$ and real $x, y, z, w$ with $z \ne w$,

$$\frac{(ax + b) - (ay + b)}{(az + b) - (aw + b)} = \frac{x - y}{z - w}.$$

In measurement theory, the admissible recodings of an interval scale are by convention the increasing ones, $a > 0$; the identity holds for every $a \ne 0$.
