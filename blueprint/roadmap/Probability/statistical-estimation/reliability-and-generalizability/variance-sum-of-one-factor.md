# Variance of a composite under a one-factor model

Let $X_i = \nu_i + \lambda_iF + E_i$, $i = 1, \dots, k$, on a probability space (a one-factor congeneric model) with $F, E_i$ square integrable and $\operatorname{Cov}(F, E_i) = 0$. Then $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_iX_i) = (\sum_i\lambda_i)^2\operatorname{Var}(F) + \sum_{i,j}\operatorname{Cov}(E_i, E_j)$.
