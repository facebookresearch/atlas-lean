# Dependability coefficient

For a person variance $\sigma_P^2$, an item variance $\sigma_I^2$, a residual variance $\sigma_e^2$ and $m \ge 1$ items in a crossed person-by-item design, the dependability coefficient is $\Phi = \sigma_P^2/(\sigma_P^2 + (\sigma_I^2 + \sigma_e^2)/m)$, with the value $0$ at a zero denominator.
