# Coefficient omega lies in the unit interval

Let $X_i = \nu_i + \lambda_iF + E_i$, $i = 1, \dots, k$, on a probability space (a one-factor congeneric model) with $F, E_i$ square integrable and $\operatorname{Cov}(F, E_i) = 0$. If $\operatorname{Var}(F) = \varphi$, $\operatorname{Cov}(E_i, E_j) = \Theta_{ij}$ and $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_iX_i) > 0$, then $0 \le \omega \le 1$.
