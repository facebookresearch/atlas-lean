# Coefficient alpha with equal error variances

Let $X_i = T + c_i + E_i$, $i = 1, \dots, k$, on a probability space (tau-equivalent items) with $T, E_i$ square integrable, $\operatorname{Cov}(T, E_i) = 0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(E_i, E_j) = 0$ for $i \ne j$. If $k \ge 2$, $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_iX_i) > 0$ and every $\operatorname{Var}(E_i) = \sigma^2$, then $\alpha = k^2\operatorname{Var}(T)/(k^2\operatorname{Var}(T) + k\sigma^2)$.
