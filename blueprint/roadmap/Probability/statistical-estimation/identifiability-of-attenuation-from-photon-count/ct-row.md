# Rank-deficient single-ray design matrix for two cells

The single-ray design over two attenuation cells is the real $1 \times 2$ matrix with both entries
$1$: one ray crosses both cells with equal weight, so the measurement of an attenuation vector
$(x_1, x_2)$ is $x_1 + x_2$. Its rank is $1$, less than the number of cells.
