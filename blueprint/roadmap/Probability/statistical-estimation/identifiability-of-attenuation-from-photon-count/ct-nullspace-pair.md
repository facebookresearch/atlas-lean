# Two distinct nonnegative attenuations with the same measurement

For the [single-ray design](ct-row.md) $A$ with both entries $1$, the attenuation vectors $(1, 0)$
and $(0, 1)$ satisfy $A(1, 0) = A(0, 1) = 1$, are distinct, and have nonnegative entries. Their
difference $(1, -1)$ lies in the kernel of $A$.
