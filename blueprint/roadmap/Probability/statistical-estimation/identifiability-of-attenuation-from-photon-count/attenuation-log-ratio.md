# For $I_0 > 0$, $\log(I_0/I)$ recovers $L$ from $I = I_0 e^{-L}$

For an incident intensity $I_0 > 0$ and every real $L$, the transmitted intensity $I = I_0 e^{-L}$
of a monochromatic beam under the Beer–Lambert law satisfies

$$\log \frac{I_0}{I} = L .$$

In the CT reading, $L$ is the line integral of the attenuation coefficient along the ray, so
$L \ge 0$ for an absorbing medium; the identity holds for every real $L$. Without a condition on
$I_0$ the identity can fail: at $I_0 = 0$ the quotient is $0 / 0 = 0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$,
and with $\log 0 = 0$ the left-hand side is $0$, so the identity fails for $L \ne 0$.
