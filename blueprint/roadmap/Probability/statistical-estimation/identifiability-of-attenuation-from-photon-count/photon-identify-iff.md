# For positive intensities, the count law is injective on $D$ iff $A$ is

Let the rays and the attenuation cells form finite sets, let $A$ be a real matrix indexed by rays
and cells, let every source intensity be positive, $I_{0,j} > 0$, and let $D$ be any set of
attenuation vectors (the admissible attenuations, for example those with nonnegative entries). Then
the map $x \mapsto P_x$ sending an attenuation vector to its [photon-count law](photon-law.md) is
injective on $D$, that is distinct vectors of $D$ have distinct laws, if and only if $x \mapsto Ax$
is injective on $D$. Identifiability of the attenuation on $D$ from the law of the counts means
exactly this injectivity, so it holds exactly when the attenuation is determined on $D$ by its
projections $Ax$.
