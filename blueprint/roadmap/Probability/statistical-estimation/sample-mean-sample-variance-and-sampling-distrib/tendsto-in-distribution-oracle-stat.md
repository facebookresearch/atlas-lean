# Asymptotic chi-square law of the oracle covariance statistic

Let $(A_m, B_m)_{m \ge 0}$ be independent, identically distributed pairs of real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, P)$ with $A_0$ and $B_0$ independent, square integrable, and of positive variance. Let $Z$ have the $\chi^2_1$ law. Then, with the means and variances known,

$$\frac{\Big(N^{-1/2} \sum_{m < N} (A_m - \mathbb{E}A_0)(B_m - \mathbb{E}B_0)\Big)^2}{\operatorname{Var}(A_0)\operatorname{Var}(B_0)} \xrightarrow{d} Z \qquad (N \to \infty).$$
