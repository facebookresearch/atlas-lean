# Squared sample correlation

For real sequences $a, b$ and $N \in \mathbb{N}$, write $\overline{g}_N = \frac1N\sum_{m<N} g(m)$. The squared sample correlation of the first $N$ pairs $(a_m, b_m)$ is

$$r_N^2 = \frac{\big(\overline{ab}_N - \bar b_N \bar a_N\big)^2}{\big(\overline{b^2}_N - \bar b_N^2\big)\big(\overline{a^2}_N - \bar a_N^2\big)},$$

which is $0$ where a sample variance vanishes (by $x/0 = 0$).
