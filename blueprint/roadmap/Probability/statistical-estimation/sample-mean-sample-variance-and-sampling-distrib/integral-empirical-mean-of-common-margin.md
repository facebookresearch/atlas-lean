# Expectation of an empirical average under common marginals

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $\nu$ a probability measure on $S$, $n \ge 1$, $g : S \to \mathbb{R}$ integrable for $\nu$, and $X_1, \dots, X_n : \Omega \to S$ each with law $\nu$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}_\mu\Big[\frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n g(X_i)\Big] = \int_S g\, d\nu.$$

No independence is assumed.
