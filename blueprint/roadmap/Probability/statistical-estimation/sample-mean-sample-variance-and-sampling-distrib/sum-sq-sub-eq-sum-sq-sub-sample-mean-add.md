# Sum of squared deviations about a point

For a finite sample $x = (x_i)_{i \in I}$ of real numbers with $n = |I|$, write $\bar x = \frac1n\sum_i x_i$ for its mean ($0$ for the empty sample). Then for every $c \in \mathbb{R}$

$$\sum_{i \in I} (x_i - c)^2 = \sum_{i \in I} (x_i - \bar x)^2 + n\,(\bar x - c)^2;$$

both sides are $0$ for the empty sample.
