# Unbiasedness of the sample variance under pairwise independence

Let $n \ge 2$ and let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ be identically distributed, square-integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$. If the $X_i$ are pairwise independent, then the sample variance $s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n (X_i - \bar X)^2$, $\bar X = \frac1n \sum_i X_i$ is integrable and

$$\mathbb{E}[s^2] = \operatorname{Var}(X_1).$$

Mutual independence is not needed.
