# With a positive count the likelihood is strictly concave in the shape

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of $n$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and size factors
$s_i$. If some count $k_i$ is positive, then for every fixed rate $b > 0$ and positive size factors
$s_i > 0$, the [log marginal likelihood](log-marginal-lik.md) $\ell(a, b)$ is strictly concave in
the shape $a$ on $(0, \infty)$.
