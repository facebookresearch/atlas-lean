# The rate root at a common size factor matches the sample mean

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. Let the size factors equal a common value, $s_i = \sigma$ for every $i$, with
$\sigma > 0$, and let the [sample mean](count-mean.md) $M$ be positive. Then for every shape $a > 0$
the rate $\hat b(a) = \sigma a / M$ solves the [rate score](rate-score.md) equation
$S_b(a, \hat b(a)) = 0$.
