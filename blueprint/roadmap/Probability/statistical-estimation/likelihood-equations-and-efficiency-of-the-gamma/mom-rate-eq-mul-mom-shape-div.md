# Moment rate estimate in terms of the moment shape

For real $\sigma$, $V$ and $M \ne 0$, the
[moment rate estimate](../../bayesian-statistics/gamma-poisson-counts-and-empirical-bayes-shrinka/mom-rate.md) is $\sigma$
times the [moment shape estimate](../../bayesian-statistics/gamma-poisson-counts-and-empirical-bayes-shrinka/mom-shape.md)
divided by $M$:

$$\frac{\sigma M}{V - M} = \frac{\sigma}{M} \cdot \frac{M^2}{V - M},$$

both sides being $0$ when $V = M$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
