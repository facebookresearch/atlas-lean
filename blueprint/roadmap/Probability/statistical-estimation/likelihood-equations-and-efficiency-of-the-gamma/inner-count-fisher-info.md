# Bilinear form of the Fisher information matrix

For all real $a$, $b$, $\sigma$, $\tau$ and all $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the
[Fisher information matrix](count-fisher-info.md) $I = I(a, b, \sigma, \tau)$ satisfies

$$
\langle I u, v \rangle = \tau u_0 v_0 - \frac{\sigma}{b (b + \sigma)} (u_0 v_1 + u_1 v_0) +
\frac{a \sigma}{b^2 (b + \sigma)} u_1 v_1,
$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
