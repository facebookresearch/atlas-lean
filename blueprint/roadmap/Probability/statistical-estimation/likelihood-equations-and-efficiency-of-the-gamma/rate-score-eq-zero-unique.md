# At most one rate root

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. For a fixed shape $a > 0$ and positive size factors $s_i > 0$, the
[rate score](rate-score.md) equation has at most one positive solution: $S_b(a, b) = S_b(a, b') = 0$
with $b, b' > 0$ implies $b = b'$.
