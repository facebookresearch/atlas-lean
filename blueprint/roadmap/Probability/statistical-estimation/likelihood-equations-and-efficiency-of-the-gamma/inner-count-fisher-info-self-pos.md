# Positive definiteness under the profile-information condition

Let $a, b, \sigma > 0$ and let the shape entry exceed $\sigma / (a (b + \sigma))$,
$\tau > \sigma / (a (b + \sigma))$: positivity of the profile information for the shape. Then the
[Fisher information matrix](count-fisher-info.md) $I = I(a, b, \sigma, \tau)$ is positive definite:
$\langle I u, u \rangle > 0$ for every nonzero $u \in \mathbb{R}^2$.
