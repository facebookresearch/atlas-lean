# With a positive count a shape root exists at every rate

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. If some count $k_i$ is positive, then for every rate $b > 0$ and positive size
factors $s_i > 0$ the [shape score](shape-score.md) equation has a positive solution:
$S_a(a, b) = 0$ for some $a > 0$. It is the [only positive solution](shape-score-eq-zero-unique.md).
