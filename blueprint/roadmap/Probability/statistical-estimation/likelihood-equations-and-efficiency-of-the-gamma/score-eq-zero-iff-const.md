# Joint score equations at a common size factor

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. Let the size factors equal a common value, $s_i = \sigma$ for every $i$, and let
$a, b, \sigma > 0$ and the [sample mean](count-mean.md) $M$ be positive. Then $(a, b)$ solves both
score equations, $S_a(a, b) = 0$ and $S_b(a, b) = 0$ for the [shape](shape-score.md) and
[rate](rate-score.md) scores, if and only if $b = \sigma a / M$ and $a$ is a root of the
[profile shape score](profile-shape-score.md), $S_p(a) = 0$.
