# Summed per-count scores give the shape and rate scores

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of observations with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$. For every real
$\sigma$ and every $\theta = (a, b) \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the sum of the
[scores of one count](count-score.md) is

$$\sum_{i \in \iota} \psi_\sigma(\theta, k_i) = \bigl(S_a(a, b),\ S_b(a, b)\bigr),$$

the [shape score](shape-score.md) and the [rate score](rate-score.md) of the sample at the common
size factor $s_i = \sigma$.
