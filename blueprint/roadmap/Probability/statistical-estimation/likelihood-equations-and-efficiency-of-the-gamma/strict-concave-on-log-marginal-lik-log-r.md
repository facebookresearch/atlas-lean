# Strict concavity in the log rate

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. For a fixed shape $a > 0$ and positive size factors $s_i > 0$, the map
$t \mapsto \ell(a, e^t)$ is strictly concave on $\mathbb{R}$, where $\ell$ is the
[log marginal likelihood](log-marginal-lik.md).
