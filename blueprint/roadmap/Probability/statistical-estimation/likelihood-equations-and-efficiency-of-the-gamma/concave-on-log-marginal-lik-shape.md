# Concavity of the log marginal likelihood in the shape

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of $n$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and size factors
$s_i$. For a fixed rate $b > 0$ and positive size factors $s_i > 0$, the
[log marginal likelihood](log-marginal-lik.md) $\ell(a, b)$ is concave in the shape $a$ on
$(0, \infty)$.
