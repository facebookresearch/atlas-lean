# All-zero counts: no rate root

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. If every count is $0$, then for $a > 0$, $b > 0$ and positive size factors
$s_i > 0$ the [rate score](rate-score.md) is strictly positive: $S_b(a, b) > 0$. The log marginal
likelihood is then strictly increasing in $b > 0$, without a critical point.
