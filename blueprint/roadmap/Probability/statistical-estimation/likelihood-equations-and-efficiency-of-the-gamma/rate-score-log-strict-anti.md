# The log-rate score is strictly decreasing in the rate

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. For $a > 0$, positive size factors $s_i > 0$ and $0 < x < y$, the
[log-rate score](rate-score-log.md)
$\tilde S_b(a, b) = \sum_i \bigl(a - (a + k_i)\, b/(b + s_i)\bigr)$ satisfies
$\tilde S_b(a, y) < \tilde S_b(a, x)$.
