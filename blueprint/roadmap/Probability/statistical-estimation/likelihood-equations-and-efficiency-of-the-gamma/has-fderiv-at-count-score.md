# The score of one count is differentiable at positive parameters

Let $\sigma \ge 0$, let $\theta = (a, b)$ with $a, b > 0$, and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the map
$\theta' \mapsto \psi_\sigma(\theta', k)$, the [score of one count](count-score.md), is
differentiable at $\theta$, with derivative the [score derivative matrix](count-hessian.md)
$H_\sigma(\theta, k)$.
