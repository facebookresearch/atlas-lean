# Trigamma increment

For a real $a$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the trigamma increment is

$$\varphi_k(a) = \sum_{j = 0}^{k - 1} \frac{1}{(a + j)^2},$$

with the convention $0^{-1} = 0$, so a term with $a + j = 0$ contributes $0$. For $a > 0$ it equals
$\psi'(a) - \psi'(a + k)$, with $\psi'$ the trigamma function, and it is minus the derivative in $a$
of the [digamma increment](digamma-increment.md).
