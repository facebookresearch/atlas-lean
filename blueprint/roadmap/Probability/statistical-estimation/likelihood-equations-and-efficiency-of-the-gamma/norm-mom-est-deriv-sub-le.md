# Lipschitz envelope of the moment derivative

For every real $\sigma$, all $\theta, \theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and every count $k$, the
[derivative matrix](mom-est-deriv.md) $D_\sigma$ of the moment estimating function satisfies

$$
\bigl\| D_\sigma(\theta, k) - D_\sigma(\theta_0, k) \bigr\|
\le \bigl(2 |\sigma| + 2 \sigma^2 + 2 k^2\bigr) \|\theta - \theta_0\|,
$$

with the operator norm on the left.
