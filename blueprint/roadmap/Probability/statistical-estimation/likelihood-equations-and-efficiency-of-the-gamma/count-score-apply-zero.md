# Shape component of the score of one count

For a real size factor $\sigma$, every $\theta = (a, b) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and every count $k$, the
first coordinate of the [score of one count](count-score.md) $\psi_\sigma(\theta, k)$ is
$\psi_k(a) + \log b - \log(b + \sigma)$, with the [digamma increment](digamma-increment.md)
$\psi_k(a)$.
