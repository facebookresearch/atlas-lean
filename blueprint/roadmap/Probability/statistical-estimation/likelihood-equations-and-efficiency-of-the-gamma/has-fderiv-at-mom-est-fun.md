# The moment estimating function is differentiable everywhere

For every real $\sigma$, every $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and every count $k$, the map
$\theta' \mapsto g_\sigma(\theta', k)$, the [moment estimating function](mom-est-fun.md), is
differentiable at $\theta$ with derivative the [matrix](mom-est-deriv.md) $D_\sigma(\theta, k)$.
