# Digamma increment

For a real $a$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the digamma increment is

$$\psi_k(a) = \sum_{j = 0}^{k - 1} \frac{1}{a + j},$$

with the convention $0^{-1} = 0$, so a term with $a + j = 0$ contributes $0$; it is $0$ for $k = 0$.
For $a > 0$ it equals $\psi(a + k) - \psi(a)$, with $\psi$ the digamma function, and it is the
derivative in $a$ of $\log\bigl(a (a + 1) \cdots (a + k - 1)\bigr)$.
