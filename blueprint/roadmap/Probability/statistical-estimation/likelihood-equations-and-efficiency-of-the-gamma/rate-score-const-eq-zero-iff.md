# Rate score equation at a common size factor

Let $\iota$ be a finite nonempty set of $n \ge 1$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and
size factors $s_i$. Let the size factors equal a common value, $s_i = \sigma$ for every $i$, with
$\sigma > 0$. For every real $a$ and every $b > 0$, the [rate score](rate-score.md) vanishes if and
only if $b M = \sigma a$, with $M$ the [sample mean](count-mean.md): the fitted marginal mean
$\sigma a / b$ equals the sample mean.
