# Strong consistency of the scaled block jackknife covariance, entrywise

Let $Z_0, Z_1, \dots$ be mutually independent, identically distributed random vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$ ($n$ finite) on a probability space with square-integrable coordinates, and let $V_B$ be the delete-one jackknife covariance of $Z_0, \dots, Z_{B-1}$. Then almost surely, for all $i, j$, $(B\,V_B)_{ij} \to \operatorname{Cov}(Z_{0,i}, Z_{0,j})$ as $B \to \infty$.
