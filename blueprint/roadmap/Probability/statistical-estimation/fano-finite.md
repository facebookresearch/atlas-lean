# Fano's inequality

Let $P_1, \dots, P_M$ be distributions on a finite type, with $M \ge 2$, such that $P_j(a) = 0$
implies $P_i(a) = 0$ for all $i, j, a$, and whose pairwise Kullback–Leibler divergences (in nats)
are at most $\beta$. Then every estimator $\hat\imath$ has average error probability
$\frac{1}{M} \sum_i P_i(\hat\imath \ne i)$ at least $1 - (\beta + \log 2) / \log M$.
