# First-order Taylor bound from a Lipschitz derivative

Let $f : E \to F$ ($E$ a real inner product space, $F$ a real normed space) be differentiable on $B(\theta_0, \delta)$ with $\|Df(\theta) - Df(\theta_0)\| \le C\|\theta - \theta_0\|$ there. Then for $\theta \in B(\theta_0, \delta)$, $\|f(\theta) - f(\theta_0) - Df(\theta_0)(\theta - \theta_0)\| \le C\|\theta - \theta_0\|^2$.
