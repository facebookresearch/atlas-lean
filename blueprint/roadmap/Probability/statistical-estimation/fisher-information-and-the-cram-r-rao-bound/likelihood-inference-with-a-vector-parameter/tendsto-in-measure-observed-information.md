# Consistency of the observed information at the true parameter

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space of parameters, $\nu$ a measure on an observation space, $p_\theta$ a density with respect to $\nu$ for each $\theta \in E$, $P_\theta$ the law with density $p_\theta$, $s_\theta(x) = \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(x)$ the score, $\dot s_\theta(x)$ its derivative in $\theta$ (an operator on $E$), and $I(\theta) = \int s_\theta s_\theta^\top\,dP_\theta$ the Fisher information operator. Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent observations on a probability space, each with law $P_{\theta_0}$. Assume $\dot s_{\theta_0}$ is strongly measurable and integrable with $\int\dot s_{\theta_0}\,dP_{\theta_0} = -I(\theta_0)$ (the information identity). Write $\hat J_n(\theta) = -\frac1n\sum_{i<n}\dot s_\theta(X_i)$ for the observed information. Then $\hat J_n(\theta_0) \to I(\theta_0)$ in probability in operator norm.
