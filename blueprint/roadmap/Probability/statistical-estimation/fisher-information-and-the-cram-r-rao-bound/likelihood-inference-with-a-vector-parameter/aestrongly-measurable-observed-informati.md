# Measurability of the observed information at a fixed parameter

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be almost everywhere measurable observations on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$) and $\theta_0$ a parameter such that $x \mapsto D_\theta s_\theta(x)|_{\theta_0}$ is strongly measurable. Then for every $n$ the observed information $\hat J_n(\theta_0)$ of $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ is almost everywhere strongly measurable.
