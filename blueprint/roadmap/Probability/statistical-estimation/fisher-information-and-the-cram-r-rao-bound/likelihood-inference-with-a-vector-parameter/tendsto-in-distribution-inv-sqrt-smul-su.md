# Central limit theorem for vector transforms

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent observations with law $\mu$ on a probability space, $g$ a measurable map into a finite-dimensional real inner product space with $\int\|g\|^2d\mu < \infty$ and $\int g\,d\mu = 0$, and $Y$ a random vector with $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \int\langle g, t\rangle^2d\mu)$ for every $t$. Then $n^{-1/2}\sum_{i<n}g(X_i)$ converges in distribution to $Y$.
