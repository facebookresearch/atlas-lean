# Score vector of a parametric family

For densities $p_\theta(x)$ indexed by a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$, the score at $\theta$ is the gradient $s_\theta(x) = \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(x) \in E$. Here $\log$ is the real logarithm extended by $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$.
