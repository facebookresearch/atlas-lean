# Variance of a projected score

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $\nu$ a measure, $p_\theta$ ($\theta \in E$) densities with respect to $\nu$, $P_\theta = p_\theta\,\nu$, $s_\theta = \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta$ the score and $I(\theta)$ the Fisher information operator. Let $X_0$ have law $P_{\theta_0}$, with $s_{\theta_0}$ measurable, $\int\|s_{\theta_0}\|^2dP_{\theta_0} < \infty$ and $\int s_{\theta_0}dP_{\theta_0} = 0$. Then $\operatorname{Var}\langle s_{\theta_0}(X_0), t\rangle = \langle I(\theta_0)t, t\rangle$ for every $t$.
