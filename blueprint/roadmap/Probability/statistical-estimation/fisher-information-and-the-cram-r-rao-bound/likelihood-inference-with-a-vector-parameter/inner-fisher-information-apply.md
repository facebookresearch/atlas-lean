# Bilinear form of the Fisher information

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $\nu$ a measure, $p_\theta$ ($\theta \in E$) densities with respect to $\nu$, $P_\theta = p_\theta\,\nu$, $s_\theta = \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta$ the score and $I(\theta)$ the Fisher information operator. If $s_\theta$ is square integrable under $P_\theta$, then $\langle I(\theta)u, v\rangle = \int\langle s_\theta, u\rangle\langle s_\theta, v\rangle\,dP_\theta$ for all $u, v$.
