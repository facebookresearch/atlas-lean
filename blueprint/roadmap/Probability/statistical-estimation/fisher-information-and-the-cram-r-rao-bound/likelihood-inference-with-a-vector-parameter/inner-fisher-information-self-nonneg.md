# The Fisher information is positive semidefinite

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $\nu$ a measure, $p_\theta$ ($\theta \in E$) densities with respect to $\nu$, $P_\theta = p_\theta\,\nu$, $s_\theta = \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta$ the score and $I(\theta)$ the Fisher information operator. Then $\langle I(\theta)u, u\rangle \ge 0$ for every $u$.
