# Measurability of the observed information at a random parameter

Suppose $(\theta, x) \mapsto D_\theta s_\theta(x)$ is jointly measurable, let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be almost everywhere measurable observations and $\hat\theta$ an almost everywhere measurable random parameter. Then for every $n$ the observed information of $X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}$ at $\hat\theta$ is almost everywhere measurable.
