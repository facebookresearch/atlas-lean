# Measure associated with a density family member

Let $(\mathcal{X}, \nu)$ be a measure space and $(p_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ real functions on $\mathcal{X}$. The measure associated with the family member $\theta$ is the measure with density $\max(p_\theta, 0)$ with respect to $\nu$.
It is a probability measure, the law of the family member, exactly when $\int \max(p_\theta, 0) \, d\nu = 1$; for example $p_\theta = 0$
gives the zero measure.
