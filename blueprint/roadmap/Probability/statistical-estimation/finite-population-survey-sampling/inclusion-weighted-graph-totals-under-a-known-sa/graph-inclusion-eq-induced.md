# Inclusion probability of a subset under the induced design

Let $V$ be a finite vertex set, $p$ a sampling design on vertex subsets, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $M$ a $k$-element subset of $V$. The inclusion probability of $M$ under the induced design, the law of the family of $k$-subsets contained in the sample, equals the graph inclusion probability $\Pr(M \subseteq s)$ under $p$.
