# Inclusion probability of a unit under a sampling design

Let $U$ be a finite population and let $p$ be a sampling design on $U$: a probability mass function on the finite subsets $s \subseteq U$ (the samples). The inclusion probability of a unit $i \in U$ is the total probability of the samples that contain it:

$$\pi_i = \sum_{s \subseteq U,\; i \in s} p(s).$$

## Sources

- D. G. Horvitz and D. J. Thompson, "A generalization of sampling without replacement from a finite universe", *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 47(260) (1952), 663–685.
