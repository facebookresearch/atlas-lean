# Second-order inclusion probability

Let $U$ be a finite population and let $p$ be a sampling design on $U$: a probability mass function on the finite subsets $s \subseteq U$ (the samples). The second-order (pair) inclusion probability of units $i, j \in U$ is the total probability of the samples that contain both:

$$\pi_{ij} = \sum_{s \subseteq U,\; i \in s,\; j \in s} p(s).$$

For $i = j$ it is the inclusion probability $\pi_i$ of $i$.
