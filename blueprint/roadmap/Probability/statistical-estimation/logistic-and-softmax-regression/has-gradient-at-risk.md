# Gradient of the empirical logistic risk

For data $(X_k, Y_k)_{k < m}$ with features $X_k \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, for a finite index set $\iota$, and Boolean labels $Y_k$, the empirical logistic risk is differentiable at every weight vector, and with $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ carrying the Euclidean inner product its gradient is the gradient of the logistic log-loss summed over the data.
