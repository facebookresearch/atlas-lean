# The identified set of the average treatment effect is the whole real line

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $T$ a measurable treatment event, $X$ a measurable covariate and $Y_0, Y_1$ measurable integrable potential outcomes. Then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$ there are measurable integrable potential outcomes $Y'_0, Y'_1$ that agree with $Y_1$ on $T$ and with $Y_0$ off $T$ (hence have the same observational law), have unit effect $Y'_1 - Y'_0 = t$ everywhere and average treatment effect $t$. No ignorability, overlap or bounds are assumed.
