# No functional of the observed law recovers the average treatment effect

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $T$ a measurable treatment event, $X$ a measurable covariate and $Y_0, Y_1$ measurable integrable potential outcomes. Then for every map $F$ from laws of observed data to $\mathbb{R}$ there are measurable integrable potential outcomes $Y'_0, Y'_1$ with the same observational law as $(Y_0, Y_1)$ and $\mathbb{E}[Y'_1 - Y'_0] \ne F(\text{observational law})$.
