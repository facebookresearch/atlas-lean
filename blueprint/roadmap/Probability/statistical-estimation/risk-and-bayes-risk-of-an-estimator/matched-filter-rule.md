# Matched-filter decision rule

For a finite index set `ι`, means `s₀, s₁ ∈ ℝ^ι`, a real `ι × ι` matrix `R`, priors, costs, a tie
decision and an observation `y`, the rule decides `true` when the
[matched statistic](matched-statistic.md) at `y` exceeds the [threshold](matched-threshold.md), the
tie decision when they are equal, and `false` when the statistic is below the threshold.
