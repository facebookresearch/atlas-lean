# One-dimensional example of the matched-filter rule

Take `s₀ = 0`, `s₁ = 1`, `R = (1)`, `π₀ = 1/4`, `π₁ = 3/4`, `C₀₀ = 0`, `C₀₁ = 4`, `C₁₀ = 2` and
`C₁₁ = 1`. The [matched statistic](matched-statistic.md) is `y` and the
[threshold](matched-threshold.md) is `log(2/9) + 1/2 ≈ -1.004`, so for either tie decision the
[rule](matched-filter-rule.md) decides `true` at `y = 0` and `false` at `y = -2`.
