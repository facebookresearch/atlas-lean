# Swapping the two error costs changes the threshold

In the [one-dimensional example](matched-filter-rule-fin-one-example.md), swapping `C₀₁` and `C₁₀`
(`C₀₁ = 2`, `C₁₀ = 4`, so still `C₀₀ < C₁₀` and `C₁₁ < C₀₁`) changes the [threshold](matched-threshold.md)
from `log(2/9) + 1/2` to `log(4/3) + 1/2`.
