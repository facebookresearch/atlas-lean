# Statistical estimation

Point estimation theory and confidence sets: bias, risk and mean squared error, likelihood and maximum likelihood (consistency and asymptotic normality), Fisher information and Cramér–Rao, sufficiency, factorization (Fisher–Neyman, Halmos–Savage) and Rao–Blackwell, Stein's lemma and James–Stein shrinkage, contiguity and Le Cam's lemmas, asymptotic efficiency (Hájek's convolution theorem and adaptive estimation), and the bootstrap with the Mallows metric. The local asymptotic normality of the log-likelihood ratio is under [local power of the likelihood-ratio test](../hypothesis-testing/local-power-of-the-likelihood-ratio-test/README.md) in Hypothesis testing. Penalized least squares (ridge regression, and the lasso with its soft-thresholding solution for an orthonormal design) is under [least squares, ridge and lasso regression](../../ComputerScience/machine-learning/supervised-learning/least-squares-ridge-and-lasso-regression/README.md) in Machine learning.
