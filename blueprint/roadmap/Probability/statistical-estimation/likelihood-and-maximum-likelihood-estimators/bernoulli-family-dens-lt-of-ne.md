# Strict maximum likelihood in the Bernoulli model

In the model of $n \ge 1$ independent Bernoulli($p$) observations, $p \in [0, 1]$, let $f_p(x)$ be the density at a sample $x \in \{0, 1\}^n$ with $k$ successes, and $\hat p = k/n$. If $0 < k < n$, then $f_p(x) < f_{\hat p}(x)$ for every $p \ne \hat p$.
