# Linear linking of score scales

For $\sigma_X, \sigma_Y > 0$, the map $\ell(x) = \mu_Y + (\sigma_Y/\sigma_X)(x - \mu_X)$ has inverse $y \mapsto \mu_X + (\sigma_X/\sigma_Y)(y - \mu_Y)$, and if $X$ has mean $\mu_X$ and standard deviation $\sigma_X$ then $\ell(X)$ has mean $\mu_Y$ and standard deviation $\sigma_Y$. This is a relation between score coordinates, not a claim that scores on different forms are interchangeable.
