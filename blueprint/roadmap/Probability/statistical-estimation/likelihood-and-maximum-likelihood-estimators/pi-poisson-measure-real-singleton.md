# Probability of a count vector under independent Poisson laws

For a finite index set $\iota$, means $E : \iota \to [0, \infty)$ and counts $k : \iota \to \mathbb{N}$, the product of the Poisson laws with means $E_i$ gives the count vector $k$ the probability

$$\prod_{i \in \iota} \frac{e^{-E_i} E_i^{k_i}}{k_i!}.$$
