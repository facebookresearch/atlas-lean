# The second-moment operator of a square-integrable score

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space and $\psi_0$ a measurable map into $E$ with $\int\|\psi_0\|^2d\mu < \infty$. Then the second-moment operator of the image law of $\mu$ under $\psi_0$ is the Bochner integral $\int\psi_0\psi_0^\top\,d\mu$ of the rank-one operators $u \mapsto \langle\psi_0(x), u\rangle\psi_0(x)$.
