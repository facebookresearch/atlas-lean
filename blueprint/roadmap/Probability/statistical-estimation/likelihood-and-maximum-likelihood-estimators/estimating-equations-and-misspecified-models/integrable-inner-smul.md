# Integrability of a projection times a square-integrable map

Let $f, g$ be almost everywhere strongly measurable maps from a measure space into a real inner product space $E$ with $\int\|f\|^2 < \infty$ and $\int\|g\|^2 < \infty$. Then for every $w \in E$, $x \mapsto \langle g(x), w\rangle f(x)$ is integrable.
