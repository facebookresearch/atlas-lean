# Consistency in probability of maximum likelihood on a closed parameter set

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a probability space, write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. Let the observations be pairwise independent and identically distributed. Let $S$ be a closed subset of a proper pseudometric space (such as $\mathbb{R}^d$), $\theta_0$ the unique maximizer of $Q$ on $S$, $\ell_{\theta_0}$ measurable, $\ell_\theta(X_0)$ integrable for every $\theta \in S$, local envelopes on $S$ at every $\theta \in S \setminus \{\theta_0\}$, and an envelope at infinity around some centre. Then every almost everywhere strongly measurable maximum-likelihood estimator on $S$ converges to $\theta_0$ in probability.
