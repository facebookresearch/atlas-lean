# Wald's argmax theorem for an upper semicontinuous limit on a compact set

Let $K$ be a compact subset of a pseudometric space, $Q$ upper semicontinuous on $K$ with unique maximizer $\theta_0 \in K$, and $Q_n \to Q$ uniformly on $K$. If $\hat\theta_n \in K$ and $Q_n(\theta_0) \le Q_n(\hat\theta_n)$ for every $n$, then $\hat\theta_n \to \theta_0$.
