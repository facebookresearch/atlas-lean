# The population criterion under correct specification

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$ and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$ for the population criterion. If $X_0$ is almost everywhere measurable with law $\mu_0$ and $\ell_\theta$ is $\mu_0$-integrable, then $Q(\theta) = \int\ell_\theta\,d\mu_0$.
