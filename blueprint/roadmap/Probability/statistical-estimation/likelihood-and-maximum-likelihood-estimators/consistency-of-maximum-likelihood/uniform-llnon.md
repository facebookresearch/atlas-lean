# Uniform law of large numbers for a log-likelihood

The uniform law of large numbers holds on a parameter set $K$ if, for almost every outcome, the average log-likelihood $\bar\ell_n(\theta) = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\ell_\theta(X_i)$ converges to the population criterion $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$ uniformly in $\theta \in K$.
