# A local envelope with small mean

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. Let $X_0$ be almost everywhere measurable, and suppose the log-likelihood has local envelopes at $\theta$ (measurable functions $u_n$, decreasing in $n$ and converging pointwise to $\ell_\theta$, with $\ell_{\theta'} \le u_n$ for $\theta' \in S$ within distance $1/(n+1)$ of $\theta$, and $u_0(X_0)$ integrable), $\ell_\theta(X_0)$ is integrable and $Q(\theta) < Q(\theta_0)$. Then some ball $B(\theta, r)$, $r > 0$, carries a single measurable envelope $u \ge \ell_{\theta'}$ ($\theta' \in S$ in the ball) with $u(X_0)$ integrable and $\mathbb{E}[u(X_0)] < Q(\theta_0)$.
