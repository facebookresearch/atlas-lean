# Wald consistency on a compact set from local envelopes

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a probability space, write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. Let the observations be pairwise independent and identically distributed. Let $K$ be compact, $\theta_0$ the unique maximizer of $Q$ on $K$, $\ell_{\theta_0}$ measurable, $\ell_\theta(X_0)$ integrable for every $\theta \in K$, and local envelopes on $K$ exist at every $\theta \in K \setminus \{\theta_0\}$. Then every maximum-likelihood estimator on $K$ converges to $\theta_0$ almost surely; no uniform law of large numbers and no continuity in $\theta$ is assumed.

## Sources

- A. Wald, "Note on the consistency of the maximum likelihood estimate", *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 20 (1949), 595–601.
- T. S. Ferguson, *A Course in Large Sample Theory*, Chapman & Hall (1996), Theorem 17.
