# Uniform law of large numbers for a finite-rank log-likelihood with continuous coefficients

Let the log-likelihood have finite rank: $\ell_\theta(x) = \sum_{j<m}a_j(\theta)g_j(x)$. If $K$ is compact, each $a_j$ is continuous on $K$, each $g_j$ is measurable with $g_j(X_0)$ integrable, and the observations are pairwise independent and identically distributed, then the uniform law of large numbers holds on $K$.
