# Continuity of the population criterion from a uniform law of large numbers

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$ and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $\bar\ell_n(\theta) = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\ell_\theta(X_i)$ for the average log-likelihood and $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$ for the population criterion. If $\theta \mapsto \ell_\theta(x)$ is continuous on $K$ for every $x$ and, along some sample path, $\bar\ell_n \to Q$ uniformly on $K$, then $Q$ is continuous on $K$.
