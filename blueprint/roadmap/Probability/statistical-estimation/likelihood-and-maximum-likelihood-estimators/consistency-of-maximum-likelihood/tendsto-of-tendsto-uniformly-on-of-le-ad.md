# Wald's argmax theorem for near-maximizers

Let $K$ be a compact subset of a pseudometric space, $Q$ upper semicontinuous on $K$ with unique maximizer $\theta_0 \in K$, and $Q_n \to Q$ uniformly on $K$. If $\hat\theta_n \in K$ and $Q_n(\theta_0) \le Q_n(\hat\theta_n) + \eta_n$ with $\eta_n \to 0$, then $\hat\theta_n \to \theta_0$.

## Sources

- A. Wald, "Note on the consistency of the maximum likelihood estimate", *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 20 (1949), 595–601.
- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press (1998), Theorem 5.7.
