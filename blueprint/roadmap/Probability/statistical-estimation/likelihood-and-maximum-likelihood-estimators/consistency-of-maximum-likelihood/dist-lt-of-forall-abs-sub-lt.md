# One step of Wald's consistency argument

Let $K$ be a subset of a pseudometric space, $\theta_0, \hat\theta \in K$, and $Q, Q_n$ real functions. Suppose $Q(\theta) + \delta \le Q(\theta_0)$ for every $\theta \in K$ with $d(\theta, \theta_0) \ge \varepsilon$, $|Q_n(\theta) - Q(\theta)| < \delta/3$ for every $\theta \in K$, and $Q_n(\theta_0) \le Q_n(\hat\theta) + \eta$ with $\eta < \delta/3$. Then $d(\hat\theta, \theta_0) < \varepsilon$.
