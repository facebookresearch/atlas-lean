# Normal location family with fixed variance

For a fixed variance $v>0$, the **normal location family** is the [normalized density family](normalized-density-family.md) on $\mathbb R$ with parameter $m\in\mathbb R$ and Lebesgue density

$$f_m(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi v}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x-m)^2}{2v}\right).$$

Only the mean $m$ varies. Each density integrates to one.
