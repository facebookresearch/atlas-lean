# Invariance of a finite-mixture two-parameter logistic model under a change of ability scale

Let $Z$ be a finite grid with probability mass function $\pi$, $\zeta : Z \to \mathbb{R}$ an ability map, and $f(\theta) = A\theta + B$ with $A > 0$. For items $j$ in a finite index set, transform item parameters by $a_j' = a_j/A$, $b_j' = Ab_j + B$. Since each item's response law is unchanged under this change of ability scale, the law of the response pattern under the mixture $\sum_z \pi(z)\prod_j P(Y_j \mid \zeta(z))$ equals the law under the pushed abilities $f \circ \zeta$ with the transformed items, and so does the law of the total score $\sum_j Y_j$. Mixing over an arbitrary latent ability distribution, rather than a finite grid, is outside this statement.

## Expansion

A sound expansion specifies the following prerequisites and links their statements.

- Invariance of the two-parameter logistic response law under $\theta \mapsto A\theta + B$, $a_j \mapsto a_j/A$, $b_j \mapsto Ab_j + B$ for $A > 0$.
- A primary source that states this result in the form given here is to be located and checked before the statement is relied on.
