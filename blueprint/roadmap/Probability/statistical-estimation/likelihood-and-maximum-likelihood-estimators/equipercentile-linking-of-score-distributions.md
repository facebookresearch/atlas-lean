# Equipercentile linking of score distributions

If probability measures $\mu, \nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ have continuous, strictly increasing distribution functions $F, G$, then $e = G^{-1} \circ F$ pushes $\mu$ forward to $\nu$ and $G^{-1} \circ F$ and $F^{-1} \circ G$ are mutually inverse. For distributions with atoms these maps need not be inverse to each other, with the quantile $G^{-1}(u) = \inf\{x : G(x) \ge u\}$ for $0 < u < 1$: for $\mu$ uniform on $\{0, 1\}$ and $\nu$ uniform on $\{0, 1, 2\}$, $G^{-1}(F(0)) = G^{-1}(1/2) = 1$ and $F^{-1}(G(1)) = F^{-1}(2/3) = 1$, so the round trip sends $0$ to $1$. This is a relation between laws, not a claim that scores on different forms are interchangeable.

## Expansion

A sound expansion specifies the following prerequisites and links their statements.

- Probability integral transform and quantile transform for continuous strictly increasing distribution functions.
