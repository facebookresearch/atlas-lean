# Chebyshev confidence band for an i.i.d. sample mean

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent, identically distributed, square-integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$, let $n \ge 1$, and let $v > 0$ be a known bound on the variance, $\operatorname{Var}(X_0) \le v$. Then for every $\alpha > 0$

$$\mu\Big(\Big|\frac1n \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} X_i - \mathbb{E}[X_0]\Big| < \sqrt{\frac{v}{n\alpha}}\Big) \ge 1 - \alpha;$$

for $\alpha \ge 1$ the bound is vacuous.
