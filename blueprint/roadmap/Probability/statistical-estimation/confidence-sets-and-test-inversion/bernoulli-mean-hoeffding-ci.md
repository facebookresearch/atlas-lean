# Hoeffding confidence interval for a Bernoulli mean

Let $X_1, \dots, X_n$, $n \ge 1$, be independent random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$, each almost surely $0$ or $1$ and almost everywhere measurable, all with mean $p$, and let $0 < \alpha < 1$. Then

$$\mu\Big(\Big|\frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n X_i - p\Big| \le \sqrt{\frac{\log(2/\alpha)}{2n}}\Big) \ge 1 - \alpha.$$
