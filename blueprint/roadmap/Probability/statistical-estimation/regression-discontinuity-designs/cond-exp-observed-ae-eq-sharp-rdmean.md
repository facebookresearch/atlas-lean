# Conditional mean of the observed outcome in a sharp design

Let $X$ be a measurable real running variable and $Y(0), Y(1)$ integrable potential outcomes on a measure space, with treatment $\mathbf 1\{X \ge c\}$ and observed outcome $Y = Y(1)$ on $\{X \ge c\}$ and $Y(0)$ otherwise. Let $m_0, m_1 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathbb{E}[Y(d) \mid X] = m_d(X)$ almost everywhere. Then $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid X] = r(X)$ almost everywhere, where $r(x) = m_1(x)$ for $x \ge c$ and $m_0(x)$ for $x < c$.
