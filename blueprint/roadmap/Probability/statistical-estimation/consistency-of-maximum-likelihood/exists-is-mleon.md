# Existence of a maximum-likelihood estimator

Let $K$ be a nonempty compact subset of a topological space and $\theta \mapsto \ell_\theta(x)$ continuous on $K$ for every $x$. Then for any observations there is an estimator sequence $\hat\theta_n \in K$ that maximizes the average log-likelihood of the first $n$ observations over $K$ for every $n$ and sample.
