# Equal density models have almost everywhere equal densities

Let $\nu$ be a measure and $P_\theta$ the measure with density $p_\theta$ with respect to $\nu$. If $\nu$ is $\sigma$-finite, $p_{\theta_0}, p_\theta$ are measurable and $\nu$-almost everywhere positive, and $P_\theta = P_{\theta_0}$, then $p_\theta = p_{\theta_0}$ $\nu$-almost everywhere.
