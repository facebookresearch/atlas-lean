# Wald consistency of maximum likelihood from finite covers

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. Let the observations be pairwise independent and identically distributed. Let $\theta_0 \in S$, $\ell_{\theta_0}$ measurable with $\ell_{\theta_0}(X_0)$ integrable, and suppose that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ the set $S$ has a Wald cover away from $\theta_0$ at radius $\varepsilon$. Then every maximum-likelihood estimator on $S$ converges to $\theta_0$ almost surely. No compactness and no uniform law of large numbers is assumed.

## Sources

- A. Wald, "Note on the consistency of the maximum likelihood estimate", *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 20 (1949), 595–601.
- T. S. Ferguson, *A Course in Large Sample Theory*, Chapman & Hall (1996), Theorem 17.
