# Strong law for averages of a function of the observations

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be pairwise independent, identically distributed observations on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$) and $g$ measurable with $g(X_0)$ integrable. Then $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}g(X_i) \to \mathbb{E}[g(X_0)]$ almost surely.
