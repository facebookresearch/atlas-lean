# An identified maximum is well separated

Let $K$ be a compact subset of a pseudometric space, $Q$ upper semicontinuous on $K$ with unique maximizer $\theta_0 \in K$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ with $Q(\theta) + \delta \le Q(\theta_0)$ for every $\theta \in K$ with $d(\theta, \theta_0) \ge \varepsilon$.
