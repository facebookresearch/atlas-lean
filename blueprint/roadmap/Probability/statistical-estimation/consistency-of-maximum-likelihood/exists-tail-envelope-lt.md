# An envelope at infinity has small mean far out

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a probability space, write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. Let $X_0$ be almost everywhere measurable and suppose the log-likelihood has an envelope at infinity around $\theta_c$ on $S$. Then for some $n$ there is a measurable $u$ with $u(X_0)$ integrable, $\ell_\theta \le u$ for every $\theta \in S$ at distance greater than $n$ from $\theta_c$, and $\mathbb{E}[u(X_0)] < Q(\theta_0)$.
