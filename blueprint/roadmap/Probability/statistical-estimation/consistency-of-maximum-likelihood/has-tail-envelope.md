# Upper envelopes of a log-likelihood at infinity

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$, $\theta$ in a pseudometric space, and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$. The log-likelihood has an envelope at infinity around $\theta_c$ on a set $S$ if there are measurable functions $v_n$, decreasing in $n$ and tending to $-\infty$ pointwise, with $\ell_\theta \le v_n$ for every $\theta \in S$ at distance greater than $n$ from $\theta_c$, and $v_0(X_0)$ integrable.
