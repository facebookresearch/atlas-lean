# Log-likelihood ratio of two positive densities

Let $\nu$ be a measure and $P_\theta$ the measure with density $p_\theta$ with respect to $\nu$. If $\nu$ is $\sigma$-finite and $p_{\theta_0}, p_\theta$ are measurable and $\nu$-almost everywhere positive, then $\log\frac{dP_{\theta_0}}{dP_\theta} = \log p_{\theta_0} - \log p_\theta$ $\nu$-almost everywhere.
