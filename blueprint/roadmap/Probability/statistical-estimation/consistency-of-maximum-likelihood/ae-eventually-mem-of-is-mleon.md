# Wald's formulation of maximum-likelihood consistency

For a log-likelihood $\ell_\theta(x)$ and observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a measure space with measure $P$ (expectations are integrals against $P$), write $\bar\ell_n(\theta) = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\ell_\theta(X_i)$ for the average log-likelihood and $Q(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\ell_\theta(X_0)]$ for the population criterion. Let $K$ be a compact subset of a pseudometric parameter space, $\theta \mapsto \ell_\theta(x)$ continuous on $K$ for every $x$, assume the uniform law of large numbers on $K$ (almost surely $\bar\ell_n \to Q$ uniformly on $K$), and assume $\theta_0 \in K$ is the unique maximizer of $Q$ on $K$. Let $\hat\theta_n$ be a maximum-likelihood estimator on $K$: $\hat\theta_n \in K$ maximizes $\bar\ell_n$ over $K$ for every $n$ and sample. Then almost surely, for every neighbourhood $U$ of $\theta_0$, $\hat\theta_n \in U$ for all large $n$.
