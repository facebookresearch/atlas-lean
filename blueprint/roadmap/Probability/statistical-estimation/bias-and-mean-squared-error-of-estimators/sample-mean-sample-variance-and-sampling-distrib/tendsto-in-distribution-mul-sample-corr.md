# N times the squared sample correlation is asymptotically chi-square

Let $(A_m, B_m)_{m \ge 0}$ be independent, identically distributed pairs of real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, P)$ with $A_0$ and $B_0$ independent, square integrable, and of positive variance. Let $Z$ have the $\chi^2_1$ law. Let $r_N^2$ be the squared sample correlation of the first $N$ pairs. Then

$$N\, r_N^2 \xrightarrow{d} Z \qquad (N \to \infty),$$

the same limit as with the means and variances known.
