# Mean squared error of the sample mean

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent, identically distributed, square-integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$ and $n \ge 1$. Then $(\bar X_n - \mathbb{E}[X_0])^2$ is integrable, $\bar X_n = \frac1n \sum_{i<n} X_i$, and the mean squared error of $\bar X_n$ as an estimator of $\mathbb{E}[X_0]$ is

$$\mathbb{E}\big[(\bar X_n - \mathbb{E}[X_0])^2\big] = \frac{\operatorname{Var}(X_0)}{n}.$$
