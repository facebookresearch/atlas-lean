# Sampling distribution of a statistic

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $\nu$ a probability measure on $S$, and $X_1, \dots, X_n : \Omega \to S$ mutually independent, each with law $\nu$. For every measurable $T : S^n \to U$, the statistic $T(X_1, \dots, X_n)$ has law $\nu^{\otimes n} \circ T^{-1}$, the image of the product law under $T$.
