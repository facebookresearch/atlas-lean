# Unbiasedness of the sample variance of the first n observations

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be independent, identically distributed, square-integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$, let $n \ge 2$, and let $s^2_n = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i<n}(X_i - \bar X_n)^2$ be the sample variance of the first $n$ observations, $\bar X_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n} X_i$. Then $s^2_n$ is integrable and $\mathbb{E}[s^2_n] = \operatorname{Var}(X_0)$.
