# The sample mean is unbiased under a common mean

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$ with a common mean $\mathbb{E}[X_i] = m$, and let $n \ge 1$. Then the sample mean $\bar X_n = \frac1n \sum_{i<n} X_i$ is an [unbiased estimator](../is-unbiased.md) of $m$ for the one-member family $\{\mu\}$. No independence or identical distribution is assumed.
