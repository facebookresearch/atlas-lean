# When the pooled mean is the average of the group means

For finite samples $x = (x_i)_{i \in I}$ and $y = (y_k)_{k \in K}$ of real numbers with $m = |I|$ and $n = |K|$, write $\bar x$, $\bar y$ for their means and $\overline{(x,y)}$ for the mean of the pooled sample (each $0$ for an empty sample). Then $\overline{(x,y)} = (\bar x + \bar y)/2$ if and only if $m = n$ or $\bar x = \bar y$. An empty group contributes the mean $0$ to the right-hand side.
