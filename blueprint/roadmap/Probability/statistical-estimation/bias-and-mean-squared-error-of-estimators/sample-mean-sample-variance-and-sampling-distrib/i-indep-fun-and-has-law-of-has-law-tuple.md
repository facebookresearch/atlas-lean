# A tuple with product law has independent coordinates

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $\nu$ a probability measure on a measurable space $S$, and $X_1, \dots, X_n : \Omega \to S$. If the tuple $(X_1, \dots, X_n)$ has law $\nu^{\otimes n}$, then $X_1, \dots, X_n$ are mutually independent and each $X_i$ has law $\nu$.
