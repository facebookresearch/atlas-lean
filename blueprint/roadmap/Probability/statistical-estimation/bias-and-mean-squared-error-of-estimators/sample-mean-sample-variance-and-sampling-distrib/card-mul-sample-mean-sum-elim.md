# Mean of a pooled sample

For finite samples $x = (x_i)_{i \in I}$ and $y = (y_k)_{k \in K}$ of real numbers with $m = |I|$ and $n = |K|$, write $\bar x$, $\bar y$ for their means and $\overline{(x,y)}$ for the mean of the pooled sample (each $0$ for an empty sample). Then

$$(m + n)\, \overline{(x,y)} = m\, \bar x + n\, \bar y,$$

with no group required to be nonempty.
