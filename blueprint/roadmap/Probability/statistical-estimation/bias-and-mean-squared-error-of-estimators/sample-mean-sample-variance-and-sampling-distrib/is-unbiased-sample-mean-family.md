# The sample mean of identically distributed observations is unbiased

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be identically distributed, square-integrable real random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$ and $n \ge 1$. Then the sample mean $\bar X_n = \frac1n \sum_{i<n} X_i$ is an [unbiased estimator](../is-unbiased.md) of $\mathbb{E}[X_0]$ for the one-member family $\{\mu\}$.
