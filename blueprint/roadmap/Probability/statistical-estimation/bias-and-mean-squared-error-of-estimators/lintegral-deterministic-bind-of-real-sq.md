# Mean squared error as the integral of the squared error under the law of the estimate

Let $W : S \to \mathbb{R}$ be a measurable estimator, $\mu$ a probability law on $S$ and $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the integral of $a \mapsto (a - \theta)^2$ under the law of $W$ equals the mean squared error $\int(W - \theta)^2\,d\mu \in [0, \infty]$; no integrability is assumed.
