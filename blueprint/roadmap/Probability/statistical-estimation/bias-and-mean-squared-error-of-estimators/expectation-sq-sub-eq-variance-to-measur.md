# Finite and measure-theoretic bias–variance decompositions agree

Let $q$ be a probability mass function on a finite set with singletons measurable, $X$ a real function and $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $\sum_a q(a)(X(a) - \theta)^2 = \operatorname{Var}(X) + \mathrm{bias}^2$, where the variance and the bias $\mathbb{E}[X] - \theta$ are computed under the law of $q$.
