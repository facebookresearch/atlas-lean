# Mean squared error under a finite probability mass function

Let $q$ be a probability mass function on a finite set with singletons measurable, $X$ a real function and $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$. The mean squared error $\mathbb{E}[(X - \theta)^2]$ of $X$ under the law of $q$ equals the finite weighted sum $\sum_a q(a)(X(a) - \theta)^2$.
