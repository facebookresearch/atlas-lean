# Rao–Blackwell inequality for one law

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mu)$ be a probability space and $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ a sub-σ-algebra. Let $L : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex and $W$ an integrable real random variable with $L(W)$ integrable. Then

$$\mathbb{E}_\mu\, L\big(\mathbb{E}_\mu[W \mid \mathcal{G}]\big) \le \mathbb{E}_\mu\, L(W).$$
