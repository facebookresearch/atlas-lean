# Contiguity of sequences of measures

Let $\Omega$ be a measurable space and $(Q_n)$, $(P_n)$ sequences of measures on $\Omega$. The sequence $(Q_n)$ is contiguous with respect to $(P_n)$, written $Q_n \lhd P_n$, if for every sequence of measurable sets $A_n$ with $P_n(A_n) \to 0$ also $Q_n(A_n) \to 0$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Definition 6.3.
