# Le Cam's third lemma

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a probability space, $h \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \ge 0$, and $\Lambda_n, S_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ with each $\Lambda_n$ measurable, such that the measures $Q_n$ with density $e^{\Lambda_n}$ with respect to $P$ are probability measures. Assume that $S_n \to N(0, v)$ in distribution under $P$ and that $\Lambda_n - h S_n \to -h^2 v/2$ in $P$-probability. Then $S_n \to N(hv, v)$ in distribution under $Q_n$.

This is the locally asymptotically normal case of Le Cam's third lemma for the central sequence itself (covariance $hv$ with $\Lambda_n$); the general form, for an arbitrary statistic jointly asymptotically normal with $\Lambda_n$, is not part of this statement.

## Sources

- L. Le Cam, *Locally asymptotically normal families of distributions*, Univ. California Publ. Statist. 3 (1960), 37–98.
- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Theorem 6.6.
