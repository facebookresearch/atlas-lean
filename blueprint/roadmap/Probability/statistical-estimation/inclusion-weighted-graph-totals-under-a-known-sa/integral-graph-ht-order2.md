# The order-two Horvitz–Thompson graph estimator is unbiased

Let $V$ be a finite vertex set, $p$ a sampling design on vertex subsets, and $y$ a real quantity on vertex subsets. If every two-element subset has positive inclusion probability under the induced design, then the order-two Horvitz–Thompson estimator is unbiased for the order-two graph total $\sum_{|M| = 2} y(M)$, a total of dyad or edge quantities, with joint inclusion probabilities $\pi_{ij}$.
