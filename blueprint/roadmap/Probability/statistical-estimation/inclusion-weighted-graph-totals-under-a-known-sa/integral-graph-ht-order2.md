# Integral graph HT order2

Assume that equality on `V` is decidable and that `V` is finite. Assume `∀ m : ↥(Finset.powersetCard 2 (Finset.univ : Finset V)), 0 < inclusionProbability (inducedSubDesign p 2) m`.

Order-2 unbiasedness: this counts dyad and edge quantities, with joint
inclusion `πᵢⱼ` (`graphInclusion_pair`, `pairInclusionProbability`).
