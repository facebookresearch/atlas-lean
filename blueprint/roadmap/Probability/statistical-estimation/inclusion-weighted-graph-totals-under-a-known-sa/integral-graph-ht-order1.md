# Integral graph HT order1

Assume that equality on `V` is decidable and that `V` is finite. Assume `∀ m : ↥(Finset.powersetCard 1 (Finset.univ : Finset V)), 0 < inclusionProbability (inducedSubDesign p 1) m`.

Order-1 unbiasedness: this counts vertex quantities, with first-order
inclusion `πᵢ` (`graphInclusion_singleton`).
