# The order-one Horvitz–Thompson graph estimator is unbiased

Let $V$ be a finite vertex set, $p$ a sampling design on vertex subsets, and $y$ a real quantity on vertex subsets. If every one-element subset has positive inclusion probability under the induced design, then the order-one Horvitz–Thompson estimator is unbiased for the order-one graph total $\sum_{|M| = 1} y(M)$, a total of vertex quantities, with first-order inclusion probabilities $\pi_i$.
