# The order-three Horvitz–Thompson graph estimator is unbiased

Let $V$ be a finite vertex set, $p$ a sampling design on vertex subsets, and $y$ a real quantity on vertex subsets. If every three-element subset has positive inclusion probability under the induced design, then the order-three Horvitz–Thompson estimator is unbiased for the order-three graph total $\sum_{|M| = 3} y(M)$, a total of motif quantities such as triangles and stars, with third-order inclusion probabilities.
