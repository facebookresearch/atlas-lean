# Integral graph HT order3

Assume that equality on `V` is decidable and that `V` is finite. Assume `∀ m : ↥(Finset.powersetCard 3 (Finset.univ : Finset V)), 0 < inclusionProbability (inducedSubDesign p 3) m`.

Order-3 unbiasedness: this counts motif quantities (triangles, stars),
with third-order inclusion `πᵢⱼₕ`.
