# Projection laws of the estimating-equation limit

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $\psi_0$ a measurable map into $E$ with $\int\|\psi_0\|^2d\mu < \infty$, $D$ an operator on $E$, and $V = \int\psi_0\psi_0^\top d\mu$. If a random vector $Y$ satisfies $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N\big(0, \int\langle\psi_0, t\rangle^2d\mu\big)$ for every $t$, then for every $t$, $\langle -D^{-1}Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N\big(0, \langle D^{-1}V(D^{-1})^*t, t\rangle\big)$, with $D^{-1} = 0$ for singular $D$.
