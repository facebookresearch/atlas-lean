# Consistency of the misspecified MLE for the Kullback-Leibler projection

Let the observations $X_0, X_1, \dots$ have true law with density $g > 0$ a.e. with respect to a $\sigma$-finite measure $\nu$, and let a model of densities $p_\theta$ be indexed by a compact subset $K$ of a pseudometric space, each $p_\theta$ ($\theta \in K$) measurable, positive a.e. and a probability density. Assume $\log g$ and every $\log p_\theta$ are integrable under the true law, $\theta \mapsto \log p_\theta(x)$ is continuous on $K$ for each $x$, and the log-likelihood obeys a uniform law of large numbers on $K$. If $\theta^*$ is the Kullback-Leibler projection of the true law onto the model over $K$, then every maximum-likelihood estimator on $K$ converges almost surely to $\theta^*$, whether or not the true law belongs to the model.

## Sources

- H. White, "Maximum likelihood estimation of misspecified models", *Econometrica* 50(1), 1982, Theorem 2.2.
