# An interior maximum-likelihood estimate solves the likelihood equation

Let $\hat\theta_n$ be a maximum-likelihood estimator on a set $K$ of a finite-dimensional real inner product space for the log-likelihood $\ell$ and observations $X_i$. If $K$ is a neighbourhood of $\hat\theta_n(\omega)$ and each $\theta \mapsto \ell_\theta(X_i(\omega))$, $i < n$, has gradient $\psi_{\hat\theta_n(\omega)}(X_i(\omega))$ at $\hat\theta_n(\omega)$, then $\sum_{i<n}\psi_{\hat\theta_n(\omega)}(X_i(\omega)) = 0$.
