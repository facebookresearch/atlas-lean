# Sandwich covariance with a rank-one meat

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $\psi_0$ a map into $E$, $\mu$ a measure, $D$ an operator on $E$, and $V = \int\psi_0\psi_0^\top\,d\mu$ (the Bochner integral, $0$ if not integrable). Since $V$ is self-adjoint, the symmetrized sandwich covariance of $(D, V)$ equals $D^{-1}V(D^{-1})^*$, with $D^{-1}$ the inverse of $D$ ($0$ if $D$ is singular).
