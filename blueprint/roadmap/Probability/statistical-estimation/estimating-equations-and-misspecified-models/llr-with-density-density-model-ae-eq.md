# Log-likelihood ratio of a true density against a model density

Let $\nu$ be $\sigma$-finite, $g$ and $p_\theta$ measurable and $\nu$-almost everywhere positive. Then $\log g - \log p_\theta = \log\frac{d(g\nu)}{d(p_\theta\nu)}$ almost everywhere under the true law $g\,\nu$; $g$ need not be a density of the model.
