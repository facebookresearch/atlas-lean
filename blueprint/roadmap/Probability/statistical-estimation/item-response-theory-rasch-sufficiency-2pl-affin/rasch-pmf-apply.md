# Response-pattern masses under the Rasch model

For a finite set of items $\iota$ with difficulties $b_i$ and an ability $\theta$, the Rasch model gives a response pattern $x \in \{0,1\}^\iota$ the mass $\prod_i \sigma(\theta - b_i)^{x_i} (1 - \sigma(\theta - b_i))^{1 - x_i}$, where $\sigma(u) = 1/(1 + e^{-u})$ is the logistic function.
