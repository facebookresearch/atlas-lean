# Category masses of the graded-response law

In the graded-response model with discrimination $a \ge 0$, nondecreasing thresholds $b_1 \le \dots \le b_m$ and ability $\theta$, the law of the response category gives each of the $m + 1$ categories $j$ the mass equal to the graded category probability, the difference of the cumulative probabilities at $j$ and $j + 1$.
