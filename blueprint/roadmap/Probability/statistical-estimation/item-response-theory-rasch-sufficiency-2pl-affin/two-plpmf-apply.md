# Response-pattern masses under the two-parameter logistic model

In the two-parameter logistic model on a finite set $\iota$ of items, with discriminations $a_i$, difficulties $b_i$ and ability $\theta$, a response pattern $x \in \{0, 1\}^\iota$ has mass $\prod_{i : x_i = 1} \sigma(a_i(\theta - b_i)) \prod_{i : x_i = 0} \bigl(1 - \sigma(a_i(\theta - b_i))\bigr)$, where $\sigma(u) = 1/(1 + e^{-u})$.
