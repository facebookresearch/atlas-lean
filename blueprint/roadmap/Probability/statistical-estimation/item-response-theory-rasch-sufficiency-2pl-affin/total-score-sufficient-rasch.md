# The raw score is sufficient in the Rasch model

Fix finitely many items with difficulties $b_i$. In the Rasch model with ability $\theta$, the responses $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$ are independent with $P(x_i = 1) = \sigma(\theta - b_i)$, where $\sigma$ is the logistic function. The raw score $\sum_i x_i$, the number of correct responses, is sufficient for $\theta$.

- P. Mair and R. Hatzinger, Extended Rasch modeling: the eRm package for the application of IRT models in R, Journal of Statistical Software 20(9) (2007), Section 3.1.
