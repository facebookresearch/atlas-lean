# Two-point lower bound for deterministic estimators via kernels

Let $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be observation laws on a measurable space and $\tau : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$ an estimand. Let $\theta, \theta'$ have the same probability law $P_\theta = P_{\theta'}$ and $|\tau(\theta) - \tau(\theta')| > 2\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon \ge 0$. Then for every measurable estimator $\delta$, the image laws $\delta_*P_\theta$ and $\delta_*P_{\theta'}$ (written as compositions with the deterministic kernel of $\delta$) satisfy $\delta_*P_\theta(|y - \tau(\theta)| > \varepsilon) \ge 1/2$ or $\delta_*P_{\theta'}(|y - \tau(\theta')| > \varepsilon) \ge 1/2$.
