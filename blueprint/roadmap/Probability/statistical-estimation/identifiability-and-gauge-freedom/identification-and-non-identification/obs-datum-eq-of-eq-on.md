# The observed datum depends only on the received arm

Let $T$ be a treatment event, $X$ a covariate and $Y_0, Y_1$ potential outcomes on a sample space, with observed datum $(X, \mathbf 1_T, Y)$, $Y = Y_1$ on $T$ and $Y_0$ off $T$. If $Y'_1 = Y_1$ on $T$ and $Y'_0 = Y_0$ off $T$, then $(Y'_0, Y'_1)$ and $(Y_0, Y_1)$ give the same observed datum at every point.
