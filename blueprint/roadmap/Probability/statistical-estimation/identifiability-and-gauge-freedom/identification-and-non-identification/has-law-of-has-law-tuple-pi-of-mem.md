# Equal product laws have equal margins

Let $n \ge 1$, $i_0$ an index, and $X = (X_1, \dots, X_n)$ random elements of a measurable space on a probability space. If $X$ has law $\nu^{\otimes n}$ and also law $\nu'^{\otimes n}$, with $\nu$ a probability measure and $\nu'$ $\sigma$-finite, then $X_{i_0}$ has law $\nu$ and $\nu = \nu'$.
