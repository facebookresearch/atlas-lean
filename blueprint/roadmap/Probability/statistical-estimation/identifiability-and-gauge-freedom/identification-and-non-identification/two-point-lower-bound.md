# Two-point indistinguishability

Let $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be observation laws on a measurable space and $\tau : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$ an estimand. Let $\theta, \theta'$ have the same probability law $P_\theta = P_{\theta'}$ and $|\tau(\theta) - \tau(\theta')| > 2\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon \ge 0$. Then for every measurable estimator $\delta$,

$$P_\theta\big(|\delta - \tau(\theta)| \le \varepsilon\big) + P_{\theta'}\big(|\delta - \tau(\theta')| \le \varepsilon\big) \le 1.$$
