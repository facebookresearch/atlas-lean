# The average treatment effect is not identified without assumptions

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $T$ a measurable treatment event and $X$ a measurable covariate. Then on the class of measurable integrable potential-outcome pairs $(Y_0, Y_1)$, the average treatment effect $\mathbb{E}[Y_1 - Y_0]$ is not identified by the observational law of $(X, \mathbf 1_T, Y)$.
