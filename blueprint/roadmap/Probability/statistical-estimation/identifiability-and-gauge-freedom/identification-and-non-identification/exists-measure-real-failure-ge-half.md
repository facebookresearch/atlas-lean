# Estimation lower bound from non-identification

Let $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be observation laws on a measurable space and $\tau : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$ an estimand. Let $\theta, \theta'$ have the same probability law $P_\theta = P_{\theta'}$ and $|\tau(\theta) - \tau(\theta')| > 2\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon \ge 0$. Then every measurable estimator $\delta$ satisfies $P_\theta(|\delta - \tau(\theta)| > \varepsilon) \ge 1/2$ or $P_{\theta'}(|\delta - \tau(\theta')| > \varepsilon) \ge 1/2$.
