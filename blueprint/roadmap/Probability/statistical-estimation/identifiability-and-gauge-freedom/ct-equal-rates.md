# Attenuations $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$ give the same transmitted rate

For every nonnegative source intensity $I_0$ of the single ray, the
[transmitted rate](transmitted-rate.md) $I_0\, e^{-(Ax)}$ of the [single-ray design](ct-row.md) $A$
is the same at the attenuation vectors $x = (1, 0)$ and $x = (0, 1)$: both equal $I_0 e^{-1}$. No
positivity of $I_0$ is needed.
