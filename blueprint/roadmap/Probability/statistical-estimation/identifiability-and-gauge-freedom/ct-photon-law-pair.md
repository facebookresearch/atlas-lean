# Attenuations $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$ give the same photon-count law

For every nonnegative source intensity $I_0$ of the single ray, the
[photon-count law](photon-law.md) of the [single-ray design](ct-row.md) (a probability measure on
the photon count of the ray, Poisson with the transmitted rate as mean) is the same at the
attenuation vectors $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$: in both cases the count is Poisson with mean
$I_0 e^{-1}$. No positivity of $I_0$ is needed.
