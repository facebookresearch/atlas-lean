# Attenuations with $Ax = Ay$ have the same transmitted rates

Let the rays be indexed by any set and the attenuation cells by a finite set, let $A$ be a real
matrix indexed by rays and cells, and let the source intensities $I_{0,j}$ be nonnegative. If two
attenuation vectors satisfy $Ax = Ay$, then their [transmitted rates](transmitted-rate.md) agree in
every ray:

$$I_{0,j}\, e^{-(Ax)_j} = I_{0,j}\, e^{-(Ay)_j} \quad \text{for every ray } j .$$

No positivity of the intensities is needed.
