# The transmitted rate as a real number is $I_{0,j} e^{-(Ax)_j}$

Let the rays be indexed by any set and the attenuation cells by a finite set. For nonnegative source
intensities $I_{0,j}$, a real matrix $A$ indexed by rays and cells, an attenuation vector $x$ and a
ray $j$, the [transmitted rate](transmitted-rate.md) of ray $j$, defined as a nonnegative real
number, is, as a real number,

$$\lambda_j(x) = I_{0,j}\, e^{-(Ax)_j} .$$

The definition truncates nothing at $0$, since the exponential factor is positive.
