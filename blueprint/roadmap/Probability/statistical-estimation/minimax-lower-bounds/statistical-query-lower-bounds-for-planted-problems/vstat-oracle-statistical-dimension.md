# VSTAT oracle and statistical dimension with average correlation

For a distribution $D$ on a domain $\mathcal{X}$ and $t > 0$, the oracle **VSTAT($t$)** answers a query $h : \mathcal{X} \to [0,1]$ with any value $v$ such that $|v - p| \le \max\{1/t,\, \sqrt{p(1-p)/t}\}$, where $p = \mathbb{E}_D[h]$; it mimics the accuracy of an empirical average over $t$ samples. For a reference distribution $D$ and distributions $D_1, D_2$ that are absolutely continuous with respect to $D$ (so the densities $\tfrac{dD_i}{dD}$ exist; two point masses at distinct points, for instance, have none with respect to each other) and have $\tfrac{dD_i}{dD} - 1 \in L^2(D)$, the **pairwise correlation** is the finite number $\chi_D(D_1, D_2) = \mathbb{E}_D\big[(\tfrac{dD_1}{dD} - 1)(\tfrac{dD_2}{dD} - 1)\big]$. For a finite family $\mathcal{H}$ of such distributions, the **statistical dimension of $\mathcal{H}$** relative to $D$ with average correlation $\gamma$, written $\mathrm{SDA}(\mathcal{H}, D, \gamma)$, is the largest $d$ such that every subfamily $\mathcal{H}' \subseteq \mathcal{H}$ of size at least $|\mathcal{H}|/d$ has average pairwise correlation $\rho(\mathcal{H}', D) = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{H}'|^2}\sum_{D_1, D_2 \in \mathcal{H}'} |\chi_D(D_1, D_2)| \le \gamma$. The **statistical dimension of the decision problem** $B(\mathcal{D}, D)$ (distinguish $D$ from the family $\mathcal{D}$), written $\mathrm{SDA}(B(\mathcal{D}, D), \gamma)$, is the largest $d$ for which **some finite witness family** $\mathcal{H} \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ of such distributions has $\mathrm{SDA}(\mathcal{H}, D, \gamma) \ge d$. The condition is on the witness only, not on all of $\mathcal{D}$: adding easy, highly correlated alternatives to $\mathcal{D}$ cannot lower the dimension of the decision problem.
