# Le Cam's two-point bound

For probability mass functions $p, q$ on a finite set and any test $\delta$ that decides for $q$ where $\delta = 1$, the sum of the two error probabilities, $p(\{\delta = 1\}) + q(\{\delta = 0\})$, is at least $1 - d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p, q)$, with $d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p, q) = \tfrac12 \sum_a |p(a) - q(a)|$.
