# Kernel direction defeats unique recovery on an affine space

Let $A$ be a real $n_m \times n_p$ matrix, $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_p}$ and $V$ a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{n_p}$, and suppose some $v_0 \in V$ with $v_0 \ne 0$ satisfies $A v_0 = 0$. For every $\theta \in \theta_0 + V$ and $y$ with $A\theta = y$, there is $\theta' \in \theta_0 + V$ with $\theta' \ne \theta$ and $A\theta' = y$.
