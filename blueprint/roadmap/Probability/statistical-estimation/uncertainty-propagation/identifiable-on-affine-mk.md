# Identifiability on an affine space through a base point

Let $A$ be a real $n_m \times n_p$ matrix, $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_p}$ and $V$ a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{n_p}$. Then $A$ is identifiable on the affine subspace $\theta_0 + V$ if and only if for all $\theta_1, \theta_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_p}$ with $\theta_1 - \theta_0 \in V$ and $\theta_2 - \theta_0 \in V$, $A\theta_1 = A\theta_2$ implies $\theta_1 = \theta_2$.
