# Mean of an affine image of a random vector

Let $\Omega$ be a finite type and $p : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ weights with $\sum_\omega p(\omega) = 1$. For $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$, a real $m \times n$ matrix $A$ and $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the mean vector satisfies

$$\mathbb{E}_p[A X + b] = A\, \mathbb{E}_p[X] + b .$$
