# Coordinatewise finite mean of a random vector

For a finite type $\Omega$, real weights $p : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and a random vector $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$, the mean vector has coordinates

$$(\mathbb{E}_p X)_i = \sum_{\omega \in \Omega} p(\omega)\, X(\omega)_i, \qquad i \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}.$$

No positivity or normalization of $p$ is required by the definition.
