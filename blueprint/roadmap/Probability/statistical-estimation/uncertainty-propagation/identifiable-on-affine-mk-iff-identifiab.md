# Affine identifiability through a basis of directions

Let $A$ be a real $n_m \times n_p$ matrix, $P$ a real $n_p \times n_d$ matrix, $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_p}$ and $V$ a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{n_p}$. Assume $P\xi \in V$ for every $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^{n_d}$, every $v \in V$ equals $P\xi$ for some $\xi$, and $P\xi = 0$ implies $\xi = 0$. Then $A$ is identifiable on $\theta_0 + V$ if and only if $\xi \mapsto (AP)\,\xi$ is injective.
