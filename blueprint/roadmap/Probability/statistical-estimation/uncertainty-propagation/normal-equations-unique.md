# Normal equations have at most one solution

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix for which $\theta \mapsto A\theta$ is injective, and let $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$. If $\theta_1, \theta_2 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ both satisfy $(A^{\mathsf{T}} A)\,\theta = A^{\mathsf{T}} y$, then $\theta_1 = \theta_2$. Existence of a solution is not asserted.
