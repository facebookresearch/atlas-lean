# Jacobian remainder

For $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ and $x, \delta \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the Jacobian remainder is

$$R_f(x, \delta) = f(x + \delta) - f(x) - Df(x)\, \delta,$$

where $Df(x)$ is the Fréchet derivative of $f$ at $x$, taken to be $0$ when $f$ is not differentiable at $x$. It is the error of the first-order (Jacobian) approximation and is not zero in general.
