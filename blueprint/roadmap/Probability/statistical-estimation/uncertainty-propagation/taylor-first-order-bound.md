# First-order Taylor remainder bound

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be twice continuously differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, let $a, b, H \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|f''(y)| \le H$ for all $y \in [a, b]$, and let $x, \delta$ be real with $x \in [a, b]$ and $x + \delta \in [a, b]$. Then

$$\big|f(x + \delta) - f(x) - f'(x)\, \delta\big| \le \frac{H}{2}\, \delta^2 .$$
