# Convolution powers

Let $E$ be a separable normed abelian group with its Borel σ-algebra. For a probability measure $\mu$ on $E$ and $m \in \mathbb{N}$, the convolution power $\mu^{*m}$ is the law of $Z_1 + \dots + Z_m$ for independent $Z_j \sim \mu$, defined by $\mu^{*0} = \delta_0$ and $\mu^{*(m+1)} = \mu^{*m} * \mu$.
