# Integrals against the empirical law

For a sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical law of the first $n$ terms (the zero measure for $n = 0$). If singletons are measurable, then for every real function $g$, $\int g\,d\hat P_n = n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}g(x_i)$ (with $0^{-1} = 0$).
