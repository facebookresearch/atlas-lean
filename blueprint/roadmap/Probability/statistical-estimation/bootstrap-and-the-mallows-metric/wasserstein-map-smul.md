# Scaling of the Mallows metric

For every real scalar $a$, every $p > 0$ and all Borel probability measures $\mu, \nu$ on a separable real normed space,

$$d_p(aU, aV) = |a| \, d_p(U, V),$$

where $d_p(aU, aV)$ is the distance between the laws of $aU$ and $aV$ for $U \sim \mu$ and $V \sim \nu$ (the images of $\mu$ and $\nu$ under $x \mapsto a x$).

The inequality $\le$ comes from mapping each coupling of $\mu$ and $\nu$ by $(x, y) \mapsto (ax, ay)$. For $a \ne 0$ the reverse inequality is the same argument with $a^{-1}$; for $a = 0$ both sides vanish.
