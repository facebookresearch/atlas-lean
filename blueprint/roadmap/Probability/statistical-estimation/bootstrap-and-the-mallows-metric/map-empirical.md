# Image of the empirical distribution

For data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space with a zero element and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical distribution ($\hat P_0 = \delta_0$). Let $f$ be a measurable map to another such space with $f(0) = 0$, singletons measurable in both. Then the image of $\hat P_n$ under $f$ is the empirical distribution of $f(x_0), \dots, f(x_{n-1})$.
