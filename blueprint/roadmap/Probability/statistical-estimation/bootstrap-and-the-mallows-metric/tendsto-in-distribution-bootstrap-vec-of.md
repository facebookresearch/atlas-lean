# Bootstrap of a smooth vector statistic for fixed data

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space. For data $x_0, x_1, \dots \in E$ let $\bar x_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}x_i$ and, under the bootstrap world of the data, $\bar x^*_n = \frac1n\sum_{k<n}\omega_{n,k}$. Let $Z$ be a random vector in $E$ whose projections satisfy $\langle Z, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, v(t))$ for every $t$ (the covariance may be singular). Suppose that for every direction $t$ the projected data $\langle x_i, t\rangle$ have plug-in variances converging to $v(t)$ and satisfy the Lindeberg condition. Suppose also $\bar x_n \to \theta$ and $g : E \to F$ (a second-countable real normed space) is measurable and strictly differentiable at $\theta$ with derivative $L$. Then, under the bootstrap world, $\sqrt n\,(g(\bar x^*_n) - g(\bar x_n))$ converges in distribution to $LZ$.
