# Resamples that reproduce the maximum

Let $x_0, \dots, x_n$ be distinct real numbers. Among the $(n+1)^{n+1}$ maps $I : \{0, \dots, n\} \to \{0, \dots, n\}$, those for which the bootstrap sample $x_{I(0)}, \dots, x_{I(n)}$ has the same maximum as the data are exactly those that hit the index of the largest value, and there are

$$(n+1)^{n+1} - n^{n+1}$$

of them: the complement consists of the maps into the other $n$ indices.

## Sources

- P. J. Bickel and D. A. Freedman, "Some asymptotic theory for the bootstrap", *Ann. Statist.* 9 (1981) 1196–1217, Section 6, Counter-example 2.
