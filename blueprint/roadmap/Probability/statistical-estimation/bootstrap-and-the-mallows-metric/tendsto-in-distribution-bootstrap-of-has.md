# Bootstrap of a smooth function of the mean for fixed data

For real data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ write $\bar x_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}x_i$, $s^2_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}(x_i - \bar x_n)^2$, and under the bootstrap world of the data (independent draws $\omega_{n,k}$ from the empirical distribution of $x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}$) let $\bar x^*_n = \frac1n\sum_{k<n}\omega_{n,k}$ be the bootstrap mean. Suppose $s^2_n \to v \ge 0$ and the centred data satisfy the Lindeberg condition: for every $\varepsilon > 0$, $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}(x_i - \bar x_n)^2\mathbf 1\{|x_i - \bar x_n| > \varepsilon\sqrt n\} \to 0$. Suppose also $\bar x_n \to \theta$ and $g$ is measurable and strictly differentiable at $\theta$ with derivative $g'$. Then, under the bootstrap world, $\sqrt n\,(g(\bar x^*_n) - g(\bar x_n))$ converges in distribution to $\mathcal N(0, g'^2v)$.
