# The empirical distribution as an average of point masses

For data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space with a zero element and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical distribution ($\hat P_0 = \delta_0$). For $n \ne 0$, $\hat P_n = n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ as a measure.
