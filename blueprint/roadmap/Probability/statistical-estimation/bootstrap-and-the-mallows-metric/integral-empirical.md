# Integrals against the empirical distribution are sample averages

For data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space with a zero element and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical distribution ($\hat P_0 = \delta_0$). If singletons are measurable and $n \ne 0$, then $\int f\,d\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}f(x_i)$ for every real function $f$.
