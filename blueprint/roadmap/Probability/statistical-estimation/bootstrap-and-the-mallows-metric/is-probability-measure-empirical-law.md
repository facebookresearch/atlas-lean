# The empirical law is a probability measure

For a sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical law of the first $n$ terms (the zero measure for $n = 0$). For $n \ge 1$, $\hat P_n$ is a probability measure.
