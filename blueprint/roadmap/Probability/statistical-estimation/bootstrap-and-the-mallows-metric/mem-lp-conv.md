# Second moments of a convolution

Let $E$ be a separable normed abelian group with its Borel σ-algebra. If probability measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ on $E$ have finite second moments, $\int \|x\|^2 \, d\mu < \infty$ and $\int \|x\|^2 \, d\nu < \infty$, then so does $\mu * \nu$, since $\|x + y\|^2 \le 2\|x\|^2 + 2\|y\|^2$.
