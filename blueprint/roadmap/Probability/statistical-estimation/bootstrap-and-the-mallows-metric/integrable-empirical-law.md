# Every function is integrable for the empirical law

For a sequence $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical law of the first $n$ terms (the zero measure for $n = 0$). If singletons are measurable, every real function is $\hat P_n$-integrable.
