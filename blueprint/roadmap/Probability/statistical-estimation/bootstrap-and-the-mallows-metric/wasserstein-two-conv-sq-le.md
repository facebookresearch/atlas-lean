# Bickel–Freedman Lemma 8.7 for $W_2$

Under the hypotheses of [Mallows metric of sums in a Hilbert space](transport-cost-two-conv-le.md) (finite second moments, equal means for the second pair), the Wasserstein distance $W_2 = d_2$ satisfies

$$W_2(\mu_1 * \mu_2, \nu_1 * \nu_2)^2 \le W_2(\mu_1, \nu_1)^2 + W_2(\mu_2, \nu_2)^2 .$$

It restates that lemma through the relation between $W_2$ and the transport cost of order $2$, of which $W_2$ is the square root.

## Informally uses

- [Mallows metric of sums in a Hilbert space](transport-cost-two-conv-le.md): the same inequality for the transport cost.

## Sources

- P. J. Bickel and D. A. Freedman, "Some asymptotic theory for the bootstrap", *Ann. Statist.* 9 (1981) 1196–1217, Lemma 8.7.
