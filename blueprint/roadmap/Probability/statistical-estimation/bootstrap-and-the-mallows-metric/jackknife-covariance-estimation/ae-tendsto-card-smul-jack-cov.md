# Strong consistency of the scaled block jackknife covariance

Let $Z_0, Z_1, \dots$ be mutually independent, identically distributed random vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$ ($n$ finite) on a probability space with square-integrable coordinates, and let $V_B$ be the delete-one jackknife covariance of $Z_0, \dots, Z_{B-1}$. Then almost surely $B\,V_B \to \operatorname{Cov}(Z_0)$ as matrices.
