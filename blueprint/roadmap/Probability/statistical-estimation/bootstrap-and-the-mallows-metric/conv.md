# Convolution of probability measures

Let $E$ be a separable normed abelian group with its Borel σ-algebra. The convolution $\mu * \nu$ of probability measures $\mu, \nu$ on $E$ is the law of $U + V$ for independent $U \sim \mu$ and $V \sim \nu$: the image of $\mu \otimes \nu$ under $(x, y) \mapsto x + y$.
