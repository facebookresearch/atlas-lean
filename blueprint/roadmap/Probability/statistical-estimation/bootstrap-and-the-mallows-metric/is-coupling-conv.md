# Coupling of convolutions

Let $E$ be a separable normed abelian group with its Borel σ-algebra. Let $\mu_1, \nu_1, \mu_2, \nu_2$ be probability measures on $E$, $\pi_1$ a coupling of $\mu_1$ and $\nu_1$, and $\pi_2$ a coupling of $\mu_2$ and $\nu_2$. Then the law of $(X_1 + X_2, Y_1 + Y_2)$ for independent pairs $(X_1, Y_1) \sim \pi_1$ and $(X_2, Y_2) \sim \pi_2$, that is, the image of $\pi_1 \otimes \pi_2$ under $((x_1, y_1), (x_2, y_2)) \mapsto (x_1 + x_2, y_1 + y_2)$, is a coupling of $\mu_1 * \mu_2$ and $\nu_1 * \nu_2$.
