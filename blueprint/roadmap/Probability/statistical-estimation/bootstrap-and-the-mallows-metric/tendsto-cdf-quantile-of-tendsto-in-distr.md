# Weak convergence implies convergence of quantiles

Let real random variables $X_n$ (on a probability space) converge in distribution to $Y$, and suppose the distribution function $F$ of $Y$ is continuous and strictly increasing. Then for every $q \in (0, 1)$, the $q$-quantile (generalized inverse of the distribution function) of the law of $X_n$ converges to the $q$-quantile of the law of $Y$.
