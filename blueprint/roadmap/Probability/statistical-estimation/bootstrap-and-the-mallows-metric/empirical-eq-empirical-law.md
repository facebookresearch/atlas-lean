# The empirical distribution for a positive sample size

For data $x_0, x_1, \dots$ in a measurable space with a zero element and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\hat P_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ be the empirical distribution ($\hat P_0 = \delta_0$). For $n \ne 0$, $\hat P_n$ is the empirical law $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}\delta_{x_i}$ of the observations; only $n = 0$ uses the convention $\delta_0$.
