# Lindeberg condition from convergent running means

For a real sequence $x$ with sample means $\bar x_n \to \theta$ and running means of squares $\frac1n\sum_{i<n}x_i^2 \to a$, for every $\varepsilon > 0$

$$\frac1n\sum_{i<n}(x_i - \bar x_n)^2\,\mathbf 1\{|x_i - \bar x_n| > \varepsilon\sqrt n\} \to 0.$$
