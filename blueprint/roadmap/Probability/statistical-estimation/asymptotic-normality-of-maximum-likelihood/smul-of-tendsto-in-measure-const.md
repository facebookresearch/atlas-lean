# Slutsky's theorem (scalar-multiple form)

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ and $(\Omega', \mu')$ be probability spaces and consider families indexed by $n \in \iota$ along a countably generated filter (for instance $n \to \infty$). Let $E$ be a second-countable real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra. If $X_n : \Omega \to E$ converge in distribution to $Z : \Omega' \to E$ and almost everywhere measurable real $Y_n$ on $\Omega$ converge in probability to a constant $c$, then $Y_n X_n$ converges in distribution to $cZ$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Lemma 2.8 (Slutsky).
