# Second-order Taylor bound

Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $f$ and $f'$ differentiable on the ball $B(\theta_0, \delta)$ and $|f''| \le m$ there. Then for every $\theta \in B(\theta_0, \delta)$

$$\big|f(\theta) - f(\theta_0) - (\theta - \theta_0)f'(\theta_0)\big| \le m(\theta - \theta_0)^2.$$
