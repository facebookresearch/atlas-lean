# Convergence in probability of reciprocals

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$ and $Y_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ converge in $\mu$-measure to a constant $c \ne 0$ (along any filter). Then $Y_n^{-1}$ converges in $\mu$-measure to $c^{-1}$ (with $0^{-1} = 0$).

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Theorem 2.3 (continuous mapping theorem).
