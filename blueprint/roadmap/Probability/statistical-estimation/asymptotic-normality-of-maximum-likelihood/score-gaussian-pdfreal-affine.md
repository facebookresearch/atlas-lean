# Score of the affine Gaussian location family

Let $c, m \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$. For the densities $p_t = N(ct + m, v)$ with respect to Lebesgue measure ($c = 0$ allowed), the [score](score.md) is

$$s_\theta(x) = \partial_\theta \log p_\theta(x) = \frac{c\,(x - (c\theta + m))}{v} \quad \text{for all } \theta, x.$$
