# Product of a distributionally convergent and a vanishing sequence

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ and $(\Omega', \mu')$ be probability spaces and consider families indexed by $n \in \iota$ along a countably generated filter (for instance $n \to \infty$). If real random variables $A_n$ on $\Omega$ converge in distribution to some $Z$ on $\Omega'$, and almost everywhere measurable $\eta_n$ on $\Omega$ converge in probability to $0$, then $A_n \eta_n$ converges in probability to $0$ (a consequence of Slutsky's theorem).
