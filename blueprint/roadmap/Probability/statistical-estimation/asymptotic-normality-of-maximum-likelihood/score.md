# Score function

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a measurable space and $p : \mathbb{R} \times \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ a one-parameter family of densities. The score is

$$s_\theta(x) = \partial_\theta \log p_\theta(x),$$

the derivative of $t \mapsto \log p_t(x)$ at $\theta$, taken to be $0$ where this map is not differentiable (and with $\log y = \log|y|$, $\log 0 = 0$).
