# Continuous mapping for convergence in probability to a constant

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$, $E, F$ pseudometric spaces, $Y_n : \Omega \to E$ a family converging in $\mu$-measure to a constant $c$ (along any filter), and $f : E \to F$ continuous at $c$. Then $f(Y_n)$ converges in $\mu$-measure to $f(c)$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Theorem 2.3 (continuous mapping theorem).
