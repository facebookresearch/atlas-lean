# Convergence in distribution depends on the limit only through its law

Convergence in distribution depends on the limit only through its law. Let $E$ be a topological space whose Borel sets are measurable, let $X_i$ be $E$-valued random variables on probability spaces converging in distribution along a filter to $Y$, defined on a probability space, and let $Z$, on any probability space, have the same law $\rho$ as $Y$. Then $X_i$ converges in distribution to $Z$.
