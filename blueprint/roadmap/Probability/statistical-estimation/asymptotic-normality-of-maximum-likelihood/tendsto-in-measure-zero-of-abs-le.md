# Convergence to zero in probability by domination on likely events

Let $P$ be a measure on $\Omega$ and $W_n, V_n : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $G_n \subseteq \Omega$, indexed along a filter. If $|W_n(\omega)| \le V_n(\omega)$ for $\omega \in G_n$, $P(G_n^c) \to 0$ and $V_n \to 0$ in $P$-measure, then $W_n \to 0$ in $P$-measure.
