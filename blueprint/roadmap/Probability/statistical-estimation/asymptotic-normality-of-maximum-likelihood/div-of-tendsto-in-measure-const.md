# Slutsky's theorem (ratio form)

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ and $(\Omega', \mu')$ be probability spaces and consider families indexed by $n \in \iota$ along a countably generated filter (for instance $n \to \infty$). If real random variables $X_n$ on $\Omega$ converge in distribution to $Z$ on $\Omega'$ and almost everywhere measurable $Y_n$ on $\Omega$ converge in probability to a constant $c \ne 0$, then $X_n / Y_n$ converges in distribution to $Z/c$.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Lemma 2.8 (Slutsky).
