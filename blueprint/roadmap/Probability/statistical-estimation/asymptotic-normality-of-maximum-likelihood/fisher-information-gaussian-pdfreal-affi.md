# Fisher information of the affine Gaussian location family

Let $c, m \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$. For the densities $p_t = N(ct + m, v)$ with respect to Lebesgue measure (known variance $v$, arbitrary real slope $c$ and offset $m$), the Fisher information of one observation is

$$I(\theta) = \frac{c^2}{v} \quad \text{for every } \theta;$$

it vanishes when $c = 0$. It is the second moment of the score $c(X - (c\theta + m))/v$ under $X \sim N(c\theta + m, v)$ ([Fisher information is the second moment of the score](integral-score-sq.md)).
