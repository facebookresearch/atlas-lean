# Relative error of a person in a crossed design

In a crossed person-by-item design with scores $X_{pi}$ over $m$ items, grand mean $\mu$, person effects $P_p$ and item effects $I_i$, the relative error of person $p$ is the random variable $\delta_p = \frac1m\sum_iX_{pi} - \big(\mu + P_p + \frac1m\sum_iI_i\big)$.
