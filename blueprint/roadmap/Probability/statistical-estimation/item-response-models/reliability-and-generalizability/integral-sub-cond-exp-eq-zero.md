# The true-score error has mean zero

Let $X$ be an integrable real random variable on a probability space $(\Omega, \mathcal F, \mu)$ and $\mathcal G \subseteq \mathcal F$ a sub-$\sigma$-algebra; write $T = \mathbb{E}[X \mid \mathcal G]$ for the true score and $X - T$ for the error. Then $\mathbb{E}[X - T] = 0$.
