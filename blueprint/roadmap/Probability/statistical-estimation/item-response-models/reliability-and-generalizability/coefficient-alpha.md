# Cronbach's coefficient alpha

For items $X_1, \dots, X_k$ on a measure space, coefficient alpha is

$$\alpha = \frac{k}{k - 1}\Big(1 - \frac{\sum_i\operatorname{Var}(X_i)}{\operatorname{Var}(\sum_iX_i)}\Big)$$

when $k \ge 2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_iX_i) > 0$, and $0$ otherwise. It is the population coefficient for a probability measure and square-integrable items.

## Sources

- Cronbach (1951), as cited in Martinková and Hladká, Chapter 4, equation 4.13.
