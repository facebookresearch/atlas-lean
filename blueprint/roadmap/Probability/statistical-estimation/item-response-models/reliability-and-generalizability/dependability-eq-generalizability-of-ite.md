# Dependability equals generalizability without item variance

For variance components $\sigma_P^2, \sigma_I^2, \sigma_e^2$ and $m \ge 1$ items, write $E\rho^2 = \sigma_P^2/(\sigma_P^2 + \sigma_e^2/m)$ for the generalizability coefficient and $\Phi = \sigma_P^2/(\sigma_P^2 + (\sigma_I^2 + \sigma_e^2)/m)$ for the dependability coefficient (both $0$ at a zero denominator). If $\sigma_I^2 = 0$, then $\Phi = E\rho^2$.
