# Admissible ratio-scale recodings

An affine recoding $x \mapsto ax + b$ of the real line is strictly increasing and preserves ratios $\frac{ax + b}{ay + b} = \frac xy$ (for all $x$ and all $y \ne 0$ with $ay + b \ne 0$) if and only if $a > 0$ and $b = 0$.
