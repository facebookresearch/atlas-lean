# Nonnegativity of the two-parameter logistic test information

Let $I$ be a finite set of items, let $a, b \in \mathbb{R}^I$ be discriminations and difficulties, let $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $\sigma(u) = 1/(1 + e^{-u})$ be the logistic function. Then

$$
0 \le \sum_{j \in I} a_j^2\, \sigma\big(a_j (\theta - b_j)\big)\,\Big(1 - \sigma\big(a_j (\theta - b_j)\big)\Big).
$$
