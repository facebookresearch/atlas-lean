# Completeness of the binomial count

Let $n \ge 1$ and $0 < a < b < 1$, and consider the family of binomial laws $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ on $\mathbb{N}$, $p \in (a,b)$. The identity statistic, the observed count, is complete for this family: every measurable $u : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ that is integrable with $\mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)}[u] = 0$ for all $p \in (a,b)$ vanishes $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$-almost everywhere for every $p \in (a,b)$.
