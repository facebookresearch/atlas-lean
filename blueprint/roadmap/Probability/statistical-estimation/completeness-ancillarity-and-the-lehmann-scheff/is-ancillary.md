# Ancillary statistic

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $\mathcal{T}$ be measurable spaces and $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ a family of probability measures on $\Omega$. A statistic $V : \Omega \to \mathcal{T}$ is ancillary for $(P_\theta)$ if $V$ is measurable and its law does not depend on the parameter:

$$P_{\theta_1} \circ V^{-1} = P_{\theta_2} \circ V^{-1} \quad \text{for all } \theta_1, \theta_2 \in \Theta.$$

No particular distribution is asserted for $V$, so an ancillary statistic need not be pivotal.

## Sources

- G. Casella and R. L. Berger, *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed., Definition 6.2.24.
