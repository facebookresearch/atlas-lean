# A function vanishing on the binomial support is almost surely zero

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0,1]$ and $u : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $u(k) = 0$ for every $k \le n$. Then $u = 0$ almost everywhere with respect to the binomial law $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ on $\mathbb{N}$.
