# The integrated discrepancy is continuous

For every finite family of counts `k₁, …, kₙ` with
[sample mean](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/count-mean.md) `M`, Levin and
Reeds's [integrated discrepancy](smoothed-discrepancy.md)
`H(u) = ∑ᵢ R_{kᵢ}(u) - n (u² / 2 - (max (u - M) 0)² / 2)` is continuous in `u ∈ ℝ`.
