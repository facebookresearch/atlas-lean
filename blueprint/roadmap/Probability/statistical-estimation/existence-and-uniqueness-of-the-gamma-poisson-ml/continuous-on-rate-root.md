# Continuity of the rate root

For positive size factors `s₁, …, sₙ` (`n ≥ 1`) and counts `k₁, …, kₙ` at least one of which is
positive, the [rate root](rate-root.md) `a ↦ b̂(a)` is continuous on `(0, ∞)`. At each `a > 0`,
`b̂(a)` is the root of the
[rate score times the rate](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/rate-score-log.md),
`b ↦ ∑ᵢ (a - (a + kᵢ) b / (b + sᵢ))`, which is continuous in `a` and strictly decreasing in `b > 0`;
an implicit-root argument gives continuity.
