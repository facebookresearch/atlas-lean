# With a positive count the rate root strictly increases with the shape

For positive size factors `s₁, …, sₙ` (`n ≥ 1`) and counts `k₁, …, kₙ` at least one of which is
positive, the [rate root](rate-root.md) is strictly increasing on `(0, ∞)`: `b̂(a) < b̂(a')` for
`0 < a < a'`. With all counts `0` it is `0` at every `a > 0`.
