# The integrated discrepancy below the sample mean

For a finite family of counts `k₁, …, kₙ` with
[sample mean](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/count-mean.md) `M` and every real
`u ≤ M`, Levin and Reeds's [integrated discrepancy](smoothed-discrepancy.md) is
`H(u) = ∑ᵢ R_{kᵢ}(u) - n u² / 2`, where `R_k` is the [ramp sum](ramp-sum.md). For `0 ≤ u ≤ M` this
reflects `F(u) = n u`: the uniform part `F` has not yet reached its total mass `n M`.
