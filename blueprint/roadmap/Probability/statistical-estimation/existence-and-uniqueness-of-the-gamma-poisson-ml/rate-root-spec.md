# With a positive count the rate root solves the rate score equation

For `a > 0`, positive size factors `s₁, …, sₙ` (`n ≥ 1`) and counts `k₁, …, kₙ` at least one of
which is positive, the [rate root](rate-root.md) is positive, `b̂(a) > 0`, and solves the
[rate score](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/rate-score.md) equation: with
`b = b̂(a)`, `∑ᵢ (a / b - (a + kᵢ) / (b + sᵢ)) = 0`. With all counts `0` there is no positive root,
and `b̂(a) = 0`.
