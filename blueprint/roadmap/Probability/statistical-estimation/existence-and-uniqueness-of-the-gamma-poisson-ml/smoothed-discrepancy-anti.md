# The integrated discrepancy is antitone beyond the sample mean

For a nonempty finite family of counts with
[sample mean](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/count-mean.md) `M` and reals
`M ≤ x ≤ y`, Levin and Reeds's [integrated discrepancy](smoothed-discrepancy.md) satisfies
`H(y) ≤ H(x)`. Beyond `M` the uniform part `F` has reached its total mass `n M = ∑ᵢ kᵢ`, and each
[ramp sum](ramp-sum.md) `R_{kᵢ}` grows with slope at most `kᵢ`.
