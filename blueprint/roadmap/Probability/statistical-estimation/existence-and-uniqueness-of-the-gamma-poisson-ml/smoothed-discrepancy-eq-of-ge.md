# Beyond every count the integrated discrepancy is n (M − V) / 2

For a nonempty finite family of counts `k₁, …, kₙ` with
[sample mean](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/count-mean.md) `M` and
[biased sample variance](../../../Probability/statistical-estimation/likelihood-equations-and-efficiency-of-the-gamma/count-var.md) `V`, and
every real `T` with `T ≥ M` and `T ≥ kᵢ` for every `i`, Levin and Reeds's
[integrated discrepancy](smoothed-discrepancy.md) is `H(T) = n (M - V) / 2`. This terminal value is
negative exactly when the sample is overdispersed, `V > M`.
