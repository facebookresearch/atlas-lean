# Mean parameter of a finite exponential family

Let $X$ be a nonempty finite set, $E$ a real inner product space and $\phi : X \to E$ a sufficient statistic. The mean parameter of $p_\theta$ is

$$
\mu(\theta) = \mathbb E_\theta[\phi(X)] = \sum_{x \in X} p_\theta(x)\,\phi(x) \in E .
$$

- M. J. Wainwright and M. I. Jordan, *Graphical models, exponential families, and variational inference*, Found. Trends Mach. Learn. 1(1–2) (2008) 1–305, Section 3.4 (mean parameters) and (3.46).
