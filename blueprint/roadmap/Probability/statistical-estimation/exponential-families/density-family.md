# Exponential family of densities on a finite set

Let $X$ be a finite set with a base weight $h \ge 0$ of positive total mass and a sufficient statistic $T : X \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$. The exponential family with densities $h(x) \exp(\eta \cdot T(x) - A(\eta))$, indexed by natural parameters $\eta \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, is a normalized family of densities with respect to counting measure on $X$, so that iid likelihoods and maximum likelihood estimators apply to it.
