# The density is a probability distribution

Let $X$ be a finite set, $E$ a real inner product space and $\phi : X \to E$ a sufficient statistic. If $X$ is nonempty then for every $\theta \in E$, $p_\theta(x) > 0$ for all $x$ and $\sum_x p_\theta(x) = 1$.
