# Partition function of a finite exponential family

Let $X$ be a finite set, $E$ a real inner product space and $\phi : X \to E$ a sufficient statistic. The partition function is

$$
Z(\theta) = \sum_{x \in X} \exp\langle\phi(x), \theta\rangle, \qquad \theta \in E .
$$
