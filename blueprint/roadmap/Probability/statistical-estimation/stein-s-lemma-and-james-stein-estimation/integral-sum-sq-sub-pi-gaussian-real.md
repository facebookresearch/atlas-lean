# Quadratic risk of the usual Gaussian mean estimator

For $X \sim \mathcal N_p(\theta, I)$, the estimator $X$ of $\theta$ has quadratic risk $\mathbb E \sum_i (X_i - \theta_i)^2 = p$ for every $\theta$: Efron (2021), equation (3.3), with $N = p$ and $\sigma_0^2 = 1$.
