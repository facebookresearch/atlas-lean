# Positive definiteness of weighted Gram matrices

Let $m, k$ be finite index sets, $B$ a real $m \times k$ matrix and $w \in \mathbb{R}^k$ with $w_e \ge 0$ for all $e$. Then $B \operatorname{diag}(w) B^\top$ is positive definite if and only if every nonzero $v \in \mathbb{R}^m$ has $(v^\top B)_e \ne 0$ for some column $e$ with $w_e > 0$, that is, the columns of $B$ with positive weight have full row rank. (The matrix is always positive semidefinite, as $C D C^\top$ with $D = \operatorname{diag}(w)$ positive semidefinite.)
