# The cone $\mathcal{C}(S; \alpha)$

For a finite index set, a subset $S$ and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, the cone

$$\mathcal{C}(S; \alpha) = \{\Delta : \|\Delta_{S^c}\|_1 \le \alpha \|\Delta_S\|_1\}$$

collects the vectors whose $\ell_1$ mass off $S$ is at most $\alpha$ times their mass on $S$.
