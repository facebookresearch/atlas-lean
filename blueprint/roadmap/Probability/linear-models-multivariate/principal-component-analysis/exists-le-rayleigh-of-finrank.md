# Courant–Fischer min–max, matrix form

Let $S$ be a real symmetric $p \times p$ matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{p-1}$ in decreasing order and a corresponding [ordered orthonormal eigenbasis](ordered-eigenbasis.md) $v_0, \dots, v_{p-1}$ of $\mathbb{R}^p$. Let $k \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$. Every subspace $W \subseteq \mathbb{R}^p$ of dimension $p - k$ contains a nonzero vector $w$ with $R_S(w) \ge \lambda_k$, where $R_S$ is the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh.md).
