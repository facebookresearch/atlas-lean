# Rayleigh quotient of an eigenvector is its eigenvalue

Let $S$ be a real symmetric $p \times p$ matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{p-1}$ in decreasing order and a corresponding [ordered orthonormal eigenbasis](ordered-eigenbasis.md) $v_0, \dots, v_{p-1}$ of $\mathbb{R}^p$. Then the Rayleigh quotient $R_S(w) = w^\top S w / w^\top w$ satisfies $R_S(v_k) = \lambda_k$ for every $k$.
