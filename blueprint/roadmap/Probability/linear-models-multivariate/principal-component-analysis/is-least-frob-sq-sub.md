# Eckart–Young, the tail is the minimum squared Frobenius error

For a real $m \times n$ matrix $A$ and $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the least value of $\|A - G\|_F^2$ over real $m \times n$ matrices $G$ of rank at most $k$ is $\sum_{j \ge k} \sigma_j(A)^2$, where $\sigma_0(A) \ge \sigma_1(A) \ge \cdots$ are the singular values of $A$.
