# Lower bound on the Rayleigh quotient for orthogonal directions

Let $S$ be a real symmetric $p \times p$ matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{p-1}$ in decreasing order and a corresponding [ordered orthonormal eigenbasis](ordered-eigenbasis.md) $v_0, \dots, v_{p-1}$ of $\mathbb{R}^p$. Let $k \in \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ and $w \ne 0$ orthogonal to $v_{k+1}, \dots, v_{p-1}$. Then the Rayleigh quotient $R_S(w) = w^\top S w / w^\top w$ satisfies $R_S(w) \ge \lambda_k$. This is the dual of [Hotelling's characterization](rayleigh-le-of-orthogonal.md).
