# Eckart–Young (Frobenius norm)

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix with singular values $\sigma_0 \ge \sigma_1 \ge \dots$ (with $\sigma_i = 0$ for $i \ge \min(m, n)$), and $\|\cdot\|$ the Frobenius norm. For every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and every real $m \times n$ matrix $B$ of rank at most $k$,

$$\sum_{k \le i < n} \sigma_i^2 \le \|A - B\|^2.$$

## Sources

- C. Eckart and G. Young, *The approximation of one matrix by another of lower rank*, Psychometrika 1 (1936), 211–218.
