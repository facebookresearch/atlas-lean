# Quadratic form expanded in the ordered eigenbasis

Let $S$ be a real symmetric $p \times p$ matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{p-1}$ in decreasing order and a corresponding [ordered orthonormal eigenbasis](ordered-eigenbasis.md) $v_0, \dots, v_{p-1}$ of $\mathbb{R}^p$. Then for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^p$,

$$x^\top S x = \sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \lambda_i\, \langle v_i, x\rangle^2.$$
