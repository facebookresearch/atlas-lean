# Right singular vectors of a matrix

Let $A$ be a real $m \times n$ matrix. Its right singular vectors form an orthonormal eigenbasis $v_0, \dots, v_{n-1}$ of $A^\top A$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$, ordered so that $A^\top A v_i = \sigma_i^2 v_i$ with $\sigma_0 \ge \sigma_1 \ge \dots$ the singular values of $A$.
