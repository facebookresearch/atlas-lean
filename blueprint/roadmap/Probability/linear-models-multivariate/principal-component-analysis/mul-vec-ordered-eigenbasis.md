# Ordered eigenbasis vectors are eigenvectors

Let $S$ be a real symmetric $p \times p$ matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_0 \ge \lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_{p-1}$ in decreasing order and a corresponding [ordered orthonormal eigenbasis](ordered-eigenbasis.md) $v_0, \dots, v_{p-1}$ of $\mathbb{R}^p$. Then $S v_k = \lambda_k v_k$ for every $k$.
