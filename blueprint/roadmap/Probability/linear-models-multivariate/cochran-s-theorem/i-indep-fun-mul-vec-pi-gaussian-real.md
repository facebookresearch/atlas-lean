# Mutual independence of orthogonal linear images of a Gaussian vector

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $Z$ a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and $(A_k)_{k \in \kappa}$ a finite family of real matrices, $A_k$ of size $m_k \times \iota$ with $m_k$ finite, such that $A_k A_l^\top = 0$ for $k \ne l$. Then the linear images $A_k Z$ are mutually independent.
