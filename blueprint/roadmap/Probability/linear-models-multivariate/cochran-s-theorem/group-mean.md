# Group mean of observations

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $\mathrm{grp} : \iota \to G$ a labelling of the observations by groups, $x \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $g \in G$. The group mean is

$$\bar x_g = \frac{\sum_{i :\ \mathrm{grp}(i) = g} x_i}{\#\{i : \mathrm{grp}(i) = g\}},$$

which is $0$ for an empty group.
