# Independence of the Gaussian sample mean and sum of squared deviations

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \ge 0$ and $X_1, \dots, X_n$ i.i.d. $N(\mu, v)$. Then the sample mean $\bar X = \frac1n \sum_i X_i$ is independent of the sum of squared deviations $\sum_i (X_i - \bar X)^2 = (n - 1) S^2$.
