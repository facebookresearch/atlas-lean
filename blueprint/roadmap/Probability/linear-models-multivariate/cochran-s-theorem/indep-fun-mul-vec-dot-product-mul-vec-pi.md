# Independence of a Gaussian linear form and a projection quadratic form

Let $\iota$ and $m$ be finite index sets, $Z$ a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, $M$ a symmetric idempotent real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix and $A$ a real $m \times \iota$ matrix with $AM = 0$. Then the linear form $AZ$ is independent of the quadratic form $Z^\top M Z$.
