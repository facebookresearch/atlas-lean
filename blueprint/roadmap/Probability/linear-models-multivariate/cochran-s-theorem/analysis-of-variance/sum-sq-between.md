# Between-group sum of squares

For observations $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ indexed by a finite set and a group assignment $g : \iota \to G$, with group means $\bar x_h$ (the average of $x_i$ over $g(i) = h$) and grand mean $\bar x$, the between-group sum of squares is $\mathrm{SSB} = \sum_{i}(\bar x_{g(i)} - \bar x)^2$. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
