# Independence of squared lengths of orthogonal Gaussian projections

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space with the standard Gaussian law, and $(K_k)_{k \in \kappa}$ a finite family of pairwise orthogonal subspaces. Then the squared lengths $\|P_{K_k} Z\|^2$ of the orthogonal projections of $Z$ onto the $K_k$ are mutually independent.
