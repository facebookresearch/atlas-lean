# Gaussian one-way layout

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $\mathrm{grp} : \iota \to G$ a labelling by a finite set of groups, and $x_i$, $i \in \iota$, i.i.d. $N(\mu, v)$ observations ($\mu \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \ge 0$). Then the vector of [group means](group-mean.md) $(\bar x_g)_{g \in G}$ is independent of the vector of within-group residuals $(x_i - \bar x_{\mathrm{grp}(i)})_{i \in \iota}$.
