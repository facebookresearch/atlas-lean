# Independence of the Gaussian sample mean and residuals

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \ge 0$ and $X_1, \dots, X_n$ i.i.d. $N(\mu, v)$. Then the sample mean $\bar X = \frac1n \sum_i X_i$ (which is $0$ for $n = 0$) is independent of the residual vector $(X_i - \bar X)_{i \le n}$.
