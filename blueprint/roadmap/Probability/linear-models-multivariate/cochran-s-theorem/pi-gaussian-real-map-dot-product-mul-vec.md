# Cochran's theorem, matrix form

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $Z$ a vector of independent standard normal coordinates in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $A$ a symmetric idempotent real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix of positive rank. Then the quadratic form $Z^\top A Z$ has the chi-square law with $\operatorname{rank} A$ degrees of freedom.
