# Covariance of linear images of a standard Gaussian vector

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $m$ and $n$ arbitrary index sets, $Z$ a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ (independent $N(0,1)$ coordinates), $A$ an $m \times \iota$ and $B$ an $n \times \iota$ real matrix. For $a \in m$ and $b \in n$,

$$\operatorname{Cov}\big((AZ)_a, (BZ)_b\big) = (AB^\top)_{ab}.$$

## Sources

- C. R. Rao, *Linear Statistical Inference and its Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1973, ch. 3b (normal distribution: linear and quadratic forms).
