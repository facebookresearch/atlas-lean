# Cochran's theorem, independence part

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $Z$ a vector of independent standard normal coordinates in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and $(A_k)_{k \in \kappa}$ a finite family of symmetric real $\iota \times \iota$ matrices with $\sum_k A_k = I$ and $\sum_k \operatorname{rank} A_k = |\iota|$. Then the quadratic forms $Z^\top A_k Z$, $k \in \kappa$, are mutually independent.
