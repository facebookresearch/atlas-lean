# Independence of orthogonal linear images of a standard Gaussian vector

Let $\iota, m, n$ be finite index sets, $Z$ a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, and $A$ ($m \times \iota$), $B$ ($n \times \iota$) real matrices with $AB^\top = 0$. Then the linear images $AZ$ and $BZ$ are independent.
