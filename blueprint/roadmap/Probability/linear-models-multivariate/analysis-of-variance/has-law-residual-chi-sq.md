# Chi-square law of the residual in nested models

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space, $P_0 \le P_1$ subspaces, and $\Pi_V$ the orthogonal projection onto a subspace $V$. Let $Z$ be a standard Gaussian vector in $E$ on a probability space (with the Borel structure), $\sigma > 0$, $m \in P_0$ and $Y = m + \sigma Z$. If $\dim E - \dim P_1 > 0$, then $\|\Pi_{P_1^\perp}Y\|^2/\sigma^2$ has the central chi-square law with $\dim E - \dim P_1$ degrees of freedom.
