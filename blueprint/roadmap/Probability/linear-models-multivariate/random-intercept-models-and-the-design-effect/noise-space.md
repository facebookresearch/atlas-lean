# Sample space of the Gaussian realization

For a set $\delta$ of donors and a set $\kappa$ of cell slots, the noise space is
$\mathbb{R}^{\delta \sqcup (\delta \times \kappa)}$, the real functions on the disjoint union of
$\delta$ and $\delta \times \kappa$: one coordinate $Z_d$ per donor effect and one coordinate $W_i$
per cell residual.
