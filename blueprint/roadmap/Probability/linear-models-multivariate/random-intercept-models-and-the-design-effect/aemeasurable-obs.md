# The observation vector is almost everywhere measurable

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\Omega$ and $U$ a noise process $U_i : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, one for
each cell $i = (d, c)$, such that the noise vector $\omega \mapsto (U_i(\omega))_i$ is $\mu$-almost
everywhere measurable into $\mathbb{R}^{\delta \times \kappa}$. Then for all real $\mu_0$, $\tau$,
$\beta$ the vector $\omega \mapsto (Y_i(\omega))_i$ of [observed cell values](obs.md) is
$\mu$-almost everywhere measurable.
