# Law of the cell-level data

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$ and $U$ a noise process
$U_i : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, one for each cell $i = (d, c)$, such that the noise vector
$\omega \mapsto (U_i(\omega))_i$ is $\mu$-almost everywhere measurable as a map into
$\mathbb{R}^{\delta \times \kappa}$ with its product $\sigma$-algebra. For real $\mu_0$, $\tau$,
$\beta$, the data law is the image of $\mu$ under the vector $\omega \mapsto (Y_i(\omega))_i$ of
[observed cell values](obs.md). That vector is then
[almost everywhere measurable](aemeasurable-obs.md), so the data law is a probability measure on
$\mathbb{R}^{\delta \times \kappa}$.
