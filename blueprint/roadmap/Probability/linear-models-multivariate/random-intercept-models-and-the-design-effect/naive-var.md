# Naive independent-cell variance of the mean

For real $\sigma_b^2$, $\sigma_e^2$ and natural numbers $D$ and $m$, put

$$V_{\mathrm{naive}}(D, m) = \frac{\sigma_b^2 + \sigma_e^2}{D m},$$

which is $0$ when $D m = 0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$. It is the variance of the mean of $D m$
uncorrelated cells of variance $\sigma_b^2 + \sigma_e^2$: the value obtained by treating the cells
as independent replicates.
