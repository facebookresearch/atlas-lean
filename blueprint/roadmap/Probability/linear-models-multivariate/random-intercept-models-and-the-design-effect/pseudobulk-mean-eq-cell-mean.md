# The mean of the pseudobulk values is the cell-level mean

Let $\delta$ be a finite set of donors and $\kappa$ a finite nonempty set of cell slots. For all
cell values $Y_i : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, the [mean of the pseudobulk values](pseudobulk-mean.md)
equals the [cell-level mean](cell-mean.md) at every $\omega$: $\bar Y_{\mathrm{pb}} = \bar Y$.
