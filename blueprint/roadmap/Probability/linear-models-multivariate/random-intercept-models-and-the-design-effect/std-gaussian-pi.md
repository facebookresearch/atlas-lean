# Independent standard Gaussian coordinates

For finite sets $\delta$ of donors and $\kappa$ of cell slots, this is the product probability
measure on the [noise space](noise-space.md) $\mathbb{R}^{\delta \sqcup (\delta \times \kappa)}$
under which the coordinates $Z_d$ and $W_i$ are independent standard Gaussian random variables, each
of law $N(0, 1)$.
