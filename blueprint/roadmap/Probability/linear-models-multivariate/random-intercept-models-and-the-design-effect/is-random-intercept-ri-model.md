# The Gaussian model is a random-intercept model

Let $\delta$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets of donors and cell slots and let $\sigma_b^2 \ge 0$ and
$\sigma_e^2 \ge 0$. Under the [independent standard Gaussian coordinates](std-gaussian-pi.md), the
[explicit model](ri-model.md) $Y_{(d, c)} = \sqrt{\sigma_b^2} Z_d + \sqrt{\sigma_e^2} W_{(d, c)}$ is
a [random-intercept model](is-random-intercept.md) with donor variance $\sigma_b^2$ and residual
variance $\sigma_e^2$.
