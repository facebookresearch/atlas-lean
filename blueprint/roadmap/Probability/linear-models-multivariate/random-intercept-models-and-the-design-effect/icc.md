# Intraclass correlation

For real $\sigma_b^2$ and $\sigma_e^2$, the intraclass correlation is

$$\rho = \frac{\sigma_b^2}{\sigma_b^2 + \sigma_e^2},$$

which is $0$ when $\sigma_b^2 + \sigma_e^2 = 0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$. For
$\sigma_b^2, \sigma_e^2 \ge 0$ with a positive sum, it lies in $[0, 1]$ and is the correlation of
two distinct cells of one donor in a [random-intercept model](is-random-intercept.md) under a
probability measure.
