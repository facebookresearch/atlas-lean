# Crossed covariance is positive semidefinite for nonnegative components

For index types `I` and `J` and real numbers `σ_s² ≥ 0` and `σ_w² ≥ 0`, the
[crossed random-effects covariance](crossed-cov.md), the diagonal matrix on `I ⊔ J` with entries
`σ_s²` on `I` and `σ_w²` on `J`, is positive semidefinite. The Gaussian results of the crossed model
use this property of the random-effects covariance.
