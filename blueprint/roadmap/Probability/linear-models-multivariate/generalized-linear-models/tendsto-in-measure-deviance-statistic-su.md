# Asymptotic equivalence of the deviance and Pearson’s statistic

Let $p_n$ be (possibly random) cell probabilities and $N_n$ nonnegative cell counts on a probability space. If Pearson's statistic $X^2_n$ converges in distribution and for some $m > 0$ the probability that some $p_{n,i} < m$ tends to $0$, then $D_n - X^2_n \to 0$ in probability, where $D_n$ is the deviance.
