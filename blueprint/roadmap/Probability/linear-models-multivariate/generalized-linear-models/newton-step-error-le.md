# One Newton step squares the error

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $U : E \to E$ with $U(b) = 0$, having derivative $H(x)$ on $B(b, r)$ with $\|H(x) - H(y)\| \le L\|x - y\|$ there, and let $H(b)$ have a left inverse of norm at most $M$. If $z \in B(b, r)$, $2ML\|z - b\| \le 1$ and $H(z)(z' - z) = -U(z)$, then $\|z' - b\| \le ML\|z - b\|^2$.
