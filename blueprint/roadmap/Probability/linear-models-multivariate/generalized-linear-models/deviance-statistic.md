# Deviance of cell counts

For $n$ observations, cell probabilities $p$ and cell counts $N$ over finitely many cells, the deviance is $D_n = \sum_i2\big(N_i\log(N_i/(np_i)) - (N_i - np_i)\big)$, the sum of unit Poisson deviances against the fitted means $np_i$; it is the likelihood-ratio statistic $G^2$ when $\sum N_i = n$ and $\sum p_i = 1$.
