# The cumulant is strictly convex with positive variance

Let $(\Theta, b, \mu, V)$ be a canonical cumulant specification: an open convex natural-parameter domain $\Theta \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, a cumulant $b$ with $b' = \mu$ and $\mu' = V \ge 0$ on $\Theta$. If $V > 0$ on $\Theta$, then $b$ is strictly convex on $\Theta$.
