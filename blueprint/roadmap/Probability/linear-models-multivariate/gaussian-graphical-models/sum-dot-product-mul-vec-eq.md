# Sum of sample quadratic forms as a trace

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $m \ge 1$, $x_1, \dots, x_m \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $K$ a real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. Then

$$\sum_{s=1}^m x_s^\top K x_s = m \operatorname{tr}(S K),$$

where $S$ is the [sample second-moment matrix](sample-cov.md).
