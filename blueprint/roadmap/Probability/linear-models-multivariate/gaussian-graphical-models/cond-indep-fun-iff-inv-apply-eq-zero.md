# Pairwise Markov property of Gaussian vectors

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a probability space on a standard Borel space, $\kappa$ a finite index set, and $V = (X_0, X_1, Y) : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^{\{0,1\} \sqcup \kappa}$ a measurable random vector with law $N(\mu, S)$, $S$ positive definite. Then $X_0$ and $X_1$ are conditionally independent given $Y$ if and only if

$$(S^{-1})_{01} = 0.$$
