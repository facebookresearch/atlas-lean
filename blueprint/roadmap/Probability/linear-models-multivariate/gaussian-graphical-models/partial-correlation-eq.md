# Partial correlation from the precision matrix

Let $\kappa$ be a finite index set and $S$ a positive definite real matrix on $\{0, 1\} \sqcup \kappa$ with precision $\Omega = S^{-1}$. Then the [partial correlation](partial-correlation.md) of coordinates $0$ and $1$ given the $\kappa$-block is

$$\rho_{01 \cdot \kappa} = -\frac{\Omega_{01}}{\sqrt{\Omega_{00}\,\Omega_{11}}}.$$
