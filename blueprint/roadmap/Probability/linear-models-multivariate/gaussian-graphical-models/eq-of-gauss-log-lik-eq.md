# Uniqueness of Dempster's MLE

Under the hypotheses of [Dempster's covariance-selection MLE](gauss-log-lik-le-of-inv-eq-on-graph.md), every positive definite $K$ supported on $G$ with $\log \det K - \operatorname{tr}(SK) = \log \det \hat K - \operatorname{tr}(S \hat K)$ equals $\hat K$.

## Sources

- A. P. Dempster, *Covariance selection*, Biometrics 28 (1972), 157–175.
- S. L. Lauritzen, *Graphical Models*, Oxford University Press, 1996, Chapter 5.
