# Partial correlation from the conditional covariance

Let $\kappa$ be a finite index set and $S$ a real matrix on $\{0, 1\} \sqcup \kappa$. With the conditional covariance $C = S_{11} - S_{12} S_{22}^{-1} S_{21}$ of the coordinates $0, 1$ given the $\kappa$-block, the partial correlation of coordinates $0$ and $1$ is

$$\rho_{01 \cdot \kappa} = \frac{C_{01}}{\sqrt{C_{00} C_{11}}},$$

with the conventions that a singular inverse is $0$, $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x < 0$, and division by $0$ gives $0$.
