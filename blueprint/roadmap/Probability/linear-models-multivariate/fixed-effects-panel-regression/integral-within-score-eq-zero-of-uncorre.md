# Strict exogeneity gives the within estimating equation

Let $X_0$ be a random real $T \times k$ matrix and $u_0$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^T$ on a measure space such that every product $(X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s$ is integrable with $\mathbb{E}[(X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s] = 0$. Then $\mathbb{E}[(QX_0)^\top Qu_0] = 0$, where $Q = I - T^{-1}J$ is the time-demeaning matrix.
