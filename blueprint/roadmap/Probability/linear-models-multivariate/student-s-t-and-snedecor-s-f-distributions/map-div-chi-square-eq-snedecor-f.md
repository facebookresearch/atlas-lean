# Snedecor's $F$ law

Let $(\Omega, P)$ be a probability space, $d_1, d_2 \ge 1$, and $V_1, V_2$ independent random variables with $V_1 \sim \chi^2_{d_1}$ and $V_2 \sim \chi^2_{d_2}$. Then $(V_1/d_1)/(V_2/d_2)$ has Snedecor's $F$ law with $(d_1, d_2)$ degrees of freedom, whose density on $x > 0$ is

$$\frac{\Gamma\big(\frac{d_1 + d_2}{2}\big)}{\Gamma\big(\frac{d_1}{2}\big)\Gamma\big(\frac{d_2}{2}\big)} \Big(\frac{d_1}{d_2}\Big)^{d_1/2} x^{d_1/2 - 1} \Big(1 + \frac{d_1 x}{d_2}\Big)^{-(d_1 + d_2)/2}.$$

## Sources

- G. Casella and R. L. Berger, *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed., Duxbury, 2002, Definition 5.3.6.
