# Independence of the least-squares estimate and the scaled residual sum of squares

In the linear model $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ with $X^\top X$ invertible, $\sigma > 0$ and
$\varepsilon / \sigma$ a standard Gaussian vector, the least-squares estimate $\hat\beta$ is independent
of $\|y - X\hat\beta\|^2 / \sigma^2$.
