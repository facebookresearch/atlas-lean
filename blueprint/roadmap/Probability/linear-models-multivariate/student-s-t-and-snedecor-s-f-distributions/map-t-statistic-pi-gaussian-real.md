# One-sample $t$ statistic

Let $n \ge 2$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$, $v > 0$ and $X_1, \dots, X_n$ i.i.d. $N(\mu, v)$. With $\bar X = \frac1n \sum_i X_i$ and $S^2 = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_i (X_i - \bar X)^2$, the statistic

$$\frac{\sqrt n\, (\bar X - \mu)}{S}$$

has the [Student $t$ law](student-tmeasure.md) with $n - 1$ degrees of freedom.

## Sources

- G. Casella and R. L. Berger, *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed., Duxbury, 2002, Theorem 5.3.1 and Definition 5.3.4.
