# Exact coverage of the upper one-sided t confidence bound

Let $n \ge 2$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$, and let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ be i.i.d. $\mathcal N(\mu, v)$ on a probability space. Write $\bar X = \frac1n\sum_i X_i$, $S = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_i(X_i - \bar X)^2}$, and $F_{n-1}$ for the distribution function of Student's $t$ law with $n - 1$ degrees of freedom. Then for every $c \in \mathbb{R}$, $P\big(\mu \le \bar X + cS/\sqrt n\big) = F_{n-1}(c)$.
