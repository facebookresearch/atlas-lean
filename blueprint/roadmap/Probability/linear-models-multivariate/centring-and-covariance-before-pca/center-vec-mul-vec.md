# Centring an outer product $u v^\top$ centres $u$

Let $u \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $v \in \mathbb{R}^p$, let $\bar u = \frac1n \sum_k u_k$ and let
$\mathbf 1 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be the all-ones vector. For every $n$, the
[centred matrix](../../../Probability/linear-models-multivariate/principal-component-analysis/center.md)
of the $n \times p$ data matrix $u v^\top$ is

$$(u v^\top)_c = (u - \bar u\, \mathbf 1)\, v^\top.$$
