# Sample covariance of an outer product has rank at most one

Let $u \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $v \in \mathbb{R}^p$. For every $n$, the
[sample covariance matrix](../principal-component-analysis/sample-cov.md)
of the $n \times p$ data matrix $u v^\top$ has rank at most one: it is a scalar multiple of
$v v^\top$.
