# Gaussian projection statistic for the zero subspace

Let `V` be a positive definite real matrix indexed by a finite set `ι`. For the zero subspace
`T = {0}` of `ℝ^ι`, the [Gaussian projection statistic](gauss-proj-stat.md) is the full quadratic
form: `S(V, {0}, z) = zᵀ V⁻¹ z` for every `z ∈ ℝ^ι`.
