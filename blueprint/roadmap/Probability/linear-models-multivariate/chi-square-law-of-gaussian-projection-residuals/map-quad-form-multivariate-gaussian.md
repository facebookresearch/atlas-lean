# Chi-square law of the inverse-covariance quadratic form

Let `ι` be a nonempty finite index set and `V` a positive definite real `ι × ι` matrix. If `Z` has
the centred Gaussian law `N(0, V)` on `ℝ^ι`, then `Zᵀ V⁻¹ Z` has the
[chi-square law](../../../Probability/distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md)
with `card ι` degrees of freedom. This is the case `T = {0}` of the
[chi-square law of the Gaussian projection statistic](map-gauss-proj-stat-multivariate-gaussia.md),
by the [zero-subspace case](gauss-proj-stat-bot.md) of the statistic.
