# Chi-square law of the Gaussian projection statistic

Let `ι` be a finite index set, `V` a positive definite real `ι × ι` matrix and `T` a linear subspace
of `ℝ^ι` of positive codimension `c = card ι - dim T > 0`. If `Z` has the centred Gaussian law
`N(0, V)` on `ℝ^ι`, then the [Gaussian projection statistic](gauss-proj-stat.md) `S(V, T, Z)` has
the
[chi-square law](../../distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md)
with `c` degrees of freedom (the Gaussian projection lemma).
