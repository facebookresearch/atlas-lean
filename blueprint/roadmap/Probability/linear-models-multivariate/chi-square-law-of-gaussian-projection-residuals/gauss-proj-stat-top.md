# Gaussian projection statistic for the whole space is zero

Let `V` be a positive definite real matrix indexed by a finite set `ι`. For the whole space
`T = ℝ^ι` (codimension `0`), the [Gaussian projection statistic](gauss-proj-stat.md) is
`S(V, ℝ^ι, z) = 0` for every `z ∈ ℝ^ι`. Its law under any probability measure on `ℝ^ι` is therefore
the Dirac mass at `0`. The
[chi-square law](../../../Probability/distributions/continuous/chi-square-distribution/chi-square-measure.md)
with `0` degrees of freedom is not that law but the zero measure, which is why the
[chi-square law of the statistic](map-gauss-proj-stat-multivariate-gaussia.md) is stated for
positive codimension.
