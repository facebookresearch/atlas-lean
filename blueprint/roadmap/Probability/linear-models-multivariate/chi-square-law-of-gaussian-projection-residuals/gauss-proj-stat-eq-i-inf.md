# Gaussian projection statistic as the minimal residual quadratic form

Let `V` be a positive definite real matrix indexed by a finite set `ι`. For every linear subspace
`T` of `ℝ^ι` and every `z ∈ ℝ^ι`, the [Gaussian projection statistic](gauss-proj-stat.md)
`S(V, T, z)` is the minimum over `T` of the residual quadratic form: it is the infimum of
`(z - u)ᵀ V⁻¹ (z - u)` over `u ∈ T`, and some `u ∈ T` attains it.
