# Closing a composition leaves its CLR unchanged

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with at least one part and $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a
[positive composition](pos-composition.md), so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$. Then its
[closure](closure-op.md), $\mathcal{C}(x)_i = x_i / \sum_{j \in \iota} x_j$, has the same
[CLR transform](clr.md) as $x$: $\operatorname{clr}(\mathcal{C}(x)) = \operatorname{clr}(x)$.
