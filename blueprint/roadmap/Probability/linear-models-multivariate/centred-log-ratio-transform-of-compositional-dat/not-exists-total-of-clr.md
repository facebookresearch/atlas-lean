# With at least one part, the CLR does not determine the total

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with at least one part. There is no function
$f : \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ such that
$f(\operatorname{clr}(x)) = \sum_{i \in \iota} x_i$ for every
[positive composition](pos-composition.md) $x$ on $\iota$: the [CLR transform](clr.md) loses
the total. For example, $x$ and $2x$ have the same CLR transform but different totals. With no
parts every total is $0$, and the constant function $0$ recovers it.
