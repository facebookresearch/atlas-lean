# Strictly positive compositions

For a set of parts $\iota$, a positive composition is a vector $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ of real
numbers with $x_i > 0$ for every part $i$. No total is imposed: the parts need not sum to $1$.
Positive compositions are the domain of the [centred log-ratio transform](clr.md), which is
defined when $\iota$ is finite.
