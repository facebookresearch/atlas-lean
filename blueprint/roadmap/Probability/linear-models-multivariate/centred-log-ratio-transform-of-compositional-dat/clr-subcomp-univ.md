# The subcomposition on all parts has the full CLR

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts and $x$ a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$ (so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$). The [subcomposition](subcomp.md) $x_\iota$ of $x$ on the
set of all parts has the same [CLR](clr.md) coordinates as $x$:
$\operatorname{clr}(x_\iota)_i = \operatorname{clr}(x)_i$ for every part $i$.
