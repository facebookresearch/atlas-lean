# Reindexing a positive composition by a permutation

Let $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on a set of parts
$\iota$, so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$, and let $\sigma$ be a permutation of $\iota$. The
reindexed composition is $x \circ \sigma$, with $(x \circ \sigma)_i = x_{\sigma(i)}$ for every
part $i$; it is again a positive composition.
