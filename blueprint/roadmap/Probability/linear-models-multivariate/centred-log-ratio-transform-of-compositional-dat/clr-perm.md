# Permutation equivariance of the CLR transform

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts, $x$ a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$ (so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$) and $\sigma$ a permutation of $\iota$. Then the
[CLR transform](clr.md) of the [reindexed composition](perm.md) $x \circ \sigma$ is the reindexed
CLR transform of $x$: $\operatorname{clr}(x \circ \sigma) = \operatorname{clr}(x) \circ \sigma$,
that is, $\operatorname{clr}(x \circ \sigma)_i = \operatorname{clr}(x)_{\sigma(i)}$ for every
part $i$.
