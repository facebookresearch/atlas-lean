# Proportions in a proper subcomposition differ from those in the whole

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts, $x$ a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$ (so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$), $S \subseteq \iota$ a set of parts and $i \in S$, so that
$S$ and $\iota$ are nonempty. The proportion of part $i$ in the [closed](closure-op.md)
[subcomposition](subcomp.md) equals its proportion in the closed full composition,

$$\frac{x_i}{\sum_{j \in S} x_j} = \frac{x_i}{\sum_{j \in \iota} x_j},$$

if and only if $S = \iota$. So, unlike differences of CLR coordinates, proportions change as
soon as a part is left out: they are not subcompositionally coherent.
