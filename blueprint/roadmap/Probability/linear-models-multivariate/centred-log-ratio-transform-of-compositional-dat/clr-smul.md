# Scale invariance of the CLR transform

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts, $x$ a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$ (so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$) and $t > 0$. Then the [rescaled composition](smul.md)
$t \cdot x = (t x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ has the same [CLR transform](clr.md) as $x$:
$\operatorname{clr}(t \cdot x) = \operatorname{clr}(x)$.
