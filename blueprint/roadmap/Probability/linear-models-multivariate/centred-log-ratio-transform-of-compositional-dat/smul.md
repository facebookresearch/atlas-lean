# Rescaling a positive composition by a positive factor

Let $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on a set of parts
$\iota$, so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$, and let $t > 0$ be a real number. The rescaled composition
is $t \cdot x = (t x_i)_{i \in \iota}$, again a positive composition. In sequencing data a
rescaling models, for example, a different sequencing depth.
