# Subcomposition of a positive composition

Let $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ be a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on a set of parts
$\iota$, so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$, and let $S \subseteq \iota$ be a finite set of parts. The
subcomposition of $x$ on $S$ is the positive composition $x_S = (x_i)_{i \in S}$, indexed by
the parts in $S$. Its entries are those of $x$, without rescaling; for nonempty $S$, its
[closure](closure-op.md) is the subcomposition with total $1$ that Aitchison uses.
