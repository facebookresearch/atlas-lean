# CLR differences in a subcomposition are log-ratios

Let $x$ be a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on a set of parts $\iota$ (so
$x_i > 0$ for every $i$; $\iota$ need not be finite), $S \subseteq \iota$ a finite set of parts
and $i, j \in S$. Then the [CLR transform](clr.md) of the [subcomposition](subcomp.md)
$x_S = (x_k)_{k \in S}$, taken over the $|S|$ parts of $S$, satisfies

$$\operatorname{clr}(x_S)_i - \operatorname{clr}(x_S)_j = \log \frac{x_i}{x_j} .$$

The logarithm is of a positive number since $x_i, x_j > 0$.
