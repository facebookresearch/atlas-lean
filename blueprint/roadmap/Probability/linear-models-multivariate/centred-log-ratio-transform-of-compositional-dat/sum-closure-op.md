# A closed composition has total one

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with at least one part and $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a
[positive composition](pos-composition.md), so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$. Then its
[closure](closure-op.md), $\mathcal{C}(x)_i = x_i / \sum_{j \in \iota} x_j$, has total one:
$\sum_{i \in \iota} \mathcal{C}(x)_i = 1$.
