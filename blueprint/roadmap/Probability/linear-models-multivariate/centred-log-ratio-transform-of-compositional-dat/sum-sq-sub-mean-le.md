# Centring over a finite set does not increase the sum of squares

Let $S$ be a finite set and $(a_i)_{i \in S}$ real numbers, with mean
$\bar a = \frac{1}{|S|} \sum_{j \in S} a_j$. Then

$$\sum_{i \in S} (a_i - \bar a)^2 \le \sum_{i \in S} a_i^2 .$$

If $S$ is empty, both sums are empty and the inequality reads $0 \le 0$; the mean is then $0/0$,
which is $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
