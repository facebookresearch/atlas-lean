# Closure is rescaling by the reciprocal of the total

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with at least one part and $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a
[positive composition](pos-composition.md), so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$. Then the
[closure](closure-op.md) of $x$ is its [rescaling](smul.md) by the reciprocal of its total:
$\mathcal{C}(x) = t \cdot x$ with $t = 1 / \sum_{j \in \iota} x_j > 0$, that is,
$\mathcal{C}(x)_i = t\, x_i$ for every part $i$.
