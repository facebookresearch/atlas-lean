# Squared Aitchison distance

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts and $x, y$ [positive compositions](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$. The squared Aitchison distance between $x$ and $y$ is the squared Euclidean distance
between their [CLR transforms](clr.md):

$$
d_A^2(x, y) = \sum_{i \in \iota} \bigl(\operatorname{clr}(x)_i - \operatorname{clr}(y)_i\bigr)^2 .
$$
