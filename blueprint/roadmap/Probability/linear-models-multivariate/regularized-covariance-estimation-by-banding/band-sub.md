# Banding is linear

For real $p \times p$ matrices $A$ and $B$ and every $k$, $B_k(A) - B_k(B) = B_k(A - B)$: the [banding operator](band.md) acts entrywise. In particular $B_k(\hat\Sigma) - B_k(\Sigma) = B_k(\hat\Sigma - \Sigma)$, the form in which the variance term of the [Bickel–Levina rate](banded-sample-covariance-rate.md) is bounded.

## Sources

- P. J. Bickel and E. Levina, "Regularized estimation of large covariance matrices", *Ann. Statist.* 36 (2008) 199–227, Section 2.1.
