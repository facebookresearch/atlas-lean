# Uniform bias bound on the bandable class

Let $\varepsilon_0, \alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, $C \ge 0$ and $M$ a matrix in the [bandable class](bandable-class.md) $\mathcal U(\varepsilon_0, \alpha, C)$. For every band width $k \ge 1$,

$$\|B_k(M) - M\| \le C k^{-\alpha}$$

in operator norm, where $B_k$ is the [banding operator](band.md).

## Sources

- P. J. Bickel and E. Levina, "Regularized estimation of large covariance matrices", *Ann. Statist.* 36 (2008) 199–227, (A6) in the proof of Theorem 1.
