# Cell-mean coefficients of a two-by-two design

For a finite sample with binary indicators $D_i$, $T_i$ and responses $y_i$, let $m_{ab}$ be the mean of $y$ over the cell $\{i : D_i = a, T_i = b\}$, with $0$ for an empty cell. The cell-mean coefficients are

$$\big(m_{00},\ m_{10} - m_{00},\ m_{01} - m_{00},\ (m_{11} - m_{10}) - (m_{01} - m_{00})\big).$$
