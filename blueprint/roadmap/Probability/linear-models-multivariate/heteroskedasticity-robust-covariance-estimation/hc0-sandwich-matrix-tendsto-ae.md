# Strong consistency of a sandwich

Let $k \ge 1$, let $\hat Q_n$ and $\hat M_n$ be random $k \times k$ matrices and $Q$, $M$ fixed $k \times k$ matrices with $\hat Q_n^{-1} \to Q^{-1}$ and $\hat M_n \to M$ almost surely (matrix inverses, zero when singular). Then $\hat Q_n^{-1}\hat M_n\hat Q_n^{-\top} \to Q^{-1}MQ^{-\top}$ almost surely.
