# Covariance of the generalized least-squares estimator

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix and $V$ a positive definite real $m \times m$ matrix such that $X^\top V^{-1} X$ is invertible, and let $B$ be the [GLS matrix](gls-matrix.md). Then the covariance of the GLS estimator is

$$B V B^\top = (X^\top V^{-1} X)^{-1}.$$
