# Unbiasedness of generalized least squares, componentwise

Let $X$ be a real $n \times p$ matrix and $V$ a real $n \times n$ matrix with $X^\top V^{-1}X$ invertible, and write $\hat\beta(y) = (X^\top V^{-1}X)^{-1}X^\top V^{-1}y$ for the generalized least-squares estimator. Let $\varepsilon$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$ on a probability space and $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p$. If the coordinates of $\varepsilon$ are integrable with mean zero, then $\mathbb{E}[\hat\beta(X\beta + \varepsilon)_j] = \beta_j$ for every $j$.
