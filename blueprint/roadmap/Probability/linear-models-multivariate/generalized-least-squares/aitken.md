# Aitken's theorem

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix and $V$ a positive definite real $m \times m$ matrix such that $X^\top V^{-1} X$ is invertible, and let $B$ be the [GLS matrix](gls-matrix.md). For every real $k \times m$ matrix $A$ with $AX = I_k$,

$$A V A^\top - B V B^\top \text{ is positive semidefinite}:$$

the GLS estimator has minimal covariance among linear unbiased estimators.

## Sources

- A. C. Aitken, *On least squares and linear combination of observations*, Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 55 (1936), 42–48.
