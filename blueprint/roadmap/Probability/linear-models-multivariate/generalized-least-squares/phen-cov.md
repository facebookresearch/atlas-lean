# Phenotypic covariance of a linear mixed model

For a real $n \times q$ design matrix $Z$, a real $q \times q$ matrix $G$ and a real $n \times n$ matrix $R$, the phenotypic covariance is

$$V = Z G Z^\top + R,$$

the covariance of $y = Xb + Zu + e$ when $\operatorname{Cov} u = G$ and $\operatorname{Cov} e = R$ with $u, e$ uncorrelated.
