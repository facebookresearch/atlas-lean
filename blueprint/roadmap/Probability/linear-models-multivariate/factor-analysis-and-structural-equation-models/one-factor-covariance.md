# Covariance of the one-factor model

For loadings $l$ and error variances $\psi$ indexed by a set of indicators, the covariance implied by the standardized one-factor model is $\Sigma = ll^\top + \operatorname{diag}(\psi)$.

## Sources

- T. W. Anderson and H. Rubin, "Statistical inference in factor analysis", *Proceedings of the Third Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability*, vol. 5, University of California Press (1956): equation (3.1), Section 2 and Theorems 5.1 and 5.5.
