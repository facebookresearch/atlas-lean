# Affine reparametrization preserves the factor-model mean

For intercepts $\nu$, a loading matrix $\Lambda$, factor means $a$, a factor covariance $\Phi$ and an error covariance $\Theta$, the factor model implies mean $\nu + \Lambda a$ and covariance $\Lambda\Phi\Lambda^\top + \Theta$. For an invertible matrix $U$ and a vector $\beta$, the parameters $(\nu + \Lambda\beta,\ \Lambda U,\ U^{-1}(a - \beta))$ imply the same mean as $(\nu, \Lambda, a)$.
