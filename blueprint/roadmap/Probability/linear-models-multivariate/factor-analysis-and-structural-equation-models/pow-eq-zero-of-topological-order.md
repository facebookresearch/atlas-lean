# Higher powers of an acyclic coefficient matrix vanish

Let $B$ be a square matrix over a commutative ring indexed by a finite set $V$ of $n$ vertices, supported on a directed acyclic graph: there is $\mathrm{ord} : V \to \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with $B_{ij} \ne 0$ only if $\mathrm{ord}(j) < \mathrm{ord}(i)$. Then $B^m = 0$ for every $m \ge n$.
