# Comparing latent means under common loadings and intercepts

Let $\Lambda$ be a matrix with $v \mapsto \Lambda v$ injective and $(U_1, \beta_1)$, $(U_2, \beta_2)$ invertible affine reparametrizations with $\Lambda U_1 = \Lambda U_2$ and $\nu + \Lambda\beta_1 = \nu + \Lambda\beta_2$. Then for factor means $a_1, a_2$, $U_1^{-1}(a_1 - \beta_1) = U_2^{-1}(a_2 - \beta_2)$ if and only if $a_1 = a_2$.

## Sources

- H. Wu and R. Estabrook, "Identification of confirmatory factor analysis models of different levels of invariance for ordered categorical outcomes", *Psychometrika* 81(4) (2016): Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 3.1.
