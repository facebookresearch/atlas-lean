# Affine reparametrization preserves the Gaussian factor-model law

For intercepts $\nu$, a loading matrix $\Lambda$, factor means $a$, a factor covariance $\Phi$ and an error covariance $\Theta$, the factor model implies mean $\nu + \Lambda a$ and covariance $\Lambda\Phi\Lambda^\top + \Theta$. For an invertible $U$ and a vector $\beta$, the normal law with the mean and covariance implied by $(\nu, \Lambda, a, \Phi, \Theta)$ equals the normal law implied by $(\nu + \Lambda\beta,\ \Lambda U,\ U^{-1}(a - \beta),\ U^{-1}\Phi U^{-\top},\ \Theta)$.
