# Identification of the one-factor model with nonzero loadings

For loadings $l$ and error variances $\psi$ indexed by a set of indicators, let $\Sigma(l, \psi) = ll^\top + \operatorname{diag}(\psi)$ be the one-factor covariance. Suppose there are at least three distinct indicators and every loading is nonzero. If $\Sigma(l', \psi') = \Sigma(l, \psi)$, then $l' = \pm l$ (one shared sign) and $\psi' = \psi$.
