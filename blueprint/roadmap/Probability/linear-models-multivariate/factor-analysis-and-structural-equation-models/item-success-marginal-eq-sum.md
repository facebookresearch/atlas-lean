# The marginal success probability is a latent average

On a finite ability grid, let $\pi$ be the probability mass function of ability and $q(\theta)\in[0,1]$ the conditional probability of success on a binary item. The [marginal success probability](item-success-marginal.md) is

$$m=\sum_\theta \pi(\theta)q(\theta).$$

This is the law of total probability for a success, including ability levels to which $\pi$ assigns zero mass.
