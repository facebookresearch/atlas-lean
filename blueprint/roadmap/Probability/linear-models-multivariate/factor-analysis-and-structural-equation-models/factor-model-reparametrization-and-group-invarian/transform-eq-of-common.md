# Common loadings and intercepts determine the affine transform

Let $\Lambda$ be a matrix with $v \mapsto \Lambda v$ injective, $\nu$ intercepts, and $(U_1, \beta_1)$, $(U_2, \beta_2)$ invertible affine reparametrizations. If $\Lambda U_1 = \Lambda U_2$ and $\nu + \Lambda\beta_1 = \nu + \Lambda\beta_2$, then $U_1 = U_2$ and $\beta_1 = \beta_2$.
