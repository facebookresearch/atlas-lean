# Affine reparametrization preserves the factor-model covariance

For intercepts $\nu$, a loading matrix $\Lambda$, factor means $a$, a factor covariance $\Phi$ and an error covariance $\Theta$, the factor model implies mean $\nu + \Lambda a$ and covariance $\Lambda\Phi\Lambda^\top + \Theta$. For an invertible matrix $U$, the parameters $(\Lambda U,\ U^{-1}\Phi U^{-\top},\ \Theta)$ imply the same covariance as $(\Lambda, \Phi, \Theta)$.

## Sources

- H. Wu and R. Estabrook, "Identification of confirmatory factor analysis models of different levels of invariance for ordered categorical outcomes", *Psychometrika* 81(4) (2016): Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 3.1.
