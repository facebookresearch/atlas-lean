# Covariance implied by a factor model

For a loading matrix $\Lambda$, a factor covariance $\Phi$ and an error covariance $\Theta$, the factor model implies the covariance $\Lambda\Phi\Lambda^\top + \Theta$.

## Sources

- H. Wu and R. Estabrook, "Identification of confirmatory factor analysis models of different levels of invariance for ordered categorical outcomes", *Psychometrika* 81(4) (2016): Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 3.1.
