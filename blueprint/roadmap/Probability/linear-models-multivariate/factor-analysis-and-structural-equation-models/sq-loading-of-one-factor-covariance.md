# Recovering a squared loading from three covariances

For loadings $l$ and error variances $\psi$ indexed by a set of indicators, let $\Sigma(l, \psi) = ll^\top + \operatorname{diag}(\psi)$ be the one-factor covariance. For distinct indicators $a, b, c$ with $l_b, l_c \ne 0$, $l_a^2 = \Sigma_{ab}\Sigma_{ac}/\Sigma_{bc}$.
