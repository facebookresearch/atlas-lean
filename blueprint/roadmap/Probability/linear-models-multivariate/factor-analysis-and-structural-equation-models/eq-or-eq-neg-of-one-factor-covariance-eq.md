# Identification of the one-factor model up to sign

For loadings $l$ and error variances $\psi$ indexed by a set of indicators, let $\Sigma(l, \psi) = ll^\top + \operatorname{diag}(\psi)$ be the one-factor covariance. Let $i, j, k$ be distinct indicators with $l_i, l_j, l_k \ne 0$. If $\Sigma(l', \psi') = \Sigma(l, \psi)$, then $l' = l$ or $l' = -l$, and $\psi' = \psi$.
