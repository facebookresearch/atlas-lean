# Law of the one-factor model observation

Let $F$ be a standard normal factor and $E$ an independent error vector with law $\mathcal N(0, \operatorname{diag}(\psi))$, $\psi \ge 0$, both measurable on a probability space, and let $X = \mu + \Lambda F + E$ almost surely, where $\Lambda$ is the one-column matrix of loadings $l$. Then $X$ has law $\mathcal N\big(\mu, ll^\top + \operatorname{diag}(\psi)\big)$; the law depends on $(l, \psi)$ only through this covariance.

## Sources

- T. W. Anderson and H. Rubin, "Statistical inference in factor analysis", *Proceedings of the Third Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability*, vol. 5, University of California Press (1956): equation (3.1), Section 2 and Theorems 5.1 and 5.5.
