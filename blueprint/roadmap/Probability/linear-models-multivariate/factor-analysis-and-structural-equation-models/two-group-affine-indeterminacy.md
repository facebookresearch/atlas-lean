# Latent means of two groups are not identified without invariance

For intercepts $\nu$, a loading matrix $\Lambda$, factor means $a$, a factor covariance $\Phi$ and an error covariance $\Theta$, the factor model implies mean $\nu + \Lambda a$ and covariance $\Lambda\Phi\Lambda^\top + \Theta$. Suppose there is at least one factor. Then there are invertible group-specific reparametrizations $(U_g, \beta_g)$, $g = 0, 1$, such that each group's normal law is unchanged while the transformed factor means $U_g^{-1}(a - \beta_g)$ of the two groups differ, although both groups share the factor mean $a$. Without invariance constraints the observed laws cannot decide whether latent means are equal.
