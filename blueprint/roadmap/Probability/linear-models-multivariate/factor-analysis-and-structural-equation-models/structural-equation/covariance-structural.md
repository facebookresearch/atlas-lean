# Covariance implied by linear structural equations

Let $B$ be a real $p \times p$ matrix with $I - B$ invertible, $\Gamma$ a real $p \times q$ matrix, and $y, x, \zeta$ random vectors on a probability space with $y = By + \Gamma x + \zeta$ for every outcome. Suppose $x$ and $\zeta$ have square-integrable coordinates with covariance matrices $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(x_k, \zeta_l) = 0$ for all $k, l$. Then

$$\operatorname{Cov}(y) = (I - B)^{-1}\big(\Gamma\Phi\Gamma^\top + \Psi\big)(I - B)^{-\top}.$$
