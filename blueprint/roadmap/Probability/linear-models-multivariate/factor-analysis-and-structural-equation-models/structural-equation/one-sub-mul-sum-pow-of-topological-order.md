# Geometric series inverse for an acyclic coefficient matrix

Let $B$ be a square matrix over a commutative ring indexed by a finite set $V$ of $n$ vertices, supported on a directed acyclic graph: there is $\mathrm{ord} : V \to \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with $B_{ij} \ne 0$ only if $\mathrm{ord}(j) < \mathrm{ord}(i)$. Then $\sum_{k<n}B^k$ is a two-sided inverse of $I - B$.
