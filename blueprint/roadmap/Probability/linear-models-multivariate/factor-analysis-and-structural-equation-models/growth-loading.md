# Loading matrix of a linear growth model

The linear growth loading matrix for three equally spaced time points is the $3 \times 2$ matrix with rows $(1, 0), (1, 1), (1, 2)$: intercept loadings $1, 1, 1$ and slope loadings $0, 1, 2$.
