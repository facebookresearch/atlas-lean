# Uncentred score second-moment matrix equals the population HC0 meat

For a regressor vector $X_0$ with values in $\mathbb{R}^k$, $k$ finite, an error $U_0$ and a measure $\mu$, the uncentred score second-moment matrix, with entries $\int U_0^2\, X_{0,a} X_{0,b} \, d\mu$ (no centring is applied), is by definition the population meat of the HC0 sandwich covariance. It is the covariance matrix of the score $U_0 X_0$ only when the score has mean zero; for a constant score $1$ the matrix is $[1]$ while the covariance is $[0]$.
