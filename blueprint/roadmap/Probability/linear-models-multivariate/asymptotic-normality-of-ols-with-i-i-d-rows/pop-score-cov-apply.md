# Population meat in score form

For a random regressor vector $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and a real error $u_0$ on a measure space, the entries of the population meat $\Omega = \mathbb{E}[u_0^2x_0x_0^\top]$ are $\Omega_{ab} = \mathbb{E}\big[(u_0x_0)_a(u_0x_0)_b\big]$, the second moments of the score $u_0x_0$.
