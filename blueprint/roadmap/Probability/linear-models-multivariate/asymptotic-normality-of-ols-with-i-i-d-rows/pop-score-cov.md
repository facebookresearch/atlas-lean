# Population score second-moment matrix

For a measure $\mu$, the population score second-moment matrix is $\Omega_{ab} = \int u_0^2\, x_{0,a} x_{0,b}\, d\mu$, the middle factor of the sandwich covariance $Q^{-1} \Omega Q^{-1}$. When $\mu$ is a probability measure, the score $x_0 u_0$ is square-integrable and $E[x_0 u_0] = 0$, it is the covariance matrix of the score.
