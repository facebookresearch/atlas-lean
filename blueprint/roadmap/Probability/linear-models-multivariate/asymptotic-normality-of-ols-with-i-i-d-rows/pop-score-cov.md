# Population score second-moment matrix

The population score second-moment matrix is $\Omega_{ab} = E[(x_0 u_0)_a (x_0 u_0)_b]$, the middle
factor of the sandwich covariance $Q^{-1} \Omega Q^{-1}$. Under the exogeneity hypothesis
$E[x_0 u_0] = 0$ it is the covariance matrix of the score $x_0 u_0$; it equals
$E[u_0^2 x_0 x_0^\top]$.
