# Working response of iteratively reweighted least squares

For a design $X$, responses $y$, mean and weight functions $\mu, V$ and coefficients $\beta$, the working response is $z_i = \eta_i + (y_i - \mu(\eta_i))/V(\eta_i)$ with $\eta = X\beta$. A zero weight $V(\eta_i) = 0$ makes the quotient $0$.
