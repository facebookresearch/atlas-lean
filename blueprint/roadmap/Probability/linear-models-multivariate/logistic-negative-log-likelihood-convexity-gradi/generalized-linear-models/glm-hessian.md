# Hessian of a canonical-link generalized linear model

Let $(\Theta, b, \mu, V)$ be a canonical cumulant specification: an open convex natural-parameter domain $\Theta \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, a cumulant $b$ with $b' = \mu$ and $\mu' = V \ge 0$ on $\Theta$. The Hessian of the log-likelihood is $-X^\top W(\beta)X/\varphi$, where $W(\beta)$ is the diagonal matrix of working weights $V(\eta_i(\beta))$. The dispersion $\varphi$ is positive.
