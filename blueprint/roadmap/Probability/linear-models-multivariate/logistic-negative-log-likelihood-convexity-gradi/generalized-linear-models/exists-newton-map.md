# The Newton iteration is well defined

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space, $U : E \to E$ any map, and let $H$ satisfy $\|H(x) - H(y)\| \le L\|x - y\|$ for $x, y \in B(b, r)$. Let $H(b)$ have a left inverse of norm at most $M$, and let $\varepsilon \le r$ with $2ML\varepsilon \le 1$. Then some map $T$ satisfies $H(x)(T(x) - x) = -U(x)$ for every $x \in B(b, \varepsilon)$.
