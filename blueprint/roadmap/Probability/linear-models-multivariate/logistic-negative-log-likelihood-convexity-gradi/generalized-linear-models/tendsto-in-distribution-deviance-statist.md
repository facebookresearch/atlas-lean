# Chi-square limit of the deviance for a simple null

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent, identically distributed observations on a probability space taking values in a finite set of $k$ cells, with $P(X_0 = i) = p_i > 0$, and let $N_i$ be the count of cell $i$ among the first $n$ observations. If $k \ge 2$, the deviance $D_n = \sum_i2\big(N_i\log(N_i/(np_i)) - (N_i - np_i)\big)$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $k - 1$ degrees of freedom.
