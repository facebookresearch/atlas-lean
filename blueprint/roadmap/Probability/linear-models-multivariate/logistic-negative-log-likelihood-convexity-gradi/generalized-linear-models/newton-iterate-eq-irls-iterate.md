# Newton–Raphson and iteratively reweighted least squares have the same iterates

For a design $X$, responses $y$, a mean function $\mu$ and a weight function $V$, write $\eta = X\beta$, $W(\beta) = \operatorname{diag}(V(\eta_i))$, the working response $z_i = \eta_i + (y_i - \mu(\eta_i))/V(\eta_i)$, and let the IRLS step be the weighted least-squares fit of $z$ on $X$ with weights $V(\eta_i)$. Suppose the score and Hessian satisfy the canonical-link identities $U(\beta) = X^\top(y - \mu(X\beta))$ and $H(\beta) = -X^\top W(\beta)X$. If the weights are nonzero and $X^\top W(\beta)X$ is invertible at every $\beta$, the Newton–Raphson and IRLS iterates from any $\beta_0$ coincide.
