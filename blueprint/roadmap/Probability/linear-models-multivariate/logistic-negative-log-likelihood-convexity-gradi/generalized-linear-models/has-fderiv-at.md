# The canonical Hessian is the derivative of the score

For a design $X$, responses $y$, a mean function $\mu$ and a weight function $V$, write $\eta = X\beta$, $W(\beta) = \operatorname{diag}(V(\eta_i))$, the working response $z_i = \eta_i + (y_i - \mu(\eta_i))/V(\eta_i)$, and let the IRLS step be the weighted least-squares fit of $z$ on $X$ with weights $V(\eta_i)$. Suppose the score and Hessian satisfy the canonical-link identities $U(\beta) = X^\top(y - \mu(X\beta))$ and $H(\beta) = -X^\top W(\beta)X$. If $\mu$ has derivative $V$ everywhere, then $H(\beta)$ is the derivative of $U$ at every $\beta$.
