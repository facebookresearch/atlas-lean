# Uniqueness of a root near a Newton attractor

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $U : E \to E$ with $U(b) = 0$, and suppose $U$ has derivative $H(x)$ at every $x \in B(b, r)$ with $\|H(x) - H(y)\| \le L\|x - y\|$ there. Let $H(b)$ have a left inverse of norm at most $M$, and let $\varepsilon \le r$ with $2ML\varepsilon \le 1$. Then $b$ is the only zero of $U$ in $B(b, \varepsilon)$.
