# Derivative of the canonical score

For a design $X$, responses $y$ and coefficients $\beta$, if $\mu$ has derivative $V(\eta_i)$ at every $\eta_i = (X\beta)_i$, then $\beta' \mapsto X^\top(y - \mu(X\beta'))$ has derivative $-X^\top W(\beta)X$ at $\beta$, with $W(\beta) = \operatorname{diag}(V(\eta_i))$.
