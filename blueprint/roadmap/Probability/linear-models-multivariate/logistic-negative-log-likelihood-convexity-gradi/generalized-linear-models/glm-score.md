# Score of a canonical-link generalized linear model

Let $(\Theta, b, \mu, V)$ be a canonical cumulant specification: an open convex natural-parameter domain $\Theta \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, a cumulant $b$ with $b' = \mu$ and $\mu' = V \ge 0$ on $\Theta$. The score is $X^\top\big(y - \mu(\eta(\beta))\big)/\varphi$, with $\mu$ applied coordinatewise to $\eta(\beta) = X\beta + o$. The dispersion $\varphi$ is positive.
