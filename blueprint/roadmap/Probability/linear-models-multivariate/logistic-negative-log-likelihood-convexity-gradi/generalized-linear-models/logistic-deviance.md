# Logistic deviance

For a design $X$, offset $o$ and responses $y$, the logistic deviance is $2(\ell_{\mathrm{sat}}(y) - \ell(\beta))$, where $\ell$ is the logistic regression log-likelihood and $\ell_{\mathrm{sat}}$ the saturated Bernoulli log-likelihood. It has a deviance meaning only for responses in $[0, 1]$.
