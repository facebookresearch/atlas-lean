# Chi-square limit of the deviance with fitted cell probabilities

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent, identically distributed observations on a probability space taking values in a finite set of $k$ cells, with $P(X_0 = i) = p_i(\theta_0) > 0$ for a model $\theta \mapsto p(\theta)$ on a finite-dimensional real normed space (nonnegative, measurable, summing to $1$ near $\theta_0$ and differentiable at $\theta_0$ with derivative $D$), and let $N_i$ be the count of cell $i$ among the first $n$ observations. Let $J$ be the weighted Jacobian $Ja = (Da_i/\sqrt{p_i(\theta_0)})_i$, $L$ a linear map with $JL$ the orthogonal projection onto the range $V$ of $J$, $R_n$ the Pearson residuals $(N_i - np_i(\theta_0))/\sqrt{np_i(\theta_0)}$, and $\hat\theta_n$ almost everywhere measurable estimators with $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta_0) - L(R_n) \to 0$ in probability. If $\dim V + 1 < k$, the deviance with fitted probabilities $p(\hat\theta_n)$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $k - 1 - \dim V$ degrees of freedom.
