# Consistency of an efficient grouped-count estimator

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be mutually independent, identically distributed observations on a probability space taking values in a finite set of $k$ cells, with $P(X_0 = i) = p_i > 0$, and let $N_i$ be the count of cell $i$ among the first $n$ observations. Let $L$ be a continuous linear map from $\mathbb{R}^k$ to a finite-dimensional real normed space and $\hat\theta_n$ almost everywhere measurable estimators with $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta_0) - L(R_n) \to 0$ in probability, where $R_n = ((N_i - np_i)/\sqrt{np_i})_i$ are the Pearson residuals. Then $\hat\theta_n \to \theta_0$ in probability.
