# Joint score of a negative binomial count

For $\theta > 0$, a count $y$ and every $\eta$, the log mass function $\log\mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^\eta)$ is differentiable in $(\theta, \eta)$ with partial derivatives the shape score $\sum_{j<y}\frac1{\theta + j} + \log\theta + 1 - \log(\theta + \mu) - \frac{y + \theta}{\theta + \mu}$ and the score $\theta(y - \mu)/(\theta + \mu)$, with $\mu = e^\eta$.
