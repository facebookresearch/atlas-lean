# Consistency of plug-in negative binomial Fisher weights

If $(\hat\beta_n, \hat\theta_n) \to (\beta_0, \theta_0)$ in probability with $\theta_0 > 0$, then for a design $X$ and offset $o$ the plug-in weights $\hat\theta_n\hat\mu_i/(\hat\theta_n + \hat\mu_i)$, $\hat\mu_i = e^{(X\hat\beta_n)_i + o_i}$, converge in probability to the weights at $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$.
