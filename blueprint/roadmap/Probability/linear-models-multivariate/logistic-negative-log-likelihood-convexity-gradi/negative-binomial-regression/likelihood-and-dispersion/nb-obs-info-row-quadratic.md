# Quadratic form of the negative binomial observed information

For a shape $\theta$, a covariate row $x$, a count $y$, a linear predictor $\eta$ and a direction $(u, s)$, the observed information quadratic form is $w(x^\top u)^2 - 2sc\,(x^\top u) + s^2v$, with $w$, $c$ and $v$ the observed weight, mixed term and observed shape information.
