# Consistency of the plug-in negative binomial coefficient information

If $(\hat\beta_n, \hat\theta_n) \to (\beta_0, \theta_0)$ in probability with $\theta_0 > 0$, then every entry of $X^\top\operatorname{diag}(F_i(\hat\beta_n, \hat\theta_n))X$ converges in probability to the corresponding entry at $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$, where $F_i = \theta\mu_i/(\theta + \mu_i)$.
