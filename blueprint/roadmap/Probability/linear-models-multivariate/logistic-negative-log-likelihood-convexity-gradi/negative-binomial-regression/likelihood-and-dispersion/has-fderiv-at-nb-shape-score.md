# Joint derivative of the negative binomial shape score

For $\theta > 0$, a count $y$ and every $\eta$, the shape score is differentiable in $(\theta, \eta)$ with partial derivatives minus the observed shape information in $\theta$ and $(y - \mu)\mu/(\theta + \mu)^2$ in $\eta$ (with $\mu = e^\eta$); the mixed partials of the log mass function agree.
