# Rao statistic with a perturbed score and estimated information

Let $A$ be symmetric positive definite on a real inner product space of finite dimension $d \ge 1$, $T_n \to V$ in distribution with $\langle V, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle At, t\rangle)$, $\tilde T_n - T_n \to 0$ and $\hat A_n \to A$ in probability (almost everywhere measurable). Then $\langle\hat A_n^{-1}\tilde T_n, \tilde T_n\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $d$ degrees of freedom.
