# Scaled coefficient score at a null point

For a null coefficient $\beta_0$, a shape $\theta$ and observations $z_0, \dots, z_{n-1}$, the scaled coefficient score is $n^{-1/2}$ times the coefficient block of $\sum_{i<n}s_{(\beta_0, \theta)}(z_i)$, with $s$ the joint negative binomial score. For $n = 0$ it is $0$, since $(\sqrt 0)^{-1} = 0$.
