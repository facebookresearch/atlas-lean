# Composite score test for negative binomial coefficients

Consider negative binomial regression with joint parameter $p = (\beta, \theta)$, coefficients $\beta$ and shape $\theta$: an observation $z = (x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$) has density $f_p(z) = \mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$, with closed-form score $s_p(z)$ and observed information $J_p(z)$. Let $z_i$ be mutually independent random-design observations with true parameter $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$, a square-integrable centred score and positive definite $I(p_0)$ (at least one coefficient). Let $\tilde\theta_n$ be a consistent restricted shape estimate under $H_0 : \beta = \beta_0$ with $\sqrt n(\tilde\theta_n - \theta_0)$ bounded in probability, assume the uniform law of large numbers for the cross average on $|\theta - \theta_0| \le \delta$ with $0 < \delta < \theta_0$, and let $\hat B_n$ and $\tilde T_n$ be almost everywhere measurable, $\hat B_n$ consistent for the coefficient block $B$ of $I(p_0)$, where $\tilde T_n$ is the scaled coefficient score at $(\beta_0, \tilde\theta_n)$. Then the statistic $\langle\hat B_n^{-1}\tilde T_n, \tilde T_n\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa|$ degrees of freedom.
