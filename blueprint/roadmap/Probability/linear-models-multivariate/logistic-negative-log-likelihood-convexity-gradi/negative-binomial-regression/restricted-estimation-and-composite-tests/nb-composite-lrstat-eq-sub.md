# The composite statistic as a difference of simple-null statistics

For a design $X$, offset $o$ and counts $y$, let $\ell(\beta, \theta)$ be the joint negative binomial log-likelihood, $\ell_0(\theta) = \ell(\beta_0, \theta)$ its restriction to the null $\beta = \beta_0$, and $\ell_{\mathrm{prof}}(\theta) = \sup_\beta\ell(\beta, \theta)$ the profile log-likelihood of the shape. For every $\theta_0$, $2(\ell(\hat p) - \ell_0(\tilde\theta)) = 2(\ell(\hat p) - \ell(\beta_0, \theta_0)) - 2(\ell_0(\tilde\theta) - \ell(\beta_0, \theta_0))$.
