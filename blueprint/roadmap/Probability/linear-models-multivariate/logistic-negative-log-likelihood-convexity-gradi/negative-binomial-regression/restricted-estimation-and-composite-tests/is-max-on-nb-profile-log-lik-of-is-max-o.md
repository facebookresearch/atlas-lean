# A joint maximizer gives a profile maximizer

For a design $X$, offset $o$ and counts $y$, let $\ell(\beta, \theta)$ be the joint negative binomial log-likelihood, $\ell_0(\theta) = \ell(\beta_0, \theta)$ its restriction to the null $\beta = \beta_0$, and $\ell_{\mathrm{prof}}(\theta) = \sup_\beta\ell(\beta, \theta)$ the profile log-likelihood of the shape. If $(\hat\beta, \hat\theta)$ with $\hat\theta > 0$ maximizes $\ell$ over $\theta > 0$, then $\hat\theta$ maximizes $\ell_{\mathrm{prof}}$ over $\theta > 0$, $\hat\beta$ maximizes the $\hat\theta$-section, and $\ell_{\mathrm{prof}}(\hat\theta) = \ell(\hat\beta, \hat\theta)$.
