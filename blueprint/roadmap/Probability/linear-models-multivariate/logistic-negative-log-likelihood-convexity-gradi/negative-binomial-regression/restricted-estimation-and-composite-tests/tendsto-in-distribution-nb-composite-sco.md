# Composite score test with the shape estimated under the null

For covariate rows $x_i$, offsets $o_i$ and counts $y_i$ of a sample, a null coefficient $\beta_0$ and a shape $\theta$, let $S_n(\theta) = \sum_{i<n}\frac{\theta(y_i - \mu_i)}{\theta + \mu_i}x_i$ with $\mu_i = e^{x_i^\top\beta_0 + o_i}$ be the coefficient score and $C_n(\theta) = n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}\frac{(y_i - \mu_i)\mu_i}{(\theta + \mu_i)^2}x_i$ the averaged cross term. Let $T_n = n^{-1/2}S_n(\theta_0)$ converge in distribution to $V$ with $\langle V, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle At, t\rangle)$, $A$ symmetric positive definite (at least one coefficient); let $\tilde\theta_n \to \theta_0 > 0$ in probability with $\sqrt n(\tilde\theta_n - \theta_0)$ bounded in probability and $\sup_{|\theta - \theta_0| \le \delta}\|C_n(\theta)\| \to 0$ in probability for some $0 < \delta < \theta_0$; and let $\hat A_n \to A$ in probability, with $\hat A_n$ and $\tilde T_n = n^{-1/2}S_n(\tilde\theta_n)$ almost everywhere measurable. Then $\langle\hat A_n^{-1}\tilde T_n, \tilde T_n\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa|$ degrees of freedom.
