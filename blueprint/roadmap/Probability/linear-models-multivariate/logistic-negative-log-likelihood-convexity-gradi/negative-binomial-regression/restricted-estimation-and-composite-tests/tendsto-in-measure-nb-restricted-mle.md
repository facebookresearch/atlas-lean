# Consistency of the restricted negative binomial shape estimate

Let $K$ be a compact set of positive shapes and $M$ continuous on $K$ with unique maximizer $\theta_0 \in K$. If the averaged restricted log-likelihood $\theta \mapsto \bar\ell_n(\beta_0, \theta)$ of a sample converges to $M$ uniformly on $K$ in probability, and $\tilde\theta_n \in K$ with probability tending to one with $P(\bar\ell_n(\beta_0, \tilde\theta_n) < \bar\ell_n(\beta_0, \theta_0) - \delta) \to 0$ for every $\delta > 0$, then $\tilde\theta_n \to \theta_0$ in probability.
