# Block-diagonal operators preserve the coefficient block

Let $I$ be a symmetric operator on the joint parameter space of $(\beta, \theta)$ with vanishing cross term $\langle I(0, 1), (t, 0)\rangle = 0$ for every $t$. Then $I(u, 0) = (Bu, 0)$ for every $u$, where $B$ is the coefficient block of $I$.
