# Wald test for negative binomial coefficients with observed information

Consider negative binomial regression with joint parameter $p = (\beta, \theta)$, coefficients $\beta$ and shape $\theta$: an observation $z = (x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$) has density $f_p(z) = \mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$, with closed-form score $s_p(z)$ and observed information $J_p(z)$. Let $z_i = (x_i, y_i)$ be mutually independent observations on a probability space with covariates of an s-finite law $\mu_X$ and counts conditionally $\mathrm{NB}(\theta_0, e^{x_i^\top\beta_0})$, $\theta_0 > 0$ (reference measure $\mu_X \otimes$ counting measure), and let $I(p_0)$ be the Fisher information. Let $0 < \delta \le \theta_0$ and let $M$ be a measurable function, integrable under the law of $z_i$, with $\|J_p(z) - J_{p_0}(z)\| \le M(z)\|p - p_0\|$ for every $z$ and every $p$ within distance $\delta$ of $p_0$; let $J_{p_0}$ be integrable with mean $I(p_0)$, and let $s_{p_0}$ be square-integrable with mean $0$. Let $\hat p_n$ be almost everywhere measurable and consistent for $p_0$, with $\sum_{i<n}s_{\hat p_n}(z_i) = 0$ with probability tending to one. Assume $|\kappa| \ge 1$ and $I(p_0)$ is positive definite and let $\hat B_n$ be the coefficient block of $n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}J_{\hat p_n}(z_i)$. Then $n\langle\hat B_n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0), \hat\beta_n - \beta_0\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa|$ degrees of freedom.
