# Density of negative binomial regression

For a joint parameter $(\beta, \theta)$ and an observation $(x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$), the negative binomial regression density with respect to (law of $x$) $\otimes$ (counting measure) is the conditional mass $\mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$ with shape $\theta$ and mean $e^{x^\top\beta}$.
