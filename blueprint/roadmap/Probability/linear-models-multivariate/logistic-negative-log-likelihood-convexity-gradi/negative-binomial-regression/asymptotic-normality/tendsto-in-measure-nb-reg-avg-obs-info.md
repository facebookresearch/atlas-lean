# Consistency of the observed information at the estimator

Consider negative binomial regression with joint parameter $p = (\beta, \theta)$, coefficients $\beta$ and shape $\theta$: an observation $z = (x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$) has density $f_p(z) = \mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$, with closed-form score $s_p(z)$ and observed information $J_p(z)$. Let $z_i = (x_i, y_i)$ be mutually independent observations on a probability space with law $f_{p_0}\,\nu$ for a reference measure $\nu$ and true shape $\theta_0 > 0$, and let $I(p_0)$ be the Fisher information. Assume, for some $0 < \delta \le \theta_0$: $\|J_p(z) - J_{p_0}(z)\| \le M(z)\|p - p_0\|$ on $B(p_0, \delta)$ for a measurable integrable $M$; and $J_{p_0}$ is integrable with mean $I(p_0)$. Let $\hat p_n$ be almost everywhere measurable and consistent for $p_0$. Then $n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}J_{\hat p_n}(z_i) \to I(p_0)$ in probability.
