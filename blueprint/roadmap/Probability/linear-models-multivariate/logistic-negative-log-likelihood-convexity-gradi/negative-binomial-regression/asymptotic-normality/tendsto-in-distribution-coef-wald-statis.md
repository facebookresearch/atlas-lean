# Wald test for negative binomial coefficients with an estimated shape

Consider negative binomial regression with joint parameter $p = (\beta, \theta)$, coefficients $\beta$ and shape $\theta$: an observation $z = (x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$) has density $f_p(z) = \mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$, with closed-form score $s_p(z)$ and observed information $J_p(z)$. Let $z_i = (x_i, y_i)$ be mutually independent observations on a probability space with law $f_{p_0}\,\nu$ for a reference measure $\nu$ and true shape $\theta_0 > 0$, and let $I(p_0)$ be the Fisher information. Assume, for some $0 < \delta \le \theta_0$: $\|J_p(z) - J_{p_0}(z)\| \le M(z)\|p - p_0\|$ on $B(p_0, \delta)$ for a measurable integrable $M$; $s_{p_0}$ is square integrable with mean zero; $J_{p_0}$ is integrable with mean $I(p_0)$; and $I(p_0)$ is invertible. Let $\hat p_n$ be almost everywhere measurable, consistent for $p_0$, and solve $\sum_{i<n}s_{\hat p_n}(z_i) = 0$ with probability tending to one. Assume $|\kappa| \ge 1$ and $I(p_0)$ is positive definite with vanishing cross information $\langle I(p_0)(0, 1), (t, 0)\rangle = 0$, and let $\hat B_n$ be almost everywhere measurable estimators of the coefficient block $B$ of $I(p_0)$, consistent in probability. Then $n\langle\hat B_n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0), \hat\beta_n - \beta_0\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa|$ degrees of freedom.
