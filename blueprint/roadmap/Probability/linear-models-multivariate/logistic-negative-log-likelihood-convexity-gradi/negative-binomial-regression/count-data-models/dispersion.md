# Dispersion of a count law

The dispersion of a count law is the coefficient $\alpha$ of its mean–variance relation $V(\mu) = \mu + \alpha\mu^2$: $\alpha = 0$ for the Poisson law and $\alpha = 1/\theta$ for the negative binomial law with shape $\theta$. With $1/0 = 0$, the negative binomial law with shape $0$ has dispersion $0$.
