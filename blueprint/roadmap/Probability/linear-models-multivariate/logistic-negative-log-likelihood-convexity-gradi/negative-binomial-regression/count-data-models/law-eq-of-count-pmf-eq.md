# The law of an entry with positive mean determines the unit’s count law

For two valid count-matrix models, if sample $j$ has positive exposure in the first and unit $g$ has the same count law in sample $j$ in both, then $g$ has the same count law (Poisson, or negative binomial with the same shape) in both: the mean $\mu$ and variance $\mu + \alpha\mu^2$ fix the dispersion $\alpha$.
