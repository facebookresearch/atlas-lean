# Strict exogeneity gives the within estimating equation

Let $X_0$ be a random real $T \times k$ matrix and $u_0$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^T$ on a measure space with measure $P$ such that every product $(X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s$ is integrable with $\int (X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s\,dP = 0$. Then $\int (QX_0)^\top Qu_0\,dP = 0$, where $Q = I - T^{-1}J$, with $J$ the $T \times T$ all-ones matrix, is the time-demeaning matrix.
