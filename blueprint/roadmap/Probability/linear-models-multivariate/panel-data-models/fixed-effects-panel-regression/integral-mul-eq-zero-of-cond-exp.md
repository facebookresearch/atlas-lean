# Conditional strict exogeneity gives a zero cross moment

Let $X_0$ be a measurable random real $T \times k$ matrix and $u_0$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^T$ on a finite measure space, and fix periods $r, s$ and a column $j$. If $(u_0)_s$ and $(X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s$ are integrable and $\mathbb{E}[(u_0)_s \mid X_0] = 0$ almost everywhere, then $\int (X_0)_{rj}(u_0)_s\,dP = 0$, where $P$ is the measure.
