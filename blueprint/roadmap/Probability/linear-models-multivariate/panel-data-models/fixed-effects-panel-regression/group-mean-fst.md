# Group means of a product grouped by its first coordinate

For finite sets $A$ and $\tau$ (with $|\tau| = T$), $x \in \mathbb{R}^{A \times \tau}$ and $a \in A$, the mean of $x$ over the group $\{(a, t) : t \in \tau\}$ is $\frac1T\sum_t x_{(a,t)}$, which is $0$ when $T = 0$ (division by zero gives $0$).
