# Time-demeaning as the within transformation of a vector

Let $\tau$ be a finite set of $T$ periods and $Q = I - T^{-1}J$ the time-demeaning matrix ($J$ the all-ones matrix). For every $v \in \mathbb{R}^\tau$, $Qv$ is the within transformation of $v$ with all periods in one group: $(Qv)_t = v_t - \frac1T\sum_s v_s$.
