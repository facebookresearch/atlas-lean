# Model-based limit of the count generalized estimating equation

For clusters $z = (x, y)$ with covariate vectors $x_t \in E$ (a finite-dimensional real inner product space) and counts $y_t$ over finitely many occasions $t$, let $\mu_t(\beta) = e^{\langle x_t, \beta\rangle}$, $v(m) = m + \alpha m^2$ the negative binomial working variance with dispersion $\alpha \ge 0$, $w_t = v(\mu_t)^{-1/2}$ and $Q$ an inverse working correlation matrix; the count GEE score is $\psi(\beta; z) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s(y_t - \mu_t)x_s$ and the model-based information is $\mathcal I(\beta; x) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s\mu_t\,x_sx_t^\top$. Let clusters $z_i$ be mutually independent random elements of a probability space, with law $\mu_x \otimes \kappa$ ($\mu_x$ a probability measure), with conditionally integrable counts and correct log-link conditional mean at $\beta_0$ ($\int y_t\,d\kappa(x) = \mu_t(\beta_0)$ for almost every $x$), any dispersion $\alpha \ge 0$ and an inverse working correlation $Q$. Suppose the counts are conditionally square-integrable with conditional second moments about $\mu(\beta_0)$ equal to $v(\mu_s)^{1/2}v(\mu_t)^{1/2}R_{st}$, where $QR = I$. Assume $\delta > 0$ and a measurable integrable envelope $M$ with $\|\partial_\beta\psi(\beta; z) - \partial_\beta\psi(\beta_0; z)\| \le M(z)\|\beta - \beta_0\|$ for all $z$ and all $\beta$ within $\delta$ of $\beta_0$; that $\|\psi(\beta_0; z)\|^2$ is integrable; that $\partial_\beta\psi(\beta_0; z)$ is strongly measurable and integrable; and that $\mathcal I(\beta_0; x)$ is integrable, with $A = \mathbb{E}[\mathcal I(\beta_0; x)]$ invertible. Let $\hat\beta_n$ be almost everywhere measurable and consistent, with $\sum_{i<n}\psi(\hat\beta_n; z_i) = 0$ with probability tending to one, and let $Y$ be almost everywhere measurable. Then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0)$ converges in distribution to $A^{-1}Y$ with $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle At, t\rangle)$: the limiting covariance is the model-based $A^{-1}$.
