# Consistency of a root of a cluster estimating equation

Let $E$ be a real inner product space, $U_i(\beta)$ cluster scores on a measure space with measure $P$, and $K \subseteq E$ compact. Suppose $n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}U_i(\beta) \to g(\beta)$ uniformly on $K$ in measure, $g$ is continuous on $K$ and every zero of $g$ in $K$ equals $\beta_0$, and $P(\hat\beta_n \notin K) \to 0$ and $P(\sum_{i<n}U_i(\hat\beta_n) \ne 0) \to 0$ (outer measures). Then $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in measure.
