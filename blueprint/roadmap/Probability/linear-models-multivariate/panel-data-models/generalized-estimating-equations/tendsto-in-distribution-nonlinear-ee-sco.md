# Standardized score central limit theorem for cluster estimating functions

Let rows of $n$ clusters contribute $\psi_{n,i}(\beta)$ on a probability space, where at the truth the scores $\psi_{n,i}(\beta_0)$ are independent within rows, measurable, mean zero and bounded by $K m_{n,i}$ for some $K \ge 0$ ($m_{n,i}$ the cluster sizes), the row variance $B_n^2 = \sum_i\mathbb{E}\psi_{n,i}(\beta_0)^2$ is eventually positive and $KM_n/B_n \to 0$ with $M_n$ the largest cluster size. Then $\Psi_n(\beta_0)/B_n$ converges in distribution to a standard normal variable.
