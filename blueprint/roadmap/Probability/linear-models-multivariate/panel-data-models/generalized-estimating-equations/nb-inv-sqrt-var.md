# Working weight of the count generalized estimating equation

For a dispersion $\alpha$, coefficients $\beta$, covariates $x$ and an occasion $t$, the working weight is $w_t = v(\mu_t)^{-1/2}$ with $v(m) = m + \alpha m^2$ and $\mu_t = e^{\langle x_t, \beta\rangle}$. Here $\sqrt x = 0$ for $x \le 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$, so $w_t = 0$ when $v(\mu_t) \le 0$.
