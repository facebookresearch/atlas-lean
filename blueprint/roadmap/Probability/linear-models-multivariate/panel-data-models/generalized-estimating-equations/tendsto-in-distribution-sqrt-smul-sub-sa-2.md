# Generalized estimating equation asymptotics with estimated weights from differentiable scores

Let $U_i(\beta, \theta)$ be cluster scores at regression parameter $\beta$ and nuisance parameter $\theta$ (working correlation and scale), and $\hat\theta_n$ a random nuisance estimate. Assume the $U_i(\beta_0, \cdot)$ are differentiable on $B(\theta_0, \delta)$ for some $\delta > 0$ with derivatives $D_\theta U_i(\theta)$, $\sup_{\theta \in B(\theta_0, \delta)}\|n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}D_\theta U_i(\theta)\| \to 0$ in probability, and $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta_0)$ is bounded in probability. Suppose the scores are differentiable in $\beta$ at every $\beta$ within $\delta$ of $\beta_0$ for $\theta$ within $\delta$ of $\theta_0$, with averaged $\beta$-Jacobians converging to $\bar A$ uniformly on the $\delta$-ball about $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ in probability, $\bar A$ continuous at $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ and $\bar A(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ invertible. Assume the sums $\sum_{i<n}U_i(\beta_0, \hat\theta_n)$ are almost everywhere measurable and the scores $U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ are measurable, mutually independent, centred and square integrable, with $n^{-1}\sum_{k<n}\mathbb{E}\langle U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0), t\rangle^2 \to \langle Bt, t\rangle$ and the array $n^{-1/2}\langle U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0), t\rangle$ satisfying the Lindeberg condition, for every $t$. Let $\hat\beta_n$ be almost everywhere measurable and consistent with $\sum_{i<n}U_i(\hat\beta_n, \hat\theta_n) = 0$ with probability tending to one. Then $\sqrt n(\hat\beta_n - \beta_0)$ converges in distribution to any almost everywhere measurable $W$ with $\langle W, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle\Sigma t, t\rangle)$ for every $t$, where $\Sigma$ is the sandwich covariance of $\bar A(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ and $B$.
