# Central limit theorem for the estimated-weight score

Let $U_i(\beta, \theta)$ be cluster scores at regression parameter $\beta$ and nuisance parameter $\theta$ (working correlation and scale), and $\hat\theta_n$ a random nuisance estimate. Assume the $U_i(\beta_0, \cdot)$ are differentiable on $B(\theta_0, \delta)$ for some $\delta > 0$ with derivatives $D_\theta U_i(\theta)$, $\sup_{\theta \in B(\theta_0, \delta)}\|n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}D_\theta U_i(\theta)\| \to 0$ in probability, and $\sqrt n(\hat\theta_n - \theta_0)$ is bounded in probability. Assume the sums $\sum_{i<n}U_i(\beta_0, \hat\theta_n)$ are almost everywhere measurable, and that the scores $U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0)$ are measurable, mutually independent, centred and square integrable, with $n^{-1}\sum_{k<n}\mathbb{E}\langle U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0), t\rangle^2 \to q(t)$ and the array $n^{-1/2}\langle U_k(\beta_0, \theta_0), t\rangle$ satisfying the Lindeberg condition, for every $t$. Then $n^{-1/2}\sum_{i<n}U_i(\beta_0, \hat\theta_n)$ converges in distribution to any almost everywhere measurable $Y$ with $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, q(t))$.
