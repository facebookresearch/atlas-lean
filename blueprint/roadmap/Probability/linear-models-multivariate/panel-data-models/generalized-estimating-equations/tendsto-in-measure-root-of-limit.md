# Consistency of a root from a limiting estimating function

Let $\beta \mapsto \Psi_n(\beta)$ be nonincreasing random functions on $\mathbb{R}$, $\hat\beta_n$ a root with probability tending to one, $a_n > 0$ eventually, and suppose $\Psi_n(\beta)/a_n \to \lambda(\beta)$ in probability for each $\beta$, with $\lambda(\beta_0 - \varepsilon) > 0 > \lambda(\beta_0 + \varepsilon)$ for every $\varepsilon > 0$. Then $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in probability.
