# Existence of a root with probability tending to one

Let $\beta \mapsto \Psi_n(\beta)$ be continuous random functions on $\mathbb{R}$ with $\Psi_n(\beta_0 - \varepsilon) > 0 > \Psi_n(\beta_0 + \varepsilon)$ with probability tending to one, for some $\varepsilon > 0$. Then with probability tending to one $\Psi_n$ has a root in $(\beta_0 - \varepsilon, \beta_0 + \varepsilon)$.
