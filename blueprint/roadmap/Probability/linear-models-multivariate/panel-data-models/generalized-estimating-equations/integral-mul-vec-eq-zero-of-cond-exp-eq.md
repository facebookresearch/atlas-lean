# Pull-out form of unbiasedness

Let $\mathcal G$ be a sub-$\sigma$-algebra on a finite measure space, $W$ a random matrix with $\mathcal G$-measurable entries and $R$ a random vector with integrable entries, $W_{kt}R_t$ integrable and $\mathbb{E}[R_t \mid \mathcal G] = 0$. Then $\mathbb{E}[WR] = 0$.
