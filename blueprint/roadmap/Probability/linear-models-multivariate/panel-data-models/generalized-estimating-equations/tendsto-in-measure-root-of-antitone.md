# Consistency of a root of a monotone estimating function

Let $\beta \mapsto \Psi_n(\beta)$ be nonincreasing random functions on $\mathbb{R}$, $\hat\beta_n$ a root of $\Psi_n$ with probability tending to one, and suppose that for every $\varepsilon > 0$, $\Psi_n(\beta_0 - \varepsilon) > 0$ and $\Psi_n(\beta_0 + \varepsilon) < 0$ with probability tending to one. Then $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in probability; no continuity in $\beta$ is needed.
