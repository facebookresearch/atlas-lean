# Meat equals bread for a correct working covariance

For clusters $z = (x, y)$ with covariate vectors $x_t \in E$ (a finite-dimensional real inner product space) and counts $y_t$ over finitely many occasions $t$, let $\mu_t(\beta) = e^{\langle x_t, \beta\rangle}$, $v(m) = m + \alpha m^2$ the negative binomial working variance with dispersion $\alpha \ge 0$, $w_t = v(\mu_t)^{-1/2}$ and $Q$ an inverse working correlation matrix; the count GEE score is $\psi(\beta; z) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s(y_t - \mu_t)x_s$ and the model-based information is $\mathcal I(\beta; x) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s\mu_t\,x_sx_t^\top$. Let clusters have law $\mu_x \otimes \kappa$ (covariates of an s-finite law $\mu_x$, counts from a Markov kernel $\kappa$) with square-integrable counts whose conditional second moments about $\mu(\beta_0)$ are $v(\mu_s)^{1/2}v(\mu_t)^{1/2}R_{st}$, $QR = I$. If, for a vector $u$, $\langle\psi(\beta_0; z), u\rangle^2$ and $\mathcal I(\beta_0; x)$ are integrable under $\mu_x \otimes \kappa$, then $\mathbb{E}\langle\psi(\beta_0; z), u\rangle^2 = \langle\mathbb{E}[\mathcal I(\beta_0; x)]u, u\rangle$.
