# Central limit theorem for independent clusters

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space. Let $U_k$ be measurable, mutually independent, mean-zero, square-integrable random vectors in $E$ on a probability space. Suppose that for every $t$, $n^{-1}\sum_{k<n}\mathbb{E}\langle U_k, t\rangle^2 \to q(t)$ and the array $n^{-1/2}\langle U_k, t\rangle$, $k < n$, satisfies the Lindeberg condition. Then $n^{-1/2}\sum_{k<n}U_k$ converges in distribution to any $Y$ with $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, q(t))$ for every $t$.
