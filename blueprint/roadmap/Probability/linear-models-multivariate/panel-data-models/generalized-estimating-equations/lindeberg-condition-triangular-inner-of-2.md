# The norm Lindeberg condition implies the projected one

Let $E$ be a real inner product space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. Let $U_k$ be measurable and square integrable with $n^{-1}\sum_{k<n}\mathbb{E}\big[\|U_k\|^2\mathbf 1\{\|U_k\| > \varepsilon\sqrt n\}\big] \to 0$ for every $\varepsilon > 0$. Then for every $t$ the array $n^{-1/2}\langle U_k, t\rangle$, $k < n$, satisfies the Lindeberg condition.
