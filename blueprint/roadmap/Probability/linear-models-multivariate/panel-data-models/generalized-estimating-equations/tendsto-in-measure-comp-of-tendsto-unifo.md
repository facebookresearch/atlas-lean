# Plug-in consistency

Let random functions $\Phi_n(\beta)$ with values in a pseudometric space converge to $\varphi(\beta)$ uniformly on a ball around $\beta_0$ in probability, with $\varphi$ continuous at $\beta_0$, and let $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in probability. Then $\Phi_n(\hat\beta_n) \to \varphi(\beta_0)$ in probability.
