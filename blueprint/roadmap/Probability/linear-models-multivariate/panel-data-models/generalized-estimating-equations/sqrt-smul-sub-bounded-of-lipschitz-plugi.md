# Plug-in of a root-n consistent argument into a locally Lipschitz estimator

Suppose $\|\hat a_n - \tilde a_n\| \le H_n\|\hat b_n - b_0\|$, where $H_n$ is bounded in probability and $\sqrt n(\hat b_n - b_0)$ is bounded in probability. If $\sqrt n(\tilde a_n - a_0)$ is bounded in probability, so is $\sqrt n(\hat a_n - a_0)$.
