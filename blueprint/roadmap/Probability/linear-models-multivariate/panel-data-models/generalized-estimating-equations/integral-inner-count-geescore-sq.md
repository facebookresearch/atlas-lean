# Meat equals bread for a correct working covariance, one cluster

For clusters $z = (x, y)$ with covariate vectors $x_t \in E$ (a real inner product space) and counts $y_t$ over finitely many occasions $t$, let $\mu_t(\beta) = e^{\langle x_t, \beta\rangle}$, $v(m) = m + \alpha m^2$ the negative binomial working variance with dispersion $\alpha \ge 0$, $w_t = v(\mu_t)^{-1/2}$ and $Q$ an inverse working correlation matrix; the count GEE score is $\psi(\beta; z) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s(y_t - \mu_t)x_s$ and the model-based information is $\mathcal I(\beta; x) = \sum_{s,t}Q_{st}w_sw_t\mu_s\mu_t\,x_sx_t^\top$. Fix covariates $x$ and a finite measure $\nu$ on counts with square-integrable coordinates, and suppose $\int(y_s - \mu_s)(y_t - \mu_t)\,d\nu = v(\mu_s)^{1/2}v(\mu_t)^{1/2}R_{st}$ with $QR = I$. Then $\int\langle\psi, u\rangle^2d\nu = \langle\mathcal I(\beta; x)u, u\rangle$ for every $u$.
