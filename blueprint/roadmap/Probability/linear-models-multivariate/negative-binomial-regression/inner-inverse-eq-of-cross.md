# The inverse-information quadratic form splits

Let $I$ be symmetric positive definite on the joint parameter space of $(\beta, \theta)$ with $\langle I(0, 1), (t, 0)\rangle = 0$ for every $t$, $B$ its coefficient block and $D = \langle I(0, 1), (0, 1)\rangle$. Then for every $s = (s_\beta, s_\theta)$, $\langle I^{-1}s, s\rangle = \langle B^{-1}s_\beta, s_\beta\rangle + s_\theta^2/D$.
