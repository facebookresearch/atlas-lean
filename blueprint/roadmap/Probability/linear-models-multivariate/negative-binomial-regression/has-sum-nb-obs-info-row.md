# Expected information of one negative binomial regression observation

For $\theta > 0$, a covariate row $x$ and every $\eta$, the observed information matrix of one observation averaged over $Y \sim \mathrm{NB}(\theta, e^\eta)$ is the block-diagonal Fisher information $\begin{pmatrix}F\,xx^\top & 0\\ 0 & v_F\end{pmatrix}$.
