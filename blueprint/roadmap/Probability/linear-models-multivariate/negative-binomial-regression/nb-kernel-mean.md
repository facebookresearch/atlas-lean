# Negative binomial kernel on the mean scale

For a shape $\theta$, a response $y$ and a mean $\mu$, the mean-scale negative binomial kernel is $y\log\mu - (y + \theta)\log(\theta + \mu)$. For any real $\theta$ and $\mu$; here $\log$ is the real logarithm extended by $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$.
