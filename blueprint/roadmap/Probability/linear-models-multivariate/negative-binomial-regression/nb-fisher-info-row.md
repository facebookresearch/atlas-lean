# Fisher information of one negative binomial regression observation

For a shape $\theta$, a covariate row $x$ and a linear predictor $\eta$, the expected information in $(\beta, \theta)$ is the block-diagonal matrix $\begin{pmatrix}F\,xx^\top & 0\\ 0 & v_F\end{pmatrix}$ with $F = \theta\mu/(\theta + \mu)$ and $v_F$ the Fisher shape information, with $\mu = e^\eta$.
