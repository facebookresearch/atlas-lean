# Joint derivative of the negative binomial score

For $\theta > 0$ and real $y, \eta$, the score $\theta(y - \mu)/(\theta + \mu)$ (with $\mu = e^\eta$) is differentiable in $(\theta, \eta)$ with partial derivatives $(y - \mu)\mu/(\theta + \mu)^2$ in $\theta$ and $-\theta(y + \theta)\mu/(\theta + \mu)^2$ in $\eta$.
