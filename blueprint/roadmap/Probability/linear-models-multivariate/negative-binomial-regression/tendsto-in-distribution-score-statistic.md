# Score test for the joint negative binomial parameter

Consider negative binomial regression with joint parameter $p = (\beta, \theta)$, coefficients $\beta$ and shape $\theta$: an observation $z = (x, y)$ (covariate row $x$, count $y$) has density $f_p(z) = \mathrm{NB}(y; \theta, e^{x^\top\beta})$, with closed-form score $s_p(z)$ and observed information $J_p(z)$. Let $z_i$ be mutually independent observations with law $f_{p_0}\,\nu$, $\theta_0 > 0$, $s_{p_0}$ square integrable with mean zero, and $I(p_0)$ positive definite; let $\hat I_n$ be almost everywhere measurable estimators converging to $I(p_0)$ in probability. With $S_n = n^{-1/2}\sum_{i<n}s_{p_0}(z_i)$, the score statistic $\langle\hat I_n^{-1}S_n, S_n\rangle$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa| + 1$ degrees of freedom ($|\kappa|$ coefficients plus the shape).
