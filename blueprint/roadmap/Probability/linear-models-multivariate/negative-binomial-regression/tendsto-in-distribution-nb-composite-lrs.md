# Composite likelihood-ratio test with the shape free

For a sample with joint estimates $\hat p_n$, restricted estimates $\tilde\theta_n$ and scaled coefficient and shape scores $T_n, U_n$ at the truth $(\beta_0, \theta_0)$, let $I = \begin{pmatrix}A & 0\\ 0 & D\end{pmatrix}$ with $A$ positive definite and $D \ne 0$. Assume $2(\ell_n(\hat p_n) - \ell_n(\beta_0, \theta_0)) - s_n^\top I^{-1}s_n \to 0$ with $s_n = (T_n, U_n)$, $2(\ell_n(\beta_0, \tilde\theta_n) - \ell_n(\beta_0, \theta_0)) - U_n^2/D \to 0$ in probability, $T_n \to V \sim \mathcal N(0, A)$ in distribution, the statistic is almost everywhere measurable and there is at least one coefficient. Then $2(\ell_n(\hat p_n) - \ell_n(\beta_0, \tilde\theta_n))$ converges in distribution to the chi-square law with $|\kappa|$ degrees of freedom.
