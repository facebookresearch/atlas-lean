# The inverse joint Fisher quadratic form splits

For a shape $\theta$, design $X$, offset $o$ and coefficients $\beta$, if $A = X^\top\operatorname{diag}(F_i)X$ is invertible and the total shape information $D$ is nonzero, then for $s = (T, U)$, $s^\top I^{-1}s = T^\top A^{-1}T + U^2/D$, with $I$ the joint negative binomial Fisher information.
