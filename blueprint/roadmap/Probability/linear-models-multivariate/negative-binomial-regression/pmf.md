# Mass function of a count law at a given mean

At mean $\mu \ge 0$, the Poisson count law has mass $e^{-\mu}\mu^k/k!$ at $k$, and the negative binomial law with shape $\theta$ has mass $\frac{\Gamma(k + \theta)}{k!\,\Gamma(\theta)}\big(\frac{\theta}{\theta + \mu}\big)^\theta\big(\frac{\mu}{\theta + \mu}\big)^k$.
