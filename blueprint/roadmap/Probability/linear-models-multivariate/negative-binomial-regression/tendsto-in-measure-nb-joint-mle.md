# Consistency of the joint negative binomial maximum-likelihood estimate

Let $K$ be a compact set of pairs $(\beta, \theta)$, all with $\theta > 0$, and $M$ a criterion continuous on $K$ with unique maximizer $p_0 \in K$. Suppose the averaged joint negative binomial log-likelihood $\bar\ell_n$ of a sample converges to $M$ uniformly on $K$ in probability, and $\hat p_n \in K$ with probability tending to one with $P(\bar\ell_n(\hat p_n) < \bar\ell_n(p_0) - \delta) \to 0$ for every $\delta > 0$ (outer probabilities). Then $\hat p_n \to p_0$ in probability.
