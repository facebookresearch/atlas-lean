# The coefficient projection of a Gaussian vector

If $Y$ is a random vector on the joint parameter space of $(\beta, \theta)$ with $\langle Y, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle It, t\rangle)$ for every $t$, then its coefficient block $Y_\beta$ satisfies $\langle Y_\beta, t\rangle \sim \mathcal N(0, \langle Bt, t\rangle)$, with $B$ the coefficient block of $I$.
