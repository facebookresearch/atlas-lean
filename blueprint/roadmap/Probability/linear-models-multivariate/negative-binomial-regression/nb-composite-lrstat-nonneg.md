# Nonnegativity of the composite likelihood-ratio statistic

For a design $X$, offset $o$ and counts $y$, let $\ell(\beta, \theta)$ be the joint negative binomial log-likelihood, $\ell_0(\theta) = \ell(\beta_0, \theta)$ its restriction to the null $\beta = \beta_0$, and $\ell_{\mathrm{prof}}(\theta) = \sup_\beta\ell(\beta, \theta)$ the profile log-likelihood of the shape. If $\hat p$ maximizes $\ell$ over $\mathbb{R}^p \times (0, \infty)$ and $\tilde\theta > 0$, then $2(\ell(\hat p) - \ell(\beta_0, \tilde\theta)) \ge 0$.
