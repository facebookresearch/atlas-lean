# The residual sum of squares of a saturated design vanishes

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix with $m = k$ and $X^\top X$ invertible, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$, and $\varepsilon$ any random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space. Then $\|y - X\hat\beta(y)\|^2$, $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$, has the Dirac law at $0$ (not a chi-square law).
