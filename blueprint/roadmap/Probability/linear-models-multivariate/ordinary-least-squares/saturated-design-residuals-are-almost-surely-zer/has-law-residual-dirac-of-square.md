# Residuals of a saturated design vanish

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix with $m = k$ and $X^\top X$ invertible, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$, and $\varepsilon$ any random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space. Then the residual vector $y - X\hat\beta(y)$, $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$, has the Dirac law at $0$.
