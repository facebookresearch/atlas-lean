# Conditional exact coverage of the contrast t statistic

Let $X$ be a random real $m \times k$ design matrix and $\varepsilon$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space, both almost everywhere measurable, let $\sigma > 0$, and suppose the conditional law of $\sigma^{-1}\varepsilon$ given $X$ is almost surely the standard Gaussian law $\mathcal N(0, 1)^{\otimes m}$. Let $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$, and call a design $x$ full rank when $x^\top x$ is invertible. If $k < m$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}^k$ is nonzero, then for every $c$ and almost every full-rank $x$, $P(|T| \le c \mid X = x) = P(-c \le T_{m-k} \le c)$, where $T$ is the contrast $t$ statistic $T = (a^\top\hat\beta - a^\top\beta)/\sqrt{s^2\,a^\top(X^\top X)^{-1}a}$, $s^2 = \|y - X\hat\beta\|^2/(m - k)$, and $T_{m-k}$ is Student's $t$ with $m - k$ degrees of freedom.
