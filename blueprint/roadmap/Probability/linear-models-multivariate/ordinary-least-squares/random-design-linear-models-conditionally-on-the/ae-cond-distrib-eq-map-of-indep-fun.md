# Conditional law of a function of an independent pair

Let $X$ and $\varepsilon$ be independent, almost everywhere measurable random elements of measurable spaces $D$ and $E$ on a probability space, and let $g : D \times E \to \Gamma$ be jointly measurable, with $\Gamma$ a nonempty standard Borel space. Then for almost every $x$ with respect to the law of $X$, the conditional law of $g(X, \varepsilon)$ given $X = x$ is the image of the law of $\varepsilon$ under $e \mapsto g(x, e)$.
