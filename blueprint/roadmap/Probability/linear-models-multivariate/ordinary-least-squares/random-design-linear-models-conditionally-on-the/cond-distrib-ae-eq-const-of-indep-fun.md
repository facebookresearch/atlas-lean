# Independence gives a constant conditional law

Let $X$ be an almost everywhere measurable random element of a measurable space $D$ and $Z$ a random element of a nonempty standard Borel space $E$ on a probability space. If $Z$ is independent of $X$ and has law $\rho$, then the conditional law of $Z$ given $X$ is $\rho$ almost surely (with respect to the law of $X$).
