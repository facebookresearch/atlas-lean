# A constant conditional law gives independence

Let $X$ and $Z$ be almost everywhere measurable random elements of a measurable space $D$ and a nonempty standard Borel space $E$ on a probability space, and $\rho$ a probability measure on $E$. If the conditional law of $Z$ given $X$ is almost surely $\rho$, then $Z$ is independent of $X$ and has law $\rho$.
