# Probability of a joint event under independence and a constant conditional law

Let $X$ and $\varepsilon$ be independent, almost everywhere measurable random elements of measurable spaces $D$ and $E$ on a probability space, $g : D \times E \to \Gamma$ jointly measurable, $F \subseteq D$ and $S \subseteq \Gamma$ measurable, and $\nu$ a measure on $\Gamma$ such that $g(x, \varepsilon)$ has law $\nu$ for every $x \in F$. Then

$$P\big(X \in F,\ g(X, \varepsilon) \in S\big) = P(X \in F)\,\nu(S).$$
