# Coverage of the one-sample t interval

Let $n \ge 2$, $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$, and let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ be i.i.d. $\mathcal N(\mu, v)$ on a probability space (the vector $X$ has law $\mathcal N(\mu, v)^{\otimes n}$). Let $\alpha \in (0,1)$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$ with $P(-c \le T \le c) \ge 1 - \alpha$ for $T$ Student's $t$ with $n - 1$ degrees of freedom. With $\bar X = \frac1n\sum_i X_i$ and $S^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_i (X_i - \bar X)^2$,

$$P\Big(|\bar X - \mu| \le \frac{cS}{\sqrt n}\Big) \ge 1 - \alpha.$$
