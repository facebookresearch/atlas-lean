# Coverage of the contrast t test acceptance region

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ design matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible and $k < m$, and consider the linear model $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ with $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and a random error vector $\varepsilon$ in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space. Write $\hat\beta(y) = (X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top y$ for the ordinary least-squares estimator. Suppose $\sigma > 0$ and $\sigma^{-1}\varepsilon$ is a standard Gaussian vector (independent standard normal coordinates). Let $a \in \mathbb{R}^k$ be nonzero, $\alpha \in [0,1]$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$ with $P(-c \le T \le c) \ge 1 - \alpha$ for $T$ Student's $t$ with $m - k$ degrees of freedom. Write $T_a(y) = (a^\top\hat\beta(y) - a^\top\beta)/\sqrt{s^2(y)\, a^\top(X^\top X)^{-1}a}$ with $s^2(y) = \|y - X\hat\beta(y)\|^2/(m - k)$. Then the law of $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ gives the set $\{y : |T_a(y)| \le c\}$ probability at least $1 - \alpha$.
