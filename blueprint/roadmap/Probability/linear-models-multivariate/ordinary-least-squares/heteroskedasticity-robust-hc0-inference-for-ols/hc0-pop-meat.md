# Population HC0 meat

Let $x_0$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^p$ and $u_0$ a real random variable on a measure space $(\Omega, \mu)$. The population HC0 meat is the $p \times p$ matrix

$$\Omega_{ab} = \int_\Omega u_0^2\, x_{0,a}\, x_{0,b}\, d\mu,$$

where the integral of a non-integrable function is taken to be $0$.
