# The HC0 sandwich estimator is measurable

Let $(x_i)_{i \ge 0}$ be measurable random vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$ and $(y_i)_{i \ge 0}$ measurable real random variables. For every sample size $n$, the HC0 sandwich $\hat Q_n^{-1}\hat\Omega_n(\hat Q_n^{-1})^\top$ of the least-squares fit on the first $n$ observations (where the inverse of a singular matrix is taken to be the zero matrix) is a measurable function of the outcome.
