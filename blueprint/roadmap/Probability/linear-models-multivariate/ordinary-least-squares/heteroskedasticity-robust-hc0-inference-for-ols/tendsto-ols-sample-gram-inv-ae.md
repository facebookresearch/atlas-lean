# Almost sure convergence of the inverse sample Gram matrix

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space and $(x_i)_{i \ge 0}$ random vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$ that are pairwise independent and identically distributed, with every product $x_{0,a}\, x_{0,b}$ integrable and with invertible population Gram matrix $Q = \mathbb{E}[x_0 x_0^\top]$. Then, for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\hat Q_n(\omega)^{-1} \longrightarrow Q^{-1} \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\hat Q_n = \frac1n\sum_{i<n} x_i x_i^\top$ and the inverse of a singular matrix is taken to be the zero matrix.
