# A finite weighted second moment makes the score integrable

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure, $x_0$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^p$ and $u_0$ a real random variable such that the pair $(x_0, u_0)$ is almost everywhere measurable and $\mathbb{E}\big[\|x_0\|^2 u_0^2\big] < \infty$. Then the score $u_0\, x_0$ is integrable.
