# Strong consistency of a sandwich, entrywise

Let $\hat Q_n$ and $\hat M_n$ be random $k \times k$ matrices and $Q$, $M$ fixed $k \times k$ matrices such that for all $a, b$ almost surely $(\hat Q_n^{-1})_{ab} \to (Q^{-1})_{ab}$ and $(\hat M_n)_{ab} \to M_{ab}$ (matrix inverses, zero when singular). Then for all $a, b$ almost surely $(\hat Q_n^{-1}\hat M_n\hat Q_n^{-\top})_{ab} \to (Q^{-1}MQ^{-\top})_{ab}$. No symmetry of $\hat Q_n$ is assumed.
