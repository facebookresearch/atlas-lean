# Factorial effects from ±1 coding in a balanced two-level design

In a full $2^k$ factorial design with $r \ge 1$ replicates of every run, code each factor as $\pm 1$ and form the main-effect and interaction columns as products of these codes. The intercept and these $2^k - 1$ columns are mutually orthogonal, each of squared length $r2^k$. Hence the least-squares coefficient of the intercept is the grand mean, and the coefficient of each main-effect or interaction column is $(\bar y_+ - \bar y_-)/2$, half the difference between the mean response where the column is $+1$ and where it is $-1$, while the conventional effect estimate is $\bar y_+ - \bar y_-$, twice the coefficient.
