# Strong law for the sample covariance

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space and $(X_i, Y_i)_{i \ge 0}$ pairs of real random variables that are pairwise independent across $i$ and identically distributed, with $X_0$ and $Y_0$ square integrable. Then, almost surely,

$$\frac1n \sum_{i<n} (X_i - \bar X_n)(Y_i - \bar Y_n) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Cov}(X_0, Y_0) \qquad (n \to \infty),$$

where $\bar X_n$ and $\bar Y_n$ are the means of the first $n$ terms.
