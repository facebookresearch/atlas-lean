# Attenuation factor

For a signal variance $\sigma_\xi^2$ and a measurement-error variance $\sigma_u^2$, the attenuation factor (reliability ratio) of the classical errors-in-variables model is

$$\lambda = \frac{\sigma_\xi^2}{\sigma_\xi^2 + \sigma_u^2},$$

with the convention that a quotient by $0$ is $0$.
