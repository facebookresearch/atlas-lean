# Population attenuation identity for uncorrelated errors

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space and $X, e, \varepsilon$ square-integrable real random variables that are pairwise uncorrelated, and let $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$. For $Y = \alpha + \beta X + \varepsilon$ and the mismeasured regressor $W = X + e$,

$$\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(W, Y)}{\operatorname{Var}(W)} = \lambda\,\beta, \qquad \lambda = \frac{\operatorname{Var}(X)}{\operatorname{Var}(X) + \operatorname{Var}(e)},$$

with the convention that a quotient by $0$ is $0$ (so both sides vanish when $\operatorname{Var}(W) = 0$).
