# Normalized cross sum as mean of products minus product of means

For real sequences $(a_i)$, $(b_i)$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $\bar a$ and $\bar b$ the means of the first $n$ terms,

$$\frac1n \sum_{i<n} (a_i - \bar a)(b_i - \bar b) = \frac1n\sum_{i<n} a_i b_i - \Big(\frac1n\sum_{i<n} a_i\Big)\Big(\frac1n\sum_{i<n} b_i\Big),$$

with $1/0 = 0$.
