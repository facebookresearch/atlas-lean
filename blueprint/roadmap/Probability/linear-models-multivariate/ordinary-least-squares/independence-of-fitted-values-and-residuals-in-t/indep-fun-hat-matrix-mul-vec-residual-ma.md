# Fitted and residual images of a Gaussian vector are independent

Let $X$ be a real $n \times p$ design matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible, let $H = X (X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top$ be its hat matrix and $M = I - H$ its residual-maker matrix, and let $Z$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$ with independent standard normal coordinates. Then $H Z$ and $M Z$ are independent.
