# Error contrasts are unique up to an invertible matrix

Let $X$ be a real $n \times p$ design matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible, and let $A$ and $B$ be real $r \times n$ error contrasts for $X$ (each annihilates $X$, has full row rank, and has $r = n - p$ rows). Then there is a real $r \times r$ matrix $Q$ with $\det Q \neq 0$ and $B = Q A$.
