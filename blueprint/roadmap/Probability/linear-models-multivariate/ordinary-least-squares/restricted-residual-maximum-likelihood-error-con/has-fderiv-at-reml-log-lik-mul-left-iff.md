# Restricted log-likelihood derivatives do not depend on the contrast basis

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $V : E \to \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ a family of positive definite matrices, $A$ a real $r \times n$ matrix with $A A^\top$ invertible, $Q$ a real $r \times r$ matrix with $\det Q \neq 0$, and $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$. For every $\theta \in E$ and every continuous linear functional $L : E \to \mathbb{R}$, the map $\theta \mapsto \ell_{QA}(V(\theta); y)$ has derivative $L$ at $\theta$ if and only if $\theta \mapsto \ell_A(V(\theta); y)$ has derivative $L$ at $\theta$.
