# The restricted score does not depend on the contrast basis

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $V : E \to \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ a family of positive definite matrices, $A$ a real $r \times n$ matrix with $A A^\top$ invertible, $Q$ a real $r \times r$ matrix with $\det Q \neq 0$, and $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$. Then the derivatives of $\theta \mapsto \ell_{QA}(V(\theta); y)$ and $\theta \mapsto \ell_A(V(\theta); y)$ agree at every $\theta \in E$, with the convention that the derivative of a function not differentiable at a point is $0$.
