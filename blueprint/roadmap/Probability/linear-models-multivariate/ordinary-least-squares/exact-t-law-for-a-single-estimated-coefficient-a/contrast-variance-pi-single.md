# Contrast variance of a coordinate contrast

Let $X$ be a real $n \times p$ design matrix and $e_j \in \mathbb{R}^p$ the $j$-th standard basis vector. The contrast variance $e_j^\top (X^\top X)^{-1} e_j$ of the coordinate contrast $e_j$ equals the diagonal entry $\big[(X^\top X)^{-1}\big]_{jj}$, where the inverse of a singular matrix is taken to be the zero matrix.
