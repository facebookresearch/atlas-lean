# Saturated two-by-two regression design

For a sample with binary indicators $D_i$ and $T_i$, the design matrix of the saturated two-by-two regression $y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1D_i + \beta_2T_i + \beta_3D_iT_i + e_i$ has $i$-th row $(1, D_i, T_i, D_iT_i)$. In a difference-in-differences design $D$ marks the treated group and $T$ the post period.

## Sources

- J. D. Angrist and J.-S. Pischke, *Mostly Harmless Econometrics*, Princeton University Press (2009), §3.1.4 and §5.2.1.
