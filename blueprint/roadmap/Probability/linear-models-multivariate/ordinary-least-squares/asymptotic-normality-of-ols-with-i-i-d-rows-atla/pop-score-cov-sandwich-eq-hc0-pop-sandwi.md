# Asymptotic covariance of least squares as a sandwich

For a random regressor vector $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and a real error $u_0$ on a measure space, with $Q = \mathbb{E}[x_0x_0^\top]$ and $\Omega = \mathbb{E}[u_0^2x_0x_0^\top]$, the matrix $Q^{-1}\Omega Q^{-1}$ equals the population sandwich $Q^{-1}\Omega Q^{-\top}$ (matrix inverses, zero when singular).
