# Expected quadratic form of an error vector with scalar second moments

Let $\varepsilon$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space such that every product $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j$ is integrable with $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j] = \sigma^2\mathbf 1[i = j]$. Then for every real $m \times m$ matrix $M$,

$$\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon^\top M\varepsilon] = \sigma^2\operatorname{tr} M.$$

No symmetry, idempotence or positivity of $M$ is needed.
