# Expected residual quadratic form

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible and $k < m$, and let $\varepsilon$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space with square-integrable, mean-zero coordinates and $\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i, \varepsilon_j) = \sigma^2\mathbf 1[i = j]$. With the residual-maker matrix $M = I - X(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top$, $\mathbb{E}\big[\varepsilon^\top M\varepsilon/(m - k)\big] = \sigma^2$.
