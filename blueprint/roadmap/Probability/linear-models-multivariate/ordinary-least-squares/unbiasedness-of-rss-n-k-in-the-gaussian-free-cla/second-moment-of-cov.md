# Second moments of centred uncorrelated errors

Let $\varepsilon$ be a random vector with square-integrable coordinates on a probability space with $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_i] = 0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i, \varepsilon_j) = \sigma^2\mathbf 1[i = j]$ for all $i, j$. Then $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j] = \sigma^2\mathbf 1[i = j]$.
