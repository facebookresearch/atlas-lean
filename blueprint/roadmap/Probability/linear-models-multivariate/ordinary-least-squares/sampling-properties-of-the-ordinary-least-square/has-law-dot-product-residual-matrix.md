# Chi-squared law of the residual quadratic form

Let $X$ be an $n \times p$ design matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible and $p < n$, and let the errors
$\varepsilon_1, \dots, \varepsilon_n$ be independent standard normal. Then $\varepsilon^\top M \varepsilon$,
the quadratic form of the residual-maker matrix, has the chi-squared law with $n - p$ degrees of freedom.
