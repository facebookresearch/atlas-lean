# Unbiasedness of ordinary least squares, componentwise

Let $X^\top X$ be invertible and let the errors $\varepsilon_i$ be integrable with mean zero. Then each
component of the least-squares estimate $\hat\beta$ of $\beta$ in $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ has
expectation $\mathbb{E}[\hat\beta_j] = \beta_j$.
