# Law of the scaled residual sum of squares

In the linear model $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ with an $n \times p$ design $X$, $X^\top X$ invertible and
$p < n$, let $\sigma > 0$ and let the standardized errors $\varepsilon_i / \sigma$ be independent standard
normal. Then $\|y - X\hat\beta\|^2 / \sigma^2$ has the chi-squared law with $n - p$ degrees of freedom.
