# Population Gram matrix of a random regressor

Let $x_0$ be a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^p$ on a measure space $(\Omega, \mu)$. Its population Gram matrix is the $p \times p$ matrix with entries

$$Q_{ab} = \int_\Omega x_0(\omega)_a\, x_0(\omega)_b \, d\mu(\omega),$$

where the integral of a non-integrable function is taken to be $0$.
