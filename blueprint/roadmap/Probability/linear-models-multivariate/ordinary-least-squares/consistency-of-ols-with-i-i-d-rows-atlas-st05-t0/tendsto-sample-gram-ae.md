# Strong law for the sample Gram matrix

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space and $(x_i)_{i \ge 0}$ random vectors in $\mathbb{R}^p$ that are pairwise independent and identically distributed, with every product of coordinates $x_{0,a}\, x_{0,b}$ integrable. Then, for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i<n} x_i(\omega)\, x_i(\omega)^\top \longrightarrow Q = \mathbb{E}\big[x_0 x_0^\top\big] \qquad (n \to \infty)$$

entrywise.
