# Consistency of OLS

Let the pairs $(X_i, U_i)$, with $X_i \in \mathbb{R}^k$, be pairwise independent and identically distributed on a probability space, with integrable entries $X_{0a} X_{0b}$, integrable $U_0 X_0$, $E[U_0 X_0] = 0$, and invertible population Gram matrix $E[X_0 X_0^\top]$. Then for responses $y_i = X_i^\top \beta + U_i$, the OLS estimate from the first $n$ observations converges almost surely to $\beta$.
