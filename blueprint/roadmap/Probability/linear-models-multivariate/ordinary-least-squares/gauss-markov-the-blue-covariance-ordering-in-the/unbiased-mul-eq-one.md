# A linear estimator unbiased for every coefficient vector is a left inverse of the design

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix and $\varepsilon$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space with integrable, mean-zero coordinates. If a real $k \times m$ matrix $A$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[A(X\beta + \varepsilon)] = \beta$ for every $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$, then $AX = I_k$.
