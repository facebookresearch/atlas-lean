# Covariance of a linear estimator in the linear model

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ matrix, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$, and $\varepsilon$ a random vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space with square-integrable coordinates and $\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i, \varepsilon_j) = \sigma^2 \mathbf 1[i = j]$. For every real $k \times m$ matrix $A$ and all $a, b$,

$$\operatorname{Cov}\big((A(X\beta + \varepsilon))_a, (A(X\beta + \varepsilon))_b\big) = \sigma^2 (AA^\top)_{ab}.$$

No mean-zero assumption is needed.
