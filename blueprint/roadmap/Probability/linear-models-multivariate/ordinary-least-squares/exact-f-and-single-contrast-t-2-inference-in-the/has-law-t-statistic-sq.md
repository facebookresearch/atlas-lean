# The squared contrast t statistic has an F law

Let $X$ be a real $m \times k$ design matrix with $X^\top X$ invertible and $k < m$, and consider the linear model $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ with $\beta \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and a random error vector $\varepsilon$ in $\mathbb{R}^m$ on a probability space. Write $\hat\beta(y) = (X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top y$ for the ordinary least-squares estimator. Suppose $\sigma > 0$ and $\sigma^{-1}\varepsilon$ is a standard Gaussian vector (independent standard normal coordinates). For a nonzero $a \in \mathbb{R}^k$, the squared contrast $t$ statistic $\big(a^\top\hat\beta - a^\top\beta\big)^2/\big(s^2\, a^\top(X^\top X)^{-1}a\big)$, with $s^2 = \|y - X\hat\beta\|^2/(m - k)$ at $y = X\beta + \varepsilon$, has Snedecor's $F$ law with $(1, m - k)$ degrees of freedom.
