# Measurability of the F statistic

For a fixed real $m \times k$ design matrix $X$, $r \times k$ matrix $C$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}^r$, the $F$ statistic $F(y) = \dfrac{(C\hat\beta - c)^\top\big(C(X^\top X)^{-1}C^\top\big)^{-1}(C\hat\beta - c)/r}{\|y - X\hat\beta\|^2/(m - k)}$, $\hat\beta = \hat\beta(y) = (X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y$, with matrix inverses zero when singular, $x/0 = 0$ and truncated subtraction in $m - k$, is a measurable function of the response $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$.
