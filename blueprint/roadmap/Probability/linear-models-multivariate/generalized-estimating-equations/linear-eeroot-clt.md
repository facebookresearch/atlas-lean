# Central limit theorem for the linear cluster estimating equation root

If the cluster scores $S_i$ are mutually independent, identically distributed and square integrable, $m \ne 0$, and $Y \sim \mathcal N(0, \operatorname{Var}S_0)$, then $\sqrt n\big(\sum_{i<n}S_i/(nm) - \mathbb{E}[S_0]/m\big)$ converges in distribution to $Y/m$.
