# Cluster central limit theorem under the growth condition

Let rows of cluster scores $U_{n,i}$, $i < n$, on a probability space be independent within rows, measurable, mean zero and bounded by $Km_{n,i}$ for some $K \ge 0$, with $\mathbb{E}[U_{n,i}^2] \ge c\,m_{n,i}$ for some $c > 0$ and nonempty clusters. If $M_n^2/N_n \to 0$, then $\sum_{i<n}U_{n,i}/B_n$ converges in distribution to a standard normal variable.
