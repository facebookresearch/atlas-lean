# Identically distributed clusters satisfy the Lindeberg condition

Let $E$ be a real inner product space with its Borel $\sigma$-algebra. If random vectors $U_k$ are identically distributed with a square-integrable $U_0$, then for every $t$ the array $n^{-1/2}\langle U_k, t\rangle$, $k < n$, satisfies the Lindeberg condition.
