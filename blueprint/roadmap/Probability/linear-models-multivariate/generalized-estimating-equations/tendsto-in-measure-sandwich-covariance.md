# Consistency of the sandwich estimator

Write $\Sigma(A, B) = A^{-1}\frac{B + B^*}2A^{-*}$ for the sandwich covariance of a bread $A$ and a meat $B$ (with $A^{-1} = 0$ for singular $A$). If $\hat A_n \to A$ in probability with $A$ invertible and $\hat B_n \to B$ in probability, then $\Sigma(\hat A_n, \hat B_n) \to \Sigma(A, B)$ in probability.
