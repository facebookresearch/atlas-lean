# Consistency of the empirical sandwich estimator

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner product space. Write $\Sigma(A, B) = A^{-1}\frac{B + B^*}2A^{-*}$ for the sandwich covariance of a bread $A$ and a meat $B$ (with $A^{-1} = 0$ for singular $A$). Let $U_i(\beta)$ be the cluster scores and $DU_i(\beta)$ operators on $E$ (in applications, the Jacobians of the scores); suppose $n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}DU_i(\beta) \to \bar A(\beta)$ and $n^{-1}\sum_{i<n}U_i(\beta)U_i(\beta)^\top \to \bar B(\beta)$ uniformly on a ball around $\beta_0$ in probability, both continuous at $\beta_0$, $\bar A(\beta_0)$ invertible, and $\hat\beta_n \to \beta_0$ in probability. Let $\hat A_n$ and $\hat B_n$ be these averages at $\hat\beta_n$. Then $\Sigma(\hat A_n, \hat B_n) \to \Sigma(\bar A(\beta_0), \bar B(\beta_0))$ in probability.
