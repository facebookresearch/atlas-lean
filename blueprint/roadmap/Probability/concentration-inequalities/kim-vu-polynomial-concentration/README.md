# Kim–Vu polynomial concentration

Concentration of polynomials with positive coefficients in independent $\{0,1\}$ variables, controlled by the expected partial derivatives (average effects of groups of variables) rather than by worst-case Lipschitz constants. Azuma's and McDiarmid's inequalities need every variable to have a small effect; in sparse random graphs some variables have a large effect with small probability, and Kim–Vu still applies.
