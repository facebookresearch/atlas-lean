# Mean of a coordinate variable

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and $p \colon \alpha \to [0, 1]$. Let $\mathbb{P}_p$ be the product measure on $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^{\alpha}$ under which the coordinates are independent and coordinate $j$ is true with probability $p_j$, and let $X_i(\omega) = 1$ if $\omega(i) = \mathrm{true}$ and $0$ otherwise. Then for every $i \in \alpha$,

$$\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}_p}[X_i] = p_i.$$
