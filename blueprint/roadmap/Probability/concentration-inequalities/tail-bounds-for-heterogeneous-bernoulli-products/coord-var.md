# Coordinate variable

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and let $\omega \colon \alpha \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$ be an outcome. For $i \in \alpha$, the coordinate variable is the real indicator

$$X_i(\omega) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \omega(i) = \mathrm{true}, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
