# Independence of the coordinate variables

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and $p \colon \alpha \to [0, 1]$. Let $\mathbb{P}_p$ be the product measure on $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}^{\alpha}$ under which coordinate $j$ is true with probability $p_j$, and let $X_i(\omega) = 1$ if $\omega(i) = \mathrm{true}$ and $0$ otherwise. Then the family $(X_i)_{i \in \alpha}$ is mutually independent under $\mathbb{P}_p$.
