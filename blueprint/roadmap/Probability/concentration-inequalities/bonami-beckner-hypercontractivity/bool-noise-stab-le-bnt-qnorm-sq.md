# (p,2)-hypercontractivity in noise-stability form

For $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and every $f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$\mathrm{Stab}_\rho[f] \le \|f\|_{1+\rho}^2, \qquad \|f\|_q = \big(\mathbb{E}|f|^q\big)^{1/q}.$$

This is the Bonami–Beckner inequality $\|T_\sigma f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1+\sigma^2}$ at
$\sigma = \sqrt\rho$, combined with $\mathrm{Stab}_\rho[f] = \|T_{\sqrt\rho} f\|_2^2$.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Chapter 9, the (p,2)-Hypercontractivity Theorem ("Equivalently,
  $\mathrm{Stab}_\rho[f] \le \|f\|^2_{1+\rho}$ for $0 \le \rho \le 1$").
