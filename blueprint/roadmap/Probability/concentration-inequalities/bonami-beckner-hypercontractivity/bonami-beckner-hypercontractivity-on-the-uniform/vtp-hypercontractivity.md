# Bonami–Beckner hypercontractive inequality

Let $0 \le \rho \le 1$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, and let $T_\rho$ be
the [noise operator](../tensorization-of-two-point-hypercontractivity-on/bnt-noise-op.md). Then,
for the uniform measure on $\{0, 1\}^n$,

$$\|T_\rho f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1 + \rho^2} .$$

## Sources

- A. Bonami, *Étude des coefficients de Fourier des fonctions de $L^p(G)$*, Annales de l'Institut
  Fourier 20 (1970), 335–402.
- W. Beckner, *Inequalities in Fourier analysis*, Annals of Mathematics 102 (1975), 159–182.
