# Induction step of hypercontractivity tensorization

For $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$, the tensorization step at $\rho$ is the statement: for every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, if $\|T_\rho g\|_2 \le \|g\|_{1 + \rho^2}$ for every
$g : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, then $\|T_\rho f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1 + \rho^2}$ for every
$f : \{0, 1\}^{n+1} \to \mathbb{R}$ (uniform measure, [noise operator](bnt-noise-op.md) $T_\rho$).
