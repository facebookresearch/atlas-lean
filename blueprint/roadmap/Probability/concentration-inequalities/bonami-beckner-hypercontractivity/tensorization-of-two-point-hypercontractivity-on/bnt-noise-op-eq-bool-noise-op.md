# Agreement of the two noise operators on the Boolean cube

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, the [noise
operator](bnt-noise-op.md) $T_\rho f(x) = \sum_y K_\rho(x, y) f(y)$ coincides with the noise
operator of the uniform cube written with the kernel
$\prod_i \frac{1 + \rho\, \epsilon(x_i)\, \epsilon(y_i)}{2}$, where $\epsilon(b) = \pm 1$ is the sign
value of a bit.
