# $L^q$ norm under the uniform measure on the cube

For a finite set $\iota$, $q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$, put
$N_q(f) = \big(\mathbb{E}|f|^q\big)^{1/q}$, the expectation being under the uniform measure, with
real powers of nonnegative numbers ($0^0 = 1$ and $0^s = 0$ for $s \ne 0$) and $1/0 = 0$. For $q \ge 1$ it is the norm $\|f\|_q$ of $f$ in $L^q$ of the
uniform measure; for $q = 0$ the formula gives $1$ for every $f$, including $f = 0$.
