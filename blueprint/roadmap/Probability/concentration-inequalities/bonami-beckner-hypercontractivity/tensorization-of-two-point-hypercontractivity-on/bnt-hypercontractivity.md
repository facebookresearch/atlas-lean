# Hypercontractivity from the vector two-point inequality

Let $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the [vector two-point inequality](bnt-vector-two-point.md)
holds at $\rho$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, for the
uniform measure, $\|T_\rho f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1 + \rho^2}$, with $T_\rho$ the [noise
operator](bnt-noise-op.md).
