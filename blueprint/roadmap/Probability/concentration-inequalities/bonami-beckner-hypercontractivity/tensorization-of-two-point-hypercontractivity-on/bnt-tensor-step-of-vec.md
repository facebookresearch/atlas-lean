# Vector two-point inequality implies the tensorization step

Let $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the [vector two-point inequality](bnt-vector-two-point.md)
holds at $\rho$, and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. If $\|T_\rho g\|_2 \le \|g\|_{1 + \rho^2}$ for every
$g : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, then $\|T_\rho f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1 + \rho^2}$ for every
$f : \{0, 1\}^{n+1} \to \mathbb{R}$ (uniform measure, [noise operator](bnt-noise-op.md) $T_\rho$).
