# Hypercontractivity from the tensorization step

Let $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the [tensorization step](bnt-tensor-step.md) holds at $\rho$.
Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, for the uniform measure,
$\|T_\rho f\|_2 \le \|f\|_{1 + \rho^2}$, with $T_\rho$ the [noise operator](bnt-noise-op.md).
