# Trivial KKL inequality with constant $0$

Let $0 \le p \le 1$, let $E$ be a nonempty finite set and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ with indicator
$f = \mathbf{1}_A$. Then
$0 \cdot \operatorname{Var}_p(f) \log(1/\max_e \mathrm{Inf}_e(f)) \le \sum_e \mathrm{Inf}_e(f)$:
the KKL inequality with constant $0$ holds, since influences are nonnegative.
