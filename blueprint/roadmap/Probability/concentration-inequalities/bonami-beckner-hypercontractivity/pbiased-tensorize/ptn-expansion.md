# $p$-biased Fourier inversion

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $0 < p < 1$. For every $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and
every $x \in \{0, 1\}^\iota$,

$$f(x) = \sum_{S \subseteq \iota} \hat f(S)\, \varphi_S(x),$$

with the [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$ and [characters](ptn-pchar.md)
$\varphi_S$.
