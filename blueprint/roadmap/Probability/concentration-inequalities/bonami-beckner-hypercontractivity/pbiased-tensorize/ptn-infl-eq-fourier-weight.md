# $p$-biased influence of a coordinate as Fourier weight

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $0 < p < 1$, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^\iota$ with indicator
$f = \mathbf{1}_A$, and $e \in \iota$. Then the influence
$\mathrm{Inf}_e(f) = \mathbb{E}_p|f(\omega^{e \to 1}) - f(\omega^{e \to 0})|$ satisfies

$$\mathrm{Inf}_e(f) = \frac{1}{p(1-p)} \sum_{S \ni e} \hat f(S)^2 ,$$

with the [$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$.
