# Expectations of product functions factor under product measure

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $p \in \mathbb{R}$ and $h : \iota \times \{0, 1\} \to \mathbb{R}$.
Then

$$\mathbb{E}_p\Big[\prod_{e \in \iota} h(e, \omega_e)\Big] = \prod_{e \in \iota} \big((1 - p)\, h(e, 0) + p\, h(e, 1)\big),$$

where $\mathbb{E}_p[g] = \sum_\omega \prod_i w_p(\omega_i)\, g(\omega)$ with $w_p(1) = p$,
$w_p(0) = 1 - p$.
