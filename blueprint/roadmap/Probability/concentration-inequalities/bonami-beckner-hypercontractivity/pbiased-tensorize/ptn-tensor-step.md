# Tensorization step for $p$-biased hypercontractivity

Let $0 < p < 1$, $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and $1 \le q \le 2$ with $\rho^2 = 4p(1-p)(q-1)$, and let
$n \in \mathbb{N}$. If $\|T_\rho g\|_{L^2(\mu_p)} \le \|g\|_{L^q(\mu_p)}$ for every
$g : \{0, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, then the same holds for every $f : \{0, 1\}^{n+1} \to \mathbb{R}$,
where $T_\rho$ is the [$p$-biased noise operator](ptn-kop.md).
