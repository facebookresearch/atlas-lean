# Sharp-threshold bound from the conditional KKL statement

Let $1/2 \le q \le 1$ be such that the [conditional KKL statement](ptn-rho-optimise.md) holds at
$q$, let $E$ be a nonempty finite set and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an increasing set, and let
$v_0, L \ge 0$. Suppose that $P_{1/2}(A) = 1/2$ and that for every $p \in (1/2, q)$,
$v_0 \le P_p(A)(1 - P_p(A))$ and $L \le \log(1 / \max_e \mathrm{Inf}_e(\mathbf{1}_A))$ under
$\mu_p$. Then

$$\frac{1}{2} + 2 v_0 L \Big(q - \frac{1}{2}\Big) \le P_q(A) .$$
