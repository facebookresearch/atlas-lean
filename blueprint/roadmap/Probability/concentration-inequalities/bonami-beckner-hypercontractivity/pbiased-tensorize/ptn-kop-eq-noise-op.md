# Kernel and Fourier noise operators agree

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $0 < p < 1$ and $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$. For every
$f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ the [kernel form](ptn-kop.md) and the [Fourier
form](ptn-noise-op.md) of the $p$-biased noise operator agree:
$\sum_y K_{p,\rho}(x, y) f(y) = \sum_S \rho^{|S|} \hat f(S) \varphi_S(x)$ for every $x$.
