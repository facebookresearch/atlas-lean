# Summed per-coordinate $p$-biased hypercontractive bound

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, let $0 < p < 1$, $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and $1 \le q \le 2$ with
$\rho^2 = 4p(1-p)(q-1)$, and let $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^\iota$ with indicator $f = \mathbf{1}_A$.
Then

$$\frac{1}{p(1-p)} \sum_{\emptyset \ne S \subseteq \iota} |S|\, \rho^{2(|S|-1)}\, \hat f(S)^2 \le \sum_{e \in \iota} \mathrm{Inf}_e(f)^{2/q}:$$

the [per-coordinate bound](ptn-per-coord-hc.md) summed over all coordinates.
