# $p$-biased total influence as Fourier weight

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $0 < p < 1$ and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^\iota$ with indicator
$f = \mathbf{1}_A$. Then

$$\sum_{e \in \iota} \mathrm{Inf}_e(f) = \frac{1}{p(1-p)} \sum_{S \subseteq \iota} |S|\, \hat f(S)^2 ,$$

with $\mathrm{Inf}_e(f) = \mathbb{E}_p|f(\omega^{e \to 1}) - f(\omega^{e \to 0})|$ and the
[$p$-biased Fourier coefficients](ptn-coeff.md).
