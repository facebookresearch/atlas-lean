# $p$-biased Fourier coefficient

For a finite set $\iota$, $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and
$S \subseteq \iota$, the $p$-biased Fourier coefficient is $\hat f(S) = \mathbb{E}_p[f\, \varphi_S]$,
where $\varphi_S$ is the [$p$-biased character](ptn-pchar.md) and
$\mathbb{E}_p[g] = \sum_{\omega} \prod_{i \in \iota} w_p(\omega_i)\, g(\omega)$ with $w_p(1) = p$ and
$w_p(0) = 1 - p$; for $0 \le p \le 1$ this is the expectation under the $p$-biased product
measure $\mu_p$.
