# $q$-th moment under the $p$-biased measure

For a finite set $\iota$, real $p, q$ and $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$, the $q$-th moment is
$\mathbb{E}_p|f|^q = \sum_\omega \prod_i w_p(\omega_i)\, |f(\omega)|^q$, with $w_p(1) = p$ and
$w_p(0) = 1 - p$, and real powers of nonnegative numbers ($0^0 = 1$ and $0^s = 0$ for $s \ne 0$).
