# Fourier form of $p$-biased hypercontractivity on finite index sets

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, let $0 < p < 1$, $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and $1 \le q \le 2$ with
$\rho^2 = 4p(1-p)(q-1)$. Then for every $f : \{0, 1\}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}$, with the [$p$-biased
Fourier coefficients](ptn-coeff.md) $\hat f(S)$,

$$\sum_{S \subseteq \iota} \rho^{2|S|}\, \hat f(S)^2 \le \|f\|_{L^q(\mu_p)}^2 .$$
