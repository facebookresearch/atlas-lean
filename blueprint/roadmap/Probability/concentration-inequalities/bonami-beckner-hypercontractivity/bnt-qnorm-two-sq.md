# Squared $L^2$ norm on the cube

For every $g : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}$, the uniform $L^2$ norm
$\|g\|_2 = (\mathbb{E}|g|^2)^{1/2}$ satisfies $\|g\|_2^2 = \mathbb{E}[g^2]$. This connects the
$L^q$ norms used in the hypercontractivity modules with the expectations of the Fourier modules.

## Sources

- R. O'Donnell, *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, Cambridge University Press, 2014 (arXiv:2105.10386), Chapter 9 (the norms $\|f\|_q = \mathbb{E}[|f|^q]^{1/q}$ of the hypercontractivity theorems).
