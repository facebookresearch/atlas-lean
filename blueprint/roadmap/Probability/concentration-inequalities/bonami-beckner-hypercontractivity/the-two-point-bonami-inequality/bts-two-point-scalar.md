# Two-point Bonami inequality

Let $0 \le \rho \le 1$ and put $q = 1 + \rho^2$. Then for all real $a$ and $b$,

$$a^2 + \rho^2 b^2 \le \Big(\frac{|a + b|^q + |a - b|^q}{2}\Big)^{2/q},$$

that is, $\|a + \rho b x\|_2 \le \|a + b x\|_q$ for a uniform random sign $x$.

- A. Bonami, *Étude des coefficients de Fourier des fonctions de $L^p(G)$*, Annales de l'Institut

- W. Beckner, *Inequalities in Fourier analysis*, Annals of Mathematics 102 (1975), 159–182.
