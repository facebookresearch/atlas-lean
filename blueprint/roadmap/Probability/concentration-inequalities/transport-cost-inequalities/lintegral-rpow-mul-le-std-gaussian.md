# Prékopa–Leindler inequality for the standard Gaussian measure

Let $\gamma$ be the
standard Gaussian on a finite-dimensional real inner product space, let $0\lt t<1$, and let $u,v,w$
be Borel measurable functions with values in $[0,\infty]$. If for all $x,y$,

$$u(x)^{1-t}v(y)^t\exp(-t(1-t)\|x-y\|^2/2)\le w((1-t)x+ty),$$

then $(\int u\,d\gamma)^{1-t}(\int v\,d\gamma)^t\le \int w\,d\gamma$.
