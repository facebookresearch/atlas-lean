# Dirac masses satisfy the Talagrand inequality

Let $X$ be a second-countable metric space with a σ-algebra containing the open sets and the
singletons, and $x_0 \in X$. For every $C > 0$, $\delta_{x_0}$ satisfies [Talagrand's inequality](satisfies-talagrand.md) with
constant $C$: $\operatorname{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \delta_{x_0}) = \infty$ unless
[$\mu = \delta_{x_0}$](eq-dirac-of-absolutely-continuous.md), and
$W_2(\delta_{x_0}, \delta_{x_0}) = 0$. With the log-Sobolev counterpart
[for Dirac masses](satisfies-log-sobolev-dirac.md) this shows that the hypotheses of the
Otto–Villani theorem are satisfiable.
