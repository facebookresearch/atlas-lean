# Interpolation identity for the standard Gaussian density

For the standard Gaussian
density $p$ on $\mathbb R^n$, all $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$ and every real $t$,

$$p(x)^{1-t}p(y)^t = p((1-t)x+ty)\exp(-t(1-t)\|x-y\|^2/2).$$
