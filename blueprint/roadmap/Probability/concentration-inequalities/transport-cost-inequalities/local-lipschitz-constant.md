# Local Lipschitz constant (slope)

For $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ on a metric space, the local Lipschitz constant is

$$|\nabla f|(x) = \limsup_{y \to x} \frac{|f(y) - f(x)|}{d(x, y)} \in [0, \infty],$$

the upper limit over $y \ne x$, equal to $0$ at isolated points. For a $C^1$ function on a
Riemannian manifold it is the norm of the gradient. It is the notion of gradient in which Gigli
and Ledoux state the logarithmic Sobolev inequality on metric measure spaces.

## Sources

- N. Gigli, M. Ledoux, *From log Sobolev to Talagrand: a quick proof*, Discrete Contin. Dyn. Syst. Ser. A (2013); preprint at https://cvgmt.sns.it/paper/1689/, Section 2.
