# Sum cube Val fourth le

Let $T \in \mathbb{N}$. For a sign vector $\sigma \in \{-1, +1\}^T$ write $S(\sigma) = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \sigma_t$. Then

$$\sum_{\sigma \in \{-1,+1\}^T} S(\sigma)^4 \le 2^{T} \cdot 3 T^2,$$

that is, the fourth moment of a sum of $T$ independent uniform random signs is at most $3T^2$.
