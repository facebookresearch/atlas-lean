# Khintchine lower

Let $T \in \mathbb{N}$. For a sign vector $\sigma \in \{-1, +1\}^T$ write $S(\sigma) = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \sigma_t$. Then

$$\sum_{\sigma \in \{-1,+1\}^T} \lvert S(\sigma) \rvert \ge 2^{T} \sqrt{T / 3},$$

that is, the mean absolute value of a sum of $T$ independent uniform random signs is at least $\sqrt{T/3}$.
