# Bernstein's inequality for bounded variables, two-sided

Under the hypotheses of the upper-tail inequality ($|X| \le b$ almost surely with $b \ge 0$,
$\mathbb{E}[X] = 0$, $\mathbb{E}[X^2] \le \sigma^2$), for every $t \ge 0$,

$$\mu(|X| \ge t) \le 2\exp\Big(-\frac{t^2}{2(\sigma^2 + bt)}\Big).$$
