# Sub-Gaussian variables are sub-exponential

If $X$ is sub-Gaussian with variance proxy $c$ under $\mu$, that is $e^{tX}$ is integrable and
$\mathbb{E}[e^{tX}] \le e^{c t^2 / 2}$ for every real $t$, then $X$ is sub-exponential with
parameters $(\sqrt{c}, \alpha)$ for every $\alpha \ge 0$.
