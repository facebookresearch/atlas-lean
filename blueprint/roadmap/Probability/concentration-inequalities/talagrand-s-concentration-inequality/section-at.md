# Section at the first coordinate

For $A \subseteq \Omega^{N+1}$ and $\omega \in \Omega$, $A(\omega) = \{x \in \Omega^N : (\omega, x)
\in A\}$ (Talagrand, *Concentration of measure and isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*,
Publ. Math. IHÉS 81 (1995), proof of Theorem 4.1.1, where the last coordinate plays this role).
