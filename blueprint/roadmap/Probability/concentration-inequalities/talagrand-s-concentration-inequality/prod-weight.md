# Product weights

For weights $p$ on a finite set $\Omega$, $P(x) = \prod_{i} p(x_i)$ for $x \in \Omega^N$. When
$p$ is a probability vector, $P$ is the product probability $p^{\otimes N}$, the setting of
Talagrand's Section 2.1 with $\Omega$ finite (Talagrand, *Concentration of measure and
isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 81 (1995)).
