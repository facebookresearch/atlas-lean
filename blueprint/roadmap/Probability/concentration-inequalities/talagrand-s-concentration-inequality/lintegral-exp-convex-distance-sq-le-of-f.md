# The convex-distance inequality for a finite probability space, measure form

For a probability measure $\mu$ on a finite set $\Omega$ with measurable singletons, $P =
\mu^{\otimes N}$ and every $A \subseteq \Omega^N$,

$$\int \exp\Big(\frac14 f_c^2(A, x)\Big)\,dP(x) \le \frac{1}{P(A)}$$

(Talagrand, *Concentration of measure and isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*, Publ.
Math. IHÉS 81 (1995), Theorem 4.1.1, (4.1.2), for a finite $\Omega$). It is the finite case of the
[general statement](lintegral-exp-convex-distance-sq-le.md), in the same form: the integral and $P(A)$ are rewritten as finite sums over
$\Omega^N$, where the sum form applies to the probability vector $p(\omega) = \mu(\{\omega\})$.
