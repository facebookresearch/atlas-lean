# Tail form on a product of finite sets

Under the hypotheses of the convex-distance inequality on a product of finite sets, for every
$t \ge 0$, $P(f_c(A, \cdot) \ge t)\cdot P(A) \le e^{-t^2/4}$ (Talagrand, *Concentration of measure
and isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 81 (1995), Theorem 4.1.1,
(4.1.3), for a finite $\Omega$), by Markov's inequality applied to $e^{f_c^2/4}$.
