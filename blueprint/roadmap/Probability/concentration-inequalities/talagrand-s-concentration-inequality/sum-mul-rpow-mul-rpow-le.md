# Weighted Hölder inequality for two factors

For nonnegative weights $w_i$ and values $a_i, b_i$ and $0 \le \lambda \le 1$,

$$\sum_i w_i\, a_i^{\lambda} b_i^{1-\lambda} \le \Big(\sum_i w_i a_i\Big)^{\lambda}\Big(\sum_i w_i b_i\Big)^{1-\lambda}.$$

This is Hölder's inequality with exponents $1/\lambda$ and $1/(1-\lambda)$, the form used in
Talagrand's proof of the convex-distance inequality.
