# Points of $A$ are at convex distance zero

If $x \in A$ then $f_c(A, x) = 0$: taking $y = x$ shows $0 \in U_A(x)$ (Talagrand, *Concentration
of measure and isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 81 (1995),
Section 4.1, which notes that $V_A(x)$ contains $0$ if and only if $x \in A$; this is the
direction from $x \in A$).
