# Projection forgetting the first coordinate

For $A \subseteq \Omega^{N+1}$, $B = \{x \in \Omega^N : \exists\,\omega,\ (\omega, x) \in A\}$
(Talagrand, *Concentration of measure and isoperimetric inequalities in product spaces*, Publ.
Math. IHÉS 81 (1995), proof of Theorem 4.1.1). Every section $A(\omega)$ is contained in $B$.
