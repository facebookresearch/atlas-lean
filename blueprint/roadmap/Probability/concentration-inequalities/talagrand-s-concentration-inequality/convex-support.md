# Talagrand's set $U_A(x)$

For $A \subseteq \Omega^N$ and $x \in \Omega^N$,

$$U_A(x) = \{ s \in \{0, 1\}^N : \exists\, y \in A,\ s_i = 0 \Rightarrow y_i = x_i \},$$

seen as a subset of $\mathbb{R}^N$ (Talagrand, *Concentration of measure and isoperimetric
inequalities in product spaces*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 81 (1995), Section 4.1). A vector $s$ lies in
$U_A(x)$ when some point of $A$ can be reached from $x$ by changing only coordinates where
$s_i = 1$. Thus $0 \in U_A(x)$ exactly when $x \in A$.
