# Expectation of a random matrix

Let $n$ be a nonempty finite index set, $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, $\mu$ a probability
measure on $\Omega$ and $M : \Omega \to \mathbb{K}^{n \times n}$ a random matrix. Its expectation is the
matrix of entrywise integrals $(\mathbb{E}\,M)_{ij} = \int M(\omega)_{ij}\, d\mu(\omega)$ (each entry
taken to be $0$ when it is not integrable). This is the expectation of a random matrix as Tropp
defines it.
