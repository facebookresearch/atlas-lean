# Duminil-Copin–Tassion differential inequality

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 1$, $0 < p < 1$ and $m \in \mathbb{R}$, and assume $\varphi_p(S) \ge m$ for every finite set $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ containing the origin, where $\varphi_p$ is the [Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/phi.md). Let $f(t) = \sum_{\eta \in A'_n} \prod_e t^{\eta_e} (1 - t)^{1 - \eta_e}$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$, where the product runs over the [unordered pairs of sites of $\Lambda_n$](box-edge-finset.md) and $A'_n$ is the set of restrictions to these pairs of the configurations in the [box-crossing event](box-crossing-event.md) $A_n$; for $t \in [0, 1]$, $f(t) = \mathbb{P}_t(A_n)$ (compare the [box-crossing polynomial](cross-poly.md)). Then

$$f'(p) \ge \frac{m}{p(1 - p)} \big(1 - \mathbb{P}_p(A_n)\big).$$
