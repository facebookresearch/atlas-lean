# Event of a connection inside a finite set

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ and sites $o, x$, the event $\{o \xleftrightarrow{S} x\}$ that $o \in S$, $x \in S$ and $o$ and $x$ are joined by an open path [inside](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $S$.
