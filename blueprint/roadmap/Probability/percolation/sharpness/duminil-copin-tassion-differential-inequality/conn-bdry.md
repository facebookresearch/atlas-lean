# Connection of a site to the box boundary

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and a site $x$, the condition that $x \in \Lambda_n$ and $x$ is joined by an open path inside $\Lambda_n$ to a site of the vertex boundary $\partial \Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$.
