# Box-crossing probability as a polynomial in the density

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the polynomial

$$t \mapsto \sum_{\eta \in A'_{n+1}} \prod_{e} t^{\eta_e} (1 - t)^{1 - \eta_e},$$

where the product runs over the [unordered pairs of sites of $\Lambda_{n+1}$](box-edge-finset.md) and $A'_{n+1}$ is the set of restrictions to these pairs of the configurations in the [box-crossing event](box-crossing-event.md) $A_{n+1}$. For $t \in [0, 1]$ it equals $\mathbb{P}_t(A_{n+1})$.
