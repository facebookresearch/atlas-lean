# Inhomogeneous Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity $\varphi(S)$

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ a real function on the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, $o \in V$ and $S \subseteq V$ finite. With $\mathbb{P}_\beta$ the product weight under which each pair $e$ is open with weight $1 - e^{-\beta J_e}$ and closed with weight $e^{-\beta J_e}$,

$$\varphi_\beta(S) = \sum_{(x, y)} \big(1 - e^{-\beta J_{xy}}\big)\, \mathbb{P}_\beta(o \xleftrightarrow{S} x),$$

the sum over the ordered pairs $(x, y)$ of adjacent vertices of $G$ with $x \in S$ and $y \notin S$, where $\{o \xleftrightarrow{S} x\}$ is the event that $o, x \in S$ are joined by a path of open edges of $G$ inside $S$.
