# Box-crossing probability

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0, 1]$, the probability $\mathbb{P}_p(A_n)$ under Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ of the [box-crossing event](box-crossing-event.md) $A_n$ that the origin is joined inside $\Lambda_n$ to the vertex boundary of $\Lambda_n$.
