# Mean-field lower bound

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\tilde p_c = \tilde p_c(d)$ be the [Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc.md). If $\tilde p_c > 0$, then for every $q$ with $\tilde p_c < q < 1$ the percolation probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ satisfies

$$\theta(q) \ge \frac{q - \tilde p_c}{q(1 - \tilde p_c)}.$$

## Sources

- Hugo Duminil-Copin and Vincent Tassion, *A new proof of the sharpness of the phase transition for Bernoulli percolation and the Ising model*, Comm. Math. Phys. 343 (2016), no. 2, 725–745, DOI 10.1007/s00220-015-2480-z.
