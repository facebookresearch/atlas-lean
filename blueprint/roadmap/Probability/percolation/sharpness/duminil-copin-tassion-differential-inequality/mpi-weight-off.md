# Product of the edge laws over all other edges

Let $V$ be a finite set, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $J$ a real function on the unordered pairs of elements of $V$, $e$ such a pair and $\omega$ a configuration. The product over all pairs $f \ne e$ of the edge weights, $1 - e^{-\beta J_f}$ if $\omega_f = 1$ and $e^{-\beta J_f}$ if $\omega_f = 0$.
