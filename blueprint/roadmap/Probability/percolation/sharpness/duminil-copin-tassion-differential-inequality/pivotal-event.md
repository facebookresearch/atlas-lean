# Pivotal edge event

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, an unordered pair $e$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and an event $A$ of bond configurations, the event that $e$ is [pivotal](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/is-pivotal.md) for $A$: opening and closing $e$ give configurations of which exactly one lies in $A$.
