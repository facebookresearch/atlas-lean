# $p_c \le \tilde p_c$ if $\theta > 0$ above $\tilde p_c$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. If the percolation probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ satisfies $\theta(t) > 0$ for every $t \in (\tilde p_c(d), 1]$, then $p_c(d) \le \tilde p_c(d)$, where $\tilde p_c(d)$ is the [Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc.md).
