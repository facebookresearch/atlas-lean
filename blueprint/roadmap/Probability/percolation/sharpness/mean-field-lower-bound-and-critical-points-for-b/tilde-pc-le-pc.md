# $\tilde p_c \le p_c$ if $\theta = 0$ on the set defining $\tilde p_c$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. If the percolation probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ vanishes at every density $t$ of the [set defining $\tilde p_c(d)$](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc-set.md) (the $t \in [0, 1]$ with $\varphi_t(S) < 1$ for some finite $S \ni 0$), then $\tilde p_c(d) \le p_c(d)$.
