# Equality of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold and $p_c$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, write $\theta(p)$ for the percolation probability and $\theta_n(p) = \mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_{n-1})$ for the [one-arm probability](../exponential-decay-below-the-critical-point/cross-prob.md). Assume that

- $\theta(1) \ne 0$;
- for every $p$ in the [set defining $\tilde p_c(d)$](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc-set.md) there are $c, C > 0$ with $\theta_n(p) \le C e^{-cn}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$;
- $\theta(q) > 0$ for every $q$ with $\tilde p_c(d) < q < 1$.

Then $\tilde p_c(d) = p_c(d)$.
