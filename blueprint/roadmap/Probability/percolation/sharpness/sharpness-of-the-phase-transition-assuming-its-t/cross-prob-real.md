# One-arm probability as a function of a real density

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$, the [one-arm probability](../exponential-decay-below-the-critical-point/cross-prob.md) $\theta_n(t) = \mathbb{P}_t(0 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_{n-1})$ of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ when $t \in [0, 1]$, extended by $\theta_n(t) = 0$ for $t \notin [0, 1]$.
