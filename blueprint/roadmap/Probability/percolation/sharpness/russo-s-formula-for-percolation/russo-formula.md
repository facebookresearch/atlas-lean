# Russo's formula, derivative form

Let $E$ be a finite set, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an [increasing](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event and $0 < p < 1$. The derivative at $p$ of the function $p \mapsto \mathbb{P}_p(A)$ of [Bernoulli probabilities](measure-prob.md) is

$$\frac{d}{dp} \mathbb{P}_p(A) = \sum_{e \in E} \mathbb{P}_p(e \text{ is pivotal for } A).$$
