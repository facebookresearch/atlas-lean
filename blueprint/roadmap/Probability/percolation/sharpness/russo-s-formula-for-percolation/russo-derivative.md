# Russo's formula

Let $E$ be a finite set, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ an [increasing](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event and $0 < p < 1$. The function $p \mapsto \mathbb{P}_p(A)$ of [Bernoulli probabilities](measure-prob.md) is differentiable at $p$ with

$$\frac{d}{dp} \mathbb{P}_p(A) = \sum_{e \in E} \mathbb{P}_p(e \text{ is pivotal for } A).$$

The same identity holds as an identity of polynomials for every real $p$ ([Russo's formula on a finite product space](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/README.md)).
