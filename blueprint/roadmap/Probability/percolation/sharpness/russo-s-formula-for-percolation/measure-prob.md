# Probability of an event under Bernoulli product measure

Let $E$ be a finite set and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$. For $p \in [0, 1]$, $\mathbb{P}_p(A)$ is the probability of $A$ under the [Bernoulli product measure](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$; for $p \notin [0, 1]$ it is the polynomial $\sum_{\omega \in A} \prod_{e \in E} p^{\omega_e} (1 - p)^{1 - \omega_e}$, which agrees with the probability on $[0, 1]$. This makes $p \mapsto \mathbb{P}_p(A)$ a function on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
