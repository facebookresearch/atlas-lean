# Auxiliary critical inverse temperature from $\tilde p_c$

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and a coupling $J \in \mathbb{R}$, the auxiliary critical inverse temperature is

$$\tilde\beta_c = -\frac{\log\big(1 - \tilde p_c(d)\big)}{J},$$

where $\tilde p_c(d)$ is the [Duminil-Copin–Tassion threshold](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc.md). When $J > 0$ and $\tilde p_c(d) < 1$ it satisfies $p(\tilde\beta_c) = \tilde p_c(d)$ for the [bond probability](p-beta.md) $p(\beta) = 1 - e^{-\beta J}$. By convention $\log 0 = 0$ and division by $0$ gives $0$, so $\tilde\beta_c = 0$ when $\tilde p_c(d) = 1$ or $J = 0$.
