# Monotonicity of connection probabilities inside a finite set

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ be finite and $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$. For $0 \le p \le q \le 1$,

$$\mathbb{P}_p(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x) \le \mathbb{P}_q(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x),$$

for the [probability of connection inside $S$](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/conn-within-prob.md) (both sides are $0$ when $0 \notin S$ or $x \notin S$). This is the monotonicity in $p$ of the probability of an increasing event, applied to $\{0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x\}$.
