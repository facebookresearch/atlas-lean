# Every density below $\tilde p_c$ admits a finite set with $\varphi_p(S) < 1$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. Every $p \ge 0$ with $p < \tilde p_c(d)$ lies in the [set defining $\tilde p_c(d)$](../the-duminil-copin-tassion-quantity-p-s-and-the-t/tilde-pc-set.md): $p \le 1$ and some finite $S \ni 0$ has $\varphi_p(S) < 1$.
