# Connection probabilities are bounded by one-arm probabilities

Let $d \ge 1$, $p \in [0, 1]$, and let $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ have [sup-norm](sup-norm.md) $\|x\|_\infty \ge 1$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow x) \le \mathbb{P}_p\big(0 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_{\|x\|_\infty - 1}\big),$$

the [one-arm probability](../exponential-decay-below-the-critical-point/cross-prob.md) $\theta_{\|x\|_\infty}(p)$: the site $x$ itself lies outside $\Lambda_{\|x\|_\infty - 1}$.
