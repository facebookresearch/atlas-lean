# Exponential decay below $p_c$

For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 1$ and every $p < p_c(d)$, there are $c, C > 0$ such that

$$\mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}) \le C e^{-cn} \quad \text{for all } n \in \mathbb{N}.$$

The lecture notes state the bound as $e^{-c_p n}$ for $n \ge 1$; the statement above carries a prefactor $C$.
