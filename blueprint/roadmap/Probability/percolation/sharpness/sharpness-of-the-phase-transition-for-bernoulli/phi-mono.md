# Monotonicity of the Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ be finite. For $0 \le p \le q \le 1$,

$$\varphi_p(S) \le \varphi_q(S),$$

where $\varphi_p(S) = p \sum_{(x, y) \in \partial S} \mathbb{P}_p(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x)$. Both factors are nondecreasing in $p$: the prefactor trivially, and each connection probability by [monotonicity of connection probabilities inside a finite set](conn-within-prob-mono.md).
