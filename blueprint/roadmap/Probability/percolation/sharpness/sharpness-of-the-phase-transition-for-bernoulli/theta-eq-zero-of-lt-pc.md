# No percolation below $p_c$

For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 1$, $\theta_d(p) = 0$ for every $p < p_c(d)$. Since $p_c$ is defined as $\sup \{p : \theta(p) = 0\}$, this needs either the monotonicity of $\theta$ or another argument; it follows from [exponential decay below $p_c$](cross-prob-exp-decay-of-lt-pc.md).

## Background

- [Percolation probability $\theta$](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/theta.md) and [critical point $p_c$](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/pc.md): $p_c$ is defined as a supremum of $\{p : \theta(p) = 0\}$, so $\theta(p) = 0$ below $p_c$ is not immediate without monotonicity of $\theta$.
- [Monotonicity of $\theta$](../../phase-transition/bounds-on-the-critical-probability/theta-mono-parameter.md): monotonicity of $\theta$ gives the same conclusion by another route.
