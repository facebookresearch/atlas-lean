# Finite susceptibility below $p_c$

For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 1$ and every $p < p_c(d)$, the susceptibility is finite:

$$\sum_{x \in \mathbb{Z}^d} \mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow x) < \infty.$$

## Informally uses

- [Exponential decay below $p_c$](cross-prob-exp-decay-of-lt-pc.md): bounds $\mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow x)$ by $C e^{-c\|x\|_\infty}$; the source instead obtains finite susceptibility directly from $\varphi_p(S) < 1$ and the BK inequality, and this proof replaces that step by exponential decay.
