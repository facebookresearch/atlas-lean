# Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0, 1]$ and let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ be finite. The Duminil-Copin–Tassion quantity is

$$\varphi_p(S) = p \sum_{(x, y) \in \partial S} \mathbb{P}_p(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x),$$

where $\partial S$ is the [oriented edge boundary](boundary-edges.md) of $S$ and $\mathbb{P}_p(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x)$ is the [probability of connection inside](conn-within-prob.md) $S$ (which is $0$ when $0 \notin S$).
