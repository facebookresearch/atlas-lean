# Probability of connection inside a set

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0, 1]$, let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$ be finite and $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$. If $0 \in S$ and $x \in S$, the quantity $\mathbb{P}_p(0 \xleftrightarrow{S} x)$ is the probability, under Bernoulli bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, that the origin and $x$ are [joined by an open path using only sites of](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $S$. If $0 \notin S$ or $x \notin S$ it is $0$ by definition.
