# Incident coupling of a vertex

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$ and $x \in V$. The incident coupling of $x$ is $\sum_{y \sim x} J_{xy}$, the sum of $J$ over the edges of $G$ at $x$ (indexed by the oriented edges starting at $x$).
