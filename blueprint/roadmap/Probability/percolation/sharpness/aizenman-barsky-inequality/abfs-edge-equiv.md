# Induced permutation of ghost-graph edges

For a set $V$ and a permutation $\sigma$ of $V$, the permutation $\{a, b\} \mapsto \{\sigma^\ast(a), \sigma^\ast(b)\}$ of unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$, where $\sigma^\ast$ is the [extension of $\sigma$ fixing the ghost](abfs-option-equiv.md).
