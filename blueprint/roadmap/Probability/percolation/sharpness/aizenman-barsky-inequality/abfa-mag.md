# Finite-volume percolation magnetization

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$ and $o \in V$. The ghost graph $G^\ast$ has vertex set $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$, the edges of $G$, and an edge $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$ from every $x \in V$ to the ghost vertex $\mathfrak{g}$. For $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$ the percolation magnetization is

$$M(\beta, h) = \mathbb{P}_{p(\beta, h)}(o \leftrightarrow \mathfrak{g}),$$

the [weight](prob-v.md) under the [edge parameters with a ghost field](abfa-params.md) $p(\beta, h)$ of the event that $o$ is joined to $\mathfrak{g}$ by a path of open edges of $G^\ast$. For $\beta, h \ge 0$ and $J \ge 0$ it is a probability.
