# Largest site magnetization in a window

Let $G$ be a graph on a set $V$, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs, $S \subseteq V$ finite and nonempty (containing a marked site $o$), and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The envelope is the largest of the [finite-volume magnetizations](abfa-mag.md) $M_x(\beta, h)$, $x \in S$, computed on the [subgraph induced on $S$](abig-window-graph.md) with the [restricted coupling](abig-window-coupling.md).
