# Bond percolation measure on the ghost graph

Let $V$ be a set, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The measure $\mathbb{P}_{\beta, h}$ is the product measure on configurations of the unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$ under which each pair $e$ is open with probability $p_e$, independently, where $p_e = \max(r_e, 0)$ for the [real edge parameters](abig-real-param.md) $r_e$.
