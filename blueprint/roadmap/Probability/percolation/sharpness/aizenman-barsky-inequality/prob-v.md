# Probability under an inhomogeneous product measure

For a finite set $E$, real parameters $(p_e)_{e \in E}$ and $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$, the weight

$$\mathbb{P}_p(A) = \sum_{\omega \in A} \prod_{e \in E} p_e^{\omega_e} (1 - p_e)^{1 - \omega_e}.$$

When all $p_e \in [0, 1]$ it is the probability of $A$ under the product measure with independent Bernoulli($p_e$) coordinates.
