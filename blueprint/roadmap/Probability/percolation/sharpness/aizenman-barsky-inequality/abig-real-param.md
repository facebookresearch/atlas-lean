# Real edge parameters with a ghost field

Let $V$ be a set, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$ and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The real edge parameters on unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$ are $1 - e^{-\beta J_{xy}}$ on physical pairs $\{x, y\}$ with $x, y \in V$, $1 - e^{-h}$ on the ghost edges $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$, and $0$ on $\{\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g}\}$.
