# Edge parameters with a ghost field

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$, and $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$. The edge parameters on unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$ are $1 - e^{-h}$ on the ghost edges $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$, $x \in V$, and $1 - e^{-\beta J'_e}$ on every other pair $e$, where $J'$ is the [lifted coupling](abfa-lift-coupling.md); so the parameter is $1 - e^{-\beta J_e}$ on the edges of $G$ and $0$ on the remaining pairs.
