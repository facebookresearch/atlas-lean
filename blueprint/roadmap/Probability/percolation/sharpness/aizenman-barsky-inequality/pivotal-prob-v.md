# Pivotal probability of an edge

For a finite set $E$, real parameters $(p_e)_{e \in E}$, $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ and $e \in E$, the weight $\sum_{\omega} \prod_{f \in E} p_f^{\omega_f} (1 - p_f)^{1 - \omega_f}$ of the configurations $\omega$ at which $e$ is [pivotal](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/is-pivotal.md) for $A$. When all $p_f \in [0, 1]$ it is the probability that $e$ is pivotal for $A$ under independent Bernoulli($p_f$) coordinates.
