# Infinite-volume percolation magnetization

Let $G$ be a graph on a vertex set $V$, $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$ and $o \in V$. For $\beta, h \in \mathbb{R}$, the percolation magnetization is

$$M(\beta, h) = \mathbb{P}_{\beta, h}(o \leftrightarrow \mathfrak{g}),$$

the probability, under the [bond percolation measure on the ghost graph](abig-measure.md), that $o$ is joined to the ghost vertex $\mathfrak{g}$ by a path of open edges of the ghost graph (the edges of $G$ together with every $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$, $x \in V$).
