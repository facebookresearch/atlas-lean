# Coupling lifted to the ghost graph

Let $G$ be a graph on a finite vertex set $V$ and $J$ a real function on unordered pairs of elements of $V$. On unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$, where $\mathfrak{g}$ is the ghost vertex, the lifted coupling equals $J_{xy}$ on $\{x, y\}$ when $\{x, y\}$ is an edge of $G$, and $0$ on every other pair (in particular on the ghost edges $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$ and on $\{\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{g}\}$).
