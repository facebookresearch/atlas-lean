# Edges used by an open walk in an induced subgraph

Let $G$ be a graph on $V$, $\omega$ a configuration on unordered pairs of elements of $V$, $A \subseteq V$, and $w$ a walk in the subgraph induced on $A$ by the open subgraph of $\omega$ (edges of $G$ with $\omega = 1$). The set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$ traversed by $w$.
