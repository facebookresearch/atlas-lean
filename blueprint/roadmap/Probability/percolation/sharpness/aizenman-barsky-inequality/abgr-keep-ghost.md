# Closing all ghost edges but one

For a finite set $V$, $x \in V$ and a configuration $\omega$ on unordered pairs of elements of $V \cup \{\mathfrak{g}\}$, the configuration equal to $\omega$ on $\{x, \mathfrak{g}\}$ and on every pair that is not a ghost edge, and to $0$ on every other ghost edge $\{y, \mathfrak{g}\}$, $y \ne x$.
