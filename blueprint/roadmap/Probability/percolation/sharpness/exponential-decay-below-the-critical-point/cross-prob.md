# One-arm probability

For $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0, 1]$, the one-arm probability is

$$\theta_n(p) = \mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^d \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}),$$

the probability under Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ of the [one-arm event](crossing-event.md) that the origin is joined by an open path to a site outside $\Lambda_{n-1}$ (with $n - 1$ truncated at $0$).
