# Translation invariance of the Bernoulli product measure

Let a group $G$ act on a set $E$ and let $p \in [0, 1]$. The [Bernoulli product measure](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ is [translation invariant](../../../random-fields/translation-invariance-and-ergodicity-of-random/is-translation-invariant.md): every [shift](../../../random-fields/translation-invariance-and-ergodicity-of-random/shift.md) $\omega \mapsto g \cdot \omega$, $g \in G$, preserves it.
