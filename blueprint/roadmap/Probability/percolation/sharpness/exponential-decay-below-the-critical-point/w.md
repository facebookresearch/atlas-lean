# Product weight of an edge configuration

For a finite set $E$, $p \in [0, 1]$ and $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^E$, the product weight $W(\omega) = \prod_{e \in E} p^{\omega_e} (1 - p)^{1 - \omega_e}$ of the [single-edge weights](w1.md), the probability of $\omega$ under the Bernoulli product measure with parameter $p$.
