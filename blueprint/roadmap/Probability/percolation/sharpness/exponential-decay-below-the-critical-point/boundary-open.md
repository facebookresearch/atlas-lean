# Open boundary edge of a cluster

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, a set $C \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$, sites $x, y$ and a bond configuration $\omega$, the condition that $x \in C$, $y \notin C$ and the edge $\{x, y\}$ is [open](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/is-open-edge.md) in $\omega$ (so $x$ and $y$ are nearest neighbours).
