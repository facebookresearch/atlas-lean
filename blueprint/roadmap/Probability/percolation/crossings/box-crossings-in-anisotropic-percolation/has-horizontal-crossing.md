# Open horizontal crossing of a box

Let $a, b \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\omega$ be an open/closed configuration of the edges of the box $[0, a] \times [0, b]$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$. The event $H(a, b)$ holds for $\omega$ when some vertex $(0, y)$ on the left side is joined to some vertex $(a, y')$ on the right side by a path of open edges inside the box, with $y, y' \in \{0, \dots, b\}$:

$$\exists\, y, y' \in \{0, \dots, b\} : (0, y) \leftrightarrow (a, y') \text{ in the open graph of } \omega.$$

For $a = 0$ the event always holds, taking $y = y'$.
