# Critical anisotropic percolation has the box-crossing property

In bond percolation on $\mathbb Z^2$ with horizontal edges open with probability $p_h$ and vertical edges with probability $p_v$ independently, let $H(a, b)$ be the event that an open path inside the box $[0, a] \times [0, b]$ joins its left and right sides. If $p_h + p_v = 1$ and $0 < p_h < 1$, then for every $k \ge 1$ there is $\delta > 0$ with $P(H(kn, n)) \ge \delta$ for all $n \ge 1$. This follows from the box-crossing property proved by Grimmett and Manolescu (2013).
