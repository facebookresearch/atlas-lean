# Box crossings in critical anisotropic percolation

In bond percolation on $\mathbb Z^2$ with horizontal edges open with probability $p_h$ and vertical edges with probability $p_v$ independently, let $H(a, b)$ be the event that an open path inside the box $[0, a] \times [0, b]$ joins its left and right sides. The claim (Björnberg's question) is that in the critical case $p_h + p_v = 1$, $0 < p_h < 1$, for every $k \ge 1$ the crossing probabilities $P(H(kn, n))$ are bounded below by a positive constant uniformly in $n \ge 1$.
