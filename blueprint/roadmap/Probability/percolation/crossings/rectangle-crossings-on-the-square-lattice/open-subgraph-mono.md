# Monotonicity of the open subgraph in the plane

If $\omega \le \omega'$ are bond configurations on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ ($\omega(e) \le \omega'(e)$ for every unordered pair $e$), then every edge of the [open subgraph](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/open-subgraph.md) of $\omega$ is an edge of the open subgraph of $\omega'$.
