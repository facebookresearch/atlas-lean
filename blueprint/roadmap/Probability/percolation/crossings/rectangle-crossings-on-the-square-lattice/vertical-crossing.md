# Open bottom–top crossing of a rectangle at the origin

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$. The configuration $\omega$ has an open bottom–top crossing of the [rectangle](rectangle.md) $R(a, b) = [0, a] \times [0, b]$ if some site of its [bottom side](bottom-side.md) is joined to some site of its [top side](top-side.md) by an open path [inside](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $R(a, b)$.
