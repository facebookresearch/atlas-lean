# Open left–right crossing of a rectangle at the origin

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$. The configuration $\omega$ has an open left–right crossing of the [rectangle](rectangle.md) $R(a, b) = [0, a] \times [0, b]$ if some site of its [left side](left-side.md) is joined to some site of its [right side](right-side.md) by an open path [inside](../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $R(a, b)$.
