# Critical bond percolation measure on the square lattice

The critical bond percolation measure $\mathbb{P}_{1/2}$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$: the [Bernoulli product measure](../../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) on $\{0, 1\}^E$, $E$ the set of unordered pairs of sites, under which every coordinate is open independently with probability $1/2$.
