# Russo–Seymour–Welsh theorem, two-sided form

For critical bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_{1/2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and every $\alpha > 0$ there is $c > 0$ such that

$$c \le \mathbb{P}_{1/2}\big(\text{open left–right crossing of } [0, \lfloor \alpha n \rfloor] \times [0, n]\big) \le 1 - c$$

for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\alpha n \ge 1$. The upper bound follows from the lower bound for the dual crossing.
