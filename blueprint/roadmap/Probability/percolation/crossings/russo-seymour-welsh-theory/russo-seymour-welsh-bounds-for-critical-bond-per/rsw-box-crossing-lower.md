# Russo–Seymour–Welsh theorem, lower bound

For critical bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_{1/2}$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and every $\alpha > 0$ there is $c > 0$ such that

$$\mathbb{P}_{1/2}\big(\text{open left–right crossing of } [0, \lfloor \alpha n \rfloor] \times [0, n]\big) \ge c \quad \text{for every } n \in \mathbb{N};$$

that is, $\mathbb{P}_{1/2}$ (constant in $n$) has the [box-crossing property](../rectangles-crossings-annuli-and-the-box-crossing/p5.md).
