# Rectangle lattice with an open cluster removed

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, let $\alpha, \beta, m, m', c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, $x_B \in O = [m, m'] \times [c, d]$, and let $C_O(x_B)$ be the [open cluster of $x_B$ inside](../../box-crossing-property-for-positively-associated/horizontal-and-vertical-crossings-meet/overlap-comp.md) $O$. The graph on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ in which $u$ and $v$ are adjacent when they are nearest neighbours, both lie in $[\alpha, \beta] \times [c, d]$, and neither lies in $C_O(x_B)$.
