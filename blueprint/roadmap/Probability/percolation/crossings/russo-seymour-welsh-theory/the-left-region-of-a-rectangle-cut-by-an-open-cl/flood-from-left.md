# Sites reachable from the left side of a rectangle

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, let $\alpha, \beta, m, m', c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $x_B \in O = [m, m'] \times [c, d]$. The set of sites $z$ reachable, in the [rectangle lattice with the open cluster $C_O(x_B)$ removed](box-minus-p.md), from some site $x$ of $[\alpha, \beta] \times [c, d]$ with $x_1 = \alpha$ and $x \notin C_O(x_B)$.
