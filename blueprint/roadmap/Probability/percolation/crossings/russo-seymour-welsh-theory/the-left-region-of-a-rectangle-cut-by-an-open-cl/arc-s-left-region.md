# Left region of a rectangle cut by an open cluster

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, let $\alpha, \beta, m, m', c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, let $O = [m, m'] \times [c, d]$ be the overlap rectangle and $x_B \in O$. The left region is the set of sites $z$ of $[\alpha, \beta] \times [c, d]$ that lie on its left side ($z_1 = \alpha$) or are [reachable from the left side](flood-from-left.md) without touching the open cluster $C_O(x_B)$ of $x_B$ inside $O$.
