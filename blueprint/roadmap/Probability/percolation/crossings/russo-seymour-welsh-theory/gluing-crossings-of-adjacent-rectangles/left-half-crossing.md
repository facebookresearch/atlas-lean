# Open path from the left side of a rectangle to a point

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $a, m, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, and let $z$ be a site of $R = [a, m] \times [c, d]$ (in applications, a site of its right side). The condition is that some site of the left side $\{x \in R : x_1 = a\}$ is joined to $z$ by an open path [inside](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $R$.
