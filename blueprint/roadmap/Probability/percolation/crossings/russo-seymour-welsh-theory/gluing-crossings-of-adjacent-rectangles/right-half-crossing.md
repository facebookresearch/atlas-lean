# Open path from a point to the right side of a rectangle

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $m, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, and let $z$ be a site of $R = [m, b] \times [c, d]$ (in applications, a site of its left side). The condition is that $z$ is joined to some site of the right side $\{x \in R : x_1 = b\}$ by an open path [inside](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $R$.
