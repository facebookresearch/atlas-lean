# Box-crossing property for a sequence of measures

A sequence $(\mu_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of real-valued functions on sets of bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ has the box-crossing property if for every $\alpha > 0$ there is $c > 0$ with

$$\mu_n\big(H_\alpha(n)\big) \ge c \quad \text{for all } n \in \mathbb{N},$$

where $H_\alpha(n)$ is the [left–right crossing event](box-crossing-event.md) of $[0, \lfloor \alpha n \rfloor] \times [0, n]$.
