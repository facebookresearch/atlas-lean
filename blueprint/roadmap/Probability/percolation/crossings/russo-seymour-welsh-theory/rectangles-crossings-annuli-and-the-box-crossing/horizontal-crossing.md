# Open left–right crossing of a general rectangle

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$. The configuration $\omega$ has an open left–right crossing of the [rectangle](rect.md) $R = [a, b] \times [c, d]$ if some site of its [left side](left-side.md) is joined to some site of its [right side](right-side.md) by an open path [inside](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $R$.
