# Dual left–right crossing steps

Let $R = [a,b] \times [c,d]$ for $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{Z}$. A primal configuration $\omega$ has a [dual left–right crossing](dual-horizontal-crossing.md) of $R$ if and only if there is a finite sequence of unit faces, with all lower-left corners in $R$, from a face whose lower-left corner has first coordinate $a$ to one whose lower-left corner has first coordinate $b$. Each consecutive pair consists of distinct faces that share a primal bond closed in $\omega$. The sequence may consist of a single face.
