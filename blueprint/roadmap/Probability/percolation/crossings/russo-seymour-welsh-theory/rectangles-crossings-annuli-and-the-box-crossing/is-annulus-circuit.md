# Open circuit lying in an annulus

For a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $z \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a closed walk $w$ in the open subgraph of $\omega$ lies in the annulus $z + (\Lambda_{2n} \setminus \Lambda_n)$ as an open circuit if it is an [open circuit](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/is-open-circuit.md) (positive length, no repeated edge) and all its vertices lie in the [annulus](annulus.md).
