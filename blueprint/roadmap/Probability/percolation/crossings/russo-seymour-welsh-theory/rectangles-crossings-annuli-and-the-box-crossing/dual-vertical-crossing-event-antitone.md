# Antitonicity of dual bottom–top crossings

Let $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, and suppose the primal configurations $\omega'$ and $\omega$ satisfy $\omega'(e) \le \omega(e)$ for every unordered pair $e$ of primal sites, with $0$ denoting closed. If $\omega$ belongs to the [dual bottom–top crossing event](dual-vertical-crossing-event.md) for $[a,b] \times [c,d]$, then $\omega'$ belongs to the same event.
