# Dual left–right crossing of a rectangle

Let $(\mathbb{Z}^2)^\ast = (\tfrac12, \tfrac12) + \mathbb{Z}^2$ be the dual lattice, whose vertices are the centres of the unit faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and whose edges join nearest neighbours; each edge $e$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ is crossed in its middle by exactly one dual edge $e^\ast$. For a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, the dual configuration is $\omega^\ast(e^\ast) = 1 - \omega(e)$: a dual edge is open exactly when the primal edge it crosses is closed.

For $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$, the configuration $\omega$ has a dual left–right crossing of $R = [a, b] \times [c, d]$ if $\omega^\ast$ has an open path of dual edges inside the dual rectangle $R^\ast = (\tfrac12, \tfrac12) + R$ from its left side $\{x_1 = a + \tfrac12\}$ to its right side $\{x_1 = b + \tfrac12\}$.
