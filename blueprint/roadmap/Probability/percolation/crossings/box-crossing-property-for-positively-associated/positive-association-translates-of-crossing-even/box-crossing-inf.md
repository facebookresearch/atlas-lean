# Infimum of the box-crossing probabilities

For a measure $\mu$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$, the infimum of the [set of long-direction box-crossing probabilities](box-crossing-probabilities.md) of $\mu$ with aspect ratio $\rho$ and sizes $n \ge n_0$. The set is nonempty and bounded below by $0$, so this is its greatest lower bound.
