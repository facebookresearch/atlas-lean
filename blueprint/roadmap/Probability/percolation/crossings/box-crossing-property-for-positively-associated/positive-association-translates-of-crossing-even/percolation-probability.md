# Percolation probability as an extended real

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0, 1]$, the percolation probability $\theta_d(p) = \mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow \infty) \in [0, \infty]$ (in fact in $[0, 1]$) of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the measure of the [event that the cluster of the origin is infinite](percolation-event.md).
