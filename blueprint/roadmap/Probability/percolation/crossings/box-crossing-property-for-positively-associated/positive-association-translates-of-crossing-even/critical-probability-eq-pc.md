# Agreement of the two critical probabilities

For every $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the [real-valued critical probability](critical-probability.md) $\sup \{p \in [0, 1] : \theta_d(p) = 0\}$ equals the [critical probability](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/pc.md) $p_c(d)$.
