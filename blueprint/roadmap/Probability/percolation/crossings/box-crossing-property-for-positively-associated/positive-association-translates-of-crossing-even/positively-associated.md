# Positively associated measure

Let $E$ be a set. A finite measure $\mu$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ is positively associated if

$$\mu(A)\, \mu(B) \le \mu(A \cap B)\, \mu(\{0, 1\}^E)$$

for all measurable [increasing](../../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) events $A, B$. The total-mass factor makes the Harris–FKG inequality invariant under multiplying $\mu$ by a positive constant.
