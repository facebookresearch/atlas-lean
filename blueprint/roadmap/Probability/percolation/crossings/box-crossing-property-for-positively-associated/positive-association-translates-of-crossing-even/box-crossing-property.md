# Box-crossing property with a given aspect ratio

A measure $\mu$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ has the box-crossing property with aspect ratio $\rho$ if $\rho > 1$ and there is $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ such that the [infimum of the box-crossing probabilities](box-crossing-inf.md) over sizes $n \ge n_0$ is positive: the probabilities of crossing all translates of $\lfloor \rho n \rfloor \times n$ (and $n \times \lfloor \rho n \rfloor$) rectangles in the long direction, $n \ge n_0$, are bounded below by a positive constant.
