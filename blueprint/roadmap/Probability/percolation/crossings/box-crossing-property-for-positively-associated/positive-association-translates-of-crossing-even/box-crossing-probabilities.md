# Set of long-direction box-crossing probabilities

For a measure $\mu$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n_0 \in \mathbb{N}$, the set of real numbers

$$\mu\big(H(\tau + [0, \lfloor \rho n \rfloor] \times [0, n])\big) \quad \text{and} \quad \mu\big(V(\tau + [0, n] \times [0, \lfloor \rho n \rfloor])\big), \qquad n \ge n_0,\ \tau \in \mathbb{Z}^2,$$

where $H(\cdot)$ and $V(\cdot)$ are the [left–right](translated-horizontal-crossing-event.md) and [bottom–top](translated-vertical-crossing-event.md) crossing events of the translated rectangles: the probabilities of crossing translates of $\lfloor \rho n \rfloor \times n$ and $n \times \lfloor \rho n \rfloor$ rectangles in the long direction.
