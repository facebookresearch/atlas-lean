# Open cluster inside the overlap rectangle

For a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, integers $m, m', c, d$ and a site $x_B$ of $O = [m, m'] \times [c, d]$, the open cluster of $x_B$ inside $O$:

$$C_O(x_B) = \{z \in O : x_B \xleftrightarrow{O} z\},$$

the sites of $O$ joined to $x_B$ by an open path [inside](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/connected-within.md) $O$.
