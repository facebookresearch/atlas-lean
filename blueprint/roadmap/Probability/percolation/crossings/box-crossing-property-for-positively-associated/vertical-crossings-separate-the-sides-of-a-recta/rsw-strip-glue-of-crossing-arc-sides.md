# RSW gluing from side separation

Let $\mu$ be a [positively associated](../positive-association-translates-of-crossing-even/positively-associated.md) probability measure on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, and let $a \le m \le m' \le b$ and $c, d$ be integers. For integers $s \le t$ write $H_{[s, t]}$ for the [left–right crossing event](../../russo-seymour-welsh-theory/rectangles-crossings-annuli-and-the-box-crossing/horizontal-crossing-event.md) of $[s, t] \times [c, d]$ and $V_{[s, t]}$ for its [bottom–top crossing event](../../russo-seymour-welsh-theory/rectangles-crossings-annuli-and-the-box-crossing/vertical-crossing-event.md). Assume that for every configuration $\omega$ the [vertical overlap crossings separate the sides](crossing-arc-sides.md) both of $[a, m'] \times [c, d]$ and of $[m, b] \times [c, d]$, with overlap $[m, m'] \times [c, d]$. Then

$$\mu(H_{[a, m']})\, \mu(V_{[m, m']})\, \mu(H_{[m, b]}) \le \mu(H_{[a, b]}).$$
