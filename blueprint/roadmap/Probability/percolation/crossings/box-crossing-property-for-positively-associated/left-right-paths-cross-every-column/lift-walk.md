# Walk in an induced open subgraph as a lattice walk

For a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and a set $R \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, a walk in the [open subgraph induced on](../../../phase-transition/percolation-probability-and-critical-point/open-subgraph-induce.md) $R$, read as a walk in the square lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$ through the same sites, its image under the [projection](proj-hom.md).
