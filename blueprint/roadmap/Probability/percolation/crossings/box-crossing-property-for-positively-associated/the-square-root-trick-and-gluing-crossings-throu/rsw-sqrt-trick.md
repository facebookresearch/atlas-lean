# Square-root trick

Let $E$ be a set, $\mu$ a [positively associated](../positive-association-translates-of-crossing-even/positively-associated.md) probability measure on $\{0, 1\}^E$, and $A, B$ measurable increasing events. Then

$$1 - \sqrt{1 - \mu(A \cup B)} \le \max\big(\mu(A), \mu(B)\big).$$
