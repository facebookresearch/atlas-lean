# Ka critical Probability eq half of self Dual Assembly

Abstract self-dual assembly lemma: `p_c(ℤ²) = 1/2` (the conclusion of Kesten's theorem, Kesten
1980), with the box-crossing hypothesis of `criticalProbability_eq_half_of_selfDualAssembly`
replaced by the RSW seed `hseed` (assumed) and translation invariance `htinv` (provable, see
`ka_criticalProbability_eq_half_of_selfDualAssembly_of_seed`). The other inputs are as in
`criticalProbability_eq_half_of_selfDualAssembly`: Harris's theorem as `hCorLowerBound`,
subcritical decay of the self-dual family `boxFam` as `hSharpThreshold`, and the self-duality
identity `hdich`.
