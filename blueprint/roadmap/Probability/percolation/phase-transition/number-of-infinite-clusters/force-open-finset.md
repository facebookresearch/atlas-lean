# Configuration with a finite edge set forced open

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $F$ be a finite set of unordered pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. The configuration $\omega^F$ obtained from $\omega$ by opening every edge of $F$ is

$$\omega^F(e) = \begin{cases} 1 & e \in F, \\ \omega(e) & e \notin F. \end{cases}$$
