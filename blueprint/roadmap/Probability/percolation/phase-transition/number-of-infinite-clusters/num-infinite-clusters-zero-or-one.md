# Zero or one infinite cluster, given ergodicity and $\mu(N \ge 2) = 0$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that is [ergodic](../../../random-fields/translation-invariance-and-ergodicity-of-random/is-ergodic.md) for the translations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. If the [event of at least two infinite open clusters](at-least-two-infinite.md) has $\mu(N \ge 2) = 0$, then either $\mu(N = 0) = 1$ or $\mu(N = 1) = 1$, where $N$ is the [number of infinite open clusters](num-infinite-clusters.md).
