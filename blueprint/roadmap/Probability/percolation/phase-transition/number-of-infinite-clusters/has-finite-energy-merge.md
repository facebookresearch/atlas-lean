# Finite energy for opening finite edge sets

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. A measure $\mu$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ has finite energy for opening edges if, for every finite set $F$ of unordered pairs of sites, the image of $\mu$ under the map $\omega \mapsto \omega^F$ that [opens every edge of $F$](force-open-finset.md) is absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$.
