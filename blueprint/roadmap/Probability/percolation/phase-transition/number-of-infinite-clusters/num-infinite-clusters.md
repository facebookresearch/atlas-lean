# Number of infinite open clusters

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. The number of infinite open clusters $N(\omega) \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots\} \cup \{\infty\}$ is the cardinality of the [set of infinite open clusters](infinite-clusters.md) of $\omega$, with $N(\omega) = \infty$ when that set is infinite.
