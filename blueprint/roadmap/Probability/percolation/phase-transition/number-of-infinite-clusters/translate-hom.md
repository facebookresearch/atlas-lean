# Translation of the open subgraph as a graph homomorphism

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, with [shift](../../../random-fields/translation-invariance-and-ergodicity-of-random/shift.md) $(g \cdot \omega)(\{x, y\}) = \omega(\{x - g, y - g\})$. Translation by $g$, $x \mapsto x + g$, is a graph homomorphism from the [open subgraph](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/open-subgraph.md) of $\omega$ to the open subgraph of $g \cdot \omega$.
