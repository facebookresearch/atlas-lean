# Configurations with $k$ infinite clusters, two merged by opening $F$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $F$ be a finite set of unordered pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $k \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots\} \cup \{\infty\}$. The event considered is the set of bond configurations $\omega$ with exactly $k$ [infinite open clusters](num-infinite-clusters.md) for which there are sites $x, y$ whose open clusters $C(x)$ and $C(y)$ are infinite and distinct, and such that $x$ and $y$ are connected by an open path in the configuration $\omega^F$ obtained by [opening every edge of $F$](force-open-finset.md).
