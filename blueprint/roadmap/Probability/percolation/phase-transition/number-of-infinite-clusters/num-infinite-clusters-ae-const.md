# Number of infinite clusters is almost surely constant

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ that is [ergodic](../../../random-fields/translation-invariance-and-ergodicity-of-random/is-ergodic.md) for the translations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then there is $k \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots\} \cup \{\infty\}$ such that the [number of infinite open clusters](num-infinite-clusters.md) satisfies $\mu(N = k) = 1$.

## Sources

- Charles M. Newman and Lawrence S. Schulman, *Infinite clusters in percolation models*, J. Stat. Phys. 26 (1981),
  no. 3, 613–628, DOI 10.1007/bf01011437.
