# Inverse translation of the open subgraph

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $g \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Translation by $-g$, $x \mapsto x - g$, is a graph homomorphism from the [open subgraph](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/open-subgraph.md) of the shifted configuration $g \cdot \omega$ to the open subgraph of $\omega$; it is the inverse of the [translation homomorphism](translate-hom.md).
