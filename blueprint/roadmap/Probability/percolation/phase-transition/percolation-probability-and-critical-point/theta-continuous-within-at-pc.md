# Right-continuity of $\theta$ at $p_c$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the [percolation probability](theta-on-unit.md) $p \mapsto \theta_d(p)$, as a function on $[0, 1]$, is right-continuous at $p_c(d)$: $\theta_d(p) \to \theta_d(p_c(d))$ as $p \downarrow p_c(d)$ within $[0, 1]$.

## Background

- [Continuity of $\theta$ away from $p_c$](theta-continuous-on-ne-pc.md): the companion statement; left-continuity at $p_c$ is the open question whether $\theta(p_c) = 0$, settled on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ by [θ(1/2) = 0](../critical-probability-of-the-square-lattice/theta-two-half-eq-zero.md).
