# Open subgraph induced on a set of sites

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^d$. The open subgraph of $\omega$ induced on $S$ is the graph with vertex set $S$ in which $x, y \in S$ are adjacent exactly when the edge $\{x, y\}$ is [open](is-open-edge.md) in $\omega$.
