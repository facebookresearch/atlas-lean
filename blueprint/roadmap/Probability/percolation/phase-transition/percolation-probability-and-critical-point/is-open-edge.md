# Open lattice edge of a bond configuration

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. A bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ is a map $\omega : E \to \{0, 1\}$, where $E$ is the set of unordered pairs $\{x, y\}$ of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. For sites $x, y$, the edge $\{x, y\}$ is open in $\omega$ if $x$ and $y$ are nearest neighbours of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ ($\sum_i |x_i - y_i| = 1$) and $\omega(\{x, y\}) = 1$. The values of $\omega$ on pairs that are not nearest neighbours play no role.
