# Open cluster of a site

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$. The open cluster of $x$ is the set

$$C(x) = \{y \in \mathbb{Z}^d : x \leftrightarrow y\}$$

of sites [connected](connected.md) to $x$ by an open path. It always contains $x$.
