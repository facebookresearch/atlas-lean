# Critical probability of bond percolation on the hypercubic lattice

For $d \in \mathbb{N}$, the critical probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ is

$$p_c(d) = \sup \{p \in [0, 1] : \theta_d(p) = 0\},$$

the supremum of the [set of densities with no percolation](subcritical-set.md), where $\theta_d$ is the [percolation probability](theta.md). The set contains $p = 0$ and is contained in $[0, 1]$, so $p_c(d) \in [0, 1]$.

The [critical point of a general countable graph](../percolation-on-product-graphs/critical-prob.md) is defined through the event that *some* infinite cluster exists; for $\mathbb{Z}^d$ the two definitions give the same value, by translation invariance.
