# Percolation probability of bond percolation

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0, 1]$. Let $\mathbb{P}_p$ be the [Bernoulli product measure](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, under which the values $\omega(e)$ are independent and equal to $1$ with probability $p$. The percolation probability is

$$\theta_d(p) = \mathbb{P}_p(0 \leftrightarrow \infty),$$

the probability of the [percolation event](percolation-event.md) that the open cluster of the origin is infinite.
