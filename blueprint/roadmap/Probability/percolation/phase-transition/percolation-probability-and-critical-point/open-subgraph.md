# Open subgraph of a bond configuration

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. The open subgraph of $\omega$ is the spanning subgraph of the nearest-neighbour lattice $\mathbb{Z}^d$ whose edges are the [open edges](is-open-edge.md) of $\omega$: two sites are adjacent exactly when they are nearest neighbours and the edge between them is open.
