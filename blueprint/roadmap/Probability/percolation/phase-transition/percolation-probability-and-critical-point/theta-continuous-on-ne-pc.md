# Continuity of $\theta$ away from $p_c$

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$. For Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the [percolation probability](theta-on-unit.md) $p \mapsto \theta_d(p)$, as a function on $[0, 1]$, is continuous on $[0, 1] \setminus \{p_c(d)\}$.

## Informally uses

- [Burton–Keane theorem](../uniqueness-of-the-infinite-cluster/burton-keane-bernoulli.md): the source's proof of continuity from the left above $p_c$ uses the uniqueness of the infinite cluster (Theorem 3.1 there).
