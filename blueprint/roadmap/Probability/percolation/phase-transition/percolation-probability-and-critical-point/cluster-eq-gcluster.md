# Lattice clusters agree with general graph clusters

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. For every site $x$, the [open cluster](cluster.md) $C(x)$ of $x$ in $\omega$ equals the [open cluster](../percolation-on-product-graphs/gcluster.md) of $x$ for bond percolation with configuration $\omega$ on the graph $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with its nearest-neighbour edges.
