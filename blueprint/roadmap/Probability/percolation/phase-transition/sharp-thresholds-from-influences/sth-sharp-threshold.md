# Sharp threshold from influences

Let $E$ be a finite set and let $A \subseteq \{0, 1\}^E$ be an [increasing](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/increasing-events-and-the-fkg-lattice-condition/is-increasing.md) event. For real $p$ let $\mathbb{P}_p(A)$ be the [weight](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/russo-s-formula-and-pivotal-coordinates/prob.md) $\sum_{\omega \in A} \prod_{e} p^{\omega_e}(1 - p)^{1 - \omega_e}$ and $I_e^p(A)$ the [influence](influence.md) of $e$ on $A$. Let $q \ge 1/2$ and $M$ be real numbers. If $\mathbb{P}_{1/2}(A) = 1/2$ and

$$\sum_{e \in E} I_e^p(A) \ge M \quad \text{for all } p \in (1/2, q),$$

then

$$\mathbb{P}_q(A) \ge \frac{1}{2} + M\Big(q - \frac{1}{2}\Big).$$
