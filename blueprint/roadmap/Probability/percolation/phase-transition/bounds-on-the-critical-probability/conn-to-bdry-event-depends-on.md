# Connection to the boundary depends only on graph edges

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph on a vertex set $V$, let $B \subseteq V$ be a set of boundary vertices and let $x \in V$. For configurations $\omega \in \{0, 1\}^{\mathcal{P}}$, where $\mathcal{P}$ is the set of unordered pairs of elements of $V$, consider the event that $x$ is joined to some vertex of $B$ by a path of edges $e$ of $G$ with $\omega(e) = 1$. Its indicator depends only on the coordinates $\omega(e)$ for $e$ an edge of $G$: two configurations that agree on the edges of $G$ both belong to the event or both do not.
