# Primal edges crossed by a dual walk

Label the unit faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ by their lower-left corners, so that a walk $c$ in the square lattice is read as a walk of faces in the dual lattice. The primal edges crossed by $c$ form the finite set of lattice edges separating two consecutive faces of $c$: for adjacent faces with corners $f$ and $g$, the separating edge is $\{(f_1, m), (f_1 + 1, m)\}$ with $m = \max(f_2, g_2)$ if $f_1 = g_1$, and $\{(m', f_2), (m', f_2 + 1)\}$ with $m' = \max(f_1, g_1)$ otherwise.
