# Wired $q = 2$ origin-to-boundary probability at most Bernoulli

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 < p < 1$, $\Lambda_n = [-n, n]^d \cap \mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\partial \Lambda_n = \Lambda_n \setminus \Lambda_{n-1}$ (with $n - 1$ truncated at $0$). Let $A_n$ be the event that the origin is joined to $\partial \Lambda_n$ by a path of open edges of $\Lambda_n$. Let $\phi^1_{\Lambda_n, p, 2}$ be the wired random-cluster measure on $\Lambda_n$ with parameters $p$ and $q = 2$, transported to bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$. Then

$$\phi^1_{\Lambda_n, p, 2}(A_n) \le \mathbb{P}_p(A_n).$$
