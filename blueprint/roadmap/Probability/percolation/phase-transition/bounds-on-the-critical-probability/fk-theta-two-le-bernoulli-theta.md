# Wired $q = 2$ percolation probability at most the Bernoulli one

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 < p < 1$. Let $\theta^1(p, 2) = \phi^1_{p, 2}(0 \leftrightarrow \infty)$ be the percolation probability of the wired infinite-volume random-cluster measure on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with parameters $p$ and $q = 2$ (a weak limit along a subsequence of the wired measures on the boxes $\Lambda_n$). Then

$$\theta^1(p, 2) \le \theta_d(p),$$

where $\theta_d$ is the [percolation probability](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/theta.md) of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$.
