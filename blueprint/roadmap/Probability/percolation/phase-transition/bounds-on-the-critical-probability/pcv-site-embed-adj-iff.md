# Plane embedding preserves and reflects adjacency

For $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, the sites $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ are nearest neighbours in $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$ if and only if $x$ and $y$ are nearest neighbours in $\mathbb{Z}^2$, where $\iota$ is the [plane embedding](pcv-site-embed.md).
