# Anchored dual cycles of a given length

For $\ell \in \mathbb{N}$, the finite set of pairs $(u, c)$ where $u$ is one of the [anchor sites](anchor-finset.md) $(j, 0), (j, -1)$, $0 \le j < \ell$, and $c$ is a cycle of length $\ell$ in the square lattice from $u$ to $u$, read as a cycle of unit faces in the dual lattice.
