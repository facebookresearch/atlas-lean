# Bernoulli percolation restricted to the plane is planar Bernoulli

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0, 1]$. The image of Bernoulli bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$ under the [restriction to the embedded plane](pcv-plane-restrict.md) is Bernoulli bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ (both as [Bernoulli product measures](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) on configurations of unordered pairs of sites).
