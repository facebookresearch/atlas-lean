# Path-counting lower bound on the critical probability

For bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 1$,

$$p_c(d) \ge \frac{1}{2d}.$$

A self-avoiding path of length $n$ from the origin is open with probability $p^n$ and there are at most $(2d)^n$ of them, so $\theta(p) \le (2dp)^n \to 0$ for $p < 1/(2d)$.

## Background

- [Positivity of the critical probability](pc-pos.md): the weaker statement $0 < p_c(d)$, proved with $p = 1/(4d)$.
- [Non-triviality of the percolation critical probability](pc-nontrivial-of-two-le.md): the matching upper bound $p_c(d) < 1$ for $d \ge 2$.
