# Positivity of the critical probability (Hammersley's bound)

For bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 1$, $p_c(d) > 0$. The proof counts paths and uses $p = 1/(4d)$; the explicit bound $p_c(d) \ge 1/(2d)$ is the [path-counting lower bound](one-div-two-mul-le-pc.md).

## Sources

- John M. Hammersley, *Percolation processes: lower bounds for the critical probability*, Ann. Math. Statist. 28
  (1957), no. 3, 790–795, DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177706894.
