# Bernoulli percolation restricted to a box is Bernoulli on the box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0, 1]$ and $\Lambda_n = [-n, n]^d \cap \mathbb{Z}^d$. The image of the [Bernoulli product measure](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) $\mathbb{P}_p$ on bond configurations of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ under the restriction map $\omega \mapsto \omega|_{\Lambda_n}$ to the unordered pairs of sites of $\Lambda_n$ is the Bernoulli product measure with parameter $p$ on configurations of those pairs.
