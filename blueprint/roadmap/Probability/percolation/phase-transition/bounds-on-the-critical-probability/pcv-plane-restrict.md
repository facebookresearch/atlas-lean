# Restriction of a configuration to the embedded plane

For $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and a bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$, the restriction to the plane is the bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ given by $\{x, y\} \mapsto \omega(\{\iota(x), \iota(y)\})$, where $\iota$ is the [plane embedding](pcv-site-embed.md).
