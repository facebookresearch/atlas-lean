# Vertices of a lattice path given by a step sequence

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $g : \mathbb{N} \to \{1, \dots, d\} \times \{+1, -1\}$, $g(j) = (i_j, s_j)$. The lattice path given by $g$ starts at the origin and takes the step $s_j e_{i_j}$ at time $j$: its vertices are $v_0 = 0$ and $v_{k+1} = v_k + s_k e_{i_k}$.
