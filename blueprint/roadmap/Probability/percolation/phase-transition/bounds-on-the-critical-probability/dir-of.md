# Step directions of an open walk from the origin

Let $d \ge 1$, let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $q = (z_0 = 0, z_1, \dots, z_m)$ be a walk from the origin in the [open subgraph](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/open-subgraph.md) of $\omega$. For $k \in \mathbb{N}$, the direction of the $k$-th step is a pair $(i, s) \in \{1, \dots, d\} \times \{+1, -1\}$ with $z_{k+1} = z_k + s\, e_i$ when $k < m$; for $k \ge m$ it is $(1, -1)$ by convention.
