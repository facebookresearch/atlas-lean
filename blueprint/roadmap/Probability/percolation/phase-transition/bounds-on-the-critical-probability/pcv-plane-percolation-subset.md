# Percolation in the plane implies percolation in $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$. If $\omega$ is a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$ whose [restriction to the plane](pcv-plane-restrict.md) lies in the percolation event $\{0 \leftrightarrow \infty\}$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$, then $\omega$ lies in the percolation event of $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$.
