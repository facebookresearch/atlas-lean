# Peierls contour bound on the percolation probability

For $p \in [0, 1]$, the Peierls contour bound at density $p$ is the inequality

$$1 - \theta_2(p) \le \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, 4^\ell (1 - p)^\ell$$

between the percolation probability of bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and the [Peierls series](pc-upper-bound.md), the series being read as $0$ when it diverges (that is, when $p \le 3/4$).
