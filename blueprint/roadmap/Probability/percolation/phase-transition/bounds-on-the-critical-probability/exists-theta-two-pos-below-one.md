# Positive planar percolation at some $p < 1$ via the Ising model

There is $p \in [0, 1)$ such that the [percolation probability](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/theta.md) of Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ satisfies $\theta_2(p) > 0$.

The argument goes through spontaneous magnetisation of the two-dimensional Ising model: by the Edwards–Sokal coupling the magnetisation is the wired $q = 2$ random-cluster percolation probability, which is positive at large inverse temperature by the Peierls argument and is at most the Bernoulli percolation probability.
