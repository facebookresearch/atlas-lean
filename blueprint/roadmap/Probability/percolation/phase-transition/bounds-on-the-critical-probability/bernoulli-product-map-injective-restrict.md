# Bernoulli product pulled back along an injection is Bernoulli

Let $I$ and $E$ be countable sets, $p \in [0, 1]$ and $f : I \to E$ injective. The image of the [Bernoulli product measure](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ on $\{0, 1\}^E$ under the map $\omega \mapsto \omega \circ f$ is the Bernoulli product measure with parameter $p$ on $\{0, 1\}^I$.
