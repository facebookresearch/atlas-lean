# Plane open connections are open connections in $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$ and let $\iota : \mathbb{Z}^2 \to \mathbb{Z}^{2+k}$ be the [plane embedding](pcv-site-embed.md). If $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ are connected by an open path in the [restriction](pcv-plane-restrict.md) of $\omega$ to the plane, then $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ are connected by an open path of $\omega$.
