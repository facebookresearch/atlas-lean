# Anchored dual-circuit enclosure property

Label the unit faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ by their lower-left corners. The anchored enclosure property holds if for every bond configuration $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite, the face-boundary graph of $C$ (faces adjacent when they share a side that is a lattice edge with exactly one endpoint in $C$) contains a cycle of some length $\ell$ based at one of the [anchor sites](anchor-finset.md) $(j, 0), (j, -1)$ with $0 \le j < \ell$.
