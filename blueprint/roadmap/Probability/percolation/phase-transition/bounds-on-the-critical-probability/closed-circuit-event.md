# Event that a dual circuit is closed

Label the unit faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ by their lower-left corners, so that a walk $c$ in the square lattice is read as a walk of faces in the dual lattice. The event that $c$ is closed is the set of bond configurations $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with $\omega(e) = 0$ for every [primal edge](primal-edge-finset.md) $e$ separating two consecutive faces of $c$.
