# Bernoulli box cylinder probability equals its probability on the box

Let $d, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in [0, 1]$, and let $\Lambda_n = [-n, n]^d \cap \mathbb{Z}^d$. Let $A$ be a set of configurations on the unordered pairs of sites of $\Lambda_n$, and let $\{\omega : \omega|_{\Lambda_n} \in A\}$ be the corresponding cylinder event for bond configurations $\omega$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$, where $\omega|_{\Lambda_n}$ is the restriction of $\omega$ to pairs of sites of $\Lambda_n$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p\big(\omega|_{\Lambda_n} \in A\big) = \mathbb{P}_p^{\Lambda_n}(A),$$

where $\mathbb{P}_p$ and $\mathbb{P}_p^{\Lambda_n}$ are the [Bernoulli product measures](../../../random-fields/bernoulli-product-measures-and-cylinder-events/bernoulli-product-measure.md) with parameter $p$ on configurations of the unordered pairs of sites of $\mathbb{Z}^d$ and of $\Lambda_n$ respectively.
