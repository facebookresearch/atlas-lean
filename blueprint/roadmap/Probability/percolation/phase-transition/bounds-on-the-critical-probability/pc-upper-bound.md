# Peierls series bounding closed dual circuits

For $p \in \mathbb{R}$, the Peierls series is

$$\sum_{\ell \ge 0} \ell\, 4^\ell (1 - p)^\ell,$$

which bounds the probability of a closed dual circuit around the origin. It converges exactly when $|4(1 - p)| < 1$, that is $3/4 < p < 5/4$; otherwise its value is $0$ by convention.
