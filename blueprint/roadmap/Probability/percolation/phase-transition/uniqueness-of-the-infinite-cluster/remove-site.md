# Configuration with all edges at a site closed

Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $x \in \mathbb{Z}^d$ and let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^d$. The configuration $\omega_x$ obtained by closing every edge at $x$ is $\omega_x(e) = 0$ if $x \in e$ and $\omega_x(e) = \omega(e)$ otherwise, for every unordered pair $e$ of sites.
