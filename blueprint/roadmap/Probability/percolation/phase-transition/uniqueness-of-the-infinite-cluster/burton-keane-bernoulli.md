# Burton–Keane theorem

For every $d \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $p \in [0, 1]$, under Bernoulli bond percolation $\mathbb{P}_p$ on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ there is almost surely at most one infinite open cluster: the [number of infinite open clusters](../number-of-infinite-clusters/num-infinite-clusters.md) satisfies $N \le 1$ $\mathbb{P}_p$-almost surely.

## Sources

- Robert M. Burton and Michael Keane, *Density and uniqueness in percolation*, Comm. Math. Phys. 121 (1989), no. 3,
  501–505, DOI 10.1007/bf01217735.
