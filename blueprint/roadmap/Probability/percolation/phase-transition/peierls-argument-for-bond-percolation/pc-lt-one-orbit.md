# $p_c(\mathbb{Z}^2) < 1$ assuming a canonical orbit cycle

Assume that the [canonical orbit cycle condition](canonical-orbit-cycle.md) holds for every bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster of the origin is finite. Then the [critical probability](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/pc.md) of bond percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ satisfies $p_c(2) < 1$.
