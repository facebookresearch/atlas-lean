# Origin cluster's enclosing contour links the two axis exits

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite. The condition is that some cycle of the face-boundary graph of $C$ (see [anchored dual cycles](anchored-cycle-exists.md)) based at the [upper exit face](exit-face-up.md) on the positive axis passes through the [upper left exit face](left-exit-face-up.md) on the negative axis.
