# Face loop of a boundary orbit in the face-boundary graph

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ be finite and let $e$ be a boundary dart of $K$. Since $K$ has finitely many boundary darts, $e$ is periodic under the successor map $\sigma$: fix some $n \ge 1$ with $\sigma^n(e) = e$. The face loop of $e$ is the [face walk along the boundary orbit](dart-orbit-face-walk.md) of $e$ of length $n$, a closed walk at the [face to the left](dart-face.md) of $e$ in the face-boundary graph of $K$. The chosen period $n$ is some period of $e$, not necessarily the minimal one.
