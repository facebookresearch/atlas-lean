# Inside region of an open cluster in the cut lattice

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $o \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. The inside region is the set of sites reachable from $o$ in the [cut lattice](cut-lattice.md) of the [open cluster](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/cluster.md) $C(o)$.
