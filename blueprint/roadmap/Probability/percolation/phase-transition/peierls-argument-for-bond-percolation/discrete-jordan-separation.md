# Discrete Jordan separation for finite open clusters

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and let $o \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ be a site whose [open cluster](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/cluster.md) $C(o)$ is finite. Let $G$ be the square lattice with every edge having exactly one endpoint in $C(o)$ removed. Then there is $R \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

- $C(o) \subseteq [-R, R]^2$;
- any two sites of $C(o)$ are connected in $G$;
- any two sites of the exterior $\{x \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : |x_1| > R \text{ or } |x_2| > R\}$ are connected in $G$;
- no site of $C(o)$ is connected in $G$ to a site outside $C(o)$.
