# Open cluster maps into the lattice minus its boundary edges

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, let $o \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ and let $C(o)$ be the [open cluster](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/cluster.md) of $o$. The inclusion $C(o) \hookrightarrow \mathbb{Z}^2$ is a graph homomorphism from the open subgraph of $\omega$ induced on $C(o)$ to the square lattice with every edge having exactly one endpoint in $C(o)$ removed.
