# Box exterior maps into the lattice minus boundary edges

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$ and $R \in \mathbb{N}$ be such that the exterior $\{x \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : |x_1| > R \text{ or } |x_2| > R\}$ of the box $[-R, R]^2$ is disjoint from $K$. The inclusion of the exterior into $\mathbb{Z}^2$ is a graph homomorphism from the square lattice induced on the exterior to the square lattice with every edge having exactly one endpoint in $K$ removed.
