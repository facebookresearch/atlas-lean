# Unit face to the left of a dart

Let $e$ be a [dart](exit-dart.md) of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with tail $t$ and direction $u = \mathrm{head} - \mathrm{tail}$, a unit vector, and let $u' = (-u_2, u_1)$ be $u$ rotated counter-clockwise by a quarter turn. The face to the left of $e$ is the unit face with lower-left corner

$$\big(t_1 + u_1^- + {u'_1}^-,\ t_2 + u_2^- + {u'_2}^-\big),$$

where $z^- = \min(z, 0)$ is the [negative part](neg-part.md) of an integer.
