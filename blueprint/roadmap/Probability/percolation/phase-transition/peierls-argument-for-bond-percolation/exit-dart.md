# Exit dart on the positive horizontal axis

A dart of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ is an ordered pair $(x, y)$ of nearest-neighbour sites, with tail $x$ and head $y$. Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite and let $j$ be its [exit index](exit-index.md). The exit dart is the dart from $(j, 0)$ to $(j + 1, 0)$. Its tail lies in $C$ and its head does not, so it is a boundary dart of $C$.
