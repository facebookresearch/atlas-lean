# Face walk along the first successors of a boundary dart

Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^2$, let $e$ be a boundary dart of $K$ (its tail lies in $K$ and its head does not), let $\sigma$ be the successor map on boundary darts of $K$ and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The walk in the face-boundary graph of $K$ through the [faces to the left](dart-face.md) of $e, \sigma(e), \dots, \sigma^n(e)$, consecutive faces being adjacent (for $n = 0$ it is the trivial walk at the face of $e$). It is the same walk as the [face walk along a boundary orbit](dart-orbit-face-walk.md).
