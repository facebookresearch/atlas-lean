# Face loop of the exit orbit of a finite cluster

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite. The face loop of the exit orbit is the [face loop of the boundary orbit](dart-orbit-face-loop.md) of the [exit dart](exit-dart.md) of $C$, a closed walk in the face-boundary graph of $C$ based at the [upper exit face](exit-face-up.md).
