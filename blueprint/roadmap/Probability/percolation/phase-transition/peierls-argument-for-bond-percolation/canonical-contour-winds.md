# Canonical contour is longer than the exit index

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite. The canonical contour winds if the minimal period of the [exit dart](exit-dart.md) under the successor map on boundary darts of $C$, which is the length of the [canonical contour](canonical-contour.md), is greater than the [exit index](exit-index.md) $j$. This is the length condition of an [anchored enclosure](../bounds-on-the-critical-probability/pc-anchored-enclosure.md).
