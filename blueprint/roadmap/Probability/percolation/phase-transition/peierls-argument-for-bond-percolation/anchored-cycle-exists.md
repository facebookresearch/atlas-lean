# Existence of an anchored dual cycle around the origin cluster

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose [open cluster](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/cluster.md) $C$ of the origin is finite. Label the unit faces of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ by their lower-left corners, the face $(a, b)$ being the square $[a, a + 1] \times [b, b + 1]$. The face-boundary graph of $C$ has the faces as vertices, two faces being adjacent when they share a side and that side is a lattice edge with exactly one endpoint in $C$.

An anchored dual cycle around $C$ exists if the face-boundary graph of $C$ contains a cycle based at the [upper exit face](exit-face-up.md) $(j, 0)$ whose length is greater than the [exit index](exit-index.md) $j$.
