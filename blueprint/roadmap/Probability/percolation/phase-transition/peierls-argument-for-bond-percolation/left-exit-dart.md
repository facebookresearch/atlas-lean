# Exit dart on the negative horizontal axis

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite and let $k$ be its [left exit index](left-exit-index.md). The left exit dart is the [dart](exit-dart.md) from $(-k, 0)$ to $(-k - 1, 0)$; its tail lies in $C$ and its head does not.
