# Darts of the canonical contour

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite, let $e$ be its [exit dart](exit-dart.md), let $\sigma$ be the successor map on boundary darts of $C$ (see the [face walk along a boundary orbit](dart-orbit-face-walk.md)) and let $T$ be the minimal period of $e$ under $\sigma$. The darts of the canonical contour are the list $\sigma(e), \sigma^2(e), \dots, \sigma^T(e)$; the last one is $e$ itself.
