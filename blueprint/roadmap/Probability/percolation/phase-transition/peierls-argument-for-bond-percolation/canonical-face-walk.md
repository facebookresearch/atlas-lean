# Closed face walk of the canonical contour

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite, let $e$ be its [exit dart](exit-dart.md) and $T$ the minimal period of $e$ under the successor map $\sigma$ on boundary darts of $C$. The closed face walk of the canonical contour is the walk through the [faces to the left](dart-face.md) of $e, \sigma(e), \dots, \sigma^T(e) = e$ in the face-boundary graph of $C$; it starts and ends at the [upper exit face](exit-face-up.md), the face to the left of $e$.
