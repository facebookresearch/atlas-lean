# Simplicity of the exit orbit's face walk

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite, let $e$ be its [exit dart](exit-dart.md) and $T$ the minimal period of $e$ under the successor map $\sigma$ on boundary darts of $C$. The exit orbit is simple if $T \ge 3$ and the [faces to the left](dart-face.md) of $\sigma^k(e)$, $0 \le k < T$, are pairwise distinct.
