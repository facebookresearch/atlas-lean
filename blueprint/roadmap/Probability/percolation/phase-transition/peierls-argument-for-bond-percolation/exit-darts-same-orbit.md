# Left and right exit darts lie in the same boundary orbit

Let $\omega$ be a bond configuration on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ whose open cluster $C$ of the origin is finite, and let $\sigma$ be the successor map on boundary darts of $C$ (see the [face walk along a boundary orbit](dart-orbit-face-walk.md)). The condition is that $\sigma^n(e) = e'$ for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$, where $e$ is the [exit dart](exit-dart.md) on the positive axis and $e'$ the [exit dart on the negative axis](left-exit-dart.md).
