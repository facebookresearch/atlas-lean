# Quasi-transitive graph

A graph $G$ with vertex set $V$ is quasi-transitive if its automorphism group acts on $V$ with finitely many orbits: there is a finite set $F \subseteq V$ such that for every $v \in V$ there are an automorphism $\varphi$ of $G$ and $u \in F$ with $\varphi(u) = v$. Benjamini and Schramm call such graphs almost transitive.
