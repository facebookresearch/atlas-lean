# Ends of infinite clusters in the nonuniqueness phase

For Bernoulli($p$) bond percolation on an infinite, connected, locally finite, quasi-transitive graph, let $p_c$ be the critical probability and $p_u$ the uniqueness threshold. The claim (Benjamini and Schramm's Conjecture 5 away from $p_c$) is that for $p_c < p < p_u$, almost surely every infinite open cluster has exactly $2^{\aleph_0}$ ends.
