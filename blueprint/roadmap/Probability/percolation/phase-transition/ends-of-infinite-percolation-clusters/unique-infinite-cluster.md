# Unique infinite open cluster

Let $G$ be a simple graph with vertex set $V$ and let $\omega$ assign open or closed to every unordered pair of vertices. For $x \in V$ let $C_x$ be the open cluster of $x$, the set of vertices joined to $x$ by a path of edges $e$ of $G$ with $\omega(e)$ open. The configuration $\omega$ has a unique infinite cluster if there is $x \in V$ with $C_x$ infinite such that $C_y = C_x$ for every $y \in V$ with $C_y$ infinite.
