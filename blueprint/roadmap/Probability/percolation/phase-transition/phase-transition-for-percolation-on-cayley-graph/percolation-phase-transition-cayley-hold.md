# Non-virtually-cyclic groups have a percolation phase transition

Let $p_c$ denote the critical probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on a Cayley graph. Every infinite Cayley graph of a finitely generated group that is not virtually cyclic has $p_c < 1$ (Duminil-Copin, Goswami, Raoufi, Severo and Yadin, 2020).

## Sources

- Problem: M. Abért, Some questions (2010), Question 26 (Lyons), https://www.renyi.hu/~abert/questions.pdf.
