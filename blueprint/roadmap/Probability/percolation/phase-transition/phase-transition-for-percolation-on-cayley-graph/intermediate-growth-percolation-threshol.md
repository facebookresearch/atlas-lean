# Percolation on groups of intermediate growth

Let $p_c$ denote the critical probability of Bernoulli bond percolation on a Cayley graph. The claim (AimPL amenability problem list, Problem 3.7) is that if a countable group has a finite generating family whose growth function is not bounded by any polynomial and is eventually at most $b^n$ for every $b > 1$, then its Cayley graph for that family has $p_c < 1$.
