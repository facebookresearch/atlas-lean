# No infinite cluster at $p = 1/2$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$

For Bernoulli bond percolation on the square lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$, the [percolation probability](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/theta.md) vanishes at $p = 1/2$:

$$\theta_2(1/2) = \mathbb{P}_{1/2}(0 \leftrightarrow \infty) = 0.$$

In particular $p_c(2) \ge 1/2$.

## Informally uses

- [Burton–Keane theorem](../uniqueness-of-the-infinite-cluster/burton-keane-bernoulli.md): uniqueness of the infinite cluster (Theorem 3.1 of the lecture notes), used in the proof of Proposition 4.1 there: if $\theta(p) > 0$, the crossing probability of $R_n$ tends to $1$.
- [Harris inequality](../../../correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/harris-inequality/harris-inequality.md): through the square-root trick (Exercise 11 of the lecture notes), used in the proof of Proposition 4.1.
