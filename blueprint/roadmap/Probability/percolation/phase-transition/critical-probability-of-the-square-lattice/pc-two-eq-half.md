# Kesten's theorem

The [critical probability](../percolation-probability-and-critical-point/pc.md) of Bernoulli bond percolation on the square lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$ equals $1/2$:

$$p_c(2) = \frac{1}{2}.$$

Together with [the absence of an infinite cluster at $p = 1/2$](theta-two-half-eq-zero.md) this gives the second assertion of the source theorem, that there is no infinite cluster at $p_c$.

## Informally uses

- [No infinite cluster at $p = 1/2$ on $\mathbb{Z}^2$](theta-two-half-eq-zero.md): gives $p_c(2) \ge 1/2$ (Corollary 4.2 of the lecture notes).
- [Exponential decay below $p_c$](../../sharpness/sharpness-of-the-phase-transition-for-bernoulli/cross-prob-exp-decay-of-lt-pc.md): for $p < p_c$ the probability of a left–right open crossing of $R_n = [0, n] \times [0, n - 1]$ tends to $0$, while it equals $1/2$ at $p = 1/2$ by self-duality (equation (4.1) of the lecture notes); hence $p_c \le 1/2$.

## Background

- [Russo–Seymour–Welsh theory](../../crossings/russo-seymour-welsh-theory/README.md): the crossing estimates that describe percolation at $p_c = 1/2$.
