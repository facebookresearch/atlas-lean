# Spectral radius of simple random walk

Let $G$ be a locally finite simple graph with vertex set $V$ and $o \in V$, and let $p_k(o, \cdot)$ be the $k$-step law of simple random walk on $G$ started at $o$. The spectral radius of $G$ seen from $o$ is

$$\rho(G) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} p_{2n}(o, o)^{1/(2n)}.$$

For vertex-transitive graphs it does not depend on the root $o$.
