# One-sided Fisher exact p-value

For a $2 \times 2$ table with group sizes $n_1$ and $n_2$ and success margin $t \le n_1 + n_2$, the upper
Fisher exact p-value of an observed cell count $x$ is the probability of a count at least $x$ under the
hypergeometric law with population $n_1 + n_2$, $n_1$ marked items and $t$ draws.
