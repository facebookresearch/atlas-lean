# Fisher's conditional law as a hypergeometric law

Let $X \sim \mathrm{Bin}(n_1, p)$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Bin}(n_2, p)$ be independent measurable counts with a common
$p \in (0, 1)$, and let $t \le n_1 + n_2$. Conditionally on the margin $X + Y = t$, the cell $X$ has the
hypergeometric law with population $n_1 + n_2$, $n_1$ marked items and $t$ draws, whatever $p$.
