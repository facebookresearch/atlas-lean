# Exact finite threshold expansion

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $p_1, \dots, p_k \in [0, 1]$ and $T \in \mathbb{R}$. Let $\mathbb{P}_p$ be the product measure on $\{0, 1\}^k$ under which the coordinates are independent and coordinate $i$ equals $1$ with probability $p_i$, and let $S(\omega) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \omega_i$. Then

$$\mathbb{P}_p(S \ge T) = \sum_{\substack{b \in \{0,1\}^k \\ \sum_i b_i \ge T}} \ \prod_{i=1}^{k} p_i^{\,b_i} (1 - p_i)^{1 - b_i}.$$
