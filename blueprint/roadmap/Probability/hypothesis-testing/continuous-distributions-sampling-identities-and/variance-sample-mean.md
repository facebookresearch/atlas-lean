# Variance of the sample mean

Let $(X_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ be real random variables on a measurable space $\Omega$ with a measure $P$, and let $\bar{X}_n(\omega) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} X_i(\omega)$ (which is $0$ for $n = 0$) be the sample mean of the first $n$ observations. Suppose $X_0$ has a finite second moment, the $X_i$ are pairwise independent, and every $X_i$ has the same distribution as $X_0$ under $P$. Then for every $n \ge 1$,

$$\operatorname{Var}_P(\bar{X}_n) = \frac{\operatorname{Var}_P(X_0)}{n}.$$
