For real numbers $X_0(\omega), \dots, X_{n-1}(\omega)$, the sample mean of the first $n$ observations, defined as the average over the index set $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, equals

$$\bar{X}_n(\omega) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} X_i(\omega).$$

For $n = 0$ both sides are $0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
