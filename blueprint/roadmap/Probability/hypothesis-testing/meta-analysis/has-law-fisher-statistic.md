# Fisher's method for combining p-values

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $k \ge 1$, and $p_1, \dots, p_k : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ mutually independent random variables, each uniform on $[0, 1]$. Then Fisher's statistic $-2 \sum_{i=1}^k \log p_i$ has the chi-square law with $2k$ degrees of freedom.

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *Statistical Methods for Research Workers*, 4th ed., Oliver and Boyd, 1932, §21.1.
