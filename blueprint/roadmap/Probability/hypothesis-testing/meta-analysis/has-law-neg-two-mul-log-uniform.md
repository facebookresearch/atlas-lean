# Minus twice the log of a uniform variable is chi-square

If $U$ is uniform on $[0, 1]$, then $-2 \log U$ has the exponential law of rate $1/2$ (the gamma law with shape $1$ and rate $1/2$), that is, the chi-square law with two degrees of freedom.
