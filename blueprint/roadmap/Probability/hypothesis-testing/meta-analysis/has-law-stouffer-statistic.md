# Stouffer's method

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$, $w \in \mathbb{R}^k$ a nonzero weight vector, and $z_1, \dots, z_k : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ mutually independent standard normal random variables. Then Stouffer's weighted statistic $\sum_i w_i z_i / \sqrt{\sum_i w_i^2}$ is standard normal.
