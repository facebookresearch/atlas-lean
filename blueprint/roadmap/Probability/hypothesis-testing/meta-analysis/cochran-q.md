# Cochran's heterogeneity statistic Q

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$, $X_1, \dots, X_k \in \mathbb{R}$ study estimates with variances $v_1, \dots, v_k$, and $w$ the [inverse-variance weights](inverse-variance-weights.md). Cochran's heterogeneity statistic is

$$Q = \sum_{i=1}^k v_i^{-1}\Big(X_i - \sum_{j=1}^k w_j X_j\Big)^2.$$
