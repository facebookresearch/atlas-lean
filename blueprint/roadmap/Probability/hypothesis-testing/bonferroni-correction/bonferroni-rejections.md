# Bonferroni rejection set

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ and $p_0, \dots, p_{m-1} \in \mathbb{R}$ be p-values of $m$ hypotheses. The Bonferroni procedure at level $\alpha$ rejects the hypotheses whose p-value is at most $\alpha/m$:

$$R = \{i \in \{0, \dots, m-1\} : p_i \le \alpha/m\}.$$
