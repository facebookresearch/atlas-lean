# Non-binding futility keeps the type I error at α

Let $f$ be an alpha-spending function for level $\alpha \le 1$ ($f(0) = 0$, $f$ nondecreasing on $[0, 1]$, $f(1) \le \alpha$), and $0 = \tau_0 \le \tau_1 \le \dots \le \tau_K \le 1$ information times; look $k$ ($k < K$) is tested at the nominal level $c_k = f(\tau_{k+1}) - f(\tau_k)$. Let the look p-values $p_k$, $k < K$, be valid under the null $P$. Then for every futility cutoff $d$ and futility p-values $q$, the trial with futility rejects with probability at most $\alpha$ under $P$.
