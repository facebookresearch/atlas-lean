# Spending accounting for planned looks

Let $f$ be an alpha-spending function for level $\alpha$ ($f(0) = 0$, $f$ nondecreasing on $[0, 1]$, $f(1) \le \alpha$), and $0 = \tau_0 \le \tau_1 \le \dots \le \tau_K \le 1$ information times; look $k$ ($k < K$) is tested at the nominal level $c_k = f(\tau_{k+1}) - f(\tau_k)$. Then $\sum_{k<K}c_k \le \alpha$.
