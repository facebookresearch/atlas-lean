# Upper tail of the binomial law

For $n, x \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p \in [0,1]$, the binomial law $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ on $\mathbb{N}$ gives the event $\{j : j \ge x\}$ the probability

$$\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)\big(\{j \ge x\}\big) = \sum_{i=x}^{n} \binom{n}{i} p^i (1-p)^{n-i},$$

which is $0$ when $x > n$.
