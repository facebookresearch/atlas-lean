# Validity of the exact conditional Poisson rate test

Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent counts on a probability space with $X \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_1r)$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_2r)$, for exposures (library sizes) $s_1, s_2 \ge 0$ with $s_1 + s_2 > 0$ and a common rate $r \ge 0$; write $\pi = s_1/(s_1 + s_2)$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be measurable. Then the exact conditional p-value $p$ is valid: $P(p \le t) \le t$ for every $t \in [0, 1]$, whatever $r \ge 0$. No calibration is asserted under overdispersion.
