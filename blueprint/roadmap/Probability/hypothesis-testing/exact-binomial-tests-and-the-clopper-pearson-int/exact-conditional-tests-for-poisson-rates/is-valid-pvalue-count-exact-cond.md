# Conditional validity of the exact Poisson rate test

Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent counts on a measure space (of total mass $1$, as the Poisson laws force) with $X \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_1r)$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_2r)$, for exposures (library sizes) $s_1, s_2 \ge 0$ with $s_1 + s_2 > 0$ and a common rate $r \ge 0$; write $\pi = s_1/(s_1 + s_2)$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be measurable. Then for every total $n$ with $P(X + Y = n) > 0$, the upper-tail p-value of $X$ under $\mathrm{Bin}(n, \pi)$ is valid under the conditional probability given $X + Y = n$, whatever the rate $r$.
