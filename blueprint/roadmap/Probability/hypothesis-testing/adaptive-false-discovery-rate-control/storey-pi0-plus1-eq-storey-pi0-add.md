# Storey’s finite-sample estimator and the original estimator

For p-values $p$ and $\lambda$, $\hat\pi_0(\lambda) = \#\{j : p_j > \lambda\}/((1 - \lambda)m) + 1/((1 - \lambda)m)$: the finite-sample estimator is Storey's 2002 estimator plus a correction.

## Sources

- J. D. Storey, "A direct approach to false discovery rates", *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B* 64 (2002), 479–498.
