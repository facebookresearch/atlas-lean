# Leave-one-out property of Storey–Benjamini–Hochberg

For p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$, a level $q$ and a tuning parameter $\lambda$, let $\hat\pi_0 = (\#\{j : p_j > \lambda\} + 1)/((1 - \lambda)m)$ be Storey's estimator, $t_k = \min(\lambda, qk/(m\hat\pi_0))$ the Storey-BH thresholds, $\hat k$ the largest $k \le m$ with at least $k$ p-values at most $t_k$, and $R$ the Storey-BH rejection set $\{i : p_i \le t_{\hat k}\}$. Let $q \ge 0$, $0 \le \lambda \le 1$, and let $p^0$ be $p$ with $p_i$ replaced by $0$. Then $i \in R$ and $|R| = k$ if and only if $p_i$ is at most the $k$-th threshold of $p^0$ and Storey-BH rejects exactly $k$ hypotheses of $p^0$.
