# Storey–Benjamini–Hochberg for mutually independent p-values

Let $p_1, \dots, p_m$ be measurable, mutually independent p-values on a probability space with every true-null p-value valid. Then for $q \ge 0$ and $0 \le \lambda < 1$, the Storey-BH procedure has false discovery rate at most $q$.

## Sources

- Storey, Taylor and Siegmund (2004), Theorem 3.
