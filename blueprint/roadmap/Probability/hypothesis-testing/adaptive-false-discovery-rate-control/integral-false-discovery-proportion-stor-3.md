# Storey–Benjamini–Hochberg under positive regression dependence

Let $p_1, \dots, p_m$ be measurable p-values on a probability space, PRDS on the true nulls $H_0$, with every true-null p-value valid. Then for $q \ge 0$ and $\lambda < 1$, the Storey-BH procedure has false discovery rate at most $q(1 - \lambda)|H_0|$.

## Sources

- Y. Benjamini and D. Yekutieli, "The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency", *Annals of Statistics* 29 (2001), 1165–1188.
