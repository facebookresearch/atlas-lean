# Complete randomization is invariant under relabeling units

Let $U$ be a finite population, $n_1 \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $\mathbb{P}_{n_1}$ be complete randomization of $n_1$ treated units (the zero measure when $n_1 > |U|$). For every permutation $\sigma$ of $U$, the image of $\mathbb{P}_{n_1}$ under $S \mapsto \sigma(S)$ is $\mathbb{P}_{n_1}$.
