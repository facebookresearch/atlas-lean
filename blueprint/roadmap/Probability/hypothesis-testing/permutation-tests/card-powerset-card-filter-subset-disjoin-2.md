# Counting $t$-subsets with a prescribed inside and outside, in general

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set with $n$ elements, $A, B \subseteq \alpha$ arbitrary and $t \in \mathbb{N}$. The number of $t$-element subsets $S \subseteq \alpha$ with $A \subseteq S$ and $S \cap B = \emptyset$ is $\binom{n - |A| - |B|}{t - |A|}$ when $A$ and $B$ are disjoint and $|A| \le t$, and $0$ otherwise.
