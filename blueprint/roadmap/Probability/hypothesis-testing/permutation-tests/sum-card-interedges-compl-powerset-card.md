# Sum of the edge cut over all $t$-subsets

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$ with $n$ vertices and $M$ edges, and let $t \ge 1$. Summing over the $t$-element subsets $S \subseteq V$ the number $e(S, S^c)$ of edges with exactly one endpoint in $S$ gives

$$\sum_{|S| = t} e(S, S^c) = 2M\binom{n - 2}{t - 1},$$

with $n - 2$ read as $0$ when $n < 2$.
