# Permutation p-value

Let a finite group $G$ act on a set $\Omega$ and let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a statistic. The permutation (group-invariance) p-value of $T$ at $\omega \in \Omega$ is the fraction of group elements $g$ with $T(g \cdot \omega) \ge T(\omega)$:

$$p_T(\omega) = \frac{\#\{g \in G : T(g \cdot \omega) \ge T(\omega)\}}{|G|}.$$
