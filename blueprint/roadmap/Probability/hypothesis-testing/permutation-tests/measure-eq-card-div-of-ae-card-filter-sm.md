# Averaging over an invariant finite group

Let a finite group $G$ act measurably on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\Omega$ invariant under the action, $A \subseteq \Omega$ measurable and $c \in \mathbb{N}$. If for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$ exactly $c$ group elements $g$ satisfy $g \cdot \omega \in A$, then

$$\mu(A) = \frac{c}{|G|}.$$

Summing $\mu(g^{-1} \cdot A) = \mu(A)$ over $G$ turns $|G|\,\mu(A)$ into the integral of the number of such $g$.
