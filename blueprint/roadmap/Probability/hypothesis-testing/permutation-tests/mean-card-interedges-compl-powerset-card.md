# Mean edge cut of a uniform $t$-subset

Let $G$ be a simple graph on a finite vertex set $V$ with $n$ vertices and $M$ edges, and let $1 \le t < n$. The average over the $\binom{n}{t}$ subsets $S$ with $t$ elements of the number $e(S, S^c)$ of edges with exactly one endpoint in $S$ is

$$\binom{n}{t}^{-1} \sum_{|S| = t} e(S, S^c) = \frac{2t(n - t)}{n(n - 1)}\, M.$$

Only $1 \le t < n$ is assumed, so $n \ge 2$; this covers the domain $n \ge 4$ of the [variance formula](variance-card-interedges-compl-powerset.md).
