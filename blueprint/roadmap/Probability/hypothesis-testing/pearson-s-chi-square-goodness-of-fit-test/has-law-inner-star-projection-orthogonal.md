# Projection of a standard Gaussian onto the hyperplane orthogonal to the square-root probabilities

Let $p$ be a probability vector on a finite set $\iota$ ($p_i \ge 0$, $\sum_i p_i = 1$), $\sqrt p = (\sqrt{p_i})_i$, and $\Pi$ the orthogonal projection of Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ onto the hyperplane $(\sqrt p)^\perp$. Under the standard Gaussian law of $Z$, for every $t \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ the coordinate $\langle\Pi Z, t\rangle$ has law $\mathcal N\big(0, \|t\|^2 - \langle\sqrt p, t\rangle^2\big)$; the covariance of $\Pi Z$ is $I - \sqrt p\sqrt p^\top$.
