# Poisson deviance as a likelihood ratio

For a count $y$ and a rate $r > 0$, the unit Poisson deviance of $y$ against $r$ equals $2\big(\log P_y(Y = y) - \log P_r(Y = y)\big)$, where $P_\lambda$ is the Poisson law with mean $\lambda$: twice the log-likelihood ratio of the saturated fit against $r$.
