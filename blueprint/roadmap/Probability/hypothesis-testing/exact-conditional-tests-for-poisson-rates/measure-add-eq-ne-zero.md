# Every total of Poisson counts has positive probability

Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent counts on a measure space (of total mass $1$, as the Poisson laws force) with $X \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_1r)$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(s_2r)$, for exposures (library sizes) $s_1, s_2 \ge 0$ with $s_1 + s_2 > 0$ and a common rate $r \ge 0$; write $\pi = s_1/(s_1 + s_2)$. If $r > 0$, then $P(X + Y = n) > 0$ for every $n$.
