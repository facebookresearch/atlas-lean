# Positive regression dependence on a subset

On a nonzero finite measure space, p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$ are positively regression dependent on a subset $H_0$ (PRDS) if, for every $i \in H_0$ and every measurable increasing set $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$, $x \mapsto P((p_1, \dots, p_m) \in D \mid p_i = x)$ has a nondecreasing version (a version of the regular conditional distribution, determined up to null sets of the law of $p_i$). Larger p-values being less significant, this is positive dependence.

## Sources

- Y. Benjamini and D. Yekutieli, "The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency", *Annals of Statistics* 29 (2001), 1165–1188.
