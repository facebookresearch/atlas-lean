# Monotone conditional tail under positive regression dependence

On a nonzero finite measure space, p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$ are positively regression dependent on a subset $H_0$ (PRDS) if for every $i \in H_0$ and every measurable increasing set $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$, $x \mapsto P((p_1, \dots, p_m) \in D \mid p_i = x)$ has a nondecreasing version. If the measurable p-values are PRDS on $H_0$, $i \in H_0$, $D$ is measurable and increasing and $s \le t$, then with $\mathbf p = (p_1, \dots, p_m)$, $P(\mathbf p \in D, p_i \le s)P(p_i \le t) \le P(\mathbf p \in D, p_i \le t)P(p_i \le s)$.
