# The p-values of a tail-calibrated family are measurable

For a [tail-calibrated family](is-tail-calibrated-family.md) of `m` tests under a measure `μ` with
true nulls `H0`, every [p-value](tail-pvalue.md) `ω ↦ G i (T i ω)`, `i = 0, …, m - 1`, null or not,
is measurable: the statistic `T i` is measurable and the nonincreasing function `G i` is measurable.
