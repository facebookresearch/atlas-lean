# Dependency control for a nonincreasing count under positive regression dependence

On a probability space, let the measurable p-values be PRDS on $H_0$, $i \in H_0$ with $p_i$ valid, and $\kappa$ a measurable function of the p-value vector $\mathbf p$, nonincreasing coordinatewise with $\kappa \le m$. Then for every $c \ge 0$, $\mathbb{E}\big[\mathbf 1\{p_i \le c\kappa(\mathbf p), \kappa(\mathbf p) \ge 1\}/\kappa(\mathbf p)\big] \le c$.

## Sources

- Y. Benjamini and D. Yekutieli, "The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency", *Annals of Statistics* 29 (2001), 1165–1188.
