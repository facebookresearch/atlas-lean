# False discovery rate control of Benjamini–Hochberg under positive regression dependence

Let $p_1, \dots, p_m$ be measurable p-values on a probability space, PRDS on the set $H_0$ of true nulls, with every true-null p-value valid ($P(p_i \le t) \le t$ for $t \in [0, 1]$). Then the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure at level $q \in [0, 1]$ has false discovery rate at most $q|H_0|/m$.

## Sources

- Benjamini & Yekutieli, *The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency*, Ann. Statist. 29 (2001), 1165–1188, Theorem 1.2.
