# Adaptive discrete critical values satisfy their defining inequality

For a level $\alpha$, null distribution functions $F_1, \dots, F_m$ and a finite support set $A$, let $\tau_m$ be the maximum of $0$ and all $t \in A$ with every $F_i(t) < 1$ and $\frac1m\sum_iF_i(t)/(1 - F_i(t)) \le \alpha$, and $\tau_k$ the maximum of $0$ and all $t \in A$ with $t \le \tau_m$ and $\frac1m\sum_iF_i(t)/(1 - F_i(\tau_m)) \le \alpha k/m$ (the discrete Benjamini–Hochberg critical values). The adaptive critical value $\tilde\tau_k$ is the maximum of $0$ and all $t \in A$ with $t \le \tau_m$ and $\frac1m\Sigma_{m \mathbin{\dot-} k + 1}\big((F_i(t)/(1 - F_i(\tau_m)))_i\big) \le \alpha k/m$, where $\Sigma_r(x)$, the [largest sum over at most $r$ coordinates](max-subset-sum.md), is the largest value of $\sum_{i \in S}x_i$ over sets $S$ of at most $r$ coordinates (for nonnegative terms, the sum of the $\min(r, m)$ largest), and the count $m \mathbin{\dot-} k + 1 = \max(m - k, 0) + 1$ uses truncated subtraction of natural numbers, so it is $1$ for every $k \ge m$ and $m + 1$ at $k = 0$, where all $m$ terms are summed. If $F_i(0) = 0$ and $\alpha \ge 0$, then $\frac1m\Sigma_{m \mathbin{\dot-} k + 1}\big((F_i(\tilde\tau_k)/(1 - F_i(\tau_m)))_i\big) \le \alpha k/m$ for every $k$.
