# Shifted leave-one-out identity for step-up procedures

Let $c$ be nondecreasing nonnegative critical values and $p$ p-values with $p_i > c_m$. Then the step-up index of $p$ with $p_i$ replaced by $0$ equals one plus the step-up index of $p$ for the shifted critical values $(c_{\min(k+1, m)})_k$.

## Sources

- S. Döhler, G. Durand and E. Roquain, "New FDR bounds for discrete and heterogeneous tests", *Electronic Journal of Statistics* 12 (2018), 1867–1900.
