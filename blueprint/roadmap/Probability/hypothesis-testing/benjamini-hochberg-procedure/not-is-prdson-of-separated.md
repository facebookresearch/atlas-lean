# A separation criterion refuting positive regression dependence

On a nonzero finite measure space, p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$ are positively regression dependent on a subset $H_0$ (PRDS) if for every $i \in H_0$ and every measurable increasing set $D \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$, $x \mapsto P((p_1, \dots, p_m) \in D \mid p_i = x)$ has a nondecreasing version. For measurable p-values and $i \in H_0$, suppose $P(p_i \le s) > 0$, $P(s < p_i \le t) > 0$, and almost surely $p_k \ge u$ when $p_i \le s$ while $p_k < u$ when $s < p_i \le t$. Then the p-values are not PRDS on $H_0$.
