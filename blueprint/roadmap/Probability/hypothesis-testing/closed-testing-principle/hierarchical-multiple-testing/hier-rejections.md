# Hierarchical testing procedure

For a tree $T$ of clusters with levels $a$ and p-values $p$, the hierarchical procedure rejects every cluster $C \in T$ such that $p_D \le a_D$ for every $D \in T$ containing $C$.

## Sources

- N. Meinshausen, "Hierarchical testing of variable importance", *Biometrika* 95 (2008), 265–278.
