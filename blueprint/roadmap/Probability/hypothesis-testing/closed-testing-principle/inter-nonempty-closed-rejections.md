# Closed testing rejection implies the intersection test rejects

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varphi$ a family of local decisions indexed by the subsets of $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ and $H_0 \subseteq \{0, \dots, m-1\}$. If the [closed testing procedure](closed-rejections.md) rejects some $H_i$ with $i \in H_0$, then the local test of the intersection hypothesis $H_{H_0}$ rejects: $\varphi(H_0)$ holds.
