# Bonferroni local test of an intersection hypothesis

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, $p_0, \dots, p_{m-1} \in \mathbb{R}$ and $J \subseteq \{0, \dots, m-1\}$. The Bonferroni local test of the intersection hypothesis $H_J$ at level $\alpha$ rejects when some $j \in J$ has $p_j \le \alpha/|J|$. It never rejects $H_\emptyset$.
