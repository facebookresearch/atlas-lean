# Holm's procedure is a closed test

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\alpha \ge 0$ and $p_0, \dots, p_{m-1} \in \mathbb{R}$. The hypotheses rejected by [Holm's step-down procedure](../holm-bonferroni-method/holm-rejections.md) at level $\alpha$ are exactly those rejected by the [closed testing procedure](closed-rejections.md) built on the [Bonferroni local tests](bonferroni-local-test.md) at level $\alpha$.

## Sources

- S. Holm, *A simple sequentially rejective multiple test procedure*, Scand. J. Statist. 6 (1979), 65–70.
- R. Marcus, E. Peritz and K. R. Gabriel, *On closed testing procedures with special reference to ordered analysis of variance*, Biometrika 63 (1976), 655–660.
