# Membership in the closed rejection set

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varphi$ a family of local decisions indexed by the subsets of $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ and $i \in \{0, \dots, m-1\}$. The [closed testing procedure](closed-rejections.md) rejects $H_i$ if and only if $\varphi(J)$ holds for every $J \subseteq \{0, \dots, m-1\}$ with $i \in J$.
