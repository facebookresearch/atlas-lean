# Empirical AUC equals the Mann–Whitney statistic with ties

Let $m, n \ge 1$, $x_1, \dots, x_m \in \mathbb{R}$ and $y_1, \dots, y_n \in \mathbb{R}$. The trapezoidal [area under the empirical ROC curve](empirical-auc.md) equals the normalised [Mann–Whitney statistic](mann-whitney-u.md):

$$\mathrm{AUC}(x, y) = \frac{U}{mn}.$$

## Sources

- D. Bamber, *The area above the ordinal dominance graph and the area below the receiver operating characteristic graph*, J. Math. Psych. 12 (1975), 387–415.
