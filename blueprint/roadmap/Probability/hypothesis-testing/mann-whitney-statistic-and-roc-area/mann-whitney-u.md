# Mann–Whitney U statistic

Let $x_1, \dots, x_m$ and $y_1, \dots, y_n$ be real numbers (positive and negative scores). The Mann–Whitney count of correctly ranked (positive, negative) pairs, with ties counted one half, is

$$U = \sum_{i=1}^m \sum_{j=1}^n \Big(\mathbf{1}[y_j < x_i] + \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}[y_j = x_i]\Big).$$
