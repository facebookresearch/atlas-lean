# Variance ratio of two samples

For samples $x \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the variance ratio is $S_x^2/S_y^2$ with the unbiased sample variances $S_x^2 = \sum_i(x_i - \bar x)^2/(m - 1)$ and $S_y^2 = \sum_j(y_j - \bar y)^2/(n - 1)$, meant for $m, n \ge 2$ (with $a/0 = 0$).

## Sources

- E. L. Lehmann and J. P. Romano, *Testing Statistical Hypotheses*, 3rd ed., Springer (2005), §5.3.
