# F law of the variance ratio under equal variances

Let $m, n \ge 2$, and let $X_1, \dots, X_m$ i.i.d. $\mathcal N(\mu_1, v_1)$ and $Y_1, \dots, Y_n$ i.i.d. $\mathcal N(\mu_2, v_2)$ be independent samples on a probability space with $v_1 = v_2 > 0$. Write $S_X^2 = \sum_i(X_i - \bar X)^2/(m-1)$, $S_Y^2 = \sum_j(Y_j - \bar Y)^2/(n-1)$ and $R = S_X^2/S_Y^2$. Then $R$ has Snedecor's $F$ law with $(m - 1, n - 1)$ degrees of freedom.
