# Pooled sample variance

For samples $x \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with means $\bar x = \frac1m\sum_i x_i$ and $\bar y = \frac1n\sum_j y_j$, the pooled sample variance is

$$S_p^2 = \frac{\sum_i (x_i - \bar x)^2 + \sum_j (y_j - \bar y)^2}{m + n - 2},$$

meant for $m + n \ge 3$ (with $a/0 = 0$).

## Sources

- E. L. Lehmann and J. P. Romano, *Testing Statistical Hypotheses*, 3rd ed., Springer (2005), §5.3.
