# Second-order Taylor remainder bound

Let $f, f_1, f_2, f_3 : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be such that, on the open ball of radius $\delta$ around $\theta_0$, $f$ has derivative $f_1$, $f_1$ has derivative $f_2$, $f_2$ has derivative $f_3$, and $|f_3| \le m$. Then for every $\theta$ in that ball,

$$\Bigl|f(\theta_0) - f(\theta) - (\theta_0 - \theta)\, f_1(\theta) - \tfrac12 (\theta_0 - \theta)^2 f_2(\theta_0)\Bigr| \le m\, |\theta - \theta_0|^3.$$
