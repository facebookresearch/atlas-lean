# At ρ = 1 the SKAT-O statistic is the burden statistic

For a real `n × q` covariate matrix `X`, a real `n × n` matrix `V`, a real `n × m` genotype matrix
`G`, variant weights `a ∈ ℝᵐ` and every phenotype `y ∈ ℝⁿ`, the [SKAT-O statistic](skat-oq.md)
`Q_ρ = (1 - ρ) Q_S + ρ Q_B` at `ρ = 1` is the [burden statistic](burden-q.md):
`Q_1(y) = Q_B(y) = (∑ⱼ aⱼ Sⱼ(y))²`, with `Sⱼ` the single-variant scores.
