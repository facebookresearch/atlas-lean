# The square of a centred Gaussian of variance v has law v χ²₁

For a real number `v ≥ 0`: if `B` is a real centred Gaussian variable with variance `v` (the point
mass at `0` when `v = 0`), then `B²` has the law `v χ²₁` of `v ξ²` for a standard normal variable
`ξ`, that is, the weighted chi-square law with the single weight `v`.
