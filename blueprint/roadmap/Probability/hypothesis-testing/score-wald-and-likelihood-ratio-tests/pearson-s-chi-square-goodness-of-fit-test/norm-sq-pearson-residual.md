# Pearson's statistic is the squared norm of the residuals

For nonnegative cell probabilities $p$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and counts $N$, the squared Euclidean norm of the Pearson residual vector $\big((N_i - np_i)/\sqrt{np_i}\big)_i$ equals Pearson's statistic $\sum_i (N_i - np_i)^2/(np_i)$ (cells with $np_i = 0$ contributing $0$ to both).
