# Ville’s inequality for e-processes

An e-process for a filtration under $P$ is a nonnegative adapted process $(E_n)$ whose value at every bounded stopping time is an e-value (nonnegative, expectation at most $1$). Then for every $c > 0$, $P(\exists n : E_n \ge c) \le 1/c$.

## Sources

- Ville (1939); Ramdas, Ruf, Larsson and Koolen (2022).
