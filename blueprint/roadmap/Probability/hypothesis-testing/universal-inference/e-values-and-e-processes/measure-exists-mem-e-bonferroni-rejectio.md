# E-Bonferroni controls the familywise error rate

An e-value under $P$ is a nonnegative measurable statistic $E$ with $\mathbb{E}[E] \le 1$. If every true-null statistic $E_i$, $i \in H_0$, is an e-value and $\alpha > 0$, the probability that e-Bonferroni rejects some true null is at most $\alpha|H_0|/m$, with no dependence assumption.
