# Anytime validity of e-processes

An e-process for a filtration under $P$ is a nonnegative adapted process $(E_n)$ whose value at every bounded stopping time is an e-value (nonnegative, expectation at most $1$). Then for every random time $T$ (not necessarily a stopping time) and $c > 0$, $P(E_T \ge c) \le 1/c$.
