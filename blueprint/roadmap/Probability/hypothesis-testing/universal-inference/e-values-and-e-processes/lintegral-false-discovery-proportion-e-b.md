# False discovery bound for e-BH, lower-integral form

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, let $\hat k$ be the largest $k \le m$ such that at least $k$ of the $e_i$ satisfy $m \le \alpha ke_i$; the e-BH procedure rejects all $i$ with $m \le \alpha\hat ke_i$ (for $\alpha > 0$ and $\hat k \ge 1$, $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$; nothing when $\hat k = 0$). If every true-null statistic is an e-value and $\alpha \ge 0$, the lower integral of the e-BH false discovery proportion is at most $\alpha|H_0|/m$.
