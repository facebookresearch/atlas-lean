# Familywise error over all looks and endpoints with e-processes

Let endpoint $i$ be rejected at look $n$ when $E_{i,n} \ge m/\alpha$, $\alpha > 0$. If every true-null endpoint $i \in H_0$ carries an e-process $(E_{i,n})_n$ for a common filtration, the probability that some true null is ever rejected, at any look, is at most $\alpha|H_0|/m$; looks need not be planned and endpoints may be dependent.
