# E-BH at a stopping time, lower-integral form

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, let $\hat k$ be the largest $k \le m$ such that at least $k$ of the $e_i$ satisfy $m \le \alpha ke_i$; the e-BH procedure rejects all $i$ with $m \le \alpha\hat ke_i$ (for $\alpha > 0$ and $\hat k \ge 1$, $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$; nothing when $\hat k = 0$). If every true-null endpoint carries an e-process for a common filtration, $\tau$ is a bounded stopping time and $\alpha \ge 0$, then e-BH applied to the stopped values $E_{i,\tau}$ has lower-integral false discovery proportion at most $\alpha|H_0|/m$.
