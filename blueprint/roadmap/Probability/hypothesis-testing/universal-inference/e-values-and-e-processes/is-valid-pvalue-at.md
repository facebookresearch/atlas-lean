# Anytime-valid p-values from an e-process

An e-process for a filtration under $P$ is a nonnegative adapted process $(E_n)$ whose value at every bounded stopping time is an e-value (nonnegative, expectation at most $1$). Under a probability measure, for every random time $T$, $1/\max(E_T, 1)$ is a valid p-value.
