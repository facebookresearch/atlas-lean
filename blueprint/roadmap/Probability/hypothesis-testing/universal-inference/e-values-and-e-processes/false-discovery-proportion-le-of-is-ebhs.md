# Pointwise false discovery proportion bound for self-consistent sets

A rejection set $R$ is self-consistent for e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ at level $\alpha$ if $m \le \alpha|R|e_i$ for every $i \in R$. If $R$ is self-consistent, $\alpha \ge 0$ and $e_i \ge 0$ for $i \in H_0$, then the false discovery proportion of $R$ is at most $\frac\alpha m\sum_{i \in H_0}e_i$.
