# Has PDF comp

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with its Borel σ-algebra and $\mu$ an additive Haar measure on $E$. Let $T : E \to E$ be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse such that $T^{-1}$ has Fréchet derivative $S'(x)$ at every $x \in E$. If a random variable $Z : \Omega \to E$ on $(\Omega, \mathbb{P})$ has a density with respect to $\mu$ ($Z$ is a.e. measurable and its law is absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$), then so does $T \circ Z$.
