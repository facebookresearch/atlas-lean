# Membership in the CQR band through the score

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a set, $q_{\mathrm{lo}}, q_{\mathrm{hi}} : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$, $Q \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{+\infty\}$, $x \in \mathcal{X}$ and $y \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $y$ lies in the [CQR band](cqr-band.md) $C_Q(x)$ if and only if the [CQR score](cqr-score.md) satisfies $E(x, y) \le Q$.
