# CQR threshold

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a set, $q_{\mathrm{lo}}, q_{\mathrm{hi}} : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$, $n, k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $Z_1, \dots, Z_{n+1} \in \mathcal{X} \times \mathbb{R}$ ($n$ calibration points followed by the test point). The CQR threshold is the $k$-th smallest of the [CQR scores](cqr-score.md) $E(Z_1), \dots, E(Z_n)$ of the calibration points, counted with multiplicity, and $+\infty$ when $k > n$; it is the [split conformal threshold](../split-conformal-prediction/split-conformal-threshold.md) of these scores. With $k = \lceil (1 - \alpha)(n + 1) \rceil$ this is the $(1 - \alpha)(1 + 1/n)$-th empirical quantile $Q_{1-\alpha}(E, I_2)$ of Romano, Patterson and Candès (NeurIPS 2019, arXiv:1905.03222, Algorithm 1), with $Q = +\infty$ when that level exceeds $1$.
