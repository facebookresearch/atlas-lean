# Posterior stochastic ordering for an antitone likelihood ratio, cross-multiplied form

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ and $L$ a likelihood family with an [antitone likelihood ratio](has-antitone-likelihood-ratio-on.md) on a set $S$ with $\mu(S^c) = 0$, such that every $L(n, \cdot)$ is $\mu$-integrable. Then for $n \le m$ and every $t \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\Big(\int_{\theta > t} L(m, \theta)\, d\mu\Big)\Big(\int L(n, \theta)\, d\mu\Big) \le \Big(\int_{\theta > t} L(n, \theta)\, d\mu\Big)\Big(\int L(m, \theta)\, d\mu\Big).$$
