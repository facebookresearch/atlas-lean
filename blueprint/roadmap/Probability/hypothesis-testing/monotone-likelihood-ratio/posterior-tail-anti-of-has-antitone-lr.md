# The posterior is stochastically nonincreasing in the observation

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ and $L$ a likelihood family with an [antitone likelihood ratio](has-antitone-likelihood-ratio-on.md) on a set $S$ with $\mu(S^c) = 0$, every $L(n, \cdot)$ being $\mu$-integrable. If $n \le m$ and both marginal likelihoods are positive, then for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$

$$\Pi(\theta > t \mid m) \le \Pi(\theta > t \mid n).$$
