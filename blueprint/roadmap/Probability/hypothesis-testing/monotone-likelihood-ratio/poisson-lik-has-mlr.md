# Poisson family with monotone mean has monotone likelihood ratio

Let $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ and $\lambda : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ nonnegative and nondecreasing on $S$. Then the [Poisson likelihood](poisson-lik.md) $L(n, \theta) = e^{-\lambda(\theta)}\lambda(\theta)^n/n!$ has a [monotone likelihood ratio](has-monotone-likelihood-ratio-on.md) on $S$.
