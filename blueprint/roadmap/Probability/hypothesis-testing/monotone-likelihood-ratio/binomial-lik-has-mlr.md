# Binomial family has monotone likelihood ratio

Let $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ and $p : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ nondecreasing on $S$ with $p(\theta) \in [0, 1]$ for $\theta \in S$. Then the [binomial likelihood](binomial-lik.md) with $N$ trials and success probability $p$ has a [monotone likelihood ratio](has-monotone-likelihood-ratio-on.md) on $S$.
