# Holm over causal nulls in a randomized trial

Let $T$ be a measurable treatment event with $0 < P(T) < 1$, independent of the potential outcomes of every causal-null endpoint (measurable and integrable), and let the p-values be calibrated for the observed-data Horvitz–Thompson null. Then for $\alpha \in [0, 1]$, Holm rejects some causal-null endpoint with probability at most $\alpha$, under arbitrary dependence.
