# Average-treatment-effect Lehmann p-values are positively regression dependent

For endpoints with observed outcomes $Y_j$ and scales $\kappa_j$, let $\theta_j = \exp(\kappa_j\Delta_j)$, where $\Delta_j$ is the inverse-probability-weighted contrast of $Y_j$, and $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ the ATE Lehmann p-values. If the noises are measurable and mutually independent on a probability space, the p-values are PRDS on every set of endpoints.
