# Benjamini–Yekutieli error control fails without positivity

On a one-point space with nobody treated and potential outcomes $Y_0 = Y_1 = 1$ for a single endpoint, with the calibrated always-reject p-value $0$ (which is PRDS), Benjamini–Hochberg at level $q/H_1$ has false discovery rate $1$ over the causal nulls for every $q \ge 0$.
