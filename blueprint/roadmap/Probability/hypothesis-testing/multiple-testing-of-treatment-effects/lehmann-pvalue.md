# Clustered Lehmann p-values

For noise variables $U_1, \dots, U_k$, a cluster map $c$ and exponents $\theta_j$, endpoint $j$ reports the Lehmann p-value $\max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$; endpoints of one cluster share their noise, and with uniform noise and $\theta_j > 0$ the p-value has the $\mathrm{Beta}(1/\theta_j, 1)$ law.
