# Joint law of null indicator and z-value

In a [two-groups model](two-groups-model.md) $(\pi_0, f_0, f_1)$, the joint law of the pair $(H, Z)$, where $H \in \{0, 1\}$ indicates whether the case is null ($H = 0$) or not and $Z$ is its z-value, is the probability measure on $\{0, 1\} \times \mathbb{R}$

$$\pi_0\, \delta_0 \otimes P_0 + (1 - \pi_0)\, \delta_1 \otimes P_1,$$

with $P_0, P_1$ the [null](null-law.md) and [non-null](alt-law.md) laws.
