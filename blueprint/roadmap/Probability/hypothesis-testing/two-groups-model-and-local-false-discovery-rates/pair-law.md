# Law of a test's null and rejection pair

Let $(\pi_0, f_0, f_1)$ be a [two-groups model](two-groups-model.md) and $\Gamma \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ a rejection region. For $h, r \in \{0, 1\}$ let

$$q_\Gamma(h, r) = P\big(\mathbf{1}\{H = 0\} = h,\ Z \in \Gamma_r\big), \qquad \Gamma_1 = \Gamma,\ \Gamma_0 = \Gamma^c,$$

the mass of the corresponding set under the [joint law](joint-law.md) of $(H, Z)$, a non-measurable set being given its outer measure. For measurable $\Gamma$, $q_\Gamma$ is the law of one test's (null, rejected) pair when the test rejects if and only if its z-value lies in $\Gamma$. For non-measurable $\Gamma$ the rejection indicator need not be a random variable, and the four masses need not sum to $1$.
