# Storey's theorem in the two-groups model

Let $(\pi_0, f_0, f_1)$ be a [two-groups model](two-groups-model.md), $\Gamma \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ measurable and $m \ge 1$. For $m$ i.i.d. tests rejecting when their z-value lies in $\Gamma$, with pair law $q_\Gamma$ ([law of the null and rejection pair](pair-law.md)),

$$\mathrm{pFDR}(q_\Gamma, m) = \frac{\pi_0 P_0(\Gamma)}{P(Z \in \Gamma)} = \operatorname{Fdr}(\Gamma),$$

the [tail-area false discovery rate](tail-fdr.md) (Storey 2002, Theorem 1).
