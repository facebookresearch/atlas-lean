# Local fdr times mixture density equals the null part

In a [two-groups model](two-groups-model.md), for every $z \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\operatorname{fdr}(z)\, f(z) = \pi_0 f_0(z),$$

where $f$ is the [mixture density](mixture-density.md); where $f(z) = 0$ both sides vanish because $\pi_0 f_0 \le f$.
