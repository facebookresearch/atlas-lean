# Probability of a test outcome vector

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $q : \{0,1\}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$. The weight of an outcome vector $x = (x_1, \dots, x_m) \in (\{0,1\}^2)^m$ is $\prod_{i=1}^m q(x_i)$; when $q$ is a probability mass function it is the probability of $x$ when the $m$ (null, rejected) pairs are i.i.d. with law $q$.
