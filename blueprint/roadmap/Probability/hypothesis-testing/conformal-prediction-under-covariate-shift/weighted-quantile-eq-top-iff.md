# The weighted quantile is infinite exactly when the finite weight is short

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $\beta > 0$, $v : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ and $p : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p_i \ge 0$ for all $i$. Then the [weighted quantile](weighted-quantile.md) $Q_\beta(v, p)$ equals $+\infty$ if and only if $\sum_i p_i < \beta$. When moreover $\sum_i p_i \le 1$ and $\beta \le 1$, this says that the level $\beta$ can only be reached with the atom at $+\infty$ of mass $1 - \sum_i p_i$.
