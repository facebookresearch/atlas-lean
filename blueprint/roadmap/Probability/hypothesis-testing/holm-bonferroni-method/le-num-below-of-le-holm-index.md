# Every step up to Holm's count succeeds

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, $p_0, \dots, p_{m-1} \in \mathbb{R}$ and $r$ [Holm's step-down count](holm-index.md). For every $k$ with $1 \le k \le r$, at least $k$ p-values are at most $\alpha/(m - k + 1)$:

$$\#\Big\{i : p_i \le \frac{\alpha}{m - k + 1}\Big\} \ge k.$$
