# Bonferroni error control fails without positivity

On a one-point space with nobody treated and potential outcomes $Y_0 = Y_1 = 1$ for a single endpoint, the always-reject p-value $0$ is calibrated for the observed-data inverse-probability-weighting null, and Bonferroni at any level $\alpha \ge 0$ rejects the causal null with probability $1$.
