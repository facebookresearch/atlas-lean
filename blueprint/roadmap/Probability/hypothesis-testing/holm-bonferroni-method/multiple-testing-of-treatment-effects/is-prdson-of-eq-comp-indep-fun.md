# Positive regression dependence from a monotone representation

Let $p_1, \dots, p_m$ be measurable p-values on a probability space and suppose that for every $i \in H_0$ the vector $(p_1, \dots, p_m) = F(p_i, W)$ for a measurable $W$ independent of $p_i$ and a jointly measurable $F$ nondecreasing in its first argument. Then the p-values are PRDS on $H_0$.
