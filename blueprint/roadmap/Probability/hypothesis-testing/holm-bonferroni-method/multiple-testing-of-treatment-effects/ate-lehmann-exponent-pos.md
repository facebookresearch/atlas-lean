# Average-treatment-effect Lehmann exponents are positive

For endpoints with observed outcomes $Y_j$ and scales $\kappa_j$, let $\theta_j = \exp(\kappa_j\Delta_j)$, where $\Delta_j$ is the inverse-probability-weighted contrast of $Y_j$, and $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ the ATE Lehmann p-values. Then $\theta_j > 0$.
