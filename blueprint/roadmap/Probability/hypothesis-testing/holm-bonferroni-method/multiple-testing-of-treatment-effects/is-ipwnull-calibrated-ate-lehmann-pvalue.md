# Calibration of the average-treatment-effect Lehmann p-values

For endpoints with observed outcomes $Y_j$ and scales $\kappa_j$, let $\theta_j = \exp(\kappa_j\Delta_j)$, where $\Delta_j$ is the inverse-probability-weighted contrast of $Y_j$, and $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ the ATE Lehmann p-values. If every noise is uniform on $[0, 1]$, the ATE Lehmann p-values are calibrated for the observed-data inverse-probability-weighting null.
