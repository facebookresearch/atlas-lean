# Benjamini–Yekutieli over causal nulls in a randomized trial

On a probability space, let $T$ be a measurable treatment event with $0 < P(T) < 1$, independent of the potential outcomes of every causal-null endpoint (measurable and integrable), and let the p-values be calibrated for the observed-data Horvitz–Thompson null. If the p-values are measurable and $q \in [0, 1]$, Benjamini–Hochberg at level $q/H_m$ has false discovery rate over the causal nulls at most $q|H_0|/m$, under arbitrary dependence.
