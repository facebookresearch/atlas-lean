# Clustered Lehmann p-values are positively regression dependent

Let $U_1, \dots, U_k$ be noise variables and $c$ a map from endpoints to clusters, and let endpoint $j$ report the Lehmann p-value $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ with exponent $\theta_j$. If the noises are measurable and mutually independent on a probability space and all $\theta_j > 0$, the Lehmann p-values are PRDS on every set of endpoints.
