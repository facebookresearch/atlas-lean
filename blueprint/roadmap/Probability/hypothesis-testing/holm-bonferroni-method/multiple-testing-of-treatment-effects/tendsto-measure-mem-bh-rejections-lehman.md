# Consistency of Benjamini–Hochberg for Lehmann p-values

Let $U_1, \dots, U_k$ be noise variables and $c$ a map from endpoints to clusters, and let endpoint $j$ report the Lehmann p-value $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ with exponent $\theta_j$. Suppose $U_{c(j)}$ is uniform on $[0, 1]$. Under a probability measure, along exponents with $\theta_j \to \infty$, the probability that Benjamini–Hochberg at level $q \in (0, 1]$ rejects endpoint $j$ tends to $1$.
