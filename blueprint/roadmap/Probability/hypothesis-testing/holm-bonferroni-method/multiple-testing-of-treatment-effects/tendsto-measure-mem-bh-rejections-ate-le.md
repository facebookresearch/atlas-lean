# Consistency of Benjamini–Hochberg on average-treatment-effect Lehmann p-values

For endpoints with observed outcomes $Y_j$ and scales $\kappa_j$, let $\theta_j = \exp(\kappa_j\Delta_j)$, where $\Delta_j$ is the inverse-probability-weighted contrast of $Y_j$, and $p_j = \max(U_{c(j)}, 0)^{\theta_j}$ the ATE Lehmann p-values. Under a probability measure, if $U_{c(j)}$ is uniform, $\kappa_j > 0$ and $\Delta_j > 0$, then as the scales are multiplied by $s \to \infty$, the probability that Benjamini–Hochberg at level $q \in (0, 1]$ rejects $j$ tends to $1$.
