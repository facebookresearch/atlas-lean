# Benjamini–Hochberg error control fails without positivity

On a one-point space with nobody treated and potential outcomes $Y_0 = Y_1 = 1$ for a single endpoint (a causal null), the always-reject p-value $0$ is calibrated for the observed-data inverse-probability-weighting null (whose contrast is $-1$), and the Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate over the causal nulls is $1$ for every level $q \ge 0$.
