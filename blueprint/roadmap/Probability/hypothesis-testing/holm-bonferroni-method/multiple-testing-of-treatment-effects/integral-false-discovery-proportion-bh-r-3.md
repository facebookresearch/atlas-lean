# Benjamini–Hochberg error control fails without positivity

On a one-point space with nobody treated and potential outcomes $Y_0 = Y_1 = 1$ for a single endpoint (a causal null), the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure applied to the always-reject p-value $0$ has false discovery rate $1$ over the causal nulls for every level $q \ge 0$. With one hypothesis the Benjamini–Yekutieli level $q/H_1$ equals $q$, so the Benjamini–Yekutieli procedure gives the same false discovery rate.
