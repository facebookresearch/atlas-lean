# Holm's count is the largest index where every step succeeds

Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, $p_0, \dots, p_{m-1} \in \mathbb{R}$ and $r \le m$. If $\#\{i : p_i \le \alpha/(m - k + 1)\} \ge k$ for every $k \in \{1, \dots, r\}$, then $r$ is at most [Holm's step-down count](holm-index.md) at level $\alpha$.
