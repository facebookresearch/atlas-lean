# Tower property, cell form

**Tower property, cell form** (total expectation). Let $\alpha$ and $C$ be finite sets, $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$, $G : \alpha \to C$ and $Y : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$. With the cell masses $m_p(c) = \sum_{x : G(x) = c} p(x)$ and cell means $E[Y \mid G = c]$ (equal to $0$ on cells of mass $0$),

$$\sum_{x \in \alpha} p(x)\, Y(x) = \sum_{c \in C} m_p(c)\, E[Y \mid G = c].$$
