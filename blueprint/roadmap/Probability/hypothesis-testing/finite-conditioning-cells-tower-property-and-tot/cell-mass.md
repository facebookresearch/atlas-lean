# Cell Mass

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set, $p : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ a weight, $G : \alpha \to C$ an observation map and $c \in C$. The mass of the cell $\{G = c\}$ is

$$m_p(c) = \sum_{x \in \alpha,\ G(x) = c} p(x).$$

When $p$ is a probability distribution on $\alpha$, $m_p(c) = P(G = c)$, and $c \mapsto m_p(c)$ is the law of $G$.
