# Coverage passes to larger confidence sets

Let $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be probability measures on a measurable space $\mathcal X$ of observations and $\tau : \Theta \to \mathcal T$ an estimand. Let $C, C'$ map observations to subsets of $\mathcal T$ with $C(x) \subseteq C'(x)$ for every $x$. If $P_\theta(\tau(\theta) \in C(x)) \ge 1 - \alpha$ for every $\theta$, then $P_\theta(\tau(\theta) \in C'(x)) \ge 1 - \alpha$ for every $\theta$.
