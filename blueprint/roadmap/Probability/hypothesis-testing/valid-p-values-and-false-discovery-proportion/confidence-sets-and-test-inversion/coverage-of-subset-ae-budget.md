# Coverage passes to a set that rarely misses

Let $(P_\theta)_{\theta \in \Theta}$ be probability measures on a measurable space $\mathcal X$ of observations and $\tau : \Theta \to \mathcal T$ an estimand. Let $C, C'$ map observations to subsets of $\mathcal T$ and $\varepsilon : \Theta \to \mathbb{R}$. If $P_\theta(\tau(\theta) \in C(x)) \ge 1 - \alpha$ for every $\theta$ and $P_\theta(\tau(\theta) \in C(x),\ \tau(\theta) \notin C'(x)) \le \varepsilon(\theta)$ for every $\theta$, then

$$P_\theta\big(\tau(\theta) \in C'(x)\big) \ge 1 - \alpha - \varepsilon(\theta) \qquad \text{for every } \theta.$$
