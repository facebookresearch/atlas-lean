# Validity of the doubled two-sided p-value

For a probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{N}$ write $U_\nu(k) = \nu(\{j \ge k\})$ and $L_\nu(k) = \nu(\{j \le k\})$ for the upper and lower tails. If a count $X$ has law $\nu$ under $\mu$, then $\min\{1, 2\min(U_\nu(X), L_\nu(X))\}$ is a valid p-value: its $\mu$-probability of being at most $s$ is at most $s$ for every $s \in [0, 1]$.
