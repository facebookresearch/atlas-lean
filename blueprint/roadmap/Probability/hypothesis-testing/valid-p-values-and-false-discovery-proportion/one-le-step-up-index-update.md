# The step-up index is positive once a p-value is zero

For thresholds $c_0, c_1, \dots$ and p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$, let $N(t) = \#\{i : p_i \le t\}$ and let the step-up index $k^*$ be the largest $k \le m$ with $N(c_k) \ge k$. If all thresholds are nonnegative, then after replacing $p_i$ by $0$ the step-up index is at least $1$.
