# Exact upper-tail p-value

For a measure $\nu$ on the natural numbers, the exact upper-tail p-value of a count $X$ is the real number
$\nu(\{j : j \ge X\})$, taken to be $0$ if this mass is infinite. When $\nu$ is the null law of the count,
it is the null probability of a count at least as large as the observed one.
