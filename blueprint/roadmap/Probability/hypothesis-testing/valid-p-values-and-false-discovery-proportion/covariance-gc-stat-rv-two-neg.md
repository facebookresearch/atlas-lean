# Two independent null genes: T₀ and T₁ have negative covariance

Let `P` be a probability measure and let the two scores `Z_0, Z_1` of the [common-inflation
model](../../../Biology/statistical-genetics/association-tests/genomic-control/common-inflation-model.md), each with law `𝒩(0, λ)` under `P` for one `λ > 0`, be measurable
and independent. Then the [corrected statistics](gc-stat-rv.md) have negative covariance:
`Cov(T_0, T_1) < 0`. Indeed the covariances [sum to zero](sum-sum-covariance-gc-stat-rv.md) and
`Var(T_0) > 0` ([positive variance](variance-gc-stat-rv-two-pos.md)).
