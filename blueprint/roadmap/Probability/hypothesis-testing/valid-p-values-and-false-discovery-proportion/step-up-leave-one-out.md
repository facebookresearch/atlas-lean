# Leave-one-out property of step-up procedures

For thresholds $c_0, c_1, \dots$ and p-values $p_1, \dots, p_m$, let $N(t) = \#\{i : p_i \le t\}$ and let the step-up index $k^*$ be the largest $k \le m$ with $N(c_k) \ge k$. Let the thresholds be nondecreasing and nonnegative, and let $k_i^*$ be the step-up index after replacing $p_i$ by $0$. Then for every $k$: hypothesis $i$ is rejected with $k^* = k$ (that is, $p_i \le c_{k^*}$ and $k^* = k$) if and only if $p_i \le c_k$ and $k_i^* = k$.
