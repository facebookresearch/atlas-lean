# Two-gene common-inflation model: the scores are a.s. not both zero

In the [common-inflation model](../../../Biology/statistical-genetics/association-tests/genomic-control/common-inflation-model.md) with two genes, where each score `Z_0`,
`Z_1` has law `𝒩(0, λ)` under `P` for one `λ > 0`, let the scores be measurable. Then almost surely
they do not vanish simultaneously: `Z_0² + Z_1² ≠ 0` almost surely. Already `{Z_0 = 0}` has
probability `0`, since `𝒩(0, λ)` with `λ > 0` has no atom; no independence is assumed.
