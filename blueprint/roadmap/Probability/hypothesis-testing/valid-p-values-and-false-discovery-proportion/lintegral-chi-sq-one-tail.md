# The χ²₁ tail integrates to 1 over the positive half-line

The [`χ²₁` tail](../../../Biology/statistical-genetics/association-tests/genomic-control/chi-sq-one-tail.md) has integral one over the positive half-line:

`∫_(0, ∞) P(χ²₁ > t) dt = 1`,

the integral of a nonnegative function against Lebesgue measure. By the layer-cake formula for the
square of a standard Gaussian variable, it is the mean `E[χ²₁] = 1`.
