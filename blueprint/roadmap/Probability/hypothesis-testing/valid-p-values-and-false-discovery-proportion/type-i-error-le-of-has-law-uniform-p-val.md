# Type I error of a uniform p-value

Let $p$ be a random variable with the uniform law on $[0, 1]$ under a measure $\mu$ and $\alpha \in [0, 1]$. Then rejecting when $p \le \alpha$ has type I error at most $\alpha$: $\mu_{\mathbb{R}}(p \le \alpha) \le \alpha$, where $\mu_{\mathbb{R}}(A)$ is the real-valued mass of $A$ ($\mu(A)$ when finite and $0$ when $\mu(A) = \infty$). The uniform law makes $\mu(p \le \alpha) = \alpha$ finite, so here the two masses agree.
