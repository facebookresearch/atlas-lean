# E-BH controls the false discovery rate under arbitrary dependence

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, let $\hat k$ be the largest $k \le m$ such that at least $k$ of the $e_i$ satisfy $e_i \ge m/(\alpha k)$; the e-BH procedure rejects all $i$ with $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$. If every true-null statistic is an e-value, every false-null statistic is measurable and $\alpha \ge 0$, then the e-BH false discovery proportion is integrable and its expectation is at most $\alpha|H_0|/m \le \alpha$.
