# The e-BH rejection set is self-consistent

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, let $\hat k$ be the largest $k \le m$ such that at least $k$ of the $e_i$ satisfy $m \le \alpha ke_i$; the e-BH procedure rejects all $i$ with $m \le \alpha\hat ke_i$ (for $\alpha > 0$ and $\hat k \ge 1$, $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$; nothing when $\hat k = 0$). Then the e-BH rejection set is self-consistent: every rejected $i$ has $m \le \alpha|R|e_i$, where $R$ is the e-BH rejection set.
