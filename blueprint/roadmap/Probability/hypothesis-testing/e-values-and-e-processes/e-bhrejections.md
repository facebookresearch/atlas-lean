# E-BH procedure

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, the e-BH procedure rejects every $i$ with $m \le \alpha\hat ke_i$, $\hat k$ the e-BH index. For $\alpha > 0$ and $\hat k \ge 1$ this reads $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$; when $\hat k = 0$ nothing is rejected.
