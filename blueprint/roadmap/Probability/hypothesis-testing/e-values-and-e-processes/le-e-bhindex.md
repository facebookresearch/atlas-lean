# Maximality of the e-BH index

For e-values $e_1, \dots, e_m$ and a level $\alpha$, let $\hat k$ be the largest $k \le m$ such that at least $k$ of the $e_i$ satisfy $e_i \ge m/(\alpha k)$; the e-BH procedure rejects all $i$ with $e_i \ge m/(\alpha\hat k)$. If $k \le m$ and at least $k$ e-values satisfy $e_i \ge m/(\alpha k)$, then $k \le \hat k$.
