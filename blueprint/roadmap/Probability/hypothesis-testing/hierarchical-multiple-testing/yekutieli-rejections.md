# Yekutieli’s hierarchical Benjamini–Hochberg procedure

Yekutieli's procedure on a tree $T$ with level $q$ and p-values $p$ is the hierarchical procedure with every node tested at the Benjamini–Hochberg cutoff of its sibling family: a node is rejected when it and every member of $T$ containing it pass their levels.

## Sources

- D. Yekutieli, "Hierarchical false discovery rate-controlling methodology", *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 103 (2008), 309–316.
