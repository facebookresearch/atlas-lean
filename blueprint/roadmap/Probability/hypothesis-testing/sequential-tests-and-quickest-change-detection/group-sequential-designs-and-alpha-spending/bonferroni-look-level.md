# Bonferroni nominal level of a look

For a spending function $f$ and information times $\tau_0, \tau_1, \dots$, the nominal level of look $k + 1$ is the spending increment $f(\tau_{k+1}) - f(\tau_k)$, used directly as a p-value cutoff. It is not a Lan–DeMets boundary, which uses the joint null law.
