# Futility costs at most β power

For efficacy cutoffs $c_k$, futility cutoffs $d_k$, efficacy p-values $p_k$ and futility p-values $q_k$, a trial with $K$ planned looks continues before look $j$ if $c_i < p_i$ and $d_i < q_i$ for all $i < j$; it rejects if some $j < K$ is reached with $p_j \le c_j$, and stops for futility if some $j < K$ is reached with $c_j < p_j$ and $q_j \le d_j$. Let $g$ be a beta-spending function for budget $\beta \le 1$ and nondecreasing information times $0 = \tau_0 \le \dots \le \tau_K \le 1$, with futility cutoffs $d_k = g(\tau_{k+1}) - g(\tau_k)$, and let the futility p-values $q_k$, $k < K$, be valid under the alternative $\nu$. Then under $\nu$ the probability that the efficacy-only design with cutoffs $c$ rejects is at most the probability that the design with futility rejects, plus $\beta$.
