# Alpha spending with multiple endpoints

Let $f$ be an alpha-spending function for level $\alpha \le 1$ ($f(0) = 0$, $f$ nondecreasing on $[0, 1]$, $f(1) \le \alpha$), and $0 = \tau_0 \le \tau_1 \le \dots \le \tau_K \le 1$ information times; look $k$ ($k < K$) is tested at the nominal level $c_k = f(\tau_{k+1}) - f(\tau_k)$. Test endpoint $j$ of a finite set $E$ at look $k$ at level $w_jc_k$, with weights $w_j \ge 0$ summing to at most $1$, and let every endpoint p-value at every look be valid under the global null. Then the probability of any rejection is at most $\alpha$.
