# Filtration of the first $n$ observations

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a measurable space. On the coordinate space $\mathcal{X}^{\mathbb{N}}$ with its product σ-algebra, the observation filtration has at time $n$ the σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ generated by the coordinates $0, \dots, n - 1$, that is by the observations $X_1, \dots, X_n$ (where $X_{i+1}(\omega) = \omega_i$); $\mathcal{F}_0$ is trivial.

A stopping time for this filtration that equals $n$ has used exactly $X_1, \dots, X_n$.
