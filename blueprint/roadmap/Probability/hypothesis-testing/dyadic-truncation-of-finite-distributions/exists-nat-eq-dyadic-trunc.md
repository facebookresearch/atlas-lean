# Exists nat eq dyadic Trunc

Let $L$ be a set, $\pi : L \to \mathbb{R}$, $t \in \mathbb{N}$ and $l \in L$, and let $q_l = \lfloor 2^t \pi_l \rfloor_+ / 2^t$ be the dyadic truncation of $\pi$ at precision $t$ (with $\lfloor x \rfloor_+ = 0$ for $x < 0$). Then there is $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with

$$q_l = \frac{n}{2^t}.$$
