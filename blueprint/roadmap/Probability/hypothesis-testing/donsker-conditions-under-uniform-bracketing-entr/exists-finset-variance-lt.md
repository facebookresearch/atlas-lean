# Total boundedness

**Total boundedness.** Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $S$ and $(F_i)_{i \in \iota}$ measurable functions $S \to \mathbb{R}$ whose uniform bracketing entropy integral is finite,

$$\int_{(0,1]} \sqrt{\log N_{[\,]}(\varepsilon, F, \lVert \cdot \rVert_\infty)} \, d\varepsilon < \infty.$$

Then for every $\delta > 0$ there is a finite set $s \subseteq \iota$ such that every $i \in \iota$ has some $j \in s$ with

$$\mathrm{Var}_\mu[F_i - F_j] < \delta^2.$$

That is, $F$ is totally bounded in the standard-deviation semimetric $\rho_\mu(f, g) = \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}_\mu[f - g]}$.
