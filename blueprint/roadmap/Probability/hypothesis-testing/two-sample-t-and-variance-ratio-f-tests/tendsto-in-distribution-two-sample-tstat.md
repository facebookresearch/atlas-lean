# Slutsky transfer for the pooled two-sample t statistic

Along a countably generated filter of indices $N$, let $X^{(N)} \in \mathbb{R}^{m_N}$ and $Y^{(N)} \in \mathbb{R}^{n_N}$ be almost everywhere measurable random samples on a probability space, and let $\delta \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\sigma > 0$. Suppose the known-variance statistic $(\bar X^{(N)} - \bar Y^{(N)} - \delta)\big/\big(\sigma\sqrt{1/m_N + 1/n_N}\big)$ converges in distribution to a random variable $Z$, and the pooled variance $S_p^2$ of $(X^{(N)}, Y^{(N)})$ converges in probability to $\sigma^2$. Then the pooled two-sample $t$ statistic $(\bar X^{(N)} - \bar Y^{(N)} - \delta)/\sqrt{S_p^2(1/m_N + 1/n_N)}$ converges in distribution to $Z$. No normality is assumed.

## Sources

- A. W. van der Vaart, "Asymptotic Statistics", Cambridge (1998), Lemma 2.8 (Slutsky).
