# Pooled two-sample t statistic

For samples $x \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $y \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and a hypothesized mean difference $\delta$, the pooled two-sample $t$ statistic is

$$T = \frac{\bar x - \bar y - \delta}{\sqrt{S_p^2\,(1/m + 1/n)}},$$

where $S_p^2$ is the pooled sample variance (with $a/0 = 0$ and $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x \le 0$).
