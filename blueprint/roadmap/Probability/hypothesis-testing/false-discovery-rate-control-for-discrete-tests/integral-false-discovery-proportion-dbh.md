# False discovery rate control of the discrete Benjamini–Hochberg procedure

Let $p_1, \dots, p_m$ be measurable, mutually independent p-values on a probability space, $H_0$ the true nulls, and $F_i$ null distribution functions with $F_i \ge 0$, $F_i(0) = 0$ and $P(p_i \le t) \le F_i(t)$ for $i \in H_0$. Then the discrete Benjamini–Hochberg step-up procedure at level $\alpha \ge 0$, with any finite support set, has false discovery rate at most $\alpha$. Super-uniformity $F_i(t) \le t$ is not needed.

## Sources

- S. Döhler, G. Durand and E. Roquain, "New FDR bounds for discrete and heterogeneous tests", *Electronic Journal of Statistics* 12 (2018), 1867–1900.
