# Critical values of the adaptive discrete Benjamini–Hochberg procedure

For a level $\alpha$, null distribution functions $F_i$, a finite support set $A$ and $k$, the adaptive critical value $\tilde\tau_k$ is the maximum of $0$ and all $t \in A$ with $t \le \tau_m$ (the last discrete critical value) such that $\frac1m\Sigma_{m \mathbin{\dot-} k + 1}\big((F_i(t)/(1 - F_i(\tau_m)))_i\big) \le \alpha k/m$, where $\Sigma_r(x)$ is the [largest sum over at most $r$ coordinates](max-subset-sum.md) (for nonnegative terms, the sum of the $\min(r, m)$ largest), and the count $m \mathbin{\dot-} k + 1 = \max(m - k, 0) + 1$ uses truncated subtraction of natural numbers: it is $1$ for every $k \ge m$, and at $k = 0$ it is $m + 1$, so all $m$ terms are summed. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
