# Critical values of the adaptive discrete Benjamini–Hochberg procedure

For a level $\alpha$, null distribution functions $F_i$, a finite support set $A$ and $k$, the adaptive critical value $\tilde\tau_k$ is the largest $t \in A \cup \{0\}$ with $t \le \tau_m$ (the last discrete critical value) such that the sum of the $m - k + 1$ largest values of $F_i(t)/(1 - F_i(\tau_m))$ is at most $\alpha k$. Real division and inversion use $x/0 = 0$ and $0^{-1} = 0$.
