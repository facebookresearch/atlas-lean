# The knockoff threshold is attained

Let $c \in \mathbb{N}$, $q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $W \in \mathbb{R}^p$. If the [knockoff threshold](knockoff-threshold.md) is finite, $T_c = t \in \mathbb{R}$, then there is $j$ with $W_j \ne 0$, $|W_j| = t$ and

$$\frac{c + \#\{i : W_i \le -|W_j|\}}{\max(\#\{i : W_i \ge |W_j|\}, 1)} \le q.$$
