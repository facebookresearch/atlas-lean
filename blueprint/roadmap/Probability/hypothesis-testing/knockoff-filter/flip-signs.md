# Sign flip on a set of coordinates

Let $p \in \mathbb{N}$, $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, p\}$ and $w \in \mathbb{R}^p$. The sign flip of $w$ on $S$ is the vector with coordinates $-w_j$ for $j \in S$ and $w_j$ for $j \notin S$. The knockoff FDR theorems need only that flipping the signs of any set of null statistics leaves the law of $W$ unchanged, the form in which Lemma 1 of Barber and Candès (2015, arXiv:1404.5609) and Lemma 3.3 of Candès, Fan, Janson and Lv (2018, arXiv:1610.02351) are established.
