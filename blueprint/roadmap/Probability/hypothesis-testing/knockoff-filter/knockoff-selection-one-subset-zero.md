# Knockoff+ is more conservative than knockoff

Let $q \in \mathbb{R}$ and $W \in \mathbb{R}^p$. The knockoff+ threshold ($c = 1$) is at least the knockoff threshold ($c = 0$), so the knockoff+ selection is a subset of the knockoff selection: $\hat S_1 \subseteq \hat S_0$ (Barber and Candès 2015, arXiv:1404.5609, remark after Definition 2).
