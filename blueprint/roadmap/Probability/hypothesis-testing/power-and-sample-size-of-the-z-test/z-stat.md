# Known-variance z statistic

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, a null mean $\mu_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, a variance $v \ge 0$ and a sample $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the known-variance z statistic is

$$z(x) = \frac{\sqrt n\,(\bar x - \mu_0)}{\sqrt v}, \qquad \bar x = \frac1n \sum_{i=1}^n x_i,$$

with the convention $a/0 = 0$.
