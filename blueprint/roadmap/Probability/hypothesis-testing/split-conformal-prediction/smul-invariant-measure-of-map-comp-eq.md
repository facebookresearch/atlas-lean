# Permutation-invariant laws are invariant under the action

Let $\iota$ be a set, $B$ a measurable space and $\mu$ a measure on $B^\iota$ that is invariant under every coordinate permutation $\omega \mapsto \omega \circ \sigma$. Then $\mu$ is invariant under the coordinate-permutation action $\omega \mapsto \sigma \cdot \omega$, $(\sigma \cdot \omega)(i) = \omega(\sigma^{-1}(i))$, of every permutation $\sigma$.
