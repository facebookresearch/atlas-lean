# The strict lower count is measurable

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The map $\omega \mapsto L(\omega)$ from $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ to $\mathbb{N}$ ([strict lower count](strict-lower-count.md)) is measurable, as a finite sum of indicators of the measurable sets $\{\omega : \omega_i < \omega_{n+1}\}$, $i \le n$.
