# Exactly $k$ coordinates have rank count below $k$

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $\omega : \iota \to \mathbb{R}$ injective and $k \le |\iota|$. Then exactly $k$ coordinates $i$ have fewer than $k$ coordinates strictly below them:

$$\#\{i : r_\omega(i) < k\} = k;$$

the rank counts are a permutation of $0, \dots, |\iota| - 1$. This is the deterministic counting step in the proof of Lemma 1 of Tibshirani, Barber, Candès and Ramdas (2019, arXiv:1904.06019).
