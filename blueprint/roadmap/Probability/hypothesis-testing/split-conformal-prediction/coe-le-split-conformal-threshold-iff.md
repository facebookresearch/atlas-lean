# Coverage through the strict lower count

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $k \ge 1$ and $\omega \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. The test score is covered by the [split conformal threshold](split-conformal-threshold.md) if and only if fewer than $k$ calibration scores lie strictly below it:

$$\omega_{n+1} \le \hat q_k(\omega) \iff L(\omega) < k,$$

where $L$ is the [strict lower count](strict-lower-count.md).
