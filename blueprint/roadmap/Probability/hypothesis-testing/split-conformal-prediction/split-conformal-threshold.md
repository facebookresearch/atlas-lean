# Split conformal threshold

Let $n, k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\omega = (\omega_1, \dots, \omega_{n+1}) \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. The split conformal threshold $\hat q_k(\omega)$ is the [$k$-th smallest](order-statistic.md) of the calibration scores $\omega_1, \dots, \omega_n$ (with multiplicity), and $+\infty$ when $k > n$.
