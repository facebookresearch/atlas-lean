# Log-sum inequality

**Log-sum inequality**: for `p ≥ 0` and `q > 0` on a finset `s`,
`(∑ i ∈ s, p i) * log ((∑ i ∈ s, p i) / ∑ i ∈ s, q i) ≤ ∑ i ∈ s, p i * log (p i / q i)`.
