# Integrable norm sq

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $E$ a normed abelian group, and $X : \Omega \to E$ an almost everywhere strongly measurable map satisfying the power constraint: $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} \lVert X \rVert^2 \, d\mu \le P$. Then the instantaneous power $\omega \mapsto \lVert X(\omega) \rVert^2$ is integrable with respect to $\mu$.
