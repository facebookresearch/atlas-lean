# Mem Lp two

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $E$ a normed abelian group, and $X : \Omega \to E$ an almost everywhere strongly measurable map satisfying the power constraint: $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} \lVert X \rVert^2 \, d\mu \le P$. Then $X \in L^2(\mu)$, that is, $X$ has a finite second moment.
