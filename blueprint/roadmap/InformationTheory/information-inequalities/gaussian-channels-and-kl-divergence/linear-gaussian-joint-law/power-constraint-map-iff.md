# Power Constraint map iff

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $E$ a normed abelian group with a measurable structure containing the open sets, $X : \Omega \to E$ almost everywhere measurable, and $P \in \mathbb{R}$. The power constraint depends only on the law of $X$: the identity map satisfies the power constraint with budget $P$ under the pushforward $X_\ast \mu$ if and only if $X$ satisfies it under $\mu$. Explicitly,

$$\Big(P \ge 0 \text{ and } \int^{-} \lVert x \rVert^2 \, d(X_\ast\mu)(x) \le P\Big) \iff \Big(P \ge 0 \text{ and } \int^{-} \lVert X(\omega) \rVert^2 \, d\mu(\omega) \le P\Big).$$
