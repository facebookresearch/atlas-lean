# Pos Semidef of covariance

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $\kappa$ a finite index set, and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\kappa$ with finite second moment. If $K \in \mathbb{R}^{\kappa \times \kappa}$ is its covariance matrix, $K_{ab} = \operatorname{Cov}_\mu(X_a, X_b)$, then $K$ is positive semidefinite. It may be singular.
