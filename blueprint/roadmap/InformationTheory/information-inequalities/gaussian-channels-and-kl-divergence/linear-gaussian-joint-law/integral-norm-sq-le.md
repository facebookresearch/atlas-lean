# A power constraint bounds the second moment

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $E$ a normed abelian group, and $X : \Omega \to E$ an almost everywhere strongly measurable map satisfying the power constraint: $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} \lVert X \rVert^2 \, d\mu \le P$. Then the integral of $\lVert X \rVert^2$ is bounded by the budget:

$$\int_\Omega \lVert X(\omega) \rVert^2 \, d\mu(\omega) \le P.$$
