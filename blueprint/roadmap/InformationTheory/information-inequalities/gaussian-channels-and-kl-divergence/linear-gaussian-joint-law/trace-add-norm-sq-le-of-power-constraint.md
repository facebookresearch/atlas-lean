# Trace add norm sq le of power Constraint

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $\kappa$ a finite index set, and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\kappa$ measurable and satisfying the power constraint: $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} \lVert X \rVert^2 \, d\mu \le P$. If $m = \int X \, d\mu$ is the mean of $X$ and $K$ its covariance matrix, $K_{ab} = \operatorname{Cov}_\mu(X_a, X_b)$, then

$$\operatorname{tr} K + \lVert m \rVert^2 \le P.$$
