# Kl Div gaussian Real zero variance

Let $m_1, m_2 \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $v_2 > 0$ be a variance. The Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(m_1, 0)$ is the point mass $\delta_{m_1}$, which is not absolutely continuous with respect to $\mathcal{N}(m_2, v_2)$ (the latter gives the singleton $\{m_1\}$ measure zero). Hence

$$\mathrm{KL}\big(\mathcal{N}(m_1, 0) \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(m_2, v_2)\big) = \infty.$$
