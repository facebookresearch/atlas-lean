# Measurable multivariate Gaussian PDFReal right

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert$, let $m \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ and let $S$ be any real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. The Gaussian density function

$$x \mapsto \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n/2} \sqrt{\det S}} \exp\left(-\frac{(x - m)^{\top} S^{-1} (x - m)}{2}\right)$$

is Borel measurable on $\mathbb{R}^{\iota}$, with the conventions $\sqrt{t} = 0$ for $t < 0$, $1/0 = 0$ and $S^{-1} = 0$ for singular $S$.
