Let $k$ be the additive Gaussian noise kernel $k(x) = \mathcal{N}(x, 1)$ with noise variance $1$. For the point-mass input $\delta_3$ and the reference law $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$,

$$\mathrm{KL}\big(\delta_3 \otimes k \,\|\, \delta_3 \times \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\big) = \frac{9}{2},$$

all of it from the input mean, since the log and variance terms cancel.
