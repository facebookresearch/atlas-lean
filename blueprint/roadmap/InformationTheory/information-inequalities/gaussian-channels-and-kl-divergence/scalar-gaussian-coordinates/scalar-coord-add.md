# Scalar Coord add

Let $e : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^1$, $e(x) = (x)$, be the scalar coordinate embedding into the one-dimensional Euclidean space, a linear isometry with inverse $e^{-1}(y) = y_0$. Then for all $x, z \in \mathbb{R}$ the embedded sum is the vector channel output at gain $[1]$:

$$e(x + z) = [1]\, e(x) + e(z).$$
