# Nonneg

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. If $X$ satisfies the scalar power bound with budget $P$, that is $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} X^2 \, d\mu \le P$, then $P \ge 0$.

Equivalently, if $P < 0$, no input, including $X \equiv 0$, satisfies the scalar power bound with budget $P$.
