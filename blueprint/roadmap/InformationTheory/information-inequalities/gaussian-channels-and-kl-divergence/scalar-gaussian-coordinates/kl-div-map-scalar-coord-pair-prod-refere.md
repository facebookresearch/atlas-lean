# KL chain rule in scalar coordinates, reference-output form

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ with marginals $\mu_X$ and $\mu_Y$, let $Q$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$, let $e(x) = (x) \in \mathbb{R}^1$, and write $E = e \times e$. Then, as an identity in $[0, \infty]$,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(E_\ast\mu \parallel e_\ast\mu_X \otimes e_\ast Q\big) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(E_\ast\mu \parallel e_\ast\mu_X \otimes e_\ast\mu_Y\big) + D_{\mathrm{KL}}(e_\ast\mu_Y \parallel e_\ast Q).$$
