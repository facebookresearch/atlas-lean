# Map swap eq map snd comp Prod cond Kernel

Let $X$ and $Y$ be measurable spaces with $X$ standard Borel and nonempty, and let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $X \times Y$ with second marginal $\mu_Y$. Let $\sigma(x, y) = (y, x)$ and let $\kappa$ be the conditional kernel of the swapped measure $\sigma_\ast\mu$, that is, the conditional law of $X$ given $Y$. Then

$$\sigma_\ast\mu = \mu_Y \otimes \kappa.$$
