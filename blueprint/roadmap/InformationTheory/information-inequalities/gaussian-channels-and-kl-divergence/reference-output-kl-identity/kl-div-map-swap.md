# Kl Div map swap

Let $X$ and $Y$ be measurable spaces, let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite measures on $X \times Y$, and let $\sigma : X \times Y \to Y \times X$, $\sigma(x, y) = (y, x)$, be the coordinate swap. Then the Kullback–Leibler divergence is invariant under the swap:

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\sigma_\ast\mu \parallel \sigma_\ast\nu) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu).$$
