Let $\mu$ be the law of $(Z, Z)$ on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, whose first marginal is $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$. For every probability measure $Q$ on $\mathbb{R}$,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(\mu \parallel \mathcal{N}(0, 1) \otimes Q\big) = +\infty,$$

so the reference-output decomposition holds as $+\infty = +\infty$.
