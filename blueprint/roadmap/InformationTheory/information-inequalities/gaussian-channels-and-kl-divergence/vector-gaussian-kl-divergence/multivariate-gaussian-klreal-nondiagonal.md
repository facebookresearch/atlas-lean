# Multivariate Gaussian KLReal nondiagonal

In dimension $n = 2$, take $m = (1, 0)$, $q = (0, 0)$, $S = I$ and

$$T = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

Then $\det T = 3$, $\operatorname{tr}(T^{-1} S) = 4/3$ and $(m - q)^{\top} T^{-1} (m - q) = 2/3$, so the Gaussian divergence closed form is

$$\frac{1}{2}\left(\log 3 - 2 + \frac{4}{3} + \frac{2}{3}\right) = \frac{\log 3}{2}.$$
