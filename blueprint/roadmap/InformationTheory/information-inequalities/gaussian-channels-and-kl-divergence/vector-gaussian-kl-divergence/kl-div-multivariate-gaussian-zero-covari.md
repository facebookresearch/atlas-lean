# Violated premise

Let $\iota$ be a nonempty finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert \ge 1$, and write $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$ for the Gaussian measure on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ with mean $m$ and covariance matrix $S$.

Let $m, q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ and let $T$ be a positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. The zero covariance gives the point mass $\mathcal{N}(m, 0) = \delta_m$, which is not absolutely continuous with respect to $\mathcal{N}(q, T)$, so

$$\mathrm{KL}\big(\mathcal{N}(m, 0) \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(q, T)\big) = \infty.$$
