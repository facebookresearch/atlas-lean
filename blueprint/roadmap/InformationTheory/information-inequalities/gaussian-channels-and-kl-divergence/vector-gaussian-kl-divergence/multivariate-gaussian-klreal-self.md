# Multivariate Gaussian KLReal self

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert$, let $m \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$, and let $S$ be a positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. The Gaussian divergence closed form vanishes at equal means and covariances:

$$\frac{1}{2}\left(\log\frac{\det S}{\det S} - n + \operatorname{tr}(S^{-1} S) + (m - m)^{\top} S^{-1} (m - m)\right) = 0.$$
