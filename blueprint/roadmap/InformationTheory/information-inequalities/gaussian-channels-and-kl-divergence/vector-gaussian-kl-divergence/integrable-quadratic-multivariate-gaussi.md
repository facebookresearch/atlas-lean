# Integrable quadratic multivariate Gaussian

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert$, and write $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$ for the Gaussian measure on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ with mean $m$ and covariance matrix $S$.

Let $m, q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$, let $S$ be a positive semidefinite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix, and let $A$ be any real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. Then the quadratic form $x \mapsto (x - q)^{\top} A (x - q)$ is integrable with respect to $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$.
