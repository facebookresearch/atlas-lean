# Multivariate Gaussian KLReal same covariance

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert$, let $m, q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$, and let $S$ be a positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix. At equal covariances the Gaussian divergence closed form is half the Mahalanobis term:

$$\frac{1}{2}\left(\log\frac{\det S}{\det S} - n + \operatorname{tr}(S^{-1} S) + (m - q)^{\top} S^{-1} (m - q)\right) = \frac{(m - q)^{\top} S^{-1} (m - q)}{2}.$$
