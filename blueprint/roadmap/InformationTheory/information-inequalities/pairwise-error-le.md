# Bhattacharyya pairwise error bound

Let $X$ be a finite alphabet and $p, q : X \to \mathbb{R}$ with $p(x) \ge 0$ for every $x$. Then the $p$-mass of the region where $q$ is at least $p$ is at most the Bhattacharyya coefficient of $p$ and $q$:

$$\sum_{x \in X,\ p(x) \le q(x)} p(x) \le \sum_{x \in X} \sqrt{p(x)\, q(x)},$$

where $\sqrt{t} = 0$ for $t < 0$. No hypothesis on $q$ is needed.
