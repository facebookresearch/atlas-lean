# Comp Prod noise Kernel eq map

Let $E$ be an additive commutative group with a measurable structure in which addition is jointly measurable, let $F$ be a measurable space, let $f : F \to E$ be measurable, let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $E$, and let $\mu$ be an s-finite measure on $F$. Let $k$ be the noise kernel sending $x \in F$ to the law of $f(x) + Z$ with $Z \sim \nu$. Then the joint law $\mu \otimes k$ is the image of the product $\mu \times \nu$ under $(x, z) \mapsto (x, f(x) + z)$:

$$\mu \otimes k = \big((x, z) \mapsto (x, f(x) + z)\big)_* (\mu \times \nu).$$
