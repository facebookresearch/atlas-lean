# Noise Kernel apply multivariate Gaussian

Let $\kappa$ and $\iota$ be finite index sets, $H$ a real $\iota \times \kappa$ matrix and $R$ a real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix, and write $\mathcal{N}(m, R)$ for the Gaussian measure on $\mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ with mean $m$ and covariance parameter $R$. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}^{\kappa}$, the law of $H x + Z$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$ is

$$\mathcal{N}(H x, R).$$
