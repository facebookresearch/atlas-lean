# Measure Mutual Information gaussian Input fin one

Let $h, k, r \in \mathbb{R}$ with $k \ge 0$ and $r > 0$. For the scalar input $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, k)$ and output $hX + Z$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, r)$ independent of $X$, the mutual information of the joint law is

$$I(X; hX + Z) = \frac{\ln\left(1 + h^2 k / r\right)}{2 \ln 2} \text{ bits}.$$
