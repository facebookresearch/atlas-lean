# Data-processing inequality for the Kullback–Leibler divergence

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite measures on a measurable space $X$ and $g\colon X \to Y$ a measurable map. Then

$$\mathrm{KL}(g_\# \mu \,\|\, g_\# \nu) \le \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)$$

in $[0, \infty]$. Here $\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) = \int \log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu}\, d\mu + \nu(X) - \mu(X)$ when $\mu \ll \nu$ and $\log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu}$ is $\mu$-integrable, and $\infty$ otherwise; for probability measures it is the Kullback–Leibler divergence.
