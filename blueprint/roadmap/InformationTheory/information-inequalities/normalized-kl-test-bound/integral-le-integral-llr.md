# Integral le integral llr

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space $\alpha$ with $\mu \ll \nu$, and suppose the log-likelihood ratio $\log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu}$ is $\mu$-integrable. Let $f : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ be $\mu$-integrable, with $e^{f}$ integrable for $\nu$ and normalized: $\int e^{f} \, d\nu = 1$. Then

$$\int f \, d\mu \le \int \log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu} \, d\mu.$$
