# Integral exp two Point Score

Let $U = \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{true}} + \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{false}}$ be the uniform law on $\{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$ and let $s(\mathrm{true}) = \ln \tfrac{3}{2}$, $s(\mathrm{false}) = \ln \tfrac{1}{2}$. Then $s$ is normalized against $U$:

$$\int e^{s} \, dU = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \tfrac{3}{2} + \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 1.$$
