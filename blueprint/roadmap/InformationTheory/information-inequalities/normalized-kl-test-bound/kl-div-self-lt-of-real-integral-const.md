The normalization $\int e^{f} \, d\nu = 1$ cannot be dropped from the normalized test bound. Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\alpha$ and $c > 0$. The constant score $f \equiv c$ has $\int e^{c} \, d\nu = e^{c} \ne 1$, and at $\mu = \nu$ the bound fails:

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\nu \parallel \nu) = 0 < c = \max\left\{\int c \, d\nu,\ 0\right\}.$$
