# Kl Div gaussian Real same variance

Let $m_1, m_2 \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $v > 0$ be a variance. The Kullback-Leibler divergence, in nats, between two real Gaussians with the common variance $v$ is

$$\mathrm{KL}\big(\mathcal{N}(m_1, v) \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(m_2, v)\big) = \frac{(m_1 - m_2)^2}{2v}.$$
