# Scalar Gaussian KLReal same variance

Let $m_1, m_2, v \in \mathbb{R}$ with $v \neq 0$. At a common variance the scalar Gaussian divergence closed form

$$D(m_1, m_2, v_1, v_2) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\log\frac{v_2}{v_1} + \frac{v_1 + (m_1 - m_2)^2}{v_2} - 1\right)$$

reduces to

$$D(m_1, m_2, v, v) = \frac{(m_1 - m_2)^2}{2v}.$$
