# Divergence defect as a first-order term

If $Q > 0$, $P, P^* \ge 0$ and $P$ vanishes wherever $P^*$ does, then

$$D(P\|Q) - D(P\|P^*) - D(P^*\|Q) = \sum_a (P(a) - P^*(a))(\log P^*(a) - \log Q(a)).$$

No normalisation is needed. By the support hypothesis, the terms with $P^*(a) = 0$ have $P(a) = 0$ and
vanish on both sides.
