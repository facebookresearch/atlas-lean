# Tangent line of $x \log x$

For $x \ge 0$ and $y > 0$: $\ y\log y - x\log x \le (y - x)(\log y + 1)$. The convex function
$t \mapsto t\log t$ lies above its tangent at $y$; the proof uses $\log u \le u - 1$.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "I-divergence geometry of probability distributions and minimization problems", *Ann. Probab.* 3(1) (1975), 146–158, proof of Lemma 2.1 (convexity of $t \log t$).
