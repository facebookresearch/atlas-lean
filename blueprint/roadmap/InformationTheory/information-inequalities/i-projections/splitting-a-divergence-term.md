# Splitting a divergence term

For $r \ge 0$ and $q > 0$: $\ r\log(r/q) = r\log r - r\log q$, both sides being $0$ at $r = 0$
(the convention $0\log 0 = 0$).

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, Section II (the conventions $0\log 0 = 0\log(0/0) = 0$).
