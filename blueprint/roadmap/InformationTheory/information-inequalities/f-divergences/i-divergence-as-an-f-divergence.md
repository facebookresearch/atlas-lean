# The I-divergence is the f-divergence of $t \log t$

For functions $P, Q$ on a finite alphabet with $Q > 0$, $D_f(P\|Q) = \sum_a P(a)\log(P(a)/Q(a))$ with
$f(t) = t\log t$: the I-divergence used
in the [method of types](../../entropy-and-mutual-information/method-of-types/README.md) and for
[I-projections](../i-projections/README.md) is an f-divergence.

## Background

- [Kullback–Leibler divergence of finite probability laws](../../entropy-and-mutual-information/shannon-entropy-and-kullback-leibler-divergence/to-real-kl-div-to-measure.md):
  for probability mass functions the same sum is the Kullback–Leibler divergence of the associated
  measures.
