# Lumping of a distribution along a map

For a map $g : \alpha \to \beta$ and $P : \alpha \to \mathbb R$, the lumping of $P$ along $g$ is
$P^g(b) = \sum_{a : g(a) = b} P(a)$: the distribution of $g$ under $P$. Taking for $g$ the map sending
each letter to its block recovers the source's $B$-lumping for a partition $B$ of $\alpha$.
