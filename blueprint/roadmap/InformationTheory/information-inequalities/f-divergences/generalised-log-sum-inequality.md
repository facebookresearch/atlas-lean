# Generalised log-sum inequality

For $f$ convex on $[0,\infty)$, a nonempty finite set $s$, $a_i \ge 0$ and $b_i > 0$,

$$\Bigl(\sum_{i\in s} b_i\Bigr) f\!\left(\frac{\sum_i a_i}{\sum_i b_i}\right) \le \sum_{i \in s} b_i\, f\!\left(\frac{a_i}{b_i}\right).$$

This is Jensen's inequality with weights $b_i / \sum_j b_j$. The source also states the equality case
for strictly convex $f$.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár and P. C. Shields, *Information Theory and Statistics: A Tutorial*, Foundations and Trends in Communications and Information Theory 1(4) (2004), 417–528, inequality (4.1).
