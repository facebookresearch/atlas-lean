# Mutual Info eq finite Mutual Information

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite types and let $p$ be a probability mass function on $\alpha \times \beta$, with marginals $p_X(x) = \sum_{y} p(x, y)$ and $p_Y(y) = \sum_{x} p(x, y)$. By definition, the mutual information of $p$ in nats equals its mutual information in bits times $\ln 2$:

$$I_{\mathrm{nats}}(p) = I_{\mathrm{bits}}(p) \cdot \ln 2.$$
