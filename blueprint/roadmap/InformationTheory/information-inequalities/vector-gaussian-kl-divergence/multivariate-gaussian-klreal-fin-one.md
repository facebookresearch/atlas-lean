# Multivariate Gaussian KLReal fin one

For all real numbers $m, q, s, t$, the Gaussian divergence closed form in dimension one, at the vectors $(m), (q) \in \mathbb{R}^1$ and the $1 \times 1$ matrices $S = (s)$, $T = (t)$, is

$$\frac{1}{2}\left(\log\frac{t}{s} - 1 + \frac{s}{t} + \frac{(m - q)^2}{t}\right),$$

with the conventions $x / 0 = 0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log \lvert x \rvert$ for $x < 0$. No hypothesis on $s, t$ is needed.
