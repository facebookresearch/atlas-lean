# Integrable llr multivariate Gaussian

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set with $n = \lvert \iota \rvert$, and write $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$ for the Gaussian measure on the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ with mean $m$ and covariance matrix $S$.

Let $m, q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota}$ and let $S, T$ be positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrices. Then the log-likelihood ratio $x \mapsto \log \frac{d\mathcal{N}(m, S)}{d\mathcal{N}(q, T)}(x)$ is integrable with respect to $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$.
