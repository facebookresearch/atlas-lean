# Kl Div multivariate Gaussian nondiagonal

On the Euclidean plane $\mathbb{R}^2$, let $\mathcal{N}(m, S)$ denote the Gaussian with mean $m$ and covariance $S$. Then

$$\mathrm{KL}\left(\mathcal{N}\big((1, 0), I\big) \,\Big\|\, \mathcal{N}\left(0, \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}\right)\right) = \frac{\log 3}{2}.$$
