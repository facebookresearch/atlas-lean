# Shannon Entropy Nats eq mul log two

Let $\alpha$ be a finite set and $p$ a probability mass function on $\alpha$. The entropy in nats is the entropy in bits times $\log 2$:

$$-\sum_{a \in \alpha} p(a) \log p(a) = \left(-\sum_{a \in \alpha} p(a) \log_2 p(a)\right) \log 2,$$

with $0 \log 0 = 0$ on both sides.
