# Rate Distortion zero zero

For the degenerate source $\mathcal{N}(0, 0) = \delta_0$, the Gaussian rate-distortion function at zero distortion is

$$R(0) = 0.$$

Hence the hypothesis $\sigma^2 > 0$ in $R(0) = \infty$ cannot be dropped. Here $R(D)$ is the infimum of the mutual information, in bits, over all probability measures $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu \le D$, taken with $\sigma^2 = 0$.
