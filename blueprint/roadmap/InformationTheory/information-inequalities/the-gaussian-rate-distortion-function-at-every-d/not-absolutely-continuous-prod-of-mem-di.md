# Not absolutely Continuous prod of mem distortion Feasible zero

Let $\sigma^2 > 0$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ that is distortion-feasible at level $0$ for $\sigma^2$ (first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu = 0$). Then $\mu$ is not absolutely continuous with respect to the product $\mu_1 \otimes \mu_2$ of its marginals:

$$\mu \not\ll \mu_1 \otimes \mu_2.$$

Indeed $\mu$ gives the diagonal full mass, while $\mu_1 \otimes \mu_2$ gives it mass zero because $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ has no atoms.
