# Distortion Feasible mono

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ and $D \le D'$ be real numbers. Every probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ that is distortion-feasible at level $D$ for $\sigma^2$ is also distortion-feasible at level $D'$; that is, the feasible set at level $D$ is contained in the feasible set at level $D'$.

A probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ is distortion-feasible at level $D$ for the source variance $\sigma^2$ if $D \ge 0$, the first marginal of $\mu$ is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu(x, y) \le D$, the integral of a nonnegative function, possibly infinite.
