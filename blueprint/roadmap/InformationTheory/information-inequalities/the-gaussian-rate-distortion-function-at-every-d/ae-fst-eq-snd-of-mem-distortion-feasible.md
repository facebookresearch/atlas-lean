# Ae fst eq snd of mem distortion Feasible zero

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ that is distortion-feasible at level $0$ for $\sigma^2$, i.e. its first marginal is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu = 0$. Then the reconstruction equals the source almost surely:

$$x = y \quad \text{for } \mu\text{-almost every } (x, y).$$
