# Diagonal Reconstruction mem distortion Feasible zero

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$. The exact reconstruction, the joint law of $(X, X)$ with $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, is distortion-feasible at level $0$ for $\sigma^2$. Hence the infimum defining $R(0)$ is not taken over the empty set.

A probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ is distortion-feasible at level $D$ for the source variance $\sigma^2$ if $D \ge 0$, the first marginal of $\mu$ is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu(x, y) \le D$, the integral of a nonnegative function, possibly infinite.
