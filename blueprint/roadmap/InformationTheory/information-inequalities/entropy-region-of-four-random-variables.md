# Entropy region of four random variables

Characterize which vectors of joint entropies are possible for four variables. This constrains network coding, distributed storage, and information inequalities.

## What would settle it

Define the closed almost-entropic region in 15-dimensional space and characterize it by an independently specified condition. Distinguish the region from its closure, and do not assume a finite list of linear inequalities suffices. A sharper exact inequality or the optimal normalized Ingleton violation is a concrete subtarget.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Csirmaz calls mapping the entropy region of at least four random variables an important open problem. Chang (preprint) improves the bound on the Ingleton score; a bound on one functional does not characterize the region.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
