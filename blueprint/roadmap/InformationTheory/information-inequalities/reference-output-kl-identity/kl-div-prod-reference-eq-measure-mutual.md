# Reference-output decomposition of mutual information

Let $X$ and $Y$ be measurable spaces with $X$ standard Borel and nonempty, let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $X \times Y$ with marginals $\mu_X$ and $\mu_Y$, and let $Q$ be a probability measure on $Y$. Let $I(\mu) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \mu_X \otimes \mu_Y) / \ln 2$ be the mutual information of $\mu$ in bits, with divergences in nats. Then, as an identity in $[0, \infty]$ including infinite values,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \mu_X \otimes Q) = \ln 2 \cdot I(\mu) + D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu_Y \parallel Q).$$
