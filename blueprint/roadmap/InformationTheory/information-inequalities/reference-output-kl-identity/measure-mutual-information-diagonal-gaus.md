Let $\mu$ be the law of $(Z, Z)$ on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$. Then its mutual information is infinite:

$$I(\mu) = \frac{D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \mathcal{N}(0,1) \otimes \mathcal{N}(0,1))}{\ln 2} = +\infty.$$
