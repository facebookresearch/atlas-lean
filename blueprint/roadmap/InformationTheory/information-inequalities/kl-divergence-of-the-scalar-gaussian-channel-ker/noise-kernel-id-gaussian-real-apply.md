# Noise Kernel id gaussian Real apply

Let $N \ge 0$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The additive noise kernel on $\mathbb{R}$ with identity gain and noise law $\mathcal{N}(0, N)$, which sends $x$ to the law of $x + Z$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, N)$, sends $x$ to the Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(x, N)$ (with $\mathcal{N}(x, 0) = \delta_x$).
