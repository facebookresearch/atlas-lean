# Relative entropy of a tilted measure

Let $\nu$ be a probability measure and $f$ a bounded measurable function, and let $\nu_f$ be the Gibbs (tilted)
measure with density $e^{f} / \int e^{f}\,d\nu$ with respect to $\nu$. Then $\mathrm{KL}(\nu_f \,\|\, \nu)$ is
finite and $\mathrm{KL}(\nu_f \,\|\, \nu) = \int f\,d\nu_f - \log \int e^{f}\,d\nu$.
