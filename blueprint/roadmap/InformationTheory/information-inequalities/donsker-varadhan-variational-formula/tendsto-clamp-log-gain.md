# Convergence of the Donsker–Varadhan gain

With $\operatorname{cl}_k(r) = \max(-k, \min(\log r, k))$ for $r > 0$ and $\operatorname{cl}_k(r) = -k$ for $r \le 0$, for $r \ge 0$ and $f_k = \operatorname{cl}_k(r)$,
$r f_k - e^{f_k} + 1 \to r \log r - r + 1$ as $k \to \infty$ through the natural numbers (with $0 \log 0 = 0$).
