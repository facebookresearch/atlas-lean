# Donsker–Varadhan lower bound on the supremum

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space. If $\mu \ll \nu$, then $\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) \le$ $\sup_f \bigl(\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^f\,d\nu\bigr)^+$, the supremum (in $[0, \infty]$) being over the bounded measurable functions $f$; the
clamped log-likelihood ratios $\operatorname{cl}_k(d\mu/d\nu)$ approach the supremum.
