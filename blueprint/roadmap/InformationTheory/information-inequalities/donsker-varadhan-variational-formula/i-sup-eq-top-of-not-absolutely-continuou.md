# The Donsker–Varadhan supremum is infinite without absolute continuity

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space. If $\mu$ is not absolutely continuous with respect to $\nu$, then
$\sup_f \bigl(\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^f\,d\nu\bigr)^+$, the supremum (in $[0, \infty]$) being over the bounded measurable functions $f$, is $\infty$.
