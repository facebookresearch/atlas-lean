# Donsker–Varadhan inequality in extended nonnegative reals

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space. For every bounded measurable $f$,
$\bigl(\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^{f}\,d\nu\bigr)^+ \le \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)$ in $[0, \infty]$, with no finiteness
assumption on the divergence. Taking the supremum over $f$ gives one half of the Donsker–Varadhan variational
formula for relative entropy (Varadhan, *Large Deviations*, NYU lecture notes, 2010, Section 3, Lemma 1.2).
