# Donsker–Varadhan inequality, integral form

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space. Suppose $\mu \ll \nu$ and the log-likelihood ratio $\log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu}$ is
$\mu$-integrable, and let $f$ be $\mu$-integrable with $e^{f}$ $\nu$-integrable. Then

$$\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^{f}\,d\nu \le \int \log \frac{d\mu}{d\nu}\,d\mu.$$

## Sources

- Varadhan, *Large Deviations*, NYU lecture notes, 2010, Section 3, Lemma 1.2 (the inequality half of the variational formula).
