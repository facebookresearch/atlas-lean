# Donsker–Varadhan inequality for bounded test functions

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space. If $\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) < \infty$ and $f$ is measurable and bounded, then
$\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^{f}\,d\nu \le \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)$.

## Sources

- Varadhan, *Large Deviations*, NYU lecture notes, 2010, Section 3, Lemma 1.2.
