# An event's probabilities differ by at most the total variation

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space and $B$ a measurable set. Then
$|\mu(B) - \nu(B)| \le \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)$, where $\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)|$.
