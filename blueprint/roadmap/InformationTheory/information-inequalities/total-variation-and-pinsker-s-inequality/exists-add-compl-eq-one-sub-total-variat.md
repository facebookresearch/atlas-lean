# The optimal test has total error 1 − TV

For probability measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ on a measurable space there is a measurable event $A$,
a Hahn set for $(\mu, \nu)$, with

$$
\mu(A) + \nu(A^c) = 1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu).
$$

With the [two-point testing bound](one-sub-total-variation-dist-le-add-comp.md), $1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)$
is the minimal total error of a test between $\mu$ and $\nu$.

## Sources

- Y. Polyanskiy and Y. Wu, Information Theory: From Coding to Learning (Cambridge University Press, 2025), Theorem 7.7(a), eq. (7.19).
