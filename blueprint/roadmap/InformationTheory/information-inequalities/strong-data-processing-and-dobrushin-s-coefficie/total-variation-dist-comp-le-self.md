# Data-processing inequality for total variation

For a Markov kernel $\kappa$ and probability measures $\mu$, $\nu$,
$\mathrm{TV}(\kappa \circ \mu, \kappa \circ \nu) \le \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)$. It follows from
[Dobrushin's contraction](total-variation-dist-comp-le.md) since $\eta_{\mathrm{TV}}(\kappa) \le 1$.
