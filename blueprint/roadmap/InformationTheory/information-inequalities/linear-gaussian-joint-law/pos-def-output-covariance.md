# Pos Def output covariance

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$ any real matrix, $K \in \mathbb{R}^{\kappa \times \kappa}$ positive semidefinite, and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive definite. Then $HKH^\top + R$ is positive definite. This holds even when $H$ is rank-deficient and $K$ is singular.
