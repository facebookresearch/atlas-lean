# Mem Lp two output

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$, and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive semidefinite. Let $Z : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ be measurable with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$, and let $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\kappa$ have finite second moment. Then the channel output $HX + Z$ has finite second moment: $HX + Z \in L^2(\mu)$.
