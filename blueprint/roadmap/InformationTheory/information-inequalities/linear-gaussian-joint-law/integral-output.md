# Output mean

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$, and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive semidefinite. Let $Z : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ be measurable with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$, and let $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\kappa$ have finite second moment and mean $m = \int X \, d\mu$. Then the output mean is

$$\int_\Omega \big(HX(\omega) + Z(\omega)\big) \, d\mu(\omega) = Hm.$$
