# Total second moment

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a probability space, $\kappa$ a finite index set, and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^\kappa$ with finite second moment, mean $m = \int X \, d\mu$ and covariance matrix $K$, $K_{ab} = \operatorname{Cov}_\mu(X_a, X_b)$. Then the total second moment splits as

$$\operatorname{tr} K + \lVert m \rVert^2 = \int_\Omega \lVert X(\omega) \rVert^2 \, d\mu(\omega).$$
