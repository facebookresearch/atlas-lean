# Integral two Point Score tilted

Let $U = \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{true}} + \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{false}}$ and $s(\mathrm{true}) = \ln \tfrac{3}{2}$, $s(\mathrm{false}) = \ln \tfrac{1}{2}$. Under the exponential tilt $U_s$ of $U$ by $s$ (density $e^{s} / \int e^{s}\, dU$, which gives masses $\tfrac{3}{4}$ and $\tfrac{1}{4}$), the score has mean

$$\int s \, dU_s = \tfrac{3}{4} \ln \tfrac{3}{2} + \tfrac{1}{4} \ln \tfrac{1}{2} \approx 0.1308.$$
