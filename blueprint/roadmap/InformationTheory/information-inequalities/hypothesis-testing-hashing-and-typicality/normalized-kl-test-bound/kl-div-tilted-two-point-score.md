# Kl Div tilted two Point Score

Let $U = \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{true}} + \tfrac{1}{2}\delta_{\mathrm{false}}$, $s(\mathrm{true}) = \ln \tfrac{3}{2}$, $s(\mathrm{false}) = \ln \tfrac{1}{2}$, and let $U_s$ be the exponential tilt of $U$ by $s$. Then the normalized test bound is attained at $\mu = U_s$:

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U_s \parallel U) = \max\left\{\int s \, dU_s,\ 0\right\}.$$
