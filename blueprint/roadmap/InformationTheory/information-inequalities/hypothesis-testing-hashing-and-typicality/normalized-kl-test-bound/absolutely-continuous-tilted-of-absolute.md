# Absolutely Continuous tilted of absolutely Continuous

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be measures on a measurable space $\alpha$ and $f : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ with $e^{f}$ integrable for $\nu$. Let $\nu_f$ be the exponential tilt of $\nu$ by $f$, the measure with density $e^{f} / \int e^{f} \, d\nu$ with respect to $\nu$, where division by zero gives zero; in particular, the tilt of the zero measure $\nu = 0$ is the zero measure. If $\mu \ll \nu$, then $\mu \ll \nu_f$.
