Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be probability measures on a measurable space $\alpha$. The zero score $f = 0$ is normalized, $\int e^{0} \, d\nu = 1$, and the normalized test bound for it reads

$$0 = \max\left\{\int 0 \, d\mu,\ 0\right\} \le D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu).$$
