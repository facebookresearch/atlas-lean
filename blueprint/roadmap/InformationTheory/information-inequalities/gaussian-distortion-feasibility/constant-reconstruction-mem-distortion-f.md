# Constant Reconstruction mem distortion Feasible

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$. The joint law $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \otimes \delta_0$ of a source $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and the constant reconstruction $\hat{X} = 0$ is distortion-feasible at level $D = \sigma^2$, since

$$\int x^2 \, d\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)(x) = \sigma^2.$$

A probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ is distortion-feasible at level $D$ for the source variance $\sigma^2$ if $D \ge 0$, the first marginal of $\mu$ is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu(x, y) \le D$, the integral of a nonnegative function, possibly infinite.
