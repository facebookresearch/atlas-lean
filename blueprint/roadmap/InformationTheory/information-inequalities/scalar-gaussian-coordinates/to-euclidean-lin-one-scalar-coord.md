# To Euclidean Lin one scalar Coord

Let $e : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^1$, $e(x) = (x)$, be the scalar coordinate embedding into the one-dimensional Euclidean space, a linear isometry with inverse $e^{-1}(y) = y_0$. The $1 \times 1$ gain matrix $[1]$, acting as a linear map on $\mathbb{R}^1$, fixes embedded scalars: $[1]\, e(x) = e(x)$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
