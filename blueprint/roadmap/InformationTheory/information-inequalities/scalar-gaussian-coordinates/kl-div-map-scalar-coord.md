# Scalar embedding preserves Kullback–Leibler divergence

Let $\iota : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^1$, $x \mapsto (x)$, be the coordinate isometry onto one-dimensional Euclidean space. For finite measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}$,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\iota_*\mu \,\|\, \iota_*\nu) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)$$

as values in $[0, \infty]$.
