# Power identification

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, $P \in \mathbb{R}$, and $e(x) = (x) \in \mathbb{R}^1$. The embedded input $e \circ X$ satisfies the vector power constraint ($P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} \lVert e(X) \rVert^2 \, d\mu \le P$) if and only if $X$ satisfies the scalar power bound ($P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} X^2 \, d\mu \le P$). A nonzero mean contributes through the norm.
