# Scalar power bound equals the vector power constraint

Let $\iota : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^1$, $x \mapsto (x)$, be the coordinate isometry onto one-dimensional Euclidean space. For any measure $\mu$ on $\Omega$, any $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and any $P \in \mathbb{R}$,

$$\Bigl(0 \le P \ \text{and}\ \int^{-} \|\iota(X(\omega))\|^2\,d\mu(\omega) \le P\Bigr) \iff \Bigl(0 \le P \ \text{and}\ \int^{-} X(\omega)^2\,d\mu(\omega) \le P\Bigr),$$

where $\int^{-}$ is the lower Lebesgue integral in $[0, \infty]$ and $P$ is compared as an element of $[0, \infty]$.
