# Mem Lp scalar Coord of scalar Power Bound

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space and $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ almost everywhere strongly measurable, satisfying the scalar power bound: $P \ge 0$ and $\int^{-} X^2 \, d\mu \le P$. Then the embedded input $\omega \mapsto (X(\omega)) \in \mathbb{R}^1$ has finite second moment: it lies in $L^2(\mu)$.
