# KL chain rule in scalar coordinates

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite measures on $\mathbb{R}$, let $\kappa$ and $\eta$ be Markov kernels from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$, let $e(x) = (x) \in \mathbb{R}^1$, and write $E = e \times e$. Then, as an identity in $[0, \infty]$,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(E_\ast(\mu \otimes \kappa) \parallel E_\ast(\nu \otimes \eta)\big) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(e_\ast\mu \parallel e_\ast\nu) + D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(E_\ast(\mu \otimes \kappa) \parallel E_\ast(\mu \otimes \eta)\big).$$
