# Scalar Power Bound

Let $(\Omega, \mu)$ be a measure space, $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ and $P \in \mathbb{R}$. The input $X$ satisfies the scalar power bound with budget $P$ if

$$P \ge 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \int^{-} X(\omega)^2 \, d\mu(\omega) \le P,$$

where the integral is the lower Lebesgue integral in $[0, \infty]$. The condition $P \ge 0$ is needed because in $[0, \infty]$ a negative budget would be read as $0$.
