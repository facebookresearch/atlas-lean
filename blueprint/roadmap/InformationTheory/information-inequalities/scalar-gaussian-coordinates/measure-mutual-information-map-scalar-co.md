# Measure Mutual Information map scalar Coord Pair

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$, let $e(x) = (x) \in \mathbb{R}^1$, and let $e \times e : (a, b) \mapsto (e(a), e(b))$. Then the mutual information in bits, $I(\mu) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \mu_X \otimes \mu_Y)/\ln 2 \in [0, \infty]$, is unchanged by the embedding, including the value $+\infty$:

$$I\big((e \times e)_\ast\mu\big) = I(\mu).$$
