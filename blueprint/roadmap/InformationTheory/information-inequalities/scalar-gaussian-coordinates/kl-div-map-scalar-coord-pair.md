# Kl Div map scalar Coord Pair

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite measures on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$, let $e(x) = (x) \in \mathbb{R}^1$, and let $e \times e : (a, b) \mapsto (e(a), e(b))$. Then

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big((e \times e)_\ast\mu \parallel (e \times e)_\ast\nu\big) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \parallel \nu).$$
