# Gaussian law, read back

Let $m \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s \ge 0$. The coordinate projection $e^{-1} : \mathbb{R}^1 \to \mathbb{R}$, $y \mapsto y_0$, is measure-preserving from the one-dimensional multivariate Gaussian $\mathcal{N}((m), [s])$ to the real Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(m, s)$:

$$(e^{-1})_\ast\,\mathcal{N}\big((m), [s]\big) = \mathcal{N}(m, s),$$

and $e^{-1}$ is measurable.
