# Scalar Power Bound gaussian Real

Let $m \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s \ge 0$. Under the real Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(m, s)$, the identity input $x \mapsto x$ satisfies the scalar power bound with budget $m^2 + s$:

$$m^2 + s \ge 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \int^{-} x^2 \, d\mathcal{N}(m, s)(x) \le m^2 + s.$$

A nonzero mean contributes to the total power.
