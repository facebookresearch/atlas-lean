# Classical Fuchs-van de Graaf upper bound

Let `p` and `q` be probability vectors on a finite type `S` (nonnegative
real functions with `∑ x, p x = 1` and `∑ x, q x = 1`). Then `TV(p, q) ≤ √(1
- B(p, q) ^ 2)`, where `B(p, q) = ∑ x, √(p x * q x)` is the Bhattacharyya
coefficient and `TV(p, q) = (1 / 2) ∑ x, |p x - q x|` is the total variation
distance.
