# Eq comp Prod cond Kernel of map fst eq

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ whose first marginal is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$. Then

$$\mu = \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \otimes \kappa,$$

where $\kappa$ is the conditional kernel of $\mu$ (the disintegration of $\mu$ with respect to its first coordinate) and $\nu \otimes \kappa$ denotes the joint law with first marginal $\nu$ and conditional law $\kappa(x)$ of the second coordinate given the first equal to $x$.
