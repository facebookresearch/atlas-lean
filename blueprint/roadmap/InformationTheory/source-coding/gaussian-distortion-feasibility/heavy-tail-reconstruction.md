# A heavy-tailed reconstruction of a Gaussian source

The heavy-tailed reconstruction for the standard Gaussian source is the joint law of $(X, \hat{X})$ with $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ and

$$\hat{X} = X - e^{X^2/4},$$

that is, the image of $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ under $x \mapsto \left(x, x - e^{x^2/4}\right)$. Its square error is $(X - \hat{X})^2 = e^{X^2/2}$.
