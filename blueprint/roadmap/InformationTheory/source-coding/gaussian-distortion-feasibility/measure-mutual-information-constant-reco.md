# Measure Mutual Information constant Reconstruction

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$. The joint law $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \otimes \delta_0$ of a source $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and the constant reconstruction $\hat{X} = 0$ has mutual information

$$I\left(\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \otimes \delta_0\right) = 0.$$
