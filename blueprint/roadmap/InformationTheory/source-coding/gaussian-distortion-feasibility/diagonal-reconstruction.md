# Diagonal Reconstruction

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$. The exact reconstruction $\hat{X} = X$ for the source $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ is the joint law of $(X, X)$, the image of $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ under the diagonal map $x \mapsto (x, x)$.

For $\sigma^2 = 1$ this is the law of $(Z, Z)$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, concentrated on the diagonal.
