# Gaussian rate-distortion with integrable distortion

For $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ and $D \in \mathbb{R}$, let $\mathcal{F}(\sigma^2, D)$ be the set of probability measures $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $D \ge 0$, first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ (for $\sigma^2 = 0$ this is the point mass at $0$) and $\int (x - \hat x)^2\,d\mu \le D$ as a lower integral in $[0, \infty]$, and let $R(D) = \inf_{\mu \in \mathcal{F}(\sigma^2, D)} I(\mu)$ in $[0, \infty]$, where $I(\mu)$ is the mutual information of the two coordinates under $\mu$. If $D \ge 0$, then $\mu \in \mathcal{F}(\sigma^2, D)$ if and only if $\mu$ has first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, $(x - \hat x)^2$ is $\mu$-integrable and $\mathbb{E}_\mu[(X - \hat X)^2] \le D$; consequently $R(D)$ is the infimum of $I(\mu)$ over the measures with these three properties.
