# Measure Mutual Information eq top of mem distortion Feasible zero

Let $\sigma^2 > 0$ and let $\mu$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ that is distortion-feasible at level $0$ for $\sigma^2$ (first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and $\int (x - y)^2 \, d\mu = 0$). Then

$$I(\mu) = \infty.$$

The mutual information $I(\mu)$ is the Kullback–Leibler divergence of $\mu$ from the product of its marginals divided by $\ln 2$, with values in $[0, \infty]$.
