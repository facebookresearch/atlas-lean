# Measure Mutual Information diagonal Reconstruction

Let $\sigma^2 > 0$. The exact reconstruction, the joint law $\mu$ of $(X, X)$ with $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, has infinite mutual information:

$$I(\mu) = \infty.$$

The mutual information $I(\mu)$ is the Kullback–Leibler divergence of $\mu$ from the product of its marginals divided by $\ln 2$, with values in $[0, \infty]$.

For example, for $\sigma^2 = 1$ the law of $(Z, Z)$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ has infinite mutual information.
