# Gallager's inner sum at $\rho = 0$

At $\rho = 0$ the inner sum is the output law:
$g_b(0) = \sum_a p(a) W(b \mid a) = q(b)$, the probability of the output $b$ when the input has
law $p$, written as a real number.
