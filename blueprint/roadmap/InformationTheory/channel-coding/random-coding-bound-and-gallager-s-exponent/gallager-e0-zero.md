# $E_0$ vanishes at $\rho = 0$

For every discrete memoryless channel $W$ on finite alphabets and every input law $p$,

$$E_0(0, p) = -\ln \sum_b \sum_a p(a) W(b \mid a) = -\ln 1 = 0.$$

Gallager states this (equation (26)) under the standing assumption $I(p) \ne 0$ of his Theorem 2;
the assumption is not needed.

## Informally uses

- [Gallager's inner sum at $\rho = 0$](gallager-inner-zero.md): the inner sums are the output
  probabilities, which sum to $1$.

## Sources

- R. G. Gallager, "A simple derivation of the coding theorem and some applications", IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory IT-11(1):3–18, January 1965, Theorem 2, equation (26).
