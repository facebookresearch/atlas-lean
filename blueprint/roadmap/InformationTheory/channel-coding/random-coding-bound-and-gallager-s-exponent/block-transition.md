# Block transition probability of a memoryless channel

For a channel $W$ used $N$ times without memory, the probability of receiving
$y = (y_1, \dots, y_N)$ when $x = (x_1, \dots, x_N)$ is sent is

$$W^N(y \mid x) = \prod_{i=1}^{N} W(y_i \mid x_i).$$
