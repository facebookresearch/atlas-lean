# Det pos noise and output

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$, $K \in \mathbb{R}^{\kappa \times \kappa}$ positive semidefinite and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive definite. Then

$$\det R > 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \det\left(R + H K H^{\mathsf{T}}\right) > 0.$$
