# Capacity region of the two-receiver broadcast channel

Characterize the achievable rates when a sender communicates independent information to two receivers through a common channel.

## What would settle it

Fix finite alphabets, the channel law, message types, and the average/maximum-error convention. For a proposed non-tautological region R(W), prove both code achievability and a matching converse. Do not merely restate the definition as a limit over all codes.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Huang, Liu and Liu (preprint) show that Marton's inner bound is not always the capacity region; the capacity region itself is not determined there.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
