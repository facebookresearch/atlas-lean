# Capacity of the binary deletion channel

Determine the maximum reliable communication rate when bits are independently deleted and deletion locations are unknown. Synchronization makes this much harder than an erasure channel.

## What would settle it

Define deletion-channel codes and vanishing-error achievable rates. Prove matching achievability and converse bounds for the capacity C(d), or a new rigorously certified narrowing at specified d. Finite-block numerical optimization alone does not establish asymptotic capacity.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Papailiopoulos (preprint) determines the capacity to within one hundredth of a bit and states that the exact capacity remains unknown.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
