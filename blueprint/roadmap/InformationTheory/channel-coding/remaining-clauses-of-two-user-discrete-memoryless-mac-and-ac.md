# Single-letter capacity region of the two-user multiple-access channel

This node covers the single-letter description of the capacity region of a two-user discrete memoryless
multiple-access channel, defined on the page listed under the dependencies.

## Expansion

A sound expansion has separate pages for:

1. **Fixed-input pentagons.** For input laws $P_{X_1} P_{X_2}$, the pentagon
   $\{R_1 \le I(X_1; Y \mid X_2),\ R_2 \le I(X_2; Y \mid X_1),\ R_1 + R_2 \le I(X_1, X_2; Y)\}$ in the nonnegative quadrant.
2. **Capacity region.** The closure of the convex hull of the union of these pentagons over all product input laws (with
   time sharing), as in the source's Theorem 26.1, (26.1); a single pentagon is not the region in general.
