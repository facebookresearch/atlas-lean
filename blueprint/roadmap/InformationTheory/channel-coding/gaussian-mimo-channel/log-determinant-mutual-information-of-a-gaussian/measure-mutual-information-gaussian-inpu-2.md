# Measure Mutual Information gaussian Input zero

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite index sets with $\iota$ nonempty, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$ and $R \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ positive definite. For the input $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0)$, the point mass at $0 \in \mathbb{R}^\kappa$, and output $HX + Z$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$ independent of $X$, the mutual information of the joint law is

$$I(X; HX + Z) = 0.$$
