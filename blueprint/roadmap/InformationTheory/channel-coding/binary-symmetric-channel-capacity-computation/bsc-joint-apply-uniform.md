# Bsc Joint apply uniform

Let $\delta \in [0, 1]$. For the binary symmetric channel with crossover probability $\delta$ and uniform input on $\{0, 1\}$, the joint law of input and output is, for all $x, y \in \{0, 1\}$,

$$P(x, y) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \begin{cases} 1 - \delta & \text{if } y = x, \\ \delta & \text{if } y \neq x. \end{cases}$$
