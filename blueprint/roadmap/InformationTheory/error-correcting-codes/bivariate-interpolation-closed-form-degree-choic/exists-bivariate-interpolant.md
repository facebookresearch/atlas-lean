# Existence of a bivariate interpolant

Over a field $F$, for any $n$ points $(\alpha_i, y_i) \in F^2$ and every $k \ge 1$, there is a nonzero polynomial $Q$ in two variables of $(1, k - 1)$-weighted degree at most $n$ (every monomial $X^a Y^b$ of $Q$ has $a + (k - 1) b \le n$) with $Q(\alpha_i, y_i) = 0$ for every $i$. The statement holds in both representations of two-variable polynomials, as a polynomial in $Y$ with coefficients in $F[X]$ and as a multivariate polynomial in $X, Y$, which correspond under the standard algebra isomorphism.
