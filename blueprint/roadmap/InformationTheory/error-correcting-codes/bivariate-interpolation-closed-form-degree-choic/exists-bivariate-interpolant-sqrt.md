# Exists bivariate interpolant sqrt

Over a field, for any `n` points `(α_i, y_i)` and `k ≥ 2`, there is a nonzero bivariate polynomial `Q` of `(1, k - 1)`-weighted degree at most `⌈√(2 (k - 1) n)⌉` with `Q(α_i, y_i) = 0` for every `i`. This is the multiplicity-one case of the degree choice in V. Guruswami and M. Sudan, [*Improved Decoding of Reed–Solomon and Algebraic-Geometry Codes*](https://people.csail.mit.edu/madhu/papers/1998/venkat-journ.pdf), Lemma 6 and its proof, with weight $k - 1$; for $n = 0$ the statement is trivial.
