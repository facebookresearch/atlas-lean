# Probability of error weight at least k on a binary symmetric channel

For $n, k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $0 \le \delta \le 1$, the [binary symmetric channel law](bsc-measure.md) on $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ gives the error words of Hamming weight at least $k$ probability $\sum_{i=k}^{n} \binom{n}{i} \delta^i (1 - \delta)^{n-i}$.
