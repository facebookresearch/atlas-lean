# Number of multiplicity constraints

There are $r(r+1)/2 = \binom{r+1}{2}$ [monomials of total degree less than $r$](low-monomials.md):
$s + 1$ of each total degree $s < r$. So a root of multiplicity $r$ at one point imposes
$\binom{r+1}{2}$ linear constraints on the coefficients of $Q$.
