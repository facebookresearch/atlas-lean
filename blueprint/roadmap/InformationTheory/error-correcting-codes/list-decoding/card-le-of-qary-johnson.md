# Johnson bound, list size (q − 1) n d

Let $\alpha$ be a finite alphabet with $q = |\alpha| \ge 2$ letters, let $n, d, e$ be natural numbers with $q e \le (q-1) n$, let $y \in \alpha^n$, and let $S \subseteq \alpha^n$ be a finite set of words whose distinct elements are at Hamming distance at least $d$ and which all lie within Hamming distance $e$ of $y$. If, in the integers,

$$(q-1)\,n\,(n-d) < (q-1)(n-e)^2 + e^2$$

then $|S| \le (q-1)\,n\,d$.
