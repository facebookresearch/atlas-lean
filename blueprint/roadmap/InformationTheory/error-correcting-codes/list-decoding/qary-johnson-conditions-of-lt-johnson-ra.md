# Below the Johnson radius the integer Johnson conditions hold

Let $J(q, \delta) = (1 - 1/q)\bigl(1 - \sqrt{\max(0,\, 1 - q\delta/(q-1))}\bigr)$ be the total $q$-ary Johnson radius, computed in $\mathbb{R}$ with $x/0 = 0$. Let $q, n, d, e$ be natural numbers with $q \ge 2$ and $e < J(q, d/n) \cdot n$ (with $d/n$ the real quotient, $0$ when $n = 0$). Then $q e < (q-1) n$ and, in the integers,

$$(q-1)\,n\,(n-d) < (q-1)(n-e)^2 + e^2$$
