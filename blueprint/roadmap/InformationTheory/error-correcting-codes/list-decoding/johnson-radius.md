# q-ary Johnson radius

For a natural number $q$ and a real number $\delta$, the (total) $q$-ary Johnson radius is the real number

$$J(q, \delta) = \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{q}\Bigr)\Bigl(1 - \sqrt{\max\bigl(0,\ 1 - \tfrac{q\delta}{q-1}\bigr)}\Bigr),$$

computed in $\mathbb{R}$ with division by $0$ giving $0$. For $q \ge 2$ and $0 \le \delta \le (q-1)/q$ it is the usual Johnson radius $J_q(\delta) = (1 - 1/q)(1 - \sqrt{1 - q\delta/(q-1)})$; for $q \ge 2$ and $\delta \ge (q-1)/q$ it equals $1 - 1/q$. Outside $q \ge 2$, $\delta \ge 0$ the same formula defines the value, without a coding interpretation.
