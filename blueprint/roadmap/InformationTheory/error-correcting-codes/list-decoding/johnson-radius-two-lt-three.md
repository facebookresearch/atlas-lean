# Binary Johnson radius at relative distance 2/n is below 3

Let $J(q, \delta) = (1 - 1/q)\bigl(1 - \sqrt{\max(0,\, 1 - q\delta/(q-1))}\bigr)$ be the total $q$-ary Johnson radius, computed in $\mathbb{R}$ with $x/0 = 0$. For every natural number $n$ (with $2/n$ read as $0$ when $n = 0$),

$$J(2, 2/n) \cdot n < 3.$$
