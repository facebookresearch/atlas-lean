# Translation of the plane

For $a, b \in F$, the $F$-algebra automorphism $Q(X, Y) \mapsto Q(X + a, Y + b)$ of $F[X][Y]$. A
root of $Q$ at $(a, b)$ becomes a root of the translate at the origin, which is how Guruswami,
Rudra and Sudan define [multiplicity](has-root-mult.md) at a general point (Definition 12.2.9).

## Sources

- V. Guruswami, A. Rudra, M. Sudan, *Essential Coding Theory*, draft of August 26, 2025, Definition 12.2.9.
