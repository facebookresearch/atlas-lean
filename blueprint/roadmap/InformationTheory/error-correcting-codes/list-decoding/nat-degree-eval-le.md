# Substitution respects the weighted degree

If $Q(X, Y)$ has [$(1, w)$-weighted degree](weighted-deg-le.md) at most $D$ and $P \in F[X]$ has
degree at most $w$, then $Q(X, P(X))$ has degree at most $D$: each monomial $X^i Y^j$ becomes
$X^i P(X)^j$, of degree at most $i + w j$.

## Sources

- V. Guruswami, A. Rudra, M. Sudan, *Essential Coding Theory*, draft of August 26, 2025, Lemma 12.2.6.
