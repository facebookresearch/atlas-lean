# Column permutations of Gallager's ensemble

Given permutations $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{j-1}$ of the $n$ columns, block $0$ of Gallager's matrix
uses the identity and block $t \ge 1$ uses $\sigma_t$; $\sigma_0$ is ignored.

## Sources

- R. G. Gallager, *Low-Density Parity-Check Codes*, MIT Press, 1963, Section 2.2 (p. 13 of the author's retypeset PDF, web.mit.edu/gallager/www/pages/ldpc.pdf).
