# Dimension of a code given by parity checks

The [code of a parity-check matrix](../linear-codes/of-parity-check.md) with $m$ rows and $n$ columns has
dimension at least $n - m$ (truncated subtraction): its dimension is $n$ minus the rank of the
matrix, and the rank is at most $m$.

## Background

- [Code with a given parity-check matrix](../linear-codes/of-parity-check.md): the code is the kernel of
  $c \mapsto Hc$, of dimension $n - \operatorname{rank} H$.
