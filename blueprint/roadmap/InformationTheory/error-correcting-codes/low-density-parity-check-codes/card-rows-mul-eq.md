# Number of rows of a regular parity-check matrix

If $H$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns and is $(j, k)$-regular, then $m k = n j$. So, when $k > 0$, an
$(n, j, k)$ parity-check matrix has $n j / k$ rows, as Gallager notes after his definition.

## Informally uses

- [Row and column weights have the same total](sum-row-weight-eq-sum-col-weight.md): the total is
  $mk$ counted by rows and $nj$ counted by columns.

## Sources

- R. G. Gallager, *Low-Density Parity-Check Codes*, MIT Press, 1963, Section 2.2 (p. 13 of the author's retypeset PDF, web.mit.edu/gallager/www/pages/ldpc.pdf).
