# Mutual information of a finite joint law

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite sets, each with the discrete σ-algebra, and $p$ a probability mass function on
$\alpha \times \beta$, the joint law of $(X, Y)$. The mutual information $I(X; Y)$, in bits, is the Kullback–Leibler
divergence of $p$ from the product of its marginals divided by $\log 2$, taken as a real number; it is finite
here ([mutual information in terms of entropies](finite-mutual-information-eq.md)).
