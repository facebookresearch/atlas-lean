# Nonnegativity of the Chow–Liu approximation

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of variables, $X = (X_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a random vector with values in $\prod_i \alpha_i$ and joint law $P$ (a probability mass function), $\tau$ a dependence forest with parent map $j$, and $P_\tau$ the Chow–Liu approximation of $P$ along $\tau$.
Then $P_\tau(x) \ge 0$ for every $x$.
