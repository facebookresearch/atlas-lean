# Mutual Info swap

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be finite types and let $p$ be a probability mass function on $\alpha \times \beta$, with marginals $p_X(x) = \sum_{y} p(x, y)$ and $p_Y(y) = \sum_{x} p(x, y)$. Let $\tilde p$ be the law on $\beta \times \alpha$ obtained by swapping coordinates, $\tilde p(y, x) = p(x, y)$. Then

$$I_{\mathrm{nats}}(\tilde p) = I_{\mathrm{nats}}(p).$$
