# Gauss Entropy

Let $v > 0$ and let $\varphi_v(x) = (2\pi v)^{-1/2} e^{-x^2/(2v)}$ be the density of the centered Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(0, v)$. Its differential entropy in nats is

$$h(v) = -\int_{\mathbb{R}} \varphi_v(x) \ln \varphi_v(x) \, dx,$$

the integral taken against Lebesgue measure. It is defined only for $v \neq 0$; the degenerate case $v = 0$, a point mass, has no such finite integral and is excluded.
