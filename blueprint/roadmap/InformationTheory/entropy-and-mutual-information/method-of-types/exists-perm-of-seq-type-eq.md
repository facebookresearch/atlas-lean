# Sequences of the same type are rearrangements

Let $\alpha$ be a set of letters. Two sequences $x, y \in \alpha^n$ with the same type (the same multiset of
letters) differ by a permutation of positions: $y \circ \sigma = x$ for some permutation $\sigma$ of
$\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ (Csiszár, "The method of types", IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 44 (1998), Section II).
