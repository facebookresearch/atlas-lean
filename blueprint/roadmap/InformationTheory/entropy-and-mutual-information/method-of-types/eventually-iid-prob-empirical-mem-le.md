# Sanov's upper bound, asymptotic form

Under the hypotheses of [Sanov's non-asymptotic upper bound](iid-prob-empirical-mem-le.md), for
every $\varepsilon > 0$, eventually

$$Q^n\{x : P_x \in \Gamma\} \le e^{-n(r - \varepsilon)}.$$

This is (III.20), $\limsup_n \frac1n \log Q^n\{P_x \in \Gamma\} \le -D(\Gamma \| Q)$, for every set
$\Gamma$, stated in exponential form, which needs no convention for $\log 0$ when the event is empty.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, Theorem III.3, (III.20).
