# Lower bound on the probability of a type class

For $n \ge 1$, an $n$-type with empirical distribution $P$, and $Q$ with $Q(a) > 0$ wherever
$P(a) > 0$, with the natural logarithm,

$$e^{-nD(P\|Q)} \le |\mathcal P_n| \cdot Q^n(T_P).$$

This is the left half of (II.5). The support hypothesis is the source's finiteness of
$D(P \| Q)$; when it fails, $Q^n(T_P) = 0$ and the source's left side is $e^{-\infty} = 0$. The
polynomial form is [the lower bound in polynomial form](exp-le-pow-mul-iid-prob-type-class.md).
