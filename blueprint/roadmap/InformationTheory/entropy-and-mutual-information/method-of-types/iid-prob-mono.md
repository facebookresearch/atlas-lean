# Monotonicity of the product law

Let $\alpha$ be a set of letters and, for $Q : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$ and a finite set $S \subseteq \alpha^n$,
$Q^n(S) = \sum_{x \in S} \prod_{i<n} Q(x_i)$. If $Q \ge 0$ and $S \subseteq T$, then $Q^n(S) \le Q^n(T)$ (Csiszár, "The method of types", IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 44 (1998), Section II).
