# The Sanov event as a union of type classes

For $Q : \alpha \to \mathbb R$ and any set $\Gamma$ of functions $\alpha \to \mathbb R$,

$$Q^n\{x : P_x \in \Gamma\} = \sum_{s \,:\, P_s \in \Gamma} Q^n(T_s),$$

the sum running over the $n$-types whose [empirical distribution](empirical.md) lies in $\Gamma$.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, equation (III.24).
