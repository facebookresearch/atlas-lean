# Total mass of the product law

Let $\alpha$ be a finite alphabet and $Q : \alpha \to \mathbb{R}$. Then
$\sum_{x \in \alpha^n} \prod_{i<n} Q(x_i) = \bigl(\sum_{a} Q(a)\bigr)^n$, which is $1$ when $Q$ is a probability
vector (Csiszár, "The method of types", IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 44 (1998), Section II).
