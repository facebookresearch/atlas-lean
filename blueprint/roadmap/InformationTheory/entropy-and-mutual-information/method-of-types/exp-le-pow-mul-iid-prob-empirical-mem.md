# Sanov's lower bound, non-asymptotic

If the empirical distribution $P$ of an $n$-type ($n \ge 1$) lies in $\Gamma$, $Q \ge 0$, and
$Q(a) > 0$ wherever $P(a) > 0$, then

$$e^{-nD(P\|Q)} \le (n+1)^{|\alpha|}\; Q^n\{x : P_x \in \Gamma\}.$$

Optimising over the types in $\Gamma$ gives the left half of Csiszár's (III.25), with
$(n+1)^{|\alpha|}$ in place of $|\mathcal P_n|$.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, (III.25) and Lemma II.1.
