# Number of types

There are at most $(n+1)^{|\alpha|}$ types of sequences of length $n$ over a finite alphabet $\alpha$: a type is
determined by its letter counts, each in $\{0, \dots, n\}$.

Csiszár's Lemma II.1 gives the exact count
$|\mathcal P_n(\mathcal X)| = \binom{n+|\mathcal X|-1}{|\mathcal X|-1}$; the polynomial bound is the form used
in every exponential estimate below.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, Lemma II.1 (exact count).
- I. Csiszár and P. C. Shields, *Information Theory and Statistics: A Tutorial*, Foundations and Trends in Communications and Information Theory 1(4) (2004), 417–528, Lemma 2.1 (exact count).
