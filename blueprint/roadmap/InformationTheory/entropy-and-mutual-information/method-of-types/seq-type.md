# Type of a sequence

The **type** of a sequence $x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ over a finite alphabet $\alpha$ records how often each
letter occurs: it is the multiset of the letters of $x$, a multiset of size $n$. The $n$-types are therefore
exactly the multisets of size $n$. Dividing the counts by $n$ gives the empirical distribution $P_x$ of
Csiszár's paper ([empirical distribution of a type](empirical.md)).

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, Section II (definition of the type $P_{\mathbf x}$ and of $\mathcal P_n(\mathcal X)$).
