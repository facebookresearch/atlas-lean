# The number of types is subexponential

For every $\varepsilon > 0$, eventually $(n+1)^{|\alpha|} \le e^{n\varepsilon}$. This is the step that
makes the polynomial factors of the [number of types](card-sym-le-pow.md) negligible on the
exponential scale, since $\log(n+1)/n \to 0$.

## Sources

- I. Csiszár, "The method of types", *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 44(6) (1998), 2505–2523, Section II (the notation $\lesssim$, inequality up to a polynomial factor, introduced after Lemma II.2), as used in the proof of Theorem III.2.
