# Conditional entropy of the output given input and auxiliary variable

For a probability
measure $\nu$ on $(X \times Y) \times S$, $H(Y \mid X, S)$ is the conditional Shannon entropy under
$\nu$, in bits and with values in $[0, \infty]$, of the coordinate $((x, y), s) \mapsto y$ given
the sigma-algebra generated by the map $((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, s)$ into $X \times S$.
