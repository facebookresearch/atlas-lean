# Scalar AWGN capacity bound

Let $X$ and $Z$ be measurable real random variables on a probability space, independent, with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, N)$ for some $N > 0$, and let $P \ge 0$ with $\mathbb{E}[X^2] \le P$; the law and mean of $X$ are otherwise arbitrary. Then the mutual information of $X$ and $X + Z$, in bits, satisfies

$$I(X; X + Z) \le \frac{\log(1 + P/N)}{2 \log 2}.$$
