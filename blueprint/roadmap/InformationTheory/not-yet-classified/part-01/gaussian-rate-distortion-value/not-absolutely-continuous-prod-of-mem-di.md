# Zero-distortion laws are singular with respect to the product of marginals

Let $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ and $D \in \mathbb{R}$. The feasible set $\mathcal{F}(\sigma^2, D)$ consists of the probability measures $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $D \ge 0$, first marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, and $\int (x - \hat x)^2 \, d\mu(x, \hat x) \le D$ (a lower integral in $[0, \infty]$).

Let $\sigma^2 > 0$ and $\mu \in \mathcal{F}(\sigma^2, 0)$, with marginals $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$. Then $\mu$ is not absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu_1 \otimes \mu_2$.
