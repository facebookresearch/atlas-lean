# Binary symmetric channel law

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\delta \in [0, 1]$, the binary symmetric channel law is the probability measure on error words $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ given by the product of $n$ independent Bernoulli($\delta$) flips: in each coordinate the value $1$ has probability $\delta$ and the value $0$ has probability $1 - \delta$. The hypotheses $0 \le \delta$ and $\delta \le 1$ are arguments of the definition.
