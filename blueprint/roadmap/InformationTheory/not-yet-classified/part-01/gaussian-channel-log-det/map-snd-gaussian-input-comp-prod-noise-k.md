# Output law of a linear Gaussian channel with Gaussian input

Let $\iota, \kappa$ be finite index sets, $H$ a real $\iota \times \kappa$ matrix, $K$ a positive semidefinite $\kappa \times \kappa$ matrix and $R$ a positive semidefinite $\iota \times \iota$ matrix (both possibly singular). Let the channel kernel send $x$ to the law of $Hx + Z$ with $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$. Then the second marginal of the joint law $\mathcal{N}(0, K) \otimes$ (kernel) is

$$\mathcal{N}(0,\; H K H^\top + R).$$
