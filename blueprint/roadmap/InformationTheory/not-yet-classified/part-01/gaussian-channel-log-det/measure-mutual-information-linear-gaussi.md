# Mutual information of the Gaussian-input linear Gaussian channel

Let $\iota, \kappa$ be finite index sets with $\iota$ nonempty, $H$ a real $\iota \times \kappa$ matrix, $K$ positive semidefinite (possibly singular) and $R$ positive definite. On a probability space, let $X$ and $Z$ be measurable, independent, with $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, K)$ and $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R)$, and let $Y = HX + Z$. Then the mutual information of the joint law of $(X, Y)$, in bits, is

$$I(X; Y) = \frac{1}{2 \ln 2}\, \ln \frac{\det(R + H K H^\top)}{\det R}.$$
