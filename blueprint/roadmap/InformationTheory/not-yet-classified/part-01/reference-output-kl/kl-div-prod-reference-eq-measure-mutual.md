# Reference-output decomposition of mutual information

Let $X$ be a nonempty standard Borel space and $Y$ a measurable space, $\mu$ a probability measure on $X \times Y$ with marginals $\mu_X$, $\mu_Y$, and $Q$ any probability measure on $Y$. Then, as an identity in $[0, \infty]$,

$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \,\|\, \mu_X \otimes Q) = \ln 2 \cdot I(\mu) + D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu_Y \,\|\, Q),$$

where $I(\mu) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu \,\|\, \mu_X \otimes \mu_Y)/\ln 2$ is the mutual information in bits and $D_{\mathrm{KL}}$ is in nats.
