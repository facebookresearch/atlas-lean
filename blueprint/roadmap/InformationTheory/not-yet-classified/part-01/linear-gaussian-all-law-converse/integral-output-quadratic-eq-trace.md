# Expected output quadratic form equals a trace

Let $\kappa$ and $\iota$ be finite index sets, $H \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \kappa}$ and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$. Let $\rho$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^\kappa$ with finite second moment, with mean $m = \int x \, d\rho(x)$ and covariance matrix $K_{ab} = \operatorname{Cov}_\rho(x_a, x_b)$. Then

$$\int (Hx - Hm)^{\mathsf T} A\, (Hx - Hm)\, d\rho(x) = \operatorname{tr}\!\left(A\, H K H^{\mathsf T}\right).$$
