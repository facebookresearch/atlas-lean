# Theorem 24.21 (Nagell-Lutz)

Let $P=(x_{0}: y_{0}: 1)$ be an affine point of finite order on the elliptic curve $y^{2}=x^{3}+a_{4}x+a_{6}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, with $a_{4}, a_{6} \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then $x_{0}, y_{0} \in \mathbb{Z}$, and if $y_{0} \neq 0$ then $y_{0}^{2}$ divides $4a_{4}^{3}+27a_{6}^{2}$.
