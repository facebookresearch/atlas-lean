# Definition 24.13

The infinite chain of groups $E^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) \supset E^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) \supset E^{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) \supset \cdots$ is called the $p$-adic filtration of $E / \mathbb{Q}$.
