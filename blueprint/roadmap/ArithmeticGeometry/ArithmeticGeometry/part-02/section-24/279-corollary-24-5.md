# Corollary 24.5

For any isogeny $\varphi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ of elliptic curves, $\# \operatorname{ker} \varphi$ divides $\operatorname{deg} \varphi$. In particular, the kernel of an isogeny is finite.
