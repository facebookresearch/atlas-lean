# Definition 24.1

Let $E_{1} / k$ and $E_{2} / k$ be elliptic curves with distinguished rational points $O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$, respectively. An isogeny $\varphi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ of elliptic curves is a surjective morphism that maps $O_{1}$ to $O_{2}$.
