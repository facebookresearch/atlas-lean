# Lemma 24.17

Suppose $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are colinear points in $E^{n}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})$, for some $n>0$, with $P_{i} = (x_{i}: 1: z_{i})$. Then $v_{p}(x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}) \geq 5n$.
