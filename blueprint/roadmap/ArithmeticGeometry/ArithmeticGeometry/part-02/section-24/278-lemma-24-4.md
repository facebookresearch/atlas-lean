# Lemma 24.4

Let $\varphi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ be an isogeny of elliptic curves. For each $P \in \operatorname{ker} \varphi$, the automorphism $\tau_{P}^{*}$ fixes $\varphi^{*}(\bar{k}(E_{2}))$, and the map $\operatorname{ker} \varphi \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(\bar{k}(E_{1}) / \varphi^{*}(\bar{k}(E_{2})))$ defined by $P \mapsto \tau_{P}^{*}$ is an injective group homomorphism.
