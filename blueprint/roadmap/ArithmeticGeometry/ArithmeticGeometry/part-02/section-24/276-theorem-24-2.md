# Theorem 24.2

Let $E_{1} / k$ and $E_{2} / k$ be elliptic curves and let $\varphi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ be an isogeny defined over $k$. Then $\varphi$ is a group homomorphism from $E_{1}(L)$ to $E_{2}(L)$, for any algebraic extension $L / k$.
