# Theorem 24.14

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve and let $p$ be a prime not dividing $\Delta(E)$. The $p$-adic filtration of $E$ satisfies (1) $E^{0}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) / E^{1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) \simeq \bar{E}(\mathbb{F}_{p})$; (2) $E^{n}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) / E^{n+1}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) \simeq \mathbb{Z} / p\mathbb{Z}$ for all $n>0$; (3) $\cap_{n} E^{n} = \{O\}$.
