# Definition 24.7

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve and let $n$ be a positive integer. The multiplication-by-$n$ map $[n]: E(\bar{k}) \rightarrow E(\bar{k})$ is the group homomorphism defined by $nP = P \oplus P \oplus \cdots \oplus P$. The points $P \in E(\bar{k})$ for which $nP = O$ are called $n$-torsion points. They form a subgroup of $E(\bar{k})$ denoted $E[n]$.
