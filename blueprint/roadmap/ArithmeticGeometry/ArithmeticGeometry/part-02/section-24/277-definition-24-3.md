# Definition 24.3

Let $\varphi: E_{1} \rightarrow E_{2}$ be an isogeny of elliptic curves over $k$. The kernel of $\varphi$, denoted $\operatorname{ker} \varphi$, is the kernel of the group homomorphism $\varphi: E_{1}(\bar{k}) \rightarrow E_{2}(\bar{k})$.
