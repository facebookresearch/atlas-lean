# Corollary 24.19

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve and let $p$ be a prime of good reduction. The torsion subgroup of $E(\mathbb{Q})$ injects into $\bar{E}(\mathbb{F}_{p})$. In particular, the torsion subgroup is finite.
