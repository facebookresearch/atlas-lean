# Corollary 18.4

Let $R$ be a local Noetherian ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$. Then $t_1, \ldots, t_n \in \mathfrak{m}$ generate $\mathfrak{m}$ if and only if their images generate $\mathfrak{m} / \mathfrak{m}^2$ as an $R / \mathfrak{m}$ vector space.
