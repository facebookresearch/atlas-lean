# Definition 18.1

A Noetherian local ring $R$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ is a regular local ring if $\dim \mathfrak{m} / \mathfrak{m}^2 = \dim R$ (note that Noetherian is included in the definition of regular).
