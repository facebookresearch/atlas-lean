# Theorem 18.5

Let $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ be valuation rings with the same fraction field, let $\mathfrak{m}_{1}$ and $\mathfrak{m}_{2}$ be their respective maximal ideals, and suppose $R_{1} \subsetneq R_{2}$. Then $\mathfrak{m}_{2} \subsetneq \mathfrak{m}_{1}$ and $\operatorname{dim} R_{2}\lt \operatorname{dim} R_{1}$. In particular, $R_{1}$ cannot be a discrete valuation ring.
