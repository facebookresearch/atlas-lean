# Lemma 18.3 (Nakayama)

Let $R$ be a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ and suppose that $M$ is a finitely generated $R$-module with the property $M = \mathfrak{m}M$. Then $M$ is the zero module.
