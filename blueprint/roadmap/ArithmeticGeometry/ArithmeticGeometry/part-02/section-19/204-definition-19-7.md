# Definition 19.7

Let $F/k$ be a function field with $k=\bar{k}$. A divisor of $F$ is a formal sum $D \coloneqq \sum_{P \in X_F} n_P P$ with $n_P \in \mathbb{Z}$ and all but finitely many $n_P = 0$. Here $X_F$ denotes the abstract curve defined by $F/k$, whose points $P$ are the maximal ideals of the discrete valuation rings of $F/k$. The divisors of $F$ form a free abelian group $\operatorname{Div} F$ under addition; if $C$ is the smooth projective curve with function field $F$, this group is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Div} C$ and we may use them interchangeably.
