# Definition 19.8

A divisor $D = \sum n_P P \in \operatorname{Div} C$ is defined over $k$ if for all $\sigma \in G_k$ we have $D^\sigma = D$, where $D^\sigma = (\sum n_P P) = \sum n_P P^\sigma$. The subset of $\operatorname{Div} C$ defined over $k$ forms the subgroup of $k$-rational divisors.
