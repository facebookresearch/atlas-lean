# Definition 19.15

For a principal divisor $\operatorname{div} f = \sum n_P P$, the divisors $\operatorname{div}_0 f = \sum_{n_P > 0} n_P P$ and $\operatorname{div}_{\infty} f = \sum_{n_P < 0} -n_P P$ are called the divisor of zeros and the divisor of poles of $f$, respectively. We have $\operatorname{div} f = \operatorname{div}_0 f - \operatorname{div}_{\infty} f$.
