# Definition 19.9

Let $C/k$ be a curve defined over $k$. The $G_k$-orbits of $C(\bar{k})$ are called closed points, which we also denote by $P$. A rational divisor of $C/k$ is a formal sum $D \coloneqq \sum n_P P$ where $P$ ranges over the closed points of $C/k$, with $n_P \in \mathbb{Z}$ and all but finitely many $n_P = 0$. The group of rational divisors on $C$ is denoted $\operatorname{Div}_k C$.
