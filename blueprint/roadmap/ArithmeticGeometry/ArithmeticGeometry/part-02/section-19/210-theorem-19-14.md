# Theorem 19.14

We have an exact sequence $1 \rightarrow k^{\times} \rightarrow k(C)^{\times} \xrightarrow{\operatorname{div}} \operatorname{Div}_k C \rightarrow \operatorname{Pic}_k C \rightarrow 0$.
