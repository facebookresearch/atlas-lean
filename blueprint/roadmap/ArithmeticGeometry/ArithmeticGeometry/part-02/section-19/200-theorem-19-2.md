# Theorem 19.2

The category of curves $C/k$ with nonconstant morphisms and the category of function fields $F/k$ with field homomorphisms that fix $k$ are contravariantly equivalent under the functor that sends a curve $C$ to the function field $k(C)$ and a nonconstant morphism of curves $\phi: C_{1} \rightarrow C_{2}$ defined over $k$ to the field homomorphism $\phi^{*}: k(C_{2}) \rightarrow k(C_{1})$ defined by $\phi^{*} f=f \circ \phi$.
