# Definition 21.1

A divisor $D \geq 0$ is said to be effective. As with principal divisors $\operatorname{div} f = \operatorname{div}_{0} f - \operatorname{div}_{\infty} f$, every divisor can be written uniquely as the difference of two effective divisors, as $D=D_{0}-D_{\infty}$, where $D_{0}\coloneqq \sum_{\operatorname{ord}_{P}(D)>0} \operatorname{ord}_{P}(D) P$ and $D_{\infty}\coloneqq \sum_{\operatorname{ord}_{P}(D)<0}-\operatorname{ord}_{P}(D) P$.
