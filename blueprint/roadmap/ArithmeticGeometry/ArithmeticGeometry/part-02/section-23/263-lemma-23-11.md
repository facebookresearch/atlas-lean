# Lemma 23.11

Let $C/k$ be a curve defined by a Weierstrass equation $y^{2} + a_{1}xy + a_{3}y = x^{3} + a_{2}x^{2} + a_{4}x + a_{6}$. If the characteristic of $k$ is not 2 (resp. not 2 or 3) then $a_{1}$ and $a_{3}$ (resp. $a_{1}$, $a_{2}$, and $a_{3}$) can be made zero via a linear change of coordinates.
