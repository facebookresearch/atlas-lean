# Corollary 23.19 (Geometric group law)

Let $P$ and $Q$ be rational points on an elliptic curve embedded in $\mathbb{P}^{2}$. Then $P \oplus Q$ is the negation of the third point in the intersection of $E$ and the line uniquely determined by $P$ and $Q$.
