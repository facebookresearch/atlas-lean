# Definition 22.16

For a nonzero Weil differential $\omega \in \Omega$ we define the divisor of $\omega$ to be the unique divisor $\operatorname{div} \omega \coloneqq D_{\omega}$ given by Lemma 22.15. A divisor $D$ is said to be canonical if $D = \operatorname{div} \omega$ for some $\omega \in \Omega$. We also define $\operatorname{ord}_{P}(\omega)\coloneqq \operatorname{ord}_{P}(\operatorname{div} \omega)$.
