# Definition 22.12

Let $F/k$ be a function field and let $\mathcal{A}$ be its adele ring. For a divisor $D \in \operatorname{Div}_{k} F$ the space of Weil differentials $\Omega(D)$ is the orthogonal complement of $\mathcal{A}(D)+F$ (its annihilator in the dual space $\mathcal{A}^{\vee}$). Explicitly, this is the set of all linear functionals $\omega: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow k$ that vanish on $\mathcal{A}(D)+F$. The $k$-vector space $\Omega=\Omega_{F}\coloneqq \cup_{D \in \operatorname{Div}_{k} F} \Omega(D)$ is the space of Weil differentials for $F/k$.
