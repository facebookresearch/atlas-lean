# Definition 22.4

Let $C/k$ be a curve of genus $g$. For $D \in \operatorname{Div}_{k} C$, the non-negative integer $i(D)\coloneqq g-1-r(D)$ is the index of speciality of $D$. Divisors for which $i(D)>0$ are said to be special.
