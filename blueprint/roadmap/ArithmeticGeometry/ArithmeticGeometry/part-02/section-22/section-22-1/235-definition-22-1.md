# Definition 22.1

The genus of the curve $C/k$ is defined by $g\coloneqq \max\{r(D)+1: D \in \operatorname{Div}_{k}(C)\}$. In other words, $g$ is the least integer for which $r(D) \leq g-1$ holds for all $D \in \operatorname{Div}_k C$.
