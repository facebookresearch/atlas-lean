# Theorem 22.21 (Riemann-Roch Theorem)

Let $W$ be a canonical divisor of the genus $g$ curve $C/k$. For every divisor $D$ we have $\ell(D) = \operatorname{deg}(D) + 1 - g + \ell(W - D)$.
