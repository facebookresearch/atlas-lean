# Corollary 22.24

For a divisor $D$ of a genus $g$ curve, the following are equivalent: (a) $D$ is a canonical divisor. (b) $\ell(D) = g$ and $\operatorname{deg} D = 2g - 2$. (c) $i(D) = 1$ and $\operatorname{deg} D$ is maximal among divisors with $i(D) = 1$.
