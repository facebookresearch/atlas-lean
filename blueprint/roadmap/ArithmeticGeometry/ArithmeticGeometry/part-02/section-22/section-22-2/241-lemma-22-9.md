# Lemma 22.9

For any two divisors $A \leq B$ we have $\mathcal{A}(A)+F \subseteq \mathcal{A}(B)+F$ and $\operatorname{dim} \frac{\mathcal{A}(B)+F}{\mathcal{A}(A)+F}=r(B)-r(A)$, as $k$-vector spaces, where $F$ is embedded diagonally in $\mathcal{A}$.
