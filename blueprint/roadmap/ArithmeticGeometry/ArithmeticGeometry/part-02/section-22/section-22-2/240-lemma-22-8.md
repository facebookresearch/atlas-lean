# Lemma 22.8

For any two divisors $A \leq B$ we have $\mathcal{A}(A) \subseteq \mathcal{A}(B)$ and $\operatorname{dim} \mathcal{A}(B) / \mathcal{A}(A)=\operatorname{deg} B-\operatorname{deg}(A)$, as $k$-vector spaces.
