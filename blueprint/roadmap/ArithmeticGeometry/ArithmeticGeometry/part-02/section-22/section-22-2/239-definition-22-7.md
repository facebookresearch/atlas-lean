# Definition 22.7

For a divisor $D$ the adele space of $D$ is the $k$-vector space $\mathcal{A}(D)\coloneqq \{\alpha \in \mathcal{A}: \operatorname{ord}_{P}(\alpha) \geq -\operatorname{ord}_{P}(D) \text{ for all places } P\}$. It contains the Riemann-Roch space $\mathcal{L}(D)=\mathcal{A}(D) \cap F$ as a subspace, and it is in turn a subspace of the adele ring $\mathcal{A}$.
