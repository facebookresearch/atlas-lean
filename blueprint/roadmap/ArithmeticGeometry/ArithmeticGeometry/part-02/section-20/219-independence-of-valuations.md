# Lemma 20.3 (Independence of valuations)

Let $P_{1}, \ldots, P_{n}$ be distinct places of a function field $F$. Then there exist $t_{1}, \ldots, t_{n}$ so that $v_{i}(t_{j})=\delta_{ij}$ (Kronecker delta), where $v_{i}$ denotes the valuation for $P_{i}$.
