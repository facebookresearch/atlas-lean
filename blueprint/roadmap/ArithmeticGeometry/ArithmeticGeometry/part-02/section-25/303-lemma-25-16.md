# Lemma 25.16

Let $k/\mathbb{Q}$ be a finite Galois extension. Then $h(P^{\sigma})=h(P)$ for all $P \in \mathbb{P}^{n}(k)$ and $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(k/\mathbb{Q})$.
