# Theorem 25.22 (Parallelogram Law)

Let $\hat{h}$ be the canonical height function of an elliptic curve $E$ over a number field $k$. Then for all $P, Q \in E(\bar{k})$ we have $\hat{h}(P+Q)+\hat{h}(P-Q)=2\hat{h}(P)+2\hat{h}(Q)$.
