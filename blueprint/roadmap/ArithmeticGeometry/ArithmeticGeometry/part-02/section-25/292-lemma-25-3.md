# Lemma 25.3

An affine point $(X,Y) \in E'(\mathbb{Q})$ lies in the image of $\varphi$ if and only if either $X \in \mathbb{Q}^{\times 2}$, or $X = 0$ and $a^2 - 4b \in \mathbb{Q}^{\times 2}$.
