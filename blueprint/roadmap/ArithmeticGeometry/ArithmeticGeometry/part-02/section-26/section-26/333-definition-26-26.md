# Definition 26.26

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. The Tate-Shafarevich group $\amalg(E)$ is the kernel of the map $$\mathrm{WC}(E/k) \rightarrow \prod_{p} \mathrm{WC}(E_{p}/k_{p})$$ where $k_{p}$ ranges over the completions of $k$ and $E_{p}$ denotes the base extension of $E$ to $k_{p}$.
