# Definition 26.21

Crossed homomorphisms of the form $\sigma \mapsto P^{\sigma}-P$ with $P \in E(\bar{k})$ are principal. The principal crossed homomorphisms form a subgroup, as do the continuous principal crossed homomorphisms.
