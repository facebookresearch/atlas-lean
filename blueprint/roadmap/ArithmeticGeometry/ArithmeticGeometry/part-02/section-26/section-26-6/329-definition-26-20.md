# Definition 26.20

A map $\alpha: \operatorname{Gal}(\bar{k}/k) \rightarrow E(\bar{k})$ that satisfies $\alpha(\tau \sigma)=\alpha(\tau)+\alpha(\sigma)^{\tau}$ for all $\sigma, \tau \in \operatorname{Gal}(\bar{k}/k)$ is called a crossed homomorphism.
