# Definition 26.22

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. The group of continuous crossed homomorphisms of $E/k$ modulo its subgroup of principal crossed homomorphisms is the first Galois-cohomology group of $E(\bar{k})$. It is denoted by $H^{1}(\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{k}/k), E(\bar{k}))$. For the sake of brevity we may also write $H^{1}(k, E)$.
