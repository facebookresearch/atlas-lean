# Theorem 26.17

Let $C/k$ be an $E$-torsor and let $Q_0 \in C(\bar{k})$. The map $\pi: \operatorname{Div}_{\bar{k}}^{0} C \rightarrow E(\bar{k})$ defined by $\sum_{i} n_{i} P_{i} \mapsto \sum_{i} n(P_{i}-Q_{0})$ is a surjective homomorphism whose kernel consists of the principal divisors, and it is independent of the choice of $Q_0$. Moreover, for any extension $L/k$ in $\bar{k}$ the map $\pi$ commutes with every element of $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{k}/L)$ and therefore induces a canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Pic}_{L}^{0} C \simeq E(L)$.
