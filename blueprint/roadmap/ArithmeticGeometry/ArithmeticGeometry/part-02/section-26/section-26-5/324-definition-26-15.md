# Definition 26.15

Let $E/k$ be an elliptic curve. A principal homogeneous space for $E$ (or $E$-torsor), is a genus one curve $C/k$ such that the set $C(\bar{k})$ is an $E(\bar{k})$-torsor and the map $C \times E \rightarrow C$ defined by $(Q, P) \mapsto Q+P$ is a morphism of varieties that is defined over $k$.
