# Lemma 26.9

Let $E: y^{2}=x^{3}+a_{4} x+a_{6}$ and $E': y^{2}=x^{3}+a_{4}' x+a_{6}'$ be elliptic curves defined over $k$, with $j(E)=j(E')$. Then for some $\lambda \in \bar{k}^{\times}$ we have $a_{4}'=\lambda^{4} a_{4}$ and $a_{6}'=\lambda^{6} a_{6}$. Moreover, the degree of $k(\lambda)/k$ divides $2,4,6$ when $a_{4} a_{6} \neq 0$, $a_{6}=0$, $a_{4}=0$, respectively.
