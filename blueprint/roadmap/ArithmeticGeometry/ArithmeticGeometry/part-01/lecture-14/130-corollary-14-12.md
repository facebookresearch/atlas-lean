# Corollary 14.12

Let $X$ and $Y$ be affine varieties defined over $k$. If $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ is a morphism defined over $k$ then the affine algebra morphism $\varphi^* \colon \bar{k}[Y] \rightarrow \bar{k}[X]$ restricts to an affine algebra morphism from $k[Y]$ to $k[X]$.
