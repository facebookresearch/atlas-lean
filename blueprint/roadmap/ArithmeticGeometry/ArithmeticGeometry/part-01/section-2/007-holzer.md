# Theorem 2.5 (Holzer)

Let $a, b, c$ be square-free integers that are pairwise coprime and suppose that the equation $a x^{2}+b y^{2}+c z^{2}=0$ has a nonzero rational solution. Then there exists a nonzero integer solution $(x_{0}, y_{0}, z_{0})$ with $|x_{0}| \leq \sqrt{|b c|}$, $|y_{0}| \leq \sqrt{|a c|}$, $|z_{0}| \leq \sqrt{|a b|}$.
