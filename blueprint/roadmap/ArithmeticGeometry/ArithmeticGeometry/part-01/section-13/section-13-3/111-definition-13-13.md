# Definition 13.13

A polynomial $f \in \bar{k}[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ is homogeneous of degree $d$ if $f(\lambda x_{0}, \ldots, \lambda x_{n}) = \lambda^{d} f(x_{0}, \ldots, x_{n})$ for all $\lambda \in \bar{k}$. Equivalently, every monomial in $f$ has total degree $d$. We say that $f$ is homogeneous if it is homogeneous of some degree.
