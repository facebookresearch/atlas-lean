# Theorem 13.23

If $V \in \mathbb{A}^{n} \subset \mathbb{P}^{n}$ is an affine variety then its projective closure $\bar{V}$ is a projective variety, and $V=\bar{V} \cap \mathbb{A}^{n}$ is an affine part of $\bar{V}$.
