# Definition 13.19

Let $V$ be a projective variety with homogeneous ideal $I=\left(f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m}\right)$. Let $I_{i}$ be the ideal generated by the dehomogenizations of $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{m}$ at $x_{i}$. Then $I_{i}$ is a prime ideal (since $I$ is) and the $i$th affine part of $V$ is the affine variety $V_{i}=V \cap U_{i}$ whose ideal is $I_{i}$. We can then write $V=\cup_{i} V_{i}$ as the union of its affine parts.
