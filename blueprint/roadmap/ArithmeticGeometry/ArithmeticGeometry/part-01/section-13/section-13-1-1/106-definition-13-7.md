# Definition 13.7

The Krull dimension of a commutative ring $R$ is the supremum of the set of integers $d$ for which there exists a chain of distinct prime $R$-ideals $\mathfrak{p}_{0} \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_{1} \subsetneq \cdots \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_{d}$.
