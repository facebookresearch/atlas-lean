# Definition 5.5

Two absolute values $\|\|$ and $\|\|^{\prime}$ on a field $k$ are said to be equivalent if there is a positive real number $\alpha$ such that $\|x\|^{\prime}=\|x\|^{\alpha}$ for all $x \in k$.
