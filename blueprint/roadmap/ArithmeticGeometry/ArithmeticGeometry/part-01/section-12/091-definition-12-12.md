# Definition 12.12

For an algebraic set $Z \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n}$, the ideal of $Z$ is the set $I(Z)=\{f \in A: f(P)=0 \text{ for all } P \in Z\}$ where $A$ is the polynomial ring $\bar{k}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$.
