# Definition 12.10

A commutative ring $R$ is noetherian if every $R$-ideal is finitely generated. Equivalently, every infinite ascending chain of $R$-ideals $I_{1} \subseteq I_{2} \subseteq \ldots$ eventually stabilizes, that is, $I_{n+1}=I_{n}$ for all sufficiently large $n$.
