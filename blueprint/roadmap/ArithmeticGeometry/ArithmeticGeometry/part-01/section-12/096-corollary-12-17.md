# Corollary 12.17

The maximal ideals of the ring $\bar{k}[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$ are all of the form $m_{P}=(x_{1}-P_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}-P_{n})$ for some point $P=(P_{1}, \ldots, P_{n})$ in $\mathbb{A}^{n}(\bar{k})$.
