# Definition 12.13

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. For any $R$-ideal $I$ we define $\sqrt{I}=\{x \in R: x^{r} \in I \text{ for some integer } r>0\}$ and say that $I$ is a radical ideal if $I=\sqrt{I}$.
