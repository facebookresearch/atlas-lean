# Theorem 3.1 (Gauss)

For all odd primes $p$ and $q$ we have $\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)=(-1)^{\left(\frac{p-1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{q-1}{2}\right)}$.
