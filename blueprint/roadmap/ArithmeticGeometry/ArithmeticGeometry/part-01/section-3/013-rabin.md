# Theorem 3.7 (Rabin)

For every pair of distinct $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{F}_{q}$ we have $$\#\left\{\delta \in \mathbb{F}_{q}: \alpha+\delta \text{ and } \beta+\delta \text{ are of different type}\right\}=\frac{q-1}{2}.$$
