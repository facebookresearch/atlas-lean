# Corollary 3.5

Let $C / \mathbb{F}_{q}$ be a conic over a finite field. Then one of the following holds: 1. $C$ is geometrically irreducible, isomorphic to $\mathbb{P}^{1}$, and has $q+1$ rational points. 2. $C$ is reducible over $\mathbb{F}_{q}$, isomorphic to the union of two rational projective lines, and has $2q+1$ rational points. 3. $C$ is reducible over $\mathbb{F}_{q}^{2}$, but not over $\mathbb{F}_{q}$, isomorphic over $\mathbb{F}_{q}^{2}$ to the union of two projective lines with a single rational point at their intersection. In every case we have $\# C(\mathbb{F}_{q}) \equiv 1 \bmod q$.
