# Definition 9.2

Two quadratic forms $f$ and $g$ are equivalent if $g(v)=f(Tv)$ for some $T \in \mathrm{GL}_{n}(k)$. This defines an equivalence relation on the set of all quadratic forms of the same dimension over the field $k$.
