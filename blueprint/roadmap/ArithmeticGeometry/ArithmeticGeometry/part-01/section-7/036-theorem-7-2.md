# Theorem 7.2

Let $L/K$ be a separable field extension of degree $n$, let $\alpha \in L$ have minimal polynomial $g_\alpha$ over $K$ and let $f_\alpha$ be the characteristic polynomial of $T_\alpha$. Then $f_\alpha = g_\alpha^{n/d}$ where $d = [K(\alpha):K]$.
