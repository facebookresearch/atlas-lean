# Definition 7.13

The completion of $k$ (with respect to $\|\|$) is the field $\hat{k}$ whose elements are equivalence classes of Cauchy sequences in $k$, where (1) $0_{\hat{k}} = [(0_k, 0_k, 0_k, \ldots)]$, (2) $1_{\hat{k}} = [(1_k, 1_k, 1_k, \ldots)]$, (3) $[(x_n)] + [(y_n)] = [(x_n + y_n)]$ and $[(x_n)][(y_n)] = [(x_n y_n)]$.
