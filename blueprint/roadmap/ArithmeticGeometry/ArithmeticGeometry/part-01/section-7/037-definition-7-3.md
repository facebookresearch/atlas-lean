# Definition 7.3

Let $\mathfrak{a}$ be a nonzero $\mathcal{O}$-ideal. The (ideal) norm $N\mathfrak{a}$ of $\mathfrak{a}$ is the cardinality of the (necessarily finite) ring $\mathcal{O}/\mathfrak{a}$, equivalently, the index $[\mathcal{O}:\mathfrak{a}]$ of $\mathfrak{a}$ as a sublattice of the $\mathbb{Z}$-lattice $\mathcal{O}$. The norm of $(0)$ is zero.
