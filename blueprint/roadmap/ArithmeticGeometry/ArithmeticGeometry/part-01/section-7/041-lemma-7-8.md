# Lemma 7.8

Let $(x_n)$ and $(y_n)$ be sequences in $k$ that converge to $x$ and $y$ respectively. Then the sequences $(x_n + y_n)$ and $(x_n y_n)$ converge to $x+y$ and $xy$ respectively.
