# Definition 7.7

A sequence $(x_n)$ of elements of $k$ converges (to $\ell$) if there is an element $\ell \in k$ such that for every $\epsilon > 0$ there is a positive integer $N$ such that $\|x_n - \ell\| < \epsilon$ for all $n \geq N$. Equivalently, $(x_n)$ converges to $\ell$ if $\|x_n - \ell\| \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$.
