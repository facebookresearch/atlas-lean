# Conjecture (Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer)

Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve with rank $r$. Then $L_E(s) = (s-1)^r g(s)$ for some complex analytic function $g(s)$ with $g(1) \neq 0, \infty$. In other words, $r$ is equal to the order of vanishing of $L_E(s)$ at 1.
