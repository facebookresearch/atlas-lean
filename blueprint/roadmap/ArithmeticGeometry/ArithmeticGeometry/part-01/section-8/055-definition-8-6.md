# Definition 8.6

Let $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{d} a_n x^n$ be a polynomial in $R[x]$. The formal derivative $f'$ of $f$ is the polynomial $f'(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{d} n a_n x^{n-1}$ in $R[x]$.
