# Lemma 8.7

Let $a \in R$ and $f \in R[x]$. Then $f(a) = f'(a) = 0$ if and only if $a$ is (at least) a double root of $f$, that is, $f(x) = (x-a)^2 g(x)$ for some $g \in R[x]$.
