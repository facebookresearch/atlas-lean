# Theorem 8.8 (Hensel's lemma)

Let $a \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}$ and $f \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}[x]$. Suppose $f(a) \equiv 0 \bmod p$ and $f'(a) \not\equiv 0 \bmod p$. Then there is a unique $b \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}$ such that $f(b)=0$ and $b \equiv a \bmod p$.
