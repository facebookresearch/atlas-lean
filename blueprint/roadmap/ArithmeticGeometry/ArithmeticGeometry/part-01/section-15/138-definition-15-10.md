# Definition 15.10

Two affine varieties $X$ and $Y$ are said to be birationally equivalent if there exist dominant rational maps $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ and $\psi \colon Y \rightarrow X$ such that $(\varphi \circ \psi)(P) = P$ for all $P \in \operatorname{dom}(\varphi \circ \psi)$ and $(\psi \circ \varphi)(P) = P$ for all $P \in \operatorname{dom}(\psi \circ \varphi)$.
