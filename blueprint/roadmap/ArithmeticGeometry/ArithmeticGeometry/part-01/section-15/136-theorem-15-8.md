# Theorem 15.8

The following hold: (i) Every dominant rational map $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ of affine varieties induces a morphism $\varphi^* \colon \bar{k}(Y) \rightarrow \bar{k}(X)$ of function fields such that $\varphi^*(r) = r \circ \varphi$. (ii) Every morphism $\theta \colon K \rightarrow L$ of function fields induces a dominant rational map of affine varieties $\theta^* \colon X \rightarrow Y$, with $K \cong \bar{k}(Y)$ and $L \cong \bar{k}(X)$, such that the image of $\theta(r)$ in $\bar{k}(X)$ is $r \circ \theta^*$. (iii) If $\varphi \colon X \rightarrow Y$ and $\psi \colon Y \rightarrow Z$ are dominant rational maps of affine varieties then $(\psi \circ \varphi)^* = \varphi^* \circ \psi^*$.
