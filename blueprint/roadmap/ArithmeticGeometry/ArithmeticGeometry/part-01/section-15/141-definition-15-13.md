# Definition 15.13

Let $X$ be a projective variety. We say that $r \in \bar{k}(X)$ is regular at a point $P \in X$ if it has a representation $\hat{r}$ that is defined at $P$. The set of points $P \in X$ at which $r$ is regular form an open subset of $X$ that we denote $\operatorname{dom}(r)$. For any point $P \in \operatorname{dom}(r)$ we define $r(P) = \hat{r}(P)$, where $\hat{r}$ is chosen so that $\hat{r}$ is defined at $P$.
