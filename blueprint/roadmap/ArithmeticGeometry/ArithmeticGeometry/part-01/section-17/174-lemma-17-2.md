# Lemma 17.2

Let $P$ be a point on an affine variety $V$. If $f_1, \ldots, f_m$ generate $I(V)$, then the corresponding affine linear forms $f_{1,P}, \ldots, f_{m,P}$ generate $I(T_P(V))$.
