# Theorem 17.3

Let $P$ be a point on an affine variety $V \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ with ideal $I(V) = (f_1, \ldots, f_m)$. If we identify $\mathbb{A}^n$ with the vector space $k^n$ with origin at $P$, the subspace of $(k^n)^{\vee}$ spanned by the linear forms $f_{1,P}, \ldots, f_{m,P}$ is $T_P(V)^{\perp}$, the orthogonal complement of $T_P(V)$.
