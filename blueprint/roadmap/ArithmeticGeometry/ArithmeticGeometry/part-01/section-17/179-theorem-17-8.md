# Theorem 17.8

Let $F/k$ be a function field of dimension $n$. Then there exist algebraically independent elements $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n \in F$ and an element $\alpha_{n+1}$ algebraic over $k(\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n)$ such that $F = k(\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_{n+1})$.
