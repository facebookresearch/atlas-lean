# Corollary 10.3

For all $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{\times}$, the following hold: (i) $(1, c)_{p}=1$. (ii) $(-c, c)_{p}=1$. (iii) $(a, c)_{p}=1 \Longrightarrow (a, c)_{p}(b, c)_{p}=(a b, c)_{p}$. (iv) $(c, c)_{p}=(-1, c)_{p}$.
