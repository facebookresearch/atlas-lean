# Lemma 10.8

Let $u, v \in \mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times}$. The equations $z^{2}-u x^{2}-v y^{2}=0$ and $z^{2}-2 u x^{2}-v y^{2}=0$ have primitive solutions over $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ if and only if they have primitive solutions modulo 8.
