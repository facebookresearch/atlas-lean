# Theorem 10.9

Write $a, b \in \mathbb{Q}_{2}^{\times}$ as $a=2^{\alpha} u$ and $b=2^{\beta} v$ with $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $u, v \in \mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times}$. Then $(a, b)_{2}=(-1)^{\epsilon(u) \epsilon(v)+\alpha \omega(v)+\beta \omega(u)}$ where $\epsilon(u)$ and $\omega(u)$ denote the images in $\mathbb{Z} / 2 \mathbb{Z}$ of $(u-1) / 2$ and $(u^{2}-1) / 8$, respectively.
