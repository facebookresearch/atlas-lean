# Definition 14.6

We say that two varieties $X \simeq Y$ are isomorphic if there exist morphisms $f: X \rightarrow Y$ and $g: Y \rightarrow X$ such that both $f \circ g$ and $g \circ f$ are the identity morphisms on $X$ and $Y$, respectively. In this case we may refer to both $f$ and $g$ as isomorphisms.
