# Definition 14.1

Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{m}$ and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n}$ be affine varieties. A morphism $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is a map $f(P)\coloneqq \left(f_{1}(P), \ldots, f_{n}(P)\right)$ defined by polynomials $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n} \in \bar{k}\left[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{m}\right]$ such that $f(P) \in Y$ for all points $P \in X$. We may regard $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n}$ as representatives of elements of the coordinate ring $\bar{k}[X]=\bar{k}\left[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{m}\right] / I(X)$; we are evaluating $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{n}$ only at points in $X$, so there is no reason to distinguish them modulo the ideal $I(X)$.
