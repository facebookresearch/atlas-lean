# Theorem 14.8

The following hold: (i) Every morphism $\phi: X \rightarrow Y$ of affine varieties induces a morphism $\phi^{*}: \bar{k}[Y] \rightarrow \bar{k}[X]$ of affine algebras such that $\phi^{*}(g)=g \circ \phi$. (ii) Every morphism $\theta: R \rightarrow S$ of affine algebras induces a morphism $\theta^{*}: X \rightarrow Y$ of affine varieties with $R \simeq \bar{k}[Y]$ and $S \simeq \bar{k}[X]$ such that the image of $\theta(g)$ in $\bar{k}[X]$ is $g \circ \theta^{*}$. (iii) If $\phi: X \rightarrow Y$ and $\psi: Y \rightarrow Z$ are morphisms of affine varieties, then $(\psi \circ \phi)^{*}=\phi^{*} \circ \psi^{*}$.
