# Theorem 4.15

The following hold: (1) $\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}-p\mathbb{Z}_{p}$; equivalently, $\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}=\{a \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}: v_{p}(a)=0\}$. (2) Every nonzero $a \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}$ can be uniquely written as $p^{n} u$ with $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ and $u \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}$.
