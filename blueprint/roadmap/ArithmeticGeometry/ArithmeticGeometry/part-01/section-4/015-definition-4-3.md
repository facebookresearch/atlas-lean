# Definition 4.3

For a prime $p$, the ring of $p$-adic integers $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$ is the inverse limit $$\mathbb{Z}_{p}=\varprojlim \mathbb{Z} / p^{n} \mathbb{Z}$$ of the inverse system of rings $(\mathbb{Z} / p^{n} \mathbb{Z})$ with morphisms $(f_{n})$ given by reduction modulo $p^{n}$ (for a residue class $\bar{x} \in \mathbb{Z} / p^{n+1} \mathbb{Z}$, pick an integer $x \in \bar{x}$ and take its residue class in $\mathbb{Z} / p^{n} \mathbb{Z}$).
