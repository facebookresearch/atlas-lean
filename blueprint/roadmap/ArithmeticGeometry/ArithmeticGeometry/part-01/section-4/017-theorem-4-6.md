# Theorem 4.6

Every element of $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$ has a unique $p$-adic expansion and every sequence $(b_{0}, b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots)$ of integers in $[0, p-1]$ is the $p$-adic expansion of an element of $\mathbb{Z}_{p}$.
