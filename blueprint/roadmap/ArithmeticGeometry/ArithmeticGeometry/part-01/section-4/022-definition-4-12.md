# Definition 4.12

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A discrete valuation (on $R$) is a function $v: R \rightarrow \mathbb{Z} \cup\{\infty\}$ that satisfies the three properties listed in Theorem 4.11: (1) $v(a)=\infty$ if and only if $a=0$, (2) $v(ab)=v(a)+v(b)$, (3) $v(a+b) \geq \min(v(a), v(b))$.
