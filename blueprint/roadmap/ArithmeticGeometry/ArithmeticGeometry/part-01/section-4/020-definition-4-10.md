# Definition 4.10

For each nonzero $a \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}$ the $p$-adic valuation of $a$, denoted $v_{p}(a)$, is the greatest integer $m$ for which $a$ lies in the image of $[p^{m}]$; equivalently, $v_{p}(a)$ is the index of the first nonzero entry in the $p$-adic expansion of $a$. We also define $v_{p}(0)=\infty$, and adopt the conventions that $n\lt \infty$ and $n+\infty=\infty$ for any integer $n$.
