# Theorem 4.11

The $p$-adic valuation $v_{p}$ satisfies the following properties: (1) $v_{p}(a)=\infty$ if and only if $a=0$. (2) $v_{p}(ab)=v_{p}(a)+v_{p}(b)$. (3) $v_{p}(a+b) \geq \min(v_{p}(a), v_{p}(b))$.
