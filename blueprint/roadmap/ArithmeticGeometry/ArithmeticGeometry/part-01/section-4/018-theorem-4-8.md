# Theorem 4.8

For each positive integer $m$, the sequence $$0 \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_{p} \xrightarrow{[p^{m}]} \mathbb{Z}_{p} \xrightarrow{\pi_{m}} \mathbb{Z} / p^{m} \mathbb{Z} \longrightarrow 0$$ is exact. Here $[p^{m}]$ is the multiplication-by-$p^{m}$ map and $\pi_{m}$ is the projection to $\mathbb{Z} / p^{m} \mathbb{Z}$.
