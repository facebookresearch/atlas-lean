# Theorem 11.1

Let $p$ be an odd prime and let $f$ be a diagonal quadratic form of dimension $n>2$ with coefficients $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n} \in \mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}$. Then $f$ represents 0 over $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$.
