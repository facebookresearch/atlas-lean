# Lemma 11.3

The nondegenerate quadratic form $ax^{2}+by^{2}$ over $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$ represents $t \in \mathbb{Q}_{p}^{*}$ if and only if $(a, b)_{p}=(t,-a b)_{p}$.
