# Definition 16.24

Let $R$ be an integral domain and let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime ideal in $R$. The subring of $R$'s fraction field defined by $R_{\mathfrak{p}} \coloneqq \{a/b : a, b \in R, b \notin \mathfrak{p}\}$ is called the localization of $R$ at $\mathfrak{p}$.
