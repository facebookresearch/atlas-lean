# Corollary 16.31

If $X$ is a variety such that for every subvariety $Z \subseteq X$ and valuation ring $R$ of $k(Z)/k$ with residue field $k$ there is a point $P \in Z$ such that $\mathcal{O}_{P,Z} \subseteq R$, then $X$ is complete.
