# Definition 16.8

Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{m}$ and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{n}$ be algebraic sets with homogeneous ideals $I(X) \subseteq k[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{m}]$ and $I(Y) \subseteq k[y_{0}, \ldots, y_{n}]$. The product $X \times Y$ is the zero locus of the $(m, n)$-homogeneous polynomials in the ideal $I(X \times Y)\coloneqq I(X) k[y_{0}, \ldots, y_{n}]+I(Y) k[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{m}]$ of $k[x_{0}, \ldots, x_{m}, y_{0}, \ldots, y_{n}]$. We say that $X \times Y$ is a variety if the ideal $I(X \times Y)$ is prime.
