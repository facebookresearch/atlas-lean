# Lemma 16.19

If $\mathfrak{a}$ and $\mathfrak{b}$ are two ideals of a valuation ring $R$ then either $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{b}$ or $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{a}$.
