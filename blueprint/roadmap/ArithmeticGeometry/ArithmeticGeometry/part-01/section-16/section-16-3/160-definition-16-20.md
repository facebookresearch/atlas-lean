# Definition 16.20

Let $R$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$. The value group of $R$ is $\Gamma = K^{\times}/R^{\times}$. The valuation defined by $R$ is the quotient map $v: K^{\times} \rightarrow \Gamma$.
