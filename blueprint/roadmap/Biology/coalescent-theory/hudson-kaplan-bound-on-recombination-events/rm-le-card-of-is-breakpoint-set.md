# $R_m$ is a lower bound on the size of a valid breakpoint set

Let $M$ be a sample of haplotypes over biallelic sites. Every [valid breakpoint set](is-breakpoint-set.md) $B$ for $M$ has at least [$R_m$](rm.md) elements.

## Sources

- R. R. Hudson and N. L. Kaplan, *Statistical properties of the number of recombination events in the history of a sample of DNA sequences*, Genetics 111 (1985), 147–164.
