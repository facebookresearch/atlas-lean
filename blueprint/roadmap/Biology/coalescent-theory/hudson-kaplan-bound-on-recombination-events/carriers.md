# Haplotypes carrying an allele at a site

For a sample $M$ of $n$ haplotypes over $s$ biallelic sites, a site $i$ and an allele $a \in \{0, 1\}$, the carriers of $a$ at $i$ form the set of haplotypes $r \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ with $M(r, i) = a$. When $a$ is the derived allele, this is the clade defined by the mutation at $i$.
