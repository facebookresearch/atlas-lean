# Four-gamete condition for two sites

Let $M$ be a sample of $n$ haplotypes over $s$ biallelic sites, recorded as a matrix with entries $M(r, i) \in \{0, 1\}$ for haplotypes $r \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and sites $i \in \{0, \dots, s-1\}$. Sites $i$ and $j$ show all four gametes if for every pair of alleles $(a, b) \in \{0, 1\}^2$ some haplotype $r$ has $M(r, i) = a$ and $M(r, j) = b$.
