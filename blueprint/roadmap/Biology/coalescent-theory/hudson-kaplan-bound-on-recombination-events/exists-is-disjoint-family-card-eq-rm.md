# $R_m$ is the maximum number of disjoint four-gamete intervals

For every sample $M$ of haplotypes over biallelic sites, some [disjoint family of four-gamete intervals](is-disjoint-family.md) has exactly [$R_m$](rm.md) members. With the previous bound, $R_m$ is the largest size of such a family, and the sweep attains it.
