# No disjoint family of four-gamete intervals has more than $R_m$ members

Let $M$ be a sample of haplotypes over biallelic sites. Every [disjoint family of four-gamete intervals](is-disjoint-family.md) $F$ for $M$ satisfies $|F| \le R_m$, where [$R_m$](rm.md) is the number of intervals kept by the Hudson–Kaplan sweep.
