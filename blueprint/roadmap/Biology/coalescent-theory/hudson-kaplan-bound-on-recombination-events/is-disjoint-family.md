# Disjoint family of four-gamete intervals

For a sample $M$ of $n$ haplotypes over $s$ biallelic sites, a set $F$ of pairs of sites is a disjoint family of four-gamete intervals if

- every $(i, j) \in F$ has $i < j$, and the sites $i$ and $j$ show [all four gametes](four-gametes.md);
- for distinct $(i, j)$ and $(i', j')$ in $F$, either $j \le i'$ or $j' \le i$.

So no breakpoint separates the end sites of two members of $F$.
