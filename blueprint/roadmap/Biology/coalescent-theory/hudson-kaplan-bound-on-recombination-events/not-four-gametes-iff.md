# Characterization of site pairs failing the four-gamete test

Let $M$ be a sample of haplotypes over biallelic sites and $i$, $j$ two sites. The sites $i$ and $j$ do not show [all four gametes](four-gametes.md) if and only if there are alleles $a$ and $b$ such that the [carriers](carriers.md) of $a$ at $i$ form a subset of the carriers of $b$ at $j$, or the two carrier sets are disjoint. These are the two ways in which two clades of one rooted tree can sit.
