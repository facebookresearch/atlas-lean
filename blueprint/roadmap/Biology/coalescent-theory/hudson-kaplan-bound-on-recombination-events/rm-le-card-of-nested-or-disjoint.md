# Hudson and Kaplan's bound for tree-like blocks

Let $M$ be a sample of haplotypes over biallelic sites and $B$ a set of sites. Suppose that for all sites $i < j$ that no element of $B$ [separates](separates.md), there are alleles $a$ and $b$ such that the [carriers](carriers.md) of $a$ at $i$ form a subset of the carriers of $b$ at $j$, or the two carrier sets are disjoint. Then $B$ has at least [$R_m$](rm.md) elements. This applies when the breakpoints cut the sequence into blocks within which all mutations sit on one tree, as under the infinite-sites model.
