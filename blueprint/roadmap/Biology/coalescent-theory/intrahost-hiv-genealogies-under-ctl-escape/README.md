# Intrahost HIV genealogies under CTL escape

Genealogies of HIV-infected cells sampled within a host after the virus escapes cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) attack at several epitopes. Successive escape sweeps act as generalized Luria–Delbrück phases, and in a small-population limit the coalescence of sampled lineages is described by explicit paintbox partitions instead of Kingman's coalescent.
