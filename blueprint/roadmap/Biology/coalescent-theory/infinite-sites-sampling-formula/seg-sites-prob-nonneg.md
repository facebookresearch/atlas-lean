# Tavaré's formula is nonnegative

For `θ ≥ 0`, every natural number `n ≥ 2` and every natural number `m`,
[Tavaré's formula](seg-sites-prob.md) satisfies `p_{θ,n}(m) ≥ 0`: it is the probability of `m`
segregating sites in the [marked Kingman model](../poisson-mutation-on-the-kingman-genealogy/kingman-mutation-measure.md).
