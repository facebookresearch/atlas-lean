# Low-mutation sampling formulas for finite-alleles models

Under Kingman's coalescent with a general $K$-allele recurrent mutation model, no closed-form sampling formula is known beyond parent-independent mutation. Expanding the sampling probability in the mutation rate $\theta$ gives a leading coefficient, carried by the most parsimonious genealogies, that does have closed forms when few distinct alleles are observed.
