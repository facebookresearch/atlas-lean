# Generating function of Tavaré's formula

For `θ ≥ 0`, every real `s` with `|s| ≤ 1` and every natural number `n ≥ 2`, the series
`∑_{m ≥ 0} p_{θ,n}(m) s^m` of [Tavaré's formula](seg-sites-prob.md) (natural powers, with `0^0 = 1`,
so that at `s = 0` the sum is `p_{θ,n}(0)`) is summable, with sum

`∑_{m ≥ 0} p_{θ,n}(m) s^m = ∏_{k=1}^{n-1} k / (k + θ (1 - s))`.
