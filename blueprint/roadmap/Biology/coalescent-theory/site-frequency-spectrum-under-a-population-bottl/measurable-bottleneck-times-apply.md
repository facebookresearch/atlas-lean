# Each time-changed holding time is measurable

Let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md)
of a sample of size `n` under a measure `μ` on `Ω`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential
law with rate `C(k, 2)` under `μ`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent.
Suppose each `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) is measurable. Then for all real numbers `a`, `d` and `f` and every
natural number `k`, the [time-changed holding time](bottleneck-times.md) `T^b_k` is measurable.
