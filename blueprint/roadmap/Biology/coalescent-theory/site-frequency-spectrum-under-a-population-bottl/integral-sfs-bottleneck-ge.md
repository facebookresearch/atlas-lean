# An expansion keeps the expected spectrum at least Fu's value

Let `μ` be a probability measure on a measurable space `(Ω, F)` and `n` a natural number. Let
`T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md)
of a sample of size `n` under `μ`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law with rate
`C(k, 2)` under `μ`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent; suppose each `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) is
measurable. Let `τ` be a random block-size history (a random list of block-size lists) such
that every preimage `τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n`
singletons, `μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps`, and independent of the family
`(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`. For real numbers `a`, `d` and `f`, let `T^b_k` be the
[time-changed holding times](bottleneck-times.md), `G^b` the σ-algebra generated by `τ` and
`(T^b_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`, and `L^b_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T^b_k` the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md)
in the time-changed genealogy, where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry
`n - k` of `τ` (the empty list if `τ` is shorter). Let `θ` be a real number and `ξ_i`
(`i ∈ ℕ`) integrable random variables with `E[ξ_i | G^b] = (θ / 2) L^b_i` almost surely for
every `i`.

If `θ ≥ 0`, `d ≥ 0` and `f ≥ 1`, then `θ / i ≤ E[ξ_i]` for every `1 ≤ i ≤ n - 1`: no entry of
the expected spectrum is below [Fu's constant-size value](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/integral-sfs.md) `θ / i`.
