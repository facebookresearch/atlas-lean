# Strict shortening of the oldest epoch under an overlap condition

Let `a ≥ 0`, `d > 0` and `0 < f < 1` be real numbers, `n ≥ 2` a natural number and `T_j`
(`j ∈ ℕ`) real numbers with `T_j ≥ 0` for `2 ≤ j ≤ n`, and let `V_3 = ∑_{j=3}^{n} T_j` (`0`
when `n = 2`). If `V_3 < a + d/f` and `T_2 > a`, the
[time-changed holding time](bottleneck-holding.md) of the epoch with two lineages satisfies
`T^b_2 < T_2`. In standard time that epoch is `[V_3, V_3 + T_2]` and the bottleneck occupies
the window `[a, a + d/f]`; the two conditions make them overlap in an interval of positive
length (the epoch starts before the window ends, and ends after it starts since `V_3 ≥ 0`).
