# The time-changed holding times generate a sub-σ-algebra of Kingman's

Let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md)
of a sample of size `n` under a measure `μ` on `Ω`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential
law with rate `C(k, 2)` under `μ`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent.
For all real numbers `a`, `d` and `f`, the σ-algebra on `Ω` generated by the family
`(T^b_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}` of [time-changed holding times](bottleneck-times.md) is contained in the
σ-algebra generated by the family `(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`.
