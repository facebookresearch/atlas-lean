# A time-changed epoch as an increment of the time change

For real numbers `a`, `d` and `f`, natural numbers `k ≤ n` and real numbers `T_j` (`j ∈ ℕ`),
the [time-changed holding time](bottleneck-holding.md) satisfies

`T^b_k = G(T_k + V_{k+1}) - G(V_{k+1})`,

with the [time change](bottleneck-time.md) `G` and `V_{k+1} = ∑_{j=k+1}^{n} T_j` (`0` when
`k = n`).
