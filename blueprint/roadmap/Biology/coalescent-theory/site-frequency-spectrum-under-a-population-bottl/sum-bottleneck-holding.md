# Real time to the most recent common ancestor under a bottleneck

For real numbers `a ≥ 0`, `d ≥ 0` and `f > 0`, a natural number `n` and real numbers `T_j`
(`j ∈ ℕ`), the [time-changed holding times](bottleneck-holding.md) satisfy

`∑_{k=2}^{n} T^b_k = G(∑_{k=2}^{n} T_k)`,

with the [time change](bottleneck-time.md) `G` (for `n < 2` both sides are `0`). For
standard-coalescent holding times, the real time to the most recent common ancestor is `G` of
the standard one.
