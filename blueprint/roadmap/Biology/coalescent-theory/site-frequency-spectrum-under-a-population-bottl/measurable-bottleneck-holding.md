# The time-changed holding times depend measurably on the standard ones

For all real numbers `a`, `d` and `f` and every natural number `n`, the map sending a real
sequence `(T_j)_{j ∈ ℕ}` to the sequence `(T^b_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}` of
[time-changed holding times](bottleneck-holding.md) is measurable for the product σ-algebra on
real sequences.
