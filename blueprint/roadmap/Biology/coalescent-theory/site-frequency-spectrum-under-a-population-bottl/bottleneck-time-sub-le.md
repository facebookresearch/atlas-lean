# For f ≤ 1 the time change grows no faster than standard time

For any real `a` and `d`, any real `f ≤ 1` and real numbers `u ≤ v`, the
[time change](bottleneck-time.md) satisfies `G(v) - G(u) ≤ v - u`. For `d ≥ 0` and
`0 < f ≤ 1`, where `G` is [nondecreasing](bottleneck-time-sub-nonneg.md), `G` is therefore
`1`-Lipschitz: a bottleneck never lengthens a stretch of standard time.
