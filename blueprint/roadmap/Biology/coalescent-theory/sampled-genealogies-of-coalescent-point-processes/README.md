# Sampled genealogies of coalescent point processes

The reconstructed tree of extant individuals of a binary branching process with time-dependent birth and age-dependent death rates is a coalescent point process: its node depths are i.i.d. and stopped at the first exceedance of the tree height. The genealogy of a uniform sample of fixed size is a mixture, over a random sampling probability, of Bernoulli-sampled coalescent point processes, which gives explicit sampling and likelihood formulas.
