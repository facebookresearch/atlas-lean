# For ρ ≥ 0 the time change never lengthens a nonnegative interval

Let `ρ ≥ 0` and let `G` be the [time change](growth-time.md) for exponential growth at rate `ρ`.
For all real numbers `a` and `b` with `0 ≤ a ≤ b`,

`G(b) - G(a) ≤ b - a`.

On `[0, ∞)` the derivative of `G` is `1 / (1 + ρ u) ≤ 1`. Together with
[monotonicity](growth-time-sub-nonneg.md), `G` is `1`-Lipschitz on `[0, ∞)`: growth never
lengthens a coalescent epoch.
