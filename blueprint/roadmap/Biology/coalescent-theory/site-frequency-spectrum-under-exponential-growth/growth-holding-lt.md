# For ρ > 0 the time change strictly shortens a positive holding time

Let `ρ > 0`, let `n` be a natural number and let `T_j` (`j ∈ ℕ`) be real numbers with
`T_j ≥ 0` for `2 ≤ j ≤ n`. Then for every `2 ≤ k ≤ n` with `T_k > 0` the
[holding time under exponential growth](growth-holding.md) satisfies

`T^ρ_k < T_k`.
