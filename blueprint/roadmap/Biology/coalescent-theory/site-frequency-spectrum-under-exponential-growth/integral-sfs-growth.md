# Expected site frequency spectrum under exponential growth

Let `μ` be a probability measure, let `ρ ≥ 0` and let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under `μ`, each `T_k` measurable: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential
law with rate `C(k, 2)`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `T^ρ_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be the
[time-changed holding times](growth-times.md) for exponential growth at rate `ρ`: the epoch with
`k` lineages lasts `T^ρ_k = G(V_k) - G(V_{k+1})` in real time, with `V_k = ∑_{j=k}^{n} T_j` and
the [time change](growth-time.md) `G`. Let `τ` be a random block-size history (a random list of
block-size lists) such that every preimage `τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n` singletons,
`μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps`, and independent of the family
`(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`. Let `L^ρ_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T^ρ_k` be the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md)
for the holding times `T^ρ_k`, where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry
`n - k` of `τ` (the empty list if `τ` is shorter), and let `𝒢` be the σ-algebra generated by `τ`
and the family `(T^ρ_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`.

Let `θ` be a real number and let `ξ_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be integrable real random variables with
`E[ξ_i | 𝒢] = (θ / 2) L^ρ_i` almost surely for every `i`. Then for `1 ≤ i ≤ n - 1`,

`E[ξ_i] = (θ / 2) ∑_{k=2}^{n} g_{n,i,k} E[T^ρ_k]`,

the [variable-size expected spectrum](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/variable-sfs.md)
at the mean holding times `E[T^ρ_k]`, with the
[Griffiths–Tavaré kernel](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/sfs-kernel.md)
`g_{n,i,k} = k C(n - i - 1, k - 2) / C(n - 1, k - 1)`. Only the independence of `τ` from the
Kingman holding times is assumed: the time-changed holding times generate a σ-algebra
[contained in that of the Kingman ones](comap-growth-times-le.md), so `τ` is independent of them
as well, and the
[spectrum formula for holding times independent of the topology](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/integral-sfs-of-indep.md)
applies. In the neutral infinite-sites model with `θ ≥ 0`, mutation at rate `θ / 2` per lineage
per unit of real time in a population that has grown exponentially at rate `ρ`, one may take
`ξ_i` to be the number of sites whose derived allele is carried by exactly `i` of the `n` sample
copies. The result assumes only the conditional mean: `ξ_i` need not be integer-valued and `θ`
may be negative, as for `ξ_i = (θ / 2) L^ρ_i`.
