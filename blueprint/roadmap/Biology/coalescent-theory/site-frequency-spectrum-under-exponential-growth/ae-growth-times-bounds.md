# Almost-everywhere bounds on the time-changed holding times

Let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under a measure `μ` on `Ω`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law
with rate `C(k, 2)` under `μ`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `ρ ≥ 0`. Then for
`μ`-almost every `ω` and every `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, the [time-changed holding times](growth-times.md)
satisfy

`0 ≤ T^ρ_k(ω) ≤ T_k(ω)`, and `T^ρ_k(ω) < T_k(ω)` if `ρ > 0`.
