# The cumulative intensity is nonnegative at nonnegative times

Let `ρ ≥ 0` and `s ≥ 0` be real numbers. The
[cumulative coalescence intensity](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/cum-rate.md)
of the [relative size](exp-growth-size.md) `η(r) = exp(-ρ r)` satisfies

`R(s) = ∫_0^s exp(ρ r) dr ≥ 0`.
