# The cumulative intensity undoes the time change when 1 + ρu > 0

Let `ρ` and `u` be real numbers with `1 + ρ u > 0`. Let `G` be the
[time change](growth-time.md) for exponential growth at rate `ρ` and let
`R(s) = ∫_0^s exp(ρ r) dr` be the
[cumulative coalescence intensity](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/cum-rate.md)
of the [relative size](exp-growth-size.md) `η(r) = exp(-ρ r)`. Then

`R(G(u)) = u`.
