# For ρ > 0, the time change strictly shortens intervals 0 ≤ a < b

Let `ρ > 0` and let `G` be the [time change](growth-time.md) for exponential growth at rate `ρ`.
For all real numbers `a` and `b` with `0 ≤ a < b`,

`G(b) - G(a) < b - a`.

Under strict growth every epoch of positive standard length is strictly shortened.
