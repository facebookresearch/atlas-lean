# Cumulative coalescence intensity under exponential growth

For real numbers `ρ ≠ 0` and `s`, the
[cumulative coalescence intensity](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/cum-rate.md)
of the [relative size](exp-growth-size.md) `η(r) = exp(-ρ r)` is

`R(s) = ∫_0^s dr / η(r) = ∫_0^s exp(ρ r) dr = (exp(ρ s) - 1) / ρ`.

The integral is oriented, and the formula holds for `s < 0` as well.
