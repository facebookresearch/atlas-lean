# Coalescent trees of density-dependent branching processes

The genealogy of a small sample from a population started from one individual that grows exponentially at first and then slows toward a carrying capacity (logistic, Gompertz and related growth). While growth is still superlinear, the sample genealogy has a universal limit determined only by the intrinsic reproduction rates.
