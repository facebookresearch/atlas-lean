# The kernel entry for two lineages is positive

For natural numbers `n` and `i` with `1 ≤ i < n`, the entry of the
[Griffiths–Tavaré kernel](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/sfs-kernel.md)
for the epoch with two lineages, the oldest one, is positive:

`g_{n,i,2} = 2 C(n - i - 1, 0) / C(n - 1, 1) = 2 / (n - 1) > 0`.

Under random joining it is the expected number of the two lineages that subtend exactly `i` of
the `n` sample copies.
