# Kingman holding times are positive almost everywhere

Let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under a measure `μ` on `Ω`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law
with rate `C(k, 2)` under `μ`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Then for `μ`-almost every
`ω`,

`T_k(ω) > 0` for every `2 ≤ k ≤ n`.

For `n ≥ 2`, `μ` is a probability measure and the holding times are almost surely positive.
