# The time change undoes the cumulative intensity

Let `ρ` be a real number, let `G` be the [time change](growth-time.md) for exponential growth at
rate `ρ` and let `R(s) = ∫_0^s exp(ρ r) dr` be the
[cumulative coalescence intensity](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/cum-rate.md)
of the [relative size](exp-growth-size.md) `η(r) = exp(-ρ r)`. Then for every real number `s`,

`G(R(s)) = s`.
