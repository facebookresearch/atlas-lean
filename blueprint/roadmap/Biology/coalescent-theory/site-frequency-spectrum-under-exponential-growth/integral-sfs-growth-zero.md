# Without growth the expected spectrum is Fu's θ / i

Let `μ` be a probability measure and let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under `μ`, each `T_k` measurable: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential
law with rate `C(k, 2)`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `T^0_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be the
[time-changed holding times](growth-times.md) at rate `ρ = 0`, which are `T^0_k = T_k` for
`k ≤ n` and `T^0_k = 0` for `k > n`. Let `τ` be a random block-size history (a random list of
block-size lists) such that every preimage `τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n` singletons,
`μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps`, and independent of the family
`(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`. Let `L_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T^0_k = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T_k` be the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md),
where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry `n - k` of `τ` (the empty list if
`τ` is shorter), and let `𝒢` be the σ-algebra generated by `τ` and the family
`(T^0_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`.

Let `θ` be a real number and let `ξ_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be integrable real random variables with
`E[ξ_i | 𝒢] = (θ / 2) L_i` almost surely for every `i`. Then for `1 ≤ i ≤ n - 1`,

`E[ξ_i] = θ / i`,

the [expected spectrum of a population of constant size](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/integral-sfs.md).
In the neutral infinite-sites model with `θ ≥ 0`, mutation at rate `θ / 2` per lineage per unit
of real time, one may take `ξ_i` to be the number of sites whose derived allele is carried by
exactly `i` of the `n` sample copies. The result assumes only the conditional mean: `ξ_i` need
not be integer-valued and `θ` may be negative, as for `ξ_i = (θ / 2) L_i`.
