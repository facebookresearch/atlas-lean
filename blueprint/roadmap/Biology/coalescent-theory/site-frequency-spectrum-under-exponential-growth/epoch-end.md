# Standard time at which the epoch with k lineages ends

For a natural number `n`, real numbers `T_j` (`j ∈ ℕ`) and a natural number `k`, let

`V_k = ∑_{j=k}^{n} T_j`,

which is the empty sum `0` when `k > n`. When `T_j` is the holding time with `j` lineages of
the coalescent genealogy of a sample of size `n` (`2 ≤ j ≤ n`) and `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `V_k` is the
standard-coalescent time at which the epoch with `k` lineages ends; `V_{n+1} = 0`, and `V_2`
is the time to the most recent common ancestor.
