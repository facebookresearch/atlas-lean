# Time-changed holding times depend measurably on the standard ones

For every real number `ρ` and natural number `n`, the map sending a sequence `(T_j)_{j ∈ ℕ}` of
real numbers to the sequence `(T^ρ_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}` of its
[holding times under exponential growth](growth-holding.md) is measurable from `ℝ^ℕ` to `ℝ^ℕ`,
both with the product σ-algebra.
