# Without growth the cumulative intensity is the elapsed time

For every real number `s`, the
[cumulative coalescence intensity](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/cum-rate.md)
of the [relative size](exp-growth-size.md) at rate `ρ = 0`, the constant `η(r) = exp(0) = 1`,
is

`R(s) = ∫_0^s dr = s`.
