# Relative size of an exponentially growing population

For real numbers `ρ` and `s`, let

`η(s) = exp(-ρ s)`,

a positive number. For `s ≥ 0` it is the size, relative to the present size `N₀`, of a
population `s` units of `2N₀` generations before the present, when the population size has
changed exponentially at rate `ρ`: `ρ > 0` is growth towards the present, `ρ = 0` a constant
size and `ρ < 0` a decline.
