# Number of ranked labeled histories

For every natural number $n$, there are exactly $\prod_{k=2}^{n} \binom{k}{2}$ [ranked labeled histories](ranked-history.md) of $n$ labeled genes.

## Sources

- A. W. F. Edwards, *Estimation of the branch points of a branching diffusion process*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 32 (1970), 155–174.
