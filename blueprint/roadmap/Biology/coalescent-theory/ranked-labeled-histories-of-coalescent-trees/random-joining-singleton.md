# Each ranked labeled history has probability $2^{n-1} / (n!(n-1)!)$

For every natural number $n$ and every [ranked labeled history](ranked-history.md) $h$ of $n$ genes, the [random-joining law](random-joining.md) gives $h$ the probability

$$\rho_n(\{h\}) = \frac{2^{n-1}}{n!\,(n-1)!},$$

where $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$.

## Sources

- A. W. F. Edwards, *Estimation of the branch points of a branching diffusion process*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 32 (1970), 155–174.
- F. Tajima, *Evolutionary relationship of DNA sequences in finite populations*, Genetics 105 (1983), 437–460.
