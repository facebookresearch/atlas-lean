# Every ranked history is equally likely under random joining

For every natural number $n$ and every [ranked labeled history](ranked-history.md) $h$ of $n$ genes, the [random-joining law](random-joining.md) gives $h$ the probability

$$\rho_n(\{h\}) = \Bigl(\prod_{k=2}^{n} \binom{k}{2}\Bigr)^{-1}.$$
