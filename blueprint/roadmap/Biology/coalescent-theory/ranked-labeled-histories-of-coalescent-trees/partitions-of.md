# Ancestral partitions along a history as sets of blocks

For a [ranked labeled history](ranked-history.md) $h$ of $n$ genes, the list of length $n$ whose entry $t$, for $t = 0, \dots, n-1$, is the set of blocks of sampled genes after the first $t$ coalescence events of $h$, forgetting the lineage labels. This sequence of ancestral partitions is the ranked labeled history in the sense of Edwards (1970).

## Sources

- A. W. F. Edwards, *Estimation of the branch points of a branching diffusion process*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 32 (1970), 155–174.
