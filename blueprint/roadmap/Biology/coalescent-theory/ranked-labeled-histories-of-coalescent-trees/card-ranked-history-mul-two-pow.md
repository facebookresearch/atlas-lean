# Number of ranked labeled histories times $2^{n-1}$

For every natural number $n$, the number $H_n$ of [ranked labeled histories](ranked-history.md) of $n$ labeled genes satisfies

$$H_n \cdot 2^{n-1} = n!\,(n-1)!,$$

where $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$. So there are $n!\,(n-1)!/2^{n-1}$ ranked labeled histories.

## Sources

- A. W. F. Edwards, *Estimation of the branch points of a branching diffusion process*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 32 (1970), 155–174.
