# Random joining

For a natural number $n$, the random-joining law $\rho_n$ on the [ranked labeled histories](ranked-history.md) of $n$ genes is the product, over the steps $i = 0, \dots, n-2$, of the uniform probability measures on the [unordered pairs](lineage-pair.md) of the $n - i$ current lineages: the pair merging at each step is uniform among the current $\binom{n-i}{2}$ pairs and independent of the earlier steps. Kingman (1982) shows that this is the law of the tree topology of the coalescent.

## Sources

- A. W. F. Edwards, *Estimation of the branch points of a branching diffusion process*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 32 (1970), 155–174.
- J. F. C. Kingman, *The coalescent*, Stochastic Process. Appl. 13 (1982), 235–248.
