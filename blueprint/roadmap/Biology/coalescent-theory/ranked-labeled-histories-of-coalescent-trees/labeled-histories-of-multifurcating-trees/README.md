# Labeled histories and maximally probable topologies of multifurcating trees

Counting the coalescence orderings (labeled histories) of rooted labeled trees whose internal nodes have between 2 and $r$ children, with or without simultaneous mergers, and identifying the tree shapes with the most histories, which are the most probable shapes under the Yule–Harding model and the coalescent; for strictly $r$-furcating trees they are $r$-ary Huffman trees.
