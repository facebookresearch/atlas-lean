# Holding times of the marked Kingman model

For a natural number `n` and `θ ≥ 0`, the functions on the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-mutation-measure.md) given by `T_k(t, N) = t_k` for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`
and `T_k = 0` for the other `k` are
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n`: under the model, `T_k` has the exponential law with rate `C(k, 2)` for
`2 ≤ k ≤ n`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent.
