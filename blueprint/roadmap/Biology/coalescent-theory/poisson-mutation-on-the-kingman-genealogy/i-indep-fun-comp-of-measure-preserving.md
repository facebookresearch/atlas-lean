# Independence is preserved by a measure-preserving change of space

Let `ι` be a finite index set, let `μ` and `P` be probability measures on `Ω` and `Ω'`, and let
`g : Ω' → Ω` be measure preserving from `P` to `μ`. If the measurable maps `f_i : Ω → β_i`
(`i ∈ ι`) are mutually independent under `μ`, then the maps `f_i ∘ g` are mutually independent
under `P`.
