# Conditional variance of a conditionally Poisson count

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω`, let `X` be a measurable random variable with values in a
measurable space `𝓧`, let `ρ` be a measurable function from `𝓧` to `[0, ∞)`, and let `N` be a
natural-number-valued random variable whose conditional law given `X` is Poisson with mean `ρ(X)`:
the joint law of `(X, N)` is the law of `X` composed with the kernel `x ↦ Po(ρ(x))`. If `ρ(X)` is
square integrable, then the conditional variance of `N` given the σ-algebra `σ(X)` generated by `X`
is

`Var(N | σ(X)) = E[(N - E[N | σ(X)])² | σ(X)] = ρ(X)` almost surely.
