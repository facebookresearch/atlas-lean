# Conditional variance of a conditionally Poisson count

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω`, let `X` be a measurable function from `Ω` to a measurable
space `𝓧`, let `ρ` be a measurable function from `𝓧` to `[0, ∞)`, and let `N` be a
natural-number-valued function on `Ω` whose conditional law given `X` is Poisson with mean `ρ(X)`:
the pair `(X, N)` is `μ`-almost everywhere measurable, and the image of `μ` under `(X, N)` is the
composition product of the image of `μ` under `X` with the kernel `x ↦ Po(ρ(x))`. If `ρ(X)` is
square integrable, then the conditional variance of `N` given the σ-algebra `σ(X)` generated by `X`
is

`Var(N | σ(X)) = E[(N - E[N | σ(X)])² | σ(X)] = ρ(X)` almost surely.
