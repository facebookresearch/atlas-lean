# Expected number of segregating sites in the marked Kingman model

For every natural number `n` and every `θ ≥ 0`, in the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-mutation-measure.md) the number of segregating sites
`S = ∑_b N_b` has mean

`E[S] = θ H_{n-1}`, with `H_m = ∑_{j=1}^{m} 1 / j`,

with no further hypothesis. Here `n - 1` is a natural-number difference; for `n ≤ 1` both sides
are `0`.
