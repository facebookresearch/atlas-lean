# Branches of a Kingman genealogy, epoch by epoch

For a natural number `n`, the branches of a Kingman genealogy of `n` gene copies are the pairs
`(k, j)` with `2 ≤ k ≤ n` and `0 ≤ j < k`: during the epoch with `k` lineages there are `k`
branches. There are `∑_{k=2}^{n} k` of them, and none when `n < 2`.
