# Epoch-length vector of a Kingman genealogy

For [Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md)
`T_k` of a sample of size `n` under a measure `μ`, the epoch-length vector at `ω` is

`E(ω) = (T_k(ω))_{2 ≤ k ≤ n}`,

a vector indexed by `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, whose entry `k` is the time during which the genealogy has `k`
lineages. It is the genealogy on which mutations are placed.
