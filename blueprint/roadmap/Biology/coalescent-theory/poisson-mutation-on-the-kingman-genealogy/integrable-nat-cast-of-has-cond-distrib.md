# A conditionally Poisson count with integrable mean is integrable

Let `μ` be a probability measure on `Ω`, let `X` be a random variable with values in a measurable
space `𝓧`, let `ρ` be a measurable function from `𝓧` to `[0, ∞)`, and let `N` be a
natural-number-valued random variable whose conditional law given `X` is Poisson with mean `ρ(X)`:
the joint law of `(X, N)` is the law of `X` composed with the kernel `x ↦ Po(ρ(x))`. If `ρ(X)` is
integrable, then `N` is integrable.
