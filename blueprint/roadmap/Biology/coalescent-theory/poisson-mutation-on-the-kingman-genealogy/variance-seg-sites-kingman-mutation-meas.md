# Variance of the segregating-site count in the marked Kingman model

For every natural number `n` and every `θ ≥ 0`, in the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-mutation-measure.md) the number of segregating sites
`S = ∑_b N_b` has variance

`Var(S) = θ a_1 + θ² a_2`, with `a_1 = ∑_{i=1}^{n-1} 1 / i` and `a_2 = ∑_{i=1}^{n-1} 1 / i²`,

with no further hypothesis; for `n ≤ 1` both sides are `0`.
