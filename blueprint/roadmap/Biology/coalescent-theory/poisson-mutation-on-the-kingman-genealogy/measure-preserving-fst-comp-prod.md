# First marginal of a measure composed with a Markov kernel

Let `μ` be an s-finite measure on `α` and `κ` a Markov kernel from `α` to `β`. The first
projection `(a, b) ↦ a` is measure preserving from the composition-product measure `μ ⊗ κ` on
`α × β`, given by `(μ ⊗ κ)(A × B) = ∫_A κ(a)(B) dμ(a)`, to `μ`: the first marginal of `μ ⊗ κ` is
`μ`.
