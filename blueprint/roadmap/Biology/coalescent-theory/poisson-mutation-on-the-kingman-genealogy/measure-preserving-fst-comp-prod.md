# First marginal of a measure composed with a Markov kernel

Let `μ` be an s-finite measure on `α` and `κ` a Markov kernel from `α` to `β`. The first projection
`(a, b) ↦ a` is measure preserving from the composition-product measure `μ ⊗ κ` on `α × β`, given on
measurable rectangles by `(μ ⊗ κ)(A × B) = ∫_A κ(a)(B) dμ(a)` for measurable `A ⊆ α` and `B ⊆ β`, to
`μ`: the first marginal of `μ ⊗ κ` is `μ`. Here `0 ≤ κ(a)(B) ≤ 1`, and the nonnegative integral may
equal `∞` (when `μ(A) = ∞`).
