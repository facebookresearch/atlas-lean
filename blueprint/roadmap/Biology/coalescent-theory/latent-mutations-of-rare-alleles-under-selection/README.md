# Latent mutations of rare alleles under selection

A sample in which an allele appears only a few times may still carry several independent mutational origins of it in its genealogy (latent recurrent mutations). Under two-way mutation and selection at stationarity, coalescence in the random background of allele frequencies shows that the number of latent mutations of a rare allele keeps an Ewens-type law as long as selection is not too strong relative to the sample size, and collapses to a single origin under strong positive selection.
