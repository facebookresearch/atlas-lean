# Complete monotonicity of the diagonal rates

Let `r(b, k)` be real numbers indexed by natural numbers that, whenever `2 ≤ k ≤ b`, are
nonnegative and satisfy Pitman's consistency relation `r(b, k) = r(b + 1, k) + r(b + 1, k + 1)`,
and let `m(j) = r(j + 2, j + 2)`. Then `(-1)^c (Δ^c m)(i) ≥ 0` for all natural numbers `c` and
`i`, where `Δ g (j) = g(j + 1) - g(j)` is the forward difference: `m` is completely monotone.
