# Closed form of the Beta-coalescent rates

For `0 < α < 2` and `2 ≤ k ≤ b`, the [merger rate](../../coalescents/merger-rate.md) of the
[Beta(2 - α, α)-coalescent](beta-lambda.md) is `λ_{b,k} = B(k - α, b - k + α) / B(2 - α, α)`,
with `B` the Beta function. The rate is defined because for these `α` the Beta-coalescent measure
is a [probability measure](is-probability-measure-beta-lambda.md), hence finite.
