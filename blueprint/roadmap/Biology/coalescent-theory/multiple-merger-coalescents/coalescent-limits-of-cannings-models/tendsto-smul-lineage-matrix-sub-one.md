# Rescaled lineage-count matrices converge to the Λ-coalescent generator

Under the [Möhle–Sagitov conditions](mohle-sagitov-lambda.md) for a sequence of Cannings models
and a finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]`, for every `n` the matrices `c_N⁻¹ (Π_N - I)` converge
entrywise, as `N → ∞`, to the [block-counting generator](../../coalescents/block-counting-generator.md)
`G_Λ` of the Λ-coalescent on the states `0, …, n`, where `Π_N` is the one-generation
[transition matrix](../cannings-models/lineage-matrix.md) of the ancestral lineage count, `c_N`
the [coalescence probability](../cannings-models/coal-prob.md) and `I` the identity matrix.
