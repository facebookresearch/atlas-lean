# The limit Λ is determined by the offspring law

For a sequence of Cannings models and two finite measures `Λ` and `Λ'` on `[0, 1]` that both
satisfy the [Möhle–Sagitov conditions](mohle-sagitov-lambda.md) for this same sequence,
`Λ = Λ'`. The condition used is `q_k / c_N → ∫ x^(k-2) Λ(dx)` for every `k ≥ 2`, with `q_k` the
probability that `k` sampled lineages share a parent and `c_N` the coalescence probability: the
moments of `Λ` and `Λ'` are the same limits, and a finite measure is
[determined by its merger rates](../../coalescents/eq-of-merger-rate-eq.md) (Pitman's uniqueness
theorem).
