# Rows b ≤ N of the lineage-count transition matrix sum to one

For a [Cannings model](../cannings-models/cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N` and
`b ≤ n`, `b ≤ N`, the [transition probabilities](../cannings-models/lineage-prob.md) satisfy
`∑_{a=0}^{n} P_{b,a} = 1`: row `b` of the
[transition matrix](../cannings-models/lineage-matrix.md) on the states `0, …, n` sums to `1`.
