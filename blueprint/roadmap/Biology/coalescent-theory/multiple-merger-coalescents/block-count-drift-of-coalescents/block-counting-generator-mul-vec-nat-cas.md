# The block-counting generator applied to the identity

Let `Λ` be a finite measure on `[0, 1]` and `Q` the
[block-counting generator](../../coalescents/block-counting-generator.md) of the Λ-coalescent on
the states `0, …, n`. The function `f(j) = j` satisfies `(Q f)(b) = -γ_b` at every state
`b ≤ n`, where `γ_b` is the [block-count drift](block-drift.md): `γ_b` is the expected rate of
descent of the block count from `b` blocks.
