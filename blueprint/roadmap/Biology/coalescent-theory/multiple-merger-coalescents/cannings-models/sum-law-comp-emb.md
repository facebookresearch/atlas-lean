# Exchangeability of samples

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, whose law is
exchangeable in the children, an injective choice `e(0), …, e(m - 1)` of `m` children and an
event `E` on maps `{0, …, m - 1} → {0, …, N - 1}`, the probability that the parents
`(f(e(0)), …, f(e(m - 1)))` satisfy `E` equals the [sample probability](sample-prob.md) of `E`,
defined with the first `m` children.
