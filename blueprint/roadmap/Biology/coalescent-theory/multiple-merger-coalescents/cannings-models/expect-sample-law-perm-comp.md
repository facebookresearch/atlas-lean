# Relabelling the parents does not change sampled expectations

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, whose law is
exchangeable in the parents, a sample size `b ≤ N`, every permutation `σ` of the parents and
every real function `F` of the parents `g` of `b` lineages, the expectations of `F(σ ∘ g)` and of
`F(g)` under the [sample law](sample-law.md) agree.
