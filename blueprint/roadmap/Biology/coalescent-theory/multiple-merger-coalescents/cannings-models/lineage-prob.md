# One-generation transition probability of the lineage count

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N` and natural numbers `b`
and `a`, `P_{b,a}` is the [probability](sample-prob.md) that `b` sampled lineages have exactly
`a` distinct parents one generation back: the one-generation transition probability of the
ancestral lineage count. It is `0` when `b > N`.
