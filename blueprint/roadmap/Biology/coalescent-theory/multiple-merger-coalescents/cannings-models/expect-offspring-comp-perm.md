# Exchangeable offspring numbers

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, whose law is
exchangeable in the parents, every permutation `σ` of the parents and every real function `F` of
the offspring vector, `E[F(ν_{σ(0)}, …, ν_{σ(N - 1)})] = E[F(ν_0, …, ν_{N - 1})]`: the
[offspring vector](offspring.md) has the same law as its permutations.
