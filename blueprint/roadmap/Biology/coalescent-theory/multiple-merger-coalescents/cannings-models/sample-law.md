# Law of the parents of a sample

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N` and a sample size `b ≤ N`,
the sample law is the law of the parents `(f(0), …, f(b - 1))` of the first `b` children, the
image of the law of the parent map. Expectations of real functions of a sample are expectations
under it.
