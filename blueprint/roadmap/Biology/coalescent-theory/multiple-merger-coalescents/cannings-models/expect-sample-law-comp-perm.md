# Relabelling the sampled children does not change sampled expectations

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, whose law is
exchangeable in the children, a sample size `b ≤ N`, every permutation `σ` of the `b` sampled
children and every real function `F` of their parents `g`, the expectations of `F(g ∘ σ)` and of
`F(g)` under the [sample law](sample-law.md) agree: the relabelling of the `b` sampled children
extends to a permutation of all `N` children.
