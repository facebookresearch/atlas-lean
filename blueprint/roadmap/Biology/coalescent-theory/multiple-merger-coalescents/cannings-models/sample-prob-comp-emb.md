# The probability of an event for a subsample

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, whose law is
exchangeable in the children, a sample of `b ≤ N` lineages, an injective choice
`u(0), …, u(m - 1)` of `m` of them and an event `E` on maps `{0, …, m - 1} → {0, …, N - 1}`, the
[sample probability](sample-prob.md) that the parents of `u(0), …, u(m - 1)` satisfy `E`,
computed in the sample of `b`, equals the sample probability of `E`.
