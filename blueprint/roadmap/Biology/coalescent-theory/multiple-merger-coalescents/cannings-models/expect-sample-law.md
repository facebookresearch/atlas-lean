# A sampled expectation is an expectation over the parent map

For a [Cannings model](cannings-model.md) of a population of size `N`, a sample size `b ≤ N` and
a real function `F` of the parents of `b` lineages, the expectation of `F` under the
[sample law](sample-law.md) is the [expectation](expect.md) `E[F(f(0), …, f(b - 1))]` over the
parent map.
