# The Laplace exponent of a Λ-coalescent

For a finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]`, the Laplace exponent of the Λ-coalescent is
`ψ(q) = ∫_{[0,1]} (e^{-qx} - 1 + qx) x^{-2} Λ(dx)` for every real `q`, with the integrand at
`x = 0` read as its continuous value `q² / 2` (the [kernel](psi-kernel.md)); an atom of `Λ` at
`0` thus contributes `Λ{0} q² / 2` (Berestycki–Berestycki–Limic, eq. (3) with `ν = x^{-2} Λ`,
the `x = 0` term taken as in eq. (6)).
