# Growth of the Laplace exponent for a Beta pushforward, 1 < α < 2

Let `1 < α < 2` and let `Λ` be a finite measure on `[0, 1]` whose pushforward to `ℝ` is the
`Beta(2 - α, α)` distribution. Then the [Laplace exponent](psi.md) satisfies
`ψ(q) / q^α → 1 / (Γ(α) α (α - 1))` as `q → ∞`, that is `ψ(q) ∼ q^α / (Γ(α) α (α - 1))`.
