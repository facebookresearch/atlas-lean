# Under Grey's condition the speed inverts the tail integral

For a finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]` satisfying [Grey's condition](grey-condition.md) and every
`t > 0`, the [speed](speed.md) `v(t)` is positive and `u(v(t)) = t`, where
`u(s) = ∫_s^∞ dq / ψ(q)` is the [tail integral](psi-tail.md) of the
[Laplace exponent](psi.md) `ψ`.
