# Speed of coming down from infinity of Λ-coalescents

This node covers how fast the number of blocks of a Λ-coalescent that comes down from infinity decreases
near time $0$. With the [speed function](speed.md) $v$, the number of blocks $N_t$ satisfies
$N_t / v(t) \to 1$ almost surely as $t \downarrow 0$ ([almost sure asymptotics](the-number-of-blocks-of-a-coalescent-is-asymptot.md)) and, when
$\Lambda$ is not a multiple of $\delta_0$, in every $L^d$, $d \ge 1$, uniformly over small times
([moment convergence](moment-convergence-of-the-number-of-blocks-of-a.md)). Explicit speeds are $v(t) = 2/t$ for Kingman's coalescent and the
[power law for the Beta-coalescent](speed-of-coming-down-from-infinity-of-the-beta-c.md).

## Sources

- J. Berestycki, N. Berestycki and V. Limic, "The Λ-coalescent speed of coming down from infinity", Annals of Probability 38 (2010), 207–233, <https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOP475>, preprint <https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4278>, Section 3, Theorems 1 and 2 and the discussion of the Beta case.
