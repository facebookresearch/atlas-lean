# Growth of the Laplace exponent of the Beta-coalescent for 1 < α < 2

For `1 < α < 2`, the [Laplace exponent](psi.md) of the
[Beta(2 - α, α)-coalescent](../merger-rates-and-beta-coalescents/beta-lambda.md) satisfies
`ψ(q) / q^α → 1 / (Γ(α) α (α - 1))` as `q → ∞` (Berestycki–Berestycki–Limic, eq. (10)). The
Laplace exponent is defined because for these `α` the Beta-coalescent measure is a
[probability measure](../merger-rates-and-beta-coalescents/is-probability-measure-beta-lambda.md),
hence finite.
