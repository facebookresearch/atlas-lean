# Grey's condition makes the tail integral of 1/ψ finite and positive

For a finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]` satisfying [Grey's condition](grey-condition.md) and every
`t > 0`, `q ↦ 1 / ψ(q)` is integrable on `(t, ∞)` and `u(t) = ∫_t^∞ dq / ψ(q) > 0`, where `ψ`
is the [Laplace exponent](psi.md) (Berestycki–Berestycki–Limic, eq. (7)).
