# One-generation transition probability between sample partitions

For a [Cannings model](../cannings-models/cannings-model.md) `W` of population size `N`, a
[partition](../../partition-valued-kingman-coalescent/sample-partition.md) `ξ` of the sample
`{0, …, n - 1}` with `b ≤ N` blocks and a partition `η` of the sample, the probability, under the
law of the parent map, that [one generation](step.md) takes `ξ` to `η`, with the lineages of the
blocks of `ξ` at the children `0, …, b - 1`. It is the entry `Π_N(ξ, η)` of the
[ancestral matrix](ancestral-matrix.md).
