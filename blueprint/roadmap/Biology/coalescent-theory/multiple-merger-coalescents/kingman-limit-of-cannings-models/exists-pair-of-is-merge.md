# A merger forces one pair of lineages to share a parent

Let `ξ` be a [partition](../../partition-valued-kingman-coalescent/sample-partition.md) of the
sample `{0, …, n - 1}` with `b ≤ N` blocks, whose lineages sit at the children `0, …, b - 1`, and
let `η`
[arise from `ξ` by merging two blocks](../../partition-valued-kingman-coalescent/is-merge.md). If
[one generation](step.md) takes `ξ` to `η` under some parent map `f₀`, then there are block labels
`k < l` such that every parent map `f` under which one generation takes `ξ` to `η` has
`f(k) = f(l)`.
