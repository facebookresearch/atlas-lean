# Triple and double mergers are negligible under Möhle–Sagitov

Let the sequence of [Cannings models](../cannings-models/cannings-model.md) `W_N` satisfy the
[Möhle–Sagitov conditions](mohle-sagitov.md), with
[pair-coalescence probabilities](pair-coalescence.md) `c_N`. Then

`(p_3(N) + D_N) / c_N → 0` as `N → ∞`,

where `p_3(N) = N μ_3 / (N)_3` is the [triple-coalescence probability](triple-coalescence.md) and
`D_N = E[X²] / (N)_4` the [double-merger bound](double-bound.md) of `W_N`. Triple mergers and
simultaneous mergers of two disjoint pairs are thus negligible on the time scale `1 / c_N`; the
bound on simultaneous mergers follows from the triple-merger condition, without a condition of its
own.
