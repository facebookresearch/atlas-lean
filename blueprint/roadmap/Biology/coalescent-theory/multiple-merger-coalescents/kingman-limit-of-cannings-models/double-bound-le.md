# Upper bound on the double-merger bound by pair and triple mergers

Let `W` be a [Cannings model](../cannings-models/cannings-model.md) of population size `N ≥ 4` whose
[pair-coalescence probability](pair-coalescence.md) `c_N = μ_2 / (N - 1)` is positive. Then its
[double-merger bound](double-bound.md) satisfies

`E[X²] / (N)_4 ≤ c_N (2 √r N⁴ + N³) / (N)_4`, where `r = μ_3 / (N² c_N) ≥ 0`.

Here `μ_m = (1/N) ∑_j E[(ν_j)_m]` is the `m`-th [factorial moment](factorial-moment.md) of the
offspring numbers `ν_j`, `X = ∑_j ν_j (ν_j - 1)` is the [pair count](pair-count.md) and
`(N)_4 = N (N - 1) (N - 2) (N - 3)`.
