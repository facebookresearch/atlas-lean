# Kingman limits of resampling particle genealogies

Genealogies of weighted interacting particle systems, in which each generation is resampled from the previous one with probabilities proportional to weights (the mechanism of sequential Monte Carlo), behave like non-neutral Cannings models. Under a random time change driven by the pair-merger probability they converge to Kingman's $n$-coalescent, which predicts the height of particle-filter genealogies.
