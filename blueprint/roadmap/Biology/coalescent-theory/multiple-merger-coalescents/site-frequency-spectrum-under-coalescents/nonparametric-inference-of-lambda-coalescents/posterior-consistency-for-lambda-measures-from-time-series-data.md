# Posterior consistency for Lambda-measures from time-series data

**Setting.** A $\Lambda$-coalescent, parametrised by a probability measure $\Lambda$ on $[0,1]$, merges any $k$ of $p$ blocks at rate $\lambda_{p,k}=\Lambda(\{0\})\mathbf 1_{k=2}+\int_{(0,1]}(1-r)^{p-k}r^{k-2}\Lambda(dr)$; it is the genealogy of the $\Lambda$-Fleming–Viot process. Mutations occur at rate $\theta/2$ along lineages with a transition matrix on $d$ types, and the data are type counts $\mathbf n\in\mathbb N^d$ of samples of size $n$. A Bayesian prior $Q$ is placed on $\Lambda$.

**Theorem (contemporaneous data are uninformative).** If the sample is taken at a single time and $\mathbf x=\lim_n\mathbf n/n$ is the limiting relative type frequency vector, then

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}Q(A\mid\mathbf n)=\frac{\int_A\pi^\Lambda(\mathbf x)\,Q(d\Lambda)}{\int\pi^\Lambda(\mathbf x)\,Q(d\Lambda)},$$

where $\pi^\Lambda$ is the stationary density of type frequencies under $\Lambda$. This is positive for every Borel set $A$ with $\int_A\pi^\Lambda(\mathbf x)\,Q(d\Lambda)>0$, in particular for every $A$ with $Q(A)>0$ when $\pi^\Lambda(\mathbf x)>0$ for all $\Lambda$, whatever $\Lambda$ generated the data: no nontrivial prior is consistent. The source states positivity "for any $A \in \mathcal B(\mathcal M_1([0,1]))$ which intersects the support of $Q$" (Theorem 1). The page differs because a $Q$-null set can meet the support of $Q$, for example a singleton $\{\Lambda\}$ in the support of a prior without atoms, and then both integrals over $A$ vanish.

**Theorem (time series are informative).** Fix $\eta>0$ and let $D_\eta$ be a collection of Lebesgue probability densities $\phi$ on $[\eta,1]$ with $\inf_{[\eta,1]}\phi>0$ and $\sup_{[\eta,1]}\phi<\infty$, identified with the measures $\Lambda(dr)=\phi(r)\,dr$. Fix a sampling interval $\Delta>0$ and a finite Borel measure $\nu$ on the simplex $\{\mathbf x\in[0,1]^d:\sum_ix_i=1\}$ that charges every nonempty open set, and give $D_\eta$ the weak topology generated by the sets $\{\phi':\|P^{\phi'}_\Delta f-P^\phi_\Delta f\|_{L^1(\nu)}<\varepsilon\}$, $\phi\in D_\eta$, $\varepsilon>0$, $f$ continuous and bounded, where $P^\phi_\Delta$ is the transition semigroup at time $\Delta$ of the $\Lambda$-Fleming–Viot process. Let the prior $Q$ be concentrated on $D_\eta$, let the data-generating density satisfy $\phi_0\in D_\eta$, and observe $m+1$ samples of size $n$ at times $0,\Delta,\dots,m\Delta$ from the stationary process. If, for every $\varepsilon>0$ and every $\phi_0\in D_\eta$, the prior gives positive mass to

$$\Big\{\phi:\int_\eta^1\Big(\Big|\log\frac{\phi_0(r)}{\phi(r)}\Big|+\Big|\frac{\phi_0(r)}{\phi(r)}-1\Big|\Big)r^{-2}\phi_0(r)\,dr<\varepsilon\Big\},$$

Assume moreover that for $Q$-almost every pair $\varphi \ne \varphi_0$ in $D_\eta$ there exist $x$ in the simplex and a continuous bounded test function $f$ with $P^{\varphi_0}_\Delta f(x) \ne P^\varphi_\Delta f(x)$ (Assumption 1 of the published paper). then the posterior is weakly consistent: $Q(U^c\mid\mathbf n_0,\dots,\mathbf n_m)\to0$ almost surely under $\phi_0$ as $n,m\to\infty$, for every neighbourhood $U$ of $\phi_0$ open in this weak topology.

## Expansion

At one time point, the sample reveals (as $n\to\infty$) one draw $\mathbf x$ from the stationary law of the $\Lambda$-Fleming–Viot process, and a single draw cannot identify the generator, so the posterior converges to a nondegenerate reweighting of the prior. In a time series, the frequencies form a Markov chain with transition operator $P^\phi_\Delta$; different $\phi$ give different $P^\phi_\Delta f$ for some test function (identifiability), the operators are uniformly equicontinuous over the prior support, and the Kullback–Leibler-type prior condition then gives consistency by Schwartz-type arguments for ergodic Markov chains. Restricting to $[\eta,1]$ excludes Kingman components and makes the forward process a compound Poisson process with drift.
