# The transition probabilities of the jump chain sum to one

For a finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]`, every real `c` and every list `b` of block sizes, the
[transition operator](jump-step.md) applied to the constant statistic `c` gives `c` at `b`: when
`λ_{|b|} ≠ 0`, the transition probabilities `λ_{|b|,|K|} / λ_{|b|}` sum to `1`.
