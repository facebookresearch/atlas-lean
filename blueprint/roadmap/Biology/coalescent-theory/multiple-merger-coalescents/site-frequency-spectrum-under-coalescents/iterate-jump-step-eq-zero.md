# A statistic vanishing on absorbed states is zero after b jumps

Let `Λ` be a nonzero finite measure on `[0, 1]` and `g` a statistic of block-size lists with
`g(s) = 0` for every list `s` of fewer than two blocks. Then for every list `b` and every
`j ≥ |b|`, the `j`-th power of the [transition operator](jump-step.md) applied to `g` vanishes at
`b`: each jump removes at least one block, so after `|b|` jumps fewer than two blocks remain.
