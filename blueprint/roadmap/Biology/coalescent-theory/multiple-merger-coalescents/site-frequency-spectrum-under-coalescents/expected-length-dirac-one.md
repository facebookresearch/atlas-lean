# Expected branch lengths of the star coalescent

For the star coalescent, `Λ = δ₁`, started from `n ≥ 2` blocks of size `1`, all `n` lineages
merge at once, after an exponential time of mean `1`, so every branch subtends one copy: the
[expected length](expected-length.md) of branches subtending `i` copies is `E L_1 = n` and
`E L_i = 0` for every `i ≠ 1`.
