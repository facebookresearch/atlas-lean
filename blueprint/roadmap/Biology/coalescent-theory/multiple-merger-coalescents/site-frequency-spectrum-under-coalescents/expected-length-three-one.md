# Expected length of singleton branches for three copies

For a nonzero finite measure `Λ` on `[0, 1]` and a sample of three copies, the
[expected length](expected-length.md) of singleton branches is
`E L_1 = 3 / λ_3 + 3 λ_{3,2} / (λ_3 λ_2)`, where `λ_{3,2}` is the
[merger rate](../../coalescents/merger-rate.md), `λ_3` the
[total merger rate](../../coalescents/total-merger-rate.md) and `λ_2 = Λ([0, 1])`.
