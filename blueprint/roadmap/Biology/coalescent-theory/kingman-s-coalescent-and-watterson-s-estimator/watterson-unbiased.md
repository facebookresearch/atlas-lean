# Watterson's estimator is unbiased

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n \ge 2$ under a measure $\mu$ on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, with total branch length $L = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k\,T_k$. Let $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a sub-σ-algebra on which $\mu$ is σ-finite, let $\theta$ be a real number, and let $S$ be a $\mu$-integrable real function on $\Omega$ whose conditional expectation given $\mathcal{G}$ equals $\frac{\theta}{2} L$ $\mu$-almost everywhere. Then Watterson's estimator is unbiased:

$$\int \frac{S}{H_{n-1}} \, d\mu = \theta,$$

where $H_{n-1} = \sum_{j=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{j}$ is the $(n-1)$-st harmonic number.
