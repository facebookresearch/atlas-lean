# Probability of no coalescence in one Wright–Fisher generation

For natural numbers $M$ and $k$, let

$$P_{\mathrm{nc}}(M, k) = \prod_{i=0}^{k-1} \Bigl(1 - \frac{i}{M}\Bigr),$$

with the convention $i/0 = 0$, so that $P_{\mathrm{nc}}(0, k) = 1$. For $M \ge 1$ this is the probability that $k$ lineages in a Wright–Fisher population of $M$ gene copies, each choosing its parent independently and uniformly, have $k$ distinct parents.
