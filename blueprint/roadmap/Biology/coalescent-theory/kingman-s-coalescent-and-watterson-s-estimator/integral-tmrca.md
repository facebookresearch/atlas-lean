# Expected time to the most recent common ancestor

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n \ge 1$ under a measure $\mu$. The time to the most recent common ancestor $T_{\mathrm{MRCA}} = \sum_{k=2}^{n} T_k$ satisfies

$$\int T_{\mathrm{MRCA}} \, d\mu = 2\Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{n}\Bigr).$$
