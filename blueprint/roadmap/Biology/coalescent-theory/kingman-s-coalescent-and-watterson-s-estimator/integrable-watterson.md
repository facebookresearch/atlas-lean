# Integrability of Watterson's estimator

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $n$ a natural number and $S$ a $\mu$-integrable real function. Then Watterson's estimator $S / H_{n-1}$ is $\mu$-integrable, where $H_m$ is the $m$-th harmonic number, $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$, and division by $0$ gives $0$.
