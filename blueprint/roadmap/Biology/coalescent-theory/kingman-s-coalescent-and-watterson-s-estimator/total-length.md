# Total branch length of a coalescent genealogy

For Kingman holding times $(T_k)$ of a sample of size $n$ (see [Holding times of Kingman's coalescent](kingman-times.md)), the total branch length of the sample genealogy is the function $L = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k\,T_k$ on $\Omega$: during the holding time $T_k$ the genealogy has $k$ branches.
