# Time to the most recent common ancestor

For Kingman holding times $(T_k)$ of a sample of size $n$ (see [Holding times of Kingman's coalescent](kingman-times.md)), the time to the most recent common ancestor of the sample is the function $T_{\mathrm{MRCA}} = \sum_{k=2}^{n} T_k$ on $\Omega$, which is $0$ when $n < 2$.
