# Canonical probability space for Kingman holding times

For a natural number $n$, the product measure $\bigotimes_{k=2}^{n} \mathrm{Exp}\bigl(\binom{k}{2}\bigr)$ on $\mathbb{R}^{\{2, \dots, n\}}$, whose factor of index $k$ is the exponential distribution with rate $\binom{k}{2}$. It is a probability measure.
