# Variance of the time to the most recent common ancestor

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under a measure $\mu$. The variance under $\mu$ of the time to the most recent common ancestor $T_{\mathrm{MRCA}} = \sum_{k=2}^{n} T_k$ is

$$\operatorname{Var}_\mu(T_{\mathrm{MRCA}}) = \sum_{k=2}^{n} \binom{k}{2}^{-2}.$$

By independence the variances of the exponential holding times add up.
