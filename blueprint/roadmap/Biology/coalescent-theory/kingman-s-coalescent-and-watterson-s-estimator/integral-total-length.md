# Expected total branch length of the coalescent

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under a measure $\mu$. The total branch length $L = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k\,T_k$ satisfies

$$\int L \, d\mu = 2 H_{n-1},$$

where $H_m$ is the $m$-th harmonic number and $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$.
