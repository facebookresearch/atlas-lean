# Sample genealogies of critical birth–death processes

Genealogies of samples from critical branching populations, conditioned on the sample size, under a fixed, infinite or random time of origin. Their coalescent point processes have explicit i.i.d. node depths, explicit expected site frequency spectra, and a Poisson large-sample limit.
