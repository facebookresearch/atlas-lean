# Kingman coalescent from coordinate projections

For every natural number $n$, the coordinate functions $T_k(\omega) = \omega_k$ on $\mathbb{R}^{\{2, \dots, n\}}$ for $2 \le k \le n$, extended by $T_k = 0$ for the other $k$, are Kingman holding times for a sample of size $n$ under the product measure $\bigotimes_{k=2}^{n} \mathrm{Exp}\bigl(\binom{k}{2}\bigr)$. In particular, Kingman holding times exist for every sample size.
