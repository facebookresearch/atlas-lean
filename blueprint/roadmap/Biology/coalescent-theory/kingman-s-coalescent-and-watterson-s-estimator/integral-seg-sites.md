# Expected number of segregating sites

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under a measure $\mu$ on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, with total branch length $L = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k\,T_k$. Let $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a sub-σ-algebra on which $\mu$ is σ-finite, let $\theta$ be a real number, and let $S$ be a $\mu$-integrable real function on $\Omega$ whose conditional expectation given $\mathcal{G}$ equals $\frac{\theta}{2} L$ $\mu$-almost everywhere. Then

$$\int S \, d\mu = \theta H_{n-1},$$

where $H_m$ is the $m$-th harmonic number and $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$.

Under infinite-sites mutation at rate $\theta/2$ per lineage per unit time, $S$ is the number of segregating sites of the sample and $\mathcal{G}$ is the σ-algebra of the genealogy.
