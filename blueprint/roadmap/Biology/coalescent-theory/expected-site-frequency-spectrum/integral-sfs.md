# Expected site frequency spectrum

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $n$ a natural number. Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under $\mu$, each $T_k$ measurable, and let $\tau$ be a random list of block-size lists such that every preimage $\tau^{-1}(\{\mathbf{p}\})$ is in $\mathcal{F}$, with law given by the [random-joining law](random-joining.md) from $n$ singletons,

$$\mu\bigl(\tau = \mathbf{p}\bigr) = R_{n-1}\bigl((1, \dots, 1), \mathbf{p}\bigr) \quad \text{for every } \mathbf{p},$$

and such that $\tau$ and the family $(T_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ are independent under $\mu$. Let $\mathcal{G}$ be the σ-algebra generated by $\tau$ and the $T_k$, let $\theta$ be a real number, and let $\xi_i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$) be real random variables such that, for every $i$, $\xi_i$ is $\mu$-integrable and its conditional expectation given $\mathcal{G}$ equals $\frac{\theta}{2} L_i$ almost surely, where $L_i$ is the [total branch length subtending $i$ sample copies](sfs-length.md) computed from $\tau$ and $(T_k)$. Then for every $i$ with $1 \le i \le n - 1$,

$$\int \xi_i \, d\mu = \frac{\theta}{i}.$$

Here $\xi_i$ is the number of sites whose derived allele is carried by exactly $i$ of the $n$ sample copies under neutral infinite-sites mutation with $\theta = 4Nu$.
