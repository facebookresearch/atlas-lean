# Expected folded site frequency spectrum under Kingman's coalescent

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $n$ a natural
number. Let $(T_k)$ be [Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md)
of a sample of size $n$ under $\mu$: for $2 \le k \le n$, $T_k$ is exponential with rate
$\binom{k}{2}$, and $T_2, \dots, T_n$ are mutually independent. Suppose moreover that every $T_k$
($k \in \mathbb{N}$) is measurable, and let $\tau$ be a random list of block-size lists such
that every preimage $\tau^{-1}(A)$ of a set $A$ of such lists is in $\mathcal{F}$, with the
[random-joining law](random-joining.md) started from $n$ singletons,

$$\mu(\tau = \mathbf{p}) = R_{n-1}\bigl((1, \dots, 1), \mathbf{p}\bigr) \quad \text{for every }
\mathbf{p},$$

and such that $\tau$ and the family $(T_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ are independent under $\mu$. Let
$\mathcal{G}$ be the σ-algebra generated by $\tau$ and the $T_k$, let $\theta$ be a real number,
and let $\xi_j$ ($j \in \mathbb{N}$) be $\mu$-integrable real random variables whose conditional
expectation given $\mathcal{G}$ equals $\frac{\theta}{2} L_j$ almost surely, where $L_j$ is the
[total branch length subtending $j$ sample copies](sfs-length.md) computed from $\tau$ and
$(T_k)$. Then for every natural number $i$ with $i \ge 1$ and $2i \le n$, the
[folded count](folded-count.md) $\eta_i$ satisfies

$$\int \eta_i \, d\mu
= \frac{\theta \left(\frac{1}{i} + \frac{1}{n-i}\right)}{1 + \delta_{i,n-i}},$$

where $\delta_{i,n-i}$ is $1$ if $i = n - i$ and $0$ otherwise: the expectation is
$\theta/i + \theta/(n-i)$ when $2i < n$ and $\theta/i$ when $2i = n$.
