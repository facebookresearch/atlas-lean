# Total branch length subtending $i$ sample copies

For natural numbers $n$ and $i$, a list $\mathbf{p} = (p_0, p_1, \dots)$ of block-size lists and real numbers $T_k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$), let

$$L_i = \sum_{k=2}^{n} c_i(p_{n-k})\, T_k,$$

where $c_i(p)$ is the number of entries of the list $p$ equal to $i$, and $p_{n-k}$ is the empty list when $\mathbf{p}$ has no entry of index $n - k$. When $p_{n-k}$ lists the block sizes while the genealogy of $n$ sample copies has $k$ lineages and $T_k$ is the holding time with $k$ lineages, $L_i$ is the total length of the branches subtending exactly $i$ of the $n$ sample copies.
