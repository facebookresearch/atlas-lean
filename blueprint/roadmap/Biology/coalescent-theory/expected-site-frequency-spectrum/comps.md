# Compositions of $N$ into $k$ positive parts

For natural numbers $N$ and $k$, the finite set of functions $c : \{0, \dots, k-1\} \to \mathbb{N}$
with $c(t) \ge 1$ for every $t$ and $\sum_{t} c(t) = N$: the compositions of $N$ into $k$ positive
parts, the order of the parts counting. It is empty when $k > N$, and for $k = 0$ it consists of
the empty function if $N = 0$ and is empty otherwise.
