# Tavaré's probabilities sum to one

For every natural number $m \ge 1$ and every real $t$,

$$\sum_{j=1}^{m} q_{mj}(t) = 1,$$

where $q_{mj}(t)$ is [Tavaré's formula for the number of ancestral lineages](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/tavare-prob.md). For $t \ge 0$, from $m$ lineages the number of ancestors at time $t$ is one of $1, \dots, m$; the identity holds for every real $t$ because the block-counting generator kills the indicator of $\{1, \dots, n\}$.
