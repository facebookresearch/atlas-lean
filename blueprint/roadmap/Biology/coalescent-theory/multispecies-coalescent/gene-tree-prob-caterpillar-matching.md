# The matching gene tree

For all real $x$ and $y$, on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) $(((0, 1), 2), 3)$ with branch lengths $x$ and $y$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the matching gene tree $(((0, 1), 2), 3)$ the weight $f(x, y)$ of [Degnan and Rosenberg's equation (1)](dr-f.md).

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (1).
