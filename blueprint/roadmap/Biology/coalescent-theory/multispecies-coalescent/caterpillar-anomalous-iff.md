# Anomalous gene tree on four species

For every real $x > 0$ and every real $y$, on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with deeper branch $x$ and shallower branch $y$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the symmetric gene tree $((0, 1), (2, 3))$ a strictly larger weight than the gene tree whose clusters are those of the species tree if and only if $y < a(x)$, where $a$ is the [boundary of the anomaly zone](anomaly-a.md).

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
