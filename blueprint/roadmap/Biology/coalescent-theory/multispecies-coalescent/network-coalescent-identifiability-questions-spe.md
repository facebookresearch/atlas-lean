# Identifiability of networks under the network multispecies coalescent

This page covers a research programme: identifiability of the network topology and of the inheritance probabilities $\gamma$ from the distribution of gene trees, or of quartets, under the [network multispecies coalescent](network-multispecies-coalescent-fixed-network-ge.md), for explicitly stated classes of networks.

Each question fixes its network class and its data type, gene trees or quartet concordance factors, before it is stated.

## Expansion

Split the programme into separately stated results, one per question, each with its network class, data type and hypotheses fixed from a source. Each question is counted only once it is a bounded statement of its own.

Prerequisites:

- [Network multispecies coalescent: the gene-tree probability kernel of a fixed network](network-multispecies-coalescent-fixed-network-ge.md)

## Sources

- C. Meng and L. S. Kubatko, Detecting hybrid speciation in the presence of incomplete lineage sorting using gene tree incongruence: a model, *Theor. Popul. Biol.* 75 (2009), 35–45 (network coalescent).
