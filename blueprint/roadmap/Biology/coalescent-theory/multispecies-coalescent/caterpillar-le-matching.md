# Only balanced gene trees can be anomalous on the caterpillar

For all real $x \ge 0$ and $y \ge 0$ and every set of clusters $G$ on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ that is not a [balanced gene tree](is-balanced-gene-tree.md), the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with branch lengths $x$ and $y$ gives $G$ at most the weight of the matching gene tree $(((0, 1), 2), 3)$. So only the three balanced gene trees can be anomalous.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
