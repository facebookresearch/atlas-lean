# The boundary $a(x)$ of the anomaly zone

For every real $x > 0$ and every real $y$, the [Degnan–Rosenberg](dr-f.md) [probabilities](dr-h.md) satisfy $f(x, y) < h(x, y)$ if and only if $y < a(x)$, where $a$ is the [boundary of the anomaly zone](anomaly-a.md). So for a deeper internal branch $x > 0$ and a shallower branch $y \ge 0$, the symmetric gene tree $((A, B), (C, D))$ is more probable than the matching gene tree exactly when $y < a(x)$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
