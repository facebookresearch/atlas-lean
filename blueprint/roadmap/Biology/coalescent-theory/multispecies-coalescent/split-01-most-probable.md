# The species quartet maximizes quartet support

Let $x$, $y$, $y_1$ and $y_2$ be real numbers with $y > 0$ and $y_1 + y_2 > 0$. Under the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md), the gene trees [displaying the quartet split](displays-split.md) $01 \mid 23$ of the species tree have strictly larger total weight than those displaying $02 \mid 13$, and than those displaying $03 \mid 12$, both on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with branch lengths $x$ and $y$ and on the [balanced species tree](balanced.md) with branch lengths $y_1$ and $y_2$. For nonnegative branch lengths these weights are probabilities; since the expected quartet support of a candidate four-species tree is then the probability of its quartet, the species tree maximizes it.

## Sources

- E. S. Allman, J. H. Degnan and J. A. Rhodes, *Identifying the rooted species tree from the distribution of unrooted gene trees under the coalescent*, J. Math. Biol. 62 (2011), 833–862.
- S. Mirarab, R. Reaz, Md. S. Bayzid, T. Zimmermann, M. S. Swenson and T. Warnow, *ASTRAL: genome-scale coalescent-based species tree estimation*, Bioinformatics 30 (2014), i541–i548.
