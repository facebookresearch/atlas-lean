# Degnan–Rosenberg probability of the matching caterpillar gene tree

For real numbers $x$ and $y$,

$$f(x, y) = 1 - \frac{2}{3} e^{-x} - \frac{2}{3} e^{-y} + \frac{1}{3} e^{-(x+y)} + \frac{1}{18} e^{-(3x+y)},$$

equation (1) of Degnan and Rosenberg (2006): for $x, y \ge 0$, the probability of the matching gene tree $(((A, B), C), D)$ on the caterpillar species tree with branch lengths $x$ and $y$ ([The matching gene tree](gene-tree-prob-caterpillar-matching.md)).

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (1).
