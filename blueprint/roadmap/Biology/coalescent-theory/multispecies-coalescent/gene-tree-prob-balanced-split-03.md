# Probability of quartet split 03|12 on the balanced tree

For all real $y_1$ and $y_2$, on the [balanced species tree](balanced.md) $((0, 1), (2, 3))$ with branch lengths $y_1$ and $y_2$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene trees that [display the discordant quartet split](displays-split.md) $03 \mid 12$ the total weight $\frac{1}{3} e^{-(y_1 + y_2)}$.
