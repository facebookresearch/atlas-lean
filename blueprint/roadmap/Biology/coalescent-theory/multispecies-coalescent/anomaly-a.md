# Boundary of the four-species anomaly zone

For a real number $x$,

$$a(x) = \log\Bigl(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{3 e^{2x} - 2}{18\,(e^{3x} - e^{2x})}\Bigr),$$

with the conventions that division by $0$ gives $0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log u = \log|u|$ for $u < 0$; in particular $a(0) = \log(2/3)$. This is equation (4) of Degnan and Rosenberg (2006), the boundary of the four-species anomaly zone, meaningful for a deeper internal branch of length $x > 0$, where the argument of the logarithm exceeds $2/3$ and $a(x) \to \infty$ as $x \to 0^+$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (4).
