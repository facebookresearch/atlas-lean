# Probability of quartet split 02|13 on the caterpillar tree

For all real $x$ and $y$, on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with branch lengths $x$ and $y$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene trees that [display the discordant quartet split](displays-split.md) $02 \mid 13$ the total weight $\frac{1}{3} e^{-y}$.
