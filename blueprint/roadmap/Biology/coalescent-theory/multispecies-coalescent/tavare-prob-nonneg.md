# Tavaré's probabilities are nonnegative

For natural numbers $1 \le j \le m$ and every real $t \ge 0$, [Tavaré's probability](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/tavare-prob.md) satisfies $q_{mj}(t) \ge 0$. The transition matrix of the block-counting process at time $t \ge 0$ is the entrywise limit of powers of the Wright–Fisher transition matrix of the lineage count, whose entries are nonnegative. For $t < 0$ the values can be negative or exceed one.
