# Clade probability under random joining for at most five genes

For every natural number $m \le 5$ and every set $S$ of $k$ genes in $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ with $1 \le k < m$, the probability under random joining that $S$ is a [clade](is-clade.md) is

$$\frac{2m}{k(k+1)\binom{m}{k}}.$$

This is the case $m \le 5$ of the [clade probability](random-joining-is-clade.md).
