# Expectation under random joining as an average over histories

For every natural number $m$ and every real function $f$ on the ranked histories of $m$ lineages, the expectation of $f$ under random joining is the average of $f$ over all ranked histories:

$$\int f \, d\rho_m = \frac{\sum_{h} f(h)}{\prod_{k=2}^{m} \binom{k}{2}},$$

where the sum runs over the $\prod_{k=2}^{m} \binom{k}{2}$ ranked histories of $m$ lineages.
