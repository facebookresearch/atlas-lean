# Anomaly-zone boundary blows up for short deep branches

The [boundary of the anomaly zone](anomaly-a.md) satisfies $a(x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to 0$ with $x > 0$. So a very short deeper internal branch produces an anomalous gene tree however long the shallower branch is.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
