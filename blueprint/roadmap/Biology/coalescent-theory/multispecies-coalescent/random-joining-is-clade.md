# Clade probability

For every natural number $m$ and every set $S$ of $k$ genes in $\{0, \dots, m-1\}$ with $1 \le k < m$, the probability under random joining that $S$ is a [clade](is-clade.md) is

$$\rho_m\bigl(\{h : S \text{ is a clade of } h\}\bigr) = \frac{2m}{k(k+1)\binom{m}{k}}.$$

## Sources

- N. A. Rosenberg, *The shapes of neutral gene genealogies in two species: probabilities of monophyly, paraphyly, and polyphyly in a coalescent model*, Evolution 57 (2003), 1465–1477.
