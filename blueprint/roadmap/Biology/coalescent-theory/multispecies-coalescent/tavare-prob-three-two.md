# Three lineages have two ancestors at time $t$

For every real $t$, [Tavaré's formula](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/tavare-prob.md) gives

$$q_{32}(t) = \frac{3}{2}\bigl(e^{-t} - e^{-3t}\bigr).$$

For $t \ge 0$ this is the probability that three lineages have two ancestors at time $t$.
