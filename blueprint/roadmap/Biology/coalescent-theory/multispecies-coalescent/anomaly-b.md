# Boundary for three anomalous gene trees

For a real number $x$,

$$b(x) = \log\Bigl(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{5 e^{2x} - 2}{6\,(3 e^{3x} - 2 e^{2x})}\Bigr),$$

with the conventions that division by $0$ gives $0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log u = \log|u|$ for $u < 0$. This is equation (5) of Degnan and Rosenberg (2006), meaningful for $x \ge 0$, where the argument of the logarithm exceeds $2/3$: below $b(x)$ the caterpillar species tree has three anomalous gene trees.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (5).
