# No anomalous unrooted quartets, balanced species tree

For all real $y_1$ and $y_2$, on the [balanced species tree](balanced.md) $((0, 1), (2, 3))$ with branch lengths $y_1$ and $y_2$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene trees that [display the quartet split](displays-split.md) $01 \mid 23$ the total weight

$$1 - \frac{2}{3} e^{-(y_1 + y_2)},$$

where $y_1 + y_2$ is the length of the internal edge of the unrooted species quartet.

## Sources

- E. S. Allman, J. H. Degnan and J. A. Rhodes, *Identifying the rooted species tree from the distribution of unrooted gene trees under the coalescent*, J. Math. Biol. 62 (2011), 833–862.
