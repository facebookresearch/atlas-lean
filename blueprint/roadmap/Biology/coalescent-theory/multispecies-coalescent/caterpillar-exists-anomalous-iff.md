# The four-species anomaly zone

For all real $x > 0$ and $y \ge 0$, the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with deeper branch $x$ and shallower branch $y$ has an anomalous gene tree, one to which the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives a strictly larger weight than to the gene tree whose clusters are those of the species tree, if and only if $y < a(x)$, where $a$ is the [boundary of the anomaly zone](anomaly-a.md).

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, Definition 1 and Figure 2.
