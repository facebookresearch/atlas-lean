# Value $\log(7/6)$ of the three-anomaly boundary at zero

The [boundary for three anomalous gene trees](anomaly-b.md) satisfies $b(0) = \log(7/6)$. With [Three-anomaly boundary is maximal at zero](anomaly-b-le-anomaly-b-zero.md), every $y \ge \log(7/6)$ satisfies $y \ge b(x)$ for all $x \ge 0$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
