# Merging lineages from singletons gives the ancestral blocks

For every natural number $n$, every ranked history $h$ of $n$ lineages and every natural number $t$, applying the first $t$ coalescence events of $h$ ([Lineages after the first coalescence events of a ranked history](merge-along.md)) to the list of singletons $\{0\}, \dots, \{n-1\}$ gives the list of blocks of sampled genes after the first $t$ coalescence events of $h$.
