# Probability of the discordant three-species gene tree with cherry $\{1, 2\}$

With one lineage per species and internal branch length $t$, the gene tree of the species tree
$((0, 1), 2)$ whose cherry is $\{1, 2\}$ has probability $\frac{1}{3} e^{-t}$. (An identity for every
real $t$; branch lengths are $t \ge 0$.)
