# Probability of the discordant three-species gene tree with cherry $\{1, 2\}$

For every real $t$, on the [three-species tree](triple.md) $((0, 1), 2)$ with internal branch length $t$ and one lineage per species, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene tree with cherry $\{1, 2\}$ the probability $\frac{1}{3} e^{-t}$. The identity holds for every real $t$; it is a probability when $t \ge 0$.
