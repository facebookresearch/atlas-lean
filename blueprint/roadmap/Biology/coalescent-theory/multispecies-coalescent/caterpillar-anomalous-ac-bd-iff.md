# Anomaly of ((A, C), (B, D)) and ((A, D), (B, C)) on the caterpillar

For every real $x \ge 0$ and every real $y$, on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with deeper branch $x$ and shallower branch $y$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene tree $((0, 2), (1, 3))$ a strictly larger weight than the gene tree whose clusters are those of the species tree if and only if $y < b(x)$, where $b$ is the [boundary for three anomalous gene trees](anomaly-b.md). The gene tree $((0, 3), (1, 2))$ has the same weight.
