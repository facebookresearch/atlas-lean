# Clusters formed along a ranked history

For a list $b$ of lineages (sets of sampled genes), a ranked history $h$ of $m$ lineages and a natural number $t$, the clusters formed along $h$ during the first $t$ events are the sets of sampled genes that occur as a lineage after the first $u$ events of $h$ started from $b$ ([Lineages after the first coalescence events of a ranked history](merge-along.md)), for some $u \le t$.
