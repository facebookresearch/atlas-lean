# Balanced four-species tree

For real numbers $y_1$ and $y_2$, the [species tree](species-tree.md) $((0, 1), (2, 3))$ on the species $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ whose internal branches above the ancestor of $0, 1$ and above the ancestor of $2, 3$ have lengths $y_1$ and $y_2$ (the length stored at the root is $0$).
