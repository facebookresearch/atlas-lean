# Rational-valued expectation under random joining

For every natural number $m$ and every rational-valued function $q$ on the ranked histories of $m$ lineages, the expectation of $q$ under random joining is the rational number

$$\int q \, d\rho_m = \frac{\sum_{h} q(h)}{\prod_{k=2}^{m} \binom{k}{2}},$$

the sum running over all ranked histories of $m$ lineages. This reduces random-joining probabilities to exact rational computations.
