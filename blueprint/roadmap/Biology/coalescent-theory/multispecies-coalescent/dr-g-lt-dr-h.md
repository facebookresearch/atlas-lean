# $g(x, y) < h(x, y)$ for a positive shallower branch

For every real $x$ and every real $y > 0$, the [Degnan–Rosenberg](dr-g.md) [probabilities](dr-h.md) satisfy $g(x, y) < h(x, y)$; for $x \ge 0$, on the caterpillar species tree with a positive shallower branch, the symmetric gene tree $((A, B), (C, D))$ is strictly more probable than $((A, C), (B, D))$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
