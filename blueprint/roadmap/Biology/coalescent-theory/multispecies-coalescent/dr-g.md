# Degnan–Rosenberg formula g for the gene tree ((A, C), (B, D))

For real numbers $x$ and $y$,

$$g(x, y) = \frac{1}{6} e^{-(x+y)} - \frac{1}{18} e^{-(3x+y)},$$

equation (2) of Degnan and Rosenberg (2006): for $x, y \ge 0$, the probability of each of the gene trees $((A, C), (B, D))$ and $((A, D), (B, C))$ on the caterpillar species tree with branch lengths $x$ and $y$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (2).
