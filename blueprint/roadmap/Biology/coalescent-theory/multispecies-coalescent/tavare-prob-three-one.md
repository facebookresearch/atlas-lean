# Three lineages have one ancestor at time $t$

For every real $t$, [Tavaré's formula](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/tavare-prob.md) gives

$$q_{31}(t) = 1 - \frac{3}{2} e^{-t} + \frac{1}{2} e^{-3t}.$$

For $t \ge 0$ this is the probability that three lineages have one ancestor at time $t$.
