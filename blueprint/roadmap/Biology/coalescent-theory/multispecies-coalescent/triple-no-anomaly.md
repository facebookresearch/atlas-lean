# No anomalous gene trees for three species

For every real $t > 0$, on the [three-species tree](triple.md) $((0, 1), 2)$ with internal branch length $t$, each of the two discordant gene trees, with cherry $\{0, 2\}$ or $\{1, 2\}$, is strictly less probable under the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) than the gene tree whose clusters are those of the species tree.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
