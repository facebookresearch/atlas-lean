# Three-species concordance

For every real $t$, on the [three-species tree](triple.md) $((0, 1), 2)$ with internal branch length $t$ and one lineage per species, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene tree with cherry $\{0, 1\}$, which matches the species tree, the probability

$$1 - \frac{2}{3} e^{-t}.$$

The identity holds for every real $t$; it is a probability when $t \ge 0$.

## Sources

- R. R. Hudson, *Testing the constant-rate neutral allele model with protein sequence data*, Evolution 37 (1983), 203–217.
- P. Pamilo and M. Nei, *Relationships between gene trees and species trees*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 5 (1988), 568–583.
