# Caterpillar four-species tree

For real numbers $x$ and $y$, the [species tree](species-tree.md) $(((0, 1), 2), 3)$ on the species $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ whose deeper internal branch, above the ancestor of $0, 1, 2$, has length $x$ and whose shallower internal branch, above the ancestor of $0, 1$, has length $y$ (the length stored at the root is $0$), as in Degnan and Rosenberg (2006).

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
