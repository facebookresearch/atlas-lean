# No anomalous gene trees on the balanced four-species tree

For all real $y_1 \ge 0$ and $y_2 \ge 0$ and every set of clusters $G$ on $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) on the [balanced species tree](balanced.md) $((0, 1), (2, 3))$ with branch lengths $y_1$ and $y_2$ gives $G$ at most the weight of the matching gene tree $((0, 1), (2, 3))$. Given the numbers of lineages entering the root population, the matching tree is already the most probable outcome of random joining.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68.
