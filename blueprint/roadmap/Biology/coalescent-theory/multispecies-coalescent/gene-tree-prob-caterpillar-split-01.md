# No anomalous unrooted quartets, caterpillar species tree

For all real $x$ and $y$, on the [caterpillar species tree](caterpillar.md) with branch lengths $x$ and $y$, the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) gives the gene trees that [display the quartet split](displays-split.md) $01 \mid 23$ of the species tree the total weight

$$1 - \frac{2}{3} e^{-y},$$

where $y$, the shallower internal branch, is the length of the internal edge of the unrooted species quartet.

## Sources

- E. S. Allman, J. H. Degnan and J. A. Rhodes, *Identifying the rooted species tree from the distribution of unrooted gene trees under the coalescent*, J. Math. Biol. 62 (2011), 833–862.
