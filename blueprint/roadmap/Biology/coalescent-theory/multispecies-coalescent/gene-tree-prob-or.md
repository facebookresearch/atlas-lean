# Additivity of gene-tree probabilities

Let $T$ be a [species tree](species-tree.md) and $E$, $F$ two disjoint sets of gene trees. Then the [multispecies coalescent](gene-tree-prob.md) satisfies $P_T(E \cup F) = P_T(E) + P_T(F)$, for arbitrary real branch lengths. With [nonnegativity](gene-tree-prob-nonneg.md) and [normalization](gene-tree-prob-true.md), $P_T$ is a probability law on gene trees when $T$ has nonnegative branch lengths.
