# Degnan–Rosenberg formula h for the gene tree ((A, B), (C, D))

For real numbers $x$ and $y$,

$$h(x, y) = \frac{1}{3} e^{-x} - \frac{1}{6} e^{-(x+y)} - \frac{1}{18} e^{-(3x+y)},$$

equation (3) of Degnan and Rosenberg (2006): for $x, y \ge 0$, the probability of the gene tree $((A, B), (C, D))$ on the caterpillar species tree with branch lengths $x$ and $y$.

## Sources

- J. H. Degnan and N. A. Rosenberg, *Discordance of species trees with their most likely gene trees*, PLoS Genet. 2 (2006), e68, equation (3).
