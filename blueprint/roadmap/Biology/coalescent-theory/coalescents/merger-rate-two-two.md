# Pair merger rate of a Λ-coalescent

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$. Its [merger rate](merger-rate.md) for two blocks is the total mass of $\Lambda$: $\lambda_{2,2} = \Lambda([0, 1])$. When $\Lambda$ is normalized to a probability measure, two blocks merge at rate $1$, as in Kingman's coalescent.
