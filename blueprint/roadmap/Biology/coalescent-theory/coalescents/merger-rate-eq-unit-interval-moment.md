# Merger rates are mixed moments of Λ for 2 ≤ k ≤ b

Let `Λ` be a finite measure on `[0, 1]`. For natural numbers `k` and `b` with `2 ≤ k ≤ b`, the
[merger rate](merger-rate.md) `λ_{b,k}` is the mixed moment of `Λ` of orders `k - 2` and `b - k`:

`λ_{b,k} = ∫_{[0,1]} x^{k-2} (1 - x)^{b-k} Λ(dx)`,

with natural-number powers, so `0^0 = 1`. Outside `2 ≤ k ≤ b` the merger rate is `0` by
definition.
