# Merger rates of the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent

Let $\Lambda$ be Lebesgue measure on $[0, 1]$. For natural numbers $2 \le k \le b$, its [merger rate](merger-rate.md) is the Beta integral

$$\lambda_{b,k} = \frac{(k-2)!\,(b-k)!}{(b-1)!}.$$

The Λ-coalescent with this $\Lambda$ is the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent.
