# Total merger rate of a Λ-coalescent

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$ with [merger rates](merger-rate.md) $\lambda_{b,k}$. For a natural number $b$, the total merger rate is

$$\lambda_b = \sum_{k=2}^{b} \binom{b}{k} \lambda_{b,k},$$

the total rate of merger events while there are $b$ blocks (an empty sum, $0$, when $b < 2$).

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Coalescents with multiple collisions*, Ann. Probab. 27 (1999), 1870–1902.
- N. Berestycki, *Recent progress in coalescent theory*, Ensaios Matemáticos 16 (2009), §3.1.
