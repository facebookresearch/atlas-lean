# Kingman's coalescent

The [merger rates](merger-rate.md) of the Dirac measure $\delta_0$ at $0$ are $\lambda_{b,k} = 1$ if $k = 2$ and $b \ge 2$, and $\lambda_{b,k} = 0$ otherwise. So the Λ-coalescent with $\Lambda = \delta_0$ is Kingman's coalescent: only binary mergers occur, each pair of blocks merging at rate $1$.

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Coalescents with multiple collisions*, Ann. Probab. 27 (1999), 1870–1902.
- N. Berestycki, *Recent progress in coalescent theory*, Ensaios Matemáticos 16 (2009), §3.1.
