# Uniqueness in Pitman's representation

Let $\Lambda$ and $\Lambda'$ be finite measures on $[0, 1]$ whose [merger rates](merger-rate.md) of complete mergers agree: $\lambda_{k,k}(\Lambda) = \lambda_{k,k}(\Lambda')$ for every natural number $k \ge 2$. Then $\Lambda = \Lambda'$. The rates $\lambda_{k,k} = \int x^{k-2} \, \Lambda(dx)$ are the moments of $\Lambda$, which determine a finite measure on $[0, 1]$; in particular a finite measure on $[0, 1]$ is determined by its merger rates.

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Coalescents with multiple collisions*, Ann. Probab. 27 (1999), 1870–1902, Theorem 1.
