# Pitman's consistency relation

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$ with [merger rates](merger-rate.md) $\lambda_{b,k}$. For natural numbers $2 \le k \le b$,

$$\lambda_{b,k} = \lambda_{b+1,k} + \lambda_{b+1,k+1}.$$

A given set of $k$ among $b$ blocks merges, when an extra block is present, either without the extra block or together with it.

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Coalescents with multiple collisions*, Ann. Probab. 27 (1999), 1870–1902.
- N. Berestycki, *Recent progress in coalescent theory*, Ensaios Matemáticos 16 (2009), §3.1.
