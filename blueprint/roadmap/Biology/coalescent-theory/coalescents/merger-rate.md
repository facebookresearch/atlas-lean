# Pitman's merger rate

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$. For natural numbers $b$ and $k$, the merger rate of $\Lambda$ is

$$\lambda_{b,k} = \int_{[0,1]} x^{k-2} (1 - x)^{b-k} \, \Lambda(dx) \quad \text{if } 2 \le k \le b,$$

with natural-number exponents (so $0^0 = 1$), and $\lambda_{b,k} = 0$ otherwise. While a Λ-coalescent has $b$ blocks, $\lambda_{b,k}$ is the rate at which a given set of $k$ of them merges into one block.

## Sources

- J. Pitman, *Coalescents with multiple collisions*, Ann. Probab. 27 (1999), 1870–1902, Theorem 1.
- S. Sagitov, *The general coalescent with asynchronous mergers of ancestral lines*, J. Appl. Probab. 36 (1999), 1116–1125.
