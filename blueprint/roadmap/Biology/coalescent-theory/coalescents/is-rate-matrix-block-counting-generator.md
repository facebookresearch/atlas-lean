# Λ-coalescent block-counting generator is a rate matrix

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$ and $n$ a natural number. The [block-counting generator](block-counting-generator.md) of the Λ-coalescent on $\{0, \dots, n\}$ is a rate matrix: its off-diagonal entries are nonnegative and each of its rows sums to $0$. The row sums vanish because the jump rates out of $b$ blocks add up to the total merger rate $\lambda_b$.
