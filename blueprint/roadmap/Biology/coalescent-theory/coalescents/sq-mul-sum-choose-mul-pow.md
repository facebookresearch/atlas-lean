# Binomial identity behind the total merger rate

For every real number $x$ and every natural number $b$,

$$x^2 \sum_{k=2}^{b} \binom{b}{k} x^{k-2} (1 - x)^{b-k} = 1 - (1 - x)^{b} - b\,x\,(1 - x)^{b-1},$$

with natural-number exponents, where $b - 1$ is read as $0$ when $b = 0$. It follows from the binomial theorem.
