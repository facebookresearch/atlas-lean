# Recursion for the total merger rate

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite measure on $[0, 1]$ with [merger rates](merger-rate.md) $\lambda_{b,k}$ and [total merger rates](total-merger-rate.md) $\lambda_b$. For every natural number $b$,

$$\lambda_{b+1} = \lambda_b + b\,\lambda_{b+1,2}.$$

Adding a block adds exactly the binary mergers of the new block with one of the $b$ old ones, a consequence of the consistency relation.
