# Canonical probability space for the skyline model

For a natural number $n$ and real numbers $\theta_k$ with $\theta_k > 0$ for $2 \le k \le n$, the product measure $\bigotimes_{k=2}^{n} \mathrm{Exp}\bigl(\binom{k}{2}/\theta_k\bigr)$ on $\mathbb{R}^{\{2, \dots, n\}}$, whose factor of index $k$ is the exponential distribution with rate $\binom{k}{2}/\theta_k$.
