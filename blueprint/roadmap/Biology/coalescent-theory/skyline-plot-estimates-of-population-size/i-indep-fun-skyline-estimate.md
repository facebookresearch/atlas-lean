# The skyline estimates are independent

Let $(T_k)$ be [coalescent intervals under the skyline model](skyline-times.md) of a sample of size $n$ with parameters $\theta$ under a measure $\mu$. The [skyline estimates](skyline-estimate.md) $\binom{k}{2} T_k$, $2 \le k \le n$, are mutually independent under $\mu$.
