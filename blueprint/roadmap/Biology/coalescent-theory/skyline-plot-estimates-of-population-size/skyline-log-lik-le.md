# The skyline estimate maximizes the interval likelihood

For real numbers $c > 0$, $w > 0$ and $\theta > 0$, the [interval log-likelihood](skyline-log-lik.md) satisfies $\ell(\theta) \le \ell(c\,w)$.

## Sources

- O. G. Pybus, A. Rambaut and P. H. Harvey, *An integrated framework for the inference of viral population history from reconstructed genealogies*, Genetics 155 (2000), 1429–1437.
