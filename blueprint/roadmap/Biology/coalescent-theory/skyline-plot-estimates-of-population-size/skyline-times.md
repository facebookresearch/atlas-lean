# Coalescent intervals under the classic skyline model

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, $n$ a natural number and $\theta_k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$) real numbers. Coalescent intervals of a sample of size $n$ under the classic skyline model with parameters $\theta$ are real functions $T_k$ on $\Omega$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, such that

- $\theta_k > 0$ for every $2 \le k \le n$;
- for every $2 \le k \le n$, the law of $T_k$ under $\mu$ is the exponential distribution with rate $\binom{k}{2}/\theta_k$;
- $T_2, \dots, T_n$ are mutually independent under $\mu$.

Here $T_k$ is the length of the interval with $k$ lineages and $\theta_k$ the population-size parameter during that interval.
