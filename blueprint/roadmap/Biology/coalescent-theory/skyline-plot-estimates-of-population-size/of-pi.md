# Coordinate projections form a skyline model

For a natural number $n$ and real numbers $\theta_k$ with $\theta_k > 0$ for $2 \le k \le n$, the coordinate functions $T_k(\omega) = \omega_k$ on $\mathbb{R}^{\{2, \dots, n\}}$ for $2 \le k \le n$, extended by $T_k = 0$ for the other $k$, are [coalescent intervals under the skyline model](skyline-times.md) with parameters $\theta$ under the [product measure](skyline-pi-measure.md) $\bigotimes_{k=2}^{n} \mathrm{Exp}\bigl(\binom{k}{2}/\theta_k\bigr)$. In particular such intervals exist whenever the parameters are positive.
