# Variance of the skyline estimate

Let $(T_k)$ be [coalescent intervals under the skyline model](skyline-times.md) of a sample of size $n$ with parameters $\theta$ under a measure $\mu$. For every $2 \le k \le n$, the variance under $\mu$ of the [skyline estimate](skyline-estimate.md) $\binom{k}{2} T_k$ is $\theta_k^2$.
