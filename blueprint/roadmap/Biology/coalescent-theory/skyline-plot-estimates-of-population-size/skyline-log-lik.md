# Log-likelihood of one coalescent interval in the skyline model

For real numbers $c$, $w$ and $\theta$, let

$$\ell(\theta) = \log\frac{c}{\theta} - \frac{c\,w}{\theta},$$

with the conventions that division by $0$ gives $0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$. For $c = \binom{k}{2}$ with $k \ge 2$, $\theta > 0$ and $w \ge 0$, it is the log-density at $w$ of the exponential distribution with rate $c/\theta$: the log-likelihood of $\theta$ for one coalescent interval of length $w$ with $k$ lineages.
