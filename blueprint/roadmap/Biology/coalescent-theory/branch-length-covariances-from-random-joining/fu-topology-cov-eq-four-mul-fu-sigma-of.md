# Fu's topology identity for samples of size at most four

For every natural number `n ≤ 4` and all `1 ≤ i, j ≤ n - 1`, the
[moment sum](fu-topology-cov.md) of the block counts of random joining and the Kingman holding
times is four times
[Fu's closed form](../fu-s-covariances-of-the-site-frequency-spectrum/fu-sigma.md):

`F_n(i, j) = 4 σ_{ij}`.

Both sides are computed from their definitions; `n = 4` already meets all six cases of Fu's
formula. It is the case `n ≤ 4` of the
[identity for every sample size](../fu-s-branch-length-covariances-for-every-sample/fu-topology-cov-eq-four-mul-fu-sigma.md).
