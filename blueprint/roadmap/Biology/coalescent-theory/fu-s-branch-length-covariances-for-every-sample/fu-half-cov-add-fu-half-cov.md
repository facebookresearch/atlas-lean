# Fu's closed form from the half covariances

For natural numbers `n`, `i` and `j` with `1 ≤ i ≤ j < n`,

`h_n(i, j) + h_n(j, i) - (2 / i)(2 / j) = 4 σ_{ij}`,

where `h_n` is [Fu's half covariance](fu-half-cov.md) and `σ_{ij}` is
[Fu's closed form](../fu-s-covariances-of-the-site-frequency-spectrum/fu-sigma.md) of the branch-length covariances.
