# Harmonic numbers

For a natural number `m`, the harmonic number is the real number `H_m = ∑_{k=1}^{m} 1/k`; in
particular `H_0 = 0` (empty sum). It is the image in the reals of the rational harmonic number $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k+1}$, by the reindexing $j = k + 1$ and the embedding of the rationals in the reals term by term.
