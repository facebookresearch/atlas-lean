# Bhaskar–Song identifiability

Let $\eta$ and $\eta'$ be the [size functions](size-fn.md) of two [piecewise-constant histories](piecewise-history.md) with $K$ pieces, and let $n \ge 2K$ be a natural number. If their [first-coalescence times](first-coal-mean.md) agree, $c_m(\eta) = c_m(\eta')$ for every $2 \le m \le n$, then $\eta(s) = \eta'(s)$ for every real $s \ge 0$. The proof writes $\binom{m}{2} c_m$ as an exponential sum in $\binom{m}{2}$ over at most $2K - 1$ rates ([closed form](choose-two-mul-first-coal-mean.md)), identifies its coefficients, recovers the size as a function of coalescent time ([dual history](dual.md)) and inverts the cumulative rate.

## Sources

- A. Bhaskar and Y. S. Song, *Descartes' rule of signs and the identifiability of population demographic models from genomic variation data*, Ann. Statist. 42 (2014), 2469–2493; arXiv:1309.5056, Corollary 7.
