# Fay and Wu's H statistic

For a natural number $n$ and real functions $\xi_i$ on a set $\Omega$, Fay and Wu's statistic is $H = \theta_\pi - \theta_H$, the difference of [Tajima's $\theta_\pi$](theta-pi.md) and [Fay and Wu's $\theta_H$](theta-h.md).

## Sources

- J. C. Fay and C.-I. Wu, *Hitchhiking under positive Darwinian selection*, Genetics 155 (2000), 1405–1413.
