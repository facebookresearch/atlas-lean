# Dual history in coalescent time

For a [piecewise-constant history](piecewise-history.md) with $K$ pieces, sizes $\nu_j$, start times $s_j$ and [size function](size-fn.md) $\eta$, the dual history is the piecewise-constant history with $K$ pieces whose piece $j$ has size $1/\nu_j$ and starts at the coalescent time $R_\eta(s_j)$, with the [cumulative rate](cum-rate.md) $R_\eta$.
