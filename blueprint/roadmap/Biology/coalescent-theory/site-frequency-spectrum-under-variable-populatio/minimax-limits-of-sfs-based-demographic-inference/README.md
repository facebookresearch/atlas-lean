# Minimax limits of SFS-based demographic inference

Information-theoretic lower bounds on how accurately any estimator can recover a population-size history from the sample frequency spectrum (SFS) of $s$ independent segregating sites under Kingman's coalescent. After a bottleneck, histories that differ in the deeper past induce nearly identical SFS distributions, so the minimax error decays no faster than $1/\log s$, regardless of the number of sampled individuals.
