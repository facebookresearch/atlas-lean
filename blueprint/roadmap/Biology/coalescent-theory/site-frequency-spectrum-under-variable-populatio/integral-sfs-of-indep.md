# Expected SFS under a variable population size

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $n$ a natural number. Let $T_k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}$) be measurable real random variables with $T_k$ integrable for $2 \le k \le n$, and let $\tau$ be a random list of block-size lists such that every preimage $\tau^{-1}(\{\mathbf{p}\})$ is in $\mathcal{F}$, with the [random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from $n$ singletons, $\mu(\tau = \mathbf{p}) = R_{n-1}((1, \dots, 1), \mathbf{p})$ for every $\mathbf{p}$, and independent of the family $(T_k)$. Let $\mathcal{G}$ be the σ-algebra generated by $\tau$ and the $T_k$, let $\theta$ be a real number, and let $\xi_i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$) be integrable random variables whose conditional expectations given $\mathcal{G}$ equal $\frac{\theta}{2} L_i$ almost surely, where $L_i$ is the [total branch length subtending $i$ sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md) computed from $\tau$ and $(T_k)$. Then for every $1 \le i \le n - 1$,

$$\mathbb{E}[\xi_i] = \frac{\theta}{2} \sum_{k=2}^{n} \frac{k \binom{n-i-1}{k-2}}{\binom{n-1}{k-1}}\, \mathbb{E}[T_k],$$

the [expected spectrum](variable-sfs.md) for the mean holding times $\mathbb{E}[T_k]$. Independence of the holding times and the topology holds for the time-changed Kingman coalescent of any deterministic population-size history; here it is a hypothesis.

- A. Polanski and M. Kimmel, *New explicit expressions for relative frequencies of single-nucleotide polymorphisms with application to statistical inference on population growth*, Genetics 165 (2003), 427–436.
