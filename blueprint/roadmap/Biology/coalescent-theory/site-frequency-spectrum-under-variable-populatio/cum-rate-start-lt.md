# Coalescent-time breakpoints are strictly increasing

Let $\eta$ be the [size function](size-fn.md) of a [piecewise-constant history](piecewise-history.md) with $K$ pieces and start times $s_j$. For every natural number $j$ with $j + 1 < K$, the [cumulative rate](cum-rate.md) satisfies $R_\eta(s_j) < R_\eta(s_{j+1})$.
