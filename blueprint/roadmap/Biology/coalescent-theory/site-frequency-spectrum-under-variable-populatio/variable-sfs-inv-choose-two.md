# Variable-size spectrum reduces to Fu's $\theta/i$ at constant size

For every real number $\theta$ and natural numbers $1 \le i < n$, the [expected spectrum under variable population size](variable-sfs.md) with the constant-size mean holding times $t_k = \binom{k}{2}^{-1}$ is

$$\frac{\theta}{2} \sum_{k=2}^{n} \frac{k \binom{n-i-1}{k-2}}{\binom{n-1}{k-1}} \binom{k}{2}^{-1} = \frac{\theta}{i}.$$
