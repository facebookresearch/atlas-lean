# The expected SFS determines the mean holding times

Let $\theta \ne 0$ be a real number, $n$ a natural number and $t$, $t'$ two real sequences such that the [expected spectra](variable-sfs.md) agree, $\xi_{n,i}(\theta, t) = \xi_{n,i}(\theta, t')$, for every $1 \le i \le n - 1$. Then $t_k = t'_k$ for every $2 \le k \le n$. The kernel is triangular with nonzero diagonal ([vanishing beyond the diagonal](sfs-kernel-eq-zero-of-lt.md), [positive diagonal](sfs-kernel-diag-pos.md)).
