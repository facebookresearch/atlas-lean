# Fay and Wu's $\theta_H$

For a natural number $n$ and real functions $\xi_i$ on a set $\Omega$, the [weighted estimator](theta-weighted.md) with weights $w_i = i/\binom{n}{2}$:

$$\theta_H = \binom{n}{2}^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} i^2\, \xi_i,$$

where division by $0$ gives $0$.

## Sources

- J. C. Fay and C.-I. Wu, *Hitchhiking under positive Darwinian selection*, Genetics 155 (2000), 1405–1413.
