# Numerator of Fu and Li's F has mean zero at constant size

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $n \ge 2$ a natural number, $\theta$ a real number and $\xi_i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$) $\mu$-integrable real functions with $\int \xi_i \, d\mu = \theta / i$ for every $1 \le i \le n - 1$. Then the [numerator of Fu and Li's $F$](fu-li-fnumerator.md) has $\mu$-integral $0$.
