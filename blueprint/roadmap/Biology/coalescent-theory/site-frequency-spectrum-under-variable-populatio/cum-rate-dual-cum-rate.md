# Dual cumulative rate inverts the cumulative rate

Let $\eta$ be the [size function](size-fn.md) of a [piecewise-constant history](piecewise-history.md) with $K \ge 1$ pieces and $\eta^*$ that of its [dual history](dual.md). For every real $s \ge 0$, the [cumulative rates](cum-rate.md) satisfy $R_{\eta^*}\bigl(R_\eta(s)\bigr) = s$.
