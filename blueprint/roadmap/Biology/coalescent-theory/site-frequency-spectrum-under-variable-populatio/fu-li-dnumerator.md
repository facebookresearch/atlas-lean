# Numerator of Fu and Li's D

For a natural number $n$ and real functions $\xi_i$ on a set $\Omega$, the numerator of Fu and Li's $D$ with an outgroup is the function

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \xi_i - a_1(n)\, \xi_1,$$

where $a_1(n) = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} 1/i$ is [Tajima's constant](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/tajima-a1.md): the number $S = \sum_i \xi_i$ of segregating sites minus $a_1$ times the number $\xi_1$ of derived singletons, the external-branch mutations.

## Sources

- Y.-X. Fu and W.-H. Li, *Statistical tests of neutrality of mutations*, Genetics 133 (1993), 693–709.
