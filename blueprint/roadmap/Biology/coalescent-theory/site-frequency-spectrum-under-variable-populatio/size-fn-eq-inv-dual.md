# Size function is the inverse of the dual size in coalescent time

Let $\eta$ be the [size function](size-fn.md) of a [piecewise-constant history](piecewise-history.md) with $K \ge 1$ pieces and $\eta^*$ that of its [dual history](dual.md). For every real $s \ge 0$, $\eta(s) = 1/\eta^*\bigl(R_\eta(s)\bigr)$, with the [cumulative rate](cum-rate.md) $R_\eta$.
