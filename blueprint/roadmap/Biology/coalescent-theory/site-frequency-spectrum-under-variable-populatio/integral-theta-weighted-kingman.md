# Achaz's estimator is unbiased under Kingman's coalescent

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $n$ a natural number. Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under $\mu$, each $T_k$ measurable, and let $\tau$ be a random list of block-size lists with every preimage $\tau^{-1}(\{\mathbf{p}\})$ in $\mathcal{F}$, with the [random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from $n$ singletons, and independent of $(T_k)$. Let $\theta$ be a real number and $\xi_i$ ($i \in \mathbb{N}$) integrable random variables whose conditional expectations given the σ-algebra generated by $\tau$ and the $T_k$ equal $\frac{\theta}{2} L_i$ almost surely, with $L_i$ the [total branch length subtending $i$ sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md). Then for all real weights $w_i$ with $\sum_{i=1}^{n-1} w_i = 1$, the [weighted estimator](theta-weighted.md) satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\theta_w] = \theta$.
