# Griffiths–Tavaré kernel

For natural numbers $n$, $i$ and $k$, let

$$g_{n,i,k} = \frac{k \binom{n-i-1}{k-2}}{\binom{n-1}{k-1}},$$

where the subtractions are those of natural numbers, truncated at $0$, and division by $0$ gives $0$. For $1 \le i < n$ and $2 \le k \le n$, it is the expected number of branches subtending exactly $i$ of the $n$ sample copies while $k$ lineages remain ([Expected number of blocks of size $i$](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/join-step-iterate-count.md)).

## Sources

- R. C. Griffiths and S. Tavaré, *The age of a mutation in a general coalescent tree*, Comm. Statist. Stochastic Models 14 (1998), 273–295.
