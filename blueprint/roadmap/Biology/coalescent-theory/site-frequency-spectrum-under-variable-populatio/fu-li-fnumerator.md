# Numerator of Fu and Li's F

For a natural number $n$ and real functions $\xi_i$ on a set $\Omega$, the numerator of Fu and Li's $F$ with an outgroup is the function $\theta_\pi - \xi_1$, where $\theta_\pi$ is [Tajima's $\theta_\pi$ in its spectrum form](theta-pi.md).

## Sources

- Y.-X. Fu and W.-H. Li, *Statistical tests of neutrality of mutations*, Genetics 133 (1993), 693–709.
