# Distinct two-piece histories with equal first-coalescence times

There are two [piecewise-constant histories](piecewise-history.md) with $2$ pieces whose [size functions](size-fn.md) differ at some time $s \ge 0$ but whose [first-coalescence times](first-coal-mean.md) agree for $m = 2$ and $m = 3$. This is the case $K = 2$ of [the tightness of $n \ge 2K$](exists-ne-first-coal-mean-eq-of-lt.md).
