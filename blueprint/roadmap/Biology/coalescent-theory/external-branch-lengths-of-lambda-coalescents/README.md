# External branch lengths of Λ-coalescents

External branches (the edges ending in the sampled leaves) carry the singleton mutations of a genealogy. For multiple-merger Λ-coalescents their joint moments satisfy a recursion in the sample size, and for the Bolthausen–Sznitman coalescent they become asymptotically independent exponentials on the scale $1/\log n$, positively correlated at every finite $n$, unlike under Kingman's coalescent.
