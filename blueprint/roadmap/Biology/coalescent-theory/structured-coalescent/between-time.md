# Mean coalescence time between demes

For a natural number $d$ and a real number $M$, $T_1 = d + \frac{d-1}{M}$, with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$ (so $T_1 = d$ when $M = 0$). For $d \ge 2$ and $M = 4Nm > 0$ it is the mean coalescence time of two lineages in different demes, in units of $2N$ generations; for $d \ge 2$ and $M = 0$ the true mean is infinite.
