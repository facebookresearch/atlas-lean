# Slatkin's $F_{ST}$ for the finite island model in closed form

For every natural number $d \ge 2$ and every real $M = 4Nm > 0$, [Slatkin's fixation index](slatkin-fst.md) is

$$F_{ST} = \frac{1}{1 + M d^2/(d-1)^2}.$$

## Sources

- M. Slatkin, *Inbreeding coefficients and coalescence times*, Genet. Res. 58 (1991), 167–175.
