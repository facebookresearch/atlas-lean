# Closed-form mean coalescence times in the island model

For a natural number $d$ and a real number $M$, the function on the [pair states](pair-state.md) with value $T_0 = d$ on "same deme", $T_1 = d + (d-1)/M$ (with $x/0 = 0$) on "different demes", and $0$ on "coalesced". It is the unique solution of the [first-step equations](first-step-equations.md) when $d \ge 2$ and $M > 0$.
