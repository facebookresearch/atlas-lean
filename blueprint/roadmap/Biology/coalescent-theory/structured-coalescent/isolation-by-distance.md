# Isolation by distance

This page covers isolation by distance: the decay of genetic similarity with geographic distance in a population spread over space. In the formulation of Malécot, the probability that two genes sampled at distance $r$ are identical by descent is expressed through the Green kernel of the motion of their two ancestral lineages, killed at the mutation rate, and its decay in $r$ depends on the dimension of space. The [structured coalescent](structured-coalescent-for-n-lineages-with-a-gene.md) and the lineage dynamics of [the spatial Λ-Fleming–Viot process](slfv-dual-lineage-process-under-the-integrabilit.md) give genealogical forms of these models.

This target is distinct from the exact stationary identity by descent of the circular stepping-stone model.

## Expansion

Fix from a source the model of lineage motion, the mutation and the dimension, then state and prove the identity-by-descent formula through the Green kernel and its dependence on the dimension, each as a page of its own.
