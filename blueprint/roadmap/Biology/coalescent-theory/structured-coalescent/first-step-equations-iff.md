# Mean coalescence times in the finite island model

Let $d \ge 2$ be a natural number and $M > 0$ a real number. A real function $h$ on the [pair states](pair-state.md) satisfies the [first-step equations](first-step-equations.md) if and only if $h$ is the [closed-form mean coalescence time](mean-coalescence-time.md): $h(\text{same}) = d$, $h(\text{different}) = d + (d-1)/M$ and $h(\text{coalesced}) = 0$. In particular the within-deme time $T_0 = d$ does not depend on the migration rate.

## Sources

- C. Strobeck, *Average number of nucleotide differences in a sample from a single subpopulation: a test for population subdivision*, Genetics 117 (1987), 149–153.
- M. Slatkin, *Inbreeding coefficients and coalescence times*, Genet. Res. 58 (1991), 167–175.
