# Slatkin's mean total coalescence time

For a natural number $d$ and a real number $M$, let

$$\bar T = \frac{T_0}{d} + \frac{d-1}{d}\, T_1,$$

with the [within-deme](within-time.md) and [between-deme](between-time.md) mean coalescence times $T_0$, $T_1$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $d \ge 2$ and $M > 0$ it is the mean coalescence time of two genes drawn from two demes chosen independently and uniformly at random.
