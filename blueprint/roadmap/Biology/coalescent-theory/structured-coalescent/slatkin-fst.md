# Slatkin's $F_{ST}$ for the finite island model

For a natural number $d$ and a real number $M$, Slatkin's fixation index is

$$F_{ST} = \frac{\bar T - T_0}{\bar T},$$

with the [mean total coalescence time](total-time.md) $\bar T$, the [within-deme time](within-time.md) $T_0 = d$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$.

## Sources

- M. Slatkin, *Inbreeding coefficients and coalescence times*, Genet. Res. 58 (1991), 167–175.
