# First-step equations for mean coalescence time

For a natural number $d$, a real number $M$ and a real function $h$ on the [pair states](pair-state.md), $h$ satisfies the first-step (backward Kolmogorov) equations for the mean time to coalescence if $h(\text{coalesced}) = 0$ and

$$\sum_t q(s, t)\,\bigl(h(t) - h(s)\bigr) = -1$$

for every state $s \ne \text{coalesced}$, with the [transition rates](rate.md) $q$. The vector of mean absorption times of a finite absorbing continuous-time Markov chain satisfies these equations.
