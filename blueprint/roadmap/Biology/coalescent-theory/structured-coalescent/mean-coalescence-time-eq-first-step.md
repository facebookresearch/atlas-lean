# First-step analysis in jump-chain form

Let $d \ge 2$ be a natural number, $M > 0$ a real number and $s \ne \text{coalesced}$ a [pair state](pair-state.md). Then the [exit rate](exit-rate.md) $q_s$ is positive and the [closed-form mean coalescence time](mean-coalescence-time.md) $h$ satisfies

$$h(s) = \frac{1}{q_s} + \sum_t \frac{q(s, t)}{q_s}\, h(t):$$

the mean holding time in $s$ plus the mean time from the next state.
