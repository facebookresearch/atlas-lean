# Two spatial Λ-Fleming–Viot lineages: displacement and separation

Under $\int u\, r^d\, \zeta(dr, du) < \infty$ for homogeneous ball-shaped events on $\mathbb R^d$, the $n$-lineage process of the spatial Λ-Fleming–Viot model [exists](slfv-dual-lineages-under-int-u-r-d-zeta-infinity.md). For two lineages, the
displacement $D = y_1 - y_2$ is a Markov process on $\mathbb R^d$ together with a coalesced state, as in the [bounded case](slfv-dual-lineages-bounded-radius-and-finite-eve.md). Because the event law is invariant under rotations, $|D|$ is a
Markov process on $[0, \infty)$ together with that state, with coalescence rate $\int u^2\, \mathrm{Vol}\bigl(B(0, r) \cap B(s e_1, r)\bigr)\, \zeta(dr, du)$ at separation $s$. On a torus the event law is not
invariant under rotations, so this reduction does not apply and the full displacement process is used. This statement concerns only the backward lineages; the forward
measure-valued spatial Λ-Fleming–Viot process is not covered.
