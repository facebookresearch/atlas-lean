# Slatkin's $F_{ST}$ for many demes

For every real $M > 0$, [Slatkin's fixation index](slatkin-fst.md) for $d$ demes tends to Wright's value $\frac{1}{1 + M} = \frac{1}{1 + 4Nm}$ as the number $d$ of demes tends to infinity.

## Sources

- M. Slatkin, *Inbreeding coefficients and coalescence times*, Genet. Res. 58 (1991), 167–175.
