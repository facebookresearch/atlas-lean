# Ohta–Kimura standardized disequilibrium $\sigma_d^2$

Let $\rho \ge 0$ and $\nu \ge 0$, let $\mu$ be a [stationary law](is-stationary-law.md) of the two-locus diffusion with parameters $\rho$ and $\nu$, and suppose that $\int p(1 - p)\, q(1 - q)\, d\mu > 0$, with the allele frequencies $p$ and $q$. Then

$$\frac{\int D^2 \, d\mu}{\int p(1 - p)\, q(1 - q)\, d\mu} = \sigma_d^2(\rho, \nu),$$

with the [linkage disequilibrium](diseq-poly.md) $D$ and the formula [$\sigma_d^2$](sigma-dsq.md).

## Sources

- T. Ohta and M. Kimura, *Linkage disequilibrium at steady state determined by random genetic drift and recurrent mutation*, Genetics 63 (1969), 229–238.
