# Recent coalescence from IBD segments

Pairs of haplotypes that share a segment identical by descent have a recent common ancestor at the sites it covers. Averaging over pairs and sites turns detected IBD into estimates of recent coalescence probabilities: a single aggregate recent effective population size, a length-based age estimate for a segment, and a site-wise density of recent coalescence used to scan for recent positive selection.
