# Wright–Fisher versus coalescent genealogies of long genomes

Coalescent simulators approximate the ancestry of a sample by Hudson's coalescent with recombination, which assumes a short region and a sample small relative to the population. Over whole genomes and in large samples these assumptions fail in ways that a discrete-time Wright–Fisher pedigree does not. This topic states the source of the discrepancy.
