# States of the two-locus ancestral process

The six states of the two-locus ancestral process of a sample of two sequences at loci $A$ and $B$:

- linked: two chromosomes, each ancestral at both loci;
- partly split: one chromosome ancestral at both loci, one at $A$ only and one at $B$ only;
- split: four chromosomes, two ancestral at $A$ only and two at $B$ only;
- $A$ coalesced: locus $A$ has coalesced and two lineages remain at locus $B$;
- $B$ coalesced: locus $B$ has coalesced and two lineages remain at locus $A$;
- both coalesced.
