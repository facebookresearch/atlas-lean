# Hill–Robertson and Ohta–Kimura equations, first row

For every vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies with $\sum_g x_g = 1$ and all real $\rho$ and $\nu$, the [two-locus generator](two-locus-generator.md) satisfies, at $x$,

$$L_{\rho,\nu} D^2 = -(3 + \rho + 8\nu)\, D^2 + D\,(1 - 2p)(1 - 2q) + p(1 - p)\, q(1 - q),$$

with the [linkage disequilibrium](diseq-poly.md) $D$ and the allele frequencies $p$ and $q$.

## Sources

- W. G. Hill and A. Robertson, *Linkage disequilibrium in finite populations*, Theoret. Appl. Genet. 38 (1968), 226–231.
- T. Ohta and M. Kimura, *Linkage disequilibrium at steady state determined by random genetic drift and recurrent mutation*, Genetics 63 (1969), 229–238.
