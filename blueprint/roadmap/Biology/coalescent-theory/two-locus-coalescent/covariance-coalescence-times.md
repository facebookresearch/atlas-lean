# Covariance of the coalescence times, path form

Let $\rho \ge 0$ and let $(X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be the continuous-time Markov chain with the [two-locus rate matrix](two-locus-rate.md) $Q_\rho$ started from two linked sequences, in its standard path construction, with hitting times $T_A$ and $T_B$ of the states where locus $A$, respectively locus $B$, has coalesced. The expectations $\mathbb{E}[T_A T_B]$, $\mathbb{E}[T_A]$ and $\mathbb{E}[T_B]$ are finite and

$$\mathbb{E}[T_A T_B] - \mathbb{E}[T_A]\, \mathbb{E}[T_B] = \frac{\rho + 18}{\rho^2 + 13\rho + 18}.$$

## Sources
