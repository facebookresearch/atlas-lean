# IBS tract length spectrum

Cutting a pairwise sequence alignment at its polymorphic sites yields tracts of identity by state (IBS). Their length distribution summarizes linked variation over all genomic scales at once: long tracts record recent common ancestry and recent gene flow, short tracts ancient coalescence. This topic states the expected spectrum under a sequentially Markov coalescent and its use for demographic inference.
