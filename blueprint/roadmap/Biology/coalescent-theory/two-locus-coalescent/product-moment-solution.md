# The explicit two-locus moments solve the moment equations

For every real $\rho \ge 0$, the functions $m_A$ and $m_B$ ([mean times](mean-time-a.md)) and the [product-moment solution](product-moment.md) $f$ satisfy the equations of [Covariance of coalescence times](covariance-of-product-moment.md):

- $(Q_\rho m_A)(i) = -1$ where locus $A$ has not coalesced;
- $(Q_\rho m_B)(i) = -1$ where locus $B$ has not coalesced;
- $f(i) = 0$ where locus $A$ or locus $B$ has coalesced;
- $(Q_\rho f)(i) = -(m_A(i) + m_B(i))$ where neither has.
