# Asymptotic expansion of two-locus sampling distributions

For two loci with recombination rate $\rho$, the sampling distribution of haplotype data has an asymptotic expansion in $1/\rho$ whose coefficients can be computed recursively to arbitrary order. Under a neutral finite-alleles model the distribution is a rational function of $1/\rho$, so finitely many terms of the expansion recover it exactly through Padé approximants.
