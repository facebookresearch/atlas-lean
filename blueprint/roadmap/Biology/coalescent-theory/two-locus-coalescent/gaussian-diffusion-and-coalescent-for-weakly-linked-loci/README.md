# Gaussian diffusion and coalescent for weakly linked loci

Simplified multilocus population-genetic models for loosely linked loci: a diffusion in which linkage disequilibrium is conditionally Gaussian around linkage equilibrium, and a coalescent coupled to the ancestral recombination graph. Both reproduce the exact sampling distribution to the first two orders in the inverse recombination rate, and the diffusion has a closed-form sampling distribution.
