# Ancestral recombination graph on a continuous chromosome

The ancestral recombination graph on a finite set of loci, viewed as a coagulation–fragmentation chain on partitions, and its projective extension to partitions of the half-line describing how ancestral "colours" are mixed along a chromosome by recombination. Under strong recombination the stationary law has an explicit approximation in terms of coalescence scenarios, which yields a scale-invariant Poisson description of the material identical by descent with a given locus.
