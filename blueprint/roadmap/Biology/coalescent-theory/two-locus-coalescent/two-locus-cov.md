# Covariance of coalescence times at two linked loci

For a real number $\rho$, let

$$\operatorname{Cov}(\rho) = \frac{\rho + 18}{\rho^2 + 13\rho + 18},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $\rho \ge 0$ it is the covariance of the coalescence times at two loci of two sequences sampled linked ([covariance of coalescence times](covariance-of-product-moment.md)).

## Sources

- R. R. Hudson, *Properties of a neutral allele model with intragenic recombination*, Theoret. Population Biol. 23 (1983), 183–201.
- N. L. Kaplan and R. R. Hudson, *The use of sample genealogies for studying a selectively neutral m-loci model with recombination*, Theoret. Population Biol. 28 (1985), 382–396.
