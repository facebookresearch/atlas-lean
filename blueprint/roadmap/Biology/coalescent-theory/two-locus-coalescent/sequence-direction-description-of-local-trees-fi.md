# The local trees along the sequence: finitely many changes, expected counts and failure of the Markov property

For the ancestral recombination graph on $[0, 1)$ with homogeneous recombination intensity $\rho\, dx$:

1. the local trees change only at [finitely many breakpoints](../pairwise-differences-under-poisson-mutation/finitely-many-breakpoints-and-a-jointly-measurab.md), and their finite-locus marginals are those of [the finite-locus coalescent with recombination](continuum-arg-homogeneous-rho-dx-finite-locus-ma.md);
2. the expected numbers of candidate and of visible changes satisfy [the counts of candidate and visible local-tree changes](../pairwise-differences-under-poisson-mutation/candidate-vs-visible-local-tree-changes-expected.md);
3. the process of local trees is [not Markov along the sequence](local-trees-are-not-markov-along-the-sequence-ve.md).

The sequential algorithm of Wiuf and Hein and the equality of its law with that of the local trees are a separate result: [the Wiuf–Hein algorithm](wiuf-hein-algorithm-reproduces-the-joint-law-of.md). The statement is for homogeneous recombination only.
