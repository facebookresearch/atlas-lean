# Sum over the two-locus ancestral states

For every function $f$ from the six [two-locus states](two-locus-state.md) to a commutative additive monoid, the sum of $f$ over the states is $f(\text{linked}) + f(\text{partly split}) + f(\text{split}) + f(A \text{ coalesced}) + f(B \text{ coalesced}) + f(\text{both coalesced})$.
