# Hill–Robertson and Ohta–Kimura equations, second row

For every vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies with $\sum_g x_g = 1$ and all real $\rho$ and $\nu$, with $z = (1 - 2p)(1 - 2q)$, the [two-locus generator](two-locus-generator.md) satisfies, at $x$,

$$L_{\rho,\nu}(D z) = 4 D^2 - \Bigl(5 + \frac{\rho}{2} + 8\nu\Bigr) D z.$$

## Sources

- W. G. Hill and A. Robertson, *Linkage disequilibrium in finite populations*, Theoret. Appl. Genet. 38 (1968), 226–231.
- T. Ohta and M. Kimura, *Linkage disequilibrium at steady state determined by random genetic drift and recurrent mutation*, Genetics 63 (1969), 229–238.
