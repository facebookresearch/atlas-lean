# Stationary law of the two-locus diffusion

For real numbers $\rho$ and $\nu$, a measure $\mu$ on the vectors $x$ of four gamete frequencies is a stationary law of the two-locus diffusion, in the weak sense, if

- $\mu$ is a probability measure;
- $\mu$-almost every $x$ lies in the simplex: $x_g \ge 0$ for every gamete $g$ and $\sum_g x_g = 1$;
- $\int (L_{\rho,\nu} f)(x)\, \mu(dx) = 0$ for every real polynomial $f$ in the gamete frequencies, where $L_{\rho,\nu}$ is the [two-locus generator](two-locus-generator.md).
