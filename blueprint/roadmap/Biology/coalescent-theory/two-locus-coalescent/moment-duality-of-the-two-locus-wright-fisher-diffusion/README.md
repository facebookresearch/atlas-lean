# Moment duality of the two-locus Wright–Fisher diffusion

The two-locus two-allele Wright–Fisher diffusion (Ohta–Kimura generator) is moment dual to the Markov chain counting the lineages of a section of the ancestral recombination graph that are ancestral at locus $A$ only, at locus $B$ only, or at both loci. The duality turns moment computations into expectations over the ancestral chain and gives closed forms for recombination-free genealogies.
