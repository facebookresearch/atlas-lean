# Genealogy-based complex trait analysis

An inferred ancestral recombination graph (ARG) of a sample determines, at every site, the pairwise times to the most recent common ancestor, and therefore the expected pattern of variation that sequencing would reveal. These articles turn the ARG into an expected genetic relationship matrix for mixed-model analyses and define a distance between ARGs based on the mutations they can generate.
