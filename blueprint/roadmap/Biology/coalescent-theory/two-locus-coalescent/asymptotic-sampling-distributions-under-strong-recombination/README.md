# Asymptotic sampling distributions under strong recombination

Closed-form expansions of multi-locus sampling probabilities under the coalescent with recombination in inverse powers of the recombination rate: the leading term is the product of one-locus sampling distributions, and the first correction is an explicit inclusion–exclusion sum, both universal over mutation models.
