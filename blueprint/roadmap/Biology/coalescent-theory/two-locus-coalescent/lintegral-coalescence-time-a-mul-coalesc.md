# Product moment of the two coalescence times, path form

Let $\rho \ge 0$ and let $(X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be the continuous-time Markov chain with the [two-locus rate matrix](two-locus-rate.md) $Q_\rho$ started from the state "linked", in its standard path construction, with hitting times $T_A$ and $T_B$ of the states where locus $A$, respectively locus $B$, has coalesced ($\inf \emptyset = \infty$). Then

$$\mathbb{E}[T_A T_B] = 1 + \frac{\rho + 18}{\rho^2 + 13\rho + 18}.$$

## Sources

- R. R. Hudson, *Properties of a neutral allele model with intragenic recombination*, Theoret. Population Biol. 23 (1983), 183–201.
- N. L. Kaplan and R. R. Hudson, *The use of sample genealogies for studying a selectively neutral m-loci model with recombination*, Theoret. Population Biol. 28 (1985), 382–396.
