# Hill–Robertson and Ohta–Kimura equations, third row

For every vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies with $\sum_g x_g = 1$ and all real $\rho$ and $\nu$, with $\Pi = p(1 - p)\, q(1 - q)$, the [two-locus generator](two-locus-generator.md) satisfies, at $x$,

$$L_{\rho,\nu} \Pi = D\,(1 - 2p)(1 - 2q) - (2 + 8\nu)\, \Pi + \nu\,\bigl(p(1 - p) + q(1 - q)\bigr).$$

For $\nu = 0$ the three rows form a closed linear system.

## Sources

- W. G. Hill and A. Robertson, *Linkage disequilibrium in finite populations*, Theoret. Appl. Genet. 38 (1968), 226–231.
- T. Ohta and M. Kimura, *Linkage disequilibrium at steady state determined by random genetic drift and recurrent mutation*, Genetics 63 (1969), 229–238.
