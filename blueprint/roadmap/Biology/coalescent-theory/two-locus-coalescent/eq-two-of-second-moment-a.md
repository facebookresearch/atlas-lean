# Second moment of the coalescence time at locus A

Let $\rho \ge 0$ and let $m$ and $g$ be real functions on the [two-locus states](two-locus-state.md), with $Q_\rho$ the [two-locus rate matrix](two-locus-rate.md), such that

- $m(i) = 0$ where locus $A$ has coalesced and $(Q_\rho m)(i) = -1$ elsewhere;
- $g(i) = 0$ where locus $A$ has coalesced and $(Q_\rho g)(i) = -2\,m(i)$ elsewhere.

Then $g(i) = 2$ in every state where locus $A$ has not coalesced, so the variance read off from these equations is $2 - 1^2 = 1$.
