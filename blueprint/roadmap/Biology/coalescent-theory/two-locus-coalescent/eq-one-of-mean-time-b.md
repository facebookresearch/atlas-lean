# Mean coalescence time at locus B

Let $\rho \ge 0$ and let $m$ be a real function on the [two-locus states](two-locus-state.md) such that $m(i) = 0$ in the states where locus $B$ has coalesced and $(Q_\rho m)(i) = -1$ in the other states, where $Q_\rho$ is the [two-locus rate matrix](two-locus-rate.md). Then $m(i) = 1$ in every state where locus $B$ has not coalesced.
