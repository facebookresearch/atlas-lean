# Solution of the two-locus product-moment equations

For a real number $\rho$, the real function on the [two-locus states](two-locus-state.md) with values $1 + (\rho + 18)/d$ at linked, $1 + 6/d$ at partly split, $1 + 4/d$ at split, and $0$ at the three states where some locus has coalesced, where $d = \rho^2 + 13\rho + 18$ and division by $0$ gives $0$.
