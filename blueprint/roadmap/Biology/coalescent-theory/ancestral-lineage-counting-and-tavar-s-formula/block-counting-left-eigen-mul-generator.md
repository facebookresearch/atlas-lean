# Left eigenvector equation for the block-counting generator

For every natural number $n$,

$$W_n G_n = D_n W_n,$$

where $G_n$ is the [generator of Kingman's block-counting process](block-counting-generator.md), $W_n$ the [matrix of left eigenvectors](block-counting-left-eigen.md) and $D_n$ the diagonal matrix with entries $-\binom{k}{2}$, $0 \le k \le n$: the rows of $W_n$ are left eigenvectors of $G_n$.
