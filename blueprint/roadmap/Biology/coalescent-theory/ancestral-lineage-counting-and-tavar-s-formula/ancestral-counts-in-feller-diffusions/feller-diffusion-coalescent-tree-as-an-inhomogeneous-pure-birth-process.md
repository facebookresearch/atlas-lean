# Feller diffusion coalescent tree as an inhomogeneous pure-birth process

**Setting.** Let $\{X(u)\}_{0 \le u \le t}$ be a Feller diffusion with generator $\tfrac12 x\,\partial_x^2 + \alpha x\,\partial_x$ ($\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$), observed at time $t$. Its coalescent (genealogical) tree is the tree, read forward in time from $0$ to $t$, whose lineages are the ancestors of the time-$t$ population. For a linear birth–death process with birth rate $\lambda$ and death rate $\mu$ observed on $[0,T]$, the **reconstructed process** $\mathcal{RP}(T,\lambda,\mu)$ is the tree of lineages with descendants alive at $T$.

**Lemma.** For $0 < s < t$, the part of the coalescent tree of the Feller diffusion lying in $[0, t-s]$ is generated by a reconstructed process in $\mathcal{RP}(t-s, \hat\lambda(s), \hat\mu(s))$ with

$$\hat\lambda(s) = \frac{\alpha}{1-e^{-\alpha s}}, \qquad \hat\mu(s) = \frac{\alpha e^{-\alpha s}}{1-e^{-\alpha s}} \qquad (\alpha \ne 0),$$

and, by continuity, $\hat\lambda(s) = \hat\mu(s) = 1/s$ for $\alpha = 0$. For $\alpha \ne 0$ these are the unique rates with $\hat\lambda - \hat\mu = \alpha$ and $\hat\mu/\hat\lambda = e^{-\alpha s}$; for $\alpha = 0$ those two relations only say $\hat\lambda = \hat\mu$, and the common value $1/s$ is the limit of the formulas as $\alpha \to 0$.

**Theorem.** The coalescent tree of a parameter-$\alpha$ Feller diffusion observed at time $t$ is a time-inhomogeneous pure-birth process whose birth rate per lineage at time $u$ after initiation is

$$\lambda^{\mathrm{coal}}(u;t) = \frac{\alpha}{1 - e^{-\alpha(t-u)}}, \qquad 0 \le u < t,\ \alpha \ne 0,$$

and $\lambda^{\mathrm{coal}}(u;t) = 1/(t-u)$ for $\alpha = 0$. The rate blows up as $u \uparrow t$, so the tree has infinitely many lineages just below the sampling time, matching the infinite population.

## Expansion

A reconstructed linear birth–death process is itself a pure-birth process with inhomogeneous rate $\lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}(u) = \lambda(\lambda-\mu)/(\lambda - \mu e^{-(\lambda-\mu)(T-u)})$. Substituting $T = t-s$ and the rates of the lemma, and letting $s \to 0$ (where $\hat\lambda(s) = s^{-1} + \alpha/2 + O(s)$ and $\hat\mu(s) = s^{-1} - \alpha/2 + O(s)$), gives the stated rate. The lemma itself follows because the Feller diffusion is the limit of near-critical birth–death processes whose ancestor counts over the window $[t-s,t]$ have the same Pólya–Aeppli law.
