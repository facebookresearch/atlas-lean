# Left eigenvectors of Kingman's block-counting generator

For a natural number $n$, the $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ real matrix $W_n$, with rows and columns indexed by $\{0, \dots, n\}$, whose row $0$ is the indicator of the column $0$ and whose entry in row $k \ge 1$ and column $j$ is

$$(W_n)_{kj} = \frac{(2k-1)(-1)^{k-j}\, j(j+1)\cdots(j+k-2)}{j!\,(k-j)!}$$

for $j \le k$, with the rising factorial of $j$ of length $k - 1$ in the numerator, and $0$ for $j > k$. Its row $k$ is a left eigenvector of the block-counting generator for the eigenvalue $-\binom{k}{2}$.
