# Spectral decomposition of the exponential of the block-counting generator

For every natural number $n$ and every real $t$,

$$e^{t G_n} = V_n \operatorname{diag}\bigl(e^{-\binom{k}{2} t}\bigr)_{0 \le k \le n}\, W_n,$$

where $G_n$ is the [generator of Kingman's block-counting process](block-counting-generator.md) and $V_n$, $W_n$ are the matrices of [right](block-counting-right-eigen.md) and [left](block-counting-left-eigen.md) eigenvectors.
