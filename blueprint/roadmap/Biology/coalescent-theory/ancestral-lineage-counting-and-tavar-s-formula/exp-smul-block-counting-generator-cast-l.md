# Truncation consistency

For natural numbers $n \le N$, every real $t$ and all $i, j \in \{0, \dots, n\}$,

$$\bigl(e^{t G_N}\bigr)_{ij} = \bigl(e^{t G_n}\bigr)_{ij},$$

where $G_m$ is the [generator of Kingman's block-counting process](block-counting-generator.md) on $\{0, \dots, m\}$. For $t \ge 0$: the top-left $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ block of the transition matrix of the process on $\{0, \dots, N\}$ is the transition matrix of the process on $\{0, \dots, n\}$, since the process only moves down.
