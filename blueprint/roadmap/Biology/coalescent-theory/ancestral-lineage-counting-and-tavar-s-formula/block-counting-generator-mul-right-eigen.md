# Right eigenvector equation for the block-counting generator

For every natural number $n$,

$$G_n V_n = V_n D_n,$$

where $G_n$ is the [generator of Kingman's block-counting process](block-counting-generator.md), $V_n$ the [matrix of right eigenvectors](block-counting-right-eigen.md) and $D_n$ the diagonal matrix with entries $-\binom{k}{2}$, $0 \le k \le n$: the columns of $V_n$ are right eigenvectors of $G_n$.
