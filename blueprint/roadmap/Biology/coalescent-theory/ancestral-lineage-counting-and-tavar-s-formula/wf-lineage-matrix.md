# Wright–Fisher transition matrix of the lineage count

For natural numbers $n$ and $M$, the $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ real matrix $P_{n,M}$, with rows and columns indexed by $\{0, \dots, n\}$, whose entry in row $k$ and column $j$ is the [Wright–Fisher transition probability of the lineage count](wf-lineage-prob.md) $p_M(k, j) = S(k, j)\, M(M-1)\cdots(M-j+1) / M^k$. For $M \ge 1$, it is the one-generation transition matrix of the number of ancestral lineages of a sample of at most $n$ gene copies in a neutral Wright–Fisher population of $M$ gene copies.
