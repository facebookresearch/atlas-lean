# Genealogy of stationary continuous-state branching populations

A population of fluctuating size modeled by a stationary sub-critical continuous-state branching process with immigration (the Q-process of a sub-critical CB conditioned on non-extinction). Its genealogy at a fixed time yields an explicit joint law for the time to the most recent common ancestor (TMRCA), the current population size and the size just before the MRCA, with a built-in mild bottleneck at the MRCA.
