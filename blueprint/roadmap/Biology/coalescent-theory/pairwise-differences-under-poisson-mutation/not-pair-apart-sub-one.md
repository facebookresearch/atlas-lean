# After $n - 1$ coalescence events any two genes share a lineage

For every `n ≥ 1`, every
[ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h`
of `n` genes and every two genes `a` and `b` of the sample, equal or not, the set `{a, b}` is not
[apart](pair-apart.md) after `n - 1` coalescence events of `h`: some lineage contains both genes.
After `n - 1` events a single lineage remains.
