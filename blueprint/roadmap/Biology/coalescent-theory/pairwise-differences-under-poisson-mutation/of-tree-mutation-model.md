# Holding times of the marked Kingman model with topology

For a natural number `n` and `θ ≥ 0`, the functions `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) on the space of the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md) given by `T_k((t, h), N) = t_k` for
`2 ≤ k ≤ n`, and `T_k = 0` for the other `k`, are
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under the marked model: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law with
rate `C(k, 2)`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent.
