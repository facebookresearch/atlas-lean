# Merging $a$ with $c$ from four lineages leaves $a$, $b$, $d$ apart

Let `h` be a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) of `n` genes and `t < n - 1`, and let the genes `a`, `b`, `c`, `d` be in
[four distinct lineages](apart4.md) after `t` events. If `{a, c}` is not [apart](pair-apart.md) after
`t + 1` events (`a` and `c` share a lineage), then after `t + 1` events `a`, `b`, `d` are in
[three distinct lineages](apart3.md), and the pairs `{c, d}` and `{b, c}` are apart.
