# The blocks after $t$ events depend only on the first $t$ steps

Let `h` and `h'` be [ranked labeled histories](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md)
of `n` genes and `t` a natural number. If `h` and `h'` record the same pair at every step
`i < t` (of the steps `i < n - 1`), then their
[lineage blocks after the first `t` coalescence events](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/blocks-after.md)
coincide.
