# Changing a step $j \ge u$ keeps apartness after $u$ events

Let `f` be a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) of `n` genes, `j < n - 1` a step, `x` a [pair of lineages](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/lineage-pair.md) among the `n - j` lineages at that
step, and `u ≤ j`. For every set `s` of sampled genes, `s` is [apart](pair-apart.md) after `u` events of the
history obtained from `f` by replacing step `j` by `x` exactly when it is apart after `u` events
of `f`.
