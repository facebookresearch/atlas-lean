# Ratio $(m - 1)/(m + 1)$ of the pair separation probability

For a natural number `m`,

`r_m = (m - 1) / (m + 1)`,

as a complex number; the denominator is at least `1`, and `r_0 = -1`, `r_1 = 0`. For `n ≥ 2` and
`2 ≤ m ≤ n`, the
[probability that a given pair lies in separate lineages](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/pair-separate-prob.md)
while there are `m` lineages is `p_{n,m} = ((n + 1) / (n - 1)) r_m`.
