# The two labels of a pair of $k$ lineages are distinct and below $k$

For a natural number `k`, every [unordered pair](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/lineage-pair.md) of
lineages among `k` lineages consists of two labels `a ≠ b` with `a < k` and `b < k`, and its
[smaller label](pair-lo.md) is `min(a, b)` and its [larger label](pair-hi.md) is `max(a, b)`.
In particular the smaller label is strictly less than the larger one.
