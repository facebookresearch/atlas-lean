# Tajima's E π = θ in the marked Kingman model with random joining

For `n ≥ 2` and `θ ≥ 0`, under the [marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md) the
[mean number of pairwise differences](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/mean-pair-diff.md)
`π = C(n, 2)⁻¹ ∑_s D_s`, the sum running over the pairs `s` of the sample and `D_s` being the
[number of differences](pair-diff.md) of `s` computed from the history coordinate and the
mutation counts, has mean

`E π = θ`

(Tajima 1983).
