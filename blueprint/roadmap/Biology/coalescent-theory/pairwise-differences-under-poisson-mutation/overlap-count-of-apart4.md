# No lineage separates both of two pairs whose four genes are apart

Let `h` be a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md)
of a sample of `n` genes, let `u ≤ n - 1`, and let `a`, `b`, `c`, `d` be genes with `a ≠ b`
and `c ≠ d`. If after the first `u` events of `h` the four genes are in
[four distinct lineages](apart4.md), then no lineage after `u` events separates both `{a, b}`
and `{c, d}`: their [overlap count](overlap-count.md) after `u` events is `0`.
