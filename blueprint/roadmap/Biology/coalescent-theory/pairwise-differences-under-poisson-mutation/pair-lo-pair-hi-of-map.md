# Smaller and larger labels of $\{u, v\}$ are its minimum and maximum

For a natural number `k`, if an [unordered pair](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/lineage-pair.md) of
lineages among `k` lineages consists, as a set of natural numbers, of the labels `u` and `v`,
then its [smaller label](pair-lo.md) is `min(u, v)` and its [larger label](pair-hi.md) is
`max(u, v)`.
