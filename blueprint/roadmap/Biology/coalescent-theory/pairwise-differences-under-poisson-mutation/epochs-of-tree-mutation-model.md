# Epoch vector of the marked model is its genealogy's epoch coordinate

For every natural number `n` and every `θ ≥ 0`, the
[vector of epoch lengths](../poisson-mutation-on-the-kingman-genealogy/epochs.md)
`(T_2, …, T_n)` of the [holding times of the marked Kingman model](of-tree-mutation-model.md) is,
at every point `((t, h), N)` of its space, the epoch-length coordinate of the genealogy:
`(T_2, …, T_n)((t, h), N) = t`.
