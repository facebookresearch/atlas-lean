# Covariance of the coalescence times of two pairs sharing a gene

Let `a`, `b`, `c` be three distinct genes of a sample of size `n`. In the
[Kingman genealogy](kingman-genealogy-measure.md) of the sample (independent epoch lengths, the
epoch with `k` lineages exponential with rate `C(k, 2) = k (k - 1) / 2`, and an independent
ranked labeled history drawn by random joining), the [coalescence times](pair-coal-time.md)
`τ_{ab}` of `{a, b}` and `τ_{ac}` of `{a, c}` have covariance

`Cov(τ_{ab}, τ_{ac}) = 1/3`:

their [product moment](integral-pair-coal-time-mul-shared.md) is `4/3` and each has mean `1`.
