# Four genes in four distinct lineages after $t$ events

For a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h` of `n` genes, genes `a`, `b`, `c`, `d` and a natural number `t`: each of the six
pairs `{a, b}`, `{a, c}`, `{a, d}`, `{b, c}`, `{b, d}` and `{c, d}` is [apart](pair-apart.md) after
`t` events of `h`, so that the four genes lie in four distinct lineages.
