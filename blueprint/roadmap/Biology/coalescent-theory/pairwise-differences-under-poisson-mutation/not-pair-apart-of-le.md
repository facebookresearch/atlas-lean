# Two genes in one lineage after $t$ events stay together afterwards

For a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h`, genes `a` and `b` and natural numbers `t ≤ u`: if `{a, b}` is not [apart](pair-apart.md)
after `t` events of `h`, it is not apart after `u` events.
