# Variance of the differences of one pair in the marked Kingman model

For `n ≥ 2`, `θ ≥ 0` and every [pair](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/sample-pairs.md)
`s` of genes of a sample of size `n`, under the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md) the
[number of differences](pair-diff.md) `D_s` of the pair has variance

`Var D_s = θ + θ²`

(Tajima 1983), the variance of a geometric law with mean `θ`. In the
[covariance identity](covariance-pair-diff-kingman-tree-mutati.md) with `s' = s`, the
[coalescence time](pair-coal-time.md) `τ_s` has the exponential law with rate `1`, so
`Var τ_s = 1`, and the path of `s` overlaps itself in its whole length `2 τ_s`, of mean `2`.
