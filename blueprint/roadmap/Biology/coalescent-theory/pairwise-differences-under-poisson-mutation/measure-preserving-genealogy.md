# Genealogy coordinate of the marked model follows the genealogy law

For every natural number `n` and every `θ ≥ 0`, the projection `((t, h), N) ↦ (t, h)` is
measurable and carries the [marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md) to the
[Kingman genealogy law](kingman-genealogy-measure.md): under the marked model the genealogy
`(t, h)` has independent epoch lengths, `t_k` exponential with rate `C(k, 2)`, and an independent
random-joining history `h`.
