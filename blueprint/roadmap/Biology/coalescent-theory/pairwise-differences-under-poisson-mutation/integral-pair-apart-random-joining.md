# Probability that a pair is apart with k lineages under random joining

Let `n` be a natural number, let `s` be a
[pair](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/sample-pairs.md) of genes of a sample of
size `n` and let `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, so that `n ≥ 2`. Under the
[random-joining law](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/random-joining.md) on the
ranked labeled histories of the `n` genes, the probability that the two genes of `s` are
[apart](pair-apart.md) after `n - k` coalescence events, that is, in different lineages during
the epoch with `k` lineages, is

`p_{n,k} = (k - 1)(n + 1) / ((n - 1)(k + 1))`,

the [probability that a pair lies in separate lineages](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/pair-separate-prob.md).
