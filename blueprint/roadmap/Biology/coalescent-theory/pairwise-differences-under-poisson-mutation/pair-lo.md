# Smaller label of a pair of lineages

For a natural number `k` and an [unordered pair](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/lineage-pair.md) `{u, v}`
of the lineage labels `0, …, k - 1`, the smaller label `min(u, v)`, as a natural number. In a
[ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) the lineage formed
by merging the pair takes this label.
