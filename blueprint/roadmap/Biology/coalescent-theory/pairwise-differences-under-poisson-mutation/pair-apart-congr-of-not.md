# After $b$ and $c$ merge, $a$ is apart from both or from neither

Let `h` be a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) of `n` genes, let `a`, `b`, `c` be genes, and let `t ≤ u ≤ n - 1`. If
`{b, c}` is not [apart](pair-apart.md) after `t` events (`b` and `c` share a lineage), then after `u` events
`{a, c}` is apart exactly when `{a, b}` is. So genes that share a lineage are interchangeable
for apartness from any third gene. No distinctness of `a`, `b`, `c` is assumed.
