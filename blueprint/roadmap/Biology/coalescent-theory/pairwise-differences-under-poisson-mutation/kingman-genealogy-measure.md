# Law of the Kingman genealogy with random-joining topology

For a natural number `n`, the law of the Kingman genealogy of a sample of `n` genes with its
topology is the product measure, on pairs `(t, h)` of real epoch lengths `t = (t_2, …, t_n)` and
[ranked labeled histories](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h`
of the `n` genes, of the
[holding-time law](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/pi-measure.md)
`⊗_{k=2}^n Exp(C(k, 2))` and the
[random-joining law](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/random-joining.md). Under it
the epoch lengths are independent, `t_k` exponential with rate `C(k, 2)`, and the history is
independent of them and follows random joining. It is a probability measure.
