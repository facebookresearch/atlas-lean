# Two genes apart after $u$ events are apart after any $t \le u$ events

For a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h`, genes `a` and `b` and natural numbers `t ≤ u`: if `{a, b}` is [apart](pair-apart.md) after
`u` events of `h`, it is apart after `t` events. Equivalently, by contraposition,
[two genes in one lineage after `t` events are in one lineage after every `u ≥ t` events](not-pair-apart-of-le.md).
