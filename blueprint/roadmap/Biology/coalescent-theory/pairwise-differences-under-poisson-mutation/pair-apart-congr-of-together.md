# Genes in one lineage are interchangeable for apartness

Let `h` be a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) of `n` genes, let `g`, `g'` and `x` be genes, and let `t ≤ u ≤ n - 1`. If
`{g, g'}` is not [apart](pair-apart.md) after `t` events (`g` and `g'` share a lineage), then after `u`
events `{g, x}` is apart exactly when `{g', x}` is.
