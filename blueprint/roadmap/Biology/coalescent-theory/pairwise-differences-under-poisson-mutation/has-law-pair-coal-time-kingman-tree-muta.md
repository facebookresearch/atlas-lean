# Coalescence time of a pair in the marked Kingman model is Exp(1)

For `n ≥ 2`, `θ ≥ 0` and every [pair](../segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/sample-pairs.md)
`s` of genes of a sample of size `n`, under the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md) the
[coalescence time](pair-coal-time.md) `τ_s` of the pair, computed from the genealogy coordinate
`(t, h)` as the sum of the `t_k`, `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, during which its two genes are in different
lineages of `h`, has the exponential law with rate `1`.
