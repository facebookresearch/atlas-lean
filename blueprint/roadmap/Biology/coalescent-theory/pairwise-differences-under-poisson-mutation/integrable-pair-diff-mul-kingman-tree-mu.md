# Product of two pairwise differences is integrable in the marked model

For every natural number `n`, every `θ ≥ 0` and all sets `s` and `s'` of genes of a sample of
size `n`, the product `D_s D_{s'}` of the [numbers of differences](pair-diff.md), computed from the
history coordinate and the mutation counts, is integrable under the
[marked Kingman model](kingman-tree-mutation-measure.md). Taking `s' = s`, every `D_s` is square
integrable.
