# Larger label of a pair of lineages

For a natural number `k` and an [unordered pair](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/lineage-pair.md) `{u, v}`
of the lineage labels `0, …, k - 1`, the larger label `max(u, v)`, as a natural number. In a
[ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) merging the pair
removes the lineage with this label, and the lineages with larger labels move down by one.
