# Three genes in three distinct lineages after $t$ events

For a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h` of `n` genes, genes `a`, `b`, `c` and a natural number `t`: each of the pairs
`{a, b}`, `{a, c}` and `{b, c}` is [apart](pair-apart.md) after `t` events of `h`, so that `a`, `b` and
`c` lie in three distinct lineages.
