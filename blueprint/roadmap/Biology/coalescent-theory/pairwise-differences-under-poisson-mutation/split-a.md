# Gene $a$ apart from $b$ and $c$, which share a lineage

For a [ranked labeled history](../ranked-labeled-histories-of-coalescent-trees/ranked-history.md) `h` of `n` genes, genes `a`, `b`, `c` and a natural number `t`: after `t` events of
`h` the pairs `{a, b}` and `{a, c}` are [apart](pair-apart.md) and `{b, c}` is not, so `b` and `c` share
a lineage that does not contain `a`.
