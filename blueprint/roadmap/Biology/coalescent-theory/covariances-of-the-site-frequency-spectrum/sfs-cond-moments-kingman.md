# Conditional SFS moments under Kingman's coalescent

Let `μ` be a probability measure and let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under `μ`: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law with rate `C(k, 2)`,
and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `τ` be a random block-size history such that every preimage
`τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n` singletons,
`μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps` (with `n - 1` truncated at `0`: for `n = 0`,
`R_0` starts from the empty list). Let `L_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T_k` be the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md),
where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry `n - k` of `τ` (the empty list, which
contributes `0`, if `τ` has no such entry), and let `G` be the σ-algebra generated by `τ` and the
`T_k`. Let `θ` be a real number and let `ξ_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be square-integrable real random variables
such that for all `i, j ∈ ℕ`, almost surely, `E[ξ_i | G] = (θ / 2) L_i` and
`E[ξ_i ξ_j | G] = (θ / 2)² L_i L_j + δ_{ij} (θ / 2) L_i`.

Then `ξ` has the [conditional moments of the site frequency spectrum](sfscond-moments.md) given `G`
with branch lengths `L`: the square integrability of every `L_i` that those conditions also require
holds automatically ([square integrability of the branch-length classes](mem-lp-sfs-length.md)). No
measurability of the `T_k` is assumed, so `G` need not be contained in the σ-algebra of `Ω`; in that
case the two conditional expectations above are `0` by convention.
