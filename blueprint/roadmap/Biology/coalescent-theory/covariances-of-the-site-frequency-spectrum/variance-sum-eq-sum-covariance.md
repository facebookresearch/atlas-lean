# Variance of a linear combination as a double sum of covariances

Let `μ` be a probability measure and let `L_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be square-integrable real random
variables. For every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all real coefficients `c_i`,

`Var(∑_{i ∈ s} c_i L_i) = ∑_{i ∈ s} ∑_{j ∈ s} c_i c_j Cov(L_i, L_j)`.

With `L_i` the branch-length classes, this expands the variance of a linear combination of
branch lengths into the branch-length covariance matrix.
