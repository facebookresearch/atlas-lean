# Covariance of SFS entries under Kingman's coalescent

Let `μ` be a probability measure and let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under `μ`, each `T_k` measurable: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential
law with rate `C(k, 2)`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `τ` be a random block-size
history such that every preimage `τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n` singletons,
`μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps`, and independent of the family
`(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`. Let `L_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T_k` be the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md),
where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry `n - k` of `τ`, and let `G` be the
σ-algebra generated by `τ` and the `T_k`. Let `θ` be a real number and let `ξ_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be
real random variables with the
[conditional moments of the site frequency spectrum](sfscond-moments.md) given `G`: for all
`i, j ∈ ℕ`, `ξ_i` and `L_i` are square integrable, and almost surely `E[ξ_i | G] = (θ / 2) L_i`
and `E[ξ_i ξ_j | G] = (θ / 2)² L_i L_j + δ_{ij} (θ / 2) L_i`. Then for every `1 ≤ i ≤ n - 1`
and every `j ∈ ℕ`,

`Cov(ξ_i, ξ_j) = δ_{ij} θ / i + (θ / 2)² Cov(L_i, L_j)`.

The diagonal term is Fu's expected spectrum `(θ / 2) E[L_i] = θ / i`
([expected site frequency spectrum](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/integral-sfs.md)), and
Fu's `σ_{ij}` is `Cov(L_i, L_j) / 4` (Fu 1995).
