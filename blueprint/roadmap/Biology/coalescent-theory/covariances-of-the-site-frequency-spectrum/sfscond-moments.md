# Conditional moments of the site frequency spectrum given the genealogy

Let `μ` be a measure on a measurable space `Ω`, let `m` be a σ-algebra on `Ω`, let `θ` be a real
number, and let `ξ_i` and `L_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be real functions on `Ω`. The counts `ξ` have the
conditional moments of the site frequency spectrum given `m`, with branch lengths `L`, when for
all `i, j ∈ ℕ`:

- `ξ_i` and `L_i` are square integrable under `μ`;
- `E[ξ_i | m] = (θ / 2) L_i` almost everywhere;
- `E[ξ_i ξ_j | m] = (θ / 2)² L_i L_j + δ_{ij} (θ / 2) L_i` almost everywhere.

These are the first two conditional moments of counts `ξ_i` that, given `m`, are independent Poisson
variables with means `(θ / 2) L_i`: the conditional covariance of `ξ_i` and `ξ_j` is
`δ_{ij} (θ / 2) L_i`. The conditions fix only these two moments; they do not assert that the `ξ_i`
are conditionally Poisson or conditionally independent. For instance, given a trivial `m`, a
variable equal to `0` or `2` with probability `1/2` each has mean and variance `1` without being
Poisson. In the coalescent, `ξ_i` is the number of sites whose derived allele is carried by exactly
`i` sample copies, `L_i` is the total length of the branches subtending exactly `i` copies, and `m`
is generated by the genealogy; neutral infinite-sites mutation at rate `θ / 2` per lineage gives
these moments, because distinct size classes occupy disjoint sets of branches. The conditional
expectation is `0` by convention when `m` is not contained in the σ-algebra of `Ω`, when `μ`
restricted to `m` is not σ-finite, or when the function is not integrable. The formulas for
unconditional moments and covariances derived from these conditions take `μ` a probability measure
and `m` contained in the σ-algebra of `Ω`; the results that establish the conditions need not assume
that containment.
