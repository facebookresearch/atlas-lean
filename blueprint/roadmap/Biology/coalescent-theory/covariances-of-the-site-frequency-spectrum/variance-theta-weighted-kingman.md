# Variance of a weighted SFS estimator

Let `μ` be a probability measure and let `T_k` (`k ∈ ℕ`) be
[Kingman holding times](../kingman-s-coalescent-and-watterson-s-estimator/kingman-times.md) of a
sample of size `n` under `μ`, each `T_k` measurable: for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, `T_k` has the exponential law
with rate `C(k, 2)`, and `T_2, …, T_n` are independent. Let `τ` be a random block-size history such
that every preimage `τ⁻¹(A)` is measurable, with the
[random-joining law](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/random-joining.md) from `n` singletons,
`μ(τ = ps) = R_{n-1}((1, …, 1), ps)` for every `ps` (with `n - 1` truncated at `0`: for `n = 0`,
`R_0` starts from the empty list), and independent of the family `(T_k)_{k ∈ ℕ}`. Let
`L_i = ∑_{k=2}^{n} c_{k,i} T_k` be the
[total length of the branches subtending `i` sample copies](../expected-site-frequency-spectrum/sfs-length.md),
where `c_{k,i}` is the number of blocks of size `i` in entry `n - k` of `τ` (the empty list, which
contributes `0`, if `τ` has no such entry), and let `G` be the σ-algebra generated by `τ` and the
`T_k`. Let `θ` be a real number and let `ξ_i` (`i ∈ ℕ`) be real random variables with the
[conditional moments of the site frequency spectrum](sfscond-moments.md) given `G`: for all
`i, j ∈ ℕ`, `ξ_i` and `L_i` are square integrable, and almost surely `E[ξ_i | G] = (θ / 2) L_i` and
`E[ξ_i ξ_j | G] = (θ / 2)² L_i L_j + δ_{ij} (θ / 2) L_i`. For real weights `ω_i`, let
`θ_ω = ∑_{i=1}^{n-1} ω_i i ξ_i` be
[Achaz's weighted estimator](../site-frequency-spectrum-under-variable-populatio/theta-weighted.md).
Then

`Var(θ_ω) = θ ∑_{i=1}^{n-1} ω_i² i + (θ / 2)² Var(∑_{i=1}^{n-1} ω_i i L_i)`

(Achaz 2009). The variance inside the second term equals `∑_{i,j} ω_i ω_j i j Cov(L_i, L_j)`, so the
second term is `(θ / 2)²` times that sum; for `1 ≤ i, j ≤ n - 1`, Fu's `σ_{ij}` is
`Cov(L_i, L_j) / 4`.
