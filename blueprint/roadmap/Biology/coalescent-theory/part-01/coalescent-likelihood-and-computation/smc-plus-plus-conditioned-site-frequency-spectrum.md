# SMC++: a distinguished pair with the conditioned site-frequency spectrum

Terhorst, Kamm and Song (2017, Online Methods) keep the hidden state of the pairwise sequentially Markov coalescent, the time to the most recent common ancestor of the two haplotypes of one distinguished individual, and enlarge its emissions: at each site they emit the pair $(a, b) \in \{0, 1, 2\} \times \{0, 1, \dots, n - 2\}$ of derived-allele counts in the distinguished individual and in the other $n - 2$ undistinguished haplotypes. The law of $(a, b)$ given that the distinguished coalescence time lies in a given interval is the conditioned site-frequency spectrum. Once the conditioning on the coalescence interval is integrated out, the expected number of sites with $k$ derived copies among all $n$ haplotypes is the sum of the joint law of $(a, b)$ over $a + b = k$, while summing over $a$ with $b$ fixed gives the spectrum of the $n - 2$ undistinguished haplotypes. The conditioned spectrum is derived in their Supplementary Note with the mutation rate assumed known. The hidden Markov model assumes that observations are conditionally independent given the hidden state, which holds for the distinguished individual alone up to the SMC′ approximation and time discretization but fails with the undistinguished haplotypes, whose branch lengths stay correlated along the genome; they therefore emit the full conditioned spectrum only at every $k$-th site, with $k = Cn$ and $C = 400$ in practice. Transitions use a continuous-time conditioned Markov chain approximation derived from the two-locus coalescent with recombination, which integrates over any number of recombinations and back-coalescences between neighbouring sites. The model is fitted by an expectation–maximization algorithm exploiting the sparsity of polymorphic sites, the size history is regularized by fitting a twice continuously differentiable cubic spline, and with several distinguished pairs the log-likelihoods are summed as a composite likelihood (Results). Because the emissions are allele counts, the method needs no phased data.

## Expansion

1. The distinguished-pair hidden state and its transition approximation from the two-locus coalescent.
2. The conditioned site-frequency spectrum given the distinguished coalescence time, and its marginalizations: the $n$-sample spectrum by summing over $a + b = k$ after integrating out the conditioning, and the $(n-2)$-sample spectrum by summing over $a$.
3. The failure of conditional independence with undistinguished haplotypes and the thinning rule $k = Cn$.
4. The sparse expectation–maximization algorithm, the spline regularization of the size history and the composite likelihood over distinguished pairs, as modelling and estimation choices distinct from the likelihood of the ancestral recombination graph.
