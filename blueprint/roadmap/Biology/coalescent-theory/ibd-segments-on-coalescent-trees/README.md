# IBD segments on coalescent trees

Around a focal genomic position, the haplotype segment two samples share identical by descent extends until the first crossover on any branch of the coalescent tree separating them from their common ancestor. Treating recombination endpoints as exponential variables attached to branches gives exact segment-length laws and explains why endpoint comparisons in simulation concentrate at the deepest coalescences, where pruning and merging of haplotypes are most effective.
