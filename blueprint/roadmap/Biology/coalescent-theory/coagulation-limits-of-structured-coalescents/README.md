# Coagulation limits of structured coalescents

A structured coalescent with $d$ colonies, fast migration and a sample of size comparable to (or larger than) the migration scale has an empirical measure of block colour-configurations that converges to a deterministic Smoluchowski-type coagulation equation with migration terms. Its solutions are represented by multi-type branching processes (critical sampling) or the entrance law of a multi-type Feller diffusion (large sampling).
