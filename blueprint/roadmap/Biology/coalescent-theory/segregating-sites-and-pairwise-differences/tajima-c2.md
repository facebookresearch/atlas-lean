# Tajima's constant $c_2$

For a natural number $n$,

$$c_2(n) = b_2(n) - \frac{n+2}{a_1(n)\, n} + \frac{a_2(n)}{a_1(n)^2},$$

where $a_1$, $a_2$ and $b_2$ are [Tajima's constants](tajima-a1.md) and division by $0$ gives $0$; the constant is used for $n \ge 2$.

## Sources

- F. Tajima, *Statistical method for testing the neutral mutation hypothesis by DNA polymorphism*, Genetics 123 (1989), 585–595.
