# Probability that a pair lies in separate lineages

For natural numbers $n$ and $k$, let

$$p_{n,k} = \frac{(k-1)(n+1)}{(n-1)(k+1)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$ (so $p_{1,k} = 0$). For $n \ge 2$ and $2 \le k \le n$, this is the probability that a given pair of genes of a sample of size $n$ is still in two distinct lineages while the genealogy has $k$ lineages.
