# Tajima's constant $e_1$

For a natural number $n$, $e_1(n) = c_1(n) / a_1(n)$, where $a_1$ and $c_1$ are [Tajima's constants](tajima-c1.md) and division by $0$ gives $0$; the constant is used for $n \ge 2$.

## Sources

- F. Tajima, *Statistical method for testing the neutral mutation hypothesis by DNA polymorphism*, Genetics 123 (1989), 585–595.
