# Tajima's constant $e_2$

For a natural number $n$, $e_2(n) = c_2(n) / \bigl(a_1(n)^2 + a_2(n)\bigr)$, where $a_1$, $a_2$ and $c_2$ are [Tajima's constants](tajima-c2.md) and division by $0$ gives $0$; the constant is used for $n \ge 2$.

## Sources

- F. Tajima, *Statistical method for testing the neutral mutation hypothesis by DNA polymorphism*, Genetics 123 (1989), 585–595.
