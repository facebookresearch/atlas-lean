# Variance of the total branch length

Let $(T_k)$ be Kingman holding times of a sample of size $n$ under a measure $\mu$, with total branch length $L = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k\,T_k$. Then

$$\operatorname{Var}_\mu(L) = 4\, a_2(n) = 4 \sum_{j=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{j^2}.$$

By independence of the holding times, $\operatorname{Var}_\mu(L) = \sum_{k=2}^{n} k^2 \operatorname{Var}_\mu(T_k)$.

## Sources

- G. A. Watterson, *On the number of segregating sites in genetical models without recombination*, Theoret. Population Biol. 7 (1975), 256–276.
