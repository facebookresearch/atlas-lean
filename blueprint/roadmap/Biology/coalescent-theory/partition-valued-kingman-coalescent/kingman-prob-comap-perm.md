# Kingman's formula is invariant under relabeling

For every permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, [Kingman's formula](kingman-prob.md) gives the relabeled partition $\sigma^{-1}(\pi)$, whose blocks are the preimages $\sigma^{-1}(B)$ of the blocks $B$ of $\pi$, the same value as $\pi$.
