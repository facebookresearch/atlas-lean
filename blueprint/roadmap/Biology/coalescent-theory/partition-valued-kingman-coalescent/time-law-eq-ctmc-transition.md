# Time-t marginal as a continuous-time chain transition

For every natural number $n$, every real $t$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, the [time-$t$ marginal](time-law.md) $e^{t Q_n}(\bot, \pi)$ is the entry, in the row of the partition into singletons and the column of $\pi$, of the matrix exponential $e^{t Q_n}$, which for $t \ge 0$ is the transition function $P_t$ of the continuous-time Markov chain with rate matrix $Q_n$.
