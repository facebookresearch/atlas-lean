# Consistency of the jump chains

Let $n \ge 1$ and let $\boldsymbol{\rho}$ be a list of $n$ partitions in $\mathcal{P}_n$. Sum the [path probability](path-prob.md) of the $(n+1)$-jump chain over all lists $(\pi_0, \dots, \pi_n)$ of $n + 1$ partitions in $\mathcal{P}_{n+1}$ such that restricting each $\pi_i$ to the first $n$ genes and deleting consecutive repetitions gives $\boldsymbol{\rho}$. The sum equals the path probability of $\boldsymbol{\rho}$ for the $n$-jump chain.
