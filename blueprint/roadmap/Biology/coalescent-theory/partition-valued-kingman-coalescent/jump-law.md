# Law of Kingman's jump chain after m jumps

For natural numbers $n$ and $m$ and a partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, the number $J_n^m(\bot, \pi)$, the entry of the $m$-th power of the [jump matrix](jump-matrix.md) in the row of the partition into singletons and the column of $\pi$: the probability that the jump chain started from singletons is at $\pi$ after $m$ jumps.
