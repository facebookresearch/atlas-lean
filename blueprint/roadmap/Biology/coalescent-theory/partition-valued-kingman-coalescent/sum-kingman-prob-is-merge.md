# Backward equation of Kingman's formula

For every partition $\pi' \in \mathcal{P}_n$ with $k$ blocks, $1 \le k < n$,

$$\sum_{\pi} p_n(\pi) = \binom{k+1}{2} p_n(\pi'),$$

where the sum runs over the partitions $\pi$ from which $\pi'$ [arises by merging two blocks](is-merge.md) and $p_n$ is [Kingman's formula](kingman-prob.md).
