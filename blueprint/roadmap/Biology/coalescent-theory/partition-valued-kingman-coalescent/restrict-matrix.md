# Restriction matrix on sample partitions

For a natural number $n$, the restriction matrix $R_n$ is the real matrix indexed by $\mathcal{P}_{n+1} \times \mathcal{P}_n$ with $R_n(\pi, \rho) = 1$ if the restriction of $\pi$ to the first $n$ genes $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ (the nonempty traces of its blocks) is $\rho$, and $R_n(\pi, \rho) = 0$ otherwise.
