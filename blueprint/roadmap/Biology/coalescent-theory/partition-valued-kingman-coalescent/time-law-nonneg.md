# Nonnegativity of the time-t marginal

For every natural number $n$, every real $t \ge 0$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, the [time-$t$ marginal](time-law.md) is nonnegative; with [the sum over all partitions](sum-time-law.md), it is a probability distribution on the partitions of the sample.
