# Partition obtained by merging two blocks

For partitions $\pi, \pi' \in \mathcal{P}_n$ of the sample $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, $\pi'$ arises from $\pi$ by merging two blocks if $\pi$ refines $\pi'$ and $|\pi| = |\pi'| + 1$. Then exactly two blocks of $\pi$ lie in one block of $\pi'$, and the other blocks of $\pi$ are blocks of $\pi'$.
