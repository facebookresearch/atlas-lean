# Kingman's formula

For a natural number $n$ and a partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$ with $k = |\pi|$ blocks, let

$$p_n(\pi) = \frac{(n-k)!\, k!\, (k-1)! \prod_{B \in \pi} |B|!}{n!\,(n-1)!},$$

where $k - 1$ and $n - 1$ are read as $0$ when $k = 0$ or $n = 0$. For $1 \le k \le n$ this is Kingman's probability of $\pi$; for the empty sample it gives $1$ to the partition with no blocks.
