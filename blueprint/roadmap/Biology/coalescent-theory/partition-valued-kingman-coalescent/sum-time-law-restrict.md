# Consistency of the partition-valued coalescents

For every natural number $n$, every real $t$ and every partition $\rho \in \mathcal{P}_n$,

$$\sum_{\pi} e^{t Q_{n+1}}(\bot, \pi) = e^{t Q_n}(\bot, \rho),$$

where the sum runs over the partitions $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_{n+1}$ whose restriction to the first $n$ genes is $\rho$. For $t \ge 0$: the time-$t$ law of the restriction of the $(n+1)$-coalescent to the first $n$ genes is the time-$t$ law of the $n$-coalescent.

- J. F. C. Kingman, *The coalescent*, Stochastic Process. Appl. 13 (1982), 235–248.
