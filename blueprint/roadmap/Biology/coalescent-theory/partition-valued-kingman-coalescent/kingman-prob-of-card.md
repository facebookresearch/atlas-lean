# Kingman's formula for a partition with k blocks

For every natural number $k$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$ with $k$ blocks,

$$p_n(\pi) = \frac{(n-k)!\, k!\, (k-1)! \prod_{B \in \pi} |B|!}{n!\,(n-1)!},$$

with $k - 1$ and $n - 1$ read as $0$ when $k = 0$ or $n = 0$ ([Kingman's formula](kingman-prob.md)).
