# Time-t marginal of Kingman's coalescent

For a natural number $n$, a real number $t$ and a partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, the time-$t$ marginal is $e^{t Q_n}(\bot, \pi)$, the entry of the matrix exponential of $t$ times the [rate matrix](partition-rate.md) in the row of the partition into singletons and the column of $\pi$. For $t \ge 0$ it is the probability that the ancestral partition of the sample at time $t$, in units of $2N$ generations, is $\pi$; the matrix exponential is defined for every real $t$.
