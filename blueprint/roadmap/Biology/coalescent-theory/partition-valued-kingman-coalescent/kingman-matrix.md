# Kingman's formula arranged by number of blocks

For a natural number $n$, the real matrix $K_n$ indexed by $\{0, \dots, n\} \times \mathcal{P}_n$ with $K_n(k, \pi) = p_n(\pi)$, [Kingman's formula](kingman-prob.md), if $\pi$ has $k$ blocks, and $K_n(k, \pi) = 0$ otherwise. For $1 \le k \le n$, row $k$ is the law of the jump chain after $n - k$ jumps ([Kingman's formula](jump-law-eq.md)).
