# The paintbox representation for the partitions of Kingman's coalescent

This page covers a transport of the paintbox theorem to the coalescent.

Through the [equivalence of encodings](bridge-from-the-shared-paintbox-to-the-coalescen.md), [Kingman's paintbox representation](../../../Probability/foundations/exchangeable-random-partitions/kingman-paintbox-representation-of-exchangeable.md) applies to [Kingman's coalescent on the natural numbers](kingman-coalescent-on-n-as-a-time-indexed-cadlag.md): for every $t$, $\Pi_t$ is an exchangeable random partition of $\mathbb{N}$ under finite permutations, hence a mixture of paintboxes, and its ranked block frequencies exist almost surely. The paintbox theorem is used, not proved a second time.

## Expansion

State each part as a page of its own, with hypotheses fixed from a source:

1. Exchangeability of $\Pi_t$ for each $t$.
2. The transport of the paintbox representation, and its compatibility with restriction and permutation.
