# Lumpability of restriction

For every natural number $n$,

$$Q_{n+1} R_n = R_n Q_n,$$

where $Q_m$ is the [partition rate matrix](partition-rate.md) of the $m$-coalescent and $R_n$ the [restriction matrix](restrict-matrix.md) to the first $n$ genes: restriction intertwines the rate matrices of the $(n+1)$- and $n$-coalescents.

## Sources

- J. F. C. Kingman, *The coalescent*, Stochastic Process. Appl. 13 (1982), 235–248.
