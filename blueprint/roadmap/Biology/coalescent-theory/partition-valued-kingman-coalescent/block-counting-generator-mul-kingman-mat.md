# Kingman's formula intertwines the block-counting generator with the partition rate matrix

For every natural number $n$,

$$G_n K_n = K_n Q_n,$$

where $G_n$ is the [generator of Kingman's block-counting process](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/block-counting-generator.md), $K_n$ is [Kingman's formula arranged by number of blocks](kingman-matrix.md) and $Q_n$ is the [partition rate matrix](partition-rate.md). This is the [backward equation of Kingman's formula](sum-kingman-prob-is-merge.md) in matrix form.
