# Exchangeability of the jump chain

For natural numbers $m < n$, every permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$, the [law of the jump chain](jump-law.md) after $m$ jumps gives the relabeled partition $\sigma^{-1}(\pi)$ the same probability as $\pi$: relabeling the sample does not change the law of the partition after $m$ coalescences.
