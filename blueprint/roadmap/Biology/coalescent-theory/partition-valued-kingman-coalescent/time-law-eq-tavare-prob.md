# Time-t marginal via Tavaré's formula

For every natural number $n \ge 1$, every real $t$ and every partition $\pi \in \mathcal{P}_n$,

$$e^{t Q_n}(\bot, \pi) = q_{n,|\pi|}(t)\; p_n(\pi),$$

where $q_{n,k}(t)$ is [Tavaré's formula](../ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/tavare-prob.md) and $p_n$ [Kingman's formula](kingman-prob.md).
