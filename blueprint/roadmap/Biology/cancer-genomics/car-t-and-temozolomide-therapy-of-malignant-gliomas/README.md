# CAR-T and temozolomide therapy of malignant gliomas

A five-compartment model of malignant glioma under combined temozolomide (TMZ) chemotherapy and CAR-T cell immunotherapy, with tumor subpopulations sensitive to both therapies, resistant to CAR-T, or resistant to TMZ (TMZ-induced), CAR-T cells stimulated and exhausted by the tumor, and impulsive drug and cell administrations; the analysis identifies the attracting carrying-capacity plane after single doses and an explicit eradication criterion under continuous dosing.
