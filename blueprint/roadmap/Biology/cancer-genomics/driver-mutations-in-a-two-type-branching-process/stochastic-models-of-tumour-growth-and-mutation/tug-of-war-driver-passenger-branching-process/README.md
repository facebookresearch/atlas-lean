# Tug-of-war driver-passenger branching process

Cancer cells accumulate rare strongly advantageous driver mutations and frequent mildly deleterious passenger mutations. When one driver's fitness effect equals that of $L$ passengers, the cell type becomes an integer fitness level and the population is a branching process with countably many types; the type along a surviving lineage is a Markov chain whose recurrence is decided by the sign of $\nu-\mu L$.
