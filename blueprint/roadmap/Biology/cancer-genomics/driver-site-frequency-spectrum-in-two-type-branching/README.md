# Driver site frequency spectrum in two-type branching

In a supercritical two-type birth-death process where wild-type cells produce fitter driver mutants, the driver site frequency spectrum counts driver clones by size. Its small-frequency part obeys a law of large numbers with a power-law profile, it has a sharp cutoff at sizes of order $e^{\lambda_0\lambda_1 t/(\lambda_0+\lambda_1)}$ where counts become Poisson, and large-clone frequencies freeze at a random point-process limit.
