# Exact size of the detection test

For $0 \le \mathrm{FPR} < 1$, every natural number $n$ and every real $p_0$, the [power](power.md) at $f = p_0$ equals $\mathrm{FPR}$: for $0 \le p_0 \le 1$, the rejection probability under the null is exactly $\mathrm{FPR}$ (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 9 with the randomization at $k - 1$).
