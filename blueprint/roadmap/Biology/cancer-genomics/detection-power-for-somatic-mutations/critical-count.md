# Critical count of the mutation detection test

For a natural number $n$ and reals $p_0$ (the null probability $\varepsilon/3$) and $\mathrm{FPR}$, the critical count is

$$k = \min\{m \in \mathbb{N} : P_{p_0}(m) \le \mathrm{FPR}\},$$

where $P_{p_0}$ is the [upper tail](upper-tail.md), with the convention $k = 0$ when the set is empty (Carter et al. 2012).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421 (Online Methods, Eqs. 9, 10).
