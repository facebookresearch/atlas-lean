# Tail gap at the critical count

For $0 \le \mathrm{FPR} < 1$, every natural number $n$ and every real $p_0$, the [critical count](critical-count.md) $k$ satisfies

$$P_{p_0}(k - 1) - P_{p_0}(k) = B(k - 1 \mid n, p_0),$$

where $P_{p_0}$ is the [upper tail](upper-tail.md) and $B$ the [binomial probability](binom-pmf.md).
