# Power increases with purity

Let $0 \le \mathrm{FPR} < 1$, $n$ a natural number, $p_0$ real, $0 \le \alpha \le \alpha' \le 1$, $q > 0$ and $0 \le s \le q$. With the allelic fraction $f(\alpha, q, s) = \alpha s / (\alpha q + 2(1 - \alpha))$, the [power](power.md) satisfies

$$\mathrm{Pow}\bigl(n, f(\alpha, q, s)\bigr) \le \mathrm{Pow}\bigl(n, f(\alpha', q, s)\bigr)$$

(Carter et al. 2012, Eqs. 2, 9, 10).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eqs. 2, 9, 10.
