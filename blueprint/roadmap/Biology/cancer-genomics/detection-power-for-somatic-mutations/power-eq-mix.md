# Detection power as a mixture of tail probabilities

For $0 \le \mathrm{FPR} < 1$, every natural number $n$ and all reals $p_0, f$,

$$\mathrm{Pow}(n, f) = (1 - d)\, P_f(k) + d\, P_f(k - 1),$$

where $\mathrm{Pow}$ is the [power](power.md), $k$ the [critical count](critical-count.md), $d$ the [randomization probability](randomization.md) and $P_f$ the [upper tail](upper-tail.md).
