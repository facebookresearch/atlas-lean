# Binomial probability mass function

For a natural number $n$, a real $p$ and a natural number $i$, put

$$B(i \mid n, p) = \binom{n}{i} p^i (1 - p)^{n - i},$$

with $\binom{n}{i} = 0$ for $i > n$ (so the truncated subtraction $n - i$ is harmless). Any real $p$ is allowed; for $0 \le p \le 1$ it is the probability of $i$ successes in $n$ independent trials with success probability $p$.
