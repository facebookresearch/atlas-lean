# Big Bang model of tumour growth

A spatial model of colorectal tumour expansion in which most detectable subclonal alterations arise
early, in the first few thousand glands, and are then carried through the growing tumour without
later selective sweeps; fitted to multi-region single-gland genomic data by approximate Bayesian
computation.
