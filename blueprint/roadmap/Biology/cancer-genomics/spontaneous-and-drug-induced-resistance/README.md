# Spontaneous and drug-induced resistance

Two-compartment tumour models separating sensitive and resistant cells in which resistance arises both spontaneously at a constant rate and in proportion to the applied drug dose. The induced component changes which treatment schedules work best, and the induction rate is structurally identifiable from tumour-volume measurements under designed dosing.
