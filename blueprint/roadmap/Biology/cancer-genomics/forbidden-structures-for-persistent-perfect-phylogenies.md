# Forbidden structures for persistent perfect phylogenies

Find a forbidden-substructure characterization of the maximal red-black graphs, those in which no character's species set is a proper subset of another's, that admit a persistent (Dollo-1) perfect phylogeny. Such a characterization would give understandable certificates of infeasibility.

## What would settle it

Define the red-black graph model and an independently specified obstruction condition. Prove that a maximal graph admits a persistent perfect phylogeny exactly when the obstruction condition is absent. The embedding relation and whether a finite obstruction list exists are part of the question; induced-subgraph closure must not be assumed without proof.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Bonizzoni, Della Vedova, Soto Gomez and Trucco give a polynomial-time recognition algorithm for maximal graphs, in which no character's species set is a proper subset of another's, and state that recognition by detecting forbidden substructures is an open problem.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
