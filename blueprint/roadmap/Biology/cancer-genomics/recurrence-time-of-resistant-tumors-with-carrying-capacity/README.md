# Recurrence time of resistant tumors with carrying capacity

Under therapy a tumor of drug-sensitive cells shrinks while pre-existing and newly mutated resistant cells regrow under a density-dependent birth rate. In the large-population limit with power-law scalings of the initial resistant fraction and the mutation rate, the recurrence time, the resistant clone sizes at recurrence and their diversity obey laws of large numbers on the $\log n$ scale, which yield consistent estimators of the evolutionary parameters.
