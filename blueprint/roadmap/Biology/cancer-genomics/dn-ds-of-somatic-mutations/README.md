# dN/dS of somatic mutations

Poisson-likelihood dN/dS ratios for somatic point mutations, with context-dependent substitution
rates, a gamma-Poisson (negative binomial) background model of the gene-level mutation rate, and the
excess of non-synonymous mutations that converts dN/dS into a count of driver mutations.

- [Poisson dN/dS model with trinucleotide substitution rates](poisson-dn-ds-model-with-trinucleotide-substitution-rates.md)
- [Gamma-Poisson shrinkage of the gene mutation rate in dNdScv](gamma-poisson-shrinkage-of-the-gene-mutation-rate-in-dndscv.md)
- [Excess driver mutations from dN/dS](excess-driver-mutations-from-dn-ds.md)
