# Mutation order and clonal diversity of malignant cells

When malignant transformation needs two driver mutations that can be acquired in either order, malignant clones arise along two pathways. Under the successive exponential approximation of multitype branching processes, the expected Simpson index of malignant clone sizes has an explicit limit that weighs the two pathways by their effective founding rates.
