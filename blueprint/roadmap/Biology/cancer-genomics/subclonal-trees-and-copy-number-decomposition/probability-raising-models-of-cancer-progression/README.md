# Probability-raising models of cancer progression

Cross-sectional mutation data from tumour samples are summarized by the marginal and joint frequencies of binary alteration events. Suppes' probability-raising notion of causation, combined with temporal priority read off event frequencies, gives a criterion for orienting edges of a progression tree (oncotree), and a shrinkage-like combination of a normalized probability-raising score with a correlation score yields a reconstruction algorithm that is consistent in the infinite-sample limit.
