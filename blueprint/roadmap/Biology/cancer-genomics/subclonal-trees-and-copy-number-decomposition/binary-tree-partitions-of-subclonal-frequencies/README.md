# Binary tree partitions of subclonal frequencies

Under the infinite sites assumption and the assumption that at most one clonal expansion starts at a time, the cellular fractions of the mutation clusters of a single tumour sample are the node weights of a complete rooted binary tree in which every internal node's weight equals the sum of its children's weights. Deciding whether a multiset of observed frequencies admits such a tree is a packing problem for "triangles" $a_i + a_j = a_k$; it is strongly NP-complete, and its maximization version admits a local-search approximation.
