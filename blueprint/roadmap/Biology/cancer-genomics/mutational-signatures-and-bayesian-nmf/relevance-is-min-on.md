# The relevance update is the exact minimizer

Let $A > 0$ and $c > 0$. Then the function $\lambda \mapsto A/\lambda + c \log \lambda$ attains its minimum over $\lambda > 0$ at $\lambda = A / c$ (Tan and Févotte, arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.3).

## Sources

- V. Y. F. Tan and C. Févotte, *Automatic relevance determination in nonnegative matrix factorization with the β-divergence*, IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell. 35 (2013), 1592–1605; arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.3.
