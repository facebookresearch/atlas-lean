# The H update does not increase the objective

Let $\phi > 0$, $\lambda_k > 0$ for every $k$, $V \ge 0$ and $W \ge 0$ entrywise with every row of $W$ having a positive entry, and $H > 0$ entrywise. Then

$$C_H(H') \le C_H(H),$$

where $C_H$ is the [H-dependent objective](objective-h.md) and $H'$ the [updated matrix](update-h.md) (Tan and Févotte, arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.2).

## Sources

- V. Y. F. Tan and C. Févotte, *Automatic relevance determination in nonnegative matrix factorization with the β-divergence*, IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell. 35 (2013), 1592–1605; arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.2.
