# Pruned components are those at the relevance floor

Let $c > 0$, let $W$, $H$ be entrywise nonnegative, $b$ real and $k$ a component. Then the [relevance update](relevance.md) satisfies $\lambda_k = b / c$ if and only if

- $w_{fk} = 0$ for every $f$, and
- $h_{kn} = 0$ for every $n$

(Tan and Févotte, arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.4).

## Sources

- V. Y. F. Tan and C. Févotte, *Automatic relevance determination in nonnegative matrix factorization with the β-divergence*, IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell. 35 (2013), 1592–1605; arXiv:1111.6085v3, §4.4.
