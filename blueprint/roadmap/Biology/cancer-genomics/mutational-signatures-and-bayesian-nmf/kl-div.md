# Generalized Kullback–Leibler divergence

For real numbers $x, y$, the generalized Kullback–Leibler divergence is

$$d(x \mid y) = x \log \frac{x}{y} - x + y,$$

with the conventions $x/0 = 0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log z = \log|z|$ for $z < 0$. In particular $d(0 \mid y) = y$; the usual reading needs $y > 0$ when $x > 0$ (Tan and Févotte, arXiv:1111.6085v3, Eq. 4 with $\beta = 1$).

## Sources

- V. Y. F. Tan and C. Févotte, *Automatic relevance determination in nonnegative matrix factorization with the β-divergence*, IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell. 35 (2013), 1592–1605; arXiv:1111.6085v3, Eq. 4.
