# The GISTIC permutation null is the law of a sum of independent per-sample draws

For all sample values $x_j(v)$ ($S$ samples, $M$ markers), every marker $m$ and every real $g$,

$$P_S(g) = \frac{N(g)}{M^S},$$

where $P_S$ is the [permutation null](permutation-null.md) at $m$ and $N(g)$ the [score count](score-count.md); in particular the null does not depend on $m$ (Mermel et al. 2011).

## Sources

- C. H. Mermel et al., *GISTIC2.0 facilitates sensitive and confident localization of the targets of focal somatic copy-number alteration in human cancers*, Genome Biol. 12 (2011), R41.
