# Survival-associated mutated subnetworks

Finding connected sets of genes in an interaction network whose mutations, taken as a union over patients, best separate survival curves under the log-rank test. The topic covers the computational hardness of the optimization and the behavior of color-coding when the subnetwork score is not set-additive.
