# Exact site frequency spectrum of birth–death tumors

Exact finite-size expected site frequency spectra of neutral mutations in a tumor growing as a binary birth–death branching process, for the surviving skeleton and for the whole population, at a fixed time or at the random time the tumor reaches a given size. Cell death changes the shape of the spectrum, interpolating between the exponential-growth $1/j^2$ law and the constant-size $1/j$ law.
