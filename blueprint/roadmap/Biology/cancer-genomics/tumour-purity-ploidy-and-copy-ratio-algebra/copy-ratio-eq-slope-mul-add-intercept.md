# The copy ratio is affine in the copy number

For all real $\alpha, \tau, q$,

$$R(\alpha, \tau, q) = b(\alpha, \tau)\, q + \delta(\alpha, \tau),$$

where $R$ is the [copy ratio](copy-ratio.md), $b$ the [slope](slope.md) and $\delta$ the [intercept](intercept.md) (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 1).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 1.
