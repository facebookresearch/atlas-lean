# Copy-ratio slope

For real numbers $\alpha$ and $\tau$, the copy-ratio slope is $b(\alpha, \tau) = \alpha / D(\alpha, \tau)$, where $D(\alpha, \tau) = \alpha\tau + 2(1 - \alpha)$, with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 1).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 1.
