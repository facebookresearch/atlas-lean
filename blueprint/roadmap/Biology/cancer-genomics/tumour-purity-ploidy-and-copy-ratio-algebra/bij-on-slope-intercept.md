# Purity and ploidy are identifiable from the copy-ratio slope and intercept

The map $(\alpha, \tau) \mapsto \bigl(b(\alpha, \tau), \delta(\alpha, \tau)\bigr)$, with $b$ the [slope](slope.md) and $\delta$ the [intercept](intercept.md), is a bijection from $\{(\alpha, \tau) : 0 < \alpha \le 1,\ \tau > 0\}$ onto $\{(b, \delta) : b > 0,\ 0 \le \delta < 1\}$.
