# Relative copy ratio

For real numbers $\alpha$ (purity), $\tau$ (cancer-cell ploidy) and $q$ (local copy number), the relative copy ratio is

$$R(\alpha, \tau, q) = \frac{D(\alpha, q)}{D(\alpha, \tau)} = \frac{\alpha q + 2(1 - \alpha)}{\alpha \tau + 2(1 - \alpha)},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 1).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 1.
