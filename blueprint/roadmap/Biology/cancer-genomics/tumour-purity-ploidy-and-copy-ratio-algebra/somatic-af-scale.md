# Ploidy scaling leaves somatic allelic fractions unchanged

For $m > 0$, $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ and all real $q, s$,

$$f(\alpha_m, mq, ms) = f(\alpha, q, s),$$

where $f$ is the [somatic allelic fraction](somatic-af.md) and $\alpha_m$ the [rescaled purity](scaled-purity.md) (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 2).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 2.
