# Expected allelic fraction of a somatic mutation

For real numbers $\alpha$ (purity), $q$ (local copy number) and $s$ (multiplicity), the expected allelic fraction of a clonal somatic mutation is

$$f(\alpha, q, s) = \frac{\alpha s}{\alpha q + 2(1 - \alpha)},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 2; Eq. 10 is $s = 1$).
