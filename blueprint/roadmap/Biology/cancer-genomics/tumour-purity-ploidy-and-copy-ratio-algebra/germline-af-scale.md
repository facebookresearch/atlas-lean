# Ploidy scaling leaves germline allelic fractions unchanged

For $m > 0$, $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ and all real $q, g$, the [germline allelic fraction](germline-af.md) at purity $\alpha_m$, copy number $mq$ and multiplicity $mg$ equals that at $\alpha$, $q$, $g$, where $\alpha_m$ is the [rescaled purity](scaled-purity.md) (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 11).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 11.
