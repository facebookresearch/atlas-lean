# Rescaled purity

For real numbers $m$ and $\alpha$, the rescaled purity is

$$\alpha_m = \frac{\alpha}{m - (m - 1)\alpha},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$. For $m > 0$ and $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ it is the purity that, together with all copy numbers multiplied by $m$, reproduces the copy ratios and allelic fractions of purity $\alpha$; this rescaling is derived from the copy-ratio and allelic-fraction formulas of Carter et al. (2012).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421.
