# Calibrated copy ratios have mean one

Let $s$ be a finite set of segments with real weights $w_i$ and copy numbers $q_i$ such that $\sum_{i \in s} w_i = 1$, let $\alpha$ be real, put $\tau = \sum_{i \in s} w_i q_i$, and suppose $D(\alpha, \tau) = \alpha\tau + 2(1 - \alpha) \ne 0$. Then

$$\sum_{i \in s} w_i\, R(\alpha, \tau, q_i) = 1,$$

where $R$ is the [copy ratio](copy-ratio.md) (Carter et al. 2012).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421 (Eqs. 1, 2, 10, 11).
