# Allelic fractions are determined by the copy-ratio slope

Let $\alpha, \tau, q, s$ be real with $D(\alpha, \tau) \ne 0$. Then

$$f(\alpha, q, s) = \frac{b(\alpha, \tau)\, s}{R(\alpha, \tau, q)},$$

where $f$ is the [somatic allelic fraction](somatic-af.md), $b$ the [slope](slope.md) and $R$ the [copy ratio](copy-ratio.md), with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (Carter et al. 2012, Eq. 2).

## Sources

- S. L. Carter et al., *Absolute quantification of somatic DNA alterations in human cancer*, Nat. Biotechnol. 30 (2012), 413–421, Eq. 2.
