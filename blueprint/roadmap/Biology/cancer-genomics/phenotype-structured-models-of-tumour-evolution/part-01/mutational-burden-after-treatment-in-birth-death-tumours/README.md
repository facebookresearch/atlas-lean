# Mutational burden after treatment in birth–death tumours

In a branching birth–death model of tumour growth, neutral mutations accumulate at cell divisions. Conditioning on survival, the expected site frequency spectrum (SFS) and total mutational burden (tMB) of mutations arising during a time window, and of mutations already present at its start, are explicit integrals of the transition probabilities of the birth–death process. Applying them to a growth phase followed by a homogeneous treatment phase gives a null model for how genetic intra-tumour heterogeneity should change under treatment.
