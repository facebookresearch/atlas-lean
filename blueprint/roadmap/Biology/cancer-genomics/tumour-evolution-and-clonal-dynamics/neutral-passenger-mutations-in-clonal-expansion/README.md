# Neutral passenger mutations in a clonal expansion

A tumour or metastasis started by one cell is modelled as a multi-type linear birth–death process with birth rate $b$, death rate $d < b$ and per-division passenger mutation probability $u$. These articles give the eventual cell fraction of a passenger, the fixation probability of the $k$-th surviving passenger, and the expected numbers of clonal and subclonal passengers, all of which depend on the death–birth ratio $\delta = d/b$.
