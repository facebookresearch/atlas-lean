# Saturation power eventually exceeds every target below one

Let $0 \le p_0 < p_1 \le 1$, $\gamma > 0$ and $\beta < 1$. Then there is a natural number $N_0$ such that the [saturation power](saturation-power.md) satisfies $\mathrm{Pow}(N) \ge \beta$ for every $N \ge N_0$ (Lawrence et al. 2014 take $\beta = 0.9$). Power is not monotone in $N$, so the statement is for all sufficiently large $N$.
