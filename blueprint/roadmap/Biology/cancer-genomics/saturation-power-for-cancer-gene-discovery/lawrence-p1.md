# Signal mutation probability per patient

For reals $p_0$ (the background probability), $r$ (the fraction of patients mutated above background) and $m$ (the miss rate), put $p_1 = p_0 + r(1 - m)$ (Lawrence et al. 2014).

## Sources

- M. S. Lawrence et al., *Discovery and saturation analysis of cancer genes across 21 tumour types*, Nature 505 (2014), 495–501 (Methods, "Power calculations").
