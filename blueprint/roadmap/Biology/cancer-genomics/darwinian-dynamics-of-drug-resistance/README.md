# Darwinian dynamics of cancer drug resistance

Eco-evolutionary ("Darwinian") models of treated tumours couple Lotka–Volterra competition between drug-sensitive and drug-resistant cells with a continuous resistance strategy that climbs the fitness gradient of a fitness-generating function. Resistance lowers intrinsic growth and lowers the drug-induced death rate, so the evolutionarily singular resistance level solves a quadratic and the stability of every equilibrium reduces to signs of competition determinants and net growth rates.
