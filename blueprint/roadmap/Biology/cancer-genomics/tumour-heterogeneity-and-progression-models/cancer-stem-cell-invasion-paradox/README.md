# Cancer stem cell invasion paradox

In tumours driven by cancer stem cells (CSCs), a higher death rate of the non-stem tumour cells can speed up invasion, because their death frees space for stem-cell expansion. Geometric singular perturbation reduces the stem/non-stem reaction–diffusion system to a Fisher–KPP equation on a slow manifold, whose linearly determined wave speed increases with the death rate.
