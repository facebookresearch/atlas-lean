# Stochastic tumour biomarker shedding

Models linking unobserved tumour and immune population dynamics to noisy circulating biomarkers such as ctDNA, with shedding driven by cell death and first-order clearance. Stochastic differential equations give the mean, variance and expected detection time of the biomarker signal.
