# Stability of persistence curves for copy-number signals

Copy-number profiles are embedded as sliding-window point clouds and summarized by Betti, lifespan and landscape curves of their persistent homology. Combinatorial caps on persistence pairs give explicit bounds on how far these curves can move under perturbation of the data.
