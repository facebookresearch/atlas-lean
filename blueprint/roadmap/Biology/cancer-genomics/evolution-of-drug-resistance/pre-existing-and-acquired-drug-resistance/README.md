# Pre-existing and acquired drug resistance

Two elementary models separate the routes by which a treated tumour becomes resistant: a biexponential model of a pre-existing resistant subpopulation growing while sensitive cells decline, and a count of birth events in a slowly growing drug-tolerant population that bounds the chance of acquiring a resistance mutation during therapy.
