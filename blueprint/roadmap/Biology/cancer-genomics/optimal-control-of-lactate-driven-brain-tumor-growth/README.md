# Optimal control of lactate-driven brain tumor growth

Optimal therapy design for a brain tumor model that couples a Fisher–Kolmogorov equation for tumor cell density with lactate kinetics, a viscoelastic force balance, and a tissue-damage inclusion. A cytotoxic drug lowers proliferation and a lactate-targeting drug scales the lactate source; optimal controls exist and satisfy an adjoint-based variational inequality.
