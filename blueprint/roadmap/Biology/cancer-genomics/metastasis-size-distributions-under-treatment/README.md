# Metastasis size distributions under treatment

Mechanistic stochastic models of the natural history of metastatic cancer: primary tumor growth, Poisson shedding of metastases at a rate depending on primary size, random latency before inception in a secondary site, and deterministic metastasis growth that treatment can alter. Conditioning on the number of detectable metastases gives an explicit size distribution, which allows growth rates before and after surgery or chemotherapy to be estimated from a single imaging snapshot.
