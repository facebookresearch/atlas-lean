# The timing closed form

Let a [gain history](gain-history.md) have descendant counts $\delta_i \le B$ for a natural number $B \ge 1$, $c_1$ its [final copy number](final-copies.md) and $a \ge 1$ its number of [amplified copies](amplified.md), and let $\mu \ne 0$. Then for every real $t$,

$$t = \frac{c_1 \sum_{m=2}^{B} E_m}{a \sum_{m=0}^{B} m\, E_m},$$

where $E_m$ is the [expected count](expected-count.md).
