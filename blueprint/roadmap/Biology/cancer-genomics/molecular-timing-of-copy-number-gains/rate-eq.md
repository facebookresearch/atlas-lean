# The mutation rate from the spectrum

Let a [gain history](gain-history.md) have descendant counts $\delta_i \le B$ for a natural number $B \ge 1$ and [final copy number](final-copies.md) $c_1 > 0$. Then for all real $\mu, t$,

$$\mu = \frac{1}{c_1} \sum_{m=0}^{B} m\, E_m,$$

where $E_m$ is the [expected count](expected-count.md).
