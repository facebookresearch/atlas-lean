# Cahn–Hilliard–Keller–Segel tumor growth

Phase-field tumor growth models in which a Cahn–Hilliard equation for the tumor phase is coupled with Keller–Segel chemotaxis of tumor-induced vasculature toward an angiogenic signal, together with nutrient dynamics. The articles cover well-posedness with a separation property and the optimal control of the vasculature source.
