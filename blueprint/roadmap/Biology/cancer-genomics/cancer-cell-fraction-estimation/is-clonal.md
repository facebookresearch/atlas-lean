# Clonal call for a mutation

For grid points, prior, purity $\alpha$, copy number $q$, read counts $n, a$ and real $t, \gamma$, the mutation is called clonal if $P(c > t \mid a) > \gamma$, where $P(c > t \mid a)$ is the [posterior tail](posterior-tail.md). Landau et al. take $t = 0.95$ and $\gamma = 0.5$.
