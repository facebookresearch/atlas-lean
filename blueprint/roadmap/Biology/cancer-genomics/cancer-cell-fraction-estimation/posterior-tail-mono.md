# The CCF posterior is stochastically increasing in the read count

In a [grid model](grid-model.md), for natural numbers $a \le a' \le n$ and every real $t$, the [posterior tail](posterior-tail.md) satisfies $P(c > t \mid a) \le P(c > t \mid a')$ (Landau et al. 2013).

## Sources

- D. A. Landau et al., *Evolution and impact of subclonal mutations in chronic lymphocytic leukemia*, Cell 152 (2013), 714–726.
