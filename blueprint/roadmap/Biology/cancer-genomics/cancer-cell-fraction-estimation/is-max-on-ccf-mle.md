# The continuous CCF MLE

Let $0 < \alpha \le 1$, $q \ge 1$ and $n, a$ natural numbers with $0 < n$ and $a \le n$. Then the function $c \mapsto B(a \mid n, f(c))$ attains its maximum over $[0, 1]$ at the [maximum-likelihood CCF](ccf-mle.md) $\hat c$, where $B(a \mid n, p) = \binom{n}{a} p^a (1 - p)^{n - a}$ and $f(c) = \alpha c / (\alpha q + 2(1 - \alpha))$.
