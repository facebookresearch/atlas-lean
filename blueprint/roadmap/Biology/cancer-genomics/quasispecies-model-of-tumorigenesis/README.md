# Quasispecies model of tumorigenesis

A replicator–mutator (quasispecies) model of three competing cell populations with increasing genomic instability: cells with anomalous growth but stable genomes, genetically unstable cells derived from them, and malignant mutant cells. Error thresholds in the two mutation rates separate dominance of the malignant population from coexistence states.
