# Extinction versus genetic loss

The [neutral clonal branching model](allele-loss-versus-extinction-in-a-neutral-two-t.md)
distinguishes loss of a designated allele from extinction of the entire
population and gives the probability of loss while another lineage survives.
A neutral allele label alone does not encode adaptive potential in a
future environment. Lande's abstract argues for considering demographic
and genetic influences together, including inbreeding depression and loss
of genetic variation, rather than identifying these events.

## Expansion

Specify a functional or environment-dependent meaning for adaptive
potential and couple population size to genotype-dependent reproduction.
Then distinguish functional loss, allele loss and extinction, and compare
loss/absorption times or persistence probabilities. The neutral independent
founder formula does not establish feedback from inbreeding to demography
or an ordering of finite expected times.

## Sources

- R. Lande, "Genetics and demography in biological conservation", Science 241 (1988), 1455--1460, <https://doi.org/10.1126/science.3420403>, abstract.
