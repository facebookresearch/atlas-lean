# Mendelian offspring law at one locus

The law of the offspring's count of the focal allele at one biallelic locus when the parents carry
`g₁` and `g₂` copies: each parent transmits the focal allele independently with probability
`gᵢ / 2` (Mendel's first law), so the count is `0`, `1` or `2`. With `pᵢ = gᵢ / 2` it is the
measure on `ℝ` with weight `(1 - p₁)(1 - p₂)` at `0`, `p₁ (1 - p₂) + (1 - p₁) p₂` at `1` and
`p₁ p₂` at `2`. For `g₁, g₂ ≤ 2` these weights are probabilities and the law is a
[probability measure](is-probability-measure-mendel-law.md). The definition accepts all natural
`g₁, g₂`: a negative weight, which occurs as soon as `g₁ > 2` or `g₂ > 2`, is replaced by `0`, and
the total mass then exceeds `1`.
