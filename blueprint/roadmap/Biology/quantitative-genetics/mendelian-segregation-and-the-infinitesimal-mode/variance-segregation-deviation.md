# Variance of the segregation deviation at independent loci

Let `P` be a probability measure and let the allele counts `X_l` (`l ∈ ℕ`) be measurable,
mutually independent (unlinked loci) and almost surely in `[0, 2]`. Then for every `M` and all
additive effects `a_l`, the [segregation deviation](segregation-deviation.md) has variance
`Var(R_M) = ∑_{l < M} a_l² Var(X_l)`.
