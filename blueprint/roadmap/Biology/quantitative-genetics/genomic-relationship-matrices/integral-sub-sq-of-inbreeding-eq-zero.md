# The KING moment for non-inbred individuals

In an [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ with frequency $p$, let $i, j$ be individuals with [inbreeding coefficients](inbreeding.md) $F_i = F_j = 0$. Then

$$\int (x_i - x_j)^2 \, d\mu = 4p(1 - p)(1 - 2\varphi_{ij}),$$

where $x$ are the [genotypes](genotype.md) and $\varphi$ the [kinship coefficient](kinship.md) (Manichaikul et al. 2010).

## Sources

- A. Manichaikul, J. C. Mychaleckyj, S. S. Rich, K. Daly, M. Sale, W.-M. Chen, *Robust relationship inference in genome-wide association studies*, Bioinformatics 26 (2010), 2867–2873.
