# The genetic relationship covariance

In every [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ with frequency $p$, and for all individuals $i, j$,

$$\int (x_i - 2p)(x_j - 2p) \, d\mu = 2p(1 - p) \cdot 2\varphi_{ij},$$

where $x$ are the [genotypes](genotype.md) and $\varphi$ the [kinship coefficient](kinship.md) (Yang et al. 2010).

## Sources

- J. Yang, B. Benyamin, B. P. McEvoy, et al., *Common SNPs explain a large proportion of the heritability for human height*, Nat. Genet. 42 (2010), 565–569.
