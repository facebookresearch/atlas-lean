# Second moment of genotypes in terms of kinship

In every [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ with frequency $p$, and for all individuals $i, j$,

$$\int x_i x_j \, d\mu = 4p^2 + 4p(1 - p)\, \varphi_{ij},$$

where $x$ are the [genotypes](genotype.md) and $\varphi$ the [kinship coefficient](kinship.md).
