# GCTA diagonal relationship estimator

In an [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) with frequency $p$ and [genotypes](genotype.md) $x$, the diagonal estimator of individual $i$ at one locus is (Yang et al. 2011)

$$\tilde A_{ii} = 1 + \frac{x_i^2 - (1 + 2p)\, x_i + 2p^2}{2p(1 - p)}.$$

## Sources

- J. Yang, S. H. Lee, M. E. Goddard, P. M. Visscher, *GCTA: a tool for genome-wide complex trait analysis*, Am. J. Hum. Genet. 88 (2011), 76–82.
