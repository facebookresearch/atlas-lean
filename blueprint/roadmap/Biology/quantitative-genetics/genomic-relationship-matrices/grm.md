# Genetic relationship matrix entry

In an [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) with frequency $p$ and [genotypes](genotype.md) $x$, the genetic relationship matrix entry of individuals $i$ and $j$ at one locus is (Yang et al. 2010)

$$A_{ij} = \frac{(x_i - 2p)(x_j - 2p)}{2p(1 - p)}.$$

## Sources

- J. Yang, B. Benyamin, B. P. McEvoy, et al., *Common SNPs explain a large proportion of the heritability for human height*, Nat. Genet. 42 (2010), 565–569.
