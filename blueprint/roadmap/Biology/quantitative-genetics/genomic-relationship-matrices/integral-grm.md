# The GRM is unbiased for twice the kinship

In every [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) on a probability measure $\mu$, and for all individuals $i, j$ (including $i = j$),

$$\int A_{ij} \, d\mu = 2\varphi_{ij},$$

where $A$ is the [genetic relationship matrix entry](grm.md) and $\varphi$ the [kinship coefficient](kinship.md) (Yang et al. 2010).

## Sources

- J. Yang, B. Benyamin, B. P. McEvoy, et al., *Common SNPs explain a large proportion of the heritability for human height*, Nat. Genet. 42 (2010), 565–569.
