# Inbreeding coefficient

In an [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md), the inbreeding coefficient of individual $i$ is $F_i = \mathrm{ibd}\bigl((i, 0), (i, 1)\bigr)$; when the values $\mathrm{ibd}$ are genuine identity-by-descent probabilities (in $[0, 1]$, as in Malécot's model), it is the probability that its two copies are identical by descent.
