# The KING moment

In every [identity-by-descent model](ibdmodel.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ with frequency $p$, and for all individuals $i, j$,

$$\int (x_i - x_j)^2 \, d\mu = 2p(1 - p)(1 + F_i) + 2p(1 - p)(1 + F_j) - 8p(1 - p)\, \varphi_{ij},$$

where $x$ are the [genotypes](genotype.md), $F$ the [inbreeding](inbreeding.md) and $\varphi$ the [kinship](kinship.md) coefficients (Manichaikul et al. 2010).

## Sources

- A. Manichaikul, J. C. Mychaleckyj, S. S. Rich, K. Daly, M. Sale, W.-M. Chen, *Robust relationship inference in genome-wide association studies*, Bioinformatics 26 (2010), 2867–2873.
