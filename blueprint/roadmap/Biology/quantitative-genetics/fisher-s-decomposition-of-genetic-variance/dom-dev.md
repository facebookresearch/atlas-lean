# Dominance deviation of a genotype

For real allele weights $p$ and genotypic values $G$, the dominance deviation of genotype $(i, j)$ is

$$\delta_{ij} = G_{ij} - \mu - \alpha_i - \alpha_j,$$

where $\mu$ is the [mean genotypic value](mean-value.md) and $\alpha$ the [average effects](avg-effect.md).
