# Multilocus genotype frequency under linkage equilibrium

Let $L$ be a finite set of loci, $A_l$ the set of alleles at locus $l$ and
$p_l : A_l \to \mathbb{R}$ real allele weights. An ordered multilocus genotype $g$ gives every locus
$l$ an ordered pair $g_l = (i_l, j_l)$ of alleles. Its weight is

$$f(g) = \prod_{l \in L} p_l(i_l)\, p_l(j_l).$$

When every $p_l$ is a probability vector, $f(g)$ is the frequency of $g$ in a population in
Hardy–Weinberg proportions at every locus and in linkage equilibrium.
