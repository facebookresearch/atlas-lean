# Dominance variance at a biallelic locus

For all real numbers $q_1, a, d$, with the [biallelic weights](biallelic-freq.md) $p = q_1$, $q = 1 - q_1$ and the [biallelic values](biallelic-value.md) $a, d, -a$, the [dominance variance](dom-var.md) is

$$V_D = (2pqd)^2$$

(Falconer & Mackay 1996). It is a variance for $0 \le q_1 \le 1$.

## Sources

- D. S. Falconer, T. F. C. Mackay, *Introduction to Quantitative Genetics*, 4th ed., Longman, 1996.
