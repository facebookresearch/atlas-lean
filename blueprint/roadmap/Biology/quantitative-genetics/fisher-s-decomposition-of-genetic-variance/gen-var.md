# Genetic variance under Hardy–Weinberg proportions

For real allele weights $p$ and genotypic values $G$ on a finite set $A$, the genetic variance is

$$V_G = \sum_{i, j \in A} p_i p_j\, (G_{ij} - \mu)^2,$$

where $\mu$ is the [mean genotypic value](mean-value.md).

When $p$ is a probability vector, it is the variance of the genotypic value of a genotype drawn under Hardy–Weinberg proportions.
