# Parent–offspring covariance is half the additive variance

Let $p$ be real allele weights with $\sum_i p_i = 1$ and $G$ symmetric genotypic values. Then the [parent–offspring covariance](cov-parent-offspring.md) is $C_{PO} = V_A / 2$, where $V_A$ is the [additive variance](add-var.md) (Fisher 1918).

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The correlation between relatives on the supposition of Mendelian inheritance*, Trans. R. Soc. Edinb. 52 (1918), 399–433.
