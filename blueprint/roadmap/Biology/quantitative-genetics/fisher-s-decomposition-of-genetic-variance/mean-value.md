# Mean genotypic value under Hardy–Weinberg proportions

Let $A$ be a finite set of alleles with real weights $p_i$ (allele frequencies) and let $G_{ij}$ be real genotypic values of the ordered genotypes $(i, j)$. The mean genotypic value is

$$\mu = \sum_{i, j \in A} p_i p_j\, G_{ij}.$$

When $p$ is a probability vector, it is the mean genotypic value of a population in Hardy–Weinberg proportions.
