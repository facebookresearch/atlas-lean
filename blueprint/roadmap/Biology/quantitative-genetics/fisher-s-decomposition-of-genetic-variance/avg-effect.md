# Average effect of an allele

For real allele weights $p$ and genotypic values $G$ on a finite set $A$ of alleles, the average effect of allele $i$ is

$$\alpha_i = \sum_{j \in A} p_j\, G_{ij} - \mu,$$

where $\mu$ is the [mean genotypic value](mean-value.md). When $p$ is a probability vector, it is the average excess of allele $i$ (Fisher 1918), which for symmetric $G$ under Hardy–Weinberg proportions is the coefficient of the least-squares additive fit ([least squares](dom-var-le-sum-sq.md)).

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The correlation between relatives on the supposition of Mendelian inheritance*, Trans. R. Soc. Edinb. 52 (1918), 399–433.
