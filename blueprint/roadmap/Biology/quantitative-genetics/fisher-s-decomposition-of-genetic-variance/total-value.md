# Genotypic value without epistasis

Let $L$ be a finite set of loci and $G_l(i, j)$ real genotypic values of the ordered one-locus
genotypes $(i, j)$ at locus $l$. The genotypic value of the multilocus genotype $g$, with
$g_l = (i_l, j_l)$ at locus $l$, is the sum of its one-locus values:

$$G(g) = \sum_{l \in L} G_l(i_l, j_l),$$

so that the loci do not interact (no epistasis).
