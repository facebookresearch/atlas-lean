# Additive variance at a biallelic locus

For all real numbers $q_1, a, d$, with the [biallelic weights](biallelic-freq.md) $p = q_1$, $q = 1 - q_1$ and the [biallelic values](biallelic-value.md) $a, d, -a$, the [additive variance](add-var.md) is

$$V_A = 2pq\, \bigl(a + d(q - p)\bigr)^2.$$

It is a variance for $0 \le q_1 \le 1$ (Falconer & Mackay 1996).

## Sources

- D. S. Falconer, T. F. C. Mackay, *Introduction to Quantitative Genetics*, 4th ed., Longman, 1996.
