# Half-sib covariance over loci is a quarter of the additive variance

Let $L$ be a finite set of loci and $A_l$ a finite set of alleles at locus $l$, and let the real
allele weights $p_l$ satisfy $\sum_{a \in A_l} p_l(a) = 1$ at every locus $l$; no sign condition is
needed. Then for all real genotypic values $G_l$, symmetric or not, the
[multilocus half-sib covariance](cov-half-sibs-multi.md) is

$$C_{HS} = \frac{V_A}{4},$$

where $V_A$ is the [multilocus additive genetic variance](multi-add-var.md) (Fisher 1918).
