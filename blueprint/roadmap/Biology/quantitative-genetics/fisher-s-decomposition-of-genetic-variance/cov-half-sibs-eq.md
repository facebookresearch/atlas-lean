# Half-sib covariance is a quarter of the additive variance

Let $p$ be real allele weights with $\sum_i p_i = 1$ and $G$ any genotypic values. Then the [half-sib covariance](cov-half-sibs.md) is $C_{HS} = V_A / 4$, where $V_A$ is the [additive variance](add-var.md) (Fisher 1918).

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The correlation between relatives on the supposition of Mendelian inheritance*, Trans. R. Soc. Edinb. 52 (1918), 399–433.
