# Full-sib covariance in terms of variance components

Let $p$ be real allele weights with $\sum_i p_i = 1$ and $G$ symmetric genotypic values. Then the [full-sib covariance](cov-full-sibs.md) is

$$C_{FS} = \frac{V_A}{2} + \frac{V_D}{4},$$

where $V_A$ and $V_D$ are the [additive](add-var.md) and [dominance](dom-var.md) variances (Fisher 1918).

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The correlation between relatives on the supposition of Mendelian inheritance*, Trans. R. Soc. Edinb. 52 (1918), 399–433.
