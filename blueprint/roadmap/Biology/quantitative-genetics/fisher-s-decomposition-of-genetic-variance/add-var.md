# Additive genetic variance

For real allele weights $p$ and genotypic values $G$ on a finite set $A$, the additive genetic variance is

$$V_A = 2 \sum_{i \in A} p_i\, \alpha_i^2,$$

where $\alpha$ are the [average effects](avg-effect.md). When $p$ is a probability vector and the two alleles $i, j$ of a genotype are drawn independently from $p$, it is the variance of the breeding value $\alpha_i + \alpha_j$.
