# Dominance variance

For real allele weights $p$ and genotypic values $G$ on a finite set $A$, the dominance variance is

$$V_D = \sum_{i, j \in A} p_i p_j\, \delta_{ij}^2,$$

where $\delta$ are the [dominance deviations](dom-dev.md).

When $p$ is a probability vector, it is the variance of the dominance deviation of a genotype drawn under Hardy–Weinberg proportions.
