# Additive and dominance codings at different loci are uncorrelated

For every [two-locus genotype under random union](random-union.md) on a probability measure $P$,

$$\int a_A\, d_B \, dP = 0,$$

where $a_A$ is the [additive coding](add-a.md) at $A$ and $d_B$ the [dominance coding](dom-b.md) at $B$.
