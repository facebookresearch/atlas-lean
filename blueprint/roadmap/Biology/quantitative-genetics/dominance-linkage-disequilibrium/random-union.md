# Two-locus diploid genotype under random union of gametes

Let $P$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$. A two-locus genotype under random union of gametes consists of real functions $X_1, Y_1, X_2, Y_2$ on $\Omega$, square integrable with respect to $P$, such that the haplotypes $(X_1, Y_1)$ and $(X_2, Y_2)$ are independent and identically distributed under $P$. Here $X_h$ and $Y_h$ code the alleles carried by haplotype $h$ at loci $A$ and $B$ (classically $0/1$ indicators, but any square-integrable coding is allowed).
