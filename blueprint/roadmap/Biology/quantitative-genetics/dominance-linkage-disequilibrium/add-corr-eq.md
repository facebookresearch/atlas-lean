# Correlation of additive codings equals the gametic correlation

For every [two-locus genotype under random union](random-union.md) on a probability measure $P$,

$$\frac{\int a_A a_B \, dP}{\sqrt{\int a_A^2 \, dP \int a_B^2 \, dP}} = \frac{D}{\sqrt{V_A V_B}},$$

where $a_A, a_B$ are the [additive codings](add-a.md), $D$ the [gametic linkage disequilibrium](ld-d.md) and $V_A, V_B$ the [haplotype variances](var-a.md), with the convention $x/0 = 0$.
