# Probability of identity by descent under gene dropping

In a [pedigree](pedigree.md) on $n$ individuals, the probability that gene copies $c$ and $d$ are identical by descent is

$$\mathrm{IBD}(c, d) = \frac{\bigl|\{\omega : o_\omega(c) = o_\omega(d)\}\bigr|}{4^n},$$

the proportion of [gene-dropping outcomes](coins.md) $\omega$ under which both copies have the same [founder origin](origin.md).
