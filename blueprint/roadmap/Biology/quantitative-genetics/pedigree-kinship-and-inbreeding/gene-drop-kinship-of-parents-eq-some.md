# Malécot's recursion for gene-dropping kinship

In a [pedigree](pedigree.md), let $j \ne i$ be an individual that is not an [ancestor](is-ancestor.md) of $i$ and has parents $f(j), m(j)$. Then the [gene-dropping kinship](gene-drop-kinship.md) satisfies

$$\varphi^{\mathrm{gd}}_{ij} = \frac12\bigl(\varphi^{\mathrm{gd}}_{i f(j)} + \varphi^{\mathrm{gd}}_{i m(j)}\bigr).$$
