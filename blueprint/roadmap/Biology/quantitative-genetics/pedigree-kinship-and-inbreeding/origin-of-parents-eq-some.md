# Origin of a gene copy of a non-founder

In a [pedigree](pedigree.md), if $c = (i, k)$ with $i$ a non-founder with parents $p_0 = f(i)$, $p_1 = m(i)$, then for every [gene-dropping outcome](coins.md) $\omega$,

$$o_\omega(i, k) = o_\omega\bigl(p_k, \omega(i, k)\bigr),$$

where $o_\omega$ is the [founder origin](origin.md).
