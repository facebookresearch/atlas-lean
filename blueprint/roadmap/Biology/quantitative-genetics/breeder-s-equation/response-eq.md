# Breeder's equation

In a [linear parent–offspring model](model.md) on $\mu$ with heritability $h^2$ and parental value $P$, let $s$ be a set in $\sigma(P)$ (selection determined by the parental value, for instance truncation selection $P \ge t$) with $\mu(s) \ne 0$. Then

$$R(s) = h^2\, S(s),$$

where $R$ is the [response to selection](response.md) and $S$ the [selection differential](selection-differential.md) (Lush 1937).

## Sources

- J. L. Lush, *Animal Breeding Plans*, Collegiate Press, Ames, Iowa, 1937.
