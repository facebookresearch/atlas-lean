# Selection differential

In a [linear parent–offspring model](model.md) on $\mu$ with mean $m$ and parental value $P$, the selection differential of a set $s \subseteq \Omega$ (the selected parents) is

$$S(s) = \int P \, d\mu(\cdot \mid s) - m,$$

where $\mu(\cdot \mid s) = \mu(\cdot \cap s)/\mu(s)$ is the conditional measure, taken to be $0$ when $\mu(s) = 0$.
