# Pure-pleiotropy model of mutation–selection balance

In a pure-pleiotropy model, mutations affecting a focal quantitative trait are neutral for that trait but carry a pleiotropic cost in fitness. Genetic variance in the trait is held at mutation–selection balance through selection on fitness, and the correlation between trait and fitness produces apparent stabilizing selection on the trait. The joint distribution of mutational effects on trait and fitness determines both.
