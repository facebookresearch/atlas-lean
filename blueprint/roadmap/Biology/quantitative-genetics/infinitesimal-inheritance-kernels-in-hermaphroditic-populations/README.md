# Infinitesimal inheritance kernels in hermaphroditic populations

Measure-valued models of a sexually reproducing hermaphroditic population in which the offspring trait is drawn from a kernel centred at the midparent value. On the carrying-capacity attractor the trait distribution follows a quadratic (Boltzmann-type) evolution equation whose contraction in Wasserstein distance yields a unique stationary trait distribution for each mean.
