# Gene-dropping sample space

For a pedigree on $n$ individuals, each carrying a paternal and a maternal gene copy, the gene-dropping
sample space consists of the Mendelian coins, one bit for each of the $2n$ gene copies, together with a
reference-allele indicator $A_c \in \{0, 1\}$ for each of the $2n$ gene copies $c$, each regarded as a
potential founder copy.
