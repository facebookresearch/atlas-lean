# Gene dropping realizes Malécot's IBD model

For a pedigree and a reference-allele frequency $0 < p < 1$, the allele indicators under gene dropping
form a Malécot identity-by-descent model whose IBD probabilities are the gene-dropping ones: every copy
carries the reference allele with probability $p$, and
$\mathbb{E}[a_c a_d] = p^2 + p (1 - p) \, \mathrm{ibd}(c, d)$.
