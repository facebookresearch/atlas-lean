# The GRM is unbiased for twice the tabular kinship

Under pedigree gene dropping with founder reference-allele frequency $0 < p < 1$, the genetic relationship
matrix entry
$$A_{ij} = \frac{(x_i - 2p)(x_j - 2p)}{2p(1 - p)},$$
where $x_i$ is the reference-allele count of $i$, has expectation $\mathbb{E}[A_{ij}] = 2 \varphi_{ij}$,
where $\varphi$ is the kinship coefficient computed by the tabular method.
