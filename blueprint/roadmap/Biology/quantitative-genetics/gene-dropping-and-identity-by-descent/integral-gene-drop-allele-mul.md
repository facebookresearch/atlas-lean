# Malécot's second moments under gene dropping

Under gene dropping on a pedigree with founder reference-allele frequency $0 \le p \le 1$, let $a_c$ be the
reference-allele indicator of a gene copy $c$, the allele of the founder copy from which $c$ descends. For
gene copies $c$ and $d$,

$$\mathbb{E}[a_c a_d] = p^2 + p (1 - p) \, \mathrm{ibd}(c, d),$$

where $\mathrm{ibd}(c, d)$ is the gene-dropping probability that $c$ and $d$ descend from the same founder
copy.
