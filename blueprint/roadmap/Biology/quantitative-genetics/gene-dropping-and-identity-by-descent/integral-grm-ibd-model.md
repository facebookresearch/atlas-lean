# The GRM is unbiased under pedigree gene dropping

Under pedigree gene dropping with $0 < p < 1$, the genetic relationship matrix entry
$$A_{ij} = \frac{(x_i - 2p)(x_j - 2p)}{2p(1 - p)},$$
where $x_i$ is the reference-allele count of $i$, has expectation $\mathbb{E}[A_{ij}] = 2 \varphi_{ij}$, with
$\varphi$ the gene-dropping kinship.
