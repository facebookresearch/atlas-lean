# Kinship in the gene-dropping model

The kinship coefficient $\varphi_{ij}$ of the gene-dropping model of a pedigree, with founder-allele
frequency $0 < p < 1$, is the gene-dropping kinship of the pedigree: the probability that a copy drawn
uniformly at random from $i$ and a copy drawn uniformly at random from $j$, independently, are identical by
descent, that is, the average over the four ordered pairs of a copy of $i$ and a copy of $j$. For $i = j$ the
two draws may pick the same copy.
