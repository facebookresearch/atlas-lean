# Expectations under the gene-dropping law

Under the gene-dropping law, expectations are weighted finite sums: for every function $f$ of the coins
$\omega$ and the founder alleles $A$,
$$\mathbb{E}[f] = 4^{-n} \sum_\omega \sum_A \Bigl(\prod_c w_p(A_c)\Bigr) f(\omega, A),$$
an average over the coins of the Bernoulli-weighted sum over founder alleles.
