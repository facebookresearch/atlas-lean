# Probability of a gene-dropping outcome

The probability of a gene-dropping outcome $(\omega, A)$ on $n$ individuals is
$$4^{-n} \prod_c w_p(A_c),$$
uniform Mendelian coins times independent Bernoulli($p$) founder alleles, where $w_p$ is the Bernoulli
weight and the product runs over all $2n$ gene copies.
