# Weight of a gene-dropping outcome

For a real number $p$, the weight of a gene-dropping outcome $(\omega, A)$ on $n$ individuals is

$$4^{-n} \prod_c w_p(A_c),$$

where $w_p$ is the Bernoulli weight ($w_p(1) = p$, $w_p(0) = 1 - p$) and the product runs over all $2n$
gene copies. For $0 \le p \le 1$ it is the probability of the outcome under uniform Mendelian coins and
independent Bernoulli($p$) founder alleles.
