# Single-locus model under random union of gametes

A single-locus model on a finite set $A$ of alleles consists of allele frequencies $p_a \ge 0$ with $\sum_{a \in A} p_a = 1$ and real genotypic values $g(a, b)$ for $a, b \in A$ with $g(a, b) = g(b, a)$ (the mean phenotype of genotype $\{a, b\}$). Genotypes are formed by random union of gametes (Hardy–Weinberg proportions $p_a p_b$).
