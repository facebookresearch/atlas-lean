# Single-locus covariance between relatives in Cockerham form

In every [single-locus model](locus-model.md) and for all real $k_0, k_1, k_2$ with $k_0 + k_1 + k_2 = 1$,

$$E(k_0, k_1, k_2) - \mu^2 = \mathrm{Cov}\Bigl(\frac{k_1}{2} + k_2,\ k_2\Bigr),$$

where $E$ is the [mean product of genotypic values](rel-prod.md), $\mu$ the [mean](mean.md), and $\mathrm{Cov}$ the [Cockerham form](cov.md) of the [components of the model](to-components.md).
