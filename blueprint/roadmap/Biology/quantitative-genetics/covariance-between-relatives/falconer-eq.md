# Falconer's estimator in terms of Cockerham components

For all [Cockerham–Kempthorne components](cockerham-components.md) $(n, V)$ and every real $C$,

$$F(C) = V_A + \sum_{\substack{0 \le i, j \le n \\ (i, j) \ne (1, 0)}} 2\bigl(1 - 2^{-i} 4^{-j}\bigr)\, V_{ij},$$

where $F$ is [Falconer's estimator](falconer.md) and $V_A$ the [additive variance](var-a-2.md).
