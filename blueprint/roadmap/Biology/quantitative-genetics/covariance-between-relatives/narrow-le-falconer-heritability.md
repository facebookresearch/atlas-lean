# Phantom heritability

For all [Cockerham–Kempthorne components](cockerham-components.md), every real $C$ and every $V_P > 0$,

$$\frac{V_A}{V_P} \le 2\Bigl(\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(1, 1) + C}{V_P} - \frac{\mathrm{Cov}(\frac12, \frac14) + C}{V_P}\Bigr),$$

where $V_A$ is the [additive variance](var-a-2.md) and $\mathrm{Cov}$ the [Cockerham form](cov.md). When $V_P$ is the common phenotypic variance of the twins in a model where $\mathrm{Cov}(1, 1) + C$ and $\mathrm{Cov}(\frac12, \frac14) + C$ are the phenotypic covariances of monozygotic and dizygotic twins, the fractions are the twin correlations $r_{MZ}$ and $r_{DZ}$, and the inequality says that the narrow-sense heritability is at most the twin estimate $2(r_{MZ} - r_{DZ})$ (Zuk et al. 2012).

## Sources

- O. Zuk, E. Hechter, S. R. Sunyaev, E. S. Lander, *The mystery of missing heritability: Genetic interactions create phantom heritability*, PNAS 109 (2012), 1193–1198.
