# The additive fit is least squares

In every [single-locus model](locus-model.md), for every real $c$ and every $h : A \to \mathbb{R}$,

$$V_D \le \sum_{a, b \in A} p_a p_b\, \bigl(g(a, b) - c - h_a - h_b\bigr)^2,$$

where $V_D$ is the [dominance variance](var-d.md), the mean squared error of the additive fit $\mu + \alpha_a + \alpha_b$: no additive fit does better (Fisher 1918).

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The correlation between relatives on the supposition of Mendelian inheritance*, Trans. R. Soc. Edinb. 52 (1918), 399–433.
