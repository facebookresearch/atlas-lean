# One-locus phantom heritability

In every [single-locus model](locus-model.md) and for every real $C$,

$$2\Bigl(\bigl(E(0, 0, 1) - \mu^2 + C\bigr) - \bigl(E(\tfrac14, \tfrac12, \tfrac14) - \mu^2 + C\bigr)\Bigr) = V_A + \tfrac{3}{2} V_D,$$

where $E$ is the [mean product of genotypic values](rel-prod.md), $\mu$ the [mean](mean.md), and $V_A$, $V_D$ the [additive](var-a.md) and [dominance](var-d.md) variances: monozygotic twins have $k_2 = 1$ and dizygotic twins $(k_0, k_1, k_2) = (\frac14, \frac12, \frac14)$.
