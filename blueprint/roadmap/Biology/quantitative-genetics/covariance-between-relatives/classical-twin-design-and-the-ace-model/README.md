# Classical twin design and the ACE model

Monozygotic and dizygotic twins share all or half of their additive genetic variance and, by assumption, the same family environment. Two correlations therefore identify a variance decomposition into additive genetic, shared-environmental and nonshared-environmental parts, fitted by maximum likelihood as a structural equation model, and its multivariate form separates genetic from environmental correlations between traits.
