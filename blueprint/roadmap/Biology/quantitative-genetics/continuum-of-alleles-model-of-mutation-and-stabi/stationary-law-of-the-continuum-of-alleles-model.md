# Equilibrium of the continuum-of-alleles model

In Kimura's continuum-of-alleles model of mutation and stabilizing selection, with fitness decreasing
quadratically from the optimum and new mutants produced according to a Gaussian density, an
equilibrium distribution of allelic effects exists and is globally stable (Bürger, 1986). Kimura (1965)
obtained by an approximate analysis a Gaussian equilibrium distribution; Bürger also derives estimates
of the exact equilibrium variance.

## Expansion

A sound expansion states Bürger's theorem with its exact hypotheses (the space of distributions, the
fitness function and the mutation kernel) and states separately the corresponding question for the
model with [Gaussian stabilizing fitness](fitness.md) $w(x) = e^{-x^2/(2V_s)}$, a different fitness
function, whose generation map is [one generation as a map on probability measures](step-pm.md).
