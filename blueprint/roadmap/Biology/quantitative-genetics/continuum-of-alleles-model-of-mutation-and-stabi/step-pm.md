# One generation as a map on probability measures

For [model parameters](params.md), with selection width `Vₛ > 0` and mutation probability
`0 < μ < 1`, the [generation map](step.md) `T ν = (1 - μ) ν_w + μ (ν_w ∗ N(0, γ²))` sends every
probability measure `ν` on `ℝ` to a probability measure; this is `T` as a map from the
probability measures on `ℝ` to themselves.
