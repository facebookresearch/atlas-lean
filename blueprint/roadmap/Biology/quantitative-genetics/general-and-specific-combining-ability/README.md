# General and specific combining ability

Decomposition of the genetic value of a single-cross hybrid between two heterotic groups into the general combining abilities (GCA) of its two parents and their specific combining ability (SCA), as random effects in a linear mixed model and, at the locus level, as founder-allele main effects and interactions in multiparental QTL mapping.
