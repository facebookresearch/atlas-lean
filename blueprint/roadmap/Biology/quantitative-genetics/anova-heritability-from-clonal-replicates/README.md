# ANOVA heritability from clonal replicates

When genetically identical individuals (clones, identical twins, or clonal pathogen genotypes found in several hosts) are measured, the share of phenotypic variance lying between genotypes estimates broad-sense heritability. One-way random-effects analysis of variance turns the between- and within-group mean squares into variance components.
