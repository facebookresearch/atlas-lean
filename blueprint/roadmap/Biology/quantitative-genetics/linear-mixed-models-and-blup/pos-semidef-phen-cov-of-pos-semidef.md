# The phenotypic covariance is positive semidefinite

Let $n$ and $q$ be finite index sets, $Z$ a real $n \times q$ matrix and $G$, $R$ positive
semidefinite real $q \times q$ and $n \times n$ matrices. Then the
[phenotypic covariance](../../../Probability/linear-models-multivariate/generalized-least-squares/phen-cov.md)
$Z G Z^\top + R$ is positive semidefinite.
