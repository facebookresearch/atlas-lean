# The Lande–Arnold selection gradient

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and differentiable everywhere with derivative $W'$, let $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$, and suppose that $W'$ is integrable with respect to $N(\mu, v)$. Then

$$\frac{S}{v} = \frac{\int W'(z) \, dN(\mu, v)(z)}{\bar w(\mu)},$$

where $S$ is the [selection differential](selection-differential.md) and $\bar w$ the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md), with the convention $x/0 = 0$: the selection gradient is the average slope of relative fitness (Lande–Arnold 1983).

## Sources

- R. Lande and S. J. Arnold, *The measurement of selection on correlated characters*, Evolution 37 (1983), 1210–1226.
