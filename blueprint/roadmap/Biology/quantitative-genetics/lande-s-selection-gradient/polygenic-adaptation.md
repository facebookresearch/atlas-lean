# Polygenic adaptation

This page covers adaptation of a quantitative trait through small changes of allele frequencies at many loci, a topic whose results depend on the model chosen. The response of the mean of a multivariate trait to one generation of selection is $\mathbf{G} \boldsymbol{\beta}$, with $\mathbf{G}$ the additive genetic covariance matrix and $\boldsymbol{\beta}$ [Lande's selection gradient](has-gradient-at-mean-fitness-mv.md). This response alone is not a model of an adaptation process: it does not say how allele frequencies, and $\mathbf{G}$ itself, change over time.

## Expansion

Specify the distribution of allele effects, the assumptions on linkage, the environmental optimum and the scaling with the number of loci, then derive the response of the trait and of the allele frequencies, each as a page of its own with a source.
