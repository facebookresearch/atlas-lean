# Derivative of mean fitness with respect to the trait mean

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable and bounded, and let $v > 0$. Then for every $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md) $m \mapsto \bar w(m)$ is differentiable at $\mu$ with

$$\bar w'(\mu) = \frac{1}{v} \int (z - \mu)\, W(z) \, dN(\mu, v)(z).$$
