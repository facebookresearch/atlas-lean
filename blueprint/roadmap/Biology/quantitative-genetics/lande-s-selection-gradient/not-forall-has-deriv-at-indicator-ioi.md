# The truncation fitness is not everywhere differentiable

There is no function $W'$ such that the truncation fitness $W = \mathbf{1}_{(0, \infty)}$
($W(z) = 1$ for $z > 0$ and $W(z) = 0$ for $z \le 0$) has derivative $W'(z)$ at every real $z$:
$W$ is not even continuous at $0$. So $W$ does not satisfy the everywhere-differentiability
hypothesis of the [Lande–Arnold form](selection-differential-div-eq.md) of the selection gradient.
