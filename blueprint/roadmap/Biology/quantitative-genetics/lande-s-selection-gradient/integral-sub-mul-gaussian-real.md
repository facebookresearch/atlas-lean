# Stein's lemma in Lande's form

Let $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $v > 0$, and let $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable everywhere with derivative $g'$, such that $g$, $z \mapsto (z - \mu)\, g(z)$ and $g'$ are integrable with respect to the normal distribution $N(\mu, v)$. Then

$$\int (z - \mu)\, g(z) \, dN(\mu, v)(z) = v \int g'(z) \, dN(\mu, v)(z)$$

(Stein 1981).

## Sources

- C. M. Stein, *Estimation of the mean of a multivariate normal distribution*, Ann. Statist. 9 (1981), 1135–1151.
