# The truncation fitness has derivative zero away from the threshold

Let $W = \mathbf{1}_{(0, \infty)}$ be the truncation fitness: $W(z) = 1$ for $z > 0$ and $W(z) = 0$
for $z \le 0$. At every real $z \ne 0$, $W$ is differentiable with derivative $0$, since it is
constant on a neighbourhood of $z$.
