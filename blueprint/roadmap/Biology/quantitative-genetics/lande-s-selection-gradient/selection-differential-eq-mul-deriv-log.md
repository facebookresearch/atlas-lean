# Lande's equation

Let $W : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be measurable and bounded, let $v > 0$, and let $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\bar w(\mu) \ne 0$, where $\bar w$ is the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md). Then the [selection differential](selection-differential.md) is

$$S = v \, \frac{d}{dm} \log \bar w(m) \Big|_{m = \mu}$$

(Lande 1979), with $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$. For $\bar w(\mu) > 0$ the derivative is that of the logarithm of mean fitness.

## Sources

- R. Lande, *Quantitative genetic analysis of multivariate evolution, applied to brain:body size allometry*, Evolution 33 (1979), 402–416.
