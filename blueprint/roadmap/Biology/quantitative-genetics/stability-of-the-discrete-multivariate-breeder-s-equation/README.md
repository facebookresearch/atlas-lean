# Stability of the discrete multivariate breeder's equation

The multivariate breeder's equation $\Delta\bar z=G\nabla\ln\bar W$ is a discrete-time modified-gradient system $x_{k+1}=x_k+P_k\nabla f(x_k)$ with positive semidefinite $P_k$. Unlike its continuous-time counterpart, positive semidefiniteness alone does not make a fitness peak stable; a step-size bound linking the $G$-matrix to the curvature of the fitness landscape is needed.
