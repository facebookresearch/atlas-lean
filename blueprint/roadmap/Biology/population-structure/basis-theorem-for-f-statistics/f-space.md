# Space spanned by the per-locus f-statistics

For a set $P$ of populations, the f-statistic space $V_F$ is the linear span, in the real vector space of functions $\mathbb{R}^P \to \mathbb{R}$, of all [f4 forms](f4-form.md) $\phi_4^{A,B;C,D}$ with $A, B, C, D \in P$. It contains every f2 form and every f3 form.
