# The f2 statistic of a point mass is the per-locus f2 form

Let `x : P → ℝ` be a frequency vector. On the one-point outcome space with the Dirac measure at its
point, take the constant frequencies `p X = x X`. Then for all populations `A`, `B` the
[f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) is the [per-locus f2 form](f2-form.md) of `x`:
`f2(A, B) = (x A - x B) ^ 2`.
