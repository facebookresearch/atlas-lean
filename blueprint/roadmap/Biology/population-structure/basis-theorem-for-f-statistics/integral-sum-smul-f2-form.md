# Integral of a linear combination of f2 forms

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for every finite index set $s$, coefficients $c_i \in \mathbb{R}$ and populations $A_i, B_i$ ($i \in s$),

$$\int \Bigl(\sum_{i \in s} c_i\, \phi_2^{A_i, B_i}\Bigr)\bigl((p_X(\omega))_X\bigr) \, d\mu(\omega) = \sum_{i \in s} c_i\, f_2(A_i, B_i),$$

where $\phi_2$ is the [f2 form](f2-form.md) and $f_2$ the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md).
