# f4 statistic as the integral of the f4 form

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be the allele frequency of each population $X$. Then for all populations $A, B, C, D$,

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \int \phi_4^{A,B;C,D}\bigl((p_X(\omega))_X\bigr) \, d\mu(\omega),$$

where $f_4$ is the [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) and $\phi_4$ the [f4 form](f4-form.md).
