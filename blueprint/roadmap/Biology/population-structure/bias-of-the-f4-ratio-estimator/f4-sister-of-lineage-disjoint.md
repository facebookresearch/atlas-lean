# Disjoint lineage differences give the f4-ratio sister condition

Let `M` be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) with
[binary loadings](../patterson-s-f-statistics/has-binary-loadings.md) (every lineage weight is `0`
or `1`), and let `L X` be the [lineage](../patterson-s-f-statistics/lineage.md) of population `X`.
If the symmetric difference `L A ∆ L O` (on a tree, the edges of the `A`–`O` path) is disjoint from
`L Y ∆ L B`, then `f4(A, O; Y, C) = f4(A, O; B, C)` for every population `C`, where `f4` is the
[f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) of the
[population frequencies](../patterson-s-f-statistics/freq.md).
