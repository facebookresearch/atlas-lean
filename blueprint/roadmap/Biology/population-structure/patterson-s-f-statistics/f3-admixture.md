# f3 of a pure admixture

In a [drift model](drift-model.md) with lineage weights $w$, let $A, B, C$ be populations and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ with $w(C, e) = \alpha\, w(A, e) + (1 - \alpha)\, w(B, e)$ for every edge $e$ (for $0 \le \alpha \le 1$, $C$ is an $\alpha : 1 - \alpha$ mixture of $A$ and $B$ with no subsequent drift). Then, for the [f2](f2.md) and [f3](f3.md) statistics of the [population frequencies](freq.md),

$$f_3(C; A, B) = -\alpha (1 - \alpha)\, f_2(A, B).$$
