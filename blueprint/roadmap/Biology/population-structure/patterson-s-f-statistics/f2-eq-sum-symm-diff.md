# f2 as a symmetric-difference drift length under binary loadings

In every [drift model](drift-model.md) with [binary loadings](has-binary-loadings.md) and drift lengths $\ell$, and for all populations $X, Y$,

$$f_2(X, Y) = \sum_{e \in L_X \triangle L_Y} \ell_e,$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](f2.md) of the [population frequencies](freq.md), $L_X$ the [lineage](lineage.md) of $X$ and $\triangle$ the symmetric difference. On a tree this is the additive tree distance between $X$ and $Y$.
