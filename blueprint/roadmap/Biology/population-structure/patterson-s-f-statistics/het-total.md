# Total heterozygosity of the pooled population

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of subpopulations with weights $c \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and allele frequencies $q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. The total heterozygosity is

$$H_T = 2 \bar q (1 - \bar q),$$

where $\bar q$ is the [mean allele frequency](mean-freq.md). When the weights are a probability vector and the frequencies lie in $[0, 1]$, it is the heterozygosity expected under random mating of the pooled population.
