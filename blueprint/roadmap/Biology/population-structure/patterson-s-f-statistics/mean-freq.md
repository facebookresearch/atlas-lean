# Mean allele frequency of a subdivided population

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of subpopulations with weights $c \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and allele frequencies $q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. The mean allele frequency is

$$\bar q = \sum_{i \in \iota} c_i q_i.$$

When the weights are a probability vector and the frequencies lie in $[0, 1]$, it is the allele frequency of the pooled population.
