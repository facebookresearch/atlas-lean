# f3 in the drift model

In every [drift model](drift-model.md) with lineage weights $w$ and drift lengths $\ell$, and for all populations $C, A, B$, the [f3 statistic](f3.md) of the [population frequencies](freq.md) is

$$f_3(C; A, B) = \sum_{e \in E} \bigl(w(C, e) - w(A, e)\bigr)\bigl(w(C, e) - w(B, e)\bigr)\, \ell_e.$$
