# f3 statistic

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be the allele frequency of each population $X$. For a target population $C$ and source populations $A, B$,

$$f_3(C; A, B) = \int (p_C - p_A)(p_C - p_B) \, d\mu,$$

with the convention that the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$.
