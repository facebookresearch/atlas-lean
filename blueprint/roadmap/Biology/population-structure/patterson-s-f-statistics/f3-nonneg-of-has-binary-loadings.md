# f3 is nonnegative under binary loadings

In every [drift model](drift-model.md) with [binary loadings](has-binary-loadings.md) and for all populations $C, A, B$, the [f3 statistic](f3.md) of the [population frequencies](freq.md) satisfies $f_3(C; A, B) \ge 0$. On a tree it is the drift length of the path from $C$ to the $A$–$B$ path.
