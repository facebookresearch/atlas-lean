# f4 in terms of f2

Let $\mu$ be a measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for all populations $A, B, C, D$,

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \frac{f_2(A, D) + f_2(B, C) - f_2(A, C) - f_2(B, D)}{2},$$

where $f_2$ and $f_4$ are the [f2](f2.md) and [f4](f4.md) statistics.
