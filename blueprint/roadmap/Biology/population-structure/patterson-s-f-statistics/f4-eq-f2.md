# f4 in terms of f2

Let $\mu$ be a measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for all populations $A, B, C, D$,

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \frac{f_2(A, D) + f_2(B, C) - f_2(A, C) - f_2(B, D)}{2},$$

where $f_2$ and $f_4$ are the [f2](f2.md) and [f4](f4.md) statistics. Equivalently,
$2 f_4(A, B; C, D) = (f_2(A, D) + f_2(B, C)) - (f_2(A, C) + f_2(B, D))$: twice $f_4(A, B; C, D)$ is
the difference of two of the three quartet pair sums $f_2(A, B) + f_2(C, D)$,
$f_2(A, C) + f_2(B, D)$ and $f_2(A, D) + f_2(B, C)$. Relabelling the quartet gives the same
expression for $f_4(A, C; B, D)$ and $f_4(A, D; B, C)$.
