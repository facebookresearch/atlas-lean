# Wahlund effect

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, and let $c, q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $c_i \ge 0$ for every $i$ and $\sum_i c_i = 1$. Then $H_S \le H_T$, where $H_S$ is the [mean within-subpopulation heterozygosity](het-sub.md) and $H_T$ the [total heterozygosity](het-total.md): subdivision lowers heterozygosity.
