# f4 vanishes for edge-disjoint paths

In a [drift model](drift-model.md) with lineage weights $w$, let $A, B, C, D$ be populations with

$$\bigl(w(A, e) - w(B, e)\bigr)\bigl(w(C, e) - w(D, e)\bigr) = 0 \qquad \text{for every edge } e.$$

Then the [f4 statistic](f4.md) of the [population frequencies](freq.md) is $f_4(A, B; C, D) = 0$. On a tree with split $AB \mid CD$ the paths $A$–$B$ and $C$–$D$ share no edge, so the hypothesis holds.
