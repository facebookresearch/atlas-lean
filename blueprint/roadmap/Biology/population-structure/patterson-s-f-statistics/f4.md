# f4 statistic

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be the allele frequency of each population $X$. For populations $A, B, C, D$,

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \int (p_A - p_B)(p_C - p_D) \, d\mu,$$

with the convention that the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$.
