# Wahlund identity

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, and let $c, q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $\sum_i c_i = 1$. Then

$$H_T - H_S = 2 \sum_{i \in \iota} c_i (q_i - \bar q)^2,$$

where $H_T$ is the [total heterozygosity](het-total.md), $H_S$ the [mean within-subpopulation heterozygosity](het-sub.md) and $\bar q$ the [mean allele frequency](mean-freq.md).
