# f2 statistic

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be the allele frequency of each population $X$. For populations $X, Y$,

$$f_2(X, Y) = \int (p_X - p_Y)^2 \, d\mu,$$

with the convention that the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$. When $\mu$ is a probability measure (the distribution of loci), it is the expected squared allele-frequency difference of $X$ and $Y$; no sampling correction is involved.
