# Nonnegativity of the fixation index $F_{ST}$

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, and let $c, q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $c_i \ge 0$ for every $i$, $\sum_i c_i = 1$ and $H_T > 0$. Then

$$\frac{H_T - H_S}{H_T} \ge 0,$$

where $H_S$ is the [mean within-subpopulation heterozygosity](het-sub.md) and $H_T$ the [total heterozygosity](het-total.md).
