# Mean within-subpopulation heterozygosity

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of subpopulations with weights $c \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and allele frequencies $q \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. The mean within-subpopulation (Hardy–Weinberg) heterozygosity is

$$H_S = \sum_{i \in \iota} c_i \cdot 2 q_i (1 - q_i).$$

When the weights are a probability vector and the frequencies lie in $[0, 1]$, it is the mean over subpopulations of the heterozygosity expected under random mating within each.
