# Nondegenerate pure admixture gives negative f3

In a [drift model](drift-model.md) with lineage weights $w$, let $A, B, C$ be populations and $0 < \alpha < 1$ with $w(C, e) = \alpha\, w(A, e) + (1 - \alpha)\, w(B, e)$ for every edge $e$, and suppose that $f_2(A, B) > 0$. Then $f_3(C; A, B) < 0$, for the [f2](f2.md) and [f3](f3.md) statistics of the [population frequencies](freq.md).
