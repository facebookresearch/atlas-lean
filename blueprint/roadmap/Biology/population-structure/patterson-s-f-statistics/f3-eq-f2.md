# f3 in terms of f2

Let $\mu$ be a measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for all populations $C, A, B$,

$$f_3(C; A, B) = \frac{f_2(C, A) + f_2(C, B) - f_2(A, B)}{2},$$

where $f_2$ and $f_3$ are the [f2](f2.md) and [f3](f3.md) statistics.
