# f4 in the drift model

In every [drift model](drift-model.md) on a measure $\mu$ with lineage weights $w$ and drift lengths $\ell$, and for all populations $A, B, C, D$, the [f4 statistic](f4.md) of the [population frequencies](freq.md) is

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \sum_{e \in E} \bigl(w(A, e) - w(B, e)\bigr)\bigl(w(C, e) - w(D, e)\bigr)\, \ell_e:$$

the root cancels and $f_4$ is the drift length shared by the paths $A$–$B$ and $C$–$D$, weighted by the differences of lineage weights.
