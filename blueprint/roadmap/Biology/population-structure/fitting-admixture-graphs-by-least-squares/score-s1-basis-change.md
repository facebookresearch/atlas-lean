# Basis independence of the qpGraph score S1

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, let $A$ be an invertible real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix, let $Q$ be any real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix and $f, g \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$. Then the [score $S_1$](score-s1.md) satisfies

$$S_1^{A Q A^\top, Af}(Ag) = S_1^{Q, f}(g),$$

where the superscripts give the covariance and the estimated statistics. For singular $Q$ both sides are $0$ by the convention that a singular matrix has inverse $0$.
