# Score S2 as score S1 with a diagonal covariance

Let $\iota$ be a finite index set, $Q$ a real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix and $\lambda$ a real number with $Q_{ii} + \lambda \ne 0$ for every $i$. Then for all $f, g \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$,

$$S_2^{Q, \lambda, f}(g) = 2\, S_1^{D, f}(g), \qquad D = \operatorname{diag}(Q_{ii} + \lambda)_{i \in \iota},$$

where $S_1$ and $S_2$ are the [generalized least-squares](score-s1.md) and [least-squares](score-s2.md) scores.
