# The qpGraph score S2 depends on the basis

There are a positive definite real $2 \times 2$ matrix $Q$, an invertible real $2 \times 2$ matrix $A$ and vectors $f, g \in \mathbb{R}^2$ such that for every real $\lambda$ the [score $S_2$](score-s2.md) changes under the change of basis (Patterson et al. 2012, after eq. 7):

$$S_2^{A Q A^\top, \lambda, Af}(Ag) \ne S_2^{Q, \lambda, f}(g).$$

## Sources

- N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, Y. Luo, S. Mallick, N. Rohland, Y. Zhan, T. Genschoreck, T. Webster, D. Reich, *Ancient admixture in human history*, Genetics 192 (2012), 1065–1093, eqs. (6)–(7).
