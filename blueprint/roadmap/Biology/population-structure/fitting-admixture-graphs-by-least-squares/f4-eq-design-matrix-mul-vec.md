# Predicted f4 statistics are linear in the edge lengths

Let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) with lineage weights $w$ and drift lengths $\ell$, and for each index $i$ let $A_i, B_i, C_i, D_i$ be populations. Then

$$\bigl(f_4(A_i, B_i; C_i, D_i)\bigr)_i = B \ell,$$

where $f_4$ is the [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) of the population frequencies and $B$ the [design matrix](design-matrix.md): with the admixture weights fixed, the predicted statistics are linear in the edge lengths.
