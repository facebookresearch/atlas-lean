# Uniqueness of the generalized least-squares maximizer

Let $\iota$ and $E$ be finite sets, $Q$ a positive definite real $\iota \times \iota$ matrix, $B$ a real $\iota \times E$ matrix of full column rank ($B\ell = 0$ only for $\ell = 0$), $f \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, and let $\hat\ell$ solve $B^\top Q^{-1} B \hat\ell = B^\top Q^{-1} f$. If $\ell \in \mathbb{R}^E$ satisfies $S_1(B\ell) = S_1(B\hat\ell)$, where $S_1$ is the [score](score-s1.md) with covariance $Q$ and estimated statistics $f$, then $\ell = \hat\ell$.
