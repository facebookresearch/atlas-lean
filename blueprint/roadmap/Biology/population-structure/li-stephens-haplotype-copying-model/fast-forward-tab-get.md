# Tabulated recursion computes the Li–Stephens forward variables

Let $R$ be a field, $\rho, \varepsilon \in R$ and $y$ a sequence of [site observations](obs.md). Then for every site $t$ and haplotype $k$, entry $k$ of the [tabulated forward vector](fast-forward-tab.md) at site $t$ equals the forward variable $\alpha_t(k)$ of the [Li–Stephens model](hmm.md).
