# Collapse of the sum over predecessor haplotypes

Let $R$ be a field and $\rho \in R$. Then for every $v \in R^K$ and every haplotype $k$,

$$\sum_{j=1}^{K} v_j\, \tau_\rho(j, k) = (1 - \rho)\, v_k + \frac{\rho}{K} \sum_{j=1}^{K} v_j,$$

where $\tau_\rho$ is the [transition weight](trans.md) and $x/0 = 0$.
