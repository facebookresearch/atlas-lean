# Observation sequence of a reference panel and target haplotype

For a reference panel $H$ of $K$ haplotypes (haplotype $k$ carries allele $H_k(t) \in A$ at site $t \in \mathbb{N}$) and a target haplotype $x : \mathbb{N} \to A$, the observation at site $t$ is the [site observation](obs.md) $y_t = \bigl((H_1(t), \dots, H_K(t)),\ x(t)\bigr)$.
