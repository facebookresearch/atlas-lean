# Li–Stephens copying transition probability

Let $R$ be a field (for instance $\mathbb{R}$), $K$ a natural number and $\rho \in R$. The copying transition weight from reference haplotype $k$ to $k'$ ($k, k' \in \{1, \dots, K\}$) is

$$\tau_\rho(k, k') = (1 - \rho)\,[k = k'] + \frac{\rho}{K},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ when $K = 0$ in $R$. For $R = \mathbb{R}$, $K \ge 1$ and $0 \le \rho \le 1$ it is a transition probability: with probability $\rho$ the copying process switches to a uniformly random reference haplotype (possibly the same one), and otherwise keeps copying $k$.
