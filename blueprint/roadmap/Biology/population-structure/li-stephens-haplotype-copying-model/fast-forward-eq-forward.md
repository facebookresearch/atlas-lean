# The collapsed forward recursion computes the forward variables

Let $R$ be a field, $\rho, \varepsilon \in R$ and $y$ a sequence of [site observations](obs.md). Then for every site $t$ and haplotype $k$, the [collapsed forward variable](fast-forward.md) $\hat\alpha_t(k)$ equals the forward variable $\alpha_t(k)$ of the [Li–Stephens model](hmm.md).
