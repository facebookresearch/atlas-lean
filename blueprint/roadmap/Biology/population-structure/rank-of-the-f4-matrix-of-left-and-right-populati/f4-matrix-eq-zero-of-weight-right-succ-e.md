# Right populations with the reference's weights give a zero f4 matrix

Let `M` be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on a measure `μ`, with
lineage weights `w`, and let `R 0, …, R r` be right populations each of which has the lineage
weights of the reference: `w (R j) e = w (R 0) e` for every `1 ≤ j ≤ r` and every edge `e`. Then for
all left populations `L 0, …, L n`, the
[qpWave matrix](../abba-baba-test-and-f-statistic-estimators/f4-matrix.md) of `L` against `R`, for
the [population frequencies](../patterson-s-f-statistics/freq.md) of `M`, is zero.
