# Rank bound by any edge set outside which one side's contrasts vanish

Let `M` be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on a measure `μ`, let
`L 0, …, L n` be left and `R 0, …, R r` right populations, with
[contrast matrices](contrast-matrix.md) `C_L` and `C_R`, and let `S` be a set of edges such that for
every edge `e` outside `S` either `C_L i e = 0` for every `i` or `C_R j e = 0` for every `j`. Then
the [qpWave matrix](../abba-baba-test-and-f-statistic-estimators/f4-matrix.md) of `L` against `R`,
for the [population frequencies](../patterson-s-f-statistics/freq.md) of `M`, has rank at most the
number of edges in `S`.
