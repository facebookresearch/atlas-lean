# Star tree with matching lengths and admixture model have equal f3

Let `α, a, b, c` be real numbers, `M` a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on
three populations and three edges with the [star-tree weights](star-weight.md) and the
[matching edge lengths](star-len.md) for `α, a, b, c`, and `M'` a drift model, on a possibly
different measure, with the [admixture weights](admix-weight.md) for `α` and edge lengths
`(a, b, c)`. Then the [f3 statistics](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of the two models'
population frequencies agree at every triple of populations: `f3(X; Y, Z)` is the same in `M` and
`M'` for all `X, Y, Z`.
