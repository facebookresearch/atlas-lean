# f2 from the centred leaf covariance

Let $\mu$ be a measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ of a finite set $P$ of populations be square integrable with respect to $\mu$. Then for all $X, Y \in P$,

$$f_2(X, Y) = K(X, X) + K(Y, Y) - 2 K(X, Y),$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) and $K$ the [centred leaf covariance](centred-cov.md).
