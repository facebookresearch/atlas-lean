# Admixture model lacks binary loadings

Let $\alpha \ne 0$ and $\alpha \ne 1$. Then no [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on three populations and three edges whose lineage weights are the [admixture weights](admix-weight.md) $w_\alpha$ has [binary loadings](../patterson-s-f-statistics/has-binary-loadings.md).
