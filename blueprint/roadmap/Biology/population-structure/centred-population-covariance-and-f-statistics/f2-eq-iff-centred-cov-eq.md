# f-statistics determine exactly the centred leaf covariance

Let $P$ be a finite set of populations, and let $p$ and $p'$ be two frequency models on measures $\mu$ and $\mu'$, each square integrable. Then the two models have the same [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) at every pair of populations if and only if they have the same [centred leaf covariance](centred-cov.md) at every pair. Since f3 and f4 are linear combinations of f2, f-statistics see a model only through its centred leaf covariance.
