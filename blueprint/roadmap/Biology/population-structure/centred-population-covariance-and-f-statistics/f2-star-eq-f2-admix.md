# Star tree and admixture model have equal f2

Let $\alpha, a, b, c$ be real numbers, let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on three populations and three edges with the [star-tree weights](star-weight.md) and the [matching edge lengths](star-len.md) $\ell_\star$, and let $M'$ be a drift model (on a possibly different measure) with the [admixture weights](admix-weight.md) $w_\alpha$ and edge lengths $(a, b, c)$. Then the [f2 statistics](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) of the two models' population frequencies agree at every pair of populations.
