# Centred leaf covariance as the double centring of f2

Let $\mu$ be a measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ of a finite set $P$ of $n$ populations be square integrable with respect to $\mu$. Then for all $X, Y \in P$,

$$K(X, Y) = \frac{1}{2}\Bigl(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z} f_2(X, Z) + \frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z} f_2(Z, Y) - f_2(X, Y) - \frac{1}{n^2}\sum_{Z, W} f_2(Z, W)\Bigr),$$

where $K$ is the [centred leaf covariance](centred-cov.md) and $f_2$ the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md): $K$ is half the double centring of the f2 matrix (the classical-scaling identity of Torgerson and Gower).
