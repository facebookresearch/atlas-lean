# Lineage weights of the three-population admixture model

For a real number $\alpha$, the admixture lineage weights on populations $A = 0$, $B = 1$, $C = 2$ and edges $0, 1, 2$ are $w_\alpha(A, \cdot) = (1, 0, 0)$, $w_\alpha(B, \cdot) = (0, 1, 0)$ and $w_\alpha(C, \cdot) = (\alpha, 1 - \alpha, 1)$: $A$ and $B$ sit on edges $0$ and $1$, and for $0 \le \alpha \le 1$, $C$ is an $\alpha : 1 - \alpha$ mixture of $A$ and $B$ followed by drift on its private edge $2$.
