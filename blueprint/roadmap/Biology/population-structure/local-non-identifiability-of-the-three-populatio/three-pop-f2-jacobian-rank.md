# Jacobian rank of the three-population admixture parametrization

At every parameter point `(α, s, a, b, c) ∈ ℝ^5` with `a + b ≠ 0`, the
[Jacobian matrix](../../../Analysis/real-functions/multivariable-calculus/jacobian-matrix/jacobian-matrix.md)
of the [polynomial f2 map](three-pop-f2.md) `ℝ^5 → ℝ^3` has rank exactly `3`, less than the `5`
parameters.
