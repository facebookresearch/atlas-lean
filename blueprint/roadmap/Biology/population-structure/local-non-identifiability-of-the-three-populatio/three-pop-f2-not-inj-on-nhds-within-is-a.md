# Local non-identifiability on the admissible parameter set

At every [admissible](is-admissible.md) parameter point `p`, including boundary points, the
[polynomial f2 map](three-pop-f2.md) is injective on no neighbourhood of `p` relative to the
admissible set: increasing the stem length by an arbitrarily small amount keeps the parameter
admissible, gives a distinct parameter, and changes no f2 value. This is the identifiability
statement for the graph, whose parameters are admissible;
[local non-identifiability everywhere](three-pop-f2-not-inj-on-nhds.md) also allows inadmissible
parameters.
