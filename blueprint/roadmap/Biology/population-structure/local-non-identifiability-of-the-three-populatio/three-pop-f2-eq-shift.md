# Stem length and the split of a + b do not change the f2 values

For all real `α, s, s', a, b, c, t`, the [polynomial f2 map](three-pop-f2.md) takes the same value
at `(α, s', a + t, b - t, c)` as at `(α, s, a, b, c)`: the stem length and the split of `a + b`
between the `A` and `B` edges are invisible to the f2 values, globally.
