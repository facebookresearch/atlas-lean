# The derivative of the three-population f2 map is nowhere injective

At every parameter point of `ℝ^5`, including those with `a + b = 0`, the derivative of the
[polynomial f2 map](three-pop-f2.md) is not injective: it vanishes on the direction of the stem
length `s`, on which the map does not depend. This concerns the derivative only; that the map itself
is injective on no neighbourhood is
[local non-identifiability everywhere](three-pop-f2-not-inj-on-nhds.md).
