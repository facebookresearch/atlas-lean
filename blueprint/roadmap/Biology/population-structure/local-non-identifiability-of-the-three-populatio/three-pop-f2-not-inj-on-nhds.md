# Local non-identifiability everywhere

At every parameter point `p ∈ ℝ^5`, the [polynomial f2 map](three-pop-f2.md) is injective on no
neighbourhood of `p`: [changing the stem length](three-pop-f2-eq-shift.md) by an arbitrarily small
amount gives a distinct, arbitrarily close parameter with the same f2 values.
