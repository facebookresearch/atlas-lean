# The star-tree map has an injective derivative everywhere

At every point of `ℝ^3`, the derivative of the
[length-to-f2 map of the three-leaf star tree](star-tree-f2.md) is injective. So the criterion "a
map between finite-dimensional real spaces that is continuously differentiable near a point, with
injective derivative there, is injective on a neighbourhood of the point" applies at every point,
unlike for the three-population admixture map, whose
[derivative is nowhere injective](three-pop-f2-not-injective-fderiv.md).
