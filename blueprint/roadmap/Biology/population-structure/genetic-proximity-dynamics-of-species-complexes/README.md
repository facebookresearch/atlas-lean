# Genetic proximity dynamics of species complexes

A metapopulation of monomorphic demes with many loci, infinite-alleles mutation and migration whose success decreases with genetic distance. In the many-loci limit the pairwise genetic proximities follow a deterministic ODE; its equilibria describe species complexes (which demes still exchange genes), and their stability determines speciation.
