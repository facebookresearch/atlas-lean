# Maximum likelihood in the admixture model

Asymptotic theory for the maximum likelihood estimators of individual ancestry proportions and ancestral allele frequencies in the admixture model underlying STRUCTURE- and ADMIXTURE-type inference: consistency up to the model's intrinsic non-identifiability, and central limit theorems for finitely many individuals and markers, including a boundary case when an ancestry proportion is zero or one.
