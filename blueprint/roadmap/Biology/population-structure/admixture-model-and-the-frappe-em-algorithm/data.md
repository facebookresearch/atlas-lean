# Allele-copy observations of a genotype matrix

Let $I$ and $J$ be finite and $g$ a genotype matrix with entries in $\{0, 1, 2\}$. The allele-copy data of $g$ is the sequence $x_0, \dots, x_{N-1}$, $N = 2|I||J|$, obtained by listing the triples $(i, j, c) \in I \times J \times \{0, 1\}$ in a fixed order and taking for each the [observation of that copy](copy-allele.md).
