# The FRAPPE EM step

Let $I$ and $J$ be finite, $J$ and $K$ nonempty, and let $g$ be a [polymorphic](polymorphic.md) genotype matrix. The FRAPPE EM step maps [admixture parameters](params.md) $\theta$ to the parameters $(Q', F')$ formed by the [updated ancestry proportions](m-step-q.md) and the [updated allele frequencies](m-step-f.md); these satisfy the constraints of the parameter set.
