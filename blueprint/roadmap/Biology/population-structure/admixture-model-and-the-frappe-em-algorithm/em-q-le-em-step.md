# The FRAPPE update maximizes the EM objective

Let $I$ and $J$ be finite, $J$ and $K$ nonempty, and let $g$ be a [polymorphic](polymorphic.md) genotype matrix. Then for all [admixture parameters](params.md) $\theta'$ and $\theta$,

$$\mathcal{Q}(\theta' \mid \theta) \le \mathcal{Q}(T\theta \mid \theta),$$

where $\mathcal{Q}$ is the EM objective of the [EM theorem](admixture-log-lik-le-of-em-q-le.md) and $T$ the [FRAPPE EM step](em-step.md).
