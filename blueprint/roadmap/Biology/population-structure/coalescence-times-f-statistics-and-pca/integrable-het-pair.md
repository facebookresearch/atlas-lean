# Integrability of pairwise heterozygosity

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for all populations $X, Y$, the function $h(p_X, p_Y)$ is $\mu$-integrable, where $h$ is the [pairwise difference probability](het-pair.md).
