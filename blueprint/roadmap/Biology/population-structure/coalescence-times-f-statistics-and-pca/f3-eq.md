# $f_3$ in terms of expected coalescence times

For every [coalescent model](coalescent-model.md) on a finite measure, with mutation rate $\theta$ and coalescence times $T$, and for all populations $C, A, B$,

$$f_3(C; A, B) = \frac{\theta}{2} \bigl(T(C, A) + T(C, B) - T(A, B) - T(C, C)\bigr),$$

where $f_3$ is the [f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of the model's frequencies.
