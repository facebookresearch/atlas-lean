# Probability that two genes differ at a biallelic site

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, put

$$h(a, b) = a (1 - b) + b (1 - a).$$

For allele frequencies $a, b \in [0, 1]$ of two populations at a biallelic site, it is the probability that a gene drawn from the first population and a gene drawn from the second carry different alleles; $h(a, a) = 2a(1 - a)$ is the heterozygosity of a population.
