# Column mean of a genotype matrix

Let $\iota$ (samples) be a finite set of $n$ elements and $S$ (loci) a finite set. For a real matrix $Z = (Z_{is})$ indexed by $\iota \times S$ and a locus $s$, the column mean is

$$\bar Z_s = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i \in \iota} Z_{is},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$.
