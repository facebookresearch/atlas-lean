# $f_4$ in terms of expected coalescence times

For every [coalescent model](coalescent-model.md) on a finite measure, with mutation rate $\theta$ and coalescence times $T$, and for all populations $A, B, C, D$,

$$f_4(A, B; C, D) = \frac{\theta}{2} \bigl(T(A, D) + T(B, C) - T(A, C) - T(B, D)\bigr),$$

where $f_4$ is the [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) of the model's frequencies.
