# Peter's formula for $f_2$ via expected heterozygosity

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure and let the allele frequencies $p_X$ be square integrable with respect to $\mu$ for every population $X$. Then for all populations $A, B$,

$$f_2(A, B) = \pi(A, B) - \frac{\pi(A, A) + \pi(B, B)}{2},$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) and $\pi$ the [mean heterozygosity](mean-het.md) (Peter 2016).

## Sources

- B. M. Peter, *Admixture, population structure, and F-statistics*, Genetics 202 (2016), 1485–1501.
