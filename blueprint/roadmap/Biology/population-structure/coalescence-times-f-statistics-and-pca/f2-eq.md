# $f_2$ in terms of expected coalescence times

For every [coalescent model](coalescent-model.md) on a finite measure, with mutation rate $\theta$ and coalescence times $T$, and for all populations $A, B$,

$$f_2(A, B) = \theta \Bigl(T(A, B) - \frac{T(A, A) + T(B, B)}{2}\Bigr),$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) of the model's frequencies.
