# Diploid genotype covariance is twice the linkage disequilibrium

Let $x : \{0, 1\}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\sum_g x_g = 1$. Then the [genotype covariance](genotype-cov.md) satisfies (Loh et al. 2013, Eqs. 2–3)

$$z(x) = 2 D(x), \qquad D(x) = x_{11} x_{00} - x_{10} x_{01}.$$

## Sources

- P.-R. Loh, M. Lipson, N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, J. K. Pickrell, D. Reich, B. Berger, *Inferring admixture histories of human populations using linkage disequilibrium*, Genetics 193 (2013), 1233–1254, Eqs. 2–3.
