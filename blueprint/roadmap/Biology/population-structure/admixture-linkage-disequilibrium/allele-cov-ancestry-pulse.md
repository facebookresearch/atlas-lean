# Covariance of local-ancestry indicators after pulse admixture

For all real numbers $\alpha, r$ and every natural number $n$,

$$\mathrm{Cov}\bigl(R_r^n(a_\alpha)\bigr) = \alpha (1 - \alpha) (1 - r)^n,$$

where $a_\alpha$ is the [joint law of local ancestry after the pulse](ancestry-pulse.md), $R_r^n$ the $n$-fold random-mating recursion with recombination fraction $r$ and $\mathrm{Cov}$ the [allele covariance](allele-cov.md). With $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ and $0 \le r \le 1/2$, it is the covariance of the local-ancestry indicators at two loci $n$ generations after the pulse (Chakraborty and Weiss 1988).

## Sources

- R. Chakraborty, K. M. Weiss, *Admixture as a tool for finding linked genes and detecting that difference from allelic association between loci*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 85 (1988), 9119–9123.
