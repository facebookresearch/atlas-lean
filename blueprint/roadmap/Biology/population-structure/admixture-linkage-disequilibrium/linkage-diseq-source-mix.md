# Admixture linkage disequilibrium right after the pulse

For all real numbers $\alpha, p_A, q_A, p_B, q_B$, the [pulse gamete frequencies](source-mix.md) $x$ have linkage disequilibrium

$$D(x) = \alpha (1 - \alpha)\, (p_A - p_B)(q_A - q_B),$$

where $D(x) = x_{11} x_{00} - x_{10} x_{01}$ (Chakraborty and Weiss 1988).

## Sources

- R. Chakraborty, K. M. Weiss, *Admixture as a tool for finding linked genes and detecting that difference from allelic association between loci*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 85 (1988), 9119–9123.
