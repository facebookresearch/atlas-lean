# Weighted LD amplitude with the true source populations

Let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on an s-finite measure $\mu$ with population frequencies $p_X$. Then for all real $\alpha, r$, natural numbers $n$ and populations $A, B$, the [weighted LD integral](weighted-ld.md) with references $R = A$, $S = B$ is

$$W = 2 \alpha (1 - \alpha) (1 - r)^n\, f_2(A, B)^2,$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md). This identity is the discrete-generation analogue of Loh et al. (2013), Eq. 4, whose formula has $e^{-nd}$ in place of $(1 - r)^n$ ([exact and continuous decay factors](exp-neg-mul-le-pow-one-sub-haldane.md)).

## Sources

- P.-R. Loh, M. Lipson, N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, J. K. Pickrell, D. Reich, B. Berger, *Inferring admixture histories of human populations using linkage disequilibrium*, Genetics 193 (2013), 1233–1254, Eq. 4.
