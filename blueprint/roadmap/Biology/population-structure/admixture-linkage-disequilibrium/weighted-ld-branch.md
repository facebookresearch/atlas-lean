# Weighted LD amplitude with two diverged references

Let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on an s-finite measure $\mu$, and let $A, B, A', B', A'', B''$ and $u_A, u_B, u_{A'}, u_{B'}$ satisfy the hypotheses of [f4 equals f2 for references branching off the path](f4-eq-f2-of-branch.md). Then for all real $\alpha, r$ and natural numbers $n$, the [weighted LD integral](weighted-ld.md) with references $A', B'$ is

$$W = 2 \alpha (1 - \alpha) (1 - r)^n\, f_2(A'', B'')^2$$

the discrete-generation analogue of Loh et al. (2013), Eq. 6, whose formula has $e^{-nd}$ in place of $(1 - r)^n$ ([exact and continuous decay factors](exp-neg-mul-le-pow-one-sub-haldane.md)).

## Sources

- P.-R. Loh, M. Lipson, N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, J. K. Pickrell, D. Reich, B. Berger, *Inferring admixture histories of human populations using linkage disequilibrium*, Genetics 193 (2013), 1233–1254, Eq. 6.
