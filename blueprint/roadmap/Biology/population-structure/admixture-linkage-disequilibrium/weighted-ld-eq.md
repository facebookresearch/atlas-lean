# Weighted linkage disequilibrium in terms of the f4 statistic

Let $\mu$ be an s-finite measure and $p_X$ allele frequencies of populations. Then for all real $\alpha, r$, natural numbers $n$ and populations $A, B, R, S$, the [weighted LD integral](weighted-ld.md) is

$$W = 2 \alpha (1 - \alpha) (1 - r)^n\, f_4(A, B; R, S)^2,$$

where $f_4$ is the [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) (both sides with the convention that the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$).
