# Local-ancestry covariance in Morgans

For all real numbers $\alpha, d$ and every natural number $n$,

$$\mathrm{Cov}\bigl(R_{r(d)}^n(a_\alpha)\bigr) = \alpha (1 - \alpha)\, e^{-nd} (\cosh d)^n,$$

where $r(d) = (1 - e^{-2d})/2$ is Haldane's map function, $a_\alpha$ the [joint law of local ancestry after the pulse](ancestry-pulse.md), $R_r^n$ the $n$-fold random-mating recursion and $\mathrm{Cov}$ the [allele covariance](allele-cov.md).

For $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ and map distance $d \ge 0$, it is the covariance of the local-ancestry labels at two loci $d$ Morgans apart after $n$ generations of random mating.
