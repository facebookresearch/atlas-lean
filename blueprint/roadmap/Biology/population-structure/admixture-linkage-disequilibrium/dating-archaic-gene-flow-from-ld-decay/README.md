# Dating archaic gene flow from LD decay

Alleles that entered a population together on one introgressed haplotype are broken apart by recombination at a constant rate per generation, so linkage disequilibrium among them decays exponentially in genetic distance with rate equal to the time since gene flow. These articles define the ascertained LD statistic and its exponential decay, and the correction for errors in the genetic map that would otherwise bias the date downward.
