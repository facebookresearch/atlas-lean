# Recombination preserves total gamete frequency

Let $x : \{0, 1\}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\sum_g x_g = 1$, let $r$ be real and $n$ a natural number. Then $\sum_g R_r^n(x)_g = 1$, where $R_r^n$ is $n$ generations of the random-mating recursion $R_r(y)_g = (1 - r)\, y_g + r\, p_{g_1}(y)\, q_{g_2}(y)$ with marginal allele frequencies $p$ and $q$.

For a gamete probability vector $x$ and $0 \le r \le 1$, the iterates stay probability vectors: $n$ generations of random mating with recombination fraction $r$.
