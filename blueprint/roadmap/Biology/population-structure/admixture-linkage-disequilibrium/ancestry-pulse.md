# Joint law of local ancestry after an admixture pulse

For a real number $\alpha$, the [pulse gamete frequency](source-mix.md) vector with $p_A = q_A = 1$ and $p_B = q_B = 0$; for $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ it is the joint law of the local-ancestry labels at two loci right after the pulse: label $1$ (descent from $A$) at both loci with weight $\alpha$, label $0$ at both loci with weight $1 - \alpha$, and weight $0$ on the mixed labels.
