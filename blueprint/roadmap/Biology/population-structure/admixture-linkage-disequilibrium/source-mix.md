# Gamete frequencies after an admixture pulse

For real numbers $\alpha, p_A, q_A, p_B, q_B$, the pulse gamete frequencies are

$$x_g = \alpha\, \beta_{p_A}(g_1)\, \beta_{q_A}(g_2) + (1 - \alpha)\, \beta_{p_B}(g_1)\, \beta_{q_B}(g_2), \qquad g \in \{0, 1\}^2,$$

where $\beta$ is the [Bernoulli frequency](bern-freq.md). With all parameters in $[0, 1]$, a gamete comes with probability $\alpha$ from population $A$, where the loci are in linkage equilibrium with allele-$1$ frequencies $p_A$ and $q_A$, and otherwise from $B$ (frequencies $p_B$, $q_B$); the definition applies to all reals.
