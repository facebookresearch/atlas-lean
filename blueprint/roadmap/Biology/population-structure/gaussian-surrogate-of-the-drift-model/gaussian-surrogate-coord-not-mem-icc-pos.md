# Surrogate frequencies of positive variance can leave the unit interval

For finitely many populations and edges, every root mean `m`, real lineage weights `w` and
nonnegative edge lengths `ℓ`, and a population `X` with `Σ X X = ∑ e, w X e ^ 2 * ℓ e > 0` (the
variance of its frequency, a diagonal entry of the [drift covariance matrix](drift-cov.md)), the
frequency `x X` of `X` lies outside `[0, 1]` with positive probability under the
[Gaussian surrogate](gaussian-surrogate.md); it is even
[negative with positive probability](gaussian-surrogate-coord-neg-pos.md).
