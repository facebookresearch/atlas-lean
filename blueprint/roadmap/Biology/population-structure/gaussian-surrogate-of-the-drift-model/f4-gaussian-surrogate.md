# f4 of the Gaussian surrogate

For finitely many populations and edges, every root mean `m`, real lineage weights `w` and
nonnegative edge lengths `ℓ`, the [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) of the
[coordinate frequencies](coord-freq.md) under the [Gaussian surrogate](gaussian-surrogate.md)
`N(m 1, W diag(ℓ) Wᵀ)` is, for all populations `A, B, C, D`,
`f4(A, B; C, D) = ∑ e, (w A e - w B e) * (w C e - w D e) * ℓ e`, the formula of
[f4 in the drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4-freq.md). It does not depend on `m`.
