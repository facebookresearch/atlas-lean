# A surrogate frequency of zero variance is almost surely the root mean

For finitely many populations and edges, every root mean `m`, real lineage weights `w` and
nonnegative edge lengths `ℓ`, and a population `X` with `Σ X X = ∑ e, w X e ^ 2 * ℓ e = 0` (every
edge `e` has `w X e = 0` or `ℓ e = 0`), the frequency `x X` of `X` equals the root mean `m` almost
surely under the [Gaussian surrogate](gaussian-surrogate.md). So for `m ≥ 0` it is negative with
probability `0`, and the hypothesis `Σ X X > 0` under which
[surrogate frequencies can be negative](gaussian-surrogate-coord-neg-pos.md) cannot be dropped.
