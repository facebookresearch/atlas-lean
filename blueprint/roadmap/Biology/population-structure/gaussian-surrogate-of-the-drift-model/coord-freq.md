# Allele frequency of a population under the Gaussian surrogate

Under the [Gaussian surrogate](gaussian-surrogate.md), a point `x ∈ ℝ^P` is a vector of population
allele frequencies, and the frequency of population `X` is its coordinate `x X`. The f-statistics of
the surrogate, such as its [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md), are those of these
coordinate functions.
