# Allele frequency of a population under the Gaussian surrogate

For a set `P` of populations, the frequency of population `X` at a point `x ∈ ℝ^P` is its
coordinate `x X`. For finite `P`, a point drawn from the [Gaussian surrogate](gaussian-surrogate.md)
is read in this way as a vector of population allele frequencies, and the f-statistics of the
surrogate, such as its [f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md), are those of these
coordinate functions.
