# Gaussian drift model with one edge and one population

The [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on the probability space
`(ℝ, N(0, 1))`, the real line with the standard normal law, with one edge and one population: root
frequency `1 / 2`, increment `δ(ω) = ω` (a standard Gaussian variable), drift length `1` (its second
moment) and lineage weight `1`. With a single edge, the condition that increments on distinct edges
are orthogonal in L² is vacuous. The example shows that the frequencies of a drift model need not
lie in `[0, 1]`.
