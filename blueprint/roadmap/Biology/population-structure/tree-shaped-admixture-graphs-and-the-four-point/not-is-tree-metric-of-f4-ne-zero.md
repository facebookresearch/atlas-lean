# Three nonzero f4 of a quartet show that f2 is not a tree metric

Let `μ` be a measure and let the allele frequency `p_X` of every population `X` be square integrable
with respect to `μ`. If for some populations `A, B, C, D` the three
[f4 statistics](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) `f4(A, B; C, D)`, `f4(A, C; B, D)` and
`f4(A, D; B, C)` are all nonzero, then the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) is not
a
[tree metric](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/is-tree-metric.md).
In particular the frequencies do not come from a drift model with the weights of a tree-shaped
admixture graph on finitely many nodes, whose
[f2 is a tree metric](is-tree-metric-f2-of-is-tree.md). This follows from
[one f4 of each quartet vanishing under the four-point condition](f4-eq-zero-or-of-four-point-condition.md),
since
[tree metrics satisfy the four-point condition](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/four-point-condition-2.md).
