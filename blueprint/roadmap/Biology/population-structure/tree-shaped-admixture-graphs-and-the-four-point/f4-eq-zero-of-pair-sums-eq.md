# Equal quartet pair sums make all three f4 vanish

Let `μ` be a measure and let the allele frequency `p_X` of every population `X` be square integrable
with respect to `μ`. If populations `A, B, C, D` have equal pair sums of the
[f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md),
`f2(A, B) + f2(C, D) = f2(A, C) + f2(B, D) = f2(A, D) + f2(B, C)`, then the three
[f4 statistics](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) `f4(A, B; C, D)`, `f4(A, C; B, D)` and
`f4(A, D; B, C)` are all `0`. No tree assumption is needed; for a tree metric, equal pair sums mean
a zero internal branch.
