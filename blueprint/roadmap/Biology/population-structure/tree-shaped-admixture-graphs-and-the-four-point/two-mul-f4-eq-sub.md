# Twice f4 is a difference of two quartet pair sums of f2

Let `μ` be a measure and let the allele frequency `p_X` of every population `X` be square integrable
with respect to `μ`. Then for all populations `A, B, C, D` the
[f4](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) and [f2](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) statistics
satisfy `2 f4(A, B; C, D) = (f2(A, D) + f2(B, C)) - (f2(A, C) + f2(B, D))`: twice `f4(A, B; C, D)`
is the difference of two of the three quartet pair sums `f2(A, B) + f2(C, D)`, `f2(A, C) + f2(B, D)`
and `f2(A, D) + f2(B, C)`. Relabelling the quartet gives the same expression for the other two f4
statistics `f4(A, C; B, D)` and `f4(A, D; B, C)`.
