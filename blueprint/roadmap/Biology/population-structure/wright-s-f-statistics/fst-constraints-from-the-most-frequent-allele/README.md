# $F_{ST}$ constraints from the most frequent allele

The range of the fixation index $F_{ST}$ is not $[0,1]$ for every locus: once the frequency $M$ of the allele that is most frequent in the pooled population is fixed, $F_{ST}$ is bounded above by a function of $M$ and the number of subpopulations $K$. Such bounds explain low $F_{ST}$ values at highly polymorphic loci (microsatellites) and in comparisons among diverse populations.
