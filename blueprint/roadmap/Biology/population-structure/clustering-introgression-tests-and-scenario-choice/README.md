# Clustering, introgression tests and scenario choice

Three model-based ways of reading population history from sequence data: clustering a sample into genetically distinct groups, testing which lineages of a donor clade introgressed, and choosing among simulated population-history scenarios. Their models are separate; reading order does not assert a mathematical dependency.
