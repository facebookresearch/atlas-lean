# Identifiability of the admixture proportion from f2 statistics

Let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md), and let $M_x, A, B$, edges $a, b, c$ and $\alpha$ satisfy the hypotheses of the [admixed-branch identity](f2-admixed-branch.md) for two references $X_1$ and $X_2$ (each with zero weight on $a, b, c$). If

$$f_2(A, X_1) - f_2(B, X_1) \ne f_2(A, X_2) - f_2(B, X_2),$$

then

$$\alpha = \frac{f_2(M_x, X_1) - f_2(M_x, X_2) - \bigl(f_2(B, X_1) - f_2(B, X_2)\bigr)}{f_2(A, X_1) - f_2(B, X_1) - \bigl(f_2(A, X_2) - f_2(B, X_2)\bigr)},$$

where $f_2$ is the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) of the population frequencies.
