# Mixed drift determined by f2 statistics

Under the hypotheses of the [admixed-branch identity](f2-admixed-branch.md),

$$\mathrm{MD}(\alpha, \ell_a, \ell_b, \ell_c) = f_2(M_x, X) - \alpha f_2(A, X) - (1 - \alpha) f_2(B, X) + \alpha (1 - \alpha) f_2(A, B),$$

where $\mathrm{MD}$ is the [mixed drift](mixed-drift.md) and $f_2$ the [f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) of the population frequencies.
