# Outgroup f3 statistic

Let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) with [binary loadings](../patterson-s-f-statistics/has-binary-loadings.md) and drift lengths $\ell$, and let $O, A, B$ be populations whose [lineages](../patterson-s-f-statistics/lineage.md) satisfy $L_O \cap L_A = L_O \cap L_B = \varnothing$. Then

$$f_3(O; A, B) = \sum_{e \in L_O} \ell_e + \sum_{e \in L_A \cap L_B} \ell_e,$$

where $f_3$ is the [f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of the population frequencies: the drift from the outgroup to the root plus the drift shared by $A$ and $B$ (Raghavan et al. 2014).

## Sources

- M. Raghavan, P. Skoglund, K. E. Graf, et al., *Upper Palaeolithic Siberian genome reveals dual ancestry of Native Americans*, Nature 505 (2014), 87–91.
