# MixMapper mixed drift

For real numbers $\alpha, \ell_a, \ell_b, \ell_c$, the mixed drift is

$$\mathrm{MD}(\alpha, \ell_a, \ell_b, \ell_c) = \alpha^2 \ell_a + (1 - \alpha)^2 \ell_b + \ell_c,$$

the only combination of the terminal drift lengths of an admixed branch that enters its f2 statistics.
