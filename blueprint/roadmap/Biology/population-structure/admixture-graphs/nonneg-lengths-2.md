# Age monotonicity gives nonnegative branch lengths

Every [phylogenetic network](phylo-network.md) has [nonnegative branch lengths](nonneg-lengths.md):
for every edge $u \to v$, from a parent $u$ to its child $v$, the branch length $t(u) - t(v)$ is at
least $0$, because a parent is never younger than its child.
