# Three-population test is nonnegative on a tree

Let $G$ be an [admixture graph](admixture-graph.md) on a finite set of nodes that is a [tree](is-tree.md), and let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on the edges of $G$ whose lineage weights are the [lineage weights](weight.md) of $G$. Then for all nodes $C, A, B$, the [f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of the population frequencies of $M$ satisfies $f_3(C; A, B) \ge 0$.
