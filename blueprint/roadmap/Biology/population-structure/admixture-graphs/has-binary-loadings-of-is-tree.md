# A tree-shaped admixture graph has binary loadings

Let $G$ be an [admixture graph](admixture-graph.md) on a finite set of nodes that is a [tree](is-tree.md), and let $M$ be a [drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on the edges of $G$ whose lineage weights are the [lineage weights](weight.md) of $G$. Then $M$ has [binary loadings](../patterson-s-f-statistics/has-binary-loadings.md).
