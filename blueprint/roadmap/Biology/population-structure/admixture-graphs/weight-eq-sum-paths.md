# Path-sum formula for lineage weights

In every [admixture graph](admixture-graph.md), for every node $X$ and every [edge](edge.md) $e = (c, p)$, the [lineage weight](weight.md) $w(X, e)$ is the sum of the [path probabilities](path-prob.md) of the [root-ward paths](paths.md) from $X$ that traverse $e$, that is, that contain $c$ immediately followed by $p$ (Patterson et al. 2012).

## Sources

- N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, Y. Luo, S. Mallick, N. Rohland, Y. Zhan, T. Genschoreck, T. Webster, D. Reich, *Ancient admixture in human history*, Genetics 192 (2012), 1065–1093, Appendix.
