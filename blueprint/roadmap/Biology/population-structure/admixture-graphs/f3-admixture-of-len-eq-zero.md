# Three-population statistic at an admixture node without edge drift

Under the hypotheses of the [three-population statistic at an admixture node](f3-admixture.md), suppose moreover that the drift lengths of the two admixture edges vanish: $\ell_{(X, A)} = \ell_{(X, B)} = 0$. Then, with $\alpha = a(X, A)$,

$$f_3(X; A, B) = -\alpha (1 - \alpha)\, f_2(A, B).$$
