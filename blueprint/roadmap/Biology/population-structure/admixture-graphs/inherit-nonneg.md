# Inheritance probabilities are nonnegative

Let $N$ be a [phylogenetic network](phylo-network.md) on a finite set $V$ of nodes, with root
$\rho$, parent sets $\mathrm{pa}(v)$ and [inheritance probabilities](inherit.md) $\gamma(v, u)$.
Then $\gamma(v, u) \ge 0$ for all nodes $v$ and $u$. This is the phylogenetic-network instance of
the nonnegativity of the proportions of an [admixture graph](admixture-graph.md).
