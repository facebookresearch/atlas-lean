# Termwise bound behind nonnegative f3 for a binary target

For real families `len`, `wC`, `wA`, `wB` on a finite index set with `len e ≥ 0`, `wC e` equal to
`0` or `1`, `0 ≤ wA e ≤ 1` and `0 ≤ wB e ≤ 1` for every `e`,
`∑ e, (wC e - wA e) * (wC e - wB e) * len e ≥ 0`: every term is nonnegative. The weights `wA`, `wB`
need not be binary. With drift lengths and lineage weights this gives the
[nonnegative f3 of a binary target](f3-nonneg-of-binary-target.md).
