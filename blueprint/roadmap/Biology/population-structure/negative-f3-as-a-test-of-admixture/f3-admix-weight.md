# Exact f3 in the three-population admixture model

For real numbers `α, a, b, c`, let `M` be a
[drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on three populations `A = 0`, `B = 1`,
`C = 2` and three edges with the
[admixture weights](../centred-population-covariance-and-f-statistics/admix-weight.md) for `α` and
edge lengths `(a, b, c)`. Then the [f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of its
population frequencies is `f3(C; A, B) = c - α (1 - α) (a + b)`: the private drift `c` of `C` minus
the admixture term. So, for every real `α`, `f3(C; A, B)` has the sign of `c - α (1 - α) (a + b)`.
