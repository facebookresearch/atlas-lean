# Matching star-tree lengths are nonnegative exactly when f3 ≥ 0

Let `0 ≤ α ≤ 1`, `a, b ≥ 0` and `c` real, and let `M` be a
[drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on three populations `A = 0`, `B = 1`,
`C = 2` and three edges with the
[admixture weights](../centred-population-covariance-and-f-statistics/admix-weight.md) for `α` and
edge lengths `(a, b, c)`. Then the three
[matching star-tree edge lengths](../centred-population-covariance-and-f-statistics/star-len.md)
`(1 - α) (a + b)`, `α (a + b)` and `c - α (1 - α) (a + b)` are all nonnegative if and only if
`f3(C; A, B) ≥ 0` for the [f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) of the population
frequencies of `M`.
