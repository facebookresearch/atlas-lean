# Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for f-statistics

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure) and let every population
frequency `p X : Ω → ℝ` be square integrable. Then for all populations `A, B, C, D`,
`f4(A, B; C, D) ^ 2 ≤ f2(A, B) * f2(C, D)`, the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for the
[L² inner product form of f4](f4-eq-inner.md); here `f2` and `f4` are the
[f2](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) and [f4](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) statistics.
