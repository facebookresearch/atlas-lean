# Matrix realized by f2 statistics of a drift model

A real matrix `D` indexed by a set `P` of populations is realizable by a drift model when some
[drift model](../patterson-s-f-statistics/drift-model.md) on a probability space, with a finite set
of edges and populations `P`, has [f2 statistics](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md)
`f2(X, Y) = D X Y` for its [population frequencies](../patterson-s-f-statistics/freq.md), for all
`X, Y ∈ P`. Beyond the conditions of a drift model (square-integrable root and increments,
increments of distinct edges orthogonal in L², second moments equal to the drift lengths), the model
is unconstrained: its lineage weights are arbitrary real numbers, and its frequencies are
real-valued and need not lie in `[0, 1]`.
