# Two populations at f2 distance four

The matrix on two populations `0`, `1` with `D 0 0 = D 1 1 = 0` and `D 0 1 = D 1 0 = 4`. It is
conditionally negative semidefinite and is the f2 matrix of a drift model, but not of frequencies in
`[0, 1]` on a probability space, which have `f2 ≤ 1`. So it separates
[realizability by a drift model](is-f2-realizable.md) from
[realizability with frequencies in the unit interval](is-f2-realizable-unit.md).
