# f4 as an L² inner product

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure) and let every population
frequency `p X : Ω → ℝ` be square integrable. Then for all populations `A, B, C, D` the
[f4 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md) is an inner product in `L²(μ)`:
`f4(A, B; C, D) = ⟪[p A] - [p B], [p C] - [p D]⟫`, where `[p X]` is the class of `p X` in `L²(μ)`.
