# Thales criterion for a negative f3

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure) and let every population
frequency `p X : Ω → ℝ` be square integrable. Then for all populations `C, A, B`, `f3(C; A, B) < 0`
if and only if the class of `p C` in `L²(μ)` lies in the open ball with centre the midpoint of the
classes of `p A` and `p B` and radius half their distance, `√f2(A, B) / 2`: strictly inside the ball
with diameter `[p A, p B]`, by the [strict Thales criterion](inner-sub-sub-neg-iff-mem-ball.md)
applied to the [inner product form of f3](f3-eq-inner.md).
