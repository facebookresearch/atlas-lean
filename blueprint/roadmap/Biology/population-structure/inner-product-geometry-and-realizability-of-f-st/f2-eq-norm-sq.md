# f2 as a squared L² distance

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure) and let every population
frequency `p X : Ω → ℝ` be square integrable. Then for all populations `X, Y` the
[f2 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f2.md) is a squared norm in `L²(μ)`:
`f2(X, Y) = ‖[p X] - [p Y]‖ ^ 2`, where `[p X]` is the class of `p X` in `L²(μ)`.
