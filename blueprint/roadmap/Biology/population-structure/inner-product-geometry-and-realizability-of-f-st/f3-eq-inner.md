# f3 as an L² inner product

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` (not necessarily a probability measure) and let every population
frequency `p X : Ω → ℝ` be square integrable. Then for all populations `C, A, B` the
[f3 statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f3.md) is an inner product in `L²(μ)`:
`f3(C; A, B) = ⟪[p C] - [p A], [p C] - [p B]⟫`, where `[p X]` is the class of `p X` in `L²(μ)`.
