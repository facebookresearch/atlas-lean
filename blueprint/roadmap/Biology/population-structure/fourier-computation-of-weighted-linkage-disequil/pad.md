# Zero padding of a sequence to $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$

For a natural number $N \ge 1$ and a real sequence $U = (U_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$, the padded sequence is the complex function on $\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$ given by $\tilde U(k) = U_{\bar k}$, where $\bar k \in \{0, \dots, N - 1\}$ is the representative of $k$. (For $N = 0$, $\mathbb{Z}/0\mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$ and the definition reads an integer $k$ through its natural-number value $|k|$.) When $U_k = 0$ for $k \ge K$ and $K \le N$, this is $U_0, \dots, U_{K-1}$ followed by $N - K$ zeros.
