# Estimator of Hudson's $F_{ST}$ denominator

For natural numbers $m_A, m_B$ and real numbers $x_A, x_B$, with $\hat a = x_A / m_A$ and $\hat b = x_B / m_B$ (and $x/0 = 0$), put (Bhatia et al. 2013)

$$\hat D = \hat a (1 - \hat b) + \hat b (1 - \hat a).$$

The corresponding numerator estimator is the [f2 estimator](f2-hat.md).

## Sources

- G. Bhatia, N. Patterson, S. Sankararaman, A. L. Price, *Estimating and interpreting $F_{ST}$: the impact of rare variants*, Genome Res. 23 (2013), 1514–1521.
