# Unbiased f2 estimator under binomial sampling

Let $\mu$ be a measure, let $m_A, m_B \ge 2$ and $q_A, q_B \in [0, 1]$, and let $X_A, X_B$ be independent under $\mu$ with binomial laws $\mathrm{Bin}(m_A, q_A)$ and $\mathrm{Bin}(m_B, q_B)$. Then the [f2 estimator](f2-hat.md) satisfies (Patterson et al. 2012, Appendix)

$$\int \hat f_2(m_A, m_B, X_A, X_B) \, d\mu = (q_A - q_B)^2.$$

## Sources

- N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, Y. Luo, S. Mallick, N. Rohland, Y. Zhan, T. Genschoreck, T. Webster, D. Reich, *Ancient admixture in human history*, Genetics 192 (2012), 1065–1093, Appendix.
