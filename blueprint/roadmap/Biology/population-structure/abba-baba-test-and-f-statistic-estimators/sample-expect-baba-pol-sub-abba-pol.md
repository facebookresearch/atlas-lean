# Expected polarized ABBA–BABA difference

For every $q = (q_1, q_2, q_3, q_4) \in \mathbb{R}^4$ (Durand et al. 2011),

$$\mathbb{E}_q[\mathrm{BABA}_{\mathrm{pol}} - \mathrm{ABBA}_{\mathrm{pol}}] = (q_1 - q_2)\, q_3\, (1 - q_4),$$

where $\mathbb{E}_q$ is the [sampling expectation](sample-expect.md) and $\mathrm{ABBA}_{\mathrm{pol}}, \mathrm{BABA}_{\mathrm{pol}}$ are the polarized [ABBA](abba-pol.md) and [BABA](baba-pol.md) indicators. When the outgroup is fixed for the ancestral allele ($q_4 = 0$) this equals the unpolarized value $(q_1 - q_2)(q_3 - q_4)$.

## Sources

- E. Y. Durand, N. Patterson, D. Reich, M. Slatkin, *Testing for ancient admixture between closely related populations*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 28 (2011), 2239–2252.
