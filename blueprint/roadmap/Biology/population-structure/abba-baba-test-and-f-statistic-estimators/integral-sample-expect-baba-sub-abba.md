# ABBA–BABA equals the f4-statistic

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be the allele frequency of each population $X$. Then for all populations $P_1, P_2, P_3, O$,

$$\int \mathbb{E}_{(p_{P_1}, p_{P_2}, p_{P_3}, p_O)}[\mathrm{BABA} - \mathrm{ABBA}] \, d\mu = f_4(P_1, P_2; P_3, O),$$

where $\mathbb{E}_q$ is the [sampling expectation](sample-expect.md) and $f_4(A, B; C, D) = \int (p_A - p_B)(p_C - p_D) \, d\mu$ is the [f4-statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md).
