# f4 estimator

For natural numbers $m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D$ and real numbers $x_A, x_B, x_C, x_D$, put

$$\hat f_4 = \Bigl(\frac{x_A}{m_A} - \frac{x_B}{m_B}\Bigr)\Bigl(\frac{x_C}{m_C} - \frac{x_D}{m_D}\Bigr),$$

with the convention $a/0 = 0$. No correction term is needed for four distinct populations.
