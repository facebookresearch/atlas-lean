# ABBA–BABA identity

For every $q = (q_1, q_2, q_3, q_4) \in \mathbb{R}^4$ (Patterson et al. 2012; Durand et al. 2011),

$$\mathbb{E}_q[\mathrm{BABA} - \mathrm{ABBA}] = (q_1 - q_2)(q_3 - q_4),$$

where $\mathbb{E}_q$ is the [sampling expectation](sample-expect.md) and $\mathrm{ABBA}, \mathrm{BABA}$ are the unpolarized [ABBA](abba.md) and [BABA](baba.md) indicators. For frequencies in $[0, 1]$ this is the expected difference of the two site patterns when one haplotype is sampled independently from each population.

## Sources

- N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, Y. Luo, S. Mallick, N. Rohland, Y. Zhan, T. Genschoreck, T. Webster, D. Reich, *Ancient admixture in human history*, Genetics 192 (2012), 1065–1093 (the D-statistic; Appendix: unbiased f-statistic estimators).
- E. Y. Durand, N. Patterson, D. Reich, M. Slatkin, *Testing for ancient admixture between closely related populations*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 28 (2011), 2239–2252.
