# Polarized ABBA site-pattern indicator

For populations $(P_1, P_2, P_3, O)$ and a configuration $x \in \{0, 1\}^4$ of derived-allele indicators, the polarized ABBA indicator is $1$ if $x = (0, 1, 1, 0)$ ($P_1$ and $O$ ancestral, $P_2$ and $P_3$ derived) and $0$ otherwise (Durand et al. 2011).

## Sources

- E. Y. Durand, N. Patterson, D. Reich, M. Slatkin, *Testing for ancient admixture between closely related populations*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 28 (2011), 2239–2252.
