# qpWave matrix of f4-statistics

Let $\mu$ be a measure, $p_X$ allele frequencies of populations, and let $L_0, \dots, L_n$ (left) and $R_0, \dots, R_r$ (right) be populations. The qpWave matrix is the real $n \times r$ matrix with entries

$$F_{ij} = f_4(L_i, L_0; R_j, R_0), \qquad 1 \le i \le n,\ 1 \le j \le r,$$

where $f_4$ is the [f4-statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md).
