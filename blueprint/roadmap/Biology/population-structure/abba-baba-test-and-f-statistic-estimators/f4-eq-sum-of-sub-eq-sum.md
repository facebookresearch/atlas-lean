# Linearity of the f4-statistic in its first pair

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $p_X$ be square-integrable allele frequencies of the populations $X$. Let $A, B, Y, Z$ be populations, $\iota$ a finite set, $C, D : \iota \to$ populations and $c \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ such that

$$p_A(\omega) - p_B(\omega) = \sum_{l \in \iota} c_l \bigl(p_{C_l}(\omega) - p_{D_l}(\omega)\bigr) \qquad \text{for every } \omega.$$

Then $f_4(A, B; Y, Z) = \sum_{l} c_l\, f_4(C_l, D_l; Y, Z)$, where $f_4$ is the [f4-statistic](../patterson-s-f-statistics/f4.md).
