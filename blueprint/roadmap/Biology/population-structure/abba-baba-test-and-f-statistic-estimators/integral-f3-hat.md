# Unbiased f3 estimator under binomial sampling

Let $\mu$ be a measure, let $m_C \ge 2$, $m_A, m_B \ge 1$ and $q_C, q_A, q_B \in [0, 1]$, and let $X_C, X_A, X_B$ have binomial laws $\mathrm{Bin}(m_C, q_C)$, $\mathrm{Bin}(m_A, q_A)$, $\mathrm{Bin}(m_B, q_B)$ under $\mu$ and be pairwise independent. Then the [f3 estimator](f3-hat.md) satisfies (Patterson et al. 2012, Appendix)

$$\int \hat f_3(m_C, m_A, m_B, X_C, X_A, X_B) \, d\mu = (q_C - q_A)(q_C - q_B).$$

## Sources

- N. Patterson, P. Moorjani, Y. Luo, S. Mallick, N. Rohland, Y. Zhan, T. Genschoreck, T. Webster, D. Reich, *Ancient admixture in human history*, Genetics 192 (2012), 1065–1093, Appendix.
