# Random direction from a normalised Gaussian vector

For a vector `G ∈ ℝⁿ`, the Gaussian direction is `‖G‖⁻¹ G`. When `G` has the standard Gaussian law
and `n ≥ 1`, this is the Gaussian representation of a uniformly random direction on the unit
sphere. For `G = 0` the convention `0⁻¹ = 0` makes it `0`; under the standard Gaussian law this
event has probability `0` when `n ≥ 1`.
