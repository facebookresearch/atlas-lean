# Large-dimension limit of the beneficial probability

Fix a distance `z > 0` to the optimum and `x > 0`, and for every `n` let `a_n ∈ ℝ^(n+1)` be a
phenotype with `‖a_n‖ = z` (dimensions `1, 2, 3, …`). Along the mutation sizes
`r_n = 2 z x / √(n + 1)`, that is with `x = r_n √(n + 1) / (2z)` held fixed, the
[beneficial probability](beneficial-prob.md) of `a_n` tends to `1 - Φ(x)` as `n → ∞`, where `Φ` is
the standard normal distribution function.
