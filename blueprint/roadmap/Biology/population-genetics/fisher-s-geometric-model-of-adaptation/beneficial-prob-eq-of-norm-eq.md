# Beneficial probability depends only on the distance to the optimum

For phenotypes `a, a' ∈ ℝⁿ` with `‖a‖ = ‖a'‖` and every real mutation size `r`, the
[beneficial probabilities](beneficial-prob.md) of `a` and `a'` are equal: the probability depends
on the phenotype only through its distance to the optimum (rotational invariance).
