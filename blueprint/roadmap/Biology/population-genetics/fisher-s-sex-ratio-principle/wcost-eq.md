# Sex-ratio fitness with costs equals Shaw–Mohler fitness of investment

For nonzero costs `cs ≠ 0` of a son and `cd ≠ 0` of a daughter, and all real `r'` and `r`, the
[fitness with unequal costs](wcost.md) is the [Shaw–Mohler fitness](w.md) of the investment
fractions: `Wcost(cs, cd, r', r) = W(r', r)`. Combined with the equivalence
[characterizing the uninvadable son fraction](forall-w-le-iff.md), this gives, for `0 < r < 1`:
`Wcost(cs, cd, r', r) ≤ Wcost(cs, cd, r, r)` for every `r'` in `(0, 1)` if and only if `r = 1/2`.
