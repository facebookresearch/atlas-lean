# Selection gradient of the sex-ratio fitness

For all real `x` and `y`, the [Shaw–Mohler fitness](w.md) `W(x, y)` is differentiable in `x` with
derivative `∂W/∂x (x, y) = 1/y - 1/(1 - y)`, independent of `x`: the selection gradient. This holds
also at `y = 0` and `y = 1`, where by the convention `a / 0 = 0` both sides are `-1` and `1`
respectively.
