# Evolutionary stability of the son fraction one half

Let `0 < x < 1` be a son fraction other than `1/2`, and let a fraction `ε` of the population, with
`0 < ε ≤ 1`, switch from `1/2` to `x`, so that the population son fraction becomes
`z = (1 - ε) / 2 + ε x`. Then the [Shaw–Mohler fitness](w.md) of the `1/2` strategy is strictly
higher than that of the mutant: `W(x, z) < W(1/2, z)`.
