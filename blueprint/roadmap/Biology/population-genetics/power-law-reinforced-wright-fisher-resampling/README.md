# Power-law reinforced Wright-Fisher resampling

Replacing the linear sampling weights of the Wright-Fisher model by the $\alpha$-th power of current counts gives a frequency-dependent resampling chain (introduced as a model of repeated voting rounds). Linear weights give the neutral Wright-Fisher model, dual to Kingman's coalescent, with absorption in order $n$ generations; superlinear reinforcement collapses this to order $\log\log n$, sublinear weights make absorption exponentially slow, and the identity of the eventual winner has a sharp all-or-nothing predictability transition at $\alpha=2^{1/k}$.
