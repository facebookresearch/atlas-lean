# Finite-horizon survival in Galton–Watson processes

The probability that a new beneficial mutant lineage survives $n$ generations decreases to Haldane's ultimate survival probability at the geometric rate $\gamma=\varphi'(P_\infty)$. Comparing the offspring generating function with a fractional-linear one that shares its extinction probability and its slope there gives explicit two-sided bounds with the correct rate for Poisson, binomial and negative-binomial offspring.
