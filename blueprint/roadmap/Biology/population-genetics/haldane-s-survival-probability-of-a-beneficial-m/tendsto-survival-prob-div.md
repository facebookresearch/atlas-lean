# Haldane's approximation $\pi(s) \approx 2s$

The [survival probability](survival-prob.md) satisfies $\pi(s)/s \to 2$ as $s \to 0$ with $s > 0$.

## Sources

- J. B. S. Haldane, *A mathematical theory of natural and artificial selection, part V: selection and mutation*, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 23 (1927), 838–844.
