# Two-locus clines in a step environment

Migration–selection clines on the real line when selection changes sign abruptly at one point: explicit single-locus clines with arbitrary intermediate dominance, and the quasi-linkage-equilibrium approximation of two-locus clines under strong recombination, showing that linkage steepens each allele-frequency cline at the environmental step.
