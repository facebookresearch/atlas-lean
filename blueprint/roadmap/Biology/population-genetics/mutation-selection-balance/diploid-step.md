# One diploid generation of selection and mutation

For real numbers $s$, $t$, $U$ and $q$, let $q' = (1 - U)\,q_{\mathrm{sel}} + U$, where $q_{\mathrm{sel}}$ is the [frequency after selection](selected.md). For $q \in [0, 1]$, nonnegative genotype fitnesses, positive mean fitness and $0 \le U \le 1$, it is the loss-of-function frequency after one generation of selection followed by mutation of wild-type alleles at rate $U$.
