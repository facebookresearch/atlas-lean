# Forward-time theory of strong background selection

At a non-recombining locus where deleterious mutations arise at rate $U_d$ and each costs log fitness $s$, the key parameter is $\lambda = U_d/s$, the mean and variance of the Poisson number of deleterious mutations per individual. Following single lineages forward in time, rather than coalescent genealogies backward, gives closed forms for lineage trajectories and for the whole-population site frequency spectrum when $\lambda \gg 1$ and $N s e^{-\lambda} \gg 1$.
