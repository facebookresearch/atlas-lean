# Equilibria lie at or below $U/s$

Let $s$, $t$, $U$ and $q$ be real numbers with $0 < s \le 1$, $t \le 1$ and $2s - s^2 \le t$ (homozygotes at least multiplicatively unfit, which includes recessive lethality $t = 1$), and $0 \le q < 1$. If $q$ is a fixed point of the [diploid generation map](diploid-step.md), then $q \le U/s$.
