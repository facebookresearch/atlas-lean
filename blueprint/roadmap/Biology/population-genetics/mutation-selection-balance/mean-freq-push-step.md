# Demography-free mean, one step

For all real numbers $s$ and $U$, every natural number $N$, every natural number $N' \ge 1$ and every real vector $\mu$ on $\{0, \dots, N\}$ with $\sum_i \mu_i = 1$, the [mean frequency](mean-freq.md) after [one generation of the chain](push-step.md) is $(1 - s)\,m + U$, where $m$ is the mean frequency of $\mu$.
