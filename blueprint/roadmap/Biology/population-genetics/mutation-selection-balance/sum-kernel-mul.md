# Binomial mean of the transition kernel

For all real numbers $s$ and $U$, every natural number $N$, every natural number $N' \ge 1$ and every count $i \le N$, the [binomial transition kernel](kernel.md) $K$ satisfies

$$\sum_{j=0}^{N'} K(i, j)\, \frac{j}{N'} = (1 - s)\frac{i}{N} + U,$$

with the convention $i/0 = 0$.
