# Law of the loss-of-function count under any demography

For real numbers $s$ and $U$, a sequence of population sizes $N_t$ and a real vector $\mu_0$ on $\{0, \dots, N_0\}$, the vectors $\mu_t$ on $\{0, \dots, N_t\}$ are defined by recursion: $\mu_0$ is the given vector and $\mu_{t+1}$ is [one generation of the chain](push-step.md) from $N_t$ to $N_{t+1}$ copies applied to $\mu_t$. When $\mu_0$ is a probability vector and the sampling probabilities lie in $[0, 1]$, $\mu_t$ is the law of the loss-of-function count in generation $t$.
