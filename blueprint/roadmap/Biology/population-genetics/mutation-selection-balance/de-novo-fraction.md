# Fraction of de novo loss-of-function alleles

For real numbers $s$, $U$ and $x$, the de novo fraction is

$$\frac{U}{(1 - s)\,x + U},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $0 \le U \le s \le 1$, $x \in [0, 1]$ and $(1 - s)\,x + U > 0$, it is the fraction of loss-of-function alleles in the next generation that are new mutations, starting from frequency $x$.
