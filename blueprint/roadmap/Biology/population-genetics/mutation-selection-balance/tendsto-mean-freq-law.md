# Expected loss-of-function frequency converges to $U/s$

Let $0 < s < 2$ and $U$ be real numbers, $N_t \ge 1$ population sizes for every $t$, and $\mu_0$ a real vector on $\{0, \dots, N_0\}$ with $\sum_i \mu_{0,i} = 1$. Then the [mean frequency](mean-freq.md) of the [vector $\mu_t$](law.md) converges to $U/s$ as $t \to \infty$, under any demography.
