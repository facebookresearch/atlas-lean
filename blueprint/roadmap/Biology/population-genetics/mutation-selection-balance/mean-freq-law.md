# Demography-free mean

Let $s$ and $U$ be real numbers, $N_t \ge 1$ population sizes for every $t$, and $\mu_0$ a real vector on $\{0, \dots, N_0\}$ with $\sum_i \mu_{0,i} = 1$. Then for every $t$, the [mean frequency](mean-freq.md) of the [vector $\mu_t$](law.md) is $L^t(m_0)$, the $t$-th iterate of the [linear recursion](lin-step.md) $L(x) = (1 - s)x + U$ applied to the mean frequency $m_0$ of $\mu_0$. The identity holds for all real $s$ and $U$; the vectors are probability laws when $0 \le U \le s \le 1$ and $\mu_0$ is one.
