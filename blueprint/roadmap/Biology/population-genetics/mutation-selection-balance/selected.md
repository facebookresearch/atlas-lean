# Loss-of-function frequency after selection

For real numbers $s$, $t$ and $q$, the loss-of-function frequency after selection is

$$\frac{q\,\bigl(1 - s(1 - q) - t q\bigr)}{\bar w(q)},$$

with the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md) $\bar w(q) = 1 - 2q(1 - q)s - q^2 t$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $q \in [0, 1]$, nonnegative genotype fitnesses $1$, $1 - s$, $1 - t$ and $\bar w(q) > 0$, it is the loss-of-function frequency after viability selection in a population in Hardy–Weinberg proportions.
