# Replicator-mutator dynamics and Bayesian filtering

The replicator-mutator equation of evolutionary dynamics, with fitness given by a time-varying log-likelihood, is formally the equation of sequential Bayesian updating. In continuous time this becomes exact: Crow-Kimura replicator-mutator dynamics driven by a smoothed observation path converge to a Stratonovich form of the Kushner-Stratonovich/Zakai filtering equation (giving the exact Bayesian filtering posterior for the weights $r=1$, $s=0$, when initialized at the signal prior $X_0 \sim f$ with independent signal and observation noises), with frequency-dependent fitness producing covariance inflation.
