# Linear mutation–selection recursion

For real numbers $s$, $U$ and $x$, let $L(x) = (1 - s)\,x + U$. For $0 \le U \le s \le 1$ and $x \in [0, 1]$ it is one generation of the linear model acting on the loss-of-function frequency $x$: selection removes the fraction $s$ of loss-of-function alleles and mutation adds $U$.
