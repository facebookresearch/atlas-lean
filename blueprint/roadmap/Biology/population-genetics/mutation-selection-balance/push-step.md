# One generation of the chain on distributions

For real numbers $s$ and $U$, natural numbers $N$ and $N'$ and a real vector $\mu$ on $\{0, \dots, N\}$, the vector on $\{0, \dots, N'\}$ with entries $\sum_{i=0}^{N} \mu_i\, K(i, j)$, where $K$ is the [binomial transition kernel](kernel.md). When every success probability $\pi_i$ of the kernel lies in $[0, 1]$ and $\mu$ is a probability vector, it is the law after one generation of the chain started from $\mu$.
