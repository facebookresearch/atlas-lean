# Haldane–Muller load

For real numbers $s \ne 0$ and $U \ne 1$, with the multiplicative homozygote cost $t = 2s - s^2$,

$$1 - \bar w(U/s) = 2U - U^2,$$

where $\bar w$ is the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md); here $U/s$ is a fixed point of the mutation–selection recursion. For $0 \le U \le s \le 1$ it is the equilibrium frequency of the loss-of-function allele, and $2U - U^2$ is the Haldane–Muller mutation load.
