# Centred quadratic stochastic operators

Quadratic stochastic operators map the trait distribution of a parental generation to that of the offspring generation under random mating. In the centred case an offspring trait is the arithmetic mean of its two parents' traits plus an independent perturbation, and the iterates are laws of weighted sums of independent variables whose weak limits are characterized through characteristic functions.
