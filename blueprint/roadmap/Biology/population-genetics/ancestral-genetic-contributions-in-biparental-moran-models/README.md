# Ancestral genetic contributions in biparental Moran models

In a biparental Moran model with a selected locus, the genetic weight of an ancestor in a present individual is the probability that a gene at an unlinked neutral locus descends from that ancestor. Large-population limits of these weights show how the mode and strength of selection (viability selection, increased attractiveness, homogamous choosiness) shape how much of today's genome comes from initially advantaged founders.
