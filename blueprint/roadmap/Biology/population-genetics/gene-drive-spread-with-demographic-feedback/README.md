# Gene drive spread with demographic feedback

A reaction–diffusion model for a homing gene drive with fitness cost in a population with logistic growth, where the drive lowers population density and the density feeds back on the spread; results on the nonexistence and on the direction of travelling waves (Girardin and Débarre 2021).
