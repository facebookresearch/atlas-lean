# Gene diversity in samples of relatives

Unbiased estimation of gene diversity (expected heterozygosity) $H = 1 - \sum_i p_i^2$ when the sample contains related or inbred individuals of arbitrary ploidy. Kinship coefficients enter as a single bias-correction factor, and the choice of linear allele-frequency estimator (sample proportion or best linear unbiased estimator) controls the variance.
