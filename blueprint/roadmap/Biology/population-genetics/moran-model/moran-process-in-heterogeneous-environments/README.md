# Moran process in heterogeneous environments

In this variant of the Moran birth–death process, an individual's reproductive rate depends both on its type and on the environment of the site it occupies. These articles define the model, give the large-population fixation probability of a randomly placed mutant, and show that heterogeneity in mutant fitness suppresses selection, while heterogeneity in resident fitness does not matter in large populations.
