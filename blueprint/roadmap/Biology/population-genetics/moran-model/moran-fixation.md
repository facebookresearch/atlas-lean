# Fixation probability of the Moran process

For a real number $r$ and natural numbers $N$ and $i$, let

$$\varphi_i = \begin{cases} i/N & \text{if } r = 1, \\[2pt] \dfrac{1 - r^{-i}}{1 - r^{-N}} & \text{if } r \ne 1, \end{cases}$$

with the conventions $0^{-1} = 0$ and that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $r > 0$, $N \ge 1$ and $i \le N$, it is the probability that the Moran process with $N$ individuals and mutant relative fitness $r$, started from $i$ mutants, reaches fixation of the mutant.
