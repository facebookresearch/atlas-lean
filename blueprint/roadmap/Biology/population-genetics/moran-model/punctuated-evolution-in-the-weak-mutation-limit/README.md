# Punctuated evolution in the weak-mutation limit

In a large Moran population with rare mutation, the frequency process follows the replicator (competitive-exclusion) flow on the type simplex, whose pure-type vertices are saddles joined by heteroclinic edges. The population therefore spends most of its time in stasis near a saddle and moves between saddles in fast logarithmic-time sweeps.
