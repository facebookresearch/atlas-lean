# Closed form of Kimura's fixation probability

For every real $\sigma \ne 0$ and every real $x$, Kimura's fixation probability with scaled selection coefficient $\sigma/2$, $u(\sigma/2, x)$, where $u(s, x) = (1 - e^{-2sx})/(1 - e^{-2s})$ for $s \ne 0$ and $u(0, x) = x$, is

$$u(\sigma/2, x) = \frac{1 - e^{-\sigma x}}{1 - e^{-\sigma}}.$$
