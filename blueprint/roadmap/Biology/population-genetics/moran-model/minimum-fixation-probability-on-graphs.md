# Minimum fixation probability of the Moran process on graphs

Determine the minimum fixation probability over connected undirected n-vertex graphs for a fixed mutant fitness r>1 in the birth–death Moran process with a uniformly placed initial mutant.

## What would settle it

Specify the exact finite Markov kernel: choose a reproducer proportional to fitness, then a neighbor uniformly. Define fixation by absorption in the all-mutant state. Determine the asymptotic order of $\min_G \mathrm{fix}(G, r)$, or improve its bounds with a graph family or universal inequality.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Goldberg, Lapinskas and Richerby prove that every sufficiently large connected undirected graph has fixation probability above (log n)^C / n for r > 1 and every C, and construct undirected graphs with fixation probability O(n^{−1/2}), narrowing what they describe as a considerable gap between that lower bound and earlier undirected suppressors.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
