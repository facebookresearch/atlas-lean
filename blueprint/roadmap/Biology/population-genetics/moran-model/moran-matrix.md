# Moran process as a stochastic matrix

For a natural number $N$ and a real number $r > 0$, the [one-step transition probabilities](moran-prob.md) of the Moran process with $N$ individuals and mutant relative fitness $r$ form a stochastic matrix on the mutant counts $\{0, \dots, N\}$: its entries are nonnegative and each row sums to $1$.
