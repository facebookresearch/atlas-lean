# Loss probability of the chain

Let $r > 0$ be real, $N \ne 0$ a natural number, $P$ the [Moran transition matrix](moran-matrix.md) on $\{0, \dots, N\}$ and $i$ a state. Then $P^t(i, 0) \to 1 - \varphi_i$ as $t \to \infty$, where $\varphi_i$ is the [fixation probability](moran-fixation.md). With [the fixation probability of the chain](tendsto-mat-pow-moran-matrix-last.md), absorption at $0$ or $N$ is certain.
