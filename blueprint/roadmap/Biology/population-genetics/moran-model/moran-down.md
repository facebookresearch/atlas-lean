# Down-step probability of the Moran process

For a real number $r$ and natural numbers $N$ and $i$, let

$$b_i = \frac{N - i}{r i + (N - i)} \cdot \frac{i}{N},$$

computed in the real numbers, with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $r > 0$ and $i \le N$, it is the probability that one step of the Moran process takes the mutant count from $i$ to $i - 1$: a wild type is chosen to reproduce, with probability $(N - i)/(r i + N - i)$, and a mutant is chosen to die, with probability $i/N$.
