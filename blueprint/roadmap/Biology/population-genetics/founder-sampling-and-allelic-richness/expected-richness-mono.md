# Expected allelic richness does not decrease with founder sample size

For admissible allele frequencies on a finite set of alleles (`p ≥ 0` and `∑_a p(a) = 1`) and
every sample size `k`, the [expected richness](expected-richness.md) satisfies
`E_k ≤ E_{k+1}`, where `E_k` is the expected richness for `k` gene copies: a larger founder sample
retains at least as many alleles in expectation.
