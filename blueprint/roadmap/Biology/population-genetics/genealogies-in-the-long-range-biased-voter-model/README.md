# Genealogies in the long-range biased voter model

A one-dimensional stepping-stone (biased voter) model of a population expanding into new territory, in which advantageous type-1 cells reproduce faster. Under a long-range scaling the type-1 density converges to a stochastic Fisher–KPP equation; its dual is branching Brownian motion with coalescence driven by intersection local time, and labeled subpopulations obey a coupled system of SPDEs.
