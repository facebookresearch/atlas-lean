# Hoppe's urn generates the Ewens sampling formula

For every real $\theta > 0$ and every integer partition $\pi$ of $n$, the [law of Hoppe's urn](hoppe-law.md) after $n$ draws is the [Ewens sampling formula](ewens-prob.md): $h_n(\pi) = E_\theta(\pi)$.

## Sources

- F. M. Hoppe, *Pólya-like urns and the Ewens sampling formula*, J. Math. Biol. 20 (1984), 91–94.
