# Exchangeability of the Chinese restaurant process, pointwise

For every real $\theta > 0$, every permutation $\sigma$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ and every set partition $\pi$ of $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, the [Chinese restaurant process](crp.md) gives the relabeled partition $\sigma^{-1}(\pi)$, with blocks $\sigma^{-1}(B)$, the same probability as $\pi$.
