# Expected number of tables

For every real $\theta > 0$ and every natural number $n$, the expected number of blocks of a partition drawn from the [Chinese restaurant process](../../../Probability/bayesian-statistics/blackwell-macqueen-urn/crp.md) $\mathrm{CRP}_n$ is

$$\sum_{\pi} \mathrm{CRP}_n(\pi)\, |\pi| = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \frac{\theta}{\theta + i},$$

the expected number of alleles in a sample of $n$ genes.

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles*, Theoret. Population Biol. 3 (1972), 87–112.
