# The Ewens sampling formula is a probability distribution

For every real $\theta > 0$ and every natural number $n$, the [Ewens sampling formula](ewens-prob.md) sums to $1$ over the integer partitions of $n$. Equivalently, $\sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \theta^{c(\sigma)} = \theta(\theta + 1) \cdots (\theta + n - 1)$, where $c(\sigma)$ is the number of cycles of $\sigma$ (Cauchy's cycle-type formula).

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles*, Theoret. Population Biol. 3 (1972), 87–112.
