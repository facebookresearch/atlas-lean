# A double sum of products is the square of a single sum

For real vectors `p` and `v` on a finite set, `∑_i ∑_j p_i p_j (v_i v_j) = (∑_i p_i v_i) ^ 2`: with
multiplicative genotype fitnesses `w_{ij} = v_i v_j`, the
[diploid mean fitness](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/diploid-mean-fitness.md)
`∑_i ∑_j p_i p_j w_{ij}` is the square of the
[haploid mean fitness](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/mean-fitness.md) `∑_i p_i v_i`
of `v`.
