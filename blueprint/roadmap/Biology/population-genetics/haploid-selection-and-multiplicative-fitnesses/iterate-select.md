# Closed form for repeated haploid selection

Let `w` be positive fitnesses on a finite set of types and `p₀` initial frequencies with
`p₀ ≥ 0` and `∑_j p₀(j) = 1`. After `t` generations of
[haploid selection](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/select.md) with the
constant fitnesses `w`, the frequency of type `i` is `p_t(i) = p₀(i) w_i ^ t / ∑_j p₀(j) w_j ^ t`.
