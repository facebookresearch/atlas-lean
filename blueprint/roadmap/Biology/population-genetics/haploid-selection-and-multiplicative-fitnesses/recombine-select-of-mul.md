# Selection then recombination keeps a product haplotype law a product

Consider two loci with two alleles each. If the haplotype frequencies are in linkage equilibrium,
`x(i, j) = p_i q_j`, and the haplotype fitnesses are multiplicative across loci,
`w(i, j) = a_i b_j`, then for every real `r` and every haplotype `(i, j)`, one round of
[haploid selection](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/select.md) on `x` with
fitnesses `w` followed by [random mating with recombination](../linkage-disequilibrium-and-haldane-s-map-functio/recombine.md)
fraction `r` gives the frequency `p'_i q'_j`, where `p'` is one round of haploid selection on `p`
with fitnesses `a` and `q'` one round on `q` with fitnesses `b`.
