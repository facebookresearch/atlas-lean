# Diploid mean fitness with multiplicative fitnesses is a square

For real vectors `p` and `v` on a finite set of alleles and multiplicative genotype fitnesses
`w_{ij} = v_i v_j`, the
[diploid mean fitness](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/diploid-mean-fitness.md)
is the square of the
[haploid mean fitness](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/mean-fitness.md) of `v`:
`∑_i ∑_j p_i p_j w_{ij} = (∑_i p_i v_i) ^ 2`.
