# Two-locus migration–selection on a quantitative trait

A deterministic continuous-time model of an additive trait controlled by two diallelic loci in a population of two demes with symmetric migration and quadratic selection towards deme-specific optima $\pm P$. It describes how migration rate, divergence of the optima, recombination and the ratio of locus effects determine which polymorphisms can be maintained.
