# One-step decay of heterozygosity under Wright–Fisher drift

For every natural number $M$ and every state $i \in \{0, \dots, M\}$, the [Wright–Fisher matrix](wright-fisher.md) and the [heterozygosity](heterozygosity.md) satisfy

$$\sum_{j=0}^{M} P_M(i, j)\, H(j) = \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{M}\Bigr) H(i),$$

with the convention $1/0 = 0$; for $M = 0$ both sides are $0$, because then $H = 0$.
