# Geometric decay of heterozygosity under Wright–Fisher drift

For all natural numbers $M$ and $t$ and every state $i \in \{0, \dots, M\}$, the [Wright–Fisher matrix](wright-fisher.md) and the [heterozygosity](heterozygosity.md) satisfy

$$\sum_{j=0}^{M} P_M^{\,t}(i, j)\, H(j) = \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{M}\Bigr)^{t} H(i),$$

with the convention $1/0 = 0$; for $M = 0$ both sides are $0$.

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *Mathematical Population Genetics I: Theoretical Introduction*, 2nd ed., Springer (2004).
