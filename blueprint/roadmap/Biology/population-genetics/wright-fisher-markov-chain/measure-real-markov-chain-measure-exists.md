# Fixation probability of the finite Wright–Fisher chain

For every natural number $M \ge 1$ and every state $i \in \{0, \dots, M\}$, under the law on paths of the Markov chain with the [Wright–Fisher transition matrix](wright-fisher.md) $P_M$ started at $i$, the probability that the path ever reaches $M$ is $i/M$, the initial allele frequency.
