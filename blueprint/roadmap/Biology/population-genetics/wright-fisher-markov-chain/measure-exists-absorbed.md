# Almost-sure absorption of the Wright–Fisher chain

Let $M$ be a natural number, $\mu$ a probability measure and $(X_t)$ a Markov chain on $\{0, \dots, M\}$ with the [Wright–Fisher transition matrix](wright-fisher.md) $P_M$ with respect to a filtration, under $\mu$, with $X_0 = i$ almost surely. Then almost surely the chain is eventually absorbed: $\mu(\exists t,\ X_t = 0 \text{ or } X_t = M) = 1$.
