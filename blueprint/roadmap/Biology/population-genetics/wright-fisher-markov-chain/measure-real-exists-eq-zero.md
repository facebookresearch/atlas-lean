# Loss probability of a Wright–Fisher chain

Let $M \ge 1$, let $\mu$ be a probability measure and let $(X_t)$ be a Markov chain on $\{0, \dots, M\}$ with the [Wright–Fisher transition matrix](wright-fisher.md) $P_M$ with respect to a filtration, under $\mu$, with $X_0 = i$ almost surely. Then the chain ever reaches $0$, the loss of $A$, with probability $\mu(\exists t,\ X_t = 0) = 1 - i/M$.
