# Fixation probability in the Wright–Fisher chain

For every natural number $M \ge 1$ and every state $i \in \{0, \dots, M\}$, the transition probabilities of the [Wright–Fisher chain](wright-fisher.md) satisfy $P_M^{\,t}(i, M) \to i/M$ as $t \to \infty$: started from $i$ copies, allele $A$ is fixed with limiting probability $i/M$.

## Sources
