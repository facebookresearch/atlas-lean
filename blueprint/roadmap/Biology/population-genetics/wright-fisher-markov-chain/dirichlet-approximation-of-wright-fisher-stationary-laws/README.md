# Dirichlet approximation of Wright–Fisher stationary laws

The stationary distribution of a finite-population Wright-Fisher chain with parent-independent mutation is approximated by the Dirichlet law of its diffusion limit. Stein's method with a prelimit generator comparison gives an explicit $O(1/N)$-type rate in a smooth-function metric.
