# Wright–Fisher rows are binomial laws

For every natural number $M$ and all states $i, j \in \{0, \dots, M\}$, the entry $P_M(i, j)$ of the [Wright–Fisher matrix](wright-fisher.md) is the probability of $\{j\}$ under the binomial distribution $\mathrm{Bin}(M, z_i)$, where $z_i = i/M$ (with $i/0 = 0$).
