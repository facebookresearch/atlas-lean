# Polynomial evolutionary operators on the simplex

Discrete-time population-genetic dynamics under mutation, recombination and weak selection iterate a polynomial map of the frequency simplex into itself; Markov chains are the degree-one case. The space of all such maps of bounded degree has a simple convex geometry, and its extreme "folding" maps generalize the logistic map $4x(1-x)$ to higher dimension and degree.
