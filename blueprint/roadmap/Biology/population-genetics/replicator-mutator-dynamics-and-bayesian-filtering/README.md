# Replicator-mutator dynamics and Bayesian filtering

The replicator-mutator equation of evolutionary dynamics, with fitness given by a time-varying log-likelihood, is formally the equation of sequential Bayesian updating. In continuous time this becomes exact: Crow-Kimura replicator-mutator dynamics driven by a smoothed observation path converge to a Stratonovich form of the Kushner-Stratonovich/Zakai filtering equation, with frequency-dependent fitness producing covariance inflation.
