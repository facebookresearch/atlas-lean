# Transition density of the multi-allelic Wright–Fisher diffusion with selection

This node covers transition-function expansions for the multi-allelic Wright–Fisher diffusion with
selection. Barbour, Ethier and Griffiths (2000) find by duality an expansion of the transition function
of the reversible $K$-allele diffusion model; with selection it depends on the law at time $t$ of a
$K$-type birth-and-death process started at infinity. Steinrücken, Wang and Song (2013) find the
eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the generator for an arbitrary number of alleles under general diploid
selection and recurrent parent-independent mutation, which gives a spectral representation of the
transition density.

## Expansion

A sound expansion builds on the biallelic case, states the multi-allelic eigenvalue problem and the
convergence of the spectral series for positive times, and states the duality expansion separately with
its construction of the birth-and-death process.
