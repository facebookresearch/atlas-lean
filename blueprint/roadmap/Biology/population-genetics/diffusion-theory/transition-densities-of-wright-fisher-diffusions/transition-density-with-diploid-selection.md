# Transition density of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with diploid selection

This node covers representations of the transition density of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with recurrent
mutation and arbitrary diploid selection. Song and Steinrücken (2012) find the eigenvalues and
eigenfunctions of the generator of this diffusion, which gives a spectral representation of the
transition density and, as a by-product, the rate of convergence to the stationary distribution under
mutation–selection balance. Barbour, Ethier and Griffiths (2000) obtain, by duality, an expansion of the
transition function of the reversible $K$-allele diffusion with selection.

## Expansion

A sound expansion first develops the neutral spectral theory of the mutation generator, then states the
eigenvalue problem with selection and the convergence of the resulting spectral series for positive
times, and identifies the series with the transition kernel.
