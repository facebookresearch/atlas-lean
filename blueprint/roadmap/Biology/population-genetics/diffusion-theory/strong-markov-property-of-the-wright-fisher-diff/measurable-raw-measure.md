# The path measure is measurable in the initial state

The map `x ↦ P_x` from `[0, 1]` to the probability measures on the
[sample paths](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/sample-path.md), where `P_x` is
the [canonical path measure](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/raw-measure.md), is
measurable for the σ-algebra on measures generated by the evaluations `ν ↦ ν(B)` at measurable sets
`B`.
