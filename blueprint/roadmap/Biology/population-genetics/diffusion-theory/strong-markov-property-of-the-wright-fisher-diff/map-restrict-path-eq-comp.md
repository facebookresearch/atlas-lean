# Path law of a Markov family

Let `μ` be a finite measure on a measurable space `Ω`, `(𝒢_u)_{u ≥ 0}` a filtration and `Y` a
[Markov family](is-neutral-markov-family.md) for the neutral Wright–Fisher semigroup with respect to
`(𝒢_u)` under `μ`. For every `A ∈ 𝒢_0`, the law of the path `(Y_u)_{u ≥ 0}` under `μ` restricted to
`A` is `∫ P_y ν(dy)`, where `ν` is the law of `Y_0` under `μ` restricted to `A` and `P_y` is the
[canonical path measure](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/raw-measure.md) of the
neutral Wright–Fisher diffusion started at `y`.
