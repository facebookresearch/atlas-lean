# Relabeling symmetry of the sojourn density under selection

For all real $\sigma$, $p$ and $x$, the Green formula $\Psi_\sigma(p, x)$ of the
[diffusion with selection](../allele-age-and-the-maruyama-effect/selection-green.md) satisfies
$\Psi_{-\sigma}(1 - p, 1 - x) = \Psi_\sigma(p, x)$. For $p \in [0, 1]$ and $x \in (0, 1)$, where
$\Psi_\sigma(p, \cdot)$ is a sojourn density, relabeling the two alleles sends the frequencies $p$
and $x$ to $1 - p$ and $1 - x$ and the selection coefficient $\sigma$ to $-\sigma$.
