# Relabeling symmetry of the sojourn density under selection

For all real $\sigma$, $p$ and $x$, the [sojourn density of the diffusion with selection](../allele-age-and-the-maruyama-effect/selection-green.md)
satisfies $\Psi_{-\sigma}(1 - p, 1 - x) = \Psi_\sigma(p, x)$: relabeling the two alleles sends
the frequencies $p$ and $x$ to $1 - p$ and $1 - x$ and the selection coefficient $\sigma$ to
$-\sigma$.
