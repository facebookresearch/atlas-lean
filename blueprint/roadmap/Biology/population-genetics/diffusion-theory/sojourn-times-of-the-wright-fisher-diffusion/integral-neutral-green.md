# Mean absorption time as a sojourn integral

For every $p \in [0, 1]$, the total expected sojourn time is

$$\int_0^1 G(p, x)\, dx = -2\bigl(p \log p + (1 - p)\log(1 - p)\bigr),$$

the [Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/mean-absorption-time.md) in units of $2N$ generations, where $G$ is the [neutral Green's function](neutral-green.md) and $0 \log 0 = 0$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
