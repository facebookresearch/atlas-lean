# Weak-selection limit of the mean absorption time

For every $p \in [0, 1]$, the [mean absorption time under selection](selection-mean-absorption-time.md) satisfies
$T_\sigma(p) \to -2\bigl(p \log p + (1 - p)\log(1 - p)\bigr)$ as $\sigma \to 0$, the
[Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/mean-absorption-time.md) (with $0 \log 0 = 0$).
