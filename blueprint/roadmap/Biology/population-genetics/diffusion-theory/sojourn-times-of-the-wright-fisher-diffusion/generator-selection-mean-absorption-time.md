# The mean absorption time under selection solves L_σ T = −1

For every real $\sigma$ and every $p \in (0, 1)$, the
[mean absorption time under selection](selection-mean-absorption-time.md) $T_\sigma$ satisfies $(L_\sigma T_\sigma)(p) = -1$,
where $L_\sigma$ is the [backward generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with
selection.
