# Without selection the mean absorption time is the Kimura–Ohta time

For every $p \in [0, 1]$, the [mean absorption time under selection](selection-mean-absorption-time.md) at $\sigma = 0$ is
the [Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/mean-absorption-time.md):

$$T_0(p) = -2\bigl(p \log p + (1 - p)\log(1 - p)\bigr),$$

with $0 \log 0 = 0$.
