# Relabeling symmetry of the mean absorption time under selection

For all real $\sigma$ and $p$, the [mean absorption time under selection](selection-mean-absorption-time.md) satisfies
$T_{-\sigma}(1 - p) = T_\sigma(p)$. For $p \in [0, 1]$ these are mean absorption times; for
$p \notin [0, 1]$ both sides are the integral of a non-integrable function, taken to be $0$, and not
mean hitting times.
