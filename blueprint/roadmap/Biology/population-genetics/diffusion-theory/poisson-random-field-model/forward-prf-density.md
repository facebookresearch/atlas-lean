# Stationary forward equation for the Poisson random field density

For all real $\theta$, $\sigma$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the weighted density $F(y) = y(1 - y)\,\varphi_{\theta,\sigma}(y)$ of the [Poisson random field density](prf-density.md) satisfies

$$\frac{1}{2} F''(x) - \sigma F'(x) = 0,$$

the stationary forward equation $\frac{1}{2}(x(1 - x)\varphi)'' - (\sigma x(1 - x)\varphi)' = 0$ of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with genic selection $\sigma$.

## Sources

- S. A. Sawyer and D. L. Hartl, *Population genetics of polymorphism and divergence*, Genetics 132 (1992), 1161–1176.
