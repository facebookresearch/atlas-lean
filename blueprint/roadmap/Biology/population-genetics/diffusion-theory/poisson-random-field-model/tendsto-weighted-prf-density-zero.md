# Boundary normalization at zero

For all real $\theta$ and $\sigma$, the weighted density $x(1 - x)\,\varphi_{\theta,\sigma}(x)$ of the [Poisson random field density](prf-density.md) tends to $\theta$ as $x \to 0^+$. This limit is not the probability current, which is [computed separately](current-prf-density.md).
