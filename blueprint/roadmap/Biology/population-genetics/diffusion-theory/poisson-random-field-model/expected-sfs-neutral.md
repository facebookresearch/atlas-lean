# Neutral site frequency spectrum

For every real $\theta$ and all natural numbers $i$ and $n$ with $1 \le i < n$, the neutral [expected site frequency spectrum](expected-sfs.md) is $\mathbb{E}\,\xi_i = \theta/i$.

## Sources

- Y.-X. Fu, *Statistical properties of segregating sites*, Theoret. Population Biol. 48 (1995), 172–197.
