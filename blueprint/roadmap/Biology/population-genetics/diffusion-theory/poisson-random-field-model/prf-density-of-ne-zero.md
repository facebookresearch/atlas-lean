# Closed form of the Poisson random field density with selection

For every real $\theta$, every real $\sigma \ne 0$ and every real $x$, the [Poisson random field density](prf-density.md) is

$$\varphi_{\theta,\sigma}(x) = \frac{\theta\bigl(1 - e^{-2\sigma(1 - x)}\bigr)}{\bigl(1 - e^{-2\sigma}\bigr)\, x(1 - x)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$.
