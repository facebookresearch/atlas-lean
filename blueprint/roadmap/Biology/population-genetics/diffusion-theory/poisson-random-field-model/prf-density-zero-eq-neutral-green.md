# The neutral density from the Green's function

For every real $\theta$ and all real $p$, $x$ with $0 < p \le x < 1$, the neutral [Poisson random field density](prf-density.md) satisfies

$$\varphi_{\theta,0}(x) = \frac{\theta}{2p}\, G(p, x),$$

where $G$ is the [neutral Green's function](../sojourn-times-of-the-wright-fisher-diffusion/neutral-green.md). For $\theta \ge 0$: if new neutral mutations enter at a small frequency $p$ at rate $\theta/(2p)$ per unit time, so that the mutational input of frequency is $\theta/2$, the expected sojourn density at $x \ge p$ is $\theta/x$.
