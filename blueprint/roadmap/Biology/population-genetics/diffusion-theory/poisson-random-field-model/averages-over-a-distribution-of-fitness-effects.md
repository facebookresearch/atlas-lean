# Diffusion observables averaged over a distribution of fitness effects

This page covers a research target: averaging diffusion observables over a distribution of fitness effects. When the selection coefficient of a new mutation is drawn from a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}$, the observables of the Poisson random field, the [density of the frequency spectrum](prf-density.md), the fixation rate, the age of a mutation and the load, become integrals against that measure. The target is to state these averages together with the integrability conditions on the measure under which they are finite, and the dependence of the averaged fixation rate on the population size.

## Expansion

Fix from a source the class of distributions, then state each averaged observable and its integrability condition as a page of its own.

Prerequisites:

- [Sawyer–Hartl Poisson random field density](prf-density.md)
