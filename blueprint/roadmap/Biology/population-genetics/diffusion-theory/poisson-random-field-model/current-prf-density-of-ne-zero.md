# Probability current of the Poisson random field density for σ ≠ 0

For every real $\theta$, every real $\sigma \ne 0$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, with the weighted
density $F(y) = y(1 - y)\,\varphi_{\theta,\sigma}(y)$ of the
[Poisson random field density](prf-density.md),

$$\sigma F(x) - \frac{1}{2} F'(x) = \frac{\theta \sigma}{1 - e^{-2\sigma}}.$$
