# Derivative jump of the killed Green's function at the diagonal

For `0 < y < 1`, the right derivative of `x ↦ G_C(x, y)` at `x = y` minus its left derivative equals
`-2 / (y (1 - y))`, where [`G_C`](killed-green.md) is the killed Green's function: the unit-source
condition for a Green's function of Patterson's Equation 4.
