# The Wronskian of Patterson's two solutions is −1

For `0 < y < 1`, the Wronskian `a₁(y) a₂'(y) - a₁'(y) a₂(y)` of Patterson's solutions
[`a₁`](patterson-aux1.md) and [`a₂`](patterson-aux2.md) equals `-1`. It fixes the size
`-2 / (y (1 - y))` of the [derivative jump](killed-green-deriv-jump.md) of the killed Green's
function at the diagonal.
