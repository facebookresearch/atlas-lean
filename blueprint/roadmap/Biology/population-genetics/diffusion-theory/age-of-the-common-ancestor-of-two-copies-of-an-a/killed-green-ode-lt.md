# The killed backward equation below the diagonal

For `0 < x < y < 1`, the killed Green's function [`G_C`](killed-green.md) satisfies
`x (1 - x) ∂²G_C/∂x²(x, y) / 2 = G_C(x, y) / x`, that is `L₀ G_C(·, y) (x) = G_C(x, y) / x` for the
[neutral generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) `L₀`: the time-integrated form of
Patterson's Equation 4 off the diagonal.
