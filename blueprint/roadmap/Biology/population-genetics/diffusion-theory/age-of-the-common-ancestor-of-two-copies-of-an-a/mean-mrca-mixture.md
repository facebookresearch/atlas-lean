# The mixture of new- and old-allele MRCA ages equals y

For `0 < y < 1`, `(1 - y) M₁(y) + y M₂(y) = y`, where [`M₁`](mean-mrca-new.md) and
[`M₂`](mean-mrca-old.md) are Patterson's functions for a new and an old allele. Since `M₂` is
defined from `M₁` by Equation 17, the identity is algebraic and holds whatever the values of `M₁`.
Patterson obtains Equation 17 by solving this mixture, whose value `y` he takes from his Theorem 5
and Corollary 1 (p. 1098); the analytic content of his Theorem 5(2) is the
[weighted-integral identity](killed-green-weighted-integral-add-patte.md).
