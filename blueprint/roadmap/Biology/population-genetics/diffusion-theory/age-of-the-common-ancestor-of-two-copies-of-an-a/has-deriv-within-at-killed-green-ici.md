# Right derivative of the killed Green's function at the diagonal

For `0 < y < 1`, the function `x ↦ G_C(x, y)`, where [`G_C`](killed-green.md) is the killed Green's
function, has at `x = y` the right derivative (derivative within `[y, ∞)`)
`-2 a₁(y) / ((1 - y) y³)`, where [`a₁`](patterson-aux1.md) is Patterson's first solution.
