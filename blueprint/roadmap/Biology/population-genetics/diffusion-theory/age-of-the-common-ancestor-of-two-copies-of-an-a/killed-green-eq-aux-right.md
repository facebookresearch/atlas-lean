# The killed Green's function above the diagonal in terms of a₁ and a₂

For real numbers `y < x` with `x ≠ 0`, `y ≠ 0` and `y ≠ 1`,
`G_C(x, y) = 2 a₁(y) a₂(x) / ((1 - y) y)`, where [`G_C`](killed-green.md) is the killed Green's
function and [`a₁`](patterson-aux1.md), [`a₂`](patterson-aux2.md) are Patterson's two solutions.
