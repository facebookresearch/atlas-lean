# Patterson's polynomial eigenfunction for eigenvalue 10

For every real number `x`, `β(x) = x² (1 - x) (28x² - 35x + 10)` satisfies
`(1 - 10x) β(x) = x² (1 - x) β''(x) / 2`. This is the instance `k = 2`, `n = 2` of Patterson's
Appendix Equation A1, with `β = x² (1 - x) w₂` for the polynomial `w₂ = 28x² - 35x + 10` of his
Table A1 and eigenvalue `10`.
