# Euler's reflection formula for Patterson's dilogarithm

For `0 < y < 1`, Patterson's dilogarithm [`dilog`](patterson-dilog.md) satisfies
`dilog(y) + dilog(1 - y) + log y · log(1 - y) = π² / 6`: Euler's reflection formula
`Li₂(z) + Li₂(1 - z) + log z · log(1 - z) = π² / 6`, since `dilog(y) = Li₂(1 - y)`.
