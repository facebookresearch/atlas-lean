# Feller families of kernels on the unit interval

A family `κ = (κ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` of kernels on `[0, 1]` is Feller if, for every time `t ≥ 0` and every
continuous `g : [0, 1] → ℝ`, the function `x ↦ ∫ g(y) κ_t(x, dy)` is continuous on `[0, 1]`: the
kernels map continuous functions to continuous functions.
