# The neutral Wright–Fisher kernels form a Feller family

The
[neutral Wright–Fisher kernels](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/neutral-kernel.md)
`(K_t)_{t ≥ 0}` form a [Feller family](is-feller-family.md): for every continuous `g : [0, 1] → ℝ`,
`x ↦ ∫ g(y) K_t(x, dy) = (T_t g)(x)` is continuous, where `T` is the
[neutral Wright–Fisher semigroup](../wright-fisher-semigroup-and-moment-duality/wf-semigroup.md).
