# The path measure is measurable in the initial state

For a family `κ = (κ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` of Markov kernels on `[0, 1]` satisfying the Chapman–Kolmogorov
equations `κ_{s + t}(x, ·) = ∫ κ_t(y, ·) κ_s(x, dy)`, the map `x ↦ P^κ_x` from `[0, 1]` to measures
on the [sample paths](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/sample-path.md), where
`P^κ_x` is the
[path measure](../../../../Probability/markov-chains/markov-processes-from-transition-semigroups/path-measure.md)
of `κ` with input `x`, is measurable for the σ-algebra on measures generated by the evaluations
`ν ↦ ν(B)` at measurable sets `B`. Here `x` is the input of the kernel construction: the time-`0`
marginal of `P^κ_x` is `κ_0(x, ·)`. This marginal is the point mass at the input for every input
exactly when `κ_0` is the identity kernel, which is not assumed here; the Chapman–Kolmogorov
equations alone do not imply it (the constant family `κ_t(x, ·) = δ_0` satisfies them, and
`κ_0(1, ·) = δ_0`).
