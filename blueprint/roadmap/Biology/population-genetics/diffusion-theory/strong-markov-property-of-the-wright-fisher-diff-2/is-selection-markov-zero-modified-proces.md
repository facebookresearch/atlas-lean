# The neutral realization satisfies the selection hypotheses at σ = 0

For every `x ∈ [0, 1]`, the
[continuous realization](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/modified-process.md)
`X` of the neutral Wright–Fisher process started at `x`, under the
[canonical path measure](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/raw-measure.md) `P_x`
and with its
[natural filtration](../neutral-wright-fisher-martingale-problem/natural-filtration.md), is a
[Wright–Fisher diffusion with selection `0`, Markov for](is-selection-markov.md) the
[neutral Wright–Fisher kernels](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/neutral-kernel.md).
