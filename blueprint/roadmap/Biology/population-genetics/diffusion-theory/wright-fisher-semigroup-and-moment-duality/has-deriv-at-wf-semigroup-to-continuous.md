# Polynomial semigroup dynamics

For every real polynomial $p$ and every real $t > 0$, the map $s \mapsto T_s\, p$, from the reals to the continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ with the sup norm, is differentiable at $t$ with derivative $T_t\,(\frac{1}{2} X(1 - X)\, p'')$, where $T_s$ is the [Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup](wf-semigroup.md).
