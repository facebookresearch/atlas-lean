# Wright–Fisher semigroup on polynomials by moment duality

For a real number $t$, the linear map $Q_t$ on real polynomials defined on the monomial basis by $Q_t(X^n) = D_{t,n}$, the [dual moment polynomial](dual-moment-poly.md), and extended linearly. For $t \ge 0$ it is the neutral Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup acting on polynomials; the formula is defined for every real $t$.
