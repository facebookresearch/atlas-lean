# Transition kernel of the neutral Wright–Fisher diffusion

For a real number $t$, the Markov kernel $K_t$ on $[0, 1]$ obtained from the [positive Wright–Fisher operator](wf-semigroup-positive.md) $T_t$, which fixes the constant $1$, by the Riesz representation: each $K_t(x, \cdot)$ is a probability measure on $[0, 1]$ with $\int f \, dK_t(x, \cdot) = (T_t f)(x)$ for every continuous $f$ ([representation of the semigroup](integral-wf-transition-kernel.md)). For $t \ge 0$ it is the transition kernel of the neutral Wright–Fisher diffusion; for $t < 0$ it is the identity kernel.
