# Convergence on polynomials

For every real polynomial $p$ and every real $t \ge 0$, $W_M^{\lfloor tM \rfloor} p \to Q_t\, p$ uniformly on $[0, 1]$ as the natural number $M$ tends to infinity, where $W_M$ is the [Wright–Fisher generation operator](wf-step.md) and $Q_t$ the [dual semigroup](poly-semigroup.md): $\lfloor tM \rfloor$ generations of the chain with $M$ gene copies converge on polynomials, by moment duality and Kingman's theorem for the lineage count.
