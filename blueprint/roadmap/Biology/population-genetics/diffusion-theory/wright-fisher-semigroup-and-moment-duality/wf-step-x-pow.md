# One Wright–Fisher generation acting on monomials

For all natural numbers $M \ne 0$ and $n$, the [Wright–Fisher generation operator](wf-step.md) maps the monomial $x \mapsto x^n$ on $[0, 1]$ to the polynomial $x \mapsto \sum_{k=0}^{n} p_M(n, k)\, x^k$, with the [Wright–Fisher lineage-count probabilities](../../../coalescent-theory/ancestral-lineage-counting-and-tavar-s-formula/wf-lineage-prob.md) $p_M(n, k)$.
