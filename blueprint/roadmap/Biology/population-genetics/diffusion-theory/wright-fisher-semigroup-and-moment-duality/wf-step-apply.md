# Wright–Fisher step is the Bernstein operator

For every natural number $M$ and every continuous function $f$ on $[0, 1]$, the [Wright–Fisher generation operator](wf-step.md) $W_M$ applied to $f$ is the Bernstein approximation $x \mapsto \sum_{k=0}^{M} f(k/M) \binom{M}{k} x^k (1 - x)^{M - k}$ of order $M$ of $f$.
