# Rescaled Wright–Fisher iterates form a Cauchy sequence

For every real $t$ and every continuous function $f$ on $[0, 1]$, the sequence $(W_M^{\lfloor tM \rfloor} f)_M$, with the [Wright–Fisher generation operator](wf-step.md) $W_M$ and the natural floor ($0$ when $t \le 0$), is a Cauchy sequence for the sup norm: it converges on the dense set of polynomials and the operators are contractions.
