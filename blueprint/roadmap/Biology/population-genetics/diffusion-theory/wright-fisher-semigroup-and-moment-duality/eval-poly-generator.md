# Polynomial generator agrees with the neutral Wright–Fisher generator

For every real polynomial $p$ and every real $x$, the [polynomial generator](poly-generator.md) evaluated at $x$, $\frac{1}{2} x(1 - x)\, p''(x)$, equals the neutral [backward generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) $L_0$ applied to $y \mapsto p(y)$ at $x$.
