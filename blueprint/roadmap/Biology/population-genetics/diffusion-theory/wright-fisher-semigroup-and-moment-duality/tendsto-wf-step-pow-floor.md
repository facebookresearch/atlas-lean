# Diffusion approximation of the Wright–Fisher transition operators

For every real $t$ and every continuous function $f$ on $[0, 1]$, $W_M^{\lfloor tM \rfloor} f \to T_t f$ uniformly on $[0, 1]$ as the natural number $M$ tends to infinity, where $W_M$ is the [Wright–Fisher generation operator](wf-step.md) and $T_t$ the [Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup](wf-semigroup.md): $\lfloor tM \rfloor$ generations of the neutral chain with $M$ gene copies converge to the diffusion, a concrete form of the Trotter–Kato theorem.

## Sources

- S. N. Ethier and T. G. Kurtz, *Markov Processes: Characterization and Convergence*, Wiley (1986), ch. 1, §6 (approximation theorems), and ch. 10.
