# Cauchy property of uniform contractions extends from a dense set

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $L_M$ ($M \in \mathbb{N}$) continuous linear operators on $E$ with $\|L_M\| \le 1$ for every $M$, and $D$ a dense subset of $E$ such that $(L_M d)_M$ is a Cauchy sequence for every $d \in D$. Then $(L_M f)_M$ is a Cauchy sequence for every $f \in E$.
