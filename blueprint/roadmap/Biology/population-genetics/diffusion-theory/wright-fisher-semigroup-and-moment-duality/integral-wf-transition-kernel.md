# Wright–Fisher transition kernel represents the semigroup

For every real $t$, every continuous function $f$ on $[0, 1]$ and every $x \in [0, 1]$,

$$\int f(y)\, K_t(x, dy) = (T_t f)(x),$$

where $K_t$ is the [Wright–Fisher transition kernel](wf-transition-kernel.md) and $T_t$ the [Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup](wf-semigroup.md).
