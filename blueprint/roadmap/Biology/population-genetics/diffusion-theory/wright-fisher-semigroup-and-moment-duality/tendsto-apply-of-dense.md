# Limits of uniform contractions extend from a dense set

Let $E$ be a real normed space, $(T_i)$ a family of continuous linear operators on $E$ indexed along a filter, with $\|T_i\| \le 1$ for every $i$, and $S$ a continuous linear operator with $\|S\| \le 1$. If $D$ is a dense subset of $E$ and $T_i d \to S d$ along the filter for every $d \in D$, then $T_i f \to S f$ along the filter for every $f \in E$.
