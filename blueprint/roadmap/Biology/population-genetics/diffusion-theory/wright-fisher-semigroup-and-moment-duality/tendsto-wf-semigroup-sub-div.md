# Generator of the Wright–Fisher diffusion on polynomials

For every real polynomial $p$, as $t \to 0$ with $t > 0$,

$$\frac{T_t\, p - p}{t} \to \frac{1}{2} x(1 - x)\, p''(x)$$

uniformly on $[0, 1]$, where $T_t$ is the [Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup](wf-semigroup.md).

- S. N. Ethier and T. G. Kurtz, *Markov Processes: Characterization and Convergence*, Wiley (1986), ch. 10.
- R. Durrett, *Probability Models for DNA Sequence Evolution*, 2nd ed., Springer (2008), ch. 7.
