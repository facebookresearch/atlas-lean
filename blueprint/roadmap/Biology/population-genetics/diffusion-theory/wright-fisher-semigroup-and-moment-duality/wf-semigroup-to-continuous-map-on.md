# Diffusion semigroup agrees with the dual semigroup on polynomials

For every real $t \ge 0$ and every real polynomial $p$, the [Wright–Fisher diffusion semigroup](wf-semigroup.md) maps $p$, restricted to $[0, 1]$, to the restriction of $Q_t\, p$, where $Q_t$ is the [dual semigroup](poly-semigroup.md).
