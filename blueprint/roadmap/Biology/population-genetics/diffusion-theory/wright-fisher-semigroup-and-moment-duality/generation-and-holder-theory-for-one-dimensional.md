# Generation and Hölder theory for Kimura operators

This page covers a research programme around the monograph of Epstein and Mazzeo:

- generation of a strongly continuous semigroup on $C[0, 1]$ by $x(1 - x) \frac{d^2}{dx^2} + b \frac{d}{dx}$ with smooth inward-pointing $b$;
- Hölder a priori estimates in the Wright–Fisher Hölder spaces;
- the identification of the generator domain at $b = 0$ with [the Feller domain of the neutral generator](explicit-feller-domain-of-the-neutral-wf-generat.md), through the factor-$2$ time change of [Kimura operators with affine drift](affine-drift-one-dimensional-kimura-operator-on.md);
- non-affine drifts, and generalized Kimura operators on manifolds with corners.

Each statement fixes the operator class, the function spaces and the boundary hypotheses from the monograph.
