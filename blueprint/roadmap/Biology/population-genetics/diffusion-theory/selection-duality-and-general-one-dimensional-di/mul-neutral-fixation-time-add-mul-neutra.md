# Neutral mean absorption time from the conditional times

For every $p \in (0, 1)$,

$$p\, \bar t_1(p) + (1 - p)\, \bar t_0(p) = -2\bigl(p \log p + (1 - p)\log(1 - p)\bigr),$$

the unconditional [mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/mean-absorption-time.md), where $\bar t_1$ and $\bar t_0$ are the neutral conditional times to [fixation](neutral-fixation-time.md) and to [loss](neutral-loss-time.md).
