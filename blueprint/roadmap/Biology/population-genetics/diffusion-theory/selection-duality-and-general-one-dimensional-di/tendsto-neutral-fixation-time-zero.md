# Neutral conditional fixation time of a new mutation

The [neutral conditional fixation time](neutral-fixation-time.md) satisfies $\bar t_1(p) \to 2$ as $p \to 0^+$: a new neutral mutation that is destined to fix takes on average $2$ time units, that is, $4N$ generations, to do so.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
