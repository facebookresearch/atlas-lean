# Global balance of the ancestral selection graph chain

For every real $\sigma$ and every natural number $k$, the [stationary weights](asg-stationary.md) satisfy the balance equation at state $k + 1$:

$$\pi^\sigma_{k+1}\Bigl((k + 1)\sigma + \binom{k + 1}{2}\Bigr) = \pi^\sigma_k\, k\sigma + \pi^\sigma_{k+2} \binom{k + 2}{2}.$$

For $k = 0$ the inflow from the unreachable state $0$ vanishes.
