# Backward equation for the neutral conditional fixation time

For every $p \in (0, 1)$, the [neutral conditional fixation time](neutral-fixation-time.md) satisfies the conditioned backward equation $\bigl(L_0\, (q \mapsto q\, \bar t_1(q))\bigr)(p) = -p$, with the neutral [backward generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) $L_0$.
