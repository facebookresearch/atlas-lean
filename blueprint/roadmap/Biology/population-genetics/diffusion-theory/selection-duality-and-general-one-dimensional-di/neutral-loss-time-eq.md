# Neutral loss time equals fixation time at $1 - p$

For every real $p$, the [neutral conditional loss time](neutral-loss-time.md) and the [neutral conditional fixation time](neutral-fixation-time.md) satisfy $\bar t_0(p) = \bar t_1(1 - p)$: loss of an allele at frequency $p$ is fixation of the other allele at frequency $1 - p$.
