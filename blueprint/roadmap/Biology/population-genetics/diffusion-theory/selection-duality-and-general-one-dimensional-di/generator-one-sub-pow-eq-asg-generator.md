# Selection duality in operator form

For every real $\sigma$, every natural number $n$ and every real $x$, the [Wright–Fisher generator with genic selection](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) applied to $y \mapsto (1 - y)^n$ at $x$ equals the [ancestral selection graph generator](asg-generator.md) $A_\sigma$ applied to $k \mapsto (1 - x)^k$ at $n$.
