# The scale density of a regular diffusion is C¹ on (0, 1)

For every [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) with drift $b$ and variance $a$, the
[scale density](scale-density.md) $\psi$ is continuously differentiable on $(0, 1)$: there
$\psi' = -(2b/a)\,\psi$, and $b/a$ is continuous on $(0, 1)$.
