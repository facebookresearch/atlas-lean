# The scale density of a regular diffusion is C¹ on (0, 1)

For every [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) ($a$ positive and continuous on $(0, 1)$,
$b/a$ continuous on $(0, 1)$ and integrable on $[0, 1]$; accessibility of the endpoints is not part
of this) with drift $b$ and variance $a$, the [scale density](scale-density.md) $\psi$ is
continuously differentiable on $(0, 1)$: there $\psi' = -(2b/a)\,\psi$, and $b/a$ is continuous on
$(0, 1)$.
