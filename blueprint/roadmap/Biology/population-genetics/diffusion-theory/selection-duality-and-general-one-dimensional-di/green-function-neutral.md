# General Green's function agrees with the neutral Wright–Fisher one

For every real $p$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [Green's function](green-function.md) of the diffusion with drift $b = 0$ and variance $a(x) = x(1 - x)$ is the [neutral Green's function](../sojourn-times-of-the-wright-fisher-diffusion/neutral-green.md) $G(p, x)$.
