# The scale-function ratio is continuous up to the boundary

For every [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) ($a$ positive and continuous on $(0, 1)$,
$b/a$ continuous on $(0, 1)$ and integrable on $[0, 1]$; accessibility of the endpoints is not part
of this) with drift $b$ and variance $a$, the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md) $u = S/S(1)$
is continuous on the closed interval $[0, 1]$.
