# Standing regularity hypotheses for a diffusion on $(0, 1)$

Real functions $b$ (drift) and $a$ (infinitesimal variance) define a regular diffusion on $(0, 1)$ if $a$ is positive and continuous on $(0, 1)$, and $b/a$ is continuous on $(0, 1)$ and integrable on $[0, 1]$, so that the [scale function](scale-function.md) is finite up to both boundaries. The values of $b$ and $a$ at $0$ and $1$ are irrelevant. These hypotheses are not Feller's classification of regular boundaries: they do not imply that either endpoint is accessible.
