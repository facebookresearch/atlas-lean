# Wright–Fisher generator with genic selection as a general diffusion

For every real $\sigma$ and every real function $u$, the [backward generator](diffusion-generator.md) with drift $b(x) = \sigma x(1 - x)$ and variance $a(x) = x(1 - x)$ is the [Wright–Fisher generator with genic selection](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md): $L_{b,a} u = L_\sigma u$.
