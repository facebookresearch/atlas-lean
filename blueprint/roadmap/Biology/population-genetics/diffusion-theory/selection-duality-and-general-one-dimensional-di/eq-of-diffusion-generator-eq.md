# Uniqueness for the Dirichlet problem

Let $b$ and $a$ define a [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md), and let $u$ and $v$ be continuous on $[0, 1]$, differentiable on $(0, 1)$ with derivatives differentiable on $(0, 1)$, such that $(L_{b,a} u)(x) = (L_{b,a} v)(x)$ for every $x \in (0, 1)$, $u(0) = v(0)$ and $u(1) = v(1)$. Then $u = v$ on $[0, 1]$. With [Green's representation](diffusion-generator-integral-green-funct.md), this identifies the Green integral as the solution of $L v = -g$, $v(0) = v(1) = 0$, whenever it has this regularity.
