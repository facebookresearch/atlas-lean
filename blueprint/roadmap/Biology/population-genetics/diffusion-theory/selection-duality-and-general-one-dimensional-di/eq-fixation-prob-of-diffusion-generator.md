# Uniqueness of the scale-function ratio

Let $b$ and $a$ define a [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md), and let $u$ be continuous on $[0, 1]$, differentiable on $(0, 1)$ with derivative differentiable on $(0, 1)$, with $(L_{b,a} u)(x) = 0$ for every $x \in (0, 1)$, $u(0) = 0$ and $u(1) = 1$. Then $u(x) = S(x)/S(1)$, the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md), for every $x \in [0, 1]$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
