# Sojourn density conditioned on fixation

For real functions $b$ and $a$ and real numbers $p$ and $x$, let

$$G^*(p, x) = \frac{G(p, x)\, u(x)}{u(p)},$$

with the [Green's function](green-function.md) $G$, the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md) $u$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$ (so $G^*(0, x) = 0$). For a [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) whose boundaries are both accessible, $0 < p \le 1$ and $0 < x < 1$, it is the sojourn density conditioned on fixation, the Doob $h$-transform of $G$ by $u$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
- W. J. Ewens, *Mathematical Population Genetics I: Theoretical Introduction*, 2nd ed., Springer (2004).
