# The scale-function ratio solves the Dirichlet problem

For every [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) with drift $b$ and variance $a$, the
[scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md) $u = S/S(1)$ is continuous on $[0, 1]$, twice
continuously differentiable on $(0, 1)$, and solves the Dirichlet problem

$$\frac{a(x)}{2}\, u''(x) + b(x)\, u'(x) = 0 \quad (0 < x < 1), \qquad u(0) = 0, \quad u(1) = 1.$$
