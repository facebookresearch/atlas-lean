# Solutions of the homogeneous backward equation

Let $b$ and $a$ define a [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md), and let $u$ be continuous on $[0, 1]$, differentiable on $(0, 1)$ with derivative differentiable on $(0, 1)$, and such that $(L_{b,a} u)(x) = 0$ for every $x \in (0, 1)$, with the [backward generator](diffusion-generator.md). Then $u(x) = u(0) + (u(1) - u(0))\, S(x)/S(1)$ for every $x \in [0, 1]$, where $S$ is the [scale function](scale-function.md).
