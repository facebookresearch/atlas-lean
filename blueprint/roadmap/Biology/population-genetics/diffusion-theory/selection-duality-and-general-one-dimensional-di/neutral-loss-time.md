# Neutral conditional mean time to loss

For a real number $p$, let $\bar t_0(p) = -\frac{2p\log p}{1 - p}$, with the same conventions for division by $0$ and the logarithm. For $p \in (0, 1)$ it is the mean time to loss of a neutral allele at frequency $p$, conditioned on loss, in units of $2N$ generations.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
