# Scale function is positive at one

For every [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) with drift $b$ and variance $a$, the [scale function](scale-function.md) satisfies $S(1) > 0$, so the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md) has a positive denominator, and so does the [Green's function](green-function.md) $G(p, x)$ at every $x \in (0, 1)$ (at a boundary, $a$ may vanish, as for the neutral Wright–Fisher variance $a(x) = x(1 - x)$).
