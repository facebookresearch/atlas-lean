# Loss probability from the stationary ancestral selection graph

For every real $\sigma > 0$ and every real $x$, the [stationary weights](asg-stationary.md) satisfy

$$\sum_{k \ge 0} \pi^\sigma_k\, (1 - x)^k = 1 - u_\sigma(x),$$

where $u_\sigma$ is [Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md). In the sampling reading, the loss probability of the favoured allele at frequency $x$ is the probability that all lines of the stationary ancestral selection graph carry the unfavoured allele.

## Sources

- S. Mano, *Duality, ancestral and diffusion processes in models with selection*, Theoret. Population Biol. 75 (2009), 164–175.
