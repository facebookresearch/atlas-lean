# Neutral conditional mean time to fixation

For a real number $p$, let $\bar t_1(p) = -\frac{2(1 - p)\log(1 - p)}{p}$, with the conventions that division by $0$ gives $0$, $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log u = \log|u|$ for $u < 0$. For $p \in (0, 1)$ it is the mean time to fixation of a neutral allele at frequency $p$, conditioned on fixation, in units of $2N$ generations; its limit as $p \to 0^+$ is $2$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
