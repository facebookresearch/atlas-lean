# Neutral conditional fixation time as a sojourn integral

For every $p \in (0, 1)$,

$$\int_0^1 G(p, x)\, \frac{x}{p}\, dx = -\frac{2(1 - p)\log(1 - p)}{p} = \bar t_1(p),$$

where $G$ is the [neutral Green's function](../sojourn-times-of-the-wright-fisher-diffusion/neutral-green.md), $u(x) = x$ the neutral fixation probability and $\bar t_1$ the [neutral conditional fixation time](neutral-fixation-time.md).
