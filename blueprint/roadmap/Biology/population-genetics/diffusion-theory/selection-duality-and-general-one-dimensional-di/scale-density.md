# Scale density of a one-dimensional diffusion

For real functions $b$ and $a$ and a real number $y$, the scale density is

$$\psi(y) = \exp\Bigl(-\int_0^y \frac{2b(z)}{a(z)}\, dz\Bigr),$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$ and the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$.
