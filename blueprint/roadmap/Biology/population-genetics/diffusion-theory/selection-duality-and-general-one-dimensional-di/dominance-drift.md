# Drift of an allele under selection with dominance

For real numbers $c$, $h$ and $x$, the drift $2c\, x(1 - x)\bigl(h + (1 - 2h)x\bigr)$ of an allele at frequency $x$ with genotype fitnesses $1$, $1 + hs$ and $1 + s$ for zero, one and two copies, where $c = Ns$ and time is in units of $2N$ generations. For $h = 1/2$ it is genic selection with $\sigma = c$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
