# Neutral conditional loss time as a sojourn integral

For every $p \in (0, 1)$,

$$\int_0^1 G(p, x)\, \frac{1 - x}{1 - p}\, dx = -\frac{2p\log p}{1 - p} = \bar t_0(p),$$

where $G$ is the [neutral Green's function](../sojourn-times-of-the-wright-fisher-diffusion/neutral-green.md) and $\bar t_0$ the [neutral conditional loss time](neutral-loss-time.md).
