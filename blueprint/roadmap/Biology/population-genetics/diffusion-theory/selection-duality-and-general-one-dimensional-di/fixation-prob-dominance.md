# Scale-function ratio with dominance

For all real $c$ and $h$ and every $p \in [0, 1]$, with $D = 2h - 1$, the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md) of the diffusion with the [drift with dominance](dominance-drift.md) and variance $x(1 - x)$ is

$$u(p) = \frac{\int_0^p e^{-2cDx(1 - x) - 2cx}\, dx}{\int_0^1 e^{-2cDx(1 - x) - 2cx}\, dx}.$$

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
