# Conditional sojourn time of the neutral diffusion

For every $p \in (0, 1)$, the total [conditional sojourn time](conditional-green.md) of the diffusion with drift $b = 0$ and variance $a(x) = x(1 - x)$ is

$$\int_0^1 \frac{G(p, x)\, u(x)}{u(p)}\, dx = -\frac{2(1 - p)\log(1 - p)}{p},$$

the [neutral conditional fixation time](neutral-fixation-time.md) $\bar t_1(p)$ of Kimura and Ohta.
