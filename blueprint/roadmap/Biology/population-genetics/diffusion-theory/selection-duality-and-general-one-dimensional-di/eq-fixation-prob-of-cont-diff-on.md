# Uniqueness of the scale-function ratio among C² solutions

Let $b$ and $a$ define a [regular diffusion](is-regular-diffusion.md) ($a$ positive and continuous
on $(0, 1)$, $b/a$ continuous on $(0, 1)$ and integrable on $[0, 1]$; accessibility of the endpoints
is not part of this), and let $u$ be continuous on $[0, 1]$ and twice continuously differentiable on
$(0, 1)$, with $(L_{b,a} u)(x) = 0$ for every $x \in (0, 1)$, where $L_{b,a}$ is the
[backward generator](diffusion-generator.md), $u(0) = 0$ and $u(1) = 1$. Then $u(x) = S(x)/S(1)$,
the [scale-function ratio](fixation-prob.md), for every $x \in [0, 1]$. This is a special case of
the [uniqueness of the scale-function ratio](eq-fixation-prob-of-diffusion-generator.md), which only
asks $u$ and $u'$ to be differentiable on $(0, 1)$.
