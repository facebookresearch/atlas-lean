# Type of the common ancestor under selection and mutation

This node covers the type of the common ancestor in a two-type Wright–Fisher diffusion with directional
selection and two-way mutation. Given today's frequency $x$ of the beneficial type, let $h(x)$ be the
probability that the individual whose progeny eventually takes over the population is of the beneficial
type. Fearnhead (2002) and Taylor (2007) obtained a series representation of $h(x)$; Lenz, Kluth, Baake
and Wakolbinger (2015) construct a pruned lookdown version of the ancestral selection graph that gives
the coefficients of this series a probabilistic meaning, and Baake, Lenz and Wakolbinger (2016) extend
the representation, in terms of the equilibrium tail probabilities of the line-counting process of the
graph, to $\Lambda$-Wright–Fisher populations.

## Expansion

A sound expansion defines the pruned lookdown ancestral selection graph and its line-counting process,
proves the properties of its equilibrium that the representation needs, and then states the series
representation of $h(x)$ exactly as in the source.
