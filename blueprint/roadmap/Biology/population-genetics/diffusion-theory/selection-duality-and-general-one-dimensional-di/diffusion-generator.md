# Backward generator of a one-dimensional diffusion

For real functions $b$, $a$ and $u$ and a real number $x$, let

$$(L_{b,a} u)(x) = \frac{a(x)}{2}\, u''(x) + b(x)\, u'(x),$$

where a derivative that does not exist is taken to be $0$. When $a \ge 0$ is the infinitesimal variance and $b$ the drift of a one-dimensional diffusion and $u$ is twice differentiable, it is the backward generator of that diffusion applied to $u$.
