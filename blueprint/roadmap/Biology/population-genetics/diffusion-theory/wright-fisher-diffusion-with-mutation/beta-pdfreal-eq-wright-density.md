# Beta density as Wright's stationary density

For all real $\theta_1$, $\theta_2$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the Beta density formula $\beta_{\theta_1,\theta_2}(x) = x^{\theta_1 - 1}(1 - x)^{\theta_2 - 1}/B(\theta_1, \theta_2)$, with $B(\theta_1, \theta_2) = \Gamma(\theta_1)\Gamma(\theta_2)/\Gamma(\theta_1 + \theta_2)$ and the convention $1/0 = 0$, equals $\pi_{\theta_1/2,\, \theta_2/2}(x)/B(\theta_1, \theta_2)$, where $\pi$ is [Wright's unnormalized density](../wright-fisher-diffusion/wright-density.md) with the scaled rates $\alpha = \theta_1/2$ and $\beta = \theta_2/2$.

For $\theta_1, \theta_2 > 0$, $\beta_{\theta_1,\theta_2}$ is the density of the Beta distribution with parameters $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$.
