# Uniqueness for the selection–mutation martingale problem by an inverse tilt

Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\theta_1, \theta_2 \ge 0$. If $Y$ solves the martingale problem for $\mathcal{L}_{\alpha, \theta}$, on any filtered space and with any initial law, then tilting by $1/g = e^{-\alpha x}$, by [the martingale problem under an exponential tilt](../../../../Probability/markov-chains/martingale-problems/martingale-problem-under-exponential-tilt-generi.md), gives a solution of the martingale problem with mutation only, whose law is unique by [its well-posedness](../neutral-wright-fisher-martingale-problem/well-posedness-of-the-mutation-martingale-proble.md). Hence the law of $Y$ is the law constructed by [the exponential tilt](existence-of-the-selection-mutation-wf-diffusion.md).

This joint well-posedness does not follow from the separate results for selection only, [the uniqueness via an inverse change of measure](../wright-fisher-diffusion-with-selection-and-mutat/uniqueness-for-the-selection-martingale-problem.md), and for mutation only.
