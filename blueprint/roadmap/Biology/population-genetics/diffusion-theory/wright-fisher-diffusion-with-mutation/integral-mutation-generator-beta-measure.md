# Weak stationarity of Wright's law

For all real $\theta_1 > 0$ and $\theta_2 > 0$ and every function $f$ twice continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$, the integral of $L_{\theta_1,\theta_2} f$ with respect to the Beta distribution with parameters $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ is $0$, where $L_{\theta_1,\theta_2}$ is the [mutation generator](mutation-generator.md): Wright's law is stationary for the diffusion with mutation.

## Sources

- S. Wright, *Evolution in Mendelian populations*, Genetics 16 (1931), 97–159.
- W. J. Ewens, *Mathematical Population Genetics I: Theoretical Introduction*, 2nd ed., Springer (2004).
