# Integrability of the mutation generator against the Beta density

For all real $\theta_1 > 0$ and $\theta_2 > 0$ and every function $f$ twice continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$, the function $x \mapsto (L_{\theta_1,\theta_2} f)(x)\, \beta_{\theta_1,\theta_2}(x)$ is integrable on $[0, 1]$, where $L_{\theta_1,\theta_2}$ is the [mutation generator](mutation-generator.md) and $\beta_{\theta_1,\theta_2}$ the Beta density.
