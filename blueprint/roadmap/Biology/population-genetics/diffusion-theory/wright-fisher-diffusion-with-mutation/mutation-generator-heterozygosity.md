# Mutation generator applied to heterozygosity

For all real $\theta_1$, $\theta_2$ and $x$, the [mutation generator](mutation-generator.md) applied to the heterozygosity $H(y) = 2y(1 - y)$ is

$$(L_{\theta_1,\theta_2} H)(x) = -2x(1 - x) + \bigl(\theta_1(1 - x) - \theta_2 x\bigr)(1 - 2x):$$

the first term, $-H(x)$, is the decay of heterozygosity by drift at rate $1$, and the second is the contribution of mutation, which is nonnegative under symmetric mutation $\theta_1 = \theta_2 \ge 0$ but can be negative otherwise.
