# Integrability of the mutation generator under Wright's stationary law

For all real $\theta_1 > 0$ and $\theta_2 > 0$ and every function $f$ twice continuously differentiable on $[0, 1]$, $L_{\theta_1,\theta_2} f$ is integrable with respect to the Beta distribution with parameters $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$, where $L_{\theta_1,\theta_2}$ is the [mutation generator](mutation-generator.md).
