# Haldane's 2s only in the joint regime, closed-form version

Let $u$ be a real function of two real variables with
$u(\sigma, x) = (1 - e^{-2\sigma x})/(1 - e^{-2\sigma})$ whenever $\sigma \ne 0$, for instance
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md), and let $(s_M)_{M \in \mathbb{N}}$ be real numbers. If
$u(M s_M, 1/M)/(2 s_M) \to 1$ as $M \to \infty$, a quotient by $0$ being read as $0$, then
$s_M \to 0$ and $M s_M \to \infty$.
