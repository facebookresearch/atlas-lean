# Fixation probability in a population of changing size

This node covers the probability of fixation of a selected allele in a population whose size changes in
time. Otto and Whitlock (1997) calculate the fixation probability of beneficial mutations for a
population whose size changes once, one growing or declining exponentially or logistically, and one that
fluctuates; their results are based on a branching process model and are shown to be approximate
solutions of the diffusion equation that describes the change of the fixation probability over time, and
the diffusion equation also gives the fixation probability of deleterious alleles in such populations.

## Expansion

A sound expansion constructs the time-inhomogeneous Wright–Fisher diffusion for a given size history,
characterizes the fixation probability as the solution of its backward equation under explicit
conditions on the size history, and states the constant-size and single-size-change cases separately.

## Sources

- S. P. Otto and M. C. Whitlock, "The probability of fixation in populations of changing size",
  Genetics 146 (1997), 723–733, <https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/146.2.723>.
