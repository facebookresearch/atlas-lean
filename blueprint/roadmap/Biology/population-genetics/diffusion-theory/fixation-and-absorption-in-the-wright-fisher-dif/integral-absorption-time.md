# Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time, process form

Let $x$ be real, $\mu$ a probability measure and $(X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ a [solution of the neutral Wright–Fisher martingale problem](is-wright-fisher-solution.md) ($\sigma = 0$) under $\mu$, with $X_0 = x$ almost surely. Then the [absorption time](absorption-time.md) $\tau$ is integrable and

$$\mathbb{E}_\mu[\tau] = -2\bigl(x \log x + (1 - x)\log(1 - x)\bigr),$$

the [Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/mean-absorption-time.md), in units of $2N$ generations.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
- W. J. Ewens, *Mathematical Population Genetics I: Theoretical Introduction*, 2nd ed., Springer (2004).
