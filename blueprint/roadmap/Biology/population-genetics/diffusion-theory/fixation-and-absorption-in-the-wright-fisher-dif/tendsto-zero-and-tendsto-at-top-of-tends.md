# Haldane's 2s approximation holds only in the joint regime

Let $(s_M)_{M \in \mathbb{N}}$ be real numbers such that
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md) satisfies $u_{M s_M}(1/M)/(2 s_M) \to 1$ as $M \to \infty$, a
quotient by $0$ being read as $0$. Then $s_M \to 0$ and $M s_M \to \infty$.
