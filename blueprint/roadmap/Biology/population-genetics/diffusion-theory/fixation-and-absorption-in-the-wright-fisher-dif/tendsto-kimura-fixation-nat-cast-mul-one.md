# At fixed s > 0 the new-mutant limit is 1 − exp(−2s) < 2s

For every real $s > 0$, [Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md) satisfies
$u_{Ms}(1/M) \to 1 - e^{-2s}$ as $M \to \infty$ through the natural numbers, and
$1 - e^{-2s} < 2s$. So at a fixed selection coefficient the fixation probability of a single new
copy among $M$ gene copies does not approach Haldane's $2s$.
