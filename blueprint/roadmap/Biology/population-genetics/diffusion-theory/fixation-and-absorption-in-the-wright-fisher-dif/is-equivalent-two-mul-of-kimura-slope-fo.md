# The function 2σ/(1 − exp(−2σ)) is asymptotic to 2σ as σ → ∞

Let $K$ be a real function with $K(\sigma) = 2\sigma/(1 - e^{-2\sigma})$ for every
$\sigma \ne 0$, for instance the [slope constant](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-slope.md) of Kimura's formula. Then $K(\alpha) \sim 2\alpha$ as
$\alpha \to \infty$, that is $K(\alpha) - 2\alpha = o(\alpha)$.
