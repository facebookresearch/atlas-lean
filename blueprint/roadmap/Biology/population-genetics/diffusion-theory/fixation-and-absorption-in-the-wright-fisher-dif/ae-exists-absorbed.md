# Absorption in finite time

Let $\sigma$ be real, $\mu$ a probability measure and $(X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ a [solution of the Wright–Fisher martingale problem](is-wright-fisher-solution.md) with selection coefficient $\sigma$ under $\mu$. Then for $\mu$-almost every $\omega$ there is a time $t_0 \ge 0$ such that $X_t(\omega) = 0$ for every $t \ge t_0$, or $X_t(\omega) = 1$ for every $t \ge t_0$.
