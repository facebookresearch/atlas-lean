# Haldane's 2s approximation holds in the joint regime

Let $(s_M)_{M \in \mathbb{N}}$ be real numbers with $s_M \to 0$ and $M s_M \to \infty$. Then
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md) satisfies

$$\frac{u_{M s_M}(1/M)}{2 s_M} \to 1 \quad (M \to \infty):$$

a single new copy among $M$ gene copies, with scaled selection coefficient $M s_M$, is fixed with
probability $2 s_M (1 + o(1))$.
