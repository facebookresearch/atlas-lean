# At fixed s > 0 the limit is 1 − exp(−2s) < 2s, closed-form version

Let $u$ be a real function of two real variables with
$u(\sigma, x) = (1 - e^{-2\sigma x})/(1 - e^{-2\sigma})$ whenever $\sigma \ne 0$, for instance
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md). For every real $s > 0$, $u(Ms, 1/M) \to 1 - e^{-2s}$ as
$M \to \infty$ through the natural numbers, and $1 - e^{-2s} < 2s$: at a fixed selection
coefficient, the fixation probability of a single new copy among $M$ gene copies with scaled
selection coefficient $Ms$ does not approach Haldane's $2s$.
