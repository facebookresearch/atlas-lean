# Generalized Kimura diffusions on manifolds with corners

Degenerate elliptic operators that generalize the Wright–Fisher (Kimura) diffusion generator on the simplex: on a compact manifold with corners, the second-order part vanishes to first order in the normal direction at each boundary face and the drift points inward. Anisotropic WF-Hölder spaces give sharp existence, uniqueness and regularity for the associated heat and resolvent equations, and the boundary stratification relative to the operator determines its nullspace and stationary measures.
