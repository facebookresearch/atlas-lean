# Kimura's fixation probability

For real numbers $\sigma$ and $x$, let

$$u_\sigma(x) = \frac{1 - e^{-2\sigma x}}{1 - e^{-2\sigma}} \quad \text{if } \sigma \ne 0, \qquad u_0(x) = x.$$

For $x \in [0, 1]$ it is Kimura's probability of fixation of an allele at initial frequency $x$ with scaled selection coefficient $\sigma = 2Ns$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
