# The neutral mean absorption time solves the backward equation

For every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [neutral mean absorption time](mean-absorption-time.md) satisfies $(L_0 T)(x) = -1$, with the neutral [backward generator](generator.md) $L_0$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
