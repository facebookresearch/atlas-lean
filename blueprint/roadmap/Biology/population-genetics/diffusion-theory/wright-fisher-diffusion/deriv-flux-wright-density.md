# Wright's stationary density solves the zero-flux equation

For all real $\alpha$, $\beta$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, with the infinitesimal variance $a(x) = x(1 - x)$ and the mutation drift $b(x) = \alpha(1 - x) - \beta x$, [Wright's density](wright-density.md) $\pi = \pi_{\alpha,\beta}$ satisfies

$$\frac{d}{dx}\bigl(a(x)\,\pi(x)\bigr) = 2\, b(x)\, \pi(x),$$

the stationary forward equation $\frac{1}{2}(a\pi)' - b\pi = 0$ with zero probability flux.
