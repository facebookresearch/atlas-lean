# Wright's stationary density of the Wright–Fisher diffusion

For real numbers $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $x$, let $\pi_{\alpha,\beta}(x) = x^{2\alpha - 1}(1 - x)^{2\beta - 1}$, with real powers, where $0^0 = 1$, $0^y = 0$ for $y \ne 0$, and $x^y = |x|^y \cos(\pi y)$ for $x < 0$. For $\alpha, \beta > 0$ and $x \in (0, 1)$ it is Wright's unnormalized stationary density of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with scaled mutation rates $\alpha$ towards the allele and $\beta$ away from it; for $\alpha = \beta = 0$ it is $1/(x(1 - x))$, which is not integrable on $(0, 1)$.

## Sources

- S. Wright, *Evolution in Mendelian populations*, Genetics 16 (1931), 97–159.
