# Kimura's fixation probability is strictly increasing

For every real $\sigma$, [Kimura's formula](kimura-fixation.md) $x \mapsto u_\sigma(x)$ is strictly
increasing on $\mathbb{R}$. Only its values for $x \in [0, 1]$ are fixation probabilities: since
$u_\sigma(1) = 1$, $u_\sigma(x) > 1$ for $x > 1$.
