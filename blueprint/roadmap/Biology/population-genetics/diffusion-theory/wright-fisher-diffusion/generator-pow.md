# Moment duality with Kingman's coalescent

For every natural number $n$ and every real $x$, the neutral [backward generator](generator.md) maps $y \mapsto y^n$ to

$$\binom{n}{2}\bigl(x^{n-1} - x^n\bigr),$$

where $n - 1$ is read as $0$ when $n = 0$. This is the generator of the block-counting process, with jumps from $n$ to $n - 1$ at rate $\binom{n}{2}$, applied to $n \mapsto x^n$.
