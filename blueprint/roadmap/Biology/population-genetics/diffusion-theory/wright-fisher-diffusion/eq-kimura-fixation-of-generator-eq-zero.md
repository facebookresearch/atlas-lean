# Uniqueness of the fixation probability

Let $\sigma$ be real and $u$ a real function that is continuous on $[0, 1]$, differentiable on $(0, 1)$, with derivative $u'$ differentiable on $(0, 1)$, such that $(L_\sigma u)(x) = 0$ for every $x \in (0, 1)$, $u(0) = 0$ and $u(1) = 1$, where $L_\sigma$ is the [backward generator](generator.md). Then $u(x) = u_\sigma(x)$, [Kimura's fixation probability](kimura-fixation.md), for every $x \in [0, 1]$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
