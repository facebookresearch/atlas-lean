# Kimura–Ohta mean absorption time of a neutral allele

For a real number $x$, let

$$T(x) = -2\bigl(x \log x + (1 - x) \log(1 - x)\bigr),$$

with the conventions $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log u = \log|u|$ for $u < 0$. For $x \in [0, 1]$ it is the mean time to absorption, by loss or fixation, of a neutral allele at frequency $x$, in units of $2N$ generations; in generations it is $-4N(x \log x + (1 - x)\log(1 - x))$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The average number of generations until fixation of a mutant gene in a finite population*, Genetics 61 (1969), 763–771.
