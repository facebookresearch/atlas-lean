# Heterozygosity decay in one Wright–Fisher generation

Let $M \ne 0$ be a natural number and $x \in [0, 1]$. If the number $X$ of copies of an allele among $M$ gene copies has the binomial distribution $\mathrm{Bin}(M, x)$, the new frequency $p' = X/M$ satisfies

$$\mathbb{E}\bigl[2p'(1 - p')\bigr] = \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{M}\Bigr) 2x(1 - x).$$
