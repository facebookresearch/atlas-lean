# Backward generator of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with selection

For a real number $\sigma$, a real function $u$ and a real number $x$, let

$$(L_\sigma u)(x) = \frac{x(1 - x)}{2}\, u''(x) + \sigma\, x(1 - x)\, u'(x),$$

where a derivative that does not exist is taken to be $0$. For $x \in [0, 1]$ and twice-differentiable $u$, it is the backward generator of the Wright–Fisher diffusion with genic selection, applied to $u$, with time in units of $2N$ generations and scaled selection coefficient $\sigma = 2Ns$.
