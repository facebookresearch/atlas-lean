# Loss of alleles and invariance of faces in the K-allele diffusion

This page covers a research target for the neutral $K$-allele Wright–Fisher diffusion on the simplex, without mutation into types that are absent. The target is a process-level statement:

- each face of the simplex, on which a given set of alleles is absent, is invariant;
- the process restricted to a face is the diffusion with the remaining alleles;
- alleles are lost one at a time until one remains, with the [fixation probabilities and mean time to monomorphism](neutral-k-allele-fixation-probabilities-and-mean.md) of the neutral $K$-allele model.

## Expansion
