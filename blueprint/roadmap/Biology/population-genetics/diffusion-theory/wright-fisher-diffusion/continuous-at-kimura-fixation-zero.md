# Kimura's fixation probability is continuous in σ at σ = 0

For every real $x$, [Kimura's formula](kimura-fixation.md) $u_\sigma(x)$, as a function of the
scaled selection coefficient $\sigma$, is continuous at $\sigma = 0$: $u_\sigma(x) \to u_0(x) = x$
as $\sigma \to 0$. So the neutral value $x$ is the continuous extension at $\sigma = 0$ of the
quotient $\sigma \mapsto (1 - e^{-2\sigma x})/(1 - e^{-2\sigma})$ defined for $\sigma \ne 0$
(evaluating the quotient itself at $\sigma = 0$ would give $0/0$). The values are fixation
probabilities for $x \in [0, 1]$.
