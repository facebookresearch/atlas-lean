# Kimura's fixation probability is continuous in σ at σ = 0

For every real $x$, [Kimura's fixation probability](kimura-fixation.md) $u_\sigma(x)$, as a function
of the scaled selection coefficient $\sigma$, is continuous at $\sigma = 0$:
$u_\sigma(x) \to u_0(x) = x$ as $\sigma \to 0$. So the neutral value $x$ is the continuous extension
at $\sigma = 0$ of $\sigma \mapsto (1 - e^{-2\sigma x})/(1 - e^{-2\sigma})$.
