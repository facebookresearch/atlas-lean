# Kimura's fixation probability solves the backward equation

For all real $\sigma$ and $x$, [Kimura's fixation probability](kimura-fixation.md) solves the backward equation:

$$\frac{x(1 - x)}{2}\, u_\sigma''(x) + \sigma\, x(1 - x)\, u_\sigma'(x) = 0,$$

that is, $L_\sigma u_\sigma = 0$ everywhere, with the [backward generator](generator.md) $L_\sigma$.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962), 713–719.
