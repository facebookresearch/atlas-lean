# Excursion theory of the Wright–Fisher diffusion

With weak two-way mutation, $0<\theta_1,\theta_2<1$, both boundaries of the Wright-Fisher diffusion are regular, so allele frequency paths keep returning to fixation and loss. Splitting a path into excursions away from a boundary point, labelled by the boundary at which they end, gives Poisson point processes of killed paths with explicit hypergeometric excursion laws, and yields Hausdorff dimensions of the time sets spent at fixation.
