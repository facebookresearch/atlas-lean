# Earlier values of the realization are measurable at later times

For every `x ∈ [0, 1]` and all times `0 ≤ s ≤ t`, the value `X_s` of the
[continuous realization](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/modified-process.md)
`X` started at `x` is measurable with respect to `ℱ_t`, the σ-algebra at time `t` of its
[natural filtration](natural-filtration.md).
