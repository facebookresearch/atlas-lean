# Semigroup identity on polynomials

For every real polynomial `p` and every time `t ≥ 0`, the
[neutral Wright–Fisher semigroup](../wright-fisher-semigroup-and-moment-duality/wf-semigroup.md) `T`
satisfies

`T_t p - p = ∫₀ᵗ T_u(L p) du`

in the Banach space of continuous real functions on `[0, 1]` with the supremum norm, where `p` is
restricted to `[0, 1]` and `L p = x (1 - x) / 2 · p''` is the
[neutral generator on polynomials](../wright-fisher-semigroup-and-moment-duality/poly-generator.md).

## Sources

- S. N. Ethier and T. G. Kurtz, *Markov Processes: Characterization and Convergence*, Wiley (1986),
  ch. 1, Prop. 1.5 (b).
