# Natural filtration of the realization

For `x ∈ [0, 1]`, the filtration `(ℱ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` on the
[sample paths](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/sample-path.md) in which `ℱ_t` is
the σ-algebra generated by the values `X_s`, `0 ≤ s ≤ t`, of the
[continuous realization](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/modified-process.md)
started at `x`.
