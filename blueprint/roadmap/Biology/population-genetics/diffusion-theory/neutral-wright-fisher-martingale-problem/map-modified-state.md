# The one-time law of the realization is the neutral kernel

For every `x ∈ [0, 1]` and every time `t ≥ 0`, the law of `X_t` under the
[canonical path measure](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/raw-measure.md) `P_x`,
where `X` is the
[continuous realization](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/modified-process.md)
started at `x`, is `K_t(x, ·)`, with `K_t` the
[neutral Wright–Fisher kernel](../construction-of-the-neutral-wright-fisher-diffus/neutral-kernel.md),
a law on `[0, 1]` for the interval-valued state. Read as a real number, `X_t` has as law the image
of `K_t(x, ·)` under the inclusion `[0, 1] → ℝ`, which is a measurable embedding, so the two
statements carry the same information.
