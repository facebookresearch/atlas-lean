# Finite-dimensional laws of the realization

At any strictly increasing list of times
`τ`, the states `(X_{τ i})ᵢ` of the realization started at `x` have the iterated-kernel law
`finiteChainLaw neutralKernel x 0 τ`.

## Sources

- Kolmogorov extension (`map_ordered_pathMeasure`) restricted along the increasing reindexing of the image of `τ`, and the fixed-time modification property of the realization.
