# The speed density of a regular diffusion is positive

If `b`, `a` define a
[regular diffusion](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/is-regular-diffusion.md) on
`(0, 1)` (`a` positive and continuous on `(0, 1)`, `b / a` continuous on `(0, 1)` and integrable on
`[0, 1]`; accessibility of the endpoints and a probability normalization are not part of this), then
the [speed density](speed-density.md) satisfies `m(x) > 0` for every `x ∈ (0, 1)`: there
`m(x) = 2 / (a(x) ψ(x))` with `a(x) > 0` and the scale density `ψ(x) > 0`.
