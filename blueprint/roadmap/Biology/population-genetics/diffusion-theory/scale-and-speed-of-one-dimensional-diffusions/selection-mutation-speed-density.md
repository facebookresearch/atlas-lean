# Speed density with selection and mutation

For real numbers `α`, `θ₁`, `θ₂` and `x`, let

`m(x) = 2 e^(2αx) x ^ (θ₁ - 1) (1 - x) ^ (θ₂ - 1)`,

with real powers. For `x ∈ (0, 1)` it [equals](selection-mutation-speed-density-eq.md)
`2 / (x (1 - x) ψ(x))`, with the
[scale density with selection and mutation](selection-mutation-scale-density.md) `ψ`. Outside
`(0, 1)` the real powers follow the conventions `0 ^ 0 = 1`, `0 ^ t = 0` for `t ≠ 0` and
`s ^ t = |s| ^ t cos (π t)` for `s < 0`.
