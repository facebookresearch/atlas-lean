# Scale density with selection and mutation solves ψ' = −(2b/a)ψ

For all real `α`, `θ₁`, `θ₂` and every `x ∈ (0, 1)`, the
[scale density with selection and mutation](selection-mutation-scale-density.md) `ψ` is
differentiable at `x` with `ψ'(x) = -(2 b(x) / a(x)) ψ(x)`, where `a(x) = x (1 - x)` and
`b(x) = α x (1 - x) + (θ₁ (1 - x) - θ₂ x) / 2`.
