# Speed density with selection and mutation is 2/(aψ)

For all real `α`, `θ₁`, `θ₂` and every `x ∈ (0, 1)`, the
[speed density with selection and mutation](selection-mutation-speed-density.md) is
`m(x) = 2 / (x (1 - x) ψ(x))`, where `ψ` is the
[scale density with selection and mutation](selection-mutation-scale-density.md); the selection
factor of `m` is `e^(2αx)`.
