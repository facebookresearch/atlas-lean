# Canonical form of the generator from the scale-density equation

Let `b`, `a`, `ψ`, `f` be real functions and `x` a real number with `a(x) ≠ 0` and `ψ(x) ≠ 0`,
such that `ψ` is differentiable at `x` with `ψ'(x) = -(2 b(x) / a(x)) ψ(x)` and `f'` is
differentiable at `x`. Then the [backward generator](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/diffusion-generator.md) satisfies

`(L_{b,a} f)(x) = (a(x) ψ(x) / 2) (f' / ψ)'(x)`.

Here `f'` is the derivative of `f`, taken to be `0` where `f` is not differentiable, as in the
generator.
