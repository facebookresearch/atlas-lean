# Canonical form of the generator of a regular diffusion

Let `b`, `a` define a [regular diffusion](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/is-regular-diffusion.md) on `(0, 1)` and let `f` be a real function whose derivative `f'`
(taken to be `0` where `f` is not differentiable) is differentiable on `(0, 1)`. Then for every
`x ∈ (0, 1)` the [backward generator](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/diffusion-generator.md) is

`(L_{b,a} f)(x) = (1 / m(x)) (f' / ψ)'(x)`,

where `m` is the [speed density](speed-density.md) and `ψ` the [scale density](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/scale-density.md): the generator is
`(d/dm)(d/dS)`, with `S` the scale function.

- S. Karlin and H. M. Taylor, *A Second Course in Stochastic Processes*, Academic Press (1981),
