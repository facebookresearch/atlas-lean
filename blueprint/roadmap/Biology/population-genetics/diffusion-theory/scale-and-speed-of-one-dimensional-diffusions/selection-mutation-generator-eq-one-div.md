# Canonical form of the selection–mutation generator

Let `α`, `θ₁`, `θ₂` be real numbers and `f` a real function whose derivative `f'` (taken to be `0`
where `f` is not differentiable) is differentiable on `(0, 1)`. Then for every `x ∈ (0, 1)` the
[selection–mutation generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion-with-selection-and-mutat/selection-mutation-generator.md)
satisfies

`(L f)(x) = (1 / m(x)) (f' / ψ)'(x)`,

where `m` and `ψ` are the [speed](selection-mutation-speed-density.md) and
[scale](selection-mutation-scale-density.md) densities with selection and mutation. No
integrability of `m` or `ψ` near `0` or `1` is assumed.
