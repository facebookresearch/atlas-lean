# Allele-relabeling symmetry of Kimura's fixation probability

For all real `σ` and `x`, [Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md)
satisfies `u_σ(x) = 1 - u_{-σ}(1 - x)`. Relabeling the two alleles sends the frequency `x` to
`1 - x` and the scaled selection coefficient `σ` to `-σ`.
