# Allele-relabeling symmetry of Kimura's fixation probability

For all real `σ` and `x`, [Kimura's formula](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md)
satisfies `u_σ(x) = 1 - u_{-σ}(1 - x)`. For an initial frequency `x ∈ [0, 1]`, where `u_σ(x)` is a
fixation probability, relabeling the two alleles sends the frequency `x` to `1 - x` and the scaled
selection coefficient `σ` to `-σ`.
