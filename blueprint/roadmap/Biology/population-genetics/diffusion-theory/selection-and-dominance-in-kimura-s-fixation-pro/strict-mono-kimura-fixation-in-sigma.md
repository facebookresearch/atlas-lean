# Kimura's fixation probability increases strictly in σ for 0 < x < 1

For every `x ∈ (0, 1)`, the map `σ ↦ u_σ(x)`, where `u_σ` is
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md), is strictly
increasing on the real line. At `x = 0` and `x = 1` it is constant, equal to `0` and to `1`.
