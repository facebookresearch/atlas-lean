# Scale-function ratio for a dominant allele

For every real `c` and every `p ∈ [0, 1]`, the
[scale-function ratio](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/fixation-prob.md) of the diffusion with variance `x (1 - x)` and the
[drift with dominance](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/dominance-drift.md) at dominance coefficient `h = 1`, that is drift
`2c x (1 - x)²`, is

`S(p) / S(1) = (∫₀ᵖ e^{2c x² - 4c x} dx) / (∫₀¹ e^{2c x² - 4c x} dx)`.
