# Derivative of Kimura's fixation probability in σ at σ = 0

For every `x ∈ [0, 1]`, the map `σ ↦ u_σ(x)`, where `u_σ` is
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md), is differentiable
at `σ = 0` with derivative `x (1 - x)`: weak genic selection changes the neutral value `x` by
`σ x (1 - x)` to first order in `σ`. At `x = 0` and `x = 1` the derivative is `0`, as `u_σ(0) = 0`
and `u_σ(1) = 1` for every `σ`.
