# At dominance h = 1/2 the scale-function ratio is Kimura's formula

For every real `c` and every `p ∈ [0, 1]`, the
[scale-function ratio](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/fixation-prob.md) `S(p) / S(1)` of the diffusion with variance
`x (1 - x)` and the [drift with dominance](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/dominance-drift.md)
`2c x (1 - x) (h + (1 - 2h) x)` at dominance coefficient `h = 1/2` equals
[Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md) with scaled
selection coefficient `σ = c`: `S(p) / S(1) = u_c(p)`.

At `h = 1/2` the drift is `c x (1 - x)`, genic selection with `σ = c`. Here `c = Ns` for genotype
fitnesses `1`, `1 + hs` and `1 + s`, so this `s` is not the per-allele coefficient of genic
selection.
