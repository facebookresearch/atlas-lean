# Without selection the scale-function ratio is the initial frequency

For every real `h` and every `p ∈ [0, 1]`, the
[scale-function ratio](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/fixation-prob.md) of the diffusion with variance `x (1 - x)` and the
[drift with dominance](../selection-duality-and-general-one-dimensional-di/dominance-drift.md) at `c = 0`, which is the zero drift whatever the
dominance coefficient `h`, is `S(p) / S(1) = p`.
