# Truncation error of spectral expansions of Wright–Fisher transition densities

This page covers a research target: tail and error bounds for truncations of spectral expansions of Wright–Fisher transition densities, neutral or with selection, such as [the Jacobi series with mutation](../transition-densities-of-wright-fisher-diffusions/jacobi-series-of-the-mutation-transition-density.md), stated with the times, the parameter ranges and the norm for which they hold.

## Expansion

Fix the expansion and the norm, then state and prove the truncation bounds, each as a page of its own.

Prerequisites:

- [Jacobi series of the Wright–Fisher transition density with mutation](../transition-densities-of-wright-fisher-diffusions/jacobi-series-of-the-mutation-transition-density.md)

- M. Steinrücken, Y. X. R. Wang and Y. S. Song, An explicit transition density expansion for a multi-allelic Wright–Fisher diffusion with general diploid selection, *Theor. Popul. Biol.* 83 (2013), 1–14.
