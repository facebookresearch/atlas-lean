# Finite-difference schemes for the backward equation

This page covers a research target: a specified finite-difference discretization of the backward equation of the Wright–Fisher diffusion for hitting probabilities and additive functionals, such as the [mean absorption time](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator-mean-absorption-time.md), with its treatment of the degenerate boundaries, and proofs of its consistency, its stability (for instance through an M-matrix structure) and its convergence in a stated norm.

## Expansion

Fix the scheme, the boundary treatment and the norm, then state and prove consistency, stability and convergence, each as a page of its own.

Prerequisites:

- [The neutral mean absorption time solves the backward equation](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator-mean-absorption-time.md)
