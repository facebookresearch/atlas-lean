# Grapheme-valued Wright–Fisher diffusion

Population-genetic resampling can drive dynamics on large dense graphs: a vertex that "poaches" another copies its connections, as a parent passes on its type, and self-isolation plays the role of mutation. Encoding graphs as graphemes with complete components, the finite chains converge to a Wright-Fisher diffusion with mutation on graphons whose stationary law is the GEM-grapheme, the graph analogue of the Poisson-Dirichlet law.
