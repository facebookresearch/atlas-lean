# Fleming–Viot well-posedness on a compact type space

Let $E$ be a compact metric space and $B$ a bounded jump-type mutation operator on $C(E)$. For every initial law on $\mathcal M_1(E)$ the Fleming–Viot martingale problem with polynomial test functions has a solution with continuous paths, and the solution is unique in law. This assembles four results:

- the empirical measures of the [Moran particle systems](moran-particle-system-with-feller-mutation-on-a.md) have generators that [converge to the Fleming–Viot generator](finite-type-moran-approximation-of-the-fleming-v.md) on polynomials, and their laws are [C-tight](tightness-of-measure-valued-approximations-in-c.md);
- every subsequential limit [solves the Fleming–Viot martingale problem](limit-martingale-problem-identification-for-m1-e.md), which gives existence;
- [uniqueness by moment duality](fleming-viot-uniqueness-by-function-valued-coale.md).

For finite $E$ with parent-independent mutation the solution is the $K$-allele Wright–Fisher diffusion of the [finite-type correspondence](finite-type-fleming-viot-k-allele-wright-fisher.md). Unbounded Feller mutation, such as Brownian mutation on a circle, is a separate target.

## Expansion

A full proof page must still check that the interfaces of the four results match exactly (test-function class, initial laws, path space) and that the restriction to finite $E$ agrees with the finite-type correspondence.
