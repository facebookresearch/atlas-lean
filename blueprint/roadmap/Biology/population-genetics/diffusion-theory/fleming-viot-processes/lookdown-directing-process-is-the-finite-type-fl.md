# The lookdown directing process is the finite-type Fleming–Viot process

Let $E$ be a finite type space with $K$ types and parent-independent mutation. The [infinite-level lookdown process](infinite-level-lookdown-process-finite-type-spac.md) is exchangeable and has a [continuous common directing process](common-directing-process-of-the-infinite-lookdow.md), which [solves the parent-independent $K$-allele Wright–Fisher martingale problem](martingale-problem-identification-of-the-lookdow.md) through the [finite-type correspondence](finite-type-fleming-viot-k-allele-wright-fisher.md). By [uniqueness in law for the $K$-allele martingale problem](../neutral-wright-fisher-martingale-problem/uniqueness-in-law-for-the-k-allele-martingale-pr.md), the directing process therefore has the law of the $K$-allele Wright–Fisher diffusion with parent-independent mutation, that is, of the finite-type Fleming–Viot process. General mutation and a compact type space are separate targets.

A full proof page must still check that the initial law, the mutation parameters and the path space of the identification and of the uniqueness result match, and that the limit is the $K$-allele diffusion of the finite-type correspondence.
