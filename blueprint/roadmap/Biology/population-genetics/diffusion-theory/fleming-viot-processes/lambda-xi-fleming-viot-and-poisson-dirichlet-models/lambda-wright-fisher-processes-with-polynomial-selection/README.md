# Λ-Wright–Fisher processes with polynomial selection

Two-type Λ-Wright–Fisher processes whose drift is an arbitrary polynomial vanishing at the boundary, modelling frequency-dependent selection together with multiple-merger neutral reproduction. Every such drift admits infinitely many decompositions into Moran-type interaction mechanisms, each of which yields a branching–coalescing ancestral selection graph; the type frequency is dual to the Bernstein coefficient vector of the ancestral selection polynomial, and minimality notions single out the most economical ancestral structures.
