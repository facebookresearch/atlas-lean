# Relabeling the alleles preserves neutral solutions

Let `μ` be a measure and `(ℱ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` a filtration. If `X` solves the
[martingale problem for the Wright–Fisher diffusion](../fixation-and-absorption-in-the-wright-fisher-dif/is-wright-fisher-solution.md)
with `σ = 0` with respect to `(ℱ_t)` and `μ`, then so does `1 - X`, the frequency of the other
allele: the neutral generator `L_0 f(y) = y (1 - y) / 2 · f''(y)` commutes with the reflection
`y ↦ 1 - y`.
