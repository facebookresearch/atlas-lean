# A neutral solution started at 0 stays at 0

Let `μ` be a probability measure, `(ℱ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` a filtration and `X` a solution of the
[martingale problem for the Wright–Fisher diffusion](../fixation-and-absorption-in-the-wright-fisher-dif/is-wright-fisher-solution.md)
with `σ = 0` with respect to `(ℱ_t)` and `μ`, with `X_0 = 0` `μ`-almost surely. Then for `μ`-almost
every `ω`, `X_t(ω) = 0` for every time `t ≥ 0`: the nonnegative martingale `X` has mean `0` at every
time.
