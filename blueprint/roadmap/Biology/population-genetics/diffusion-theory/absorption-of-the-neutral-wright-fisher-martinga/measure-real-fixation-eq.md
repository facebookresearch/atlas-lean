# A neutral solution started at x is fixed with probability x

Let `μ` be a probability measure, `(ℱ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` a filtration and `X` a solution of the
[martingale problem for the Wright–Fisher diffusion](../fixation-and-absorption-in-the-wright-fisher-dif/is-wright-fisher-solution.md)
with `σ = 0` with respect to `(ℱ_t)` and `μ`, with `X_0 = x` `μ`-almost surely. Then the allele is
eventually fixed with probability `x`: `μ(there is t₀ ≥ 0 with X_t = 1 for every t ≥ t₀) = x`.

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *On the probability of fixation of mutant genes in a population*, Genetics 47 (1962),
  713–719, with `s = 0`.
- W. J. Ewens, *Mathematical Population Genetics I: Theoretical Introduction*, 2nd ed., Springer
  (2004), ch. 4.
