# The neutral allele frequency is a martingale

Let `μ` be a finite measure, `(ℱ_t)_{t ≥ 0}` a filtration and `X` a solution of the
[martingale problem for the Wright–Fisher diffusion](../fixation-and-absorption-in-the-wright-fisher-dif/is-wright-fisher-solution.md)
with `σ = 0` with respect to `(ℱ_t)` and `μ`. Then `X` is an `(ℱ_t)`-martingale under `μ`: take
`f(y) = y` in the martingale problem, for which `L_0 f = 0`.
