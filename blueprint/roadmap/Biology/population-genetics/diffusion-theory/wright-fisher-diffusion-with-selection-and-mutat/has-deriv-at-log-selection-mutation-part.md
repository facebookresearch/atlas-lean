# The log partition function has derivative twice the stationary mean

For all real `θ₁ > 0` and `θ₂ > 0` and every real `α`, the map `α ↦ log Z(α, θ₁, θ₂)`, with `Z`
the [partition function](selection-mutation-partition.md), is differentiable at `α` with derivative `2 μ(α, θ₁, θ₂)`, where `μ` is the [stationary mean](selection-mutation-mean.md).
