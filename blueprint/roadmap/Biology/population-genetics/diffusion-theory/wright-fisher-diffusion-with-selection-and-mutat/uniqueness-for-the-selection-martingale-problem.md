# Uniqueness for the selection martingale problem by an inverse change of measure

Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\theta = 0$. If $Y$ solves the [martingale problem for the Wright–Fisher diffusion](../fixation-and-absorption-in-the-wright-fisher-dif/is-wright-fisher-solution.md) with selection $\alpha$, then changing measure by $1/Z^\alpha$, with the density process $Z^\alpha$ built from $Y$, gives a solution of the neutral martingale problem. By the [neutral uniqueness in law](../neutral-wright-fisher-martingale-problem/uniqueness-in-law-for-the-neutral-martingale-pro.md) the law of $Y$ is then $P^\alpha$. Together with [existence under $P^\alpha$](under-p-alpha-the-canonical-process-solves-the-s.md), the selection martingale problem is well posed.
