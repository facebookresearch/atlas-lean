# The stationary mean has derivative twice the stationary variance

For all real `θ₁ > 0` and `θ₂ > 0` and every real `α`, the [stationary mean](selection-mutation-mean.md) `α ↦ μ(α, θ₁, θ₂)` is
differentiable at `α` with derivative `2 v(α, θ₁, θ₂)`, where `v` is the [stationary variance](selection-mutation-variance.md).
