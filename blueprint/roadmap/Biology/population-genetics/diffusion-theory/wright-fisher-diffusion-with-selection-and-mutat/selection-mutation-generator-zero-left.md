# Without selection the generator is the mutation generator

For all real `θ₁`, `θ₂`, every real function `f` and every real `x`, the [selection–mutation generator](selection-mutation-generator.md) with `α = 0` is
the [mutation generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion-with-mutation/mutation-generator.md): `(L_{0,θ₁,θ₂} f)(x) = x (1 - x) / 2 · f''(x) + (θ₁ (1 - x) - θ₂ x) / 2 · f'(x)`.
