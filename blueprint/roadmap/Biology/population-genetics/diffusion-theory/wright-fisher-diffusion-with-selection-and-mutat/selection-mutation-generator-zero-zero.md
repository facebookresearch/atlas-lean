# Without mutation the generator is the selection generator

For every real `α`, every real function `f` and every real `x`, the [selection–mutation generator](selection-mutation-generator.md) with
`θ₁ = θ₂ = 0` is the [selection generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) with scaled selection coefficient `α`:
`(L_{α,0,0} f)(x) = x (1 - x) / 2 · f''(x) + α x (1 - x) f'(x)`.
