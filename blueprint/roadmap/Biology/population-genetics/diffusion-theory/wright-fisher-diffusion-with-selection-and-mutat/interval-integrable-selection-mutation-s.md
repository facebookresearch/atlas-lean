# The selection–mutation speed density is integrable for θ₁, θ₂ > 0

For every real `α` and all real `θ₁ > 0` and `θ₂ > 0`, the [selection–mutation speed density](../scale-and-speed-of-one-dimensional-diffusions/selection-mutation-speed-density.md)
`m(x) = 2 e^(2αx) x ^ (θ₁ - 1) (1 - x) ^ (θ₂ - 1)` is integrable on `[0, 1]`.
