# Coupled Wright–Fisher diffusion duality

A multi-locus Wright–Fisher diffusion with single-locus and pairwise selection has a Dirichlet-times-exponential stationary density. Its moment dual is the block-counting process of coupled ancestral selection graphs, in which pairwise selection causes simultaneous branching at two loci.
