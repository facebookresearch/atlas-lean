# Error of the diffusion approximation to the Wright–Fisher model

This node covers the error made by the diffusion approximation of the Wright–Fisher model. Ethier and
Norman (1977) compare the Wright–Fisher model and its approximating diffusion through the expected value
of a smooth but arbitrary function of the gene frequency in generation $n$, and show that, in the absence
of selection, the two expectations differ by at most a linear combination of the maximum mutation rate
and the reciprocal of the population size, with coefficients depending only on the derivatives of the
function.

## Expansion

A sound expansion fixes from the paper the class of test functions, the time scaling that matches
generation $n$ with diffusion time, and the form of the coefficients, and states the result for the
neutral model with mutation; any extension to selection needs its own source.

## Sources

- S. N. Ethier and M. F. Norman, "Error estimate for the diffusion approximation of the Wright–Fisher
  model", Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA 74 (1977), 5096–5098,
  <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.74.11.5096>.
