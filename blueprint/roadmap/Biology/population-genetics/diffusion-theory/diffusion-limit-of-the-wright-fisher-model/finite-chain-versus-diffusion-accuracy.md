# Accuracy of the diffusion approximation for finite-population fixation

The fixation probability of an allele in the finite Wright–Fisher chain is the solution of a linear system, and the diffusion formula $(1 - e^{-4Nsp})/(1 - e^{-4Ns})$ is an approximation to it. For the neutral model with mutation, Ethier and Norman bound the difference between the expectations of a smooth function of the frequency under the chain and under the diffusion by a combination of the maximum mutation rate and the reciprocal of the population size.
