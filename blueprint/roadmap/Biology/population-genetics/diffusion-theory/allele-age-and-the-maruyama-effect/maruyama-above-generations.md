# Sojourn density above the current frequency in generations

For all real $N > 0$, $s \ne 0$, $x$ and $z$, with $\gamma = 4Ns$,

$$2N\, \Psi_2^{2Ns}(x, z) = \frac{\bigl(e^{-\gamma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(e^{\gamma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(1 - e^{\gamma x}\bigr)}{s z (1 - z)\bigl(e^{-\gamma(1 - x)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(1 - e^{\gamma}\bigr)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$: the right side is the formula printed by Kiezun et al. (2013), in generations, and the left side is the [closed form above the current frequency](maruyama-above.md) at $\sigma = 2Ns$.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
