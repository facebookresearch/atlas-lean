# Mean age of a neutral allele

For every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [mean age](mean-age.md) of a neutral allele at frequency $x$ is

$$a_0(x) = -\frac{2x \log x}{1 - x}$$

in units of $2N$ generations, that is, $-4Nx \log x/(1 - x)$ generations.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and T. Ohta, *The age of a neutral mutant persisting in a finite population*, Genetics 75 (1973), 199–212.
