# Neutral case of the sojourn density

For every real $p$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [sojourn density](selection-green.md) without selection is the [neutral Green's function](../sojourn-times-of-the-wright-fisher-diffusion/neutral-green.md): $\Psi_0(p, x) = G(p, x)$, where $G(p, x) = 2(1 - p)/(1 - x)$ for $x \le p$ and $G(p, x) = 2p/x$ for $p < x$.
