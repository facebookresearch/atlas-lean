# The Maruyama effect

For every real $\sigma \ne 0$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [mean age](mean-age.md) satisfies $a_\sigma(x) < a_0(x)$: an allele under selection, advantageous or deleterious, is on average strictly younger than a neutral allele at the same frequency.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
