# Sojourn density below the current frequency in generations

For all real $N > 0$, $s \ne 0$ and $z$, with $\gamma = 4Ns$,

$$2N\, \Psi_1^{2Ns}(z) = \frac{\bigl(e^{-\gamma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(e^{-\gamma z} - 1\bigr)}{s z (1 - z)\bigl(1 - e^{-\gamma}\bigr)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$: the right side is the formula printed by Kiezun et al. (2013) for $N$ diploid individuals, selection coefficient $s$ and time in generations, and the left side is the [closed form below the current frequency](maruyama-below.md) at $\sigma = 2Ns$, converted from units of $2N$ generations.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
