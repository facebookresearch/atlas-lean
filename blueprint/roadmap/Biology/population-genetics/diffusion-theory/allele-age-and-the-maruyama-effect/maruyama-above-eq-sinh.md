# Hyperbolic form of the sojourn density above the current frequency

For every real $\sigma \ne 0$ and all $x, z \in (0, 1)$, the [closed form above the current frequency](maruyama-above.md) is

$$\Psi_2^\sigma(x, z) = \frac{2 \sinh(\sigma(1 - z))^2 \sinh(\sigma x)}{\sigma \sinh \sigma\, \sinh(\sigma(1 - x))\, z(1 - z)}.$$
