# Right derivative of the sojourn density at the diagonal

For every real $\sigma$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the function $p \mapsto \Psi_\sigma(p, x)$, the [sojourn density](selection-green.md), has right derivative

$$-\frac{2\, u_\sigma(x)}{x(1 - x)}$$

at $p = x$, where $u_\sigma$ is [Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md).
