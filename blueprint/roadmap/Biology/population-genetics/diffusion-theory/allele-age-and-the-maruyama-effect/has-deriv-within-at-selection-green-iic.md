# Left derivative of the sojourn density at the diagonal

For every real $\sigma$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the function $p \mapsto \Psi_\sigma(p, x)$, the [sojourn density](selection-green.md), has left derivative

$$\frac{2\bigl(1 - u_\sigma(x)\bigr)}{x(1 - x)}$$

at $p = x$, where $u_\sigma$ is [Kimura's fixation probability](../wright-fisher-diffusion/kimura-fixation.md). With the [right derivative](has-deriv-within-at-selection-green-ici.md), the derivative in $p$ jumps by $-2/(x(1 - x))$ at $p = x$, so that $L_\sigma \Psi_\sigma(\cdot, x) = -\delta_x$ in the distributional sense.
