# Stronger positive selection, younger alleles

For all real $\sigma$ and $\tau$ with $0 \le \sigma < \tau$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$, the [mean age](mean-age.md) satisfies $a_\tau(x) < a_\sigma(x)$: at a fixed frequency, the mean age is strictly decreasing in $\sigma \ge 0$. Kiezun et al. (2013, Figure 1b) show this numerically from the closed forms.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Figure 1b.
