# Mean age of an allele at a given frequency

For real numbers $\sigma$ and $x$, let

$$a_\sigma(x) = \int_0^x \Psi_1^\sigma(z)\, dz + \int_x^1 \Psi_2^\sigma(x, z)\, dz,$$

with the closed forms [below](maruyama-below.md) and [above](maruyama-above.md) the current frequency, where the integral of a non-integrable function is taken to be $0$. For $0 < x < 1$ it is the mean age of an allele at current frequency $x$, in units of $2N$ generations.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
