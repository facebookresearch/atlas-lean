# Mean age as the limit of conditional sojourn times

For every real $\sigma$ and every $x \in (0, 1)$,

$$\int_0^1 \Psi_\sigma(p, x; z)\, dz \to a_\sigma(x) \quad \text{as } p \to 0^+,$$

where $\Psi_\sigma(p, x; z)$ is the [conditional sojourn density](age-density.md) and $a_\sigma(x)$ the [mean age](mean-age.md): the total conditional sojourn time of a path from initial frequency $p$ to current frequency $x$ tends to the mean age as the initial frequency tends to $0$.

## Sources

- T. Maruyama, *The age of a rare mutant gene in a large population*, Am. J. Hum. Genet. 26 (1974), 669–673.
- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
