# Closed-form sojourn density below the current frequency

For real numbers $\sigma$ and $z$, let $\Psi_1^\sigma(z) = 2$ if $\sigma = 0$ and otherwise

$$\Psi_1^\sigma(z) = \frac{\bigl(e^{-2\sigma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(e^{-2\sigma z} - 1\bigr)}{\sigma z (1 - z)\bigl(1 - e^{-2\sigma}\bigr)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $0 < z < x < 1$ it is the closed form of Kiezun et al. (2013) for the conditional sojourn density at the frequency $z$ below the current frequency $x$, in units of $2N$ generations with $\sigma = 2Ns$; the value $2$ is its continuous extension to $\sigma = 0$. It does not depend on $x$.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
