# Closed-form sojourn density above the current frequency

For real numbers $\sigma$, $x$ and $z$, let $\Psi_2^\sigma(x, z) = \frac{2x(1 - z)}{(1 - x)z}$ if $\sigma = 0$ and otherwise

$$\Psi_2^\sigma(x, z) = \frac{\bigl(e^{-2\sigma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(e^{2\sigma(1 - z)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(1 - e^{2\sigma x}\bigr)}{\sigma z (1 - z)\bigl(e^{-2\sigma(1 - x)} - 1\bigr)\bigl(1 - e^{2\sigma}\bigr)},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $0 < x < z < 1$ it is the closed form of Kiezun et al. (2013) for the conditional sojourn density at the frequency $z$ above the current frequency $x$, in units of $2N$ generations with $\sigma = 2Ns$; the neutral formula is its continuous extension to $\sigma = 0$.

## Sources

- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
