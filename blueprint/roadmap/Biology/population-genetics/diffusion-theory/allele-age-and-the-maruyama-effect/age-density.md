# Maruyama–Kimura conditional sojourn density

For real numbers $\sigma$, $p$, $x$ and $z$, let

$$\Psi_\sigma(p, x; z) = \frac{\Psi_\sigma(p, z)\, \Psi_\sigma(z, x)}{\Psi_\sigma(p, x)},$$

with the [sojourn density](selection-green.md) $\Psi_\sigma$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. For $p, x, z \in (0, 1)$, where $\Psi_\sigma(p, x) > 0$: given the initial frequency $p$ and the current frequency $x$, it is the density of the mean time spent at frequency $z$ along the path, in units of $2N$ generations.

## Sources

- T. Maruyama and M. Kimura, *Moments for sum of an arbitrary function of gene frequency along a stochastic path of gene frequency change*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 72 (1975), 1602–1604.
- A. Kiezun, S. L. Pulit, L. C. Francioli, et al., *Deleterious alleles in the human genome are on average younger than neutral alleles of the same frequency*, PLoS Genet. 9 (2013), e1003301, Methods.
