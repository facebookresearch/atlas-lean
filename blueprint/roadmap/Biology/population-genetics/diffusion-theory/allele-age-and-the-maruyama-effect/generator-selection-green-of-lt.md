# Sojourn density solves the backward equation below the target

For all real $\sigma$, $p$ and $x$ with $p < x$, the [sojourn density](selection-green.md) solves the homogeneous backward equation in its first argument at $p$: $(L_\sigma \Psi_\sigma(\cdot, x))(p) = 0$, with the [backward generator](../wright-fisher-diffusion/generator.md) $L_\sigma$.
