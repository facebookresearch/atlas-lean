# Modified process has the raw finite-dimensional laws

For every $x \in [0, 1]$ and every finite set $J$ of times, the law of $(Y_t)_{t \in J}$ under the [canonical path measure](raw-measure.md) $P_x$, where $Y$ is the [continuous modified process](modified-process.md), equals the law of $(X_t)_{t \in J}$ for the [raw process](raw-process.md) $X$.
