# Modified process has the canonical coordinate laws

For every $x \in [0, 1]$ and every finite set $J$ of times, the law of $(Y_t)_{t \in J}$ under the [canonical path measure](raw-measure.md) $P_x$, where $Y$ is the [continuous modified process](modified-process.md), equals the law of the coordinates $(\omega(t))_{t \in J}$, viewed as real numbers.
