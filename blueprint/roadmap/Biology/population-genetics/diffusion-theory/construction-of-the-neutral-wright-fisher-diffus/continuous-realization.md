# Continuous realization of the raw Wright–Fisher process

For $x \in [0, 1]$, a continuous realization of the [raw process](raw-process.md) is a real process $(Y_t)_{t \ge 0}$ on the [sample paths](sample-path.md) such that each $Y_t$ is measurable, every path $t \mapsto Y_t(\omega)$ is continuous, $Y_t = X_t$ almost surely under the [canonical path measure](raw-measure.md) $P_x$ for each $t$, $Y_0(\omega) = x$ for every $\omega$, and $Y_t(\omega) \in [0, 1]$ for all $t$ and $\omega$.
