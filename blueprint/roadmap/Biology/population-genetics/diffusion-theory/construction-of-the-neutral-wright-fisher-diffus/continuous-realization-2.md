# Continuous modification selected by Kolmogorov's theorem

For every $x \in [0, 1]$, a chosen [continuous realization](continuous-realization.md) of the raw Wright–Fisher process: it exists by Kolmogorov's continuity theorem, since the raw process satisfies [Kolmogorov's condition](is-kolmogorov-process-raw-process.md) with $q = 2 > 1$, and its values stay in the closed interval $[0, 1]$.
