# Canonical path measure of the raw Wright–Fisher process

For $x \in [0, 1]$, the probability measure $P_x$ on the [sample paths](sample-path.md) $\omega : [0, \infty) \to [0, 1]$ given by the Kolmogorov extension of the Markov family of [neutral Wright–Fisher kernels](neutral-kernel.md) started at $x$: its finite-dimensional laws are those of the Markov chain with these transitions, consistent by the [Chapman–Kolmogorov equation](neutral-kernel-add.md).
