# Kolmogorov continuity condition for the raw Wright–Fisher process

For every $x \in [0, 1]$, the [raw process](raw-process.md) $(X_t)_{t \ge 0}$ under the [canonical path measure](raw-measure.md) $P_x$ satisfies Kolmogorov's continuity condition with exponents $p = 4$, $q = 2$ and constant $\frac{3}{16}$: every pair $(X_s, X_t)$ is measurable and

$$\mathbb{E}_x\bigl[|X_s - X_t|^4\bigr] \le \frac{3}{16}\, |s - t|^2 \quad \text{for all } s, t \ge 0.$$
