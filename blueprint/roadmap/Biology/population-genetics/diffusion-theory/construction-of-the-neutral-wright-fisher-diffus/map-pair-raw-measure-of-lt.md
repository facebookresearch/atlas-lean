# Two-time law of the raw Wright–Fisher path measure

For every $x \in [0, 1]$ and all real $0 \le s < t$, the joint law of $(\omega(s), \omega(t))$ under the [canonical path measure](raw-measure.md) $P_x$ is the composition $K_s(x, \cdot) \otimes K_{t-s}$: the law of $\omega(s)$ followed by the [neutral Wright–Fisher transition](neutral-kernel.md) over the time gap $t - s$.
