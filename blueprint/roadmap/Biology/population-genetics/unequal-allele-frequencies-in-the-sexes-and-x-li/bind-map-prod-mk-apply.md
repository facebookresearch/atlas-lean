# Random union of a female and a male gamete

Let `p_f` and `p_m` be probability distributions on a set `A` of alleles, the allele laws of
female and male gametes. Draw a female gamete from `p_f` and, independently, a male gamete from
`p_m`, and record the ordered genotype (maternal allele, paternal allele). For all alleles `a`
and `b`, the probability of the ordered genotype `(a, b)` is `p_f(a) p_m(b)`.
