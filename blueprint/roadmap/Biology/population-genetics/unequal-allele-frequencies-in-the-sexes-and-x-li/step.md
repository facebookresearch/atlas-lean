# One generation of X-linked transmission

At an X-linked biallelic locus with no selection, mutation, migration or drift, one generation
maps the allele frequencies `(p_f, p_m)` in females and males to `((p_f + p_m) / 2, p_f)`:
daughters receive one X chromosome from each parent, sons receive their X from the mother. The map
is defined on all real pairs.
