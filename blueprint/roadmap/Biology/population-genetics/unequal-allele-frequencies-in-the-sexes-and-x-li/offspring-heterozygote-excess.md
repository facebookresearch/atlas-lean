# First-generation heterozygote excess from the parental genotype laws

At a biallelic locus with alleles `A` and `a`, let `G_f` and `G_m` be the genotype laws of the
female and male parents and `p_f`, `p_m` the frequencies of `A` among their
[random gametes](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md). In the [two-sex offspring law](offspring.md),
the heterozygote frequency (genotypes `(A, a)` and `(a, A)` together) exceeds the Hardy–Weinberg
value `2 p̄ (1 - p̄)` at the offspring frequency `p̄` of `A` by `(p_m - p_f) ^ 2 / 2`.
