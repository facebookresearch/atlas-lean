# Weighted mean allele frequency at an X-linked locus

For allele frequencies `p_f` in females and `p_m` in males at an X-linked locus, the weighted mean
frequency is `p̄ = (2 p_f + p_m) / 3`. With equal positive numbers of females and males (per
female–male pair), it is the allele frequency among all X chromosomes, each counted once (females
carry two, males one); with `F` females and `M` males, when `2F + M > 0`, that frequency is
`(2F p_f + M p_m) / (2F + M)` instead. The weights `2 : 1` make `p̄`
[invariant under X-linked transmission](pbar-step.md), whatever the numbers of each sex.
