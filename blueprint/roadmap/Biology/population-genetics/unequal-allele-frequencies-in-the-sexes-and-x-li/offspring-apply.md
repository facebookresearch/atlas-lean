# The two-sex offspring law is the product of the gamete laws

For genotype laws `G_f` and `G_m` of the female and male parents and all alleles `a` and `b`, the
[two-sex offspring law](offspring.md) gives the ordered genotype `(a, b)` the probability
`g_f(a) g_m(b)`, where `g_f` and `g_m` are the [random-gamete laws](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md)
of `G_f` and `G_m`.
