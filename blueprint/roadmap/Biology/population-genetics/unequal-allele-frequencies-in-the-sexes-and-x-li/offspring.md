# Offspring genotype law with separate sexes

For probability distributions `G_f` and `G_m` of ordered genotypes on `A × A`, the genotype laws
of the female and male parents, the offspring genotype law is the law of the ordered pair
(maternal allele, paternal allele) of a
[random gamete](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md) of `G_f` and an independent random gamete of
`G_m`. Since offspring sex is independent of genotype, the same law describes sons and daughters.
