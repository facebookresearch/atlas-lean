# Second generation: Hardy–Weinberg product of the averaged frequencies

For genotype laws `G_f` and `G_m` of the female and male parents and all alleles `a` and `b`, one
more generation of random union of gametes, applied to the
[two-sex offspring law](offspring.md) of sons and daughters, gives the ordered genotype `(a, b)`
the probability `(g_f(a) / 2 + g_m(a) / 2) (g_f(b) / 2 + g_m(b) / 2)`, the Hardy–Weinberg product
of the averaged allele frequencies; here `g_f` and `g_m` are the
[random-gamete laws](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md) of `G_f` and `G_m`.
