# Hardy–Weinberg proportions persist after the second generation

For genotype laws `G_f` and `G_m` of the female and male parents, let `G_1` be their
[two-sex offspring law](offspring.md) and `G_2` the
[offspring genotype law under random union of gametes](../hardy-weinberg-law/offspring.md) of
`G_1`. Then random union of gametes leaves `G_2` unchanged: the offspring law of `G_2` is `G_2`.
