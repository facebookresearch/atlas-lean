# Heterozygote excess is positive exactly when the frequencies differ

For all real `p_f` and `p_m` (the frequencies of allele `A` in females and in males at a
biallelic autosomal locus), the first-generation heterozygote excess
`p_f (1 - p_m) + p_m (1 - p_f) - 2 p̄ (1 - p̄)`, with `p̄ = (p_f + p_m) / 2`, is positive if and
only if `p_m ≠ p_f`.
