# Offspring allele frequency is the average of the two sexes

For genotype laws `G_f` and `G_m` of the female and male parents on an arbitrary set `A` of
alleles and every allele `a`, the frequency of `a` among the gametes of the
[two-sex offspring law](offspring.md) is `g_f(a) / 2 + g_m(a) / 2`, where `g_f` and `g_m` are the
[random-gamete laws](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md) of `G_f` and `G_m`.
