# Biallelic offspring allele frequency is the average of the sexes

At a biallelic locus, let `p_f` and `p_m` be the frequencies of allele `A` among the
[random gametes](../hardy-weinberg-law/gamete.md) of the female and male genotype laws `G_f` and
`G_m`. Then the frequency of `A` among the gametes of the [two-sex offspring law](offspring.md)
is `(p_f + p_m) / 2`, as real numbers.
