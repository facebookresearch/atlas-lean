# Closed form for the female frequency at an X-linked locus

For all real initial frequencies `p_f(0)` and `p_m(0)` and every `t`, after `t` generations of
[X-linked transmission](step.md) the female frequency satisfies
`p_f(t) - p̄ = (-1/2) ^ t (p_f(0) - p̄)`, where `p̄ = (2 p_f(0) + p_m(0)) / 3` is the
[weighted mean frequency](pbar.md).
