# Heterozygote excess for female and male allele frequencies

At a biallelic autosomal locus, let allele `A` have frequency `p_f` in females and `p_m` in males
(any real numbers). The offspring heterozygote frequency `p_f (1 - p_m) + p_m (1 - p_f)` exceeds
the Hardy–Weinberg value `2 p̄ (1 - p̄)` at the offspring allele frequency `p̄ = (p_f + p_m) / 2`
by `(p_m - p_f) ^ 2 / 2`.
