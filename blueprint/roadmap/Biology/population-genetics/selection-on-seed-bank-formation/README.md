# Selection on seed-bank formation

A Wright–Fisher model of annual plants in which a mutant allele lets seeds stay dormant for up to $K$ generations (germination-delay distribution $\mathbf b=(b_0,\dots,b_K)$, mean delay $B=\sum_i ib_i$), but is otherwise neutral. Invasion splits into a branching phase, in which the mutant is rare and its family is a critical multitype branching process, and a diffusion phase on a slow manifold. In a constant environment the seed bank is selected against through a strong drift effect.
