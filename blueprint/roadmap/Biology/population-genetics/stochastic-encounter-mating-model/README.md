# Stochastic encounter-mating model

A bottom-up model of permanent monogamous pair formation in a finite population of multitype females and males: each animal fires at the points of its own point process, encounters a uniformly chosen single of the opposite sex, and the temporary pair becomes permanent with a type-dependent mating probability. The resulting mating pattern (contingency table of pair types) is panmictic exactly under a fine balance condition on the encounter-mating law, and has a deterministic fluid limit governed by replicator equations.
