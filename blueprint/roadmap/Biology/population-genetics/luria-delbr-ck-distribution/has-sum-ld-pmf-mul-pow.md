# Lea–Coulson generating function

For every real $m \ge 0$ and every real $0 < z < 1$,

$$\sum_{n \ge 0} p_n(m)\, z^n = \exp\Bigl(m \Bigl(\frac{1}{z} - 1\Bigr) \log(1 - z)\Bigr) = e^{m(h(z) - 1)},$$

with the [Luria–Delbrück probabilities](ld-pmf.md) $p_n(m)$ and the [clone-size generating function](clone-pgf.md) $h$.

## Sources

- D. E. Lea and C. A. Coulson, *The distribution of the numbers of mutants in bacterial populations*, J. Genet. 49 (1949), 264–285.
