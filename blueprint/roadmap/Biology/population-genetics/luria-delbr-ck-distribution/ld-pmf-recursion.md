# Ma–Sandri–Sarkar recursion

For every real $m$ and every natural number $n \ge 1$, the [Luria–Delbrück probabilities](ld-pmf.md) satisfy

$$p_n(m) = \frac{m}{n} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \frac{p_i(m)}{n - i + 1}.$$

## Sources

- W. T. Ma, G. vH. Sandri and S. Sarkar, *Analysis of the Luria–Delbrück distribution using discrete convolution powers*, J. Appl. Probab. 29 (1992), 255–267.
