# Clone-size law from the founding-time density

For every natural number $j \ge 1$,

$$\int_0^\infty e^{-\tau}\, e^{-\tau} (1 - e^{-\tau})^{j-1}\, d\tau = \frac{1}{j(j+1)}.$$

A clone founded a time $\tau$ before the end has geometric size $e^{-\tau}(1 - e^{-\tau})^{j-1}$; mixing over the founding-time density $e^{-\tau}$ on $(0, \infty)$ gives the [clone-size law](clone-prob.md).
