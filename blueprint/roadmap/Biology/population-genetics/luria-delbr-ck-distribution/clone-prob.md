# Lea–Coulson clone-size law

For a natural number $j$, let $c_j = \frac{1}{j(j+1)}$ if $j \ge 1$ and $c_0 = 0$: the probability that a clone of mutants has size $j$.

## Sources

- D. E. Lea and C. A. Coulson, *The distribution of the numbers of mutants in bacterial populations*, J. Genet. 49 (1949), 264–285.
