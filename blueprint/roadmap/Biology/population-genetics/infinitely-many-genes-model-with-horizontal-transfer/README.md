# Infinitely many genes model with horizontal transfer

Population-genetic model of bacterial pangenomes: genes are gained from an infinite pool, lost, and transferred horizontally between individuals of a Moran population. The gene frequency spectrum and pangenome-size statistics have explicit series expansions in the transfer rate, with a genealogical interpretation through the ancestral gene transfer graph.
