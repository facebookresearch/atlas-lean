# Adaptive dynamics in periodically changing environments

In individual-based adaptive dynamics with moderately rare mutations, subpopulation sizes are tracked on the logarithmic scale $K^{\beta}$ and grow at rates given by invasion fitness. When birth, death and competition rates switch periodically on a time-scale between the resident's relaxation time and the mutants' growth time, mutant growth is governed by the time-averaged invasion fitness, giving piecewise affine exponent trajectories and a sequence of resident traits.
