# Tournament Wright–Fisher diffusions

Multitype Wright–Fisher diffusions in which selection acts through pairwise contests decided by a tournament (as in rock–paper–scissors lizard morphs) have a frequency-dependent, skew-symmetric drift. Their moments are dual to a branching–coalescing process on Bernstein coefficients, the multivariate analogue of the ancestral selection graph.
