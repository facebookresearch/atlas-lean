# Multilocus diffusion with assortative mating

Large-population limits of a multilocus Moran model with weak assortative mating, loose linkage and weak mutation. Recombination drives genotype frequencies to products of allele frequencies on a fast time scale, and the allele frequencies follow a reversible diffusion on the hypercube whose drift and stationary density are expressed through mean assortment coefficients over sets of differing loci.
