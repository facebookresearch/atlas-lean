# Entropy rate of Moran processes

The entropy rate of a stationary Markov chain, the stationary average of the entropies of its one-step transition laws, measures the long-run variability of a multitype Moran process with mutation and selection: it vanishes when the process tends to fixation and is large when many types coexist in the long run.
