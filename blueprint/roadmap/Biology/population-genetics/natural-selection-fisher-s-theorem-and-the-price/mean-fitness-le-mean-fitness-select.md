# Mean fitness never decreases under haploid selection

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of types, $p$ a real vector with $p_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_i p_i = 1$, and $w$ a real vector with $w_i > 0$ for every $i$. Then $\bar w(p) \le \bar w(p')$, where $p'$ are the frequencies after [one generation of haploid selection](select.md) and $\bar w$ is the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md).
