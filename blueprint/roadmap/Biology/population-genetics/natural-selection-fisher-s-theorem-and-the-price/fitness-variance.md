# Variance of fitness

For a finite set $\iota$ of types and real vectors $p$ and $w$ on $\iota$, the variance of fitness is $\operatorname{Var}_p(w) = \sum_{i \in \iota} p_i \bigl(w_i - \bar w(p)\bigr)^2$, with the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md) $\bar w(p)$. When $p$ is a probability vector, it is the variance of fitness of a type drawn from $p$.
