# One generation of haploid selection

For a finite set $\iota$ of types and real vectors $p$ and $w$ on $\iota$, let $p'_i = p_i w_i / \bar w(p)$, with the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md) $\bar w(p)$ and the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. When $p$ is a probability vector, the fitnesses are nonnegative and $\bar w(p) > 0$, $p'$ are the type frequencies after one generation of haploid selection.
