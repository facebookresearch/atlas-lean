# Fisher's fundamental theorem of natural selection

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of types and $p$, $w$ real vectors on $\iota$ with $\sum_i p_i = 1$ and $\bar w(p) \ne 0$. Then the frequencies $p'$ after [one generation of haploid selection](select.md) satisfy

$$\bar w(p') - \bar w(p) = \frac{\operatorname{Var}_p(w)}{\bar w(p)},$$

with the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md) and the [variance of fitness](fitness-variance.md). This is the exact haploid, discrete-generation form of the theorem; Fisher states it for the additive genetic variance. The model domain is the simplex with positive fitnesses.

## Sources

- R. A. Fisher, *The Genetical Theory of Natural Selection*, Clarendon Press, Oxford (1930).
