# Diploid selection preserves nonnegative allele frequencies

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of alleles, $p$ a real vector with $p_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_i p_i = 1$, and $w$ a real matrix with $w_{ij} > 0$ for all $i, j$. Then every allele frequency after [one generation of diploid viability selection](diploid-select.md) is nonnegative.
