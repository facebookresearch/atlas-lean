# Linkage disequilibrium after one generation of recombination

For every real vector $x$ of gamete frequencies with $\sum_{a,b} x_{ab} = 1$ and every real $r$, the [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md) after [random mating with recombination](recombine.md) is $D' = (1 - r)\, D$. For a gamete probability vector and $0 \le r \le 1$, linkage disequilibrium thus decays by the factor $1 - r$ per generation.
