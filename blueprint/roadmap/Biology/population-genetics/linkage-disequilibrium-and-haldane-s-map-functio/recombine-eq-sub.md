# Classical form of the recombination recursion

For every real vector $x$ of gamete frequencies with $\sum_{a,b} x_{ab} = 1$, every real $r$ and every gamete $ab$, the frequency after [random mating with recombination](recombine.md) is

$$x'_{ab} = x_{ab} - \varepsilon_{ab}\, r D,$$

where $D$ is the [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md), $\varepsilon_{ab} = 1$ for the coupling gametes $AB$ and $ab$ and $\varepsilon_{ab} = -1$ for the repulsion gametes $Ab$ and $aB$. The identity holds for every real $r$; the biological range is $0 \le r \le 1/2$.
