# Linkage disequilibrium of a two-locus gamete law

For a real vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies, with $A$ and $B$ the alleles $1$ at the two loci and $a$, $b$ the alleles $0$, the linkage disequilibrium is $D = x_{AB}\, x_{ab} - x_{Ab}\, x_{aB}$.
