# Recombination preserves second-locus allele frequencies

For every real vector $x$ of gamete frequencies with $\sum_{a,b} x_{ab} = 1$, every real $r$ and every allele $b$, the [frequency of $b$ at the second locus](allele-freq-snd.md) is unchanged by [random mating with recombination](recombine.md).
