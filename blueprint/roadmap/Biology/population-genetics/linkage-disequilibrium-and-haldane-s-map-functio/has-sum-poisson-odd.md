# Series for the probability of an odd Poisson count

For every real number $d$,

$$\sum_{k \text{ odd}} e^{-d} \frac{d^k}{k!} = \frac{1 - e^{-2d}}{2},$$

[Haldane's map function](haldane.md) at $d$. For $d \ge 0$ the left side is the probability that a Poisson count with mean $d$ is odd.

## Sources

- J. B. S. Haldane, *The combination of linkage values, and the calculation of distances between the loci of linked factors*, J. Genet. 8 (1919), 299–309.
