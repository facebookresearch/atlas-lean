# Linkage disequilibrium after $t$ generations

For every real vector $x$ of gamete frequencies with $\sum_{a,b} x_{ab} = 1$, every real $r$ and every natural number $t$, the [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md) after $t$ generations of [random mating with recombination](recombine.md) is $D_t = (1 - r)^t D_0$; for a gamete probability vector and $0 \le r \le 1$ this is its geometric decay.
