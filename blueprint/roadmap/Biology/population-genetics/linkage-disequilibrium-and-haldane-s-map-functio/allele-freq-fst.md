# Allele frequency at the first locus

For a real vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies $x_{ab}$, indexed by the alleles $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ at the first and second locus, the frequency of the allele $a$ at the first locus is $p_a = \sum_b x_{ab}$.
