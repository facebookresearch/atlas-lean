# Pairwise disequilibrium in the biallelic case

Let $x$ be a real vector indexed by the four gametes of two loci with two alleles each, with
$\sum_g x_g = 1$, and let $A$ and $B$ be the alleles $1$ and $a$, $b$ the alleles $0$ at the two
loci. Then the [pairwise linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq-2.md) $D_{AB} = x_{AB} - p_A\, q_B$ of
the alleles $A$ and $B$, with $p$ and $q$ the marginals of $x$, equals the
[linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md) $D = x_{AB}\, x_{ab} - x_{Ab}\, x_{aB}$ of the two-allele
case.
