# Linkage disequilibrium tends to zero

For every real vector $x$ of gamete frequencies with $\sum_{a,b} x_{ab} = 1$ and every real $0 < r \le 1$, the [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md) after $t$ generations of [random mating with recombination](recombine.md) tends to $0$ as $t \to \infty$. This includes the biological range $0 < r \le 1/2$; for $r = 0$ the disequilibrium is constant.
