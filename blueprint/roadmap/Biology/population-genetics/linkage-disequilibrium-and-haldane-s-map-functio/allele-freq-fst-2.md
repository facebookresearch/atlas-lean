# Allele frequency at the first of two multi-allelic loci

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets of alleles at a first and a second locus, and let $x$ be a real vector indexed by $\iota \times \kappa$. When the entries of $x$ are nonnegative and sum to $1$, $x_{ij}$ is the frequency of the gamete carrying the alleles $i$ and $j$. The marginal $p_i = \sum_{l \in \kappa} x_{il}$ of allele $i \in \iota$ is, when
$x$ is a probability vector, the frequency of $i$ at the first locus.

This generalises [the allele frequency at the first locus](allele-freq-fst.md) from two alleles at
each locus to any finite numbers of alleles.
