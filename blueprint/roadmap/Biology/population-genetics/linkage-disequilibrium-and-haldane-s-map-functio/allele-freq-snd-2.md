# Allele frequency at the second of two multi-allelic loci

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets of alleles at a first and a second locus, and let $x$ be a real vector indexed by $\iota \times \kappa$. When the entries of $x$ are nonnegative and sum to $1$, $x_{ij}$ is the frequency of the gamete carrying the alleles $i$ and $j$. The marginal $q_j = \sum_{k \in \iota} x_{kj}$ of allele $j \in \kappa$ is, when
$x$ is a probability vector, the frequency of $j$ at the second locus.

This generalises [the allele frequency at the second locus](allele-freq-snd.md) from two alleles at
each locus to any finite numbers of alleles.
