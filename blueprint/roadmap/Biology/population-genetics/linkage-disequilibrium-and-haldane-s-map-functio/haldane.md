# Haldane's map function

For a real number $d$, $r(d) = \frac{1 - e^{-2d}}{2}$. For $d \ge 0$ it is the recombination fraction between two loci at map distance $d$ Morgans when crossovers form a Poisson process, without interference.

## Sources

- J. B. S. Haldane, *The combination of linkage values, and the calculation of distances between the loci of linked factors*, J. Genet. 8 (1919), 299–309.
