# Haldane's map function from Poisson crossovers

For every real $d \ge 0$, a Poisson random variable with mean $d$ is odd with probability $\frac{1 - e^{-2d}}{2}$, [Haldane's map function](haldane.md) at $d$. If the number of crossovers in an interval of map length $d$ Morgans is Poisson with mean $d$, this is the probability of an odd number of crossovers, that is, of recombination between the ends of the interval.

## Sources

- J. B. S. Haldane, *The combination of linkage values, and the calculation of distances between the loci of linked factors*, J. Genet. 8 (1919), 299–309.
