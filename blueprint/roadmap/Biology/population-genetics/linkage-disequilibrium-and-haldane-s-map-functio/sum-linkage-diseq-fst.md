# Disequilibria sum to zero over the first-locus alleles

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets of alleles at a first and a second locus, and let $x$ be a
real vector indexed by $\iota \times \kappa$ with $\sum_{g} x_g = 1$. For every allele
$j \in \kappa$ at the second locus, the [pairwise linkage disequilibria](linkage-diseq-2.md) sum to
zero over the alleles at the first locus: $\sum_{i \in \iota} D_{ij} = 0$.
