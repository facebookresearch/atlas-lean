# Random mating with recombination

For a real number $r$ and a real vector $x$ indexed by the four gametes, let

$$x'_{ab} = (1 - r)\, x_{ab} + r\, p_a\, q_b,$$

where $p_a$ and $q_b$ are the [allele frequencies at the first](allele-freq-fst.md) and [second locus](allele-freq-snd.md). When $x$ is a probability vector and $0 \le r \le 1$, $x'$ are the gamete frequencies after one generation of random mating with recombination fraction $r$.
