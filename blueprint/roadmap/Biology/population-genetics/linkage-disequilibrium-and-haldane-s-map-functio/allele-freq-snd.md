# Allele frequency at the second locus

For a real vector $x$ of four gamete frequencies $x_{ab}$, indexed by the alleles $a, b \in \{0, 1\}$ at the first and second locus, the frequency of the allele $b$ at the second locus is $q_b = \sum_a x_{ab}$.
