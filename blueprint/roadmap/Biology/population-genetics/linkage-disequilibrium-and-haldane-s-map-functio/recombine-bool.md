# Multi-allelic recombination specialises to the biallelic map

For every real number $r$ and every real vector $y$ indexed by the four gametes of two loci with two
alleles each, [random mating with recombination for two multi-allelic loci](recombine-2.md) gives
the same vector as [random mating with recombination](recombine.md) for two biallelic loci: both are
$y'_{ij} = (1 - r)\, y_{ij} + r\, p_i\, q_j$, with $p$ and $q$ the marginals of $y$.
