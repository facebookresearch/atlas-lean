# Pairwise linkage disequilibria of two multi-allelic loci

Let $\iota$ and $\kappa$ be finite sets of alleles at a first and a second locus, and let $x$ be a real vector indexed by $\iota \times \kappa$. When the entries of $x$ are nonnegative and sum to $1$, $x_{ij}$ is the frequency of the gamete carrying the alleles $i$ and $j$, and let its marginals $p_i = \sum_{l \in \kappa} x_{il}$ and $q_j = \sum_{k \in \iota} x_{kj}$. The linkage disequilibrium of the alleles
$i \in \iota$ and $j \in \kappa$ is

$$D_{ij} = x_{ij} - p_i\, q_j .$$

When each locus has two alleles and the entries of $x$ sum to $1$, $D_{11}$ is the
[linkage disequilibrium](linkage-diseq.md) $D = x_{AB}\, x_{ab} - x_{Ab}\, x_{aB}$ of the two-allele
case.
