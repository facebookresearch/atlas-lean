# Runs of homozygosity

Long stretches of homozygous genotypes in an individual genome, which arise when the two haplotypes descend from a common ancestor (autozygosity). Statistical calling of runs from SNP genotypes by a windowed likelihood ratio, and classification of runs by length into components that reflect ancient linkage disequilibrium, background relatedness and recent inbreeding.
