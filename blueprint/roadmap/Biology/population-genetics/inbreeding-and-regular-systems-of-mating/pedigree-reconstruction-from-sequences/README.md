# Pedigree reconstruction from sequences

Can a pedigree (the directed acyclic graph of parent–child relations in a population) be recovered from the joint distribution of the DNA sequences of its extant members? Under simple Markov models of recombination and mutation along the sequence, the distribution of an alignment is a hidden Markov model whose hidden states are spanning forests of the pedigree, and counts of spanning-forest sequences of each shape are identifiable when crossovers are rare.
