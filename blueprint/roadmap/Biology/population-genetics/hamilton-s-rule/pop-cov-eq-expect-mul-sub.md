# Covariance as mean of the product minus product of the means

For real functions `x` and `y` on a finite set of individuals, the
[population covariance](pop-cov.md) is `Cov(x, y) = 𝔼 i, x i * y i - (𝔼 i, x i) * (𝔼 i, y i)`,
where `𝔼 i` is the average over the individuals. For an empty set of individuals every average is
`0`, and both sides are `0`.
