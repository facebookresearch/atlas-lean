# Price equation with population covariance and transmission term

For a finite set of individuals with fitnesses `w` of nonzero total, `∑ i, w i ≠ 0`, parental
traits `z` and mean offspring traits `z'` (all real, the fitnesses of any sign),
`w̄ Δz̄ = Cov(w, z) + E[w (z' - z)]`, where `w̄ = 𝔼 i, w i` is the mean fitness, `Δz̄` the
[change in mean trait](mean-trait-change.md), `Cov` the [population covariance](pop-cov.md) and
`E[w (z' - z)]` the [transmission term](transmission.md).
