# Non-reproductive workers in haplodiploid colonies

Colony-level population genetics of a worker-sterility allele in haplodiploid social insects whose queens mate with $n$ males. Exact invasion and evolutionary-stability conditions depend on the colony reproductive rate $r_z$ and the queen's share $p_z$ of males as functions of the fraction $z$ of non-reproductive workers, rather than on relatedness alone.
