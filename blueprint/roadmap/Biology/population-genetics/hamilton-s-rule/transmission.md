# Transmission term of the Price equation

For a finite set of individuals with fitnesses `w i`, parental traits `z i` and mean offspring
traits `z' i` (all real), the transmission term is `E[w (z' - z)] = 𝔼 i, w i * (z' i - z i)`, the
average over the individuals of fitness times the change from parental to offspring trait. It is
`0` for an empty set of individuals.
