# Population covariance

For real functions `x` and `y` on a finite set `ι` of individuals, the population covariance is
`Cov(x, y) = 𝔼 i, (x i - x̄) * (y i - ȳ)`, where `𝔼 i` is the average over `ι` (divisor `|ι|`:
population, not sample, moments), `x̄ = 𝔼 i, x i` and `ȳ = 𝔼 i, y i`. The population variance of
`z` is `Var(z) = Cov(z, z)`. For an empty set of individuals every average, and so the covariance,
is `0`. It is the covariance term of the
[Price equation](../natural-selection-fisher-s-theorem-and-the-price/price.md).
