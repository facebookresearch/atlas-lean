# With zero trait variance the response is the transmission term alone

For a finite set of individuals with fitnesses `w` of nonzero total, `∑ i, w i ≠ 0`, parental
traits `z` of zero variance, `Var(z) = 0`, and mean offspring traits `z'` (all real),
`w̄ Δz̄ = E[w (z' - z)]`, where `w̄ = 𝔼 i, w i` is the mean fitness, `Δz̄` the
[change in mean trait](mean-trait-change.md), `Var` the [population variance](pop-cov.md) and
`E[w (z' - z)]` the [transmission term](transmission.md). This holds for any fitnesses, additive or
not: there is no selection on the trait. The [regression relatedness](relatedness.md) is then `0`
by the convention `x / 0 = 0`, and Hamilton's rule, which needs `Var(z) ≠ 0`, does not apply.
