# A constant transmission bias contributes its size times mean fitness

For fitnesses `w` and parental traits `z` on a finite set of individuals and a real `δ`, if the
mean offspring traits are `z' i = z i + δ` for every `i` (a constant transmission bias, for example
from biased mutation), the [transmission term](transmission.md) is `E[w (z' - z)] = δ w̄`, where
`w̄ = 𝔼 i, w i` is the mean fitness.
