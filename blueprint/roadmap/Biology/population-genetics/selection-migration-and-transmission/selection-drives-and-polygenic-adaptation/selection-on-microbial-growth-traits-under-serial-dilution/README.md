# Selection on microbial growth traits under serial dilution

In a serial-dilution evolution experiment a batch culture grows until a single limiting resource is exhausted, is diluted by a fixed factor into fresh medium, and the cycle repeats. A minimal model in which strains interact only through competition for the resource expresses selection, fixation and the evolved covariation of traits in terms of three measurable growth traits: lag time, exponential growth rate and yield.
