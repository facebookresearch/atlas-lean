# SAS-CFF model with demography

Gillespie's stochastic additive scale and concave fitness function (SAS-CFF) model of balancing selection in a randomly fluctuating environment, extended with density-dependent population size. Stochastic persistence theory, applied through invasion growth rates of rare alleles and of the population at low density, decides when environmental noise maintains allelic diversity and when it also keeps the population from extinction.
