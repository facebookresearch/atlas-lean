# Selection against introgressed ancestry

After a pulse of admixture, introgressed alleles that are deleterious in the recipient population are purged, and neutral introgressed alleles linked to them are purged with them until recombination moves them onto a recipient background. These articles give the expected frequency of a linked neutral introgressed allele in discrete and continuous time, its long-run limit (the gene flow factor), and the genome-wide model that averages over the position of the nearest selected site to estimate the strength of selection from introgressed-ancestry maps.
