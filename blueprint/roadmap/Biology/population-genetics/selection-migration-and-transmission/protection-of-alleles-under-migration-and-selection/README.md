# Protection of alleles under migration and selection

Conditions under which a rare allele must increase in a subdivided population with spatially varying viability selection and an arbitrary backward migration matrix, obtained by linearizing the migration–selection recursion at a monomorphic state and analyzing the Perron–Frobenius eigenvalue of the migration-weighted fitness matrix.
