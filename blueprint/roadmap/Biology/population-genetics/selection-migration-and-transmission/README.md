# Selection, migration and transmission

These topics distinguish one-locus deterministic recurrences, spatial selection, finite-population linkage interference, modifiers of transmission, the structure of fitness landscapes, incompatibilities between diverged genomes in hybrids, sexual selection together with conflicts over transmission, the deterministic recombination and selection–recombination equations solved by ancestral partitioning processes, and the fitness trajectory of long-term adaptation under diminishing-returns epistasis. This reading order does not assert a common model or a mathematical dependency.
