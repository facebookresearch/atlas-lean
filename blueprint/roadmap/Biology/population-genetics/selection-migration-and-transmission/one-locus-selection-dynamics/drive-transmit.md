# Transmission under meiotic drive

At a locus with two alleles $A$ and $a$, with genotypes taken as ordered pairs (maternal allele,
paternal allele), let $c$ be a probability distribution on the two alleles and $k = c(A)$. Under
meiotic drive with law $c$, acting in both sexes, a homozygote transmits its own allele, and a
heterozygote, $(A, a)$ or $(a, A)$, transmits an allele drawn from $c$: $A$ with probability $k$ and
$a$ with probability $1 - k$. For the uniform distribution, $k = 1/2$, this is
[Mendel's law of segregation](../../hardy-weinberg-law/transmit.md).
