# Allele-frequency map under meiotic drive

For real numbers $k$ and $p$, with $q = 1 - p$, the allele-frequency map of meiotic drive is
$p \mapsto p' = p^2 + 2pq\,k$. When heterozygotes transmit $A$ with probability $k$ and $p$ is the
frequency of $A$ among newborns in Hardy–Weinberg proportions, $p'$ is its frequency among the newborns
of the next generation
([Allele frequency change under meiotic drive without viability differences](meiotic-drive-without-viability-differences.md)).
