# Genotypes of the next generation under meiotic drive

Let the newborns of a generation have a probability distribution $G$ of ordered genotypes over two
alleles $A$ and $a$, and let parents transmit by [transmission under meiotic drive](drive-transmit.md)
with law $c$. With no viability differences the adults also have the genotype distribution $G$; each
parent transmits an allele by meiotic drive, and gametes unite at random. So the genotype of a newborn
of the next generation is an ordered pair of independent alleles, each drawn from the gamete law: the
allele transmitted by a parent drawn from $G$.
