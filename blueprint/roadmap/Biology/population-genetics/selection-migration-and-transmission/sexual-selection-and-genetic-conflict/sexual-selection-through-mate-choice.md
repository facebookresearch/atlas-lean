# Sexual selection through mate choice

The [normalized preference-kernel model](female-choice-with-a-normalized-preference-kerne.md)
gives the joint parent-type law and both parental contributions, with
neutral preferences, a common preference kernel and pair-specific fertility
as explicit cases. It does not identify the selected fathers' trait
frequencies with offspring trait frequencies without a transmission rule.
The [linear preference–trait model](runaway-criterion-in-a-linear-model-of-female-pr.md)
is a separate model for changes in two quantitative means.

## Expansion

The finite kernel's fertility effects are now explicit. The remaining
contract specifies a joint preference/trait inheritance law, recombination
and any direct viability cost of preference, then derives offspring
frequencies or moments. An assertion that preference changes only through
association with the trait requires excluding direct selection and other
transmission effects; it is not a conclusion of the kernel formula alone.
Lande's abstract concerns polygenic preferences, stabilizing male selection,
initial conditions and drift along equilibrium lines; reproducing that
model requires its full equations, not just the kernel specialization.
