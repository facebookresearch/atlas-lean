# Fisherian runaway

Fisherian runaway concerns joint evolution of preference and trait despite
stabilizing selection on the trait. The [explicit linear discrete model](runaway-criterion-in-a-linear-model-of-female-pr.md)
has a line of equilibria, a contracting regime, a same-direction unstable
regime and an alternating unstable regime. A state exactly on the line
remains fixed even when the line is unstable. The signed displacement and
its unsigned distance are not interchangeable.

## Expansion

Nonlinear preference saturation or evolving genetic variances require a
separate state space and recurrence. A local unstable eigenvalue there does
not prove globally unbounded trait growth. Lande's abstract supports
parameter- and initial-state-dependent outcomes and drift along a line,
not every global consequence of this chosen linear model.

## Sources

- R. Lande, "Models of speciation by sexual selection on polygenic traits", PNAS 78 (1981), 3721--3725, <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.78.6.3721>, abstract.
