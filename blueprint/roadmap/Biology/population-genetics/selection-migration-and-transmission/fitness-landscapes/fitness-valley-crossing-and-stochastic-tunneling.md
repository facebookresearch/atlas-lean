# Fitness-valley crossing and stochastic tunneling

This page covers the crossing of fitness valleys, a topic whose results depend on the model chosen. A population at a fitness peak reaches a fitter genotype that differs from it by several mutations, through intermediates of lower fitness, either by sequential fixation, in which an intermediate first fixes, or by stochastic tunneling, in which the fitter genotype arises within a lineage of intermediates that never fixes. The two are different mechanisms with different claims.

## Expansion
