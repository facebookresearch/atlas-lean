# Fitness landscapes as mathematical objects

This page covers fitness landscapes as explicit mathematical objects, a topic whose results depend on the model chosen. A fitness landscape consists of a finite genotype space, a mutational-neighbour graph on it, and a fitness function on the genotypes, possibly indexed by the environment. Its local optima are the genotypes whose fitness is at least that of every neighbour, and its global optima those of maximal fitness.
