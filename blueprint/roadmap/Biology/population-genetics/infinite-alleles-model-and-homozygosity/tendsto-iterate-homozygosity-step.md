# Convergence of homozygosity to equilibrium

For every natural number $M \ge 1$, every real $0 < u \le 1$ and every real $F_0$, the iterates of the [homozygosity recursion](homozygosity-step.md) with parameters $M$ and $u$ starting from $F_0$ converge to the [equilibrium homozygosity](eq-homozygosity.md) $F^*$.
