# Shannon entropy bounds from ranked abundances

Sharp upper and lower bounds on the Shannon entropy of a species-abundance or allele-frequency vector when the frequency of its most abundant, or its i-th most abundant, class is fixed. The bounds show how much of a diversity value is forced by a few dominant classes.
