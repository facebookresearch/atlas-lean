# Kimura–Crow limit

For every real $\theta > 0$, the [equilibrium homozygosity](eq-homozygosity.md) with $M = 2N$ gene copies and mutation probability $u = \theta/(4N)$ tends to $1/(1 + \theta)$ as the natural number $N$ tends to infinity.

## Sources

- M. Kimura and J. F. Crow, *The number of alleles that can be maintained in a finite population*, Genetics 49 (1964), 725–738.
