# Product measure of independent exponential clocks

For a sample size `n` and a real `θ`, the clock measure is the product, over the clocks `i`
(two per level `2 ≤ k ≤ n`), of the exponential laws with the [clock rates](killing-rate.md)
`λ_i`, a measure on the real families `(t_i)` indexed by the clocks. The exponential law of a rate
`λ ≤ 0` is taken to be the zero measure; the intended domain is `θ > 0`, where it is a
[probability measure](is-probability-measure-killing-measure.md) under which the coordinates are
independent exponential clocks.
