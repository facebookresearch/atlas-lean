# Allele-count configuration of an allelic partition

Let `π` be the allelic partition of a sample of `n` genes: an integer partition of `n` with one
part per allele, the part being the number of genes carrying that allele. Its allele-count
configuration is `a_j`, the number of parts of `π` equal to `j`, for every natural number `j`: the
number of alleles carried by exactly `j` genes of the sample. It is `0` for `j = 0` and for
`j > n`.
