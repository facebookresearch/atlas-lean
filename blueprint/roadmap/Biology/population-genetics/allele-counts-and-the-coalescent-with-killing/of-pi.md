# A coalescent with killing on the product of exponential clocks

For every sample size `n` and every real `θ > 0`, under the
[product measure of the exponential clocks](killing-measure.md), the coordinate functions form a
[coalescent with killing](killing-coalescent.md): `C k` is the coordinate of the coalescence clock
and `M k` that of the mutation clock of level `k` for `2 ≤ k ≤ n`, and both are `0` for other `k`.
So coalescents with killing exist for every sample size and every `θ > 0`.
