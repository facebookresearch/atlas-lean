# Clock rates of the coalescent with killing

For a sample size `n` and a real `θ`, the clocks of the
[coalescent with killing](killing-coalescent.md) are indexed by two copies of the levels
`2 ≤ k ≤ n`, one for the mutation clocks and one for the coalescence clocks. The rate of the
mutation clock of level `k` is `k θ / 2`, and the rate of its coalescence clock is
`k (k - 1) / 2`, the number of pairs of lineages.
