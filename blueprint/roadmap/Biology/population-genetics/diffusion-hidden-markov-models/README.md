# Diffusion hidden Markov models

Hidden Markov models whose latent signal is a population-genetic diffusion (a Wright-Fisher or Poisson-Dirichlet allele-frequency process) observed through discrete samples at a few times. Duality with coalescent death processes makes the predictive, filtering and smoothing laws finite or countable mixtures of conjugate laws, with explicit large-sample behaviour.
