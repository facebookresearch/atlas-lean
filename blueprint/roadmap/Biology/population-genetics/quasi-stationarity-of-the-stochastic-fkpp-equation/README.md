# Quasi-stationarity of the stochastic FKPP equation

The stochastic FKPP equation with Wright-Fisher noise is the continuum-space limit of a spatial population with local drift and selection. On a circle every solution eventually fixes at $0$ or $1$; conditioned on non-fixation it converges to a unique quasi-stationary distribution, the fixation time has a geometric tail, and in the neutral case the rate is explicit through a two-particle coalescing Brownian dual.
