# Seed-bank diffusions with interaction rules

Wright-Fisher models in which offspring types result from interactions among several potential parents (rock-paper-scissors, tournaments, majority voting) and parents may be drawn from past generations (dormancy). Their scaling limits are coupled active/seed-bank diffusions in which the seed bank acts as a temporal refuge for cyclic competition.
