# Diploid selection strictly increases mean fitness off equilibria

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of alleles, $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ allele frequencies with $p_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_i p_i = 1$, and $w_{ij} \ge 0$ genotype fitnesses with $\bar w(p) > 0$, and let $p'$ be the allele frequencies after one generation of viability selection, $p'_i = p_i m_i / \bar w(p)$. If $p' \ne p$, then $\bar w(p) < \bar w(p')$.
