# Mean fitness never decreases

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of alleles, $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ allele frequencies with $p_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_i p_i = 1$, and $w_{ij} \ge 0$ genotype fitnesses with $\bar w(p) = \sum_{i,j} p_i p_j w_{ij} > 0$. Let $p'_i = p_i m_i / \bar w(p)$, with $m_i = \sum_j \frac{w_{ij} + w_{ji}}{2} p_j$, be the allele frequencies after one generation of viability selection with random mating. Then $\bar w(p) \le \bar w(p')$.
