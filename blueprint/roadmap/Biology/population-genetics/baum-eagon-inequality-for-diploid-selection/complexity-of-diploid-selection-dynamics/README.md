# Complexity of diploid selection dynamics

The single-locus, multi-allele diploid selection model is a discrete replicator dynamics on a symmetric coordination game. Its stable fixed points are the local maxima of mean fitness, and deciding whether a stable polymorphism (genetic diversity) exists is computationally intractable, although diversity survives for a constant fraction of random fitness landscapes.
