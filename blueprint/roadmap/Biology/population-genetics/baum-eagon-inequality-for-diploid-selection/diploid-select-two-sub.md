# Wright's formula

Let $w$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix of genotype fitnesses and $x$ a real number with $\bar w(x) \ne 0$, where $\bar w(y)$ is the mean fitness at the allele frequencies $(y, 1 - y)$. Then one generation of viability selection changes the frequency of allele $0$ by

$$p'_0 - x = \frac{x(1 - x)\, \bar w'(x)}{2\, \bar w(x)},$$

where $p'_0 = x\, m_0 / \bar w(x)$ and $m_0$ is the marginal fitness of allele $0$. The identity is algebraic and holds for every real $x$ with $\bar w(x) \ne 0$; the model domain is $x \in [0, 1]$ with nonnegative fitnesses.

## Sources

- S. Wright, *The distribution of gene frequencies in populations*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 23 (1937), 307–320.
