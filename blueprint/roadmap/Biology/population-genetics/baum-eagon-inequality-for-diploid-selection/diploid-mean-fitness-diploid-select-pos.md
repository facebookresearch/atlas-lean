# Mean fitness stays positive under diploid selection

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of alleles, $p \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with $p_i \ge 0$ for every $i$, and $w_{ij} \ge 0$ genotype fitnesses with positive mean fitness $\bar w(p) = \sum_{i,j} p_i p_j w_{ij} > 0$. Let $p'_i = p_i m_i / \bar w(p)$, with the marginal fitnesses $m_i = \sum_j \frac{w_{ij} + w_{ji}}{2} p_j$, be the allele frequencies after one generation of viability selection with random mating. Then $\bar w(p') > 0$. The frequencies need not sum to $1$.
