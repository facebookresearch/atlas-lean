# Hardy–Weinberg frequency $q^2$ of the aa homozygote

For a probability distribution $G$ of ordered genotypes over two alleles $A$ and $a$, the [offspring genotype law](offspring.md) gives the homozygote $aa$ the probability $q^2$, where $q$ is the frequency of $a$ in the [law of a random gamete](gamete.md) of $G$.
