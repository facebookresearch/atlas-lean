# Hardy–Weinberg proportions

For a set $A$ of alleles, a probability distribution $G$ on $A \times A$ and alleles $a, b$, the [offspring genotype law](offspring.md) gives $(a, b)$ the probability $p(a)\, p(b)$, where $p$ is the [law of a random gamete](gamete.md) of $G$: after one generation of random mating the genotype law is the product of the parental allele frequencies.

## Sources

- G. H. Hardy, *Mendelian proportions in a mixed population*, Science 28 (1908), 49–50.
- W. Weinberg, *Über den Nachweis der Vererbung beim Menschen*, Jahreshefte des Vereins für vaterländische Naturkunde in Württemberg 64 (1908).
