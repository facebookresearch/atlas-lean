# Law of a random gamete

For a set $A$ of alleles and a probability distribution $G$ of ordered genotypes on $A \times A$, the law of a random gamete is the probability distribution on $A$ obtained by drawing a parent genotype from $G$ and then one of its two alleles by [Mendel's law of segregation](transmit.md). Its mass at $a$ is the frequency of the allele $a$ ([Gamete law equals the allele frequency](gamete-apply.md)).
