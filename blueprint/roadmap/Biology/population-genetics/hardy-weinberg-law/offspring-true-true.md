# Hardy–Weinberg frequency $p^2$ of the AA homozygote

For a probability distribution $G$ of ordered genotypes over two alleles $A$ and $a$, the [offspring genotype law](offspring.md) gives the homozygote $AA$ the probability $p^2$, where $p$ is the frequency of $A$ in the [law of a random gamete](gamete.md) of $G$.
