# Gamete law equals the allele frequency

For a finite set $A$ of alleles, a probability distribution $G$ on $A \times A$ and an allele $a$, the [law of a random gamete](gamete.md) gives $a$ the probability

$$\frac{1}{2} \sum_{b \in A} G(a, b) + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{b \in A} G(b, a),$$

half the expected number of copies of $a$ in a parental genotype.
