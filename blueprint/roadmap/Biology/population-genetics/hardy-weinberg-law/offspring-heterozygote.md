# Hardy–Weinberg frequency $2pq$ of the heterozygote

For a probability distribution $G$ of ordered genotypes over two alleles $A$ and $a$, the [offspring genotype law](offspring.md) gives the two ordered heterozygotes $(A, a)$ and $(a, A)$ the total probability $2pq$, where $p$ and $q$ are the frequencies of $A$ and $a$ in the [law of a random gamete](gamete.md) of $G$.
