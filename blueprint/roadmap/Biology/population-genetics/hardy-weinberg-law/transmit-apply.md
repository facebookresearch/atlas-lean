# Allele transmission law of a parental genotype

For a set $A$ of alleles, a genotype $g = (g_1, g_2)$ and an allele $a$, the [transmission law](transmit.md) of $g$ gives $a$ the probability $\frac{1}{2}[g_1 = a] + \frac{1}{2}[g_2 = a]$: mass $1/2$ on each of the two alleles of $g$, and mass $1$ on $a$ for the homozygote $(a, a)$.
