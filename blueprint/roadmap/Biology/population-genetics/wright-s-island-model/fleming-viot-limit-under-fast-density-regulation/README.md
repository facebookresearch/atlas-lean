# Fleming–Viot limit under fast density regulation

Spatial or trait-structured multi-type populations whose birth and death rates regulate total density on a fast time scale while individuals move slowly. In the limit the density is pinned at its equilibrium and the spatial distribution follows a Fleming–Viot process whose mutation, selection and resampling terms are averages weighted by the equilibrium left eigenvector.
