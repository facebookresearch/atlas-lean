# Equilibrium $F_{ST}$ of Wright's island model

For a natural number $M$ and a real number $m$, let

$$F^* = \frac{(1 - m)^2}{M - (M - 1)(1 - m)^2},$$

with the convention that division by $0$ gives $0$. Its denominator is positive for $M \ge 1$ and $0 \le m \le 1$; for $M \ge 2$ and $0 \le m \le 1$ it is the equilibrium identity-by-descent probability, Wright's $F_{ST}$, of the island model.
