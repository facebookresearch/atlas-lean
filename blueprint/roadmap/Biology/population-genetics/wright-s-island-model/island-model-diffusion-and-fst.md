# The island-model diffusion and $F_{ST}$

This page covers a research target: to construct the diffusion of allele frequencies in Wright's island model with $d$ demes and to derive from its moment equations the equilibrium $F_{ST}$ with its corrections for finite $d$, connecting it with [the identity-by-descent recursion](island-step.md), with Wright's limit [$F_{ST} = 1/(1 + 4Nm)$](tendsto-island-equilibrium.md), and with [Slatkin's $F_{ST}$ from coalescence times](../../coalescent-theory/structured-coalescent/slatkin-fst.md).

## Expansion

Construct the $d$-deme diffusion, with hypotheses fixed from a source, derive the moment equations, then state the finite-$d$ value of $F_{ST}$ and the limit regime, each as a page of its own.

Prerequisites:

- [Identity-by-descent recursion in Wright's island model](island-step.md)
- [Wright's $F_{ST} = 1/(1 + 4Nm)$](tendsto-island-equilibrium.md)
- [Slatkin's $F_{ST}$](../../coalescent-theory/structured-coalescent/slatkin-fst.md)
