# Geometric approach to equilibrium

For every natural number $M \ge 1$, every real $0 \le m \le 1$, every real $F_0$ and every natural number $t$, the $t$-th iterate $F_t$ of the [island-model recursion](island-step.md) satisfies

$$F_t - F^* = \Bigl((1 - m)^2 \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{M}\Bigr)\Bigr)^{t} (F_0 - F^*),$$

with the [equilibrium](island-equilibrium.md) $F^*$.
