# Support- and length-weighted quartet scores

Summary methods estimate a species tree as the tree sharing the most quartets with a collection of gene trees. Weighting each gene-tree quartet by the statistical support of its internal path, by the shortness of its terminal paths, or by both, down-weights quartets likely to be wrong. The weighted score keeps statistical consistency under the multispecies coalescent and extends it to models of biased gene-tree error under which the unweighted score fails.
