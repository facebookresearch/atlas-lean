# Wald space and information geometry of phylogenetic trees

A space of phylogenetic forests in which trees are close when they induce similar distributions on character data: the two-state symmetric substitution process on a tree, the Fisher information metric it induces (locally any $f$-divergence), and the embedding of forests into covariance matrices through a Gaussian process on the tree (Garba, Nye, Lueg and Huckemann 2021).
