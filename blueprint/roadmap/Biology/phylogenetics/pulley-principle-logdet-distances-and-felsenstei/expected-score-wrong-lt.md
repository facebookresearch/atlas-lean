# The Felsenstein zone

Let $0 \le Q < \frac12$ and $Q(1 - Q) < P^2$. With change parameters $P$ on the branches to $A$ and $C$ and $Q$ on the other three branches, the [expected parsimony score](expected-score.md) of the wrong quartet $AC \mid BD$ is strictly smaller than that of the true quartet $AB \mid CD$ and strictly smaller than that of the third quartet $AD \mid BC$ (Felsenstein 1978).

## Sources

- J. Felsenstein, *Cases in which parsimony or compatibility methods will be positively misleading*, Syst. Zool. 27 (1978), 401–410.
