# Additivity of the LogDet distance along a path

Let $p_i > 0$ for every $i$ and let $M_0, M_1, \dots$ be real square matrices with nonnegative entries, unit row sums and nonzero determinants. Then for every natural number $n$,

$$d_{LD}\bigl(\operatorname{diag}(p)\, \Pi_n\bigr) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} d_{LD}\bigl(\operatorname{diag}(p\, \Pi_i)\, M_i\bigr),$$

where $d_{LD}$ is the [LogDet distance](paralinear.md) and $\Pi_i$ the [product along the path](path-prod.md) (Steel 1994; Lockhart et al. 1994).

## Sources

- M. A. Steel, *Recovering a tree from the leaf colourations it generates under a Markov model*, Appl. Math. Lett. 7 (1994), 19–23.
- P. J. Lockhart, M. A. Steel, M. D. Hendy and D. Penny, *Recovering evolutionary trees under a more realistic model of sequence evolution*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 11 (1994), 605–612.
