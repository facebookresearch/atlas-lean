# Probability of a site pattern on a quartet

For reals $p_A, p_B, p_C, p_D, p_I$ and a site pattern $\sigma$, the pattern probability is the [site likelihood](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/likelihood.md) of the [quartet](quartet.md) with the [leaf weights](site-obs.md) of $\sigma$ and the uniform root distribution $(\frac12, \frac12)$.
