# Pulley principle, sliding

Let $(P_t)$ be a [reversible semigroup](is-reversible-semigroup.md) for $\pi$, and let $s, t, s', t'$ be real with $s + t = s' + t'$. Then for all real leaf weights and trees $l, r$, the [site likelihood](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/likelihood.md) of the root with children $l, r$ on edges $P_s, P_t$ equals that with edges $P_{s'}, P_{t'}$ (Felsenstein 1981).

## Sources

- J. Felsenstein, *Evolutionary trees from DNA sequences: a maximum likelihood approach*, J. Mol. Evol. 17 (1981), 368–376.
