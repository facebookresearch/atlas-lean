# JC-corrected distances are path lengths

For every real $\mu$, every list $t_1, \dots, t_k$ and every base $i$,

$$d_{JC}\Bigl(\sum_{j \ne i} M_{ij}\Bigr) = \mu (t_1 + \cdots + t_k),$$

where $M$ is the [path matrix](jc-path.md) and $d_{JC}$ the [Jukes–Cantor distance](../jukes-cantor-model/jc-distance.md) (Jukes & Cantor 1969).

## Sources

- T. H. Jukes and C. R. Cantor, *Evolution of protein molecules*, in Mammalian Protein Metabolism, Academic Press, 1969, 21–132.
