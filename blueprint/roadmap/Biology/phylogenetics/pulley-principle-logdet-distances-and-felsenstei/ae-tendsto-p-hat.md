# Strong law for the p-distances

Let $X$ be a finite set of taxa, $P$ a probability measure, and let the sites $\mathrm{site}_0, \mathrm{site}_1, \dots$ (random maps from $X$ to the four bases) be measurable, pairwise independent and identically distributed. Then almost surely, for all taxa $x, y$, the [empirical p-distance](p-hat.md) of the observed alignment converges to $\Pr(\mathrm{site}_0(x) \ne \mathrm{site}_0(y))$.
