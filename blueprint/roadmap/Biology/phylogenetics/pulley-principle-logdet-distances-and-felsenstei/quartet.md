# Four-taxon quartet tree model

For reals $p_A, p_B, p_C, p_D, p_I$, the quartet $((A, B), (C, D))$ on the taxa $0 = A$, $1 = B$, $2 = C$, $3 = D$ is the [tree with transition weights](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/tree.md) whose root has a child $u$ on an edge with the [two-state model](two-state.md) of parameter $0$ and a child $v$ on an edge with parameter $p_I$, where $u$ has the leaves $A, B$ on edges with parameters $p_A, p_B$ and $v$ the leaves $C, D$ on edges with parameters $p_C, p_D$.
