# Wrong split against true split frequency difference

For all real $P, Q$, with change parameters $P$ on the branches to $A$ and $C$ and $Q$ on the branches to $B$ and $D$ and on the internal branch,

$$f(AC \mid BD) - f(AB \mid CD) = (1 - 2Q)\bigl(P^2 - Q(1 - Q)\bigr),$$

where $f$ is the [expected split frequency](split-freq.md) (Felsenstein 1978).

## Sources

- J. Felsenstein, *Cases in which parsimony or compatibility methods will be positively misleading*, Syst. Zool. 27 (1978), 401–410.
