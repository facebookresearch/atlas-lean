# Fitch parsimony score on a quartet

For taxa $a, b, c, d$ and a site pattern $\sigma$, the parsimony score is the score computed by [Fitch's algorithm](../small-parsimony-problem/fitch.md) for $\sigma$ on the [rooted quartet](quartet-shape.md) $((a, b), (c, d))$; it equals the small-parsimony score.
