# Expected Fitch parsimony score on a quartet

For reals $p_A, p_B, p_C, p_D, p_I$ and taxa $a, b, c, d$, the expected score is $\sum_\sigma \Pr(\sigma)\, s_{ab|cd}(\sigma)$, the sum over the site patterns of the [pattern probability](pattern-prob.md) times the [parsimony score](parsimony-score.md) on $((a, b), (c, d))$.
