# Level-1 networks from triplets and clusters

Binary level-1 phylogenetic networks, in which every biconnected component of the underlying graph carries at most one reticulation, and the question of how much of their induced triplet system or softwired cluster system is needed to recover them uniquely. Includes size bounds for such networks and their cluster systems and uniqueness results for simple and for 4-outwards saturated networks.
