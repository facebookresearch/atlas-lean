# HKY85 rate matrix

For real base frequencies $\pi$ on the four nucleotides and a real $\kappa$, the HKY85 rate matrix is the [GTR matrix](gtr-rate.md) with the [HKY85 exchangeabilities](hky-exchangeability.md): rate $\kappa \pi_j$ for a transition to $j$ and $\pi_j$ for a transversion (Hasegawa–Kishino–Yano 1985).

## Sources

- M. Hasegawa, H. Kishino and T. Yano, *Dating of the human–ape splitting by a molecular clock of mitochondrial DNA*, J. Mol. Evol. 22 (1985), 160–174.
