# K80 rate matrix

For a real $\kappa$, the K80 rate matrix is the [HKY85 matrix](hky-rate.md) with base frequencies $\frac14$: rate $\kappa/4$ for a transition and $1/4$ for each transversion (Kimura 1980).

## Sources

- M. Kimura, *A simple method for estimating evolutionary rates of base substitutions through comparative studies of nucleotide sequences*, J. Mol. Evol. 16 (1980), 111–120.
