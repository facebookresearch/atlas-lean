# General time-reversible (GTR) rate matrix

For a finite alphabet $I$, real base frequencies $\pi_i$ and a real matrix $s$ of exchangeabilities, the GTR rate matrix is

$$Q_{ij} = s_{ij} \pi_j \ (i \ne j), \qquad Q_{ii} = -\sum_{k \ne i} s_{ik} \pi_k$$

(Tavaré 1986). The diagonal of $s$ is not used.

## Sources

- S. Tavaré, *Some probabilistic and statistical problems in the analysis of DNA sequences*, Lectures on Mathematics in the Life Sciences 17 (1986), 57–86.
