# Equal-rates GTR chain is the Jukes–Cantor model

For all real $\mu, t$ and nucleotides $i, j$, the entry $(i, j)$ of $\exp(tQ)$, where $Q$ is the [GTR matrix](gtr-rate.md) with base frequencies $\frac14$ and exchangeabilities $\frac{4\mu}{3}$, is the [Jukes–Cantor transition probability](../jukes-cantor-model/jc-prob.md) $P_{ij}(t)$ with rate $\mu$.
