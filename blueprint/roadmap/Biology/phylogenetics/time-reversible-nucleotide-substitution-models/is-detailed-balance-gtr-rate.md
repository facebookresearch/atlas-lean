# GTR is reversible

If $s$ is symmetric, then for all real $\pi$ the [GTR matrix](gtr-rate.md) satisfies $\pi_i Q_{ij} = \pi_j Q_{ji}$ for all $i, j$ (Tavaré 1986).

## Sources

- S. Tavaré, *Some probabilistic and statistical problems in the analysis of DNA sequences*, Lectures on Mathematics in the Life Sciences 17 (1986), 57–86.
