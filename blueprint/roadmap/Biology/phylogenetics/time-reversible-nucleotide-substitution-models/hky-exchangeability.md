# HKY85 exchangeabilities

On the nucleotides $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ ($A, C, G, T$), for a real $\kappa$, the HKY85 exchangeabilities are $s_{ij} = \kappa$ if $i$ and $j$ have the same parity (a transition $A \leftrightarrow G$ or $C \leftrightarrow T$, including $i = j$) and $s_{ij} = 1$ otherwise (a transversion).
