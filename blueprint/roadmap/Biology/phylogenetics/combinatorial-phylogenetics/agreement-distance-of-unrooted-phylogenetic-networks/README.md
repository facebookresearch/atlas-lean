# Agreement distance of unrooted phylogenetic networks

Maximum agreement graphs generalize maximum agreement forests from unrooted phylogenetic trees to proper unrooted binary phylogenetic networks. The induced metrics, the agreement distance and the endpoint agreement distance, bound the TBR- and PR-distances between networks within constant factors.
