# Infinitary species in genealogical graphs

Under the idealization of an eternal biosphere, the genealogical graph of all organisms is infinite. Infinite ancestrally closed sets of organisms (infinitary genera) and their minimal elements (infinitary species) have several ideal properties of species, and the Knight–Darwin law on occasional outcrossing dualizes inside them.
