# Variance of leaf depths as a balance index

Sackin's original suggestion for measuring tree shape: the spread of the leaves' depths. The variance of leaf depths is maximized exactly by the comb, but among bifurcating trees its minimizers are, for almost all leaf numbers, not the maximally balanced trees; its expectation has closed forms under the Yule and uniform models.
