# Counting serially sampled ranked trees

How many ranked genealogies are compatible with samples taken at several times, as in time-stamped virus or ancient-DNA data? Counting backwards in time through sampling epochs gives recursions for fully ranked trees, and for trees in which sampled individuals may be direct ancestors of later samples, computable in time linear in the number of epochs times the number of samples.
