# The row marginal of the pair-site law is the base-frequency vector

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. If `(π, s)` lies in the [GTR parameter domain](is-gtrparam.md),
`π i > 0` for every `i`, `∑_i π i = 1`, `s` symmetric and `s i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`, the diagonal of
`s` being free, then for every real branch length `t` and every state `i` the
[pair-site law](gtr-pair-law.md) `F(π, s, t) i j = π i · exp (t · Q(π, s)) i j` satisfies
`∑_j F(π, s, t) i j = π i`, where `Q(π, s)` is the
[GTR rate matrix](../../time-reversible-nucleotide-substitution-models/gtr-rate.md).
