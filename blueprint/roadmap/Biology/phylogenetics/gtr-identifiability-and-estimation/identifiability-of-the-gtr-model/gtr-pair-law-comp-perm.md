# Relabeling equivariance of the pair-site law

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. For all real base frequencies `π`, exchangeabilities `s`, every
branch length `t` and every permutation `σ` of the states, relabeling the parameters as
`(π ∘ σ) i = π (σ i)` and `s_σ i j = s (σ i) (σ j)` relabels the [pair-site law](gtr-pair-law.md):
`F(π ∘ σ, s_σ, t) i j = F(π, s, t) (σ i) (σ j)` for all `i`, `j`, where
`F(π, s, t) i j = π i · exp (t · Q(π, s)) i j` and `Q(π, s)` is the
[GTR rate matrix](../../time-reversible-nucleotide-substitution-models/gtr-rate.md).
