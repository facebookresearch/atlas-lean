# The transition function determines the generator

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. If two real `ι × ι` matrices `Q` and `Q'` have the same
exponentials `exp (t · Q) = exp (t · Q')` at every time `t ≥ 0`, then `Q = Q'`: the generator is the
right derivative at `0` of its
[transition matrix](../../../../Probability/markov-chains/finite-continuous-time-markov-chains/the-transition-semigroup-of-a-finite-continuous/ctmc-transition.md)
function `t ↦ exp (t · Q)`. No rate-matrix condition is needed.
