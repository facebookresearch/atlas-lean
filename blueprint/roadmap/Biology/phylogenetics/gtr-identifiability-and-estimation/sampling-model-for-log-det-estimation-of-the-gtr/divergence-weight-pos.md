# Positive GTR divergence weights at positive time

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Let `π i > 0` for every `i`, let `s` be a real `ι × ι` matrix
with `s i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`, and let `t > 0`. Then every site pair has positive
[divergence weight](../identifiability-of-the-gtr-model/gtr-pair-law.md)
`w(i, j) = π i · exp (t · Q) i j`: `w(a) > 0` for every `a ∈ ι × ι`, where `Q` is the
[GTR rate matrix](../../time-reversible-nucleotide-substitution-models/gtr-rate.md) with base
frequencies `π` and exchangeabilities `s`. The base frequencies need not sum to `1`, so the weights
need not form a probability law.
