# Law of an i.i.d. sequence of GTR site pairs

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. For real `π`, `s` and `t` with
[GTR divergence law](divergence-law.md) `μ = ∑_a max (w(a), 0) · δ_a`, the sample law `S` is the
product measure of countably many copies of `μ` on the sequences `ω = (ω 0, ω 1, …)` of site pairs:
the law of an i.i.d. sequence of aligned site pairs with law `μ`. The product is formed only when
`μ` is a probability measure; otherwise `S` is the zero measure.
