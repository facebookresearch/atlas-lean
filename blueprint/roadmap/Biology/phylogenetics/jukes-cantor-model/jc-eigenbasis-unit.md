# Invertible Jukes–Cantor eigenbasis

The [eigenbasis matrix](jc-eigenbasis.md) $E$ is invertible, with inverse

$$E^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac14 & \frac14 & \frac14 & \frac14\\ \frac14 & -\frac34 & \frac14 & \frac14\\ \frac14 & \frac14 & -\frac34 & \frac14\\ \frac14 & \frac14 & \frac14 & -\frac34\end{pmatrix}.$$
