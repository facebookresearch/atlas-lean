# Right-hand side of the Jukes–Cantor forward equation in closed form

For all real $\mu, t$ and bases $i, j$,

$$\sum_k P_{ik}(t) Q_{kj} = \begin{cases} -\mu e^{-4\mu t/3} & i = j,\\ \frac{\mu}{3} e^{-4\mu t/3} & i \ne j,\end{cases}$$

where $P$ is the [transition probability](jc-prob.md) and $Q$ the [rate matrix](jc-rate.md).
