# Jukes–Cantor transition probability

For real numbers $\mu$ (the substitution rate) and $t$ (the branch length) and nucleotides $i, j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, put

$$P_{ij}(t) = \begin{cases} \frac14 + \frac34 e^{-4\mu t/3} & i = j,\\[2pt] \frac14 - \frac14 e^{-4\mu t/3} & i \ne j.\end{cases}$$

For $\mu t \ge 0$ it is the probability of base $j$ at the end of a branch given base $i$ at its start.
