# Greedy maximization of phylogenetic diversity

This node covers the following result. Let $T$ be an unrooted phylogenetic $X$-tree with nonnegative edge weights $\lambda$, and for $W \subseteq X$ let $PD(W)$ be the total weight of the minimal subtree of $T$ connecting $W$. For $k \ge 2$, the $k$-element subsets of $X$ of maximum $PD$ are exactly those obtained by choosing a pair of leaves at maximum distance in $(T, \lambda)$ and then repeatedly adding a leaf that maximizes the increase in $PD$.
