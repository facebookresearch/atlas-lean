# Splits of two tree edges are compatible

If $G$ is a tree and $\{a, b\}$, $\{c, e\}$ are edges, then one of the four inclusions holds: the $b$-side of $\{a, b\}$ is contained in the $c$-side or in the $e$-side of $\{c, e\}$, or the $a$-side of $\{a, b\}$ is contained in the $c$-side or in the $e$-side of $\{c, e\}$ (sides as in [taxa on one side of a tree edge](side.md)).
