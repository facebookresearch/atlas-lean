# On a tree metric, a pair of taxa minimizing the Q-criterion is a cherry

Let $(G, \iota)$ be a [binary phylogenetic tree](is-binary-phylogenetic-tree.md) on a finite set $X$ with $|X| \ge 4$ and edge lengths $w_e > 0$, and let $d(x, y)$ be the total length of the path from $\iota(x)$ to $\iota(y)$. If distinct taxa $x, y$ minimize the [Q-criterion](q-criterion.md) of the [initial dissimilarity](initial-dist.md) over all pairs of distinct singletons, then $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ have a common neighbour (Saitou & Nei 1987; Studier & Keppler 1988; Bryant 2005).

## Sources

- N. Saitou and M. Nei, *The neighbor-joining method: a new method for reconstructing phylogenetic trees*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 4 (1987), 406–425.
- J. A. Studier and K. J. Keppler, *A note on the neighbor-joining algorithm of Saitou and Nei*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 5 (1988), 729–731.
- D. Bryant, *On the uniqueness of the selection criterion in neighbor-joining*, J. Classif. 22 (2005), 3–15.
