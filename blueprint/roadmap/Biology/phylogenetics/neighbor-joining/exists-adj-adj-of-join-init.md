# The first join of neighbor joining on a tree metric joins a cherry

Let $(G, \iota)$, $w$ and $d$ be as in [the cherry theorem](exists-adj-adj-of-q-criterion-min.md), with at least four singleton clusters. If distinct singleton clusters $a, b$ minimize the [Q-criterion](q-criterion.md) of the [initial dissimilarity](initial-dist.md) over all pairs of distinct singletons, then $a = \{x\}$ and $b = \{y\}$ for distinct taxa $x, y$ such that $\iota(x)$ and $\iota(y)$ have a common neighbour.
