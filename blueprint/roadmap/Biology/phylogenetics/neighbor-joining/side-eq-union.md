# Edge side at an internal vertex as a disjoint union

Let $(G, \iota)$ be a [binary phylogenetic tree](is-binary-phylogenetic-tree.md), $a$ a vertex that is not a taxon vertex and $b$ adjacent to $a$. Then there are distinct neighbours $c, e$ of $a$, both different from $b$, such that the $c$-side of $\{c, a\}$ and the $e$-side of $\{e, a\}$ are disjoint and their union is the $a$-side of $\{a, b\}$ (sides as in [taxa on one side of a tree edge](side.md)).
