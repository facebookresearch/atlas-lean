# The four-gamete test

For all binary characters $\chi, \psi$ on a finite set $X$, the [splits](char-split.md) of $\chi$ and $\psi$ are [compatible](compatible.md) if and only if $\chi, \psi$ do not [show all four gametes](four-gametes.md) (Buneman 1971; Estabrook, Johnson & McMorris 1976).

## Sources

- P. Buneman, *The recovery of trees from measures of dissimilarity*, in Mathematics in the Archaeological and Historical Sciences, Edinburgh University Press, 1971, 387–395.
- G. F. Estabrook, C. S. Johnson Jr. and F. R. McMorris, *A mathematical foundation for the analysis of cladistic character compatibility*, Math. Biosci. 29 (1976), 181–187.
