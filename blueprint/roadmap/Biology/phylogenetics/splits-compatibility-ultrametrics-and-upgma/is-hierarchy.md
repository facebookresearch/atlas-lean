# Hierarchy on a finite set

A hierarchy on a finite set $S$ is a family $H$ of subsets of $S$ such that $S \in H$, every member is nonempty, $\{x\} \in H$ for every $x \in S$, and any two members $C, D$ satisfy $C \subseteq D$, $D \subseteq C$ or $C \cap D = \emptyset$. These are the cluster systems of the rooted phylogenetic trees on $S$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
