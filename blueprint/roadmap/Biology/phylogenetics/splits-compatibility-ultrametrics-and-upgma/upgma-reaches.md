# States reachable by UPGMA

For a real function $d$ on pairs of taxa of a finite set $X$, the states reachable by UPGMA are the pairs $(P, c)$ of a set $P$ of blocks and recorded heights $c(x, y)$ generated by:

- the start: $P$ is the set of singletons and $c = 0$;
- a step: from a reachable $(P, c)$ and distinct $C, D \in P$ with $\bar d(C, D) \le \bar d(E, F)$ for all distinct $E, F \in P$ (the [average linkage](avg-dist.md)), pass to $\bigl((P \setminus \{C, D\}) \cup \{C \cup D\},\ c'\bigr)$, where $c'(x, y) = \bar d(C, D)$ if one of $x, y$ is in $C$ and the other in $D$, and $c'(x, y) = c(x, y)$ otherwise

(Sokal & Michener 1958).

## Sources

- R. R. Sokal and C. D. Michener, *A statistical method for evaluating systematic relationships*, Univ. Kansas Sci. Bull. 38 (1958), 1409–1438.
