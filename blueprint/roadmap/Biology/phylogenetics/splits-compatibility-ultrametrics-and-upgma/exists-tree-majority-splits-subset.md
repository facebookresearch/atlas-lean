# The majority-rule consensus tree

Let $(T_i)_{i \in I}$ be a finite family of trees on $X$ and $a$ a taxon with $X \setminus \{a\}$ nonempty. Then some [unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md) $T_0$ has every [majority split](majority-splits.md) among its clusters seen from $a$.
