# Perfect phylogeny and the four-gamete condition

Let $X$ be a finite set with at least two elements and $M : X \times J \to \{0, 1\}$ a binary matrix. Then $M$ has a [perfect phylogeny](is-perfect-phylogeny.md) if and only if no two columns $j, k$ [show all four gametes](four-gametes.md) (Estabrook, Johnson & McMorris 1976; Gusfield 1991).

## Sources

- G. F. Estabrook, C. S. Johnson Jr. and F. R. McMorris, *A mathematical foundation for the analysis of cladistic character compatibility*, Math. Biosci. 29 (1976), 181–187.
- D. Gusfield, *Efficient algorithms for inferring evolutionary trees*, Networks 21 (1991), 19–28.
