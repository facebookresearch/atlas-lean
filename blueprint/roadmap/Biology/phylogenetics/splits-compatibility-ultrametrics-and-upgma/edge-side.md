# Side of a tree edge containing a vertex

For an [unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md) and vertices $p, v$, the $v$-side of $p\,v$ is the set of taxa $x$ whose leaf $\ell_x$ satisfies $\mathrm{dist}(\ell_x, v) < \mathrm{dist}(\ell_x, p)$.
