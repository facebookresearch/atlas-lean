# Majority splits of a family of trees

For a finite family $(T_i)_{i \in I}$ of [unrooted binary phylogenetic trees](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md) and a taxon $a$, the majority splits are the sets $A$ with

$$|I| < 2\, \#\{i \in I : A \in \mathcal{C}_a(T_i)\},$$

the clusters seen from $a$ ([cluster sets](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/cluster-set.md)) of more than half of the trees, repetitions counted.
