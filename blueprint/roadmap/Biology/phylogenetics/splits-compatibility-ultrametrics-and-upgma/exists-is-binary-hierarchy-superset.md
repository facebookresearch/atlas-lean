# Hierarchies are refined by binary trees

Let $S$ be a nonempty finite set and $F$ a family of nonempty subsets of $S$, any two of which are nested or disjoint. Then $F$ is contained in some [binary hierarchy](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/is-binary-hierarchy.md) on $S$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
