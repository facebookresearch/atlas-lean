# Ultrametrics satisfy the four-point condition

Every [ultrametric](is-ultrametric.md) $d$ satisfies the [four-point condition](../tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/four-point-condition.md): $d(x, y) + d(z, t) \le \max\bigl(d(x, z) + d(y, t),\ d(x, t) + d(y, z)\bigr)$ for all $x, y, z, t$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
