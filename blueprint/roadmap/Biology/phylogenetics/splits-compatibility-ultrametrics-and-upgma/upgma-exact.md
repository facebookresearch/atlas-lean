# UPGMA is exact on ultrametrics

Let $d$ be an [ultrametric](is-ultrametric.md) and $(P, c)$ a [state reachable by UPGMA](upgma-reaches.md) on $d$. Then for every block $B \in P$ and all $x, y \in B$, $c(x, y) = d(x, y)$. In particular a completed run, with a single block, reconstructs $d$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
