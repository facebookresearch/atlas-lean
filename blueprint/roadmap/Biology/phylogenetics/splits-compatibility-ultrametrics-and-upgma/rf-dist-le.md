# Maximum of the Robinson–Foulds distance

On a finite set $X$ with $|X| \ge 3$, for all trees $T, T'$ and every taxon $a$, the [Robinson–Foulds distance](rf-dist.md) satisfies $d_{RF}(T, T') \le 2(|X| - 3)$ (Robinson & Foulds 1981).

## Sources

- D. F. Robinson and L. R. Foulds, *Comparison of phylogenetic trees*, Math. Biosci. 53 (1981), 131–147.
