# Robinson–Foulds distance as half the split symmetric difference

For all trees $T, T'$ and every taxon $a$, $2\, d_{RF}(T, T') = |\Sigma_a(T) \,\triangle\, \Sigma_a(T')|$, where $d_{RF}$ is the [Robinson–Foulds distance](rf-dist.md) computed from $a$ and $\Sigma_a$ the [split set](split-set.md) from $a$.
