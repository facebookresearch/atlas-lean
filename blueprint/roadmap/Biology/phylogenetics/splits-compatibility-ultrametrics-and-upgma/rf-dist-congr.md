# Isomorphism invariance of the Robinson–Foulds distance

If $T_1$ and $T_2$ are [isomorphic](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/iso.md), then $d_{RF}(T_1, T') = d_{RF}(T_2, T')$ for every tree $T'$ and every taxon $a$, where $d_{RF}$ is the [Robinson–Foulds distance](rf-dist.md).
