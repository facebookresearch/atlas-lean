# Robinson–Foulds distance vanishes exactly on isomorphic trees

For all trees $T, T'$ and every taxon $a$, the [Robinson–Foulds distance](rf-dist.md) satisfies $d_{RF}(T, T') = 0$ if and only if $T$ and $T'$ are [isomorphic](../counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/iso.md) (Robinson & Foulds 1981).

## Sources

- D. F. Robinson and L. R. Foulds, *Comparison of phylogenetic trees*, Math. Biosci. 53 (1981), 131–147.
