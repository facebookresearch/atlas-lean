# Splits-equivalence theorem

Let $X$ be a finite set with at least two elements and $F$ a family of subsets $A \subseteq X$ with $A$ and $X \setminus A$ both nonempty. Then there is an [unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md) $T$ on $X$ whose [edge splits](edge-splits.md) contain $F$ if and only if any two members of $F$ are [compatible](compatible.md) (Buneman 1971; Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- P. Buneman, *The recovery of trees from measures of dissimilarity*, in Mathematics in the Archaeological and Historical Sciences, Edinburgh University Press (1971), 387–395.
- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
