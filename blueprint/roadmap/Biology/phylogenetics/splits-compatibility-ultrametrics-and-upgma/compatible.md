# Compatible splits

For a finite set $X$ and subsets $A, B \subseteq X$ (sides of the splits $A \mid X \setminus A$ and $B \mid X \setminus B$), the splits are compatible if at least one of $A \cap B$, $A \cap B^c$, $A^c \cap B$, $A^c \cap B^c$ is empty, complements taken in $X$ (Semple & Steel 2003).

## Sources

- C. Semple and M. Steel, *Phylogenetics*, Oxford University Press (2003).
