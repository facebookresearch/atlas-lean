# An unrooted binary tree resolves every four-taxon set

Let `X` be a finite set of taxa. Every
[unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md)
`T` on `X` [induces](induces.md) at least one resolution of every four-taxon set `q ⊆ X`, a subset
with exactly four elements: some edge of `T` separates two of the taxa of `q` from the other two.
