# Splits of a taxon set with a two-element side

Let `X` be any set of taxa. For a finite set `q ⊆ X`, the family of resolutions of `q` consists of
the pairs `{A, q \ A}` with `A ⊆ q` and `|A| = 2`, each recorded as the pair of its two sides. For a
four-element set `q` these are its [three](card-resolutions.md) resolutions `A | q \ A` into two
pairs of taxa. The family is defined for every `q`: for `|q| = 3` its members have sides of sizes
`2` and `1`, for `|q| = 2` it is `{{q, ∅}}`, and for `|q| < 2` it is empty.
