# A binary tree induces at most one resolution of each four-taxon set

Let `X` be a finite set of taxa. Let `T` be an
[unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md)
on `X` and `q ⊆ X` a four-taxon set. If `T` [induces](induces.md) two resolutions `ρ` and `σ` of
`q`, then `ρ = σ`: two edge splits of `T` are compatible.
