# Forest-based phylogenetic networks

Multiply rooted phylogenetic networks obtained from a phylogenetic forest by adding arcs only between different trees, modelling introgression or lateral gene transfer between distinct lineages. Covers their characterization through the trivial forest, their place among tree-child and tree-based networks, and the arboreal case.
