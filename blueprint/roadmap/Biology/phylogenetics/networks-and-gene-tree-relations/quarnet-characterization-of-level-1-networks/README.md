# Quarnet characterization of level-1 networks

Binary level-1 phylogenetic networks (networks whose cycles are vertex-disjoint) are determined by their four-leaf subnetworks, the quarnets, exactly as phylogenetic trees are determined by their quartets. Systems of quarnets, and of quartets, arising from level-1 networks are characterized by local five-leaf conditions and by closure under inference rules.
