# Binets of phylogenetic networks

Binets are the two-leaf subnetworks displayed by a rooted phylogenetic network: for binary level-1 networks each pair of leaves shows either a tree-type binet or one of two reticulate-type binets. Binets are the smallest informative building blocks for assembling networks from small pieces; they already fix the number of reticulations of a level-1 network, while deciding compatibility of arbitrary binet sets is hard.
