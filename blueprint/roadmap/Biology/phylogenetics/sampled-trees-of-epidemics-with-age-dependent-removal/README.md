# Sampled trees of epidemics with age-dependent removal

Phylogenetic trees of sequentially sampled infectives in an epidemic where transmission occurs at a constant rate but recovery and sampling happen after arbitrarily distributed times since infection. Via the Lévy-process contour of the underlying splitting tree, the pairs of coalescence and sampling times read from left to right form a killed Markov chain, giving an explicit tree likelihood.
