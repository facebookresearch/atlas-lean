# Microergodicity of Ornstein–Uhlenbeck tree models

Trait data observed at the tips of a phylogeny are hierarchically autocorrelated, so adding species to a bounded-height tree resembles infill asymptotics in spatial statistics. For the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck model on ultrametric trees this decides which parameters can ever be estimated consistently: the optimum never can, the selection strength and stationary variance can only under conditions on the distribution of node ages, and when they can they may converge more slowly than the combinations that are always estimable.
