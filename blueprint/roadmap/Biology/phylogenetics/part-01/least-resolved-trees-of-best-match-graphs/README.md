# Least resolved trees of best match graphs

Best match graphs record, for each gene, its evolutionarily closest relatives in every other species of a gene tree. Every best match graph is explained by a unique least resolved tree, and best match graphs are characterized by consistency of triples read off small induced subgraphs, recovered by the Aho (BUILD) construction.
