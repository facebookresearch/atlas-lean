# Encoding phylogenetic networks by induced subnetworks

Rooted phylogenetic networks model reticulate evolution (hybridization, recombination, horizontal transfer). Many reconstruction methods assemble a network from small building blocks, such as triplets, displayed trees, clusters, or trinets (induced subnetworks on three leaves). Whether such blocks determine the network is an encoding question: trees are encoded by their triplets and some restricted network classes by their trinets, but general networks are not encoded even by all of their proper induced subnetworks together.
