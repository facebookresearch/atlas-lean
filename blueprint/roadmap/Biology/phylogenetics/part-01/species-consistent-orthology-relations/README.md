# Species-consistent orthology relations

Orthology and paralogy relations of a gene family are encoded as a relation graph whose edges are orthologous pairs. Such a graph comes from some duplication/speciation-labelled gene tree iff it is $P_4$-free (a cograph), and from one that agrees with a given species tree iff, in addition, every induced path on three vertices yields a species triplet displayed by the species tree. Correcting relations or gene trees towards these conditions is NP-hard.
