# Minimum deep coalescence species trees

Species-tree estimation from a multiset of gene trees by minimizing the total number of extra lineages (deep coalescences) needed to embed the gene trees, a parsimony criterion for incomplete lineage sorting. Its per-branch cost has a clade-counting formula; the criterion is not hereditary under taxon deletion.
