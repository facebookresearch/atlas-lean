# Fringe patterns of random ranked tree-child networks

Ranked tree-child networks are phylogenetic networks built by successive branching and reticulation events with a total order on events. In a uniformly random ranked tree-child network with $n$ leaves, counts of small fringe patterns obey normal, Poisson or degenerate limit laws, proved by coupling with Markov chains and the method of moments.
