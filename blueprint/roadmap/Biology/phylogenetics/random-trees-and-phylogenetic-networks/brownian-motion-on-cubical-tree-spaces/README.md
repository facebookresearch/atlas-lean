# Brownian motion on cubical tree spaces

Brownian motion and random walks on cubical complexes, the class of metric spaces that includes BHV space of rooted phylogenetic trees. Transition kernels of the Brownian motion are the natural analogue of multivariate normal distributions on tree space, and they arise as limits of simple random walks that hop between orthants across codimension-1 faces.
