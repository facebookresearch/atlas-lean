# Divergence time under dependent-site models

Estimating the evolutionary time separating two aligned sequences when substitution rates at a site depend on neighbouring states. Although the likelihood is intractable, posterior mass on the divergence time provably concentrates within a constant (or logarithmic) multiple of the observed fraction of differing sites.
