# Information inequality

Let `σ` be a finite set and `law` a map from a set `Θ` to the functions `σ → ℝ` that is positive and
normalized: `law θ a > 0` for all `θ` and `a`, and `∑_a law θ a = 1` for every `θ`. For all `θ₀` and
`θ` the expected log-likelihood under `law θ₀` is largest at `θ₀`:
`∑_a law θ₀ a · log (law θ a) ≤ ∑_a law θ₀ a · log (law θ₀ a)`.
