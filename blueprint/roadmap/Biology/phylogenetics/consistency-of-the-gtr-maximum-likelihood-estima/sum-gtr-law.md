# The GTR pair-site law sums to one

Let `ι` be a finite set of states and let `π`, `s` and `t` give each parameter `θ` base frequencies,
exchangeabilities and a real branch length `t θ`, with every `(π θ, s θ)` in the
[GTR parameter domain](../gtr-identifiability-and-estimation/identifiability-of-the-gtr-model/is-gtrparam.md): `π θ i > 0`,
`∑_i π θ i = 1`, `s θ` symmetric and `s θ i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`, the diagonal of `s θ` being free.
Then for every parameter `θ` the [pair-site law](gtr-law.md) sums to `1` over pairs of states:
`∑_{i, j} π θ i · exp (t θ · Q_θ) i j = 1`, where `Q_θ` is the GTR rate matrix of `(π θ, s θ)`. No
sign condition on `t θ` is needed.
