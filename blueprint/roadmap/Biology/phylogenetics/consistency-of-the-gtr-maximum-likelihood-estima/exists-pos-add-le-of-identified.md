# An identified maximum is well separated on a compact space

Let `Θ` be a compact metric space, `M : Θ → ℝ` continuous, `θ₀ ∈ Θ`, and `E ⊆ Θ` a set containing
every `θ` with `M θ ≥ M θ₀`, so that the maximum at `θ₀` is identified up to `E`. For every `ε > 0`
there is `δ > 0` such that `M θ + δ ≤ M θ₀` for every `θ` at distance at least `ε` from `E`, that
is, with `inf_{θ' ∈ E} d(θ, θ') ≥ ε`.
