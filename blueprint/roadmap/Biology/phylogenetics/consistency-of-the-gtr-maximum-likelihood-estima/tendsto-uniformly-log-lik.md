# Uniform convergence of the criterion

Let `Θ` be a compact topological space, `σ` a finite set and `law : Θ → (σ → ℝ)` continuous with
`law θ a > 0` for all `θ` and `a`. If frequencies `fₙ : σ → ℝ` converge to `q`, the
[log-likelihood criteria](log-lik.md) `θ ↦ L(fₙ, θ)` converge to `θ ↦ L(q, θ)` uniformly on `Θ`.
