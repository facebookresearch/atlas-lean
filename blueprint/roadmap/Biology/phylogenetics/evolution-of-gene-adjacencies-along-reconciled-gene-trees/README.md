# Evolution of gene adjacencies along reconciled gene trees

Parsimonious reconstruction of ancestral gene neighbourhoods: adjacency trees that follow pairs of reconciled gene trees through speciation, duplication and loss, with gains and breakages of adjacencies (from rearrangements) minimized by a Sankoff-type dynamic program.
