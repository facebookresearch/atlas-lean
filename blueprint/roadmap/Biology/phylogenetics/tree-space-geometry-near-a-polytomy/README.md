# Tree-space geometry near a polytomy

Local polyhedral geometry of the space of tree metrics (ordinary or equidistant) near a tree with an unresolved node: the three binary resolutions of a tritomy meet along a common facet and form a planar fan whose angles depend on clade sizes, which in turn controls how distance-based methods such as least squares and UPGMA resolve noisy data.
