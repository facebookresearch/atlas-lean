# Nonbinary maximum agreement forests

Maximum agreement forests (MAF) and maximum acyclic agreement forests (MAAF) of two rooted phylogenetic trees that may contain polytomies, where an agreement forest only needs to be refined compatibly by both trees. Their sizes give the rooted SPR distance and the hybridization number for nonbinary trees.
