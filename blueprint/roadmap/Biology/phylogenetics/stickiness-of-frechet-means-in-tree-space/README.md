# Stickiness of Fréchet means in tree space

In Billera–Holmes–Vogtmann tree space, sample Fréchet means of phylogenetic trees can become permanently trapped on a lower-dimensional stratum, producing multifurcating mean trees. Locally the space is an open book, and the time at which the mean sticks is a maximum of last-passage times of folded one-dimensional random walks, which makes its tail computable and gives a test of whether an observed polytomy is hard or soft.
