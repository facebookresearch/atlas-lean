# Each site pair has the GTR divergence law

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Give `ι` any σ-algebra. If `(π, s, t)` are
[sampling parameters](is-sampling-params.md): `π i ≥ 0` for every `i`, `∑_i π i = 1`, `s i j ≥ 0`
for `i ≠ j` and `t ≥ 0`, then under the [sample law](sample-law.md) `S` every [site pair](site.md)
`ω ↦ ω n` has law the [GTR divergence law](divergence-law.md) `μ = ∑_a max (w(a), 0) · δ_a`, for
every `n`.
