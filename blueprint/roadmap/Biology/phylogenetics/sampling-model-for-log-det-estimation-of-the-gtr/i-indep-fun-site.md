# The site pairs are mutually independent under the sample law

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Give `ι` any σ-algebra. If `(π, s, t)` are
[sampling parameters](is-sampling-params.md): `π i ≥ 0` for every `i`, `∑_i π i = 1`, `s i j ≥ 0`
for `i ≠ j` and `t ≥ 0`, then the [site pairs](site.md) `ω ↦ ω n`, `n ∈ ℕ`, are mutually independent
under the [sample law](sample-law.md) `S`.
