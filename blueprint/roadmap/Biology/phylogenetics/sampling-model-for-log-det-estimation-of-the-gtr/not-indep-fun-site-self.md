# Full support on multiple states precludes site self-independence

Let `ι` be a finite set of at least two states. Let every subset of `ι` be measurable. Let `π i > 0`
for every `i` with `∑_i π i = 1`, let `s` be a real `ι × ι` matrix with `s i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`, and
let `t > 0`. Then under the [sample law](sample-law.md) `S` of `(π, s, t)` the first site pair
`ω ↦ ω 0` is not independent of itself. So the constant sequence of maps `X n = (ω ↦ ω 0)`, whose
terms are identically distributed with the GTR divergence law, is not pairwise independent and does
not satisfy the hypotheses of the
[log-det consistency theorem](../log-det-estimation-of-the-gtr-rate-matrix/ae-tendsto-rate-estimate-empirical.md).
