# Full support of the GTR divergence law for a positive branch length

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Let `π i > 0` for every `i`, let `s` be a real `ι × ι` matrix
with `s i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`, and let `t > 0`. Then every site pair has positive
[divergence weight](divergence-weight.md) `w(i, j) = π i · exp (t · Q) i j`: `w(a) > 0` for every
`a ∈ ι × ι`, where `Q` is the
[GTR rate matrix](../time-reversible-nucleotide-substitution-models/gtr-rate.md) with base
frequencies `π` and exchangeabilities `s`.
