# Topology of the Fréchet mean in BHV tree space

The Fréchet mean of a sample of phylogenetic trees in Billera-Holmes-Vogtmann space often lies in a lower-dimensional stratum (it has fewer internal edges than the sample trees), which makes iterative algorithms oscillate between topologies. Directional derivatives of the Fréchet function give certificates that a split must belong to the mean, and show when Sturm's stochastic algorithm keeps leaving the stratum of the mean.
