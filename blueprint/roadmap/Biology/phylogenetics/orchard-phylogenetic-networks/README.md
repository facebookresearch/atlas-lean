# Orchard phylogenetic networks

Rooted phylogenetic networks that can be reduced to a single leaf by repeatedly picking cherries and reticulated cherries. They are exactly the networks obtained from a tree by adding horizontal (time-consistent) transfer arcs, and their space is connected under local rearrangement moves.
