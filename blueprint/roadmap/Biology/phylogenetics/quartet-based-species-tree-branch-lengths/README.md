# Quartet-based species tree branch lengths

Under the multispecies coalescent with branch-specific substitution rates, gene trees are non-ultrametric and their expected branch lengths depend on both coalescent-unit lengths and rates. Comparing gene trees that do and do not match a species-tree quartet isolates the contribution of each species-tree branch and gives estimators of its length in substitution units.
