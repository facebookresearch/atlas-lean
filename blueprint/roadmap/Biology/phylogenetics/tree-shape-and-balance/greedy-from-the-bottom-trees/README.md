# Greedy-from-the-bottom trees

The greedy-from-the-bottom (GFB) tree on $n$ leaves is built by repeatedly joining two smallest trees of a forest of single leaves. It coincides with Fill's complete tree and is the unique minimizer of every imbalance index of the form $\sum_v f(n_v)$ over internal subtree sizes with $f$ strictly increasing and strictly concave, which includes the $\widehat s$-shape and $Q$-shape statistics.
