# Tropical weight of a labeling equals its edge cost

For observed states $\sigma$, every tree $t$ with costs, every root state $s$ and every labeling $z$, the [weight](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/weight.md) of $z$ for the [tropical leaf weights](leaf-obs.md) of $\sigma$ is the [total cost](edge-cost.md) $C(t, s, z)$ if $z$ [agrees](agrees.md) with $\sigma$, and $\infty$ otherwise.
