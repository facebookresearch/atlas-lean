# Species tree recovery under random lateral gene transfer

Probabilistic model in which gene trees arise from a species phylogeny by random subtree prune-and-regraft moves (lateral gene transfers between contemporaneous lineages), and conditions on the total transfer weight under which a majority-of-quartets estimator recovers the species tree from logarithmically many genes.
