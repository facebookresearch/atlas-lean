# Sum over darts is twice the sum over edges

For every [unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md) and every real function $f$ on unordered vertex pairs,

$$\sum_{(p, v)} f(pv) = \sum_{e} 2 f(e),$$

the left sum over the darts and the right sum over the edges of the tree.
