# The side of a pendant edge is the singleton of its leaf

Let $T$ be an [unrooted binary phylogenetic tree](../../../Combinatorics/elementary-enumeration/counting-binary-phylogenetic-trees/unrooted-binary-tree.md), $x$ a taxon with leaf $\ell_x$ and $p$ a neighbour of $\ell_x$. Then the set of taxa $y$ with $\mathrm{dist}(\ell_y, \ell_x) < \mathrm{dist}(\ell_y, p)$ is $\{x\}$.
