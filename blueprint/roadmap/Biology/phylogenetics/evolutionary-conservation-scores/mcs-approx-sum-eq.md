# Motif conservation score approximation as a de Moivre–Laplace sum

For real functions $X_0, X_1, \dots$ on a set $\Omega$, every real $p$, every natural number $n$ and every $\omega \in \Omega$,

$$z\Bigl(n, \sum_{i<n} X_i(\omega), p\Bigr) = \frac{\sum_{i<n} X_i(\omega) - np}{\sqrt{np(1 - p)}},$$

the de Moivre–Laplace standardized sum, where $z$ is the [binomial z-score approximation](mcs-approx.md).
