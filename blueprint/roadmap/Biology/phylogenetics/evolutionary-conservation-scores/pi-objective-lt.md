# The constraint-vector update maximizes the concave objective uniquely

Let $c_b > 0$ for every $b$, and let $\gamma$ satisfy $d_b + \gamma > 0$ for every $b$ and $\sum_b c_b / (d_b + \gamma) = 1$. Then every $\pi$ with $\pi_b > 0$, $\sum_b \pi_b = 1$ and $\pi \ne \hat\pi$, where $\hat\pi_b = c_b / (d_b + \gamma)$, satisfies

$$F(c, d; \pi) < F(c, d; \hat\pi),$$

where $F$ is the [constraint-vector objective](pi-objective.md).
