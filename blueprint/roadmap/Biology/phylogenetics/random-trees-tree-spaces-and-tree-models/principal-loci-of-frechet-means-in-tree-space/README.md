# Principal loci of Fréchet means in tree space

Principal component analysis for samples of phylogenetic trees in the Billera–Holmes–Vogtmann (BHV) space. Since tree space is not a vector space, the $k$-th principal component is replaced by the locus of weighted Fréchet means of $k+1$ vertex trees as the weights range over the simplex; this locus is generically a $k$-dimensional submanifold, piecewise affine where the relevant geodesics are simple.
