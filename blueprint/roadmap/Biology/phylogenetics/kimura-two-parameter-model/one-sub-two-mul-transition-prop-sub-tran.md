# Kimura's identity for 1 − 2P − Q

`P(M, i)` and `Q(M, i)` are the [transition](transition-prop.md) and
[transversion](transversion-prop.md) proportions in row `i` of a matrix `M`. For all real `a` and
`b` and every nucleotide `i`, the proportions `P = P(T(a, b), i)` and `Q = Q(T(a, b), i)` of the
[Kimura matrix](kimura-prob.md) satisfy `1 - 2P - Q = e^(-2(a + b))`.
