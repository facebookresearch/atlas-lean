# Recovery of the transversion parameter

`P(M, i)` and `Q(M, i)` are the [transition](transition-prop.md) and
[transversion](transversion-prop.md) proportions in row `i` of a matrix `M`. For all real `a` and
`b` and every nucleotide `i`, the [transversion parameter](kimura-transversion-param.md)
`b(Q) = -(1/4) · log (1 - 2Q)` returns `b` from the transversion proportion of the
[Kimura matrix](kimura-prob.md): `b(Q(T(a, b), i)) = b`.
