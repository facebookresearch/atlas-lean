# Transitions and transversions together are all the differences

`P(M, i)` and `Q(M, i)` are the [transition](transition-prop.md) and
[transversion](transversion-prop.md) proportions in row `i` of a matrix `M`. For every real `4 × 4`
matrix `M` and every nucleotide `i`, `P(M, i) + Q(M, i) = ∑_{j ≠ i} M i j`.
