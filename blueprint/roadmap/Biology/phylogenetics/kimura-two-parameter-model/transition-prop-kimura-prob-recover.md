# Every point of the algebraic domain is attained

If `1 - 2Q > 0` and
`1 - 2P - Q > 0`, the recovered parameters (possibly negative) reproduce the transition
proportion `P`. Together with `transversionProp_kimuraProb_recover` and the recovery theorems,
`(a, b) ↦ (P, Q)` is a bijection from `ℝ × ℝ` onto this algebraic domain.
