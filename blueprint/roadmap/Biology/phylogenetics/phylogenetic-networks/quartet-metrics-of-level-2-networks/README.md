# Quartet metrics of level-2 networks

Pairwise leaf distances built from quartet data (the quartet metric of a tree, the displayed quartet metric and the NANUQ metric of a network) are circular decomposable for outer-labeled planar networks, so they are represented by circular split systems. For semi-directed, outer-labeled planar, galled level-2 networks this makes the network's canonical form identifiable, the theoretical basis for consistent inference beyond level 1.
