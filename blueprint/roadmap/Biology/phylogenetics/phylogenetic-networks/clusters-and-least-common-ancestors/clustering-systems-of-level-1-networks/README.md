# Clustering systems of level-1 networks

The hardwired clusters of a rooted phylogenetic network (the leaf sets below its vertices) form a clustering system. A tree is determined by its clusters, a general network is not, but each clustering system has a canonical regular network (its Hasse diagram) and a canonical cluster network, and set-system axioms (closure under intersection, property (L), no three pairwise overlaps) characterize exactly which clustering systems come from level-1 networks and galled trees.
