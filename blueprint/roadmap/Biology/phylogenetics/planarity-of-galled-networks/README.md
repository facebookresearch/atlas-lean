# Planarity of galled networks

Planarity hierarchies for rooted phylogenetic networks (planar, upward planar, terminal planar, outerplanar) collapse or become locally checkable on galled networks, where every reticulation lies in its own galled cycle. Galled planarity reduces to upward planarity, and terminal planarity and outerplanarity are characterised by forbidden tree-vertex configurations.
