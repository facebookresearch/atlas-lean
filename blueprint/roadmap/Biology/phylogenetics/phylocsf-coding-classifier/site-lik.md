# Likelihood of one alignment column

For a [tree with real transition weights](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/tree.md) $t$ on a finite codon set $S$, a root distribution $\pi : S \to \mathbb{R}$ and a column $\mathrm{col}$, the site likelihood is the [site likelihood](../felsenstein-s-pruning-algorithm/likelihood.md) of $t$ and $\pi$ for the [leaf data](leaf-data.md) of $\mathrm{col}$.
