# Stationary rate of nonsynonymous substitutions

For the [Goldman–Yang matrix](gy-rate.md) $Q$ with frequencies $\pi$, the nonsynonymous rate is

$$\rho_N(\omega) = \sum_{i} \sum_{j \,:\, \mathrm{aa}(j) \ne \mathrm{aa}(i)} \pi_i Q_{ij}.$$

When $\pi_i \ge 0$ with $\sum_i \pi_i = 1$ and $\kappa, \omega \ge 0$, it is the stationary rate of nonsynonymous substitutions per codon; for unnormalized weights the formula is multiplied by $(\sum_i \pi_i)^2$.
