# Stationary rate of synonymous substitutions

For the [Goldman–Yang matrix](gy-rate.md) $Q$ with frequencies $\pi$, the synonymous rate is

$$\rho_S(\omega) = \sum_{i} \sum_{j \ne i,\ \mathrm{aa}(j) = \mathrm{aa}(i)} \pi_i Q_{ij}.$$

When $\pi_i \ge 0$ with $\sum_i \pi_i = 1$ and $\kappa, \omega \ge 0$, it is the stationary rate of synonymous substitutions per codon; for unnormalized weights the formula is multiplied by $(\sum_i \pi_i)^2$.
