# Number of nonsynonymous sites per codon

The number of nonsynonymous sites per codon, counted under $\omega = 1$, is

$$N = \frac{3 \rho_N(1)}{\rho_S(1) + \rho_N(1)},$$

where $\rho_S$, $\rho_N$ are the [synonymous](syn-rate.md) and [nonsynonymous](nonsyn-rate.md) rates, with the convention $x/0 = 0$.
