# Dependence of Goldman–Yang exchangeabilities on ω

For all labels, amino-acid maps and real $\kappa, \omega$, the [Goldman–Yang exchangeability](gy-exchange.md) satisfies

$$s_{ij}(\kappa, \omega) = a_{ij}\, s_{ij}(\kappa, 1),$$

where $a_{ij} = 1$ if $\mathrm{aa}(i) = \mathrm{aa}(j)$ and $a_{ij} = \omega$ otherwise.
