# Goldman–Yang codon rate matrix

For a finite set $I$ of codon states, real codon frequencies $\pi_i$, labels, an amino-acid map and real $\kappa, \omega$, the Goldman–Yang rate matrix is

$$Q_{ij} = s_{ij} \pi_j \ (i \ne j), \qquad Q_{ii} = -\sum_{k \ne i} s_{ik} \pi_k,$$

where $s$ is the [Goldman–Yang exchangeability](gy-exchange.md): the [general time-reversible matrix](../time-reversible-nucleotide-substitution-models/gtr-rate.md) with frequencies $\pi$ and exchangeabilities $s$ (Goldman & Yang 1994).

## Sources

- N. Goldman and Z. Yang, *A codon-based model of nucleotide substitution for protein-coding DNA sequences*, Mol. Biol. Evol. 11 (1994), 725–736.
