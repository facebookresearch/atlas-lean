# Positivity of the nonsynonymous rate

Let $\pi_i \ge 0$ for every $i$ and $\kappa > 0$, and suppose there are codon states $i, j$ with $\mathrm{aa}(i) \ne \mathrm{aa}(j)$, $\nu(i), \nu(j)$ forming a [single change](is-single-change.md), $\pi_i > 0$ and $\pi_j > 0$. Then $\rho_N(1) > 0$, where $\rho_N$ is the [nonsynonymous rate](nonsyn-rate.md).
