# Identification is well separated on a compact parameter space

Let `Θ` be a compact metric space, `σ` a finite set and `law : Θ → (σ → ℝ)` continuous, with the sup
distance `d` on `σ → ℝ`. Fix `θ₀ ∈ Θ` and let `F = {θ : law θ = law θ₀}` be its fibre. For every
`ε > 0` there is `δ > 0` such that `d(law θ, law θ₀) ≥ δ` for every `θ` at distance at least `ε`
from the fibre, that is, with `inf_{θ' ∈ F} d(θ, θ') ≥ ε`.
