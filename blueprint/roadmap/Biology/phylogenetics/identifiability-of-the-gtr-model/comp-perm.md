# Relabeling the states preserves the GTR domain

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. If `(π, s)` lies in the [GTR parameter domain](is-gtrparam.md)
(`π i > 0`, `∑_i π i = 1`, `s` symmetric and `s i j > 0` for `i ≠ j`) and `σ` is a permutation of
the states, then the relabeled parameters `(π ∘ σ, s_σ)`, with `(π ∘ σ) i = π (σ i)` and
`s_σ i j = s (σ i) (σ j)`, lie in it too.
