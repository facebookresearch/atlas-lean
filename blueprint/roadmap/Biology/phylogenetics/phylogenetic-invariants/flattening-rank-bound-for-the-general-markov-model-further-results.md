# Flattening rank bounds for the general Markov model: further results

Allman and Rhodes (2008) prove more than the [rank bound for split flattenings](flattening-rank-bound-for-the-general-markov-model.md) and the [generation of the two-state phylogenetic ideal by edge invariants](../../not-yet-classified/general-markov-flattening/phylogenetic-ideal-eq-span-edge-minors.md) (their Theorem 4). For any number of states $\kappa$, a set of polynomials defining the variety of the three-taxon tree yields, through flattenings at the nodes, polynomials whose zero set is the phylogenetic variety of any binary tree (their Theorem 17), so that for $\kappa = 2, 3$ explicit set-theoretic defining polynomials exist for every binary tree (their Corollary 18). The four-taxon question whether the rank conditions identify the topology is [the generic rank of non-edge flattenings](../../not-yet-classified/general-markov-flattening/exists-card-lt-rank-flatten-non-edge.md).
