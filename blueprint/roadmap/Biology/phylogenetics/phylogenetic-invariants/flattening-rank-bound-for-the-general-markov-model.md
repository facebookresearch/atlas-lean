# Edge flattenings of the general Markov model have rank at most the number of states

This node covers the following result. For the $\kappa$-state general Markov model on a tree $T$ and an edge $e$ inducing the split $A \mid B$ of the leaves, the flattening $\mathrm{Flat}_e(P)$ of any leaf distribution $P$ of the model, the $\kappa^{|A|} \times \kappa^{|B|}$ matrix of $P$, has the form $M_1^{\mathsf T} \operatorname{diag}(\pi) M_2$ with Markov matrices $M_1, M_2$ and $\pi$ the distribution at an endpoint of $e$. Hence it has rank at most $\kappa$ and all its $(\kappa+1) \times (\kappa+1)$ minors vanish.
