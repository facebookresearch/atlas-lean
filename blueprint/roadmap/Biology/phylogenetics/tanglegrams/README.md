# Tanglegrams

Many questions about pairs of leaf-labelled trees, such as tree distances, cophylogeny and rearrangement problems, depend only on the relative labelling of the two leaf sets. Tanglegrams formalize this relative labelling as a bijection between the leaves of two trees, and their isomorphism types, automorphisms and labelings are described by double cosets of the symmetric group with respect to the leaf actions of the tree automorphism groups.
