# Continuity of the logarithm of the normalized divergence matrix

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Let `F₀` be a real `ι × ι` matrix with
[base-frequency estimates](base-freq.md) `b` and [normalized divergence matrix](norm-divergence.md)
`N` such that `b(F₀) i > 0` for every `i` and every eigenvalue of `N(F₀)` is positive. Then
`F ↦ log N(F)` is continuous at `F₀`, where the real logarithm of a symmetric matrix applies `log`
to its eigenvalues in an orthonormal eigenbasis, with the conventions `log 0 = 0` and
`log x = log |x|` for `x < 0`.
