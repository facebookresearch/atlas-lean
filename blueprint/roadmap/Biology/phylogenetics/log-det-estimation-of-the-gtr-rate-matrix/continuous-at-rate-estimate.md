# Continuity of the log-det rate estimate

Let `ι` be a finite set of states. Let `F₀` be a real `ι × ι` matrix with
[base-frequency estimates](base-freq.md) `b`, [normalized divergence matrix](norm-divergence.md) `N`
and [log-det distance](log-det-distance.md) `Δ` such that `b(F₀) i > 0` for every `i`, every
eigenvalue of `N(F₀)` is positive and `Δ(F₀) ≠ 0`. Then the
[log-det rate estimate](rate-estimate.md) `R` is continuous at `F₀`: `F ↦ R(F)` is continuous there.
