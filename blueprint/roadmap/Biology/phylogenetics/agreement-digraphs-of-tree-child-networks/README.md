# Agreement digraphs of tree-child networks

Agreement forests characterize rearrangement distances between two phylogenetic trees. Agreement digraphs generalize them to two rooted binary phylogenetic networks: a collection of leaf-labelled acyclic digraphs that embeds in both networks. For tree-child networks the minimum cut size of a tree-child agreement digraph bounds the weighted SNPR distance within a factor of two.
