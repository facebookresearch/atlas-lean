# The balanced minimum-evolution length of a tree metric is the tree length

Let $T$ be an [unrooted binary tree](utree.md) and $d$ a symmetric real dissimilarity such that $d(x, y) = \ell$ for every [leaf pair](pairs-2.md) $(x, y, \ell, \tau)$ of $T$. Then $\mathrm{BME}(T, d) = L(T)$, where $\mathrm{BME}$ is the [balanced minimum-evolution length](bme-length.md) and $L$ the [total edge length](length-2.md).
