# Total edge length of an unrooted binary tree

The total edge length of an [unrooted binary tree](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/utree.md) with sides $l, r$ and root edge length $c$ is $L(l) + c + L(r)$, where $L$ is the [total edge length of a rooted tree](length.md).
