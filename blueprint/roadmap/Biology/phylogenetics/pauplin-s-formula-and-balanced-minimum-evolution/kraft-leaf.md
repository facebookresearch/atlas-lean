# Kraft equality for unrooted binary trees

Let $T$ be an [unrooted binary tree](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/utree.md) with pairwise distinct [leaf labels](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/labels.md), and $x$ one of its labels. Then

$$\sum_{(u, v, \ell, \tau) \,:\, x \in \{u, v\}} 2^{1 - \tau} = 1,$$

the sum over the [leaf pairs](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/pairs-2.md) of $T$ containing $x$.
