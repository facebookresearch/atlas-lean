# Kraft's equality

For every [rooted binary tree](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/rtree.md), $\sum 2^{-k} = 1$, the sum over its [leaves](../../../GraphTheory/graph-families-and-operations/tree-metrics-and-the-four-point-condition/leaves.md) $(x, d, k)$, where $k$ is the number of edges from the leaf to the root.
