# Trinet encoding of phylogenetic networks

Rooted triplets (rooted trees on three leaves) determine a rooted phylogenetic tree, but they do not determine a rooted phylogenetic network. Trinets, the rooted networks on three leaves that a network exhibits, are the natural replacement. These articles state how trinets recover the cut-arc decomposition of a recoverable binary network (networks having no in-degree-1 out-degree-1 vertices and no unary root stem), which classes of recoverable binary networks are determined by their trinets among all recoverable networks, and the resulting metric on those classes.
