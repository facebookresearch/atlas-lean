# Trinet encoding of phylogenetic networks

Rooted triplets (rooted trees on three leaves) determine a rooted phylogenetic tree, but they do not determine a rooted phylogenetic network. Trinets, the rooted networks on three leaves that a network exhibits, are the natural replacement. These articles state how trinets recover the cut-arc decomposition of a binary network, which classes of networks are determined by their trinets, and the resulting metric on those classes.
