# Species trees under the coalescent and gene transfer

These articles infer a species tree from many gene trees or loci whose histories differ from it, either through incomplete lineage sorting under the multispecies coalescent or through random lateral (horizontal) gene transfer. Each article keeps its own gene-tree model and data assumptions; reading order does not assert a mathematical dependency.
