# Taxonomy–phylogeny discord and taxonomic rooting

Discord between a phylogeny and a taxonomy at a given rank is quantified by the largest convex subcoloring of the taxonomically colored leaves, and a tree can be rooted taxonomically through arrows pointing towards the most diverse subtrees.
