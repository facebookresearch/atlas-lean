# Fossilized birth–death process

A tree prior for dated phylogenies of extant and fossil taxa. Lineages speciate and go extinct at constant rates, fossils are sampled along lineages by a Poisson process, and extant species are sampled at the present. Fossils can be direct ancestors of other samples (sampled ancestors). Combined with morphological and molecular data, it supports total-evidence divergence dating.
