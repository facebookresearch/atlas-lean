# Least-squares dating of serially sampled trees

When the tips of a phylogeny are sampled at different known times, as for fast-evolving viruses, a Gaussian approximation to the Langley–Fitch molecular clock turns the estimation of the substitution rate and all ancestral dates into a weighted least-squares problem. Its tree structure allows exact solution in linear time.
