# Toric reciprocal varieties of leaf-colored Brownian motion tree models

**Setting.** Let $T$ be a rooted phylogenetic tree with root leaf $0$, non-root leaves $1, \dots, n$ and internal nodes $\mathrm{Int}(T)$. Color the nodes by $\lambda_{\mathcal{T}}$ (leaves and internal nodes never share a color) and choose a set $Z(\mathcal{T}) \subseteq \mathrm{Int}(T)$ of zeroed nodes. The associated linear space of symmetric matrices is

$$L_{\mathcal{T}} = \{M \in \mathrm{Sym}_n(\mathbb{R}) : M_{ij} = 0 \text{ if } \mathrm{lca}(i,j) \in Z(\mathcal{T}),\ M_{ij} = t_{\lambda_{\mathcal{T}}(\mathrm{lca}(i,j))} \text{ otherwise}\},$$

so the uncolored, unzeroed case is the Brownian motion tree (BMT) model, whose covariances $\Sigma_{ij}$ depend only on $\mathrm{lca}(i,j)$. Its reciprocal variety is $L_{\mathcal{T}}^{-1} = \overline{\{\Sigma : \Sigma^{-1} \in L_{\mathcal{T}}\}}$. Reading $L_{\mathcal{T}}$ as concentration matrices gives a colored Gaussian graphical model on a colored graph $\mathcal{G}$ (the *BMT-derived graph*) with $L_{\mathcal{T}} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{G}}$. The *reduced graph Laplacian transformation* is the linear change of variables $p_{ij} = -\sigma_{ij}$ for $1 \le i < j \le n$ and $p_{0i} = \sum_{j=1}^n \sigma_{ij}$.

**Background (uncolored BMT).** The vanishing ideal of $L_T^{-1}$ under this transformation is the toric ideal $I_T$ generated by the binomials $p_{ik}p_{j\ell} - p_{i\ell}p_{jk}$ for every quadruple $i, j, k, \ell \in \{0, \dots, n\}$ whose induced 4-leaf subtree has cherries $\{i,j\}$ and $\{k,\ell\}$; equivalently $I_T$ is the kernel of the path map $p_{ij} \mapsto \prod_{(\ell,k) \in i \leftrightsquigarrow j} \theta_k$ over the edges of the path from $i$ to $j$.

**Theorem.**

1. If only leaves are colored ($Z(\mathcal{T}) = \emptyset$ and distinct internal colors), the complete BMT-derived graph is *vertex-regular* (equal vertex colors force equal colors on the corresponding edges) if and only if leaves of the same color have the same parent.
2. For a colored tree $\mathcal{T} = (T, \lambda_{\mathcal{T}}, \emptyset)$ with BMT-derived graph $\mathcal{G}$ and vertex-regular completion $\overline{\mathcal{G}}$,

    $$\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{G}}^{-1} = L_T^{-1} \cap \mathcal{L}_{\overline{\mathcal{G}}}^{-1}$$

    is a toric variety under the reduced graph Laplacian transformation, with toric vanishing ideal $\sqrt{I_T + \mathcal{I}_{\overline{\mathcal{G}}}}$, the kernel of a generalized path map in which leaves of one color share a parameter.

3. More generally, for colored and zeroed trees whose BMT-derived graph $\mathcal{G}$ is a vertex-regular block graph, $L_{\mathcal{T}}^{-1}$ is toric with vanishing ideal $\sqrt{I_T + \mathcal{I}_{\mathcal{G}} + \mathcal{I}_{\overline{\mathcal{G}}}}$ under a $\mathcal{G}$-derived Laplacian transformation.

## Expansion

The proofs rest on a general principle: if a birational map sends two irreducible varieties to varieties whose ideals become toric under one common linear change of coordinates, and their intersection is irreducible, then the image of the intersection is toric with ideal the radical of the sum. Coloring leaves intersects the BMT reciprocal variety with a colored Gaussian graphical model, and vertex-regularity is what makes the latter toric in the same coordinates. Toricity yields monomial parametrizations and makes maximum likelihood degree and model-membership questions combinatorial, which matters for phylogenetic comparative models with shared rates.
