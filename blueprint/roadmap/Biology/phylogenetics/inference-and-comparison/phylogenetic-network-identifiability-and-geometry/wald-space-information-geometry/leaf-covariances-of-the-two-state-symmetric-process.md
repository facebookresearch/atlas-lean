# Leaf covariances of the two-state symmetric process on a tree

Let $T$ be a phylogenetic tree with edge lengths and $\ell_{t_1 t_2}$ the path length between points $t_1, t_2$ of $T$. The two-state symmetric Markov process assigns $X(t) \in \{0, 1\}$ to each point, is stationary with $X(t) \sim \mathrm{Bern}(1/2)$, and has

$$\Pr\bigl(X(t_2) = X(t_1)\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 + e^{-\ell_{t_1 t_2}}\bigr), \qquad \Pr\bigl(X(t_2) \ne X(t_1)\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 - e^{-\ell_{t_1 t_2}}\bigr).$$

Then for leaves $u, v$ with values $X_u, X_v$,

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_u, X_v) = \tfrac{1}{4} e^{-\ell_{uv}},$$

and the conditional law of $X(t_2)$ given $X(t_1) = \omega \in \{0, 1\}$ has variance $\tfrac{1}{4}\bigl(1 - e^{-2\ell_{t_1 t_2}}\bigr)$. On a forest the process is taken independent on each component, so leaves in different components are uncorrelated ($\ell_{uv} = \infty$).

**Wald space** (Section 2.4 of the source). Write edge weights $\lambda_e = 1 - e^{-\ell_e} \in [0, 1]$, so $\lambda_e = 1$ means $\ell_e = \infty$. Let $\widetilde{\mathcal W}_N$ be the set of forests $F$ (disjoint unions of unrooted trees with weighted edges) such that

1. $F$ has exactly $N$ labelled vertices, the leaves $1, \dots, N$;
2. no unlabelled vertex has degree $0$, $1$ or $2$;
3. for every two leaves $u, v$ in the same tree of $F$, some edge on the path from $u$ to $v$ has $\lambda_e > 0$ (no two leaves coincide).

Two forests are identified by the equivalence relation generated by two rules. *BHV boundary rule*: delete every internal edge with $\lambda_e = 0$ and merge its endpoints. *Boundary at infinity rule*: delete every edge with $\lambda_e = 1$, which disconnects its tree; then suppress each resulting unlabelled vertex of degree $2$, replacing its two incident edges of weights $\lambda_e, \lambda_{\tilde e}$ by one edge of weight $\lambda_e + \lambda_{\tilde e} - \lambda_e\lambda_{\tilde e}$ (lengths add). Forests with identical results are equivalent. The **wald space** is $\mathcal W_N = \widetilde{\mathcal W}_N/\!\sim$; it contains the trees of [tree space](../../../../not-yet-classified/phylogenetic-tree-space/tree-space.md) as its points with all $\lambda_e < 1$, and its point classes $[F]$ are exactly the fibres of $F \mapsto p_F$, the law of the leaf values: $F_1 \sim F_2$ if and only if $p_{F_1} = p_{F_2}$ (Lemma 2.2).

## Expansion

$\operatorname{Cov}(X_u, X_v) = \Pr(X_u = X_v = 1) - \tfrac14 = \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12(1 + e^{-\ell_{uv}}) - \tfrac14$. Given $X(t_1) = \omega$, $X(t_2)$ is Bernoulli with success probability $\tfrac12(1 \pm e^{-\ell})$, whose variance is $\tfrac14(1 - e^{-2\ell})$. Equivalently, the parity of $X$ flips at the events of a Poisson process of rate $1/2$ per unit of edge length, since a Poisson count of rate $r$ over length $\ell$ is even with probability $\tfrac12(1 + e^{-2r\ell})$. The source's simulation remark says rate one, which would give $e^{-2\ell}$ in place of $e^{-\ell}$; this page follows the transition law (2.1). The paper reparametrizes edges by $\lambda_e = 1 - e^{-\ell_e} \in [0, 1]$, so that infinite edges ($\lambda_e = 1$), i.e. disconnected forests, are included in wald space.
