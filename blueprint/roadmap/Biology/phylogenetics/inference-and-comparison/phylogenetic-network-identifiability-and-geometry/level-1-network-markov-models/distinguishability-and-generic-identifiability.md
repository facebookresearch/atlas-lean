# Distinguishability and generic identifiability of network parameters

For a class $\{\mathcal M_N\}_{N \in \mathcal N}$ of network-based Markov models indexed by $n$-leaf semi-directed networks, let $V_N$ be the network variety, the Zariski closure of the complexified parametrization of $\mathcal M_N$.

- The network parameter is **generically identifiable** if for all distinct $N_1, N_2 \in \mathcal N$ the set of numerical parameters of $N_1$ that the parametrization maps into $\mathcal M_{N_2}$ has Lebesgue measure zero (Definition 2).
- Distinct $N_1, N_2$ are **distinguishable** if $V_{N_1} \cap V_{N_2}$ is a proper subvariety of both, i.e. $V_{N_1} \not\subseteq V_{N_2}$ and $V_{N_2} \not\subseteq V_{N_1}$ (Definition 3).

**Distinguishability suffices** (Proposition 2, from Gross and Long 2017): if every two distinct networks in $\mathcal N$ are distinguishable, the network parameter is generically identifiable.

**Restriction lemma** (Lemma 3): let $N_1 \ne N_2$ be $n$-leaf semi-directed networks on $X$ and $A \subseteq X$, with restrictions $N_i|_A$ (the union of all directed paths between leaves of $A$, where undirected edges are treated as bidirected, followed by suppression of degree-two vertices and removal of parallel edges, Definition 5; paths in the underlying undirected graph that enter a vertex through two incoming reticulation edges are not included). If $V_{N_1|_A} \not\subseteq V_{N_2|_A}$, then $V_{N_1} \not\subseteq V_{N_2}$.

## Expansion

Since $V_{N_1}$ is irreducible, $V_{N_1} \not\subseteq V_{N_2}$ means $V_{N_1} \cap V_{N_2}$ is a proper closed subvariety of $V_{N_1}$, whose preimage under the polynomial parametrization has measure zero. For the restriction lemma, a phylogenetic invariant in the ideal of $N_2|_A$ but not of $N_1|_A$, written in the marginal coordinates on $A$, lifts to an invariant of $N_2$ that does not vanish on $V_{N_1}$. Consequently it suffices to find four-leaf subsets on which the restricted networks are distinguishable, or one subset for each direction of non-containment. Invariants such as the [edge-flattening rank conditions of the general Markov model](../../models-alignment-and-identifiability/phylogenetic-invariants/flattening-rank-bound-for-the-general-markov-model.md) are one source of such certificates; neither result on this page uses them.
