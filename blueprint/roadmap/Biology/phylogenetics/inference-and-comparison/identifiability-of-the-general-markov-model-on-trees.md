# Consistency of maximum likelihood for the general Markov model on trees

Under the hypotheses of [full identifiability of the general Markov model](../../not-yet-classified/general-markov-identifiability/identifiable-of-reconstructible-from-row.md) (Chang 1996, Theorem 4.1): a tree without nodes of degree $2$, a node whose marginal distribution charges every state, and edge transition matrices that are invertible, are not permutation matrices and belong to a fixed class reconstructible from rows, Chang (1996, Theorem 5.1) proves that the maximum likelihood estimator of the topology and of the transition matrices from independent observations of the joint distribution of states at the terminal nodes is consistent.

## Depends on

- [Full identifiability of the general Markov model](../../not-yet-classified/general-markov-identifiability/identifiable-of-reconstructible-from-row.md)

## Expansion

1. The parameter space: topologies and transition matrices satisfying the hypotheses of Theorem 4.1, with its topology and compactness properties.
2. Wald's consistency argument (Chang, Lemma 5.1), using identifiability from Theorem 4.1.

## Sources

- J. T. Chang, Full reconstruction of Markov models on evolutionary trees: identifiability and consistency, Math. Biosci. 137 (1996), 51–73, Theorems 4.1 and 5.1. <https://doi.org/10.1016/s0025-5564(96)00075-2>
