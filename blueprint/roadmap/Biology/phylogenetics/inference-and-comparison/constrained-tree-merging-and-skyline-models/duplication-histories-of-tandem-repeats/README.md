# Duplication histories of tandem repeats

When two orthologous sequences consist of tandem copies of a repeat unit, each aligned column relates two paralogous copies in two species, and Fitch's small-parsimony rule on the resulting four-leaf duplication–speciation tree apportions mutations between the periods before and after speciation. Comparing sequences with one extra copy also locates a duplication or loss.
