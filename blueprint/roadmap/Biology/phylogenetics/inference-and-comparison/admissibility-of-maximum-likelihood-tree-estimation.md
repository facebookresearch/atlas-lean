# Admissibility of maximum-likelihood tree estimation

Is maximum-likelihood phylogenetic estimation admissible when branch lengths are unknown? This asks whether another estimator can be at least as accurate for every permitted tree and branch parameter, and strictly better somewhere.

## What would settle it

Fix the model, sample size, loss, parameter space, and tie-breaking. Either construct a dominating estimator, or prove that no estimator dominates ML. Write the universal comparison over both topology and branch parameters; proving optimality at a single parameter value is insufficient.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Steel lists this as question SC3 in his July 2018 problem list and notes that maximum likelihood is admissible when the branch lengths are fixed and known for each tree.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
