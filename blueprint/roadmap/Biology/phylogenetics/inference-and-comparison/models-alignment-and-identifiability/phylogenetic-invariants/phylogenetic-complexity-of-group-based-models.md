# Phylogenetic complexity of group-based models

The general Sturmfels–Sullivant degree-bound conjecture asks whether the phylogenetic complexity φ(G) is at most |G| for every finite abelian group G. It would bound the algebraic tests needed across trees in those substitution models.

## What would settle it

Define the group-based model's polynomial parameter map and its kernel ideal. Prove that, for every relevant tree, the ideal is generated by elements of degree at most |G|. This is ideal generation, not merely describing the same zero set.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Michałek and Ventura state the general bound as Conjecture 4.1, attributing it to Sturmfels and Sullivant. The Kimura three-parameter case, in which ideals are generated in degree four, is proved by the same authors and is not the open question.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
