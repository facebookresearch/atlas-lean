# SI epidemics on geometric inhomogeneous random graphs

Spatial scale-free contact networks (geometric inhomogeneous random graphs, GIRGs) carry heavy-tailed degrees and long-range edges. When transmission along an edge slows down with the degrees of its endpoints and with its length, the early growth of an SI epidemic interpolates between explosive, (stretched) exponential, polynomial and purely geometric growth, depending on explicit inequalities between the four exponents.
