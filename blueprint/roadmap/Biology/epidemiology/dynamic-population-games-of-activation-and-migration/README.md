# Dynamic population games of activation and migration

A large population of agents in an SAIR epidemic over several zones repeatedly chooses how many contacts to make and which zone to move to, trading social benefit against lockdown costs, migration costs and the risk of illness. Stationary equilibria of this discrete-time mean-field (dynamic population) game exist, and the disease-free ones are characterized explicitly by dominant activation and migration choices.
