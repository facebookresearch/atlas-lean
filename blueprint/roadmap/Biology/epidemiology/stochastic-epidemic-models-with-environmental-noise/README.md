# Stochastic epidemic models with environmental noise

Compartmental models in which parameters fluctuate as white noise become stochastic differential equations with multiplicative noise. Persistence of the disease is expressed through existence of a unique ergodic stationary distribution, established with Khasminskii's criterion and stochastic Lyapunov functions; the Itô correction shifts each death rate by half the noise variance.
