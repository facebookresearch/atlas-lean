# Spectral bound and reproduction number for infinite-dimensional population structure

This page covers a research target from Thieme (2009): the threshold relation between the growth of an infection and its reproduction number when the population structure is infinite-dimensional, as for age, space or a continuum of types. The linearized infection dynamics are written $u' = (B + C)u$ on an ordered Banach space, with $B$ the generator of transitions and removals and $C$ the operator of new infections. The reproduction number is the spectral radius $\mathcal{R}_0 = r(-C B^{-1})$ of the next-generation operator, and the target is the sign relation between the spectral bound $s(B + C)$ and $\mathcal{R}_0 - 1$, the infinite-dimensional counterpart of [the threshold for the linearized infected subsystem](is-hurwitz-sub-iff-r0-lt-one.md).

Only the infinite-dimensional statement belongs to this page; the finite-dimensional case is [that threshold](is-hurwitz-sub-iff-r0-lt-one.md).

## Expansion

Fix from the source the hypotheses on the space and its cone, on $B$ (resolvent positivity and a negative spectral bound) and on $C$ (positivity), state the sign relation as a theorem, and prove it.
