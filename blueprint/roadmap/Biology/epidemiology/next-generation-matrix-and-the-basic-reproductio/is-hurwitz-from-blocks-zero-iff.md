# Hurwitz criterion for block lower-triangular matrices

Let $A$, $C$ and $D$ be real matrices of sizes $\iota \times \iota$, $\kappa \times \iota$ and
$\kappa \times \kappa$, for finite index sets $\iota$ and $\kappa$. The block lower-triangular matrix
$\begin{pmatrix} A & 0 \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$ is Hurwitz (all its complex eigenvalues have negative
real part) if and only if both $A$ and $D$ are Hurwitz.
