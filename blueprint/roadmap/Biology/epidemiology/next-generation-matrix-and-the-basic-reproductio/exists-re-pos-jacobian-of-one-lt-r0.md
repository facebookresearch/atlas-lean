# Threshold theorem, instability half, spectral form

Let $F$ and $V$ be real square matrices indexed by a finite set $\iota$, with $F$ entrywise
nonnegative and $V$ a nonsingular M-matrix (nonpositive off-diagonal entries, all complex eigenvalues
with positive real part), and let $C$ (a $\kappa \times \iota$ matrix) and $D$ (a $\kappa \times \kappa$
matrix), for a finite set $\kappa$, be arbitrary real matrices. If the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) satisfies $R_0 = \rho(F V^{-1}) > 1$, then the block matrix

$$J = \begin{pmatrix} F - V & 0 \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$$

has a complex eigenvalue with positive real part (van den Driessche–Watmough 2002, Theorem 2).
