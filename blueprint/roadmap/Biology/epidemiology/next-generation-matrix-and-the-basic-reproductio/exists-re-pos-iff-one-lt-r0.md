# Unstable mode of the infected subsystem exactly when $R_0 > 1$

Let $F$ and $V$ be real square matrices indexed by a finite set, with $F$ entrywise nonnegative and $V$
a nonsingular M-matrix (its off-diagonal entries are nonpositive and all its complex eigenvalues have
positive real part). Then $F - V$ has a complex eigenvalue with positive real part if and only if the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) $R_0 = \rho(F V^{-1})$ exceeds $1$ (van den Driessche–Watmough 2002,
proof of Theorem 2).
