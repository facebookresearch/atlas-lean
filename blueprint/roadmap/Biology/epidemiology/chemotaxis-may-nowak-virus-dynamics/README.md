# Chemotaxis May–Nowak virus dynamics

Spatial versions of the May–Nowak model of within-host virus infection in which uninfected cells move chemotactically along the virus gradient, with superlinear damping of uninfected cells. Questions are global existence and boundedness in every dimension.
