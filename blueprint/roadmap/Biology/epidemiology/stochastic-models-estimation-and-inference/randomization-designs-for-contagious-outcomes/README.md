# Randomization designs for contagious outcomes

Causal effects of an infectious-disease intervention in clustered randomized trials when infection outcomes are contagious, so that one subject's outcome depends on the treatments and infections of others. A structural transmission hazard separates the effect of treatment on the recipient's susceptibility from its effect on infectiousness and shows how the randomization design determines what the usual direct-effect contrast measures.
