# Branching-process extinction in environmental-virus models

Stochastic host–virus models in which free virus particles in the environment, such as water in salmon aquaculture, mediate transmission. Near the disease-free state the infected-host and virion counts are approximated by a multitype branching process. Its generating function gives the probability that an introduced infection dies out, in closed form for single-patch models.
