# Metapopulation SEIR on travel networks

Discrete-time stochastic SEIR dynamics in a network of city subpopulations, with within-city
chain-binomial transitions and Poisson transmission, coupled by Bernoulli movement of infectious
individuals along a time-varying origin-destination matrix.

- [Chain-binomial SEIR step with Poisson transmission](chain-binomial-seir-step-with-poisson-transmission.md)
- [Stochastic movement of infectives on a travel network](stochastic-movement-of-infectives-on-a-travel-network.md)
