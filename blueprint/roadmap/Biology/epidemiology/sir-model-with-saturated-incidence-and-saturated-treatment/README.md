# SIR model with saturated incidence and saturated treatment

SIR models in which transmission saturates through an inhibitory factor, $\beta SI/(1+\alpha I)$, and treatment is limited by medical capacity, $ru_2I/(1+bu_2I)$, with vaccination of susceptibles as a second control. The limited treatment capacity can create a backward bifurcation at the reproduction threshold, and the two controls can be chosen optimally by Pontryagin's maximum principle.
