# Backward bifurcation at the epidemic threshold

In many compartmental models (imperfect vaccination, reinfection, treatment returning individuals to susceptibility, environmental reservoirs) a stable endemic equilibrium coexists with the stable disease-free equilibrium for $\mathcal{R}_0$ slightly below one. The direction of the transcritical bifurcation at $\mathcal{R}_0 = 1$ is decided by centre-manifold coefficients.
