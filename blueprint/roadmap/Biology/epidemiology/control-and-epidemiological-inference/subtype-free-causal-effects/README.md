# Subtype-free causal effects

Causal effects of an exposure on one subtype of a heterogeneous disease (for example molecular subtypes of colorectal cancer), when the subtypes compete: an exposure can move a person from one subtype to another, so the total effect on a subtype mixes causation with subtype switching. Principal stratification on the other subtype gives an estimand that avoids switching, identified under monotonicity assumptions and bounded otherwise.
