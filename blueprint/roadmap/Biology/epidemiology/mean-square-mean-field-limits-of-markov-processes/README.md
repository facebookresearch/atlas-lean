# Mean-square mean-field limits of Markov processes

Elementary convergence theory for mean-field (fluid) limits of scaled Markov processes such as stochastic SIS, SIR and sexually transmitted SI epidemics: Kolmogorov equations for the first moment and the summed second moment, closed by a Taylor bound on the variance, plus continuous dependence of ODE solutions, give mean-square convergence to the deterministic mean-field equations.
