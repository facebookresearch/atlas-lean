# Harmonic seasonal regression

Seasonal patterns of infectious disease incidence are summarized by regressing a surveillance time series on sine and cosine terms at the annual and semi-annual frequencies. The fitted coefficients give the amplitude of each periodicity, the timing of the annual peak, and a ratio measuring how semi-annual the pattern is.
