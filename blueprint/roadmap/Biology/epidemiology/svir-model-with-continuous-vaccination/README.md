# SVIR model with continuous vaccination

The SIR model with vital dynamics extended by a vaccinee class: susceptibles enter vaccination at a constant rate, vaccinees acquire immunity at a finite rate and can be infected (at a reduced rate) before they do. Covers the threshold dynamics of the uncontrolled model and the optimal time-varying social-restriction policy that trades transmission against an economic cost.
