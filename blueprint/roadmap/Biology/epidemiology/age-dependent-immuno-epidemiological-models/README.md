# Age-dependent immuno-epidemiological models

Epidemic models structured by chronological age in which infectivity is the age-dependent total viral load produced by a within-host immunological model, and recovery and death occur at distributed times after infection. Special choices of the recovery and death kernels reduce the model to an age-structured SIR system or to an age-dependent delay system, each with an explicit reproduction number and final-size relation.
