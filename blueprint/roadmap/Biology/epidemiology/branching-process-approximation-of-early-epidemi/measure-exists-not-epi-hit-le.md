# Birthday bound on wasted contacts

For a [model](is-early-epidemic.md) with population size `N`, a set `S₀` of initially susceptible
individuals of the population `{0, …, N − 1}` and `M ∈ ℕ`, the probability that some contact `j < M`
does not [hit](epi-hit.md) is at most `(M (N − #S₀) + C(M, 2)) / N`, in `[0, ∞]` with `1/0 = ∞`; `P`
of an event whose measurability is not assumed is its outer measure.
