# Contacts made by the first n generations of the branching process

For offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), the
number of contacts made by generations `0, …, n − 1` of the branching process is
`D_n = ∑_{m < n} Z_{m+1}`, where `Z_m` are the [generation sizes](bp-size.md) of the branching
process; `D_0 = 0`.
