# Survival to generation n is at least the survival probability

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℕ` (the offspring law). For every `n`, `1 − q ≤ 1 − 𝓛_n({0})`,
where `𝓛_n` is the
[law of the `n`-th generation size](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/law.md)
of the Galton–Watson process with offspring law `ν` and `q` is
[extinction probability](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/extinction-prob.md)
`q` of `ν`. In the [large-population limit](tendsto-measure-real-epi-size-ne-zero.md),
`1 − 𝓛_n({0})` is the probability that the epidemic survives `n` generations.
