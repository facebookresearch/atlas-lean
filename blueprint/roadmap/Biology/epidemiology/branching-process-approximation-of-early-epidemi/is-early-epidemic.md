# Hypotheses of the coupled early-epidemic model

For a measure `P` on a measurable space `Ω`, a measure `ν` on `ℕ` and `N ∈ ℕ`, random offspring
numbers `ξ_n(i) : Ω → ℕ` and contact targets `U_j : Ω → {0, …, N − 1}` form a model if `ξ` is an
[offspring family](is-offspring-family.md) with law `ν`, the targets `U_j` are independent under
`P`, and `P(U_j = a) = 1/N` for every contact `j` and every individual `a` of the population
`{0, …, N − 1}`: the targets are uniform. Nothing is assumed about the joint law of the offspring
numbers and the targets. The index case is not an individual of the population, or is an individual
left out of the set `S₀` of initially susceptible individuals.
