# Zero is absorbing for the epidemic

For offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), contact
targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and a finite
set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially susceptible individuals, and every `n`, if the
[number of infectives](epi-size.md) `I_n` of generation `n` of the epidemic is `0` then
`I_{n+1} = 0`.
