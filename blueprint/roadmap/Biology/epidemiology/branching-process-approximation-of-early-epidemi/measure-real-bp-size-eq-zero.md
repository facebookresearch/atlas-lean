# Extinction of the branching process by generation n

Let `ξ` be an [offspring family](is-offspring-family.md) under `P` whose law `ν` is a probability
measure. Then for every `n`, `P(Z_n = 0) = 𝓛_n({0})`, where `Z_n` is the
[generation size](bp-size.md) and `𝓛_n` is the
[law of the `n`-th generation size](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/law.md)
of the Galton–Watson process with offspring law `ν`; this value is
[the n-th iterate of the offspring generating function at 0](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/law-real-zero.md).
