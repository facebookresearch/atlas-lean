# Survival to generation n is non-increasing in n

Let `ν` be a probability measure on `ℕ` (the offspring law). Then `n ↦ 1 − 𝓛_n({0})` is
non-increasing, where `𝓛_n` is the
[law of the `n`-th generation size](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/law.md)
of the Galton–Watson process with offspring law `ν`.
