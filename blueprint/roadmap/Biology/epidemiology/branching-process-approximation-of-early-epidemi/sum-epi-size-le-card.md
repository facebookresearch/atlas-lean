# Total infectives with an index case inside a finite population

Let the type `α` of individuals be finite, let `S₀ ⊆ α`, and suppose that some individual `i₀ ∈ α`
is not in `S₀` (for example an index case `i₀` from the population, who is not initially
susceptible). Then for all offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of
generation `n`), all contact targets `U_0, U_1, …` (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and every `n`,
`∑_{m ≤ n} I_m ≤ |α|`, where `I_m` is the [number of infectives](epi-size.md) of generation `m`: the
infections of generations `1, …, n` go to distinct individuals of `S₀`, and `#S₀ < |α|`.
