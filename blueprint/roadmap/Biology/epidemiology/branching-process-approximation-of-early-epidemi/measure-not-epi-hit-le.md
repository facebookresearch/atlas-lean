# A contact is wasted with probability at most (N − #S₀ + j)/N

For a [model](is-early-epidemic.md) with population size `N`, a set `S₀` of initially susceptible
individuals of the population `{0, …, N − 1}` and a contact number `j`, the probability that contact
`j` does not [hit](epi-hit.md) (its target is not initially susceptible, or was contacted before) is
at most `(N − #S₀ + j) / N`, in `[0, ∞]` with `1/0 = ∞`; `P` of an event whose measurability is not
assumed is its outer measure.
