# Canonical probability space of the coupled model

For a probability measure `ν` on `ℕ` and `N ≥ 1`, the product measure on
`ℕ^(ℕ × ℕ) × {0, …, N − 1}^ℕ` under which the offspring coordinates `ξ_n(i)` are independent with
law `ν`, the target coordinates `U_j` are independent and uniform on `{0, …, N − 1}`, and the two
families are independent.
