# The epidemic is dominated by the branching process

For offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), contact
targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and a finite
set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially susceptible individuals, the [number of infectives](epi-size.md) `I_n` of
generation `n` of the epidemic and the [generation sizes](bp-size.md) `Z_n` of the branching process
satisfy `I_n ≤ Z_n` for every `n`.
