# Equal generation sizes while every contact hits

For offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), contact
targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and a finite
set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially susceptible individuals, and natural numbers `M` and `n`, suppose that
each of the first `M` contacts (numbers `j < M`) [hits](epi-hit.md) and that the
[contact count](bp-contacts.md) of the branching process satisfies `D_n ≤ M`. Then the
[number of infectives](epi-size.md) `I_n` of generation `n` of the epidemic equals the
[generation size](bp-size.md) of the branching process: `I_n = Z_n`.
