# Almost-sure eventual extinction with finitely many susceptibles

For a probability measure `P` on a measurable space `Ω`, any random offspring numbers `ξ_n(i)` (the
contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), any random contact targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a
type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and a finite set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially
susceptible individuals, `P(I_n = 0 for some n) = 1`, where `I_n` is the
[number of infectives](epi-size.md) of generation `n`. No law or measurability is assumed: the event
is the whole space, since [`I_{#S₀ + 1} = 0` always](epi-size-card-add-one-eq-zero.md). Eventual
extinction therefore cannot distinguish minor from major outbreaks.
