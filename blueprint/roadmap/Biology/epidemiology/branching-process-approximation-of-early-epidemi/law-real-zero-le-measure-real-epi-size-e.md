# Extinction by generation n is at least as likely for the epidemic as for the branching process

Let `ξ` be an [offspring family](is-offspring-family.md) under `P` whose law `ν` is a probability
measure, let the contact targets be `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j`
goes to `U_j`) (no measurability or law is assumed), and let `S₀` be a finite set of initially
susceptible individuals. Then for every `n`, `𝓛_n({0}) ≤ P(I_n = 0)`, where `𝓛_n` is the
[law of the `n`-th generation size](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/law.md)
of the Galton–Watson process with offspring law `ν` and `I_n` the
[number of infectives](epi-size.md) of generation `n`; here `P` of the event `{I_n = 0}`, whose
measurability is not assumed, is its outer measure.
