# Infections in generations 1 to n are the hits

For offspring numbers `ξ_n(i) ∈ ℕ` (the contacts of the `i`-th infective of generation `n`), contact
targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`) and a finite
set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially susceptible individuals, and every `n`, `∑_{m < n} I_{m+1}` equals the
number of contacts `j < D'_n` that [hit](epi-hit.md), where `I_m` is the
[number of infectives](epi-size.md) of generation `m` of the epidemic and `D'_n` is the number of
contacts made by its generations `0, …, n − 1`, the second component of its [state](epi.md).
