# At most as many hits as initially susceptible individuals

For contact targets `U_0, U_1, …` in a type `α` of individuals (contact number `j` goes to `U_j`), a
finite set `S₀ ⊆ α` of initially susceptible individuals and `D ∈ ℕ`, the number of contacts `j < D`
that [hit](epi-hit.md) is at most `#S₀`: hits go to distinct initially susceptible individuals.
