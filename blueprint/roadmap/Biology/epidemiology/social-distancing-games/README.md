# Social distancing games

Voluntary social distancing costs the individual who practices it but lowers transmission for everyone, an externality that depends on what the rest of the population does. Treating the daily investment in distancing as a strategy in a population game coupled to SIR dynamics, the equilibrium behaviour is a feedback rule in which each susceptible person balances the marginal cost of distancing against the marginal reduction in the expected cost of becoming infected.
