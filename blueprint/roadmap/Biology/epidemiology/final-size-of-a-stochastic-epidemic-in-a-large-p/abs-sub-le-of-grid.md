# Monotone H θ-close to f of slope ≤ 1 on a d-grid is (θ + d)-close

Let $H : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ be nondecreasing and $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy
$f(y) - f(x) \le y - x$ whenever $0 \le x \le y$. Let $d \ge 1$ and $c$ be natural numbers and
$\theta$ a real number. If
$|H(i d) - f(i d)| \le \theta \quad \text{for every } i \le \lfloor c/d \rfloor + 1$, then
$|H(c) - f(c)| \le \theta + d$.
