# For $R > 1$, near-roots in the unit interval lie near $0$ or $\tau(R)$

Let $R > 1$ and $\delta > 0$ be real, and let $\tau(R) \in (0, 1)$ be the
[positive root](final-size-root.md) of the final-size equation $1 - x = e^{-Rx}$. There is
$\eta > 0$ such that every $x \in [0, 1]$ with $\bigl|1 - e^{-Rx} - x\bigr| \le \eta$ satisfies
$x < \delta$ or $|x - \tau(R)| < \delta$.
