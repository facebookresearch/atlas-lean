# For s ≤ n, n ≥ 1, s(1 − (1 − 1/n)^c) = n(1 − e^(−c/n)) ± (n − s + c/n)

For all reals $n \ge 1$ and $s \le n$ and every $c \in \mathbb{N}$,
$|s (1 - ((n-1)/n)^c) - n (1 - e^{-c/n})| \le (n - s) + c/n$. With $n = N$ and $s = \#S_0$, the
first term is the mean number of hits among $c$ contacts
([Chebyshev bound for the hits](measure-real-le-abs-hits-sub.md)).
