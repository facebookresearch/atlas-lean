# Configurations: offspring numbers and contact targets

For $N \in \mathbb{N}$, a configuration is a pair $w = (w_1, w_2)$ of an array
$w_1 : \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, where $w_1(n, i)$ is the number of contacts
made by the $i$-th individual of generation $n$, and a sequence
$w_2 : \mathbb{N} \to \{0, \dots, N-1\}$, where $w_2(j)$ is the target of contact number $j$.
