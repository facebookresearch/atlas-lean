# Configuration of the model at an outcome

For maps $\xi_{n,i} : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ and $U_j : \Omega \to \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ and an outcome
$\omega \in \Omega$, the configuration at $\omega$ is the [configuration](config.md)
$w(\omega) = (w_1, w_2)$ with $w_1(n, i) = \xi_{n,i}(\omega)$ and $w_2(j) = U_j(\omega)$.
