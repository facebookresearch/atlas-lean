# One offspring number is independent of the rest of the configuration

Let the offspring numbers $\xi_{n,i}$ and the contact targets $U_j$ form a
[final-size epidemic model](is-final-size-epidemic.md) on a probability space $(\Omega, P)$, in the
population $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$, with offspring law $\nu$, a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$. For
every index $p_0 = (n_0, i_0)$, the [configuration without $p_0$](config-without.md),
$\omega \mapsto w^{p_0}(\omega)$, which holds all the other offspring numbers and all the targets,
is independent of $\xi_{n_0,i_0}$.
