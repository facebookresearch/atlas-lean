# With a finite second moment, the extended mean is the real mean

Let $\nu$ be a measure on $\mathbb{N}$ with $\int x^2 \, d\nu(x) < \infty$. Its
[mean offspring number](../../../Probability/branching-processes/galton-watson-process/mean.md)
$\int x \, d\nu(x) \in [0, \infty]$ is finite and equals its [real mean](offspring-mean.md) $m$.
