# Concentration of a count of pairwise non-positively correlated events

Let $P$ be a probability measure, $(E_i)_{i \in \iota}$ measurable events, $s \subseteq \iota$ a
finite set of $\#s$ indices and $p$ a real number with $P(E_i) = p$ for $i \in s$ and
$P(E_i \cap E_j) \le p^2$ for distinct $i, j \in s$. For every real $t > 0$, the number
$Z = \sum_{i \in s} \mathbf{1}_{E_i}$ of these events that occur satisfies
$P\bigl(|Z - \#s\, p| \ge t\bigr) \le \frac{\#s\, p}{t^2}$.
