# Markov bound for the repeated contacts among the first m

Let the offspring numbers $\xi_{n,i}$ and the contact targets $U_j$ form a
[final-size epidemic model](is-final-size-epidemic.md) on a probability space $(\Omega, P)$, in the
population $\{0, \dots, N-1\}$, with offspring law $\nu$, a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$. For
every $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and every real $t > 0$, $P(R_m \ge t) \le \frac{m^2}{N t}$, where $R_m$ is
the [number of the first $m$ contacts to an individual contacted before](repeat-contacts.md) for the
targets $U_j(\omega)$.
