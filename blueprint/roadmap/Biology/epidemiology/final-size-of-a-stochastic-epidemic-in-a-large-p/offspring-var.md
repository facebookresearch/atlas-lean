# Real-valued variance of an offspring law

For a measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb{N}$ with [real mean](offspring-mean.md) $m$ let
$\sigma^2 = \int (x - m)^2 \, d\nu(x)$, a Bochner integral; it is $0$ when $(x - m)^2$ is not
$\nu$-integrable, since the integral of a non-integrable function is $0$. For a probability measure
$\nu$ with $\int x^2 \, d\nu(x) < \infty$, $\sigma^2$ is the variance of $\nu$.
