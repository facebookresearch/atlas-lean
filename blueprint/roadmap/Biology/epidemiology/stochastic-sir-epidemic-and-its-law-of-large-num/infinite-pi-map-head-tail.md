# Joint law of the head and the tail of an i.i.d. sequence

Let `μ` be a probability measure on a measurable space `α`, and let `μ^ℕ` be the infinite product
measure on the space `α^ℕ` of sequences `f = (f 0, f 1, …)` in `α`, with the product σ-algebra:
the law of an i.i.d. sequence with marginal `μ`. The map `f ↦ (f 0, tail f)`, which sends a
sequence to its first term and its [tail](seq-tail.md) `(f 1, f 2, …)`, pushes `μ^ℕ` forward to
the product measure `μ ⊗ μ^ℕ` on `α × α^ℕ`.

So under `μ^ℕ` the head `f 0` and the tail are independent, the head has law `μ`, and the tail has
the same law `μ^ℕ` as the whole sequence.
