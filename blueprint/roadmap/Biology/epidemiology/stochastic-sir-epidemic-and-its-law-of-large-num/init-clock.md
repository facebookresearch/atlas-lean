# Initial clock configuration of two unit clocks

For real sequences $e_1$ and $e_2$, the interarrival times of the infection and recovery clocks,
the initial [clock configuration](clock-config.md) has residual clocks $(e_1(0), e_2(0))$ and
future interarrival times $(e_1(1), e_1(2), \dots)$ and $(e_2(1), e_2(2), \dots)$, the
[tails](seq-tail.md) of the two sequences.
