# A rate-one exponential clock is nonpositive with probability zero

The exponential law `Exp(1)` with rate `1`, the probability measure on `ℝ` with
[density](exp-dens.md) `exp(-x)` for `x ≥ 0` and `0` for `x < 0`, gives the half-line `(-∞, 0]`
mass `0`: `Exp(1)((-∞, 0]) = 0`. So a rate-`1` exponential clock is nonpositive with probability
`0`.

Equivalently, almost every `s ∈ ℝ` satisfies `s > 0` under `Exp(1)`: a rate-`1` exponential clock
is positive almost surely.
