# For $u \le t$, integral of a function equal to $C$ for $u \le s < t$

Let $u \le t$ and $C$ be real numbers and $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ a function with $g(s) = C$
for $u \le s < t$. Then

$$\int_u^t g(s)\, ds = C\,(t - u).$$

Nothing is assumed about $g$ outside $u \le s < t$; in particular the value $g(t)$ does not matter.
