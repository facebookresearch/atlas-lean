# Susceptible count of the SIR path

For real numbers $\beta, \gamma$, a population size $N$, an initial [SIR state](sirstate.md) $x_0$
and real sequences $e_1, e_2$ of interarrival times, the susceptible count
$S(t) \in \{0, \dots, N\}$ at a real time $t$ is the first coordinate of the
[SIR path](sir-path.md) $X(t) = (S(t), I(t))$.
