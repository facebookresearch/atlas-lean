# Clock configuration of a two-channel chain

A clock configuration of a [two-channel chain](two-channel.md) is an element
`((r₁, r₂), (u₁, u₂))` of `(ℝ × ℝ) × (ℝ^ℕ × ℝ^ℕ)`: the residual internal times `r₁, r₂` until the
next ring of each channel, and the sequences `u₁ = (u₁ 0, u₁ 1, …)` and `u₂ = (u₂ 0, u₂ 1, …)` of
the future interarrival times of each channel's unit clock. No sign condition is imposed: the
entries are arbitrary real numbers.
