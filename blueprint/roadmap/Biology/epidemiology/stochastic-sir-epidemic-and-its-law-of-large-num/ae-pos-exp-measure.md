# A rate-one exponential clock is almost surely positive

Under the exponential law `Exp(1)` with rate `1`, the probability measure on `ℝ` with
[density](exp-dens.md) `exp(-x)` for `x ≥ 0` and `0` for `x < 0`, almost every `s ∈ ℝ` satisfies
`s > 0`. So a rate-`1` exponential clock is positive almost surely.
