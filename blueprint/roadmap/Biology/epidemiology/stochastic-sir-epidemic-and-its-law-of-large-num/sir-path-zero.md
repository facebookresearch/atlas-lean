# The SIR path starts at its initial state

Let $\beta, \gamma \ge 0$, let $x_0 = (S_0, I_0)$ be an [SIR state](sirstate.md) with
$S_0 + I_0 \le N$, and let $e_1, e_2$ be sequences of positive interarrival times. Then the
[SIR path](sir-path.md) $X$ driven by $e_1, e_2$ from $x_0$ satisfies $X(0) = x_0$.
