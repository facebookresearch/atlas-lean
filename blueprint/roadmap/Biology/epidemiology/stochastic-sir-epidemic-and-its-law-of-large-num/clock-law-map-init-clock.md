# Law of the initial clock configuration of two independent rate-one exponential clocks

Let $\mathbb{P}$ be the [clock law](clock-law.md) on pairs $(e_1, e_2)$ of real sequences, and let
$\mu = \operatorname{Exp}(1)$. The image of $\mathbb{P}$ under the map sending $(e_1, e_2)$ to its
[initial clock configuration](init-clock.md) $((e_1(0), e_2(0)), (u_1, u_2))$, with
$u_i = (e_i(1), e_i(2), \dots)$, is the [law of a clock configuration](clock-measure.md)

$$(\mu \otimes \mu) \otimes (\mu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}} \otimes \mu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}):$$

its two residual clocks and all its future interarrival times are independent exponentials of
rate 1.
