# Law of the interarrival times of two independent unit clocks

The clock law is the probability measure

$$\mathbb{P} = \mu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}} \otimes \mu^{\otimes \mathbb{N}}$$

on pairs $(e_1, e_2)$ of real sequences, where $\mu = \operatorname{Exp}(1)$ is the exponential law
of rate 1 (density $e^{-x}$ on $[0, \infty)$): the interarrival times $e_1(0), e_1(1), \dots$ and
$e_2(0), e_2(1), \dots$ are all independent, each with law $\mu$. Under it the
[counting path](clock-count.md) of each sequence is a unit-rate Poisson process (for the
[infection clock](is-poisson-process-clock-count-fst.md) and the
[recovery clock](is-poisson-process-clock-count-snd.md)).
