# The path of the clock chain on the real line

Let $K$ be a [two-channel chain](two-channel.md) on a set $\iota$, $x \in \iota$ and $c$ a
[clock configuration](clock-config.md). The real path extends the [path of the clock chain](path.md)
$X_t(x, c)$, defined for $t \ge 0$, to all real times by

$$\tilde X_t(x, c) = X_{\max(t, 0)}(x, c), \qquad t \in \mathbb{R}:$$

it is constant, equal to $X_0(x, c)$, for $t \le 0$.
