# The clock count is $k$ between the $k$-th and the next arrival

Let $e$ be a sequence of positive interarrival times, with
[arrival times](arrival.md) $A(k) = e(0) + \dots + e(k - 1)$ (so $A(0) = 0$). If $v \ge 0$ and
$A(k) \le v < A(k + 1)$, then the [counting path](clock-count.md) of $e$ at $v$ is $Y(v) = k$.
