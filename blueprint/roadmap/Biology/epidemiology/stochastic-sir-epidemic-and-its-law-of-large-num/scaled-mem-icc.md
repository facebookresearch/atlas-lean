# The scaled counts of a chain path lie in the unit interval

Let $N \ge 1$ and let $(S, I, R)$ satisfy the [random time-change equations of the SIR chain](is-chain-path.md) with population size $N$, for any
real $\beta$ and $\gamma$ and any driving functions $y_1$, $y_2$. Then $S(t)/N$, $I(t)/N$ and
$R(t)/N$ lie in $[0, 1]$ for every $t \ge 0$.
