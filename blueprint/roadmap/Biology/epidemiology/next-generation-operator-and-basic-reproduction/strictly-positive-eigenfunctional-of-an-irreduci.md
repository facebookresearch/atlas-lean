# Strictly positive eigenfunctional of an irreducible compact positive operator

Let `μ` be a σ-finite measure on `Ω` and `K` a [positive](is-positive.md), compact,
[ideal-irreducible](is-ideal-irreducible.md) operator on `L¹(Ω, μ)` whose spectral radius, the
[basic reproduction ratio](generation-growth.md) `R₀ = r(K)`, is positive. Then the adjoint of
`K` has a strictly positive eigenfunctional for `R₀`: there is `ψ ∈ L^∞(Ω, μ)` with `ψ > 0`
`μ`-almost everywhere and `∫ ψ (K φ) dμ = R₀ ∫ ψ φ dμ` for every `φ ∈ L¹(Ω, μ)`.

This is the Perron–Frobenius theory of irreducible positive operators whose spectral radius is a
pole of the resolvent (Schaefer 1974, §V.5), on the Banach lattice `L¹(Ω, μ)`, whose closed ideals
are the functions vanishing off a measurable set and whose dual is `L^∞(Ω, μ)` for σ-finite `μ`.
A positive spectral radius of a compact operator is a pole of its resolvent, and a positive
functional on `L¹(Ω, μ)` is strictly positive exactly when its density is positive almost
everywhere.
