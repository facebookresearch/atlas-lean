# Generating function of the negative-binomial law in closed form

Let $k > 0$, $R \ge 0$ and $s \in [0, 1]$. The probability generating function
$\sum_n \nu(\{n\}) s^n$ of the [negative-binomial offspring law](nb-offspring.md) $\nu$ with mean $R$
and dispersion $k$ equals $G_{R,k}(s) = (1 + (R/k)(1 - s))^{-k}$, the
[negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md).
