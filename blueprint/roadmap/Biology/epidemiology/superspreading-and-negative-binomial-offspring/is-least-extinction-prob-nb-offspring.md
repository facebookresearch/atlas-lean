# Negative-binomial extinction probability is the least fixed point

Let $k > 0$ and $R \ge 0$. The extinction probability $\sup_n \mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0)$ of the
Galton–Watson process started from one individual with the
[negative-binomial offspring law](nb-offspring.md) of mean $R$ and dispersion $k$ is the least
$s \in [0, 1]$ with $G_{R,k}(s) = s$, where $G_{R,k}(s) = (1 + (R/k)(1 - s))^{-k}$ is the
[negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md).
