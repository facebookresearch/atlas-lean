# Negative-binomial law with dispersion one is geometric

For all real $R$ and $s$, the [negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md) with dispersion
$k = 1$ is

$$G_{R,1}(s) = \frac{1}{1 + R(1 - s)},$$

with $1/0$ read as $0$. For $R \ge 0$ this is the generating function of the geometric law on
$\{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$ with mean $R$.
