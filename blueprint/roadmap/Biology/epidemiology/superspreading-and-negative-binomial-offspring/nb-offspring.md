# Negative-binomial offspring distribution

For real $R$ and $k$, the negative-binomial offspring law is the measure on $\mathbb{N}$ that gives
each $n$ the mass $\max(P_{R,k}(n), 0)$, where $P_{R,k}(n)$ are the
[negative-binomial probabilities](nb-pmf.md). For $k > 0$ and $R \ge 0$ these masses are the
probabilities themselves.
