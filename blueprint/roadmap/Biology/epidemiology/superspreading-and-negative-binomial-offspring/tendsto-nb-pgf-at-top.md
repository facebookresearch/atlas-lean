# Poisson limit

For all real $R$ and $s$, the [negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md) satisfies
$G_{R,k}(s) \to e^{-R(1 - s)}$ as $k \to \infty$. For $R \ge 0$ the limit is the generating function of the
Poisson law with mean $R$; for $R < 0$ it is not the generating function of a probability law (at $R = -1$,
$s = 0$ it equals $e > 1$).
