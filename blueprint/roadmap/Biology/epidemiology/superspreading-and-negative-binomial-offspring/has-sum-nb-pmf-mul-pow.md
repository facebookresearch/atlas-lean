# Generating-function identity

Let $k > 0$, $R \ge 0$ and $s \in [0, 1]$. The series $\sum_{n \ge 0} P_{R,k}(n) \, s^n$ of the
[negative-binomial probabilities](nb-pmf.md) converges to

$$G_{R,k}(s) = \left(1 + \frac{R}{k}(1 - s)\right)^{-k},$$

the [negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md).
