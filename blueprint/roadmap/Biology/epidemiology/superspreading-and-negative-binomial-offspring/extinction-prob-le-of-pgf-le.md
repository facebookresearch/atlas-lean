# Comparison principle for Galton–Watson extinction

Let $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$ be probability distributions on $\mathbb{N}$ with generating functions
$f_i(s) = \sum_{n} \nu_i(\{n\}) s^n$. If $f_2(s) \le f_1(s)$ for every $s \in [0, 1]$, then the
extinction probabilities of the Galton–Watson processes with offspring laws $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$, each
started from one individual, satisfy $q_2 \le q_1$. Here the extinction probability is
$q = \sup_n \mathbb{P}(Z_n = 0)$, where $Z_n$ is the size of generation $n$.
