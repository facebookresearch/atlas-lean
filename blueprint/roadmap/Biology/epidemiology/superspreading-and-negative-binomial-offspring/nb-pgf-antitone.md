# Monotonicity in the dispersion

Let $R \ge 0$, $s \in [0, 1]$ and $0 < k_1 \le k_2$. The
[negative-binomial generating function](nb-pgf.md) satisfies $G_{R,k_2}(s) \le G_{R,k_1}(s)$.
Equivalently, with $x = R(1 - s) \ge 0$, the function $k \mapsto k \log(1 + x/k)$ is nondecreasing on
$(0, \infty)$.
