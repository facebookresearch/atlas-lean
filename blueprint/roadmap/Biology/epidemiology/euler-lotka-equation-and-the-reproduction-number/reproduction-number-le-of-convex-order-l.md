# More spread in the generation interval lowers the reproductive number

Let $g_1$ and $g_2$ be probability measures on $\mathbb{R}$ with $g_1 \le_{\mathrm{cx}} g_2$ in the
[convex order](convex-order-le.md), let $r \in \mathbb{R}$, and suppose $a \mapsto e^{-ra}$ is
integrable with respect to both $g_1$ and $g_2$. Then the [reproductive numbers](reproduction-number.md)
at growth rate $r$ satisfy $R(g_2) \le R(g_1)$.
