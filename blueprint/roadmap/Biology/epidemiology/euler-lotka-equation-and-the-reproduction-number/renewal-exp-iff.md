# Renewal equation for exponential growth

Let $g$ be a measure on $\mathbb{R}$ and $r, c, R \in \mathbb{R}$ with $c \ne 0$. The exponential
$b(t) = c\,e^{rt}$ satisfies $b(t) = R \int b(t - a) \, g(da)$ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$ if and
only if $R \, M_g(-r) = 1$, where $M_g(-r) = \int e^{-ra} \, g(da)$.

Integrals of non-integrable functions are taken to be $0$ here. So if $a \mapsto e^{-ra}$ is not
$g$-integrable, both the integral in the renewal equation and $M_g(-r)$ are $0$, and both sides of the
equivalence are false.
