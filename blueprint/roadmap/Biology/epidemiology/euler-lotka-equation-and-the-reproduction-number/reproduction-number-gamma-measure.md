# Gamma generation interval

Let $\alpha > 0$ and $\beta > 0$, and let $g$ be the gamma distribution with shape $\alpha$ and rate
$\beta$, with density $\beta^\alpha a^{\alpha - 1} e^{-\beta a} / \Gamma(\alpha)$ for $a \ge 0$. For
every real $r > -\beta$, the [reproductive number](reproduction-number.md) of $g$ at growth rate $r$
is $R = (1 + r/\beta)^\alpha$.
