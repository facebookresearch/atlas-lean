# Two-strain competition with asymmetric temporary immunity

When two strains of a pathogen confer temporary immunity of different durations and only partial cross-immunity, competitive exclusion by the strain with the larger basic reproduction number can fail. Invasion reproduction numbers that combine transmissibility with immunity periods and cross-protection decide exclusion versus coexistence, and the coexistence equilibrium is unique.
