# Discrete-time Markov SIRS epidemics on networks

The exact $3^n$-state discrete-time Markov chain of an SIRS epidemic on a graph with $n$ nodes, compared with its $2n$-dimensional mean-field map and that map's linearisation. One spectral threshold, $\beta\lambda_{\max}(A)/\delta < 1$, gives global stability of the disease-free fixed point for both deterministic maps and logarithmic mixing time for the exact chain. Direct immunization moves the threshold.
