# Infection-age SIR on heterogeneous random graphs

Individual-based SIR epidemics with random infection-age dependent infectivity, individual characteristics (location, age class) and a non-homogeneous random contact graph whose edge probabilities depend on those characteristics. Under a scaling in which the mean infection pressure per individual stays of order one, the measure-valued epidemic converges to a deterministic system driven by an integrated graphon-like kernel, with no density assumption beyond vanishing variance of the edge weights.
