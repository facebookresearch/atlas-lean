# Jacobian of the transfer rates at the disease-free equilibrium

For all real `σ` and `γ`, the [transfer rates](transfers.md) `𝒱(E, I) = (σ E, -σ E + γ I)` have,
at `E = I = 0`, the derivative with matrix

`V = [[σ, 0], [-σ, γ]]`

(rows listed in order); indeed `𝒱` is the linear map with matrix `V`.
