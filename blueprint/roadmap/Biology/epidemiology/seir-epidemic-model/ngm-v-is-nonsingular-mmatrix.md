# For σ, γ > 0, the transfer matrix V is a nonsingular M-matrix

For `σ, γ > 0`, the matrix `V = [[σ, 0], [-σ, γ]]`, the
[Jacobian of the transfer rates](has-fderiv-at-transfers.md), is a
[nonsingular M-matrix](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-nonsingular-mmatrix.md):
its off-diagonal entries `0` and `-σ` are nonpositive, and its complex eigenvalues `σ` and `γ` have
positive real part.
