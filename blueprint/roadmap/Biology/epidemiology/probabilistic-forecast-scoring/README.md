# Probabilistic forecast scoring

Epidemic forecasts of targets such as peak week, peak incidence and seasonal incidence are issued as probability distributions over bins. The logarithmic score rewards forecasts that put high probability on the outcome that occurs, is strictly proper, and explains a structural advantage of equal-weight ensembles of forecasts.
