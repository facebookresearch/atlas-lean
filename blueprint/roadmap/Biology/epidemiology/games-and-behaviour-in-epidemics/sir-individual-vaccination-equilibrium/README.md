# Individual vaccination equilibria in the SIR model

Self-interested vaccination decisions in an SIR epidemic, modeled as a mean-field timing game: each susceptible individual weighs the cost of vaccination against its residual risk of infection, and an equilibrium is a societal vaccination policy reproduced by the aggregate of individual best responses. Covers the optimal individual strategy, existence and uniqueness of the equilibrium, and its comparison with the societal optimum.
