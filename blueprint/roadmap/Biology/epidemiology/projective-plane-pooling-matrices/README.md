# Projective-plane pooling matrices

Non-adaptive pooling designs for SARS-CoV-2 group testing built by packing the pencils of lines of a finite projective plane: samples are lines, pools are points, and any two samples share at most one pool, which guarantees exact two-stage decoding when positives are few.
