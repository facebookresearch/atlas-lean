# Chase-escape predator–prey SIR dynamics

A variant of SIR dynamics in which infected individuals infect susceptible neighbours at rate $\lambda$ while recovery itself spreads, recovered individuals converting infected neighbours at rate $1$ (the chase-escape or predator–prey process). On large complete graphs the process has a sharp critical value $\lambda = 1$ for global infection and two further phase transitions inside the subcritical regime.
