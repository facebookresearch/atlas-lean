# Chain-binomial SVIR model with contact-driven vaccination

Discrete-time Reed–Frost-type stochastic SVIR models for influenza in a closed population, in which each susceptible in a time step either becomes infected, becomes vaccinated after a non-infecting contact with infectious individuals, or stays susceptible, giving trinomial Markov transition probabilities, with fixed periods of vaccine protection and infectiousness.
