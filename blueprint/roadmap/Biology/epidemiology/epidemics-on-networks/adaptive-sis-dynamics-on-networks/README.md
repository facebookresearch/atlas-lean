# Adaptive SIS dynamics on networks

Individual-based SIS dynamics on contact networks in which infection and cure rates vary in time, either exogenously or through adaptive defense. Die-out is governed by the leading adjacency eigenvalue compared with the ratio of time-averaged cure and infection rates, and cure rates that grow with local infection drive the infection to extinction with bounded cumulative burden.
