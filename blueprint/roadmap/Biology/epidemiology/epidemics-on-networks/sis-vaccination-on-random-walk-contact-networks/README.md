# SIS vaccination on random-walk contact networks

SIS epidemics with vaccination and waning protection among individuals who move as random walkers and contact others within individual-specific interaction radii. In the mean-field limit the basic reproduction number is a weighted second moment of the radius distribution and governs extinction versus endemic persistence.
