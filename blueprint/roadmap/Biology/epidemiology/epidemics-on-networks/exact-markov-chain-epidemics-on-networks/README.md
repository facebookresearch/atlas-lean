# Exact Markov-chain epidemics on networks

Discrete-time SIS epidemics on an arbitrary contact graph, modelled exactly as $2^n$-state Markov chains, compared with their $O(n)$-dimensional mean-field maps. The spectral threshold $\beta\lambda_{\max}(A)/\delta$ separates $O(\log n)$ extinction of the exact chain from a unique, globally attracting endemic fixed point of the mean-field map.
