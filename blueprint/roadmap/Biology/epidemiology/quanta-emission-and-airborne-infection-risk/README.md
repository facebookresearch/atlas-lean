# Quanta emission and airborne infection risk

A well-mixed box model turns the emission rate of infectious "quanta" by an infected occupant into an indoor quanta concentration, an inhaled dose and, through an exponential dose-response law, a probability of infection. Averaging that probability over the variability of emission gives an individual risk and, multiplied by the number of exposed susceptibles, an event-level reproduction number.
