# Two-strain competition with delayed cross-immunity

A susceptible–infected model with demography in which an emerging strain can reinfect individuals recovered from the established strain after a fixed period of temporary cross-immunity, modelled by a discrete delay. The strain-specific reproduction numbers govern existence and stability of the boundary equilibria, the delay never changes their stability, and immune evasion forces the emerging strain's reproduction number to be below the established strain's, by a margin that grows with the latter, for the established strain to exclude it.
