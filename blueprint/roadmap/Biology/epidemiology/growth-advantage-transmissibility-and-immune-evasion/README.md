# Growth advantage, transmissibility and immune evasion

When a new pathogen variant replaces a resident one, its observed growth advantage can come from higher intrinsic transmissibility, from escape of existing immunity, or from both. A simple transmission model makes the trade-off between the two explicit and shows that the split cannot be identified from growth data alone without knowing population immunity.
