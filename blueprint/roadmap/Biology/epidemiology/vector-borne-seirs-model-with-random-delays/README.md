# Vector-borne SEIRS model with random delays

Host compartments of a mosquito-borne infection (malaria type) in which the incubation period in the vector, the incubation period in the host and the period of acquired immunity are random delays with arbitrary densities, and transmission enters through a saturating nonlinear incidence. Extinction and persistence are governed by a constant-delay reproduction number together with the expected survival probability of the parasite over its full life cycle.
