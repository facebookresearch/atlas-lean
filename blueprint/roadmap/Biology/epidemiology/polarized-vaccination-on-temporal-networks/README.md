# Polarized vaccination on temporal networks

SIS-type epidemic models with testing and quarantine on activity-driven temporal networks, where vaccinated and non-vaccinated subpopulations interact preferentially within their own group. The mean-field epidemic threshold gives the testing rate needed to stabilize the disease-free state, as a function of vaccine efficacy against transmission and against severe illness.
