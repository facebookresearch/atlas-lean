# Structural identifiability and observability of compartmental models

Before calibrating an epidemic compartmental model to data, one should check whether its parameters and unmeasured states can in principle be recovered from the chosen outputs. Structural local identifiability and observability are decided symbolically by the rank of a matrix of Lie derivatives of the output along the dynamics, with unknown parameters appended to the state and time-varying parameters treated as unknown inputs.
