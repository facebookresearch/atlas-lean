# An SIR solution is an integral curve on the half-line

A solution $(S, I, R)$ of the SIR system for $t \ge 0$ packs into an integral curve of the SIR vector field
on $[0, \infty)$: at each $t \ge 0$, the derivative of the curve within $[0, \infty)$ is the field at the
curve's position. The two-sided derivative at $0$ is weakened to a right derivative.
