# An integral curve on the half-line with a derivative at zero gives an SIR solution

An integral curve $\alpha$ of the SIR vector field $F$ on $[0, \infty)$ gives a solution of the SIR system,
provided $\alpha$ also has the two-sided derivative $F(\alpha(0))$ at $0$.

An integral curve on $[0, \infty)$ only provides a right derivative at $0$, whereas a solution needs a
two-sided derivative there; at every $t > 0$ the half-line is a neighbourhood of $t$, so the derivative
is already two-sided. The hypothesis at $0$ cannot be dropped.
