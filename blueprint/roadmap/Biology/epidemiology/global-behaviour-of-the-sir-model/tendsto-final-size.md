# Limits and final size of the SIR epidemic

Along a solution of the SIR system with $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$, $N \ge 0$ and nonnegative initial
compartments, there are limits $S(t) \to S_\infty$, $I(t) \to 0$ and $R(t) \to R_\infty$ as
$t \to \infty$, with $0 \le S_\infty \le S(0)$, $S_\infty + R_\infty = S(0) + I(0) + R(0)$, and the
final-size relation

$$S_\infty = S(0) \, e^{-R_0 (S(0) + I(0) - S_\infty) / N}, \qquad R_0 = \beta / \gamma,$$

where the exponent is taken to be $0$ when $N = 0$. The relation does not assume $R(0) = 0$.
