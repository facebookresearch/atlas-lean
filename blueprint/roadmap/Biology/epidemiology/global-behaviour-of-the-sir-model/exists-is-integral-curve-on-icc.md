# Local existence of SIR integral curves (Picard–Lindelöf)

For all real parameters $\beta, \gamma, N$ and every point $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^3$, there are
$\varepsilon > 0$ and an integral curve $\alpha$ of the SIR vector field on $[-\varepsilon, \varepsilon]$
with $\alpha(0) = x_0$. Only local existence is claimed; nonnegativity and global existence are not.
