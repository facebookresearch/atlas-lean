# Infectives in integrating-factor form on an interval

If $(S, I, R)$ satisfy the SIR equations at every time of $[0, T]$, with two-sided derivatives, then

$$I(t) = I(0) \exp\Bigl(\int_0^t \Bigl(\frac{\beta S(s)}{N} - \gamma\Bigr) ds\Bigr) \quad \text{for } t \in [0, T].$$

Here $\beta S(s) / N$ is read as $0$ when $N = 0$; the $-\gamma$ term remains.
