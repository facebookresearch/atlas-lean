# SIR solutions as packed curves

The functions $(S, I, R)$ solve the SIR system for all times $t \ge 0$ (that is, at every $t \ge 0$,
including $t = 0$, each of $S$, $I$, $R$ has the two-sided derivative given by the SIR equations)
exactly when, at every $t \ge 0$, the packed curve has the two-sided derivative
$F(S(t), I(t), R(t))$, where $F$ is the SIR vector field.
