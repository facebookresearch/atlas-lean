# The SIR field is locally Lipschitz in space and time

Viewed as a function of time and state, the SIR vector field $F$ is locally Lipschitz in the state,
uniformly in time: every point $(t_0, x_0)$ has a neighbourhood $U$ and a constant $C$ with
$\|F(x) - F(y)\| \le C \|x - y\|$ whenever $(t, x)$ and $(t, y)$ lie in $U$. One can take the slab
$\mathbb{R} \times \bar B(x_0, 1)$, on which the continuously differentiable, time-independent field is
Lipschitz in the state.
