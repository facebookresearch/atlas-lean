# Forward uniqueness of SIR solutions

Two solutions of the SIR system, for any real parameters, with the same initial values agree at every
time $t \ge 0$.

Proof: on $[0, t]$ both packed curves are continuous, hence lie in a common closed ball, on which the
continuously differentiable field is Lipschitz; the uniqueness theorem for Lipschitz equations then
applies, and one-sided derivatives at the initial time suffice.
