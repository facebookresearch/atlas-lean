# Uniqueness of SIR integral curves around zero

Integral curves of the SIR vector field on $[-\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_1]$ and
$[-\varepsilon_2, \varepsilon_2]$, with $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2 > 0$, that have the same value at $0$
agree on $[-\min(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2), \min(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2)]$.
