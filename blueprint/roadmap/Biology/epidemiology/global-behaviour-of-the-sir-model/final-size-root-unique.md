# Uniqueness of the final size

For $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$, $N > 0$, $S_0 \ge 0$ and $I_0 > 0$, the final-size equation
$$x = S_0 \, e^{-R_0 (S_0 + I_0 - x) / N}, \qquad R_0 = \beta / \gamma,$$
has at most one root $x \le S_0$. For $\beta = 0$ the root is $S_0$. For $I_0 = 0$, $x = S_0$ is always a
root, and when $R_0 S_0 / N > 1$ there is a second root in $(0, S_0)$ (stated here without proof), so the
hypothesis $I_0 > 0$ is needed.
