# Susceptibles in integrating-factor form on an interval

If $(S, I, R)$ satisfy the SIR equations at every time of $[0, T]$, with two-sided derivatives, then

$$S(t) = S(0) \exp\Bigl(-\int_0^t \frac{\beta I(s)}{N} \, ds\Bigr) \quad \text{for } t \in [0, T].$$

Here $\beta I(s) / N$ is read as $0$ when $N = 0$.
