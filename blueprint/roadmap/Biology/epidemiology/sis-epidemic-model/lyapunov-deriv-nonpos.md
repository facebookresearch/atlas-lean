# The Lyapunov derivative is nonpositive

For real numbers $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma$, $N$ and $S > 0$, with $S^* = N\gamma/\beta$,

$$-\frac{\beta (S - S^*)^2}{S} \le 0.$$

Along a [solution of the SIS system](is-solution.md) with $0 < \gamma < \beta$, $N > 0$ and
$S(0) + I(0) = N$, at times $t \ge 0$ with $S(t), I(t) > 0$, this expression at $S = S(t)$ is the
derivative of the [Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md)
([Derivative of the Lyapunov function along solutions](has-deriv-at-lyapunov.md)). For $\beta = 0$
it is $0$ (and $S^* = 0$ by the convention $x/0 = 0$).
