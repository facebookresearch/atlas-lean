# Linearized SIS threshold, stable side

For $\beta \ge 0$ and $\gamma > 0$, let $F = (\beta)$ and $V = (\gamma)$ be the $1 \times 1$ new-infection
and transfer matrices of the SIS model at the disease-free equilibrium. Every eigenvalue of
$F - V = (\beta - \gamma)$ has negative real part if and only if $\beta < \gamma$, that is, if and only if
$R_0 < 1$, where $R_0 = \beta / \gamma$ is the basic reproduction number. This is the local spectral statement
only; global extinction is the extinction theorem for every SIS solution.
