# Solutions from the open segment keep both compartments positive

Let $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma \ge 0$ and $N > 0$, and let $(S, I)$ be a [solution of the SIS system](is-solution.md) with
$S(0) + I(0) = N$, $S(0) > 0$ and $I(0) > 0$. Then $S(t) > 0$ and $I(t) > 0$ for every $t \ge 0$.

Since $S + I$ is [conserved](total-eq.md), the open segment $\{S + I = N,\ S > 0,\ I > 0\}$ is
therefore forward invariant. The proof uses the
[explicit form of the solution](infected-eq-sis-infected.md).
