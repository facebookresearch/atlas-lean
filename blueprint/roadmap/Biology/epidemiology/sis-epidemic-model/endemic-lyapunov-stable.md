# Lyapunov stability of the endemic equilibrium

Let $\gamma > 0$, $R_0 = \beta/\gamma > 1$ and $N > 0$, and let
$(S^*, I^*) = (N\gamma/\beta,\ N(1 - \gamma/\beta))$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that every [solution of the SIS system](is-solution.md) with
$S(0) + I(0) = N$, $S(0) > 0$, $I(0) > 0$ and $d((S(0), I(0)), (S^*, I^*)) < \delta$ satisfies
$d((S(t), I(t)), (S^*, I^*)) < \varepsilon$ for every $t \ge 0$.

Here $d$ is the maximum distance on $\mathbb{R}^2$,
$d((a, b), (a', b')) = \max(|a - a'|, |b - b'|)$, and $\delta$ depends only on $\varepsilon$,
$\beta$, $\gamma$ and $N$, not on the solution.
