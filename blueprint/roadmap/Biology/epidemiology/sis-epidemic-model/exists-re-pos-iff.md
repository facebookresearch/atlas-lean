# Linearized SIS threshold, unstable side

For $\beta \ge 0$ and $\gamma > 0$, let $F = (\beta)$ and $V = (\gamma)$ be the $1 \times 1$ new-infection
and transfer matrices of the SIS model at the disease-free equilibrium. Then $F - V = (\beta - \gamma)$ has
an eigenvalue with positive real part if and only if $\gamma < \beta$, that is, if and only if $R_0 > 1$,
where $R_0 = \beta / \gamma$ is the basic reproduction number. This is the local spectral statement only;
global convergence to the endemic level is the endemic-limit theorem for every SIS solution.
