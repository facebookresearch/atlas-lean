# Basic reproduction number of the SIS model

For $\beta \ge 0$ and $\gamma > 0$, let $F = (\beta)$ and $V = (\gamma)$ be the $1 \times 1$ new-infection
and transfer matrices. The basic reproduction number, the spectral radius of the next-generation matrix
$F V^{-1} = (\beta / \gamma)$, is $R_0 = \beta / \gamma$: the next-generation matrix is the scalar matrix
$(\beta / \gamma) I$, with spectrum $\{\beta / \gamma\}$. For a population size $N \ne 0$, $F$ and $V$ are the
derivatives at the disease-free equilibrium, in the infected variable, of the new-infection rate
$\beta S I / N$ with $S = N$ and of the recovery transfer $\gamma I$; for $N = 0$ the new-infection term, read
as $0$, is identically zero.
