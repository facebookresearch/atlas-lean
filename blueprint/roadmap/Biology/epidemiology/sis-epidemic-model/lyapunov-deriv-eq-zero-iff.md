# On $S + I = N$ the Lyapunov derivative vanishes exactly at equilibrium

Let $\beta > 0$, let $\gamma$ and $N$ be real, and let $S^* = N\gamma/\beta$ and
$I^* = N(1 - \gamma/\beta)$. For real $S > 0$ and $I$ with $S + I = N$,

$$-\frac{\beta (S - S^*)^2}{S} = 0 \iff (S, I) = (S^*, I^*).$$

No sign condition on $I$, $\gamma$ or $N$ is needed. For $0 < \gamma < \beta$ and $N > 0$, the
left-hand side is the derivative of the [Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md) along
[SIS solutions](has-deriv-at-lyapunov.md) with $S + I = N$, at times where $S, I > 0$.
