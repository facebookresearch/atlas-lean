# For $\beta, S > 0$ on $S + I = N$, the Lyapunov derivative vanishes exactly at equilibrium

Let $\beta > 0$, let $\gamma$ and $N$ be real, and let $S^* = N\gamma/\beta$ and
$I^* = N(1 - \gamma/\beta)$. For real $S > 0$ and $I$ with $S + I = N$,

$$-\frac{\beta (S - S^*)^2}{S} = 0 \iff (S, I) = (S^*, I^*).$$

No sign condition on $I$, $\gamma$ or $N$ is needed. Along a
[solution of the SIS system](is-solution.md) with $0 < \gamma < \beta$, $N > 0$ and
$S(0) + I(0) = N$, at every time $t \ge 0$ with $S(t), I(t) > 0$, the left-hand side at $S = S(t)$
is the derivative of the [Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md)
([Derivative of the Lyapunov function along solutions](has-deriv-at-lyapunov.md)).
