# Coexistence of drug-sensitive and resistant strains

Two-strain SIS models of a drug-sensitive and a costly drug-resistant strain competing for the same hosts. In a well-mixed, partially treated population, competitive exclusion leaves only the strain with the larger basic reproductive number. When the population is divided into demes with preferential within-deme transmission and clustered treatment, both strains can coexist stably, and resistance frequencies can differ widely between neighboring regions.
