# Complexity of infection

The complexity of infection (COI) of a malaria patient is the number of genetically distinct parasite lineages in the blood sample. Biallelic SNP barcodes of a haploid pathogen reveal COI statistically: the more lineages are mixed, the more assays return both alleles, at a rate governed by the allele frequencies and the genotyping error.
