# Kinetic SEIR models with contact distributions

Epidemic models in which each compartment carries a distribution over the individual number of social contacts, evolving by a fast Fokker–Planck relaxation of contact behavior coupled to contact-dependent SEIR transmission, with uncertain social parameters. Their well-posedness underlies selective (contact-targeted) control of non-pharmaceutical interventions.
