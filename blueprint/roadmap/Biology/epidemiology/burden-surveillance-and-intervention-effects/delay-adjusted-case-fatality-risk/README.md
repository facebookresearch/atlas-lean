# Delay-adjusted case fatality risk

Severity of an emerging infection is measured per symptomatic case, but deaths lag onsets and onsets lag infections. Chaining incidence through delay distributions gives the observable death curve, explains why crude death-to-case ratios mislead during epidemic growth, and the delays themselves are estimated from censored and truncated observations.
