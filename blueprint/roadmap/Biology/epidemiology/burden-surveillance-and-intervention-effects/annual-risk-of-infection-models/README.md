# Annual risk of infection models

Catalytic models that convert a time series of the annual hazard of infection into the age-specific probability of having ever been infected, and into the fraction infected recently. They are the standard way to estimate the prevalence of persistent infections, such as latent tuberculosis, from infection-risk surveys and disease prevalence.
