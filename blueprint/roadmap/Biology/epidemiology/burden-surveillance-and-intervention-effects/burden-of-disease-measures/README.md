# Burden-of-disease measures

Summary measures of population health that put fatal and non-fatal outcomes on comparable scales. Disability-adjusted life-years add years of life lost to years lived with disability; years lived with disability weight prevalence by severity-specific disability weights, with a combination rule for co-occurring health states; and direct age standardization makes rates comparable across populations with different age structures.
