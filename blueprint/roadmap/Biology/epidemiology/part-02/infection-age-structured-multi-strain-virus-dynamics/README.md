# Infection-age-structured multi-strain virus dynamics

Within-host virus models in which several strains compete for one target-cell population and infected cells carry an age since infection that modulates virion production and death. Semigroup methods, persistence theory and Volterra-type Lyapunov functionals give threshold and competitive-exclusion results in terms of strain reproduction numbers.
