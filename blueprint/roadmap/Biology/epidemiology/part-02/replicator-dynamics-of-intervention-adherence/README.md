# Replicator dynamics of intervention adherence

Behavioural–epidemiological models in which adherence to a non-pharmaceutical intervention spreads by social learning (replicator dynamics) driven by perceived infection risk and adherence cost, coupled to SIRS transmission. Equilibrium adherence depends only on the cost-to-risk ratio.
