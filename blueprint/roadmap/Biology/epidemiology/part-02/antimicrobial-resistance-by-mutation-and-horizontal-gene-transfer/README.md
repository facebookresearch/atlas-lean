# Antimicrobial resistance by mutation and horizontal gene transfer

Within-host competition between antibiotic-susceptible and resistant bacteria sharing a carrying capacity, where susceptible cells become resistant through spontaneous mutation and through horizontal gene transfer on contact with resistant cells. Threshold numbers built from growth, antibiotic killing, immune clearance and transfer rates decide whether bacteria are cleared, resistance takes over, or both strains coexist; preventive controls on mutation and transfer enter as reductions of these rates.
