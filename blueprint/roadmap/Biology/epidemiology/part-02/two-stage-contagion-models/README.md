# Two-stage contagion models

Compartmental models of contagion in which a first contact with an infectious individual may either infect directly or only weaken a susceptible, who becomes infectious on a second contact. The quadratic second-stage transition produces backward bifurcations and bistability, and with renewal of susceptibles also periodic orbits.
