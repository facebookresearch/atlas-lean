# Waning and boosting of immune status

An individual's immune level against a circulating pathogen decays deterministically between exposures and jumps up at each exposure, by an amount that depends on the level at the time of exposure. With exposures arriving as a Poisson process, the immune level is a piecewise-deterministic Markov process whose transition kernel solves a renewal equation, and the population distribution of immune status converges to a unique stationary density.
