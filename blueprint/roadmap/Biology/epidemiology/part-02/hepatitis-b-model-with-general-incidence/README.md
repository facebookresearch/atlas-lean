# Hepatitis B model with general incidence

A susceptible–infected–recovered hepatitis B model with vaccination of susceptibles and a general incidence function. Its global threshold dynamics are governed by the basic reproduction number, and a nonstandard finite difference (NSFD) discretization preserves positivity, boundedness and the stability of equilibria for every step size.
