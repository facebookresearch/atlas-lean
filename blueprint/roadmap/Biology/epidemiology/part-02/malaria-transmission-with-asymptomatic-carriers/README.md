# Malaria transmission with asymptomatic carriers

Naturally acquired immunity to *Plasmodium* accumulates with repeated exposure and makes a growing share of infections asymptomatic, yet asymptomatic carriers still infect mosquitoes. A host–vector SEYAR–SEI model whose exposed humans split into symptomatic and asymptomatic classes, with the asymptomatic share driven by an exposure-dependent immunity equation, has a square-root reproduction number in which asymptomatic carriers add to the human-to-mosquito term; neglecting them underestimates the threshold.
