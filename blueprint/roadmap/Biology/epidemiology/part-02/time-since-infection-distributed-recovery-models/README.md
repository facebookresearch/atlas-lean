# Time-since-infection distributed recovery models

Compartmental SIR models assume exponentially distributed infectious periods. Making recovery and death rates depend on time since infection, through kernels applied to the incidence history, gives integro-differential models that fit observed onset-to-recovery and onset-to-death distributions, reduce to SIR in a limiting case, and let the transmission rate be read off directly from incidence and prevalence data.
