# Branching processes with lagged Poisson offspring

When each case generates new cases with a delay of $l = 1,\dots,d$ time steps, with Poisson numbers of mean $\Psi_l$, the case counts $I_n$ form a multitype Bienaymé–Galton–Watson process on the vector of the last $d$ generations. Its growth rate, extinction probability, extinction-time distribution and total outbreak size have explicit forms in terms of $R_\infty = \sum_l \Psi_l$.
