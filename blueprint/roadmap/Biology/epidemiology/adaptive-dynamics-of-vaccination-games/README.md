# Adaptive dynamics of vaccination games

Adaptive (gradient) dynamics of vaccination probabilities in the individual-payoff vaccination game, in which the perceived infection risk decreases with population uptake. Equilibria exist below the herd-immunity threshold, are unique and stable for convex risk functions, and otherwise alternate in stability and change only through saddle-node bifurcations.
