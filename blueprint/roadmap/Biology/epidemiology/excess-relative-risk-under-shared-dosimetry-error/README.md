# Excess relative risk under shared dosimetry error

Radiation risk estimation when doses come from a Monte Carlo dosimetry system whose errors are shared across person-years. A Poisson excess-relative-risk hazard is fitted to mean doses; Berkson errors with shared and unshared, multiplicative and additive components inflate the score variance, and the shared multiplicative component makes the dose-slope estimator a scale mixture rather than a normal variable.
