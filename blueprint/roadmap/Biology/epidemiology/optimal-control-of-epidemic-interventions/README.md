# Optimal control of epidemic interventions

Time-varying interventions (contact tracing and quarantine, isolation, non-pharmaceutical measures, vaccination) can be chosen to minimize a weighted combination of infection burden and intervention cost over a finite horizon. Pontryagin's maximum principle reduces the problem to a forward state system coupled to a backward adjoint system, with each control given by a clipped ratio of adjoint-weighted marginal benefit to its cost weight. Multi-pathogen models add the question of which threshold governs elimination: for co-infection models with a block-triangular linearization it is the larger of the single-pathogen reproduction numbers.
