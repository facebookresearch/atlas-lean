# Rate constants of the SEIR network

For real `β`, `σ`, `γ`, `μ`, the rate constants of the eight reactions of the
[SEIR reaction network](network.md): `μ` for birth, `β` for infection, `σ` for progression, `γ` for
recovery and `μ` for each of the four deaths, that is `(μ, β, σ, γ, μ, μ, μ, μ)` in the order birth,
infection, progression, recovery, deaths in `S`, `E`, `I`, `R`.
