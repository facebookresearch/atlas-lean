# Mass-action rates of the SEIR network are the scaled intensities

For real `β`, `σ`, `γ`, `μ`, every `u ∈ ℝ^{S, E, I, R}` and every [channel](channel.md) `c`, the
[mass-action rate](../../systems-biology/complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/rate.md)
`κ_c ∏_X u_X^(k_X)` of reaction `c` of the [SEIR reaction network](network.md), with the
[rate constants](rate-const.md) `κ` and the [reactant complex](network-src.md) `k` of `c`, equals
the [scaled intensity](scaled-rate.md) `λ_c(u_S, u_E, u_I, u_R)`.
