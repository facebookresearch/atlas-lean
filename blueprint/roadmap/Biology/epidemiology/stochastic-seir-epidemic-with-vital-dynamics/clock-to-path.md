# At nonnegative times the two forms of the internal clocks agree

Let `β`, `σ`, `γ`, `μ` be nonnegative. For every `N ∈ ℕ`, count paths `S, E, I, R : ℝ → ℕ`,
[channel](channel.md) `c` and `t ≥ 0`, the internal clock `max(∫₀ᵗ λ^N_c(Z(x)) dx, 0)` of reaction
`c` of the [stochastic mass-action system](chain-crn.md) along the [count vector path](to-path.md)
`Z` equals the [internal clock](clock.md) `A_c(t)` of `c`: on `[0, t]` the two integrands agree
([propensities](propensity-chain-crn.md)).
