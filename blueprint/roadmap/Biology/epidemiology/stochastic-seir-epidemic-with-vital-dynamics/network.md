# The SEIR reaction network with vital dynamics

The [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) on the four
[compartments](compartment.md) whose reactions are the eight [channels](channel.md), with the
[reactant complexes](network-src.md) and [product complexes](network-prod.md): birth `∅ → S`,
infection `S + I → E + I`, progression `E → I`, recovery `I → R`, and death `X → ∅` for each
compartment `X`.
