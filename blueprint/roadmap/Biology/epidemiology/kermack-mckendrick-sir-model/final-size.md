# Final-size relation (Kermack–McKendrick 1927)

Let $(S, I, R)$ be a [solution of the SIR system](is-solution.md) with transmission rate $\beta$, removal rate $\gamma$ and population size $N$, with $\gamma \ne 0$. If $S(t) \to S_\infty$ and $R(t) \to R_\infty$ as
$t \to \infty$, then

$$S_\infty = S(0) \exp\left(-\frac{R_0 (R_\infty - R(0))}{N}\right), \qquad R_0 = \frac{\beta}{\gamma},$$

where the quotient by $N$ is read as $0$ when $N = 0$. The [final-size equation](final-size-eq.md) is
the classical form of this relation.
