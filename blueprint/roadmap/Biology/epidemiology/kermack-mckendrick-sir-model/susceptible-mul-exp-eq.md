# Conserved quantity behind the final-size relation

Let $(S, I, R)$ be a [solution of the SIR system](is-solution.md) with transmission rate $\beta$, removal rate $\gamma$ and population size $N$, with $\gamma \ne 0$, and let $R_0 = \beta/\gamma$. Then for every $t \ge 0$,

$$S(t) \, e^{R_0 R(t)/N} = S(0) \, e^{R_0 R(0)/N},$$

where the quotients by $N$ are read as $0$ when $N = 0$.
