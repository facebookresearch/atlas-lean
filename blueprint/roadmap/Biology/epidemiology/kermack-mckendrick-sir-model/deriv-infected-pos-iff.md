# Threshold theorem (Kermack–McKendrick 1927)

Let $(S, I, R)$ be a [solution of the SIR system](is-solution.md) with transmission rate $\beta$, removal rate $\gamma$ and population size $N$, with $\gamma > 0$ and $N > 0$, and suppose $I(0) > 0$. Then the number of
infectives initially increases, $I'(0) > 0$, if and only if $R_0 S(0)/N > 1$, where
$R_0 = \beta/\gamma$.
