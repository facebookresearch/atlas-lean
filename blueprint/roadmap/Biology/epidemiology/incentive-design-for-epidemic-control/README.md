# Incentive design for epidemic control

Principal–agent formulations of epidemic control in which a government (principal) chooses a testing policy and a tax indexed on the epidemic's observed path, and the population (agent) chooses its contact rate in response; the epidemic follows a stochastic SIS/SIR-type diffusion.
