# Resource-limited elimination with importations

Optimal control of an outbreak in a community that keeps receiving infected travellers. Community test-trace-isolate and traveller quarantine are both limited by maximal intensities and by finite cumulative budgets. Minimising cumulative infections over a fixed horizon yields front-loaded bang-bang policies, and which strategy is feasible (elimination, mitigation or resurgence) depends on regional capacity and travel volume.
