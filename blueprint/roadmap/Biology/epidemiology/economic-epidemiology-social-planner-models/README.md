# Economic epidemiology social planner models

Infinite-horizon growth models in which a social planner chooses consumption, health spending and direct epidemic-control investment, coupled to an SIR epidemic whose transmission and recovery rates depend on health capital and accumulated control knowledge, and in which infection reduces effective labour.
