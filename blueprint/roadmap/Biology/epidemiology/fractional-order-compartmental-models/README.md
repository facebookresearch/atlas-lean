# Fractional-order compartmental models

Replacing the time derivative of a compartmental epidemic model by a fractional derivative of order $p \in (0, 1]$ (Caputo, or Atangana–Baleanu–Caputo with a Mittag-Leffler kernel) introduces memory into the dynamics. Equilibria are unchanged, but stability is governed by the Matignon sector condition on Jacobian eigenvalues and by fractional versions of Lyapunov's direct method; existence and uniqueness follow from Picard iteration on the equivalent Volterra integral equation.
