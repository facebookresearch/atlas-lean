# Pathway decomposition in cholera models with environmental transmission

Susceptible–infected–recovered–water (SIWR) cholera models in which infection spreads both directly and through a pathogen reservoir with saturating (Monod) dose response. The control reproduction number splits additively over the two routes, which fixes its elasticities and gives a closed-form synergy between sanitation measures targeting different routes; concavity of the dose response excludes backward bifurcation.
