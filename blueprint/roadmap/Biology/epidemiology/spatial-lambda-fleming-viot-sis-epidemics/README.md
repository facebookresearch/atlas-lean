# Spatial Λ-Fleming–Viot SIS epidemics

The epidemiological spatial $\Lambda$-Fleming–Viot (EpiSLFV) process is an SIS-type model in continuous space $\mathbb R^d$: a local density $\omega_t(z)\in[0,1]$ of healthy individuals is driven by Poisson-distributed infection events over balls together with exponential recovery. Its dual is a spatial branching process with competition that tracks the genealogy of infected individuals, which makes the long-term fate of the epidemic computable in terms of a candidate reproduction number $\mathrm R_0(\gamma,\nu)$.
