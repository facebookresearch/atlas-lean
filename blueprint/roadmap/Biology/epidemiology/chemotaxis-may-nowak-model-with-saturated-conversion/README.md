# Chemotaxis May–Nowak model with saturated conversion

Reaction–diffusion–taxis models of virus infection (uninfected cells, infected cells, free virus) in which uninfected cells are attracted by infected cells and the infection rate is a general, possibly saturating, function of the uninfected density. The growth exponent of the infection rate relative to the space dimension decides whether solutions stay bounded.
