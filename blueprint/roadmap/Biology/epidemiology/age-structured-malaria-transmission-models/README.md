# Age-structured malaria transmission models

Host–vector malaria models structured by the chronological age of humans, with age-dependent transmission, recovery, waning immunity and mortality, analyzed as abstract Cauchy problems with integrated semigroups. The reproduction number is the square root of a double integral over the age at infection and the time since infection, and it is a sharp global threshold for parasite extinction.
