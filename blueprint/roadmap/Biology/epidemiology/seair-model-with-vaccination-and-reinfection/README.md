# SEAIR model with vaccination and reinfection

A COVID-19-type compartmental model with susceptible, vaccinated, latent, asymptomatic, symptomatic and recovered classes, leaky vaccination with waning, and reinfection of recovered individuals, together with its reaction–diffusion extension under no-flux boundary conditions. The control reproduction number is the same in both settings and decides local stability of the disease-free state; reinfection and leaky vaccination allow endemic equilibria below threshold.
