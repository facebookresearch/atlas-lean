# SEIR models with density-dependent diffusion

Reaction–diffusion SEIR-type models of a non-sedentary population in which each compartment diffuses with its own coefficient depending on position, time and total population density, and infection can be transmitted by both exposed and infectious individuals. Weak solutions exist globally and are bounded; uniqueness holds under structural assumptions; and diffusion coefficients, viewed as mobility controls, can be optimized to reduce infection in a target region.
