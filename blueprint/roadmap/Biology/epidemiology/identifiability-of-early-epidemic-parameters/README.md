# Identifiability of early epidemic parameters

During the exponential phase of an outbreak, compartmental models fitted to case counts can only pin down the growth rate and the amplitude of the epidemic curve. Combinations of transmission rate, recovery rate, reporting fraction and initial size that leave these two quantities unchanged fit the early data equally well, yet produce very different forecasts of the peak height and final size.
