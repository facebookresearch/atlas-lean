# Exactness of pair-approximation moment closures

When do pair-based moment closures for stochastic epidemics on networks reproduce triple probabilities exactly? On trees the states that drive infection close exactly for any escape-independent model, and with constant infectious periods all closures on three nodes are exact, while Markovian infectious periods break exactness.
