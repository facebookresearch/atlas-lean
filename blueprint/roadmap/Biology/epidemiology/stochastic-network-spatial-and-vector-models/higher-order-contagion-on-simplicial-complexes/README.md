# Higher-order contagion on simplicial complexes

Contagion models where infection can pass along pairwise links and also through group interactions, represented as simplices (filled triangles) or hyperedges. When transmission inside a group needs several infectious members at once, the transition to endemicity can become discontinuous and bistable.

The homogeneous mean-field phase diagram of the order-two simplicial model, with its bistable region and discontinuous transition, is [Bistability and discontinuous transition in simplicial contagion](../simplicial-contagion-model/bistability-and-discontinuous-transition-in-simplicial-contagion.md), stated for the [simplicial contagion model](../simplicial-contagion-model/simplicial-contagion-model-and-its-mean-field-equation.md).
