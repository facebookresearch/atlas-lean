# Spatial transmission kernels

In outbreaks among fixed units such as farms, the hazard of transmission between two units is modelled as a decreasing function of the distance between them, the transmission kernel. The kernel is estimated by maximum likelihood from the timing of infections, and it converts the local density of units into unit-level reproduction numbers and risk maps.
