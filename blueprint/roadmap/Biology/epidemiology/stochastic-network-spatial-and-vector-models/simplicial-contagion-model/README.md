# Simplicial contagion model

Social contagion often needs reinforcement from a group rather than from a single contact. The simplicial contagion model places an SIS-type process on a simplicial complex and lets a susceptible node be infected both through links and through higher-dimensional simplices whose other members are all infectious. In mean field, the group channel turns the continuous epidemic transition of the SIS model into a discontinuous one with a bistable region and a critical mass of initial adopters.
