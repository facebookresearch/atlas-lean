# Cofactor interactions between sexually transmitted infections

One infection can raise both the susceptibility to and the infectivity of another, as genital herpes does for HIV. Population attributable fractions for the susceptibility channel, the infectivity channel and both together quantify the direct share of incident infections caused by the cofactor, while excess prevalence captures indirect effects through onward transmission.
