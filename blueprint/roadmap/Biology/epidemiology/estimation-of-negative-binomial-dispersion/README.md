# Estimation of the negative binomial dispersion parameter

The negative binomial law with mean $m$ and dispersion $k$ models overdispersed counts such as the numbers of secondary cases caused by infectious individuals. Small $k$ means strong overdispersion (superspreading). This topic covers maximum-likelihood estimation of $k$ and how incomplete or selected transmission data change the estimates.
