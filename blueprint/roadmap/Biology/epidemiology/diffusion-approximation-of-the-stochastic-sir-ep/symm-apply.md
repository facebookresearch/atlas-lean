# Symmetric 2 × 2 matrix stored as a triple, applied to a vector

For a symmetric $2 \times 2$ matrix stored as a triple $c = (c_1, c_2, c_3)$, standing for
$\begin{pmatrix} c_1 & c_2 \\ c_2 & c_3 \end{pmatrix}$, and $v \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the product is
$C v = (c_1 v_1 + c_2 v_2,\ c_2 v_1 + c_3 v_2)$. The [infinitesimal covariance](diffusion-matrix.md)
and the [Lyapunov field](lyapunov-field.md) use the same storage.
