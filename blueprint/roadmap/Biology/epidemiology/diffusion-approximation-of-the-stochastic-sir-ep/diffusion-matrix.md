# Infinitesimal covariance of the SIR chain

For real $\beta$, $\gamma$ and $p = (s, i)$, the infinitesimal covariance of the SIR chain is
$G(p) = \sum_\ell \lambda_\ell(p)\, \ell \ell^{\mathsf T}$, over the jump vectors $\ell_1 = (-1, 1)$
and $\ell_2 = (0, -1)$ with intensities $\lambda_1 = \beta s i$ and $\lambda_2 = \gamma i$, stored
as its entries

$$(G_{11}, G_{12}, G_{22}) = (\beta s i,\ -\beta s i,\ \beta s i + \gamma i).$$

It is positive semidefinite when both intensities are nonnegative.
