# Drift of the SIR chain in densities

For real $\beta$, $\gamma$ and $p = (s, i) \in \mathbb{R}^2$, the drift of the SIR chain in
densities is

$$F(p) = (-\beta s i,\ \beta s i - \gamma i) = (-1, 1)\, \beta s i + (0, -1)\, \gamma i,$$

the sum over the two transitions, infection and removal, of the jump vector times the intensity. The
Kermack–McKendrick system in densities is $x' = F(x)$.
