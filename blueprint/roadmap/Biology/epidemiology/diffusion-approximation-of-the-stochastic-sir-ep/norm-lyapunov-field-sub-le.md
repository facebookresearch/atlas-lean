# Lipschitz bound for the Lyapunov field

Let $\beta, \gamma \ge 0$, $B$ real and $p \in \mathbb{R}^2$ with $\|p\| \le B$ in the sup norm. For
all stored symmetric matrices $c, c'$, with the norm $\max(|c_1|, |c_2|, |c_3|)$, the
[Lyapunov field](lyapunov-field.md) satisfies

$$\|L_p(c) - L_p(c')\| \le 2 (2 \beta B + \gamma)\, \|c - c'\|.$$
