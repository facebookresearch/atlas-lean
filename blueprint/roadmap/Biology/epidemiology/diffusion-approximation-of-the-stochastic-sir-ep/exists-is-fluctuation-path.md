# Existence of the fluctuation path for a continuous driving path

Let $\beta, \gamma \ge 0$, and let $x$ and the driving path $m$ be continuous on $[0, T]$. Then for
every initial value $u_0$ there is a [fluctuation path](is-fluctuation-path.md) driven by $m$ from
$u_0$ around $x$. It is obtained by extending $x$ and $m$ continuously to $\mathbb{R}$, solving the
globally Lipschitz equation $v' = DF(x)\, (v + m - m(0))$ with $v(0) = u_0$, and setting
$U = v + m - m(0)$.
