# Vaccine allocation at high reproduction numbers

Pre-outbreak allocation of a limited stock of a leaky vaccine across the groups of a multigroup SIR model so as to minimize the final epidemic size, and how the optimal allocation changes as the basic reproduction number grows. At high $R_0$ the optimum prioritizes the groups that are least exposed, and in a two-group model the optimum jumps from one group to the other at an explicit threshold.
