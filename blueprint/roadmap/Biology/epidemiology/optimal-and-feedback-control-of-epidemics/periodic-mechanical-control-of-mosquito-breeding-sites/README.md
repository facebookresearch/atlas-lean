# Periodic mechanical control of mosquito breeding sites

Two-stage (aquatic, adult) mosquito model whose larval carrying capacity is cut by a fixed fraction at periodic mechanical-elimination campaigns and regrows in between. The impulsive carrying capacity has an explicit globally attracting periodic orbit, and explicit thresholds on the basic offspring number decide between persistence on a periodic orbit and extinction; the cut fraction depends on household protective behavior, which can erode the control.
