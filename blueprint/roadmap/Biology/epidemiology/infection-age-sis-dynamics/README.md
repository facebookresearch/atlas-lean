# Infection-age SIS dynamics

SIS-type epidemic models in which infectiousness and the chance of recovery depend on the time since infection, formulated as nonlinear renewal equations or age-structured PDEs. The basic reproduction number fixes the stability of the disease-free state, while the shape of the infection-duration distribution controls oscillations and bistability around the endemic state.
