# Hydra effects in discrete-time epidemic models

A hydra effect is an increase in population size caused by something that harms individuals, such as extra mortality. In discrete-time host models with overcompensatory (Ricker) reproduction, an infection that lowers the fecundity or survival of infected hosts can relax density dependence enough to raise the total equilibrium, or average cycle, population.
