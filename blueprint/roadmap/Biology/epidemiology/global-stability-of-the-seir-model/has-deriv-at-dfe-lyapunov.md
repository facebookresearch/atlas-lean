# Derivative of the disease-free Lyapunov function along solutions

Let `(S, E, I, R)` be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the SEIR system with vital dynamics, with
`σ ≠ 0` and `N ≠ 0` and no sign conditions. At every `t ≥ 0` with `S(t) ≠ 0`, the function
`s ↦ V₀(S(s), E(s), I(s))`, for the [disease-free Lyapunov function](dfe-lyapunov.md) `V₀`, has the
two-sided derivative `V̇₀(S(t), I(t))` at `t`, where `V̇₀` is the
[disease-free Lyapunov derivative](dfe-lyapunov-deriv.md).
