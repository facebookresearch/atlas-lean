# Lyapunov stability of the disease-free state relative to the open positive orthant when R₀ ≤ 1

Under `β ≥ 0`, `σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ ≤ 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) `R₀ = β σ / ((μ + σ) (μ + γ))`, for every `ε > 0` there is
`δ > 0` such that every [solution](is-solution.md) `(S, E, I, R)` of the SEIR system with vital
dynamics with `S(0), E(0), I(0) > 0` and `(S(0), E(0), I(0))` within distance `δ` of `(N, 0, 0)`
stays within distance `ε` of it: `(S(t), E(t), I(t))` is within `ε` of `(N, 0, 0)` for every
`t ≥ 0`. Distances on `ℝ³` are the maximum of the coordinate distances. Only solutions starting in
the open positive orthant are covered; initial states on its boundary are not. The equilibrium
`(N, 0, 0)` itself lies on the boundary.
