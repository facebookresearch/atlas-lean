# Convergence to the endemic equilibrium from the open positive orthant when R₀ > 1

Let `(S, E, I, R)` be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the SEIR system with vital dynamics, under
`σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ > 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) `R₀ = β σ / ((μ + σ) (μ + γ))`, with `S(0), E(0), I(0) > 0`. Then
`(S(t), E(t), I(t)) → (S*, E*, I*)` as `t → ∞`, where `S*`, `E*`, `I*` are the
[susceptible](endemic-s.md), [exposed](endemic-e.md) and [infectious](endemic-i.md) endemic levels.
Only solutions starting in the open positive orthant are covered; initial states on its boundary are
not.
