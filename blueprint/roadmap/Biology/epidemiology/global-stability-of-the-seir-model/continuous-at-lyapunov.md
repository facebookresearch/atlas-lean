# The Lyapunov function is continuous at the endemic equilibrium

Under `σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ > 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) `R₀ = β σ / ((μ + σ) (μ + γ))`, the
[Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md) `V`, as a function on `ℝ³`, is continuous at the endemic
equilibrium `(S*, E*, I*)`.
