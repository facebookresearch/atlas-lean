# Exposed level of the endemic equilibrium

For real parameters `β, σ, γ, μ, N`, `E* = μ (N − S*) / (μ + σ)`, where `S*` is the
[susceptible level](endemic-s.md); with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `E* = 0` when `μ + σ = 0`. Under
`σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ > 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md), it is the exposed coordinate of the endemic equilibrium, and it
is positive.
