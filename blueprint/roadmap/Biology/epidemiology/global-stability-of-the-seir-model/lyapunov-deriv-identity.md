# Algebraic identity behind the Lyapunov derivative

Let `β, σ, γ, μ, N` be real with `σ ≠ 0`, `μ + σ ≠ 0` and `N ≠ 0`, and let `a, e, i, S, E, I` be
nonzero reals with `μ N = μ a + β a i / N`, `β a i / N = (μ + σ) e` and
`β a / N = (μ + σ) (μ + γ) / σ` (the equilibrium identities, with `a, e, i` in the role of
`S*, E*, I*`). Then

`(1 − a/S) (μ N − β S I/N − μ S) + (1 − e/E) (β S I/N − (μ + σ) E)`
`+ ((μ + σ)/σ) (1 − i/I) (σ E − (μ + γ) I)`

equals `−μ (S − a)² / S − (β a i / N) (a / S + S I e / (a i E) + E i / (e I) − 3)`: the chain-rule
expression for the derivative of the [Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md) is the
[Lyapunov derivative](lyapunov-deriv.md).
