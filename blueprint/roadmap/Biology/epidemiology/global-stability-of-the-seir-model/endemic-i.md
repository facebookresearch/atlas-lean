# Infectious level of the endemic equilibrium

For real parameters `β, σ, γ, μ, N`, `I* = σ E* / (μ + γ)`, where `E*` is the
[exposed level](endemic-e.md); with the convention `x / 0 = 0`, `I* = 0` when `μ + γ = 0`. Under
`σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ > 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md), it is the infectious coordinate of the endemic equilibrium, and
it is positive.
