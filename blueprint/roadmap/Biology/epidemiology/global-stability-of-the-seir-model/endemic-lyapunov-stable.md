# Lyapunov stability of the endemic equilibrium when R₀ > 1

Under `σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ > 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) `R₀ = β σ / ((μ + σ) (μ + γ))`, for every `ε > 0` there is
`δ > 0` such that every [solution](is-solution.md) `(S, E, I, R)` of the SEIR system with vital
dynamics with `S(0), E(0), I(0) > 0` and `(S(0), E(0), I(0))` within distance `δ` of `(S*, E*, I*)`
stays within distance `ε` of it: `(S(t), E(t), I(t))` is within `ε` of `(S*, E*, I*)` for every
`t ≥ 0`, where `(S*, E*, I*)` is the endemic equilibrium. Distances on `ℝ³` are the maximum of the
coordinate distances. Only solutions starting in the open positive orthant are covered; initial
states on its boundary are not.
