# Stability and attractivity of the disease-free state from the open positive orthant when R₀ ≤ 1

Under `β ≥ 0`, `σ > 0`, `γ ≥ 0`, `μ > 0`, `N > 0` and `R₀ ≤ 1`, where `R₀` is the
[basic reproduction number](r0.md) `R₀ = β σ / ((μ + σ) (μ + γ))`, the disease-free equilibrium
`(N, 0, 0)` is Lyapunov stable relative to the open positive orthant and attracts it. Stability: for
every `ε > 0` there is `δ > 0` such that every [solution](is-solution.md) `(S, E, I, R)` of the SEIR
system with vital dynamics with `S(0), E(0), I(0) > 0` and `(S(0), E(0), I(0))` within distance `δ`
of `(N, 0, 0)` stays within distance `ε` of it: `(S(t), E(t), I(t))` is within `ε` of `(N, 0, 0)`
for every `t ≥ 0`. Attractivity: every [solution](is-solution.md) `(S, E, I, R)` of the SEIR system
with vital dynamics with `S(0), E(0), I(0) > 0` satisfies `(S(t), E(t), I(t)) → (N, 0, 0)` as
`t → ∞`. Distances on `ℝ³` are the maximum of the coordinate distances. Only solutions starting in
the open positive orthant are covered; initial states on its boundary are not. The equilibrium
itself lies on the boundary, so this is not global asymptotic stability on the nonnegative orthant.
