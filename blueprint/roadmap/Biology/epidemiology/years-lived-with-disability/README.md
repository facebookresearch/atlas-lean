# Years lived with disability

Non-fatal disease burden is measured by weighting prevalence with disability weights on a scale from perfect health to death. Two mathematical points govern the construction: severity levels must be split before weighting, and an individual carrying several health states cannot be charged the sum of their weights, which forces a combination rule and a redistribution of the combined disability among causes.
