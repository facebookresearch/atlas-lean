# Integral input-to-state stability of epidemic models

Compartmental epidemic models viewed as networks of positive subsystems with inputs (births, immigration, vaccination). A small-gain theorem for networks with balancing kinetics gives explicit linear Lyapunov functions and shows that models such as SIR are input-to-state stable, even though the infected subsystem alone is not.
