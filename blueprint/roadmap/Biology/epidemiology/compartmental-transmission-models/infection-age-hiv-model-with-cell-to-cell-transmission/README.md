# Infection-age HIV model with cell-to-cell transmission

Within-host HIV dynamics with logistic proliferation of CD4+ T cells, two infection routes (free virus and direct cell-to-cell contact) and an infection-age structured class of infected cells whose infectivity switches on after an eclipse period.
