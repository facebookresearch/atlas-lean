# Spatial SIR with power-saturated incidence and heterogeneous recovery

Spatial spread of an outbreak seeded by a few imported cases: susceptibles are immobile, infected hosts diffuse, transmission uses the incidence $\tau I^p S/(1+\kappa I^q)$, and the recovery rate varies in space. Solutions exist globally, the infection always dies out, and susceptibles are never exhausted.
