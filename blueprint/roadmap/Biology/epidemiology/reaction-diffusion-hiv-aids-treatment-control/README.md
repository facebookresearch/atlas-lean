# Reaction–diffusion HIV/AIDS treatment control

Spatially distributed HIV/AIDS transmission among susceptible, HIV-infected pre-AIDS, chronic (treated) and AIDS classes with diffusion of every class, and a space–time treatment control that returns infected individuals to the susceptible class. Semigroup theory gives a global strong nonnegative solution, and the treatment that balances infection burden against quadratic cost exists and is characterized through an adjoint system.
