# Parasite clearance half-life

After effective antimalarial treatment, parasite density in blood typically declines log-linearly after an initial lag phase. The slope of that decline, expressed as a half-life, is the standard in-vivo phenotype of artemisinin susceptibility: slow clearance is the hallmark of resistance.
