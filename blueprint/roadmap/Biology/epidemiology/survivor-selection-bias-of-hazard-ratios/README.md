# Survivor selection bias of hazard ratios

Why the hazard ratio estimated from a randomized trial generally differs from the causal hazard ratio: conditioning on survival to time $t$ selects individuals with favourable latent frailty or favourable individual effects, differently in each arm, so randomization is lost among survivors. Structural causal models with latent frailty and effect modifiers make the bias explicit.
