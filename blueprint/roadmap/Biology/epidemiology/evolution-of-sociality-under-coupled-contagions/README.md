# Evolution of sociality under coupled contagions

Models in which the social contact rate of individuals is an evolving trait that simultaneously spreads a beneficial contagion (information, innovations, microbiota) and a costly one (infectious disease). Fast SIS dynamics set the equilibrium prevalences, a utility of those prevalences sets fitness, and replicator or adaptive dynamics act on the contact rate, revealing a gap between socially optimal and evolutionarily stable sociality.
