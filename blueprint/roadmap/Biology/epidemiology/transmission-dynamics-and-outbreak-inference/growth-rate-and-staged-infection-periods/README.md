# Growth rate and staged infection periods

The early exponential growth rate of an epidemic is observable; the basic reproduction number is not. Converting one into the other requires the distribution of the latent and infectious periods, and the conversion is sensitive to their shape, not only their means: replacing exponential waiting times by Erlang-distributed ones with the same mean raises the reproduction number implied by a given growth rate.
