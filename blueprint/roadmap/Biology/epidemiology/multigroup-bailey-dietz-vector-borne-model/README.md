# Multigroup Bailey–Dietz vector-borne model

Host and vector populations of a vector-borne infection split into $n$ groups (patches, neighbourhoods or behavioural classes), with host infection driven by vectors of every group and vector infection driven by hosts of every group through general nonnegative contact matrices. The host–vector contact structure is a bipartite directed graph, and its strong connectivity alone gives a sharp global threshold.
