# Behavioral-feedback epidemic models

Epidemic models in which contact and transmission rates respond endogenously to the epidemic state, through voluntary distancing or self-protection, possibly differently across interacting subpopulations. Stability of infection-free states is governed by the spectral abscissa of the state-dependent next-generation operator, and under suitable monotonicity the aggregate epidemic curve remains single-peaked.
