# Fixation of pathogen strains in stochastic epidemics

Probability that a mutant pathogen strain invades and replaces a resident strain in a finite host population described by a multi-strain stochastic SIR model with demography, in the strong-selection regime (distinct basic reproduction numbers, branching-process approximation) and the weak-selection regime (reproduction numbers differing by order $1/n$, diffusion on the coexistence set), and the consequences for virulence evolution.
