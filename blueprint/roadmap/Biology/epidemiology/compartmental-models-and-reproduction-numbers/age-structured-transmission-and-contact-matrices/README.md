# Age-structured transmission and contact matrices

Respiratory pathogens spread along social contacts whose rates differ strongly by age and by setting (household, school, work, transport, leisure). Age-structured compartmental models encode this through a contact matrix inside the force of infection; the basic reproduction number becomes the spectral radius of an age-structured next-generation matrix, and social-distancing interventions become entrywise reductions of setting-specific contact matrices.
