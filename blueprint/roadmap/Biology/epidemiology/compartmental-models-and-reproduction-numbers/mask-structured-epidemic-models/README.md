# Mask-structured epidemic models

Compartmental epidemic models in which every compartment is split into mask wearers and non-wearers, with masks reducing the susceptibility of healthy wearers and the transmissibility of infectious wearers. They are used to compare allocation policies for a limited supply of masks and the effect of universal face coverings on the reproduction number.
