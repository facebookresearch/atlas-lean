# Free-living inoculum transmission models

Respiratory transmission can be split into two steps: infectious individuals shed virus into short-lived airborne droplets and into a longer-lived reservoir on surfaces (fomites), and susceptibles are infected by taking up that inoculum. Writing the inoculum pools as explicit state variables gives SEIR-type models in which facemasks and lock-downs act on separate shedding and uptake rates, with a closed-form basic reproduction number.
