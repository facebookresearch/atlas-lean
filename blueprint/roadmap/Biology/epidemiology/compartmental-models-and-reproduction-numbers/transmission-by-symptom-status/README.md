# Transmission by symptom status

Total transmission splits into contributions from people who never develop symptoms, from symptomatic people before onset, and from symptomatic people after onset. The split is determined by the never-symptomatic fraction and relative infectiousness, and by how the infectiousness profile overlaps the incubation-period distribution; it bounds what symptom-based isolation can achieve.
