# Quarantine, isolation and face-mask models

Kermack–McKendrick-type compartmental models of respiratory epidemics that add traced and
quarantined susceptible and exposed classes, isolation of detected cases, and a community
face-mask term that scales the force of infection, with control reproduction numbers computed by
the next-generation method.
