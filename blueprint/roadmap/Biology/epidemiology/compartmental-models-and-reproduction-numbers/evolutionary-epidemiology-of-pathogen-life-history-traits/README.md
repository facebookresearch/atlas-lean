# Evolutionary epidemiology of pathogen life-history traits

Selection on mutations that change a pathogen's transmission rates, progression rates, asymptomatic fraction or virulence, analysed inside the epidemiological model that sets the pathogen's fitness. Frequency change is written as genetic variation times a selection coefficient that depends on the current epidemiological state.
