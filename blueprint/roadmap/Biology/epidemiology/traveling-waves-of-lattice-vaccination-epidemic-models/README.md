# Traveling waves of lattice vaccination epidemic models

Epidemic models with vaccination posed on a one-dimensional chain of patches (niches) $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, where individuals move only to neighbouring patches through the discrete Laplacian. Invasion of the infection is described by traveling wave solutions of the lattice system that join the disease-free state to the endemic state, with a minimal speed computed from the linearisation at the disease-free equilibrium.
