# Within-host viral dynamics

Ordinary differential equation models of a virus spreading among target cells inside one host, with a within-host basic reproductive number playing the role of $R_0$ in population epidemic models. They explain the setpoint of chronic infection, the decay of viral load under antiviral therapy, and the slow decay of a latent reservoir of infected cells.
