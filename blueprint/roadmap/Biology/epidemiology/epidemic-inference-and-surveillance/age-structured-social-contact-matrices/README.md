# Age-structured social contact matrices

An age-structured contact matrix has entry $(a, \alpha)$ equal to the mean number of contacts per day that an individual in age group $a$ has with individuals in age group $\alpha$. It is usually split by setting (home, work, school, community), estimated from contact diaries or synthesized from demographic data, and used as the mixing kernel of age-structured transmission models.
