# Joint estimation of complexity of infection and allele frequencies

Bayesian estimation, from SNP genotypes of mixed parasite infections, of each host's complexity of
infection (number of distinct parasite lineages) together with the population allele frequency at
each locus, using either heterozygous/homozygous calls or quantitative allele-signal proportions.
