# Phenomenological epidemic growth models

Phenomenological growth models describe the cumulative case curve $C(t)$ of an outbreak directly, without compartments, and are used to characterize and forecast early growth. A "deceleration of growth" exponent $p \in [0,1]$ interpolates between constant incidence, polynomial (sub-exponential) growth and exponential growth, and a saturation term turns the early-growth law into a logistic-type curve with a final size.
