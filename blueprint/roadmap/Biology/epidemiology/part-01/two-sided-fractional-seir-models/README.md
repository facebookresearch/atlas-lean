# Two-sided fractional SEIR models

Rewriting a compartmental epidemic model as Volterra integral equations and inserting power-law memory kernels with compartment-specific exponents gives incommensurate (two-sided) Caputo fractional systems. Their local stability is decided by a characteristic equation in a rescaled variable, and the disease-free equilibrium keeps the usual threshold at $R_0 = 1$.
