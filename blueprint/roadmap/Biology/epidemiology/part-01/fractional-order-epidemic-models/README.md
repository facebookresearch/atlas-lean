# Fractional-order epidemic models

Fractional-order compartmental models replace the time derivative of each compartment by a Caputo derivative of order $\vartheta \in (0, 1]$, giving the dynamics a power-law memory, or by an Atangana–Baleanu derivative with a Mittag-Leffler kernel. Equilibria are unchanged; stability of an equilibrium is decided by the arguments rather than the real parts of Jacobian eigenvalues (Matignon's criterion), and global stability is proved with fractional versions of Lyapunov's direct method, using inequalities that bound the Caputo derivative of a convex function of the state. Existence and uniqueness follow from Picard iteration on the equivalent Volterra integral equation.
