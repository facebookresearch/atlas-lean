# Social distancing games with heterogeneous players

Dynamic games in which a continuum of individuals, differing in vulnerability, prior infection probability and value placed on social contact, each choose a time-varying level of socialization while an SIR epidemic runs. With piecewise-constant decisions, individual best responses are bang-bang threshold rules ordered by a single scalar type, and Nash equilibria exist, solve a nonlinear complementarity problem, and use at most one more distinct action than there are decision periods.
