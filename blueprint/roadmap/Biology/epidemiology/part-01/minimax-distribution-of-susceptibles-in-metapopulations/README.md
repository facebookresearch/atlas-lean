# Minimax distribution of susceptibles in metapopulations

Early-epidemic control in a metapopulation framed as a zero-sum game. The susceptible population chooses how to distribute itself over the groups (through its migration pattern), and infected individuals migrate to make spread as large as possible. On the multitype branching-process approximation of the early epidemic, explicit saddle-point distributions minimise the worst-case growth rate, reproduction number, extinction probability deficit and expected total size.
