# Age-structured dengue model with time-varying parameters

A host age-structured dengue model (susceptible, vaccinated, asymptomatic, symptomatic, hospitalised and recovered hosts; susceptible and infected mosquitoes) in which every rate may depend on both calendar time and host age, and in which vaccination is imperfect. A Lotka characteristic equation obtained by linearising at the disease-free state gives the basic effective reproduction number. Its time-dependent analogue gives the time-varying effective reproduction number, which is linked to the instantaneous growth rate of mosquito infection.
