# Two-strain periodic reaction–diffusion malaria model

Competition between drug-sensitive and drug-resistant malaria strains in a spatially heterogeneous, seasonally forced environment where mosquitoes preferentially bite infectious humans (vector bias). The outcome is decided by the two basic reproduction numbers and two invasion reproduction numbers, defined as spectral radii of periodic next-generation operators.
