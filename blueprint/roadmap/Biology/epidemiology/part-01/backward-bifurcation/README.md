# Backward bifurcation

In many compartmental models (imperfect vaccination, reinfection, treatment returning individuals to susceptibility, environmental reservoirs, co-infection) endemic equilibria exist for reproduction numbers slightly below one. The direction of the bifurcation of endemic equilibria at a reproduction number of one is decided by centre-manifold coefficients, and the number of endemic equilibria is often read off a quadratic in the equilibrium force of infection.
