# Endemic stability via second additive compounds

Local stability of an endemic equilibrium requires all eigenvalues of a Jacobian to have negative real part. For three-dimensional (sub)systems this reduces to sign conditions on the trace, the determinant and the determinant of the second additive compound matrix, which avoids computing the characteristic polynomial's Routh–Hurwitz conditions explicitly and also underlies the geometric approach to global stability.
