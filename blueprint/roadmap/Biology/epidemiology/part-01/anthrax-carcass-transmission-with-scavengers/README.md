# Anthrax carcass transmission with scavengers

In this model zebras grow logistically and die naturally or from anthrax contracted at infected carcasses, carcasses decompose or are eaten by obligate scavengers, and scavengers reproduce on carcasses of either kind. Two anthrax thresholds, without and with scavengers, and two scavenger demographic reproduction numbers, without and with anthrax, determine which of four equilibria is stable.
