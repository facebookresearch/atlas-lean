# Fast periodic switching interventions

Alternating lockdown and non-lockdown days with a fixed short period modulates transmission rates periodically. For control-affine compartmental models, the epidemic under fast switching tracks the epidemic of an averaged model whose transmission rates are the time averages, with an error that shrinks linearly in the switching period.
