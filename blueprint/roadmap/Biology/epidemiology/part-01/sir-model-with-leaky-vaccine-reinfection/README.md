# SIR model with leaky-vaccine reinfection

In the weak-immune SIRVI model, vaccinated individuals can still be infected at a reduced contact rate, so the vaccinated compartment feeds back into the infected one. Every equilibrium is disease free, forming a segment of vaccinated/removed mixtures, and whether infection can grow near such a state depends on the vaccinated fraction relative to the ratio of recovery rate to the vaccine-breakthrough rate.
