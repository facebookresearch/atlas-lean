# HIV/AIDS model with unaware infectives

A four-class HIV/AIDS transmission model separating HIV-positive individuals who are unaware of their infection from those who are aware, with progression to AIDS. Its threshold is a sum of four reproduction contributions, and condom use, screening of unaware infectives and treatment enter as time-dependent controls.
