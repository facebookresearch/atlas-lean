# Higher-order contagion on simplicial complexes

Contagion models where infection can pass along pairwise links and also through group interactions, represented as simplices (filled triangles) or hyperedges. When transmission inside a group needs several infectious members at once, the transition to endemicity can become discontinuous and bistable.
