# Sellke construction for degree-heterogeneous epidemics

Individual-level non-Markovian SIR epidemics in a finite population whose members carry integer contact levels (degrees) and mix proportionately. A generalized Sellke construction with degree-dependent resistances gives the exact final size as an order-statistic stopping rule, and the Markovian version has the annealed-network ODEs as its large-population limit.
