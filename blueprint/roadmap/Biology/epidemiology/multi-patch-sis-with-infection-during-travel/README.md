# Multi-patch SIS models with infection during travel

Regions with their own SIS dynamics are linked by one-way journeys of fixed duration, during which travellers keep transmitting. The travel stage turns into delayed coupling terms, and the next-generation matrix gains off-diagonal entries amplified by $e^{(\beta^T - \delta^T)\tau}$. Global dynamics are governed by the block structure of the transportation network, and the basic reproduction number can respond non-monotonically to travel restrictions.
