# A zero-width window has zero reporting intensity

For all real numbers $\rho$, $\beta$, $c$, $N$ and $a$ and all functions
$S, I : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, the interval intensity of the window from $a$ to $a$ is zero:

$$\rho \int_a^a \frac{\beta\, S(t)\, I(t)}{N}\, dt + c\,(a - a) = 0.$$

No sign, continuity or integrability condition is needed.
