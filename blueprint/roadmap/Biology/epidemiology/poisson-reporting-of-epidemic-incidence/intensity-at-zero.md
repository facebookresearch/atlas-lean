# At zero reporting the intensity vector is the background vector

For every number $n$ of intervals, every background vector $c \in [0, \infty)^n$ and every
incidence vector $x \in [0, \infty)^n$, the [interval intensity vector](intensity-at.md) at
reporting fraction $\rho = 0$ is the background vector: $\lambda_j = c_j + 0 \cdot x_j = c_j$ for
every interval $j$. It does not depend on the incidence.
