# Interval intensity vector at a reporting fraction ρ

For a number $n$ of intervals, a reporting fraction $\rho$ with $0 \le \rho \le 1$, a background
vector $c \in [0, \infty)^n$ and an incidence vector $x \in [0, \infty)^n$ (the integrated epidemic
incidence of each interval), the interval intensity vector is $\lambda \in [0, \infty)^n$ with

$$\lambda_j = c_j + \rho\, x_j, \qquad j = 0, \dots, n - 1:$$

the background of interval $j$ plus the reported fraction of its incidence.
