# The reporting law determines its intensities

For every number $n$ of intervals, the [independent Poisson reporting law](reporting-law.md) is
injective in the intensity vector: if $\lambda, \lambda' \in [0, \infty)^n$ give the same law of
the count vector,

$$\bigotimes_{j} \operatorname{Po}(\lambda_j) = \bigotimes_{j} \operatorname{Po}(\lambda'_j),$$

then $\lambda = \lambda'$.
