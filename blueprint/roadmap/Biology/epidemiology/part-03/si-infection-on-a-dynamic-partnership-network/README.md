# SI infection on a dynamic partnership network

A deterministic model of a sexually transmitted infection spreading on a network in which individuals have up to $n$ simultaneous partners, partnerships form and dissolve, and individuals enter and leave the population. Individuals are viewed as collections of $n$ binding sites; under the mean field at distance one closure, the basic reproduction number reduces to the dominant eigenvalue of a small matrix acting on binding-site states.
