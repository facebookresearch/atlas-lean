# Awareness-dependent SIS dynamics on networks

Behavioural responses to an epidemic can be modelled as a reduction of each susceptible node's *admission rate* (its susceptibility per infected neighbour) that depends on its own degree (contact awareness), on the fraction of its neighbours that are infected (local awareness) and on the overall prevalence (global awareness). In the heterogeneous mean-field SIS model on uncorrelated networks, contact and local awareness raise the epidemic threshold, while global awareness lowers prevalence but leaves the threshold unchanged.
