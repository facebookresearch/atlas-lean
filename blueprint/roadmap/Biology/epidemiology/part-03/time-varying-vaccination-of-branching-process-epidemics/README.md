# Time-varying vaccination of branching-process epidemics

Early epidemic spread approximated by a Crump–Mode–Jagers branching process, with a vaccination programme acting as time-dependent random pruning of the infection tree. A monotone coupling gives stochastic ordering and continuity of extinction time, outbreak size and their quantiles in the vaccination function, the basis for choosing optimal vaccination schedules.
