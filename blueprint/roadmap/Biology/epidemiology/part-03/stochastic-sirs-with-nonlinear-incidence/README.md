# Stochastic SIRS with nonlinear incidence

Environmental fluctuations in the transmission rate, modelled as white noise added to $\beta$, turn an SIRS model with general incidence $\beta S G(I)$ into an Itô stochastic differential equation. The noise lowers the effective threshold: extinction and persistence in the mean are governed by a stochastic reproduction number $R_0^S$ that subtracts a noise penalty from the deterministic $R_0$, and sufficiently strong noise eradicates the infection regardless of $R_0$.
