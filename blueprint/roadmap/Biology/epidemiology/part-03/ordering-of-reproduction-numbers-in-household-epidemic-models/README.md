# Ordering of reproduction numbers in household epidemic models

Epidemic models with households (and workplaces) admit many competing reproduction numbers: household-level, individual-level, vaccination-based and growth-rate-based. All share the threshold value $1$, but away from it they satisfy strict universal orderings, with direct consequences for critical vaccination coverage.
