# Periodic solutions of the seasonally forced SIR model

The SIR endemic model with a periodic contact rate is the standard model of seasonally recurring childhood diseases. Whatever the complexity of its observed dynamics (subharmonic or chaotic), it always has a positive solution with the period of the forcing once the time-averaged reproduction number exceeds one.
