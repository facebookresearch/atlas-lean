# Stochastic SEIRS with white-noise transmission

SEIRS dynamics with temporary immunity in which the transmission rate fluctuates around a deterministic baseline because of multiplicative Gaussian white noise, interpreted in the Stratonovich sense. Converting to the Itô form exposes moment conditions from which the time-varying baseline transmission rate and the noise intensity can be estimated locally from incidence data.
