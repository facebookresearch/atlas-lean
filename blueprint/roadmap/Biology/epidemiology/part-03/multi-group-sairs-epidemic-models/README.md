# Multi-group SAIRS epidemic models

Epidemic models on a network of communities that separate asymptomatic from symptomatic infectious individuals and include vaccination and waning immunity. A spectral-radius $\mathcal R_0$ governs elimination and the existence of a unique endemic equilibrium, whose global stability is proved with graph-theoretic Lyapunov functions.
