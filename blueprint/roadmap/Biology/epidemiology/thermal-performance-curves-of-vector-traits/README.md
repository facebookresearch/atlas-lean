# Thermal performance curves of vector traits

Mosquito and parasite traits that drive vector-borne transmission respond unimodally to temperature. Asymmetric rate traits are fitted with Brière curves and symmetric probability traits with concave quadratics, and the curves are composed into a temperature-dependent reproduction number whose thermal limits are set by the most restrictive trait.
