# SIR epidemics on random intersection graphs

Stochastic SIR epidemics on random graphs of overlapping cliques (households, workplaces, groups) built from a bipartite individual–group graph with mixed-Poisson memberships and group sizes. The early epidemic is approximated by a forward branching process typed by infectious period and the final size by a single-type backward (susceptibility-set) branching process, giving the threshold parameter, the probability of a major outbreak and the final fraction infected.
