# Transmission-rate estimation in reaction–diffusion SIRS models

Fitting a time-varying infection rate of a spatially diffusing SIRS model (with temporary immunity, as used for dengue) to observed infected densities by Tikhonov-regularized PDE-constrained least squares: well-posedness and positivity of the forward model, threshold stability, and existence of an optimal rate.
