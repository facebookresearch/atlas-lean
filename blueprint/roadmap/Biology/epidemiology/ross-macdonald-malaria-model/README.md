# Ross–Macdonald malaria model

The classic Ross–Macdonald theory describes *Plasmodium* transmission through mosquito parameters measured in the field: the daily mortality $g$, the human feeding rate $a$, the extrinsic incubation period $n$, the mosquito density per human $m$ and the transmission efficiencies $b$ (mosquito to human) and $c$ (human to mosquito). Under homogeneous random biting and constant adult mortality these give closed formulas for the sporozoite rate, the entomological inoculation rate, the vectorial capacity and $R_0$, and show why adult mosquito mortality is the most sensitive control target.
