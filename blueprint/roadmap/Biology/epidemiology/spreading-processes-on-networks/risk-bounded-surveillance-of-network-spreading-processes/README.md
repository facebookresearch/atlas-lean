# Risk-bounded surveillance of network spreading processes

Surveillance scheduling for epidemics on networks: the expected discounted cost of a network SIR process is upper-bounded by a linear positive system whose cost-to-go is a vector of node impacts, and revisit intervals inversely proportional to impact times outbreak rate keep the risk of an undetected outbreak below a target.
