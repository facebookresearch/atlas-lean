# Density-dependent two-strain coinfection

Two pathogen strains and their coinfected class circulate in a host population with logistic (density-dependent) growth. With the carrying capacity $K$ as bifurcation parameter, the unique locally stable equilibrium moves through a fixed succession of disease-free, single-strain and coinfection states, with thresholds expressed through the strain-specific critical susceptible densities $\sigma_i = \mu_i/\alpha_i$ and normalised coinfection rates.
