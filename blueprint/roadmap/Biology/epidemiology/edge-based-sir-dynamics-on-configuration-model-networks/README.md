# Edge-based SIR dynamics on configuration-model networks

On a large random network with a prescribed degree distribution, SIR dynamics close in a few edge-level quantities through the probability generating function of the degree distribution. The resulting low-dimensional system gives the epidemic curve, the threshold in terms of the mean excess degree, and the final size.
