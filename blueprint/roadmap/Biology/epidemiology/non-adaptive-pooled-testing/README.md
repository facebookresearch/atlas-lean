# Non-adaptive pooled testing

Screening a large population for an infection with a single round of pooled PCR tests, each sample placed in several pools and declared positive when all its pools test positive. Combinatorial designs in which two samples share at most one pool make the false-positive rate explicit, and lines of a finite affine plane give such designs.
