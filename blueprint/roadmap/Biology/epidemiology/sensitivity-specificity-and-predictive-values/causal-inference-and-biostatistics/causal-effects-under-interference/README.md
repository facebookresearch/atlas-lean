# Causal effects under interference

Estimands and design-based estimators for randomized experiments in which one unit's treatment can affect another unit's outcome (spillover, herd immunity): average direct and indirect effects and their link to policy effects (Hu, Li and Wager 2022), and exposure effects that stay well defined when the exposure mapping is misspecified (Sävje 2023).
