# At full prevalence, sensitivity and specificity the PPV is one

At $(\pi, \mathrm{se}, \mathrm{sp}) = (1, 1, 1)$ the [positive predictive value](ppv.md) is $1$,
while the probability of a negative test, $(1 - \pi)\, \mathrm{sp} + \pi (1 - \mathrm{se})$, is $0$:
a zero denominator of the negative predictive value does not affect the PPV, whose denominator is
$1$ here.
