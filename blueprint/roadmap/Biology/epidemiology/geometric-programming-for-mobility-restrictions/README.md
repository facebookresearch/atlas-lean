# Geometric programming for mobility restrictions

Non-pharmaceutical interventions act multiplicatively on transmission through human mobility. In a discrete-time SEIR-type model whose infection rate is a posynomial of mobility levels, hospitalizations and deaths are posynomials of the controls, so optimal mobility restrictions under economic budgets and hospital-capacity limits are geometric programs, convex after a logarithmic change of variables.
