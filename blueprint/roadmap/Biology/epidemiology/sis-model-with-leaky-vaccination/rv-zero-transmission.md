# Without transmission the vaccinated reproduction number is zero

For all real $\gamma$, $\varepsilon$ and $p$, the [vaccinated reproduction number](rv.md) at
transmission rate $0$ is $R_v(0, \gamma, \varepsilon, p) = 0$. So without transmission $R_v < 1$ at
every coverage, while the threshold equivalences with the [critical coverage](critical-coverage.md)
need $\beta > 0$.
