# Derivative of the Lyapunov function along the infected field

For real $\beta$, $\gamma$, $\varepsilon$, $N$, $a$, $c$, $x$ and $y$, put

$$
\dot V(x, y) = -\frac{\beta (x + y)(x - a)^2}{N x} -
\frac{(1 - \varepsilon) \beta (x + y)(y - c)^2}{N y} -
\frac{\gamma (x c - y a)^2}{(a + c) x y},
$$

with the convention $z / 0 = 0$. At an [endemic equilibrium](is-endemic-eq.md) $(a, c)$, for
$N \ne 0$ and $x, y \ne 0$, it is the [derivative](lyapunov-deriv-eq.md) of the
[Lyapunov function](lyapunov.md) along the infected vector field; the last term is the two-group
Cauchy–Schwarz defect.
