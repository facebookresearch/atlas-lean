# Forward invariance of the nonnegative orthant

Let $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma \ge 0$, $\varepsilon \le 1$ and $N > 0$, and let $(S_u, I_u, S_v, I_v)$
be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the SIS model with leaky vaccination, with transmission rate
$\beta$, recovery rate $\gamma$, vaccine efficacy $\varepsilon$ and population size $N$, with
$S_u(0), I_u(0), S_v(0), I_v(0) \ge 0$. Then $S_u(t), I_u(t), S_v(t), I_v(t) \ge 0$ for every
$t \ge 0$.
