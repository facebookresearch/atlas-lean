# Below the critical coverage, solutions tend to an endemic equilibrium

Let $\beta, \gamma, N > 0$, $0 < \varepsilon < 1$ and $0 < p < 1$, with $p < p_c$ for the
[critical coverage](critical-coverage.md) $p_c = (1 - \gamma / \beta) / \varepsilon$. Then there is
an [endemic equilibrium](is-endemic-eq.md) at coverage $p$ such that every
[solution](is-solution.md) $(S_u, I_u, S_v, I_v)$ of the SIS model with leaky vaccination, with
transmission rate $\beta$, recovery rate $\gamma$, vaccine efficacy $\varepsilon$ and population
size $N$, with group sizes $S_u(0) + I_u(0) = (1 - p) N$ and $S_v(0) + I_v(0) = p N$,
$S_u(0), S_v(0) \ge 0$ and $I_u(0), I_v(0) > 0$, satisfies $S_u(t) \to (1 - p) N - a$,
$I_u(t) \to a$, $S_v(t) \to p N - c$ and $I_v(t) \to c$ as $t \to \infty$.
