# Infectives present in both groups at time zero stay positive

Let $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$, $\varepsilon < 1$ and $N > 0$, and let $(S_u, I_u, S_v, I_v)$ be a
[solution](is-solution.md) of the SIS model with leaky vaccination, with transmission rate $\beta$,
recovery rate $\gamma$, vaccine efficacy $\varepsilon$ and population size $N$, with
$S_u(0), S_v(0) \ge 0$ and $I_u(0), I_v(0) > 0$. Then $I_u(t) > 0$ and $I_v(t) > 0$ for every
$t \ge 0$; the proof bounds them below by $I_u(0) e^{-\gamma t}$ and $I_v(0) e^{-\gamma t}$.
