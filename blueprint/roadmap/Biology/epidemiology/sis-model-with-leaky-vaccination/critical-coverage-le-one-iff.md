# For ε > 0, the critical coverage is at most one iff ε ≥ 1 − γ/β

For $\varepsilon > 0$ and all real $\beta$ and $\gamma$, the
[critical coverage](critical-coverage.md) $p_c = (1 - \gamma / \beta) / \varepsilon$ satisfies

$$p_c \le 1 \iff 1 - \frac{\gamma}{\beta} \le \varepsilon,$$

with the convention $x / 0 = 0$ ($\gamma / \beta = 0$ at $\beta = 0$). For $\beta, \gamma > 0$,
where $p_c$ and $R_0 = \beta / \gamma$ have their epidemiological meaning, herd immunity is
attainable by vaccination, $p_c \le 1$, exactly when the efficacy is at least $1 - 1 / R_0$.
