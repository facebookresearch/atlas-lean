# Nonnegativity propagates along a differential inequality

Let $T$ be real and $u, u' : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, with $u$ differentiable at every
$t \in [0, T]$ with derivative $u'(t)$. If $u(0) \ge 0$ and $u'(t) \ge 0$ at every $t \in [0, T]$
where $u(t) < 0$, then $u(t) \ge 0$ for every $t \in [0, T]$: after the last zero before a negative
value, $u$ would be negative and nondecreasing.
