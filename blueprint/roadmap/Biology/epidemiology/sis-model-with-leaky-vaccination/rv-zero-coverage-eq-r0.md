# At zero coverage the vaccinated reproduction number is the SIS R₀

For $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$ and every real $\varepsilon$, the
[vaccinated reproduction number](rv.md) at coverage $0$ is the
[next-generation reproduction number](../next-generation-matrix-and-the-basic-reproductio/r0.md) of
the SIS model with the $1 \times 1$ matrices $F = (\beta)$ and $V = (\gamma)$, the spectral radius
of $F V^{-1}$:

$$R_v(\beta, \gamma, \varepsilon, 0) = R_0(F, V) = \frac{\beta}{\gamma}.$$

The last equality is the [SIS reproduction number](../sis-epidemic-model/r0-eq.md).
