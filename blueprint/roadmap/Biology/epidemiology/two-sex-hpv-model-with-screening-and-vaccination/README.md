# Two-sex HPV model with screening and vaccination

Heterosexual transmission model for human papillomavirus with symptomatic and unaware infected females, screening that makes unaware infections aware, pre-exposure vaccination of adolescents and vaccination of sexually active adults, and waning, imperfect vaccine protection. The effective reproduction number, a geometric mean of the two cross-sex transmission numbers, governs elimination and underlies cost-effectiveness comparisons of interventions.
