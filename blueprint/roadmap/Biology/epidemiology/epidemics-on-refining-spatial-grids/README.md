# Epidemics on refining spatial grids

Individual-based epidemic models on a grid of patches with mesh $\varepsilon$, $N$ individuals per patch and random-walk migration at rate $\propto\varepsilon^{-2}$ converge, as $N\to\infty$ and $\varepsilon\to 0$, to reaction-diffusion systems, with infection-age dependent infectivity giving PDE/integral systems. Fluctuations around the limit are Gaussian and solve linear SPDEs driven by space-time white noise.
