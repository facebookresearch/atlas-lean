# The effect concentration solves the lag equation

For every lag rate `ke0`, initial effect `Ce0` and time `t`, if the plasma concentration `Cp` is
integrable on every bounded interval and continuous at `t`, then the
[effect-compartment concentration](effect-conc.md) `Ce` is differentiable at `t` with
`Ce'(t) = ke0 * (Cp(t) - Ce(t))`.
