# Zero-order lower bound

Let `Vmax ≥ 0`, `Km > 0` and `R ≥ 0`, and let `C` solve the
[saturable-elimination equation](is-solution.md) `C'(t) = R - Vmax * C(t) / (Km + C(t))` at every
time `t ≥ 0`, with `C(0) ≥ 0`. Since elimination is at most `Vmax`, every `t ≥ 0` satisfies
`C(0) + (R - Vmax) t ≤ C(t)`.
