# Doubling the dose more than doubles the bolus AUC

For `Vmax > 0`, every real `Km` and an initial concentration `C₀ > 0`, the [bolus AUC](bolus-auc.md)
`AUC(C₀) = C₀ * (Km + C₀ / 2) / Vmax` satisfies `2 * AUC(C₀) < AUC(2 * C₀)`. For `Km > 0`, where
`AUC(C₀)` is the area under the concentration curve after the bolus, doubling a positive dose, hence
the initial concentration, more than doubles the AUC.
