# Unbounded accumulation

Let `Km > 0` and `0 ≤ Vmax < R`, and let `C` solve the
[saturable-elimination equation](is-solution.md) `C'(t) = R - Vmax * C(t) / (Km + C(t))` at every
time `t ≥ 0`, with `C(0) ≥ 0`. Then `C(t) → ∞` as `t → ∞`: infusion faster than the elimination
capacity accumulates without bound.
