# Shared random intercept cancels within subject

For real numbers `m1` and `m2` (fixed parts), `b` (the subject's random intercept) and `e1` and `e2`
(visit errors), the two visit log-concentrations `m1 + b + e1` and `m2 + b + e2` of one subject have
the difference `(m1 + b + e1) - (m2 + b + e2) = (m1 + e1) - (m2 + e2)`, which does not depend on
`b`.
