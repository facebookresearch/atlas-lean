# Zero latent variance collapses the subject law

For real means `muV` and `muCL`, let the latent log-volume and log-clearance have the Gaussian laws
`N(muV, 0)` and `N(muCL, 0)` of variance zero, which are the point masses at their means. The image
of their product law under `(x, y) ↦ (exp x, exp y)` is the point mass at `(exp muV, exp muCL)`: at
zero latent variance the population law of the subject parameters `(V, CL)` is the fixed-parameter
law at `V = exp muV` and `CL = exp muCL`.
