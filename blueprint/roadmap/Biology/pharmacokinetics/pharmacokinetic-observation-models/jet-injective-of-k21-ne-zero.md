# A nonzero return rate makes the two output jets identify the state

For real rates `k10`, `k12`, `k21` and a central volume `V1` with `k21 ≠ 0` and `V1 ≠ 0`, the jet
map `x ↦ O x` is injective, where `O = [[1 / V1, 0], [-(k10 + k12) / V1, k21 / V1]]` has the rows
`H` and `H M` for the central output `H = (1 / V1, 0)` and the
[two-compartment generator](../two-compartment-pharmacokinetics/pk2-generator.md)
`M = [[-(k10 + k12), k21], [k12, -k21]]`: the central concentration and its first derivative at one
time determine the amounts in both compartments. Its [determinant](observability-det.md) is
`k21 / V1 ^ 2 ≠ 0`.
