# At distinct rates the canonical central amount is the closed form

For distinct rates `ka ≠ k`, all real `F` and `D` and every real `t`, the
[canonical central amount](central-amount-canonical.md)
`Ac(t) = ∫₀ᵗ exp (-k * (t - s)) * (F * ka * D * exp (-ka * s)) ds` equals the closed form
`F * D * ka * (exp (-k * t) - exp (-ka * t)) / (ka - k)` of the
[distinct-rate central amount](central-amount.md).
