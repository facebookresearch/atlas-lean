# With full bioavailability only central elimination removes drug

For distinct rates `ka ≠ k` and all real `D` and `t`, at full bioavailability `F = 1` the total
amount `Ag + Ac`, with the [depot amount](depot-amount.md) `Ag(t) = D * exp (-ka * t)` and the
[distinct-rate central amount](central-amount.md)
`Ac(t) = F * D * ka * (exp (-k * t) - exp (-ka * t)) / (ka - k)`, has at `t` the derivative
`-k * Ac(t)`: the absorption-loss term vanishes.
