# Distinct-rate central equation

For distinct rates `ka ≠ k` and all real `F`, `D` and `t`, the
[distinct-rate central amount](central-amount.md)
`Ac(t) = F * D * ka * (exp (-k * t) - exp (-ka * t)) / (ka - k)` has at `t` the derivative
`F * ka * Ag(t) - k * Ac(t)`, where `Ag(t) = D * exp (-ka * t)` is the
[depot amount](depot-amount.md).
