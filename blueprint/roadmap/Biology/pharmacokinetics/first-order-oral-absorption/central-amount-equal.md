# Central amount at equal absorption and elimination rates

For a bioavailability `F`, a dose `D`, a common absorption and elimination rate `ka` and a time `t`
(real numbers), the central amount at equal rates is `F * D * ka * t * exp (-ka * t)`. The formula
is defined for all real inputs; the intended domain is `0 ≤ F ≤ 1`, `D ≥ 0`, `ka > 0` and `t ≥ 0`.
