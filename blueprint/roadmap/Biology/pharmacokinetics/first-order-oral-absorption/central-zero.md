# The distinct-rate central amount starts at zero

For all real `F`, `D`, `ka` and `k`, the [distinct-rate central amount](central-amount.md)
`Ac(t) = F * D * ka * (exp (-k * t) - exp (-ka * t)) / (ka - k)` at time `0` is `0`; the numerator
vanishes, so this also holds at `ka = k`, where the formula is `0` by the convention `x / 0 = 0`.
