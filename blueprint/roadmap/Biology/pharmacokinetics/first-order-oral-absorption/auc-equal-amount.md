# Absorption AUC, equal rates

For an absorption rate `ka > 0` equal to the elimination rate, and all real `F` and `D`, the
[equal-rate central amount](central-amount-equal.md) `Ac(t) = F * D * ka * t * exp (-ka * t)` is
integrable on `[0, ∞)` and `∫₀^∞ Ac(t) dt = F * D / ka`, the distinct-rate value `F * D / k` at
`k = ka`.
