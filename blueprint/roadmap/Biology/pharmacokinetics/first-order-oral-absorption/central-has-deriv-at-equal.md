# Equal-rate central derivative

For all real `F`, `D`, `ka` and `t`, the [equal-rate central amount](central-amount-equal.md)
`Ac(t) = F * D * ka * t * exp (-ka * t)` is differentiable at `t` with derivative
`F * ka * Ag(t) - ka * Ac(t)`, where `Ag(t) = D * exp (-ka * t)` is the
[depot amount](depot-amount.md).
