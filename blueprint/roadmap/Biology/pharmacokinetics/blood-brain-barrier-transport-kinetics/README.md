# Blood–brain barrier transport kinetics

Drug exchange between plasma and brain is governed by the permeability–surface area (PS) products of influx and efflux across the blood–brain barrier, by binding to plasma proteins during the brief capillary transit, and, for nutrients and some drugs, by saturable carriers. Capillary physiology turns these quantities into measurable extraction fractions and steady-state partition coefficients.
