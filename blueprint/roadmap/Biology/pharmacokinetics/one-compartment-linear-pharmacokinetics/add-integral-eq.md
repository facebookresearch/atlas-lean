# Mass balance

For all real `k` and `R`, let `A` be differentiable at every time `t ≥ 0` with
`A'(t) = R - k * A(t)`, a solution of the one-compartment equation with elimination rate `k` and
constant input `R`. Then for every `t ≥ 0`, `A(t) + ∫₀ᵗ k * A(s) ds = A(0) + R * t`: the amount
present plus the amount eliminated equals the initial amount plus the amount infused.
