# Two exact bolus observations determine volume and clearance

Let `D > 0` be a known dose and `t > 0` a sampling time, and let `(V₁, CL₁)` and `(V₂, CL₂)` be
parameter pairs with volumes `V₁, V₂ > 0` and clearances `CL₁, CL₂ ≥ 0`. If they give the same exact
bolus concentrations at `0` and at `t`, `D / V₁ = D / V₂` and
`(D / V₁) * exp (-(CL₁ / V₁) * t) = (D / V₂) * exp (-(CL₂ / V₂) * t)`, then `V₁ = V₂` and
`CL₁ = CL₂`.
