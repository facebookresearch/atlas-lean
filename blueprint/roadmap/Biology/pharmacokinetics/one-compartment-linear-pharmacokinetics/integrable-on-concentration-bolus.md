# For CL > 0 the bolus concentration is integrable on the half-line

For a clearance `CL > 0`, a volume `V > 0` and every real dose `D`, the bolus
[concentration](concentration-bolus.md) `C(t) = (D / V) * exp (-(CL / V) * t)` after a dose `D` with
no infusion is integrable on `[0, ∞)`. At zero clearance the concentration is the constant `D / V`,
which is not integrable on `[0, ∞)` unless `D = 0`.
