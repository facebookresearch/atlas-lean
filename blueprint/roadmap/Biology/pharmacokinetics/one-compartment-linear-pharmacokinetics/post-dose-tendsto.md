# Steady-state post-dose limit

For `0 ≤ q < 1` and all real `D` and `x₀`, the post-dose amounts
`x(n) = q ^ n * x₀ + D * ∑_{j=0}^{n-1} q ^ j` tend to `D / (1 - q)` as `n → ∞`: the limiting peak
(post-dose) amount. With `q = exp (-k * τ)` and `x₀ = D` they are the
[post-dose amounts](post-dose-amount-closed-form.md) of the periodic schedule.
