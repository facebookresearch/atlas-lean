# The finite-horizon AUC tends to the total AUC

For a clearance `CL > 0`, a volume `V > 0` and every real dose `D`, the finite-horizon AUC
`∫₀ᵀ C(t) dt` of the bolus [concentration](concentration-bolus.md)
`C(t) = (D / V) * exp (-(CL / V) * t)` after a dose `D` with no infusion tends to the total AUC
`D / CL` as `T → ∞`.
