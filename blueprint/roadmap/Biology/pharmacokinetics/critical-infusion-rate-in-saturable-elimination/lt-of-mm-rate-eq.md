# A nonnegative equilibrium forces the infusion rate below Vmax

Let `Vmax > 0` and `Km > 0`, and let `c ≥ 0` be an equilibrium of the infusion rate `R`:
`Vmax * c / (Km + c) = R`. Then `R < Vmax`: elimination at a nonnegative concentration never reaches
its capacity `Vmax`, so an infusion rate `R ≥ Vmax` has no nonnegative equilibrium.
