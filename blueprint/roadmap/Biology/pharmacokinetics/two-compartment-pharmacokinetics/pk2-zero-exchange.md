# Zero transfer and peripheral amount leave pure central elimination

For all real `k10`, `k21` and `A1`, with no transfer to the periphery, `k12 = 0`, and no peripheral
amount, `A2 = 0`, the central component of the [two-compartment generator](pk2-generator.md) applied
to `(A1, 0)` is `-k10 * A1`: the central equation is pure elimination.
