# Constructive neutral evolution

Excess capacity can make a change that creates dependence selectively
neutral, while a later loss of the supporting interaction would be harmful.
The [three-state dependency model](a-three-state-model-of-constructive-neutral-evol.md)
makes its allowed reversals explicit, with a stationary regime when
self-sufficiency can be regained and an absorbing regime when that rate is
zero. The specified zero-rate transition is distinct from a slow positive
rate. Origin-fixation interpretation also requires a stated clock and
separation of mutation introduction from fixation.

## Expansion

Extend the explicit model to several components, such as gene duplication
or splicing dependencies, and state its transition rates and accessibility
or absorption theorem. Increasing the number of components alone does not
prove a complexity trend without those rates. The three-state calculation
does not establish every multicomponent mechanism discussed by Stoltzfus.
