# Phenotypic branching models of viral evolution

Multitype Galton–Watson models in which viral particles are typed by their replication capacity and each copy may lose capacity by a deleterious mutation. Spectral analysis of the mean matrix gives a lethal-mutagenesis (extinction) threshold and a binomial stationary distribution of replication classes.
