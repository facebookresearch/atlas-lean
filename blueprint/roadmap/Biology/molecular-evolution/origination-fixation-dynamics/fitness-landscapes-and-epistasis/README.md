# Fitness landscapes and epistasis

Combinatorially complete sets of mutants assayed for fitness, growth or drug resistance, and the coordinates in which their landscapes are decomposed: Möbius (background-relative) coefficients and their sparse estimation across genetic and cellular contexts, Walsh (average-relative) coefficients and the partition of landscape variance by epistatic order, and landscapes indexed by drug concentration; how many parents to carry forward when a directed-evolution round searches such a landscape, and how long an origination–fixation walk in sequence space takes to discover a peak.
