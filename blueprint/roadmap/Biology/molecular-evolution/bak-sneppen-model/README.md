# Bak–Sneppen model

A minimal model of coevolution: $n$ species on a ring carry fitnesses in $[0,1]$, and at each step the least fit species and its two neighbours receive fresh independent uniform fitnesses. For small $n$ the stationary fitness distribution can be computed exactly.
