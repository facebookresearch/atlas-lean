# Phenotypic mutation rates and translational errors

Errors during transcription and translation, phenotypic mutations, make nonfunctional proteins that must be replaced. These articles give the expected amount of abnormal protein a cell synthesizes under a per-codon error rate, and the mutation-selection balance that sets the evolved phenotypic error rate relative to the genotypic mutation rate.
