# Birth, death and innovation models of gene family size

Master equations for the number of gene (domain) families of each size under duplication, loss and de novo origination, deterministic or as a birth–death chain, and the asymptotic laws of the equilibrium family-size distribution: power-law tails arise exactly when the rates are balanced to second order, and many rate laws share one stationary distribution while differing in their dynamics. A related birth–death–shift chain tracks the copy number and genomic location of transposable elements.
