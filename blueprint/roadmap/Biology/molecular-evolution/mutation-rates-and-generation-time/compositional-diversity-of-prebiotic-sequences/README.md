# Compositional diversity of prebiotic sequences

Biased monomer reactivity or abundance would confine early nucleic-acid polymers to a small, low-diversity corner of sequence space. These articles define the k-mer entropy measures of within-sequence and pool-level diversity used to quantify that bias, and a probability argument for why template-directed ligation favors compositionally diverse templates.
