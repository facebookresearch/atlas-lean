# Coevolutionary Potts landscapes of protein families

Statistical models of a protein family inferred from a multiple sequence alignment of homologs,
whose log-probability serves as a genotype-to-phenotype map: an independent-site profile and a
pairwise Potts model (direct coupling analysis) that makes the effect of a mutation depend on the
rest of the sequence.
