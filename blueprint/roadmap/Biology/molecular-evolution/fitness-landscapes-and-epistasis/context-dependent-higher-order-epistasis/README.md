# Context-dependent higher-order epistasis

Combinatorially complete sets of mutants, assayed in several genetic backgrounds and cellular environments, can be decomposed into main effects and interactions of every order. These articles give the regression form of that decomposition, its relation to the exact Möbius-inversion coefficients, and sparse estimation by the lasso.
