# Parallel mutation and selection waiting times

How long natural selection needs to assemble a specific genomic word when correct letters, once found, are retained. Guessing positions in parallel and keeping matches reduces the expected number of rounds from $K^L$ (blind search) to about $\log L/\log\frac{K}{K-1}$, the expected maximum of $L$ geometric waiting times.
