# Chemotaxis–Stokes system with signal consumption

The Tuval et al. model of aerobic bacteria (such as *Bacillus subtilis*) swimming up oxygen gradients in a fluid drop: bacteria of density $n$ are transported by the fluid velocity $u$ and move chemotactically toward oxygen $c$, which they consume, while their buoyancy drives the fluid. Signal consumption, unlike signal production, bounds the signal by its initial maximum, but whether this excludes finite-time singularities for large data in three dimensions was a long-standing question.
