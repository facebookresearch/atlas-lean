# Eikonal activation maps and earliest activation sites

Activation of excitable cardiac tissue modelled by an anisotropic eikonal equation: the arrival time of the excitation front is a minimum over stimulation sites of start time plus Riemannian geodesic distance. Inverse problems that locate the earliest activation sites from measured activation then reduce to optimizing over finitely many sites, with gradients supplied by the geodesics.
