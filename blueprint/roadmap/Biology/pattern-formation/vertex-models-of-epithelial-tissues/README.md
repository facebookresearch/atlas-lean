# Vertex models of epithelial tissues

Mechanical models of confluent epithelia in which cells are convex polygons tiling the plane and
the tissue minimizes (or relaxes along the gradient of) an energy penalizing deviations of cell
area and perimeter and weighting junction tensions; active self-propelled variants track cell
centres of a Voronoi tessellation with polarity-driven motion.
