# Couette flow suppression of chemotactic blow-up

Chemotactic cells living in a moving fluid can be prevented from aggregating into a singularity by strong shear: transport by a Couette flow $(Ay,0)$ mixes the non-zero Fourier modes in the streamwise direction and enhances their dissipation, while the remaining streamwise-averaged density is effectively one-dimensional. For Keller–Segel–Navier–Stokes systems with density-dependent motility this yields global solutions with arbitrarily large cell mass.
