# Turing patterns in bilayer domains

Many patterning tissues are stratified, for example a thin epithelium over a mesenchyme in which cells move chemotactically. Modelling each layer by its own reaction-(cross-)diffusion system and coupling them through a linear interface exchange law, one can ask whether the coupling raises or lowers the Turing threshold of the layer that patterns on its own. Perturbation in the coupling strength gives explicit first-order formulas in terms of cofactors of the uncoupled dispersion matrices.
