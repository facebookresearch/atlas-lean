# Chemotaxis with singular sensitivity and nonlocal damping

Keller–Segel models whose chemotactic sensitivity $\chi/v^k$ blows up where the signal is weak (the Weber–Fechner response, $k = 1$, and its weakly singular variants $0 < k < 1$), combined with population growth limited by a nonlocal, total-population damping $u^\alpha(r - \mu\int_\Omega u^\beta)$. The singular sensitivity enhances aggregation, the nonlocal term suppresses growth, and their balance determines global boundedness.
