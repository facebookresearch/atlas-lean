# Keller–Segel Jacobian ((−r, 0), (α, −β)) at (1, α/β)

For real $r$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$, the Keller–Segel Jacobian is the matrix

$$J = \begin{pmatrix} -r & 0 \\ \alpha & -\beta \end{pmatrix},$$

the
[Jacobian matrix](../../../Analysis/real-functions/multivariable-calculus/jacobian-matrix/jacobian-matrix.md)
of the [Keller–Segel kinetics](keller-segel-kinetics.md) at $(1, \alpha/\beta)$, read as $(1, 0)$
when $\beta = 0$ ([computation](jacobian-matrix-keller-segel.md)).
