# The Keller–Segel kinetics have Jacobian ((−r, 0), (α, −β)) at (1, α/β)

For real $r$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$, the
[Jacobian matrix](../../../Analysis/real-functions/multivariable-calculus/jacobian-matrix/jacobian-matrix.md)
of the [Keller–Segel kinetics](keller-segel-kinetics.md) at the
[state](keller-segel-steady-state.md) $(1, \alpha/\beta)$ is
$\begin{pmatrix} -r & 0 \\ \alpha & -\beta \end{pmatrix}$. This holds for all real inputs, including
$\beta = 0$, where the state is $(1, 0)$ by the convention $\alpha/0 = 0$.
