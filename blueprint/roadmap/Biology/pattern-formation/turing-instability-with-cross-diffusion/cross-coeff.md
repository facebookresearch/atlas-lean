# Mixed coefficient b of the cross-diffusion dispersion polynomial

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal). The mixed coefficient of the cross-diffusion
dispersion polynomial is

$$b = J_{00} D_{11} + J_{11} D_{00} - J_{01} D_{10} - J_{10} D_{01}.$$

The last two terms are the contribution of cross-diffusion; for a diagonal $D$ they vanish.
