# With f_u, g_v ≤ 0, no diagonal diffusion destabilizes stable kinetics

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix with
$\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$ and both species self-limiting, $f_u \le 0$ and $g_v \le 0$.
Then for all $d_u, d_v \ge 0$ no real wavenumber $k$ gives a
[linearly unstable](../turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/is-unstable-mode.md) Fourier
mode of $J - k^2 \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$. Turing instability with diagonal diffusion needs an
activator.
