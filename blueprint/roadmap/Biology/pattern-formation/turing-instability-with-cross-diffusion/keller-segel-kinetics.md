# Keller–Segel kinetics: logistic cells and a decaying chemoattractant

For real $r$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$, the Keller–Segel kinetics are the map $(u, v) \mapsto (f, g)$ on
$\mathbb{R}^2$ with

$$f(u, v) = r\, u (1 - u), \qquad g(u, v) = \alpha u - \beta v:$$

logistic growth of the cells $u$ (carrying capacity scaled to $1$), production $\alpha u$ and decay
$\beta v$ of the chemoattractant $v$. With $r = 0$ this is the minimal Keller–Segel model.
Biologically $\alpha, \beta > 0$ and $r \ge 0$.
