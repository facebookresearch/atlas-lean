# Keller–Segel flux (d_u u_x − χ u v_x, d_v v_x)

For real $d_u$, $d_v$ and $\chi$, the Keller–Segel flux at the state $(u, v)$ and the gradient
$(u_x, v_x)$ is

$$(d_u u_x - \chi u v_x,\ d_v v_x),$$

so that the system reads $w_t = (\text{flux})_x + \text{kinetics}$: the cells diffuse and, for
$\chi > 0$, climb the gradient of the chemoattractant. In the model $d_u, d_v > 0$, $u \ge 0$ and
$\chi \ge 0$.
