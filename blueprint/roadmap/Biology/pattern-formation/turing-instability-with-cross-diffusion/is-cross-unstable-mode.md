# Linearly unstable Fourier mode with cross-diffusion

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal), and let $k$ be real. The Fourier mode of
wavenumber $k$ is linearly unstable for the cross-diffusion system when the
[mode matrix](cross-mode-matrix.md) $J - k^2 D$ has a complex eigenvalue $\mu$ with
$\operatorname{Re} \mu > 0$: $(J - k^2 D)\, v = \mu v$ for some nonzero $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$.
