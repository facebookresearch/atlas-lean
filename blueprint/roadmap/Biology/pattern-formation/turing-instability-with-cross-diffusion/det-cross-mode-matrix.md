# The determinant of J − k²D is the dispersion polynomial at k²

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal), and let $k$ be real. The
[mode matrix](cross-mode-matrix.md) $J - k^2 D$ has determinant $\det(J - k^2 D) = h(k^2)$, where
$h(q) = \det D\, q^2 - b\, q + \det J$ is the [dispersion polynomial](cross-dispersion-det.md) and
$b = J_{00} D_{11} + J_{11} D_{00} - J_{01} D_{10} - J_{10} D_{01}$.
