# Linearized Keller–Segel diffusion matrix ((d_u, −χu₀), (0, d_v))

For real $d_u$, $d_v$, $\chi$ and $u_0$, the linearized Keller–Segel diffusion matrix at cell
density $u_0$ is

$$D = \begin{pmatrix} d_u & -\chi u_0 \\ 0 & d_v \end{pmatrix},$$

with cell motility $d_u$, chemical diffusivity $d_v$ and chemotactic sensitivity $\chi$. It is the
diffusion matrix of the system linearized at a homogeneous state with cell density $u_0$
([linearized flux](keller-segel-flux-add.md)). In the model $d_u, d_v > 0$, $u_0 \ge 0$ and
$\chi \ge 0$ (attraction for $\chi > 0$).
