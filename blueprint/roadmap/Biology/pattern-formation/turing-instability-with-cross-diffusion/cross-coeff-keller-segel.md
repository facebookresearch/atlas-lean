# For Keller–Segel, the mixed coefficient is b = αχu₀ − r d_v − β d_u

For real $r$, $\alpha$, $\beta$, $d_u$, $d_v$, $\chi$ and $u_0$, the
[mixed coefficient](cross-coeff.md) of the Keller–Segel [Jacobian](keller-segel-jacobian.md)
$J = \begin{pmatrix} -r & 0 \\ \alpha & -\beta \end{pmatrix}$ and
[diffusion matrix](keller-segel-diffusion.md)
$D = \begin{pmatrix} d_u & -\chi u_0 \\ 0 & d_v \end{pmatrix}$ is
$b = \alpha \chi u_0 - r d_v - \beta d_u$.
