# Stable kinetics and det(J − k²D) < 0 give diffusion-driven instability

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal), and let $k$ be real. Assume the kinetics are
stable without diffusion, $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$ and $\det J > 0$, and that
$\det(J - k^2 D) < 0$. Then the homogeneous mode $0$ is not
[linearly unstable](is-cross-unstable-mode.md) while the mode $k$ is.
