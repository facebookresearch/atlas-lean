# Minimal Keller–Segel: h(k²) = k²(d_u d_v k² − (αχu₀ − βd_u))

For real $\alpha$, $\beta$, $d_u$, $d_v$, $\chi$, $u_0$ and $k$, the
[dispersion polynomial](cross-dispersion-det.md) of the minimal Keller–Segel Jacobian
$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ \alpha & -\beta \end{pmatrix}$ ($r = 0$) and the
[diffusion matrix](keller-segel-diffusion.md)
$D = \begin{pmatrix} d_u & -\chi u_0 \\ 0 & d_v \end{pmatrix}$ satisfies

$$h(k^2) = k^2 \bigl(d_u d_v k^2 - (\alpha \chi u_0 - \beta d_u)\bigr).$$
