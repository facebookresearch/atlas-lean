# Dispersion relation with cross-diffusion

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal), and let $k$ be real, and let
$\mu \in \mathbb{C}$ and $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$. The
[Fourier mode](../turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/fourier-mode.md)
$w(t, x) = e^{\mu t} \cos(k x)\, v$ is a [classical solution](is-cross-linearized-solution.md) of
$w_t = D w_{xx} + J w$ on $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ if and only if $(J - k^2 D)\, v = \mu v$.
