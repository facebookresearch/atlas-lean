# Discriminant Δ = b² − 4 det D det J of the cross-diffusion band

Let $J$ and $D$ be real $2 \times 2$ matrices (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a homogeneous steady
state and a diffusion matrix, possibly non-diagonal). The band discriminant is
$\Delta = b^2 - 4 \det D \det J$, the discriminant of the
[dispersion polynomial](cross-dispersion-det.md) $h(q) = \det D\, q^2 - b\, q + \det J$, with
$b = J_{00} D_{11} + J_{11} D_{00} - J_{01} D_{10} - J_{10} D_{01}$.
