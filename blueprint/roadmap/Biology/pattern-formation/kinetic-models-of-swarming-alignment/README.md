# Kinetic models of swarming alignment

One-dimensional kinetic (two-velocity) models of self-propelled entities (insects, fish, bacteria, cells) whose orientation switching depends on the local densities of the two orientations. Isotropic (diffusive) and aligned (swarming) equilibria coexist; their macroscopic limits are, respectively, a linear diffusion equation and a traveling wave.
