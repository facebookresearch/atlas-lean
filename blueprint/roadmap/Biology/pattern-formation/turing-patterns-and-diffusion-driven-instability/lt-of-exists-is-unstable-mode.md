# The inhibitor diffuses faster

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$, $d_u \ge 0$, $d_v \ge 0$, and suppose
$u$ is the activator, $f_u > 0$ (then $g_v < 0$ follows from $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$). If some
Fourier mode $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md), then $d_u < d_v$.
