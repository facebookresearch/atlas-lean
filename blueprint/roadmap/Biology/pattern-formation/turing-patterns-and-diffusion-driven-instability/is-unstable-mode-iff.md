# Instability criterion for a Fourier mode via trace and determinant

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, let $k \in \mathbb{R}$ and $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$. The mode $k$ is
[unstable](is-unstable-mode.md) if and only if $\operatorname{tr}(J - k^2 D) > 0$ or $\det(J - k^2 D) < 0$.
