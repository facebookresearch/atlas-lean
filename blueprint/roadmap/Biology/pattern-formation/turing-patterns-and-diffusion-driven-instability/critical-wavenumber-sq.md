# Squared critical wavenumber

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients. The squared critical wavenumber is

$$k_c^2 = \frac{d_v f_u + d_u g_v}{2 d_u d_v},$$

read as $0$ when $d_u d_v = 0$. For $d_u, d_v > 0$ it is the
[minimizer](dispersion-det-critical-wavenumber-sq-le.md) of the dispersion polynomial.
