# Turing condition in squared form

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, and let $S = d_v f_u + d_u g_v$. Then $2\sqrt{d_u d_v \det J} < S$ if and only if $S > 0$ and
$4 d_u d_v \det J < S^2$. This holds for all real inputs, with the square root of a negative number read as $0$: when
$d_u d_v \det J < 0$ both sides reduce to $S > 0$.
