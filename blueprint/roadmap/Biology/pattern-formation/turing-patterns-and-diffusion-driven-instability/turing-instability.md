# Diffusion-driven instability (Turing 1952)

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$ and $\det J > 0$ (stability without diffusion),
$d_u \ge 0$ and $d_v \ge 0$, and let $k \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\det(J - k^2 D) < 0$, where
$D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$. Then the homogeneous mode $0$ is not [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md), while the
mode $k$ is unstable.
