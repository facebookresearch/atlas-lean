# Jacobian of the Schnakenberg kinetics at its steady state

For real $a$ and $b$, the Jacobian of the Schnakenberg kinetics at its steady state is

$$J_{\mathrm{S}} = \begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{b - a}{a + b} & (a + b)^2 \\ -\dfrac{2b}{a + b} & -(a + b)^2 \end{pmatrix},$$

with quotients by $0$ read as $0$.
