# Dispersion polynomial is negative exactly on the unstable band

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$ and $d_v > 0$. For every real $q$, the
[dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) satisfies $h(q) < 0$ if and only if the
[discriminant](band-discriminant.md) $\Delta$ is positive and $q_- < q < q_+$, where $q_\pm$ are the
[lower](band-lower.md) and [upper](band-upper.md) ends of the unstable band.
