# Computation of the Gierer–Meinhardt Jacobian at the steady state

For real $a$ and $b$ with $a + 1 \ne 0$ and $b \ne 0$, the Jacobian matrix of partial derivatives of the
[Gierer–Meinhardt kinetics](gierer-meinhardt.md) at the
[steady state](gierer-meinhardt-steady-state.md) $\bigl((a + 1)/b, ((a + 1)/b)^2\bigr)$ is the matrix
$J_{\mathrm{GM}}$ of the [Gierer–Meinhardt Jacobian](gierer-meinhardt-jacobian.md).
