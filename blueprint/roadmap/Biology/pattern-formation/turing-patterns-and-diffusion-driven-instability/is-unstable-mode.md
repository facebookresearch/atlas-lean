# Linearly unstable Fourier mode

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, and let $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$. The Fourier mode of wavenumber
$k \in \mathbb{R}$ is linearly unstable when the [mode matrix](mode-matrix.md) $J - k^2 D$ has a
complex eigenvalue $\mu$ with $\operatorname{Re} \mu > 0$, that is, $(J - k^2 D) v = \mu v$ for some
nonzero $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$.
