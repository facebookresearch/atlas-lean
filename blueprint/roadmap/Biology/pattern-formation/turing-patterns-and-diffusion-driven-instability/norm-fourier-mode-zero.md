# Amplitude of a Fourier mode

For $\mu \in \mathbb{C}$, $k \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$ and real $t$, the
[Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $w(t, x) = e^{\mu t} \cos(kx)\, v$ satisfies
$\|w(t, 0)\| = e^{(\operatorname{Re} \mu)\, t} \|v\|$, with $\|\cdot\|$ the maximum norm on
$\mathbb{C}^2$.
