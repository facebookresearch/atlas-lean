# Real 2 × 2 matrices: an eigenvalue with Re > 0 iff tr > 0 or det < 0

A real $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$ has a complex eigenvalue with positive real part (some $\mu \in \mathbb{C}$
with $\operatorname{Re} \mu > 0$ and a nonzero $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$ with $M v = \mu v$) if and only if
$\operatorname{tr} M > 0$ or $\det M < 0$.

In spectral form: some element of the
[spectrum of $M$ over $\mathbb{C}$](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/mem-spectrum-iff-is-char-root.md),
that is, of the complex matrix with the same entries, has positive real part if and only if
$\operatorname{tr} M > 0$ or $\det M < 0$.
