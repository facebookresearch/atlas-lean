# Unstable Neumann modes on a finite interval

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$ and $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, and let $L > 0$ and
$n \in \mathbb{N}$. The mode with [Neumann wavenumber](neumann-wavenumber.md) $k_n = n\pi/L$ is
[unstable](is-unstable-mode.md) if and only if the [discriminant](band-discriminant.md) $\Delta$ is positive and
$q_- < (n\pi/L)^2 < q_+$, where $q_\pm$ are the [lower](band-lower.md) and [upper](band-upper.md)
ends of the unstable band.
