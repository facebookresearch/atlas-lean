# Small domains admit no pattern

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$, $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$ and $\det J > 0$, and let $L > 0$.
If the first nonhomogeneous Neumann mode already lies beyond the band, $q_+ \le (\pi/L)^2$ with $q_+$
the [upper end](band-upper.md) of the unstable band, then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the mode with
[Neumann wavenumber](neumann-wavenumber.md) $n\pi/L$ is not [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md).
