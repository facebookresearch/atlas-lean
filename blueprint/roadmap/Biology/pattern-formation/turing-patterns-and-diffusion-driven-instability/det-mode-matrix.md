# Determinant of the mode matrix is the dispersion polynomial

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients. For every real $k$, the [mode matrix](mode-matrix.md) satisfies
$\det(J - k^2 D) = h(k^2)$, where $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$ and $h$ is the
[dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md).
