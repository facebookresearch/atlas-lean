# Fourier mode grows unboundedly iff its growth rate is positive

Let $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$ be nonzero, $\mu \in \mathbb{C}$ and $k \in \mathbb{R}$. The
[Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $w(t, x) = e^{\mu t} \cos(kx)\, v$ satisfies $\|w(t, 0)\| \to \infty$
as $t \to \infty$ if and only if $\operatorname{Re} \mu > 0$.
