# Turing's criterion for diffusion-driven instability

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$ and $\det J > 0$. The
[dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) is negative at some $q \ge 0$ if and only if
$2\sqrt{d_u d_v \det J} < d_v f_u + d_u g_v$.
