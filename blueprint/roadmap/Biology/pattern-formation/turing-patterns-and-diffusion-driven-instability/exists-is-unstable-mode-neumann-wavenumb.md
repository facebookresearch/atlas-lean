# Long enough intervals have an unstable Neumann mode

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$, $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$ and
$2\sqrt{d_u d_v \det J} < d_v f_u + d_u g_v$. Then there is $L_0 > 0$ such that for every $L \ge L_0$
some [Neumann wavenumber](neumann-wavenumber.md) $k_n = n\pi/L$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, is an
[unstable](is-unstable-mode.md) mode.
