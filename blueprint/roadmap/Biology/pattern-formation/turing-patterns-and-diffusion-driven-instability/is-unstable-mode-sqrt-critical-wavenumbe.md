# Critical wavenumber is unstable under Turing's criterion

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$, $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$ and
$2\sqrt{d_u d_v \det J} < d_v f_u + d_u g_v$. Then the
[squared critical wavenumber](critical-wavenumber-sq.md) is positive, $k_c^2 > 0$, and the mode
$k_c = \sqrt{k_c^2}$ is [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md).
