# Neumann modes have zero flux at the boundary

Let $L \ne 0$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mu \in \mathbb{C}$, $v \in \mathbb{C}^2$ and $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
For the [Neumann wavenumber](neumann-wavenumber.md) $k_n = n\pi/L$, the
[Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $x \mapsto e^{\mu t} \cos(k_n x)\, v$ has derivative $0$ at $x = 0$ and
at $x = L$: it has zero flux at both ends of the interval between $0$ and $L$.
