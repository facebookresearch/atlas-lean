# Discriminant of the dispersion polynomial

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients. The discriminant of the [dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) is

$$\Delta = (d_v f_u + d_u g_v)^2 - 4 d_u d_v \det J.$$
