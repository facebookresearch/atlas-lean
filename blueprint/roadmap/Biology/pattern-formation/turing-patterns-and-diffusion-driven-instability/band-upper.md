# Upper end of the unstable wavenumber band

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, let $S = d_v f_u + d_u g_v$ and let $\Delta$ be the [discriminant](band-discriminant.md) of the
dispersion polynomial. The upper end of the unstable band of squared wavenumbers is

$$q_+ = \frac{S + \sqrt{\Delta}}{2 d_u d_v},$$

where the square root of a negative number is read as $0$ and the quotient is read as $0$ when $d_u d_v = 0$. The band is meaningful
for $d_u, d_v > 0$ and $\Delta > 0$.
