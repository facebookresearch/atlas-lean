# $f_u$ and $g_v$ have opposite signs

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $\det J > 0$ (stability without diffusion),
$d_u \ge 0$ and $d_v \ge 0$. If some Fourier mode $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md), then
$f_u g_v < 0$.
