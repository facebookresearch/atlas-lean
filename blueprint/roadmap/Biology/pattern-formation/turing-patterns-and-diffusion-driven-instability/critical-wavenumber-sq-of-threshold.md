# Critical wavenumber at the Turing bifurcation threshold

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$, $d_v > 0$ and $\det J \ge 0$. At the bifurcation threshold
$d_v f_u + d_u g_v = 2\sqrt{d_u d_v \det J}$, the [squared critical wavenumber](critical-wavenumber-sq.md)
is $k_c^2 = \sqrt{\det J/(d_u d_v)}$, and the [dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) vanishes there,
$h(k_c^2) = 0$.
