# Complex Fourier mode

For a growth rate $\mu \in \mathbb{C}$, a wavenumber $k \in \mathbb{R}$ and an amplitude vector
$v \in \mathbb{C}^2$, the complex Fourier mode is

$$w(t, x) = e^{\mu t} \cos(kx)\, v \in \mathbb{C}^2 \qquad (t, x \in \mathbb{R}).$$

The real part of a complex solution of a real linear system is a real solution.
