# Trace of the mode matrix

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients. For every real $k$, the [mode matrix](mode-matrix.md) has trace
$\operatorname{tr}(J - k^2 D) = \operatorname{tr} J - k^2 (d_u + d_v)$, with
$D = \operatorname{diag}(d_u, d_v)$.
