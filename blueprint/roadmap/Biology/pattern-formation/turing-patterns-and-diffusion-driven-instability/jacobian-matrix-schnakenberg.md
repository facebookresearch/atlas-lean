# Computation of the Schnakenberg Jacobian at the steady state

For real $a$ and $b$ with $a + b \ne 0$, the Jacobian matrix of partial derivatives of the
[Schnakenberg kinetics](schnakenberg.md) at the [steady state](schnakenberg-steady-state.md)
$\bigl(a + b, b/(a + b)^2\bigr)$ is the matrix $J_{\mathrm{S}}$ of the
[Schnakenberg Jacobian](schnakenberg-jacobian.md).
