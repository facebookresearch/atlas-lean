# Mode instability iff the dispersion polynomial is negative

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $\operatorname{tr} J < 0$, $d_u \ge 0$ and $d_v \ge 0$. For every real $k$, the
mode $k$ is [unstable](is-unstable-mode.md) if and only if the [dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) satisfies
$h(k^2) < 0$.
