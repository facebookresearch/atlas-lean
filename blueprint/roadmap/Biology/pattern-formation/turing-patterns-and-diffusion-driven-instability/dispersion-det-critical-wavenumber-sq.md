# Minimum value of the dispersion polynomial

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} f_u & f_v \\ g_u & g_v \end{pmatrix}$ be a real $2 \times 2$ matrix (the Jacobian of the kinetics at a steady state) and let $d_u, d_v$ be real diffusion coefficients, with $d_u > 0$ and $d_v > 0$. At the
[squared critical wavenumber](critical-wavenumber-sq.md) $k_c^2$, the
[dispersion polynomial](dispersion-det.md) takes the value

$$h(k_c^2) = \det J - \frac{(d_v f_u + d_u g_v)^2}{4 d_u d_v}.$$
