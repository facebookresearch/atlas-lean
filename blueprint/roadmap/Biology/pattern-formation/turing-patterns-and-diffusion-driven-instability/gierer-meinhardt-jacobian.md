# Jacobian of the Gierer–Meinhardt kinetics at its steady state

For real $a$ and $b$, the Jacobian of the Gierer–Meinhardt kinetics at its steady state is

$$J_{\mathrm{GM}} = \begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{b(1 - a)}{1 + a} & -\left(\dfrac{b}{a + 1}\right)^2 \\ \dfrac{2(a + 1)}{b} & -1 \end{pmatrix},$$

with quotients by $0$ read as $0$.
