# Gierer–Meinhardt activator-inhibitor kinetics

For real $a$ and $b$, the Gierer–Meinhardt activator–inhibitor kinetics in Murray's nondimensional form
are the map $(u, v) \mapsto (f, g)$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with

$$f(u, v) = a - b u + \frac{u^2}{v}, \qquad g(u, v) = u^2 - v,$$

where $u^2/v$ is read as $0$ when $v = 0$. Biologically $a, b > 0$ and $u, v > 0$.
