# Turing instability in reaction networks with conservation laws

For mass-action networks with conserved totals, the characteristic polynomial of the Jacobian scaled by the inverse diffusion matrix has a zero root of multiplicity equal to the number of conservation laws. Opposite signs of its leading and lowest nonzero coefficients, together with a lower bound on the domain size, force a diffusion-driven instability; monomial steady-state parameterizations turn these conditions into polynomial inequalities in the rate constants.
