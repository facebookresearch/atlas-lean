# Wavetrains of generalized predator–prey reaction–diffusion systems

Travelling wavetrains of two-species predator–prey reaction–diffusion equations with general prey growth and predator death functions, studied through Hopf bifurcations of the travelling-wave ODEs with the wave speed as parameter; supercritical bifurcations give stable periodic wavetrains and subcritical ones can lead to temporally chaotic wavetrains.
