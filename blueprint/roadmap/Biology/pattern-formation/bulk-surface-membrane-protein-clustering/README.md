# Bulk-surface membrane-protein clustering

Bulk-surface reaction-diffusion models of protein aggregation on the plasma membrane: a cytosolic ligand binds the membrane, forms laterally diffusing oligomers, and the largest oligomers recruit more ligand. The articles cover mass conservation and the reduction to a nonlocal surface system, and a threshold on the recruitment feedback below which no Turing patterns (clusters) can form.
