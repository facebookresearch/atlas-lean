# Differential adhesion in multispecies Keller–Segel systems

Steinberg's Differential Adhesion Hypothesis states that cell populations sort by minimizing an interfacial adhesion energy, as immiscible fluids minimize surface tension. A rescaled multispecies Patlak–Keller–Segel system with nonlocal attraction of strengths $\alpha_{ij}$ and a shared nonlinear pressure realizes this: in the short-range limit the species phase-separate, the energy converges to a multiphase perimeter functional, and the dynamics converge to a multiphase Hele–Shaw flow with surface tension.
