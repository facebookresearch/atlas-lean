# Projecting a single-species field

For a topological space $K$, species indices $k, i \in \{0, 1\}$ and $\varphi \in C(K, \mathbb{R})$,
the [projection onto species $k$](species-proj.md) of the [single-species field](species-emb.md)
$\varphi\, e_i$ is $\varphi$ if $k = i$ and the zero function if $k \neq i$.
