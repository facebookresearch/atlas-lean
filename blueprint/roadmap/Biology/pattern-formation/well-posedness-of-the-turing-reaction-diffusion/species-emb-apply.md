# A single-species field at a point

For a topological space $K$, a species index $i \in \{0, 1\}$, a function
$\varphi \in C(K, \mathbb{R})$ and a point $x \in K$, the [single-species field](species-emb.md) of
$\varphi$ takes the value $\varphi(x)\, e_i$ at $x$: the vector of $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $\varphi(x)$
in coordinate $i$ and $0$ in the other one.
