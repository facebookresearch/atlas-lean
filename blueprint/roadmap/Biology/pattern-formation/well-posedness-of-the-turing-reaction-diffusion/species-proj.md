# Concentration field of one species

For a topological space $K$ and a species index $i \in \{0, 1\}$ (species $u$ for $i = 0$, $v$ for
$i = 1$), the projection onto species $i$ is the continuous linear map
$C(K, \mathbb{R}^2) \to C(K, \mathbb{R})$ that sends a field $w$ to its coordinate
$x \mapsto w(x)_i$.
