# Field of one species alone

For a topological space $K$ and a species index $i \in \{0, 1\}$, the embedding of species $i$ is
the continuous linear map $C(K, \mathbb{R}) \to C(K, \mathbb{R}^2)$ that sends $\varphi$ to the
field $x \mapsto \varphi(x)\, e_i$, with $\varphi$ in coordinate $i$ and $0$ in the other one
($e_0, e_1$ the standard basis vectors of $\mathbb{R}^2$).
