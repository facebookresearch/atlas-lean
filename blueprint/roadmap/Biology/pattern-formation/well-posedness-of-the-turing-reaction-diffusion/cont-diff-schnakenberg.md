# The Schnakenberg kinetics are smooth

For all real $a$ and $b$, the
[Schnakenberg kinetics](../turing-patterns-and-diffusion-driven-instability/schnakenberg.md)
$(u, v) \mapsto (a - u + u^2 v,\ b - u^2 v)$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ are $C^n$ for every $n$, including
$n = \infty$, and analytic: their components are polynomials.
