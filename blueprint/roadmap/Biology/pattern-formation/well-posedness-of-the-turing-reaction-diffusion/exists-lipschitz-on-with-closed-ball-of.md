# C¹ maps on a finite-dimensional space are Lipschitz on closed balls

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space and let $f : E \to E$ be continuously
differentiable ($C^1$). Then for every real $R$ there is a constant $L \ge 0$ such that $f$ is
$L$-Lipschitz on the closed ball $\{x : \lVert x \rVert \le R\}$ (empty for $R < 0$).

Applied pointwise, this makes the reaction term $w \mapsto f \circ w$ of a reaction–diffusion system
with $C^1$ kinetics Lipschitz on every closed ball of $C(K, E)$ with the sup norm, with the same
constants, as the local existence theory for mild solutions requires.
