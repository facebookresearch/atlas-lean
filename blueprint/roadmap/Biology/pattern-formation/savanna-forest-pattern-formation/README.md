# Savanna–forest pattern formation

Nonlocal (integro-differential) mean-field models of grass, savanna trees and forest trees in mesic tropical landscapes, where fire spreads through flammable grass and acts as a threshold disturbance on trees. Threshold fire feedback alone does not destabilize homogeneous states, but adding resource competition among forest trees over a longer range than seed dispersal produces Turing-type instabilities and savanna–forest mosaics.
