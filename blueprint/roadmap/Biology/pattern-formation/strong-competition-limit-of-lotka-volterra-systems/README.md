# Strong-competition limit of Lotka-Volterra systems

For the stationary Gause-Lotka-Volterra competition-diffusion system with interspecific competition of strength $\beta \to \infty$, species segregate into disjoint regions separated by interfaces. The converse problem asks which segregated limit profiles are actually reached by positive solutions at large but finite $\beta$. In one dimension with drift and in the radial setting, every ordering of species that admits a segregated profile is approached at rate $\beta^{-1/3}$ by a positive solution, with a universal two-species inner layer at each interface; counting orderings gives a lower bound on the number of positive steady states.
