# Traveling waves of chemotaxis models with logistic source

Front propagation for a species that grows logistically, diffuses, and moves up ($\chi > 0$) or down ($\chi < 0$) the gradient of a chemical it produces, modeled by the one-dimensional parabolic–elliptic system $u_t = u_{xx} - \chi(u^m v_x)_x + u(1-u^\alpha)$, $0 = v_{xx} - v + u^\gamma$. At $\chi = 0$ it reduces to the Fisher–KPP equation with minimal front speed $2$; chemorepulsion speeds fronts up, and fast waves are unique and stable.
