# Fronts at or above the minimal speed lie in the open trapping triangle

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c \ge 2\sqrt{rD}$. If $(U, V)$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$, with
$V$ the derivative of $U$, then $(U(z), V(z))$ lies for every $z$ in the open trapping triangle
$\{(u, v) : u < 1,\ v < 0,\ v + c u/(2D) > 0\}$, the interior of the triangle with vertices
$(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(1, -c/(2D))$.
