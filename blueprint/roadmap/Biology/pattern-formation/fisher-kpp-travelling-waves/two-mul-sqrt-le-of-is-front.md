# Every Fisher–KPP front speed is at least the minimal speed

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$. If $(U, U')$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$, then
$c \ge 2\sqrt{rD}$: no front is slower than the minimal speed $2\sqrt{rD}$. Equivalently, the set of
speeds $c$ for which a traveling front of speed $c$ exists is contained in $[2\sqrt{rD}, \infty)$.
