# Time derivative of a traveling wave

Let $D, r, c \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $(U, U')$ be a [traveling-wave profile](is-wave-profile.md) of speed $c$. For all real $x$ and $t$, the function
$s \mapsto U(x - cs)$ has derivative $-c\,U'(x - ct)$ at $s = t$: the wave $u(x, t) = U(x - ct)$
satisfies $\partial_t u = -c\,U'(x - ct)$.
