# Along a Fisher–KPP front the derivative tends to 0 at −∞

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$. If $(U, V)$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$, with
$V$ the derivative of $U$, then $V(z) \to 0$ as $z \to -\infty$: the orbit leaves the saddle
$(1, 0)$.
