# For c² < 4rD the linearization at U = 0 has two complex roots

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$, and let $c$ be real with $c^2 < 4rD$. A complex number $w$ is a root of the
characteristic polynomial $D w^2 + c w + r$ of the profile equation linearized at $U = 0$ if and
only if

$$
w = \frac{-c + i\sqrt{4rD - c^2}}{2D} \qquad \text{or} \qquad
w = \frac{-c - i\sqrt{4rD - c^2}}{2D}.
$$
