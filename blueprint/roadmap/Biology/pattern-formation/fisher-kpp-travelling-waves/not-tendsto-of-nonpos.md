# For c ≤ 0 no wave profile connects 1 at −∞ to 0 at +∞

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c \le 0$. No
[traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md)
$(U, U')$ of speed $c$ has $U(z) \to 1$ as $z \to -\infty$ and $U(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$; no
sign condition on $U$ is assumed. The proof uses the energy $E = D U'^2/2 + F(U)$,
$F(u) = r(u^2/2 - u^3/3)$, which satisfies $E' = -c\, U'^2 \ge 0$.
