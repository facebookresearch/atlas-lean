# For c² < 4rD a profile decaying to 0 is ≤ 0 arbitrarily far out

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$, and let $c$ be real with $c^2 < 4rD$. If $(U, U')$ is a
[traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md) of
speed $c$ with $U(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$, then for every $Z$ there is $z \ge Z$ with
$U(z) \le 0$.
