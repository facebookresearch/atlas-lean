# A front of positive speed has negative derivative everywhere

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c > 0$. If $(U, V)$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$, with
$V$ the derivative of $U$, then $V(z) < 0$ for every $z$, so $U$ is strictly decreasing.
