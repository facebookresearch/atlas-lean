# No Fisher–KPP traveling front below the minimal speed

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$. For every real $c < 2\sqrt{rD}$, no pair $(U, U')$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$;
negative speeds are included. The case $c^2 < 4rD$ is
[no Fisher–KPP traveling front with $c^2 < 4rD$](not-is-front-of-sq-lt.md).
