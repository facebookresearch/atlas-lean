# No eventually positive wave decaying to $0$ with $c^2 < 4rD$

Let $D > 0$, $r > 0$ and $c^2 < 4rD$. If $(U, U')$ is a
[traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md) of speed $c$ with $U(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$, then
$U$ is not eventually positive; equivalently, for every $Z$ there is $z \ge Z$ with
$U(z) \le 0$. This excludes
$|c| < 2\sqrt{rD}$, not $c < 2\sqrt{rD}$ for negative $c$.
