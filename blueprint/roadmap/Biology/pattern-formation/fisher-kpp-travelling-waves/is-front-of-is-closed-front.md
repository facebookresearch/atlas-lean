# For D > 0 every closed traveling front is a traveling front

Let $D > 0$ and let $r$ and $c$ be real. Every [closed traveling front](is-closed-front.md)
$(U, U')$ of speed $c$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md): the values $0$ and
$1$ are attained at no point, so $0 < U < 1$.
