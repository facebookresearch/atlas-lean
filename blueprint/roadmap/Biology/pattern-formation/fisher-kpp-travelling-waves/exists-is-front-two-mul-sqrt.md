# A strictly decreasing front exists at the minimal speed

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$. There is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) $(U, U')$ of the
minimal speed $2\sqrt{rD}$ with strictly decreasing profile $U$. It is the case $c = 2\sqrt{rD}$ of
the [Kolmogorov–Petrovsky–Piskunov theorem](exists-is-front-of-le.md).
