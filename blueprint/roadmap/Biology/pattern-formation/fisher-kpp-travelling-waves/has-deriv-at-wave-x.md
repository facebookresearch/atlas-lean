# Space derivative of a traveling wave

Let $D, r, c \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $(U, U')$ be a [traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md) of speed $c$. For all real $x$ and $t$, the function
$y \mapsto U(y - ct)$ has derivative $U'(x - ct)$ at $y = x$: the wave $u(x, t) = U(x - ct)$
satisfies $\partial_x u = U'(x - ct)$.
