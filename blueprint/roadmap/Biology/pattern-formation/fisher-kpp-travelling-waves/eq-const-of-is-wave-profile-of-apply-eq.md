# A bounded profile through an equilibrium state is constant

Let $D > 0$, let $r$ and $c$ be real, and let $e \in \{0, 1\}$. If $(U, U')$ is a
[traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md) of
speed $c$ with $|U(z)| \le 2$ for every $z$, and $U(z_0) = e$ and $U'(z_0) = 0$ at one point $z_0$,
then $U(z) = e$ and $U'(z) = 0$ for every $z$.
