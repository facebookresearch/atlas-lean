# A Fisher–KPP front is a heteroclinic orbit in the phase plane

Let $D > 0$ and $r > 0$. If $(U, V)$ is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) of speed $c$, then
the curve $z \mapsto (U(z), V(z))$, an orbit of the planar system $U' = V$,
$V' = -(c V + r U(1 - U))/D$, tends to the saddle $(1, 0)$ as $z \to -\infty$ and to the node
$(0, 0)$ as $z \to +\infty$.
