# Closed traveling front of the Fisher–KPP equation

For real $D$, $r$ and $c$, a pair of functions $U, U' : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a closed
traveling front of speed $c$ when it is a
[traveling-wave profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-wave-profile.md) of
speed $c$, $0 \le U(z) \le 1$ for every $z$, $U(z) \to 1$ as $z \to -\infty$ and $U(z) \to 0$ as
$z \to +\infty$. It is a
[traveling front](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/is-front.md) with the strict
bounds $0 < U < 1$ relaxed to $0 \le U \le 1$.
