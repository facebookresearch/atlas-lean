# Reduced dynamics of meandering spiral waves

Justify the low-dimensional equations used to describe a spiral wave after a Hopf instability. Re-entrant waves motivate the connection to arrhythmia, but this is a reaction–diffusion theorem, not a treatment claim.

## What would settle it

In the setting of the cited conjecture, assume that the critical spectrum consists of a simple pair of imaginary eigenvalues crossing the imaginary axis, in addition to the neutral eigenvalues from translation and rotation, so that the problem on a disk of radius R has a five-dimensional center manifold. Prove that the Taylor jet of the vector field on this manifold converges exponentially as R grows, with a bound of the form $C e^{-\eta R}$ in fixed coordinates and norms, and that the limit is the skew-product system of the cited reference. Existence of a center manifold alone is insufficient.
