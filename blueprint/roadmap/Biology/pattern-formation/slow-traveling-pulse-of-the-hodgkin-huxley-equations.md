# Slow traveling pulse of the Hodgkin–Huxley equations

Prove the existence of the numerically observed slow traveling pulse of the unmodified Hodgkin–Huxley equations at explicitly fixed parameters.

## What would settle it

Define the traveling-wave ODE with the exact rate laws, units, constants, and temperature. Construct a nonconstant homoclinic solution whose speed lies in an interval distinguishing it from the fast branch. A verified shooting/interval certificate could establish existence.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Hastings (slides, 2015) records proofs of a fast pulse for the Hodgkin–Huxley equations and of fast and slow pulses for the simpler FitzHugh–Nagumo equations, and states that there has been no proof of the existence of a slow pulse for the Hodgkin–Huxley equations.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
