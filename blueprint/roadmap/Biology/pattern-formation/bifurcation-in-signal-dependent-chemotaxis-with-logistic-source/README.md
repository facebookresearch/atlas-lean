# Bifurcation in signal-dependent chemotaxis with logistic source

Steady patterns of the one-dimensional parabolic–elliptic chemotaxis model $u_t = u_{xx} - \chi_0(u^m(1+v)^{-\beta}v_x)_x + au - bu^{1+\alpha}$, $0 = v_{xx} - \mu v + \nu u^\gamma$ on $(0,L)$, as the chemotactic sensitivity $\chi_0$ crosses the Turing-type threshold of a positive constant state. The signal-dependence exponent $\beta$ (receptor saturation) both raises the threshold and selects whether the pitchfork is super- or sub-critical; global branches extend to large $\chi_0$, where steady states develop spikes.
