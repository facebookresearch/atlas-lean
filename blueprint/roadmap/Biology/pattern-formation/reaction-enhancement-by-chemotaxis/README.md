# Reaction enhancement by chemotaxis

Two-species models in which a mobile density is chemotactically attracted to a stationary target that emits the signal, and the two are consumed on contact (fertilization by attracted sperm, immune cells attracted to infected tissue). Rigorous scaling laws quantify how much chemotaxis, here with a biologically realistic speed limit (flux limitation), shortens the reaction half-time compared with pure diffusion.
