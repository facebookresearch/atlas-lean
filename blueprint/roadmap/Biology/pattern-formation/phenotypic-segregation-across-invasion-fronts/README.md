# Phenotypic segregation across invasion fronts

Pressure-driven models of growing cell populations made of several discrete phenotypes that differ in proliferation and mobility. Cells move down the gradient of a cellular pressure built from all phenotypes; when the most proliferative phenotype is the least mobile, travelling-wave solutions show the phenotypes ordered in space, with the most mobile cells at the invading edge.
