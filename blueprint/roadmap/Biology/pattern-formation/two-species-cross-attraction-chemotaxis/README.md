# Two-species cross-attraction chemotaxis

Systems in which each of two populations is attracted to a signal produced by the other (for example macrophages and tumor cells), with degenerate diffusion. A free energy with a Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev interaction term determines a sharp threshold between global existence and blow-up.
