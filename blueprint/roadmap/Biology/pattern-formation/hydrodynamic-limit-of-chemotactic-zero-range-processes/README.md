# Hydrodynamic limit of chemotactic zero-range processes

Derivation of the cell equation of Keller–Segel-type chemotaxis models from stochastic many-particle systems on a lattice. Cells hop between neighbouring sites at rates that depend on the local chemical environment and on the number of cells at the site (a zero-range interaction); in the diffusive scaling the empirical density converges to a nonlinear diffusion equation whose chemotactic drift emerges from the slowly varying mean of the chemical field, without cells sensing gradients.
