# Proliferating–quiescent tumour invasion waves

Reaction-diffusion models of glioma that split tumour cells into proliferating and quiescent classes, with birth and quiescence depending on available growth factor. Their travelling-wave profiles tie the enhancing and edematous rims seen on MRI to the model parameters.
