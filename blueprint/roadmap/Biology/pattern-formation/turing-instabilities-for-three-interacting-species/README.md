# Turing instabilities for three interacting species

For two species the Turing condition is a classical pair of inequalities. For three species, diffusion can destabilize a stable steady state either through a real eigenvalue (stationary Turing pattern) or through a complex pair (Turing–Hopf, oscillatory pattern), and both cases are characterized by explicit polynomial inequalities in the Jacobian and diffusion coefficients.
