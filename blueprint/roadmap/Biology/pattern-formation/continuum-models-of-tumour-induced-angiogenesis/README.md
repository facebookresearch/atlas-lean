# Continuum models of tumour-induced angiogenesis

Angiogenesis, the growth of new capillaries from existing vessels, drives both wound healing and the vascularization of solid tumours. Continuum models describe it with partial differential equations for the density of migrating endothelial tip cells, driven by chemotaxis up gradients of a tumour-secreted angiogenic factor and by haptotaxis up gradients of matrix-bound fibronectin.
