# Asymptotic stability of the critical constant steady state of the parabolic–elliptic Keller–Segel system

**Model.** On $\mathbb{R}^N$, $N \ge 3$, consider the simplified parabolic–elliptic Keller–Segel system for a cell density $u$ and a chemoattractant $\psi$:

$$
\partial_t u - \Delta u + \nabla\cdot(u\nabla\psi) = 0, \qquad -\Delta\psi + \psi = u, \qquad u(\cdot,0) = u_0 .
$$

Every constant $(u,\psi) = (A,A)$, $A \in \mathbb{R}$, is a steady state. Writing $\psi = K * u$ with $K$ the Bessel kernel of $(1-\Delta)^{-1}$ and $G$ the heat kernel, a (mild) solution is $u \in C([0,T); L^{N/2}_{\mathrm{ul}}(\mathbb{R}^N))$ with

$$ u(t) = G(t)*u_0 - \int_0^t \nabla\cdot\big[G(t-s)*(u(s)\nabla K*u(s))\big]\,ds , $$

where $L^p_{\mathrm{ul}}$ is the uniformly local Lebesgue space ($\sup_z \|f\|_{L^p(B_1(z))} < \infty$) and $\mathcal{L}^p_{\mathrm{ul}}$ its closed subspace generated by bounded uniformly continuous functions.

**Known dichotomy.** Constants with $A < 1$ are asymptotically stable and constants with $A > 1$ are Lyapunov unstable under small $L^p$ perturbations, so $A = 1$ is the stability threshold.

**Theorem (critical case $A = 1$).** Let $N \ge 3$ and set $\langle t\rangle_L = \sqrt{1+t^2}$, $\langle t\rangle_S = t/\langle t\rangle_L$. There are $\varepsilon_* > 0$ and $C > 0$ such that, if $u_0 \in \mathcal{L}^{N/2}_{\mathrm{ul}}(\mathbb{R}^N)$ and $\|u_0 - 1\|_{L^{N/2}(\mathbb{R}^N)} \le \varepsilon_*$, then there is a global solution $u$ with $u, \nabla u \in C((0,\infty); \mathcal{L}^r_{\mathrm{ul}})$ and, for every $r \in [N/2,\infty]$ and $t > 0$,

$$
\langle t\rangle_S^{\frac N2(\frac2N - \frac1r)}\langle t\rangle_L^{\frac N4(\frac2N - \frac1r)}\|u(t)-1\|_{L^r}
+ \langle t\rangle_S^{\frac N2(\frac2N - \frac1r)+\frac12}\langle t\rangle_L^{\frac N4(\frac2N - \frac1r)+\frac14}\|\nabla u(t)\|_{L^r}
\le C\,\|u_0 - 1\|_{L^{N/2}} .
$$

In particular $u(t) - 1 \to 0$ in $W^{1,r}(\mathbb{R}^N)$ for every $r \in (N/2,\infty]$.

The large-time decay rate $t^{-\frac N4(\frac2N-\frac1r)}$ is half the heat-like rate $t^{-\frac N2(\frac2N-\frac1r)}$ obtained for subcritical constants $A \in [0,1)$.

## Expansion

Linearizing at $A$, the perturbation $v = u - A$ has Fourier symbol $h_A(\xi) = |\xi|^2 - A|\xi|^2/(1+|\xi|^2)$. For $A < 1$ this behaves like $(1-A)|\xi|^2$ at low frequency (heat-like), but for $A = 1$ it is $h_1(\xi) = |\xi|^4/(1+|\xi|^2)$: biharmonic at low frequency and heat-like at high frequency. The low-frequency dynamics is formally $\partial_t v + (-\Delta)^2 v + \nabla\cdot(v\nabla v) = 0$, which is scale invariant in $L^{N/2}$, explaining the choice of space. The proof splits frequencies, proves $L^p$–$L^q$ estimates for the mixed semigroup with the two-regime time weights $\langle t\rangle_S$, $\langle t\rangle_L$, and closes a fixed point with a bilinear estimate for the chemotactic term.
