# Duration of a complete deterministic sweep

Let $s \ne 0$ and $N > 1$. Then the [sweep duration](sweep-duration.md) from $1/(2N)$ to $1 - 1/(2N)$ is

$$\tau_s\Bigl(\frac{1}{2N}, 1 - \frac{1}{2N}\Bigr) = \frac{2}{s} \log(2N - 1).$$

For $s > 0$ this is the time of a complete deterministic sweep from one copy in $N$ diploids; the familiar $\frac{2}{s}\log(2Ns)$ is the conditional expected duration of a stochastic sweep, a different quantity.
