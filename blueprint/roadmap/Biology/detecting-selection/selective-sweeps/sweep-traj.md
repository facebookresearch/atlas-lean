# Logistic trajectory of a selective sweep

For real numbers $s$ (the genic selection coefficient), $x_0$ (the initial frequency) and $t$ (time), the logistic trajectory is

$$x(t) = \frac{x_0 e^{st}}{1 - x_0 + x_0 e^{st}},$$

with the convention $a/0 = 0$. For $0 < x_0 < 1$ it solves $x' = s x (1 - x)$ with $x(0) = x_0$ (Maynard Smith and Haigh 1974).

## Sources

- J. Maynard Smith and J. Haigh, *The hitch-hiking effect of a favourable gene*, Genetical Research 23 (1974), 23–35.
