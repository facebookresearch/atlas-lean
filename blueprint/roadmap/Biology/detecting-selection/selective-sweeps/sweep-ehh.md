# EHH in the star-genealogy sweep model

For real numbers $r$ (the recombination rate per unit genetic length), $T$ (the age of the sweep) and $d$ (the genetic length of the window), put

$$\mathrm{EHH}^\star(r, T, d) = e^{-2 r d T}.$$

For $r, T, d \ge 0$, in the star-genealogy model of Voight et al. (2006), where all carriers coalesce at the start of the sweep, a time $T$ ago, it is the probability that two carriers are identical over the window, that is, that neither of their two lineages recombined (each at rate $rd$).

## Sources

- B. F. Voight, S. Kudaravalli, X. Wen and J. K. Pritchard, *A map of recent positive selection in the human genome*, PLoS Biology 4 (2006), e72.
