# EHH after a complete sweep

Let $s > 0$, $N > 1$ and $r, d$ real. Then

$$\mathrm{EHH}^\star\Bigl(r, \tau_s\bigl(\tfrac{1}{2N}, 1 - \tfrac{1}{2N}\bigr), d\Bigr) = (2N - 1)^{-4rd/s},$$

where $\mathrm{EHH}^\star$ is the [star-genealogy EHH](sweep-ehh.md) and $\tau_s$ the [sweep duration](sweep-duration.md). The right side is a power of $2N - 1$; as a function of the genetic distance $d$ it is the exponential $e^{-(4r/s)\log(2N - 1)\, d}$, so for $r \ge 0$ and $d \ge 0$ haplotype homozygosity after a complete sweep decays exponentially in $d$.
