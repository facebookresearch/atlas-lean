# Variance of the divergence

Under the hypotheses of the [expected divergence](integral-hka-divergence.md), suppose moreover that $D$ is square integrable and that its conditional variance given $\mathcal{G}$ equals $\theta(T + \tau)$ $\mu$-almost everywhere (as for a Poisson count). Then

$$\operatorname{Var}(D) = \theta\Bigl(T + \frac{1 + f}{2}\Bigr) + \Bigl(\frac{\theta (1 + f)}{2}\Bigr)^2 = v_D(\theta, T, f)$$

(Hudson, Kreitman & Aguadé 1987).

## Sources

- R. R. Hudson, M. Kreitman and M. Aguadé, *A test of neutral molecular evolution based on nucleotide data*, Genetics 116 (1987), 153–159.
