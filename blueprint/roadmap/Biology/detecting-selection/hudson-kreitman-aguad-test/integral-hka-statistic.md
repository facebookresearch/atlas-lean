# The HKA statistic has mean 3L at the true parameters

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $L$ a natural number, $\theta_i > 0$ for every locus, $T \ge 0$, $f > 0$ and $n_A, n_B \ge 2$. Let $S^A_i, S^B_i, D_i$ be square-integrable random variables with

- $\int S^A_i \, d\mu = m_S(\theta_i, n_A)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(S^A_i) = v_S(\theta_i, n_A)$;
- $\int S^B_i \, d\mu = m_S(f\theta_i, n_B)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(S^B_i) = v_S(f\theta_i, n_B)$;
- $\int D_i \, d\mu = m_D(\theta_i, T, f)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(D_i) = v_D(\theta_i, T, f)$.

Then the [HKA statistic](hka-statistic.md) has $\int X^2 \, d\mu = 3L$.
