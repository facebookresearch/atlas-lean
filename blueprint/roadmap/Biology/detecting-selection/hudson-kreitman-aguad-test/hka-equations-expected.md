# True parameters solve the HKA estimating equations

For all $L$, $n_A, n_B$ and real parameters $\theta_1, \dots, \theta_L, T, f$, the [HKA estimating equations](hka-equations.md) hold at $S^A_i = m_S(\theta_i, n_A)$, $S^B_i = m_S(f\theta_i, n_B)$ and $D_i = m_D(\theta_i, T, f)$, the [means](hka-poly-mean.md) of the counts; for $\theta_i \ge 0$, $T \ge 0$ and $f > 0$ these are the expected counts.
