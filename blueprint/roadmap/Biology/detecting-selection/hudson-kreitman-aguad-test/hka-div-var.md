# HKA variance of the divergence

For real numbers $\theta, T, f$, put

$$v_D(\theta, T, f) = m_D(\theta, T, f) + \Bigl(\frac{\theta (1 + f)}{2}\Bigr)^2,$$

where $m_D$ is the [expected divergence](hka-div-mean.md). For $\theta \ge 0$, $T \ge 0$ and $f > 0$ it is the variance of the number of fixed differences in that model.
