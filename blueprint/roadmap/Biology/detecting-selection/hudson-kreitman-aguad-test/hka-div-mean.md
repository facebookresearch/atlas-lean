# HKA expected divergence

For real numbers $\theta$, $T$ (the split time) and $f$ (the relative size of the second species), put

$$m_D(\theta, T, f) = \theta\Bigl(T + \frac{1 + f}{2}\Bigr).$$

For $\theta \ge 0$, $T \ge 0$ and $f > 0$ it is the expected number of fixed differences between the two species in the model of Hudson, Kreitman and Aguadé.
