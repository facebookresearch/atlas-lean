# HKA expected number of segregating sites

For a real $\theta$ (a scaled mutation rate) and a natural number $n$ (the sample size), put

$$m_S(\theta, n) = \theta\, a_1(n), \qquad a_1(n) = \sum_{j=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{j},$$

For $\theta \ge 0$ and $n \ge 1$, it is the expected number of segregating sites in a sample of $n$ genes under the neutral infinite-sites model (use $f\theta$ for the second species).
