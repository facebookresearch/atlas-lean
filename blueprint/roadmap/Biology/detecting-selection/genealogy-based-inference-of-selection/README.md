# Genealogy-based inference of selection

Likelihood inference of a selection coefficient and of the allele-frequency trajectory at one biallelic site from the local genealogy at that site, treated as a structured coalescent within the derived and ancestral allelic classes, with the trajectory integrated out by a hidden Markov model and the genealogy integrated out by importance sampling of neutral ancestral recombination graphs.
