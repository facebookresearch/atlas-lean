# A bound for Tajima's D when its numerator is controlled by S

For `n ≥ 4` genes and reals `π`, `S` and `β` with `S ≥ 1` and `|π - S / a₁| ≤ β S`,
[Tajima's D](../tajima-s-d/tajima-d.md) satisfies `|D| ≤ β / √(min(e₁, e₂) / 2)`. For real data
the hypothesis holds with `β = max(n / (2 (n - 1)), 1 / a₁)`, since each segregating site
contributes between `0` and `n / (2 (n - 1))` to `π`.
