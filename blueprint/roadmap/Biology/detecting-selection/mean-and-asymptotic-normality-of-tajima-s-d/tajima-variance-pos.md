# Positivity of Tajima's variance term for at least four genes

For every number of genes `n ≥ 4` and every `θ > 0`, `c₁ θ + c₂ θ² > 0`, where `c₁` and `c₂` are
Tajima's constants (see [Tajima's D](../tajima-s-d/tajima-d.md)). Under the neutral model,
`c₁ θ + c₂ θ²` is the
[variance of Tajima's numerator](../../coalescent-theory/segregating-sites-and-pairwise-differences/variance-tajima-numerator.md)
`π - S / a₁`.
