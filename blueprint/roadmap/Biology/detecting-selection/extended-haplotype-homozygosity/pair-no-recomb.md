# Probability of no recombination on a pair's genealogy

For real numbers $\rho$ (the scaled recombination rate per site, $\rho = 4Nr$), $d$ (the distance from the core) and $t$ (the pair coalescence time, in units of $2N$ generations), put

$$q(\rho, d, t) = e^{-\rho d t}.$$

For $\rho, d, t \ge 0$ it is the probability that no recombination falls on the genealogy of a pair of lineages within distance $d$ of the core, given that they coalesce at time $t$. In a mutation-free model it is a lower bound for the probability that the two windows are identical, not that probability itself.
