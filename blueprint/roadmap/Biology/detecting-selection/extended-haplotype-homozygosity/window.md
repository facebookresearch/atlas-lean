# Haplotype window around the core

A sample of haplotypes is a family $h = (h_i)_{i \in \iota}$ of sequences $h_i : \mathbb{N} \to A$, where $h_i(k)$ is the allele of haplotype $i$ at the site $k$ steps away from the core in a fixed direction. The window of radius $d$ of haplotype $i$ is the restriction $(h_i(0), \dots, h_i(d - 1))$ of $h_i$ to the first $d$ sites.
