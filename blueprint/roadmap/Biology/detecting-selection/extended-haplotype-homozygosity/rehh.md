# Relative extended haplotype homozygosity (REHH)

For a haplotype sample with core-allele labels, finite sets $C$ (carriers of a core allele) and $D$ (carriers of the other core alleles) and a radius $d$,

$$\mathrm{REHH}(d) = \frac{\mathrm{EHH}^{\mathrm{core}}_C(d)}{\mathrm{EHH}^{\mathrm{core}}_D(d)},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$, where $\mathrm{EHH}^{\mathrm{core}}$ is the [core-labelled EHH](ehh-core.md): the statistic of the long-range haplotype test (Sabeti et al. 2002).
