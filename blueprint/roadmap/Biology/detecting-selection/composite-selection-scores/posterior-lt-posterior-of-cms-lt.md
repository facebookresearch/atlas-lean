# Posterior probability is strictly increasing in the CMS score

In every [composite-score model](composite-model.md), if two observations satisfy $\mathrm{CMS}(x) < \mathrm{CMS}(y)$, then $\mathrm{Post}(x) < \mathrm{Post}(y)$, where $\mathrm{CMS}$ is the [CMS score](cms.md) and $\mathrm{Post}$ the [normalized composite score](posterior.md): ranking sites by either gives the same order.
