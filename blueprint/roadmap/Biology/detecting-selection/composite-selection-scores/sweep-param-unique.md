# Identifiability of sweep parameters from frequency spectra

Let $f, g$ be real functions on a set $B$ with $f \ne g$ (the neutral and post-sweep spectra), and let $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, x_1, x_2$ be real numbers with $\alpha_2 > 0$ such that for every site $y$ and every $b \in B$,

$$e_{\alpha_1}(x_1, y) f(b) + \bigl(1 - e_{\alpha_1}(x_1, y)\bigr) g(b) = e_{\alpha_2}(x_2, y) f(b) + \bigl(1 - e_{\alpha_2}(x_2, y)\bigr) g(b),$$

where $e$ is the [escape probability](escape-prob.md). Then $x_1 = x_2$ and $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2$.
