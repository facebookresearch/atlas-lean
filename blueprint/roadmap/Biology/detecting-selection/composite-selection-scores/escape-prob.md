# Probability of escaping a selective sweep

For real numbers $\alpha$ (the compound strength parameter, proportional to the recombination rate per unit distance divided by the selection coefficient), $x$ (the sweep position) and $y$ (a site), the escape probability is

$$e_\alpha(x, y) = 1 - e^{-\alpha |y - x|},$$

the probability that a lineage at $y$ escapes a sweep at $x$ when $\alpha > 0$ (Kim and Stephan 2002; Nielsen et al. 2005).

## Sources

- Y. Kim and W. Stephan, *Detecting a local signature of genetic hitchhiking along a recombining chromosome*, Genetics 160 (2002), 765–777.
- R. Nielsen, S. Williamson, Y. Kim, M. J. Hubisz, A. G. Clark and C. Bustamante, *Genomic scans for selective sweeps using SNP data*, Genome Research 15 (2005), 1566–1575.
