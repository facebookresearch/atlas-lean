# Level of the Chebyshev test

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure, $n$ a natural number, $\pi, S$ real random variables and $\theta_0, \alpha$ real with $\alpha > 0$. Suppose that Tajima's numerator $\pi - S/a_1$ is square integrable, has mean $0$ and variance $c_1 \theta_0 + c_2 \theta_0^2$, and that $c_1 \theta_0 + c_2 \theta_0^2 > 0$. Then the [Chebyshev test](tajima-chebyshev-test.md) rejects with probability at most $\alpha$.
