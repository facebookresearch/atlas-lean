# Chebyshev test of neutrality

For a natural number $n$ and real numbers $\theta_0$ (the hypothesized mutation parameter), $\alpha$ (the level), $\pi$ and $S$, the Chebyshev test rejects neutrality when

$$\Bigl|\pi - \frac{S}{a_1}\Bigr| \ge \sqrt{\frac{c_1 \theta_0 + c_2 \theta_0^2}{\alpha}},$$

where $a_1, c_1, c_2$ are Tajima's constants (see [Tajima's D](tajima-d.md)), with the conventions $x/0 = 0$ and $\sqrt{x} = 0$ for $x \le 0$.
