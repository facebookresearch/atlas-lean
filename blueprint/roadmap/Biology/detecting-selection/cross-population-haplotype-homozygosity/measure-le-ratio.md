# Equal tails of the ratio of an exchangeable pair

Let $(X, Y)$ be an [exchangeable pair](is-exchangeable-pair.md) under $\mu$ with $X$ and $Y$ measurable and $X(\omega) > 0$, $Y(\omega) > 0$ for every $\omega$, and let $c > 0$. Then

$$\mu(\{c \le X / Y\}) = \mu(\{X / Y \le c^{-1}\}).$$

This is the form used for relative extended haplotype homozygosity.
