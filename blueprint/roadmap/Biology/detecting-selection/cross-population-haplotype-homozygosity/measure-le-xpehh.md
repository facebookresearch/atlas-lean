# Symmetry of the XP-EHH law under exchangeability

Let $(I_1, I_2)$ be an [exchangeable pair](is-exchangeable-pair.md) under $\mu$ with $I_1$ and $I_2$ measurable. Then for every real $t$,

$$\mu(\{t \le \mathrm{XPEHH}(I_1, I_2)\}) = \mu(\{\mathrm{XPEHH}(I_1, I_2) \le -t\}),$$

where $\mathrm{XPEHH}$ is the [XP-EHH score](xpehh.md): the two tails of the two-sided test carry the same mass.
