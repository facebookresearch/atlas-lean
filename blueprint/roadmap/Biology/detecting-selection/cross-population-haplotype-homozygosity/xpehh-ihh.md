# XP-EHH in star-genealogy sweep models

Let $r > 0$, $T_1 > 0$ and $T_2 > 0$. Then

$$\mathrm{XPEHH}\bigl(\mathrm{iHH}(r, T_1), \mathrm{iHH}(r, T_2)\bigr) = \log \frac{T_2}{T_1},$$

where $\mathrm{XPEHH}$ is the [XP-EHH score](xpehh.md) and $\mathrm{iHH}(r, T)$ the [integrated haplotype homozygosity of the star-genealogy sweep model](../selective-sweeps/ihh.md) with recombination rate $r$ and sweep age $T$.
