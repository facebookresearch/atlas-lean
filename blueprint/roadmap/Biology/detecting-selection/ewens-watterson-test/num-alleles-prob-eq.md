# Law of the number of alleles

For every real $\theta$ and natural numbers $n, k$,

$$P_\theta(K = k) = \Bigl[{n \atop k}\Bigr] \frac{\theta^k}{\theta^{(n)}},$$

where $P_\theta(K = k)$ is the [probability of $k$ alleles](num-alleles-prob.md), $[{n \atop k}]$ the unsigned Stirling number of the first kind, $\theta^{(n)}$ the rising factorial and $x/0 = 0$ (Ewens 1972). It is a probability for $\theta > 0$.

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles*, Theor. Popul. Biol. 3 (1972), 87–112.
