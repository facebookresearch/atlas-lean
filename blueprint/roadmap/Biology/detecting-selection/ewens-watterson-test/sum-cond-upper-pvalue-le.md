# Validity of the Ewens–Watterson test

Let $\theta > 0$, let $T$ be a real statistic of the partitions of $n$ and $t \ge 0$. Then

$$\sum_{\pi \,:\, p_T(\pi) \le t} P_\theta(\pi) \le t,$$

where $p_T$ is the [conditional upper-tail p-value](cond-upper-pvalue.md) and $P_\theta$ the Ewens sampling formula (Watterson 1978). With $T$ the [sample homozygosity](homozygosity.md) this is the test against excess homozygosity, with $T$ its negative the test against a deficit.

## Sources

- G. A. Watterson, *The homozygosity test of neutrality*, Genetics 88 (1978), 405–417.
