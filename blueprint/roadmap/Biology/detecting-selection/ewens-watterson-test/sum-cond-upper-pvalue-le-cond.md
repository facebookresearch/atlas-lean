# Conditional validity of the Ewens–Watterson test

Let $\theta > 0$, let $T$ be a real statistic of the partitions of $n$, let $k$ be a natural number and $t \ge 0$. Then

$$\sum_{\pi \,:\, K(\pi) = k,\ p_T(\pi) \le t} P_\theta(\pi \mid K = k) \le t,$$

where $p_T$ is the [conditional upper-tail p-value](cond-upper-pvalue.md) and $P_\theta(\cdot \mid K = k)$ the [conditional probability](ewens-cond-prob.md).
