# Sample homozygosity of an allelic partition

For a partition $\pi$ of $n$ with parts $n_1, \dots, n_K$ (the allele counts), the sample homozygosity is

$$F(\pi) = \sum_{i=1}^{K} \Bigl(\frac{n_i}{n}\Bigr)^2,$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (Watterson 1978).

## Sources

- G. A. Watterson, *The homozygosity test of neutrality*, Genetics 88 (1978), 405–417.
