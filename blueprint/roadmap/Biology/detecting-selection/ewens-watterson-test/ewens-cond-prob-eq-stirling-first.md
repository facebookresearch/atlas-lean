# The Ewens–Watterson conditional law

Let $\theta > 0$. Then for every partition $\pi$ of $n$ with $K(\pi)$ parts,

$$P_\theta(\pi \mid K = K(\pi)) = \frac{w(\pi)}{\bigl[{n \atop K(\pi)}\bigr]},$$

where the left side is the [conditional probability](ewens-cond-prob.md), $w$ the [cycle-type weight](cycle-type-weight.md) and $[{n \atop k}]$ the unsigned Stirling number of the first kind (Ewens 1972; Watterson 1978).

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles*, Theor. Popul. Biol. 3 (1972), 87–112.
- G. A. Watterson, *The homozygosity test of neutrality*, Genetics 88 (1978), 405–417.
