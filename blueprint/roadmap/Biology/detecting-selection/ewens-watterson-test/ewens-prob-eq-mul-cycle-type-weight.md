# Factorization of the Ewens sampling formula

For every real $\theta$ and every partition $\pi$ of $n$ with $K(\pi)$ parts,

$$P_\theta(\pi) = \frac{\theta^{K(\pi)}}{\theta^{(n)}}\, w(\pi),$$

where $P_\theta$ is the Ewens sampling formula, $\theta^{(n)}$ the rising factorial, $w$ the [cycle-type weight](cycle-type-weight.md), and $x/0 = 0$ (Ewens 1972).

## Sources

- W. J. Ewens, *The sampling theory of selectively neutral alleles*, Theor. Popul. Biol. 3 (1972), 87–112.
