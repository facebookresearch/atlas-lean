# DFE spectrum is homogeneous in the measure

For every $c \in [0, \infty]$, every measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$, every real $\theta$ and natural numbers $n, i$, the [spectrum under a distribution of fitness effects](dfe-sfs.md) satisfies $\mathbb{E}\xi^{c\mu}_i = \bar c\, \mathbb{E}\xi^\mu_i$, where $\bar c = c$ if $c < \infty$ and $\bar c = 0$ if $c = \infty$.
