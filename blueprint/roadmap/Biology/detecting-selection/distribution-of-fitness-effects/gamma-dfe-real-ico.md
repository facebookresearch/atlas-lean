# Fraction of mutations in a strength interval under the gamma DFE

For $a > 0$, $r > 0$ and $0 \le c_1 \le c_2$, the [gamma DFE](gamma-dfe.md) gives the interval $[-c_2, -c_1)$ the probability

$$\frac{\Gamma(a, r c_1) - \Gamma(a, r c_2)}{\Gamma(a)},$$

where $\Gamma(a, x) = \int_x^\infty t^{a-1} e^{-t} \, dt$ is the upper incomplete gamma function.
