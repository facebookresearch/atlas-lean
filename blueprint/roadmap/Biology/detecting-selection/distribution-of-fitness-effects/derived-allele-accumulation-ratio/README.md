# Derived-allele accumulation ratio

Comparing the burden of derived alleles carried by genomes from two populations, relative to an outgroup, tests whether mutations have accumulated, or been removed by selection, at different rates since the populations split. The statistic is a ratio of expected counts of derived alleles present in one population and absent in the other; under neutrality and equal mutation rates its expectation is one regardless of demographic history.
