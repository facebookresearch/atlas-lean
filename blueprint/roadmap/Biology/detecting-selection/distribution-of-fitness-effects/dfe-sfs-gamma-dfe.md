# Site-frequency spectrum under the gamma DFE

For all real $a, r, \theta$ and natural numbers $n, i$, the [spectrum](dfe-sfs.md) under the [gamma DFE](gamma-dfe.md) is

$$\mathbb{E}\xi^{\mathrm{DFE}}_i = \int \mathbb{E}\xi_i(\theta, -t) \, d\Gamma_{a, r}(t),$$

the integral against the measure $\Gamma_{a,r}$ with the gamma density of shape $a$ and rate $r$.
