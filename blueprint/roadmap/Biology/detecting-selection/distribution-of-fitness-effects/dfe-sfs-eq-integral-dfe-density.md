# Mixture identity

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $\mathbb{R}$, $\theta$ real and $1 \le i < n$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}\xi^\mu_i = \int_0^1 \binom{n}{i} x^i (1 - x)^{n - i}\, \varphi^\mu_\theta(x) \, dx,$$

where $\mathbb{E}\xi^\mu_i$ is the [spectrum under the distribution of fitness effects](dfe-sfs.md) and $\varphi^\mu_\theta$ the [averaged frequency density](dfe-density.md): averaging the spectra equals sampling from the averaged density, which is the expected spectrum under the distribution of fitness effects when $\mu$ is a probability measure and $\theta \ge 0$.
