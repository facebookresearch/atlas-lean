# Smith–Eyre-Walker estimator of adaptive substitutions

For real counts $D_n, D_s$ (nonsynonymous and synonymous fixed differences) and $P_n, P_s$ (nonsynonymous and synonymous polymorphisms), the estimator of the proportion of adaptive nonsynonymous substitutions is

$$\hat\alpha = 1 - \frac{D_s P_n}{D_n P_s},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$.

## Sources

- N. G. C. Smith and A. Eyre-Walker, *Adaptive protein evolution in Drosophila*, Nature 415 (2002), 1022–1024.
