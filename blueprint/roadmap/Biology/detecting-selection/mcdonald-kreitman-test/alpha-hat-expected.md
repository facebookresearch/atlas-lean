# The estimator at the expected counts is the adaptive proportion

In every [expected-count model](mkmodel.md),

$$1 - \frac{\mathbb{E}D_s\, \mathbb{E}P_n}{\mathbb{E}D_n\, \mathbb{E}P_s} = \alpha,$$

that is, the [estimator](alpha-hat.md) evaluated at the expected counts equals the [proportion of adaptive substitutions](alpha.md) (Smith & Eyre-Walker 2002).

## Sources

- N. G. C. Smith and A. Eyre-Walker, *Adaptive protein evolution in Drosophila*, Nature 415 (2002), 1022–1024.
