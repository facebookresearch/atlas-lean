# Expected-count model of the McDonald–Kreitman table

An expected-count model of the McDonald–Kreitman table consists of real numbers

- $L_s > 0$ and $L_n > 0$ (synonymous and nonsynonymous mutational targets);
- $f_0$ with $0 < f_0 \le 1$ (the neutral fraction of nonsynonymous mutations);
- $\theta_P > 0$ and $\lambda_D > 0$ (expected polymorphisms and neutral fixed differences per unit of neutral target);
- $A \ge 0$ (the expected number of adaptive nonsynonymous substitutions).

Synonymous mutations are neutral; non-neutral nonsynonymous mutations are either strongly deleterious (never observed) or advantageous (contributing substitutions but no polymorphism). The model has no slightly deleterious class.
