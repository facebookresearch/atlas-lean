# Microtubule length regulation

Deterministic and stochastic models of dynamic instability of microtubules in cells, in which ends switch between growth and shrinkage. A finite tubulin pool and a catastrophe rate that increases with length are minimal ingredients that regulate the mean microtubule length to a unique steady value.
