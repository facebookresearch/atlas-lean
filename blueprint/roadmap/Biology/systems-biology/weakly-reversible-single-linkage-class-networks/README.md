# Weakly reversible single-linkage-class networks

Weakly reversible reaction networks are conjectured to have bounded, persistent deterministic dynamics and positive recurrent stochastic dynamics. For networks with a single linkage class, a partition of complexes into tiers along a trajectory diverging to the boundary or infinity produces a conservation law that forbids the divergence, giving boundedness for deterministic mass action, and the same tier argument applied to embedded jump chains gives positive recurrence for binary stochastic networks.
