# Total weight of spanning in-trees

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$. The total weight of the spanning in-trees rooted at $r \in \iota$ is

$$w(r) = \sum_{t} \prod_{i \ne r} Q_{i\, t(i)},$$

the sum over the [spanning in-trees](is-in-tree.md) $t$ rooted at $r$.
