# Concentration summation theorem

Let $M$ (metabolites) and $R$ (reactions) be index sets with $R$ finite, and let $\sigma$ be a [regular steady state](is-regular-steady-state.md) at enzyme levels $e_0$ for a stoichiometric matrix $N$ and rate laws $f$. For every metabolite $m$ with $\sigma_m(e_0) > 0$, the
[concentration control coefficients](conc-control.md) satisfy $\sum_{i \in R} C^{s_m}_{e_i} = 0$ at
$e_0$ (Heinrich & Rapoport 1974).
