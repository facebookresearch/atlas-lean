# Concavity of the flux balance optimum in the right-hand side

Let $S \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ be a stoichiometric matrix, $l, u, c \in \mathbb{R}^n$ flux bounds and objective, and let $b_1, b_2 \in \mathbb{R}^m$ have nonempty [feasible sets](fba-feasible.md).
For every $t \in [0, 1]$, the [optimal value](fba-value.md) satisfies

$$t\, V(b_1) + (1 - t)\, V(b_2) \le V\bigl(t b_1 + (1 - t) b_2\bigr).$$
