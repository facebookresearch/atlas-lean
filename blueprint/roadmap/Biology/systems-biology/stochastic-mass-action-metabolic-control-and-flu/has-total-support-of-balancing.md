# Necessity of total support

Let $n \ge 1$, $A$ a real $n \times n$ matrix and $d \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^n$. If
$\operatorname{diag}(d)\, A \operatorname{diag}(d)$ is doubly stochastic (nonnegative entries, all row and column sums $1$), then $A$ has
[total support](has-total-support.md) (Sinkhorn & Knopp 1967; Csima & Datta 1972).
