# Markov chain tree theorem

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{\iota \times \iota}$ with zero row sums. Then the [in-tree weights](tree-weight.md) $w$ form an invariant row vector: $w Q = 0$ (King &
Altman 1956; Hill 1966). The argument of Anantharam and Tsoucas (1989) matches both sides with the
maps $g \colon \iota \to \iota$ whose functional graph has its cycle through a given state.
