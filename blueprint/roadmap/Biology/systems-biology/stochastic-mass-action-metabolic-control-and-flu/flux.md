# Reaction fluxes proportional to enzyme levels

Let $M$ (metabolites) and $R$ (reactions) be index sets with $R$ finite, and let $f_i \colon \mathbb{R}^M \to \mathbb{R}$, $i \in R$, be rate laws. For enzyme levels
$e \in \mathbb{R}^R$ and concentrations $s \in \mathbb{R}^M$, the reaction fluxes are
$v_i(e, s) = e_i f_i(s)$.
