# Symmetric matrix balancing

Let $A$ be a symmetric $n \times n$ real matrix with positive entries. There is exactly one vector
$d \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^n$ such that $\operatorname{diag}(d)\, A \operatorname{diag}(d)$ is doubly stochastic (nonnegative entries, all row and column sums $1$)
(Sinkhorn 1964; the "equal visibility" normalization of Imakaev et al. 2012).
