# Flux summation theorem

Let $M$ (metabolites) and $R$ (reactions) be index sets with $R$ finite, and let $\sigma$ be a [regular steady state](is-regular-steady-state.md) at enzyme levels $e_0$ for a stoichiometric matrix $N$ and rate laws $f$. For every reaction $j$ with $J_j(e_0) \ne 0$, the
[flux control coefficients](flux-control.md) satisfy $\sum_{i \in R} C^{J_j}_{e_i} = 1$ at $e_0$
(Kacser & Burns 1973; Heinrich & Rapoport 1974).
