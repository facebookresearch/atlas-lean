# Total support of a square matrix

A real $n \times n$ matrix $A$ has total support when it is nonzero and every positive entry lies on a
positive diagonal: for every $A_{ij} > 0$ there is a permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1, \dots, n\}$ with
$\sigma(i) = j$ and $A_{k \sigma(k)} > 0$ for every $k$ (Sinkhorn & Knopp 1967; Csima & Datta 1972).
