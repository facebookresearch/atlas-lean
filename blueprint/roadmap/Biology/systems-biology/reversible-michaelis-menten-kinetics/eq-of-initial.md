# Forward uniqueness of reversible Michaelis–Menten solutions

For all real rate constants, two [solutions](is-solution.md) of the reversible Michaelis–Menten
system with the same values at $t = 0$ agree at every $t \ge 0$. The case $k_{-2} = 0$ is the
[forward uniqueness of Michaelis–Menten solutions](../michaelis-menten-kinetics-and-the-quasi-steady-s/global-positive-trajectories-of-the-michaelis-me/eq-of-initial.md).
