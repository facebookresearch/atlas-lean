# A reversible solution started at an equilibrium stays there

For all real rate constants, let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the reversible
Michaelis–Menten system whose initial state $(s(0), c(0), e(0), p(0))$ is a zero of the
[reversible field](rev-field.md). Then $s(t) = s(0)$, $c(t) = c(0)$, $e(t) = e(0)$ and $p(t) = p(0)$
for every $t \ge 0$.
