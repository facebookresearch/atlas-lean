# Equilibria of the reversible field are the detailed-balanced states

For all real rate constants and every state $x = (s, c, e, p) \in \mathbb{R}^4$, the
[reversible field](rev-field.md) vanishes at $x$ if and only if both reversible steps are balanced:

$$k_1 e s = k_{-1} c \qquad \text{and} \qquad k_2 c = k_{-2}\, e p.$$
