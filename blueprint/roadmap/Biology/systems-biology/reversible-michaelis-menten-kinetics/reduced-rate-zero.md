# With k₋₂ = 0 the reduced rate is the Michaelis–Menten rate law

Let $k_1 > 0$ and let $k_{-1}, k_2, M, x$ be real. With $k_{-2} = 0$, the
[reduced rate](reduced-rate.md) $v(x, M - x)$ is the
[Michaelis–Menten rate law](../michaelis-menten-kinetics-and-the-quasi-steady-s/mm-rate.md)
$k_2 x/(K_M + x)$ with $K_M = (k_{-1} + k_2)/k_1$, whatever the total mass $M$ is.
