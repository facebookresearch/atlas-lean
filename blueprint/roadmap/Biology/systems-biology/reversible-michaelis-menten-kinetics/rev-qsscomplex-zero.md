# With k₋₂ = 0 the reversible QSS complex is the irreversible one

Let $k_1 > 0$ and let $k_{-1}, k_2, e_T, s, p$ be real. With $k_{-2} = 0$, the
[reversible quasi-steady-state complex](rev-qsscomplex.md) is the
[irreversible one](../michaelis-menten-kinetics-and-the-quasi-steady-s/qss-complex.md),
$q(s, p) = e_T s/(K_M + s)$ with $K_M = (k_{-1} + k_2)/k_1$, whatever $p$ is.
