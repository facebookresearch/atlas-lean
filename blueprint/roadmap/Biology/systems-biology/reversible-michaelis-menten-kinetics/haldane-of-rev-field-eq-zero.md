# Haldane relation at equilibria with nonzero free enzyme

For all real rate constants, every zero $(s, c, e, p)$ of the [reversible field](rev-field.md) with
free enzyme $e \ne 0$ satisfies the Haldane relation

$$k_1 k_2\, s = k_{-1} k_{-2}\, p.$$

The condition $e \ne 0$ cannot be dropped: every state $(s, 0, 0, p)$ is a zero of the field,
whether or not $k_1 k_2 s = k_{-1} k_{-2} p$.
