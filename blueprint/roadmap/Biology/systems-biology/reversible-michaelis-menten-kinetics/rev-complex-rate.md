# Complex rate with a reversible product step

For real rate constants $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$ and concentrations $s, c, e, p$ of substrate,
complex, free enzyme and product, the mass-action rate of the complex is

$$k_1 e s - (k_{-1} + k_2)\, c + k_{-2}\, e p,$$

the [irreversible complex rate](../michaelis-menten-kinetics-and-the-quasi-steady-s/complex-rate.md)
plus the rebinding term $k_{-2} e p$. Here $k_1$ is the binding, $k_{-1}$ the unbinding of $S$,
$k_2$ the catalysis and $k_{-2}$ the rebinding of $P$ in the mechanism
$E + S \rightleftharpoons C \rightleftharpoons E + P$.
