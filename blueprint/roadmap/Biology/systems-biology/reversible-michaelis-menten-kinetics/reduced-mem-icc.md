# The reduced reversible equation keeps solutions between 0 and M

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_{-1}, k_{-2} \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$ and $M \ge 0$, and let
$\bar S : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable at every $\tau \ge 0$ with
$\bar S'(\tau) = -v(\bar S(\tau), M - \bar S(\tau))$ there, for the [reduced rate](reduced-rate.md).
If $0 \le \bar S(0) \le M$, then $0 \le \bar S(\tau) \le M$ for every $\tau \ge 0$.
