# Rate constants of the reversible mechanism as a network

For real $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$, the rate constants of the
[reversible mechanism as a network](mm-network.md) are $k_1$ for $E + S \to C$ (binding), $k_{-1}$
for $C \to E + S$ (unbinding of $S$), $k_2$ for $C \to E + P$ (catalysis) and $k_{-2}$ for
$E + P \to C$ (rebinding of $P$).
