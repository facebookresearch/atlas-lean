# Equilibria of the reversible field are complex balanced

For all real rate constants, every zero $x$ of the [reversible field](rev-field.md), a
[detailed-balanced state](rev-field-eq-zero-iff.md), is
[complex balanced](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/is-complex-balanced.md) for
the [reversible mechanism](mm-network.md) with [its rate constants](mm-rates.md).
