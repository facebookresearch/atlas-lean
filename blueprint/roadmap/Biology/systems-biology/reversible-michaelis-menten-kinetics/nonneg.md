# For nonnegative rates and data, reversible solutions stay nonnegative

Let $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2} \ge 0$ and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the
reversible Michaelis–Menten system with $s(0), c(0), e(0), p(0) \ge 0$. Then
$s(t), c(t), e(t), p(t) \ge 0$ for every $t \ge 0$: the nonnegative orthant is invariant.
