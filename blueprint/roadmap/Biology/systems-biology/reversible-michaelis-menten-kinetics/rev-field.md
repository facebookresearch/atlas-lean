# Reversible Michaelis–Menten field on four coordinates

For real rate constants $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$, the reversible Michaelis–Menten field on
$\mathbb{R}^4$, with coordinates $x = (s, c, e, p)$, is

$$F(s, c, e, p) = \bigl(-k_1 e s + k_{-1} c,\ r,\ -r,\ k_2 c - k_{-2}\, e p\bigr),$$

where $r = k_1 e s - (k_{-1} + k_2)\, c + k_{-2}\, e p$ is the [complex rate](rev-complex-rate.md).
