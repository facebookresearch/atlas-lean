# For positive rates and totals all concentrations are positive at t > 0

Let $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2} > 0$ and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the
reversible Michaelis–Menten system with $s(0), c(0), e(0), p(0) \ge 0$, total enzyme
$E_T = e(0) + c(0) > 0$ and total substrate $S_T = s(0) + c(0) + p(0) > 0$. Then
$s(t), c(t), e(t), p(t) > 0$ for every $t > 0$, even when some of them start at $0$.
