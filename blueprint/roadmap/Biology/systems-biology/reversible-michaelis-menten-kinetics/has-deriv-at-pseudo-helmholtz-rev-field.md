# Orbital derivative of the pseudo-Helmholtz function at positive states

For all real rate constants and every $c^* \in \mathbb{R}^4$, let
$\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^4$ have the derivative $\varphi'(t) = F(\varphi(t))$ at a time
$t$, for the [reversible field](rev-field.md) $F$, with all coordinates of $\varphi(t)$ positive.
Then $\tau \mapsto G(\varphi(\tau))$, for the
[pseudo-Helmholtz function](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/pseudo-helmholtz.md)
$G$ relative to $c^*$, has at $t$ the derivative

$$\sum_i \bigl(\log \varphi_i(t) - \log c^*_i\bigr)\, F_i(\varphi(t)),$$

the
[rate of change of the pseudo-Helmholtz function](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/helmholtz-rate.md)
for the [reversible mechanism](mm-network.md), with $\log 0 = 0$ and $\log x = \log|x|$ for $x < 0$.
This is the case of the reversible mechanism of the
[pseudo-Helmholtz function along positive mass-action solutions](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/has-deriv-at-pseudo-helmholtz.md).
