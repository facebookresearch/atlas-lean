# Horn–Jackson inequality for the reversible Michaelis–Menten mechanism

Let $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2} > 0$, let $c^*$ be a zero of the [reversible field](rev-field.md) $F$
with positive coordinates, and let $x \in \mathbb{R}^4$ have positive coordinates. Then the
[rate of change of the pseudo-Helmholtz function](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/helmholtz-rate.md)
relative to $c^*$ satisfies

$$\sum_i (\log x_i - \log c^*_i)\, F_i(x) \le 0,$$

with equality if and only if $F(x) = 0$. This is the
[Horn–Jackson inequality](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/helmholtz-rate-nonpos.md)
and its
[equality case](../complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/helmholtz-rate-eq-zero-iff.md)
for the [reversible mechanism](mm-network.md), whose equilibria are
[complex balanced](is-complex-balanced-mm-network.md).
