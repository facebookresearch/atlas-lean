# With k₋₂ = 0 the reversible field is the irreversible field

For all real $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2$, the [reversible field](rev-field.md) with $k_{-2} = 0$ is the
[irreversible mass-action field](../michaelis-menten-kinetics-and-the-quasi-steady-s/global-positive-trajectories-of-the-michaelis-me/mm-field.md)
with $k_b = k_{-1}$.
