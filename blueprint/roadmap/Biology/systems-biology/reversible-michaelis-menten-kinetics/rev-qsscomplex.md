# Quasi-steady-state complex of the reversible mechanism

For real $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$, total enzyme $e_T$ and concentrations $s, p$, the
quasi-steady-state complex of the reversible mechanism is

$$q(s, p) = \frac{e_T (k_1 s + k_{-2} p)}{k_1 s + k_{-2} p + k_{-1} + k_2},$$

with the quotient read as $0$ when the denominator is $0$. In the model $k_1, k_2 > 0$,
$k_{-1}, k_{-2} \ge 0$ and $s, p \ge 0$, where the denominator is at least $k_{-1} + k_2 > 0$. When
the denominator is nonzero, it is the value of $c$ at which the [complex rate](rev-complex-rate.md)
with free enzyme $e = e_T - c$ vanishes.
