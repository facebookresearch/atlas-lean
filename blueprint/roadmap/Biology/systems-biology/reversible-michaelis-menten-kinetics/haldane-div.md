# Haldane equilibrium constant p/s at equilibria with e, s, k₋₁, k₋₂ ≠ 0

Let $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$ be real with $k_{-1} \ne 0$ and $k_{-2} \ne 0$. At every zero
$(s, c, e, p)$ of the [reversible field](rev-field.md) with $e \ne 0$ and $s \ne 0$, the
product-to-substrate ratio is the equilibrium constant

$$\frac{p}{s} = K_{eq} = \frac{k_1 k_2}{k_{-1} k_{-2}}.$$
