# Rate of the reduced reversible equation

For real $k_1, k_{-1}, k_2, k_{-2}$, total mass $M$ and $x$, the rate of the reduced equation is
$v(x, M - x)$, the [reversible rate law](rev-mmrate.md) at substrate $x$ and product $M - x$. The
reduced equation in slow time is $\bar S' = -v(\bar S, M - \bar S)$.
