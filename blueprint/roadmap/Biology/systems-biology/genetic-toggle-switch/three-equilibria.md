# Three equilibria: the outer two linearly stable, the middle a saddle

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, if the toggle switch with Hill exponents `β = 1`, `γ = 2` and rates
`a = α₁`, `b = α₂` has exactly three [equilibria](is-equilibrium.md), they are
`(u_k, b / (1 + u_k²))` for `k = 1, 2, 3` with `u₁ < u₂ < u₃` (so their second coordinates
decrease), the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md)
at the first and the third, and it has [saddle spectrum](has-saddle-spectrum.md) at the second.
