# For β = 1 and γ ≥ 1, at most γ + 1 equilibria

For `a > 0` and `b > 0` and `γ ≥ 1`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle switch
with Hill exponents `β = 1` and `γ`, and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂`, has at most `γ + 1` elements.
Here the number of elements of a set is its cardinality when it is finite and `0` when it is
infinite. The set is finite: it has [at least one element](one-le-ncard-equilibria-one.md) in this
count.
