# Two distinct roots of the cubic give its factorization

For reals `a, b` and distinct roots `r ≠ s` of the [equilibrium cubic](cubic.md)
`c(u) = u³ − a u² + (1 + b) u − a`, for every real `u`, `c(u) = (u − r)(u − s)(u − t)` with
`t = a − r − s`, and `c'(u) = (u − r)(u − s) + (u − s)(u − t) + (u − t)(u − r)`.
