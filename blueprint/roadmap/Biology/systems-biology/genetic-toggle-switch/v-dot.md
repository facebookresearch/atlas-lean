# Right-hand side of the v equation

For a real `α₂`, `γ ∈ ℕ` and reals `u, v`, `v' = α₂ / (1 + u^γ) − v`, the [Hill repression](hill.md)
of `v` by `u` minus first-order degradation, with the convention `x / 0 = 0` of the Hill term. In
the model the synthesis rates `α₁, α₂` are positive, the Hill exponents `β, γ` are at least `1`, and
the concentrations `u, v` are nonnegative.
