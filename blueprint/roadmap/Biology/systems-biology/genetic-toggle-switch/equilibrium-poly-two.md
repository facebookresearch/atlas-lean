# Equilibrium quintic for Hill exponents β = γ = 2

For reals `a, b`, the polynomial `X⁵ − a X⁴ + 2 X³ − 2a X² + (1 + b²) X − a` with real coefficients.
For `a, b > 0`, the [equilibria](is-equilibrium.md) of the toggle switch with Hill exponents
`β = γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂` correspond to its [real roots](is-equilibrium-two-iff.md),
which are [positive](pos-of-is-root-equilibrium-poly-two.md).
