# For β = 1 and positive rates, at least one equilibrium

For `a > 0` and `b > 0` and every `γ ∈ ℕ`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle
switch with Hill exponents `β = 1` and `γ`, and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂`, is finite and nonempty:
its number of elements is at least `1`, an infinite set being counted as `0`.
