# For β = γ = 2 and positive rates, at least one equilibrium

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle switch with Hill
exponents `β = γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂` is finite and nonempty: its number of elements is
at least `1`, an infinite set being counted as `0`.
