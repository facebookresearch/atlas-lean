# Equilibrium polynomial for Hill exponent β = 1

For reals `a, b` and `γ ∈ ℕ`, the polynomial `X^{γ+1} − a X^γ + (1 + b) X − a` with real
coefficients. For `a, b > 0`, the [equilibria](is-equilibrium.md) of the toggle switch with Hill
exponent `β = 1`, any `γ ∈ ℕ`, and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂` correspond to its
[positive roots](is-equilibrium-one-iff.md). For odd `γ` it can have negative roots, which are not
equilibria.
