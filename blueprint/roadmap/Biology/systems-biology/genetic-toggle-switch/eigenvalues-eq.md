# For nonnegative rates and states, the eigenvalues are −1 ± √P

For `α₁, α₂ ≥ 0`, `β, γ ∈ ℕ` and `u, v ≥ 0`, the set of [eigenvalues](eigenvalues.md) of the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) at `(u, v)` is `{−1 + √P, −1 − √P}`, where `P ≥ 0` is the
[loop gain](loop-gain.md); it is `{−1}` when `P = 0`.
