# Saddle spectrum of the toggle-switch Jacobian

For reals `α₁, α₂`, `β, γ ∈ ℕ` and reals `u, v`, the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) at `(u, v)` has
saddle spectrum if it has an eigenvalue with positive real part and an eigenvalue with negative real
part. At an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md) this makes the equilibrium a saddle; the predicate does
not require `(u, v)` to be an equilibrium.
