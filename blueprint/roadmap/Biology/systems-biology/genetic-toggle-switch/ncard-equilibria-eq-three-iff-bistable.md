# Exactly three equilibria iff two distinct linearly stable ones exist

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, the toggle switch with Hill exponents `β = 1`, `γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`,
`b = α₂` has exactly three [equilibria](is-equilibrium.md) if and only if it has two equilibria
`(u₁, v₁)` and `(u₂, v₂)` with `u₁ ≠ u₂` at which the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md)
(bistability).
