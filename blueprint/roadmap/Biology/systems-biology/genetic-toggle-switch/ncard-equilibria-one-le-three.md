# For β = 1, at most three equilibria

For `a > 0` and `b > 0` and every `γ ∈ ℕ`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle
switch with Hill exponents `β = 1` and `γ`, and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂`, has at most three
elements, by Descartes' rule of signs: the [equilibrium polynomial](equilibrium-poly-one.md)
`X^{γ+1} − a X^γ + (1 + b) X − a` has at most four nonzero coefficients, hence at most three sign
changes. Here the number of elements of a set is its cardinality when it is finite and `0` when it
is infinite. The set is finite: it has [at least one element](one-le-ncard-equilibria-one.md) in
this count.
