# For β = γ = 2, at most five equilibria

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle switch with Hill
exponents `β = γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂` has at most five elements, the degree of the
[equilibrium quintic](equilibrium-poly-two.md) `X⁵ − a X⁴ + 2 X³ − 2a X² + (1 + b²) X − a`. Here the
number of elements of a set is its cardinality when it is finite and `0` when it is infinite. The
set is finite: it has [at least one element](one-le-ncard-equilibria-two.md) in this count.
