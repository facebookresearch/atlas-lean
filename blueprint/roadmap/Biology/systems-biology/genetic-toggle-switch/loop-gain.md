# Loop gain of the toggle switch

For reals `α₁, α₂`, `β, γ ∈ ℕ` and reals `u, v`, `P = h₁'(v) h₂'(u)`, the product of the two
off-diagonal entries of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md), with `h₁'`, `h₂'` the
[derivative of the Hill repression](hill-deriv.md) functions. Written out,
`P = α₁ α₂ β γ v^{β−1} u^{γ−1} / ((1 + v^β)² (1 + u^γ)²)`, with the conventions of the two factors.
