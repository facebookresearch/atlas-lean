# For a, b > 0 the equilibria are the image of the roots of the cubic

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, the [set of equilibria](equilibria.md) of the toggle switch with Hill
exponents `β = 1`, `γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`, `b = α₂` is the image of the set of real roots of the
[equilibrium cubic](cubic.md) `c(u) = u³ − a u² + (1 + b) u − a` under `u ↦ (u, b / (1 + u²))`.
