# Exactly two equilibria at a tangency of the nullclines off the cusp

For `a > 0` and `b > 0`, the toggle switch with Hill exponents `β = 1`, `γ = 2` and rates `a = α₁`,
`b = α₂` has exactly two [equilibria](is-equilibrium.md) if and only if it has an equilibrium
`(u, v)` with [tangent nullclines](nullclines-tangent.md) and `3u ≠ a`. At an equilibrium, tangency
means a zero eigenvalue of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) and a double root `u` of the
[equilibrium cubic](cubic.md); `3u = a` would make it a triple root.
