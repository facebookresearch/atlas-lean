# Equilibrium cubic for Hill exponents 1 and 2

For reals `a, b` and `u`, `c(u) = u³ − a u² + (1 + b) u − a`. For `a, b > 0`, the
[equilibria](is-equilibrium.md) of the toggle switch with Hill exponents `β = 1`, `γ = 2` and rates
`a = α₁`, `b = α₂` are the points `(u, b / (1 + u²))` with `c(u) = 0`
([correspondence](is-equilibrium-iff.md)).
