# Nascent RNA fluctuations in segmented elongation models

A gene of length $L$ segments carries RNA polymerases that initiate from a telegraph (on/off) promoter, hop forward segment by segment, may detach prematurely, and on reaching the end release a mature RNA that later degrades. Because every reaction is first order, means and covariances of polymerase occupancy (nascent RNA) and mature RNA are given exactly by linear and Lyapunov equations.
