# Potential-landscape models of cell differentiation

Cell states modelled as a drift-diffusion process driven by the gradient of a potential (a quantitative Waddington landscape), with the potential learned from cross-sectional single-cell snapshots by minimizing Wasserstein distances between observed and simulated populations.
