# Differential geometry of single-cell vector fields

A continuous vector field $f$ learned from single-cell expression states and RNA velocities turns sparse velocity samples into a dynamical system $\dot x = f(x)$ on expression space. Its Jacobian, divergence, curl, acceleration and curvature are then computable pointwise and quantify regulation, sources and sinks, and the bending of differentiation trajectories.
