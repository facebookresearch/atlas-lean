# Identifiability of linear compartmental models

A linear compartmental model is a directed graph of compartments with flow rates on edges, leaks to the environment, and designated input and output compartments. Its parameters are generically locally identifiable when the input–output data determine them up to finitely many alternatives. For trees with bidirected edges and for directed cycles, identifiability is decided by the numbers and positions of leaks, inputs and outputs.
