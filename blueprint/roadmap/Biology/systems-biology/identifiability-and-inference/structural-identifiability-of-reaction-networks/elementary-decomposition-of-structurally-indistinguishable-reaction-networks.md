# Elementary decomposition of structurally indistinguishable reaction networks

**Setting.** Two reaction sets $\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{G}'$ on the same species are *structurally indistinguishable* under deterministic mass-action kinetics if there exist positive rate constants for each of them under which the two induce the same ODE (the empty set induces the zero vector field). This is an existence notion: for instance $\{X\to\varnothing,\ X\to2X\}$ is indistinguishable from $\varnothing$ (take equal rate constants), although unequal rate constants give a vector field the empty set cannot produce. For a complex $y$, let $\mathrm{Cone}(y;\mathcal{G}) = \{\sum_{r: y_r^-=y}u_r m_r : u_r>0\}$ be the open cone generated by the reaction vectors $m_r$ of reactions with reactant complex $y$ (the zero vector if there are none). A pair $(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{G}')$ of structurally indistinguishable reaction sets is *elementary* if they are not both empty and no pair of subsets $(\mathcal{G}^{*},\mathcal{G}'^{*})\ne(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{G}')$, not both empty, is itself structurally indistinguishable. For example $(\{X\to\varnothing,\ X\to2X\},\ \varnothing)$ is elementary.

**Theorem.**

1. (*Cone criterion.*) $\mathcal{G}$ and $\mathcal{G}'$ are structurally indistinguishable if and only if $\mathrm{Cone}(y;\mathcal{G})\cap\mathrm{Cone}(y;\mathcal{G}')\ne\varnothing$ for every complex $y$.
2. (*Decomposition.*) Every pair of structurally indistinguishable reaction sets is a componentwise union of finitely many elementary pairs: $(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{G}') = \big(\bigcup_i\mathcal{G}_i, \bigcup_i\mathcal{G}'_i\big)$ with each $(\mathcal{G}_i,\mathcal{G}'_i)$ elementary.

## Expansion

Mass-action vector fields are sums over reactant complexes $y$ of $x^y$ times a nonnegative combination of reaction vectors, and the monomials $x^y$ are linearly independent, so equality of vector fields reduces to equality, complex by complex, of points in the two cones. For the decomposition, the condition at each complex says that a vector $(u, -u')$ lies in the kernel of the matrix of reaction vectors with signs; by Rockafellar's conformal decomposition, every such vector is a sum of elementary (minimal-support) vectors conforming to it, and their supports give the elementary pairs. Elementary pairs can be enumerated and used to add indistinguishable alternatives to a library of candidate networks when quantifying structural uncertainty in network inference.
