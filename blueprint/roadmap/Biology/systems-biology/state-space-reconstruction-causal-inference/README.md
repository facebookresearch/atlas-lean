# State-space reconstruction causal inference

Model-free causal discovery from observational biological time series includes Granger causality (does the history of $X$ improve prediction of $Y$?) and state-space reconstruction methods such as convergent cross mapping (is there a continuous map from delay vectors of the putative effect to the putative cause?). The latter rests on Takens' delay-embedding theorem for deterministic dynamics and can fail under noise, shared periodic drivers, synchrony, or symmetry.
