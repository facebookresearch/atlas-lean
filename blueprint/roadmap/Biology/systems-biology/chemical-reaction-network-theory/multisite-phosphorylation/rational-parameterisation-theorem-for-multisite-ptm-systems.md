# Rational parameterisation of multisite modification steady states

For a multisite post-translational modification system of the class considered by Thomson and Gunawardena (2009) — a substrate with several modification sites, $L$ enzymes acting by mechanisms built from binding, unbinding and catalysis, with mass-action kinetics and positive rate constants, satisfying the source's two strong-connectivity conditions (Condition 1: for each enzyme sub-network $T$, the labelled graph $G_T$ on its enzyme–substrate complexes and free enzyme is strongly connected; Condition 2: every connected component of the labelled graph $G_S$ on the modforms is strongly connected) — the steady-state concentrations of the modforms (substrate modification states), taken up to scale, have a rational parameterisation: there are rational functions of the $L$ free enzyme concentrations that map onto the projectivized steady-state variety of modform concentrations, well defined away from the locus where the source's normalizing quantity vanishes (their Theorem 4).

## Expansion

1. The model: modforms, enzyme mechanisms in the source's class, mass-action steady states and the projectivized variety of modform concentrations.
2. The linear framework: steady states of each enzyme's action as a Laplacian on a labelled graph, solved by the matrix-tree theorem (see the [Markov chain tree theorem](../../stochastic-mass-action-metabolic-control-and-flu/tree-weight-vec-mul-eq-zero.md)).
3. The rational parameterisation and its domain of definition.
4. Consequences for multistability (the number of steady states can grow with the number of sites), as separate statements.
