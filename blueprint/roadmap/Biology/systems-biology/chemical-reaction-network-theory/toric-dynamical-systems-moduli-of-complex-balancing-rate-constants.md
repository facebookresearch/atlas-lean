# Toric dynamical systems: the moduli of complex-balancing rate constants

A mass-action system with reaction graph $G$ on $n$ complexes and complex matrix $Y$ is toric (complex balanced) for a choice of rate constants $\kappa$ when it has a positive complex-balanced equilibrium. Craciun, Dickenstein, Shiu and Sturmfels (2009) define the moduli ideal $M_G$ of rate constants for which the system is toric, in coordinates $K = (K_1, \dots, K_n)$ given by the maximal nonzero minors of the Laplacian of $G$ — positive polynomials in the $\kappa_{ij}$ given by the matrix-tree theorem — and prove (Theorem 9) that $M_G$ equals the toric ideal of the Cayley matrix $\mathrm{Cay}_G(Y)$: it is generated by the binomials $K^u - K^v$ with $\mathrm{Cay}_G(Y)(u - v) = 0$, and its codimension equals the [deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) $\delta$.
