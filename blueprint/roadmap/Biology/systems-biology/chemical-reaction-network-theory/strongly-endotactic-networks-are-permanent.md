# Strongly endotactic networks are permanent

Gopalkrishnan, Miller and Shiu (2014) prove that every strongly endotactic reaction network, with mass-action kinetics and rate constants allowed to vary in a compact subset of $(0, \infty)$, is permanent (Theorem 1.1). Since permanence implies the global attractor conjecture for complex-balanced systems, the conjecture holds for strongly endotactic complex-balanced systems (Theorem 1.2): each positive [complex-balanced](../../../Biology/systems-biology/complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/is-complex-balanced.md) equilibrium attracts every positive trajectory in its compatibility class. Weakly reversible networks with a single linkage class are strongly endotactic (their Corollary 3.20), so every weakly reversible network with exactly one linkage class is permanent (their Theorem 1.3), and the conjecture holds for such complex-balanced systems by Theorem 1.2.

## Expansion

1. Definitions: endotactic and strongly endotactic networks, and permanence for systems with varying rate constants.
2. The tropical (projective) argument of the source showing that trajectories cannot approach the boundary or infinity.
3. Theorem 1.1, and the implication from permanence to global attraction for complex-balanced systems using the [pseudo-Helmholtz Lyapunov function](../../../Biology/systems-biology/complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/has-deriv-at-pseudo-helmholtz.md).
4. Theorems 1.2 and 1.3; Anderson's 2011 proof for single linkage classes as an alternative route.
5. The relation with the [extended permanence conjecture](../../../Biology/systems-biology/extended-permanence-conjecture.md).
