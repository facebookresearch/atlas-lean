# Global attractor conjecture for strongly endotactic complex-balanced systems

Gopalkrishnan, Miller and Shiu (2014) prove that every strongly endotactic reaction network, with mass-action kinetics and rate constants allowed to vary in a compact subset of $(0, \infty)$, is permanent (Theorem 1.1); this is stated in [strongly endotactic networks are permanent](../../not-yet-classified/strongly-endotactic-permanence/is-permanent-of-is-strongly-endotactic.md), together with its consequence for [weakly reversible networks with one linkage class](../../not-yet-classified/strongly-endotactic-permanence/is-permanent-of-is-weakly-reversible-of.md) (Theorem 1.3). The statement here is what remains. Since permanence implies the global attractor conjecture for complex-balanced systems, the conjecture holds for strongly endotactic complex-balanced systems (Theorem 1.2): each positive [complex-balanced](../../../Biology/systems-biology/complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/is-complex-balanced.md) equilibrium attracts every positive trajectory in its compatibility class. Weakly reversible networks with a single linkage class are strongly endotactic (their Corollary 3.20), so the conjecture holds for complex-balanced systems on weakly reversible networks with exactly one linkage class by Theorem 1.2.

1. Definitions: endotactic and strongly endotactic networks, and the global attractor property for complex-balanced systems.
2. The implication from permanence to global attraction for complex-balanced systems using the [pseudo-Helmholtz Lyapunov function](../../../Biology/systems-biology/complex-balancing-and-the-deficiency-zero-theore/has-deriv-at-pseudo-helmholtz.md), giving Theorem 1.2.
