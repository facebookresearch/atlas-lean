# Generalized mass action systems

Intracellular reactions are often better described by power-law rates whose exponents (kinetic orders) differ from the stoichiometric coefficients. Separating the stoichiometric complexes from the kinetic-order complexes gives generalized mass action systems, for which complex balancing equilibria are parametrized by the kinetic-order subspace, and their existence and uniqueness in each stoichiometric compatibility class is governed by sign vectors of the two subspaces.
