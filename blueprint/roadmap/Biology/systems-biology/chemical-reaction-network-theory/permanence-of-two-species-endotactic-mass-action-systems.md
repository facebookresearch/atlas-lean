# Permanence of two-species endotactic mass-action systems

A reaction network is endotactic if, for every direction, the reactions whose source complexes are extreme in that direction among all source complexes point inward (weakly into the half-space); a $\kappa$-variable mass-action system allows the rate constants to vary in time within a compact subset of $(0, \infty)$. Craciun, Nazarov and Pantea (2013, Theorem 6.4) prove that every two-species endotactic $\kappa$-variable mass-action system is permanent: for each positive initial condition there is a compact subset of the open positive orthant, within its compatibility class, that the trajectory eventually enters and never leaves. The same paper proves the global attractor conjecture for complex-balanced systems with three species (its Theorem 7.2); that result is a separate statement and is not claimed here.

## Expansion

1. Definitions: endotactic networks, $\kappa$-variable mass-action systems, and permanence.
2. The construction of a family of invariant polygons $P(\alpha)$ in the positive quadrant, decreasing with $\alpha$, from the endotactic condition (the source's construction before Theorem 6.4).
3. The proof that trajectories enter and remain in a polygon, giving permanence.
4. The relation with the [extended permanence conjecture](../../../Biology/systems-biology/extended-permanence-conjecture.md).
