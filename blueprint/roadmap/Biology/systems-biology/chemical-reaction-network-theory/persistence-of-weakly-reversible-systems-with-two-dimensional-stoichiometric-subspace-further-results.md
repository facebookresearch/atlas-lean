# Persistence of $\kappa$-variable lower-endotactic systems with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace

Pantea (2012) proves, beyond the [persistence of weakly reversible systems with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace](persistence-of-weakly-reversible-systems-with-two-dimensional-stoichiometric-subspace.md), the following more general result. In any $\kappa$-variable mass-action system (mass action whose rate constants are given by a piecewise differentiable $\kappa : [0, \infty) \to (\eta, 1/\eta)^{\mathcal R}$ for some $0 < \eta < 1$, where $\mathcal R$ is the set of reactions; his Definition 2.6) with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace and lower-endotactic stoichiometric subnetworks (his Definition 2.8: the subnetworks whose complexes form the blocks of the partition of the complexes in which two complexes lie in the same block if and only if their difference lies in the stoichiometric subspace; each is therefore a union of connected components of the reaction graph), all bounded trajectories are persistent (his Theorem 5.1); weak reversibility implies the lower-endotactic hypothesis, which gives the candidate statement as a corollary. His other main result, the global attractor conjecture in dimension three (Theorem 6.3), is stated in [the global attractor conjecture in dimension three](../../not-yet-classified/low-dimensional-persistence/tendsto-equilibrium-of-finrank-three.md).
