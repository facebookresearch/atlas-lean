# Persistence of $\kappa$-variable lower-endotactic systems with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace

Pantea (2012) proves, beyond the [persistence of weakly reversible systems with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace](persistence-of-weakly-reversible-systems-with-two-dimensional-stoichiometric-subspace.md), the following more general result. In any $\kappa$-variable mass-action system (mass action in which each rate parameter may vary in time within a compact subset of $(0, \infty)$) with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace and lower-endotactic stoichiometric subnetworks (unions of connected components of the reaction graph), all bounded trajectories are persistent (his Theorem 5.1); weak reversibility implies the lower-endotactic hypothesis, which gives the candidate statement as a corollary. His other main result, the global attractor conjecture in dimension three (Theorem 6.3), is stated in [the global attractor conjecture in dimension three](../../not-yet-classified/low-dimensional-persistence/tendsto-equilibrium-of-finrank-three.md).
