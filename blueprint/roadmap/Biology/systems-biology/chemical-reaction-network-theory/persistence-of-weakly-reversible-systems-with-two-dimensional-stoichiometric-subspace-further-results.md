# Persistence in two-dimensional stoichiometric subspaces: further results

Pantea (2012) proves, beyond the [persistence of weakly reversible systems with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace](persistence-of-weakly-reversible-systems-with-two-dimensional-stoichiometric-subspace.md), two further results. (1) In any $\kappa$-variable mass-action system — mass action in which each rate parameter may vary in time within a compact subset of $(0, \infty)$ — with two-dimensional stoichiometric subspace and lower-endotactic stoichiometric subnetworks (unions of connected components of the reaction graph), all bounded trajectories are persistent (his Theorem 5.1); weak reversibility implies the lower-endotactic hypothesis, which gives the candidate statement as a corollary. (2) The global attractor conjecture holds in dimension three: for a complex-balanced weakly reversible mass-action system with three-dimensional stoichiometric compatibility classes, every solution with positive initial condition $c_0$ converges to the unique positive equilibrium stoichiometrically compatible with $c_0$ (his Theorem 6.3).

## Expansion

1. $\kappa$-variable mass-action systems, stoichiometric subnetworks and the lower-endotactic property (his Definitions 2.6, 2.8 and 3.4).
2. Theorem 5.1 and the construction of invariant regions near the boundary of the compatibility class.
3. Theorem 6.3, via persistence on lower-dimensional faces and the pseudo-Helmholtz Lyapunov function of complex-balanced systems.
4. The relation with the global attractor conjecture for three-species systems, which Pantea cites as known from Craciun, Nazarov and Pantea.
