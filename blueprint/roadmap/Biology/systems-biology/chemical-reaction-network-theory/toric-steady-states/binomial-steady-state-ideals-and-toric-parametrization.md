# Binomial steady-state ideals and monomial parametrization of positive steady states

**Setting.** A mass-action chemical reaction system has species $x_1,\dots,x_s$, complexes $y_1,\dots,y_m \in \mathbb{Z}^s_{\ge 0}$ (rows of $Y \in \mathbb{Z}^{m \times s}$) and rate constants $\kappa_{ij} > 0$ for reactions $y_i \to y_j$. Let $A_\kappa$ be the $m \times m$ matrix with off-diagonal entries $\kappa_{ij}$ and zero row sums, $\Psi(x) = (x^{y_1},\dots,x^{y_m})^t$, and $\Sigma = Y^t A_\kappa^t \in \mathbb{R}^{s \times m}$. The ODE is $\dot x = \Sigma\,\Psi(x)$, and the *steady-state ideal* $J_{\Sigma\Psi}$ is generated by the coordinates of $\Sigma\Psi(x)$. The system has *toric steady states* if $J_{\Sigma\Psi}$ is binomial and admits a positive zero.

**Conditions.** Let $d = \dim \ker \Sigma$.
- (C1) There are a partition $I_1,\dots,I_d$ of $\{1,\dots,m\}$ and a basis $b^1,\dots,b^d$ of $\ker\Sigma$ with $\operatorname{supp}(b^j) = I_j$.
- (C2) For each $j$, all nonzero entries of $b^j$ have the same sign.
- (C3) With $\Delta$ the matrix whose columns are $(y_{j_1} - y_{j_2})^t$ for $j_1, j_2 \in I_j$, $1 \le j \le d$, and $\Theta_\kappa = \big(\ln(b^j_{j_1}/b^j_{j_2})\big)$, the linear system $\Delta^t \ln x = \Theta_\kappa^t$ is solvable; equivalently $\Theta_\kappa U = 0$ for an integer matrix $U$ whose columns span $\ker\Delta$.

**Theorem.**
1. Under (C1), $J_{\Sigma\Psi}$ is generated by the binomials $b^j_{j_1}x^{y_{j_2}} - b^j_{j_2}x^{y_{j_1}}$ for $j_1, j_2 \in I_j$, $1 \le j \le d$.
2. Under (C1), the system has a positive (toric) steady state if and only if (C2) and (C3) hold.
3. Under (C1)–(C3), let $A \in \mathbb{Z}^{w \times s}$ have maximal rank with $\ker A = \operatorname{span}\{y_{j_2} - y_{j_1} : j_1, j_2 \in I_j\}$ and columns $A_i$. If $\tilde x$ is one positive steady state, the positive steady states are exactly

$$x = \big(\tilde x_1 t^{A_1}, \dots, \tilde x_s t^{A_s}\big), \qquad t \in \mathbb{R}^w_{>0}.$$

4. If (C1) holds with a fixed partition for all rate constants, then a rate vector $\kappa^*$ yields a positive steady state iff it satisfies (C2) and (C3).
5. (Application.) The $n$-site sequential distributive phosphorylation system has toric steady states, and its steady-state locus is a $3$-dimensional monomially parametrized variety whose coefficients are ratios of polynomials in the rate constants that never vanish and share one sign on the positive orthant.

## Expansion

Since $\Sigma\Psi(x) = 0$ iff $\Psi(x) \in \ker\Sigma$, (C1) means $\Psi(x)$ must be a combination $\sum_j \lambda_j b^j$ of disjointly supported vectors, so within each block the monomials are proportional with fixed ratios, which is exactly a set of binomials. Positivity of $\Psi(x)$ needs equal signs within each block (C2), and taking logarithms turns the binomials into the affine system $\Delta^t \ln x = \Theta_\kappa^t$ (C3), whose solution set is a translate of the row space of $A$, giving the monomial parametrization. Enlarging the system by monomial multiples of the steady-state equations gives a weaker sufficient condition (Theorems 3.19–3.20) that covers networks where (C1) fails for the original equations.
