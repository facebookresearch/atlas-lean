# Toric steady states of the multisite phosphorylation system

For a mass action system with $s$ species, $m$ complexes $y_1, \dots, y_m \in \mathbb{Z}^s_{\ge 0}$ and complex-to-species rate matrix $\Sigma$, Pérez Millán, Dickenstein, Shiu and Conradi (2012) say that the system has toric steady states if its steady-state ideal is a binomial ideal admitting real zeros. Condition 3.1 asks for a partition $I_1, \dots, I_d$ of the complexes and a basis $b_1, \dots, b_d$ of $\ker\Sigma$ with $\operatorname{supp}(b_j) = I_j$; it makes the steady-state ideal binomial. The criterion of Theorem 3.8 (Conditions 3.4 and 3.6) and the monomial parametrization of Theorem 3.11 are stated in [existence of positive toric steady states](../../not-yet-classified/toric-steady-states/exists-pos-steady-state-iff.md) and [monomial parametrization of the positive steady states](../../not-yet-classified/toric-steady-states/pos-solutions-eq-monomial-image.md). Theorem 4.3 shows that the $n$-site phosphorylation system has toric steady states with a three-dimensional steady-state locus.

## Expansion

1. The model: mass action systems, the matrix $\Sigma$, the steady-state ideal and toric steady states.
2. Condition 3.1 with its linear-algebra data and the binomial generators of the steady-state ideal, and Lemma 3.5 for checking [Condition 3.4](../../not-yet-classified/toric-steady-states/condition34.md).
3. Checking Conditions 3.1, 3.4 and 3.6 for the $n$-site phosphorylation system, so that [Theorem 3.8](../../not-yet-classified/toric-steady-states/exists-pos-steady-state-iff.md) and [Theorem 3.11](../../not-yet-classified/toric-steady-states/pos-solutions-eq-monomial-image.md) apply.
4. The application to the $n$-site phosphorylation system (Theorem 4.3), with its explicit parametrization; compare [rational parameterisation of multisite modification steady states](multisite-phosphorylation/rational-parameterisation-theorem-for-multisite-ptm-systems.md).
