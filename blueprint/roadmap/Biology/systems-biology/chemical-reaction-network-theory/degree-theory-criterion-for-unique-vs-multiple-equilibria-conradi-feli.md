# A degree-theory criterion for unique versus multiple equilibria

Let a reaction network with stoichiometric matrix $N$ of rank $s$ have rate functions satisfying Eq. (2) of Conradi, Feliu, Mincheva and Wiuf (2017), and let $\varphi_c$ be the map obtained from the species-formation rates by replacing redundant equations with the conservation laws $Wx = c$, with Jacobian $M(x)$, which does not depend on $c$. Theorem 1 of the source states, for a stoichiometric compatibility class $P_c$ with positive points, a dissipative network, and no boundary equilibria in $P_c$: (A′) if $\operatorname{sign}\det M(x) = (-1)^s$ at all positive equilibria in $P_c$, there is exactly one positive equilibrium in $P_c$, and it is non-degenerate; (B′) if $\operatorname{sign}\det M(x) = (-1)^{s+1}$ at some positive equilibrium in $P_c$, there are at least two positive equilibria in $P_c$, at least one non-degenerate, and at least three, always an odd number, if all are non-degenerate. Corollary 2 restates this when the positive equilibria admit a positive parametrization $\Phi$, with $a(\hat x) = \det M(\Phi(\hat x))$: if $\operatorname{sign} a = (-1)^s$ everywhere there is exactly one positive equilibrium in each class with positive points, and if $\operatorname{sign} a(\hat x) = (-1)^{s+1}$ for some $\hat x$ the class through $\Phi(\hat x)$ is multistationary. The proof relates $\det M(x)$ to the Brouwer degree of $\varphi_c$ at $0$.

## Expansion

1. The model: rate functions satisfying Eq. (2), dissipativity, boundary equilibria, the map $\varphi_c$ (Eq. (6)) and non-degenerate equilibria.
2. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1, with the Brouwer degree argument of the supporting information.
3. Positive parametrizations of the equilibrium set, the function $a$ (Eq. (10)) and Corollary 2.
4. The procedure of the source for finding parameter regions of mono- and multistationarity, and its applications, as separate statements.
