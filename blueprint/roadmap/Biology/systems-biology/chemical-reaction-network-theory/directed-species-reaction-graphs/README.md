# Directed species-reaction graphs

The Jacobian of a reaction system with non-autocatalytic kinetics factors as stoichiometry times rate derivatives. Encoding the two factors as the two edge types of a bipartite directed graph, its cycle structure gives a term-by-term expansion of the determinant, principal minors and characteristic-polynomial coefficients, which yields graphical sign conditions for injectivity and against multistationarity and saddle-node bifurcations.
