# Strong error of Euler tau-leaping under the classical scaling

Consider a family $X^V$ of stochastic reaction networks indexed by a system size $V$, in the classical scaling in which concentrations are of order one and propensities of order $V$, and the Euler tau-leap approximation $Z^V$ with step $h_V = V^{-\beta}$, $\beta > 0$, coupled to $X^V$ through common Poisson processes. Under the moment and truncation assumptions of Anderson, Ganguly and Kurtz (2011), there is $C = C(T)$ with

$$\sup_{t \le T} E\,|X^V(t) - Z^V(t)| \le C\, V^{-\beta} = C\, h_V$$

(their Theorem 3.2), and $V^\beta (X^V - Z^V)$ converges to an explicit limit, which gives the exact first-order asymptotics of the error (their Theorem 3.10); weak-error asymptotics are their Theorem 4.1.

## Expansion

1. The model: random time-change representation of $X^V$, the Euler tau-leap process $Z^V$ and their coupling, with the scaling assumptions.
2. The strong error bound by a Grönwall argument on $E|X^V - Z^V|$ (Theorem 3.2).
3. The limit of $V^\beta(X^V - Z^V)$ (Theorem 3.10).
4. The weak-error expansion (Theorem 4.1), and the comparison with the midpoint method, as separate statements.
