# Algebraic criterion for product-form stationary distributions of conservative reaction networks

**Setting.** Let $(\mathcal{G}, \kappa)$ be a stochastic reaction network on species $S_1, \dots, S_n$ whose state space decomposes into irreducible, positive recurrent components that are the integer points of dilated simplices,

$$\Gamma_l = \Big\{x \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^n : \sum_i x_i = l\Big\}, \qquad l \in \mathcal{I} = \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1},$$

with stationary distributions $\pi_l$ positive on $\Gamma_l$. The network has *product form* if there are $f_i : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ with $\pi_l(x) = Z_l^{-1}\prod_i f_i(x_i)$ on every $\Gamma_l$.

**Theorem (compatibility relations).** $(\mathcal{G}, \kappa)$ has product form if and only if:

(a) for all $x, x + e_j - e_k \in \Gamma_i$ and $y, y + e_j - e_k \in \Gamma_{i+1}$ with $x_j = y_j$, $x_k = y_k$,

$$\pi_i(x + e_j - e_k)\,\pi_{i+1}(y) = \pi_{i+1}(y + e_j - e_k)\,\pi_i(x);$$

(b) for all $x, z \in \Gamma_i$; $x + e_j, y, z + e_k, w \in \Gamma_{i+1}$; $y + e_j, w + e_k \in \Gamma_{i+2}$ with $x_j = y_j$ and $z_k = w_k$,

$$\pi_{i+1}(x+e_j)\,\pi_{i+1}(y)\,\pi_{i+2}(w+e_k)\,\pi_i(z) = \pi_{i+1}(z+e_k)\,\pi_{i+1}(w)\,\pi_{i+2}(y+e_j)\,\pi_i(x).$$

If $\mathcal{I} = \mathbb{Z}_{\ge m}$ with $m \ge 2$, product form still implies (a) and (b).

**Theorem (semi-algebraic set of product-form rates).** Assume moreover that every transition intensity is linear in the rate constants $\kappa_{\nu \to \nu'}$. Then each unnormalized stationary vector $h_l(\kappa)$ can be chosen as a vector of homogeneous polynomials in $\kappa$ with positive coefficients, and substituting $h_l(\kappa)$ into the relations (a)–(b) maps them to an ideal $I \subset \mathbb{R}[\kappa]$. For every $a \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{R}}_{>0}$,

$$(\mathcal{G}, a) \text{ has product form} \iff a \in \mathbb{V}(I),$$

so the product-form rate vectors form the semi-algebraic set $V_{\mathcal{G},>0} = \mathbb{V}(I) \cap \mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{R}}_{>0}$. By the Hilbert basis theorem $I$ is generated by the relations from finitely many components $\Gamma_m, \dots, \Gamma_N$.

## Proof sketch

Product form makes ratios $\pi_l(x + e_j - e_k)/\pi_l(x) = \frac{f_j(x_j+1) f_k(x_k-1)}{f_j(x_j) f_k(x_k)}$ depend only on the coordinates $x_j, x_k$, which is what (a) and (b) express across adjacent simplices; conversely these relations let one define the $f_i$ consistently by walking through $\Gamma_l$ and $\Gamma_{l+1}$. Under linear kinetics the generator restricted to $\Gamma_l$ has rank $|\Gamma_l| - 1$ over $\mathbb{R}(\kappa)$, so its kernel is spanned by a polynomial vector (the matrix-tree form), and the relations become polynomial equations in $\kappa$. For weakly reversible mass-action networks the positive toric variety of the network is contained in $V_{\mathcal{G},>0}$, with equality (all positive rates) at deficiency zero, recovering the complex-balanced product-form Poisson laws as a special case.
