# Multistationarity of conjunctive networks

Conjunctive networks are ODE models of gene or neural circuits in which each node is driven by a product (AND gate) of steep Hill functions of its inputs. For large Hill coefficients their stable steady states are counted combinatorially from the wiring diagram, which gives a modular way to engineer any desired number of stable states.
