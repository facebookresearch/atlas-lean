# For nonnegative rates and s₀, e₀ ≥ 0, a positive complex stays so

Let $k_1, k_b, k_2 \ge 0$ and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](../is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with $c(0) > 0$, $s(0) \ge 0$ and $e(0) \ge 0$; no condition is
put on $p(0)$. Then $c(t) > 0$ for every $t \ge 0$.
