# With k₁ > 0 and s₀, e₀ > 0, the complex is positive at every t > 0

Let $k_1 > 0$ and $k_b, k_2 \ge 0$, and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](../is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with $s(0) > 0$, $e(0) > 0$ and $c(0) \ge 0$; no condition is
put on $p(0)$. Then $c(t) > 0$ for every $t > 0$. This covers the unprepared start $c(0) = 0$, where
$c(0)$ differs from the positive [quasi-steady-state value](../qss-complex.md)
$e(0) s(0)/(K_M + s(0))$, with $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$.
