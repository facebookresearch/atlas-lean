# A solution is a curve whose derivative is the field from time zero

For all real $k_1, k_b, k_2$, functions $s, c, e, p$ form a [solution](../is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system if and only if the [packed curve](mm-curve.md)
$x(t) = (s(t), c(t), e(t), p(t))$ has the derivative $x'(t) = F(x(t))$ at every $t \ge 0$, for the
[mass-action field](mm-field.md) $F$.
