# A solution starting without substrate or complex stays at rest

For all real $k_1, k_b, k_2$, every [solution](../is-solution.md) $(s, c, e, p)$ of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with $s(0) = 0$ and $c(0) = 0$ satisfies $s(t) = 0$, $c(t) = 0$,
$e(t) = e(0)$ and $p(t) = p(0)$ for every $t \ge 0$: it is the
[zero-substrate equilibrium](is-solution-zero-substrate.md) from time $0$ on.
