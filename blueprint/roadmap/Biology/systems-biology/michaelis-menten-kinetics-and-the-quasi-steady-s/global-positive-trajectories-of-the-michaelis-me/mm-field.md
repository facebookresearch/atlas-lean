# Michaelis–Menten mass-action field on four coordinates

For real rate constants $k_1, k_b, k_2$, the Michaelis–Menten mass-action field on $\mathbb{R}^4$,
with coordinates $x = (s, c, e, p)$ (substrate, complex, free enzyme, product), is

$$
F(s, c, e, p) = \bigl(-k_1 e s + k_b c,\ k_1 e s - (k_b + k_2) c,\
-(k_1 e s - (k_b + k_2) c),\ k_2 c\bigr).
$$
