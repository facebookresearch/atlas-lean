# With nonnegative rates and data, every solution stays nonnegative

Let $k_1, k_b, k_2 \ge 0$ and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](../is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with $s(0), c(0), e(0), p(0) \ge 0$. Then for every $t \ge 0$,
$s(t), c(t), e(t) \ge 0$ and $p(t) \ge p(0) \ge 0$: the nonnegative orthant is invariant, and the
product does not decrease below its initial value.
