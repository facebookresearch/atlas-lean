# The fast-to-slow time-scale ratio is below ε

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$, $s_0 > 0$ and $e_0 > 0$. Then the
[fast time scale](fast-time-scale.md) $t_C = 1/(k_1 (s_0 + K_M))$ and the
[slow time scale](slow-time-scale.md) $t_S = (s_0 + K_M)/(k_2 e_0)$ satisfy $t_C/t_S < \varepsilon$,
with the [Segel–Slemrod parameter](qssa-epsilon.md) $\varepsilon = e_0/(s_0 + K_M)$: a small
$\varepsilon$ separates the two time scales.
