# Initial-layer gap of the complex at slow time zero

Let $k_1, k_b, k_2, s_0$ and $e_0$ be real with $\kappa = K_M/s_0 > 0$, the
[dimensionless Michaelis constant](qssa-kappa.md), and let $c, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with
$c(0) = 0$ and $u(0) = 1$. Then the outer complex error at slow time $\theta = 0$ is

$$\Bigl|\frac{c(t_S \cdot 0)}{e_0} - h(u(0))\Bigr| = \frac{1}{1 + \kappa},$$

with the [slow manifold](slow-manifold.md) $h(x) = x/(\kappa + x)$ and the
[slow time scale](slow-time-scale.md) $t_S$, so that the left side is $|c(0)/e_0 - h(1)|$: the
complex starts at $v = 0$, the slow manifold at $1/(1 + \kappa)$, whatever $e_0$ is.
