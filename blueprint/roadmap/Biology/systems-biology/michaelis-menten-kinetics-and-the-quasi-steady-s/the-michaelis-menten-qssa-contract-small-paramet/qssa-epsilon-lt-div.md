# The Segel–Slemrod parameter is below the Heineken–Tsuchiya–Aris one

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$, $s_0 > 0$ and $e_0 > 0$. Then the
[Segel–Slemrod parameter](qssa-epsilon.md) $\varepsilon = e_0/(s_0 + K_M)$ satisfies
$\varepsilon < e_0/s_0$, the Heineken–Tsuchiya–Aris parameter; here $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1 > 0$ is
the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md).
