# When C ε is below the initial-layer gap, the bound fails at θ = 0

Let $k_1, k_b, k_2, s_0, e_0$ and $C$ be real with $\kappa = K_M/s_0 > 0$, and let
$c, u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $c(0) = 0$ and $u(0) = 1$. If
$C \varepsilon < 1/(1 + \kappa)$, with the [Segel–Slemrod parameter](qssa-epsilon.md)
$\varepsilon = e_0/(s_0 + K_M)$, then

$$\Bigl|\frac{c(t_S \cdot 0)}{e_0} - h(u(0))\Bigr| > C \varepsilon,$$

with the [slow manifold](slow-manifold.md) $h(x) = x/(\kappa + x)$ and the
[slow time scale](slow-time-scale.md) $t_S$: no bound of size $C \varepsilon$ on the complex error
holds at $\theta = 0$ once $C \varepsilon$ is below the [initial-layer gap](initial-layer-gap.md).
This is why the [QSSA contract](qssacontract.md) claims the complex bound only for
$\theta \ge \delta > 0$.
