# A global solution solves the system on every window

Let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](../is-solution.md) of the Michaelis–Menten mass-action system
with total enzyme $e(0) + c(0) = e_0$. Then for every real $t_1$, $(s, c)$ is a
[solution on the window](is-solution-on.md) $[0, t_1]$ with total enzyme $e_0$.
