# Ratio of the fast to the slow time scale

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$, $s_0 > 0$ and $e_0 > 0$. Then the
[fast time scale](fast-time-scale.md) $t_C = 1/(k_1 (s_0 + K_M))$ and the
[slow time scale](slow-time-scale.md) $t_S = (s_0 + K_M)/(k_2 e_0)$ satisfy

$$\frac{t_C}{t_S} = \varepsilon\, \frac{k_2}{k_1 s_0 + k_b + k_2},$$

with the [Segel–Slemrod parameter](qssa-epsilon.md) $\varepsilon = e_0/(s_0 + K_M)$ and
$K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md).
