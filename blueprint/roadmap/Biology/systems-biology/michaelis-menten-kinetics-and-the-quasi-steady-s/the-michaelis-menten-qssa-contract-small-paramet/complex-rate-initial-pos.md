# At the standard initial data the complex rate is positive

Let $k_1 > 0$, $s_0 > 0$ and $e_0 > 0$, and let $k_b$ and $k_2$ be real. At substrate $s_0$, complex
$0$ and free enzyme $e_0$, the [complex rate](../complex-rate.md) is $k_1 e_0 s_0 > 0$. So a
[solution](../is-solution.md) with $s(0) = s_0$, $c(0) = 0$ and $e(0) = e_0$ has $c'(0) > 0$: the
equation $c' = 0$ fails at the initial point.
