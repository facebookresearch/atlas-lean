# In scaled variables the QSS complex is the slow manifold

Let $s_0 \ne 0$ and $e_0 \ne 0$, and let $k_1, k_b, k_2$ and $u$ be real. Then the
[quasi-steady-state complex](../qss-complex.md) at the substrate $s = s_0 u$, divided by $e_0$, is
the [slow manifold](slow-manifold.md) $h(x) = x/(\kappa + x)$ at $u$:

$$
\frac{1}{e_0} \cdot \frac{e_0\, s_0 u}{K_M + s_0 u} = \frac{u}{\kappa + u},
\qquad \kappa = \frac{K_M}{s_0},
$$

both sides being $0$ when $K_M + s_0 u = 0$, by the convention $x/0 = 0$. This is algebra on the
manifold, not a statement about solutions.
