# Segel–Slemrod small parameter

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2, s_0$ and $e_0$, the Segel–Slemrod small parameter is

$$\varepsilon = \frac{e_0}{s_0 + K_M},$$

with the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md) $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ and quotients by $0$
read as $0$: it is $0$ when $s_0 + K_M = 0$. In the model $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$,
$s_0 > 0$ and $e_0 > 0$, and $\varepsilon$ is the dimensionless ratio of the total enzyme to
$s_0 + K_M$.
