# Reduced vector field in slow time

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2, s_0$ and $u$, with the [dimensionless Michaelis constant](qssa-kappa.md)
$\kappa = K_M/s_0$, the reduced vector field for the scaled substrate $u = s/s_0$ in slow time
$\theta$ is

$$F(u) = -\frac{(1 + \kappa)\, u}{\kappa + u},$$

minus the [Michaelis–Menten rate law](../mm-rate.md) with maximal rate $1 + \kappa$ and Michaelis
constant $\kappa$, with the quotient read as $0$ when $\kappa + u = 0$. The reduced equation is
$du/d\theta = F(u)$.
