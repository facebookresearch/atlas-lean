# Slow time scale of substrate depletion

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2, s_0$ and $e_0$, the slow (substrate depletion) time scale is

$$t_S = \frac{s_0 + K_M}{k_2 e_0},$$

with the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md) $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ and quotients by $0$
read as $0$: it is $0$ when $k_2 e_0 = 0$. In the model $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$, $s_0 > 0$
and $e_0 > 0$, and then $t_S > 0$.
