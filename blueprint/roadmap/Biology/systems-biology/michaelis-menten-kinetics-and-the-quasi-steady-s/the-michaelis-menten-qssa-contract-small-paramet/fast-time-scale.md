# Fast time scale of complex formation

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2$ and $s_0$, the fast (complex formation) time scale is

$$t_C = \frac{1}{k_1 (s_0 + K_M)},$$

with the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md) $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ and quotients by $0$
read as $0$: it is $0$ when $k_1 (s_0 + K_M) = 0$. In the model $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$
and $s_0 > 0$, and then $t_C > 0$.
