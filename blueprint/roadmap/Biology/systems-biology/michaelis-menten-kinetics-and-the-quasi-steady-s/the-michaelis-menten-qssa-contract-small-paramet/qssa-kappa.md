# Dimensionless Michaelis constant

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2$ and $s_0$, the dimensionless Michaelis constant is $\kappa = K_M/s_0$, with
the [Michaelis constant](../michaelis-constant.md) $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ and quotients by $0$ read
as $0$: it is $0$ when $s_0 = 0$. It does not depend on $e_0$, so it is fixed in the limit
$e_0 \to 0$. In the model $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$ and $s_0 > 0$.
