# A reduced solution that starts positive stays positive

Let $k_2 > 0$ and $K > 0$, and let $\bar S : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable at every
$\tau \ge 0$ with $\bar S'(\tau) = -k_2 \bar S(\tau)/(K + \bar S(\tau))$ there, a solution of the
reduced (Michaelis–Menten) equation with the [rate law](mm-rate.md) for $V_{\max} = k_2$ and
Michaelis constant $K$; the quotient is read as $0$ when $K + \bar S(\tau) = 0$. If $\bar S(0) > 0$,
then $\bar S(\tau) > 0$ for every $\tau \ge 0$.
