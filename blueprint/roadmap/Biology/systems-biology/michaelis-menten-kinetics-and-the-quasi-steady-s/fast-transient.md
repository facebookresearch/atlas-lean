# Fast transient of the complex with frozen substrate

For real $k_1, k_b, k_2, e_0, \bar s, c_0$ and $t$, the fast transient of the complex with the
substrate frozen at $\bar s$ is

$$c(t) = c^* + (c_0 - c^*)\, e^{-k_1 (\bar s + K_M) t}, \qquad c^* = \frac{e_0 \bar s}{K_M + \bar s},$$

with $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md) and quotients by $0$ read as $0$.
