# For s₀ > 0 and c₀, e₀ ≥ 0, c stays at most e₀ + c₀ and e nonnegative

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$ and $k_2 > 0$, so that the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md)
$K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ is positive, and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with initial values $s(0) > 0$, $c(0) \ge 0$ and $e(0) \ge 0$.
Then $c(t) \le e(0) + c(0)$ for every $t \ge 0$: the complex never exceeds the total enzyme.
Equivalently, since [enzyme conservation](enzyme-conservation.md) gives $e(t) + c(t) = e(0) + c(0)$
for $t \ge 0$, the free enzyme stays nonnegative: $e(t) \ge 0$ for every $t \ge 0$.
