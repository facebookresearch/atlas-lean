# For K > 0 a positive reduced solution passes through every s₀ > 0

Let $K > 0$, $s_0 > 0$ and $k_2$ real. Then there is a function
$\bar S : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, positive and differentiable everywhere, with $\bar S(0) = s_0$
and

$$\bar S'(\tau) = -\frac{k_2 \bar S(\tau)}{K + \bar S(\tau)} \quad \text{for every real } \tau.$$

The proof takes $\bar S(\tau) = \Phi^{-1}(\Phi(s_0) - k_2 \tau)$, where $\Phi(x) = x + K \log x$ is
an increasing bijection from $(0, \infty)$ onto $\mathbb{R}$, the implicit integral of the
Michaelis–Menten equation.
