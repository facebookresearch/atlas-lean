# Michaelis–Menten rate stays below its maximum

Let $V_{\max} > 0$ and $K_M > 0$. For every $s \ge 0$, the [Michaelis–Menten rate law](mm-rate.md) $v(s) = V_{\max} s/(K_M + s)$ satisfies $v(s) < V_{\max}$.

So under [saturable elimination](../../pharmacokinetics/saturable-michaelis-menten-elimination/is-solution.md)
with a constant infusion rate $R$, an equilibrium concentration $c \ge 0$, where $v(c) = R$, exists
only if $R < V_{\max}$: an infusion rate $R \ge V_{\max}$ has no nonnegative equilibrium.
