# Standard data: the slow-time substrate tends to the reduced solution

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$ and $s_0 > 0$, with $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ the
[Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md), and let $\bar S : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be
differentiable at every $\tau \ge 0$ with $\bar S'(\tau) = -k_2 \bar S(\tau)/(K_M + \bar S(\tau))$
there, a solution of the reduced (Michaelis–Menten) equation, with $\bar S(0) = s_0$. For every
$\varepsilon > 0$ let $(s_\varepsilon, c_\varepsilon, e_\varepsilon, p_\varepsilon)$ be a
[solution](is-solution.md) of the Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with the standard data
$s_\varepsilon(0) = s_0$, $c_\varepsilon(0) = 0$ and $e_\varepsilon(0) = \varepsilon$. Then for
every slow time $\tau \ge 0$, $s_\varepsilon(\tau/\varepsilon) \to \bar S(\tau)$ as
$\varepsilon \to 0^+$.
