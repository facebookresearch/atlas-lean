# Substrate consumption at the Michaelis–Menten rate under QSSA

Let $k_1 > 0$ and $k_b, k_2, e_0, s \in \mathbb{R}$ with $K_M + s \ne 0$, where $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ is the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md). On the
quasi-steady-state manifold, with $c = c^* = e_0 s/(K_M + s)$ and $e = e_0 - c^*$, the substrate rate
is $-k_1 e s + k_b c = -V_{\max} s/(K_M + s)$ with $V_{\max} = k_2 e_0$: the substrate is consumed at
the Michaelis–Menten rate.
