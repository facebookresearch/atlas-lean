# With s₀ > 0 and c₀, e₀ ≥ 0, the substrate stays positive

Let $k_1 > 0$, $k_b \ge 0$ and $k_2 > 0$, so that the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md)
$K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ is positive, and let $(s, c, e, p)$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the
Michaelis–Menten mass-action system with initial values $s(0) > 0$, $c(0) \ge 0$ and $e(0) \ge 0$.
Then $s(t) > 0$ for every $t \ge 0$. This is a special case of
[positivity for nonnegative rate constants](global-positive-trajectories-of-the-michaelis-me/substrate-pos-of-nonneg.md),
which needs only $k_1, k_b, k_2 \ge 0$.
