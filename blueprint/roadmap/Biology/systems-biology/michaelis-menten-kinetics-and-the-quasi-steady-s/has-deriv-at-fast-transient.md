# Fast transient solves the boundary-layer equation

Let $k_1 > 0$ and $k_b, k_2, e_0, \bar s \in \mathbb{R}$ with $K_M + \bar s \ne 0$, where
$K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ is the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md). For every $c_0$ and $t$, the [fast transient](fast-transient.md) $c$ has derivative
$k_1 (e_0 - c(t)) \bar s - (k_b + k_2) c(t)$ at $t$: it solves the boundary-layer equation.
