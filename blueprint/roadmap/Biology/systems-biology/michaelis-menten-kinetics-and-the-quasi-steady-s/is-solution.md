# Solution of the Michaelis–Menten mass-action system

Let $k_1, k_b, k_2 \in \mathbb{R}$. Functions $s, c, e, p \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (substrate,
complex, free enzyme, product) solve the Michaelis–Menten mass-action system on $[0, \infty)$ when, at
every $t \ge 0$, they are differentiable with

$$s' = -k_1 e s + k_b c, \qquad c' = k_1 e s - (k_b + k_2) c, \qquad e' = -c', \qquad p' = k_2 c.$$
