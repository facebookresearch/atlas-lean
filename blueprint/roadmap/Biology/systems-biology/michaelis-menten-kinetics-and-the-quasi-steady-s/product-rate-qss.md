# Briggs–Haldane rate law

For all real $k_1, k_b, k_2, e_0$ and $s$, on the quasi-steady-state manifold the product forms at rate
$k_2 c^* = V_{\max} s/(K_M + s)$, where $c^* = e_0 s/(K_M + s)$ is the
[quasi-steady-state complex](qss-complex.md), $V_{\max} = k_2 e_0$ and $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md) (quotients by
$0$ read as $0$).
