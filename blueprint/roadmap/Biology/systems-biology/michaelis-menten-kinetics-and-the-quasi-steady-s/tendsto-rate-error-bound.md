# For τ₀ > 0 the rate-law error bound tends to zero as ε → 0⁺

Let $k_b \ge 0$, $k_2 > 0$ and $\tau_0 > 0$, and let $k_1$, $s_0$ and $T$ be real. Then the
[rate-law error bound](rate-error-bound.md) $B(\varepsilon)$ tends to $0$ as $\varepsilon \to 0^+$:
the window $[\tau_0, T]$ excludes the initial layer.
