# Fast transient converges to the quasi-steady state

Let $k_1 > 0$ and $k_b, k_2, e_0, \bar s \in \mathbb{R}$ with $\bar s + K_M > 0$, where $K_M = (k_b + k_2)/k_1$ is the [Michaelis constant](michaelis-constant.md).
For every $c_0$, the [fast transient](fast-transient.md) converges, as $t \to \infty$, to
the [quasi-steady-state complex](qss-complex.md) $c^* = e_0 \bar s/(K_M + \bar s)$.
