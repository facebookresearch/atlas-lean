# Half mean squared reporter difference equals intrinsic variance

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space with a probability measure $\mu$, let $m \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a sub-σ-algebra (the extrinsic information), and let $X, Y \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable reporters forming an [identical reporter pair](is-identical-reporter-pair.md) given $m$. Then $\mathbb{E}[(X - Y)^2]/2 = \mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Var}(X \mid m)]$.
