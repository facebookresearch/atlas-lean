# Extrinsic noise strength

For a measure $\mu$ and real functions $X, Y$, the two-reporter extrinsic noise strength is

$$\eta_{\mathrm{ext}}^2 = \frac{\mathbb{E}[XY] - \mathbb{E}[X]\,\mathbb{E}[Y]}{\mathbb{E}[X]\,\mathbb{E}[Y]},$$

with $\mathbb{E}$ the integral against $\mu$ (taken to be $0$ for non-integrable functions) and the
quotient read as $0$ when $\mathbb{E}[X]\,\mathbb{E}[Y] = 0$.
