# Two-reporter formula for extrinsic noise

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ be a measurable space with a probability measure $\mu$, let $m \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a sub-σ-algebra (the extrinsic information), and let $X, Y \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable reporters forming an [identical reporter pair](is-identical-reporter-pair.md) given $m$. Then the [extrinsic noise strength](extrinsic-noise-sq.md) is
$\eta_{\mathrm{ext}}^2 = \operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{E}[X \mid m]) / \mathbb{E}[X]^2$, with the quotient
read as $0$ when $\mathbb{E}[X] = 0$ (Swain et al. 2002, Eq. 8).
