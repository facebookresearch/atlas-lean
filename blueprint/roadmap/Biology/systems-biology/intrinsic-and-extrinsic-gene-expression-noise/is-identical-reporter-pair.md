# Identical reporter pair

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ and $m$ a σ-algebra on $\Omega$ (the extrinsic
information). Two reporters $X, Y \colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ form an identical reporter pair given $m$
when, $\mu$-almost everywhere,

$$\mathbb{E}[X \mid m] = \mathbb{E}[Y \mid m], \qquad \mathbb{E}[X^2 \mid m] = \mathbb{E}[Y^2 \mid m], \qquad \mathbb{E}[XY \mid m] = \mathbb{E}[X \mid m]\, \mathbb{E}[Y \mid m]:$$

equal conditional means, equal conditional second moments, and conditionally uncorrelated. Here the
conditional expectation $\mathbb{E}[f \mid m]$ of each of the functions $X, Y, X^2, Y^2, XY$ is taken to be $0$
when $m$ is not a sub-σ-algebra of $\mathcal{F}$, when $\mu$ is not σ-finite on $m$, or when that function is not
$\mu$-integrable. In the usual setting ($\mu$ a probability measure, $m \subseteq \mathcal{F}$, and $X, Y$
square-integrable, so that all five functions are integrable), conditional independence of $X$ and $Y$ with
identical conditional laws given $m$ implies these three conditions.
