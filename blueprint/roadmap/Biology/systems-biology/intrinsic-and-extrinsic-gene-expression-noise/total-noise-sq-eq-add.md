# Two-reporter decomposition

For every measure $\mu$ and square-integrable $X, Y$, the two-reporter noise strengths satisfy
$\eta_{\mathrm{tot}}^2 = \eta_{\mathrm{int}}^2 + \eta_{\mathrm{ext}}^2$, where these are the
[total](total-noise-sq.md), [intrinsic](intrinsic-noise-sq.md) and [extrinsic](extrinsic-noise-sq.md)
noise strengths (Swain et al. 2002; Elowitz et al. 2002).
