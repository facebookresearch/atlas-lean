# Slowest first passage times

Extreme value theory for the slowest of many independent and identically distributed first passage times, and for the $(k+1)$-st slowest. The limits are governed only by the large-time decay of the survival probability of a single searcher: exponential decay gives Gumbel-type limits with logarithmic growth, power-law decay gives Fréchet-type limits with polynomial growth.
