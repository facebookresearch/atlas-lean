# Thermodynamic limits of stochastic phosphorylation networks

For a stochastic dual distributive sequential phosphorylation network with $N$ substrate molecules and enzyme totals proportional to $N$, the naive deterministic limit is wrong because complex-formation rates are of order $N^2$. Instead some species stay $O(1)$ while the rest scale with $N$, and the $O(1)$ species equilibrate fast as networks of $M/M/\infty$ queues, averaging the slow dynamics. Which species are $O(1)$ is determined by whether enzymes or substrate are in excess.
