# Thermodynamic loop law in constraint-based models

A steady-state flux distribution of a metabolic network is thermodynamically infeasible if it contains an internal loop, a nonzero flux pattern through internal reactions with zero net production of every metabolite. The loopless constraint, which asks for reaction potentials of opposite sign to the fluxes lying in the row space of the internal stoichiometric matrix, removes exactly the flux distributions with loops, as a sign-pattern version of Gordan's alternative shows.
