# Metastable switching in processes with internal states

Weak-noise analysis of bistable stochastic processes whose external state (for example a protein copy number) is driven by a fast discrete internal state (for example a gene's activation state). A WKB expansion with a matrix-determinant Hamiltonian gives the quasi-stationary density, and matched asymptotics at the saddle give the mean switching time including its pre-exponential factor.
