# Uniqueness of weakly reversible deficiency-zero realizations

**Definitions.** A reaction network $\mathcal N$ on $n$ species is a finite directed graph whose vertices (complexes) lie in $\mathbb R^n$; its linkage classes are the connected components, and its deficiency is $\delta=|V|-\ell-\dim S_{\mathcal N}$. It is weakly reversible if every component is strongly connected, and $\mathrm{WR}_0$ if moreover $\delta=0$. A mass-action system $(\mathcal N',\kappa')$ is a realization of $(\mathcal N,\kappa)$ if both give the same vector field on $\mathbb R^n_{>0}$. $\mathcal N$ is realizable by $\mathcal N'$ if for every $\kappa$ there is $\kappa'$ with $f_{\mathcal N}(\kappa,\cdot)=f_{\mathcal N'}(\kappa',\cdot)$; this holds iff every source complex $y$ of $\mathcal N$ is a source of $\mathcal N'$ and $\mathrm{Cone}_{\mathcal N}(y)\subseteq\mathrm{Cone}_{\mathcal N'}(y)$, where $\mathrm{Cone}(y)$ is the cone spanned by the reaction vectors $y'-y$ leaving $y$.

**Theorem.** (i) A mass-action ODE system $\dot x=Wx^Y$ has at most one $\mathrm{WR}_0$ realization. (ii) Suppose $\mathcal N$ is $\mathrm{WR}_0$-realizable, i.e. $(\mathcal N,\kappa)$ has a $\mathrm{WR}_0$ realization $(\mathcal N_\kappa,k_\kappa)$ for every $\kappa$. Then $\mathcal N_\kappa$ does not depend on $\kappa$: there is a unique $\mathrm{WR}_0$ network $\mathcal N'$ such that $\mathcal N$ is realizable by $\mathcal N'$.

**Consequence.** For such $\mathcal N$ and every $\kappa$, the deficiency-zero theorem applied to $\mathcal N'$ gives exactly one positive equilibrium in each stoichiometric compatibility class, asymptotically stable within it.

## Expansion

For a $\mathrm{WR}_0$ system, $\ker W$ has a nonnegative basis $c_1,\dots,c_\ell$ supported on the linkage classes, and these are the extreme rays of $\ker W\cap\mathbb R^m_{\ge0}$; so the linkage-class partition of the source complexes is recovered from $W$ alone. Deficiency zero makes the complexes of each linkage class affinely independent, so each column $w_y$ of $W$ has a unique expression as a nonnegative combination of the vectors $y'-y$ within its class, which recovers the reactions $y\to y'$ as the face of the linkage cone generated by $w_y$. For generic $\kappa$ these faces are the largest possible, and the realizability cone condition forces the same network for every $\kappa$. The construction yields an algorithm that tests $\mathrm{WR}_0$-realizability and outputs $\mathcal N'$.
