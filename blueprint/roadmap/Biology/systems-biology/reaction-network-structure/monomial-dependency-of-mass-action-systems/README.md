# Monomial dependency of mass-action systems

Equilibrium equations of mass-action reaction networks viewed as generalized polynomial systems. The monomial dependency, which generalizes the deficiency, controls the dimension of the binomial part of the solution set; dependency at most one per independent subnetwork, with sign conditions, gives a unique positive equilibrium in every compatibility class and extends Feinberg's deficiency one theorem.
