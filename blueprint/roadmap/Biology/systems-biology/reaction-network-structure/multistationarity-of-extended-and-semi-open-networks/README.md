# Multistationarity of extended and semi-open networks

How the number of positive steady states of a mass-action network changes when intermediate species are added or when some species are put in exchange with a reservoir. Intermediates can lift nondegenerate multistationarity from a reduced core network, and minimal sets of complexes needing intermediates form circuits of multistationarity; opening substrates preserves the bistability of multisite phosphorylation, while opening independently conserved enzymes yields absolute concentration robustness and monostationarity.
