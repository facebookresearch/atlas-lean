# Absolute concentration robustness

Homeostatic behaviour of reaction networks in which the steady-state concentration of a species is invariant, exactly or asymptotically, under changes in total supply. Asymptotic robustness follows from elimination theory and positive conservation laws, and is generic in biochemical networks with conserved moieties.
