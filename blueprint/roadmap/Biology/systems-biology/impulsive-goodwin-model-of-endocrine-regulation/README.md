# Impulsive Goodwin model of endocrine regulation

Hybrid models of pulsatile hormone secretion in which a linear hormone cascade is driven by impulses whose timing and amplitude are modulated by feedback from the downstream hormone. Periodic solutions with one pulse per period are fixed points of a return map, and their existence, uniqueness and parameter dependence can be established explicitly.
