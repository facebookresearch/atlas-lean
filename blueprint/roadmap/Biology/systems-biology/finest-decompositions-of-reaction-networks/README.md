# Finest decompositions of reaction networks

A decomposition of a reaction network partitions its reaction set into subnetworks. Two algebraic notions single out decompositions that transfer equilibrium information between a network and its parts: independence (the stoichiometric subspaces form a direct sum) and incidence-independence (the images of the incidence maps form a direct sum). Each has a unique finest representative, and the way these finest decompositions sit relative to the linkage-class decomposition classifies networks.
