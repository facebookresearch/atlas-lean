# Nucleated polymerization models of prion strains

Moment-closed ODE models of prion aggregation in which normal monomers are recruited into misfolded aggregates that fragment and are cleared. With several conformational strains competing for the same monomers, at most one strain persists; inserting a nonlinear templating step through an intermediate subunit species makes the disease-free state always locally stable and permits co-stability and coexistence of strains.
