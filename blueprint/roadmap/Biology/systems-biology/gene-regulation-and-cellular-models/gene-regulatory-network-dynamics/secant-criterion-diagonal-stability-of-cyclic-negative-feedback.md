# Secant criterion for diagonal stability of cyclic negative feedback

This node covers the following result. Let $n \ge 3$ and $\alpha_i, \beta_i > 0$. Let $A$ be the $n \times n$ matrix with diagonal entries $-\alpha_1, \dots, -\alpha_n$, entries $\beta_1, \dots, \beta_{n-1}$ in positions $(i+1, i)$, entry $-\beta_n$ in position $(1, n)$ and zeros elsewhere. Then $A$ is diagonally stable, that is $DA + A^{\mathsf T} D \prec 0$ for some positive diagonal matrix $D$, if and only if $\dfrac{\beta_1 \cdots \beta_n}{\alpha_1 \cdots \alpha_n} < \sec(\pi/n)^n$.
