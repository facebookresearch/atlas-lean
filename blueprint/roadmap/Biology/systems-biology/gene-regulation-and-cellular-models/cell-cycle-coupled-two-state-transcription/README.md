# Cell-cycle-coupled two-state transcription

The two-state (telegraph) model of a promoter switching ON and OFF is embedded in a cell cycle with two exponentially timed stages: before replication of the gene (one copy) and after it (two independent copies, followed by binomial partitioning of mRNA at division). Exact stationary moments show that the mean mRNA ratio between the stages is not pinned at the gene-dosage value $2$.
