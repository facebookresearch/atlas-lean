# Delay-induced oscillations in regulatory networks

Time delays in biochemical feedback (transcription, translation, transport) as a source of sustained oscillations. In monotone regulatory systems with an activator–inhibitor or substrate-depletion structure, or with a single negative feedback loop, a delay can destabilize a stable steady state through a Hopf bifurcation but never stabilize an unstable one.
