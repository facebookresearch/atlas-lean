# Identifiability of ERK phosphorylation kinetics

Structural and practical identifiability of mass-action models of the dual phosphorylation of ERK by MEK, and of their quasi-steady-state reductions. Structural identifiability asks whether parameters are determined by noise-free outputs at the measured time points; practical identifiability asks whether likelihood confidence regions are bounded.
