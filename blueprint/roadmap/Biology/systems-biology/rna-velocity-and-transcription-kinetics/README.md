# RNA velocity and transcription kinetics

Linear first-order models of transcription, splicing and degradation, and their closed-form solutions, underlie the estimation of RNA velocity (the time derivative of a cell's expression state) from single-cell data. Metabolic labelling adds a known clock: labelled RNA has been synthesized during an experimentally fixed time window, which turns relative rate constants into absolute ones.
