# Degree-preserving randomization of signed directed networks

Whether a pathway optimization result or a network statistic is meaningful is often judged against a null model of randomized networks that keep each node's connectivity. For signed, directed networks such as signalling pathways, the relevant connectivity is the functional characterization of each entity: how often it activates, inhibits, is activated and is inhibited. Such randomization reduces to the edge-switching Markov chain on two bipartite graphs, with an explicit estimate of the number of switching steps needed to forget the original network.
