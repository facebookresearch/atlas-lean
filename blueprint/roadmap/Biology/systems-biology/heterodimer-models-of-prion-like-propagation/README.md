# Heterodimer models of prion-like propagation

In heterodimer models a healthy protein is produced at a constant rate and degraded linearly, and is converted into a toxic, self-templating form on contact with toxic particles. With a slow conversion rate $\varepsilon$, the dynamics pass through a healthy phase, a mixed phase in which the toxic species grows exponentially at rate $f(v_0)u_0 - 1$ on the slow time scale, and a toxic phase in which it saturates logistically near the toxic steady state; matched asymptotics give these growth laws in closed form.
