# Crossing speed of the eigenvalue curve

For `n ∈ ℕ` and real `β` and `μ`, `s(μ) = a'(μ) β c / w`, where `a'` is the
[derivative of the loop gain](gain-deriv.md), `c = 1/2 + i √3/2` and `w = (1 + β) + 2 i ω(a(μ), β)`
is the [square root of the discriminant](hopf-root.md) at the loop gain `a(μ)`; with the convention
`x / 0 = 0`, `s(μ) = 0` when `w = 0`. At a Hopf level `μ > 0` with `β > 0` it is the
[derivative](has-deriv-at-hopf-eigenvalue.md) of the eigenvalue curve.
