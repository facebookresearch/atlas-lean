# Matrix of the linearization at a nonnegative symmetric equilibrium

For `n ∈ ℕ`, a real `β` and `p ≥ 0`, the matrix of the derivative at `(p, …, p)` of the
[six-variable field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n`, rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` and ratio `β`
is the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a(p), β)`, where `a` is the
[loop gain](../three-variable-repressilator/gain.md) `a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`.
