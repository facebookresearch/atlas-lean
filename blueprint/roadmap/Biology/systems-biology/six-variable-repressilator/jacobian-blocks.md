# Block form of the six-variable Jacobian matrix

For real `a` and `β`, the block matrix `[[−I, −a P], [β I, −β I]]` indexed by two copies of
`{0, 1, 2}` (the mRNA block first), where `I` is the `3 × 3` identity and `P` the
[cyclic shift](shift.md). Its [reindexing](jacobian-eq-reindex.md) to `{0, …, 5}` is the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a, β)`.
