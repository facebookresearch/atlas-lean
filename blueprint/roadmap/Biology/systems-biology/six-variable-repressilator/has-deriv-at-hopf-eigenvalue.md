# Derivative of the eigenvalue curve at a positive Hopf level

For `n ∈ ℕ`, `β > 0` and `μ > 0` satisfying the [Hopf condition](is-hopf.md)
`3 a(μ)² β = (4 − 2 a(μ)) (1 + β)²`, where `a` is the
[loop gain](../three-variable-repressilator/gain.md) `a(μ) = n μⁿ / (1 + μⁿ)`, the
[eigenvalue curve](hopf-eigenvalue.md) `λ` has derivative `s(μ) = a'(μ) β c / w` at `μ`, the
[crossing speed](hopf-speed.md), with `w = (1 + β) + 2iω`.
