# Factorization of the characteristic polynomial over ℂ

For all real `a` and `β`, the characteristic polynomial `det(X I − J(a, β))` of the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a, β)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, is
`(Q + a β)(Q − a β c)(Q − a β c̄)`, where `Q = (X + 1)(X + β)` is the [quadratic](quad.md),
`c = 1/2 + i √3/2` and `c̄ = 1/2 − i √3/2`.
