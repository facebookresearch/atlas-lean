# Parameters and states with positive concentrations

The set of pairs `(μ, x) ∈ ℝ × ℝ⁶` with every `x_j > 0`; the parameter `μ` is arbitrary. The proof
of the [periodic-orbit theorem](exists-periodic-orbit.md) applies the Hopf theorem on this set, to
the map `(μ, x) ↦ F(x)` with `F` the [six-variable field](vector-field.md) with rate `μ (1 + μⁿ)`
and ratio `β`.
