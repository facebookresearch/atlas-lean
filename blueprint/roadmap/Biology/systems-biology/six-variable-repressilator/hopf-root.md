# Square root of the discriminant at a Hopf point

For real `a` and `β`, the complex number `w = (1 + β) + 2 i ω`, where `ω` is the
[Hopf frequency](hopf-freq.md) `ω = √3 a β / (2 (1 + β))`. When `β > 0` and the
[Hopf condition](is-hopf.md) `3 a² β = (4 − 2 a) (1 + β)²` holds, `w² = (1 − β)² + 4 a β c` with
`c = 1/2 + i √3/2`.
