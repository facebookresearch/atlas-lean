# The complex number (1 + β) + 2iω

For real `a` and `β`, the complex number `w = (1 + β) + 2 i ω`, where `ω` is the
[Hopf frequency](hopf-freq.md) `ω = √3 a β / (2 (1 + β))`. At the loop gain `a = a(μ)` it is the
denominator of the [crossing speed](hopf-speed.md) `s(μ) = a'(μ) β c / w` of the eigenvalue curve.
