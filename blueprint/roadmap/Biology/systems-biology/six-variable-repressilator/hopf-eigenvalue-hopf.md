# The eigenvalue curve passes through iω at a Hopf level

For `n ∈ ℕ`, `β > 0` and a real `μ` satisfying the [Hopf condition](is-hopf.md)
`3 a(μ)² β = (4 − 2 a(μ)) (1 + β)²`, where `a` is the
[loop gain](../three-variable-repressilator/gain.md) `a(μ) = n μⁿ / (1 + μⁿ)`, the
[eigenvalue curve](hopf-eigenvalue.md) satisfies `λ(μ) = iω`, with `ω = ω(a(μ), β)` the
[Hopf frequency](hopf-freq.md).
