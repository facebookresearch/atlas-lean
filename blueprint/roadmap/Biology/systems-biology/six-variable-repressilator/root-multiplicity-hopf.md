# At a Hopf point with a > 0, iω is a simple eigenvalue

For `a > 0` and `β > 0` satisfying the [Hopf condition](is-hopf.md) `3 a² β = (4 − 2 a) (1 + β)²`,
`iω` is a root of multiplicity `1` of the characteristic polynomial `det(X I − J(a, β))` of the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a, β)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, where `ω` is the
[Hopf frequency](hopf-freq.md) `ω = √3 a β / (2 (1 + β))`.
