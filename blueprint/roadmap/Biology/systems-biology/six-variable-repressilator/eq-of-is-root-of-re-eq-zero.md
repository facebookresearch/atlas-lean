# For a ≥ 0 and β > 0, imaginary-axis eigenvalues are ±iω

For `a ≥ 0` and `β > 0`, every complex root `z` of the characteristic polynomial
`det(X I − J(a, β))` of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a, β)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, with
`Re z = 0` is `iω` or `−iω`, where `ω` is the [Hopf frequency](hopf-freq.md)
`ω = √3 a β / (2 (1 + β))`. No Hopf condition is assumed; off the boundary there may be no such
root.
