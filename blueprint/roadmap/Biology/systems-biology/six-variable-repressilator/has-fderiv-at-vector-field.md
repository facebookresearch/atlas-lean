# Linearization at a nonnegative symmetric equilibrium

For `n ∈ ℕ`, a real `β` and `p ≥ 0`, the [six-variable field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient
`n`, rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` and ratio `β` is differentiable at `(p, …, p)` with derivative the
[linear map](jacobian-clm.md) `v ↦ J(a(p), β) v`, where `J` is the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md)
and `a` is the [loop gain](../three-variable-repressilator/gain.md) `a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`.
