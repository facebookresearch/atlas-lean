# Symmetric equilibrium with p ≥ 0 inside the boundary: linear stability

For `n ∈ ℕ`, `β > 0` and `p ≥ 0` with `3 a² β < (4 − 2 a) (1 + β)²`, where `a = a(p)` is the
[loop gain](../three-variable-repressilator/gain.md) `a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`, every complex root
`z` of the
[characteristic polynomial of the linearization](../three-variable-repressilator/lin-charpoly.md) of
the [six-variable field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n`, rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` and ratio
`β` at the symmetric state `(p, …, p)` satisfies `Re z < 0`.
