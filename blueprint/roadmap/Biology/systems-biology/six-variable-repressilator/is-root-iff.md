# Eigenvalues as roots of three quadratic equations

For all real `a` and `β` and every `z ∈ ℂ`, `z` is a root of the characteristic polynomial
`det(X I − J(a, β))` of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a, β)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, if
and only if `(z + 1)(z + β)` equals `−a β`, `a β c` or `a β c̄`, where `c = 1/2 + i √3/2` and
`c̄ = 1/2 − i √3/2`.
