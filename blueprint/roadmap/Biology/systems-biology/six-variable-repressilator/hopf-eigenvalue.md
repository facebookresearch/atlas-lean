# Eigenvalue curve of the six-variable repressilator

For `n ∈ ℕ` and real `β` and `μ`, `λ(μ) = (−(1 + β) + √D(μ)) / 2`, where `D` is the
[discriminant](disc.md) and `√` is the principal square root `√z = exp((log z)/2)` (argument in
`(−π/2, π/2]`, and `√0 = 0`). It [solves](quad-hopf-eigenvalue.md) `(λ + 1)(λ + β) = a(μ) β c` for
every `μ`.
