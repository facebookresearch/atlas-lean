# The standard regime n = 2, α = 216, β = 5 is linearly unstable

Let `p > 0` with `p (1 + p²) = 216`. Then some complex root `z` of the
[characteristic polynomial of the linearization](../three-variable-repressilator/lin-charpoly.md) of
the [six-variable field](vector-field.md) with `n = 2`, `α = 216` and `β = 5` at `(p, …, p)`
satisfies `Re z > 0`. These are Elowitz and Leibler's standard parameters.
