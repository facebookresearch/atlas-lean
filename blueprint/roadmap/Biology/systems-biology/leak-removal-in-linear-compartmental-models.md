# Leak removal in linear compartmental models

Bortner–Meshkat Conjecture 5.7 concerns how pharmacokinetic compartment models can be simplified without changing the dimension of their input–output coefficient map.

## What would settle it

Preserve the complete hypotheses: an output-connectable graph, one output, and retained leak set L containing every input and output. Prove equality of coefficient-map image dimensions between the all-leaks model and the L-leaks model, equivalently suitable generic Jacobian ranks. It is not the unrestricted claim that removing any leak preserves identifiability.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

Bortner and Meshkat state this as Conjecture 5.7 and prove it as Theorem 5.8 in the case where the image dimension equals |E| + |In ∪ Out|.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
