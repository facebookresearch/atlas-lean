# Approximate majority reaction networks

The approximate majority (AM) population protocol, written as a four-reaction catalytic network, converts the minority of two species into the majority quickly, reliably and robustly. Successive network transformations under biochemical constraints turn it into the core cell-cycle (Cdk/Wee1/Cdc25) switch, which inherits its switching behaviour.
