# Describing function approximation of biomolecular systems

The describing function replaces a nonlinear input–output system driven by a (biased) sinusoid with the gain and phase of the first harmonic of its periodic output. Applied to enzymatic and covalent-modification signalling, hysteretic positive-feedback switches and ring oscillators, it gives amplitude-dependent frequency responses, and the Fourier tail bound for functions of bounded variation turns it into an approximation with a computable error that propagates into limit-cycle predictions.
