# Extreme first passage times of piecewise deterministic searchers

Statistics of the fastest of many searchers when each searcher moves at finite speed, as in run-and-tumble bacteria, motor-driven cargo or sperm cells, modeled as piecewise deterministic Markov processes. Unlike diffusion, a single searcher has a strictly positive minimal arrival time, which sets the extreme first passage time and fixes the scaling of its fluctuations.
