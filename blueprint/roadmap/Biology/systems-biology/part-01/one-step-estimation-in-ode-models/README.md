# One-step estimation in ODE models

Parameter estimation in ordinary differential equation models of biological systems by a single Newton correction of a cheap preliminary estimator. Started from a root-n consistent smoothing-based estimator, the one-step estimator is asymptotically as efficient as nonlinear least squares.
