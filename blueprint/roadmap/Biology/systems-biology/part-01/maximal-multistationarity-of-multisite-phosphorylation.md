# Maximal multistationarity of multisite phosphorylation

Can the sequential, distributive n-site phosphorylation network attain its upper bound of 2n−1 positive steady states for every n? This probes how many distinct signaling states such a mechanism can support; steady-state count is not the number of stable memory states.

## What would settle it

For n=5, a concrete first target is positive rate constants and conserved totals yielding exactly nine distinct positive steady states. Certify roots, positivity, conservation laws, and the correspondence between any reduced polynomial and the full network. The all-n conjecture requires a uniform construction.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status. The supporting open-problem statement is older or its status evidence is incomplete; check later literature before relying on it.

The upper bound of 2n − 1 positive steady states is due to Wang and Sontag. Flockerzi, Holstein and Conradi exhibit a 3-site system with 5 steady states. Feliu, Kaihnsa, de Wolff and Yürük (2023) state that attaining 2n − 1 is conjectured and established only for n ≤ 4.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
