# Phototransduction recovery kinetics

After a saturating flash, a rod photoreceptor's circulating current stays suppressed for a time that grows with flash strength and then recovers. The shape-preserving shift of recovery curves with flash intensity pins down an exponential decay of a transduction intermediate, and in a cascade of first-order reactions the slowest stage sets that decay at long times.
