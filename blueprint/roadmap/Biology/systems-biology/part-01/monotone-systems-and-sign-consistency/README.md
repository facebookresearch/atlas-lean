# Monotone systems and sign consistency

Biochemical systems whose signed species graph admits a consistent spin assignment are monotone with respect to an orthant order: perturbations propagate predictably and bounded solutions generically converge to equilibria. The consistency deficit measures how far a network is from this class, and monotone input-output blocks connected in feedback can be analyzed from their steady-state characteristics alone.
