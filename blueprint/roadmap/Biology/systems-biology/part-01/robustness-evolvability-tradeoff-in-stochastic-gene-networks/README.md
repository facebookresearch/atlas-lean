# Robustness–evolvability tradeoff in stochastic gene networks

Linearized gene regulatory networks with random kinetic-parameter fluctuations modelled as multiplicative Itô noise; Lyapunov matrix inequalities certify robustness to genetic fluctuations and bound the attenuation of environmental disturbances, exposing a tradeoff between the two.
