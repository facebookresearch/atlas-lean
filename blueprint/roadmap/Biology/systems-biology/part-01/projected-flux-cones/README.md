# Projected flux cones

Projecting the steady-state flux cone of a metabolic network onto the coordinates of a subnetwork of interest gives a lower-dimensional polyhedral cone whose elementary modes (ProCEMs) can be computed by block elimination without enumerating all elementary modes of the full network. ProCEMs are projections of elementary modes, and every elementary flux pattern of the subnetwork is the support of a ProCEM.
