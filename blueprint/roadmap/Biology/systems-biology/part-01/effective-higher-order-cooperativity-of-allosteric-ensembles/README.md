# Effective higher-order cooperativity of allosteric ensembles

A target molecule with $n$ ligand-binding sites that switches among conformations integrates binding information through cooperativity: binding at a set $S$ of sites changes the association constant at another site. Coarse-graining the conformational ensemble at thermodynamic equilibrium gives effective association constants and effective higher-order cooperativities (HOCs). For Monod–Wyman–Changeux-like ensembles the effective HOCs are ratios of moments of the conformational association constants and grow with the order; with enough conformations and no intrinsic cooperativity at all, any pattern of effective HOCs can be realised.
