# Ribosome flow model with extended objects and drop-off

A deterministic mean-field model of unidirectional transport of particles that cover $\ell$ consecutive sites (ribosomes on mRNA, motor proteins on filaments), that can detach from any site, and that may attract or repel their neighbours. The state is confined to a compact convex invariant set, the dynamics are contractive after an arbitrarily short transient, and the steady state is unique and globally attracting; in the interaction-free case the steady-state production rate is monotone in every rate.
