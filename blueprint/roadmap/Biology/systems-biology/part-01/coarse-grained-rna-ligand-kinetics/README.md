# Coarse-grained RNA–ligand kinetics

RNA folding kinetics is modelled as a continuous-time Markov process on secondary structures with Metropolis rates; coarse graining into gradient-basin macrostates makes it tractable. Adding a small-molecule ligand that binds only structures carrying a binding pocket couples a "monomer" and a "dimer" energy landscape. The macrorates inside the dimer landscape coincide with those of the corresponding monomer structures, association and dissociation macrorates are ratios of restricted partition functions, and under ligand excess the whole system becomes a linear master equation whose rate matrix depends affinely on the ligand concentration.
