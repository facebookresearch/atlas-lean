# Two-compartment pump–leak model

Pump–leak equations describe how a cell regulates its ion concentrations and volume by balancing passive ion leaks, active Na$^+$/K$^+$-ATPase pumping and osmotic water flow. Coupling an epithelial cell to an enclosed lumen gives a ten-dimensional system in which, without active transport, both compartments relax to an explicit Donnan equilibrium fixed by the bath composition and the valence of each compartment's impermeant solute.
