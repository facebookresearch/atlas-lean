# Extended Permanence Conjecture

Must every endotactic mass-action reaction network be permanent, including the source's time-varying-rate setting? Permanence means concentrations eventually stay uniformly away from zero and infinity within a fixed stoichiometric class. It concerns robustness of biochemical dynamics, not convergence to one equilibrium.

## What would settle it

For each finite endotactic network and each admissible κ-variable rate function, prove global existence and permanence on every positive stoichiometric class. Preserve the source's piecewise-differentiable rates bounded away from zero and infinity and its quantifier order: fix the system and class before obtaining the eventual compact region. Do not silently strengthen this to one region uniform over every possible rate function.

## Status

The cited sources pose this as an open question in the versions cited; this page does not certify its present status.

Craciun, Nazarov and Pantea state the Extended Permanence Conjecture (§4) and prove persistence and permanence for two species. Feliu (2026 preprint) lists it among open boundary conjectures for reaction networks and distinguishes it from the cases treated there.

This page records the cited research question and asserts no theorem or present-status certification.
