# Stochastic synchronization of genetic oscillators

Synchronization of networks of identical genetic oscillators (for example coupled repressilators or circadian clocks) driven by additive noise. Synchronization is measured in an $H_\infty$-like sense, with the weighted synchronization error bounded by a multiple of the noise energy, and sufficient conditions follow from a quadratic Lyapunov function; for oscillators written as Lur'e systems with Hill-type nonlinearities the conditions become linear matrix inequalities.
