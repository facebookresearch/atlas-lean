# Frequency–amplitude coordination of biological oscillators

Tuning the frequency and amplitude of a biological oscillation (neuronal, circadian or biochemical) independently by a linear intervention on the interactions of its components. For two-component reaction–diffusion oscillators born in a Hopf bifurcation, spectral analysis of the linearized operator and the Poincaré normal form express frequency and amplitude in terms of the intervention intensities.
