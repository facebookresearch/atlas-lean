# Chemical organization theory

Chemical organization theory studies reaction networks through sets of species that are closed (they produce nothing outside themselves) and self-maintaining (every consumed species can be regenerated at a nonnegative net rate). The species present at any fixed point of the kinetics form an organization, so the lattice of organizations constrains the possible steady states from network structure and stoichiometry alone, without rate constants.
