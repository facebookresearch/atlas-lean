# Opposer and balancer modules of robust perfect adaptation

A network exhibits robust perfect adaptation (RPA) when its output returns to the same steady state after any persistent change of input, without fine-tuned parameters. For networks of arbitrary size, RPA reduces to an algebraic condition on the steady-state Jacobian, and its solutions decompose into exactly two kinds of modules: feedback-based Opposer Modules and feedforward-route-based Balancer Modules, each implementing a distinct form of integral control. For the linear integral-feedback view of perfect adaptation see [Perfect adaptation](../../integral-feedback-and-perfect-adaptation/has-perfect-adaptation.md) and [Integral feedback is necessary](../../integral-feedback-and-perfect-adaptation/has-integral-feedback-iff-has-perfect-ad.md).
