# Glucose–insulin regulation with the β-cell cycle

Ordinary differential equation models coupling blood glucose, blood insulin, an insulin storage of releasable and reserve granules, and the proliferation cycle of pancreatic β-cells. Glucose accelerates the passage of β-cells from growth into DNA synthesis, which creates a glucose threshold separating β-cell mass expansion from decline and pins the non-trivial steady state.
