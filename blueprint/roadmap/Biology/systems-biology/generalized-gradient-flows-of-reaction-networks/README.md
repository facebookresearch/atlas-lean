# Generalized gradient flows of reaction networks

Reversible mass-action networks with a detailed-balanced equilibrium can be written as generalized gradient flows driven by the Kullback-Leibler divergence to equilibrium. This structure yields convergence-rate bounds for relaxation to equilibrium in terms of the stoichiometric matrix and reaction activities.
