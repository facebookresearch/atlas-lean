# Travelling waves of mutant epithelial cells in inflammatory bowel disease

A reaction–diffusion model of inflammatory bowel disease in which death-resistant mutant epithelial cells and immune cells spread together along the intestine. Geometric singular perturbation reduces the dynamics to a scalar reaction–diffusion equation whose nonlinearity is bistable with cut-off, cubic Fisher, or KPP depending on a single bifurcation parameter.
