# Pseudo-RAF sets of catalytic reaction systems

Reflexively autocatalytic food-generated (RAF) sets formalize self-sustaining, collectively catalytic reaction networks. Relaxing food generation to "every reactant and some catalyst is food or produced inside the set" gives pseudo-RAFs, whose maximum is computable by Horn-clause propagation without closure computations; alternating the two prunings recovers the maximum RAF faster. Limited inhibition reduces to RAF detection over subsets of the inhibitor set.
