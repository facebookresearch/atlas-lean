# Mass-balanced randomization of metabolic networks

Null models for metabolic networks that keep every reaction chemically feasible. Compounds are vectors of element counts; substituting a substrate or product by another compound with a proportional mass vector, and re-solving for stoichiometric coefficients, preserves mass balance and reaction degrees. The resulting Markov chain on networks samples uniformly when only single compounds are substituted.
