# Oscillations in deficiency-one mass-action systems

Planar mass-action reaction networks of deficiency one with a unique positive equilibrium can, unlike deficiency-zero networks, oscillate: they admit stable and multiple limit cycles, centers and homoclinic orbits depending on rate constants. Geometric conditions on the complexes can still guarantee global stability.
