# Hierarchic first-order reaction networks

Stochastic reaction networks whose reactions each consume at most one molecule, arranged so that an independent subnetwork drives a dependent one through catalytic and splitting reactions, have chemical master equations with closed-form generating functions. The independent species are Poisson or binomial, while the dependent species follow generalized (compound) distributions that can be multimodal even in simple gene-expression models.
