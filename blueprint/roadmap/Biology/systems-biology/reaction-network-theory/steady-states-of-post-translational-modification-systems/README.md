# Steady states of post-translational modification systems

Algebraic descriptions of the steady states of substrates that are reversibly modified at several sites by opposing enzymes, with arbitrary enzyme mechanisms summarized by generalized Michaelis–Menten parameters. These reductions turn questions of multistationarity and parametric robustness into the study of low-dimensional polynomial systems.
