# Minimal experiments for identifiable compartmental models

This node records a research direction: for a linear compartmental model with a given graph, which placements of inputs and outputs are minimal among those that make the parameters identifiable?

## What would settle it

Fix a family of graphs and the notion of identifiability (generic local or generic global). Then give a structural characterization of the minimal input and output configurations yielding it, together with an efficient method that finds them. Enumerating designs and running an existing identifiability test does not by itself answer the structural question.

## Status

Question 5.4 below explicitly poses the generic-local version. The generic-global variant and the request for an efficient design method are extensions considered here, not part of that question's wording. This page does not certify their present status or novelty.

Meshkat and Shiu survey structural identifiability of compartmental models and pose, as Question 5.4, which minimal configurations of inputs and outputs make a model with a given graph generically locally identifiable.
