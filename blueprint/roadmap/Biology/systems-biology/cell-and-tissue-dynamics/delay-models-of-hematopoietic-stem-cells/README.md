# Delay models of hematopoietic stem cells

Models of blood-cell production in the bone marrow in which resting stem cells enter a proliferating phase of finite (possibly distributed or maturity-dependent) duration and divide into two daughters. The cell-cycle delay drives stability changes and oscillations associated with periodic hematological diseases.
