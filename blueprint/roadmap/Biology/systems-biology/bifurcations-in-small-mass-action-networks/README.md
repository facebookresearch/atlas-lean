# Bifurcations in small mass-action networks

Which local bifurcations of positive equilibria can occur in mass-action reaction networks with very few species and reactions. Rescaling by monomials of the rate constants reduces an $(n, m, n)$ network to an $(m-2)$-parameter family of vector fields, which caps the codimension of bifurcations and forbids cusps when $m = n + 2$; planar quadratic networks with four reactions realize exactly fold, Andronov–Hopf, Bogdanov–Takens and Bautin bifurcations.
