# Linear framework for gene regulation

The states of a regulatory DNA region (which transcription factors are bound, nucleosome and chromatin marks, polymerase status) form a labelled directed graph whose edge labels are transition rates. The master equation for microstate probabilities is linear in the graph Laplacian; its steady states are given by equilibrium path products under detailed balance and by rooted-spanning-tree sums away from equilibrium, and independent regulatory modules combine by Kronecker products.
