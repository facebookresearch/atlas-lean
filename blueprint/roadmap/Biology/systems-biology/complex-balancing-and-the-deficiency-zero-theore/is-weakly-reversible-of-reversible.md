# Reversible networks are weakly reversible

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and reaction set $\rho$. If every reaction $r$ has a reverse reaction, that is, a
reaction $r'$ with $\mathrm{src}(r') = \mathrm{prod}(r)$ and $\mathrm{prod}(r') = \mathrm{src}(r)$,
then $N$ is [weakly reversible](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/is-weakly-reversible.md).
