# Equality in the Horn–Jackson inequality

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$, let $k_r > 0$ be rate constants and let $c^* \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ be [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md) for $k$. For every $c \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$, the
[rate of change of the pseudo-Helmholtz function](helmholtz-rate.md) vanishes at $c$ if and only if
$c$ is an equilibrium of the [mass-action vector field](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/mass-action-field.md) $f$, $f(c) = 0$ (Horn and Jackson 1972).
