# Horn–Jackson

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$, let $k_r > 0$ be rate constants and let $c^* \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ be [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md) for $k$. Then every $c \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ with $f(c) = 0$, where $f$ is the
[mass-action vector field](mass-action-field.md), is complex balanced for $k$: if a mass-action system with positive rate constants has a
positive complex-balanced equilibrium, every positive equilibrium is complex balanced.

## Sources

- F. Horn and R. Jackson, *General mass action kinetics*, Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 47 (1972) 81–116.
- M. Feinberg, *Foundations of Chemical Reaction Network Theory*, Springer (2019), Chapter 15.
