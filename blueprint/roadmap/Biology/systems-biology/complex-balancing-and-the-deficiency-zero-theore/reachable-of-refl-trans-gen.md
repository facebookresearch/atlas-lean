# A directed reaction path from a complex stays in its linkage class

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and reaction set $\rho$, let $y$ be a
[complex](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md) of $N$ and let $y' \in \mathbb{N}^S$. If
there is a directed path of reactions $y = y_0 \to y_1 \to \dots \to y_m = y'$ with $m \ge 0$ (see
the [reaction relation](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reacts.md)), then $y'$ is also a complex
of $N$, and $y$ and $y'$ lie in the same linkage class: they are joined by a path in the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md).
