# At least as many linkage classes as pairwise unreachable complexes

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and reaction set $\rho$, with finitely many
[complexes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md), and let $\ell$ be its
[number of linkage classes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/num-linkage-classes.md). If $T$ is a
finite set of complexes of $N$ no two distinct members of which lie in the same linkage class (no
path in the [linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md) joins two distinct
members of $T$), then $|T| \le \ell$.
