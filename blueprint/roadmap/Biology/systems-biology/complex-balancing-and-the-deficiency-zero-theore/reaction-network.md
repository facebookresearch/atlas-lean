# Chemical reaction network

A chemical reaction network on a set $S$ of species, with reactions indexed by a set $\rho$, assigns to
each reaction $r$ a source (reactant) complex $\mathrm{src}(r) \in \mathbb{N}^S$ and a product complex
$\mathrm{prod}(r) \in \mathbb{N}^S$, the vectors of stoichiometric coefficients: reaction $r$ turns
$\mathrm{src}(r)$ into $\mathrm{prod}(r)$.
