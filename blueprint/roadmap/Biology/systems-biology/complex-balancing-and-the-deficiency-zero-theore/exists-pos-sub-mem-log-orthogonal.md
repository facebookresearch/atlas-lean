# Birch's theorem, existence

Let $S$ be a finite set, $W$ a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^S$, and $a, b \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$. Then
there is $c \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ with $c - b \in W$ such that the vector
$(\log c_s - \log a_s)_{s \in S}$ is orthogonal to $W$ for the dot product (Birch 1963; Horn and
Jackson 1972).
