# Horn–Jackson: one equilibrium per compatibility class

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$, let $k_r > 0$ be rate constants and let $c^* \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ be [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md) for $k$. Then for every $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ there is exactly one
$x \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ with $x - x_0$ in the [stoichiometric subspace](stoichiometric-subspace.md)
and $f(x) = 0$, where $f$ is the [mass-action vector field](mass-action-field.md): each positive stoichiometric compatibility class
contains exactly one positive equilibrium (Horn and Jackson 1972; Feinberg 2019, ch. 15).
