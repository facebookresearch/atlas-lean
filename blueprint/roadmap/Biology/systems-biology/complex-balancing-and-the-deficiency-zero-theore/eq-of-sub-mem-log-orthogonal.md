# Birch's theorem, uniqueness

Let $S$ be a finite set, $W$ a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^S$, and $c, d \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$. If
$c - d \in W$ and the vector $(\log c_s - \log d_s)_{s \in S}$ is orthogonal to $W$ for the dot
product, then $c = d$.

## Sources

- M. W. Birch, *Maximum likelihood in three-way contingency tables*, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 25 (1963) 220–233.
- F. Horn and R. Jackson, *General mass action kinetics*, Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 47 (1972) 81–116.
