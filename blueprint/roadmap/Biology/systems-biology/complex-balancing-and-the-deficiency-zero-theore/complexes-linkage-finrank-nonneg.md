# Networks with finitely many reactions have nonnegative deficiency

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. Let $n$ be the number of its
[complexes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md), which are finitely many, $\ell$ the
number of connected components of its
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md) and $s$ the dimension of its
[stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md). Then
$n - \ell - s \ge 0$, computed in the integers: the
[deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) of $N$ is nonnegative. This is the
case of [Nonnegative deficiency for indexed complexes](card-sub-card-sub-finrank-nonneg.md) in which
the complexes are indexed by themselves.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Foundations of Chemical Reaction Network Theory*, Springer (2019), § 6.4.
- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal
  reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987)
  2229–2268, § 3.
