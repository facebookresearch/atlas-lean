# Deficiency-zero theorem, non-weakly-reversible case

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. If $N$ has [deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) zero and is not
[weakly reversible](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/is-weakly-reversible.md), then for every choice of rate constants $k_r > 0$ the
[mass-action vector field](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/mass-action-field.md) $f$ has no zero in $\mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$: there is no positive equilibrium.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987) 2229–2268.
