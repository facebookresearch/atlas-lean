# A network with linearly independent complexes has deficiency zero

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. If its
[complexes](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md), viewed as vectors of $\mathbb{R}^S$,
are linearly independent over $\mathbb{R}$, then $n - \ell - s = 0$, computed in the integers, where
$n$ is the number of complexes, $\ell$ the number of connected components of the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md) and $s$ the dimension of the
[stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md): the
[deficiency](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/deficiency.md) of $N$ is zero. This is the case of
[Linearly independent indexed complexes give deficiency zero](card-sub-card-sub-finrank-eq-zero-of-lin.md)
in which the complexes are indexed by themselves.

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Foundations of Chemical Reaction Network Theory*, Springer (2019), § 6.4.
