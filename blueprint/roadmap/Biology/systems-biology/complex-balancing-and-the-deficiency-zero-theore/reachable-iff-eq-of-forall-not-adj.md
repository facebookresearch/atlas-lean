# A complex isolated in the linkage graph is alone in its linkage class

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and reaction set $\rho$, and let $c$ be a
[complex](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/complexes.md) of $N$ adjacent to no complex in the
[linkage graph](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/linkage-graph.md) of $N$; for example, a complex
that occurs only in reactions $c \to c$ whose source and product coincide. Then for every complex
$c'$ of $N$, the complexes $c$ and $c'$ are joined by a path in the linkage graph if and only if
$c' = c$: the linkage class of $c$ is $\{c\}$.
