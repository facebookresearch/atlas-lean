# Horn–Feinberg: complex balancing for every choice of rate constants

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. If $N$ has [deficiency](deficiency.md) zero and is
[weakly reversible](is-weakly-reversible.md), then for every choice of rate constants $k_r > 0$ there
is a concentration vector $c \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ that is [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md)
for $k$. Together with the [Horn–Jackson theorem](exists-unique-equilibrium-of-is-complex.md) this gives
the [deficiency-zero theorem](exists-unique-equilibrium.md).

## Sources

- M. Feinberg, *Complex balancing in general kinetic systems*, Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 49 (1972) 187–194.
- M. Feinberg, *Chemical reaction network structure and the stability of complex isothermal reactors I. The deficiency zero and deficiency one theorems*, Chem. Eng. Sci. 42 (1987) 2229–2268.
