# Stoichiometric matrix rank equals stoichiometric subspace dimension

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with
species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$, and let $\Gamma$ be its stoichiometric matrix: the
real $S \times \rho$ matrix whose column $r$ is the reaction vector
$\mathrm{prod}(r) - \mathrm{src}(r)$. The rank of $\Gamma$ over $\mathbb{R}$ equals the dimension of
the [stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md) of $N$.
