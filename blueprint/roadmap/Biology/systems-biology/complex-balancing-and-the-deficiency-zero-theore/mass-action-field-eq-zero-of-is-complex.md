# Complex-balanced states are equilibria

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. For all rate constants $k \in \mathbb{R}^\rho$ and concentrations $c \in \mathbb{R}^S$, if
$c$ is [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md) for $k$, then the [mass-action vector field](mass-action-field.md) $f$ vanishes at $c$
(Horn and Jackson 1972).
