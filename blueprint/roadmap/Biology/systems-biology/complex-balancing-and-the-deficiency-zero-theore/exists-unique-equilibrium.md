# Deficiency-zero theorem, weakly reversible case

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$. If $N$ has [deficiency](deficiency.md) zero and is
[weakly reversible](is-weakly-reversible.md), then for all rate constants $k_r > 0$ and every
$x_0 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ there is exactly one $x \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ with $x - x_0$ in the
[stoichiometric subspace](stoichiometric-subspace.md) and $f(x) = 0$, where $f$ is the [mass-action vector field](mass-action-field.md).
