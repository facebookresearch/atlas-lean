## Related topics

- [Deficiency-one theory of reaction networks](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/deficiency-one-theory-of-reaction-networks/README.md): for finitely many species and reactions, the deficiencies of the linkage classes sum to at most the deficiency of the network, with equality exactly when the stoichiometric subspaces of the linkage classes are independent, and then the equilibrium equations decouple across linkage classes: the linear algebra of the condition `∑_θ δ_θ = δ` of Feinberg's deficiency-one theorem.
- [Product-form laws on stoichiometric compatibility classes](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/product-form-laws-on-stoichiometric-compatibilit/README.md): for finitely many species and reactions and a complex-balanced state with positive coordinates, the product-form Poisson law conditioned on the stoichiometric compatibility class of a state is a probability law on that class of molecule counts that solves the stationary chemical master equation; for positive rate constants it does not depend on which positive complex-balanced state is used.
- [Ergodic theorem for complex-balanced reaction networks](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/ergodic-theorem-for-complex-balanced-reaction-ne/README.md): for finitely many species and reactions, the product-form Poisson weights of a complex-balanced state solve the stationary chemical master equation (Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz); for nonnegative rate constants and a complex-balanced state with positive coordinates, a jointly measurable non-explosive process on molecule counts, defined on a probability space, that has the product-form law at every time `t ≥ 0` and regenerates at a state is positive recurrent, and the time averages of each bounded observable converge almost surely to its product-form mean.
- [Regeneration of stochastic reaction networks](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/regeneration-of-stochastic-reaction-networks/README.md): the same limits for the stochastic mass-action chain itself, from every initial state, for finitely many species and reactions, nonnegative rate constants and a complex-balanced state with positive coordinates, under a Foster–Lyapunov drift bound `L V ≤ a V + b` with `∑_s x_s ≤ V(x)` and the reachability of every state from the regeneration state along reactions of positive propensity.
- [Kurtz's law of large numbers for reaction networks](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/kurtz-s-law-of-large-numbers-for-reaction-networ/README.md): for finitely many species and reactions and nonnegative rate constants, random count paths that solve the random time-change equations of the stochastic mass-action network at system size `N`, driven by unit-rate Poisson processes, converge after scaling by `N`, in probability and uniformly on every `[0, T]`, to a solution `x` of the mass-action equation defined for all `t ≥ 0`, when the scaled initial states converge in probability to `x(0)`.
- [Non-explosion of stochastic reaction networks](../../../Chemistry/chemical-kinetics/non-explosion-of-stochastic-reaction-networks/README.md): a Foster–Lyapunov criterion: if a nonnegative function `V` satisfies `L V ≤ a V + b` with `a, b ≥ 0` and the total propensity is bounded on each sublevel set `{V < m}`, the jump times of the jump–hold chain of a stochastic reaction network tend to `∞` almost surely from every initial state.
