# Horn–Jackson: the positive equilibria

Let $N$ be a [chemical reaction network](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/reaction-network.md) with finite species set $S$ and finite reaction set $\rho$, let $k_r > 0$ be rate constants and let $c^* \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ be [complex balanced](is-complex-balanced.md) for $k$. Then the positive equilibria of the [mass-action vector field](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/mass-action-field.md) $f$ are exactly the
$c \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^S$ for which the vector $(\log c_s - \log c^*_s)_{s \in S}$ is orthogonal, for
the dot product, to the [stoichiometric subspace](../../../Dynamics/smooth-dynamics/stoichiometric-subspace.md) (Horn and Jackson 1972;
Feinberg 2019, ch. 15).
