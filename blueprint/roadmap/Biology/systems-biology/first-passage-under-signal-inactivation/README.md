# First passage under signal inactivation

A signaling molecule that must diffuse from the cell membrane to the nucleus while being inactivated at a constant rate reaches its target less often but faster. Monotonicity in the inactivation rate and a strong-inactivation limit governed by shortest-path distances explain how inactivation sharpens signals and buffers the effect of organelle barriers.
