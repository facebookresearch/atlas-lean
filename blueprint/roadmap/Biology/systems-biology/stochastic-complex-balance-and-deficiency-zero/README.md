# Stochastic complex balance and deficiency zero

Stochastic (continuous-time Markov chain) counterparts of the classical complex-balance and deficiency-zero theory of deterministic reaction networks: complex-wise balance of probability flux, its equivalence with deterministic complex balance under mass action, product-Poisson stationary laws, and the structure of irreducible components for deficiency-zero networks.
