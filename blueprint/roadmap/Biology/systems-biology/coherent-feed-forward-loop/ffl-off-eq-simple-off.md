# No OFF delay

Let $\alpha_z, \beta_z, K_{yz}, z_0, t \in \mathbb{R}$ and $K_{xz} \ge 0$, and let
$Y \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be any trajectory of the intermediate factor. After an OFF step,
the [feed-forward loop target](ffl-off.md) and the [simple-regulation target](simple-off.md) coincide
at time $t$, and both equal $z_0 e^{-\alpha_z t}$.
