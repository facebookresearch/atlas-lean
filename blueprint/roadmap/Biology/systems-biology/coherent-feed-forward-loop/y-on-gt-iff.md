# Intermediate factor exceeds the threshold exactly after the ON delay

Let $\alpha_y > 0$, $\beta_y > 0$ and $K_{yz} < \beta_y/\alpha_y$. For every real $t$, the
[intermediate factor](y-on.md) satisfies $K_{yz} < Y(t)$ if and only if $T_{\mathrm{on}} < t$, where
$T_{\mathrm{on}}$ is the [ON delay](on-delay.md).
