# Delayed rise

Let $\alpha_y > 0$, $\beta_y > 0$, $0 < K_{yz} < \beta_y/\alpha_y$, $\alpha_z > 0$, $K_{xz} < 1$ and
$\beta_z \in \mathbb{R}$. For every $t \ge T_{\mathrm{on}}$, where $T_{\mathrm{on}}$ is the
[ON delay](on-delay.md), the [feed-forward loop target after an ON step](ffl-on.md) is

$$Z(t) = \frac{\beta_z}{\alpha_z}\left(1 - e^{-\alpha_z (t - T_{\mathrm{on}})}\right).$$
