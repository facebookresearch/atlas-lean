# ON delay

Let $\alpha_y > 0$, $\beta_y > 0$ and $K_{yz} < \beta_y/\alpha_y$, and let $\alpha_z, \beta_z, K_{xz}$ be
real. For every $t$ with $0 \le t \le T_{\mathrm{on}}$, where $T_{\mathrm{on}}$ is the
[ON delay](on-delay.md), the [feed-forward loop target after an ON step](ffl-on.md) satisfies
$Z(t) = 0$.
