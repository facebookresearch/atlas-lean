# Persistence of reaction networks

Conditions under which positive trajectories of mass-action systems stay away from the boundary of the positive orthant, so that no species is driven asymptotically to extinction. Geometric conditions on siphons and the polytopes of their complexes rule out boundary $\omega$-limit points and, for complex-balanced systems, yield global convergence.
