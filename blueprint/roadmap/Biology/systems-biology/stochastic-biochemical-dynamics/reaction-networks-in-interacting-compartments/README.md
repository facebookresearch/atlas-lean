# Reaction networks in interacting compartments

Stochastic mass-action chemistry running inside a fluctuating population of compartments that arrive, exit, merge and split. When splitting is driven by the compartments' own contents, explosion, positive recurrence and transience depend on explicit balances between internal production and compartment-level loss.
