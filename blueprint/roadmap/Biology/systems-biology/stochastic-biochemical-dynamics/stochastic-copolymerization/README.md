# Stochastic copolymerization

Markov models of a polymer growing and shrinking at its tip by attachment and detachment of several monomer types. As a random walk on a tree of words, the process has an explicit recurrence threshold, and in the growing regime its monomer composition and elongation speed are given by the root of a single scalar equation.
