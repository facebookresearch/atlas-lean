# Jump-diffusion approximation of reaction networks

Multi-scale biochemical reaction networks mix reactions that fire rarely (low copy numbers) with reactions that fire often. Keeping the rare reactions as Poisson counting processes and replacing the frequent ones by diffusions gives a jump-diffusion model whose reaction-counter law obeys a hybrid master equation, the basis for Bayesian state and rate inference from partial observations.
