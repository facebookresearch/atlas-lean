# Uniqueness of the steady state

Let $v > 0$, $K_1 > 0$, $K_2 > 0$ and $0 < W < 1$. If the [rate ratio](rate-ratio.md) $\rho(W) = W(1 - W + K_1)/((1 - W)(W + K_2))$ satisfies $\rho(W) = v$, then
$W$ is the value of the [Goldbeter–Koshland function](goldbeter-koshland.md) at $(v, K_1, K_2)$ (Goldbeter–Koshland 1981, Eq. 7).
