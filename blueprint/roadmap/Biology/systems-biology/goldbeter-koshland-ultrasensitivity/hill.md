# Hill response function

For real $n$, $K$ and $S$, the fraction of a Hill response with half-saturation constant $K$ and Hill
coefficient $n$ is

$$h(S) = \frac{S^n}{K^n + S^n},$$

with the quotient read as $0$ when $K^n + S^n = 0$, and with real powers $x^y$ with the conventions $0^0 = 1$, $0^y = 0$ for $y \ne 0$, and $x^y = |x|^y \cos(\pi y)$ for $x < 0$ (so $(-8)^{1/3} = 1$). It is the Hill response for
$S, K > 0$ and $n > 0$.
