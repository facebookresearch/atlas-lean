# Michaelis–Menten response

Let $K > 0$ and $S_9, S_1 > 0$, and suppose the [Hill response](hill.md) with $n = 1$ (the
Michaelis–Menten response $S/(K + S)$) equals $0.9$ at $S_9$ and $0.1$ at $S_1$. Then $S_9/S_1 = 81$:
going from 10% to 90% of the response takes an 81-fold increase of the stimulus.
