# Rate ratio is strictly increasing in the modified fraction

For $K_1 > 0$ and $K_2 > 0$, the [rate ratio](rate-ratio.md) $\rho(W) = W(1 - W + K_1)/((1 - W)(W + K_2))$ is strictly increasing on $(0, 1)$: there it is the
product of the positive increasing functions $W/(W + K_2)$ and $1 + K_1/(1 - W)$.
