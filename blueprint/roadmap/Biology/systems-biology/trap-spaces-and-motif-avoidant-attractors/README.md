# Trap spaces and motif-avoidant attractors

In Boolean network models of gene regulation, phenotypes are attractors of the state transition graph, and trap spaces (subcubes the dynamics cannot leave) are the computable proxy for them, since they depend only on the update functions and not on the update scheme. Under asynchronous update an attractor can lie outside every minimal trap space; such motif-avoidant attractors are rare in practice and can be destroyed by inserting delays on a few regulatory edges.
