# Long-time behavior of piecewise deterministic Markov processes

Piecewise deterministic Markov processes (deterministic flows interrupted by random jumps or switches) model gene expression, cell cycles, neural activity and structured populations. Their densities evolve by stochastic semigroups on $L^1$, and partial integrality plus irreducibility give a dichotomy between asymptotic stability and sweeping, checkable for switching flows through path connectivity and a Hörmander bracket condition.
