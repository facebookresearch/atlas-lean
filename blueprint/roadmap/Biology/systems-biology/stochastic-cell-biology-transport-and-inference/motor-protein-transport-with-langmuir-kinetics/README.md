# Motor protein transport with Langmuir kinetics

Mean-field descriptions of molecular motors walking along a microtubule as a totally asymmetric exclusion process with attachment and detachment (Langmuir kinetics), including microtubules with a defective region where motility changes. In the hydrodynamic limit the motor density is the outer solution of a singularly perturbed boundary-value problem, and traffic jams appear as shocks and boundary layers.
