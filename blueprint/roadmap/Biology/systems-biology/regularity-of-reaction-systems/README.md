# Regularity of reaction systems

Conditions guaranteeing that stochastic reaction networks do not explode and that deterministic mass-action ODEs have global solutions. A linear growth bound along a positive direction suffices, which covers all binary and all second-order endotactic mass-action systems and reduces positive recurrence to the existence of a stationary distribution.
