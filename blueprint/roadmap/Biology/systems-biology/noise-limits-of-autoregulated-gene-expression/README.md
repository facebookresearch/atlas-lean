# Noise limits of autoregulated gene expression

In the three-stage model of gene expression (gene switching, transcription, translation) with the protein feeding back on transcription, a central limit theorem under stochastic averaging of the fast gene and mRNA dynamics yields an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck description of protein fluctuations and a closed-form equilibrium Fano factor. Negative autoregulation can lower the protein Fano factor by at most a factor of two relative to the unregulated gene, whereas positive autoregulation can increase it without bound.
