# For n ≤ 2, the nonnegative symmetric equilibrium is linearly stable

For `n ≤ 2` and every `p ≥ 0`, every complex root `z` of the
[characteristic polynomial of the linearization](lin-charpoly.md) of the
[repressilator field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n` and rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` at the
symmetric state `(p, p, p)` satisfies `Re z < 0`; in particular no eigenvalue lies on the imaginary
axis. Every `α > 0` equals `p (1 + pⁿ)` for [exactly one](exists-unique-eq-level.md) `p > 0`, and
`α = 0` corresponds to `p = 0`, so this covers the symmetric equilibrium of nonnegative level at
every rate `α ≥ 0`. Symmetric equilibria at negative levels are not covered, and they can be
unstable: for `n = 1` the state `(−3/2, −3/2, −3/2)` is an equilibrium for `α = 3/4`, with loop
gain `3`, and its linearization has the eigenvalues `−4` and `1/2 ± i 3√3/2`.
