# Transcription rate of the symmetric equilibrium of level p

For `n ∈ ℕ` and a real `p`, `α(p) = p (1 + pⁿ)`. For `p ≥ 0`, the constant state `(p, p, p)` is an
equilibrium of the [repressilator field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n` exactly when the
rate is `α(p)` ([constant equilibria](vector-field-const-eq-zero-iff.md)).
