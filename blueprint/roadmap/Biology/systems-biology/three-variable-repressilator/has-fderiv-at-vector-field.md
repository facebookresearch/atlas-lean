# Linearization at a nonnegative symmetric equilibrium

For `n ∈ ℕ` and `p ≥ 0`, the [repressilator field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n` and
rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` is differentiable at `(p, p, p)`, with derivative the
[linear map](jacobian-clm.md) `v ↦ J(a) v`, where `J(a) = −I − a P` is the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) and `a` is the [loop gain](gain.md) `a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`.
