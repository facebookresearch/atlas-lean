# Characteristic polynomial of a linearization

For `N ∈ ℕ`, a map `G : ℝᴺ → ℝᴺ` and a point `x ∈ ℝᴺ`, this is the characteristic polynomial
`det(X I − A)`, with coefficients mapped to `ℂ`, of the matrix `A` of the derivative of `G` at `x`.
Its complex roots are the eigenvalues of `A`. When `G` is not differentiable at `x`, the derivative
is taken to be `0`, so `A = 0` and the polynomial is `Xᴺ`.
