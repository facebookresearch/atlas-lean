# Derivative of the eigenvalue curve at a nonnegative level

For `n ∈ ℕ` and `μ ≥ 0`, the [eigenvalue curve](hopf-eigenvalue.md) `λ` has derivative
`(1/2 + i √3/2) a'(μ)` at `μ`, where `a'(μ) = n² μⁿ⁻¹ / (1 + μⁿ)²` is the
[derivative of the loop gain](has-deriv-at-gain.md) (with `n − 1` read as `0` for `n = 0`).
