# Matrix of the linearization at a nonnegative symmetric equilibrium

For `n ∈ ℕ` and `p ≥ 0`, the matrix of the derivative at `(p, p, p)` of the
[repressilator field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n` and rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` is the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a(p))`, where `a` is the [loop gain](gain.md)
`a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`.
