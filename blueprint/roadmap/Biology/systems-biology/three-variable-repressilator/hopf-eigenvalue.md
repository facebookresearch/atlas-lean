# Eigenvalue curve of the three-variable repressilator

For `n ∈ ℕ` and a real `μ` (the equilibrium level), `λ(μ) = −1 + a(μ)/2 + i (√3/2) a(μ)`, where `a`
is the [loop gain](gain.md) for the Hill coefficient `n`. It is an [eigenvalue](is-root-iff.md) of
the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a(μ))`, and `λ(μ) = i√3` when `a(μ) = 2`.
