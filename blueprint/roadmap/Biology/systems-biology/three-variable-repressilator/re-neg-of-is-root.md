# For 0 ≤ a < 2, every eigenvalue has negative real part

For `0 ≤ a < 2`, every complex root `z` of the characteristic polynomial `det(X I − J(a))` of the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, satisfies `Re z < 0`. Some lower
bound on `a` is needed: the conclusion holds exactly for `−1 < a < 2`, and for `a ≤ −1` the real
eigenvalue `−1 − a` is nonnegative.
