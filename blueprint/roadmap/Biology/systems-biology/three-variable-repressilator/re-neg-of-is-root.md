# For 0 ≤ a < 2, every eigenvalue has negative real part

For `0 ≤ a < 2`, every complex root `z` of the characteristic polynomial `det(X I − J(a))` of the
[Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, satisfies `Re z < 0`. The
hypothesis `a ≥ 0` is needed: for `a < −1` the real eigenvalue `−1 − a` is positive.
