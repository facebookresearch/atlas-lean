# Linear instability when the loop gain exceeds 2

For `n ∈ ℕ` and `p ≥ 0` with `a(p) > 2`, where `a` is the [loop gain](gain.md)
`a(p) = n pⁿ / (1 + pⁿ)`, some complex root `z` of the
[characteristic polynomial of the linearization](lin-charpoly.md) of the
[repressilator field](vector-field.md) with Hill coefficient `n` and rate `α = p (1 + pⁿ)` at the
symmetric state `(p, p, p)` satisfies `Re z > 0`.
