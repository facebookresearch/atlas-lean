# The three eigenvalues of the Jacobian matrix

For every real `a` and every `z ∈ ℂ`, `z` is a root of the characteristic polynomial
`det(X I − J(a))` of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian.md) `J(a)`, with coefficients in `ℂ`, if and
only if `z` is one of `−1 − a`, `−1 + a/2 + i (√3/2) a` and `−1 + a/2 − i (√3/2) a`.
