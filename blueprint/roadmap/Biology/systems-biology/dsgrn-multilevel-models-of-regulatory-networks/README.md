# DSGRN multilevel models of regulatory networks

DSGRN represents the dynamics of a signed regulatory network over a finite parameter graph of logic and threshold-order parameters, each giving a multilevel asynchronous update on a product of finite chains. Monotone Boolean models sit inside this graph as a special subset of parameters, and their dynamics is refined by the multilevel update.
