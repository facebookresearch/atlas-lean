# Robust synchronization of stochastic genetic oscillators

Populations of synthetic genetic oscillators coupled through quorum sensing are perturbed by intrinsic kinetic parameter fluctuations (multiplicative Wiener noise) and extrinsic environmental molecular noise (an exogenous disturbance). Their synchronization error obeys a stochastic differential equation, and an $H_\infty$ bound on the energy of the error relative to the energy of the extrinsic noise follows from a Hamilton–Jacobi inequality, which fuzzy interpolation reduces to linear matrix inequalities for design.
