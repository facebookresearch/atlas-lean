# Statistical computation with reaction networks

Mass-action reaction networks can be designed so that their steady states are the outputs of statistical procedures. Detailed-balanced networks built from a lattice basis of a design matrix converge to maximum likelihood estimates of log-affine models, and modular catalytic networks with dual-rail encoding compute least-squares regression.
