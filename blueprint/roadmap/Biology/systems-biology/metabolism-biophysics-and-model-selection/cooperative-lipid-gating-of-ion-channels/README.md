# Cooperative lipid gating of ion channels

Kinetic description of ion channels that require a membrane phosphoinositide to open, in which
fast lipid binding and a cooperative square-law between bound fraction and current shape the time
course of current inhibition and recovery when the lipid pool is depleted and resynthesized.
