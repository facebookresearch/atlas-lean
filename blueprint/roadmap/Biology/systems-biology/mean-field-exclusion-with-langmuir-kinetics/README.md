# Mean-field exclusion with Langmuir kinetics

The mean-field approximation of the asymmetric simple exclusion process with Langmuir kinetics (MFALK) describes particles such as ribosomes or RNA polymerases moving in both directions along a chain of sites with soft exclusion, while attaching to and detaching from every site. It is a cooperative tridiagonal compartmental system that contracts in the $\ell_1$ norm, so it has a unique globally attracting steady state and entrains to periodic rates.
