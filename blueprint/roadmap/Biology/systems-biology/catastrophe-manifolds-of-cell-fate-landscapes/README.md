# Catastrophe manifolds of cell-fate landscapes

Gradient-like Morse-Smale flows give a rigorous version of Waddington's landscape for gene-regulatory models of cell-fate decisions. For two-parameter families, the surface of rest points over parameter space (the catastrophe manifold) carries fold curves and cusps, and topological invariants of line bundles over closed curves of rest points force cusps and three-way decision structures to appear.
