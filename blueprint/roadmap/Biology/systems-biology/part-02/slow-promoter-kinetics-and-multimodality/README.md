# Slow promoter kinetics and multimodality

Stochastic gene regulatory networks in which promoter (DNA-binding) transitions are much slower than protein production and decay: the limiting stationary distribution as a mixture of Poisson modes, one per promoter configuration, weighted by a reduced finite Markov chain.
