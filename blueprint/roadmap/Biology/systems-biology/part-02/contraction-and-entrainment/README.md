# Contraction and entrainment

A system is contracting when the distance between any two trajectories shrinks exponentially, as certified by a negative matrix measure of its Jacobian. Contracting systems forget their initial conditions; when forced periodically they settle onto a unique periodic orbit with the forcing period. This gives checkable conditions for biochemical and transcriptional modules to entrain to periodic signals such as circadian or cell-cycle inputs.
