# Stochastic robustness of gene regulatory networks

Gene regulatory networks modeled as stochastic differential equations with state-dependent (intrinsic) noise and an external (extrinsic) disturbance. Lyapunov-type matrix inequalities certify stability in probability under intrinsic fluctuations and bound the gain by which each gene attenuates or amplifies extrinsic noise.
