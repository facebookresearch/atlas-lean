# Multivalent protein phase separation

Proteins and nucleic acids with multiple interaction motifs ("stickers") separated by flexible "spacers" form condensates through coupled density transitions (phase separation) and networking transitions (percolation). In mixtures driven by obligate heterotypic interactions, the percolation line and the two-phase region in the plane of the two concentrations are bounded curves, producing reentrant phase behavior and making saturation concentrations depend on composition.
