# The fitted prior mean tends to the pooled Poisson rate as a → ∞

For positive size factors `s₁, …, sₙ` (`n ≥ 1`) and counts `k₁, …, kₙ` at least one of which is
positive, the fitted prior mean `a / b̂(a)` along the [rate root](rate-root.md) tends to the
[pooled Poisson rate](pooled-mean.md) `μ̂ = (∑ᵢ kᵢ) / (∑ᵢ sᵢ)` as `a → ∞` (the Poisson limit of the
Gamma–Poisson hierarchy).
