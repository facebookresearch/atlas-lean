# Continuity of the moment estimates at overdispersed moments

Along any filter, let `Mₙ → M` and `Qₙ → Q`, with `s > 0` and `0 < M < Q − M²`, where `M` is a mean
and `Q` a second moment, so that `Q − M²` is a variance. Then the [shape estimate](mom-shape.md)
`â(Mₙ, Qₙ − Mₙ²)` tends to `â(M, Q − M²)`, the [rate estimate](mom-rate.md) `b̂(s, Mₙ, Qₙ − Mₙ²)`
tends to `b̂(s, M, Q − M²)`, and for every `k ∈ ℕ` the
[empirical-Bayes posterior mean](eb-posterior-mean.md) at `(s, Mₙ, Qₙ − Mₙ², k)` tends to its value
at `(s, M, Q − M², k)`.
