# The CLR coordinates sum to zero

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of parts and $x$ a [positive composition](pos-composition.md) on
$\iota$ (so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$). Then the coordinates of its [CLR transform](clr.md) sum
to zero: $\sum_{i \in \iota} \operatorname{clr}(x)_i = 0$. The transform therefore takes values
in the hyperplane of vectors in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ whose coordinates sum to zero.
