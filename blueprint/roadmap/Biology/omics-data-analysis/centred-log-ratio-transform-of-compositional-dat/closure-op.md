# Closure of a positive composition to total one

Let $\iota$ be a finite set with at least one part and $x = (x_i)_{i \in \iota}$ a
[positive composition](pos-composition.md), so $x_i > 0$ for every $i$. The closure of $x$ is
the positive composition $\mathcal{C}(x)$ with

$$\mathcal{C}(x)_i = \frac{x_i}{\sum_{j \in \iota} x_j}, \qquad i \in \iota,$$

which has total $1$. The total $\sum_{j \in \iota} x_j$ is positive because there is at least
one part. With no parts the total would be $0$ and no rescaling would have total $1$, so the
closure is defined only for a nonempty set of parts.
