# With a positive count there is at most one shape root

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of $n$ observations, with counts $k_i \in \mathbb{N}$ and size factors
$s_i$. If some count $k_i$ is positive, then for every real $b$ and all real size factors the
[shape score](shape-score.md) equation $S_a(a, b) = 0$ has at most one positive solution:
$S_a(a, b) = S_a(a', b) = 0$ with $a, a' > 0$ implies $a = a'$.
