# The rate score equation at the truth forces the marginal mean

Let $\mu_X$ be a probability measure on $\mathbb{N}$, the law of a count $K$, and write $E$ for
expectations under $\mu_X$. Let $\sigma \ge 0$, let $\theta_0 = (a, b)$ with $a, b > 0$, and let $K$
be integrable. If the [score of one count](count-score.md) has mean zero at $\theta_0$,
$E[\psi_\sigma(\theta_0, K)] = 0$, then $E K = \sigma a / b$, since the rate coordinate gives
$a / b - (a + E K)/(b + \sigma) = 0$.
