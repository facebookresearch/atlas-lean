# Immune and nucleic-acid engineering

Concepts from refereed work on engineered nucleic-acid therapeutics for cancer and on local immune modulation, organized from prerequisite expositions to open research frontiers. Empirical claims retain their reported model, comparison and timing; missing abstract details stay unspecified, and no human efficacy is inferred from cell or animal evidence.

## Topics

- [Nucleic acid therapeutics](./nucleic-acid-therapeutics/README.md) - Engineered RNA cargos and the devices that hold them: pairing chemistry, microRNA silencing, assembled triplex constructs, and the preclinical results and design rationales that follow.
- [Innate immune agonist engineering](./innate-immune-agonist-engineering/README.md) - Turning the cGAS-STING axis into a therapeutic target: agonist delivery constraints, endogenous agonist production, cell-to-cell transfer, and depot behavior.
- [Local immunomodulation and tolerance](./local-immunomodulation-and-tolerance/README.md) - Acting on the immune system at one tissue site: regulatory T cell recruitment and expansion, devices that dose and sample the same skin, and the dose behavior of local depots.
- [Preclinical model systems](./preclinical-model-systems/README.md) - What each experimental system can support: species, transplant origin and host immune status, and batch-level structural heterogeneity in nanomedicines.
