# Species, model system and the scope of a preclinical claim

Every efficacy statement in this subfield is indexed by the system that produced it. A model is specified by three independent axes - anatomical site, origin of the transplanted material, and host immune status - and conflating them produces claims no experiment supports.

The three axes, kept separate:

- *Anatomical site.* Orthotopic placement is at the tissue or tumor's native site; ectopic placement is elsewhere. Subcutaneous placement is not automatically ectopic, since the native site can itself be subcutaneous. Site says nothing about where the cells came from.
- *Transplant origin.* Syngeneic material comes from the same inbred background as the host; xenogeneic material, for example a human cell line, comes from another species; a humanized graft is human tissue carried by an animal host.
- *Host immune status.* Record the particular immune functions that are intact or deficient. The inference a model supports depends on the specific compartment, assay and controls; immunodeficiency does not eliminate every immune mechanism.

Worked combinations from the pages in this subfield: a human breast cancer line implanted orthotopically in immunodeficient mice combines orthotopic site, xenogeneic origin and a compromised host; a murine line implanted orthotopically in immunocompetent mice of the matching background combines orthotopic site, syngeneic origin and an intact host; a study pairing a murine disease model with a humanized skin graft reports two different systems whose results support different statements. Cell cultures isolate particular components; their results do not by themselves establish whole-animal or clinical outcomes.

When citing an outcome, report the system, endpoint, comparator, follow-up and applicable model axes. Mark details absent from an abstract as unspecified; do not invent them or discard otherwise usable evidence merely because an axis is inapplicable. These study pages make no clinical efficacy claim.

## Context

Undergraduate level, and a prerequisite rather than a caveat. The distinction is a working tool here because single studies in this subfield combine systems - for instance a mouse disease model alongside a human-tissue graft - and the two arms license different conclusions.

## Expansion

A rigorous version would require: an explicit transportability framework stating which causal quantities transfer between populations and under what invariance assumptions; interspecies exposure scaling; and treatment of model-selection bias, since models are chosen partly for tractability. These are interpretive prerequisites, not proofs of any experiment.
