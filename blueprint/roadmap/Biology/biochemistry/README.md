# Biochemistry

Molecular biochemistry connects binding, folding and membrane equilibria to biomaterials, drug delivery and local immune modulation. Mathematical model results are distinguished from empirical findings and research questions, with source-qualified examples from Natalie Artzi and collaborators.

## Reading order

- [Molecular foundations](molecular-foundations/README.md): undergraduate binding equilibria, membrane partitioning and optical measurements; then graduate allostery, statistical thermodynamics and nucleic-acid stability.
- [Biomaterials and transport](biomaterials-and-transport/README.md): polymer networks and swelling, diffusion and release models, responsive materials, and tissue adhesion.
- [Immune and nucleic-acid engineering](immune-and-nucleic-acid-engineering/README.md): non-procedural pathway concepts, model-qualified therapeutic findings and structural-nanomedicine research questions.

## Routes from foundations to research

**Molecular recognition and RNA delivery.** Start with [single-class binding](molecular-foundations/ligand-binding-equilibria/single-class-binding-isotherm.md), then [binding polynomials](molecular-foundations/cooperativity-and-allostery/adair-binding-polynomial.md) and [duplex stability](molecular-foundations/folding-and-hybridization-thermodynamics/nearest-neighbor-model-of-duplex-stability.md). [Base-pairing motifs](immune-and-nucleic-acid-engineering/nucleic-acid-therapeutics/watson-crick-and-hoogsteen-pairing.md) lead to the [RNA triple-helix delivery construct](immune-and-nucleic-acid-engineering/nucleic-acid-therapeutics/rna-triple-helix-delivery-construct.md) and its [breast-cancer model study](immune-and-nucleic-acid-engineering/nucleic-acid-therapeutics/dual-microrna-modulation-breast-cancer-model.md). DNA duplex parameters do not transfer unchanged to RNA triplexes.

**Networks, swelling and release.** Follow [mixing free energy](biomaterials-and-transport/network-formation-and-swelling/flory-huggins-mixing-free-energy-of-a-swollen-network.md) and [network elasticity](biomaterials-and-transport/network-formation-and-swelling/gaussian-network-stretching-free-energy.md) to [neutral-gel equilibrium](biomaterials-and-transport/network-formation-and-swelling/free-swelling-equilibrium-of-a-neutral-gel.md), [slab diffusion](biomaterials-and-transport/drug-release-kinetics/fickian-release-from-a-slab.md) and [degradation-controlled release](biomaterials-and-transport/degradable-and-responsive-release/degradation-controlled-release-from-hydrogels.md). Ideal model assumptions are not claims that a particular material obeys them.

**Interfaces and local therapy.** [Fracture energy](biomaterials-and-transport/tissue-adhesion-and-fracture/fracture-energy-of-a-hydrogel.md) provides the mechanical language for [tissue-specific adhesion](biomaterials-and-transport/tissue-adhesion-and-fracture/tissue-specific-aldehyde-amine-adhesion.md). [Preclinical model scope](immune-and-nucleic-acid-engineering/preclinical-model-systems/species-and-model-system-scope.md) precedes interpretation of local-delivery outcomes; a cell assay, an animal study and a clinical result are different evidence.

## Evidence and scope

Each leaf distinguishes its mathematical assumptions or study model and names a public source with a locator. Abstract-only evidence remains labeled as such. Empirical observations and perspectives carry no mathematical kind; their expansions call for a precise model or measurement interface, not a proof of experimental or clinical efficacy. Mathematical nodes identify the remaining model and prerequisite decomposition. The reading routes above are pedagogical; the leaves' dependency sections record their specific prerequisites.
