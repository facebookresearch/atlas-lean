# Collective-level heredity in nested populations

When collectives (communities) of two particle types undergo repeated cycles of growth for a fixed time and dilution through a bottleneck, the map from a collective's founding composition to its adult composition determines whether offspring collectives resemble their parents. Fixed points of this deterministic growth map, governed by competitive Lotka–Volterra ecology within collectives, classify whether one type takes over or a stable interior composition is reliably re-established (developmental correction, a form of collective-level inheritance).
