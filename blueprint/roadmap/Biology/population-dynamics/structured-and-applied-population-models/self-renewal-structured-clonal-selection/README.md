# Self-renewal-structured clonal selection

Integro-differential models of a stem/progenitor cell population structured by a continuous trait $x$ that sets the fraction of self-renewing divisions, with nonlocal feedback from the total mature-cell mass. Selection without mutation drives the population to concentrate as measures on the traits of maximal self-renewal, a model of clonal dominance in hematopoiesis and leukemia.
