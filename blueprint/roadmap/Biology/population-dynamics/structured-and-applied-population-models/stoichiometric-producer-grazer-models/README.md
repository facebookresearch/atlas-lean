# Stoichiometric producer–grazer models

Producer–grazer models in the Loladze–Kuang–Elser tradition, in which phosphorus is conserved and both producer growth and grazer conversion efficiency are limited by food quality (the producer's phosphorus-to-carbon ratio). Including a feeding effort with an energetic cost, fixed or adaptively evolving, keeps the dynamics in an explicit invariant rectangle and yields threshold conditions for grazer extinction.
