# Host–pathogen coevolution by adaptive dynamics

Joint evolution of host resistance and pathogen virulence in a susceptible–infected model with logistic (density-dependent) host mortality, analysed with the invasion-fitness machinery of adaptive dynamics. Trade-offs between resistance and birth rate and between virulence and transmission decide whether coevolution settles at a continuously stable strategy, cycles, or branches into coexisting host or pathogen strains.
