# Asymptotic stability of the stochastic two-stage cell lineage model

**Model.** Let $\xi_1(t)$ be the density of undifferentiated (stem) cells and $\xi_2(t)$ that of mature cells. Stem cells proliferate at rate $p > 0$; a fraction $a/(1 + k\xi_2)$ of their progeny self-renews, with maximal self-renewal fraction $a \in (0, 1)$ and feedback strength $k > 0$ from mature cells; the rest differentiate. Mature cells die at rate $\mu > 0$. With environmental noise acting multiplicatively and independently on each population, the Itô system (Equation 8) is

$$d\xi_1 = \Big(\frac{2a}{1 + k\xi_2} - 1\Big) p\, \xi_1\, dt + \alpha_1 \xi_1\, dW_{1,t},$$

$$d\xi_2 = \Big(2\Big(1 - \frac{a}{1 + k\xi_2}\Big) p\, \xi_1 - \mu \xi_2\Big) dt + \alpha_2 \xi_2\, dW_{2,t},$$

with $\alpha_1, \alpha_2 > 0$, independent Wiener processes $W_1, W_2$, and positive initial data. Solutions exist, are unique and stay in $(0, \infty)^2$; let $\{P(t)\}_{t \ge 0}$ be the Markov semigroup acting on densities of $(\xi_1, \xi_2)$, generated by the corresponding Fokker–Planck equation.

**Proposition 1.** If

$$(2a - 1)\, p - \frac{\alpha_1^2}{2} > 0,$$

then $\{P(t)\}$ is asymptotically stable: there is a unique stationary density $u_*$ and every initial density $u$ satisfies $\|P(t)u - u_*\|_{L^1} \to 0$. If the inequality is reversed, $\xi_1(t) \to 0$ almost surely, and then $\xi_2(t) \to 0$ almost surely.

## Expansion

At low mature-cell density the stem cells grow geometrically with rate $(2a - 1)p$, and multiplicative noise lowers the almost-sure growth rate of $\ln \xi_1$ by $\alpha_1^2/2$ (Itô correction), giving the threshold. The proof uses the Markov semigroup approach of Pichór and Rudnicki: the diffusion is nondegenerate, so the semigroup is an integral (partially) operator; a $C^2$ Khasminskii function $V$ with $\sup_{x \notin B(r)} \mathcal{A}^* V(x) \le -\varepsilon$, where $\mathcal{A}^*$ is the adjoint of the generator, then forces asymptotic stability. Below the threshold, the one-dimensional comparison for $\ln \xi_1$ and the scale-function test for one-dimensional diffusions show extinction of stem cells, after which the mature population decays because its only source is $\xi_1$.
