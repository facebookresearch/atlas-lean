# Bet-hedging in fluctuating environments

Lineages that randomize offspring phenotypes across environments that fluctuate independently from generation to generation, modelled as Kelly's horse-race gambling problem with a full fitness matrix. Covers the growth-rate-optimal (Kelly) strategy and its game-theoretic optimality against competing lineages over finite horizons.
