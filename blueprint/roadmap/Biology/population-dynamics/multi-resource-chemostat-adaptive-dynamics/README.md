# Multi-resource chemostat adaptive dynamics

Adaptive dynamics in which competition between phenotypes is mediated by the consumption of several resources with their own chemostat dynamics, rather than by a direct competition kernel. A strict Lyapunov functional gives global convergence of the ecological system to a unique saturated equilibrium, from which invasion fitness and coexistence criteria for mutants follow.
