# Power-law-decaying growth-rate models

Population growth laws $N' = \xi(t)N$ whose per-capita rate decays in time, interpolating between the Gompertz law (exponential decay of the rate) and the Korf law (power-law decay from a singular start), together with time-inhomogeneous linear birth–death processes whose mean follows the deterministic curve.
