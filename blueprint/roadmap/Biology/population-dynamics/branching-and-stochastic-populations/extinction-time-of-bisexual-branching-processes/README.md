# Extinction time of bisexual branching processes

In a bisexual Galton–Watson branching process females and males pair through a mating function before reproducing. When extinction is certain, the time to extinction is unbounded but light-tailed, and extreme value theory (Gumbel domain, peaks over threshold) gives a practical approximation of its tail.
