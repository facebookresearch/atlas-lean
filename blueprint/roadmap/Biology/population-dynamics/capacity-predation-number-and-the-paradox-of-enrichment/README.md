# Capacity–predation number and the paradox of enrichment

The Rosenzweig–MacArthur predator–prey model in dimensionless form, where prey carrying capacity $b$ and predation half-saturation $h$ enter only through the capacity–predation number $H=h/b$; enrichment (larger $b$) and more aggressive predation (smaller $h$) are the same perturbation, which recasts the destabilization known as the paradox of enrichment.
