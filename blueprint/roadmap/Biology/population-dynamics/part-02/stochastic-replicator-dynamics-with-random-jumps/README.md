# Stochastic replicator dynamics with random jumps

Replicator dynamics driven by both white noise and compensated Poisson jumps modelling rare catastrophic or windfall events. Long-run behavior covers attraction to monomorphic states, stochastic stability of strict Nash equilibria, extinction of dominated strategies and recurrence near interior evolutionarily stable strategies.
