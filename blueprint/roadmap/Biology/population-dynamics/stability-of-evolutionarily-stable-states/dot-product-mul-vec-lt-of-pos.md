# An interior evolutionarily stable state beats all other mixed states

For a finite set `ι` of strategies, a payoff matrix `A` on `ι`, an
[evolutionarily stable state](is-ess.md) `p` with `p_i > 0` for every `i`, and a mixed strategy
`q ≠ p` (`q_i ≥ 0`, `∑_i q_i = 1`, the boundary of the simplex included), `q · A q < p · A q`
(Hofbauer and Sigmund 1998, Theorem 6.4.1).
