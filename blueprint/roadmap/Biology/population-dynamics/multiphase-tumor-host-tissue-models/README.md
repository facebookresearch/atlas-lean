# Multiphase tumor–host tissue models

Mixture-theory models of a tumor growing inside a host tissue: tumor and host cells are phases of a saturated mixture with nonlinear (possibly degenerate) stress-driven diffusion, coupled to a diffusing nutrient supplied by the vasculature. Constitutive guidelines on growth, death and uptake terms secure nonnegativity, physiological bounds and uniqueness of weak solutions.
