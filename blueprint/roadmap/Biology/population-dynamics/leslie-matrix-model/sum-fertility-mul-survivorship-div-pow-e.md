# Discrete Euler–Lotka equation

Let $L$ be the Leslie matrix of $n \ge 1$ age classes with fertilities $f_0, \dots, f_{n-1}$ and
real survival coefficients $s_0, \dots, s_{n-1}$. If $Lv = \lambda v$ for some $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with
$v_0 \ne 0$ and some real $\lambda \ne 0$, then

$$\sum_i \frac{f_i l_i}{\lambda^{i+1}} = 1,$$

where $l_i = s_0 s_1 \cdots s_{i-1}$ is the survivorship, with $l_0 = 1$.
