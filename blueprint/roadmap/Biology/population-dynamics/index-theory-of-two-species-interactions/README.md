# Index theory of two-species interactions

For planar Kolmogorov systems $\dot x=xf(x,y)$, $\dot y=yg(x,y)$ whose interaction signs may switch with density (mutualism to parasitism), Poincare index and Dulac arguments constrain the arrangement of equilibria and the possibility of oscillations independently of the functional forms.
