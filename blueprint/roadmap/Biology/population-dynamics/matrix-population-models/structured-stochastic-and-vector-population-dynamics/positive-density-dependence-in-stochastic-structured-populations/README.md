# Positive density dependence in stochastic structured populations

Structured populations (spatial patches, age classes) with Allee effects such as mate limitation, living in uncorrelated random environments. Monotone random matrix models with nondecreasing entries have Lyapunov exponents at low density and near infinity that sort long-term behavior into unconditional persistence, unconditional extinction, or a bistable regime.
