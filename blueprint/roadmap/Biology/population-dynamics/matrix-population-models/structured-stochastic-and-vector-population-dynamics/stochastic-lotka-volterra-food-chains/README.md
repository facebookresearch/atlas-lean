# Stochastic Lotka–Volterra food chains

A linear food chain of one prey and $n-1$ successive predators, each with intraspecific competition, whose growth and death rates are perturbed by correlated white noise. Persistence and extinction are classified exactly by a sequence of explicit invasion rates computed from noise-corrected rates and the boundary equilibria of the shorter chains.
