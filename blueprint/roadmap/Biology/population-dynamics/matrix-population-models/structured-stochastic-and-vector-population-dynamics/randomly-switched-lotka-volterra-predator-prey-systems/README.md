# Randomly switched Lotka–Volterra predator-prey systems

Predator-prey dynamics in a random environment modeled as a piecewise deterministic Markov process that switches between two Lotka–Volterra vector fields. When the two systems share their positive equilibrium, the switching destroys the neutral cycles and every non-equilibrium trajectory oscillates between $0$ and $\infty$.
