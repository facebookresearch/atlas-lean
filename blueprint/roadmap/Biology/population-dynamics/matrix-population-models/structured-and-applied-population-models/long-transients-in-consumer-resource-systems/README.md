# Long transients in consumer–resource systems

Ecological systems can spend long stretches far from their eventual attractor: near a saddle (crawl-by transients) or near a vanished cycle just past a bifurcation (ghost attractors). In a pulsed consumer–resource model with group defense, both appear, and the duration of the crawl-by transients follows from linearization at the saddle.
