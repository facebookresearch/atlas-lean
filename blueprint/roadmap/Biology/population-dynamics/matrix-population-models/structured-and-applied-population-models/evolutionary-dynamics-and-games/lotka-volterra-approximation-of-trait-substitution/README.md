# Lotka–Volterra approximation of trait substitution

Justification of Lotka–Volterra competition models as local approximations of general community dynamics during the invasion of a mutant phenotype similar to a resident. Under axioms for smooth, phenotype-exchangeable fitness-generating functions, clusters of similar phenotypes can be lumped into approximate phenotypes whose transient dynamics are Lotka–Volterra up to errors quadratic in the mutational step, which in turn yields "invasion implies substitution" results.
