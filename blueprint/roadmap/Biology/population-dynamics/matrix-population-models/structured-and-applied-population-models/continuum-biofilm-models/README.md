# Continuum biofilm models

One-dimensional free-boundary models of biofilm growth in which microbial volume fractions are advected by a growth-induced velocity field, dissolved substrates diffuse and react inside the film, and the film thickness moves with the boundary velocity minus detachment. Rewriting the hyperbolic equations along characteristics gives local existence and uniqueness by a contraction argument.
