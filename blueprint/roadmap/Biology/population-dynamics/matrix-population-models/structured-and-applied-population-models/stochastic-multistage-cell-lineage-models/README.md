# Stochastic multistage cell lineage models

Multistage models of stem cell differentiation in which the self-renewal fraction is down-regulated by mature cells, perturbed by environmental noise proportional to each population. For two stages, the noisy model has a stationary distribution exactly when the stem-cell growth rate at zero density exceeds the Itô noise correction; otherwise the lineage dies out almost surely.
