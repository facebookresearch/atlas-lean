# Periodic scalar models of tumour immunotherapy

Tumour–immune models under periodic vaccination whose long-term dynamics collapse to a single scalar equation $\dot x = x F(t,x)$ with time-periodic per-capita growth. Monotonicity of $F$ in $x$ and the sign of its time average at zero decide between unconditional tumour escape and a threshold in which a unique periodic solution separates cure from escape.
