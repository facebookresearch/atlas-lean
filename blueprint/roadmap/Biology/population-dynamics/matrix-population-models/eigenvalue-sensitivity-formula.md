# Sensitivity of the population growth rate to matrix entries

This node covers the following result. Let $A$ be a population projection matrix whose dominant eigenvalue $\lambda$ is simple, with right eigenvector $w$ ($Aw = \lambda w$) and left eigenvector $v$ ($v^{\mathsf T} A = \lambda v^{\mathsf T}$). Then $\partial \lambda / \partial a_{ij} = v_i w_j / \langle w, v \rangle$.
