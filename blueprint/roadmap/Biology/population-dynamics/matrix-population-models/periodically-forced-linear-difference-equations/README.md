# Periodically forced linear difference equations

Daily-step population models with seasonal emergence and seasonally varying survival are linear difference equations with periodic coefficients and a periodic source term. Their long-run behaviour is governed by the monodromy matrix over one period: when its spectral radius is below one, every solution converges to a unique periodic orbit given in closed form. This underlies seasonal models of mosquito populations and malaria transmission in mosquitoes, from which field-measurable quantities such as the sporozoite proportion and the entomological inoculation rate are computed.
