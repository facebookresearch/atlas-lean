# Dispersal and environmental stochasticity in patch models

A population spread over $n$ patches, with density-dependent reproduction in each patch and dispersal between patches, persists or goes extinct according to the sign of a single metapopulation growth rate, a Lyapunov exponent of the linearization at zero, even when growth, competition and dispersal all fluctuate randomly. Small-noise expansions around the deterministic equilibrium give the change in expected total population size caused by noise, whose sign depends on which parameters fluctuate and on their covariances.
