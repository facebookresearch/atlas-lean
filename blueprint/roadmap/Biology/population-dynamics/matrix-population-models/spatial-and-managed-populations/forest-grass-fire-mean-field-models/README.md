# Forest-grass-fire mean-field models

Mean-field limits of spatial stochastic vegetation-fire models track forest, grass, burning and ash cover; sigmoidal grass flammability and fire spread produce a unique grassland steady state whose resistance to forest invasion is an explicit rate inequality, the basis of forest-savanna bistability.
