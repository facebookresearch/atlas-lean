# Steady states of diffusion across an environmental interface

A population diffuses on an interval made of two habitats with different diffusion rates and growth laws, glued by continuity of density and flux. Positive steady states always exist; uniqueness follows from Chicone-type period-map arguments for the associated planar Hamiltonian systems under convexity conditions on the growth potentials, which hold for generalized logistic growth.
