# Fecundity–survival trade-off in delayed maturation

Discrete-time delay models of mature individuals in which offspring mature after $\tau$ breeding cycles, die with constant per-cycle probability before maturing, and are produced at a rate that increases with $\tau$. The combined recruitment factor $m(\tau)$ is non-monotone in $\tau$, so persistence occurs only for an intermediate window of maturation delays.
