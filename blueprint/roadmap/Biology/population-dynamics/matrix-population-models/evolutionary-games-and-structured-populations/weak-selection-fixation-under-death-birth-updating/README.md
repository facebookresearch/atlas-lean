# Weak-selection fixation under death-Birth updating

Fixation probability of a mutant with constant fitness on a weighted graph under death-Birth updating, expanded to first order in the selection coefficient through coalescence times of random walks. The first-order term defines an effective population size that decides amplification or suppression of weak selection, and yields the first transient amplifiers for death-Birth updating.
