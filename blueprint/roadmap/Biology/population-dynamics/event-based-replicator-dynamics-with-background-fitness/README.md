# Event-based replicator dynamics with background fitness

Deriving eco-evolutionary game dynamics from the rates at which individuals meet in different kinds of events. Vital rates are event rates times per-event fertility and mortality payoffs; singling out a focal game and lumping all other activities into background rates gives replicator equations coupled to logistic-type population growth, and shows when background mortality can be folded into the game payoffs.
