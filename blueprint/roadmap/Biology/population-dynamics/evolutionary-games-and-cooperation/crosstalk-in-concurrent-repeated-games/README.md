# Crosstalk in concurrent repeated games

A player engaged in several repeated games at once may carry the state of one game over into
another. These articles give the linear system that determines the long-run cooperation
probabilities under such crosstalk and the resulting steady-state payoffs.

- [Stationary cooperation probabilities under crosstalk](stationary-cooperation-probabilities-under-crosstalk.md)
- [Steady-state payoffs under crosstalk](steady-state-payoffs-under-crosstalk.md)
