# Partner strategies among reactive-n strategies

A reactive-$n$ strategy for the repeated prisoner's dilemma conditions its cooperation probability on the co-player's last $n$ moves. Partners are reactive-$n$ strategies that are never the first to defect and form a Nash equilibrium, so they sustain full cooperation without being exploitable. Their characterization rests on a reduction: against a reactive-$n$ strategy, some deterministic self-reactive-$n$ strategy, which conditions only on the player's own last $n$ moves, is always a best response. In the donation game this yields explicit partner conditions for $n = 2$ and $n = 3$, and for counting strategies with any $n$.
