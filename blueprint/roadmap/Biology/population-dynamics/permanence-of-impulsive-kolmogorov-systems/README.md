# Permanence of impulsive Kolmogorov systems

Species interacting through $\dot x_i=x_if_i(x)$ and subjected to periodic multiplicative pulses (culling, chemotherapy, pesticide, pulsed births) coexist when, on every boundary invariant piece, a positive combination of per capita growth rates, augmented by the averaged pulse term $\ln(1+h_i)/\tau$, is positive.
