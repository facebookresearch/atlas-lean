# Poissonized prediction of unsampled species coverage

Predicting the total relative abundance of species (urn colours) not yet observed in a sample, as in microbial amplicon surveys, by embedding further draws in an independent homogeneous Poisson process so that the uncovered probability becomes the rate of a thinned process.
