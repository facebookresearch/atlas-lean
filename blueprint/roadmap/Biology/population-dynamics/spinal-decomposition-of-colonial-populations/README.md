# Spinal decomposition of colonial populations

Colonial species (corals, social spiders, ants, colonial tunicates) reproduce by fission of whole colonies. Modelling colonies as interacting branching diffusions with shared environmental noise, a size-biased spine yields many-to-one formulas that describe a typical colony and the long-run distribution of resources.
