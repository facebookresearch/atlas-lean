# Invariance of the simplex (Hofbauer–Sigmund 1998, §7.1)

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of strategies, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function and $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a [solution of the replicator equation](is-solution.md) for $f$, with $f$ continuous. If $x(0)$ lies in the standard simplex ($x_i(0) \ge 0$ for every
$i$ and $\sum_i x_i(0) = 1$), then $x(t)$ lies in it for every $t \ge 0$.
