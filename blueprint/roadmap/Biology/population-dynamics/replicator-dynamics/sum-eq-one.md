# Invariance of the simplex hyperplane

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of strategies, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function and $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a [solution of the replicator equation](is-solution.md) for $f$, with $f$ continuous. If $\sum_i x_i(0) = 1$, then $\sum_i x_i(t) = 1$ for every
$t \ge 0$.
