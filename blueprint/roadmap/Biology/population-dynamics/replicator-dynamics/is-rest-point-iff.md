# Rest points in the interior are equalizers

Let $\iota$ be a finite set, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$ with
$x_i > 0$ for every $i$. Then $x$ is a [rest point](is-rest-point.md) of the replicator equation if and
only if $f_i(x) = \bar f(x)$ for every $i$, where $\bar f$ is the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md).
