# Evolution of the total frequency under replicator dynamics

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of strategies, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function and $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a [solution of the replicator equation](is-solution.md) for $f$. For every $t \ge 0$, the total frequency $s \mapsto \sum_i x_i(s)$ has derivative
$\bar f(x(t)) \bigl(1 - \sum_i x_i(t)\bigr)$ at $t$, where $\bar f$ is the
[mean fitness](mean-fitness.md).
