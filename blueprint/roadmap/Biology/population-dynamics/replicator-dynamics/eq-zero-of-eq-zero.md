# Invariance of the faces of the simplex

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of strategies, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function and $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a [solution of the replicator equation](is-solution.md) for $f$, with $f$ continuous. If strategy $i$ is absent at time $0$, $x_i(0) = 0$, then
$x_i(t) = 0$ for all $t \ge 0$.
