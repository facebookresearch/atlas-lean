# Present strategies stay present

Let $\iota$ be a finite set of strategies, $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function and $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a [solution of the replicator equation](is-solution.md) for $f$, with $f$ continuous. If $x_i(0) > 0$, then $x_i(t) > 0$ for every $t \ge 0$.
