# Solution of the replicator equation

Let $\iota$ be a finite set and $f \colon \mathbb{R}^\iota \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ a fitness function. A
function $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^\iota$ solves the replicator equation when, for every $i$ and
every $t \ge 0$, $s \mapsto x_i(s)$ has derivative

$$x_i(t) \bigl(f_i(x(t)) - \bar f(x(t))\bigr)$$

at $t$, where $\bar f$ is the [mean fitness](mean-fitness.md).
