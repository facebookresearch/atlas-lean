# Ergodic harvesting under biomass-dependent quotas

Long-run average yield from a diffusive population whose harvesting rate is capped by a quota depending on the current biomass. Optimal strategies are of threshold type (harvest at the maximal permitted rate above a level $b^*$, not at all below it); the threshold may be $0$, and the classical singular-control barrier strategy is recovered as the quota is relaxed.
