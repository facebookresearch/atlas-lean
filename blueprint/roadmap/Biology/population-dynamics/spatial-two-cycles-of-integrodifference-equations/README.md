# Spatial two-cycles of integrodifference equations

An integrodifference equation $N_{t+1}=\int K(x-y)F(N_t(y))\,dy$ couples discrete-generation growth with nonlocal dispersal. When the growth map has a period-2 cycle, the spatial model can carry 2-cycles that sit at opposite phases of the non-spatial cycle at the two ends of the line. For the Laplace kernel these are exactly heteroclinic orbits of a reversible four-dimensional ODE.
