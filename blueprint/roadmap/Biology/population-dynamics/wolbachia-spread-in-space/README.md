# Wolbachia spread in space

Spatial models of the invasion of a mosquito population by the maternally transmitted endosymbiont *Wolbachia*. Cytoplasmic incompatibility creates a frequency threshold, so spread proceeds as a bistable travelling wave whose speed and required release size depend on local growth and on the shape of the dispersal kernel.
