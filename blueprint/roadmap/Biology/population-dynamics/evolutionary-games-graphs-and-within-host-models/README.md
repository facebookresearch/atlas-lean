# Evolutionary games, graph dynamics and within-host models

Stochastic and game-theoretic models of evolution and of populations inside hosts: Moran processes and games on graphs, with amplifiers of selection and fixation probabilities and times; repeated games and the evolution of cooperation; and models of viruses and tumours within a host, of competing microbes and of replicators in protocells.
