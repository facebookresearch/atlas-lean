# Amplifiers of natural selection

Population structures for the birth–death Moran process on weighted directed graphs that raise the fixation probability of an advantageous mutant above its well-mixed value. Strong amplifiers drive this probability to one as the population grows, at a necessary but possibly marginal cost in fixation time; edge weights and self-loops are essential ingredients.
