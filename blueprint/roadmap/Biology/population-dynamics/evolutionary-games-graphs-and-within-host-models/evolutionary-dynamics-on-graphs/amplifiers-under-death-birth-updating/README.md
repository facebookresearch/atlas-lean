# Amplifiers of selection under death-Birth updating

Upper bounds on the fixation probability of an advantageous mutant in the death-Birth Moran
process on a graph, and the two negative results they imply: every amplifier of selection under
death-Birth updating is bounded, and every amplifier on a non-complete graph is transient.
