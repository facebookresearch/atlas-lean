# Large-deviation growth rate of source–sink metapopulations

Density-independent branching models of a species on habitat patches of different quality with offspring dispersing by a Markov chain. The metapopulation growth rate and the patch-occupancy frequencies of the ancestral lines of survivors are given by a large-deviation variational formula that separates demography (mean offspring numbers) from dispersal (the rate function of a random disperser).
