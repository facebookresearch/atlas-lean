# Bifurcating processes with dependent lifetimes

Cell-population growth models in which each cell divides into two and the lifetimes along the binary genealogical tree are dependent (for example a bifurcating Markov chain), rather than independent as in the Bellman–Harris process. Under stationarity and multiplicative ergodicity the population still grows exponentially, with a Malthusian parameter defined through the Laplace transforms of birth dates.
