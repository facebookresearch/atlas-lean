# Continued-fraction methods for birth–death processes

Exact and numerical computation of transition probabilities and first-passage times of general birth–death processes with arbitrary rates, through continued-fraction representations of their Laplace transforms. These underpin likelihood-based inference for population, epidemic and evolutionary counting models.
