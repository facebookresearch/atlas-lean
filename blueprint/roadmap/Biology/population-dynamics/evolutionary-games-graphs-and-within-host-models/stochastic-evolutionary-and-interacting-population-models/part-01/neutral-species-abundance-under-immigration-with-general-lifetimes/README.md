# Neutral species abundance under immigration with general lifetimes

Neutral island community in which individuals reproduce at constant rate during lifetimes of arbitrary distribution and new species arrive by immigration. The species abundance counts are independent Poisson variables following Fisher's log-series at every time, and conditioning on community size gives Ewens' sampling formula, independently of time and of the lifetime law.
