# Rolling-carpet eradication of bistable invasions

Eradicating an invading population with a strong Allee effect (bistable reaction–diffusion) by acting only on a moving interval, either by killing individuals there or by releasing sterile males there. Both strategies succeed when the interval and effort are large enough to generate a travelling wave that moves against the natural invasion front.
