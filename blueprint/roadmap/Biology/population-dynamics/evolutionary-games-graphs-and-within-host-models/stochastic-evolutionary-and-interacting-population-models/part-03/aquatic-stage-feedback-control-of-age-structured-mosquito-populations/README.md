# Aquatic-stage feedback control of age-structured mosquito populations

Age-structured logistic models of immature, female and male mosquitoes in which vector control acts only on the aquatic stage through a time-varying removal rate. Writing solutions as equilibrium age profiles modulated by a scalar amplitude and renewal-driven shape perturbations, explicit feedback laws globally stabilize the population and let the emergent aquatic density track prescribed trajectories with bounded positive controls.
