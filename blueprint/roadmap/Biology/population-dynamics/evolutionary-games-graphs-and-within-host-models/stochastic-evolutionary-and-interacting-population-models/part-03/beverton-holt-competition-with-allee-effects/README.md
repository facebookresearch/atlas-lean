# Beverton–Holt competition with Allee effects

Discrete-time contest-competition models whose per-capita growth increases at low density (Allee effects). The single-species sigmoid Beverton–Holt map has an explicit threshold separating extinction from bistability, and its two-species competitive generalization has equilibrium dynamics whenever intraspecific competition is at least as intense as interspecific competition.
