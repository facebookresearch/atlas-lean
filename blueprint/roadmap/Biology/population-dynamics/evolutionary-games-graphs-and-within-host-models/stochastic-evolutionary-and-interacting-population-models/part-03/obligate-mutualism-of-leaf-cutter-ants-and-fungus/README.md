# Obligate mutualism of leaf-cutter ants and their fungus garden

A two-variable model of the early colony in which ants grow by feeding on fungus and the fungus grows through ant labour with a Michaelis–Menten (Hill-type) response. A single division-of-labour parameter decides between certain colony failure and bistability, in which small founding colonies collapse and large ones establish.
