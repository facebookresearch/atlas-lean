# Purely competitive evolutionary game dynamics

Evolutionary dynamics in which randomly paired individuals compete head to head and the winner's type is propagated with probability proportional to its payoff in the pairing, as in tournaments, intrasexual selection or emulation learning. Dominant types take over and cyclic dominance gives an unstable interior equilibrium; numerically, small mutation rates appear to stabilize it through a Hopf bifurcation.
