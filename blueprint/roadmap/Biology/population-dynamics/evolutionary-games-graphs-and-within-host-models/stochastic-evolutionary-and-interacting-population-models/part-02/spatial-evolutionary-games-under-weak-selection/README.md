# Spatial evolutionary games under weak selection

Evolutionary games on $\mathbb Z^d$ with payoffs close to $1$ behave as voter model perturbations; after rescaling, frequencies follow a reaction–diffusion equation whose reaction term is the replicator field of a spatially modified game, which decides takeover, bistability and coexistence.
