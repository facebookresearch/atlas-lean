# Habitat selection in stochastic patchy environments

Populations spread across patches whose growth rates fluctuate stochastically. Two morphs differing only in how they distribute time across patches compete through shared patch-level density dependence; invasion rates decide exclusion or a protected polymorphism, and the evolutionarily stable patch-selection strategy practises spatial bet hedging.
