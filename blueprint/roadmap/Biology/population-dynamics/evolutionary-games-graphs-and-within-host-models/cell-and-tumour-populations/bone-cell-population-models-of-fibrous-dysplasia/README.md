# Bone-cell population models of fibrous dysplasia

Fibrous dysplasia is a mosaic skeletal disorder in which mutant osteoprogenitors replace normal bone with fibro-osseous tissue. Compartment ODE models of osteoprogenitors, osteocytes and osteoclasts with a shared logistic niche, a regulated stem-cell inflow and a phenocopying interaction show how a small mutant inflow comes to dominate.
