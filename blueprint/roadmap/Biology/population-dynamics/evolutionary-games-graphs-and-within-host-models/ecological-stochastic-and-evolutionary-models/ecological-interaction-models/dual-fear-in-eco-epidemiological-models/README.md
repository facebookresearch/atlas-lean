# Dual fear in eco-epidemiological models

Predators can depress prey populations without killing them: fear lowers prey reproduction and can also reduce contacts and hence disease transmission among prey. In a susceptible-infected prey and predator model with prey aggregation (attack rate on $S^r$), these two fear effects act as bifurcation parameters that create or destroy oscillations and change which boundary equilibria are stable.
