# Inflation in two-patch periodic sink environments

Two habitats that are both sinks on average, with growth rates alternating out of phase, can sustain a growing metapopulation once individuals disperse between them (inflation, or dispersal-induced growth). For a piecewise-constant switching model the metapopulation growth rate has a closed form, inflation sets in at a dispersal rate that is exponentially small in the period, and it survives random switching times and random choices of patch quality.
