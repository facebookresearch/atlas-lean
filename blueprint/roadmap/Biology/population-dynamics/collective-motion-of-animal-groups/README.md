# Collective motion of animal groups

Mathematical models of flocks, schools and swarms in which individuals align their velocities with neighbours through a time-varying directed interaction network. Consensus of velocities is governed by the strongly connected structure of the interactions that persist for infinite time, which explains velocity inheritance from leading subgroups.
