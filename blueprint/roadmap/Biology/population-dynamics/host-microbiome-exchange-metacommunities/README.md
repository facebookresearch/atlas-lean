# Host-microbiome exchange metacommunities

Hosts on a contact network exchange fractions of their microbiomes at discrete Poisson interaction times while each microbiome follows its own local dynamics. Rare interactions reduce to a Markov chain on basins of attraction; frequent interactions reduce to diffusively coupled or fully mixed deterministic dynamics.
