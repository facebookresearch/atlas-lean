# Coexistence in flow-kick systems

Population models that alternate continuous growth with discrete disturbances (harvests, fires, dilutions, pulses) at state-dependent times. Coexistence and extinction of species are decided by invasion growth rates computed along the kick-to-kick dynamics of subcommunities.
