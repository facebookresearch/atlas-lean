# Inverse problems for tumor growth reaction–diffusion models

Recovery of spatially varying growth parameters and initial tumor distributions in Fisher–KPP-type models of cell invasion, $\partial_t\rho=\nabla\cdot(\gamma(\rho)\nabla\rho)+\mu\rho-\xi\rho^2$, from imaging snapshots, with uniqueness and stability obtained through Carleman estimates.
