# Reaction–diffusion sterile insect model with Ricker recruitment

A sex-structured reaction–diffusion model of wild males, wild females and released sterile males of *Aedes aegypti*, with mating competition, Ricker-type larval density dependence and spatial dispersal, used to plan spatially heterogeneous and impulsive sterile-male releases.
