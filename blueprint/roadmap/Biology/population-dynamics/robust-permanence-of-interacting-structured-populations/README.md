# Robust permanence of interacting structured populations

Continuous-time models of several interacting, internally structured populations of the form $\dot x_i=A_i(x)x_i$ with quasi-positive matrices, and necessary and sufficient conditions for permanence that survives small perturbations of the model, expressed through dominant Lyapunov exponents (invasion rates) of each population with respect to invariant measures on the extinction boundary.
