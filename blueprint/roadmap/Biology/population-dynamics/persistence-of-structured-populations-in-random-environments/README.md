# Persistence of structured populations in random environments

Coexistence criteria for interacting, stage- or space-structured species described by nonlinear matrix models with stochastically varying parameters. Long-term growth rates are dominant Lyapunov exponents of random matrix products evaluated at the boundary of the community, and persistence follows when a positive combination of them is positive whenever some species are rare.
