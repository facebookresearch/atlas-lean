# With a positive reference frequency the Lotka–Volterra time increases

For a finite set `ι`, a continuous fitness function `f` and a
[replicator solution](../replicator-dynamics/is-solution.md) `x` of `x_k' = x_k (f_k(x) - f̄(x))` for
`t ≥ 0` with `x_o(0) > 0`, the derivative `x_o(max(t, 0))` of the
[Lotka–Volterra time](time-change.md) is positive at every real `t`.
