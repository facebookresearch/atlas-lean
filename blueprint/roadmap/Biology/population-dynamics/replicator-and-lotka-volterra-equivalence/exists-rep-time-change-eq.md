# Every replicator time is reached by the replicator time change

For a finite set `ι`, a payoff matrix `A` on the strategies `ι ∪ {o}` and a positive solution `y`
of the Lotka–Volterra equation `y_i' = F_i(y)` for `τ ≥ 0` (with `F` the
[Lotka–Volterra field](lv-field.md) of the [growth rates](lv-growth.md) and
[interaction matrix](lv-interaction.md) of `A`, and `y_i(τ) > 0` for all `i` and `τ ≥ 0`), every
`t ≥ 0` equals `t(τ)` for some `τ ∈ [0, t]`, where `t(·)` is the
[replicator time](rep-time-change.md).
