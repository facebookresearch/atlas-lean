# Frequency ratios grow at the fitness difference

For a finite set `ι`, a fitness function `f` on states of the strategies `ι ∪ {o}`, a curve `x` and
a time `t`, suppose every `x_k` has derivative `x_k(t) (f_k(x(t)) - f̄(x(t)))` at `t`, the
replicator equation with [mean fitness](../replicator-dynamics/mean-fitness.md) `f̄`, and
`x_o(t) ≠ 0`. Then for every `i ∈ ι` the ratio `y_i = x_i / x_o` has derivative
`y_i(t) (f_i(x(t)) - f_o(x(t)))` at `t`: the mean fitness cancels.
