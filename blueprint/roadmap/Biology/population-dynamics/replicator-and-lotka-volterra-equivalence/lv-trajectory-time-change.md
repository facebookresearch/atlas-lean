# The Lotka–Volterra trajectory at time τ(t) is the image of x(t)

For a finite set `ι`, a continuous fitness function `f` and a
[replicator solution](../replicator-dynamics/is-solution.md) `x` of `x_k' = x_k (f_k(x) - f̄(x))` for
`t ≥ 0` with `x_o(0) > 0`, the [Lotka–Volterra trajectory](lv-trajectory.md) at time `τ(t)`, where
`τ` is the [Lotka–Volterra time](time-change.md), is `(x_i(t) / x_o(t))_i`, for every real `t`.
