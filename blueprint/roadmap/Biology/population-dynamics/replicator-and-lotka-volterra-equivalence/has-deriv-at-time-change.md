# Derivative of the Lotka–Volterra time change

For a finite set `ι`, a fitness function `f` and a [replicator solution](../replicator-dynamics/is-solution.md)
`x` of `x_k' = x_k (f_k(x) - f̄(x))` for `t ≥ 0`, the [Lotka–Volterra time](time-change.md) `τ` has
derivative `x_o(max(t, 0))` at every real `t`: the reference frequency `x_o(t)` for `t ≥ 0` and
`x_o(0)` for `t < 0`.
