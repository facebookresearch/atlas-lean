# Positive frequencies give positive frequency ratios

For a finite set `ι` and a state `x` on the strategies `ι ∪ {o}` with every `x_k > 0`, every
[Lotka–Volterra coordinate](to-lv.md) `x_i / x_o` is positive: the interior of the simplex, and
indeed every positive vector, is mapped into the open positive orthant.
