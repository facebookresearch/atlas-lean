# The replicator trajectory solves the replicator equation

For a finite set `ι`, a payoff matrix `A` on the strategies `ι ∪ {o}` and a positive solution `y`
of the Lotka–Volterra equation `y_i' = F_i(y)` for `τ ≥ 0` (with `F` the
[Lotka–Volterra field](lv-field.md) of the [growth rates](lv-growth.md) and
[interaction matrix](lv-interaction.md) of `A`, and `y_i(τ) > 0` for all `i` and `τ ≥ 0`), the
[replicator trajectory](rep-trajectory.md) is a
[solution of the replicator equation](../replicator-dynamics/is-solution.md) of the game,
`x_k' = x_k ((A x)_k - f̄(x))`, for all times `t ≥ 0`.
