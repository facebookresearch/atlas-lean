# The replicator time is at least the Lotka–Volterra time

For a finite set `ι`, a payoff matrix `A` on the strategies `ι ∪ {o}` and a curve `y` that solves
the Lotka–Volterra equation `y_i' = F_i(y)` at every `τ ≥ 0`, with `F` the
[Lotka–Volterra field](lv-field.md) of the [growth rates](lv-growth.md) and
[interaction matrix](lv-interaction.md) of `A`, and stays positive (`y_i(τ) > 0` for all `i` and
`τ ≥ 0`), the [replicator time](rep-time-change.md) satisfies `τ ≤ t(τ)` for every `τ ≥ 0`.
