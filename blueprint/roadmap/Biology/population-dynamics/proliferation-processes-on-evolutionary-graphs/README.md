# Proliferation processes on evolutionary graphs

Variants of the Moran process on graphs in which a reproducing mutant may replace several neighbours at once (Bernoulli or binomial proliferation with intensity $p$). A critical intensity makes proliferation neutral for fixation probability, and absorption and fixation times can be compared with the Moran process explicitly on complete graphs, cycles and stars.
