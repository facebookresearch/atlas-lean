# Harvested predator–prey with species-dependent refuge

Predator–prey models with Holling type II predation in which the fraction of prey in refuge grows with predator density, and both species are harvested with independent efforts. The refuge coefficient acts as a control that can destroy limit cycles; bionomic equilibria and optimal harvesting are analyzed on top of the population dynamics.
