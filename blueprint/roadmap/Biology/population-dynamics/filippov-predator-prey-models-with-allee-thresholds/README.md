# Filippov predator-prey models with Allee thresholds

Idealized Allee effects in both prey and predator can be modeled by Heaviside switches at abundance thresholds, turning a logistic Lotka-Volterra system into a piecewise-smooth (Filippov) system on four regions. Each smooth piece has explicit, globally attracting equilibria (coexistence, prey only, extinction), sliding along the switching lines is only of escaping type, and periodic forcing of the prey growth rate near the natural period of the damped oscillations can push trajectories across a threshold, producing frequency-induced tipping.
