# Schaefer maximum sustainable yield and the Gordon bionomic equilibrium

In the Gordon–Schaefer model a fish stock $X$ grows logistically and is harvested with effort $E$ and catchability $q$: $\dot X = rX(1 - X/K) - qXE$ (Schaefer 1954; Clark 2010, Chapters 1–2). At the steady state $X = K(1 - qE/r)$ the sustainable yield is $h(E) = qK(1 - qE/r)E$, which is maximized at $E_{MSY} = r/(2q)$, with stock $K/2$ and maximum sustainable yield $rK/4$. In Gordon's open-access fishery effort enters until total revenue equals total cost; with price $p$ and cost $c$ per unit effort, the bionomic equilibrium solves $pqK(1 - qE/r)E - cE = 0$, so $E_\infty = r(pqK - c)/(pq^2K)$ when $c < pqK$, and the corresponding stock is $X_\infty = K(1 - qE_\infty/r) = c/(pq)$, the stock at which revenue per unit effort $pqX$ equals its cost (Gordon 1954; Clark 2010, Chapter 2).

## Expansion

1. The Schaefer model, its steady states under constant effort and the yield–effort curve.

3. Stability of the steady stock under constant effort $E < r/q$.
