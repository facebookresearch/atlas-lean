# Uniqueness of the logistic solution

Let $K > 0$, $x_0 > 0$, $r \ge 0$ and $T \in \mathbb{R}$. If $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is
continuous on $[0, T]$, has right derivative $r x(t)(1 - x(t)/K)$ at every $t \in [0, T)$, and
$x(0) = x_0$, then $x$ coincides on $[0, T]$ with the [explicit logistic solution](logistic.md) $x(t) = K x_0 e^{rt}/(K + x_0(e^{rt} - 1))$.
