# Derivative of a weighted total along a solution

For a finite set `ι` of patches, a real matrix `C`, real rates `e`, a [solution](is-solution.md)
`p`, real weights `w_j` and `t ≥ 0`, the function `s ↦ ∑_j w_j p_j(s)` has derivative
`∑_j w_j F(p(t))_j` at `t`, where `F` is the [vector field](field.md); at `t = 0` the derivative is
two-sided.
