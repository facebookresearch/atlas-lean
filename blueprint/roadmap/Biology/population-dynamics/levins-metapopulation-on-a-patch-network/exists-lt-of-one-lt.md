# Uniform weak persistence above the threshold on an irreducible network

For a nonempty finite set `ι` of patches, an irreducible matrix `C` (nonnegative entries, and the
directed graph on the patches with an edge between `i` and `j` when `C_ij > 0` is strongly
connected), positive rates `e` and [persistence number](persistence-number.md) `R₀ > 1`, let `p` be
a [solution](is-solution.md) with `p_i(0) ∈ [0, 1]` for every patch `i` and `p_i(0) > 0` for some
patch `i`. Then for every `δ > 0` and every time `T` there are `t ≥ T` and a patch `i` with
`p_i(t) > 1 − 1/R₀ − δ`, that is, `limsup_{t → ∞} max_i p_i(t) ≥ 1 − 1/R₀`. The bound does not
depend on the solution.
