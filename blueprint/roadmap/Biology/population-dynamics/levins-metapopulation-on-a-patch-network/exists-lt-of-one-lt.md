# Uniform weak persistence above the threshold on an irreducible network

For a nonempty finite set `ι` of patches, an irreducible matrix `C` (nonnegative entries, and for
all patches `i` and `j`, `i = j` included, a chain `i = k₀, k₁, …, k_m = j` with `m ≥ 1` and
`C_{k_l k_{l+1}} > 0` for every `l`), positive rates `e` and
[persistence number](persistence-number.md) `R₀ > 1`, let `p` be a [solution](is-solution.md) with
`p_i(0) ∈ [0, 1]` for every patch `i` and `p_i(0) > 0` for some patch `i`. Then for every `δ > 0`
and every time `T` there are `t ≥ T` and a patch `i` with `p_i(t) > 1 − 1/R₀ − δ`, that is,
`limsup_{t → ∞} max_i p_i(t) ≥ 1 − 1/R₀`. The bound does not depend on the solution.
