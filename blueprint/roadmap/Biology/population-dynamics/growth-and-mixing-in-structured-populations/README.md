# Growth and mixing in structured populations

Linear models of populations spread over sites with site-specific growth rates and movement between sites. The asymptotic growth rate is the Perron root of growth plus mixing; it decreases with mixing intensity (Karlin's theorem and its continuous-time extension), which underlies source–sink dynamics and the reduction principle for dispersal.
