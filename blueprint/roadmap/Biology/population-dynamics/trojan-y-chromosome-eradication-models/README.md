# Trojan Y-chromosome eradication models

The Trojan Y-chromosome strategy releases genetically engineered YY supermales whose offspring are all male, so that the sex ratio collapses and an invasive population declines. The classical three-compartment ODE model of females, males and supermales is ill-posed (males can turn negative and solutions can blow up in finite time), while a discrete two-stage model with density-dependent juvenile survival always has a locally stable extinction state and at least one positive equilibrium, giving bistability.
