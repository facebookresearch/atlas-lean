# Cost-associated prey cooperation under predation fear

Prey whose per capita reproduction increases sublinearly with density (partial cooperation, $u^N$ with $1\le N<2$) and is suppressed by fear of predators show an Allee threshold, bistability and a Hopf bifurcation driven by the degree of cooperation.
