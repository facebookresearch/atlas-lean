# Genealogically reinforced branching processes

In many populations an individual's reproduction depends on its living relatives, for example through resource sharing that follows genealogical proximity. Letting offspring probabilities depend on a distance-weighted count of living kin within a bounded genealogical distance gives a branching process with genealogical reinforcement; its parameters are identifiable from a few observed branching events with varied kin structure, independent branching can be tested by a likelihood ratio with a simulated null, and the process is a multitype branching process on the finite space of bounded-depth subtrees.
