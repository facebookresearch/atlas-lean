# Clonal statistics of stem cell self-renewal

Multi-type branching-process models of cell fate in homeostatic tissues, and what lineage-tracing clone sizes can and cannot reveal about how stem cells renew. Homeostasis forces a renewing compartment at the apex of the cell-state network; whether its cell number is conserved splits all models into two classes, each with a universal rescaled clone size distribution in the long-time or high-renewal limit.
