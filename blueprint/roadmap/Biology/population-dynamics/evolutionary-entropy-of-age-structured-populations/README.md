# Evolutionary entropy of age-structured populations

Evolutionary entropy $H=S/T$ measures how spread out reproduction is over the life cycle per unit generation time, where $S$ is the entropy of the age distribution of reproduction in a stable population and $T$ the generation time. It splits the Malthusian growth rate as $r=H+\Phi$ with the reproductive potential $\Phi$, transforms covariantly under changes of time unit, and can be recovered exactly from age-class data when within-class information is retained.
