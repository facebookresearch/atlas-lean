# Logistic branching conditioned on survival

The logistic branching process (linear births and deaths plus pairwise competition) goes extinct almost surely. Conditioning on survival, through the Yaglom limit or the Q-process, is analyzed through a moment duality with a Wright–Fisher diffusion with selection and mutation.
