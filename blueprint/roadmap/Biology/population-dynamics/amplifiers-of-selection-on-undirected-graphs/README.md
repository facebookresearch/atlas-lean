# Amplifiers of selection on undirected graphs

An amplifier of selection is a population structure on which an advantageous mutant fixes with higher probability than in a well-mixed population of the same size. On undirected graphs the Star was long the strongest known amplifier. These articles give the exact fixation probability on the Star, a modular lower bound for Metastars (Stars whose leaves are small graphs), and the Comet and Comet-swarm graphs that beat the Star for some fitness values.
