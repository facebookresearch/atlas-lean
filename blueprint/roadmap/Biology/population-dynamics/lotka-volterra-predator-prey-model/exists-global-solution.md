# Positive initial populations have a positive solution on all of ℝ

Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta > 0$ and $x_0, y_0 > 0$. Then there are
$x, y : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $x(0) = x_0$ and $y(0) = y_0$, differentiable at every real
$t$ with

$$x'(t) = \alpha x(t) - \beta x(t) y(t), \qquad y'(t) = \delta x(t) y(t) - \gamma y(t)$$

and with $x(t) > 0$ and $y(t) > 0$ for every real $t$, negative times included.
