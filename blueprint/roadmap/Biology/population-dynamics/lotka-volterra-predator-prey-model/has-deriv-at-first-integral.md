# Lotka–Volterra first integral

Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, t \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $x, y \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ have
derivatives $x'(t) = \alpha x(t) - \beta x(t) y(t)$ and $y'(t) = \delta x(t) y(t) - \gamma y(t)$ at $t$,
with $x(t) > 0$ and $y(t) > 0$. Then $s \mapsto V(x(s), y(s))$ has derivative $0$ at $t$, where $V$ is
the [first integral](first-integral.md).
