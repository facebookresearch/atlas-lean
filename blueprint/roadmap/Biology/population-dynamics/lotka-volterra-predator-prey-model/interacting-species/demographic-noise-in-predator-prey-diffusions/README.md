# Demographic noise in predator–prey diffusions

Diffusion approximations of predator–prey dynamics inherit their noise covariance from the stoichiometry of the underlying events, not from the deterministic drift. Representing predation and conversion as one coupled event or as two independent channels gives the same ODE but different covariance matrices, and only the coupled representation produces the negative predator–prey cross-covariance.
