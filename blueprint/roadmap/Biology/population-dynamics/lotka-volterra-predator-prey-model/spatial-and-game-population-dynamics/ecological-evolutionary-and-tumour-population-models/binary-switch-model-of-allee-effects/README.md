# Binary switch model of Allee effects

A continuum limit of a lattice population model in which proliferation and death rates switch when the number of occupied neighbours exceeds a threshold. Depending on the switch, the per-capita growth rate is logistic, a weak Allee effect, a strong Allee effect, or extinction.
