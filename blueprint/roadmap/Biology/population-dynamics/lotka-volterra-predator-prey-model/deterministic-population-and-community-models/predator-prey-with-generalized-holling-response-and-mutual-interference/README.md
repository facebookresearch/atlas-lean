# Predator-prey models with generalized Holling response and mutual interference

When the functional response $(x/(x+d))^{m_1}$ has exponent $m_1 < 1$ it has infinite slope at zero prey, and predator mutual interference enters as a power $y^{m_2}$ with $m_2 \le 1$. The vector field is then not Lipschitz on the prey-free axis, uniqueness fails there, and prey can be driven to extinction in finite time rather than only asymptotically. A prey refuge that shields a fixed fraction of prey from predation removes this finite-time extinction.
