# Periodic eco-epidemiological models with infected prey

In eco-epidemiological models a disease spreads within a prey population that is also eaten by a predator, and predators may preferentially catch infected prey. When the vital and contact rates oscillate seasonally with a common period, endemic coexistence appears as a periodic orbit rather than an equilibrium. Coincidence-degree methods give threshold conditions, in terms of time-averaged parameters and a family of auxiliary reproduction numbers, under which a positive periodic orbit with all three classes present exists.
