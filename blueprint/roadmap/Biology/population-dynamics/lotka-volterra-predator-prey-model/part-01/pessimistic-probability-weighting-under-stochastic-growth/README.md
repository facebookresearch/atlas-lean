# Pessimistic probability weighting under stochastic growth

In a stochastic age-structured population, fitness is the long-run exponential growth rate, which environmental variance in vital rates lowers below the growth rate of the mean projection matrix. A decision maker that ranks risky options by their mean vital rates (expected utility) therefore overvalues risky options, and the correction selection favors is pessimistic rank-dependent probability weighting.
