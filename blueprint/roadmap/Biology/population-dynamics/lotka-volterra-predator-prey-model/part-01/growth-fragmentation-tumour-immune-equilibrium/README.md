# Growth-fragmentation tumour-immune equilibrium

Tumour cells structured by size grow and divide according to a growth-fragmentation equation while diffusing antitumour and protumour immune cells, driven by chemotaxis and tumour-secreted cytokines, kill or support them. An equilibrium (dormancy) phase occurs when immune kill balances the principal growth-fragmentation eigenvalue; protumour activation above a critical tumour mass can break that equilibrium.
