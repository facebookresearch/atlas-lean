# Exclusion of species `2`

**Exclusion of species `2`** (first species wins): for positive parameters with
`α₁₂ < K₁ / K₂` and `α₂₁ > K₂ / K₁`, every solution on `[0, ∞)` with both initial densities
positive converges to `(K₁, 0)`.

## Sources

- S.-B. Hsu, lecture notes, §3.12 "Competition models, Mutualism or Symbiosis" (NTHU), case (ii) / Fig. 12.2 (global convergence over all interior orbits derived from the planar phase analysis).
