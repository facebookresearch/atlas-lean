# Stable coexistence

For positive parameters with `α₁₂ < K₁ / K₂` and
`α₂₁ < K₂ / K₁`, the interior equilibrium has both coordinates positive, and every solution on
`[0, ∞)` with both initial densities positive converges to it.

## Sources

- S.-B. Hsu, lecture notes, §3.12 "Competition models, Mutualism or Symbiosis" (NTHU), case (iii) / Fig. 12.3 (global convergence over all interior orbits derived from the planar phase analysis).
