# Ecological network inference from steady-state samples

Cross-sectional samples of a microbial community taken at different equilibria, each containing a different subset of taxa, carry information about who promotes or inhibits whom. If the sign of each pairwise interaction does not change across the observed states, differences between steady states sharing a taxon constrain the sign pattern of that taxon's row of the Jacobian; under generalized Lotka–Volterra dynamics the steady states sharing a taxon lie exactly on a hyperplane whose coefficients are the interaction strengths.
