# Optimal harvesting of size-structured populations with environmental feedback

In a size-structured population whose growth and mortality depend on a scalar environmental output (for example total weighted biomass), and whose recruits enter at a fixed flux, a stationary harvesting policy determines the equilibrium only through a scalar fixed-point equation for the environmental level. The derivative of that closure map controls everything: it decides whether equilibria vary smoothly or fold, it guarantees existence of an optimal policy on a nonresonant branch, and, through a rank-one adjoint correction, it shapes the bang-bang structure of optimal size-selective harvesting.
