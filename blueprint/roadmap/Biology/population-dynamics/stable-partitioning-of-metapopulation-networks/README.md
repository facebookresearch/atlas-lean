# Stable partitioning of metapopulation networks

Metapopulation networks with symmetric dispersal are stabilized by the Laplacian of the dispersal graph, and a Fiedler value (algebraic connectivity) above a threshold $\tau$ guarantees stability of the linearized dynamics. Loss of dispersal links splits the network; spectral inequalities on internal and external dispersal costs decide whether the resulting sub-networks remain stable.
