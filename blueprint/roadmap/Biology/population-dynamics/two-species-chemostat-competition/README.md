# Two-species chemostat competition

Two microbial species competing for a single limiting substrate in a chemostat. Each species is characterized by its break-even substrate concentration, and the species with the lower one excludes the other; at equal break-even concentrations the equilibria merge into a non-hyperbolic point where numerical transport codes can fail to reproduce the switch.
