# Optimal harvest allocation in stream metapopulations

Logistic patch populations linked by random dispersal and downstream drift, harvested with a fixed total effort split across patches. Biomass and yield at the positive equilibrium respond differently to the split; in a two-patch stream the optimum is always a corner (all upstream or all downstream) for biomass, with switching thresholds in the growth rate.
