# Task allocation in social insect colonies

Dynamical models of division of labour in social insect colonies coupling colony growth with an Allee effect, task-specific mortality, response thresholds to task demand, dynamically changing demands, and social-contact-driven task switching. For two task groups (inside versus outside the nest) the models give explicit equilibrium allocations, the dependence of mature colony size on allocation, and the direction in which social interactions shift workers.
