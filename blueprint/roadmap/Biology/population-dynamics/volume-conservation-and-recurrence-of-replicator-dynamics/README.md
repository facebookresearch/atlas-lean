# Volume conservation and recurrence of replicator dynamics

Replicator dynamics of zero-sum evolutionary games, the prototype being Rock-Paper-Scissors with its cyclic dominance, neither converge nor diverge in the interior. In cumulative-payoff coordinates the flow conserves volume exactly when every self-interaction game is equivalent to a zero-sum game, and then the presence or absence of an interior Nash equilibrium decides between Poincaré recurrence and collapse of all interior orbits to a boundary face. The results cover one-population evolutionary games and graphical polymatrix games with self-loops.
