# Backward bifurcation and strong Allee effects in matrix models

Nonlinear matrix models of structured populations lose stability of extinction exactly when the inherent growth rate (equivalently the net reproductive number) crosses $1$, where a continuum of positive equilibria bifurcates. When positive density feedback (component Allee effects) dominates at low density, the bifurcation is backward, and with negative feedback at high density the result is a strong Allee effect: extinction and survival are both stable for growth rates just below $1$.
