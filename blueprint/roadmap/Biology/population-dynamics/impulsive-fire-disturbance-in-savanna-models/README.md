# Impulsive fire disturbance in savanna models

Tree–grass dynamics modeled by a one-sided competitive Lotka–Volterra flow, interrupted by periodic fires that instantly remove grass and, depending on how much grass burns, trees. The resulting flow-kick map has grassland, forest and savanna fixed points whose transcritical bifurcations in fire frequency and intensity can be located by invasion multipliers, and it exhibits several bistability regimes absent from the underlying flow.
