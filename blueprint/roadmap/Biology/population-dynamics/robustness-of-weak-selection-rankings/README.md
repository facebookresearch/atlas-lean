# Robustness of weak-selection rankings

Many analytical results on evolutionary games in finite populations are derived for weak selection, where the abundance of each strategy in the mutation–selection equilibrium is a first-order perturbation of the neutral uniform distribution. Whether the resulting ranking of strategies persists at stronger selection depends on the imitation function: for two-strategy pairwise games under the Fermi rule the ranking never changes, but for multiplayer games, other imitation functions, or three or more strategies the ranking can change at intermediate selection intensities.
