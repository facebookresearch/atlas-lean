# Evolutionary regime transitions on graphs

Classification of small undirected population graphs under the birth–death Moran process into isothermal graphs, amplifiers and suppressors of selection by comparing their fixation probability with that of a well-mixed population. Exact symbolic computation shows that the regime can change with the mutant's fitness, once or several times.
