# Quasispecies distribution on the sharp peak landscape

Explicit stationary solutions of Eigen's quasispecies model: on the sharp peak landscape in the long-chain regime the distribution of Hamming classes around the master sequence has closed forms in terms of Eulerian and Stirling numbers, and in general the Perron–Frobenius quasispecies admits a probabilistic representation through excursions of the mutation chain weighted by fitness.
