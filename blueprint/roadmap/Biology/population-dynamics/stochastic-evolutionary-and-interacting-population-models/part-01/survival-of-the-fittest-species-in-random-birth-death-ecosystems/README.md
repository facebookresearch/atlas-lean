# Survival of the fittest species in random birth–death ecosystems

Stochastic models of species turnover in which new species arrive with random fitness and the least fit species dies, related to the Bak–Sneppen model of self-organized criticality. A critical fitness threshold separates species doomed to extinction from those that survive, and fluctuations at the threshold follow Brownian local-time laws.
