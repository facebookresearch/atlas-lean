# Stochastic metapopulation growth with dispersal

Long-term growth of populations spread over patches with spatially correlated environmental noise and dispersal between patches. The growth rate is an average over the stationary patch distribution, and fast dispersal reduces the variance penalty, so dispersal can rescue populations that decline in every patch.
