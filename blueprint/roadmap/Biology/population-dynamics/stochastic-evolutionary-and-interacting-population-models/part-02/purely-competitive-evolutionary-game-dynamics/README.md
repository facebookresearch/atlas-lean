# Purely competitive evolutionary game dynamics

Evolutionary dynamics in which randomly paired individuals compete head to head and the winner's type is propagated with probability proportional to its payoff in the pairing, as in tournaments, intrasexual selection or emulation learning. Dominant types take over, cyclic dominance gives an unstable interior equilibrium, and small mutation rates can stabilize it through a Hopf bifurcation.
