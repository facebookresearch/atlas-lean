# Permanence of discrete two-species models with Allee effects

Coexistence (permanence) for discrete-time two-species interactions whose per capita growth rates are non-monotonic, as with weak or strong Allee effects. External Lyapunov exponents measure invasion rates into possibly complicated boundary dynamics, and a relative-nonlinearity correction reduces their sign to conditions at a single boundary point.
