# Discrete plant–herbivore maps with monotone plant growth

Discrete-time plant–herbivore models $P_{t+1}=F(P_t)e^{-aH_t}$ in which plant biomass alone follows a Beverton–Holt-type monotone map $F$ and herbivores convert either the pre-growth or the post-growth biomass they consume; the attack rate times the plant equilibrium governs herbivore extinction, the transcritical birth of a unique coexistence state, and uniform persistence.
