# Generalized modelling of predator–prey cycles

Generalized models analyze ecological dynamics without fixing functional responses, parametrizing the system by scale and elasticity parameters. Extended from equilibria to periodic orbits, these parameters become periodic functions constrained by a "moduli-space flow", and the Floquet stability of predator–prey cycles is expressed through their Fourier coefficients.
