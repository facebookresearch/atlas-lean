# Wave-speed sign in bistable Lotka–Volterra competition

Which of two strongly competing species invades is decided by the sign of the speed of the bistable travelling wave connecting the two single-species states. When the species differ only in diffusion rate, the "unity is not strength" conjecture predicts that the faster diffuser wins.
