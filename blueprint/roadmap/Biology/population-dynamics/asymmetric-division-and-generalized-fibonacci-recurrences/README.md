# Asymmetric division and generalized Fibonacci recurrences

Deterministic branching model of bacteria that divide asymmetrically: every division yields a faster-dividing and a slower-dividing child. Counts of cells, divisions and lifetime classes all satisfy the generalized Fibonacci recurrence $g_n = g_{n-k} + g_{n-m}$, and counting lattice paths in a weighted Pascal's triangle gives closed binomial formulas for them.
