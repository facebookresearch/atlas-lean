# Crop–pest–natural enemy model with maturation delay

A three-level food chain in which a crop is eaten by a pest and the pest by a stage-structured natural enemy whose juveniles need a fixed time to mature. The maturation delay enters both the interior equilibrium and its stability, producing stability switches and Hopf bifurcations.
