# Hopf cycles blowing up to infinity

Parameter families of planar population models in which the limit cycle created at a Hopf bifurcation grows without bound over a finite parameter interval. A Conley-index argument gives sufficient conditions, applied to Lotka–Volterra predators that cooperate at low density and compete at high density.
