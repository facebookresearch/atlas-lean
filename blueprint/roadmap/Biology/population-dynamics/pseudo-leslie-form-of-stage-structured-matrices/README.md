# Pseudo-Leslie form of stage-structured matrices

Stage-structured (Usher or Lefkovitch) projection matrices are similar to Leslie-shaped matrices whose first row may contain negative entries, which makes their powers and characteristic polynomials explicit; the associated ancestral Markov chains are similar but have different entropies.
