# Stationary stability in finite-population evolutionary dynamics

Evolutionary stability for finite populations with mutation, defined through the stationary distribution of Moran-type (incentive) and Wright–Fisher processes. For large populations the extrema of the stationary distribution are incentive-stable-state candidates and are local minima of a family of divergences between a state and its expected next state, which play the role of Lyapunov functions.
