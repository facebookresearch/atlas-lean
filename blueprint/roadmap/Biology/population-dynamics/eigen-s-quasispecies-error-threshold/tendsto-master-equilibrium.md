# Continuity of the interior equilibrium in the copying fidelity

Let $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $(Q_L)_{L \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of fidelities with
$Q_L \to Q_\infty$. Then the [interior equilibrium](master-equilibrium.md) $x^* = (\sigma Q - 1)/(\sigma - 1)$ satisfies $x^*(Q_L) \to x^*(Q_\infty)$ (with the quotient read
as $0$ when $\sigma = 1$).
