# Convergence above the threshold

Let $\sigma > 1$ and $\sigma Q > 1$. If $x \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ solves the [master-sequence equation](master-field.md) $x' = \sigma Q x - (\sigma x + (1 - x)) x$ at
every time $t \ge 0$ and $x(0) > 0$, then $x(t) \to x^* = (\sigma Q - 1)/(\sigma - 1)$ as
$t \to \infty$.
