# Population shares sum to one when the total size is nonzero

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories and real sizes $N_i$ with $\sum_j N_j \ne 0$, the
[population shares](population-share.md) sum to one: $\sum_i N_i / \sum_j N_j = 1$. The sizes may
have any sign, so the shares need not lie in $[0, 1]$; they do for a
[valid population](is-valid-population.md) ([bounds](population-share-mem-icc.md)). When the total
is $0$, every share is $0$ by the convention $x / 0 = 0$, and the shares sum to $0$.
