# With 0 ≤ deaths ≤ survivors at every age, zero survivors stay zero

Let $(l_x)$ and $(d_x)$ be real sequences indexed by the ages $x \in \mathbb{N}$ with
$l_{x+1} = l_x - d_x$ and $0 \le d_x \le l_x$ for every age $x$. If $l_k = 0$ at some age $k$, then
$l_x = 0$ at every age $x \ge k$: once nobody is left there are no deaths, so a closed cohort that
has died out stays extinct.
