# Directly standardized rate is the crude rate with the standard's sizes

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories and arbitrary real standard sizes $S_i$ and rates $T_i$, the
[directly standardized rate](directly-standardized-rate.md) of $T$ under the standard $S$ equals the
[crude rate](crude-rate.md) of a population with sizes $S_i$ and rates $T_i$,
$(\sum_i S_i T_i) / \sum_i S_i$; both are $0$ when $\sum_i S_i = 0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$.
This is the definition of the directly standardized rate.
