# Directly standardized rate is the crude rate with the standard's sizes

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories and arbitrary real standard sizes $S_i$ and rates $T_i$, the
directly standardized rate of $T$ under the standard $S$ equals the crude rate of a population with
sizes $S_i$ and rates $T_i$, the
[weighted mean](../../statistical-genetics/mendelian-randomization/weighted-mean.md)
$(\sum_i S_i T_i) / \sum_i S_i$ of the rates with the sizes $S_i$ as weights; both are $0$ when
$\sum_i S_i = 0$, by the convention $x / 0 = 0$. This is the definition of the directly
standardized rate.
