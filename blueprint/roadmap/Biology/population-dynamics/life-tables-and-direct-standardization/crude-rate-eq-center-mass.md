# Crude rate as the centre of mass of the rates with sizes as masses

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories and arbitrary real sizes $N_i$ and rates $T_i$, the
[crude rate](../../statistical-genetics/mendelian-randomization/weighted-mean.md) is the centre of mass of the points $T_i$ with masses $N_i$,
$(\sum_i N_i)^{-1} \sum_i N_i T_i$. The masses may be negative and need not sum to $1$; with the
convention $0^{-1} = 0$, the centre of mass, and so the crude rate, is $0$ when $\sum_i N_i = 0$.
This is the definition of the crude rate.
