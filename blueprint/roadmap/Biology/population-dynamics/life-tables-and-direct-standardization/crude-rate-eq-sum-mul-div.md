# Crude rate as total events over total size

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories and arbitrary real sizes $N_i$ and rates $T_i$, the
[crude rate](crude-rate.md) equals $(\sum_i T_i N_i) / \sum_i N_i$. With $T_i$ the rate of category
$i$ and $N_i$ its size, $T_i N_i$ is its number of events, so the crude rate is the total number of
events over the total size. The identity holds for all real sizes: when the total size is $0$, both
sides are $0$, by the conventions $0^{-1} = 0$ in the crude rate and $x / 0 = 0$ on the right.

Source: P. Das Gupta, *Standardization and Decomposition of Rates: A User's Manual*, U.S. Bureau of
the Census (1993), eq. (5.1).
