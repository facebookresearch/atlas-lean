# Deaths equal the number at risk times the death probability

For real sequences $(l_x)$ and $(d_x)$ indexed by the ages $x \in \mathbb{N}$, and an age $x$ with
$l_x > 0$, the [death probability](death-probability.md) $q_x = d_x / l_x$ satisfies
$l_x \, q_x = d_x$. No condition on $d_x$ is needed.

Source: Methods Protocol for the Human Mortality Database, version 6, eq. (80).
