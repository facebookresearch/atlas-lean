# For 0 ≤ deaths ≤ number at risk, death probability is between 0 and 1

For real sequences $(l_x)$ and $(d_x)$ indexed by the ages $x \in \mathbb{N}$, and an age $x$ with
$l_x > 0$ and $0 \le d_x \le l_x$, the [death probability](death-probability.md) $q_x = d_x / l_x$
satisfies $0 \le q_x \le 1$.
