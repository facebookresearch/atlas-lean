# Rate standardized to a valid standard lies between the extreme rates

For a finite set $\iota$ of categories, a [valid](is-valid-population.md) standard population with
real sizes $S_i \ge 0$ and $\sum_j S_j > 0$, real rates $T_i$, and real numbers $a$ and $b$ with
$a \le T_i \le b$ for every category $i$, the
[directly standardized rate](directly-standardized-rate.md) $(\sum_i S_i T_i) / \sum_i S_i$ lies in
$[a, b]$. The bounds are required for every category, including categories of standard size $0$. A
valid standard has at least one category, so taking for $a$ and $b$ the smallest and the largest
rate shows that the standardized rate lies between them.
