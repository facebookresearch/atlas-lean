# Two-scale selection ball-and-urn process

A Moran-type model of a trait favored within groups but disfavored between groups (for example, a fast-replicating virus strain that impairs host transmission). Each of $m$ groups holds $n$ individuals; groups are particles on $\{0,1/n,\dots,1\}$ located at their fraction of group-favored individuals. Large-population limits give a nonlinear integro-differential equation with Beta-distribution equilibria, or a Fleming–Viot process.
