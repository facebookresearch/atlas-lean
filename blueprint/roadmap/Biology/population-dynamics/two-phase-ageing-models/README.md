# Two-phase ageing models

Population models in which individuals pass through two successive ageing phases, coupled through births (newborns enter phase 1) and competition-dependent transition and death rates. Their age-structured PDE and ODE reductions have a reproduction-number threshold separating extinction from a unique globally stable coexistence state.
