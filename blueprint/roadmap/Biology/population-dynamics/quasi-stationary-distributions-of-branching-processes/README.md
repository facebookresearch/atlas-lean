# Quasi-stationary distributions of branching processes

Long-time behaviour of subcritical branching populations conditioned on survival: existence and uniqueness of quasi-stationary distributions, identification with the Yaglom limit, and particle-system approximations that make these conditioned laws computable, including for age-structured (non-Markov in size) Bellman-Harris processes.
