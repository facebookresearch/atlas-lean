# Exclusion of species `1`

**Exclusion of species `1`** (second species wins): for positive parameters with
`α₁₂ > K₁ / K₂` and `α₂₁ < K₂ / K₁`, every solution on `[0, ∞)` with both initial densities
positive converges to `(0, K₂)`.

## Sources

- S.-B. Hsu, lecture notes, §3.12 "Competition models, Mutualism or Symbiosis" (NTHU), case (i) / Fig. 12.1 (global convergence over all interior orbits derived from the planar phase analysis).
