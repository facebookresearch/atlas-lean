# Exclusion of species `2`

**Exclusion of species `2`** (case (iii)): for positive parameters with `α₁₂ < K₁ / K₂` and
`α₂₁ > K₂ / K₁`, every solution on `[0, ∞)` with both initial densities positive converges to
`(K₁, 0)`.

## Sources

- S.-B. Hsu, lecture notes, §3.12 "Competition models, Mutualism or Symbiosis" (NTHU), case (iii) (global convergence over all interior orbits derived from the planar phase analysis).
