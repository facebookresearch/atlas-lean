# Multilevel selection in group-structured Moran processes

Two strategies $A$ and $B$ play a $2\times2$ game in a finite population subdivided into groups. Individuals reproduce in proportion to fitness; groups that reach a maximal size occasionally split, and a random group is removed to keep the number of groups constant. When splitting is rare, fixation decomposes into fixation within a group followed by fixation of the group among groups. Mapping payoff to fitness exponentially makes both stages exactly solvable at every intensity of selection.
