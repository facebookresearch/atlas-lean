# Lotka–Volterra competition travelling waves

In the diffusive two-species Lotka–Volterra competition system, invasion of an empty habitat by two weakly competing species is carried by travelling waves joining extinction to coexistence. Their minimal speed is the larger of the two Fisher–KPP speeds, and near the boundary of the weak-competition regime the wave profiles need not be monotone.
