# Large-population limits of structured population PDMPs

Individual-based structured population models written as piecewise-deterministic Markov processes on finite measures: individuals move along a deterministic flow on a trait or age space and jump (birth, death, transition) at state-dependent rates. Under renormalization by the population size these converge to deterministic integro-differential equations, which become transport PDEs when densities exist. Examples include basic and age-structured SIR models, host–pathogen interactions and the Bell–Anderson cell-size model.
