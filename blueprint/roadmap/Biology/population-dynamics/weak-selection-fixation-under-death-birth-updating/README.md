# Weak-selection fixation under death-Birth updating

For death-Birth updating on a weighted undirected graph, the first-order expansion of the fixation probability of a single random mutant in its selection coefficient is governed by coalescence times of random walks. This defines an effective population size; isothermal graphs then match the well-mixed population to first order, and graphs such as the Fan amplify selection only over a bounded range of mutant fitness.
