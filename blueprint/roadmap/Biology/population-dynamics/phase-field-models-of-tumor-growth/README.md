# Phase-field models of tumor growth

Diffuse-interface (phase-field) models describe a tumor by a volume fraction that is close to $1$ inside the tumor, close to $0$ in healthy tissue, and varies smoothly across an interface of small width. Tumor fractions evolve by mass balance driven by the gradient of a chemical potential, the variational derivative of a free energy, and are coupled to nutrients, flow and, in nonlocal variants, long-range adhesion.
