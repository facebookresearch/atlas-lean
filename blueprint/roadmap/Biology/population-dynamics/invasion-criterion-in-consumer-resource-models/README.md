# Invasion criterion in consumer-resource models

In consumer-resource models where strains grow on shared or private nutrients and may be harmed by toxins, a rare strain can establish in a resident community exactly when its per capita growth rate at the resident equilibrium exceeds its dilution rate. Nutrient competition and interference competition enter this criterion through different terms, which explains when a newcomer can displace a resident strain.
