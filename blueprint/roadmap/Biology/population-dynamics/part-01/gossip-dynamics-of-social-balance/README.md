# Gossip dynamics of social balance

Continuous-time matrix differential equations for a network of signed reputations, whose normalized solutions converge in finite time to a balanced two-faction sign pattern; the choice of gossip mechanism decides whether balance is generic and whether the resulting reputation strategy resists defectors in evolutionary simulations of indirect reciprocity.
