# Pulsed immunochemotherapy of tumour–immune dynamics

Impulsive differential equations model periodic infusions of cytotoxic T lymphocytes (CTLs) and chemotherapy doses in a Kuznetsov-type tumour–immune system. Floquet theory for the linearisation around the tumour-free periodic orbit yields an explicit minimum CTL dose per period for tumour eradication, beyond which a positive periodic orbit bifurcates.
