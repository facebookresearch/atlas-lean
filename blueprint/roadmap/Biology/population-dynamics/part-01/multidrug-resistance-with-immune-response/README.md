# Multidrug resistance with immune response

Within-host models of a bacterial infection treated with several antibiotics couple logistic growth of drug-sensitive and drug-resistant strains, drug-induced mutation to resistance, drug killing, and an immune response recruited in proportion to the bacterial load. Their equilibria separate treatment outcomes in which only resistant bacteria persist from those in which both strains coexist with the immune response.
