# Evolution of Bayesian behavior

Binary-choice models of natural selection under systematic reproductive risk: the behavior that dominates the population in the long run maximizes the expected log of offspring number conditional on whatever environmental information individuals observe. The selected behavior is therefore a posterior expectation, so populations of mindless individuals appear to perform Bayesian inference, with probability matching as the stationary special case.
