# PDE models of multilevel selection

In the limit of many large groups, simultaneous within-group (individual) and among-group (collective) competition yields a nonlocal hyperbolic PDE for the distribution of group compositions. Cooperation survives iff among-group selection exceeds an explicit threshold, and the long-run collective payoff is capped by the payoff of all-cooperator groups.
