# Fear, refuge and hunting cooperation in predator–prey models

A planar predator–prey model in which predator presence lowers prey births and raises prey crowding (fear), a predator-density-dependent fraction of prey hides (refuge), predators cooperate while hunting, and predators are harvested at a saturating rate. Its boundary equilibria are linked by a transcritical bifurcation, the predator invades the prey-only state at an explicit threshold, and a seasonally forced version is permanent under explicit inequalities.
