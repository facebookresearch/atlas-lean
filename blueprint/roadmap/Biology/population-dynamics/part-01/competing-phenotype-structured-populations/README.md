# Competing phenotype-structured populations

Two populations that differ only in their rate of spontaneous phenotypic variation compete for a shared nutrient whose level sets the fittest phenotype. Gaussian solutions of the non-local parabolic model reduce the dynamics to ODEs, showing that low variability wins in constant environments while the outcome in periodic environments depends on a trade-off between lag behind the moving optimum and variance load.
