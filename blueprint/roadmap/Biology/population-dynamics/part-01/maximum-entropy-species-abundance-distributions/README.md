# Maximum-entropy species abundance distributions

The maximum entropy formalism predicts the species abundance distribution (SAD) of a highly diverse community as the distribution of maximal entropy relative to a scale-invariant prior, subject to a few constraints. With the uninformative prior uniform in $\log n$ and a fixed mean abundance it yields Fisher's log-series; adding constraints on $\log n$ moves the prediction toward gamma-type and log-normal-type distributions.
