# Oncolytic virus tumour specificity

Oncolytic viruses replicate preferentially in tumour cells but also infect some normal tissue. In models that couple tumour cells, normal cells, free virus and immune responses, the virus reproduction number splits into a tumour and a normal-cell contribution, and their ratio quantifies how tumour-specific the infection is.
