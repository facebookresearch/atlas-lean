# Quasi-stationarity of density-dependent birth-and-death processes

One-dimensional birth-and-death models of a population with carrying-capacity scale $K$ (logistic and generalizations) go extinct almost surely, but only after a time exponentially large in $K$. Spectral analysis of the generator on $\ell^2(\pi)$ gives sharp asymptotics for the mean extinction time from the quasi-stationary distribution, a $1/\log K$ spectral gap, and a Gaussian approximation of the quasi-stationary law.
