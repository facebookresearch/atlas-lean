# Gut transit compartment models

Ingested bacteria pass through a sequence of gut regions while growing, dying or being retained. Modelling the regions as a chain of linear compartments links the distribution of times at which the bacteria appear in faeces to their region-specific net growth rates, so fecal time series can be used to infer where in the gut a population grows or declines.
