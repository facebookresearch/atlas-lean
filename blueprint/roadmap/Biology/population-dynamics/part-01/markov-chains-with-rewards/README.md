# Markov chains with rewards

Absorbing Markov chains for life cycles in which every transition pays a random reward, such as offspring produced. A closed form gives every moment of the accumulated lifetime reward, and hence the variance, coefficient of variation and skewness of lifetime reproductive output beyond its mean; backward recursions give the same moments over a finite horizon and in variable environments, and the variance splits into parts within and between life trajectories.
