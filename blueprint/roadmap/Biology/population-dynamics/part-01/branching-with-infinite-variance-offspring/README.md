# Branching with infinite-variance offspring

Continuous-time Galton–Watson processes and branching Brownian motions whose offspring law is in the domain of attraction of a stable law (Lamperti's family, $1 < \gamma < 2$), used as models of epidemic outbreaks with superspreading. Closed-form extinction-time laws and hitting probabilities show how infinite offspring variance changes the tails compared with binary branching.
