# Positive solutions give solutions of Wright's equation

For real numbers `r`, `K > 0` and `τ ≥ 0`, if `x` is a [solution](is-solution.md) of the delayed
logistic equation with `x(t) > 0` for every `t ≥ -τ`, then `y = log(x / K)` is a
[solution of Wright's equation](is-wright-solution.md) `y'(t) = -r (e^{y(t - τ)} - 1)`.
