# With zero delay every solution is the logistic solution

For `r ≥ 0`, `K > 0` and a function `φ` with `φ(0) > 0`, every [solution](is-solution.md) `x` of the
delayed logistic equation with delay `τ = 0` and history `φ` equals the
[explicit logistic solution](../logistic-growth-equation/logistic.md)
`t ↦ K φ(0) e^{r t} / (K + φ(0) (e^{r t} - 1))` on `[0, ∞)`.
