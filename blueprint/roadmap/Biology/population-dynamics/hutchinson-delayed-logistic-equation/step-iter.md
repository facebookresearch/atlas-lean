# Iterates of the method-of-steps map

For real numbers `r`, `K` and `τ`, a function `ψ` and a natural number `n`, the `n`-th iterate is
obtained by applying the [step map](step-map.md) with history `ψ` to `ψ` itself `n` times; the
`0`-th iterate is `ψ`. The [method-of-steps solution](solution.md) evaluates these iterates, started
at the [extended history](history-ext.md).
