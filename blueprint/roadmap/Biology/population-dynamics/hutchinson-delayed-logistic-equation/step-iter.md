# Iterates of the method-of-steps map

For real numbers `r`, `K` and `τ`, a function `ψ` and a natural number `n`, the `n`-th iterate is
obtained by applying the [step map](step-map.md) with history `ψ` to `ψ` itself `n` times. For
`τ ≥ 0` the iterates of order at least `n` agree on `(-∞, n τ]`, and for `τ > 0`, starting from the
[extended history](history-ext.md), the `n`-th iterate equals the
[method-of-steps solution](solution.md) on `(-∞, n τ]`.
