# Local asymptotic stability of the carrying capacity for rτ < √5 − 1

For `r > 0`, `K > 0` and `τ > 0` with `r τ < √5 - 1`, the positive equilibrium `K` of the delayed
logistic equation `x'(t) = r x(t) (1 - x(t - τ) / K)` is locally asymptotically stable: for every
`ε > 0` there is `δ > 0` such that every [solution](is-solution.md) `x` whose history `φ` satisfies
`|φ(s) - K| ≤ δ` for every `s ∈ [-τ, 0]` has `|x(t) - K| ≤ ε` for every `t ≥ -τ` and
`x(t) → K` as `t → ∞`. Since [`√5 - 1 < π / 2`](sqrt-five-sub-one-lt-pi-div-two.md), the range
`0 < τ < (√5 - 1) / r` is a proper part of the range `0 ≤ τ < τ_c` of the
[characteristic-root condition](re-neg-of-char-fun-eq-zero.md).
