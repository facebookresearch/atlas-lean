# Equation for the relative deviation from the carrying capacity

For real numbers `r`, `K ≠ 0` and `τ`, a [solution](is-solution.md) `x` of the delayed logistic
equation and `t ≥ 0`, the relative deviation `u = x / K - 1` from the equilibrium `K` has right
derivative `u'(t) = -r u(t - τ) (1 + u(t))` at `t`. Its linear part is the linearized equation
`u'(t) = -r u(t - τ)`.
