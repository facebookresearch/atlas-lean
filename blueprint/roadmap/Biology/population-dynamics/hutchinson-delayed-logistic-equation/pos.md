# Solutions with a positive initial value stay positive

For real numbers `r`, `K` and `τ ≥ 0`, a [solution](is-solution.md) `x` of the delayed logistic
equation with history `φ` and `φ(0) > 0` satisfies `x(t) > 0` for every `t ≥ 0`. The result holds
for all real `r` and `K`; in the model they are positive.
