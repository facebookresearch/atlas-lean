# The stability range rτ < √5 − 1 as a range of delays

For `r > 0` and every real `τ`, `r τ < √5 - 1` if and only if `τ < (√5 - 1) / r`. This writes the
delay range of [local asymptotic stability](local-asymptotic-stability.md) in terms of `τ`; the
bound lies [below the critical delay](sqrt-five-sub-one-div-lt-critical-delay.md).
