# Above the critical delay a characteristic root has positive real part

For `r > 0` and `τ > τ_c = π / (2 r)`, the [critical delay](critical-delay.md), the
[characteristic function](char-fun.md) `z + r e^{-z τ}` has a root `z` with `Re z > 0` and
`0 < Im z < π / τ`; so the linearized equation `u'(t) = -r u(t - τ)` has the
[exponential solution](has-deriv-at-cexp-iff-char-fun-eq-zero.md) `e^{z t}`, which grows without
bound.
