# A small history bounds a Wright solution on the first two windows

For `r ≥ 0`, `τ > 0`, a [solution](is-wright-solution.md) `y` of Wright's equation
`y'(t) = -r (e^{y(t - τ)} - 1)` and a real `δ` with `|y(s)| ≤ δ` for every `s ∈ [-τ, 0]` and
`δ (1 + 2 r τ)² ≤ 1`, `|y(s)| ≤ δ (1 + 2 r τ)²` for every `s ∈ [-τ, 2 τ]`.
