# Below the critical delay characteristic roots have negative real part

For `r > 0` and `0 ≤ τ < τ_c = π / (2 r)`, the [critical delay](critical-delay.md), every root `z` of
the [characteristic function](char-fun.md) `z + r e^{-z τ}` has `Re z < 0`, the characteristic-root
condition for the linearized equation `u'(t) = -r u(t - τ)`.
