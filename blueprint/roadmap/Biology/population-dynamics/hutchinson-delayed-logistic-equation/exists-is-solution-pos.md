# Global existence of a positive solution from a positive history value

For a delay `τ ≥ 0`, a growth rate `r ≥ 0`, a carrying capacity `K > 0` and a history `φ` continuous
on `[-τ, 0]` with `φ(0) > 0`, the delayed logistic equation has a [solution](is-solution.md) `x`
with history `φ` and `x(t) > 0` for every `t ≥ 0`.
