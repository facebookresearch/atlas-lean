# Juvenile–adult delay predator–prey models

Predator–prey systems with an age-structured predator (juveniles that mature at a fixed age, then adults), reduced from McKendrick-type PDEs to (neutral) delay differential equations. The derivation fixes the juvenile survival factor and the admissible histories, and delay-dependent stability switches of the coexistence equilibrium are located by the Beretta–Kuang method.
