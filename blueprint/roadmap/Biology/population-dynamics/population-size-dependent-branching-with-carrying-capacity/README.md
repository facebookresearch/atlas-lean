# Population-size-dependent branching with carrying capacity

General branching populations whose reproduction is supercritical below a carrying capacity $K$, critical at $K$ and subcritical above it. Such populations establish within $O(\log K)$ time with positive probability, linger near $K$ for a time exponential in $K$, and finally go extinct in $O(\log K)$ time once they leave the band around $K$. A binary-splitting example makes the exponential persistence constant explicit.
