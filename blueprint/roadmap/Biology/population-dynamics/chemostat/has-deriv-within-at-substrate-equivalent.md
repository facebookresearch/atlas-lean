# Rate of change of the substrate equivalent

Let $\mu : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and let $D$, $S_f$ and $y$ be real, a quotient by $0$ being read as $0$. If
a curve $t \mapsto (X(t), S(t))$ has at a time $t$, within a set $s \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, the derivative given
by the [chemostat vector field](vector-field.md), then its [substrate equivalent](substrate-equivalent.md)
$Z = S + X / y$ has derivative $D (S_f - Z(t))$ at $t$ within $s$: the growth terms $\mu(S) X / y$ cancel.
