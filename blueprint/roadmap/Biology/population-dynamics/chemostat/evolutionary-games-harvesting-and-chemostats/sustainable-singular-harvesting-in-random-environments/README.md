# Sustainable singular harvesting in random environments

Ergodic singular stochastic control of a harvested diffusion population: maximize the long-run average yield, in expectation or almost surely, over all non-decreasing cumulative harvest processes. The optimum is a threshold policy that reflects the population at a level where the per-capita growth balances the scale and speed of the diffusion.
