# Rock–paper–scissors coevolution of payoffs

Generalized rock–paper–scissors interactions in which the payoff matrix of one evolving population is itself shaped by a second population that tracks it, modeling an environment or a coevolving partner that responds to the dominant strategy. Replicator dynamics then form a volume-preserving system, and Poincaré recurrence shows that diversity is maintained by cycling rather than by convergence.
