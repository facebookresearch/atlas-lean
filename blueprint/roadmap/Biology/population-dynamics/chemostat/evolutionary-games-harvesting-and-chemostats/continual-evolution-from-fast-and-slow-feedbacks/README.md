# Continual evolution from fast and slow feedbacks

General eco-evolutionary models in which a single evolving trait experiences a fast positive (Allee-type) feedback on its own phenotype and a slow negative feedback through an external variable such as a predator, host defense or abiotic factor. With sufficient separation of the ecological, mutational and feedback time scales, these systems evolve continually (Red Queen dynamics without coevolution) for almost all initial states, regardless of the precise functional forms.
