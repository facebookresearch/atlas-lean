# Stability of Lotka-Volterra equilibria

Conditions on the interaction matrix of the generalized Lotka-Volterra equations under which an equilibrium (with all species present, or with some absent) is stable. Diagonal stability and the $P$-matrix property give sufficient conditions; positivity of principal-minor sums and non-invasibility by absent species give necessary ones.
