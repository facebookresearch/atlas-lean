# Age-structured cell division rates from intermitotic times

Parameterizing the McKendrick–von Foerster age-structured model of a proliferating cell population (with quiescence and death, as under targeted anticancer drugs) from intermitotic-time distributions measured by live-cell microscopy, through the hazard-rate inversion linking the division rate to the observed lifespan density.
