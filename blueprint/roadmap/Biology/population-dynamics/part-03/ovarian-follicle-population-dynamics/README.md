# Ovarian follicle population dynamics

Compartmental (ODE), structured (PDE) and stochastic jump models of the ovarian follicle reserve: a large quiescent pool is slowly activated into growing follicles that mature or die quickly, with feedback of the growing population onto activation and death. The timescale contrast is exploited by singular perturbation, reducing the growing compartments to quasi-steady states driven by the quiescent pool.
