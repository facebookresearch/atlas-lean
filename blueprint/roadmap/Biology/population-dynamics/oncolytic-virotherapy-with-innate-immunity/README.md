# Oncolytic virotherapy with innate immunity

Ordinary differential equation models of oncolytic virotherapy follow uninfected tumour cells, virus-infected tumour cells and free virus; adding an innate immune population that is stimulated by infected cells and clears both infected cells and virions creates extra equilibria and makes the outcome depend jointly on the viral burst size and on the relative immune killing and decay rates.
