# Life-history speed under mass-mortality events

Two types competing for one renewable resource with equal lifetime reproductive success $\nu=\beta_i/\mu_i$ but different life-history speeds $\beta_i$ are quasi-neutral: the deterministic chemostat dynamics have a line of equilibria. With demographic noise and catastrophic mortality events, the large-carrying-capacity limit is a one-dimensional jump-diffusion for the type frequency on that line, and its fixation probability decides which speed selection favors.
