# Identifiability of stochastic flocking models

Parameters of stochastic differential equation models of collective motion are estimated by Euler-Maruyama quasi-likelihood. The growth rate of the minimum eigenvalue of the loss Hessian distinguishes identifiable from unidentifiable parameterizations, and in flocking models unidentifiability can arise from exact redundancy or from the ordered dynamical regime itself.
