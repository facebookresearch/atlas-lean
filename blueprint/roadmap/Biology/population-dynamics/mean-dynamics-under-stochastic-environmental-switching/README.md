# Mean dynamics under stochastic environmental switching

When a population experiences environment $E_1$ with probability $p$ and $E_2$ with probability $1-p$, the mean evolutionary dynamics are driven by the averaged landscape $pA_1+(1-p)A_2$ or the averaged incentive $p\varphi+(1-p)\psi$. The switching probability then decides fixation, phase portraits of $2\times2$ games, and stability of mixed states.
