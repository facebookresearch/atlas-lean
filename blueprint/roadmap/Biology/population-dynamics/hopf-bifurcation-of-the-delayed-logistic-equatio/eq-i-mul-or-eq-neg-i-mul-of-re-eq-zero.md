# At the critical delay every root with zero real part is ± i r

For `r > 0` and a root `z` of the
[characteristic function](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/char-fun.md) `z + r e^{-z τ}` at
the [critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md)
`τ = τ_c = π / (2 r)` with `Re z = 0`, `z = i r` or `z = -i r`. Conversely, both values are
roots: see [the root `i r`](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/char-fun-critical-delay-i-mul.md)
and [the root `-i r`](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/char-fun-critical-delay-neg-i-mul.md).
