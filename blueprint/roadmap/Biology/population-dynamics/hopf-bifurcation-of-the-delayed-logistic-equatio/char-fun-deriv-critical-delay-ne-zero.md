# At the critical delay, i r is a simple characteristic root

For `r > 0`, the [derivative in `z`](char-fun-deriv.md) of the characteristic function
`z + r e^{-z τ}` at `z = i r` and the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md) `τ_c = π / (2 r)` is
nonzero. Since `i r` is a
[root at the critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/char-fun-critical-delay-i-mul.md),
it is a simple root.
