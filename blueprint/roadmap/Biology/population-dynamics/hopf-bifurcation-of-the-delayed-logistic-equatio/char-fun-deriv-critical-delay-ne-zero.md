# At the critical delay the termwise z-derivative at i r is nonzero

For `r > 0`, the [termwise derivative formula in `z`](char-fun-deriv.md) of the characteristic
function `z + r e^{-z τ}` at `z = i r` and the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md) `τ_c = π / (2 r)` is
nonzero. There `i r` is a
[root of the characteristic function](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/char-fun-critical-delay-i-mul.md).
