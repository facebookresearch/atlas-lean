# Divided difference of the profile polynomial in its cos argument

For real numbers `ε`, `C`, `S` and `C'`, the polynomial
`D₁ = ε + ε² (2 (C + C') / 5 + S / 5) + ε³ ((C² + C C' + C'²) / 20 - 3 / 80)`. It is the divided
difference of the [profile polynomial](profile-poly.md) `P` in its first argument:
`P(ε, C, S) - P(ε, C', S) = (C - C') D₁`.
