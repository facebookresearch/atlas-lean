# Divided-difference formula of the profile polynomial, sin argument

For real numbers `ε`, `S`, `C'` and `S'`, the polynomial
`D₂ = ε² (C' / 5) + ε³ (9 / 80 - 3 (S² + S S' + S'²) / 20)`. It is the divided-difference
formula of the [profile polynomial](profile-poly.md) `P` in its second argument, at first argument
`C'`.
