# Divided difference of the profile polynomial in its sin argument

For real numbers `ε`, `S`, `C'` and `S'`, the polynomial
`D₂ = ε² (C' / 5) + ε³ (9 / 80 - 3 (S² + S S' + S'²) / 20)`. It is the divided difference of the
[profile polynomial](profile-poly.md) `P` in its second argument, at first argument `C'`:
`P(ε, C', S) - P(ε, C', S') = (S - S') D₂`.
