# Just above the critical delay the root branch has positive real part

For `r > 0`, there is `η > 0` such that for every delay `τ` with `τ_c < τ < τ_c + η`, where
`τ_c = π / (2 r)` is the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md), the
[root branch](root-branch.md) at `τ` has positive real part. With the left-side result, the root
crosses the imaginary axis from left to right as `τ` increases through `τ_c`.
