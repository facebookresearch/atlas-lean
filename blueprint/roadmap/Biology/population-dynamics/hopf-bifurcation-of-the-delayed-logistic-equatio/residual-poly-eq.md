# The Poincaré–Lindstedt cancellation of the Hopf residual

For real numbers `ε`, `c` and `s` with `c² + s² = 1`,
`ω P'(ε, c, s) + α P(ε, c ε² / 20 + s, s ε² / 20 - c) (1 + P(ε, c, s)) = ε⁴ R(ε, c, s)`, with
`ω = π / 2 - ε² / 20` and `α = π / 2 + (3π - 2) ε² / 40`, where `P` is the
[profile polynomial](profile-poly.md), `P'` the [derivative polynomial](profile-deriv-poly.md) and
`R` the [residual coefficient](residual-coeff.md). Here `ω` is the frequency [`ω(ε)`](hopf-freq.md),
`α` the parameter [`α(ε)`](hopf-param.md), and the shifted arguments approximate `cos(θ - ω)` and
`sin(θ - ω)` for `c = cos θ`, `s = sin θ`: with these approximations, the residual of the profile
has no terms below `ε⁴`.
