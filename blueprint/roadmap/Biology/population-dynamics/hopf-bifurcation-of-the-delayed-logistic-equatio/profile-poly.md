# Profile polynomial P(ε, C, S) of the Hopf expansion

For real numbers `ε`, `C` and `S`, the polynomial
`P(ε, C, S) = ε C + ε² ((2 C² - 1) / 5 + 2 S C / 10)`
`+ ε³ ((4 C³ - 3 C) / 80 + 3 (3 S - 4 S³) / 80)`.
It is the [profile](hopf-profile.md) formula `W_ε(θ)` with `cos θ`, `cos 2θ`, `sin 2θ`, `cos 3θ`
and `sin 3θ` written as `C`, `2 C² - 1`, `2 S C`, `4 C³ - 3 C` and `3 S - 4 S³`.
