# Hopf profile as a polynomial in cos θ and sin θ

For real numbers `ε`, `C` and `S`, the polynomial
`P(ε, C, S) = ε C + ε² ((2 C² - 1) / 5 + 2 S C / 10)`
`+ ε³ ((4 C³ - 3 C) / 80 + 3 (3 S - 4 S³) / 80)`.
For `C = cos θ` and `S = sin θ` it equals the [profile](hopf-profile.md) `W_ε(θ)`, by the double-
and triple-angle formulas.
