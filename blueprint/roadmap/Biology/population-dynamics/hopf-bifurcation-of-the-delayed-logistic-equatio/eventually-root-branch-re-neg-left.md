# Just below the critical delay the root branch has negative real part

For `r > 0`, there is `η > 0` such that for every delay `τ` with `τ_c - η < τ < τ_c`, where
`τ_c = π / (2 r)` is the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md), the
[root branch](root-branch.md) at `τ` has negative real part.
