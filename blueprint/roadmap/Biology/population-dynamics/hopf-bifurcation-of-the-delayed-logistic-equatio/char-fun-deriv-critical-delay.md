# At the critical delay the z-derivative at i r is 1 + (π / 2) i

For `r > 0`, the [derivative in `z`](char-fun-deriv.md) `1 - r τ e^{-z τ}` of the characteristic
function, at `z = i r` and the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md) `τ = τ_c = π / (2 r)`,
equals `1 + (π / 2) i`.
