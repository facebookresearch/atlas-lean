# At the critical delay the termwise z-derivative at i r is 1 + πi/2

For `r > 0`, the [termwise derivative formula in `z`](char-fun-deriv.md) `1 - r τ e^{-z τ}` of the
characteristic function, at `z = i r` and the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md) `τ = τ_c = π / (2 r)`,
equals `1 + (π / 2) i`.
