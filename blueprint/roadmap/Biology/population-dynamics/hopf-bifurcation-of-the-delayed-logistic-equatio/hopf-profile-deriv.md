# Termwise θ-derivative formula of the Hopf profile

For real numbers `ε` and `θ`, the value
`-ε sin θ + ε² (-2 sin 2θ / 5 + cos 2θ / 5) + ε³ (-3 sin 3θ / 80 + 9 cos 3θ / 80)`: the formula
of the [profile](hopf-profile.md) `W_ε(θ)` with each `cos(kθ)` replaced by `-k sin(kθ)` and each
`sin(kθ)` by `k cos(kθ)`.
