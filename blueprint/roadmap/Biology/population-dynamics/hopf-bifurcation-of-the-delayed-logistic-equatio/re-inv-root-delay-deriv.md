# Transversality: real part of the inverse root-delay derivative

For `r > 0`, the [derivative of the root delay](root-delay-deriv.md) at `i r`,
`d = (1 + (π / 2) i) / r²`, satisfies `Re(1 / d) = r² / (1 + (π / 2)²) > 0`. By the inverse function
rule, `1 / d` is the derivative at the critical delay of the [root branch](root-branch.md) through
`i r`, so the real part of that root increases at a positive rate at the critical delay.
