# Approximate Hopf orbit of the delayed logistic equation

For real numbers `r`, `K`, `ε` and `t`, the approximate orbit is `x_ε(t) = K (1 + v_ε(t / τ(ε)))`,
where `v_ε` is the [approximate orbit in scaled time](hopf-approx.md) and `τ(ε) = α(ε) / r` the
[delay](hopf-delay.md). With the convention `a / 0 = 0`, for `r = 0` it is the constant
`K (1 + v_ε(0))`; the intended domain is `r > 0`, where `τ(ε) > 0`.
