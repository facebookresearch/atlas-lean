# Approximate Hopf orbit in scaled time

For real numbers `ε` and `s`, the approximate orbit is `v_ε(s) = W_ε(ω(ε) s)`, where `W_ε` is the
[profile](hopf-profile.md) and `ω(ε) = π / 2 - ε² / 20` the [frequency](hopf-freq.md). It is an
[approximate solution](exists-hopf-approx-residual-le.md) of the scaled equation
`v'(s) = -α v(s - 1) (1 + v(s))` with [`α = α(ε)`](hopf-param.md).
