# Derivative of the Hopf profile as a polynomial in cos θ and sin θ

For real numbers `ε`, `C` and `S`, the polynomial
`-ε S + ε² (-2 (2 S C) / 5 + (2 C² - 1) / 5) + ε³ (-3 (3 S - 4 S³) / 80 + 9 (4 C³ - 3 C) / 80)`.
For `C = cos θ` and `S = sin θ` it equals the [derivative of the profile](hopf-profile-deriv.md)
at `θ`.
