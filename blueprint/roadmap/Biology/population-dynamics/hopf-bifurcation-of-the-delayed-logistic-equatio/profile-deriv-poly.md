# Polynomial P′(ε, C, S) of the termwise derivative formula

For real numbers `ε`, `C` and `S`, the polynomial
`-ε S + ε² (-2 (2 S C) / 5 + (2 C² - 1) / 5) + ε³ (-3 (3 S - 4 S³) / 80 + 9 (4 C³ - 3 C) / 80)`.
It is the [termwise derivative formula](hopf-profile-deriv.md) with `sin θ`, `sin 2θ`, `cos 2θ`,
`sin 3θ` and `cos 3θ` written as `S`, `2 S C`, `2 C² - 1`, `3 S - 4 S³` and `4 C³ - 3 C`.
