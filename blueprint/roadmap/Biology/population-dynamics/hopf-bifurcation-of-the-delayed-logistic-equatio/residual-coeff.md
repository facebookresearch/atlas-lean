# Coefficient of ε⁴ in the Hopf residual

For real numbers `ε`, `c` and `s`, an explicit polynomial `R(ε, c, s)` in `ε`, `c` and `s`, of
degree 10 in `ε`, with coefficients in `ℚ + ℚ π`; its terms free of `ε` are
`-3 c⁴ π / 50 - 2 c³ π s / 25 + 33 c² π / 400 + 43 c π s / 400 - 3 π / 160`. It is obtained by
reducing the residual of the profile modulo `c² + s² = 1`: on the unit circle `ε⁴ R(ε, c, s)` is
the residual of the [Poincaré–Lindstedt cancellation](residual-poly-eq.md).
