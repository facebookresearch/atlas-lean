# For ε ≠ 0 the approximate orbits lie beyond the critical delay

For `r > 0` and `ε ≠ 0`, the [delay](hopf-delay.md) `τ(ε)` of the approximate orbit satisfies
`τ(ε) > τ_c = π / (2 r)`, the
[critical delay](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/critical-delay.md). Beyond `τ_c` the
characteristic function has a
[root with positive real part](../hutchinson-delayed-logistic-equation/exists-char-fun-eq-zero-re-pos.md).
The statement concerns the approximate orbits; it does not give the direction of a true
bifurcating orbit.
