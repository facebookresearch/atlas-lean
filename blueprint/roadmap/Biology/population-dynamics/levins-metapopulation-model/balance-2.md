# Colonization–extinction balance of the Levins model

For real numbers `c`, `e` and `p`, the colonization–extinction balance of the Levins model is
`c p (1 - p) - e p`. Here `p` is the fraction of occupied habitat patches: occupied patches colonize
the empty fraction `1 - p` at rate `c`, and local populations go extinct at rate `e`. The intended
domain is `c, e ≥ 0` and `0 ≤ p ≤ 1`, but the expression is defined for all real arguments. It is
the right-hand side of the [Levins equation](is-solution-2.md) `p' = c p (1 - p) - e p`.
