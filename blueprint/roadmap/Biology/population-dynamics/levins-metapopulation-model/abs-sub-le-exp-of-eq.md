# For m > 0, exponential decay of the distance to a positive equilibrium

For `c ≥ 0`, `m > 0` and every real `e`, let `q > 0` be a zero of the [balance](balance.md),
`f(q) = 0`. Every [forward solution of the equation with immigration](is-solution.md) `p` with
`p(0) ≥ 0` satisfies `|p(t) - q| ≤ |p(0) - q| e^{-(m / q) t}` for every `t ≥ 0`.
