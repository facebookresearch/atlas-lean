# For m > 0, the immigration equilibrium is positive and at most 1

For `c ≥ 0`, `e ≥ 0` and `m > 0`, the [immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md) satisfies
`0 < p̂ ≤ 1`. This includes `c ≤ e` with `e > 0`, where without immigration every Levins solution
starting in `[0, 1]` tends to `0`
([global stability of extinction](extinction-is-globally-stable.md)).
