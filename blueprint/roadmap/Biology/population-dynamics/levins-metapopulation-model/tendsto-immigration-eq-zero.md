# For c > 0, as m ↓ 0, p̂ tends to the larger Levins equilibrium

For `c > 0` and `e ≥ 0`, as the immigration rate `m` tends to `0` from above, the
[immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md) `p̂` tends to `max(1 - e / c, 0)`. This is the larger
of the two equilibria `0` and `1 - e / c` of the [Levins balance](balance-2.md): the
[persistence equilibrium](persistence-eq.md) when `c > e`, which attracts every Levins solution
starting in `(0, 1]` ([global stability of persistence](persistence-is-globally-stable.md)), and
extinction when `c ≤ e`, which attracts every Levins solution starting in `[0, 1]`
([global stability of extinction](extinction-is-globally-stable.md)).

It is a limit only: at `m = 0` the formula gives `p̂ = 0`, even when `c > e`.
