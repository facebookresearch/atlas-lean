# For c > 0 and e < c, closed form of the gap p(t) − p*

For real numbers `c > 0` and `e < c` and real `p₀` and `t` with
`p* + p₀ (e^{(c - e) t} - 1) ≠ 0`, where `p* = 1 - e / c` is the
[persistence equilibrium](persistence-eq.md), the [explicit solution](levins.md) `p` with
`p(0) = p₀` satisfies `p(t) - p* = p* (p₀ - p*) / (p* + p₀ (e^{(c - e) t} - 1))`. The
denominator is positive when `p₀ > 0` and `t ≥ 0`.
