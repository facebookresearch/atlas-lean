# For c > 0, e < c and p₀ > 0, p(t) is never farther from p* than p₀

For real numbers `c > 0` and `e < c`, an initial value `p₀ > 0` and a time `t ≥ 0`, the
[explicit solution](levins.md) `p` with `p(0) = p₀` satisfies `|p(t) - p*| ≤ |p₀ - p*|`, where
`p* = 1 - e / c` is the [persistence equilibrium](persistence-eq.md): at no time `t ≥ 0` is the
distance to `p*` larger than at time `0`.
