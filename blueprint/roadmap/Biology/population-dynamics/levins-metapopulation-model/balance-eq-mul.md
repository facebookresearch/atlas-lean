# For m > 0, the balance factors through the immigration equilibrium

For `c ≥ 0`, `e ≥ 0`, `m > 0` and every real `p`, the [balance](balance.md) factors as
`f(p) = -(p - p̂) (c p + m / p̂)`, where `p̂ > 0` is the
[immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md). For `p ≥ 0` the second factor is positive, so there
`f(p)` has the sign of `p̂ - p`.
