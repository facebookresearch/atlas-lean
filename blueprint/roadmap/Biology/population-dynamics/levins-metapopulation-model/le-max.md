# For m > 0, a solution never exceeds the larger of its start and p̂

For `c ≥ 0`, `e ≥ 0` and `m > 0`, every
[forward solution of the equation with immigration](is-solution.md) `p`, whatever `p(0)`, satisfies
`p(t) ≤ max(p(0), p̂)` for every `t ≥ 0`, where `p̂` is the
[immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md).
