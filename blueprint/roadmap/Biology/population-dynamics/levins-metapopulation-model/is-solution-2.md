# Solution of the Levins equation

For real numbers `c` and `e`, a function `p : ℝ → ℝ` is a solution of the Levins equation
`p' = c p (1 - p) - e p` if, at every time `t ≥ 0`, it has a two-sided derivative equal to the
[balance](balance-2.md) `c p(t) (1 - p(t)) - e p(t)`. Nothing else is required: no initial value
is prescribed, `p(t)` need not lie in `[0, 1]`, and at negative times `p` is constrained only
through its two-sided derivative at `t = 0`.
