# For m > 0, the extinction rate in terms of the equilibrium p̂

For `c ≥ 0`, `e ≥ 0` and `m > 0`, the [immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md) `p̂` satisfies
`(1 - p̂) (c + m / p̂) = e`. This is the [factorization of the balance](balance-eq-mul.md) at
`p = 1`, where `f(1) = -e` ([balance at full occupancy](balance-one.md)).
