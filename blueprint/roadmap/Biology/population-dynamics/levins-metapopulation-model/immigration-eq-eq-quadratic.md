# For c > 0 and m > 0, the equilibrium is the quadratic-formula root

For `c > 0`, `e ≥ 0` and `m > 0`, the [immigration equilibrium](immigration-eq.md) is
`p̂ = (c - e - m + √D) / (2 c)`, where `D = (c - e - m)² + 4 c m` is the [discriminant](disc.md):
the larger root of `c p² - (c - e - m) p - m = 0`.
