# Mirollo–Strogatz synchronization of pulse-coupled oscillators

This node covers the following result. Let $f : [0,1] \to [0,1]$ be smooth with $f(0) = 0$, $f(1) = 1$, $f' > 0$ and $f'' < 0$. Consider $N \ge 2$ identical, all-to-all coupled oscillators with states $x = f(\phi)$, where the phase $\phi$ of every oscillator increases at the same constant rate $1/T$ for a period $T > 0$. When an oscillator reaches the threshold $x = 1$ it fires and its state jumps back to $0$; firing raises the state of every other oscillator by $\varepsilon > 0$ or to the threshold, whichever is less, and oscillators that reach the threshold together fire together and stay together. Then for almost all initial conditions the population eventually fires synchronously.
