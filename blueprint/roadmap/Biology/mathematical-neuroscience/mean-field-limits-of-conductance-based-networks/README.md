# Mean-field limits of conductance-based networks

Large networks of stochastic spiking neurons, each modeled by Hodgkin–Huxley or FitzHugh–Nagumo dynamics with noisy synaptic conductances, are described in the limit of many neurons per population by McKean–Vlasov stochastic differential equations: a typical neuron of each population evolves under the average influence of the laws of all populations. The limit equation is well posed under local Lipschitz and monotone-growth conditions satisfied by both models, and finite groups of neurons become asymptotically independent (propagation of chaos).
