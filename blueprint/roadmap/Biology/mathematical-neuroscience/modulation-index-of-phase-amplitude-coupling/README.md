# Modulation index of phase–amplitude coupling

Phase–amplitude coupling, the modulation of a fast rhythm's amplitude by a slow rhythm's phase, is commonly quantified by Tort's modulation index: the normalized Kullback–Leibler divergence of the phase-binned amplitude profile from uniform. Its null distribution for uncoupled signals is not degenerate at zero, and a moment-matched beta law gives analytic $p$-values.
