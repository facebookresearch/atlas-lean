# Stability of neural fields with propagation delays

Linear stability of stationary states of multi-population neural field equations on a bounded cortical domain with space-dependent axonal delays. The generator of the linearized delay equation has an essential spectrum fixed by the membrane time constants and countably many isolated characteristic values; stability is decided by the characteristic values alone.
