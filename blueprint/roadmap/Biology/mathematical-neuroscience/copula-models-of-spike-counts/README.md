# Copula models of spike counts

Joint spike counts of several neurons in a short time bin are discrete, over-dispersed and can have dependence concentrated in particular regions (for example strong co-activation only at high counts). Copula-based models attach arbitrary discrete marginals, such as negative binomial, to a copula that carries the dependence; the flashlight transformation moves the tail dependence of any copula into any orthant, and finite mixtures of copulas combine several dependence structures.
