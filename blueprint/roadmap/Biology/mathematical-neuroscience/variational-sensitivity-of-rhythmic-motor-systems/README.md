# Variational sensitivity of rhythmic motor systems

Rhythmic behaviors such as walking, swimming and feeding are modeled as stable limit cycles of coupled neural (central pattern generator) and mechanical dynamics, often split into a powerstroke phase that produces progress and a recovery phase that does not. Infinitesimal shape and local timing response curves give first-order formulas for how a sustained perturbation, such as a change in load, alters the cycle's shape, its phase durations and its average performance, which lets different sensory feedback architectures be compared for robustness.
