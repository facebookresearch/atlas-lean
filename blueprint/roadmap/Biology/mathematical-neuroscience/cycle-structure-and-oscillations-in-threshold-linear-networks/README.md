# Cycle structure and oscillations in threshold-linear networks

Threshold-linear networks model the firing rates of interacting excitatory and inhibitory neural populations. Their ability to oscillate is constrained by connectivity alone: without directed cycles every trajectory converges to a unique fixed point, and on a single directed cycle sustained oscillations require an odd number of inhibitory nodes together with loop gain above $1/\cos(\pi/n)$, a sharpened form of Thomas's conjecture that negative feedback loops are necessary for oscillation.
