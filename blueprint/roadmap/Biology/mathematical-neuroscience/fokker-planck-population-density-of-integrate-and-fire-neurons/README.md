# Fokker–Planck population density of integrate-and-fire neurons

The mean-field description of a large population of leaky integrate-and-fire neurons: under the diffusion approximation the density of membrane potentials obeys a Fokker–Planck equation with an absorbing threshold and reinjection at reset, and its stationary solution gives the population firing rate as a function of the mean and variance of the input. Self-consistency of these rates across interacting populations yields the stationary states of spiking networks.
