# Multivariate spike–field coupling

Measures of synchronization between the spikes of many neurons and the oscillatory phase of field potentials recorded on many channels. Collecting all pairwise couplings into one complex matrix and taking its leading singular triplet gives a single coupling strength with spatial patterns for spikes and fields, together with a random-matrix significance test.
