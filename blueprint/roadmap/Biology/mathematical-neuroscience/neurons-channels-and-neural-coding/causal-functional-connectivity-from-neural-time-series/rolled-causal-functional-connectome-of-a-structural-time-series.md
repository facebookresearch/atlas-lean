# Rolled causal functional connectome of a structural time series

**Definitions.** Let $V$ be a set of neurons with activities $X_v(t)$, $t \in \{0,1,\dots,T\}$. An *unrolled causal graph* is a DAG $G$ on the nodes $(v,t)$ such that the joint law of $\{X_v(t)\}$ satisfies the directed Markov property with respect to $G$; its edges point forward in time, $(u,t_1) \to (v,t_2)$ with $t_1 < t_2$, or are contemporaneous, $(u,t) \to (v,t)$ with $u \ne v$, which makes $G$ acyclic. For a maximum interaction delay $\tau \ge 0$, the *rolled causal functional connectome* $F_\tau$ is the directed graph on $V$ (self-loops and cycles allowed) with $u \to v$ whenever $(u,t_1) \to (v,t_2) \in G$ for some $t_1 \le t_2 \le t_1 + \tau$ (with $t_1 < t_2$ when $u = v$).

**Theorem (consistency with time-series causal relations).** Suppose each activity is generated by a structural equation

$$X_v(t) = g_{v,t}\big(X_{u_{v,1}}(t_{v,1}),\dots,X_{u_{v,p}}(t_{v,p}),\ \epsilon_v(t)\big),$$

with $p \ge 1$, inputs $u_{v,i} \in V$ at times $t_{v,i} \in \{t-\tau,\dots,t\}$ such that $u_{v,i} \ne v$ or $t_{v,i} \ne t$, measurable $g_{v,t}$, and mutually independent noises $\epsilon_v(t)$, and suppose the contemporaneous inputs form an acyclic relation: at each time $t$, the edges $(u_{v,i},t) \to (v,t)$ with $t_{v,i} = t$ contain no directed cycle. Then the inputs define an unrolled causal graph (the parents of $(v,t)$ are the $(u_{v,i}, t_{v,i})$), and the graph $F_\tau$ that rolls up this DAG, with parent sets $\mathrm{pa}_{F_\tau}(v) = \{u_{v,1},\dots,u_{v,p}\}$ is the rolled causal functional connectome of $X$. Theorem 1 assumes (5) without requiring contemporaneous acyclicity; at $T=\tau=0$, $X_1=X_2+\epsilon_1$, $X_2=X_1/2+\epsilon_2$ satisfy (5) but their parent graph is not a DAG.

**Corollary (interventions).** Under the same dynamics: ablating neurons $v_1,\dots,v_k$ removes all edges into and out of them in $F_\tau$; externally controlling $v_1,\dots,v_k$ during a set of times $I$ removes, for $t \in I$, all edges into them while all other edges remain.

## Expansion

The structural equations, with acyclic contemporaneous inputs, define a DAG on neuron–time pairs whose parents are exactly the inputs of each $g_{v,t}$; independent noises make the joint law Markov with respect to that DAG, and rolling it up over the delay window recovers the input sets. No linearity or Gaussianity is assumed, in contrast to Granger causality, which presumes a stationary Gaussian vector autoregression. Estimation (the Time-Aware PC algorithm) additionally assumes faithfulness and causal sufficiency: it runs PC-type conditional-independence tests on the unrolled nodes, orients edges forward in time, rolls up the result and keeps edges that are stable under bootstrap resampling. Interventions act as graph surgery on the unrolled DAG: an ablated neuron has a fixed value, so it neither receives nor transmits influence, and an externally driven neuron's own inputs are cut only while the drive is on.
