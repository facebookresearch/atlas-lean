# Stochastic shielding of ion-channel Markov chains

Populations of ion channels and other first-order transition networks are simulated as Markov chains with one noise source per edge. Stochastic shielding drops the noise on edges whose fluctuations the observable cannot see directly; exact edge decompositions of the stationary variance quantify when this is accurate and when hidden edges dominate.
