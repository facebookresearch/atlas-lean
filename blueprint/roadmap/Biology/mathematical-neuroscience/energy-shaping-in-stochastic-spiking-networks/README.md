# Energy shaping in stochastic spiking networks

A recurrent network of stochastically firing neurons samples network states from a stationary distribution, equivalently an energy landscape whose low-energy states it visits most often. Adding auxiliary circuits that satisfy a local membrane-potential condition adds their energy terms linearly, so higher-order constraints of combinatorial problems can be wired in motif by motif.
