# Causal emergence in neural dynamics

When does a macroscopic variable computed from many neurons (a decoded movement, a population rate) carry predictive power about the future that no individual recording channel carries? Partial information decomposition gives a formal criterion: a supervenient feature is causally emergent when it has unique predictive information beyond every small group of parts, and a system can host such features exactly when its parts are synergistic about their own future. Pairwise mutual-information bounds make the criterion testable on large recordings such as electrocorticography.
