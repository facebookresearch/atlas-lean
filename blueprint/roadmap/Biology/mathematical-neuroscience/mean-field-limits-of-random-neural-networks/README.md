# Mean-field limits of random neural networks

Large networks of rate neurons organized in a few populations, with random synaptic weights and noisy inputs, are summarized in the infinite-size limit by one stochastic equation per population. When weights fluctuate, the population-to-population interaction becomes a non-Markovian Gaussian process whose statistics depend self-consistently on the solution, and the naive "neural mass" equations are recovered only when membrane-potential variances vanish.
