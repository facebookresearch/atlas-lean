# Maximum entropy models of neural populations

The joint spiking patterns of a population of neurons in a short time bin can be described by the least structured probability distribution consistent with measured statistics. Matching firing rates and pairwise correlations gives an Ising-type model; adding the distribution of the total number of active cells gives the K-pairwise model. The statistical-mechanics form of these models also yields practical estimators of the entropy of the population's activity.
