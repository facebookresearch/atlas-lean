# Synchronization of Rayleigh–van der Pol oscillator networks

Hybrid Rayleigh–van der Pol oscillators model the rhythmic limb movements of individual people; coupling them over a graph models joint coordination in groups. With heterogeneous oscillators only bounded synchronization is possible, and contraction or QUAD-type Lyapunov arguments give explicit coupling thresholds and error bounds that decrease with coupling strength.
