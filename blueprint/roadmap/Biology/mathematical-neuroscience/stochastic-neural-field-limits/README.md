# Stochastic neural field limits

Limit theorems connecting microscopic Markov jump models of many finite neuron populations, tiling a spatial domain, to deterministic neural field equations of Wilson–Cowan type, together with the martingale central limit theorems that justify Langevin (diffusion) corrections.
