# Factorization of a quartic with Δ₃ = 0 and a₁, a₃ > 0

Let `a₁ > 0` and `a₃ > 0`, and let `a₂`, `a₄` be real numbers for which the
[third Hurwitz determinant](hurwitz3.md) `Δ₃ = a₁ a₂ a₃ - a₃² - a₁² a₄` vanishes. Put
`ω = √(a₃ / a₁)`, so that `ω² = a₃ / a₁`, and `c = a₁ a₄ / a₃`. Then for every complex `μ` the
[monic quartic](quartic.md) factors as

`μ⁴ + a₁ μ³ + a₂ μ² + a₃ μ + a₄ = (μ² + ω²) (μ² + a₁ μ + c)`.

No sign is assumed for `a₄`; `c` has the sign of `a₄`.
