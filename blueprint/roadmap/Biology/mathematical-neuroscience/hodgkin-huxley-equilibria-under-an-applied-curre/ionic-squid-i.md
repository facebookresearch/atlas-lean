# The squid ionic current does not depend on the applied current

For every real `I`, the [ionic current](../hodgkin-huxley-model/ionic.md) of the
[squid parameters with applied current I](squid-i.md) is that of the
[squid parameters](../hodgkin-huxley-model/squid.md): for all real `V, m, h, n` both are
`120 m³ h (V - 115) + 36 n⁴ (V + 12) + 0.3 (V - E_L)`, with `E_L` the
[squid leak reversal potential](../hodgkin-huxley-model/squid-el.md).
