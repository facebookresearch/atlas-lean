# An arrowhead matrix has eigenvalue 0 iff its determinant is 0

For any [arrowhead entries](arrow.md), `0` is a complex eigenvalue of the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md) `M` iff the [constant coefficient](coef-4.md)
`p₄ = -a k₁ k₂ k₃ - b₁ c₁ k₂ k₃ - b₂ c₂ k₁ k₃ - b₃ c₃ k₁ k₂` of its characteristic quartic vanishes;
`p₄ = det M`.
