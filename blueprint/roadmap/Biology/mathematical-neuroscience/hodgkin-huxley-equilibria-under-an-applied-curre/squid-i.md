# Squid giant axon parameters with applied current I

For real `I`, the [squid giant axon parameters](../hodgkin-huxley-model/squid.md) with applied
current `I`: membrane capacitance `C = 1` µF/cm², maximal conductances `g_Na = 120`, `g_K = 36` and
`g_L = 0.3` mS/cm², reversal potentials `E_Na = 115` mV and `E_K = -12` mV, the
[squid leak reversal potential](../hodgkin-huxley-model/squid-el.md) `E_L`, and applied current `I`
µA/cm², which may have either sign.
