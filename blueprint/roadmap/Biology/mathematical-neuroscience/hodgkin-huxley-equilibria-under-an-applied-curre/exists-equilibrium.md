# Existence of an equilibrium for every applied current

For every real `I` there is a real `V` with `I∞(V) = I`, where `I∞` is the
[squid steady-state current](iss.md), and the [branch point](eq-point.md)
`(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))` is then an equilibrium of the
[squid system with applied current I](squid-i.md). The statement does not assert that this
equilibrium is unique.
