# A nonzero imaginary eigenvalue of an arrowhead matrix forces Δ₃ = 0

For any [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) and real `ω ≠ 0`: if `i ω` is a complex eigenvalue of the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md), then the [third Hurwitz determinant](hdet.md)
`Δ₃ = p₁ p₂ p₃ - p₃² - p₁² p₄` of its [characteristic quartic](char-q.md)
`μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄` is `0`.
