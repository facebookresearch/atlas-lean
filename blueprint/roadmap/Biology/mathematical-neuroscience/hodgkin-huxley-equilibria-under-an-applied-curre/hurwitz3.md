# Third Hurwitz determinant of a monic quartic

For real `a₁, a₂, a₃, a₄`, `Δ₃ = a₁ a₂ a₃ - a₃² - a₁² a₄`. It is the determinant of
`[[a₁, a₃, 0], [1, a₂, a₄], [0, a₁, a₃]]`, the third leading principal minor of the Hurwitz matrix
of the [monic quartic](quartic.md) `μ⁴ + a₁ μ³ + a₂ μ² + a₃ μ + a₄`.
