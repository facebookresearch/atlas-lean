# Sufficient Routh–Hurwitz conditions for a real quartic

Let `a₁, a₂, a₃, a₄` be real with `a₁ > 0`, `a₃ > 0`, `a₄ > 0` and with positive
[third Hurwitz determinant](hurwitz3.md) `Δ₃ = a₁ a₂ a₃ - a₃² - a₁² a₄`. Then every complex root
`μ` of the [monic quartic](quartic.md) `μ⁴ + a₁ μ³ + a₂ μ² + a₃ μ + a₄` has `Re μ < 0`. No sign is
assumed for `a₂`; these conditions force `a₂ > 0`.
