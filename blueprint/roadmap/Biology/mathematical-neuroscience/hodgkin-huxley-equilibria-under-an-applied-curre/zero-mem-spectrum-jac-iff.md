# The Jacobian on the branch has eigenvalue 0 iff I∞'(V) = 0

For every real `V`, `0` is a complex eigenvalue of the [Jacobian](../hodgkin-huxley-model/jac.md) of
the squid Hodgkin–Huxley field at the [branch point](eq-point.md) `(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))` iff
`I∞'(V) = 0`, where `I∞'` is the derivative of the [squid steady-state current](iss.md): zero
eigenvalues on the branch occur exactly at the critical points of the steady-state
current–voltage curve.
