# Squid equilibria at current I are the branch points with I∞(V) = I

For every real `I` and every state `x = (V, m, h, n)` in `ℝ⁴`, `x` is an equilibrium, a zero of the
[Hodgkin–Huxley field](../hodgkin-huxley-model/field.md) of the
[squid parameters with applied current I](squid-i.md), iff `x` is the [branch point](eq-point.md)
`(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))` over its own voltage `V` and the [squid steady-state current](iss.md)
satisfies `I∞(V) = I`.
