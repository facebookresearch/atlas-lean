# Roots of μ² + p μ + q with p, q > 0 have negative real part

For real `p > 0` and `q > 0`, every complex root `μ` of `μ² + p μ + q` has `Re μ < 0`: both roots
lie in the open left half-plane.

With `τ = −p` and `Δ = q`, this is the stability test for a planar linear system: for real
`τ < 0 < Δ`, every [characteristic root](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/is-char-root.md)
`z` of `(τ, Δ)`, a root of `z² − τ z + Δ`, has `Re z < 0`.
