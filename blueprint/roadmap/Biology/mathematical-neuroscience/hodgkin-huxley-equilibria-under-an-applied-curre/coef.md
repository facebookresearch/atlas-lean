# Cubic coefficient of the arrowhead characteristic polynomial

For [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) `a, b₁, …, k₃`, `p₁ = -a + k₁ + k₂ + k₃`, minus the trace of the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md) `M`. It is the coefficient of `μ³` in the
[characteristic quartic](char-q.md), which [equals](det-eq-char-q.md) `det(μ I - M)`.
