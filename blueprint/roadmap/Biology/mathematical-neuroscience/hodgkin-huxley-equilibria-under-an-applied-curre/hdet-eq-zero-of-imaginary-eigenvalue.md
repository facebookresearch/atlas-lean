# A nonzero imaginary eigenvalue on the branch forces Δ₃ = 0

For real `V` and real `ω ≠ 0`: if `i ω` is a complex eigenvalue of the
[Jacobian](../hodgkin-huxley-model/jac.md) of the squid Hodgkin–Huxley field at the
[branch point](eq-point.md) `(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))`, then the
[third Hurwitz determinant](hdet.md) `Δ₃(V)` of the characteristic quartic of the
[arrowhead entries at V](arrow-at.md) is `0`.
