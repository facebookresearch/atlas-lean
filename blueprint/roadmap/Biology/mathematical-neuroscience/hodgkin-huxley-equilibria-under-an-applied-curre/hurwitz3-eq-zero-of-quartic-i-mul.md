# A nonzero purely imaginary root of a real quartic forces Δ₃ = 0

For any real `a₁, a₂, a₃, a₄` and real `ω ≠ 0`: if `i ω` is a root of the
[monic quartic](quartic.md) `μ⁴ + a₁ μ³ + a₂ μ² + a₃ μ + a₄`, then `a₃ = a₁ ω²` and the
[third Hurwitz determinant](hurwitz3.md) `Δ₃ = a₁ a₂ a₃ - a₃² - a₁² a₄` is `0`.
