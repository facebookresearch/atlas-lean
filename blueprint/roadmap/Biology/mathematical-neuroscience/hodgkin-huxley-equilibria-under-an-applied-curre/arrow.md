# Entries of a 4 × 4 arrowhead matrix

Ten real numbers `a`, `b₁, b₂, b₃`, `c₁, c₂, c₃` and `k₁, k₂, k₃`, read as the entries of the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md)
`[[a, b₁, b₂, b₃], [c₁, -k₁, 0, 0], [c₂, 0, -k₂, 0], [c₃, 0, 0, -k₃]]`. No sign condition is
imposed on them. This is the shape of the Hodgkin–Huxley [Jacobian](../hodgkin-huxley-model/jac.md);
along the equilibrium branch its entries are the [arrowhead entries at V](arrow-at.md), which have
`k₁, k₂, k₃ > 0`.
