# The third Hurwitz determinant is negative at V = 15 mV

At `V = 15` mV above rest, the [third Hurwitz determinant](hdet.md) `Δ₃ = p₁ p₂ p₃ - p₃² - p₁² p₄`
of the characteristic quartic `μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄` of the
[arrowhead entries at V](arrow-at.md), the entries of the squid Jacobian at the branch point over
`V`, is negative, so the Routh–Hurwitz condition `Δ₃ > 0` fails there.
