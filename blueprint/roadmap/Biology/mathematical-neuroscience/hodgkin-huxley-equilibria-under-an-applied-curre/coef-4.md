# Constant coefficient of the arrowhead characteristic polynomial

For [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) `a, b₁, …, k₃`,

`p₄ = -a k₁ k₂ k₃ - b₁ c₁ k₂ k₃ - b₂ c₂ k₁ k₃ - b₃ c₃ k₁ k₂`,

the constant term of the [characteristic quartic](char-q.md) of the [arrowhead matrix](mat.md) `M`;
it equals `det M`.
