# Routh–Hurwitz conditions make an arrowhead matrix Hurwitz

Let the [characteristic quartic](char-q.md) `μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄` of
[arrowhead entries](arrow.md) have `p₁ > 0`, `p₃ > 0`, `p₄ > 0` and positive
[third Hurwitz determinant](hdet.md) `Δ₃ = p₁ p₂ p₃ - p₃² - p₁² p₄`. Then the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md) is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md):
every complex eigenvalue has negative real part.
