# The arrowhead characteristic quartic equals det(μI - M)

For [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) and every complex `μ`,
`det(μ I - M) = μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄`, where `M` is the [arrowhead matrix](mat.md), viewed
as a complex matrix, and `p₁, p₂, p₃, p₄` are the [cubic](coef.md), [quadratic](coef-2.md),
[linear](coef-3.md) and [constant](coef-4.md) coefficients of the entries: the
[characteristic quartic](char-q.md) is the characteristic polynomial of `M`.
