# Characteristic quartic of an arrowhead matrix

For [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) and complex `μ`, `χ(μ) = μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄`: the
[monic quartic](quartic.md) whose coefficients are the [cubic](coef.md), [quadratic](coef-2.md),
[linear](coef-3.md) and [constant](coef-4.md) coefficients `p₁, p₂, p₃, p₄` of the entries. It is
the characteristic polynomial `det(μ I - M)` of the [arrowhead matrix](mat.md) `M`
([determinant formula](det-eq-char-q.md)).
