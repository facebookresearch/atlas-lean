# Signs of the quadratic, linear and constant coefficients at V = 15 mV

At `V = 15` mV above rest, the characteristic quartic `μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄` of the
[arrowhead entries at V](arrow-at.md), the entries of the squid Jacobian at the branch point over
`V`, has [quadratic coefficient](coef-2.md) `p₂ < 0`, [linear coefficient](coef-3.md) `p₃ > 0` and
[constant coefficient](coef-4.md) `p₄ > 0`.
