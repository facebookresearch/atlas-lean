# Point of the equilibrium branch at voltage V

For real `V`, the state `(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))` in `ℝ⁴`, with the steady-state gating values
[`m∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/m-inf.md), [`h∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/h-inf.md) and
[`n∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/n-inf.md) at `V`. It is an equilibrium of the
[squid system with applied current](squid-i.md) `I∞(V)`, the [squid steady-state current](iss.md)
at `V`, and [every equilibrium](field-squid-i-eq-zero-iff.md) of the squid system with a constant
applied current has this form. At `V = 0` it is the
[resting state](../hodgkin-huxley-model/rest.md).
