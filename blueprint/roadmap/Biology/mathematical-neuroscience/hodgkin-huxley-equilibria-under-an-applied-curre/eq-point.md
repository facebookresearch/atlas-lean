# Point of the equilibrium branch at voltage V

For real `V`, the state `(V, m∞(V), h∞(V), n∞(V))` in `ℝ⁴`, with the steady-state gating values
[`m∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/m-inf.md), [`h∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/h-inf.md) and
[`n∞`](../hodgkin-huxley-model/n-inf.md) at `V`. By the
[characterisation of the equilibria](field-squid-i-eq-zero-iff.md), it is an equilibrium of the
[squid system with applied current](squid-i.md) `I∞(V)`, the [squid steady-state current](iss.md) at
`V`, and every equilibrium of the squid system with a constant applied current has this form.
