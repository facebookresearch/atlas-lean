# The constant coefficient on the branch is k₁ k₂ k₃ I∞'(V)

For every real `V`, the [constant coefficient](coef-4.md) `p₄(V)` of the characteristic quartic of
the [arrowhead entries at V](arrow-at.md), which is the determinant of the squid Jacobian at the
branch point over `V`, is

`p₄(V) = k₁ k₂ k₃ I∞'(V)`,

with `k₁ = α_m(V) + β_m(V)`, `k₂ = α_h(V) + β_h(V)`, `k₃ = α_n(V) + β_n(V)` and `I∞'` the
derivative of the [squid steady-state current](iss.md), which is differentiable on `ℝ`. Since
`k₁, k₂, k₃ > 0`, `0` is an eigenvalue of the Jacobian at the branch point over `V` iff
`I∞'(V) = 0` ([zero eigenvalues on the branch](zero-mem-spectrum-jac-iff.md)).
