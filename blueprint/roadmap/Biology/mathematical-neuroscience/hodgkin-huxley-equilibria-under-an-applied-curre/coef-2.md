# Quadratic coefficient of the arrowhead characteristic polynomial

For [arrowhead entries](arrow.md) `a, b₁, …, k₃`,

`p₂ = -a (k₁ + k₂ + k₃) + (k₁ k₂ + k₁ k₃ + k₂ k₃) - (b₁ c₁ + b₂ c₂ + b₃ c₃)`,

the coefficient of `μ²` in the [characteristic quartic](char-q.md) of the
[arrowhead matrix](mat.md) `M`, which [equals](det-eq-char-q.md) `det(μ I - M)`.
