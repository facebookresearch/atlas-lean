# Spectral Hopf conditions from Δ₃ = 0 with positive coefficients

Let the [characteristic quartic](char-q.md) `μ⁴ + p₁ μ³ + p₂ μ² + p₃ μ + p₄` of
[arrowhead entries](arrow.md) have `p₁ > 0`, `p₃ > 0`, `p₄ > 0` and vanishing
[third Hurwitz determinant](hdet.md) `Δ₃ = p₁ p₂ p₃ - p₃² - p₁² p₄` (then `p₂ > 0` as well). Then
the [arrowhead matrix](mat.md) satisfies the
[spectral Hopf conditions](is-spectral-hopf-candidate.md) at `ω = √(p₃ / p₁)`: `i ω` and `-i ω`
are eigenvalues, `i ω` is a simple root of the characteristic quartic, and every other complex
eigenvalue has negative real part.
