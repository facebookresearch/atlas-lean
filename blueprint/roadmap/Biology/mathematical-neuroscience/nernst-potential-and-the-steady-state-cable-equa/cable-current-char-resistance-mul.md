# With V₀ = r∞ I₀, the transfer-form current is I₀ e^(−x/λ)

For real numbers `r_m > 0` and `r_i > 0` and all real `I₀` and `x`, the
[transfer-form current](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/cable-current.md) with inlet voltage `V₀ = r∞ I₀` and inlet current
`I₀`, `I₀ cosh (x / λ) - (V₀ / r∞) sinh (x / λ)`, is the pure decaying exponential

`I₀ e^(-x / λ)`,

where `λ = √(r_m / r_i)` is the [length constant](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/length-constant.md) and `r∞ = √(r_m r_i) > 0` the [characteristic resistance](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/char-resistance.md).
