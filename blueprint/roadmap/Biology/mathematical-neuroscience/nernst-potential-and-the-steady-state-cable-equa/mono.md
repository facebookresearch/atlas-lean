# A steady state restricts to a steady state on every subset

For all real `r_m` and `r_i` and sets `t ⊆ s` of real positions, if `V` and `I` form a [steady state](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/is-steady-state.md) of the
passive cable on `s` (at every `x ∈ s`, `V' = -r_i I(x)` and `I' = -V(x) / r_m`, derivatives
within `s`), then they form a steady state on `t`, with derivatives within `t`.
