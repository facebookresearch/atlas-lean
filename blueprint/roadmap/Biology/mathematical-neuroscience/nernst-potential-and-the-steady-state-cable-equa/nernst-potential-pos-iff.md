# For RT, zF > 0, the Nernst potential is positive iff c_in < c_out

Let `R`, `T`, `z` and `F` be real numbers with `R T > 0` and `z F > 0` (for example a cation, with
positive gas constant, temperature and Faraday constant), and let the inside and outside
concentrations `c_in` and `c_out` be positive. Then the [Nernst potential](nernst-potential.md)
`(R T / (z F)) log (c_out / c_in)` is positive if and only if `c_in < c_out`.
