# For zF ≠ 0, equal electrochemical potentials iff the Nernst voltage

Let `R`, `T`, `z`, `F` and `μ₀` be real numbers with `z F ≠ 0`, let the inside and outside
concentrations `c_in` and `c_out` be positive, and let `φ_in` and `φ_out` be real electric
potentials. Then the [molar electrochemical potentials](electrochemical-potential.md) inside and
outside, with the same standard potential `μ₀`,

`μ₀ + R T log c_in + z F φ_in = μ₀ + R T log c_out + z F φ_out`,

are equal if and only if the membrane voltage is the [Nernst potential](nernst-potential.md):

`φ_in - φ_out = (R T / (z F)) log (c_out / c_in)`.
