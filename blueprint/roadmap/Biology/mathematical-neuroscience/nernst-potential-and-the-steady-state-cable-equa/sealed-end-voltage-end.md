# Voltage at the sealed end of a finite cable

Let `r_m > 0`, `r_i > 0` and `L > 0`, and let `V` and `I` be a [steady state](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/is-steady-state.md) of the passive cable on
`[0, L]` (derivatives within `[0, L]`) with a sealed far end, `I(L) = 0`. Then the voltage at the
sealed end is

`V(L) = V(0) / cosh (L / λ)`,

where `λ = √(r_m / r_i)` is the [length constant](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/length-constant.md). This is the [sealed-end voltage profile](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/sealed-end.md)
at `x = L`.
