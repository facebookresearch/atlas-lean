# Boltzmann form of the Nernst equilibrium

Let `R`, `T`, `z` and `F` be real numbers with `R T ≠ 0` and `z F ≠ 0`, and let the inside and
outside concentrations `c_in` and `c_out` be positive. Then, with `E` the
[Nernst potential](nernst-potential.md) `(R T / (z F)) log (c_out / c_in)`, the concentration ratio
is

`c_out / c_in = exp (z F E / (R T))`.
