# Reversing the valence reverses the Nernst potential

For all real `R`, `T`, `z`, `F`, `c_in` and `c_out`, replacing the valence `z` by `-z` reverses the
sign of the [Nernst potential](nernst-potential.md):

`(R T / (-z F)) log (c_out / c_in) = -(R T / (z F)) log (c_out / c_in)`.

This holds for all real inputs, including `z F = 0`, where both sides are `0` by the convention
`u / 0 = 0`.
