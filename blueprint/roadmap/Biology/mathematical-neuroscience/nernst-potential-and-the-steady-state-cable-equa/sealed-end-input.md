# Input relation V(0) = r∞ coth(L/λ) I(0) of a sealed finite cable

Let `r_m > 0`, `r_i > 0` and `L > 0`, and let `V` and `I` be a [steady state](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/is-steady-state.md) of the passive cable on
`[0, L]` (derivatives within `[0, L]`) with a sealed far end, `I(L) = 0`. Then

`V(0) = r∞ I(0) cosh (L / λ) / sinh (L / λ)`,

that is, `V(0) = r∞ coth (L / λ) I(0)`, where `λ = √(r_m / r_i)` is the [length constant](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/length-constant.md) and `r∞ = √(r_m r_i)`
the [characteristic resistance](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/char-resistance.md); `sinh (L / λ) > 0`. This is the inlet relation `V(0) sinh (L / λ) = r∞ I(0) cosh (L / λ)`
of the [sealed-end cable](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/sealed-end.md), divided by `sinh (L / λ)`.
