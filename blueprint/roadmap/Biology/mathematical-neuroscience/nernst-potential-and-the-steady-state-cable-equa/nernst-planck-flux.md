# One-dimensional Nernst–Planck flux

For real numbers `D`, `R`, `T`, `z`, `F`, `c`, `c'` and `φ'`, the one-dimensional Nernst–Planck
flux is

`J = -D (c' + (z F / (R T)) c φ')`:

Fickian diffusion plus electrical drift of an ion species of valence `z` with diffusion
coefficient `D`, at a point where the concentration is `c`, its gradient is `c'` and the gradient of
the electric potential is `φ'`; `R` is the gas constant, `T` the absolute temperature and `F` the
Faraday constant. The three local quantities `c`, `c'` and `φ'` enter as numbers.

The formula is defined for all real inputs; with the convention `u / 0 = 0` the drift term
vanishes when `R T = 0`. In the model `D`, `R`, `T`, `F` and `c` are positive.
