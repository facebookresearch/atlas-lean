# A bounded semi-infinite steady state falls by e per length constant

Let `r_m > 0` and `r_i > 0`, and let `V` and `I` be a [steady state](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/is-steady-state.md) of the passive cable on `[0, ∞)`
(derivatives within `[0, ∞)`) whose voltage is bounded on `[0, ∞)`. Then for every `x ≥ 0`

`V(x + λ) = e^(-1) V(x)`,

where `λ = √(r_m / r_i)` is the [length constant](../../../Analysis/ordinary-differential-equations/linear-systems-and-fundamental-matrices/length-constant.md): the voltage falls by the factor `e` over each length
constant.
