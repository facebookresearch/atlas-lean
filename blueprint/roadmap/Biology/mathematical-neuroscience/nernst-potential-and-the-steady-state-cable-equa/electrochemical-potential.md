# Molar electrochemical potential of an ion species

For real numbers `R`, `T`, `z`, `F`, `μ₀`, `c` and `φ`, the molar electrochemical potential is

`μ̃ = μ₀ + R T log c + z F φ`,

for an ion species of valence `z` at concentration `c` and electric potential `φ`, with standard
chemical potential `μ₀`, gas constant `R`, absolute temperature `T` and Faraday constant `F`.

The formula is defined for all real inputs, with the conventions `log 0 = 0` and `log c = log |c|`
for `c < 0`: at `c = 0` it gives `μ₀ + z F φ`. In the model the concentration `c` is positive.
