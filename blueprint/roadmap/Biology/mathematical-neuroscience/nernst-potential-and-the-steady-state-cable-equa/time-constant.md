# Membrane time constant of a passive cable

For real numbers `r_m` and `c_m`, the membrane time constant of a passive cable is `τ = r_m c_m`,
where `r_m` is the membrane resistance (resistance times unit length) and `c_m` the membrane
capacitance per unit length. In the model both are positive and the product has units of time; the
formula is defined for all real inputs.
