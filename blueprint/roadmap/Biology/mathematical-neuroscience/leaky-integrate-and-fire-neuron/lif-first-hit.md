# First hit of the threshold under superthreshold drive

Let `u_r < θ < u_∞` and `τ > 0`, and let `u(t) = u_∞ - (u_∞ - u_r) e^(-t/τ)` be the voltage relaxing
from the reset value `u_r` toward the steady value `u_∞` under constant drive. Then
`T = τ log((u_∞ - u_r)/(u_∞ - θ))` is positive, `u(T) = θ`, and `u(t) < θ` for `0 ≤ t < T`: `T` is
the first time at which the voltage reaches the threshold `θ`.
