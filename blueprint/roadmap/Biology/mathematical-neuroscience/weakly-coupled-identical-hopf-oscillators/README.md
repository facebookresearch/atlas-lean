# Weakly coupled identical Hopf oscillators

Two identical systems undergoing a supercritical Hopf bifurcation, with symmetric coupling of strength $\epsilon$. The uncoupled limit $\epsilon\to 0$ is degenerate (a torus filled with periodic orbits), and the coupled normal form organizes in-phase, anti-phase and winner-take-all oscillations used in models of perceptual bistability.
