# Singular bifurcations of slow–fast excitable neurons

Planar slow–fast neuron models $\dot w = \varepsilon g(w,v)$, $\dot v = f(w,v)$ with a cubic critical manifold, analysed with geometric singular perturbation theory. The transition from rest to repetitive firing happens near the lower fold of the critical manifold, where a two-parameter normal form organizes singular Andronov–Hopf bifurcations with canard explosions (type II excitability), a singular Bogdanov–Takens/SNIC point (type I), and an incomplete canard explosion in between.
