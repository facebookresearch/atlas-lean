# Homogenized two-population neural fields

Excitatory-inhibitory neural field models in which the connectivity has a periodic microstructure, obtained as two-scale limits. Solutions stay close to those of the averaged translation-invariant field for weak microstructure, and the linear stability of constant states splits into a family of Fourier modes in the microscopic variable.
