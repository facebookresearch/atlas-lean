# Neural fields with delays and diffusion

Neural field equations with distance-dependent transmission delays, extended by a diffusion term that models gap-junction (electrical) coupling between neighbouring neurons. Treating the model as an abstract delay differential equation with an unbounded operator, sun-star calculus gives well-posedness, a reduction of the spectrum of the linearization to a characteristic equation on the spatial state space, and the tools for Hopf normal-form computations.
