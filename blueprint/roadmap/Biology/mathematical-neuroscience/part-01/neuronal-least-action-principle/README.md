# Neuronal least-action principle

A variational account of cortical dynamics and learning. A Lagrangian built from somato-dendritic mismatch energies plus a nudged output cost is made stationary along trajectories of discounted future voltages; the Euler–Lagrange equations give leaky-integrator dynamics with prospective rates and backpropagated prospective errors, and a local dendritic plasticity rule then descends the global cost at every instant.
