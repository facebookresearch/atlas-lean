# Z2-symmetric two-delay neocortex model

A lumped model of two identical cortical populations with delayed self-inhibition and delayed mutual excitation. Its swap symmetry forces all equilibria to be symmetric and splits the characteristic equation into in-phase and anti-phase factors, which gives explicit Hopf curves and a delay-independent stability region.
