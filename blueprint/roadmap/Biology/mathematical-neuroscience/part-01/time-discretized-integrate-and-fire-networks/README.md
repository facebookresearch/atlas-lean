# Time-discretized integrate-and-fire networks

Discretizing an integrate-and-fire network with conductance-based synapses at the time scale of a spike turns its dynamics into a piecewise affine map indexed by the history of firing patterns (the raster plot). Each branch contracts phase space, so the long-run behaviour is decided by how close orbits come to the firing threshold: away from it the dynamics is periodic and coded one-to-one by raster plots, near it sensitivity to initial conditions appears.
