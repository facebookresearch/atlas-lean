# Bayesian mechanics of Markov blankets

The density-dynamics formulation of the free energy principle. A random dynamical system with a non-equilibrium steady-state density whose internal and external states are conditionally independent given blanket states has a flow that splits into a dissipative gradient part and a solenoidal part on the surprisal; matching of conditional modes across the blanket then reads as approximate Bayesian inference.
