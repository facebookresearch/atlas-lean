# Mean-field limits of conductance-based networks

Large networks of stochastic spiking neurons, each modeled by Hodgkin–Huxley or FitzHugh–Nagumo dynamics with noisy synaptic conductances, are described in the limit of many neurons per population by McKean–Vlasov stochastic differential equations: a typical neuron of each population evolves under the average influence of the laws of all populations. Local Lipschitz conditions alone do not make the limit equation well posed; for these models it is, because the law enters only through the bounded mean synaptic variables and the membrane drift is one-sided Lipschitz, and finite groups of neurons become asymptotically independent (propagation of chaos).
