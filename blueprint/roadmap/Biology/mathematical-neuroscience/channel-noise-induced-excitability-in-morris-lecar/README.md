# Channel-noise-induced excitability in Morris–Lecar

Spontaneous action potentials of a Morris–Lecar neuron under subthreshold input, driven by random opening and closing of finitely many sodium and potassium channels. The voltage–channel process is a stochastic hybrid system whose rare excursions are described by a WKB quasipotential and most probable paths obtained from a Perron-eigenvalue Hamiltonian.
