# Flux of the field through the centred energy levels

For real parameters `a`, `b`, `ε`, `I`, a reference state `(vr, wr)` and a state `(v, w)`, the flux
is

`2 (v - vr)(v - v³/3 - w + I) + (2 (w - wr)/ε) · ε (v + a - b w)`,

the inner product of the gradient of the [centred energy](rest-energy.md) with the
[field](field.md); it is the derivative of the centred energy along trajectories
([derivative along a trajectory](has-deriv-at-rest-energy.md)). With the convention `x / 0 = 0` the
second term is `0` at `ε = 0`.
