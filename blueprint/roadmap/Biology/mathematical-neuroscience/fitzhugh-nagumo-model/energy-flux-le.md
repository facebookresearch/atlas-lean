# For b, ε > 0, the energy flux obeys a dissipation inequality

Let `b > 0`, `ε > 0` and `a`, `I` real. At every state `(v, w)` the [energy flux](energy-flux.md)
`F(v, w)` satisfies

`F(v, w) ≤ 2 (C - κ (v² + w²/ε))`,

where `C = 5 + I²/2 + a²/(2 b)` is the [constant](absorb-const.md), `κ = min(1, b ε / 2)` the
[decay rate](decay-rate.md) and `v² + w²/ε` the [energy](energy.md).
