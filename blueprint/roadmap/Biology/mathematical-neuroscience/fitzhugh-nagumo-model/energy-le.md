# For b, ε > 0, large energy ellipses are forward invariant

Let `b > 0`, `ε > 0`, `a` and `I` real, let `(v, w)` be a [solution](is-solution.md) of the
FitzHugh–Nagumo system, and let `R` exceed the [trapping radius](trap-radius.md). If
`v(0)² + w(0)²/ε ≤ R`, then `v(t)² + w(t)²/ε ≤ R` for every `t ≥ 0`. The energy can reach the level
`R` only with negative derivative ([trapping](energy-flux-neg-of-energy-eq.md)), so it stays below
that level.
