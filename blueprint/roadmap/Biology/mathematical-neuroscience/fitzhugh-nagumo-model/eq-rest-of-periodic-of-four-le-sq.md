# For b, ε > 0 and vr² ≥ 4, periodic solutions are at rest

Let `a`, `I` be real, `b > 0`, `ε > 0`, `(vr, wr)` an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md) with
`vr² ≥ 4`, and `T > 0`. Every [solution](is-solution.md) with `v(t + T) = v(t)` and
`w(t + T) = w(t)` for every `t ≥ 0` satisfies `v(t) = vr` and `w(t) = wr` for every `t ≥ 0`: there
is no nonconstant periodic orbit.
