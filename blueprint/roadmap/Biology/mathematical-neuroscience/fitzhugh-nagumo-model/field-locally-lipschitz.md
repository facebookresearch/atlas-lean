# The FitzHugh–Nagumo field is locally Lipschitz

For all real parameters `a`, `b`, `ε`, `I` and every point `p` of `ℝ × (ℝ × ℝ)`, there are `C ≥ 0`
and a neighbourhood `U` of `p` such that `dist(F(x), F(y)) ≤ C dist(x, y)` whenever `(t, x)` and
`(t, y)` lie in `U`, where `F` is the [field](field.md), which does not depend on the time `t`.
Distances on `ℝ × ℝ` are the maximum of the coordinate distances. This is the local Lipschitz
condition under which maximal solutions exist and are unique.
