# The FitzHugh–Nagumo vector field

For real parameters `a`, `b`, `ε` and `I`, the FitzHugh–Nagumo field on the state space `ℝ × ℝ` of
pairs `(v, w)` is `(v, w) ↦ (v - v³/3 - w + I, ε (v + a - b w))`: its components are the
[voltage component](v-field.md) and the [recovery component](w-field.md). In the model `b > 0` and
`ε > 0` (classically `0 < b < 1` and `ε` small); the definition takes any real values.
