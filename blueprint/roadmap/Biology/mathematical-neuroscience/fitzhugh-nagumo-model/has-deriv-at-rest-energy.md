# Derivative of the centred energy along a trajectory

Let `a`, `b`, `ε`, `I`, `vr`, `wr` be real, `v, w : ℝ → ℝ` and `t` a time at which `v` has
derivative `v(t) - v(t)³/3 - w(t) + I` and `w` has derivative `ε (v(t) + a - b w(t))`. Then
`s ↦ (v(s) - vr)² + (w(s) - wr)²/ε` has at `t` the derivative given by the [flux](rest-flux.md) at
`(v(t), w(t))`. This holds for all real parameters, `ε = 0` included, where both sides use
`x / 0 = 0`; `(vr, wr)` need not be an equilibrium.
