# Linearly stable rest states with −2 ≤ v < −1 and post-pulse drift

Let `0 < b < 1`, `ε > 0`, `a` and `I` real, and let `(v, w)` be an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md)
with `-2 ≤ v < -1`. Then every root of `z² - τ z + Δ` has negative real part, where
`τ = 1 - v² - ε b` and `Δ = ε (1 - b + b v²)` are the [trace](jac-trace.md) and
[determinant](jac-det.md) of the Jacobian at `v`; the [lower root](pulse-root-low.md)
`r₋ = (-3v - √(12 - 3v²))/2` is positive; the voltage falls after every pulse `0 < δ < r₋`; and it
rises after every pulse `r₋ < δ < r₊`, where `r₊ = (-3v + √(12 - 3v²))/2` is the
[upper root](pulse-root-high.md). At `v = -2` the roots coincide (`r₋ = r₊ = 3`) and the last
statement is vacuous.
