# Trace of the FitzHugh–Nagumo Jacobian

For real `b`, `ε` and `v`, `τ = 1 - v² - ε b`. With the [determinant expression](jac-det.md) `Δ` it
gives the [characteristic polynomial](det-jacobian-sub.md) `det(J - z 1) = z² - τ z + Δ` of the
[Jacobian](jacobian.md) `J` at voltage `v`, so `τ` is the trace of `J`.
