# Linearly stable rest states with −2 < v < −1 have a rising window

Let `0 < b < 1`, `ε > 0`, `a` and `I` real, and let `(v, w)` be an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md)
with `-2 < v < -1`. Then every root of `z² - τ z + Δ` has negative real part, where
`τ = 1 - v² - ε b` and `Δ = ε (1 - b + b v²)` are the [trace](jac-trace.md) and
[determinant](jac-det.md) of the Jacobian at `v`, and `0 < r₋ < r₊`, where
`r₋ = (-3v - √(12 - 3v²))/2` and `r₊ = (-3v + √(12 - 3v²))/2` are the [lower](pulse-root-low.md) and
[upper](pulse-root-high.md) roots of the post-pulse factor: the voltage falls after every pulse
`0 < δ < r₋` and rises after every pulse in the nonempty window `r₋ < δ < r₊`.
