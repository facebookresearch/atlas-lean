# For b, ε > 0, the field points strictly inward on large ellipses

Let `b > 0`, `ε > 0`, `a` and `I` real, and let `R` exceed the [trapping radius](trap-radius.md)
`C / κ`. At every state `(v, w)` with `v² + w²/ε = R`, the [energy flux](energy-flux.md) is
negative: the field points strictly into the ellipse `v² + w²/ε ≤ R` at every point of its boundary.
