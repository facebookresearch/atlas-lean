# Forward uniqueness of FitzHugh–Nagumo solutions

For any real parameters `a`, `b`, `ε` and `I`, two [solutions](is-solution.md) `(v, w)` and
`(v', w')` with `v(0) = v'(0)` and `w(0) = w'(0)` agree at every `t ≥ 0`. On `[0, t]` both curves
stay in a common closed ball, on which the polynomial field is Lipschitz.
