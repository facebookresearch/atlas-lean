# Post-pulse factor at a rest voltage

For real `vr` and `x`, the post-pulse factor is `1 - vr² - vr x - x²/3`. At an
[equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md) `(vr, wr)`, the [voltage component](v-field.md) of the field at a
state `(v, w)` is `x (1 - vr² - vr x - x²/3) - (w - wr)` with `x = v - vr`
([centred form](v-field-eq-rest-factor.md)); at `x = δ` and `w = wr` this is the post-pulse field
`δ (1 - vr² - vr δ - δ²/3)`.
