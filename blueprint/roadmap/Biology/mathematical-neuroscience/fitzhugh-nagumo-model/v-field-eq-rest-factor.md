# Voltage field in coordinates centred at an equilibrium

Let `a`, `b`, `ε` and `I` be real and `(vr, wr)` an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md). At every state
`(v, w)`,

`v - v³/3 - w + I = (v - vr)(1 - vr² - vr (v - vr) - (v - vr)²/3) - (w - wr)`,

that is, `x` times the [post-pulse factor](rest-factor.md) at `(vr, x)`, minus `w - wr`, with
`x = v - vr`.
