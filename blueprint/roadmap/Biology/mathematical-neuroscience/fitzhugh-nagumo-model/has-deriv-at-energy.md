# Derivative of the energy along a trajectory

Let `a`, `b`, `ε`, `I` be real, `v, w : ℝ → ℝ` and `t` a time at which `v` has derivative
`v(t) - v(t)³/3 - w(t) + I` and `w` has derivative `ε (v(t) + a - b w(t))` (the FitzHugh–Nagumo
equations at that time). Then `s ↦ v(s)² + w(s)²/ε` has at `t` the derivative given by the
[energy flux](energy-flux.md) at `(v(t), w(t))`. This holds for all real parameters, `ε = 0`
included, where both sides use `x / 0 = 0`.
