# Voltage field after a pulse from the voltage nullcline

Let `I` be real and `(v, w)` a state on the voltage nullcline, `v - v³/3 - w + I = 0` (for instance
a rest state). For every real `δ`, the [voltage component](v-field.md) at the post-pulse state is

`(v + δ) - (v + δ)³/3 - w + I = δ (1 - v² - v δ - δ²/3)`.
