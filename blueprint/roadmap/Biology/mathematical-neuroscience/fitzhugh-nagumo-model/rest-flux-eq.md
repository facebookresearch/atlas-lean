# For ε ≠ 0, closed form of the centred flux at an equilibrium

Let `a`, `b`, `I` be real, `ε ≠ 0`, and `(vr, wr)` an [equilibrium](is-equilibrium.md). At every
state `(v, w)` the [flux](rest-flux.md) through the centred energy levels is

`2 ((v - vr)² (1 - vr² - vr (v - vr) - (v - vr)²/3) - b (w - wr)²)`:

the coupling terms `∓ 2 (v - vr)(w - wr)` cancel.
