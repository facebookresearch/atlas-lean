# For v² < 4, the two pulse roots are distinct

For real `v` with `v² < 4`, the [lower root](pulse-root-low.md) `(-3v - √(12 - 3v²))/2` is smaller
than the [upper root](pulse-root-high.md) `(-3v + √(12 - 3v²))/2`. At `v² = 4`, and for `v² > 4` by
the convention `√x = 0` for `x < 0`, the two values coincide.
