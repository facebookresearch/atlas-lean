# Voltage rising after a pulse

For real `I`, a state `(v, w)` and an amplitude `δ`, the voltage is rising after the pulse if the
[voltage component](v-field.md) of the field is positive at the post-pulse state `(v + δ, w)`:
`(v + δ) - (v + δ)³/3 - w + I > 0`. The pulse is an instantaneous jump of the voltage by `δ`. This
is the instantaneous drift at the post-pulse state only; it says nothing about the later trajectory.
