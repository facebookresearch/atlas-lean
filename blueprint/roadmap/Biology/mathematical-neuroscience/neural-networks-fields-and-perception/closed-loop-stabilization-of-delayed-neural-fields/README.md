# Closed-loop stabilization of delayed neural fields

Delayed neural field models of interconnected populations, here the subthalamic nucleus (STN) and globus pallidus externus (GPe) whose oscillations are linked to parkinsonian symptoms. A Lyapunov–Krasovskii functional shows that proportional feedback stimulation of one population, with high enough gain, removes the oscillations whenever the other population is not intrinsically unstable, regardless of the axonal delays.
