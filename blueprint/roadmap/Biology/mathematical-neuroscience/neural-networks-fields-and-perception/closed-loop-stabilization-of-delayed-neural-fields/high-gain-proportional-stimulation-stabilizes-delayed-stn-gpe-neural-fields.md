# High-gain proportional stimulation stabilizes delayed STN–GPe neural fields

**Model.** On a compact spatial domain $\Omega$, let $z_1(r,t)$ and $z_2(r,t)$ be the normalized firing rates of the STN and the GPe. With time constants $\tau_i > 0$, synaptic kernels $w_{ij}(r,r')$, axonal delays $0 \le d_j(r,r') \le d_{\max}$, external inputs $I_i(r)$ and a target pattern $z_{\rm ref}(r)$, the STN receives proportional closed-loop (for example optogenetic) stimulation with gain $k_c \ge 0$ and spatial photosensitization profile $\alpha(r)$:

$$\tau_1\partial_t z_1 = -z_1 + S_1\Big(\sum_{j=1}^2\int_\Omega w_{1j}(r,r')\,z_j\big(r', t - d_j(r,r')\big)\,dr' + I_1(r) - k_c\,\alpha(r)\big(z_1(r,t) - z_{\rm ref}(r)\big)\Big),$$

$$\tau_2\partial_t z_2 = -z_2 + S_2\Big(\sum_{j=1}^2\int_\Omega w_{2j}(r,r')\,z_j\big(r', t - d_j(r,r')\big)\,dr' + I_2(r)\Big).$$

**Theorem.** Let $S_1, S_2$ be bounded and increasing with maximal slopes $\ell_1, \ell_2$, let the inputs $I_1, I_2$ be constant in time, let $\inf_{r\in\Omega}\alpha(r) > 0$, and assume the GPe lateral kernel satisfies

$$\Big(\int_\Omega\int_\Omega w_{22}(r,r')^2\,dr'\,dr\Big)^{1/2} < \frac{1}{\ell_2}.$$

Then for every sufficiently large gain $k_c$ the closed-loop neural field is stable, and from almost all admissible initial conditions the STN and GPe activities converge exponentially to constant patterns. The condition does not involve the delays or the STN kernels $w_{11}$, $w_{12}$, $w_{21}$: feedback on the STN can suppress oscillations generated within the STN or by STN–GPe interactions, but not oscillations generated by the GPe's own lateral connectivity.

## Expansion

Shift to deviations $x_i = z_i - z_i^*$ from an equilibrium pattern, which turns the nonlinearities into functions $\mathcal S_i$ with $\mathcal S_i(0) = 0$ and $|\mathcal S_i(x)| \le \ell_i|x|$. Use the Lyapunov–Krasovskii functional

$$\mathcal V(t) = \tfrac12\sum_i\int_\Omega \tau_i x_i(r,t)^2\,dr + \sum_i\int_\Omega\beta_i(r)\int_\Omega\int_{-d_i(r,r')}^0 x_i(r',t+\theta)^2\,d\theta\,dr'\,dr,$$

with positive weights $\beta_i$. Along solutions, $\dot{\mathcal V}$ is bounded by negative quadratic terms in the current and delayed states minus a gain-dependent term $\gamma(k_c)\int x_1^2$ with $\gamma(k_c) \to \infty$ as $k_c \to \infty$; the coupling terms are controlled with Young's inequality through tunable weights $\lambda_{ij}$ and the quantities $\ell_i^2\int_\Omega w_{ij}(r,r')^2dr'$. Large $k_c$ dominates every term involving the STN, so only the GPe self-coupling remains, and the norm condition on $w_{22}$ makes it negative, giving exponential decay of $\mathcal V$.
