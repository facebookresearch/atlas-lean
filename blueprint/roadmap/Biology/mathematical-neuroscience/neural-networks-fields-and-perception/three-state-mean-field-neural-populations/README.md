# Three-state mean-field neural populations

Mean-field equations for populations of neurons that cycle through sensitive, active and refractory states, derived from a continuous-time Markov network model. Keeping the refractory fraction as a separate variable doubles the dimension of the Wilson–Cowan equations and permits oscillations, and even chaos, in purely excitatory networks.
