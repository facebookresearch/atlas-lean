# Robust heteroclinic cycles in neural networks

Heteroclinic cycles between saddle states model sequential switching such as winnerless competition in neural microcircuits. They are structurally unstable in general vector fields, but become robust when symmetry or coupling structure forces invariant subspaces; dimension counts relative to those subspaces decide robustness.
