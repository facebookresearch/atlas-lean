# Spatial derivative function of the infinite expansion for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with
`∑_n |a_n| < ∞` and every time `t > 0`, the derivative of the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) `x ↦ V(t, x)` is the function

`x ↦ ∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n e^(-k_n t) φ_n'(x)`, where `φ_n'(x) = -(n π / L) sin (n π x / L)`

is the [derivative of the n-th mode](deriv-cos-mode.md) and `k_n` its [rate](decay-rate.md).
