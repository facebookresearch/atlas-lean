# Replacing L by −L negates the sealed-cable Green's function

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L`, `x₀`, `t` and `x`, the [Green's function of the sealed cable](sealed-green.md) of length `-L` is minus that of length `L`:

`G_(-L)(t, x; x₀) = -G_L(t, x; x₀)`.

Replacing `L` by `-L` leaves the modes and rates unchanged and negates every
[point-charge coefficient](green-coeff.md).
