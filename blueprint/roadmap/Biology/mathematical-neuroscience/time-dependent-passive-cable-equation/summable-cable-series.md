# For τ > 0 and t ≥ 0, absolutely summable aₙ give absolute convergence

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with `∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time
`t ≥ 0` and every real `x`, the terms `a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)` of the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) form an absolutely convergent series. Each term is bounded by
`|a_n| e^(-t/τ) ≤ |a_n|`.
