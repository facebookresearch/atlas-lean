# For τ > 0 and absolutely summable aₙ, a uniform bound for t ≥ 0

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with `∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time
`t ≥ 0` and every real `x`, the [infinite expansion](cable-series.md) satisfies

`|V(t, x)| ≤ e^(-t/τ) ∑_{n ≥ 0} |a_n|`.

The bound does not depend on `x`: the expansion decays uniformly at least at the membrane rate
`1 / τ`.
