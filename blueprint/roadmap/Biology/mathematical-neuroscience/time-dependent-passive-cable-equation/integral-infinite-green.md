# For t > 0, the infinite-cable kernel integrates to e^(−t/τ)

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real source position `x₀` and every time `t > 0`, the
[Green's function of the infinite cable](infinite-green.md) has total charge

`∫_ℝ G(t, x; x₀) dx = e^(-t/τ)`:

the injected unit charge leaks through the membrane at exactly the rate `1 / τ`.
