# Away from the input site, the infinite-cable kernel vanishes as t → 0⁺

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0` and positions `x ≠ x₀`, the [Green's function of the infinite cable](infinite-green.md) tends to `0` as the time
decreases to `0`: `G(t, x; x₀) → 0` as `t → 0⁺`. Together with the total charge `e^(-t/τ) → 1`
([total charge](integral-infinite-green.md)), this expresses that the input at time `0` is a unit
charge concentrated at `x₀`; the statement is pointwise in `x`.
