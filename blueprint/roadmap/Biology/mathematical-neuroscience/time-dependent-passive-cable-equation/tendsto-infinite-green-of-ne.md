# Away from the input site, the infinite-cable kernel vanishes as t → 0⁺

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0` and positions `x ≠ x₀`, the [Green's function of the infinite cable](infinite-green.md) tends to `0` as the time
decreases to `0`: `G(t, x; x₀) → 0` as `t → 0⁺`, pointwise in `x`. Together with the total charge
`e^(-t/τ) → 1` ([total charge](integral-infinite-green.md)), this is the pointwise form of the
localization of the input at `x₀`; it does not by itself give convergence to a unit point charge at
`x₀` against test functions.
