# Bounded coefficients: cosine coefficients of the expansion at t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`, real coefficients `a_n`
bounded by a real `B` (`|a_n| ≤ B` for every `n`), every time `t > 0` and every natural number
`m`, the [infinite expansion](cable-series.md) satisfies

`∫₀ᴸ V(t, x) cos (m π x / L) dx = a_m e^(-k_m t) c_m`,

where `k_m` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `m`-th mode and `c_m` its self-inner-product:
`c_0 = L` and `c_m = L / 2` for `m ≥ 1`. The integral is oriented: for `L < 0` it is minus the
integral over `[L, 0]`.
