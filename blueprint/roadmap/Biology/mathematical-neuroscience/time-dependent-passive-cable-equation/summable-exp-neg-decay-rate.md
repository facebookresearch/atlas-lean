# For τ > 0, ε > 0 and ℓ, L ≠ 0, the mode factors are summable

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0`, `L ≠ 0` and `ε > 0`, the factors `e^(-k_n ε)`,
`n = 0, 1, 2, …`, form a summable series, where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th
mode: they are bounded by the geometric sequence `e^(-c n)` with `c = ℓ² π² ε / (L² τ) > 0`.
For `ℓ = 0` or `L = 0` every factor equals `e^(-ε/τ)` and the series diverges.
