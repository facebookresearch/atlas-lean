# Termwise second spatial derivative of the expansion for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with
`∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time `t > 0` and every real `x`, the spatial derivative `∂ₓV(t, ·)` of the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) is differentiable at `x`, with derivative

`∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n e^(-k_n t) (-(n π / L)² cos (n π x / L))`,

where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th mode.
