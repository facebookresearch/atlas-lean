# Splitting off the uniform mode of the finite expansion

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L`, `t` and `x`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers not containing
`0` and all real coefficients `a_n`, the [finite expansion](cable-sum.md) over `s ∪ {0}` is the
uniform mode plus the expansion over `s`:

`V_{s ∪ {0}}(t, x) = a_0 e^(-t/τ) + V_s(t, x)`.

This holds for every real `τ`; at `τ = 0` both `e^(-t/τ)` and `e^(-k_0 t)` are `1`, by the
convention `u / 0 = 0`.
