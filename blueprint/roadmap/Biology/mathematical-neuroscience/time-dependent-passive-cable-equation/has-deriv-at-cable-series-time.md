# Termwise time derivative of the infinite expansion for t > 0

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with `∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time
`t > 0` and every real `x`, the [infinite expansion](cable-series.md) `s ↦ V(s, x)` is
differentiable at `t`, with derivative

`∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n (-k_n) e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)`,

where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th mode.
