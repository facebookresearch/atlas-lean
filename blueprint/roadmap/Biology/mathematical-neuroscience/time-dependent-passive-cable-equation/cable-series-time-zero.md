# Initial profile of the infinite expansion

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L` and `x` and all real coefficients `a_n`, the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) at time `0` is the cosine series

`V(0, x) = ∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n cos (n π x / L)`.

No summability is needed: the two series have the same terms, so both are `0` by convention when
they are not summable.
