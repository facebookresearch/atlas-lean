# Time shift of the infinite expansion

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L`, `ε` and `t` and all real coefficients `a_n`, the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) at time `t` equals, as a function of position, the expansion
with coefficients `a_n e^(-k_n ε)` at time `t - ε`:

`∑_n a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L) = ∑_n (a_n e^(-k_n ε)) e^(-k_n (t - ε)) cos (n π x / L)`

for every real `x`, where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th mode. No summability is
needed: the two series have the same terms, so both are `0` by convention when they are not
summable.
