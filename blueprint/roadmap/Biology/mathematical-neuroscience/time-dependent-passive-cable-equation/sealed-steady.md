# Steady profile of a cable sealed at x = L

For real numbers `ℓ`, `L` and `x`, the steady profile of a cable sealed at `x = L` is

`W(x) = cosh ((L - x) / ℓ)`.

For `ℓ ≠ 0` it solves the steady cable equation `ℓ² W'' = W` ([equation](sealed-steady-steady.md))
and has zero slope at `x = L` ([far end](deriv-sealed-steady-length.md)), so multiples of `W` are
the steady responses of a cable sealed at `x = L` to a constant current injected at `x = 0`. The
formula is defined for all real inputs: with the convention `u / 0 = 0`, `W` is the constant `1`
when `ℓ = 0`.
