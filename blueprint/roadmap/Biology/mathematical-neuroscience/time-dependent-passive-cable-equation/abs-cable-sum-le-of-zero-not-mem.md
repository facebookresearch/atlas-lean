# For τ > 0, without the uniform mode a bound at rate k₁ for t ≥ 0

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers not containing `0`,
all real coefficients `a_n`, every time `t ≥ 0` and every real `x`, the
[finite expansion](cable-sum.md) satisfies

`|V(t, x)| ≤ e^(-k_1 t) ∑_{n ∈ s} |a_n|`,

where `k_1` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the first nonuniform mode: without the uniform mode the
expansion decays at least at rate `k_1`, since `k_1 ≤ k_n` for every `n ≥ 1`
([monotonicity](decay-rate-mono.md)).
