# For τ > 0, the finite expansion decays to rest

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all real
coefficients `a_n`, the [finite expansion](cable-sum.md) tends to `0` at every position `x`:
`V(t, x) → 0` as `t → ∞`. It follows from the [uniform bound](abs-cable-sum-le.md)
`|V(t, x)| ≤ e^(-t/τ) ∑_{n ∈ s} |a_n|` for `t ≥ 0`.
