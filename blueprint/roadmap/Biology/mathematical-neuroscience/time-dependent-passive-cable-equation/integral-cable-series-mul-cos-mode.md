# For absolutely summable aₙ and t ≥ 0, cosine coefficients of the sum

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `L ≠ 0`, every real `ℓ`, real coefficients `a_n` with
`∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time `t ≥ 0` and every natural number `m`, the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) satisfies

`∫₀ᴸ V(t, x) cos (m π x / L) dx = a_m e^(-k_m t) c_m`,

where `k_m` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `m`-th mode and `c_m` its self-inner-product
`∫₀ᴸ cos (m π x / L)² dx`: `c_0 = L` and `c_m = L / 2` for `m ≥ 1`. The integral is oriented: for
`L < 0` it is minus the integral over `[L, 0]`.
