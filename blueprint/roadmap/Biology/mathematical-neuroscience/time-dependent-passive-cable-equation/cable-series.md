# Sealed-end mode expansion as an infinite series

For real numbers `τ`, `ℓ` and `L`, real coefficients `a_n` (`n = 0, 1, 2, …`) and real `t` and
`x`, the sealed-end mode expansion is

`V(t, x) = ∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)`,

where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th [mode](cos-mode.md). The sum is taken with
the convention that a series that is not summable (for real terms: not absolutely convergent) has
sum `0`. For `τ > 0`, `t ≥ 0` and `∑_n |a_n| < ∞` the series converges absolutely
([summability](summable-cable-series.md)).
