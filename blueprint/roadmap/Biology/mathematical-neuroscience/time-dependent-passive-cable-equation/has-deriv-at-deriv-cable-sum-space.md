# Second spatial derivative of the finite expansion

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L`, `t` and `x`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all real
coefficients `a_n`, the spatial derivative `∂ₓV(t, ·)` of the [finite expansion](cable-sum.md) is
differentiable at `x`, with derivative

`∑_{n ∈ s} a_n e^(-k_n t) (-(n π / L)² cos (n π x / L))`,

where `k_n` is the [rate](decay-rate.md) of the `n`-th mode.
