# For ℓ ≠ 0, the sealed profile solves the steady cable equation

For real `ℓ ≠ 0` and all real `L` and `x`, the [steady profile](sealed-steady.md)
`W(x) = cosh ((L - x) / ℓ)` satisfies the steady cable equation

`ℓ² W''(x) = W(x)`,

where `W''` is the derivative of `W'`. The condition `ℓ ≠ 0` is needed: for `ℓ = 0` the left-hand
side is `0` and `W = 1`.
