# Bounded coefficients give a solution of the cable equation for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0` and real coefficients `a_n`
bounded by a real `B` (`|a_n| ≤ B` for every `n`), the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) `V(t, x) = ∑_n a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)` is a
[classical solution](is-cable-solution.md) of `τ ∂ₜV = ℓ² ∂ₓ²V - V` on `[0, L]` at every time
`t > 0`. Bounded coefficients include those of a point charge ([coefficients](green-coeff.md)). At
a time `t > 0` the expansion is the [shifted expansion](cable-series-eq-shift.md) from time `t / 2`,
whose coefficients `a_n e^(-k_n t / 2)` are absolutely summable
([summable factors](summable-exp-neg-decay-rate.md)).
