# For τ > 0, the charging transient tends to the steady profile

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ`, `L`, `c` and `x`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all
real coefficients `a_n`, the [charging transient](charging-sum.md) converges to the steady profile:

`V(t, x) → c cosh ((L - x) / ℓ)` as `t → ∞`,

since its [finite expansion](cable-sum.md) part [decays to rest](tendsto-cable-sum.md).
