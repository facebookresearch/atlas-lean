# The sealed-cable Green's function solves the cable equation for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0` and every real source position `x₀`, the
[Green's function of the sealed cable](sealed-green.md) `G(t, x; x₀)` is a [classical solution](is-cable-solution.md) of
`τ ∂ₜG = ℓ² ∂ₓ²G - G` on `[0, L]` at every time `t > 0`.
