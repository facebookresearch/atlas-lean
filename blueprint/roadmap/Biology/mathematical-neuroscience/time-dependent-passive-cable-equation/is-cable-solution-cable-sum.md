# For τ ≠ 0, the finite expansion solves the cable equation

For real `τ ≠ 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all real
coefficients `a_n`, the [finite expansion](cable-sum.md)
`V(t, x) = ∑_{n ∈ s} a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)` is a
[classical solution](is-cable-solution.md) of `τ ∂ₜV = ℓ² ∂ₓ²V - V` on `[0, L]` at every time
`t ∈ ℝ`.
