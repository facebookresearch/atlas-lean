# Termwise spatial derivative of the infinite expansion for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real `L`, real coefficients `a_n` with
`∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, every time `t > 0` and every real `x`, the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) `y ↦ V(t, y)` is differentiable at `x`, with derivative

`∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n e^(-k_n t) φ_n'(x)`, where `φ_n'(x) = -(n π / L) sin (n π x / L)`

is the [derivative of the n-th mode](deriv-cos-mode.md) and `k_n` its [rate](decay-rate.md). At a
positive time the factors `e^(-k_n t)` compensate the growth of `n π / L`, so the differentiated
series converges uniformly.
