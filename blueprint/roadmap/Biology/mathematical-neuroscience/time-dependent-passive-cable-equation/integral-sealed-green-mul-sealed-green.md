# Semigroup law of the sealed-cable Green's function

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`, all times `s > 0` and `t > 0` and all real `x` and
`z`, the [Green's function of the sealed cable](sealed-green.md) satisfies

`∫₀ᴸ G(s, x; y) G(t, y; z) dy = G(t + s, x; z)`.

For `L > 0` and `z ∈ [0, L]`, propagating the response to a unit charge at `z` for time `t` and
then for time `s` gives the response at time `t + s` (the Chapman–Kolmogorov relation); for
`L < 0` the integral is oriented.
