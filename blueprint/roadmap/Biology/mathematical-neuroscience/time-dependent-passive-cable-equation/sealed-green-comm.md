# Reciprocity of the sealed-cable Green's function

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L`, `x₀`, `t` and `x`, the [Green's function of the sealed cable](sealed-green.md) satisfies

`G(t, x; x₀) = G(t, x₀; x)`:

the response at `x` to an input at `x₀` equals the response at `x₀` to an input at `x`. The two
series have the same terms, so the identity holds at every time, also where they are not summable
and both are `0` by convention.
