# For τ > 0 and absolutely summable aₙ, the expansion decays to rest

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L` and real coefficients `a_n` with `∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) tends to `0` at every position `x`: `V(t, x) → 0` as
`t → ∞`. It follows from the [uniform bound](abs-cable-series-le.md)
`|V(t, x)| ≤ e^(-t/τ) ∑_n |a_n|` for `t ≥ 0`.
