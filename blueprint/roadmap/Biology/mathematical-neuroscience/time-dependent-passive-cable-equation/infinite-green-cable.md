# At t > 0 the infinite-cable kernel solves the cable equation on ℝ

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real source position `x₀`, every time `t > 0` and
every real position `x`, including `x < 0`, the [Green's function of the infinite cable](infinite-green.md) `G(t, x; x₀)` is
differentiable in time at `t`, differentiable in position at `x` together with its spatial
derivative, and satisfies

`τ ∂ₜG(t, x; x₀) = ℓ² ∂ₓ²G(t, x; x₀) - G(t, x; x₀)`.
