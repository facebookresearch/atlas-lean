# For absolutely summable aₙ, a cable solution at every t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0` and `ℓ ≠ 0`, every real `L` and real coefficients `a_n` with
`∑_n |a_n| < ∞`, the [infinite expansion](cable-series.md)
`V(t, x) = ∑_{n ≥ 0} a_n e^(-k_n t) cos (n π x / L)` is a
[classical solution](is-cable-solution.md) of `τ ∂ₜV = ℓ² ∂ₓ²V - V` on `[0, L]` at every time
`t > 0`.
