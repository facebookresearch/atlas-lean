# Bounded coefficients give sealed ends for t > 0

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0` and real coefficients `a_n`
bounded by a real `B` (`|a_n| ≤ B` for every `n`), the
[infinite expansion](cable-series.md) has [sealed ends](sealed-ends.md) at every time `t > 0`:
`∂ₓV(t, 0) = ∂ₓV(t, L) = 0`.
