# With bounded coefficients, the profile at t > 0 is continuous

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`, real coefficients `a_n`
bounded by a real `B` (`|a_n| ≤ B` for every `n`) and every time `t > 0`, the profile
`x ↦ V(t, x)` of the [infinite expansion](cable-series.md) is continuous on `ℝ`.
