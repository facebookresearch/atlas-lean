# For τ > 0, uniform bound on the finite expansion at times t ≥ 0

For real `τ > 0`, all real `ℓ` and `L`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers, all real
coefficients `a_n`, every time `t ≥ 0` and every real `x`, the
[finite expansion](cable-sum.md) satisfies

`|V(t, x)| ≤ e^(-t/τ) ∑_{n ∈ s} |a_n|`.

The bound does not depend on `x`: the expansion decays uniformly at least at the membrane rate
`1 / τ`, since `1 / τ ≤ k_n` for every mode ([rates](one-div-le-decay-rate.md)).
