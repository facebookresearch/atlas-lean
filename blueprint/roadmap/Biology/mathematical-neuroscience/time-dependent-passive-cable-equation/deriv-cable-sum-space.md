# Spatial derivative of the finite expansion as a function

For all real `τ`, `ℓ`, `L` and `t`, every finite set `s` of natural numbers and all real
coefficients `a_n`, the derivative of the [finite expansion](cable-sum.md) `x ↦ V(t, x)` is the
function

`x ↦ ∑_{n ∈ s} a_n e^(-k_n t) φ_n'(x)`, where `φ_n'(x) = -(n π / L) sin (n π x / L)`

is the [derivative of the n-th mode](deriv-cos-mode.md) and `k_n` its [rate](decay-rate.md).
