# For τ ≠ 0 and ℓ, L > 0, Rall's electrotonic-length formula

For real numbers `τ ≠ 0`, `ℓ > 0` and `L > 0`, the cable length in units of the length constant is
recovered from the [rates](decay-rate.md) `k_0` and `k_1`:

`L / ℓ = π / √(k_1 / k_0 - 1)`.

Here `k_1 / k_0 - 1 = (π ℓ / L)² > 0`. For `τ > 0`, `k_0` and `k_1` are the two smallest decay
rates ([monotonicity](decay-rate-mono.md)).
