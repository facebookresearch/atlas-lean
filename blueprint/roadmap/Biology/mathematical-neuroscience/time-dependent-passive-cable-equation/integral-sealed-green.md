# For t > 0, the sealed-cable Green's function integrates to e^(−t/τ)

For real numbers `τ > 0`, `ℓ ≠ 0` and `L ≠ 0`, every real source position `x₀` and
every time `t > 0`, the [Green's function of the sealed cable](sealed-green.md) satisfies

`∫₀ᴸ G(t, x; x₀) dx = e^(-t/τ)`:

the total potential after a unit charge decays at exactly the membrane rate `1 / τ`, wherever the
input is. The integral is oriented: for `L < 0` it is minus the integral over `[L, 0]`, which
compensates the sign of the kernel ([sign](sealed-green-neg-length.md)). The physical case is
`L > 0` and `x₀ ∈ [0, L]`.
