# The squid I∞ termwise derivative formula is positive for −12 ≤ V ≤ 115

For the [squid parameters](squid.md) and every $V$ with $-12 \le V \le 115$, the
[termwise derivative formula](steady-current-deriv.md) of the steady-state current is positive. The
proof splits $[-12, 115]$ into 18 cells and bounds $m_\infty$, $h_\infty$ and $n_\infty$ by rational
enclosures at the cell endpoints.

The squid steady-state current is [strictly increasing](strict-mono-on-steady-current-squid.md) on
the same interval.
