# The squid steady-state current has positive slope for −12 ≤ V ≤ 115

For the [squid parameters](squid.md) and every $V$ with $-12 \le V \le 115$, the
[derivative of the steady-state current](steady-current-deriv.md) is positive: $I_\infty'(V) > 0$.
The proof splits $[-12, 115]$ into 18 cells and bounds $m_\infty$, $h_\infty$ and $n_\infty$ by
rational enclosures at the cell endpoints.
