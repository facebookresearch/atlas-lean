# Logarithmic derivative λ_h of α_h/β_h

For every real $V$, $\lambda_h(V) = -1/20 - (1 - \beta_h(V))/10$, where
$\beta_h(V) = 1/(e^{(30 - V)/10} + 1)$ is the [sodium inactivation rate](beta-h.md). By the chain
rule, it is the derivative of $\log(\alpha_h/\beta_h)$ at $V$, with $\alpha_h(V) = 0.07\,e^{-V/20}$.
