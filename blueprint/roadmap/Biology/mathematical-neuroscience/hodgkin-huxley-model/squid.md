# Squid giant axon parameters

The Hodgkin–Huxley [parameter set](params.md) of the squid giant axon: $C = 1$ µF/cm²;
$g_{Na} = 120$, $g_K = 36$ and $g_L = 0.3$ mS/cm²; $E_{Na} = 115$ and $E_K = -12$ mV; the
[leak reversal potential](squid-el.md) $E_L$ that makes the total ionic current vanish at the
resting state; and no applied current, $I = 0$. Voltages are depolarizations from rest, in mV. The
parameters are [valid](squid-valid.md).
