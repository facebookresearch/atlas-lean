# Steady-state sodium activation m∞ is strictly increasing

The [steady-state sodium activation](m-inf.md) $m_\infty$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$:
its derivative $m_\infty (1 - m_\infty)\,\lambda_m$ is positive, since $0 < m_\infty < 1$ and the
[logarithmic derivative](lam-m.md) satisfies $\lambda_m \ge 1/18$.
