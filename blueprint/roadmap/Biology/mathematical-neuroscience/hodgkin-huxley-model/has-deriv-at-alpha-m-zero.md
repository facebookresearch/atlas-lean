# Derivative of the sodium activation opening rate at rest

The [sodium activation opening rate](alpha-m.md) $\alpha_m$ is differentiable at the resting
potential $V = 0$, with

$$\alpha_m'(0) = \frac{\tfrac32 e^{5/2} + 1}{10\,(e^{5/2} - 1)^2}.$$

This number satisfies $0 < \alpha_m'(0) \le 0.0164$ ([bounds](deriv-alpha-m-zero-le.md)).
