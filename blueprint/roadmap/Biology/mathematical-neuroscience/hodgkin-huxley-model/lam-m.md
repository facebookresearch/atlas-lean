# Logarithmic derivative λ_m of α_m/β_m

For every real $V$,

$$\lambda_m(V) = \frac{-\varphi'(u)}{10\,\varphi(u)} + \frac{1}{18}, \qquad u = \frac{25 - V}{10},$$

where $\varphi$ is the [extension of $x/(e^x - 1)$ by $1$ at $0$](vtrap.md), which is
[positive](vtrap-pos.md). Since $\alpha_m(V) = \varphi(u)$ and $\beta_m(V) = 4 e^{-V/18}$
([rates](alpha-m.md)), by the chain rule $\lambda_m(V)$ is the derivative of
$\log(\alpha_m/\beta_m)$ at $V$.
