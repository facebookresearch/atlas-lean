# The function x/(eˣ − 1) extended by 1 at 0

For a real number $x$, let $\varphi(x) = x/(e^x - 1)$ if $x \ne 0$, and $\varphi(0) = 1$. The value
$1$ is the limit of $x/(e^x - 1)$ as $x \to 0$, so $\varphi$ extends $x/(e^x - 1)$
[continuously](cont-diff-vtrap.md) across its removable singularity at $0$. The Hodgkin–Huxley
opening rates [$\alpha_m$](alpha-m.md) and [$\alpha_n$](alpha-n.md) are written with $\varphi$,
which defines them at $V = 25$ and $V = 10$ mV.
