# The extension φ of x/(eˣ − 1) is positive

For every real $x$, $\varphi(x) > 0$, where $\varphi$ is the
[extension of $x/(e^x - 1)$ by $1$ at $0$](vtrap.md), $\varphi(x) = x/(e^x - 1)$ for $x \ne 0$ and
$\varphi(0) = 1$. For $x \ne 0$ the numerator $x$ and the denominator $e^x - 1$ have the same sign.
