# Logarithmic derivative λ_n of α_n/β_n

For every real $V$,

$$\lambda_n(V) = \frac{-\varphi'(u)}{10\,\varphi(u)} + \frac{1}{80}, \qquad u = \frac{10 - V}{10},$$

where $\varphi$ is the [extension of $x/(e^x - 1)$ by $1$ at $0$](vtrap.md), which is
[positive](vtrap-pos.md). Since $\alpha_n(V) = \varphi(u)/10$ and $\beta_n(V) = 0.125\,e^{-V/80}$
([rates](alpha-n.md)), by the chain rule $\lambda_n(V)$ is the derivative of
$\log(\alpha_n/\beta_n)$ at $V$.
