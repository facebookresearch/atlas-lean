# Derivative of x/(eˣ − 1) away from 0

For every real $x \ne 0$, the [extension $\varphi$ of $x/(e^x - 1)$](vtrap.md),
$\varphi(x) = x/(e^x - 1)$ for $x \ne 0$ and $\varphi(0) = 1$, is differentiable at $x$, with

$$\varphi'(x) = \frac{e^x - 1 - x e^x}{(e^x - 1)^2}.$$

At $x = 0$ the derivative is [$-1/2$](deriv-vtrap-zero.md).
