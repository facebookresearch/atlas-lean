# Gating kinetics α(V)(1 − y) − β(V)y

For functions $\alpha, \beta \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ (an opening and a closing rate), a
voltage $V$ and a gating value $y$, the gating kinetics are

$$G(V, y) = \alpha(V)\,(1 - y) - \beta(V)\,y,$$

the right-hand side of the first-order gating equation $y' = \alpha(V)(1 - y) - \beta(V)\,y$. The
formula is taken for all real inputs; in the model the rates are positive and $y \in [0, 1]$ is the
fraction of open gates.
