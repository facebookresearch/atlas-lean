# The Jacobian at the squid resting state is Hurwitz

The [Jacobian matrix](jac.md) of the Hodgkin–Huxley vector field of the [squid parameters](squid.md)
at the [resting state](rest.md) is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md):
all its complex eigenvalues have negative real part. It is an arrowhead matrix, to which the
[arrowhead criterion](is-hurwitz-arrowhead.md) applies with the bounds $m_\infty(0) \le 0.054$,
$h_\infty(0) \le 0.6$, $n_\infty(0) \ge 0.3$ and $\alpha_m'(0) \le 0.0164$.
