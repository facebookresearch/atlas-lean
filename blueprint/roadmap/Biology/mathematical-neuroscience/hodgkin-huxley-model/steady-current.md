# Steady-state current–voltage relation I∞

For a [parameter set](params.md) and a voltage $V$, the steady-state current is the
[total ionic current](ionic.md) with the gating variables at their steady states
[$m_\infty$](m-inf.md), [$h_\infty$](h-inf.md) and [$n_\infty$](n-inf.md):

$$I_\infty(V) = g_{Na}\, m_\infty(V)^3 h_\infty(V)\,(V - E_{Na}) +
g_K\, n_\infty(V)^4\,(V - E_K) + g_L\,(V - E_L).$$

It does not involve the applied current $I$. For [valid parameters](valid.md) the
[equilibria](field-eq-zero-iff.md) are the states $(V, m_\infty(V), h_\infty(V), n_\infty(V))$ with
$I_\infty(V) = I$.
