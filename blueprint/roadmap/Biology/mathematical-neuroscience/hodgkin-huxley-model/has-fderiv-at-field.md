# The Jacobian matrix is the derivative of the Hodgkin–Huxley field

For every [parameter set](params.md), valid or not, and every state $x \in \mathbb{R}^4$, the
[Hodgkin–Huxley vector field](field.md) is differentiable at $x$, and its derivative is the linear
map $v \mapsto J(x)\,v$ given by the [Jacobian matrix](jac.md) $J(x)$.
