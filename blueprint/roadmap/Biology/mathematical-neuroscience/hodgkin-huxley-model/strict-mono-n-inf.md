# Steady-state potassium activation n∞ is strictly increasing

The [steady-state potassium activation](n-inf.md) $n_\infty$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$:
its derivative $n_\infty (1 - n_\infty)\,\lambda_n$ is positive, since $0 < n_\infty < 1$ and the
[logarithmic derivative](lam-n.md) satisfies $\lambda_n \ge 1/80$.
