# With positive rates, gating kinetics vanish exactly at steady state

Let $\alpha, \beta \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $V$ be real with $\alpha(V) > 0$ and
$\beta(V) > 0$. For every real $y$,

$$\alpha(V)\,(1 - y) - \beta(V)\,y = 0 \iff y = \frac{\alpha(V)}{\alpha(V) + \beta(V)}:$$

the [gating kinetics](gate.md) vanish exactly at the [steady state](steady.md).
