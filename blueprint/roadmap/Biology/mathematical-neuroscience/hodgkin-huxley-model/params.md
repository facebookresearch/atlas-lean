# Hodgkin–Huxley membrane parameters

A parameter set of the Hodgkin–Huxley equations consists of eight real numbers: the membrane
capacitance $C$ (µF/cm²), the maximal sodium, potassium and leak conductances $g_{Na}$, $g_K$, $g_L$
(mS/cm²), the reversal potentials $E_{Na}$, $E_K$, $E_L$ (mV) and the applied current $I$ (µA/cm²).
No condition is imposed on them; the physical sign conditions are those of
[valid parameters](valid.md).
