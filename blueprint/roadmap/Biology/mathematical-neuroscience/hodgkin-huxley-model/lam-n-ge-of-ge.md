# Lower bound on λ_n for p ≤ V ≤ q with q ≥ 10

Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers with $q \ge 10$. For every $V$ with $p \le V \le q$, the
[logarithmic derivative](lam-n.md) $\lambda_n$ of $\alpha_n/\beta_n$ satisfies

$$\lambda_n(V) \ge \frac{1}{80} + \frac{1}{10}\Bigl(\frac12 + \frac{10 - q}{120}\Bigr);$$

the bound depends only on $q$.
