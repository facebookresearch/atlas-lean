# Derivative of a gating steady state

Let $\alpha, \beta \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable at $V$, with $\alpha'(V) = a$
and $\beta'(V) = b$, and let $\alpha(V) > 0$ and $\beta(V) > 0$. Then the [steady state](steady.md)
$y_\infty = \alpha/(\alpha + \beta)$ is differentiable at $V$, with

$$y_\infty'(V) = y_\infty(V)\,\bigl(1 - y_\infty(V)\bigr)
\Bigl(\frac{a}{\alpha(V)} - \frac{b}{\beta(V)}\Bigr).$$
