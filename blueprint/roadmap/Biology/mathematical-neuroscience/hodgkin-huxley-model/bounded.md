# For valid parameters, solutions from the gating cube stay bounded

Let the parameters be [valid](valid.md) and let $x$ be a [solution](is-solution.md) on $[0, \infty)$
whose initial state $x(0)$ lies in the [gating cube](gating-region.md) (gating variables in
$[0, 1]$). Then there is a [trapping interval](is-trapping.md) $[V_{lo}, V_{hi}]$ such that
$x(t) \in [V_{lo}, V_{hi}] \times [0, 1]^3$ for every $t \ge 0$.
