# Hurwitz criterion for a 4 × 4 arrowhead matrix

Let $a, b_1, b_2, b_3, c_1, c_2, c_3$ be real numbers and $k_1, k_2, k_3 > 0$. If $b_1 c_1 \ge 0$,
$b_2 c_2 \le 0$, $b_3 c_3 \le 0$ and $a + b_1 c_1 / k_1 < 0$, then the arrowhead matrix

$$\begin{pmatrix}
a & b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \\
c_1 & -k_1 & 0 & 0 \\
c_2 & 0 & -k_2 & 0 \\
c_3 & 0 & 0 & -k_3
\end{pmatrix}$$

is
[Hurwitz](../../../LinearAlgebra/special-matrices/m-matrices-and-hurwitz-stability/nonsingular-m-matrices/is-hurwitz.md):
every complex eigenvalue has negative real part.
