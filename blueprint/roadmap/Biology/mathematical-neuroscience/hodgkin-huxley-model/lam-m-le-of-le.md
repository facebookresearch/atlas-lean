# Upper bound on λ_m for p ≤ V ≤ q with q ≤ 25

Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers with $q \le 25$. For every $V$ with $p \le V \le q$, the
[logarithmic derivative](lam-m.md) $\lambda_m$ of $\alpha_m/\beta_m$ satisfies

$$\lambda_m(V) \le \frac{1}{18} + \frac{1}{10}\Bigl(\frac12 + \frac{25 - p}{120}\Bigr).$$
