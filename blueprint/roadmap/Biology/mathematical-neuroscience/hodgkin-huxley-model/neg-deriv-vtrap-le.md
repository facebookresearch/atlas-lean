# The extension φ of x/(eˣ − 1) satisfies −φ′ ≤ φ

For every real $x$, $-\varphi'(x) \le \varphi(x)$, where $\varphi$ is the
[extension of $x/(e^x - 1)$ by $1$ at $0$](vtrap.md), $\varphi(x) = x/(e^x - 1)$ for $x \ne 0$ and
$\varphi(0) = 1$. Since $\varphi > 0$, this bounds the logarithmic derivative:
$-\varphi'(x)/\varphi(x) \le 1$.
