# Steady-state sodium inactivation h∞

For every real $V$, $h_\infty(V) = \alpha_h(V)/(\alpha_h(V) + \beta_h(V))$, the
[steady state](steady.md) of the sodium inactivation kinetics with the rates
[$\alpha_h$](alpha-h.md) and [$\beta_h$](beta-h.md). Both rates are positive, so the denominator is
positive and $0 < h_\infty(V) < 1$.
