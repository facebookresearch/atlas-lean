# The sodium activation opening rate α_m is positive

For every real $V$, $\alpha_m(V) > 0$, where $\alpha_m$ is the
[sodium activation opening rate](alpha-m.md): $\alpha_m(V) = 0.1\,(25 - V)/(e^{(25 - V)/10} - 1)$
for $V \ne 25$ and $\alpha_m(25) = 1$. It is the [positive function](vtrap-pos.md) $\varphi$
evaluated at $(25 - V)/10$.
