# For valid parameters, solutions starting in a trapping box stay in it

Let the parameters be [valid](valid.md) and let $[V_{lo}, V_{hi}]$ be a
[trapping interval](is-trapping.md). Every [solution](is-solution.md) $x$ on $[0, \infty)$ with
$x(0)$ in the [box](box.md) $[V_{lo}, V_{hi}] \times [0, 1]^3$ satisfies
$x(t) \in [V_{lo}, V_{hi}] \times [0, 1]^3$ for every $t \ge 0$.
