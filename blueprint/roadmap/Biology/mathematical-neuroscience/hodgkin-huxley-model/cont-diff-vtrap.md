# The extension φ of x/(eˣ − 1) is C¹

The [extension $\varphi$ of $x/(e^x - 1)$ by $1$ at $0$](vtrap.md), $\varphi(x) = x/(e^x - 1)$ for
$x \ne 0$ and $\varphi(0) = 1$, is continuously differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, including at $0$,
where [its derivative is $-1/2$](deriv-vtrap-zero.md). It is the reciprocal of the positive $C^1$
function equal to $(e^x - 1)/x$ for $x \ne 0$ and to $1$ at $0$.
