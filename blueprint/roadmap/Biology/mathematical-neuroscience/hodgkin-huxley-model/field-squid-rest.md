# The resting state is an equilibrium of the squid equations

The [Hodgkin–Huxley vector field](field.md) of the [squid parameters](squid.md) vanishes at the
[resting state](rest.md) $(0, m_\infty(0), h_\infty(0), n_\infty(0))$: the gating variables are at
their steady states, and the [leak reversal potential](squid-el.md) makes the total ionic current,
hence the voltage derivative, $0$ there.
