# The squid steady-state current is positive for V ≥ 115

For the [squid parameters](squid.md) and every $V \ge 115$, the
[steady-state current](steady-current.md) satisfies $I_\infty(V) > 0$. Indeed
$V \ge E_{Na} = 115 > E_L > E_K = -12$ ([leak potential](squid-el-mem.md)), so the sodium and
potassium currents are nonnegative and the leak current is positive.
