# Auditory streaming competition models

Firing-rate models of how alternating tone sequences are heard as one integrated rhythm or two segregated streams. Two tonotopic populations, driven by periodic square-wave inputs, interact through fast direct excitation and slow delayed inhibition; in the singular slow–fast limit with a Heaviside gain, the possible periodic states and their existence regions can be derived exactly.
