# Compound Poisson models of higher-order spike correlations

Higher-order correlations among $N$ simultaneously recorded spike trains are measured by the cumulants of the population spike count. In a compound Poisson process (CPP) model, synchronous events of a carrier Poisson process are copied into several neurons at once; the cumulants of the population count are then linear in the rates of events of each size, which makes the maximal correlation order testable from low-order cumulants.
