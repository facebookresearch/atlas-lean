# Free-energy principle

The free-energy principle states that an adaptive agent minimizes variational free energy, an upper bound on the surprise of its sensory data under its generative model, by updating its beliefs (perception and learning) and by acting on the world. Under Gaussian assumptions free energy reduces to precision-weighted prediction error, and its time course can be used to define affective quantities such as emotional valence.
