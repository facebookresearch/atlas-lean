# Integrated information decomposition

Decomposition of the time-delayed mutual information between the past and future of a multivariate system into redundant, unique and synergistic modes of information flow, built on a product of two partial-information-decomposition lattices. Transfer entropy, active information storage and integrated-information measures become sums of these atoms.
