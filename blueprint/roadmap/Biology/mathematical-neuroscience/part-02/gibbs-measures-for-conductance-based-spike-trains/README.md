# Gibbs measures for conductance-based spike trains

Rigorous statistics of the raster plots generated by generalized integrate-and-fire networks with conductance-based synapses and noise: the spike process is a chain with complete connections whose transition probabilities are Gaussian tail probabilities, and it has a unique Gibbs distribution with infinite-range potential.
