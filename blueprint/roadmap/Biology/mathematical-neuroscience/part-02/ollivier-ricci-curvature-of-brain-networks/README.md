# Ollivier–Ricci curvature of brain networks

Coarse (Ollivier–Ricci) curvature transfers the Riemannian idea that small balls around nearby points are closer than their centres (positive curvature) or farther apart (negative curvature) to weighted graphs, using optimal transport between neighbourhood distributions. Applied to structural connectomes it gives an edge- and node-level measure linked to entropy and robustness.
