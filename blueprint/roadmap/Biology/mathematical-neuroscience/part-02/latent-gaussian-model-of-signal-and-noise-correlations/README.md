# Latent Gaussian model of signal and noise correlations

Signal correlations (shared stimulus tuning) and noise correlations (shared trial-to-trial variability) of a neural ensemble can be defined as normalized covariances of a hierarchical model in which a Gaussian latent process and a linear stimulus term drive Bernoulli spiking through a logistic link, and spikes are seen only through calcium dynamics and noisy fluorescence. Estimating the covariances directly from fluorescence avoids the bias of correlating deconvolved spikes.
