# Optimal control of arm movements

Point-to-point arm movements are modelled as solutions of optimal control problems for the limb's mechanical system, with joint torques as controls and an integral cost that the nervous system is assumed to minimize. Whether the cost contains a non-smooth energy term, such as the absolute work of the muscles, decides structural features of the optimal motor commands that can be compared with electromyographic recordings, for example silent periods of agonist and antagonist muscles in fast movements.
