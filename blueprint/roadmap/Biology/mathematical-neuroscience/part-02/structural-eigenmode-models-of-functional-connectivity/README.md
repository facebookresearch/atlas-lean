# Structural eigenmode models of functional connectivity

Eigenvectors of the normalised graph Laplacian of a structural connectome form an orthonormal basis of spatial patterns ordered by spatial frequency. Functional connectivity, static or time-resolved, can be approximated by re-weighting these structural eigenmodes, with weights given in closed form by Rayleigh quotients.
