# LFP spectra of excitatory–inhibitory networks

A sparsely connected network of leaky integrate-and-fire pyramidal cells and interneurons, driven by a time-varying thalamic input, produces local field potentials (LFPs) whose power spectrum encodes the stimulus in two largely independent channels: slow input fluctuations entrain low frequencies, while recurrent excitation and inhibition generate gamma-band oscillations. Mutual information and its redundancy across frequency bands quantify this code.
