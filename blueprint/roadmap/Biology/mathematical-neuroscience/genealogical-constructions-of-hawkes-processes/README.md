# Genealogical constructions of Hawkes processes

Branching (Crump–Mode–Jagers) representations of self-exciting point processes used for spike trains: linear Hawkes processes as birth times in a CMJ forest with immigration, and nonlinear Hawkes processes with inhibition or refractory periods as birth times of a pruned, type-dependent sub-forest.
