# Exact variance in a Bernoulli text

**Exact variance in a Bernoulli text** (Régnier and Szpankowski 1998, Theorem 2.2 (i),
equations (14)–(16) with `E_1 = E_2 = 0`): for i.i.d. letters with law `q` (`q ≥ 0`, total mass
`1`) on a finite alphabet, a pattern `H` of length `m ≥ 1`, `P(H) = ∏_j q(h_j)` and every
`n ≥ 2m - 1`, the variance `E O_n² - (E O_n)²` equals `n c_1 + c_2` with
`c_1 = P(H)(2A(1) - 1 - (2m - 1)P(H))` and
`c_2 = P(H)((m - 1)(3m - 1)P(H) - (m - 1)(2A(1) - 1) - 2A'(1))`.

## Sources

- Régnier and Szpankowski (1998), Theorem 2.2 (i).
