# Exact mean in a stationary Markov text

**Exact mean in a stationary Markov text** (Régnier and Szpankowski 1998, Theorem 2.2 (i),
equation (13)): for a stationary Markov chain `(π, P)` on a finite alphabet, a pattern `H` of
length `m ≥ 1` and `n ≥ m`,
`∑_{w} P(w) O_n(H)(w) = P(H) (n - m + 1)` with `P(H) = π(h_1) ∏_{j < m} P(h_j, h_{j+1})`.

## Sources

- Régnier and Szpankowski (1998), Theorem 2.2 (i).
