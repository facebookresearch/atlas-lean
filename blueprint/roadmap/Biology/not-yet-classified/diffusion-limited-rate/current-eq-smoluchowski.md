# Smoluchowski's rate constant

**Smoluchowski's rate constant** (Smoluchowski 1917; Debye 1942, equation (4′)): for
`D, kT, R > 0` and no interaction (`U = 0`, `U' = 0`), every steady radial profile `n` with current
`J`, `n(R) = 0` and `n(r) → n₀` has `J = 4π D R n₀`.

## Sources

- Debye (1942), equation (4′); Smoluchowski (1917).
