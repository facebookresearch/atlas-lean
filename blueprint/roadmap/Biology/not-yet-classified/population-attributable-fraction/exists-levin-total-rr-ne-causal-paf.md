# Levin's formula is biased under confounding, even with an adjusted risk ratio

**Levin's formula is biased under confounding, even with an adjusted risk ratio** (Khosravi
et al. 2021). There is a finite strata model with two strata, `P(z) > 0`, `∑_z P(z) = 1`,
`0 < e(z) < 1` and `0 ≤ r₀(z), r₁(z) ≤ 1`, in which Levin's formula with `Pe` and the risk ratio
standardized to the total population differs from the causal attributable fraction.

## Sources

- Khosravi et al. (2021), on Levin's formula being "biased in the presence of confounding even if the adjusted RR is used"; the example is derived.
