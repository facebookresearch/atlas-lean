# Second Cushing–Henson theorem

**Second Cushing–Henson theorem** (Elaydi and Sacker 2005; Kon 2004; Kocic 2005; Stević
2006, Theorem 1.3). Under the same hypotheses, the average of the positive `p`-periodic
solution over a period is at most the average carrying capacity, and strictly smaller when `K`
is not constant.

## Sources

- Cushing and Henson (2002), Conjecture 2; Stević (2006), Theorem 1.3.
