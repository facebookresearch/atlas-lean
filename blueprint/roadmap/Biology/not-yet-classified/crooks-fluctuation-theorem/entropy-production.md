# Entropy Production

The path log-ratio `ω[x] = ln ρ(x_0) - ln ρ'(x̄_n) - β Q[x]` of a path of the process with
initial density `ρ` and heat functional `Q` (that of its own protocol), where `ρ'` is the initial
density of the other process; `ρ` and `ρ'` are arbitrary positive laws. This is Crooks's entropy
production (Eq. (6)) when the reverse initial density matches the time reversal of the final
density of the forward process; in general it is only this finite log-ratio.
