# Crooks fluctuation theorem

**Crooks fluctuation theorem** (Crooks 1999, Eq. (FT)). Assume the setting
`MicroReversible` (`bar` an involution; positive initial probability vectors `ρ_F`, `ρ_R`;
stochastic matrices `M`, `MR`; odd heat `Q`; microscopic reversibility). Then for every real
`ω`, the probability `P_F(ω) = ∑_{x : ω_F[x] = ω} P_F[x]` that the forward process produces
entropy `ω` and the probability `P_R(-ω) = ∑_{y : ω_R[y] = -ω} P_R[y]` that the reverse process
produces entropy `-ω` satisfy `P_F(+ω) = e^{ω} P_R(-ω)`, where `ω_F` and `ω_R` are the entropy
productions of the forward and reverse processes (`entropyProduction` with `ρ_F, ρ_R` and with
`ρ_R, ρ_F`).
