# Dependence breaks the case-only design

**Dependence breaks the case-only design** (VanderWeele et al. 2010). There are positive
cell masses `a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h` with no multiplicative interaction on either scale,
`SRR = 1` and `SOR = 1`, whose case-only odds ratio `a g / (c e)` is not `1`; in such a law `G`
and `E` are dependent.

## Sources

- VanderWeele et al. (2010), on the sensitivity of the design to the independence assumption; the example is derived.
