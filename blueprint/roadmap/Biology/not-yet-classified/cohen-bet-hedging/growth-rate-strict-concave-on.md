# Strict concavity of the long-run growth rate

**Strict concavity of the long-run growth rate** (derived for Cohen's 1966 seed-bank model):
under the hypotheses of the criterion (`μ` a probability measure, `0 < s < 1`, `Y` measurable with
`Y > 0` and `log Y`, `1 / Y` integrable), if moreover `Y` is not almost surely equal to the
dormant survival `s` (it is not the case that `Y = s` almost everywhere), then
`ρ(H) = E[log(H Y + (1 - H) s)]` is strictly concave on `[0, 1]`. (If `Y = s` almost surely,
`ρ` is the constant `log s`.)

## Sources

- Model provenance — D. Cohen, "Optimizing reproduction in a randomly varying environment", J. Theor. Biol. 12 (1966) 119–129, doi:10.1016/0022-5193(66)90188-3; strict concavity derived from strict concavity of `log`.
