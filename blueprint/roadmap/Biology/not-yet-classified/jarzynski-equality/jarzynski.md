# Jarzynski equality for the isolated system

Under the standing [hypotheses](hypotheses.md) ($H_\lambda(q, p)$ is $C^2$, $\beta > 0$, $t_s > 0$, $\varphi$ is a flow of the switched Hamiltonian system on $[0, t_s]$, and $e^{-\beta H_0}$, $e^{-\beta H_1}$ are integrable): the [partition functions](partition-function.md) satisfy $Z_0 > 0$ and $Z_1 > 0$, the canonical average of $e^{-\beta W}$ (with the trajectory [work](work.md) $W$) satisfies

$$\int Z_0^{-1} e^{-\beta H_0(z_0)} e^{-\beta W(z_0)}\,dz_0 = \frac{Z_1}{Z_0} = e^{-\beta \Delta F},$$

where $\Delta F = F_1 - F_0 = -\beta^{-1} \ln Z_1 + \beta^{-1} \ln Z_0$.
