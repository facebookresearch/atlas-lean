# Semigroup identity on polynomials

For a polynomial `p` and `T ≥ 0`, the neutral
Wright–Fisher semigroup satisfies `P_T p - p = ∫₀ᵀ P_u (L p) du` in `C([0, 1], ℝ)`, where
`L = polyGenerator` is the neutral generator `x (1 - x) / 2 · p''`.
