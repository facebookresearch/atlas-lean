# The moduli ideal is toric

**The moduli ideal is toric** (Craciun–Dickenstein–Shiu–Sturmfels 2009, Theorem 9, first
part). For a mass-action graph whose connected components are strongly connected, with finitely
many nodes (`[Fintype V]`) and species (`[Fintype Sp]`), the moduli ideal `M_G = T_G ∩ ℚ[K]`
equals the toric ideal of the Cayley matrix `Cay_G(Y)`: the ideal of `ℚ[K]` generated by the
binomials `K^u - K^v` with `u, v ∈ ℕ^n` and `Cay_G(Y) u = Cay_G(Y) v`.

## Sources

- G. Craciun, A. Dickenstein, A. Shiu, B. Sturmfels, J. Symbolic Comput. 44 (2009) 1551–1565, Theorem 9.
