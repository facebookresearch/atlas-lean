# The moduli ideal is toric

**The moduli ideal is toric** (Craciun–Dickenstein–Shiu–Sturmfels 2009, Theorem 9, first
part). For a mass-action graph whose connected components are strongly connected, the moduli
ideal `M_G` is the ideal generated by the binomials `K^u - K^v` with
`Cay_G(Y) u = Cay_G(Y) v`, that is, the image of the toric ideal of the Cayley matrix under
`K_i ↦ K_i(κ)`.

## Sources

- G. Craciun, A. Dickenstein, A. Shiu, B. Sturmfels, J. Symbolic Comput. 44 (2009) 1551–1565, Theorem 9.
