# Codimension of the moduli ideal

**Codimension of the moduli ideal** (Craciun–Dickenstein–Shiu–Sturmfels 2009, Theorem 9,
second part). For a mass-action graph whose connected components are strongly connected, with
finitely many nodes (`[Fintype V]`) and species (`[Fintype Sp]`), the codimension (height) of the
toric ideal of the Cayley matrix in `ℚ[K_1, …, K_n]` equals the deficiency `δ`.

## Sources

- G. Craciun, A. Dickenstein, A. Shiu, B. Sturmfels, J. Symbolic Comput. 44 (2009) 1551–1565, Theorem 9.
