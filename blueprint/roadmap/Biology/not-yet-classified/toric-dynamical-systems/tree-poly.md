# Tree Poly

For a finite vertex set `V` (`[Fintype V]`), the Matrix-Tree polynomial
`K_i = ∑_{T an i-tree} κ^T` in the rate constants `κ_{jk}`, where `κ^T` is the product of the
`κ_{jk}` over the edges `j → k` of `T`. The `K_i` are algebraically independent (Lemma 5 of the
source), so the source's subring `ℚ[c, K]` of `ℚ[c, κ]` is a polynomial ring: the toric steady
state ideal and the moduli ideal are defined in the polynomial ring with one variable `K_i` per
node.
