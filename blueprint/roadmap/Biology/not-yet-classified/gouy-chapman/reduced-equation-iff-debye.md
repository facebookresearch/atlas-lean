# Reduction to the dimensionless equation

**Reduction to the dimensionless equation** (Tokmakoff, Section 6.7): for `e, ε, k_B, T, C₀ > 0`
and `λ = λ_D`, if `ψ` has derivative `ψ'` and `ψ'` has derivative
`(2 e C₀ / ε) sinh(e ψ(x) / (k_B T))` at every real `x`, then `Φ(X) = e ψ(λ X) / (k_B T)` has
derivative `Φ'(X) = e λ ψ'(λ X) / (k_B T)` and `Φ'` has derivative `sinh Φ(X)` at every real `X`;
conversely, if for some `λ > 0` such a `ψ` with `ψ(x₁) ≠ 0` for some `x₁` makes the rescaled `Φ`
satisfy `Φ_XX = sinh Φ` everywhere, then `λ = λ_D`.
