# The IFD is an ESS

**The IFD is an ESS** (Cressman and Křivan 2006, Appendix A): let the total density be
`M > 0` and let every patch payoff `Vᵢ` be strictly decreasing on `[0, ∞)`. If `p*` is an ideal
free distribution, then for every distribution `p ≠ p*` (every `pᵢ ≥ 0`, `∑ pᵢ = 1`),
`∑ᵢ pᵢ Vᵢ(pᵢ M) < ∑ᵢ pᵢ* Vᵢ(pᵢ M)`.

## Sources

- Cressman and Křivan (2006), Appendix A; Fretwell and Lucas (1969).
