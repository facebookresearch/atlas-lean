# No nested ESS supports

Let `A` be a real square matrix indexed by a finite type and `p` an
ESS. If `q` is a mixed strategy (`IsMixed q`) with `q ≠ p` whose support is contained in the
support of `p` (`qᵢ > 0 → pᵢ > 0` for every `i`), then `q` is not an ESS.

## Sources

- Bishop and Cannings (1976); Bishop and Cannings (1978), Theorem 3.
