# The sphere condition

**The sphere condition** (Ou-Yang and Helfrich 1987; Tu and Ou-Yang 2008): for `R > 0`, the
round sphere of radius `R` (`H = -1/R`, `K = 1/R²`, `∇²H = 0`) satisfies the shape equation iff
`p R² + 2λR - k_c c₀ (2 - c₀ R) = 0`.
