# Is Jump Hold

`(Y, E)` under `P` is a jump–hold realization of the chain with rates `q`: `P` is a
probability measure; each `Y n` is measurable; the finite-dimensional laws of `Y` are those of a
Markov chain with transition probabilities `jumpProb q` (and arbitrary initial law); the clocks
`E n` are independent and exponential of rate `1`; and the clocks are independent of `Y`.
