# Ukkonen's conjecture, proved by Pevzner

For a gram length `q ≥ 2` and two strings `s`,
`t` of length at least `q` each (so each contains a q-gram) with the same q-gram multiset, `s` and
`t` are related by a finite sequence of transpositions and rotations.

## Sources

- Pevzner (1995), settling Ukkonen (1992).
