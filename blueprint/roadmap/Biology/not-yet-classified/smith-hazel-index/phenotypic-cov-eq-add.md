# Phenotypic covariance decomposes

**Phenotypic covariance decomposes** (Hazel 1943, equation (8)). Let `μ` be a probability
measure on `Ω` and `M` a Smith–Hazel model on `n` traits under `μ`. Then the phenotypic covariance
matrix is the sum of the additive genetic and residual covariance matrices,
`M.phenotypicCov = M.geneticCov + M.residualCov`, entry by entry including cross-trait entries.
