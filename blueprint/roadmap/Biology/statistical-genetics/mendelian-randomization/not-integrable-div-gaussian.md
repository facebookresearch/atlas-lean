# Ratio of independent normal variables is not integrable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, and let $N, D$ be real random variables, independent under $\mu$, with laws $N(m_N, v_N)$ and $N(m_D, v_D)$, where $v_N, v_D \ge 0$, $v_D \ne 0$, and $m_N \ne 0$ or $v_N \ne 0$. Then $N/D$ is not $\mu$-integrable.
