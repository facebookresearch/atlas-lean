# Wald ratio identity

Let $(Z, X, Y, U, \alpha, \beta)$ be a [linear moment model](linear-moment-model.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ with $\operatorname{Cov}(Z, U) = 0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X) \ne 0$. Then

$$\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Z, Y)}{\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X)} = \beta.$$

For a two-valued $Z$ the left side is the grouping estimator of Wald (1940), the difference of the group means of $Y$ divided by that of $X$. This is a moment identity, not a causal identification result.

## Sources

- A. Wald, *The fitting of straight lines if both variables are subject to error*, Ann. Math. Statist. 11 (1940), 284–300.
