# Reverse two-stage least squares

In two-sample instrumental-variable analyses such as transcriptome-wide association studies and Mendelian randomization, the exposure and the outcome are measured in different samples. Instead of regressing the outcome on a genetically predicted exposure (two-stage least squares), one can regress the observed exposure on a genetically predicted outcome; the resulting estimate is attenuated by exactly the stage-1 coefficient of determination, which can be corrected.
