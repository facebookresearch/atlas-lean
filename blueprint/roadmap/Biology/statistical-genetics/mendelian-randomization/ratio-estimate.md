# Ratio estimate of a single SNP

Let $\iota$ be a set of SNPs with SNP–exposure associations $\hat\beta_{X}$ and SNP–outcome associations $\hat\beta_{Y}$ in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$. The ratio estimate of SNP $j$ is

$$\hat\beta_{Yj} / \hat\beta_{Xj},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$ (it is $0$ when $\hat\beta_{Xj} = 0$; such a SNP has [inverse-variance weight](ivw-weight.md) $0$).
