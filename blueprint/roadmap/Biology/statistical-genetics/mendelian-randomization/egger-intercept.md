# MR-Egger intercept

For a finite set $\iota$ of SNPs, weights $v$ and associations $\hat\beta_X, \hat\beta_Y$ in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$, the MR-Egger intercept is

$$a_E = \bar{\hat\beta}_{Y,v} - b_E\, \bar{\hat\beta}_{X,v},$$

where the bars denote [weighted means](weighted-mean.md) and $b_E$ is the [MR-Egger slope](egger-slope.md).
