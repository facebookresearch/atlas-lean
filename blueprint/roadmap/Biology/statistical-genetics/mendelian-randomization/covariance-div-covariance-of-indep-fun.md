# Wald ratio identity for an instrument independent of the residual

Let $(Z, X, Y, U, \alpha, \beta)$ be a [linear moment model](linear-moment-model.md) on a probability measure $\mu$ such that $Z$ and $U$ are independent under $\mu$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X) \ne 0$. Then

$$\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Z, Y)}{\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X)} = \beta.$$
