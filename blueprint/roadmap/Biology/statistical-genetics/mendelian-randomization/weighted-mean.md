# Weighted mean

For a finite set $\iota$ and real vectors $v, a \in \mathbb{R}^\iota$, the weighted mean of $a$ with weights $v$ is

$$\bar a_v = \frac{\sum_j v_j a_j}{\sum_j v_j},$$

with the convention $x/0 = 0$.

In demography, for a population with category sizes $N_i$ and rates $T_i$, the weighted mean of the rates
with the sizes as weights is the crude rate of the population; with the sizes $S_i$ of a standard
population as weights, it is the rate directly standardized to that standard.
