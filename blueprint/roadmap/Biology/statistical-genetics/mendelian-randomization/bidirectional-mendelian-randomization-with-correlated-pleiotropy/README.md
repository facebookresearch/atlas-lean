# Bidirectional Mendelian randomization with correlated pleiotropy

Inferring the direction of causation between two traits from GWAS summary statistics when the traits may cause each other and some variants act on both through shared pathways or confounders. On the scale of variant–trait correlations, each direction has a slope bounded by $1$ in absolute value, invalid instruments enter as sparse intercepts, and constrained maximum likelihood with BIC selects them consistently.
