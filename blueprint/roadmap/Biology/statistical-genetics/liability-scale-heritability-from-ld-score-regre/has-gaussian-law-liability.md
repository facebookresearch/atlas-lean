# Independent Gaussian G and E make G and G + E jointly Gaussian

Let `μ` be a measure on `Ω` and let `G` and `E` be real functions on `Ω`, each `μ`-almost everywhere
measurable with a Gaussian law under `μ` (possibly degenerate; this makes `μ` a probability
measure), and independent under `μ`. Then the pair `(G, G + E)` has a Gaussian law on `ℝ²`: in the
liability model `L = G + E`, the genetic value and the liability are jointly Gaussian.
